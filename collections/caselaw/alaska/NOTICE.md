# Alaska CAP jurisdiction collection

Case data and metadata are designated CC0 1.0 Universal by the Caselaw Access
Project, Harvard Law School Library. Credit is voluntary under CAP community
norms. Source: https://static.case.law/. Terms: https://case.law/terms/.

This collection preserves all 10,749 records assigned by CAP to jurisdiction 53,
Alaska. This includes 21 records from the U.S. District Court for the District
of Alaska printed in Federal Supplement, Third Series. Court names remain
explicit in the table of contents and metadata. CAP's jurisdiction assignment
is retained without silently reclassifying or dropping records; this is not an
exclusively state-court collection.

Published coverage is historical CAP coverage, not every Alaska decision or a
statement of current legal validity. OCR is retained without correction.
HTML is normalized to Markdown; separate opinions, page references, footnotes,
case metadata and source hashes are preserved. Website prose, scans and PDFs
are excluded. CAP provides no warranty or clearance of third-party rights.

Full CC0 text, CAP terms evidence and per-case provenance accompany each archive.
