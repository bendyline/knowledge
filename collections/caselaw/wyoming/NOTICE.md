# Wyoming CAP collection

This configuration selects case records whose CAP jurisdiction is Wyoming
(ID 33), including records in regional reporters. Coverage reports identify the
pinned metadata, source-selection scope, source hashes, and any unresolved
records. A matching advertised count is a reconciliation of that source scope,
not proof that every historical Wyoming decision exists in CAP.

CAP's case data and metadata are designated CC0 1.0 Universal under
<https://case.law/terms/>. Credit: Caselaw Access Project, Harvard Law School
Library. Credit and unrestricted sharing of improvements are voluntary norms.
CAP offers no warranty or clearance of third-party rights. Historical opinions
and OCR do not establish current legal authority. Each generated archive carries
the full CC0 text, pinned rights assessment, source hashes, and exact case list.

Pipeline code and original project documentation use the repository's MIT
license. Generated coverage and selection metadata derived from CAP retain the
CC0 designation. Generated documents, source ZIPs, the SQLite inventory, and
archives live in the ignored local workspace. Publication is disabled.
