## Watch: Steps to sign in to Outlook

Check out this video and others on the [YouTube channel](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2198010).

> [!VIDEO e30573c6-b0d5-4007-8ff6-8c9dfd4e39fe]

After you install the apps in Microsoft 365, set up Outlook to start using email, calendar, and contacts. Here's how:

1. Open the Start menu. Search for Outlook, and select it.
1. Enter your Microsoft 365 email address, and select **Connect**.
1. Enter any other email addresses that you want to use, such as your previous or personal email address. Select **Next**.
1. If prompted, enter a password, and then select **Sign in**.
1. After you add all of your accounts, choose if you want to set up Outlook mobile or wait until later.
1. Select **Done**. It might take several minutes for Outlook to download your email and other data.

Now you can view email for the accounts you added. You can also view your calendar, contacts, and tasks.
