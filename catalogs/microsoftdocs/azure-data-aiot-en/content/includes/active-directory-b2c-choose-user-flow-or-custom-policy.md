***Before you begin***, use the **Choose a policy type** selector at the top of this page to choose the type of policy you’re setting up. Azure Active Directory B2C offers two methods to define how users interact with your applications: through predefined [user flows](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/azure-docs/blob/4260367da6fe93d74e80662f882dd4e9f52b8924/articles/active-directory-b2c/user-flow-overview.md) or through fully configurable [custom policies](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/azure-docs/blob/4260367da6fe93d74e80662f882dd4e9f52b8924/articles/active-directory-b2c/custom-policy-overview.md). The steps required in this article are different for each method.
