Source URLs, immutable revisions, and transformation details are recorded per
file in provenance.jsonl. LICENSES/assessment.json records automatic comparison
against standard license texts under the standard-open-v1 policy.

## Scope

The Search subtree and two directly used Foundry include fragments are selected.
Include fragments are expanded into their containing documents and retained as
source records, but are not indexed as independent articles. Landing and FAQ
YAML pages become Markdown. Navigation remains YAML.

Eleven pages listed in manifest.json source.exclude depend on external publishing
mounts. Two upstream shared-content repositories are not publicly accessible.
Those pages are excluded explicitly until their dependencies can be imported;
links to them point to the corresponding Microsoft Learn pages. No partial
version of an excluded article is included in the catalog.
