Some regions support the ability to deploy Azure Bastion in an availability zone (or multiple, for zone redundancy). To deploy by zone, deploy Bastion using manually specified settings (don't deploy using the automatic default settings). Specify the desired availability zones at the time of deployment. You can't change availability zone settings after Azure Bastion is deployed.


Support for Availability Zones is currently in preview. During preview, the following regions are available:

* East US
* Australia East
* East US 2
* Central US
* Qatar Central
* South Africa North
* West Europe
* West US 2
* North Europe
* Sweden Central
* UK South
* Canada Central
