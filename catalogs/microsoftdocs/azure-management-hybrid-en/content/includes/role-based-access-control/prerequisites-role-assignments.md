To assign Azure roles, you must have:

- `Microsoft.Authorization/roleAssignments/write` permissions, such as [Role Based Access Control Administrator](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/azure-docs/blob/4260367da6fe93d74e80662f882dd4e9f52b8924/articles/role-based-access-control/built-in-roles.md#role-based-access-control-administrator) or [User Access Administrator](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/azure-docs/blob/4260367da6fe93d74e80662f882dd4e9f52b8924/articles/role-based-access-control/built-in-roles.md#user-access-administrator)
