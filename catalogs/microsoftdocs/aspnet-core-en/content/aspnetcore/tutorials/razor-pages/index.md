This series of tutorials explains the basics of building a Razor Pages web app.

For a more advanced introduction aimed at developers who are familiar with controllers and views, see [razor-pages/index](../../razor-pages/index.md).

If you're new to ASP.NET Core development and are unsure of which ASP.NET Core web UI solution will best fit your needs, see [tutorials/choose-web-ui](../choose-web-ui.md).


This series includes the following tutorials:

1. [Create a Razor Pages web app](razor-pages-start.md)
1. [Add a model to a Razor Pages app](model.md)
1. [Scaffold (generate) Razor pages](page.md)
1. [Work with a database](sql.md)
1. [Update Razor pages](da1.md)
1. [Add search](search.md)
1. [Add a new field](new-field.md)
1. [Add validation](validation.md)

At the end, you'll have an app that can display and manage a database of movies.
