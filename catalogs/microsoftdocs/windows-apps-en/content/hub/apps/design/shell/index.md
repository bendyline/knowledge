# Shell

Learn how to use tiles, badges, toasts, and notifications to provide entry points into your app and keep users up-to-date.



### [Toast notifications](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/blob/aabd22a9113af71e56bd6f5af8fde06df68162e0/hub/apps/design/shell/tiles-and-notifications/toast-notifications-overview.md)
Adaptive and interactive toast notifications let you create flexible notifications with text, images, and buttons/inputs.


### [Badge notifications](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/blob/aabd22a9113af71e56bd6f5af8fde06df68162e0/hub/apps/design/shell/tiles-and-notifications/badges.md)
A notification badge conveys summary or status information specific to your app.





### [Push notifications](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/blob/aabd22a9113af71e56bd6f5af8fde06df68162e0/hub/apps/design/shell/tiles-and-notifications/windows-push-notification-services--wns--overview.md)
Push notifications let third-party developers send toast, tile, badge, and raw updates from their own cloud service.


### [Taskbar](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/windows-dev-docs/blob/aabd22a9113af71e56bd6f5af8fde06df68162e0/hub/apps/design/shell/pin-to-taskbar.md)
Ask your user to pin your app to the taskbar to make it easier to find.





### [Title bar](../../develop/title-bar.md)
Extend your app content into the title bar and add custom content.
