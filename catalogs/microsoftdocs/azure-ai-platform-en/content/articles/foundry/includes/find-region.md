> **Tip:**
> To open your Foundry resource in Azure portal:
> 1. 

Sign in to 
[Microsoft Foundry](https://ai.azure.com/?cid=learnDocs)
. Make sure the **New Foundry** toggle is on. These steps refer to **Foundry (new)**.



> 1. Select **Manage** from the upper-right navigation.
> 1. Select **Resource details**.
> 1. Select **Manage this resource in the Azure portal** under the **View resource** heading in the upper-right.
