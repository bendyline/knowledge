[(*The Guardian*)](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/nov/05/car-french-island-ile-d-oleron-france)

- [Human rights in Malaysia](../articles/3645990.md)&#x20;

  - A [Malaysian](../articles/3607937.md) [high court](../articles/15497851.md) rules that [police](../articles/11760718.md) and [the government](../articles/47566717.md) are responsible for the [enforced disappearances](../articles/686148.md) of activist [Amri Che Mat](../articles/53719135.md) and pastor [Raymond Koh](../articles/53693520.md) in 2016 and 2017, respectively, ordering investigations to be reopened and awarding [damages](../articles/8134.md) exceeding [RM](../articles/360278.md) 33 million ([US\$](../articles/18717338.md)8 million) to their families. The decision marks the first judicial finding of state involvement in enforced disappearances in Malaysia. [(AP)](https://apnews.com/article/malaysia-religious-abduction-enforced-disappearance-dcd95173a461162fd382b8f31abd2e31)

- A [court](../articles/265708.md) in [Aachen](../articles/1520.md), [North Rhine-Westphalia](../articles/38414.md), [Germany](../articles/11867.md), sentences a 44-year-old [palliative care](../articles/275509.md) nurse to [life imprisonment](../articles/24154539.md) for [murdering](../articles/26346147.md) 10 patients and attempting to murder 27 others with [lethal injections](../articles/62745.md) at a hospital in [Würselen](../articles/2031511.md) between December 2023 and May 2024. [(AFP via CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nurse-life-sentence-murder-10-patients-germany/)

- [Japanese](../articles/15573.md) [defense minister](../articles/12082857.md) [Shinjiro Koizumi](../articles/27407451.md) says that [troops](../articles/2785204.md) were sent to [Akita Prefecture](../articles/61729.md) after a series of more than 50 [bear attacks](../articles/20632326.md) since April, which resulted in 12 deaths and over 100 injuries. [Governor](../articles/24009688.md) [Kenta Suzuki](../articles/81519323.md) requested aid, saying that local authorities lack manpower to handle the situation. [(AP)](https://apnews.com/article/japan-bear-attack-army-12a2a3233790deeefd9ec379d0121f33)
