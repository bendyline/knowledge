**International relations**&#x20;

- [Democratic Republic of the Congo–Rwanda relations](../articles/74945445.md)&#x20;

[(RNZ)](https://www.rnz.co.nz/international/pacific-news/578139/australia-begins-deporting-non-citizens-to-nauru-under-multimillion-dollar-resettlement-plan)

  - [Japan](../articles/15573.md) reports that [seafood exports](../articles/15578.md) to [China](../articles/5405.md) have resumed for the first time since China imposed a ban in August 2023 over [treated wastewater releases](../articles/67385737.md) from the [Fukushima Daiichi nuclear plant](../articles/11986472.md). However, China maintains restrictions on products from [Fukushima](../articles/211914.md) and surrounding [prefectures](../articles/74517.md). [(AP)](https://apnews.com/article/japan-china-seafood-export-fukushima-nuclear-9acffde0a742704428864490a1433317)
