# News of the day: 2025-12-07

**Armed conflicts and attacks**&#x20;

- [2025 Beninese coup attempt](../articles/81783113.md)&#x20;

  - Elements of the [Benin Armed Forces](../articles/68980.md), led by [Pascal Tigri](../articles/81783113.md), announce a [coup d'état](../articles/25914154.md) against [president](../articles/897527.md) [Patrice Talon](../articles/49884967.md) and the creation of a [military junta](../articles/216815.md), the Military Committee for Refoundation, after seizing [state television](../articles/79721981.md). The coup attempt is ultimately unsuccessful. [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/7/soldiers-appear-on-benin-state-television-announcing-apparent-coup)

  - The [Nigerian Air Force](../articles/538869.md) launches precision [airstrikes](../articles/323167.md) against pro-coup forces in [Cotonou](../articles/56647.md) in support of the [Beninese government](../articles/68976.md), killing several putschists. [(Sahara Reporters)](https://saharareporters.com/2025/12/07/nigerian-air-force-launches-precision-airstrikes-fleeing-benin-republic-coup-plotters) [(Zagazola)](https://zagazola.org/index.php/breaking-news/nigerian-air-force-conducts-multiple-precision-airstrikes-on-fleeing-coup-plotters-in-benin-republic)

  - [ECOWAS](../articles/36848.md) announces the deployment of ground troops to Benin from [Ghana](../articles/12067.md), [Ivory Coast](../articles/57744.md), [Nigeria](../articles/21383.md), and [Sierra Leone](../articles/27308.md). [(Sahara Reporters)](https://saharareporters.com/2025/12/07/ecowas-deploys-immediate-standby-force-benin-after-failed-coup-attempt)

  - [France](../articles/5843419.md) provides [military intelligence](../articles/146678.md), [surveillance aircraft](../articles/46818.md), and logistical assistance to Benin and shares intelligence with Nigeria to help thwart the coup attempt. [(Reuters)](https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/france-provided-logistical-support-benin-thwart-coup-elysee-says-2025-12-09/)
