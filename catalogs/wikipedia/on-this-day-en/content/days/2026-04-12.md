**Politics and elections**&#x20;

- [2026 Peruvian general election](../articles/69679018.md)&#x20;

  - [Peruvians](../articles/8796244.md) vote to elect 130 members of the [Chamber of Deputies](../articles/1571007.md) and 60 members of a newly created [senate](../articles/44417.md), while also determining the top two [presidential](../articles/239109.md) candidates that will advance to a [run-off election](../articles/25932.md). [(AP)](https://apnews.com/article/peru-presidential-election-crime-fujimori-aliaga-980a5ee78d1fed794dc6c9b90f9a1ce4)

  - [Peruvian police](../articles/14896441.md) and prosecutors conduct searches at the [electoral authority](../articles/5446067.md)'s headquarters and a private company as part of an investigation into delays in delivering voting materials that prevent more than 60,000 people from voting. Officials attribute the issue to logistical failures affecting multiple polling stations in [Lima](../articles/85423.md). The electoral authority later announces that some polling stations will reopen. [(AFP via *Free Malaysia Today*)](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/world/2026/04/13/perus-police-investigators-raid-election-hq-amid-vote-delays) [(AFP via NAMPA)](https://www.nampa.org/text/22908458)

  - Exit polls show [Popular Force](../articles/15247524.md)'s [Keiko Fujimori](../articles/4643445.md) leading the presidential election with about 16 percent of the vote, with no candidate securing a majority and a run-off expected between Fujimori and [Popular Renewal](../articles/66197743.md)'s [Rafael López Aliaga](../articles/65483323.md). [(AFP with *Le Monde*)](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/04/13/peru-exit-polls-show-fujimori-ahead-runoff-likely_6752352_4.html)

- [2026 Hungarian parliamentary election](../articles/79337461.md)&#x20;

  - [Hungarians](../articles/17106358.md) vote to elect all 199 members of the [National Assembly](../articles/585416.md). The incumbent [prime minister](../articles/378359.md), [Viktor Orbán](../articles/750582.md), who was seeking a fifth consecutive term in office, [concedes](../articles/2538448.md) defeat and calls [Tisza Party](../articles/76606756.md) leader [Péter Magyar](../articles/76453946.md) on the telephone to congratulate him on his victory. The [pro-European](../articles/631596.md) Tisza Party wins two-thirds of the seats in Hungary's legislature. The turnout is the highest since the [fall of communism](../articles/24259849.md), at 77.8%. [(BBC)](https://www.bbc.com/news/live/c2d8zw2d3rkt) [(*The Guardian*)](https://www.theguardian.com/world/live/2026/apr/12/hungary-election-latest-results-viktor-orban-peter-magyar-fidesz-tisza-russia-europe-live-news-updates#maincontent)
