The **president of Burkina Faso** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Président du Burkina Faso*) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of [Burkina Faso](3470.md) as well as the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burkina_Faso_Armed_Forces). It is the highest office in Burkina Faso and has significant executive power, including appointing the [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_prime_ministers_of_Burkina_Faso) and other government officials. The president also has the power to dissolve parliament and issue decrees.

The office was first established in August 1960 as president of [Upper Volta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Upper_Volta) (French: *Président du Haute-Volta*) after the country gained [independence](62450.md) from [France](5843419.md). [Maurice Yaméogo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Yam%C3%A9ogo) was the first holder of that office. On 4 August 1984, the day before the 24th anniversary of independence, [Thomas Sankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Sankara), who was the president from exactly one year earlier, became the president of Burkina Faso after the country changed its name the same day.

A total of seven people have served as president of Upper Volta/Burkina Faso (not counting four transitional heads of state/presidents and one acting president in rebellion).

The current interim president is [Capt.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_\(armed_forces\)) [Ibrahim Traoré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Traor%C3%A9), who took power during a [coup d'état](71882074.md) on 30 September 2022.[^cite_note-Ndiaga_Mimault-7]

## Term of office

The president is [elected directly](652563.md) to a five-year term. Following amendments in the [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Burkina_Faso) in 2000, the president should serve no more than two terms even though the term limit has not been met by any president yet.[^cite_note-termlimits-8]

## Titles

- **1960–1980**: President of the Republic

- **1980–1982**: President of Military Committee of Recovery for National Progress

- **1982**: Chairman of Provisional Committee of Popular Salvation

- **1982–1983**: Head of State

- **1983–1987**: Chairman of National Revolutionary Council and Head of State

- **1987–1991**: President of [Popular Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Front_\(Burkina_Faso\)) and Head of State

- **1991–2014**: President of the Republic

- **2015**: Chairman of [National Council for Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Council_for_Democracy)

- **2015–present**: President of the Republic

- **2022–present**: President of the [Patriotic Movement for Safeguard and Restoration](69877187.md)
