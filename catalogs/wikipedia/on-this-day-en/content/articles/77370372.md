The **Volgograd Refinery** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Лукойл-Волгограднефтепереработка) is an [oil refinery](195137.md) plant in the Russian city of [Volgograd](32585.md), one of the largest in the country.&#x20;

## History

In February 1952, construction of a new oil refinery plant near [Volgograd](32585.md) began, which was completed by December 1957 and began operations soon after.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] In the following years, the refinery significantly expanded its production capacity and range.[^cite_note-ibp-3][^cite_note-eb-4]

In 2005, the production of [petroleum coke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleum_coke) through calcination began at the refinery, with production at about 100,000 tons per year.[^cite_note-eb-4]

In 2006, the refinery began to produce [gasoline](23639.md) that fit the [European emission standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_emission_standard) of Euro-3. The refinery also began production of [diesel fuel](81761.md).[^cite_note-Facts_2014-5]

In June 2015, the introduction of a new refining unit to facilitate the production of [LNG](832128.md), which increased the refinery's processing capacity to 14.5 million tons per year.[^cite_note-6]

In May 2016, the refinery began producing fuel that fulfill the [Euro-5 standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_emission_standard#Euro_5).[^cite_note-7]



[NASA](18426568.md)'s [FIRMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_Information_for_Resource_Management_System) detected extensive fire on 13 August 2025 21\:46\:00 ([UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC)) at the Volgograd Refinery

In February 2024, during the [Russo-Ukrainian War](70149799.md), the refinery was hit by a drone from the [Ukrainian military](42799055.md).[^cite_note-8] On 31 January 2025, Ukrainian drones attacked the refinery again, causing a fire at the facility. Russian sources said the fire was quickly extinguished.[^cite_note-9] On 14 August 2025 the Russian defense ministry claimed to have downed 9 drones overnight in Volgograd Oblast. Volgograd's governor claimed that falling drone debris caused a fire at the Volgograd Refinery.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] Extensive fire at the refinery was confirmed by [NASA](18426568.md)'s [FIRMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_Information_for_Resource_Management_System), see image. On 15 October 2025, the refinery was struck by Ukrainian drones, with locals reporting explosions and fires.[^cite_note-12]
