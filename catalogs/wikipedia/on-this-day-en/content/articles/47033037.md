This list consists of people who have reached the summit of [Mount Everest](42179.md) more than once. By 2013, 6,871 summits had been recorded by 4,042 people.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] By the end of 2016 there were 7,646 summits by 4,469 people.[^cite_note-3] In 2018 about 800 people summited, breaking the record for most in one year compared to 2013, in which 667 summited Mount Everest.[^cite_note-\:0x-4][^cite_note-5]

As of December 2024, there have been approximately 12,884 summit ascents by 7269 people.[^cite_note-6]

*Information in this list may be outdated; it can take months or years for summit counts to be reliably confirmed. Confirmations can also be made difficult by many sherpas having similar names.*
