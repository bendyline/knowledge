**Vijayawada** ([/ˈvɪdʒəjəˈwɑːdə/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/0/08/Vijaywada.ogg/Vijaywada.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/0/08/Vijaywada.ogg/Vijaywada.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> [*Vijay-uh-waw-duh*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key)), also called as **Bezawada**, is the second largest city and a major commercial hub in the Indian [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_India) of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md).[^cite_note-8] The city forms an integral part of the [Andhra Pradesh Capital Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Pradesh_Capital_Region) and is situated on the banks of the [Krishna River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishna_River), flanked by the [Eastern Ghats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Ghats) and the Indrakeeladri Hills.[^cite_note-9]

Vijayawada is an important agricultural trade hub in the state of Andhra Pradesh, particularly for mango commerce. The city's surrounding regions, including [Nunna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nunna) and [Nuzvid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuzvid), are well known for extensive mango cultivation and wholesale trading. The mango-growing belt around Vijayawada produces popular varieties such as [Banganapalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banganapalle_\(mango\)), [Totapuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totapuri), Cheruku Rasalu, and Suvarnarekha, which are distributed to several Indian states and export markets.Vijayawada is known for mango trade due to the Nunna mango market.[^cite_note-10]

It is renowned for its iconic [Kanaka Durga Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanaka_Durga_Temple), an important Hindu shrine that attracts millions of devotees each year.[^cite_note-11] Geographically positioned near the centre of the state, Vijayawada is popularly described as the commercial, political, cultural, and educational capital of Andhra Pradesh.[^cite_note-12] It also serves as the administrative headquarters of the newly formed [NTR district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NTR_district).[^cite_note-13] The [Prakasam Barrage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prakasam_Barrage) across the Krishna River is a pivotal infrastructure asset that connects NTR and [Guntur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guntur) districts.[^cite_note-14]

Vijayawada is recognised as one of India's fastest growing urban areas. The 2023 Oxford Economics report ranked it among the top 10 fastest growing cities in the world.[^cite_note-15]

Vijayawada is considered to be a sacred place due to it being home to one of the most visited and famous temples in Andhra Pradesh and India, the [Kanaka Durga Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanaka_Durga_Temple) of the [Hindu Goddess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devi) [Durga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durga) residing on the Indrakeeladri hill.[^cite_note-16] It also serves as the ritual host of [Pushkaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushkaram) (a river worshipping ritual in India) of the [River Krishna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishna_River).[^cite_note-17] There is a legend which says that [Arjuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arjuna), one of the heroes of the Indian epic *[Mahabharata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahabharata)*, prayed on top of the Indrakeeladri Hill in the city and won the blessings of the [Lord Shiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiva) to get the [Pashupatastra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashupatastra) to win the [Kurukshetra War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurukshetra_War).[^cite_note-vijayawadapolice.ap.gov.in-18] It was called Vijayavatika (meaning Land of Victory in [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language)) when [Goddess Durga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durga) killed the demon [Mahishasura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahishasura) and rested on the Indrakeeladri Hill by the River Krishna establishing the [victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victory) over [evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahishasura) hence the place got its name Vijayavatika, "Vijaya" meaning victory, and "Vatika" meaning place or land in [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language).

The city is the third most densely populated urban built-up area in the world.[^cite_note-density-19] and is classified as a *Y-grade* city by the [Sixth Central Pay Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixth_Central_Pay_Commission).[^cite_note-Personnel-20] The city is the [second most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Andhra_Pradesh_by_population) in the state with a population of more than one million.[^cite_note-21] It was recognised as a "Global City of the Future" by *[McKinsey Quarterly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McKinsey_Quarterly)*, which expected an increase to GDP of \$17 billion by 2025.[^cite_note-mckinseyquarterly-22][^cite_note-CDIA_city_economy-23] In October 2018, it was awarded with [ISO 37120](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_37120) platinum level certification and has been added to the "Global Cities Registry".[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

Due to the presence of several well-known educational institutions, the city has emerged as a major educational hub in recent times, with many of the nation's students studying in the city. It is predicted to be the world's, and India's, tenth fastest growing city economy through 2035 by an Oxford Economics report.[^cite_note-nickname-26] Due to its high ratings in entertainment, construction, food, education, health care, and transport,[^cite_note-27] it is ranked as India's ninth most liveable city as per Ease of Living Index 2018, and the [Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Housing_and_Urban_Affairs)[^cite_note-28] and the second most liveable city in the state of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md).[^cite_note-29]

The [Vijayawada Junction railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayawada_Junction_railway_station) is one of the busiest in the country.[^cite_note-30] It is the tenth [busiest railway junction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_railway_junction_stations_in_India) in the country.

## Toponymy

There are many legends behind the origin of the name Vijayawada. It is said that [Goddess Durga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durga) killed [Mahishasura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahishasura) and relaxed at this place. As she was victorious, the place came to be known as Vijayawada (*vijaya* translates to victory[^cite_note-31] and *wada* as place, literally meaning *The Place of Victory*).[^cite_note-32] The hill was called as *Indrekeeladri* since it was frequently visited by [Indra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indra) and his affiliates.[^cite_note-33] The epic [Mahabharata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahabharata) refers to the Indrakiladri hills as the place where [Arjuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arjuna) secured [Pashupatastra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashupatastra) from [Lord Shiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiva). One of the names of [Arjuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arjuna) is "Vijaya" (invincible). Thus city thereafter came to be known as *Vijayavatika* ('Vatika' translates to place in Telugu) and later as Vijayawada.[^cite_note-aptourism-34]

A tale behind its acquiring the name Bezawada is that Goddess Krishnaveni ([River Krishna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishna_River)) requested Arjuna to make a passage for her to merge into the [Bay of Bengal](47892.md). Hence, Arjuna made a *bejjam* (hole) through the mountains and the place came to be known as *Bejjamwada* which later changed to *Bezawada*.[^cite_note-bezwada-35][^cite_note-36] Other names of Vijayawada were being Vijayavata,[^cite_note-bezwada-35] Beejapuram, Kanakaprabha, Kanakapuram, Kanakawada, Jayapuri, Vijayapuri, Phalguna Kshetram and Jananathpura in the twelfth century CE.[^cite_note-Sajnani-37]

Bezawada (as Vijayawada was known then) was founded around 626 A.D. by [Paricchedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pericchedi) Kings. Vijayawada history reveals that Bezawada (Vijayawada) was ruled by King Madhava Varma (one of the kings of [Vishnukundina dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishnukundina_dynasty)).[^cite_note-38] [Chinese Buddhist scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Buddhism) [Xuanzang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xuanzang) stayed a few years in Bezawada (Vijayawada) in around 640 A.D. to copy and study the [Abhidhamma Pitaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abhidhamma_Pi%E1%B9%ADaka), the last of the three pitakas (Pali for baskets) constituting the Pali canon, the scriptures of [Theravada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theravada) [Buddhism](3267529.md).[^cite_note-39]

[Mogalrajapuram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mogalrajapuram) hills have five rock-cut temples, built during the fourth–ninth centuries. Some of the caves can be attributed to [Vishnukundina dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishnukundina_dynasty). [Akkana Madanna Caves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akkana_Madanna_Caves), at the foot of Indrakeeladri Hill, is a monument of national importance.[^cite_note-ASI-40]

At the foot of Indrakeeladri hills is the temple of Malleswara. The temple has inscriptions dating back to ninth century AD to 16th century AD by various kings. There are ten pillars and a mutilated slab (recognised as monuments by [Archaeological Survey of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaeological_Survey_of_India)) with inscriptions in the Telugu language. Of them, the inscriptions issued by Yudhamalla I and II of [Eastern Chalukyas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Chalukyas) are important.[^cite_note-ASI-40]

In the early 16th century, during the reign of [Qutb Shahi dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qutb_Shahi_dynasty) (also known as Golconda Sultanate), [diamond mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diamond_mines) were found near Vijayawada on the banks of [Krishna River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishna_River).[^cite_note-41]

## Geography

Vijayawada lies on the banks of [Krishna river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishna_River),[^cite_note-42] covered by hills and canals.[^cite_note-density-19][^cite_note-43] and at an altitude of 11 m (36 ft) above sea level. Three [canals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canal) originating from the north side of the [Prakasam Barrage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prakasam_Barrage) reservoir — Eluru, Bandar, and [Ryves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryves_Canal) — flow through the city.[^cite_note-44]

### Climate

Vijayawada has a [tropical wet and dry climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_wet_and_dry_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Aw*).[^cite_note-climate-45] The annual mean temperatures range between 23.4–34 °C (74–93 °F); with maximum temperatures often crossing 40 °C (104 °F) in the month of May and the minimum in December and January.[^cite_note-IMD-46] The highest maximum temperature ever recorded was 48.8 °C (119.8 °F) in May 2002. May is the hottest and January is the coldest month of the year.[^cite_note-IMD-46][^cite_note-47] It receives rainfall from the South-west and North-east [monsoons](57630.md)[^cite_note-climate-45] and the average annual rainfall recorded is 977.9 mm (38.50 in).[^cite_note-IMD-46]

## Demographics

The city is the second most populous in the state and the third most densely populated urban built-up areas in the world, with approximately 31,200 people per square km.[^cite_note-52] As of 2011 [Census of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Census_of_India), it had a population of 1,021,806, of which males are 524,918 and females are 523,322—for a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_sex_ratio) of 997 females per 1000 males—higher than the national average of 940 per 1000.[^cite_note-population-53][^cite_note-54] 92,848 children were in the age group of 0–6 years, of which 47,582 were boys and 45,266 were girls: a ratio of 951 per 1000. The average literacy rate stood at 82.59% (male 86.25%; female 78.94%) with 789,038 literates, significantly higher than the national average of 73.00%.[^cite_note-population-53][^cite_note-55]

### Civic administration

[Vijayawada Municipal Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayawada_Municipal_Corporation) is the civic governing body of the city and was the first [ISO 9001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_9001) certified [urban local body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_governance_in_India) in the country.[^cite_note-60]

It was constituted on 1 April 1888 and was upgraded to selection grade municipality in 1960, and, to the corporation in 1981. The jurisdictional area of the corporation is spread over an area of 230 km<sup>2</sup> (89 sq mi) with 64 wards.[^cite_note-metro-61][^cite_note-ulb-62] The present Municipal Commissioner of the city is Sri Dhyanachandra H M, [IAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Administrative_Service)[^cite_note-63] and the present [Mayor](101146.md) is Rayana Bhagya Lakshmi.[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65] Vijayawada is the headquarters of [Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amaravati_Metropolitan_Region_Development_Authority).[^cite_note-66]

### Pollution control

The report on solid waste generation in 46 metro cities for the year 2015–16 shows Vijayawada produces 550 [tonnes](31185.md) of solid waste per day.[^cite_note-solid_waste-77] Vijayawada is one of the cities to be covered under the Solar/Green Cities scheme launched by the [Ministry of New and Renewable Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_New_and_Renewable_Energy) and is one of the 15 cities in the country listed in Pilot Solar Cities.[^cite_note-78]

In 2007, the [Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Pradesh_State_Road_Transport_Corporation) (APSRTC) that operates the city's public transport system introduced less-polluting [CNG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compressed_natural_gas) fuelled public transport busses in a drive to reduce the operational cost and protect the environment.[^cite_note-79] In 2019, with the cost of CNG and fleet maintenance on the rise, the APSRTC had begun replacing the CNG run busses with BS4 and BS6 standard diesel-fuelled buses.[^cite_note-80] However, APSRTC continues to be committed to alternative fuels for its fleet. It has since added more [electric](279350.md),[^cite_note-81] and [biodiesel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biodiesel) fuelled busses.[^cite_note-82]

Likewise, the city has a large number of CNG fuelled [auto-rickshaws](593765.md) that support public transportation needs. However, auto-rickshaw operators have frequently suffered disruption in CNG supply and usually endure long queues to refuel.[^cite_note-83][^cite_note-84] In February 2020, [Piaggio Vehicles Private Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piaggio) launched the sale of electric auto-rickshaws in the city.[^cite_note-85]

### Mango trade and horticulture

The mango market located at Nunna, part of the Vijayawada metropolitan area, is widely recognized as one of the largest mango markets in Asia. During the peak summer season, thousands of tonnes of mangoes are traded through this market, supporting farmers, traders, transporters, and processing industries. Mango cultivation and trade contribute significantly to the regional economy and reinforce Vijayawada’s role as a major horticultural distribution center in Andhra Pradesh.[^cite_note-98]

Mangoes also hold cultural importance in the Vijayawada region, being extensively used in traditional Telugu cuisine, especially in seasonal preparations such as mango pickle (Avakaya) and raw mango dishes during summer. This agricultural and cultural significance has led to Vijayawada being popularly associated with mango trade in Andhra Pradesh[^cite_note-99]

### Arts, crafts and artefacts

The Vijayawada Art Society promotes Telugu arts.[^cite_note-111] The city hosted Poetic Prism 2015, a multilingual poet's meet on 19 September 2015.[^cite_note-prism-103] All these activities are organised in collaboration with the *Cultural Centre of Vijayawada*.

[Kondapalli Toys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kondapalli_Toys) — which were granted [geographical indication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographical_indication) in 2007[^cite_note-112] — are handmade by the artisans of [Kondapalli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kondapalli), a suburb of Vijayawada.[^cite_note-113] Kondapalli toys, also known as Kondapalli Bommalu, are traditional wooden toys crafted by skilled artisans in Kondapalli, Andhra Pradesh, India. [Victoria Jubilee Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bapu_Museum,_Vijayawada) is an [archaeological museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaeology_museum) in the city, which houses sculptures, paintings, and artefacts of [Buddhist](3267529.md) and Hindu [relics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relic), dating back to the second and third centuries.

**Golden Mile Project**

Main article: [Golden Mile Project (Vijayawada)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Mile_Project_\(Vijayawada\))

MG Road in Vijayawada is home to the Golden Mile Project, India’s first smart street initiative. Covering a 2.9 km stretch from the Police Control Room to Benz Circle, it was launched as a Proof of Concept for the Smart City initiative by the Government of Andhra Pradesh.[^cite_note-147] Supported by [Cisco Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cisco), the project provides facilities such as free Wi-Fi, intelligent street lighting, smart parking, and digital kiosks. The total cost of ₹7.83 crore was shared between Cisco, the Andhra Pradesh Urban Finance & Infrastructure Development Corporation, and the [Vijayawada Municipal Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayawada_Municipal_Corporation). The project enables real-time monitoring through the City Digital Platform at the municipal office.[^cite_note-148] It includes 101 surveillance cameras, with some capable of calculating traffic density and providing live reports. Additionally, 35 Wi-Fi access points and 240 smart solar-powered lights were installed for public use. Parking sensors help reduce congestion, and motion sensors manage traffic and detect violations. The project also introduced Remote Expert Government Services, allowing citizens to communicate with officials via video calls.[^cite_note-149]

The international flights to Singapore stopped after 27 June 2019 because the State Government stopped [viability gap funding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public%E2%80%93private_partnership) in June 2019.[^cite_note-airport-167] As of June 2019, it registered a domestic passenger movement growth rate of 70.0% with a total of 1,192,000 passengers in 2019.[^cite_note-169] Aircraft movement recorded a growth of 65.0%.[^cite_note-170]

## Education



NTR University of Health Sciences



Administrative office of V.R. Siddhartha Engineering College

The Primary and Secondary School Education is imparted by the Government, Aided and Private Schools, under the State School Education Department.[^cite_note-171][^cite_note-172] As per the school information report for the academic year 2015–16, the city has 133,837 students enrolled in 529 Schools.[^cite_note-173] The state and [CBSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Board_of_Secondary_Education) syllabus are followed by schools for the [Secondary School Certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_School_Certificate).[^cite_note-174][^cite_note-175] The languages of instruction are English, Urdu and Telugu.[^cite_note-176]

For [10+2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10+2) education, there are two government junior colleges, namely S.R.R. & C.V.R. Government College and Government Junior College (at Vinchipeta); a railway junior college; three co-operative, 12 private aided, including the oldest, S.R.R & C.V.R College (established in 1937), [Andhra Loyola College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Loyola_College) (established in December 1953), Maria Stella College for Girls (established in 1962), [KBN College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakaraparti_Bhavanarayana_College) (established in 1965), [Velagapudi Ramakrishna Siddhartha Engineering College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velagapudi_Ramakrishna_Siddhartha_Engineering_College) (established in 1977), the oldest engineering college in Andhra Pradesh, and many private unaided colleges.[^cite_note-colleges-177] [School of Planning and Architecture, Vijayawada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Planning_and_Architecture,_Vijayawada) (SPAV) was established in 2008, a higher education institute in Vijayawada. It is one of the three Schools of Planning and Architecture (SPAs) established by the [Ministry of Human Resource Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Human_Resource_Development) in 2008 as an autonomous institute and a fully central funded institution.

The Sarvotthama Grandhalaya is a city library established on 30 March 1987. It serves an average daily readership of 200, equipped with 22,000 books related to many fields.[^cite_note-178] VMC & VBFS Research and Reference Library is a reader's room/library maintained by Vijayawada Book Festival Society.[^cite_note-179]

## Sports



Vijayawada PWD ground

[Indira Gandhi Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indira_Gandhi_Stadium,_Vijayawada) in the city is the headquarters of the Sports Authority of Andhra Pradesh.[^cite_note-186] It hosted its only men's [One Day International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Day_International) (ODI) on 24 November 2002, played between [India](407754.md) and [West Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Indies_cricket_team).[^cite_note-187]

The only women's ODI was hosted on 12 December 1997, played as a group match of [1997 Women's Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_Women's_Cricket_World_Cup) between [England women's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_women's_cricket_team) and [Pakistan women's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_women's_national_cricket_team) teams.[^cite_note-188] Makineni Basavapunnaiah Stadium at Ajit Singh Nagar is another stadium that caters the sporting needs of the northern part of the city.[^cite_note-189] Indoor stadiums include Dandamudi Rajagopala Rao Indoor Stadium (DRRIS) at M. G.Road and Chennupati Ramakotaiah Indoor Stadium (CRIS) at Patamatalanka. The DRRIS was named after the former [weightlifter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_weightlifting), [Dandamudi Rajagopal Rao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dandamudi_Rajagopal), who participated in the [1951 Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951_Asian_Games) and the [1956 Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-indoor-190] The DRRIS hosted several national and international sporting events, such as the 79th Senior Badminton Champions.[^cite_note-191]

[Andhra Cricket Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Cricket_Association) (ACA) International Cricket Stadium is being built on a 30-acre (12 ha) site at [Navuluru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navuduru) village, [Mangalagiri Mandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangalagiri_mandal) of [Guntur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guntur_district), 15 km (9.3 mi) from the city. It serves as the Andhra Cricket Association stadium.[^cite_note-192] Vijaya Madhavi Tennis Academy is in Loyola College. Loyola College Grounds hosted many national-level events which includes a Ranji match.[^cite_note-193]

The Railway Sports Stadium, near the railway station, hosted several national level railway competitions such as bodybuilding competitions, Under-19 [Cricket](25675557.md) championships and a few non-railway events. This stadium has a gallery facility of over 10,000 people. Makineni Basava Punnaiah stadium is another open stadium in the city. Dandamudi Rajagopal Rao Indoor Stadium is one of the noted [Badminton](3956.md) stadiums in Andhra Pradesh. It has hosted several national level Badminton and [Table Tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Table_tennis) championships.[^cite_note-194][^cite_note-195]

## Awards

The Swachh Survekshan 2021 has identified Vijayawada as the third cleanest city in the nation. In the category of cities with more than 10 lakh inhabitants, the city rose from sixth place in 2020 to third place this year. A five-star designation for "Garbage Free City" has also been given to Vijayawada.[^cite_note-196] Such a good ranking can be accredited to policy measures such as the introduction of waste disposal vehicles in the city by [N. Chandrababu Naidu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N._Chandrababu_Naidu).[^cite_note-197]
