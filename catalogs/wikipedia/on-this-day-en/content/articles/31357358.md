The **Bolivian Yungas** is a [tropical and subtropical moist broadleaf forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_and_subtropical_moist_broadleaf_forest) [ecoregion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecoregion) in the [Yungas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yungas) of central [Bolivia](3462.md).[^cite_note-wwf-2]

## Setting

The ecoregion occurs in elevations ranging from 400 to 3,500 metres (1,300 to 11,500 ft) on the eastern slopes of the [Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andes) in Bolivia, extending into a small portion of southeastern Peru. It forms a transition zone between the [Southwest Amazon moist forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Amazon_moist_forests) to the northeast and the [Central Andean puna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Andean_puna) and [wet puna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Andean_wet_puna) to the southwest.[^cite_note-wwf-2]

## Climate

The climate in this ecoregion varies from [tropical rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) to [tropical monsoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate). Fog and rain deposited by northern trade winds contribute to the high humidity and precipitation of the Yungas.[^cite_note-wwf-2]

## Flora

[Epiphytes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epiphyte) are abundant and include [bromeliads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromeliad), [orchids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orchid), and tree-ferns (*[Cyathea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyathea)*). *[Chusquea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chusquea)* bamboo is an indicator species of the ecoregion.[^cite_note-wwf-2]

## Fauna

Mammals found in this ecoregion include the [spectacled bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectacled_bear) (*Tremarctos ornatus*), [lowland tapir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowland_tapir) (*Tapirus terrestris*) [dwarf brocket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwarf_brocket) deer (*Mazama chunyi*),[^cite_note-wwf-2] [jaguar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaguar) (*Panthera onca*), [jaguarundi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaguarundi) (*Herpailurus yagouaroundi*), [pacarana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacarana) (*Dinomys branickii*), [Geoffroy's cat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffroy's_cat) (*Leopardus geoffroyi*), and [tilcayo](84257539.md) cat (*Leopardus tilcayo*)[^cite_note-3].

Bird species include the [diademed tapaculo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diademed_tapaculo) (*Scytalopus schulenbergi*), [green-capped tanager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green-capped_tanager) (*Stilpnia meyerdeschauenseei*), [Andean cock-of-the-rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andean_cock-of-the-rock) (*Rupicola peruvianus*), and [southern helmeted curassow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_helmeted_curassow) (*Pauxi unicornis*).[^cite_note-wwf-2]

## Human use

The Bolivian Yungas is the center of the [Afro-Bolivian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Bolivian) community.

The [Yungas Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yungas_Road), known for being dangerous, connects [La Paz](42821.md) to the Bolivian Yungas.

## Protected areas

Steep terrain, high precipitation, and difficult access have kept much of this ecoregion in a natural state. 49.37% of the ecoregion is in protected areas.[^cite_note-dopa-1] They include:

- [Amboró National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambor%C3%B3_National_Park)

- [Bahuaja-Sonene National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahuaja-Sonene_National_Park)

- [Carrasco National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrasco_National_Park)

- [Cotapata National Park and Integrated Management Natural Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotapata_National_Park_and_Integrated_Management_Natural_Area)

- [Espejillos Natural Monument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espejillos_Natural_Monument?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Incacasani Altamachi National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incacasani_Altamachi_National_Park?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Isiboro Sécure National Park and Indigenous Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isiboro_S%C3%A9cure_National_Park_and_Indigenous_Territory)

- [Madidi National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madidi_National_Park)

- [Pilón Lajas Biosphere Reserve and Communal Lands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pil%C3%B3n_Lajas_Biosphere_Reserve_and_Communal_Lands)

- [Tunari National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunari_National_Park)
