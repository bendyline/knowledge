The **first lady of the Republic of Korea** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 대한민국 대통령 배우자), commonly known as the **first lady of South Korea**, is the title held by the hostess of the [presidential residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Residence_of_South_Korea), usually the wife of the [president of South Korea](98630.md).

The position of first lady is currently held by [Kim Hea Kyung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Hea_Kyung), as president [Lee Jae-myung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Jae-myung)'s wife.

## History

During the administration of President [Park Chung Hee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Chung_Hee), the first lady [Yuk Young-soo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuk_Young-soo) was killed. Afterwards, Park's daughter, [Park Geun-hye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Geun-hye), assumed the duties of first lady after her mother.[^cite_note-1]

Park Geun-hye later became president herself, making her the first and only female president of South Korea, However, as she was never married while in office, there has yet to be a first gentleman of Korea.[^cite_note-2]
