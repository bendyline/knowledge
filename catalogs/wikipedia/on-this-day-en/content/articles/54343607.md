# Terrorist activity in Belgium

| --- | --- |

This article covers attacks and activity of [terrorism](30636.md) in [Belgium](3343.md).

## Islamic terrorism

In the 1990s Belgium was a transit country for Islamist terrorist groups like the [Armed Islamic Group of Algeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Islamic_Group_of_Algeria) (GIA) and the [Moroccan Islamic Combatant Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moroccan_Islamic_Combatant_Group) (GICM).[^cite_note-\:8-1]

Belgium has a population of 11 million including large numbers of immigrants from Muslim countries. 100,000 [Moroccan citizens live in Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moroccans_in_Belgium), often descended from Moroccans recruited to work in the mining industry in the 1960s; a small fraction of the children and grandchildren of the immigrant generation have been attracted to Militant Islamism and Jihad. A tiny fraction of this large Muslim population has participated in terrorist attacks.[^cite_note-SchreuerJihadistBase2-2] In a report by the [Combating Terrorism Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combating_Terrorism_Center), of the 135 individuals surveyed in connection with terrorism, there were 12 different nationalities. Of those 65% had Belgian citizenship and 33% were either [Moroccan](19291.md) citizens or had ancestral roots there.[^cite_note-ctc_beyond_caliph_belgium2-3]

In 2016, Belgian researcher estimated that about 562 individuals had travelled to become [foreign fighters in the Syrian and Iraqi Civil Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_fighters_in_the_Syrian_and_Iraqi_Civil_Wars), the majority of whom joined the Islamic State with others joining the al-Qaida-affiliated group [Al-Nusra Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Nusra_Front).[^cite_note-4] The majority of those who went to the Syria in the 2012-2016 time span were of Moroccan descent according to U.S. and Belgian authorities.[^cite_note-5]

Belgium has been the base of operations for a number of terrorist attacks in the 2010s, including the [November 2015 Paris attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_2015_Paris_attacks).[^cite_note-SchreuerJihadistBase2-2] It has also been the place where some Islamist militants developed militant views before going to the Middle East to fight with [ISIS](9087364.md).[^cite_note-SchreuerJihadistBase2-2]

In June 2016, with 451 fighters having travelled to join the [Syrian Civil War](30741795.md), Belgium had the highest number of foreign fighters per capita.[^cite_note-\:8-1]

The [November 2015 Paris attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_2015_Paris_attacks) in France were coordinated and planned from Belgium. The overall leader of that terrorist cell was believed to be [Mohamed Belkaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Belkaid), an Islamic State operative from Algeria who previously had lived in Sweden. Belkaid was killed in a shootout in the Foret district of Brussels, during which Belkaid was firing on police to allow [Salah Abdeslam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salah_Abdeslam) to escape. Salah Abdeslam was arrested a few days later and the surviving members of the cell, including brothers Najim Laachraoui and Khalid and Ibrahim Bakraoui (previously armed robbers) launched the [2016 Brussels bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Brussels_bombings) targeting Brussels airport and metro killing 32.[^cite_note-\:8-1]

Terrorism experts regard [Islamic State](9087364.md) activities in Europe's Francophone area as a single, French-Belgian junction of ISIS activity and attacks.[^cite_note-BrisardFrenchBelgianNexus-6]

## List of terrorist incidents

### Attacks related to Middle East politics

1985

- 1 August 1985: [Silco incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silco_incident). Kidnapping of Belgian-French family by the government of [Libya](17633.md); they were held for almost five years.

  [^cite_note-NassarSaeed-7]

### Attacks related to Northern Ireland

1979

[^cite_note-8]

- 22 March 1979: A Belgian bank employee was shot by the [Provisional IRA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_IRA), who thought it was [Sir John Killick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Killick).

- 25 June 1979: An IRA bomb in Brussels targeted but failed to hit a British general.

- 6 July 1979: An IRA bomb detonates in a British consulate building in Antwerp.

- 29 August 1979: [1979 Brussels bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_Brussels_bombing). The IRA bombs the central square of Brussels whilst targeting British troops, injuring 15.

- 3 November 1979: The British consulate in Antwerp is bombed by the [Irish National Liberation Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_National_Liberation_Army) (INLA).

  [^cite_note-9]

  [^cite_note-10]

- 3 December 1980 - There was an assassination attempt on the then British European Commissioner Christopher Tugendhat who was living in Belgium at the time, when a passing car fired at him but missed just after he left his Brussels home. The PIRA later claimed responsibility for the attack.

  [^cite_note-11]

- 12 August 1988 - A British Army Sergeant-Major Richard Heakin was shot dead by the Provisional IRA in [Ostend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostend), Belgium.

  [^cite_note-12]

- 10 December 1989 - Two members from the [Irish Republican & Socialist paramilitary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_republicanism) the [Irish People's Liberation Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_People's_Liberation_Organization) (IPLO) shot and injured a gendarme in the Belgian port of Antwerp and escaped on foot. They had been intending to smuggle a small consignment of arms aboard a freighter bound for Ireland. Afterwards, Dutch police carried out raids on four homes and arrested an Irish citizen in Amsterdam.

  [^cite_note-13]

### Antisemitic attacks

1980

- 28 July 1980: [1980 Antwerp summer camp attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980_Antwerp_summer_camp_attack). A [Syria-born Palestinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinians_in_Syria), [Said Al Nasr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Said_Al_Nasr), used [grenades](20353360.md) to attack a group of 40 Jewish children waiting with their families for a bus to take them to summer camp.

  [^cite_note-GlobeYouthDies-14]

  &#x20;The explosion killed one boy, identified as 15-year-old Parisian David Kuhan, and wounded 20, aged 13 to 27, eight of whom had to be hospitalized.

  [^cite_note-GlobeYouthDies-14]

1981

- 20 October 1981: [1981 Antwerp synagogue bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981_Antwerp_synagogue_bombing). The [Black September Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_September_Organization) carried out a bombing attack at the Antwerp synagogue. Three people were killed in the attack, while 103 others were injured.

  [^cite_note-UPI-15]

### Far-left attacks

See also: [Communist Combatant Cells § Attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Combatant_Cells#Attacks)

1985

- 20 April 1985: A bomb exploded in the secretariat of the [North Atlantic Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Atlantic_Assembly), which the [Revolutionary Front for Proletarian Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_Front_for_Proletarian_Action) (FRAP) claimed responsibility for via Belgian radio.

  [^cite_note-Second_Blast-16]

- 21 April 1985: A bomb exploded in the [AEG Telefunken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEG_Telefunken) building in [Brussels](3708.md), blowing out several windows in the AEG Telefunken building and its neighboring structure. The attack was attributed to FRAP after the organisation's acronym was found spray painted on the site.

  [^cite_note-Second_Blast-16]

- 1 May 1985: A [car bomb](155282.md) exploded in front of the [Federation of Belgian Enterprises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Belgian_Enterprises) building in [Brussels](3708.md). Two firemen were killed when trying to extinguish a fire that the [Communist Combatant Cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Combatant_Cells) (CCC) deliberately set on the car, with 13 other people injured.

  [^cite_note-17]

### Islamist attacks

2014

- 24 May 2014: [Jewish Museum of Belgium shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_Museum_of_Belgium_shooting). [ISIS](9087364.md) directed attack on the [Jewish Museum of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_Museum_of_Belgium) in Brussels by a French national in contact with ISIS.

  [^cite_note-18]

2016

- 22 March 2016: [2016 Brussels bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Brussels_bombings). Two [ISIS](9087364.md) [suicide bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide_bomb) occurred at [Brussels Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels_Airport) in [Zaventem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaventem), and one at [Maalbeek metro station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maalbeek/Maelbeek_metro_station) in central [Brussels](3708.md).

  [^cite_note-auto-19]

  &#x20;Thirty-two civilians and three [perpetrators](18835454.md) were killed, and more than 300 people were injured. Another bomb was found during a search of the airport. [Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant](9087364.md) (ISIL) claimed responsibility for the attacks.

  [^cite_note-20]

- 6 August 2016: [2016 stabbing of Charleroi police officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_stabbing_of_Charleroi_police_officers). [ISIS](9087364.md) claimed responsibility for an attack on Belgian police officers by an Algerian national who had been scheduled for deportation.

  [^cite_note-PolicièresAttaquéesBelgique-21]

- 5 October 2016: [2016 stabbing of Brussels police officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_stabbing_of_Brussels_police_officers) [ISIS](9087364.md)-inspired lone wolf stabbing of three police officers in Brussels.

  [^cite_note-SamuelStabbing-22]

  [^cite_note-CNNTerrorism-23]

  [^cite_note-TerrorismCharges-24]

2017

- 20 June 2017: an [explosion occurred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2017_Brussels_attack) in the [Brussels Central train station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels_Central_Station), a failed bombing attempt by [Moroccan](19291.md) lone actor Oussama Zariouh.

  [^cite_note-25]

- 25 August 2017: Two soldiers [were lightly wounded after a machete-wielding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_2017_Brussels_attack) man attacked them in [Brussels](3708.md) close to the [Grand-Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand-Place). The perpetrator was shot dead at the scene, and the surrounding area was promptly evacuated.

  [^cite_note-26]

2018

- 29 May 2018: [2018 Liège attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Li%C3%A8ge_attack). A prisoner left prison, stabbed two female police officers, took their guns, shot and killed them and a civilian in Liège, Belgium. [ISIS](9087364.md) later claimed responsibility for the attack done by a soldier of the caliphate.

  [^cite_note-27]

2022

- 10 November 2022: [2022 Brussels stabbing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Brussels_stabbing). A police officer was killed together while another officer were injured in a stabbing attack in Brussels. The perpetrator was shot and injured and later brought to the hospital.

  [^cite_note-28]

2023

- 16 October 2023: A [shooting occurred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Brussels_terrorist_attack) in central Brussels before a match between [Belgium](679716.md) and [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_men's_national_football_team) at the [King Baudouin Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Baudouin_Stadium). Two Swedish nationals were shot and killed and one severely wounded. According to several sources, it has been reported that the shooter was working on the behalf of [ISIS](9087364.md). The suspected gunman was killed by the police after an all-night manhunt.

  [^cite_note-29]
