**Covo** ([Bergamasque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergamasque): *Cóf*) is a *[comune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comune)* (municipality) in the [Province of Bergamo](987207.md) in the [Italian](14532.md) region of [Lombardy](43807.md), located about 45 kilometres (28 mi) east of [Milan](36511.md) and about 25 kilometres (16 mi) southeast of [Bergamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergamo). As of 31 December 2021, it had a population of 4,218 and an area of 12.7 square kilometres (4.9 mi<sup>2</sup>).[^cite_note-istat-3]

Covo borders the following municipalities : [Antegnate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antegnate), [Barbata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbata), [Calcio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcio,_Lombardy), [Cortenuova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cortenuova), [Fara Olivana con Sola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fara_Olivana_con_Sola), [Isso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isso,_Italy), [Romano di Lombardia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romano_di_Lombardia).
