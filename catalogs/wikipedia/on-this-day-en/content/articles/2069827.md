**Santo Tomas**, officially the **Municipality of Santo Tomas** ([Ibanag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibanag_language): *Ili na Santo Tomás*; [Ilocano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_language): *Ili ti Santo Tomás*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Santo Tomás*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Isabela](271106.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 25,997 people.[^cite_note-7]

## History

The municipality was established in 1949 from several barrios of [Cabagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabagan).[^cite_note-8]

In 1952, Santo Tomas lost two barrios when the barrios of Abut and Minagbag were transferred to the newly created town of [Mallig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mallig,_Isabela).[^cite_note-9] In 1961, those barrios were transferred to the newly created municipality of [Quezon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon,_Isabela).[^cite_note-10]

## Geography

Santo Tomas is a landlocked municipality situated in the northern portion of the province of Isabela. It is bounded to the west by [Quezon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon,_Isabela), to the south by [Delfin Albano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delfin_Albano,_Isabela), to the southeast by [Tumauini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumauini,_Isabela), to the north and northeast by [Cabagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabagan,_Isabela) and the [Cagayan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cagayan_River).

Santo Tomas is situated 45.06 kilometres (28.00 mi) from the provincial capital [Ilagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilagan), and 473.98 kilometres (294.52 mi) from the country's capital city of [Manila](184334.md).

Farming is its primary source of income. The most widespread plantations were [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice), corn and tobacco farms. Locals also raise livestock for extra income.

## Government

### Local government

Main article: [Sangguniang Bayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangguniang_Bayan)

As a municipality in the Province of Isabela, government officials at the provincial and municipal levels are voted by the town. The provincial government has political jurisdiction over most local transactions of the municipal government.

The Municipality of Santo Tomas is governed by a mayor, designated as its local chief executive, and by a municipal council as its legislative body in accordance with the Local Government Code. The mayor, vice mayor, and the municipal councilors are elected directly in polls held every three years.

Barangays are also headed by elected officials: [Barangay Captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barangay_Captain), [Barangay Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangguniang_Barangay), whose members are called [Barangay Councilors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barangay_Kagawad). The barangays have SK federation which represents the barangay, headed by SK chairperson and whose members are called SK councilors. All officials are also elected every three years.

## Education

The Schools Division of Isabela governs the town's public education system.[^cite_note-30] The division office is a field office of the [DepEd](5464548.md) in [Cagayan Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cagayan_Valley) region.[^cite_note-31] The Sto. Tomas Schools District Office governs the public and private elementary and high schools throughout the municipality.[^cite_note-32]
