**Khalida Boufedeche** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): خليدة بوفدش; born 27 May 1982) is an Algerian physician and politician and a member of the [People's National Assembly](2806769.md) of [Algeria](358.md). On 28 July 2026, she was elected the first woman president of the country's People's National Assembly.[^cite_note-CV-1]

## Career

Boufedeche began her professional career as a physician after obtaining her degree in general medicine from the [University of Algiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Algiers). She later specialized in allergology and clinical immunology and worked as a specialist physician at the [Béni Messous University Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A9ni_Messous_University_Hospital?action=edit\&redlink=1). She also served as head of department at the Directorate of Health and Population of [Algiers Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algiers_Province).[^cite_note-CapDZ-2]

Before entering national politics, she was a member of the [People's Municipal Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Municipal_Assembly) of [Bordj El Bahri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bordj_El_Bahri) from 2012 to 2017, and later served as a member of the [People's Provincial Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Provincial_Assembly) of Algiers for the 2021–2026 term.[^cite_note-CapDZ-2]

Boufedeche was elected as a member of the [People's National Assembly](2806769.md) representing [Algiers Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algiers_Province) in the [2026 Algerian parliamentary election](80597641.md). She is a member of the [National Liberation Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Liberation_Front_\(Algeria\)) (FLN) and serves on the party's Central Committee.[^cite_note-CapDZ-2]

On 28 July 2026, she was elected president of the [People's National Assembly](2806769.md), becoming the first woman to hold this position in Algeria.[^cite_note-3]

## Personal life

Khalida Boufedeche is married and is the mother of two children.[^cite_note-CV-1]
