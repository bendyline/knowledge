**Mohammed Umar** (born 22 February 1974) is a Nigerian banker and politician who is the current [governor of Niger State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Niger_State) since 29 May 2023.

He was born in [Minna](1658964.md), [Niger State](1723431.md), to a [Nupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_people) family.[^cite_note-1] He was a member of the House of Representative representing [Chanchaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chanchaga) Federal Constituency.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Mohammed Bago emerged the winner of the [2023 gubernatorial elections in Niger State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Niger_State_gubernatorial_election) after defeating [Isah Liman Kantigi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isah_Liman_Kantigi?action=edit\&redlink=1) of [PDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peoples_Democratic_Party_\(Nigeria\)).[^cite_note-4]

## Early life and education

Bago was born on 22 February 1974 in Minna, Niger State, Nigeria. He attended Marafa Primary School, [Minna](1658964.md) and Federal Government College, Jos. He earned his West Africa School Certificate from the [WAEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_African_Examinations_Council). He earned a bachelor's degree in political science at [Usman Danfodio University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usman_Danfodio_University), [Sokoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_\(city\)).[^cite_note-5]

Hon. Umaru Bago has obtained several postgraduate diplomas and master's degrees, including a postgraduate diploma in management from the [Federal University of Technology Minna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Technology_Minna) in 2001, a Master of Business Administration (MBA) in economics at the Ambrose Ali University, Ekpoma, in 2003, and a master's degree in finance at University of Calabar in 2005. He is also a distinguished alumnus from Cambridge University in the United Kingdom in 2014.[^cite_note-6]

## Career

Bago worked with the [United Bank for Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Bank_for_Africa) (UBA), [First City Monument Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_City_Monument_Bank) (FCMB) and Afri-Bank PLC respectively. He served as manager at [First City Monument Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_City_Monument_Bank).[^cite_note-7] He officially joined politics in 2007.

## Political career

He contested for the Speakership position of the [House of Representatives of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_Nigeria) coming second to [Femi Gbajabiamila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Femi_Gbajabiamila) in the [9th National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_Nigeria_National_Assembly).[^cite_note-8] He stated, "I won't back down, I just want to correct the mistake of the party leaders by opinion of zonings."[^cite_note-9]

He chaired the House Committee on Maritime Safety, Education and Administration. In 2016 he advised the management of the Maritime Commission that 83 staff should be relived of their duties. He stated, "That is how touts are employed."[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] He urged the NIMASA to spend money wisely in a well and prepared process.[^cite_note-12] He won the APC gubernatorial primary elections in Niger state ahead of the 2023 general elections. He became the winner of the gubernatorial election after pulling a total of 469,896 votes to his closest rival Alhaji Isah Liman Kantigi who got 387,476 votes.[^cite_note-13]

## Personal life and philanthropy

Bago is married with children. His mother, Hajiya Aisha Mohammed, died on 1 June 2019 after a brief illness.[^cite_note-14]

To improve the educational quality of his constituency, he bought 1000 [JAMB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Admissions_and_Matriculation_Board) forms to his people.[^cite_note-15] And also distributed 11 trailer loads of rice to his constituency as a palliative during COVID-19 pandemic period.[^cite_note-16]
