# Megale (woreda)

**Megale** is one of the [Districts of Ethiopia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Ethiopia), or *woredas*, in the [Afar Region](2483633.md) of [Ethiopia](187749.md). Part of the [Administrative Zone 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_Zone_2_\(Afar\)), Megale is located at the base of the eastern escarpment of the [Ethiopian Highlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopian_Highlands), and bordered on the south by the [Administrative Zone 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_Zone_4_\(Afar\)), on the west by the [Tigray Region](357350.md), on the north by [Abala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abala_\(Ethiopian_District\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), and on the east by [Erebti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erebti). The administrative center is at [Nehile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nehile?action=edit\&redlink=1).

The Leile hot springs is a notable point of interest, which is visited not only by local residents and people from the Tigray Region, but by inhabitants from the [Amhara Region](1746709.md), who arrive by foot.[^cite_note-1] Rivers in this woreda include the Erebti, a stream that flows east from the [Ethiopian Highlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopian_Highlands) into the [Afar Depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afar_Depression).

## Demographics

Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [Central Statistical Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Statistical_Agency_\(Ethiopia\)) of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 28,113, of whom 15,340 are men and 12,773 women; with an area of 1,548.33 square kilometers (597.81 square miles), Megale has a population density of 18.16 people per square kilometer (47.03 people per square mile). While 703 or 2.50% are urban inhabitants, a further 8,551 or 30.42% are pastoralists. A total of 4,714 households were counted in this woreda, which results in an average of 6.0 persons to a household, and 4,794 housing units. 99.22% of the population said they were [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Ethiopia).[^cite_note-2]
