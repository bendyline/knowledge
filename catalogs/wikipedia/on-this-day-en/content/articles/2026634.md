**Kebbe** is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Sokoto State](2227660.md), [Nigeria](21383.md). The LGA shares a border with [Zamfara State](2227664.md) in the east and [Kebbi State](2227643.md) in the south and west.

It has an area of 2,618 km<sup>2</sup> (1,011 sq mi) and a population of 124,658 at the 2006 census.[^cite_note-2]

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 850.[^cite_note-3]

## Climate

The climate features a hot, unpleasant rainy season with predominantly cloudy skies and a blistering, partly cloudy dry season with temperatures ranging from 18 °C (65 °F) to 39 °C (103 °F).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
