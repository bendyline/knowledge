**Los Lobos** (English: *The Wolves*) is an Ecuadorian criminal and terrorist organization that specializes in [drug trafficking](218449.md) and working as [hitmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_killing) for international partners or allied groups.[^cite_note-10] Los Lobos began as a splinter group of the [Los Choneros](72171124.md) drug cartel, but broke away in 2020 after the death of [Jorge Luis Zambrano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Luis_Zambrano), along with [Los Chone Killers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Chone_Killers) and [Los Tiguerones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Tiguerones). The group has over 8,000 members and mainly operates in cities of [Latacunga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latacunga), [Cuenca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuenca,_Ecuador), and [Machala](441979.md), and also in the province of [Pastaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pastaza_Province).[^cite_note-11] Los Lobos participates in exporting cocaine from [Ecuador](9334.md).[^cite_note-12]

## History

Los Lobos began as a splinter group of the [Los Choneros](72171124.md) drug cartel. After the assassination of [Jorge Luis Zambrano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Luis_Zambrano), the gang's leader, in 2020, a vacuum was left in the organization's leadership. Due to their weakened position, several gangs, including Los Lobos, broke away and formed a new alliance to combat them. They referred to themselves as the Nueva Generación.[^cite_note-13]

Members of Los Lobos were involved in several prison riots across Ecuador. On 28 September 2021, a dispute between Los Lobos and Los Choneros in the [Litoral Penitentiary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litoral_Penitentiary), [Guayaquil](215586.md), led to [a riot that killed 123 inmates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/September_2021_Guayaquil_prison_riot) and injured over 80. It was the deadliest prison riot in Ecuador's history.[^cite_note-14] On 4 April 2022, riots broke out in Turi prison, [Cuenca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuenca,_Ecuador) which killed at least 20 inmates. Alexander Quesada, the leader of Los Lobos, and Marcelo Anchundia, the leader of rival gang R7, were reportedly behind the riot.[^cite_note-15] On 9 May 2022, at least 44 inmates were killed and over 100 managed to escape after a riot in Bellavista prison, [Santo Domingo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santo_Domingo,_Ecuador). The riot was fought between members of Los Lobos and R7 in an attempt to kill Anchundia, who had been transferred to the prison along with Quesada.[^cite_note-16]

In November 2022, Los Lobos, along with their ally Los Tiguerones, were behind a wave of violence across Ecuador after many of their members were transferred out of the Litoral Penitentiary, which they feared would result in a loss of control over the prison. During the wave of violence, two headless bodies were found hanging from a bridge,[^cite_note-17] inmates took eight guards hostage,[^cite_note-18] nine car bombs were set off in the provinces of [Esmeraldas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esmeraldas_Province) and [Guayas](215591.md),[^cite_note-19] and five police officers were shot dead.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

On 9 August 2023, masked men claiming to be members of Los Lobos claimed responsibility for the [assassination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Fernando_Villavicencio) of [Fernando Villavicencio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Villavicencio) in a video. In a second video, other men purporting to be members of Los Lobos denied playing a role in the assassination and said that they were being framed.[^cite_note-22] An investigation found that the order to kill Villavicencio came from inside a prison. An inmate imprisoned in the Latacunga jail who had sent messages to one of the gunmen and a woman who was accused by prosecutors of providing the assailants with logistical support were both allegedly members of the gang.[^cite_note-23]

In 2025, the United States designated the Los Lobos and [Los Choneros](72171124.md) gangs as foreign terrorist organizations.[^cite_note-24] On 16 November, Los Lobos leader Wilmer "Pipo" Chavarria, who faked his death in 2021, was arrested in [Málaga](184763.md), Spain.[^cite_note-25]

In March 2026, Ángel Esteban Aguilar Morales aka Lobo Menor, a suspected leader of Los Lobos accused of involvement in the Villavicencio assassination, was arrested in Mexico and extradited to Colombia, where he is also wanted on charges of involvement with [FARC dissidents](57074954.md).[^cite_note-26]
