The **2026 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup** was the 20th edition of the [FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup](5779563.md), the quadrennial world championship for women's national [basketball](3921.md) teams, sanctioned by [FIBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA). The tournament was hosted in [Berlin](3354.md), Germany, from 4 to 13 September 2026.[^cite_note-1] This was Germany's second time hosting after previously organising the event in [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women), after being awarded the hosting rights in spring 2023.

Sixteen teams took part in the tournament, four more than the previous edition but returning to the same number as was between [1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women) and [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup). The hosts automatically qualified. Qualifiers were decided via a mixture of continental champions and teams progressing from the [qualifying tournaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup_Qualifying_Tournaments). [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary_women's_national_basketball_team) and [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_women's_national_basketball_team) returned after long absences.

The [United States](7129278.md) were the four-time defending champion, and retained the title after a finals win over [France](6787937.md).[^cite_note-2] It also represented the second final between those teams at an international event since the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_tournament#Gold_medal_game) gold medal match.

### Expansion

On 25 March 2022, following a review of the current FIBA women's basketball system, [FIBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA) expanded the World Cup back to 16 teams after reducing the teams to 12 teams in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup).[^cite_note-7] The expansion brought the numbers of teams that qualified back to the same count as the World Cups between [1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women) and [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup).

### Qualification

Germany as the hosts automatically qualified for the tournament in April 2023. Each winner of the continental championship qualified. All other teams played in a qualifying tournament after finishing as the top teams during their regional tournament. A total of 24 teams played in those qualifying tournaments for the remaining eleven spots.[^cite_note-8]

### Qualified teams



[2026 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup Qualifying Tournaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup_Qualifying_Tournaments) played in China, France, Puerto Rico and Turkey

Of the sixteen teams who qualified, nine took part in the previous edition. For the first time since [1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women), no teams debuted at this edition. Of the seven returnees, [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_women's_national_basketball_team) and [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary_women's_national_basketball_team) came after long absences after last appearing in [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women) and [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women) respectively. Hosts [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany_women's_national_basketball_team) also returned after their only appearance was hosting in [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women). [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic_women's_national_basketball_team) made an appearance after missing out on the previous two editions. [Spain](13486344.md) and [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey_women's_national_basketball_team) progressed this time round after failing to advance in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup), while [Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria_women's_national_basketball_team) took part after withdrawing in 2022.

Of the absentees, the most notable absentee is 2022 fourth place finishers [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_women's_national_basketball_team), who failed to qualify for the first time since [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women) after missing out on a three way tie in the Qualifying Tournaments. 2022 debutants, [Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnia_and_Herzegovina_women's_national_basketball_team), alongside [Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia_women's_national_basketball_team), couldn't qualify after their sporadic appearances in 2022.&#x20;

After the Qualifying Tournaments, the regional team split as follows: eight from [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Europe), four from [Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Asia) and [Oceania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Oceania) and two from [Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Africa) and [Americas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Americas). Europe's eight teams breaks their record of most European teams at a single World Cup (previously held by [1957](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1957_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women) with seven); while on the other hand, Americas' two representatives ([Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico_women's_national_basketball_team) and [United States)](7129278.md) marks the lowest number of teams at the World Cup from the region since [1967](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women) (in which only 11 teams competed).

[Mali](13561581.md) qualified for a second successive edition for the first time, while [Belgium](40566612.md) and Puerto Rico continue their qualification streaks since their debuts in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup). [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea_women's_national_basketball_team) and the United States continue their longest active qualification streaks with 18 and 17 respectively.

The highest ranked team to not qualify was Canada, ranked 7th, while the lowest ranked team to qualify was Hungary, ranked 19th.

## Venues

### Marketing

#### Logo

The logo was unveiled on 12 August 2024.[^cite_note-18] According to [FIBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA), the logo is supposed to depict "Berlin's status as a united metropolis that loves to party and celebrate".[^cite_note-19]

#### Mascot

The mascot, "Bearlina", was revealed during the HipHop Ball at [Red Town Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Town_Hall_\(Berlin\)) on 15 November 2025. Bearlina is a [bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bear) (which is featured on the [coat of arms of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coat_of_arms_of_Berlin)), whose design represents the lifestyle of the host city, including elements of punk, hip hop, graffiti art, and the design of the basketball used for FIBA competitions.[^cite_note-20]

## Draw



The [Kraftwerk](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraftwerk%20Berlin) in [Berlin](3354.md) hosted the draw.

The draw took place on 21 April 2026 at 18\:00 [CET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Time) at the [Kraftwerk](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraftwerk%20Berlin) in [Berlin](3354.md).[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] The draw was hosted by American actress, [Brittney Elena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brittney_Elena?action=edit\&redlink=1), and German presenter, [Matthias Killing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthias_Killing?action=edit\&redlink=1). Australian basketball legend, [Lauren Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Jackson), German handball player, [Stefan Kretzschmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Kretzschmar) and breakdancer [Jilou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jilou?action=edit\&redlink=1) were the guests and assisted with the draw. The draw started with, in order, pots 1, 2, 3 and 4 being drawn, with each team selected then allocated into the first available group alphabetically. The position for the team within the group would then be drawn (for the purpose of the schedule). Before the draw, hosts Germany were pre-drawn into Group A. Regarding restrictions, a maximum of two teams from [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Europe) are allowed in each group, while a maximum of one team from [Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Africa), [Americas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Americas) and [Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Asia)/[Oceania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Oceania) will be drawn in each group.

### Classification of teams

1. Highest number of points earned, with each game result having a corresponding point:&#x20;

   - Win: 2 points

   - Loss: 1 point

   - Loss by [default](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forfeit_\(sport\)#Basketball): 1 point, with a final score of 2–0 for the opponents of the defaulting team if the latter team is not trailing or if the score is tied, or the score at the time of stoppage if they are trailing.

   - Loss by [forfeit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forfeit_\(sport\)): 0 points, with a final score of 20–0 for the opponents of the forfeiting team.

2. Head-to-head record via points system above

3. Point difference in games among tied teams

4. Points for in games among tied teams

5. Point difference in all group games

6. Points for in all group games
