**Abbosbek Saidzhon ugli Fayzullaev** ([Uzbek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbek_language): *Abbosbek Sayidjon oʻgʻli Fayzullayev*;[^cite_note-president.uz-1] born 3 October 2003) is an Uzbek professional [footballer](10568.md) who plays as an [attacking midfielder](548981.md#Attacking_midfielder) or [winger](548981.md#Winger) for [Süper Lig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%BCper_Lig) club [İstanbul Başakşehir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0stanbul_Ba%C5%9Fak%C5%9Fehir_F.K.) and the [Uzbekistan national team](1032413.md).

## Club career



Fayzullaev with [CSKA Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PFC_CSKA_Moscow) in 2024

On 6 May 2021, he made his debut in the [Uzbekistan Super League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan_Super_League) against [Turon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Turon). On 29 June 2022, he scored his first goal for [Pakhtakor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakhtakor_FC) in a game against [Bunyodkor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Bunyodkor).[^cite_note-5]

On 4 August 2023, [CSKA Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PFC_CSKA_Moscow) announced the signing of Fayzullaev to a three-year contract from Pakhtakor.[^cite_note-\:0-6] On August 19, he made his debut for his new club in a match against Dynamo Moscow (1–2), coming on as a substitute in the 87th minute.[^cite_note-7] On September 24, he scored his first goal for CSKA in a 3\:3 match against Rostov.[^cite_note-8]

In December 2023, he was nominated for the [IFFHS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFFHS) award for the Best Young Player of the Year.[^cite_note-9]

On 26 May 2024, Fayzullaev received the Breakthrough of the Season award in the [2023–24 Russian Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Russian_Premier_League).[^cite_note-10]

On 29 October 2024, he was named the AFC Youth Player of the Year for 2023.[^cite_note-autoname1-11]

On 30 July 2025, Fayzullaev signed a five-year contract with [İstanbul Başakşehir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0stanbul_Ba%C5%9Fak%C5%9Fehir_F.K.) in Turkey.[^cite_note-12]

## International career



Abbosbek Fayzullaev, [Mirjalol Kasymov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirjalol_Kasymov) and [Davron Fayziev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davron_Fayziev)



Abbosbek Fayzullaev scored goal. Uzbekistan vs. Spain, 2024 Summer Olympic men's association football, Parc des Princes (Paris).

Fayzullaev was called up to the Uzbekistan under-19 squad while still in the academy of Pakhtakor Tashkent.[^cite_note-FIFA-13] He was called up to the nation's under-23 squad in October 2021, for [2022 AFC U-23 Asian Cup qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_AFC_U-23_Asian_Cup_qualification), and marked his debut with a goal against [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_national_under-23_football_team).[^cite_note-14] For his performances at the [2022 AFC U-23 Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_AFC_U-23_Asian_Cup), in which Uzbekistan were beaten in the final by [Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia_national_under-23_football_team), Fayzullaev won the Uzbekistan FA's Young Star of the Year award.[^cite_note-15]

He was called up to the under-20 side for the [2023 AFC U-20 Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_AFC_U-20_Asian_Cup), and went on to star in the competition. In the final against [Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq_national_under-20_football_team), he won the penalty which sealed the 1–0 victory for his side, as teammate Umarali Rahmonaliyev converted from the spot, giving Uzbekistan their first ever Under-20 Asian Cup title.[^cite_note-16] For his performances, he was named the tournament's [Most Valuable Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Most_Valuable_Player).[^cite_note-17]

On 11 June 2023, he debuted for the [senior national team](1032413.md) in a match against [Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oman_national_football_team), which ended in a 3–0 victory.[^cite_note-18] On June 17, in a match against [Tajikistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajikistan_national_football_team), he scored his first goal for the national team, contributing to a 5–1 victory.[^cite_note-19] On 4 January 2024, he was included in the final squad for the [2023 AFC Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_AFC_Asian_Cup). During the tournament, he played in five matches and scored two goals, against the [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India_national_football_team) and [Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand_national_football_team) teams.

In 2024, he participated in the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics) in [Paris](22989.md) as part of Uzbekistan's Olympic football team.[^cite_note-20] He played in the match against [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_Olympic_football_team).

On 14 November 2024, during a [2026 World Cup qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(AFC\)) match against [Qatar](1111350.md), he scored a brace, marking his first double for the national team in a match that ended 3–2.[^cite_note-21]

On 2 June 2026, he was included in the 26-man squad selected by head coach [Fabio Cannavaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabio_Cannavaro) for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) in the country's first-ever appearance in the tournament.[^cite_note-22] On 17 June, in their opener against [Colombia](680175.md), he scored the country's first ever goal in a World Cup finals in a 1–3 defeat.[^cite_note-23]
