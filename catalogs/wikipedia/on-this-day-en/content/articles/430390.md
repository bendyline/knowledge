The **president of the Republic of Mali** ([Bambara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambara_language): *Mali Jamanakuntigi*) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [head of government](37570.md) of [Mali](19127.md). The president leads the government and is the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Malian Armed Forces](19135.md).

A total of seven people have served as head of state of Mali (excluding three acting presidents). Additionally, two people, [Amadou Toumani Touré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amadou_Toumani_Tour%C3%A9) and [Assimi Goïta](65000306.md), have served on two non-consecutive occasions.

The current head of state of Mali is interim president [Assimi Goïta](65000306.md), who took power for a second time on 24 May 2021, after dismissing previous interim president [Bah Ndaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bah_Ndaw) in the [2021 coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Malian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat).[^cite_note-2021_coup-3] He has since been constitutionally declared interim president of Mali.[^cite_note-interim1-4][^cite_note-interim2-5] Since the [2023 constitutional referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Malian_constitutional_referendum) held during Goita's leadership, Mali has shifted from a [semi-presidential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-presidential) to a full [presidential system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_system), with the president serving as the [head of government](37570.md) and dictating the policies of the government.[^cite_note-6]

## Term limits

As of 2021, there is a two-term limit for the president in the [Constitution of Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Mali). The term limit has not been met by any president yet.[^cite_note-termlimits-7]

## Succession

Article 53 of the Constitution entrusts the [Prime Minister](889867.md) with the duties of the presidency during temporary absences. In the case of a definitive vacancy due to resignation, death, or permanent incapacitation, the [President of the National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_presidents_of_the_National_Assembly_of_Mali) assumes the functions of the president for a period of 90 to 120 days until a new president can be elected.

## Titles

- **1960–1965**: Head of State

- **1965–1968**: President of the Republic

- **1968–1969**: Chairman of the [Military Committee for National Liberation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Committee_for_National_Liberation_\(Mali\))

- **1969–1979**: Head of State

- **1979–1991**: President of the Republic

- **1991**: Chairman of the National Reconciliation Council

- **1991–1992**: Chairman of the Transitional Committee for the Salvation of the People

- **1992–2012**: President of the Republic

- **2012**: Chairman of the National Committee for the Restoration of Democracy and State

- **2012–2020**: President of the Republic

- **2020**: Chairman of the [National Committee for the Salvation of the People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Committee_for_the_Salvation_of_the_People)

- **2020–2025**: President of the Transition

- **2025–present**: President of the Republic
