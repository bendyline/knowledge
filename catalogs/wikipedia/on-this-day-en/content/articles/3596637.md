The **Prime Minister of the State of Qatar** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): رئیس الوزراء القطري) is the [head of government](37570.md) of [Qatar](8238258.md), and technically the second most [powerful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_\(social_and_political\)) official in the country after the [Emir](399230.md), who in practice holds all power and authority.

[Khalifa bin Hamad Al Thani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalifa_bin_Hamad_Al_Thani), the [Emir of Qatar](399230.md) since the [coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Qatari_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) of 1972, was the first holder of this position from its establishment in 1970. He served as prime minister until the [coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Qatari_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) of June 1995, when he was deposed by his son [Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani](295929.md).

In March 2023, emir [Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani](6146151.md) replaced [Khalid bin Khalifa bin Abdul Aziz Al Thani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalid_bin_Khalifa_bin_Abdul_Aziz_Al_Thani) with his minister of foreign affairs, current prime minister [Mohammed bin Abdulrahman bin Jassim Al Thani](49695374.md).[^cite_note-1]

## Constitutional role and authority

The Prime Minister of Qatar holds the position of head of government, and is technically the second most powerful official in the country after the Emir, in a [form of government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_forms_of_government) which is officially a [constitutional monarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_monarchy),[^cite_note-BBC9Sep05-2][^cite_note-USState2011-3] but in practice is more often characterized as an [absolute monarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolute_monarchy)[^cite_note-ftManage-4][^cite_note-5] and [autocracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autocracy).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

The prime minister's authority is entirely derivative of the Emir's power. Analysts consistently note that the Emir holds the power to appoint, dismiss, and accept the resignation of the Prime Minister and all ministers, with the emir maintaining ultimate [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) and [legislative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) authority, and the prime minister acting as a supervisor and operational manager implementing the emir's vision.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]
