**Yahalom** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): יהל"ם, meaning *Diamond*; also a [backronym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backronym) of יחידה הנדסית למשימות מיוחדות, *Special Operations Engineering Unit*)[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] is a [*sayeret*](77766.md) (special operations forces) unit of the [Israeli Combat Engineering Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Combat_Engineering_Corps) of the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) (IDF).

## Organization and activities

### Role

Yahalom specializes in special engineering missions that include:

- Accurate demolition through pinpoint planting of explosives

- [CBRNE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBRNE) consequence management

- Searching, extracting, and destroying CBRN weapons and explosives

- Tactical emergency medical service and [medevac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medevac) when injured by CBRN weapons, and after incidents associated with defusing and disposing of bombs or high-yield explosives

- Commando-style stealth operations

- Counterinsurgency and counterterrorism (CT)

- Military special operations against guerrilla warfare

- Defusing and disposal of bombs, land mines, and [unexploded ordnance](406804.md)¹

- Developing advanced methods and tools for demolition and [EOD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosive_Ordnance_Demolition)

- Teaching and training engineering corps soldiers and other special units in demolition and EOD

- Maritime sabotage and obstacle breaching

- Route reconnaissance and clearing routes of [IEDs](265112.md)

- Searching and destroying [smuggling tunnels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smuggling_tunnels)

### General activities

Yahalom is a classified unit and almost none of its special activities are exposed to the public. Public accounts of its activities usually just credit a "combat engineering force"; a term that can equally describe regular Engineering [sappers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapper), [IDF Caterpillar D9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDF_Caterpillar_D9) operators and infantry engineering companies. [Jane's Defence Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane's_Defence_Weekly) has claimed that Yahalom are working closely with [Sayeret Matkal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayeret_Matkal) and [Shayetet 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shayetet_13), by providing them with demolition, explosive and sabotage skills. Most of the equipment that Yahalom has developed for its missions is classified. Yahalom maintains secrecy to make it more difficult for enemies to develop countermeasures.

### Sub-units

- Yael ("[Ibex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibex)") - a commando demolition unit that conducts long range sabotage, [combat engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_engineering), [counter terror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter_terror), and maritime engineering missions. The YAEL team has destroyed weapon caches hidden in military and civilian infrastructure, tunnels used to smuggle weapons, and firing positions.

- SAP ("EOD Unit") - an EOD unit that removes [land mines](18172.md) and missile [warheads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warhead), handles [nuclear](21785.md), [biological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_warfare) and [chemical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_warfare) ([NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapons_of_Mass_Destruction)) threats, performs [bomb disposal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bomb_disposal), and accompanies special operations units on missions where their units are at risk of encountering explosive booby traps. YACHSAP is constantly engaged in defusing large IEDs that [Hezbollah](13919.md) has set along the [Israel-Lebanon border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Line_\(Lebanon\)) and bombs Palestinians have planted in the [West Bank](33209.md) and the [Gaza Strip](12047.md). They often use [armored bulldozers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armored_bulldozer) when executing those missions. SAP is initials for Bomb disposal.

- Samur ("[Weasel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weasel)") - a commando unit whose expertise is finding and destroying and if needed fighting in [smuggling tunnels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smuggling_tunnels) and hidden weapon caches.

  [^cite_note-3]

  &#x20;The unit dates back to 2004 when the IDF merged the [Southern Command](5216006.md) [Philadelphi Route](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphi_Route) [Smuggling tunnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smuggling_tunnel) experts team into Yahalom to form the Samur platoon.. Samur is also initials for Slikim (Caches) and Minharot (Caves or Tunnels)

- Midron Mushlag ("Snowy Slope") - a unit whose expertise is penetrating buildings using hot breaching techniques using explosives charges, or cold breaching techniques using hydraulic expanders, crow bars and lock picks.

- Hevzek ("Flash") - a unit that operated military [robots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robot) - defunct.

- SAPIR ("Sapphire") - Aerial ordnance EOD Unit, stationed at major [IAF](177619.md) bases and tasked with removal of air ordnance such as bombs, missiles, rockets, etc. It is also tasked with safe removal of unexploded enemy ordnance inside the base perimeter. SAPIR is initials for SAP (Bomb disposal) and Avir (air.)

- Sayfan ("Avocet") - [CBRNE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBRNE) unit, trained to respond to threats from weapons of mass destruction, mostly focused on chemical weaponry.

- Givol ("Flower stem") - [CBRN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBRN) disposal unit, composed of reserve service personnel.

### Arms and equipment

- Firearms include the [M4 Carbine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M4_Carbine), [handguns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handgun), [IWI Negev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IWI_Negev) Commando [SAW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squad_automatic_weapon), [sniper rifles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sniper_rifle) and combat [shotguns](26838.md).

- Yahalom operates remote-controlled [robots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robot) inserted into tunnels, obviating the need for human operators to risk their lives. EOD robots for handling [improvised explosive devices](265112.md) (IEDs), bombs and [explosive](10192.md) charges, and advanced EOD vehicles are standard equipment for all engineering units handling explosives. The [Israel Border Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Border_Police) [bomb disposal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bomb_disposal) experts use similar equipment. Yahalom also uses the up-armored [IDF Caterpillar D9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDF_Caterpillar_D9) [bulldozer](313830.md) and the remote-controlled Raam HaShachar ("Morning thunder") version of the D9 (which were found extremely useful for special operation and CT duties), an armored [excavator](312903.md) with drills, the [IDF Puma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDF_Puma) [combat engineering vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_engineering_vehicle) (CEV), and [Nakpuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakpuma?action=edit\&redlink=1), a [Nakpadon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakpadon) [APC](19278686.md) modified to Yahalom's requirements.

In order to be accepted into the unit one must enlist with the [Israeli Engineering Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Engineering_Corps) and go through basic training ("[Tironut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tironut)"), where commanders identify the best trainees and select them for "Gibbush" (a grueling five-day test of physical and mental condition in intensive field trials). The best graduates of Gibbush are invited to join the unit and receive advance training, which takes another year. Because the training takes a total of 1.4 years, the volunteers must agree to serve an extra year (in addition to the mandatory three year service in the IDF). The training includes training in engineering, [explosive ordnance disposal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosive_ordnance_disposal), advanced combat and [counter-terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-terrorism).
