The **prosecutor of the International Criminal Court** is the officer of the [International Criminal Court](14880.md) (ICC), whose duties include the investigation and [prosecution](440936.md) of the crimes under the jurisdiction of the ICC, namely [genocide](12441.md), [crimes against humanity](61099.md) and [war crimes](48626.md) as well as the [crime of aggression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_of_aggression). The prosecutor heads the **Office of the Prosecutor** (**OTP**).

The position of Prosecutor of the ICC is currently vacant following the removal of [Karim Ahmad Khan](11606957.md), [KC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_Counsel), who served as Prosecutor from 16 June 2021 until 24 July 2026, when he was sacked by the [Assembly of States Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_of_States_Parties) following a vote.[^cite_note-khan_2026_07_24_reuters-2]

## Elections of the prosecutor

The **first election** of the prosecutor took place on 21 April 2003, during the second resumption of the first session of the [Assembly of States Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_of_States_Parties) in [New York](645042.md).[^cite_note-first_report_addendum_contents-1] The only official candidate was [Luis Moreno Ocampo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Moreno_Ocampo). Moreno Ocampo was elected with 78 votes with no votes against and no abstentions. Nine states parties did not vote.

The **second election** of the prosecutor took place during the tenth session of the Assembly of States Parties in New York on 12 December 2011. A Search Committee was established which received 52 communications regarding a new prosecutor. In the end, the states parties achieved consensus to elect the then-deputy prosecutor, [Fatou Bensouda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatou_Bensouda), as the new prosecutor.[^cite_note-3] She was elected by acclamation.

The **third election** of the prosecutor took place during the second resumed nineteenth session of the Assembly of States Parties in New York on 12 February 2021. In its first ballot, [Karim Ahmad Khan](11606957.md), [QC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_Counsel) received 59 votes, [Fergal Gaynor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fergal_Gaynor?action=edit\&redlink=1) (Ireland) 47, [Carlos Castresana Fernández](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Castresana_Fern%C3%A1ndez?action=edit\&redlink=1) (Spain) 12, and [Francesco Lo Voi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Lo_Voi?action=edit\&redlink=1) (Italy) 5. In its second ballot, Karim Khan was elected with 72 votes, surpassing the majority requirement of 62. Fergal Gaynor received 42 votes, Carlos Castresana Fernández 5, and Francesco Lo Voi 3.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
