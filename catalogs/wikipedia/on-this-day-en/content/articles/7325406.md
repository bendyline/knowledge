The **Netherlands' national women's field hockey team** is currently number one on the [International Hockey Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Hockey_Federation) (FIH) world rankings and the reigning [world champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Women's_Hockey_World_Cup). The Netherlands is the most successful team in [World Cup history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Hockey_World_Cup), having won the title a record nine times.[^cite_note-2] The team has also won ten [Olympic medals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_hockey_at_the_Olympic_Games).
