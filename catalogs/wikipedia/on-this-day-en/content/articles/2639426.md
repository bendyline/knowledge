The **S\&P/TSX 60 Index** is a [stock market index](23408957.md) of 60 large companies listed on the [Toronto Stock Exchange](205584.md). Launched on December 30, 1998 by the Canadian S\&P Index Committee,[^cite_note-2] a unit of [S\&P Dow Jones Indices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S\&P_Dow_Jones_Indices), the index has components across nine sectors of the Canadian economy. The index forms the [S\&P/TSX Composite Index](1840566.md) alongside the S\&P/TSX Completion Index, as well as being the Canadian component of the [S\&P Global 1200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S\&P_Global_1200).[^cite_note-tmx-3]

## TSX 60-based funds

The [iShares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IShares) S\&P/TSX 60 Index Fund ([TSX](205584.md): [XIU](https://money.tmx.com/en/quote/XIU)) and the Global X S\&P/TSX 60 Index ETF ([TSX](205584.md): [HXT](https://money.tmx.com/en/quote/HXT)) are Canadian [exchange-traded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exchange-traded_fund) [index funds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_fund) that are tied to the S\&P/TSX 60.
