**Kharg Petrochemical Company** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): شرکت پتروشیمی خارگ, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Sherkat-e Petroshimi-ye Kharg*), is an Iranian [petrochemical](67728.md) company in [Kharg Island](2210066.md), a [continental island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island#Continental_islands) of [Iran](14653.md) in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md).

## History



A gas flare and pipelines of Kharg Petrochemical Company

The Khark Petrochemical Company was established on [Kharg Island](2210066.md) in 1965, originally named the Khark Chemical Company. Its primary purpose was to recycle sour gas and various byproducts from [Iranian offshore oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Offshore_Oil_Company) well gases, including [propane](23643.md), [butane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butane), [naphtha](184342.md), and [sulfur](27127.md). The company was founded as a 50/50 joint venture between [NIOC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Iranian_Oil_Company) and [Amoco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amoco). An American company, J.E. Prichard, developed the initial engineering design for the operational units, while [Chiyoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiyoda_Corporation) executed the construction and installation. Construction began in early 1967, and the facility was commissioned in 1969. Over time, the company expanded its operations to include methanol production, with its [methanol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methanol) plant. The construction of the methanol plant, designed by [Lurgi AG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lurgi_AG), began in 1994. This factory was put into operation in 1999. The company was converted into a public company on September 11, 1996, and listed on the [Tehran Stock Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran_Stock_Exchange) on January 16, 1999, as "PKHA1".[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Products

The Kharg Petrochemical produces [sulfur](27127.md), [propane](23643.md), [butane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butane), [pentane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentane), and [methanol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methanol) by purifying sour gases extracted from the [Iranian offshore oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Offshore_Oil_Company) wells.[^cite_note-4]

## Sanctions

In 2022, the [United States Treasury Department](53667.md) sanctioned Kharg, [Marun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marun_petrochemical_company), and [Fanavaran petrochemical company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanavaran_petrochemical_company) in order to target the export of Iranian prochemical products.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]
