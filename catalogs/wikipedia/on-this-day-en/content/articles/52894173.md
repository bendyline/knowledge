**Kamjong district** ([Meitei pronunciation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meitei_language): /kām-jōng/[^cite_note-Manipuri-2]) is one of the 16 districts of [Manipur](422642.md), India. It was created in 2016 from the [Ukhrul district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukhrul_district),[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] from the former subdivisions [Kamjong-Chassad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamjong), [Phungyar-Phaisat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phungyar) and [Kasom Khullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasom_Khullen).[^cite_note-districtcensus-1]

The district headquarters is located at [Kamjong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamjong). It shares a long international border with Myanmar. The district is bounded by Myanmar in the east, [Kangpokpi district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kangpokpi_district) in the West, [Ukhrul district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukhrul_district) in the north and [Tengnoupal district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tengnoupal_district) in the south. The terrain of the district is hilly with varying heights of 913 m to 3114 m (MSL). The district headquarter is linked with state highway of 120 Km from Imphal.

Kamjong district has a population of 45,616, entirely rural. Kamjong had a sex ratio of 943 females per 1000 males. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes made up 0.05% and 96.56% of the population respectively.[^cite_note-districtcensus-1]

Languages of Kamjong district (2011)

[^cite_note-language-7]

1. [Tangkhul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangkhul_language) (89.1%)

2. [Thadou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thadou_language) (6.61%)

3. [Kuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuki-Chin_languages) (1.63%)

4. Others (2.64%)

At the time of the 2011 census, 89.12% of the population spoke [Tangkhul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangkhul_language), 6.61% [Thadou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thadou_language) and 1.63% [Kuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuki-Chin_languages) as their first language.[^cite_note-language-7]
