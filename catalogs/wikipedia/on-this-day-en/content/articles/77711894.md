The **2025–26 Premier League** was the 34th season of the [Premier League](11250.md) and the 127th season of top-flight [English football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_in_England). The fixtures were released on 18 June 2025.[^cite_note-1] The season consisted of 33 weekend and five midweek rounds of matches.

[Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.) were the [defending champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Premier_League), but were unable to defend their title, with [Arsenal](2174.md) winning their fourth Premier League title and 14th English title with one match to spare, ending their 22-year Premier League drought since they last won in [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003%E2%80%9304_FA_Premier_League), following [Manchester City](165813.md)'s 1–1 draw away against [Bournemouth](451121.md) on 19 May 2026, combined with their 1–0 win at home against [Burnley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnley) the previous day.[^cite_note-SKY1-2]

This was the first season to feature the [Tyne–Wear derby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyne%E2%80%93Wear_derby) in the Premier League since the [2015–16 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_Premier_League), following [Sunderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland_A.F.C.)'s promotion via the [Championship play-offs](6043445.md).

The summer transfer window opened on 16 June 2025 and closed at 19\:00 [BST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Summer_Time) on 1 September 2025.[^cite_note-3] The winter window opened on 1 January 2026 and closed at 19\:00 [GMT](12701.md) on 2 February 2026.

## Summary

### Title race

The early pace-setters were [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.), who won their first five games and spent nearly all of August and September combined on top of the Premier League table.[^cite_note-ATH1-4] However, Arsenal went top of the table on 4 October after a 2–0 win over [West Ham United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham_United_F.C.) was followed on the same day by a 2–1 defeat for Liverpool against [Chelsea](7473.md); they would remain virtually top for the rest of the season,[^cite_note-NBC1-5] leading the table for a total of 238 days.[^cite_note-GAR1-6] Their lead from [Manchester City](165813.md) fluctuated and at one point on 22 April, City were ahead of Arsenal on goals scored.[^cite_note-NBC1-5] However, Arsenal won their next four games and with City's draws at [Everton](91155.md) on 4 May and [Bournemouth](451121.md) on 19 May, they were confirmed champions with a game to spare.[^cite_note-SKY1-2]

On 24 May, Arsenal were crowned champions after being presented with the Premier League trophy after a 2–1 away win against [Crystal Palace](385313.md) on the final day of the season.[^cite_note-7]

### European places

Nine clubs will play in European competitions next season.[^cite_note-SKY3-8][^cite_note-9] The Premier League will have their usual four [UEFA Champions League](44220.md) places plus a fifth place due to the European Performance Spot (EPS), awarded to the two best performing associations from the previous season; Arsenal, Manchester City, [Manchester United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C.) and [Aston Villa](42173.md) all confirmed their places with games to spare, and the fifth place went to [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.) on the last day of the season.[^cite_note-\:3-10] Since the [FA Cup](79604093.md) and [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_EFL_Cup) winners Manchester City qualified for the Champions League and with the sixth place team qualifying for the [UEFA Europa League](232175.md) through the league route, their Europa League place went to the seventh place team and their [UEFA Conference League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Conference_League) place was given to the eighth place team.[^cite_note-SKY3-8] Aston Villa won the [Europa League final](76987231.md) on 20 May, but as they finished fourth on the final day, instead of a sixth place in the Champions League being awarded to the Premier League, the access list was rebalanced and [Sporting Lisbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_CP) were given the spot in the league phase instead.[^cite_note-SKY3-8] Sixth-placed [Bournemouth](451121.md) alongside seventh-placed [Sunderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland_A.F.C.) qualified for the Europa League while [Brighton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighton_&_Hove_Albion_F.C.) finished eighth and qualified for the UEFA Conference League play-off round.[^cite_note-\:3-10] [Crystal Palace](385313.md) also gained a Europa League place as the winners of the [Conference League final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_UEFA_Conference_League_final) against [Rayo Vallecano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayo_Vallecano) on 27 May.[^cite_note-11]

### Relegation

[Wolverhampton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverhampton_Wanderers_F.C.) became the first team to be relegated to the [EFL Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship) on 20 April 2026, after a goalless draw between West Ham United and Crystal Palace combined with their 3–0 loss away to Leeds United two days previously.[^cite_note-wolves-rel-12] This ended an eight-year stay in the Premier League,[^cite_note-wolves-rel-12] and also meant that this would be the first time since the [2022–23 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Premier_League) where at least one promoted club avoided immediate relegation, after all three of the promoted teams went straight back down in [2023–24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Premier_League) and [2024–25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Premier_League).[^cite_note-sund\&leeds-13][^cite_note-opta-relegation-14]

[Burnley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnley_F.C) became the second team to be relegated to the [EFL Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship) on 22 April 2026 following their 1–0 loss at home to [Manchester City](165813.md).[^cite_note-15]

The last relegation spot was decided on the final day of the season between London [rivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.%E2%80%93West_Ham_United_F.C._rivalry) [West Ham United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham_United_F.C.) and [Tottenham Hotspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.), with Spurs having a two point advantage. West Ham defeated [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.) 3–0 in their last match, but due to Tottenham also beating [Everton](91155.md), West Ham became the third team to be relegated.[^cite_note-16] This was the first season since [2010–11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010%E2%80%9311_Premier_League) that a club was relegated with as many as 39 points.[^cite_note-17]

### Promoted clubs

Following a 1–1 draw against already relegated [Wolverhampton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverhampton_Wanderers_F.C.) on 2 May, [Sunderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland_A.F.C.) became mathematically safe from relegation.[^cite_note-18] [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.)'s safety was secured following a 1–0 win for [Arsenal](2174.md) against [West Ham United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham_United_F.C.) on 10 May.[^cite_note-19] Sunderland and Leeds were the first promoted clubs to avoid relegation since the [2022–23 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Premier_League).[^cite_note-sund\&leeds-13] Sunderland finished the season in seventh, a joint best for a newly promoted side alongside Wolverhampton Wanderers in the [2018–19 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_Premier_League), and with 54 points, the most since Leeds United's 59 in the [2020–21 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Premier_League).[^cite_note-20] This is also the third time Sunderland finished the season in seventh with over 50 points, following the [1999–2000 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999%E2%80%932000_FA_Premier_League) and the [2000–01 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000%E2%80%9301_FA_Premier_League) finishing with 58 and 57 points respectively.

### Developments

This was the first full season with [semi-automated offside technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-automated_offside_technology) in use, following its introduction during the previous season on 12 April 2025.[^cite_note-\:0-21][^cite_note-22]

[Puma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puma_\(brand\)) also replaced [Nike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nike,_Inc.) as the official match ball supplier, ending a 25-year partnership between the Premier League and Nike, from the [2000–01 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000%E2%80%9301_FA_Premier_League) to the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Premier_League).[^cite_note-\:1-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] Ref-cam technology also appeared in selected matches during its opening round with a view to making the new technology a permanent feature of live TV games for the rest of the season.[^cite_note-27]

## Teams

Twenty teams competed in the league – the top seventeen teams from the previous season and three promoted from the [Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_EFL_Championship). The promoted teams were [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.), [Burnley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnley_F.C.), and [Sunderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland_A.F.C.), returning to the top flight after absences of two, one, and eight years, respectively. They replaced [Leicester City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester_City_F.C.), [Ipswich Town](352843.md), and [Southampton](81576.md), who were all relegated to the [Championship](79604105.md) after one year in the top flight. This marked the second consecutive season, and only the third time in Premier League history, in which all three promoted teams were relegated after just one season.[^cite_note-opta-relegation-14]
