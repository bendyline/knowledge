**Nepal–South Korea relations** are the [foreign relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations) between [Nepal](171166.md) and [South Korea](27019.md).

## History

Nepal–South Korea relations were officially established on 15 May 1974.[^cite_note-\:0-1] The same year, South Korea opened its embassy in [Kathmandu](17168.md), and Nepal opened its embassy in Seoul in 2007.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-2]

In 2016, former [Speaker of the National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_National_Assembly_of_South_Korea), [Chung Ui-hwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chung_Ui-hwa) visited Nepal, and [Chairman of the National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Nepal\)) of Nepal, [Ganesh Prasad Timilsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganesh_Prasad_Timilsina) visited South Korea in 2019.[^cite_note-3]

In 2004, it was reported that there were more than 4,600 Nepali migrant workers in South Korea.[^cite_note-\:1-4] The same year, it was also reported that more than every year around 10,000 South Koreans visit Nepal; remarkably, 29,680 tourists visited Nepal in 2019.[^cite_note-\:1-4][^cite_note-5]

South Korea provided US\$1 million in humanitarian aid to Nepal following the [April 2015 Nepal earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2015_Nepal_earthquake).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
