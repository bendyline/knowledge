**Al Batinah North Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة شمال الباطنة Muḥāfaẓat Šamāl al-Bāṭinah) is one of the eleven [governorates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorate) of [Oman](22316.md). It was created on 28 October 2011 when [Al Batinah Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Batinah_Region) was split into Al Batinah North Governorate and [Al Batinah South Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Batinah_South_Governorate).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The centre of the governorate is the wilayat of [Sohar](1921981.md).

## History

Al Batinah was originally part of [Al Batinah Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Batinah_Region), a coastal strip lying along the [Gulf of Oman](12896.md), bordered by the [Hajar Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajar_Mountains) to the west. As a significant region in pre‑modern Oman, it served as a strategic trading and maritime zone. The city of [Sohar](1921981.md), located in the governorate was the capital of Oman in ancient times.[^cite_note-5]

## Provinces

Al Batinah North Governorate consists of six [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Oman) ([wilayat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilayat)):

- [Sohar](1921981.md)

- [Shinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinas)

- [Liwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liwa_Province)

- [Saham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saham)

- [Al Khaboura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Khaboura)

- [Suwayq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwayq)
