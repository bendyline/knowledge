**Sarmada** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): سرمدا) is a town in the [Harem District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harem_District), [Idlib Governorate](7445043.md) of [Syria](7515849.md).[^cite_note-1] It is in the extreme northwest of Syria near the border with [Turkey](11125639.md).

A church was consecrated in Sarmada by [Patriarch Elias of Antioch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriarch_Elias_of_Antioch) in 722 CE.[^cite_note-GinkelBerg2005-2] It is also the place in which the [Battle of Sarmada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Ager_Sanguinis) took place between the [Principality of Antioch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Antioch) and the [Artukids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artukids) on June 28, 1119.

## Monuments

### Column of Sarmada

The town is distinguished by the [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome) tomb of Alexandras, dated to the second century CE.[^cite_note-Ball2000-3] The tomb is rectangular and supports two columns, composed of thirteen cylicrical stones, joined together at the tenth cylinder by a horizontal piece with a further [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_\(architecture\)) on top.[^cite_note-Saouaf1957-4]

### Monastery of Saint Daniel and Hisn ad-Dair

The **Monastery of Saint Daniel** (also known as **Breij** or **Braij** or **al-Breij**) is located 2 km west of the town, perched in a hillside location about 400 metres from the road.[^cite_note-Darke2010-5] The monastery is dated to the 6th century CE during the later monastic phase of the [Dead Cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Cities).[^cite_note-BeattiePepper2001-6]

A monastery called **Hisn ad-Dair** near Sarmada was given to Alan of Gael by [Baldwin II of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldwin_II_of_Jerusalem) in 1121 AD, when it was described as a fortified monastery.[^cite_note-Asbridge2000-7] There is also mention of a [castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crusader_castle) with three [watchtowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watchtower) in the area.[^cite_note-Kennedy2006-8]

### Roman temple

A further 4 km along the road towards [Baqirha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baqirha) is a [Roman temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_temple) dedicated to [Zeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeus). [Epigraphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epigraphic) evidence was found dating the structure to c. 169 CE. The temple features a massive gateway and [cella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cella) along with one surviving column of what once was a four-columned [portico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portico).[^cite_note-BeattiePepper2001-6]
