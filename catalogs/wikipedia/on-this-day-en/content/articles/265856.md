**Law enforcement in the People's Republic of China** is the responsibility of [public security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_security) agencies, including the [People's Police](62010887.md) and [People's Armed Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Armed_Police) in [mainland China](55079.md). [Hong Kong](13404.md) and [Macau](19068.md) have separate law enforcement agencies, different legal systems, and are classified as separate jurisdictions under the [one country, two systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_country,_two_systems) framework. However, the [Hong Kong Police Force](1276176.md) (HKPF) and [Public Security Police Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Security_Police_Force) in Macau often cooperate with the mainland MPS on cases involving cross border crime.

## Overview

The national security system is made up of the [Ministry of Public Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Public_Security_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) (MPS) and the [Ministry of State Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_State_Security_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) (MSS), the [People's Armed Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Armed_Police) (PAP), the [People's Liberation Army](66890.md) (PLA), and the state judicial, procuratorial, and penal systems. The [Central Political and Legal Affairs Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Political_and_Legal_Affairs_Commission) vets all law enforcement officers and legal officials for political reliability.[^cite_note-1]

The [Ministry of Public Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Public_Security_\(China\)) (MPS) oversees all domestic police activity in China. The ministry is responsible for police operations and detention centers and has dedicated departments for internal political, economic, and communications security. The [Ministry of State Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_State_Security_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) (MSS) was established in 1983 to ensure "the security of the state through effective measures against enemy agents, spies, and counterrevolutionary activities designed to sabotage or overthrow China's socialist system." The [Ministry of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) maintains oversees the operation of prisons.

### Judicial system

This paragraph is an excerpt from [Judicial system of China](15169660.md).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Judicial_system_of_China\&action=edit)]

The [judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary) of the [People's Republic of China](5405.md) (PRC) is organized under the principle of [unified state power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_state_power) of the [National People's Congress](38878740.md) (NPC) and is represented by its two primary judicial organs, the [Supreme People's Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_People's_Court) and the [Supreme People's Procuratorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_People's_Procuratorate). These organs are [constitutionally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_China) inferior and accountable to the NPC as the [supreme state organ of power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_state_organ_of_power), and implement its political will.

## Key organizations

### Ministry of Public Security

This paragraph is an excerpt from [Ministry of Public Security (China)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Public_Security_\(China\)).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ministry_of_Public_Security_\(China\)\&action=edit)]

The [Ministry of Public Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Public_Security_\(China\)) (MPS; [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): [公安部](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%85%AC%E5%AE%89%E9%83%A8); [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Gōng'ānbù*)[^cite_note-2] is the primary law enforcement agency of the [People's Republic of China](5405.md) (PRC). A [constituent department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituent_departments_of_the_State_Council) of the [State Council](44498.md), the ministry oversees more than 1.9 million of the country's law enforcement officers and as such the vast majority of the [People's Police](62010887.md). While the MPS is a nationwide police force, conducting [counterintelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterintelligence) and maintaining the [political security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_security) of the [Chinese Communist Party](7175.md) (CCP) are also core functions.

The People's Armed Police Force[^cite_note-3], abbreviated PAP,[^cite_note-4] is a Chinese [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie) organization[^cite_note-People's_Armed_Police_Sun2009-5]<sup>: 121 </sup> primarily responsible for [internal security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_security), [riot control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riot_control), [counter-terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-terrorism), [disaster response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disaster_response), [law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement) and [maritime rights protection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiralty_law)[^cite_note-People's_Armed_Police_ChinaDaily2009-6] as well as providing support to the [People's Liberation Army](66890.md) (PLA) during wartime.[^cite_note-People's_Armed_Police_Blasko2006-7]<sup>: 87 </sup>

### Chengguan

This paragraph is an excerpt from [Chengguan (agency)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengguan_\(agency\)).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Chengguan_\(agency\)\&action=edit)]

[*Chengguan*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengguan_\(agency\)) (城管; 'Urban management', short for 城市管理执法; ''Urban Management and Law Enforcement'') is an administrative practice of city-level local governments in the [People's Republic of China](5405.md) to oversee and manage city appearance and public environments according to the region's bylaws. Chengguan are non-sworn civil agencies and are not entitled police powers.[^cite_note-Chengguan_(agency)_\:0-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]
