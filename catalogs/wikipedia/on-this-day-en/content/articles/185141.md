**Bay** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Baay*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): باي,) is an administrative region (*[gobol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Somalia)*) in southern Somalia within the [Southwest state](483484.md).[^cite_note-Cwfsom-5]

## Overview

It is bordered by the Somali regions of [Bakool](185137.md), [Hiran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiran,_Somalia), [Lower Shebelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Shebelle) (Shabeellaha Hoose), [Middle Juba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Juba) (Jubbada Dhexe), and [Gedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gedo).

Baidoa used to be the capital of the old Upper [Juba](38364812.md) region, which today also includes Gedo, Bakool, and most parts of the Middle Juba region. The present regions were created in the 1970s by the then-ruling military regime. The capital of Bay is [Baidoa](1648752.md).

During the [Ethiopian invasion of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Somalia_\(2006%E2%80%932009\)) in late December 2006, Bay region witnessed a series of fierce, concurrent battles lasting nine days between the Ethiopian military and the [Islamic Courts Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Courts_Union).[^cite_note-\:30-6]

## Demographics

The region is mainly inhabited by the [Rahanweyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahanweyn) people. According to the Population Estimation Survey (2014) (282), the division of population in the Bay region was as follows: 93,046 urban inhabitants, 463,330 rural settlers, 195,986 nomadic and 39,820 internally displaced persons (IDPs), making a total of 792,182.[^cite_note-ecoi-7]
