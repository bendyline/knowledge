The **2025 FIFA U-20 World Cup** was the 24th edition of the [FIFA U-20 World Cup](1168189.md), the biennial international men's youth [football](10568.md) championship contested by the under-20 national teams of the member associations of [FIFA](11049.md), since its inception in [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_FIFA_World_Youth_Championship) as the FIFA World Youth Championship. It was held in [Chile](5489.md) from 27 September to 19 October 2025.[^cite_note-host_country-1][^cite_note-2]

Reigning champions [Uruguay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguay_national_under-20_football_team) could not defend their title as they finished fifth in the [2025 South American U-20 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_South_American_U-20_Championship). In doing so, they became the eighth consecutive title holders to fail to qualify for the subsequent tournament.

[Morocco](15604185.md) defeated [Argentina](12504513.md) to win their first FIFA tournament title. This was the fourth consecutive U-20 World Cup to produce a new winning team, dating back to [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup).

## Host selection

Chile were announced as the 2025 U-20 World Cup hosts following the [FIFA Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Council) meeting on 17 December 2023 in [Jeddah](277718.md), Saudi Arabia.[^cite_note-host_country-1] It is believed the decision was made as a compensation for Chile losing hosting rights for the [2030 FIFA World Cup](39367087.md) to Morocco, Portugal and Spain, as Chile was the only nation in the [four-nation bid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguay%E2%80%93Argentina%E2%80%93Chile%E2%80%93Paraguay_2030_FIFA_World_Cup_bid) of Uruguay, Argentina and Paraguay not included in the eventual outcome for the opening matches.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

This was the second time that Chile hosted the tournament, after doing so in [1987](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_FIFA_World_Youth_Championship).[^cite_note-5] Only [Australia](4689264.md) ([1981](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981_FIFA_World_Youth_Championship), [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_FIFA_World_Youth_Championship)) and [Argentina](18951905.md) ([2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_FIFA_World_Youth_Championship), [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup)) had also hosted the tournament multiple times.

## Venues

On 18 December 2023, [Pablo Milad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_Milad), president of the [Football Federation of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Federation_of_Chile), revealed a preliminary list with 12 stadia in 10 cities. Milad also announced that between four and six stadia would be chosen by FIFA for the final tournament.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

On 26 May 2025, four [Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Chile) cities of [Santiago](51572.md), [Rancagua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rancagua), [Valparaíso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valpara%C3%ADso) and [Talca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talca) were announced chosen to host the competition.[^cite_note-8]

A total of 24 teams qualified for the final tournament. In addition to [Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chile_national_under-20_football_team), who qualified automatically as the hosts, 23 other teams qualified through six separate continental competitions. The slot allocation remained unchanged from the previous editions and was confirmed by the FIFA Council on the same day the host country was announced.[^cite_note-host_country-1]

- [AFC](715249.md) (Asia): 4

- [CAF](715265.md) (Africa): 4

- [CONCACAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF) (North America, Central America and the Caribbean): 4

- [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL) (South America): 5 (including the hosts [Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chile_national_under-20_football_team))

- [OFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceania_Football_Confederation) (Oceania): 2

- [UEFA](32332.md) (Europe): 5

[New Caledonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Caledonia_national_under-20_football_team) made their debut in the tournament.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] Of the returnees, [Morocco](15604185.md) made a return after last qualifying in [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_FIFA_World_Youth_Championship). [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_national_under-20_football_team), [Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chile_national_under-20_football_team), [Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuba_national_under-20_football_team), [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt_national_under-20_football_team), [Paraguay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraguay_national_under-20_football_team) and [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_national_under-20_football_team) made a return after last qualifying in [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup). [Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia_national_under-20_football_team), [Mexico](17736508.md), [Panama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panama_national_under-20_football_team), [Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway_national_under-20_football_team), [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa_national_under-20_soccer_team) and [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine_national_under-20_football_team) made a return from [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup).

Reigning champions [Uruguay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguay_national_under-20_football_team) failed to qualify for the first time since 2005, while [Honduras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honduras_national_under-20_football_team) and [Senegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senegal_national_under-20_football_team) failed to qualify after appearing in the previous four consecutive editions. [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_national_under-20_football_team) failed to qualify after finishing third in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup). [Dominican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Republic_national_under-20_football_team), [Ecuador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuador_national_under-20_football_team), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-20_football_team), [Fiji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiji_national_under-20_football_team), [the Gambia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambia_national_under-20_football_team), [Guatemala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guatemala_national_under-20_football_team), [Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq_national_under-20_football_team), [Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia_national_under-20_football_team), [Tunisia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisia_national_under-20_football_team), and [Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan_national_under-20_football_team) failed to qualify after appearing in 2023.

## Draw

The draw took place at [Chilevisión](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilevisi%C3%B3n)'s Machasa studios in [Santiago](51572.md) on 29 May 2025.[^cite_note-13]

The 24 teams were organised into four pots of six teams based on a performance ranking system, in order to be subsequently drawn into six groups of four teams. The hosts Chile were automatically seeded to Pot 1 and assigned into the first position of Group A, while the remaining teams were seeded into pots based on their results in the last five FIFA U-20 World Cups (with more recent tournaments weighted more heavily), and with five bonus points added to each of the six continental champions from the qualifying tournaments, as follows:[^cite_note-14]

## Group stage

The top two teams of each group and the four best third-placed teams advanced to the round of 16.

All times are local, [Chile Summer Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Chile#Summer_time_\(CLST/EASST\)) ([UTC–3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC%E2%88%9203\:00)).[^cite_note-schedule-18]

### Tiebreakers

The rankings of teams in each group were determined as follows (regulations Article 13.1):[^cite_note-regulations-19]

1. points obtained among games between tied teams;

2. goal difference among games between tied teams;

3. goals scored among games between tied teams;

4. goal difference in all group matches;

5. goals scored in all group matches;

6. fair play points:&#x20;

   - first yellow card: –1 point;

   - indirect red card (second yellow card): –3 points;

   - direct red card: –4 points;

   - yellow card and direct red card: –5 points;

7. drawing of lots.

## Knockout stage

In the knockout stage, if a match was level at the end of 90 minutes of normal playing time, [extra time](633242.md#Association_football) was played (two periods of 15 minutes each). If the score was still tied after extra time, the match was decided by a [penalty shoot-out](511302.md).[^cite_note-regulations-19]

## Marketing

### Emblem

The Emblem was revealed on 5 February 2025. It drew inspiration from Chile's unique geography and heritage. The design prominently featured elements such as the Andes Mountains, the Pacific Ocean, and the vibrant colors of Chilean culture, reflecting the nation's deep love for football. The emblem was expected to resonate with both local fans and the global football community.[^cite_note-27]

### Theme song

One day before the tournament began, FIFA revealed the official song titled "**El Alma en la Cancha (Olé Olé Olé)**" (Spanish for "The Soul on the Pitch") sung by [Shirel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirel_\(singer\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) and produced by [Taffy Dönicke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taffy_D%C3%B6nicke?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-28]

### Mascot

The mascot was revealed on 22 May 2025. His name was Vito, a [viscacha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viscacha) characteristic of the region. Considered sociable, charming, and energetic, this animal resembled a rabbit or chinchilla.[^cite_note-29]
