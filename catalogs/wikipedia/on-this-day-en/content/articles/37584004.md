**People's Solidarity for Participatory Democracy** (**PSPD**; [Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 참여연대) is a South Korean non-governmental organization that was established on 10 September 1994 with around 200 members.[^cite_note-1]

In 2004, PSPD obtained a special [consultative status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consultative_status) with the [UN ECOSOC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consultative_Status) and started advocating before various UN bodies including the [UN human rights council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Rights_Council).

As of July 2012, around 13,000 individuals are members of PSPD. It has ten departments and three auxiliaries.
