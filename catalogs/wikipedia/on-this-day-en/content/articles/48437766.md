# List of ethnic armed organisations in Myanmar



Cadets of the [Kachin Independence Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kachin_Independence_Army) (KIA) preparing for military drills at the group's headquarters in [Laiza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laiza), [Kachin State](493369.md).

The following is a list of non-state armed groups involved in the [Myanmar conflict](13299430.md), officially called **ethnic armed organisations** (**EAOs**) by the government of [Myanmar](19457.md).[^cite_note-1]

## Terminology

The term "ethnic armed organisation" ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): တိုင်းရင်းသား လက်နက်ကိုင် အဖွဲ့အစည်း) emerged in Myanmar during the [Nationwide Ceasefire Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationwide_Ceasefire_Agreement) negotiations from 2013 to 2015.[^cite_note-\:0-2] Various other terms, including "ethnic organisation", "ethnic resistance force", and "ethnic rebel group" have also been used to describe ethnic armed organisations.&#x20;

These organisations typically:

- claim to represent an [ethnic identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnicity)

  [^cite_note-\:0-2]

- aim to garner mutual recognition from other ethnic armed organisations through alliances and coalitions

  [^cite_note-\:0-2]

- have an armed wing, and often a separate political wing

  [^cite_note-\:0-2]
