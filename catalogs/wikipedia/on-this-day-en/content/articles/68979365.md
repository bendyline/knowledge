The **Progress Party[^cite_note-3]** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): حزب تقدم, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Ḥizb Taqadum*), also known as the **Al-Takadum Movement** or by its Arabic short form **Takadum** (*Progress*), is an [Iraqi](7515928.md) political party formed to contest the [2021 general election.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Iraqi_parliamentary_election)[^cite_note-4]

The political groups Anbar is Our Identity and Qimam Alliance are also led by Al-Halbousi, serving as political proxies to maintain ties with local leaders, and thus are included in the seat count and considered to be extensions of the party.

## History

The party was founded in 2019 by [Mohamed Al Halbousi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_al-Halbousi), the incumbent [Speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Speakers_of_the_Council_of_Representatives_of_Iraq) of the [Council of Representatives](1445450.md) of [Iraq](7515928.md), Ali Farhan, the governor of [Anbar](467036.md), and the politician Yahia Al Mohamadi.[^cite_note-5]

## Election results

In the [2021 Iraqi parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Iraqi_parliamentary_election), the party won 7.2% of the vote, becoming the second-largest individual party behind the [Sadrists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadrist_Movement).[^cite_note-6] The party primarily receives support from Sunni Arabs, with its performance highest in [Anbar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anbar_Governorate) and [Nineveh](200568.md) governorates, including the city of [Mosul](200475.md).
