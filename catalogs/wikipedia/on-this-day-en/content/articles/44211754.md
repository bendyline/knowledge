# Technology company

A **technology company**, or **tech company**, is a company that focuses primarily on the [manufacturing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacturing), support, [research and development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_and_development) of—most commonly [computing](5213.md), [telecommunication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunication) and [consumer electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumer_electronics)–based—[technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology)-intensive products and services, which include businesses relating to digital electronics, software, optics, new energy, and Internet-related services such as cloud storage and [e-commerce](9611.md) services. *[Big Tech](57174306.md)* refers to the six largest companies,[^cite_note-1] both in the United States and [globally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_public_corporations_by_market_capitalization), symbolized by the [metonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metonym) '[Silicon Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Valley)', where four of them are based.[^cite_note-2]

## Details

According to *[Fortune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortune_Magazine)*, as of 2020, the ten largest technology companies by revenue are: [Apple Inc.](856.md), [Samsung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung), [Foxconn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hon_Hai_Precision_Industry), [Alphabet Inc.](47489893.md), [Microsoft](19001.md), [Huawei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei), [Dell Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dell_Technologies), [Hitachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi), [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM), and [Sony](26989.md).[^cite_note-Fortune_Tech-3] [Amazon](90451.md) has higher revenue than Apple, but is classified by *Fortune* in the retail sector.[^cite_note-4] The most profitable listed in 2020 are Apple Inc., Microsoft, Alphabet Inc., [Intel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intel), [Meta Platforms](62420226.md), Samsung, and [Tencent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tencent).[^cite_note-Fortune_Tech-3]

Apple Inc., Alphabet Inc. (owner of [Google](1092923.md)), Meta Platforms (owner of [Facebook](7529378.md)), Microsoft, and Amazon.com, Inc. are often referred to as the [Big Five](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Five_\(technology_companies\)) multinational technology companies based in the United States. These five technology companies dominate major functions, [e-commerce](9611.md) channels, and information of the entire [Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet) ecosystem. As of 2017, the Big Five had a combined valuation of over \$3.3 trillion and make up more than 40 percent of the value of the [Nasdaq-100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasdaq-100) index.[^cite_note-5]

Many large tech companies have a reputation for innovation, spending large sums of money annually on [research and development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_and_development). According to [PwC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PwC)'s 2017 Global Innovation 1000 ranking, tech companies made up nine of the 20 most innovative companies in the world, with the top R\&D spender (as measured by expenditure) being Amazon, followed by Alphabet Inc., and then Intel.[^cite_note-6]

As a result of numerous influential tech companies and tech [startups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Startup_company) opening offices in proximity to one another, a number of [technology districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_technology_centers) have developed in various areas across the globe.[^cite_note-7] These include: [Silicon Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Valley) in the [San Francisco Bay Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Bay_Area), [Silicon Wadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Wadi) in Israel, [Silicon Docks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Docks) in [Dublin](8504.md), [Silicon Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Hills) in [Austin](1998.md), [Tech City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tech_City) in [London](17867.md); [Digital Media City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Media_City) in [Seoul](19159283.md), [Zhongguancun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongguancun) in [Beijing](18603746.md), [Cyberjaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyberjaya) in Malaysia and [Cyberabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyberabad) in [Hyderabad](37534.md), India.

As of 2026, Europe has six of the world's one hundred most valuable tech companies, compared with 56 in the United States and 16 in China.[^cite_note-8]
