# Ghana at the FIFA World Cup

For the women's team, see [Ghana at the FIFA Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghana_at_the_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup).



Ghana at the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).

[Ghana](1029620.md) have appeared in the finals of the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) on five occasions, in [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup), [2010](168079.md) (where they reached the quarter-finals), [2014](656933.md), [2022](17742072.md) and [2026](36581929.md).

## Record at the FIFA World Cup

### Overall record

Ghana have qualified for five FIFA World Cup tournaments; 2006, 2010, 2014, 2022 and 2026.

In [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup), Ghana were the only African side to advance to the [second round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup_knockout_stage) of the tournament, and were the sixth nation in a row from Africa to progress beyond the group stages of the World Cup.[^cite_note-Ghana_2-1_USA-1] Ghana had the youngest team in the competition with an average age of 23 years and 352 days,[^cite_note-Ghana_2-1_USA-1] and were praised for their improving performance.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] FIFA ranked Ghana 13th out of the 32 countries who competed in the tournament.[^cite_note-4]

In the [2010 World Cup](168079.md), Ghana progressed beyond the group stage and reached the quarter-finals, where they were eliminated by [Uruguay](679782.md). Ghana was defeated by Uruguay on penalties after [Luis Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Su%C3%A1rez_\(Uruguayan_footballer\)) controversially handballed on the [goal line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_pitch#Pitch_boundary) deep into extra time, denying Ghana an almost certain winning goal.[^cite_note-5] Had Ghana won their quarter-final, they would have become the first African nation to progress to the semi-finals of the world cup. Of the 32 countries that participated in the [2010 FIFA World Cup](168079.md), FIFA ranked Ghana 7th.[^cite_note-6]

In the [2014](656933.md) and [2022 World Cup](17742072.md), Ghana was eliminated in the group stage. In [2026](36581929.md), they were eliminated in the round of 32.

#### United States vs Ghana

[United States](582488.md) vs [Ghana](1029620.md) was played on 26 June 2010 at the [Royal Bafokeng Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Bafokeng_Stadium) in [Rustenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rustenburg). The match was watched by 19 million Americans, making it the most watched association football match in American television history.[^cite_note-13] The match was won by Ghana in extra time, after [Asamoah Gyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asamoah_Gyan) broke a 1–1 deadlock. [Kevin-Prince Boateng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin-Prince_Boateng) scored the opening goal of the match for Ghana in the 5th minute. The goal followed an error by [Ricardo Clark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Clark), who lost the ball to Ghana in midfield. Boateng took the ball to the edge of the penalty area, beating US goalkeeper [Tim Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Howard) with a low left foot shot. [Landon Donovan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landon_Donovan) equalised with a penalty kick in the 62nd minute, awarded after [Jonathan Mensah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Mensah) fouled [Clint Dempsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clint_Dempsey). The US had chances to win the game thereafter, but they were unable to get past Ghana's goalkeeper [Richard Kingson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Kingson). The match thus went to extra time. In the third minute, Gyan latched onto a high long ball, chesting it down and holding off two defenders before scoring the winner.[^cite_note-14] After the match, Ghana's coach [Milovan Rajevac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milovan_Rajevac) hailed his side's achievement in becoming one of the "best eight teams in the world", but regretted the number of players that would miss the quarter-final against [Uruguay](679782.md) because of injury or suspension.[^cite_note-15] The president of the [Soccer Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Soccer_Federation), [Sunil Gulati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunil_Gulati), lamented the team's failure to make the quarter-finals and thereby further raise the profile of the [sport in the US](579105.md).[^cite_note-16]

#### Uruguay vs Ghana



Ghana vs. Uruguay in the 2010 FIFA World Cup [quarter-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_FIFA_World_Cup_knockout_stage#Uruguay_vs_Ghana) match at [Soccer City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soccer_City), [Johannesburg](58893.md) on 2 July 2010.

[Uruguay](679782.md) and [Ghana](1029620.md) met on 2 July 2010 at [Soccer City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soccer_City), [Johannesburg](58893.md) for a place in the semi-final against the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_national_football_team). It was the first time that the teams had ever played each other in a senior competitive football match. After a dramatic 120 minutes of play (including extra time) that finished 1–1, Uruguay won in a penalty shoot-out 4–2.[^cite_note-Lynch-18] Uruguay dominated the early periods of the match, but suffered an injury to captain [Diego Lugano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Lugano) in the first half. Just before half-time, Ghana took the lead when [Sulley Muntari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulley_Muntari) was allowed time on the ball by Uruguay, and took advantage by scoring with a shot from 40 yards. After half-time, [Diego Forlán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Forl%C3%A1n) pulled Uruguay level with a free kick from the left side of the field that went over the head of Ghana's goalkeeper [Richard Kingson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Kingson). While both teams had chances to win, the match proceeded to extra time as the scores remained level. Late in extra time, Ghana sent a [free kick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_free_kick) into the [box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_area); [Luis Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Su%C3%A1rez_\(Uruguayan_footballer\)) blocked [Stephen Appiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Appiah)'s shot on the [goal line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_pitch#Pitch_boundary).[^cite_note-FIFA_Uru-Gha-19] On the rebound, [Dominic Adiyiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_Adiyiah)'s header was heading into the goal, but Suárez blatantly blocked the shot with his hands[^cite_note-Echo_2-3-20] to save what would have been the extra-time winner[^cite_note-Hand_of_God-21] and he was [red carded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_card#Red_card). [Asamoah Gyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asamoah_Gyan) missed the ensuing [penalty kick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_kick_\(association_football\)) off the crossbar[^cite_note-FIFA_Uru-Gha-19] and Suárez celebrated the miss.[^cite_note-Marcus-22][^cite_note-reuters-23] In the shoot-out, Gyan converted his penalty,[^cite_note-Hand_of_God-21] as did everybody else until the 4th round of penalty kicks when Adiyiah's penalty was saved by Uruguayan goalkeeper [Fernando Muslera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Muslera). Uruguay's [Maxi Pereira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxi_Pereira) then hit his penalty kick over the bar. Muslera saved Captain [John Mensah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Mensah)'s, and Ghana's fifth, penalty.[^cite_note-Lynch-18] [Sebastián Abreu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebasti%C3%A1n_Abreu) converted Uruguay's fifth spot kick by lightly chipping it Panenka-style to win the match.[^cite_note-24] After the game, Suárez said, "I made the save of the tournament,"[^cite_note-Hand_of_God-21] and, referring to the infamous [handball goal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hand_of_God_goal) scored by [Diego Maradona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Maradona) in the 1986 World Cup, claimed that "The 'Hand of God' now belongs to me." Suárez claimed he had no alternative and was acting out of instinct.[^cite_note-Doward-25] Forlán agreed that Suárez saved the game, "Suárez this time, instead of scoring goals, he saved one, I think he saved the game.[^cite_note-Hand_of_God-21] Ghana coach [Milovan Rajevac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milovan_Rajevac) said the play was an "injustice"[^cite_note-Marcus-22] and Suárez was labeled a "villain"[^cite_note-Doward-25][^cite_note-Suarez_soccernet_stats-26] and a "cheat".[^cite_note-Echo_2-3-20][^cite_note-Jim_White-27] But Uruguay coach, [Óscar Tabárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%93scar_Tab%C3%A1rez), said these labels were too harsh, "Well, there was a handball in the penalty area, there was a red card and Suárez was thrown out. Saying that Ghana were cheated out of the game is too harsh. We have to go by the rules. It might have been a mistake by my player but I do not like that word ‘cheating’."[^cite_note-28] Ghana was the last African team left in the tournament and if they had won, they would have been the first team from Africa to ever make the semi-finals.[^cite_note-Chadband-29] But others viewed him as a hero[^cite_note-Echo_2-3-20][^cite_note-30] who sacrificed himself in the semi-final for the unlikely chance that his team could win.[^cite_note-Jim_White-27][^cite_note-Lyttleton-31] A distraught Gyan conceded, "I would say Suárez is a hero now in his own country, because the ball was going in and he held it with his hand. He is a hero now."[^cite_note-Chadband-29]
