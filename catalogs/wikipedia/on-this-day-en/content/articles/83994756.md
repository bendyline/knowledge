In 2026, many [wildfires](56106.md) occurred across [Croatia](5573.md) during the [2026 European heatwaves](83266395.md). As of August 15, over 5,143 ha (51.43 km<sup>2</sup>) have been burnt, according to the [European Forest Fire Information System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Forest_Fire_Information_System), with 42 fires larger than 30 ha being recorded.[^cite_note-effis-1] According to the [Croatian Firefighting Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatian_Firefighting_Association?action=edit\&redlink=1), 9,646 ha (96.46 km<sup>2</sup>) have been burnt as of August 12.[^cite_note-2]

## June

From June 22 to June 25, three wildfires broke out on the island of [Krk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krk). The first was recorded on June 22 near [Dobrinj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dobrinj), between the villages of [Klimno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klimno) and [Šilo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%A0ilo). Two days later, on June 24, also in the Dobrinj municipality, a new fire broke out near [Sužan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Su%C5%BEan). A third fire broke out on June 25 in the [Punat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punat) municipality. All three fires were quickly contained and extinguished, but a total of around 3,500 square meters was burned.[^cite_note-3]

## July

On July 5, a fire broke out near [Vinišće](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vini%C5%A1%C4%87e) which spread towards the village, fueled by strong [bora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bora_\(wind\)) winds. Firefighters from the surrounding area quickly intervened and put the fire under control.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

On July 11, a large fire broke out near the village of [Blato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blato,_Kor%C4%8Dula) on the island of [Korčula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kor%C4%8Dula). More than 100 firefighters from other [counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Croatia) were sent to help bring it under control. Overnight, rain fell which helped the firefighters, and the fire was brought under control by the evening of July 12.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## August

On August 11, a fire broke out in [Vinišće](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vini%C5%A1%C4%87e), leaving the entire village without electricity.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] A total of 27 firefighters with 10 fire trucks took part in the response, and the fire was extinguished early on August 12.[^cite_note-11]

On August 12, a fire broke out along the [A3 highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A3_\(Croatia\)) in the area of [Hrvatski Leskovac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hrvatski_Leskovac) and spread to both sides of the road. A total of 13.5 hectares burned, and the fire was extinguished within three hours.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

During the night of August 13, a large fire broke out on the island of [Brač](226332.md). A total of 250 hectares burned, and 106 firefighters with 31 vehicles took part in extinguishing it, assisted by four [Canadair CL-415](1898178.md) aircraft. The fire was extinguished in the afternoon after 13 hours of firefighting.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]



Satellite image of the fire that started in [Lokva Rogoznica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokva_Rogoznica) and spread to [Omiš](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omi%C5%A1) on August 14

On August 13, at 8\:15 PM, the County Firefighting Operations Center of [Split-Dalmatia County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split-Dalmatia_County) received a report of a fire in the area of [Lokva Rogoznica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokva_Rogoznica). The fire quickly spread to [Omiš](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omi%C5%A1), and a total of around 1,000 hectares of grass, low vegetation, and [pine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine) forest burned. Firefighters from 6 other counties took part in the response, totaling 253 firefighters with 83 fire vehicles, assisted by four Canadair CL-415 aircraft and four [Air Tractors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Tractor_AT-802). In total, more than 300 firefighters were deployed. One person was killed in the fire, 43 people were injured along with three firefighters, and around 2,500 people were evacuated.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-DVOC16Aug-19] The fire destroyed 58 residential buildings, two garages, 36 vehicles, 25 boats, two trailers, one motorcycle, and two cargo vehicles.[^cite_note-20]

Early morning on August 14, a fire broke out in the area of [Kuna Pelješka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuna_Pelje%C5%A1ka) on the [Pelješac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pelje%C5%A1ac) peninsula. 250 firefighters with 73 fire vehicles from [Dubrovnik-Neretva County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubrovnik-Neretva_County), along with an extraordinary deployment of firefighters from two additional counties, took part in extinguishing the fire, assisted by four Canadair CL-415 aircraft, one Air Tractor, and one [Mil Mi-17](1493374.md) helicopter. Around 500 hectares burned, and the fire was stopped early in the morning on August 16.[^cite_note-DVOC16Aug-19][^cite_note-21]

On August 14 at 9\:40 AM, the County Firefighting Operations Center of [Šibenik-Knin County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%A0ibenik-Knin_County) detected a wildfire in the area of the settlement of [Polača](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pola%C4%8Da,_%C5%A0ibenik-Knin_County), which had spread into Croatia from [Bosnia and Herzegovina](3463.md). 37 firefighters with 19 fire vehicles were deployed to fight the fire, along with 26 members of the [Croatian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatian_Army) Firefighting Forces, assisted by four Canadair CL-415 firefighting aircraft.[^cite_note-DVOC16Aug-19][^cite_note-22]

In the afternoon on August 14, a fire broke out in the area of [Ninski Stanovi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninski_Stanovi) and [Žerava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%BDerava). 79 firefighters with 29 fire vehicles took part in extinguishing it, assisted by three Canadair CL-415 aircraft and one Air Tractor. Firefighting efforts were hampered by strong bora winds, but the fire was successfully brought under control by the evening.[^cite_note-DVOC16Aug-19][^cite_note-23]

On the evening of August 14, a fire broke out on [Dugi Otok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dugi_Otok). 50 firefighters with 19 fire vehicles brought the fire under control on the afternoon of August 15, but it soon flared up again. Two Air Tractors and one Canadair CL-415 were also deployed. The fire was again brought under control in the evening. A total of 300 hectares of land burned.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-DVOC17Aug-25]

On August 16, a fire broke out in the area of the village of [Jovići](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jovi%C4%87i?action=edit\&redlink=1). 56 firefighters with 17 fire vehicles took part in extinguishing it, assisted by three Canadair CL-415 firefighting aircraft and two Air Tractor AT-802 aircraft. The fire was brought under control at 7\:30 PM.[^cite_note-DVOC17Aug-25][^cite_note-26]
