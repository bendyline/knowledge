**Thialf** (Dutch pronunciation: [\[ˈtijɑlf\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Dutch)) is an ice arena in [Heerenveen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heerenveen), [Netherlands](21148.md). Thialf is used for [long track speed skating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_track_speed_skating), [short track speed skating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_track_speed_skating), [ice hockey](14790.md), [figure skating](11152.md), [ice speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_speedway),[^cite_note-2] and non-sporting events. The outdoor rink was opened in 1967, and the indoor stadium was opened in 1986. Several world records have been set in the indoor stadium.

Annually, Thialf hosts two [Speed Skating World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Speed_Skating_World_Cup) events. [Jan de Jong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_de_Jong) was the ice rink master at Thialf for many years. Thialf will host the speed skating competition during the [2030 Winter Olympics](57658858.md).[^cite_note-3]

## History



Ice skaters and their coaches during the World Cup in the season 2007–2008



Main entrance after renovation

Thialf is named after [Thialfi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thialfi), a character in [Norse mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norse_mythology), who was [Thor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thor)'s servant and had to race a giant.[^cite_note-thialfi-4]

Construction on the artificial outdoor ice rink was started in 1966, and it was opened on 14 October 1967 by [Princess Christina of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Christina_of_the_Netherlands). It was the third 400m artificial ice rink in the Netherlands, after the [Jaap Eden baan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaap_Eden_baan) in [Amsterdam](844.md) and the IJsselstadion in [Deventer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deventer). Several national and international tournaments have been held in Thialf, but only one world record has been set on the outdoor rink, by [Andrea Schöne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Sch%C3%B6ne) on the [5000 m](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_record_progression_5000_m_speed_skating_women) in 1983.

The roofed stadium, which seats 12,500 people, opened on 17 November 1986, about a year after [Sportforum Hohenschönhausen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportforum_Hohensch%C3%B6nhausen) in [Berlin](3354.md), which was the first 400m indoor speed skating oval in the world. Thanks to the indoor conditions, allowing climate control, almost all world speed skating records were broken at Thialf in the first season.[^cite_note-World_records_at_Thialf-5] Since 1988 it has been overtaken as the "fastest ice in the world" by the high-altitude indoor rinks in [Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_Oval) and [Salt Lake City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Olympic_Oval), which have the additional benefit of low air pressure.

Every year there are main skating events like the Dutch, European and World championships, and one or two [Speed Skating World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Speed_Skating_World_Cup) events in Thialf.

The 2500-seat ice hockey arena adjacent to the speed skating oval is the home arena of the [Heerenveen Flyers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heerenveen_Flyers), one of the Netherlands' most successful ice hockey clubs. It is also the main arena used in the Netherlands for international ice hockey tournaments, hosting the [IIHF World U18 Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_IIHF_World_U18_Championships) (Division II, Group A) in late March 2012.

The stadium was renovated in 2016.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

### 2030 Winter Olympics

In April 2025, the organizing committee for the [2030 Winter Olympics](57658858.md) in the [French Alps](961317.md) started to consider having long track speed skating in Thialf.[^cite_note-NOS_April2025-8][^cite_note-OF-9][^cite_note-10] The organizing committee determined that it was not economical to develop a new or temporary speed skating venue in France for the games and instead looked for an existing venue elsewhere. In June 2026, Thialf was confirmed as the speed skating venue, making the 2026 games the first Winter Olympics (and the fourth overall, after [1920](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_at_the_1920_Summer_Olympics), [1956](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equestrian_at_the_1956_Summer_Olympics), and [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equestrian_at_the_2008_Summer_Olympics)) to have events that would be hosted outside of the host country. It will be the first Olympic events held in The Netherlands in 102 years, following the [1928 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1928_Summer_Olympics) in [Amsterdam](844.md).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Other events

The arena has hosted concerts by many famous artists, including [Whitney Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Houston), [Tina Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_Turner), [André Rieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Rieu), [TOTO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toto_\(band\)), [Trance Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trance_Energy) and [Prince](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_\(musician\)), among others.

Also, the [Miss Universe](150340.md) competition was held in Thialf several times.
