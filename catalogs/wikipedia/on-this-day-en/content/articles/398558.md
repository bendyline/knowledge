**Villajoyosa**,[^cite_note-3] in [Spanish](26825.md), or ***la Vila Joiosa***,[^cite_note-4] in [Valencian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencian_language) (officially ***la Vila Joiosa*** **/** ***Villajoyosa***),[^cite_note-5] both meaning "The Joyful Town", is a coastal town and municipality in the south of the [Valencian Community](20514168.md), [Spain](26667.md), by the [Mediterranean Sea](19006.md). It is the historic and administrative capital of [Marina Baixa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Baixa) region, and is located in the [province of Alicante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Alicante) (*Alacant*). The town is known locally simply as ***la Vila***.[^cite_note-6]

## Geography

It is the historic and administrative capital of the *[comarca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comarcas_of_Spain)* of [Marina Baixa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Baixa) and is located 32 km from the city of [Alicante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicante), in the coastal area known as [Costa Blanca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Blanca) (*White Coast*). It has over three kilometers of beaches,[^cite_note-7] including the *Platja Centre* (Central Beach), close to the town center. The river of la Vila (incorrectly also known as Amadòrio) runs through the town.

## Transport

Access to la Vila Joiosa / Villajoyosa can be either through the expressway [N-332](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-332_road_\(Spain\)), the motorway [AP-7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autopista_AP-7) or also through the [Alicante Metropolitan TRAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicante_Tram) Line 1.

## Attractions

The area is famous for its chocolate[^cite_note-8] industry and tourism. One of the most popular tourist attractions is its [Gothic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_architecture) Catholic Church of the Assumption, with a [Baroque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baroque) altar piece. Another popular destination, the Royal Palm Casino[^cite_note-9] (formerly Casino Costa Blanca), is reopened after major renovation. The town's festival of *[Moros i Cristians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moros_y_Cristianos)* (Moors and Christians),[^cite_note-10] celebrated at the end of July, was declared an International Tourist Interest Festival. Currently, this festivity commemorates a boat landing from the Berber pirates that was repelled by the population of the town.[^cite_note-11]
