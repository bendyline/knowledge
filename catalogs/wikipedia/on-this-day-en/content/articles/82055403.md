# Accra–Kumasi Expressway

&#x20; **Accra–Kumasi Expressway** is a proposed 198.7 km [expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited-access_road) starting from [Accra](56824.md) to [Kumasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumasi) that, when completed, will become Ghana's first six-lane access-controlled [tolled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toll_road) expressway. The [Parliament of Ghana](3166296.md) has approved the construction of the road. It is expected that multiple local contractors will be responsible for the construction with a limit of not more than 10 kilometres (6.2 mi) per contractor.

## Description

When completed, Accra–Kumasi Expressway will be a 198.7 km [expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited-access_road) connecting [Accra](56824.md) to [Kumasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumasi), making it Ghana's first six-lane access-controlled [tolled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tollway) expressway.[^cite_note-graphic01-1][^cite_note-ghanaweb01-2][^cite_note-citi01-3][^cite_note-myjoy01-4] The expressway will include two 20-lane automated [toll plazas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toll_plaza) and go through the [Greater Accra](2227696.md), [Eastern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Region_\(Ghana\)) and [Ashanti Regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashanti_Region).[^cite_note-citi01-3]

## History

Accra–Kumasi Expressway was proposed by the [Ghanaian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Ghana) in the 2026 Budget Statement and Economic Policy on 13 November 2025.[^cite_note-graphic01-1] \$1.7 billion was set aside for the road,[^cite_note-birimso-5] and, as part of the Big Push Infrastructure Programme,[^cite_note-6] the [Parliament of Ghana](3166296.md) approved the construction of the road in the following month.[^cite_note-ghanaweb01-2][^cite_note-7] The Minister of Roads and Highways, [Kwame Governs Agbodza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwame_Governs_Agbodza), said that the Accra–Kumasi Expressway would not replace the current highway[^cite_note-ghanaweb01-2] In January 2026, Ghanaian president [John Mahama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Mahama) announced that construction would begin,[^cite_note-myjoy01-4] It is expected that multiple local contractors will be responsible for the construction with a limit of not more than 10 kilometres (6.2 mi) per contractor in order for Accra–Kumasi Expressway to be completed by January 2029.[^cite_note-gbc01-8]

### Birimso Bridge crash

On 3 August 2026,[^cite_note-9] a multi-vehicle crash occurred at Birimso Bridge on the Accra–Kumasi Highway, killing at least 15 people and urging the Minority in Parliament to improve the highway's safety,[^cite_note-birimso-5] requesting that the government use \$300 million of the Accra–Kumasi Expressway's funds to complete unfinished sections of the highway.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

### 13 August 2026 Crash

On 13 August 2026, twelve[^cite_note-12] people were killed and 13 others were injured after two trucks, two minibuses, two cars and a tricycle collided.[^cite_note-13]
