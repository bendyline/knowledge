**Anastasiia Nikolaevna Metelkina** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Анастасия Николаевна Метёлкина; [Georgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_language): ანასტასია ნიკოლაევნა მეტელკინა; born 10 March 2005) is a Russian-born [pair skater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pair_skater) who competes for [Georgia](48768.md). With current partner [Luka Berulava](62856231.md), she is an Olympic silver medalist ([2026](82110766.md)), World silver medalist ([2026](82193351.md)), European champion ([2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Figure_Skating_Championship)), two-time [European Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Figure_Skating_Championships) medalist (2024, 2025), [2024–25 Grand Prix Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024-25_Grand_Prix_of_Figure_Skating_Final) bronze medalist, a three-time [Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Grand_Prix_of_Figure_Skating) medalist, and a four-time [Challenger Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Challenger_Series) medalist. On the junior level, Metelkina/Berulava are two-time [World Junior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Junior_Figure_Skating_Championships) champions ([2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_World_Junior_Figure_Skating_Championships), [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Junior_Figure_Skating_Championships)), the [2023–24 Junior Grand Prix Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023-24_Grand_Prix_of_Figure_Skating_Final) champion, and a two-time [Junior Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Junior_Grand_Prix) gold medalist.

She and Berulava are the first Georgian athletes to ever win a medal at a Winter Olympic Games.

Metelkina previously competed with [Daniil Parkman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniil_Parkman), winning a bronze medal at the [2022 Grand Prix of Espoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Grand_Prix_of_Espoo) and a silver medal at the [2021 Golden Spin of Zagreb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_CS_Golden_Spin_of_Zagreb). Metelkina/Parkman were the first Georgian pairs team to compete at a [World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Figure_Skating_Championships), doing so in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_World_Figure_Skating_Championships).[^cite_note-FSG210226-1]

## Personal life

Metelkina was born on March 10, 2005, in [Vladimir, Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir,_Russia).[^cite_note-ISU-2223-2]

## Career

Metelkina began figure skating in 2009.[^cite_note-ISU-2223-2] Her first pair partners were Daniil Shvetsov and Ilya Krasnikov, with whom she represented Russia.[^cite_note-AS-AM-3]

### Pair skating with Daniil Parkman

#### 2020–21 season: Debut of Metelkina/Parkman

In December 2020, it was announced that Metelkina had teamed up with fellow [Russian](59510.md) pair skater, [Daniil Parkman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniil_Parkman), and that they would be representing [Georgia](48768.md) together. In February 2021, it was announced that the pair had earned the minimum technical scores to compete at the [2021 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_World_Figure_Skating_Championships) in [Stockholm](26741.md), [Sweden](5058739.md).[^cite_note-winterstar-pairs-4][^cite_note-FSG210226-1]

Making their debut as a pair team at these Championships, Metelkina/Parkman placed fourteenth in the short program and sixteenth in the free skate, finishing the event in sixteenth-place overall. They became the first Georgian pair team to compete at a [World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Figure_Skating_Championships).[^cite_note-FSG210226-1][^cite_note-ISU-2021-5]

#### 2021–22 season

Metelkina/Parkman began their season with a silver medal at the [2021 Budapest Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest_Trophy), before going on to finish fifth at the [2021 Warsaw Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_CS_Warsaw_Cup). At the [2021 Golden Spin of Zagreb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_CS_Golden_Spin_of_Zagreb), the pair took the silver medal.[^cite_note-ISU-2122-6]

Although assigned to compete at both the [2022 European Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_European_Figure_Skating_Championships) and the [2022 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_World_Figure_Skating_Championships), the pair ultimately withdrew from both events and were replaced by [Safina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karina_Safina)/[Berulava](62856231.md).[^cite_note-22euentries-pairs-7][^cite_note-22wcentries-pairs-8]

#### 2022–23 season: Grand Prix medal

Metelkina/Parkman opened their season at the [2022 Nebelhorn Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_CS_Nebelhorn_Trophy), finishing fifth. They went on to compete on the [Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_ISU_Grand_Prix_of_Figure_Skating) series, finishing fourth at the [2022 MK John Wilson Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_MK_John_Wilson_Trophy) as well as taking bronze at the [2022 Grand Prix of Espoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Grand_Prix_of_Espoo).[^cite_note-ISU-2223-2]

Despite being selected to compete at the [2023 European Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_European_Figure_Skating_Championships), Metelkina/Parkman ultimately ended their partnership in December 2022.[^cite_note-9]

### Pair skating with Luka Berulava

#### 2023–24 season: Debut of Metelkina/Berulava, European silver medal, and World Junior Champions



Metelkina and Berulava at the [2024 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_World_Figure_Skating_Championships)

In June 2023, it was announced that Metelkina had former a new partnership with [Luka Berulava](62856231.md) and that they would train in [Perm, Russia](389777.md) under Pavel Sliusarenko, Egor Zukroev, and [Maxim Trankov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_Trankov).[^cite_note-MB-23-10][^cite_note-ISU-2324-11] Metelkina/Berulava opted to make their competitive debut on the [Junior Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023-24_ISU_Junior_Grand_Prix), a first for Metelkina, winning the gold medal at the [2023 JGP Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Junior_Grand_Prix_in_Turkey) in [Istanbul](3391396.md). Despite two falls in their free skate, their margin of victory over American silver medalists [Flores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Flores)/[Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Wang) was almost 27 points.[^cite_note-ISU230911-12] Two weeks later they competed their second event, the [2023 JGP Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Junior_Grand_Prix_in_Hungary) in [Budapest](36787.md). They again won easily, despite difficulties on their jump elements. Metelkina/Berulava's results qualified them to the [Junior Grand Prix Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023-24_Grand_Prix_of_Figure_Skating_Final) in December; they said they planned on getting senior-level experience in the meantime.[^cite_note-ISU230925-13]

Metelkina/Berulava made their senior debut at the [Warsaw Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_CS_Warsaw_Cup), winning the gold medal.[^cite_note-ISU-AMLB-14] They then entered the Junior Grand Prix Final as heavy favourites to take the title, and won by a 34-point margin over Canadian silver medalists [Kemp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ava_Kemp)/[Elizarov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yohnatan_Elizarov). They were the first Georgian pair team to win the Junior Grand Prix Final gold.[^cite_note-GS231209-15]

Entering the [2024 European Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_European_Figure_Skating_Championships) in [Kaunas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaunas) as among the favourites, Metelkina/Berulava took first place in the short program, winning a gold small medal. Metelkina erred on both of her jump sequences in the free skate, and they came fifth in that segment, dropping to second place overall. They finished 3.05 points back of champions [Beccari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucrezia_Beccari)/[Guarise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matteo_Guarise) of Italy. Berulava remarked that "not everything worked out. But it's only our first season together and silver is also a medal."[^cite_note-GS240111-16]

Having earned a medal at a senior championship event before returning to junior competition, Metelkina/Berulava were heavy favourites at the [2024 World Junior Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_World_Junior_Figure_Skating_Championships) in [Taipei](57648.md). They won the short program by a margin of 9.20 points. Although they struggled in the free skate, with errors on all four jumping elements, they ultimately claimed the gold medal. Both vowed to work harder in training after the difficulties in the free program.[^cite_note-GS240229-17]

Making their senior [World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Figure_Skating_Championships) debut as a team at the [2024 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_World_Figure_Skating_Championships) in [Montreal](7954681.md), Metelkina/Berulava were fifth in the short program. They were only 0.08 points behind fourth-place Germans [Hase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minerva_Fabienne_Hase)/[Volodin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Volodin), thus narrowly missing participation in the final flight of the free skate. In that segment, they struggled with several errors, including an aborted lift, which saw them come tenth in the free skate and drop to seventh overall. Speaking afterward, a "despondent" Berulava said: "Right now, I don't have words to comment on this performance."[^cite_note-GS240322-18]

#### 2024–25 season: First Grand Prix gold, European bronze medal, and second World Junior gold medal



Metelkina and Berluava performing their short program at the [2025 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Figure_Skating_Championships)

Metelkina/Berulava did not compete on the [Challenger circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024-25_ISU_Challenger_Series) at the start of the season, as he explained their belief that they had begun the prior season "too early," as a result of which "we ended up feeling exhausted by the time the most important tournaments came by."[^cite_note-GS240922-19] They began the [Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024-25_ISU_Grand_Prix_of_Figure_Skating) at the [2024 Skate America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Skate_America), where they came third in the short program but dropped to fourth overall after a fourth-place free skate that featured multiple jump errors and a fall in their death spiral element. They missed the bronze medal by a fraction of a point, but Metelkina said that the "short margin to the bronze medal is not what really matters. The mistakes are what we have to improve and work on."[^cite_note-GS241020-20] At their second event, the [2024 NHK Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_NHK_Trophy), Metelkina/Berulava won the gold medal, defeating reigning World champions [Miura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riku_Miura)/[Kihara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryuichi_Kihara) in the process. This was the first Grand Prix medal of any colour for a Georgian pair team, of which Berulava said they were "just so proud."[^cite_note-GS241109-21] Shortly afterward, they competed at and won the [2024 CS Warsaw Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_CS_Warsaw_Cup).[^cite_note-ISU-AMLB-14]

The team's results at their Grand Prix events qualified them for the [Grand Prix Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024-25_Grand_Prix_of_Figure_Skating_Final) in [Grenoble](50972.md). They were third in the short program with a new personal best score of 72.26 points. They were second in the free skate with the lone error being an underrotated jump by Metelkina, remaining third overall and taking the bronze medal.[^cite_note-GS241207-22] The following month at [2025 European Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_European_Figure_Skating_Championships), Metelkina/Berulava entered as podium favourites, but came ninth in the short program after multiple errors, including an invalid death spiral. They rebounded in the free skate, coming third in that segment and rising to third overall for their second bronze medal at a major event of the season.[^cite_note-GS250130-23]

Metelkina/Berulava opted to return to the junior level to compete at the [2025 World Junior Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Junior_Figure_Skating_Championships) in [Debrecen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debrecen), for which they were subject to some criticism in skating circles. Berulava defended the decision, noting that it was allowed by the ISU's rules, and citing a desire to gain competitive experience and [World Standing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_World_Standings_and_Season's_World_Ranking) points.[^cite_note-GS250130-23] They won both segments of the competition by a wide margin, taking their second consecutive World Junior title (and Berulava's third) by a margin of almost 27 points.[^cite_note-GS250301-24]

At the [2025 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Figure_Skating_Championships), they finished fourth in the short program, sixth in the free skate, and fourth overall.[^cite_note-GS032825P-25] "Actually, we’re happy with the fourth place, considering what we did," said Berulava. "It was a long season, but due to the good preparation by our coaches, we are not feeling tired and exhausted. We still feel ready to go, and we’re actually really hoping to make the World Team Trophy.”[^cite_note-GS032825P-25]

The team capped off the season with [2025 World Team Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_ISU_World_Team_Trophy_in_Figure_Skating) where they placed third in both the short program and free skate, with Team Georgia taking sixth place overall. At this event, they earned a new personal best for both the short program and total score.[^cite_note-GS041825-26][^cite_note-GS041925-27] “It feels easier to skate in this event as there is so much support,” said Metelkina. “We hope to get into the Olympic team event and this competition was a good practice. It was very important for us to be here.”[^cite_note-GS041925-27]

#### 2025–26 season: Milano Cortina Olympic Pairs silver, World silver, and European champions



Metelkina and Berulava doing their opening pose for their short program at [2025 Skate America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Skate_America)

Metelkina/Berulava opened the 2025–26 season in September at [2025 CS Kinoshita Group Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_CS_Kinoshita_Group_Cup) where they earned the silver medal. The following month, they took gold at [2025 CS Trialeti Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_CS_Trialeti_Trophy).

Two weeks later, the team competed at [2025 Cup of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cup_of_China) where they won their second individual Grand Prix gold.[^cite_note-GS102625P-28] "Today was a rather difficult day," said Berulava after the free skate. "I'm glad we fought for this gold medal and didn't give up after the mistake on the jump."[^cite_note-GS102625P-28]

The following month, Metelkina/Berulava took silver at [2025 Skate America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Skate_America).[^cite_note-GS111525P-29] They placed first in the short program, but made costly mistakes in the free skate where they finished fourth. “Unfortunately, our performance today did not work out, but we will work hard,” said Berulava. “We want to improve, and we also will work on the mental side." After medaling in both Grand Prix events, they qualified for the 2025-26 [Grand Prix of Figure Skating Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Prix_of_Figure_Skating_Final). “Now we will take a deep breath after this, and we will work on our mistakes,” Metelkina summed up. “And I'm also very pleased that the Final is in Japan. We always skate very well in Japan."[^cite_note-GS111525P-29]



Metelkina and Berulava performing a [death spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_spiral_\(figure_skating\)) during their free skate at [2025 Skate America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Skate_America)

In December, Metelkina/Berulava finished fourth at the [2025–26 Grand Prix Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Grand_Prix_of_Figure_Skating_Final). They placed third in the short program and fourth in the free skate after a shaky lift. "The skate was OK, we made some mistakes," Berulava acknowledged. "After the Final we will go home and will train hard to win the Europeans."[^cite_note-GS120525P-30]

The following month, the duo won the gold at the [2026 European Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_European_Figure_Skating_Championships), becoming the first Georgian Pairs team to do so. "We are crazy happy to have won the European championships!" said Metelkina. "We’ve had the bronze, the silver, and now we finally got the gold medal. We couldn't be happier right now!"[^cite_note-GS011526P-31] The team will compete next at the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md). "Right now, the situation is very interesting," said Berulava of the upcoming Olympics. "It's fascinating because there's not a clear leader. There are six top teams and all together, and everyone is aiming for the gold medal. Each of us, each athlete, we all have the same goal."[^cite_note-GS011526P-31]

On 6 February, Metelkina/Berulava placed second in the short program in the [2026 Winter Olympics Figure Skating Team Event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Team_event). "It was a calm skate, a good warm up," said Metelkina. "We are confident and our coaches and our federation has confidence in us, too. We can do it all and practices were excellent. We had a very good week of training before the Olympic Games, therefore we went out calmly and did what we know we can do."[^cite_note-GS020626P-32] Two days later, they placed second in the free skate and Team Georgia finished in fourth place overall.[^cite_note-SS-AMLB-33]



Metelkina and Berulava after their performance at the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md)

On 15 February, Metelkina/Berulava competed in the short program of the [Pairs](82110766.md) event. They placed second in the short program despite Metelkina stepping out of the throw triple flip. "I'm always a maximalist, so I'm not satisfied that I made a mistake on an element that is usually the most consistent for me,” said Metelkina. “I will try to do everything clean tomorrow. I'm absolutely satisfied with what my partner did today. I am also grateful about the great support my partner gave me."[^cite_note-34] The following day, the pair skated a clean free program aside from Metelkina stepping out of the throw triple loop, placing second in that segment and winning the silver medal overall.[^cite_note-SS-AMLB-33] With this result, Metelkina/Berulava made history as the first Georgian athletes to medal at a Winter Olympic Games.[^cite_note-GS021626P-35] "It is a huge honor, and I am incredibly happy that our names are now written as the first Georgians to win a medal at the Olympic Winter Games. It's a huge honor. It was a long path for us, and we are unbelievably happy about it," said Berulava following the result. "I hope that after us, a big team will develop in Georgia — that we will have second, third, fourth pairs, and that there will be competition inside the country. After the end of my career, I want to take care of this, because I love figure skating and I want to do everything to develop pair skating in Georgia."[^cite_note-36]

On March 26, 2026, Metelkina/Berulava made history by becoming the first Georgian pair team to earn a world medal (silver) at the [2026 World Figure Skating Championships](82193351.md). They placed second in the short program and fourth in the free skate. "I'm very happy because this is our first medal at the World Championships and also that we ended the season on a high note," said Metelkina. "There's a medal and there are a lot of emotions. This season, I really want to thank everybody who was part of our preparation—our coaches, our federation. We tried our best and we will continue to try our best."[^cite_note-GS032626P-37]
