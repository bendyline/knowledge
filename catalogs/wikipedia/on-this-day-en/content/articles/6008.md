The **Comorian Armed Forces** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Armée nationale de développement, AND*; lit. 'National Army for Development'[^cite_note-USAC2014-1][^cite_note-2]) are the national [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military) of the [Comoros](5403.md).&#x20;

The armed forces consist of a small standing army and a 500-member police force, as well as a 500-member defense force. A defense treaty with [France](5843419.md) provides naval resources for protection of territorial waters, training of Comorian military personnel, and air surveillance. France maintains a small troop presence in the Comoros at government request. France maintains a small [Navy](383256.md) base and a [Foreign Legion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Foreign_Legion) [Detachment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Legion_Detachment_in_Mayotte) (DLEM) (now the [5th Foreign Infantry Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_Foreign_Infantry_Regiment)) in [Mayotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayotte).[^cite_note-3] In 2008, the size of the armed forces was estimated to be 1,060.[^cite_note-4]

The AND has worked with the United States[^cite_note-USAC2014-1] and [African Union](10634933.md) armed forces as well. In 2008, the African Union helped the AND retake control of [Anjouan](184799.md) after the island had been taken over by rebels.[^cite_note-5]

## Structure

The AND consists of the following components:

- Comorian Ground Defense Force

- Comorian National Gendarmerie

- National School of the Armed Forces and Gendarmerie

- Comorian Air Force

- Comorian Presidential Guard

- Comorian Military Health Services

- Comorian Coast Guard
