**Jasmine Paolini**[^cite_note-5] (born 4 January 1996) is an Italian professional [tennis](29773.md) player. She has been ranked world No. 4 in singles by the [WTA](57768.md), the joint-highest-ranked Italian woman in singles (alongside [Francesca Schiavone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesca_Schiavone)), and No. 3 in doubles. Paolini is a [Grand Slam](197638.md#Tournaments) champion in doubles at the [2025 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_French_Open_-_Women's_doubles), partnering [Sara Errani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Errani).[^cite_note-6] Representing her country, Paolini won an [Olympic gold medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_-_Women's_doubles) in doubles at the [2024 Paris Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Paris_Olympics), also with Errani. On the [WTA Tour](1398173.md), she has won three singles titles and ten in doubles, including two singles and five doubles [WTA 1000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_1000) titles. She is also a three-time major finalist, contesting the [singles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open_-_Women's_singles) and [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open_-_Women's_doubles) finals of the [2024 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open) and the [singles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wimbledon_Championships_-_Women's_singles) final of the [2024 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wimbledon_Championships). Paolini led [Italy](11233225.md) to the [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Billie_Jean_King_Cup) and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Billie_Jean_King_Cup) [Billie Jean King Cup](1108893.md) titles, winning the [Heart Award](1108893.md#Heart_Award) for the 2024.

## Early life

Jasmine Paolini was born in [Castelnuovo di Garfagnana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castelnuovo_di_Garfagnana).[^cite_note-Corriere-7] Her father, Ugo, is [Italian](385155.md) and her mother, Jacqueline, is of [Ghanaian](35867237.md) and [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_people) descent,[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-La_Nazione-9] originally from [Łódź](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%81%C3%B3d%C5%BA).[^cite_note-Corriere-7] In 2001, at the age of five, Paolini was introduced to tennis by her father and uncle,[^cite_note-FiveThings-10] and trained at the Mirafiume Tennis Club in [Bagni di Lucca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagni_di_Lucca) in her youth.[^cite_note-La_Nazione-9] In 2011, at the age of 15, Paolini moved to [Tirrenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirrenia) for training.[^cite_note-Il_Tirreno-11][^cite_note-12] Her younger brother, William, also plays tennis.[^cite_note-Corriere-7]

## Career

### Juniors

In January 2013, Paolini won her only junior title at the J4 Wilson ITF Junior Classic in [Bergheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergheim,_Austria).[^cite_note-13] She made her Junior Grand Slam debut after qualifying for the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_US_Open_\(tennis\)) later that year. She reached the third round in [singles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Girls'_singles), before losing to eventual finalist [Tornado Alicia Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_Alicia_Black).[^cite_note-14] She also reached the third round of the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Girls'_singles) in January 2014.[^cite_note-15]

### 2015–17: WTA Tour debut, first 100k title



Paolini at the [2017 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_US_Open_\(tennis\))

In May 2015, Paolini received a wildcard into the main draw of the doubles tournament at the [Italian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Italian_Open_\(tennis\)) with [Nastassja Burnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nastassja_Burnett). The pair lost in the first round to [Alla Kudryavtseva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alla_Kudryavtseva) and [Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasia_Pavlyuchenkova).[^cite_note-16] In June 2017, Paolini won her first 100k title at [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Open_F%C3%A9minin_de_Marseille) where she defeated [Taylor Townsend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Townsend), [Sara Cakarevic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Cakarevic), [Anhelina Kalinina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhelina_Kalinina), [Dalma Gálfi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalma_G%C3%A1lfi), and top seed [Tatjana Maria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatjana_Maria).[^cite_note-17] She made her WTA Tour singles debut in the following month at the [Swedish Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Swedish_Open) but lost in the first round to fifth seed [Carla Suárez Navarro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carla_Su%C3%A1rez_Navarro).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] She also entered the [Guangzhou International Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Guangzhou_International_Women's_Open) and lost in the first round to third seed [Anett Kontaveit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anett_Kontaveit).[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

### 2018–20: WTA Tour wins, top 100



Paolini in the qualifiers of the [2018 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Wimbledon_Championships)

After failing to qualify for the [2018 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Australian_Open), Paolini represented Italy in the [Fed Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Fed_Cup) against Spain in the [World Group II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Fed_Cup_World_Group_II) and against Belgium in the [World Group play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Fed_Cup_World_Group_play-offs).[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] In April 2018, she recorded her first WTA Tour win at the [Copa Colsanitas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Copa_Colsanitas) against qualifier [Lizette Cabrera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizette_Cabrera).[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] The following month, as a lucky loser at the [Prague Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_J\&T_Banka_Prague_Open), she upset third seed [Daria Kasatkina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daria_Kasatkina)[^cite_note-26] and defeated [Anna Karolína Schmiedlová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Karol%C3%ADna_Schmiedlov%C3%A1) to reach her first WTA quarterfinal.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

In May 2019, Paolini made her WTA 1000 singles debut as a wildcard entrant at the [Italian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Italian_Open_\(tennis\)), but lost in the first round to [Sofia Kenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Kenin).[^cite_note-29] Later that month, she qualified for her first Grand Slam tournament at the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_French_Open) by defeating [Anna Zaja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Zaja), [Rebecca Šramková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_%C5%A0ramkov%C3%A1), and [Allie Kiick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allie_Kiick) in the qualifiers without dropping a set.[^cite_note-30] She then reached the quarterfinals of the [Palermo Ladies Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Internazionali_Femminili_di_Palermo), defeating [Laura Siegemund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Siegemund) and [Irina-Camelia Begu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irina-Camelia_Begu)[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32] before losing to top seed [Kiki Bertens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiki_Bertens).[^cite_note-33] She also qualified for the main draw in [Guangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Guangzhou_International_Women's_Open)[^cite_note-34] and reached the quarterfinals.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] After reaching the final of the [Tokyo Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Ando_Securities_Open) in November 2019, Paolini reached a new career-high of world No. 96. She was the first Italian to reach the top 100 since [Camila Giorgi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camila_Giorgi) in 2012.[^cite_note-37]

In 2020, the Italian entered directly into the main draws of the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Australian_Open) and the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_US_Open_\(tennis\)), but failed to advance past the first round of either.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39] She recorded her first WTA 1000 level win at the [Italian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Italian_Open_\(tennis\)) by defeating [Anastasija Sevastova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasija_Sevastova) in the first round.[^cite_note-40] At the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_Open), which was postponed to September due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), Paolini recorded her first major win by defeating [Aliona Bolsova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliona_Bolsova) in the first round.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42] She also recorded her first doubles win at a major, partnering [Varvara Gracheva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varvara_Gracheva) and defeating [Lyudmyla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyudmyla_Kichenok) and [Nadiia Kichenok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadiia_Kichenok) in the first round.[^cite_note-43]

### 2021: Tour singles and doubles titles



Paolini at the [2020 - 2021 Billie Jean King Cup play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_play-offs)

At the [Gippsland Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Gippsland_Trophy) in February 2021, Paolini reached her first WTA 500 third round, defeating [Rebecca Marino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Marino) and tenth seed [Wang Qiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Qiang_\(tennis\)), before losing to eighth seed [Karolína Muchová](55266410.md).[^cite_note-44] She reached her first WTA 125 final at [Saint-Malo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_L'Open_35_de_Saint-Malo) by defeating [Elsa Jacquemot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Jacquemot), [Nina Stojanović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Stojanovi%C4%87), Anna Karolína Schmiedlová, and Varvara Gracheva, before ultimately losing to [Viktorija Golubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktorija_Golubic).[^cite_note-45] She reached her second consecutive second round at the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_French_Open) with a win over [Stefanie Vögele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefanie_V%C3%B6gele).[^cite_note-46]

In June, the Italian won her first WTA 125 title seeded third at the [Bol Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Bol_Open), defeating [Viktória Hrunčáková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikt%C3%B3ria_Hrun%C4%8D%C3%A1kov%C3%A1), [Olga Govortsova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Govortsova), [Kristína Kučová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krist%C3%ADna_Ku%C4%8Dov%C3%A1), top seed [Anna Blinkova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Blinkova), and second seed [Arantxa Rus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arantxa_Rus).[^cite_note-47] The following month, she won her first WTA Tour doubles title at the [Hamburg European Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Hamburg_European_Open), partnering [Jil Teichmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jil_Teichmann).[^cite_note-48] At the [Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics), Paolini represented Italy in both [singles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics_-_Women's_singles) and [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics_-_Women's_doubles). In singles, she lost in the first round to tenth seed [Petra Kvitová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra_Kvitov%C3%A1).[^cite_note-49] In doubles, she and partner [Sara Errani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Errani), who had entered as alternates, reached the second round by upsetting the eighth-seeded team of [Nicole Melichar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Melichar-Martinez) and [Alison Riske](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alison_Riske-Amritraj).[^cite_note-FiveThings-10][^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51]

In September, Paolini won her first career title at the [Slovenia Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Zavarovalnica_Sava_Portoro%C5%BE) in Portorož, defeating sixth seed [Dayana Yastremska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayana_Yastremska), Anna Kalinskaya, fourth seed [Sorana Cîrstea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorana_C%C3%AErstea), second seed [Yulia Putintseva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yulia_Putintseva), and third seed Alison Riske.[^cite_note-52] The following month, she reached her first WTA 1000 third round, after qualifying for the [Indian Wells Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_BNP_Paribas_Open) and defeating [Mai Hontama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mai_Hontama) and 14th seed [Elise Mertens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elise_Mertens).[^cite_note-53][^cite_note-54] She ended the season by reaching the semifinals of the [Courmayeur Ladies Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Courmayeur_Ladies_Open)[^cite_note-55] and the quarterfinals of the [Linz Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Upper_Austria_Ladies_Linz).[^cite_note-56]

### 2022: Top 50, first top-10 win

At the [Melbourne Summer Set 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Melbourne_Summer_Set_1_-_Women's_doubles), Paolini and partner Sara Errani reached the doubles final, but lost to the second-seeded team of [Asia Muhammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Muhammad) and [Jessica Pegula](32944701.md). Following the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Australian_Open), the Italian entered the WTA top 50 for the first time.[^cite_note-57] She reached back-to-back third rounds at the [Indian Wells Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_BNP_Paribas_Open), upsetting second seed [Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md) in the second round for her first top 10 win.[^cite_note-58]

Paolini reached back-to-back WTA 250 quarterfinals in [Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Internazionali_Femminili_di_Palermo_-_Singles) and [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_WTA_Poland_Open_-_Singles).[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-60] Attempting to defend her title in [Portorož](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Zavarovalnica_Sava_Portoro%C5%BE_%E2%80%93_Singles), she defeated [Tara Würth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tara_W%C3%BCrth) and [Kaja Juvan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaja_Juvan), before losing to eventual champion [Kateřina Siniaková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate%C5%99ina_Siniakov%C3%A1) in the quarterfinals.[^cite_note-61] She reached her second WTA final at the [Transylvania Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Transylvania_Open_-_Singles), defeating sixth seed [Marta Kostyuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marta_Kostyuk), Dayana Yastremska, [Jule Niemeier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jule_Niemeier), and seventh seed [Wang Xiyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Xiyu),[^cite_note-AONewWave-62][^cite_note-Il_Tirreno-11] before ultimately losing to qualifier Anna Blinkova.[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64] She ended her season by winning the 100k [Torneig Internacional Els Gorchs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Torneig_Internacional_Els_Gorchs).[^cite_note-65]

### 2023: Italian No. 1, BJK Cup final



Paolini at the [2023 Birmingham Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Birmingham_Classic)

Paolini's 2023 season started out slow, with first-round exits at the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Australian_Open), [Dubai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Dubai_Tennis_Championships), [Indian Wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_BNP_Paribas_Open), [Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Miami_Open), and [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Mutua_Madrid_Open). She won her second WTA 125 title in May, seeded fourth at the [Firenze Ladies Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Firenze_Ladies_Open),[^cite_note-66] and reached the final of the [Makarska International Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Makarska_International_Championships) the following month.[^cite_note-67] In July, she reached the final at the [Palermo Ladies Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Internazionali_Femminili_di_Palermo) as the fourth seed, defeating [Arantxa Rus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arantxa_Rus), Dayana Yastremska, top seed Daria Kasatkina, and [Sara Sorribes Tormo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Sorribes_Tormo),[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69] before losing to second seed Zheng Qinwen.[^cite_note-70] Paolini qualified for the [Cincinnati Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Western_&_Southern_Open), where she reached the quarterfinals with wins over Marta Kostyuk and [Cristina Bucșa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristina_Buc%C8%99a),[^cite_note-71] and an early retirement by [Elena Rybakina](55541443.md).[^cite_note-72] The following month, she reached the first WTA 1000 doubles semifinal of her career, partnering [Mayar Sherif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayar_Sherif), in [Guadalajara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Guadalajara_Open_Akron).[^cite_note-73]

At the [China Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_China_Open_\(tennis\)), Paolini reached the third round in singles with wins over [Beatriz Haddad Maia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beatriz_Haddad_Maia) and [Yuan Yue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuan_Yue).[^cite_note-74][^cite_note-75] Following this result, she reached a new career-high ranking of No. 31 on 9 October and surpassed [Elisabetta Cocciaretto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elisabetta_Cocciaretto) as the No. 1 Italian female tennis player.[^cite_note-76] At the [Zhengzhou Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Zhengzhou_Open), she reached the semifinals defeating [Moyuka Uchijima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moyuka_Uchijima), world No. 10 [Caroline Garcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caroline_Garcia), and Laura Siegemund,[^cite_note-77][^cite_note-78] before losing to eventual champion [Zheng Qinwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheng_Qinwen).[^cite_note-79] The following week, she reached the final of the [Jasmin Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Jasmin_Open_-_Singles) in both singles and doubles. As the top seed in singles, she defeated [Alizé Cornet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliz%C3%A9_Cornet), [Petra Marčinko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra_Mar%C4%8Dinko), sixth seed [Lucia Bronzetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucia_Bronzetti), and fourth seed [Lesia Tsurenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesia_Tsurenko), before losing to second seed and defending champion Elise Mertens.[^cite_note-80] In [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Jasmin_Open_-_Doubles), she and partner Sara Errani won the title after saving a match point in the semifinals.[^cite_note-81] In the [Billie Jean King Cup Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_finals) in November, Paolini and [Team Italy](11233225.md) defeated teams from [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_team), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_team), and [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_team), before losing to [Team Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_team) in the final.[^cite_note-82]

### 2024: Two major finals, Olympic gold

At the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Australian_Open_-_Women's_singles), Paolini reached the fourth round of a major for the first time with wins over [Diana Shnaider](68209798.md), Tatjana Maria, and Anna Blinkova,[^cite_note-AO1/19-83][^cite_note-84] before being beaten by fellow first-time fourth rounder [Anna Kalinskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Kalinskaya). Despite the loss, she reached a new career-high ranking of 24.[^cite_note-85] Playing with Sara Errani, she won the doubles title at the [Linz Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Upper_Austria_Ladies_Linz_-_Doubles) in February, defeating top seeds [Nicole Melichar-Martinez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Melichar-Martinez) and [Ellen Perez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellen_Perez) in the final.[^cite_note-86]

At the [Dubai Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Dubai_Tennis_Championships_-_Women's_singles), Paolini reached her second quarterfinal at WTA 1000 level, defeating 11th seed Beatriz Haddad Maia, [Leylah Fernandez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leylah_Fernandez)[^cite_note-87] and eighth seed [Maria Sakkari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Sakkari). She reached her first final at this level, following a win over Sorana Cîrstea in the semifinals.[^cite_note-88] In the final, she defeated qualifier Anna Kalinskaya to win her first WTA 1000 title, avenging her Australian Open loss earlier in the season.[^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90] As a result, she moved into the top 15 of the rankings on 26 February 2024.[^cite_note-91]

At the [Indian Wells Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_BNP_Paribas_Open_-_Women's_singles), Paolini reached the fourth round, defeating Tatjana Maria and 21st seed Anna Kalinskaya again, before losing to 28th seed [Anastasia Potapova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasia_Potapova).[^cite_note-92][^cite_note-93] She reached a new career-high ranking of No. 13 after a quarterfinal appearance at the [Porsche Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Porsche_Tennis_Grand_Prix_-_Singles) in Stuttgart, where she defeated compatriot Sara Errani and seventh seed [Ons Jabeur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ons_Jabeur), before losing to eventual champion Elena Rybakina.[^cite_note-94][^cite_note-95] Following a fourth-round run in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Mutua_Madrid_Open), she rose further to a career-high of No. 12.[^cite_note-96]



Paolini and Sara Errani following the [women's doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles) final at the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics)

Partnering with Sara Errani, Paolini reached the doubles final at the [Italian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Italian_Open_-_Women's_doubles)[^cite_note-97] and defeated the third-seeded team of [Coco Gauff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_Gauff) and [Erin Routliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erin_Routliffe) to win her first WTA 1000 doubles title.[^cite_note-98] As a result, she reached the top 25 in doubles on 20 May 2024. Seeded 12th at the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Paolini reached a major quarterfinal for the first time with wins over [Daria Saville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daria_Saville), lucky loser [Hailey Baptiste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hailey_Baptiste), 2019 US Open champion [Bianca Andreescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bianca_Andreescu), and [Elina Avanesyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elina_Avanesyan).[^cite_note-99][^cite_note-100] She then upset fourth seed Elena Rybakina for her first top-five win at a major.[^cite_note-101][^cite_note-102] In the semifinals, she defeated [Mirra Andreeva](71915009.md) to reach her first major final,[^cite_note-103][^cite_note-104][^cite_note-105] which she lost to world No. 1, Iga Świątek.[^cite_note-106] With Errani, she also reached the [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open_-_Women's_doubles) final, but the pair lost to the fifth-seeded team of Coco Gauff and Kateřina Siniaková.[^cite_note-107]

Having never won a match at the tournament, Paolini, seeded seventh, reached the final at [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wimbledon_Championships_-_Women's_singles), recording wins over Sara Sorribes Tormo,[^cite_note-108] [Greet Minnen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greet_Minnen),[^cite_note-109] Bianca Andreescu,[^cite_note-110] [Madison Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Keys),[^cite_note-111] [Emma Navarro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Navarro),[^cite_note-112][^cite_note-113] and [Donna Vekić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donna_Veki%C4%87).[^cite_note-114][^cite_note-115][^cite_note-116] She became the first Italian woman to reach the Wimbledon semifinals in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Era),[^cite_note-117][^cite_note-118] and the first woman to reach both the French Open and Wimbledon finals in the same season since [Serena Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serena_Williams) in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_WTA_Tour).[^cite_note-119][^cite_note-120] Paolini ultimately lost in the final to 31st seed Barbora Krejčíková in three sets.[^cite_note-121] Following her run, she reached a new career-high ranking of world No. 5, on 15 July 2024.[^cite_note-122]

Seeded fourth in singles at the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_-_Women's_singles), Paolini was upset in the third round by world No. 67, Anna Karolína Schmiedlová.[^cite_note-123] Seeded third in doubles, she and Errani won the gold medal, defeating Mirra Andreeva and Diana Shnaider in the final.[^cite_note-124][^cite_note-125] In October, Paolini and Errani won the doubles title at the [China Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_China_Open_-_Women's_doubles), defeating [Chan Hao-ching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chan_Hao-ching) and [Veronika Kudermetova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veronika_Kudermetova) in the final and becoming the first Italian doubles champions at the tournament.[^cite_note-126] Following the title win, Paolini reached the top 10 in doubles at world No. 9 on 7 October 2024, becoming the only active player at the time, male or female, to be ranked in the top 10 in both singles and doubles.[^cite_note-127] In singles, at the same tournament, Paolini reached the third round, where she lost to 31st seed [Magda Linette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magda_Linette).[^cite_note-128] At the next WTA 1000, the [Wuhan Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wuhan_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), on her debut, she reached the singles quarterfinals with wins over [Yuan Yue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuan_Yue)[^cite_note-129] and lucky loser [Erika Andreeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erika_Andreeva).[^cite_note-130] Paolini lost to fifth seed and eventual runner-up Zheng Qinwen.[^cite_note-131] As a result, she reached a new career-high ranking of world No. 4 tying the record for the highest-ranked Italian singles female player with [Francesca Schiavone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesca_Schiavone) on 28 October 2024.[^cite_note-career-high-3]

Aged 28, Paolini became the second oldest player, after [Li Na](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Na), to make their debut at the [WTA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_WTA_Finals_-_Singles) in November, defeating Elena Rybakina in straight sets in her opening round-robin match.[^cite_note-132] She lost her second match to [Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md).[^cite_note-133] The Italian was defeated by eventual runner-up Zheng Qinwen in her final round-robin match and subsequently did not progress to the semifinals.[^cite_note-134] Partnering with Sara Errani, she also exited the [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_WTA_Finals_-_Doubles) in the round-robin stage after compiling a record of one win and two losses.[^cite_note-135] At the [Billie Jean King Cup finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_finals) in Spain, Paolini defeated [Moyuka Uchijima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moyuka_Uchijima) and then teamed with Sara Errani to overcome [Shuko Aoyama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuko_Aoyama) and [Eri Hozumi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eri_Hozumi) as Italy won their quarterfinal tie against Japan.[^cite_note-136][^cite_note-137] Italy faced Poland in the semifinals and, after losing her singles match to Iga Świątek, Paolini combined again with Sara Errani to defeat [Katarzyna Kawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katarzyna_Kawa) and Świątek in the decisive doubles and secure a place in the final.[^cite_note-138][^cite_note-139] She then defeated [Rebecca Šramková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_%C5%A0ramkov%C3%A1) to claim the winning points as Italy overcame Slovakia in the final to take the title.[^cite_note-140][^cite_note-141] In December, Paolini and Errani were named [WTA Doubles Team of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_Awards#Doubles_Team_of_the_Year).[^cite_note-142][^cite_note-143]

### 2025: Rome singles and Roland Garros doubles titles

Seeded fourth at the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Australian_Open_-_Women's_singles), Paolini defeated qualifier [Wei Sijia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wei_Sijia)[^cite_note-144] and [Renata Zarazúa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renata_Zaraz%C3%BAa)[^cite_note-145] to reach the third round, where she lost to 28th seed [Elina Svitolina](27630639.md) in three sets.[^cite_note-146] Paolini and Errani won their third WTA 1000 doubles title together at [Doha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Qatar_TotalEnergies_Open_-_Doubles), defeating [Jiang Xinyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiang_Xinyu) and [Wu Fang-hsien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wu_Fang-hsien) in the final.[^cite_note-147] The following week, her defense of her [Dubai Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Dubai_Tennis_Championships_-_Women's_singles) title ended in the third round with a loss to [Sofia Kenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Kenin) during which she suffered an ankle injury that forced her to withdraw from the doubles competition at the tournament.[^cite_note-148]

At the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Miami_Open_-_Women's_singles) in March, wins over Rebecca Šramková,[^cite_note-149] Ons Jabeur,[^cite_note-150] [Naomi Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Osaka)[^cite_note-151] and Magda Linette[^cite_note-152] saw Paolini reach the semifinals, where she lost to world No. 1, Aryna Sabalenka.[^cite_note-153] In April, the Italian defeated wildcard entrants [Eva Lys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Lys)[^cite_note-154] and [Jule Niemeier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jule_Niemeier)[^cite_note-155] and then overcame fourth seed Coco Gauff[^cite_note-156] to make it through to the semifinals at the [Stuttgart Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Porsche_Tennis_Grand_Prix_-_Singles), where her run was once again ended by Aryna Sabalenka.[^cite_note-157] Paolini changed tennis coaches at this time. She broke with longtime coach [Renzo Furlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renzo_Furlan) (2020 - Apr 2025) and joined with [Marc López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_L%C3%B3pez)[^cite_note-158][^cite_note-159]

In May, having never gone beyond the second round in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Italian_Open_-_Women's_singles), Paolini defeated [Lulu Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lulu_Sun), Ons Jabeur, and Jeļena Ostapenko to reach the quarterfinals of her home WTA 1000 for the first time. She defeated Diana Shnaider and [Peyton Stearns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peyton_Stearns) to reach her second WTA 1000 final and first on clay. She defeated Coco Gauff in the final to win her third title, becoming the second Italian woman in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Era), after [Raffaella Reggi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raffaella_Reggi) in [1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Italian_Open_-_Women's_singles), and the first to do so when the tournament was at Tier 5 or WTA 1000 level.[^cite_note-160][^cite_note-161] Paolini won also the doubles title, defeating Elise Mertens and Veronika Kudermetova in the final, defending the title won the previous year.[^cite_note-162] She became the first Italian woman to win both the singles and doubles titles at the tournament in the same year since Raffaella Reggi in [1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Italian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), and the first woman to win both the singles and doubles titles at the tournament in the same year since [Monica Seles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monica_Seles) in [1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Italian_Open_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-163]

At the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_French_Open_-_Women's_doubles) with her partner Sara Errani, Paolini lifted her first major tournament doubles trophy defeating [Anna Danilina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Danilina) and [Aleksandra Krunić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandra_Kruni%C4%87) in the final.[^cite_note-164] In singles, also at the French Open, she reached the fourth round and was defeated by Elina Svitolina for the second successive major tournament.[^cite_note-165] Moving onto the grass-court season, Paolini and Errani were runners-up at the [Berlin Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Berlin_Tennis_Open_-_Doubles), losing [Tereza Mihalikova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tereza_Mihalikova) and [Olivia Nicholls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Nicholls).[^cite_note-166] The following week, she reached the semifinals at the [Bad Homburg Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Bad_Homburg_Open_-_Singles), at which point she lost to Iga Świątek.[^cite_note-167] The Italian defeated [Anastasija Sevastova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasija_Sevastova) in the first round at [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Wimbledon_Championships_-_Women's_singles),[^cite_note-168] before losing her next match to [Kamilla Rakhimova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamilla_Rakhimova).[^cite_note-169] After her loss, Paolini fired her coach Marc López.[^cite_note-170] In July, she announced Italian player [Federico Gaio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federico_Gaio) as her interim coach until the US Open.[^cite_note-171] Paolini then reached the final of the [Cincinnati Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cincinnati_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) in August, where she was defeated in straight sets by Iga Świątek.[^cite_note-172] At the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_US_Open_\(tennis\)), Paolini lost to Markéta Vondroušová in the [singles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_US_Open_-_Women's_singles) third round,[^cite_note-173] while she and Errani made it through to the [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_US_Open_-_Women's_doubles) semifinals, losing to eventual champions Gabriela Dabrowski ### 2026: Out of top 10

As top seed at the [Mérida Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_M%C3%A9rida_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), Paolini received a bye and then recorded wins over lucky loser [Priscilla Hon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priscilla_Hon)[^cite_note-180] and wildcard entrant [Katie Boulter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Boulter) to make it into the semifinals,[^cite_note-181] at which point she lost to eventual champion Cristina Bucșa[^cite_note-182] In March at the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Miami_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), she and Sara Errani were runners-up in the doubles, losing in the final to Kateřina Siniaková and Taylor Townsend.[^cite_note-183] Defending her title at the [Italian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Italian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) in May, Paolini lost to 21st seed Elise Mertens in the third round and as a consequence saw her 101 week run in the world's top 10 come to an end.[^cite_note-184]

Paolini went into the [French Open](81641720.md) hampered by a foot injury which forced her to withdraw from attempting to defend her [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles) title.[^cite_note-185] In the [singles](83033049.md), Paolini defeated Dayana Yastremska,[^cite_note-186] before losing in the second round to world No. 68 [Solana Sierra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solana_Sierra) after letting slip a lead of a set and a break of serve.[^cite_note-187] Beginning her grass court season at the [Eastbourne Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Eastbourne_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she lost to eventual finalist Tatjana Maria in the first round.[^cite_note-188] Seeded 13th at [Wimbledon](83354904.md), Paolini defeated qualifier [Robin Montgomery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Montgomery),[^cite_note-189] Viktorija Golubic,[^cite_note-190] [Maria Sakkari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Sakkari)[^cite_note-191] and 29th seed [Alexandra Eala](62712906.md) to reach the quarterfinals,[^cite_note-192] where she was eliminated by 12th seed Marta Kostyuk.[^cite_note-193] She was scheduled to play in the [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles) with Sara Errani but withdrew as she continued to manage her ongoing foot injury.[^cite_note-194]

At the start of the North American hard court swing of the season, her ongoing foot problem forced Paolini to pull out of the [Washington Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Mubadala_Citi_DC_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) and [Canadian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_National_Bank_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-195] She later also withdrew from the [Cincinnati Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Cincinnati_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) for the same reason.[^cite_note-196] Seeded 19th, Paolini made her comeback at the [US Open](84044135.md) and recorded wins over [Veronika Erjavec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veronika_Erjavec)[^cite_note-197] and qualifier [Lucrezia Stefanini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucrezia_Stefanini),[^cite_note-198] before losing to 16th seed [Sorana Cîrstea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorana_C%C3%AErstea) in the third round.[^cite_note-199]

## Playing style

Paolini's playing style is dynamic and aggressive, characterized by solid [groundstrokes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groundstroke).[^cite_note-200][^cite_note-201] She uses heavy [topspin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topspin) on her forehand, but hits flat on her backhand.[^cite_note-AONewWave-62][^cite_note-202] Due to her height 163 cm (5 ft 4 in), she has stated, "I wish I was taller, because I could [serve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serve_\(tennis\)) better."[^cite_note-203] However, she possesses great speed and agility, a credit to her shorter stature, allowing her to outmaneuver opponents.[^cite_note-204][^cite_note-205]

## Rivalries

Paolini has forged rivalries with several top players on the WTA Tour as well as with fellow Italians. Her matches against major champions and established top 10 players have become key markers in her rise, with some encounters seen as among the highlights of her career. Against [Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md), the Italian has played eight times, trailing 2–6.[^cite_note-210] Their encounters have often come deep in tournaments, including the [Stuttgart Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Porsche_Tennis_Grand_Prix_-_Singles) quarterfinal, where Sabalenka prevailed in straight sets. Both of Paolini’s wins came on clay, reflecting her comfort on slower surfaces against the Belarusian’s power. Paolini has faced [Iga Świątek](51838558.md) seven times, losing first six meetings.[^cite_note-211] They played in 2024 the [2024 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open) final. The latest clash came at the Italian defeating Świątek in straight sets, 6–1, 6–2, in the quarterfinals of the Master 1000 [2025 Wuhan Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Wuhan_Open).[^cite_note-212] The rivalry highlights Świątek’s dominance from the baseline against Paolini’s counterpunching game.

Paolini and [Elena Rybakina](55541443.md) have met six times, with the head-to-head tied 3–3.[^cite_note-213] Their most significant encounter came at the [2024 French Open quarterfinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open_-_Women's_singles), where the Italian scored one of the biggest wins of her career by defeating Rybakina in three sets to reach her first major semifinal. Against former Top-5 [Caroline Garcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caroline_Garcia), the French won their head-to-head, 5–3, in their eight meetings.[^cite_note-214] Last time the Italian defeated Garcia was at the [2025 Qatar Ladies Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Qatar_TotalEnergies_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) second round in straight sets, 6–3, 6–4. Paolini has played [Coco Gauff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_Gauff) seven times and the American leads the series 4–3.[^cite_note-215] Their most notable clash came in the [Italian Open final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Italian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), where Paolini defeated Gauff to win the biggest title of her career in front of a home crowd.[^cite_note-216]

Her rivalry with [Daria Kasatkina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daria_Kasatkina) is evenly balanced at 3–3 after six meetings.[^cite_note-217] The most recent contest came at the [Eastbourne International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Eastbourne_International_-_Women's_singles), where Kasatkina rallied from a set down to defeat Paolini in three sets. Paolini and [Ons Jabeur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ons_Jabeur) have met six times, with the head-to-head tied at 3–3.[^cite_note-218] Their rivalry produced one of Paolini’s career highlights at the [2024 Wimbledon semifinals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), where she came from behind to defeat Jabeur and advance to her first major final.[^cite_note-219] Among Italians, Paolini has shared a rivalry with [Martina Trevisan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martina_Trevisan), meeting seven times with Paolini leading 5–2.[^cite_note-220] Their encounters have often come at WTA events and Fed Cup ties, symbolizing the growing depth of Italian women’s tennis.
