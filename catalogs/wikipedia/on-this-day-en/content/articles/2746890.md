**Hamirpur district** is one of the 75 [districts of Uttar Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Uttar_Pradesh) [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_territories_of_India) of [India](14533.md) and [Hamirpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamirpur,_Uttar_Pradesh) town is the district headquarters. Hamirpur district is a part of [Chitrakoot Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitrakoot_division). The district occupies an area of 4,121.9 km<sup>2</sup>. The district has a population of 1,104,285 (2011 census). As of 2011 it is the third least populous district of Uttar Pradesh (out of [71](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_districts_of_Uttar_Pradesh)), after [Mahoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahoba_district) and [Chitrakoot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitrakoot_district).[^cite_note-districtcensus-1] Two major rivers [Yamuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamuna_River) and [Betwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betwa_River) meet in the district.

According to the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India) Hamirpur district, Uttar Pradesh has a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_India) of 1,104,285,[^cite_note-districtcensus-1] roughly equal to the nation of [Cyprus](5593.md)[^cite_note-cia-4] or the US state of [Rhode Island](25410.md).[^cite_note-5] This gives it a ranking of 417th in India (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-districtcensus-1] The district has a population density of 268 inhabitants per square kilometre (690/sq mi) .[^cite_note-districtcensus-1] Its [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_India) over the decade 2001-2011 was 5.78%.[^cite_note-districtcensus-1] Hamirpur has a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) of 860 [females](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_India) for every 1000 males,[^cite_note-districtcensus-1] and a [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of 70.16%. 19.00% of the population lives in urban areas. Scheduled Castes make up 21.84% of the population.[^cite_note-districtcensus-1]

### Languages

Languages of Hamirpur district (2011)

[^cite_note-languages-6]

1. [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi) (66.4%)

2. [Bundeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundeli_language) (32.0%)

3. [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu) (1.29%)

4. Others (0.27%)

At the time of the [2011 Census of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Census_of_India), 66.43% of the population in the district spoke [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi), 32.01% [Bundeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundeli_language) and 1.36% [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu) as their first language.[^cite_note-languages-6]

Tongues spoken in Hamirpur include [Bundeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundeli_language), which has a [lexical similarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexical_similarity) of 72-91% with [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi)[^cite_note-bfy-7] (compared to 60% for German and English)[^cite_note-8] and is spoken by about 7,800,000 people in [Bundelkhand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundelkhand).[^cite_note-bfy-7]

## Economy

In 2006 the [Ministry of Panchayati Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Panchayati_Raj) named Hamirpur one of the country's 250 [most backward districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_India) (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-brgf-9] It is one of the 34 districts in Uttar Pradesh currently receiving funds from the [Backward Regions Grant Fund Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backward_Regions_Grant_Fund) (BRGF).[^cite_note-brgf-9]

[Hindustan Unilever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan_Unilever), [J.K. Cements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._K._Organisation), Rimjhim Ispat Limited[^cite_note-10] and Hans Metal Limited[^cite_note-11] have factories in [Sumerpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumerpur,_Uttar_Pradesh) town.[^cite_note-HUL-12][^cite_note-13]
