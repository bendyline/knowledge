The **president of the Republic of Uganda** is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and the [head of government](37570.md) of [Uganda](31816.md). The [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)) leads the [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) of the [government of Uganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Uganda) and is the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Uganda People's Defence Force](56928.md).[^cite_note-Government_of_Uganda-2]

## Background

The office of the president of Uganda was formed on 9th October 1963 to replace the queen of Uganda (which was last held by [Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II)) as head of state. It was entirely a ceremonial role i.e without executive powers during the time of the first holder [Mutesa II of Buganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutesa_II_of_Buganda) until the end of the [Mengo Crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mengo_Crisis) in 1967 when [Milton Obote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_Obote) took over ending the alliance between the [Uganda People's Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uganda_People's_Congress) and the [Kabaka Yekka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabaka_Yekka) parties combining the roles of [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister) and president and therefore creating the first president of Uganda with executive powers. The office has been held by 9 people, 8 of whom (besides Edward Muteesa) came into power through military coups and civil war.&#x20;

The incumbent [Yoweri Museveni](293409.md) came to power in 1986 and is the longest serving president of Uganda, ahead of [Idi Amin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idi_Amin) who ruled from 1971 to 1979.

## Qualifications

In 2005 presidential [term limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_limits) were removed,[^cite_note-termlimits-3] and in 2017, the previous upper age limit of 75 was also removed.[^cite_note-4]

Under Article 102 of the Constitution, in order to be allowed to run for the presidency one must be a citizen of Uganda by birth, be no younger than thirty-five and eligible to be a member of Parliament.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## State House

[State House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_House_\(Uganda\)) is the [official residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_residence) of the president of Uganda. The main State House was constructed in 1925 during the governorship of Sir [Geoffrey Archer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Archer_\(colonial_administrator\)) at [Entebbe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entebbe) the then capital to serve as the official viceregal residence ([Government House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_House)) of the [Governor of Uganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Uganda). After the country gained independence in 1962, the residence continued to be the official residence of the president of the Republic of Uganda. However, it was first inhabited by Sir [William Frederick Gowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Frederick_Gowers), while the second State House is at [Nakasero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakasero).[^cite_note-8]
