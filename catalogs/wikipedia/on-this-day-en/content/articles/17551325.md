# Regions of Taiwan

&#x20; The **regions of [Taiwan](25734.md)** are based on historical administrative divisions. However, most of the definitions are not precise.

## Division into two regions

- **Eastern and Western Taiwan**: the [Central Mountain Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Mountain_Range) separates Taiwan into east and west.

* *Eastern Taiwan*: [Yilan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yilan_County,_Taiwan), [Hualien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hualien_County) and [Taitung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taitung_County).

* *Western Taiwan*: other divisions from [Taipei](57648.md) to [Pingtung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pingtung_County).

- **Northern and Southern Taiwan**: [Zhuoshui River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhuoshui_River), the longest [river of Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_rivers_of_Taiwan), flows through about the middle of the island.

* *Northern Taiwan*: [Taipei](57648.md), [New Taipei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Taipei), [Keelung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keelung), [Taoyuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taoyuan,_Taiwan), [Hsinchu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hsinchu) (City/[County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hsinchu_County)), [Miaoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miaoli_County), [Taichung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taichung), [Changhua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changhua_County), and [Nantou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nantou_County).

* *Southern Taiwan*: [Yunlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yunlin_County), [Chiayi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiayi) (City/[County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiayi_County)), [Tainan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tainan), [Kaohsiung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaohsiung), and [Pingtung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pingtung_County).

## Division into four regions

The most widely used definition is from the [Council for Economic Planning and Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_for_Economic_Planning_and_Development) (經濟建設委員會), [Executive Yuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Yuan). This division into four regions (tetrachotomy) scheme corresponds to the prefectures [under Qing dynasty rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_under_Qing_rule).

## Division into five regions

The scheme of division into five regions (pentachotomy) is a fusion of the tetrachotomy and hexachotomy schemes. Although no specific names are given in each division, it is the most commonly used scheme among the highest divisions of the [central government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Republic_of_China). This scheme is used by the Joint Service Centers (JSC, 區域聯合服務中心) under [Executive Yuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Yuan) (行政院) and the jurisdiction of High Court Branches (高等法院分院) under [Judicial Yuan](378790.md) (司法院).[^cite_note-1]

## Division into six regions



Political division of Taiwan in 1945

The division into six regions (hexachotomy) scheme corresponds to the prefectures [under Japanese rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_under_Japanese_rule). This scheme was used for national electoral districts in the [legislative elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_elections_in_Taiwan) in [1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Taiwan_legislative_election), [1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975_Taiwan_legislative_election), [1980](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980_Taiwan_legislative_election), [1983](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_Taiwan_legislative_election), and [1986](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Taiwan_legislative_election). The discussion of this scheme became popular after the [elections of five new municipalities in 2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Taiwanese_local_elections).[^cite_note-2]
