**Cedarbluff** (or **Cedar Bluff**) is an [unincorporated community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unincorporated_area) in [Clay County, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_County,_Mississippi), United States.[^cite_note-2] It is located in south central Clay County along [Mississippi Highway 50](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_Highway_50).

## History

Cedarbluff is located on the former [Southern Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Railway_\(U.S.\)).[^cite_note-3] Cedarbluff was formerly home to a school and two churches.[^cite_note-4]

A post office operated under the name Cedar Bluff from 1847 to 1895 and began operating under the name Cedarbluff in 1895.[^cite_note-5]

In 1915 an unnamed black man was lynched in Cedarbluff for allegedly entering the room of a white woman.[^cite_note-6] In 1916, an African-American man, Jeff Brown was [lynched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynching_in_the_United_States) by a mob "for accidentally bumping into a white girl as he ran to catch a train." Pictures of his lynching were sold to white citizens for five cents each and were used to intimidate African-Americans in the region.[^cite_note-lynching13-7]

In 1920, a destructive tornado struck near Cedarbluff, causing major damage and slaying 10.[^cite_note-8]

On January 9, 2026, a [mass shooting](31883778.md) at three different locations in the area of Cedarbluff killed six people, including a 7-year-old child.[^cite_note-\:0-9] A man was arrested and charged with multiple counts of murder as well as attempted murder, attempted sexual battery, burglary and vehicle theft.[^cite_note-10] Four of the victims were his family members.[^cite_note-\:0-9]
