The **States of [South Sudan](32350676.md)** were created out of the three historic former provinces (and contemporary regions) of [Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahr_el_Ghazal_\(region_of_South_Sudan\)) (northwest), [Equatoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equatoria) (southern), and [Greater Upper Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Upper_Nile) (northeast). The states are further divided into [79 counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_South_Sudan).

In October 2015, South Sudan's President [Salva Kiir Mayardit](2363203.md) issued a decree establishing 28 states in place of the 10 that was previously established.[^cite_note-1] The decree established the new states largely along ethnic lines. A number of opposition parties challenged the constitutionality of this decree and the decree was referred to parliament for approval as a constitutional amendment.[^cite_note-2] In November, the South Sudanese parliament approved the creation of the new states.[^cite_note-3] In January 2017, President Salva Kiir stated a decreed of further subdivision of the country from 28 into 32 states.[^cite_note-4]

In February 2020, as a result of a peace agreement that ended the [South Sudanese Civil War](41382446.md), the country returned to the original 10 states plus two administrative areas, [Greater Pibor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Pibor_Administrative_Area) and [Ruweng](49119309.md), and the special administrative status area of [Abyei](13885196.md).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

As a result of the [Comprehensive Peace Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comprehensive_Peace_Agreement) signed in 2005, the [Abyei Area](13885196.md) is considered to be simultaneously part of the Republic of Sudan and the Republic of South Sudan, effectively a [condominium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condominium_\(international_law\)). The [Kafia Kingi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kafia_Kingi) area is disputed between South Sudan and Sudan and the [Ilemi Triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilemi_Triangle) is disputed between South Sudan and Kenya.

## Ten states and three areas (2020–present)



The administrative subdivisions of South Sudan grouped in the three historical provinces of:&#x20;

&#x20; [Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahr_el_Ghazal_\(region_of_South_Sudan\))

&#x20; [Greater Upper Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Upper_Nile)

Under the terms of a peace agreement signed on 22 February 2020, South Sudan is divided into ten states, two administrative areas and one area with special administrative status.[^cite_note-8] The states and administrative areas are grouped into the three historical regions of the Sudan: [Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahr_el_Ghazal_\(region_of_South_Sudan\)), [Equatoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equatoria), [Greater Upper Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Upper_Nile). Each state is headed by a [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_current_state_governors_in_South_Sudan) and administrative areas are led by Chief Administrators.

## 32 states (2017–2020)



The 32 states of South Sudan grouped in the three historical provinces of the Sudan&#x20;

&#x20; [Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahr_el_Ghazal_\(region_of_South_Sudan\))

&#x20; [Equatoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equatoria)

&#x20; [Greater Upper Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Upper_Nile)

On 14 January 2017, President Kiir issued a presidential decree that increased the number of federal states from 28 to 32.

- The now defunct *Eastern Nile State* was split into 2 individual states, [Central Upper Nile State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Upper_Nile_State) with [Malakal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malakal) as its capital city and [Northern Upper Nile State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Upper_Nile_State) with [Renk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renk,_South_Sudan) as its capital city.

- [Akobo State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akobo_State) was formed and split from [Bieh State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bieh_State), with [Akobo](21081734.md) as its capital. It comprises Akobo county in the *Greater Upper Nile Region*.

- [Maiwut State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maiwut_State) was formed and split from [Latjoor State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latjoor_State) in the *Greater Upper Nile Region* retaining Koma, Longuchuk, and Maiwut counties.

- [Tambura State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambura_State) was formed and split from [Gbudwe State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gbudwe_State), with Tambura as its capital. It comprises Tambura and Nagero counties in the *Equatoria Region*.

## SPLM-IO declaration



21 States of South Sudan as declared by the SPLM-IO

On 22 December 2014, leader of the [SPLM-IO](42792943.md) and former vice president [Riek Machar](2407248.md) declared the 10 states of South Sudan dissolved and the formation of 21 new states in a federal system. The declaration was not recognised by the South Sudanese government.[^cite_note-26] The *[Sudan Tribune](6227213.md)* reported on 1 January 2015 that Machar appointed "military governors" for several of his declared states.[^cite_note-27] These states became defunct when the SPLM-IO joined the unity government formed by the [R-ARCSS](41382446.md#2018_peace_agreement_\(2018%E2%80%932020\)) in February 2020.
