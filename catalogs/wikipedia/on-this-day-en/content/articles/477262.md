The **MTV Video Music Award for Best Pop** is given at the annual [MTV Video Music Awards](295217.md). The category was first presented in [1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) under the name of **Best Pop Video**, as MTV began to put several [teen pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_pop) acts in heavy rotation. Nominations, however, were not just limited to pop acts, as dance, R\&B, pop/rock, and reggaeton artists have also received nominations throughout the award's history.&#x20;

In [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), MTV eliminated this award along with all of the genre categories, but it returned in 2008. From [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), the word "Video" was removed from the names of all genre categories, leaving this award with its current name: **Best Pop**. In [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), the nominees were based on the artists, instead of music videos.

[Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears), [Taylor Swift](5422144.md), and [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande) have received the most wins in this category, winning three awards each. [NSYNC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSYNC), Spears, and Swift are the only acts to win the award for two consecutive years. Grande has received the most nominations in this category with eight.

## Recipients



[Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears) ties for the record for the most wins (3).



[Taylor Swift](5422144.md) ties for the record for the most wins (3).



[Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande) ties record for the most wins (3) and holds the record for nominations (8).
