The **members of the 25th Knesset** were [elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Israeli_legislative_election) on 1 November 2022[^cite_note-1] and sworn in on 15 November.[^cite_note-2] As of September 2026, this list reflects the current composition of the Knesset, incorporating changes resulting from resignations, replacements under the [Norwegian Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Law_\(Israel\)), and other personnel shifts since the election.

## Composition

Three [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze_in_Israel) lawmakers, 29 women, 23 new [MKs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_Knesset_members) and three openly gay MKs were elected to the 25th [Knesset](74656.md).[^cite_note-3] The number of [Arab](492331.md) MKs from Arab parties was the lowest in two decades, with 10 MKs.[^cite_note-AP-4]

On 30 June 2024, the [Israeli Labor Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Labor_Party) announced plans to merge with [Meretz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meretz) to become [The Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Democrats_\(Israel\)),[^cite_note-haaretz-hebrew-5] with Labor MKs expected to become MKs for the new party; the merger was approved on 12 July.[^cite_note-tjp12july-6] However, Likud MK and chair of the Knesset House Committee [Ofir Katz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ofir_Katz) denied the February 2025 request by Labor chairwoman [Efrat Rayten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Efrat_Rayten) to change the name of the Knesset faction to The Democrats and she withdrew it.[^cite_note-tjp25feb-7]

On 11 November 2024 [Hadash-Ta'al](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadash-Ta'al) MK [Ofer Cassif](60517491.md) was suspended from the Knesset for six months for supporting [South Africa's genocide case against Israel](75736183.md).[^cite_note-8]
