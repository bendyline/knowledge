**Titan-Barrikady** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Титан-Баррикады) is a [military-industrial company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_industry_of_Russia) based in [Volgograd](32585.md), Russia. It was formed in 2014, after the merger between the [Barrikady Production Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrikady_Production_Association) and the [Titan Design Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titan_Design_Bureau).[^cite_note-titanbarrmerge-1] It is a subsidiary of the [Moscow Institute of Thermal Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Thermal_Technology).[^cite_note-edisc-2]

## History

The plant was established in 1914 as the Tsaritsyn Weapons Factory, and renamed Red Barricades after the Russian Revolution.[^cite_note-hellbeck-3] It was for a time the largest [munition](18940621.md) factory in Europe.[^cite_note-hellbeck-3] Titan had its origins as the factory's design bureau.[^cite_note-rbthhisttitan-4]

The factory was overrun and destroyed by the Germans in 1942 during the [Battle of Stalingrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Stalingrad), but resumed production in 1944.[^cite_note-rbthhisttitan-4]

In 1975 the plant was selected to manufacture the gun and mount for the [2S7 Pion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2S7_Pion).[^cite_note-am2s7-5]

The Titan Design Bureau was separated from the factory in 1990.[^cite_note-rbthhisttitan-4]

In 2014 the Titan Design Bureau and the Barrikady factory were merged again.[^cite_note-titanbarrmerge-1]

The "Joint Stock Company Federal Scientific and Production Center Titan Barrikady" was sanctioned by various countries including the United States ([OFAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OFAC)), EU, UK, Switzerland, Canada, Australia, Japan, and Ukraine, for materially supporting Russia's military.[^cite_note-opensanctions-titan-barrikady-6]

## Barrikady plant



Satellite imagery of Titan-Barrikady plant

Barrikady is a major manufacturer of heavy machinery and large steel castings and forgings. Its manufacturing facilities include the [Barrikady Drilling Equipment Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrikady_Drilling_Equipment_Plant?action=edit\&redlink=1), one of two large producers of oil drilling rigs in Russia (the other being [Uralmash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uralmash) in [Yekaterinburg](66524.md)).[^cite_note-rdbd2725-7]

Barrikady assembles mobile launchers for ballistic missiles and artillery pieces. Barrikady is located near the large [Krasny Oktyabr Steel Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krasny_Oktyabr_Steel_Plant).[^cite_note-rdbd2725-7]

In July 2019, the Barrikady plant assembled a new, fully low floor tram of the model 71-142.[^cite_note-8]

On June 27, 2026, [Ukrainian National News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_National_News) reported that [FP-5 Flamingo](80778495.md) missiles were used in a strike against the plant. OSINT reporters placed three hits on the plant.[^cite_note-unn-flamingo-volgograd-20260627-9]
