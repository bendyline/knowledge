**Djugu territory** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Territoire de Djugu*) is a district of [Ituri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ituri), [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md).[^cite_note-3] Its capital is also named [Djugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djugu).

## History

[Armed conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_conflict) first appeared in Djugu in December 2017, resulting in the [displacement](427408.md) of 20,000 people.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Since then 4,000 people have been killed by various armed organizations, including [CODECO](65758310.md).[^cite_note-7]



[MONUSCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MONUSCO) troops in Kobu, 2021.

In 2021, during an [ongoing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021-2022_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo_attacks) [insurgency](589841.md) by [Islamist](15012.md) rebels, Djugu fell into a [humanitarian crisis](1251700.md). Since November 2021, rebels have attacked several separate IDP sites, including Drodro, a camp for internally [displaced people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Displaced_people),[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] resulting in heavy casualties. At least 58 [civilians](145804.md) were killed since October.[^cite_note-\:0-10] On 2 February 2022, a CODECO attack [killed over 60 people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaine_Savo_massacre). A few weeks later, CODECO slew 18 people in the village of Banyali Kilo.[^cite_note-11]

600,000 people living in Djugu territory are IDPs, 85,000 of whom became displaced in 2021.[^cite_note-\:0-10]

In March 2023, CODECO abducted and killed 17 people who were driving on a road in Djugu.[^cite_note-12] On February 14th 2024, CODECO killed at least 12 people, and kidnapped another 16 near a gold mine in Djugu.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] Later in the same week on February 17th, another 15 people were killed on a road near the village of Tali.[^cite_note-Barrons2024-1]
