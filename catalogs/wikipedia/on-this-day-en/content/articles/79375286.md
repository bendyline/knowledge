**Sudan Shield Forces** (*SSF*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): قوات درع السودان, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Qūwāt Dirʻ al-Sūdān*) is an armed group which was created in 2022, and is led by [Abu Aqla Kaikal](78338174.md). Sudan Shield Forces is fighting alongside the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) (SAF), in the current [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md).

## History

Sudan Shield Forces was created by Kaikal in 2022, in the state of [Al-Gadarif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Gadarif).[^cite_note-3] According to Kaikal the creation of the group was "to maintain security and restore strategic balance in the country, in light of the [Juba Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juba_Peace_Agreement)".[^cite_note-4] SSF fighters were mainly recruited from Arab communities in [Gezira State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gezira_State).[^cite_note-Keikal-5]

### Civil war

After the current civil war started Kaikal first sided with SAF but in August 2023 it stopped co-operation and defected to the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) (RSF).[^cite_note-Keikal-5] SSF took part in the [First battle of Wad Manadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Wad_Madani).[^cite_note-6] The group was reportedly in "full control" of Gezira state by December 2023.[^cite_note-7] In October of 2024 Kaikal and his forces defected back to the SAF, which lead to a series of [violent retaliatory attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_eastern_Gezira_State_massacres) by the RSF on civilians in Gezira State.[^cite_note-8] SSF participated in the [Second battle of Wad Madani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Wad_Madani) and with SAF they retook the town in 11 of January, 2025.[^cite_note-9]

## War crimes

### El-Gezira

During the RSF occupation of Gezira state, SSF forces were accused of [war crimes](48626.md) including extajudicial killings of civilians, looting and rape.[^cite_note-10]

### Gezira State canal killings

Further information: [Gezira State canal killings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gezira_State_canal_killings)

According to the investigation published by [CNN](62028.md) and [Lighthouse Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lighthouse_Reports), after taking control of Wad Manadi in January 2025, SAF and SSF forces were connected to attacks against civilians, especially of the Kanabi ethnic group.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] [Jaafar Mohamedein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaafar_Mohamedein?action=edit\&redlink=1), the Secretary-General of the Kanabi Congress accused SSF of horrific acts such as “raping women, killing men, and burning children".[^cite_note-13]
