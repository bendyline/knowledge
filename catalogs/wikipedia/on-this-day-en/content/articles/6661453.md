**Šolta** (pronounced [\[ʃɔ̂ːlta\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Serbo-Croatian); [Italian](14708.md): *Solta*; [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_language): *Solentium*) is an [island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island) in [Croatia](5573.md). It is situated in the [Adriatic Sea](23275478.md) in the central [Dalmatian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalmatia) [archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archipelago).

## Geography

Šolta is located west of the island of [Brač](226332.md), south of [Split](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split_\(city\)) (separated by [Split Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split_Channel)) and east of the Drvenik islands, [Drvenik Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drvenik_Mali) and [Drvenik Veli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drvenik_Veli) (separated by the [Šolta Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%A0olta_Channel?action=edit\&redlink=1)).[^cite_note-kroatien-online-2] Its area is 58.98 square kilometres (22.77 mi<sup>2</sup>).[^cite_note-Duplancic-Cala-Ujevic-3][^cite_note-yearbook-4]

## Island morphology

The highest peak of Šolta is the summit Vela Straža[^cite_note-JLZ_8-6] which is 236 metres high.[^cite_note-yearbook-4] On the north-eastern coast of the island there are the large [bays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay) of Rogač and Nečujam. In the western part of the interior of Šolta there is a [field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_\(agriculture\)) approximately 6 km (4 mi) long and 2 km (1 mi) wide.

## Economy

The economy of the island is based on [vineyards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vineyard), [olives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive), [fruit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit), [fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing) and [tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism).[^cite_note-kroatien-online-2][^cite_note-JLZ_8-6] While the main settlements ([Grohote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grohote), [Gornje Selo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gornje_Selo,_Split-Dalmatia_County), [Srednje Selo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srednje_Selo,_Split-Dalmatia_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Donje Selo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donje_Selo,_Split-Dalmatia_County?action=edit\&redlink=1)) are inland, the main fishing centres are [Maslinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maslinica),[^cite_note-JLZ_8-6] which is exposed to only north-westerly winds, making it a good shelter for smaller boats.[^cite_note-kroatien-online-2] [Rogač](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roga%C4%8D) is the main port and [Nečujam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ne%C4%8Dujam) the centre of the island's tourism.

### Viticulture - Dobričic ancestor of Zinfandel

[Viticulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viticulture) exists on the island since antiquity. The most famous autochthonous red wine variety is the [Dobričić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dobri%C4%8Di%C4%87). According to DNA analysis, this variety is one of the original varieties of the American [Zinfandel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinfandel) along with the [Kaštelanski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ka%C5%A1telanski?action=edit\&redlink=1) from the Split area.[^cite_note-7] The American wine pioneer [Mike Grgich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Grgich) from Croatia brought the varieties to California. The Split noble family of the father of Croatian literature, [Marko Marulić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marko_Maruli%C4%87) (1450-1524), who also lived on Šolta, were among the first to cultivate the Dobričic intensively. In the 19th century viticulture in Šolta suffered severe setbacks. A trade agreement between Austria and Hungary with Italy allowed low-cost Italian wines to be imported duty-free. Diseases such as [downy mildew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downy_mildew) and [phylloxera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phylloxera) reduced the stocks. The best qualities of national importance were created in the 1870s and 80s by Pietro degli Alberti from Porto Oliveto di Solta (Maslinica). At the Vienna World Fair in 1873 he received an honorary prize.[^cite_note-8] At the Triester exhibition in 1882 he received a gold medal.[^cite_note-9] Even in the [interwar period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interwar_period) there was intensive viticulture.[^cite_note-10] As a result of the [Titoism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titoism) [command economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Command_economy), there is still no large wine industry upon the island. The cultivation is almost only for the own use. Some smaller winemakers, e.g. in Donje Selo, sell wine directly. It is also available at the market in Grohote. A large [vineyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vineyard) (on state leasehold land) was laid out for the first time again around 2015 on a hill on the road to Maslinica.

## History

During the time of the [Austro-Hungarian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) the villages still have their [Italian](14708.md) names. *Villa Grohote*, *Villa Inf.* short for *Villa Inferior* (Donje Selo) with the harbor *Porto Olivetto* (Maslinica), *Villa Media* (Srednje Selo) and *Villa Superior* (Gornje Selo) with the harbor *Stomosca* (Stomorska).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] The property tax structure of the Austrian-Hungarian administration was based on "Fractionen". The local parish of Šolta was divided into the [cadastral community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadastral_community) Grohote, Donje-Selo, Gornje-Selo and Srednje-Selo, i.e. under the Croatian place names.[^cite_note-13] However, Šolta was no longer fully recorded in the [land register](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_register) by the Austrian administration. For parts of the island there is only the [cadastre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadastre), i.e. site plans of the real estate, in which ownership and usage rights are not directly registered. This makes real estate transactions sometimes difficult.

The [Telegraph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telegraphy) arrived late at Šolta. In 1874, the k.k. State telegraph station Grohote handed over to the operation at "limited daily services". That was at the same time as in other smaller places of the monarchy as for example in the [Carinthian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carinthia) [Sankt Paul im Lavanttal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sankt_Paul_im_Lavanttal) or in Pontafel [Pontafel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontebba).[^cite_note-14]

In April 1941, Šolta came under [Axis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axis_powers) occupation, first by Italy. [Nazi Germany](21212.md) took over after the [September 1943 Armistice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armistice_of_Cassibile). In March and April 1944, Šolta was the site of two British-American [commando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commando) raids ten days apart which took many Nazi prisoners and destroyed numerous installations.[^cite_note-15]
