The **Australia national speedway team** is one of the leading teams in international [motorcycle speedway](230182.md), with the country regarded as the birthplace of the sport in the 1920s. The team are six-times champions of the world having won the sports premier team competition in [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_Speedway_World_Team_Cup), [1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_Speedway_World_Team_Cup), [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Speedway_World_Cup), [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Speedway_World_Cup), [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Speedway_of_Nations) and [2025](78640169.md).[^cite_note-1]&#x20;

## History

The national speedway team held test matches against the [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_speedway_team) as early as 1928, although the first official test match is listed as 30 June 1930 at [Wimbledon Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wimbledon_Stadium).[^cite_note-2]&#x20;

The team won its first [Speedway World Team Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedway_World_Team_Cup) in 1976, with captain [John Boulger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Boulger), [Phil Crump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Crump), [Billy Sanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Sanders) and [Phil Herne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Herne).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] They had to wait over 30 years before winning three tournaments out of four, between 1999 and 2002. Key riders during the period were [Jason Crump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Crump), [Leigh Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leigh_Adams), [Ryan Sullivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Sullivan) and [Todd Wiltshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Wiltshire), who all took part in three wins, while [Jason Lyons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Lyons) rode in two (1999 and 2002) and [Craig Boyce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_Boyce) rode in 2001.[^cite_note-6]&#x20;

The fifth and sixth successes came in the [2022 Speedway of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Speedway_of_Nations) and the [2025 Speedway of Nations](78640169.md) respectively (the alternate competition to the World Cup) with the squad members being [Jack Holder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Holder), [Brady Kurtz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brady_Kurtz), [Max Fricke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Fricke) and [Jason Doyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Doyle).[^cite_note-7]&#x20;

Four times [Australian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Individual_Speedway_Championship) champion [Jim Airey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Airey) is the only Australian rider to win the World Team Cup not riding for Australia. He won the [1971 Speedway World Team Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1971_Speedway_World_Team_Cup) in Wrocław, Poland, as part of the [Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain_national_speedway_team) team.&#x20;
