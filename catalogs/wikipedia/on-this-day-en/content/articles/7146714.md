**Jablonov nad Turňou** ([Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_language): *Szádalmás*) is a [village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village) and [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality) in the [Rožňava District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ro%C5%BE%C5%88ava_District) in the [Košice Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ko%C5%A1ice_Region) of middle-eastern [Slovakia](26830.md).

## History

In [historical records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History) the village was first mentioned in 1235. Before the establishment of independent [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia) in 1918, Jablonov nad Turňou was part of [Abaúj-Torna County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aba%C3%BAj-Torna_County) within the [Kingdom of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hungary). From 1938 to 1945, it was again part of [Hungary](13275.md) as a result of the [First Vienna Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Vienna_Award).

## Geography

The [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality) lies at an [altitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altitude) of 227 metres (745 ft)[^cite_note-base_info-2] and covers an [area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area) of 24.46 km<sup>2</sup> (9.44 sq mi) (2025).[^cite_note-M-SK-A-1]

## Facilities

The village has a [pharmacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmacy) and a doctors surgery. It also has a public [library](17727.md) and a [football pitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football_pitch).
