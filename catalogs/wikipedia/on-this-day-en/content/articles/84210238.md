On 11 September 2026, a passenger train derailed at [Cléon](16037809.md), [Seine-Maritime](85874.md), [France](5843419.md). Forty-four people were injured. The cause is suspected to be [sabotage](29462.md).

## Derailment

At about 19\:25 [CEST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Summer_Time) (16\:25 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)) on 11 September 2026, a passenger train from [Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouen-Rive-Droite_station) to [Caen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caen_station) derailed at [Cléon](16037809.md), [Seine-Maritime](85874.md), [France](5843419.md).[^cite_note-BP3318495-1] The train was formed of two [SNCF X 76500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNCF_X_76500?action=edit\&redlink=1) [diesel multiple units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel_multiple_units), 76577 and 76578.[^cite_note-SudOuest120926-2][^cite_note-FranceInfo818974-3] One carriage was reported to have overturned and four more were derailed.[^cite_note-Telegraph120926-4] Of the 186 passengers on board,[^cite_note-BP3318495-1] 44 were injured, one critically.[^cite_note-BBCc8d37dm8d8mo-5] The train was operated by [TER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_express_r%C3%A9gional).[^cite_note-LaSicilia3072621-6] It was reported to have struck an object on the line before it derailed.[^cite_note-Independentb3048993-7]

The local authorities, notability the [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Regional_Council_\(France\)) of the [Regional Council of Normandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Council_of_Normandy), [Hervé Morin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herv%C3%A9_Morin) announced that the suspected object that it struck was a suspected 300 kilograms (660 lb) piece of rail.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Hervé Morin in an interview with French newspaper [Ouest-France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouest-France) said that the metal bar didn't arrive on the rails "by accident".[^cite_note-10]

## Aftermath

Authorities activated a major casualty response plan. More than 140 emergency service workers were dispatched to the scene. [SNCF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNCF) stated that there was major disruption in the area and buses would replace trains.[^cite_note-BBCc8d37dm8d8mo-5] The lines between [Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouen) and [Caen](92443.md), and between Caen and [Le Havre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Havre_station) were closed.[^cite_note-Independentb3048993-7] The train driver was tested for drugs and alcohol after the crash. The results were negative. Police stated that [sabotage](29462.md) was suspected to be the cause of the derailment.[^cite_note-BP3318495-1]

## Investigation

The [Bureau d'Enquêtes sur les Accidents de Transport Terrestre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_d'Enqu%C3%AAtes_sur_les_Accidents_de_Transport_Terrestre) is responsible for investigating rail accidents in France.[^cite_note-BEA-TT-11] They opened an investigation into the derailment.[^cite_note-Open-12]
