**Pomas** (French pronunciation: [\[pɔmas\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French), [\[pɔmɑ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French); [Occitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occitan_language): *Pomars*, [\[ˈpumaɾs\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Occitan)) is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in the [French department](38523.md) of [Aude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aude), and the [region](58846.md) of [Occitania](45093609.md), [southern France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_France). Located 4 km northwest of [Saint-Hilaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Hilaire,_Aude), 15 km south of [Carcassonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carcassonne), 100 km southeast of [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse).

## History

Pomas dates back to the 9th century, when it was known as Pomaris.[^cite_note-3]

In 1212, the local lord, Bernard de Pomars, a [Cathar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathar), was denounced as a heretic; his lands were taken from him and given to Philippe Golhoin. In 1559, the Rabot family took over the lordship of Pomas. The lordship was sold off in 1719.

On 24 August 2026, a large and intense tornado struck Pomas, leaving 300 homes damaged or almost entirely destroyed. Over 2 thousand houses were left without electricity for a day and a half. A castle was partly damaged as it was struck by the tornado, over 1 thousand people were in critical need. 41 people were injured, with 13 being evacuated to hospitals. 3 people are missing, likely stuck under debris. The tornado crossed the entirety of Pomas, later dissipating after a multiple kilometer path.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-ESWD-5]

## Attractions

The château de Pomas dates back to the 12th century and is notable for its square tower and cruciform archers' windows.

Pomas has a small single track [railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gare_de_Pomas) on the [Carcassonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carcassonne) - [Rivesaltes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivesaltes) railway line, between [Verzeille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verzeille) and [Limoux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limoux).

Shops include a bakery, a specialist local wine shop, and a tobacconist. It has a small private museum specialising in exotic shells.[^cite_note-6]

There are attractive walking routes in the hills around Pomas.[^cite_note-7]
