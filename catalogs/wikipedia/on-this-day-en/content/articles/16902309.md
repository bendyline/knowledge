The **Equatorial Guinean Council of Ministers** or Council of Ministers of Equatorial Guinea ([Spanish](26825.md): *Consejo de Ministros*), is chaired by the President of Equatorial Guinea [Teodoro Obiang Nguema](263622.md), and constituted by the [Vice President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Equatorial_Guinea) [Teodorín Nguema Obiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teodoro_Nguema_Obiang_Mangue), his Prime Minister [Manuel Osa Nsue Nsua](77653363.md) along with the other Ministers members of the government.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-\:0-2]

According to the constitution, the council is in charge of assisting the president with the execution of the national policy and his political and administrative duties.[^cite_note-constitution-3]
