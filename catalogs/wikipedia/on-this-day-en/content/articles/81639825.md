# Curaçao at the FIFA World Cup

&#x20;[Curaçao](24862626.md) appeared in the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) for the first time in [2026](36581929.md).[^cite_note-1] On 18 November 2025, they topped their qualifying group and qualified for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md), becoming the smallest nation by both population and area to qualify for the tournament.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-cbc-4]

## Background

Curaçao is a constituent country of the Kingdom of the Netherlands with a population of about 156,000.[^cite_note-cbc-4] Its national team is the sporting successor to the [Netherlands Antilles national football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Antilles_national_football_team), whose records and FIFA ranking it inherited before adopting the Curaçao name in 2011.[^cite_note-5] The team reached the World Cup largely through players of Curaçaoan descent who were born and developed in the Netherlands, an approach that broadened under former Netherlands striker [Patrick Kluivert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Kluivert) from 2015. Of the 26 players named for the 2026 finals, 25 were born in the Netherlands.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## 2026 FIFA World Cup

Main article: [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md)

Curaçao were drawn in [Group E](81660195.md) alongside Germany, Ecuador and Ivory Coast, with their matches staged in Houston, Kansas City and Philadelphia.[^cite_note-8] At the age of 78, Advocaat became the oldest manager to take charge of a match at a World Cup when the team opened against Germany.[^cite_note-9]
