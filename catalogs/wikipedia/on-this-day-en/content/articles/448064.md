**Hormozgan province** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): استان هرمزگان)[^cite_note-4] is one of the 31 [provinces of Iran](448160.md). Its capital is the city of [Bandar Abbas](464348.md).[^cite_note-Hormozgan_Province_Structure-5]

The province is in the south of the country, in Iran's [Region 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Iran)[^cite_note-6] facing [Oman](22316.md), the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md), and the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md). Its area is 70,697 km<sup>2</sup> (27,296 sq mi).[^cite_note-7] The province has 14 islands in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md) and 1,000 km (620 mi) of coastline.

## History

Although Hormozgan is known to have been settled during the [Achaemenid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaemenid) era when [Nearchus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nearchus) passed through the region, recorded history of the main [port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port) of Hormozgan (Bandar‑e Hormoz) begins with [Ardashir I of Persia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardashir_I_of_Persia) of the [Sassanid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sassanid) empire.

The province is said to have been particularly prosperous between 241 BC and 211 BC, but retained a lack of significance with the beginning of the Islamic era.

[Marco Polo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Polo) visited the port of [Bandar Abbas](464348.md) in 1272 and 1293. He reported trading in Persian jewelry, [ivory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory) and [silk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silk) of [Indochina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indochina), and [pearls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl) from [Bahrain](18933277.md) in the bazaar of the port of Hormuz.

In 1497 Europeans landed in the region for the first time, headed by [Vasco da Gama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasco_da_Gama). In 1508 the [Portuguese](23033.md), led by [Afonso de Albuquerque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afonso_de_Albuquerque) anchored in the area with seven warships, as part of protecting their interests in [Egypt](8087628.md) and [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice). The fishing port of Hormuz at the time was considered strategically positioned for commercial interests in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md).

[Ismail I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismail_I) who was trying to counter the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md) to the west, was unable to save the port from the Portuguese, until [Shah Abbas I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_I_of_Safavid) was finally able to drive them out of the Persian Gulf with the aid of the British. The name of [Bandar Abbas](464348.md) comes directly from the name of Shah [Abbas I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_the_Great).

The British, meanwhile, were competing for influence in the region with Dutch colonialists, who developed [Qeshm Island](2252168.md) and dispatched warships to [Bandar Abbas](464348.md) to get wet during the final years of Shah Abbas' reign. The British government was unable to defend itself against this attack. With the souring of British and Dutch relations, military tensions grew further in the region. The Dutch finally resorted to moving their base up to [Kharg Island](2210066.md).

The [Amir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir) of [Kharg](2210066.md), Mir Mahna Baloch and [Mir Hammal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammal_Jiand) [Kalmati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalmati) with [Baloch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baloch_people) army expelled the Europeans from [Bander Abbas](464348.md) to [Karachi](17123.md),[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] so with the Dutch and other forces at Kharg, the British were firmly in charge of the entire region. Soon Britain took control over the entire Persian Gulf via the British [East India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_Company). The British adopted policy encouraging local autonomy throughout the Persian Gulf to in order to prevent a formidable unified force from threatening their establishments in the gulf. The Omani Emirate of Hormuz later joined a federated Persia.

The strategic importance of the Persian Gulf further increased after [World War I](4764461.md) with the discovery of oil in the region.

### Languages

Hormozgan is dominated by a variety of Persian language. Northwest Iranian [Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language) is present as well. [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic), a [Semitic language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semitic_languages), and [Kholosi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kholosi_language), an [Indic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Aryan_languages) language, are also found among a minority of speakers within the province.

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the province's population was 136,537 people in 30,332 households.[^cite_note-2006_Hormozgan_Province-11] There were 157,818 inhabitants in 39,692 households at the following census of 2011.[^cite_note-2011_Hormozgan_Province-12] The 2016 census measured the population of the province as 177,641 in 49,366 households.[^cite_note-2016_Hormozgan_Province-2]

## Geography and climate

The province is primarily mountainous, consisting of the southern tip of the [Zagros Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Range). The province experiences a very hot and humid climate, with temperatures sometimes exceeding 120 °F (49 °C) in summers. There is very little precipitation year-round.

## Hormozgan today

Hormozgan today has 11 ports, five air strips, and three national airports. The province has an active agriculture sector, ranking first in Iran in [lime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lime_\(fruit\)) production and second in [date](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Date_Palm) production. 30% of Iran's fishery produce comes from this province. Three major hydro [dams](51518.md) serve the water needs of the province – Esteghlal Dam (i.e., Minab Dam, which supplies major part of consuming water of the Bandar Abbas), Jegin Dam, and Shemil Dam.

Germany has recently offered to build a bridge that would connect Qeshm island to the mainland.

Hormozgan has two [free trade zones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_trade_zone), one in [Kish](847277.md), the other on [Qeshm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qeshm) island. Kish Island, situated in a [free-trade zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free-trade_zone), is home of the [Iranian oil bourse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_oil_bourse) (one of five exchanges of its kind in the world, and the only one explicitly not trading oil and derivatives in [U.S. dollars](18717338.md)).

On July 18, 2026, a United States airstrike struck a water desalination plant in the village of Bunji, Hormozgan province. The attack disabled the facility, cutting off the drinking water supply to twenty nearby villages and leaving an estimated 10,000 residents without access to potable water, according to Iranian provincial officials.[^cite_note-17]
