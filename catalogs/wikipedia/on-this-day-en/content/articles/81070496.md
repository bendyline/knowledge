The **54th [World Rowing Championships](1148937.md)** were held from 24 to 30 August 2026 in [Amsterdam](844.md), Netherlands, under the organization of [World Rowing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Rowing) and the [Royal Dutch Rowing Federation](36763547.md) (KNRB).[^cite_note-worldrowing-2025-07-10-1][^cite_note-2]

The event took place at the [Bosbaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosbaan), the venue that had previously hosted the World Rowing Championships of [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_World_Rowing_Championships) and [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_World_Rowing_Championships).[^cite_note-3] The Bosbaan underwent extensive renovations in order to be ready for the 2026 championship.[^cite_note-worldrowing-2025-07-10-1]

Unlike all previous World Rowing Championships, the event had a dual leadership. Former Olympic champions [Ilse Paulis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilse_Paulis) and [Nico Rienks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_Rienks) jointly took on the role of Regatta Director.[^cite_note-worldrowing-2025-07-10-1]
