**Ministry of Health of the Republic of Poland** ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *Ministerstwo Zdrowia Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej*) is one of the [ministries of the Republic of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministries_of_Poland). It is responsible for overseeing the organization of the [healthcare system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_care_in_Poland), supervision of medical services, health promotion, and disease prevention. The Ministry develops legal regulations, coordinates healthcare financing, and supervises the [National Health Fund (NFZ)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Health_Fund). It is also in charge of reimbursement of medicines, medical devices, and foodstuffs for special medical purposes; oversight of medical professions; development and implementation of public health programs; organization and supervision of the State Medical Emergency System; and activities related to health resort treatment.[^cite_note-1]

## Headquarters

Main article: [Pac Palace, Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pac_Palace,_Warsaw)

The seat of the Ministry of Health is the [Pac Palace in Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pac_Palace,_Warsaw). It was rebuilt between 1948 and 1951 according to the design of Czesław Konopka and Henryk Białobrzeski, with the elevation from the courtyard being rebuilt according to the design of Marconi, and the garden elevation according to the modified original Baroque design of [Tylman van Gameren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tylman_van_Gameren).

## Air Ambulance

Further information: [Polish Medical Air Rescue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Medical_Air_Rescue)

The [Polish Medical Air Rescue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Medical_Air_Rescue) (Polish: *Lotnicze Pogotowie Ratunkowe*, lit. 'Aerial Emergency Medical Service', *LPR*) is an air ambulance service subordinate to the Ministry of Health of Poland. Its fleet includes 27 [EC135](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocopter_EC135) helicopters, and 2 [Piaggio P.180 Avanti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piaggio_P.180_Avanti) fixed wing airplanes, which operate out of 22 locations throughout Poland.
