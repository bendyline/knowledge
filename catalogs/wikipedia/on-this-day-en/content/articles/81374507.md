**Diana Germanovna Loginova** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Диа́на Ге́рмановна Ло́гинова; born 10 March 2007) is a Russian singer performing under the stage name **Naoko**[^cite_note-\:5-2][^cite_note-\:ins-3] and frontwoman of the band *[Stoptime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoptime?action=edit\&redlink=1)*.[^cite_note-\:5-2] She gained her greatest fame in October 2025, when she was arrested for performing songs by artists banned in Russia on the streets of [St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-sv-9][^cite_note-10]

## Biography

Her mother's name is Irina.[^cite_note-male-11][^cite_note-12] Diana has been involved in music since childhood.[^cite_note-mr7-13] She is a piano student at the [Rimsky-Korsakov College of Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rimsky-Korsakov_College_of_Music?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-ria-14] Diana has won numerous prestigious awards — 2024, she won the Polyphonics International Competition of Contemporary Sacred and Polyphonic Music Performers, and 2025, she won a prize at the Russian Student Spring All-Russian Festival.[^cite_note-ria-14][^cite_note-ekho-15] Loginova previously performed as part of the band *NeXti*.[^cite_note-mr7-13]

Diana lives in the urban-type settlement [Rakhya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rakhya).[^cite_note-ria-14]

Loginova and the guitarist from the *Stoptime* band, Alexander Orlov, got engaged while in police custody. Alexander proposed to Diana in a police van shortly after their initial arrest in St. Petersburg on 17 October 2025. He made a makeshift engagement ring out of a napkin from a cigarette box.[^cite_note-nge-16]

## Arrest and criminal case



Poster (2025)

In October 2025, a video of the St. Petersburg street band *Stoptime* performing [Noize MC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noize_MC)'s song *Cooperative Swan Lake* (see also *[Ozero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozero)*),[^cite_note-sv-9][^cite_note-uk-17] which had been banned by the [Primorsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primorsky_District,_Saint_Petersburg) Court of St. Petersburg, went viral on social media. On 15 October Naoko was detained by police, and the following day, she and the band's percussionist were each jailed for 13 days, while the guitarist was jailed for 12.[^cite_note-ria-14][^cite_note-le-18][^cite_note-19] The charges stemmed from Stoptime's performance near the [Ploshchad Vosstaniya metro station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ploshchad_Vosstaniya_\(Saint_Petersburg_Metro\)) on 11 October.[^cite_note-ria-14] The Dzerzhinsky District Court ruled that the band's performance had not been approved by authorities and was obstructing public passage. It also became known that the singer will be charged with a more serious offense under Article 20.3.3 (discrediting the [Armed Forces of the Russian Federation](25709.md)) of the [Code of the Russian Federation on Administrative Offenses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_of_the_Russian_Federation_on_Administrative_Offenses).[^cite_note-ria-14] A denunciation of the musicians was written by [State Duma](1010187.md) deputy [Mikhail Romanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Romanov_\(politician\)).[^cite_note-ekho-15][^cite_note-wp-20][^cite_note-ng-21]

That same day, a petition was created on the website [Change.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Change.org) demanding Diana's release.[^cite_note-ср-22]

Music critic [Artemy Troitsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemy_Troitsky),[^cite_note-23] journalist [Dmitry Kolezev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Kolezev),[^cite_note-24] pianist [Yevgeny Alekseyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yevgeny_Alekseyev_\(pianist\)?action=edit\&redlink=1),[^cite_note-25] film critics Nikita Nikitin[^cite_note-26] and [Ekaterina Barabash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaterina_Barabash),[^cite_note-27] opposition politicians [Boris Nadezhdin](71749458.md),[^cite_note-bo-28][^cite_note-bor-29] [Lyubov Sobol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyubov_Sobol),[^cite_note-sob-30] [Nikolay Rybakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolay_Rybakov),[^cite_note-31] [Ekaterina Duntsova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaterina_Duntsova),[^cite_note-32] and [Maxim Reznik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_Reznik),[^cite_note-sv-9] and musicians [Monetochka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monetochka),[^cite_note-wp-20][^cite_note-33][^cite_note-do-34] [Noize MC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noize_MC), [Mikhail Novitsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Novitsky),[^cite_note-sv-9] [Kasta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasta),[^cite_note-papper-35] [Pornofilmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pornofilmy), and [Yuri Shevchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Shevchuk)[^cite_note-36] spoke out in support of Diana. In Russia and many other countries, rallies and performances began to take place in support of Loginova and her musical band.[^cite_note-\:ins-3][^cite_note-мартын-37][^cite_note-фк-38][^cite_note-sota-39][^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

Official and pro-government sources, on the contrary, supported the intervention of law enforcement officers.[^cite_note-sv-9] In turn, independent journalists pointed out that the authorities' response was disproportionately harsh and demonstrated a fear of any manifestations of civil solidarity and anti-war sentiment among young people.[^cite_note-sv-9]

[La Trobe University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Trobe_University) academic Dr. Robert Horvath (is a specialist on Russian Politics) responded to Loginova's arrest this way: "Russian culture in handcuffs. The captive is Diana Loginova ('Naoko'), an 18-year singer whose street performance of Noize MC's anti-Putin song 'Cooperative Swan Lake', with the backing voices of a sizeable crowd in central St. Petersburg, went viral on social media... The bigots who claim that the Putin regime's crimes are a product of Russian culture might reflect, for a moment at least, on her bravery and what it tells us".[^cite_note-42]

Two protocols under the article on public discrediting of the Russian army, which the singer is accused of, were filed with the courts of St. Petersburg on 20 October 2025.[^cite_note-43] Diana's lawyer, Maria Zyryanova, appealed the arrest.[^cite_note-bo-28]

On 28 October, it was reported that Loginova had been arrested again.[^cite_note-forbes-44][^cite_note-ббс-45] The day before, guitarist Alexander Orlov was also arrested again.[^cite_note-forbes-44][^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47][^cite_note-TheInsider29102025-48][^cite_note-Meduza29102025-49][^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51] At the police station after the trial, Diana became ill due to the stress of recent weeks and needed medical attention.[^cite_note-sotav-52]

Leontyev was released on the evening of November 9. Loginova and Orlov were not released from the detention center; on November 10, they were taken to St. Petersburg Police Station No. 2.[^cite_note-53] On November 11, they were remanded in custody for another 13 days. Their appeal was denied.

Loginova and Orlov left Russia on November 23.[^cite_note-54][^cite_note-55][^cite_note-56] The musicians were later seen performing in [Yerevan](34352.md).[^cite_note-57] On December 29, Diana Loginova gave an interview to [Yuri Dud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Dud).[^cite_note-дудь-1]
