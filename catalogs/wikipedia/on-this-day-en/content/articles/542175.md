The **Nationalist Republican Alliance** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Alianza Republicana Nacionalista*, abbreviated **ARENA**) is a [conservative](6675.md),[^cite_note-auto-6] [center-right](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center-right_politics)[^cite_note-New_York_Times-7][^cite_note-Middle_East_Monitor-8] to [right-wing](56522.md)[^cite_note-auto1-10][^cite_note-auto2-11][^cite_note-auto3-12][^cite_note-auto4-13] political party of [El Salvador](9356.md). It was founded on 30 September 1981 by retired Salvadoran Army Major [Roberto D'Aubuisson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_D'Aubuisson). It defines itself as a political institution constituted to defend the [democratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy), [republican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republicanism), and [representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representative_democracy) system of government, the [social market economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_market_economy) system and [nationalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalism).

ARENA controlled the [Legislative Assembly of El Salvador](1608500.md) until 1985, and its party leader [Alfredo Cristiani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfredo_Cristiani) was elected to the presidency in [1989](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Salvadoran_presidential_election). ARENA controlled the [presidency](466532.md) from 1989 until [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Salvadoran_presidential_election). The party gained a plurality in the Legislative Assembly in 2012. Maintaining only two seats in the [2024 legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Salvadoran_legislative_election), it has been the leading opposition party to the near-unanimous [Nuevas Ideas](61703199.md).

## History



[Alfredo Cristiani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfredo_Cristiani), [Antonio Saca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Saca), [Armando Calderón Sol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armando_Calder%C3%B3n_Sol), and [Francisco Flores Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Flores_P%C3%A9rez), ARENA's Presidents of El Salvador

The Nationalist Republican Alliance was founded on 30 September 1981 during the [Salvadoran Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvadoran_Civil_War). Its founding leader was [Roberto D'Aubuisson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_D'Aubuisson), a former [major](201920.md) in the [Salvadoran Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvadoran_Army) who was most well-known for commanding various [death squads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_squads_in_El_Salvador) and ordering the assassination of Archbishop [Óscar Romero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%93scar_Romero) in 1980.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBrockett2005240-19] At the time of ARENA's establishment, it was considered a [far-right](221220.md) political party. The [Central Intelligence Agency](5183633.md) (CIA) of the United States described ARENA as "reflect\[ing] the personality" of D'Aubuisson, and that the party's primary goals were to defeat the far-left [Farabundo Martí National Liberation Front](327525.md) (FMLN) and "restore El Salvador to the days before the [1979 officers' coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_Salvadoran_coup_d'%C3%A9tat)".[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECentral_Intelligence_Agency20135_&_14-15]

The party's initial membership primarily consisted of members of the [Salvadoran Nationalist Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvadoran_Nationalist_Movement?action=edit\&redlink=1) (MNS) and the [National Broad Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Broad_Front?action=edit\&redlink=1) (FAN) opposed to the ruling [Revolutionary Government Junta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_Government_Junta_of_El_Salvador) (JRG) and the preceding government of the [National Conciliation Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Coalition_Party_\(El_Salvador\)) (PCN).[^cite_note-Monti_Granados-20]

The party arose in response to "the insurgency of the [Farabundo Martí National Liberation Front](327525.md), FMLN, a group that united peasant farmers, unionists and intellectuals, which tried, through arms, to overthrow the dictatorship and to install a state regime inspired by the governments of revolutionary [Cuba](5042481.md) and [Sandinista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandinista) [Nicaragua](21362.md)".

The ideology the party claims to believe in is a system of democratic and representative government, emphasizing individual rights, the family as the nucleus of society and the respect for private property.

In February 2007, three ARENA politicians [were murdered in Guatemala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_2007_Guatemalan_congressmen_killings), including Eduardo D'Aubuisson, the son of party founder [Roberto D'Aubuisson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_D'Aubuisson), in what was considered by the police as a crime related to drugs.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

In 2009, ARENA took out a full-page ad in a Salvadoran newspaper calling on President [Mauricio Funes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauricio_Funes) to recognise the interim Honduran government of [Roberto Micheletti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Micheletti) installed after the military had expelled President [Manuel Zelaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Zelaya).

On 21 July 2022, [Gustavo López Davidson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavo_L%C3%B3pez_Davidson), the leader of ARENA from August 2019 to February 2020, committed suicide amidst an ongoing investigation against him for [embezzlement](209411.md) and [arms trafficking](42210.md).[^cite_note-ElMundo-23][^cite_note-LPG-24]

From [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Salvadoran_legislative_election) to [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Salvadoran_legislative_election), the party was the second largest in the [Legislative Assembly](1608500.md). Following the election of [Nuevas Ideas](61703199.md)' [Nayib Bukele](46452145.md), the party has lost members of its party delegation to the ruling party.[^cite_note-25]

## Structure

The National Executive Council (*Consejo Ejecutivo Nacional*, abbreviated COENA) is the leadership board of ARENA.[^cite_note-26] Carlos García Saade has been the president of COENA since 27 February 2023.[^cite_note-27]

In addition to the COENA, there are 14 Directors-in-Chief, one for each department and departmental councils called "Juntas Directivas Conjuntas" to coordinate political work in their respective department. In each department, a director is chosen who works with a specific member of COENA. The director's role is to organize and co-ordinate electoral campaigns and help the councils form party structures in the municipalities of their departments.

ARENA's party headquarters is located at 2429 Arce Street & North 45-47 Avenue in San Salvador.[^cite_note-28] As of 2024, ARENA had 116,000 members.[^cite_note-membership-1]

## Electoral record

At the [legislative elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_Salvadoran_legislative_election) held on 16 March 2003, the party won 32.0% of the popular vote and 27 out of 84 seats in the [Legislative Assembly](1608500.md). ARENA's successful candidate in [El Salvador's 2004 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Salvadoran_presidential_election) was [Antonio Saca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Saca). On 21 March 2004, Saca defeated [Schafik Hándal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schafik_H%C3%A1ndal), the candidate of the left-wing [Farabundo Martí National Liberation Front](327525.md) (FMLN), by a margin of 58% to 36% with 70% turnout. He was sworn in as president the following 1 June.

In the 12 March 2006 [legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Salvadoran_legislative_election), the party won 39.4% of the popular vote and 32 out of 84 seats. At the [18 January 2009 legislative elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Salvadoran_legislative_election), the party received 38.55% of the vote, and again won 32 of the 84 seats.

On 15 March 2009, ARENA candidate [Rodrigo Ávila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodrigo_%C3%81vila) lost the presidential election to [Mauricio Funes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauricio_Funes) of the FMLN. After elections, the party president was changed to [Alfredo Cristiani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfredo_Cristiani).

On 9 March 2014, [Salvador Sánchez Cerén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvador_S%C3%A1nchez_Cer%C3%A9n) of FMLN narrowly defeated the ARENA candidate [Norman Quijano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Quijano) by 0.2% in a run-off vote in a controversial election.[^cite_note-29]

ARENA also lost both [2019 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Salvadoran_presidential_election) and [2021 legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Salvadoran_legislative_election) dominated by [Nuevas Ideas](61703199.md), the party of current president [Nayib Bukele](46452145.md).[^cite_note-2019P-30][^cite_note-AQ-31] In 2024 [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Salvadoran_legislative_election), ARENA got only two seats in the Legislative Assembly, while Bukele's Nuevas Ideas got supermajority.[^cite_note-32]
