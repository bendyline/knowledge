The **Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore** (**CAAS**) is Singapore's [civil aviation authority](20778386.md) and a [statutory board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statutory_boards_of_the_Singapore_Government) under the [Ministry of Transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Transport_\(Singapore\)) of the [Government of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Singapore). Its head office is located on the fourth storey of [Singapore Changi Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Changi_Airport)'s Terminal 2.[^cite_note-4]

## History

Established on 1 September 1984, the CAAS regulates civilian air traffic within the airspace jurisdiction of the Republic. It also helps enable the growth of the air hub and aviation industry in [Singapore](27318.md), promotes safe industry practices and engages in civilian air-service agreements with air-service operators. This includes the usage of [unmanned aerial vehicles](58900.md) (UAVs) such as drones and [quadcopters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadcopter) by individuals or organisations. The agency also operates the [Air Traffic Control Tower](48563.md), providing air navigation services to ensure faultless movements of civilian aircraft at Singapore's airports and in the Singapore [flight information region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_information_region) (FIR).

CAAS supports and controls the [Singapore Aviation Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Aviation_Academy) (est. 1958).

CAAS also issues [revenue stamps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revenue_stamps_of_Singapore#Passenger_Service_Charge) to pay for the passenger service charge on flights.

In 2009, CAAS updated its logo, positioning, and values with brand consultancy DIA.[^cite_note-5]

In 2015, Shum Jin-Chyi Kevin assumed the position of [Director-General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Director_general) (DG) of CAAS.[^cite_note-HKJ-3]

In July 2017, CAAS and the [European Union Aviation Safety Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union_Aviation_Safety_Agency) (EASA) entered into a working arrangement to recognize each other's certifications.[^cite_note-6]

In 2018, CAAS underwent a brand refresh with [Tangible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangible_Brand_Consultants?action=edit\&redlink=1), a Singapore-based brand consultancy.[^cite_note-7]

On 2 August 2021, Han Kok Juan, the [returning officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Returning_officer#Singapore) for [Elections Department Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_Department_Singapore), assumed the role of DG of CAAS.[^cite_note-HKJ-3][^cite_note-8]
