The **municipalities of Colombia** are decentralized subdivisions of the [Republic of Colombia](5222.md). Municipalities make up most of the [departments of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Colombia), with 1,104 [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality) (*[municipios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipio)*). Each one of them is led by a [mayor](101146.md) (*[alcalde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcalde)*) elected by [popular vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_vote) and represents the maximum [executive government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_government) official at a municipality level under the mandate of the [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor) of their department which is a representative of all municipalities in the department; municipalities are grouped to form departments.

The municipalities of Colombia are also grouped in an association called the *Federación Colombiana de Municipios* (Colombian Federation of Municipalities), which functions as a union under the [private law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_law) and under the [constitutional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_Constitution_of_1991) [right to free association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_of_association) to defend their common interests.[^cite_note-1]

## [Amazonas Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonas_\(Colombian_department\))



Municipalities of **Amazonas** and its Non-municipalized areas.

Amazonas Department is composed of two municipalities, Leticia and Puerto Nariño, and by department corregimientos, which have special combined functions between a presidential power and a [corregimiento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corregimiento). The reason for this classification is that the large territory is mostly inhospitable, inhabited only by [indigenous peoples](4053513.md), and within the [Amazon rainforest](48139.md).
