**Naseem Shah**[^cite_note-3] (Pashto: [\[nəsɪm ʃɑh\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Pashto); born 15 February 2003) is a Pakistani international [cricketer](25675557.md).[^cite_note-Bio-4][^cite_note-5] In October 2019, at the age of 16, he was called up to the [Pakistan cricket team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_national_cricket_team) for their [Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_cricket) series against [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_national_cricket_team).[^cite_note-6] He made his international debut for Pakistan in November 2019 against Australia, becoming the ninth-youngest player to make his debut in Test cricket.[^cite_note-7] In December 2019, in the second Test match against [Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka_national_cricket_team), he became the second youngest bowler to take a [five-wicket haul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-wicket_haul) in a Test match, and also the youngest [pace bowler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pace_bowling) to do so.[^cite_note-8] In February 2020, in the first Test against [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_national_cricket_team), he became the youngest bowler to [take a hat-trick in a Test match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Test_cricket_hat-tricks).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

## Early and personal life

Naseem hails from [Mayar Jandool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayar,_Lower_Dir), a town in the [Lower Dir district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Dir_District) of [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) province, Pakistan and was born into a [Pashtun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns) family. He is fluent in [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) and [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

His father, Abbas Shah, was against his decision to play cricket, preferring him to concentrate on his studies. Naseem eventually completed his education to the [tenth grade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenth_grade) following which his father wanted him to seek employment abroad.[^cite_note-15] His mother died in 2019, just a day before his Test debut at the age of 16.[^cite_note-16]

He has two sisters and four brothers, including [Hunain Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunain_Shah), his younger brother, who is a fast bowler playing at first-class level.[^cite_note-17] Their youngest brother, [Ubaid Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubaid_Shah), also a fast bowler, made his Under-19 debut in October 2023[^cite_note-18] was selected for the [Under-19 Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_ACC_Under-19_Asia_Cup) in December 2023,[^cite_note-19] and played for the Pakistan U19 cricket team at the [2024 U19 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Under-19_Cricket_World_Cup).[^cite_note-20]

In February 2023, Naseem Shah was given the honorary rank of [Deputy Superintendent of Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Superintendent_of_Police) (DSP) in the [Balochistan Police](21591023.md), with high-level officials in attendance in [Quetta](93764.md). He expressed pride in being an ambassador for the province and pledged to represent it.[^cite_note-21]

## Early career

After playing [tape ball cricket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tape_ball_cricket) for a few years in Lower Dir, Naseem began his professional journey at the age of 13 when his uncle enrolled him in the [Abdul Qadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Qadir_\(cricketer\)) Cricket Academy in [Lahore](125315.md), where he would be coached by [Sulaman Qadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulaman_Qadir), before getting selected for the Lahore Under-16 team at the age of 13 and later for the Pakistan Under-16 team at the age of 14.[^cite_note-22]

## Domestic career

He made his [first-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-class_cricket) debut for [Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarai_Taraqiati_Bank_Limited_cricket_team) in the [2018–19 Quaid-e-Azam Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_Quaid-e-Azam_Trophy) on 1 September 2018.[^cite_note-FC-23] He made his [List A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_A_cricket) debut for Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited in the [2018–19 Quaid-e-Azam One Day Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_Quaid-e-Azam_One_Day_Cup) on 16 October 2018.[^cite_note-LA-24]

In September 2019, he was named in [Central Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Punjab_cricket_team)'s squad for the [2019–20 Quaid-e-Azam Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Quaid-e-Azam_Trophy) tournament.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] In October 2019, the [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) (PCB) named him as one of the six players to watch ahead of the [2019–20 National T20 Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_National_T20_Cup) tournament.[^cite_note-27] He made his [Twenty20](770690.md) debut on 13 October 2019, for Central Punjab in the [2019–20 National T20 Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_National_T20_Cup).[^cite_note-T20-28]

## Franchise career

### PSL

Shah played for [Quetta Gladiators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetta_Gladiators) from [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Pakistan_Super_League) to [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistan_Super_League), in the [Pakistan Super League](38199013.md). He was traded to [Islamabad United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_United) before the [2024 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Pakistan_Super_League).[^cite_note-29]

On 11 Feb 2026, the Rawalpindi franchise signed him for PKR 8.65 crore, making him the most expensive player in the first-ever [Pakistan Super League](38199013.md) auction.[^cite_note-30]

### Other leagues

On 15 September 2021, Naseem Shah took the crucial two wickets in the final of the [2021 Caribbean Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Caribbean_Premier_League) (CPL), which helped [st Kitts and Nevis Patriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Kitts_and_Nevis_Patriots) register their first CPL title.

In January 2022, he was signed by [Gloucestershire County Cricket Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloucestershire_County_Cricket_Club) to play in domestic tournaments during the first half of the 2022 season in England.[^cite_note-31] In April 2022, he was bought by the [Welsh Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_Fire) for the [2022 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_season_of_The_Hundred) of [The Hundred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hundred_\(cricket\)), also in England.[^cite_note-32] However, Shah was ruled out of matches for a month, after picking up a shoulder injury in his first match for Gloucestershire.[^cite_note-33]

## International career

In October 2019, he was named in Pakistan's [Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_cricket) squad for their series [against Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_Australia_in_2019%E2%80%9320).[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35] In November 2019, he played in the three-day warm-up match for Pakistan against [Australia A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_A_cricket_team).[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37] He made his Test debut for Pakistan, against [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_national_cricket_team), on 21 November 2019.[^cite_note-38] His debut was appreciated by many senior cricketers, such as former Pakistan fast bowler [Waqar Younis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waqar_Younis) and former England captain [Michael Vaughan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Vaughan), the latter being impressed by the 16-year-old touching 147 km/h, or 91.3 mph, in his first ever over.[^cite_note-39] Australian batsman [David Warner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Warner_\(cricketer\)) praised Naseem's pace, control, and aggression after the teenager repeatedly troubled him despite going wicketless in his debut innings, highlighted by a disallowed caught-behind dismissal due to a no-ball. Warner described Naseem as an exceptional talent capable of replicating the impact [Mohammad Amir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Amir) had early in his career.[^cite_note-WarnerNaseem2019-40]

In December 2019, he was named in Pakistan's Test squad for the two-match series [against Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_cricket_team_in_Pakistan_in_2019%E2%80%9320).[^cite_note-41] In the second match, he took his first [five-wicket haul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-wicket_haul) in Test cricket.[^cite_note-42] At the age of 16 years and 307 days, he became the youngest [fast bowler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pace_bowling), and the second-youngest among all bowlers,[^cite_note-43] to take a five-wicket haul in a Test match.[^cite_note-44]

He was initially named in Pakistan's squad for the [2020 Under-19 Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Under-19_Cricket_World_Cup),[^cite_note-45] but after his impressive start in Test cricket, he was withdrawn from the U19 team.[^cite_note-46] In January 2020, the [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) (PCB) issued a statement on Naseem being withdrawn from their U19 squad. They reiterated that it was due to cricketing reasons, and not concerns about his age, stating they have checked and verified the ages of all cricketers who could play in their U19 team.[^cite_note-47]

On 9 February 2020, in the first Test [against Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladeshi_cricket_team_in_Pakistan_in_2019%E2%80%9320), at the age of 16 years and 359 days, he became the youngest bowler to take a Test [hat-trick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hat-trick_\(cricket\)).[^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49] In May 2020, the PCB awarded him a central contract ahead of the 2020–21 season.[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51]

In June 2020, he was named in a 29-man squad for Pakistan's [tour to England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_England_in_2020) during the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53] In July, he was shortlisted for Pakistan's 20-man squad for the Test matches against England.[^cite_note-54][^cite_note-55] On 21 August, he was named in Pakistan's [Twenty20 International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twenty20_International) (T20I) squad, also for the series against England.[^cite_note-56] In November 2020, he was named in Pakistan's 35-man squad for their [tour to New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_New_Zealand_in_2020%E2%80%9321).[^cite_note-57] In October 2021, he was named in the [Pakistan Shaheens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_A_cricket_team) squad for their tour of Sri Lanka.[^cite_note-58]

In August 2022, he was named in Pakistan's ODI squad, for their [tour of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_the_Netherlands_in_2022).[^cite_note-59] He made his ODI debut on 16 August 2022, for Pakistan, against the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_national_cricket_team).[^cite_note-60] During the last ODI, Naseem took his maiden wicket in ODIs.[^cite_note-61]

Later the same month, he was named in Pakistan's T20I squad for the [2022 Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Asia_Cup).[^cite_note-62] He made his T20I debut on 28 August 2022, against [India](407754.md).[^cite_note-63]

On 7 September 2022, Naseem Shah made history by delivering 2 [sixes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six_\(cricket\)) against [Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghanistan_national_cricket_team) to save [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_national_cricket_team) from a defeat and help qualify for the finals.[^cite_note-64]

In September 2022, he was named in Pakistan's T20I squad for [England's tour of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_cricket_team_in_Pakistan_in_2022%E2%80%9323).[^cite_note-65] In October 2022, Naseem was named in Pakistan's T20I squad for the [Tri-Series in New Zealand.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_New_Zealand_Tri-Nation_Series)[^cite_note-66] Later in October, Naseem was named in Pakistan's T20I squad for the [2022 ICC Men's T20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Men's_T20_World_Cup).[^cite_note-67] He had a proficient run in the World Cup and helped Pakistan qualify for the [2022 ICC Men's T20 World Cup Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Men's_T20_World_Cup_final), but lost it.[^cite_note-68]

In November 2022, he was named in Pakistan's Test squad for [England's tour of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_cricket_team_in_Pakistan_in_2022%E2%80%9323). After the first match, Naseem suffered a shoulder injury that ruled him out of the series.[^cite_note-69][^cite_note-70]

In December 2022, he was named in Pakistan's Test squad for [New Zealand's tour of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_cricket_team_in_Pakistan_in_2022%E2%80%9323_\(December_2022\)) and later in the ODI squad in January 2023.[^cite_note-71][^cite_note-72] In the first ODI against New Zealand, Naseem took his second fifer in ODIs and a consecutive fifer.[^cite_note-73]

In September, he was ruled out of 2023 World Cup due to an injury.[^cite_note-74]

In May 2024, he was named in Pakistan's squad for the [2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Men's_T20_World_Cup) tournament.[^cite_note-75]

## Playing style



Shah during his bowling action

In February 2020, *[ESPNcricinfo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPNcricinfo)* journalist Osman Samiuddin wrote a reflective piece on Naseem Shah's emergence, praising the technical precision of his bowling action. Samiuddin noted that Naseem's mechanics evoked shades of former New Zealand internationals [Richard Hadlee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Hadlee) and [Shane Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Bond), highlighting the measured run-up, the compact alignment at the crease, and the assertive follow-through. He described the action as unusually complete for a teenager, "as ready and beautiful as a butterfly at birth", suggesting an innate fluency rather than something molded over years of coaching.[^cite_note-LetterNaseem2020-76]
