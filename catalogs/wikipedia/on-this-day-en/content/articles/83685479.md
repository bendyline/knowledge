During 2026, many [wildfires](56106.md) have occurred across the [United Kingdom](31717.md), fuelled by [several record-breaking heatwaves](83274214.md). As of 10 August, over 23,359 ha (57,720 acres; 90.19 sq mi; 233.59 km<sup>2</sup>) have been burnt, with 72 fires larger than 30 ha (74 acres) being recorded. Several of these fires forced evacuations and damaged property and protected habitats, with many requiring multi-agency responses as fire authorities raised risk levels and issued warnings amid deteriorating conditions.

## Overview

The [European Forest Fire Information System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Forest_Fire_Information_System) (EFFIS) keeps records of wildfires in Europe and has maintained a database since 2004. This year's fires have not been as severe as [the previous year's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_United_Kingdom_wildfires), which saw both the highest burned area and number of fires since the EFFIS's records began, reaching 47,879 ha (118,310 acres; 184.86 sq mi; 478.79 km<sup>2</sup>) and 181 fires greater than 30 ha (74 acres). As of 10 August, 23,359 ha (57,720 acres; 90.19 sq mi; 233.59 km<sup>2</sup>) have been burnt, with 72 fires at least 30 ha (74 acres) in size being mapped during 2026.[^cite_note-effis-1] These fires were exacerbated by [a series of record-breaking heatwaves](83274214.md) that saw extreme temperatures and dry conditions throughout May, June and July.[^cite_note-2] To respond to the wildfires, firefighting techniques have been deployed including helicopters.[^cite_note-3] A study has found that large fires in the northern hemisphere could increase UK air pollution.[^cite_note-4] The wildfires have been reportedly affected by [climate change](25369297.md).[^cite_note-5] There were particularly destructive wildfires on the hottest day of the year.[^cite_note-6] On 17 August, the [Met Office](319671.md) forecast rain and thunderstorms.[^cite_note-7]

## March



Fire damage in [Calderdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calderdale).

On 4 March, a wildfire broke out on [Marsden Moor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marsden_Moor) and grew to 400 m<sup>2</sup> (4,300 sq ft) before being brought under control.[^cite_note-8] On 23 March, eight appliances tackled a blaze at the [Stiperstones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stiperstones) which burned around 1.5 ha (3.7 acres) of gorse.[^cite_note-9] Two fires covering around 2 ha (4.9 acres) and 4 ha (9.9 acres) respectively started close to the [Scout Moor Wind Farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scout_Moor_Wind_Farm) and took crews around four hours to bring under control.[^cite_note-10] On 28 March, over 60 firefighters tackled fires on heathland near [Sandford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandford,_Dorset), the first of which affected around 2,100 m<sup>2</sup> (23,000 sq ft) before multiple others were found nearby, all of which were believed to have been started deliberately.[^cite_note-11]

## May



A fire on [Arthur's Seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur's_Seat) on 25 May.

A wildfire which broke out in the [Peak District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak_District) late on 30 April escalated at the start of May.[^cite_note-MEN1-32] The fire, which was burning close to the summit of the [A57](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A57_road) [Snake Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snake_Pass) road, expanded to cover 450,000 m<sup>2</sup> (110 acres) by dawn on 1 May[^cite_note-33] and forced the closure of the Snake Pass for two days.[^cite_note-34] Nine fire engines attended the fire alongside specialists from the [Derby](18727355.md) Mountain Rescue unit and a water-bombing helicopter.[^cite_note-35] Smoke from the fire drifted over the town of [Glossop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossop), with residents warned to keep doors and windows closed.[^cite_note-MEN1-32] The fire burned 900,000 m<sup>2</sup> (220 acres) of moorland before being brought under control on 2 May.[^cite_note-36]

On 25 May, a wildfire broke out on [Arthur's Seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur's_Seat) in [Edinburgh](9602.md), with crews working into the following day to bring it under control.[^cite_note-37] On 28 May, a fire started in the [Pentland Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentland_Hills), with six engines tackling it at its height.[^cite_note-38]

## June

A woodland fire broke out close to houses and a railway line in [Beighton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beighton,_South_Yorkshire), [Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield) during the night of 19 June, with [South Yorkshire Fire and Rescue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Yorkshire_Fire_and_Rescue) sending crews from four fire stations to tackle the blaze.[^cite_note-39] The fire burned for several hours before being brought under control, and subsequent investigation alongside the [British Transport Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Transport_Police) determined the source of the fire to be [arson](184695.md) in a pile of disused railway sleepers on [Network Rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_Rail) land.[^cite_note-40]

On 24 June, a blaze started on moorland near [Tintwistle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tintwistle) which ultimately spread to over 600 ha (1,500 acres; 6.0 km<sup>2</sup>; 2.3 sq mi) as it continued to burn more than a month later. By the next day, the wildfire had significantly escalated, as high winds caused it to spread from low-lying moorland into the canopy of surrounding woodlands.[^cite_note-shefftrib1-41] A [United Utilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Utilities) water-bombing helicopter conducted around 2,000 sorties over the fire, dropping more than 10 million litres of water onto it from above over three days[^cite_note-shefftrib1-41] at a cost of at least £500,000;[^cite_note-shefftrib2-42] the fire was initially contained by 26 June, before re-escalating in mid-July.[^cite_note-bbc-tintwistle-43]

## July

### England

On 4 July, a wildfire believed to have been started deliberately near [Wareham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wareham,_Dorset) was tackled by crews from 13 stations.[^cite_note-44] On 6 July, another fire believed to have been started deliberately burnt 3 ha (7.4 acres) of heathland at [Bourne Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourne_Valley).[^cite_note-45] Also on 6 July, a fire broke out in woodland at the Strawberry Hill Heath nature reserve near [Mansfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansfield) in [Nottinghamshire](68049.md);[^cite_note-46] the fire burned for around seven hours before being brought under control, although remained smouldering underground for a considerable time afterwards.[^cite_note-47] On 7 July, four engines and several specialist vehicles fought a blaze that affected 4 ha (9.9 acres) at [Brentmoor Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brentmoor_Heath).[^cite_note-48] On 10 July, 13 vehicles including eight fire engines were sent to a blaze at [Devil's Dyke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devil's_Dyke,_Sussex) which continued into the following day.[^cite_note-49]

On 11 July, a large wildfire began near [Dove Stone Reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dove_Stone_Reservoir) and later merged with a pre-existing fire that had started on 24 June at [Tintwistle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tintwistle) before meeting another fire at [Saddleworth Moor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddleworth_Moor) which started on 14 July, combining to destroy around 885 ha (2,190 acres; 8.85 km<sup>2</sup>; 3.42 sq mi) of moorland. The Tintwistle Moor fire re-escalated due to high winds on 13 July, and combined with other fires in the area this prompted the [Derbyshire Fire and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derbyshire_Fire_and_Rescue_Service) to declare a [major incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_incident)[^cite_note-bbc-tintwistle-43] whilst [National Highways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highways) closed the [Woodhead Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A628_road) for several days.[^cite_note-50] One person was charged with aggravated arson whilst another two were arrested on suspicion of arson in relation to the Dove Stone fire,[^cite_note-51] with footage on social media showing fireworks being set off close to the reservoir in the hours before the fire was first reported.[^cite_note-52] Wildfire smoke drifting into Greater Manchester resulted in [PM<sub>2.5</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particulate_matter) pollution levels peaking at 100 μg/m<sup>3</sup> in [Manchester city centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_city_centre) on 12 July, more than five times higher than the [WHO](33583.md) safe legal limit, and 150 μg/m<sup>3</sup> in residential areas of [Tameside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tameside).[^cite_note-shefftrib2-42] Another moorland fire started nearby close to [Penistone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penistone) on 16 July,[^cite_note-53] this time caused by a single-vehicle road accident where a car left the road on the Woodhead Pass and flipped over into a field before catching fire.[^cite_note-54] By 21 July, [pub](24578.md) owners in the moorlands above [Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield) said that persistent road closures due to the [Peak District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak_District) wildfires were affecting their trade, with the landlord of the Dog & Partridge Inn at [Flouch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flouch_Roundabout) warning that staff may have to be made redundant to cut costs due to lost income.[^cite_note-55] The Woodhead Pass eventually reopened on 31 July after five weeks of wildfires in the area.[^cite_note-56]

Over 60 firefighters worked to control a wildfire near [Blackwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackwater,_Hampshire) which burnt around 10 ha (25 acres) of heath and woodland.[^cite_note-57] Crews in Devon tackled four separate grass fires, including one near [Collaton St Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaton_St_Mary) that spread to 11 ha (27 acres).[^cite_note-58] On 12 July, four appliances extinguished a fire that burnt around 10 ha (25 acres) of fields close to the [Maiden Castle Sports Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maiden_Castle_Sports_Centre).[^cite_note-59] A fire broke out in a field close to residential properties on Grange Road in [Rawmarsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawmarsh), [Rotherham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotherham) on 17 July in an apparent arson attack.[^cite_note-60] On 18 July, a heathland fire destroyed tents at the Rustic Stomp festival near [Wareham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wareham,_Dorset) in [Dorset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorset).[^cite_note-61] On 20 July, a wildfire which was tackled by eight appliances damaged around 12 ha (30 acres) of ancient woodland at [Abbot's Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbot's_Wood?action=edit\&redlink=1) near [Polegate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polegate).[^cite_note-62] On 22 July, a car fire spread to a nearby field at [Ireland's Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland's_Cross) near [Woore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woore) in [Shropshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shropshire).[^cite_note-63]

A significant wildfire broke out in [Grenoside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoside), [Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield) during the early hours of 23 July. The fire began in a field close to a residential area and subsequently spread rapidly into gardens,[^cite_note-itv-fh-64] ultimately destroying a row of five terraced houses between Grimsell Crescent and Fox Hill Road and causing damage to five more homes.[^cite_note-bbc-fh-65] A large area was evacuated during the height of the fire,[^cite_note-bbc-fh-65] which was tackled by crews from eight fire engines at its peak.[^cite_note-66] Two people suffered minor injuries,[^cite_note-itv-fh-64] and the fire was brought under control during the morning. Later that day, a 59-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of arson and criminal damage offences.[^cite_note-bbc-fh-65] Local MP [Marie Tidball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Tidball) said that her thoughts were with those who had been made homeless or otherwise affected by the fire in Sheffield.[^cite_note-67] Officers from [South Yorkshire Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Yorkshire_Police), who had been out on routine patrol in the area and first spotted the fire, were praised for their quick actions in evacuating residents from their homes as the wildfire approached before the fire brigade were able to arrive on scene.[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69]

On 23 July a heathland fire in a rare lowland coastal heath habitat at Headon Warren on the Isle of Wight caused around 9 ha (22.2 acres) of damage. Around 80 firefighters were involved in tackling the blaze, and fire engines had to be brought across the Solent from mainland UK to help the local fire service. It took 5 days to get the blaze under control, and at its height involved the use of helicopters scooping up seawater to drop on areas that could not be reached on foot. The cause of the fire has not been definitely identified, however remains of a campfire were found by Fire and Rescue Services in the vicinity, and it is thought likely that this was the source of the blaze.[^cite_note-70][^cite_note-71]

Train services between [Bridlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridlington_railway_station) and [Scarborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarborough_railway_station) on the Yorkshire coast were suspended on 28 July due to a 20 acres (8.1 ha) wildfire burning close to the railway line outside [Filey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filey).[^cite_note-72] Also on 28 July, a grass fire was reported in [Longley Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longley_Park) in [Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield), causing significant damage to the green space.[^cite_note-73] On 29 July, [a large heathland fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Dunwich_Heath_wildfire) broke out at [Dunwich Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunwich_Heath) near [Leiston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiston), [Suffolk](27886.md), shortly after 17\:30 BST.[^cite_note-74] A major incident was declared.[^cite_note-75] Driven by strong winds, dry conditions, and a regional heatwave, the fire spread rapidly over an area equivalent to roughly 14 football pitches.[^cite_note-76] At 22\:30 BST, [Suffolk Fire and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffolk_Fire_and_Rescue_Service) declared a major incident due to the size and operational complexity of the blaze.[^cite_note-77] Around 90 firefighters from 14 stations across the region were deployed, utilising specialist off-road [Unimog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unimog) vehicles, 4x4 quad bikes, and thermal imaging drones to track fire [Romford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romford) and [Rainham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainham,_London) in east London.[^cite_note-84] Two people were hospitalised following a blaze at Eastern Avenue West.[^cite_note-85] There were 63 grass fires across Greater London.[^cite_note-86]

On 30 July, a major wildfire broke out in [Hornchurch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornchurch).[^cite_note-87] Eight fire engines and dozens of firefighters were deployed to tackle the blaze near the area of South End Road.[^cite_note-88] Another fire was reported in woodlands and fields close to houses at the Hartley Brook Dike wildlife reserve in [Shiregreen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiregreen), [Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield) on 30 July.[^cite_note-89] Multiple wildfires were burning across [South Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Yorkshire) by 31 July, including in fields off Penrith Road in [Shirecliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirecliffe)[^cite_note-star2-90] and in woodland close to [Beighton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beighton,_South_Yorkshire),[^cite_note-star3-91] both in Sheffield; in grasslands close to new residential developments at [Waverley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waverley,_South_Yorkshire) in [Rotherham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotherham);[^cite_note-star3-91] and on land close to Elm Wood Farm in [Swallownest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swallownest), just outside Sheffield.[^cite_note-star2-90] Also on 31 July, a wildfire broke out in [Colchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colchester).[^cite_note-92] Firefighters tackled the blaze on [Ministry of Defence](203793.md) land at the Middlewick Ranges on Mersea Road.[^cite_note-93]

Several wildfires occurred throughout July/August in Surrey including Whitmoor Common (Worplesdon), Brentmoor Heath (West End), Hankley Common (Farnham), Riverside Nature Reserve (Guildford) and outdoor areas in Fetcham, Shackleford and Tongham. Some of these fires have been officially attributed to accidental/negligent cause but a few of them are believed to have started deliberately, with a small number of them being officially treated as arson.

## August

### England

By 1 August, the wildfire at [Dunwich Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunwich_Heath) was still being tackled.[^cite_note-103] On 2 August, multiple wildfires broke out in [Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham).[^cite_note-104] The [West Midlands Fire Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Midlands_Fire_Service) were called to respond.[^cite_note-105] On 2 August, a wildfire damaged a "tiny forest" at [Chesterton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesterton,_Cambridge), [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge).[^cite_note-106] On 2 August an second fire broke out in [Colchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colchester) on Mersea Road on [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence) land at the [Middlewick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middlewick) Ranges in the exact same spot and was contained quickly.[^cite_note-107] On 3 August, [Derbyshire Fire and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derbyshire_Fire_and_Rescue_Service) were stood down in [Glossop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossop) after fighting the Tintwistle wildfire after 23 days.[^cite_note-108] The same day a wildfire near the [M1 motorway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M1_motorway) at [East Ardsley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Ardsley) in [West Yorkshire](149139.md) was extinguished.[^cite_note-109] On 3 August, four homes were destroyed by fire in [Crawley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crawley).[^cite_note-110]

On 7 August, field fires broke out near both [Bradford-on-Avon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford-on-Avon) and [Westbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westbury,_Wiltshire).[^cite_note-111] On 8 August, firefighters were called to a wildfire at [Holme Fen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holme_Fen) in Huntingdonshire. 80 firefighters and 14 appliances attended the fire.[^cite_note-112][^cite_note-113][^cite_note-114] The fire lasted a week and local [Member of Parliament](3776360.md) (MP) [Sam Carling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Carling) called for more government support.[^cite_note-115] The same day a wildfire was tackled in [Horsham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horsham).[^cite_note-116]

On 9 August, [a wildfire broke out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_Forest_wildfire) in the [New Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Forest) after a vehicle fire spread to nearby heathland outside [Ringwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ringwood,_Hampshire) in [Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampshire).[^cite_note-117] The [A31 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A31_road) was closed temporarily between [Picket Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picket_Post) and [Stoney Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoney_Cross).[^cite_note-118] Drivers were instructed to abandon their cars and leave the area on foot.[^cite_note-119] The [Hampshire and Isle of Wight Fire and Rescue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampshire_and_Isle_of_Wight_Fire_and_Rescue_Service) sent 20 fire engines to respond.[^cite_note-120] Also on 9 August, a wildfire at Strawberry Hill Heath near [Mansfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansfield) that had been smouldering underground since early July re-ignited significantly, prompting a response from the [Nottinghamshire Fire and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottinghamshire_Fire_and_Rescue_Service);[^cite_note-121] around 9 acres (3.6 ha) of woodland was destroyed, and the cause could not be determined.[^cite_note-122]

On 10 August, the Dunwich Heath wildfire officially ended and was eradicated.

On 11 August, wildfires broke out in [Mawgan Porth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mawgan_Porth) and [Porth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porth,_Cornwall) in [Cornwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornwall).[^cite_note-123] On 12 August, a fire at [Crows-an-Wra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crows-an-Wra) in the west of Cornwall led to the closure of [Land's End Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land's_End_Airport); [Cornwall Fire and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornwall_Fire_and_Rescue_Service) said that four fire engines were attending and [National Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Trust) workers rounded up and evacuated grazing horses in the area.[^cite_note-124] A house and grassland were burned in Kings Norton, Birmingham.[^cite_note-125]

On 12 August, a [Cobra meeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cobra_meeting) was held on the wildfires.[^cite_note-126] Prime Minister [Andy Burnham](414818.md) described Britain as a "tinder box".[^cite_note-127] The military have been deployed to aid the firefighting efforts.[^cite_note-128]

On 13 August, multiple fires broke out across England including in the [West Midlands](351076.md).[^cite_note-129] It was the hottest day of the year.[^cite_note-130] A wildfire covering about 16 hectares near a golf course in [Stourbridge](412092.md).[^cite_note-131] Dozens of firefighters attended the scene near Stourbridge golf course.[^cite_note-132] Three people were hospitalised.[^cite_note-133] Multiple homes were destroyed by fire.[^cite_note-134] The [A451 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A451_road) was blocked between [Kidderminster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidderminster) and [Stourbridge](412092.md).[^cite_note-135] Firefighters were called to the [B\&M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B\&M) on Weavers Wharf in Kidderminster.[^cite_note-136] Homes were damaged by fire in the Sandyford area of [Stoke-on-Trent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoke-on-Trent).[^cite_note-137] [Warwickshire Fire and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warwickshire_Fire_and_Rescue_Service) responded to a large fire in [Salford Priors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salford_Priors), [Stratford-upon-Avon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stratford-upon-Avon).[^cite_note-138] A large fire also broke out around Price Avenue in [Tamworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamworth,_Staffordshire).[^cite_note-139]

On 14 August, an alert was sent to phones in England and Wales about the risk of wildfires.

A wildfire on moorland was put out near Holmfirth Road in [Oldham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oldham).[^cite_note-140] A wildfire broke out at [Mousehold Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mousehold_Heath) in [Norfolk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk).[^cite_note-141] Large plumes of smoke were spotted across [Norwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich).[^cite_note-142] [Norfolk Fire and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk_Fire_and_Rescue_Service) declared a major incident.[^cite_note-143] A fire was reported at a school in [Shipdham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shipdham).[^cite_note-144] A large grassland fire broke out at [Westbury White Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westbury_White_Horse) in [Wiltshire](51231.md).[^cite_note-145][^cite_note-146] A garden fire was put out in [Trowbridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trowbridge).[^cite_note-147] In [Dorset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorset), a wildfire broke out at [Bishop's Caundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop's_Caundle) between [Sturminster Newton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sturminster_Newton) and [Sherborne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherborne).[^cite_note-148] [Dorset & Wiltshire Fire and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorset_&_Wiltshire_Fire_and_Rescue_Service) declared a major incident.[^cite_note-149] A wildfire ravaged Riverside Nature Reserve, between the [River Wey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Wey) and the [A3 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A3_road), in [Guildford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guildford), [Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrey).[^cite_note-150] The Rural Life Living Museum in Surrey cancelled the steam rally due to the fire risk.[^cite_note-151] An unattended barbeque reportedly sparked a blaze at [Sefton Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sefton_Park) in [Liverpool](18081.md).[^cite_note-152]

On 14 August, the government issued a temporary ban on [disposable barbecues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disposable_grill).[^cite_note-153][^cite_note-154][^cite_note-155] On the same day, an [emergency alert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_Alerts_\(United_Kingdom\)) was issued to phones in England and Wales warning of risk of wildfires.[^cite_note-156][^cite_note-157] [Judith Blake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judith_Blake,_Baroness_Blake_of_Leeds) was appointed Fire Minister amid the crisis.[^cite_note-158]

On 15 August, a wildfire broke out in Worcestershire.[^cite_note-159] [Hereford and Worcester Fire and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hereford_and_Worcester_Fire_and_Rescue_Service) attended to the scene at Maypole Piece in [Bewdley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bewdley).[^cite_note-160] On 16 August, firefighters tackled a large fire at a warehouse in [Harwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harwich).[^cite_note-161] The same day residents in Stourbridge were allowed to return to their properties.[^cite_note-162]

A rest area was set up for those affected.[^cite_note-164] The fires are suspected to have been started deliberately.[^cite_note-165]

On 14 August, a wildfire was reported in [Carmarthenshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmarthenshire).[^cite_note-166] A major incident was declared near the market town of [Llandovery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llandovery) and dozens of homes were evacuated.[^cite_note-167]
