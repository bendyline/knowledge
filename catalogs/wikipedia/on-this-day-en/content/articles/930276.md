This article lists **[political parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Burkina Faso](3470.md)**. Since gaining independence as the [Republic of Upper Volta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Upper_Volta) in 1960, the political system has been volatile, moving from a [multi-party system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party_system) to a [single party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single_party) to no parties at all, depending on the ruling regime.[^cite_note-1]

On 30 January 2026, junta leader [Ibrahim Traoré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Traor%C3%A9) formally dissolved and banned all political parties in the country.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
