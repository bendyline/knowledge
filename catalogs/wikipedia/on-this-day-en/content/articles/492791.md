**Ouest** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language), pronounced [\[wɛst\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/f/f4/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-WikiLucas00-ouest.wav/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-WikiLucas00-ouest.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/f/f4/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-WikiLucas00-ouest.wav/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-WikiLucas00-ouest.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) or **Lwès** ([Haitian Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Creole); both meaning "West") is one of the ten [departments of Haiti](10937444.md). It is located in south-central Haiti, linking the Great-North and the [Tiburon Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiburon_Peninsula).

It is the jurisdictional seat of the national capital, the city of [Port-au-Prince](57391.md).

It has an area of 4,982.56 square kilometres (1,923.78 sq mi) and a population of 4,029,705 (2015 estimate), making it both the largest department by area and the most populated department in Haiti.

## History

### Taino period

The department was part of the [Xaragua kasika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaragua,_Hispaniola) under the leadership of Anacaona.

There are many native settlements in the department notably around [Akaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcahaie,_Arcahaie,_Ouest), [Azuei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etang_Saum%C3%A2tre) Lake, [Yaguana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A9og%C3%A2ne), the [island of Guanabo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gon%C3%A2ve_Island) and [Goava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petit-Go%C3%A2ve). It is understood that the Azuei Plaine region was used as a hunting ground for the natives.

The island of Gonâve is the last point of refuge for the Taino natives.

### Spanish period

One of the only Spanish settlements in the area was a destroyed Spanish town called Santa-Maria-de-la-Paz-Verdadera, the actual city of [Tabarre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabarre).

### French period

The earliest French settlement in the area was a hospital named Morne L'Hopital. In the second half of the 18th century, the French wanted to build a new colonial capital outside the troubled North. One of the suggestions was Petit-Goâve but later they chose the region of l'Hopital building which would become the City of [Port-au-Prince](57391.md).

### English period

In 1793 the city fell to the British troops where they built the Fort-National until Toussaint gained it back for the French government.

### Haitian period

#### Haitian Revolution

Many [maroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maroons) troops controlled the mountain around Port-au-Prince mainly [Romaine-la-Prophetesse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romaine-la-Proph%C3%A9tesse) and [Lamour Dérance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamour_Desrances) and Cangé.

Around 1772, [Romaine-la-Prophétesse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romaine-la-Proph%C3%A9tesse) acquired a plantation named Trou Coffy in the department (likely in what is now [Fondwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fondwa?action=edit\&redlink=1)), becoming a prominent coffee grower and trader.[^cite_note-3] In 1791 and 1792, during the early [Haitian Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Revolution), Romaine led some thirteen thousand slaves and rebels in freeing slaves from and burning the provinces plantations and briefly controlling two major cities, [Léogâne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A9og%C3%A2ne) and [Jacmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacmel).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-Dun_2016_65-6][^cite_note-7] Ogé and Chavannes organized more than 300 blacks the Suisses to rebel against their so-called master. Caradeux the rough slave master in the region orderer their execution in [Omòl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B4le-Saint-Nicolas).In 1791, Pétion, Pinchinnat, Beauvais, and more rebelled against the French authority and reclaimed their civil rights. After the Battles of Pernier, [Croix-des-Bouquets](7669872.md), and [Mirebalais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirebalais) they gained a Concordat, although the slaves did not gain anything.

In Arcahaie, on May 18, 1803, the generals of the North and West and Maroons met for a Congress and chose [Jean-Jacques Dessalines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Jacques_Dessalines) as commander of the [Armée Indigènes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arm%C3%A9e_Indig%C3%A8ne) and chose the [Haiti Flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Haiti) designed by [Catherine Flon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Flon).

#### Independence

According to [Thomas Madiou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Madiou)'s recount of the Haitian Revolution, the fight for [Port-au-Prince](57391.md) is one of the most important fights. After Gabart gained control of [St-Marc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Marc), the entire [Artibonite](489220.md) region was under the control of [Dessalines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dessalines_\(disambiguation\)) and the [Armée Indigène](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arm%C3%A9e_Indig%C3%A8ne). In the south, only the major port town of [Les Cayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Cayes) was still under French control. In the north, [Cap-Haitian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap-Ha%C3%AFtien) and [Mole-St-Nicolas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B4le-Saint-Nicolas) are still under French control. [Rochambeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Baptiste_Donatien_de_Vimeur,_comte_de_Rochambeau) anticipated that the next step for [Dessalines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dessalines_\(disambiguation\)) would be the conquer of Port-au-Prince, so he moved the capital of the colony back to [Cap-Francois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap-Ha%C3%AFtien) which would be harder for the British Navy to put under blockade versus Port-au-Prince located in the [Gulf of Gonave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Gon%C3%A2ve). Leaving [Petite-Rivière-de-l'Artibonite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petite_Rivi%C3%A8re_de_l'Artibonite) on September 15, the general stopped in St-Marc by [Gabart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arm%C3%A9e_Indig%C3%A8ne) and was in [Arcahaie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcahaie) by [Pétion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_P%C3%A9tion) on September 17. The same night for [Croix-des-Bouquets](7669872.md) where he camped by Frère. Pétion continued to march toward [P-au-P](57391.md) and was by habitation Drouillard the next day. The French general of Croix-des-Bouquets Lux left the arrondissement to go help the French troops in P-au-P, he passed by Damien, Sarthe and the convoy was attacked by the Indigènes. By September 23, [Cangé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arm%C3%A9e_Indig%C3%A8ne) left [Léogane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A9og%C3%A2ne), marched toward Port-au-Prince, and attacked the Fort-Bizoton in [Carrefour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrefour,_Ouest), Gabart was at the St-Joseph gate and Dessalines camped in Turgeau a neighborhood located east of the city. The city of Port-au-Prince is officially sieged by the Armée Indigénes. The very next day Pétion was located in the habitation Philippeaux and started bombarding the French troops. By October 5, an [aide-de-camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aide-de-camp) of [Lavalette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Parisot_de_Valette) brought a letter to Dessalines with the terms of their capitulation. Dessalines accepted with an exchange of prisoners and [adjuvant-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjutant_general) Bonnet came back to the Haitians and gave the French 4 days to leave the city. Thus on September 9 Port-au-Prince was freed from the French troops and Dessalines enter the city proudly with Pétion on his right and Gabart on his left.&#x20;

[Pétion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_P%C3%A9tion), [Germain Frère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arm%C3%A9e_Indig%C3%A8ne) and [Yayou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arm%C3%A9e_Indig%C3%A8ne) are signatories of the [Haitian Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Declaration_of_Independence). Ouest was part of the Second Military Division of the West with Pétion as commander.

Pétion build the twin forts [Jacques and Alexandre](https://visithaiti.com/things-to-do/fort-jacques/) on the order of Dessalines.

On October 17, 1806, coming from [Dessalinesville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dessalines), [Emperor Jacques 1st](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Jacques_Dessalines) was ambushed and assassinated plunging the country into a civil war. His body was buried in St-Anne Cemetery. On January 2, 1904, he received state funerals and later on his body was moved to the Autel de la Patrie in Champs-de-Mars along with [Pétion's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_P%C3%A9tion) body.

On January 1, 1807, only three years after the [Haitian Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Declaration_of_Independence), the northern troops fought the western troops in the Sibert Battle, where Pétion almost died. [Cabaret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabaret,_Ouest) was the de facto border between the two states.

Under [Jean-Pierre Boyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Pierre_Boyer), fearing the return of the French boat, he ordered the construction of a city in the heights like Toussaint with [Ennery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ennery,_Artibonite), Dessalines with [Dessalines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dessalines) and Christophe with [Milot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milot,_Haiti) and naming it after his mentor [Pétionville](10671007.md).

Being the political center of the country, most of Haiti's political instability and economical fight happen in the Port-au-Prince area since 1749 until today.

## Geography

The department is bordered to the north by [Artibonite](489220.md) and [Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_\(department\)) and the [Mattheux mountain chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Haiti), the west by the Gulf of Gonave, the south by the Sud-Est department and the [La Selle Mountain Chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Haiti), and the [Dominican Republic](8060.md) to the east through the province of [Independencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independencia_Province).

It is the second largest department in Haiti after [Artibonite](489220.md). [Gonâve Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gon%C3%A2ve_Island) is also part of the Ouest department. The department's stretch of coastline lies entirely in the [Gulf of Gonâve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Gon%C3%A2ve).

A part majority of the department lies in the [Plaine-de-Cul-de-Sac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_of_the_Cul-de-Sac) which continues to the Dominican Rep. as the [Hayo de Enriquillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enriquillo_wetlands). Two other plains that lie in the region are the Plain of Arcahaie-Cabaret and the [Plain of Léoganes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Haiti).

### Gulf and coast

The Golfe is more than 500 km in length from Mole-Saint-Nicolas to Abricots and it consists of more than a dozen bays and harbors. The Bay of Port-au-Prince is the largest in the country and one of the biggest in the Caribbean harboring many islands, cays, and reefs. The island of Gonave is the largest satellite island on Hispaniola being the size of [Singapore](27318.md).

### Environment

The largest lake in the country is a salty lake in a depression in the plain of the same name, Azuei Lake. A smaller lake lies next to it Caiman Lake. Both lakes form a national park harboring [flamingos](165927.md) and [caimans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caiman).

The hills of Port-au-Prince consist of a mountain from the [Massif de la Selle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cha%C3%AEne_de_la_Selle)-[Cordillera Meridional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_the_Dominican_Republic), where one can find multiple forests such as Foret-des-Pins and Parc-la-Visite.

The most important rivers in the department are Blanche River, Froide River, Monance River, and Bois-de-Chène Ravine.

### Geology

The earthquake activities in the area are a result of the [Enriquillo-Plantain-Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enriquillo%E2%80%93Plantain_Garden_fault_zone) fault going from [Jamaica](15660.md) to the [Dominican Rep](8060.md) passing through the [Xaragua Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiburon_Peninsula) and the [Gonave Microplate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gon%C3%A2ve_Microplate). Two well-documented earthquakes happen in the area.

- 1751 earthquake

- [2010 earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Haiti_earthquake) or *Goudougoudou*. The epicenter was in Léoganes affecting [Jacmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacmel), [Léoganes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A9og%C3%A2ne), and Port-au-Prince and causing millions in damages and counting hundreds-thousand of casualties.

On 12 January 2010, a magnitude 7.0 earthquake struck in the department, the largest in Haiti in two centuries.[^cite_note-8] In the [Port-au-Prince Arrondissement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port-au-Prince_Arrondissement), many areas suffered damage.[^cite_note-9] In the [Léogâne Arrondissement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A9og%C3%A2ne_Arrondissement), the city of [Léogâne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A9og%C3%A2ne) suffered 80-90% destruction.[^cite_note-NoOther-10] Other places in Léogâne were also affected.

## Economy

Ouest is the wealthiest department in Haiti, with economic poles in different sectors.

### Tourism

Ouest's tourism sector touches on different branches such as leisure tourism in the Cotes-des-Arcadins, [eco-tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_national_parks_of_Haiti) with Park Azuei-Caiman and Park La Selle, historical tourism with the countless historical monuments especially the [National Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mus%C3%A9e_du_Panth%C3%A9on_National_Ha%C3%AFtien), and cultural tourism.

### Agriculture

For many years, agriculture was a part of the economy of the department, with the Arcahaie plain being a center for [plantain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooking_banana), the Cul-de-Sac and Léogane being known for large [sugar-cane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugarcane) plantations mostly for the production of [rum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rum), and the hills of [Kenscoff](7629601.md) cultivating different vegetables. Unfortunately, over-construction and the ever-expansion of the city, those areas see their fertile land now covered with asphalt and concrete. [^cite_note-11]

### Transport

Much of Haiti's road network extends out of Ouest.

- The RN1 connects the West to the North by the coast

- The RN2 connects the West to the South

- The RN3 connects the West to the North through the mountains

- The RN4 connects the West to the South-East

- The RN8 connects the West to the [Jimani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiman%C3%AD), in the Dominican Rep.

The bay harbors many ports of sizable capacity owned or operated by the state or private parties.

Haiti's largest airport, the [Toussaint Louverture Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toussaint_Louverture_International_Airport), is located in [Delmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delmas,_Ouest).
