**Lin Jingzhen** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 林景臻; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Lín Jǐngzhēn*; born May 1965) is a retired Chinese banker who served as vice governor of the [Bank of China](303789.md) from 2018 to 2025. He was investigated by China's top anti-graft agency in September 2025.

## Early life and education

Lin was born in the town of Wangchuan in [Hui'an County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hui'an_County), [Fujian](88585.md), in May 1965.[^cite_note-ab-1][^cite_note-bee-2] He graduated from [Xiamen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiamen_University) with a bachelor's degree in economics and a master's degree in business administration.

## Career

Lin joined the [Bank of China](303789.md) in 1987. In his early years, he was sent to work in [Hong Kong](13404.md) and later worked at the [Xiamen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiamen) Branch. In October 2010, he was promoted to general manager at the Corporate Finance Headquarters of Bank of China, and held that office until May 2015, when he was appointed vice president of the BOC Hong Kong (Holdings) Limited. In March 2018, he was promoted again to vice governor of the Bank of China, concurrently serving as chairman of the [BOC International Holdings Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BOC_International), chairman of the BOC International Securities Limited, and non-executive director of the BOC Hong Kong (Holdings) Limited. He retired on 7 January 2025.[^cite_note-3]

## Downfall

On 9 September 2025, Lin was put under investigation for alleged "serious violations of discipline and laws" by the [Central Commission for Discipline Inspection](1526134.md) (CCDI), the party's internal disciplinary body, and the [National Supervisory Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Supervisory_Commission), the highest anti-corruption agency of China.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

The next year in February he was expelled from the [Chinese Communist Party](7175.md).[^cite_note-6]
