The **Air Force Intelligence Directorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): إدارة المخابرات الجوية, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Idarat al-Mukhabarat al-Jawiyya*) was an air force intelligence service of [Ba'athist Syria](57134800.md) from 1970 until 2024,[^cite_note-espionageinfo1-2] owing its importance to [Hafez al-Assad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafez_al-Assad)'s role as the [Syrian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Air_Force) commander.[^cite_note-espionageinfo1-2] Despite its name, it was mainly involved with issues other than air force intelligence,[^cite_note-3] and took an active part in the suppression of the [Muslim Brotherhood rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamist_uprising_in_Syria) in the 1980s.[^cite_note-www1-4] Agents of this service had frequently been stationed in Syrian embassies or branch offices of the [national airline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Air).[^cite_note-5]

## History

The service was headed for seventeen years by Maj. Gen. [Muhammad al-Khuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_al-Khuli), who was trusted by [Hafez al-Assad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafez_al-Assad) and had an office adjacent to the president's in the presidential palace.[^cite_note-www1-4] Between 1987 and 2002, it was headed by [Ibrahim Huwayji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Huweija).[^cite_note-6] The service also took part in the efforts to put down the [2011 Syrian uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Syrian_uprising) against Bashar al-Assad's government. It is known to have been active in the town of [Talkalakh](10549735.md) near the [Lebanese border](46232625.md).[^cite_note-7] From 2009 until July 2019, the agency was headed by Major General [Jamil Hassan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamil_Hassan), who is from [Alawite sect](436616.md).[^cite_note-8]

From 2019 to 2024, it was headed by Major General [Ghassan Ismail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghassan_Ismail).[^cite_note-Ghassan-9][^cite_note-Hendi-10] Ismail previously served as a security official in the eastern [Deir ez-Zor Governorate](7445749.md). He was a part of [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md)'s inner circle.[^cite_note-11]

## Role

Military experts considered SAFI "the most powerful and notorious intelligence and security service in Syria", while the [European Center for Constitutional and Human Rights](31683151.md) called it the "most powerful and most brutal" of Syria's state security agencies. SAFI had a broad mandate as Assad's "personal machinery of repression and extermination," serving as the president's personal action service and having a broad role in external clandestine and covert operations.[^cite_note-Axe2024-12]

Through its extensive network of prisons, SAFI imprisoned, tortured, and killed hundreds of thousands of Syrians.[^cite_note-Axe2024-12]

## International investigations

After the [fall of the Assad regime](78548066.md), the [U.S. Department of Justice](52563.md) charged former SAFI officers [Jamil Hassan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamil_Hassan) and Abdul Salam Mahmoud with "conspiracy to commit war crimes through the infliction of cruel and inhuman treatment on detainees under their control."[^cite_note-Axe2024-12]
