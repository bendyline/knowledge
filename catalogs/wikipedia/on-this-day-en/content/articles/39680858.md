The **Lagos–Badagry Expressway** is the local name for the Nigerian section of the [Trans–West African Coastal Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans%E2%80%93West_African_Coastal_Highway).[^cite_note-Vanguard2013-1] The expressway connects [Lagos, Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagos,_Nigeria) with [Dakar, Senegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakar,_Senegal).

Extensive reconstruction of the Lagos portion of the expressway began in 2010.[^cite_note-Vanguard2013-1][^cite_note-BusinessDay2013-02-2] When those renovations are completed the Lagos portion of the expressway will be widened from four lanes to ten lanes for road vehicles and a new [mass transit line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagos_Rail_Mass_Transit) will operate in the median. Two of the expressway's lanes are intended to be exclusively used by the [Lagos Bus Rapid Transit System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagos_Bus_Rapid_Transit_System).[^cite_note-3]
