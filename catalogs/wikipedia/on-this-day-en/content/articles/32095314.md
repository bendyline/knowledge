General elections were held in the [Bahamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahamas) on 14 March 1997.[^cite_note-DN-1] The result was a victory for the [Free National Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_National_Movement), which won 34 of the 40 seats. [Hubert Ingraham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Ingraham) was sworn in for a second term as Prime Minister on 18 March.[^cite_note-2]

The 1997 elections were the last time that a Prime Minister was re-elected for a second consecutive term until [2026](68515581.md).
