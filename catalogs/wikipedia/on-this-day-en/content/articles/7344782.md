# List of diplomatic missions of Iran

&#x20;This is a **list of diplomatic missions of Iran**. [Iran](14653.md) has a substantial diplomatic network, reflecting its foreign affairs priorities within the Islamic and Non-Aligned world.

In [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) the [Embassy of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interests_Section_of_the_Islamic_Republic_of_Iran_in_the_United_States) [looks after the interests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interests_section) of Iran in the United States.[^cite_note-1]
