The **federal government of Iraq** is the central executive authority of the [Republic of Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Iraq). The [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_\(Iraq\)) is the organ that represents it.

## Government

### Executive branch

The executive branch is composed of the [President](209174.md) and the [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Iraq).[^cite_note-1]

#### President

Main article: [President of Iraq](209174.md)

The President of the Republic is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and "safeguards the commitment to the Constitution and the preservation of Iraq's independence, sovereignty, unity, the security of its territories in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution."[^cite_note-2] The President is elected by the Council of Representatives by a two-thirds majority,[^cite_note-ReferenceA-3] and is limited to two four-year terms.[^cite_note-ReferenceC-4] The President ratifies treaties and laws passed by the Council of Representatives, issues [pardons](288593.md) on the recommendation of the [Prime Minister](208879.md), and performs the "duty of the Higher Command of the armed forces for ceremonial and honorary purposes."[^cite_note-ReferenceA-3]

There is also a Vice President which assumes the office of the President in case of his absence or removal.[^cite_note-ReferenceC-4]

#### Council of Ministers

Main articles: [Prime Minister of Iraq](208879.md) and [Council of Ministers of Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Iraq)



Composition of the Iraqi Government in September 2009

The Council of Ministers is composed of the [Prime Minister](208879.md) as [head of government](37570.md) and his [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_\(government\)). The President of Iraq names the nominee of the Council of Representatives bloc with the largest number to form the Cabinet.[^cite_note-5] The Prime Minister is the direct executive authority responsible for the general policy of the State and the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the armed forces, directs the Council of Ministers, and presides over its meetings and has the right to dismiss the Ministers on the consent of the Council of Representatives.[^cite_note-6]

The cabinet is responsible for overseeing their respective ministries, proposing laws, preparing the budget, negotiating and signing international agreements and treaties, and appointing undersecretaries, ambassadors, the Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces and his assistants, Division Commanders or higher, the Director of the National Intelligence Service, and heads of security institutions.[^cite_note-7]
