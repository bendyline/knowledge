**Bimbo** (also, **Bimo**) is the [second-largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_the_Central_African_Republic)[^cite_note-1] of the [Central African Republic](5478.md), located southwest of the capital, [Bangui](56617.md). Bimbo had a population of 124,176 as of the 2003 census and a calculated 2013 population of 267,859.[^cite_note-WG-2] Bimbo used to be the capital city of Ombella-M'Poko until December 2020. Since December 2020, Bimbo has been part of [Bangui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangui_\(Prefecture\)) Prefecture.[^cite_note-3]

Bimbo is the site of the country's only sex-segregated women's prison, Bimbo Central Prison. The prison was built in 1980 to hold 200 prisoners, although as of 2005 it only held 44, most of whom were pre-trial detainees.[^cite_note-4]

## History

Bimbo was founded in 1908.[^cite_note-5]

On 20 November 1964, Bimbo became the capital of Ombella-M'Poko for two years until it was replaced by [Boali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boali). However, Bimbo was reinstated as the capital of Ombella-M'Poko in 1969.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] On 10 December 2020, Bimbo was transferred from Ombella-M'Poko prefecture to Bangui prefecture, and once again Boali became the capital of Ombella-M'Poko.

## Climate

[Köppen-Geiger climate classification system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen-Geiger_climate_classification_system) classifies its climate as [tropical wet and dry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_wet_and_dry_climate) (Aw).[^cite_note-Climate-Data.org-8]

The highest record temperature was 44 °C (111 °F) on February 25, 2004, while the lowest record temperature was 3 °C (37 °F) on January 22, 1993.[^cite_note-Voodoo_Skies-9]
