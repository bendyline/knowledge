**Tangail** ([Bengali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_language): টাঙ্গাইল, [\[ʈaŋgail\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Bengali)) is a city of [Tangail District](1897981.md) in central [Bangladesh](3454.md).[^cite_note-3] The establishment of the Tangail began with 3269 square kilometres of land requisitioned from Mymensingh. Earlier it was a Mahakuma of Mymensingh district. It was once contributed 9% GDP while being part of Greater Mymensingh and was rich area in Bangladesh. After being independent district, the economy of Tangail collapse. Now several development project taken to establish and strengthen the economy of poorly developed Tangail under Dhaka Division. Tangail lies on the bank of the [Louhajang River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louhajang_River).[^cite_note-The_Daily_Star,_Bangladesh-4][^cite_note-\:0-5]

## Etymology

Many believe the name *Tangail* originated from the [Bengali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_language) word *[tanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanga_\(cart\))*, meaning horse cart.

## History

Tangail has been a local business center since the early 19th century.[^cite_note-6] In 1860, Tangail became the 4th ranking area of the [Greater Mymensingh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mymensingh_Division) District due to its fertile land near the [Louhajong River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louhajang_River). It was close to [Begun Bari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Begun_Bari), [Mymensingh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mymensingh). In 1969, [Tangail District](1897981.md) was established.

### Tangail Airdrop

The [Tangail Airdrop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangail_Airdrop) was a successful battalion-size operation by India's [Para Commandos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_\(Special_Forces\)), mounted on 11 December 1971, by the 2nd Battalion (Special Operations) (2 PARA) of the Indian Army's [Parachute Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parachute_Regiment_\(India\)) during the [Indo-Pakistani War of 1971](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Pakistani_War_of_1971) for the liberation of Bangladesh. The operation's main objective was capturing Poongli Bridge on the [Jamuna River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamuna_River), which would cut off the Pakistani 93rd Brigade, which was retreating from Mymensingh in the north to defend the capital of East Pakistan, Dhaka, and its approaches. The paratroop unit was also tasked with linking up with the advancing [Maratha Light Infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maratha_Light_Infantry) on the ground to advance toward the East Pakistani capital.

The [Pourasabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Municipal_Corporations_of_Bangladesh) ([municipal corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_corporation)) was established on 1 July 1887, and initially divided the city into five wards:

This was later restructured into four wards - Ward No. 1 (Central), 2 (Betka), 3 (Dighulia), and 4 (Santosh) - and then, in 1988, into six wards with the addition of Wards 5 (Zila Sadar) and 6 (Kazipur).

The first city election was held in November 1887, when the citizens elected eight ward commissioners from four wards. The Subdivision Commissioner of Tangail, Shashi Shekhar Dutt, was appointed as the first administrator of Tangail City. The city needed to be better developed, lacking paved roads and roadside lamps; subsequently, the regional [zamindars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamindar) and subdivision board provided financial support to dig ponds, lakes, and [canals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canal), creating a safe [water supply](684928.md).[^cite_note-\:1-7]

Electricity was established in the city in the early 1930s. [Paved roads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paved_road) were constructed in the 1960s, connecting the town to [Dhaka](56656.md). Simultaneously, [bridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridges) and [culverts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culverts) were installed.[^cite_note-\:3-8]

In 1985, Tangail was promoted from a *C* to a *B Class* city. In 1989, the [Pourashava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pourashava) was promoted to *A-Class*. In the 1990s, the city was financed by the [Asian Development Bank](2512.md) and the [Government of Bangladesh](18420484.md) to develop [water supply](684928.md), [sanitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanitation), [wastewater drainage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wastewater), bus terminals, supermarkets, and other infrastructure.[^cite_note-\:4-9]

In 1999, the city was restructured again into 18 wards (its current organization).[^cite_note-10]

## Geography and climate

The city of Tangail is in [Dhaka Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhaka_Division), Central [Bangladesh](3454.md). The town lies in a low-lying floodplain near [the Jamuna River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamuna_River_\(Bangladesh\)), south of the high plateau of [the Madhupur tract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhupur_tract). The average elevation of Tangail is 14 meters (49 feet).[^cite_note-13] Tangail experiences a [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) *([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): Cwa)* with a hot, humid tropical [wet season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_season) (monsoon season) and warm, dry [winter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season) with high humidity year-round. The yearly average temperature in Tangail is 25.1 °C, and the average rainfall is 1817 mm.

According to the [2022 Bangladesh census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Bangladeshi_census), Tangail Paurashava had 52,553 households and a population of 212,887. Tangail had a literacy rate of 86.24%: 88.63% for males and 83.88% for females, and a sex ratio of 99.83 males per 100 females. 6.82% of the population was under 5 years of age.[^cite_note-2022census-17]

According to the [2011 Bangladesh census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Bangladesh_census), Tangail Paurashava had 37,607 households and a population of 167,412. 30,882 (18.45%) were under 10 years of age. Tangail has a literacy rate (age 7 and over) of 71.78%, compared to the national average of 59.5%, and a sex ratio of 976 females per 1000 males.[^cite_note-community-18]

## Sports

At the city's centre, the multipurpose [Tangail Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangail_Stadium) regularly hosts sporting events.[^cite_note-19] The stadium has hosted national events.

## Transport



The "New Bus Terminal", Dewla, Tangail.

[Jamuna Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamuna_Bridge), the second-longest [bridge in Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_bridges_in_Bangladesh), connects Tangail and [Sirajganj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirajganj).[^cite_note-20]

It takes approximately 1 hour and 55 minutes to reach Tangail from [Dhaka](56656.md) (about 98 km away) via [Kaliakair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaliakair) and [Tongi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tongi). Several bus lines operate between Tangail and Dhaka's [Mohakhali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohakhali) (মহাখালী) bus terminal. The Nirala, Dhaleshwari, Jathika, and High Choice bus lines are among them.

[Tangail railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangail_railway_station) offers travel to Dhaka and other cities within Bangladesh. The [inter-city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-city_rail) [Ekota Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekota_Express), [Sundarban Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundarban_Express), [Rangpur Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rangpur_Express), Intercity Tangail Commuter and Sirajganj Express (among others) serve the station alongside [commuter rail](6286.md) and mail train services.[^cite_note-Bangladesh_Railway-21]

[Tangail Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangail_Airport) was opened in 1967 for agricultural purposes but has been unmaintained since 1976.

## Parks

Tangail contains numerous parks, including Tangail Poura Uddan (one of the city's most-visited places), DC Lake, SP Park, and Soul Park. The town also contains part of the [Madhupur National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhupur_National_Park), one of Bangladesh's oldest [national parks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_park).
