**İlker Çatak** (born in 1984 in [Berlin](3354.md)) is a German film director and screenwriter of Turkish origin. He is known for *[The Teachers' Lounge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Teachers'_Lounge)* (2023), which received a nomination for the [Academy Award for Best International Feature Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film) at the [96th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/96th_Academy_Awards)[^cite_note-1], and for *[Yellow Letters](82175912.md)* (2026), which won the [Golden Bear](21547166.md) at the [76th Berlin International Film Festival](81825397.md) on 13 February 2026.[^cite_note-2]

## Career



Çatak in 2015

İlker Çatak was born in [Berlin](3354.md) in 1984 to [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people) immigrants. At the age of twelve, he moved to [Istanbul](3391396.md), Turkey, and graduated there from the embassy school. He then returned to Germany and worked for German and international cinema productions. From 2005, he directed short films and commercials. In 2009, he graduated with a bachelor's degree in Film and Television Directing at the Dekra Medienhochschule Berlin.[^cite_note-3] His short film *When Namibia was a city...* (Original: *Namibya şehir iken...*, co-directed by Johannes Duncker) premiered at the [Hof International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hof_International_Film_Festival) in 2010.[^cite_note-4] From 2012-2014, he studied Directing at the Hamburg Media School. The short film *Where we are* (German: *Wo wir sind*) was selected for the talent showcase Next Generation Short Tiger[^cite_note-5] and a finalist for the 2014 [Student Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student_Academy_Awards).[^cite_note-6] His graduation short *Fidelity* (Original: *Sadakat*) won the Foreign Film Gold Medal at the 2015 [Student Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student_Academy_Awards).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] Both films won the Best Short Film Award at the Filmfestival Max-Ophüls-Prize Saarbrücken in 2014 and 2015,[^cite_note-9] among other awards. Çatak's feature directorial debut *Once upon a time... Indianerland* (German: *Es war einmal Indianerland*) was released in German cinemas in 2017. His next feature film *[I Was, I Am, I Will Be](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Was,_I_Am,_I_Will_Be)* (German: *Es gilt das gesprochene Wort*, 2019) won the [German Film Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Film_Award) (Bronze) in 2020 and was nominated for Best Director, Best Screenplay, Best Actress and Best Supporting Actor.[^cite_note-10] *Blurred Lines* (German: *Räuberhände*, 2020) won the [Bavarian Film Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Film_Awards) for Best Young Actor in 2022.

In February 2023, his feature *[The Teachers' Lounge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Teachers'_Lounge)* (German: *Das Lehrerzimmer*) premiered at the [73rd Berlin International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/73rd_Berlin_International_Film_Festival). The film won five [German Film Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Film_Award) including [Best Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Film_Award_for_Best_Fiction_Film) (Gold), [Best Director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Film_Award_for_Best_Director), Best Screenplay, Best Actress and Best Editing.[^cite_note-11] Çatak was also nominated for the [European Film Award for Best Screenwriter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Film_Award_for_Best_Screenwriter) for the film.[^cite_note-12] The film screened at numerous international film festivals and was released theatrically in many countries. In 2024, *The Teachers' Lounge* was nominated for the [Academy Award for Best International Feature Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film).[^cite_note-13]

In June 2024, Çatak was invited to become a member of the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences).[^cite_note-14]

In 2026, his feature film *[Yellow Letters](82175912.md)* (German: *Gelbe Briefe*) had its world premiere at the main competition of the [76th Berlin International Film Festival](81825397.md), where it won the [Golden Bear](21547166.md), the festival's main prize.
