**Tanhuato** is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Michoac%C3%A1n) in the [Mexican](3966054.md) [state](8196151.md) of [Michoacán](195805.md). It is located approximately 135 kilometres (84 mi) northwest of the state capital of [Morelia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morelia).

## Geography

The municipality of Tanhuato is located at an elevation between 1,600 and 2,000 metres (5,200–6,600 ft) in the [Bajío](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baj%C3%ADo) region in northwestern Michoacán. It borders the Michoacanese municipalities of [Yurécuaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yur%C3%A9cuaro) to the east, [Ecuandureo](46784367.md) to the southeast, [Ixtlán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ixtl%C3%A1n,_Michoacan) to the southwest, and [Vista Hermosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vista_Hermosa,_Michoac%C3%A1n) to the west.[^cite_note-compendium-6] Additionally, the [Lerma River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lerma_River) forms the border between Tanhuato and the Jaliscan municipalities of [Ayotlán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayotl%C3%A1n) to the northeast and [La Barca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Barca_Municipality) to the northwest. The municipality covers an area of 228.732 square kilometres (88.314 sq mi)[^cite_note-infocard-3] and comprises 0.39% of the state's area.[^cite_note-compendium-6]

As of 2009, the land cover in Tanhuato consists of tropical forest (31%) and grassland (9%). More than half (57%) of the land is used for agriculture and 1.9% consists of urban areas. The main soil types are [vertisols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertisol) (74%) and [phaeozems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phaeozem) (23%).[^cite_note-compendium-6] The Gonzalo Reservoir lies on the border between Tanhuato and Vista Hermosa and has a total capacity of 7,600,000 cubic metres (270,000,000 cu ft) and a useful capacity of 6,600,000 cubic metres (230,000,000 cu ft).[^cite_note-7]

Tanhuato has a temperate climate with rain in the summer.[^cite_note-encyclopedia-2] Average temperatures in the municipality range between 18 and 22 °C (64–72 °F), and average annual precipitation ranges between 700 and 1,000 millimetres (28–39 in).[^cite_note-compendium-6]

## History

The place name Tanhuato is of [Chichimeca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chichimeca) origin, meaning "near the hill". In pre-Hispanic times the area was part of the [Purépecha Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pur%C3%A9pecha_Empire).[^cite_note-encyclopedia-2]

Tanhuato was one of the original municipalities established in Michoacán in 1831.[^cite_note-encyclopedia-2] The municipality was disestablished and incorporated into Yurécuaro at various periods in the 19th century (1856–1861, 1863–1868, and 1874–1877).[^cite_note-10] In 1902, the municipal seat was renamed Tanhuato de Guerrero in honour of former Mexican president [Vicente Guerrero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Guerrero).[^cite_note-encyclopedia-2]

## Administration

The municipal government of Tanhuato comprises a president, a councillor ([Spanish](26825.md): *síndico*), and seven trustees (*regidores*), four elected by [relative majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_majority) and three by [proportional representation](23298.md).[^cite_note-encyclopedia-2] The current president of the municipality is Héctor Daniel Aranda Pérez.[^cite_note-11]

## Demographics

In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Tanhuato recorded a population of 15,176 inhabitants living in 3767 households.[^cite_note-catalog-12] The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 15,352 inhabitants in Tanhuato.[^cite_note-survey-4]

[INEGI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INEGI) lists 18 localities in the municipality,[^cite_note-snim-1] of which only the municipal seat Tanhuato de Guerrero is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 9546 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.[^cite_note-catalog-12]

## Economy and infrastructure

Agriculture is the main economic activity in Tanhuato. Major crops grown include wheat, sorghum, corn, vegetables (e.g., chiles[^cite_note-13] and hydroponically grown tomatoes), blue agave and beans. Tanhuato is particularly known for its alfalfa and chickpea production. Livestock raised include cattle, pigs, goats, poultry and bees.[^cite_note-plan-14]

The toll expressway [Federal Highway 15D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Federal_Highway_15D) runs through the municipality, as does [Federal Highway 110](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Federal_Highway_110).
