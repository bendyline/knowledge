# Iran at the FIFA World Cup



Iran national football team at the [2018 FIFA World Cup](13327177.md) in Russia

[Iran](272865.md) have appeared in the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) on seven occasions: in [1978](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_FIFA_World_Cup), [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup), [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup), [2014](656933.md), [2018](13327177.md), [2022](17742072.md), and [2026](36581929.md). They are yet to progress from the group stages.[^cite_note-\:1-1][^cite_note-2]

In March 2025, they qualified for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) following a 2–2 home draw against [Uzbekistan](1032413.md) in the [third round of AFC qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_%E2%80%93_AFC_third_round) and will make a seventh finals appearance.[^cite_note-3]
