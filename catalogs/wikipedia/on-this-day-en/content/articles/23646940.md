# Multilateral trade negotiations

The term **multilateral trade negotiations** (MTN) initially applied to negotiations between [General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Agreement_on_Tariffs_and_Trade) (GATT) member nations conducted under the auspices of the GATT and aimed at reducing tariff and nontariff trade barriers. In 1995 the [World Trade Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Organization) (WTO) replaced the GATT as the administrative body. A current round of multilateral trade negotiations was conducted in the [Doha Development Agenda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doha_Development_Agenda) round.

Prior to the ongoing [Doha Development Round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doha_Development_Round), eight GATT sessions took place:&#x20;

- 1st Round: Geneva Round, 1947

- 2nd Round: [Annecy Round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annecy_Round), 1949

- 3rd Round: [Torquay Round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torquay_Round), 1950-51

- 4th Round: [Geneva Round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_Round), 1955-56

- 5th Round: [Dillon Round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dillon_Round), 1960-61

- 6th Round: [Kennedy Round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Round), 1963-67

- 7th Round: [Tokyo Round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Round), 1973-79

- 8th Round: [Uruguay Round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguay_Round), 1986-94

The third [WTO Ministerial Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministerial_Conference) in Seattle, 1999, was intended to start the [Millennium Round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennium_Round) of negotiations.
