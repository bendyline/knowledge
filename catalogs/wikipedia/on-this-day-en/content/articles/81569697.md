The **2027 NCAA Division I men's basketball tournament** will involve a total of 76 teams playing in a [single-elimination tournament](939680.md) to help determine the [National Collegiate Athletic Association](60706.md) (NCAA) [Division I](669931.md) men's [college basketball](768856.md) national champion for the [2026–27 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_NCAA_Division_I_men's_basketball_season).[^cite_note-1] The 88th edition of the tournament is scheduled to begin on March 16, 2027, and it will conclude with the [championship game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_NCAA_Division_I_men's_basketball_championship_game) on April 5, 2027, at [Ford Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Field) in [Detroit](8687.md). This tournament will mark the introduction of the Opening Round and an expansion of the field of participants from 68 teams to 76.

## Tournament procedure

Further information: [NCAA Division I men's basketball tournament § Format](507399.md#Format)

On May 7, 2026, the NCAA announced that the men's and women's Division I tournaments will expand to 76 teams beginning in 2027. The field will consist of 32 automatic bids awarded to each program that wins a [conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NCAA_conferences#Division_I) championship, and 44 "at-large" bids (an increase from 36) extended by the NCAA selection committee. The selection committee will then [seed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seeding_\(sports\)) the teams from 1 to 76. Twenty-four teams (the 12 lowest-seeded automatic qualifiers and the 12 lowest-seeded at-large teams) will compete in the "March Madness Opening Round" (an expanded version of the First Four); the winners of these games will advance to the main 64-team bracket.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Media coverage

### Television

Main article: [NCAA March Madness (TV Program)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_March_Madness_\(TV_Program\))

[CBS Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS_Sports) and [TNT Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TNT_Sports_\(United_States\)) will have US television rights to the tournament.[^cite_note-wp=2032extend-6] As part of a cycle that began in 2016, [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS) will televise the Final Four and the national championship game.

#### Television channels

- Selection Show – [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS)

- Opening round – [truTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TruTV)

- Regional semifinals and finals – CBS and TBS

- National semifinals (Final Four) and championship – CBS
