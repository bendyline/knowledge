The **Kosovo Specialist Chambers** (**KSC**; [Albanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_language): *Dhomat e Specializuara të Kosovës*; [Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): Специјализована већа Косова, *Specijalizovana veća Kosova*) is a court of [Kosovo](17391.md), located in [The Hague](30269.md) (Netherlands), hosting four *Specialist Chambers* and the *Specialist Prosecutor's Office*, which may perform their activities either in the [Netherlands](21148.md) or in Kosovo. The court is currently set up for delegating the trials of the [crimes](25576082.md) committed by members of the [Kosovo Liberation Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosovo_Liberation_Army) (KLA), an [ethnic-Albanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanians) [paramilitary](146730.md) organisation which sought the separation of Kosovo from [Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FR_Yugoslavia) during the 1990s.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The alleged crimes concern the period 1998–2000, during and at the end of the [Kosovo War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosovo_war) and directed afterwards against "ethnic minorities and political opponents".[^cite_note-announcementNL-6] The court was formally established in 2016.[^cite_note-letterNL-7] It is separate from other Kosovar institutions, and independent. It is composed of a Specialist Prosecutor's Office and four Specialist Chambers, with themselves comprising Judges' Chambers and a Registry.

In December 2016 [Ekaterina Trendafilova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaterina_Trendafilova) was elected first president.[^cite_note-8] Among the people charged with [war crimes](48626.md) and [crimes against humanity](61099.md) are Kosovo former president [Hashim Thaçi](3626980.md) and senior [Kosovar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Kosovo) politician [Kadri Veseli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadri_Veseli).[^cite_note-9] On 15 September 2021 the court's first trial opened, the case against [Salih Mustafa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salih_Mustafa).[^cite_note-10]

In 2010, Swiss politician [Dick Marty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Marty) authored a [Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Europe)-report in which he noted [war crimes](48626.md) had been committed by the KLA. Partly based on that report, the prosecutor of the Special Investigative Taskforce (SITF) of the [European Union Rule of Law Mission in Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union_Rule_of_Law_Mission_in_Kosovo) (EULEX Kosovo) concluded sufficient evidence existed for prosecution of "war crimes, crimes against humanity as well as certain crimes against Kosovan law".[^cite_note-letterNL-7] The court is located outside Kosovo on request of the prosecutor in order to provide adequate protection to witnesses.[^cite_note-letterNL-7]

The Kosovo authorities have agreed with the EU on modalities of dealing with those serious allegations. On 3 August 2015, the [Kosovo Assembly](1068656.md) adopted Article 162 of the [Kosovo Constitution](9516144.md) and the Law on Specialist Chambers and Specialist Prosecutor's Office, following the Exchange of Letters between the President of Kosovo and the High Representative of the European Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy in 2014. The Specialist Chambers are attached to each level of the court system in Kosovo – Basic Court, Court of Appeals, Supreme Court and Constitutional Court. They will function according to relevant Kosovo laws as well as customary international law and international human rights law.

The EU has supported the process from the outset and together with other contributing countries ([Canada](5042916.md), [Norway](21241.md), [Switzerland](26748.md), [Turkey](11125639.md), and the [United States of America](3434750.md)) will financially support the work of the court.

The Specialist Chambers comprises two organs, the Chambers and the Registry. The Specialist Prosecutor's Office is an independent office for the investigation and prosecution of the crimes within the jurisdiction of the Specialist Chambers. The Specialist Chambers and the Specialist Prosecutor's Office are staffed with international judges, prosecutors and officers and have a seat in The Hague, the Netherlands.

## Legal basis and organisation

Unlike many other non-Dutch judicial institutions in The Hague, the Kosovo Relocated Specialist Judicial Institution isn't an [international court](433437.md), but a court constituted through Kosovan legislation. To provide a proper legal basis for the court, Kosovo's [constitution](9516144.md) was amended (amendment 24)[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-letterNL-7] and Law No.05/L-053 *on specialist chambers and specialist prosecutor's office* was approved.[^cite_note-12]

The court will be staffed by EU personnel and will have international judges only. The costs of the court will be borne by the EU[^cite_note-13] as part of its [Common Foreign and Security Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Foreign_and_Security_Policy).[^cite_note-letterNL-7] The four specialized chambers are all chambers of corresponding regular Kosovar institutions:

- The court of first instance of Pristina

- The court of Appeal

- Supreme Court

- Constitutional Court
