The **National Anthem of the Islamic Republic of Iran**[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-\:0-2] was adopted in 1990, replacing the [previous anthem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payandeh_Bada_Iran) used during the rule of [Ruhollah Khomeini](26232.md).[^cite_note-3] It was composed by Hassan Riyahi, and the lyrics were written by Sayed Bagheri. It is the fourth national anthem of [Iran](14653.md) overall.[^cite_note-4]

## History

Due to the death of [Supreme Leader](36814344.md) Ruhollah Khomeini in 1989, Iran adopted its current anthem after a competition the following year.[^cite_note-5] At the time of its adoption, it was the second shortest anthem in the world; the first being [Japanese](15573.md) national anthem "[Kimigayo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimigayo)" of seven lines.
