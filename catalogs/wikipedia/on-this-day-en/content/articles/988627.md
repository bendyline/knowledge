**Shangla District** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): شنګله ولسوالۍ, [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضلع شانگلہ) is a [district](639402.md) in [Malakand Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malakand_Division) of [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md), Pakistan. The district's headquarters are located at [Alpuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpuri).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEPCO19988-3] The district was established in 1995, having previously been a subdivision of [Swat District](1236283.md).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEPCO19981-4] The total area of the district is 1,586 square kilometers.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEPCO19981-4] Shangla comprises five subdivisions, [Alpuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpuri_Tehsil), [Puran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puran_Tehsil), [Chakesar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chakesar), [Martung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martung) and [Besham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Besham) tehsils.

## Location

The district is bounded in the north by [Kohistan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohistan_District,_Pakistan), in the east by [Battagram District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battagram_District) and [Torghar District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torghar_District), in the west by [Swat District](1236283.md), and in the south by [Buner District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buner_District).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEPCO19981-4]

## History

Shangla was a sub-division of the Swat district in the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province of Pakistan until it was granted the status of a separate district in 1995. This was primarily due to the efforts of Haji Badi uz Zaman, a political leader and community figure who was assassinated in 1994, and his son, Afsar ul Mulk.&#x20;

There are relics of the ancient Greek period at Pirsar, [Chakesar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chakesar) and Daut. It is believed that [Alexander the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_the_Great) camped at Pirsar for a few days.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] There are also relics of the Hindu Shahi in Olandar-Ajmair.[^cite_note-10] [^cite_note-11]

A number of [Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautama_Buddha) sculptures are also found in Shangla district which indicate that Shangla was also a part of the previous Buddhist civilization.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

The district was hit by the [2015 Hindu Kush earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_2015_Hindu_Kush_earthquake) in which more than 18 people were killed.[^cite_note-15]

## Geography

Shangla district consists of small valleys and is situated between the hillocks and is surrounded by high mountains full of forests comprising [Pindrow Fir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abies_pindrow), [Morinda Spruce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picea_smithiana), [Blue Pine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinus_wallichiana) (Kail), [Chir Pine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinus_roxburghii) and [Deodar Cedar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cedrus_deodara) trees. The average elevation of the district is 2000 to 3000 meters above sea level.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEPCO19981-4] The highest point (3,440 m) is near Kuz Ganrshal in the north of the district.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEPCO19981-4] Shangla District is connected to the [Swat District](1236283.md) by the [Shangla Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shangla_Pass).

As of the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), Shangla district has 125,540 households and a population of 891,252. The district has a sex ratio of 106.65 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 33.74%: 48.82% for males and 17.82% for females. 307,444 (34.51% of the surveyed population) are under 10 years of age. The entire population lives in rural areas.[^cite_note-2023_census-1] 2,410 (0.27%) people in the district are from religious minorities, mainly Christians.[^cite_note-2023censusreligion-17]

Languages of Shangla district (2023)

1. [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) (95.4%)

2. [Kohistani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dardic_languages) (1.03%)

3. Others (3.57%)

[Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) was the predominant language, spoken by 95.40% of the population. 1.03% of the population spoke [Kohistani languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dardic_languages).[^cite_note-2023censuslanguage-18]

The main tribe of Shangla is [Yusufzai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yusufzai), that contribute more than half of the district population, the dense and well populated area of Shangla is Alpuri tehsil (Gwarband) that contributes to more than 253000+ of the district population. Shangla is one of the unique districts of Pakistan that contains more than 80% of forest land. The youngest noble laurate [Malala Yousafzai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malala_Yousafzai)'s father, [Ziauddin Yousafzai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziauddin_Yousafzai), was born in Shangla, however, she herself was born in Mingora, Swat.[^cite_note-19]

## Education

In November 2022, the Chief Minister of [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md), [Mahmood Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmood_Khan), inaugurated University of Shangla.[^cite_note-20] The university was earlier a campus of University of Swat.[^cite_note-21]

## National and provincial assembly

This district is represented by one elected MNA (Member of National Assembly) in [Pakistan National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_National_Assembly). Its constituency is [NA-31](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency_NA-31).[^cite_note-22]
