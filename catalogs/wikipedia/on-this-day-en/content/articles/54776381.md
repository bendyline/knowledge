The **Ministry of Energy** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): وزارت توانائی, [*Trans.*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Urdu) *Wazarat-e-Tawanai*, abbreviated as: **MoE**) is a [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Pakistan)-level [executive ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) of the federal [Government of Pakistan](1897833.md) that is charged with implementation of the national [energy policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_policy_of_Pakistan) and [energy production and electricity transmission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electricity_sector_in_Pakistan) throughout the country.

The MoE functions are split in two divisions: [Petroleum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleum_Division_\(Pakistan\)) and [Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_Division_\(Pakistan\)) – each independent of its tasks and objectives. The MoE oversees overall domestic [hydropower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydropower_in_Pakistan) generation, implementing the energy security policies, petroleum and energy production by means of alternative energy sources, and [energy conservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_conservation).&#x20;

The [MoE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Energy_\(Petroleum_Division\)) is headed by an elected Minister of Energy who is assisted by Minister of States (as its deputies) and secretaries of each division to implement the MoE's policies and works.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

In spite of [nuclear power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_power_in_Pakistan) serving as a critical component of the energy grid system, the Ministry of Energy does not oversee the nuclear power generation but its responsibility is vested with the [Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Atomic_Energy_Commission) (PAEC).

## Overview

### History

From [1947](23235.md) until 2017, the former Ministry of Water and Power (MoWP) was charged with overseeing the energy projects and water resources management. In Pakistan, the word ["Power"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_\(physics\)) is used to address energy, energy conservation and production as it deals directly with the subject of [electricity](9550.md), which includes generation, [transmission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transmission_tower) and distribution.[^cite_note-MoE,_History-3]

The [Ministry of Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Energy_\(Petroleum_Division\)) **(**&#x4D;o&#x45;**)** was created per the recommendation from the [World Bank](36752.md) to "help the federal government to improve coordination in power production and installation of new energy projects under the [China–Pakistan Economic Corridor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China%E2%80%93Pakistan_Economic_Corridor) (CPEC).[^cite_note-Gulf_Times,_2017-4]

## Power Division

Main article: [Power Division (Pakistan)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_Division_\(Pakistan\))

Responsible for electricity generation, transmission, distribution and policy matters pertaining to these three functions.[^cite_note-MoE,_History-3] The MoE funds twenty two public sector companies and two regulatory bodies that works under the Power Division.[^cite_note-MoE,_History-3] The Power Division has its own departmental director – the [Power Secretary of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_Secretary_of_Pakistan).[^cite_note-MoE,_History-3]
