The **Progressive Union Renewal** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Union progressiste le renouveau*, **UPR**), formerly called **Progressive Union**, is a political party in Benin, led by [Joseph Djogbenou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Djogbenou). It tends to be more popular in the south of the country.[^cite_note-2]

In the [2019 Beninese parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Beninese_parliamentary_election), the party came first, winning 47 of 83 seats in the [National Assembly](4458651.md).[^cite_note-2019_election-3] Both the Progressive Union and the only other party in the National Assembly, [Republican Bloc](63274694.md), are allied with President [Patrice Talon](49884967.md).[^cite_note-2019_election-3][^cite_note-BBC-4] The current [Vice President of Benin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Benin), [Mariam Chabi Talata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariam_Chabi_Talata), is a UPR member.[^cite_note-5] In July 2022, Bruno Amoussou retired and left the presidency of the UP to [Joseph Djogbenou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Djogbenou).[^cite_note-6] In August 2022, the party merged with the [Democratic Renewal Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Renewal_Party_\(Benin\)) and changed to its present name.[^cite_note-7]
