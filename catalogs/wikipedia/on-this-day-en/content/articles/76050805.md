# List of communes in France with over 20,000 inhabitants

For all communes of France sorted by department, see [Lists of communes of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_communes_of_France).



Map of [metropolitan France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_France)

As of January 2023, there were 482 [communes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in [France](5843419.md) ([metropolitan territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_France) and [overseas departments and regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_departments_and_regions_of_France)) with population over 20,000, 287 communes with population over 30,000, 133 communes with population over 50,000 and 42 communes with population over 100,000.[^cite_note-pop2023-1] All figures reflect the municipal population ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *population municipale*), meaning people who have their usual residence in the commune,[^cite_note-def-2] excluding [population counted apart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_without_double_counting).[^cite_note-3] The population of the matching [urban unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_unit) is usually several times that of its central commune. Populations as of 2006 and 2013 are also shown.[^cite_note-pop2006-4][^cite_note-pop2013-5] Communes in the [overseas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_collectivity) and *[sui generis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_France#Sui_generis_collectivity)* collectivities are listed in a [separate table](#Overseas_and_sui_generis_collectivities) below.
