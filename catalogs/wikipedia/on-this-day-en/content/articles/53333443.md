**France–South Africa relations** are the [diplomatic relations](28165911.md) between [France](5843419.md) and [South Africa](17416221.md). Between 1961 and 1974, France was South Africa's largest supplier of weaponry. Both nations are members of the [G-20 major economies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-20_major_economies) and the [United Nations](31769.md). The two states have strong economic ties; France was South Africa's 9th largest importer as of 2015.[^cite_note-1]

## History

France already maintained strong ties to South Africa during the [apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid) era, becoming the foremost [arms exporter](608296.md) to the regime from the 1960s onwards. As the international pressure to [sanction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_sanctions_during_apartheid) and [disinvest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disinvestment_from_South_Africa) from South Africa increased, France continued these exports, but sought more covert ways to do so (namely [outsourcing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outsourcing) its weapons production to South Africa and using [third-party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third-party_source) countries that had secret agreements with the South Africans).[^cite_note-2] It also provided substantial assistance to the [nuclear research and weapons programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa_and_weapons_of_mass_destruction#Nuclear_weapons) of South Africa throughout the 1960s and 1970s.[^cite_note-3]

France cut off diplomatic relations with South Africa in 1981 and restored relations in 1992.[^cite_note-AldenMartin2003-4] More recently, France has responded to its [diminishing influence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_military_withdrawal_from_West_Africa_\(2022%E2%80%93present\)) in [Francophone Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7afrique) by strengthening economic ties with African [Anglophone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English-speaking_world) nations, particularly [Nigeria](21383.md) and South Africa, which became France's largest trading partners in Africa.[^cite_note-\:5-5]

## Resident diplomatic missions

- France has an embassy in [Pretoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretoria) and consulates-general in [Cape Town](6653.md) and [Johannesburg](58893.md).

- South Africa has an embassy in [Paris](22989.md).
