**Emiliano Buendía Stati** (born 25 December 1996) is an Argentine professional [footballer](10568.md) who plays as an [attacking midfielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacking_midfielder) or [winger](548981.md#Winger) for [Premier League](11250.md) club [Aston Villa](42173.md) and the [Argentina national team](454699.md).

Buendía's senior career began at Spanish club [Getafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Getafe_CF) with whom he spent four years, including a short spell with [Cultural Leonesa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_y_Deportiva_Leonesa), before he moved to England, joining [Norwich City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich_City_F.C.) in 2018. His first season at Norwich saw the club achieve promotion to the [Premier League](11250.md). However, [the next season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Premier_League) saw them relegated back to the [Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship). In his final season at Norwich, he won the [EFL Championship Player of the Season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Awards#2021_Awards) award.

Buendía transferred to Aston Villa in 2021. After falling out of favour by the end of the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Aston_Villa_F.C._season), he re-emerged as a focal player in the [2025–26 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Aston_Villa_F.C._season). In 2026, he won the [Europa League](78224540.md), being named man of the match in the [final](76987231.md).

## Club career

### Getafe

Born in [Mar del Plata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mar_del_Plata), Argentina, Buendía moved to Spain at the age of eleven to join the academy of [Real Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_C.F.).[^cite_note-4] After two years, he moved to [Getafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Getafe_CF).[^cite_note-5] On 30 March 2014, still a junior, he made his senior debut, starting for [the reserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Getafe_CF_B) in a 2–1 away win against [Puerta Bonita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CD_Puerta_Bonita) in the [Segunda División B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segunda_Divisi%C3%B3n_B).[^cite_note-6] He scored his first goal on 13 April, with the first of a 2–0 win away to [Peña Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pe%C3%B1a_Sport_FC).[^cite_note-7] Buendía started the [2014–15 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014%E2%80%9315_Segunda_Divisi%C3%B3n_B) still with the reserve team and in the third level,[^cite_note-8] notably scoring twice in a 3–0 home win over [Fuenlabrada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CF_Fuenlabrada).[^cite_note-9] He played his first match as a professional on 5 December 2014, as a [substitute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitute_\(association_football\)) for [Ángel Lafita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81ngel_Lafita) in a 3–0 home win against [Eibar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SD_Eibar) in the [Copa del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_del_Rey).[^cite_note-10]

Buendía made his [La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga) debut on 1 February 2015, coming on as a late substitute in a 1–0 away loss against [Almería](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UD_Almer%C3%ADa).[^cite_note-11] He scored his first goal in the same competition on 27 September, with the second in a 3–0 home win against [Levante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levante_UD). On 5 July 2016, despite suffering relegation with Getafe at the end of the [2015–16 La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_La_Liga) season, Buendía signed a new five-year contract with the club.[^cite_note-12]

#### Loan to Cultural Leonesa

On 27 July 2017, he was [loaned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loan_\(association_football\)) to fellow second-tier club [Cultural Leonesa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_y_Deportiva_Leonesa), for one year.[^cite_note-13]

### Norwich City

#### 2018–2020



Buendía playing for [Norwich City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich_City_F.C.) in 2019

On 8 June 2018, Buendía signed a four-year deal with [EFL Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship) club [Norwich City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich_City_F.C.).[^cite_note-14] He scored his first goal for Norwich in a 1–0 win over [Brentford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brentford_F.C.) on 27 October,[^cite_note-15] and played an integral role as one of the main creative attacking players in the team – scoring 7 further league goals as the club swept to promotion to the [Premier League](11250.md) and the Championship title.[^cite_note-5year-16] Fans subsequently voted him into third place in the [annual Player of the Season awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich_City_F.C._Player_of_the_Season).[^cite_note-17]

On 8 July 2019, he signed a new five-year contract to stay at Norwich until the June 2024.[^cite_note-5year-16] During December 2019, Buendía created 29 chances for his teammates, which was the most chances created by a player in a single month in the Premier League since the stat began to be recorded in 2003–04.[^cite_note-18]

On 7 July 2020, Buendía scored his first goal in the Premier League, coming in a 2–1 away defeat to [Watford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watford_F.C.).[^cite_note-19] Norwich would lose their next match to [West Ham United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham_United_F.C.), a result which confirmed their relegation back to the Championship after only a single season in the top flight.[^cite_note-20]

#### 2020–21 season: Championship Player of the Season

On 6 April 2021, Buendía scored once and provided three assists in a 7–0 victory over [Huddersfield Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huddersfield_Town_A.F.C.).[^cite_note-21] On 17 April, Norwich secured automatic promotion back into the Premier League,[^cite_note-22] and secured the [Championship title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_EFL_Championship) on 1 May with a 4–1 win over [Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reading_F.C.).[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

On 29 April, following a [campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Norwich_City_F.C._season) in which Buendía scored 15 league goals and added a further 16 assists, he won the [EFL Championship Player of the Season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Awards#2021_Awards) award.[^cite_note-25] He was also named as [Norwich's Player of the Season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich_City_F.C._Player_of_the_Season), becoming only the fourth player from outside the British Isles to win the award.[^cite_note-NorPOY-26]

### Aston Villa

#### 2021–2025

On 7 June 2021, it was announced that an agreement had been reached for 24-year-old Buendía to be transferred to fellow Premier League club [Aston Villa](42173.md) once he had returned from international duty with Argentina.[^cite_note-27] The deal was completed on 10 June. The transfer fee was believed to be worth around £33 million, rising to £38 million with potential bonuses,[^cite_note-28] and was confirmed to be both Aston Villa's record signing[^cite_note-29] and Norwich City's record sale.[^cite_note-30] Buendía made his debut on 14 August 2021, in a 3–2 away loss to [Watford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watford_F.C.) on the opening day of the [2021–22 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_Aston_Villa_F.C._season).[^cite_note-31] He scored his first goal for Villa on 28 August 2021, in a 1–1 draw at home to [Brentford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brentford_F.C.).[^cite_note-32]

On 9 August 2023, on the eve of the Premier League season, Buendía suffered what was described as a "significant knee injury" in training and would require a scan and treatment from a specialist.[^cite_note-33] The injury was confirmed to be [anterior cruciate ligament injury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anterior_cruciate_ligament_injury) which required surgery and meant that Buendía missed the entirety of the [2023–24 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Aston_Villa_F.C._season). Buendía returned to training in May 2024 after nine months out.[^cite_note-34] Buendía's return to football came on 27 July 2024, almost a year to the day of his injury, in a pre-season friendly against [Columbus Crew](245695.md).[^cite_note-35] Buendía made his Premier League comeback on 14 September 2024, as a late substitute in a 3–2 victory over [Everton](91155.md),[^cite_note-36] after appearing as one of the permitted overage players in [Aston Villa's U21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Villa_F.C._Under-21s_and_Academy) team in the [EFL Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Trophy) earlier in the month, scoring twice in a 3–2 victory over [Fleetwood Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fleetwood_Town_F.C.).[^cite_note-37]

#### Loan to Bayer Leverkusen

On 29 January 2025, Aston Villa announced that Buendía had signed a contract extension with the club, and would join [Bundesliga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundesliga) club [Bayer Leverkusen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayer_04_Leverkusen) on loan until the end of the season.[^cite_note-38]

#### 2025–26 season: Europa League victory

Despite being told he could leave in the summer, at the start of the [2025–26 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Aston_Villa_F.C._season), Buendía re-established himself as a first team regular for Aston Villa.[^cite_note-39] He scored three times in the first 12 games of the season, including finishing off a team goal in a 2–1 away victory over [Tottenham Hotspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.) which was awarded [BBC Goal of the Month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Goal_of_the_Month) for October.[^cite_note-\:0-40] On 7 November 2025, the same goal was awarded the official [Premier League Goal of the Month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League_Goal_of_the_Month).[^cite_note-\:1-41] On 7 May 2026, he netted a goal in a 4–0 win over [Nottingham Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_Forest_F.C.) in the [Europa League semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_UEFA_Europa_League_knockout_phase) second leg, helping his team secure a 4–1 aggregate victory and a place in the [final](76987231.md).[^cite_note-42] In the Europa League final, he scored a goal and provided an assist in a 3–0 victory over [SC Freiburg](1438835.md), earning the Man of the Match award.[^cite_note-43] Ben Fisher of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* called Buendía's goal "a left-foot peach into the top corner".[^cite_note-44] After the match, Buendía said: "I've had some very bad moments, I've been very low, injuries, finding myself in a difficult momentum to play more often, but I kept believing in myself all the time. ... I always trusted my work ethic and also I always thought I could be important for this club and team. I have to enjoy it."[^cite_note-45]

## International career



Buendía about to make his debut for [Argentina](454699.md) in 2022

On 16 September 2014, Buendía was called up to the [Spain under-19s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_national_under-19_football_team).[^cite_note-46] On 29 April of the following year, he was called up by [Argentina under-20s](12504513.md) for [2015 FIFA U-20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup),[^cite_note-47] and joined the latter's training camp on 5 May 2015.[^cite_note-48] In May 2021, Buendía was called up to the [Argentina senior squad](454699.md) for the first time.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50] On 1 February 2022, he made his debut in a 1–0 home victory over [Colombia](680175.md).[^cite_note-51]

After a three-year absence, Buendía was called up to the Argentina side again in November 2025 for a friendly against [Angola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angola_national_football_team).[^cite_note-52] On 14 November, Buendía came off the bench in a 2–0 victory.[^cite_note-53]
