**Pitrufquén** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[pitɾufˈken\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a Chilean [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Chile) and [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Chile) in [Cautín Province](4138209.md), [Araucanía Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Araucan%C3%ADa_Region). The city is located 30 km south of [Temuco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temuco) and lies immediately south of the [Toltén River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolt%C3%A9n_River), along [Chile Highway 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chile_Highway_5).

## History

Pitrufquén was initially founded as *Lisperguer*, name that honors the creator of the plane of the urban area, the French engineer Lisperguer.

According to the 2002 census of the [National Statistics Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Statistics_Institute_\(Chile\)), Pitrufquén spans an area of 580.7 km<sup>2</sup> (224 sq mi) and has 21,988 inhabitants (10,902 men and 11,086 women). Of these, 13,420 (61%) lived in urban areas and 8,568 (39%) in rural areas.[^cite_note-INE-3]

Pitrufquén's population is characterized as varied because its members have different culture backgrounds, highlighting the historical legacy [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Chilean) and immigrants principally from: France, Italy, Germany, The Netherlands, Austria, Belgium, Switzerland and Hungary. Currently, the largest foreign colony is in "[Faja Maisan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faja_Maisan)" composed mostly of descendants of Germans, Dutchmen and Swiss, whose ancestors arrived in La Araucanía between 1905 and 1912.[^cite_note-4]

Since 1898, Pitrufquén begins its development under the impetus of its people and its agricultural potential. Besides immigrants mostly [Dutchmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_diaspora) and [Germans](152735.md).

## Administration

As a commune, Pitrufquén is a third-level [administrative division of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_division_of_Chile) administered by a [municipal council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_council), headed by an [alcalde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcalde) who is directly elected every four years. The 2008–2012 alcalde is Humberto Catalán Candia ([PS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Party_of_Chile)).[^cite_note-Munitel-1][^cite_note-Official-2]

Within the [electoral divisions of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_divisions_of_Chile), Pitrufquén is represented in the [Chamber of Deputies](716259.md) by José Manuel Edwards ([RN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Renewal_\(Chile\))) and Joaquín Tuma ([PDC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Democratic_Party)) as part of the 51st electoral district, together with [Carahue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carahue), [Nueva Imperial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nueva_Imperial), [Saavedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saavedra,_Chile), [Teodoro Schmidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teodoro_Schmidt,_Chile), [Freire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freire,_Chile) and [Cholchol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cholchol). The commune is represented in the [Senate](683411.md) by José Garcia Ruminot ([RN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Renewal_\(Chile\))) and Eugenio Tuma Zedan ([PPD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_for_Democracy_\(Chile\))) as part of the 15th senatorial constituency (Araucanía-South).
