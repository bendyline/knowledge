**Soda Zhemu** (born 4 June 1974) is a Zimbabwean politician, [member of parliament](3394393.md) and cabinet minister.[^cite_note-parlzim-1] Zhemu is a member of the ruling [Zanu-PF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZANU%E2%80%93PF) party and represents the constituency of Muzarabani North.[^cite_note-parlzim-1]

In August 2020, Zhemu was appointed as the Minister of Energy and Power Development, replacing [Fortune Chasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortune_Chasi) who was fired by [President Emmerson Mnangagwa](6390734.md) for conduct that "had become incompatible with the President’s expectations." [^cite_note-iHarare-2][^cite_note-Sunday_News-3]

## Education

Zhemu attended Muzarabani Primary and Secondary Schools as well as Machaya Secondary School.[^cite_note-TechZim-4]

The Minister attained a BCOM degree in accounting from the [Zimbabwe Open University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe_Open_University). According to the Parliament of Zimbabwe website, Zhemu has a Masters in Business Administration (MBA) although the website does not specify the institution which awarded Zhemu the qualification.[^cite_note-TechZim-4]
