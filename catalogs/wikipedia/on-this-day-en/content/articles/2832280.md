**Larak Island** (also **Lark Island**) is a small [Iranian island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_islands) located off the coast of [Bandar Abbas](464348.md), [Iran](14653.md), east of [Qeshm Island](2252168.md) and south of [Hormuz Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hormuz_Island). The narrowest part of the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md), 24 miles (39 km), lies between this Iranian island and Oman's [Great Quoin Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_Salamah_Archipelago).[^cite_note-Joyner1990-4] Larak Island is part of [Hormozgan province](448064.md).

## Geography and demographics

Larak Island is a [salt plug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_dome), with its surface consisting of [sandstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandstone), rock salt and iron oxide.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEAnonbyYousefian201141-5] It has a number of hills, which rise as high as 138 metres (453 ft).[^cite_note-6]

The island is administered as the [Larak Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larak_Rural_District), within the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Qeshm_County\)) of [Qeshm County](7349780.md). The only settlement on the island now is [Larak Shahri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larak_Shahri), on the north-eastern coast. It has a population of 466 people and 98 families. Another settlement, Larak Kuhi, was in the hills in the interior of the island, but was abandoned in the mid-1970s. There have been settlements at Salmi on the west coast and Mowrona on the north-west coast, but they also have been abandoned.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEAnonbyYousefian201141–43-7]

Laraki, a dialect of the [Kumzari language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumzari_language), is spoken on Larak Island. Research in 1977 identified that Laraki was the main language in Larak Shahri, while Arabic was spoken in Larak Kuhi.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEAnonbyYousefian201143-8]

Laraki-speakers mostly work in fishing, with some goat husbandry, and trade. In the past there was production of dates and barley, and export of salt.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEAnonbyYousefian201144-9]

## History

During their occupation in the [16th century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_century), during the [Portuguese–Safavid wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese%E2%80%93Safavid_wars), the Portuguese built fortresses here, and on the nearby [Qeshm](2252168.md) and [Hormuz Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hormuz_Island).[^cite_note-10]

### Modern

After Iraq attacked Iran's main oil export terminal at [Kharg Island](2210066.md) in 1985 during the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War), Iran established new export facilities, including a floating terminal that opened at Larak Island in June 1986. Larak island was bombed by Iraq in November 1986.[^cite_note-Kostiner-11]

Larak has an Iranian military base with several Chinese-made [Silkworm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silkworm_missile) HY-2 surface-to-surface missiles placed there in 1987.[^cite_note-Metz1989-12]

In April 1988, during [Operation Praying Mantis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Praying_Mantis), the [Iranian frigate *Sahand*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRIS_Sahand_\(1969\)) was sunk by the [United States Navy](20518076.md), 200 meters southwest of Larak Island. On 14 May 1988, the largest ship at the time, the Liberian [supertanker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supertanker), *[Seawise Giant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seawise_Giant)* was sunk by Iraqi [anti-ship missiles](140725.md) off the coast of Larak Island while carrying crude Iranian oil.[^cite_note-13] The ship was later refloated, repaired and used for another few years.

In late March during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), Iran began routing [oil tankers](15923603.md) north of Larak Island for a fee ("Tehran's Tollbooth");[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] distinct from the main channel, this route affords visual inspection of the ships by the [IRGC Navy](4676345.md) and port authorities.[^cite_note-16] Accordingly Larak Island has been suggested as a military objective.[^cite_note-17]

In August 2026, the US [attacked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Larak_Island_attack) two missile launchers of the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) on Larak Island.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

## Flora and fauna

The island has very little [vegetation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetation), with just some low acacia trees, palms, bushes and grasses. It once had a population of wild [gazelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gazelles).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEAnonbyYousefian201141-5] The sea around the island is one of the most diverse [coral reef](87410.md) areas in the environment of the Persian Gulf, with 37 species of [scleractinian corals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scleractinia) identified in the waters.[^cite_note-20]
