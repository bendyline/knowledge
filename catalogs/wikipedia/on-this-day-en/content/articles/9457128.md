**Michael Tan Defensor** (Tagalog pronunciation: [\[dɪfɛnˈsɔɾ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Tagalog), born June 30, 1969) is a Filipino politician and businessman who served as the [party-list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party-list_representation_in_the_House_of_Representatives_of_the_Philippines) [representative](293605.md) for [Anakalusugan](69959027.md) from 2019 to 2022. He is also the chief executive officer of Pax Libera Mining, Inc.

A member of the Defensor political family, Defensor is the nephew of former senator [Miriam Defensor Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miriam_Defensor_Santiago).[^cite_note-1] He began his political career in [Quezon City](284223.md), where he served as a member of the [city council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon_City_Council) from 1992 to 1995. He was elected to the [House of Representatives](293605.md) representing [Quezon City's 3rd congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon_City's_3rd_congressional_district) in 1995, becoming the youngest member of Congress at the time. As a congressman, he was a member of the "[Spice Boys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spice_Boys_\(Congressmen\))", who led congressional efforts to impeach President [Joseph Estrada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Estrada). The impeachment trial and the widespread protests of the [Second EDSA Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_EDSA_Revolution) in January 2001 resulted in Estrada's resignation and Vice President [Gloria Macapagal Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Macapagal_Arroyo)'s accession to the presidency.

Defensor later served in various positions in the [Arroyo administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Gloria_Macapagal_Arroyo), including chairman of the [Housing and Urban Development Coordinating Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_and_Urban_Development_Coordinating_Council), [Secretary of Environment and Natural Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Environment_and_Natural_Resources), and [Malacañang Chief of Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Staff_\(Philippines\)). He was also appointed to positions at [Ninoy Aquino International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninoy_Aquino_International_Airport) and the [Philippine National Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_National_Railways).

In 2019, he was elected again to the House of Representatives as the representative of the Anakalusugan party-list. He was among the 70 representatives who voted against the renewal of the [ABS-CBN franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABS-CBN_franchise_renewal_controversy).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] During the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_Philippines), he promoted and distributed the antiparasitic drug [ivermectin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivermectin) as a possible prophylaxis and treatment for [COVID-19](63030231.md), despite the lack of scientific evidence supporting its efficacy. In 2026, he and 18 other individuals drew controversy over their claims that kickbacks from [flood control projects](80804811.md) were being sent to several politicians and activists.[^cite_note-4] Defensor unsuccessfully ran for senator in [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Philippine_Senate_election) and for [Mayor of Quezon City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Quezon_City) in [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Quezon_City_local_elections) and [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Quezon_City_local_elections).

Defensor was arrested on July 6, 2026, after the [Sandiganbayan](8563865.md) charged him with plunder and issued an arrest warrant in connection with an undeclared [₱](227672.md)75 million donation that he and two others allegedly made to Senator [Rodante Marcoleta](68240498.md) during the 2025 election campaign. He is currently awaiting trial.[^cite_note-5]

## Early life and education

Mike Defensor was born on June 30, 1969 to lawyer [Matias Defensor Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matias_Defensor_Jr.) and Florence Pamintuan Tan Defensor.[^cite_note-SWLustre-6][^cite_note-MSDaza-7] Matias Jr. was a prominent lawyer during the administration of President [Ferdinand Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Marcos), being friends with former cabinet member [Francisco Tatad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Tatad) and serving as spokesman during Marcos' presidential campaign in the [1986 snap election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Philippine_presidential_election) that preceded the [People Power Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_Power_Revolution).[^cite_note-SWLustre-6][^cite_note-8] Mike's younger sister, Maria Theresa ("Maite"), is a businesswoman and former politician.[^cite_note-SWLustre-6]

He finished his elementary education and received both his Bachelor of Arts in History and Masters in [Public Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Administration) at the [University of the Philippines Diliman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Philippines_Diliman), where he became a member of the [UP Alpha Sigma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UP_Alpha_Sigma). From 1988 to 1989, he was vice chairman of the [UP Diliman University Student Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UP_Diliman_University_Student_Council), serving alongside chairman Bong Bongolan.[^cite_note-MSDaza-7] He finished his secondary education at [Niles McKinley High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niles_McKinley_High_School) in Niles, Ohio.

## Political career

### Quezon City councilor

Defensor was elected as a [Quezon City](284223.md) [councilor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon_City_Council) from the third district in the 1992 elections at the age of 23, making him the youngest member of that body.[^cite_note-9] He received the Manuel Quezon Bantayog Award for Most Outstanding QC Councilor for the year 1994.[^cite_note-10]

### Congressman

Defensor ran for congressman of the [third district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon_City's_3rd_congressional_district) of [Quezon City](284223.md) against reelectionist [Dennis Roldan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Roldan) in the [1995 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Philippine_House_of_Representatives_elections), which he subsequently won, becoming at age 26 the youngest member of the [Philippine House of Representatives](293605.md) at the time.[^cite_note-MSVillanueva-11] Though he ran under the party of [Laban ng Demokratikong Pilipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laban_ng_Demokratikong_Pilipino), he switched to the [Liberal Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Party_\(Philippines\)) in June 1995, a month before the 10th Congress convened.[^cite_note-MSVillanueva-11] He won reelection in [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Philippine_House_of_Representatives_elections).

#### Estrada impeachment

Defensor was part of the [Spice Boys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spice_Boys_\(Congressmen\)) group of congressmen that spearheaded the filing of the [impeachment case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impeachment_of_Joseph_Estrada) against then-President [Joseph Estrada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Estrada) at the [House of Representatives](293605.md).

### Arroyo cabinet

After the [Second EDSA Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_EDSA_Revolution) ousted President Estrada in January 2001, Defensor was appointed as Secretary of the [Housing and Urban Development Coordinating Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_and_Urban_Development_Coordinating_Council) by President [Gloria Macapagal Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Macapagal_Arroyo), vacating his congressional seat five months before the end of his second term. He held that post until August 2004, when he was appointed Secretary of the [Department of Environment and Natural Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Environment_and_Natural_Resources).

In the [2004 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Philippine_presidential_election), he served as the official campaign spokesperson of President Arroyo, who would win the presidency to secure a full term. After his tenure as DENR Secretary, he was appointed Presidential [Chief of Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Staff_\(Philippines\)). He resigned from that post on February 10, 2007, to run for senator.

In June 2008, he was appointed head of [Ninoy Aquino International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninoy_Aquino_International_Airport) Terminal 3 (NAIA-3) by virtue of Executive Order No. 732 (creating the Presidential Task Force on the NAIA-3 that was "mandated to ensure the immediate opening and operation of Terminal III.") The order provides for the airport terminal's opening based on decisions of the Supreme Court and applicable laws.[^cite_note-12]

On October 9, 2008, Defensor was named acting chairman of the [Philippine National Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_National_Railways) (PNR).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

### Senate candidacy



Defensor in 2007

Defensor was among the first to file for candidacy for the Senate on February 12, 2007. Prior to filing, one of his campaign proposals was to temporarily release deposed former president [Joseph Estrada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Estrada) from his arrest while his [trial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trial_of_Joseph_Estrada) at the Sandiganbayan was ongoing, prompting criticism from prosecutors at the trial who called it a "politician's gimmick".[^cite_note-15] He employed popular gossip show host [Boy Abunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boy_Abunda) as his campaign manager. He was named to the [TEAM Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TEAM_Unity_\(Philippines\)) coalition backed by the Arroyo administration. However, he lost the race, placing 15th overall out of the 12 seats.

### Quezon City mayoral candidacy

In [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Quezon_City_local_elections), Defensor ran for [Mayor of Quezon City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Quezon_City) under the [People's Reform Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Reform_Party) (PRP). However, he lost to outgoing Vice Mayor [Herbert Bautista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Bautista), who won via [landslide](41053306.md).

In [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Quezon_City_local_elections), Defensor ran for [Mayor of Quezon City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Quezon_City) under the [Partido Federal ng Pilipinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partido_Federal_ng_Pilipinas) (PFP). During his campaign, he wore the color red in openly expressing his support for the [UniTeam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UniTeam) candidacies of former senator [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md) and [Davao City](489401.md) Mayor [Sara Duterte](42504861.md) for the presidency and vice presidency respectively; both Marcos and Duterte ultimately won the national election.[^cite_note-16] Defensor lost to incumbent Mayor [Joy Belmonte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joy_Belmonte), who emerged victorious in the local election.

### Representative for Anakalusugan

#### Denial of the franchise renewal of ABS-CBN

Main article: [ABS-CBN franchise renewal controversy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABS-CBN_franchise_renewal_controversy)

Defensor is one of the vocal opponents against the franchise renewal of [ABS-CBN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABS-CBN), together with Sagip Party-list Rep. [Rodante Marcoleta](68240498.md) and [Cavite 7th District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavite's_7th_congressional_district) Rep. [Jesus Crispin Remulla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_Crispin_Remulla). He is one of the 70 congressmen who voted "yes" to "kill" (deny) the franchise renewal of ABS-CBN, in favor of the report from the Technical Working Group. After claiming continuous victory, Defensor inititiated an online forum via [Zoom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoom_\(software\)) and streamed live on [Facebook](7529378.md)[^cite_note-17] along with Marcoleta, Remulla, [Yedda Romualdez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yedda_Romualdez), Journalie Payonan, [Dan Fernandez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Fernandez), [Jose Antonio Sy-Alvarado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jose_Antonio_Sy-Alvarado), [Inno Dy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inno_Dy) and Alfred delos Santos to discuss the fate of ABS-CBN. On July 18, 2020, they discussed the potential takeover of the ill-fated network due to alleged tampered [torrens title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torrens_title), fine for the alleged illegal sale of [ABS-CBN TV Plus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABS-CBN_TV_Plus) for almost [₱](227672.md)2 trillion, and the total closure of [Sky Cable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Cable) as suggested by [Camarines Sur 2nd District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camarines_Sur's_2nd_congressional_district) Rep. [LRay Villafuerte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Raymund_Villafuerte). Meanwhile, pro-ABS-CBN advocate [Christine Bersola-Babao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christine_Bersola-Babao) slammed the forum and called the congresspersons as "evil", and [Senate President](8108148.md) [Tito Sotto](3873709.md) slammed the possible takeover of ABS-CBN by calling the compound as constitutionally protected.[^cite_note-18]

#### Ivermectin pantry

In April 2021, Defensor and SAGIP party-list representative [Rodante Marcoleta](68240498.md) initiated an "ivermectin pan-three" that distributes the anti-parasitic drug [ivermectin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivermectin), despite warnings from the World Health Organization on the lack of evidence to support ivermectin's efficacy against COVID-19.[^cite_note-20]

## Controversies

### Ador Mawanay fiasco

In August 2001, Defensor spearheaded the presentation of Antonio Luis Marquez (alias Ador Mawanay) to the public, positioning the whistleblower as a witness against the alleged criminal activities of newly elected Senator [Panfilo Lacson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panfilo_Lacson).[^cite_note-21] Acting as a primary defender of the Arroyo administration, Defensor championed Mawanay’s testimonies until Mawanay recanted his statements and claimed that he was coerced.[^cite_note-scrutiny-22]

### Vagrancy accusation

In 2001, Defensor faced allegations of hiring women for sex following the arrest of four individuals for [vagrancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vagrancy) in Quezon City. He denied the claims, accusing the Quezon City Police of using a fabricated witness to falsely link him to the controversy.[^cite_note-scrutiny-22]

### Involvement in ZTE-NBN corruption scandal

Main article: [NBN–ZTE deal corruption scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBN%E2%80%93ZTE_deal_corruption_scandal)

Mike Defensor, on July 4, 2008, filed [perjury](23688.md) lawsuit versus [Rodolfo Noel Lozada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodolfo_Noel_Lozada) for testifying under [oath](273418.md) that he had paid Lozada ₱50,000 (equivalent to ₱75,233 in 2021) to change his statement that he was not kidnapped at [Ninoy Aquino International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninoy_Aquino_International_Airport) (NAIA) when he arrived from [Hong Kong](13404.md) at the height of the [NBN–ZTE deal corruption scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBN%E2%80%93ZTE_deal_corruption_scandal).[^cite_note-23]

### Criticism vs. Bongbong Marcos

Since 2025, Defensor emerged as a critic to the administration of his former ally, President [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md), leading high-profile legal and political challenges, citing systemic corruption and governance failures. He had publicly called for Marcos's resignation and joined United People's Initiative (UPI) rallies.[^cite_note-24] In January 2026, he led a group of complainants in filing impeachment case against Marcos, but withdrew the document after the office of the House Secretary General declined to accept it.[^cite_note-25]

### Flood control scandal and *maleta* allegations

In February 2026, he was involved in the "Marines Controversy," providing logistics, including a vehicle registered to his wife Julie, for 18 individuals identifying as former Marines. The group alleged they had delivered over ₱800 billion in kickbacks contained in suitcases (*maleta* in [Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language)) to government officials. Malacañang dismissed the claims as a "destabilization plot," while the [Philippine Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Navy) reported that several witnesses were either never enlisted or had been dishonorably discharged.[^cite_note-26]

On July 8, 2026, Senator [Erwin Tulfo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Tulfo) filed grave oral [defamation](28661.md) complaints with the Mandaluyong City Prosecutor's Office against Defensor, two of [Zaldy Co](78297130.md)'s former bodyguards, their lawyer [Levito Baligod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levito_Baligod), Quezon City Councilor Ranulfo Ludovica, and lawyer Virgilio Garcia. The complaints stemmed from an event at the Doublegem EDSA Garden Events Place in [Mandaluyong](359686.md) on June 16, 2026, which, after being cancelled as a [Senate Blue Ribbon Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_Blue_Ribbon_Committee) hearing because no senators attended, proceeded as a press conference where the two ex-bodyguards accused Tulfo of receiving cash kickbacks from an alleged [flood control scam](80804811.md).[^cite_note-27] Tulfo, who served as the committee's chairperson under a rival Senate faction, denied the accusations and alleged that Defensor and Baligod spearheaded the event, citing his lack of jurisdiction over infrastructure projects.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

### Human trafficking complaints

In April 2026, Defensor became a central figure in a high-profile human trafficking investigation following a raid by the [National Bureau of Investigation](2489044.md) (NBI) on the Chicago Family KTV and Bleu Hotel in [Pasig](359702.md). On April 21, authorities rescued 54 women from an alleged "sex-for-hire" operation, leading the NBI to file complaints against Defensor, his wife Julie, and their child Miguel (who is a trans woman) as the registered owners of Cliffpoint Development Corporation, which handles the property.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32] Defensor declined to comment on the trafficking complaints against his wife and child, stating he was following the advice of their legal counsel to remain silent since he was not personally named in that specific case.[^cite_note-33] On June 26, 2026, the NBI filed qualified human trafficking cases against Defensor and his wife anew before the Pasig City Prosecutor’s Office.[^cite_note-34]

### Rodante Marcoleta campaign donation controversy



Mug shot of Defensor after his arrest by the Philippine National Police on July 6, 2026.

In May 2026, the [Office of the Deputy Ombudsman](1866220.md)'s field investigation bureau for Luzon filed plunder and indirect bribery complaints against Senator [Rodante Marcoleta](68240498.md) and three of his [2025 senatorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Philippine_Senate_election) campaign donors that includes Defensor. The complaint alleged that in January 2025, Defensor contributed ₱30 million to Marcoleta's campaign, which combined with contributions from two other donors for a total of ₱75 million—above the ₱50 million statutory threshold for plunder and allegedly left undeclared in Marcoleta's [Statement of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Worth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statement_of_Assets,_Liabilities,_and_Net_Worth) (SALN).[^cite_note-35]

On July 6, 2026, Defensor, alongside Marcoleta and two other campaign donors, was arrested by the [Philippine National Police](1313055.md) on non-bailable charges of [plunder](209811.md). He is detained at the [New Quezon City Jail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon_City_Jail#Payatas) in [Payatas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payatas).[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37] On July 8, another warrant of arrest was issued against Defensor and Marcoleta over the alleged violation of Presidential Decree No. 46 that bans the exchange of gifts between public officials and private individuals.[^cite_note-38]

### Director, Petron Corporation

On December 4, 2007, Mike Defensor quietly joined to the board of directors of [Petron Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petron_Corporation) with former budget secretary Emilia Boncodin. Defensor had been frequenting [Macau](19068.md). Boncodin stated that Defensor was invited to the board by Nicasio Alcantara, government's team head / Chair, Petron. Membership in the Petron board is a lucrative job, as Defensor was offered a board seat in sequestered [United Coconut Planters Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Coconut_Planters_Bank).
