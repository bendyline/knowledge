The **Philippines–Vietnam relations** refers to the bilateral relations of the [Republic of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_the_Philippines) and the [Socialist Republic of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Republic_of_Vietnam). Since the end of the [Cold War](325329.md), relations between the two countries have warmed significantly. [Vietnam](202354.md) is sometimes referred to as the only communist military ally of the [Philippines](23440.md), especially after Vietnam joined [ASEAN](106539.md) in 1995. Both nations have cooperated in the fields of education, tourism, agriculture, aquaculture, trade, and defense. Additionally, both nations have similar positions on the [South China Sea issue](32005536.md), with [Vietnam](202354.md) backing the [Philippine](23440.md) victory in the ICC against [China](5405.md), and the [Philippines](23440.md) backing to a certain extent the claim of [Vietnam](202354.md) in the [Paracels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracel_Islands). Both nations have overlapping claims in the [Spratlys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spratly_Islands), but have never made military confrontations as both view each other as diplomatic allies and [ASEAN](106539.md) brethren.

Relations between the [Philippines](23440.md) and [Vietnam](202354.md) began centuries ago. There was proof that inhabitants of both countries were already involved in maritime trade prior to the arrival of Europeans. Ships from [Luzon](69375.md) in the Philippine archipelago came to the great port of Vietnam in the [Gulf of Tonkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Tonkin) to trade.

By 1500 BC, [jade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade) that was manufactured in the Philippines using raw materials from Taiwan, ended up in Vietnamese ports.[^cite_note-1] Likewise, by 1000 BC, bronze drums from the [Dong Son Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dong_Son_Culture) in Hanoi, were exported to ports in the Philippines.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

The [Song dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_dynasty)'s "[The History of Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Song_\(book\))", show that the Filipino nation of [Ma-i](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma-i) at [Mindoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindoro) was a mere two days’ sail from [Champa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champa) (a nation that once occupied central Vietnam) and that Champa’s and Mai’s merchants traded frequently with each other.[^cite_note-4]

During 1000 AD, there was a commercial rivalry for Chinese trade between the [Champa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champa) Civilization in Central and South Vietnam vs the [Rajahnate of Butuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajahnate_of_Butuan) in Southern Philippines.[^cite_note-5]

The [Chams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chams) then migrated to the [Sulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulu) archipelago, home of the then Hindu would-be [Sulu Sultanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulu_Sultanate). These Chams in southwestern Philippines were called Orang Dampuan.[^cite_note-6] Champa and Sulu engaged in commerce with each other which resulted in merchant Chams settling in Sulu during the 10th-13th centuries. The Orang Dampuan were slaughtered by envious native Sulu Buranuns due to the wealth of the Orang Dampuan.[^cite_note-7] The Buranun were then subjected to retaliatory slaughter by the Orang Dampuan. Harmonious commerce between Sulu and the Orang Dampuan was later restored.[^cite_note-8] The Yakans were descendants of the Taguima-based Orang Dampuan who came to Sulu from Champa.[^cite_note-9] Sulu received civilization in its Indic form from the Orang Dampuan.[^cite_note-10]

During the transition from the precolonial to colonial eras, both the Philippines and Vietnam witnessed the actions of the [Pirates of the South China Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_South_China_Coast) and how Fujianese pirates and [Wokous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wokou) from Japan intervened in the political, military and commercial affairs of the Vietnamese coast and Philippine archipelago.[^cite_note-11]

Maritime trade relations were then disrupted with the conquest of the Philippines by the Spaniards in the 16th century and the conquest of Vietnam by the French in the 19th century.[^cite_note-asianstudies-12]

The French occupation of Vietnam was successful partially because of assistance from Spanish-Philippines as soldiers from Spanish-Philippines which also included Mexican immigrants, were used by the French to augment the forces that invaded Saigon.[^cite_note-nigelgooding.co.uk-13]

Members of the [Philippine Civic Action Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Civic_Action_Group) (PHILCAG) arriving in [Tay Ninh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%A2y_Ninh_province) who were assisting the [South Vietnamese government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_South_Vietnam) in the [Vietnam War](32611.md).

The [Philippines](23440.md) was initially aligned with [South Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Vietnam) and provided them humanitarian aid in the [Vietnam War](32611.md).

Before the [Fall of Saigon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Saigon), which preludes the disestablishment of [South Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Vietnam), the [Philippines](23440.md) was already preparing to establish relations with [North Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Vietnam). [President](271126.md) [Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Marcos) authorised his wife, [First Lady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Spouse_of_the_Philippines) [Imelda Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imelda_Marcos), to make direct contacts while she was conducting state visits to Middle-eastern countries in early 1975. The communist takeover of [Cambodia](334751.md) and the impending defeat of South Vietnamese forces led [Manila](184334.md) to establish ties with [Hanoi](56667.md). The move was not seen as surprising as it was in line with [Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Marcos)' foreign policy to strengthen ties with socialist states in order to broaden economic and trade ties.[^cite_note-asianstudies-12]

On July 9, 1976, Vietnam Deputy Foreign Minister Phan Hien arrived in [Manila](184334.md) to discuss the formal establishment of ties between the two countries. On July 12, 1976, formal relations were finally established with the [Philippines](23440.md)[^cite_note-14] the fourth country in the [ASEAN](106539.md) to establish relations with the Socialist Republic of Vietnam after Malaysia, Indonesia and Singapore. The Philippines and Vietnam opened their respective embassies in 1978.[^cite_note-asianstudies-12][^cite_note-15]

Among the first problems to test the relation between the two countries was the repatriation of 14 Filipinos and 10 Vietnamese families who were still in [Ho Chi Minh City](66956.md), attempts of [Vietnamese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_people) nationals to illegally enter the [Philippines](23440.md) by claiming themselves to be members of Filipino families, and the involvement of [Filipinos](2298105.md) in the black market was received by the Philippine embassy. These issues hampered relations until the early 1980s.[^cite_note-asianstudies-12]

## Post-Cold War relations



[President](271126.md) [Benigno Aquino III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benigno_Aquino_III) and [President](466686.md) [Nguyễn Minh Triết](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Minh_Tri%E1%BA%BFt) during a bilateral meeting in 2010



President [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md) and [Prime Minister](439887.md) [Phạm Minh Chính](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph%E1%BA%A1m_Minh_Ch%C3%ADnh) during a bilateral meeting in 2023

Despite Vietnam's alignment with the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) and the Philippines with the United States during the Cold War, bilateral ties between the two countries can be recently described as friendly. On October 26, 2011, [Vietnamese President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_President) [Truong Tan Sang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truong_Tan_Sang) made a state visit to the Philippines where he met with his Filipino counterpart, [President](271126.md) [Benigno Aquino III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benigno_Aquino_III). The two countries signed four agreements on naval, coast guard and tourism as part of the Philippine-Vietnam Action Plan 2011–2016 framework.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-businessmirror-18] A Memorandum of Understanding agreement supported information sharing between the [Philippine Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Navy) and the [Vietnam People's Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_People's_Navy).[^cite_note-golbalnation-19][^cite_note-20]

Both being victims of [Typhoon Haiyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Haiyan) (though Vietnam was not as greatly affected as previously expected),[^cite_note-21] the Vietnamese still donated to help the Philippines in its rehabilitation efforts after the natural disaster, through the [Vietnam Red Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Red_Cross) Society.[^cite_note-22]

### [Disputes in the South China Sea](32005536.md)

The Philippines and Vietnam have territorial disputes over the [Spratly Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spratly_Islands), along with [Brunei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunei), [China](5405.md), [Malaysia](3607937.md), and [Taiwan](25734.md). The Philippines and Vietnam both disapprove of China's [nine-dash map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine-dash_map) which China uses as justification for its claim in the [South China Sea](74209.md).[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] Both countries were also committed to a multilateral diplomatic approach to the resolution of disputes in the South China Sea with the [UNCLOS](31940.md) taken into account.[^cite_note-golbalnation-19]

In December 2015, pirates believed to be from the [Moro Pirates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moro_Pirates), rogue Muslim Filipinos wanted for war crimes in the [Philippines](23440.md), murdered a Vietnamese fisherman in the [South China Sea](74209.md), which sparked a strong protest from the Vietnamese side. The event also sparked outrage from the Philippine side, who was persecuting the wanted criminals who may have been influenced by [Islamic terrorism](566821.md). Both [Vietnam](202354.md) and the [Philippines](23440.md) have upheld a variety of international agreements against [terrorism](30636.md) in the region.[^cite_note-26]

In 2016, after a legal battle in [The Hague](30269.md), the International Court of Justice of the United Nations ruled in favor of all arguments of the [Philippines](23440.md) against [China](5405.md)'s claims in the [South China Sea](74209.md), effectively nullifying [China](5405.md)'s claims in the international legal arena. However, [China](5405.md) waived the international court's ruling despite being a signatory with respect to international laws and UNCLOS and sent more military vessels to the [South China Sea](74209.md), replacing corals reefs with man-made islands that house military bases and missile launchers. [Vietnam](202354.md) formally supported the [Philippines](23440.md) in its [arbitration case against China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_v._Philippines) regarding China's [nine-dash claim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine-dotted_line) over the [South China Sea](74209.md) in the entirety of the case.[^cite_note-27]

In August 2017, Vietnam pushed for the inclusion of a stronger statement against Chinese activities in the [South China Sea](74209.md).

In May 2019, after a Chinese vessel rammed and sank a civilian Filipino vessel and left 22 Filipino fishermen adrift and exposed to the elements, [Vietnam](202354.md) sent a ship that successfully rescued the drowning Filipinos, gaining praise from the Philippine side.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

On 1 June 2026, the Philippines and Vietnam upgraded their ties to an enhanced strategic partnership. Both countries stressed that peace, stability, and freedom of navigation in the South China Sea are "non-negotiable," signaling a shared stance against China’s assertive actions in the region. They also agreed to deepen cooperation in defense, security, trade, and food supplies, while committing to resolve disputes peacefully under international law.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32]

A Memorandum of Understanding agreement supported information sharing between the [Philippine Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Navy) and the [Vietnam People's Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_People's_Navy).

On November 23, 2014, two frigates from the [Vietnamese People's Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_People%E2%80%99s_Navy) made its first port call to the [Philippines](23440.md). Vessels [HQ-011 Dinh Tien Hoang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HQ-011_Dinh_Tien_Hoang) and [HQ-012 Ly Thai To](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HQ-012_Ly_Thai_To) docked at the [Manila South Harbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_South_Harbor) for a goodwill visit which lasted three days. The move was initiated by former [President](271126.md) [Benigno Aquino III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benigno_Aquino_III).[^cite_note-portvisit-33]

In August 2024, the Vietnamese Coast Guard sent the ship CSB 8002 to take part in the first joint naval drills with the [Philippine Coast Guard](1954144.md) by the Philippines and Vietnam, and will be the first Vietnamese Coast Guard vessel to visit the Philippines.[^cite_note-34]

## Cyber Crime

| \[icon] | This section **needs expansion**. You can help by [adding missing information](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Philippines%E2%80%93Vietnam_relations\&action=edit\&section=). *(March 2025)* |

On February 27, 2025, Philippine authorities arrested 401 individuals, including 132 Vietnamese nationals, in a raid on a suspected online gambling and scam hub in [Pasay City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasay). The suspects were accused of involvement in illegal online gaming, cryptocurrency, romance, and investment scams. The operation followed a report from a concerned citizen and led to the seizure of electronic equipment used for scamming. The incident highlights ongoing concerns about cybercrime and exploitation in [Southeast Asia](28741.md), with many workers, including those from Vietnam, being coerced into participating in such operations.[^cite_note-35]
