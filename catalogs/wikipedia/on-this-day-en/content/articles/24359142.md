The **Grande River** is a river of [Rio de Janeiro](229419.md) state in southeastern [Brazil](3383.md).[^cite_note-1] It flows through the city of [Rio de Janeiro](25936.md), is briefly renamed Arroio Fundo, and discharges into the Comorim Lagoon.
