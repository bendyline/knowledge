The **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas** (**MoPNG**) is a [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Indian_agencies) of the [Government of India](553883.md) responsible for the exploration, production, refining, distribution, marketing, import, export, and conservation of [petroleum](23195.md), [natural gas](22131.md), [petroleum products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleum_product), and [liquefied natural gas](832128.md) in the country.&#x20;

The ministry is headed by [Cabinet minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Council_of_Ministers) [Hardeep Singh Puri](30909065.md), while its secretary is Pankaj Jain, a 1990-batch IAS officer of the [Assam](186162.md)-[Meghalaya](326019.md) cadre.[^cite_note-3] [Dharmendra Pradhan](7119332.md), who served from 26 May 2014 to 7 July 2021, is its longest serving minister till date.

## Research institutes

### Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology

Main article: [Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajiv_Gandhi_Institute_of_Petroleum_Technology)

RGIPT is a training and education institute that gives technical and management training to the [petroleum industry](994887.md) and was formally opened in July 2008. The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MOP\&NG), Government of India founded the institute through an Act of Parliament ("Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology Act 2007").[^cite_note-topnews.in-4]

#### Campuses

- [Jais Campus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jais): Started academic sessions in June 2008 at a temporary campus at [Rae Bareli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rae_Bareli). On 15 October 2016, the institute moved from temporary campus to the permanent campus at [Jais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jais).

  [^cite_note-indiatimes2-5]

- [Noida Campus](986046.md): Besides main center at Jais, Amethi, the RGIPT also have this campus for MBA and related courses.

- [Assam Campus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sivasagar): New campus is under construction at [Sivasagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sivasagar), [Assam](186162.md). In consonance with the charter of RGIPT, the primary objective of the Assam Center of RGIPT is envisaged to offer programmes of education and training of skilled technical manpower at the diploma and advance diploma levels including B.Sc. - M.Sc. integrated courses in various areas in the domain of the petroleum sector as per requirements of the oil, gas and [petrochemical industry](2293201.md).

  [^cite_note-rgipt.ac.in-6]

  [^cite_note-thehindu-7]

  &#x20;On 13 May 2017, [Chief Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister) of [Assam](186162.md) [Sarbananda Sonowal](7149468.md) and Minister of Petroleum and Natural Gas [Shri Dharmendra Pradhan](7119332.md) jointly launched the full swing construction work of second campus at [Sivasagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sivasagar_district), [Assam](186162.md).

- [Bangalore Campus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore): In July 2013, [Karnataka](16880.md) government agreed to offer 200 acres of land to set up the [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore) centre which is going to be the Asia's first centre on fire and safety for oil and gas sector.

### Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy

Main article: [Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Petroleum_and_Energy)

&#x20;Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy (IIPE), Visakhapatnam, a domain-specific Institute at par with IITs and IIMs, is established by the Government of India under the aegis of the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MoPNG) in the year 2016. The Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy Act, 2017 (No.3 of 2018) enacted by the Parliament and declared the Institute as an 'Institution of National Importance'. IIPE has achieved this prestigious status by joining with the major oil and gas corporations such as HPCL, IOCL, ONGC, OIL & GAIL.

IIPE is a research focussed Institute with an emphasis on the emerging areas in the energy sectors, namely Shale Gas, Coal Bed Methane, Gas Hydrates, Conventional Energy Sources, Renewable Energy Sources, Storage, and downstream activities of oil & gas. A primary goal of research at IIPE has been to meet the country's fossil fuel and renewable energy demands.&#x20;

The Institute presently offers 4-year B.Tech courses in Petroleum, Chemical and Mechanical Engineering based on JEE (Advanced) rankings and MSc Applied Geology and Ph.D. programmes. The Institute is presently operating its academic activities from its temporary campus. The Institute has its own state-of-the-art laboratories, research infrastructure, e-library, etc. The permanent campus of IIPE is being constructed in the beautiful and natural environment of hilly ranges in the Anakapalli district in Andhra Pradesh.
