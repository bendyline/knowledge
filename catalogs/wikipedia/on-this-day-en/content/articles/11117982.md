**Unit 217**, frequently called **Duvdevan** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): דובדבן, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'cherry'), is a [*mistaʽaravim*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mista'arvim) unit in the [Commando Brigade](49096690.md) of the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md).

It conducts undercover operations in urban areas, during which its members often wear civilian clothing to disguise themselves among the local [Arab](2185.md) populace.[^cite_note-4]

## History

According to the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) (IDF), Unit 217[^cite_note-5] was established in June 1986 to deal with "security events" in the [West Bank](33209.md), especially in its densely populated civilian areas. During the [2015–2016 violence wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%932016_wave_of_violence_in_Israeli%E2%80%93Palestinian_conflict), the unit was involved in the arrests of the killers of [Eitam and Naʽama Henkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Eitam_and_Na'ama_Henkin), Hafna Meir, and [rabbi](51273.md) Yaʽakov Littman (sometimes spelled Litman) and his son. For its work during this period, Unit 217 received the [Israeli Chief of Staff](3011266.md) citation.[^cite_note-IDF1-6]

[Roni Numa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roni_Numa) was a commander of the Duvdevan Unit before being promoted to *[aluf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluf)* (major general).[^cite_note-7]

Upon joining the Commando Brigade, Unit 217 expanded its operational activities.[^cite_note-IDF1-6]

According to the documentary, *[Who Killed Shireen?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Who_Killed_Shireen%3F)*, in 2022 a member of the Duvdevan unit killed Palestinian-American journalist [Shireen Abu Akleh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shireen_Abu_Akleh), who was reporting for *[Al Jazeera](30204052.md)* in [Jenin](50997.md).[^cite_note-8]

## Organization and mission

The unit is part of the ["Oz" 89th Commando Brigade](49096690.md) (a brigade dedicated solely to special operations), but *Duvdevan* operators undergo basic training in the [Paratroopers Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paratroopers_Brigade). Unit 217 is most similar to the [Yamas unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamas_\(Israel_Border_Police_unit\)) of the [Israel Border Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Border_Police).

## In fiction

The [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix) television series *[Fauda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fauda)* focuses on a fictional Israeli elite unit modeled after Duvdevan.[^cite_note-Stein-9] The series was co-created by [Lior Raz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lior_Raz) and [Avi Issacharoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avi_Issacharoff); Raz formerly served in the unit.[^cite_note-Stein-9][^cite_note-10]
