**Sud-Ubangi** (French for "South Ubangi") is one of the 21 [provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo](3984115.md) created in the [2015 repartitioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_the_DR_Congo#New_provinces). It lies in the northwest of the country on the [Ubangi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubangi_River).

Sud-Ubangi, [Équateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89quateur_Province), [Mongala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongala), [Nord-Ubangi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nord-Ubangi), and [Tshuapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tshuapa) provinces are the result of the dismemberment of the [former Équateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89quateur_\(former_province\)) province.[^cite_note-2] Sud-Ubangi was formed from the **Sud-Ubangi district** and the independently administered city of [Zongo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zongo,_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo). The town of [Gemena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemena) was elevated to capital city of the new province.

The 2020 population was estimated to be 2,987,100.[^cite_note-3]
