**Figueira da Foz**,[^cite_note-1] officially the **City of Figueira da Foz**,[^cite_note-2] often called simply **Figueira** for short, is a city and a municipality in the [Coimbra District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coimbra_District), in Portugal. Practically at the midpoint of the [Iberian Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberian_Peninsula)'s [Atlantic](698.md) coast, it is located at the mouth of the [Mondego River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mondego_River), 40 km (25 mi) west of [Coimbra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coimbra) and sheltered by hills ([Serra da Boa Viagem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serra_da_Boa_Viagem)), sharing about the same latitude with [Philadelphia](50585.md), [Baku](4566.md) and [Beijing](18603746.md). The population of the municipality in 2011 was 62,125,[^cite_note-ine-3] in an area of 379.05 km<sup>2</sup> (146.35 sq mi).[^cite_note-dgt-4] The city of Figueira da Foz proper has a population of 46,600. It is the second largest city in the district of Coimbra.

It is a coastal city with several beaches, summer and seaport facilities on the Atlantic Ocean coast. As a [tourism city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourist_city), it plays an important part in the centre of the country. A zone of legal gambling, one can find in Figueira one of the biggest [casinos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casino) of the [Iberian Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberian_Peninsula) – the Casino Figueira.

## History

According to the legend, the place's name is due to a [fig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ficus) tree, which stood at the quay of Salmanha, where the fishermen used to tie up their boats. Foz means "place of discharge", often used at the point a river meets another body of water, specially where there is a clear energy or water flow clash. Some historical traces show that people were settling in this region since the [Neolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic) age. The oldest known document, however, dates from the year 1096. In this, an abbot named Pedro donates estates, which belonged to the church of S. Julião, to the cathedral [Sé Velha of Coimbra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9_Velha_of_Coimbra).

Knowing the great importance rivers had in the development of cities and of ancient civilizations, the mouth of the Mondego must have played a central role in the fixation of men in this region and for the formation of settlements, which were the beginning of the city of Figueira da Foz. Figueira da Foz had a huge development during the 18th and 19th centuries due to the immense port movements and the expansion of the [shipbuilding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shipbuilding) and cod drying industry, supplying the city with new communication routes, housing and other facilities.[^cite_note-5] It was elevated to *vila* (small town) on 12 March 1771 and turned city (*cidade*) on 20 September 1882. Discovered as a sea resort by the end of the 19th century, it gained a great reputation in the 1920s and 1930s. The city had the Portuguese nickname of *Rainha das Praias* (Queen of the Beaches).

The municipality has some noted landmarks like the Sotto Mayor Palace, the old fishing village of [Buarcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buarcos), the [Serra da Boa Viagem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serra_da_Boa_Viagem) – a small forested mountain by the Atlantic Ocean, several beaches, and its large seaside [promenade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promenade) paved with typical [Portuguese pavement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_pavement). Figueira da Foz has several Churches, many of them in the rich Baroque style, a Municipal Museum with the archaeological, ethnographic and artistic collection, the Santa Catarina Fort and the old Buarcos Fortress, the *Relógio Tower* by the main sandy beach, several archaeological vestiges throughout the municipality, several Palaces and Manor Houses (like the Sotto Mayor Palace), as well as several green spaces and small gardens like those in the area of *Abadias*.

In January 2026, [Storm Kristin](82263504.md) caused a catastrophic impact in the municipality. Losses of several tens of millions were estimated in the industry sector of Figueira da Foz. [^cite_note-6]

## Economy

The city is an important industrial centre, producing wood pulp and paper ([Navigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Navigator_Company), formerly and still commonly known as Soporcel, and Celbi, part of [Altri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altri)) and glass (Verallia, part of [Saint-Gobain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Gobain)). It also has notable naval construction and fish industries. Until the turn of the 21st century, a factory of the [CIMPOR Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIMPOR) was installed in the Mondego Cape *([Cabo Mondego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabo_Mondego))*. The seaport and the fishing harbour are also important for its economy.

With its old and renowned [casino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casino) (*Casino da Figueira*), the marina, several hotels, restaurants and other tourist facilities, Figueira is actually a very important Atlantic beach resort, in the center of Portugal's Silver Coast/*Costa de Prata* [tourism region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_region). A generally windy coastal area, its relatively cold seawaters are particularly renowned as a [surf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfing) and [kitesurf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitesurf) hotspot in the same fashion as other places throughout the west coast of Portugal, such as [Nazaré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazar%C3%A9,_Portugal), [Peniche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peniche,_Portugal) or [Ericeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ericeira). In July and August, its main beaches are usually packed with beachgoers too.

## Education

The city has well-equipped kindergartens, schools and high schools. It had two [private universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_university): the [Catholic University of Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_Portugal) (Figueira da Foz branch) and the [Internacional University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internacional_University)[^cite_note-7] (Figueira da Foz branch) but they were closed in 2009 due to lack of funds and academic integrity. The municipality of Figueira da Foz is served by the ancient [University of Coimbra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Coimbra) and the [Polytechnic Institute of Coimbra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_Institute_of_Coimbra) located 40 kilometers to the east of the city, in the [district capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Portugal) Coimbra.

## Geography

Figueira is about 40 kilometers west of Coimbra by the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md) and less than 200 kilometers to the north of Lisbon, on the mouth of the [Mondego River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mondego_River) and at the foothills of the [Serra da Boa Viagem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serra_da_Boa_Viagem) (mountain). The [Cabo Mondego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabo_Mondego) (cape) is inserted in the context of the Lusitanian Basin and is located on the western edge of the Serra da Boa Viagem (latitude 40º 11´ 3´´ N, longitude 08º 54´34´´W), approximately 6 km to the northwest of the city of Figueira da Foz proper. Classified as a Natural Monument since 2007, the cape is one of the most important testimonies for understanding the geological history of Portugal; represents, in a particularly complete way, some of the most important episodes in the history of the Earth that occurred during the Jurassic, for a time interval that is, approximately, between 185 and 140 million years, which justifies, at international level, the relevance of its classification, conservation and dissemination. The outcrop comprises a series of marine and fluvial-lake sediments that extend from the Upper Toarciano to the Titonian. This record, on some levels, is particularly continuous and rich in paleontological, sedimentological and paleomagnetic information, which are associated with exceptional observation conditions. It includes levels with the oldest footprints of megalosaurs (bipedal and carnivorous dinosaurs) described in Portugal and whose first reference dates from 1884.

## Climate

Figueira da Foz has a [warm-summer Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warm-summer_Mediterranean_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Csb*) characterized by mild, rainy winters and dry, warm summers. The annual average temperature is around 15 °C (59 °F) and the annual average rainfall is about 600 mm (24 in). The [sea surface temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_surface_temperature) varies from 14–15 °C (57–59 °F) in January and February to 18–19 °C (64–66 °F) in August and September. On summer afternoons moderate to (occasionally) strong north-westerly winds are common. [Subtropical Storm Alpha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical_Storm_Alpha_\(2020\)), notable for being the only tropical or subtropical cyclone to make landfall in Portugal on record, made landfall at peak intensity in the general vicinity of the city on 18 September 2020.[^cite_note-10] Figueira da Foz was, however, particularly hit hard when [Storm (Hurricane) Leslie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Leslie_\(2018\)) made landfall in mainland Europe right within the city on 13 October 2018.[^cite_note-11] On 28 January 2026, [Storm Kristin](82263504.md) caused a catastrophic impact in Figueira da Foz, causing tens of millions of damage.

## Sports

### Football

Figueira da Foz was home to [Associação Naval 1º Maio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associa%C3%A7%C3%A3o_Naval_1%C2%BA_Maio), best known for its [football](10568.md) team which spent six seasons between 2005 and 2011 in the national top tier, the [Primeira Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primeira_Liga). The club dissolved in 2017 and was succeeded by the phoenix club [Associação Naval 1893](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associa%C3%A7%C3%A3o_Naval_1893).

### Basketball

The city is also the home of the basketball department of [Ginásio Clube Figueirense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ginasio_\(basketball\)) which competes in the [Portuguese basketball league](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_basketball_league), adding rowing and swimming to their more notorious activities.[^cite_note-16]

### Tennis

The *Tennis Club da Figueira da Foz* was founded in 1917 and is still located next to the Santa Catarina Fort at the mouth of the Mondego River today.

### Beach soccer

The city hosted the [Mundialito de Futebol de Praia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mundialito_de_Futebol_de_Praia) (also known as the Mundialito of Beach Soccer), an international beach soccer tournament, from 1997 to 2004.[^cite_note-rsssf-17] Held annually at Praia da Claridade beach in Figueira da Foz, the event featured national teams and served as a prominent international competition in the sport's early development under Beach Soccer Worldwide (BSWW) organization.[^cite_note-figueirabeach-18]

The tournament followed an inaugural edition in 1994 in [Rio de Janeiro, Brazil](25936.md), but from 1997 onward, Figueira da Foz became its consistent host for eight consecutive years (1997–2004), establishing the city as a key hub for beach soccer in Europe and a "cradle" for major competitions in Portugal.[^cite_note-fpf-19][^cite_note-rsssf-17]

[Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_national_beach_soccer_team) dominated most editions, winning in 1997, 1999, 2000, 2001, and 2002, while the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_men's_national_beach_soccer_team) won in 1998, and for the home country, [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal_national_beach_soccer_team) claimed the 2003 title.[^cite_note-rsssf-17] The 2004 edition marked the final year in Figueira da Foz before the tournament later moved to other locations.[^cite_note-figueirabeach-18] The event contributed significantly to the growth of beach soccer in Portugal, paving the way for the country's later successes in competitions like the [Euro Beach Soccer League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro_Beach_Soccer_League) and [FIFA Beach Soccer World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Beach_Soccer_World_Cup).[^cite_note-fpf-19]

After 2004, Figueira da Foz continued to host major beach soccer events, such as stages of the Euro Beach Soccer League Superfinal.[^cite_note-figueirabeach-18]

### Surfing

International surf competitions frequently take place in Figueira like the World Surf Championship 2010[^cite_note-20] or the IFCA Slaloms Worlds 2008.[^cite_note-21]

## Transport

### Road

Figueira da Foz is accessed by motorways A14, linking it to [Coimbra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coimbra) and [A1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_motorway_\(Portugal\)), and A17, linking it to [Aveiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aveiro,_Portugal) and [A25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A25_motorway_\(Portugal\)) (reaching [Spain](26667.md)) to the north and [Leiria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiria) and [A8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A8_motorway_\(Portugal\)) to the south. As an alternative to tolled motorways, it is served by routes [EN109](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EN109?action=edit\&redlink=1), linking Leiria to Gaia (near Porto), and [EN111](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EN111?action=edit\&redlink=1), linking Figueira da Foz to Coimbra and [IC2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IC2_\(Portugal\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), the toll-free alternative to A1. There are intercity bus services to Lisbon, Porto, Leiria, Aveiro and Viseu. There are also regional bus services to Coimbra and places in between.

### Rail

The city is served by its own railway terminus, [Figueira da Foz railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figueira_da_Foz_railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1), with suburban trains linking it to Coimbra and some regional trains running along the [Western Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linha_do_Oeste), although there is no direct railway service to Lisbon or Porto. The closest station to be served by intercity railway services is [Alfarelos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfarelos_railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1), at the [Northern Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linha_do_Norte), 18 km to the east. [Coimbra-B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coimbra-B?action=edit\&redlink=1), 36 km to the east, is the closest station serving high-speed [Alfa Pendular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfa_Pendular) trains. Both have scheduled links to Lisbon, Porto and other major cities across Portugal. [Pombal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pombal_railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1), slightly closer at 32 km to the southeast, also serves Alfa Pendular trains, but there is no direct railway link between the two cities.

### Water

Figueira da Foz has its own seaport, [Port of Figueira da Foz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Figueira_da_Foz?action=edit\&redlink=1) along river Mondego, featuring a marina, a container terminal and naval shipyards. In addition, there are facilities for watersports, in particular [rowing](25736.md).

### Air

The city does not have its own airport. The closest international airport to Figueira da Foz is [Porto Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto_Airport) (OPO), located 120 km to the north and offering several international destinations primarily in Europe, although [Lisbon Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon_Airport) (LIS), located 155 km to the south, offers more intercontinental destinations, in particular to North America, South America and Africa. The closest aerodrome handling private flights is [Bissaya Barreto Aerodrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coimbra_Airport) (CBP) in Coimbra, 32 km to the east, where the Aeroclube da Figueira da Foz is based. The closest runway capable of handling widebody aircraft is at [Monte Real Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Real_Air_Base) in Leiria, some 40 km to the south, but it is home to the [Portuguese Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Air_Force)'s jet fighter aircraft, making it difficult to convert the airbase to commercial aviation due to its strategic position in Portugal's defence system.

An international airport in Figueira da Foz, the [Silver Coast International Airport](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeroporto%20Internacional%20da%20Costa%20de%20Prata), was planned in the early 1990s, which was to be located in Lavos, 7 km south of the city centre—it has never been built.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]
