**Ebrahim Azizi** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): ابراهیم عزیزی; Born 1963 - [Kermanshah](1489244.md)) is an Iranian politician, who currently serves as a member of the [Iranian Parliament](383276.md) representing [Tehran, Rey, Shemiranat and Eslamshahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran,_Rey,_Shemiranat_and_Eslamshahr_\(electoral_district\)) since 2024.[^cite_note-1]

## Career

Azizi was a member and spokesman of the [Guardian Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardian_Council).[^cite_note-2] He also served as a representative of Kermanshah in the Iranian parliament.

IranWire reported in May 2026 that Azizi had described a draft plan targeting [Donald Trump](4848272.md), [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) and the [CENTCOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CENTCOM) commander over their alleged role in the [assassination of Ali Khamenei](82537558.md), including a proposed €50 million reward for killing Trump.[^cite_note-3]
