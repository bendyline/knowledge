***The Studio*** is an American satirical [cringe comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cringe_comedy) television series created by [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen), [Evan Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Goldberg), [Peter Huyck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Huyck), Alex Gregory, and Frida Perez. It stars Rogen as an embattled [Hollywood studio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_film_studios) head struggling to balance corporate demands with his own film production desires. [Catherine O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_O'Hara) (in her final appearance before her death), [Ike Barinholtz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ike_Barinholtz), [Chase Sui Wonders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Sui_Wonders), and [Kathryn Hahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Hahn) play supporting roles. The series was released on [Apple TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_\(streaming_service\)) with the first two episodes on March 26, 2025, with Rogen and Goldberg directing all episodes.[^cite_note-ReleaseDate-6] The series has been renewed for a second season.[^cite_note-7]

*The Studio* received acclaim for its direction, performances, cinematography, humor, and satire of the film industry. Viewers and critics have highlighted its extensive use of [long takes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_take),[^cite_note-8] as well as for featuring high-profile celebrity cameos in every episode. In its first season, the show earned 23 nominations at the [77th Primetime Emmy Awards](79427113.md), making it the most-nominated comedy debut in history and tying the record for the most nominations for a single comedy season.[^cite_note-9] It went on to win 13 Emmys from those nominations (including [Outstanding Comedy Series](4719642.md) and [Outstanding Lead Actor in a Comedy Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Comedy_Series) for Rogen), breaking the record for most wins for a comedy series in a single season before it was overtaken by the first season of *[Widow's Bay](79205666.md)* the [next year](81631496.md).[^cite_note-10] The season also received the [Television Critics Association Award for Outstanding Achievement in Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TCA_Award_for_Outstanding_Achievement_in_Comedy) and [Best Television Series – Musical or Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Television_Series_%E2%80%93_Musical_or_Comedy) at the [83rd Golden Globe Awards](81659305.md).

## Premise

Matt Remick is the newly appointed head of the floundering film production company Continental Studios. A self-described [cinephile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinephilia), Matt struggles to balance Continental's corporate aims in an increasingly [intellectual property](14724.md)-driven entertainment landscape with his own ambition to produce films.

Matt brings out the team to pay tribute to them, then introduces Griffin who drops down to the stage via wires. ## Production

On November 14, 2022, [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV+) announced it had acquired and given a straight-to-series order to an untitled [showbiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Show_business) comedy series starring [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen).[^cite_note-deadline-11-22-18][^cite_note-19] The series is executive produced by Rogen, Evan Goldberg, and James Weaver for [Point Grey Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_Grey_Pictures), and Frida Perez, Peter Huyck, Alex Gregory, Alex McAtee, and Josh Fagen. Production companies involved are Point Grey Pictures and [Lionsgate Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate_Television).[^cite_note-hollywoodreporter-03-24-20][^cite_note-deadline-03-24-21] On March 25, 2024, the untitled series co-created by Rogen, [Evan Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Goldberg), Frida Perez, Peter Huyck, and Alex Gregory was named *The Studio*. Rogen and Goldberg would also co-write and direct the series. It was reported that [Catherine O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_O'Hara), [Kathryn Hahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Hahn), [Ike Barinholtz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ike_Barinholtz), and [Chase Sui Wonders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Sui_Wonders) had joined the main cast, and [Bryan Cranston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Cranston), [Keyla Monterroso Mejia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keyla_Monterroso_Mejia), and [Dewayne Perkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewayne_Perkins) would guest star.[^cite_note-hollywoodreporter-03-24-20][^cite_note-deadline-03-24-21][^cite_note-tvline-03-24-22]

The series began filming in March 2024.[^cite_note-deadline-03-24-21] It was shot on location in Los Angeles, primarily at [Warner Bros. Studios Burbank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Studios_Burbank). The set for the Continental Studios was modeled after [Frank Lloyd Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Lloyd_Wright)'s [Mayan Revival architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayan_Revival_architecture) and inspired by the design of the [Ennis House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ennis_House). Several filming locations included real homes designed by architect [John Lautner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lautner), including Harvey House, Reiner-Burchill Residence (aka Silvertop), and Foster Carling House.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

In May 2025, the series was renewed for a second season.[^cite_note-25] It began filming in January 2026.[^cite_note-26] O'Hara died on January 30, 2026;[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] she did not film for the second season before her death.[^cite_note-29] In March 2026, the series was filming in [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice).[^cite_note-Venice-14] [Sarah Polley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Polley) joined the writing staff for the second season.[^cite_note-30]

## Release

The series premiered at [SXSW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest) on March 7, 2025,[^cite_note-31] and debuted on Apple TV+ with its first two episodes on March 26, 2025.[^cite_note-32]
