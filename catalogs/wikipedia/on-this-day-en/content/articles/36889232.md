**Sarrin** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): صرين),[^cite_note-2] also spelled **Serrin** or **Sareen** is a town in northern [Syria](7515849.md), administratively part of the [Aleppo Governorate](7444618.md), located northeast of [Aleppo](159244.md). It is situated 3 kilometers east of the [Euphrates River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphrates_River), south of [Kobanî](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koban%C3%AE) and east of [Manbij](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manbij).[^cite_note-3]



*Sarrin* is the administrative center of [Nahiya Sarrin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya_Sarrin?action=edit\&redlink=1) of the [Ayn al-Arab District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayn_al-Arab_District).

In the 2004 census, the town of Sarrin had a population of 6,140, while the Sarrin subdistrict had a total population of 70,522.[^cite_note-census2004-1] The inhabitants of the town are predominantly [Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab).[^cite_note-4]

## Civil war and conflict

During the [Syrian Civil War](30741795.md), the [Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant](9087364.md) seized control of the town in September 2013.[^cite_note-5]

In March 2015, Kurds from the [People's Protection Units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Protection_Units) (YPG), alongside [Free Syrian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Syrian_Army) (FSA) rebels, launched an [attack to take control of the strategic town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Sarrin_\(March%E2%80%93April_2015\)).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] On July 27, 2015, the town came under the control of Kurdish YPG forces.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

On 29 January 2025, as a result of a Turkish airstrike 13 people were killed and 20 others wounded.[^cite_note-10]

In January 2026, Sarrin [came under the control of forces](82106178.md) affiliated with the [Syrian transitional government](79584988.md).
