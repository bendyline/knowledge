# Sudanese Civil War

The term **Sudanese Civil War** refers to at least three separate conflicts, intermittently ongoing for more than 70 years, in [Sudan](27421.md):

- [First Sudanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Sudanese_Civil_War) (1955–1972)

- [Second Sudanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sudanese_Civil_War) (1983–2005)

- [Sudanese civil war (2023–present)](73556699.md)

It could also refer to other internal conflicts in Sudan:

- [Sudanese nomadic conflicts](23013592.md)

- [War in Darfur](5043637.md) (2003–2020)

- [Sudanese conflict in South Kordofan and Blue Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_conflict_in_South_Kordofan_and_Blue_Nile) (2011–2020)

- [Blue Nile clashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Nile_clashes_\(2022%E2%80%932023\)) (2022–2023)
