# List of world records in Olympic weightlifting



Pictogram

This is a **list of world records in [Olympic weightlifting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_weightlifting)**. These records are maintained in each [weight class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_weightlifting#Competition) for the [snatch](1699032.md) lift, [clean and jerk](537948.md) lift, and the total for both lifts.

The [International Weightlifting Federation](3228206.md) (IWF) restructured its weight classes in 1993, 1998, 2018, 2025[^cite_note-1] and 2026[^cite_note-2] nullifying earlier records.

## Current records

**Key to tables**:   *Awaiting ratification*   *Not ratified or later rescinded by IWF*

When the previous records and weight classes were discarded, the IWF defined "world standards" as the minimum lifts needed to qualify as world records in the new weight classes. Wherever *World Standard* appears in the list below, no qualified weightlifter has yet lifted these weights in a sanctioned competition.
