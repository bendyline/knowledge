**Hokkons Baules** (born 7 June 1948) is a Palauan businessman and a politician, and currently a member of the [Senate of Palau](4503866.md).[^cite_note-1] He has been the president of the Senate since 19 January 2017.[^cite_note-2] He was elected in 2008 to fill the seat of the late Senate president [Johnny Reklai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Reklai). He had previously served in the Senate of the Second Olbiil Era Kelulau from 1985 until 1988.[^cite_note-senators-3]

## Career

Baules was elected to the Senate of the Seventh Olbiil Era Kelulau in a nationwide special election on 9 May 2007, following the passing of the late Senate president [Johnny Reklai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Reklai). He was the top vote-getter in virtually every state of the republic. He graduated from Seventh-day Adventist Elementary School in Koror and went on to graduate from the Palau Mission Academy. He received a Bachelor of Arts degree in history from [Philippine Union College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Union_College).[^cite_note-senators-3]

Baules advocates for Palau to switch its [recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Taiwan) from the [Republic of China](25734.md) to the [People's Republic of China](5405.md).[^cite_note-4]

In July 2025, Baules co-signed a letter with House of Delegates speaker [Gibson Kanai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibson_Kanai) stating that the [National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palau_National_Congress) would not accept a United States proposal for Palau to host third country asylum seekers.[^cite_note-5] In February 2026, Baules was barred from entering the U.S. due to allegations of corruption linked to the People's Republic of China.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
