**Songkhla** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): สงขลา, pronounced [\[sǒŋ.kʰlǎː\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Thai)), also known as **Singgora** or **Singora** ([Pattani Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelantan-Pattani_Malay): ซิงกอรอ, *Singoro*), is a city (*[thesaban nakhon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesaban_nakhon)*) in [Songkhla Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songkhla_Province) of southern [Thailand](30128.md), near the border with [Malaysia](3607937.md). Songkhla lies 968 km (601 mi) south of [Bangkok](56615.md) and as of 2020 had a population of 61,758.[^cite_note-population-1]

Despite being smaller than the neighboring city [Hat Yai](482222.md), Songkhla is the capital of Songkhla Province as well as the [Mueang Songkhla District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mueang_Songkhla_District) (Songkhla town district). Together with Hat Yai, Songkhla is part of the **Greater Hat Yai-Songkhla Metropolitan Area** (a [conurbation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conurbation) with a population of around 800,000), the third largest metropolitan area in Thailand.

At the opening of [Songkhla Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songkhla_Lake) to the [Gulf of Thailand](198107.md), Songkhla is a fishing town and also an important [harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harbour). It is the major seaport on the east side of the [Isthmus of Kra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isthmus_of_Kra).

## History

The name *Songkhla* means 'the city of lions' (not to be confused with [Singapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Singapura)) and is the [Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language) [variant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_change) of "Singgora" ([Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language) and [Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_script): سيڠڬورا). This refers to a lion-shaped mountain near the city of Songkhla. Archaeological excavations on the isthmus between Lake Songkhla and the sea reveal that in the 10th through the 14th century, this was a major urbanized area, and a center of international maritime trade, in particular with [Quanzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quanzhou) in China. The long Sanskrit name of the state that existed there has been lost; its short Sanskrit name was *Singhapura* ('Lion City') (not to be confused with [Singapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Singapura)), a city state. The short vernacular name was *Satingpra*, coming from the [Mon-Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mon-Khmer) *sting*/*steng*/*stang* (meaning 'river') and the Sanskrit *pura* ('city').[^cite_note-2]<sup>: 320–321 </sup>

The ruins of the ancient [port city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_city) of [Satingpra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satingpra) are just few kilometers north of Songkhla. It was one of the most important trading centers of the [Tambralinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambralinga) Kingdom. Archaeological digs and investigations conducted toward the end of the 20th century testify the existence of a fortified [citadel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citadel) protected by a [moat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moat) and a quadrangular surrounding wall made of brick. A sophisticated system of canals connected the sea to the Songkhla Lake permitting the circulations of ships. The excavations brought to light artifacts of great historical and artistic value.[^cite_note-3]

The precursor of the present-day town of Songkhla was The [Sultanate of Singora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Singora), a heavily fortified port city. It was founded in the early 17th century by a Persian, Dato Mogol, and flourished during the reign of his son, Sultan Sulaiman Shah. In 1680, after decades of conflict, the city was destroyed and abandoned; remains include forts, city walls, a Dutch cemetery and the tomb of Sultan Sulaiman Shah.

On 8 December 1941 local time, the [Imperial Japanese army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_army) landed in Songkhla, [invading Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Invasion_of_Thailand). Because of the [International Date Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Date_Line), this actually occurred hours before the 7 December (Hawaii time) [attack on Pearl Harbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_on_Pearl_Harbor), making it the first major action of the [Pacific War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_War). The Japanese forces then moved south towards [Perlis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perlis) and [Penang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penang) as part of the [Malayan campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Malaya) which culminated in the [capture of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Singapore).[^cite_note-4]

Since 2003, Songhkla has been affected by [separatist insurgencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Thailand_insurgency) in neighboring [Narathiwat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narathiwat_Province), [Pattani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pattani_Province), and [Yala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yala_Province).[^cite_note-5]

The municipality's mayor, Peera Tantiserane, was murdered in Songkhla in 2014.[^cite_note-6]

## Climate

Songkhla has a [tropical monsoon climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Am*). Temperatures are very warm to hot throughout the year with only minor variation. There is a short dry season in February and March; the rest of the year is wet, with especially heavy rainfall from October to December.

## Population

The majority of the population is [Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist) with a large proportion of Muslims, especially in the rural areas near the [Malaysian](3607937.md) border. These Muslims speak the [Yawi language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yawi_language), a language closely related to [Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language), which has some Thai influence, especially in [loan words](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loan_word) borrowed from the [Thai language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language).[^cite_note-10]

Songkhla city takes up the entire Bo Yang sub-district. As of 2020 Songkhla city had a population of 61,758.[^cite_note-population-1]
