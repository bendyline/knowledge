**Emirates SkyCargo Flight 9788** was a scheduled international cargo flight operated by [Air ACT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_ACT) for [Emirates SkyCargo](1692704.md) from [Al Maktoum International Airport](3260694.md), [Dubai](211583.md), to [Hong Kong International Airport](248457.md), [Hong Kong](13404.md). On 20 October 2025, the aircraft operating the flight, a [Boeing 747-400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_747-400), suffered a [runway excursion](8731144.md) at Hong Kong International Airport at around 03\:53 [HKT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Time) (19\:53 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)). The aircraft [collided](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_collision) with a ground vehicle and subsequently crashed into the [South China Sea](74209.md). The two airport security staff in a ground vehicle were killed, while all four occupants of the aircraft survived. The aircraft, which split into two pieces, was destroyed and written off.[^cite_note-1]

[Airport Authority Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport_Authority_Hong_Kong) later announced runway 07L, the incident runway, was fit for use after examination. It is the first fatal aviation incident to occur at Hong Kong International Airport since [China Airlines Flight 642](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Airlines_Flight_642) in 1999.[^cite_note-BBCNMYSL-2]

## Background

### Aircraft

The aircraft involved was TC-ACF, a [Boeing 747-481BDSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_747-400#Freighter_conversions) operated by [Air ACT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_ACT) for [Emirates SkyCargo](1692704.md). The aircraft was first delivered as a passenger plane to [All Nippon Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Nippon_Airways) (ANA) on 13 June 1993 with registration JA8962, and previously bore a [Pokémon special livery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Jet) during its time with ANA. Following its retirement from ANA, the aircraft was converted to a [freighter](2375692.md) in 2011 and delivered to Air ACT in 2013.[^cite_note-flightradar24-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] It was one of two aircraft operated by Air ACT at the time of the accident.[^cite_note-6]

### Flight crew

The flight crew consisted of 35-year-old Captain Atilla Yilmaz, 44-year-old [first officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_officer_\(aviation\)) Candemir Ulker, 35-year-old [loadmaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loadmaster) Caner Durgut and 46-year-old [aircraft maintenance engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_maintenance_engineer) Muzaffer Tuydu. All of them were Turkish nationals.[^cite_note-7]

### Ground crew

The two airport security staff killed in the collision were a 41-year-old man and a 30-year-old man.[^cite_note-HK01-8]

## Accident

The aircraft departed Al Maktoum International Airport in Dubai on 19 October 2025 at about 16\:59 local time (12\:59 UTC). Takeoff and cruise went uneventfully. On 20 October, at about 03\:53 [HKT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Time) (19 October, 19\:53 UTC), the aircraft landed at Hong Kong International Airport on runway 07L. The thrust reverser for engine four was inoperative. Upon landing, thrust reversers for engines one, two and three were activated and the aircraft had decelerated to about 90 knots (170 km/h; 100 mph) when the number four engine spooled to 95% forward thrust, and the aircraft veered off the left side of the runway about 5,000 feet (1,500 m) past the threshold.[^cite_note-prelim-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-flightradar24-3] During the excursion, the aircraft struck a patrol vehicle that was carrying out patrolling duty on the road outside the fenced perimeter of the runway. The aircraft came to rest in the sea with its tail section separated from the rest of the fuselage, being nearly submerged in the water with large cracks clearly visible aft of the rear pressure bulkhead.[^cite_note-BBCNMYSL-2] The [Hong Kong Fire Services Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Fire_Services_Department) had received notification of the incident at 03\:55 HKT and began the rescue operation two minutes later.[^cite_note-11]

[Emirates](142715.md) confirmed the aircraft was not carrying any cargo.[^cite_note-12] The ground vehicle was also dragged five metres from shore and trapped seven metres under water.[^cite_note-13] The aircraft did not send an emergency signal and did not respond to [air traffic control](48563.md).[^cite_note-SCMP-14] According to [Flightradar24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flightradar24) data, the aircraft hit the water at about 49 knots (91 km/h; 56 mph). At the time of the crash, the wind speed was 4 knots (7.4 km/h; 4.6 mph; 2.1 m/s) with gusts of up to 21 knots (39 km/h; 24 mph; 11 m/s).[^cite_note-flightradar24-3] At least one of the aircraft's [evacuation slides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evacuation_slides) deployed successfully.[^cite_note-BBCNMYSL-2]

All four crew members on the aircraft survived and were transported to a hospital. One man in the vehicle struck was killed, while a second man was sent to [North Lantau Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Lantau_Hospital) where he later died.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] The ground crew killed were a 41-year-old driver of the vehicle, and a 30-year-old passenger.[^cite_note-HK01-8] The two ground staff had 7 and 12 years of experience respectively. At least 210 firefighters and first aid officers, 45 vehicles, ships and a flying service helicopter assisted in the rescue efforts.[^cite_note-17] The affected runway was closed, but the two other runways remained in operation.[^cite_note-BBCNMYSL-2] 12 cargo flights were cancelled throughout the day while passenger flights were not affected.[^cite_note-SCMP-14]

### Official response

[Airport Authority Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport_Authority_Hong_Kong) confirmed the deaths of two ground crew members and offered its condolences.[^cite_note-18]

## Investigation

Hong Kong's [Civil Aviation Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Aviation_Department_\(Hong_Kong\)) said in a statement it was following up with the airline and other parties involved in the crash.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] The [National Transportation Safety Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Transportation_Safety_Board) said in a [Twitter](9988187.md) post that they were sending a team of five investigators to assist in the investigation.[^cite_note-21] Turkey's [Transport Safety Investigation Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_Safety_Investigation_Center) and experts from [Boeing](18933266.md) were also part of the investigation. The city's [Air Accident Investigation Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Accident_Investigation_Authority) said it would release a preliminary report within one month.[^cite_note-CBS-22]

All flight recorders were recovered.[^cite_note-prelim-9] On 26 October, salvage crews recovered the fuselage and loaded it onto a vessel. The aircraft's tail and other parts including the flight data recorder and the cockpit voice recorder were lifted and sent to a laboratory for preliminary examination. Before the salvage operation, experts conducted an underwater sonar survey.[^cite_note-CBS-22]

On 18 November, the AAIA released their preliminary report on the accident. The report found that the thrust reverser on engine four was inoperative at the time of the accident. Upon landing, the flight crew received a message on the [engine-indicating and crew-alerting system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engine-indicating_and_crew-alerting_system) alerting them that the autobrakes disconnected, prompting the captain to take over control and begin manual braking. Thrust reversers for engines two and three were activated to 90% reverse thrust, while engine one's thrust reverser was selected to idle reverse thrust. 30 seconds after touchdown, engine four spooled up to 95% forward thrust, causing the airframe to swerve left. The crew then deactivated the thrust reversers to try to regain equal engine thrust. The number four engine continued to accelerate to 106% forward thrust. The aircraft then veered off the runway, and thrust reversers were again reactivated. The aircraft then struck the ground vehicle and plunged into the sea.[^cite_note-prelim-9]
