The **2025–26** season is **Lech Poznań's** 104th season in existence and the club's 24th consecutive season in the top flight of [Polish football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_in_Poland). In addition to the [domestic league](79467685.md), Lech Poznań participates in this season's edition of the [Polish Cup](79976641.md), the [Polish Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Polish_Super_Cup) and the [UEFA Champions League](74127997.md). The season covers the period from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

Lech is defending the championship title they won last season.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

Lech Poznań plays their official home matches at the [Stadion Miejski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pozna%C5%84_Stadium) branded on 24 July 2023 as **Enea Stadion** for sponsorship reasons for four next seasons.[^cite_note-6]

The team started their preparations for the 2025–26 season on 19 June 2025.[^cite_note-7] Between 23 June and 5 July they will hold a summer training camp at the Research and Development Center - the campus of their football academy located in [Wronki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wronki).[^cite_note-8] As part of the preparations, Lech has originally scheduled 3 friendly games, but due to [Polish Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Polish_Super_Cup) game scheduled on 13 July 2025 the last game against [Teplice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FK_Teplice) has been cancelled.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

Lech kicked off the 2025–26 season on 13 July 2025 with a clash for the [Polish Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Polish_Super_Cup) against their longtime rival - [Legia Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legia_Warsaw) - who won [last season’s Polish Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Polish_Cup). Then, on 19 July 2025, they started the new [Ekstraklasa](30871677.md) season with a home game against [Cracovia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KS_Cracovia). On 23 July 2025, they played the first leg of the [UEFA Champions League](44220.md) [second qualifying round](74127997.md#Second_qualifying_round).

Lech started the season in bad style, losing their first two home matches - the Polish Super Cup and the first round of the Ekstraklasa. Despite the initial difficulties, the team went on to win the next four matches in a row, climbing up the Ekstraklasa table and advancing to the third qualifying round of the UEFA Champions League.
