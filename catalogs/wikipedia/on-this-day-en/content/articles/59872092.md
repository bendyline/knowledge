**Giovanni Castellucci** (born 23 July 1959) is an [Italian](385155.md) former [businessman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Businessperson) who served as the [chief executive officer](52234.md) (CEO) of [Autostrade per l'Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autostrade_of_Italy) and [Atlantia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantia_\(company\)).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-\:0-7] In 2023, he was convicted of [manslaughter](7397035.md) and negligence in relation to the [2013 Monteforte Irpino bus crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Monteforte_Irpino_bus_crash), after having been acquitted at the initial trial. The conviction was upheld by the [Court of Cassation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_cassation) in 2025, and Castellucci was sentenced to six years in [prison](19008450.md).[^cite_note-appealscourt-1][^cite_note-8]

## Early life and education

Castellucci was born on 23 July 1959 in [Senigallia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senigallia), [Italy](14532.md). He earned a degree in [mechanical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering) from the [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence) and later earned a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from the [Bocconi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bocconi_University) School of Management in [Milan](36511.md).[^cite_note-\:0-7]

## Career

### Early career

Castellucci worked for the [Boston Consulting Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Consulting_Group) (BCG) from 1988 to 1999 at its [Paris](22989.md) and [Milan](36511.md) offices, where he became a partner and lead of the Italian customer service and pharmaceutical practices.

### Autostrade

In January 2000, Castellucci was appointed CEO of the [Barilla Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barilla_Group). The following year, he joined the Autostrade Group as a [general manager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Director_general). From April 2005 to January 2019, he served as CEO of [Autostrade per l'Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autostrade_of_Italy).&#x20;

### Atlantia and the Abertis operation

In April 2006, Castellucci became CEO of the Autostrade Group, which was later renamed [Atlantia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantia_\(company\)). In March 2018, the Atlantia Group acquired a 15.49% stake in the [Eurotunnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Getlink).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] Castellucci took on the position of Chief Executive Officer of the new holding. In November 2013, he became a board director of [Aeroporti di Roma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeroporti_di_Roma).[^cite_note-12] Castellucci acquired the Spanish infrastructure company [Abertis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abertis),[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] which has been acquired by Atlantia following an agreement with [ACS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACS_Group) and [Hochtief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hochtief).[^cite_note-18] On the 17<sup>th</sup> of September 2019, Castellucci resigned from both his positions as [Chief Executive Officer](52234.md) and General Manager of Atlantia.[^cite_note-19]

### 2013 Bus Crash Trials

On July 28, 2013, [a bus on the A16 motorway plunged off a viaduct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Monteforte_Irpino_bus_crash) near [Monteforte Irpino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monteforte_Irpino) ([Avellino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avellino)), killing 40 people. Multiple former and current managers at [Autostrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autostrade_of_Italy), including Castellucci, were charged with multiple counts of negligent homicide and criminal negligence in connection with the incident.[^cite_note-20]

On January 11, 2019, Castellucci was acquitted by the Court of Avellino.[^cite_note-21] In 2023, the [Court of Appeal of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Appeal_of_Naples?action=edit\&redlink=1) overturned the acquittal and sentenced him to serve six years in prison. The [Court of Cassation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Cassation) upheld the conviction on 11 April 2025.[^cite_note-appealscourt-1]

### 2018 Morandi Bridge Collapse Trial and Conviction



View of the [Ponte Morandi](58143323.md) in [Genoa](47332321.md) after the [collapse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Ponte_Morandi_collapse) in August 2018

On August 14, 2018 at 11\:36am, the [Ponte Morandi Bridge](58143323.md) in [Genoa](47332321.md) collapsed, killing 43 people.[^cite_note-22] Caselucci was tried along with 59 other individuals for culpability in the collapse.[^cite_note-23] On July 16, 2026, he was convicted and sentenced to 12 years in prison[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] adding to his previous sentence for the 2013 bus crash.

## Awards

In 2016, he was appointed a Knight of the Legion of Honour during a ceremony at [Palazzo Farnese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palazzo_Farnese).[^cite_note-26]
