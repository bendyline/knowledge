**Benue** is a [state](226734.md) in the [North Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Central_Nigeria) region of [Nigeria](21383.md). It is popularly referred to as part of the [Middle Belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Belt). It has an estimated population of about 4,253,641 in [the 2006 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population). The state was [created](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_date_of_statehood) in 1976 and was among the seven states created at that time.[^cite_note-5] The state derives its name from the [Benue River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue_River) initially called *Ber-nor*, a compound word in [Tiv language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiv_language) which means river or lake of hippopotamus (B*er* meaning river or lake, while *nor* is the name for [hippopotamus](37764.md)) the name *Ber-nor* was corrupted to BENUE by colonial masters, the river is the second largest river in [Nigeria](21383.md) after the [River Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Niger).[^cite_note-6] The state borders [Nasarawa State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasarawa_State) to the north; [Taraba State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taraba_State) to the east; [Kogi State](299399.md) to the west; [Enugu State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu_State) to the south-west; [Ebonyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebonyi_State) and [Cross River State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_River_State) to the south; and has an international border with [Cameroon](5447.md) to the [south-east](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon-Nigeria_border).[^cite_note-7] It is inhabited predominantly by the [Tiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiv_people), [Idoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idoma_people) and [Igede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igede) people. Minority ethnic groups in Benue are [Etulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etulo_language), [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people) and [Jukun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jukun_people_\(West_Africa\)) people etc. Its capital is [Makurdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makurdi).[^cite_note-8] Benue is a rich agricultural region; common crops cultivated in the state include [oranges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_\(fruit\)), yams, [mangoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango), [sweet potatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_potato), [cassava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassava), [soya bean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soybean), [guinea corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorghum_bicolor), [flax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flax), [sesame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesame), [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice), [groundnuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut) and [palm trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_tree).

## History

Benue State is a legacy of an administrative entity that was carved out of the protectorate of [northern Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nigeria) at the beginning of the twentieth century. The territory was initially known as Munshi Province until 1918 when the name of its dominant geographical feature, the [Benue River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue_River) was adopted.[^cite_note-9]

Benue State is named after the [Benue River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue_River) and was formed from the former [Benue-Plateau State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue-Plateau_State) in 1976, along with [Igala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igala_people) and some parts of [Kwara State](2227645.md).[^cite_note-\:0-10] In 1991, some areas of Benue state (mostly the [Igala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igala_people)-speaking area), along with areas in [Kwara State](2227645.md), were clubbed together to form the new [Kogi State](299399.md). [Igbo people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_people) are found in the boundary areas like the [Obi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obi,_Nigeria), [Oju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oju,_Nigeria) and Ado Local Government Areas. It is known as the "Heartbeat of the Middle Belt" and the "Entertainment Capital of the [Middle Belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Belt)" north of the [Niger River](21437.md). [Otukpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpo), the [Idoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idoma_people) people's traditional and administrative capital, is also known as the Lion's Heart and the Land of the Brave.[^cite_note-11]

### Population structure and distribution

Benue State, which is located in the North Central region of [Nigeria](21383.md), has a total population of 4,253,641 in the [2006 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population), with an average population density of 99 persons per km<sup>2</sup>. This makes Benue the 9th most populous state in Nigeria. However, the distribution of the population according to Local government areas shows marked duality.[^cite_note-12]

There are areas of low [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_\(human_biology\)) density, such as [Guma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guma,_Nigeria), [Ohimini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohimini), Tarka, [Apa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apa,_Benue), [Logo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logo,_Nigeria) and [Agatu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agatu), each with less than seventy persons per km<sup>2</sup>, while [Vandeikya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandeikya), Ukum, Kwande, [Otukpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpo), [Okpokwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okpokwu), and [Gboko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gboko) have densities ranging from 160 persons to 200 persons per km<sup>2</sup>. Makurdi LGA has over 380 persons per km2. The males are 49.8 percent of the total population while females constitute 50.2 per cent.

### Settlement pattern and urbanization

Benue State region was depleted of its human population during the [slave trade](49005.md). It is largely rural, with scattered settlements mainly in tiny compounds or homesteads, whose population range from 630 people, most of whom are farmers.

Urbanization in Benue State did not predate the colonial era. The few towns established during colonial rule remained very small (less than 30,000 people) up to the creation of Benue State in 1976.

Benue towns can be categorised into three groups. The first group consists of those with a population of 80,000 to 500,000 people. These include [Makurdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makurdi), the State Capital, [Gboko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gboko) and [Otukpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpo) the "headquarters" of the two dominant ethnic groups (125,944 and 88,958 people respectively). The second group comprises towns with a population of between 20,000 and 50,000 people and includes [Katsina-Ala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina-Ala), [Zaki-Biam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukum), [Vandeikya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandeikya) and [Adikpo](24822416.md). These are all local government headquarters. The third category comprises towns with a population of 10,000 to 19,000 people and includes, Lessel, [Ihugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandeikya), [Naka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naka_Benue_Nigeria), Adoka, Aliade, Okpoga, [Igumale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igumale), [Oju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oju,_Nigeria), Utonkon, Ugbokolo, Wannune, Ugbokpo, [Otukpa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpa), [Ugba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ugba) and Korinya. Most of these towns are headquarters of recently created Local Government Areas and/or district headquarters or major market areas. Some of the headquarters of the newly created LGAs have populations of less than 10,000 people. Such places include Tse-Agberaba, Gbajimba, [Buruku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buruku), Idekpa, [Obagaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obagaji) and Obarikeito. Apart from earth roads, schools, periodic markets and chemists (local drug stores), the rural areas are largely used for farming, relying on the urban centres for most of their urban needs. Benue State has no problem of capital city primacy. Rather, three towns stand out very clearly as important urban centres which together account for more than 70 per cent of the social amenities provided in the state and almost all the industrial establishments. These centres are [Makurdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makurdi), [Gboko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gboko) and [Otukpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpo). They are among the oldest towns in the state and are growing at a much faster rate than the smaller younger towns.

[Makurdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makurdi) doubles as the capital of the state and the headquarters of Makurdi LGA, while [Gboko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gboko), [Otukpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpo) and [Oju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oju,_Nigeria) double as the local government and ethnic headquarters (i.e. for [Tiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiv_people), [Idoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idoma_people), and Igede). All the roads in the state radiate from these three centres. As an administrative unit, Benue State was first created on 3 February 1976. It was one of the seven states created by the military administration headed by [General Murtala Mohammed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murtala_Mohammed), which increased the number of states in the country from 13 to 19. In 1991, its boundaries were re-adjusted with the creation of [Kogi State](299399.md). The new Benue State of today has twenty-three (23) local government areas,[^cite_note-13] which are administered by local government councils.

In recent period, Benue State has been a flashpoint for [insecurity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insecurity) in Nigeria; it has witnessed frequent [clashes between indigenous farmers and Fulani herders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herder-farmer_conflict_in_Nigeria) and other [jihadist attacks](27740659.md) which has led to incessant killings, difficulty in farming and has displaced lots of people.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] In June 2025, [over 100 people were murdered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yelwata_massacre) in Yelwata village.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-Shibayan-20]

## Geography



Mkar Hill

Benue State lies within the lower [river Benue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue_River) trough in the middle belt region of Nigeria. Its geographic coordinates are longitude 7° 47' and 10° 0' East. Latitude 6° 25' and 8° 8' North; and shares boundaries with five other states namely: [Nasarawa State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasarawa_State) to the north for 231 km (partly across the Benue River), [Taraba State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taraba_State) to the east for 235 km, [Cross-River State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_River_State) to the south for 96 km, [Enugu State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu_State) to the south-west for 84 km, and [Kogi State](299399.md) to the west for 153 km. The state also shares a common boundary for 22 km with the [Nord-Ouest Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nord-Ouest_Province,_Cameroon), claimed by both [Ambazonia](841514.md) and the [Republic of Cameroon](5447.md) on the south-east. Benue occupies a landmass of 34,059 square kilometres (13,150 mi<sup>2</sup>).

### Climate

Benue state has a Tropical wet and dry or [savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savanna). The city's yearly temperature is 29.38 °C (84.88 °F) and it is −0.08% lower than Nigeria's averages. The state receives about 135.2 millimetres (5.32 in) of [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation) and has 160.01 rainy days (43.84% of the time) annually.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-\:1-22]

### Geology

Based on [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification), Benue State lies within the [AW climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) and experiences two distinct seasons, the [Wet season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_season) and the [Dry season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season). The rainy season lasts from April to October with annual rainfall in the range of 100–200 mm (3.9–7.9 in). The [dry season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season) begins in November and ends in March. [Temperatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature) fluctuate between 21–37 °C (70–99 °F) in the year. The south-eastern part of the state adjoining the Obudu-Cameroun mountain range, however, has a cooler climate similar to that of [Plateau State](1723458.md).[^cite_note-\:1-22]

Much of Benue State falls within the Benue Valley/trough which is believed to be structurally developed. During the Tertiary and possibly the [Interglacial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interglacial) periods of the [Quaternary glaciation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaternary_glaciation), the Benue and Niger Valleys, otherwise known as the Niger/Benue trough, were transgressed by the waters of the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md). As a result, marine sediments form the dominant surface geology of much of Benue State.

### Mineral resources

Benue State is blessed with abundant mineral resources. These resources are distributed in the Local Government Areas of the state. Of these mineral resources, only [limestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone) at [Tse-Kucha, Gboko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gboko) and [Kaolinite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaolinite) at [Otukpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpo) are being commercially exploited. Other mineral deposits include [baryte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baryte), [gypsum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gypsum), [feldspar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feldspar), [wolframite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolframite), [kaolinite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaolinite), mineral salts and [gemstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemstone) etc.

### Agriculture

[Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) is the mainstay of the economy of Benue State, engaging over 75% of the state's workforce. Prominent crops include [yam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yam_\(vegetable\)), rice, [beans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bean), [cassava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassava), [sweet potato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_potato), maize, [soybean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soybean), [sorghum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorghum), [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet), [sesame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesame), [cocoyam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoyam) etc. The state also accounts for over 70% of Nigeria's [soybean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soybean) production.[^cite_note-23]

The vegetation of the southern parts of the state is characterised by forests, which yield trees for timber and provide a suitable habitat for rare animals types and species. The state thus possesses potential for the development of viable forest and wildlife reserves.

### Relief and drainage

The land is generally low lying (averaging 100 m \[330 ft]-250 m \[820 ft]) and gently undulating with occasional [inselbergs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_inselbergs), [knoll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillock), [laterite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laterite) etc. It is only at the boundary area with [Cameroon](5447.md), also [Kwande](24822416.md) and [Oju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oju,_Nigeria) Local Government Areas that hilly terrain with appreciable local relief is encountered. Here, the terrain is characterised by steep slopes, deep incised [valleys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valleys) and generally rugged relief. Elsewhere, gradients average less than 4°. [River Benue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Benue) is the dominant geographical feature in the state. It is one of the few large rivers in Nigeria. The [Katsina-Ala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina-Ala_River) is the largest tributary, while the smaller rivers include [Mkomon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mkomon?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Amile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amile), [Duru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duru), [Loko Konshisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loko_Konshisha?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Kpa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kpa_River?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Okpokwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okpokwu), [Mu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_River_\(Nigeria\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Be](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Be_River_\(Nigeria\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Aya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aya_River?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Apa Ogede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apa_Ogede?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Ombi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ombi). The flood plains which are characterised by extensive swamps and ponds are good for dry season irrigated farming.

Though Benue State has high drainage density many of the streams are seasonal. Hence, there is an acute water shortage in the dry season in Local Government Areas such as [Guma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guma,_Nigeria), [Okpokwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okpokwu), [Ogbadibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogbadibo), [Gwer West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwer_West) ([Naka, Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naka,_Benue_Nigeria?action=edit\&redlink=1)) and [Oju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oju,_Nigeria). This river system has great potential for a viable fishing industry, dry season farming through irrigation and for an inland water highway.

### Local Government Areas

The state is populated by several ethnic groups: [Tiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiv_people), [Idoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idoma_people), [Igede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igede_language), [Etulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etulo_language), Ufia [Orring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korring), Abakpa, [Jukun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jukun_people_\(West_Africa\)), [Igbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_people), [Akweya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akweya?action=edit\&redlink=1), [hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people) and [Nyifon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyifon_language).

The people of the state are famous for their cheerful and hospitable disposition as well as rich cultural heritage.

## People and culture

### Traditional rulers

The Benue State Government accords high respect to traditional rulers in recognition of their role as custodians of culture and as agents of development. Also, their roles in enhancing peace and order at the grassroots level are also recognised. To enhance their contribution to the affairs of the state, government has established a three-tier traditional council system made up of Local Government Area Traditional Councils, Area Traditional Councils and the State Council of Chiefs with the Tor Tiv (Tiv King) as chairman.

The two Area Councils are the [Tiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiv_people) Traditional Council and the [Idoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idoma_people) Traditional Council. The former has the [Tor Tiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tor_Tiv) (Tiv King) as chairman of all the traditional rulers from the [Tiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiv_people) dominant Local Government Areas, while the latter has the Och' Idoma ([Idoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idoma_people) king) as the chairman of all the traditional rulers from the [Idoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idoma_people) dominant Local Government Areas.

The State Council of Chiefs has the Tor Tiv, Orchivirigh Professor [James Ayatse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Ayatse) (Tor Tiv V) ([^cite_note-24]), with Och'Idoma and all second class Chiefs/Chairmen of the Local Government Traditional Council as members.

### Culture



Dancers in Benue state attire



Benue state contingent

Benue State possesses a rich and diverse cultural heritage which finds expression in colourful cloths, exotic masquerades, supplicated music and dances. Traditional dances from Benue State have won acclaim at national and international cultural festivals. The most popular of these dances include [Ingyough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingyough?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Ange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ange_\(band\)), [Anchanakupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anchanakupa?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Swange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swange) and [Ogirinya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogirinya?action=edit\&redlink=1) among others.

The socio-religious festivals of the people, colourful dances, dresses and songs are also of tourist value. The Alekwu ancestral festival of the Idoma people, for instance, is an occasion when the local people believe their ancestors re-established contact with the living in the form of masquerades.

The [Igede Agba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igede_Agba) is a Yam Festival, marked every year in September by the Igede people of [Oju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oju,_Nigeria) and [Obi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obi,_Nigeria) local government areas. Among the Tiv, the Tiv Day, marriage ceremonies and dance competitions (e.g. Swange dance) are often very colourful. [Kwagh-Hir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwagh-Hir) is a very entertaining Tiv puppet show.

Social life in Benue State is also enriched by the availability of diverse recreational facilities. Apart from parks, beaches, dancing and masquerades mentioned above, the big hotels in [Makurdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makurdi), [Gboko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gboko) and [Otukpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpo) are equipped with several indoor sporting facilities. There are golf clubs, the Makurdi Club, Railway Club, Police Club and Air Force Club, where, apart from the sale of drinks and snacks, various types of games are played.

There is a standard Arts Theatre, a modern Sports Complex which includes the [Aper Aku Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aper_Aku_Stadium), which has provisions for such games as tennis, basketball, volleyball and handball, an indoor sports hall and Olympic size swimming pool in Makurdi. Smaller stadia exist in [Gboko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gboko) (J. S. Tarka Stadium), [Katsina-Ala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina-Ala), [Adikpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adikpo?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Vandeikya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandeikya) and [Otukpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpo). The BCC Lawn Tennis tournament attracts players from all over the country. Benue State has three teams in the professional soccer ranks, [Lobi Stars F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobi_Stars_F.C.) in Division One, [BCC Lions FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BCC_Lions_FC) in Division Two, Mark mentors as the basketball premier league team.

## Economy

### Agricultural production

Agriculture forms the backbone of the Benue State economy, engaging more than 70 per cent of the working population. This has made Benue the major source of food production in the Nation. Experts say that it can still be developed because most of the modern techniques are not yet popular to majority of Nigerian farmers. Mechanization and plantation agriculture/agro-forestry are still at its infancy. Farm inputs such as fertilizers, improved seed, insecticides and other foreign methods are being increasingly used. However, cost and availability is still a challenge. Important cash crops include [soybeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soybean), rice, peanuts, [mango varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango), [citrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citrus), etc.

Other cash crops include [palm oil](57561.md), melon, [African pear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dacryodes_edulis), [chili pepper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chili_pepper), [tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomatoes) etc. Food crops include [Yam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yam_\(vegetable\)), [Cassava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassava), [Sweet potato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_potato), [Beans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bean), Maize, [Millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet), [Guinea corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinea_corn), Vegetables etc. There is very little irrigation agriculture and techniques.

Animal production includes: Cattle, Pork, Poultry and Goat but no dairy and dairy products yet.

[The Zaki Ibiam International Yam Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaki_Biam_Yam_Market) is the biggest market for a single product in Nigeria.[^cite_note-27]

### Commerce and industry

Banking services are available as all the banks in Nigeria have branches in the state, with the [Central Bank of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Nigeria) having its regional headquarters in Makurdi. Dangote Cement Company is a private company that operates within the state and provides a little employment to the people.

This is a rough estimated employment percentage in the state: 75% are farmers, 10% traders, 6% civil servants, 9% private.

Benue State has the capacity to support a wide range of industries using local agricultural and mineral resources although potentials are yet to be exploited. Private initiatives in commerce and industry, particularly by indigenes, is as also on a small scale and is limited to carpentry, shoe repairs and manufacturing, small scale rice mills, leather and plastic industries, weaving, printing, catering, block making, food processing, etc. Industry and commerce have been greatly retarded by the absence of capital funds, basic infrastructure and the frequent political changes.

### Infrastructure and transportation

The location of Benue State in the centre of the country plus the presence of bridges on two large rivers, the [Benue River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue_River) and [Katsina-Ala River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_Ala_River), make the state a major cross-roads centre. The present government has shown commitment to the construction of roads within the state capital Makurdi and other major roads leading in and out of the state. The roads are in an excellent condition.[^cite_note-28]

Two Federal Highways connect the Eastern States to the North and Northeast.&#x20;

- [A3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A3_highway_\(Nigeria\)) north from [Enugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu_\(city\)) via [Otukpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpo) and [Makurdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makurdi) to [Lafia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafia), Nasarawa State.

- [A4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A4_highway_\(Nigeria\)) northeast from Benue State at Gakem to [Taraba State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taraba_State) near Kado as the Katsina Ala-Zaki Biam road.

Other major roads include:&#x20;

- Makurdi-Gboko road southeast via Yandev to Ugbema where the Katsina Ala Road continues southwest via Adikpo to [Cross River State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_River_State) at Gakem,

- Makurdi-Naka-Adoka-Ankpa-Okene road west,

- Makurdi-Yandev-Katsina Ala-Wukari road east,

- Makurdi North-Uda Road west to [Nasarawa State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasarawa_State) at Utagudu,

- Adoka-Weto Road north from Adoka across the [Benue River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue_River) by the two km [Loko-Oweto Bridge](http://Loko%20Oweto%20Bridge) (2022),

- Gboko-Mbasere Road east from [A3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A3_highway_\(Nigeria\)) at Aliade

- Katsina Ala-Takum road east to [Taraba State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taraba_State) at Dogon Gawa.

The Federal government is also remodelling and expanding the [Makurdi Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makurdi_Airport) to become the largest agricultural cargo airport in the country.

The rivers [Benue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue_River) and [Katsina-Ala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_Ala_River) only need to be dredged and a modern river port built at [Makurdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makurdi), Turan, Buruku and [Katsina-Ala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_Ala_River) to transport goods all over the nation since the state is at the middle of the country. Ships are sighted very occasionally offloading cargo for Benue company at the Abinsi jetty.

The Eastern rail line north from Enugu across the Benue River by the [Makurdi Rail Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makurdi_Rail_Bridge?action=edit\&redlink=1) (1932) to [Nasarawa State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasarawa_State) connects [Makurdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makurdi), [Otukpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otukpo), [Taraku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taraku?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Utonkon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utonkon) and [Igumale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igumale) with the rest of the country. Altogether, about 180 km of rail line traverses the state.

### Tourism and recreation

Benue State possesses tourism potentials which are yet to be explored. Since some of the tourist attractions are best selected by the tourist, Benue State in her bid to develop tourism embarked on infrastructural development necessary for the overall well-being of the tourist. The options are still opened to foreign investors.

The programme of road development, hotels, postal, telephone and Email services, electricity, potable water, recreational facilities and the general hospitality of the people will eventually open up the state to tourists. An amusement park and zoo have been developed at Makurdi. There is a wildlife park at lkwe where a conference hall and chalets have been built to serve tourists. Tourist attractions in the state may be grouped into natural scenic, historical monuments and festivals.

Natural Attractions in Benue State include hills and ranges such as Ikyogen, Abande, Ngokur, Mkar, Ushongo and Harga. There is a heavily wooded natural trench at Tse-Mker which is believed to be inhabited by dangerous creatures including Pythons. Gurgul is a waterfall on [Katsina Ala River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_Ala_River). The border stretch adjoining the [Obudu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obudu_Plateau)/[South Cameroon Plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Cameroon_Plateau) ranges features forests, dissected hills, [Dykes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dike_\(geology\)), dozens of fast flowing streams and incised valleys. In the [Dry season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season), the water level in both the [Benue River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue_River) and [Katsina-Ala River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_Ala_River) falls, giving rise to sandy river beaches and clean shallow waters. The waters are used for recreational boating and swimming.[^cite_note-33] At Orokam in [Ogbadibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogbadibo) Local government areas, occurs the Enumabia Warm Spring. At Epwa-Ibilla, Andi-Ibilla, Uchenyum-Ibilla, Okochi-Uwokwu, Irachi-Uwokwu, Ette-Uda-Uwokwu, Odepa-Uwokwu, Igbegi-Ipinu-Uwokwu, Edde-Ibilla-Uwokwu, and Ohuma-Uwokwu of Oju, are springs of clean water. There are masses of unusually tall trees in the deep valleys and on the pretty steep highlands, which are all however inhabited.

Historical monuments in the state include the Royal Niger Company Trading Stores at Makurdi and Gbileve near [Katsina-Ala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina-Ala); the tombs of the first Dutch missionaries at Harga and Sai in [Katsina-Ala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina-Ala) Local Government Areas; the tombs of the legendary politician, [Mr. J. S. Tarka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Tarka) and past three Tor Tiv in [Gboko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gboko) town; and the trench fortifications in Turan district of [Kwande](24822416.md) local government areas which were dug by the Tiv to ward off [Chamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamba_people) invasions in the 19th century.

At Utonkon, is a thick forest with tall giant trees, the centre of which used to serve as a slave trade market but is now the site of shrines and a periodic market.
