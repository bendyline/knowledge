The **79th Tony Awards** were held on June 7, 2026, to recognize excellence in [Broadway](725252.md) productions during the 2025–26 season. The ceremony took place at [Radio City Music Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_City_Music_Hall) in [New York City](645042.md), and was broadcast on [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS) and streamed on [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount+).[^cite_note-1]

## Ceremony information

[Pluto TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pluto_TV) streamed the pre-ceremony broadcast *The Tony Awards: Act One*, hosted by [Laura Benanti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Benanti) and [Tituss Burgess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tituss_Burgess).[^cite_note-2]

Raj Kapoor, Sarah Levine Hall, and Jack Sussman produced the ceremony, replacing [Ricky Kirshner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Kirshner) and [Glenn Weiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Weiss) at White Cherry Entertainment who produced from 2004 to 2025.[^cite_note-3] Pop singer [Pink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_\(singer\)), whose Broadway-adjacent credits include having her songs featured in *[& Juliet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/&_Juliet)* and *[Moulin Rouge!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moulin_Rouge!_\(musical\))*, served as host.[^cite_note-4]

The musical adaptation of the television series *[Schmigadoon!](76848716.md)* won Best Musical and three others including two for the show's co-creator [Cinco Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinco_Paul), while the play *[Liberation](79781080.md)* won its sole Tony for Best Play.

The revival of [Arthur Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Miller)'s *[Death of a Salesman](156620.md)* won the most awards at the ceremony with six in total.

## Eligibility

The Tony Awards eligibility dates for the 2025–2026 Broadway season were April 28, 2025, through April 26, 2026. Productions were also required to meet all other eligibility criteria as previously set forth by the [American Theatre Wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Theatre_Wing) and [the Broadway League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Broadway_League). There are 41 [legitimate Broadway-eligible theaters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Broadway_theaters) in which a production must be performed to attain eligibility for award consideration. Nominations for the 2026 Tony Awards were announced on May 5, 2026.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
