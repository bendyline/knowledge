The **Ministry of Interior** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الداخلية الكويتية) is one of [the governmental bodies](16688.md) of [Kuwait](7515890.md). Its minister in charge is a member of the [Cabinet of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Kuwait) and the current minister of interior is [Sheikh Fahad Yousef Al-Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahad_Yusuf_Al-Sabah).

## History

The early organization which dealt with internal affairs was the [Directorate of Public Security Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Kuwait), formed during the [Interwar period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interwar_period) in 1938 by [Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_Al-Jaber_Al-Sabah) and mainly taking effect following [World War II](32927.md). Prior to 1938, internal security was dependent on the [Defense Security Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Kuwait) which were headed by Sheikh Ali Salem Al-Mubarak Al-Sabah. Following the [Battle of Al-Regeai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Al-Regeai), command of the Defense and Security Forces was assigned to Shiekh Abdullah Jaber Al-Abdullah II Al-Sabah, who was appointed by Sheikh Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah and was commanding from 1928 to 1938.

## Directors (1938–1962)

The [Kuwait Army](13558747.md) split from the [Directorate of Public Security Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Kuwait) in 1953; subsequently, the latter merged with [Directorate of Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_Police) to form [Directorate of Public Security and Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Kuwait) in 1959; following the demolition of the [defensive wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensive_wall) of Kuwait in 1957 which later formed the Kuwait Ministry of Interior including the [Kuwait Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_Police).

## Organization and activities

The major responsibilities of the ministry are public security, and maintenance of law and order.[^cite_note-mon-13] The major internal security organization under the ministry is [the national police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_Police).[^cite_note-mon-13] The ministry is also responsible for managing the election process together with the ministry of justice.[^cite_note-14]

The ministry has 48 service centers and 62 police stations in Capital, [Farwaniya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farwaniya), [Jahra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jahra), [Hawalli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawalli,_Kuwait), [Ahmadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadi_District), and [Mubarak Al Kabir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mubarak_Al-Kabeer_Governorate) regions across Kuwait.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] In 2008, a rehabilitation center was launched in the ministry in order to deal with those Kuwaiti citizens holding radical religious views.[^cite_note-17] The ministry established special teams consisting of members of non-governmental organizations to eliminate vote-buying before [the 2012 general elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwaiti_general_election,_February_2012).[^cite_note-18]

The ministry in 2024 has been working to combat the surge of drugs, holding those accountable for forgery in citizenship and those with dual citizenships, to implement and pass through new traffic laws, building its data base with the biometric system and many other things.
