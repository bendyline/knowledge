**Chiyoda** ([Japanese](15606.md): 千代田区, [Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepburn_romanization): *Chiyoda-ku*; IPA: [\[tɕijoda\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Japanese) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/54/TomJ-Chiyoda.ogg/TomJ-Chiyoda.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/54/TomJ-Chiyoda.ogg/TomJ-Chiyoda.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>), officially known as **Chiyoda City** in English,[^cite_note-Profile-2] is a [special ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_wards_of_Tokyo) of [Tokyo](30057.md), Japan. Located in the heart of Tokyo's 23 special wards, Chiyoda consists of [the Imperial Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Imperial_Palace) and a surrounding radius of about a kilometer (1000 yards), and is known as the political and financial center of Japan. As of October 2020, the ward had a population of 66,680, and a population density of 5,709 people per km<sup>2</sup> (14,786 per sq. mi.), making it by far the least populated of the special wards. The residential part of Chiyoda is at the heart of [Yamanote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamanote_and_Shitamachi), Tokyo's traditional upper-class residential area, with [Banchō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banch%C5%8D), [Kōjimachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C5%8Djimachi), and [Kioichō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kioich%C5%8D,_Chiyoda,_Tokyo) considered the most exclusive neighbourhoods in the entire city. The total area is 11.66 km<sup>2</sup> (4½ sq. mi.), of which the Imperial Palace, [Hibiya Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hibiya_Park), [National Museum of Modern Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Museum_of_Modern_Art,_Tokyo), and [Yasukuni Shrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasukuni_Shrine) take up approximately 2.6 km<sup>2</sup> (1 sq. mi.), or 22%.

Chiyoda is known as the economic center of Japan; the districts of [Otemachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Ctemachi), [Marunouchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marunouchi) and [Yurakucho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y%C5%ABrakuch%C5%8D) east of the palace (an area colloquially known as "Daimaruyu") house the headquarters of 19 [Fortune 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortune_500) companies, are together the source of roughly 10% of the combined revenue of all Japanese companies[^cite_note-3] With a day population of around 850,000, its day/night population ratio is by far the highest of all municipalities in Japan. [Tokyo Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Station), Tokyo's main [inter-city rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-city_rail) terminal and the busiest train station in Japan in terms of scheduled trains, is also located in Chiyoda.



[National Diet Building](925262.md)

Chiyoda is also the political center of the country.[^cite_note-4] Chiyoda, literally meaning "[field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_field) of a thousand generations", inherited the name from the Chiyoda Castle, the other name for [Edo Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_Castle), which is the site of the present-day Imperial Palace. With the seat of the [Emperor](10110.md) in the Imperial Palace at the ward's center, many government institutions, such as the [National Diet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diet_of_Japan), the [Prime Minister's Official Residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister's_Official_Residence_\(Japan\)), the [Supreme Court](2251732.md), [ministries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministries_of_Japan) in [Kasumigaseki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasumigaseki), and agencies are also located in Chiyoda, as are Tokyo landmarks such as Yasukuni Shrine and the [Nippon Budokan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nippon_Budokan). Other notable neighborhoods of Chiyoda include [Akihabara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akihabara), [Iidabashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iidabashi) and [Kanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanda,_Tokyo).

The ward was formed in 1947 as a merger of Kanda and [Kōjimachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C5%8Djimachi) wards following [Tokyo City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_City)'s [transformation into Tokyo Metropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Autonomy_Act). The modern Chiyoda ward exhibits contrasting [Shitamachi and Yamanote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shitamachi_and_Yamanote) geographical and cultural divisions. The Kanda area is in the core of [Shitamachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shitamachi),[^cite_note-kokushishitamachi-5] the original commercial center of Edo-Tokyo. On the other hand, the western part of the Kōjimachi area typically represents a Yamanote district.

## History

Chiyoda has been a site of a number of historical events.

- In 1860, the assassination of [Ii Naosuke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ii_Naosuke) took place outside the Sakurada Gate of the Imperial Palace.

- In 1932, assassins attacked and killed prime minister [Inukai Tsuyoshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inukai_Tsuyoshi).

- In 1936, an attempted coup d'état, the [February 26 Incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_26_Incident), occurred.

- In 1960, Socialist Party leader [Inejirō Asanuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inejir%C5%8D_Asanuma) was assassinated in Hibiya Hall.

- In 1995, members of [Aum Shinrikyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aum_Shinrikyo) carried out the [Tokyo subway sarin attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_subway_sarin_attack).

## Geography

At the tip of [Musashino plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musashino_Terrace), Chiyoda is located at the very heart of former Tokyo City in eastern mainland Tokyo. The central area of the ward is furthermore occupied by the [Imperial Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C5%8Dkyo). The east side of the ward, bordering [Chūō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C5%AB%C5%8D,_Tokyo), is the location of [Tokyo Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Station). The south side, bordering [Minato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minato,_Tokyo), encompasses [Hibiya Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hibiya_Park) and the [National Diet Building](925262.md). It is almost exclusively occupied by administrations and agencies. The west and northwest are primarily upper class residential; the [Yasukuni Shrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasukuni_Shrine) is also there. The "high lantern" of Kudanzaka slope was not originally built as a lighthouse, but was built as a lantern for the Shrine. Originally steep and with steps, the slope was considerably softened during remediation after the Kanto earthquake.[^cite_note-6] To the north and northeast of the ward are several residential neighborhoods and the [Akihabara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akihabara) commercial district.

## Politics and government

### Local government

Chiyoda is run by a directly elected mayor and a city assembly of 25 elected members. The current [mayor](101146.md) is Takaaki Higuchi.

### Metropolitan representation

For the [Metropolitan Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metropolitan_Assembly), Chiyoda forms a single-member electoral district. It had been represented by Liberal Democrats for 50 years until the landslide [2009 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Tokyo_prefectural_election) when then 26-year-old Democratic newcomer [Zenkō Kurishita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zenko_Kurishita) unseated 70-year-old former Metropolitan Assembly president and six-term assemblyman, Liberal Democrat Shigeru Uchida.[^cite_note-7] In the [2013 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Tokyo_prefectural_election), no Democrat contested the seat and Uchida won back the district against a Communist and two independents.

The [Tokyo Fire Department](24023956.md) has its headquarters in [Ōtemachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Ctemachi) in Chiyoda.[^cite_note-8]

### National representation

For the national [House of Representatives](217778.md), Chiyoda, together with Minato and Shinjuku, forms the prefecture's [1st electoral district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_1st_district) since the electoral reform of the 1990s. The single-seat constituency is currently represented by [Liberal Democrat](164231.md) [Miki Yamada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miki_Yamada).

The ward is also home to the [National Diet](163847.md), the [Supreme Court of Japan](2251732.md) and the residence of the [Prime Minister of Japan](24833.md) and is the political nerve center of [Japan](15573.md).

## Cityscape

On December 31, 2001, Chiyoda had 6,572 buildings which were four stories or taller.[^cite_note-Profile-2]

Some of the districts in Chiyoda are actually not inhabited, either because they are parks (Hibiya Koen), because they consist only of office buildings (Otemachi or Marunouchi), and/or because they are extremely small. The area on the eastern side of [Akihabara Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akihabara_Station) is the location of several districts that cover at most a few buildings. [Kanda-Hanaokachō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanaokach%C5%8D,_Tokyo) is, for example, limited to the [Akihabara Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akihabara_Station) and the [Yodobashi Camera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yodobashi_Camera) store. Understanding the [address system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_addressing_system) in the Kanda area can be particularly troublesome for non-locals.

## Parks and recreation



Chidorigafuchi

East Imperial Garden, located on the eastern portion of the [Tokyo Imperial Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Imperial_Palace) grounds and housing the castle tower and the outer defense positions of the former [Edo Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_Castle), opened to the public in 1968. [Kitanomaru Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitanomaru_Park), located on Edo Castle's former northern section, has the [Tokyo National Museum of Modern Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_National_Museum_of_Modern_Art) and the Nippon Budokan, a venue for performances. Chidorigafuchi Boat Arena and Chidorigafuchi Moat Path includes a waterway for boats. National Diet Building Park, located adjacent to the Diet Building and divided in two by a street, includes American [dogwoods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dogwood) planted to symbolize the relations between the United States and Japan. Hibiya Park, Japan's first western-style park, includes restaurants, open-air concert halls, and tennis courts. Imperial Palace Outer Garden, in the front of Nijubashi Bridge, serves as a jogging trail and a tourist site.[^cite_note-10]

### Colleges and universities

[Hitotsubashi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitotsubashi_University)'s [Graduate School of International Corporate Strategy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_School_of_International_Corporate_Strategy) is located in the National Center of Sciences in [Hitotsubashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitotsubashi,_Chiyoda). Both of the [Sophia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophia_University) Campuses are in western Chiyoda. The main Yotsuya campus lies adjacent to [Yotsuya Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yotsuya_Station) and the Ichigaya Campus just south of [Ichigaya Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ichigaya_Station).

[Globis University Graduate School of Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Globis_University_Graduate_School_of_Management) which is the largest business school in Japan is also located in Chiyoda. [Hosei University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hosei_University), [Meiji University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_University), [Senshu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senshu_University), [Nihon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nihon_University) and so on are located in the area.

### Libraries

Chiyoda operates four public libraries; they are the Chiyoda Library, Yobancho Library, Shohei Library, and Kanda Library. Tokyo operates the [Tokyo Metropolitan Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metropolitan_Library) Hibiya Library. The Japanese government operates the [National Diet Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Diet_Library) and the [National Archives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Archives_\(Japan\)?action=edit\&redlink=1). Ishikawa Enterprise Foundation Ochanomizu Library is a nearby private library.[^cite_note-101]
