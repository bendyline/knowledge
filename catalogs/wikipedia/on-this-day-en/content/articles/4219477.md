The **Guyana Defence Force** (**GDF**) is the armed forces of [Guyana](17238691.md), established in 1965. It has military bases across the nation.[^cite_note-11] The Commander-in-Chief of the Defence Force is always the incumbent [President of Guyana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Guyana).[^cite_note-\:0-12] Service Branches include the Army, Air Corps, and Coast Guard.[^cite_note-13]

Although a small nation, Guyana's [recent petro wealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleum_industry_in_Guyana) has destabilized the region, re-igniting [border disputes](6896458.md) with [Venezuela](32374.md) inherited from the colonial era.

## History

The nation of Guyana became independent in the 1960s, before that defense was the responsibility of the United Kingdom. A modest defense force for small country, is back-dropped by alliances with major regional players including the United States, the United Kingdom, and Brazil. Guyanese forces participated in the multinational 1994 mission to Haiti.

### Twentieth century

The GDF was formed on 1 November 1965. Members of the new Defence Force were drawn from the British Guiana Volunteer Force (BGVF), Special Service Unit (SSU), British Guiana Police Force (BGPF) and civilians. Training assistance was provided by [British](31717.md) instructors.

A survey party sent by the Surinamese government, which at the time was still under [Dutch colonial rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suriname_\(Kingdom_of_the_Netherlands\)), was sent to the [Tigri Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tigri_Area) in late 1967 as part of Suriname's "[Operation Grasshopper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Grasshopper)", prompting the GDF to confiscate their weapons and expel them from the territory on December 12.[^cite_note-\:2-14]

On January 2, 1969, the [Rupununi uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupununi_uprising) by native [Pemon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pemon) and [Wapishana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wapishana) led by [Valerie Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valerie_Hart), with reported support from [Venezuela](32374.md),[^cite_note-PopMech-8] began with an attack against [Lethem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethem,_Guyana), the capital of the [Upper Takutu-Upper Essequibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Takutu-Upper_Essequibo) region, resulting in the deaths of five police officers and two civilians and the destruction of Guyanese government buildings.[^cite_note-\:423-15] The rebels locked townsfolk in their homes, blocked airstrips in the area, and took Guyanese government officials into their custody.[^cite_note-\:032-16][^cite_note-\:423-15] Guyanese forces mobilized from the nearest unblocked airstrip and in two days the insurrection was quelled, with many rebels fleeing into [Venezuela](32374.md) and [Brazil](3383.md), with Hart fleeing to the former.[^cite_note-\:032-16][^cite_note-SRL-17] Accusations of massacres by Guyanese forces of native Amerindians have been put forth, which the Guyanese government denied and the Guyanese government accused the rebels of being backed by Venezuela and attempting to cede themselves to Venezuela.[^cite_note-\:14-18][^cite_note-\:423-15]



Omar Khan, Chief of Staff of the GDF

In mid–August 1969, patrolling GDF forces discovered a Surinamese camp and partially completed airstrip in the Tigri Area. This discovery prompted the GDF to launch an operation on August 19, 1969 against the camp and airstrip, resulting in the expulsion of Surinamese forces from the area.[^cite_note-\:2-14]

A platoon of GDF soldiers, part of a contingent of [Caribbean Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caribbean_Community) troops, were involved in the 1994 US-led intervention [Operation Uphold Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Uphold_Democracy) in [Haiti](13373.md).[^cite_note-\:04-19]

### Twenty-first century



Guyanese Defense Force unload supplies from a helicopter with international partners, as part of the mission Continuing Promise in 2008

On 28 November 2023, the U.S. Embassy in Guyana announced that the United States military ([1st SFAB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Security_Force_Assistance_Brigade)) and the Guyana Defence Force would strengthen their military partnership.[^cite_note-20] This occurred during the [2023 Guyana–Venezuela crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Guyana%E2%80%93Venezuela_crisis) when its neighboring country, Venezuela, announced the annexation of the majority of Guyana's western territories via the [2023 Venezuelan referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Venezuelan_referendum).[^cite_note-21] The GDF is multiple factors smaller than the [National Bolivarian Armed Forces of Venezuela](32460.md) and would have great difficulty defending itself from Venezuela without foreign support.[^cite_note-PopMech-8]

In the 2020s a major shift took place, the percent of GDP of defense decreased but there was a big increase in overall budget due to changes GDP.[^cite_note-auto-10] From 1990 to 2020, non-inflation adjusted currency the defense increased went from 142 thousand to 16 billion.[^cite_note-auto-10]

In early 2024, a plan was announced to procure more helicopters, a maritime defense vessel, and a drone.[^cite_note-auto1-22]

## Roles and functions



Guyanese soldier at the Colombian National Training Center

Three main roles exist for the GDF:

- Defend the [territorial integrity](31335.md) of Guyana.

- Assist the civil power in the maintenance of law and order when required to do so.

- Contribute to the economic development of Guyana.

The GDF is an integral part of the Guyanese nation. Resources and equipment of the GDF are used to help other Guyanese; examples include medical mercy flights and the construction of roads and airstrips by the Engineering Corps.

Enlistment into the force is [voluntary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volunteering) for [officers](36301328.md) and [soldiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soldier). Basic training is done within GDF training schools, which has also trained officers and soldiers from [Commonwealth Caribbean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Caribbean) territories. However, officers are trained at one of two British officer training schools: [Royal Military Academy Sandhurst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Military_Academy_Sandhurst) (Infantry Training) and [Britannia Royal Naval College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britannia_Royal_Naval_College) (Coast Guard Training).

The training and skills gained by the members of the GDF have been used when they move either into civilian life or into the sister military organizations, the [Guyana National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guyana_National_Reserve?action=edit\&redlink=1) (now the Second Infantry Battalion Group Reserve) and formerly the [Guyana National Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guyana_National_Service) (disbanded in 2000) and the Guyana People's Militia.

### 1st Infantry Battalion Group

In the 1980s, the Guyana Defence Force manned the Amawai, Roraima, Ireng, and Takutu sectors, while the Kutari Sector was manned by the then Guyana National Service. In 1988, these GDF battalions were merged to create the 1st Infantry Battalion, later known as the 1st Infantry Battalion Group. At that time, the Force had more resources, including human, financial, and materiel, compared to its current state. However, today, the amalgamated battalion faces significant challenges as it is now required to carry out the tasks previously handled by multiple units. This poses a considerable challenge, especially given the changing dynamics in the global environment, which are impacting the nation's security.[^cite_note-\:1-23]

### 31 Special Forces Squadron

The 31 Special Forces Squadron is a [special forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_forces) group that is specialized in [airborne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airborne_forces), [jungle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jungle_warfare) and river warfare operations.[^cite_note-PopMech-8]

### GDF Band Corps

The [Guyana Defence Force Band Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guyana_Defence_Force_Band_Corps) is the official musical unit of the GDF whose role is to provide musical accompaniment for ceremonial functions of the GDF.[^cite_note-24] The members were drawn from the Rifle Companies and the defunct Volunteer Force and were brought out during regimental military parades. The Guyana Defence Force Steel Band would soon follow the main band's lead after its own establishment in 1970, three years after the original band was founded.[^cite_note-25]

### Medical Corps

The Medical Corps provides medical and dental care to all the members of the GDF and their immediate families. It often liaises with the Health ministry for medical procedures and protocol to be carried out effectively. The corps is situated in Base Camp Ayanganna which includes facilities such as a medical laboratory and a dental lab.[^cite_note-26]

## Equipment

Much of the equipment belonging to Guyana are [Cold War era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_War_era) weapons, with some [World War II](32927.md) armaments still supplied.[^cite_note-PopMech-8] Guyana is a member of the Caribbean Basin Security Initiative. It maintains strong military relations with [Brazil](3383.md), with which it collaborates on border security through yearly regional military exchange gatherings. Moreover, the nation has bilateral pacts with [China](5405.md), [France](5843419.md), and the [USA](3434750.md). The GDF engages in regular training and participates in bilateral activities. Equipment is mostly composed of ex-Soviet and British weapons and vehicles. The air force has enhanced its limited air-transport capabilities with second-hand aircraft primarily from Brazilian and North American origins. With the exception of maintenance facilities, a defence-industrial sector does not exist.[^cite_note-IISS2023-27]

## Aircraft inventory



Two new Dornier-HAL 228 were delivered in 2024 from India (type of aircraft pictured).

The Defence force air wing was formed in 1968 and was then renamed the Guyana Defence force air command in 1973. Seven [Britten-Norman BN-2A Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britten-Norman_BN-2_Islander) were delivered over a five-year period in the 1970s and then were supplemented by [Short Skyvans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_SC.7_Skyvan) series 3Ms in 1979. In 1986 3 Mil Mi-8 were delivered. The GDF currently operates 8 [fixed-wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixed-wing_aircraft) aircraft and 4 [helicopters](8286923.md). Recently, the GDF had ordered two [Dornier Do-228](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dornier_228) aircraft from Indian Manufacturer [Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan_Aeronautics_Limited), which were delivered on 1 April 2024.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

In early 2024, a plan was announced to among other things to buy some new helicopters.[^cite_note-auto1-22] One crashed in 2023, which, because only a few helicopters are operated represented a significant loss.

## Coast Guard
