**[Badminton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badminton_at_the_Asian_Games)** at the **[2026 Asian Games](51790406.md)** is held between 20 and 29 September at [Ichinomiya City Municipal Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ichinomiya_City_Municipal_Gymnasium?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] This will be the [sport's consecutive seventeenth appearance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badminton_at_the_Asian_Games) as an official sport at the [Games](39205.md), since the [1962 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badminton_at_the_1962_Asian_Games).
