**Mir Ali** or **Mirali** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): میرعلي) is a town in [North Waziristan District](1131187.md), in Pakistan's [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) province. Mirali is located in the [Tochi Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tochi_Valley), about 12 kilometres (7.5 mi) east of [Miramshah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miramshah) (capital of North Waziristan), 40 kilometres (25 mi) west of [Bannu](3769844.md), [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md), and 70 kilometres (43 mi) southeast of the city of [Khost](400068.md), Afghanistan. Mirali is at an altitude of 674 metres (2,211 ft).[^cite_note-2]

The residents of Mirali are the [Dawars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawar_\(Pashtun_tribe\)) and the [Utmanzai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utmanzai_\(Wazir_clan\)) [Wazirs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wazir_\(Pashtun_tribe\)). The Wazirs reside in the areas of North Waziristan such as Spinwam, Shawa, and Khiasur, while the Dawars reside in the plains on both sides of the [Tochi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tochi_River). Some well-known villages of the Dawars in Mirali area are Hassu Khel, Haider Khel, Mussaki, Idaak, Khaddi, Hurmaz, Zeraki, Hakim Khel and Daulat Khel.

## History

The famous [Pashtun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtun) freedom fighter and tribal leader [Mirzali Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirzali_Khan) (the Faqir of Ipi) based his movement in Ipi, a village on the outskirts of Mirali, for more than 10 years. In 1938, Mirzali Khan shifted from Ipi to [Gurwek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurwek), [Waziristan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waziristan).

[Abu Yahya al-Libi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Yahya_al-Libi), the number two in [Al-Qaeda](1921.md) at the time, was killed by a [drone strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drone_strikes_in_Pakistan) carried out by the [United States](3434750.md) on June 4, 2012 in Mirali.[^cite_note-3]

On December 30, 2021, a [gunfight](2296086.md) during Pakistani troops' raid against a hideout of [Tehrik-i-Taliban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehrik-i-Taliban) terrorists in the city killed four people.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

On August 11, 2024, heavy exchange of fire between [Pakistan Army](1965925.md) and the militant insurgents of the outlawed [Pakistani Taliban](18953699.md) was reported.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
