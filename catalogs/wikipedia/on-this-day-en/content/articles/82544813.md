The [medal table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_medal_table) of the [2026 Winter Paralympics](41045653.md) ranks the participating [National Paralympic Committees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Paralympic_Committee) (NPCs) by the number of gold medals that were won by their athletes during the competition. The 2026 Paralympics were the fourteenth [Games](18670284.md) held, a quadrennial competition open to athletes with [physical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_disability) and [intellectual disabilities](18567040.md). The games were held in [Milan](36511.md), [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md), and other areas in [Lombardy](43807.md) and [Northeast Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_Italy) from 6 to 15 March 2026. There were 79 medal events. A [mixed doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_doubles) event made its debut in [wheelchair curling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheelchair_curling).[^cite_note-1]

[Brazil](82569844.md) and [Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvia_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics) both won the first-ever Winter Paralympic medal in their histories, with [Cristian Ribera](70214846.md) winning the silver medal in the [Men's sitting cross-country skiing sprint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_cross-country_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_sprint_classical), and [Poļina Rožkova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Po%C4%BCina_Ro%C5%BEkova) and [Agris Lasmans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agris_Lasmans) winning the bronze in the [mixed doubles curling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheelchair_curling_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics) respectively.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

Ribera's medal was also the first in Winter Paralympic history won by a tropical, [Latin American](18524.md) or [South American](26769.md) [National Paralympic Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Paralympic_Committee).

## Medals

On 15 July 2025, the Milano Cortina 2026 Olympic and Paralympic Organising Committee unveiled the designs of the medals. The reverse of each medal represents [Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braille) on the left and the Games logo on the right, with the Paralympics logo on the obverse. Each medal is 80mm wide and 10mm thick.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] They were created by the [Istituto Poligrafico e Zecca dello Stato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Poligrafico_e_Zecca_dello_Stato) (IPZS).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
