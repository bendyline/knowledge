The **Khan Yunis Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة خان يونس, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Muḥāfaẓat Ḫān Yūnis*) is one of 16 [Governorates of Palestine](5811262.md), located in the southern [Gaza Strip](12047.md). Its district capital is [Khan Yunis](101330.md). According to the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics), the governorate had a population of 426,056 in mid-2022.[^cite_note-2] Its land area is 69.61% urban, 12.8% rural, and 17.57% comprising the [Khan Yunis refugee camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khan_Yunis_Camp).[^cite_note-3]
