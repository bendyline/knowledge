# List of NBA game sevens



Since its opening in 1995, twelve game sevens have been played at the [TD Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TD_Garden), formerly known as FleetCenter and TD Banknorth Garden.

A concrete building with the words "Great Western Forum" on top situates across an empty parking lot

[The Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Forum_\(Inglewood,_California\)) in [Inglewood, California](107640.md) hosted eight game sevens when it was the home of the Los Angeles Lakers.

In the [National Basketball Association](22093.md) (NBA), a game seven is the final game of a [best-of-seven](2847115.md) series in the [NBA playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_playoffs). Based on the playoffs format arrangement,<sup>[\[a\]](#endnote_Note1a)</sup> it is played in the [venue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena) of the team holding [home-court advantage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_advantage) for the series. The necessity of a game seven is not known until the outcome of game six is determined, assuming that a series reaches that far. In other words, game seven is the only one in a series that is not guaranteed more than one game in advance. Due to the decisive nature of game sevens, they are often "played more conservatively" and receive more media and fan attention.[^cite_note-1]

The [NBA Finals](890793.md) has always employed a best-of-seven series format. The [Conference (or Division) Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Conference_finals) have used this format since [1958](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958_NBA_playoffs);[^cite_note-2] the Conference or Division semifinals since [1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_NBA_playoffs);[^cite_note-3] and the First Round since [2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_NBA_playoffs).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] During the [1947](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1947_BAA_Playoffs) and [1948](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_BAA_Playoffs) playoffs, in which teams from different divisions met each other in the opening rounds, the first-place teams from each division played a best-of-seven series against each other before one advanced to the league finals. The other teams in the playoffs competed against each other in three best-of-three series to determine the other spot in the finals.[^cite_note-6]

Since the inception of the NBA, 160 game sevens have been played. Of those, only six went into overtime, and one into double overtime. 43 game sevens have been won by the road team.[^cite_note-7] Every active NBA franchise has played in at least one game seven. There have been thirteen NBA playoff seasons in which no game seven was played: [1947](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1947_BAA_Playoffs), [1949](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1949_BAA_Playoffs), [1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_NBA_playoffs), [1953](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1953_NBA_playoffs), [1956](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_NBA_playoffs), [1958](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958_NBA_playoffs), [1967](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967_NBA_playoffs), [1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_NBA_playoffs), [1983](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_NBA_playoffs), [1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_NBA_playoffs), [1989](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_NBA_playoffs), [1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_NBA_playoffs), and [1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_NBA_playoffs). The [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_NBA_playoffs), [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_NBA_playoffs), [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_NBA_playoffs), and [2026 postseasons](79524481.md) hold the record for most game sevens played, with five. In [1979](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_NBA_playoffs), [1981](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981_NBA_playoffs), [1988](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_NBA_playoffs), [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_NBA_playoffs), [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_NBA_playoffs), [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_NBA_playoffs), [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_NBA_playoffs), [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_NBA_playoffs), [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_NBA_playoffs), and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NBA_playoffs), four game sevens were played. The [Los Angeles Lakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Lakers) and [New York Knicks](72855.md) both hold the record for most game sevens played in a single postseason, having played three game sevens in 1988 and 1994, respectively, the maximum possible at that time.

## Notes

- [**<sup>a</sup>**](#ref_Note1a) The home-and-away format in every round of the playoffs is the 2–2–1–1–1 format (the team with the better regular season record plays on their home court in games one, two, five and seven). The format of the finals from 1985 to 2013 used the 2–3–2 format (the team with the better regular season record plays on their home court in games one, two, six and seven).

  [^cite_note-141]

  &#x20;In the past, there have been several other formats used in the playoffs.

- [**<sup>b</sup>**](#ref_Note2b) Won as the designated road team at a neutral-site game.
