The **National Assembly** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Assemblée nationale*) is the [unicameral house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicameral_house) of the [Parliament of the Central African Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_the_Central_African_Republic). Members are elected in single-member [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency) using the two-round (or [run-off](25932.md)) system. Members serve seven-year terms.[^cite_note-1]

## Legislative history

The first legislative arm was established in January 1947, called [Representative Council of Ubangi-Shari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubangi-Shari). It was replaced by the 50-member Territorial Assembly of Ubangi-Shari in April 1952. Constituent Legislative Assembly was established in December 1958. The 50-member Legislative Assembly of the CAR (*Assemblée législative de la RCA*) began its functions in February 1959. It was replaced by National Assembly on 12 December 1960.[^cite_note-histdic-2]

[Jean-Bédel Bokassa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-B%C3%A9del_Bokassa) dissolved the National Assembly in 1966 and imprisoned a number of its deputies. The constitution of [Central African Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_African_Empire) in 1976 outlined the National Assembly with elections planned in 1979, however, Bokassa was overthrown before the elections took place.[^cite_note-histdic-2]

The legislature of the Central African Republic was previously (at least as of 1990)[^cite_note-3] a bicameral institution known as Congress, of which the National Assembly was the lower house; the upper house was called the Economic and Regional Council (*Conseil Economique et Regional*).

In 2013, it was planned to dissolve the National Assembly by Jan 11, 2014 and hold new legislative elections, according to a ceasefire agreement signed between the government and the [Seleka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seleka) rebel coalition on Jan 11, 2013 in [Libreville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libreville), [Gabon](12027.md). According to the agreement, a national unity government will be formed and a prime minister will be chosen from the opposition parties.[^cite_note-4]

The National Assembly formed following [elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Central_African_Republic_general_election) held on 13 March and 8 May 2005, and had a total of 105 members.

The National Assembly is the lower house of the [Parliament of the Central African Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_the_Central_African_Republic) since the ratification of the [Constitution of the Central African Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Central_African_Republic) on 27 March 2016.

The Senate was abolished upon the adoption of the [2023 constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Central_African_Republic), and the National Assembly became the sole chamber of the unicameral legislature.
