The **Iberville Parish Courthouse** is a historic building located at 57735 Main Street in [Plaquemine, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaquemine,_Louisiana).&#x20;

Built in 1848 by George and Thomas Weldon, of Natchez, it served as a [courthouse](58609.md) until 1906. It served as **Plaquemine City Hall** from 1906 until 1985,[^cite_note-ladoc-2][^cite_note-nrhpdoc-3] and was and later restored for its present use as Iberville Museum.&#x20;

It is a stuccoed brick building in [Greek Revival architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_Revival_architecture) that is five bays wide with a central, [pedimented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedimented) [portico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portico) of four [Doric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doric_order) columns.[^cite_note-ladoc-2][^cite_note-nrhpdoc-3]&#x20;

The building was added to the [National Register of Historic Places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places) on May 31, 1980.[^cite_note-nris-1]&#x20;

On October 7, 2025, 27-year-old Latrell Clark opened fire inside of the courthouse during booking process for a sexual assault investigation, killing a deputy and injuring a detective, before being fatally shot at the scene. Clark resisted arrest and attempted to disarm the detective, causing several shots to be fired. Clark and the deputy were transported to hospital where they were pronounced dead while the detective was in critical but stable condition.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]&#x20;
