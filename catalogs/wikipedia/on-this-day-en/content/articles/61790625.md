**Fuerte Tiuna** or **Fort Tiuna** (more formally the **Military Complex of Fort Tiuna**) is a military installation in [Caracas](55289.md), Venezuela.

## Fort

There are important institutions[^cite_note-1] of various types, such as the headquarters of the [Ministry of Popular Power for Defense](3003969.md), the Escuela de Formación de Oficiales de las Fuerzas Armadas de Cooperación (EFOFAC), The General Command of the Army, El Libertador Shooting Range, the Army Food Center, the [Caracas Military Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caracas_Military_Circle), [Paseo Los Próceres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paseo_Los_Pr%C3%B3ceres), the Bolívar Battalion, the Ayala Battalion, [La Viñeta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Vi%C3%B1eta?action=edit\&redlink=1), which is the official residence of the [vice president](23535071.md),[^cite_note-ElTi-2] and some units of the [Venezuelan Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Academy_of_the_Bolivarian_Army).

It is located between [Coche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coche_Parish) and [El Valle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Valle_Parish) parishes, both south of the [Libertador Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libertador_Bolivarian_Municipality) and southwest of the [Caracas Metropolitan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caracas_Metropolitan_District), to the north center of Venezuela.

It houses military structures and sports, urban, cultural, financial spaces.[^cite_note-3] The Tiuna City complex is thousands of homes built by the Ministry of Habitat and Housing and Residences, and assigned to military personnel.

It is bordered largely by the [Regional Highway of the Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Highway_of_the_Center?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the [Valle Coche Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valle_Coche_Highway?action=edit\&redlink=1).

It was struck in the [2026 United States intervention in Venezuela](82021123.md) among other targets, such as [Generalissimo Francisco de Miranda Air Base](6558466.md).[^cite_note-TuTo-4] The strike prompted an evacuation of the base.[^cite_note-ElTi-2] Both then-[president](3329692.md) [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md) and then-[first lady](17596329.md) [Cilia Flores](9530834.md) were captured by the [US military](32212.md)'s [Delta Force](328809.md) at the facility. They were extracted from the country and taken to [USS Iwo Jima](583918.md), before being transported to [Guantanamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guantanamo_Bay_Naval_Base), before finally being taken by plane to [New York City](645042.md) to face trial.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]
