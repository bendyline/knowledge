Main article: [Swimming at the 2026 Asian Games](84060195.md)

The **men's 200 metre breaststroke** event at the [2026 Asian Games](51790406.md) took place on 24 September 2026 at the [Tokyo Aquatics Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Aquatics_Centre).[^cite_note-1] Japan's Shin Ohashi won the gold medal in a world-record time of 2\:04.83. Ohashi also lowered his own world junior record of 2\:06.59.[^cite_note-2]
