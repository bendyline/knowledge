**Guye Adola Idemo** (born 20 October 1990) is an Ethiopian [long-distance runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-distance_runner) who specialises in the [half marathon](1019031.md).

## Biography

Born in [Adola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adola) in Ethiopia's [Oromiya Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oromiya_Region), he began training with [Gianni Demadonna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gianni_Demadonna)'s group and came to prominence in 2014.[^cite_note-2] His first high level race was the [Marrakech Half Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marrakech_Half_Marathon?action=edit\&redlink=1) in January that year, which he won in a best of 61\:26 minutes.[^cite_note-3] He then placed fourth at the Ethiopian half marathon championships.[^cite_note-4]

His international debut shortly followed and he was Ethiopia's best performer at the [2014 IAAF World Half Marathon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_IAAF_World_Half_Marathon_Championships), taking the individual bronze medal in a personal best of 59\:21 minutes to lead the Ethiopian men to third in the team competition.[^cite_note-5] Following his first major medals, he ran on the circuit and took third at the [Giro Media Blenio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giro_Media_Blenio) in Switzerland and the [Luanda Half Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luanda_Half_Marathon?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Angola.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] He won the 2014 [Delhi Half Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Half_Marathon) in New Delhi with his personal best of 59\:06.[^cite_note-8]

In his marathon debut in the [2017 Berlin Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Berlin_Marathon) he challenged [Eliud Kipchoge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliud_Kipchoge) and came second with a finishing time of 2\:03\:46, the fastest marathon-debut ever at that time.

In 2020, he competed in the men's race at the [2020 World Athletics Half Marathon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_World_Athletics_Half_Marathon_Championships) held in Gdynia, Poland.[^cite_note-men_half_marathon_championships_2020-9]

In 2021, he won the men's race at the [2021 Berlin Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Berlin_Marathon).[^cite_note-adola_gebreslase_win_berlin_marathon_as_bekele_fails_to_threaten_world_record_2021-10]

In 2026, he won the men's race at the 2026 [Rotterdam Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotterdam_Marathon) in 2\:03\:54,[^cite_note-11] and the men's race at the [2026 Berlin Marathon](84342841.md) in 2\:02\:50, a personal best.[^cite_note-wa-27sep2026-12]
