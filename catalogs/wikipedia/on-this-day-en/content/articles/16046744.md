**Capão da Canoa** is a municipality in the state of [Rio Grande do Sul](256268.md), Brazil. With miles of beaches, Capão da Canoa is a popular beach resort for residents of nearby [Porto Alegre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto_Alegre).

## History

A small plane crashed into a closed restaurant in the municipality on 3 April 2026, killing all four people aboard the plane.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
