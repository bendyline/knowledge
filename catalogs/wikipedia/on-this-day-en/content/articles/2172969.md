**Shariff Aguak**, officially the **Municipality of Shariff Aguak** ([Maguindanaoan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_language): *Kuta Shariff Aguak*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Maguindanao del Sur](67871248.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it had a population of 29,707.[^cite_note-5]

It was formerly officially known as **Maganoy**.[^cite_note-6]

Despite only being the *de jure* (by law) [seat of Maguindanao's provincial government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao#Provincial_capital) from 1973 to 1977, the town—being home to several previous governors—has served as the *de facto* (by practice) capital during the governorships of Sandiale Sambolawan (1980–1986), [Andal Ampatuan, Sr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andal_Ampatuan,_Sr.) (2001–2008) and [Sajid Ampatuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sajid_Ampatuan) (2008–2009).

## History

Shariff Aguak was founded as **Maganoy** in September 11, 1963, when President [Diosdado Macapagal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diosdado_Macapagal) signed Executive Order No. 47 stipulating the creation of the municipality within the old province of [Cotabato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotabato_\(historical_province\)).[^cite_note-maganoy1963-7] It was created from the southern 28 barangays of [Datu Piang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datu_Piang).

The town's name was changed from *Maganoy* to *Shariff Aguak* by virtue of *Muslim Mindanao Autonomy Act No. 45* in 1996.[^cite_note-RLA-ARMMGovPH-8]

On July 30, 2009, upon the ratification of Muslim Mindanao Autonomy Acts No. 225 (as amended by MMAA 252) and MMAA 220, the municipalities of [Shariff Saydona Mustapha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shariff_Saydona_Mustapha) and [Datu Hoffer Ampatuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datu_Hoffer_Ampatuan), respectively, were created from a total of 13 barangays of the 26 Shariff Aguak, in addition to other barangays from [Datu Piang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datu_Piang), [Datu Saudi Ampatuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datu_Saudi_Ampatuan), [Datu Unsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datu_Unsay) and [Mamasapano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamasapano).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

Shariff Aguak (then Maganoy) was Maguindanao's provincial capital from its creation in 1973 to 1977. In 1977, [President](271126.md) [Ferdinand Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Marcos) moved the province's seat of government to the municipality of [Sultan Kudarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Kudarat,_Maguindanao_del_Norte) by virtue of Presidential Decree No. 1170. Batas Pambansa Blg. 211, enacted in 1982, aimed to formally restore the status of Maganoy as Maguindanao's seat of provincial government[^cite_note-bp211-11] but the plebiscite scheduled for December 18, 1982[^cite_note-proc2245-12] was never administered,[^cite_note-philstar2-13] thereby making the law not legally binding. However, the town has served as the *de facto* capital during the tenure of previous governors who hails from the town. By law (*de jure*), Sultan Kudarat was historically recognized as the capital of Maguindanao from 1977 until 2014 when the Sangguniang Panlalawigan of Maguindanao approved a resolution formally recognizing [Buluan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buluan) as the new provincial capital.[^cite_note-mb-14] In 2019 incumbent Governor Bai Mariam Mangudadatu expressed plans to move the provincial capitol back to Shariff Aguak.[^cite_note-15]

Pending the completion of the new provincial capitol complex at Buluan, the executive branch of provincial government holds offices in that town's Rajah Buayan Silongan Peace Center. On the other hand, the legislative branch of provincial government, the Sangguniang Panlalawigan of Maguindanao, continues to hold sessions in the rehabilitated buildings of the old provincial capitol in Barangay Simuay Crossing in the town of Sultan Kudarat.[^cite_note-mindanews-16] The [Ampatuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ampatuan)-built [former provincial capitol complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_Provincial_Capitol_\(Shariff_Aguak\)) in Shariff Aguak, initially planned to be converted for public school use,[^cite_note-17] is set to become the new regional headquarters of ARMM's Bureau of Fire Protection but eventually became an infantry brigade of the Philippine Army.[^cite_note-18]

On January 25, 2026, there was an attempted assassination and ambush against the mayor of Shariff Aguak, Akmad B. Ampatuan. The armored vehicle he was riding in was attacked using an [RPG-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RPG-2) (B-40) anti-tank weapon and an automatic rifle. He managed to survive although two of his bodyguards were injured, while the three attackers died on the spot.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20]
