**IRIS *Jamaran*** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): جماران) was the [lead ship](855210.md) of the [Iranian](14653.md) [*Moudge*-class](10946960.md) [frigate](11464.md)[^cite_note-Janes_1-6][^cite_note-globalsecurity.org-7][^cite_note-naval_institute-8] launched in early 2010 in [Bandar-e-Abbas](464348.md), Iran. Iran stated that the design and building of *Jamaran* was among the greatest achievements of the [Iranian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Navy) and [IRGC Navy](4676345.md) and the ship's launch marked a major technological leap for Iran's naval industries.[^cite_note-9] The ship was designed for a crew of 140.[^cite_note-10] *Jamaran* combined anti-submarine assets with other systems of weapons capable of dealing with surface and air threats as well.&#x20;

*Jamaran* was sunk on 28 February 2026 by a U.S. airstrike while in port.[^cite_note-11]

## Description

### Weapons systems

See also: [List of military equipment manufactured in Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_military_equipment_manufactured_in_Iran)

The primary weapon deployed by *Moudge*-class vessels is the [Sikorsky SH-3 Sea King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_SH-3_Sea_King), which acts in concert with shipboard sensors to seek out and destroy submarines at long range. The *Moudge* class also carries a close-in anti-submarine torpedo system, a 324 mm (13 in) light torpedo with a 30 kilometres (19 mi) range, mounted in triple torpedo launchers on either side of the stern.

To deal with surface forces, the vessel is equipped with four [Noor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noor_\(missile\)) / [C-802](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-802) surface-to-surface anti-ship cruise missiles, mounted in box launchers on the roof of the upper deck level between the radar and the main mast pointing towards either sides.[^cite_note-fnc-12] The single shot hit probability of the Noor, with a range of 170 km (110 mi), is estimated to be as high as 98%.[^cite_note-C-802GS-13]

For anti-aircraft self-defense, the *Moudge* class is equipped with four medium range Fajr surface-to-air missiles (reversed engineered from the [RIM-66/SM-1 standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIM-66_Standard) missile)[^cite_note-SM1-14] with 74 km (46 mi) range, and 24.4 km (15.2 mi) flight ceiling, mounted in box launchers at the deck above the main deck level in front of the helicopter landing pad. The Moudge class also carries two 20 mm gunner-operated [Oerlikon cannons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oerlikon_20_mm_cannon) and a 40 mm Fateh-40 autocannon (reverse engineered from [Bofors L/70](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bofors_40_mm_Automatic_Gun_L/70)) with 12.5 km (7.8 mi) aerial range, to provide a shipboard point-defense against incoming anti-ship missiles and aircraft.[^cite_note-payvand-15]

The main gun on the [forecastle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forecastle) of the *Moudge* class is a 76 mm (3 in) [Fajr-27](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fajr-27) gun.[^cite_note-globalsecurity.org-7] The gun is capable of firing at a rate of 85 rounds per minute at a range of more than 17 km (11 mi) towards surface targets and 12 km (7.5 mi) towards aerial targets. The Fajr-27 is a multi-purpose weapon, capable of dealing with surface, air, and onshore targets.

### Countermeasures

*Jamaran* possessed chaff and flare systems and electronic warfare capabilities.[^cite_note-16]

### Sensors and equipment

The ship was equipped with one [Asr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asr_\(radar\)) passive electronically scanned array long-range radar for air and surface search and tracking, installed on the roof of forward of the funnel.[^cite_note-naval_institute-8] The ship was also fitted with two navigation radars on the mainmast. The ship was equipped with one fire control radar.

### Propulsion

The *Moudge*-class vessels are powered by two 7,500 kW (10,000 hp) engines, and uses four diesel generators which each generate 550 kW (740 hp). The *Moudge* class can reach a maximum speed of 30 [kn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (56 km/h; 35 mph).

### Helicopter landing platform

*Jamaran* could accommodate a medium-sized [helicopter](8286923.md), and could also perform helicopter in-flight refueling (HIFR) operations.[^cite_note-17]

## Service history

The frigate was involved in a deadly friendly fire incident in the [Gulf of Oman](12896.md) on 10 May 2020, resulting in the loss of at least 19 Iranian sailors. According to multiple media reports, during a naval exercise near the southern city of [Jask](19973566.md), south of the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md), *Jamaran* mistakenly launched a missile at the support vessel [*Konarak*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRIS_Konarak). The support ship sustained significant damage, leading to the casualties and injuries of numerous crew members.[^cite_note-18]

On 1 September 2022, *Jamaran* took two [Saildrone Explorers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saildrone_\(company\)#Vehicles) that were property of the [United States Navy](20518076.md). According to a statement by the [United States Fifth Fleet](418867.md), two nearby U.S. Navy guided-missile destroyers, [USS *Nitze*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Nitze) and [USS *Delbert D. Black*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Delbert_D._Black), responded quickly. According to a U.S. defense official who spoke on condition of anonymity, *Nitze* sent a [MH-60R Sea Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MH-60R_Sea_Hawk) helicopter ahead to the Iranian ship as the destroyers made their way to intercept. The helicopter crew spotted the Saildrones on the ship's deck, but as they approached, the Iranian crew began to throw tarps on top of them "to try to hide the fact that they had seized these unmanned vessels." The two destroyers arrived and stayed in the area, speaking with the Iranian ship "to deescalate the situation and recover the seized Saildrones", according to the statement. The Iranians eventually said that they had been told by their headquarters to return the vessels, the defense official said, but they wanted to wait until the sun came up. The drones were released at 8\:00 am local time Friday morning.[^cite_note-19]

In the wake of [rising tensions between Iran and Israel in April 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Iran%E2%80%93Israel_conflict), Iran announced the frigate would be convoying Iranian commercial traffic through the [Red Sea](26191.md).[^cite_note-20]

### Sinking

On 28 February 2026, the opening day of hostilities in the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), *Jamaran* was struck by United States forces at [Chabahar Port](15313649.md), set ablaze, and sunk.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]
