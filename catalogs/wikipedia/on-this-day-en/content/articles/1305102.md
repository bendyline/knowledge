**Brazil's Legal Amazon** ([abbreviated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbreviated) **BLA**),[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] in [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language) **Amazônia Legal** (Brazilian Portuguese: [\[amaˈzonjɐ leˈɡaw\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Portuguese)), is the largest [socio-geographic division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_socio-geographic_division) in [Brazil](3383.md), containing all nine states in the [Amazon basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_basin). The government designated this region in 1948 based on its studies on how to plan the economic and social development of the Amazon region.

## Area

The official designation Amazônia Legal encompasses all seven Brazilian states of the [North Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Region,_Brazil) ([Acre](229497.md), [Amapá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amap%C3%A1), [Amazonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonas_State,_Brazil), [Pará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par%C3%A1), [Rondônia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rond%C3%B4nia), [Roraima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roraima) and [Tocantins](48764.md)), as well as most of [Mato Grosso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mato_Grosso) in the [Center-West Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center-West_Region,_Brazil) and the western part of [Maranhão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maranh%C3%A3o) in the [Northeast Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_Region,_Brazil).[^cite_note-3] Amazônia Legal is a 5,016,136.3 km<sup>2</sup> region with around 24 million inhabitants; in other words 59% of the geographic territory of Brazil is part of Amazônia Legal, but only 12.34% of the Brazilian population lives there. The administrative unit was initially established by Federal Law No. 5.173 (Art. 2).[^cite_note-4]

Although called *Amazônia Legal,* the region overlaps three different [biomes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biome): all of Brazil's [Amazon biome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_biome), 37% of the [Cerrado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerrado) biome, and 40% of the [Pantanal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantanal) biome.[^cite_note-5] The main characteristic of the region is the abundant and [tropical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical) [vegetation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetation), including large sections of [rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainforest).

The Legal Amazon is divided into two parts: Western Amazon (Amazônia Ocidental, in Portuguese) and Eastern Amazon (Amazônia Oriental, in Portuguese).

**Amazônia Ocidental** comprises the states of: Acre, Amazonas, Roraima and, Rondônia.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-\:0-7]

**Amazônia Oriental** comprises the states of: Pará, Maranhão, Amapá, Tocantins and, Mato Grosso.[^cite_note-\:0-7]

## Subdivisions

### The western region

The western region corresponding to 44.54% of the territorial extension of the Legal Amazon (72% of the [Amazon Forest](48139.md)), most preserved part.[^cite_note-\:0-7] It is made up of the Brazilian states of: Amazonas, Acre, Rondônia and, Roraima.[^cite_note-\:0-7] The economy of region is based on: agroindustry, fish farming, livestock farming, food industry, plant and mineral extraction, agriculture and ecotourism, in which economy and sustainability go hand in hand.[^cite_note-\:0-7]

### The eastern region

The western region is made up of the Brazilian states of: Pará, Maranhão, Amapá, Tocantins and, Mato Grosso.[^cite_note-\:0-7] This is also formed by 20% of the [cerrado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerrado) biome and part of the [pantanal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantanal).[^cite_note-\:0-7] Its population is 12.4% of the national population (21,056,532 inhabitants).[^cite_note-\:0-7]

## Demographics

A total population of 24.7 million inhabitants lives in the area,[^cite_note-8] including more than 300,000 [indigenous people](55510.md) belonging to more than 170 ethnicities. Among these are many traditional [extractive communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extractive_reserve_\(Brazil\)).

Because of its remoteness, this region was the last to be [colonized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Settler_colonialism) by [Brazilians of European descent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Brazilians). It still has a very low [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density).
