**Nagaoka** (長岡市, *Nagaoka-shi*) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_Japan) located in [Niigata Prefecture](181660.md), Japan. It is the second largest city in the prefecture, after the capital city of [Niigata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niigata,_Niigata). As of 4 August 2021, the city had an estimated population of 264,611 in 109,283 households[^cite_note-1] and a [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) of 300 inhabitants per square kilometre (780/sq mi). The total area of the city was 891.06 square kilometres (344.04 sq mi).[^cite_note-2]

## Geography



Nagaoka in winter from the sky

Nagaoka is in the center of Niigata prefecture and the surrounding [Chūetsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C5%ABetsu) region of Japan, between [longitude 138°E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/138th_meridian_east) and [latitude 37°N](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/37th_parallel_north). It is 80 minutes from [Tokyo](30057.md) by way of the [Joetsu Shinkansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joetsu_Shinkansen) or three hours on the [Kan-Etsu Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kan-Etsu_Expressway) and is considered a strategic traffic point in the region. Nagaoka was an inland city until January 1, 2006, when the city merged with four municipalities; two were touching the [Sea of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_of_Japan). The [Shinano River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinano_River) flows through the city from south to north and industrial development is on both banks of the river. The Higashiyama mountain range lies to the east.

### Climate

Nagaoka has a [humid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) (Köppen *Cfa*) characterized by warm, wet summers and cold winters with heavy snowfall. The average annual temperature in Nagaoka is 13.3 °C (55.9 °F). The average annual rainfall is 2,349.3 mm (92.49 in) with September as the wettest month. The temperatures are highest on average in August, at around 26.2 °C (79.2 °F), and lowest in January, at around 1.6 °C (34.9 °F).[^cite_note-normals-3]

## History



[Ukiyo-e](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukiyo-e) of the [Battle of Hokuetsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Hokuetsu) ([Boshin War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boshin_War)) by Utagawa Kuniteru II



Picture of annual event of the Nagaoka castle – going into the castle for New Year greeting

The area of present-day Nagaoka was part of ancient [Echigo Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echigo_Province). Under the [Tokugawa shogunate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokugawa_shogunate), a [castle town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C5%8Dkamachi) was constructed by [Hori Naoyori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hori_Naoyori?action=edit\&redlink=1) lord of [Nagaoka Domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagaoka_Domain) in 1616. However, as the initial castle was located in an area prone to flooding by the [Shinano River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinano_River), a new castle was built at the site of present-day [Nagaoka Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagaoka_Station) in 1617. Nagaoka flourished as under the reign of the 13 generations of the [Makino clan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makino_clan) during the [Edo period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_period). In the [Boshin War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boshin_War) of 1868 during the [Meiji Restoration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_Restoration), Nagaoka Domain was a member of the [Ōuetsu Reppan Dōmei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Cuetsu_Reppan_D%C5%8Dmei) against the imperial forces, and the city was reduced to rubble during the [Battle of Hokuetsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Hokuetsu). A gift of one hundred sacks of rice from a neighboring province was sold to finance a new school during the reconstruction of Nagaoka, from which the anecdote of *[Kome Hyappyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kome_Hyappyo)* was born.

With the Meiji period creation of the modern municipalities system on April 1, 1889, the towns of Nagaoka and Nagaoka-honmachi were established. The two towns were merged on November 1, 1901 with the towns of Senju, Kusouzu, Ara, and village of Ouchi to form the modern town of Nagaoka, which was then raised to city status on April 1, 1906.

## Government



Nagaoka City Hall

Nagaoka has a [mayor-council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor-council) form of government with a directly elected mayor and a [unicameral](204304.md) city legislature of 34 members. The city contributes six members to the Niigata Prefectural Assembly. In terms of national politics, the city is part of [Niigata 5th district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niigata_5th_district) of the [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_Japan) of the [Diet of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diet_of_Japan).

## Economy

### Industry

The manufacturing industry prospered in Nagaoka following World War II, due in part to favorable location and good transportation infrastructure. Current industrial production includes precision instruments and [machine tools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_tool).

- [Nippon Seiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nippon_Seiki?action=edit\&redlink=1), a manufacturer of automotive instruments, is headquartered in Nagaoka.

  [^cite_note-13]

- TDK-Lambda, a manufacturer of [switched-mode power supplies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switched-mode_power_supply), has a plant in Nagaoka.

- [SLB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schlumberger), an oilfield services company, has a location in Nagaoka.

### Primary and secondary education

The city of Nagaoka operates 61 public elementary schools and 27 public middle schools. There is also one public middle school operated by the national government. Nagaoka also has nine public high schools operated by the Niigata Prefectural Board of Education and three private high schools. The prefecture also operates three special education schools in the city.

### Bus

Local bus service in Nagaoka is managed by [Echigo Kotsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echigo_Kotsu). The bus terminal located at the east entrance of Nagaoka Station serves as the hub for the route network. Service extends to all parts of the city as well as many outlying suburban areas and villages. A bus service connecting Nagaoka Station to [Niigata, Niigata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niigata,_Niigata) Station runs at intervals of approximately 30 minutes. Additionally, long-distance bus services are available to all major cities in the prefecture as well as Tokyo.
