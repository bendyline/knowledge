# List of diplomatic missions of France

&#x20;The **[French Republic](5843419.md)** has one of the world's largest diplomatic networks, and is a member of more multilateral organisations than any other country.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

France's permanent representation abroad began in the reign of [Francis I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_I_of_France), when in 1522 he sent a delegation to the [Swiss](26748.md). Despite its reduced presence following [decolonization](302084.md), France still has substantial influence throughout the world.&#x20;

Honorary consulates are excluded from this listing.
