The **Cabinet of Rodrigo Paz** constitutes the 223rd cabinet of the [Plurinational State of Bolivia](3462.md). It was formed on 8 November 2025 after [Rodrigo Paz Pereira](69731047.md) was sworn in as the 68th [President of Bolivia](188806.md), following the [2025 general election](77237927.md), succeeding the [Arce Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Luis_Arce).[^cite_note-3] Paz announced that he would reduce his cabinet from 17 to 12 ministries as part of a policy of austerity and state efficiency.[^cite_note-4]

## Transition

The transfer of presidential power between the outgoing government of [Luis Arce](38820569.md) and the incoming government of [Rodrigo Paz](69731047.md) began on October 22, 2025, and concluded on November 8, 2025, after his swearing-in before the Legislative Assembly.

### Transitional cabinet

After becoming president elect, Rodrigo Paz announced that José Luis Lupo and Gabriel Espinoza would be part of his governing team. They immediately traveled to the United States to meet with leaders of the International Monetary Fund (IMF), World Bank, the Inter-American Development Bank, and United States Secretary of State, Marco Rubio.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Inauguration

The official inauguration ceremony for the [President](188806.md) and [Vice President of Bolivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Bolivia) took place on November 8, 2025, in the city of La Paz (the seat of government of Bolivia).&#x20;

### Armed Forces

On 13 November 2025, president Paz swore in the new High Command of the Armed Forces of Bolivia, designating General [Víctor Hugo Balderrama Quezada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%ADctor_Hugo_Balderrama_Quezada?action=edit\&redlink=1) as Commander in Chief, General [Héctor Alejandro Alarcón Antezana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9ctor_Alejandro_Alarc%C3%B3n_Antezana?action=edit\&redlink=1) as Commander of the Army, General [Sergio Fernando Lora Araoz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Fernando_Lora_Araoz?action=edit\&redlink=1) as Commander of the Air Force and Counter Admiral [Ernesto Adalid Alfaro Palma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernesto_Adalid_Alfaro_Palma?action=edit\&redlink=1) as Commander of the Navy.[^cite_note-12]
