**Shin Ohashi** (born 5 March 2009) is a Japanese swimmer specializing in [breaststroke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaststroke). At the [2026 Pan Pacific Swimming Championships](82841723.md), he broke his own [junior world record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Junior_world_records_in_swimming) to win silver in the [100 metres breaststroke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_metres_breaststroke).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] He also holds the world record in the [200 metres breaststroke](18215551.md).[^cite_note-oly-26-3]

He was named the 2025 Male World Junior Swimmer of the Year by *Swimming World*, for his performances including five medals at the [2025 World Aquatics Junior Swimming Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Aquatics_Junior_Swimming_Championships).[^cite_note-4] Ohashi became the Japanese national champion in all three breaststroke events at age 17, the youngest ever to do so.[^cite_note-oly-26-3]

At the [2026 Asian Games](84060195.md), Ohashi broke the [world record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_swimming_world_records) in the [200 metre breaststroke](84315837.md), finishing in 2\:04.83. He became the youngest male swimmer to break an individual long-course world record since [Michael Phelps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Phelps) in 2002.[^cite_note-5]
