[Alexander Zverev](39755766.md) defeated [Ben Shelton](71310148.md) in the final, 6–3, 7–6<sup>(7–2)</sup>, 5–7, 6–2 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [2026 US Open](80928175.md).[^cite_note-1] It was his second [major](197638.md#Tournaments) title, following the [2026 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles). Zverev was the fourth man in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Era) to win his first two majors in the same year (after [Jimmy Connors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Connors) in [1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_Grand_Prix_\(tennis\)), [Guillermo Vilas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_Vilas) in [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_Grand_Prix_\(tennis\)), and [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md) in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_ATP_Tour)), and the first German man to win the [US Open](82729.md) since [Boris Becker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Becker) in [1989](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles).[^cite_note-sascha-2][^cite_note-3]

[Carlos Alcaraz](63121147.md) was the defending champion, but lost in the quarterfinals to Shelton. Their quarterfinal match marked the latest finish in tournament history, at 3\:33 a.m.,[^cite_note-4] surpassing the previous record from the [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) quarterfinal between Alcaraz and Sinner.[^cite_note-5]

[Novak Djokovic](16100029.md)'s first-round defeat to [Mariano Navone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariano_Navone) marked only his third career loss in the opening round of a major, his first opening-round loss at the US Open, and his earliest loss at a major since the [2006 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles).[^cite_note-6] This marked the first time that none of the [Big Three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Three_\(tennis\)#Grand_Slam_events) reached the second round of a major since the [2003 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles).[^cite_note-7] As a result of his loss, Djokovic dropped out of the top 10 in the ATP rankings for the first time since July 2018, marking the first time since October 2002 in which none of the Big Three were ranked in the top 10.[^cite_note-8]

[Arthur Géa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_G%C3%A9a) was the first [lucky loser](2086074.md#Lucky_loser) to reach the fourth round since [Corentin Moutet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corentin_Moutet) in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), and the second to do so in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Era).[^cite_note-9] With his win over 12th seed [Rafael Jódar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_J%C3%B3dar), [Bu Yunchaokete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bu_Yunchaokete) became the first lucky loser to defeat a top 15 seed in the first round of the US Open in the Open Era.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

This tournament marked the first absence from a major of [world No. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ATP_number_1_ranked_singles_players) Sinner, since making his major debut at the [2019 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles). It was the first time the reigning world No. 1 did not participate at the US Open.[^cite_note-13]

This tournament marked the final major appearances of [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) champion, three-time major champion and former world No. 3 [Stan Wawrinka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stan_Wawrinka), and former world No. 6 and 2016 semifinalist [Gaël Monfils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ga%C3%ABl_Monfils).[^cite_note-14] Wawrinka lost in the first round to [Matteo Berrettini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matteo_Berrettini),[^cite_note-15] while Monfils lost in the second round to [Learner Tien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learner_Tien).[^cite_note-16]
