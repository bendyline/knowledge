The **2026 Australian Open** was a [Grand Slam](197638.md) level [tennis](29773.md) tournament that was held at [Melbourne Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne_Park), from 18 January to 1 February 2026, with the preliminary rounds played from 12 to 15 January. It was the 114th edition of the [Australian Open](97851.md), the 58th in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Era) and the first [major](197638.md#Tournaments) of the year. The tournament consisted of events for professional players in singles, doubles and mixed doubles. Junior and wheelchair players competed in singles and doubles tournaments.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

The defending men's singles champion, [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md), lost in the semifinals.[^cite_note-3] The defending women's singles champion, [Madison Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Keys), lost in the fourth round.[^cite_note-4] [Venus Williams](167378.md) became the oldest player to play in the women's singles draw at age 45.[^cite_note-5]

Russian and Belarusian players were still required to participate as neutral athletes. Their national flags were not shown against their names and both flags were still banned from the tournament venue due to the ongoing [war between Russia and Ukraine](70149799.md).

## Exhibition events

### 1 Point Slam

The 2026 1 Point Slam was held on 14 January, with significantly increased prize money for the champion. Amateur tennis player Jordan Smith defeated [Joanna Garland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanna_Garland) in the final, winning a \$1 million prize, along with \$50,000 in funding for his tennis club.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] (Alec Reverente defeated Smith in a bonus round to win a [Kia EV3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kia_EV3).)[^cite_note-14]

The list of professional players who participated in this edition included [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md), [Iga Świątek](51838558.md), [Maria Sakkari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Sakkari), [Nick Kyrgios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Kyrgios), [Amanda Anisimova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Anisimova), [Carlos Alcaraz](63121147.md), [Coco Gauff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_Gauff), [Laura Pigossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Pigossi) and others.[^cite_note-15]

### AO Pickleball Slam

The [AO Pickleball Slam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AO_Pickleball_Slam) is scheduled to return for its second edition.[^cite_note-16]

### Charity matches

On 13 January, [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md) and [Lorenzo Musetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Musetti) played an exhibition match, with the latter retiring injured while trailing 6–7<sup>(7–9)</sup>.[^cite_note-17] Two days later, [Carlos Alcaraz](63121147.md) played [Alex de Minaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_de_Minaur), with the Spaniard triumphing 6–3, 6–4.[^cite_note-18]

In the women's exhibitions, [McCartney Kessler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McCartney_Kessler) defeated [Amanda Anisimova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Anisimova) 6–3, 3–6, \[10–7].[^cite_note-19] Anisimova played [Elina Svitolina](27630639.md) in a subsequent match.[^cite_note-20]

[Jannik Sinner](60061043.md) defeated [Félix Auger-Aliassime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9lix_Auger-Aliassime) 6–4, 4–6, \[10–4] in the final charity match.[^cite_note-21]

### Red Bull Bassline

The [Red Bull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Bull) Bassline was an event characterized by a non-traditional format that combined short tie-break matches with live music played by a DJ. Matches were conducted without official umpires, with players responsible for making line calls and resolving points on court. Audience participation was encouraged, including involvement in disputed calls.[^cite_note-Carayol-22]

Matches were played in a rapid best-of-three format, with each set consisting of a tie-break to five points. Six players participated, divided into two groups, usually identified as Red and Blue. Within each group, the competition followed a round-robin structure, with each player facing all other players in the same group. Music was played throughout the matches, and spectators were invited to engage through voting and audible support, contributing to the event’s informal presentation.[^cite_note-Carayol-22]

[Nicolai Budkov Kjaer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolai_Budkov_Kjaer) was the winner.

### Mixed doubles showdown

A mixed doubles tournament where matches were timed at 10 minutes with a 15-second shot clock, and the serve order followed a 1–2–2–2 sequence, as in a tiebreak. The team with the most points at the end of the match was declared the winner. At the conclusion of the group stage, the team with the highest number of points in each group advanced to the final. In the event of a tie, a golden point was to be played to determine the winner. All other rules applicable to Grand Slam mixed doubles tennis would apply unless otherwise specified.[^cite_note-23]

The final was played by Australians [Ellen Perez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellen_Perez) and [Nick Kyrgios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Kyrgios) against Brazilian [Luisa Stefani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luisa_Stefani) and Salvadoran [Marcelo Arevalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcelo_Arevalo). Perez and Kyrgios were the winners with a score of 10/9.[^cite_note-24]
