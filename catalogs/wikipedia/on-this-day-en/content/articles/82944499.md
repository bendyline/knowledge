The **2026 AFC Women's Champions League final** was the final match of the [2025–26 AFC Women's Champions League](78265767.md), the 6th season of Asia's premier club [women's football](191466.md) tournament organised by the [Asian Football Confederation](715249.md) (AFC), and the second since it was rebranded as the [AFC Women's Champions League](64565584.md). The match involved Japanese club [Tokyo Verdy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nippon_TV_Tokyo_Verdy_Beleza) ([WE League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WE_League) champions) and North Korean side [Naegohyang](77150401.md) ([DPR Korea Women's Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPR_Korea_Women's_Premier_League) champions).[^cite_note-bbc-2][^cite_note-3]

Naegohyang's appearance in the 2026 AFC Women's Champions League playoffs received interest.[^cite_note-xinhua-4] This marks the first time a group of North Korean athletes went to South Korea for a competitive game since the [2018 ITTF World Tour Grand Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_ITTF_World_Tour_Grand_Finals).[^cite_note-5]

Naegohyang won the final and qualified for the [2027 FIFA Women's Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_Champions_Cup).[^cite_note-xinhua-4]
