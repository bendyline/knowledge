**Kherbet Selem** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): خربة سلم)[^cite_note-1] is a municipality in southern [Lebanon](17771.md).

The municipality of Kherbet Selem is located in the [Kaza of Bint Jbeil](12027489.md), one of the eight mohafazats (governorates) of Lebanon. Khirbet Selm is 104 kilometers (64.6256 mi) away from [Beirut](37428.md) the capital of Lebanon. The village's elevation is 630 meters (2067.03 ft – 688.968 yd) above sea level. Kherbet Selem surface stretches for 379 hectares (12.60 km<sup>2</sup> – 9.46294 mi<sup>2</sup>).[^cite_note-2]

## Etymology

The name of the town means "the ruins of Selem". *Kherbeh* means ruins, and Selem is likely [Salem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shalim), a [Canaanite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canaan) deity.

## History

In 1875 [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) described it: "In the middle of a deep and broad ravine the Wady el-Hadjr, a sort of rocky islet lying north and south. Oblong and narrow, it serves as the site of a village called Kh. Selem, which contains a population of 130 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh)."[^cite_note-3]

He further noted: "Here are found the remains of an ancient church, such as cut stones, shafts more or less broken, and a circular [baptismal font](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptismal_font). Here I also saw several Greek crosses engraved on [lintels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lintel) of doors, [cisterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cistern), presses cut in the rock, and even some houses still standing which may be older than the Mussulman invasion."[^cite_note-4]

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described it: "A village, built of stone, containing 200 Metawileh, on ridge, with spring and cisterns; arable cultivation around."[^cite_note-5]

They further noted: "South of the village there is a level area of rock, in the middle of which one [column](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Column) is standing. Another column and two or three pedestals form part of a wall on the west. There are no [capitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_\(architecture\)). This was probably a Christian church. The columns are not well dressed."[^cite_note-6]

On july, 12 2026, the [Hezbollah](13919.md) militia buried in a mass funeral ceremony 30 of its fighters originary from the village and killed during [the 2026 conflict](82549840.md) with Israel [^cite_note-7] .

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.24% of registered voters in Kherbet Selem. 97.60% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-8]
