**Ratschings** (German pronunciation: [\[raˈtʃɪŋs\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Standard_German); [Italian](14708.md): *Racines* [\[raˈtʃiːnes\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Italian)) is a *[comune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comune)* (municipality) in [South Tyrol](169611.md) in northern [Italy](14532.md), located about 45 kilometres (28 mi) north of the city of [Bolzano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolzano), on the border with [Austria](26964606.md).

## Geography

As of 30 November 2010, it had a population of 4,363 and an area of 203.6 square kilometres (78.6 mi<sup>2</sup>).[^cite_note-istat-3]

Ratschings borders the following municipalities: [Brenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brenner,_South_Tyrol), [Freienfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freienfeld), [Moos in Passeier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moos_in_Passeier), [St. Leonhard in Passeier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Leonhard_in_Passeier), [Sarntal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarntal), [Sterzing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterzing), [Neustift im Stubaital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neustift_im_Stubaital) (Austria) and [Sölden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%B6lden) (Austria).

### Frazioni

The municipality of Ratschings contains the *[frazioni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazione)* (subdivisions, mainly villages and hamlets) Außerratschings, Innerratschings, Gasteig (Casateia), Mareit (Mareta), Ridnaun (Ridanna), Telfes (Telves), and Jaufental (Valgiovo).

## History

### Coat of arms

The emblem show a rampant [argent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argent) [wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolves_in_heraldry) on [azure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azure_\(heraldry\)). The arms is similar to that of *Wolf von Mareit*, but with the opposite colors. The emblem was adopted in 1969.[^cite_note-4]

## Society

### Linguistic distribution

According to the 2024 census, 96.65% of the population speak German, 3.21% Italian and 0.14% [Ladin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladin_language) as first language.[^cite_note-Volkszählung_2024/Censimento_della_popolazione_2024-5]
