The **Airbus Helicopters H125** (previously the **Eurocopter AS350**) **Écureuil**, or **Squirrel**, is a single-engine light [utility helicopter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utility_helicopter) designed and originally manufactured by the French corporation [Aérospatiale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%A9rospatiale), later by [Eurocopter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocopter), which became Airbus Helicopters. In North America, the H125 is marketed as the **AStar**. The [AS355 Ecureuil 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocopter_AS355_%C3%89cureuil_2) is a twin-engine variant, marketed in North America as the TwinStar.&#x20;

The [Eurocopter EC130](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocopter_EC130) is a derivative of the AS350 airframe and is considered by the manufacturer to be part of the Écureuil single-engine family.[^cite_note-4]

## Development

In the early 1970s, Aérospatiale initiated a development program to produce a replacement for the aging [Aérospatiale Alouette II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%A9rospatiale_Alouette_II).[^cite_note-euro_his-5] While the [Aérospatiale Gazelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%A9rospatiale_Gazelle), which had been developed in the 1960s and 1970s, had been met with numerous orders by military customers, commercial sales of the type had been less than anticipated, thus the need for a civil-oriented development was identified.[^cite_note-euro_his-5]

The development of the new rotorcraft, which was headed by Chief Engineer [René Mouille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Mouille), was focused on the production of an economic and cost-effective aerial vehicle, thus both Aérospatiale's Production and Procurement departments were heavily involved in the design process.[^cite_note-euro_his-5] One such measure was the use of a [rolled sheet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_\(metalworking\)) structure, a manufacturing technique adapted from the [automotive industry](163778.md); another innovation was the newly developed *Starflex* main rotor. It was also decided that both civil and military variants of the emergent helicopter would be developed to conform with established military requirements.[^cite_note-euro_his-5]

On 27 June 1974, the first prototype, an AS350C powered by a [Lycoming LTS101](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycoming_LTS101) [turboshaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turboshaft) engine, conducted its maiden flight at [Marignane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marignane), [France](5843419.md); the second prototype, powered by a [Turbomeca Arriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turbomeca_Arriel) 1A, following on 14 February 1975.[^cite_note-euro_his-5][^cite_note-jawa03_p244-6] The Arriel-powered version, the AS350B, intended for sale throughout the world except for North America, was [certified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_certificate) in France on 27 October 1977, while the Lycoming powered AS350C (or AStar) was certified by the US [Federal Aviation Administration](11186.md) on 21 December 1977. In March 1978, deliveries to customers began for the AS350B, deliveries of the AS350C began in April 1978.[^cite_note-jawa82_p56-7]

Over time, the AS350 Écureuil/AStar has received further development; while the aircraft's design remains broadly similar, aspects such as the [rotor system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter_rotor), powerplants, and avionics have been progressively improved. On 6 February 1987, a prototype AS350 flew with a [fenestron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenestron) tail-rotor in the place of its conventional counterpart. On 1 March 1997, the first AS350 B3, equipped with an Arriel 2B engine, performed its first flight.[^cite_note-euro_his-5]

Conversion programs and addons for AS350s have been produced and are offered by third-party 'aftermarket' aerospace firms in addition to those available directly from the type's prime manufacturer. Variants of the Arriel-powered AS350B, AS350 BA, AS350 B1, AS350 B2, and AS350 B3, were progressively introduced; the later B3 differing from preceding models by the increasing use of digital systems, such as the [Garmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garmin)-built G500H avionics suite and [full authority digital engine control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_authority_digital_engine_control) (FADEC) system.[^cite_note-Lombardi-8]

Prior to 2013, the type had been manufactured principally at Eurocopter's Marignane facility, near [Marseille](40888948.md), France; Eurocopter opted to, as part of a move to disperse its helicopter production activities, begin AS350 production and final assembly activities at its factory in [Columbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus,_Mississippi), Mississippi, for deliveries to U.S. commercial helicopter market. The Astar has been Eurocopter's biggest-selling product in the US commercial market, at one point selling roughly one AS350 every business day.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] In March 2015, the first Columbus-assembled AS350 B3e received its [FAA](11186.md) certification.[^cite_note-11] In December 2015, Airbus Helicopters reported their intention to double the rate of AS350 production at Columbus in 2016 over the previous year, and that the facility is capable of producing up to 65 AS350s per year.[^cite_note-12]

### Indian assembly line

On 26 January 2024, while the [French president](24899.md), [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md), was in India as the [chief guest of the Republic Day parade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_chief_guests_at_the_Delhi_Republic_Day_parade), Airbus Helicopters announced its partnership with the [Tata Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Group) to establish a [Final Assembly Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_line) (FAL) in India for helicopter production. The FAL would deliver the H125 for civilian markets in India and South Asia. The facility would be set up by the [Tata Advanced Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Advanced_Systems) along with Airbus within 24 months with the first [Made in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Make_in_India) helicopter, with [EASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union_Aviation_Safety_Agency)-certified quality assurance, expected to be produced in 2026. The facility's location was yet to be decided by the companies.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] Ten helicopters will be assembled each year, potentially increasing to 20, 30, or 50 in the future. As of July 2024, 500 units are expected to be produced in 20 years. The H125 base variant produced in India, will cost above Euro 3.2 million.[^cite_note-15]

On 23 July 2024, TASL and Airbus signed the deal for production. The H125's engine and gearbox will come from France, the main airframe from Germany, and the tail boom from Spain. The component assemblies to be done in India include avionics and mission systems, flight controls, hydraulic circuits, fuel system and the engine.[^cite_note-16]

As of July 2024, Airbus identified 8 locations to become potential sites for the FAL,[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] which was shortlisted to four by January 2025.[^cite_note-19] The sites were located in [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md), [Gujarat](53707.md), [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md) and [Karnataka](16880.md).[^cite_note-\:2-20] In May, it was decided the new site will be erected in the Vemagal Industrial Area of [Kolar, Karnataka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katar,_Karnataka).[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] This would be the fourth such assembly line for the helicopter in the world.[^cite_note-\:2-20] The first helicopter is expected to be delivered in early 2027.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

On 26 February 2025, the [Indian Army chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Army_chief), [General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_\(India\)) [Upendra Dwivedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upendra_Dwivedi), visited the Airbus facility in France where he was demonstrated the military capabilities of the helicopter.[^cite_note-\:2-20][^cite_note-25] On 28 August, Airbus Helicopters signed a contract with [Mahindra Aerostructures Pvt. Ltd.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahindra_&_Mahindra#Aerospace) (MAPL) as part of which MAPL will supply the main [fuselage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuselage) of the H125 helicopter. The manufacturing is expected to begin immediately at MAPL's existing plant in [Bengaluru](44275267.md) with the first unit scheduled to be delivered in 2027.[^cite_note-26]

The H125 is reportedly one of the frontrunner in the [Reconnaissance and Surveillance Helicopters (RSH) tender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_of_the_Indian_Air_Force#Helicopters) launched in August 2026.[^cite_note-27]

On 17 February 2026, the [Prime Minister of India](24452.md), [Narendra Modi](444222.md), and the [President of France](24899.md), [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md), virtually inaugurated the Final Assembly Line at Vemegal from Mumbai. The investment in the programme will likely exceed [₹](490783.md)1,000 [billion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1,000,000,000) (US\$10 billion).[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

## Design

The AS350 is a single engine helicopter, powered either by a [Lycoming LTS101](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycoming_LTS101) or [Turbomeca Arriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turbomeca_Arriel) powerplant that drives a three-[blade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotor_blade) main rotor, which is furnished with a *Starflex* rotor head. The type is known for its high-altitude performance and has seen use by operators in such environments.[^cite_note-Lombardi-8] Both the main and tail rotors make use of composite material and are designed to minimize corrosion and maintenance requirements.[^cite_note-r\&w_07-31]

The AS350 was also developed to comply with the noise requirements in place in locations such as [national parks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_park); the in-cabin noise levels are such that passengers may also readily converse during flight. The aircraft can also be quickly started up and shut down, which is often useful during [emergency medical services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_medical_services) roles.[^cite_note-r\&w_07-31] It is equipped with [hydraulically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulic)-assisted [flight controls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter_flight_controls); these controls remain operational, albeit operated with greater physical effort, in the event of a hydraulic failure.[^cite_note-32]

Much of the AS350's avionics are provided by [Garmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garmin), such as the GI 106A course-deviation indicator, GNS 430 [VHF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VHF)/VOR/localizer/glideslope indicator/[Global Positioning System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Positioning_System) receiver, GTX 327 Mode A and C transponder, and GMA 340H intercom.[^cite_note-r\&w_07-31] The Vehicle and Engine Multifunction Display (VEMD) and the First Limit Indicator (FLI) both serve to increase the aircraft's safety during flight, reducing the number of gauges that need to be monitored by the pilot and thereby reducing their workload.[^cite_note-air_civ-33] For increased smoothness in flight, which positively affects passenger comfort as well as safety, stability augmentation systems can be installed. Later-production aircraft feature new avionics and systems such as the integration of an Automatic Flight Control System (AFCS) and autopilot, a [glass cockpit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_cockpit) featuring three [liquid-crystal displays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid-crystal_display) (LCDs) and digital avionics, such as the synthetic-vision terrain mapping system and Airbus's *Multibloc* center console upon which radios may be mounted.[^cite_note-Lombardi-8][^cite_note-hebr_upgr-34]

The AS350 has proven popular in a range of roles; multiple cabin configurations can be used, between four and six passengers in a typical seating configuration, and large sliding doors can be fitted to either side of the cabin. In some operators' fleets, the furnishings of the cabin has been designed to enable the internal space and/or equipment fit-out to be rapidly reconfigured to enable aircraft to be switched between roles.[^cite_note-Lombardi-8]

&#x20;Public service operators, such as those in [law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement), often have [forward looking infrared](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_looking_infrared) (FLIR) cameras and other mission systems installed on their aircraft. Other optional equipment on offer to operates had included [real-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real-time_data) [data links](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_link), rescue hoists, underslung cargo hooks, electrical external [mirrors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror), [search lights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_light), tactical consoles, [night vision goggle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Night_vision_goggle)-compatibility, moving-map system, internal cabin tie-downs, second [battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_\(electricity\)) kit, sand filters, [wire strike protection system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wire_strike_protection_system), 4-channel radio, tail rotor arch, cabin floor windows, and removable seats.[^cite_note-Lombardi-8][^cite_note-air_civ-33]

Modern aircraft have refinements beyond those featuring on older models; these changes include dual-channel FADEC-equipped engines, increasing use of digital avionics in the cockpit, decreased maintenance costs, a re-designed cabin, and a comfortable *Stylence* interior (optional).[^cite_note-Lombardi-8][^cite_note-air_civ-33] Older aircraft often undergo refurbishment programs to install aftermarket features, or for the addition of functionality common to newer production aircraft, such as [retrofitting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retrofit) of the glass cockpit.[^cite_note-hebr_upgr-34]

A [French Gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Gendarmerie) AS350, 2005

On 14 May 2005, an AS350 B3 piloted by Eurocopter test pilot [Didier Delsalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Didier_Delsalle) touched down on the top of [Mount Everest](42179.md), at 8,848 m (29,030 ft),[^cite_note-everestnet-35][^cite_note-36][^cite_note-as350ng-37] a record that has been confirmed by the [Fédération Aéronautique Internationale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9d%C3%A9ration_A%C3%A9ronautique_Internationale).[^cite_note-alt-38] Although Delsalle used a standard version of the Eurocopter, he removed unnecessary elements, such as passenger seats, to reduce the standard weight by 120 kg and increase the fuel range by an additional hour.[^cite_note-39] On 29 April 2010, a stripped-down AS350 B3 rescued three Spanish alpinists, one at a time, from the slopes of [Annapurna I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annapurna), Nepal at an altitude of 6,900 m (22,640 ft); this set a new record for the highest such rescue.[^cite_note-40] The record was increased to 7,800 m (25,590 ft), during the rescue of [Sudarshan Gautam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudarshan_Gautam) between Camps III & IV in [Everest's Yellow Band](42179.md#Geology) on the morning of 20 May 2013.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44] On 2 June 2014, an AS350 B3e broke a national record in Mexico by successfully landing on the peak of [Pico de Orizaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pico_de_Orizaba), at 5,636 m (18,490 ft), the nation's tallest mountain.[^cite_note-45]

The AS350 AStar has been successful in the US market, having become the most popular helicopter platform in use with American governmental agencies, [law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement) being a typical use of the type, by 2015.[^cite_note-46] By 1999, the AS350 had become the prime helicopter being used by the [United States Customs Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Customs_Service) for light enforcement operations;[^cite_note-47] by 2007, the agency had become the single largest operator of the type in the world.[^cite_note-48] By 2012, out of the 3,300 AS350s in operation across the world, 783 of them were in service with American-based operators.[^cite_note-49]

In the Russian market since 2006, the AS350 and other helicopters built by the manufacturer have been sold and supported by wholly owned subsidiary *Eurocopter Vostok*; [UTair Aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTair_Aviation) soon emerged as the largest Russian operator of the AS350 B3e with a fleet of at least 20 of the type.[^cite_note-50]

In December 2014, [EASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Aviation_Safety_Agency) validation was issued for Airbus Helicopters China to conduct training and support activity at their facility in [Shenzhen](83368.md), China; various components of the AS350 (such as the main and tail gearboxes) are now maintained locally.[^cite_note-51] On 9 September 2015, China's first helicopter leasing company, CM International Financial Leasing Corp Ltd (CMIFL), placed an order for 100 Ecureuil-series helicopters, these are to be a mix of H125 and H130 helicopters.[^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53]



An airbus H125 (Eurocoptes As350) operated by Rotortec

Brazil has been an extensive operator of the AS350; by 2011 more than 300 helicopters were operating in the country, including from the flight deck of the [Brazilian aircraft carrier São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_aircraft_carrier_S%C3%A3o_Paulo_\(A12\)).[^cite_note-hebr_upgr-34][^cite_note-54] Since 1984, the [Brazilian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Navy) has used AS350s to support the [Brazilian Antarctic Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Antarctic_Program).[^cite_note-55] [Helibras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helibras), a wholly owned subsidiary of Eurocopter, operates in the country; of the 600 helicopters it had domestically manufactured for the Brazilian market by 2012, 70% were AS350s.[^cite_note-56] In January 2011, [Helibras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helibras) signed a contract with the Brazilian Army to substantially upgrade and refurbish their existing fleet of 36 AS350 Ecureuils.[^cite_note-57]



AS350 B2, 2001

In the United Kingdom, the [Defence Helicopter Flying School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Helicopter_Flying_School) operated 26 AS350, designated *Squirrel HT1*, for the training of pilots of Britain's armed forces; the type was introduced from 1997 onwards as a replacement for the [Aérospatiale Gazelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%A9rospatiale_Gazelle).[^cite_note-58] In September 2014, the UK's [Ministry of Defence](203793.md) issued a [request for proposals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Request_for_proposals) to replace the Squirrel HT1; Airbus Helicopters has already announced its intention to offer a mixed fleet of [Eurocopter EC130s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocopter_EC130) and [Eurocopter EC135s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocopter_EC135) in response.[^cite_note-59] Since May 1984, the [Royal Australian Navy](26327.md)'s [Fleet Air Arm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fleet_Air_Arm_\(RAN\)) has operated a fleet of AS350s, these were upgraded to the AS350 BA standard in 1995; the [Royal Australian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Australian_Air_Force) had previously operated the AS350 for training purposes, and briefly for [search and rescue](172599.md) missions, but these were later transferred to the [Australian Army](2795.md).[^cite_note-60] In 2017, the AS350 was retired from Australian Navy service.[^cite_note-61]

Between June 2007 and December 2007, the [Danish Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_Air_Force) operated a deployment of four AS350 helicopters at [Basra International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basra_International_Airport), [Iraq](7515928.md), to perform liaison and reconnaissance missions in support of coalition forces during the [Iraq War](5043324.md).[^cite_note-62] In June 2015, the [Argentine Defense Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defense_\(Argentina\)) ordered 12 H125s to replace their 1970s era [Aérospatiale SA 315B Lamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%A9rospatiale_SA_315B_Lama) for para-public support missions, such as search and rescue operations, inside Argentina.[^cite_note-63] By June 2020, 3,663 H125 were operational, the largest number of any type.[^cite_note-64]

In June 1978, [Transport Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_Canada) (TC) issued the Canadian [type certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_certificate) for the AS 350 C model, marking it as the first of the AS 350 series certified in Canada. The AS 350 B was added to the [type certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_certificate)'s data sheet in February 1980, and the AS 350 B1 received certification from TC in July 1988. AS350s are widely utilized in Canada for fire suppression and mountain operations. This versatile helicopter is frequently equipped with a [Bambi Bucket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter_bucket), capable of carrying up to 1,500 liters of water, making it an effective tool for [aerial firefighting](1143974.md). The AS350's agility, power, and ability to operate in challenging terrains, such as dense forests and mountainous regions, make it invaluable for combating wildfires.[^cite_note-65]

## Notable accidents and incidents

- On 14 January 1986, the AS-350 used by [Dakar Rally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakar_Rally) organiser [Thierry Sabine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thierry_Sabine) as his personal transport during the event, crashed into a sand dune in [Mali](19127.md). The helicopter had just taken off again after it had been forced to land in the desert by a sudden sandstorm. All five on board were killed. Among the victims were Thierry Sabine, French singer-songwriter [Daniel Balavoine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Balavoine) and pilot [François-Xavier Bagnoud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FXB_International).

- On 22 February 1995, Massachusetts State Police helicopter N20SP carrying two troopers and two AT\&T engineers crashed into the yacht club building. All on board died in the crash, which was attributed to significant operating deficiencies within the Police Air Wing which allowed contamination of the fuel bunker to go undetected.

  [^cite_note-135]

- On 27 April, 1996, 2 Japanese AS-350 news helicopters [collided](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagano_News_Helicopter_Collision) mid-air over [Nagano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagano_\(city\)), [Japan](15573.md) while covering a brush fire early that morning. All 6 occupants aboard both aircraft were killed.

  [^cite_note-136]

- On 13 May 1996, a severe snowstorm took the lives of several climbers and stranded two others high atop Mount Everest. Winds were estimated as gusting up to 70 miles per hour with temperatures down to -50F. Two climbers were frostbitten and near death. Several civil helicopter operators refused the mission, but a Nepalese pilot, Madan Khatri Chhetri, attempted the risky, high-altitude rescue. Climbing nearly 3,000 feet above the service ceiling of the Ecureuil AS350 B2 helicopter, he succeeded at making the highest altitude rescue in a rotary wing aircraft -- not once, but twice.

  [^cite_note-137]

- On 19 October 2001, a [New Mexico State Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico_State_Police) AS 350B medical helicopter suffered a mechanical failure and crashed during a training exercise, killing officers Damon Talbott and Ramon Robert Solis.

  [^cite_note-138]

  [^cite_note-139]

- On 15 September 2007, former [World Rally Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Rally_Championship) driver [Colin McRae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_McRae) and three passengers were killed when his AS350 B2 Squirrel,

- **[Never exceed speed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V_speeds#VNE):** 287 km/h (178 mph, 155 kn)

- **Range:** 662 km (411 mi, 357 nmi)

- **Endurance:** 4 hours 6 minutes

- **Service ceiling:** 4,600 m (15,100 ft)

- **Rate of climb:** 8.5 m/s (1,670 ft/min)

**Avionics**\
&#x20;Vehicle and Engine Monitoring Display (VEMD) with First Limit Indicator (FLI) fitted as standard.
