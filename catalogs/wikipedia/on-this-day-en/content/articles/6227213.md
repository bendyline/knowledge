The ***Sudan Tribune*** is an electronic [news portal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_portal) on [Sudan](27421.md) and [South Sudan](32350676.md)[^cite_note-aljazeera-1] and neighbouring countries including news coverage, analyses and commentary, official reports and [press releases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Press_release) from various organizations, and maps. It is based in [Paris](22989.md), France, and run by a team of Sudanese and international editors and journalists. The Sudan Tribune claims to have had over 5 million page views in 2005 and more than 12 million page views (almost a million absolute unique visitors) in 2008.

## History

The *Sudan Tribune* was started in 2003. In July 2017, the Sudanese media accused the South Sudanee government of blocking their websites, including that of the *Tribune*.[^cite_note-2]
