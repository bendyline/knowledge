**Wildlife Crime Control Bureau** (**WCCB**) is a statutory body established by the [Government of India](553883.md) under the [Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Environment,_Forest_and_Climate_Change) to combat organised [wildlife crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife_crime). The Wild Life (Protection) Amendment Act, 2006 provisions came in to force on 6 June 2007. It became operational in the year 2008.[^cite_note-2]

WCCB won the 2010 Clark R. Bavin Wildlife Law Enforcement Awards for its outstanding work on wildlife law enforcement in the country, which was received by Ramesh K Pandey, Deputy Director, on behalf of WCCB.[^cite_note-3] UNEP has also awarded WCCB with Asia Environment Enforcement Award, 2018.

WCCB is also partnering with [United Nations University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_University) and [CIESIN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIESIN)-[Earth Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth_Institute) at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) through the [Wildlife Enforcement Monitoring System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife_Enforcement_Monitoring_System) Initiative.
