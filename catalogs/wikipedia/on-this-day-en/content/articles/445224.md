# Explosive device



A stick of [dynamite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamite)&#x20;

1. [Diatomaceous earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diatomaceous_earth) (or any other type of absorbent material) soaked in nitroglycerin

2. Protective coating surrounding the explosive material

3. Blasting cap

4. Wire connected to the blasting cap

An **explosive device** typically refers to a [bomb](47628.md) that is not used by a [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military).[^cite_note-1] It contains an [explosive](10192.md) with a [detonator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detonator) connected to a triggering mechanism to form a device used for a specific purpose.[^cite_note-2]

## Applications

## Commercial

The largest commercial application of explosives is [mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining). The detonation or deflagration of either a high or low explosive in a confined space can be used to liberate a fairly specific sub-volume of a brittle material (rock) in a much larger volume of the same or similar material. The mining industry tends to use nitrate-based explosives such as emulsions of fuel oil and ammonium nitrate solutions,[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] mixtures of ammonium nitrate prills (fertilizer pellets), fuel oil ([ANFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANFO)), and gelatinous suspensions or slurries[^cite_note-5] of ammonium nitrate and combustible fuels.

In [materials science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_science) and engineering, explosives are used in [cladding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cladding_\(metalworking\)) ([explosion welding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosion_welding)). This is a [solid state (solid-phase)](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/solid%20state) [process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Process_\(science\)) where [welding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welding) is accomplished by accelerating one of the components at extremely high velocity through the use of explosives. This process is often used to clad [carbon steel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_steel) or [aluminium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminium) plate with a thin layer of a harder or more [corrosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corrosion)-resistant material.[^cite_note-6]
