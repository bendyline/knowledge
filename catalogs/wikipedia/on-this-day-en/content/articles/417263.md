**Votkinsk** ([/ˈvɒtkɪnsk/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English); [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Воткинск), also known as **Votka** ([/ˈvɒtkə/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English); [Udmurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udmurt_language): Вотка), is an industrial [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) in the [Udmurt Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udmurt_Republic), Russia. As of 2021, its population was 97,471.[^cite_note-9]

## History

Votkinsk was established in 1759,[^cite_note-gr-2] initially as a center for metallurgical enterprises. It still has a major economic focus on these industries. It was officially granted town status in 1935.

It was one of the residence centers of the Udmurt Jews, who spoke [Udmurt Yiddish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yiddish_dialects#Udmurt_Yiddish).[^cite_note-10]

The town is famous as the birthplace of composer [Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyotr_Ilyich_Tchaikovsky), who was born there on May 7, 1840.

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), Votkinsk serves as the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of [Votkinsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Votkinsky_District), even though it is not a part of it.[^cite_note-Ref149-1] As an administrative division, it is incorporated separately as the [town of republic significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance) of Votkinsk—an administrative unit with the status equal to that of the [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_the_Udmurt_Republic).[^cite_note-Ref149-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), the town of republic significance of Votkinsk is incorporated as Votkinsk Urban Okrug.[^cite_note-Ref912-5]

## Economy

The [Moscow Institute of Thermal Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Thermal_Technology) operates a machine plant in the town, the [Votkinsk Machine Building Plant](5145252.md), which produces some of Russia's long-range ballistic missiles. Under the [Intermediate-Range Nuclear Forces Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermediate-Range_Nuclear_Forces_Treaty) (INF) concluded between the United States and the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), the [RSD-10 Pioneer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSD-10_Pioneer) missile production facility at Votkinsk was selected for long-term on-site monitoring by U.S. inspectors. The corresponding site for the Soviet Union in the United States was the Hercules missile production facility in [Salt Lake City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Lake_City), [Utah](31716.md).

The town gives its name to the nearby [Votkinsk Reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Votkinsk_Reservoir), filled in the 1960s following the construction of a dam for the [Votkinsk Hydroelectric Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Votkinsk_Hydroelectric_Station).
