**Iraquara** is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Brazil) in the [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Brazil) of [Bahia](199070.md) in the [North-East region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordeste) of Brazil.

## Geography

In 2020, its estimated population was 25,478 inhabitants. It lies in the center of [Bahia](199070.md), in the [Chapada Diamantina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapada_Diamantina). Tourists visit to see the caves such as: Pratinha, Lapa Doce, Torrinha, Gruta Azul, Gruta da Fumaça, among others.[^cite_note-IBGE_DTB_2008-2][^cite_note-IBGE_Pop_2009-3][^cite_note-PNUD_IDH_2000-4][^cite_note-IBGE_PIB_2005-5]
