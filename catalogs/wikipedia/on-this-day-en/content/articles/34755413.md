**USS *Rafael Peralta* (DDG-115)** is an [*Arleigh Burke*-class](197341.md) ([Flight IIA Restart](197341.md#Characteristics)) [Aegis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aegis_combat_system) [guided missile destroyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_missile_destroyer) in the [United States Navy](20518076.md). The destroyer can operate with a Carrier Strike Group (CSG), Expeditionary Strike Group (ESG), as an element of a Surface Action Group (SAG), or independently. The ship can conduct a variety of missions in support of national military strategy. From peacetime presence and crisis management to sea control and power projection, 115 will be capable of carrying out Integrated Air and Missile Defense (IAMD), Undersea Warfare (USW), Surface Warfare (SW), and Strike Warfare (STW) in multi-threat environments.[^cite_note-7]

The \$679.6 million contract to build her was awarded on 26 September 2011 to [Bath Iron Works](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bath_Iron_Works) of [Bath, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bath,_Maine).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] On 15 February 2012, Secretary of the Navy [Ray Mabus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Mabus) announced the ship's named to be *Rafael Peralta* in honor of Marine [Rafael Peralta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Peralta), who was petitioned for the [Medal of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_of_Honor) for shielding several Marines from a grenade in November 2004 during the [Iraq War](5043324.md); however, he was posthumously awarded the [Navy Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navy_Cross) instead.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

## Namesake

[Rafael Peralta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Peralta) was born in [Mexico City](18987.md) and immigrated to the United States as a child. Peralta joined the [United States Marine Corps](17349325.md) when he received his green card in 2000 and became a U.S. citizen while serving in the Marine Corps. Peralta was killed during the [Second Battle of Fallujah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Fallujah) in Iraq when he was wounded by small-arms fire while clearing houses with his fellow Marines. The insurgents threw a hand grenade into the room. Despite being injured, Peralta pulled the grenade underneath his body (thus absorbing most of the blast), killing him instantly and saving his fellow Marines. For his actions, Peralta was recommended for the Medal of Honor but was posthumously awarded the Navy Cross instead.

## Design

*Rafael Peralta* is the 65th ship of the *Arleigh Burke* class of destroyers, the first of which, [USS *Arleigh Burke*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Arleigh_Burke), was commissioned in July 1991.[^cite_note-12] With 75 ships planned to be built in total, the class has the longest production run for any U.S. Navy surface combatant warship.[^cite_note-13] As an *Arleigh Burke*-class ship, *Rafael Peralta*'s roles will include [anti-aircraft](146640.md), [anti-submarine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-submarine_warfare), and [anti-surface warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-surface_warfare), as well as strike operations.[^cite_note-FAS.org-6] During its long production run, the class was built in three flights—Flight I (DDG-51 to DDG-76), Flight II (DDG-72 to DDG-78), and Flight IIA (DDG-79 onward).[^cite_note-14] *Rafael Peralta* is a Flight IIA ship, and as such, features several improvements in terms of [ballistic missile](38025.md) defense, an embarked [air wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wing_\(military_aviation_unit\)), and the inclusion of [mine-detecting](2206580.md) ability.[^cite_note-FAS.org-6]

## Construction and career

By January 2014, the aft portion of the ship had been completed and had begun outfitting[^cite_note-15] and she was laid down on 30 October 2014.[^cite_note-Keel_laid-1][^cite_note-16]

The ship was christened on 31 October 2015 at Bath Iron Works by Rosa Maria Peralta, Sgt. Peralta's mother.[^cite_note-Christened-2][^cite_note-17] In February 2017, the ship was accepted by the United States Navy.[^cite_note-Delivered-3]



Commissioning of *Rafael Peralta* on 29 July 2017

*Rafael Peralta* was commissioned at [Naval Air Station North Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Air_Station_North_Island) in [San Diego, California](28504.md) on 29 July 2017[^cite_note-Commissioned-4] and was homeported at [Naval Base San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Base_San_Diego)[^cite_note-AP-18] before moving to Japan in 2021.

The *Arleigh Burke*-class guided-missile destroyers *Rafael Peralta* and [USS *Russell*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Russell_\(DDG-59\)) arrived in [Sasebo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasebo,_Nagasaki) for a port visit on 8 February 2020.[^cite_note-19]

*Rafael Peralta* joined [HMAS *Stuart*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMAS_Stuart_\(FFH_153\)), [HMAS *Sirius*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMAS_Sirius), [KDB *Darulehsan*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KDB_Darulehsan) and [RSS *Supreme*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSS_Supreme) on their way to [Pearl Harbor, Hawaii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl_Harbor,_Hawaii) in preparation for [RIMPAC 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exercise_RIMPAC#RIMPAC_2020) on 6 August 2020.[^cite_note-20]

During [Operation Epic Fury](82533582.md), *Rafael Peralta* had been deployed as part of the [2026 United States naval blockade of Iran](82937256.md) and had been used to turn ships entering or exiting the Iranian ports to turn around or return to port.[^cite_note-21]
