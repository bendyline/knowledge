**Autumn Cheyenne Durald Arkapaw** [ASC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Society_of_Cinematographers) (née **Durald**; born December 14, 1979)[^cite_note-auto3-1] is an American cinematographer. For her work on the film *[Sinners](75895455.md)* (2025), she became the first [woman of color](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Person_of_color) to be nominated, and the first woman and first Black person to win, an [Academy Award for Best Cinematography](61834.md). On television, she received a nomination at the [Primetime Creative Arts Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award) for [Outstanding Cinematography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Cinematography_for_a_Series_\(One_Hour\)) for her work on the *[Loki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loki_\(TV_series\))* episode "[Lamentis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamentis)" (2021).

## Early life and education

Arkapaw was born Autumn Cheyenne Durald in [Oxnard, California](108337.md),[^cite_note-2] grew up in the [San Francisco Bay Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Bay_Area), and is of [Filipino](2298105.md) descent on her mother's side and [Black Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creoles_of_color) on her father's side.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-auto-4] The Filipino side of her family is [Catholic](606848.md).[^cite_note-5] She has described her maternal grandfather, Guillermo Pagan Bautista of [Pampanga](255602.md), who was a resistance fighter during the [Japanese occupation of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_occupation_of_the_Philippines) and a survivor of the [Bataan Death March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bataan_Death_March), "the most important man in my life" and "one of the biggest influences on my childhood and my family".[^cite_note-\:0-3] She has a tattoo of her grandfather’s initials, in *[Baybayin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baybayin)* script, on her wrist.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

Arkapaw graduated from [San Ramon Valley High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Ramon_Valley_High_School#Notable_alumni) in [Danville, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danville,_California) in 1996.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-danville-7] She graduated in 2002 from [Loyola Marymount University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_Marymount_University) where she studied art history.[^cite_note-rockthetribes-8] She graduated from the [AFI Conservatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFI_Conservatory)'s cinematography program in 2009.[^cite_note-9]

## Career

After graduating from college, Arkapaw worked in advertising and later as a camera assistant on films.[^cite_note-rockthetribes-8] In 2019, she was invited to join the Cinematographers Branch of the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences).[^cite_note-10] In 2022, she became a member of the [American Society of Cinematographers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Society_of_Cinematographers).[^cite_note-11]

Arkapaw shot the 2025 film *[Sinners](75895455.md)* on [65 mm film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70_mm_film) using a combination of [IMAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX) 15-perf and [Ultra Panavision 70](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra_Panavision_70) cameras,[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] which made her the first female director of photography to shoot any movie on large format [IMAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX) film.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] For her work on *Sinners*, she became the first woman of color (Arkapaw is of [Filipino](2298105.md) and [Black Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creoles_of_color) descent) to be nominated for the [Academy Award for Best Cinematography](61834.md).[^cite_note-auto-4][^cite_note-auto2-16] She won the award at the [98th Academy Awards](79369474.md), becoming the first woman, first [black person](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_people), and first [Filipina](2298105.md) to win in the category.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

Arkapaw was awarded the AFI [Franklin J. Schaffner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_J._Schaffner) Alumni Medal at the 2026 AFI Life Achievement Award Gala.[^cite_note-20]

## Personal life

Arkapaw married Australian cinematographer [Adam Arkapaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Arkapaw) in 2015.[^cite_note-21] They have one child.[^cite_note-22]
