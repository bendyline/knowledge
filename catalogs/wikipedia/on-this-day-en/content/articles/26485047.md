&#x20;**Al Dabbah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الدبة), also known as *Ed Debba*, *El Debba*, *El Debbah* or *Ed Debbah*, is a town in [Sudan](27421.md) on the river banks of the [Nile](21244.md), which is served by the [Al Dabbah Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Debba_Airport). It has an estimated population of 52,000.[^cite_note-1]

## History

On 7 February 2025, the Sudanese military repelled a drone attack by the RSF on the town, shooting down seven drones.[^cite_note-2]
