# 2026 FIFA World Cup Group H

&#x20; Group H of the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) took place from June 15 to 26, 2026.[^cite_note-match_schedule-1] The group consisted of [Spain](609825.md), [Cape Verde](1205575.md), [Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia_national_football_team), and [Uruguay](679782.md).

Spain finished at the top of the group with two group game wins, allowing them to advance to the [round of 32](81667041.md), where they defeated multiple teams in the [knockout stage](81667005.md) and became champions of this World Cup.

Cape Verde finished as runners-up after drawing all three of its group matches, becoming the first World Cup debutant (and the smallest nation) to advance to the knockout stage since [Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia_national_football_team) in [2010](168079.md).

Uruguay finished third in the group and were eliminated, as their performance meant it would be impossible for them to be among the top eight third-place teams. Saudi Arabia finished fourth based on goal difference, and were also eliminated.[^cite_note-regulations-2]

### Spain vs Cape Verde

The teams had never met before. The fixture marked Cape Verde's debut at the FIFA World Cup. The country was the third-smallest by population to qualify for the tournament, before [Curaçao](24862626.md) achieved qualification.

In the first half Spain's [Ferran Torres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferran_Torres) hit the bar with a shot from close range after a header back from [Marc Cucurella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Cucurella) on the left. In the second half [Mikel Oyarzabal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikel_Oyarzabal) had a shot blocked by [Pico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pico_Lopes).[^cite_note-8] Late on [Diney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diney_\(footballer,_born_1995\)) almost put Cape Verde into the lead when he headed a corner straight at Spanish goalkeeper [Unai Simón](58209770.md) with the game finishing 0–0 and producing one of the biggest shocks of the World Cup, with [Vozinha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vozinha) as the Man of the Match for making seven saves in his World Cup debut.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

Cape Verde committed a single foul during this game, the fewest in a FIFA World Cup match since data collection began in 1966.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

### Saudi Arabia vs Uruguay



Pre-match ceremony between Saudi Arabia and Uruguay.

The teams had previously faced each other thrice, most recently in [Uruguay's 1–0 win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_A#Uruguay_v_Saudi_Arabia) at the [2018 FIFA World Cup](13327177.md).[^cite_note-16]

In the 41st minute, Saudi Arabia went in front, [Hassan Al-Tambakti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Al-Tambakti)'s header from a corner on the right was parried out by Uruguay keeper [Fernando Muslera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Muslera) where [Abdulelah Al-Amri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulelah_Al-Amri) finished the rebound with his right foot to the net from close range. With ten minutes to go, [Maximiliano Araújo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximiliano_Ara%C3%BAjo) scored for Uruguay with a shot from the left after [Mohammed Al-Owais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_Al-Owais) had initially made a save to make it 1–1, which also became the final result of the affair.[^cite_note-17]

### Spain vs Saudi Arabia

The teams had met three times, including their first encounter in [Spain's 1–0 group stage victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_H#Saudi_Arabia_vs_Spain) at the [2006 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup).[^cite_note-20] Spain won all matches, most recently a 5–0 friendly in 2012.[^cite_note-21]

The fixture was Saudi Arabia's worst defeat at the World Cup since their [5−0 loss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_A#Russia_vs_Saudi_Arabia) to hosts [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia_national_football_team) in [2018](13327177.md), and their third-largest defeat in tournament history, tied with their 4–0 losses against hosts [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_C#France_vs_Saudi_Arabia) in [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup) and [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_H#Saudi_Arabia_vs_Ukraine) in [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup).

### Uruguay vs Cape Verde



[Hard Rock Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_Rock_Stadium) during the match.

The two teams had never met before.

In the 21st minute, [Kevin Pina](68633042.md) scored with a low long range right footed free-kick from 30 yards that went through the middle of the defensive wall and into the bottom right corner of the net, it was the first ever goal for Cape Verde in the World Cup Finals. [Maximiliano Araújo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximiliano_Ara%C3%BAjo) made it 1–1 the 44th minute when he dived low to head to the net from close range after [Sidny Lopes Cabral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidny_Lopes_Cabral) had headed against his own post.[^cite_note-24] In first-half stoppage time, [Agustín Canobbio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agust%C3%ADn_Canobbio) put Uruguay into a 2–1 lead when he finished low to the net from six yards out after a headed pass from Araújo from the left.

In the 61st minute it was 2–2 when a mistake by [Mathías Olivera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Math%C3%ADas_Olivera) trying to play the ball across his own box allowed [Hélio Varela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9lio_Varela) to intercept and touch the ball to the side of Uruguay goalkeeper [Fernando Muslera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Muslera) before side footing into the empty net from outside the penalty area on the right.[^cite_note-25]

## Discipline

The team conduct ("fair play") score would have been used as a tiebreaker if the head-to-head and overall records of teams were tied. It would also be used as a tiebreaker for the [third-place ranking](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams) between groups if the overall records of teams were tied. The score was calculated based on yellow and red cards received by players and team officials in all group matches as follows:[^cite_note-regulations-2]

- yellow card: −1 point;

- indirect red card (second yellow card): −3 points;

- direct red card: −4 points;

- yellow card and direct red card: −5 points;

Only one of the above deductions could be applied to a player or team official in a single match.
