The **Head of Kyiv City** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Київський міський голова, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *Kyivskyi miskyi holova*), unofficially and more commonly the **Mayor of Kyiv** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Мер Києва, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *Mer Kyieva*), is a city official elected by popular vote who serves as a head of the [City State Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_City_State_Administration) of [Kyiv](585629.md) (the capital of [Ukraine](31750.md)) and a chairperson the [Kyiv City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_City_Council).

The mayor is elected for the term of four years.[^cite_note-KMKCMterm-2] Current mayor [Vitali Klitschko](555483.md) was sworn in on 5 June 2014.[^cite_note-KKMs5614-1] Klitschko was last reelected in the [2020 Kyiv local election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Kyiv_local_election) with 50.52% of the votes, in the first round of the election.[^cite_note-3131537Klitschko-3]

## Elections of mayor of Kyiv

In March 1990, Kyiv's "First Truly Democratic" elections were announced (see [Demokratizatsiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demokratizatsiya_\(Soviet_Union\)), part of Soviet [Perestroika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perestroika)).[^cite_note-ukrrudprom-4] [Dmytro Tabachnyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmytro_Tabachnyk) (ran as the [Communist party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Ukraine_\(Soviet_Union\)) candidate) and Mikhail Pogrebinsky (ran as an unaffiliated candidate) in an interview with [Segodnya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segodnya_\(1997\)) claimed that they were truly the most honest elections.[^cite_note-ukrrudprom-4] The elections took place alongside the [1990 Ukrainian Supreme Soviet election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Ukrainian_Supreme_Soviet_election).

Following the political crisis of 1993–94, snap elections were held for to elect the country's president and parliament, as well as local elections.

In 2006, according to the Kyiv City Electoral Commission, [Leonid Chernovetskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Chernovetskyi) won 31.83% of the popular vote, [Vitali Klitschko](555483.md) placed second with 23.7%, and incumbent [Oleksandr Omelchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleksandr_Omelchenko) placed third with 21.2%.[^cite_note-5]

As of December 2006, Chernovetskyi's rating had decreased to 8%. This was mostly due to his betrayal of those who elected him, most notably through his increasing of the price of household services (such as water and gas) by 340%.

However, Chernovetskyi won a second term as Mayor of Kyiv with 38% of the vote in the 25 May 2008 [snap local election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Kiev_mayoral_election), called by the [Verkhovna Rada](844993.md) in March.[^cite_note-6] From the resignation of Chernovetskyi[^cite_note-nocomeback-7][^cite_note-MARIA_DANILOVA-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-QofEngland-10] in July 2012 until fresh elections in 2014, Kyiv City Council Secretary and Deputy Mayor [Halyna Hereha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halyna_Hereha) was the acting Mayor of Kyiv.[^cite_note-risnennia_pro_mera-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-kmr.gov.ua-14]

In the [2014 Kyiv local election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Kyiv_local_election), [Vitali Klitschko](555483.md) was elected as mayor of Kyiv with almost 57% of the votes.[^cite_note-votecountKMEIU4614-15] Klitschko was sworn in as mayor on 5 June 2014.[^cite_note-KKMs5614-1][^cite_note-IUKe25214-16]

The [2015 Kyiv local election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Kyiv_local_election) (including mayoral elections) took place on 25 October 2015.[^cite_note-17] A second round of mayoral elections was held on 15 November 2015 between Klitschko and [Boryslav Bereza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boryslav_Bereza) after incumbent Mayor Klitschko scored 40.5% of the vote and Bereza 8.8% in the first round.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] Klitschko won the second round with 66.5%; Bereza gained 33.51% of the votes.[^cite_note-20]

The [2020 Kyiv local election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Kyiv_local_election) (including mayoral elections) took place on 25 October 2020.[^cite_note-ukrinform3123543KM-21] Incumbent Mayor Klitschko won the election with 50.52% of the votes, in the first round of the election. None of the other candidates had more than 10% of the vote.[^cite_note-3131537Klitschko-3]

## Dualism of authority in Kyiv

An October 2010 [Presidential](20688479.md) decree relieved then-mayor of Kyiv [Leonid Chernovetskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Chernovetskyi) of the office of [Head of Kyiv City Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_Kyiv_City_Administration), while still preserving the post of mayor.[^cite_note-Marta_Grimskaya-22] This led to Chernovetskyi being deprived of his official decision-making role and most power in the capital was handed over to the Head of Kyiv City Administration.[^cite_note-QofEngland-10][^cite_note-limited-23][^cite_note-2012electionsKiev-24] At the time that was [Oleksandr Popov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleksandr_Popov), who was appointed by President [Viktor Yanukovych](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Yanukovych) on 16 November 2010.[^cite_note-QofEngland-10][^cite_note-limited-23][^cite_note-2012electionsKiev-24] Before these amendments, the elected mayor of the [Kyiv City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_City_Council) was automatically appointed also as head of the Kyiv City Administration.[^cite_note-Marta_Grimskaya-22][^cite_note-Grimskaya-25]

Chernovetskyi was not seen in Kyiv for several months after Popov's appointment,[^cite_note-QofEngland-10][^cite_note-Finding_Cherno-26] but returned to the public eye in early 2011.[^cite_note-MARIA_DANILOVA2-27][^cite_note-Marta_Grimskaya-22] By that time, Chernovetskyi had become extremely unpopular among the residents of Kyiv.[^cite_note-unpopular-28]

Chernovetskyi tendered his resignation on 1 June 2012.[^cite_note-KievMayrno-29] The city council decided on 12 July 2012 that [Halyna Hereha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halyna_Hereha) would temporarily act as the mayor of the capital city.[^cite_note-KievMayrno-29] A petition to the [Ukrainian Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Parliament) on holding an early mayoral election in the city was sent (the dates of the early mayoral elections are set by Ukraine's parliament).[^cite_note-KievMayrno-29] New elections were held in 2014; [Vitali Klitschko](555483.md) was elected as mayor of Kyiv with almost 57% of the votes.[^cite_note-votecountKMEIU4614-15][^cite_note-30]

After 25 June 2014 the post of mayor of Kyiv and Head of Kyiv City Administration were being held by a single person again[^cite_note-Poroshenko_appoints_Klitschko_head_of_Kiev_city_administration_-_decree-31] after [Vitali Klitschko](555483.md), who had been sworn in as mayor on 5 June 2014,[^cite_note-KKMs5614-1] was appointed Head of Kyiv City Administration by [Ukrainian President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_President) [Petro Poroshenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petro_Poroshenko).[^cite_note-Poroshenko_appoints_Klitschko_head_of_Kiev_city_administration_-_decree-31] However, on 1 March 2022, during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine), President [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) introduced a [military administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_administration) in Kyiv, appointing [major general](36301471.md) Mykola Zhyrnov as wartime head of the city authority.[^cite_note-32] On 21 October 2022 Zhyrnov was replaced on his post by colonel general [Serhiy Popko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serhiy_Popko). Mayor Klitschko continues to execute his functions as head of Kyiv's civilian authority and is also part of the city's Defense Council.[^cite_note-chesnopost582-33]
