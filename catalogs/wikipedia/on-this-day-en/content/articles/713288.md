**Ica** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˈika\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish); [Quechua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechua_language): *Ika*) is a [department of Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Peru). It borders the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md) on the west; [Lima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lima_Region) to the north; [Huancavelica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huancavelica_Region) and [Ayacucho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayacucho_Region) to the east; and [Arequipa](713212.md) to the south. It is administered by a [regional government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Government_of_Ica). Its capital is the city of [Ica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ica_\(city\)).

## Geography

The Department of Ica has a remarkable geography. It is the only region of the southern coast formed by plains, also called coast plains, since the [Andean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andean) [Cordillera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordillera) rise up inland. Some [geological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geology) folds have determined the formation of dunes moving toward the sea, which form much of the [Paracas Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_Peninsula). Some isolated formations located at the southern part created the Marcona complex, with the biggest deposits of [iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron) in the [Pacific](23070.md) coast.

Ica's configuration is due to the [geomorphology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geomorphology) of its two big and unique fluvial watersheds: the [Pasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Pasco?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Ica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ica_River) rivers. Also, it has a waterway called the [Rio Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Grande_\(Peru\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), although its waters do not reach the ocean. Some waters are diverted for irrigation and agriculture in the provinces of [Palpa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palpa_Province), [Nazca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazca_Province) and Ingenio; the Rio Grande's final [riverbed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stream_bed) is dry since sand and dried lands absorb its limited resources. There are extensive [deserts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert) in Ica, such as the *[Lancha Pampas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancha_Pampas?action=edit\&redlink=1)*. *Pozo Santo* and *Villacuri* pampas are extremely hot areas. Strong and persistent winds called *paracas* are present and stir up large sandstorms.

Ica has a rich history. The first settlers are from 10,000 years ago, from which the [Wari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huari_culture), [Chincha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chincha_culture), [Nazca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazca), Ica and [Paracas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_culture) cultures developed, the latter being the most important.

The [Paracas culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_culture) developed from the seventh through the 2nd century BC. It is distinguished by its matchless [textile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile) skills, trephinations, and the art of [mummifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mummy) their dead.

The Nazca culture, on the contrary, well known for its artistic [pottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pottery), in which colorful designs and representations excel over the form, the same as their lines and figures that have undergone implausible interpretations. This culture expanded from the 2nd century BC through the 7th century AD. They have left us their wonderful aqueducts that made good use of underground water, of rivers and rain, showing a great knowledge of hydraulic engineering.

In the 15th century, during the [Inca Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inca_Empire), [Pachacuti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pachacuti) incorporated the territories of Ica, Nazca and the Chincha valley.

Years later, in 1563, with the arrival of the [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_people), Jerónimo Luis de Cabrera founded the Villa de Valverde del Valle de Ica. Since then, the area became an important [vineyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vineyard) and cotton center.

During the independence war, General [José de San Martín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_de_San_Mart%C3%ADn) landed in Paracas and fixed his headquarters in Pisco, to start the fight for the independence of Peru.

### City of Ica

Capital of the Ica Department. A very modern clean city that has Peruvian street markets, many old churches and landmarks, modern malls, hotels, coffee shops, theatres, and hotels as well.

### Huacachina

Located on the west side of the city of Ica, capital of the Ica Department. One of the most popular places to visit in Ica is La [Huacachina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huacachina). The desert oasis is located 5 km (3.1 mi) from Ica. It is a small lake with medicinal water, lying in the middle of a spectacular sand desert.

### Pisco

[Pisco](614013.md) is the most important port in Ica and a litoral province. The most important attractions within this province are likely [Paracas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_\(municipality\)), [Paracas Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_Bay) and the [Paracas National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_National_Reserve). Pisco was home of an ancient pre-Hispanic culture, [Paracas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_culture), who are known for their exquisite textiles.

### Paracas

[Paracas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_\(municipality\)) (a municipality within the [Paracas District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_District)) is a small town catering to tourism. It serves as the jumping point for tours to [Islas Ballestas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islas_Ballestas) and to [Paracas National Reservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_National_Reservation). [Paracas Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_Bay), protected by [Paracas Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_Peninsula), gives these shallow, warmer waters break from ocean waves permitting life to flourish, particularly near its south western edge encompassed within [Paracas National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_National_Reservation). The [Paracas Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_Museum?action=edit\&redlink=1), also found just near the south western edge of [Paracas Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_Bay), provides excellent information about [Paracas culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_culture) and the many unique species, in particular, the [birds of Paracas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_Birds?action=edit\&redlink=1).

### Nazca

The [Ica-Nazca culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ica-Nazca_culture) flourished along the southern coast of Peru from around 200 BC to 600 AD. This area is extremely dry. The Nazca developed extensive irrigation systems, including underground canals, that allowed them to farm the land. The Nazca are known for their textiles and pottery which feature images of animals and mythological beings.

They are even better known, however, for a unique set of creations known as the [Nazca Lines](216283.md), which are [geoglyphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoglyph) and geometric line clearings in the [Atacama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atacama) desert, in the district of [Nazca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazca). On a large, rock-strewn plain, the Nazca made huge drawings by scraping away stones to reveal the lighter soil underneath. The drawings depict various plants and animals, including humans, a monkey, birds, and other creatures, as well as lines and geometric shapes. These drawings are so huge, however, that they can be seen only from the sky. Scientists believe that the Nazca made these drawings for their gods. The area of the Nazca lines is called the Pampa Colorada (red plain).

### Cachiche

A small village near Ica, [Cachiche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cachiche) is well known for its history of witches. Doña Julia, Cachiche's first witch, was known to practice "good magic," curing and helping villagers with her spells. Near the entrance to the town, a carving from a single huarango tree [^cite_note-1] depicts this first "bruja de Cachiche" (witch of Cachiche).

## Tourism

Ica has significant [wine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wine) and [pisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisco) industries, annual fiestas, a museum and historic colonial churches.[^cite_note-LP-2] The climate is generally sunny and dry due to its elevation above coastal fog and mist.[^cite_note-LP-2] As of 2020, the [Peruvian desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sechura_Desert), around the [Huacachina Oasis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huacachina_Oasis?action=edit\&redlink=1), has gotten significantly popular among tourists for sandboarding and sand buggy tours.

There is also a Regional Museum, which exhibits prehistoric artefacts as well as paintings and furniture that date back to the Spanish Colonial era. Moreover, in the museum, mummies with typical [Paracas culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracas_culture) skulls can be found.[^cite_note-3]
