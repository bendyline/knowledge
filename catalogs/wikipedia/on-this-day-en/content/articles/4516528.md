The **2008 NBA Finals** was the [championship series](890793.md) of the [National Basketball Association](22093.md)'s (NBA) [2007–08 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_NBA_season) and conclusion of [the season's playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_NBA_playoffs). A [best-of-seven playoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best-of-seven_playoff) series that was played from June 5–17, 2008, the series was contested between the [Eastern Conference](1305847.md) champion [Boston Celtics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_Boston_Celtics_season) and the [Western Conference](28141073.md) champion [Los Angeles Lakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_Los_Angeles_Lakers_season). It was the eleventh Finals meeting in the history of the [Celtics–Lakers rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celtics%E2%80%93Lakers_rivalry). The Celtics defeated the Lakers in six games to win their first championship since [1986](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_NBA_Finals) and their seventeenth title overall.&#x20;

[Paul Pierce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Pierce) was named the [Finals MVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Finals_Most_Valuable_Player_Award). Rodd Houston narrated the Boston Celtics' championship season documentary on [NBA Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Entertainment). The made-for-TV version of this documentary is narrated by [Kevin Harlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Harlan).

The Celtics would not win another championship until [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_NBA_Finals).

## Background

See also: [Celtics-Lakers rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celtics-Lakers_rivalry)

The Celtics made their first NBA Finals appearance since a six-game loss to the Lakers in [1987](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_NBA_Finals). Over the next 20 years the Celtics endured lean years, not making the playoffs in nine of those years. Following the dissolution of the team's original "Big Three" through the retirements of [Larry Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Bird) and [Kevin McHale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_McHale_\(basketball\)) and the departure of [Robert Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Parish), the Celtics suffered through several tragedies such as the passing of [Reggie Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reggie_Lewis) in 1993; a franchise-worst 15-win [1996–97 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996%E2%80%9397_NBA_season); the ill-fated hiring of head coach [Rick Pitino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Pitino); and the deaths of franchise patriarch [Red Auerbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Auerbach) and former player [Dennis Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Johnson), which culminated in a 24-win [2006–07 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006%E2%80%9307_NBA_season), highlighted by a franchise-worst 18 straight losses.

Nevertheless, the 2007 off-season saw Celtics general manager [Danny Ainge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Ainge) acquire [Ray Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Allen) and [Kevin Garnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Garnett) to join franchise star [Paul Pierce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Pierce). This new formation became regarded as the most talented Boston Celtics team since the duo of [Antoine Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoine_Walker) and [Paul Pierce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Pierce) in the early 2000s. The newly formed 'Big Three,' joined by second year point guard and future all star [Rajon Rondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajon_Rondo), led the Celtics to a dramatic 42-game turnaround, finishing with 66 wins.



Exterior view of the [TD Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TD_Garden) ahead of Game 2 of the 2008 NBA Finals.

In the playoffs, the Celtics were pushed to the brink by the [Atlanta Hawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Hawks) and [Cleveland Cavaliers](72868.md), and despite not winning on the road, managed to prevail in a pair of Game 7s on their [home court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TD_Garden). The Celtics would finally break their road woes in a six-game win against the perennial Eastern Conference powerhouse [Detroit Pistons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_Pistons), earning their first finals berth since 1987.

After losing to the Pistons in the [2004 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_NBA_Finals), despite a talent-laden roster, [Phil Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Jackson) abruptly retired. Soon after, the Lakers decided to rebuild by trading away [Shaquille O'Neal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaquille_O'Neal) to the [Miami Heat](72850.md) for [Lamar Odom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamar_Odom), [Caron Butler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caron_Butler) and [Brian Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Grant). The [2004–05 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004%E2%80%9305_NBA_season) saw the Lakers miss the playoffs for only the fifth time in team history. Even though he had written a book called *[The Last Season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Season:_A_Team_in_Search_of_Its_Soul)*, going as far as calling [Kobe Bryant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_Bryant) "uncoachable," Jackson returned to the Lakers for the [2005–06 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005%E2%80%9306_NBA_season). Jackson and Bryant would patch up their differences, but after a pair of first-round exits, Bryant demanded, but later retracted, a trade in the off-season.

The Lakers' 2007–08 season saw the team win 57 games, along the way adding [Spanish](26667.md) forward [Pau Gasol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pau_Gasol) in mid-season while [Andrew Bynum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Bynum) was recovering from a mid-season knee injury. The Lakers eliminated the [Denver Nuggets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver_Nuggets) 4–0, then the [Utah Jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Jazz) 4–2 and then dethroned the erstwhile defending champion [San Antonio Spurs](72889.md) 4–1 in the conference finals, making their 29th NBA Finals appearance.

On December 30, 2007, the Celtics routed the Lakers at the [Staples Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crypto.com_Arena) on throwback jersey night by a score of 110–91. The Lakers also played the first half in throwback shorts, but went back to their regular shorts at halftime after they were down big.[^cite_note-1] With the win, the Celtics also prevented Lakers coach Phil Jackson from surpassing [Red Auerbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Auerbach) for seventh all-time in coaching wins.[^cite_note-2] With it being the first time the Celtics Big 3 visited the Lakers as a unit and it being throwback jersey night, there was a lot of emotion in the building; as such, there were seven technical fouls assessed to both teams in the game.[^cite_note-3] The Celtics moved to 26–3, which continued one of their best starts in franchise history.

The game was also a harbinger of things to come in the 2008 Finals. Bryant scored 22 points, but did so inefficiently, going 6-for-25.[^cite_note-4] This would be a theme in the Finals, as Bryant still was able to score 25.7 PPG, but shot just 40 percent from the field. On the other end, [Paul Pierce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Pierce) scored 33 points and [Kevin Garnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Garnett) shut down the Lakers' big men, two things that continued in the Finals. A little over one month after this game, the Lakers traded for Memphis Grizzlies star forward [Pau Gasol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pau_Gasol), largely in part to match-up with Garnett.[^cite_note-5]

Game 1 running score.

Going into the game, LA have found themselves on the road in game 1 for the first time that playoffs. On the other hand, Boston, who were the league's best team, had home court advantage for the series. The game started very tight, with teams trading buckets and turnovers. For Boston, the go-to guy was [Kevin Garnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Garnett), who had racked up 10 first-quarter points. For the Lakers, Kobe was struggling with his shot, as he went 1/7 for the quarter, but was dishing out assists and playing defense very solid, as he looked to get others involved early. Paul Pierce was also struggling, and has gotten himself into foul trouble early, as he had 3 points and 3 fouls going into the third quarter.

In the second quarter, Garnett had racked up 8 more points and had already matched his playoff points average at 18.8. Despite Garnett's monstrous efforts, the Celtics found themselves down 51–46 at halftime. The main reason for the Lakers lead was [Pau Gasol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pau_Gasol), who was hitting tough fadeaways and layups, and had also gotten P.J. Brown in foul trouble with his physicality and footwork. On the other hand, Kobe Bryant was a non-factor offensively but was making up for it with the same kind of defense that earned him the [1st team All-defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_All-Defensive_Team). The Lakers ended the half on a 10–2 run.

In the beginning of the third quarter, Celtics forward [Paul Pierce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Pierce) was injured after a collision with Celtics center and teammate, [Kendrick Perkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendrick_Perkins). He was taken out of the game with 8\:42 left in the quarter. The following offensive possession, Perkins was hit in the leg by the sneaker of Pau Gasol. He soon also left the game. But despite the injuries of their star players, the Celtics went on a 6–0 run and have regained the lead. But then Paul Pierce miraculously came back in the game, reinvigorating the crowd, and carried the Boston Celtics in the third quarter. Following his return Paul Pierce would shoot 6-6 from the field and hit three straight three pointers. Not to ignore the fact that Kobe exploded in that third quarter, hitting and defending shots from anywhere inside the arc, before getting himself in foul trouble late in the third. Paul Pierce scored 15 points on 4-4 shooting from the field in the third quarter to give Boston the lead going into the fourth.



Players lineup prior to Game 2 for the national anthem at the TD Garden

Then in the fourth, the Celtics never trailed. Kobe sitting out the first half of the quarter due to foul trouble allowed Boston to slowly increase the lead all the way up to 10. Garnett's putback dunk over 2 Lakers defenders sealed the Celtics victory, completing his 24 points alongside 13 rebounds performance. For the Lakers, [Kobe Bryant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_Bryant) had 24 points. The Boston Celtics ended up winning game 1 with a final score of 98–88.

Pierce apparently injured his knee by falling awkwardly on Kendrick Perkins' leg, and was taken off the court in a wheelchair. Despite what Pierce's reaction suggested was a highly debilitating injury, he returned to action minutes later to raucous cheering from the crowd. Pierce admitted during coverage of the [2019 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_NBA_Finals) that he just had to use the bathroom.[^cite_note-7] He soon hit two three-pointers on consecutive offensive possessions that gave Boston the lead for good and finished with 22 points. It was later dubbed by some as the "Wheelchair Game".[^cite_note-8]

The Lakers, who had had home court advantage throughout the first three rounds and had not trailed a series in that same time, now had to do without both luxuries for the first time.

The opening tipoff of Game 2

From the tip-off, this game showed the Lakers' need for a win. The Lakers established an early 15–8 lead in the first quarter, highlighted by an early play where [Pau Gasol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pau_Gasol) scored a drop step, and-one poster layup and drew a foul against [Kevin Garnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Garnett) after a defensive rebound by [Kobe Bryant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_Bryant). Boston responded by substituting sophomore forward [Leon Powe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Powe), whose aggressive, physical style of play of drawing fouls drew ten free-throw attempts within his first three minutes on the court, helping the Celtics close the deficit. Despite Powe's heroics, the Lakers maintained their advantage to end the first quarter ahead 22–20.

In the second quarter, the Celtics decided to up the pace, and were able to rack up 10 unanswered points, as their defense has stopped LA's offense who haven't scored for the first 3 minutes of the quarter. The Celtics' defense also forced 9 turnovers by the Lakers (for reference, they had 8 turnovers in all of game 1) Paul Pierce decided to make up for his poor offensive efforts in the first half of game 1, as he scored 16 points in the first half. For the Lakers, Kobe got himself into foul trouble and the technical, which he got for taunting [Ray Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Allen) after making a tough lefty layup over him, didn't help. The Lakers team effort was the only thing keeping them alive in this game. The first half ended with the [Boston Celtics](43376.md) holding a commanding 54–42 lead.

The Celtics have held the Lakers scoreless until 7\:52 remaining in the third as their lead began getting bigger and bigger. The game fell for the Celtics, including back-to-back triples by Paul Pierce and Ray Allen. The Celtics built up a comfortable 20 point lead to end the third. The exclamation point on the blowout was Leon Powe with back-to-back poster slams over the Lakers' defense.



[Leon Powe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Powe) (center) had a memorable performance in Game 2

The Celtics' dominance transferred into the beginning of the fourth, as they jumped to a 24-point lead with little bit more than 7 minutes to play. Vladimir Radmanović and Saša Vujačić started shooting the lights out of the Celtics defense which had fallen asleep in the fourth, and before you knew it, the lead was down to 12. Kobe's shots also started falling, as he scored 22 points in the second half alone. Phil Jackson then decided to go small and has left only Lamar Odom on the court of the big men, and has subbed in 4 of the best shooters that he had. That has showed out to be very effective, as the Lakers would go on a historic 31–9 run spanning 7\:34 to the final 38.4 seconds. But all that effort was nothing for Paul Pierce, who seemed unfazed by the pressure, and who has scored 2 free throws with 20 seconds remaining, and then blocked Vujačić's three to potentially make it a one-point game. After the defensive play, James Posey hit two free throws to make it a 6-point game, and then the Celtics proved to have the league's best defense by forcing another Lakers turnover. After a hard-fought battle, the Celtics walked away with a 6-point win and the 2–0 series lead as the series shifted to L.A.

[Kobe Bryant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_Bryant) finished the game with 30 points and 8 assists. For the Celtics, Paul Pierce scored 28 points, Rajon Rondo had a game-high 16 assists and [Leon Powe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Powe) scored 21 points on 6–7 shooting from the field and 9–13 from the line in 15 minutes of play, including back-to-back dunks in the last minute of the 3rd quarter, causing the Boston crowd to chant his name. Over the course of the game, Powe shot 13 free throws while the Lakers shot 10.

Despite injuries suffered by Pierce (sprained knee) and [Kendrick Perkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendrick_Perkins) (high ankle sprain), both players started in Game 2 and appeared to be mostly unhampered by the injuries, especially Pierce who finished with 28 points.

Boston finished the game 27-for-38 from the line, while the Lakers were 10-for-10. Some analysts viewed this as favorable treatment toward the Celtics,[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] while others noted that a difference in playing styles may have led to the discrepancy, and that the actual foul discrepancy was only 28–21 in favor of Boston.[^cite_note-11]

[Boston Celtics](43376.md) fans, players, coaching personnel, and staff celebrate the franchise's 17th title on June 17



The Celtics needed 26 games to win their 17th title. By a twist of fate, Coach [Doc Rivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doc_Rivers) played for the 1993–94 [New York Knicks](72855.md), which held the previous record for most playoff games played in one season, with 25.

Entering Game 6, the Celtics set a record of most playoff games played in one season, with 26, breaking the previous record of 25 set by both the [1994 New York Knicks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993-94_New_York_Knicks_season), whom Celtics Coach [Doc Rivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doc_Rivers) played for, and the [2005 Detroit Pistons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004%E2%80%9305_Detroit_Pistons_season), both of whom lost in their respective finals in seven games (Knicks in [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_NBA_Finals), Pistons in [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_NBA_Finals)).[^cite_note-cnnsi-2008-15][^cite_note-MostGames-16] However, for the 1994 Knicks, the first round was a best-of-five. Since the NBA Finals used the 2-3-2 format from [1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_NBA_Finals) until [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_NBA_Finals), no team has ever won the last two games on the road.[^cite_note-17]

After a rocky first quarter, the Celtics dominated the rest of the game. Maintaining a lead of more than 25 points, the Celtics' Big Three performed phenomenally, while the whole team smothered the Lakers' offense with their tight defense. Boston dominated in numerous statistical categories, including rebounds (48–29, with a 14–2 disparity in offensive boards), turnovers (7–19), steals (18–4), assists (33–16) and blocks (4–0). Five Celtics finished in double figures. [Ray Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Allen) hit seven three-pointers to tie what was then the Finals record (which he subsequently broke during the [2010 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_NBA_Finals) against the Lakers during game two), [Rajon Rondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajon_Rondo) had an all-around spectacular performance (21 points, 8 rebounds, 7 assists, 6 steals), the Celtics only turned the ball over seven times and set a Finals record with 18 steals, and every Boston player who saw action scored.[^cite_note-globe-18]

[Kevin Garnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Garnett) delivered his famous "anything is possible" speech during an interview with [Michele Tafoya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michele_Tafoya) directly after the game.[^cite_note-19]

The 39-point margin of victory was the largest ever in an NBA championship-clinching game, breaking the old record of 33, also set by the Celtics over the Lakers in Game Five of the [1965 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1965_NBA_Finals), 129–96.[^cite_note-cnnsi-2008-15] This lead was close to the Finals point-spread record set in Game 3 in [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_NBA_Finals) where a [Michael Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jordan)-led [Chicago Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Bulls) team beat the [Utah Jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Jazz) by 42 points, 96–54.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] The Celtics also improved their overall record against the Lakers to 9–2 in Finals meetings, beating them in the Finals for the first time since [1984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984_NBA_Finals).[^cite_note-globe-18]

This was the Celtics' 17th championship, their first since [1986](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_NBA_Finals), extending their [record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NBA_champions) for most NBA championships won by a single team. All this capped off the Celtics' best regular season (66–16) since their [previous championship season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985-86_Boston_Celtics_season) in which they went 67–15. It was also a sense of relief, as the Celtics set an NBA record for most playoff games ever needed to win a championship, with 26, surpassing the previous record of 24 by the Lakers in [1988](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_NBA_Finals).[^cite_note-cnnsi-2008-15]

The Celtics' win was also seen as an addition to the recent success of [Boston-area sports teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_in_Boston), following the wins by the [New England Patriots](21719.md) in Super Bowls [XXXVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXXVI), [XXXVIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXXVIII), and [XXXIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXXIX) (2001, 2003 and 2004 seasons) and the [Red Sox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Red_Sox)'s World Series wins in [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_World_Series) (death of the [Curse of the Bambino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curse_of_the_Bambino)) and [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_World_Series).

The Celtics won this series by winning Games 1, 2, 4, and 6; coincidentally, the last time the two teams met in [1987](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_NBA_Finals), the Lakers won that series in identical fashion. It was also the same identical fashion when the Celtics won the NBA Finals in [1986](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_NBA_Finals) before this championship.

## Broadcasting

The Finals, produced by [ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_on_ESPN), were aired on [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_on_ABC) in the United States for the sixth consecutive year. This was also [Michele Tafoya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michele_Tafoya)'s last Finals appearance as a sideline reporter due to her resignation shortly before the [2008–09 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008%E2%80%9309_NBA_season).[^cite_note-StarTrib1-23] She was later succeeded by [Doris Burke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doris_Burke) (2009–19), [Rachel Nichols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Nichols_\(journalist\)) (2020), [Malika Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malika_Andrews) (2021), and [Lisa Salters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Salters) (2022–present).

Local TV broadcasts of the competing franchises were Los Angeles' [KABC-TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KABC-TV) and Boston's [WCVB-TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WCVB-TV).

## Aftermath

Further information: [2010 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_NBA_Finals)



The 2008 NBA champion Boston Celtics with President George W. Bush at the White House

In the [2008–09 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008-09_NBA_season), the [Celtics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008-09_Boston_Celtics_season) got off to an impressive start with a 27–2 record, marking the best start through 29 games in NBA history. However, their momentum halted when they faced the Lakers on [Christmas Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Christmas_games) in a fateful finals rematch, bringing an end to their franchise-high 19-game winning streak. Shortly after the [2009 All-Star Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_NBA_All-Star_Game), [Kevin Garnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Garnett) suffered a devastating right knee injury which sidelined him for the remainder of the season. Despite finishing the season with 62 wins, the absence of Garnett had a noticeable impact on the team's performance as they were eliminated in seven games by the [Dwight Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwight_Howard)-led [Orlando Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008-09_Orlando_Magic_season) in the [2009 Eastern Conference semifinals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_NBA_playoffs#Conference_semifinals).

The Lakers experienced a resurgence in the subsequent season, winning 65 games and clinching the [2009 NBA championship title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_NBA_Finals) in five games after overcoming the [Dwight Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwight_Howard)-led [Orlando Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008-09_Orlando_Magic_season). Even though another mid-season injury to [Andrew Bynum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Bynum) threatened their title drive, he did come back late in the season. The [2008–09 Los Angeles Lakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008-09_Los_Angeles_Lakers_season) became the first team since the [1988–89 Detroit Pistons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988-89_Detroit_Pistons_season) to win the NBA Finals after losing it in the previous year.

Both teams would eventually meet again in [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_NBA_Finals), where the [Lakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009-10_Los_Angeles_Lakers_season) exacted revenge by finding themselves pitted against the [Boston Celtics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009-10_Boston_Celtics_season) for the second time in three years. The Lakers avenged their 2008 NBA Finals loss and repeated as NBA Champions after besting Boston in seven games, winning their fifth NBA Championship title in the last ten seasons. The Lakers would not return to the NBA Finals again until a decade later, where the [2019–2020 team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Los_Angeles_Lakers_season), led by [LeBron James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LeBron_James) and [Anthony Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Davis), won the [NBA championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_NBA_Finals). The Celtics would not return to make another appearance in the Finals again until 12 years later in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NBA_Finals), where the [Jaylen Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaylen_Brown) and [Jayson Tatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayson_Tatum)-led [team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021-22_Boston_Celtics_season) eventually succumbed to the [Stephen Curry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Curry)-led [Golden State Warriors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021-22_Golden_State_Warriors_season) in six games. Two years thereafter, the [Celtics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023-24_Boston_Celtics_season) would reach the NBA Finals yet again in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_NBA_Finals), emerging victorious in a five-game series over the [Luka Dončić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luka_Don%C4%8Di%C4%87) and [Kyrie Irving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrie_Irving)-led [Dallas Mavericks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023-24_Dallas_Mavericks_season), garnering their first championship title in 16 years and 18th overall, effectively consolidating their position as the NBA franchise with the [highest number of NBA championship titles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NBA_champions#Results_by_team).[^cite_note-25] On the 2024 Celtics championship team as an assistant coach was 2008 Celtics alum [Sam Cassell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Cassell).
