The **Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture – Drama** is a [Golden Globe Award](142724.md) that has been awarded annually from 1944 until 2023 by the [Hollywood Foreign Press Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Foreign_Press_Association) (HFPA).[^cite_note-1] Since its institution in 1943, the Hollywood Foreign Press Association is an organization of journalists who cover the [film industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_industry) in the United States, but are affiliated with publications outside North America. Since 2023, the award is now presented by [Dick Clark Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Clark_Productions).

When the awards were introduced, there was a single category for Best Picture. Starting with the [9th Golden Globe Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_Golden_Globe_Awards), the Golden Globes split the Acting and Best Picture awards into Drama and [Musical or Comedy](186063.md) categories.[^cite_note-histawards-2] Since 1951, the only time the awards were reunified was in 1953.[^cite_note-nonoms53-3]

This award goes to the [producers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_producer) of the film. In the following lists, the first titles listed are winners. These are also in **bold** and in blue background; those not in bold are nominees.

The years given are those in which the films under consideration were released, not the year of the ceremony, which always takes place the following year.
