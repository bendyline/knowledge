**Umm Ruwaba**, also **Umm Ruwabah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): أم روابة; **Ruaba** and **Umm Ruaba**), is a city in the [state](579458.md) of [North Kordofan](767669.md) in [Sudan](27421.md) and is the capital of the [Um Rawaba District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Um_Rawaba_District). By road it is located 147 kilometres (91 mi) southeast of [El Obeid](1481380.md), and 184 kilometres (114 mi) west of [Rabak](14063078.md).[^cite_note-1] Founded by the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md) in late 1820s , it is at the junction of important roads and camel caravan routes.[^cite_note-Lavergné1989-2]

## History

On 27 April 2013, the town was attacked by the [Sudan Revolutionary Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_Revolutionary_Front), a rebel alliance. The government was able to 'contain' the attack, but sparked local protests over lack of security in the region.[^cite_note-3]

## Geography

Umm Ruwaba lies in southern central Sudan in the semi-arid region of the [Sahel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahel). It has an average annual precipitation of 300 to 450 [millimeters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millimeters).[^cite_note-4] Local aquifers are considered to be productive and are the source of water for many wells in the region.[^cite_note-Geological_Survey_Water-supply_Paper-5][^cite_note-Water-supply_Paper-6] Water is typically extracted from depths of 300 to 400 ft, although some boreholes have been dug as far as 1000 ft.[^cite_note-Imām1972-7] Geologically it lies in the Tertiary-Quaternary [Umm Ruwaba Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umm_Ruwaba_Basin?action=edit\&redlink=1), composed mainly of fine-grained [lacustrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lacustrine_delta) and [fluviatile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluviatile) sediments.[^cite_note-Shahin2007-8][^cite_note-al-ʻUlūm1988-9][^cite_note-GeziraDevelopment1979-10]

## Economy

Lying in this part of the Sahel, Umm Ruwaba is prone to recurring periods of drought, [land degradation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_degradation) and famine. The famine of 1983–1985 had a severe impact on the area and caused food riots in the city.[^cite_note-Waal1997-11] Its principal crops are millet and sorghum for home consumption, and sesame for sale in the local markets, and the city is an important storage area for many farmers in the district.[^cite_note-HassanRay2009-12] The [Sudan Roads and Bridges Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_Roads_and_Bridges_Department?action=edit\&redlink=1) has been looking to improve road communications between the city and [El Obeid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ubayyid).[^cite_note-Shea2009-13]

## Demographics and religion

The main tribes in the city are [Jawamma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gawamma?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Shanabla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanabla?action=edit\&redlink=1) peoples. Traditionally it is an Islamic area,[^cite_note-JosephNaǧmābādī2003-14] but a 1980 report documented that an anti-Islamic organisation had converted some 40 families in Umm Ruwaba from Islam to Christianity.[^cite_note-Sudanow-15]
