The **Lebanese Civil Defense** officially known as the **General Directorate of the Lebanese Civil Defense** is a public [emergency medical service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_medical_service) of [Lebanon](17771.md) that carries out patient transportation, search and rescue activities and fire-fighting response.[^cite_note-TintinalliCameron2010-1][^cite_note-2]

It is funded and administered by the [Ministry of Interior and Municipalities (Lebanon)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_and_Municipalities_\(Lebanon\)). The directorate works in conjunction with the [Lebanese Red Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Red_Cross) along with other pre-[hospital](18994221.md) service organizations in the country.[^cite_note-TintinalliCameron2010-1][^cite_note-Fisk2001-3] The current Director General of the Lebanese Civil Defence is General Raymond Khattar.[^cite_note-4]
