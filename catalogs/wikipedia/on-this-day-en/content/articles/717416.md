# Bluefin tuna

**Bluefin tuna&#x20;**&#x20;is a common name used to refer to several species of [tuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuna) of the genus *[Thunnus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunnus)*.

| Species | Common name | Image | Range | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *[T. maccoyii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_bluefin_tuna)* | Southern bluefin tuna |  | found in southern hemisphere waters of all the world's oceans |  |
| *[T. orientalis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_bluefin_tuna)* | Pacific bluefin tuna |  | found widely in the northern Pacific Ocean and locally in the south | Formerly known as northern bluefin tuna. |
| *[T. thynnus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_bluefin_tuna)* | Atlantic bluefin tuna |  | found in both the western and eastern Atlantic Ocean, and also in the Mediterranean Sea | Formerly known as northern bluefin tuna. |
| *[T. tonggol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunnus_tonggol)* | Longtail tuna |  | found in tropical Indo-West Pacific waters | Known as the northern bluefin tuna in Australia, as it is found off their northern coast. |
