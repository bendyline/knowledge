**Nabatieh Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة النبطية, *_Muḥāfaẓat an-Nabaṭiyyah_*) is one of the nine [governorates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorates_of_Lebanon) of [Lebanon](17771.md). The area of this governorate is 1,058 km<sup>2</sup>. The capital is [Nabatieh](43794798.md).

## Districts



Districts of the Nabatieh Governorate

The governorate is divided into four districts (Aqdiya, singular [qadaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qadaa)) containing 130 [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities). The capitals are in brackets:

- [Bint Jbeil](12027489.md) ([Bint Jbeil](6124393.md)) – 36 municipalities

- [Hasbaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasbaya_District) ([Hasbaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasbaya)) – 20 municipalities

- [Marjayoun](8968039.md) ([Marjayoun](6236310.md)) – 32 municipalities

- [Nabatieh](12169367.md) ([Nabatieh](43794798.md)) – 42 municipalities
