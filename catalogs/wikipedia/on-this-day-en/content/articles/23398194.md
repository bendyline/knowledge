The **Odisha Police** is the law enforcement agency of the Indian [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_territories_of_India) of [Odisha](250724.md). Headquartered in [Cuttack](1776398.md),[^cite_note-2] it Police is headed by a [Director General of Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Director_General_of_Police) and falls under the purview of the Home Department of the [Government of Odisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Odisha). It has a sanctioned personnel strength of 72,145, with one-third of its sanctioned strength in the directly recruited posts of constable, sub-inspector, and deputy superintendent of police being allocated to women.

## History

Along with the formation of [Odisha](250724.md), the "Orissa Police" was established on April 1, 1936. The department was a force comprising 4000 trained men of all ranks. The Orissa Police Manual Rules (OPMR) was unveiled in 1940 which boasted rules and regulations of the powers, functions and duties of the police department at various levels.

## Department organisation

### Ranges

The Odisha Police is organized into 7 Police Ranges, most of which are further divided into 32 Police Districts. The Ranges are:

- Central Range at [Cuttack](1776398.md) – Districts of [Khordha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khordha_district), [Cuttack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuttack_district), [Kendrapara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendrapara_district), [Jagatsinghpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagatsinghpur_district), [Jajpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jajpur_district), [Puri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puri_district), and [Nayagarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nayagarh_district)

- Eastern Range at [Balasore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balasore) – Districts of [Balasore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balasore_district), [Mayurbhanj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayurbhanj_district), and [Bhadrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhadrak_district)

- Western Range at [Rourkela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rourkela) – Districts of [Sundargarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundargarh_district), [Keonjhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendujhar_district) and [Rourkela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rourkela)

- Northern Range at [Sambalpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sambalpur) – Districts of [Sambalpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sambalpur_district), [Bargarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bargarh_district), [Bolangir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolangir_district), [Sonepur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonepur_district), and [Jharsuguda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jharsuguda_district)

- Southern Range at [Berhampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhampur) – Districts of [Ganjam](1857310.md), [Gajapati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gajapati_district), [Kandhamal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandhamal_district), [Boudh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boudh_district), and [Berhampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhampur)

- South Western Range at [Koraput](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koraput) – Districts of [Nuapada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuapada_district), [Koraput](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koraput_district), [Nabarangpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabarangpur_district), [Rayagada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayagada_district), [Malkangiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malkangiri_district), and [Kalahandi](2561069.md)

- North Central Range at [Talcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talcher) – Districts of [Dhenkanal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhenkanal_district), [Angul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angul_district), and [Deogarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deogarh_district)

Additionally, there are:

- [Bhubaneswar–Cuttack Police Commissionerate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhubaneswar%E2%80%93Cuttack_Police_Commissionerate) at [Bhubaneswar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhubaneswar) – Controls the Capital City [Bhubaneswar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhubaneswar) and Twin City [Cuttack](1776398.md)

- Railway Range at [Bhubaneswar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhubaneswar) – Used for the [Indian Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Railways), SRP at [Cuttack](1776398.md) and [Rourkela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rourkela)

- Technical Range at [Cuttack](1776398.md) – Used for Police Transportation Services

## Special Forces

### Special Operation Group

The [Special Operation Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Operation_Group_\(Odisha\)) (SOG) is an elite paramilitary unit specializing in neutralizing terrorists, insurgents and extremists. The force currently, is primarily being used to counter left-wing extremism.

### Special Tactical Unit

The [Special Tactical Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Tactical_Unit) (STU) is a dedicated [urban warfare](319027.md) [counter-terrorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-terrorism) force consisting of Special Operations Group (SOG) personnel who are trained alongside the [National Security Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Guard) (NSG). The force made Odisha, third state in the country, after [Maharashtra](20629.md) and [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md), to have a dedicated force to counter terrorism in urban areas.

### District Voluntary Force

The District Voluntary Force (DVF) is a special constabulary unit specializing in [anti-Maoist](46636.md) operations.

### Odisha Special Armed Police (OSAP)



[Bhubaneswar–Cuttack Police Commissionerate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhubaneswar%E2%80%93Cuttack_Police_Commissionerate) Headquarters, [Bhubaneswar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhubaneswar), [Odisha](250724.md)

The Odisha Special Armed Police consists of 8 Odisha Special Armed Police Battalions, 6 Specialized Indian Reserved (IR) Battalions, 4 Social Security (SS) Battalions, 2 Specialized Indian Reserved (IR) Battalions and a Special Security Battalion, working under the [Bhubaneswar–Cuttack Police Commissionerate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhubaneswar%E2%80%93Cuttack_Police_Commissionerate).

The Orissa Military Police was formed under the Orissa Military Police Act VII (Government of Odisha) of 1946 which was formed on 1 March 1946. Till 1980, the Orissa Military Police was serving the state with its two battalions i.e. the Gurkhas and the Oriyas. After the Orissa Military Police (Amendment) Act 1980, the name “Orissa Military Police’’ was changed to “Odisha Special Armed Police (OSAP)”. There after, the department has achieved numerous remarkable feats advancing regularly with latest gadgets, arms and ammunition.

### Odisha Industrial Security Force (OISF)

The Odisha Industrial security Force (OISF) is a security force which was constituted to protect public and private sector industrial undertakings in [Odisha](250724.md). The force was formed under the Odisha Industrial Security Force Act of the [Government of Odisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Odisha) in 2012.

### Odisha Auxiliary Police Force (OAPF)

[Government of Odisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Odisha) sanctioned altogether 5600 posts of Special Police Officers (SPOs) keeping in mind of SC and ST's employment in 2008. Currently, the force has a sanctioned strength of 1521 personnel.
