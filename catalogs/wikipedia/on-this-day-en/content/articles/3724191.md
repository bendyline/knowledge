**Darra Adam Khel** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): درہ آدم خیل) is the main town of [Dara Adam Khel Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dara_Adam_Khel_Tehsil) (formerly known as "[Frontier Region Kohat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontier_Region_Kohat)") in the [Kohat District](4466450.md) of [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md), [Pakistan](23235.md). It has gained fame and notoriety for its bazaars packed with [gunsmiths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunsmith) and weapons [merchants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merchant). The town consists of one main street lined with multiple shops, while side-alleys and streets contain workshops.

## Economy

A wide variety of firearms are produced in the town, ranging from [anti-aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-aircraft) guns to [pen guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pen_gun). Weapons are handmade by individual craftsmen using traditional manufacturing techniques, which are usually handed down from father-to-son. Guns are regularly tested by test-firing into the air.[^cite_note-1] Darra is controlled by the local tribesmen.[^cite_note-OnlineNEW\.PK-2] The town has certain special laws compared to the rest of Pakistan. Most of the people here make or sell just one thing, i.e., guns, while the second largest business of the inhabitants is transport. Manufacturing of heavy ammunition, however, has been closed down.

Tourism is not advisable because tribal police ([Khasadar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khasadar_Forces)) visit the market to check for any local rules and law violations. Foreigners without permits are taken to secure places to avoid any mishaps.

The Darra arms trade first fired up in 1897 and became popular with the [Adam Khel Afridis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afridi_\(Pashtun\)).

According to [Vice News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_News), the gun trade in the area was affected by [Taliban](30635.md) and was forced to go underground.[^cite_note-3]

## Tourism

[Michael Palin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Palin) visited the town as part of his *[Himalaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himalaya:_Michael_Palin)* television series, as did [Ethan Casey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Casey) in his travel book *Alive and Well in Pakistan* while Australian film director Benjamin Gilmour's feature drama *[Son of a Lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Son_of_a_Lion)* set in Darra Adam Khel premiered at the [Berlin International Film Festival](18948039.md) 2008. Suroosh Alvi of Vice Media also entered the market-town in 2006 for a segment of *Vice Guide to Travel*.[^cite_note-4]
