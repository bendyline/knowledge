**Tank District** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): ټانک ولسوالۍ, [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضلع ٹانک *_ṭāṅk_*; [Saraiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraiki_language): ٹاک *_ṭāk_*) is a [district](639402.md) in the [Dera Ismail Khan Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dera_Ismail_Khan_Division) of the [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) province of [Pakistan](23235.md).

## History

### Ancient history

At the [Battle of the Hydaspes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hydaspes) (now the [Beas River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beas_River)), fought between [Alexander the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_the_Great)'s army and the Indian king [Purushotthama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purushotthama) (better known as [Porus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porus)), the [Macedonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macedon) army refused to go any further. It is said that the Alexander's army was at risk of being trapped, or was faced by an enemy army too big to defeat, and had to retreat southwards through the [Makran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makran) desert.

Islam came in the Tank region in the eighth century A.D., when [Umayyad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umayyad) general [Muhammad Bin Qasim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Bin_Qasim) attacked the [Multan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan) region and the nearby areas. The tribe known as [Arain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arain) in the Tank district is the generation of those Arab soldiers who came towards Multan with Muhammad Bin Qasim.

### Modern history



Portrait of Nawab Allahdad Khan of Tank, sketched by G. T. Vigne, published by The Illustrated London News, 25 April 1846

Finally, the Sikhs from the south overran the local tribes. They annexed the land in 1838. Somewhere in the midst of this turmoil, the British were assembling against the Pashtuns and the [First British-Afghan War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Anglo-Afghan_War) commenced. The British took over in 1848. The British regiments weren't able to occupy the entire territory and remained in the camps at the foothills of the mountains, while the harsh and dangerous upland terrain remained unexplored.

"...even the shadows of the hills were hazardous."

[^cite_note-3]

The eastern border of the Kingdom of [Kabul](16826.md) was undefined until 1893 when the Durand Line was established. Under the same agreement, the tribes of [Waziristan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waziristan) were clearly designated as being under the [British](31717.md) rule.

The British negotiated with the tribes through their agents in the border towns and Tank was a centre of negotiation with the [Mahsud tribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahsud). For the Britishers, the Mahsud tribe was the most difficult to control. In 1860, when the Mullah Shaleem Kaka Machi Khel Mahsud's army attacked the British with a 3000 strong armed-men, the British were forced to penetrate into the territory of Tank to control them.

In January 1899, [Lord Curzon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Curzon) was appointed as the [Viceroy of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viceroy_of_India). Reaching India shortly after the suppression of the frontier risings of 1897–98, he paid special attention to the independent tribes of the north-west frontier and inaugurated a new province called the [North-West Frontier Province (now Khyber Pakhtunkhwa)](21950.md), and pursued a policy of forceful control mingled with conciliation. The only major armed outbreak on this frontier during the period of his administration was the [Mahsud Waziri campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahsud_Waziri_blockade) of 1901.

### Post-independence

After the partition of the subcontinent, the Tank district became a part of Pakistan. In 2018, the [Frontier Region Tank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank_Subdivision) was formally merged with Tank district.

## Geography

The city of [Tank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank_\(city\)) is the headquarter of the district, which consists of Union Council City I and Union Council City II. There are sixteen Union councils in the district Tank. Until 1992, Tank was a tehsil within [Dera Ismail Khan district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dera_Ismail_Khan_District).[^cite_note-1998census-4]<sup>: 1 </sup> Tank is bordered with the district of [Lakki Marwat](3828439.md) in the northeast, [Dera Ismail Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dera_Ismail_Khan_District) in the east, [FR Tank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FR_Tank) in the north and the [South Waziristan district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Waziristan_Agency) in the west. The temperature of Tank reaches 110–120 °F in summer. However, in the winter, it is normal. People of the mountainous regions of the west usually come to Tank to avoid cold weather and then return during the summer.

## Administration

The district contains two [Tehsils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tehsils_of_Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa) - Tank and Jandola. And is represented in the [Provincial Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_Assembly_of_Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa) by one elected MPA, who represents the PK-108 (Tank) constituency.[^cite_note-5]

People make their livelihood by farming, falcon catching, migration for employment to the Persian Gulf, or through businesses in Tank.

"Jirga" means *council*. These are the religious circles and a group of people that decide the fate of the dwellers and rule the people by their sets of laws and principles.

## Tourism

### Shrines

The Shrine of Pir Sabir Shah Baba is one of the most visited places in the city. A number of devotees from the city and adjacent districts including Bannu, Lakki and Dera Ismail Khan visit this shrine. The three-day Urs of Pir Sabir Shah Baba is annually organized from June 2 to June 5. Earlier, the Urs was considered to be one of the big festivals of the city and a large number of people attended the Urs. The Shrine Custodian still distribute foods among people visiting the shrine and Halwa (a traditional sweet) is usually given on every Wednesday. Second popular shrine in the Tank region is the Mama Pir Ziarat which is located near Umar Adda and a number of devotees visit the shrine of Mama Pir. The Urs of Mama Pir is also organized annually.

### Nawab Qilla

Nawab Qilla is a mud fort built most probably by Nawab Katal Khan with views over an area of more than two hundred Kanals of land. The Qilla is situated at one corner of the Tank city and that is the imaginary line-dividing Tank into upper and lower part. The mud walls are several feet high approximately equal to a modern-day three-story building. Huge trunks of trees can be seen poking out from the walls of Qilla. These were used to give strength and stability to the mud walls. There were four huge burgs at each corner of the Qilla with several intervening pickets at regular distance all around. The four main burgs were used as an entrance for armed men who could move around and reach guard posts from within the Qilla without being seen from outside. The Qilla had three gigantic gates. The diameter or thickness of the wall from the top was initially equal to so that a Tanga (Horse Cart) could easily move over it. The residence of the Nawab Sahib was in the center of Qilla.

### Nawab of Tank Garden

The Nawab Bagh was situated at the back of Nawab Qilla, spread over an area of more than five hundred Kanals of land. This bagh was well looked after before, during Nawab Qasim Khan time and perhaps in the early days of Qutbuddin Khan. Nawab Qutbuddin Khan had kept large herd of buffalos, cows and it was not unusual to see a herd of two hundred or more buffalos, cows moving toward Nawab Qilla every day. Nawab Bagh as said before even in the sixties was deserted, has now completely disappeared as the land was sold by Qutbuddin s’ sons and a housing colony named Qutub colony has been constructed over its place right up to city police station and Pir Sabir Shah mausoleum.
