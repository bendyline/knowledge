**USS *Newport* (LST-1179)** was the third ship of the [United States Navy](20518076.md) (USN) to bear the name of the [Rhode Island](25410.md) [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport,_Rhode_Island). The first of [her class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport-class_tank_landing_ship) of [landing ship tanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landing_ship_tank) (LST), she was capable of a sustained speed of 20 [knots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (37 km/h; 23 mph). Her ability to adjust her draft, accompanied by her unique bow-ramp design, helped bring a new degree of responsiveness to the [amphibious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_warfare) [fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_fleet). The ship was [launched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceremonial_ship_launching) in 1968 and entered service with the USN in 1969. Assigned to the [United States Atlantic Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Atlantic_Fleet) for the entirety of her career, *Newport* made deployments to the [Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean) and [Caribbean Seas](7822.md). The vessel was taken out of service in 1992 and laid up until 2001.

In 2001, the ship was sold to the [Mexican Navy](1225277.md) and initially renamed **ARM *Sonora*** before becoming **ARM *Rio Papaloapan*** (or just ARM *Papaloapan* for short). In Mexican service, the LST has participated in humanitarian missions in the aftermath of the hurricanes [Katrina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Katrina) and [Sandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Sandy) and taken part in multi-national [naval exercises](1225714.md).

## Description

*Newport* was the first of [her class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport-class_tank_landing_ship) which were designed to meet the goal put forward by the United States [amphibious forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_warfare) to have a [tank landing ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank_landing_ship) (LST) capable of over 20 [knots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (37 km/h; 23 mph). However, the traditional [bow door](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bow_\(watercraft\)) form for LSTs would not be capable. Therefore, the designers of the *Newport* class came up with a design of a traditional [ship hull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hull_\(watercraft\)) with a 112-foot (34 m) [aluminum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminum) ramp slung over the bow supported by two [derrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrick) arms. The 34-[long-ton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_ton) (35 t) ramp was capable of sustaining loads up to 75 long tons (76 t). This made the *Newport* class the first to depart from the standard LST design that had been developed in early [World War II](32927.md).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBlackman1972504-1][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGardinerChumbleyBudzbon1995621-2][^cite_note-FOOTNOTECouhat1986655–666-3]

*Newport* had a [displacement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Displacement_\(ship\)) of 4,793 long tons (4,870 t) when light and 8,342 long tons (8,476 t) at full load. The LST was 522 feet 4 inches (159.2 m) [long overall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length_overall) and 562 ft (171.3 m) over the [derrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrick) arms which protruded past the bow.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGardinerChumbleyBudzbon1995621-2][^cite_note-FOOTNOTECouhat1986655–666-3] The vessel had a [beam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beam_\(nautical\)) of 69 ft 6 in (21.2 m), a [draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draft_\(hull\)) forward of 11 ft 5 in (3.5 m) and 17 ft 5 in (5.3 m) at the stern at full load.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMoore1975486-4]

*Newport* was fitted with six [General Motors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Motors) 16-645-ES [diesel engines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel_engine) turning two [shafts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propeller), three to each shaft. The system was rated at 16,500 [brake horsepower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horsepower#Brake_horsepower) (12,300 kW) and gave the ship a maximum speed of 22 knots (41 km/h; 25 mph) for short periods and could only sustain 20 knots (37 km/h; 23 mph) for an extended length of time. The LST carried 1,750 long tons (1,778 t) of [diesel fuel](81761.md) for a range of 2,500 [nautical miles](21289.md) (4,600 km; 2,900 mi) at the cruising speed of 14 knots (26 km/h; 16 mph). The ship was also equipped with a [bow thruster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bow_thruster) to allow for better maneuvering near causeways and to hold position while offshore during the unloading of amphibious vehicles.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECouhat1986655–666-3][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMoore1976614-5]

The *Newport* class were larger and faster than previous LSTs and were able to transport tanks, heavy vehicles and engineer groups and supplies that were too large for helicopters or smaller landing craft to carry.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMoore1974467-6] The LSTs have a ramp forward of the [superstructure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superstructure) that connects the lower tank deck with the main deck and a passage large enough to allow access to the parking area amidships. The vessels are also equipped with a stern gate to allow the unloading of amphibious vehicles directly into the water or to unload onto a [utility landing craft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landing_Craft_Utility) (LCU) or pier. At either end of the tank deck there is a 30 ft (9.1 m) turntable that permits vehicles to turn around without having to reverse.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBlackman1972504-1][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGardinerChumbleyBudzbon1995621-2] The *Newport* class has the capacity for 500 long tons (508 t) of vehicles, 19,000 ft<sup>2</sup> (1,800 m<sup>2</sup>) of cargo area and could carry up to 431 troops.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBlackman1972504-1][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMoore1978690-7] The vessels also have [davits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davit) for four [vehicle and personnel landing craft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LCVP_\(United_States\)) (LCVPs) and could carry four pontoon causeway sections along the sides of the hull.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGardinerChumbleyBudzbon1995621-2][^cite_note-FOOTNOTECouhat1986655–666-3]

*Newport* was initially armed with four Mark 33 [3-inch (76 mm)/50 caliber guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-inch/50-caliber_gun) in two twin [turrets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gun_turret). The vessel was equipped with two Mk 63 [gun control fire systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_gun_fire-control_system) (GCFS) for the 3-inch guns, but these were removed in 1977–1978.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECouhat1986655–666-3] The ship also had SPS-10 surface search [radar](25676.md).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESharpe1990761-8] Atop the stern gate, the vessels mounted a [helicopter deck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter_deck). They had a maximum complement of 213 including 11 officers.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMoore1974467-6]

## Construction and career

### United States Navy service



*Newport*'s ship patch

The vessel was ordered as part of Fiscal Year 1965 from the [Philadelphia Naval Shipyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_Naval_Shipyard) of [Philadelphia](50585.md), Pennsylvania.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMoore1974467-6] The ship's [keel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel) was [laid down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel_laying) on 1 November 1966 and the vessel was [launched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_naming_and_launching) on 3 February 1968, [sponsored](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_sponsor) by Nuella Pell, wife of Rhode Island [Senator](24909346.md) [Claiborne Pell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claiborne_Pell). *Newport*, named for [the city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport,_Rhode_Island) in [Rhode Island](25410.md), was [commissioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning) into the [United States Navy](20518076.md) on 7 June 1969.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEDANFS-9]

Upon entering service, *Newport* was assigned to the [Atlantic Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Fleet_Forces_Command) as part of the amphibious force. The vessel was deployed in the [Mediterranean](19006.md) and [Caribbean Seas](7822.md) along with training operations along the [United States East Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_East_Coast). The development of the [landing craft air cushion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landing_Craft_Air_Cushion), which allowed the United States Navy to launch over-the-horizon amphibious landings, made the *Newport* class obsolete.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGardinerChumbleyBudzbon1995621-2] USS *Newport* was decommissioned on 1 October 1992.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEDANFS-9]

### Mexican Navy service



ARM *Papaloapan* off the coast of [Mississippi](16949861.md) after [Hurricane Katrina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Katrina)

After several years laid up *Newport* was sold to the [Mexican Navy](1225277.md) on 18 January 2001 as part of the Security Assistance Program in a cash sale and renamed *Sonora* (A-04).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESaunders2004479-10][^cite_note-nvr-11] On 23 May 2001, the vessel joined the Mexican Navy and was renamed ARM *Rio Papaloapan* or *Papaloapan* (A 411).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESaunders2004479-10] The vessel was stricken from the United States [Naval Vessel Register](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Vessel_Register) on 13 July 2001.[^cite_note-nvr-11]

In late 2005, ARM *Papaloapan* brought aid and supplies to [Mississippi](16949861.md) after [Hurricane Katrina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Katrina) hit the state, with her crew taking part in cleanup efforts near [Biloxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biloxi).[^cite_note-12] In January 2010, she was deployed with 5,000 tons of cargo in a humanitarian mission to Haiti.[^cite_note-13] In November 2012, she was sent to Cuba with supplies to help the victims of [Hurricane Sandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Sandy) after the hurricane caused severe damage to the island.[^cite_note-14]

In 2015, *Papaloapan* participated in the multi-national [naval exercise](1225714.md) UNITAS.[^cite_note-15] In 2017, the ship took part in the multi-national naval exercise Bold Alligator on the US East Coast.[^cite_note-16] In 2018, the ship took part in another multi-national naval exercise, Panamax 2018, off the coast of the United States.[^cite_note-17]
