The **2026 [Memorial Van Damme](1111662.md)** was the 50th edition of the annual outdoor track and field meeting in [Brussels](3708.md), [Belgium](3343.md). Held on 4 and 5 September, it was the fifteenth leg and served as the finals of the [2026 Diamond League](81417246.md) – the highest-level international track and field circuit.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]
