The **Galkynyş Gas Field**, formerly known as **Ýolöten gas field** or **South Ýolöten – Osman field**, is a large natural gas field near [Ýolöten, Mary Province, Turkmenistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%9Dol%C3%B6ten). It is the world's second-largest [gas field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_gas_field).[^cite_note-economist140913-1]

## History

The discovery of the gas field was announced on 2 November 2006.[^cite_note-reuters021106-2] Late Turkmen president [Saparmyrat Nyýazow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saparmyrat_Ny%C3%BDazow) invited [Chinese](5405.md) [CNPC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Petroleum_Corporation) and [Turkish](11125639.md) [Çalik Enerji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%87alik_Enerji) to participate in the exploration and development of the Ýolöten field.[^cite_note-reuters211106-3] In 2008, the gas field was audited by [Gaffney, Cline & Associates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaffney,_Cline_&_Associates) (GCA).[^cite_note-reuters1-4][^cite_note-wsj1-5] According to GCA, Galkynyş is five times larger than the [Döwletabat gas field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dauletabad_gas_field) and the fourth- or fifth-[largest gas field in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_natural_gas_fields#20_Largest_non-associated_gas_fields).[^cite_note-6]

In December 2009, the contracts to develop the field were awarded to CNPC, [Hyundai Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Engineering) and [Petrofac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrofac).[^cite_note-reuters291209-7]

In November 2011, the field was renamed as Galkynyş.[^cite_note-turkmenistan201111-8] Production started in September 2013.[^cite_note-reuters130313-9]

## Description

The gas field ranks among the world's five largest with estimated reserves of between 4 and 14 trillion cubic metres (140×1&#x30;~~^~~<sup>12</sup> and 490×1&#x30;~~^~~<sup>12</sup> cu ft) of natural gas and proven commercial reserves of 2.8 trillion cubic metres (99×1&#x30;~~^~~<sup>12</sup> cu ft).[^cite_note-reuters1-4][^cite_note-wsj1-5][^cite_note-Times-10] It lies on 2,700 square kilometres (1,000 sq mi) zone of 90 kilometres (56 mi) in length and 30 kilometres (19 mi) in width at a depth of 3,900 to 5,100 metres (12,800 to 16,700 ft).[^cite_note-trend1-11] Galkynyş consists of Ýolöten, Minara, Osman and Ýaşlar fields.[^cite_note-turkmenistan201111-8] Other nearby gas areas are Günorta Garaköl, Garaköl, Jürji, Gazanly, Gündogar Ýolöten and Günbatar Ýandakly.[^cite_note-trend1-11]

Oil reserves are 300 million tons. The crude oil extracted at Galkynyş is transported to the [Seýdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Se%C3%BDdi) Oil Refinery.

## Development

The field is developed by [Türkmengaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%BCrkmengaz). CNPC, [Hyundai Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Engineering) and [Petrofac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrofac) built gas processing plants.[^cite_note-reuters291209-7][^cite_note-trend2-12]
