**Generalissimo Francisco de Miranda Air Base** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **N/A**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **SVFM**), commonly called by its former name of ***La Carlota***, is located near [Caracas](55289.md), [Venezuela](32374.md). The airport is named for [Francisco de Miranda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_de_Miranda), a South American revolutionary. It is close to [La Carlota, Miranda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Carlota,_Miranda).

The airport was used for tourist and private flights.[^cite_note-1] On 27 November 1992, the airport was bombed during [Hugo Chávez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Ch%C3%A1vez)'s [coup attempt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_1992_Venezuelan_coup_attempt).[^cite_note-2] It has been closed to public use since 2005 and is used only for military purposes and aeromedic flights (EMS).

In April 2019, the airport was the starting point of the [2019 Venezuela uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Venezuela_uprising).[^cite_note-3]

On 3 January 2026 at 02\:00 [VET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Venezuela), the airport was hit by the US military as part of an [operation](82021123.md) to abduct [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md).[^cite_note-4]
