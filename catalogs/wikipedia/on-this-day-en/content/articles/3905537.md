**Titao** is a town located in the province of [Loroum](2874557.md) in [Burkina Faso](3470.md). It is the capital of Loroum Province. Since 2019 it has been under siege by Islamic rebels.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
