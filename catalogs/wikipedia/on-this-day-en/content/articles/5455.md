The **Cameroon Armed Forces** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Forces armées camerounaises (FAC)*) are the military of the [Republic of Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Cameroon). It has three branches – the army, air force, and navy – and the gendarmerie.

It has generally remained loyal to the government and acted to ensure the stability of the regime, and not acted as an independent political force.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Traditional dependence on the French defense capability, although reduced, continues to be the case as French military advisers remain closely involved in preparing the Cameroonian forces for deployment to the contested [Bakassi Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakassi_Peninsula).

The [African Union](10634933.md) maintains its continental logistics base in Douala.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEThe_International_Institute_for_Strategic_Studies2025460-1]

## Army

The army has 35,500 troops.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEThe_International_Institute_for_Strategic_Studies2025460-1]

Currently, the organization dates from 2001 with a distribution in several types of units: combat units, response units (*unités d'intervention*), *unités de soutien et d'appui*, and finally special reserve units as part of 3 joint military régions (*interarmées*) and the 10 military land sectors.[^cite_note-4]

Army units have been trained and equipped to fight in the swampy coastal terrain facing the [Bakassi peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakassi_peninsula). Although prepared for an armed conflict with Nigeria in recent years, the Cameroon Army does not have operational experience against other forces, therefore, it is not possible to assess its ability to respond to changing threats and opposing tactics.

Combat units of the army include:[^cite_note-5]

- The Headquarters Brigade, located in Yaoundé. This brigade is responsible for protecting the capital and supporting the institutions. The President of the Republic has to allow any of its deployments. The brigade consists of acommand and support battalion; a support battalion; a *bataillon honneurs et protection* (BHP); and three infantry battalions.

  [^cite_note-6]

- Three command and support battalions;

- The [Rapid Intervention Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_Intervention_Brigade) (*Brigade d'Intervention Rapide*, BIR) is made up of three rapid response battalions, stationed in Douala, Tiko and Koutaba. These three battalions are respectively the Special Amphibious Battalion (Bataillon Spécial Amphibie; BSA), the Bataillon des Troupes Aéroportées (BTAP), and the Armored Reconnaissance Battalion (Bataillon Blindé de Reconnaissance; BBR)

  [^cite_note-7]

  &#x20;equipped with [Type 07P](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_07P) infantry fighting vehicle and [PTL-02](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PTL-02) tank destroyer bought recently from China.

  [^cite_note-8]

  &#x20;The BSA is inspired by the French Special Forces. This brigade is a tactical battle unit under the authority of the Chief of Staff of the armed forces. For this to be engaged, the President's agreement is necessary.

- Five motorised infantry brigades, supposed to be stationed in one military sector but which can then be engaged without any regard to the territorial division of the country. These brigades currently do not have a general staff. In theory, they consist of 11 motorised infantry battalions; 5 support battalions and 3 backing battalions; however, the motorised battalions are in reality not operational due to a lack of staff, equipment and vehicles.

## Air force

Main article: [Cameroon Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon_Air_Force)

The air force has 1,000 troops, including one security battalion. Its combat capable fixed-wing aircraft are six [Dassault/Dornier Alpha Jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dassault/Dornier_Alpha_Jet), although they may not be serviceable.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEThe_International_Institute_for_Strategic_Studies2025461-11]

## Navy



Cameroonian sailors reviewed by an American admiral in Douala, 2015.

The navy has approximately 1,500 troops,[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEThe_International_Institute_for_Strategic_Studies2025460-1] including a three marine battalions.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEThe_International_Institute_for_Strategic_Studies2025461-11] The headquarters is in Douala.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEThe_International_Institute_for_Strategic_Studies2025460-1]

### History

Around May 1999, [Philip Njaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Njaru) wrote a newspaper article where he alleged ill-treatment of civilians conducted by the 21st Navy Battalion based in Ekondo-Titi. In late May Njaru was approached by the local [captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_\(Navy\)) who asked Njaru "to stop writing such articles and to disclose his sources". Refusing to do this, Njaru five days later found his house encircled by armed soldiers, and escaped to [Kumba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumba,_Cameroon).[^cite_note-UN-12] Here, he was assaulted by police in June 2001, with no particular reason stated.[^cite_note-UN-12] Njaru complained to the local authorities, but later learned that "his complaint had not been received".[^cite_note-UN-12]

The navy has 14 patrol and coastal ships,[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEThe_International_Institute_for_Strategic_Studies2025460-1] and five landing craft.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEThe_International_Institute_for_Strategic_Studies2025461-11]

## Gendarmerie

The paramilitary Gendarmerie has 9,000 troops.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEThe_International_Institute_for_Strategic_Studies2025461-11]

## Military education

The Combined Services Military Academy (EMIA) is the military college for [officer candidates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Officer_candidate) and senior officers. It was founded in 1959[^cite_note-13] and opened in 1961.[^cite_note-14]
