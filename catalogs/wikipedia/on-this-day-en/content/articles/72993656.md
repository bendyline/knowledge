**Virginie Ruffenach** (born 19 September 1973 in [Noumea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noumea)) is a politician from [New Caledonia](21342.md). She is a member of [The Rally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rally%E2%80%93UMP) and the chairperson of the [Future with Confidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_with_Confidence) alliance in the Congress of New Caledonia.

## Biography

Ruffenach was born in Noumea and grew up in town of [Dumbéa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumb%C3%A9a) where her parents worked. She left New Caledonia to study a degree in chemistry at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg) before working as a teacher and a school inspector.[^cite_note-1]

She has been a member of [The Rally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rally%E2%80%93UMP) since 2007 and is a vice-president of the party.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
