On 25 April 2026, a bomb exploded along the [Pan-American Highway](301108.md) in the municipality of [Cajibío](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cajib%C3%ADo,_Cauca), department of [Cauca](549662.md), Colombia, setting fire to a bus and damaging several vehicles, which resulted in the killing of 20 civilians and the wounding of 36 more.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## History

Authorities accused the [main dissident group of the FARC guerrillas](57074954.md),[^cite_note-bbcb-3] and Defence Minister [Pedro Arnulfo Sánchez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Arnulfo_S%C3%A1nchez) offered a 5 billon [peso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_peso) (US\$1.4m, €1.2m) reward for the suspect [Iván Jacob Idrobo Arredondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iv%C3%A1n_Jacob_Idrobo_Arredondo?action=edit\&redlink=1), aka "Marlon".[^cite_note-bbcc-4]

The previous day, an attack on a military base in [Cali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cali), the country's third-largest city, left one person dead and marked the beginning of a series of attacks in the Cauca region, a stronghold of FARC dissidents.[^cite_note-bbcb-3]
