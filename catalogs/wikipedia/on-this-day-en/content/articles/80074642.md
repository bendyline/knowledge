The **2026 Indian Premier League final** was a [Twenty20](770690.md) (T20) [cricket](25675557.md) match played at the [Narendra Modi Stadium](80201506.md) in [Ahmedabad](206337.md), India, on 31 May 2026 to determine the winner of the [2026 Indian Premier League](79331486.md) (IPL). The defending champions [Royal Challengers Bengaluru](15861688.md) qualified for the final after winning the Qualifier 1 against [Gujarat Titans](70008752.md); who went on to defeat [Rajasthan Royals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan_Royals) in Qualifier 2 to secure their place in the final.

Bengaluru won the toss and elected to field first; Gujarat scored 155/8 in their innings, which Bengaluru chased down in 18 overs scoring 161/5, to win their second IPL title and become the third team to win two consecutive IPL finals. They also became the first team to win the IPL and the [Women's Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Premier_League_\(cricket\)) in the same year. A closing ceremony was held during the match's [innings break](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innings_break). Bengaluru's captain [Rajat Patidar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajat_Patidar) dedicated the victory to the victims of the [2025 Bengaluru crowd crush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Bengaluru_crowd_crush).

## Background

Main article: [2026 Indian Premier League](79331486.md)

The [2026 Indian Premier League](79331486.md) was the 19th edition of the [Indian Premier League](13260340.md) (IPL), a professional [Twenty20](770690.md) (T20) [cricket](25675557.md) league, organised by the [Board of Control for Cricket in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Control_for_Cricket_in_India) (BCCI).[^cite_note-1] The season commenced on 28 March[^cite_note-sch1-2] and consisted of 10 teams competing in 74 matches.[^cite_note-sch2-3][^cite_note-faq-4][^cite_note-5] After the conclusion of the group stage, four teams advanced to the playoffs, which began on 26 May[^cite_note-pos1-6] and concluded with the final played on 31 May at the [Narendra Modi Stadium](80201506.md), [Ahmedabad](206337.md). This was the fourth IPL final held at that venue, after the [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Indian_Premier_League_final), [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Indian_Premier_League_final) and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indian_Premier_League_final) events.[^cite_note-pos2-7][^cite_note-8]

The defending champions [Royal Challengers Bengaluru](15861688.md) qualified for their fifth IPL final. They were the fourth team to qualify for two consecutive IPL finals and had previously been runners-up in [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Indian_Premier_League_final), [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Indian_Premier_League_final) and [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Indian_Premier_League_final).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] Their opponents were the [Gujarat Titans](70008752.md), who qualified for their third IPL final: they were champions in 2022 and runners-up in 2023.[^cite_note-11] Both teams were competing for their second titles and had won their maiden titles at this same venue.[^cite_note-hist-12]

### Royal Challengers Bengaluru

Royal Challengers Bengaluru began their season with two wins against [Sunrisers Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunrisers_Hyderabad)[^cite_note-15] and [Chennai Super Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai_Super_Kings).[^cite_note-16] They lost to [Rajasthan Royals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan_Royals),[^cite_note-17] won against [Mumbai Indians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Indians)[^cite_note-18] and [Lucknow Super Giants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucknow_Super_Giants),[^cite_note-19] lost to [Delhi Capitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Capitals),[^cite_note-20] and won against Gujarat[^cite_note-RCB-GT-1-21] and Delhi.[^cite_note-22] Then they lost to Gujarat[^cite_note-RCB-GT-2-23] and Lucknow,[^cite_note-24] won against Mumbai,[^cite_note-25] [Kolkata Knight Riders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata_Knight_Riders)[^cite_note-26] and [Punjab Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Kings)[^cite_note-27] and ended the group stage with a loss to Hyderabad.[^cite_note-28] With nine wins from 14 matches, they finished the league stage in first place and advanced to Qualifier 1 in the playoffs.[^cite_note-pts-13][^cite_note-IPL-14] They defeated Gujarat in Qualifier 1, to advance to the final.[^cite_note-QLF1-29][^cite_note-IPL-14]

### Gujarat Titans

Gujarat Titans began their season with two losses against Punjab[^cite_note-30] and Rajasthan.[^cite_note-31] They won their next three matches against Delhi,[^cite_note-32] Lucknow[^cite_note-33] and Kolkata;[^cite_note-34] lost to Mumbai[^cite_note-35] and Bengaluru;[^cite_note-RCB-GT-1-21] won against Chennai,[^cite_note-36] Bengaluru,[^cite_note-RCB-GT-2-23] Punjab,[^cite_note-37] Rajasthan[^cite_note-38] and Hyderabad;[^cite_note-39] lost to Kolkata[^cite_note-40] and won against Chennai.[^cite_note-41] They also had nine wins from 14 matches, finishing the league stage in second place, behind Bengaluru on the [tiebreaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiebreaker) ([net run rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_run_rate)). They advanced to Qualifier 1 in the playoffs,[^cite_note-pts-13][^cite_note-IPL-14] which they lost to Bengaluru.[^cite_note-QLF1-29] However, Gujarat then defeated Rajasthan in Qualifier 2, to reach the final.[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-IPL-14]

This was the first time in an IPL final that all five officials were Indian.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44]

### Gujarat Titans innings

Put in to [bat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batting_\(cricket\)) first, the Gujarat openers Shubman Gill and Sai Sudharsan were [dismissed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dismissal_\(cricket\)) in the third and fourth [overs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Over_\(cricket\)), bowled by Bhuvneshwar Kumar and Josh Hazlewood respectively. ### Royal Challengers Bengaluru innings

Virat Kohli in 2024

[Virat Kohli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virat_Kohli) scored 75 runs for Bengaluru in the final, and received the player of the match award.

[Chasing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Run_chase) the target of 156, Bengaluru openers Venkatesh Iyer and Virat Kohli shared an opening [partnership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partnership_\(cricket\)) of 62 runs before Iyer was dismissed for 32. Devdutt Padikkal and Krunal Pandya were dismissed for one run each, while Rajat Patidar made 15. The score was 91 for 4 after 8.5 overs, with Kohli still [not out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Not_out). The following batters Tim David and Jitesh Sharma contributed 24 and 11 runs respectively, while Kohli increased the [run rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Run_rate); he would finish with a [strike rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_rate) of 178. Bengaluru reached a score of 161 runs for the loss of five wickets after 18 overs, passing the target and securing the title. Bowler Rashid Khan took two wickets for Gujarat,[^cite_note-sc1-51][^cite_note-sc2-52][^cite_note-sc3-53] while Kohli was the highest run-scorer with 75 runs [not out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Not_out) from 42 balls and received the [player of the match](906698.md) award.[^cite_note-57]

## Aftermath

Bengaluru became the third team to win two consecutive IPL finals after Chennai and Mumbai.[^cite_note-consec-win-48] They also became the first team to win the IPL and the [Women's Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Premier_League_\(cricket\)) in the same year, having won the [women's tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Women's_Premier_League_\(cricket\)) earlier in the year.[^cite_note-rec-win-50] Bengaluru received ₹20 crore (US\$2.1 million) and Gujarat received ₹12.5 crore (US\$1.3 million) in prize money.[^cite_note-58] Bengaluru captain Patidar dedicated the victory to the victims of [the crowd crush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Bengaluru_crowd_crush) that occurred during [last year's victory celebrations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indian_Premier_League_final#Celebrations).[^cite_note-59]
