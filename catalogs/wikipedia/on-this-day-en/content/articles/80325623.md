**Abdali** (Arabic: العبدلي) is a village in the [Al Jahra Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Jahra_Governorate) in northern [Kuwait](7515890.md), near the border with [Iraq](7515928.md). It is known for its agricultural productivity, strategic location, and its role in Kuwait's economic development.

## Geography

**Abdali** is located approximately 90 kilometers north of [Kuwait City](57021.md), at an elevation of about 20 meters (66 feet) above sea level. The village has a population of around 6,840 residents. It lies near Highway 80, historically referred to as the “[Highway of Death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highway_of_Death)” due to its significance during the [1991 Gulf War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Gulf_War).[^cite_note-1]

## History

Due to its proximity to the Iraqi border, Abdali has held strategic importance, especially during regional conflicts. During the [2003 Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_Iraq_War), the area was designated a closed military zone, and nearby oil fields such as Abdali and Ratqa were temporarily shut down for security reasons.[^cite_note-2]

## Economy

Abdali is part of Kuwait's **Economic Zones** initiative. The **Abdali Economic Zone (AEZ)** is being developed as a smart city and logistics hub, with plans to attract over one billion Kuwaiti Dinars in investment and create more than 26,000 jobs. The project is aligned with Kuwait's national vision to diversify its economy and enhance trade connectivity.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Agriculture



Abdali is one of Kuwait's primary agricultural regions, alongside [Wafra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wafra) and Sulaibiya. It hosts over 1,900 farms producing vegetables, fruits, and flowers. The area is also known for its biodiversity, attracting birdwatchers to species like the [Afghan Babbler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_Babbler) and [Red-wattled Lapwing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-wattled_Lapwing).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Tourism and culture



Kite flying by locals during winter season

Abdali has become a popular weekend destination for locals and expatriates. Attractions include **Blue Lake Farm**, **Al Boharya Farm**, petting zoos, and seasonal flower gardens. Visitors enjoy fresh produce, sugarcane juice, and scenic countryside views.[^cite_note-7]

## Transportation

The village is accessible via [Highway 80](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highway_80_\(Kuwait\)), connecting [Kuwait City](57021.md) to the Iraqi border. Future infrastructure projects like the [GCC Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GCC_Railway) and [Mubarak Al Kabeer Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mubarak_Al_Kabeer_Port) are expected to enhance Abdali's role as a trade gateway.[^cite_note-8]
