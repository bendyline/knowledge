The ***Tehran Times*** is an English-language daily newspaper published in Iran, founded in 1979 as a "voice of the [Islamic Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Revolution)". While not state-owned, it is considered state-controlled and closely tied to the hardline factions within the Iranian government.[^cite_note-regime-1]

## History

The newspaper was founded by [Mohammad Beheshti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Beheshti) in 1979 following the [Iranian Revolution](347268.md) as the "voice of the Islamic Revolution".[^cite_note-NPR-2][^cite_note-book-3]

In Oktober 2010 Reza Moqaddasi took over as the new head of the *Tehran Times* (TT) and the *[Mehr News Agency](2707394.md)* (MNA) for a four-year term, replacing the former Managing Director Parviz Esmaeili. He resigned after 3 and half years for health reasons. Therefore in May 2014 [ Hajottoleslam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hujjat_al-Islam) Khamoshi, the head of Iran’s [ Islamic Ideology Dissemination Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Development_Organization) (IIDO), appointed Ali Askari as the new director of TT and MNA.[^cite_note-4] In September 2019 Mohammad Shojaeian was introduced as the new managing director of the TT and the MNA.[^cite_note-5]

In April 2020 the [CEO](52234.md) of Mehr Media Group, Mohammad Shojaeian, appointed Ali Akbar Jenabzadeh (Persian: علی اکبر جناب‌زاده) – as successor of Mohammad Ghaderi – as the [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of the *Tehran Times* daily newspaper.[^cite_note-6]

In April 2023 the head of IIDO, [ Hojjat aleslam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hujjat_al-Islam) Mohammad Qomi, appointed Mohammad Mehdi Rahmati as the new managing director of the Mehr Media Group to replace Mohammad Shojaeian Zanjani.[^cite_note-7] Since early 2023 Tehran Times Editor-in-Chief is Mohammad Sarfi (Persian: محمد صرفی).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

In August 2023, *Tehran Times* published a [United States Department of State](31975.md) memo informing U.S. Special Envoy for Iran [Rob Malley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Malley) of his security clearance suspension. The publication prompted calls by [Republicans](32070.md) in the [U.S. House of Representatives](19468510.md) and [U.S. Senate](24909346.md) for a probe into how the newspaper acquired the information.[^cite_note-11]

## Editorial position

[Ayatollah](63899.md) [ Mohammad Hossein Beheshti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Beheshti), second in line in the political hierarchy following the 1979 [Islamic Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Revolution), stated: "*Tehran Times* is not a [state-owned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government-owned_corporation) newspaper, rather it is a loud voice of the Islamic Revolution and the oppressed people of the world.”[^cite_note-12] Although the newspaper is not state-owned, "it aims to disseminate key tenets of the Islamic Revolution and is therefore generally supportive of the Islamic Republic of Iran's ideology".[^cite_note-13]
