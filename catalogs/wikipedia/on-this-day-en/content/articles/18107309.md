**Deir Sharaf** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): دير شرف) is a [Palestinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories) town in the [Nablus Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nablus_Governorate) of the [State of Palestine](241405.md), in the northern [West Bank](33209.md), located northwest of [Nablus](233369.md). According to the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics) (PCBS), the town had a population of 2,949 inhabitants in 2017.[^cite_note-PrelimCensus2017-1]

## Location

Deir Sharaf is located 7.8 kilometers (4.8 mi) northwest of [Nablus](233369.md). It is bordered by [An Naqura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Naqura), [Beit Iba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_Iba), and [Sabastiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastia,_Nablus) to the east, [Burqa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burqa,_Nablus) and [Ramin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramin,_Tulkarm) to the north, [Beit Lid](4996688.md) to the west, and [Qusin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qusin) to the south.[^cite_note-3]

## History

Pottery [sherds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherd) from the [Iron Age II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Age#Near_East_timeline), [Byzantine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Empire), early [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim) and Medieval era have been found here.[^cite_note-4]

### Ottoman era

Deir Sharaf, like the rest of [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_\(region\)), was incorporated into the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md) in 1517, and in the [census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defter) of 1596 it was a part of the *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya)* ("subdistrict") of Jabal Sami, which was part of the [Sanjak of Nablus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjak_of_Nablus). The village had a population of 55 households, all [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim). The villagers paid a fixed tax rate of 33.3% on wheat, barley, summer crops, olive trees, beehives and/or goats, in addition to occasional revenues, a press for olive oil or grape syrup, and a tax on Muslims in the Nablus area; a total of 9,372 [akçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e). The whole of the revenue went to a [Waqf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waqf) for the [Madrasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrasa) of [Ramla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramla).[^cite_note-5]

In 1838, Deir Sheraf was located in the *Wady esh-Sha'ir* District, west of Nablus.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

In 1870, [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) noted “a small square in front of the [mosque](19894.md) paved by ancient slabs” in the village, which he called *Deir Ech-Cheraf*.[^cite_note-8]

In 1870/1871 (1288 [AH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anno_Hegirae)), an Ottoman census listed the village in the *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiyah)* (sub-district) of Wadi al-Sha'ir.[^cite_note-9]

In 1882, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s *[Survey of Western Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine)* described *Deir Sheraf*: "A village of small size, situate\[d] in a hollow. Above it, beside the road on the east, is a good spring, apparently perennial, and round this are vegetable gardens irrigated with its waters. Figs and olives also grow in the vicinity."[^cite_note-10]

### British Mandate era



Deir Sharaf 1920

In the [1922 census of Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1922_census_of_Palestine), conducted by the [British Mandate authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine), Deir Sharaf had a population of 487, all Muslims,[^cite_note-Census1922-11] increasing in the [1931 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1931_census_of_Palestine) to 572, still all Muslim, in a total of 118 houses.[^cite_note-Census1931-12]

In the [1945 statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_Statistics,_1945), *Deir Sharaf* had a population of 800, all Muslims,[^cite_note-1945p18-13] with 7,190 [dunams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunam) of land, according to an official land and population survey.[^cite_note-14] Of this, 391 dunams were for plantations and irrigable land, 4,335 used for cereals,[^cite_note-15] while 71 dunams were built-up (urban) land.[^cite_note-16]

### Jordanian era

In the wake of the [1948 Arab–Israeli War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Arab%E2%80%93Israeli_War) Deir Sharaf came under [Jordanian rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordanian_annexation_of_the_West_Bank).

The Jordanian census of 1961 found 1,241 inhabitants in Deir Sharaf.[^cite_note-17]

### Post-1967

Since the [Six-Day War](29328.md) in 1967, Deir Sharaf has been under [Israeli occupation](59076253.md). The population in the 1967 census conducted by Israel was 973, of whom 46 originated from the Israeli territory.[^cite_note-67census-18]

After the [1995 accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo_II_Accord), 23% of village land was classified as [Area B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_enclaves), the remaining 77% as [Area C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_C_\(West_Bank\)). 236 dunams Deir Sharaf’s land has been confiscated by the Israel for the [Israeli settlement](15123.md) of [Shavei Shomron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shavei_Shomron), located just north of Deir Sharaf.[^cite_note-19]

On 3 July 2014, Israeli authorities stated that they were confiscating 16 dunams of land near the village for “military purposes”.[^cite_note-20]

## Demography

The village's residents have their origins in various places, including [Ramin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramin,_Tulkarm), '[Atara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/'Atara), [Qusin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qusin), [Kafr Aqab](18080956.md) and others.[^cite_note-21]
