**1776** ([MDCCLXXVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals)) was a [leap year starting on Monday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leap_year_starting_on_Monday) of the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar) and a [leap year starting on Friday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leap_year_starting_on_Friday) of the [Julian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_calendar), the 1776th year of the [Common Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era) (CE) and *[Anno Domini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anno_Domini)* (AD) designations, the 776th year of the [2nd millennium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_millennium), the 76th year of the [18th century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/18th_century), and the 7th year of the [1770s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1770s) decade. As of the start of 1776, the Gregorian calendar was 11 days ahead of the Julian calendar, which remained in localized use until 1923.&#x20;

1776 took place during the height of the [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution), and was singled out by [Abraham Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lincoln) as a turning point through the signing of the [Declaration of Independence](31874.md).[^cite_note-1]

## Events

### January–February

- [January 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_1) – [American Revolutionary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War): [Burning of Norfolk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burning_of_Norfolk) – The town of [Norfolk, Virginia](57898.md) is destroyed, by the combined actions of the [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Great_Britain) [Royal Navy](26061.md) and occupying [Patriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriot_\(American_Revolution\)) forces.

- [January 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_10) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): [Thomas Paine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Paine) publishes his pamphlet *[Common Sense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Sense_\(pamphlet\))*, arguing for independence from British rule in the [Thirteen Colonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteen_Colonies).

  [^cite_note-2]

- [January 20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_20) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): [South Carolina](27956.md) [Loyalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyalist_\(American_Revolution\)) led by Robert Cunningham sign a petition from prison, agreeing to all demands for peace by the formed state government of South Carolina.

- [January 24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_24) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): [Henry Knox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Knox) arrives at [Cambridge, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts), with the [artillery that he has transported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noble_train_of_artillery) from [Fort Ticonderoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Ticonderoga).

- [February 17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_17) – [Edward Gibbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Gibbon) publishes the first volume of *[The History of the Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_History_of_the_Decline_and_Fall_of_the_Roman_Empire)*.

- [February 27](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_27) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): [Battle of Moore's Creek Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Moore's_Creek_Bridge) – Scottish [North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Colony_of_North_Carolina) Loyalists charge across Moore's Creek Bridge near [Wilmington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilmington,_North_Carolina) to attack what they mistakenly believe to be a small force of rebels. Several leaders are killed in the ensuing battle. The patriot victory

  [^cite_note-Cassell's_Chronology330331-3]

  &#x20;ends virtually all British authority in the province.

### March–April

- [March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March) – Restrictions on the [grain trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grain_trade) in [Sweden](5058739.md) are lifted.

- [March 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_1) – The [Peshwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshwa) of the [Maratha Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maratha_Empire) and the [British East India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_East_India_Company) sign the Treaty of Purandar in which the British are able to secure [Salsette Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salsette) and accept [Sawai Madhavrao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sawai_Madhavrao) as a new Peshwa. The Marathas agree not to recognise existence of the French in India.

- [March 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_2)–[3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_3) – [American Revolutionary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War):&#x20;

  - [Battle of Nassau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Nassau): The American [Continental Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Navy) and [Marines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Marines) make a successful assault on [Nassau, Bahamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nassau,_Bahamas).

  - [Battle of the Rice Boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Rice_Boats): American [Patriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriot_\(American_Revolution\)) resist the Royal Navy on the [Savannah River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savannah_River); British control over the [Province of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Georgia) is lost.

- [March 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_4) – [American Revolutionary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War): [American Patriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriot_\(American_Revolution\)) [capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortification_of_Dorchester_Heights) [Dorchester Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorchester_Heights), dominating the port of [Boston](24437894.md).

- [March 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_9) – [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_people) economist [Adam Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Smith) publishes *[The Wealth of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wealth_of_Nations)* in London.

- [March 17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_17) – [American Revolutionary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War): Threatened by [Patriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriot_\(American_Revolution\)) cannons on [Dorchester Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorchester_Heights), the British evacuate [Boston](24437894.md), ending the 11‑month [Siege of Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Boston).

  [^cite_note-Cassell's_Chronology330331-3]

- [March 25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_25) – [American Revolutionary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War): [American Patriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriot_\(American_Revolution\)) conduct a [Raid on Tybee Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raid_on_Tybee_Island), primarily seeking to capture runaway slaves who sought refuge with British forces stationed there.

  [^cite_note-4]

- [March 28](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_28)&#x20;

  - [Juan Bautista de Anza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Bautista_de_Anza) finds the site for the [Presidio of San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidio_of_San_Francisco).

  - Foundation of the [ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballet) troupe which becomes the world-renowned [Bolshoi Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolshoi_Ballet) in [Moscow](19004.md), [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire).

    [^cite_note-5]

- [April 12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_12) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): The Royal Colony of North Carolina produces the [Halifax Resolves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax_Resolves), making it the first British colony to officially authorize its [Continental Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Congress) delegates to vote for independence from the [Kingdom of Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Great_Britain).

- [April 27](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_27) – [Jeongjo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeongjo_of_Joseon) becomes the [King of Joseon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Joseon) following the death of his grandfather [Yeongjo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeongjo_of_Joseon).

### May–June

- [May 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_1) – [Adam Weishaupt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Weishaupt) founds the *[Illuminati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illuminati)* in [Ingolstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingolstadt), [Bavaria](3764.md).

- [May 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_4) – [Rhode Island](25410.md) becomes the first American colony to renounce allegiance to King [George III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_III) of Great Britain.

- [May 15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_15)–[26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_26) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): [Battle of the Cedars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Cedars) – British forces skirmish with the American [Continental Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Army) around [Les Cèdres, Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_C%C3%A8dres,_Quebec).

- [June 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_6) – A fire destroys major parts of the town of [Askersund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Askersund), [Sweden](5058739.md).

  [^cite_note-6]

- [June 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_7) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): [Lee Resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Resolution) – [Richard Henry Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Henry_Lee) of [Virginia](32432.md) proposes to the [Second Continental Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Continental_Congress) (meeting in Philadelphia) that "these united colonies are, and of right ought to be, free and independent states."

- [June 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_8) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): [Battle of Trois-Rivières](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Trois-Rivi%C3%A8res) – The invading American Continental Army is driven back at [Trois-Rivières, Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trois-Rivi%C3%A8res,_Quebec).

- [June 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_10) – Death of King [Hsinbyushin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hsinbyushin) of the [Konbaung dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konbaung_dynasty) in Burma.

- [June 11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_11) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): The [Continental Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Congress) appoints a [Committee of Five](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_of_Five) to draft a [Declaration of Independence](31874.md).

- [June 12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_12) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): The *[Virginia Declaration of Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Declaration_of_Rights)* (by [George Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Mason)) is adopted by the Virginia Convention of Delegates.

- [June 15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_15) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): Delaware Separation Day – The [Delaware General Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware_General_Assembly) votes to suspend government under the British Crown.

- [June 17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_17) – Lt. [José Joaquín Moraga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Joaqu%C3%ADn_Moraga) leads a band of colonists from [Monterey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterey,_California) Presidio, landing on [June 29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_29) and, with Father [Francisco Palóu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Pal%C3%B3u), constructing the [Mission San Francisco de Asís](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission_San_Francisco_de_As%C3%ADs) ("Mission Dolores") of the new Presidio of [San Francisco](49728.md), the oldest surviving building in the modern-day city.

- [June 28](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_28) – [American Revolutionary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War): [Battle of Sullivan's Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Sullivan's_Island) – [South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_South_Carolina) militia repel a British attack on [Charleston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charleston,_South_Carolina).

- [June 29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_29) – [American Revolutionary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War): [Battle of Turtle Gut Inlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Turtle_Gut_Inlet) – The American [Continental Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Navy) successfully challenges the British [Royal Navy](26061.md) blockade off [New Jersey](21648.md).

### July–August

- [July 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_2) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): The Continental Congress adopts the [Lee Resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Resolution).

- [July 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_4) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): [United States Declaration of Independence](31874.md) – The Continental Congress approves the declaration by the United States of its independence from the [Kingdom of Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Great_Britain).

  [^cite_note-Pocket_On_This_Day-7]

  &#x20;The text was drafted during June by [Benjamin Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Franklin).

- [July 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_4) - The [Albertina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albertina) art museum is founded in [Vienna, Austria](55866.md).

- [July 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_8) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution): The [Liberty Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_Bell) rings in Philadelphia for the first public reading of the Declaration of Independence.

- [July 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_9) – [American Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolution) – An angry mob in New York City topples and beheads the equestrian statue of [George III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_III) of Great Britain in [Bowling Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowling_Green_\(New_York_City\)); much of its metal content will be used to make musket balls.

- [July 12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_12) – Captain [James Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cook) sets off from [Plymouth](23508.md), England, in [HMS *Resolution*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Resolution_\(1771\)) on his [third voyage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_voyage_of_James_Cook), to the Pacific Ocean and [Arctic](36971.md), which will be fatal to him.

- [July 21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_21) – [Mozart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_Amadeus_Mozart)'s [Serenade No. 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serenade_No._7_\(Mozart\)) (the "Haffner") is first performed in [Salzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salzburg), Austria.

- [July 29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_29) – [Domínguez–Escalante expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dom%C3%ADnguez%E2%80%93Escalante_expedition): Francisco Silvestre Vélez de Escalante, Francisco Atanasio Domínguez and eight other Spaniards set out from Santa Fe on an 1800-mile (2900 km) trek through the American Southwest. They are the first Europeans to explore the vast region between the Rockies and the Sierras.

  [^cite_note-Saunt-8]

- [August 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_1) – The [Viceroyalty of the Río de la Plata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viceroyalty_of_the_R%C3%ADo_de_la_Plata) is established in southern South America.

- [August 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_2) – Delegates of most of the American colonies sign the [Declaration of Independence](31874.md).
