**Lordegan** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) and [Luri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luri_language): لردگان)[^cite_note-6] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Lordegan_County\)) of [Lordegan County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lordegan_County), [Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari](448057.md) province, [Iran](14653.md), serving as capital of both the county and the district.[^cite_note-Chaharmahal_and_Bakhtiari_Province_Structure-7]

## Demographics

### Ethnicity

The city is populated by [Lurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lurs).[^cite_note-8]

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 22,728 in 4,459 households.[^cite_note-2006_Chaharmahal_and_Bakhtiari_Province-9] The following census in 2011 counted 35,276 people in 8,037 households.[^cite_note-2011_Chaharmahal_and_Bakhtiari_Province-10] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 40,528 people in 10,482 households.[^cite_note-2016_Chaharmahal_and_Bakhtiari_Province-4]

## Civilian casualties of 2025-2026 Iranian protests in Lordegan

Beginning on 28 December 2025, mass demonstrations erupted across multiple cities in [Iran](14653.md) amid a deepening [economic crisis](78620967.md) and widespread dissatisfaction with the [government](50913538.md). While initially sparked by frustration over skyrocketing inflation, rising food prices, and the severe depreciation of the [Iranian rial](708656.md), the protests quickly evolved into a broader movement demanding an end to the Islamic Republic's rule.[^cite_note-11]

[Hengaw](72014392.md) Organization of Human Rights reported the killings of Ahmad Jalil, 21, and Sajjad Valamanesh, 28, both Lors from Lordegan.[^cite_note-\:0-12][^cite_note-\:1-13][^cite_note-14] According to information received by the Hengaw Organization for Human Rights, Jalil and Valamanesh were shot with live ammunition by government forces during public protests on Thursday, 1 January 2026, and later died from their injuries.[^cite_note-\:0-12][^cite_note-\:1-13]
