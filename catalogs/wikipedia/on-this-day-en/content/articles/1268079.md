**Isa Air Base** (**IAB**), formerly known as **Shaikh Isa Air Base** ([ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **OBBS**), is on the shore of the [Persian Gulf](24761.md) in southern [Bahrain](18933277.md).

It hosts the [Royal Bahraini Air Force](1268112.md) (RBAF) Fighter Wing and the two squadrons that comprise it.

## Gulf War

During the first [Gulf War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War), some of the [Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalition_of_Gulf_War) forces, having trouble with the Arab pronunciation, knew the base under the name "[Shakey's Pizza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakey's_Pizza)".[^cite_note-2]

The [384th Air Expeditionary Wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/384th_Air_Expeditionary_Wing) operated from the base in 2003–2004.

## 2020s



[U.S. Marine Corps](17349325.md) [Marine Aircraft Group 11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_Aircraft_Group_11) aircraft at Sheik Isa, in 1991.

### Bahrain

The base is home to the RBAF's **1st Fighter Wing**:

- 1st Fighter Squadron – [Lockheed Martin F-16 Fighting Falcon C/D Block 40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-16_Fighting_Falcon)

- 2nd Fighter Squadron – [F-16C/D Block 40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-16_Fighting_Falcon)

- 6th Fighter Squadron – [Northrop F-5E/F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_F-5)

- 5th Squadron – [British Aerospace Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Aerospace_Hawk)

- 4th Squadron – [Slingsby T-67 Firefly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slingsby_T-67_Firefly)

### United States

Further information: [Military intervention against ISIL order of battle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_intervention_against_ISIL_order_of_battle)

In March 2009, the United States Air Force established a camp on Isa Air Base to support aerial port operations. The unit was designated a detachment of the [379th Air Expeditionary Wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/379th_Air_Expeditionary_Wing) of [Al Udeid Air Base](3830783.md), [Qatar](8238258.md). This detachment's purpose was to accelerate the delivery of [mine-resistant ambush-protected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mine-resistant_ambush-protected) all-terrain vehicles to U.S. forces in Afghanistan.

As part of its [operations against ISIL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_intervention_against_ISIL), the United States [deployed various aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_intervention_against_ISIL_order_of_battle) there.[^cite_note-AFMMAY15-59-3]

In August 2020, [KBR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KBR_\(company\)) took over base operations support services at Isa Air Base.[^cite_note-4]
