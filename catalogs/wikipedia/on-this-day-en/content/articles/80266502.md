The **Bolojan cabinet** is the 135th [government](6046282.md) of [Romania](25445.md), incumbent since 23 June 2025, and in a [caretaker capacity](240683.md) since 5 May 2026. The government is led by [Ilie Bolojan](78639092.md), the leader of the [National Liberal Party](863906.md) (PNL). On 5 May 2025, the day after the [2025 Romanian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Romanian_presidential_election), then incumbent prime minister [Marcel Ciolacu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Ciolacu) of the [Social Democratic Party](407177.md) (PSD) announced his resignation.[^cite_note-2]

On 16 June, President [Nicușor Dan](35543359.md) hinted that he would appoint Bolojan prime minister later that week, with a coalition deal with the PSD being confirmed the following day.[^cite_note-3] The deal involved Bolojan being appointed prime minister first yet with the mention that he should leave office in 2027 for a PSD prime minister to take over the position.[^cite_note-agreement-1] Bolojan was formally nominated on 20 June and confirmed by the [Chamber of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies_\(Romania\)) in a 301 to 9 vote on 23 June.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

The PSD subsequently withdrew from the government in April 2026 and redacted a [motion of no confidence](208101.md) together with the far-right party [Alliance for the Union of Romanians](64666536.md) (AUR). According to *[The Wall Street Journal](173070.md)*, the United States ambassador to Greece [Kimberly Guilfoyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimberly_Guilfoyle) allegedly said at a dinner in [Athens](1216.md) in March 2026 that the United States had been involved in the fall of the Bolojan government, adding that the United States could do the same to any country; Guilfoyle's lawyer disputed the account,[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] while the *[Financial Times](136566.md)* independently corroborated *The Wall Street Journal*'s report.[^cite_note-9]
