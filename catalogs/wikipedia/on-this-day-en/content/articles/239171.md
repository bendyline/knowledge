[Administratively](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_division), [Cuba](5042481.md) is divided into 15 provinces and one special [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality) (the [Isla de la Juventud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isla_de_la_Juventud)). The current structure has been in place since August 2010, when the [then-La Habana Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Habana_Province_\(1976%E2%80%932010\)) was divided into [Artemisa Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemisa_Province) and [Mayabeque Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayabeque_Province).

## History

### 1879–1976



Cuba's provinces, 1879 to 1976



Cuba's provinces on a 1910s map

The provinces were created in 1879 by the Spanish colonial government. From 1879 to 1976, Cuba was divided into six provinces, which maintained with little changes the same boundaries and capital cities, although with modifications in official names. These "historical" provinces are the following (from west to east):

1. Pinar del Río

2. La Habana, included the city of [Havana](49719.md), current [Mayabeque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayabeque_Province), some municipalities of current [Artemisa Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemisa_Province) (prior to 1970: 5 municipalities; from 1970 to 2011, 8 municipalities, including [Artemisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemisa) city itself). [Isla de Pinos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isla_de_Pinos) ("Isle of Pines") was considered a "special municipality" in the province of La Habana.

3. Matanzas

4. Las Villas (before 1940 named "[Santa Clara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara_Province)"), contained the present-day provinces of [Cienfuegos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cienfuegos_Province), [Villa Clara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villa_Clara_Province), [Sancti Spíritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sancti_Sp%C3%ADritus_Province), and Southern [Matanzas](1386915.md) Province.

5. Camagüey (before 1899 named "Puerto Príncipe"), contained the present-day provinces of [Camagüey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camag%C3%BCey_Province) and [Ciego de Ávila](460424.md), as well as two municipalities of current [Las Tunas Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Tunas_Province) (prior to 1970).

6. [Oriente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oriente_Province) (before 1905 named "Santiago de Cuba"), contained the present-day provinces of [Las Tunas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Tunas_Province), [Granma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granma_Province), [Holguín](460435.md), [Santiago de Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_de_Cuba_Province) and [Guantánamo](18952312.md)

### 1976–2011



Cuba's provinces as of December 1976

In December 1976, the original six provinces were reconstituted into 14 provinces plus one special municipality:[^cite_note-2]

1. [Pinar del Río](460401.md)

2. [La Habana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Habana_Province_\(1976%E2%80%932010\))

3. [Ciudad de La Habana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciudad_de_La_Habana_Province)

4. [Matanzas](460412.md)

5. [Cienfuegos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cienfuegos_Province)

6. [Villa Clara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villa_Clara_Province)

7. [Sancti Spíritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sancti_Sp%C3%ADritus_Province)

8. [Ciego de Ávila](460424.md)

9. [Camagüey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camag%C3%BCey_Province)

10. [Las Tunas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Tunas_Province)

11. [Granma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granma_Province)

12. [Holguín](460435.md)

13. [Santiago de Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_de_Cuba_Province)

14. [Guantánamo](18952312.md)

15. [Isla de Pinos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isla_de_Pinos) ("special municipality")

Isla de Pinos was renamed [Isla de la Juventud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isla_de_la_Juventud) on 3 August 1978.[^cite_note-3]

### 2011–present

In August 2010, the [Cuban National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_National_Assembly) split the [then-La Habana Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Habana_Province_\(1976%E2%80%932010\)) into two new provinces: [Artemisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemisa_Province) (which incorporated three eastern municipalities of neighboring [Pinar del Río](460401.md)) and [Mayabeque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayabeque_Province). The new provinces started functioning from 1 January 2011.[^cite_note-4] Havana City Province ([Ciudad de La Habana Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciudad_de_La_Habana_Province)) recovered its original name, [La Habana Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Habana_Province).
