# Southern Iran

Not to be confused with administrative [Regions of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Iran).



The provinces of Southern Iran



A satellite view of southern Iran

**Southern Iran** consists of the southern mountain ranges of [Zagros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros) and [Central Iranian Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Iranian_Range), [Khuzestan Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan_Plain) and the northern coasts of [Persian Gulf](24761.md) and [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md).

## Definition

It includes the provinces of [Fars](448049.md) ([Persia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fars_territory)), [Kohgiluyeh and Buyer Ahmad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohgiluyeh_and_Buyer_Ahmad_Province), [Hormozgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hormozgan), and [Bushehr](448037.md). Sometimes [Khuzestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan) and [Kerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerman_province) are also included in this region. The major cities in the region are [Shiraz](39622.md), [Bandarabbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandarabbas), and [Kerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerman).[^cite_note-1]

## Demographics

The south of Iran is very diverse in terms of ethnicity, which includes [Persians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persians) (including Kohmereh, Basseri, Achomi, etc.), [Arabs](2185.md) and [Iranians of African origin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Iranians). In addition, other ethnic minorities of Iran migrated to this region for various reasons; these include [Azerbaijanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijanis), [Mazandaranis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazanderani_people), [Gilaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilaks), [Talysh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talysh_people), [Tats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tat_people_\(Iran\)), [Kurds](17068.md), and [Lurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lurs), who migrated from other regions of Iran.

## Climate

- [Hot desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BWh) (BWh) in the plains and the coast of the Persian Gulf.

- [Cold semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSk) (BSk) in the mountain ranges.
