**Bhamo** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): ဗန်းမော်မြို့ *ban: mau mrui.*, also spelt **Banmaw**), historically known as **Man Maw** ([Shan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shan_language): မၢၼ်ႈမူဝ်ႇ; [Tai Nüa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tai_N%C3%BCa_language): ᥛᥫᥒᥰ ᥛᥨᥝᥱ) or **Hsinkai** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 新街; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Xīnjiē*) is a city in [Kachin State](493369.md) in northern [Myanmar](19457.md), 186 km (116 mi) south of the state capital, ([Myitkyina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myitkyina)). It is on the [Ayeyarwady River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayeyarwady_River). It lies within 65 km (40 mi) of the border with Yunnan Province, China. The population consists primarily of residents [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_Chinese) or [Shan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shan_people) descent, with [Kachin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kachin_people) peoples primarily occupying the hills around the town. It is the administrative center of [Bhamo District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhamo_District) and [Bhamo Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhamo_Township).

## Etymology

Bhamo is derived from the [Shan language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shan_language) placename "Man Maw" ([Shan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shan_language): မၢၼ်ႈမေႃႇး, [\[maan˧ mɔ˧\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Shan_and_Tai_Lue); [Tai Nüa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tai_N%C3%BCa_language): ᥛᥫᥒᥰ ᥛᥨᥝᥱ), meaning "potter's village" or "village of jars."[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## History



Bhamo was once called **Sampanago**, the capital of the now-extinct [Shan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shan_people) predecessor kingdom of [Man Maw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_Maw_State).[^cite_note-5] The ruins of the old city walls, dating from the fifth century, are found some 5 kilometres (3.1 mi) from the modern town.

From 1869 to 1879, it was the seat of British colonial Assistant political agent, subordinate to the Resident with the [king](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ava_Kingdom) of and in [Ava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inwa). In the early 20th century, due to its location at the highest navigable point of the river, it formed a part of [caravan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caravan_\(travellers\)) routes bringing [jade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade) from [India](14533.md) to [China](5405.md).[^cite_note-6]

## Contemporary



Bhamo is the terminus of river ferries from Mandalay. There is no river ferry between Bhamo and the state capital Myitkyina. The land route between Bhamo and [Mu Se District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_Se_District) (Muse, part of northern [Shan State](493376.md)) is in poor condition.

Bhamo is one of the official border trading towns between China and Myanmar.

## Education

There are three universities in Bhamo: Banmaw University; Technological University (Banmaw); and University of Computer Science (Banmaw).

## Climate



Bhamo has a climate that lies in the transition between [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Aw*) and [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Cwa*). Temperatures are very warm throughout the year, although the winter months (December–February) are milder. There is a winter dry season (November–April) and a summer wet season (May–October).
