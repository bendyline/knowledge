A **provisional government**, also called an **interim,** **emergency**, or **transitional government**,[^cite_note-1] is a temporary government formed to manage a period of transition, often following [state collapse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_collapse), [revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution), [civil war](7085.md), or some combination thereof.

Provisional governments generally come to power in connection with the sudden, catastrophic and irreversible collapse of the previous political system, resulting from [revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution), [coup d'état](25914154.md), [civil war](7085.md), [debellatio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debellatio), [economic collapse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_collapse), the death of a [strongman ruler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_strongman), or other circumstances which have resulted in [state collapse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_collapse). Questions of [democratic transition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_transition) and [state-building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State-building) are often fundamental to the formation and policies of such governments.

Provisional governments maintain [power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_\(social_and_political\)) until a permanent government can be appointed by a regular political process, which is generally an [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election).[^cite_note-2] They are usually heavily involved with the process of defining the [legal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_structure) and [constitutional basis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution) of their permanent successors (or at least the [constituent assembly](266437.md) entrusted to draw up a such an arrangement), including institutional structure, [human rights](13831.md) regimes, macroeconomic structure, and foreign relations.[^cite_note-ShainLinz1992-3]

Provisional governments differ from [caretaker governments](240683.md), which are responsible for governing within an established [parliamentary system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system) and serve temporarily after an election, [vote of no confidence](208101.md) or [cabinet crisis](52674452.md), until a new government can be appointed.[^cite_note-ShainLinz1992-3] Caretaker governments operate entirely within the existing constitutional framework and most countries tightly circumscribe their authority, either by [convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convention_\(political_norm\)) or more formal legal means. Conversely, provisional governments, which typically arise from catastrophic institutional collapse, often have the ability to [rule by decree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_by_decree) in the absence of a functional [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) (at least until a [constituent assembly](266437.md) or other transitional legislative assembly can be convened) and, of necessity given their role in crisis response, exercise broad powers with few restrictions (often without even a national constitution).

In opinion of Yossi Shain and Juan J. Linz, provisional governments can be classified to four groups:[^cite_note-ShainLinz_BetweenStates_1995_5psl-4]

1. Revolutionary provisional governments (when the former regime is overthrown and the power belongs to the people who have overthrown it).

2. Power sharing provisional governments (when the power is shared between former regime and the ones who are trying to change it).

3. Incumbent provisional governments (when the power during transitional period belongs to the former regime).

4. International provisional governments (when the power during the transitional period belongs to the international community).

The establishment of provisional governments is frequently tied to the implementation of [transitional justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_justice).[^cite_note-5] Provisional governments may be responsible for implementing transitional justice measures as part of the path to establishing a permanent government structure.

The early provisional governments were created to prepare for the return of royal rule. Irregularly convened assemblies during the [English Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Revolution), such as [Confederate Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_Ireland) (1641–49), were described as "provisional". The [Continental Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Congress), a convention of delegates from [13 British colonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteen_Colonies) on the east coast of North America, became the provisional government of the [United States](3434750.md) in 1776, during the [American Revolutionary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War). The government shed its provisional status in 1781, following ratification of the [Articles of Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Articles_of_Confederation), and continued in existence as the [Congress of the Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_the_Confederation) until it was supplanted by the [United States Congress](31756.md) in 1789.

The practice of using "provisional government" as part of a formal name can be traced to [Talleyrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talleyrand)'s government in France in 1814. In 1843, [American pioneers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_pioneer) in the [Oregon Country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_Country), in the [Pacific Northwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Northwest) region of North America established the [Provisional Government of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_Oregon)—as the U.S. federal government had not yet extended its jurisdiction over the region—which existed until March 1849. The numerous provisional governments during the [Revolutions of 1848](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutions_of_1848) gave the word its modern meaning: a temporary central government appointed following the overthrow or collapse of the previous regime, with a mandate to prepare for national elections.

## Africa

### 20th century

-  [Provisional Government of the Algerian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_the_Algerian_Republic) (1958–1962) (guerrilla movement)

-  The [Derg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derg) (1974–1987), formed during the [Ethiopian Civil War](6572888.md) following the [overthrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_Ethiopian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) of emperor [Haile Selassie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haile_Selassie). Ultimately absorbed into the [People's Democratic Republic of Ethiopia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Democratic_Republic_of_Ethiopia).

-  [Zimbabwe Rhodesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe_Rhodesia) (1979–1980), a short-lived yet internationally unrecognized sovereign state formed in the waning years of the [Rhodesian Bush War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodesian_Bush_War), in contrast with the reestablished [Southern Rhodesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Rhodesia). Ultimately dissolved in favor of granting independence to Southern Rhodesia as [Zimbabwe](34399.md).

-  [Southern Rhodesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Rhodesia) (1979–1980), a provisional reestablishment of the British colony in contrast with the internationally unrecognized [Zimbabwe Rhodesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe_Rhodesia). The brief return to colonial rule served to provide British supervision over the end of the [Rhodesian Bush War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodesian_Bush_War) and Rhodesia's transition to majority rule as an independent [Zimbabwe](34399.md).

-  [Transitional Military Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Military_Council_\(1985\)) (1985–1986), interim government of Sudan following the [1985 Sudanese coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Sudanese_coup_d'%C3%A9tat), dissolved following the [1986 Sudanese parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Sudanese_parliamentary_election).

-  [Transitional Government of National Unity (Namibia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Government_of_National_Unity_\(Namibia\)) (1985–1989), interim government during the end of the [South African Border War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Border_War)

-  [Political Bureau of the Central Committee of FRELIMO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_Bureau_of_the_Central_Committee_of_FRELIMO) (1986), interim ruling body of the [People's Republic of Mozambique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Republic_of_Mozambique) following the death of president [Samora Machel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samora_Machel) in the [1986 Mozambican Tupolev Tu-134 crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Mozambican_Tupolev_Tu-134_crash). Dissolved following the election of [Joaquim Chissano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joaquim_Chissano) as Machel's successor later that year.

-  [Transitional Government of Ethiopia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Government_of_Ethiopia) (1991–1995), established upon the [Ethiopian People's Revolutionary Democratic Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopian_People's_Revolutionary_Democratic_Front)'s overthrow of the [People's Democratic Republic of Ethiopia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Democratic_Republic_of_Ethiopia) at the end of the [Ethiopian Civil War](6572888.md). Succeeded by the [Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia](187749.md).

-  [Interim Government of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interim_Government_of_Somalia) (1991–1997), established after the collapse of the [Somali Democratic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_Democratic_Republic) and the onset of the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md).

-  [Provisional Government of Eritrea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_Eritrea) (1993), established after [independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_of_Eritrea) from [Ethiopia](187749.md)

-  [Armed Forces Provisional Ruling Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_Provisional_Ruling_Council) (1994–1996), interim ruling body of [the Gambia](65656.md) following the [1994 Gambian coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Gambian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat). Dissolved after [Yahya Jammeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahya_Jammeh), the head of the ruling council, was [elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996_Gambian_presidential_election) and inaugurated as [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Gambia).

### 21st century

As of 2025, nine African countries currently have provisional governments: [Burkina Faso](3470.md), [Guinea](12176.md), [Guinea-Bissau](12186.md), [Libya](17633.md), [Madagascar](18964.md), [Mali](19127.md), [Niger](21373.md), [South Sudan](32350676.md), and [Sudan](27421.md).

-  [Transitional National Government of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_National_Government_of_Somalia) (2000–2004), established at the Somalia National Peace Conference in opposition to the [Somalia Reconciliation and Restoration Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalia_Reconciliation_and_Restoration_Council), formed by rival political factions. Succeeded by the [Transitional Federal Government of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Government_of_Somalia).

-  [Transitional Government of the Democratic Republic of the Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Government_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) (2003–2006), established in 2003 following the conclusion of the [Second Congo War](877492.md).

-  [National Transitional Legislative Assembly of Liberia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Transitional_Legislative_Assembly_of_Liberia) (2003–2006), [Liberia](17791.md)'s legislative body during the country's transition from civil war to democratic rule.

-  [Transitional Federal Government of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Government_of_Somalia) (2004–2012), established as the successor to the [Transitional National Government of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_National_Government_of_Somalia) as part of an effort to end the [Islamic Courts Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Courts_Union)'s rule over the nation. Dissolved following the ratification of a new constitution which declared Somalia an [Islamic state](2646438.md).

## Americas

### Pre-19th century

-  [Second Continental Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Continental_Congress) (1775–1781), interim ruling body of the [United Colonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Colonies) and [United States](3434750.md) immediately before and after [declaring independence](31874.md) from the [British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire). Following the ratification of the [Articles of Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Articles_of_Confederation), the Continental Congress was absorbed into the [Congress of the Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_the_Confederation), which subsequently became the [United States Congress](31756.md) upon ratification of the [Constitution](31644.md).

### 19th century

-  [Primera Junta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primera_Junta) (1810), interim ruling body of the [Viceroyalty of the Río de la Plata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viceroyalty_of_the_R%C3%ADo_de_la_Plata) following [Baltasar Hidalgo de Cisneros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltasar_Hidalgo_de_Cisneros)' resignation during the [May Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_Revolution). Succeeded by the [Junta Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junta_Grande) after seven months in power.

-  [Junta Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junta_Grande) (1810–1811), successor to the [Primera Junta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primera_Junta). Aimed to facilitate the transition to a junta that represented all cities in the [Viceroyalty of the Río de la Plata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viceroyalty_of_the_R%C3%ADo_de_la_Plata), only to meet limited recognition within its own territory. Succeeded by the [First Triumvirate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Triumvirate_\(Argentina\)).

-  [Government Junta of Chile (1810)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Junta_of_Chile_\(1810\)), interim government of Chile following the deposition and imprisonment of King [Ferdinand VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_VII) of Spain by [Napoleon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon).

-  [Provisional Government of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_Mexico) (1823–1824), interim ruling body of [Mexico](3966054.md) following the dissolution of the [First Mexican Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Mexican_Empire). Succeeded by the [First Mexican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Mexican_Republic) following the ratification of the [1824 Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1824_Constitution_of_Mexico).

-  [Provisional governorship of José Rondeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_governorship_of_Jos%C3%A9_Rondeau) (1828–1830), interim ruling body of [Uruguay](31843.md) following the [Preliminary Peace Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preliminary_Peace_Convention_\(1828\)), in which the [Empire of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_of_Brazil) and the [United Provinces of the Río de la Plata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Provinces_of_the_R%C3%ADo_de_la_Plata) recognized the country's independence. Dissolved following Rondeau's resignation.

-  [Texian Consultation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consultation_\(Texas\)) (1835–1836), interim ruling body of [Mexican Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Texas) during the [Texas Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Revolution). Ultimately collapsed due to political tensions following the [Matamoros Expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matamoros_Expedition).

-  Presidency of [David G. Burnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_G._Burnet) (1836), appointed by the [Convention of 1836](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convention_of_1836) in the aftermath of the [Texas Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Declaration_of_Independence) and Mexico's victory in the [Battle of the Alamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Alamo). Succeeded by the [Sam Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Houston) administration following the latter's [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1836_Republic_of_Texas_presidential_election) as the first official [president of the Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Republic_of_Texas).

-  [Provisional Government of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_Oregon) (*1841*/1843–1849), interim ruling body of [Oregon Country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_Country) formed by settling [pioneers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_pioneer) in the midst of the [Oregon boundary dispute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_boundary_dispute). While the government was established during the [Champoeg Meetings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champoeg_Meetings) in 1841, it did not exercise rule until the adoption of the [Organic Laws of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organic_Laws_of_Oregon) and the meetings' conclusion two years later, with the organic laws specifying that the government would only administer the territory "until such time as the United States of America extend their jurisdiction over us."

  [^cite_note-21]

-  [Interim government of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interim_government_of_California) (1846–1850), military government of [California](5407.md) established during the [Mexican–American War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican%E2%80%93American_War). Dissolved following the ratification of the [California Statehood Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Statehood_Act), which [admitted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admission_to_the_Union) California to the Union as the 31st state.

-  [U.S. provisional government of New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._provisional_government_of_New_Mexico) (1846–1850), interim ruling body of [New Mexico](21649.md) following the region's occupation by the [United States](3434750.md) during the [Mexican–American War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican%E2%80%93American_War). Succeeded by the [New Mexico Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico_Territory) in 1850 before being [admitted to the Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admission_to_the_Union) as the 47th state in 1912.

-  [Provisional Congress of the Confederate States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Congress_of_the_Confederate_States) (1861–1862), interim ruling body of the [Confederate States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_America) following the member states' [secession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secession_in_the_United_States) from the [United States of America](3434750.md). Dissolved following the [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1861_Confederate_States_presidential_election) and inauguration of [Jefferson Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jefferson_Davis) as [President of the Confederate States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Confederate_States_of_America).

-  [Confederate government of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_government_of_Kentucky) (1861–c. 1865), a [shadow government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_of_government) formed by pro-Confederate state legislators in [Kentucky](16846.md). Though it never replaced the official state government, it was recognized by and admitted to the [Confederate States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_America). No documentation exists regarding when the provisional government dissolved, but historians assume that it did so upon the conclusion of the [American Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War).

### World War II

-  [Provisional Government of the Republic of China (1937–40)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_the_Republic_of_China_\(1937%E2%80%9340\)), established by the [Empire of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_of_Japan) after its invasion of Eastern China

- [Azad Hind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azad_Hind) [Provisional Government of Free India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azad_Hind) (1943–1945), commonly known as *Azad Hind*, established by Indian nationalists in southeast Asia, had nominal sovereignty over Axis-controlled Indian territories, and had diplomatic relationships with eleven countries including Germany, Italy, Japan, Philippines, and the Soviet Union. It was headed by [Subhas Chandra Bose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subhas_Chandra_Bose), who was the Head of the State and Prime Minister, who was also Supreme Commander of the [Indian National Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Army). Future Indonesian President [Sukarno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukarno) and Vice President [Mohammed Hatta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_Hatta) were largely involved in both organizations, and they were ultimately succeeded by the present-day [Government of Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Indonesia) in accordance with the [Constitution of Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Indonesia) (which was drafted during the two committees' existence).

## Europe

### Pre-World War I

-  [Confederate Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_Ireland) (1642–1652), formed by Catholic aristocrats, [landed gentry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landed_gentry), clergy, and military leaders after the [Irish Rebellion of 1641](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Rebellion_of_1641). Overthrown by the [Commonwealth of England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_England) in the [Cromwellian conquest of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cromwellian_conquest_of_Ireland).

-  [National Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Convention) (1792–1795), interim ruling body of the [Kingdom of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_France_\(1791%E2%80%9392\)) and the [French First Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_First_Republic) during the [French Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolution). Succeeded by the [French Directory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Directory) following the [fall of Maximilien Robespierre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Maximilien_Robespierre) and the drafting of a new constitution.

-  [Provisional Council of the Duchy of Masovia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Council_of_the_Duchy_of_Masovia) (1794), interim government of the [Masovian Voivodeship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masovian_Voivodeship_\(1526%E2%80%931795\)) established during the [Kościuszko Uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ko%C5%9Bciuszko_Uprising). Ultimately subordinated to the [Supreme National Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_National_Council).

-  [Provisional Representatives of the People of Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Representatives_of_the_People_of_Holland) (1795–1796), interim government of the [Batavian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batavian_Republic) following the abolition of the [Dutch Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Republic) in the [Batavian Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batavian_Revolution). Abolished following the [States General of the Batavian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_General_of_the_Batavian_Republic)'s replacement by the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_the_Batavian_Republic).

-  [Lithuanian Provisional Governing Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Provisional_Governing_Commission) (1812–1813), established by [Napoleon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon) during the [French invasion of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_invasion_of_Russia).

  [^cite_note-GenzelisLRT-85]

  &#x20;Merged with the [General Confederation of the Kingdom of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Confederation_of_the_Kingdom_of_Poland) to create the united Kingdom of Poland.

-  [Provisional Government of Belgium (1814–1815)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_Belgium_\(1814%E2%80%931815\)), interim ruling body of the [Southern Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Netherlands) following the collapse of French rule during the [War of the Sixth Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Sixth_Coalition). Dissolved following the annexation of the region by the [United Kingdom of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom_of_the_Netherlands).

-  [French Provisional Government of 1814](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Provisional_Government_of_1814), interim ruling body of the [First French Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_French_Empire) following the surrender of Paris and deposition of [Napoleon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon) during the [War of the Sixth Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Sixth_Coalition). Succeeded by the [government of the first Bourbon restoration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_first_Bourbon_restoration) following [Louis XVIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XVIII)'s return to the throne and the reestablishment of the [Kingdom of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_France).

-  [First Hellenic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Hellenic_Republic) (1822–1832), established by anti-Ottoman rebels during the [Greek War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_War_of_Independence). Succeeded by the [Kingdom of Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Greece) following the assassination of [Augustinos Kapodistrias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustinos_Kapodistrias) and subsequent intervention by [Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom_of_Great_Britain_and_Ireland), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_Monarchy) and [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire).

-  [Provisional Government of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_Belgium) (1830–1831), established as a successor to the Revolutionary Committee following the success of the [Belgian Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgian_Revolution). Dissolved following the proclamation of the [Constitution of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Belgium) and the consequent establishment of the [Kingdom of Belgium](3343.md).

-  [Wellington caretaker ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellington_caretaker_ministry) (1834), interim ruling body of the [United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom_of_Great_Britain_and_Ireland) following [William IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_IV)'s dismissal of [William Lamb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lamb,_2nd_Viscount_Melbourne)'s administration. [Arthur Wellesley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Wellesley,_1st_Duke_of_Wellington) recommended [Robert Peel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Peel) as Lamb's successor; because Peel was in the [Kingdom of Sardinia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Sardinia) at the time, Wellesley headed a caretaker administration until Peel's return to Britain.

-  [French Provisional Government of 1848](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Provisional_Government_of_1848), interim ruling body of the [French Second Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Second_Republic) established after the [French Revolution of 1848](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolution_of_1848), which ended the [July Monarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_Monarchy). Succeeded by the [French Executive Commission of 1848](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Executive_Commission_of_1848).

-  [Provisional Government of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Days_of_Milan) (1848), formed by Milanese insurgents during the [First Italian War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Italian_War_of_Independence). Dissolved following the [Austrian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Empire)'s victory in the [Battle of Custoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Custoza_\(1848\)).

-  Guvernul vremelnicesc (1848), formed by revolutionary forces during the [Wallachian Revolution of 1848](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallachian_Revolution_of_1848). Dissolved following the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md)'s intervention in the battle of Dealul Spirii.

-  [Dictator Executive Commission in Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dictator_Executive_Commission_in_Warsaw) and [National Civil Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Civil_Government) (1863), interim ruling bodies of [Congress Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_Poland) during the [January Uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_Uprising), formed as the successor to the underground [Polish National Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_National_Government_\(January_Uprising\)). The coexisting provisional governments were both *[de jure](43594.md)* ruled by [Marian Langiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marian_Langiewicz), whose arrest led to their dissolution in favor of a single coalition government.

-  [Provisional Government of Spain (1868–1871)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_\(1868-1871\)), established after the [Spanish Glorious Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glorious_Revolution_\(Spain\)) pending the election of a new Constitutional Monarch.

-  [Government of National Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_National_Defense) (1870–1871), interim ruling body of the [French Third Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Third_Republic) following the collapse of the [Second French Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_French_Empire) during the [Franco-Prussian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco-Prussian_War). Replaced by the [Cabinet Dufaure I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_Dufaure_I_\(France\)) following the [1871 French legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1871_French_legislative_election).

-  [Provisional Government of the Portuguese Republic (1910–1911)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5_October_1910_revolution#The_first_steps_of_the_Republic), established in the aftermath of the [Republican Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5_October_1910_Revolution) that overthrow the Portuguese monarchy.

-  [Democratic Government of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Government_of_Albania) (1944–1946), formed by the [National Liberation Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Liberation_Movement_\(Albania\)) following the successful expulsion of the [German occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_occupation_of_Albania). Succeeded by the [People's Republic of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Socialist_Republic_of_Albania) after interim prime minister [Enver Hoxha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enver_Hoxha)'s expulsion of king [Zog I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zog_I) and the [Democratic Front of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Front_of_Albania)'s dominance of the [1945 Albanian parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945_Albanian_parliamentary_election).

-  [Provisional Government of the Republic of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_the_Republic_of_Poland) (1944–1945), established by the [State National Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_National_Council) with the intention of creating a Soviet-aligned communist Poland, contrary to the western-aligned [Polish government-in-exile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_government-in-exile) (which it did not recognize). Succeeded by the [Provisional Government of National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_National_Unity).

-  [Flensburg Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flensburg_Government) (1945), established following the suicides of [Adolf Hitler](2731583.md) and [Joseph Goebbels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Goebbels) during the closing days of the [Third Reich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Reich).

-  [Provisional Government of National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_National_Unity) (1945–1947), established as the successor to the [Provisional Government of the Republic of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_the_Republic_of_Poland). Dissolved following the establishment of the [Polish People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_People's_Republic).

-  [Interim National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interim_National_Assembly_\(Czechoslovakia\)) (1945–1946), provisional ruling body of the [Third Czechoslovak Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Czechoslovak_Republic). Succeeded by the [Constituent National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituent_National_Assembly_\(Czechoslovakia\)) following the [1946 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1946_Czechoslovak_parliamentary_election).

Provisional governments were also established throughout Europe as [occupied nations were liberated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberation_of_Europe) from [Nazi occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupied_Europe) by the [Allies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allies_of_World_War_II).

### Cold War

-  [Provisional Democratic Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Democratic_Government) (1947–1950), formed by the [Communist Party of Greece](356444.md) during the [Greek Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_Civil_War) as the successor to the World War II-era [Political Committee of National Liberation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_Committee_of_National_Liberation). Withdrew from their territories in northern Greece after their defeat during [Operation Pyrsos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Pyrsos) in 1949 and continued as a [government-in-exile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government-in-exile) before dissolving fourteen months later.

-  [Revolutionary Workers'-Peasants' Government of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_Workers'-Peasants'_Government_of_Hungary) (1956), interim government of the [Hungarian People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_People's_Republic) established by pro-Soviet forces during the [Hungarian Revolution of 1956](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Revolution_of_1956), in opposition to the revolutionary-backed [governments of Imre Nagy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governments_of_Imre_Nagy). Succeeded by the mainline [János Kádár](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%A1nos_K%C3%A1d%C3%A1r) government following the Soviet invasion of Hungary and consequent suppression of the third Nagy government.

-  [National Salvation Junta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Salvation_Junta) and [six provisional governments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_transition_to_democracy) that followed after the [Carnation Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnation_Revolution) and until the [first democratically elected government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Constitutional_Government_of_Portugal) under the new [Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Constitution) was sworn in (25 April 1974 – 23 July 1976)

### Collapse of the USSR and aftermath

-  Government of National Understanding, established in [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia) after the [Velvet Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velvet_Revolution) of 1989.

-  [National Salvation Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Salvation_Front_\(Romania\)) (1989–1990), established in [Romania](25445.md) after the fall and execution of [Nicolae Ceaușescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolae_Ceau%C8%99escu) and [the end](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Revolution) of the [Socialist Republic of Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Republic_of_Romania) in 1989. Later restructured into a [big tent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_tent) political party following the establishment of a post-communist successor government in 1990, winning the [general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Romanian_general_election) that year before breaking up into the [Democratic National Salvation Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_National_Salvation_Front) and the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Romania\)) in 1992.

-  [Estonian Interim Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_restoration_of_Independence) (1990–1992)

-  [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-Soviet_transition_in_Ukraine)'s transitional government (1991–1996) formed after the [1991 Ukrainian independence referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Ukrainian_independence_referendum), which dissolved the [Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic) and formalized Ukraine's independence from the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). Officially declared the legal successor of the Ukrainian SSR after [Mykola Plaviuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mykola_Plaviuk), president of the [government in exile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Ukrainian_People's_Republic_in_exile), ceded his powers to [Leonid Kravchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Kravchuk), the winner of the [1991 Ukrainian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Ukrainian_presidential_election). Ultimately succeeded by the current [government](1372585.md) following a parliamentary motion to adopt [a new constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Ukraine), ending the post-Soviet transition.

-  [United Nations Transitional Administration for Eastern Slavonia, Baranja and Western Sirmium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Transitional_Administration_for_Eastern_Slavonia,_Baranja_and_Western_Sirmium) (1996–1998), formed to peacefully reintegrate self-proclaimed [Eastern Slavonia, Baranja and Western Syrmia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Slavonia,_Baranja_and_Western_Syrmia_\(1995%E2%80%931998\)) to [Croatia](5573.md) after the signing of the 1995 [Erdut Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdut_Agreement).

-  [United Nations Interim Administration Mission in Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Interim_Administration_Mission_in_Kosovo) (1999–*[de facto](43594.md)* 2008), formed to stabilize Kosovo in the aftermath of the [Kosovo War](16760.md). Never formally dissolved, but *[de facto](43594.md)* replaced by the [Republic of Kosovo](17391.md) after [unilaterally declaring independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Kosovo_declaration_of_independence) in 2008.

### 21st century

As of 2025 in Europe, only [Belarus](3457.md) and territories of [European Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Russia) and [Ukraine](31750.md) occupied by each other during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) have provisional governments. The former was established by the opposition in parallel with the [government of the Republic of Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Belarus), while the latter two exist as occupation governments in opposition to the [government of Russia](9787373.md) and the [government of Ukraine](1372585.md), respectively.

-  [Provisional Institutions of Self-Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Institutions_of_Self-Government) (2002–*[de facto](43594.md)* 2008), established by the [United Nations Interim Administration Mission in Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Interim_Administration_Mission_in_Kosovo) to facilitate local governance. Like the UN administration, the provisional institutions were never formally dissolved, but were *[de facto](43594.md)* replaced by the [Republic of Kosovo](17391.md) following the [2008 Kosovo declaration of independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Kosovo_declaration_of_independence).

-  [Belarusian Coordination Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordination_Council_\(Belarus\)) (2020–present) and the [United Transitional Cabinet of Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Transitional_Cabinet_of_Belarus) (2022–present), established in the aftermath of the [2020 Belarusian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Belarusian_presidential_election) and during subsequent [protests](64399515.md). The transitional government is supported and recognized by [Lithuania](17675.md).

-  [Russian temporary administrative agencies in occupied Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_temporary_administrative_agencies_in_occupied_Ukraine) (2022–present), a series of puppet governments installed in the [Russian-occupied territories of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian-occupied_territories_of_Ukraine) during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine).

-  [Military commandant's office for the Kursk region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_occupation_of_Kursk_Oblast) (2024–present), provisional administration established to administer parts of [Kursk Oblast](480916.md) controlled by Ukraine.

## Oceania

-  [Provisional Government of Hawaii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_Hawaii) (1893–1894), established in 1893 after the [overthrow of the Kingdom of Hawaii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overthrow_of_the_Kingdom_of_Hawaii) and renamed to the [Republic of Hawaii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Hawaii) in 1894.

-  [Naval Government of Guam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Government_of_Guam) (1898–1950), established by the United States following its [capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capture_of_Guam) during the [Spanish–American War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish%E2%80%93American_War). Dissolved following the [Guam Organic Act of 1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guam_Organic_Act_of_1950), which established [Guam](11974.md) as an [unincorporated organized territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territories_of_the_United_States).

-  [1901 caretaker government of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caretaker_government_of_Australia#The_1901_caretaker_government), established pending the [first election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1901_Australian_federal_election) to the newly established [Commonwealth of Australia](4689264.md).

-  [Vanuaaku People's Provisional Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanuaaku_People's_Provisional_Government) (1977–1978), established in the [New Hebrides Condominium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hebrides_Condominium) by [Walter Lini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Lini) as a precursor to the [Republic of Vanuatu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Vanuatu) which was internationally recognised as independent in 1980.

## Transcontinental entities

As of 2026, only [Russia](25391.md) has a provisional government, which exists parallel and in opposition to the current [government of Russia](9787373.md).

-  [Russian Provisional Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Provisional_Government) (1917), established as a result of the [February Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_Revolution) which led to the abdication of [Tsar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsar) [Nicholas II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_II_of_Russia).

-  [Provisional All-Russian Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_All-Russian_Government) (1918), a short-lived anti-communist government formed during the [Russian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War). Overthrown by the [white movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_movement) in the [Kolchak Coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolchak_Coup) and replaced by the [1918—1919 Russian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Government_\(1918%E2%80%931919\)).

-  [Russian Government (1918–1919)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Government_\(1918%E2%80%931919\)), a military government formed by the [white movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_movement) following the overthrow of the [Provisional All-Russian Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_All-Russian_Government) in the [Kolchak Coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolchak_Coup). Dissolved during the fall of Omsk, with its members forming the [South Russian Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Russian_Government) and [Eastern Okraina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Okraina) the following year.

-  [Government of the Grand National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Grand_National_Assembly) (1920–1923), established as an alternative government to the Allied-occupied [Ottoman Empire](22278.md) during the [Turkish War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_War_of_Independence).

-  [State Committee on the State of Emergency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Committee_on_the_State_of_Emergency) (1991), formed by the leaders of the [1991 Soviet coup d'état attempt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Soviet_coup_d'%C3%A9tat_attempt). Dissolved after the coup's failure, with [Mikhail Gorbachev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gorbachev) being reinstated as the Soviet head of state.

-  [Administration of South Ossetia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_of_South_Ossetia) (2007–2026), appointed by the government of [Georgia](48768.md) as [South Ossetia](318381.md)'s official ruling body in opposition to secessionist movements in the region. After being driven out of the country during the [Russo-Georgian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Georgian_War) in 2008, it operated as a government in exile in opposition to [the current, Russian-backed government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_South_Ossetia) before ultimately being abolished at the start of 2026.
