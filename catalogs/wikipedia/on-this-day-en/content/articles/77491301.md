This page documents [notable tornadoes](37530.md) and [tornado outbreaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak) worldwide in 2025. Strong and destructive tornadoes form most frequently in the [United States](3434750.md), [China](5405.md), the [Pampas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pampas), the [European Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Plain), [South Africa](17416221.md), and [Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal), but they can occur almost anywhere under the right conditions. Tornadoes also develop occasionally in southern [Canada](5042916.md) during the [Northern Hemisphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Hemisphere)'s summer and somewhat regularly at other times of the year across [Europe](9239.md), [Asia](689.md), and [Australia](4689264.md). Tornadic events are often accompanied by other forms of [severe weather](11593538.md), including strong [thunderstorms](70807.md), winds, and [hail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hail). Worldwide, at least 104 tornado-related deaths have been confirmed: 67 in the [United States](3434750.md), 12 in [Vietnam](202354.md), seven in [Brazil](3383.md), four each in [China](5405.md), [Spain](26667.md), and [Uganda](31816.md), two in the [Philippines](23440.md), and one each in [France](5843419.md), [Japan](15573.md), [Portugal](23033.md), and [Turkey](11125639.md).&#x20;

In the United States, the first two months of the year started off slightly below average, with generally scattered tornado activity.&#x20;

However, a major increase in activity began in March as the synoptic pattern became much more favorable for tornado outbreaks, [including the largest tornado outbreak on record for the month, which occurred mid-month.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak_of_March_14%E2%80%9316,_2025) Significant and damaging outbreaks continued in the months of April and May, while June featured [the first EF5 tornado](81275417.md) in the United States since the [2013 Moore tornado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Moore_tornado), which occurred near [Enderlin, North Dakota](150859.md) on June 20.&#x20;

In contrast to 2024, however, 2025 had a significantly inactive late season, with 172 tornadoes occurring from August through December, compared to 1,106 from March to June. This was due to multiple Arctic blasts bringing very cold, unsuitable air, and the fact that no hurricanes made landfall in the United States during the [hurricane season](65411671.md). 2025 also had the highest number of violent tornadoes in a year since [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornadoes_of_2013), with eight tornadoes worldwide receiving a rating of (E)F4 or higher. Six of these tornadoes occurred in the United States, and two in Brazil.&#x20;

## January

### January 3–7 (United States)

Main article: [January 5–6, 2025 United States blizzard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_5%E2%80%936,_2025_United_States_blizzard)

| EFU | EF0 | EF1 | EF2 | EF3 | EF4 | EF5 |

The [Tehama County, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehama_County,_California) EFU tornado on January 3, 2025.

From January 3 to January 7, a [major winter storm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_5-6,_2025_United_States_blizzard) passed over the [contiguous United States](83759.md). The storm system reached the [West Coast of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Coast_of_the_United_States),[^cite_note-8] where it produced the first tornado of the year, a brief EFU tornado in rural [Tehama County, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehama_County,_California), which caused no damage.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] On January 4, a [low pressure system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low_pressure_system) organized over the [Texas Panhandle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Panhandle) and [Oklahoma](22489.md). Interacting with the existing cold air, the system’s [warm front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warm_front) was predicted to generate severe thunderstorms across [Southern United States](179553.md) on January 5, prompting the [Storm Prediction Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Prediction_Center) to issue an enhanced risk for the region. The outlook included a 10% risk area for tornadoes, along with the potential for large hail and damaging winds.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]&#x20;



High-end EF2 damage to a well-constructed shop building east of [Marion, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion,_Louisiana)

On January 5, a small [tornado outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak) occurred as a [squall line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squall_line) of severe thunderstorms passed through the region, producing multiple tornadoes in [Louisiana](18130.md), [Arkansas](1930.md), [Mississippi](16949861.md), and [Alabama](303.md), including two strong tornadoes. A high-end EF2 tornado touched down near [Marion, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion,_Louisiana). It completely destroyed a mobile home and a well-built shop building, damaged the roofs of multiple houses, and snapped numerous large trees. In Mississippi, a low-end EF2 tornado snapped or uprooted numerous trees and downed power poles as it passed to the north of [Brooksville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooksville,_Mississippi). High-end EF1 tornadoes caused moderate tree and structure damage in the rural Mississippi communities of [Boon](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Boon,_Mississippi\&action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Gwinville](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Gwinville,_Mississippi\&action=edit\&redlink=1). A PDS tornado warning was issued for an EF1 tornado near [Pelahatchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pelahatchie,_Mississippi),[^cite_note-13] damaged chicken houses, downed trees and power lines, and caused an indirect injury when a train ran into a downed tree that had fallen onto railroad tracks.[^cite_note-14] A total of 14 tornadoes were confirmed, resulting in one indirect injury and no fatalities.[^cite_note-15]&#x20;

### January 6 (Saudi Arabia)

As a red alert for severe weather was issued by the National Centre of Meteorology over the region around [Mecca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mecca) on January 6, a tornado was reported to have struck [Rabigh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabigh), which was reported by *[Gulf News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_News)* to be the strongest ever recorded in the coastal region of [Saudi Arabia](349303.md). The National Centre of Meteorology also discussed plans to further research the tornado.[^cite_note-16]&#x20;

### January 17 (Italy)

| IFU | IF0 | IF0.5 | IF1 | IF1.5 | IF2 | IF2.5 | IF3 | IF4 | IF5 |

[Storm Gabri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_European_windstorm_season#Storm_Gabri) struck [Italy](14532.md) on January 17, causing torrential rainfall, thunderstorms, strong winds, and multiple tornadoes, all of which occurred on the island of [Sicily](27619.md). An IF2 tornado impacted areas near [Catania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catania), causing significant roof and tree damage, bending a metal truss electrical transmission tower in half, and rolling a [roulotte trailer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_van) 30 m (33 yd). Metal light poles were also bent, a semi-trailer was overturned, and sheet metal from buildings was scattered 500 m (550 yd) up a hillside. An IF1.5 tornado struck the northern suburbs of Catania and blew a camper over, downed several trees and damaged the roofs of multiple buildings. A strong, long-tracked [multiple-vortex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple-vortex_tornado) IF2 tornado that struck areas outside of [Avola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avola) downed numerous trees and power lines, toppled another electrical transmission tower, destroyed a masonry outbuilding, unroofed a house, and damaged the roofs of other homes.[^cite_note-ESWD-17] In [Augusta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augusta,_Sicily), an IF0.5 tornado overturned a small [ape car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piaggio_Ape), damaged concrete monuments and decorations at a cemetery, downed trees and power lines, and tore sheet metal roofing from buildings. An IF1 tornado struck the outskirts of [Licata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licata) as well, damaging crops and destroying greenhouses. A total of five tornadoes were confirmed.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]&#x20;

### January 21 (Portugal)

[Storm Garoé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_European_windstorm_season#Storm_Garoe) made landfall in [Spain](26667.md) and [Portugal](23033.md) on January 21, bringing strong winds, torrential rain, and severe weather in coastal areas.[^cite_note-20] During the late evening, an IF2 tornado tracked through rural areas near the community of [Montevil](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Montevil\&action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Setúbal District, Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set%C3%BAbal_District). Many large trees were snapped or uprooted along the path, and some were defoliated and stripped of their branches. Some crop damage also occurred, and power lines were downed.[^cite_note-ESWD-17]&#x20;

### January 23 (United Kingdom)

Main article: [Storm Éowyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_%C3%89owyn)

On January 23, Storm Éowyn made landfall in [United Kingdom](31717.md), resulting in strong winds and heavy rain. An IF2 tornado moved through [Holywell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holywell,_Cornwall) and [Quintrell Downs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quintrell_Downs), causing severe damage at the Trevornick Caravan Park, snapping many trees, and causing roof damage to homes. Multiple mobile homes and RVs were tossed and destroyed at the caravan park, resulting in one injury.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-21]&#x20;

### January 26 (New Zealand)

A [low-pressure system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-pressure_area) organized to the west of [New Zealand](4913064.md), causing heavy rain and severe [thunderstorms](70807.md) on the [North Island](62046.md).[^cite_note-22] In the early morning of January 26, a damaging tornado hit [Mangawhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangawhai) and surrounding areas in the [Northland Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northland_Region), downing many trees, snapping concrete power poles, and ripping roofs off of homes. A media business sustained extensive structural damage, cars were damaged, debris was left deposited in trees, and two people were seriously injured. There was damage to about 50 properties, and about 4,700 homes lost power.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] The tornado was not officially rated, but was estimated to have reached high-end EF1 or low-end EF2 intensity.[^cite_note-25]&#x20;

### January 27 (Europe)

| IFU | IF0 | IF0.5 | IF1 | IF1.5 | IF2 | IF2.5 | IF3 | IF4 | IF5 |

On January 27, a damaging IF2 tornado struck [Torre de Juan Abad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torre_de_Juan_Abad), [Spain](26667.md). The strong tornado tracked through the northern part of town, causing significant damage to cars, homes, restaurants, auto repair shops, and warehouses. Several brick and masonry buildings suffered major structural damage, including total roof loss and collapse of multiple exterior walls. The tornado also severely damaged farms in agricultural areas, and snapped or uprooted more than 1,000 olive trees. In England, an IF1 tornado downed trees and destroyed some sheds near [Whitchurch Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitchurch_Hill) and an IF1.5 tornado damaged multiple homes and trees in and around [Henley-on-Thames](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henley-on-Thames). An IF1 tornado also occurred in the outskirts of [Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rennes) in [France](5843419.md).[^cite_note-ESWD-17]&#x20;

A mobile home that was destroyed at high-end EF2 intensity near [Deer Lodge, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deer_Lodge,_Tennessee), resulting in two fatalities.

On February 5, a [winter storm](244878.md) hit the [Ohio Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_Valley), creating the potential for severe weather and a few tornadoes on the warm side of the system in [Kentucky](16846.md) and [Tennessee](30395.md). A line of severe thunderstorms impacted the region later that night and continued into the early morning hours of February 6. The storms produced several tornadoes, including two strong tornadoes. Three tornadoes occurred in Kentucky, including an EF1 tornado near [Island City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_City,_Kentucky) in [Owsley County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owsley_County,_Kentucky). The tornado snapped or uprooted trees, destroyed a small shed-like residence, damaged two mobile homes, and was only the third tornado to be confirmed in the county since reliable records began in 1950. Two other EF1 tornadoes passed near [Magnolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnolia,_Kentucky) and [Dango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dango,_Kentucky), downing trees, inflicting minor to moderate damage to homes, and damaging or destroying multiple barns and outbuildings. In Tennessee, a high-end EF2 tornado snapped or uprooted countless trees in wooded areas and completely destroyed two mobile homes near the community of [Deer Lodge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deer_Lodge,_Tennessee), killing two people inside one of the mobile homes and injuring two others. Another EF2 tornado touched down near [Center Grove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_Grove,_Tennessee), causing major tree damage and destroying multiple barns. Two EF1 tornadoes touched down near [Algood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algood,_Tennessee) and [Crawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crawford,_Tennessee), downed many trees, inflicted minor to moderate damage to various structures, and each injured one person. The final tornado of the event caused high-end EF1 damage near [Thorn Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorn_Hill,_Tennessee), destroying a barn, overturning a tanker trailer, and inflicting window and siding damage to a church. A total of eight tornadoes were confirmed, resulting in two fatalities and four injuries.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]&#x20;

Two additional weak tornadoes occurred in [North Carolina](21650.md) and [Virginia](32432.md) on March 5 before the outbreak came to an end. A total of 39 tornadoes were confirmed, resulting in no fatalities and 11 injuries.&#x20;

Radar animation of the tornado outbreak and floods.

A slow-moving weather system resulted in both a devastating tornado outbreak as well as historic life-threatening flash floods throughout the [Southern](179553.md) and [Midwestern United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midwestern_United_States) from April 2–7, spawning 157 tornadoes in total. The [Storm Prediction Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Prediction_Center) (SPC) issued a [high risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Storm_Prediction_Center_high_risk_days) for the first day of the outbreak, April 2, which would be the busiest day as 74 tornadoes touched down across nine states. That evening, a high-end EF3 tornado destroyed multiple homes near [Latty, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latty,_Missouri). Later, a large, [multiple-vortex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple-vortex_tornado) high-end EF3 tornado impacted [Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay,_Arkansas), [Lake City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_City,_Arkansas), and [Monette, Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monette,_Arkansas), derailing two freight trains, and destroying high-tension power line trusses and several homes. A [tornado emergency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_emergency) was declared for Lake City as the tornado struck,[^cite_note-92][^cite_note-The_Weather_Channel-93] and injured eight people.[^cite_note-DS2-94] Additionally, one indirect fatality occurred in association with an EF2 tornado that passed through [Delta, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta,_Missouri) as it moved along [Route 25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_Route_25); this tornado damaged approximately 200 structures along its path.[^cite_note-PAHsum-95][^cite_note-96] Tornado continued throughout the overnight hours into April 3. Another EF3 tornado damaged or destroyed buildings on the east side of [Jeffersontown, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffersontown,_Kentucky) while an EF2 tornado injured three people along the [Mississippi](16949861.md)-[Tennessee](30395.md) state line northwest of [Walnut, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut,_Mississippi).[^cite_note-DAT-34] A destructive high-end EF3 tornado devastated [Selmer, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selmer,_Tennessee), heavily damaging or destroying homes and businesses, obliterating manufactured homes, and killing five people.[^cite_note-DS2-94] Soon after, a second tornado emergency was issued as another large, long-track high-end EF3 tornado passed near [Slayden, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slayden,_Mississippi) before crossing the state line and striking [Grand Junction, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Junction,_Tennessee) at EF2 strength, heavily damaging or destroying several homes, businesses, and manufactured homes, and collapsing a metal truss tower along its path. Two people were killed and three others were injured in a mobile home that was obliterated as the tornado crossed the state line.[^cite_note-DS2-94][^cite_note-97]&#x20;

Relatively lower, but still significant, tornadic activity continued over the next several days. On April 4, an EF2 tornado impacted the [Lone Star, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lone_Star,_Texas) area, heavily damaging homes, snapping trees, and tossing six vehicles.[^cite_note-SHVsum0423-98] The next morning on April 5, another EF2 tornado damaged or destroyed outbuilding and manufactured homes and injured one person near [Wynne, Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wynne,_Arkansas).[^cite_note-MEGsum0423-99] Later, a long-tracked EF2 tornado caused widespread damage to homes, outbuildings, power poles, and trees as it passed near [Thaxton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thaxton,_Mississippi), [Hurricane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane,_Mississippi), and [New Albany, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Albany,_Mississippi).[^cite_note-MEGsum0423-99] On April 6, an EF2 tornado struck [Stringer, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stringer,_Mississippi) killing a person in a [mobile home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_home) that was obliterated.[^cite_note-JM-71] Weak tornadoes were observed on April 7 as the outbreak came to an end.[^cite_note-DAT-34] Overall, nine tornadic fatalities occurred as a result of this outbreak, with many more being killed by the devastating flooding that accompanied it.[^cite_note-100]&#x20;

### July 23 (Australia)

On the evening of July 23, a rare tornado struck the coastal suburb of [City Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_Beach,_Western_Australia) in [Perth](24355.md), in the state of [Western Australia](33613.md). The tornado developed from a severe thunderstorm that rapidly intensified as it moved ashore, likely originating as a waterspout over the [Indian Ocean](14580.md) before making landfall. The rotating storm produced strong, concentrated winds that damaged homes and infrastructure across the western suburbs. Multiple residences in City Beach and surrounding areas experienced significant roof damage, with sections of roofing removed and trees uprooted or snapped by the intense winds. Residents reported debris strewn across roads and gardens, and emergency services responded to dozens of calls for assistance as power outages and fallen trees disrupted daily life. Local meteorologists and witnesses described the storm's sudden onset, noting that the rapid development of the convective cell provided little warning before the tornado struck. The [Bureau of Meteorology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Meteorology) confirmed that the severe cell responsible for the damage exhibited characteristics consistent with tornadic activity. No fatalities were reported, though several households were left uninhabitable and repairs continued in the days following the storm.[^cite_note-193][^cite_note-194]&#x20;

## August

### August 3 (Russia)

On August 3, a damaging tornado struck a rural area of the [Jewish Autonomous Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_Autonomous_Oblast) in [Russia's Far East](173135.md), producing localized but significant infrastructure damage. The tornado snapped and uprooted trees and caused structural impacts to utility infrastructure, including the bending and partial collapse of several high-voltage power transmission towers, resulting in temporary power disruptions to nearby communities. Light structural and agricultural damage was also reported along the tornado's path. Damage surveys and photographic evidence indicated wind intensities consistent with an IF2 rating, making it the strongest documented tornado to occur in the Jewish Autonomous Oblast. No fatalities were reported, though emergency crews were dispatched to repair damaged power lines and clear debris following the storm.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-197][^cite_note-198]&#x20;

### August 25–26 (Typhoon Kajiki)

Two days of tornadic unrest began late in the evening on August 25, as [Typhoon Kajiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Kajiki_\(2025\)) made landfall in Vietnam. A powerful tornado struck [Kim Bảng district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_B%E1%BA%A3ng,_Ninh_B%C3%ACnh) in [Ninh Bình](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninh_B%C3%ACnh_province).[^cite_note-199] The tornado, though lasting only a few minutes, caused extensive damage to more than 200 homes and businesses. [^cite_note-236] On September 23, a tornado lifted several roofs off and blew over road signs in [Taitung County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taitung_County), Taiwan.[^cite_note-237] On the same day an EF1 tornado struck [Baras, Rizal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baras,_Rizal). At 14\:00 PhST, an EF1 tornado struck [Olongapo, Zambales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olongapo), where it damaged 4 houses and toppled trees in the area.[^cite_note-238] At around 16\:00 PhST, another tornado through a public market in [Masinloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masinloc), a tornado in [Meycauayan,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meycauayan) Bulacan damaged 7 houses and scattered multiple debris. One person was injured after falling from a motorcycle due to strong winds.[^cite_note-239]&#x20;

### September 25–29 (Typhoon Bualoi)

[Typhoon Bualoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Bualoi) spawned at least two tornadoes in [Oriental Mindoro, Philippines](261948.md). On September 25 a EFU [Multi-vortex tornado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-vortex_tornado) struck two villages in [Calapan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calapan). the next day a EFU tornado struck a village in [Baco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baco,_Oriental_Mindoro), where it tore off roofs of a nearby house.[^cite_note-240] Then on September 28, five tornadoes would spawn in Northern [Vietnam](202354.md),[^cite_note-241] ultimately causing 11 fatalities. A strong tornado in [Ninh Bình province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninh_B%C3%ACnh_province) killed nine people and injured 18 others.[^cite_note-242] Another tornado in [Hưng Yên province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C6%B0ng_Y%C3%AAn_province) killed two and injured nine,[^cite_note-243] and a third tornado in [Haiphong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haiphong) injured eight people.[^cite_note-244] Two other tornadoes caused a total of nine injuries in [Quảng Ninh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qu%E1%BA%A3ng_Ninh_province) and north of [Hanoi](56667.md). Additionally, 20 tornadoes were confirmed in Southern China, specifically the [Zhanjiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhanjiang) area, from September 25–29. Most were rated EFU, EF0 or EF1, although one was rated EF2.[^cite_note-245]&#x20;

## October

### October 4 (Norway)

A rare, strong landspout struck the village of [Oppdal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oppdal_\(village\)) in Norway. The landspout was recorded as it moved through downtown Oppdal where it downed trees, overturned caravans, and smashed windows. At least 3 people were injured. This landspout was rated IF2 and information is still preliminary and subject to change.[^cite_note-ESWD-17]&#x20;

### October 5 (Bangladesh)

During the morning hours of October 5, a damaging tornado struck portions of [Nilphamari District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilphamari_District) and [Rangpur Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rangpur_Division) in northern Bangladesh amid heavy rainfall and convective storms. The tornado destroyed or damaged hundreds of homes across several villages, particularly within [Garagram Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garagram_Union) and parts of [Gangachara Upazila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangachara_Upazila). Numerous trees were uprooted, power lines were downed, and roads were obstructed by debris. At least 30 people were injured, several of whom required hospitalization. Local authorities and relief agencies conducted damage assessments and distributed emergency assistance to affected residents.[^cite_note-246][^cite_note-247][^cite_note-248]&#x20;

### October 20 (Europe)

A widespread severe weather outbreak produced multiple tornadoes across western Europe on October 20, affecting the [United Kingdom](31717.md), [France](5843419.md), and the [Netherlands](21148.md), resulting in one fatality and numerous injuries.&#x20;

In the United Kingdom, a brief IF0.5 tornado struck [Dereham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dereham), causing minor damage to homes and trees. The event was confirmed by [TORRO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TORRO), which documented light structural and roof damage along a short path.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-249] In the Netherlands, a brief IF0.5 tornado was observed near [Woudsend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woudsend), where part of a barn roof was damaged and tree branches were snapped.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-250]&#x20;

The most significant impacts occurred in France, where several tornadoes touched down as supercell thunderstorms moved northeastward across the country. The strongest tornado struck [Ermont](3582040.md) and surrounding communities including [Argenteuil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argenteuil), [Eaubonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eaubonne), and [Franconville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franconville,_Val-d%27Oise). Rated IF2, the tornado carved a damage path up to 14 km (8.7 mi) long and as wide as 600 m (660 yd), tearing roofs from buildings, collapsing three tower cranes, overturning vehicles, and scattering debris across streets. A 23-year-old worker was killed, four people were left in critical condition, and several others sustained injuries. Dozens of residents were temporarily displaced as emergency shelters were opened.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-251][^cite_note-252] Additional weaker tornadoes occurred elsewhere in France. IF1 to IF0.5 tornadoes caused roof and tree damage near [Anglesqueville-la-Bras-Long](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglesqueville-la-Bras-Long), [Michery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michery), [Chaumont-en-Vexin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaumont-en-Vexin), and [Chambley-Bussières](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chambley-Bussi%C3%A8res), where farms, homes, and agricultural structures sustained varying degrees of damage, with sheets of metal and debris carried hundreds of meters from their points of origin.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-253]&#x20;

### October 25–27 (United States)

A small QLCS tornado event occurred mainly along the [Gulf Coast](11969.md) of the [Southern United States](179553.md) from October 25–27. A total of ten tornadoes were confirmed, including two waterspouts that came ashore in [Mexico Beach, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_Beach,_Florida), one of which damaged or rolled over several RVs, resulting in 10 injuries.[^cite_note-254][^cite_note-255] In addition, a rare EF0 rated landspout touched down north of [Rigby, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigby,_Idaho).[^cite_note-256]&#x20;

### October 27–28 (Turkey)

| IFU | IF0 | IF0.5 | IF1 | IF1.5 | IF2 | IF2.5 | IF3 | IF4 | IF5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

A series of tornadoes associated with severe thunderstorms swept across northwestern Turkey, impacting several rural communities and causing structural damage and one fatality across two days. On October 27, an IF1 tornado struck [Enez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enez) and an IF1.5 occurred in the area of [Eşeler](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=E%C5%9Feler,_Bal%C4%B1kesir_Province\&action=edit\&redlink=1) village, where roofs were partially removed from homes and telegraph and light poles were damaged or knocked down.[^cite_note-ESWD-17] More significant damage occurred on October 28 as stronger tornadoes tracked across villages in [Ankara Province](370068.md), including an IF2 impacting [Çantırlı](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%87ant%C4%B1rl%C4%B1,_Beypazar%C4%B1), an IF1.5 striking [Dibecik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dibecik,_Beypazar%C4%B1), and a strong IF2 affecting [İlhanköy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0lhank%C3%B6y,_Aya%C5%9F). Entire roofs were lifted and thrown from residences, garden structures and fences were destroyed, and several mosque towers sustained heavy damage, with at least one partially collapsing. In İlhanköy, a 40-year-old man was killed after being struck by debris from an overturned container, while three children sheltering inside were injured. Emergency crews responded across the region to clear debris, assess damage, and assist displaced residents.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-257][^cite_note-258]&#x20;

### October 29 (Spain)

On the morning of October 29, a vigorous storm system producing torrential rain and severe thunderstorms affected parts of [Andalusia](2736.md), including the coastal [province of Huelva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Huelva). In [Gibraleón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibrale%C3%B3n), a strong tornado struck the town, toppling trees and damaging roofs and structures amid heavy winds. The [State Meteorological Agency (AEMET)](21111793.md) later confirmed that the circulation responsible for the damage reached wind speeds of up to 220 km/h (140 mph), consistent with an IF2 tornado. A 59-year-old man in Gibraleón was seriously injured by a falling structure during the event and died two days later from his injuries.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-259][^cite_note-260] Around the same time, a waterspout came ashore in the [Isla Cristina](4242965.md) area of the Huelva coast, where it caused additional damage to trees, roofs, and other light structures amid the broader storm impacts. This tornado was rated IF1. Although no serious injuries were reported from the Isla Cristina event, emergency services responded to numerous incidents across the region as heavy rain and strong winds produced flooding, fallen trees, and infrastructure disruptions.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-261]&#x20;

## November

### November 6 (Typhoon Kalmaegi)

Outer rainbands from [Typhoon Kalmaegi](81485989.md) produced a tornado that struck [Long Phung](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Long_Ph%E1%BB%A5ng_commune\&action=edit\&redlink=1), [Quảng Ngãi](2015207.md), in Vietnam, causing localized but significant damage to residential areas. Numerous homes sustained roof loss or structural damage, with several light structures and outbuildings destroyed. Trees and power poles were snapped or uprooted, blocking roads and disrupting electrical service to surrounding neighborhoods. Multiple residents were injured by flying debris and collapsing materials, and several families were temporarily displaced as local authorities conducted damage assessments and cleared debris. Emergency crews and provincial officials provided assistance and began repairs shortly after the storm passed.[^cite_note-262][^cite_note-263][^cite_note-264]&#x20;

### November 7 (Brazil)

Main articles: [2025 Southern Brazil tornado outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Southern_Brazil_tornado_outbreak) and [2025 Rio Bonito do Iguaçu tornado](81549673.md)

Damage from Rio Bonito do Iguaçu tornado seen from satellite.

A small but significant outbreak of tornadoes caused widespread damage within [Southern Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Region,_Brazil), including a violent tornado that devastated the town of [Rio Bonito do Iguaçu](25552210.md), where an estimated 90% of the town's structures were damaged or destroyed. At least five fatalities and upwards of 780 injuries have been reported in the city; hospitals are reportedly inundated with casualties.[^cite_note-265][^cite_note-266][^cite_note-267] Experts from [PREVOTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PREVOTS), MetSul Meteorologia and SIMEPAR rated this tornado an F4.[^cite_note-prevotsRBI-268][^cite_note-metsulEF4-269][^cite_note-270] The event has been called "unprecedented," with [the governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratinho_J%C3%BAnior) of [Paraná](229411.md) adding that the tornado was the strongest to hit the state in decades.[^cite_note-271][^cite_note-272] At least seven other tornadoes were recorded in neighboring regions,[^cite_note-prevotsRBI-268] one of which, also rated F4, killed another person in the neighboring city of [Guarapuava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guarapuava). Another death was recorded in the [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Brazil) of [Rolante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolante), [Rio Grande do Sul](256268.md) after a man was struck by a falling tree as a result of non-tornadic winds from the extratropical cyclone that produced these storms.[^cite_note-273] The tornado outbreak was the first recorded instance of multiple violent tornadoes in South America on a single day since [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornadoes_of_2009#September_7-8_\(South_America\)).[^cite_note-274]&#x20;

### November 15 (Portugal and Italy)

| IFU | IF0 | IF0.5 | IF1 | IF1.5 | IF2 | IF2.5 | IF3 | IF4 | IF5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

A damaging tornado struck the [Albufeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albufeira) area of [Algarve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algarve), Portugal on November 15, impacting the Eden Resort campsite and nearby neighborhoods. Numerous mobile homes and light structures were overturned or destroyed, and many trees were snapped or uprooted. An 85-year-old British woman was killed, and 28 people were injured, including two seriously. The tornado was assigned a preliminary IF2 rating based on the extent of structural and tree damage.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-275][^cite_note-276] A second tornado touched down near [Lagoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagoa,_Portugal), where trees and trailers were damaged along a northeastward track. The circulation originated as a waterspout before moving inland and continued through rural areas toward [Aivados](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Aivados\&action=edit\&redlink=1), [Poço Deão](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Po%C3%A7o_De%C3%A3o\&action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Ribeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ribeira,_Algarve\&action=edit\&redlink=1). Damage surveys indicated major branch loss and structural impacts consistent with a preliminary IF2 intensity. Together, the two tornadoes represented one of the most significant late-season severe weather events in southern Portugal in recent years.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-277]&#x20;

The same storm system produced multiple tornadoes and waterspouts across coastal [Liguria](71864.md) in Italy, particularly around the [Genoa](47332321.md) [metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_City_of_Genoa). The strongest tornado, rated IF1.5, struck [Pegli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegli), where trees and large branches were downed, roofs and windows were damaged, light objects were lofted or displaced, a small boat was moved, and a truck was overturned.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-278] Additional weaker tornadoes affected nearby communities. Containers were tipped over in [Prà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pra%27), light roof and tree damage occurred in [Sestri Ponente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sestri_Ponente) and [Quarto dei Mille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarto_dei_Mille), and several brief waterspouts or weak tornadoes were documented near [Crevari](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Crevari\&action=edit\&redlink=1), [Albaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albaro), and [Bogliasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogliasco), producing little to minor damage. No casualties were reported in Italy.[^cite_note-ESWD-17][^cite_note-279]&#x20;

### November 16–17 (Vietnam)

Two tornadoes were reported from November 16 to 17 in the south-central coast of Vietnam. The first was a short-lived but destructive tornado that impacted [Phú Ninh District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph%C3%BA_Ninh_District), south of [Da Nang](425140.md) city, injuring four people. The tornado damaged around 56 structures, including 27 homes with torn roofs and three houses being destroyed.[^cite_note-280] A second tornado occurred a few hours later on November 17 in Nguyễn Nghiêm, [Quảng Ngãi](2015207.md), damaging at least 20 homes.[^cite_note-281]&#x20;

### November 25 (Philippines)

&#x20;Two tornadoes were spawned by [Typhoon Koto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Koto) (Verbena). In [Labangan, Zamboanga del Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labangan,_Zamboanga_del_Sur) an EF1 tornado struck a village where at least 2 houses were damaged.[^cite_note-282] In [Nabas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabas,_Aklan) and [Tangalan, Aklan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangalan,_Aklan), an EF2 tornado tore off roofs of multiple houses, and snapped trees.[^cite_note-283][^cite_note-284]

## December

### December 8 (Brazil)



A sports gymnasium partially destroyed by the tornado.

On December 8, a significant tornado struck the municipality of [Flores da Cunha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flores_da_Cunha) in the state of [Rio Grande do Sul](256268.md), Brazil, causing extensive structural damage in the rural community of [Alfredo Chaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Alfredo_Chaves,_Flores_da_Cunha\&action=edit\&redlink=1). The tornado tore roofs off dozens of homes, damaged public buildings including a school and a health unit, and uprooted trees, leaving many residents’ houses exposed to the elements. Aerial and field assessments by local civil defense authorities identified damage patterns consistent with tornadic winds exceeding 100 km/h (62 mph), and between 40 and 80 homes were reported damaged, with agricultural areas such as vineyards also affected. Power outages left approximately 1,000 customers without electricity, and municipal emergency teams, supported by state defense forces and volunteer firefighters, conducted debris removal and structural safety inspections. No fatalities or serious injuries were reported, though hundreds of residents were affected by the storm's impacts. [PREVOTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PREVOTS) rated the tornado F2 while MetSul Meteorologia gave it a high-end F1 to low-end F2 estimate.[^cite_note-285][^cite_note-286][^cite_note-287][^cite_note-288]&#x20;

### December 28 (United States)

EF2 damage to a house in [Mount Zion, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Zion,_Illinois).

A tornado outbreak affected [Illinois](14849.md) and [Indiana](21883857.md). An isolated EF1 tornado destroyed two barns south of [Groveland, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groveland,_Illinois) in the morning.[^cite_note-DAT-34][^cite_note-289] Later that afternoon and evening, a [QLCS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QLCS) with some [supercells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercell) moved across the region and produced at least twelve more confirmed tornadoes. A long-tracked low-end EF2 tornado unroofed two houses and destroyed garages in [Mount Zion, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mt._Zion,_Illinois), with damage to several other homes. Twin tornadoes, rated EF0 and EF1, occurred to the east of [Clifton, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifton,_Illinois), causing minor damage to a few residences and farmsteads. An EF1 tornado moved through downtown [Linton, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linton,_Indiana), causing roof and wall damage to several masonry buildings, and injuring one person.[^cite_note-DAT-34][^cite_note-290] In all, 13 tornadoes were confirmed, resulting in one injury.[^cite_note-DAT-34]&#x20;
