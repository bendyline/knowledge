The **Ministry of Civil Defence, Emergencies and Disaster Relief**[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] is a [Russian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_government) agency overseeing the civil [emergency services](146330.md) in [Russia](25391.md).

President [Boris Yeltsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Yeltsin) established EMERCOM on 10 January 1994, though the ministry can be traced back to 27 December 1990, when the [Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Soviet_Federative_Socialist_Republic) (RSFSR) established the **Russian Rescue Corps** (**RRC**) and assigned it the mission of rapid response to emergencies.[^cite_note-5]

## History

The history of civil defence services in Russia traces to the years of Muscovy rule and the 1649 "Direction on Municipal rescue" decree of [Tsar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsar) [Alexis of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexis_of_Russia) which officially raised the Moscow Municipal Fire Service, the first active fire department in Russia. When [Peter the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_the_Great) was Tsar, Saint Petersburg was given its own fire department modeled on Western practices of the time. By 1863 it was transformed, by orders of Tsar [Alexander II of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_II_of_Russia), as the first ever professional fire service in Russia and Eastern Europe.

Starting in 1932, civil defence matters were performed by the Local Air Defence Units (*Местная противовоздушная оборона PBO-C*, *Mestnaya protivovozdushnaya oborona PVO-S*) under the nascent [Soviet Air Defence Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Air_Defence_Forces), which were transferred to the [NKVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NKVD) in 1940 (and served with distinction, together with the NKVD Fire Services Command founded in 1918, in the [Great Patriotic War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Front_\(World_War_II\))). In 1960 it was returned to the Ministry of Defence as a service branch of the [Soviet Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Armed_Forces) (the Civil Defence Forces of the Ministry of Defence) and a directly reporting agency, while the MVD retained the firefighting service.

In the aftermath of the events of the [1988 Armenian earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Armenian_earthquake) and the [Chernobyl disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernobyl_disaster), on 17 July 1990 a directive decision of the Presidium of the Supreme Council of Russian Socialist Soviet Republic led to the formation of the Russian Rescue Corps (Российский корпус спасателей), which eventually was formed by the Soviet Government on 27 December 1990.[^cite_note-6] This date is marked as the official anniversary of the EMERCOM.

On 17 April 1991 the Presidium of the Supreme Council of Russia appointed [Sergei Shoigu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Shoigu) as Chairman of the State Committee for Extraordinary Situations (Государственный Комитет по чрезвычайным ситуациям, ГКЧС), which succeeded the RRC.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

On 19 November 1991, the State Committee was merged with the Headquarters for Civil Defence of the USSR (under the Ministry of Defence) to create the State Committee of the Russian Federation for Civil Defence, Emergency Situations and Elimination of the Consequences of Natural Disasters (Государственный комитет по делам гражданской обороны, чрезвычайным ситуациям и ликвидации последствий стихийных бедствий при Президенте РСФСР) and was subordinated to the [President of Russia](239646.md). Sergei Shoigu was appointed as a chairman by [Boris Yeltsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Yeltsin).

On 10 January 1994 the State Committee became part of the [Government of Russia](9787373.md) and the ministry was named the Ministry of Civil Defence, Emergencies and Disaster Relief, with Sergei Shoigu as a minister.

On 1 January 2002, the [Russian State Fire Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_State_Fire_Service), the national fire service, became part of the ministry with 278,000 firefighters, removed from [Ministry of Internal Affairs](53056.md) control after 84 years.

On 12 May 2012, [Vladimir Puchkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Puchkov) was appointed as the new minister, replacing Shoigu who was later appointed as Defense Minister after a brief stint as Governor of Moscow Oblast.[^cite_note-9]

On 1 July 2016, an EMERCOM firefighting [Il-76](229943.md) [crashed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Russian_Ministry_of_Emergency_Situations_Il-76_crash) after taking off from [Irkutsk International airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Airport_Irkutsk) while on its way to dump water as to help douse wildfires in Siberia.

## Duties



[Russian Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Post) stamp about EMERCOM



Russian Post stamp about EMERCOM

According to an EMERCOM publication, the Ministry is an agency of federal executive power with the following tasks:

- developing proposals and initiatives in the sphere of State policy on issues within the Ministry's competence;

- managing the [civil defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_defence_in_Russia?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [search and rescue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_and_rescue_in_Russia) Service in the Russian Federation;

- providing for the functioning and further development of the [Russian System of Disaster Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_System_of_Disaster_Management) ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): ОКСИОН OKSION};

- directing activities aimed at eliminating the consequences of large-scale disasters, catastrophes and other emergencies;

- conducting special submarine activities;

- supervising the use of finance resources allocated to the [Government](9787373.md) for disaster management and response;

- organizing the training of the population, and governing agencies and the [RSDM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_System_of_Disaster_Management) forces for disaster management and response;

- and organizing international cooperation in the fields of the Ministry's competency.

## Ministers

Main article: [Minister of Emergency Situations (Russia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Emergency_Situations_\(Russia\))

### Sergei Shoigu



[Sergei Shoigu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Shoigu) was Minister from 1991 to 2012

The first Minister in charge of EMERCOM was [Sergei Shoigu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Shoigu). He was appointed by the Presidium of the Supreme Council of Russia as Chairman of the State Committee for Extraordinary Situations on 17 April 1991 and by President Yeltsin on 19 November 1991 as Chairman of the State Committee of the Russian Federation for Civil Defense, Emergency Situations and Elimination of the Consequences of Natural Disasters. Shoigu was given the rank of [Major General](36301471.md) in October 1994, and his committee became a ministry on 10 January 1994 with Shoigu as a minister. President Yeltsin showed his faith in the importance of EMERCOM by designating Minister Shoigu a member of the [Russian Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_Council_of_Russia) by [Presidential Decree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Decree) on 1 February 1994. On 11 May 2012 he was appointed as [Governor of Moscow Oblast](18790128.md) and he resigned from his office.

### Vladimir Puchkov



[Vladimir Puchkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Puchkov) was Minister from 2012 to 2018

[Vladimir Puchkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Puchkov) was the Deputy Minister of Emergencies. On 17 May 2012 he was appointed as Minister [first government of Dmitry Medvedev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Medvedev's_First_Cabinet). Six years later, when the [new government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Medvedev's_Second_Cabinet) was formed, he did not join the government.

### Yevgeny Zinichev



[Yevgeny Zinichev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yevgeny_Zinichev) was Minister from 2018 to 2021

[Yevgeny Zinichev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yevgeny_Zinichev) was appointed minister on 18 May 2018, in the second Medvedev government. Zinichev was not a career lifeguard and had served all his life in security agencies such as the [KGB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KGB), the [FSO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Protective_Service_\(Russia\)) and the [FSB](385990.md). Prior to appointment, Zinichev held the position of deputy director of the FSB. After the resignation of the [Second Medvedev cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Medvedev_cabinet) in 2020, he retained his post in the [new government of Mikhail Mishustin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Mishustin's_First_Cabinet).

Yevgeny Zinichev tragically died on 8 September 2021, aged 55, during the filming of an interdepartmental exercise to protect the [Arctic zone of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_policy_of_Russia). According to the ministry, he fell off a cliff while trying to save the life of director and cameraman [Aleksandr Melnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Melnik), who also died. His death was the first case in the history of post-Soviet Russia of the death of an incumbent federal minister.

[Alexander Kurenkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Kurenkov) is Minister from 2022

[Aleksandr Kurenkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Kurenkov) was appointed minister on 25 May 2022.[^cite_note-10]

Working through the office of the [Prime Minister](339344.md), the Ministry can ask for private, [Ministry of Defence](7875884.md) or [National Guard of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Guard_of_Russia) assistance. That is, the Ministry has international coordination power and the ability to tap local resources if required.

The Department of International Cooperation, to present an example of the activities of one of these departments and commissions, has already signed agreements on cooperation during disaster response and prevention with Germany, Italy, France, Switzerland, Poland, [Belarus](3457.md), Georgia, and [Kazakhstan](16642.md). Mutual assistance pacts are ready for signing with [Mongolia](19271.md), [Latvia](17514.md), Finland, [Armenia](10918072.md), [Moldova](19260.md), [Serbia](29265.md) and [Estonia](28222445.md). An agreement also exists with the [U.N. High Commission for Refugees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNHCR) (UNHCR), and agreements are sought with the [OSCE](22796.md) and [NATO](21133.md).

## Internal organizations

- **Regional Centres** – EMERCOM centres are located in Moscow, [St. Petersburg](24320051.md), [Nizhny Novgorod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizhny_Novgorod), [Rostov-on-Don](317562.md), [Samara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samara,_Russia), [Yekaterinburg](66524.md), [Novosibirsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk), [Krasnoyarsk](72233.md), [Chita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chita,_Zabaykalsky_Krai) and [Khabarovsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khabarovsk).

- **Civil Defence and Emergency Headquarters** – Many regions, provinces, autonomous administrative units, districts and towns possess these headquarters.

- **Command and Control Center** – These centers are located in Moscow, and in each region and oblast.

- **Training and Education Facilities** – These include the [Civil Defense Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Defense_Academy_of_the_Ministry_of_Emergency_Situations), [Training and Methodology centres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Training_and_Methodology_centres?action=edit\&redlink=1), an [All-Russia Scientific Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-Russia_Scientific_Research_Institute?action=edit\&redlink=1), an [All-Russian Monitoring and Laboratory Control Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-Russian_Monitoring_and_Laboratory_Control_Centre?action=edit\&redlink=1), and a [Centre for Scientific Analysis of Civil Defence Issues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Scientific_Analysis_of_Civil_Defence_Issues?action=edit\&redlink=1).

To perform rapid response operations the following forces and equipment are available:

- **[Russian State Fire Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_State_Fire_Service)** – The highest fire service body in Russia.

- **Central Air-Mobile Rescue Team** – These teams are equipped with aviation facilities that include helicopters and cargo aircraft ([Ilyushin Il-76](229943.md), [Antonov An-72](537492.md), [An-148](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An-148)-100EM,

  [^cite_note-11]

  &#x20;[Ka-32](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ka-32)A11VS). The teams have taken part in United Nations' humanitarian delivery operations.

* **Civil Defense Forces Command** – These troops consist of military troop divisions and regiments/brigades stationed in various regions of the country aimed at civil defense measures during natural and man-made disasters.

* **[EMERCOM Civil Defense Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Defense_Academy_of_the_Ministry_of_Emergency_Situations)** – trains all officers and non-commissioned personnel of the Ministry in the duties of civil defense.

* **[Search and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_and_rescue_in_Russia)** – This service maintains 30 units in various republics, regions and provinces.
