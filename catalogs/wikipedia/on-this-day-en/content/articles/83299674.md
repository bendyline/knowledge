**Severe Tropical Storm Jangmi**[^cite_note-2], known in the Philippines as **Typhoon Domeng**, was a large and moderately strong tropical cyclone that enhanced the [southwest monsoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_monsoon) in the [Philippines](23440.md), which indirectly affected the country in late May, and then hit the [Ryukyu Islands](8458939.md) and mainland [Japan](15573.md) in early June. It is also the sixth named storm of the [2026 Pacific typhoon season](80781992.md).&#x20;

The precursor disturbance to Jangmi formed on May 25, over 400 km east-southeast of [Yap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yap). Its chances for development later rose as it moved into more favorable conditions. The JMA identified the system as a tropical depression on May 26, before it intensified into a tropical storm on May 27, assigning it the name *Jangmi*. The [Joint Typhoon Warning Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Typhoon_Warning_Center) (JTWC) followed suit that day, designating the system as *Tropical Depression 06W*. On May 29, Jangmi entered the [Philippine Area of Responsibility](4272946.md) (PAR), receiving the local name *Domeng* from PAGASA, before it was upgraded into a severe tropical storm. The JTWC upgraded the storm into a Category 1 typhoon on May 30, although it would be downgraded back to a tropical storm on June 1 as it encountered less favorable conditions. On June 3, Jangmi landfall over [Wakayama Prefecture](179766.md) in [Japan](15573.md), before skirting eastwards past the rest of mainland Japan over the next day. By the next morning local time, Jangmi had completed its transition into an [extratropical cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extratropical_cyclone), and ultimately ceased to exist as a weather system on June 6.

Damage to infrastructure caused by Jangmi in the Philippines topped to [₱](227672.md)3.4 million, specifically in the [Bangsamoro Autonomous Region of Muslim Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_Region_of_Muslim_Mindanao) (BARMM) region. In Japan, several airline companies in the country, including the [Haneda Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haneda_Airport), have cancelled nearly 900 international and nationwide flights, while some bullet train and railway services, including the [East Japan Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Japan_Railway) were suspended in some areas of the country. 57 houses were partially damaged, while 23 injuries were also reported in the country nationwide. Some road intersections, especially in the [Tokyo](30057.md) area, were either flooded or collapsed due to the storm.

On May 25, the JTWC reportedly marked a low-pressure area for development that spawned 239 nm (443 km) east-southeast of [Yap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yap), with satellite imagery indicating that the disturbance was in a moderately favorable environment for [tropical cyclogenesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclogenesis). The system was described as broad and poorly organized partially obscured by flaring convection.[^cite_note-3] At 00\:00 UTC of the following day, the disturbance's chances for tropical cyclone development in the next 24 hours were upgraded to medium, as it continued to show signs of organization and development with flaring convection within the southern periphery.[^cite_note-4] At 00\:00 UTC on May 26, the JMA initiated its first prognostic reasoning of the system; upgrading it as a tropical depression. The storm slowly moved west-northwestward along the southern portion of a [subtropical high](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical_high).[^cite_note-5] At 15\:00 UTC, JTWC issued a [Tropical Cyclone Formation Alert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Cyclone_Formation_Alert) (TCFA), predicting tropical cyclone formation within 24 to 48 hours. The storm had a favorable environment with low-to-moderate vertical [wind shear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_shear), warm [sea surface temperatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_surface_temperature) (SST), and moderate poleward [upper-level outflow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper-level_outflow).[^cite_note-6]

Early on May 27, JMA reportedly upgraded the depression into a tropical storm, receiving the name *Jangmi*.[^cite_note-7] JTWC later followed suit at 21\:00 UTC and designated Jangmi as *06W*.[^cite_note-8] Later, the system was cited with an ill-defined [low-level circulation center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-level_circulation_center) (LLCC), with a stronger mid-level vortex that generates strong relative vorticity.[^cite_note-9] On May 28, the storm moved north-northwestward along the southern periphery of a subtropical high, although it maintained its intensity due to limited firmness.[^cite_note-10] Later at 15\:00 UTC, JTWC then upgraded Jangmi into a tropical storm, entering favorable conditions for continuous development, and having a partially exposed LLCC from infrared imagery.[^cite_note-11] At 00\:30 PHT on May 29 (16\:30 UTC on the previous day), Jangmi entered the [Philippine Area of Responsibility](4272946.md) (PAR), assigning the local name *Domeng* by PAGASA.[^cite_note-12]Satellite imagery indicated that Jangmi had significantly improved convective signature associated with the storm as it transits along the southwestern periphery of a building [subtropical ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical_ridge) centered to the northeast.[^cite_note-13] Meanwhile, an animated [multispectral satellite imagery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multispectral_imaging) (MSI) showed some [cumulonimbus clusters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumulonimbus_clouds) gathering around the storm's cloud system center (CSC), forming a curved band.[^cite_note-14]



Jangmi intensifying east of the [Philippines](23440.md) on May 29

At 23\:00 PHT (15\:00 UTC), PAGASA upgraded Jangmi into a severe tropical storm.[^cite_note-15] JMA followed suit on the next day, also classifying Jangmi as a severe tropical storm.[^cite_note-16] On May 30, at 09\:00 UTC, the presence of favorable environment later helped Jangmi strengthen into a Category 1-typhoon, with satellite imagery depicting an irregular LLCC, and four [mesovortices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesovortex) imparting a quasi-quadrilateral eyewall geometry.[^cite_note-17] The JMA reported that the storm had temporal suspension of development, causing it to maintain its intensity.[^cite_note-18] However, the storm later showed good cloud characteristics of [anticyclonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anticyclonic) [outflow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outflow_\(meteorology\)).[^cite_note-19] On May 31, satellite imagery reported that Jangmi had a very large (70 nm diameter) eye feature as it depicted the improvement of the convective structure.[^cite_note-20] But at 18\:00 UTC, Jangmi entered an insufficient environment, inhibiting further development, while still moving north-northwestwards between two subtropical highs.[^cite_note-21]

On June 1, at 09\:00 UTC, the JTWC downgraded Jangmi into a tropical storm with a ragged, cloud-filled LLCC south of [Kadena Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadena_Air_Base), due to dry, mid-level [subsident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsidence) air that causes the southwestern eyewall to rapidly degrade.[^cite_note-22] The storm started to move northward, with low sea-surface temperatures, low [tropical cyclone heat potential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Cyclone_Heat_Potential), and weak [vertical wind shear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_wind_shear).[^cite_note-23] As the storm transited to the south end of [Kyushu](16914.md) on June 2, Jangmi discovered highly unfavorable conditions with dry air [entrainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrainment_\(meteorology\)) and weak poleward outflow.[^cite_note-24] Then, the storm started moving northeastward under the influence of prevailing [westerlies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerlies) concurrent to active convective cloud clusters separating from the CSC.[^cite_note-25] On June 3, at 04\:30 JST (19\:30 UTC of the previous day), Jangmi made landfall over [Wakayama Prefecture](179766.md) in western [Japan](15573.md).[^cite_note-26] Microwave imagery revealed that the storm had poorly defined lower-level cloud banding.[^cite_note-27] At 12\:00 UTC, while moving in an east-northeastwards direction, JMA issued their final advisory on Jangmi as the system continued to weaken, declaring it an [extratropical cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extratropical_cyclone).[^cite_note-28]

## Preparations

### Philippines

Although Jangmi did not directly affect the country, it prompted the [PAGASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Atmospheric,_Geophysical_and_Astronomical_Services_Administration) to declare the start of the [southwest monsoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_monsoon) on May 30, citing the presence of southwesterly winds over the western section of the country over the past days.[^cite_note-29] PAGASA also mentioned that despite the storm having a small chance of making landfall in the Philippines, its trough could still bring cloudy skies with moderate to heavy rains over [Eastern Visayas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Visayas) and [Caraga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caraga) over the weekend.[^cite_note-30]

Early on May 26, the [Department of Social Welfare and Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Social_Welfare_and_Development) prepared 4.7 million family food packs to be distributed to families who need assistance amid the storm's impact. The department also prepared standby funds and stockpiles that top up to [₱](227672.md)5.3 billion ([US\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_dollar)86.17 million) in case if the storm makes an impact to the country.[^cite_note-31] On May 29, the [Department of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Health) (DOH) raised a code white alert for those areas that are going to be affected by the storm. Regional offices of the department were also said to be on guard.[^cite_note-32] Two days later, the [Philippine National Police](1313055.md) (PNP) Chief General [Jose Melencio Nartatez Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jose_Melencio_Nartatez) said that police offices in areas that may be affected by Jangmi were directed to remain alert for possible effects, and to respond to requests for assistance in coordination with [local government units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_units) (LGUs).[^cite_note-33]

### Japan

On June 1, residents on [Okinawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okinawa_Island) and the [Amami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amami_Islands) islands were urged by authorities to stay on high alert, while elderly people were warned to evacuate in parts of the aforementioned islands.[^cite_note-34] On the same day, during a news conference, government spokesperson [Minoru Kihara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minoru_Kihara) warned that public transport in [Tokyo](30057.md) and nearby cities would face disruptions as the storm approaches on June 3. He also told those who are living in areas that are likely to be storm-affected to stay mindful of early evacuation, and to remain vigilant and take action to protect their lives.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] On June 2, the [Embassy of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Singapore,_Tokyo) in Tokyo advised those [Singaporeans](38208458.md) in Japan to keep their valuables in waterproof bags to prevent damage to these items. This caused the [Singapore Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Airlines) to reschedule at least 14 flights from [Singapore](27318.md) to Japan between June 2–3.[^cite_note-37]

Authorities raised a level-4 flood emergency warning near the [Kumozu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumozu_River), Kumozu Furukawa, [Zenpukuji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zenpukuji_River), [Meguro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meguro_River), [Kanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanda_River), [Nogawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nogawa_River), Sengawa, and Tategawa rivers. Another level-4 flood emergency warning was also raised in the Hiroto River of [Nichinan, Miyazaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nichinan,_Miyazaki). A level-5 flood emergency warning, which became the highest warning of the country's new weather warning system, was raised in the Kozagawa river of [Wakayama Prefecture](179766.md).[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

## Impact

### Philippines

According to the [National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Disaster_Risk_Reduction_and_Management_Council) (NDRRMC), 85,295 individuals were displaced by the storm in the [Bangsamoro Autonomous Region of Muslim Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_Region_of_Muslim_Mindanao) (BARMM) alone. Damage to infrastructure in the region reached [₱](227672.md)3.4 million ([US\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_dollar)55,055.42), according to the [Office of Civil Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Civil_Defense) (OCD), which were reported in [Sultan sa Barongis, Maguindanao del Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_sa_Barongis) alone. Authorities said that all affected facilities in the municipality are not serviceable.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

### Japan

82,921 people were evacuated across five municipalities in [Okinawa Prefecture](22477.md).[^cite_note-42] Twenty-seven municipalities in the prefecture lost power.[^cite_note-43] On June 1, 250 flights were cancelled across Japan, with 200 of them being cancelled at [Haneda Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haneda_Airport).[^cite_note-44] On June 3, [Japan Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Airlines) and [All Nippon Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Nippon_Airways) cancelled nearly 900 international and domestic flights. [Toyota Motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_Motor) suspended operations in 13 domestic plants, while [Suzuki Motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki_Motor) halted work at all plants in [Shizuoka Prefecture](180999.md).[^cite_note-45]Some bullet train services in [Kyūshū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ky%C5%ABsh%C5%AB) and the southwest and west areas of Japan ([Kansai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansai), [Shikoku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shikoku), [Chūgoku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C5%ABgoku), and [Hokuriku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hokuriku_region) regions) were delayed, while the [East Japan Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Japan_Railway) suspended some services in the [Tokyo](30057.md) area.[^cite_note-46]Authorities urged the entire population of [Miyazaki City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyazaki_City), which is around 390,000 people, to evacuate their homes.[^cite_note-47]Few trees and debris fell and scattered across the [Torii Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torii_Station) of [Yomitan, Okinawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yomitan,_Okinawa), while a large [*banyan*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banyan_tree) tree in Music Town Otoichiba toppled during the storm's onslaught.[^cite_note-48]

17 injuries were reported in Okinawa, while two injuries were reported in [Kagoshima Prefecture](217439.md). The [Miyazaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyazaki_Prefecture), [Tokushima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokushima_Prefecture), [Nara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nara_Prefecture), and [Aichi](524618.md) prefectures each reported one injury, totalling up to 23 injuries. According to the [Fire and Disaster Management Agency](64630578.md), 57 houses were partially damaged, including 48 of them, which were reported in Kagoshima. A road intersection in [Saiwai-ku, Kawasaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saiwai-ku,_Kawasaki) flooded, while a section of a national highway of [Ōta, Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Cta,_Tokyo) inundated. A section of the [National Route 371](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_National_Route_371), which runs along the Kozagawa River, collapsed. 1,800 households in [Ōme, Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Cme,_Tokyo) were left without water supply, while some tree branches entangled to overhead wires in the [Hachiko Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hachiko_Line), specifically between the [Haijima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haijima_Station) and [Komagawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Komagawa_Station) stations.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50]The [Zenpukuji River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zenpukuji_River) in [Suginami, Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suginami,_Tokyo) also got swollen in the middle of heavy rainfall.[^cite_note-51]The [Himeji Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himeji_Castle) in [Hyōgo Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hy%C5%8Dgo_Prefecture), which is a [UNESCO](21786641.md) world heritage site in western Japan, sustained damage in its exterior walls.[^cite_note-52]In [Tsu City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsu_City) of [Mie Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mie_Prefecture), some riverbanks of the [Kumozu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumozu_River) and Kumozu Furukawa rivers bursted, while a nearby curry restaurant in the city was badly hit. A part of the [National Route 260](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Route_260) highway that connects towns along the east coast of the prefecture collapsed, cutting off access to residents in [Minamiise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minamiise). Some large seawalls got crashed by large waves in the [Izu Ōshima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Izu_%C5%8Cshima) island, while some roads in [Chiyoda, Tokyo](47836792.md), especially in the [Kasumigaseki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasumigaseki) district, submerged. In [Hiratsuka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiratsuka) of [Kanagawa Prefecture](61757.md), a large [*zelkova*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zelkova) tree snapped through a university parking lot.[^cite_note-53]
