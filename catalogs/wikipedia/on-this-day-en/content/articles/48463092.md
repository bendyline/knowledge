The **minister of housing and infrastructure** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *ministre du logement et de l'infrastructure*) is the minister of the Crown responsible for the [Department of Housing, Infrastructure and Communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing,_Infrastructure_and_Communities_Canada), as well as several agencies including the [Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Mortgage_and_Housing_Corporation) and the [Canada Infrastructure Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Infrastructure_Bank). The minister is a member of the [King's Privy Council for Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_Privy_Council_for_Canada) and the [Canadian Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Canada).

[Gregor Robertson](6832515.md) has been the minister of housing and infrastructure since May 13, 2025. The minister is selected by the [prime minister](24135.md) and appointed by the Crown. The role has had several titles in the past, including most recently, **Minister of Housing, Infrastructure and Communities** from 2023 to 2025, and **Minister of Infrastructure and Communities** from 2015 to 2023.

## Portfolio

The minister is responsible for several federal organizations including:[^cite_note-2]

- [Housing, Infrastructure and Communities Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing,_Infrastructure_and_Communities_Canada)

- [Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Mortgage_and_Housing_Corporation) (CMHC)

- [Canada Infrastructure Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Infrastructure_Bank) (CIB)

- [Toronto Waterfront Revitalization Initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterfront_Toronto) (Waterfront Toronto; in partnership with the [Province of Ontario](2670174.md) and [City of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_government_of_Toronto))

- [Jacques Cartier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Cartier_Bridge) and [Champlain Bridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel-De_Champlain_Bridge) Incorporated

- [Windsor–Detroit Bridge Authority](26342191.md)

## History

From 2006 to 2013, infrastructure and communities was the responsibility of the [Minister of Transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Transport_\(Canada\)), which was informally styled **Minister of Transport, Infrastructure, and Communities**. From 2013 to 2015, infrastructure and communities was the responsibility of the [Minister of Intergovernmental Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Intergovernmental_Affairs_and_Youth_\(Canada\)), then styled **Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs**. (Technically, the portfolios of infrastructure and intergovernmental affairs were both the responsibility of the [President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Queen's_Privy_Council_for_Canada)[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]). From 2015 to 2021, during the [29th Canadian Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/29th_Canadian_Ministry), the portfolio was assigned its own ministry. From 2021 to 2023, during the [29th Canadian Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/29th_Canadian_Ministry), the portfolio was given back to the [Minister of Intergovernmental Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Intergovernmental_Affairs). However, the portfolio was assigned its own ministry in 2023, and the housing portfolio was also merged into this ministry, styled as **Minister of Housing, Infrastructure and Communities**, with [Sean Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Fraser_\(politician\)) as the minister.
