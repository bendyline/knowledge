**JS *Kunisaki* (LST-4003)** is the third ship of the [*Ōsumi*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Csumi-class_tank_landing_ship) [tank landing ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank_landing_ship) of the [Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force](2370744.md) (JMSDF).

## Construction and career

*Kunisaki* was [laid down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel_laying) on 7 September 2000 and [launched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Launched_\(ship\)) on 13 December 2001 by [Hitachi Zosen Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitachi_Zosen_Corporation), [Maizuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maizuru). She was [commissioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commissioned_\(ship\)) into the 1st Landing Group on 26 February 2003.[^cite_note-1]

This ship was one of several in the JMSDF fleet that participated in disaster relief after the [2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_T%C5%8Dhoku_earthquake_and_tsunami).[^cite_note-2] *Kunisaki* was the first-ever JMSDF ship to serve as the primary mission platform for the 2014 [Pacific Partnership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Partnership) annual humanitarian and disaster response-focused mission.[^cite_note-3] On 29 May 2014, she departed [Yokosuka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yokosuka) to participate in the 2014 Pacific Partnership. She provided Vietnam, Cambodia, and the Philippines with medical aid and carried out cultural exchanges. On 24 July the ship returned to [Kure](329326.md).[^cite_note-4]

The vessel participated in the integrated training Dawn Blitz 15 in the United States from 18 August to 9 September 2015 with [JS *Hyūga*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JS_Hy%C5%ABga) and [JS *Ashigara*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JS_Ashigara) under the command of the [Mine Warfare Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mine_Warfare_Force), and amphibious warfare training.[^cite_note-5] From 3 to 9 September *Kunisaki* participated in the [ADMM Plus Humanitarian Aid / Disaster Relief and Defense Medical College Exercise (AM-HEx2016)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ADMM-Plus?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Thailand from September 3 to 9 of the same year.[^cite_note-6]

Beginning on 5 May 2017, *Kunisaki* performed joint training with the [French Navy](383256.md)'s [*Mistral*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_ship_Mistral_\(L9013\)) in the waters around Japan.[^cite_note-7]

On 11 and 12 January 2019, the ship conducted a joint cruise training with several ships including [USS *Wasp*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Wasp_\(LHD-1\)) and [USS *Green Bay*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Green_Bay_\(LPD-20\)) in the waters west of [Kyushu](16914.md).[^cite_note-8] From 3 June to 21 August, the ship was under the command of the Mine Warfare Force commander during training with the U.S. military ([Talisman Saber 19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exercise_Talisman_Saber)) at the [Shoalwater Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoalwater_Bay) Exercise Area in [Queensland](59051.md), Australia and the surrounding waters.[^cite_note-9] On 18 June, while participating in the training, she was towed by a tugboat and tried to turn around to leave [Brisbane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brisbane), Australia, but she collided with the quay of the port. The stern door broke, and the quay also broke.[^cite_note-10]

In August 2026, *Kunisaki* was deployed to help fight [forest fires](84028953.md) in [Indonesia](14579.md).[^cite_note-11]
