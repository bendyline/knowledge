The **2026 [IFAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_of_American_Football) Women's Flag Football World Championship** was the 12th [World Championships in women's flag football](43253761.md). The tournament took place in [Düsseldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%BCsseldorf), [Germany](11867.md), from 13 to 16 August 2026.

The [United States](77202200.md) were the defending champions coming into the tournament, having beaten [Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_women's_national_flag_football_team) 31-18 in the 2024 World Championship.[^cite_note-1]

The top two teams (except the host nation United States) qualified to compete at the [2028 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_football_at_the_2028_Summer_Olympics) in [Los Angeles](18110.md).[^cite_note-QS-2] Gold medalists Canada, and bronze medalists Mexico were the teams that qualified.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Venues

The championship will take place at [Düsseldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%BCsseldorf)'s flag football complex in Garath. The complex was inaugurated by the City of Düsseldorf and the [New England Patriots](21719.md) in April 2025.[^cite_note-5] Two fields are within the complex, which are the New England Patriots Flag Football Field and the Sebastian Vollmer Flag Football Field.[^cite_note-6]

## Teams

### Qualifiers

The International Federation of American Football introduced a new qualifying format for the Flag Football World Championships.[^cite_note-7]

- 16 teams for each division (men's and women's)

- Host nation automatically qualifies

- Qualification via IFAF Continental Championships, in which the quotas are,&#x20;

  - Europe: 6 berths

  - Americas: 4 berths

  - Asia-Oceania: 2 berths (one for each continent)

  - Africa: 1 berth

- Additional merit-based allocations for two continents based on past performances

- A maximum of six teams per continent (not including hosts)

On 10 August 2026, the Nigerian Federation of American Football informed the International Federation of American Football of their withdrawal due to the inability of the traveling delegation to secure the necessary visas in time for the tournament.[^cite_note-9] As there was no time to replace Nigeria, all their matches were forfeited with a score of 1-0.

## Television

In May 2026, streaming network [DAZN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DAZN) acquired global broadcasting rights to all IFAF events in a multi-year agreement, including the women's world championship.[^cite_note-10]

In Canada, all games are also broadcast on [CBC Gem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_Television) and the CBC Sports website.[^cite_note-11]
