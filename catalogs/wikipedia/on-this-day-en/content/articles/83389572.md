A series of major [wildfires](56106.md) are currently burning throughout the [U.S. state](18618239.md) of [Utah](31716.md).

## Background



Utah drought monitor on June 9

While the typical wildfire season in Utah varies, most fires occur in between July and October.[^cite_note-1] Fire conditions can heavily depend on [monsoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsoons) that last from late June to September. Dry monsoons can allow fires to start and spread easier, while wet ones can cause fire relief. Additionally, hot temperatures and overall dry conditions play a large role.[^cite_note-2]

## Summary

The [2025–26 winter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025-26_North_American_winter) was the warmest on record for Utah and had one of the lowest recorded [snowpack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowpack) for the state, leaving many fuels dry enough to foster fire conditions as early as March.[^cite_note-SLT_1-3] In Southern Utah, vegetation is some of the driest as it has been the past century.[^cite_note-KUER_1-4] Forecasts predict a potentially active [monsoon](57630.md) season,[^cite_note-KSL_1-5] however, it is likely to be late onset until late July.[^cite_note-SLT_1-3]

For June 2026, the [National Interagency Fire Center](5818754.md) (NIFC) has predicted above-average fire potential throughout all of Utah, with the Great Basin Coordination Center predicting the highest fire activity in [Northern Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Utah) from August to September.[^cite_note-KSL_1-5]

In June, drought and multi-day [red flag conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_flag_warning) fueled multiple fast-moving and significant wildfires, with the largest at the time being the Iron fire and [Cottonwood fire](83553516.md). These fires and other fires around them resulted in a state and nationwide response that required numerous resources.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Three firefighters were killed and two were sent to the hospital on June 27 when the Snyder fire overran them as they were trying to take shelter; a fourth died from injuries sustained in the fire on July 24.[^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] That fire has since crossed into neighboring Colorado. The [babylon fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babylon_Fire) exploded a few days after these fires started grabbing resources from other states, showcasing just how dry and volatile the fuels were.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

On June 25, [Utah Governor Spencer Cox](40785284.md) announced a temporary statewide fireworks restrictions amid historic wildfire conditions, as woodland firefighters fought blazes around the state. In the announcement, it was noted that human causes were suspected in 75% of the wildfires in the state thus far in 2026.[^cite_note-13]
