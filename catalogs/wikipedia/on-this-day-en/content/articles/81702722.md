**Horta Inta-A Na Man**[^cite_note-8] (born c. 1960) is a Bissau-Guinean [general](148537.md) who has served as the [transitional](598010.md) [president of Guinea-Bissau](392259.md) and [Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_the_High_Command) of the [High Military Command for the Restoration of National Security and Public Order](81695873.md) since the [2025 coup d'état](81695397.md).[^cite_note-9]

He was born in the 1960s to a family from the [Balanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balanta_people) ethnic group.[^cite_note-auto-10] He attended a Soviet military school, and he wears a badge on his uniform from his time in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

He was the commander of a battalion of the Presidential Guard, and helped defend then-president [Umaro Sissoco Embaló](52335811.md) from a coup-attempt in February 2022.[^cite_note-auto-10] He was then promoted to Brigadier General and made the commander of the National Guard, a position he held until June 2023, when he was promoted to Major General.[^cite_note-auto-10]

Between 2023 and 2025 he was the chief of general staff of the [Revolutionary Armed Forces of the People](12194.md), the military of Guinea-Bissau.

As transitional president, he has installed a 27-person government, led by Prime Minister [Ilídio Vieira Té](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il%C3%ADdio_Vieira_T%C3%A9), the former Finance Minister under Embaló.[^cite_note-13]

## Coup and presidency (2025–present)

Main article: [2025 Guinea-Bissau coup d'état](81695397.md)

On 26 November 2025, Inta-A participated in the [coup](81695397.md) that deposed President [Umaro Sissoco Embaló](52335811.md) which was led by [Brigadier General](206220.md) [Dinis Incanha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinis_Incanha).[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-auto3-15] The formation of the [High Military Command for the Restoration of National Security and Public Order](81695873.md) was announced with Incanha becoming its [chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_the_High_Command), and thus Guinea-Bissau's *[de facto](43594.md)* leader.[^cite_note-auto3-15][^cite_note-bbc-20251126-16] The following day, Incanha stepped down and Inta-A was appointed and subsequently sworn in as transitional president, and thus became the new head of the [military junta](216815.md).[^cite_note-auto1-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

In his first act as transitional president and as head of the junta, Inta-A appointed [Ilídio Vieira Té](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il%C3%ADdio_Vieira_T%C3%A9), an ally of Embaló and the current finance minister as [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_prime_ministers_of_Guinea-Bissau).[^cite_note-20]

He proclaimed on 5 December 2025 that there will be zero tolerance in the fight against drug dealers.[^cite_note-21]

On 22 January 2026, Inta-A announced a decree authorising [elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Guinea-Bissau_general_election) for the legislature and the presidency to be held on 6 December 2026, after it deemed that "all the conditions for organising free, fair and transparent elections have been met".[^cite_note-22]

On 4 March 2026 Inta-A met with Bishop of [Roman Catholic Diocese of Bafatá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Bafat%C3%A1), [Víctor Luís Quematcha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%ADctor_Lu%C3%ADs_Quematcha).[^cite_note-23]

On 11 March 2026, the transitional government set [price control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_control) for nuts. Inta-A said he wants the 2026 cashew campaign to be “the ultimate opportunity for the valorisation” of the product.[^cite_note-24]

In late March the body of anti-military junta activist [Vigario Luis Balanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vigario_Luis_Balanta?action=edit\&redlink=1) of the [Po di Terra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Po_di_Terra?action=edit\&redlink=1) movement was found lifeless in a rice feld, due this protests started against the [military junta](216815.md), the protesters claim that the military junta is behind the death of Balanta. It's speculated that [Umaro Sissoco Embaló](52335811.md) might be installed as [President](392259.md), to stop the protests.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

On 10 August 2026, Inta-A had his first foreign trip, the first country he visited was [Republic of Chad](5488.md).[^cite_note-27]

On 30 August 2026, Inta voted in the [constitutional referendum](83685925.md).[^cite_note-28]
