The **president of the Autonoumous Government of Extremadura**[^cite_note-1] ([Spanish](26825.md): *Presidente de la Junta de Extremadura*) is the head of government of the Spanish [autonomous community](55002.md) of [Extremadura](68204.md).&#x20;

[María Guardiola](72221033.md) from the [PP](404727.md) is the current president of the Government of Extremadura; she was elected with the support of [Vox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vox_\(Spanish_political_party\)).
