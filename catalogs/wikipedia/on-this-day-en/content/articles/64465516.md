The **Commission for Missing Persons and Victims of Violence** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Komisi untuk Orang Hilang dan Korban Tindak Kekerasan*, **KontraS**) is an Indonesian human rights organization established in 1998 to investigate [forced disappearances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forced_disappearance) and acts of violence. It was founded by human rights activist [Munir Said Thalib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munir_Said_Thalib), who in 2004 was poisoned on a flight to Amsterdam.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

[Fatia Maulidiyanti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatia_Maulidiyanti) served as KontraS' coordinator between 2020 and 2023.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

In March 2026, [Andrie Yunus](84209237.md), KontraS' deputy coordinator, was the victim of an [acid attack](30862640.md) in [Jakarta](16275.md), suffering severe chemical burns.[^cite_note-5]
