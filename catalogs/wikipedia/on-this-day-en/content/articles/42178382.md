# Peninsular Spain



A map of peninsular Spain

**Peninsular Spain** is the part of the territory of [Spain](26667.md) located within the [Iberian Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberian_Peninsula), excluding other parts of Spain: the [Canary Islands](5717.md), the [Balearic Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balearic_Islands), [Ceuta](6443.md), [Melilla](20226.md), and several islets and crags off the coast of [Africa](5334607.md) known collectively as *[plazas de soberanía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plazas_de_soberan%C3%ADa)* (places of sovereignty). In Spain, it is mostly known simply as *la Península*. It has land frontiers with [France](5843419.md) and [Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra) to the north, [Portugal](23033.md) to the west, and the [British](31717.md) [overseas territory](207114.md) of [Gibraltar](7607314.md) to the south.

## Characteristics

Peninsular Spain has an area of 492,175  km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-1] In 2013, the population was 43,731,572.[^cite_note-2] It contains 15 of the [autonomous communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_communities) of Spain.

Occupying the central part of Spain, it possesses much greater resources and better interior and exterior communications than other parts of the country. To redress this imbalance, Spanish residents outside the peninsula receive a state subsidy for transport to and from the peninsula.[^cite_note-3]
