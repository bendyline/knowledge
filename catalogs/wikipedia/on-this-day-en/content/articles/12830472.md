The **Texas Military Forces** (**TXMF**) are the principal instrument through which the [Texas Military Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Military_Department) (TMD) executes security policy for [Texas](29810.md), which has the second-largest [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_U.S._states_and_territories_by_population) and [border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_border_states_of_the_United_States) in the United States.[^cite_note-1]

The Texas Military Forces have a budget of \$1.851 billion as of 2023.[^cite_note-2] Current forces include the [Texas Army National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Army_National_Guard), [Texas Air National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Air_National_Guard), and [Texas State Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_State_Guard) of the [Texas Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Militia). Former forces include the [Texian Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Militia), [Texian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Army), [Texian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Navy), [Texas Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_the_Republic_of_Texas), [Texas Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Navy), and [Texas Marines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Marines). It also included the [Texas Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division) from their inception until 1935.[^cite_note-3]

The Texas Military Forces are administered by the Texas Military Department under command of the [adjutant general of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjutant_General_of_Texas), who is appointed by and subordinate to the [governor of Texas](214883.md), the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief)-in-Texas, and is also subordinate to the president of the United States, the commander-in-chief.[^cite_note-\:5-4]

## History

See also: [Texas Military Forces Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Military_Forces_Museum) and [Texas State Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_State_Cemetery)

Texas Military Forces are inextricably linked and have served an integral role in the development, [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Texas), [culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Texas), and international reputation of Texas.[^cite_note-5] They were established with the [Texian Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Militia) in 1823 (thirteen years before the [Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Texas) and twenty-two years before the [State of Texas](29810.md)) by [Stephen Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_F._Austin) to defend the [Old Three Hundred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Three_Hundred) in the [Colony of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coahuila_y_Tejas).[^cite_note-6]

Texas Military Forces sparked the [Texas Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Revolution) at the [Battle of Velasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Velasco) and became [legendary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_and_take_it) at the [Battle of Gonzales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gonzales) (the "[Lexington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battles_of_Lexington_and_Concord) of Texas").[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Their legend continued at their defeat by Mexican forces at [Siege of the Alamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_the_Alamo), with events such as the [Immortal 32](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immortal_32) and [To the People of Texas & All Americans in the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_the_People_of_Texas_&_All_Americans_in_the_World), which resulted in one of the most notable [last stands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_stand) in history.[^cite_note-\:4-10] As of 2018, the [Alamo Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alamo_Mission_in_San_Antonio) is the most visited tourist attraction in Texas and one of ten manmade [UNESCO World Heritage Sites in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_World_Heritage_Sites_in_the_United_States).[^cite_note-11] The Texas Military's legend was sealed at the [Battle of San Jacinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_San_Jacinto), when they defeated [Santa Anna's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_L%C3%B3pez_de_Santa_Anna) army in 18 minutes, achieving independence from the [Centralist Republic of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralist_Republic_of_Mexico) and establishing the Republic of Texas, one of three colonies to [win independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_wars_of_independence) without foreign aid in world history and the only [American state](12610470.md) (the [Thirteen Colonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteen_Colonies) were [aided](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_in_the_American_Revolutionary_War) by France, and the [California Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Republic) and [Republic of Hawaii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Hawaii) were aided by the United States).[^cite_note-\:4-10] The artillery used during the battle, the [Twin Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Sisters_\(cannons\)), are considered the "[Holy Grail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Grail_\(disambiguation\)) of Texas".[^cite_note-12]

During the 19th century, the [Texas Rangers'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division) service in the [Texas-Indians Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas%E2%80%93Indian_wars) and fighting [outlaws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlaw) significantly contributed to the [folklore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folklore) of the "[Wild West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_frontier)".[^cite_note-13]

During the [American Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War), Texas Military Forces served under the command of [Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Texas_Civil_War_Union_units) and [Confederate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Texas_Civil_War_Confederate_units) militaries. When the first units reached Virginia, [Jefferson Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jefferson_Davis) greeted them by declaring: *"Texans! The troops of other states have their reputations to gain, but the sons of the defenders of the Alamo have theirs to maintain."*[^cite_note-14] The [Texas Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Brigade) achieved distinction as Confederate [shock troops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shock_troops), while the [Davis Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_W._Dowling) maintained Texas as the only [Confederate State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_America), along with Florida, unconquered by the [Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_\(American_Civil_War\)) with their victory at [Second Battle of Sabine Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Sabine_Pass). It is referred to as the "[Thermopylae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Thermopylae) of the Confederacy" and the most one-sided Confederate victory of the war.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] Texas Military Forces also fought at the [Battle of Palemito Ranch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Palmito_Ranch).[^cite_note-17]

Texas Military Forces have not waged a [domestic combat operation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_conflicts_involving_the_Texas_Military) since the 19th century. Throughout the 20th and 21st centuries, they have been primarily engaged in [military operations other than war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_operations_other_than_war_\(US\)), including [manmade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropogenic_hazard) and [natural](58921.md) disaster operations, [search and rescue operations](172599.md), [counterdrug operations](1181646.md), and [border security operations](247927.md). Most notably, the [Mexican drug war](8442323.md), [Texas City Disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_City_disaster), [Hurricane Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Harvey), [Hurricane Katrina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Katrina), [Hurricane Rita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Rita), [Bastrop County Complex Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bastrop_County_Complex_Fire), [Operation Jump Start](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Jump_Start), [Operation Phalanx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Phalanx_\(2010-2016\)), [Operation Faithful Patriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Faithful_Patriot), [COVID-19 pandemic](63136490.md), [George Floyd protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Floyd_protests), and [Operation Lone Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Lone_Star).

Under command of the [United States Department of Defense](7279897.md), Texas Military Forces have served in the [Mexican War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_States_military_and_volunteer_units_in_the_Mexican%E2%80%93American_War#Texas), [Spanish War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rough_Riders), [Philippine War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine%E2%80%93American_War), [Mexican Expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pancho_Villa_Expedition), [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/36th_Infantry_Division_\(United_States\)#World_War_I), [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/36th_Infantry_Division_\(United_States\)#World_War_II), [Cold War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Air_National_Guard) ([Korea](16772.md) and [Vietnam](32611.md) campaigns), and [War on Terror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/36th_Infantry_Division_\(United_States\)#Global_War_on_Terror). Since the [September 11 attacks](5058690.md), Texas Military units have been deployed for the war on terror more than any other state.[^cite_note-\:0-18]

## Administration

Main articles: [Texas Military Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Military_Department) and [Adjutant General of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjutant_General_of_Texas)

Texas Military Forces are administered by the Texas Military Department under command of the [Adjutant General of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjutant_General_of_Texas), who is appointed by and subordinate to the [governor of Texas](214883.md), the [commander in chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief).[^cite_note-\:5-4]

## Authority

See also: [Texas Code of Military Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Code_of_Military_Justice), [Civilian control of the military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civilian_control_of_the_military), and [Civil-military co-operation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil-military_co-operation)

Texas Military Forces exist under [civilian control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civilian_control_of_the_military). Since 1903, Texas Military Forces are authorized by [Title 32](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title_32_of_the_United_States_Code) of the United States Code and [Article 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Texas) of the Texas Constitution to "execute the laws of the State, to suppress insurrections, and to repel invasions."[^cite_note-\:1-19][^cite_note-\:2-20] Texas Army National Guard and Texas Air National Guard units are also subject to [Title 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title_10_of_the_United_States_Code) of the United States Code, which legally empowers the [Government of the United States](195149.md) to [mobilize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobilization) them when more resources are needed than available in the [United States Armed Forces](32212.md) for war, national emergency, or national security.[^cite_note-\:3-21] Under Title 10, operations are conducted under command of the [United States Department of Defense](7279897.md) by the [Secretary of Defense](43998.md).

## Conflicts

Main article: [List of conflicts involving the Texas Military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_conflicts_involving_the_Texas_Military)

The history of conflicts involving the Texas Military Forces spans over two centuries, from 1823 to the present, under the command authority (the ultimate source of lawful military orders) of four governments including the Texan government (in 3 incarnations), the [U.S.](3434750.md) government, the [Mexican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Mexican_Republic) government, and the [Confederate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_America) government.

## Capability

The Texas Military Forces are the most capable forces among the state National Guards (Army and Air).[^cite_note-\:6-22][^cite_note-\:7-23][^cite_note-\:0-18] They include [infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infantry), [paratroopers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paratrooper), [special forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_special_operations_units), [armored cavalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armoured_cavalry), [field artillery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_artillery), [communication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_communications), [cyber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyber_force), [intelligence](146678.md), [support](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_service_support), [medical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_medicine), [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_engineering), [civil affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_affairs), and [weapon of mass destruction response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Support_Team) units totaling over 23,000 service members. It also maintains a fleet of [manned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircrew) and [unmanned](58900.md) aircraft with [strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_aircraft), [reconnaissance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reconnaissance_aircraft), and [transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_transport_aircraft) capabilities, a fleet of [rotorcraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotorcraft), and a fleet of [riverine watercraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown-water_navy). It maintains a statewide network of [garrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garrison), training, and monitoring installations. It maintains [command and control](7092305.md) through shelter and mobile [tactical operations centers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tactical_operations_center).[^cite_note-\:7-23][^cite_note-\:0-18]

## Units

Texas Military Forces have undergone many re-designations, reorganizations, and reformations since 1823. Since 1846, [Texas Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Militia) units constitute the entirety of the Texas Military Forces.

### Current forces

#### Texas Army National Guard

Main article: [Texas Army National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Army_National_Guard)

The Texas Army National Guard is the current [land warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_warfare) branch of the Texas Military Forces for the State of Texas. Its major units include the 36th Infantry Division, the 56th Infantry Brigade Combat Team, the 72nd Infantry Brigade Combat Team, the [36th Combat Aviation Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/36th_Combat_Aviation_Brigade), the 71st Battlefield Surveillance Brigade, the 36th Sustainment Brigade, the 176th Engineer Brigade, the 136th Maneuver Enhancement Brigade, the 136th Expeditionary Signal Battalion, and the 136th Regiment (CA) (RTI).

#### Texas Air National Guard

Main article: [Texas Air National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Air_National_Guard)

The Texas Air National Guard is the current [air warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerial_warfare) branch of the Texas Military Forces for the State of Texas. It is composed of the 149th Fighter Wing, the 136th Airlift Wing, the 147th Attack Wing, the 254th Combat Communications Group, the 272nd Engineering Installation Squadron, and the 204th Security Forces Squadron. The 149th Fighter Wing prepares pilots for combat, the 136th Airlift Wing flies C-130s in-and out of theater and the 147th Reconnaissance Wing has recently acquired [Reapers](4769988.md) to be the eyes in the hostile sky.

#### Texas State Guard

Main article: [Texas State Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_State_Guard)

The Texas State Guard is the current [state defense force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_defense_force) branch of the Texas Military Forces for the State of Texas. It assists and augments Texas military and civil authorities in times of state emergencies, and in on-going support of National Guard units and local communities.

### Former forces

#### Texian Militia

Main article: [Texian Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Militia)

The Texian Militia was the [militia](20622.md) forces of the [Texian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texians) [Colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coahuila_y_Tejas) from 1823 to 1835 and the inaugurate force of the Texas Military. It was established by [Stephen F. Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_F._Austin) on August 5, 1823, for defense of the [Old Three Hundred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Three_Hundred) colonists against the [Karankawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karankawa_people), [Comanche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comanche), and [Cherokee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherokee) tribes; among others. Its most notable unit, the [Texas Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division), remained in continuous service of Texas Military Forces until 1935.

#### Texas Rangers

Main article: [Texas Ranger Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division)

The Texas Rangers were established as a unit of the [Texian Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Militia) in the [Texian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texians) [Colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coahuila_y_Tejas). During the [Texas Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Revolution), it served as a unit of the [Texian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Army) providing [cavalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavalry_tactics) and conducting [special operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_operations) such as demolition of [Vince's Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vince's_Bridge) and [Immortal 32](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immortal_32) relief force. During the [Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Texas), it continued to serve as a special forces unit employing [guerrilla warfare](12720.md) in the [Texas-Indian Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas%E2%80%93Indian_wars).

#### Texian Army

Main article: [Texian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Army)

The Texian Army, also known as the Revolutionary Army and Army of the People, was the [land warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_warfare) branch of the [Texian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texians) armed forces during the [Texas Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Revolution). It spontaneously formed from the [Texian Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Militia) in October 1835 following the [Battle of Gonzales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gonzales). Along with the [Texian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Navy), it helped the [Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Texas) win independence from the [Centralist Republic of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralist_Republic_of_Mexico) on May 14, 1836, at the [Treaties of Velasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaties_of_Velasco). Although the [Texas Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_the_Republic_of_Texas) was officially established by the [Consultation of the Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consultation_\(Texas\)) on November 13, 1835, it did not replace the Texian Army until after the [Battle of San Jacinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_San_Jacinto).

#### Texian Navy

Main article: [Texian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Navy)

The Texian Navy, also known as the Revolutionary Navy and First Texas Navy, was the [naval warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_warfare) branch of the [Texian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texians) armed forces during the [Texas Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Revolution). It was established by the [Consultation of the Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consultation_\(Texas\)) on November 25, 1835. Along with the [Texian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Army), it helped the [Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Texas) win independence from the [Centralist Republic of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralist_Republic_of_Mexico) on May 14, 1836, at the [Treaties of Velasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaties_of_Velasco). It was replaced by the [Texas Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Navy) on March 23, 1839.

#### Texas Army

Main article: [Army of the Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_the_Republic_of_Texas)

The Texas Army, officially the Army of the Republic of Texas, was the [land warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_warfare) branch of the Texas Military Forces during the [Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Texas). It descended from the [Texian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Army), which was established in October 1835 to fight for independence from [Centralist Republic of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralist_Republic_of_Mexico) in the [Texas Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Revolution). The Texas Army was provisionally formed by the [Consultation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consultation_\(Texas\)) in November 1835, however it did not replace the Texian Army until after the [Battle of San Jacinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_San_Jacinto). The Texas Army, [Texas Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Navy), and [Texas Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Militia) were officially established on September 5, 1836, in Article II of the [Constitution of the Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Republic_of_Texas). The Texas Army and Texas Navy were merged with the [United States Armed Forces](32212.md) on February 19, 1846, after the Republic of Texas became the [28th state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_annexation) of [America](3434750.md).

#### Texas Navy

Main article: [Texas Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Navy)

The Texas Navy, officially the Navy of the Republic of Texas, also known as the Second Texas Navy, was the [naval warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_warfare) branch of the Texas Military Forces during the [Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Texas). It descended from the [Texian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Navy), which was established in November 1835 to fight for independence from [Centralist Republic of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralist_Republic_of_Mexico) in the [Texas Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Revolution). The Texas Navy, [Texas Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_the_Republic_of_Texas), and [Texas Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Militia) were officially established on September 5, 1836, in Article II of the [Constitution of the Republic of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Republic_of_Texas). The Texas Navy and Texas Army were merged with the [United States Armed Forces](32212.md) on February 19, 1846, after the Republic of Texas became the [28th state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_annexation) of [America](3434750.md).

#### Texas Marines

Main article: [Texas Marines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Marines)

The Texas Marines, officially the Marine Corps of the Republic of Texas, were the [naval infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marines) of the [Texas Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Navy) tasked with enforcing discipline aboard ships, providing security at [shore stations](291782.md), [sharpshooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharpshooter), and [naval boarding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_boarding). It was officially established on January 14, 1836, and modeled after the [United States Marines Corps](17349325.md).

#### Texas Militia

Main article: [Texas Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Militia)

Since 1846, Texas Militia units have constituted the entirety of the Texas Military Forces. Current units are the [Texas Army National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Army_National_Guard), [Texas Air National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Air_National_Guard), and [Texas State Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_State_Guard). Former units include the Texas Home Guard/State Troops (1861–65), Texas Volunteer Guard (1871-1904), Texas Reserve Militia (1905-1913), Texas Home Guard (1914-1918), Texas Reserve Militia (1919-1940), Texas Defense/State Guard (1941–45), and Texas State Guard Reserve Corps (1945-1965).

## Portrayal in media

- 1945: *[The Battle of San Pietro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Battle_of_San_Pietro),* a documentary film based on the [Battle of San Pietro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_San_Pietro_Infine). Depicts the [36th Infantry Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/36th_Infantry_Division_\(United_States\)).

- 1945: *[A Walk in the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Walk_in_the_Sun_\(1945_film\))*, a feature film based on the [Battle of Salerno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Avalanche). Depicts the [36th Infantry Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/36th_Infantry_Division_\(United_States\)).

- 1960: *[The Alamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Alamo_\(1960_film\))*, a feature film based on the [Battle of the Alamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Alamo). Depicts [Texas Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division) and [Texian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Army).

- 1993: *[Gettysburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gettysburg_\(1993_film\))*, a feature film based on the [Battle of Gettysburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gettysburg). Depicts the [Texas Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Brigade).

- 1997: *[Rough Riders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rough_Riders_\(miniseries\)),* a feature film based on the [Battle of San Juan Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_San_Juan_Hill). Depicts the [Texas Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division) serving in the 1st United States Volunteer Cavalry.

- 1989: *[Lonesome Dove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lonesome_Dove_\(miniseries\))*, a miniseries based on a fictional plot. Depicts the [Texas Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division).

- 1998: *[Dead Man's Walk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Man's_Walk_\(miniseries\))*, a miniseries based on the [Texan Santa Fe Expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texan_Santa_Fe_Expedition). Depicts the [Texas Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division) and [Texas Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_the_Republic_of_Texas).

- 2004: *[The Alamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Alamo_\(2004_film\))*, a feature film based on the [Battle of the Alamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Alamo). Depicts [Texas Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division) and [Texian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Army).

- 2010: *Choctaw Code Talkers*: a documentary film based on the [Choctaw Code Talkers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choctaw_code_talkers) of the [142nd Infantry Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/142nd_Infantry_Regiment_\(United_States\)) during the [Battles of the Meuse–Argonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meuse%E2%80%93Argonne_offensive).

- 2015: *[Texas Rising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Rising),* a 10-hour miniseries based on the [Texas Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Revolution). Depicts [Texas Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division) and [Texian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Army).

- 2018: *[The Men Who Built America: Frontiersmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Men_Who_Built_America:_Frontiersmen)* ("Empire or Liberty"), an episode based on the [Battle of the Alamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Alamo). Depicts [Texas Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Ranger_Division) and [Texian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texian_Army).
