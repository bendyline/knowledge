The **General Commissariat of Judiciary Police** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Comisaría General de Policía Judicial*, **CGPJ**) is an intelligence service within the [National Police Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Police_Corps) of [Spain](26667.md) responsible for the investigation of [organized crime](22625.md), [economic and monetary crimes](11656148.md) or [cybercrime](341598.md). CGPJ is also in charge of investigating [drug trafficking](218449.md), [gambling control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaming_control_board) and collecting intelligence for the police corps.

The CGPJ was created in 1958 as General Commissariat of Criminal Investigation (CGIC)[^cite_note-3] and renamed as General Commissariat of Judiciary Police in 1978.[^cite_note-4]

## Units of the CGPJ

The units that form the General Commissariat of Judiciary Police are:[^cite_note-5]

### General Secretariat



The Secretary-General supports and assists the Commissary-General, analyzes and plans its general lines of action, and manages matters relating to the personnel regime and means attached to it. It is also responsible for the data banks of the General Commissariat.

### Organized Crime and Drugs Unit

The Organized Crime and Drugs Unit (**UDYCO**) investigates and prosecutes criminal activities related to [drug trafficking](218449.md) and [organized crime](22625.md) at both national and [transnational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnationality) levels. It also works as the operational coordination and technical support of its territorial units. This unit also contains:&#x20;

- **Central Brigade of Narcotics**, which investigates and prosecutes crimes related to the [illegal](218449.md) traffic of drugs, according to the legislation on the matter.

- **Central Organized Crime Brigade**, which investigates and prosecutes criminal activities linked to [organized crime](22625.md). The leadership on the Response Groups against Organized Crime (GRECO) stands out.

- **Unit attached to the [State Attorney General's Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Attorney_General)**, which carries out the duties assigned by the [Judiciary Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary_Police) to the body to which it is attached.

### Unit of Specialized and Violent Delinquency

The **Unit of Specialized and Violent Delinquency** (**UDEV**) assumes the investigation and prosecution of criminal activities, at national and transnational levels, related to [heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Property), especially [historical and artistic heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_heritage_site), [consumption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumption_\(economics\)) and [environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environment_\(biophysical\)), criminal offenses in the field of [doping in sport](860860.md), the use of new Technologies, family and children, and crimes against persons and [sexual freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_freedom), as well as the operational coordination and technical support of the respective territorial units. From this Unit will depend:

- **Brigade of Investigation of the Specialized Delinquency**, in charge of the investigation and persecution of the crimes related to the patrimony, the consumption and the environment, the [intellectual](14724.md) and [industrial property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_property). Of this brigade will depend the Service of Control of [Gaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaming_control_board), with the mission of monitoring and inspection of the compliance with the norms on the game and investigation of the crimes that are generated in this area.

- **Central Investigation Brigade for Crimes Against Persons**, charged with the investigation and prosecution of crimes against [life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life), [liberty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty), [homicides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homicides), [sexual assaults](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_assaults) and [missing persons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missing_persons); As well as those committed in the [family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family) and in the field of [minors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_\(law\)).

- **Historical Heritage Brigade**, in charge of the investigation and prosecution of criminal activities related to the historical-artistic heritage.



GRECO Unit emblem

### Criminal Intelligence Central Unit

The **Criminal Intelligence Central Unit** (**UCIC**) is responsible for the collection, reception, analysis, treatment and development of information related to [criminality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminality), as well as activities in the field of foresight and strategy. UCIC also assume the function of coordinating information of police interest. This Unit and the territorial units of intelligence, on which they are functionally dependent, assume the development of the [criminal intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_intelligence) function.

### Unit of Economic and Fiscal Delinquency

The **Unit of Economic and Fiscal Delinquency** (**UDEF**) assumes the investigation and prosecution of criminal activities, nationally and internationally, in matters of [economic and fiscal delinquency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_crime), as well as the operational coordination and technical support to the respective Territorial Units. From this Unit will depend:

- **Central Brigade of Economic and Fiscal Delinquency**, which is responsible for investigating crimes related to Public Treasury, against [Social Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_security_in_Spain), financial [fraud](58790.md), [stock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock) offenses and [scams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scams) of special importance.

- **Central Anti-corruption and Money Laundering Research Brigade**, which is responsible for investigating crimes related to [money laundering](19390.md) arising from criminal acts, [international piracy](50715.md), [corruption](3608404.md) in various forms and recovery of assets.

- **Central Brigade of Economic Intelligence**, which is responsible for investigating criminal offenses related to activities subject to control, [surveillance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveillance) or inspection of the bodies responsible for the prevention of [money laundering](19390.md).

- **Brigade of Investigation of the Bank of Spain**, which assumes the investigation of the crimes related to the [falsification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterfeit_money) of national and foreign [currency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currency).

- **Unit attached to the Office of the Special Prosecutor against Corruption and Organized Crime**, which will carry out the duties of the [Judiciary Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary_Police) in the attached body.

### Technological Research Unit

The **Technological Research Unit** (**UIT**) assumes the investigation and prosecution of criminal activities that involve the use of [ICT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_and_communications_technology) and [cybercrime](341598.md) at national and transnational level. This unit will depend on:

- **Central Brigade of Technological Investigation**, corresponding to the investigation of criminal activities related to the protection of [minors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_\(law\)), [privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privacy), [intellectual](14724.md) and [industrial property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_property) and [telecommunications](33094374.md) [fraud](58790.md).

- **Central Computer Security Brigade**, which is responsible for investigating criminal activities that affect [logical security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical_security) and [fraud](58790.md).
