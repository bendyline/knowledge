# Cape Verde at the FIFA World Cup



Cape Verde facing Uruguay at the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md)

[Cape Verde](1205575.md) appeared in their first [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) in [2026](36581929.md). In their debut match against Spain, they drew 0–0, earning their first point in World Cup history.[^cite_note-1]

On 13 October 2025, they [qualified](60000294.md) for the World Cup for the first time after a 3–0 victory over [Eswatini](1212508.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] At the time of their qualification, Cape Verde was noted as the smallest country by land area and the second-least-populated country to qualify for the World Cup, with a land area of 4,033 square kilometres (1,557 sq mi) and a population of just under 525,000.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] However, after the qualification of [Curaçao's team](24862626.md) a month later, Cape Verde became the second-smallest country by land area and the third-least-populated.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

The team was drawn into Group H alongside Spain, Uruguay, and Saudi Arabia. In their first ever World Cup game, they caused a major upset by drawing 0–0 to Spain. That upset was furthered by a 2–2 draw against Uruguay[^cite_note-dawan-8] and a 0–0 draw against Saudi Arabia[^cite_note-9] to finish second in their group, thereby becoming the smallest-ever nation to reach the [knockout stages](939680.md) of a FIFA World Cup.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

In the round of 32, Cape Verde lost 3-2 to defending champions Argentina in extra-time, eliminating Cape Verde from the competition.

#### Off-field developments

During the tournament, [New Zealand Police](406756.md) confirmed that captain [Ryan Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Mendes) was under investigation over an allegation of rape relating to an incident reported at a hotel in [Auckland](18660332.md) in March 2026, when the squad had been in the city for [FIFA Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Series) friendlies. The complainant had worked as an interpreter for the delegation. No charges were laid, and the allegation has not been tested in court.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] FIFA said it was in contact with the New Zealand authorities, and neither FIFA nor the Cape Verdean federation announced any disciplinary action.[^cite_note-21]
