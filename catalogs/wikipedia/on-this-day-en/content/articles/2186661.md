**Jalpaiguri** (Bengali pronunciation: [\[d͡ʒɔlpai̯guɽi\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Bengali)) is a city in the Indian state of [West Bengal](34040.md).[^cite_note-10] It is the headquarter of the [Jalpaiguri district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_district) and the [Jalpaiguri division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_division), covering the five districts of [North Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Bengal).[^cite_note-11] The city is located on the banks of the [Teesta River](1365385.md) and [Karala River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karala_River), on the foothills of the [Himalayas](37966.md).[^cite_note-12] The city is home to the [circuit bench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bench_\(law\)) of the [Calcutta High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta_High_Court), the other seat being at [Port Blair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Blair) in the [Andaman and Nicobar Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaman_and_Nicobar_Islands).[^cite_note-Census2011-5][^cite_note-CensusIndia2011-6] Jalpaiguri features the [Jalpaiguri Government Engineering College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_Government_Engineering_College), the second campus of the [University of North Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Bengal) and the Biswa Bangla Krirangan/ Jalpaiguri Sports Village.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] It lies about 35 km (22 mi) south of its [twin city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_cities) [Siliguri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siliguri). The merging of the two cities makes it the largest metropolis of the [region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Bengal).

## Etymology

The name "Jalpaiguri" comes from the [Bengali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_language) words "*Jalpai*" meaning [*Ceylon olive*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elaeocarpus_serratus), which grew in the city and adjacent areas, and "Guri" meaning *a place*.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

## Geography

Jalpaiguri is the district headquarter of [Jalpaiguri District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_District). It is situated at a height of 89m(292ft) from Mean Sea Level. The city lies very close to the [Himalayas](37966.md) and on the left bank of [Teesta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teesta) river. The Karla river is another major river of Jalpaiguri which flows through the city.

### Climate

#### Temperature

The annual average temperature of Jalpaiguri city is 24.8 °C. In summer, the temperature varies from a minimum of 20–22 °C to a maximum of 30–35 °C. August is the hottest month with an average temperature of 29.2 °C. The temperature in peak summer sometimes exceeds 37 °C.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] The highest temperature ever recorded in Jalpaiguri was 40.9 °C, recorded on 21 July 1986. The winter maximum temperature here hovers around 22–25 °C and minimum drops to 8–10 °C. The minimum temperature sometimes falls below 7 °C.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] The lowest temperature ever dropped in Jalpaiguri city was 2.2 °C, recorded on 5 February 1914.[^cite_note-22]

#### Rainfall and other conditions

As Jalpaiguri is located on the base of the [Himalayas](37966.md), the city receives a high amount of rainfall throughout the year. The average annual rainfall is approximately 3400 mm.[^cite_note-auto-23] A distinct season, monsoon is observed from June to September. About 917.3 mm rain falls in July. Though the amount plummets to 3.3 mm in December.[^cite_note-auto-23] On an average, the rainy days in Jalpaiguri city are 100.[^cite_note-auto-23] The heaviest one-day rainfall occurred on 10 July 1999 when 474 mm rain fell on the city. The wettest month was July 1996. In that month, a total of 1546.2 mm of rain was received.[^cite_note-auto-23] Jalpaiguri doesn't get snowfall. Humidity is very high in the air.

According to the 2011 census, Jalpaiguri had a population of 107,341 people (53,708 male and 53,633 female)[^cite_note-Census2011-5] while the Metropolitan region had a population of 169,002 people (85,226 males and 83,787 females). The population for children aged 0-6 was 14,522. ## Civic administration

Civic administration is the responsibility of the [Jalpaiguri Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_Municipality) which is the tenth oldest in West Bengal[^cite_note-26] after that of [Kolkata](47905.md),[^cite_note-27] [Uttarpara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttarpara),[^cite_note-28] [Shantipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantipur),[^cite_note-29] [Howrah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howrah),[^cite_note-30] [Krishnanagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishnanagar,_Nadia),[^cite_note-31] [Burdwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burdwan),[^cite_note-32] [Basirhat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basirhat),[^cite_note-33] [Berhampore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baharampur)[^cite_note-34] and [Siuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suri,_Birbhum).[^cite_note-35] Set up in 1885, it is headed by a chairman who is elected by the residents of the city. It has 25 wards that elect the ward councillors. The [All India Trinamool Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_India_Trinamool_Congress) is in the power of this municipal body. The chairperson of the municipality is Papia Pal.[^cite_note-delimit-36]

## Economy

Jalpaiguri has its own bank, the Jalpaiguri Central Co-operative Bank, whose head office is located at Temple Street of the city. Founded in 1919, it has more than 20 branches with ATMs spread across the District.[^cite_note-37]

## Utility services

The municipality is responsible for providing basic services, such as [potable water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potable_water) and [sanitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanitation). The water is supplied by the municipal authorities using its groundwater resources, and almost all the houses in the municipal area are connected through the system.[^cite_note-38]

## Transport

### Rail

Jalpaiguri city region has four important railway stations:-

1. [Jalpaiguri Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_railway_station) is the oldest station in the area.

2. [Jalpaiguri Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_Road_railway_station) was opened in 1944 and is the primarily crucial railway station of the city.

3. [Mohitnagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohitnagar_railway_station) lies at Mohitnagar in the [Haldibari-New Jalpaiguri line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haldibari-New_Jalpaiguri_line).

4. [Raninagar Jalpaiguri Junction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raninagar_Jalpaiguri_Junction_railway_station) is situated at the further western part of the city. The railway station serves Jalpaiguri, [Raninagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raninagar,_Jalpaiguri) and other nearby places.

### Road



Goshala More, Highway connector of Jalpaiguri

The [Asian Highway 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Highway_2) and the National Highway 31D \[[NH 27](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_27_\(India\)) (New Numbering)] passes just through the city connecting it right with the rest of the country.

The Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Central Bus Terminus situated next to the North Bengal State Transport Corporation (NBSTC) Bus Terminal is a major stop for most private and state-owned buses in the region. The Royal Government of [Bhutan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan) also operates buses from Jalpaiguri to its border town [Jaigaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaigaon)/ [Phuentsholing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phuentsholing), [Bhutan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan). Air-conditioned sleeper coach bus service is also available from Jalpaiguri to [Kolkata](47905.md) (Banglashree Express).[^cite_note-39]

There are several bus stands[^cite_note-40] in Jalpaiguri:-

- NBSTC Depot: Government buses are available from here.

- Kadamtala Bus Terminus: Private buses to [Siliguri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siliguri) and [Islampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islampur,_Uttar_Dinajpur) are available from here.

- Super Bus Stand near the [Jalpaiguri railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_railway_station) – Super (or non-stop) bus services to Siliguri are available from here.

- Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Private Bus Terminus: Buses to the [Dooars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dooars) region, i.e., [Malbazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malbazar), Birpara, are available from here.

- Bowbazaar Bus Stand: Bus services to [Haldibari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haldibari,_West_Bengal) are available from here.

### Air

The nearest airport is [Bagdogra Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagdogra_Airport), [Siliguri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siliguri), which lies 45 km north-west from Jalpaiguri city centre, with regular flights to major cities like [Kolkata](47905.md), [New Delhi](51585.md), [Mumbai](19189.md), [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai), [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore), [Hyderabad](37534.md), [Ahmedabad](206337.md), [Guwahati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guwahati) and has international connectivity with [Paro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paro,_Bhutan) and [Bangkok](56615.md).

## Education



[Jalpaiguri Government Engineering College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_Government_Engineering_College) (JGEC) campus

Jalpaiguri's schools are run by the state government or private organisations, many of which are religious. Bengali and English are the primary languages of instruction; [Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language) and [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi) are also used.[^cite_note-educationsystem-41][^cite_note-42] Schools in Jalpaiguri follow the ["10+2+3" plan](293121.md). After completing their secondary education, students typically enroll in schools that have a higher secondary facility and are affiliated with the [West Bengal Council of Higher Secondary Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bengal_Council_of_Higher_Secondary_Education), the [Indian Certificate of Secondary Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Certificate_of_Secondary_Education) (ICSE), or the [Central Board of Secondary Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Board_of_Secondary_Education) (CBSE).[^cite_note-educationsystem-41] They usually choose a focus on liberal arts, business, or science. Vocational programs are also available.[^cite_note-educationsystem-41] In 2012, the Government proposed a medical college for the suburb of Paharpur.[^cite_note-43]

Colleges include:&#x20;

- [EIILM-Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EIILM-Kolkata), Jalpaiguri Campus is a business and management Institute, established in 2018. The college offers BBA, BCA and MBA courses which are affiliated to [Raiganj University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raiganj_University).

  [^cite_note-44]

- [Jalpaiguri Government Engineering College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_Government_Engineering_College) is an autonomous institution of the [Government of West Bengal](6629274.md), one of premier technical institutes is present in Jalpaiguri, best known for scientists like [Promode R. Bandyopadhyay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promode_R._Bandyopadhyay). It is located near the [National Highway 27](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_27_\(India\)) (West Bengal State Highway 12A) at Denguajhar, Jalpaiguri.

  [^cite_note-45]

  &#x20;The institute is one of the oldest institutes of engineering in the state.

- [Jalpaiguri Government Medical College and Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_Government_Medical_College_and_Hospital), established in 2022, is a full-fledged tertiary referral Government Medical college. It is located at Jalpaiguri town of West Bengal. The college imparts the degree of Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery (MBBS). The hospital associated with the college is one of the largest hospitals in the Jalpaiguri district. The yearly undergraduate student intake is 100 from the year 2022.

- [Jalpaiguri Law College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpaiguri_Law_College) is the city's law college.

- [North Bengal St. Xavier's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Bengal_St._Xavier's_College), all of which are affiliated with the [University of North Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Bengal). Also, since 2011, the second campus of the university with the same name provides with master's degrees in arts, sciences, and commerce.
