**Kutum** is a town in the [Sudanese](27421.md) state of [North Darfur](767666.md). It lies 120 kilometers (75 mi) northwest of the state capital, [Al-Fashir](3956787.md). The town is located along a [wadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadi) and therefore also known as **Wadi Kutum**. It lies north along the [Marrah Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marrah_Mountains); the Kutum [volcanic field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanic_field) is better known as the [Tagabo Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagabo_Hills). As of 2006, it had a population of 45,000, predominantly of the [Fur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fur_people), [Tunjur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunjur) and [Berti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berti_people) ethnicities. Kutum lies on one of the traditional north–south migration routes used by Darfuri [pastoralists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pastoralism).

## Facilities

The town hosts a local market which operates on Mondays and Thursdays. Other facilities in the town includes a hospital with basic medical and surgical services and a small botanical gardens alongside the Wadi and the market. Within the town two primary health care clinics also operate.

The town hosts a number of mosques, with the mosque at the market having a notable [minaret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minaret).

Commercial services includes bakeries, groceries, public phone outlets, as well as mechanical services. As of November 2006 no formal banking services were available in the town.

Electricity is available a few hours most days from a communal generator. For most of the population, water is fetched from wells around the town.

## History

Main article: [History of Darfur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Darfur)

From September to November 1989, during the [Chadian–Libyan conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chadian%E2%80%93Libyan_conflict), Kutum was briefly occupied by Chadian forces.[^cite_note-1]

Kutum is a city in [North Darfur](767666.md) state, inhabited primarily by the non-Arab [Fur people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fur_people), with minorities of non-Arab [Tunjur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunjur_people) and Berti groups. The city lies on a route often used by nomadic Arab tribes, and as a result, many of the villages around Kutum are populated by Arab groups.

Throughout the [war in Darfur](5043637.md), while mostly government-controlled, the ethnic groups of the inhabitants are associated with the rebel groups, and the town was briefly taken by the rebels in August 2003.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Kutum was a hotbed of anti-Bashir activity, and was the site of several battles between various rebel groups, including the [Sudanese Liberation Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_Liberation_Army) and [National Redemption Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Redemption_Front), against [Janjaweed](683270.md) and [Sudanese armed forces](27429.md).[^cite_note-4] The area northeast of town is largely controlled by the rebel [Sudan Liberation Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_Liberation_Movement), while 'Arab' militias such as the Janjaweed are to the south and west.[^cite_note-tensions-5] Janjaweed also attacked non-Arab civilians in Kutum throughout the war.[^cite_note-6] In the late 2010s, these attacks became sporadic, and occurred against civilians in central Kutum.[^cite_note-7]

Two camps for [internally displaced persons](427408.md), Fatta Borno and Kassab, formed near the town.[^cite_note-tensions-5] In 2018, many refugees returned to Kutum, as attacks were dying down in the area.[^cite_note-8] Following the end of the war in Darfur in 2020, attacks continued by gunmen in and around Kutum.[^cite_note-9] Many refugees in Kutum live in Kassab refugee camp, just north of the town.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

Following the Sudanese revolution, Nimir Mohammed Abdelrahman was appointed governor, and Mohammed Hassan Arabi was dismissed.[^cite_note-12]

The [Battle of Kutum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kutum) broke out in the [War in Sudan (2023)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Sudan_\(2023\)) and ended with a [RSF](43053874.md) victory gaining control of the town.[^cite_note-13] On March 17, [Hemedti](60669608.md) threatened to dismantle [IDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDPs) camps across Sudan, including those in Kutum.[^cite_note-14]
