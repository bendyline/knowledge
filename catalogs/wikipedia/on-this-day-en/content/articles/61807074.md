**Voznesenovka** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Вознесеновка) is a [rural locality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) (a [selo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village#Russia)) and the administrative center of Voznesenovskoye Rural Settlement, [Shebekinsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shebekinsky_District), [Belgorod Oblast](478857.md), Russia. The population was 1,466 as of 2010.[^cite_note-2] There are 20 streets.

## Geography

Voznesenovka is located 15 km east of [Shebekino](4570394.md) (the district's administrative centre) by road. Nezhegol is the nearest rural locality.[^cite_note-3]
