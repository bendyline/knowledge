**Temnine Et Tahta** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): تمنين التحتا, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Temnīn et-Taḥtā*) is a village located in the [Baalbek District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baalbek_District) of the [Baalbek-Hermel Governorate](53254600.md) in [Lebanon](17771.md).

## History

In 1838, [Eli Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Smith) noted *Temnin the lower'*&#x73; population as being predominantly [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh).[^cite_note-1]
