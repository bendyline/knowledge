**Oliver Stephen Millroy** (born 21 April 1990) is a British [racing driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_driver) and [stunt driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stunt_driver) who competes in the [IMSA SportsCar Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMSA_SportsCar_Championship) for [Inception Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimum_Motorsport) and the China GT Championship for FIST Team AAI.

A regular co-driver of [Brendan Iribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Iribe) at [Inception Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inception_Racing), Millroy has driven [McLaren](20994.md) and [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari) sportscars in [IMSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMSA_SportsCar_Championship) and the [24 Hours of Le Mans](1401596.md) since 2021. He is also a champion in [International GT Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_GT_Open), [Asian Le Mans Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Le_Mans_Series) and the [GT World Challenge Europe Endurance Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GT_World_Challenge_Europe_Endurance_Cup).

## Career



Millroy driving for [Manor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manor_Motorsport) at [Oulton Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oulton_Park) in [Formula Renault 2.0 UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_Renault_2.0_UK) in 2010.

Millroy made his single-seater debut in 2008, competing in [Formula BMW Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Formula_BMW_Europe_season) for [FMS International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coloni_Motorsport), finishing 21st overall with a best result of ninth at the [Hockenheimring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockenheimring).[^cite_note-1] Millroy also raced in the Americas and Pacific counterparts, in which he scored a lone win at [Shanghai](952143.md) in the latter for Motaworld Racing,[^cite_note-2] as well as finishing sixth in the end-of-year [Formula BMW World Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Formula_BMW_World_Final) for them.[^cite_note-3] A part-time campaign in [Formula BMW Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Formula_BMW_Europe_season) followed in 2009,[^cite_note-4] before spending two seasons in the [Formula Renault 2.0 UK Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_Renault_2.0_UK_Championship), in which he most notably finished sixth in 2010 with a win at [Snetterton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snetterton_Circuit) for [Manor Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manor_Motorsport).[^cite_note-5]

In 2012, Millroy moved to sportscars, joining BMW-fielding [Ecurie Ecosse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecurie_Ecosse) to compete in the Pro-Am class of the [Blancpain Endurance Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Blancpain_Endurance_Series).[^cite_note-6] After scoring a lone point with them, Millroy continued with the team to compete in the following year's [European Le Mans Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_European_Le_Mans_Series) in the GTC class.[^cite_note-7] He won the season-opening [3 Hours of Silverstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_3_Hours_of_Silverstone) and took two other podiums to end the year third in points.[^cite_note-8] Moving to Asia for 2014, Millroy contested most of the [Asian Le Mans Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Asian_Le_Mans_Series) for fellow BMW customer team AAI-Rstrada,[^cite_note-9] finishing on the podium in all three races he started to take fourth in the GT standings.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] Continuing with the team for the [2015–16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_Asian_Le_Mans_Series) season,[^cite_note-12] Millroy took three podiums and once again finished fourth in the GT standings.[^cite_note-13] Continuing with the team for the next two seasons, Millroy most notably took a win at [Fuji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuji_Speedway) in the [2017–18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017%E2%80%9318_Asian_Le_Mans_Series) season en route to runner-up honours in the GT class.[^cite_note-14]



Millroy began his GT3 spell at [Inception Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inception_Racing) in 2020.

After spending most of 2019 on the sidelines, Millroy won the end-of-year [Gulf 12 Hours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Gulf_12_Hours) for McLaren-fielding Balfe Motorsport in the GT4 class.[^cite_note-15] Returning to GT3 for 2020, Millroy began a long association with [Optimum Motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimum_Motorsport) and [Brendan Iribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Iribe) as he contested the [International GT Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_International_GT_Open) in a [McLaren 720S GT3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McLaren_720S_GT3).[^cite_note-16] Millroy and Iribe took seven Pro-Am podiums to end the year fifth in class.[^cite_note-17] The pair began 2021 by taking two podiums in the [Asian Le Mans Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Asian_Le_Mans_Series) to take fourth in the GT standings, partnering [Ben Barnicoat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Barnicoat) under the *Inception Racing* banner.[^cite_note-18] Returning to [International GT Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_International_GT_Open) for the rest of the year, Millroy and Iribe took three class wins as they secured the Pro-Am title at the finale in [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_Barcelona-Catalunya).[^cite_note-19] During 2021, Millroy also took a lone Pro-Am win in [GT World Challenge America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_GT_World_Challenge_America),[^cite_note-20] as well as making appearances in the [GT World Challenge Europe Endurance Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_GT_World_Challenge_Europe_Endurance_Cup) and the [24 Hours of Le Mans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans), the latter in a [Ferrari 488 GTE Evo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_488_GTE_Evo).[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]



Millroy's [Inception Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inception_Racing) McLaren at the [2024 24 Hours of Le Mans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans).

In 2022, Millroy continued with Inception Racing with [Optimum Motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimum_Motorsport) for a part-time campaign in the GTD class of the [IMSA SportsCar Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_IMSA_SportsCar_Championship),[^cite_note-23] in which he finished second at the [Six Hours of The Glen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Sahlen's_Six_Hours_of_The_Glen) with Iribe and [Jordan Pepper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Pepper).[^cite_note-24] He found more success elsewhere, as he sealed the GT title in the [Asian Le Mans Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Asian_Le_Mans_Series) with Iribe and Barnicoat,[^cite_note-25] as well as the Gold Cup title of the [GT World Challenge Europe Endurance Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_GT_World_Challenge_Europe_Endurance_Cup) with Iribe and [Frederik Schandorff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederik_Schandorff).[^cite_note-26] In parallel, Millroy also raced in the first five races of the [FIA World Endurance Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIA_World_Endurance_Championship) for Porsche-fielding [Team Project 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_1_Motorsport),[^cite_note-27] in which he finished third in LMGTE Am at the [1000 Miles of Sebring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_1000_Miles_of_Sebring).[^cite_note-28] In 2023, Millroy continued with Inception to race in the enduros of the [IMSA SportsCar Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_IMSA_SportsCar_Championship),[^cite_note-29] in which he finished third at the [24 Hours of Daytona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_24_Hours_of_Daytona).[^cite_note-30] Millroy also entered the [24 Hours of Spa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_24_Hours_of_Spa) and the [Gulf 12 Hours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Gulf_12_Hours), winning the latter in GT3 Pro-Am.[^cite_note-31] Across the following two seasons, Millroy assisted Inception Racing's transition to the [Ferrari 296 GT3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_296_GT3) in IMSA,[^cite_note-32] most notably taking an outright GTD win at [Indianapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_IMSA_Battle_on_the_Bricks) in 2025 with Iribe and Schandorff.[^cite_note-33] During this time period, Millroy also returned to the [24 Hours of Le Mans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans) in 2024, where Iribe set LMGT3 pole,[^cite_note-34] and raced at the [24 Hours of Spa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24_Hours_of_Spa) for Optimum in both years.[^cite_note-35]

In 2026, Millroy remained with Inception for another part-time campaign in the [IMSA SportsCar Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_IMSA_SportsCar_Championship), as well as returning to Team AAI to race in the [China GT Championship](82802381.md).[^cite_note-36]

On 5 September 2026, at the penultimate round of China GT at [Shanghai](952143.md), Millroy was involved in a crash with two cars at the turn 14 hairpin, causing his Ferrari to burst into flames.[^cite_note-37] He was rescued from the wreckage by rival [Loek Hartog](74225727.md), whom he later credited for saving his life.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39] Millroy was taken to hospital, where it was revealed he had suffered five broken ribs, a broken collarbone, hand and finger, and a bruised lung.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

## Other ventures

In addition to his motorsport career, Millroy works within the film and television industry as a [stunt performer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stunt_performer) and precision driver.[^cite_note-OfficialBio-42] Millroy's credits as a stunt driver include *[Mission: Impossible – Rogue Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission:_Impossible_%E2%80%93_Rogue_Nation)* (2015), *[Transformers: The Last Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformers:_The_Last_Knight)* (2017), *[Men in Black: International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black:_International)* (2019), and *[Mission: Impossible – Dead Reckoning Part One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission:_Impossible_%E2%80%93_Dead_Reckoning_Part_One)* (2023). He also featured in racing video game adaptation *[Gran Turismo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Turismo_\(film\))* (2023) and Apple Original Films production *[F1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F1_\(film\))* (2025), starring [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt).[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44] Beyond feature films, Millroy is regularly contracted for television commercials and promotional campaigns by automotive manufacturers, including [McLaren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McLaren_Automotive), [Mercedes-Benz](18564164.md), and [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari).[^cite_note-45]

Millroy is also a driver coach, primarily for co-driver [Brendan Iribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Iribe),[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47] as well as the co-founder and director of travel agencies Penguin London and Penguin Middle East.[^cite_note-48]
