On 25 June 2026 at 18\:04 CET, two passenger trains collided near [Białośliwie railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bia%C5%82o%C5%9Bliwie_railway_station) on the [Kutno–Piła railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kutno%E2%80%93Pi%C5%82a_railway?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Białośliwie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bia%C5%82o%C5%9Bliwie), [Piła County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi%C5%82a_County) in western [Poland](22936.md). A [Polregio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polregio) train collided into a [PKP Intercity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_Intercity) train.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] A total of around 200 passengers were evacuated, eleven of whom suffered minor injuries.[^cite_note-\:0-4]

## Collision

On 25 June 2026, at 18\:04 CET, near [Białośliwie railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bia%C5%82o%C5%9Bliwie_railway_station),[^cite_note-\:0-4] a [Polregio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polregio) service, operating from [Piła Główna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi%C5%82a_G%C5%82%C3%B3wna_railway_station) to [Bydgoszcz Główna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bydgoszcz_G%C5%82%C3%B3wna) collided [rear-end](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rear-end_collision) into the rear carriages of a [PKP Intercity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_Intercity) service, "Staszic" operating from [Białogard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bia%C5%82ogard) to [Warszawa Wschodnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warszawa_Wschodnia).[^cite_note-5] Upon impact, the Polregio train [derailed](1093599.md), which also resulted in the three rear carriages of the PKP Intercity train to derail.[^cite_note-6]

## Casualties and response

Eleven passengers of the PKP Intercity train suffered injuries, three of whom where hospitalised. None of the 20 passengers of the Polregio train were injured. Some 200 passengers were evacuated from both trains.[^cite_note-\:1-1]

Two rescue trains, and a crane, were dispatched to rerail the derailed trains. [Polskie Linie Kolejowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polskie_Linie_Kolejowe) (PLK) dismantled the [overhead wires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overhead_wires) to rerail the trains, and repaired all damaged tracks and rail infrastructure.[^cite_note-\:1-1][^cite_note-7] Both trains were rerailed at midnight on 27 June, with one of the tracks reopening to traffic on the same day. Repair works on the other track and rail infrastructure are still underway by PLK.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]
