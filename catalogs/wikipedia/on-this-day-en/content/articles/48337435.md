# Conditional release

**Conditional release** is a method of release from detention that is contingent upon obeying conditions under threat of return to detention under reduced [due process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Due_process) protections.[^cite_note-1]

When applicable in the context of post-conviction detention, unconditional release can be a synonym of [parole](342305.md).

Suspects may also receive a conditional release from investigative detention. In many jurisdictions this can be synonymous with release under investigation (RUI), in the UK, or judicial interim release in Canada.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## In a psychiatric setting

Conditional release can also be used as a less restrictive alternative to hospitalizing psychiatric patients.[^cite_note-4]
