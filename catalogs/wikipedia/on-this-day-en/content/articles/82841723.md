The **2026 [Pan Pacific Swimming Championships](2319707.md)**, a long course (50 m) event, were held at the [William Woollett Jr. Aquatics Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Woollett_Jr._Aquatics_Center) in [Irvine, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irvine,_California), from August 12 to 15, 2026.[^cite_note-1] The 2022 edition, set to be held in Canada, was skipped due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-2]

The roster for the United States was set in September 2025.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

Australian swimmers [Mollie O'Callaghan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mollie_O'Callaghan) and [Kaylee McKeown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaylee_McKeown) withdrew from the championships due to health concerns; for O'Callaghan, who was managing a spinal stress fracture, it would have been her first appearance at the senior Pan Pacs, while McKeown was forced to pull out after being diagnosed with glandular fever.[^cite_note-SwimSwamOCallaghan-15][^cite_note-NBCMcKeown-16]

[Cameron McEvoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_McEvoy) and [Kyle Chalmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyle_Chalmers) both chose not to participate in the Pan Pacific Championships, opting to focus on the [2026 Commonwealth Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Commonwealth_Games) instead.[^cite_note-17]

A report by *[Swimming World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_World)* highlighted several major omissions from the United States roster—including American record-holder [Anna Moesch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Moesch), [Isabelle Stadden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabelle_Stadden), Ryan Erisman, and Luka Mijatovic—due to USA Swimming selecting the team a year in advance based on 2025 competition times rather than holding a dedicated 2026 selection meet.[^cite_note-SwimmingWorldPanPacsRoster-18]
