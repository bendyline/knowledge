**Gaspar Prim Díaz** (28 December 2002 – 14 June 2026), better known as **Gaspi**, was an Argentine [YouTuber](57313961.md) and Internet personality.

He opened his [YouTube](3524766.md) channel in late 2013, but Prim initially gained traction a few years later on [Instagram](31591547.md), before returning to YouTube and achieving significant popularity in 2021, mainly posting street interviews incorporating dark humor and sexually suggestive jokes, while also posting himself making crude remarks to strangers.&#x20;

On 14 June 2026, Prim died in an [aviation collision](83453101.md) in [Rio De Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_De_Janeiro), Brazil, which also resulted in the deaths of five others including singer/songwriter [Oliver Tree](58040727.md).

## Life and career

Prim was born on 28 December 2002,[^cite_note-auto-1] in [Buenos Aires](255919.md). He also held Chilean dual citizenship, living in [La Serena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Serena,_Chile) for four years, and had formerly played [football](10568.md) for [Club Deportivo Universidad de Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Deportivo_Universidad_de_Chile) and [Deportes La Serena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deportes_La_Serena).[^cite_note-cnn-2][^cite_note-elpais-3]

Starting his [YouTube](3524766.md) channel in December 2013, Prim initially gained a following on [Instagram](31591547.md), though once he worked more on his YouTube he began rising to greater popularity, accumulating, as of July 2026, 3.26 million subscribers to his channel, despite having published only a handful of videos.[^cite_note-\:0-4] Between 2022 and 2024, he took a hiatus from posting content.[^cite_note-\:0-4]

Prim was known for his provocative street interviews where he asked controversial questions to passers by, with his content often featuring [dark humor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_humor) and [sexual references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_comedy). His controversial style resulted in a number of his videos being removed from online platforms.[^cite_note-\:0-4]



Prim at [La Velada del Año 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Velada_del_A%C3%B1o_5) with his team

On 26 July 2025, he participated in [La Velada del Año 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Velada_del_A%C3%B1o_5), the fifth event of the [La Velada del Año](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Velada_del_A%C3%B1o), an annual boxing competition featuring various online personalities.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] He lost by knockout in the first round against [Perxitaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perxitaa).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

Over time, Prim widened the format of his videos, incorporating [vlogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Videoblog), interviews, and direct rebroadcasting. He also started addressing more serious themes, tending to politics and social questions, using his comic style to comment upon actuality.[^cite_note-9] In May 2026, he announced the series *Gaspi visita tu hogar* (Gaspi visits your home).**[^cite_note-\:0-4]**

## Death

Main article: [2026 Rio de Janeiro mid-air collision](83453101.md)

In June 2026, Prim and fellow Argentine [Lucas A. Vignale](83454866.md) were in Brazil and took part in events linked to American singer [Oliver Tree](58040727.md)'s first world tour, which promoted his album *[Love You Madly Hate You Badly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_You_Madly_Hate_You_Badly)*. On 14 June, Prim, Tree, Vignale, and three other people were killed in an [aerial collision](83453101.md) that occurred at [Recreio dos Bandeirantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recreio_dos_Bandeirantes), in [Rio de Janeiro](25936.md). Prim was 23 years old.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] After his death, *Gaspi visita tu hogar* was cancelled.
