**Abdul Wahid Mohamed al-Nur** (also **Abdel Wahid el-Nur** or **Abdulwahid Mohammed Nour**; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): عبد الواحد محمد نور, *ʿAbd al-Wāḥid Muḥammad Nūr*; born in 1968) is the leader of the rebel [Sudan Liberation Movement (al Nur)](2031543.md#Sudan_Liberation_Movement_\(al-Nur\)) faction.[^cite_note-WhoAre-4][^cite_note-5]

A [Zaghawa](1467480.md) born in [Zalingei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zalingei), [West Darfur](767645.md), he was educated at the [University of Khartoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Khartoum), where he graduated in 1995 with a [law degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_degree) before working as a [lawyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawyer).[^cite_note-tribune-6] The SLM was founded around 2001 with a decisive split in 2006 following the [Darfur Peace Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darfur_Peace_Agreement) when al-Nur refused to sign while [Minni Minawi](5906722.md) agreed to.

## Life

Abdul Wahid Mohamed al-Nur is a lawyer, born in 1968 in [Zalingei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zalingei), [Darfur](605837.md), [Sudan](27421.md).

He was a supporter of the [Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_Communist_Party) in his youth.[^cite_note-7] And following the start of the current [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md), allied again with the Communist Party by having SLM-al Nur signing a revolutionary charter with the Party.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

He expressed officially, and widely, both in the Arab and Western media, his vision which is to establish a secular, liberal, democratic, and federal Sudan, where religion will be separated from the state.

Al-Nur cooperated with the [International Criminal Court](14880.md) and provided elements that led to the indictment of [Omar al-Bashir](393625.md) and several of his officers. He has been in contact with [Fatou Bensouda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatou_Bensouda), the ICC's chief prosecutor from 2012 to 2021. Al-Nur is in favour of empowering the ICC.
