The **Government of Sindh** ([Sindhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhi_language): حڪومت سنڌ) ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): حکومتِ سندھ) is the [provincial government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_government) of the [province](446017.md) of [Sindh](28972.md), [Pakistan](23235.md). Its powers and structure are set out in the provisions of the [1973 Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Pakistan), in which [30 Districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Sindh) of [7 Divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisions_of_Sindh) under its authority and jurisdiction.&#x20;

The province's executive head and head of the government is the [Chief Secretary Sindh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Secretary_Sindh). All officers and cabinet members report to the Chief Secretary. The Chief Secretary is an appointed official by the Prime Minister of Pakistan. The [Chief Secretary of Sindh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Secretary_of_Sindh) is usually a [Grade 22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grade_22) officer, belonging to the [Pakistan Administrative Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Administrative_Service).&#x20;

Although the Governor is the head of the province on paper, it is largely a ceremonial position; and the main powers lie with the [Chief Secretary of Sindh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Secretary_of_Sindh) and [Chief Minister of Sindh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_Sindh).

The province is governed by a [unicameral legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicameral_legislature) with the [head of government](37570.md) known as the [Chief Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_Sindh). The Chief Minister, invariably a leader of a political party represented in the Assembly, selects members of the provincial Cabinet.&#x20;

The terms *Government of Sindh* or *Sindh Government* are often used in official documents. The [seat of Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seat_of_government) is in [Karachi](17123.md), thus serving as the [capital](181337.md) of the province.

## Executive

The [Chief Secretary Sindh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Secretary_Sindh), as head of the provincial bureaucracy, is the boss of the province. The provincial Chief Secretary of [Grade-22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grade_22) is equivalent in rank to a Federal Secretary, is appointed by the [Prime Minister of Pakistan](461896.md). Under him comes the entire Government of Sindh.

## Governance

At the Ministerial level, each department is headed by a Provincial Secretary. The Secretary is in-charge of all workings of their department, and in most cases are the Principal Accounting Officers of their department. Provincial Secretaries are usually Grade-20 Officers. There may also be Grade-21 Secretaries, in cases where the post has not been upgraded by the Chief Minister to that of an Additional Chief Secretary.&#x20;

Apart from the Ministerial level, the Government of Sindh exercises its powers and function through Divisions and Districts. The Commissioners are in-charge of Divisions while Deputy Commissioners are in-charge of Districts. Commissioners report directly to the Chief Secretary are responsibly for everything in their Divisions. There are 6 Divisions, namely; Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana, Mirpurkhas, and Shaheed Benazirbad. Commissioners are usually Officers of the rank of Grade-20 while the Commissioner Karachi Division is of the Grade-21 rank, as Karachi Division is the most important Division in all of Pakistan.&#x20;
