[**Changning County**](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/b/be/Chang2ning2.ogg/Chang2ning2.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 长宁县; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 長寧縣; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Chángníng Xiàn*; lit. 'long peace') is a county of [Sichuan Province](65185.md), China. It is under the administration of [Yibin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yibin) City.

## Administrative divisions

Changning County administers 13 towns:[^cite_note-1]

- Changning 长宁镇

- Meidong 梅硐镇

- Shuanghe 双河镇

- Dongdi 硐底镇

- Huatan 花滩镇

- Zhuhai 竹海镇

- Laoweng 老翁镇

- Guhe 古河镇

- [Longtou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longtou,_Sichuan) 龙头镇

- Tonggu 铜鼓镇

- Jingjiang 井江镇

- Tongluo 铜锣镇

- Meibai 梅白镇
