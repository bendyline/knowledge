The **Eastern Naval Command** is one of the three command-level formations of the [Indian Navy](604522.md).[^cite_note-1] It is headquartered in [Visakhapatnam](238760.md), [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md).[^cite_note-2] The command is responsible for the all naval forces in the [Bay of Bengal](47892.md) and parts of the [Indian Ocean](14580.md) and the naval establishments on the east coast of India.

The Command was established in 1983.[^cite_note-3] The Command is commanded by a [Three Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-star_rank) Flag Officer of the rank of Vice Admiral with the title Flag Officer Commanding-in-Chief Eastern Command (FOC-in-C). [Vice Admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_Admiral) [Sanjay Bhalla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjay_Bhalla) is the current FOC-in-C ENC, who took over on 31 October 2025.

## History

After the [independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_of_India) and the [partition of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_India) on 15 August 1947, the ships and personnel of the [Royal Indian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Indian_Navy) were divided between the [Dominion of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_India) and the [Dominion of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_Pakistan). The division of the ships was on the basis of two-thirds of the fleet to India, one third to Pakistan.[^cite_note-4]

The Surface Fleet of the Navy was called Indian Fleet and was commanded by the Flag Officer Commanding Indian Fleet (FOCIF). Initially, the shore establishments on the eastern coast were headed by a captain in the appointment of Naval Officer-in-Charge, Vishakapatnam (NOIC).[^cite_note-Ind_Navy_Appts-5] This was subsequently upgraded to the appointment of Commodore East (COMEAST), a [One Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-star_rank) appointment. The FOCIF and COMEAST reported into the [Chief of the Naval Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_the_Naval_Staff_\(India\)). In July 1967, COMEAST was upgraded to the [two-star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-star_rank) appointment of Flag Officer, East Coast (FOEC).[^cite_note-Flag_East-6] On 1 March 1968, the Eastern Fleet was also created. With this, the appointment of FOEC was re-designated Flag Officer Commanding-in-Chief Eastern Naval Command (FOC-in-C ENC). The Indian Fleet was split between the [Western Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Fleet_\(India\)) and the [Eastern Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Fleet_\(India\)) with the Fleet Officer Commanding Eastern Fleet (FOCEF) reporting into the FOC-in-C ENC.[^cite_note-7] On 1 March 1971, [Vice Admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_admiral_\(India\)) [Nilakanta Krishnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilakanta_Krishnan) took over as FOC-in-C, the first three-star officer to lead the command.[^cite_note-8]

The oldest naval establishment of the Eastern Seaboard was the [INS Circars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Circars), [Visakhapatnam](238760.md), which was identified as "an important convoy assembly point" for the [Royal Indian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Indian_Navy) flotillas. A small Naval Base was established there on 12 December 1939 and the base was designated as HMIS Circars on 12 April 1942.[^cite_note-9] Now, the base is the base depot ship of the Eastern Naval Command. It is the logistics and administrative support establishment of the Command, supporting all ships and units based in Visakhapatnam. As of 2018, the establishment supports 52 additional units with strength of 2,300 sailors and 400 officers, besides accommodating live-in sailors of Circars.[^cite_note-10]

## Area of responsibility

Under the Flag Officer Commanding-in-Chief Eastern Naval Command (FOC-in-C East) is the [Flag Officer Commanding Eastern Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_Officer_Commanding_Eastern_Fleet) (FOCEF), [Commodore Commanding Submarines (East)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commodore_Commanding_Submarines_\(East\)) (COMCOS (E)), Admiral Superintendent Dockyard for [Visakhapatnam](238760.md), shore establishments, and five Naval Officers-in-Charge (NOICs).

The Eastern naval Command has the states of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md), [Odisha](250724.md) and [Tamil Nadu](29918.md) etc., under its area of responsibility.[^cite_note-inenca-11]

The FOC-in-C (East) is the submarine operating authority, under whom COMCOS (E) operates. The 11th ([*Sindhughosh* class submarine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhughosh_class_submarine)) and 8th ([Foxtrot class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxtrot_class_submarine)) Submarine Squadrons operate under COMCOS (E). [INS *Virbahu*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Virbahu), a submarine base commissioned on 19 May 1971, is the alma mater of the Indian Navy submariners.

## Naval bases



Admiral DK Joshi, then Chief of the Naval Staff, receiving a guard of honour during a visit to the Eastern Naval Command in November 2013

The headquarters in [Visakhapatnam](238760.md), is also a strategically important dockyard for two nuclear-powered submarines of the [Arihant-class submarine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arihant-class_submarine). Due to congestion and heavy shipping traffic, a new 20 square km base [INS Varsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Varsha) is being developed for exclusive naval use about 50 km south of Visakhapatnam.

The Eastern Navy fleet is distributed among its bases at [Paradip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradip), [Tuticorin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuticorin), [Kakinada](1684096.md) and [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai) on the east coast, and in the [Andaman and Nicobar Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaman_and_Nicobar_Islands).[^cite_note-inenca-11] The Navy has opened its latest naval air base, [INS Baaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Baaz), at the southernmost tip of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands to secure the strategically important [Straits of Malacca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straits_of_Malacca), and another naval air station in Kolkata to base an unmanned aerial vehicle squadron.[^cite_note-niex-20][^cite_note-\:0-21]

## Capabilities

In 2005, Eastern Naval Command was home to 30 warships. Since then it has grown to include [INS *Jalashwa*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Jalashwa) is the flagship of Eastern Fleet and provides amphibious capabilities to the [Indian Navy](604522.md) in the [Bay of Bengal](47892.md). Eastern Fleet is equipped with submarine pens and maintenance dockyards. The Amphibious Task Group of Eastern Naval Fleet has INS *Jalashwa* (LPD), [INS Vikrant (Aircraft carrier)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Vikrant_\(2013\)) It's CBG includes the [Delhi-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi-class_destroyer) & [*Rajput*-class destroyers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajput-class_destroyer), [Nilgiri-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilgiri-class_frigate_\(2019\)) & [*Shivalik*-class frigates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivalik-class_frigate), [Arnala-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Submarine_Warfare_Shallow_Water_Craft), 2 [Kora-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kora-class_corvette), [Kamorta-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamorta-class_corvette) & [Khukri-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khukri-class_corvette) corvettes, [*Sindhughosh*-class submarines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhughosh-class_submarine), [Airhant-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arihant-class_submarine) (SSBNs) and the [*Akula*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akula-class_submarine) [submarine](28825.md) [INS *Chakra*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Chakra_\(2011\)). Naval aviation is provided by [Westland Sea King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westland_Sea_King), [MH-60R](202031.md), [Kamov-31](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamov_Ka-31) & [Kamov-27](1067665.md) helicopters. For Fixed-wing aircraft it's [P-8I](731169.md) and [Mig-29K](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikoyan_MiG-29K) naval fighters. Apart from these, a number of smaller vessels such as Patrol Vessels, [fast attack craft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_attack_craft), support vessels and other auxiliary ships.&#x20;

## Future

Until 1997, [INS *Vikrant* (1961)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Vikrant_\(1961\)) was the flagship of Eastern Fleet. After her de-commissioning in 1997, the Eastern Fleet has been without an [aircraft carrier](2219.md).[^cite_note-niex-20][^cite_note-24] Its aircraft carrier capability will get restored after first indigenously built aircraft carrier [INS *Vikrant* (2013)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Vikrant_\(2013\)) joins the Eastern Fleet by 2023 after completing extensive sea trials and is likely to based in [Vishakapatnam](238760.md).[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]
