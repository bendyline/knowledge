**Ori Ire** is a [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Oyo State](2227658.md), [Nigeria](21383.md). Its headquarters are in the town of Ikoyi-Ile.

It has an area of 2,116 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 150,628 at the 2006 census.

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 210.[^cite_note-1]

## Climate

The average temperature in Ikoyi-Ile is high, with the hottest month being March, with a high of 33 °C (91 °F).[^cite_note-2]
