**Urubamba** is one of thirteen [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Peru) in the [Cusco Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cusco_Region) in the southern highlands of [Peru](170691.md).

## Geography

The province is bounded to the north by the [province of La Convención](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Convenci%C3%B3n_Province), to the east by the [province of Calca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calca_Province), to the south by the [province of Cusco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cusco_Province) and the [province of Anta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anta_Province), and to the west by the [province of La Convención](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Convenci%C3%B3n_Province).

## Ethnic groups

The people in the province are mainly [indigenous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_of_the_Americas) citizens of [Quechua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechua_people) descent. [Quechua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechua_language) is the language which the majority of the population (51.34%) learnt to speak in childhood, 46.78% of the residents started speaking using the [Spanish](26825.md) language ([2007 Peru Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Peru_Census)).[^cite_note-2]
