**Robotniczy Klub Sportowy Raków Częstochowa [Spółka Akcyjna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sp%C3%B3%C5%82ka_Akcyjna)** (commonly referred to as **Raków Częstochowa**, or simply **Raków**) is a Polish professional [football](10568.md) club, based in [Częstochowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cz%C4%99stochowa), that competes in the [Ekstraklasa](30871677.md), the top tier of [national football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_football_league_system).

## History



Poster advertising a friendly match between Raków Częstochowa and KKS Częstochowa, 1946

Sports club "Racovia" was established in 1921 in the village of [Raków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rak%C3%B3w,_Cz%C4%99stochowa). The club dissolved in 1925 due to lack of registration. In 1927, the club was reactivated under the name of the Robotniczy Klub Sportowy (Workers Sports Club) Raków. A year later, the village became a district of Częstochowa.[^cite_note-1] The club operated under the patronage of the [Polish Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Socialist_Party) and was financially supported by the Częstochowa steelworks. In 1937, the club was promoted to [Klasa A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klasa_A) (third level). During the [German occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Poland_\(1939%E2%80%931945\)) ([World War II](32927.md)), the club did not function. In the years 1951–1955, a [Raków Municipal Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rak%C3%B3w_Municipal_Stadium) with an athletics track was built. In the years 1962–1966, the football team competed in the [second league](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_liga).&#x20;

On 9 July 1967, Raków lost 0–2 to [Wisła Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wis%C5%82a_Krak%C3%B3w) in their first [Polish Cup](772047.md) final appearance. In 1972, Raków's players reached the semi-finals of the Polish Cup, which they lost to [Legia Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legia_Warsaw). In the years 1978–1980, 1981–1984 and 1990–1994 the club played in the second league. In 1993, the junior team took 2nd place in the [Football Junior Championships of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Junior_Championships_of_Poland).[^cite_note-2] In 1994, for the first time in club history, Raków were promoted to [Ekstraklasa](30871677.md). They competed in the top-flight for four seasons until being relegated after the [1997–98 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997%E2%80%9398_Ekstraklasa). The club suffered back-to-back relegations in 1999–2000 and 2000–01, dropping down to the [IV liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/III_liga). The club finally made it to back to the Polish second division, [I liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_liga), in 2016.

### Promotion and European football

The club won the I liga in [2018–19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_I_liga), earning promotion to Ekstraklasa for the first time in 21 years.[^cite_note-3] In the same season, they impressed in the [2018–19 Polish Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_Polish_Cup), reaching the semi-finals of the competition by beating the likes of [Lech Poznań](677622.md) in the round of 32[^cite_note-4] and [Legia Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legia_Warsaw) in the quarter-finals.[^cite_note-5] They finished the [2019–20 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Ekstraklasa) in 10th place.



Match between Raków and [Lechia Gdańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lechia_Gda%C5%84sk)

In the [2020–21 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Ekstraklasa), Raków finished the league season as runners-up. This was their highest ever league position in their history, and it secured their place in the newly formed [UEFA Europa Conference League qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_UEFA_Europa_Conference_League_qualifying_phase_and_play-off_round) for the [2021–22 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_UEFA_Europa_Conference_League), their maiden appearance in [European football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_competitions).[^cite_note-polishcup-6][^cite_note-7] Further success followed when Raków won the [2020–21 Polish Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Polish_Cup), their first major trophy, defeating I liga side [Arka Gdynia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arka_Gdynia) in the final 2–1 on 2 May 2021. On 17 July 2021, Raków Częstochowa defeated reigning Ekstraklasa title holders [Legia Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legia_Warsaw) on penalties to win the [2021 Polish Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Polish_Super_Cup).[^cite_note-8]

Their first ever appearance in European football was in the second qualifying round of the [2021–22 UEFA Europa Conference League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_UEFA_Europa_Conference_League), facing Lithuanian team [Sūduva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FK_S%C5%ABduva), with the game finishing 0–0 (4–3 pens) after both legs.[^cite_note-9] They faced [Russian Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Premier_League) side [Rubin Kazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Rubin_Kazan) in the third qualifying round, beating them 1–0 on aggregate score.[^cite_note-10] In the final qualifying round, they faced Belgian side [Gent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAA_Gent), which they beat 1–0 in the first leg,[^cite_note-11] but lost 0–3 in the second leg, losing 1–3 on aggregate,[^cite_note-12] eliminating them from the competition.

On 2 May 2022, Raków defeated [Lech Poznań](677622.md) 3–1 and secured its second consecutive [Polish Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_Polish_Cup).[^cite_note-PucharPolski2022-13] In the race for the league title that [season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_Ekstraklasa), Raków lost out to the same opponents by just five points, with the champions being decided on the penultimate day of the season. On 9 July 2022, Raków won their second consecutive Polish Super Cup with a 2–0 win over Lech Poznań.[^cite_note-14] In the [UEFA Europa Conference League qualifying phase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_UEFA_Europa_Conference_League_qualifying_phase_and_play-off_round), the team defeated [Astana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Astana) (6–0 agg.) and [Spartak Trnava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Spartak_Trnava) (3–0 agg.), but lost to [Slavia Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SK_Slavia_Prague) (2–3 agg.) in the play-off round.



Raków Częstochowa players before match vs [Aris Limassol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aris_Limassol_FC) in 2023

On 7 May 2023, Raków won the [Ekstraklasa](30871677.md) championship for the first time in their history,[^cite_note-15] and thus qualified for the [Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_UEFA_Champions_League), where they reached the qualifying play-off round, losing to [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Copenhagen) from Denmark. Raków subsequently played in the [Europa League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_UEFA_Europa_League) that season, where they were knocked out in the group stages.

In the [2023–24 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Rak%C3%B3w_Cz%C4%99stochowa_season), Raków failed to defend their league title, finishing in 7th, as well as being knocked out of the Polish Cup quarter-finals. The [following season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Rak%C3%B3w_Cz%C4%99stochowa_season), however, would see them return to form, resulting in another 2nd place finish, their third top-two finish in five years, finishing a point behind champions Lech Poznań once again.&#x20;

In 2025, Raków made their second European appearance in the [2025–26 UEFA Conference League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_UEFA_Conference_League), defeating [Zilina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C5%A0K_%C5%BDilina), [Maccabi Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maccabi_Haifa_F.C.) and [Arda Kardzhali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Arda_Kardzhali) in the qualifying rounds to reach the league phase. Subsequently, they achieved a impressive 2nd place finish, qualifying them directly for the knockout rounds of a European competition for the first time ever. They would bow out to [Fiorentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACF_Fiorentina) in the round of 16. Domestically, they reached another [Polish Cup final](79976641.md), where they would lose to [Górnik Zabrze](677580.md), and finished 4th in the [Ekstraklasa](79467685.md).
