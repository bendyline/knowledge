The **Cook Islands United Party** is a political party in the [Cook Islands](7067.md). The party was founded in October 2018 by former Cabinet ministers [Nandi Glassie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nandi_Glassie) and [Teariki Heather](25567244.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

Heather contested the [2019 Ivirua by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Ivirua_by-election), but was unsuccessful.[^cite_note-3] The party did not contest the [March 2019 Tengatangi-Areora-Ngatiarua by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_2019_Tengatangi-Areora-Ngatiarua_by-election), with Glassie instead standing as a [Democratic party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Cook_Islands\)) candidate.[^cite_note-4]

In December 2021 the party announced eleven candidates for the [2022 Cook Islands general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Cook_Islands_general_election).[^cite_note-5] The party platform included a two-term limit for MPs and the introduction of import levies to promote local business.[^cite_note-6] Initial results showed the party winning 4 seats, with a 5th seat tied.[^cite_note-7] The final results gave the party three seats.[^cite_note-8]

The party won four seats in the [2026 election](82397368.md).[^cite_note-9] Stating that he wanted to focus on rebuilding the party for the 2030 election, party leader Teariki Heather nominated his younger brother and MP for [Akaoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akaoa), Robert Stanley Heather, as leader of the official [Opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_opposition).[^cite_note-10] In addition, [Taneao Ngamata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taneao_Ngamata?action=edit\&redlink=1) was named as the deputy leader and [Tim Varu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Varu?action=edit\&redlink=1) as the party [whip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whip_\(politics\)).[^cite_note-11]
