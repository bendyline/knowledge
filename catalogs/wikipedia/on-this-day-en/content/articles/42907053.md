**Shebaa** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شبعا, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Shibʿā*) is a municipality on the south-eastern tip of [Lebanon](17771.md). It has a largely [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni) Muslim population of 25,000 people. It is situated at an altitude of approximately 1,700 m (5,600 ft) above sea level, spreading across two steep rocky mountainsides.[^cite_note-1] It lies adjacent to the Lebanese claimed but Israeli occupied [Shebaa farms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shebaa_farms)—which sit between the town and the [Golan Heights](7515865.md).[^cite_note-2] Before 1967, residents of Shebaa farmed in the disputed Shebaa farms territory.[^cite_note-3]

## History



Chebaa, in the 1880s

[^cite_note-4]

In 1838, [Eli Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Smith) noted Shebaa's population as being [Sunni Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Muslim) and [Greek Orthodox Christians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_Orthodox_Patriarchate_of_Antioch).[^cite_note-5]

Following the [1982 Lebanon War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_Lebanon_War), Shebaa became part of [Israel’s security zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_occupation_of_Southern_Lebanon_\(1982%E2%80%932000\)) with [Norwegian soldiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Army) from [UNIFIL](787766.md) stationed in the area. On one night in late January 1989, the Israeli backed [SLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Lebanon_Army) expelled 40 villagers, ordering them not to return. This brought the total over a period of several months to around 80. In the words of the UNIFIL spokesperson those expelled were “mostly women and children”.[^cite_note-6] Israel withdrew from South Lebanon, including the town of Shebaa, in 2000.

As of 2025, the town housed 4,500 [Syrian refugees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_refugees).[^cite_note-7]

## Demographics

In 2014, [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 96.96% and [Christians](4796145.md) made up 2.89% of registered voters in Shebaa. 92.99% of the voters were [Sunni Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Sunni_Muslims). The Christian population is mostly [Greek Orthodox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Greek_Orthodox_Christians).[^cite_note-8]
