**Baboua** is a town and an administrative area ([sub-prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub-prefectures_of_the_Central_African_Republic)) located within the [prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_of_the_Central_African_Republic) of [Nana-Mambéré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nana-Mamb%C3%A9r%C3%A9) in the [Central African Republic](5478.md) (CAR), approximately 550 kilometres (340 mi) north-west of [Bangui](56617.md), the nation's capital. The town is located on the CAR's border with [Cameroon](5447.md), and is across the border from the Cameroonian town of [Garoua-Boulaï](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garoua-Boula%C3%AF). Baboua is home to the Evangelical Lutheran Seminary and Lutheran Bible School. Tongo Lutheran Church is also located in Baboua.[^cite_note-2]

## History

Since around 2013 Baboua area had been under control of [FDPC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Front_of_the_Central_African_People) armed group.[^cite_note-3] On 16 July 2015 multiple people including major, sub-prefect and priest were kidnapped by FDPC in Baboua. They were released in July 2016.[^cite_note-4] In May 2017 it was reported that Baboua was under control of security forces.[^cite_note-5]

On 1 May 2020 [3R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return,_Reclamation,_Rehabilitation) rebel group took control of Baboua.[^cite_note-6] They withdrew on 5 May.[^cite_note-7] On 21 December 2020 Baboua was captured by rebels from [Coalition of Patriots for Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalition_of_Patriots_for_Change).[^cite_note-8] It was recaptured by government forces on 10 February 2021.[^cite_note-9]

## Schools

In Baboua there are at least 4 levels of schooling: preschool, elementary, bible school, and seminary. Over half of the children in the town are not enrolled in school.[^cite_note-autogenerated1-10]

All schools in Baboua are affiliated with the Evangelical Lutheran Church. Both Christian and Muslim students attend the schools.&#x20;

At the Lutheran Bible School, students can study the Bible, human rights, agriculture, and health.[^cite_note-11]

## Festivals

There is an annual parade in Baboua to celebrate CAR's independence from France.[^cite_note-autogenerated1-10]
