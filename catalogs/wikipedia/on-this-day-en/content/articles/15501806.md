***Current Biology*** is a biweekly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that covers all areas of [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology), especially [molecular biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_biology), [cell biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_biology), [genetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetics), [neurobiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neurobiology), [ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology), and [evolutionary biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolutionary_biology). The journal includes research articles, various types of [review articles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_article), as well as an editorial magazine section.&#x20;

The journal was established in 1991[^cite_note-1] by the Current Science group, was acquired by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) in 1998, and has since 2001 been part of [Cell Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_Press), a subdivision of Elsevier.[^cite_note-2] According to *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2020 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 10.834.[^cite_note-WoS-3][^cite_note-4] It was categorized as a "high impact journal" by the [Superfund Research Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superfund_Research_Program).[^cite_note-5]
