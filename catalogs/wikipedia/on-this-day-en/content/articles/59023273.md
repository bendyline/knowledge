**Eastern Indonesia** (or **East Indonesia**; [Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Indonesia Timur*, pronounced [\[ɪndoˌnes(i)ja ˈt̪imʊr\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Malay))[^cite_note-2] is one of the two main geographical [regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Indonesia) of [Indonesia](14579.md), the other being [Western Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Indonesia).[^cite_note-kemenhub-3] It comprises four geographical units: [Lesser Sunda Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesser_Sunda_Islands), [Sulawesi](28740.md), [Maluku Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maluku_Islands) and [Papua](1432822.md). [Central Indonesian Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Indonesia) and [Eastern Indonesia Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Indonesia) are the national [standard time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_time) designated for Eastern Indonesia; it falls within the [UTC+8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+08\:00) and [UTC+9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+09\:00) [time zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_zone), respectively.

Eastern Indonesia borders the [Southern Philippines](20546.md) and [Palau](23372.md) in the North, [Papua New Guinea](22943.md) in the East, and [Northern Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Australia) in the South.

## History and background

During the last stages of the Dutch colonial era, the area east of Java and Kalimantan was known as the [Great East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_East) and later known as **Eastern Indonesia**. After [Denpasar Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denpasar_Conference), on 24 December 1946, the [State of East Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_East_Indonesia) was formed, covering the same area, excluding [Western New Guinea](1432822.md), previously included during [Malino Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malino_Conference). It was a component of the [United States of Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_Indonesia), and was dissolved into the unitary Republic of Indonesia in 17 August 1950 .[^cite_note-FOOTNOTERicklefs2008362,_374-4] Currently, Eastern Indonesia consists of 17 provinces: [Bali](4147.md), [East Nusa Tenggara](453728.md), [West Nusa Tenggara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Nusa_Tenggara), [Central Sulawesi](355454.md), [Gorontalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorontalo), [North Sulawesi](453688.md), [South Sulawesi](389458.md), [Southeast Sulawesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeast_Sulawesi), [West Sulawesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Sulawesi), [Maluku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maluku_\(province\)), [North Maluku](453682.md), [Central Papua](71191665.md), [Highland Papua](71191966.md), [Papua](15199.md), [South Papua](59803872.md), [Southwest Papua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Papua), and [West Papua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Papua_\(province\)).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

### Climate

In Eastern Indonesia, the days are generally dry and sunny from October through March with the warm tropical rain season occurring between May and August; temperatures are typically in the 27 °C (81 °F) to 30 °C (86 °F) range throughout the year.[^cite_note-JakartaPost-8]

## Economy

| \[icon] | This section **needs expansion**. You can help by [adding missing information](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Eastern_Indonesia\&action=edit\&section=). *(November 2023)* |
| --- | --- |



Most people in eastern Indonesia work as fisherman and most consume fish, except in [Bali](4147.md) and [East Nusa Tenggara](453728.md) where most people work as farmers.

[Seaweed farming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seaweed_farming) has traditionally been a common commercial activity along the coasts of Eastern Indonesia; however, in the 2020s [climate change in Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change_in_Indonesia) has been causing seaweed farmers in Eastern Indonesia to lose revenue and harvests.[^cite_note-10] In the consumer shopping industry, Eastern Indonesia experienced a rapid increase in online shopping in the 2020s, with overall transactions in the region doubling from 2020 to 2021; this growth has been led by Indonesian [e-commerce](9611.md) company [Tokopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokopedia), with the top product types sold in the region being health and beauty, fashion, food and beverage, and electronics.[^cite_note-TJP-11]
