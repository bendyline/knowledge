**Rodrigo Hernández Cascante** (born 22 June 1996), known simply as **Rodri** or **Rodrigo**, is a Spanish professional [footballer](10568.md) who plays as a [defensive midfielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensive_midfielder) for [La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga) club [Barcelona](68187.md) and [captains](633462.md) the [Spain national team](609825.md). Widely regarded as one of the best midfielders in the world, he is known for his passing, composure, playmaking and physical attributes.[^cite_note-10] He is one of eleven players to have won the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md), [UEFA Champions League](44220.md), and [Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d'Or) in their career, and the only one to win the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md), [FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Club_World_Cup), [UEFA European Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_European_Championship), [UEFA Nations League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Nations_League), [UEFA Champions League](44220.md) and the [Best Player Award](235000.md#Association_football) of each tournament.

Rodri began his senior career with [Villarreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarreal_CF), progressing through the club's [academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarreal_CF_\(youth\)) before making his first-team debut in December [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_Villarreal_CF_season). In 2018, he joined [Atlético Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid), where he established himself as a promising midfielder and won the [UEFA Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Super_Cup) in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_UEFA_Super_Cup).[^cite_note-11] His performances earned him interest from Manchester City manager [Pep Guardiola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pep_Guardiola). After a single season at Atlético, he signed for City in 2019 for £62.6 million, a then-record fee for a Spanish player.&#x20;

At City, Rodri developed into a centrepiece of Guardiola's team. He played a decisive role in the club's domestic dominance, winning four consecutive Premier League titles and completing the [continental treble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_treble) in [2022–23](70467955.md). Rodri scored the only goal in the [2023 UEFA Champions League final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_UEFA_Champions_League_final) against [Inter Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter_Milan), as City won their first [Champions League](44220.md) title; he was awarded the [UEFA Champions League Player of the Season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League_Player_of_the_Season). He also scored in the [2023 FIFA Club World Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_FIFA_Club_World_Cup_final) as City claimed the title for the first time. During the [2023–24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Manchester_City_F.C._season) season, he helped the club win an unprecedented [fourth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Premier_League) consecutive Premier League title.

Rodri is a Spain international and former youth international. He made his debut for the senior national team in 2018 and represented his country at two [UEFA European Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_European_Championship) (in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2020) and [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2024)) and two [FIFA World Cups](11370.md) (in [2022](17742072.md) and [2026](36581929.md)). He won the [UEFA Nations League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_UEFA_Nations_League) in 2023, being named the best player of the [finals tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_UEFA_Nations_League_Finals), and was an integral part in Spain's victory at Euro 2024, for which he was named [Player of the Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_European_Championship_awards#Player_of_the_Tournament). Combining his international success with City's achievements, Rodri won the [2024 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Ballon_d'Or), becoming the first defensive midfielder since [Lothar Matthäus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lothar_Matth%C3%A4us) in [1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Ballon_d'Or) to receive the award, as well as the second Spanish player[^cite_note-BBCBallon2024-12] and the first City player to do so.[^cite_note-13] At the 2026 World Cup, he won the [Golden Ball](3482503.md#Golden_Ball) as Spain claimed their second title.

## Club career

### Early life and career

Rodrigo Hernández Cascante[^cite_note-14] was born on 22 June 1996[^cite_note-15] in [Madrid](41188263.md).[^cite_note-16] He joined [Atlético Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid)'s youth setup in 2007 aged 11, from [CF Rayo Majadahonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CF_Rayo_Majadahonda).[^cite_note-17] Released in 2013 due to a "lack of physical strength", he subsequently signed with [Villarreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarreal_CF).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

On 7 February 2015, while still a junior, Rodri made his senior debut for [the reserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarreal_CF_B), coming on as a late [substitute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitute_\(association_football\)) in a 3–1 away win against [RCD Espanyol B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCD_Espanyol_B) in the [Segunda División B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segunda_Divisi%C3%B3n_B).[^cite_note-20] He was handed his first start 15 days later, in a 2–0 victory at [Real Zaragoza B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Zaragoza_B).[^cite_note-21]

Rodri made his [first-team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_team_\(association_football\)) debut on 17 December 2015, starting in a 2–0 home win against [SD Huesca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SD_Huesca) for [that season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_Copa_del_Rey)'s [Copa del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_del_Rey).[^cite_note-22] His first [La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga) appearance was on 17 April 2016, when he came on as a second-half substitute for [Denis Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Su%C3%A1rez) in a 2–1 away loss to [Rayo Vallecano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayo_Vallecano).[^cite_note-23]

On 4 December 2017, having already established himself as a regular, Rodri renewed his contract until 2022.[^cite_note-24] He scored his first goal in the [Spanish top level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_football_league_system) on 18 February 2018, the opener in a 1–1 away draw against [Espanyol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCD_Espanyol).[^cite_note-25]

### Atlético Madrid

On 24 May 2018, Rodri returned to Atlético Madrid after the club reached an agreement with Villarreal for his transfer. He signed a five-year contract with the club,[^cite_note-26] for a fee in the region of €20 million, plus €5 million in variables.[^cite_note-27] He made his debut on 15 August in the [2018 UEFA Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_UEFA_Super_Cup) in [Tallinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallinn), playing the first 71 minutes of a 4–2 extra-time win over [city rivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid_derby) [Real Madrid](26413.md).[^cite_note-super-28] He scored his first goal for the club on 10 November 2018, netting in a 3–2 win against [Athletic Bilbao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletic_Bilbao).[^cite_note-29]

### Manchester City

#### 2019–20 season: First season in England

On 3 July 2019, [Manchester City](165813.md) met the terms of Rodri's £62.6 million release clause, enabling him to buy out the remainder of his contract with Atlético and leave the club. The transfer was a new record fee paid by Manchester City.[^cite_note-\:6-30][^cite_note-31] He signed a five-year contract.[^cite_note-32]

Rodri, aged 23, made his debut in the [2019 FA Community Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_FA_Community_Shield) on 4 August at [Wembley Stadium](8913012.md), playing the full 90 minutes as City won on penalties against [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.) after a 1–1 draw.[^cite_note-shield-33] He made his [Premier League](11250.md) debut against [West Ham United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham_United_F.C.) six days later in a 5–0 away win,[^cite_note-34] and on 14 September he scored his first goal in a 3–2 away defeat against [Norwich City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich_City_F.C.).[^cite_note-35]

It was announced in October 2019 that Rodri would be out for a month due to a hamstring injury.[^cite_note-36] On 1 March 2020, City won the [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) at Wembley Stadium for the third consecutive time, beating [Aston Villa](42173.md) 2–1 in the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_EFL_Cup_final). Rodri headed in City's second goal from a corner, which ultimately turned out to be the winner.[^cite_note-McNulty2020-37]

#### 2020–2024: Premier League success and continental treble



Rodri playing for [Manchester City](165813.md) in 2023

On 13 February 2021, Rodri scored City's first goal from a penalty in a 3–0 home win over [Tottenham Hotspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.).[^cite_note-38] During the [2021–22 domestic campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_Premier_League), Rodri had a pass completion count of 82.0 per 90 minutes and a pass completion rate of 91.8% over the course of the whole season. These numbers put Rodri at the top of all midfielders in the Premier League in both categories. Multiplying those two numbers together reveals that on average, Rodri attempted one pass every 60.5 seconds of the season, leading all Premier League midfielders in that category too, with only Liverpool's [Thiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Alc%C3%A2ntara) coming close.[^cite_note-39]

On 12 July 2022, Rodri agreed to extend his contract with Manchester City until 2027.[^cite_note-40] On 11 April 2023, he scored his first [UEFA Champions League](44220.md) goal in a 3–0 home win over [Bayern Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Bayern_Munich) in the quarter-finals first leg.[^cite_note-41] On 10 June, he scored the only goal in the [Champions League final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_UEFA_Champions_League_final) against [Inter Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter_Milan), securing City's win for their first title in the competition, and completing the [continental treble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_treble).[^cite_note-McNulty2023-42] Rodri was named [player of the match](906698.md),[^cite_note-43] and was selected as [the tournament's Player of the Season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League_Player_of_the_Season).[^cite_note-POTS-44][^cite_note-\:1-45]

On 23 September 2023, Rodri was sent off in a Premier League match against [Nottingham Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_Forest_F.C.) for lashing out at [Morgan Gibbs-White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_Gibbs-White) following a tussle, which sparked a melee. This was Rodri's first red card in the Premier League.[^cite_note-46] City went on to lose each of the three subsequent games (against [Wolverhampton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverhampton_Wanderers_F.C.), [Arsenal](2174.md), and [Newcastle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United_F.C.)) for which he was suspended.[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49] After his fifth [yellow card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_card_\(football\)) against Tottenham, Rodri was suspended as City went on to lose the game again – that time against Aston Villa.[^cite_note-50]

On 19 May 2024, Rodri would score a decisive goal into the bottom corner to ensure Manchester City's 3–1 win over West Ham United on the last match day of the season; City's victory saw them become the first club in English history to lift the league trophy in [four consecutive seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_English_football_champions).[^cite_note-51] Additionally, he reached his 50th consecutive undefeated Premier League match with his club, placing him second only to [Sol Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sol_Campbell) with 56 games.[^cite_note-52]

#### 2024–2026: Injury, return and departure

On 22 September 2024, Rodri was withdrawn in the first half of Manchester City's 2–2 draw with Arsenal, due an [ACL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anterior_cruciate_ligament_injury) and [meniscus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meniscus_tear) injury, ruling him out for the majority of the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Manchester_City_F.C._season).[^cite_note-53] In October 2024, he was awarded the [2024 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Ballon_d'Or).[^cite_note-54] On 20 May 2025, he made his comeback from the long-term injury, coming on as a late substitute in a 3–1 win against [Bournemouth](451121.md) in the penultimate match of the season.[^cite_note-55] He appeared in 33 fixtures for Manchester City in all competitions during their [2025–26 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Manchester_City_F.C._season), and he was sidelined for two spells due to hamstring and groin injuries.[^cite_note-56]

### Barcelona

On 18 August 2026, Rodri signed for La Liga club [Barcelona](68187.md) on a four-year contract.[^cite_note-57] The transfer fee was reportedly €76.5m, fixed fee of €60m and €16.5m add-ons.[^cite_note-58] In the meantime, Real Madrid coach [José Mourinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mourinho) revealed that the club had made Rodri a very good offer, but the midfielder's hesitation over the move ultimately led Madrid to abandon their pursuit.[^cite_note-59]

On 27 August 2026, Rodri made his La Liga debut for Barcelona against Athletic Bilbao, coming on as a second-half substitute to replace [Marc Bernal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Bernal) in a 2–0 home win.[^cite_note-60][^cite_note-61]

## International career

### Youth career

After playing for [Spain](609825.md) at [under-16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_national_under-16_football_team), [under-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_national_under-19_football_team) and [under-21 levels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_national_under-21_football_team), Rodri was first selected by the full side on 16 March 2018 for two friendlies with [Germany](250204.md) and [Argentina](454699.md).[^cite_note-62] He made his debut five days later, replacing [Thiago Alcântara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiago_Alc%C3%A2ntara) late in the 1–1 draw against Germany in [Düsseldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%BCsseldorf).[^cite_note-63]

### Senior career

#### 2018–2021: First international tournaments



Rodri playing for [Spain](609825.md) in 2019

After missing out on selection for the [2018 FIFA World Cup](13327177.md), Rodri was named in Spain's squad for their first two [UEFA Nations League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_UEFA_Nations_League) fixtures against [England](9904.md) and [Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatia_national_football_team) in September 2018. After being an unused substitute in the first match at [Wembley Stadium](8913012.md), he made his competitive debut as a 59th minute substitute for [Sergio Busquets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Busquets) in the 6–0 win over Croatia on 11 September.[^cite_note-64] A month later, Rodri made his first start for Spain in a 4–1 friendly win over [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales_national_football_team) in Cardiff and was named [man of the match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_of_the_match) by the [BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Broadcasting_Corporation).[^cite_note-65]

Rodri played in seven of Spain's ten matches during [UEFA Euro 2020 qualifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2020_qualifying_Group_F), starting in five.[^cite_note-66]

On 17 November 2020, Rodri scored his first senior international goal in Spain's 6–0 win over Germany in their final group phase match of the [2020–21 UEFA Nations League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_UEFA_Nations_League). The win in Seville ensured *La Roja* would finish top of [Group 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_UEFA_Nations_League_A#Groups#Group_4) and qualify for the [2021 UEFA Nations League Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_UEFA_Nations_League_Finals) in Italy.[^cite_note-67]

In June 2021, he was included in [Luis Enrique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Enrique)'s [24-man squad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2020_squads#Spain) for the postponed [UEFA Euro 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2020) finals.[^cite_note-68] In the absence of captain Sergio Busquets due to [COVID-19](63030231.md), Rodri started the team's first two matches of the tournament as they drew 0–0 with [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland_national_football_team) and 1–1 with [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_men's_national_football_team).[^cite_note-69] With Busquets' return for the final [Group E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2020_Group_E) match against [Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia_national_football_team), Rodri was an unused substitute in the 5–0 win that secured Spain's place in the [knockout stage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2020_knockout_stage).[^cite_note-70] In the round of 16 match against Croatia, Rodri replaced Busquets in the 102nd minute as the team won 5–3 after [extra time](633242.md).[^cite_note-71] He again appeared as a substitute in the quarter-final draw with [Switzerland](681862.md), coming on for [Pedri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedri) in the final minute of extra time ahead of the [penalty shootout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shootout). Although Rodri's kick was saved by Swiss goalkeeper [Yann Sommer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yann_Sommer), Spain won the shootout 4–2 to reach the semi-final.[^cite_note-72] Rodri appeared as a 70th-minute substitute for [Koke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koke_\(footballer,_born_1992\)) as Spain drew 1–1 with [Italy](362466.md) in the semi-final, losing 4–2 in the penalty shootout.[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74]

In October 2021, Rodri was included in Spain's squad for the Nations League finals. On 10 October, he started in Spain's 2–1 defeat against France in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_UEFA_Nations_League_final), playing 84 minutes at the [San Siro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Siro) in Milan.[^cite_note-UNLRU-75]

#### 2022–2023: World Cup and Nations League title

Rodri was included in Spain's squad for the [2022 FIFA World Cup](17742072.md)[^cite_note-76] and was used as a central defender.[^cite_note-77] He played the full 90 minutes in Spain's three [Group E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_E) matches, where they won 7–0 against [Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Rica_national_football_team), drew 1–1 with Germany and lost 2–1 to [Japan](993546.md). In the [round of 16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIFA_World_Cup_knockout_stage#Round_of_16), he played 120 minutes of the team's 0–0 draw with [Morocco](1024865.md), as *La Roja* were eliminated after losing 3–0 in the penalty shootout.[^cite_note-78] Throughout the tournament, Rodri broke compatriot [Xavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavi_\(footballer,_born_1980\))'s [2010](168079.md) record for most passes completed in a single edition of the World Cup (599), completing 638 out of 684 attempted passes, with a 93% success rate.[^cite_note-79]

After the international retirement of Sergio Busquets on 16 December 2022,[^cite_note-80] Rodri succeeded him as Spain's midfield pivot.[^cite_note-81] On 28 March 2023, he captained the team for the first time in their second [UEFA Euro 2024 qualifier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2024_qualifying), where they lost 2–0 to [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland_national_football_team) at [Hampden Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampden_Park).[^cite_note-82]

In June 2023, Rodri was named as Player of the Finals for the [2023 UEFA Nations League Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_UEFA_Nations_League_Finals).[^cite_note-UNLPotTAward-83] He started in both the semi-final against Italy and the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_\(competition\)) against Croatia, the latter of which Spain won via a penalty shootout, with Rodri scoring his spot kick. He was named the best player of the tournament.[^cite_note-UNLWin-84]

On 26 March 2024, Rodri captained Spain for the second time in a friendly match against [Brazil](149286.md). He scored his second and third senior international goals in the 3–3 draw, both from penalty kicks.[^cite_note-85]

#### UEFA Euro 2024 title

In June 2024, Rodri was named in Spain's squad for [UEFA Euro 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2024) in Germany.[^cite_note-86] In the team's final warm-up match – a 5–1 win over [Northern Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Ireland_national_football_team) in Mallorca[^cite_note-87] – he won his 50th [cap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap_\(sport\)) for Spain.[^cite_note-88]

In the team's opening match of the tournament, he played 86 minutes before being substituted for [Martin Zubimendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Zubimendi) as *La Roja* beat Croatia 3–0 at Berlin's [Olympiastadion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympiastadion_\(Berlin\)).[^cite_note-89]

Rodri helped Spain reach and win the [UEFA Euro 2024 final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2024_final) with an outstanding tournament performance, as he was awarded the best player of the tournament, scoring one goal, and having a 92% passing accuracy. Spain defeated England 2–1 to secure their fourth European championship.[^cite_note-90] In addition, he became one of 12 players who won both youth and senior Euro titles, alongside his teammates [Nacho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nacho_\(footballer,_born_1990\)), [Ferran Torres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferran_Torres), [Álvaro Morata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lvaro_Morata) and [Mikel Merino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikel_Merino).[^cite_note-91]

During Spain's celebrations of their Euro 2024 victory over England, Rodri and Spanish captain [Álvaro Morata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lvaro_Morata) were filmed chanting "Gibraltar is Spanish". The chants were labelled "rancid", "discriminatory" and "hugely offensive to Gibraltarians" by the [Government of Gibraltar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Gibraltar), and led to an official complaint to [UEFA](32332.md) by the government and the [Gibraltar Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibraltar_Football_Association).[^cite_note-92][^cite_note-93] After an investigation was opened on 19 July, Rodri and Morata were formally charged under Article 11 of UEFA on 23 July.[^cite_note-94] The pair were given a one match ban.[^cite_note-95]

#### 2026 FIFA World Cup title



Rodri kissing the [FIFA World Cup Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_World_Cup_Trophy) after winning the [2026 final](75845879.md)

On 25 May 2026, Rodri was named in Spain’s squad for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).[^cite_note-96] At the tournament, he served as Spain's captain.[^cite_note-97] Rodri started in all eight of Spain's matches, and following a 1–0 win over [Argentina](454699.md) in the [final](75845879.md), he was awarded the [Golden Ball](3482503.md#Golden_Ball).[^cite_note-98] While Rodri did not score or assist during the tournament, he was credited with effective passing that allowed Spain to control the matches they played,[^cite_note-\:7-99] completing more passes than any other player – a 747 out of 799 attempted (over 93 passes per match) – with a 93% passing accuracy, a record for a single tournament, since statistics began to be collected in [1966](61629.md), breaking the previous record he had set four years earlier; he also completed the most touches (910), passes in the opposition half (428), and line-breaking passes (122) throughout the tournament, including in the final third (76). Moreover, with 101 passes in the final, he became one of only three players, aside from compatriot [Pau Cubarsí](74669990.md) (121) in the same match, and [Brazil's](149286.md) [Dunga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunga) in [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_FIFA_World_Cup_final) (130), to complete over 100 passes in a World Cup final.[^cite_note-100][^cite_note-101][^cite_note-102][^cite_note-103] He also covered more distance than any other player throughout the tournament (96233.71m).[^cite_note-104]

## Player profile

### Style of play

Rodri is a physically strong and tall player. His physique helps him to frequently win tackles, challenges on opponent players, as well as aerial duels. He has a 73% success rate at tackles on opponent players,[^cite_note-\:0-105] and has won 100% of his aerial duels in the 2022–23 season, according to Squawka. He averages a passing accuracy of 91–92%.[^cite_note-\:0-105]

Rodri is further known for his versatility and tactical intelligence.[^cite_note-Smith2021-106][^cite_note-\:2-107][^cite_note-\:3-108] He is known for his contributions to his team's build-up play.[^cite_note-\:4-109][^cite_note-\:5-110] Often used as an anchor, or water carrier, his primary role is to win back [possession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football_tactics_and_skills) in [midfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midfield) and distribute the ball to start counterattacks.[^cite_note-\:2-107][^cite_note-\:3-108][^cite_note-\:5-110] His ability to maintain possession under pressure allows him to perform this role effectively.[^cite_note-\:2-107][^cite_note-111]

Rodri's tactical evolution under [Pep Guardiola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pep_Guardiola) has redefined the modern [holding midfielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holding_midfielder) role. According to a [Sky Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Sports) analysis, his positional intelligence and composure under pressure are essential to Manchester City's midfield control. He often positions himself between the centre-backs to facilitate build-up play and maintain possession, allowing the team to transition smoothly from defence to attack. His precise distribution and spatial awareness make him a central figure in Guardiola's system.[^cite_note-Smith2021-106]

In addition to his defensive abilities, Rodri also possesses accurate passing and vision, allowing him to operate as a [deep-lying playmaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep-lying_playmaker) from the heart of the midfield.[^cite_note-\:2-107][^cite_note-\:3-108][^cite_note-\:5-110] In the 2023–24 season, he was involved in more sequences of play than any other player in his team or in the Premier League.[^cite_note-\:5-110] He is also known for his ability to set the tempo of the game.[^cite_note-\:2-107][^cite_note-\:3-108][^cite_note-112]

Besides his regular position of a holding midfielder, he has occasionally been used as a ball-playing [centre-back](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-back), in particular by national team manager [Luis Enrique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Enrique) during the 2022 World Cup.[^cite_note-113]

During his Manchester City tenure, he also worked to improve his goalscoring, and became known for his long-range shooting, which resulted in several goals, such as his match-winning goal from outside the box against Inter in the 2023 UEFA Champions League Final.[^cite_note-114][^cite_note-115][^cite_note-116][^cite_note-117] Despite not being particularly renowned for his pace, he has been credited by pundits for his ability to catch up with much faster attackers in midfield.[^cite_note-\:2-107][^cite_note-118][^cite_note-119] Rodri was awarded the Ballon d'Or in 2024, recognising his outstanding performances throughout the season.[^cite_note-BBCBallon2024-12][^cite_note-120][^cite_note-121]

### Reception

Rodri is widely regarded as one of the best midfielders in the world.[^cite_note-\:8-3][^cite_note-\:9-4][^cite_note-\:10-5][^cite_note-\:11-6][^cite_note-\:12-7][^cite_note-\:13-8][^cite_note-\:14-9] In January 2024, members of the *[ESPN FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN_FC)* staff debated whether Rodri was in fact that best player in the world at the moment.[^cite_note-122][^cite_note-123] In March 2024, his Manchester City manager [Pep Guardiola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pep_Guardiola) described him as "the best midfield player, currently, in the world by far."[^cite_note-124] That same month, *[The New York Times](30680.md)* rated him as the best player in the Premier League,[^cite_note-125] while former Manchester United defender [Rio Ferdinand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Ferdinand) believed that Rodri was already arguably the best defensive midfielder in the league's history.[^cite_note-126] He was nominated for the [2023 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Ballon_d'Or), ultimately finishing fifth.[^cite_note-127] He won the [2024 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Ballon_d'Or), finishing ahead of [Vinícius Júnior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vin%C3%ADcius_J%C3%BAnior) and [Jude Bellingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Bellingham), becoming the first Manchester City player, as well as the first Premier League player since [Cristiano Ronaldo](623737.md) in 2008, to win the award.[^cite_note-128] His sustained performances sparked debates over his status among the greatest players in Premier League history.[^cite_note-Cunningham2026-129] Following Spain's victory at the 2026 FIFA World Cup, where he was awarded the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player, he became only the seventh player in history, and the first based in the Premier League, to win the World Cup, the UEFA European Championship, the UEFA Champions League, and the Ballon d'Or.[^cite_note-Cunningham2026-129]

Rodri's critical importance to Manchester City's tactical system has been widely noted by sports analysts and statisticians. His influence is statistically reflected in the team's overall performance; data consistently shows a significant decline in Manchester City's win rate and points-per-game average during his absences. Following a season-ending knee injury in September 2024, manager Pep Guardiola described him as an "irreplaceable" player, stating that the squad lacked another midfielder capable of replicating his specific tactical contributions.[^cite_note-130] Guardiola commended his broader influence on the sport, stating that Rodri "perfectly represents City, but also Spanish football and their influence in world football."[^cite_note-Cunningham2026-129] Pundits and sports journalists have frequently highlighted his unmatched statistical consistency in passing accuracy, possession recovery, and positional intelligence, crediting him with evolving the traditional defensive midfield role into a primary deep-lying playmaking pivot.[^cite_note-Cunningham2026-129]

## Personal life

While a Villarreal youth player, Rodri studied at the [Jaume I University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_I_University) in nearby [Castellón de la Plana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castell%C3%B3n_de_la_Plana) for four years to gain a degree in business administration and management.[^cite_note-131][^cite_note-132] At university, he met his longterm girlfriend Laura, who was studying medicine.[^cite_note-133] Rodri is a [Roman Catholic](606848.md), in an interview after the 2024 Euro final he said: "In life and in sport, when you do things right, in the end, God rewards you for it. I’m grateful.” And made a similar statement after the 2026 World Cup tournament.[^cite_note-134][^cite_note-135][^cite_note-136] He avoids using social media.[^cite_note-137]
