The [Grammy Award](44635.md) for **Best New Artist** has been awarded since the [2nd Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Annual_Grammy_Awards) in 1960 (except in 1967)[^cite_note-1] "for a new artist who releases, during the Eligibility Year, the first recording which establishes the public identity of that artist." Since 2022, the award is presented by the previous year's winner during the televised ceremony.

The Best New Artist award has a reputation for being given to artists whose music industry success ends up being short-lived; it is sometimes asserted, with varying degrees of sincerity, that the award itself brings a [curse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curse).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] This viewpoint was expressed by [Taffy Danoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taffy_Nivert), a former member of [Starland Vocal Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starland_Vocal_Band) (known for "[Afternoon Delight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afternoon_Delight)"), in an interview for [VH1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VH1)'s *[100 Greatest One Hit Wonders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Greatest_\(VH1_TV_series\))*: "We got two of the five Grammys – one was Best New Artist. So that was basically the kiss of death and I feel sorry for everyone who's gotten it since."[^cite_note-4]

The category is also notable for being the only category to date in which a Grammy Award has been revoked: this occurred in [1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/32nd_Annual_Grammy_Awards), when [Milli Vanilli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milli_Vanilli) originally won the award, but band members [Fab Morvan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fab_Morvan) and [Rob Pilatus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Pilatus) later admitted that they did not contribute their own vocals on their album *[Girl You Know It's True](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girl_You_Know_It's_True_\(album\))*.[^cite_note-Revoked-5] The award was subsequently vacated.[^cite_note-Vacated-6]

Of the 65 acts who have won the award since its inception, 34 are solo female artists, 19 are duos or groups, and 12 are solo male artists. Of the solo male artists, half were given the award in its first decade; since 1970, only six solo male artists have won the award, the most recent being [Chance the Rapper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chance_the_Rapper) in 2017. From 1997 to 2003, and again from 2018 to 2025, all the winners were solo female artists.

Only five artists have won both Best New Artist and [Album of the Year](196513.md) in the same year: [Bob Newhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Newhart) in 1961, [Christopher Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Cross) in 1981, [Lauryn Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauryn_Hill) in 1999, [Norah Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norah_Jones) in 2003, and [Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish) in 2020. Of these, Cross, Jones, and Eilish had songs winning [Record of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Record_of_the_Year) and [Song of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year) for the same year, with Cross as the sole songwriter, Eilish as co-writer, and Jones lacking songwriting credit, which therefore made her miss out on completing the single year big four Grammy achievement, a feat that only Cross and Eilish attained; while [Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele) was the only artist to win all General field Grammys from separate occasions. Only two artists have *lost* Best New Artist yet won Album of the Year in the same year: [Vaughn Meader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaughn_Meader) in 1963 and [Alanis Morissette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alanis_Morissette) in 1996.

For the award's first several years of existence, comedians and comic acts were regularly nominated, and one, Bob Newhart, won the award. However, this ended abruptly after 1963, and since then, only one comedian has been nominated for the award: [Robin Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Williams) in 1980. (That same year, the semi-comic act [The Blues Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blues_Brothers) was also nominated.) Of all the winners, only three have been [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music) artists. In 1997, [LeAnn Rimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LeAnn_Rimes) became the first country artist and (at age 14) the youngest artist to win the award.[^cite_note-Grein-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-Country_Grammy_moments-9] She was followed by [Carrie Underwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_Underwood) in 2007[^cite_note-Country_Grammy_moments-9][^cite_note-10] and [Zac Brown Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zac_Brown_Band) in 2010.[^cite_note-11] Additionally, 2017 marked the first time that two country artists were nominated in this category in the same year, in which [Maren Morris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maren_Morris) and [Kelsea Ballerini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsea_Ballerini) were both nominated.[^cite_note-12] In 1990, [Tone Lōc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tone_Loc) became the first rap artist to be nominated. In 2012, [Skrillex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skrillex) became the first electronic-music artist to be nominated.

Eleven female rappers have been nominated for the award; [Neneh Cherry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neneh_Cherry), [Lauryn Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauryn_Hill), [Nicki Minaj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicki_Minaj), [Iggy Azalea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iggy_Azalea), [Saweetie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saweetie), [Latto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latto), [Ice Spice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_Spice) and [Doechii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doechii); with 2021 marking the first time that multiple female rappers were nominated in the same year, when [Chika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chika_\(rapper\)), [Doja Cat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doja_Cat) and [Megan Thee Stallion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Thee_Stallion) were all nominated. Hill and Megan Thee Stallion are the only winners.

## Process

From 1995 to 2021, members of the [National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Recording_Arts_and_Sciences) nominated their choices for the best new artist. A list of the top twenty artists was given to the Nominations Review Committee, a specially selected group of anonymous members, who initially selected the top five artists to gain a nomination in the category in a special ballot; the number of nominated artists was increased to eight in 2018.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] The rest of the members then vote on a winner from the final nominees.[^cite_note-16] In 2021, it was announced that the Nomination Review Committees would be disbanded, and the final nominees for best new artist would be decided by votes from members.[^cite_note-17] Starting in 2022, the number of nominees in the category increased to 10.[^cite_note-18] However, the decision to expand the number of nominees in this category was made 24 hours before the nominees were announced after an early version of the nominations list had already been circulated. This allowed [Baby Keem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_Keem) and [Arooj Aftab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arooj_Aftab) to be nominated as they were the artists who received the most votes besides the other eight nominees.[^cite_note-19] As of the [2024 ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_Annual_Grammy_Awards), the number of nominees has been reduced to eight.[^cite_note-20]

## Rules changes

Over the years, the eligibility rules for this category have changed several times. In 2010, [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga)'s exclusion from the Best New Artist category caused the [Recording Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recording_Academy) to change eligibility requirements for the next ceremony. She was ineligible for the nomination because her hit "[Just Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Just_Dance_\(song\))" had been nominated in 2008. The new rule stated that an artist may be nominated as long as that artist has not previously released an entire [album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_album) and has subsequently not won a Grammy.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] In June 2016, the Grammy organization amended the Best New Artist rules once again, to remove the album barrier "given current trends in how new music and developing artists are released and promoted".[^cite_note-23] To be eligible in the category of Best New Artist, the artist, duo, or group:

- Must have released a minimum of five singles/tracks or one album (until 2020 there was a maximum of 30 singles/tracks or three albums, but this maximum limit was removed for the 2021 awards season)

- May not have entered into this category more than three times, including as a performing member of an established group.

- Must have achieved a breakthrough into the public consciousness and impacted the musical landscape during the eligibility period.

These new rules were put in effect with the [59th Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/59th_Annual_Grammy_Awards). The category was then expanded to include eight nominees in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/61st_Annual_Grammy_Awards).[^cite_note-24] Starting in 2021, screening committees were charged with determining whether the artist had attained a breakthrough or prominence prior to the eligibility year. Such a determination would result in disqualification.[^cite_note-25] As of the [69th Annual Grammy Awards](80454676.md), an artist is allowed to submit up to a maximum of four times overall for the category, instead of three.[^cite_note-26]
