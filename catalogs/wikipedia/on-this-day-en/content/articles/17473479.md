**Ketapang**, or *Tau-pang* in [Teochew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_dialect), is the [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town) and [regency seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regency_seat) of [Ketapang Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ketapang_Regency) (*Kabupaten Ketapang*), one of the regencies of [West Kalimantan](453636.md) province on the island of [Borneo](4518.md) in [Indonesia](14579.md). Ketapang town is located at [1°51′S 109°59′E  /  1.850°S 109.983°E  / -1.850; 109.983](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Ketapang\&params=1_51_S_109_59_E_) and is a town on the [delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_delta) of the [Pawan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pawan_River). Ketapang is served by the [Rahadi Oesman Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahadi_Oesman_Airport).

## History



[Tua Pek Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tua_Pek_Kong) Ketapang, icon of the town

Ketapang is named from the local [Malay name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language) for ketapang tree (*[Terminalia catappa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminalia_catappa)*).

In 1936, Ketapang Regency became one of the Afdeling (district in Dutch) in Keresidenan Kalimantan Barat (Residentis Western Afdeling van Borneo). In 1956, Ketapang Regency became an autonomous regency in [West Kalimantan](453636.md) province, and led by a regent (bupati).



Keraton Matan (also known as Keraton G.M.Saunan, name of the last King) used to be a [Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malays_\(ethnic_group\))'s kingdom palace. It is now a museum

## Administration

In formal Indonesian administrative subdivision, Ketapang is a town district officially called *Kecamatan Delta Pawan* ("Pawan Estuary District"), not a city. The name of Ketapang is only officially applied to [Ketapang Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ketapang_Regency). Although there is no exact border of the built-up area, most of the government offices and the central of business of Ketapang Regency are located in **Delta Pawan** [District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District) (Kecamatan Delta Pawan) and because of that the district is considered as the capital of the regency. The district itself is located on the delta of the [Pawan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pawan_River). [Benua Kayong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benua_Kayong) District borders the town on its south side and forms part of the Ketapang urban area on the south side of the river (apart from the single inland village of Negeri Baru), while [Muara Pawan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muara_Pawan) District borders the town on its north side.

## Economy

The main industries consists in the production of [palm oil](57561.md), [rubber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubber) and wood. And also there are some mining like [alumina](141888.md), [bauxite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bauxite), [zircon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zircon) sand and [lead](17747.md) (Batu Galena)

## Education

There are 37 high schools operated by state, private and religious institutions. The town has a polytechnic (Politeknik Ketapang, opened in 2008) and an Open University (Universitas Terbuka).

## Health

The three hospitals located in the town are RSUD Dr. Agoesdjam (public hospital), RS Fatima (private hospital), and RS Permata Bunda (private hospital).

## Demographics

Ketapang is a multicultural town. It has a large population of [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Indonesians), [Madurese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madurese_people) and [Javanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javanese_people) people. Most Chinese residents in Ketapang are of either [Teochew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_people) or [Hakka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakka_people) descent with Teochew being the majority. Because of this, Teochew is the main dialect used among Chinese residents in Ketapang. The native Indonesians living there are mainly of [Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malays_\(ethnic_group\)) and [Dayak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayak_people) descent. In regards to speech, most citizens of Ketapang incorporate a type of Malay accent in their Indonesian, which is somewhat similar to that used in [Malaysia](3607937.md).

## Transportation



Rahadi Osman Airport, Ketapang, West Borneo, Indonesia

Ketapang is served by the [Rahadi Oesman Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahadi_Oesman_Airport). The airport has some connecting flights to [Pontianak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontianak,_Indonesia) and [Semarang](1134311.md) via [Pangkalan Bun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangkalan_Bun). Since February 2011, Ketapang also has direct flight to [Jakarta](16275.md), by [Aviastar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviastar_\(Indonesia\)).[^cite_note-soc-2] Aviastar stopped the service in 2018. Ketapang can also be reached via ship from [Pontianak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontianak,_Indonesia) (a six-hour speedboat ride, departing Pontianak daily at 6 am). The ship also stopped the service in 2018. But there are some ship from [Teluk Melano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teluk_Melano?action=edit\&redlink=1), 2 hours drive from Ketapang and home pick-up included, to [Pontianak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontianak) every day. Pontianak's airport serves flights to [Jakarta](16275.md). By 2024, it is possible to drive to Pontianak by car in about 9 hours and 30 minutes passing [Trans-Kalimantan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Kalimantan?action=edit\&redlink=1) road.

## Main sights



Tugu Ale-ale

- [Gunung Palung National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunung_Palung_National_Park), a rainforest park that can be reached from Ketapang. It was once wholly part of Ketapang regency, but now only a small part is, the rest in the regency that is named [Kayong Utara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayong_Utara) or North Kayong (created 2007). Kayong is the nickname of Ketapang.

- [Pematang Gadung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pematang_Gadung), The Peat Forest, the home of wild [Orangutan](22433.md).

- [Kelenteng Tua Pek Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelenteng_Tua_Pek_Kong?action=edit\&redlink=1) (大伯公), a Chinese [temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple).

- [Tugu Ale-ale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tugu_Ale-ale?action=edit\&redlink=1). Ale-ale is a kind of [shell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exoskeleton).

- Beaches in Ketapang include Sungai Jawi beach (10 km from the town), Tanjung Batu beach (35 km), and Pulau Datok beach (80 km).

- [Makam Raja-Raja Tanjungpura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makam_Raja-Raja_Tanjungpura?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([Tanjungpura Royal Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanjungpura_Royal_Cemetery?action=edit\&redlink=1)), 3 hour from Ketapang town, by motorcycle and small boat that carries motorcycle through a section of road that is flooded. The flooded section is being repaired in 2019.
