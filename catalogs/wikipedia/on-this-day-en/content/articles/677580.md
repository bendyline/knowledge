**Górnik Zabrze [Spółka Akcyjna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sp%C3%B3%C5%82ka_Akcyjna)**, commonly referred to as **Górnik Zabrze** (Polish pronunciation: [\[ˈɡurɲiɡ ˈzabʐɛ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Polish)), is a Polish [football](10568.md) club from [Zabrze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zabrze). Górnik is one of the most successful Polish football clubs in history, winning the second-most [Polish Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Championship_in_Football) titles together with [Ruch Chorzów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruch_Chorz%C3%B3w). The club was a dominant force in the 1960s and 1980s. Górnik holds the record for winning the most consecutive Polish Championship titles (5) and Polish Cup titles (5). In addition, the club was [1969–70 Cup Winners' Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969%E2%80%9370_European_Cup_Winners'_Cup) runners-up.

They currently compete in the [Ekstraklasa](30871677.md), the top tier of the [national football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_football_league_system). The club plays in a white or dark blue-red kit, and is based at the [Arena Zabrze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_Zabrze). Their main local rival is [Ruch Chorzów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruch_Chorz%C3%B3w).[^cite_note-3]

## History

### First years

The club was founded in 1948 after several smaller sports associations – KS Zjednoczenie, KS Pogoń, KS Skra, and KS Concordia – were merged into a single organization, on the initiative of Julian Gajdzicki, a former [deportee to Siberia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sybirak) and a soldier of the Polish [Anders' Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anders'_Army) during the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War).[^cite_note-75lat-4] The club took the name "Górnik", the [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language) word for "Miner", reflecting the fact that Zabrze was an important coal-mining centre. It was a multi-sports club with several departments, including [athletics](43719.md), swimming and boxing, however, football remained the leading department.[^cite_note-5]

In 1949, Górnik has entered the regional Klasa A of Opole Silesia (third tier) replacing Pogoń Zabrze.[^cite_note-75lat-4] In 1950 the club was promoted to the [Polish Second Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland_II_Liga).[^cite_note-75lat-4] Their first game in the second tier was against [Skra Częstochowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skra_Cz%C4%99stochowa), and was witnessed by 20,000 fans, with Górnik winning 5–1.[^cite_note-6] The whole season was very successful and Górnik finished second overall, behind [Górnik Wałbrzych](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G%C3%B3rnik_Wa%C5%82brzych). In November 1951, Górnik played their first friendly match against a foreign club, [Dinamo Tbilisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Dinamo_Tbilisi).[^cite_note-75lat-4]

The club was promoted to the top division in 1955. In their first game in the top flight Górnik beat local rivals [Ruch Chorzów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruch_Chorz%C3%B3w) 3–1, with 25,000 in attendance;[^cite_note-75lat-4] the club finished the season in sixth place.

### First successes

In 1957, just a year after promotion, Górnik won its first championship of Poland. The team, with star, [Ernest Pohl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Pohl), was third in 1958, to regain the crown in 1959 and 1961, together with such players as [Stanisław Oślizło](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanis%C5%82aw_O%C5%9Bliz%C5%82o) and [Hubert Kostka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Kostka). In 1961 Górnik for the first time appeared in European Cups, losing in the first round to [Tottenham Hotspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.). In the [1962 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_Ekstraklasa), Górnik set its record victory in a league match, beating [Cracovia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KS_Cracovia) 9–0,[^cite_note-75lat-4] a result matched in the [2002–03 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002%E2%80%9303_Ekstraklasa) in a game against [Pogoń Szczecin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pogo%C5%84_Szczecin).[^cite_note-7]

### Golden years

The next championship, won in 1963, marked the beginning of an unusual streak of five consecutive titles (1963, 64, 65, 66 and 67), which is a Polish record, followed by five consecutive [Polish Cup](772047.md) wins (1968, 1969, 1970, 1971 and 1972), also a Polish record, and two more Polish Championships in 1971 and 1972. Between 1957 and 1972, Górnik set a Polish record of finishing in the top three of the league for fifteen consecutive seasons.

Two Górnik players, [Włodzimierz Lubański](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W%C5%82odzimierz_Luba%C5%84ski) and [Zygfryd Szołtysik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zygfryd_Szo%C5%82tysik), were ranked in the [1967 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967_Ballon_d'Or) plebiscite, as the first Poles ever to feature in a Ballon d'Or poll.[^cite_note-75lat-4] Lubański was ranked in the plebiscite three more times, reaching a high of seventh place[^cite_note-75lat-4] in [1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Ballon_d'Or). [Jerzy Gorgoń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerzy_Gorgo%C5%84) was ranked 15th in the [1974 Ballon d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_Ballon_d'Or) plebiscite, as the only Polish [defender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defender_\(association_football\)) to date.[^cite_note-75lat-4]



Ticket to a match against [Manchester City](165813.md) in the [1970–71 European Cup Winners' Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970%E2%80%9371_European_Cup_Winners'_Cup)

Górnik's biggest success in European football took place in 1970 (even though in Poland the team was second, after [Legia Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legia_Warsaw)). In the [UEFA Cup Winners Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Cup_Winners_Cup), Gornik beat all their opponents – [Olympiacos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympiacos_F.C.), [Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rangers_F.C.), [Levski Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PFC_Levski_Sofia) and [AS Roma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AS_Roma), reaching the final, which took place in [Vienna](55866.md). There, [Manchester City](165813.md) turned out to be the better team, winning 2–1. The following season Górnik would once again play Manchester City, with the 1970 final being repeated this time in the quarter-final.

### Late 1970s and early 1980s

During the mid-1970s Górnik form deteriorated and in late spring of 1978, the team was relegated to the Second Division. However, it returned after one year and in games of 1979–80, Zabrze's side finished sixth. In 1984, after purchasing of a group of talented players ([Ryszard Komornicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryszard_Komornicki), [Waldemar Matysik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waldemar_Matysik), [Eugeniusz Cebrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugeniusz_Cebrat), [Andrzej Zgutczyński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrzej_Zgutczy%C5%84ski), [Tadeusz Dolny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadeusz_Dolny), [Andrzej Pałasz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrzej_Pa%C5%82asz)), Górnik finished fourth, which was a sign of better times.

### Successes of other departments

In 1955, [Jerzy Chromik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerzy_Chromik), [long-distance runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-distance_runner) from Górnik Zabrze, became the first Polish athlete after World War II to set a new world record.[^cite_note-75lat-4] Several [track and field](194904.md) athletes from Górnik Zabrze have won Olympic medals, i.e. [Halina Górecka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halina_G%C3%B3recka) and [Tadeusz Ślusarski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadeusz_%C5%9Alusarski) won gold in [1964](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_1964_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_4_%C3%97_100_metres_relay) and [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_1976_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_pole_vault), respectively, and [Zbigniew Jaremski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zbigniew_Jaremski), [Jerzy Pietrzyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerzy_Pietrzyk) and [Zenon Licznerski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zenon_Licznerski) won silver—the former two together in [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_1976_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_4_%C3%97_400_metres_relay), and the latter in [1980](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_1980_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_4_%C3%97_100_metres_relay).[^cite_note-8] Up until the 1980s, athletes from the swimming, [diving](8402.md), weightlifting and wrestling departments of Górnik Zabrze won medals at the Polish national championships.[^cite_note-9]

### Late 1980s until now

Between 1985 and 1988, Górnik again marked a magnificent streak, with four consecutive championships. Zabrze's side also played versus renowned European powerhouses, such as [Bayern Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Bayern_Munich), [Anderlecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSC_Anderlecht), [Hamburger SV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburger_SV), [Juventus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juventus_FC) and [Real Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_C.F.).



[Arena Zabrze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_Zabrze)

In 1994, Górnik again competed for the title, and with players as [Jerzy Brzęczek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerzy_Brz%C4%99czek), [Grzegorz Mielcarski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grzegorz_Mielcarski), [Tomasz Wałdoch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomasz_Wa%C5%82doch), hopes were high. Before the last round of the league, the standings at the top saw Legia with 47 points and Górnik with 45. Since the two teams were to face each other in [Warsaw](32908.md), Górnik still had a chance to win the title. However, the game ended in a 1–1 tie, which gave the title to Legia. Before Legia scored the championship-winning equaliser, the referee of the match Stanisław Redziński sent off three Górnik players in quick succession. It was the last match of Redziński's refereeing career.

In the spring of 2007, Górnik acquired a new sponsor – a German insurance company [Allianz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allianz). However, after finishing 16th in the [Ekstraklasa](30871677.md) in the [2008–09 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008%E2%80%9309_Ekstraklasa), the club was relegated to the [I liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_liga), the second level of Polish football. In June 2010, the club earned promotion back to the Ekstraklasa for the [2010–11 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010%E2%80%9311_Ekstraklasa). Since then, Górnik has promoted a number of players to the [Poland national team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland_national_football_team) and transferred several players to stronger leagues, including [Arkadiusz Milik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkadiusz_Milik), [Łukasz Skorupski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%81ukasz_Skorupski), [Szymon Żurkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szymon_%C5%BBurkowski) and [Paweł Bochniewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pawe%C5%82_Bochniewicz).

In 2016, the Sports Department was established at the Municipal Museum in Zabrze, where most of the exposition is devoted to Górnik Zabrze.[^cite_note-10] In 2022, the women's section of Górnik Zabrze was established through the merger with the previously separate women's club, KKS Zabrze.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]



[Lukas Podolski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Podolski) as a Górnik Zabrze player in 2023

In the 2025–26 season, Górnik won the [Polish Cup](79976641.md), ending a 38-year trophy drought.[^cite_note-13] On 21 May 2026, having acquired the remaining shares owned by Allianz a year prior, [Lukas Podolski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Podolski) became Górnik's majority owner after purchasing 86% of the club's shares from the Zabrze local government.[^cite_note-LPHold-2]

## Supporters and rivalries



Górnik Zabrze supporters during the Great Silesian Derby.



[Graffiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graffiti) on a wall in [Zabrze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zabrze).

Górnik Zabrze is believed to have one of the largest and most loyal fanbases in Poland, especially in the [Katowice metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katowice_metropolitan_area). In the 2016–17 season, Górnik Zabrze drew the highest average home attendance (10,636)[^cite_note-16] of all second level Polish football clubs. They also drew the highest attendance in their league (20,987). After their comeback to the top flight in 2017, Górnik drew the highest average home attendance in Polish football, surpassing current top teams [Lech Poznań](677622.md) and [Legia Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legia_Warsaw),[^cite_note-17] with most league games being sold-out.[^cite_note-18]

Górnik holds a long-standing rivalry with Upper Silesian side [Ruch Chorzów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruch_Chorz%C3%B3w), known as the [Great Silesian Derby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Silesian_Derby?action=edit\&redlink=1). Other main rivals are [Piast Gliwice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piast_Gliwice), [Polonia Bytom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polonia_Bytom), [Legia Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legia_Warsaw) and [Zagłębie Sosnowiec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zag%C5%82%C4%99bie_Sosnowiec).

*Torcida Zabrze* is named after the [ultras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultras) of [Torcida Split](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torcida_Split), with whom they have friendly relations; together they are called *United Torcida*. They have also friendly relations with fans of [Wisłoka Dębica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wis%C5%82oka_D%C4%99bica) and German club [Schalke 04](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schalke_04);[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] the latter in past used to be a rival. Fans of [Concordia Knurów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concordia_Knur%C3%B3w), [Naprzód Rydułtowy](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naprz%C3%B3d_Rydu%C5%82towy), [Slavia Ruda Śląska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavia_Ruda_%C5%9Al%C4%85ska?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Czarni Pyskowice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czarni_Pyskowice?action=edit\&redlink=1) are also Górnik fan-clubs.

### Notable supporters

Well-known supporters of Górnik Zabrze include [Zbigniew Religa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zbigniew_Religa), pioneer in human heart transplantation in Poland, [Adam Małysz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Ma%C5%82ysz), one of the most successful [ski jumpers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ski_jumper) in history, [Wojtek Wolski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wojtek_Wolski), former [National Hockey League](21809.md) player, and international footballers [Jerzy Dudek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerzy_Dudek), [Lukas Podolski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Podolski) and [Ireneusz Jeleń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireneusz_Jele%C5%84).[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]
