**Bakori,** is a town and [local government area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Katsina State](2227638.md), [Nigeria](21383.md). It was created on 15 May 1989 during the military regime of General [Ibrahim Badamasi Babangida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Badamasi_Babangida).

It has an area of 679 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 641,576 as of the 2006 census. It shares border with [Funtua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funtua) by the west, [Malumfashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malumfashi) in the north, [Danja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danja,_Nigeria) in the south and [Kankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankara) in the east. Federal Government Girls College is located at Bakori.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 831.[^cite_note-2]

## Geography

Bakori local government area covers a total land area of 679 square kilometres (262 square miles) with wind speed of 4 km/h. The area has an average temperature of 36 °C, while the humidity of the area is recorded at 14%.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

### Climate

Yearly temperature in Bakori varies between rainy season and dry season, with oppressive cloudy conditions and hot temperatures.[^cite_note-\:1-3][^cite_note-4]

Climate change is causing a warmer, colder climate in Bakori, as evidenced by the positive trend of temperature increases and warming stripes.[^cite_note-5]

#### Average Temperature

With an average daily high temperature of 34 °C (94 °F), the hot season spans 1.9 months, from March 9 to May 4. At an average high temperature of 36 °C (96 °F) and low temperature of 22 °C (71 °F), April is Bakori's hottest month of the year. With an average daily maximum temperature below 29 °C (85 °F), the cool season spans 2.8 months, from July 7 to October 1. January is the coldest month of the year in Bakori, with an average high temperature of 29 °C (85 °F) and low temperature of 13 °C (55 °F).[^cite_note-\:1-3]

## Notable people

Notable people from Bakori include:

- [Amina Lawal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amina_Lawal) - sentenced to death by stoning for adultery, but eventually acquitted

- Senator [Mohammed Tukur Liman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_Tukur_Liman) - former Senate Majority Leader (1999-2003)

- [Amiruddin Tukur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amiruddin_Tukur) is a Nigerian politician. He represented the Bakori / Danja Federal Constituency of Katsina State in the 8th and 9th Nigerian house of representatives from 2015 to 2023. He is a member of the All Progressives Congress (APC).
