**Kumamoto Prefecture** (熊本県, *Kumamoto-ken*; Japanese pronunciation: [\[kɯ.ma.mo.to, -toꜜ.keɴ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Japanese)[^cite_note-2]) is a [prefecture](74517.md) of [Japan](15573.md) located on the island of [Kyūshū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ky%C5%ABsh%C5%AB).[^cite_note-3] Kumamoto Prefecture has a population of 1,683,115 (as of 1 October 2025) and has a geographic area of 7,409 square kilometres (2,861 sq mi). Kumamoto Prefecture borders [Fukuoka Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukuoka_Prefecture) to the north, [Ōita Prefecture](221919.md) to the northeast, [Miyazaki Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyazaki_Prefecture) to the southeast, and [Kagoshima Prefecture](217439.md) to the south.

[Kumamoto](6793346.md) is the capital and largest city of Kumamoto Prefecture, with other major cities including [Yatsushiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yatsushiro,_Kumamoto), [Amakusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amakusa,_Kumamoto), and [Tamana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamana,_Kumamoto).[^cite_note-4] Kumamoto Prefecture is located in the center of Kyūshū on the coast of the [Ariake Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariake_Sea), across from [Nagasaki Prefecture](221380.md), with the mainland separated from the [East China Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_China_Sea) by the Amakusa [Archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archipelago). Kumamoto Prefecture is home to [Mount Aso](956535.md), the largest active [volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcano) in [Japan](15573.md) and among the largest in the world, with its peak 1,592 metres (5,223 ft) above [sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_level).

## History

Main article: [History of Kumamoto Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Kumamoto_Prefecture)

See also: [Historic Sites of Kumamoto Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historic_Sites_of_Kumamoto_Prefecture)

Historically, the area was called [Higo Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higo_Province); the province was renamed Kumamoto during the [Meiji Restoration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_Restoration).[^cite_note-5] The creation of prefectures was part of the [abolition of the feudal system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abolition_of_the_han_system). The current Japanese [orthography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthography) for Kumamoto literally means "bear root/origin," or "origin of the bear." However, [Man'yōgana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man'y%C5%8Dgana)-like phonetic transcriptions based on the [kanji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanji) for "a ball, a sphere" (球 /kɯ\~kʲɯː/) and "to polish, to grind, to brush (one's teeth)" (磨 /ma/) are used for the names of the [Kuma River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuma_River_\(Japan\)) (球磨川) and [Kuma District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuma_District,_Kumamoto) (球磨郡). The *Kuma* element also appears in the ancient [Kumaso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumaso).

## Geography



Map of Kumamoto Prefecture showing municipal boundaries\

Kumamoto Prefecture is in the center of [Kyushu](16914.md), the southernmost of the four major Japanese islands. It is bordered by the [Ariake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariake_Sea) inland sea and the [Amakusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amakusa) [archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archipelago) to the west, [Fukuoka Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukuoka_Prefecture) and [Ōita Prefecture](221919.md) to the north, [Miyazaki Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyazaki_Prefecture) to the east, and [Kagoshima Prefecture](217439.md) to the south.

[Mount Aso](956535.md) (1,592 m (5,223 ft)), an extensive active [volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcano), is in the east of Kumamoto Prefecture. This volcano is located at the centre of the [Aso caldera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aso_Caldera).

As of 31 March 2019, 21% of the total land area of the prefecture was designated as [natural parks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_national_parks_of_Japan#History): the [Aso Kujū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aso_Kuj%C5%AB_National_Park) and [Unzen-Amakusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unzen-Amakusa_National_Park) National Parks; [Kyūshū Chūō Sanchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ky%C5%ABsh%C5%AB_Ch%C5%AB%C5%8D_Sanchi_Quasi-National_Park) and [Yaba-Hita-Hikosan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaba-Hita-Hikosan_Quasi-National_Park) Quasi-National Parks; and [Ashikita Kaigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashikita_Kaigan_Prefectural_Natural_Park), [Itsuki Gokanoshō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itsuki_Gokanosh%C5%8D_Prefectural_Natural_Park), [Kinpōzan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinp%C5%8Dzan_Prefectural_Natural_Park), [Misumi-Ōyano Umibe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misumi-%C5%8Cyano_Umibe_Prefectural_Natural_Park), [Okukuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okukuma_Prefectural_Natural_Park), [Shōtaisan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dtaisan_Prefectural_Natural_Park), and [Yabe Shūhen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yabe_Sh%C5%ABhen_Prefectural_Natural_Park) Prefectural Natural Parks.[^cite_note-6]

## Demographics

As of 1 June 2019, the population was 1,748,134 inhabitants with a population density of 236 inhabitants per square kilometre (610/sq mi). The prefecture ranks 23rd in Japan.

Kumamoto Prefecture hosted the [2019 World Women's Handball Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_World_Women's_Handball_Championship), having previously hosted the [1997 World Men's Handball Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_World_Men's_Handball_Championship).

## Sister cities

Kumamoto Prefecture is the 'sister state/prefecture' of [Montana](19978.md) in the [United States](3434750.md).

[San Antonio](53848.md), Texas is Kumamoto's sister city; it holds an annual fall festival 'akimatsuri' for its Japanese citizens. In 2015 the mascot, 'Kumamon' visited as an honorary ambassador during the festival located at the Japanese Tea Gardens.
