The **president of the Republic of Bulgaria** ([Bulgarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_language): Президент на Република България, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Bulgarian): *Prezident na Republika Balgariya*) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of [Bulgaria](3415.md) and the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Bulgarian Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Armed_Forces). The official residence of the president is the Boyana Residence in [Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia).

The president's primary function is to serve as the [arbitrator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbitrator) of disputes between Bulgaria's different institutions. They are not the [head of government](37570.md) or part of the nation's [executive power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_power); however, when the [National Assembly](2122384.md) fails to elect a [prime minister](335071.md), the president appoints an interim government. As of 2026, this happened on 12 occasions: [Zhelyu Zhelev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhelyu_Zhelev) in 1994–1995; [Petar Stoyanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petar_Stoyanov) in 1997; [Rosen Plevneliev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosen_Plevneliev) in 2013 and 2014; [Rumen Radev](51575780.md) in 2017, 2021, 2022, and 2024; and [Iliana Iotova](34677899.md) in 2026.[^cite_note-2]

The president is elected for a five-year term and is restricted to two terms, even if they are non-consecutive. After an individual has served two terms as president, that individual would forever be barred from being elected to the presidency again under Bulgaria's Constitution.[^cite_note-\:0-3] The president addresses the nation on national television annually on [New Year's Eve](277922.md).[^cite_note-4]

## Election

### Eligibility for election

For a Bulgarian citizen to be able to run for the office of President of Bulgaria, they must fulfil the following conditions:[^cite_note-\:0-3]

- Must be a Bulgarian citizen by birth

- Must be at least 40 years old

- Must have lived in Bulgaria for the five years prior to the candidacy

- Must fulfil all conditions that would be required for election as a representative to Bulgaria's National Assembly

### Electoral system

The president is elected directly by the Bulgarian people in a [two-round election](25932.md) for a five-year term. If a candidate obtains at least 50% of the vote with at least 50% [voter turnout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voter_turnout), that candidate is elected. If this isn't the case, the top two candidates face off in a runoff election.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Restrictions

The president is barred from being a member of the [National Assembly](2122384.md), as well as holding any other government, public or private offices for the duration of their term. The president is also constitutionally forbidden from being involved in a leadership position of a [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) while in office.[^cite_note-\:0-3] The president is however allowed to be a member of a political party.

## Powers and privileges

The president of Bulgaria has a number of functions and powers that are regulated in Chapter 4 of the 1991 [Constitution of Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Bulgaria).

### Presidential powers

The following powers belong to the president of Bulgaria:[^cite_note-\:0-3]

- The ability to give, restore or revoke

  [^cite_note-6]

  &#x20;[Bulgarian citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_citizenship), and [refugee status](45547.md).

* Appoint and dismiss senior government officials

* Exercise the right to [pardon](288593.md) convicted criminals, as well as forgive 'uncollectible' debts owed to the government

* Rename villages, towns and cities, as well as objects of national importance

* Act as Supreme commander-in-chief of the [Bulgarian Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Armed_Forces)

* Represent Bulgaria at home and abroad

* Schedule the National and local elections within the intervals designated by law

* Veto any bill coming from the National Assembly by refusing to sign it after its passage in the assembly.

  [^cite_note-7]

* Declares war, [martial law](50477.md) or any other [state of emergency](298608.md) (This is done with the assistance of the Consultative Council on National Security)

* Grant and award the [orders, decorations, and medals of Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders,_decorations,_and_medals_of_Bulgaria)

### Immunity

The president enjoys blanket [legal immunity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_immunity) during his or her tenure and is not held responsible for any act performed while on duty, with the exception of treason or violation of the Bulgarian constitution. His or her authority may only be stripped via impeachment and may not be removed by any other institution. The president cannot be [detained](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detention_\(imprisonment\)) and may not be prosecuted.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Vice president

Main article: [Vice President of Bulgaria](4684983.md)

The president is assisted in their duties by the [vice president of Bulgaria](4684983.md). The vice president replaces the president in case of absence. Upon the death, resignation or removal from office of the president, the vice president assumes the presidential office until the end of the term. The Constitution permits the president to delegate to the vice president certain powers, notably: to appoint and dismiss certain officials, issue pardons and amnesty, provide citizenship and refugee status. The VP enjoys the same privileges of immunity as the president and can only be dismissed from his office under the same procedure.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Termination of office

According to the constitution, the mandate of the president is completed if and when:[^cite_note-\:0-3]

- The presidential term expires

- The president resigns before the Constitutional Court

- The president is permanently unable to perform his/her duties due to serious illness

- The president dies while in office

- The president is [impeached](15328.md)

### Impeachment

[Impeachment](15328.md) can begin if the president has committed [treason](31292.md) or has violated the [Constitution of Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Bulgaria), after at least a quarter of the members of the National Assembly deposit an accusatory act before the assembly. The act must be approved by a 2/3 [supermajority](638313.md) of MPs in order to be accepted. If accepted, the case is referred to the [Constitutional Court of Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Bulgaria), which must decide within a one-month time period whether the president is guilty or innocent of the crime they have been accused of by the Assembly. If the constitutional court finds that the president has committed treason or violated the constitution, the president is impeached and is stripped of their authority.[^cite_note-\:0-3]
