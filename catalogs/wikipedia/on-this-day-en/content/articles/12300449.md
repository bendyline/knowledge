**Steamboat Springs Airport** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **SBS**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **KSBS**, [FAA](11186.md) [LID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Location_identifier): **SBS**) (Bob Adams Field) is a city-owned [airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport) three miles northwest of [Steamboat Springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steamboat_Springs,_Colorado), in [Routt County, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Routt_County,_Colorado).[^cite_note-FAA-1] The [National Plan of Integrated Airport Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Plan_of_Integrated_Airport_Systems) for 2011–2015 [categorized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAA_airport_categories) it as a *[general aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_aviation)* facility.[^cite_note-2]

Larger commercial jet aircraft by scheduled passenger airlines operates into the [Yampa Valley Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yampa_Valley_Airport) (HDN) 25 miles (40 km) west of [Steamboat Springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steamboat_Springs,_Colorado) near [Hayden, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayden,_Colorado) which can handle [Boeing 737](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737) and [757](151995.md) jetliners as well as [Airbus A319](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A319) and [A320](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A320) jets. Year-round service to Denver is provided at HDN by [United Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Express) and [Southwest Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Airlines) while multiple carriers with mainline jet flights operate to several major cities during the winter ski season.

## Historical airline service

According to the [Official Airline Guide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_Airline_Guide) (OAG), Steamboat Springs Airport previously had scheduled passenger flights to Denver (DEN) operated by [Rocky Mountain Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Mountain_Airways) with small [de Havilland Canada DHC-6 Twin Otter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Havilland_Canada_DHC-6_Twin_Otter) aircraft during the mid 1970s followed by larger 50-passenger [de Havilland Canada DHC-7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Havilland_Canada_DHC-7) [Dash 7s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Havilland_Canada_Dash_7) by the late 1970s.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The twin engine Twin Otter turboprop as well as the four engine Dash 7 turboprop both feature [short takeoff and landing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STOL) ([STOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STOL)) performance which was required due to Steamboat Springs' relatively short runway and high elevation. Rocky Mountain Airways later operated Dash 7 service into the airport on behalf of [Continental Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Airlines) via a [code sharing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_sharing) agreement from 1986 through 1994 with up to ten flights a day to Denver.[^cite_note-5] In 1997, [Maverick Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maverick_Airways) was operating Dash 7 service to Denver on behalf of the current version of [Frontier Airlines](163354.md) via a code sharing agreement from January 1997 until their bankruptcy in May 1997.[^cite_note-6] Since Maverick Airways shut down, the airfield has remained a [general aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_aviation) airfield with no scheduled passenger airline service at the present time.

## Facilities

The airport covers 436 [acres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre) (176 [ha](24919513.md)) at an [elevation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elevation) of 6,882 feet (2,098 m). Its one [runway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway), 14/32, is 4,452 by 100 feet (1,357 x 30 m) asphalt.[^cite_note-FAA-1]

In 2008, the airport had 11,522 aircraft operations, average 31 per day: 95% [general aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_aviation) and 5% [air taxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_taxi). 82 aircraft were then based at this airport: 82% single-engine, 9% multi-engine, 2% jet, 6% [helicopter](8286923.md), and 1% glider.[^cite_note-FAA-1]

There is a small [terminal](423849.md) for [private aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_aviation) and several [hangars](1011776.md) on the property. There are about 30 spots for aircraft on the apron.

The [Civil Air Patrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Air_Patrol) has an office and aircraft at the airport. The Steamboat Springs Police Department provide security for the airport, while the Steamboat Springs Fire Department provides [firefighting](855608.md) and [emergency medical services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_medical_services). There are no [Aircraft Rescue and Firefighting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_Rescue_and_Firefighting) (ARFF) trucks at the airport. Snow removal equipment is operated by the airport operations department.

Steamboat has two non-precision instrument approach procedures; a RNAV(GPS)-E and a VOR/DME-C. The RNAV is the preferred approach with 1300/1 & 1/2 ceiling/vis requirements and both approaches are cat A/B only. HDN, 16NM to the west has an ILS approach with a 10,000ft runway.

There is no [air traffic control tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_traffic_control_tower). Aircraft communicate on a [Common Traffic Advisory Frequency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Traffic_Advisory_Frequency) (CTAF). The airport warns pilots about the high volume of wildlife that wander on airport property. Snow removal operations happen between 6\:00am and 6\:00pm during the winter season. The airport is closed when there is snow removal equipment on the runway.
