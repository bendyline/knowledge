The **speaker of the National Assembly of Hungary** ([Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_language): *Az Országgyűlés elnöke*, lit. 'President of the National Assembly', colloquially *házelnök*, lit. 'house-president') is the [presiding officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_\(politics\)) of the [National Assembly](585416.md) of [Hungary](13275.md). The current speaker is [Ágnes Forsthoffer](82940266.md), since 9 May 2026.

Since 1990, the speaker of the National Assembly also serves as [acting](691917.md) [President of Hungary](380393.md) if the elected president vacates their office before the expiration of their term or if the president is temporarily incapacitated.
