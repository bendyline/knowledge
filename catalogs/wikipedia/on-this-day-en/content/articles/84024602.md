The **2026 Southeast Asian haze crisis** is a large [air pollution](10934212.md) crisis and the current continuous transboundary haze season that affects several countries in [Southeast Asia](28741.md), which is still ongoing since 1 August 2026, including [Brunei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunei), [Indonesia](14579.md), [Malaysia](3607937.md), [Philippines](23440.md), [Thailand](30128.md), [Singapore](27318.md) and [Vietnam](202354.md).

## Background and causes

See also: [2019 Southeast Asian haze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Southeast_Asian_haze), [2026 East Malaysia haze](84024602.md), and [2026 Indonesia wildfires](84028953.md)

The haze is attributed to the ongoing [2026 Indonesia wildfires](84028953.md), particularly fires in parts of [Sumatra](28679.md) and [Kalimantan](165470.md). The forecasted ["super" El Niño](83914690.md) also intensifies drought and wildfire haze spread, challenging wildfire reduction.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Moreover, positive [Indian Ocean Dipole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean_Dipole) (IOD) amplifies the risk of wildfire and haze.[^cite_note-\:2-5] Combination both El Niño and positive IOD causes drier ground cover and adds the risk for severe fires in September–October.[^cite_note-6] During periods of dry weather, vegetation and peatlands become highly susceptible to fire. Smoke and fine particulate matter generated by these fires can be transported by prevailing winds across national borders, affecting air quality in neighbouring countries such as Malaysia and Singapore.[^cite_note-\:2-5]

### Malaysia

The [government of Sarawak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Sarawak), in response to the rising API levels, activated its Sarawak State Disaster Management Committee (SDMC or JPBNS) to monitor air quality and issue protective measures for its citizens, including face mask mandates and the cancellation or closure of events and public buildings.[^cite_note-malaymail-8][^cite_note-9]

On August 21, [Sarawak](28258.md) continued to experience the second wave of transboundary haze season, with [Serian](990538.md) surging the highest [Air Pollutant Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pollution_index) (API) reading in Sarawak at 242, placing the area in the "Very Unhealthy" category. The SDMC data showed eight other areas in Sarawak recording the unhealthy air quality, underscoring the widening impact of the transboundary haze. These affected areas were Serian at 191, [Lubok Antu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubok_Antu) (189), [Kuching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuching) (188), [Sri Aman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simanggang) and [Lundu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lundu,_Sarawak) (178), [Samarahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kota_Samarahan) (171), [Tebedu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tebedu) (164), [Sarikei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarikei) (153), and [Sibu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sibu) (125).[^cite_note-10]

The SDMC said that they were closely monitoring weather conditions, air quality, hotspots, the risk of forest and peatland fires, and the status of the state's water supply was continuing in coordination with relevant agencies. The intensified monitoring reflects growing concern over the wider environmental and public health risks posed by the worsening haze, particularly in areas close to the [Indonesian border](165470.md).[^cite_note-thevibes-11]

On August 29, Serian recorded a hazardous air quality (302). The condition worsens, and on 4 September 2026, *[Yang di-Pertuan Agong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_di-Pertuan_Agong)*, [Sultan Ibrahim](8717363.md), with advice from Prime Minister [Anwar Ibrahim](19264815.md) and Sarawak Premier [Abang Johari Openg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abang_Johari_Openg), declares emergency after the AQI reaches over 500.[^cite_note-12] [Cloud seeding](449660.md) operations have been approved in affected areas to mitigate the air quality issues and supplement the area's available water resources.[^cite_note-\:1-13][^cite_note-14]

On September 18, according to Environment Department's Air Pollutant Index Management System (APIMS), 34 of 68 stations nationwide recording unsafe levels, with several regions edging dangerously close to the "Very Unhealthy" level. Johan Setia in [Selangor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selangor) recorded the nation's worst air quality at 198, followed closely by [Kuching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuching) (192) and [Samarahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kota_Samarahan) (184) in [Sarawak](28258.md). Heavy haze also gripped [Peninsular Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peninsular_Malaysia), with Nilai logging a reading of 181, Seri Manjung at 176, [Putrajaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putrajaya) at 173, and both [Shah Alam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shah_Alam) and [Seremban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seremban) at 171. In Sarawak, [Serian](990538.md) also recorded an unhealthy reading of 180. Out of 68 stations nationwide, only three logged "Good" air quality, all located in [East Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Malaysia): [Limbang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limbang), Sarawak (49); [Sandakan](1732138.md), Sabah (46); and [Kota Kinabalu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kota_Kinabalu), Sabah (44).[^cite_note-15]

### Brunei Darussalam



Haze covering over Ong Sum Ping Armada Properties buildings, Brunei Darussalam

The [Department of Environment, Parks and Recreation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Environment,_Parks_and_Recreation) under the [Ministry of Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Development_\(Brunei\)) on 31 August 2026 at 11 am recorded 94 [pollutant standard index (PSI)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollutant_Standards_Index) for [Brunei-Muara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunei%E2%80%93Muara_District), 79 at [Tutong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutong_District), 95 at [Belait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belait_District) and 69 at [Temburong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temburong_District) which was considered in the 'moderate' category (51 PSI - 100 PSI).[^cite_note-16]

On 1 September 2026 at 4 pm, after taking account of both [particulate matter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particulate_matter) (PM10) and particulate matter 2.5 (PM2.5), readings from two districts were categorized in the 'Very unhealthy' category (201 PSI - 300 PSI). [Brunei-Muara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunei%E2%80%93Muara_District) recorded 215 PSI, 168 at [Tutong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutong_District), 211 at [Belait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belait_District) and 164 at [Temburong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temburong_District).[^cite_note-17] A [2026–27 Malaysia FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_Malaysia_FA_Cup) home fixture between [DPMM FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPMM_FC) and [Terengganu FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terengganu_FC) on this date was postponed due to the worsening conditions.[^cite_note-18]

The [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_\(Brunei\)) and [Ministry of Religious Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Religious_Affairs_\(Brunei\)) are advising all government and private institutions to implement a Home-Based Learning (HBL) from 2 to 3 September 2026.[^cite_note-19]

### Singapore

Night shot of Tanjong Rhu with a clear sky

[Tanjong Rhu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanjong_Rhu) as viewed with clear skies in the early morning of 1 August 2026.

Haze over Tanjong Rhu

Haze settled over Tanjong Rhu in the afternoon of 14 September 2026.

Photographs taken in the vicinity of Victoria Street.

In May 2026, the [National Environment Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Environment_Agency) (NEA) and [Meteorological Service Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteorological_Service_Singapore) (MSS) warned that there is an increased likelihood that Singapore could experience haze from June to October due to the effects of [El Niño–Southern Oscillation](317311.md) and the [Indian Ocean Dipole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean_Dipole).[^cite_note-\:2-5][^cite_note-20]

On 4 September 2026 at 7 am, the Central Region[^cite_note-21] recorded a [PSI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollutant_Standards_Index) of 101, categorising it in the 'Unhealthy' category (100 - 200 PSI), this was the first time since October 2023 that the PSI hit the 'Unhealthy' category.[^cite_note-22] At 9 pm, the [Eastern Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Region,_Singapore) recorded a PSI of 102, the Central Region recorded a PSI of 120, categorizing both in the 'Unhealthy' category, while the [Western Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Region,_Singapore) recorded a PSI of 97, the [Northern Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Region,_Singapore) 86, and the [Southern Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Region,_Singapore) 85, placing all three in the 'Moderate' category (51 - 100 PSI).[^cite_note-23]

The Haze Task Force announced on 4 September 2026, that should the PSI enter the 'Very Unhealthy' category (201 - 300 PSI), air-conditioned rooms in all [community centres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_centre#Urban_planning) (CCs) and selected residents’ committee centres (RCs) will be on standby. The NEA also announced that it would begin providing daily haze advisories from the evening of September 4, and will include the additional 24-hour PSI forecast.[^cite_note-24]

On 5 September around midnight, the Western Region recorded a PSI of 101, categorizing it in the 'Unhealthy' category (100 - 200 PSI). At 7 am, the Western Region recorded a PSI of 102, and the Central Region 119, placing both in the 'Unhealthy' category, while the Eastern Region recorded a PSI of 99, the Northern Region 85, and the Southern Region 88, placing all three in the 'Moderate' category.[^cite_note-25]

On 14 September at 4 pm, the Central Region recorded a PSI of 115, while the West and East regions both recorded a PSI of 102, placing all three regions in the 'Unhealthy' category. The North and South regions recorded a PSI of 87 and 91, respectively, placing both regions in the 'Moderate' category.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] By the morning of 15 September, all five regions were in the unhealthy range, which was the first time it has happened since September 2019, with the Northern Region recording a PSI of 104 at 6 am.[^cite_note-cna1-28] However, air quality improved as the day progressed, with the Southern and Northern region going back into the moderate range by 1pm.[^cite_note-cna1-28] By the midnight of 16 September, the air quality for the Eastern and Western region went back to moderate range,[^cite_note-29] and the Central region doing so at 11am.[^cite_note-30]

On 17 September, the air quality in the Central region briefly went back to unhealthy levels,[^cite_note-31] but returned to normal levels and continued to improve on 18 September.[^cite_note-32] However, the Central region went back to the unhealthy range on 19 September at 1pm.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34]

PSI readings went back to the unhealthy range on 26 September in the Central region, and 27 September in the East and West regions.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] This is after the air quality had seen improvements as a result of rain in the country. Due to the haze, the [half-marathon](1019031.md) of [Decathlon's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decathlon_\(retailer\)) inaugural Kiprun race on 27 September was shortened from the standard 21.1 kilometres (13.1 mi) to 10 kilometres (6.2 mi), with the awards ceremony being cancelled and all categories becoming non-competitive.[^cite_note-37]

### Philippines

In early September 2026, the haze had reached and affected air quality across several parts of the Philippines after smoke from fires in Kalimantan, Indonesia was carried by prevailing monsoon winds. [Metro Manila](72340.md), [Calabarzon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calabarzon) and [Mimaropa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimaropa) were among the affected areas, with several cities recording *very unhealthy* to *acutely unhealthy* air quality levels. In [Quezon City](284223.md), face-to-face classes in public schools were suspended due to hazardous air pollution, while similar suspensions were implemented in parts of [Metro Cebu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Cebu) as the haze persisted.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

Authorities advised residents to limit outdoor exposure and wear properly fitted particulate masks, such as [N95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N95_respirator) or [KN95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_filter_\(respirator\)) masks, particularly in areas with unhealthy air quality. The haze also affected parts of the Visayas and Mindanao, including Iloilo, Cebu and [Zamboanga City](25167165.md), highlighting the wide geographical reach of the transboundary pollution.[^cite_note-40]

### Vietnam

On 14 September, the haze reached southern Vietnam as the winds over the southern ASEAN region were forecasted to blow mainly from the southeast.[^cite_note-41]

## Impacts

### Cancellations of outdoor activities and events

Several outdoor activities and events in [Kuching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuching), [Samarahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kota_Samarahan), and [Serian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serian,_Sarawak) have been postponed or delayed due to the ongoing haze season in [Sarawak](28258.md). This including 53rd Sarawak Schools Sports Council (MSS Sarawak) Athletics Championship 2026 which is actually scheduled and take place on August 10 until August 14, but postponed due to unhealthy air quality in Samarahan. Sarawak Education Department (*Jabatan Pendidikan Sarawak*) also reminded to all district education offices (*Pejabat Pendidikan Daerah*) and all educational institutions under its purview to take seriously preventive measures to safeguard students and school staff during [hot weather](293270.md) and [haze](998156.md) conditions.[^cite_note-42]

On August 19, Deputy Minister in the Sarawak Premier's Department, Datuk Dr. Abdul Rahman Junaidi, said the 2026 Sarawak-level Maulidur Rasul celebration, will be held at Dewan Sentral Sarikei on August 25, which is will feature only a gathering, without a procession due to the [unhealthy haze situation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeast_Asian_haze) and [hot weather](293270.md). This Maulidur Rasul celebration was originally to be scheduled and take place at the Sarikei Sports Complex, but the venue and programme were changed as a precautionary measure.[^cite_note-43]

### Closure of schools and education institutions

On August 12, [Sarawak government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Sarawak) announced a total of 108 schools in [Serian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serian,_Sarawak) area have been closed after the [air pollutant index (API)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Pollution_Index) exceeded over 200. [Deputy Premier of Sarawak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Sarawak), Datuk Amar Douglas Uggah Embas said this solution was in line with the National Haze Action Plan (*Pelan Tindakan Jerebu Kebangsaan*), which allowed the immediate closure of all schools, kindergartens and childcare centres once the API breaches 200 and above. At the same time, it is also as the precautionary solutions to protect the safety, health and well-being of all students and school staff in Serian. To ensure the continuous learning for the students, home-based teaching and learning (PdPR) will be implemented during the closure period.[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45]

On August 20, despite the second wave of transboundary haze situation, the deteriorating air quality in [Sarawak](28258.md) has prompted the closure of all 591 schools under 10 district education offices across Sarawak, amid growing concerns over the impact of the transboundary haze on pupils and students. Sarawak Education Department said in a statement yesterday that the closures involved 509 primary schools with a combined enrolment of 107,590 pupils, as well as 82 secondary schools involving 89,733 students. The affected schools fall under the [Kuching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuching), [Padawan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padawan,_Sarawak), [Bau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bau,_Sarawak), [Lundu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lundu,_Sarawak), [Samarahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kota_Samarahan), [Simunjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simunjan_District), [Serian](990538.md), [Sri Aman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simanggang), [Lubok Antu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubok_Antu) and [Betong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betong,_Sarawak) district education offices.[^cite_note-thevibes-11]

Meanwhile, in [Indonesia](14579.md), dozens of schools in [Pontianak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontianak) were closed on August 11, due to the spreading haze caused from the wildfires and open burning activities in [West Kalimantan](453636.md) and [Central Kalimantan](453643.md). The mayor of Pontianak, Edi Rusdi Kamtono, ordered all schools should be closed due to the smoke wafting over the city from fires elsewhere.[^cite_note-46]

## Responses

As [Malaysia](3607937.md) and [Singapore](27318.md) requested [Indonesia](14579.md) to immediately conduct firefighting operations, [DPR RI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPR_RI) member [Marwan Dasopang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marwan_Dasopang?action=edit\&redlink=1) (PKB-North Sumatra II) has instead criticised both countries for not contributing because there were Malaysian and Singaporean palm oil companies operating and told both countries to "not just protest".[^cite_note-47]

Former [Governor of Jakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Jakarta) and [2024 presidential candidate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Indonesian_presidential_election) [Anies Baswedan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anies_Baswedan) teased the government of Indonesia as fires spread into neighbouring countries. As previously during the campaign, Anies debated that "the wind doesn't have an ID card' when asked about air pollution in Jakarta to which he was ridiculed by [Prabowo Subianto](2829084.md). As condition became worse, Anies' words were vindicated[^cite_note-48] and he fired another statement, "the wind doesn't have a passport".[^cite_note-49]

Prime Minister of Malaysia [Anwar Ibrahim](19264815.md) and Sultan of Brunei [Hassanal Bolkiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassanal_Bolkiah) jointly agreed to bring up the issue towards [ASEAN](106539.md).[^cite_note-50] Former Malaysian health minister [Khairy Jamaluddin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khairy_Jamaluddin) labelled Indonesia a "[banana republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana_republic)" and a "proper [third-world country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_World)" criticising the country for failing to take proper action following repeated crises since 1990.[^cite_note-51]

After a trilateral summit with Indonesian defence minister [Sjafrie Sjamsoeddin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sjafrie_Sjamsoeddin) and Australian deputy prime minister [Richard Marles](4386805.md), Japanese defence minister [Shinjirō Koizumi](27407451.md) announced that Japan will dispatch [JS Kunisaki](31244097.md) along with 3 [JGSDF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JGSDF) [CH-47](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CH-47) heavy transport helicopters to aid firefighting efforts in Indonesia.[^cite_note-52]

### Cloud seeding

The [Sarawak government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Sarawak) approved the plans for large-scale [cloud seeding](449660.md) operation in effort to increase the potential of heavy rainfall in the targeted areas that records hazy and unhealthy air quality levels in [Sarawak](28258.md), and also to subsequently help maintain the availability of the region's water resources. As a result of the implementation of cloud seeding operation, the haze situation in Sarawak shows an improvement from unhealthy into moderate air quality level.

According to Malaysia's Deputy Prime Minister, Datuk Seri Dr Ahmad Zahid Hamidi, the National Disaster Management Agency (NADMA) will take a proactive approach to extensive cloud seeding operations in [Sarawak](28258.md) rather than wait for requests from the [Sarawak state government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Sarawak). He also said the approach would ensure extensive [cloud seeding](449660.md) operations were carried out at the appropriate time and locations, particularly when water levels in catchment areas fell to critical levels.

On disaster-response assets, he said the meeting also agreed that NADMA would take over the procurement of disaster-related assets for use by enforcement agencies including the Royal Malaysia Police (PDRM), Fire and Rescue Department of Malaysia (JBPM) and Civil Defence Force (APM). This was to ensure sufficient assets, such as boats and heavy vehicles, were available to deal with disasters, thereby strengthening the country's disaster preparedness.[^cite_note-53]

### Sanctions against forest fire perpetrators

[Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Indonesia) President [Prabowo Subianto](2829084.md) has called for very strict regulations and sanctions against slash-and-burn land clearances, branding the practice as "ecological terrorism", as the country struggles to contain wildfires that have been raging for weeks. [Indonesia](14579.md) is battling its most intense wildfires in 11 years, with "[Super El Nino](83914690.md)" weather conditions parching the country's forests and fields.

The islands of [Borneo](4518.md) and [Sumatra](28679.md) have been the worst hit, with the hazardous haze causing a spike in respiratory illnesses. Transboundary pollution has also caused unhealthy air conditions in neighbouring countries including [Singapore](27318.md) and [Malaysia](3607937.md). During a meeting with his government cabinet, Prabowo said there will be zero tolerance for people or corporations found responsible for starting wildfires. He also instructed more regional governments to immediately abolish exemptions that allow smallholders to clear small plots of land using fire.

Prabowo also called for the police to launch criminal investigations against corporations found to be violating fire safeguards. Following Prabowo's remarks, Indonesian police official Mohammad Irhamni told reporters on Thursday (September 17) that police have launched 219 criminal investigations linked to the fires, with 162 people named as suspects so far. He added that the police were also investigating 95 companies, but he did not disclose how many of them are suspected of criminal offences.[^cite_note-54]
