***Spider-Man: Brand New Day*** is a 2026 American [superhero film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superhero_film) based on the [Marvel Comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Comics) character [Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man). Produced by [Columbia Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Pictures), [Marvel Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Studios), and [Pascal Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascal_Pictures), and distributed by [Sony Pictures Releasing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Releasing), it is [the 38th film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_films) in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) (MCU) and the fourth film in the [MCU *Spider-Man* film series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_\(2017_film_series\)) following *[Spider-Man: No Way Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_No_Way_Home)* (2021). The film was directed by [Destin Daniel Cretton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destin_Daniel_Cretton), written by [Chris McKenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_McKenna_\(writer\)) and [Erik Sommers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Sommers), and stars [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland) as [Peter Parker / Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Parker_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) alongside [Zendaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zendaya), [Sadie Sink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadie_Sink), [Jacob Batalon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Batalon), [Jon Bernthal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Bernthal), [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh), [Tramell Tillman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tramell_Tillman), [Marisa Tomei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marisa_Tomei), and [Mark Ruffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Ruffalo). In the film, Parker is on his own and dedicated to protecting [New York City](645042.md) as the hero Spider-Man. His personal struggles and overworking cause his superpowers to evolve while he faces a new [telepathic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telepath) threat.

Sony was developing a fourth MCU *Spider-Man* film by August 2019, and producer [Amy Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Pascal) revealed in November 2021 that it was intended to be the first in a new trilogy starring Holland. Work on the story began that December, with Holland more involved in development than he was for the previous films. McKenna and Sommers returned as writers by February 2023, Cretton was hired to direct by October 2024, and the title—derived from the 2008 comic book storyline "[Brand New Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brand_New_Day_\(comics\))" that reset Spider-Man's status quo—was announced in March 2025. The producers wanted Holland's Parker to be a "proper Spider-Man" for the first time, on his own and fighting street-level crime. Cretton rewrote the script with [Justin Kuritzkes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Kuritzkes). Ahead of filming, Bernthal and Ruffalo were revealed to be reprising their MCU roles of [Frank Castle / Punisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Castle_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) and [Bruce Banner / Hulk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Banner_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)). Filming took place from August to December 2025, with location filming in [Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow), Scotland, and throughout England. Soundstage work occurred at [Pinewood Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinewood_Studios) in England.

*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* premiered at the [Dolby Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolby_Theatre) in [Hollywood, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood,_Los_Angeles), on July 27, 2026, and was released in the United States on July 31 as part of [Phase Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_Six) of the MCU. It received largely positive reviews from critics, with praise for Holland's performance, the action sequences, and the more mature, character-driven story compared to the previous MCU *Spider-Man* films. The film has grossed \$2.498 billion worldwide and set multiple box office records. It became the [highest-grossing *Spider-Man* film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_in_film#Box_office), the [highest-grossing film of 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_film#Highest-grossing_films), the [highest-grossing film in the United States and Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films_in_the_United_States_and_Canada), and the [third-highest grossing film of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films).

## Plot

After Dr. [Stephen Strange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Strange_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\))'s magic spell makes the world forget that [Peter Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Parker_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) exists, he chooses to protect his girlfriend [Michelle "MJ" Jones-Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MJ_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) and best friend [Ned Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ned_Leeds_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) by not reintroducing himself to them. Over the next four years, Parker anonymously protects New York City as its celebrated hero Spider-Man. He defeats major criminals, including [Mac Gargan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mac_Gargan_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) and [the Hand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hand_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) ninjas, earning the trust of police detective [Jean DeWolff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_DeWolff_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)). Parker regularly visits his aunt [May](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_Parker_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\))'s grave.

Parker stops [Frank Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Castle_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) from causing collateral damage in pursuit of a tank that crashes into a [Department of Damage Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Damage_Control) (DODC) prison. The driver is possessed by a [telepath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telepath) who [body hops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_transfer_\(biological\)) from person to person to help Gargan escape, but is unable to enter Parker's mind. [Yelena Belova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yelena_Belova_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) informs Parker that multiple DODC facilities have been attacked by someone searching for "V-Max". DODC director [Bill Metzger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Metzger_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)), now using the Hand for security, denies any knowledge of "V-Max" to Parker.

Ned and MJ move back to New York after graduating from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT). Parker encounters them and sees MJ kiss her new boyfriend. Returning home in distress, he wakes up the next day with heightened senses and [spinnerets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinnerets) in his wrists. When the telepath attacks another DODC facility with a possessed Gargan, Parker loses control of his new abilities and brutally beats Gargan, inadvertently injuring a police officer. After DeWolff tells him to regain control, Parker learns from [Bruce Banner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Banner_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)), who has an inhibitor device that uses [siRNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SiRNA) to suppress his super-powered [alter ego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alter_ego), the Hulk, and builds his own inhibitor. While he tests it, the telepath finds his apartment and learns about MJ from an unsent reintroduction letter he wrote to her. Parker races to get MJ to safety, but the telepath possesses and almost kills her. He rescues MJ and identifies the telepath as teenager [Jean Grey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Grey_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)).

Parker takes MJ to Castle's hideout for protection, where she helps complete his inhibitor and a universal inhibitor. Jean attacks the DODC's headquarters with a possessed Banner, whose inhibitor is removed, causing him to become the Hulk. While Parker fights the Hulk, Jean possesses Castle to open a vault hiding DODC agents. Parker calms the Hulk, then blocks Jean's powers with the universal inhibitor while Metzger tranquilizes her. Jean mentions "Sara" before losing consciousness; Metzger claims he remembers thinking of a colleague by that name while possessed by Jean. Parker returns to MJ, who has found the letter. He apologizes for not telling her and declares his love, but MJ does not remember him nor reciprocate his feelings. Despite this, she invites him to her and Ned's apartment, where a masked Parker bonds with them both. After MJ describes not remembering anything while possessed, Parker realizes Metzger lied. Parker contacts Yelena, who learns from redacted DODC files that [Sara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Grey_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) is Jean's sister, another telepath kidnapped by the DODC.

Metzger experiments on Jean at a DODC facility on [Roosevelt Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roosevelt_Island) that Parker infiltrates. Jean deduces that "V-Max" is the weathered label on the air vent in her cell, the last thing Sara saw—and mentally communicated to Jean—before she died. Enraged, Jean creates a [psionic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psionic) field that extends beyond the facility, freezing everyone within it except for Parker, including MJ and Ned. Jean rebuffs Parker's apologies and attacks him using the Hand. He removes his inhibitor and embraces his new abilities, defeating the Hand and stopping Jean from killing Metzger. Parker lets her enter his mind, where she experiences a memory of May saying she loves him for who he is. Parker and Jean reconcile over their respective losses. He senses Castle shooting at Jean from a distance and shields her, sustaining a critical injury. Jean drops the psionic field.

Parker wakes up in a hospital with a remorseful Castle, who says Jean remotely kept him alive with her powers. Jean leaves the city, Metzger disappears, and the DODC comes under investigation. At May's grave, Parker vows to stop trying to do everything alone, later agreeing to let DeWolff handle a robbery while he recovers. Peter encounters Ned and greets him with their secret handshake; Ned performs it, looking confused, until they complete it and he says Peter's name.

## Production

### Development

#### Interest and writing delay

Refer to caption

Star [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland) (pictured in July 2016) was more involved in *Brand New Day*'s development than he was for the previous [MCU *Spider-Man* films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_\(2017_film_series\)).

[Sony Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures) said in August 2019 that it would not be making future [*Spider-Man* films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_\(2017_film_series\)) with [Marvel Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Studios) and its president, [Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige), after negotiations to update [the production deal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_films#Spider-Man_films) between the two companies failed. Sony co-produced *[Spider-Man: Homecoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Homecoming)* (2017) and *[Spider-Man: Far From Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Far_From_Home)* (2019) with Marvel Studios and [Amy Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Pascal),[^cite_note-NoDealAug2019Sony-54] and was reported to be developing two more films.[^cite_note-NoDealAug2019-55] Following a negative fan reaction, Sony and Marvel's parent company [Disney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney) returned to negotiations and announced a new agreement at the end of September, allowing Marvel Studios and Feige to produce *[Spider-Man: No Way Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_No_Way_Home)* (2021) with Pascal.[^cite_note-NewDealSep2019-56] In February 2021, [Peter Parker / Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Parker_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) star [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland) said *No Way Home* was the final film in his contract but he hoped to continue playing the character,[^cite_note-HollandFeb2021Collider-57][^cite_note-HollandFeb2021GQ-58] and later added that any further *Spider-Man* films would differ from the previous trilogy and feature a tonal change.[^cite_note-HollandOct2021-59]

Around the release of *No Way Home* in December 2021, Holland said he was unsure if he should continue making *Spider-Man* films, feeling he would have done something wrong if he were still portraying the character in his thirties. He was interested in a film being made about [Miles Morales / Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miles_Morales) instead,[^cite_note-HollandNov2021-60] and added that he would be saddened to leave the franchise but he had achieved what he wanted with the character.[^cite_note-HollandDec2021-61] Despite these comments, Pascal hoped to continue working with Holland on future *Spider-Man* films,[^cite_note-HollandNov2021-60] and said work on the first film in a new trilogy was about to begin.[^cite_note-PascalNov2021-62] Aaron Couch at *[The Hollywood Reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter)* said Sony did not yet have official plans for further MCU *Spider-Man* films but hoped to continue its collaboration with Marvel Studios.[^cite_note-THRNov2021-63] Pascal hoped the partnership between Sony and Disney would last forever, and Feige promised that it would not break down again as it did during *No Way Home*'s development. He said Holland would return in another MCU film and confirmed that Marvel Studios had begun discussing the story for the next *Spider-Man* film with Pascal, Sony, and Disney.[^cite_note-FeigePascalDec2021-64]

By April 2022, Sony was expecting director [Jon Watts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Watts) to return from the previous trilogy after he exited as director of Marvel Studios' [*Fantastic Four* film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fantastic_Four:_First_Steps) (2025).[^cite_note-WattsFFExit-65] Sony Pictures CEO and chairman [Tom Rothman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Rothman) hoped the "whole group" would return, including Watts, Holland, and [Zendaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zendaya)—Holland's then-girlfriend and later wife who portrays [Michelle "MJ" Jones-Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MJ_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)).[^cite_note-RothmanMay2022-66] In February 2023, Feige said writers were working on a story with "big ideas",[^cite_note-FeigeFeb2023-67] but this was put on hold in May because of the [2023 Writers Guild of America strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Writers_Guild_of_America_strike). Pascal said work would resume after the strike concluded.[^cite_note-PascalMay2023-68] Holland said he was in meetings about ideas and "ambitions" for the film,[^cite_note-HollandMay2026Empire-69] and these were also put on hold due to the strike.[^cite_note-HollandJun2023Variety-70] He was happy with the creative reasoning for why a fourth film should be made, but was apprehensive about the stigma surrounding fourth films. He felt they "hit a home run" with the first three films,[^cite_note-HollandJun2023THR-71] calling *No Way Home* the "perfect send-off",[^cite_note-HollandJun2026Playlist-72] and part of him wanted to exit the franchise on that high note and "pass the baton" to another actor.[^cite_note-HollandJun2023THR-71] Holland only wanted to return if they could "do justice to the character".[^cite_note-HollandJun2023Variety-70]

#### Restarting active work

The writers' strike ended in September 2023,[^cite_note-WGAStrikeEnds-73] and Holland confirmed in November that discussions had resumed.[^cite_note-HollandNov2023-74] He had more input on the story than with the previous films because he was involved in the creative process much earlier.[^cite_note-HollandApr2024-75] Holland tried to understand fan sentiment on the internet because "we make these movies for \[the fans]", and said fans were interested in a more relatable film where Spider-Man does not have to save the world.[^cite_note-HollandSep2025LADBible-76][^cite_note-HollandJun2026RT-77] He presented this "vibe" to the studios,[^cite_note-HollandSep2025Esquire-78] who also believed a more character-focused approach made sense after struggling to determine how they could go "bigger" than *No Way Home*'s [multiverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiverse_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\))-based [crossover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossover_\(fiction\)) story.[^cite_note-RothmanAug2026-79] Holland [pitched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_\(filmmaking\)) that Parker's powers should go through puberty-like changes, which the creative team liked and developed further.[^cite_note-HollandMay2026Empire-69] Another proposal was to give him a pet dog, which was rejected.[^cite_note-DogScrappedIdea-80]

Journalist [Jeff Sneider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Sneider) reported in early 2024 that [Justin Lin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Lin) had been in contention to direct;[^cite_note-SneiderFilming-81] that [Chris McKenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_McKenna_\(writer\)) and [Erik Sommers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Sommers) had returned from the prior three films to write the script;[^cite_note-SneiderMcKennaSommers-82] and that filming was set to begin in September or October of that year, delaying filming for the [third season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_season_3) of *[Euphoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphoria_\(American_TV_series\))* (2026) to accommodate Zendaya's schedule.[^cite_note-SneiderFilming-81] In April, Holland said they were giving the writers the time they needed to work on the script following the strike.[^cite_note-HollandApr2024-75] Feige said in July that a script draft was being submitted "relatively soon",[^cite_note-FeigeJuly2024Draft-83] and Watts might not direct due to his schedule.[^cite_note-FeigeJuly2024Watts-84] Watts ultimately chose not to direct the film.[^cite_note-NoWatts-85]

Destin Daniel Cretton pictured in July 2019

[Destin Daniel Cretton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destin_Daniel_Cretton) was hired to direct the film after working on other [Marvel Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Studios) projects.

After further work on the script, the studios began meeting with directors. [Destin Daniel Cretton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destin_Daniel_Cretton) was the top choice to direct by September 2024.[^cite_note-CrettonTalksDeadline-86][^cite_note-CrettonTalksTHR-87] He directed the film *[Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shang-Chi_and_the_Legend_of_the_Ten_Rings)* (2021) for Marvel Studios and signed an overall deal with them in December 2021 that included directing a sequel to that film and developing the [Disney+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney+) series *[Wonder Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_Man_\(TV_series\))* (2026). Cretton was also hired to direct the MCU crossover film *[Avengers: The Kang Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_The_Kang_Dynasty)* in July 2022,[^cite_note-CrettonTKDVariety-88] but stepped down in November 2023;[^cite_note-CrettonTKDExitTHR-89] *The Kang Dynasty* was subsequently reworked into *[Avengers: Doomsday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Doomsday)* (2026).[^cite_note-SDCC2024Avengers-90] Cretton later said he did not feel like the right fit for that story.[^cite_note-CrettonAug2026Wrap-18] He discussed other potential projects with Feige and the fourth *Spider-Man* film was raised as a possibility.[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026IW-91] It was said to be the priority for Marvel Studios over a sequel to *Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings*.[^cite_note-CrettonTalksVariety-92] Feige and Pascal informed Cretton of their plans for a more grounded story that focused on Parker's isolation following the events of *No Way Home*, and he was interested in exploring the character's loneliness.[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026Credits-11] As Cretton entered talks to direct in September, Holland and Zendaya were also reportedly in talks to return, McKenna and Sommers were confirmed to be writing, and filming was expected to begin in early 2025.[^cite_note-CrettonTalksDeadline-86][^cite_note-CrettonTalksTHR-87]

In October 2024, Holland was cast in *[The Odyssey](78079604.md)* (2026),[^cite_note-HollandOct2024Schedule-93] and Sony approved his request to delay filming by six months so he could complete that film first.[^cite_note-HollandJun2026GoodHang-94][^cite_note-HollandKuritzkes-95] Cretton denied that he only joined the film because of this delay, but appreciated the extra development time.[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026IW-91] Holland was excited after reading a script draft with Zendaya, but he felt further work was needed,[^cite_note-HollandOct2024-96] and said the script was "going in a hundred different directions" before Cretton's hiring.[^cite_note-PlaylistJul2026-97] He confirmed that filming would now begin in mid-2025,[^cite_note-HollandOct2024Filming-98] and said it would be challenging but achievable to fit into the overall plan for the MCU.[^cite_note-HollandOct2024-96] At the end of October, Holland and Cretton were confirmed to have signed on and the film was scheduled for release on July 24, 2026.[^cite_note-Jul2026Date-5] [Justin Kuritzkes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Kuritzkes), who worked with Zendaya on the film *[Challengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Challengers_\(film\))* (2024), rewrote the script with Cretton during the additional development period,[^cite_note-HollandJun2026GoodHang-94][^cite_note-HollandKuritzkes-95][^cite_note-KuritzkesWrap-99] after McKenna and Sommers left to work on *Doomsday* and *[Avengers: Secret Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Secret_Wars)* (2027). Cretton said Kuritzkes brought a different perspective and wrote "some of the most beautiful scenes", including the memory Parker has of his aunt [May](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_Parker_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)). Kuritzkes continued working with Cretton until the film was finished,[^cite_note-CrettonAug2026Wrap-18] and received an offscreen "additional literary material" credit; McKenna and Sommers were officially credited as writers.[^cite_note-WGACredits-100]

Throughout late 2024, Sneider reported on shifting production schedules and potential changes to Zendaya's role due to her commitments to *Euphoria*'s third season, *The Odyssey*, and the film *[Dune: Part Three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune:_Part_Three)* (2026), which were all scheduled to be filmed in 2025.[^cite_note-Sneider2026Release-101][^cite_note-SneiderDec2024Updates-102] Katcy Stephan at *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\))* also reported that Zendaya's role in the next *Spider-Man* film was unclear following her November 2024 casting in *The Odyssey*.[^cite_note-ZendayaReturnUnclear-103] Sneider reported that [Charlie Cox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Cox) would reprise his role as [Matt Murdock / Daredevil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Murdock_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) from *No Way Home* and other MCU media,[^cite_note-SneiderDec2024Updates-102] but Cox denied his involvement in October 2025.[^cite_note-CoxDenial-104]

#### Story

Holland and Cretton described *Brand New Day* as the start of a new chapter for Parker that would have a more mature tone than the previous trilogy.[^cite_note-HollandJun2026Playlist-72][^cite_note-HollandSep2025Complex-128][^cite_note-CrettonJan2026CB-129] Those films explored an expanded version of the [first act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-act_structure) of [Sam Raimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Raimi)'s film *[Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_\(2002_film\))* (2002)—which shows Maguire's [Peter Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Parker_\(2002_film_series_character\)) in high school—that concludes with Holland's Parker living on his own at the end of *No Way Home*.[^cite_note-FeigePascalJul2026Collider-130] Feige said this was a promise that audiences would see him as a "proper Spider-Man" for the first time, with "classic elements" from the comics and Spider-Man fighting street-level crime rather than dealing with "world-ending events".[^cite_note-FeigeBNDJul2025-6][^cite_note-FeigeMay2026Empire-7] Marvel wanted to include other street-level heroes that Holland's Spider-Man had not interacted with before, such as the Punisher.[^cite_note-FeigeBNDJul2025-6] Unlike the previous trilogy, which used each film's major MCU co-star as a mentor for Parker, *Brand New Day* depicts him having more of a coworker relationship with the supporting characters. McKenna and Sommers said this was part of Parker growing up and no longer looking to more experienced heroes for guidance.[^cite_note-McKennaSommersAug2026Wrap-131]

*No Way Home* ends with Dr. [Stephen Strange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Strange_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) casting a spell to make the world forget that Parker exists. MJ and Ned remember their interactions with Spider-Man but not who he is.[^cite_note-HollandJun2026IGN-132] Cretton said there were many discussions about the mechanics of the spell, and they followed set rules such as not showing any pictures that Parker should appear in.[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026IW-91] *Brand New Day* is set four years after the spell was cast,[^cite_note-FeigePascalJul2026Collider-130] allowing the characters to grow up since *No Way Home*.[^cite_note-DoGCovers-133] Holland said Parker is in "the darkest chapter of his life",[^cite_note-ParkerDarkestChapter-134] fully dedicating himself to being Spider-Man and neglecting his civilian life. He said Parker's inability to [balance professional and personal life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Work%E2%80%93life_balance) was one of the film's core themes, and an important message for young people in the [digital age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_age).[^cite_note-HollandJun2026Playlist-72] The character's trauma and stress trigger an evolution in his superpowers, including gaining the ability to shoot organic webs from his wrists similar to the 2005–06 comic book storyline "[The Other](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Other_\(comic_book_storyline\))". Cretton said this is frightening for Parker, who initially believes he is becoming a monster.[^cite_note-NewPowers-9][^cite_note-ArtBookReview-28] Sommers said Parker always struggles to balance his civilian life with being Spider-Man, and in this film he "has given up so much of his humanity that the spider side of him is starting to fill that vacuum". After trying to suppress the transformation, Parker learns to embrace both sides of himself and find balance. McKenna said the film's title applies to its end as much as its beginning, with Parker's [character arc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character_arc) ending with him able to "actually have a brand new day and move forward".[^cite_note-McKennaSommersAug2026CS-114]

McKenna and Sommers thought the shapeshifting villain [Chameleon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Smerdyakov_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) was an appropriate [classic Spider-Man antagonist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Spider-Man_enemies) to use for a "metamorphosis" story. They considered giving him the [telepathic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telepathic) ability to [possess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirit_possession) people instead of making him a physical shapeshifter, to differentiate him from the shapeshifting alien [Skrulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skrulls_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) seen in previous MCU projects. Chameleon was unable to be included after Sony used him in the film *[Kraven the Hunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraven_the_Hunter_\(film\))* (2024), part of [Sony's Spider-Man Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony's_Spider-Man_Universe) (SSU), so other telepathic characters were considered as the main antagonist,[^cite_note-McKennaSommersAug2026Chameleon-135] including an obscure comic book character who was deemed unsatisfying for fans, and a character suggested by Holland that also could not be used.[^cite_note-FeigeVillainOptions-136] One of the characters considered was Jean Grey, a young telepath who has a similar journey of trauma and loss to Parker, with no mentor figures or community of support.[^cite_note-FeigeVillainOptions-136][^cite_note-CrettonAug2026Deadline-137] Cretton said Jean became the "perfect" option once Sink's casting was suggested, and denied that she was included as part of a mandate to set up Marvel Studios' [untitled *X-Men* film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men_\(upcoming_film\)) (2028).[^cite_note-CrettonAug2026Wrap-18][^cite_note-FeigeVillainOptions-136]

*Brand New Day* takes place before *Doomsday*, which was originally scheduled to be released first. Cretton said its delay to December 2026 allowed *Brand New Day* to stand alone and include elements that would not have been possible if it came after *Doomsday*.[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026SR-138] Pascal said the Punisher and the Hulk were included because they face similar problems to Parker,[^cite_note-FeigePascalJul2026Collider-130] with McKenna adding that Banner was the perfect character to help Parker with his mutating powers. MCU character [Yelena Belova / Black Widow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yelena_Belova_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) was featured in the script from the first draft.[^cite_note-McKennaSommersAug2026Wrap-131] The creative team wanted Parker to be able to defeat the film's minor antagonists within a day or two.[^cite_note-ArtBookReview-28] They include [Tombstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lonnie_Lincoln_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)), [Boomerang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boomerang_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)), [Tarantula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarantula_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)),[^cite_note-TrailerTFVillains-42] [Ramrod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramrod_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)),[^cite_note-Ramrod-139] and [the Hand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hand_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) ninjas who were previously seen in [Marvel's Netflix television series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel's_Netflix_television_series).[^cite_note-TheHand-140] [Paste-Pot Pete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trapster) was considered,[^cite_note-CrettonAug2026OutNow-141] while Parker uses [Shocker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shocker_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\))'s gauntlet from *Homecoming* to fight the Hulk.[^cite_note-ShockerGauntlet-142] Cretton said there were discussions about many other characters being added, including *Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings* protagonist [Xu Shang-Chi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xu_Shang-Chi).[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026Polygon-143]

#### Design

A 3D printer working on a piece of red and blue cloth

Sony and Little Caesars created a replica of Parker's apartment from *Brand New Day* as a fan experience, including his "Fabricator" which builds and repairs his Spider-Man suit.

Charles Wood returned as production designer from previous MCU media,[^cite_note-WoodAug2026-144] and costume designer Sanja Milkovic Hays also returned from *No Way Home* and other MCU films. She said the first conversations for *Brand New Day* were about Parker being on his own, with no access to [Tony Stark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\))'s money and resources, so he had to build his own [Spider-Man suit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_suit_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) which Cretton wanted to look more homemade than Holland's previous costumes.[^cite_note-HaysAug2026Credits-145] Parker also builds an [artificial intelligence](1164.md) (AI) called [E.V.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.V._\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)),[^cite_note-ScriptPagesEW-146] voiced by [Naomi Watts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Watts) who worked with Holland on the film *[The Impossible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Impossible_\(2012_film\))* (2012);[^cite_note-WattsEV-46] and a homemade "Fabricator", a combination of a [sewing machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sewing_machine) and a [3D printer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_printing) that builds and repairs his suit.[^cite_note-ArtBookReview-28][^cite_note-ScriptPagesEW-146]

Holland was involved in designing the new Spider-Man suit.[^cite_note-HaysAug2026Credits-145] It is similar to the suit that was introduced at the end of *No Way Home*, which was made by Holland's Parker following his interactions with Maguire's and Garfield's Spider-Men in that film. The script for *No Way Home* explains that Parker took inspiration from the suits worn by those characters when designing his own.[^cite_note-CostumeSR-147] Holland liked the idea of Parker "trying to be his big brothers, that he looks up to them and sees little details on their suits that he thinks are really cool".[^cite_note-HollandSep2025Complex-128] He tried on costumes originally made for Maguire and Garfield,[^cite_note-HollandJul2026NJ-148] and researched which details fans liked most about those suits.[^cite_note-HollandJun2026Esquire-149] The previous films used visual effects to perfect the suit, but for *Brand New Day* the creative team chose not to do this so wrinkles and imperfections could be seen, including Holland's face moving underneath the mask. Holland wears the suit for around 80 percent of the film, much more than the previous films,[^cite_note-HaysAug2026Credits-145][^cite_note-HaysAug2026CB-150] and he said it was built completely differently from his previous costumes, which made it comfier and more flexible.[^cite_note-HollandSep2025Complex-128]

Jean Grey wears a hood to hide her identity, with green and yellow colors used as a reference to the comics.[^cite_note-HaysAug2026Credits-145] Influences on Castle's appearance include the cover of *The Punisher War Journal* #15 by [Jim Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Lee) and [John Buscema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Buscema)'s design for the character.[^cite_note-ArtBookReview-28] His vest was made by a manufacturer of real military vests and then painted with the Punisher's skull symbol.[^cite_note-HaysAug2026Credits-145] The filmmakers considered using the Hulk's original gray look from the comics, dubbed "Gray Hulk", before deciding the audience should see the more familiar green "Savage Hulk". Ruffalo's facial features were de-emphasized in the design.[^cite_note-ArtBookReview-28] Scorpion's armor and the end of his mechanized tail were built by specialty costume company FBFX. The rest of his tail was created with visual effects.[^cite_note-HaysAug2026Credits-145]

Wood did extensive research in New York City before recreating parts of it on the streets of [Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow), Scotland, and on sets at [Pinewood Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinewood_Studios) in [Buckinghamshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckinghamshire), England. Two or three city blocks of buildings and rooftops were built on a [backlot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backlot), including a rooftop with a large "Silver Cup" sign. Other sets included Parker's apartment, which has a view of [Queensboro Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queensboro_Bridge) to show that "he had this view of the city he could never join"; MJ and Ned's apartment in [Queens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens); Jean's cell, which was built inside a larger space that is revealed when the walls are raised; and Yelena's [bathhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_bathing) "office", inspired by a [Victorian era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victorian_era) bathhouse in [Manchester](20206.md).[^cite_note-ArtBookReview-28][^cite_note-WoodAug2026-144] For the tank seen early in the film, Wood requested several symbols be put on it including a devil and a panther. The devil symbol was compared to the logo for [Mister Negative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mister_Negative)'s [Inner Demons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inner_Demons_\(comics\)) gang from the comics.[^cite_note-NegativeSetPhotos-151][^cite_note-TankSymbols-152]

### Filming

[Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography) began on August 3, 2025, in Glasgow under the [working title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_title) *Blue Oasis*,[^cite_note-GlasgowFilmingStart-153][^cite_note-VarietyFilmingStart-154] with [Brett Pawlak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Pawlak) returning as cinematographer from many of Cretton's previous films.[^cite_note-CrettonAug2026Deadline-137] The film was shot in two aspect ratios, [2.39\:1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2.39\:1) for wide screens and [1.90\:1 for digital IMAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX#Digital),[^cite_note-Trailer2Marvel-155] with Pawlak shooting on the [Arri Alexa 265](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arri_Alexa#Alexa_265) camera with [Panavision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panavision)'s Primo 65 and Panaspeed lenses. Pawlak wanted the film's [color grading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_grading) to feel "grounded", having "saturated color with deep blacks and shadow" to provide "a strong sense of contrast", mimicking the art style of Spider-Man comics.[^cite_note-Panavision-156] Holland was inspired by director [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan)'s efficient approach on *The Odyssey* and wanted to bring a similar work ethic to *Brand New Day*, insisting that they have each day's filming planned ahead of time,[^cite_note-HollandKuritzkes-95][^cite_note-HollandJun2026GQ-157] and that practical stunts be performed when possible.[^cite_note-HollandJul2026TF-158] Peng Zhang coordinated the fight sequences, returning from *Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings*.[^cite_note-CrettonAug2026Wrap-18][^cite_note-CrettonJul2026Inverse-159] Some stunts were inspired by the *[Marvel's Spider-Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel's_Spider-Man)* video game series,[^cite_note-CrettonInsomniacInspiration-160] including its finishing moves.[^cite_note-HollandInsomniacInspiration-161] The production team analyzed footage of [wingsuit flyers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wingsuit_flying) and [parkour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkour) athletes for the scenes where Spider-Man swings on his webs.[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026Polygon-143] For the fight between Parker and the Hand, when the latter are under Jean's control, Zhang choreographed the ninjas in a bird wing-inspired formation, referencing Jean's comic book connection to the [Phoenix Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Force).[^cite_note-ZhangAug2026-162] Dance choreographer Nathan Kim designed the body movements for when Jean is [body hopping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_hopping), which were not altered during post-production.[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026Inverse-159][^cite_note-EditorsAotC-163]

### Post-production

In January 2026, Justin Kroll from *[Deadline Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood)* reported that Feige had been solely focused on [the production of *Doomsday* and *Secret Wars*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Production_of_Avengers:_Doomsday_and_Avengers:_Secret_Wars) for the last year, and had let Pascal handle much of the production for *Brand New Day*;[^cite_note-KrollFeigeSoleFocus-191] Cretton disputed this.[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026SR-138] In addition to Feige and Pascal, other producers on the film included frequent *Spider-Man* film producer [Avi Arad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avi_Arad) and Pascal's producing partner Rachel O'Connor.[^cite_note-TrailerWrap-192][^cite_note-PascalAug2026Deadline-193]

Florence Pugh pictured in April 2025

[Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) reprised her MCU role of [Yelena Belova / Black Widow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yelena_Belova_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)). Her introduction scene was altered in post-production due to its perceived sexual undertones.

The first trailer was released in March, publicly revealing the Hand's return from the Marvel Netflix series;[^cite_note-TrailerTFVillains-42] [Zabryna Guevara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zabryna_Guevara) reprising her role as Sheila Rivera from *Born Again* for a shot in which her character hands Spider-Man [the key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key_to_the_city) to New York City;[^cite_note-Guevara-50] [Eman Esfandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eman_Esfandi) as a new love interest for MJ;[^cite_note-TrailerTF-12] and [Keith David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_David) as the narrator of a [nature documentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_documentary) about spiders.[^cite_note-TrailerGizmodo-10][^cite_note-SREasterEggs-47] Feige indicated in mid-July that [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) would reprise her MCU role of Yelena Belova ahead of her appearance in *Doomsday*,[^cite_note-FeigePughTease-194] which was confirmed at the end of the month,[^cite_note-Pugh-29] when [Rosario Dawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosario_Dawson) revealed that she reprised her role as [Claire Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Temple_\(Marvel_Cinematic_Universe\)) from Marvel's Netflix series for a scene that had been cut.[^cite_note-Dawson-195] It showed her meeting Parker at a hospital after he fights Scorpion and before he seeks advice from Banner.[^cite_note-McKennaSommersAug2026Wrap-131] Additionally, [Marisa Tomei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marisa_Tomei) reprised her role as Parker's Aunt May from the previous trilogy.[^cite_note-DeadlineCast-30]

*Brand New Day* was edited by [Nat Sanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nat_Sanders), Gina Sansom, and [Harry Yoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Yoon), all reuniting with Cretton from previous projects. Sanders and Sansom began work during pre-production and filming, working with Cretton on the [previsualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Previsualization) process, with Yoon joining later. Several rounds of test screenings were held during the editing process.[^cite_note-EditorsAotC-163] The first [assembly cut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_cut) was around 170 to 180 minutes and was edited down to 144 minutes.[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026Collider-196] This was primarily done by reducing scenes, particularly action sequences, rather than cutting full scenes. Sanders and Sansom worked on the opening montage and tried to balance a fun opening with the sadness of Parker's situation.[^cite_note-EditorsAotC-163] In April 2026, [additional photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Additional_photography) was taking place in London after the second test screening,[^cite_note-EditorsAotC-163][^cite_note-HollandApr2026-197] adding new footage to clarify the connection between Parker's evolving abilities and his isolation, such as mentions of headaches and pain in his wrists early in the film.[^cite_note-EditorsAotC-163][^cite_note-HollandApr2026-197] Holland said they were putting "icing on the cake", adding humor and incorporating a villain plotline in a new way.[^cite_note-HollandApr2026-197] There was a cut that was influenced by the test screenings that he felt "totally didn't work",[^cite_note-HollandTestScreening-198] although Cretton said it was similar to the final version and had just been "overcut".[^cite_note-CrettonAug2026Wrap-18] Yelena's introduction, seemingly naked underwater in the bathhouse, was discussed for its perceived sexual undertones. The writers enjoyed how she was "putting \[Parker] on his heels" in the scene, and the creative team felt it was appropriate for a more grown-up Spider-Man film.[^cite_note-McKennaSommersAug2026Wrap-131] Adjustments were made in post-production to make the water more opaque in response to concerns from younger test screen viewers who found the scene uncomfortable.[^cite_note-EditorsAotC-163]

The film's version of Marvel Studios' opening logo explains the impact of Strange's spell on MJ and Ned's memories. It includes scenes from the previous MCU *Spider-Man* films where Parker is with those characters, and shows him disappearing from the footage.[^cite_note-ScriptPagesEW-146] This idea was suggested by Cretton in response to feedback from test audiences who were not familiar with the ending of *No Way Home*. For the main-on-end credits, they wanted the audience to sit with the film's ending and Parker's character growth rather than jump to a "fun and inventive main title" sequence,[^cite_note-EditorsAotC-163] and Cretton wanted to reflect the themes of connection.[^cite_note-CrettonAug2026OutNow-141] The original suggestion was to show everyday people seeing Spider-Man swing by, which evolved to a simpler sequence that shows casual images of New York City,[^cite_note-EditorsAotC-163] captured by director Lloyd Lee Choi for the film.[^cite_note-CrettonAug2026OutNow-141] After the credits, Ned's "Spidey Tracker" app is seen detecting a Spider-Man sighting away from Earth, leading to speculation about which future project it could be teasing.[^cite_note-PostCredits-199]

Visual effects were supervised by [Jerome Chen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerome_Chen_\(visual_effects_supervisor\)) and provided by [Sony Pictures Imageworks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Imageworks), [Framestore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framestore), [Industrial Light & Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_Light_&_Magic) (ILM), [Rise FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rise_FX), [Cantina Creative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantina_Creative), Soho VFX, and SDFX Studios.[^cite_note-ArtofVFX-200] Framestore contributed to 342 shots with their studios in Montréal and Mumbai. Framestore worked on Scorpion, the armored vehicle crashing into the DODC facility, the Silvercup rooftop scene, Spider-Man swinging to Frank Castle's hideout and at MJ's apartment and the final battle with the Hand.[^cite_note-FramestoreVFX-201] ILM handled much of the Hulk–Spider-Man fight sequence. The studio studied Hulk's appearance in previous MCU films and updated the Savage Hulk model, giving him a more mottled texture while differentiating his appearance from Ruffalo's. Smart Hulk was also slightly updated to match Ruffalo's current appearance. As Ruffalo was working on another film at the time, he captured most of his performance from his hotel room via [Zoom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoom_\(software\)). ILM also created the effects for Shocker's punch glove, Spider-Man's swing to the DODC's Roosevelt Island Facility, and the "Mindwave" during the film's climax.[^cite_note-ILMEffects-202]

### Theatrical

*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* premiered at the [Dolby Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolby_Theatre) in [Hollywood, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood,_Los_Angeles), on July 27, 2026.[^cite_note-WorldPremiere-246] Distributed by [Sony Pictures Releasing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Releasing),[^cite_note-TrailerWrap-192] the film's international release began on July 29. *Brand New Day* is part of [Phase Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_Six) of the MCU.[^cite_note-PhaseSix-255] It was previously scheduled for release on July 24, one week after *The Odyssey*'s debut, but it was delayed by a week to avoid contention with that film.[^cite_note-Jul2026DateDelay-106][^cite_note-Jul2026DateDelay2-107] Disney had intended to release an unspecified Marvel Studios film on July 24, but removed this from the company's release schedule in August 2024, which allowed Sony to schedule *Brand New Day* then.[^cite_note-GizmodoDisneyAug2024Dates-256]

## Reception

### Box office

As of October 2, 2026, *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* has grossed \$953 million in the United States and Canada, and \$1.545 billion in other territories, for a worldwide total of \$2.498 billion.[^cite_note-BOM-3][^cite_note-NUM-4] It is the [third-highest grossing film of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films);[^cite_note-ThirdHighestAllTime-262] the [highest-grossing film of all time in the United States and Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_films_in_the_United_States_and_Canada);[^cite_note-DeadlineHighestDomesticGross-263] the [highest-grossing film of 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_film#Highest-grossing_films) worldwide, in the United States and Canada, and outside of the United States and Canada;[^cite_note-HighestBOTuesday-245][^cite_note-264] and Sony's highest-grossing film of all time worldwide and in the United States and Canada.[^cite_note-SonyHighestGrossing-265] Its \$932 million opening weekend was the [second-best of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_openings_for_films#Biggest_worldwide_openings_on_record) behind *[Avengers: Endgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Endgame#Box_office)* (2019).[^cite_note-DeadlineBO1stWkndRecords-266] On August 3, it became the [second-fastest film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fastest-grossing_films#Fastest_to_\$1_billion) to gross \$1 billion, doing so in six days.[^cite_note-BillionSixDaysDeadline-267] On August 16, it became the second-fastest film to gross \$2 billion and the [first *Spider-Man* film to do so](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_in_film#Box_office).[^cite_note-2BillionTHR-268]

#### United States and Canada

The film had the best first-day ticket pre-sales in five years since *No Way Home* in 2021, which had an estimated \$78 million in pre-sales.[^cite_note-BestUSTicketPresales-269] Total pre-sales were \$150 million, a record.[^cite_note-DeadlineBO1stWkndRecords-266] It had the [highest opening weekend gross of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_openings_for_films#Biggest_openings_in_the_United_States_and_Canada) at \$360 million, surpassing *Endgame* (\$357.1 million). Wednesday and Thursday preview screenings accounted for \$72 million, surpassing the record previously held by *Endgame* (\$60 million). The opening day gross (including previews) of \$169.8 million also surpassed *Endgame*'s record (\$157.4 million). The Saturday and Sunday grosses of \$101.5 million and \$88.7 million, respectively, were both second-best all-time to *Endgame* (\$109.2 million and \$90.3 million).[^cite_note-DeadlineBO1stWknd-248] Pre-release estimates had the film grossing \$260–280 million in its opening weekend, with Sony estimating a \$195 million opening.[^cite_note-VarietyBOTracking-270][^cite_note-DeadlineBOTracking-2] Its fourth and fifth days of release saw the highest Monday and Tuesday grosses of any film at \$47 million and \$42 million, respectively.[^cite_note-BillionSixDaysVariety-271][^cite_note-HighestBOTuesday-245] The second weekend gross of \$144 million was the third-biggest behind *Endgame* (\$147 million) and *[Star Wars: The Force Awakens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Force_Awakens)* (\$149 million),[^cite_note-OtherTerritories1Billion-272][^cite_note-DeadlineBO2ndWknd-273] while its third weekend gross of \$71 million was the second highest behind *The Force Awakens* (\$90.2 million).[^cite_note-DeadlineBO3rdWknd-274] The film remained top of the box office through its sixth weekend,[^cite_note-DeadlineBO4thTo6thWknd-275] and stayed in the top ten through its tenth weekend.[^cite_note-DeadlineBOTop10Wknds-276] It became the [fastest film to reach cumulative grosses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fastest-grossing_films#Fastest-grossing_films_%E2%80%93_US_and_Canada) through \$900 million.[^cite_note-FastestBOM-277][^cite_note-DeadlineHighestDomesticGross-263] On September 15, it surpassed the \$936 million gross of *The Force Awakens* to become the highest-grossing film of all time after 47 days of release.[^cite_note-DeadlineHighestDomesticGross-263][^cite_note-THRHighestDomesticGross-278]

#### Other territories

The film grossed \$573 million its opening weekend,[^cite_note-VarietyBO1stWknd-279] surpassing pre-release estimates of \$275–300 million.[^cite_note-VarietyBOTracking-270][^cite_note-DeadlineBOTracking-2] It opened as the number one film in 66 [box office markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box_office_market),[^cite_note-BillionSixDaysDeadline-267] and had the highest-grossing opening weekend all-time in at least 11 countries, including France (\$26.6 million), Brazil (\$23.8 million), Spain (\$15.6 million), and Peru (\$8.6 million).[^cite_note-DeadlineBO1stWkndRecords-266] In its second weekend, it remained the top film in most of its 67 markets. It surpassed \$1 billion in 12 days, the second-fastest after *Endgame*.[^cite_note-OtherTerritories1Billion-272] It became the highest-grossing Hollywood film in India.[^cite_note-HighestGrossingIndia-280] By its fourth weekend, *Brand New Day* had become the highest-grossing Sony film in 56 markets.[^cite_note-DeadlineBO4thWkndOther-281] As of September 13, 2026, the film's top-grossing markets include China (\$233.0 million), the United Kingdom (\$132 million), Mexico (\$90.7 million), France (\$86.5 million), and Brazil (\$76.7 million).[^cite_note-DeadlineBO7thWkndOther-282]

### Critical response

[Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland) (pictured in July 2026) received praise for his performance.

*Brand New Day* received largely positive reviews from critics, with praise for Holland's performance, the action sequences, and the more mature, character-driven story compared to the previous MCU *Spider-Man* films.[^cite_note-RTReviewRoundup-283][^cite_note-BBCReviewRoundup-284][^cite_note-THRSuccess5Reasons-285][^cite_note-EWReviewRoundup-286] On review aggregator [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), 90% of 435 critics gave a positive review and the average of rated reviews was 7.6/10. The critics consensus reads, "Spinning a web of spectacular set pieces together with mature emotional stakes and Tom Holland's soulful performance, *Brand New Day* is a promising reset that portends Spidey will remain sticky on the big screen for years to come."[^cite_note-287] [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic), which uses a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_average), assigned a score of 66 out of 100 based on 56 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.[^cite_note-288] Audiences polled by [CinemaScore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaScore) gave the film an average grade of "A" on an A+ to F scale, while 88% of those surveyed by [PostTrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PostTrak) said they would definitely recommend the film.[^cite_note-DeadlineBO1stWknd-248]

Pete Hammond for *[Deadline Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood)* called *Brand New Day* a "dark and thrilling new chapter" for the franchise, praising Holland's performance, Cretton's direction, and the film's balance of character drama and superhero action.[^cite_note-DeadlineReview-289] [Owen Gleiberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Gleiberman) at *Variety* praised the film for its action, villain, and Holland's performance, but described the film as "arduous", "clunkily episodic", and "half an hour too long", arguing that it is more self-serious than emotionally involving.[^cite_note-VarietyReview-290] [Peter Bradshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Bradshaw) for *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* praised the ambitious tone, strong performances and emotional focus, particularly highlighting Zendaya and Holland's chemistry. However, he found the film uneven, with some familiar superhero elements limiting its impact.[^cite_note-TheGuardianReview-291]

William Bibbiani of *TheWrap* praised *Brand New Day* as "a fantastic film about Peter Parker" and "one of the highlights of the whole franchise", commending its emotional, character-driven story and the performances of Holland and Bernthal, despite criticizing the villain's plan as illogical.[^cite_note-TheWrapReview-292] *[Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_\(magazine\))*'s John Nugent gave the film four out of five stars, praising its grounded, comic-faithful approach and Holland's performance, describing Spider-Man as "still Marvel's ace in the pack", while noting that the second half loses momentum due to unnecessary ties to the wider MCU.[^cite_note-EmpireReview-293] David Rooney of *The Hollywood Reporter* praised Cretton's direction for balancing "adrenaline and feeling", while highlighting "the humanity that has always been Spidey's greatest strength", Holland's "never-better" performance, and the film's emotional focus on Peter, though he criticized occasional pacing issues and plot convolutions.[^cite_note-THRReview-294]

[David Ehrlich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ehrlich) at *[IndieWire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndieWire)* gave the film a C−, describing it as a "dull and downbeat action sequel" that "causes more problems than it cures", criticizing its pacing, its reliance on MCU setup, and its failure to meaningfully advance Peter's story, although he praised Holland's performance and considered Cretton "a more sure-footed stylist" than Watts.[^cite_note-IndieWireReview-295] David Fear for *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone)* criticized the film for relying too heavily on fan service, MCU crossovers and franchise setup at the expense of Spider-Man's story, arguing that Peter risks becoming "a supporting player in his own movie", while praising Holland's performance and the emotional scenes between Peter and MJ.[^cite_note-RollingStoneReview-296]

## Future

Pascal stated in November 2021 that *Brand New Day* was planned to be the first film in a new *Spider-Man* trilogy starring Holland.[^cite_note-PascalNov2021-62] In early 2026, Holland revisited his previous comments about not wanting to play Spider-Man past the age of 30 and hoping to pass the mantle to another actor. Since the latter had not yet happened by his 30th birthday, Holland said he may need to "change the quote to 37" and added that "playing Spider-Man has been the joy of my life... I'll do it for as long as they'll have me."[^cite_note-HollandJun2026GQ-157] In July, Holland said he had a clear vision for how he wanted to pass the mantle.[^cite_note-HollandJul2026Variety-298] Feige said Miles Morales would be introduced in live-action following the release of the animated film *[Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Beyond_the_Spider-Verse)* (2027), and he had discussed ideas for the next five live-action *Spider-Man* films with Pascal.[^cite_note-FeigeShanghaiMediaJul2026-299] The latter hoped Cretton would be involved in future films,[^cite_note-CrettonJul2026CB-300] and Cretton said he was open to directing another.[^cite_note-CrettonAug2026Wrap-18] Following *Brand New Day*'s release, Rothman said there had not yet been concrete discussions about Holland returning for a fifth film,[^cite_note-RothmanAug2026-79] while Feige said Holland and Zendaya would be taking a break before any new plans were confirmed.[^cite_note-FeigeAug2026-301]
