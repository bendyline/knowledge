The **men's sprint** competition in [ski mountaineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ski_mountaineering_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics) at the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md) was held on 19 February at the [Stelvio Ski Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stelvio_\(ski_course\)) in [Bormio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bormio), Italy.[^cite_note-1] The event made its Olympic debut. It was won by [Oriol Cardona](81975466.md) of Spain. This was only the second Winter Olympic gold for Spain; [Francisco Fernández Ochoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Fern%C3%A1ndez_Ochoa) won the previous one in 1972 in alpine skiing. Russian [Nikita Filippov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Filippov_\(ski_mountaineer\)), competing as an [Individual Neutral Athlete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individual_Neutral_Athletes_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics), won the silver medal and [Thibault Anselmet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thibault_Anselmet) of France won bronze.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Background

At the [2025–26 ISMF Ski Mountaineering World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_ISMF_Ski_Mountaineering_World_Cup), only three sprint events were held before the Olympics. [Jon Kistler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Kistler), [Oriol Cardona](81975466.md) and [Thibault Anselmet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thibault_Anselmet) won one each. The [2025 World champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Championship_of_Ski_Mountaineering) was Cardona.[^cite_note-4]

Ski mountaineering consists of skiing uphill with "skins" on the bottom of the ski; these are carpet-like coverings that provide grip to the skier. The athletes then remove their skis and place them in their backpack, before climbing a rise on foot. They then return to their skis to finish their climb. Once they reach the ascent, they remove the skins and ski downhill to the finish line.[^cite_note-5]
