**Louis John Crishock** (born 8 February 1971) is an American diplomat currently serving as the acting [High Representative for Bosnia and Herzegovina](2386573.md) since 1 July 2026. He also serves as [International Supervisor for Brčko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Supervisor_for_Br%C4%8Dko).

## Biography

Louis John Crishock was born on 8 February 1971 in [Arlington, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington,_Virginia). Crishock attended [Bishop Ireton High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_Ireton_High_School) in [Alexandria, Virginia](18940583.md)—where he graduated in 1989—followed by the [Catholic University of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_America), where he earned a [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in Religion *[summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude)* in 1993, and an [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in Sociology in 1995. Afterwards, he served a year as a sociology teacher at [Ural State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_State_University) in [Yekaterinburg](66524.md), Russia, as part of the [Civic Education Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civic_Education_Project). Crishock worked as the Civic Education Project's Deputy Director for Russia from 1996 to 1998. Crishock later attended the [Dwight D. Eisenhower School for National Security and Resource Strategy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwight_D._Eisenhower_School_for_National_Security_and_Resource_Strategy) of the [National Defense University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Defense_University_\(United_States\)), where he was a distinguished graduate in 2017.[^cite_note-auto-1]

Crishock joined the [United States Foreign Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Foreign_Service) in January 1999. In the subsequent years, he served at several overseas posting as well as in Washington, D.C. Among his positions were *chargé d'affaires* in [St. George's, Grenada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._George's,_Grenada).[^cite_note-auto-1] A senior advisor to the [United States Department of State](31975.md), Crishock also held the title of United States [Consul General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consul_General) in [Vladivostok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladivostok), Russia, served as Senior United States Official for the Arctic from 2021 to 2023, and was a State Department Resident Fellow at the [Woodrow Wilson International Center for Scholars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson_International_Center_for_Scholars).[^cite_note-auto1-2]

In August 2024, Crishock was appointed [Principal Deputy High Representative for Bosnia and Herzegovina](2386573.md) and thus also as the [International Supervisor for Brčko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Supervisor_for_Br%C4%8Dko), the representative of the international community in the [Brčko District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Br%C4%8Dko_District).[^cite_note-auto1-2] On 1 July 2026, following the resignation of High Representative [Christian Schmidt](39499479.md), Crishock assumed the role of Acting High Representative for Bosnia and Herzegovina until Schmidt's successor could be named.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

Crishock speaks [Serbo-Croatian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbo-Croatian), [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language), and some [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language).[^cite_note-auto-1]
