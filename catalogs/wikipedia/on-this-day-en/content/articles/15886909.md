**Tomblaine** (French pronunciation: [\[tɔ̃blɛn\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French)) is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in the [Meurthe-et-Moselle](90583.md) [department](38523.md) in north-eastern France. Stage 7 of the [Tour de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Tour_de_France) on 7 July 2012 started in Tomblaine. The [Stade Marcel Picot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stade_Marcel_Picot), football stadium to [Ligue 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ligue_1) side [AS Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AS_Nancy), is located within the area.

## Geography

Tomblaine is located north-east of France, in the eastern suburbs of [Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy,_France). The town is separated from the city of [Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy,_France) by the river [Meurthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meurthe_Department). The two cities are connected by two main bridges. It is bordered to the north by the municipalities of [Saint-Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Max) and [Essey-lès-Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essey-l%C3%A8s-Nancy) to the south by [Jarville-la-Malgrange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarville-la-Malgrange), [Laneuveville-devant-Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laneuveville-devant-Nancy), [Art-sur-Meurthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art-sur-Meurthe) and [Saulxures-lès-Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saulxures-l%C3%A8s-Nancy).

## History

The history of the site Tomblaine dates back to at least 500 [BC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Before_Christ) in [Gallo-Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallo-Roman) times. In the seventeenth century, wars and famines spread across Tomblaine. This resulted in a large decrease in population which has since risen. In 1770, the castle within the town belonged to Prince [Louis XVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XVI), the future [King of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_France). The commune was destroyed by the [Nazis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazis) in 1944. However, reconstructions have taken place, returning the town to its former glory.

## Sports

[Football](10568.md) is the preferred sport within Tomblaine. The commune pays host to the professional football club, [AS Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AS_Nancy), who currently reside at the [Stade Marcel Picot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stade_Marcel_Picot).
