The **Second Takaichi cabinet** is the 105th and present [Cabinet of Japan](573784.md), formed on 18 February 2026 when [Sanae Takaichi](8125532.md) was reappointed [Prime Minister](24833.md) by Emperor [Naruhito](266108.md) as designated by the [National Diet](163847.md) following the [2026 general election](77996830.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

The first reshuffle took place on 17 September 2026 and one Cabinet minister, two [State ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Minister_\(Japan\)), three Parliamentary Vice-Ministers from the Japan Innovation Party joined the Cabinet for the first time under the LDP–JIP coalition.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
