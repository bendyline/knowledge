**Kwango** is a [province of the Democratic Republic of the Congo](3984115.md). It is one of the 21 provinces created in the [2015 repartitioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_the_DR_Congo#New_provinces). Kwango, [Kwilu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwilu_Province), and [Mai-Ndombe](8502103.md) provinces are the result of the dismemberment of the former [Bandundu province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandundu_Province).[^cite_note-2] Kwango was formed from the [Kwango district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwango_District) whose town of [Kenge](21192344.md) was made the provincial capital and thus gained [city status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo).

The province takes its name from the [Kwango River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwango_River), a tributary of the [Kasai River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasai_River) that defines part of the international boundary between the DRC and Angola.

## Geography

Map of Kwango showing tree-cover loss years, with forest remaining since 2000 in green and loss years shaded yellow through purple.

Tree-cover loss year in Kwango, 2001-2024, from the [Global Forest Change dataset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Forest_Change_dataset).

Kwango is located in the southwestern Democratic Republic of the Congo. It has an [international border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angola%E2%80%93Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo_border) with [Angola](701.md) to the south, and borders on the provinces of [Kongo Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kongo_Central) to the west, [Kinshasa](45773.md) to the northwest, [Kwilu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwilu_Province) to the north, and [Kasaï](4225839.md) to the east. The entire province has a [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) by the [Köppen-Geiger climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen-Geiger_climate_classification).[^cite_note-3]

## Administration

The capital of Kwango is [Kenge](21192344.md),[^cite_note-4] and other towns in the region are [Popokabaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popokabaka), [Feshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feshi), [Kasongo Lunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasongo_Lunda), [Lusanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusanga,_Kwango) and [Kahemba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kahemba).[^cite_note-5]

## History

Kwango previously existed as a province from 1962 to 1966. Presidents (from 1965, governors)

- 23 September 1962 – 11 November 1962 Albert Delvaux (fl. 1918)

- November 1962 Emmanuel Mayamba

- 1962 – April 1963 Alphonse Pashi

- August 1963 – April 1964 Pierre Masikita (1st time)

- April 1964 – 30 September 1964 Belunda Kavunzu

- 30 September 1964 –24 August 1965 Joseph Kulumba

- 24 August 1965 – 25 April 1966 Pierre Masikita (2nd time)

From 1966 to 2015, Kwango was administered as a district as part of [Bandundu Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandundu_Province), later returning to full provincial status with its capital based at Kenge on 18 July 2015.[^cite_note-6]

In December 2024, deputy provincial governor Rémy Saki reported that between 10 and 25 November, between 67 and 143 people had died of an [unknown disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Kwango_province_disease_outbreak) in the Panzi health zone. Apollinaire Yumba, the province's health minister, requested that citizens stay away from dead bodies and urged outside groups to give medical aid. [World Health Organization](33583.md) officials were also in the region to collect samples.[^cite_note-7]
