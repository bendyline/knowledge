**Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei**[^cite_note-10] (born 8 September 1969) is an Iranian politician and [Shia cleric](3180480.md) who has served as the [supreme leader of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_leader_of_Iran) since 2026. A member of the [Khamenei family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khamenei_family) and the second son of second supreme leader [Ali Khamenei](385653.md), he previously served as [Vakil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vakil) of the [Supreme Leader](51025254.md) during his father's rule from 2008[^cite_note-termstart-8] to 2026.

Born in [Mashhad](406512.md), Khamenei was raised in a conservative religious household, and graduated from high school in [Tehran](57654.md). He then studied [Islamic theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schools_of_Islamic_theology) under the guidance of his father and [Mahmoud Hashemi Shahroudi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmoud_Hashemi_Shahroudi). He joined the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC) in 1987 and served in the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War). In 1989, he began studying in [Qom](638742.md) to become a cleric, and later joined the [Qom Seminary](32825717.md) as a theology teacher. He took control of the [Basij](2892082.md) paramilitary volunteer militia in 2009.

Western analysts long considered Khamenei a likely candidate to replace his father as Supreme Leader, despite some unverified reports that Ali Khamenei had opposed his accession. Following [his father's assassination](82537558.md) during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), Khamenei was [elected the third supreme leader](59042456.md) by the [Assembly of Experts](512725.md). Khamenei was injured in the airstrike that killed his father and has remained absent from public view since his appointment, causing confusion over his fate. The US has placed a \$10 million bounty via the [Rewards for Justice Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rewards_for_Justice_Program) for information on him.[^cite_note-11]

In political ideology and jurisprudence, Khamenei is considered to be more hardline than his father and has close ties to some of the "most ideologically extremist clerics" according to a report from the [Atlantic Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Council), a pro-[NATO](21133.md) think tank.[^cite_note-\:82-12]

## Early life and education



Khamenei (second from left) in 2024 with his three brothers, from left to right: [Mostafa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mostafa_Khamenei), [Masoud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masoud_Khamenei), and Meysam

Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei was born on 8 September 1969 in [Mashhad](406512.md) as the second child of [Ali Khamenei](385653.md) and [Mansoureh Khojasteh Bagherzadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansoureh_Khojasteh_Bagherzadeh).[^cite_note-shadow-13][^cite_note-mehdi2012-14] He was named after [Navvab Safavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navvab_Safavi) (born Mojtaba Mir-Lohi), a radical Shia cleric about whom Ali Khamenei had said that he "first kindled the fire of revolutionary Islam in my heart."[^cite_note-\:10-15] His five siblings are [Mostafa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mostafa_Khamenei), his elder brother, [Masoud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masoud_Khamenei) and Meysam, his younger brothers, and Boshra and Hoda, his younger sisters. His paternal grandfather, [Javad Khamenei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javad_Khamenei), was a [Twelver Shia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelver_Shia) cleric and scholar.[^cite_note-16]

Khamenei has [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persians) ancestry, but to a lesser extent, [Azerbaijani Turk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Azerbaijanis) ancestry, with his Azeri roots being traced back to [Khamaneh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khamaneh), a small town in [East Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Azerbaijan) where his surname originated from, and he also has distant roots from [Tafresh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tafresh).[^cite_note-\:16-17][^cite_note-18] His family traces its lineage to [Husayn ibn Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Husayn_ibn_Ali), the son of [Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali), the first [Shia Imam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imamate_in_Shia_doctrine), and the maternal grandson of the [Islamic prophet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prophets_and_messengers_in_Islam) [Muhammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_in_Islam), hence Khamenei's middle name is Hosseini (spelled Husayni in [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language); meaning "descendant of Husayn").[^cite_note-19]

Khamenei was raised in a conservative religious household, where he encountered Islamic learning and revolutionary ideas from a young age.[^cite_note-20] After graduating from Alavi high school in Tehran,[^cite_note-auto3-21] he studied Islamic theology. His early teachers included his own father and Ayatollah [Mahmoud Hashemi Shahroudi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmoud_Hashemi_Shahroudi).[^cite_note-shadow-13] In 1989, Khamenei began his studies in [Qom](638742.md) to become a cleric. [Mohammad-Taqi Mesbah-Yazdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad-Taqi_Mesbah-Yazdi), Ayatollah [Lotfollah Safi Golpaygani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotfollah_Safi_Golpaygani) and [Mohammad Bagher Kharazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Bagher_Kharazi) were among his teachers there.[^cite_note-shadow-13][^cite_note-diba4mar-22] Khamenei has completed specialized studies in [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology_in_the_medieval_Islamic_world) and [psychoanalysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychoanalysis).[^cite_note-IranWire-23]

## Scholarly career

Khamenei taught theology in the [Qom Seminary](32825717.md);[^cite_note-usip-24] from 2004 he taught *Kharij-e Fiqh* (advanced jurisprudence) courses for approximately two decades and was considered the instructor of one of the most heavily attended advanced seminary classes.[^cite_note-IranWire-23] The *Dars-e Kharij* is the highest level of jurisprudential instruction and a prerequisite for attaining the rank of [*mujtahid*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ijtihad), a scholar qualified to perform independent legal reasoning (*[ijtihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ijtihad)*) in [Islamic law](28840.md).[^cite_note-\:4-25]

## Early political career

Khamenei first joined the [Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC) around 1987 after finishing high school at age 17.[^cite_note-auto3-21] He was part of the Habib bin Muzahir Battalion, and he served together with [Qasem Soleimani](16805217.md), [Hossein Hamadani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hossein_Hamadani) and [Hossein Taeb](24451534.md), who would later command the IRGC.[^cite_note-\:14-26][^cite_note-27] He took part in several operations of the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War), including [Operation Beit ol-Moqaddas 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Beit_ol-Moqaddas_2), [Operation Dawn 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Dawn_10) and [Operation Mersad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Mersad).[^cite_note-28]

In 2009, it was reported that Khamenei had assumed leadership of the [Basij](2892082.md), one of five branches of the IRGC.[^cite_note-Borger-29] Reformist critics blamed him for suppressing the [protests over the 2009 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009%E2%80%932010_Iranian_election_protests).[^cite_note-Aljazeera-Mojtaba-30] Despite having control over the Basij, *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* argued in 2009 that "\[t]he strength of Mojtaba's following has not been demonstrated", and that he "by no means has the theological status" to rise to Supreme Leader.[^cite_note-Borger-29]

In 2023, *[Iran International](58555280.md)* published leaked reports from the IRGC, revealing that Khamenei effectively controlled the Basij and that he exercised significant influence over the assignment and dismissal of personnel in the [Intelligence Protection Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Protection_Organization_of_the_Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps).[^cite_note-31]

## Supreme Leadership (2026–present)

### Background

Several Western analysts considered Khamenei a possible successor to his father,[^cite_note-Borger-29][^cite_note-Slavin-32] and multiple Western sources considered him Ali Khamenei's [heir apparent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heir_apparent).[^cite_note-LATimes-2009-33][^cite_note-guardian_20090622-34] In 2014, as well as 2025,[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] it was reported that Ali's successor was deemed to have been decided but not disclosed publicly.[^cite_note-Guardian-37] Prior to his death, Ali had not publicly discussed his replacement,[^cite_note-\:0-38][^cite_note-39] and some reports said that he opposed Mojtaba succeeding him;[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-auto-41][^cite_note-washingtoninstitute.org-42] US intelligence indicated that Ali considered Mojtaba unintelligent and unqualified.[^cite_note-TelegraphEscapedSeconds-43]

Some analysts considered Mojtaba Khamenei's potential accession as presenting a problem, as the Supreme Leader needs to be elected by the [Assembly of Experts](512725.md), though *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* noted that the first Supreme Leader, [Ruhollah Khomeini](26232.md), exerted a strong influence in favour of the choice of Ali Khamenei.[^cite_note-guardian_20090622-34] The [Iranian constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Iran) dictates the adherence to Khomeini's interpretation of the principle of [Guardianship of the Islamic Jurist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardianship_of_the_Islamic_Jurist) (*velayat-e faqih*). According to this interpretation, the Supreme Leader must be a *[mujtahid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mujtahid)*, capable of interpreting Sharia law.[^cite_note-44] Khamenei is a *[hojjatoleslam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hojjatoleslam)* (a rank below that of *ayatollah*).[^cite_note-\:3-45][^cite_note-\:03-46]

During the presidency of [Ebrahim Raisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebrahim_Raisi), Khamenei was one of the clerics speculated as a possible successor as supreme leader.[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48] However, he was considered unlikely to succeed his father.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51] The Middle East Institute opined that Khamenei appointing his own son as successor would cause conflict within the Iranian political and religious leadership,[^cite_note-52] as it would be a sign that the revolutionary Islamic system of government had evolved to [dynastic rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynastic_rule).[^cite_note-\:03-46]

### Election

See also: [2026 Iranian supreme leader election](59042456.md)

After [his father's assassination](82537558.md) in the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), Khamenei was considered a likely candidate to replace him as Supreme Leader.[^cite_note-\:2-53][^cite_note-54][^cite_note-55][^cite_note-LATimes-2009-33] *[The Daily Telegraph](23797577.md)* reported that over the years Khamenei had built a "loyalty structure that spanned the country", at the apex of which stood the IRGC, and it would "legitimise his leadership when the moment came".[^cite_note-\:14-26] According to *[Iran International](58555280.md)*, the IRGC pressured the Assembly of Experts to elect Khamenei.[^cite_note-\:6-56][^cite_note-IranIntl_guards_push_fast_Mojtaba-57] There was strong opposition from some members of the council,[^cite_note-58] including eight who stated that they would boycott an online electoral meeting planned for 5 March.[^cite_note-IranIntl_guards_push_fast_Mojtaba-57] Questions regarding the voting process itself also arose; the assembly building had been destroyed prior to the vote, and unlike the election of Ali Khamenei, which followed a period of relative tranquility allowing multiple meetings on the issue, the election of Mojtaba occurred in the absence of such stability.[^cite_note-\:14-26] On 8 March, the Assembly of Experts announced that Mojtaba was elected Supreme Leader in a "unanimous vote".[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-60][^cite_note-Aljazeera-Mojtaba-30][^cite_note-\:4-25][^cite_note-61]

On 5 March, US president [Donald Trump](4848272.md) said that Khamenei's election would be "unacceptable" and that he had to be involved in the selection of Iran's next leader.[^cite_note-62][^cite_note-63] Following his election, Trump said that he was "disappointed" by the selection,[^cite_note-64] and that Khamenei "cannot live in peace".[^cite_note-65] Israel said it considered Khamenei, like his father, a target for assassination.[^cite_note-NBC_March8-66] According to an *Iran International* analysis, Khamenei's election marked the completion of the Islamic Republic's shift away from [public backing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_opinion).[^cite_note-IranInt10Mar-67] Analysts and scholars described it as an indication that Iran had returned to [hereditary rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hereditary_rule) after abandoning it following the [1979 revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_revolution).[^cite_note-WSJMonarchy-68][^cite_note-69] Many Iranian social media users mocked the appointment,[^cite_note-70] and [Iranian protesters](81976180.md) chanted "[Death to Mojtaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_to_Mojtaba)" from their homes.[^cite_note-protester2026-71]

### Iran war

Further information: [2026 Iran war](82533582.md)

On 17 March, a senior Iranian official stated that Khamenei had rejected de-escalation proposals conveyed by intermediaries. According to the official, Khamenei demanded that the US and Israel be "brought to their knees".[^cite_note-72]

#### Absence and speculation

Main article: [2025–2026 Iranian de facto leadership speculations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_Iranian_de_facto_leadership_speculations)

Khamenei was injured in the airstrike that killed his father, wife, and other family members, and his appointment was followed by a prolonged absence from public view; no confirmed footage of him has been released since the appointment.[^cite_note-\:11-73] Supposed recent photographs of Khamenei distributed by the Iranian government were [manipulated with artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence_visual_art), according to Shayan Sardarizadeh, a senior journalist with [BBC Verify](74154160.md)*.*[^cite_note-74][^cite_note-\:11-73] His absence caused widespread confusion over the extent of the injuries he sustained during the attack on the residence of the Supreme Leader on 28 February. *[The Media Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Media_Line)* reported that "the survival of Mojtaba Khamenei remains a subject of considerable speculation".[^cite_note-\:32-75]

In a statement to ISNA, Yousouf, son of President [Masoud Pezeshkian](2650383.md), said that Khamenei was "safe and sound",[^cite_note-\:11-73] and Iranian officials said that he had sustained minor injuries to the face and foot.[^cite_note-\:12-76] *[The Daily Telegraph](23797577.md)* obtained a recording of an address from Mazaher Hosseini, Ali Khamenei's [chief of protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_protocol), to the IRGC and senior clerics. Hosseini said that the younger Khamenei survived the airstrike because he had gone outside for a walk several minutes before Israel struck the [Supreme Leader's compound](51025254.md). According to Hosseini, Khamenei suffered a minor leg injury.[^cite_note-TelegraphEscapedSeconds-43] In contrast, Voice of Emirates reported that Khamenei's health had severely deteriorated, that he had sustained life-threatening [lacerations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wound) and fractures to his abdomen and legs, and had entered a [semi-comatose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consciousness) state; his treatment including being put on a ventilator in the intensive care unit of [Sina Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sina_Hospital).[^cite_note-\:13-77] On 15 March, *[Al-Jarida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Jarida)* reported that Khamenei had been transferred to [Moscow](19004.md) for treatment in an operation ordered by Russian president [Vladimir Putin](32817.md).[^cite_note-ToI:_TrumpBriefed-78][^cite_note-79]

An Iranian official told *The Telegraph*: "No one knows anything about Mojtaba, whether he is alive or dead or how badly injured. We are all just told that he's injured. He has no control over the war because he is not here. The majority of commanders, or more correctly, all commanders, have no news about him."[^cite_note-\:14-26] The reports prompted a former Iranian diplomat to question why he was chosen as Supreme Leader,[^cite_note-\:14-26] and several senior Iranian clerics called for the return of the [Interim Leadership Council](82544455.md).[^cite_note-80] *The Washington Post* says that Khamenei is believed to be "badly wounded."[^cite_note-81] According to a report in *[The Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times)*, Khamenei is unconscious and has been receiving medical treatments for a "severe" medical condition in [Qom](638742.md).[^cite_note-\:17-82]

Khamenei did not attend the memorial ceremony for his wife in Tehran on 1 July.[^cite_note-FassihiNYT-83] The *[New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times)* reported that he had told two government officials he wanted to attend his father's burial in Mashhad on 9 July.[^cite_note-FassihiNYT-83] While his brothers participated in the ceremonies, Khamenei was absent from all public funeral events and the burial ceremony.[^cite_note-84]

#### Written statements

On 12 March, four days after his appointment, a written message attributed to Khamenei—containing no video or audio—was read on [state television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_in_Iran),[^cite_note-85] promising revenge for the "[martyrs](35420678.md)".[^cite_note-86] According to *The Media Line*, the message contained a number of errors in typography and clerical language, and "a knowledgeable source in Tehran" said it had been dictated by the IRGC and released under Khamenei's name.[^cite_note-\:32-75] *Al-Jarida* reported that [Ali Larijani](531848.md), [Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council](82647047.md), was likely the actual author, as the message was similar to those Larijani had released in preceding days.[^cite_note-ToI:_TrumpBriefed-78]

A second message attributed to Khamenei, read on state television on 16 March, asked government officials appointed by his father to remain in their posts,[^cite_note-87] a third message, read on 18 March, vowed to avenge [Larijani's assassination](82710049.md),[^cite_note-88] and a fourth message, read on 20 March, denied that Iran was involved in the recent attacks on Turkey and Oman.[^cite_note-89] Following the [2026 Iran war ceasefire](82888027.md), a written statement was read saying to stop firing for the time being,[^cite_note-90] with another message promising revenge.[^cite_note-91]

On 6 April, an [AI-generated video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI-generated_video) of Khamenei was released by Iranian state-run media.[^cite_note-92] On 18 April, a message attributed to Khamenei said that the Iranian navy was ready to inflict "new bitter defeats" on enemies.[^cite_note-93] On 18 June, Khamenei issued a written statement, saying that he endorsed the [Islamabad Memorandum](83441543.md) to end the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md) despite misgivings.[^cite_note-94]

Analysts consider Khamenei to be aligned with the [ultraconservatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultraconservatism#Iran) among the [Iranian principlists](46181393.md). He is a devotee of and heavily informed by the views of [Taqi Yazdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taqi_Yazdi), being a patron of Yazdi's party, the [Front for Islamic Revolution Stability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front_of_Islamic_Revolution_Stability).[^cite_note-\:82-12][^cite_note-95] According to the [Atlantic Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Council), he is closely associated with those who hold [fundamentalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_fundamentalism_in_Iran) and [Mahdist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahdism) views.[^cite_note-96] Mehmet Ozalp wrote in *[The Conversation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Conversation_\(website\))* that Khamenei "\[might] lean more heavily on the might of the IRGC" than his father did.[^cite_note-\:7-97] *The Telegraph* predicted that he would view the United States as "implacable enemy", would likely escalate the conflict, and was unlikely to make any compromises.[^cite_note-\:5-98] In *[The Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Atlantic)*, [Karim Sadjadpour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karim_Sadjadpour) presented him as "more radical" than his father, who, for instance, would read [Western literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_literature) while Mojtaba has mostly limited himself to [Islamic texts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_literature) and the poetry of [Hafez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafez).[^cite_note-\:10-15] *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent)* quoted [Jaber Rajabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaber_Rajabi), a former classmate of Khamenei and foreign policy adviser to President [Mahmoud Ahmadinejad](7907317.md), as saying that Khamenei is more ruthless than his father and "a better liar".[^cite_note-\:15-99] Rajabi also stated that Khamenei does not value human life and is "not afraid to kill thousands of people."[^cite_note-\:15-99]

In a partisan documentary, Khamenei is portrayed as an "ethics-oriented jurist" and a "professor of human sciences" with extensive knowledge of religious texts; he is also described as familiar with various fields including modern technology, military science, security affairs, political theory, and matters related to state administration.[^cite_note-IranWire-23] One source who knew him as a student described Khamenei as focused on the end of the world and apocalyptic questions, that he thought that Iran had a mission from [God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/God_in_Islam) to bring about the end of the world,[^cite_note-100][^cite_note-101] and that he had a role in accelerating the process.[^cite_note-\:15-99]

### Ties within the IRGC

Khamenei was an influential figure in his father's inner circle, cultivating deep ties to the IRGC. He used the Basij to crack down on peaceful protesters during the [Iranian Green Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Green_Movement) of 2009. Before becoming Supreme Leader, local and foreign-based democracy activists had linked Khamenei to the violent suppression of Iranian protesters.[^cite_note-Aljazeera-Mojtaba-30] According to [Patrick Wintour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Wintour) of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)*, Khamenei was viewed as someone who "would put the IRGC fully at the helm of Iranian politics", sidelining spiritual and moderate political leadership.[^cite_note-102] Despite the successive deaths of Iranian leaders during the 2026 Iran war, analysts noted the IRGC's constant activity and increasing influence within Iran's political structure under Khamenei.[^cite_note-103]

### Support for Ahmadinejad

Khamenei was affiliated with Iranian president [Mahmoud Ahmadinejad](7907317.md),[^cite_note-guardian_20090622-34] and supported Ahmadinejad in the [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Iranian_presidential_election) and [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Iranian_presidential_election) presidential elections.[^cite_note-latimes20090625-104][^cite_note-\:3-45] Journalists stated that Khamenei may "have played a leading role in orchestrating" Ahmadinejad's electoral victory in 2009,[^cite_note-Borger-29][^cite_note-shadow-13] and he was speculated to have been instrumental in orchestrating the crackdown against [anti-government protesters in June 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Iranian_presidential_election_protests).[^cite_note-ctrib_20090625-105]

During the 2009 protests, protesters accused Ali Khamenei of working to facilitate Mojtaba's succession.[^cite_note-106] The slogan "Mojtaba may you die, and not see the leadership" or "Mojtaba die, may you not take the leadership" was chanted by protesters on 9 July in [Tehran](57654.md).[^cite_note-107][^cite_note-108][^cite_note-109] In an open letter, [Mehdi Karroubi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehdi_Karroubi), a [reformist](491098.md) candidate in the 2009 election, accused Khamenei of conspiring to rig the election in Ahmadinejad's favor.[^cite_note-atimes_20090619-110] In 2012, Ahmadinejad accused Khamenei of embezzling from the state treasury.[^cite_note-alarabiya.net-111]

In February 2021, *[Independent Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Persian)* claimed that Ahmadinejad met with Mojtaba Khamenei, who reportedly supported both his views and his candidacy in the [2021 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Iranian_presidential_election).[^cite_note-112] Mehdi Fazaili denied the meeting.[^cite_note-113]

## Personal life



Mojtaba Khamenei and his children on [Quds Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quds_Day) in 2018



Mojtaba Khamenei and other members of the [Qom Seminary](32825717.md), March 2016

He married Zahra Haddad-Adel, a daughter of the ultraconservative politician and writer [Gholam-Ali Haddad-Adel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gholam-Ali_Haddad-Adel),[^cite_note-114][^cite_note-bazoobandi2-115] in 1999.[^cite_note-EN-ZHA-116][^cite_note-117] Their first child was born in 2007.[^cite_note-wltele-118] Their first son is Mohamed Amin, who was followed by their daughter, Fatemeh Sadat, and their second son, Mohammad Bagher. There were reports that due to [fertility issues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fertility_issues), the couple travelled to the United Kingdom four times, staying at [Wellington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellington_Hospital,_London) and [Cromwell Hospitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cromwell_Hospital) for treatment, and that the child was delivered in Iran.[^cite_note-wltele-118][^cite_note-119]

Khamenei's father, wife and one of his sisters [were killed](82537558.md) at the onset of the 2026 Iran war.[^cite_note-NYT-120][^cite_note-121][^cite_note-122]

In addition to his native [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language), he is fluent in [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) and [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language).[^cite_note-IranWire-23]

### Health and public silence

Khamenei had not given public lectures, Friday sermons, or political addresses by March 2026 – to the point that many Iranians have not heard his voice, despite knowing for years that he was a rising power within the theocratic establishment.[^cite_note-Aljazeera-Mojtaba-30] On 6 April, *[The Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times)* reported diplomatic correspondence based on US and Israeli intelligence asserting that Khamenei is unconscious and has been receiving medical treatments for a "severe" medical condition.[^cite_note-\:17-82]

Meanwhile, Iranian deputy foreign minister Saeed Khatibzadeh claimed that Khamenei "is well and in full health, is present in his office, and is in fact in control of everything."[^cite_note-123] Other Iranian officials claim that he is taking part in meetings and making decisions about important issues, including the war,[^cite_note-124][^cite_note-125] while an Iranian official was quoted as saying that he suffered a leg injury only.[^cite_note-126]

### Wealth

Khamenei is widely believed to control significant financial assets in banks such as [Bank Ayandeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_Ayandeh).[^cite_note-alarabiya.net-111][^cite_note-Borger-29]

A year-long investigation by *[Bloomberg](3057255.md)*, citing assessments from people familiar with the matter, reported in January 2026 that Khamenei is linked to an offshore financial network used to hold and move assets outside Iran. The reported holdings include high-value real estate in London and Dubai, as well as interests connected to shipping, banking relationships, and hospitality assets in Europe. According to the investigation, the assets were generally not held in Khamenei's name but structured through intermediaries and layered corporate entities across multiple jurisdictions. Some of these assets have since been sold or restructured amid increased scrutiny.[^cite_note-auto1-127]

*Bloomberg* identified [Ali Ansari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Ansari_\(banker\)), an Iranian businessman sanctioned by the United Kingdom, as a central alleged facilitator in the network. Property records and corporate filings reportedly link Ansari and associated companies to several London properties, including residences on [The Bishops Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bishops_Avenue), and to hotel ownership and management entities in Germany and other countries. Ansari has denied any financial or personal relationship with Khamenei and has stated that he intends to challenge the UK sanctions.[^cite_note-auto1-127]

The investigations further alleged that funds linked to the network largely originated from Iranian oil revenues and were routed through financial institutions in multiple jurisdictions, despite international sanctions imposed on Mojtaba Khamenei in 2019.[^cite_note-auto1-127]

On 14 January 2026, during the [2025–2026 Iranian protests](81976180.md), [US Treasury Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Treasury_Department) Secretary [Scott Bessent](38532044.md) announced that "millions and tens of millions" of dollars have been wired by Iran's leaders to financial institutions worldwide, and Israel's [Channel 14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_14_\(Israel\)) reported that \$1.5 billion in [cryptocurrency](36662188.md) had been sent to an account in Dubai with the involvement of Khamenei (who alone sent approximately \$328 million).[^cite_note-\:1-128]

#### Sanctions

In 2019, Khamenei was placed under [US sanctions](9941859.md) for acting in place of the Supreme Leader without ever being elected or appointed to any official position[^cite_note-Home-Treasury-5] and for working closely with the commander of the [Quds Force](4028355.md), responsible for "covert operations including lethal aid, intelligence, financing, and training" of [Hezbollah](13919.md), [Hamas](13913.md), [Palestinian Islamic Jihad](13093253.md), [Popular Mobilization Forces Iraq](45671374.md) and others;[^cite_note-129] and for fostering close ties with the [Basij](2892082.md) paramilitary group as well as advancing "his father's destabilizing regional ambitions and oppressive domestic objectives".[^cite_note-Home-Treasury-5]
