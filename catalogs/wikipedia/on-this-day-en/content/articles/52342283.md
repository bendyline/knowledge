**Cannabis in North Macedonia** is legal for medical purposes, but illegal for recreational purposes.

## Medical cannabis

On February 9, 2016, the Macedonian Parliament Health Committee gave its approval for the legalization of medical marijuana.[^cite_note-1] Beginning in June 2016, patients without a prescription were allowed to buy oil with 0.2 per cent cannabinoids or less; more concentrated forms require prescription.[^cite_note-2]

## Recreational use

On November 20 2020, the prime minister [Zoran Zaev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoran_Zaev) said the government is looking to legalize the recreational use of marijuana in the country's hospitality places and tourist hotspots, including [Skopje](29618.md) and [Ohrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohrid).[^cite_note-3] However, Zoran Zaev has no longer been Prime Minister of North Macedonia since 2022 and his plans have not been implemented.
