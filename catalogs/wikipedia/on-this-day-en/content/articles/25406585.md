A **multinational force** is a multinational operation which may be [defensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_\(military\)), [offensive](15077993.md), or for [peacekeeping](84617.md) purposes. In multinational operations, many countries form an alliance to carry them out.

Multinational forces include:

- [Supreme Headquarters Allied Expeditionary Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SHAEF) (1943-1945)

- [Multinational Force and Observers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multinational_Force_and_Observers) (1981-present)

- [Operation Uphold Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Uphold_Democracy) (1994-1995)

- [United Nations Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Command) (1950-present)

- [Free World Military Assistance Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_World_Military_Assistance_Forces) (1965-1973)

- [Caribbean Peace Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caribbean_Peace_Force) (1983-1985)
