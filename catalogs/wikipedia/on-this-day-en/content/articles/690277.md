The [Republic of Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Yemen) is divided into twenty-one [governorates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorate) (*[muhafazah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhafazah)*) and one municipality ([amanah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanah_\(administrative_division\))).[^cite_note-1]

The governorates are subdivided into 333 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Yemen) (*muderiah*), which are subdivided into 1,996 sub-districts, and then into 40,793 villages and 88,817 sub villages (as of 2013).[^cite_note-CSO-2]

Before reunification in 1990, Yemen existed as two separate countries. [South Yemen](718749.md) consisted of modern Aden, Abyan, Mahrah, Hadramaut, Socotra, Lahij, and Shabwah Governorates, while the rest made up [North Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemen_Arab_Republic) (also 70% of the area of Dhale Governorate). For more information, see [Historic Governorates of Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historic_Governorates_of_Yemen).
