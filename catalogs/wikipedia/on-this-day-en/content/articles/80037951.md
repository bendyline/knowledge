The 2025–26 **Saudi Pro League** (known as the **[Roshn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshn) Saudi League** for sponsorship reasons) was the 69th season of the [top-tier Saudi football league](4525043.md), established in 1957, and the 18th season since it was rebranded as the **Saudi Pro League** in [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008%E2%80%9309_Saudi_Pro_League).

[Al-Ittihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ittihad_Club_\(Jeddah\)) were the defending champions, having won their 14th title [last season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Saudi_Pro_League), however they failed to defend the league title this season, losing out to [Al-Nassr](2535118.md), who won their 11th league title. [^cite_note-1] [Al-Najma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Najma_SC_\(Saudi_Arabia\)) and [Neom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neom_SC) earned automatic promotion to the Pro League, while [Al-Hazem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hazem_FC) earned the last promotion spot through the play-offs. [Al-Raed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Raed_S.FC), [Al-Orobah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Orobah_FC), and [Al-Wehda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Wehda_FC) were relegated to the [2025–26 Saudi First Division League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Saudi_First_Division_League).

18 teams will compete in the league – the top 15 teams from the previous season and the 3 teams promoted from the [FD League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Saudi_First_Division_League).

### Teams who were promoted to the Pro League

On 22 April 2025, [Neom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neom_SC) became the first team to be promoted following a 3–0 away win against [Al-Arabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Arabi_SC_\(Saudi_Arabia\)).[^cite_note-2] They were crowned champions following a 6–0 away win against [Jeddah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeddah_Club) on 29 April 2025.[^cite_note-3] Neom will play in the top flight of Saudi football for the first time in their history and will be the 40th side to participate in the Saudi Pro League since its inception. In addition, they will become the second side to represent the [Tabuk Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabuk_Province) following [Al-Watani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Watani_Club)'s promotion in 2007.

On 12 May 2025, [Al-Najma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Najma_SC_\(Saudi_Arabia\)) became the second club to be promoted following a 2–0 home win against [Ohod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohod_Club).[^cite_note-4] Al-Najma return to the top flight for the first time since getting relegated in the [2002–03 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002%E2%80%9303_Saudi_Premier_League) and will play in their 12th season in the top flight. In addition, this will be their first season in the Pro League era.

On 29 May 2025, [Al-Hazem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hazem_FC) became the third and final club to be promoted after defeating [Al-Adalah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Adalah_FC) 3–0 in the promotion play-offs final.[^cite_note-5] This was Al-Hazem's fourth promotion in seven years and will play in their 11th season in the top flight.

### Teams who were relegated to the FD League

[Al-Raed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Raed_S.FC) were the first team to be relegated following a 1–0 defeat away to [Damac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damac_FC) on 11 May 2025.[^cite_note-6] Al-Raed were relegated after 17 consecutive seasons in the flight. This was also their first-ever relegation in the Pro League era.

On 25 May 2025, [Al-Orobah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Orobah_FC) became the second team to be relegated after they were docked three points for fielding an ineligible player against [Al-Nassr](2535118.md).[^cite_note-7] Al-Orobah were relegated after one season in the top flight.

[Al-Wehda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Wehda_FC) were the third and final club to be relegated, following a 2–1 away defeat to [Al-Ettifaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ettifaq_Club) on the final matchday.[^cite_note-8] Al-Wehda were relegated after three seasons in the top flight. In addition, this was their eighth relegation in history.
