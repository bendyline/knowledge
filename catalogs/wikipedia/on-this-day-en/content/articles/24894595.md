**Bukkuyum** (or **Bukwium**) is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Zamfara State](2227664.md), [Nigeria](21383.md). It has an area of 3,214 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 211,633 at the 2006 [census](6889.md).

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 891.[^cite_note-1]

## Geography

Bukkuyum local government area spans 3,214 [square kilometres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_kilometre) or 1,241 [square miles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_miles), experiencing an average temperature of 35 [degrees Celsius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celsius) or 95 [degrees Fahrenheit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degrees_Fahrenheit). It is densely covered with [forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest), including the Gando Forest Reserve. The humidity is measured at 15 percent, and the average wind speed is around 10 [km/h](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometres_per_hour) or 6 [mph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miles_per_hour).[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-3]

### Climate and Temperature

The environment has a hot, oppressive rainy [season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Season) with predominantly cloudy skies and a blistering, partly cloudy dry season with [temperatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature) ranging from 16 °C (61 °F) to 39 °C (103 °F).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

The temperature trend in Bukkuyum is rising, with warmer years denoting a more hospitable [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) and colder years denoting a less hospitable climate.[^cite_note-6]

## Economy



Bukkuyum Local Government Area, Zamfara state, Nigeria

The primary livelihood in Bukkuyum Local Government Area revolves around crop [cultivation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop_farming), particularly known for cultivating [crops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop) like [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton) and [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet). Moreover, the region possesses abundant [mineral resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_resource), including lead and [gold](12240.md), leading to a notable surge in mining activities in recent times.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

In March 2017 more than 300 children died of lead poisoning from an illegal mining site at yar Galma village at [12°08′00″N 5°28′00″E  /  12.13333°N 5.46667°E  / 12.13333; 5.46667](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Bukkuyum\&params=12_08_00_N_5_28_00_E_region\:AU_type\:city_source\:GNS-enwiki) in the north-west of the area.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]
