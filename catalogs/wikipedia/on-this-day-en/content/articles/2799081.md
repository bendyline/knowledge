This article lists the **heads of state of South Sudan** since the establishment of the [Southern Sudan Autonomous Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Sudan_Autonomous_Region_\(1972%E2%80%931983\)) within [Sudan](27421.md) in 1972.

The president of the Republic of South Sudan is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [head of government](37570.md) of [South Sudan](32350676.md). The president leads the [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) of the [Government of South Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_South_Sudan) and is the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [South Sudan People's Defence Forces](19196675.md). The official residence of the president is State House, J1.

## History of the office

The region of Southern Sudan (currently the independent republic of [South Sudan](32350676.md)) became autonomous for the first time, within [Sudan](27421.md), in 1972, through the [Addis Ababa Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addis_Ababa_Agreement_\(1972\)) meant to end the [First Sudanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Sudanese_Civil_War), and its local government had five [presidents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)) until 1983, when the Sudanese central government revoked the autonomy. Autonomy was gained again in 2005, through the [Comprehensive Peace Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comprehensive_Peace_Agreement) meant to end the [Second Sudanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sudanese_Civil_War), and the position of president of Southern Sudan was restored. Then, on 9 July 2011, [South Sudan](32350676.md) became independent and a new constitution was adopted.[^cite_note-newConstitution-1]

## Term limits

[Salva Kiir](2363203.md)'s term as elected president ended in 2015, but constitutional amendments of 2018 and the [Revitalised Transitional Government of National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_South_Sudan#Revitalized_Transitional_Government_of_National_Unity_\(RTGoNU\)) (RTGoNU) formed in 2020 extended his mandate until 2023[^cite_note-termlimits-3] and later 2024.[^cite_note-4]

## Titles of heads of state

- **1972–1983**: President of the High Executive Council

- **2005–2011**: President of the Government

- **2011–present**: President
