# Government contractor

A **government contractor** is a company ([privately owned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_company), [publicly traded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_company) or a [state-owned enterprise](204768.md)) – either [for profit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/For_profit) or [non-profit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-profit) – that produces [goods or services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goods_and_services) under contract for the [government](12229.md).[^cite_note-1] Some communities are largely sustained by government contracting activity; for instance, much of the economy of [Northern Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Virginia) consists of government contractors employed directly or indirectly by the [federal government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_government) of the [United States](3434750.md).[^cite_note-fedjobs-2]

## Terminology

Frequently a term **public contractor** is used to describe the government contractor.

In [ancient Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome), term [publican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publican) was used for private individuals that performed work on public buildings, supplied Roman armies, or collected taxes.[^cite_note-3]

## United Kingdom

Section 12(2) and (3) of the [Official Secrets Act 1989](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_Secrets_Act_1989) define the expression "Government Contractor" for the purposes of that Act.[^cite_note-4]

## United States

The [United States federal government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_federal_government) seeks to select contractors in a way that is fair to all and promotes the [free market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_market) system. Contractors are selected via [Federal Acquisition Regulations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Acquisition_Regulations) which are intended by [Congress](31756.md) to ensure that taxpayer money is used to benefit the public or nation as a whole. Contractors must learn how to operate within these regulations.[^cite_note-5]

### Prime contractor

**Prime contractor** is a term defined in the US law.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENicastro20231-6][^cite_note-FOOTNOTECarrilDuggan2020-7] Statutory definitions of prime contract, prime contractor, subcontract, and subcontractor are in [41 U.S.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title_41_of_the_United_States_Code) [§ 8701](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/41/8701).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENicastro20231Note_5-8] The prime contractor term was already defined before the 8 March 1946 passage of&#x20;*&#x20;An Act To eliminate the practice by subcontractors, under cost-plus-a-fixed-fee or cost reimbursable contacts of the United States, of paying fees or kick-backs, or of granting gifts or gratuities to employees of a cost-plus-a-fixed-fee or cost reimbursable prime contractors or of higher tier subcontractors for the purpose of securing the award of subcontracts or orders.* (Pub. L.Tooltip Public Law (United States) 79–319, 60 Stat. 37)
