**Krasnodar Krai**[^cite_note-9] is a [federal subject](701438.md) of [Russia](25391.md) (a [krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krai)) located in the [North Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Caucasus) region in [Southern Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Russia) and administratively part of the [Southern Federal District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Federal_District). Its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) is the [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) of [Krasnodar](474128.md). The [third most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_federal_subjects_of_Russia_by_population) federal subject in Russia, it had a population of 5,838,273 as of the [2021 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Census_\(2021\)).[^cite_note-2021Census-6]

Krasnodar Krai is formally and informally referred to as **Kuban** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Кубань), a term denoting the historical region of [Kuban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban) situated between the [Sea of Azov](78019.md) and the [Kuban River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban_River), which is predominantly within the krai. It is bordered by [Rostov Oblast](478744.md) to the north, [Stavropol Krai](474074.md) to the east, [Karachay-Cherkessia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachay-Cherkessia) to the south-east. [Adygea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adygea) is an [enclave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enclave) entirely within the krai. Krasnodar Krai shares an international border with [Georgia](48768.md) and the partially-recognized republic of [Abkhazia](18933375.md) to the south, and borders [annexed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Crimea_by_the_Russian_Federation) [Crimea](163045.md) to the west, across the [Kerch Strait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerch_Strait).

The northern part of the krai takes in part of the [Don Steppe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Steppe), while the southern part has a distinctive [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate), which has made it a popular tourist destination. [Novorossiysk](1190336.md) is Russia's main port on the [Black Sea](3386.md), one of the few cities awarded the title of the [Hero City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_City_\(Soviet_Union\)), and [Sochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sochi) was the host of the [XXII Olympic Winter Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XXII_Olympic_Winter_Games) in 2014. Krasnodar Krai is home to significant infrastructure of the [Russian Navy](383258.md)'s [Black Sea Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea_Fleet).

Krasnodar Krai is located in the southwestern part of the [North Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Caucasus) and borders [Rostov Oblast](478744.md) in the northeast, [Stavropol Krai](474074.md) and [Karachay-Cherkessia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachay-Cherkessia) in the east, and the [Abkhazia](18933375.md) region (internationally recognized as part of [Georgia](48768.md)) in the south.[^cite_note-OffSiteGeo-10] The [Republic of Adygea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Adygea) is completely encircled by the krai territory. The krai's [Taman Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taman_Peninsula) is situated between the [Sea of Azov](78019.md) in the north and the [Black Sea](3386.md) in the south.[^cite_note-Gorshenyov-11] In the west, the [Kerch Strait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerch_Strait) separates the krai from the [contested](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Status_of_Crimea) [Crimean Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean_Peninsula), internationally recognised as [part](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_Republic_of_Crimea) of [Ukraine](31750.md) but under *[de facto](43594.md)* [Russian control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Crimea_\(Russia\)).[^cite_note-Gorshenyov-11] At its widest extent, the krai stretches for 327 kilometers (203 mi) from north to south and for 360 kilometers (220 mi) from east to west.[^cite_note-OffSiteGeo-10]

The krai is split into two distinct parts by the [Kuban River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban_River), which gave its name to this entire geographic region.[^cite_note-Gorshenyov-11] The southern, seaward part is the western extremity of the [Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus) range, lying within the [Crimean Submediterranean forest complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean_Submediterranean_forest_complex) ecoregion;[^cite_note-WWF-12] the climate is [Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate) or, in the southeast, [subtropical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropics).[^cite_note-Gorshenyov-11] Historically it is known as [Circassia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circassia). The northern part is a [steppe zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontic%E2%80%93Caspian_steppe) which shares [continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_climate) patterns.[^cite_note-Gorshenyov-11] It is also known as [Kuban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban) region.

See also: [Kuban Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban_Oblast) and [Black Sea Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea_Governorate)

The Black Sea coast stretches from the Kerch Strait to [Adler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adler_Microdistrict) and is shielded by the [Caucasus Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus_Mountains) from the cold northern winds.[^cite_note-Gorshenyov-11] Mountains exceed 3,000 meters (9,800 ft) in height, with [Mount Tsakhvoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Tsakhvoa) being the highest at 3,346 meters (10,978 ft),[^cite_note-Gorshenyov-11] with [Mount Fisht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Fisht), at 2,867 meters (9,406 ft), being the Great Caucasus' westernmost peak with a [glacier](12463.md).[^cite_note-Gorshenyov-11] Numerous small mountain rivers flow in the coastal areas, often creating picturesque waterfalls.[^cite_note-Gorshenyov-11] [Lake Abrau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Abrau), located in the wine-making region of [Abrau-Dyurso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abrau-Dyurso), is the largest lake in the northeastern Caucasus region.[^cite_note-Gorshenyov-11]

## History



[Phanagoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phanagoria), ancient Greek city and former capital of early medieval [Old Great Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Great_Bulgaria)

The region's earliest known inhabitants are referred to, generally, as the [*Maiōtai*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maeotians), who are the ancestors of the modern-day [Circassians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circassians), [Abkhazians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abkhazians) and [Abazins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abazins)[^cite_note-13] (after the Greek name for the [Sea of Azov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maeotian_Swamp)). During the 6th century BC, [Pontic Greeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontic_Greeks) founded the area's first cities, such as *[Phanagoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phanagoria)* (near modern [Sennoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sennoy,_Krasnodar_Krai)) and *[Hermonassa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermonassa)* (on the [Taman Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taman_Peninsula)), who traded with nomadic tribes including the [*Skuthai*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scythians) (Scythians) and [*Sindi*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindi_people).

In the 7th century, Phanagoria served as the capital of [Old Great Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Great_Bulgaria). From the 8th to the 10th centuries, the area was dominated by the [Khazars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khazars), a [Turkic people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkic_people) who had earlier migrated from the east onto the [Pontic-Caspian Steppe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontic-Caspian_Steppe), where [a hypothesis claims that they converted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khazar_hypothesis_of_Ashkenazi_ancestry) to [Judaism](15624.md). After the defeat of the [Khazar Khanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khazar_Khanate) in 965 [Kievan Prince](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Prince_of_Kiev) [Svyatoslav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svyatoslav_I_of_Kiev) conquered the area, it came under the rule of [Kievan Rus'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kievan_Rus'), and it then formed the [Tmutarakan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tmutarakan) principality. Later, due to the increasing claims of the [Eastern Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Empire) at the end of the 11th century, the Tmutarakan principality came under the authority of the Eastern Roman Emperors (until 1204).

In that period of history, the [Circassians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circassians) were first mentioned, under the ethnonym *[Kassogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kassogs)*. For example, the [Kassog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kassoges) Prince [Rededya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rededya) was mentioned in *[The Tale of Igor's Campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tale_of_Igor's_Campaign)*.[^cite_note-14]

In 1243–1438, the current territory of the Kuban was part of the [Golden Horde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Horde). After the latter's collapse, parts of Kuban were held under the [Crimean Khanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean_Khanate), [Circassia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circassia), and the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md), which dominated the region. The [Lesser Nogai Horde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesser_Nogai_Horde) was the [Nogai Tatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nogais) territory in Kuban, allied with the Crimean Khanate.[^cite_note-15] The [Tsardom of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsardom_of_Russia) began to challenge the protectorate of the Ottoman Empire in the area during the [Russo-Turkish wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Russo-Turkish_wars).



Krasnodar Krai

In April 1783, after the liquidation of the [Crimean Khanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean_Khanate), right-bank Kuban and Taman Peninsula were annexed to the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) by decree of [Catherine II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_II). In 1792–1794, the [Cossacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cossacks) moved there from [Zaporizhzhia](1873579.md), now located in Ukraine, and formed the Black Sea Area troops, with the creation of a solid cordon line for the [Kuban River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban_River) and [the marginalization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circassian_genocide) of the neighboring [Circassians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circassians). The administrative region was accorded the status of "Land of Black Sea Cossack Army", with its center in the city of [Yekaterinodar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yekaterinodar) (renamed Krasnodar by the Bolsheviks in 1920).

In 1900, the region's population numbered around two million people. In 1913, the gross grain harvest Kuban region entered second place in Russia, for the production of marketable grain – in first place. During the [Russian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War), the anti-communist [Kuban People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban_People's_Republic) was established on January 28, 1918. It sought union with the [Ukrainian People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_People's_Republic) during its brief independence until Soviet forces occupied the latter in May 1920.

Krasnodar Krai was founded on September 13, 1937, when [Azov-Black Sea Krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azov-Black_Sea_Krai) of the [Russian SFSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Socialist_Federative_Soviet_Republic) was split up in Krasnodar Krai and [Rostov Oblast](478744.md). The [Greek Autonomous District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_Autonomous_District) and [Shapsug national district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shapsug_national_district) were located within the Krai. On January 30, 1996, Krasnodar Krai signed a power-sharing agreement with the federal government, granting it autonomy.[^cite_note-asymmetries-16] This agreement would be abolished on April 12, 2002.[^cite_note-demokratiztsiya-17]

### 2012 floods

Main article: [2012 Russian floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Russian_floods)

On July 7, 2012, at least 171 people died in Krasnodar Krai, after torrential rains overnight caused the worst flooding and landslides in more than seventy years.[^cite_note-Russia144-18][^cite_note-Russia150-19] Over 280 millimeters (11 in) of rain – the typical amount for a four- or five-month period – was reported to have fallen within forty-eight hours.[^cite_note-Russia100-20] A local police spokesman stated that most of the dead were in [Krymsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krymsky_District), where at least 159 died when a wave of water 5 meters (16 ft) high swept through the town of [Krymsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krymsk) in the middle of the night.[^cite_note-Russia150-19][^cite_note-Russia100-20] Ten more deaths occurred in [Gelendzhik](1462700.md), including five electrocuted when a [transformer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformer) fell into the floodwater, and two in [Novorossiysk](1190336.md).[^cite_note-Russia144-18][^cite_note-Russia100-20] Authorities stated that 17 people had been officially reported missing, and there were fears the death toll would rise further, while medics had hospitalized 210 people, including 16 children.

The regional government claimed that over 24,000 people were affected by the floods, with more than 3,000 evacuated, and that more than 10,000 rescuers and 140 helicopters were searching for victims and evacuating survivors.[^cite_note-Russia144-18][^cite_note-Russia100-20] In [Krymsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krymsk), 14 temporary shelters were set up to house around 2,000 evacuees. The transport system in the region was said to have collapsed, while oil shipments from Novorossiysk were halted when [the port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Novorossiysk), located in the lower part of the city, was threatened by landslides.[^cite_note-Russia144-18][^cite_note-Russia100-20][^cite_note-Putin-21] Russia's President [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) flew to the area to hold emergency talks with officials in Krymsk, while authorities in [Perm Krai](1765550.md) dispatched a rescue team to evacuate dozens of children from the region, who had been staying at summer camps on the [Black Sea](3386.md) coast.[^cite_note-Russia144-18][^cite_note-Putin-21]

Residents of Krymsk claimed the wave of water that hit the town resulted from the [sluice gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sluice_gate) of a nearby reservoir being opened, although the prosecutor general's investigative committee denied this. Local prosecutors had earlier confirmed that the gates were opened, but stated that it was too early to determine whether this was the cause of the flooding.[^cite_note-Russia150-19]

## Politics



Krai Administration building in Krasnodar

During the [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) period, the high authority in the krai was shared between three persons: the first secretary of the Krasnodar CPSU Committee (who in reality had the greatest authority), the chairman of the Krai Soviet (legislative power), and the chairman of the Krai Executive Committee (executive power). In 1991, the CPSU lost power, and the head of the krai administration, and eventually the governor have been since appointed/elected alongside elected [regional parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_parliaments_of_Russia).

The Charter of Krasnodar Krai is the fundamental law of the region. The [Legislative Assembly of Krasnodar Krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Krasnodar_Krai) is the province's standing legislative (representative) body. The assembly exercises its authority by passing laws, resolutions, and other legal acts and by supervising the implementation and observance of the laws and other legal acts passed by it. The highest executive body is the krai government, which includes territorial executive bodies such as district administrations, committees, and commissions that facilitate development and run the day-to-day matters of the province. The krai administration supports the governor's activities, who is the highest official and acts as the guarantor of the observance of the Krai Charter in accordance with the [Constitution of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Russia).

## Administrative divisions

Main article: [Administrative divisions of Krasnodar Krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Krasnodar_Krai)

Krasnodar Krai is administratively divided into thirty-eight [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raion) (*raions*) and fifteen [cities of district equivalence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance). The districts and cities are further subdivided into eleven [towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_of_district_significance), plus [urban-type settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban-type_settlement_of_district_significance), and [rural okrugs and stanitsa okrugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selsoviet).

## Economy

As a result of [2014 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Winter_Olympics), Krasnodar Krai has seen significant infrastructure spending.[^cite_note-22] Over \$50 billion has been spent on various infrastructure projects including a bullet train.

Large companies in the region include [Tander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnit), Novorossmetal, Autonomous Heat Energy Company, Gazprom gas distribution Krasnodar, and Evrokhim Chemical Fertilizers.[^cite_note-23]

Krasnoday Krai's top export is refined petroleum, with other significant exports including wheat, hot-rolled iron bars, seed oils, and asphalt mixtures.[^cite_note-OEC-24]

### Transportation

Several lines of [Russian Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Railways) cross the region and link it with [Abkhazia](18933375.md), [Ukraine](31750.md), and neighboring Russian regions. There are direct trains from resort cities like [Sochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sochi) and [Anapa](1194038.md) to [Moscow](19004.md), via [Krasnodar](474128.md), which become very popular during the summer vacation season. There are also suburb train connections. The [Apsheronsk narrow-gauge railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apsheronsk_narrow-gauge_railway), the longest [mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_railway) [narrow-gauge railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrow-gauge_railway) in Russia, runs through Krasnodar Krai.

There are several airports in the region, including [Krasnodar International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krasnodar_International_Airport), [Sochi International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sochi_International_Airport), [Anapa Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anapa_Airport), and [Gelendzhik Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelendzhik_Airport).

The biggest ports are [Novorossiysk](1190336.md) and [Tuapse](1623160.md). Others are [Eisk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisk) and [Temryuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temryuk) on the [Azov Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azov_Sea), and [Port Kavkaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Kavkaz), [Taman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taman,_Russia), [Anapa](1194038.md), [Gelendzhik](1462700.md), and [Sochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sochi) on the [Black Sea](3386.md). The population of Krasnodar Krai is concentrated in the [Kuban River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban_River) drainage basin, which was traditionally [Cossack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cossack) land (se&#x65;*&#x20;[History of Cossacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Cossacks)*). The [Kuban Cossacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban_Cossacks) are now generally considered to be ethnic Russians, even though they are still an important minority in their own right in the area. [Historically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban_Cossacks#National_identity), they were considered to be ethnic Ukrainian, and reported their language as Ukrainian in censuses well into the 20th century (see [*National Identity of Kuban Cossacks*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuban_Cossacks#National_identity)). This change in identity is due to assimilation and historical persecution of the Kuban Cossacks, which was particularly prominent due to questions of their loyalty to Moscow and the Communist state during the Russian Revolution and First World War.

Other notable ethnic groups include the Adyghe, who are the region's indigenous population and were nearly completely annihilated following the [Circassian genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circassian_genocide),[^cite_note-RLS-31] and the [Armenians](387816.md) (including Christian [Hamsheni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamsheni) and [Cherkesogai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherkesogai)), who have lived in the region since at least the 18th century.

According to a 2012 survey[^cite_note-2012ArenaAtlas-34] 52.2% of the population of Krasnodar Krai adheres to the [Russian Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Orthodox_Church), 3% are [unaffiliated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nondenominational_Christianity) generic [Christians](18337522.md), 1% are either Orthodox Christian believers who do not belong to church or members of non-Russian Orthodox churches, and 1% are [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim). In addition, 22% of the population declares to be "spiritual but not religious", 13% is [atheist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheism), and 7.8% follows other religions or did not give an answer to the question.[^cite_note-2012ArenaAtlas-34]
