**Rio Branco** (Portuguese pronunciation: [\[ˈʁi.u ˈbɾɐ̃ku\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Portuguese); lit. 'White River') is a Brazilian [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Brazil), capital of the state of [Acre](229497.md).[^cite_note-4] Located in the valley of the [Acre River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre_River) in [northern Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Brazil), it is the most populous municipality in the [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Brazil), with 387,852 inhabitants, according to 2024 [IBGE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBGE) estimates,[^cite_note-auto-5] almost half the state population. Rio Branco was one of the [first settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Brazil#Rubber) to develop in the region, being the westernmost major settlement in the country and the 4th-oldest state capital city in [Northern Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Region,_Brazil), after [Belém](409573.md), [Manaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manaus) and [Macapá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macap%C3%A1).

In 1913, it became a county. In 1920, it became the capital of the territory of Acre, and in 1962, the state capital. It is the administrative center for the economic, political and cultural region.

The capital of the state of [Acre](229497.md) developed from the [rubber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_rubber) plantation founded on December 28, [1882](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1882), by migrant settlers from the [Northeastern Region of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_Region,_Brazil). Then called Seringal Volta da Empresa, it was located on the right bank of the [Acre river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre_river). The [Gameleira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strangler_fig) tree marks this site. This is now classified as the Second District of the city.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

Almost 150 years ago, the construction of workers' shacks had begun, in lands previously occupied by the [Aquiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquiri?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Canamari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canamari?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Maneteri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maneteri?action=edit\&redlink=1) Amazonian indigenous tribes. Years later, the same Gameleira tree survived the battles fought at Volta da Empresa, between Acre revolutionaries and Bolivian troops, during the critical period of the [Acre War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre_War). As a result, Brazil acquired Acre at the beginning of the 20th century.[^cite_note-8]

By August 1904, Villa Rio Branco had become the main urban center of the entire Acre valley, and was the richest and most productive in the region. During this period of the rubber boom, the streets around the Gameleira were the center of commercial and urban life in this part of the [Amazon](48139.md). Bars, cafes, and casinos supplied the city's nightlife; commercial representatives came here of the chief national and foreign [Aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviation) houses that handled thousands of [Contos de Réis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_real_\(old\)). There were many wealthy families in the city.[^cite_note-9]

The urban elite was largely composed of liberal professionals and civil servants. The political administration of the Territory was transferred to the left bank of the Acre River, because its lands were higher and not as subject to [flooding](50482.md) as the old city centre. The early commercial zone gradually became dominated by [Syrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Brazilians) and [Lebanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Brazilian) immigrants. By the mid-[1930s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930s), one area was known as *"Bairro Beirut"* (Beirut neighborhood).[^cite_note-10]

By the [1950s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950s), this older section had become run down and known as the Second District. As a large part of its main commercial houses had transferred to the city's First District, on the left bank of the river, following the construction there of the main public offices and residences of the most important families. Today, Rio Branco is seeking to find ways to reinvent itself, through relations with neighboring countries, developing heritage and eco-tourism, building on the local fish farming industry, and so on. In spite of its remote location, Rio Branco is one of the oldest cities in the region, predating other [state capitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_capitals_of_subdivisions_of_Brazil) of the [Brazilian interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_interior?action=edit\&redlink=1), like Goiânia, Belo Horizonte, Porto Velho, Brasília, Palmas, and Boa Vista.[^cite_note-11]

## Geography

Rio Branco is located at 9° 58' 29" south and 67° 48' 36" west, at an altitude of 143 metres (469 ft) above sea level. The city has developed on both sides of the [Acre River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre_River), with areas known as the First (left bank) and Second Districts. The river is crossed by six bridges, the newest bridge being the Joaquim Macedo Catwalk.

Rio Branco is located in the [mesoregion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesoregion_\(Brazil\)) of Vale do Jurua and the [microregion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microregion_\(Brazil\)) of Rio Branco. It is bordered on the north by the municipalities of [Bujari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bujari) and [Porto Acre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto_Acre); to the south by the municipalities of [Xapuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xapuri), [Capixaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capixaba,_Acre), and [Plácido de Castro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pl%C3%A1cido_de_Castro,_Acre); to the east by the municipality of [Senador Guiomard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senador_Guiomard); and to the west by the municipality of [Sena Madureira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sena_Madureira).

### Vegetation

The municipality contains part of the 931,537 hectares (2,301,880 acres) [Chico Mendes Extractive Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chico_Mendes_Extractive_Reserve), a sustainable use environmental unit created in 1990.[^cite_note-12]

### Climate



Rio Branco on a cool day of 16 °C or 60.8 °F



Rio Branco faces [seasonal floodings](50482.md) at times, as the one pictured; March 2023, when over 15,000 people were displaced, due to a total of 585.9 mm of [rain](19009110.md), in that month, only.

The city of Rio Branco has the lowest average annual temperature among [Legal Amazonian](1305102.md) and [Northeastern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_Region,_Brazil) capitals. The city has a [tropical monsoon climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate), with temperatures between 23 and 33 °C or 73.4 and 91.4 °F during the hottest days of the year. The lowest temperatures occur at night, with frequent records of 22 °C or 71.6 °F at dawn. The period from December to March is the hottest time of year, with highs of 38 °C or 100.4 °F or more. Usually between May and August, the region experiences a cooler weather, registering lower temperatures (around 15 °C or 59 °F) compared to regional standards. In July 2010 the city experienced record low temperatures. On the afternoon of the 17th, temperatures were registered to be 14.7 °C or 58.5 °F on average with a minimum of 12.1 °C or 53.8 °F. The 19th stood out with a minimum at 9.8 °C or 49.6 °F. However, the month's maximum reached 28 °C or 82.4 °F.[^cite_note-weather-13]

Rio Branco has a lengthy [wet season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_season) spanning from October through May and a relatively short [dry season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season) covering the remaining four months. The city on average sees roughly 1.9 metres or 6 feet 3 inches of rainfall annually. Rio Branco experiences its heaviest rainfall from December through March, averaging over 200 millimetres or 8 inches of precipitation per month during that timeframe. The [Acre River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre_River) has many [floodplain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floodplain) spots.

## Economy



1st District of Rio Branco, a historically neglected region, which, over time, became the part of the city where the commerce concentrates the most. On the left, the south part of Rio Branco, a region linked to the [ring road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_road) of the city (Via Verde) which has the presence of large enterprises such as; shopping malls, [wholesale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wholesale) stores, government agencies, universities, soccer stadiums, convention centers, parks, etc. On the right, one can see the neighborhoods of Bosque, Estação Experimental, Jardim Europa, Abraão Alab, Cerâmica, Downtown Rio Branco among others.

Historically, the economy of Rio Branco has been based on the extraction of [rubber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_rubber) and [brazil nuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_nut). Currently, [wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood) is the main product of export in the state of Acre, which it is also a major producer of [açaí fruit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%A7a%C3%AD_palm), [farinha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farofa), [copaiba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copaiba) oil and [beef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beef). Rio Branco is currently undergoing a transformation in its economy; transformation that includes an expansion to [Agribusiness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agribusiness), [Aquaculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquaculture) and [Ecotourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecotourism). Brazil's largest oil and gas company [Petrobras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrobras), says the region has potential for future [extraction of petroleum](8082027.md).[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

### Transportation



The Terminal Urbano bus station is located in the city centre.



The Via Verde Ring Road is the city's [ring road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_road) and it connects the main highways that reach Rio Branco, which are; the [BR-364](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BR-364) and the [Interoceanic Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interoceanic_Highway). In it, one can also find the [Via Verde Mall](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Via%20Verde%20Shopping) - one of the city's shopping malls - important hospitals, schools, hotels and the [Rio Branco International Bus Station](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodovi%C3%A1ria%20Internacional%20de%20Rio%20Branco).

The public transport system has improved in recent years, mainly due to the construction of the Terminal Urbano (Urban Bus Terminal) near the center of town. However, the buses are still delayed. Taxis, including motorcycle taxis, are widely used by the population. Rio Branco is the [Brazilian state capital city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_capitals_of_subdivisions_of_Brazil) with the largest proportional [bike path](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bike_path) network per inhabitant in the country.[^cite_note-25]

[Rio Branco-Plácido de Castro International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Branco_International_Airport) is 18 kilometres (11 mi) away on the [BR-364](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BR-364) highway in the rural area. This new airport was officially opened on November 22, 1999, when the older facility, [Presidente Médici International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidente_M%C3%A9dici_International_Airport), was closed. The BR-364 was twinned to facilitate access to the airport, which serves domestic and international aviation and military operations, with scheduled airlines and air taxis. The terminal is ready to receive 320,000 passengers per year and performs about 14 operations a day. By August 2010 it was the seventh-busiest airport in [Northern Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Brazil), and the 38th busiest airport in the country.

The [BR-364](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BR-364) and the [BR-317](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BR-317?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([Interoceanic Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interoceanic_Highway)) are the main highways in Acre, and the Via Verde Ring Road is the one that connects them. To the east, the BR-364 connects Rio Branco to [Southeastern Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeast_Region,_Brazil). The highway cuts west across the state, linking Rio Branco to [Cruzeiro do Sul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruzeiro_do_Sul,_Acre), the second major city of the state, through the municipalities of [Sena Madureira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sena_Madureira), [Manoel Urbano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manoel_Urbano), [Feijó](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feij%C3%B3,_Acre), [Tarauacá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarauac%C3%A1), and [Rodrigues Alves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodrigues_Alves,_Acre). The BR-317 has a length of 330 kilometres (210 mi), and links the capital to the south of the state, through the municipalities of Senador Guiomard, Capixaba, and [Epitaciolândia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epitaciol%C3%A2ndia), on the border with Republic of [Bolivia](3462.md). From [Brasiléia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brasil%C3%A9ia) the road continues for another 110 kilometres (68 mi) to reach the city of [Assis Brasil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assis_Brasil) on the border with [Peru](170691.md). The highway continues on the Peruvian side as part of the [Interoceanic Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interoceanic_Highway)[^cite_note-nyt-2014feb19-26] and as Highway 30C and Highway 26 to the city of [Cuzco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuzco).

### Attractions

The Rio Branco Palace was built in 1930 for the seat of the state government. The building was restored beginning in 1999 to preserve its historical character. In 2008 it was transformed into a museum of history.

Gameleira is a historical site located in the curve of the Acre River, where city was first founded. Today, more than a century later, the [strangler fig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strangler_fig) located there is a vigorous tree, measuring 2.5 metres (8.2 ft) in diameter at the trunk and 20 metres (66 ft) in height.

Opened in 1958, the Cathedral of [Our Lady of Nazareth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legend_of_Nazar%C3%A9) was constructed in the style of an ancient Roman basilica. Its interior has three naves separated by 36 stained-glass windows, donated by families of Acre. The exterior is composed of gables, the cross, and the churchyard. In 2007 the church was considered public property of the state of Acre.



[Rodeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodeo) at ExpoAcre [Field Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_day_\(agriculture\)), in Rio Branco, held in the months of July and August.

Also known as the Square of the Revolution, Square Plácido de Castro is located in the heart of the city, opposite the headquarters of the Military Police of Acre. Recently, the square underwent renovation. The Autonomists Memorial has a museum, exhibitions of paintings by regional artists, a coffee shop, and a drama theatre. The museum possesses a large collection of historic photos of the state, as well as historical objects used during the Revolution. The [Museum of Rubber](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museu%20da%20Borracha) includes sections on archeology and paleontology, and a historical collection of manuscripts and documents relating to the history of Acre.

The Catwalk Joaquim Macedo is a pedestrian bridge linking the two districts of the city. Passing over the Acre River, the catwalk is a cable suspension bridge completed in 2006. The nearby Old Market, built in the 1920s, was recently refurbished. Maternity Park was opened on September 28, 2002. It has sports facilities, kiosks, restaurants, biking trails, and skate parks.

Rio Branco has two shopping malls: the Mira Shopping and the [Via Verde Shopping](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Via%20Verde%20Shopping), which was completed in 2011.[^cite_note-29]

The sepulcher of [Raimundo Irineu Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestre_Irineu), founder of the religion known internationally as [Santo Daime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santo_Daime), is located in the Alto Santo neighborhood and has become a place of international pilgrimage.

## Demographics

The population of the municipality in 2024 is estimated at 387,852 inhabitants by the [Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Institute_of_Geography_and_Statistics) (IBGE), almost half of the population of [Acre](229497.md). It is the largest city in the state and one of Brazil's most populous. The population density of 43.89 inhabitants per square kilometer. According to the 2000 census, 51% of the population are male and 48.2% female; 92.73% of the population lives in urban areas and 7.22% live in rural areas. According to the Atlas of Human Development in Brazil, the population of Rio Branco is 0.16% of the national population. According to the Superior Electoral Court, Rio Branco has 271,518 voters in 2024.[^cite_note-30]

The Municipal [Human Development Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Development_Index) of Rio Branco is considered average by the United Nations Development Programme, with a value of 0.754. The education index is 0.860, while Brazil's is 0.849. The rate of longevity is 0.697 (the Brazilian rate is 0.638) and income is 0.704 (Brazil's is 0.723). The [Gini coefficient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gini_coefficient), which measures inequality, is 0.52, where 1.00 is the worst result and 0.00 is the best. The incidence of poverty, as measured by the IBGE, is 37.21 per cent, and the incidence of subjective poverty is 39.39 per cent.

## Education



Rio Branco is the most important educational centre of the state.

In 2009 the city of Rio Branco had 211 elementary schools, with 64,349 students and 2,367 teachers. The Index of Basic Education Development for elementary schools was 4.9, ranked 10th among Brazilian capitals, and above the national average of 4.6. There are an estimated 70 kindergarten preschools with 402 teachers and 10,168 students.

Higher education institutions include the [Federal University of Acre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Acre) (UFAC), the Northern Educational Union, the College of the Western Amazonia, and the Community College of Acre, among others. These absorb the bulk of registrations, especially the Federal University of Acre, as the only public college in the state.

In 2008, the illiteracy rate in the state was 13 per cent, with 36.2 per cent of the population functionally illiterate.

## Sports



[Arena da Floresta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_da_Floresta), Rio Branco, Brazil

Rio Branco provides visitors and residents with various sport activities. The three main [football](10568.md) teams in [Acre](229497.md) are based in Rio Branco: [Rio Branco FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Branco_Football_Club), [Juventus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Clube_Juventus) and [Atlético Acreano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Acreano). Stadiums in the city are the [Arena da Floresta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_da_Floresta); [José de Melo Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Est%C3%A1dio_Jos%C3%A9_de_Melo); [Federação Acreana de Futebol Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Est%C3%A1dio_Ant%C3%B4nio_Aquino_Lopes); Dom Giocondo Maria Grotti Stadium; and Adauto de Brito Stadium. Rio Branco was one of 18 candidates shortlisted to host games of the [2014 FIFA World Cup](656933.md), for which Brazil was the only South American bidder. The city's bid was rejected.
