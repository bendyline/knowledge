The **2025–26 UEFA Women's Champions League** was the 25th edition of the European women's club [football](10568.md) championship organised by [UEFA](32332.md), and the 17th edition since being rebranded as the [UEFA Women's Champions League](1985338.md). It started on 30 July 2025 and ended with the final on 23 May 2026. This was the first season under a new format, which had 18 participating teams, playing six games each against different opponents in a league phase, all in a joint group. The winners automatically qualified for the [2026–27 UEFA Women's Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_UEFA_Women's_Champions_League) league phase, the [2027 FIFA Women's Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_Champions_Cup) semi-finals and the [2028 FIFA Women's Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2028_FIFA_Women's_Club_World_Cup). [Video assistant referee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_assistant_referee) (VAR) was used from the league phase onwards.

The [Ullevaal Stadion](712728.md) in [Oslo](22309.md) hosted the [final](76987356.md). [Barcelona](24763114.md) won the match 4–0 against [OL Lyonnes](8587183.md) for their fourth title.[^cite_note-3]

[Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_W.F.C.) were the defending champions, but were eliminated by OL Lyonnes in the [semi-finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_UEFA_Women's_Champions_League_knockout_phase#Semi-finals).

## Association team allocation

The association ranking based on the [UEFA women's country coefficients](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_coefficient#Women's_association_coefficient) was used to determine the number of participating teams for each association:[^cite_note-format-4]

- Associations 1–7 each had three teams qualify.

- Associations 8–17 each had two teams qualify.

- All other associations, if they entered, each had one team qualify.

- The winners of the [2024–25 UEFA Women's Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UEFA_Women's_Champions_League) were given an additional entry if they did not qualify for the 2025–26 UEFA Women's Champions League through their domestic league.

An association had to have an eleven-a-side women's domestic league to enter a team. As of 2019–20, 52 of the 55 UEFA member associations had organized a women's domestic league, with the exceptions being Andorra (1 club in Spain), Liechtenstein (3 clubs in Switzerland) and San Marino (1 club in Italy).

### Association ranking

For the 2025–26 UEFA Women's Champions League, the associations were allocated places according to their 2024 [UEFA women's Association coefficients](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_coefficient#Women's_association_coefficient), which took into account their performance in European competitions from 2019–20 to 2023–24.[^cite_note-accesslist-5]

Due to the suspension of Russia, the following changes to the access list were made:

- The champions of associations 29 (Poland) entered the second qualifying round (Champions Path) instead of the first qualifying round (Champions Path).

As the Champions League title holders ([Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_W.F.C.)) qualified for the third qualifying round (League Path) via their domestic league's standard berth allocation, the following changes to the default access list were made:

- [BK Häcken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BK_H%C3%A4cken_FF) as the club with the highest club coefficient that would otherwise have entered the second qualifying round (League Path), entered the third qualifying round (League Path) instead.

Since this season marked the inaugural edition of the [UEFA Women's Europa Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Women's_Europa_Cup), with no title holders from previous season, the following changes to the default access list were made:

- [St. Pölten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SKN_St._P%C3%B6lten_\(women\)) as the club with the highest club coefficient that would otherwise have entered the second qualifying round (Champions Path), entered the third qualifying round (Champions Path).

- [Gintra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Gintra) as the club with the highest club coefficient that would otherwise have entered the first qualifying round (Champions Path), entered the second qualifying round (Champions Path).

Location of teams of the **2025–26 UEFA Women's Champions League** league phase

The league phase draw for the 2025–26 UEFA Women's Champions League took place on 19 September 2025. The 18 teams were divided into three pots of six teams. Each team faced two opponents from each of the three pots, one at home and one away.

Sweden's [Damallsvenskan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damallsvenskan), despite being one of the strongest leagues in the world, was without any Champions League teams this season for the first time in history, after [BK Häcken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BK_H%C3%A4cken_FF), [Hammarby IF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammarby_Fotboll_\(women\)) and [FC Rosengård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Roseng%C3%A5rd) were knocked out and sent to the [Europa Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_UEFA_Women's_Europa_Cup).[^cite_note-9]
