The **Afghanistan National Disaster Management Authority** (**ANDMA**; [Dari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dari_language): اداره ملی مبارزه با حوادث افغانستان, [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): د افغانستان د طبیعي پېښو پر وړاندې د مبارزې ملي اداره) also called the **Ministry of Disaster Management**,[^cite_note-2] is a ministry and authority of [Afghanistan](737.md).[^cite_note-3] It was established on 16 February 1973 as the Department of Disaster Preparedness (DDP).[^cite_note-4]

In October 2015, Ghani decided to upgrade the directorate of disaster management to the State Ministry for Disaster Management and appointed Wais Barmak as the minister (he was later replaced).[^cite_note-5] The currently responsible minister is [Mohammad Abbas Akhund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Abbas_Akhund).[^cite_note-pajhwok-6]
