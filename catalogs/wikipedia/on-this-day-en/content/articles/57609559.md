**Banalia** is a [territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_territories_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) and a locality of [Tshopo](33835756.md) province in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md). It is located in the north-central part of the country, 1,300 km northeast of the capital [Kinshasa](45773.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

The locality was one of the milestones during the [Emin Pasha relief expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emin_Pasha_Relief_Expedition).

The region is also rich in iron ore, which was to feed the Maluku steel plant.

In the surroundings around Banalia grows mainly clean green deciduous forest.[^cite_note-nasalandcover-4] Around Banalia, it is very sparsely populated, with 6 inhabitants per square kilometer.[^cite_note-nasapop2-5] Tropical monsoon climate prevails in the area.[^cite_note-koppen-6] Annual average temperature in the funnel is 21 °C. The warmest month is May, when the average temperature is 22 °C, and the coldest is March, at 20 °C.[^cite_note-nasa-7] Average annual rainfall is 1,540 millimeters. The rainy month is October, with an average of 236 mm rainfall, and the driest is January, with 28 mm rainfall.[^cite_note-nasarain-8]
