**Chin State** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): ချင်းပြည်နယ်, [MLCTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLC_Transcription_System): *hkyang: pranynai*, pronounced [\[tɕʰɪ́ɰ̃ pjìnɛ̀\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Burmese)) is a [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Myanmar) in Northwestern [Myanmar](19457.md) (Burma). Chin State is bordered by [Sagaing Region](493361.md) and [Magway Region](493360.md) to the east, [Rakhine State](493373.md) to the south, the [Chattogram Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chittagong_Division) of [Bangladesh](3454.md) to the west, and the [Indian](14533.md) states of [Mizoram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizoram) to the west and [Manipur](422642.md) to the north. The population of Chin State is 488,801 according to the 2014 census, and its capital city is [Hakha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakha).[^cite_note-5] It is divided into 6 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Myanmar) and 9 main [townships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_Myanmar).

The state is named after the [Chin people](1828130.md), a collective term encompassing numerous tribes native to Chin State and the neighboring [Rakhine State](493373.md). It is the only state in Myanmar with a [Christian](18337522.md)-majority population. Located in the [Chin Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_Hills), much of the state is mountainous and sparsely populated, with few transportation links and low levels of [economic development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_development). It also has Myanmar's highest poverty rate, at 58%, according to a 2017 report.[^cite_note-undp-6]

## History

### Early history

Main article: [Chin chieftainship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_chieftainship)

Chin State was traditionally autonomous and far from its neighboring powers, like the Burman kingdoms in the east and Indian states in the west.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECareyTuck201812–33-7] Until the British advancement in the region, independent [city-states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City-state) such as [Ciimnuai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciimnuai) (Chinwe/Chin Nwe) later shifted to [Tedim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tedim) and [Vangteh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vangteh) in the north,[^cite_note-8] [Tlaisun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tashons_people) (also recorded as Tashon) and Rallang in the mid-land, and [Hakha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakha), [Thantlang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thantlang) and Zokhua (Yokwa) in the south played important political roles in securing peace of the region,[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECareyTuck201817–18,_23–24-9] and each city-state practised its own independent sovereignty in its own rights.&#x20;

Traditionally, the people of Chin State practiced indigenous belief systems often described as [animism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animism).**[^cite_note-\:7-10]**[^cite_note-11] [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_missions) and [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism) [missionaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missionary) first arrived in the late 1800s and converted Chin peoples throughout the following century.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEDua20193–62-12][^cite_note-FOOTNOTESon200717–18-13][^cite_note-14]

### 1900–present

Upon [Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma)'s independence from the United Kingdom in 1948, the Chin Hills Special Division was created, with its capital at [Falam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falam,_Burma). Hakha later became the capital. However, three townships that are today part of present-day Chin State ([Mindat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindat_Township), [Kanpetlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanpetlet_Township) and [Matupi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matupi_Township)) were previously part of the Pakokku Hill Tracts of [Pakokku District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakokku_District) and [Paletwa Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paletwa_Township) of the Arakan Hill Tracts, until 4 January 1974. On this date, the Chin Hills Special Division was granted state status and became Chin State.[^cite_note-md-15]

"[Chin National Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_National_Day)" is designated on 20 February to commemorate the "General Assembly of Chinland" held in 1948.[^cite_note-16] The first celebration of Chin National Day was held in 1951, but it was not recognized by the Myanmar government until the 2010s.[^cite_note-17]

From 2007 to 2009, Chin State was affected by [Mautam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mautam), a cyclic ecological phenomenon triggered by a bamboo [masting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mast_seeding) event.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESingletonBelmainBrownHardy201079-18] In [Thantlang Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thantlang_Township), rodents began attacking rice crops in October 2007,[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESingletonBelmainBrownHardy201085-19] while the first outbreak in Paletwa Township was reported in September 2007.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESingletonBelmainBrownHardy201084-20] By the end of that year, 30 villages in southern Paletwa had been affected, while a further 77 villages in northern Paletwa were impacted in 2008.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESingletonBelmainBrownHardy201079-18] Hmawlzauk village in [Falam Township](15141612.md) had 100% of [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize) crops damaged.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESingletonBelmainBrownHardy201089-21] The event increased [malnutrition](258979.md) levels for Chin residents.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESollomRichardsParmarMullany20112-22]

#### Myanmar civil war

Main article: [Chin theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_theater)

Chin State, like much of Myanmar, has been deeply affected by the [Myanmar civil war](69807451.md) since it broke out in 2021. Tens of thousands of Chin State residents have fled to neighboring [Mizoram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizoram), India, and towns such as [Thantlang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thantlang)[^cite_note-aljazeera-23] and Nakzang have been destroyed.[^cite_note-24] Since the war broke out, several armed opposition groups have emerged calling themselves the [Chinland Defense Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinland_Defense_Force). The groups are reportedly funded by the Chin diaspora and by the [National Unity Government of Myanmar](67413816.md), an opposition government-in-exile.[^cite_note-Reuters-25]

On 6 December 2023, the [Chin National Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_National_Front) adopted a Chinland Constitution, proclaiming the state of [Chinland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinland).[^cite_note-26] But resistance groups from 5 [townships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_Myanmar) ([Falam](15141612.md), [Kanpetlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanpetlet_Township), [Matupi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matupi_Township), [Mindat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindat_Township), and [Tedim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tedim_Township)) out of 9 townships in Chin State objected to this constitution.[^cite_note-27]

As of November 2025, ethnic Chin resistance forces, such as the [Chin National Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_National_Army) and the [Chin National Defence Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_National_Defence_Force), maintain [de facto](43594.md) control over approximately 80 percent of Chin State, encompassing most of its townships.[^cite_note-28] Ongoing conflict has displaced 160,000 people—about one third of Chin State's population—either internally or across the [Indian border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India%E2%80%93Myanmar_border).[^cite_note-29]

## Government

### Executive

Main article: [Chin State Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_State_Government)

The **Chin State Government** is the [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) government of Chin State, led by Dr Vung Suan Thang. It became defunct following the [2021 Myanmar coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Myanmar_coup) but was re-established on 10 April 2026. Before the Chin State Government resumed its duties, Chin State was administrated by the [State Administration Council](66589972.md) (SAC).

### Legislature

Main article: [Chin State Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_State_Hluttaw)

The **Chin State Hluttaw** is the [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) of Chin State, responsible for local governance, passing state laws, approving localized budgets, and overseeing the state cabinet. It has 24 seats and is [unicameral](204304.md).

## Economy

Chin State has little infrastructure and remains undeveloped,[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEYamahataTakeda202378-33] with over 70% of its population living below the poverty line.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBonnerjee2017375-34]

Agriculture is a key source of income and part of the daily livelihood for many residents of Chin State. Most people rely on rotational, [slash-and-burn farming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slash-and-burn_farming),[^cite_note-35] growing just enough to feed themselves and their families.[^cite_note-36] [Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice), [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), and [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet) are the primary crops grown in Chin State.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESingletonBelmainBrownHardy201083-37][^cite_note-38] Chin households are moving towards a cash-oriented economy based on vegetables and [perennial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perennial) crops (i.e., coffee, avocado, grapes, [elephant foot yam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephant_foot_yam), [tree bean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkia_timoriana), and [sericulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sericulture)).[^cite_note-39] Some earn a living as traders, selling cattle and other goods across the border in Mizoram, India.[^cite_note-40]

Chin State is Myanmar's largest producer of [konjac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konjac) (elephant foot yam) with an estimated annual output exceeding 250,000 tonnes.[^cite_note-41]

## Healthcare

Healthcare and medical resources in Chin State are limited.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBonnerjee2017379-42] According to [UNICEF](19698110.md), one in 10 children will not reach the age of five.[^cite_note-43] In 2015, 41% of children in the state were affected by [child stunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stunted_growth), the highest prevalence recorded in Myanmar.[^cite_note-44] Human rights abuses are correlated with poorer health outcomes among Chin residents.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESollomRichardsParmarMullany20112,_11-45]

The [Chin peoples](1828130.md) make up the majority of Chin State's population, with small [Rakhine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rakhine_people) and [Bamar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamar_people) minorities. The people of Chin State are made up of many tribes, which, though historically related, now speak divergent languages and have different cultural identities. Some consider the name Chin an [exonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exonym), given by the Bamar people.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEHock2021369-47] Chin tribes in the state include the [Zo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zou_people), [Zomi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zomi_people), [Zotung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zotung_people), [Laimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai_people), [K'Cho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%CA%BCch%C3%B2_language), [Khumi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khumi_people), [Daai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daai_Chin), and [Asho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asho_Chin_people).

After the [2014 Myanmar census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Myanmar_census), the Burmese government indefinitely withheld the release of detailed ethnicity data, citing concerns about political and social concerns surrounding the issue of ethnicity in Myanmar.[^cite_note-\:73-48] In 2022, researchers published an analysis of the [General Administration Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Administration_Department)'s nationwide 2018-2019 township reports to tabulate the ethnic makeup of Chin State.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-\:73-48]

As of February 2024, [United Nations](31769.md) estimates 60,000 Chin people have fled to the Indian states of [Mizoram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizoram) and [Manipur](422642.md), while another 61,000 remain internally displaced following the [2021 Myanmar coup d'état](66567997.md). Chin humanitarian organizations suggest that the real figures are higher.[^cite_note-50]

## Education

See also: [List of universities in Chin State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_universities_in_Chin_State)



Students in [Hakha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakha)

Education in Chin State faces significant challenges. Schools often lack a sufficient amount of [textbooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook), [whiteboards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whiteboard), [markers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marker_pen), [pencils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pencil), tables, and chairs.[^cite_note-56] Since the 1990s, teaching [Chin languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_languages) as a separate subject has been banned in primary schools.[^cite_note-57] According to Health\&Hope, only \~5.5% of youths in Chin State will make it through school to pass their grade 10 exams.[^cite_note-58] In 2003, official statistics indicated that Chin State had 25 high schools;[^cite_note-cso-edu-59] most rural villages have no high school of their own.[^cite_note-60]
