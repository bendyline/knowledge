**Narathiwat** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): นราธิวาส, pronounced [\[nā.rāː.tʰí.wâːt\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Thai); [Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language): Menara[^cite_note-5]) is one of the southern [provinces](204637.md) (*changwat*) of [Thailand](30128.md). Neighbouring provinces are (from west clockwise) [Yala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yala_province) and [Pattani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pattani_province). To the south it [borders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysia-Thailand_border) the [Malaysian](3607937.md) states of [Kelantan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelantan) and [Perak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perak). The southern railway line ends in this province, which is one of the nation's four provinces that border Malaysia.[^cite_note-Tourism-6] The province features a range of cultures as well as natural resources, and is relatively fertile. Narathiwat is about 1,140 kilometers south of [Bangkok](56615.md) and has an area of 4,475 km<sup>2</sup> (1,728 sq mi). Seventy-five percent of the area is jungle and mountains and has a tropical climate.

## Toponymy

The former name of Narathiwat was **Menara** ([Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_script): منارا), meaning 'minaret' or 'tower' in [Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language), the pre-Islamic name is unknown. This became **Bang Nara** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): บางนรา) or **Bang Nak** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): บางนาค) in Thai,[^cite_note-7] but was changed to Narathiwat by King [Rama VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rama_VI) in 1915.[^cite_note-8] "Narathiwat", from the [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit) (Nara+adhivāsa), means *the residence of wise people*.

## History

Inscribed bricks have been recorded at Khok It, and a Buddha image of Amaravati type at Su-ngai Kolok.[^cite_note-sanghiran2019-9]<sup>: 73 </sup>

Historically, Narathiwat was the part of the semi-independent [Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_people) [Sultanate of Patani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patani_kingdom), paying tribute to the Thai kingdoms of [Sukhothai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhothai_kingdom) and Siamese [Ayutthaya Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayutthaya_Kingdom). After the fall of Ayutthaya in 1767, the Sultanate of Patani gained full independence but returned under Thai control during the reign of King [Rama I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rama_I) (r. 1782–1809), 18 years later, and in the early–1800s was divided into seven smaller kingdoms.

In 1909, Narathiwat was fully integrated into Siam as part of [Anglo-Siamese Treaty of 1909](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Siamese_Treaty_of_1909) negotiated with the [British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire). Along with Yala, Narathiwat was then part of *Monthon Pattani*.

## Geography

Narathiwat province is on the [Gulf of Thailand](198107.md), on the [Malay Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_Peninsula). The Bang Nara is the main river and enters the [Gulf of Thailand](198107.md) at the town of Narathiwat. Narathat Beach, the most popular in the province, is near the estuary. The total forest area is 1,196 km<sup>2</sup> (462 sq mi) or 26.6 percent of provincial area.[^cite_note-AREA-1]

## Demographics

Narathiwat is one of four Thai provinces (along with [Yala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yala_province), [Pattani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pattani_province), and [Satun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satun_province)) with a predominantly Muslim population; 82% are Muslim and 17.9% are Buddhist. Also 80.4% speak the [Patani Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yawi_language) as their first language.[^cite_note-Tourism-6] Narathiwat Malays are very similar in ethnicity and culture to the Malays of [Kelantan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelantan), [Malaysia](3607937.md).

In 1963, the Thai government launched the *Nikhom Sang Ton Eng Pak Tai* ('self-development community in the south') program to move families from Thailand's northeastern and central provinces to the Sukhirin and Chanae Districts of Narathiwat. A total of 5,633 families were relocated to Narathiwat, where each family was rewarded with 18 [rai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rai_\(unit\)) of land. In Phukhao Thong Subdistrict as of 2019, most [inhabitants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isan_people) migrated from the [Northeast region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isan). They speak [Isan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isan_language) and are 90% Theravadin Buddhists in what is a predominantly Muslim province.[^cite_note-BP-20190905-10]

The inhabitants of Narathiwat are largely farmers and fishermen.[^cite_note-Tasanasuwan-11] Narathiwat is an area with various religious places of historical significance.

## Symbols

The [provincial seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seals_of_the_provinces_of_Thailand) shows a sailing boat with a [white elephant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_elephant) on the sail. A white elephant is a royal symbol, and was put on the seal to commemorate the white elephant *Phra Sri Nararat Rajakarini* which was caught here and presented to the king.

The provincial symbol is the longkong fruit (*[Lansium parasiticum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lansium_parasiticum)*). The [provincial tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Thai_provincial_trees) is the Chengal (*[Neobalanocarpus heimii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neobalanocarpus)*), and the provincial flower is the *[Odontadenia macrantha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odontadenia_macrantha)*. The ornamental fish [harlequin rasbora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harlequin_rasbora) (*[Trigonostigma heteromorpha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigonostigma_heteromorpha)*), are fish that live in swampy water conditions. In Thailand, they are found only in Narathiwat's [Pa Phru To Daeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pa_Phru_To_Daeng), and on the ground, this species of [cyprinid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprinid) is the provincial aquatic life.

### Local government



Princess of Naradhiwas University

As of 26 November 2019 there are:[^cite_note-12] one Narathiwat Provincial Administration Organisation (*_ongkan borihan suan changwat_*) and 16 municipal (*thesaban*) areas in the province. Narathiwat, Sungai Kolok and Tak Bai have town (*[thesaban mueang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesaban_mueang)*) status. Further 13 subdistrict municipalities (*[thesaban tambon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesaban_tambon)*). The non-municipal areas are administered by 72 Subdistrict Administrative Organisations – SAO (*ongkan borihan suan tambon*).

## Education

[Princess of Naradhiwas University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_of_Naradhiwas_University) is a university in Narathiwat province which aims to distribute the equality, equity, and opportunity to local citizen by merging all educational institutions in the province. Those institutions are Narathiwat Technical College, Narathiwat Agricultural & Technology College, Takbai Vocational College, and Boromarjonani College of Nursing, Narathiwat.

## Transportation

### Air

As of January 2026 [Narathiwat Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narathiwat_Airport) (Code: NAW) has two direct flights from and to Bangkok's Don Mueang Airport (DMK) daily, as well as one direct flight from and to Bangkok's Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK). All three flights arrive at and depart from NAW between the late morning to early afternoon hours. These are all operated by [Air Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Asia), the only airline currently offering flights to and from Narathiwat.

### Rail

Although there is no direct access to Mueang Narathiwat District, Narathiwat's main railway station and nearest to Mueang District is [Tanyong Mat Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanyong_Mat_Railway_Station), on the Southern Line, in [Ra-ngae District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra-ngae_District). Other major stations along the line in Narathiwat include [Rueso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rueso_Railway_Station), [Su-ngai Kolok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Su-ngai_Kolok_Railway_Station), [Su-ngai Padi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Su-ngai_Padi_Railway_Station) and [Cho-airong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cho-airong_Railway_Station). Distance to Narathiwat by rail is roughly 1100 kilometres from [Bangkok Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok_Railway_Station).

### Road

[Highway 42](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highway_42_\(Thailand\)) is considered as main highway in Narathiwat and its terminus, Su-ngai Kolok.

## Health

Narathiwat is mostly served by public hospitals. Its main hospital is [Naradhiwas Rajanagarindra Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naradhiwas_Rajanagarindra_Hospital) and the province has one university hospital which is [Galyanivadhanakarun Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galyanivadhanakarun_Hospital) of the [Faculty of Medicine, Princess of Naradhiwas University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faculty_of_Medicine,_Princess_of_Naradhiwas_University).

## Recent history

Main article: [South Thailand insurgency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Thailand_insurgency)

There has been growing violence in southern Thailand since 4 January 2004, especially in the majority Muslim provinces of Narathiwat, [Yala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yala_province), and [Pattani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pattani_province). Most of the inhabitants of these provinces are ethnic [Malays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_people), though the cities are mainly Thai, Thai Chinese, and Indian. Violent [mujahideen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mujahideen) activity has happened since the 1980s, but this lessened when [Thaksin Shinawatra](360815.md) became prime minister in 2001.

Most of the violence has been directed towards the minority Buddhist population in the province.

## Religious sites



300 Years Mosque

### Central Mosque of Narathiwat

The important religious site for Muslims is the central mosque of Narathiwat. This [mosque](19894.md) is on Pitchitbamrung Road, [Tambon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambon) Bangnark, [Amphoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphoe) Muang, about 1 kilometer from the provincial town. This mosque is the center for Thai Muslims who come to worship on Fridays. It was built in 1981 in a three-storied Arabian-style building with a high tower and a domed roof as other mosques. The tower is used to call Muslims to prayer.[^cite_note-Government-13]

### Khao Kong Buddhist Park

A minority of the people in Narathiwat are Buddhist. Although a minority, there are Buddhist temples in the same amphoe as the mosque. These include Khao Kong Buddhist Park, which occupies an area of 142 rai (227,200 square metres (2,446,000 sq ft)) in Tambon Lamphu about nine kilometers from town on the Narathiwat-[Rangae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rangae) Road.[^cite_note-Tourism-6]

#### Phra Buddha Thaksin Ming Mongkol

The main attraction in this site is a southern [Buddha image](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddha_image), the golden "Pra Buddha Thaksin Ming Monkol",[^cite_note-Government-13] which is seated in the lotus position and giving posture. "This mountaintop Buddha image which is considered to be the most beautiful and largest (17 meters wide and 24 meters high) outdoor Buddha image in southern Thailand is decorated in the South Indian style".[^cite_note-Tourism-6] It is highly respected by locals and Buddhists in the south.[^cite_note-Tasanasuwan-11]
