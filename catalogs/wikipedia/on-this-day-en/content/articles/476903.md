The **[MTV Video Music Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Award) for Video of the Year** is the most prestigious competitive award and the final award presented at the annual [MTV Video Music Awards](295217.md).[^cite_note-1] The award was created by the U.S. network [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV) to honor artists with the best [music videos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_videos).[^cite_note-2] At the first MTV Video Music Awards ceremony in [1984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), the Video of the Year honor was presented to [The Cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cars) for the video "[You Might Think](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Might_Think)".[^cite_note-VMA1984-3] Originally, all winners were determined by a special panel of music video directors, producers, and record company executives.[^cite_note-4] Since the [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) awards, winners of major categories are determined by viewers' votes through MTV's website, while the jury decides in the technical categories.[^cite_note-5] From [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) to [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), [Burger King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burger_King) sponsors the category through a partnership with [Paramount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Global), resulting in the award being presented as **MTV Video Music Award for Video of the Year Presented by Burger King**.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

[Taylor Swift](5422144.md) holds the record for the most wins, with a total of six for "[Bad Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Blood_\(Taylor_Swift_song\))" ([2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), "[You Need to Calm Down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Need_to_Calm_Down)" ([2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), *[All Too Well: The Short Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Too_Well:_The_Short_Film)* ([2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), "[Anti-Hero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Hero_\(song\))" ([2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), "[Fortnight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortnight_\(song\))" ([2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), and "[The Fate of Ophelia](81126617.md)" ([2026](82993690.md)). [Eminem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eminem) and Swift holds the record for the most nominations, with Eminem having eight as lead artist.[^cite_note-9] [David Lee Roth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Lee_Roth) ([1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), U2 ([1988](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga) ([2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)) and [Bruno Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Mars) ([2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)) are the only acts to have had two Video of the Year nominations in a single ceremony.[^cite_note-Multiple_winners-10] Two acts have won both the Video of the Year and the honorary [Michael Jackson Video Vanguard Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jackson_Video_Vanguard_Award) in the same night—[Peter Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Gabriel) in [1987](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) with "[Sledgehammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sledgehammer_\(Peter_Gabriel_song\))" and [Justin Timberlake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Timberlake) in [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) with "[Mirrors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirrors_\(Justin_Timberlake_song\))".[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] Swift is the first artist to win Video of the Year for a self-directed video, with *All Too Well: The Short Film*; she also ranks as the artist with the most Video of the Year trophies for self-directed videos, with a total of four. [Kendrick Lamar](29909823.md), Swift, [Lil Nas X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Nas_X), and [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande) have further won the award for a video they co-directed: Lamar for "[Humble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humble_\(song\))" in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), Swift for "You Need to Calm Down" in 2019, Lil Nas X for "[Montero (Call Me By Your Name)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montero_\(Call_Me_By_Your_Name\))" in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), and Grande for *[Brighter Days Ahead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighter_Days_Ahead)* in [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_MTV_Video_Music_Awards).[^cite_note-14]

## Recipients

A woman performing on a microphone, wearing a black shirt and pants

[Taylor Swift](5422144.md) is the category's most awarded artist, winning six times: "[Bad Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Blood_\(Taylor_Swift_song\))" ([2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), "[You Need to Calm Down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Need_to_Calm_Down)" ([2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), *[All Too Well: The Short Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Too_Well:_The_Short_Film)* ([2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), "[Anti-Hero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Hero_\(song\))" ([2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), "[Fortnight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortnight_\(song\))" ([2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_MTV_Video_Music_Awards)), and "[The Fate of Ophelia](81126617.md)" ([2026](82993690.md)).

Side profile of a man wearing black hat, black coat and black shirt

The first artist to win Video of the Year twice is [Eminem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eminem), in [2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) for "[The Real Slim Shady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Real_Slim_Shady)" and in [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) for "[Without Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Without_Me_\(Eminem_song\))".

A woman performing on a microphone

[Rihanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rihanna) became the first woman to win the award twice as a lead artist, in [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) for "[Umbrella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umbrella_\(song\))", and [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) for "[We Found Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Found_Love_\(music_video\))".

A woman holding a microphone, wearing a bodysuit

Two-time winner [Beyoncé](83688.md), did so with "[Single Ladies (Put a Ring on It)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single_Ladies_\(Put_a_Ring_on_It\))" in [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) and "[Formation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formation_\(song\))" in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_MTV_Video_Music_Awards).
