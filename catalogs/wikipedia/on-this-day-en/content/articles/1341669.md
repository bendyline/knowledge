**Elbit Systems Ltd.** is an Israel-based international [military technology](90815.md) company and [defense contractor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_contractor). Founded in 1966 by [Elron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elron_Electronic_Industries), Elbit Systems is the primary provider of the [Israeli military](39237.md)'s land-based equipment and [unmanned aerial vehicles](58900.md). It is an important company within the [defense industry of Israel](39305449.md).

Elbit Systems also has subsidiary factories around the world, and sells its products to various countries' militaries. At its subsidiary factories, especially in the United Kingdom, the United States and Germany, the company has faced protests for its support of the Israeli military in the ongoing [Israeli–Palestinian conflict](46216.md). The company has also been [divested](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divested) from by international investment firms due to its involvement in the conflict.&#x20;

In a 2025 [investor relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investor_relations) presentation, Elbit Systems reported having 20,000 employees, with 3,200 of them based in the United States.[^cite_note-2] Elbit Systems' shares are traded on the [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_Stock_Exchange) and [Nasdaq](21559.md) stock exchanges.

## History

Elbit Systems was founded in 1966 by [Elron Electronic Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elron_Electronic_Industries) and the Israel's [Ministry of Defense](3226446.md), under the name 'Elbit Computers Ltd.',[^cite_note-3] to develop computers and electronics for the [Israeli military](39237.md), its first product was a [minicomputer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minicomputer) named '[Elbit 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_100)'.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The minicomputer was successfully sold for civilian use,[^cite_note-7] and in July 1970 the American company [Control Data Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_Data_Corporation) entered into an agreement to acquire the Israeli government's interest in Elbit Computers for US\$1 million.[^cite_note-8] Control Data Corporation marketed and distributed Elbit Computers' around the world.[^cite_note-9]

In 1978 Elbit had an [initial public offering](63879.md) on the [Tel Aviv Stock Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_Stock_Exchange) and continued to develop minicomputers, including a model called ANAT, that was developed and sold in corporation with German company [Nixdorf Computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nixdorf_Computer).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] During the early 1980s the company refocused on defense electronics and developed systems and sub-components for the [Lavi fighter jet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Lavi) and for the [Merkava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merkava) tank.[^cite_note-12] The company also changed its name from 'Elbit Computers Ltd.' to 'Elbit Ltd.' and Control Data Corporation sold its stake in the company.

In 1996, Elbit was spun off into three independent companies: [Elbit Medical Imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Medical_Imaging), Elbit Systems, and Elbit.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] From 1999 to 2000, [Elscint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elscint) (another affiliate of Elron) and Elbit Medical Imaging sold their imaging activities to General Electric Medical Systems and Picker. Elbit Systems was created as the defense electronics arm of the company. Elbit, which focused on communications activities, led the consortium that founded [Partner Communications Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partner_Communications_Company) in 1999.

In 2000, Elbit Systems merged with El-Op (then controlled by current Elbit chairman, [Michael Federmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Federmann)),[^cite_note-15] creating the largest non-governmental defense electronics company in Israel. Elron sold its shares in Elbit Systems for approximately \$197 million in 2004.[^cite_note-16] Following the merger with El-Op, Federmann became the largest shareholder of the combined group. Following its merger, Elbit acquired [Elisra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elisra), assets of [Israel Military Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Military_Industries)' Aircraft Systems Division, and Mikal Ltd.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] Elbit subsequently announced a cash tender offer for the 12.15% remainder of ITL Optronics Ltd held by the public.[^cite_note-19]

In 2002, Elbit was merged into Elron.[^cite_note-20]

In 2006, Elbit acquired 20% of [Chip PC Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chip_PC_Technologies), a developer and manufacturer of client solutions for server-based computing.[^cite_note-21] In 2016, Elbit launched [Everysight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everysight), a developer and manufacturer of an [augmented reality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmented_reality) [smartglasses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smartglasses) for cyclists, founded in 2016.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

In 2016, *[The Intercept](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Intercept)* reported that, starting in 2008, the US [National Security Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Agency) and UK [GCHQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GCHQ) had been [tapping into](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiretapping) live feeds from Elbit drones (among other Israeli air force equipment) as they were flown on missions by the Israeli military and air force against targets in Gaza, the West Bank, Syria, and Lebanon.[^cite_note-intercept_2016-24]

In November 2018, Elbit completed its acquisition of [IMI Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMI_Systems).[^cite_note-25]

## Global operations

### Israel

Elbit Systems is the largest Israeli military manufacturer and produces 85% of the [Israel Defense Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Force)'s land-based equipment as well as 85% of the drones used by the [Israeli Air Force](177619.md).[^cite_note-27] Elbit advertises that its equipment has been battle-tested by the IDF in operations in Gaza and the West Bank.[^cite_note-28] Elbit's drones have been involved in multiple incidents involving civilian deaths.[^cite_note-the_intercept-29] While Israel does not officially acknowledge that its drones are used to carry out strikes in Gaza or elsewhere, a 2016 report published by *The Intercept* suggested that Israel has used attack drones in fatal attacks.[^cite_note-intercept_2016-24]



Matam hi-tech park (Haifa)

In August 2019, Elbit Systems won an [Israel Ministry of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Ministry_of_Defense) contract to install the [Iron Fist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Fist_\(countermeasure\)) [active protection system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_protection_system) on the IDF's new [Eitan AFV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eitan_AFV) and the IDF's fleet of [armored D9 bulldozers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDF_Caterpillar_D9).[^cite_note-30]

An Elbit Hermes 450 drone was used in the 2024 [World Central Kitchen aid convoy attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Central_Kitchen_aid_convoy_attack) which killed seven aid workers in multiple missile strikes against three marked aid vehicles.[^cite_note-haaretz1-31][^cite_note-32]

In May 2026, [Israel’s Ministry of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Ministry_of_Defense) signed a \$34 million contract with Elbit Systems’ Cyclone subsidiary to develop external fuel tanks for the [F-35I “Adir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F-35I_Adir),” manufactured by [Lockheed Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin). The goal is to extend the Jet’s operational range and reduce reliance on aerial refuelling during long-range missions.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35]

### Canada

Elbit Systems has had a presence in Canada for over 10 years. In January 2012, Advionix Systems Ltd, a company that had been founded in 2001, renamed itself as Elbit Systems of Canada Limited.[^cite_note-50] In October 2014, it was announced that Elbit had been selected by Rheinmetall Canada Inc to provide ELSAT 2100 satellite-on-the-move (SOTM) systems for the Canadian armed forces.[^cite_note-51]

In December 2016, Elbit acquired Geospectrum Technologies Inc., a small technology company based in [Dartmouth, Nova Scotia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth,_Nova_Scotia).[^cite_note-52] In March 2018, it was announced that GeoSpectrum had delivered the first version of its Towed Reelable Active Passive Sonar (TRAPS) to the Royal Canadian Navy.[^cite_note-53]

In May 2025, the [Investigative Journalism Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investigative_Journalism_Foundation) reported that Elbit Systems had offered to sell [Ontario](22218.md) drones to combat [forest fires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_fires).[^cite_note-54]

### United Kingdom

Elbit activities in the United Kingdom started in 1995, when it acquired 'Alvis UAV Engines Limited' from [Alvis plc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alvis_plc).[^cite_note-FG-55] A company that was founded in 1992 by engineer David Garside, to develop [Wankel engines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wankel_engine) for use in UAVs.[^cite_note-UST-56] Garside has been developing Wankel engines since the late 1960s for [BSA motorcycles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSA_motorcycles) and later [Norton Motorcycle Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norton_Motorcycle_Company),[^cite_note-57] and had the idea of using the engines originally developed for motorcycles, for UAV's that required small but powerful source of power.[^cite_note-UST-56] Elbit, through its subsidiary 'Silver Arrow', was the company's only major customer when it was acquired.[^cite_note-FG-55][^cite_note-ben-58] Since then the company has become a major global supplier of UAV Engines.[^cite_note-ben-58]

Its subsidiary Elbit Systems UK Ltd. was incorporated on September 24, 2004, as a holding company for Elbit's activities in the United Kingdom.[^cite_note-59] The company was incorporated after Elbit and French company [Thales Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thales_Group) won a tender (code-named "Watchkeeper program") to supply the [British Army](4887.md) with unmanned aerial vehicles.[^cite_note-60] The tender condition included a requirement to set up a local manufacturing facility for the program.[^cite_note-61] Therefore, at the end of 2005, a joint venture company was established with the Thales Group. The joint venture, named UAV Tactical Systems Ltd (U-TacS), was based in [Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester) and manufactured the [Watchkeeper WK450](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watchkeeper_WK450).[^cite_note-62] A flight testing facility was also set up at [ParcAberporth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ParcAberporth) in Wales.[^cite_note-63]

In 2007, Elbit acquired Ferranti Technologies, based in [Oldham, Greater Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oldham), a company that was part of the historic [Ferranti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferranti) group and provided solutions in the aerospace and defence markets.[^cite_note-avtoday-ferranti-64]

In 2010, via the acquisition of Azimuth Technologies in Israel, Elbit also came to own 100% of its UK subsidiary, Instro Precision Limited, which is engaged primarily in target acquisition and fire control systems.[^cite_note-65]

In February 2016, Affinity Flying Services Limited, a joint venture between Elbit and [Kellog, Brown and Root](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KBR_\(company\)), was awarded a £500 million contract to manage the UK Military Flight Training System (UKMFTS) program for the [Ministry of Defence](203793.md) to deliver aircrew training for over an 18-year period.[^cite_note-66]

In 2021, Instro Precision was awarded a £11.5 million contract from the UK Ministry of Defence to supply XACT Night Vision Goggles to the British Armed Forces.[^cite_note-67] Extensions to the contract were awarded in 2022 and 2025.[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69]

Elbit sold its Ferranti subsidiary in 2022 for £9 million, approximately £6 million less than it had purchased it for fifteen years prior. Activists attributed the sale to their years-long [direct action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_action) campaign against Elbit's presence in Oldham.[^cite_note-novara-70]

Elbit opened an advanced manufacturing and development facility in Bristol in July 2023.[^cite_note-71] As of 2023, Elbit has multiple UK subsidiaries across sixteen sites in the country, with more than 680 employees.[^cite_note-72]

In 2024, Elbit announced the sale of the Elite KL factory in [Tamworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamworth,_Staffordshire).[^cite_note-73]

In late August 2025, Elbit Systems was described by *[Private Eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_Eye)* as "close" to a gaining a 15 year, £2 billion Ministry of Defence contract training 60,000 British soldiers annually.[^cite_note-74][^cite_note-75][^cite_note-76]

In January 2026, Elbit announced that it was buying Thales' 49% share in their drone manufacturing joint venture, UAV Tactical Systems. Industry commentators noted that Elbit had indicated that it planned to produce UAVs for customers across Europe from the Leicester-based factory. [^cite_note-77]

As a result, Elbit Systems currently operates five businesses in the UK, four of which are wholly-owned subsidiaries and one of which is a joint venture:

- Elbit Systems UK Ltd

- UAV Engines Ltd

- UAV Tactical Systems Ltd

- Instro Precision Ltd

- Affinity Flying Services Limited (50%)

### Germany

According to company information, Elbit's German operations originate in the radio communications business of [Telefunken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telefunken) in [Ulm](189455.md). Following a series of corporate reorganisations involving AEG, DASA and [EADS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EADS), the radio communications business was spun off in 2000 as EADS Radio Communication System GmbH & Co. KG (EADS RACOMS). [^cite_note-78] In 2003, Israeli communications manufacturer [Tadiran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadiran) acquired a controlling interest in EADS RACOMS, which was subsequently renamed Telefunken Radio Communication Systems GmbH & Co. KG (Telefunken RACOMS).[^cite_note-79]

Telefunken RACOMS became part of Elbit Systems in 2007, when Elbit completed its acquisition of Tadiran Communications.[^cite_note-80] Elbit subsequently described the Ulm-based company as its wholly-owned German subsidiary, active in military and civilian communications projects in Germany and internationally.[^cite_note-81] Elbit further expanded its German presence by opening an office in [Berlin](3354.md) in 2018.[^cite_note-82]

In 2020, Elbit announced that Telefunken RACOMS would be rebranded as Elbit Systems Deutschland.[^cite_note-83] In March 2021, the company opened an additional office in [Koblenz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koblenz), joining its headquarters and development and manufacturing facility in Ulm and its Berlin office.[^cite_note-84]

For the [German military](288188.md), Elbit produces the [E-LynX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-LynX) [SDR radio system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software-defined_radio), night vision and thermal imaging devices, and [Directional Infrared Countermeasure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directional_Infrared_Counter_Measures) (DIRCM) for the [A400M](371265.md), radar warning receivers, and EW controllers for the [CH-53GS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_CH-53_Sea_Stallion)/GE.[^cite_note-85]

In May 2026, Elbit Systems signed a memorandum of understanding with thyssenkrupp Marine Systems (TKMS) to expand cooperation on naval defense technologies and maritime systems. The agreement built on existing cooperation between the companies, including the manufacturing of submarine components in Israel.[^cite_note-86][^cite_note-87]

Germany has also purchased several Israeli defense systems, including the Arrow-3 missile defense system and Heron TP drones.[^cite_note-88][^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90]

### Romania

Romanian subsidiary was awarded, in cooperation with other Romanian companies and led by the Romanian Aeronautical Industry, a four-year contract valued at approximately \$18.6 million to upgrade the [Romanian Air Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Air_Forces)' [C-130](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-130) transport aircraft.[^cite_note-herculesUpgradeRomania2011-91]

### Sweden

On June 17, 2021, Elbit Systems announced the establishment of its Swedish subsidiary Elbit Systems Sweden.[^cite_note-92] On January 13, 2022, Elbit Systems Sweden was awarded with a contract to supply combat management systems to the [Royal Swedish Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Navy).[^cite_note-93][^cite_note-94] On January 24, 2023, Elbit Systems Sweden announced that it was awarded a contract valued at approximately \$48 million to equip the [Swedish Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Armed_Forces) with new mobile tactical communications stations.[^cite_note-95][^cite_note-96][^cite_note-auto1-97][^cite_note-auto-98] According to the contract, Elbit Systems Sweden is to develop and deliver two Technical High Mobility Shelter (THMS) variants, the RL HYTT 24A and RL HYTT 24B.[^cite_note-auto-98][^cite_note-auto1-97]

### Belgium

On July 1, 2003, Elbit acquired the Belgian company [OIP Sensor Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OIP_Sensor_Systems), based on [Oudenaarde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oudenaarde).[^cite_note-OIPElbit-99]

In 2020, it was announced that OIP, alongside Elbit subsidiary Telefunken RACOMS based in [Ulm](189455.md), Germany, had been jointly awarded a contract from the French [Direction générale de l'armement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale_de_l'armement) (DGA) to deliver approximately 2620 FN SCAR-H PR rifles plus TIGRIS clip-on sights.[^cite_note-100]

### Australia

In April 2021, the [Australian Army](2795.md) announced that Elbit's battlefield management system would be withdrawn from use with short notice, effective May 15, 2021. The [Australian Broadcasting Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Broadcasting_Corporation) reported that this was due to concerns regarding the Elbit system's security.[^cite_note-ABC_News_7_May_21-101] An *[Australian Financial Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Financial_Review)* columnist stated that the security problems comprised "a '[backdoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backdoor_\(computing\))' security vulnerability reportedly uncovered by the [Australian Signals Directorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Signals_Directorate)".[^cite_note-102] The *Australian Defence Magazine* reported that the system had failed two security milestones in 2020.[^cite_note-103] Elbit denied that the system posed any security risks.[^cite_note-ABC_News_7_May_21-101]

Despite previous concerns, the Australian Army made a US\$600 million contract with Elbit Systems in 2024.[^cite_note-104]

### Brazil

In 2001, Elbit Systems Group completed its purchase of Aeroeletronica, a Brazilian company in the defense industry.[^cite_note-105] In January 2011, Elbit announced that it had bought two additional Brazilian companies: Ares Aeroespecial e Defesa and Periscopio Equipamentos Optronicos.[^cite_note-106] In April 2011, the Brazilian aircraft manufacturer [Embraer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embraer) entered into a strategic agreement with [AEL Sistemas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEL_Sistemas) S.A, a subsidiary of Elbit Systems. ### Other international activities

#### France

On June 19, 2011, Elbit announced that its subsidiary Elisra Electronic Systems Ltd. was awarded a contract valued at approximately €5 million to supply hundreds of units of its AN/PRC-684 [Personal Locator Beacon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_Locator_Beacon) to the [French Ministry of Defense](37707412.md), equipping the [French Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Air_Force), [Army](611740.md), [Navy](383256.md) and DGA ([Direction Générale de l'Armement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_G%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale_de_l'Armement)).[^cite_note-francePLB2011-108]

#### Italy

On June 22, 2011, Elbit announced that it was awarded a three-year, US\$15 million contract by Elettronica S.p.A. to participate in a program to supply the ELT/572 DIRCM system for installation on various platforms of the [Italian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Air_Force), including the [C130J](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C130J), [C27J](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C27J) and [AW101](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AW101).[^cite_note-italyAFparis2011-111]

Israel supplies Azerbaijan advanced military equipment and helps train [its army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_Armed_Forces). As a part of the cooperation between the two states, Elbit Systems opened an office in Azerbaijan in 2011, with plans to build a plant for the joint production of unmanned aerial vehicles.[^cite_note-112] In 2023, it was reported that Elbit (along with other Israeli defense manufacturers) had played an extensive role in discreetly arming the Azerbaijani army for the [Nagorno-Karabakh conflict](4695860.md)[^cite_note-ctech-113] that culminated in accusations of an Armenian genocide.[^cite_note-114]

#### Japan

Elbit won its first contract in the Philippines for the supply of upgraded armored personnel carriers to the Philippine military in June 2014. The upgrade included 25-mm unmanned turrets, 12.7-mm remote controlled weapon stations (, and fire control systems for 90-mm turrets.[^cite_note-116][^cite_note-117] In 2015, the [Armed Forces of the Philippines](23448.md) received 114 M113 tracked APCs from the United States,[^cite_note-118] with remote control weapons systems designed by Elbit.[^cite_note-119]

On October 23, 2020, Elbit won contracts to produce 18 Sabrah [ASCOD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASCOD) Light Tanks and 10 [Pandur II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandur_II) Tank Destroyers along with 1 ASCOD 2 Armored Command Vehicle, 1 ASCOD, and 2 Armored Recovery Vehicles, as well as another contract to supply 28 [Iveco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iveco) [VBTP-MR Guarani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VBTP-MR_Guarani) APCs for the [Philippine Army](6032559.md)'s Light Tank and APC Acquisition Projects.[^cite_note-120]

## Controversies

### Colombia bribery allegations

In March 2010, a \$28-million contract for Hermes 450 UAVs was suspended by the Colombian government after media reports based on anonymous letters alleged that Colombian officials had been bribed.[^cite_note-ynetcol-121][^cite_note-tiempo-122] The Israeli Ministry of Defense investigated and found that the letters likely came from [Israel Aerospace Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Aerospace_Industries), a competitor that lost the tender.[^cite_note-ynetcol-121] The incident led to a review of the regulations governing overseas competition between Israeli defense companies.[^cite_note-ynetcol-121]

### Alleged surveillance of Ethiopian dissidents

On December 6, 2017, the [Citizen Lab](514259.md) reported that Ethiopian dissidents and journalists in the US, UK, and other countries were targeted by sophisticated commercial spyware, sold and operated by Cyberbit, a wholly owned subsidiary of Elbit Systems.[^cite_note-123][^cite_note-124] Ronald Deibert, director of the Citizen Lab, said "the findings raised questions about the company's human rights due-diligence practices and processes for preventing misuse of its software."[^cite_note-125] In a reply to an inquiry from [Human Rights Watch](46530.md) on the topic, Cyberbit did not deny selling this kind of technology, and rejected responsibility for its possible misuse on its customers.[^cite_note-126]

### NATO corruption investigation

The [NATO](21133.md) affiliated [procurement agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO_Support_and_Procurement_Agency) barred Elbit from receiving [arms contracts](608296.md) since May 2025, due an ongoing corruption investigation.[^cite_note-127]

### Ethical concerns and divestment

On September 3, 2009, the [Government Pension Fund of Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Pension_Fund)'s ethical council decided to sell the fund's stocks in Elbit due to the company's supply of surveillance systems for the [Israeli West Bank barrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_West_Bank_barrier).[^cite_note-globes-128] At a press conference to announce the decision, [Minister of Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Finance_\(Norway\)) [Kristin Halvorsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristin_Halvorsen) said "We do not wish to fund companies that so directly contribute to violations of international humanitarian law".[^cite_note-haaretz_2009-09-03-129] The Norwegian Ambassador to Israel, [Hans Jacob Biørn Lian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Jacob_Bi%C3%B8rn_Lian), was called to a meeting at the [Israeli Foreign Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Foreign_Ministry) where the decision was protested.[^cite_note-haaretz_2009-09-03-129]

In January 2010, [Danske Bank](738035.md) added Elbit to the list of companies that fail its Socially Responsible Investment policy. A bank spokesman noted that it was acting in the interests of its customers by not "placing their money in companies that violate international standards".[^cite_note-danske-130][^cite_note-Maan-131] The Danish financial watchdog *DanWatch* placed Elbit on its ethical [blacklist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blacklist) in 2011.[^cite_note-danwatch-132] In 2014, one of Denmark's largest [pension fund](43615.md) administrators PKA Ltd announced it will no longer consider investing in Elbit, stating "The [ICJ](14918.md) stated that the barrier only serves military purposes and violates Palestinian human rights. Therefore we have looked at whether companies produce custom-designed products to the wall and thus has a particular involvement in repressive activities."[^cite_note-Maan-131]

In March 2010, a Swedish [pension fund](43615.md), not wanting to be associated with companies violating international treaties, boycotted Elbit Systems for its involvement in the construction of Israel's West Bank barrier wall.[^cite_note-Ynetnews-133]

In December 2018, [HSBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HSBC) divested from Elbit[^cite_note-FIFAD-134] following Elbit's acquisition of [IMI Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMI_Systems). HSBC cited IMI's manufacturing of [cluster bombs](79745.md), which violated the bank's ethics policy of not investing in companies linked to the manufacturing or marketing of cluster munition.[^cite_note-135]

In 2019, [Axa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axa) partially divested from Elbit Systems following pressure from the [Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boycott,_Divestment_and_Sanctions) movement.[^cite_note-FIFAD-134][^cite_note-\:0-136][^cite_note-137] The move followed several years of campaigning by NGOs, including an April 2018 petition launched by [SumOfUs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SumOfUs) that received 140,000 signatures. Axa "quietly reduce\[d]" its investments in Elbit and Israeli banks.[^cite_note-FIFAD-134] Axa remains indirectly invested in Elbit and Israeli banks through a [non-controlling interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-controlling_interest) in its former subsidiary [Alliance Bernstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_Bernstein).[^cite_note-FIFAD-134][^cite_note-\:0-136]

### Instro Precision and Touche Ltd

In August 2026, UK Investigative magazine [Private Eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_Eye) reported that around a fifth of the controlled military and dual use goods sent from the UK to Israel in 2023 and 2024 - including technology for military aircraft and the conversion of manned to unmanned aircraft - had been exported by an Israeli couple working out of a house in cul-de-sac in Kent. According to the article, Touche Ltd, founded in 2019 by a lead engineer for Elbit's UK subsidiary Instro Precision, was granted an Open Individual Export Licence in 2022 to export military equipment to Israel in 2022, has no employees, no website, no contact details and files only micro-company accounts, and has a registered address only a short distance from Instro's factory in Sandwich, Kent.[^cite_note-PEye_20260821-138]

### Belgian seizures and allegations of illegal shipments

#### October 2025 seizures

In October 2025, four crates of radar and electronics parts for UAVs from Renens-based company SwissTo12, with consignee Elbit Systems in Israel, were intercepted and seized by Belgian customs authorities in Bierset Airport in Liège while transiting from Zurich to Tel Aviv. [^cite_note-139] The region of Walloon within Belgium has had an embargo on military shipments to Israel since 2009, and is not issuing any export or transit permits to Israel for goods which might "increase Israel's military capability."[^cite_note-140]

In 2021 it had been announced that Elbit and SwissTo12 were partnering on the development and production of specialist RF antennas and subsystems for Elbit Systems’ Naval EW programs.[^cite_note-141]



Liege airport

#### April 2026 seizures

In April 2026, Belgian authorities reported the interception of two separate shipments of military-related equipment at [Liège Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li%C3%A8ge_Airport) that had originated in the United Kingdom and were destined for Elbit Systems in Israel. Belgian media reported that the cargo was inspected following concerns that it contained controlled military items, and that once it had been determined that the shipments contained parts for military aircraft, laser targeting and fire control systems, a criminal investigation had been opened to determine whether export control or transit regulations had been breached.[^cite_note-142][^cite_note-143]

According to Walloon minister-president, [Adrien Dolimont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrien_Dolimont), “We have to see if the legislation has been respected. Here, in this case, it's clear that it hasn't. No transit licence was requested; if it had been, it would have been refused”.[^cite_note-DC20260414-144]

Statements from the Belgian government and media reports identified one of the seized shipments as coming from the Wolverhampton factory of [Moog Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moog_Inc.), a US defense supplier, and bound for Israel's [M-346](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alenia_Aermacchi_M-346_Master) fast jet trainer program.[^cite_note-145] In May 2026, UK investigative newspaper *[Private Eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_Eye)* published allegations that, subsequent to the April 2026 seizures in [Liège Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li%C3%A8ge_Airport), Moog's Wolverhampton facility continued to ship M-346 components to Elbit Systems along flight paths that overflew Belgian [airspace](353557.md), in apparent breach of the 16 January 2026 Royal Decree prohibiting use of Belgian airspace for any flights containing military materiel for use by the Israeli armed forces.[^cite_note-PEye-146]

## Protest activity

### Czech Republic

Further information: [2026 Pardubice arson attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pardubice_arson_attack)

In March 2026, a warehouse in [Pardubice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pardubice) was destroyed by [arson](184695.md).[^cite_note-147] The building was owned by LPP Holding, a drone manufacturing company. The warehouse had been publicized as a "Centre of Excellence" for building drones in partnership with Elbit Systems, however company spokesman said that the operations had not initiated. The fire was extinguished without threat of public harm and no people were injured.[^cite_note-148][^cite_note-149][^cite_note-150] A group called the Earthquake Faction claimed responsibility for the attack, stating that the facility was targeted due to LPP Holding's planned cooperation with Elbit.[^cite_note-151]

### Germany

Further information: [Ulm 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulm_5)

In 2025, Elbit Germany saw several protests such as a protest camp in April, banners hung on the [Ulm Minster](853115.md) in July,[^cite_note-152] demonstrations in August demanding the closure of Elbit’s German locations.[^cite_note-153]

In September 2025, Palestine Action Germany activists were arrested after breaking in and damaging property at the Elbit plant in [Ulm, Germany](189455.md). The protestors, who have since been in [pre-trial detention](14632360.md), maintain that their goal was to highlight the complicity of Elbit Systems in the killing of Gazan civilians. The lawyer of the '[Ulm 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulm_5)' has called for the immediate release of the activists and an investigation into the close involvement of Elbit in war crimes, crimes against humanity, and genocide in Gaza.[^cite_note-154]

### United Kingdom

Further information: [Palestine Action](67777543.md)

Following the [2014 Gaza War](43243199.md), activists began protesting outside Ferranti's factory, primarily against Elbit's production of various bombs and other military technology used by the Israeli Air Force and resulting in Palestinian casualties.[^cite_note-novara-70] Activists particularly cited Elbit's production of the Hermes drone, which was involved in the deaths of at least four Palestinian children.[^cite_note-the_intercept-29]

During the [2021 Israel–Palestine crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Israel%E2%80%93Palestine_crisis), on May 19, 2021, four members of [Palestine Action](67777543.md) dressed in red [boiler suits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiler_suits) and climbed onto the roof of an Elbit Systems-owned drone factory in Meridian Business Park, [Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester).[^cite_note-bbc-155][^cite_note-156][^cite_note-157] The [occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_\(protest\)) lasted 6 days, and a total of 10 arrests were made for [conspiracy](3406102.md) to commit criminal damage and aggravated [trespass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trespass).[^cite_note-bbc-155] Palestine Action group has staged similar occupations of Elbit Systems sites in [Bristol](36741.md),[^cite_note-158] [Oldham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oldham) in collaboration with [Extinction Rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extinction_Rebellion),[^cite_note-159][^cite_note-160][^cite_note-161] and [Tamworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamworth,_Staffordshire)[^cite_note-162] in collaboration with [Animal Rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Rebellion).[^cite_note-163]

Beginning in October 2023, when the [Gaza war](74998519.md) began, several Elbit factories were protested by activists with Palestine Action. At the Instro Precision Factory in [Sandwich, Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandwich,_Kent), more than 100 people blocked the entrances. In [Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester), several activists climbed on the roof of the Howmet Fastening Systems factory, and a single protester chained themself to the entrance of the U-TacS factory.[^cite_note-164]

On 6 August 2024, [6 activists drove a repurposed prison van into Elbit's compound at Aztec West](81690072.md), breached security fences, entered the facility and caused substantial damage to property and equipment.[^cite_note-BBC251125-165] The action also involved confrontations with security personnel and police, during the course of which a police officer sustained a fracture to her lumbar spine.[^cite_note-BBC251125-165] Six activists were arrested at the scene and there were raids on homes subsequently.[^cite_note-NallyTurnnidge-166] 24 people were charged in all.

In September 2024, the London headquarters of [APCO Worldwide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APCO_Worldwide) was targeted by [Palestine Action](67777543.md) over its representation of the UK interests of Elbit Systems.[^cite_note-167][^cite_note-168]

In the UK Elbit have attempted to coordinate with the UK government to suppress illegal destructive activity against their offices and factories, especially in relation to the [Palestine Action](67777543.md) direct action network. Reporting in *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* showed attempted coordination between Elbit and the [Embassy of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Israel,_London) with the [Home Office](225695.md), [Attorney General for England and Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney_General_for_England_and_Wales) and [Crown Prosecution Service](383064.md) regarding the prosecution of [Palestine Action](67777543.md) activists, dating back to April 2022.[^cite_note-169] In August 2020, one month after the emergence of Palestine Action, then UK foreign secretary [Dominic Raab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_Raab) met with [Orit Farkash-Hacohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orit_Farkash-Hacohen), Israel's former minister of strategic affairs, who "pressed Raab on direct action protests against Israeli companies in Britain, noting how “the London offices of Elbit Systems” had been attacked for the fourth time in as many weeks."[^cite_note-DC_12Mar25-170] In 2022, then-Home Secretary [Priti Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priti_Patel) met with [Martin Fausset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Fausset?action=edit\&redlink=1), the CEO of Elbit UK to “discuss protests and security”. The meeting produced a series of suggested actions, which were entirely redacted when the document was made public under FOI.[^cite_note-DC_12Mar25-170]

In 2024, CPS used powers under the [Terrorism Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorism_Act_2000) to detain activists without charges.[^cite_note-171]

In 2025, following a sustained campaign of direct action by groups, including [Palestine Action](67777543.md), which targeted their offices with protests and occupations, both [Allianz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allianz) and [Aviva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviva) terminated their insurance contracts with Elbit's UK subsidiaries. Allianz ended its coverage of Elbit Systems UK, while Aviva ceased insuring UAV Engines.[^cite_note-172][^cite_note-173][^cite_note-174]

### United States

In response to the [Gaza war](74998519.md), protesters chained themselves to the entrance of Elbit's office in [Cambridge, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts), on October 12.[^cite_note-175] Later that month, protesters from [Palestine Action US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Action_US) claimed to have completely shut down the company's operations in an incident resulting in nine arrests.[^cite_note-176] The Cambridge office closed in August 2024 following weekly protests.[^cite_note-177]

In November 2023, three protesters were arrested for vandalism at an Elbit location in [Merrimack, New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merrimack,_New_Hampshire).[^cite_note-178] According to an [NBC News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC_News) report, the "Merrimack Fire Department was also called to the scene due to a report of smoke coming from the roof \[...] at least one of the main lobby doors had been locked shut with a bicycle lock."[^cite_note-179] Elbit's institutional shareholders were also subject to pro-Palestine protests with demands for companies targeted, including [BNY Mellon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BNY_Mellon) and [JPMorgan Chase](231001.md), to divest from the company and sell their stock in the Israeli defense company.[^cite_note-180][^cite_note-181]
