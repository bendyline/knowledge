**Masai Russell** ([/məˈsaɪ/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/8/8a/LL-Q1860\_%28eng%29-Flame%2C_not_lame-Masai_Russell.wav/LL-Q1860\_%28eng%29-Flame%2C_not_lame-Masai_Russell.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/8/8a/LL-Q1860_%28eng%29-Flame%2C_not_lame-Masai_Russell.wav/LL-Q1860_%28eng%29-Flame%2C_not_lame-Masai_Russell.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> [*mə-SY*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key); born June 17, 2000)[^cite_note-2] is an American track and field sprinter and hurdler. She won the gold medal at the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics) in the [100 m hurdles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_100_metres_hurdles) event. Russell is the [world record holder](3443107.md) in the [100 m hurdles](4064591.md), running a time of 12.09 seconds on August 27, 2026.

In addition to her world record, Russell holds personal bests of 7.74 seconds over the [60-meter hurdles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/60_metres_hurdles), which is tied for [thirteenth-fastest of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/60_metres_hurdles#Women), and 54.66 seconds over the [400-meter hurdles](707859.md).

## Career

In 2021, Russell ran her first sub-13s 100 m hurdles in placing second in her heat at the SEC Championships. She went on to place sixth in the final.[^cite_note-WAprofile-3] Later that year, Russell ran 12.93s to make the final of the [NCAA Division I Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_NCAA_Division_I_Outdoor_Track_and_Field_Championships), where she finished sixth. She also placed fourth in the 400 meter hurdles with a time of 56.18s, a personal best at the time.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

At the [2022 NCAA Division I Outdoor Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NCAA_Division_I_Outdoor_Track_and_Field_Championships), Russell placed third in the 100 m hurdles and fourth in the 400 m hurdles.[^cite_note-WAprofile-3]

In 2023, Russell broke the collegiate record for the 60 m hurdles, running 7.75s to beat [Grace Stark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grace_Stark)'s time of 7.78s set the year before.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] In April, she ran a [collegiate/NCAA record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_States_collegiate_records_in_track_and_field#Women) of 12.36s over 100 m hurdles at the [Texas Relays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Relays).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] In June, she placed second in both the 100 m hurdles and 400 m hurdles at the [2023 NCAA Division I Outdoor Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_NCAA_Division_I_Outdoor_Track_and_Field_Championships).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] After the conclusion of the 2023 NCAA season, Russell turned professional.[^cite_note-13] At the [2023 USA Outdoor Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_USA_Outdoor_Track_and_Field_Championships), Russell finished third qualifying to represent the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_at_the_2023_World_Athletics_Championships) at the [World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_World_Athletics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_100_metres_hurdles) in [Budapest](36787.md).[^cite_note-14] She made the semi-finals, but did not finish there after hitting a hurdle.[^cite_note-15]



Russell at the [2024 United States Olympic trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Olympic_trials_\(track_and_field\))

Russell finished fourth in the 60 m hurdles at the [2024 World Indoor Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_World_Athletics_Indoor_Championships).[^cite_note-16] In April 2024, she signed a sponsorship deal with [Nike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nike,_Inc.) after previously being signed with [Gymshark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymshark).[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] She placed first at the [2024 U.S. Olympic Trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Olympic_trials_\(track_and_field\)) in the 100 m hurdles in a personal best time of 12.25 to qualify her for the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics) in Paris.[^cite_note-19] At the Olympics, Russell won gold with a time of 12.33s, narrowly beating [Cyréna Samba-Mayela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyr%C3%A9na_Samba-Mayela).[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] In September 2024, it was announced that Russell had signed up for the inaugural season of the [Michael Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Johnson_\(sprinter\))-founded [Grand Slam Track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Slam_Track).[^cite_note-22] She ended her 2024 season by finishing third at the inaugural [Athlos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athlos) meet.[^cite_note-23]

On May 2, 2025, she ran the 100-meter hurdles in a time of 12.17 seconds, an [American record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_States_records_in_track_and_field), at the [2025 Miami Grand Slam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Miami_Slam) meet.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-\:2-25] On July 5, she ran to fourth place at the [2025 Prefontaine Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Prefontaine_Classic).[^cite_note-26] On August 2, she competed in the [2025 USA Outdoor Track and Field Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_USA_Outdoor_Track_and_Field_Championships) and won the gold medal with a time of 12.22 seconds.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] At the [2025 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Athletics_Championships), Russell won both her heat and semi-final race but only finished fourth in the final.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30] She ended the season with winning the 2025 Athlos meet for the 100 m hurdles with a wind-assisted 12.52 seconds.[^cite_note-31]



Russell after breaking the [100 metres hurdles](4064591.md) world record at the [2026 Weltklasse Zürich](84064248.md)

In May 2026, Russell further improved on her personal best to 12.14 seconds after winning the event at the [Xiamen Diamond League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Xiamen_Diamond_League).[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33] On June 24, 2026, Russell competed in the [Los Angeles Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Grand_Prix) in the 100 meter hurdles and won with a time of 12.26 seconds.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] On July 4, she won the same event at the [2026 Prefontaine Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Prefontaine_Classic) with a time of 12.24, equaling a [meet record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefontaine_Classic#Women).[^cite_note-37]

On August 27, 2026, Russell won the 100 m hurdles in the [2026 Zürich Diamond League](84064248.md) with a [world record](3443107.md) time of 12.09 seconds, one of two world records set at that meet.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40] This broke the previous record time of 12.12s by [Tobi Amusan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobi_Amusan) back in 2022.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42] The victory marked her 11th consecutive win in the event. Reflecting on her performance, Russell suggested that a stronger [tailwind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headwind_and_tailwind) could have pushed her under the 12-second barrier. "If we get a 1.9 wind behind my back, that could have been a sub-12 race," she noted, adding, "Everything that I’ve said that I wanted to do has come into fruition, so why not say I think I can be the first one to run sub-12?"[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44]

## Education

Russell graduated from [Bullis School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullis_School) in 2018.[^cite_note-45] She graduated from the [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky) with a degree in Health Science in 2023.[^cite_note-Paris24bio-1]
