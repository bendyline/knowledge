[Georgia](48768.md) competed at the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md) in [Milan](36511.md) and [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md), Italy, from 6 to 22 February 2026.

The Georgian team consisted of eight athletes (four per gender) competing in two sports: alpine skiing and figure skating.[^cite_note-1] Figure skaters [Luka Berulava](62856231.md) and [Diana Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diana_Davis) were the country's flagbearer during the [opening ceremony](75430691.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Meanwhile, [Luka Berulava](62856231.md) and [Nino Tsiklauri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nino_Tsiklauri) were the country's flagbearer during the [closing ceremony](76159922.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

On 16 February, [Luka Berulava](62856231.md) and [Anastasiia Metelkina](67224137.md) won the silver medal in the [figure skating pairs event](82110766.md), marking the country's first ever [Winter Olympic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_Olympic) medal.[^cite_note-6]

## Figure skating

Main articles: [Figure skating at the 2026 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics) and [Figure skating at the 2026 Winter Olympics – Qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Qualification)

In the [2025 World Figure Skating Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Figure_Skating_Championships) in Boston, the United States, Georgia secured one quota in each of the men's singles, the pair skating, and the ice dance.[^cite_note-8] Georgia earned one quota in women's singles at the [ISU Skate to Milano Figure Skating Qualifier 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Skate_to_Milano) in Beijing, China.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] Furthermore, Georgia qualified to the team event.[^cite_note-11]
