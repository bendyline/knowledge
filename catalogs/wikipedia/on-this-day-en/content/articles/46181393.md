The **Principlists** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): اصول‌گرایان, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Osul-Garāyān*, lit. 'followers of principles,[^cite_note-33] [fundamentalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_fundamentalism)[^cite_note-Islamism-10][^cite_note-Harris-34]'), also interchangeably known as the **Iranian hardliners**, **Iranian Conservatives**[^cite_note-\:01-1][^cite_note-SRD-2] and formerly referred to as the **Right** or **Right-wing**,[^cite_note-SRD-2][^cite_note-\:1-35][^cite_note-\:2-36] are one of two main political [camps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_factions_in_Iran) in post-[revolutionary](347268.md) Iran; the [Reformists](491098.md) are the other camp. The term *[hardliners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardliner)* that some Western sources use in the Iranian political context usually refers to the faction.[^cite_note-37] The faction rejects the [status quo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Status_quo) internationally,[^cite_note-CI-25] but favors domestic preservation.[^cite_note-38]

## Demographics

According to a poll conducted by the Iranian Students Polling Agency (ISPA) in April 2017, 15% of Iranians identify as leaning Principlist. In comparison, 28% identify as leaning [Reformist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Reformists).[^cite_note-39]

In April 2021, a joint public opinion survey conducted by the [Chicago Council on Global Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Council_on_Global_Affairs) and IranPoll found out that 19% of Iranians identified as Principlist while 7% were leaning Principlist, and if Reformists (21%) and leaning Reformist (10%) were still higher, they also noted that "the support base for the reformists has shrunk by about 8 percentage points since 2017, while the support base for the conservatives has grown by 4 percentage points."[^cite_note-40]

## Factions

- **Ultra conservatives**—also known as **neoconservatives**. This grouping is more aggressive and openly confrontational toward the West.

  [^cite_note-sherill-orbis-19]

  &#x20;Many ultra- or neo-Principlists are laymen representing the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC) collectively.

  [^cite_note-sherill-orbis-19]

- **Traditional conservatives** are a political faction that helped form [the Revolutionary government](347268.md) and can point to personal ties with [Ruhollah Khomeini](26232.md).

  [^cite_note-sherill-orbis-19]

  &#x20;These conservatives support the Islamist government and advocate for clerical rule. (See also: [Clericalism in Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clericalism_in_Iran))

  [^cite_note-41]

- **[Deviant current](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deviant_current)** are a political faction led by Mahmoud Ahmadinejad that espouses Islamic populism and [Iranian nationalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_nationalism).
