The **Berlin House of Representatives** ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): *Abgeordnetenhaus* (German: [\[ˈʔapɡəʔɔʁdnətn̩ˌhaʊs\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Standard_German) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/b/b8/De-Abgeordnetenhaus.ogg/De-Abgeordnetenhaus.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/b/b8/De-Abgeordnetenhaus.ogg/De-Abgeordnetenhaus.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is the [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Germany) parliament (*[Landtag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landtag)*) of [Berlin](3354.md), [Germany](11867.md) according to the [city-state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City-state)'s constitution. In 1993, the parliament moved from [Rathaus Schöneberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rathaus_Sch%C3%B6neberg) to its present house on [Niederkirchnerstraße](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niederkirchnerstra%C3%9Fe) in [Mitte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitte_\(locality\)), which from 1899 until 1934 was the seat of the [Prussian House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prussian_House_of_Representatives). The current president of the parliament is [Cornelia Seibeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornelia_Seibeld) ([CDU](60584.md)).

## History



Staircase

The House of Representatives was established by the new constitution of [West Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Berlin) in 1951. It replaced the former city legislature called *Stadtverordnetenversammlung* (city deputies assembly), established by the [Prussian Reforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prussian_Reforms) in 1808 and re-established by [Allied](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allied-occupied_Germany)-initiated state elections of 1946.

Between 1951 and 1990 the House of Representatives was a parliament of restricted autonomy, since the [Allied Control Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allied_Control_Council) required that all its legislation and its elections, such as those of mayors and the senators (then still elected and not yet appointed by the mayor), be subject to Western Allied confirmation or rejection. After [reunification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_reunification) the House of Representatives continued to be the parliament of united Berlin.

## Elections

See also: [Elections in Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Berlin)

The parliament of Berlin is chosen every five years in a general, free, secret and direct ballot according to the principle of [proportional representation](23298.md). It consists of at least 130 representatives, 78 chosen directly in the [electoral districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_district) of the [Berlin boroughs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boroughs_and_localities_of_Berlin), and 52 indirectly from land or district lists. If a party wins more constituency seats than its overall share of the vote, the overall size of the House of Representatives increases because of these [overhang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overhang_seat) mandates.

The current distribution of seats as of the re-run [2023 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Berlin_state_election) (compared to the [2021 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Berlin_state_election)) is:

- [Christian Democratic Union](60584.md) (CDU): 52 seats (+22)

- [Social Democratic Party](60585.md) (SPD): 34 seats (−2)

- [Alliance 90/The Greens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_90/The_Greens): 34 seats (+2)

- [The Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Left_\(Germany\)): 22 seats (−2)

- [Alternative for Germany](38708229.md) (AfD): 17 (+4)

In 2023, the [Free Democratic Party (FDP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Democratic_Party) failed to reach the 5% bar and so was not eligible for representation. The 2023 election was a re-run of the 2021 election after the [Constitutional Court of the State of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_the_State_of_Berlin) [declared the results invalid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Berlin_state_election#Background) due to numerous irregularities and ordered a repeat election within 90 days.[^cite_note-Void-1][^cite_note-2]

In 2016, the [Pirate Party Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirate_Party_Berlin) lost the 15 seats it won for the first time in the 2011 election due to their failure to cross the 5% [election threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election_threshold) necessary to qualify for representation while the AfD joined the assembly for the first time and the FDP returned following a recovery in voter support.

## Functions



Nameplate

The consideration and passing of [legislation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislation) is the most important task, including the [government budget](4360909.md). In addition, the parliament chooses the [Governing Mayor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governing_Mayor_of_Berlin), and checks the city's government, the [Senate of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Berlin).

Citizens can petition the House of Representatives to debate topics via [agenda initiatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_democracy_in_Berlin).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
