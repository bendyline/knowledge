The **Minister of Home Affairs** ([Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): गृहमन्त्री, romanized: *Gṛha Mantrī*; or simply, the **Home Minister**) is the head of the [Ministry of Home Affairs](41156390.md) of the [Government of Nepal](19233409.md). One of the senior-most officers in the [Federal Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Nepal), the chief responsibility of the home minister is the maintenance of Nepal's [internal security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_security); the country's large police force comes under its jurisdiction.
