**Gelida** (Catalan pronunciation: [\[ʒəˈliðə\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Catalan)) is a town and [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Spain) in the *[comarca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comarques_of_Catalonia)* of [Alt Penedès](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alt_Pened%C3%A8s), [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Barcelona), [Catalonia](6822.md), Spain.

The town is built on a hillside above the [Anoia river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anoia_river), the [AP-7 motorway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autopista_AP-7), and [line 4 of the Barcelona commuter rail network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodalies_Barcelona_line_4). It is connected to its railway station via [a funicular railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funicular_de_Gelida).
