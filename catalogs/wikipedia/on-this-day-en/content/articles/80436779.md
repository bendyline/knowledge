The **2025–26 UCLA Bruins women's basketball team** represented the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) during the [2025–26 NCAA Division I women's basketball season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_season).[^cite_note-2025media-1] The Bruins were led by head coach [Cori Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cori_Close) in her fifteenth year, and played their home games at [Pauley Pavilion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pauley_Pavilion). The Bruins went 37–1 and won the program's second women's basketball national championship, and the first basketball championship in the NCAA era.[^cite_note-NYTchamp-2]

In the preseason, the Bruins were voted the favorite to win the Big Ten conference, with senior center [Lauren Betts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Betts) named the 2025-26 Big Ten Preseason Player of the Year.[^cite_note-pre10-3] Betts and senior guard [Kiki Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiki_Rice) were named to the All-Big Ten preseason conference team.[^cite_note-pre10-3] Laren Betts was named to the Associated Press Preseason All-America team.[^cite_note-4]

In November 2025, the first month of the season, the Bruins faced ranked teams in Oklahoma, Texas, North Carolina, and Tennessee, going 3-1, and won their other games, to establish their ranking in the top 5.[^cite_note-USWBANOV2026-5] Past the mid-point of the conference schedule at the end of January 2026, the Bruins had defeated 10 conference teams, including four ranked teams, to solidify a number 2 national ranking.[^cite_note-6] The entire starting lineup was projected to be taken in the first round of the WNBA draft.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] The Bruins clinched a share of the Big Ten conference title on February 19, 2026 by defeating Washington.[^cite_note-10] The Bruins won the outright regular season [Big Ten conference championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Big_Ten_Conference_women's_basketball_season) on February 22 against Wisconsin on Senior night.[^cite_note-11] They finished the conference season undefeated on March 1, 2026 with a win at USC.[^cite_note-12]

Starting the postseason seeded number 1 in the [Big Ten tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Big_Ten_women's_basketball_tournament), the Bruins won all three games, concluding with a tournament record-setting win over number 8 ranked Iowa.[^cite_note-iowa1-13] For the [NCAA tournament](76476380.md), the Bruins were the number 2 seed overall and number one in the Sacramento region.[^cite_note-SACseed1-14] The Bruins needed a comeback in the second half against Duke to reach the final four.[^cite_note-15] In the national semi-final game, the Bruins won the re-match with the Texas Longhorns, the one loss they experienced all season.[^cite_note-16] In a dominating performance, UCLA defeated [South Carolina](81087913.md) 79-51 in the [2026 NCAA Division I women's basketball championship game](79646889.md).[^cite_note-NYTchamp-2] All six senior players were selected in the [2026 WNBA draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_WNBA_draft), five in the first round, setting a record.[^cite_note-17]

## Previous season

The Bruins finished the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UCLA_Bruins_women's_basketball_team) with a 34–3 record, including 16–2 in Big Ten play to finish in second place to [USC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_USC_Trojans_women's_basketball_team). UCLA proceeded to beat USC in the [2025 Big Ten conference tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Big_Ten_women's_basketball_tournament) to win the championship.[^cite_note-18] They were ranked No. 1 in the polls for the first time in program history.[^cite_note-19] They received the number 1 overall seed in the [2025 NCAA Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NCAA_Division_I_Women's_Basketball_Tournament) and reached their first NCAA Final Four game[^cite_note-20],[^cite_note-21] where they were eliminated by the eventual national champion [UConn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UConn_Huskies_women's_basketball_team).[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] A documentary named *You See LA* produced by Fox Sports was broadcast on March 1, 2026 on FS1 immediately following the 2026 UCLA-USC women's basketball game.[^cite_note-youseela1-24][^cite_note-youseela2-25] The episode includes footage from games and post-game locker room team meetings, team gatherings, and interviews.[^cite_note-26]

## Offseason

In the offseason, the Bruins did not lose any players to graduation.[^cite_note-reload-27] Charlisse Leger-Walker entered the lineup following a 650-day recovery from an ACL injury she suffered while playing against the Bruins for Washington State before her transfer.[^cite_note-28] The team was joined by Lauren Betts' younger sister Sienna,[^cite_note-reload-27] Gianna Kneepkens, who transferred from Utah,[^cite_note-29] and [Megan Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Grant), a member of the UCLA Softball team.[^cite_note-30] New recruit Lena Bilić earned a medal at FIBA summer international play representing Croatia,[^cite_note-FIBA-31] while Sienna Betts and Gianna Kneepkens also earned medals for UCLA.[^cite_note-FIBA-31] The Bruins also signed Christina Karamouzi from Sweden.[^cite_note-32] With six senior starters available, Angela Dugalić asked to come off the bench.[^cite_note-33]

The entire highly ranked[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35] 2024–25 freshman recruiting class, along with Janiah Barker and Londynn Jones, entered the transfer portal within days following the end of the season.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37] They wanted more playing time and larger roles within the team.[^cite_note-hoops1-38] Elina Aarnisalo, who transferred to North Carolina, would have played significant minutes.[^cite_note-hoops1-38] The Bruin team would play all six of the teams of the transfers during the season and defeat them.

Former player Michaela Onyenwere became part of the coaching staff of the 2025-26 team.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40]
