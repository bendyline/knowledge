**Clearlake Capital Group, L.P.** is an American [private equity firm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_equity_firm) founded in 2006 that focuses on the technology, industrial and consumer sectors.[^cite_note-Buyout-2][^cite_note-FT1-3][^cite_note-WSJ1-4][^cite_note-Forbes1-6] The firm has more than \$90 billion of assets under management and is headquartered in [Santa Monica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica,_California), California, with offices in [Dallas](53838.md), [London](17867.md), [Dublin](8504.md), [Luxembourg](17515.md), [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md), [New York](645042.md) and [Singapore](27318.md).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] In 2022, the firm was a part of an investor consortium named [BlueCo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BlueCo) that purchased [Chelsea F.C.](7473.md)[^cite_note-FT1-3][^cite_note-Forbes1-6][^cite_note-\:5-10]

## Background

Clearlake Capital was founded in 2006 by Steven Chang, [Behdad Eghbali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behdad_Eghbali) and [José E. Feliciano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_E._Feliciano).[^cite_note-Buyout-2][^cite_note-FT1-3][^cite_note-WSJ1-4][^cite_note-Forbes1-6] The strategy was to shift between [buyouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buyout) and [distressed securities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distressed_securities) depending on economic conditions.[^cite_note-FT1-3] This allowed the firm to take advantage of the [2008 financial crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_financial_crisis) to acquire distressed securities and then sell them for a profit after recovery.[^cite_note-FT1-3]

In 2015, Chang left the firm leaving Eghbali and Feliciano as the remaining managing partners.[^cite_note-Buyout-2][^cite_note-\:1-11]

In May 2018, Clearlake sold a minority stake of up to 20% of its shares to [Dyal Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyal_Capital), [Petershill Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petershill_Partners) and [Landmark Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landmark_Partners).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-\:0-13] The purpose of the sale was to fund the creation of a [credit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_credit) unit.[^cite_note-\:0-13]

In June 2020, Clearlake acquired a majority interest in WhiteStar Asset Management from Pine Brook Road Partners.[^cite_note-FT1-3][^cite_note-\:4-14] WhiteStar Asset Management serves as the credit platform of Clearlake.[^cite_note-FT1-3][^cite_note-\:4-14]

In May 2022, Clearlake became the biggest user of "[General partner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_partner)-led [Private-equity secondary markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private-equity_secondary_market)".[^cite_note-FT1-3]

In September 2024, Clearlake acquired [Natixis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natixis)' pan-European [private credit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_credit) business MV Credit. [^cite_note-15]

In May 2025, Clearlake launched Clearlake Credit, which underwrites investments up to \$1 billion across private and liquid credit, and which is expected to triple the firm's credit asset base by 2030.[^cite_note-16]

In June 2026, Clearlake completed the acquisition of investment management firm [Pathway Capital Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pathway_Capital_Management), in a deal valued at nearly \$1 billion. Pathway expanded Clearlake's offering in the private-wealth market.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] The firm also closed its 8th flagship fund with \$14.8 billion, including co-investments and separately managed accounts, which the [Wall Street Journal](173070.md) called one of the largest funds in recent years.[^cite_note-19]

## Investments

### Consumer

In 2014, Clearlake acquired the women's clothing company [Ashley Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Stewart) for \$18 million after it had recently filed for bankruptcy.[^cite_note-\:1-11] After the company underwent significant restructuring including cost-cutting, Clearlake sold the company to Invus Group for more than \$140 million in 2016.[^cite_note-\:1-11]

### Sports

On May 30, 2022, an investor consortium named [BlueCo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BlueCo), co-led by Clearlake and [Todd Boehly](35675191.md), acquired [Chelsea F.C.](7473.md) for \$3 billion.[^cite_note-FT1-3][^cite_note-Forbes1-6][^cite_note-\:5-10][^cite_note-\:6-20] Clearlake, which owns about 60% of Chelsea F.C., shares joint control and equal governance of the club with Boehly.[^cite_note-Forbes1-6]

On September 16, 2026, Chelsea announced that Clearlake Capital acquired the ownership interest of Todd Boehly and Mark Walter’s ownership interest and therefore acquire full control of the Club.[^cite_note-ChelseaOwnership-21]

### Industrials

In March 2022, Clearlake acquired [Intertape Polymer Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intertape_Polymer_Group_Inc.) for \$2.6 billion, taking the company private.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

### Tech and software

In 2015, Clearlake took a controlling stake in Syncsort.[^cite_note-\:2-24] In 2016, Clearlake took a controlling stake in Vision Solutions.[^cite_note-\:2-24] In 2017, Clearlake sold both companies to [Centerbridge Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centerbridge_Partners) for \$1.26 billion and were merged.[^cite_note-\:2-24][^cite_note-\:3-25] In 2020, the merged company rebranded to [Precisely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precisely_\(company\)). In 2021, Clearlake and TA Associates reacquired Precisely for \$3.5 billion.[^cite_note-\:2-24][^cite_note-\:3-25]

Following the acquisition, Clearlake combined Endurance's Web Presence division with [Web.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web.com) to form Newfold Digital, a global web presence and domain name registration market leader.[^cite_note-26]

In October 2021, the firm acquired [Cornerstone OnDemand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornerstone_OnDemand), a [NASDAQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASDAQ)-listed software company, for \$5.2 billion and took the company private.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

In December 2023, Clearlake announced it would take [Alteryx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alteryx) private in a deal valued at \$4.4 billion.[^cite_note-29]

In October 2024, Clearlake acquired [Black Duck Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Duck_Software) for \$2.1 billion.[^cite_note-30]

In August 2025, the firm acquired [Dun & Bradstreet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dun_&_Bradstreet) for \$7.7 billion and took the company private.[^cite_note-31]
