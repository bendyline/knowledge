The **Grammy Award for Best Song Written for Visual Media** (including its previous names) is the [Grammy Awards](44635.md) awarded to songs written for films, television, video games or other visual media.

[Alan Menken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Menken) has won five awards (out of nine nominations) in this category for his work on the [Walt Disney animated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walt_Disney_Animation) films: *[The Little Mermaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Little_Mermaid_\(1989_film\))* (1989), *[Beauty and the Beast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beauty_and_the_Beast_\(1991_film\))* (1991), *[Aladdin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aladdin_\(1992_Disney_film\))* (1992), *[Pocahontas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocahontas_\(1995_film\))* (1995), and *[Tangled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangled_\(film\))* (2010). [Randy Newman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randy_Newman) has won three awards (out of seven nominations) for his work on the [Disney-Pixar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixar_Animation_Studios) films *[Toy Story 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toy_Story_2)* (1999), *[Monsters, Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsters,_Inc.)* (2001) and *[Cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cars_\(film\))* (2006). [Diane Warren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Warren) has received the most nominations with 11 in this category, winning once.

## Recipients



Two-time winner [James Horner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Horner) was the first recipient of the award alongside [Barry Mann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Mann) and [Cynthia Weil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cynthia_Weil).



[Phil Collins](153557.md) won in 1989 with [Lamont Dozier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamont_Dozier).



1990 award-winner [Carly Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carly_Simon).



[Bryan Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Adams) won alongside [Robert John "Mutt" Lange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_John_%22Mutt%22_Lange) and [Michael Kamen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Kamen) in 1992



Five-time recipient [Alan Menken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Menken) was awarded for his work in numerous [Disney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney) films and is the first person to win this category for consecutive years, achieving the feat in 1993 and 1994.



[Tim Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Rice) won alongside Alan Menken in 1994.



1995 award-winner [Bruce Springsteen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Springsteen).



Eleven-time nominee and 1997 winner [Diane Warren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Warren).



1996 winner and four-time nominee [Stephen Schwartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Schwartz).



[Madonna](142056.md) won in 2000 with [William Orbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Orbit).



Three-time winner and seven-time nominee, [Randy Newman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randy_Newman).



[Christopher Guest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Guest) won with Levy and McKean in 2004.



[Eugene Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Levy) won with Guest and McKean in 2004.



[Michael McKean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_McKean) won with Guest and Levy in 2004.



[Alan Silvestri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Silvestri) won in 2006.



[Annie Lennox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Lennox) won in 2005 alongside [Howard Shore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Shore) and [Fran Walsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran_Walsh).



[Peter Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Gabriel) won with Newman in 2009.



[Thomas Newman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Newman) won with Gabriel in 2009.



[A. R. Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._R._Rahman) won in 2010 alongside [Gulzar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulzar) and [Tanvi Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanvi_Shah).



[T Bone Burnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T_Bone_Burnett) received the award for 2011 and 2013.



[Taylor Swift](5422144.md) won in 2013 alongside The Civil Wars and [T Bone Burnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T_Bone_Burnett).



[Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele) won in 2014 with [Paul Epworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Epworth).



Husband and wife duo [Robert Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Lopez) and [Kristen Anderson Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Anderson_Lopez) won the award in 2015 and were nominated in 2019, 2021 and 2022.



[Common](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_\(rapper\)) won in 2016 with [Rhymefest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhymefest) & [John Legend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Legend).



[John Legend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Legend) won with Common in 2016.



[Justin Timberlake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Timberlake) won alongside [Max Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Martin) and [Shellback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shellback_\(record_producer\)) in 2017.



Two-time winner [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga) is the first woman to receive the award in consecutive years and the only person to win this category multiple times for the same media soundtrack, achieving the feat in 2019 and 2020.



[Lin-Manuel Miranda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lin-Manuel_Miranda) won in 2018 and 2023.



[Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish) won in 2021 and 2024 alongside her brother, [Finneas O'Connell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finneas_O'Connell), and is the youngest person to win this category.



[Jon Batiste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Batiste) won with [Dan Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Wilson_\(musician\)) in 2025.

## Name changes

- 1988–1999: The Grammy Award for Best Song Written Specifically for a Motion Picture or for Television

- 2000–2011: The Grammy Award for Best Song Written for a Motion Picture, Television or Other Visual Media

- 2012–present: The Grammy Award for Best Song Written for Visual Media
