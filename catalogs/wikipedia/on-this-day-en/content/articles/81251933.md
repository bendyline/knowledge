**Storm Amy** (known as **Storm Detlef** in Germany) was a powerful [extratropical cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extratropical_cyclone) which impacted parts of [Northern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Europe) and [Western Europe](33800.md) in October 2025. The first named (using the western group naming list) storm of the [2025–26 European windstorm season](80394473.md), Amy was named by the UK [Met Office](319671.md) on 1 October 2025.[^cite_note-4] The storm caused widespread damage and led to four deaths: two in France, one in Ireland and one in Poland.&#x20;

## Impact



Track of the centre of Storm Amy

### Belgium

On 3 October, the [Royal Meteorological Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Meteorological_Institute) issued yellow wind warnings covering much of the country from 8 a.m. on 4 October to 8 a.m. on 5 October.[^cite_note-5]&#x20;

On 4 October, the fire department in [Brussels](3708.md) was called to at least 90 incidents, including scaffolding being blown off an apartment building in [Woluwe-Saint-Pierre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woluwe-Saint-Pierre) and a statue falling off its pedestal in [Jubelpark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jubelpark).[^cite_note-6] A tree fell onto a van in [Kinrooi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinrooi) and damaged some power lines; no one was injured, but some properties lost power.[^cite_note-7] A large tree fell onto the recently-restored [beguinage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beguinage) in [Hasselt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasselt), damaging the roof and façade,[^cite_note-8] and several parked cars were damaged in [Aalst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalst,_Belgium) after trees fell on them.[^cite_note-9]&#x20;

### Denmark

On 3 October, the [Danish Meteorological Institute](4219738.md) (DMI) issued category 1 (yellow) warnings for wind and rain covering most of the country. High-speed trains between [Aalborg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg) and [Aarhus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus) were cancelled from 11 p.m. to 1\:45 p.m. the following day.[^cite_note-10] On 4 October, gusts of 35 m/s (78 mph; 130 km/h) were recorded in [Frederikshavn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederikshavn).[^cite_note-11] Riders in the [Red Bull King of the Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Bull_King_of_the_Air) *Cold Hawaii* [kiteboarding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiteboarding) competition in [Vorupør](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vorup%C3%B8r) took advantage of the storm gusts and high waves on 5 October, and flew 20 meters into the air.[^cite_note-12]&#x20;

### France

On 3 October, [Météo-France](4732627.md) placed 55 departments in the northern half of the country on yellow alerts for wind, with some also receiving warnings for rain, storm surges and thunderstorms.[^cite_note-13] The following day, six departments were placed on orange alert for strong winds.[^cite_note-14]&#x20;

On 4 October, one man died after drowning in [Étretat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89tretat), whilst a tree fell on a car in [Aisne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aisne), leaving one man dead and another seriously injured.[^cite_note-15] A gust of 134 km/h (83 mph) was recorded in the [Baie de Somme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baie_de_Somme) as a home had its roof torn off in [Hervilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hervilly); over 13,000 people were left without power in [Normandy](21724.md), according to [Enedis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enedis).[^cite_note-16]&#x20;

### Ireland

On 2 October, [Met Éireann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Met_%C3%89ireann) issued a Status Orange wind warning for the counties of [Donegal](45576.md), [Galway](92632.md), [Leitrim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Leitrim), [Mayo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Mayo) and [Sligo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Sligo) from 2 to 10 p.m. on 3 October, whilst the whole country was under a Status Yellow warning until midnight.[^cite_note-17] On 3 October, a Status Red wind warning was issued for County Donegal from 4 to 6 p.m. advising residents to shelter in place, whilst a Status Red marine warning was issued between [Loop Head](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_Head) and [Fair Head](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_Head) from 2 to 8 p.m. Status Orange warnings were issued for Donegal, Leitrim and Sligo counties from 4 to 8 a.m. for 4 October, whilst [Clare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Clare), [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Dublin), Galway, [Kerry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Kerry), [Louth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Louth), Mayo and [Wicklow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Wicklow) counties were under Status Yellow warnings until midday.[^cite_note-18]&#x20;

On 3 October, a man died in what [gardaí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garda%C3%AD) described as a "weather related incident" in [Letterkenny](620333.md) as a gust of 92 mph (148 km/h) was recorded at [Malin Head](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malin_Head). Over 184,000 homes were without power across Ireland, whilst 115 flights were cancelled at [Dublin Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_Airport), with 18 others diverted.[^cite_note-19]&#x20;

### Netherlands

On 3 October, the [Royal Netherlands Meteorological Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Meteorological_Institute) (KNMI) issued a Code Yellow warning lasting throughout 4 October covering the provinces of [Friesland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friesland), [North Brabant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Brabant), [North Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Holland), [South Holland](28687.md) and [Zeeland](52229.md). [KLM](16882.md) cancelled 70 flights to and from [Schiphol Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schiphol_Airport) for the following day in anticipation of the storm.[^cite_note-20] On 4 October, an [oil tanker](15923603.md) with 21 crew drifted rudderlessly in the [North Sea](21179.md) near the [Hollandse Kust Zuid Offshore Wind Farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollandse_Kust_Zuid_Offshore_Wind_Farm) amid force 9 winds and waves exceeding 5 m (16 ft).[^cite_note-21] Flooding was reported in multiple locations across the country, including in [Oudeschild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oudeschild) and [Vlaardingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vlaardingen). The Code Yellow warning for wind was extended to cover the whole country until the morning of 5 October.[^cite_note-22]&#x20;

### Norway



Partial road closure in Øvre Fyllingsveien in Bergen on 5 October

On 3 October, over 14,000 homes in [Agder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agder) and [Vestland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vestland) were without electricity. Five planes en route to [Værnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A6rnes) had to turn back whilst a number of flights were cancelled at [Stavanger Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavanger_Airport); overnight trains from [Oslo](22309.md) and [Stavanger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavanger) were cancelled and some [Color Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_Line_\(ferry_operator\)) ferries were cancelled. Emergency services were called to a [Scandic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandic) hotel in [Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergen) where windows on the fourth and fifth floors were about to fall out. The [Norwegian Water Resources and Energy Directorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Water_Resources_and_Energy_Directorate) (NVE) issued an orange flood warning for Southern and Eastern Norway whilst the [Norwegian Meteorological Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Meteorological_Institute) issued red, orange and yellow warnings for wind and rain.[^cite_note-23]&#x20;

By 4 October, over 150,000 homes had lost power and there had been nearly 2,500 damage reports.[^cite_note-24] The wall of a [VARD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VARD) shipyard in [Tomrefjord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomrefjord) collapsed, and roofs were torn off countless buildings, including at [Langesund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langesund) port and a block of flats in [Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergen). In [Notodden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notodden_\(town\)), 27 people were evacuated due to rising water levels in a local river and a waterspout damaged buildings in [Oppdal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oppdal_\(village\)). Over 170 roads were closed across the country and [Ørland Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%98rland_Municipality) was left isolated due to cancelled ferries and closed roads.[^cite_note-25] A number of wildfires were reported across the country due to trees falling onto power lines, including in [Bergsøya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergs%C3%B8ya,_Gjemnes), [Frei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frei_\(island\)), [Gjøra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gj%C3%B8ra) and [Isfjorden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isfjorden_\(village\)).[^cite_note-26] Winds of 41.5 m/s (93 mph; 149 km/h) were recorded at [Folgefonna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folgefonna), whilst wind speeds exceeding 40 m/s (89 mph; 140 km/h) were recorded in six other counties.[^cite_note-27] Police in [Trøndelag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%C3%B8ndelag) said at least three people had been injured in incidents relating to the storm in their county.[^cite_note-28] By 10 October, over 11,000 damages had been reported, estimated to cost between 1.5 and 2.1 billion [NOK](64149.md).[^cite_note-29]&#x20;

### Poland

The storm affected the coastal regions of [Poland](22936.md) as it moved over the [Baltic Sea](3335.md), prompting the [Institute of Meteorology and Water Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Institute_of_Meteorology_and_Water_Management\&action=edit\&redlink=1) (IMGW) to issue a second-degree storm warning across the Baltic coast and a first-degree storm warning in the [Pomeranian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomeranian_Voivodeship) and [West Pomeranian Voivodeships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Pomeranian_Voivodeship).[^cite_note-30] On 4 October, a 59-year-old kitesurfer died in [Jastarnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jastarnia) when he was lifted from the beach and thrown onto a concrete sidewalk from a height of several meters.[^cite_note-31] A [regatta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regatta) in the [Gdańsk Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gda%C5%84sk_Bay) was abandoned as strong winds caused several boats to capsize and snapped many masts.[^cite_note-32] In West Pomeranian Voivodeship, the fire brigade recorded 134 interventions between 4 and 5 October, mostly to fallen trees.[^cite_note-33]&#x20;

### Sweden

On 3 October, the [Swedish Meteorological and Hydrological Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Meteorological_and_Hydrological_Institute) issued warnings.[^cite_note-34] On 4 October, Amy made landfall in [Southern Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Sweden), causing power outages, disrupted communications and fallen trees.[^cite_note-35]&#x20;

On 4 October, a car on [Lisebergbanan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisebergbanan) at [Liseberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liseberg) crashed into a fallen tree, leaving a number of people on the ride injured.[^cite_note-36] Over 55,000 homes were left without power across the country; [Ellevio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellevio) said Amy had caused the worst power outage since Alfrida in 2019.[^cite_note-37]&#x20;

### United Kingdom

4 October



5 October

Weather warnings issued for Storm Amy in the UK

On 2 October, the [Met Office](319671.md) issued an [amber wind warning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_warning#United_Kingdom) covering much of northwestern Scotland from 5 p.m. on 3 October to 9 a.m. on 4 October. A further yellow wind warning was in place across Northern Ireland, Scotland and parts of England and Wales from 5 p.m. on 3 October to midnight on 4 October; a yellow warning for rain was also issued for Northern Ireland and Scotland.[^cite_note-38] According to a report from the [Energy Networks Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_Networks_Association_\(United_Kingdom\)) on 6 October, power outages had affected around 365,000 homes in [Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain) as a result of the storm.[^cite_note-39]&#x20;

#### England

In [England](9316.md), damage was less severe than the rest of the UK, although the [River Duddon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Duddon) flooded part of the [A595 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A595_road) and over 1,000 people lost power across [Cumbria](73021.md) on 4 October.[^cite_note-40] At [Gatwick Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gatwick_Airport), strong winds led to 153 flights being delayed, whilst some [DFDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DFDS) ferries between [Dover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dover) and [Dunkirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunkirk) were cancelled. A tree blocked the railway line between [Rye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rye_railway_station_\(East_Sussex\)) and [Ashford International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashford_International) and heavy rain led to flooding at [Earlswood railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earlswood_railway_station_\(Surrey\)).[^cite_note-41] The [Patterdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patterdale) mountain rescue team spent five hours rescuing a group of 13 people from [Great Dodd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Dodd) amid winds of up to 80 mph (130 km/h), during which two people were injured;[^cite_note-42] three men were trapped in a cave in the [Yorkshire Dales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire_Dales) due to flooding for over 30 hours before being rescued by over 100 volunteers with a number of rescue teams, including the [Royal Air Force Mountain Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Air_Force_Mountain_Rescue_Service), [Swaledale Mountain Rescue Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swaledale_Mountain_Rescue_Team), [Upper Wharfedale Fell Rescue Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Wharfedale_Fell_Rescue_Association) and Cave Rescue Organisation.[^cite_note-43]&#x20;

#### Isle of Man

On the [Isle of Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Man), a yellow weather warning for wind was in place from 4 p.m. on 3 October to 6 p.m. on 4 October.[^cite_note-44] A gust of 89 mph (143 km/h) was recorded at [Snaefell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snaefell) on 4 October, whilst wind speeds at [Ronaldsway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronaldsway) reached 68 mph (109 km/h) twice during the storm.[^cite_note-45] The storm caused widespread disruption on the island, with the [Department of Infrastructure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Infrastructure_\(Isle_of_Man\)) saying they responded to 59 reports of fallen trees, including some which fell on cars and houses, and 18 reports of flooding; a landslide blocked a road in [Malew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malew) and a wall partially collapsed in [Laxey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laxey).[^cite_note-46]&#x20;

#### Northern Ireland

In [Northern Ireland](21265.md), a gust of 92 mph (148 km/h) was recorded at the weather station on [Magilligan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magilligan), which is provisionally a new record for October, whilst over 65,000 properties lost power. [Translink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translink_\(Northern_Ireland\)) closed all rail lines in Northern Ireland and numerous roads were closed across the country, including the [Foyle Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foyle_Bridge) and parts of the [M1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M1_motorway_\(Northern_Ireland\)) and [M22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M22_motorway_\(Northern_Ireland\)) motorways. The [Education Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_Authority) (EA) told schools in the counties of [Antrim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Antrim), [Fermanagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Fermanagh), [Londonderry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Londonderry) and [Tyrone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Tyrone) to close at midday.[^cite_note-47] A large number of flights were impacted, with 13 being cancelled at [Belfast International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belfast_International_Airport) and another at [Derry Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derry_Airport). The EA said 18 schools had reported incidents to them by midday on 4 October, which were mainly damaged roofs and fallen trees.[^cite_note-48] On 5 October, [Northern Ireland Electricity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Ireland_Electricity) said around 1,500 properties were still without power. [Liz Kimmins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liz_Kimmins), the [Minister for Infrastructure](6934160.md), said 1,457 incidents, including 1,018 obstructions and over 350 floods, had been reported to her department.[^cite_note-49]&#x20;

#### Scotland

In [Scotland](26994.md), a gust of 96 mph (154 km/h) was recorded on [Tiree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiree) whilst power cuts were reported in the Highlands. In [Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow), a derelict building collapsed onto a car amid high winds on [Broomielaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broomielaw), forcing the road to close. A train travelling between [Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen) and [Inverurie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverurie) hit a fallen tree, leaving around a dozen passengers stranded on the train. The [Forth Road Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forth_Road_Bridge) was closed, whilst high-sided vehicles were expected to be prevented from crossing the [Clackmannanshire Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clackmannanshire_Bridge), [Kincardine Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kincardine_Bridge), [Tyne Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyne_Bridge) and possibly the [Queensferry Crossing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queensferry_Crossing). The [Highland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highland_Main_Line), [Kyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyle_Line), [West Highland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Highland_Line) and [Wick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_North_Line) railway lines were closed and speed restrictions were placed on all Scottish routes, whilst many ferries from [Cairnryan Harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairnryan_Harbour) to Northern Ireland were cancelled.[^cite_note-50] A [Ryanair](262878.md) flight from [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa_International_Airport) to [Glasgow Prestwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_Prestwick) was diverted several times and was in the air for over ten hours before landing at [Manchester Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Airport). The [Boeing 737](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737) came close to running out of fuel whilst it was in the air, prompting an investigation by the [Air Accidents Investigation Branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Accidents_Investigation_Branch).[^cite_note-51]&#x20;

By 4 October, over 75,000 homes had lost power across Scotland, as [Network Rail Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Network_Rail_Scotland\&action=edit\&redlink=1) said over 170 incidents had occurred across its network. A large sign was torn off a shop near [Inverness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverness) and the boundary wall at [Links Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Links_Park) in [Montrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montrose,_Angus) was blown over. Competition at the [Alfred Dunhill Links Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Dunhill_Links_Championship) was suspended and some events at the [Great Scottish Run](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Scottish_Run) were cancelled. The storm had a central pressure of 947.9 hPa (13.748 psi) at [Baltasound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltasound), setting a new October record in the UK.[^cite_note-52] On 5 October, [Scottish and Southern Electricity Networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_and_Southern_Electricity_Networks) said they had restored power to 71,000 homes, although 17,000 remained disconnected. Meanwhile, Network Rail Scotland said its teams had attended over 420 incidents, including debris, fallen trees and flooding blocking railway lines.[^cite_note-53] On 9 October, engineers found that the [submarine communications cable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submarine_communications_cable) which brings internet services to Tiree via [Tobermory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobermory,_Mull) had been damaged during the storm, whilst another submarine cable connecting [Shetland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shetland) to the mainland was damaged on 3 October.[^cite_note-54]&#x20;

#### Wales

In [Wales](69894.md), up to 2,665 properties had lost power by 4 October as gusts exceeded 85 mph (137 km/h). The [Severn Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severn_Bridge) was closed in both directions as well as part of the [M4 motorway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M4_motorway) at [Kenfig Viaduct](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Kenfig_Viaduct\&action=edit\&redlink=1). A fallen tree near [Gobowen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gobowen) forced rail services to be suspended between [Shrewsbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrewsbury) and [Wrexham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrexham), whilst a number of ferries were cancelled after one sailing from [Holyhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holyhead) to [Dublin](8504.md) was stranded offshore for hours.[^cite_note-55]&#x20;
