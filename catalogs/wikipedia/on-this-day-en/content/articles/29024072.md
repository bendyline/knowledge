**Senior Advisor to the President** is a title used by high-ranking political advisors to the [president of the United States](24113.md). Senior advisors to the president do not have formal government decision making authority, but they can have significant influence over decisions. Their role is to provide strategic advice, analysis, and recommendations to the president on key issues.

White House senior advisors are senior members of the [White House Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Office). The title has been formally used since 1993.

## Prior administrations

In prior administrations before 1993, the position of "[senior advisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_advisor)" was a title used for various other purposes. Numerous examples of the position also exist throughout the [executive departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_federal_executive_departments) and in the branch's [independent agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_agencies_of_the_United_States_government). For example, the [Food and Drug Administration](11632.md) includes a position with the title Senior Advisor for Science; the [Department of the Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_the_Interior) has a position with the title Senior Advisor for [Alaskan](624.md) Affairs.

Examples of people who had the responsibilities and/or influence of a senior advisor without the title included [Edward M. House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_M._House) (to [Woodrow Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson)) and [Louis Howe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Howe) (to [Franklin D. Roosevelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_D._Roosevelt))
