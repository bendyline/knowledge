**Vladikavkaz**,[^cite_note-11] formerly known as **Ordzhonikidze**,[^cite_note-12] **Dzaudzhikau**,[^cite_note-13] is the [capital city](181337.md) of [North Ossetia–Alania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Ossetia%E2%80%93Alania), Russia. It is located in the southeast of the republic at the foothills of the [Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus), situated on the [Terek River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terek_\(river\)). The city's population was 311,693 as of the [2021 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census), making it one of the most populous cities in the [North Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Caucasus) region.

The city is an [industrial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_sector) and [transportation centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport). Manufactured products include processed [zinc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinc) and [lead](17747.md), [machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machinery), [chemicals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_substance), [clothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clothing) and [food products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_products).

## Etymology

The name *Vladikavkaz*, derived from Russian, literally means 'ruler of the [Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus)'. The Ossetian name *Dzæudžyqæu* literally means '[Dzaug's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dzaug?action=edit\&redlink=1) settlement',[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] where *qæu* ('village') is a cognate of the [Khotanese Saka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khotanese_Saka) word *āguv* ('village') and the [Avestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avestan) word *gava* ('district').[^cite_note-17]

&#x20;In 1911, [Dmitry Rakovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Rakovich?action=edit\&redlink=1) wrote that the Ossetians prove that fortress was founded on the site of the Ingush village [Zaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaur_\(village\)) by the name of Vladikavkaz in the Ossetian language:[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEРакович19113-18]

> ...by their name of Vladikavkaz Dzaudzhi-Kau, the Ossetians confirm this opinion, since Dzauag is a proper name Zaur, and Kau means a village; otherwise - the village of Zaur.

In 1931, at the suggestion of the Ingush Regional Executive Committee, the city of Vladikavkaz was renamed Ordzhonikidze in honor of the Soviet political and military leader [Sergo Ordzhonikidze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergo_Ordzhonikidze), who during the [Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War) established Soviet power in the region.

In 1944, after the deportation of the Chechens and Ingush, the city of Ordzhonikidze was renamed the city of Dzaudzhikau, and in 1954 again Ordzhonikidze.[^cite_note-19] In 1990, the city acquired a double name: *Vladikavkaz* in Russian and *Dzaudzhikau* in Ossetian.

## History

The city was founded in 1784 as a Russian fortress at the entrance to the Darial Gorge near to the [Ingush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingush_people) village [Zaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaur_\(village\)),[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEТерскій_календарь189512-20][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] which had the purpose of serving as an outpost for the routes of communication between Russia and Georgia.[^cite_note-23] But according to a lot of other sources, Vladikavkaz was founded on the site of the Ossetian village of Kapkai.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]

The [Georgian Military Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_Military_Highway), crossing the mountains, was constructed in 1799 to link the city with [Georgia](48768.md) to the south, and in 1875 [a railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladikavkaz_Railway) was built to connect it to [Rostov-on-Don](317562.md) and [Baku](4566.md) in [Azerbaijan](746.md). Vladikavkaz has become an important industrial centre for the region, with smelting, refining, chemicals and manufacturing industries. During the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire), the settlement was the administrative capital of the [Vladikavkazsky Okrug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladikavkazsky_Okrug) of the [Terek Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terek_Oblast).



A monument in the city

The city is one of the largest in the Russian-controlled Caucasus, along with [Grozny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grozny), and was the capital of the [Mountain Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_Autonomous_Soviet_Socialist_Republic), a Soviet Republic established after the annexation of the [Mountainous Republic of the North Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountainous_Republic_of_the_North_Caucasus). It existed from 1921 to 1924 and comprised most of the modern-day territories of [Chechnya](6095.md), [North Ossetia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Ossetia%E2%80%93Alania) and [Kabardino-Balkaria](817725.md).

Vladikavkaz was fought over in both the [Russian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War) and [World War II](32927.md). In February 1919, the anti-Communist [Volunteer Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volunteer_Army) under General [Anton Denikin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Denikin) seized the city, before being expelled by the [Red Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Army) in March 1920. In early November 1942, the forces of [Nazi Germany](21212.md) tried unsuccessfully to seize the city but were repelled by the Soviet army. The Nazis left North Ossetia in January 1943.

On 26 November 2008, [Vitaly Karayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitaly_Karayev), the mayor of Vladikavkaz, was assassinated by an unidentified gunman.[^cite_note-28] On 31 December 2008, his successor, [Kazbek Pagiyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazbek_Pagiyev), was also assassinated by unidentified gunmen.

## Administrative and municipal status

The [Administrative divisions of Vladikavkaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Vladikavkaz?action=edit\&redlink=1) is composed of four districts.

- [Zaterechny district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaterechny_district?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Iristonsky district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iristonsky_district?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Promishlenny district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promishlenny_district,_Vladikavkaz?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Severo-Zapadny district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severo-Zapadny_district,_Vladikavkaz?action=edit\&redlink=1)

Vladikavkaz is the [capital](181337.md) of the republic.[^cite_note-Constitution-7] Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), it is, together with six rural localities, incorporated as **Vladikavkaz [City Under Republic Jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance)**—an administrative unit with the status equal to that of the [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_the_Republic_of_North_Ossetia%E2%80%93Alania).[^cite_note-Ref137-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), Vladikavkaz City Under Republic Jurisdiction is incorporated as **Vladikavkaz Urban Okrug**.[^cite_note-Ref831-8]

## Transportation

The city is served by the bus network (*[marshrutkas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshrutka)*). There are also [tram](30733.md) (since 1904) and [trolleybus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trolleybus) (since 1977) networks, plus the main [Vladikavkaz railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladikavkaz_railway_station).

The city is served by [Beslan Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beslan_Airport) located about 9 kilometres from the city.

The [Georgian Military Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_Military_Road), which is a part of [European route E117](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_route_E117), starts in Vladikavkaz and it connects the city with the [South Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Caucasus).

[FC Spartak Vladikavkaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Spartak_Vladikavkaz) was an [association football](10568.md) club based in Vladikavkaz, which won the [Russian Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Premier_League) in 1995. The club folded in 2020, and was succeeded by [FC Alania Vladikavkaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Alania_Vladikavkaz_\(2019\)).

Vladikavkaz is home the Wrestling Academy of Aslan Khadartsev - the biggest wrestling academy in the South of Russia.[^cite_note-32] It provides access to a number of facilities including a swimming pool, sauna, gym, personal dietitians, dorm rooms (which include a TV, comfortable beds, wardrobes, en-suite bathroom and showers),[^cite_note-33] for 45 athletes and the main training hall, consisting of six mats- this academy is capable of hosting 250 wrestlers at one time.&#x20;

## Religion



St. George's [Orthodox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox) Cathedral



Mukhtarov Mosque

The city's primary religion is [Eastern Orthodox Christianity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Christianity), which is followed by the majority of Ossetians, Russians and Georgians. The rest of the Ossetian population adheres to the next largest religion, [Sunni Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam), and to [Uatsdin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uatsdin), an Ossetian folk religion, which is followed by 29% of the population nationwide. The remainder follow Protestantism, Armenian Orthodoxy and other beliefs.
