The **Ministry of Defence** of the [Republic of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Latvia) ([Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language): *Latvijas Republikas Aizsardzības ministrija*) is the Latvian [government ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_ministry) in charge of the formation and implementation of [national security](240468.md) and defence policy, and for the overall management and control of related subordinate agencies, such as the [Latvian National Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_National_Armed_Forces). The ministry is headed by the politically appointed **[Minister of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Ministers_of_Defence_of_Latvia)**.

The ministry was established on 22 November 1918 as the **Ministry of Protection** ([Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language): *Apsardzības ministrija*), just four days after the [proclamation of the independence of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proclamation_Day_of_the_Republic_of_Latvia). [Jānis Zālītis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C4%81nis_Z%C4%81l%C4%ABtis?action=edit\&redlink=1), a member of the [People's Council of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Council_of_Latvia), was appointed as minister.[^cite_note-1] After the end of the [Latvian War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_War_of_Independence), in 1922 the ministry was renamed as the **Ministry of War** ([Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language): *Kara ministrija*).&#x20;

After the [occupation of Latvia in 1940](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_occupation_of_Latvia_in_1940) by the Soviet Union, the ministry was formally dissolved on 27 September 1940, and its building taken over by the HQ of the [Baltic Military District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltic_Military_District) of the [Soviet Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Army) until August 1994.[^cite_note-2] On 13 November 1991, the ministry was re-established in its current form as the Ministry of Defence on the basis of the Public Security Department by the [Supreme Council of the Republic of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Council_of_the_Republic_of_Latvia).

## Core tasks

- Strategic analysis, research and development (R\&D)

- The development of long-term policy including future strategic concepts and doctrines

- Perspective and structural planning

- Planning, budgeting and implementation in the medium and short term

- Overall management of agencies’ activities during the budget year

- Operational policy, planning and management at a strategic level

- Exercise policy, planning and management at a strategic level

- Emergency planning, policy and management at a strategic level

- Crisis management

- Development and implementation of security policy, both nationally and internationally

- Development of defence cooperation with allied- and partner countries

- Strategic personnel management

- Information, communication and press relations

- Strategic leadership and management in the field of ICT

- Organisational development

- Preventive security at a strategic level

- Legal questions

- Controller / Internal audit

- Internal administration

## Symbols

As all Latvian government institutions since January 2015, the Ministry of Defence uses the [Large Coat of Arms of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coat_of_arms_of_Latvia) as its logo, sometimes topped with a bar of the respective color of the ministry ([HEX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hex_color) #666633 or olive green in this case).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] A previous symbol, which is still occasionally used by the ministry, consists of a rising sun with three stars in the center, similar to the [cap badge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap_badge) of the [Latvian Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_National_Armed_Forces) adopted during the [Latvian War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_War_of_Independence) and still in use today.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

&#x20;The rising sun symbol is also visible on the top left corner of the personal Standard of the Minister of Defence ([Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language): *Aizsardzības ministra karogs*), adopted on 25 August 1922 and still in use today.[^cite_note-\:0-5][^cite_note-6]

## Agencies

Subordinate institutions of the Ministry of Defence of Latvia are:

- Military Intelligence and Security Service (MIDD)

- [Latvian Geospatial Information Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_Geospatial_Information_Agency) (LGIA)

- State Agency "[Latvian War Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_War_Museum)"

- National Defence Military Facilities and Procurement Centre (VAMOIC)

- Youth Guard and Information Centre

- [Latvian National Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_National_Armed_Forces)

- [National Defence Academy of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Defence_Academy_of_Latvia)
