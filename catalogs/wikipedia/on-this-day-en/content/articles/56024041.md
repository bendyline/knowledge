**First Cabinet of Mateusz Morawiecki** formed the previous government of [Poland](22936.md) between 2017 and 2019, following [Szydło's cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Beata_Szyd%C5%82o). Governing during the 8th legislature of the [Sejm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sejm_of_the_Republic_of_Poland) and the 9th legislature of the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_the_Republic_of_Poland), it was led by Prime Minister [Mateusz Morawiecki](48505487.md).

## Policy

### History Law

In early 2018, both chambers of the Polish parliament (the [Sejm](28167.md) and [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Poland)) adopted an [Amendment to the Act on the Institute of National Remembrance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amendment_to_the_Act_on_the_Institute_of_National_Remembrance) criminalising the ascription to Poles collectively of complicity in [World War II](32927.md) [Jewish-genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust)-related or other [war crimes](48626.md) or [crimes against humanity](61099.md) that had been committed by the [Axis powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axis_powers), and condemning use of the expression, ["Polish death camp"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%22Polish_death_camp%22_controversy).[^cite_note-wapo20180127-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-SmithsonianJan2018-12] The law sparked a crisis in [Israel–Poland relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93Poland_relations).[^cite_note-13]

### Social

In March 2018 a new Polish law took effect, banning nearly all commerce on Sundays, with supermarkets and most other retailers closed Sundays for the first time since liberal shopping laws were introduced in the 1990s. The law had been passed by the [Law and Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_and_Justice_\(Poland\)) party with Morawiecki's support.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] The Bill had the support of [Solidarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solidarity_\(Polish_trade_union\)) which In 2017, backed a proposal to implement [blue laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_laws) to prohibit [Sunday shopping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunday_shopping), a move supported by Polish bishops.[^cite_note-18]
