A **share** (sometimes referred to as [stock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_capital) or [equity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equity_\(finance\))) is a unit of [equity ownership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equity_\(finance\)) in the [capital stock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_capital) of a corporation. It can refer to units of [mutual funds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutual_fund), [limited partnerships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_partnership), and [real estate investment trusts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_estate_investment_trust).[^cite_note-invest-1] [Share capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_capital) refers to all of the shares of an enterprise. The owner of shares in a company is a [shareholder](146081.md) (or stockholder) of the [corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporation).[^cite_note-2] A share expresses the ownership relationship between the company and the shareholder.[^cite_note-invest-1] The denominated value of a share is its face value, and the total of the face value of issued shares represent the [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_capital) of a company,[^cite_note-3] which may not reflect the market value of those shares.

The income received from the ownership of shares is a [dividend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dividend). There are different types of shares such as equity shares, [preference shares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preference_share), deferred shares, redeemable shares, bonus shares, right shares, and employee stock option plan shares.

## Terminology

- [Stock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_capital)

- [Equity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equity_\(finance\))

- [Shares outstanding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shares_outstanding) are shares that are authorized by the government, issued by the company, and held by third parties.

- [Treasury shares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasury_share) are shares that are authorized, issued, and held by the company itself.

- [Issued shares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Issued_shares) are the sum of shares outstanding and treasury shares.

- [Shares authorized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shares_authorized) include both issued (by the board of directors or shareholders) and unissued but authorized by the company's [constitutional documents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_documents).

## Valuation

Further information: [Stock valuation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_valuation)

Shares are valued according to the various principles in different [markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_\(economics\)), but a basic premise is that a share is worth the price at which a transaction would be likely to occur were the shares to be sold. The [liquidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquidity) of markets is a major consideration as to whether a share is able to be sold at any given time. An actual sale transaction of shares between buyer and seller is usually considered to provide the best prima facie market indicator as to the "true value" of shares at that particular time. A [minority discount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minority_discount) is usually applied when valuing a minority shareholding (less than 50%), where ownership of the shares offers limited control over the business if this is held by a majority shareholder.

## Tax treatment

Tax treatment of dividends varies between tax jurisdictions. For instance, in [India](14533.md), dividends are [tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tax) free in the hands of the shareholder up to INR 1 million, but the company paying the dividend has to pay dividend distribution tax at 12.5%. There is also the concept of a deemed [dividend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dividend), which is not tax free. Further, Indian tax laws include provisions to stop [dividend stripping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dividend_stripping).[^cite_note-4]

## Share certificates

Historically, investors were given [share certificates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_certificate) as evidence of their ownership of shares. In modern times, certificates are not always given and ownership may be recorded electronically by a system such as [CREST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CREST_\(securities_depository\)) or [DTCC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depository_Trust_&_Clearing_Corporation), a [central securities depository](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_securities_depository).

## Tracing shares

As of May 2022, the [United States Supreme Court](31737.md) was considering the case of *[Slack Technologies, LLC v. Pirani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slack_Technologies,_LLC_v._Pirani)*, No. 22-200, 598 U.S. 759 (2023), with regard to whether [Sections 11 and 12(a)(2)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Securities_Act_of_1933#Civil_liability;_Sections_11_and_12) of the [Securities Act of 1933](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Securities_Act_of_1933) require plaintiffs to plead and prove that they acquired shares of stock registered under and traceable to the [registration statement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Registration_statement) they claim was misleading.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]
