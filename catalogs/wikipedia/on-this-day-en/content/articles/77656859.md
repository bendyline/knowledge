The **National Nutrition Agency** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Badan Gizi Nasional*; BGN), is a cabinet-level agency formed by [President](10011631.md) [Joko Widodo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joko_Widodo) to initiate [Prabowo Subianto](2829084.md)'s [free lunch for students](82783358.md) and provide nutritional adequacy for four vulnerable groups: students (of elementary to senior high school, special education, and [Islamic boarding school](4978770.md)), toddlers, pregnant women, and lactating women.[^cite_note-3] 82.9 million people of total Indonesian population will be targeted as beneficiary of this program.[^cite_note-\:2-4]

This agency is realization of Prabowo's campaign for providing [free, nutritious food](2829084.md#Free_school_meals).[^cite_note-\:0-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-\:1-7]

## History

During his [campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prabowo_Subianto_2024_presidential_campaign), Prabowo promised that he will provide lunches for students.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

As the foundation of the agency, Deputy II (Food and Nutritional Insecurity), a deputy of the [National Food Agency](77135634.md), was removed from the agency and transformed into the new agency.[^cite_note-8] Consequently, the National Nutrition Agency shared the same office with the National Food Agency during its formation period.[^cite_note-9]

On 15 August 2024, Joko Widodo signed the formation of two of Prabowo's future cabinet-level agencies. The National Nutrition Agency is one of these agencies, established to facilitate and fulfil Prabowo's mission.[^cite_note-10]

Although it was created to support Prabowo's free lunch programme, Joko Widodo expects that in the future, the agency will not only serve as a facilitator of the free lunch programme but also [promote and cultivate a healthy lifestyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_promotion) in Indonesia.[^cite_note-\:2-4]

In October 2024, Dadan Hindayana, Head of the National Nutrition Agency, announced that the [Indonesian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_government) plans to allocate up to Rp 800 billion (US \$51.16 million) per day for a free meals programme to feed children across the country.[^cite_note-11]

On 2 June 2026, President Prabowo removed Dadan as chair of BGN and Sony Sonjaya and Lodewyk Pusung as deputies; Dadan was succeeded by Nanik S Deyang.[^cite_note-Kompas_Cyber_Media-12] The following day, on 3 June, Dadan, Sony, and Lodewyk were arrested by the [Attorney General's Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney_General's_Office_of_Indonesia). The BGN headquarters was also searched by the AGO on the same day.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] Subsequently, the deputy head of the agency, [Nanik Sudaryati Deyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanik_Sudaryati_Deyang) was appointed as the new agency head.[^cite_note-bgn-15] However, she left the agency after 45 days due to health issues.[^cite_note-16] Deputy Agriculture Minister Sudaryono was appointed on 22 July 2026 as the new agency head.[^cite_note-17]

## Funding

IDR 71 trillion (US\$4.542 billion) was allocated in 2025 when the National Nutrition Agency was fully operational to implement the free meals programme.[^cite_note-\:1-7] [Sri Mulyani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Mulyani), then-Minister of Finance, revealed that the IDR 71 trillion allocated to fund the programme has already been set aside and will not divert funds previously allocated for educational matters.[^cite_note-18] For 2026, the budget was increased to IDR 335 trillion (US\$21 billion), making it the largest budget allocation among all Indonesian ministries and agencies.[^cite_note-19]

## Controversies

The MBG programme has faced criticism from civil society for alleged budget mismanagement, with only a small fraction of the state budget (APBN) allocation spent on food. Critics highlight excessive spending on SPPG kitchen staff salaries and "supporting attributes"—such as socks and electric motorcycles—procured at prices significantly above market value, leading to allegations of [corruption](33094689.md).[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

Another controversy revolves around the procurement of 21,801 e-motorcycles from PT Adlas Sarana Elektrik and PT Yasa Artha Trimanunggal (Yasa Group). BGN purchased the "Emmo" brand bikes for up to Rp 50 million per unit, even though nearly identical models were listed on [Alibaba.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alibaba.com) for approximately Rp 10 million.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]
