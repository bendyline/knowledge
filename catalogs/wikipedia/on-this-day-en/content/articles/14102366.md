**Filingué** is a town in southwestern [Niger](21373.md) and is the capital city of [Filingué Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filingue_Department) and [Kourfeye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kourfeye)[^cite_note-1]. It is situated on the eastern bank of the [Dallol Bosso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallol_Bosso) valley, some 183 km northeast of Niger's capital city [Niamey](57061.md).

[Abdourahamane Tchiani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdourahamane_Tchiani), a president of Niger, hails from Filingué.[^cite_note-2]

As pools of stagnant water remain in this valley in the dry season there's a lot of [mudbrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mudbrick) making.
