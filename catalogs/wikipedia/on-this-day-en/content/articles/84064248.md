The **2026 Weltklasse Zürich** was the 65th edition of the [annual outdoor track and field meeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weltklasse_Z%C3%BCrich) held in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z%C3%BCrich). Held on 26 and 27 August at [Letzigrund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letzigrund), it was the fourteenth leg of the [2026 Diamond League](81417246.md) – the highest level international track and field circuit.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]
