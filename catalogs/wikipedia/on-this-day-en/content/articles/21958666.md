The **minister of agriculture** is a [minister](739237.md) in the [Cabinet of South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_South_Africa) since 2024, when the corresponding department was split from the [Department of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Agriculture,_Forestry_and_Fisheries_\(South_Africa\)). It holds the political executive responsibility for the [Department of Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Agriculture_\(South_Africa\)), and for the [Agricultural Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_Research_Council_\(South_Africa\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), the National Agricultural Marketing Council, Onderstepoort Biological Products, the Perishable Products Export Control Board, and Ncera Farms.[^cite_note-1]

The agriculture portfolio was created in the 2024 cabinet reorganisation after the re-election of [President](321700.md) [Cyril Ramaphosa](1089194.md) as part of the government of national unity. The current minister of agriculture is [Willie Aucamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Aucamp), who was appointed on 30 June 2026.[^cite_note-2]
