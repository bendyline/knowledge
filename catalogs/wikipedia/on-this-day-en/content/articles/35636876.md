The **Ministry of National Defence** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 국방성; formerly **Ministry of the People's Armed Forces** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 인민무력성; [Hanja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanja): 人民武力省)) is the government agency under the [State Affairs Commission](50991731.md) tasked with general administrative and logistical coordination of the [Korean People's Army](21263.md) (KPA). The current Minister of National Defence is General [Kim Song-gi](84103926.md).

## History

Until December 1972, the Minister of the People's Armed Forces was called the Minister of National Defence (민족보위상).[^cite_note-nkne_Theu-1] It then changed to the Ministry of the People's Armed Forces.

Prior to 1992, it was under the direct control of [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-Chief_of_North_Korea) and [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_leaders_of_North_Korea#%22Eternal_President%22), with guidance from the [National Defence Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Defense_Commission_\(North_Korea\)) and the [Workers' Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers'_Party_of_Korea) [Central Military Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Military_Commission_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea). The [1992 amendment](4872690.md#1992_amendment) to the [Constitution of North Korea](4872690.md) shifts its control to the National Defence Commission. The [2016 amendment](4872690.md#2016_amendment) shifted its control to the [State Affairs Commission](50991731.md). It was renamed to the Ministry of National Defence in October 2020.[^cite_note-2]

## Duties

The Ministry of Defence handles the KPA's logistical, administrative, and diplomatic functions and components.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-\:0-5] The ministry also coordinates relations with foreign militaries.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-\:0-5]

Until the late 2000s, the [General Staff Department (GSD)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff_Department_of_the_Korean_People's_Army)--which is responsible for the daily operational planning and management of the KPA's ground, naval, and air commands--and the [General Political Bureau (GPB)](44059604.md) fell under the ministry. During that time, the ministry conveyed the orders and guidance of the [KPA Commander-in-Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-Chief_of_North_Korea)[^cite_note-\:0-5] and--through the GSD and GPB--developed strategy, conducted [education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_education) and training, and completed certain signals intelligence tasks.[^cite_note-7] However, the ministry's role is currently limited to military administration, logistics, and diplomacy.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-\:0-5]

The ministry reportedly has or had departments tasked with regulating [Government-owned corporations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government-owned_corporation) related to the [defence industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_industry_of_North_Korea) and other [foreign currency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_currency) earning ventures.[^cite_note-9]

## Departments

The Ministry of National Defence contains the following departments:[^cite_note-10]

- [General Political Bureau](44059604.md)

- [General Staff Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff_Department_of_the_Korean_People's_Army)

- [Security Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Security_Command_of_the_Korean_People's_Army)

Both the Director of the General Political Bureau and Chief of the General Staff have more power than the Minister.
