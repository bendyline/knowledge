**Cambodia–South Korea relations** are foreign relations between [Cambodia](334751.md) and [South Korea](27019.md). South Korea has an embassy in [Phnom Penh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phnom_Penh) and Cambodia has an embassy in [Seoul](19159283.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## Free trade agreements

See also: [South Korea free trade agreements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea_free_trade_agreements)

The two countries agreed to hold a joint FTA committee in the first half of 2024.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-4]

## Cooperation

South Korea has played an important role in promoting Cambodia's socioeconomic development, especially in helping the country develop major industries such as infrastructure, agriculture, healthcare, and education.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

In Phnom Penh in March 2024, South Korea's [Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Trade,_Industry_and_Energy) and Cambodia's [Ministry of Commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Commerce_\(Cambodia\)) discussed ways to strengthen bilateral cooperation in areas such as trade, infrastructure and carbon neutrality.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

> "Based on the young human resources, Cambodia is a nation with high growth potential, posting annual economic growth of 7 percent over the past 20 years......We hope that Cambodia emerges as the key economic partner in the ASEAN bloc through the bilateral FTA."

— Cheong In-kyo, Trade Minister of South Korea

In Cambodia's clothing and textiles sector, South Korean companies are investing heavily to build factories and create jobs for locals.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

## Summits

South Korean President [Yoon Suk Yeol](62877952.md) and Cambodian Prime Minister [Hun Manet](42929019.md) agreed at bilateral talks to further increase bilateral trade, which hit a record \$1.05 billion in 2022.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Disputes

[Cambodian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambodian_government) has officially requested South Korea to deport [Buth Vichhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buth_Vichhai?action=edit\&redlink=1) who has been living in South Korea since 2016, participating as a pro-democracy activist. He is an [Influencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influencer) and a [Rapper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapper) and makes various songs and videos on [Hun Sen](393779.md) who has been ruling Cambodia under the system of [Dictatorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dictatorship).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] His actions and protests includes criticisms towards [Hun Sen](393779.md) and [Hun Manet](42929019.md).[^cite_note-11]

On 10 October 2025, the Cambodian envoy, [Khuon Phon Rattanak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuon_Phon_Rattanak?action=edit\&redlink=1) was summoned for the death of a South Korean man caused by [Scam centers in Cambodia](80461265.md) by the Korean minister of foreign affairs, [Cho Hyun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cho_Hyun_\(diplomat\)).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]
