**Sinja** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): سنجة) is a town located in the [Sennar State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sennar_\(state\)) of [Sudan](27421.md) at an elevation of 439 meters (1,440 feet) above sea level. The town is situated on the west bank of the [Blue Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Nile) at a distance of 360 kilometers (220 miles) to the southeast of the capital, [Khartoum](17320.md), and 60 kilometers from the city of [Sennar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sennar). Its population was recorded to be 259,000 in 2002.[^cite_note-3]

Sinja is the capital of Sennar State and is characterized by the diversity of its natural environment, [habitat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habitat), water resources, and livestock. It remains publicly unclear whether Sinja or [Sennar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sennar) is the state capital. A large veterinary research station has been founded in the city. Its economic activities vary from [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) to [pastoralism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pastoralism). Important crops are [sorghum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorghum), [gum arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gum_arabic), and fruits such as [guava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guava), banana and [mango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango).

On 29 June 2024, the city was [captured](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Battle_of_Sinja?action=edit\&redlink=1) by the [RSF](43053874.md) during the [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md).[^cite_note-4] In November 2024 the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) [retook the city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Sinja?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-XinhuaSinnar-5]

## Archaeological discovery



Replica of the Sinja Skull, [National Museum of Natural History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Museum_of_Natural_History)

Sinja is notable for the archaeological discovery of an old human fossil, the '[Sinja Skull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinja_Skull?action=edit\&redlink=1)' which was discovered in Sinja in 1924 by the British governor of Blue Nile Province. The skull is about 160,000 years old and belongs to the [Stone Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_Age), [Pleistocene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleistocene) Age. It coincided with the [Neanderthals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neanderthal) and the [Peking Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_Man). Currently the skull is in the collection of the [Natural History Museum, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_History_Museum,_London).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-NHMSinga-8]

## Connections

Sinja is served by several bank branches, a hospital, and a hotel. There is no airport in the town; the nearest airport is [Kosti](6671707.md) airport, at a distance of 129 kilometers (80 miles). A network of track roads and railways connects the city with its major neighboring towns and cities.[^cite_note-9]

In 2011, the [Library of Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_of_Congress) still presented maps indicating that [Sennar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sennar) was the capital of [Sennar State](767672.md),[^cite_note-10] but more recent works indicate that the capital is at Sinja.[^cite_note-11]
