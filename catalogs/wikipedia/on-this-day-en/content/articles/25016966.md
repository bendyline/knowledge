**Shahr-e Naw** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): شهر نو; [Dari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dari_language): شهرنو) is one of the affluent neighborhoods in [Kabul](16826.md), [Afghanistan](737.md).[^cite_note-1] It is a [downtown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown) commercial area housing most of the bigger buildings such as office centers, shopping centers, embassies, supermarkets, hotels, popular restaurants, banks, hospitals, mosques, and luxury apartments.[^cite_note-2] To its west lies [Karte Sakhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karte_Sakhi), and to its east is the affluent [Wazir Akbar Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wazir_Akbar_Khan,_Kabul) neighborhood.[^cite_note-3] Its name in a general sense means *[New Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planned_community)*, which is taken as it was first built in the early 20th century as a 'new' urban extension north of the historic city of Kabul.[^cite_note-4]

## Economy

[Ariana Afghan Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Afghan_Airlines)'s corporate headquarters are in Shahr-e Naw.[^cite_note-5] [Kam Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kam_Air) has a ticketing office in the Kabul Business Centre in Shāre Naw.[^cite_note-6] At one time the facility housed the airline's head office.[^cite_note-Contactus2008-7] [Safi Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safi_Airways) also had its head office in Shahr-e Naw.[^cite_note-8]

## Government and infrastructure



Former [presidential palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arg,_Kabul) of Afghanistan is in Shahr-e Naw.

[Arg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arg,_Kabul), which is the former presidential palace of Afghanistan, is located in the southeastern part of Shahr-e Naw. The [Shahr-e Naw Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahr-e_Naw_Park) is located in the northwestern part. The [Kabul Serena Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabul_Serena_Hotel), [Jamhuriat Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamhuriat_Hospital) and [Abdul Rahman Mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Rahman_Mosque) are also in Shahr-e Naw.

## Education

The [Lycee Esteqlal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycee_Esteqlal), a [Franco](5843419.md)-[Afghan](737.md) school and one the most prestigious in the country, is located in Shahr-e Naw. The [Deutsche Schule Kabul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Schule_Kabul?action=edit\&redlink=1) was also there.[^cite_note-9]

## Postal code

The area's government [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) is 1003.[^cite_note-10]
