# Anti-Ballistic Missile Coalition

The **Anti-Ballistic Missile Coalition** is a multinational coalition established on 13 July 2026 by [Denmark](76972.md), [France](5843419.md), [Germany](11867.md), [Italy](14532.md), the [Netherlands](21148.md), [Norway](21241.md), [Spain](26667.md), [Sweden](5058739.md), [Ukraine](31750.md), and the [United Kingdom](31717.md) (later joined by Poland) to develop an integrated anti-ballistic missile defense capability for Europe.[^cite_note-declaration-1][^cite_note-reuters-2]

## Background

On the sidelines of a July 2026 meeting of the [Coalition of the willing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalition_of_the_willing_\(Russo-Ukrainian_war\)) in [Paris](22989.md), a joint statement from 10 founding nations established the initiative to strengthen Europe's air defense, in the wake of growing threats of ballistic missiles being on [Europe](9239.md).[^cite_note-euronews-3]

## Formation

The statement said the project would not replace existing systems, but would complement existing [ballistic missile defense systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparison_of_anti-ballistic_missile_systems) in Europe.[^cite_note-reuters-2] The leaders of the coalition stated the project would be "purely defensive" in nature.[^cite_note-dw-4][^cite_note-declaration-1]

The scope of involvement of the ten nations includes defense industry, research capacity and operational experience.[^cite_note-defenceindustry-5]

## Freyja air defense project

The coalition of countries announced the development of the pan-European [Freyja air defense system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freyja_\(missile_system\)), architected by [Fire Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_Point_\(Ukrainian_firm\)) to provide sovereign, modular, and available air defence.[^cite_note-6] Ukrainian president [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) said the initiative would focus on integrating the Ukrainian-made interceptor–intended to be 80% cheaper per interdiction than the American [MIM-104 Patriot](52024.md)–with European radars and control systems.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-eurom20260709-8] The first prototype is desired to be operational by mid-2027.[^cite_note-9]
