The **1982 European Cup final** was played on 26 May 1982 at the end of the [1981–82 European Cup season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981%E2%80%9382_European_Cup_season). [Football League First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980%E2%80%9381_Football_League_First_Division) winners [Aston Villa](42173.md) defeated [Bundesliga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980%E2%80%9381_Fu%C3%9Fball-Bundesliga) winners [Bayern Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Bayern_Munich) 1–0 at [De Kuip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Kuip) in [Rotterdam](26049.md), Netherlands, to win their first [European Cup](44220.md); this continued the streak of English teams winning the competition in six straight seasons. This was the only time Villa won a European final until [44 years later](76987231.md).

## Match

### Summary





Two moments of the match, (left): Des Bremner, Dieter Hoeneß, Paul Breitner, and Kenny Swain in action; (right): Players of Aston Villa celebrating their victory

After 10 minutes, Aston Villa goalkeeper [Jimmy Rimmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Rimmer) suffered a repeat of a recurring shoulder injury. His replacement, [Nigel Spink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigel_Spink), subsequently made his second first team appearance for the club.[^cite_note-1] His performance in helping prevent Bayern from scoring throughout the match was highly praised, and is seen by many as the making of a player who would be Villa's first choice goalkeeper for the following 10 seasons.[^cite_note-2]

Bayern did find the net with three minutes of play remaining, but the goal was disallowed for offside. Villa also got the ball in the net for a second time a few seconds before the end of the match but this goal was also disallowed.

[Brian Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Moore_\(commentator\))'s commentary on the winning goal is displayed on a giant banner across the Doug Ellis Stand of [Villa Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villa_Park):

> [Shaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Shaw_\(footballer,_born_1961\)), [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Williams_\(footballer,_born_1960\)) prepared to venture down the left. There's a good ball in for [Tony Morley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Morley). Oh, it must be and it is! It's [Peter Withe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Withe).

As defending European champions, Villa were invited into the European Cup, [European Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Super_Cup) and the [Intercontinental Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercontinental_Cup_\(1960%E2%80%932004\)) for the following season. Their defence of the European Cup ended in a quarter-final defeat to [Juventus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juventus_FC). They beat [Barcelona](68187.md) 3–1 on aggregate to win the Super Cup, but lost 2–0 to Uruguayan club [Peñarol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pe%C3%B1arol) for the Intercontinental Cup in [Tokyo](30057.md), Japan.

After the game, a number of players swapped shirts; Peter Withe and Nigel Spink later received their shirts back.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] As of 2024, captain [Dennis Mortimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Mortimer) was still looking for his shirt.[^cite_note-5]
