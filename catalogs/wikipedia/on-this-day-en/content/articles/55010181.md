The **Ministry of Transport** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة النقل العراقية) is a [central government](11752991.md) [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) of Iraq tasked with managing the affairs of [transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport). It is currently headed by Wahab al-Hasani.
