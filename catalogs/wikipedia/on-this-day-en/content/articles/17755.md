In [Luang Prabang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luang_Prabang), a woman at the time of a Hmong Meeting Festival.

Some specialists are in agreement as to the ethnolinguistic classification of the ethnic groups of Laos.[^cite_note-Indigenous_Peoples_Development_Planning_Document-10] For the purposes of the 1995 census, the government of Laos recognised 149 ethnic groups within 47 main ethnicities.[^cite_note-Indigenous_Peoples_Development_Planning_Document-10] whereas the [Lao Front for National Construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lao_Front_for_National_Construction) (LFNC) revised the list to include 49 ethnicities consisting of over 160 ethnic groups.[^cite_note-Indigenous_Peoples_Development_Planning_Document-10]

The term *ethnic minorities* is used by some to classify the non-Lao ethnic groups, while the term *indigenous peoples* is not used by the LPDR.[^cite_note-Indigenous_Peoples_Development_Planning_Document-10] These 160 ethnic groups speak a total of 82 living languages.[^cite_note-Ethnologue-11]
