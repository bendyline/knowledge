**Timur Mykhailovych Mindich**[^cite_note-1] ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Тімур Михайлович Miндіч; born 19 September 1979) is a Ukrainian-Israeli [entrepreneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneur), [film producer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_producer) and the co-owner of [Kvartal 95 Studio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kvartal_95_Studio).

He is also accused of being the "mastermind behind a \$100 million embezzlement scheme involving top officials and Ukraine's state nuclear power company."[^cite_note-ToI-2]

## Early life

Mindich was born on 19 September 1979 in Dnipropetrovsk (now [Dnipro](989795.md)[^cite_note-3]), [Ukrainian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic) into a family of [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Ukraine) entrepreneurs. His father died in Israel in 2006.[^cite_note-4]

## Career

Mindich has produced several [films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Films), including *Shchurolyov* (*Rat Catcher*) and *Ukus volchytsi* (*Bite of the She-Wolf*).[^cite_note-5]

He is directly associated with the creative association [Kvartal 95 Studio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kvartal_95_Studio) and is also linked to Ukrainian [oligarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_oligarchs) [Ihor Kolomoyskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ihor_Kolomoyskyi), being a co-owner of the television channel Kvartal TV.[^cite_note-6]

According to Kolomoyskyi, Mindich is the former fiancé of his daughter and his "business partner in some minor projects, such as development ones".[^cite_note-7] Mindich is a business partner of [Serhiy Shefir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serhiy_Shefir) (former first assistant to President [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md)) in *Kvartal 95* LLC and the Cypriot company *Green Family Ltd*. Green Family Ltd owns Kvartal 95's film business in [Russia](25391.md).[^cite_note-8]

## Personal life

He is married to Kateryna Verber, daughter of Alla Verber (d. 2019), who was the fashion director of Moscow's [TsUM department store](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TsUM_\(Moscow\)) and vice president of the luxury goods company Mercury.[^cite_note-telegraf-9] The couple married in 2010 in [Jerusalem](16043.md), [Israel](9282173.md). They have three children: daughters Michelle and Elizabeth, and a son, Michael.[^cite_note-10]

## Investigations

See also: [Operation Midas](81589122.md)

On 4 June 2025, the [High Anti-Corruption Court of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Anti-Corruption_Court_of_Ukraine) ordered the detention of Mindich's cousin Leonid, suspected of organizing a scheme to misappropriate ₴12.5 million and attempting to seize an additional ₴120 million from [Kharkivoblenergo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkivoblenergo?action=edit\&redlink=1) during the procurement of transformer equipment and measuring devices.

On 10 November 2025, NABU detectives searched the residence of Tymur Mindich.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

### Kinokit

According to an investigation by [Bihus.Info](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bihus.Info?action=edit\&redlink=1), a legal advisor to Mindich's company became the owner of *Kinokit*, which between 2019 and 2025 received over ₴440 million in state contracts.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

### Fire Point

Mindich has also been linked to the company [Fire Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_Point_\(Ukrainian_firm\)), which is under NABU investigation over possible overpricing and supply irregularities in drone procurement for the Ministry of Defense.[^cite_note-16]

MP [Yaroslav Zheleznyak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaroslav_Zheleznyak) reported that Mindich had left Ukraine right before the [NABU](33096574.md) raid on his home on 10 November 2025, and said Mindich would either be in Austria or Israel.[^cite_note-17] According to [Times of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Times_of_Israel), Mindich had fled to Israel in 2025.[^cite_note-ToI-2] According to [Censor.net](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Censor.net?action=edit\&redlink=1), Mindich was hiding in Austria as of 11 November 2025.[^cite_note-novynarnia-18][^cite_note-censor-19]
