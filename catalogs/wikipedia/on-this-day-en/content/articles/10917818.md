The **head of the Chechen Republic** or **head of Chechnya** ([Chechen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chechen_language): Мехкада Нохчийн Республика, romanized: *Mehkada Nohçiyn Respublika*; [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Глава Чеченской Республики, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Russian): *Glava Chechenskoy Respubliki*; formerly **president of the Chechen Republic** or **president of Chechnya** until 5 March 2011) is the highest office within the [political system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Chechnya) of the [Chechen Republic](6095.md), as [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [head of government](37570.md) of Chechnya. The office was instituted in 2003 during the course of the [Second Chechen War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Chechen_War), when the [Russian federal government](25704.md) regained control over the region and after a constitutional referendum approved the current [Constitution of the Chechen Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Chechnya).

## Eligibility

According to the article 66 of the [Constitution of the Chechen Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Chechnya), a citizen of [Russia](25391.md), no younger than thirty years old, may be elected Head. The term is for five years, with no limits on serving multiple terms (prior to the [2007 constitutional referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Chechen_constitutional_referendum) the Head was elected for four years, with a two term limit). The Head is not allowed to be at the same time a deputy of the [Parliament of the Chechen Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_the_Chechen_Republic), or deputy of a representative body of local self-government.
