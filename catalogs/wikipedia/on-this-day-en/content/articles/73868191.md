**Denis Yevgenyevich Kapustin** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Денис Евгеньевич Капустин; born 6 March 1984), also known as **Denis Nikitin** (Денис Никитин) or by his *[nom de guerre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nom_de_guerre)* **White Rex**, is a Russian [neo-Nazi](54361.md) activist and militant. After moving to Ukraine, he founded the [Russian Volunteer Corps](71652852.md) (RVC) in 2022 and has been the group's leader, fighting on the side of Ukraine in the [Russo-Ukrainian war](42085878.md).

## Early life

Kapustin was born on 6 March 1984, and raised in a middle class family in [Moscow](19004.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-BBCDud-2] In 2001, when he was 17, Kapustin and his parents moved to [Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne), Germany,[^cite_note-Dettmer-2024-3] under a program related to [Jewish ancestry](25955086.md).[^cite_note-MeduzaDud-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Kapustin said his family is Russian, while his maternal grandfather, Efim Aronovich Karpmanskiy, was Jewish.[^cite_note-MeduzaDud-4][^cite_note-7] Kapustin attended school in Moscow and in Germany, then studied international business at [University College Maastricht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Maastricht) in the Netherlands but did not graduate.[^cite_note-BBCDud-2][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-Denysiaka-2025-9]

## Football hooliganism and mixed martial arts

Kapustin entered the [football hooliganism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_hooliganism) scene in Germany, where he was a hooligan for his local Cologne team.[^cite_note-Parkin-2018-10] He said later that in 2004, he had been "just a common street gang skinhead, bashing immigrants in the street, setting their cars on fire, like breaking faces, whole program."[^cite_note-Hewson-2023-11]

At age 22, Kapustin returned to Moscow and subsequently joined Yaroslavka, a football hooligan group related to [CSKA Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSKA_Moscow).[^cite_note-BPB-2018-12] He was deeply rooted in the football hooliganism scene and led a small group during the [riots at the 2016 European Football Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2016_riots) in the port of Marseille.[^cite_note-BPB-2018-12] Radicalized by his involvement in German hooliganism, he participated in hooligan fights and attacks on [minority groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minority_group) before shifting focus from street violence towards promoting his views.[^cite_note-Parkin-2018-10] He said that street violence was ineffective because "if we kill one immigrant every day, that's 365 immigrants in a year. But tens of thousands more will come anyway", while promoting his views on social media was more effective as it addressed the "underlying reason".[^cite_note-Parkin-2018-10]

After identifying a demand for far-right clothing in Russia and re-selling clothes by [Thor Steinar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thor_Steinar), Kapustin launched his own clothing brand, White Rex, which featured violent, [white nationalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_nationalist), and [xenophobic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xenophobic) elements.[^cite_note-René_Nissen-13][^cite_note-17] He wanted the brand to be a [National Socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Socialist) [complete outfitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lifestyle_brand).[^cite_note-BPB-2018-12] Under the same label, Kapustin organised and facilitated meet-ups of radical right groups in Europe and across Russia through [mixed martial arts](228344.md) events.[^cite_note-BPB-2018-12] He also offered martial arts seminars and weapon training from Wales to Switzerland, and maintained good contacts in the fan scenes of [Sparta Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparta_Prague) and [Legia Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legia_Warsaw). He appeared as a fighter and speaker at the "*Kampf der Nibelungen*" (Battle of the Nibelungs) event.[^cite_note-BPB-2018-12] In Switzerland, Kapustin provided combat training to members of the far-right [Swiss Nationalist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Nationalist_Party) (PNOS).[^cite_note-SRF-2023-16] He also gave training to members of [National Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Action_\(UK\)), a British neo-Nazi group banned by the U.K. government.[^cite_note-New_Republic-2023-14] In 2013, he spoke at the Iona right-wing forum held in London, and in 2014, he taught martial arts to [British nationalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_nationalists) at the Sigurd Cultural Camp.[^cite_note-times2026-18]

In 2019, Germany revoked Kapustin's residence permit due to his hooliganism-related activities,[^cite_note-Pikulicka-Wilczewska-2023-15][^cite_note-19] and issued him an entry ban into Europe's [Schengen Area](16565139.md) for "efforts against the liberal democratic constitution" and his significant contribution to professionalizing the right-wing extremist martial arts scene.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-René_Nissen-13]

## Ukraine and Russian Volunteer Corps

Kapustin was in Kyiv for [Euromaidan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euromaidan) in 2014,[^cite_note-Denysiaka-2025-9] and moved to Ukraine in 2017.[^cite_note-Pikulicka-Wilczewska-2023-15] He worked with the [Azov movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azov_Brigade#Azov_movement) in Ukraine.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]<sup>: 93, 136 </sup>[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] For example, he travelled with [National Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Corps) international secretary [Olena Semeniaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olena_Semeniaka?action=edit\&redlink=1) to participate in a far-right conference in Warsaw in November 2017, and organised mixed martial arts events at Azov's Reconquista Club.[^cite_note-René_Nissen-13][^cite_note-26]

In August 2022, Kapustin founded the Russian Volunteer Corps (RVC), a unit composed of Russians fighting against Russia on the side of Ukraine.[^cite_note-27] He was awarded the [Order of Bohdan Khmelnytsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Bohdan_Khmelnytsky) III class in 2026.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

According to Ukrainian war veteran [Viktor Pylypenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Pylypenko_\(military\)) and activist [Diana Berg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diana_Berg), Kapustin attempted to tear away an [LGBT flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT_flag) carried by Pylypenko at the 2025 funeral of artist and military serviceman [Davyd Chychkan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davyd_Chychkan), which resulted in a fight stopped by both being [pepper sprayed](157302.md).[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32]

### 2023 incursions into Russia

Further information: [2023 Bryansk Oblast raid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Bryansk_Oblast_raid) and [2023 Belgorod Oblast incursions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Belgorod_Oblast_incursions)



Russian Volunteer Corps members during a press conference on 24 May 2023; Kapustin in the front in a black uniform

In March 2023, Kapustin and several other RVC members [performed an incursion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Bryansk_Oblast_raid) into Russia's [Bryansk Oblast](476473.md).[^cite_note-33] On 22 May, Kapustin and the RVC were involved in the [Belgorod Oblast attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Belgorod_Oblast_incursions), a cross-border raid into Russia's [Belgorod Oblast](478857.md).[^cite_note-34] On 24 May, he said the group had left Russian territory after 24 hours, and that they were satisfied with the raid at a press conference given on Ukrainian territory.[^cite_note-35]

Following the March incursion, Kapustin was added to [Rosfinmonitoring's list of terrorists and extremists](57212865.md). Russia's [Federal Security Service](385990.md) said it had foiled an attempt to assassinate [Konstantin Malofeev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konstantin_Malofeev) organized by Kapustin, and stated the RVC was involved in an attack on an oil and gas plant in [Volgograd Oblast](478748.md) in 2022.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37] Russia's 2nd Western District Military Court subsequently sentenced Kapustin [in absentia](582826.md) to life imprisonment and also convicted him of [treason](31292.md) and terrorist activities.[^cite_note-38]

### Staged death

On 27 December 2025, the RVC reported that Kapustin had been killed in a drone strike while fighting for Ukraine in its [Zaporizhzhia Oblast](494936.md) and vowed to avenge his death.[^cite_note-Denysiaka-2025-9][^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40] However, the following week, the [Main Directorate of Intelligence of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_Directorate_of_Intelligence_of_Ukraine) (HUR) published a video of Kapustin and stated he was still alive. They said that Russian special services had ordered Kapustin's death, but the HUR had foiled the killing through a special operation lasting more than a month. The false reports of death were part of the special operation. The HUR reported having identified the individuals behind the assassination attempt and obtained half a million US dollars paid for the task.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43]

## Political views

The Antifascist Europe monitoring project described Kapustin as a [neo-Nazi](54361.md) and [white supremacist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_supremacist).[^cite_note-Pikulicka-Wilczewska-2023-15] Pavel Klymenko, a Ukrainian researcher who studies football hooligans, described Kapustin in 2018 as holding "outright neo-Nazi ideology" such as "preaching racial war and the need to 'reconquer' the 'living space' of the white man in Europe."[^cite_note-Winston-2018-44] Kapustin has rejected such characterizations and said "you'll never find me waving a flag with a [swastika](45943.md), you'll never find me raising my hand in a [Hitler sign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitler_salute)."[^cite_note-Pikulicka-Wilczewska-2023-15][^cite_note-Al_Jazeera-2023-45] In a 2023 report, *[PBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PBS)* found a clip of Kapustin from a 2013 mixed martial arts event where he appeared to mirror Nazi salutes given by the audience.[^cite_note-Hewson-2023-11] Kapustin said to *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* in 2023 that although he believes "genocide and gas chambers are bad, regardless of who does it", he admires the culture, style and military of [Nazi Germany](21212.md).[^cite_note-Walker-2023-46] *[The Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times)* wrote that Kapustin had been inspired by the ideology of [Corneliu Codreanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corneliu_Codreanu).[^cite_note-times2026-18]

Kapustin has said he believes in the [Great Replacement conspiracy theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Replacement_conspiracy_theory), that "European culture is definitely endangered", and that he opposes [multiculturalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiculturalism) as well as "Islamisation", "[cultural Marxism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_Marxism)", and "[LGBTQ](78174325.md) propaganda".[^cite_note-Hewson-2023-11][^cite_note-Walker-2023-46] He has called himself a nationalist who believes that Russia belongs to ethnic Russians,[^cite_note-Pikulicka-Wilczewska-2023-15] and said his views were conservative,[^cite_note-Dettmer-2024-3] traditionalist, and right-wing.[^cite_note-Al_Jazeera-2023-45] In 2018, *[ProPublica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ProPublica)* quoted journalist Karim Zidan saying that Kapustin had "repackaged older white supremacist ideas for a new generation, rebranding them as 'nationalist concepts'".[^cite_note-Winston-2018-44]

In 2024, Kapustin said the right-wing movement he belongs to was no longer about viewing immigrants as the enemy, but rather about opposing the government of Russian President [Vladimir Putin](32817.md),[^cite_note-Dettmer-2024-3][^cite_note-Walker-2023-46] which he considers multiculturalist.[^cite_note-Hewson-2023-11] He rejected the adulation of Putin as a white nationalist icon outside Russia, arguing that Moscow has the biggest Muslim population on the European continent.[^cite_note-Hewson-2023-11] Regarding [antisemitism](1078.md), Kapustin has said he does not understand why his American counterparts who "seem to share" his ideology have an "obsession" with Jews and believe "Jews control everything".[^cite_note-Hewson-2023-11]

Kapustin has said that the Russian Volunteer Corps has ideological lectures in addition to physical and military training, as he believes that "ideology is something that fortifies a unit".[^cite_note-Dettmer-2024-3] He said he would not accept a Black, homosexual, or transgender person into the RVC, because they "would not feel comfortable" around each other.[^cite_note-Dettmer-2024-3] In a separate interview, Kapustin said the RVC includes former supporters of [Alexei Navalny](29174356.md), one of whom has more [liberal political views](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberalism) than those of others in the unit.[^cite_note-47]

## Influence

Kapustin has been described as a key figure of the European far-right political activists and hooligans.[^cite_note-BPB-2018-12] Named the "chief architect" of European far-right combat sports, he was the "definitive head" of the scene's tournament network from 2013 to 2019.[^cite_note-René_Nissen-13] According to *[Mother Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_Jones_\(magazine\))*, Kapustin's White Rex has inspired groups like the American [Rise Above Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rise_Above_Movement).[^cite_note-48] Its leader, Robert Rundo, has been reported to have a White Rex tattoo and co-hosted a [podcast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podcast) with Kapustin.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-Winston-2018-44][^cite_note-50] The [interior ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior_ministry) of [Herbert Reul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Reul) in [North Rhine-Westphalia](38414.md), Germany, called Kapustin "one of the most influential neo-Nazi activists".[^cite_note-51]
