**Yohmor** ([Levantine Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levantine_Arabic_language): يحمر, romanized: *Yoḥmor*) is a municipality in the [Nabatieh Governorate](4981449.md) of [Lebanon](17771.md). It is located about 8 km from the town of [Nabatieh](43794798.md). It lies above the bend of the [Litani River](1920925.md), north of the lower Litani and west of the upper Litani. [Beaufort Castle](6853436.md) is located on the east side of the village.&#x20;

## History

Yohmor has been proposed as the place of origin of Jesus of Yohmor, whose name appears in a [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language) inscription from the [late antique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Late_antiquity) [Jewish necropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_She'arim_necropolis) at [Beit She'arim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_She'arim_\(Roman-era_Jewish_village\)), although [Yohmor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yohmor,_Beqaa) in the [Beqaa Valley](2162385.md) has also been proposed as an alternative identification.[^cite_note-1]

In the 1596 [tax records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daftar), it was named as a village, *Yuhmur*, in the [Ottoman](22278.md) *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya)* (subdistrict) of [Sagif](6853436.md) under the *[liwa'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liwa_\(Arabic\))* (district) of [Safad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safad), with a population of 42 households and 5 bachelors, all Muslim. The villagers paid a fixed tax-rate of 25 % on agricultural products, such as [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), olive trees, goats and beehives, in addition to "occasional revenues" and a press for [olive oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_oil) or [grape syrup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grape_syrup); a total of 4,800 *[akçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e)*.[^cite_note-Hutteroth-2][^cite_note-3]

In 2024 till 2026, the village was bombed with [white phosphorus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_phosphorus_munition) by the [Israel Defense Force](39237.md).[^cite_note-4]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.59% of registered voters in Yohmor. 98.86% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-5]

The village has a population of about 2,000, although the population swells to a much higher number in the summer. Prime agricultural crops include olives and tobacco.
