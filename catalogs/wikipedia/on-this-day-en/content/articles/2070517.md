**Marantao**, officially the **Municipality of Marantao** ([Maranao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maranao_language): *Inged a Marantao*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Marantao*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Lanao del Sur](297370.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 46,233 people.[^cite_note-5]

Marantao (Merantau) is a Malay word that means "to go on adventure, travel or hunting or even on a war expedition".

## Culture

### Kawayan Torogan

The [Kawayan Torogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawayan_Torogan) is the oldest known [torogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torogan) in the Philippines. It is a traditional Maranao house built for royal Maranao families. There was once a campaign where the people wanted the municipalities of Marantao and Tugaya to pass an ordinance where the architectural scheme of the two towns will follow only the totogan style, in effect, preserve the Maranao people's most royal architectural style and lead to the first two towns with a planned town landscape under an indigenous Maranao architecture. However, the campaign led to deaf ears due to little funding available.
