**Hossein Taeb** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): حسین طائب) (born 1963) is an Iranian [Shia](26961.md) cleric and a senior [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC) official who is the commander of the [Basij](2892082.md) paramilitary unit. He was formerly head of the [Intelligence Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Organization_of_the_Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps) of the IRGC.[^cite_note-qline-1]

## Early life and education

Taeb was born in 1342 (1963/4).[^cite_note-2]

After his middle education, he went to seminary school and reached an advanced degree (*[kharej](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharej?action=edit\&redlink=1)*) in [Islamic jurisprudence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_jurisprudence) after studying in [Tehran](57654.md), [Mashhad](406512.md), and [Qom](638742.md). He had studied with, among others, [Supreme Leader](36814344.md) [Ali Khamenei](385653.md).&#x20;

## Career

Taeb joined the IRGC (which supervises the [Basij](2892082.md)) in 1361 (1982/3), beginning his work in Region 10 of Tehran, and continued on to Qom and Mashhad. For a while he was IRGC coordinator with the [Supreme Leader of Iran](36814344.md) as well as the cultural commander of [Imam Hossein College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imam_Hossein_University).

Under Taeb's command, the Basij played a key role in suppressing [protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Iranian_presidential_election_protests) over the controversial [2009 Iranian presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Iranian_presidential_election). The suppression saw the death of at least dozens of protesters on the streets or in prison.[^cite_note-abdo-3] He was also involved in other controversial activities, such as: involvement in the "[chain murders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain_murders)" of Iranian intellectuals in the 1990s, and targeting of Iranian dissidents abroad, including assassinations.[^cite_note-4]

In public statements Taeb cautioned Iranians that the United States was "hiring agents and mercenaries in an effort to continue its plots for a [soft overthrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velvet_Revolution) of the Islamic Republic," according to the Iranian Fars news agency.[^cite_note-5] Taeb has also stated that the post-election ["anti-government riots"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Iranian_election_protests) "killed eight members of the Basij and wounded 300 others."[^cite_note-6]

In 2022, he and his family members were sanctioned by the [US Department of State](31975.md) for his involvement in human rights violations in Iran.[^cite_note-7]

Taeb was dismissed from his position as head of Intelligence of the IRGC in June 2022. This is speculated to be due to an incident where an alleged Iranian operation to attack Israeli tourists in Turkey was outed, resulting in the arrest of the agents and a diplomatic spat with Turkey, as well as other incidents that suggested successful Israeli spy operations that Taeb failed to prevent. The dismissal also coincided with the arrest of Brigadier General Ali Nasiri on suspicion of spying for Israel.[^cite_note-8]

On 10 August 2026, Taeb was appointed commander of the [Basij](2892082.md), four years after former Supreme Leader [Ali Khamenei](385653.md) dismissed him as head of the [IRGC Intelligence Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Organization_of_the_Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps). The appointment came amid uncertainty over Mojtaba Khamenei's status, with unconfirmed reports questioning whether he remains alive.[^cite_note-9]

## Personal life

Taeb lost a brother in the [Operation Karbala-5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Basra) during the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War). He is married with three children.[^cite_note-qline-1]

## International sanctions

Private property of Hossein Taeb is frozen by the [European Union](9317.md) and he is not allowed to enter Europe[^cite_note-eu-14] because "forces under his command participated in mass beatings, murders, detentions and tortures of peaceful protestors."[^cite_note-eu-14] He has also been blacklisted by the U.S. government.[^cite_note-15]
