The **Beijing Municipal Public Security Bureau** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 北京市公安局; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Běijīng Shì Gōng'ānjú*) is a department of the [Beijing Municipal People's Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Municipal_People's_Government). It serves as the city's [public security bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_security_bureau_\(China\)) and branch of the [people's police](62010887.md) under the [Ministry of Public Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Public_Security_\(China\)) (MPS) The headquarters is in [Qianmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qianmen), [Dongcheng District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongcheng_District,_Beijing).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

The bureau is the host of the [Beijing SWAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Special_Weapons_and_Tactics_Unit), reportedly one of the most well trained and best equipped special police (特警; *Tèjǐng*) teams in the entire country.[^cite_note-\:0-2] The director of the Beijing Public Security Bureau generally serves concurrently as a [Vice Minister of Public Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Public_Security_\(China\)).[^cite_note-\:03-3]

In 2010, the agency started a new public relations program by allowing Hong Kong and Macau media to attend press conferences and adding new spokespersons.[^cite_note-4]

## Aircraft accidents

On 17 August 2011, a Beijing Municipal Public Security Bureau [AW109E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AgustaWestland_AW109) police helicopter crashed into [Miyun reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyun_reservoir?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Miyun District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyun_District), Beijing during a search and rescue training exercise. Four crewmen were killed in the accident.[^cite_note-5]

## Equipment

### Firearms

All police officers of the Beijing police are armed with a variety of guns on the beat, e.g. [QSZ-92](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QSZ-92), [QSW-06](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QSW-06), officers in special units carry shotguns and SWAT members are armed with the domestically produced [Norinco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norinco) [QBZ-95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QBZ-95) Assault Rifle and [QSZ-92](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QSZ-92) Pistols.
