**Włodzimierz Czarzasty** (born 3 May 1960)[^cite_note-ktokogo-1][^cite_note-2] is a Polish politician who serves as the chairperson of the [New Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Left_\(Poland\)) party (*Nowa Lewica*).[^cite_note-3] He has served as the [marshal of the Sejm](1418762.md) ([Speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_\(politics\))) since 18 November 2025. He also served as a [deputy marshal of the Sejm](14103549.md) from 2019 to 2025.

## Biography

Born in [Warsaw](32908.md), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_People's_Republic), as the son of Wincenty and Irena,[^cite_note-\:0-4] Czarzasty graduated with degrees in journalism and political science from the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) in the 1980s. Simultaneously, he was a member of [Polish Students' Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Students'_Association) (ZSP), leader of association *Ordynacka*, and a member of the communist [Polish United Workers' Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_United_Workers'_Party) (PZPR) from 1983 to 1990.[^cite_note-sld-5]

Following the fall of Communism, Czarzasty became a member of [SLD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Left_Alliance_\(Poland\)), the successor of PZPR. In the [1997 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_Polish_general_election), he ran for the SLD but lost.[^cite_note-6] In May 1999, he was appointed to the [National Broadcasting Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Broadcasting_Council) by the President [Aleksander Kwasniewski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksander_Kwasniewski). He became a point of controversy during the [Rywin corruption scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rywin_affair), but he was not formally charged with any crime.[^cite_note-7]

After a long extra-parliamentary career, Czarzasty ran as part of [United Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Left_\(Poland\)) (ZL) party in the [2015 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Polish_general_election). Since ZL did not pass the 8% threshold required to hold seats in the [Sejm](28167.md), none of the candidates, including Czarzasty, won.[^cite_note-8]

During the [party leadership election in January 2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Democratic_Left_Alliance_leadership_election), Czarzasty defeated former MP [Jerzy Wenderlich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerzy_Wenderlich) and was elected as the new president of Democratic Left Alliance,[^cite_note-9] replacing the incumbent leader and former Prime Minister [Leszek Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leszek_Miller). He was a candidate for the [Masovian Regional Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masovian_Regional_Assembly) during the [local elections in 2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Polish_local_elections), but lost.[^cite_note-10]

### Sejm



Włodzimierz Czarzasty and [Grzegorz Schetyna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grzegorz_Schetyna) at the [European Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Coalition_\(Poland\)) rally

In the [2019 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Polish_parliamentary_election), Czarzasty co-led the *[Lewica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Left_\(Poland\))* coalition, together with [Adrian Zandberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Zandberg) and [Robert Biedroń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Biedro%C5%84), and was elected to the *Sejm*. He was a candidate from [Sosnowiec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sosnowiec) constituency. On 12 November 2019, Czarzasty was elected Deputy Marshal of the Sejm in representation of *Lewica*.

In July 2021, Czarzasty suspended the party member rights of [Karolina Pawliczak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karolina_Pawliczak) and five other New Left MPs after they disagreed with him and made their disagreement public.[^cite_note-auto1-11] The suspended politicians sent a letter to Czarzasty in which they questioned the fact that certain political commitments were made on behalf of the party, and allowed their letter to be public.[^cite_note-auto1-11] Czarzasty accused them of an action grossly violating the obligation to care for the good name of the party.[^cite_note-auto1-11] Pawliczak said: "Democracy, not bullying; talking, not suspending and expelling from the party ... I want to continue building such a democratic party with my friends."[^cite_note-auto1-11]

In the [2023 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Polish_parliamentary_election), he successfully ran for parliamentary re-election, winning 22,332 votes.[^cite_note-2023-election-12] On 13 November of that year, he was elected [Deputy Marshal of the Sejm](14103549.md) under [Szymon Hołownia](49446101.md).[^cite_note-13]

On 5 February 2026, US ambassador to Poland [Tom Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Rose) announced that the United States would have “no further dealings, contacts, or communications” with Czarzasty in response to what Rose said were “outrageous and unprovoked insults directed against [President Trump](4848272.md).[^cite_note-14]

## Personal life

Czarzasty is married to Małgorzata, a business executive and academic with a PhD from the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw). She is the vice-president of a management board of Muza SA and is also involved in the publishing industry. The couple have one daughter, Iga. Czarzasty became a grandfather in 2019 with the birth of his granddaughter Zoja.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-\:0-4]
