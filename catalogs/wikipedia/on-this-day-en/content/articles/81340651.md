The **Administrative and Technical Services Personnel Administration Corps** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Corps d'Administration du Personnel des Services Administratifs et Techniques*), abbreviated **CAPSAT**, is a politically powerful unit of the [Madagascar Armed Forces](50881.md).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Function

CAPSAT is based in Soanierana, on the outskirts of [Antananarivo](56597.md). It is not a [front-line](11043.md) combat unit but plays a central role in the management of [military logistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_logistics), [technical operations](90815.md), and [personnel administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personnel_Administration).[^cite_note-FP-4]

## History

Due to hazy record keeping during socialist rule, the exact foundation date of CAPSAT is unknown. It is possible that the unit, or one that had a nearly identical function, was established as part of the reorganization of the armed forces during the creation of the Marxist-Leninist [Democratic Republic of Madagascar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Madagascar) in 1975; however, the first explicit mention of CAPSAT came in the re-organization of the army on 2 December 2003, when CAPSAT, or its predecessor that was renamed to CAPSAT, was reorganized into the new army of the [Third Republic of Madagascar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Republic_of_Madagascar).[^cite_note-Formed-1][^cite_note-5]

CAPSAT has been a very powerful group in Madagascar for several decades mostly due to connections with local business elites and sections of the [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie), but also because CAPSAT officers were frequently promoted into higher leadership positions, thus spreading the unit's influence over the entire army.[^cite_note-FP-4]

Due to CAPSAT's role they have structural control over key army functions, namely logistics and salaries. Since it is CAPSAT that pays army personnel, they've gained a reputation as the [kingmaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingmaker) of Malagasy politics.[^cite_note-FP-4]

[Their mutiny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Camp_Capsat_mutiny), led by [Colonel](199023.md) Noël Rakotonandrasana, during the [2009 Malagasy political crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Malagasy_political_crisis) caused the resignation of President [Marc Ravalomanana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Ravalomanana) and the installation of [Antananarivo](56597.md) mayor [Andry Rajoelina](21298487.md) as [President](30876601.md) of the "[High Transitional Authority of Madagascar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Transitional_Authority)" after a coup d'état.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-6] CAPSAT had been staunch allies of Rajoelina, who in turn tried and failed to install loyalists and allies into CAPSAT.[^cite_note-FP-4] Due to this attempted infiltration, starting from 2020–2021 CAPSAT and Rajoelina had a falling out, and CAPSAT "questioned whether \[their and Rajoelina's] interests align."[^cite_note-FP-4]

CAPSAT launched a [coup d'état](81321149.md) on 14 October 2025 and its current commander, [Colonel](199023.md) [Michael Randrianirina](81340595.md), became interim [President of Madagascar](30876601.md), although this was disputed by [Andry Rajoelina](21298487.md) who fled the country and is derelict of his duties.[^cite_note-\:2-7]
