The **head of the Judicial System of the Islamic Republic of Iran** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): رئیس قوه قضائیه جمهوری اسلامی ایران, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Raeis Ghooh Ghazaiyeh Jamehori Eslami Iran*), also known as the **chief justice of Iran**, is the leader of the [judicial system of the Islamic Republic of Iran](66501437.md) and is responsible for its administration and supervision.

The position is required to be an "honorable man" according to Article 157 \[Head of Judiciary] of the [Constitution of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Iran).[^cite_note-2] The [supreme leader of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_leader_of_Iran) has the authority to appoint and dismiss the chief justice. The chief justice may serve up to two five-year terms. The chief justice is also the highest-ranking judge of the [Supreme Court of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Iran). Additionally, the chief justice nominates several candidates for the position of [Minister of Justice](15207354.md), from whom the president of Iran selects one.
