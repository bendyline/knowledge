On 24 May 2026, a vehicle-borne suicide bombing targeted a [shuttle train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuttle_train) near Chaman Phatak in [Quetta](93764.md), Balochistan, Pakistan. The explosion killed at least 47 people and injured 98 others.[^cite_note-Xinhua-1][^cite_note-Reuters2-3]

The train was carrying Pakistani security personnel and their family members from Quetta's [cantonment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantonment) area to connect with the *[Jaffar Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffar_Express)*.[^cite_note-Reuters1-4] The [Balochistan Liberation Army](6010135.md) claimed responsibility for the attack.[^cite_note-Reuters1-4][^cite_note-AlJazeera-5]

## Background

Further information: [Insurgency in Balochistan](4591548.md)

[Quetta](93764.md) is the capital of [Balochistan](4687.md), a province affected by a long-running [separatist](21017661.md) [insurgency](4591548.md). Militants in the province have targeted security forces, government installations, civilians and infrastructure.[^cite_note-DW-6]

Before the 2026 bombing, there had been several high-profile incidents involving railway and security targets in Balochistan, including the [2024 Quetta railway station bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Quetta_railway_station_bombing) and the [2025 *Jaffar Express* hijacking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Jaffar_Express_hijacking).[^cite_note-DW-6][^cite_note-CBS-7]

The BLA has claimed that the Pakistani government profits from Balochistan's mineral resources without sufficient benefit to local communities.[^cite_note-8]

A day before the attack, nine suspected militants and four officials from the [Counter Terrorism Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter_Terrorism_Department_\(Pakistan\)) were killed in a shootout on the outskirts of Quetta.[^cite_note-9]

## Attack

Shortly after 8 a.m. [PKT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Standard_Time), an explosives-laden vehicle struck one of the carriages of a shuttle train near Chaman Phatak as the train was travelling through Quetta.[^cite_note-Reuters1-4][^cite_note-Dawn-10]

Railway authorities said the train was travelling from Quetta Cantonment towards the city's railway station, where passengers were to connect with the *[Jaffar Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffar_Express)*.[^cite_note-Dawn-10] Reuters, citing Pakistan's railways ministry, reported that the blast derailed the engine and three coaches, and that two coaches overturned.[^cite_note-Reuters1-4]

The explosion occurred in a residential area and damaged nearby buildings and vehicles.[^cite_note-Reuters1-4][^cite_note-AlJazeera-5] Images and videos from the scene showed burned vehicles, damaged buildings, smoke and train carriages on their sides.[^cite_note-Reuters1-4][^cite_note-Euronews-11]

[Police](9371583.md), the Counter-Terrorism Department, bomb disposal personnel, security forces and rescue workers responded to the site. [Pakistan Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Railways) dispatched rescue trucks and a relief train.[^cite_note-Dawn-10]

## Casualties

Rescue officials said 47 people were killed, including 20 soldiers, and 98 others were injured.[^cite_note-Xinhua-1]

Officials said security personnel, passengers, bystanders and residents of nearby houses were among the casualties.[^cite_note-Dawn-10][^cite_note-Reuters1-4] The Balochistan government said three [Frontier Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontier_Corps) personnel and a family of four were among those killed.[^cite_note-Dawn-10] Women and children were also reported among the injured.[^cite_note-Dawn-10][^cite_note-AlJazeeraVideo-12]

A medical emergency was declared in public hospitals in Quetta, and doctors, paramedics and other staff were ordered to remain on duty.[^cite_note-AlJazeera-5][^cite_note-Dawn-10] According to the [Associated Press](18935732.md), doctors said 20 of the injured were in critical condition.[^cite_note-AP-13]

## Aftermath

The [Balochistan Liberation Army](6010135.md) (BLA) claimed responsibility for the attack and said it had targeted security personnel on the train.[^cite_note-Reuters1-4][^cite_note-AlJazeera-5] Reuters reported that it could not independently verify the group's casualty claim.[^cite_note-Reuters1-4]

### Perpetrator

The BLA identified the alleged bomber as 25-year-old **Bilal Shahwani**, a resident of Killi Sarde, and said he was a member of the [Majeed Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majeed_Brigade).[^cite_note-Shahwani-2] Pakistani security forces reportedly raided Shahwani's home and detained several of his relatives.[^cite_note-14]

## Reactions

### Domestic

Security forces cordoned off the area after the explosion, and the district administration established a control room at the office of the Quetta deputy commissioner. The provincial Home Department also activated a monitoring and coordination cell.[^cite_note-Dawn-10]

Prime Minister [Shehbaz Sharif](6502561.md), President [Asif Ali Zardari](993655.md), Balochistan chief minister [Sarfraz Bugti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarfraz_Bugti), railways minister [Hanif Abbasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanif_Abbasi) and Deputy Prime Minister [Ishaq Dar](988764.md) condemned the attack.[^cite_note-Dawn-10][^cite_note-15]

The [Human Rights Commission of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Rights_Commission_of_Pakistan) said it was alarmed by the security situation in Balochistan and by attacks on civilians, workers, passengers, law enforcement personnel and public infrastructure.[^cite_note-Dawn-10]

### International

[China](5405.md), [Qatar](8238258.md) and [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) condemned the attack.[^cite_note-16]

[António Guterres](471806.md), the secretary-general of the [United Nations](31769.md), condemned the attack and wished a swift recovery to those affected.[^cite_note-17]

The [Afghan Ministry of Foreign Affairs](8980088.md), the [United States Embassy in Islamabad](32553327.md), the [Chinese Embassy in Islamabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_China,_Islamabad) and [Global Affairs Canada](256258.md) also condemned the attack.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

Iran's ambassador to Pakistan, [Reza Amiri-Moghadam](82895952.md), condemned the attack, describing it as a "cowardly and heinous suicide terrorist act," stating "Terrorism is a condemnable phenomenon in all its forms all over the world."[^cite_note-22]
