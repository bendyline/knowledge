**Niger State** is a state located in northwestern [Nigeria](21383.md), bordered to the east by [Kaduna State](2227637.md) and the [Federal Capital Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Capital_Territory_\(Nigeria\)), to the north by [Kebbi State](2227643.md) and [Zamfara State](2227664.md), and to the south by [Kogi](299399.md) and [Kwara](2227645.md) states, while its western border makes up part of the [international border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benin%E2%80%93Nigeria_border) with [Benin](3459.md). It is the [largest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_area) [state](226734.md) in the country by area. The state capital is [Minna](1658964.md), and the state is divided into 25 local government areas. Other major cities include [Bida](5328621.md), [Kontagora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kontagora), and [Suleja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suleja).[^cite_note-\:0-5][^cite_note-\:1-6]

Of the 36 [states of Nigeria](226734.md), Niger is the [eleventh most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population) with an estimated population of about 6.7 million as of 2020.[^cite_note-7] Geographically, the state is split between the [West Sudanian savanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Sudanian_savanna) in the east and the [Guinean forest–savanna mosaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinean_forest%E2%80%93savanna_mosaic) [ecoregion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ecoregions_in_Nigeria) in the rest of the state. The [Kainji Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainji_Lake) as well as the [Niger](21437.md) and [Kaduna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_River) rivers are the state's major water bodies, with the River Niger flowing through from [Kainji Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainji_Lake) through the western part of the state and forming the border with [Kwara State](2227645.md). Niger State is also home to the [Kainji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainji_Dam), [Jebba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jebba_Hydroelectric_Power_Station), and [Shiroro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiroro_Hydroelectric_Power_Station) Dams, which are major sources of hydroelectric power. Also situated there is [Kainji National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainji_National_Park), the largest National Park in Nigeria, which contains [Kainji Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainji_Lake), the [Borgu Game Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borgu_Game_Reserve) and the [Zugurma Game Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zugurma_Game_Reserve).

Niger State is ethnically diverse, including communities of [Adara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adara_language), [Fulani](102383.md), [Gbagyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gbagyi_people), [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people), [Hun-Saare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hun-Saare_language), [Kambari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kambari_languages), [Kamuku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamuku_languages), [Koro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jijili_language) Gungawa, [Nupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_people), and other ethnic groups.

Historically, parts of modern-day Niger State were centres of powerful pre-colonial states, including the [Nupe Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_Kingdom), Gbagyi states, and parts of the [Hausa Bakwai states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banza_Bakwai). In the early nineteenth century, parts of the state were incorporated into the [Sokoto Caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_Caliphate) following the [Fulani jihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jihad_of_Usman_dan_Fodio). With British colonisation in the early 1900s, the area became part of the [Northern Nigeria Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nigeria_Protectorate). Upon Nigeria's independence in 1960, Niger State was initially part of the [Northern Region](2292852.md) until 1967, when it became part of the [North-Western State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-Western_State). In 1976, Niger State was created as a distinct entity following the breakup of North-Western State and in the 1990s, the state gained the Borgu region from Kwara. Since the late 2010s, Niger has been one of the states hit hardest by [banditry](68366617.md) and terrorism.

Economically, Niger State is largely driven by agriculture along with livestock farming and herding. The state plays a key role in Nigeria's energy sector due to its hydroelectric dams. Niger State has the [tenth-highest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_GDP) [GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP) in the country but has a [low](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_Human_Development_Index) [Human Development Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Development_Index) ranking.[^cite_note-8]

## Government

Like most Nigerian states, Niger State is governed by a democratically-elected [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Governors_of_Niger_State), who is currently [Mohammad Umar Bago](61340414.md). The governor works with the [Niger State House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger_State_House_of_Assembly).[^cite_note-auto-9][^cite_note-\:1-6] The capital city is [Minna](1658964.md).[^cite_note-10]

The state has three Senatorial Zones/Districts: [Niger East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger_East_Senatorial_District), [Niger North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger_North_Senatorial_District), [Niger South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger_South_Senatorial_District).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

### Electoral system

The governor is selected using a modified two-round system. To be elected in the first round, a candidate must receive the plurality of the vote and over 25% of the vote in at least two -thirds of the State [local government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government) areas. If no candidate passes threshold, a second round will be held between the top candidate and the next candidate to have received a plurality of votes in the highest number of local government Areas.[^cite_note-auto-9]

## Transport

Federal Highways are:

- [A1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_highway_\(Nigeria\)) north from [Ilorin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilorin) in [Kwara State](2227645.md) across the [Niger River](21437.md) by a four-lane bridge at [Jebba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jebba) for 388 km as part of the [African Unity Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Sahara_Highway) (TAH2: Trans-African Highway 2) to [Kontagora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kontagora), then via Ibeto to Yelwa in [Kebbi State](2227643.md).

- [A125](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A125_highway_\(Nigeria\)) continuing [TAH2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TAH_2) east from [A1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_highway_\(Nigeria\)) at Kontagora via Tegina to [Kaduna State](2227637.md) at Gishiri.

- [A124](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A124_highway_\(Nigeria\)) east from A1 north of Mokwa via [Bida](5328621.md) and [Agaie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agaie) as the Bida-Abuja Rd to [Abuja, FCT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abuja,_FCT) at Kudan.

Two roads to Benin:&#x20;

- The Wana-Babana Rd at Babana to [Basso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basso,_Benin).

- Agwara Tungar Jatau Swate Maje Rd from Agwarra via Konkwesso to RNIE 7 to [Ségbana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9gbana).

Other Major Roads include:

- The Minna-Tagbari Rd northeast to [Kaduna State](2227637.md) as the Chibian-Zazaga-Sarkin Pawa Rd

- The Minna-Bida Rd

- The Mararaba-Makutu-Mapi Rd southeast from [Minna](1658964.md) via Matumbi and Paiko to [A124](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A124_highway_\(Nigeria\)) at Daga

- Yelwa-Mokwa Rd across [the Niger River](21437.md) by a bridge from Kato to Jinjima

Railways:

the [Western Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Railways) north from [Lagos](85232.md) via [Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibadan) and [Ilorin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilorin) across the [Niger River](21437.md) at [Jebba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jebba) by a 547 m bridge (1915) via [Minna](1658964.md) to [Kaduna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_\(city\)), with a branch line from [Minna](1658964.md) 155 km to the port of [Baro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baro_\(Nigeria\)) on the Niger River, which is connected with [Kano](508782.md) by the [Northern Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Railways).

## Geography

### Climate

The state has very warm [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) with a yearly average of 34° [Celsius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celsius). It is warm or hot all year, but there are few typically tropical and sultry months, although humidity is sometimes uncomfortably high in June to September. The best time for traveling is from November to April, when there is less rain. The rainiest days occur in May to October. September has the rainiest days, and December the least. January is the sunniest month, and August the least sunny.[^cite_note-Climate:_Niger_in_Nigeria-26]
