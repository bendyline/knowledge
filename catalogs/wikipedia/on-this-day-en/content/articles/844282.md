The **Central District** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): מָחוֹז הַמֶרְכָּז, *Meḥoz haMerkaz*; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): المنطقة الوسطى, *Minṭaqat al-Wasaṭ*) of [Israel](9282173.md) is one of [six administrative districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Israel), including most of the [Sharon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharon_plain) region. It is further divided into four sub-districts: [Petah Tikva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petah_Tikva_Subdistrict), [Ramla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramla_Subdistrict), [HaSharon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HaSharon_Subdistrict), and [Rehovot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehovot_Subdistrict). The district's largest city is [Rishon LeZion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rishon_LeZion). The district's population as of 2024 was 2,409,464. According to the Israeli Central Bureau of Statistics, 89% of the population is [Jewish](25955086.md), 8.4% is [Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab), and the remainder are "non-classified", being mostly [former Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-Soviet_states) immigrants of partial or nominal Jewish ethnic heritage or household members of Jews.[^cite_note-5]
