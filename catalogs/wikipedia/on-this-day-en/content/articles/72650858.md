The **Athens metropolitan area** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Μητροπολιτική Περιοχή της Αθήνας) spans 2,928.717 km<sup>2</sup> (1,131 sq mi) within the [Attica region](26280929.md) and consists of 58 municipalities plus parts of [East Attica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Attica) and [West Attica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Attica), having reached a population of 3,638,281 according to the 2021 census.[^cite_note-2] The municipalities of [Athens](1216.md) and [Piraeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus) both serve as the two metropolitan centres of the Athens metropolitan area.[^cite_note-3]

According to the [Hellenic Telecommunications and Post Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hellenic_Telecommunications_and_Post_Commission), the [Athens](1216.md) metropolitan area consists of [all areas with a dialing code of 21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telephone_numbers_in_Greece) and includes areas such as [Salamina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salamis_Island), [Elefsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elefsina), [Kifissia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kifissia), [Mandra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandra), [Magoula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magoula,_Attica), [Aspropyrgos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspropyrgos), [Pallini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pallini), [Agios Stefanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agios_Stefanos,_Attica), [Dionysos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dionysos,_Greece), [Parnitha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnitha), [Koropi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koropi), [Vari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vari), [Vouliagmeni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vouliagmeni), [Voula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voula) and the [Athens International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_International_Airport).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Administration

The [Athens Urban Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_Urban_Area), also known as Greater Athens,[^cite_note-8] consists of 40 municipalities, 35 of which make up what was referred to as the former [Athens Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_Prefecture) municipalities, located within 4 regional units ([North Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Athens_\(regional_unit\)), [West Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Athens_\(regional_unit\)), [Central Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Athens_\(regional_unit\)), [South Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Athens_\(regional_unit\))); and a further 5 municipalities, which make up the former [Piraeus Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus_Prefecture) municipalities, located within the [regional unit of Piraeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus_\(regional_unit\)). The Athens Municipality forms the core and centre of Greater Athens, which in turn consists of the Athens Municipality and 40 more municipalities, divided into four [regional units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_units_of_Greece), accounting for 2,611,713 people (in 2021) within an area of 361 km<sup>2</sup> (139 sq mi). The regional units of Central, North, South, and West [Athens](1216.md), [Piraeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus) and parts of East[^cite_note-9] and West Attica[^cite_note-eranet.gr-10] regional units combined make up the continuous Athens Urban Area.[^cite_note-eranet.gr-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Climate

Due to its large area metropolitan [Athens](1216.md) has a variety of climates. The largest part of the Athens metropolitan area has a hot-summer [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Csa*), while some areas of the [Athens Riviera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_Riviera), some areas of the [Thriasio Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thriasio_Plain) and some areas in the west have a [hot semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_semi-arid_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *BSh*). According to the meteorological stations of the [National Observatory of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Observatory_of_Athens) and the [Hellenic National Meteorological Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hellenic_National_Meteorological_Service) areas in the north have wetter and colder winters. [Dionysos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dionysos,_Greece) has a January average high of around 9 °C and an average low of 4 °C, while [Parnitha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnitha) mountain has a January average high of around 4 °C and an average low below 0 °C.[^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-14][^cite_note-Dionysos-15][^cite_note-Parnitha-16] Areas in the south have very mild winters with [Nea Smyrni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nea_Smyrni) recording a January average high of 15 °C and an average low of 9 °C.[^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-14][^cite_note-Nea_Smyrni-17]

The highest summer maximum temperatures are recorded in the interior of the Athens Basin and also in west areas with [Magoula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magoula,_Attica), [Nea Filadelfeia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nea_Filadelfeia), [Harokopio University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harokopio_University) and [Salamina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salamis_Island) registering July mean maximum temperatures as high as 36.5 °C.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-Meteoclub-19][^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-14][^cite_note-Salamina-20] Due to land breezes, summer nights can be particularly hot in the south and especially around [Piraeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus) which records an average August low of around 27 °C.[^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-14][^cite_note-Piraeus-21] The highest average annual temperature is recorded in [Piraeus Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus) registering around 20.9 °C, while the lowest is recorded in [Parnitha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnitha) mountain with less than 11.0 °C.[^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-14][^cite_note-Parnitha_2-22][^cite_note-23] [Neos Kosmos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neos_Kosmos) is the warmest area of [Downtown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown) [Athens](1216.md) with an average annual temperature of 20.2 °C and a [hot semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_semi-arid_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *BSh*) closely bordering a [hot-summer mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot-summer_mediterranean) (*Csa*) climate. During July 2024 minimum temperatures remained over 30 °C (86 °F) for 12 consecutive days in metropolitan [Athens](1216.md), breaking all known records for any area in the country.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

The lowest average annual precipitation in metropolitan [Athens](1216.md) is recorded in [Piraeus Kallipoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus) with 304 mm, while the highest average annual precipitation is recorded in [Parnitha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnitha) mountain with around 720 mm.[^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-14][^cite_note-Parnitha_3-26][^cite_note-27] The highest temperature ever recorded in the Athens metropolitan area was 48.0 °C in [Elefsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elefsina) and [Tatoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatoi) on July 10, 1977 which according to the [World Meteorological Organization](33584.md) was also the highest official temperature ever recorded in [Europe](9239.md) until 2021.[^cite_note-wmo.asu.edu-28] The lowest temperature ever recorded was −12.0 °C in [Parnitha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnitha) mountain on January 9, 2017.[^cite_note-Parnitha_4-29] The highest minimum temperature of 32.9 °C (91 °F) was recorded on July 26, 2023 in [Hellinikon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hellinikon).[^cite_note-30] Furthermore, Metropolitan Athens has experienced temperatures of [47.5 °C and over in four different locations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_extreme_temperatures_in_Greece).

Regarding the whole of the [Attica](26280929.md) peninsula, the lowest average annual precipitation is found in [Anavysos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anavysos), [Athens Riviera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_Riviera) with around 290 mm, while the highest average annual precipitation is found in [Avlonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avlonas,_Attica) with around 910 mm.[^cite_note-Anavysos-31] [Lavrio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavrio) is the only area in mainland [Attica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attica) that has never recorded an air frost according to the [National Observatory of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Observatory_of_Athens) station which operates since 2008.[^cite_note-Lavrio-32][^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-14]

## Economy

The [Gross domestic product](12594.md) (GDP) of the region was 87.4 billion € in 2018, accounting for about 47% of the Greek economic output. GDP per capita adjusted for purchasing power was 28,000 € or 93% of the EU27 average in the same year. The GDP per employee was 99% of the EU average. Attica is the region in Greece with the highest GDP per capita.[^cite_note-44]
