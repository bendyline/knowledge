# List of terrorist incidents in France



Terrorist incidents map of France (1970–2015). Paris, Corsica and Southwestern France are major places of incidents. A total of 2,616 incidents were plotted.

&#x20;This is a list of [terrorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist) attacks in [France](5843419.md) from 1800 to the present. Several 19th-century French rulers were targeted in unsuccessful assassination attempts which killed innocent bystanders.

Since 1970, France experienced 2,654 terrorist incidents, resulting in 1,247 terrorist-related deaths and 2,559 injuries, the second highest in western Europe after the [United Kingdom](18563581.md).[^cite_note-globalterrorismdb_0617dist-1][^cite_note-gtd1993_0617dist-2] [France](5843419.md) remains the country most affected by Islamist terrorism within [Europe](9239.md), with recent data showcasing a total of 82 [Islamist attacks](566821.md) and 332 deaths from 1979 to 2021.[^cite_note-3]

## Background



Etching of the December 1800 attack of the [plot of the rue Saint-Nicaise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plot_of_the_rue_Saint-Nicaise)

France has a lengthy history of terrorist attacks carried out by a variety of groups from the extreme right, extreme left, extreme [Basque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basques), [Breton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bretons), [Savoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savoyard) and [Corsican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corsicans) nationalists, [Algerian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Algeria) insurgent groups and [Islamist extremists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamist_extremist).[^cite_note-LeFigaro-7-Jan-2015-4] Most of the attacks have been bombings utilising [improvised explosive devices](265112.md) (IEDs). [Anarchists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anarchist) carried out a series of bombings and assassination attempts in the 19th century. A number of attacks associated with the [Algerian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algerian_War) took place in the 1950s and 1960s, including the deadliest terrorist attack in France in the 20th century, the [1961 Vitry-Le-François train bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1961_Vitry-Le-Fran%C3%A7ois_train_bombing) carried out by the pro-colonialist French nationalist [Organisation armée secrète](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organisation_arm%C3%A9e_secr%C3%A8te).

Various Middle Eastern factions carried out shootings and bombings in the 1970s and 1980s, mainly in Paris, while during the [Algerian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algerian_Civil_War) of the 1990s, insurgents associated with the [Armed Islamic Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Islamic_Group_of_Algeria) (GIA) carried out a series of major attacks against the [Paris](22989.md) public transport system. Nationalist extremists from the Basque, Breton and Corsican communities carried out a number of assassinations and targeted bomb attacks in the 1990s and 2000s. Islamist extremists have carried out numerous attacks in the 2010s, of which the [November 2015 Paris attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_2015_Paris_attacks) have been the bloodiest to date. Although 2015 has been the deadliest year so far in terms of fatalities caused by terrorist attacks, the number of separate terrorist attacks in previous years has been far higher. The highest number of attacks recorded in a single year was 270 in 1996, largely carried out by Algerian Civil War insurgents. The last year without any recorded terrorist attacks was 1971.[^cite_note-globalterrorismdb_0616dist-5]

## List of incidents

Outside France, the terrorist attack with the most French victims was the 19 September 1989 bombing of [UTA Flight 772](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTA_Flight_772) over [Niger](21373.md), in which 170 people died, 54 of them French citizens.[^cite_note-LeParisien-7-Jan-2015-6]
