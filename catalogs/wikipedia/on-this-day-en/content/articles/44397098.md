**National Highway 39** (**NH 39**) is a [National Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_\(India\)) in [India](14533.md). This highway passes through the Indian states of [Madhya Pradesh](47945.md), [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md) and [Jharkhand](197225.md).[^cite_note-renumber-1]\


## Route

This 869-kilometre-long (540 mi) highway passes through [Jhansi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhansi), [Chhatarpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chhatarpur), [Panna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panna,_India), [Satna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satna), [Rewa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rewa,_India), Rampur Naikin, [Sidhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidhi), [Singrauli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singrauli), [Renukut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renukut), [Shaktinagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaktinagar,_Uttar_Pradesh), and some major town in Jharkhand with 604-kilometre-long (375 mi) length in [Madhya Pradesh](47945.md) and [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md) and rest 265-kilometre-long (165 mi) stretch in Jharkhand, starting from Kanhar near [Garhwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garhwa) , connecting [NH-139](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_139_\(India\)) in [Daltonganj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daltonganj), then passes through [Latehar](1673467.md), connecting [ NH-22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_22_\(India\)) at [Chandwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandwa), connecting [ NH-143A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_143A_\(India\)) in [Kuru, Lohardaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuru,_Lohardaga) then passes through [Chanho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chanho), [Mandar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandar,_Ranchi), [Ratu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratu,_Ranchi), and finally ends at Ring Road, Tupudana, [Ranchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranchi)

## Route conditions

NH39 is one of the worst highways in India as per route conditions. The highway is 4 laned between [Jhansi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhansi)-[Khajuraho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khajuraho), [Satna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satna)-[Rewa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rewa,_Madhya_Pradesh), [Sidhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidhi)-Tenduha, [Garhwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garhwa)-Kundi and [Kuru, Lohardaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuru,_Lohardaga)-[Ranchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranchi) stretches covering just 370-kilometre-long (230 mi) of distance. Rest 499-kilometre-long (310 mi) is 2-laned and has serious bad road conditions. NHAI is working to make 192-kilometre-long (119 mi) more stretch into 4 lane by 2026.
