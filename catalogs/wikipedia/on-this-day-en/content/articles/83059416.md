The **National Transitional Council**[^cite_note-2] was established in [Mali](19127.md) after the [2020 coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Malian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) to replace the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Mali\)) as a legislative body.

## History

After allegations of fraud during the [2020 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Malian_parliamentary_election) for the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Mali\)) led to [mass protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Malian_protests) and [military coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Malian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) against President [Ibrahim Boubacar Keïta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Boubacar_Ke%C3%AFta), the transitional government appointed a new legislature in the form of the National Transitional Council. In practice, since Col. [Assimi Goïta](65000306.md) assumed power after the [2021 military coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Malian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat), the council has not been an effective counterweight to the power of the executive.[^cite_note-BTI-3]

In July 2025, the [military junta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Committee_for_the_Salvation_of_the_People) introduced a measure that gave Goïta a five-year term that is renewable indefinitely without elections, and it was passed by the transitional council.[^cite_note-4]

## Composition

The council, whose members were appointed by a decree of the [transitional president of Mali](430390.md) on 5 December 2020, originally consisted of 147 members.[^cite_note-IPU-1] Members can be dismissed at will by the transitional president,[^cite_note-BTI-3] and as of 2026 it has 133 members, including 93 men and 40 women.[^cite_note-IPU-1]

[Malick Diaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malick_Diaw) has been the president of the council since it was appointed.[^cite_note-IPU-1]
