The **Division of Farrer** is an [Australian electoral division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electorates_of_the_Australian_House_of_Representatives) in the [state](4689096.md) of [New South Wales](21654.md). Located in the southwestern part of the state bordering both [South Australia](26716.md) and [Victoria](4689460.md), at 126,563 km<sup>2</sup> (48,866.2 sq mi) it is the second-largest division by land area in the state after the neighbouring [Division of Parkes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_Parkes). It includes the cities of [Albury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albury,_New_South_Wales) and [Griffith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griffith,_New_South_Wales), and has always included Albury since the creation of the division in 1949.

Following the [2026 Farrer by election](82405071.md), the electorate is represented by [One Nation’s](83013.md) [David Farley](83140164.md), the first member of the party to be elected to the House of Representatives.

## History



[William Farrer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Farrer), the division's namesake

The division was created in 1949 and is named for [William Farrer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Farrer), an agricultural scientist.

It has always been a safe non-Labor seat, alternating between the Liberal Party and the [National Party](21927.md). All four of its members have gone on to serve in cabinet, most notably [Tim Fischer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Fischer), leader of the National Party from 1990 to 1999 and [Deputy Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Australia) from 1996 to 1999 during the first half of the [Howard government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_government).

Fischer's predecessor, [Wal Fife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wal_Fife), transferred to the [Division of Hume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_Hume) in 1984 after Farrer ceded [Wagga Wagga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagga_Wagga) to Hume in a redistribution.

The member from [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Australian_federal_election) until [2026](82405071.md) was [Sussan Ley](577186.md), a member of the [Liberal Party](18453.md) and that party's deputy leader from 2022 to 2025, and leader from 2025 to 2026.[^cite_note-1] She resigned in February 2026 after losing a [leadership spill](82390513.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

[David Farley](83140164.md) of [One Nation](83013.md) won the resulting [by-election](82405071.md) in May 2026, the party's first elected member in the House of Representatives.[^cite_note-aec-results-4]

## Boundaries

Since 1984, federal electoral division boundaries in Australia have been determined at redistributions by a redistribution committee appointed by the [Australian Electoral Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Electoral_Commission). Redistributions occur for the boundaries of divisions in a particular state, and they occur every seven years, or sooner if a state's representation entitlement changes or when divisions of a state are malapportioned.[^cite_note-5]

When the division was first created in 1949, it covered areas that were previously part of the [Division of Hume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_Hume), such as [Wagga Wagga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagga_Wagga) and [Albury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albury).[^cite_note-hume-6] It lost Wagga Wagga to Hume in 1984, but significantly expanded westwards to the [South Australian border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Australian_borders). The expansion replaced the southern portion of the [Division of Riverina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_Riverina) which was renamed [Division of Riverina-Darling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_Riverina-Darling) that year.[^cite_note-riverina-7][^cite_note-boundaries-8] Since then, the division has always covered the south-west corner of the state, the [MacCabe Corner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacCabe_Corner).

Farrer gained the [Far West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_West_\(New_South_Wales\)), including [Broken Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broken_Hill) from the [Division of Parkes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_Parkes) in the 2006 redistribution.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-parkes-10] The gain also included the north-west corner of the state, the [Cameron Corner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Corner_Survey_Marker). Much of this gain was reverted in the 2016 redistribution, ceding the area back to Parkes.[^cite_note-11] At the same time Farrer absorbed the [Murrumbidgee Irrigation Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murrumbidgee_Irrigation_Area), including [Griffith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griffith,_New_South_Wales) and [Leeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeton,_New_South_Wales) from [Riverina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_Riverina).[^cite_note-riverina-7][^cite_note-12] There were no changes to the boundaries in the subsequent 2024 redistribution.[^cite_note-boundaries-8]

Since 2016, the division is located in the far south-western area of the state, and covers the entirety of the local government areas of [City of Albury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Albury), [Balranald Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balranald_Shire), [Berrigan Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berrigan_Shire), [Carrathool Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrathool_Shire), [Edward River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_River_Council), [Federation Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_Council_\(New_South_Wales\)), [Greater Hume Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Hume_Shire), [City of Griffith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Griffith), [Hay Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hay_Shire), [Leeton Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeton_Shire), [Murray River Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murray_River_Council), [Murrumbidgee Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murrumbidgee_Council), [Narrandera Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrandera_Shire) and [Wentworth Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wentworth_Shire).[^cite_note-13] These include the cities or towns of [Albury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albury), [Corowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corowa), [Narrandera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrandera), [Leeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeton,_New_South_Wales), [Griffith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griffith,_New_South_Wales), [Deniliquin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deniliquin), [Hay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hay,_New_South_Wales), [Balranald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balranald) and [Wentworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wentworth,_New_South_Wales). The division has covered Albury throughout its existence.[^cite_note-boundaries-8]
