**Gonzalo Edmundo Celorio Blasco**[^cite_note-1] (born 25 March 1948) is a Mexican writer, essayist, literary critic, and academic. Since 2019 he has served as the director of the [Mexican Academy of Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Mexicana_de_la_Lengua). From 2000 to 2002, he was director of the [Economic Cultural Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fondo_de_Cultura_Econ%C3%B3mica). He won the [Miguel de Cervantes Prize](3930514.md), the most prestigious literary award in the Spanish-speaking world, in 2025.[^cite_note-2]

He obtained a bachelor's degree in language and literature from the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) (UNAM), and a doctorate in [Ibero-American literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_American_literature) from the same university.

## Biography

Celorio was born in Mexico City in 1948. His paternal grandfather Emeterio Celorio Santoveña immigrated to Mexico from [Llanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llanes), [Asturias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asturias), in 1874 and his maternal grandparents originated from Cuba.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] His family’s life was narrated in the novels *Tres lindas cubanas*, published in 2006, where he talks about his mother's family;[^cite_note-5] *El metal y la escoria*, published in 2014, about his father's family and *Los apóstatas*, published in 2020, about two of his twelve brothers.[^cite_note-wmagazin-6]

Celorio studied literature and philology at the [School of Philosophy and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Philosophy_and_Letters,_UNAM) of the [Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Nacional_Aut%C3%B3noma_de_M%C3%A9xico) (UNAM). He was a student of historian [Edmundo O'Gorman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmundo_O'Gorman). Between 2000 and 2002 he was the director of the [Fondo de Cultura Económica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fondo_de_Cultura_Econ%C3%B3mica).[^cite_note-wmagazin-6][^cite_note-academia-7]

Celorio has written five [novels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novel), *Amor Propio*,[^cite_note-8] a coming-of-age story, *Y retiemble en sus centros la Tierra*[^cite_note-9] and the *Una familia ejemplar* trilogy about his family[^cite_note-wmagazin-6] (*Tres lindas cubanas*, *El metal y la escoria* and *Los apóstatas*).[^cite_note-apost-10]

## Awards, fellowships and recognition

In 1986, he received the Cultural Journalism Award, granted by the [Instituto Nacional de Bellas Artes y Literatura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_Nacional_de_Bellas_Artes_y_Literatura) (INBA), for *Los subrayados son míos*; in 1997, he was awarded the Prix des deux océans, given by the Biarritz Festival, for his work *El viaje sedentario*, translated into French; and in 1999, the National IMPAC/CONARTE/ITESM Novel Award for the novel *Y retiemble en sus centros la tierra*.[^cite_note-inbalit-11]

He was elected as a member of the [Academia Mexicana de la Lengua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Mexicana_de_la_Lengua) (Mexican Academy of Language) in 1995. In February 2019 he became director of the institution.[^cite_note-academia-7]

In 2011, he was awarded the [National Prize for Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Prize_for_Arts_and_Sciences_\(Mexico\)) in the Linguistics and literature, the highest distincion the Mexican government gives to artists.[^cite_note-lajornada2010-12]

He is also a member of the [Academia Cubana de la Lengua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Cubana_de_la_Lengua).[^cite_note-13]

In 2015, he was awarded the [Premio Mazatlán de Literatura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premio_Mazatl%C3%A1n_de_Literatura?action=edit\&redlink=1) for his novel *El metal y la escoria*. In 2023, he received the [Xavier Villaurrutia Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavier_Villaurrutia_Award) for his memoirs *Mentideros de la memoria*.[^cite_note-inba2022xva-14][^cite_note-oaxaca-15]

Celorio received Spain's [Cervantes Prize](3930514.md) in 2025 for his "exceptional literary work and intellectual endeavours, through which he has made a profound and sustained contribution to the enrichment of the Spanish language and culture".[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]
