# Lebanese prisoners in Israel

&#x20;**Lebanese prisoners in Israel** have been a source of contention between [Lebanon](17771.md) and [Israel](9282173.md) and were an issue in the [2006 Lebanon War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Lebanon_War). The number of such detainees is disputed. According to the Lebanese paramilitary and political party [Hezbollah](13919.md), there are two Lebanese citizens in Israeli prisons, but Israel denies holding them.[^cite_note-BBC-1] Hezbollah had demanded the release of Lebanese prisoners as condition for releasing Israeli reservists [Ehud Goldwasser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehud_Goldwasser) and [Eldad Regev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eldad_Regev), captured in the Hezbollah raid which started the 2006 Lebanon War.[^cite_note-ynet-2][^cite_note-3] On July 16, 2008, the [Israel Prison Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Prison_Service) released five Lebanese prisoners in exchange for the remains of Goldwasser and Regev.[^cite_note-4]

## Context

Following the attempted assassination of the Israeli Ambassador to the [United Kingdom](31717.md), [Shlomo Argov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shlomo_Argov), by the [Abu Nidal Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Nidal_Organization), [Israel](9282173.md) initiated [Operation Peace of the Galilee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Peace_of_the_Galilee) in June 1982, in order to terminate the military activity of the [Palestine Liberation Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Liberation_Organization) and [Syrian](7515849.md) forces around the Israeli-Lebanese border. With U.S. assistance, Israel and Lebanon reached a [withdrawal accord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_17_Agreement) in May 1983, which was then recalled by Lebanon in March 1984, due to pressure from Syria. In 1985, Israel withdrew most of its troops, leaving the [South Lebanon Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Lebanon_Army), an Israeli-supported Lebanese militia, to protect a [security buffer zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_occupation_of_Southern_Lebanon_\(1982%E2%80%932000\)) in southern Lebanon, which Israel considered necessary to prevent attacks on its northern territory. Israel relinquished the security zone and withdrew behind the [Blue Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Line_\(Lebanon\)) in May 2000.

## Persons named by Hezbollah

Hezbollah has named two individuals who it says are Lebanese citizens held in Israeli jails.

- [Yehia Skaff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yehia_Skaff) is accused of participating in the 1978 [Coastal Road massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_Road_massacre). According to Hezbollah, several former Lebanese detainees said they have seen him in an Israeli prison. Israeli sources said he was killed in the massacre with his body never found.

  [^cite_note-5]

- Ali Faratan is a fisherman.

  [^cite_note-6]

  &#x20;Hezbollah gave no reason for his alleged detention. Israel denies holding him.

[Hezbollah](13919.md) demanded the release of [Samir Kuntar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samir_Kuntar), [Nissim Nasser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nissim_Nasser), and Skaff at the time of the [2004 Israel-Hezbollah prisoner exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_MIA_prisoner_exchanges#Elchanan_Tenenbaum,_Adi_Avitan,_Benyamin_Avraham_&_Omar_Sawaid). On June 1, 2008, Nasser was released in exchange for the body parts of Israeli soldiers killed during the 2006 Lebanon war.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Kuntar was released in a prisoner exchange on July 16, 2008.

In addition to the above, the following people were listed in a letter[^cite_note-letter-10] dated March 2004 to the [United Nations](31769.md) [Commission on Human Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Commission_on_Human_Rights) by Lebanon's UN permanent representative as having been arrested during the 1982 Israeli occupation of Lebanon and still missing. The allegations have been denied by Israel.

- Mooussa El Sheikh Selman, from [Maarka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maarka?action=edit\&redlink=1) village. Said to have been arrested on June 8, 1982, and wounded when transported by an [ICRC](5023832.md) staffer from the rest house of [Tyre](30796.md) to the field hospital of the Israeli army near [Mealliya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mealliya?action=edit\&redlink=1). His fate remains unknown.

- Bilal Samadi, said to have been seized on June 4, 1982, with five other persons on the bridge of the [Awali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awali) River following an Israeli ambush.

- Ibrahim Zein El Din, a teacher in \[Baaklin] Secondary School. Said to have been seized by the Israeli forces at his fiancée’s house on October 8, 1982, and transferred to the Israeli intelligence center located then in [Ras El Jabal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_El_Jabal?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Aley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aley) ([Mount Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Lebanon)). Two of his friends, Nazih Abou Ajram and Mounir Chami, who were arrested later on, professed to having seen him chained and naked with torture marks on his body.

- Mohamad Said El Jarrar, from [Chebaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebaa) village. Said to have been seized in 1979 by Israeli forces and imprisoned in [Tal Nahas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tal_Nahas?action=edit\&redlink=1) camp, then transferred to Israeli intelligence in [Metlli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metlli?action=edit\&redlink=1). Toufic Fakhouri, one of the released detainees, has allegedly said to have recognized Mohamad El Jarrar in 1987, in one of the Israeli detention centers located in [Ramallah](44132.md).

- Jamil Amhaz. Said to have been kidnapped by the Israeli forces in 1984. According to his family, he is imprisoned in the Chateh jail in Israel. Israel denies this.

- Mohamad Ali Hawa. Said to have been arrested by the Israeli forces in 1984 on [Bater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bater,_Lebanon?action=edit\&redlink=1) crossing in [Jezzine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jezzine) (Lebanon) and taken to the [Aber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aber_\(Israel\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) center. Allegedly, his mother saw him wounded with a bleeding leg.

- Mohamad Ali Gharib is said to have been kidnapped on August 12, 1984, by the Israeli forces on the Bater crossing in Jezzine. He was allegedly transferred to the [Homsieh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homsieh?action=edit\&redlink=1) hill then to Israel. His family reported that the freed detainee Ali Noura has reported to have seen him in [Atlit Prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlit_Prison?action=edit\&redlink=1) in 1985.

- Hassan Ramez Ballout was said to have been arrested in 1984, in his hometown [Kfarmelky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kfarmelky?action=edit\&redlink=1) by the Israeli forces. Ballout allegedly stayed in the Jezzine barracks for eight months and was told to be wounded with infections in his leg. His fate remains unknown.

- Maher Kassir from [Deir Kanoun el Naher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deir_Kanoun_el_Naher?action=edit\&redlink=1) village. He was allegedly kidnapped by the Israeli forces on June 17, 1984, in the area of the [Sciences University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sciences_University?action=edit\&redlink=1) located in [Chweifat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chweifat?action=edit\&redlink=1), southern Beirut. His fate is unknown.

- Mohamad El Abouchi from [Mina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Mina,_Lebanon) in northern Lebanon. He was said to have been kidnapped in 1990. His family asserts that he is detained in Israel. Israel denies this.

- Jammal Habba from [Sidon](28366.md), southern Lebanon was said to have been kidnapped at the [Barbarra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbarra?action=edit\&redlink=1) checkpoint in 1982. His family asserts that he is in Israel. Israel denies this.

- Samir El Khorfan, a soldier in the Lebanese army is said to have been kidnapped in the [Majdalyoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majdalyoun?action=edit\&redlink=1) area on October 30, 1983. His fate remains unknown.

- Saadeldine Ahmad Kheir, a Lebanese citizen who was taken in 1982 in Khalda. His fate remains unknown.
