**Tocumbo** is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Mexico) in the [Mexican](3966054.md) [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_state) of [Michoacán](195805.md). The municipality has an area of 506.85 square kilometres (195.70 sq mi) (0.86% of the surface of the state) and is bordered to the north by [Tingüindín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ting%C3%BCind%C3%ADn), to the east by [Los Reyes](13641959.md), to south by the state of [Jalisco](16020434.md), and to the west by [Cotija](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotija,_Michoac%C3%A1n). The municipality had a population of 8,820 inhabitants according to the 2005 census.[^cite_note-1] Its municipal seat is the city of the same name. The economy of the municipality is mostly based on agriculture and ranching.
