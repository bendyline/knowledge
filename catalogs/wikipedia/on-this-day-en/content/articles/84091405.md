**National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority** (**NDRRMA**; [Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): राष्ट्रिय विपद् जोखिम न्यूनीकरण तथा व्यवस्थापन प्राधिकरण) is a central resource body of [Government of Nepal](19233409.md) responsible for coordinating and implementing disaster risk reduction and management activities in the country.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] Established under the Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act, 2017, the authority operates under the [Ministry of Home Affairs](41156390.md) and is responsible for [disaster preparedness, risk assessment, mitigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disaster_risk_reduction), response, recovery and rehabilitation. NDRRMA also coordinates disaster-related activities among [federal](19233409.md), [provincial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Nepal) and [local governments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_in_Nepal) and maintains national disaster information and early warning systems.[^cite_note-3]
