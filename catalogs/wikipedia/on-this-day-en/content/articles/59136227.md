# Maslova Pristan

**Maslova Pristan** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Ма́слова При́стань) is an [urban-type settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) in [Shebekinsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shebekinsky_District) of [Belgorod Oblast](478857.md), Russia. Population: 5,916 ([2021 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2021Census-1] 5,819 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2010Census-2] 5,597 ([2002 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Russian_census));[^cite_note-PopCensus-3] 4,052 ([1989 Soviet census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Soviet_census)).[^cite_note-Census1989-4]
