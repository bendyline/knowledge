# Human-to-human transmission

&#x20;**Human-to-human transmission** (HHT) is an [epidemiologic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epidemiologic) [vector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_\(epidemiology\)),[^cite_note-9] especially in case the disease is borne by individuals known as [superspreaders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superspreaders). In these cases, the [basic reproduction number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_reproduction_number) of the virus, which is the average number of additional people that a single case will infect without any preventative measures, can be as high as 203.9.[^cite_note-li20-10][^cite_note-riou20b-11] **Interhuman transmission** is a synonym for HHT.[^cite_note-meyer91-12]

The [World Health Organization](33583.md) designation of a [pandemic](24255.md) hinges on the demonstrable fact that there is sustained HHT in two regions of the world.[^cite_note-mfce-13]

## Synopsis

Relevant [microbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microbe) may be [viruses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viruses), [bacteria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacteria), or [fungi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fungi), and they may be spread through breathing, talking, coughing, sneezing, spraying of liquids, toilet flushing or any activities which generate [aerosol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerosol) particles or droplets or generate [fomites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fomites), such as raising of dust.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECook2013208-14][^cite_note-abad94-15]

Transfer efficiency depends not only on surface, but also on pathogen type. For example, [avian influenza](442916.md) survives on both porous and non-porous materials for 144 hours.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECook2013208-14]

The microbes may also be transmitted by poor use of cutlery or improper sanitation of dishes or bedlinen. Particularly problematic are toilet practices, which lead to the [fecal–oral route](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fecal%E2%80%93oral_route). [STDs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexually_transmitted_disease) are by definition spread through this vector.
