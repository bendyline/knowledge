***Myanmar Alin*** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): မြန်မာ့အလင်း; also known as ***Myanma Alinn***) is a state-run [Burmese language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language) daily [newspaper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newspaper) and the longest running newspaper in circulation in [Myanmar](19457.md). The paper is considered to be the official mouthpiece of the government of [Myanmar](19457.md).[^cite_note-BBC_News_2011-1][^cite_note-Lay_Thanegi_2018_p._122-2][^cite_note-Seekins_2017_p._591-3]

## History

*Myanmar Alin* was founded as a magazine by U [Shwe Kyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shwe_Kyu)[^cite_note-Tun_Mranʻ_mā_Nuiṅʻ_ṅaṃ_Samuiṅʻʺ_Koʻmarhanʻ_2004_p.-4] ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): ဦးရွှေကြူး) and published by [Ledi Pandita U Maung Gyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ledi_Pandita_U_Maung_Gyi) in 1914 during the [British colonial era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_rule_in_Burma) in Yangon. The paper was known for its anti-colonialist stance before [World War II](32927.md). The paper was nationalised in 1969 by General [Ne Win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ne_Win)'s military government.

## Content

The front and back pages of all [Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language) newspapers are dedicated almost entirely to government-related news. Most domestic news comes from the official government news bureau, [Myanmar News Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar_News_Agency) (MNA). Papers are read not for the news but for advertisements and announcements like weddings and obituaries.

## Broadcasting

Myanmar Radio and Television broadcasts [free-to-air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free-to-air) [terrestrial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrestrial_television) [television stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_station) such as [MRTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRTV_\(TV_network\)), [MRTV News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRTV_\(TV_network\)), [MITV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar_International), MRTV NRC, MRTV Parliament, MRTV Farmer, and MRTV Sport.
