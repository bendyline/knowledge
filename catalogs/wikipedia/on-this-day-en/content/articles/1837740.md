**Wales** ([Welsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_language): *Cymru* [\[ˈkəmrɨ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Welsh) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/3/32/Cymru.ogg/Cymru.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/3/32/Cymru.ogg/Cymru.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) was a [constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency) of the [European Parliament](9581.md). It elected 4 [MEPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Members_of_the_European_Parliament) using the [D'Hondt method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D'Hondt_method) of [party-list proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party-list_proportional_representation),[^cite_note-1] until the [UK exit](41688778.md) from the [European Union](9317.md) on 31 January 2020.

## Boundaries

The constituency corresponded to the boundaries of [Wales](69894.md), one of the [four countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countries_of_the_United_Kingdom) of the United Kingdom.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## History

It was formed as a result of the [European Parliamentary Elections Act 1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Parliamentary_Elections_Act_1999), replacing a number of single-member constituencies. These were [Mid and West Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid_and_West_Wales_\(European_Parliament_constituency\)), [North Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Wales_\(European_Parliament_constituency\)), [South Wales Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Wales_Central_\(European_Parliament_constituency\)), [South Wales East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Wales_East_\(European_Parliament_constituency\)), and [South Wales West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Wales_West_\(European_Parliament_constituency\)).
