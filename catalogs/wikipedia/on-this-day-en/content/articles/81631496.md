The **78th Primetime Emmy Awards** honored the best in American [prime time](24973.md) television programming from June 1, 2025, until May 31, 2026, as chosen by the [Academy of Television Arts & Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Television_Arts_&_Sciences).[^cite_note-Rules_and_Procedures-2] The awards ceremony was held live on September 14, 2026, at the [Peacock Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_Theater) in [Downtown Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Los_Angeles), California, and was preceded by the [78th Primetime Creative Arts Emmy Awards](83647131.md) on September 5 and 6. The ceremony was produced by Jesse Collins Entertainment and broadcast in the United States by [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC) and [Peacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_\(streaming_service\)).[^cite_note-Airdate-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-Calendar-5][^cite_note-6] NBC's *[Law & Order: Special Victims Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_&_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit)* star [Mariska Hargitay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariska_Hargitay) hosted the event.[^cite_note-Hargitay-7]

## Ceremony information

Mariska Hargitay in 2025.

[Mariska Hargitay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariska_Hargitay) hosted the ceremony.

The nominations for the 78th Primetime Emmy Awards were announced on July 8, 2026, at the Television Academy's Wolf Theatre in [North Hollywood, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Hollywood,_Los_Angeles), hosted by actor [Jeff Hiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Hiller) and actress [Liza Colón-Zayas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liza_Col%C3%B3n-Zayas), along with Television Academy chair [Cris Abrego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cris_Abrego).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] The nominations for [Outstanding Variety Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Variety_Series) and [Outstanding Reality Competition Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Reality_Competition_Program) were announced three hours before the rest of the categories.[^cite_note-15] On July 7, actress [Mariska Hargitay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariska_Hargitay) was announced as the ceremony's host.[^cite_note-Hargitay-7] [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC) and the [Television Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Television_Arts_&_Sciences) considered talent both within and outside of [NBCUniversal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBCUniversal) for the role, but ultimately hired NBC's *[Law & Order: Special Victims Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_&_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit)*'s lead actress in honor of the network's 100th anniversary. This was the first Primetime Emmys ceremony that the host is not a comedian, comedy performer, reality host or television personality since [Angela Lansbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Lansbury) in [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/45th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards), and the first time that the event was hosted by a woman since [Jane Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Lynch) in [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/63rd_Primetime_Emmy_Awards).[^cite_note-Hargitay-7]

Jesse Collins, Dionne Harmon, and Jeannae Rouzan-Clay of Jesse Collins Entertainment served as [executive producers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_producer) of the telecast.[^cite_note-16] To avoid conflicts with the network's broadcast of *[NBC Sunday Night Football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC_Sunday_Night_Football)*, the event was scheduled on a Monday night, instead of a Sunday, which NBC had previously done when they aired the awards ceremony every four years since 2014.[^cite_note-Airdate-3]

### Category and rule changes

In January 2026, the Television Academy announced plans to present its first new major award in nearly twenty years with the creation of the Legacy Award, which will be presented annually to television programs that have made a "profound and lasting impact" on audiences, and remain relevant to culture, society and the industry.[^cite_note-17] Additionally, [Outstanding Scripted Variety Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Scripted_Variety_Series) and [Outstanding Talk Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Talk_Series) were merged into Outstanding Variety Series. The new Variety Series category is tracked, meaning the number of nominees from each format is proportional to the number of submissions, and it became an area award where voters select any and all nominees they believe deserve an award, with the potential for multiple winners.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20]

In July, the Television Academy voted to move five categories to the Creative Arts ceremonies on the preceding weekend: [Outstanding Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actor_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie), [Supporting Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actress_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie), [Directing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Directing_for_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie), and [Writing for a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Writing_for_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie) and [Writing for a Variety Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Writing_for_a_Variety_Series). Abrego justified the changes as a way to add other elements to the show such as musical performances or comedy segments, with the moves selected to "be as balanced and as equitable as possible".[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] A joint statement from the [Directors Guild of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directors_Guild_of_America), the [Writers Guild of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writers_Guild_of_America), and [SAG-AFTRA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAG-AFTRA) criticized the changes and commented, "The decision to eliminate these categories from the prime-time broadcast devalues the contributions of the talented people the Academy is meant to honor."[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] "The move doesn't make much sense. All they've done is piss off a lot of the people and stars they are trying to court and attend their ceremony. I think this will blow up in their face", one outraged awards strategist told *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\))*.[^cite_note-27] Additionally, [Outstanding Variety Special (Live)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Variety_Special_\(Live\)) was shifted back to the Creative Arts ceremonies, leaving 19 categories for the main ceremony (a decrease from last year's 26).[^cite_note-28]
