**Azna** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): ازنا)[^cite_note-3] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Azna_County\)) of [Azna County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azna_County), [Lorestan](433072.md) province, [Iran](14653.md), serving as capital of both the county and the district.[^cite_note-Azna,_Pol-e_Dokhtar,_and_Selseleh_Counties_Establishment-4]

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 37,645 in 9,178 households.[^cite_note-2006_Lorestan_Province-5] The following census in 2011 counted 40,145 people in 11,128 households.[^cite_note-2011_Lorestan_Province-6] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 47,489 people in 14,208 households.[^cite_note-2016_Lorestan_Province-1]

## Geography

In the [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains), the city is 133  km. east of [Khoramabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khoramabad) and 75 km. south of [Arak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arak,_Iran). The city is en route [Esfahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esfahan)–[Khuzestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan) and is connected to the railway network of the country.

### Climate

Azna has a [dry-summer continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate)(*Dsa*) in [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) with precipitation being higher in winter months than summer. Summers are dry and hot with very low precipitation from June to September. Winters are very cold and relatively wet.

## Civilian casualties of 2025-2026 Iranian protests in Azna, Lorestan

Beginning on 28 December 2025, [mass demonstrations](81976180.md) erupted across multiple cities in [Iran](14653.md) amid a deepening [economic crisis](78620967.md) and widespread dissatisfaction with the [government](50913538.md). While initially sparked by frustration over skyrocketing inflation, rising food prices, and the severe depreciation of the [Iranian rial](708656.md), the protests quickly evolved into a broader movement demanding an end to the Islamic Republic's rule.[^cite_note-9]

On 1 January 2026, [Reza Moradi Abdolvand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Reza_Moradi_Abdolvand), an 18-year-old citizen from Azna, fell into a coma after being shot directly by forces of the [Islamic Republic of Iran](14653.md), and died on 5 January 2026. He was the sixth civilian to die in Azna during the 2025–2026 Iranian protests.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

[Hengaw](72014392.md) Organization of Human Rights reported the killing of three protestors, including a child in the city of Azna, in Iran’s Lorestan province, after Iranian forces opened fire.[^cite_note-\:0-12][^cite_note-\:1-13] Hengaw Organization for Human Rights confirmed that the victims were Mostafa (surname unknown), 15, [Shayan Asadollahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Shayan_Asadollahi), 30, and Vahab Musavi, all residents of Azna. They were killed on the evening of Thursday, 1 January 2026, when Iranian forces opened fire on demonstrators.[^cite_note-\:0-12][^cite_note-\:1-13] The killing of Ahmadreza Amani was also reported on the same date.[^cite_note-\:2-14] Amani was struck in the chest by live ammunition fired by state forces. Amani, a law graduate and trainee lawyer, was rushed to hospital following the shooting but died shortly after arrival due to severe internal bleeding.[^cite_note-\:2-14]
