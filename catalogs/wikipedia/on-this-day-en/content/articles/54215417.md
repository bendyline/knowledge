# List of openly LGBTQ state leaders

[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavier_Espot_Zamora)

[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgars_Rink%C4%93vi%C4%8Ds)

[](58754918.md)

Incumbent openly LGBTQ state leaders:&#x20;

- [Xavier Espot Zamora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavier_Espot_Zamora), [Prime Minister of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Andorra)

- [Edgars Rinkēvičs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgars_Rink%C4%93vi%C4%8Ds), [President of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Latvia)

- [Rob Jetten](58754918.md), [Prime Minister of the Netherlands](273726.md)

Politicians openly identifying themselves as members of the [LGBTQ community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_community) have served as national leaders in ten [sovereign states](23604120.md). Of those countries, two have had an openly LGBTQ [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state), who serves as the country's public [persona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persona). Eight other sovereign states have had an openly LGBTQ [head of government](37570.md), who is the highest official in that state's government. No country has had both an openly LGBTQ head of state and openly LGBTQ head of government, nor more than one openly LGBTQ national leader. Additionally, no sovereign state outside of [Europe](9239.md) has had an openly LGBTQ national leader.

[Jóhanna Sigurðardóttir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%B3hanna_Sigur%C3%B0ard%C3%B3ttir) became the first openly LGBTQ+ politician to serve as a country's head of government upon the start of her tenure as [Prime Minister of Iceland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Iceland) in 2009. Two years later, [Elio Di Rupo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elio_Di_Rupo) became the first openly gay head of government upon becoming [Prime Minister of Belgium](204622.md). Upon assuming the office of a [captain regent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_regent) of [San Marino](27248.md) on 1 April 2022, [Paolo Rondelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Rondelli) became the first openly LGBTQ+ head of state.

[Gay men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay_men) have been the majority of openly LGBTQ+ national leaders, with eight of the ten politicians in this role identifying as such. The other two openly LGBTQ+ heads of government identify as [lesbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesbian). Other identities in the LGBTQ community, including [bisexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bisexual), [transgender](19904525.md), and [intersex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersex), have therefore not been openly represented in world heads of state and heads of government.

The totals for this list include only openly identifying members of the LGBTQ community who have [come out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_out) prior to the end of their term as head of state or head of government of a [United Nations member state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_member_state). Therefore, LGBTQ national leaders who were [closeted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closeted) or [outed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outed) during their tenure as a national leader are not included. Moreover, openly LGBTQ politicians who have served in other positions, such as [acting president](352134.md), [deputy prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_prime_minister), and leaders of [subnational entities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subnational_entities), are excluded from this list.
