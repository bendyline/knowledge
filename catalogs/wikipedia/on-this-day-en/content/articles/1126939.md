The **Italian Navy** ([Italian](14708.md): *Marina Militare*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Military Navy'; abbreviated as **MM**) is one of the four branches of [Italian Armed Forces](14705.md) and was formed in 1946 from what remained of the *[Regia Marina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regia_Marina)* (Royal Navy) after World War II. The Italian Navy has a strength of approximately 29,300 active personnel, with 184 vessels and 70 aircraft in service.[^cite_note-rapporto2023-2] It is considered a multiregional and a [blue-water navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue-water_navy).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-Coffey-5]

## History

### Before and during World War II

Main article: [Regia Marina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regia_Marina)

The *[Regia Marina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regia_Marina)* was formed on 17 March 1861, after the [proclamation of the Kingdom of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proclamation_of_the_Kingdom_of_Italy).[^cite_note-6] The Italian Navy assumed its present name after the Italian monarchy was abolished following a popular referendum held on 2 June 1946.

### After World War II

At the end of its five-year involvement in [World War II](32927.md), Italy was a devastated nation. After the end of hostilities, the *Regia Marina* – which at the beginning of the war was the fourth largest navy in the world, with a mix of modernised and new battleships – started a long and complex rebuilding process. The combat contributions of the Italian naval forces after the signing of the armistice with the Allies on 8 September 1943, and the subsequent cooperation agreement on 23 September 1943, left the Regia Marina in a poor condition, with much of its infrastructure and bases unusable and its ports mined and blocked by sunken ships. However, a large number of its naval units had survived the war, albeit in a low efficiency state, which was due to the conflict and the age of many vessels. The vessels that remained were:

- 5 battleships

- 10 cruisers

- 10 destroyers

- 20 frigates

- 20 corvettes

- 50 fast coastal patrol units

- 50 [minesweepers](217118.md)

- 19 amphibious operations vessels

- 5 [school ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_ship)

- 1 support ship and plane transport

### The peace treaty

The [peace treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Peace_with_Italy,_1947) signed on 10 February 1947 in Paris was onerous for Regia Marina. Apart from territorial and material losses, also the following restrictions were imposed:

- A ban on owning, building or experimenting with atomic weapons, self-propulsion projectiles or relative launchers, etc.

- A ban on owning battleships, aircraft carriers, submarines and amphibious assault units.

- A ban on operating military installations on the islands of [Pantelleria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantelleria), [Pianosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pianosa) and on the archipelago of the [Pelagian Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pelagian_Islands).



Aircraft carrier [*Aquila*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_aircraft_carrier_Aquila) just before being scrapped in [La Spezia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Spezia), 1951

The treaty also ordered Italy to put the following ships at the disposals of the victorious nations United States, [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), the United Kingdom, France, [Greece](12108.md), [Yugoslavia](34244.md) and [Albania](738.md) as war compensation:

- 3 battleships: *Giulio Cesare*, *Italia*, *Vittorio Veneto*;

- 5 cruisers: *Emanuele Filiberto Duca d'Aosta*, *Attilio Regolo*, *Scipione Africano*, *Eugenio di Savoia* and *Eritrea*;

- 7 destroyers, 5 of the [Soldati class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soldati-class_destroyer) and *Augusto Riboty* and *Alfredo Oriani*;

- 6 minesweepers: like *Aliseo* and *Fortunale*;

- 8 submarines: 3 of the *Acciaio* class;

- 1 sailing school ship: *Cristoforo Colombo*.

Great changes in the international political situation, which were developing into the Cold War, convinced the United Kingdom and United States to discontinue the transfer of Italy's capital ships as war reparations. Some had already been dismantled in [La Spezia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Spezia) between 1948 and 1955, including the aircraft carrier [*Aquila*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_aircraft_carrier_Aquila). However, the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) demanded the surrender of the battleship *Giulio Cesare* and other naval units designated for transfer. The [cruisers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruiser) *Attilio Regolo* and *Scipione Africano* became the French *Chateaurenault* and *Guichen*, while *[*Eugenio di Savoia*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_cruiser_Eugenio_di_Savoia)* became the Greek *Elli*. After break up and transfers, only a small part of the fleet remained to be recommissioned into the Marina. As Western attention turned to the Soviets and the [Mediterranean Sea](19006.md), Italian seas became one of the main sites of confrontation between the two superpowers, and Italy's geographical position gave it renewed naval importance within NATO.



The [carrier *Cavour*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_aircraft_carrier_Cavour) in the [Gulf of Oman](12896.md), 2013

With the new elections in 1946, the Kingdom of Italy became a republic, and the Regia Marina took the name of *Marina Militare* (lit. 'Military Navy'). As the [Marshall Plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_Plan) began to rebuild Italy and Europe was rapidly being divided into two geopolitically antagonistic blocs, Italy began talks with the United States to guarantee adequate security considerations. The US government in [Washington](108956.md) wished to keep its own installations on the [Italian Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Peninsula) and relaxed the Treaty restrictions by including Italy in the Mutual Defense Assistance Programme (MDAP). On 4 April 1949, Italy joined the [North Atlantic Treaty Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Atlantic_Treaty_Organization) (NATO) and, in order for the navy to contribute actively in the organization, the Treaty restrictions were definitively repealed by the end of 1951, with the consent of all of Western nations.

Within NATO, the Italian Navy was assigned combat control of the [Adriatic Sea](23275478.md) and [Strait of Otranto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Otranto), as well as the defence of the naval routes through the [Tyrrhenian Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrrhenian_Sea). To ensure these tasks a *Studio sul potenziamento della Marina italiana in relazione al Patto Atlantico* (Study on the development of the Italian Navy with reference to the Atlantic Pact) was undertaken, which researched the structures and the methods for the development of the navy.

## Naval ensign



Naval ensign of Italy

The [ensign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ensign_\(flag\)) of the Italian Navy is the [flag of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Italy) bearing the coat of arms of the Italian Navy. The shield's quarters refer to the four Medieval Italian [Maritime Republics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_Republics):

- 1st quarter: on red, a golden winged lion (the [lion of St. Mark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lion_of_St._Mark)) wielding a sword ([Republic of Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Venice))

- 2nd quarter: on white field, red cross, the [Saint George's Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_George's_Cross) ([Republic of Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Genoa))

- 3rd quarter: on blue field, white [Maltese cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maltese_cross) ([Republic of Amalfi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Amalfi))

- 4th quarter: on red field, white [Pisan cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisan_cross) ([Republic of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Pisa))

The coat of arms is surmounted by a golden crown, which distinguishes military vessels from those of the merchant navy.

The crown, *corona [rostrata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostrata)*, was proposed in 1939 as a conjectural link to the [Roman navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_navy) by Admiral [Domenico Cavagnari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domenico_Cavagnari), then a member of the [Chamber of Fasces and Corporations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Fasces_and_Corporations) in the Fascist government. In the proposal, Admiral Cavagnari wrote that "in order to recall the common origin \[of the Navy] from the Roman mariners, the Insignia will be surmounted by the towered Crown with *[rostra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostrum_\(ship\))*, the emblem of honour and valour the [Roman Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Senate) awarded to the leaders of naval victories, conquerors of lands and cities across the seas".

A further difference is that St. Mark's lion, symbolising the Republic of Venice, does not hold the gospel in its paw (as it does on [the civil ensign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_the_Republic_of_Venice), where the book is open at the words "*Pax tibi Marce, evangelista meus*", meaning "peace to you, Mark, my evangelist") and is wielding a sword instead: such an image is consistent with the pictorial tradition from Venetian history, in which the book is shown open during peacetime and closed during wartime.

### Coast Guard

The [Corps of the Port Captaincies – Coast Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corps_of_the_Port_Captaincies_%E2%80%93_Coast_Guard) (*Corpo delle Capitanerie di porto – Guardia costiera*) is part of the [coast guard](146790.md) forces of Italy and is part of the Italian Navy under the control of the [Ministry of Infrastructures and Transports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Infrastructures_and_Transports_\(Italy\)), the [Ministry of the Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Environment_\(Italy\)), the [Ministry of Agricultural, Food and Forestry Policies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Agricultural,_Food_and_Forestry_Policies_\(Italy\)), as well as the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(Italy\)). In Italy, it is commonly known as simply the *Guardia costiera* or *Capitaneria di Porto*. The Coast Guard has approximately 11,000 staff. [^cite_note-CG-17]

### Corps

The Italian Navy is divided into six corps (by precedence):

- *Corpo di stato maggiore* – Staff Officers Corps (SM): [line officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_officer)

- *Corpo del genio della Marina* – [Naval Engineers Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corps_of_Naval_Engineering) (GM)&#x20;

  - *Specialità genio navale* – Marine engineering branch (GM/GN): [engineer officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer_officer)

  - *Specialità armi navali* – Naval ordnance branch (GM/AN): weapon engineer officers

  - *Specialità genio infrastrutture* – Infrastructure engineering (GM/INFR): [civil engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_engineer) officers

- *Corpo sanitario militare marittimo* – Maritime Military Medical Corps: (MD) for medics, (FM) for pharmacists

- *Corpo di commissariato militare marittimo* – Military Maritime Supply Corps (CM): [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purser), [paymaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paymaster), [legal executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_executive), [supply](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supply_officer), [logistics officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logistics_officer)

- *Corpo delle capitanerie di porto* – [Port Captaincies Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corps_of_the_Port_Captaincies_%E2%80%93_Coast_Guard) (CP): the search and rescue corp for Italy

- *Corpo degli equipaggi militari marittimi* – Military Maritime Crews Corps (CEMM)

- 9× [maritime patrol aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_patrol_aircraft) (currently 4 x [ATR 72](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATR_72)MP in service)

- 30× heavy helicopters (currently 22 x [AW101](505833.md) in service)

- 12× light utility helicopters, a new acquisition of [AW169](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AgustaWestland_AW169) helicopters

- 16× tactical [unmanned aerial vehicles](58900.md) (currently 10 x [ScanEagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Insitu_ScanEagle) in service)

For the [San Marco Marine Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Marco_Marine_Brigade), the Navy plans to acquire following assets:[^cite_note-LDIS-23]

- 72× [Iveco SuperAV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iveco_SuperAV) amphibious 8x8 combat vehicles (36 vehicles ordered December 2022)

  [^cite_note-34]

- 90× [VTMM Orso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GFF4) armored 4x4 vehicles
