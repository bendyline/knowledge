**Manay** ([\[mɐnaɪ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Tagalog), also spelled **Man-ay**), officially the **Municipality of Manay** ([Mandaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandaya_language): *Banwa nang Manay*), is a first class [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Davao Oriental](296986.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 39,271 people.[^cite_note-5] The epicenter of the [2025 Davao Oriental earthquakes](81304378.md) was recorded off the coast of Manay.

## Geography

### Climate

Manay has a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) (Af) with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
