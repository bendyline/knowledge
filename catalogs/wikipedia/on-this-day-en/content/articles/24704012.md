The **minister of foreign affairs of Iran** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): وزیر امور خارجه ایران, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Vazir Amor Kharjeh Iran*) is the head of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](1166971.md) of [Iran](14653.md) and a member of the [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Iran). The office is currently held by [Abbas Araghchi](21951274.md) since 2024.

## Duties

Functions of Minister have largely been moved to [Supreme Leader](36814344.md)'s [Supreme Strategic Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Strategic_Council?action=edit\&redlink=1) for [foreign relations](64709.md).[^cite_note-1]
