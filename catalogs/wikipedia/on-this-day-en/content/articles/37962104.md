The **Americana Music Honors & Awards** is the marquee event for the [Americana Music Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americana_Music_Association). Beginning in 2002, the Americana Music Association honors distinguished members of the music community. Six member-voted awards and several Lifetime Achievement Awards are handed out while over 2000 artists, music-loving fans and entertainment industry executives look on.

The Honors & Awards have featured a variety of musical events, including [Johnny Cash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Cash) and [June Carter Cash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_Carter_Cash)'s last live performance together.[^cite_note-Freedom_Forum-1] Artists such as [The Carolina Chocolate Drops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolina_Chocolate_Drops), [Robert Plant & His Band of Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Plant_and_the_Band_of_Joy), [Steve Earle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Earle) & [Allison Moorer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allison_Moorer), [Rosanne Cash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosanne_Cash) and [John Fogerty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Fogerty) have also performed for the event, and special appearances by artists like [Kris Kristofferson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kris_Kristofferson) (2003), [Loretta Lynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loretta_Lynn) (2004/2014), [Billy Bob Thornton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Bob_Thornton) (2005), [Bonnie Raitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonnie_Raitt) (2012/2016), [Dwight Yoakam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwight_Yoakam) (2016) and [George Strait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Strait) (2016) demonstrate the prestige of the genre and awards show.

The Honors & Awards show was first broadcast on national television in 2005. Also in 2005, [SiriusXM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SiriusXM), [BBC2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC2), and [Voice of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_of_America) began broadcasting the show on radio. [PBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PBS) began airing an abbreviated version during [Austin City Limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_City_Limits) in 2011.

The Americana Honors & Awards Show returned to the historic [Ryman Auditorium](1034005.md) on September 12, 2012. It was once again hosted by [Jim Lauderdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Lauderdale), while the All Star Band was led by [Buddy Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddy_Miller). The 2012 Honors & Awards show was aired live on [AXS TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AXS_TV)[^cite_note-AXS-TV-2] and later aired on [Austin City Limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_City_Limits) on [PBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PBS)[^cite_note-PBS-3] and replayed on AXS TV. Lauderdale stepped down from hosting duties after the 2017 ceremony and the 2018–19 Honors & Awards were hosted by [The Milk Carton Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Milk_Carton_Kids).[^cite_note-4] The show did not include a host in 2021.

## Nomination process

The eligibility period for the Americana Music Association's “Of The Year” categories typically runs from April 1 through March 31 of the following year. Only active members of the Americana Music Association may participate in the nomination and election process. The winners are announced at the annual Americana Music Honors & Awards show.[^cite_note-AMA_Awards-5]

Below are the Criteria for each of the following categories:[^cite_note-AMA_Awards-5]

- Album of the Year – Americana albums released during the eligibility period.

- Song of the Year – Americana songs released from Albums released during the eligibility period.

- Artist of the Year – An Artist who has had an exemplary year based on overall recognition and activity whether through album, song, radio airplay, publicity, touring, or other criteria within the eligibility period.

- Emerging Act of the Year – Solo artist, duo, or group having an exemplary year and first blush of national public awareness through overall recognition and activity, whether through album, song, radio airplay, sales, publicity, touring, or other criteria during eligibility period who has not previously been nominated in any category.

- Duo/Group of the Year – Duo/Group – have had an exemplary year based on overall recognition and activity, whether through album, song, radio airplay, publicity, touring, or other criteria within the eligibility period.

- Instrumentalist of the Year : An award given to a collaborative musician having an exemplary year within the eligibility period based on overall recognition and activity, whether through work on an album, song, touring, or other criteria.

## UK Americana Music Association Awards

The inaugural UK AMA awards were presented by the UK Americana Music Association were held at [St John at Hackney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John_at_Hackney) in [London](17867.md) on 3 February 2016 and was hosted by [Bob Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Harris_\(radio_presenter\)) and [Ethan Johns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Johns).[^cite_note-30] The second annual UK Americana awards were held on 2 February 2017 and the third awards were held on 1 February 2018.[^cite_note-31] The fifth awards were held on 30 January 2020.[^cite_note-32] The 2021 ceremony was held virtually on 29 January due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-33] The 2022 ceremony was held at the [Hackney Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hackney_Empire) and was hosted by Harris and Baylen Leonard on 26 January 2023.[^cite_note-34]

## Australian Americana Music Association Honours

The inaugural annual Australian American Music Honours Night, a celebration of Australian roots music, was held on October 2, 2017. The ceremony, which was televised on the [Country Music Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_Music_Channel) in early 2018, featured performances from many of Australia's top roots performers alongside [Valerie June](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valerie_June) and [Old Crow Medicine Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Crow_Medicine_Show) who acted as "US Americana Ambassadors". The inaugural ceremony also featured the presentation of two honours.[^cite_note-38] The second event was held on October 11, 2018, and featured [Margo Price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margo_Price) and [Joshua Hedley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joshua_Hedley) as Americana Ambassadors.[^cite_note-39]
