A **volunteer military system** or **all-volunteer military system** (**AVMS**) is a military service system that maintains the military only with applicants without conscription. A country may offer attractive pay and benefits through [military recruitment](3135672.md) to attract potential recruits. Many countries with volunteer militaries reserve the right to renew conscription in the event of an emergency. The [Indian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Army) is the world's largest standing volunteer army.[^cite_note-1]

In recent decades, the trend among numerous countries has been to move from conscription to all-volunteer military forces. One significant example is in [France](5843419.md), which has historically been [the first to introduce modern conscription](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lev%C3%A9e_en_masse) and whose model was followed by many other countries in Europe and elsewhere around the world.
