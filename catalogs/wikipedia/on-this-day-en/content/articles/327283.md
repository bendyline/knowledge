# State visit



*The Review in Windsor Great Park in Honour of the Shah of Persia, 24 June 1873* ([Nicholas Chevalier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Chevalier), 1877). [Naser al-Din Shah Qajar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naser_al-Din_Shah_Qajar), the first Iranian monarch to make state visits to Europe, visited [Queen Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Victoria) twice.

A **state visit** is a formal visit by the [head](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of a [sovereign country](23604120.md) (or [representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-general) of the head of a sovereign country) to another sovereign country, at the invitation of the head of state (or representative) of that foreign country, with the latter also acting as the [official host](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospitality) for the duration of the state visit. Speaking for the host, it is generally called a **state reception**. State visits are considered to be the highest expression of friendly [bilateral relations](429154.md) between two [sovereign states](23604120.md), and are in general characterised by an emphasis on official public ceremonies.

A less formal visit, with less emphasis on ceremonial events, can be classified in descending order of formality as an **official visit**, an **official working visit**, a **working visit**, a **guest-of-government visit**, or a **private visit**.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

In [parliamentary democracies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system), while heads of state (or their representative) may formally issue and accept invitations, they do so on the [advice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advice_\(constitutional_law\)) of their [heads of government](37570.md), who usually decide on when the invitation is to be issued or accepted in advance.

[Queen Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_II) was the most travelled head of state in world history, having made 261 official overseas visits and 96 state visits to 116 countries by the time of her [Diamond Jubilee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Jubilee_of_Elizabeth_II) in 2012.[^cite_note-3] Although she was sovereign of each of the [Commonwealth realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_realm), in practice, she usually performed full state visits as [Queen of the United Kingdom](47063.md), while the relevant [governor-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-general#Modern_Commonwealth) undertook state visits for their respective country on the sovereign's behalf. However, the Queen occasionally made some state and official visits representing one of her other Commonwealth realms.

## Components of a state visit

State visits typically involve some or all the following components (each host country has its own traditions):

- The visiting [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) (or representative) is immediately greeted upon arrival by the host (or by a lesser [official](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official) representative, if the two heads of state are to meet later at another location) and by their [ambassador](68651.md) (or other [head of mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_mission)) [accredited to the host country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_accreditation).

- A [21-gun salute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21-gun_salute) is fired in honor of the visiting head of state.

- The playing of the two [national anthems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_anthem) by a [military band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_band). The guest country's anthem is usually played first.

- A review of a military [guard of honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guard_of_honour).

- The visiting head of state (or representative) is formally introduced to senior officials/representatives of the host country and the hosting head of state is introduced to the delegation accompanying the visiting head of state.

- An exchange of gifts between the two heads of state (or representatives).

- A [state dinner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_dinner), either [white tie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_tie) or [black tie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie), is celebrated by the hosting head of state (or representative), with the visiting head of state being the guest of honour.

- A visit to the [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) of the host country, often with the visiting head of state (or representative) being invited to deliver a formal address to the assembled members of the legislature.

- High-profile visits by the visiting heads of state (or representative) to host country [landmarks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landmark) such as laying a wreath at a military shrine or [cemetery](63752.md).

- The staging of cultural events celebrating links between the two nations.



The [German presidential standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_German_flags#Presidential_standard) displayed at [Blair House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blair_House), the American presidential guest house in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md), during the visit of [Richard von Weizsäcker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_von_Weizs%C3%A4cker), [President of Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Germany). Several countries maintain guest houses used for state visits.

The visiting head of state (or their representative) is usually accompanied by a senior government minister, usually by a [foreign minister](317153.md). Behind the [diplomatic protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protocol_\(diplomacy\)), delegations made up from [trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade) organizations also accompany the visiting head of state, offer an opportunity to network and develop economic, cultural, and social links with industry leaders in the nation being visited. At the end of a state visit, the foreign head of state (or representative) traditionally issues a formal invitation to the head of state (or representative) of the nation being visited who at another time in the future, would pay a reciprocal state visit.

While the costs of a state visit are usually borne by state funds of the host country, most nations host fewer than ten state visits per year, with some as few as two. Most foreign heads of state (or their representative) will stay in the [official residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_residence) of the head of state (or representative) who is hosting the state visit, in a guest house reserved for foreign visitors, or in their own nation's [embassy](8970.md) located in the foreign nation being visited.

State visits by well-known global leaders or figures, such as the [British monarch](47063.md), the [president of the United States](24113.md) or the [pope](23056.md), often draw much publicity and large crowds. Occasionally, these include protesters.

## State visits by country

### Armenia

See also: [List of visitors to Tsitsernakaberd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_visitors_to_Tsitsernakaberd)

State visits to [Armenia](10918072.md) are held in the capital of [Yerevan](34352.md), with a welcoming ceremony usually being held at [Zvartnots International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zvartnots_International_Airport). Foreign heads of state are welcomed at the [President's Residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President's_Residence,_Yerevan) while heads of government are welcomed at the [Residence of the Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister's_Residence,_Yerevan). These visits consist of the following components:

|  | Rank of visitor | State dinner | Arrival ceremonies | Exchange of diplomatic gifts | Meeting with religious leaders | Address to the National Assembly |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prime Minister's Residence | President's Residence | Airport |  |  |  |  |  |  |
| **State visit** | head of state | Yes | No | Yes | Maybe | Yes | Yes | Maybe |
| **Official visit** | head of government | Yes | Yes | No | Maybe | Maybe | Maybe | Maybe |

Since 1991, foreign leaders who embark on visits to Armenia have paid tribute to the victims of the [Armenian genocide](140376.md) at the [Tsitsernakaberd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsitsernakaberd) complex.[^cite_note-4] During a visit to the complex, most leaders receive a tour of the museum, plant trees near the memorial, and lay wreaths at the eternal flame.

### Belgium

A state visit in Belgium starts with an inspection of the troops in front of the [Royal Palace of Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Palace_of_Brussels), whereafter the [King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Belgian_monarchs) and [Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Belgian_consorts) have a private audience with the visiting head of state and their spouse. The first day of the state visit traditionally comes to a close with a state banquet at the [Royal Palace of Laeken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Palace_of_Laeken), which is the official residence of the [King and Queen of the Belgians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Belgium).

It is customary to be awarded the Knight Grand Cross of the [Order of Leopold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Leopold_\(Belgium\)) during state visits.

### Canada

See also: [List of state and official visits by Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_state_and_official_visits_by_Canada)



The [Governor General's Foot Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_General's_Foot_Guards) mounting the [guard of honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guard_of_honour) during state visits of Italian President [Sergio Mattarella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Mattarella)

The Office of Protocol coordinates the operational aspects of state and official visits to Canada and manages all events that are related to the visit. It also defines the protocol standards for state visits of heads of state and government.[^cite_note-5]

The [Governor General's Foot Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_General's_Foot_Guards), one of two [household](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household_Division) [foot guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foot_guards), take part in state and official visits to [Ottawa](22219.md). Arrival ceremonies take place at either [Parliament Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_Hill) or [Rideau Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rideau_Hall), where the visitor will be received by the [governor general of Canada](38152.md) (for state visits) or [prime minister of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Canada) (for official visits). State visits also include a visit to the [National War Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_War_Memorial_\(Canada\)).

[State and official visits by Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_state_and_official_visits_by_Canada) are performed by the [Canadian monarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Canada) or a representative—the governor general, a [lieutenant governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_governor_\(Canada\)), or another member of [the royal family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Canada#Royal_family_and_house). The first state visit by Canada was to the United States in 1937, when the US accorded the governor general the equivalent status given to a visiting head of state.[^cite_note-GGoC-6]

Tours of Canada by the country's monarch (and other members of the royal family) are not state or official visits, as the monarch conducts [royal tours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_tours_of_Canada_by_the_Canadian_Royal_Family) in his capacity as the Canadian head of state, not as a foreign head of state. Additionally, because the Canadian sovereign is shared with 14 other [Commonwealth realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_realms), state visits are not conducted between realms, with official visits performed by the realms' respective [governor-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-general), or [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister).

### China



The [Beijing Garrison Honor Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Garrison_Honor_Guard_Battalion) with the [Order of Friendship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Friendship_\(China\)), an award occasionally given during state visits

State arrival ceremonies in [China](5405.md) take place at the East Court of the [Great Hall of the People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Hall_of_the_People) on [Tiananmen Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiananmen_Square) in [Beijing](18603746.md). The guard of honour for the ceremony is provided by the [Beijing Garrison Honor Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Garrison_Honor_Guard_Battalion), with musical accompaniment provided by the [Central Military Band of the People's Liberation Army of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Military_Band_of_the_People's_Liberation_Army_of_China).

As the military band plays the national anthems of the two countries, an artillery battery fires a 21-gun salute. After the band finishes its performance, the two leaders then inspect the guard of honour at the invitation of the guard commander. Following the inspection, both leaders embrace schoolchildren who wave flowers and the flags of both countries. At this time, the band performs a military march or folk song from the guest country. If a prime minister or chancellor or crown prince visits China, the welcoming ceremony is held by the premier. If a president, governor-general or king visits, the welcoming ceremony is held by the president.

The People's Liberation Army (PLA) Honor Guard then marches off the square to the tune of the [March of the Chinese People's Liberation Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Anthem_of_the_People's_Liberation_Army). The ceremony ends with the marching band of the PLA performing an [exhibition of military drill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exhibition_drill).

During state visits, national awards are presented to visiting dignitaries, including the [Order of Friendship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Friendship_\(China\)). Since 1954, the State Protection Unit has provided [motorcades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorcade) for visiting dignitaries traveling from the airport to their meeting place.[^cite_note-7]

### Republic of China (Taiwan)

A 30-minute ceremony at the Plaza of the [National Theater and Concert Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Theater_and_Concert_Hall) in [Taipei](57648.md) takes place to honor visiting dignitaries to the [Republic of China (Taiwan)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China). After the anthems are played, the president escorts the visitor past the tri-service honor guard of the [Republic of China Armed Forces](30095.md), led by a colonel. After inspecting the troops, the [president of Taiwan](61155.md) delivers welcome remarks, after which the foreign leader speaks, before receiving a key to the city from the [mayor of Taipei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Taipei), and the director of the Department of Protocol of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Taiwan\)) then introducing members of the cabinet to the dignitary and members of the delegation to the ROC president.[^cite_note-8]

### Czech Republic

The military welcome accorded to foreign leaders at [Prague Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague_Castle) are provided by troops of the [Prague Castle Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague_Castle_Guard), [Honor Guard of the Czech Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honor_Guard_of_the_Czech_Armed_Forces), the [Czech Army Central Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Army_Central_Band), the [Band of the Castle Guards and Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_of_the_Castle_Guards_and_the_Police_of_the_Czech_Republic) and units of the [Prague Garrison Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague_Garrison_Command).

### Finland



US President [Joe Biden](145422.md) meeting the leaders of the [Nordic countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordic_countries) in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki), in July 2023

The Protocol Services of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Finland\)) is responsible for arranging high-level international visits and Finland's own state events. The task of the protocol services is to take care of receiving, escorting, accommodation, transportation and meal arrangements for guests.[^cite_note-9]

In Finland, state visits usually follow a certain pattern that has already become a tradition. The official reception ceremonies take place in front of the [Presidential Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Palace,_Helsinki) or alternatively at [Helsinki Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki_Airport). At the reception ceremony, the head of the visiting country, together with the [President of Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Finland), inspects the guard of honor to the rhythm of the *[Björneborgarnas marsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bj%C3%B6rneborgarnas_marsch)*. After the inspection of the honor guard and the performance of the national anthems, the heads of state greet the public from the balcony of the Presidential Palace, from where they move to the yellow salon to drink welcome toasts, take official photos and exchange gifts and badges of honor.[^cite_note-ulkopolitist-10]

Usually, the official program includes at least the laying a wreath on [Marshal Mannerheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Gustaf_Emil_Mannerheim)'s grave at the [Hietaniemi Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hietaniemi_Cemetery),[^cite_note-11] and a visit to the [Parliament House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_House,_Helsinki) or the [Helsinki City Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki_City_Hall).[^cite_note-ulkopolitist-10] The visit usually ends with state dinners held at the Presidential Palace, where the heads of state give their speeches.[^cite_note-ulkopolitist-10]

### France



Donald Trump at [Les Invalides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Invalides) during his state visit to France, 13 July 2017. Arrival ceremonies typically take place at Les Invalides, or the [Elysee Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elysee_Palace).

State arrival ceremonies are held at either the [Elysee Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elysee_Palace) or [Les Invalides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Invalides), with the participation of the Infantry and Fanfare Band of the [French Republican Guard Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Republican_Guard_Band) and the 1st Infantry Regiment of the [Republican Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Guard_\(France\)). The Vestibule d'Honneur (Hall of Honour) in the Elysee is where the [president of France](24899.md) meets visiting dignitaries and holds bilateral meetings. Towards the end of the visit, the head of state or governments will give a speech at the [Palais Bourbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palais_Bourbon) to the [Senate of France](567323.md), and will hold meetings with members of parliament. Sometime during the state visit, the visiting dignitary will lay a wreath at the [Tomb of the Unknown Soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomb_of_the_Unknown_Soldier) at the [Arc de Triomphe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arc_de_Triomphe).

### Georgia

State visits in Georgia are held outside the [Presidential Palace of Georgia](68206220.md) in [Tbilisi](42298.md). Ceremonial honours are provided by the Honour Guard and the [Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Band_of_the_National_Guard_of_Georgia) of the [National Guard of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Guard_of_Georgia). The band plays Georgian Army songs during the inspection of the honour guard. During the presidency of [Mikheil Saakashvili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikheil_Saakashvili), the band played the [March of the Preobrazhensky Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_of_the_Preobrazhensky_Regiment) during state visits.

During state visits, dignitaries usually meet with the [prime minister](563908.md), [chairperson of the Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairperson_of_the_Parliament_of_Georgia), and the [Catholicos-Patriarch of All Georgia](435047.md) separately from the president. State dinners are also held inside the presidential palace with officials from both countries present.

### Germany

During state visits to Germany, honours are provided by the German [Wachbataillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wachbataillon) (Guard battalion) and the [Staff Band of the Bundeswehr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staff_Band_of_the_Bundeswehr). An exception to this was on 3 May 2007 during the visit of French President [Jacques Chirac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Chirac) to Berlin, during which the [Franco-German Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco-German_Brigade) provided the honors. Depending on the status of the guest, state ceremonies are either held at the [Bellevue Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellevue_Palace_\(Germany\)) or the [Federal Chancellery Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Chancellery_\(Berlin\)). In recent years, state visits have been marked with dignitaries paying homage at memorials such as the [Berlin Victory Column](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_Victory_Column), the [Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_to_the_Murdered_Jews_of_Europe), and the [Soviet War Memorial (Tiergarten)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_War_Memorial_\(Tiergarten\)).

During state visits, foreign dignitaries typically visit German federal states outside the capital. It this regard, a full honors ceremony is also held with the minister-president being the presiding officer.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] In break with the traditional [Berlin](3354.md) military protocol, chairs have since July 2019 been set up outside the Chancellery due to Chancellor [Angela Merkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Merkel)'s unusual episodes of shaking and uneasiness during the honours ceremony. Currently, only Moldovan Premier [Maia Sandu](40796149.md) and Danish Prime Minister [Mette Frederiksen](26040044.md) have had to be received under this arrangement.[^cite_note-14]

### India

See also: [List of diplomatic visits to India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_visits_to_India) and [List of chief guests at Delhi Republic Day parade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_chief_guests_at_Delhi_Republic_Day_parade)



Barack and Michelle Obama met by President [Pratibha Patil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratibha_Patil) and the Prime Minister [Manmohan Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manmohan_Singh), together with their spouses, 8 November 2010. Foreign leaders in India are typically received at the [Rashtrapati Bhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashtrapati_Bhavan,_New_Delhi), the [official residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_residence) for the [president of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_India).

Foreign leaders are received at the [Rashtrapati Bhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashtrapati_Bhavan,_New_Delhi) in [New Delhi](51585.md) during state visits. The dignitary first receives the salute from the [President's Bodyguard (PBG)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President's_Bodyguard), and the [Tri-Services Guard of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tri-Services_Guard_of_Honour). Heads of state are also given a 21-gun salute, with a [19-gun salute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19-gun_salute) being to [heads of government](37570.md). The massed bands and the commander of the guard of honour is chosen by a rotation between the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force. In 2015, Wing Commander Pooja Thakur became the first female officer to lead the guard of honour for a foreign leader.[^cite_note-thehindu-15] All military honours are organized by Section D of the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(India\)).[^cite_note-16]

State banquets are also held for foreign dignitaries at the [Rashtrapati Bhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashtrapati_Bhavan,_New_Delhi) which are hosted by the [president of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_India). Over 100 guests are invited to attend state banquets, including the [vice-president of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice-President_of_India), the [prime minister of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_India), as well as [government officials](553883.md) and leaders of the ruling party.

### Israel

The [president of the State of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_State_of_Israel), in their position as head of state, leads the welcoming events and is the official host of foreign leaders who visit [Israel](9282173.md). A guard of honor made up of personnel of all the service branches of the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) (IDF) are usually lined up at the main V.I.P terminal of [Ben Gurion International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Gurion_International_Airport). Following the British precedent, the chief of military protocol reports to the visitor, in Hebrew:

"\[תואר רשמי של המבקר: לדוגמא, כבוד הנשיא], משמר כבוד של חיילי צבא ההגנה לישראל, ערוך ונכון לסקירתך. אדוני!"

"\[title of the visitor: e.g. Honorable President], a guard of honor of the soldiers of the Israel Defense Forces, is formed up and ready for your inspection. Sir!"

While historically, guests were also greeted by a [21-gun salute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21-gun_salute), the practice has since been discontinued.[^cite_note-17]

During some state visits, the itinerary has included a visit to the [Yad Vashem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yad_Vashem) memorial. There the leader, accompanied by the prime minister, will rekindle the Eternal Flame, and will also lay a wreath in memorial of the 6 million Jews murdered in the holocaust.

### Italy



[John F. Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_F._Kennedy) arrives at the [Tomb of the Unknown Soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomb_of_the_Unknown_Soldier_\(Italy\)) in Rome with [Italian Minister of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Minister_of_Defence) [Giulio Andreotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giulio_Andreotti), 1 July 1963. Visits to the tomb are a typical component of state visits to Italy.

When dignitaries arrive in the Italian capital of [Rome](25458.md), they are received in a short reception ceremony at the airport by the head of the Diplomatic Ceremonial Office among other officials, who then briefly entertain the guest in the boardroom to allow the handling of airport formalities. Arrival honors for foreign dignitaries visiting [Rome](25458.md) are held either at the [Quirinal Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quirinal_Palace) (official residence of the president of the Italian Republic) or the [Palazzo Chigi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palazzo_Chigi) (official residence of the prime minister of the Italian Republic). During the ceremony, the guard of honor is provided by a military unit (most likely the [Corazzieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corazzieri) and a selected ceremonial unit such as the Honour Company "Goito" from the 1° Regiment "Granatieri di Sardegna") and a supporting military band that performs 3 [Ruffles and flourishes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruffles_and_flourishes) known as "Onori" ("Honors") prior to playing the national anthem of the dignitary's home country and [Il Canto degli Italiani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il_Canto_degli_Italiani). In recent years, the [Bersaglieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bersaglieri), the [Carabinieri](45173.md), and the [Italian Navy](1126939.md) have provided honor guards for the ceremony.

The rest of the day includes bilateral meetings, one on one conversations, a joint press conference, and a state dinner, where the exchange of gifts and the awarding of honors takes place. The next day sees the laying of wreaths at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier in the [Altar of the Homeland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altare_della_Patria). All protocol events in the capital are organized by the State Ceremonial Office. In particular, the Ceremonial Office of the Ministry of Defense handles protocol related to the [Italian Armed Forces](14705.md), and the Diplomatic Ceremonial Office of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs handles protocol related to the diplomatic corps.[^cite_note-18]

### Japan



Donald Trump with [Prime Minister](24833.md) [Shinzō Abe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinz%C5%8D_Abe), during an American state visit to Japan in May 2019

Japan extends invitations for state visitors and official visitors aims to promote the friendship and relation between Japan and the country of the invitee.[^cite_note-19] In addition to state and official visits, the Japanese government also extends several other forms of invitations to visit the country.

The invitation of official practical visitors, practical visitors, and visitors to [Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs](868429.md) aim to promote negotiations, to strengthen security and to be used for policy coordination, etc.

The invitation of ministers is an invitation extended to ministers from other countries, and aims to have them deepen their understanding of Japan and send information about Japan based on the knowledge obtained through invitations through conference, inspection, etc. with Japanese [VIPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Very_important_person) and experts.

The invitation of strategic practitioners is an invitation extended to certain members in certain positions or those who are expected to hold a certain leadership position in the future, in political, economic, governmental, academic, etc., in foreign countries or international organizations. It aims to promote understanding of Japan's various policies and fields such as culture and society, to facilitate the promotion of Japan's foreign policy through personal connections with Japanese experts, and to promote pro-Japan in the medium- and long-term through briefings with private experts, Japanese cultural experiences, and local visits.

### Mexico

State visits to [Mexico City](18987.md) are held on a regular basis.[^cite_note-33] Arrival ceremonies are usually held at the [National Palace](5124367.md), the president's workplace or at [Mexico City International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City_International_Airport). On certain occasions, a full honors ceremony with the participation of the [National Guard](61282251.md)'s Presidential Guards Corps are held at [Campo Marte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campo_Marte), where the corps' two military police brigades are stationed.



[Marcelo Rebelo de Sousa](1652059.md) inspecting the guard of honor, 17 July 2017. An inspection of the Mexican guard of honour typically occurs near the end of the state arrival ceremony.

The arrival ceremony begins with a platoon of National Guards presenting arms at the arrival of the state guest at the entrance of the National Palace. After the state guest arrives with the [president of Mexico](24356889.md) (and their first partner, if present) in the palace' south courtyard the NG's Presidential Guards honor guard company presents arms while the [Representative Music Band of the Mexican Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representative_Music_Band_of_the_Mexican_Armed_Forces) plays the anthems of the two countries (the [Himno Nacional Mexicano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himno_Nacional_Mexicano) is always played first). Then the president of Mexico introduces members of their cabinet before the visiting dignitary introduces members of their delegation accompanying them on this visit. After this is done, the commander of the National Guard's honour guard company then delivers the following report to the dignitary:

“With your permission Mr/Madam President of the [United Mexican States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Mexican_States), I am the commander of the honour guard of the (states dignitary's position) and I invite you to review the guard of honour present, sir/ma'am."

The president and the dignitary then inspect the guard, paying homage to the [Mexican flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_flag) and the flag of the guest country along the way. The ceremony then ends and the two go into the palace to begin their bilateral meeting. The [Campo Marte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campo_Marte) ceremony is similar in style to the palace ceremony with the main differences being a [21-gun salute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21-gun_salute), [opening remarks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_speaking), and the [military parade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_parade) being added to the program. The unit responsible currently for the 21-gun salute is the State Honors Artillery Battery, coming under the 1st Artillery Battalion (Separate) of the 1st Army Corps.

### Netherlands



[Queen Beatrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Beatrix) and [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) participating in a formal welcome ceremony during his state visit to the Netherlands, 1 November 2005

State visits in the [Netherlands](21148.md) revolve around and are centered on the capital of [Amsterdam](844.md). A characteristic of a state visit is that it is usually carried out by a foreign [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state), rather than a [head of government](37570.md). Upon arrival to the country, the dignitary will be given a tour through the capital, which includes a wreath-laying ceremony at the [National Monument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Monument_\(Amsterdam\)). Foreign dignitaries are usually received by the monarch at the [Royal Palace of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Palace_of_Amsterdam), where they receive a guard of honour formed by the [Grenadiers' and Rifles Guard Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenadiers'_and_Rifles_Guard_Regiment) upon arrival. It is here where all matters of state and bilateral meetings take place over the course of the visit.

During a state visit, the [Dutch monarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_monarch) always hosts a [state dinner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_dinner) in honor of the guest, sometimes at the Royal Palace in Amsterdam and at other times at the monarch's residence at the [Noordeinde Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noordeinde_Palace) in The Hague. It is customary to be awarded the Knight Grand Cross [Order of the Netherlands Lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Netherlands_Lion) during state visits.

### North Korea

Although state visits to [North Korea](21255.md) do not happen often, they are held to the highest standard when they occur. Portraits of the visiting leader and the [Supreme Leader of North Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_leaders_of_North_Korea) are hung all over the city and at [Pyongyang Sunan International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyongyang_Sunan_International_Airport), where the arrival ceremony takes place. During the ceremony, the Guard of Honour Company of the [Korean People's Army](21263.md)'s [Supreme Guard Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Guard_Command), made up of servicemen from the Ground Forces, Navy, Air Force, Special Operations and the Strategic Forces, together with a platoon of the Worker-Peasant Red Guards renders honours while the [Central Band of the KPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Military_Band_of_the_Korean_People's_Army) performs the visiting anthem and *[Aegukka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aegukka)*, the anthem of the DPRK. After this, the commander of the guard reports to the leader before they inspect the troops and review a marchpast.[^cite_note-38] They then ride in a [motorcade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorcade) that takes them to the Paekhwawon State Guest House and or the Kumsusan Guesthouse for bilateral negotiations.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43] The former guest house was first used for state visits in the early 2000s for the visits of [Madeleine Albright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeleine_Albright) and [Junichiro Koizumi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junichiro_Koizumi) and became relevant once again during the [2018–19 Korean peace process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_Korean_peace_process).[^cite_note-44] The latter was only built in the spring of 2019 in time for the visit of the [Chinese President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_China) and [General Secretary of the Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Secretary_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party). En route to the guesthouse, a [Korean Children's Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Children's_Union) guard of honour company takes its position to award an honorary red neckerchief to the visiting leader in the name of all members present, and in addition, flower bouquets can be given by representatives on behalf of the KPA, the people of Pyongyang, and the city government.

Notable state visits held in the 21st century were those by [South Korean](27019.md) leaders such as [Roh Moo-hyun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roh_Moo-hyun) in 2007 and [Moon Jae In](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon_Jae_In)[^cite_note-sept18-45] in 2018, as well as Chinese leader [Xi Jinping](2017814.md) that same year. During Xi's visit, he became the first Chinese [Paramount leader](262766.md) to visit the [Kumsusan Palace of the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumsusan_Palace_of_the_Sun) and took part in the reopening of the [Arirang Mass Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arirang_Mass_Games) at [Rungrado 1st of May Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rungrado_1st_of_May_Stadium).[^cite_note-46] For many state visits, the [Korean People's Army State Merited Chorus and Symphony Orchestra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_People's_Army_State_Merited_Chorus_and_Symphony_Orchestra) have performed pieces native to the visitors homeland, such as *[Katyusha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katyusha_\(song\))* during [Vladimir Putin](32817.md)'s visit in 2000. Other lower-level world leaders who have undertaken state visits to the DPRK include Cuban leader [Miguel Diaz-Canel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_Diaz-Canel) in November 2018 and Mongolian President [Tsakhiagiin Elbegdorj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsakhiagiin_Elbegdorj) in 2013, with the latter being played down as it did not include a meeting with Chairman [Kim Jong Un](5962511.md).[^cite_note-47]

### Philippines

See also: [List of diplomatic visits to the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_visits_to_the_Philippines)



[Pope Francis](1687680.md) with [President](271126.md) [Benigno Aquino III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benigno_Aquino_III) heading towards the [Malacañan Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaca%C3%B1an_Palace) during [the papal visit to the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visit_by_Pope_Francis_to_the_Philippines) in January 2015

In the Philippines, state visits are organized by the [Department of Foreign Affairs](4718612.md) and the [Office of the President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_the_President_of_the_Philippines), adhering to established diplomatic protocols. Visiting dignitaries typically arrive at [Villamor Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villamor_Air_Base) in [Pasay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasay). Upon arrival, they are accorded military honors and welcomed by local government officials, usually [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_Philippines) members, and occasionally, the [President of the Philippines](271126.md). For instance, during [Pope Francis](1687680.md)'s [visit in 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visit_by_Pope_Francis_to_the_Philippines), he was welcomed by then-President [Benigno Aquino III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benigno_Aquino_III) and several Cabinet officials.[^cite_note-49]

A customary component of state visits is the wreath-laying ceremony at the [Rizal Monument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rizal_Monument) in [Rizal Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rizal_Park), [Manila](184334.md), where the visiting dignitary is received by the [Mayor of Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Manila). This act honors Dr. [José Rizal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Rizal), widely considered to be the [national hero of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_hero_of_the_Philippines), and symbolizes the visiting leader's respect for Filipino history and values.[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51]

The official welcome continues at [Malacañang Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaca%C3%B1ang_Palace), where the visiting leader is received at the [Kalayaan Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalayaan_Hall) Grounds. Arrival honors are rendered once more, including the playing of both national anthems by a [military band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_band#Philippines). Afterwards, they ascend the grand staircase and sign the Official Guest Book in the Reception Hall. Later that evening, a state dinner is hosted in honor of the visiting leader, often held at the Aguinaldo State Dining Room or the Rizal Ceremonial Hall. The event includes an exchange of toasts and may feature cultural performances showcasing [Filipino heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_the_Philippines). As a gesture of goodwill, the Philippines may confer [national honors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders,_decorations,_and_medals_of_the_Philippines) upon the visiting dignitary, such as the [Order of Lakandula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Lakandula) or the [Order of Sikatuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Sikatuna).[^cite_note-52]

To ensure public safety and facilitate the smooth conduct of state visits, local authorities may declare suspensions of classes and government work. In 2015, several local governments and schools suspended class during Pope Francis's visit due to road closures for the pontiff's motorcade.[^cite_note-53][^cite_note-54] During Chinese leader [Xi Jinping](2017814.md)'s [state visit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_international_trips_made_by_Xi_Jinping) to the Philippines in November 2018, the government of Manila and the *[barangays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barangay)* of [Fort Bonifacio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Bonifacio), [Pinagsama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinagsama), and [Western Bicutan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Bicutan) in [Taguig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taguig) suspended classes and government work due to road closures and security.[^cite_note-55]

### Russia



The [Russian Foreign Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Foreign_Ministry) organizes the itinerary for official visits to the country, scheduling events such as [Robert Gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Gates) laying a wreath at the [Tomb of the Unknown Soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomb_of_the_Unknown_Soldier_\(Moscow\)), 22 March 2011.

The Department of State Protocol of the [Russian Foreign Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Foreign_Ministry) is responsible for organizing the itinerary for state visits to the country. It was founded on 18 December 1991, by Decree No. 291 of President [Boris Yeltsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Yeltsin) on the basis of the Foreign Policy Service of the RSFSR. The decree, in effect, provided a new unified protocol practice in the [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation).[^cite_note-57] Its duties range from organizing invitations to the country to developing an event schedule for the dignitary's visit. Common practices organized by the department include protocol events meetings, wreath-laying, breakfast, and lunch, among others. The Presidential Protocol Office in the [Presidential Administration of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Administration_of_the_Russian_Federation) also serves a similar purpose.[^cite_note-58]

A foreign head of state or government receives arrival honours from the [154th Preobrazhensky Independent Commandant's Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/154th_Preobrazhensky_Independent_Commandant's_Regiment) and the [Special Exemplary Military Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Exemplary_Military_Band_of_the_Guard_of_Honor_Battalion_of_Russia) upon their arrival at the VIP terminal at [Vnukovo International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vnukovo_International_Airport) in [Moscow](19004.md), where they are usually greeted by the Russian deputy foreign minister. Heads of state also take part in a welcoming ceremony with the [president of Russia](239646.md) in the Hall of the [Order of St. George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Saint_George_\(Russia\)) of the [Grand Kremlin Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Kremlin_Palace).

> "Russian diplomacy has a centuries-old tradition, I consider the Russian protocol to be one of the best in the world. The protocol service of Russia is very correct."[^cite_note-59]

— [Tiit Matsulevitš](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiit_Matsulevit%C5%A1), [Estonian Ambassador to Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_Ambassador_to_Russia) from 1999–2001

### Spain

See also: [List of state visits received by Felipe VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_state_visits_received_by_Felipe_VI)



The [president of Israel](153049.md), [Reuven Rivlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reuven_Rivlin), addresses the [Cortes Generales](519703.md) during his state visit Spain.

State visits to the [Kingdom of Spain](26667.md) are held with the participation of the [King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Spain) and [Queen of Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Spanish_royal_consorts). The day's events begin as the visiting dignitary arrives in a special vehicle at the [Palacio Real](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Palace_of_Madrid) in the Spanish capital of [Madrid](41188263.md). As the dignitary arrives, units of the [Spanish Royal Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Royal_Guard) (including the Mounted Band of Timpani and Bugles and the Lancers Troop) prepares for the start of the arrival ceremony. The ceremony starts with all units [presenting arms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presenting_arms) at the sound of a bugle call and performing a royal salute, with the playing of the guest anthem and the *[Marcha Real](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcha_Real)* taking place. Once the regiment is orders arms, three senior officers from the guard (a senior commander, a bugler and a guidon bearer) approach the central grandstand to report to the guest and the king on the readiness of the regiment for inspection. They then begin their inspection, reviewing the infantry as well as the mounted units. Once the review is complete, a large military parade in the forecourt of the palace is held with the massed bands playing military music as the units march past.

The other notable state ceremony that takes place in the palace is the state dinner, which is hosted by the king, and is attended by the visiting dignitary, their delegation, the [Spanish royal family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_royal_family), the [prime minister](203556.md), and other government officials. During the course of the visit, the visiting leader may deliver an address to a joint session of the bicameral [Cortes Generales](519703.md) (composed of the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Spain) and the [Congress of Deputies](352254.md)).

### Ukraine



The official visit of Prince Nikolaus of Liechtenstein to [Kyiv](585629.md) in June 2018

State visits to [Ukraine](31750.md) are managed by the Main Department on State Protocol and Ceremonies of the [Office of the President of Ukraine](19362024.md) as well as the State Protocol Department of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Ukraine\)). State visits involve the [president of Ukraine](20688479.md), the [chairman of the Verkhovna Rada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Verkhovna_Rada), and the [prime minister of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Ukraine). Heads of state are met at the [Boryspil International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boryspil_International_Airport), during which they are met by children in Ukrainian national costumes handing them bread. after which they are driven to the [Mariinskyi Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariinskyi_Palace) to receive a guard of honour from the [Kyiv Presidential Honor Guard Battalion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_Presidential_Honor_Guard_Battalion).[^cite_note-60] The [battalion band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_of_the_Kyiv_Presidential_Honor_Guard_Battalion) performs national anthems of both countries, during which a 21-gun artillery salute. As the guest bows their head to the [state flag of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Ukraine), they greet the honor guard with the [battle cry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_cry) of *[Glory to Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glory_to_Ukraine)* (the official greeting of the [Armed Forces of Ukraine](42799055.md)[^cite_note-61]). After the ceremony, negotiations and a working breakfast/lunch are arranged in honor of the guest. In [Kyiv](585629.md), there is a tradition of laying wreaths at the [Tomb of the Unknown Soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomb_of_the_Unknown_Soldier_\(Kyiv\)), the ceremony of which is as follows: a march of the three man team carrying a wreath through the Alley of Glory, wreath laying in front of the eternal flame, performance of national anthems, a march past of a guard of honour. On the first day of the visit, as a rule, a public dinner is held on behalf of the president of Ukraine in honor of the head of state in the Red Hall. At the end of the state dinner, a concert of musicians is held in the White Hall. Protocol stipulates that a state visit lasts longer (3–4 days) than an official visit (2–3 days).[^cite_note-62]

### United Arab Emirates

The [United Arab Emirates Air Force](10815770.md) does a flyover while painting the sky in the colors of the visiting country's flag as the visiting leader's car arrives at the [presidential palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qasr_Al_Watan) with an Army mounted escort. Upon arrival, the visiting head of state is greeted by the [president of the United Arab Emirates](9480572.md) or the deputy supreme commander of the [United Arab Emirates Armed Forces](31841.md), where they both receive [military honors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_honors) from the [United Arab Emirates Presidential Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Arab_Emirates_Presidential_Guard) as well as a [21-gun salute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21-gun_salute) from members of the Army. While inside the palace, they are also greeted by children holding flags of the UAE and of the visiting country. The entire city of [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md) is usually decorated for the visit, with flags laid out in the streets and large pictures of the two countries' leaders erected on large buildings. During the visit of [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) in October 2019, police cars were sprayed with the [Cyrillic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrillic) letters "ДПС", which stands for "Дорожно-патрульная служба" (English: Roads Patrol Service).[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64]

### United Kingdom

Main article: [State and official visits to the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_and_official_visits_to_the_United_Kingdom)



[Hassan Mohamud](36973989.md), [President of Somalia](516101.md), with [British Prime Minister](24150.md) [David Cameron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Cameron), during a Somalian state visit to the United Kingdom

A state visit to the [United Kingdom](31717.md) is a royal event which involves the King and other members of the royal family. An arrival ceremony usually takes place on [Horse Guards Parade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_Guards_Parade) (there are also some instances where it takes place at [Buckingham Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckingham_Palace) or [Windsor Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windsor_Castle)) with a guard of honour being provided by members of the [King's Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_Guard). The guard of honour will always report to the dignitary in the language of the visitor, with the report being along the lines of the following:

"Your Excellency, the guard of honour, provided by the (states name of unit), is formed up, and ready for inspection."

Depending on the area where the ceremony takes place, a march past will immediately take place following the inspection. The foreign guest and the King then travel to Buckingham Palace in a carriage procession escorted by a large number of mounted soldiers from the Household Cavalry. The welcome ceremony is accompanied by 21-gun salutes fired from [Green Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Park) and the [Tower of London](31165.md). Around 150 guests are invited to Buckingham Palace for the state banquet in the evening in the ballroom.[^cite_note-royal-65][^cite_note-changing-guard-66]

State visits do not formally occur between the United Kingdom and the 14 other [Commonwealth realms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_realms), as the realms all share a common monarch and head of state. A visit conducted by the monarch to another Commonwealth realm is also not considered a state visit, but is termed a **royal tour**. Tours of Commonwealth realms by the monarch are conducted in their capacity as the monarch of that respective realm, and not as the British monarch. As a result, visits on behalf of the United Kingdom to other Commonwealth realms are typically conducted as official visits by the [prime minister of the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_the_United_Kingdom). Conversely, official visits to the United Kingdom by another Commonwealth realm is typically performed by their respective prime minister or governor-general.

### United States

Main article: [State visits to the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_visits_to_the_United_States)



[Deng Xiaoping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deng_Xiaoping) and [Jimmy Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Carter) inspecting the joint-service honor guard during the [arrival ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deng_Xiaoping's_1979_visit_to_the_United_States) for Deng in 1979

State visits to [Washington, D.C.](108956.md), occur only on the invitation of the president of the United States in their position as head of state and head of the [federal government of the United States](195149.md). Official visits refer to a visit by a head of government to Washington. During state visits, there are usually many events taking place in the capital, with the participation of hundreds of individuals. The first state visit took place in 1874 by [Kalākaua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kal%C4%81kaua) of the [Kingdom of Hawaii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hawaii), followed by [Pedro II of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_II_of_Brazil) in 1876.

Events can range from a flight line ceremony at [Joint Base Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_Andrews), an arrival ceremony (either at the [White House](33057.md) or [The Pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pentagon) depending on the guest), a [State Department](31975.md) luncheon, a state dinner and an address to the [Congress](31756.md).
