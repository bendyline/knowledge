**Geoffrey John Venning** is a New Zealand lawyer and jurist. He was a [High Court judge](3902269.md#Judges_of_the_High_Court) from 2002 to 2026, and served as the chief High Court judge from 1 June 2015 to 31 May 2020. He retired from the bench in 2026 and returned to legal practice.

## Education

Venning graduated from the [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury) in 1978 with a [Bachelor of Commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Commerce) degree in economics & accounting, and in 1981 with a [Bachelor of Laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Laws) with honours. During his time at university, he received several awards for being top of his class: the Sweet & Maxwell Prize for Legal System in 1979, followed by the Butterworths Prize in Equity and Land Law and the Macarthur Prize in Commercial Law the next year.[^cite_note-auto1-1]

## Career

From 1982 to 1986, Venning served as a tutor in commercial law at the University of Canterbury and has been a moderator in the law of torts for New Zealand since 1992. He has conducted several seminars for the [New Zealand Law Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Law_Society) and has also tutored in their litigation skills course.[^cite_note-auto1-1]

In 1981, Venning joined the Christchurch law firm [Wynn Williams & Co](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wynn_Williams_&_Co), becoming a [litigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litigation) partner in 1987. He was appointed as a master of the High Court (now called associate judge) in 1995 sitting in Christchurch. He was a member of both the Rules Committee and the Judicial Information Consultative Committee from 1999 to 2005 and a member of the Council of Legal Education from 2004 to 2015.[^cite_note-auto1-1][^cite_note-auto-2]

On 17 October 2002, the [attorney-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney-General_\(New_Zealand\)), [Margaret Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Wilson), announced Venning's appointment as a judge of the High Court sitting in [Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland_High_Court).[^cite_note-auto1-1] In June 2015, Venning was appointed chief High Court judge. Venning maintained the list managing [Christchurch earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Christchurch_earthquake) litigation and established a panel to deal with commercial cases.[^cite_note-3] He presided over the trial in 2019 of serial rapist [Malcolm Rewa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malcolm_Rewa), for the murder in 1992 of Auckland woman [Susan Burdett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teina_Pora). He also presided over the [deputy prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_New_Zealand) [Winston Peters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winston_Peters)' suit over politicians being briefed that he had been overpaid superannuation.[^cite_note-4] Venning resumed his role as a full-time judge of the High Court on stepping down as the chief judge on 31 May 2020.[^cite_note-auto-2]

In 2026, Venning retired as a High Court judge, and was granted retention of the title *The Honourable* for life.[^cite_note-5] He returned to practice with Britomart Chambers in Auckland.[^cite_note-6]
