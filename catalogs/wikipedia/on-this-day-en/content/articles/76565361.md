**Yván Eduardo Gil Pinto** (born 15 August 1972) is a Venezuelan diplomat and politician serving as the Minister of Science and Technology of Venezuela since 2026. He previously served as the [Minister of Foreign Affairs](26105673.md) from January 2023 to July 2026.

## Biography

Gil holds a degree in [agricultural engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_engineering) from the [Central University of Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_University_of_Venezuela), and also pursued a doctorate in [biotechnology](4502.md) in [Montpellier](73369.md), [France](5843419.md). Prior to his political career, he was president of the National Institute of Agricultural Research (INIA), and of the state-owned agricultural supply company [Agropatria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agropatria).[^cite_note-EMOL-1]

Gil is a member of the ruling [United Socialist Party of Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Socialist_Party_of_Venezuela) (PSUV).[^cite_note-2] In 2013–2014 and 2015–2016, he served as Minister of Agriculture and Land, and in 2017, he was appointed Vice Minister for Europe at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.[^cite_note-EMOL-1] In 2021–2022, he served as [chargé d'affaires](856252.md) of Venezuela to the [European Union](9317.md) in [Brussels](3708.md), [Belgium](3343.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

Gil succeeded [Carlos Faría](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Far%C3%ADa) as Minister of Foreign Affairs in January 2023; one commentator saw Gil's appointment as an attempt by president [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md) to strengthen relations between Venezuela and the European Union, as his predecessor was viewed as close to the [Russian](25391.md) government.[^cite_note-EMOL-1] In February 2024, Gil announced that the government of Venezuela ordered the [United Nations](31769.md) human rights office in [Caracas](55289.md) to shut down all operations, ordering the staff to leave the country within three days.[^cite_note-6]

On 14 July 2026, interim president [Delcy Rodríguez](44199228.md) appointed [Félix Plasencia](81332725.md) to replace Gil as foreign minister and announced that Gil would be appointed Minister of Science and Technology.[^cite_note-7]
