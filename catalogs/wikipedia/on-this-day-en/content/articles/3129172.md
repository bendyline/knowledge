**Valsad district** (previously **Bulsar district**) is one of the 34 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Gujarat) in the [Western Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_India) state of [Gujarat](53707.md). It is bound by [Navsari district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navsari_district) to the north, [Nashik district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashik_district) of [Maharashtra](20629.md) state to the east, and [Dadra and Nagar Haveli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli) district of the [Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli_and_Daman_and_Diu) (DNHDD) union territory and the [Palghar district](43059179.md) of Maharashtra to the south. The [Arabian Sea](2747.md) lies west of the district. The coastal [Damaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daman_district,_India) enclave of DNHDD is bounded by Valsad district on the north, east, and south.[^cite_note-indiaonapage.com-2] The district's administrative capital is [Valsad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valsad). The district's largest city is [Vapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valsad).[^cite_note-3]

The district covers 3008 square kilometres and is divided into six [talukas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taluka): [Valsad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valsad), [Vapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vapi), [Pardi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pardi), [Umargam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umargam), [Kaparada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaparada) and [Dharampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharampur,_Gujarat).[^cite_note-4] The population was 1,705,678 in 2011, up from 1,410,553 in 2001.[^cite_note-5] Valsad is well known for its production of [mangoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango), [sapodilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapodilla), [teak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teak) and industrial hubs based in Vapi, Pardi and Atul.

## History

On 1 June 1966, Valsad district was formed after Surat district was bifurcated into Valsad and Surat district. Then, [Valsad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valsad), [Dharampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharampur,_Gujarat), [Pardi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pardi), [Umargam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umargam), [Navsari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navsari), [Vansda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vansda), [Chikhli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikhli,_Gujarat) and [Gandevi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandevi) were talukas of Valsad district.[^cite_note-6]

On 2 October 1997, [Navsari district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navsari_district) was formed after Valsad district was bifurcated into Valsad and Navsari district. The taluka of Kaparada was separated from Dharampur on 15 October 1997. After newly formed Kaparada taluka - Valsad, [Dharampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharampur,_Gujarat), [Kaparada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaparada), Pardi, Umargam were talukas of Valsad district.[^cite_note-7]

On 9 September 2013, Vapi taluka was separated from Pardi taluka. After newly formed Vapi taluka - Valsad, [Dharampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharampur,_Gujarat), Kaparada, Pardi, [Vapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vapi), Umargam are talukas of Valsad district.[^cite_note-8]

## Transport

Valsad lies on [NH 48](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_48_\(India\)). Valsad is connected to many cities in [Gujarat](53707.md) by the [Gujarat State Road Transport Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujarat_State_Road_Transport_Corporation) bus service. Valsad is one of the 16 divisions of [GSRTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GSRTC) referred to as [Daman Ganga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daman_Ganga_River). Following bus depots come under the Valsad division: Valsad, Vapi, Navsari, Bilimora, Dharampur, and Ahwa.

[Valsad railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valsad_railway_station) is managed by the [Mumbai WR railway division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_WR_railway_division) of the [Western Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Railway_\(India\)). It lies on the [New Delhi–Mumbai mainline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi%E2%80%93Mumbai_main_line). The present railway station building was established in 1925. Adjacent to the railway station is the [Valsad Electric Loco Shed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_Loco_Shed,_Valsad) which houses over 100 electric locomotives. Other railway stations in the district are [Umargam Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umargam_Road_railway_station), [Sanjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjan_railway_station), [Bhilad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhilad_railway_station), [Vapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vapi_railway_station), [Udvada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udvada_railway_station), [Pardi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pardi_railway_station) and [Dungri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungri_railway_station).

The nearest airport is [Surat Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surat_Airport) in [Surat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surat).

## Economy

### Agriculture

Valsad is a horticulture hub in Gujarat. Major horticulture crops that are produced in the district are [mango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango), [papaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papaya), [guava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guava), [cucurbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cucurbits), [chikoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikoo), [banana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana) and [sugarcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugarcane). Valsad is also known as the mango capital of Gujarat and is famous for its [Alphonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonso_\(mango\)) mango (Valsadi Haafus). Valsadi [chikoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikoo) is in high demand nationwide as well as around the world.[^cite_note-15]

### Industry

Valsad is an industrial base for sectors such as chemicals, textiles, and paper & pulp industries. Since the 1980s, textile and chemicals have been the major sectors of investments and employment in the district. Valsad is emerging as a horticulture hub of the state, witnessing significant production in food grains and crops.

Valsad has 7 Industrial Estates located in Dungra, Pardi, Dharampur, Bhilad, Valsad, Umargam, Sarigam, Vapi (the [chemical hub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_hub) of Gujarat) and 1 Industrial park in Kalgam.[^cite_note-16] With over 300 medium and large scale industries, Vapi is a major industrial center in Valsad. One of Asia's largest Common Effluent Treatment Plant (CETP) is present in Vapi, owned by Vapi Waste & Effluent Management Company and promoted by Vapi Industrial Association.[^cite_note-17]

Over 10,716 units of small and medium enterprises (SMEs), involved in different sectors, such as chemicals, textiles, engineering, and paper industry, are present in the district. Several private conglomerates are present in Valsad, such as Alok Industries Ltd., Wyeth, [Welspun India Ltd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welspun_India_Ltd). Hubergroup India Private Limited (formerly Micro Inks Ltd.), Meril Group of companies ([Meril Life Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meril_Life_Sciences) Pvt. Ltd., Meril Healthcare Pvt. Ltd., Meril Endo Surgery Pvt. Ltd. and Meril Diagnostics Pvt. Ltd.), Aarti Industries, [Atul Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atul_Limited), Gujarat Heavy Chemicals Ltd. (GHCL), [Raymond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond), [Sun Pharmaceuticals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Pharmaceuticals), United Phosphorus, [Pidilite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pidilite), Exemed Pharmaceuticals, Polyols & Polymers and [Vadilal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vadilal), Unique Polymers.

## Education

The Government Polytechnic, established in 1965, is one of the prominent institutions in Valsad.[^cite_note-18] The Government Engineering College was established in 2004. The GMERS Medical college with an 800-bed hospital was also set up in Valsad in 2014.[^cite_note-19]

Many colleges offer various courses in science, commerce and arts in Valsad district.[^cite_note-20] S.K.M. Law College is also established.[^cite_note-21]

## Important places



Tithal Beach

[Tithal Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tithal_Beach) is located near [Tithal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tithal) town of Valsad district. The sand of the beach is black sand. Tithal Beach Festival and International Kite Festival are also organized on the shore of the beach.

[Shree Shirdi Saibaba Sansthapan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shree_Shirdi_Saibaba_Sansthapan?action=edit\&redlink=1) is located on the shore of the beach in [Tithal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tithal) town of Valsad district. The temple was established in 1982.



Swaminarayan Temple, Tithal

[Shri Swaminarayan Mandir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shri_Swaminarayan_Mandir,_Tithal) is located on the shore of the beach in [Tithal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tithal) town of Valsad district. This is a temple of Lord Swami Narayan.

Shanti Dham Aradhana Kendra in Tithal is a meditation center.[^cite_note-22]

[Tadkeshwar Mahadev Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadkeshwar_Mahadev_Temple) is a temple of Lord Shiva located in [Abrama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abrama). This temple is more than 800 years old.

Morarji Desai Museum is built to dedicate the former 5th Prime Minister of India, [Morarji Desai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morarji_Desai) in Valsad. The museum was developed by Valsad municipality at a cost of Rs. 4 crores. The museum was inaugurated by former Chief Minister of Gujarat, [Anandiben Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anandiben_Patel).[^cite_note-23]

Callian Baug is an urban park in Valsad. In 1929, Mr. Motilal Callianji presented a donation for the park to Valsad Municipality in memory of his father. In 1931, Callian Baug was opened by J.H.Garrett.[^cite_note-24]



Wilson Hills, Dharampur

[Wilson Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilson_Hills,_Gujarat) is located in Dharampur tehsil. Wilson Hills was named in memory of Lord Wilson, the Governor of Bombay from 1923 to 1928, by Vijay Devji, the last King of Dharampur. A monument to their memory remains on the peak.

Lady Wilson Museum is located in [Dharampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharampur,_Gujarat) town of Valsad district. It was established in 1928. It is one of the popular tourist attraction of Valsad district.[^cite_note-25]

District Science Centre is located in [Dharampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharampur,_Gujarat). It was established in 1984.[^cite_note-26]



Simandhar Swami Jain Derasar, Nandigram

Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram is located in [Dharampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharampur,_Gujarat) town of Valsad district. It is the International Headquarters of Shrimad Rajchandra Mission Dharampur.[^cite_note-27]

Simandhar Swami Jain Derasar, also known as Nandigram Derasar, is at Nandigram village near Bhilad in Valsad district. A function was organised on Vaisakh Sudh 6th on its foundation day, 10 May 1989.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29] This temple is dedicated to Shri Simandhar Swami who is considered living tirthankar on another world in the Jain mythological universe.[^cite_note-30]



Fort of Parnera

[Parnera Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnera_Hill) is located in [Parnera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnera) town of Valsad district. It is well known for Shree Maha Kali Mata Temple, Shree Chandika Mata Temple, Shree Swayambhu Mahadev Temple, Chand Pir Baba Dargah, Stepwells, Old Fort of The Great [Shivaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivaji) Maharaj.[^cite_note-gujarat1-31]

Maa Vishvambhari Tirthyatra Dham is located near [Rabda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabda). It has a temple of Maa Vishvambhari, Pathshala, Himalayas, Govardhan mountain, Hut of Nand Baba, Gaushala and Shri Ram Kutir.[^cite_note-32]

Bagwada is home to Arjungad Fort and several temples, including a Jain temple.

Shri Bhav Bhaveshwar Mahadev Temple is located in Barumal. Barumal is located 37 km from Valsad and 7 km from Dharampur. This is a temple of Lord Shiva.[^cite_note-33]

Nargol Beach is located in [Nargol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nargol). It has lush palm trees and greenery all around on the beach.[^cite_note-34]

Umargam Beach is located in [Umargam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umargam).[^cite_note-35]



Udvada Atash Behram, Udvada

[Udvada Atash Behram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udvada_Atash_Behram) is the oldest [fire temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_temple) of Zoroastrianism in India. It is located in [Udvada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udvada). [Sanjan Stambh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjan_Stambh) is a memorial of the Zoroastrianism in [Sanjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjan,_Gujarat).
