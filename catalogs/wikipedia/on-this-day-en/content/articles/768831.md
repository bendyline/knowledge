**Ouham-Pendé** is one of the 20 [prefectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_of_the_Central_African_Republic) of the [Central African Republic](5478.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Its capital is [Bozoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bozoum).

The prefecture covers an area of 18,520 km² and, according to official estimates, its population was 254,649 inhabitants in 2024.[^cite_note-2024-POP-EST-2] At the time of the country's last official census, in 2003, the population was 430,506 inhabitants in an area of 32,100 km².[^cite_note-5] These are data from before December 2020, when part of the territory was dismembered to create the [Lim-Pendé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lim-Pend%C3%A9) prefecture.[^cite_note-6]

In the north it has a border with [Cameroon](5447.md) and [Chad](5488.md). In the south is the prefecture [Ombella-Mpoko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ombella-Mpoko), in the south-west the prefecture [Nana-Mambéré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nana-Mamb%C3%A9r%C3%A9) and in the east the prefecture [Ouham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouham).

The region has contained several [ghost towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghost_town) such as [Goroumo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goroumo) due to government forces ransacking them and armed bandits killing all the male inhabitants over the years from 2005 to 2008.[^cite_note-BBC-7][^cite_note-8]
