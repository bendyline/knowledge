The [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md) issues [passports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passport) to its citizens for international travel.

On 1 January 2010, the Government officially invalidated all passports not of the series OB, even if the expiry date was beyond 1 January 2010. Because passports not of the series OB were no longer considered valid travel documents from that date onwards, holders were obliged to apply for new DRC passports in order to travel.[^cite_note-2]

Under a previous 2015 agreement, the passport cost \$185. It was reported that the Congolese government received a share of \$65, with the remainder split between the Belgian company [Semlex](71412427.md) and the Emirati company LRPS. The contract was awarded without a public tender, leading to allegations of ties to then-President [Joseph Kabila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Kabila).[^cite_note-3] However, the fee was reduced to \$99 in 2020.[^cite_note-4] After the German company Dermalog was awarded the contract in 2022, the price was further reduced to \$75 in 2025.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

As of 1 January 2017, Democratic Republic of the Congo citizens had visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 40 countries and territories, ranking the Democratic Republic of the Congo passport 94th in terms of travel freedom (tied with [Djiboutian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djiboutian_passport) and [North Korean passports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korean_passport)) according to the [Henley visa restrictions index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_\(document\)#Visa_restrictions).[^cite_note-7]
