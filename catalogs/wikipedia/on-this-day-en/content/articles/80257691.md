The **2026 Belmont Stakes** was the 158th running of the [Belmont Stakes](62398.md) and took place on June 6, 2026, at [Saratoga Race Course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratoga_Race_Course). The post time for the 2026 “Run for the Carnations” was at 7\:01 p.m. [ET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_time_zone), 3 minutes earlier due to impending weather.

This was the third time in as many years that the last leg of the [Triple Crown](19116721.md) would take place at Saratoga due to ongoing construction at the race's usual home of [Belmont Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belmont_Park).[^cite_note-amny-1]

The winner was [Golden Tempo](83090873.md) who was also the winner of the [2026 Kentucky Derby](82792534.md). Winning trainer [Cherie DeVaux](83090954.md) became the second woman to train a Belmont winner, after [Jena Antonucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jena_Antonucci) in [ 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Belmont_Stakes).

## Background

New York Governor [Kathy Hochul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathy_Hochul) announced that the 2026 Belmont Stakes would be the third and final time Saratoga Race Course in [Saratoga Springs, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratoga_Springs,_New_York), would host the race. Due to Saratoga's smaller main track, the race was run at 1+1⁄4 mi (10 furlongs; 2,012 m), shorter than the usual 1+1⁄2 mi (12 furlongs; 2,414 m). Belmont Park is scheduled to reopen in the fall of 2026.[^cite_note-amny-1]

The most recent time a Belmont Stakes was held elsewhere was from 1963 to 1967 at [Aqueduct Race Track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aqueduct_Race_Track) in [Ozone Park, Queens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozone_Park,_Queens), the last time Belmont Park was under construction.[^cite_note-espnsara1-2]

TV broadcast was won by [Fox Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Sports), which was also show on [Fox Sports 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Sports#United_States) and FS2. Streaming coverage was free on [Fubo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fubo). FS2 early coverage began at 10\:30 a.m. [ET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_time_zone) and the main channel Fox coverage began at 3 p.m. [ET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_time_zone).

The race itself was set to start at 7\:04 p.m., also known as 'post time', but started at 7\:01 p.m. due to impending weather.

## Entries

A field of 9 was drawn on June 1st. Kentucky Derby winner [Golden Tempo](83090873.md) drew post no. 9 and was given a morning line of 9-2. [Renegade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renegade_\(horse\)) was installed as the favorite with 2-1 morning line odds.[^cite_note-3]
