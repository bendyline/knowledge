**Tom Berend Willem Berendsen** (born 11 April 1983) is a Dutch politician who has served as [Minister of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Netherlands\)) in the [Jetten cabinet](82284736.md) since 2026. From [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_European_Parliament_election_in_the_Netherlands) until his appointment to the government he served as a [Member of the European Parliament](258909.md) (MEP).[^cite_note-EP-1] He is a member of the [Christian Democratic Appeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Democratic_Appeal) (CDA).

Berendsen studied public administration at [Tilburg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilburg_University), as well as at the [Catholic University of Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KU_Leuven) as part of an [Erasmus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Programme) exchange.

Following his studies, Berendsen moved to Brussels where he became an intern at the lobbying office for [North Brabant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Brabant). From 2009 until 2015, he worked for the CDA delegation at the European Parliament. He later worked as a sustainability consultant for [PricewaterhouseCoopers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PwC) (PwC) in the Netherlands.

## Political career

Berendsen took office as a [Member of the European Parliament](258909.md) following the [2019 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_European_Parliament_election). In the European Parliament, he has since served on the [Committee on Industry, Research and Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Industry,_Research_and_Energy) and the [Committee on Regional Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Regional_Development). In addition to his committee assignments, he is part of the European Parliament's delegation for relations to the [Pan-African Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan-African_Parliament).[^cite_note-EP-1] He is also a member of the URBAN Intergroup.[^cite_note-2] He ran for [re-election in June 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_European_Parliament_election_in_the_Netherlands) as the CDA's [lead candidate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lead_candidate). As part of the campaign, Berendsen stressed the importance of the European defense industry during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine), and he advocated for an [environmentally-friendly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmentally-friendly) [industrial policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_policy).[^cite_note-3] The CDA secured three seats in the European Parliament, and Berendsen received a second term.[^cite_note-eu24-4] He has subsequently served as his party's [parliamentary leader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_leader).[^cite_note-5]

## Personal life

Berendsen was born in [Breda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breda),[^cite_note-6] and he has a wife. He is a supporter of football club [NAC Breda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAC_Breda).[^cite_note-7]
