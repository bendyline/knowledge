**Emmanuel Wanyonyi** (born 1 August 2004)[^cite_note-3] is a Kenyan [middle-distance runner](89280.md) who specialises in the [800 metres](585165.md). He won the gold medal in this event at the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_800_metres) and the [2025 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Athletics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_800_metres), and holds the world record over the [1000 metres](18950056.md) with a time of 2\:11.83 set in 2026.

With his personal best of 1\:41.11 set at the [2024 Lausanne Diamond League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Athletissima), Wanyonyi and [Wilson Kipketer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilson_Kipketer) are tied for second place in the all-time 800 metre list, behind only Wanyonyi's compatriot [David Rudisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Rudisha), who holds the world record.[^cite_note-4] Wanyonyi also briefly held the world record in the road [mile](5227492.md), with a time of 3\:54.56, from April 2024 to September 2024.

## Early and personal life

Wanyonyi is the fifth of twelve children. He has five brothers and six sisters, and is the only member of his family who runs. Due to insufficient funds, he dropped out of primary school at age 10 and worked as a cattle herder to help his family financially.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

When Wanyonyi was 18, his father died, forcing his mother to move away with his younger siblings. Wanyonyi continued to run in his free time and was noticed by a teacher who convinced him to return to school. Wanoynyi's running ability caught the attention of [Janeth Jepkosgei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janeth_Jepkosgei), who connected him with his current coach, Claudio Berardelli.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

Wanyonyi is married to [Nelly Chepchirchir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelly_Chepchirchir). Their daughter was born on 4 June 2026.[^cite_note-6]

## Career

### 2021–2023

Wanyonyi won the gold medal at the [2021 World Under-20 Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_World_Athletics_U20_Championships), setting a championship record in the process.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] At the age of 18, he placed fourth at the [2022 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_World_Athletics_Championships).[^cite_note-WA2022RS-9]

At the [2023 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_World_Athletics_Championships) in [Budapest](36787.md), Wanyonyi finished second to [Marco Arop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Arop) in the [800 metres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_World_Athletics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_800_metres), with a time of 1\:44.53 while Arop ran 1\:44.24.[^cite_note-10]

### 2024: 800 metres Olympic Champion

Wanyonyi broke the world record in the road mile with a time of 3\:54.56 at the [Adizero Road to Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adizero:_Road_to_Records) event in [Herzogenaurach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herzogenaurach), [Germany](11867.md), on 27 April. The previous record of 3\:56.13 belonged to American athlete [Hobbs Kessler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hobbs_Kessler), who finished second to Wanyonyi in a time of 3\:56.18.[^cite_note-11] Wanyonyi's record has since been broken by British athlete [Elliot Giles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliot_Giles), who ran 3\:51.3 in September.[^cite_note-12]

In June, Wanyonyi ran 1\:41.70 to win the 800 metres at the [Athletics Kenya Olympic Trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Athletics_Kenya_Olympic_Trials), making him then the 3rd-fastest performer ever over this event, behind [Wilson Kipketer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilson_Kipketer)'s 1\:41.11 and [David Rudisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Rudisha)'s world record of 1\:40.91.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

On 7 July, at the [Meeting de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meeting_de_Paris), Wanyonyi came in second place to [Djamel Sedjati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djamel_Sedjati) in the 800 meters, running a personal best of 1\:41.58 while Sedjati ran 1\:41.56. At the time, this made Wanyonyi and Sedjati the fourth and third fastest 800 meter runners in history, respectively.[^cite_note-15]

At the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics), Wanyonyi won the gold medal in the [800 metres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_800_metres), in a time of 1\:41.19, surpassing Djamel Sedjati to become the third fastest man in history at the distance, behind only compatriot David Rudisha and Danish athlete Wilson Kipketer.[^cite_note-16] Only one hundredth of a second behind Wanyonyi was the 2023 800 metre World Champion [Marco Arop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Arop), who set a new North, Central American, and Caribbean area record.[^cite_note-17]

On 22 August, Wanyonyi lowered his 800-metre personal best by 0.08 seconds to 1\:41.11 at the [Lausanne Diamond League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Athletissima). This time tied Wilson Kipketer's 1997 personal best, making Wanyonyi and Kipketer tied for second place in the all-time 800 meter list, behind only David Rudisha.[^cite_note-18]

### 2025: 800 metres World Champion

On 5 April, at the [2025 Grand Slam Track Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Grand_Slam_Track_Kingston) meeting, Wanyonyi, competing as a challenger, won the [1500 metres](2707254.md) over [2024 Olympic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_1500_metres) medalists [Cole Hocker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Hocker), [Josh Kerr](54894149.md), and [Yared Nuguse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yared_Nuguse), in a time of 3\:35.18.[^cite_note-19] The next day, he finished second in the 800 metres behind Marco Arop. With 12 points earned in the 1500 metres and 8 points earned in the 800 metres, Wanyonyi was crowned the men's short distance Slam Champion of the meeting.[^cite_note-20] On 26 April, Wanyonyi defended his title in the road mile at the [Adizero Road to Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adizero:_Road_to_Records) event, setting a new personal best of 3\:52.45, which stands as the third fastest performance in history.[^cite_note-21]

On 15 June, Wanyonyi ran a world lead of 1\:41.95 in the [Stockholm Diamond League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_BAUHAUS-galan). He improved his world lead to 1\:41.44 at [Herculis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Herculis) on 11 July.[^cite_note-22] On 19 July, Wanyonyi won the [London Athletics Meet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_London_Athletics_Meet) in a time of 1\:42.00.[^cite_note-23] On 28 August, he won the [Zürich Diamond League Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Weltklasse_Z%C3%BCrich) in 1\:42.37.[^cite_note-24]

On 20 September, Wanyonyi won the [800 metres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Athletics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_800_metres) at the [2025 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Athletics_Championships) in [Tokyo, Japan](30057.md), in a championship record time of 1\:41.86.[^cite_note-25]

### 2026: 1000 metres world record

On 10 July, Wanyonyi broke [Noah Ngeny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Ngeny)'s 27-year-old [1000 metres](18950056.md) world record of 2\:11.96 at the [Monaco Diamond League](83662105.md), in a time of 2\:11.83.[^cite_note-26]
