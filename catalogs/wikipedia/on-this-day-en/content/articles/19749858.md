The **Ministry of Emergency Situations of Kyrgyzstan** ([Kyrgyz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_language): Кыргыз Республикасынын Өзгөчө кырдаалдар министрлиги, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Kyrgyz): *Kyrgyz Respublikasynyn Özgöchö kyrdaaldar ministrligi*, [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Министерство по чрезвычайным ситуациям Кыргызстана, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Russian): *Ministerstvo po chrezvychaynym situatsiyam Kyrgyzstana*) is a special ministry in [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md) dedicated to the response of [natural disasters](58921.md) such as [earthquakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthquakes) or [landslides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landslides) or serious accidents. The current emergencies minister is Boobek Ajikeev.

## History

On July 1, 1991 the [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers) of the [Kyrgyz SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_SSR) established the State Commission for Emergency Situations of the Kyrgyz Republic based on the resolution of the establishment of a state all-union system for preventing emergency situations and emergency response by the [Council of Ministers of the USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_the_USSR). On January 6, 1992 the State Commission for Emergency Situations of the Kyrgyz Republic was reorganized into the modern Ministry of Emergency Situations of Kyrgyzstan.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] Between 1999 and 2005, during the process of [Police reform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_reform_in_Kyrgyzstan) in the [Ministry of Internal Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Interior_\(Kyrgyzstan\)), the State Fire Service was transferred to the command of the Emergency Situations Ministry. On October 6, 2008 the ministry was called upon to take action after the [2008 Kyrgyzstan earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Kyrgyzstan_earthquake).

## Cooperation

Since 2011, the Ministry of Emergency Situations has been cooperating with the [International Civil Defence Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Civil_Defence_Organisation) (ICDO). In April 2015, Minister Boronov was elected as a Vice-President of the ICDO.[^cite_note-6] In August 2018, the ministry received 22 new vehicles from the [Japanese government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_government), allowing the rescue services to render assistance at a high level on time.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]
