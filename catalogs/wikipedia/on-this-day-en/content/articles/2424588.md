**Balıkesir** (Turkish pronunciation: [\[baˈlɯcesiɾ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Turkish)) is a city in the [Marmara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marmara_Region) region of Turkey. It is the seat of [Balıkesir Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bal%C4%B1kesir_Province), which is also a [metropolitan municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_municipalities_in_Turkey). As of 2022, the population of Balıkesir Province is 1,257,590,[^cite_note-tuik-2] of which 314,958 in the city proper (the urban part of the districts [Altıeylül](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alt%C4%B1eyl%C3%BCl) and [Karesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karesi,_Bal%C4%B1kesir)).[^cite_note-citypop-1] Between 1341 and 1922, it was the capital of [Karasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjak_of_Karasi).

## History

Close to modern Balıkesir was the [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome) town of *[Hadrianutherae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadrianotherae)*, founded, as its name commemorates, by the emperor [Hadrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadrian).[^cite_note-3] Hadrian came to the region in 124 A.D., as a result of a successful bear hunting he had established a city called his name here. It is estimated that the city consisted of the castle, the homestead, the stud and a few homes. It is thought that the small town was where the current stadium is present.[^cite_note-4]

Members of the Roman and Pre-Byzantine dynasty had used this castle as a vacation area and for hunting. During the [Byzantine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine) period, the small town which had become increasingly neglected was known as *Palaiokastron* ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Παλαιόκαστρον) meaning Old Castle.[^cite_note-5]

Also, when the [Turkomans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkoman_\(ethnonym\)) came from Middle Asia to Mysia, they called it Balukiser because of the remains of the castle, as *Hisar* is the Turkish word for castle. Balıkesir's former name was Karasi because Balıkesir city was founded by [Karasi Bey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karasi_Bey) in the 13th century as using the remains of the small town. 1297 is considered as the date of establishment of the city which was one of the few to be founded by the Turks in [Anatolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatolia). The [Karasids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karasids) was a Turkic principality in [Mysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mysia). Since the 13th century, Balıkesir city have been the administrative centre of the Mysia region.

In 1345, Balıkesir city was annexed by the [Ottomans](22278.md). In 1898 an earthquake destroyed much of the city. The number of buildings that were not destroyed in the 1898 earthquake was only 51.[^cite_note-6] On 30 June 1919 Balıkesir city was occupied by the invading [Greeks](42056.md) but on 6 September 1922, the [Turkish Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Army) took back the city. During the [Turkish War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_War_of_Independence), Balıkesir was the main centre of the [Turkish militias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuva-yi_Milliye) in Western Anatolia against the occupying [Greek Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_Army).

On 3 August 1950, a major fire destroyed the centre of the city which was rebuilt later. That fire destroyed an important part of the city. According to estimates, it was the result of the firing of firecrackers belonging to child guns in a shop. According to another opinion, it was caused by the gnawing of the firecrackers of child guns by rats and this fire spread to the electrical contact. 498 shops completely burned. It is the second biggest disaster to befall in Balıkesir after the 1898 earthquake. Nearly a thousand people were unemployed. The tents were set up by the Turkish Red Crescent and food aid was provided for a long time.[^cite_note-7]

In January 1974, a cassette player and five tapes were stolen from a car belonging to Mehmet Aygün, located in the Yalın Apartment area, by unknown individuals.[^cite_note-8]

## Main sights



The historical clock tower

- *[Zaganos Pasha Mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagan_Pasha_Mosque)* built by and named after the Grand Vizier of [Mehmet the Conqueror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehmed_the_Conqueror), [Zaganos Pasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagan_Pasha) in 1461. ## Sports



Stadium of Balıkesir

Most known sports club in Balıkesir is [Balıkesirspor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bal%C4%B1kesirspor) which is established in 1966. Balıkesirspor is the [football](10568.md) team played in the [Süper Lig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%BCper_Lig) after achieving promotion having finished as runners-up of the [TFF First League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TFF_First_League) in [2013–14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013%E2%80%9314_TFF_First_League). The team's previous promotion was 40 years before that. Their stadium, the all-seater [Balıkesir Atatürk Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bal%C4%B1kesir_Atat%C3%BCrk_Stadium), has a capacity of 13,732.

## Climate

Balıkesir has a fairly continental[^cite_note-\:0-9] [hot-summer Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot-summer_Mediterranean_climate) (Csa) under the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) and a [temperate oceanic climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanic_climate) (Do) under the [Trewartha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trewartha_climate_classification) classification. Winters are cool and wet with frequent frosts and occasional snowfall, while summers are hot and dry. The continentality increases as one moves from west to east and north to south. Therefore, winters are colder and snowier in the inland parts.[^cite_note-\:0-9]
