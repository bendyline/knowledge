**Borgorose** ([Sabino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabino_dialect): *Ju Burgu*) is a *[comune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comune)* (municipality) in the [Province of Rieti](1448595.md) in the [Italian](14532.md) region of [Lazio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazio), located about 70 kilometres (43 mi) northeast of [Rome](25458.md) and about 40 kilometres (25 mi) southeast of [Rieti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rieti).

Borgorose borders the following municipalities: [L'Aquila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Aquila), [Lucoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucoli), [Magliano de' Marsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magliano_de'_Marsi), [Pescorocchiano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pescorocchiano), [Sante Marie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sante_Marie), [Tornimparte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornimparte). The *frazione* of Corvaro was the birthplace of [Antipope Nicholas V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipope_Nicholas_V).

Until 1960, the town was denominated Borgocollefegato. Near the town is the ruins of the [Romanesque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanesque_architecture) church and crypt of [San Giovanni in Leopardis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Giovanni_in_Leopardis).
