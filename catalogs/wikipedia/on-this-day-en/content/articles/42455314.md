In France, both [rape](7980471.md) and [marital rape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marital_rape) are illegal. In recent years there has been increase of reported rape cases in [France](5843419.md).

## Studies

Rape has been documented across French history. [Georges Vigarello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Vigarello) in his 2001 book writes about the history of rape in France, highlighting events from 16th to 20th century. He states that rape has historically been seen as a form of violence, but not punished as such.[^cite_note-1]

## Statistics



Annual rape and all forms of [sexual assaults](218407.md) per 100,000 people.

In 1971, the rate of declared rapes stood at 2.0 per 100,000 people.[^cite_note-2] In 1995, it was 12.5.[^cite_note-3] In 2009, it stood at 16.2.[^cite_note-4]

According to a 2012 report, about 75,000 rapes take place each year.[^cite_note-5] In 2012, there were 1,293 reported rapes in a population of 66 million,[^cite_note-6] and 1,188 rapes in 2013 in a population of 66 million.[^cite_note-7]

In 2015, **rape** rate for **France** was 20.1 cases per 100,000 population. **Rape** rate of **France** increased from 15.9 cases per 100,000 population in 2006 to 20.1 cases per 100,000 population in 2015 growing at an average annual rate of 2.72%.[^cite_note-8]

## Marital rape

The [Court of Cassation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Cassation_\(France\)) authorized prosecution of spouses for rape or sexual assault in 1990.[^cite_note-RJSimon20-9] In 1992, the [Court of Cassation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Cassation_\(France\)) convicted a man of the rape of his wife, stating that the presumption that spouses have consented to sexual acts that occur within marriage is only valid when the contrary is not proven.[^cite_note-RJSimon20-9] In 1994, Law 94-89 criminalised marital rape;[^cite_note-RJSimon20-9] a second law, passed 4 April 2006, makes rape by a partner an aggravating circumstance in prosecuting rape.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] In 2025, the [European Court of Human Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Court_of_Human_Rights) ruled in *[H.W. v. France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H.W._v._France)* that under French law and the [European Convention on Human Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Convention_on_Human_Rights), "marital duties" do not and cannot include an obligation to have sex with one's spouse without one's consent (which is not presumed to exist within marriage), overruling earlier case law from lower courts in France.[^cite_note-12]

## Gang rape

According to a 2014 report, about 5,000 to 7,000 of the rapes are [gang rapes](1371871.md).[^cite_note-13] Gang-rapes are referred to as [*tournantes*](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/viol%20en%20r%C3%A9union#Le_ph%C3%A9nom%C3%A8ne_dit_des_%C2%AB_tournantes_%C2%BB), or "pass-arounds".[^cite_note-Ireland_131–141-14][^cite_note-Hron_2010-15] One of the first people to bring public attention to the culture of gang rape was [Samira Bellil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samira_Bellil), who published a book called *[Dans l'enfer des tournantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dans_l'enfer_des_tournantes)* ("In Gang Rape Hell").[^cite_note-Ireland_131–141-14][^cite_note-Hron_2010-15]

In September 2024 he went on trial along with 50 other men. He was found guilty of the aggravated rape of his wife and the attempted aggravated rape of a co-defendant's wife and sentenced to 20 years in prison. All his co-defendants were found guilty of aggravated rape, attempted rape or sexual assault.
