The **2024 CONCACAF Under-20 Championship** was the 8th edition of the [CONCACAF Under-20 Championship](18650041.md) (29th edition if all eras included), the men's under-20 international [football](10568.md) tournament organized by [CONCACAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF). It was held in [Mexico](3966054.md) from 18 July to 4 August 2024.[^cite_note-host_and_dates-1]

The tournament featured a reduction in the number of participating teams following the format changes adopted by CONCACAF in February 2023. It was contested by 12 teams instead of the 20 that participated in previous editions.[^cite_note-Format_changes-2]

Same as previous editions, the tournament acted as the CONCACAF qualifiers for the [FIFA U-20 World Cup](1168189.md). The top four teams of the tournament (the four semi-finalists) qualified for the [2025 FIFA U-20 World Cup](73421401.md) in [Chile](5489.md) as the CONCACAF representatives.

The [United States](10986555.md) were the three-time defending champions.

## Competition format

In February 2023, CONCACAF announced format changes for its youth competitions approved by its council. The following changes were adopted for the U-17 and U-20 Championships compared to previous editions:[^cite_note-Format_changes-2]

- The number of teams automatically qualified for the final tournament was reduced to 6 from the 16 that previously qualified. These were the six top-ranked teams according to the CONCACAF Men's Under-20 Ranking while the teams ranked between 7 and 41 had to participate in qualifying stage.

- The group stage consisted of three groups instead of the four groups that were previously played. This stage included the six pre-seeded teams into this round and the six teams coming from the qualifying stage.

- The round of 16 was removed from the knockout stage, which started directly with the quarter-finals.

The tournament was divided into two stages: the group stage and the knockout stage.[^cite_note-regulations-3] The group stage featured twelve teams, including the six top-ranked teams that qualified directly and the six teams that qualified via the qualifying round. The twelve teams were split into three groups of four, and competed in a single [round-robin](939742.md). Eight teams, the top two teams of each group and the two-best third-placed teams, advanced to the knockout stage, which was played on a [single-elimination](939680.md) format and consisted of the quarter-finals, semi-finals and the final. The quarter-final winners advanced to the semi-finals and qualified for the [2025 FIFA U-20 World Cup](73421401.md). The semi-final winners played the final to determine the champion.

## Teams

### Qualification

Main article: [2024 CONCACAF U-20 Championship qualifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_CONCACAF_U-20_Championship_qualifying)

The U-20 Championship qualifying featured a new format approved by the CONCACAF Council in February 2023. The qualifying groups were increased to six instead of the four from previous editions, with the six group winners qualifying for the final tournament.[^cite_note-Format_changes-2]

A total of 27 (out of 41) CONCACAF national teams entered the qualifying round, which took place from 23 February to 2 March 2024.

The six teams from the qualifying round joined the six top-ranked teams (based on the CONCACAF men's under-20 rankings as of 12 June 2023) in the group stage of the final tournament for a total of 12 qualified teams.

### Squads

Main article: [2024 CONCACAF U-20 Championship squads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_CONCACAF_U-20_Championship_squads)

Each team was required to register a squad of up to 21 players, three of whom had to be goalkeepers. Players born between 1 January 2005 and 31 December 2009 (ages 15 to 19) were eligible to compete in the tournament (Regulations Articles 9 and 10).[^cite_note-regulations-3]

## Group stage

The top two teams of each group and the two-best third-placed teams advanced to the quarter-finals.&#x20;

### Tiebreakers

The ranking of teams in each group was determined as follows (Regulations Article 12.7):[^cite_note-regulations-3]

1. [Points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Points_\(association_football\)) obtained in all group matches (three points for a win, one for a draw, zero for a loss).

2. [Goal difference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_difference) in all group matches.

3. Number of goals scored in all group matches.

If two or more teams were still tied after applying the above criteria, their rankings were determined as follows:

1. Points obtained in matches played between the tied teams.

2. Goal difference in matches played between the tied teams.

3. Number of goals scored in matches played between the tied teams.

4. Lower number of disciplinary points based on the number of yellow and red cards in all group matches, according the following additions:&#x20;

   - Yellow card: 1 point

   - Indirect red card (second yellow card): 3 points

   - Direct red card: 4 points

   - Yellow card and direct red card: 5 points

5. Drawing of lots.

The fixture list was confirmed by CONCACAF on 11 April 2024, after the draw.[^cite_note-10] All kick-off times are [local times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Mexico), [CST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Time_Zone) ([UTC−6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC%E2%88%9206\:00)), and the [Eastern Daylight Time](496028.md) ([UTC−4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC%E2%88%9204\:00)) are in parentheses, as listed by CONCACAF.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-schedule_updated-12]
