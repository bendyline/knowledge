**Kwamouth** is a small community in [Mai-Ndombe](8502103.md) province in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md). It is the headquarters of the Kwamouth territory. The town lies at the mouth of the [Kwah River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwah_River), the last stretch of the [Kasai River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasai_River) after it has been joined by the [Fimi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fimi_River), where it enters the [Congo River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congo_River).[^cite_note-1]

On 2 February 1961 a military [Fairchild C-119G Flying Boxcar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairchild_C-119G_Flying_Boxcar) operated by the [Italian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Air_Force) was damaged beyond repair at Kwamouth. There were no fatalities.[^cite_note-2]
