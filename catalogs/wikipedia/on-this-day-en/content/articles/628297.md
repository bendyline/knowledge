**Siegburg** (German pronunciation: [\[ˈziːkbʊʁk\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Standard_German) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/e/e6/De-Siegburg.ogg/De-Siegburg.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/e/e6/De-Siegburg.ogg/De-Siegburg.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>; i.e. *[fort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort) on the [Sieg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sieg_\(river\)) river*; [Ripuarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ripuarian_language): *Sieburch*) is a city in the district of [Rhein-Sieg-Kreis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhein-Sieg-Kreis) in [North Rhine-Westphalia](38414.md), [Germany](11867.md). It is located on the banks of the rivers [Sieg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sieg_\(river\)) and [Agger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agger_\(river\)), 10 kilometres (6.2 mi) from the former seat of West German government [Bonn](3295.md) and 26 kilometres (16 mi) from [Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne). The resident population of the city was 42,204 as of December 2025.[^cite_note-pop2025-4]

## Geography

Siegburg is located approximately 8 kilometres (5.0 mi) east of the river [Rhine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhine), at the [confluence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confluence) where the [Agger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agger_\(river\)) joins the [Sieg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sieg_\(river\)), in the southeast corner of the [Cologne Lowland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne_Lowland). Neighbouring towns include [Troisdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troisdorf), [Lohmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lohmar), [Sankt Augustin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sankt_Augustin) and [Hennef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hennef_\(Sieg\)). The nearby cities of [Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne) and [Bonn](3295.md) are easily accessible through good transport links. The highest point of the urban area is 220 metres (720 ft) above sea level ([NHN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normalh%C3%B6hennull)) in the [Braschoß](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brascho%C3%9F?action=edit\&redlink=1) area and the lowest point is just under 54 metres (177 ft) above sea level at the mouth of the [Agger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agger_\(river\)).

St. Servatius and the *Zum Winter* House

[Archbishop-Elector Anno II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archbishop_Anno_II) of [Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archbishop-Electorate_of_Cologne) founded a [Benedictine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_of_Saint_Benedict) [monastery](45856.md) in 1064, known as [Michaelsberg Abbey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michaelsberg_Abbey,_Siegburg), on top of the hill also called the Michaelsberg. A settlement that arose from that was first mentioned as a city in 1182. Siegburg reached the height of its prosperity in the 15th and 16th century. It is famous for its [pottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pottery), especially for the Siegburg pitchers (*Siegburger Krüge*).

Siegburg has been the county seat of the Rhein-Sieg-Kreis since 1816.

Siegburg's [synagogue](48559.md) was destroyed on *[Kristallnacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristallnacht)*, signaling the demise of its Jewish community.

Municipal elections are held every five years, in which the mayor and city council of Siegburg, as well as the district administrator ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): *Landrat*) and district council ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): *Kreistag*) of the Rhein-Sieg-Kreis are chosen.[^cite_note-5] The last election took place on 14 September 2025.[^cite_note-6]

### City council

The [city council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_council) ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): *Stadtrat*) of Siegburg consists of 44 councillors, half of which are chosen directly in 22 constituencies. The remaining 22 seats are distributed via party lists, resulting in a proportional representation.

On 30 October 2020 the Social Democrats, Greens, and Liberals agreed to form a coalition government.[^cite_note-\:0-7]

### Mayor

The mayor of Siegburg is Stefan Rosemann, who was elected with 57.54% of the vote.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] He stated that he would withdraw from party politics and completely focus on the mayoral office.[^cite_note-\:0-7] He was sworn into office on 5 November 2020.[^cite_note-10]

## Transport

[Siegburg/Bonn station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegburg/Bonn_station) is a railway station on the [Cologne-Frankfurt high-speed railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne-Frankfurt_high-speed_railway) and the [Sieg Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sieg_Railway). The name of the station derives from it having been rebuilt for the high-speed line in order to serve [Bonn](3295.md). It is connected to Bonn by the Siegburg line of the [Bonn Stadtbahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn_Stadtbahn) every 10 or 15 minutes. The station is located in the network area of the [Verkehrsverbund Rhein-Sieg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkehrsverbund_Rhein-Sieg) (Rhine-Sieg Transport Association, VRS) public transit authority.
