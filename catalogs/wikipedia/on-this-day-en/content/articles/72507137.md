**Ceer Motors** is a Saudi Arabian [electric vehicle](279350.md) brand[^cite_note-1] that makes [sedans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedan_\(automobile\)) and [SUVs](47696.md) for the [GCC](220950.md) region.[^cite_note-2] Ceer Motors plans to manufacture seven different models by 2030.[^cite_note-arstechnica-3] The name "Ceer" means "drive forward" in Arabic.[^cite_note-4]

## History

Ceer Motors was formed through a partnership between the Saudi [Public Investment Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Investment_Fund) and the Taiwanese electronics contract manufacturer [Foxconn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxconn) in 2022.[^cite_note-5] Saudi Arabia's [sovereign wealth fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereign_wealth_fund) is projected to partner with [Apple Inc.](856.md) to build a \$9 billion facility that manufactures microchips, electric-vehicle components, and other electronics.[^cite_note-6] The joint venture is part of an effort to diversify the kingdom's economy away from being entirely oil-dependent, in line with [Saudi Vision 2030](50299103.md).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

Ceer Motors will license component technology from [BMW](3772.md) to use in the electric vehicle development process. Foxconn will develop the electrical architecture of the products.[^cite_note-10]

In June 2024, a contract worth \$2.2 billion was signed between Ceer and [Hyundai Transys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Transys), through which the Korean company will supply Ceer vehicles with integrated electric vehicle drive systems.[^cite_note-11]

## Investments

Ceer Motors is projected to contribute directly with over \$150 million of foreign investment to the kingdom and create up to 35,000 direct and indirect job opportunities.[^cite_note-arstechnica-3][^cite_note-12] By 2034, Ceer Motors is expected to contribute \$8 billion to Saudi Arabia's [GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP).[^cite_note-13] In February 2026, Ceer signed 16 agreements worth 3.7 billion [Saudi riyals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_riyal) to support the localization of electric vehicle manufacturing in the nation.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]
