The **Ministry of Health** is a [cabinet-level government ministry of Uganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Uganda). It is responsible for planning, delivering, and maintaining an efficient and effective healthcare delivery system, including preventive, curative, and rehabilitative services, in a humane, affordable, and sustainable manner. The ministry is headed by Minister of Health [Jane Aceng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Aceng).[^cite_note-1]

## Location

The [headquarters of the ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_Uganda_Headquarters) are located at Plot 6 Lourdel Road, in the [Wandegeya neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wandegeya), [Kampala Central Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampala_Central_Division), in [Kampala](56689.md), Uganda's capital and largest city, about 2.5 kilometres (2 mi) north of the city's business district.[^cite_note-2] The coordinates of the building are 0°19'59.0"N, 32°34'39.0"E (Latitude\:0.333044; Longitude\:32.577486).[^cite_note-3]
