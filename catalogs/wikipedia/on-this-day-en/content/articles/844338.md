The **Southern District** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): מָחוֹז הדָּרוֹם, *Meḥoz HaDarom*; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): لواء الجنوب) is one of [Israel](9282173.md)'s [six administrative districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Israel), the largest in terms of land area but the most [sparsely populated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density). It covers most of the [Negev](142273.md) desert, as well as the [Arava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabah) valley. The population of the Southern District is 1,444,259 and its area is 14,185 km<sup>2</sup>. Its population is 79.66% Jewish and 12.72% Arab (mostly Muslim), with 7.62% of other origins.[^cite_note-4]



Geographical distribution of the main ethno-cultural communities in the Southern District in 2008.

[^cite_note-Population_in_localities_and_statistical_areas,_by_religion,_end_of_2019-5]

The district capital is [Beersheba](312755.md), while the largest city is [Ashdod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashdod). Beersheba's [dormitory towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dormitory_town) of [Omer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omer,_Israel), [Meitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meitar), and [Lehavim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehavim) are affluent relative to other Israeli cities, while the [development towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Development_town) of [Dimona](9103.md), [Sderot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sderot), [Netivot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netivot), [Ofakim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ofakim), and [Yeruham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeruham) and the seven [Bedouin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negev_Bedouin) cities are lower on the socio-economic scale.[^cite_note-6]
