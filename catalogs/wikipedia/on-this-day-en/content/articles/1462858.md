**Tizi Ouzou** ([Kabyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabyle_language): Tawilayt n Tizi Wezzu, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ولاية تيزي وزو) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Algeria) (*[wilayah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilayah)*) of [Algeria](358.md) in the [Kabylia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabylia) region. Its capital is [Tizi Ouzou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tizi_Ouzou), with a population of 1,198,561 inhabitants in 2019. The population density is 405 people per square kilometer.

## History

In 1984, [Boumerdès Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boumerd%C3%A8s_Province) was carved out of its territory.

## Administrative divisions

The province is divided into 21 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Algeria) (*[daïras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Da%C3%AFra)*), which are further divided into 67 [*communes*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Algeria) or municipalities.
