**Verónica del Socorro Alcocer García** (born 26 May 1976) is Colombian social issues advocate who was served as [First Lady of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Lady_of_Colombia) from 2022 to 2026. She was married to [Gustavo Petro](13391962.md), the 36th [president of Colombia](251302.md), from 2000 to 2026. Previously, she served as [First Lady of Bogotá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Lady_of_Bogot%C3%A1) from 2012 to 2015. She is the third wife of a [Mayor of Bogotá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Bogot%C3%A1) to become First Lady since [Nohra Puyana de Pastrana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nohra_Puyana_de_Pastrana) in 1998.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

Verónica Alcocer was born in [Sincelejo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sincelejo). She met Gustavo Petro in 2000 at the age of 20, and the two married in December of that year. That same year, they moved to [Bogotá, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogot%C3%A1,_D.C.), where Petro already had an established political career. The couple had two children between 2002 and 2005. She had previously become a mother in 1996 to her eldest son, [Nicolás](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_of_Gustavo_Petro#Nicol%C3%A1s_Alcocer).[^cite_note-6] She has actively supported her husband in his political career, campaigning alongside him during his [2011 mayoral campaign](13391962.md) in Bogotá. Alcocer became the First Lady of Bogotá after her husband's victory in the [2011 regional elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Colombian_regional_and_municipal_elections). As First Lady, she assumed the role of social gestor of the city.

Alcocer became First Lady in 2022 after her husband [was inaugurated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inauguration_of_Gustavo_Petro) as president. Her role as First Lady diverged from the mold established by her predecessors, as she had, for the first time, her own agenda, separate from that of the president. Diplomacy and strategic negotiation became their greatest allies in promoting social causes such as the eradication of [sexual violence](731637.md) against children, child malnutrition, and access to [healthcare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Healthcare) for low-income people.

## Early life, education and marriage

Verónica del Socorro Alcocer García was born 26 May 1976 in [Sincelejo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sincelejo), [Sucre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sucre_Department) grew up in a [Conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative) family in Sincelejo, born to Jorge Emilio Alcocer (1943–2012)[^cite_note-7] and Elisabeth García de Alcocer.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Her father was the son of Sucre politician José Eustorgio Alcocer, two-time mayor of Sincelejo. Verónica's middle name was *del Socorro* in honor of the Virgin of Socorro, a saint to whom her parents have traditionally been devoted.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

As a child, Verónica dreamed of becoming a nun, showing a keen interest in helping without reward. As a teenager, she began studying law at the [Caribbean University Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caribbean_University_Corporation?action=edit\&redlink=1), a degree she would postpone twice and ultimately never complete.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

She met politician Gustavo Petro in April 2000. They met while he was giving a conference at the Caribbean University Corporation. Although he was almost twenty years her senior, she was impressed by his intelligence. During their first date, her father and uncle made it their mission to get to know him beforehand. Meanwhile, she was struggling with the stigma of being a single mother.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

Veronica and Gustavo Petro were married on 17 December 2000, in a private ceremony in [Bogotá, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogot%C3%A1,_D.C.)[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] The wedding took place months after Gustavo's divorce from Mary Luz Herrán.[^cite_note-20] Petro raised Veronica's eldest son, [Nicolás](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_of_Gustavo_Petro#Nicol%C3%A1s_Alcocer), as his own and formally adopted him in 2022.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

Despite being the wife of a left-wing president, Alcocer has expressed on multiple occasions her devotion to the [Catholic faith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_faith) and her personal views against abortion,[^cite_note-23] without expressing her repudiation of the right to abortion.[^cite_note-24]

## Role in 2022 presidential campaign

Main article: [Gustavo Petro 2022 presidential campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavo_Petro_2022_presidential_campaign)

Alcocer was characterized by being potentially active during Gustavo Petro's presidential campaign,[^cite_note-25] which was unprecedented, since traditionally no spouse of a political candidate in the [history of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Colombia) had carried out politics as a couple along with her husband, which allowed her to obtain consistent leadership as well as a positive image from the female population towards their [husband](13391962.md).[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]

In June 2022, a series of [secretly recorded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden_camera) videos of private meetings of Petro's presidential campaign were leaked, in one of which Alcocer was heard saying that all female journalists slept their way to the top. Alcocer later apologized, saying that the recordings were "taken out of context" and did not represent her ideas.[^cite_note-28]

## First Lady of Colombia (2022–2026)



Alcocer during her husband's inauguration on 7 August 2022.

Verónica became the third first lady to have also served as first lady of Bogotá, on 7 August 2022, during [Petro's inauguration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inauguration_of_Gustavo_Petro). She had previously stated that, from her position, she had viewed the role as "historically detached from the needs of the people."[^cite_note-29]

### Domestic initiatives and activities

During the heavy storms of November 2022, Alcocer was active in emergency response; on November 8, she landed in [Cartagena](63927.md)[^cite_note-30] and led a Unified Command Post which also involved Mayor [William Dau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Dau) the governor of Bolívar, Vicente Blel, the Minister of Labor, [Gloria Inés Ramírez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_In%C3%A9s_Ram%C3%ADrez), the governor of Atlántico,[^cite_note-31] [Elsa Noguera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Noguera), the general director of Social Prosperity, [Cielo Rusinque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cielo_Rusinque) the Minister of the Interior, [Alfonso Prada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Prada) and the director of the Risk Management Unit, Javier Pava.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33]

On 24 May 2023 she traveled to [La Guajira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Guajira_Department), to meet the indigenous communities in [Uribia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uribia,_La_Guajira) and [Manaure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manaure,_La_Guajira),[^cite_note-34] where she observed and listened to the main complaints of the inhabitants of these remote areas of the country,[^cite_note-35] as well as promising to be a voice from the people to the [government](71877268.md).[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37]



Verónica Alcocer during the event to eradicate sexual violence in childhood on 7 November 2024

On 7 November 2024, she attended the event against the eradication of [sexual violence](731637.md) against [children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Children). During her speech, she stated that making solutions visible for children is her main objective.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

### Policy initiatives

Alcocer has been the most politically active first lady since [Bertha Hernández de Ospina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertha_Hern%C3%A1ndez_de_Ospina).[^cite_note-40]

Alcocer's first official act as First Lady took place on the morning of Monday, 8 August 2022, when she declared her support for and subsequently signed the [gender equality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_equality) bills to be presented in [Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_Colombia) by the congresswomen of the [Historic Pact for Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historic_Pact_for_Colombia).[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42] In April 2023, she joined as a spectator the debates in [Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_Colombia) on the Health reform proposed by the [Petro administration](71877268.md), where minutes before she held a private meeting with Ministers [Carolina Corcho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolina_Corcho) of Health and [Alfonso Prada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Prada) of the Interior, in the company of Representative to the Chamber [Agmeth Escaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agmeth_Escaf) as well as some other members of the [Historic Pact](70336228.md) bench where she discussed some points of the reform and shared her points of view.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45]

On 7 June, two months later, he would again attend the debate on [pension reform](71877268.md). Later, he would participate in the marches called by the Petro administration in support of the health and pension reforms.[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48]

### Approval ratings, popularity and controversy

Within a week of assuming her role, Alcocer drew criticism from both her husband's [supporters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrism) and detractors for adopting a [white savior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_savior) attitude towards the [Afro-Colombian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Colombians) community.[^cite_note-110] Alcocer has faced criticism from her detractors, who on multiple occasions have accused her of seeking to monopolize the functions and obligations of a public office; detractors have also claimed that the title of First Lady is a burden,[^cite_note-111] merely protocol and ceremonial.[^cite_note-112][^cite_note-113]

In September 2022, vice president [Francia Márquez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francia_M%C3%A1rquez) expressed that her relationship with the [President](13391962.md) was strong. She also stated that she herself had refused the possibility of attending the [funeral of the Queen Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_state_funeral_of_Elizabeth_II), saying that when the [Minister of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Colombia\))[^cite_note-114][^cite_note-115] had proposed her to represent Colombia at the funeral[^cite_note-116] she said she was not interested in attending the funeral of a representative of [African oppression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonisation_of_Africa) and slavery, taking as a reference that Great Britain was one of the most extensive [colonial empires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonial_empires) that oppressed the [African race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_people) for many years,[^cite_note-117] Marquez urged the media and the nation to take for granted that she was chosen to represent Afro-Colombians[^cite_note-118] of her nation and to contribute from her position to the construction of peace.[^cite_note-119]

In February 2023, Alcocer joined the celebration of the [Carnival of Barranquilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barranquilla's_Carnival);[^cite_note-120][^cite_note-121][^cite_note-122] this was the first time that a First Lady in office participated in this event. Alcocer had previously been the target of criticism for the celebrations held on the night of August 7 after the [presidential inauguration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inauguration_of_Gustavo_Petro) of her husband, where videos were leaked in which she appeared dancing to the rhythm of cumbia, fandango and porro, native sounds of [Córdoba](549670.md) and [Sucre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sucre_Department), native departments of the presidential couple.[^cite_note-123][^cite_note-124]

Alcocer was harshly criticized, after a series of videos were leaked during her stay in [Madrid](41188263.md), in which she made funny faces while the [national anthem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Anthem_of_Colombia) was playing, which was classified by some as disrespectful and rude,[^cite_note-125][^cite_note-126] at the same time she was criticized for dancing in the street to the rhythm of the native [mapalé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapal%C3%A9) dance[^cite_note-127][^cite_note-128] from the [Pacific region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific/Choc%C3%B3_natural_region) of Colombia, performed by a group of Colombian musicians based in Spain.

In January 2024, Alcocer was a trend, after it was known through an investigation carried out by the newspaper *[La Silla Vacia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Silla_Vacia)* the budget allocated to its private staff, which included transportation, makeup artist, fashion advisor and photographer, this news caused great controversy, since a [first lady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Lady_of_Colombia) is not a public servant, which caused different opinions from different sectors.[^cite_note-129][^cite_note-130]

In June 2024, Alcocer denounced a campaign to smear her name by members of the [Petro administration](71877268.md).[^cite_note-131][^cite_note-132] Through a press release, Alcocer asked the Attorney General, [Luz Adriana Camargo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luz_Adriana_Camargo), to investigate the disinformation campaign against her.[^cite_note-133][^cite_note-134]

In January 2025, She participated with her daughter [Sofía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sof%C3%ADa_Petro) in the Celebrations of Saint [Francis of Assisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_of_Assisi), in [Sincelejo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sincelejo), Sucre.[^cite_note-135][^cite_note-136]

In September 2025, her public absence would become a source of speculation among the public. In November 2025, Alcocer was approached by the Swedish newspaper [Expressen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expressen) while walking through the streets of Stockholm hand in hand with her youngest daughter, [Antonella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petro_family#Antonella), and the Spanish businessman Manuel Grau.[^cite_note-137] She had established her residence at the [Strand Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand_Hotel) in [Stockholm](26741.md).[^cite_note-138][^cite_note-139]

### Fashion and style

On the day of the presidential election, Alcocer chose black pants with a blue wool vest. When she voted for her husband, she inspired everything from memes to analysis of what her four years in this position will be like.[^cite_note-140][^cite_note-141]

On 7 August 2022, for her [husband's inauguration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inauguration_of_Gustavo_Petro), Alcocer used a design made up of a jumpsuit chosen for the occasion and designed exclusively for her.[^cite_note-142][^cite_note-143] One detail included a brooch with the image of the Virgin of the Miraculous, symbol of devotion and faith; this was made in [Santa Cruz de Mompox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Cruz_de_Mompox) by designer Virgilio Madinah, and brought together faith and indigenous heritage.[^cite_note-144][^cite_note-145]

Alcocer seeks to showcase the work of Colombian artisans, appearing in public with items such as Metalero handmade jewelry, a bag by designer Adriana Ureta and gift shoes from Norberto and Antonio.[^cite_note-146]

During her state visit to unlink Spain in May 2023,[^cite_note-147] she stood out for her use of practical [ethnic outfits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_costume), representative of [Colombian culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_culture), especially with the use of [symbolism of the colors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_symbolism) fungi and white.[^cite_note-148]

For her arrival at the [Palacio Real de Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palacio_Real_de_Madrid) on 3 May 2023, Alcocer wore an ecru crepe dress, 100 percent wool, decorated with handcrafted [appliqués](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appliqu%C3%A9s), which were made by Diana Chirimía, from the [Embera katío tribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ember%C3%A1_people).[^cite_note-149] The fabrics were decorated with beads, by the indigenous embera Gladys Nacavera Güipa, while [Cali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cali) designer Andres Totalora oversaw the completion of the ensemble.[^cite_note-150][^cite_note-151]

While representing Colombia at the [Coronation of Charles III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronation_of_Charles_III), which she attended with the Minister of Foreign Affairs, [Álvaro Leyva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lvaro_Leyva), she wore a white dress with headdresses of unlink blue flowers, complemented with an unlink pale pink hat.[^cite_note-152][^cite_note-153]

## Public image

Alcocer's public interactions began to be numerous, especially when she appeared individually, without depending on her husband's schedule as has traditionally been the case for first ladies in Colombia. The public sees Alcocer as an unconventional first lady.

Over the years during the [Petro administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petro_administration), her interactions and public presence diminished rapidly, prompting speculation about her relationship with President [Gustavo Petro](13391962.md). This speculation was later confirmed by the president in November 2025, after he, along with her and several other members of the Petro administration, was included on the [Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specially_Designated_Nationals_and_Blocked_Persons_List) by US President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) in response to their actions against the [Trump administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_administration)'s anti-drug policies. President Gustavo Petro described Alcocer as a free woman, considering this action extremely unjust.[^cite_note-154][^cite_note-155][^cite_note-156]
