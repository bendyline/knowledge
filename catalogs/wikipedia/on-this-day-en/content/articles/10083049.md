**San Andros Airport** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **SAQ**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **MYAN**) is an airport near [Nicholls Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholls_Town) on [Andros Island](612578.md) in The Bahamas.[^cite_note-GCM-2]

## Facilities

The airport resides at an elevation of 5 ft (1.5 m) above [mean sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_sea_level). It has one [runway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway) designated 12/30 with a [bitumen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitumen) surface measuring 1,524 m × 23 m (5,000 ft × 75 ft).[^cite_note-WAD-1]

The airport has services from [Western Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Air), [Bahamasair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahamasair), Lynx (from Fort Lauderdale) and other small twin-engine charter planes that run between the islands.

Since November 2006, the airport has [Avgas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avgas) and [Jet A](1205435.md) available from Western Air.
