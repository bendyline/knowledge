**Zoji La** (sometimes **Zojila Pass**) is a high [mountain pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mountain_passes#India) in the [Himalayas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himalaya). It is located in the [Ganderbal district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganderbal_district) of [Jammu and Kashmir](61449812.md) and the [Kargil district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kargil_district) of [Ladakh](303611.md), both union territories of India. This pass connects the [Kashmir Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir_Valley) to its west with the [Dras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dras_River) and [Suru valleys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suru_valley) to its northeast and the [Indus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_River) valley further east. [National Highway #1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_1_\(India\)) between [Srinagar](294148.md) and [Leh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leh) in the western section of the Himalayan mountain range, traverses the pass. As of late 2022, an all-weather [Zoji-la Tunnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoji-la_Tunnel) is under construction to mitigate seasonal road blockages due to heavy snowfall.

## Etymology

According to some sources, *Zoji La* means the "mountain pass of blizzards".[^cite_note-zojitank1-2] The word for blizzards, however, is བུ་ཡུག་ (*wylie bu-yug*). Based on oral tradition that survived among the local people, Zoji refers to Du-Zhi-la, the goddess of Tibet’s four seasons. The *[Du-Zhi-lha-mo legend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Du-Zhi-lha-mo_legend?action=edit\&redlink=1)* (དུས་བཞི་ལྷ་མོ ) describes her as the wife of [Naropa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naropa).[^cite_note-KL-3]

The pass is also referred to as "Zojila Pass", which is a [misnomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misnomer). The word "pass" is redundant because the suffix "La/Lah" itself means a mountain pass in [Tibetan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibetic_languages), [Ladakhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladakhi_language), and several other languages spoken in the Himalayan region.[^cite_note-a-4]

## Location



Snow cutting machine on the Zoji La

Zoji La is about 100 km from [Srinagar](294148.md), the capital of the [Union Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Territory) of [Jammu and Kashmir](61449812.md), and 15 km from [Sonmarg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonamarg). It provides a vital link between Ladakh and the Kashmir Valley. It runs at an elevation of approximately 5,008 metres (16,430 ft), and is the second-highest pass after [Fotu La](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fotu_La) on the Srinagar–Leh National Highway. It is often closed during winter, though the [Border Roads Organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Roads_Organisation) (BRO) works to keep the pass open when possible; driving through the pass in winter means driving between thick walls of ice on both sides.[^cite_note-KL-3]

## History

### First Kashmir War

During the [First Kashmir War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Kashmir_War), Zoji La was seized by [Gilgit rebels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgit_Scouts) in 1948 in their campaign to capture [Ladakh](303611.md). Pakistan Army led by Maj. Muhammad Khan Jarral conquered Zojila pass in 1948. The pass was recaptured by Indian forces on 1 November in an assault codenamed [Operation Bison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Bison_\(Jammu_&_Kashmir_1948\)), which achieved its objective primarily due to the surprising use of tanks by Indian forces. At the time, this was the highest altitude at which tanks had operated in combat in the world.[^cite_note-Rescue-6]

## Zoji La tunnel

Main article: [Zoji-la Tunnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoji-la_Tunnel)

The Zoji-la Tunnel project was approved by the government in January 2018. Construction was inaugurated by [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister) [Narendra Modi](444222.md) in May 2018.[^cite_note-7] The 14 km long tunnel will reduce the time to cross the Zoji La from over 3 hours to just 15 minutes. The initial cost of the tunnel is US\$930 million. When completed, it will be the longest bidirectional tunnel in Asia.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]
