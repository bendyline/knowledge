Th&#x65;**&#x20;Ministry of Armed Forces** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Ministère des Armées*, pronounced [\[ministɛʁ dez‿aʁme\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French), lit. 'Ministry of the Armies') is the ministry of the [Government of France](3398091.md) in charge of managing the [French Armed Forces](10724.md) inside and outside French territory. Its head is the [Minister of the Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_the_Armed_Forces_\(France\)). From 1947 until 2017, the Ministry was designated the **Ministry of Defence** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Ministère de la Défense*). It is France's [ministry of defence](9473138.md).&#x20;

## Organisation

### Minister of the Armed Forces

The head of the department is the [Minister of the Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_the_Armed_Forces_\(France\)). The current officeholder has been [Sébastien Lecornu](54364868.md) since 2022. He reports directly to the [President of the Republic](24899.md), the [Commander-in-Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-Chief) of the [French Armed Forces](10724.md).&#x20;

His mission is to organize and manage the country's Defence Policy in liaison with other departments. He is also in charge of mobilizing troops and managing the military infrastructure. He is responsible for the French Armed forces' security to [Parliament](1009776.md).[^cite_note-3]&#x20;

### Chief of the Defence Staff



[Hexagone Balard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexagone_Balard), the headquarters of the [French Armed Forces](10724.md)

The [Chief of the Defence Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_the_Defence_Staff_\(France\)) (CEMA) reports directly to the Minister. He is in charge of conducting operations, troops training, troops inspection, programming the force's future, and gathering and analyzing Intelligence. He is also in charge of maintaining relationships with other countries' armed forces.&#x20;

The position of Chief of the Defence Staff was held by [French Army](611740.md) [General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_general_\(France\)) [Pierre de Villiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_de_Villiers) until 20 July 2017, when he resigned without an official reason. However, sources suggest that this was done as a protest against the defence budget cuts announced contrary to previous assurances to increase defence spending.[^cite_note-4] [French Army](611740.md) [General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_general_\(France\)) [François Lecointre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Lecointre) took over as Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces the following day.[^cite_note-5]&#x20;

### SGA

The Secretary-General for Administration is in charge of the general administration of the Department. He assists the Minister for:&#x20;

- Elaborating Budget

- Legal advice

- Human resources policy

- Housing resources

- Social Management

The position is held by Jean-Paul Bodin.[^cite_note-6]&#x20;

### DGA

The *[Direction Générale de l'Armement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale_de_l%27armement)* is the [research and development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_and_development) service of the Department. It is in charge of furnishing equipment to all branches of the Armed Forces and creating future equipment for them. The service manages more than 80 projects and contributed more than 7.5 billion euros to the national industry in 2011.&#x20;

## Headquarters

The headquarters of the Ministry of the Armies is at the Hotel de Brienne, in the 7th Arrondissement of Paris, but all services have since been moved to a new headquarters.&#x20;

On 5 November 2015, French president [François Hollande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Hollande) inaugurated The new French Defence Ministry headquarters at Balard Site, nicknamed [Hexagone Balard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexagone_Balard) or "Balardgon" to mimic its American counterpart [The Pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pentagon).[^cite_note-7]&#x20;

Hexagone Balard concentrates all components of the French Armed Forces, and houses the [Chief of Staff of the Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Staff_of_the_French_Army), [Chief of Staff of the Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Staff_of_the_French_Navy), [Chief of Staff of the Air and Space Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Staff_of_the_French_Air_and_Space_Force), the [Direction générale de l'armement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale_de_l%27armement), the General Secretary for the Administration and the [Chief of the Defence Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_the_Defence_Staff_\(France\)), while the office of the [Minister of the Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_the_Armed_Forces_\(France\)) remained in the Hotel de Brienne. It is a 250,000 square metres (2,690,978 Sq Ft) building on grounds measuring 39.5 acres (16.5 hectares).&#x20;

Its nickname "Hexagon" was given to the project because of the shape of the ministry building. The centre of the quadrilateral that forms the whole of the West plot consists of two buildings of hexagonal shape.[^cite_note-8]&#x20;
