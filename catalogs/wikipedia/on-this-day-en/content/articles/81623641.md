In the early hours of 17 November 2025, 45 Indian [Umrah](1591421.md) [pilgrims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_pilgrimage) were killed in [Medina](36636.md), Saudi Arabia in a bus crash after it caught fire.[^cite_note-BBC-1][^cite_note-AL-2][^cite_note-nytimes20251117-3]

## Background

The passengers were travelling from [Medina](36636.md) to the holy city of [Mecca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mecca) for the [Umrah](1591421.md), a lesser pilgrimage in [Islam](6037917.md). All of the victims were from India, including a [Karnataka](16880.md) man who had been working in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md),[^cite_note-gulf-4] with most of them from two [Hyderabad](37534.md)-based families.[^cite_note-Hindu-5]

Road accidents have been a major public safety issue in Saudi Arabia, recording significant casualties. According to a 2023 [World Health Organization](33583.md) report, the country reduced traffic deaths by nearly 35% between 2016 and 2021 due to government actions.[^cite_note-nytimes20251117-3]

## Crash

The crash happened approximately 25 kilometres (16 mi) before reaching Medina when a [diesel tanker](2863776.md) collided with the bus and the latter was engulfed by flames. Of the 45 passengers, only one survived.[^cite_note-nytimes20251117-3] It is unclear if the vehicle hit the bus while it was moving or was stationary. Saudi authorities have started an investigation about the circumstances that led to the accident.[^cite_note-6]

## Reactions

Indian Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](444222.md) said in a post on [X](9988187.md) that he was "deeply saddened" by the accident and that Indian authorities were in close contact with officials in Saudi Arabia.[^cite_note-BBC-1] The [Iranian Ministry of Foreign Affairs](1166971.md) offered condolences to the "Indian government and nation".[^cite_note-7]

[Hyderabad City Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_City_Police) Commissioner [V. C. Sajjanar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V._C._Sajjanar) stated that "the tragic bus accident involving Indian pilgrims in Saudi Arabia was deeply distressing".[^cite_note-AL-2]

[Chief Minister of Telangana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_Telangana) [Revanth Reddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revanth_Reddy) expressed shock over the crash and said that the government is in contact with the Indian Embassy in [Riyadh](51554.md), while the [Telangana](990267.md) government announced 500,000 [rupees](490783.md) ex gratia to the families of the victims.[^cite_note-Hindu-5]

The Indian Consulate General in [Jeddah](277718.md) established a camp office at the Indian Haj Pilgrims Office in Medina and helplines were activated in Hyderabad and [New Delhi](51585.md) to assist families and ensure uninterrupted communication.[^cite_note-gulf-4]
