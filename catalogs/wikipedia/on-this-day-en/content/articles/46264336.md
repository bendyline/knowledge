**Kata'ib al-Imam Ali** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): كتائب الإمام علي, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Kataʾib al-ʾImām ʿAlī*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Imam Ali Battalion'), also known as the **Imam Ali Brigades**, are an Iraqi [Shiite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiite) paramilitary organization and the armed wing of the Islamic Movement of Iraq (Harakat al-Iraq al-Islamiyah), and serves as part of the umbrella organization, [Popular Mobilization Forces](45671374.md) (PMF). Kata'ib al-Imam Ali rose to prominence during the [War in Iraq (2013–2017)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Iraq_\(2013%E2%80%932017\)), when it fought against the [Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant](9087364.md) (ISIL) alongside Iraqi government forces.[^cite_note-4] In 2026, Imam Ali Brigades have proposed a plan to place their weaponry under government control.[^cite_note-5]

## History

Kata'ib al-Imam Ali came into existence in June 2014 as the armed wing of the Harakat al-Iraq al-Islamiyah (Movement of the Islamic Iraq) party. While its emergence was linked to the large-scale Shia mobilization after the escalation of Iraq's Sunni Arab [insurgency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_insurgency_\(2011%E2%80%9313\)) into a full-out civil war, Kata'ib al-Imam Ali is closely connected to older Iraqi Shia Islamist organizations, parties and militias, as well as to the Iranian [Quds Force](4028355.md). The group's secretary general Shibl al-Zaydi is affiliated with the [Sadrist Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadrist_Movement), and was at one point member of the [anti-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Americanism) [Mahdi Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_Companies). Kata'ib al-Imam Ali also appears to enjoy the favour of [Abu Mahdi al-Muhandis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Mahdi_al-Muhandis), leader of the Popular Mobilization Forces, who has at times even personally led the group into battle. Thanks to these links, Kata'ib al-Imam Ali is well-equipped and has been able to recruit veteran militants, allowing a "meteoric growth".[^cite_note-Jihad_Intel-1][^cite_note-portrait-2]

Notably, Kata'ib al-Imam Ali has also attempted to rally [Christian Assyrians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Assyrians) to its cause since its formation, based on a purported affinity between Shia Islam and Christians and the supposed betrayal of the Iraqi Christians by [Iraqi Kurdistan](679693.md) in course of the [Fall of Mosul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Mosul). In line with these attempts, the group formed its own, though minor, Christian unit, the [Kataib Rouh Allah Issa Ibn Miriam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kataib_Rouh_Allah_Issa_Ibn_Miriam), or "Spirit of God Jesus Son of Mary Battalions".[^cite_note-Christian-3]

In late 2014, one of the group's commanders, [Abu Azrael](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Azrael), gained prominence after appearing in the media armed with axes, swords and machine guns.[^cite_note-6]

In 2015, Kata'ib al-Imam Ali began to send its fighters to [Syria](7515849.md), allegedly to protect the [Sayyidah Zaynab Shrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayyidah_Zaynab_Mosque),[^cite_note-Jihad_Intel-1] and participated in the [Second Battle of Tikrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Tikrit).[^cite_note-Tikrit-7] In early 2016, its fighters were involved in the [Syrian government offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmyra_offensive_\(March_2016\)) to reconquer [Palmyra](46709309.md) and [Tadmur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadmur) from ISIL,[^cite_note-Palmyra-8] and later that year, Kata'ib al-Imam Ali took part in the [Battle of Mosul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Mosul_\(2016\))[^cite_note-PMF_cut_off-9] and the [Aleppo offensive (November–December 2016)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleppo_offensive_\(November%E2%80%93December_2016\)).[^cite_note-array-10] In 2026, Kata'ib al-Imam Ali announced a plan to put weaponry under Iraqi governmental control.[^cite_note-11]
