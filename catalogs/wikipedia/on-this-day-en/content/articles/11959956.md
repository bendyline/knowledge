The **Internal Security Forces** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): المديرية العامة لقوى الأمن الداخلي, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *al-Mudiriyya al-'aamma li-Qiwa al-Amn al-Dakhili*; [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Forces de Sécurité Intérieure*; [abbreviated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbreviation) **ISF**) are the national police and [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie) of [Lebanon](17771.md).

Modern police were established in Lebanon in 1861, with the creation of a gendarmerie force.[^cite_note-1] In April 2005, [Ashraf Rifi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashraf_Rifi) became head of the ISF, replacing [Ali Al Hajj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Al_Hajj).[^cite_note-2] Rifi then started to recruit younger members to become part of Lebanese Intelligence. His term ended in April 2013, and he was replaced by Roger Salem,[^cite_note-tds30mar-3] and Ibrahim Basbouss subsequently. On March 8, 2017, the [Lebanese Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_government_of_December_2016) appointed Imad Othman as the ISF director general. He took command the following day.[^cite_note-4]

The number of ISF personnel reached 100,000 by 2000 and grew to over 131,000 by 2013. The ISF National Day in Lebanon is on the 9th of June.[^cite_note-5]

- Inspector-General

- Directorate General of Internal Security Forces&#x20;

  - Staff

  - Territorial Gendarmerie

  - Mobile Gendarmerie

  - Judicial Police

  - Central Administration

  - Beirut Police

  - ISF Institute

  - Social Services

  - Commander of the Security of Embassies and Public Administration

## Elite Forces

Three Special Forces Teams operate in the ISF.

1. Special Operations Group-Judicial Police.\
   &#x20;It consists of SWAT unit and BRI unit.

2. Strike Force - [Information Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_Branch).

3. Black Panthers - Mobile Forces
