**Sehlahle Fannie Masemola** (born 1964) is a South African [police officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_officer) who served as the [National Commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Commissioner) of the [South African Police Service](1228194.md) from 2022 until his suspension in April 2026.[^cite_note-v524-1][^cite_note-e770-2]

## Early life

Sehlahle Fannie Masemola was born in 1964 in [Groblersdal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groblersdal) in the [Limpopo](365944.md) province of [South Africa](17416221.md). Masemola grew up in a family of policemen and was fascinated by the law enforcement profession from a young age.[^cite_note-Cit-3] In 1987 he joined the South African Police Services.[^cite_note-h761-4]

## Education and career

Masemola has both a Diploma and a Higher Diploma in Policing and as well as a Masters in Public Management. He served 10 years in [KwaZulu-Natal](193596.md) where he was also the Deputy Provincial Commissioner, at the rank of Major General, in charge of operations before he was transferred to [Pretoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretoria) in 2010 as the Acting Divisional Commissioner of Protection and Security Services. He remained a Major General until October 2010 when he was promoted to the rank of Lieutenant General to head the Division, Protection and Security Services.[^cite_note-Cit-3][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-j244-6]

## Corruption

In August 2025, it was reported that Masemola faced arrest over allegations of corruption.[^cite_note-7] Masemola is alleged to have abused slush funds from the secret service account of crime intelligence and purchased two properties in [Pretoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretoria) (Veroz Boutique Hotel) and [Midrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midrand) (Kyalami Lodge) without adhering to proper procedures.[^cite_note-8] One of Masemola's accomplices, Lieutenant-General Dumisani Khumalo who heads crime intelligence, has been arrested for fraud and corruption related to the purchase of a boutique hotel in Pretoria North, reportedly acquired for R22.7 million and a commercial building in [Durban](313650.md), valued at R22.8m million[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] The Investigating Directorate Against Corruption denied that any warrant for his arrest had been issued, calling the reports unfounded, and emphasized that these allegations remain unproven in court.[^cite_note-11]

In September 2025, Masemola suspended his deputy, [Shadrack Sibiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadrack_Sibiya), over allegations by Kwazulu Natal Police Commissioner Lt-Gen, [Nhlanhla Mkhwanazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nhlanhla_Mkhwanazi), that Sibiya tried to disband the task team investigating political assassinations in the province.[^cite_note-12] Masemola also appeared in front of [Madlanga Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madlanga_Commission) to give evidence into the allegations that the police were working with and protecting criminals. He accused suspended Police Minister [Senzo Mchunu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senzo_Mchunu) of exceeding his constitutional mandate by ordering the immediate disbandment of the Political Killings Task Team in 2024.[^cite_note-h963-13]

In April 2026, suspended [Mpumalanga](313647.md) Police Commissioner Daphney Manamela hosted a media briefing in [Mbombela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbombela) and accused football administrator and businessman [Bobby Motaung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Motaung) of giving a bribe totaling 5 million rand to Masemola.[^cite_note-f208-14][^cite_note-15] This was for the murder case of [Jimmy Mohlala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Mohlala), who blew the whistle on the construction of the [Mbombela Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbombela_Stadium), where Motaung had interests.[^cite_note-16]

### Medicare24 Tender Scandal

In April 2024, a [R](298686.md) 360 million contract was awarded to [Vusimuzi Matlala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vusimuzi_Matlala)'s Medicare24 company to provide medical services within the [Tshwane District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tshwane). The contract was cancelled by Masemola in May 2025 after an audit report found irregularities.[^cite_note-d068-17]

In November 2025, [Brown Mogotsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_Mogotsi) testified at the [Madlanga Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madlanga_Commission) that Masemola allegedly received R7 million rand from Vusimuzi Matlala after he got the police tender worth R360 million rand.[^cite_note-18] In March 2026, Masemola was served with a warrant to appear on April 21, 2026 for his role in the awarding and approving of the tender.[^cite_note-e671-19] Masemola appeared in the Pretoria Magistrate's Court on April 21, 2026, facing four charges under the [Public Finance Management Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Finance_Management_Act).[^cite_note-j211-20] Twelve police officials, three company directors, and Matlala were also charged with [fraud](58790.md), [corruption](3608404.md), and [money laundering](19390.md) related to the procurement of the police healthcare contract.[^cite_note-e671-19]

Masemola did not enter a [plea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plea) and the case was postponed to May 13, 2026, when all co-accused are scheduled to appear together in court.[^cite_note-z549-21] Masemola has denied the allegations.[^cite_note-e770-2]

### Suspension

On April 23, 2026, President Cyril Ramaphosa placed Masemola on [precautionary suspension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspension_\(punishment\)), making him the fourth permanent head of the South African Police Service to be removed or suspended before completing a term in office.[^cite_note-v991-22] [Lieutenant-General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant-General) Puleng Dimpane, currently the [CFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CFO) for the SAPS, was appointed as [acting](691917.md) national police commissioner.[^cite_note-z549-21]

In his address, Ramaphosa stated: "In consideration of the seriousness of these charges and the critical role that the national commissioner of police plays in leading the fight against crime, I have agreed with Gen Masemola that he be on precautionary suspension pending the conclusion of the case."[^cite_note-v991-22]
