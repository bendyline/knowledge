**El Obeid Airport** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **EBD**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **HSOB**) is an [airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport) serving [El Obeid](1481380.md),[^cite_note-WAD-1][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] the [capital city](181337.md) of the [North Kurdufan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Kurdufan) state in [Sudan](27421.md).

## Facilities

The airport resides at an [elevation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elevation) of 1,927 feet (587 m) above [mean sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_sea_level). It has one [runway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway) designated 01/19 with an [asphalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphalt_concrete) surface measuring 3,000 by 45 metres (9,843 ft × 148 ft).[^cite_note-WAD-1]

## El Obeid Air Base

The airport hosts [Sudanese Air Force](550818.md) Helicopter Squadron operating [Mil Mi-24](384591.md) and [Mil Mi-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mil_Mi-35) attack helicopters.[^cite_note-5]
