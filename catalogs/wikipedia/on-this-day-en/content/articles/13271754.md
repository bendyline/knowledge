The **Chief of the General Staff** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Начальник Генерального штаба) is the head of the [General Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff_of_the_Armed_Forces_of_the_Russian_Federation) and the highest ranking officer of the [Russian Armed Forces](25709.md) or is also the senior-most uniformed military officer. He is appointed by the [President of Russia](239646.md), who is the [Supreme Commander-in-Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Commander-in-Chief_of_the_Russian_Armed_Forces). The position dates to the period of the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire). The current Chief of the General Staff is [Army General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_general_\(Russia\)) [Valery Gerasimov](37587470.md).
