A **[presidential](466686.md) election** was held in Vietnam on 7 April 2026. The election took place after a day following the first session of the [16th National Assembly of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_National_Assembly_of_Vietnam).[^cite_note-1]

General Secretary [Tô Lâm](54878346.md) was elected as Vietnam's 15th president by the [National Assembly of Vietnam](4420212.md), with all 495 delegates present voting in favor.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The current Vice President [Võ Thị Ánh Xuân](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%B5_Th%E1%BB%8B_%C3%81nh_Xu%C3%A2n) was reelected as Vietnam’s Vice President for second term with all 485 delegates present voting in favor.

## Election

### Election of President

Tô Lâm as the only candidate in Vietnam's [one-party system](102459.md), won all votes from the 495 delegates attending.
