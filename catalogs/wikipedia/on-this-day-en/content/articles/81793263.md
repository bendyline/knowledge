On 8 December 2025, at 23\:15\:10 [JST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Standard_Time) (14\:15\:10 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)), a [M<sub>w</sub>](6207163.md#Mw) 7.6 [earthquake](10106.md) struck in the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md) off the coast of [Aomori Prefecture](61732.md), [Sanriku](31176917.md), Japan.[^cite_note-anss-2] The earthquake generated tsunamis that reached heights of up to 70 cm (28 in) along the coast of northern Japan. The earthquake resulted in injuries to 47 people, damaged more than 5,000 buildings in Aomori and Hokkaido, and triggered the [Off the Coast of Hokkaido and Sanriku Subsequent Earthquake Advisory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off_the_Coast_of_Hokkaido_and_Sanriku_Subsequent_Earthquake_Advisory?action=edit\&redlink=1) due to fears of a more powerful earthquake along the Sanriku and Hokkaido coasts.

## Tectonic setting

The northern part of [Honshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honshu) and [Hokkaido](58092.md) lie above the [convergent plate boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convergent_boundary) where the [Pacific plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_plate) is [subducting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subduction) beneath the [Okhotsk microplate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okhotsk_microplate). The convergence rate across this boundary lies in the range of 7.9–9.2 cm (3.1–3.6 in) per year.[^cite_note-Nishimura_2000-3] The plate interface in the area of the earthquake epicenter shows an abrupt increase in dip from about 5° to about 15°, 80 km (50 mi) landward of the [Japan Trench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Trench).[^cite_note-Ito-4] This part of the plate boundary has been the location of many powerful historical earthquakes, dating back to the [869 Sanriku earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/869_Jogan_Sanriku_earthquake) and most recently of the devastating [2011 Tōhoku earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_T%C5%8Dhoku_earthquake_and_tsunami). Most of these events relate to rupture along the plate interface, but some, such as the [1933 Sanriku earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1933_Sanriku_earthquake),[^cite_note-Kanamori-5] involved deformation within the subducting plate. According to the studies of past great earthquakes, a weak seismic coupling is suggested in the offshore [Sanriku](31176917.md) region.[^cite_note-Takahashi-6] From the distribution of past seismicity, the width of coupling at 40°N was assumed to be about 150 km (93 mi).[^cite_note-Kawasaki_2001-7]

## Earthquake

[Map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special\:Map/7/41.1/142.6/en)

Map of aftershocks ([map data](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Data\:2025%20Aomori%20earthquake%20-%20JMA.map))

The earthquake occurred on 8 December 2025 at 23\:15 [JST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Standard_Time) (14\:15 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)) with an epicenter off the coast of [Aomori Prefecture](61732.md). The [United States Geological Survey](23814944.md) said it measured the earthquake as [M<sub>w</sub>](6207163.md#Mw) 7.6 and was caused by thrust faulting at a depth of 40.7 km (25.3 mi).[^cite_note-anss-2] The [Japan Meteorological Agency](457886.md) placed the earthquake's magnitude at [M<sub>w</sub>](6207163.md#Mw) 7.5[^cite_note-8] and measured a maximum intensity of upper 6 on the [Shindo scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Meteorological_Agency_seismic_intensity_scale) in [Hachinohe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hachinohe).[^cite_note-nhk-9] Shaking was felt as far as [Tokyo](30057.md)[^cite_note-tokyo-10] and lasted for more than 30 seconds.[^cite_note-cnnduration-11] A USGS seismic installation at [Noheji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noheji) recorded 0.3326 *g* in ground acceleration (pga) and 44.82 cm/s (17.65 in/s) in ground velocity (pgv); the station data corresponded to a [Modified Mercalli intensity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modified_Mercalli_intensity) (MMI) of VIII (*Severe*); another installation at [Hachinohe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hachinohe) recorded a pga of 0.472 *g*.[^cite_note-12] A 5.5 [mb](6207163.md#mb) aftershock occurred at 23\:33 JST (14\:33 UTC),[^cite_note-13] followed by a M<sub>ww</sub>6.0 aftershock at 03\:56 JST (18\:56 UTC)[^cite_note-14] and a M<sub>ww</sub>6.6 aftershock at 06\:52 JST (21\:52 UTC).[^cite_note-15] A M<sub>ww</sub>6.7 aftershock occurred at 11\:44 JST (02\:44 UTC) on 12 December.[^cite_note-16] A M<sub>ww</sub>6.0 aftershock occurred at 23\:26 JST (14\:26 UTC) on 31 December.[^cite_note-17]

## Tsunami

Tsunami warnings for waves of up to 3 m (9.8 ft)[^cite_note-tokyo-10] were issued along the Pacific coasts of Aomori, [Iwate](183477.md), and [Hokkaido](58092.md) Prefectures,[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] while a tsunami advisory was issued in [Miyagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyagi_Prefecture) and [Fukushima Prefectures](211914.md).[^cite_note-nhk-9] More than 90,000 people were ordered to be evacuated.[^cite_note-21] The [Pacific Tsunami Warning Center](1359407.md) said hazardous waves were possible as far as 1,000 km (620 mi) from the epicenter, affecting the coasts of Japan and Russia.[^cite_note-aj-22] The JMA lifted all tsunami advisories at 06\:30 JST on 9 December.[^cite_note-oyster-23]

Waves reaching up to 70 cm (28 in) were observed in [Kuji](328212.md),[^cite_note-kyodotsunheight-24] while 50 cm (20 in) waves were observed in [Urakawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urakawa,_Hokkaido). Waves reaching 40 cm (16 in) were observed in [Mutsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutsu,_Aomori) and [Hachinohe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hachinohe). In [Erimo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erimo,_Hokkaido), wave heights reached 30 cm (12 in), and in [Tomakomai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomakomai), [Shiraoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiraoi,_Hokkaido), [Kamaishi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamaishi), [Ishinomaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ishinomaki), and [Sendai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sendai), it was 20 cm (7.9 in).[^cite_note-25]

Following the M<sub>ww</sub>6.7 aftershock on 12 December, the JMA issued tsunami advisories for waves of up to 1 m (3 ft 3 in) off the Pacific coast of Hokkaido, Aomori, Iwate and Miyagi prefectures.[^cite_note-26] An evacuation order was issued by authorities in Sendai.[^cite_note-27] Waves measuring 20 cm (7.9 in) were observed in Erimo and Hachinohe, after which the advisory was lifted at 14\:05 JST (05\:05 UTC).[^cite_note-aftershock-28]

## Impact

At least 47 people were injured including 32 in Aomori, 11 in Hokkaido, and 4 in [Iwate](183477.md).[^cite_note-fdma-29] In Aomori, 65 homes collapsed and 5,044 structures were damaged overall, including 2,436 homes, 460 commercial and industrial buildings, 271 schools, 214 welfare facilities, 63 hospitals, 27 tourist facilities, 7 ports, and 5 prefectural facilities. There were 16 injuries in [Hachinohe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hachinohe), 3 in [Gonohe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonohe), 2 each in [Mutsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutsu,_Aomori), [Oirase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oirase), [Tōhoku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C5%8Dhoku,_Aomori) and [Misawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misawa,_Aomori), and 1 each in [Hashikami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hashikami), [Aomori City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aomori), [Nanbu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanbu,_Aomori), [Hiranai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiranai), [Goshogawara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goshogawara).[^cite_note-aomori-30] In Hachinohe, 57 homes partially or fully collapsed, 2,013 others[^cite_note-aomori-30] and a restaurant were damaged, windows shattered and ground heaves were reported.[^cite_note-31] [Liquefaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquefaction) damage also occurred at the [Port of Hachinohe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Hachinohe), with [Higashidōri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higashid%C5%8Dri), [Yokohama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yokohama,_Aomori) and [Misawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misawa,_Aomori)'s ports also suffering damage.[^cite_note-aomori-30] Two fires occurred in Aomori City,[^cite_note-32] while one person was injured after his car fell into a hole in [Tōhoku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C5%8Dhoku,_Aomori).[^cite_note-apnews1-33] An elderly woman was wounded by a falling cabinet in [Oirase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oirase), while another woman was injured by falling debris in her home in [Goshogawara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goshogawara). Two people were also injured after falling in [Tomakomai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomakomai), Hokkaido.[^cite_note-iwate3-34] Two homes were damaged in the prefecture.[^cite_note-fdma-29]

Services on the [Tōhoku Shinkansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C5%8Dhoku_Shinkansen) between [Shin-Aomori Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shin-Aomori_Station) and [Fukushima Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukushima_Station_\(Fukushima\)) were suspended.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] A [shinkansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinkansen) train carrying 94 passengers was stranded in Aomori.[^cite_note-tokyo-10] In total, 26 trains were suspended and nine were delayed for up to 71 minutes, affecting around 17,000 people. Sixty passengers were transferred to buses.[^cite_note-37] The release of treated wastewater from the [Fukushima Daiichi Nuclear Power Plant](11986472.md) into the Pacific Ocean was suspended.[^cite_note-nhk-9] Around 480 L (110 imp gal; 130 US gal) of water spilled from a spent fuel cooling area at the [Rokkasho Reprocessing Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rokkasho_Reprocessing_Plant). The [Nuclear Regulation Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_Regulation_Authority) said the facility's water level remained within the normal range and there was no safety concern.[^cite_note-oyster-23]

Power outages were reported in Iwate and Hokkaido.[^cite_note-38] Around 2,700 houses in Aomori Prefecture lost electricity.[^cite_note-39] Several oyster rafts were damaged by tsunami waves. The ceiling of the domestic terminal at [New Chitose Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Chitose_Airport) partially cracked and collapsed, stranding 200 passengers.[^cite_note-oyster-23] A hospital in [Mutsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutsu,_Aomori) was partially flooded after its sprinkler system was damaged, forcing the evacuation of 31 patients. Around 1,360 homes in Aomori and Iwate lost water due to damaged pipes.[^cite_note-mutsu-40] Classes were suspended at more than 300 schools in Hokkaido, Aomori, Iwate, Miyagi and Fukushima prefectures.[^cite_note-suspended-41] At least 450 buildings in Aomori were damaged.[^cite_note-42]

Following the M<sub>ww</sub>6.7 aftershock on 12 December, authorities in Hachinohe ordered the evacuation of 48 households near a telecom tower owned by [NTT East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nippon_Telegraph_and_Telephone) that was damaged during the mainshock on 8 December, citing the risk of a total collapse.[^cite_note-43] Services on the Tohoku Shinkansen were again suspended due to power outages between Sendai and Aomori stations.[^cite_note-aftershock-28] A woman was injured due to a fall in [Hiranai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiranai).[^cite_note-44]

## Response



Japan's Defense Minister [Shinjiro Koizumi](27407451.md) comments on the earthquake response

The Japanese government established a task force to respond to the earthquake.[^cite_note-nhk-9] Around 620 residents and 270 vehicles sheltered at [JSDF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JSDF) facilities in Hachinohe,[^cite_note-mutsu-40] while 18 JSDF helicopters were mobilized to conduct a damage assessment.[^cite_note-oyster-23] Up to 342 evacuation shelters were opened across five prefectures and used by around 9,200 people.[^cite_note-suspended-41]

Following the earthquake, the JMA issued the first "[Off the Coast of Hokkaido and Sanriku Subsequent Earthquake Advisory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off_the_Coast_of_Hokkaido_and_Sanriku_Subsequent_Earthquake_Advisory?action=edit\&redlink=1)", advising of an increased possibility of a stronger earthquake hitting the Hokkaido and Sanriku coasts in the following days.[^cite_note-tokyo-10] The advisory is issued following a large (7+ [M<sub>w</sub>](6207163.md#Mw)) earthquake in the region of the [Kuril–Kamchatka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuril%E2%80%93Kamchatka_Trench) and [Japan Trenches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Trench) and analysis determines that the chance of a larger earthquake increases from 0.1% per week to 1% per week.[^cite_note-45] For example, the M<sub>w</sub>9.1 [2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_T%C5%8Dhoku_earthquake_and_tsunami) was preceded by a M<sub>w</sub>7.2 [foreshock](1160429.md) two days prior.[^cite_note-46] The purpose of the advisory is to increase disaster preparedness in the potentially affected areas along Japan's Pacific coasts, however it does not begin evacuation procedures.[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48] The advisory affected 182 municipalities from Hokkaido to [Chiba Prefecture](179796.md).[^cite_note-oyster-23] Its issuance led to an increase in northeastern Japan of purchases of disaster-related items such as bottled water, disaster kits, torches, water storage tanks and support poles.[^cite_note-49] The JMA lifted the advisory on 16 December.[^cite_note-50]
