**Ermont** (French pronunciation: [\[ɛʁmɔ̃\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/0/0b/Fr-Paris--Ermont.ogg/Fr-Paris--Ermont.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/0/0b/Fr-Paris--Ermont.ogg/Fr-Paris--Ermont.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in the [Val-d'Oise](90616.md) department, in the northern suburbs of [Paris](22989.md), [France](5843419.md). It is located 17.2 km (10.7 mi) from the [center of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometre_Zero). It has around 29,000 inhabitants, which makes Ermont one of the most important cities in Val d'Oise. Ermont has experienced rapid urbanization thanks to railway transport and industrialization, with the population of Ermont being just 9,000 after the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War).

## Transport

Ermont is served by [Ermont–Eaubonne station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ermont%E2%80%93Eaubonne_station) which is an interchange station on Paris [RER line C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RER_C), on the [Transilien Paris-Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transilien_Paris-Nord) suburban rail line, and on the [Transilien Paris-Saint-Lazare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transilien_Paris-Saint-Lazare) suburban rail line.

Ermont is also served by [Cernay station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cernay_station) which is an interchange station on Paris [RER line C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RER_C) and on the Transilien Paris-Nord suburban rail line.

Finally, Ermont is also served by two stations on the Transilien Paris – Nord suburban rail line: [Ermont-Halte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ermont-Halte_station) and [Gros Noyer–Saint-Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gros_Noyer%E2%80%93Saint-Prix_station).
