**Kwara** ([/ˈkwɑːrə/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English); [listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/2/26/LL-Q34311_%28yor%29-Pet002-Kwara_state.wav/LL-Q34311_%28yor%29-Pet002-Kwara_state.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a [state](226734.md) in Western [Nigeria](21383.md), bordered to the east by [Kogi State](299399.md), to the north by [Niger State](1723431.md), and to the south by [Ekiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekiti_State), [Osun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osun_State), and [Oyo](2227658.md) states, while its western border makes up part of the [international border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benin%E2%80%93Nigeria_border) with [Benin](3459.md).[^cite_note-\:4-5][^cite_note-Creation-6] Its capital is the city of [Ilorin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilorin) and the state has 16 [local government areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_areas_of_Nigeria).

Of the 36 [states of Nigeria](226734.md), Kwara is the [ninth largest in area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_area) but the [sixth least populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population) with an estimated population of about 3.2 million as of 2016.[^cite_note-\:5-7] Geographically, Kwara is split between the [West Sudanian savanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Sudanian_savanna) in the east and the [Guinean forest–savanna mosaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinean_forest%E2%80%93savanna_mosaic) [ecoregion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ecoregions_in_Nigeria) in the rest of the state. Important geographic features include rivers with the [Niger](21437.md) flowing along the northern border into [Lake Jebba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Jebba?action=edit\&redlink=1) before continuing as the border while the Awun, Asa, Aluko, and Oyun rivers flow through the interior. In the far northwest of the state is the [Borgu section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borgu_Game_Reserve) of the [Kainji National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainji_National_Park), a large [national park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_national_parks_of_Nigeria) that contains populations of [grey heron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grey_heron), [kob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kob), [hippopotamus](37764.md), [African bush elephant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_bush_elephant), [olive baboon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_baboon), and [roan antelope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roan_antelope), along with some of the last remaining [West African lions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_African_lion) on earth.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] In the far southwest, a small part of the [Old Oyo National Park](4562292.md) contains [crowned eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowned_eagle), [martial eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martial_eagle), [African buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_buffalo), [oribi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oribi), and [patas monkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patas_monkey) populations.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

Kwara State has been inhabited for ages by various ethnic groups, primarily the majority [Yoruba people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoruba_people) that live throughout the state. There are also sizable minorities of [Nupe people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_people) in the northeast, [Bariba (Baatonu)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bariba_people) and [Busa (Bokobaru)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bissa_people) peoples in the west, and [Fulani](102383.md) people in [Ilorin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilorin) and moving through the state as nomadic [herders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulani_herdsmen).[^cite_note-14]

In the pre-colonial period, the majority of the area that is now Kwara state was part of the [Oyo Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyo_Empire), with part of the western portions in the [Borgu Kingdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borgu) peopled by the Bariba, [Boko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boko_language) and Bissa people, and [Nupe Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_Kingdom) (1531–1835). In the mid-1800s, the [Fulani jihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulani_jihad) annexed some part of what is now the state of Kwara and placed the area under the Gwandu sphere of the [Sokoto Caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_Caliphate). In the 1890s and 1900s, British expeditions occupied the area and incorporated it into the [Northern Nigeria Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nigeria_Protectorate). The [Northern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nigeria) Nigeria Protectorate later merged into [British Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Nigeria) in 1914, before becoming part of independent [Nigeria](21383.md) in 1960. Originally, the modern-day Kwara state was a part of the post-independence [Northern Region](2292852.md) until 1967, when the region was split and the area became the [West Central State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Central_State). In 1976, the state was renamed Kwara State and the name remained until the 1990s when its southeast was split off to form a part of [Kogi state](299399.md) and its far northwest Borgu division was annexed into the Borgu division of [Niger state](1723431.md).

Economically, Kwara state is largely based around agriculture, mainly of [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffea_robusta), [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton), [groundnut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut), [cocoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_bean), [oil palm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_palm), and [kola nut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kola_nut) crops. Other key industries are services, especially in the city of [Ilorin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilorin), and the [livestock herding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herding) and [ranching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranching) of cattle, goats, and sheep. Kwara state has the [joint-twentieth highest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_Human_Development_Index#2019) [Human Development Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Development_Index) in the country and numerous institutions of tertiary education.[^cite_note-15]

## History

Kwara State was created on 27 May 1967,[^cite_note-kwasang.org.uk-16] when the Federal Military Government of General [Yakubu Gowon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakubu_Gowon) broke the four regions that then constituted the [Federation of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Nigeria) into 12 states. At its creation, the state was made up of the former [Ilorin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilorin) and [Kabba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabba) provinces of the then [Northern Region](2292852.md) and was initially named the West Central State but later changed to "Kwara", a local name for the [River Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Niger), in the [Hausa language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language).[^cite_note-17]

Kwara state has since 1976 reduced considerably in size due to further state creation exercises in Nigeria. On 13 February 1976, the [Idah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idah)/[Dekina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dekina) part of the state was carved out and merged with a part of the then [Benue/Plateau state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue/Plateau_State), to form [Benue state](1556364.md).[^cite_note-18]

On 27 August 1991, five Local Government areas, namely [Oyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyi), [Yagba](https://www.behance.net/gallery/18479533/Yagba-VillageKogi-stateNigeria), [Okene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okene), [Okehi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okehi) and [Kogi](299399.md) were also excised to form part of the new [Kogi state](299399.md), while a sixth, [Borgu Local Government Area](29291193.md), was merged with [Niger state](1723431.md).[^cite_note-19] The major populated local governments are [Ilorin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilorin) and [Offa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offa,_Kwara).[^cite_note-20]

Kwara state has numerous mineral resources such as [tourmaline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourmaline), [tantalite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tantalite), and many mineral deposits in the northern part. [Cocoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_bean) and [kolanut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolanut) in the Southern parts [Oke Ero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oke_Ero), [Ekiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekiti,_Kwara) and [Isin LGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isin,_Kwara).[^cite_note-21]

## Population

As of 2006, the population of Kwarans was 2.37 million, based on the Nigerian [2006 Census](21386.md).[^cite_note-PHC_Tables-1] This population size constitutes about 1.69% of the nation's total population having relied upon immigration for population growth and [socio-economic development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socio-economic_development).[^cite_note-22] The principal ethnic groups are Yoruba, Nupe, Fulani, and Baruba.[^cite_note-\:2-23] The population estimate of Kwara State, as at July 2024, is put at 3,390,330.[^cite_note-24]

Residents of the state are referred to as Kwarans.[^cite_note-25] [Christianity](12241851.md), [Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Nigeria) and [traditional faiths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_traditional_religions) coexist in the state.[^cite_note-26]

## Politics

Like every other state in Nigeria, Kwara State has had a mix of civilian and military Governors. The first Governor of Kwara state was [David Bamigboye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bamigboye). The current [governor of Kwara State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Kwara_State) is Governor [Abdulrazaq Abdulrahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulrazaq_Abdulrahman), who emerged victorious in the [9 March 2019 governorship election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Nigerian_gubernatorial_elections), under the [APC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Progressives_Congress). Abdulrahman Abdulrazak was sworn in on 29 May 2019, making him the 4th democratic governor of Kwara State and the 20th governor of Kwara State overall. Kayode Alabi is the deputy governor of Kwara state under AbdulRahman's administration.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30] On 19 March 2023, Abdulrazak won re-election[^cite_note-31] and will remain governor until 2027 when he will be constitutionally ineligible to re-contest for a third term in office.[^cite_note-32] The governor heads the [Kwara State Executive Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwara_State_Executive_Council). The legislature is the [Kwara State House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwara_State_House_of_Assembly).

## Climate

Located at an elevation of 286.86 metres (941.1 ft) above sea level, Kwara has a [Tropical wet and dry or savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) (Classification: Aw). The city's yearly temperature is 29.54 °C (85.17 °F) and it is 0.08% higher than Nigeria's averages. Kwara typically receives 101.45 mm (4 in) of [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation) and has 148.38 rainy days (40.65% of the time) annually.[^cite_note-\:3-52]

## Incidents

See also: [Kwara boat disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwara_boat_disaster)

According to reports, a boat capsized and broke in two on 12 June 2023, in the Niger River close to Pategi, in Kwara State, Nigeria. The guests on the boat were originally arriving on motorcycles, but the intense rain left them stranded. It was confirmed that at least 108 people have died and many are absent.[^cite_note-\:4-5]

On 25 October 2023, a tree fall killed two people and injured at least five.[^cite_note-54][^cite_note-55]

On 2 February 2024, Ejire Adeyemi Toun, the traditional ruler was assassinated in his palace, and his wife was kidnapped.[^cite_note-56]

On 4 June 2025, unknown militants ambushed a mining site at Oreke-Okeigbo, the attackers killed two police officers, Assistant Superintendent Haruna Watsai and Inspector Tukur Ogah. The attackers also abducted two workers David Adenaiye and a Chinese national Sam Xie Wie.[^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58]

## Economy

Agriculture is the main source of the state's economy and the principal cash crops are: cotton, [cocoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_bean), coffee, [kola nut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kola_nut), tobacco, [sesame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesame) and palm produce. Kwara state is home to [Shonga Farms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shonga_Farms), a product of the Back-to-Farm project of one of the past administrators of the state, Dr. [Abubakar Bukola Saraki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bukola_Saraki). Shonga Farms is made up of 13 commercial farmers. [Mineral resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_resource) in the state are petroleum, gold, [limestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone), [marble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble), [feldspar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feldspar), [clay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay), [kaolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaolin), [quartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartz), [granite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granite), and [laterite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laterite) found majorly in Omuaran.[^cite_note-60]

In recent years, Kwara state has made efforts to promote technology and innovation as part of its economic growth strategy. [Ilorin Innovation Hub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilorin_Innovation_Hub) is one such initiative aimed at supporting startups and driving skills development across the state.[^cite_note-\:5-7][^cite_note-61]

### Infrastructure

- **Hygiene and Sanitation**

The government of Nigeria is increasingly aware of problems emanating from poor environmental sanitation, and Kwara state is working on improving its environment and sanitation.[^cite_note-62] On 22 September 2020, the Kwara state governor officially commenced the 'Clean Kwara' Campaign to end open defecation and promote good [hygiene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hygiene) in the state.[^cite_note-\:0-63] The state government is working to fix all the roads and waterways to ensure free water flow,[^cite_note-64] putting in efforts to ensure [water sanitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water,_Sanitation_and_Hygiene_Monitoring_Program),[^cite_note-\:1-65] building [toilets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toilets), and making clean water accessible.[^cite_note-\:1-65] This is also to promote [Sustainable Development Goals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_Development_Goals) 3 & 6 (general access to safe and affordable drinking water and access to adequate and equitable sanitation, hygiene for all, and end [open defecation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_defecation) by 2030)[^cite_note-\:0-63]

## Activities

In 2025, the Kwara state Government launched a one-day Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Pedagogy training for public senior secondary school teachers to equip educations with digital teaching skills. The initiative aims to modernize classroom learning, promote AI regulation in education, and prepare teachers and students for the demand of the digital age.[^cite_note-66][^cite_note-67][^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69]

2026, Kwara State Governor AbdulRahman AbdulRasaq called for strong partnerships with researchers, investors, and innovators to scale AI solutions across agriculture, healthcare, education, and governance. He also stressed the need for ethical AI regulations while highlighting the state's investment in digital skills, innovation hubs, and partnership with organizations such as MTH and IHS Towers to position Kwara as a region technology hub.[^cite_note-70] [^cite_note-71][^cite_note-72][^cite_note-73]

2026, The Ilorin Innovation Hub (IIH) hosted its first Demo Day "Convergence," bringing together investors, government officials, and industry leaders to showcase startups from its incubation and acceleration programmes. The event marked the Hub's first anniversary and highlighted efforts by the Kwrara State Government and IHS Tower to strengthen innovation, entrepreneurship, and digital talent development in the state.[^cite_note-74][^cite_note-75][^cite_note-76]

The Global Feminism in Solidarity and Action initiative, in partnership Webfala Digital Skills for All Initiative, launches a four-month digital skills training programme for 150 women in Kwara State. The programme aims to equip participants with digital and STEM skills, promote women's economic empowerment, and help address gender inequality and sexual and gender-based violence(SGBV). ## Sports

Sporting activities are managed by the Kwara State Sports Commission.[^cite_note-81][^cite_note-82] The importance attached to sports led to the construction of a stadium, named—[Kwara State Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwara_State_Stadium) Complex. The facilities available at the stadium complex are the mainbowl, indoor sports hall, hostel, recreational press centre as well as an Olympic size swimming pool. The state is actively represented both in football and basketball. The state is the home to the [Kwara United Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwara_United_FC), [ABS FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABS_FC) and [Kwara Falcons Basketball Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwara_Falcons_Basketball_Club?action=edit\&redlink=1).
