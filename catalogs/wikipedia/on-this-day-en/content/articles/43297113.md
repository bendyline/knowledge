**Albulena Haxhiu**[^cite_note-2] (born 11 May 1987) is a Kosovar politician who has served as the acting [president of Kosovo](1260820.md) since 2026.[^cite_note-3] She has previously served as the Minister of Justice of Kosovo, and as a member of parliament for [Vetëvendosje](3225595.md) in the Assembly of Kosovo.[^cite_note-Assembly-4][^cite_note-Kallxo-5]

## Biography

### Early life and education

Albulena Haxhiu was born on 11 May 1987 in [Pristina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pristina) what was then the [Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia) (now [Pristina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pristina), [Kosovo](17391.md)).[^cite_note-bio-6] Her grandfather was Kosovar political activist [Ahmet Haxhiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmet_Haxhiu).[^cite_note-Epoka-7] Haxhiu studied law and finance at the [University of Pristina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pristina).[^cite_note-Assembly-4] She then studied criminal law at the [South East European University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_East_European_University) in [Tetovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetovo).[^cite_note-8]

### Early political career

A member of [Vetëvendosje](3225595.md) (LVV), Haxhiu was elected as a member of the [Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo](1068656.md) in [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Kosovan_parliamentary_election). She was re-elected in [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Kosovan_parliamentary_election), [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Kosovan_parliamentary_election), and [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Kosovan_parliamentary_election). In the Assembly, she sat on or chaired several committees, including Committee on Legislation and the Subcommittee on Mandates, Immunities and Rules of Procedure.[^cite_note-bio-6]

After LVV became the largest party in the Assembly in 2019, Haxhiu was appointed as Minister of Justice in February 2020 by Prime Minister [Albin Kurti](4774301.md) in [his first cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_cabinet_of_Albin_Kurti), but only served until June 2020 after the government lost a no-confidence vote.[^cite_note-WhoisWho-9] After LVV won an even larger share of the vote in the [2021 Kosovan local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Kosovan_local_elections), Haxhiu was re-appointed as Minister of Justice in the [second Kurti cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_cabinet_of_Albin_Kurti).[^cite_note-bio-6] As Minister of Justice, she faced criticism for failing to enact judicial reform and violating [judicial independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_independence).[^cite_note-WhoisWho-9]

### Assembly Speaker

Despite LVV losing seats in the [February 2025 Kosovan parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_2025_Kosovan_parliamentary_election), Haxhiu was selected as the party's candidate for [Assembly speaker](1260708.md). Other factions in the Assembly repeatedly blocked her candidacy, and in August 2025 [Dimal Basha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimal_Basha) was elected instead.[^cite_note-10] After the [December 2025 Kosovan parliamentary election](81646979.md), Haxhiu was elected as speaker by a vote of 66-44, with eight abstentions.[^cite_note-11]

### Acting presidency

After President [Vjosa Osmani](61979540.md)'s term expired in April 2026 without a new president having been elected, Haxhiu automatically became acting president as speaker of the Assembly. She will serve until a new president is elected, or for at most six months.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-Hasani-13] On 28 April, the Assembly again failed to [elect a president](67301945.md). As a result, it was dissolved and [new elections](82589105.md) held.[^cite_note-14]

### Personal life

She is married and has three children.[^cite_note-Kallxo-5][^cite_note-WhoisWho-9] Haxhiu's husband, Alban Krasniqi, was a candidate for mayor of [Malishevë](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malishev%C3%AB) in 2013, 2021 and 2025.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]
