[Christian Harrison](38874563.md) and [Neal Skupski](40157604.md) defeated [Kevin Krawietz](23521626.md) and [Tim Pütz](41501290.md) in the final, 6–4, 7–6<sup>(8–6)</sup> to win the men's doubles tennis title at the [2026 US Open](80928175.md). It was Skupski's third [major](197638.md#Tournaments) title and Harrison's second, as well as their second major as a team this year, following the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles). They did not drop a set en route to the title.[^cite_note-1]

[Marcel Granollers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Granollers) and [Horacio Zeballos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horacio_Zeballos) were the defending champions,[^cite_note-2] but lost in the quarterfinals to [Rajeev Ram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajeev_Ram) and [Joe Salisbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Salisbury), in what was both of their final professional appearances. The former world No. 1s, two-time ATP Finals champions, four-time major champions, and three-time [US Open](82729.md) champions lost in the semifinals to Krawietz and Pütz.[^cite_note-3]
