**Taiz** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): تَعِزّ, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Taʿizz*) is a [governorate](690277.md) of [Yemen](350939.md). Its capital [Taiz](545270.md), the third-largest city in Yemen, is among the most important commercial centres in the country, owing to its proximity to farmland, the [Red Sea](26191.md) port of [Mokha](2082636.md) and [Taiz International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiz_International_Airport).

## Geography

Taiz Governorate has an extraordinarily diverse geography. The western half is part of the [Tihamah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tihamah) [coastal plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_plain) and has an exceedingly [hot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat), [humid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humidity) and [arid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arid) [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate). The eastern half is very mountainous, with the major peak being 3,070-metre-high [Jabal Sabir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabal_Sabir), near Taiz city. These mountains trap the moisture created by an upper-level wind reversal between April and October, so that annual [rainfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainfall) increases from 200 millimetres (8 inches) in the foothills to more than 1,000 millimetres (40 inches) near Jebel Saber. Temperatures in the highlands remain high during the daytime, but at the highest elevations they can fall to -5 °C (23 °F) overnight.

### Mountain ranges



Road in [Jabal Sabir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabal_Sabir)

The governorate has many mountains ranging in height from 1,000 to 3,200 metres. The most important mountains are Jabal Sabir, [Jabal Habashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabal_Habashi), [Jabal Samaʿa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same'a_District), [Jabal Qadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabal_Qadas), [Jabal As-Silw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabal_As-Silw), the mountains of [al-Wazi'iyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Wazi'iyah_District) and the Mountains of [Shar'ab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shara'b_As_Salam_District) and [Maqbanah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maqbanah_District).[^cite_note-NIC-3][^cite_note-4]

### Streams

There are many [streams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stream) (*wadi* [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وادي), perennial and [seasonal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermittent_river):[^cite_note-NIC-3]

#### Perennial streams

- Wadi Rasian, in [Mawza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mawza_District), [Shara'b Ar Rawnah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shara'b_Ar_Rawnah_District), [al-Makha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Mukha_District) and [Maqbanah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maqbanah_District).

- Wadi Warzan, in [Khadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimnat_Khadir_District) and [al-Mawasit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Mawasit_District).

- Wadi al-Dhabab, in al-Mawasit and [Hayfan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayfan_District).

- Wadi Ar-Ram, in al-Mafalis region, Hayfan.

- Wadi al-Dhabab, in [Sabir al-Mawadim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabir_Al_Mawadim_District).

- Wadi al-Ashroh or Wadi al-Barakani, in al-Mawasit.

- Wadi al-Janah, in al-Wazi'iyah.

- Wadi al-Lahya, in Mawza.

## Economy

In the Tihamah region, [irrigated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrigation) crops include [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton), [sorghum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorghum) and [sesame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesame). [Mangoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango), [papayas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papaya) and [bananas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana) are the major crops of the western escarpment, and further up [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee) and [khat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khat) are grown. Although [wine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wine) is prohibited in Islam, [grapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grape) are grown to produce [raisins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raisin).

## Wildlife

The most common wild animals are [hyenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyena), [rock hyrax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_hyrax), [rabbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbit), [hedgehog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedgehog), and [monkeys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monkey). The most common wild birds are [vultures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulture), [falcons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falcon), [partridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partridge), [owls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owl), [pigeons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pigeons), [hoopoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoopoe), [bulbuls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulbul).[^cite_note-NIC-3]

## Regions



Regions of Taiz



al-Hujariah region

- [Shamir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamir,_Yemen?action=edit\&redlink=1): ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شمير) also called "Shamir Maqbanah". It is a mountainous region part of Maqbanah district.

- [Al-Hugariyyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hugariyyah): ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الحجرية) It is the largest region in Taiz governorate. It is sometimes mistakenly called al-Hujariah district. The region is part of As-Silw, al-Ma'afer, Hayfan, Sama', al-Mawasit, Jabal Habashy, Dimnat Khadir, and al-Wazi'iyah districts.
