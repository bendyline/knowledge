**Eliodoro Camacho** is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Bolivia) in the [La Paz Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Paz_Department,_Bolivia) in [Bolivia](3462.md) situated at [Lake Titicaca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Titicaca). Its seat is [Puerto Acosta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Acosta).

Camacho Province is situated in the western region of the La Paz Department bordered to the north by the [Muñecas Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu%C3%B1ecas_Province), to the east by the [Larecaja Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larecaja_Province), to the south by the [Omasuyos Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omasuyos_Province), to the west by [Lake Titicaca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Titicaca) and [Moho Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moho_Province) located in the [Puno Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puno_Region) of [Peru](170691.md).

Camacho Province was created during the presidency of General [Ismael Montes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismael_Montes) on November 5, 1908,[^cite_note-2] named after the Bolivian politician and officer [Eliodoro Camacho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliodoro_Camacho). Originally it was composed of the cantons Huaycho, Escoma, Carabuco, Italaque, Mocomoco and Ambaná. Huaycho became the capital of the province under the name "Puerto Acosta" (meaning: "Port Acosta") in honor of the writer Nicolás Acosta (1844 - 1893).[^cite_note-3]
