The **Canadair CL-415** (**Super Scooper**,[^cite_note-Frawley_2003-2] later **Bombardier 415**) and the **De Havilland Canadair DHC-515** are a series of [amphibious aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_aircraft) built originally by [Canadair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadair) and subsequently by [Bombardier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardier_Aviation) and [De Havilland Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Havilland_Canada). The CL-415 is based on the [Canadair CL-215](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadair_CL-215) and is designed specifically for [aerial firefighting](1143974.md), although it can perform various other roles, such as [search and rescue](172599.md) and utility transport.

Development of the CL-415 began in the early 1990s, shortly after the success of the CL-215T retrofit programme had proven a viable demand for a [turboprop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turboprop)-powered model of the original CL-215. Entering production in 2003, in addition to its new engines, the aircraft featured numerous modernisation efforts and advances over the CL-215, particularly in terms of its cockpit and [aerodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerodynamics), to yield improved performance. By the time the programme's production phase had begun, it was owned by Bombardier, who continued production up until 2015. In October 2016, the CL-415 programme was acquired by Viking Air, aiming to produce an updated CL-515,[^cite_note-Viking515-3] since renamed the **De Havilland Canadair 515**, and to be produced in both Victoria and Calgary by De Havilland Canada.[^cite_note-skiesmag31mar2022-4]

## Development

### Origins

Introduced during 1966, the CL-215 was the first aircraft specifically designed to be a [water bomber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_bomber).[^cite_note-fi_871-5] A total of 125 aircraft were constructed prior to the final CL-215 being delivered during May 1990.[^cite_note-flightest_2009-6]

During 1987, in response to prevailing market trends towards more efficient, powerful and reliable [turboprop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turboprop) powerplants, Canadair undertook the task of retrofitting 17 *CL-215* airframes with the [Pratt & Whitney Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratt_&_Whitney_Canada) [PW123AF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratt_&_Whitney_Canada_PW100) engines. This engine provided a 15 percent power increase over the original [piston engines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reciprocating_engine), as well as enhanced reliability and safety. The retrofitted aircraft were designated *CL-215T*.[^cite_note-goold_23-7] Speaking during the new model's development, company officials recognised that market demand for the CL-215T was marginal, and thus not enough to justify developing an all-new aircraft.[^cite_note-goold_23-7] Despite this, it featured numerous enhancements, including the addition of powered flight controls, [air conditioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_conditioning) in the [cockpit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cockpit), as well as various upgraded electrical and avionics systems. The most notable external features of the CL-215T retrofit were the [aerodynamic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerodynamics) additions to the [wings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wing) and [empennage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empennage).[^cite_note-flightest_2009-6]

Having conducted the relatively successful CL-215T programme, the company decided to develop the *CL-415*, which would be a new production series. On 6 December 1993, the CL-415 conducted its maiden flight, while the first deliveries commenced during November 1994.[^cite_note-8] One year later, a 180-day sales tour traversing 21 countries commenced using a CL-415 owned by the [Quebec Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec_Government).[^cite_note-9] That same year, Bombardier stated that it was in the planning phase of a six-point improvement plan for the CL-415, which was principally intended to diversify its capabilities.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

Orders for the type were promptly received from several countries, which included several [lease](635681.md) and purchase arrangements; by July 1996, 37 examples were reportedly in service with operators in Canada, France, Italy, and Spain.[^cite_note-12] Starting in 1998, the CL-415 was being assembled at Bombardier Aerospace's facility near [North Bay/Jack Garland Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Bay/Jack_Garland_Airport) in [North Bay, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Bay,_Ontario), and tested on [Lake Nipissing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Nipissing).[^cite_note-13] During the 2010s, according to aerospace periodical [Flight International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_International), there was a downturn in sales of the type. A total of ninety-five CL-415s had been completed when Bombardier closed down the production line in October 2015, although the company continued to actively market the type as well as to provide support for the existing fleet beyond this date.[^cite_note-flight_closure2015-14]

### Viking era

For several decades, Bombardier had experienced a period of significant expansion until encountering financial hardship during the 2010s, largely brought on by the very high costs involved in developing the [CSeries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSeries) narrow-body [airliner](51215.md).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-AvWeek27Oct2017-16] The much smaller [Viking Aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viking_Air) started off as a component manufacturing specialist, which came to include the [licensed production](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licensed_production) of parts of several of Bombardier's discontinued aircraft range, helping operators to keep them in service.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-skiesmag2018-08-22-18] During 2008, Bombardier and Viking Air reached an arrangement under which the former sold the design documents and all intellectual property rights of all out-of-production de Havilland aircraft from the DHC-1 Chipmunk through the DASH-7 50 passenger STOL regional airliner to Viking.[^cite_note-wingsmag-19][^cite_note-20] Its unit cost in 2014 was 36.9 million US dollars.[^cite_note-21]

On 20 June 2016, it was announced that Viking Air was in the process of purchasing the CL-415 [type certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_certificate) from [Bombardier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardier_Aviation), along with the older CL-215 and CL-215T models.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] The acquisition was finalised on 3 October 2016.[^cite_note-25] Shortly following the acquisition, Viking began work on the design of a modernized *CL-515* version.[^cite_note-skiesmag2018-08-22-18][^cite_note-flightglobal2018-10-31-26]

In May 2018 Bridger Aerospace of Bozeman, Montana became the launch customer for the CL-415EAF "Enhanced Aerial Firefighter" with a \$204 million contract for up to six aircraft.[^cite_note-27]

During December 2018, a full-flight CL-415 simulator, capable of simulating water scoop and bombing operations, received [European Aviation Safety Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Aviation_Safety_Agency) (EASA) certification. Prior to this a full flight simulator was built and establish in Sault Ste Marie Ontario in May of 2013. Certified to FTD Level 6 by Transport Canada, the Mechtronix CL-415 FFT X has been acquired to allow provincial CL-415 pilots and engineers to train in Ontario on a simulator tailored to their needs rather than travelling outside the province, saving considerable time and costs for the Ontario Government.[^cite_note-28]

On 21 June 2019, the Indonesian Ministry of Defense announced it was purchasing six CL-515s and one CL-415EAF for delivery in 2024.[^cite_note-Airmed_&_Rescue-29][^cite_note-wingsmagazine.com-30]

On 31 March 2022, [Viking Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viking_Air) through De Havilland Canada renamed the CL-515 as the DHC-515, planning for production and final assembly in [Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calgary), Alberta, Canada, where the [CL-215](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadair_CL-215) and CL-415 are supported, with 22 letters of intent from European customers.[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32]

On 25 March 2024, Greek prime minister [Kyriakos Mitsotakis](2780816.md) and Canadian prime minister [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) announced a deal to purchase \$530-million worth of DHC-515s from De Havilland to help deal with the country's severe wildfire seasons.[^cite_note-33]

In March 2026, De Havilland announced that assembly of the first DHC-515 had started in Calgary, with a view to first delivery in 2028.[^cite_note-34]

## Design

Further information: [Canadair CL-215 § Design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadair_CL-215#Design)

The CL-415 has an updated cockpit, aerodynamics enhancements, and changes to the water-release system creating a modern [firefighting](855608.md) amphibious [flying boat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flying_boat) for use in detecting and suppressing [forest fires](56106.md). Compared to the CL-215, the CL-415 has increased operating weight and speed, yielding improved productivity and performance. Due to the increased power of its pair of [Pratt & Whitney Canada PW123AF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratt_&_Whitney_Canada_PW100) [turboprop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turboprop) engines, each capable of generating up to 1,775 kW of power, these are located closer to the fuselage in comparison to the CL-215's arrangement.[^cite_note-flightest_2009-6] While this repositioning would typically reduce lateral stability on its own, this is rectified via the addition of an inverted fixed [leading edge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_edge) [slat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slat_\(aircraft\)) forward of the righthand horizontal stabiliser. Furthermore, [winglets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winglet) have been adopted on this model for the purpose of improving directional stability.[^cite_note-flightest_2009-6]

The CL-415 can scoop up to 6,140 L (1,350 imp gal; 1,620 US gal) of water from a nearby water source, mix it with a chemical [foam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firefighting_foam) if desired, and drop it on a fire without having to return to base to refill its tanks.[^cite_note-flightest_2009-6] The CL-415 was specifically developed to provide the capability to deliver large quantities of [suppressant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_retardant) in quick response to fires. This is stored within large tanks which are located mostly beneath the cabin floor within the hull, although a header tank above this level is present on either side of the fuselage.[^cite_note-flightest_2009-6] The airframe is built for reliability and longevity, making extensive use of [corrosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corrosion)-resistant materials, predominantly treated aluminium, that facilitates its use in salt water. According to Flight International, the CL-415 has good handling on the water, being relatively easy to operate in comparison with several other amphibious aircraft.[^cite_note-flightest_2009-6] The CL-515 can hold up to 7,000 litres (1,850 US gallons), and has a refill time of 14 seconds.[^cite_note-Viking515-3]

The aircraft requires 1,340 m (4,400 ft) of flyable length to descend from 15 m (49 ft) altitude, scoop 6,137 L (1,350 imp gal; 1,621 US gal) of water during a twelve-second 410-metre-long (1,350 ft) run on the water at 70 [knots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (130 km/h; 81 mph), then climb back to 15 m (49 ft) altitude. The aircraft can also pick up partial loads in smaller areas, and can turn while scooping if necessary.[^cite_note-35] Management of the water bombing system is centralised via a water status panel on the flight instrumentation, giving direct control to the pilots; various dispersal patterns and sequences can be selected. A manually operated emergency dump lever is also present, bypassing this system.[^cite_note-flightest_2009-6] Bombardier have claimed that the type performs 6.9 water drops for every flight hour of the type.[^cite_note-flightest_2009-6] The *CL-415GR* variant features higher operating weights, while the CL-415 multi-role model is available for purposes in a paramilitary [search and rescue](172599.md) role and utility transport.

Two [Hellenic Air Force](363254.md) CL-415 refilling off the coast of [Atlit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlit_\(modern_town\)) to fight the [2010 Mount Carmel forest fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Mount_Carmel_forest_fire)

Derived from its predecessor's nickname, the aircraft is known as the "Superscooper"[^cite_note-36] in light of its greatly enhanced performance as a water bomber and fire suppressor. In recognition of its abilities, the aircraft was awarded in 2006 the *Batefuegos de oro* (gold fire extinguisher) by the *Asociacion para la Promocion de Actividades Socioculturales* (APAS) in Spain; the award citation in part read, "This is the most efficient tool for the aerial combat of forest fires, key to the organization of firefighting in a large number of countries. The continuous improvements to meet the needs of forest firefighting have made these aircraft the aerial means most in demand over more than 30 years."[^cite_note-37]

By 1999, a total of 51 orders had been secured for the type; operators have reportedly predominantly used the CL-415 for firefighting and maritime search and rescue purposes.[^cite_note-flight415gr_1999-38] Of the 95 built, seven had reportedly been removed from service as a result of several accidents by December 2007.[^cite_note-39]
