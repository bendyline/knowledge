**Sophie, Hereditary Princess of Liechtenstein, Countess of Rietberg** ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A9e) **Sophie Elisabeth Marie Gabrielle Herzogin in Bayern**;[^cite_note-1] born 28 October 1967) is married to [Alois, Hereditary Prince and Regent of Liechtenstein](30873557.md). She was born a member of the [House of Wittelsbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Wittelsbach), with the courtesy title of [Duchess in Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_in_Bavaria), and second in line for the [Jacobite succession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobite_succession).[^cite_note-times-2]

## Early life and education

Duchess Sophie was born in Bavaria, [Munich](19058.md), the eldest of the five daughters of [Prince Max, Duke in Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Max,_Duke_in_Bavaria), and Princess Elisabeth, Duchess in Bavaria ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names) Countess [Douglas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clan_Douglas#The_Swedish-German_branch)),[^cite_note-burke's_peerage-3] as well as a patrilineal great-great-granddaughter of the last King of Bavaria, [Ludwig III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_III_of_Bavaria).[^cite_note-liechweb-4] She was born in [Munich](19058.md) on 28 October 1967 and baptised as Sophie Elizabeth Marie Gabrielle[^cite_note-liechweb-4] in the chapel of her family's [Kreuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kreuth) home on 18 November. Her godparents were her maternal aunt the [Duchess of Marlborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosita_Spencer-Churchill,_Duchess_of_Marlborough) and [Princess Anna Gabriele of Wrede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archduke_Rudolf_of_Austria_\(1919%E2%80%932010\)#Second_marriage).

Sophie spent her childhood together with her parents and sisters in [Wildbad Kreuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildbad_Kreuth). From 1978 to 1980, Sophie attended the Girls' Home Primary School of the English Lady in [Heiligenstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heiligenstadt_\(Upper_Franconia\)). She then moved to the Girls' Secondary Boarding School Hohenburg in [Lenggries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenggries). Sophie then studied history and English language and literature at the [Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_Eichst%C3%A4tt-Ingolstadt).[^cite_note-liechweb-4] She also attended [Inchbald School of Design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inchbald_School_of_Design) in London.[^cite_note-lexicon-5]

## Marriage and children

Duchess Sophie in Bavaria met [Alois, Hereditary Prince of Liechtenstein](30873557.md) at her cousin's birthday party in Munich.[^cite_note-jan19-6] The couple married on 3 July 1993 at [Cathedral of St. Florin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaduz_Cathedral) in [Vaduz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaduz), Liechtenstein.[^cite_note-burke's_peerage-3] They lived in London from September 1993 until May 1996, and have since then resided in Vaduz, Liechtenstein.[^cite_note-lexicon-5] They have four children:

- [Prince Joseph Wenzel Maximilian Maria of Liechtenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Joseph_Wenzel_of_Liechtenstein), Count of Rietberg (born 24 May 1995 at [Portland Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_Hospital) in [London](17867.md), England, United Kingdom). Educated at [Malvern College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malvern_College) in [Worcestershire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcestershire), England.

## Illness

At the beginning of 2003, it was made public that Sophie had been diagnosed with a [benign brain tumor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meningioma), from which she has recovered.

## Activities

Hereditary Princess Sophie serves as a patron for many organizations and events, often relating to children, education and the arts. She regularly visited social institutions such as hospitals, nursing homes and auctions.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] The Hereditary Princess often accompanies her husband on foreign visits, as well as many events within Liechtenstein itself.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

In 2006, the Hereditary Princess founded the *Sophie von Liechtenstein Stiftung für Frau und Kind* (Sophie of Liechtenstein Foundation for Woman and Child). The foundation's purpose is to give women who unintentionally became pregnant a more positive life perspective for themselves and their children.[^cite_note-18] The foundation has three offices – in Liechtenstein, [Vorarlberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vorarlberg,_Austria) and [St. Gallen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_St._Gallen)[^cite_note-svl-19] – and is funded by the Liechtenstein princely family and by private donations.[^cite_note-svl-19] The Hereditary Princess serves as president and trustee.[^cite_note-svl-19] Sophie also founded a pregnancy counseling service named schwanger.li in [Schaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schaan), [Buchs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buchs,_St._Gallen), and [Feldkirch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feldkirch,_Vorarlberg).[^cite_note-lexicon-5][^cite_note-20] In April 2022, the foundation and Liechtenstein Red Cross launch the "Liechtenstein Family Network" project to support parents with young children from the age of 0 to 5 years in order to promote healthy development of children.[^cite_note-21]

Hereditary Princess Sophie has served as a president of the [Liechtenstein Red Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liechtenstein_Red_Cross) since 2015.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] In March 2022, as president of the Red Cross, Hereditary Princess Sophie was interviewed by Radio Liechtenstein, discussing Liechtenstein's contribution to the [International Red Cross](5023832.md)'s Ukraine relief funds during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md).[^cite_note-24] She is also a patron of the Liechtenstein Animal Welfare Association.[^cite_note-25] In addition, she supports Caritas Liechtenstein, an organisation that provides financial support to those facing severe financial troubles.[^cite_note-26] She also visited Gamander Children's Home, a shelter home for orphaned and abandoned children.[^cite_note-27] In September 2022, Sophie became the patron of the *Heilpädagogische Zentrum in Liechtenstein* (Special Education Center in Liechtenstein).[^cite_note-28]
