**Yanouh** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): يانوح, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Yānūḥ*) is a municipality in [Tyre District](11166788.md), [Governorate of South Lebanon](1283570.md).

## Etymology

According to [E. H. Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Henry_Palmer) in 1881, the name *Yânûh* is one of two possible "Yanoah of the Bible".[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## History

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described *Yanuh*: "A village built of stone, containing 150 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh), on a hill-top, surrounded by figs, olives, and pomegranates, and arable land. A spring and [cisterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cistern) are found here."[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Demographics

In 2014, [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.28% of registered voters in Yanouh. 98.66% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-6]
