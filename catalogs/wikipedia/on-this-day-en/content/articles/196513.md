The **Grammy Award for Album of the Year** is an award presented by the [National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences](8850489.md) of the United States to "honor artistic achievement, technical proficiency and overall excellence in the recording industry, without regard to [album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Album) sales, chart position, or critical reception."[^cite_note-Overview-1] Album of the Year is the most prestigious category at the [Grammy Awards](44635.md) and is one of the general field categories that have been presented annually since the [1st Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Annual_Grammy_Awards) in 1959 alongside [Best New Artist](198014.md), [Record of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Record_of_the_Year), and [Song of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year).

## Credit rules

Over the years, the rules on who was presented with an award have changed:

- 1959–1965: Artist only.

- 1966–1998: Artist and producer.

- 1999–2002: Artist, producer, and recording engineer or mixer.

- 2003–2017: Artist, featured artist, producer, mastering engineer, and recording engineer or mixer.

- 2018–2020: Artist, featured artist, producer, songwriter (of new material), mastering engineer, and recording engineer or mixer (only those who were credited on at least 33% playing time of the album)

- 2021–2023: Artist, featured artist, producer, songwriter (of new material), mastering engineer, and recording engineer or mixer (regardless of credited playing time)

- 2024–future: Artist, featured artist, producer, songwriter (of new material), mastering engineer, and recording engineer or mixer (only those who were credited on at least 20% playing time of the album)

The category expanded to include eight nominees in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/61st_Annual_Grammy_Awards)[^cite_note-2] and ten in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/64th_Annual_Grammy_Awards).[^cite_note-3] Beginning with the [2024 ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_Annual_Grammy_Awards), the number of nominees has been reduced back to eight.[^cite_note-auto-4]

Album of the Year is awarded for a whole album, and the award is presented to the artist, featured artist, producer, songwriter, mastering engineer, and recording engineer or mixer with significant contributions to that album. The similarly titled Record of the Year is awarded for a single or for one track from an album. This award goes to the artist, producer, mastering engineer, and recording engineer or mixer for that song.[^cite_note-5]

## Achievements



&#x20;Four-time winner [Taylor Swift](5422144.md). She is the first woman to win the award two and three times and the only main credited artist to win four times. She won in [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/52nd_Annual_Grammy_Awards), [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/58th_Annual_Grammy_Awards), [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/63rd_Annual_Grammy_Awards) and [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_Annual_Grammy_Awards). She is the most nominated female act with 7 nominations.



&#x20;[Frank Sinatra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Sinatra) was the first two-time winner and three-time winner. He won in [1960](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Annual_Grammy_Awards), [1966](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) and [1967](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_Annual_Grammy_Awards). He is the most nominated artist in this category with 8 nominations.



&#x20;Three-time winner [Stevie Wonder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevie_Wonder) won in [1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_Annual_Grammy_Awards), [1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/17th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) and [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19th_Annual_Grammy_Awards).



&#x20;Three-time winner [Paul Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Simon) won twice as the main credited artist, in [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/18th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) and [1987](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/29th_Annual_Grammy_Awards).



&#x20;[U2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U2) is the only group act to win twice, in [1988](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) and [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/48th_Annual_Grammy_Awards).



&#x20;Two-time winner [Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele) won in [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/54th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) and [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/59th_Annual_Grammy_Awards).

[Serban Ghenea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serban_Ghenea) (as engineer/mixer) is the most frequent winner in this category with five awards. [Taylor Swift](5422144.md) (as performer); [John Hanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hanes_\(audio_engineer\)) (as engineer/mixer); and [Tom Coyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Coyne_\(music_engineer\)) and [Randy Merrill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randy_Merrill) (as mastering engineers) have won the award four times.[^cite_note-6] They are followed by [Frank Sinatra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Sinatra), [Stevie Wonder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevie_Wonder), and [Paul Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Simon) (as performers); [Jack Antonoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Antonoff), [David Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Foster), [Daniel Lanois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Lanois), [Phil Ramone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Ramone), and [Ryan Tedder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Tedder) (as album producers); [Tom Elmhirst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Elmhirst) and [Mike Piersante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Piersante) and Laura Sisk (as engineers/mixers); and [Bob Ludwig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Ludwig) (as mastering engineer) with three victories each. Coyne, Ghenea, Hanes, and Ludwig are the only people to win the award in three consecutive years. [Paul McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_McCartney) leads all performers with nine nominations: five as a member of [The Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles), three for solo albums, and one as a member of [Wings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wings_\(band\)). McCartney's former bandmate [George Harrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Harrison) has a total of eight nominations: five as a member of The Beatles, one for a solo album, one for a live album with friends, and one as a member of the [Traveling Wilburys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traveling_Wilburys). Sinatra leads solo performers with eight nominations: seven for solo albums and one for a duet album. [Taylor Swift](5422144.md) has the most nominations amongst female artists with seven, followed by [Barbra Streisand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbra_Streisand) with six.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

The first woman to win the award was [Judy Garland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judy_Garland) in 1962, for *[Judy at Carnegie Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judy_at_Carnegie_Hall)*. Taylor Swift is the first solo female artist to win the award two, three, and four times. [Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele) has won twice as main credited artist, and [Lauryn Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauryn_Hill), [Norah Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norah_Jones), and [Alison Krauss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alison_Krauss) also won two times each, first as lead artists for their respective albums, *[The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Miseducation_of_Lauryn_Hill)*, *[Come Away with Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_Away_with_Me)*, and *[Raising Sand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raising_Sand)* (Krauss' collaboration album with [Robert Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Plant)); Hill won her second Album of the Year as a producer of her collaboration on [Santana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santana_\(band\))'s *[Supernatural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supernatural_\(Santana_album\))* (featured artists on non-soundtrack albums did not share in the award before 2008), while Jones won again as a featured artist on [Herbie Hancock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbie_Hancock)'s *[River: The Joni Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River:_The_Joni_Letters)*, and Krauss won again having been a featured artist on the *[O Brother, Where Art Thou? – Soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O_Brother,_Where_Art_Thou%3F_\(soundtrack\))*.

[Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish) is the youngest main credit artist to win in the category, winning for her debut album *When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?* in 2020 at the age of 18. [The Peasall Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Peasall_Sisters), Sarah, Hannah, and Leah, are the category's youngest credited winners, winning for their contributions to the *O Brother, Where Art Thou? – Soundtrack* at ages 13, 9, and 7 respectively. Leah Peasall is the youngest winner of any Grammy in any category.[^cite_note-9] The youngest person to make an appearance on an Album of the Year is Stevie Wonder's daughter Aisha Morris, who appeared as an infant for "[Isn't She Lovely?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isn't_She_Lovely%3F)" on the album *[Songs in the Key of Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs_in_the_Key_of_Life)*.[^cite_note-10]

[Christopher Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Cross) and Billie Eilish are the only artists to receive Grammys for Album of the Year as well as [Record of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Record_of_the_Year), [Song of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year), and [Best New Artist](198014.md), each winning all four "general field" categories in a single ceremony year. Adele was the first artist to win awards for Album of the Year, Record of the Year, Song of the Year, and Best New Artist on separate occasions. Five artists have won both Album of the Year and Best New Artist in the same year: [Bob Newhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Newhart) (*[The Button-Down Mind of Bob Newhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Button-Down_Mind_of_Bob_Newhart)* in 1961), Lauryn Hill (*The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill* in 1999), and Norah Jones (*Come Away with Me* in 2003) along with Christopher Cross (*[Christopher Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Cross_\(album\))* in 1981) and Billie Eilish (*When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?* in 2020) mentioned above.

Frank Sinatra's *[Come Dance with Me!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_Dance_with_Me!_\(album\))* was the first album by a [traditional pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_pop) artist to win, [Stan Getz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stan_Getz)'s & [João Gilberto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Gilberto)'s *[Getz/Gilberto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Getz/Gilberto)* was the first by [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz) artists, The Beatles' *[Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sgt._Pepper's_Lonely_Hearts_Club_Band)* was the first by [rock and roll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_and_roll) artists, [Glen Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Campbell)'s *[By the Time I Get to Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/By_the_Time_I_Get_to_Phoenix_\(Glen_Campbell_album\))* was the first by a [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music) artist, Lauryn Hill's *The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill* was the first by a [hip hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip_hop) artist, Santana's *[Supernatural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supernatural_\(Santana_album\))* was the first by a Hispanic artist, [Arcade Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcade_Fire)'s *[The Suburbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Suburbs)* was the first by [indie rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_rock) artists, and [Daft Punk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daft_Punk)'s *[Random Access Memories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_Access_Memories)* was the first by [electronic music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_music) artists. *[Un Verano Sin Ti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Un_Verano_Sin_Ti)* and *[Debí Tirar Más Fotos](78731423.md)* by [Bad Bunny](55943877.md) are the only Spanish-language albums to be nominated, with the latter being the first Spanish-language album to win.

Only Frank Sinatra and Stevie Wonder have won in two consecutive years as main artists: Sinatra won in 1966 (*[September of My Years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/September_of_My_Years)*) and 1967 (*[A Man and His Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Man_and_His_Music)*) and Wonder won in 1974 (*[Innervisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innervisions)*) and 1975 (*[Fulfillingness' First Finale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulfillingness'_First_Finale)*). Lauryn Hill and [Bruno Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Mars) have also won in consecutive years, with one win credited as producer rather than artist. Hill won as a producer in 2000 after winning in 1999 as artist and producer. [Bruno Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Mars) won as a producer in 2017 before winning as both artist and producer in 2018.

The Beatles were the first and only artists to date to receive Album of the Year nominations in five consecutive years (1966–1970). Frank Sinatra was the first to receive four nominations in consecutive years, 1959–1961 (two nominations at the first ceremony in 1959), receiving 3 more consecutive nominations for 1966–1968. Barbra Streisand (1964–1967)[^cite_note-11] and [Kendrick Lamar](29909823.md) (2016–2019) also received nominations in four consecutive years while [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga) (2010–2012) was nominated in three consecutive years.

[Kendrick Lamar](29909823.md) is the only performer with Album of the Year nominations for five consecutive studio albums, for *[Good Kid, M.A.A.D City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Kid,_M.A.A.D_City)*, *[To Pimp a Butterfly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Pimp_a_Butterfly)*, *[Damn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damn_\(album\))*, *[Mr. Morale & the Big Steppers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Morale_&_the_Big_Steppers)* and *[GNX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNX_\(album\))*. [Beyoncé](83688.md), [Billy Joel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Joel) and [Taylor Swift](5422144.md) follow with four nominations; while [The Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles), [Stevie Wonder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevie_Wonder), [Steely Dan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steely_Dan), [Bonnie Raitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonnie_Raitt), [Dixie Chicks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chicks), [Kanye West](523032.md), [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga), [Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele) and [Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish) each received nominations for three consecutive studio albums.

[Stevie Wonder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevie_Wonder) and [Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele) are the only artists to win the award for consecutive studio albums in this category, winning for *[Innervisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innervisions)* and *[Fulfillingness' First Finale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulfillingness'_First_Finale)* and *[Songs in the Key of Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs_in_the_Key_of_Life)*; and *[21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21_\(Adele_album\))* and *[25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/25_\(Adele_album\))*, respectively.

[Quincy Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Jones), Lauryn Hill and Bruno Mars are the only performers to win the award both as main-credit artists and as record producers, winning as lead artists for their albums *[Back on the Block](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_on_the_Block)*, *The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill* and *[24K Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24K_Magic_\(album\))*, and as producers for *[Thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thriller_\(album\))* by [Michael Jackson](14995351.md), *Supernatural* by Santana, and *25* by Adele, respectively. Mars' work on the album 25 is credited under his production team name [The Smeezingtons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Smeezingtons).

To date, there have been four "live" albums to win the award: *Judy at Carnegie Hall*, *[The Concert for Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Concert_for_Bangladesh_\(album\))*, and two *[MTV Unplugged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Unplugged)* albums ([Eric Clapton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unplugged_\(Eric_Clapton_album\))'s and [Tony Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Unplugged_\(Tony_Bennett_album\))'s), which were performed in front of a small live audience. One television soundtrack recording,*[The Music from Peter Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Music_from_Peter_Gunn)*, has won. Two comedy albums have also won this category: *The Button-Down Mind of Bob Newhart* and *[The First Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_First_Family_\(album\))*.

## Process

From 1995 to 2021, members of the [National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Recording_Arts_and_Sciences) nominated their choices for album of the year. A list of the top twenty records was given to the Nominations Review Committee, a specially selected group of anonymous members, who then selected the top five records to gain a nomination in the category in a special ballot.[^cite_note-20] The rest of the members then voted on a winner from the five nominees.[^cite_note-21] In 2018, it was announced the number of nominated albums would be increased to eight.[^cite_note-22] In 2021, it was announced that the Nomination Review Committees would be disbanded, and the final nominees for album of the year would be decided by votes from members.[^cite_note-23] Starting in 2022, the number of nominees in the category increased to 10.[^cite_note-24] However, the decision to expand the number of nominees in this category was made 24 hours before the nominees were announced after an early version of the nominations list had already been circulated. This allowed [Taylor Swift](5422144.md)'s *[Evermore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evermore)* and [Kanye West](523032.md)'s *[Donda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donda)* to be nominated as they were the albums that received the highest number of votes besides the other eight nominees.[^cite_note-25] As of the [2024 ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_Annual_Grammy_Awards), the number of nominees has been reduced back to eight.[^cite_note-auto-4]
