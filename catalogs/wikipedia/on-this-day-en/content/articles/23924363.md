[Odisha](250724.md), one of the 28 [states of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_India), has two basic kinds of [forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest): in the northeast region of the state the forest is classified as the tropical-moist-deciduous type, blanketing hills, plateaus and other high-altitude isolated areas; in the southwest the tropical-dry-deciduous variety dominate.

## Features

Odisha's forests are vast. Out of the total geographical area of 155,707 km<sup>2</sup>, the State records 52,472 km<sup>2</sup> (\~33.50%) as some version of forest. The actual forest cover may be less, according to the [Forest Survey of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_Survey_of_India), [rosewood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosewood), [sal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sal_tree), [piasal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pterocarpus_marsupium), [teak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teak) and [haldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turmeric). The forests' naturally vigorous growth accounts for a tremendous wealth of [biodiversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biodiversity), filling many catalogues of the wild [plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant) and [animal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal) species dwelling within.

The state has declared large parcels of land as [protected areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protected_land) with the purpose being to allow animals and plants who are sensitive to cohabitation with humans places of relative freedom from interference and habitat loss. These protected areas constitute 10.37% of the total forest area and 4.1% of the total geographical area of the state.&#x20;

### Forest fauna

The [IUCN Red List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IUCN_Red_List) has recorded a total of 473 species of [bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird), 86 species of [mammal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mammal), 19 species of [amphibian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibian) and 110 species of [reptile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reptile) including three [crocodilian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crocodile) species. Out of these around 54 species are considered endangered. Home to a variety of wild animals, the state has declared considerable tracts of land as areas protected for these animals only. These protected areas constitute 10.37% of the total forest area and 4.1% of the total geographical area of the state. Not only this, the state also has the distinction of possessing three mass nesting beaches of endangered olive ridley sea turtles which makes it the largest nesting ground of the species.

&#x20;  \
&#x20;The state has three mass nesting beaches of endangered [olive ridley sea turtles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_ridley_sea_turtle) which when combined makes it the world's largest nesting ground for this species.[^cite_note-2]

Indenting Orissa's ocean coast is [Chilika lagoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilika), a semi-saline [wetland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wetland) used by many species of [migratory birds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migratory_bird), and also which the endangered [Irrawaddy dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrawaddy_dolphin) uses as part of its range. In spite of considerable human interactivity here, the rare dolphin survives. The Orissa government is conducting various programmes to protect the species.[^cite_note-3]
