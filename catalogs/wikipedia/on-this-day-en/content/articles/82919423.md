**Kornoi** or **Karnoi** is a town in [North Darfur](767666.md), [Sudan](27421.md).

## History

Kornoi has been affected by the [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md). In August 2025, a senior [Zaghawa](1467480.md) traditional leader was abducted in the town.[^cite_note-1] In October 2025, hundreds of refugees fled the town for [Tina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_\(Sudan\)) at the [Chad–Sudan border](62069174.md).[^cite_note-2] On 3 November 2025, it was reported that a [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) (RSF) drone strike on a hospital in Kornoi, left multiple casualties and forced the withdrawal of [Médecins Sans Frontières](20498.md) from the parts of the state.[^cite_note-3] On 25 December 2025, The RSF claimed to have taken Kornoi, which was denied by the JDF.[^cite_note-4] On 13 May 2026, a drone struck a local market and civilian vehicles.[^cite_note-5]
