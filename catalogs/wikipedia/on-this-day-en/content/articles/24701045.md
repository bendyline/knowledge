**Xuan'en County** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): [宣恩县](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%AE%A3%E6%81%A9%E5%8E%BF); [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Xuān'ēn Xiàn*) is a county of southwestern [Hubei](83319.md) province, People's Republic of China, bordering [Hunan](166409.md) province to the south. It is under the administration of the [Enshi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshi_Tujia_and_Miao_Autonomous_Prefecture).

A remarkable topographic feature found in Xuan'en County is a 290-meter deep [karst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karst) sinkhole, located near the village of Luoquanyan ([锣圈岩](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E9%94%A3%E5%9C%88%E5%B2%A9#hans)[村](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%9D%91#hans)). According to news reports, the sinkhole, which occupies around 100 *[mu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_\(unit\))* (6 hectares), has its own unique ecosystem.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
