# United Bowl (UFL)

The **United Bowl** is the annual league championship game for the [United Football League](75688380.md) (UFL). In [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_UFL_championship_game) and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_UFL_championship_game), the game—then known as the **UFL Championship Game**— was held at [The Dome at America's Center](452735.md) in [St. Louis, Missouri](27687.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-\:1-2] In 2026, the game was given its current title and was held at [Audi Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_Field) in [Washington, DC](108956.md).[^cite_note-3]

[Credit One Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Credit_One_Bank) is the game's [presenting sponsor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naming_rights) as of 2026.[^cite_note-4] The 2025 game was presented by [Underdog Fantasy Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underdog_Fantasy_Sports).[^cite_note-announcement-5]
