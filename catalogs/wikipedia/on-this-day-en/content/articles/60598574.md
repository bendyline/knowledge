# List of highest-grossing R-rated films

refer to caption

Two of the highest-grossing R-rated films, *[The Odyssey](78079604.md)* and *[Oppenheimer](66850554.md)*, were written and directed by [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan).

This is a list of the highest-grossing [R-rated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association_film_rating_system#R-rated) films. An R-rated film is a film that has been assessed as having material which may be unsuitable for children under the age of 17 by the [Motion Picture Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association); the MPA writes "Under 17 requires accompanying parent or adult guardian". The R-rating applies to the United States, with similar ratings given in [TV-MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_MA) for television and home video releases, as well as other countries such as [14A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_motion_picture_rating_system) and [18A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_motion_picture_rating_system) in [Canada](5042916.md), [15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Board_of_Film_Classification#Current_certificates) or [18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/18_\(British_Board_of_Film_Classification\)) in the United Kingdom, and [MA 15+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Classification_Board#Restricted) and [R 18+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Classification_Board#Restricted) in Australia. All lists in this article are unadjusted for [inflation](38286.md) except where specifically noted.

*[The Odyssey](78079604.md)* is the highest-grossing R-rated film of all time. *[Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demon_Slayer:_Kimetsu_no_Yaiba_\(TV_series\))* anime film series is the highest-grossing R-rated animated and non-English-language film franchise, with *[Infinity Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demon_Slayer:_Kimetsu_no_Yaiba_%E2%80%93_The_Movie:_Infinity_Castle)* as the highest-grossing R-rated animated and non-English-language film of all time.
