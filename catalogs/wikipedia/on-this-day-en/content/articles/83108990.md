On 4 May 2026, an explosion occurred at a fireworks factory operated by Huasheng Fireworks Manufacturing and Display Co. in [Guandu, Liuyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guandu,_Liuyang), a county-level city administered by [Changsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changsha) in [Hunan](166409.md), China. The blast occurred at about 16\:43 local time in a workshop at the plant. At least 37 people were killed, 51 others were injured and one person remains missing.[^cite_note-Reuters-1][^cite_note-AP-2][^cite_note-\:0-3]

The explosion caused severe damage at the plant and it complicated rescue work because of the presence of [black powder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunpowder) and other flammable materials. Authorities evacuated nearby areas, detained the person in charge of the company and ordered fireworks manufacturers in Liuyang to halt production for safety inspections.[^cite_note-Reuters-1][^cite_note-AP-2]

## Background

Further information: [Liuyang fireworks](34992030.md)

[Liuyang](322068.md) is one of China's best-known centres of fireworks production and is often described in Chinese and international reporting as a major fireworks manufacturing base.[^cite_note-Reuters-1] The explosion occurred at Huasheng Fireworks Manufacturing and Display Co., a fireworks enterprise located in Guandu, Liuyang.[^cite_note-Xinhua21-4]

## Explosion



The factory building, which was still ablaze on the night of the explosion.

The explosion occurred at about 16\:43 on 4 May 2026 in a workshop of Huasheng ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 华盛; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *huáshèng*) Fireworks Manufacturing and Display Co. in Liuyang.[^cite_note-Xinhua21-4] Footage published or cited by the media showed large plumes of smoke rising from the site and extensive damage to the factory buildings.[^cite_note-Reuters-1][^cite_note-AP-2] Nearby residents reported that windows and doors in their homes were shattered or deformed by the blast, and that debris had been thrown onto nearby roads.[^cite_note-SingTao-5]

The casualty figures changed as search and rescue work continued. Xinhua reported that, after an initial search, 21 people had been killed and 61 injured by 08\:00 on 5 May.[^cite_note-Xinhua21-4] Later reports put the death toll at 37, with the number of injured changing to 51 with one person remaining missing.[^cite_note-Reuters-1][^cite_note-AP-2][^cite_note-\:0-3] Of the 51 people hospitalised, 5 people are in serious condition.[^cite_note-\:1-6]

## Rescue and investigation

After the explosion, Hunan authorities activated an emergency response plan and established an on-site rescue command. Five rescue teams comprising 482 personnel were initially dispatched to the site, and medical resources from Hunan province and Changsha were mobilised to treat the injured.[^cite_note-Xinhua21-4] The [Ministry of Emergency Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Emergency_Management) sent specialists to guide rescue and emergency response work.[^cite_note-XiXinhua-7]

Because two black-powder warehouses at the plant posed a high risk of secondary explosions, rescuers established isolation belts and firebreaks and used spraying and humidification measures to reduce hazards.[^cite_note-Xinhua21-4] Robots, drones and search teams were used during the operation.[^cite_note-Reuters-1][^cite_note-AP-2]

Police detained the person in charge of Huasheng Fireworks Manufacturing and Display Co. after the explosion. The cause of the explosion remains under investigation.[^cite_note-Xinhua21-4][^cite_note-AP-2] According to state media cited by Reuters, [China's State Council](44498.md) set up an accident investigation team following the explosion.[^cite_note-Reuters-1] Eight people have been summoned for questioning on suspicion of causing the explosion.[^cite_note-8]

Huasheng Fireworks Manufacturing had repeatedly been cited for safety risks in the past, with a fine of 15,000 yuan (\$2,205) in January 2026 for two safety violations. The violations involved mixing reducing agents and oxidisers in its workshop, significantly elevating the risk of an explosion.[^cite_note-\:1-6]

## Response



The official press conference held following the explosion

[Xi Jinping](2017814.md), the General Secretary of the [Chinese Communist Party](7175.md), issued instructions after the explosion, calling for searches for missing people, treatment of the injured, proper handling of the aftermath, a prompt investigation into the cause and strict accountability. Premier [Li Qiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Qiang) also issued instructions calling for rescue work, prevention of secondary disasters and an investigation in accordance with law and regulations.[^cite_note-XiXinhua-7]

Vice Premier [Zhang Guoqing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Guoqing) travelled to Liuyang to guide the emergency rescue and response work.[^cite_note-Reuters-1] Local authorities ordered all fireworks and [firecracker](418188.md) manufacturers in Liuyang to suspend production for safety inspections.[^cite_note-Reuters-1][^cite_note-AP-2]
