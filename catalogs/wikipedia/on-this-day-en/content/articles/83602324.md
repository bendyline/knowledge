**Typhoon Bavi**,[^cite_note-4] known in the Philippines as **Super Typhoon Inday**, was an extremely powerful, large, and long-lived [tropical cyclone](8282374.md) that affected the Northern Mariana Islands, Guam, the Philippines, China, and parts of Taiwan in July 2026. The ninth named tropical storm, third [typhoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon) and second [violent typhoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violent_typhoon) of the [2026 Pacific typhoon season](80781992.md), Bavi was the second violent typhoon of the season to affect the Mariana Islands, after [Typhoon Sinlaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Sinlaku_\(2026\)) made landfall on [Tinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinian) in April.

The origins of Bavi started as a disturbance just east-southeast of [Kwajalein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwajalein) on June 25, over favorable conditions. Seven days later, the [Joint Typhoon Warning Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Typhoon_Warning_Center) (JTWC) designated the disturbance as *Tropical Depression 09W*. It later received the name *Bavi* by the [Japan Meteorological Agency](457886.md) (JMA) early on July 2 as it continued to organize over very warm sea surface temperatures and low vertical wind shear. Both agencies later upgraded Bavi into a typhoon as it quickly intensified. [Rapid intensification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_intensification) ensued, and Bavi became a super typhoon less than a day later as a [Category 5-equivalent](255313.md#Category_5) typhoon according to the JTWC. An [eyewall replacement cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyewall_replacement_cycle) ensued causing some slight weakening overnight on July 4, but the typhoon quickly re-strengthened the following day before it made landfall on [Rota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rota,_Northern_Mariana_Islands) in [Northern Mariana Islands](21402.md) at peak intensity; the JMA estimated [maximum sustained winds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_sustained_winds) of 205 km/h (125 mph) and a central pressure of 910 hPa (27 inHg), while the JTWC assessed peak 1-minute sustained winds of 285 km/h (180 mph) and central pressure of 901 hPa (26.6 inHg). Late on July 7, PAGASA gave the local name *Inday* even though it was about to enter the [Philippine Area of Responsibility](4272946.md) (PAR) until it did so at 03\:00 PHT of July 8 (19\:45 UTC of the previous day). The storm made a final landfall in [Zhejiang](96602.md) at tropical storm-equivalent intensity on July 11 before eventually curving towards the [Yellow Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_Sea), dissipating on July 16.

Bavi triggered heavy rains and landslides that killed 23 people and left 18 missing across the Philippines. Injuries were also recorded in Guam, the Ryukyu Islands in Japan, the Northern Mariana Islands and Taiwan.

On June 25, 2026, the [Joint Typhoon Warning Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Typhoon_Warning_Center) (JTWC) marked a low-pressure area that formed 901 [nautical miles](21289.md) (1,669 [km](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometre); 1,037 [mi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mile)) east-southeast of [Kwajalein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwajalein), with satellite imagery indicated that the disturbance was in a favorable development for tropical cyclogenesis.[^cite_note-5] Four days later, at 13\:00 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC), the Joint Typhoon Warning Center (JTWC) issued a Tropical Cyclone Formation Alert (TCFA) for the system, indicated that the disturbance had been gradually intensifying over favorable conditions.[^cite_note-6] Early on July 1, JTWC upgraded the disturbance into a tropical depression, assigning the designation as 09W.[^cite_note-7] At 00\:00 UTC on July 2, 09W was upgraded into a tropical storm, gaining the name *Bavi* by the JMA.[^cite_note-8]

Due to increasingly favorable conditions for tropical development, consisting of [sea surface temperatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_surface_temperature) (SSTs) of 29–30 [°C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celsius) (84–86 [°F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahrenheit)), low [wind shear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_shear) of 5–10 kn (5.8–11.5 mph; 9.3–18.5 km/h) and high [moisture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moisture) levels, Bavi quickly intensified, with the JTWC upgrading Bavi to a [typhoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon) at 18\:00 UTC that day, with the JMA following suit six hours later.[^cite_note-9] Bavi then began a period of [explosive intensification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_intensification), with the JTWC assessing the system's 1-minute sustained winds increasing from 70 kn (80 mph; 130 km/h) at 18\:00 UTC July 2 to 125 kn (145 mph; 230 km/h) at 12\:00 UTC July 3, in only 18 hours, making it a Category 4-equivalent typhoon, as a well-defined [eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_\(cyclone\)) quickly emerged within a sharply defined [central dense overcast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_dense_overcast) (CDO) surrounding it.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] The outflow also expanded massively and improved in all quadrants. At the same time, the JMA upgraded Bavi to a very strong typhoon.[^cite_note-12]

At 21\:00 UTC the same day, JTWC upgraded Bavi into a [super typhoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_typhoon), with estimated 1-minute winds of 145 knots (269 km/h; 167 mph), making it a Category 5-equivalent super typhoon.[^cite_note-auto1-13] Later on, JMA upgraded Bavi to a violent typhoon.[^cite_note-auto1-13] On July 4, the typhoon weakened slightly due to an [eyewall replacement cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyewall_replacement_cycle),[^cite_note-14] however by the following day it re-intensified back to a Category 5-equivalent super typhoon. At 08\:40 local time on July 6 (22\:40 UTC on July 5), Bavi made landfall on the northern coast of the island of [Rota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rota,_Northern_Mariana_Islands) at peak intensity, with JTWC assessing its 1-minute sustained winds of 155 knots (287 km/h; 178 mph) and a central pressure of 901 hPa (mbar; 26.61 inHg), becoming one of the strongest typhoons in history to affect the [Northern Mariana Islands](21402.md).[^cite_note-Landfall-1][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] On July 7, another eyewall replacement cycle occurred, causing the storm to weaken below super typhoon strength before reintensifying back into a super typhoon.[^cite_note-20] Even though it was still outside the [Philippine Area of Responsibility](4272946.md) (PAR) on July 7, PAGASA subsequently gave the local name *Inday*.[^cite_note-21] This was the third time on record after [Tropical Storm Pabuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Pabuk_\(2024\)) in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Pacific_typhoon_season) and [Typhoon Fung-wong](81509932.md) in [2025](66578517.md) that PAGASA named a system outside the PAR.[^cite_note-22]

However, on July 9, increasing wind shear and a third [eyewall replacement cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyewall_replacement_cycle) caused the storm to weaken below super typhoon strength as the storm made a sharp turn to the northwest due to a subtropical ridge pulling it in. The system started to gradually weaken, with PAGASA downgrading it to Typhoon status on July 9.[^cite_note-23] The system weakened quicker than originally forecasted and was downgraded to a category 1-equivalent typhoon on July 10 as a result of an incomplete eyewall replacement cycle, combined with the effects of cooler sea surface temperatures and increasing wind shear. As the typhoon proceeded northwest, it continually ballooned in size, and according to the [Joint Typhoon Warning Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Typhoon_Warning_Center), Bavi's wind field ranked in the top 3% of all western Pacific typhoons during the previous decade, making it exceptionally large.[^cite_note-24] At around 15\:20 UTC on July 11, Bavi made landfall on the Chinese coast near [Yuhuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuhuan), part of [Zhejiang](96602.md), and a second landfall at [Yueqing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yueqing), part of [Wenzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wenzhou).[^cite_note-25] Whilst inland, Bavi began to exhibit [subtropical characteristics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical_cyclone) as it interacted with a strong upper-level trough allowing it to maintain a shallow convective pattern, prompting the JTWC to classify the system as a subtropical depression. On the following day, the JMA declared that Bavi had transitioned into an [extratropical low](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extratropical_low). The remnants of Bavi accelerated to the northeast as it passed over the [Korean Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Peninsula) and traversed through the [Sea of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_of_Japan) before being absorbed by a larger [low-pressure area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-pressure_area) towards the north of [Hokkaido](58092.md).[^cite_note-26]

## Preparations

### China

#### Mainland

Panic buying at a snack shop Hangzhou due to Typhoon Bavi. A woman looks at rows of half-empty shelves with overturned boxes and a measly amount of packaged noodles left.

[Panic buying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panic_buying) at a snack shop in [Hangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou) due to Typhoon Bavi

On July 8, the National Meteorological Center (NWC) issued a blue warning for a typhoon (lowest tier) for Fujian and Zhejiang provinces in China. On July 10, China's State Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters raised the emergency response for flood control and typhoon prevention in [Zhejiang](96602.md) and [Fujian](88585.md) from Level III to Level II under the nation's four-tier system, while activating a Level IV response for [Hebei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebei) and [Liaoning](155516.md). The Ministry of Emergency Management dispatched working groups to Zhejiang and Fujian to guide local response efforts. Ahead of landfall, Zhejiang water resources authorities initiated a Level IV emergency response and maritime safety authorities activated a Level III response. Coastal regions were ordered to recall fishing vessels to port, evacuate offshore platform workers, and reinforce infrastructure against destructive winds; coastal tourist destinations were placed on high alert, with authorities advised to suspend operations if necessary. The public was urged to monitor forecasts, avoid travel, and prepare emergency supplies.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-auto-28]



Bavi approaching eastern China on July 11

#### Hong Kong

The outer subsiding air stream of Bavi brought extremely hot weather to [Hong Kong](13404.md), with temperatures rising up to 35 °C (95 °F) over the weekend. The Hong Kong Observatory warned the public of dangerous coastal swells and monitored the tightening pressure gradient to determine if a Strong Monsoon Signal was required as local winds veered southwesterly. The storm caused significant travel disruptions; dozens of weekend flights to Taiwan, the mainland of China, and Japan operated by local carriers were cancelled. Additionally, cross-border rail infrastructure was affected, leading to the cancellation of several high-speed rail services between [Hong Kong West Kowloon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_West_Kowloon_station) and Shanghai. Contingency plans were also highlighted for the upcoming release of the Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education (HKDSE) examination results, which faced potential postponement if severe weather conditions persisted into the following week.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-auto-28]

### Taiwan

At Fugang Fishing Harbor in Taitung, more than 10 fishing boat owners hired a crane to lift inflatable fishing rafts onto the shore to protect them from strong winds and waves, as previous typhoons had caused vessels to collide in the harbor.[^cite_note-30]

Officials warned of wind gusts of 133–149 km/h (83–93 mph) and as much as 400 mm (16 in) of accumulated rainfall during the peak of the storm, with mountainous areas facing risk of extremely heavy rain.[^cite_note-31] President [Lai Ching-te](22609914.md) urged people in a Facebook post to prepare supplies from food to torches, and posted a video teaching people how to pull together an emergency grab bag that could give life support for three days.[^cite_note-32]

### Guam

On July 2, Guam Governor [Lou Leon Guerrero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lou_Leon_Guerrero) declared a state of emergency as territory officials began preparing for Bavi.[^cite_note-33] Two days later, a typhoon warning was issued for Guam and surrounding islands.[^cite_note-34] Emergency evacuations were carried out on July 5.[^cite_note-BBC-35] Authorities said the island could see 8–12 inches (200–300 mm) of precipitation, resulting in potential [flash floods](244391.md).[^cite_note-36] All flights were canceled at main airports in Guam and operations were suspended at several ports.[^cite_note-NYT-37]

### Northern Mariana Islands

An [extreme wind warning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_wind_warning) was issued for [Rota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rota,_Northern_Mariana_Islands), with widespread destructive winds in excess of 150 miles per hour (240 km/h) expected. The [National Weather Service](286989.md) warned that going outside could result in death from flying projectiles, with unreinforced structures being destroyed and utility poles and power lines being downed.[^cite_note-38] They added that nearly all trees will be snapped or uprooted, power outages will last for weeks to possibly months and much of the area will be uninhabitable for weeks, perhaps longer.[^cite_note-DW-39] Emergency evacuations were carried out on July 5.[^cite_note-BBC-35] On July 6, Typhoon Bavi became the strongest tropical cyclone on record to strike U.S. territory.[^cite_note-40]

### Philippines

Even though Bavi was still outside the Philippine Area of Responsibility (PAR), the typhoon was given the local name *Inday*. PAGASA raised [Tropical Cyclone Warning Signal No. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Cyclone_Warning_Signal) over the northeastern portion of [Cagayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cagayan) (later the whole province).[^cite_note-41] The agency also subsequently raised Signal No. 1 over [Apayao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apayao), [Babuyan Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babuyan_Islands), [Batanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batanes), northern and central portions of [Catanduanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catanduanes) and [Isabela](271106.md), and the northern portion of [Aurora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurora_\(province\)) when Bavi entered PAR.[^cite_note-42] Signal number 2 was later raised over Batanes and eastern Babuyan Islands, while signal no. 1 was extended to [Ilocos Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocos_Norte), [Kalinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalinga_\(province\)), and parts of [Abra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abra_\(province\)), [Ifugao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ifugao), [Mountain Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_Province) and [Quirino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quirino_\(province\)).[^cite_note-43] All wind signals were lifted after Bavi exited the PAR on July 11.[^cite_note-44]

### Guam

On [Guam](11974.md), the typhoon caused torrential rains and hurricane-force winds.[^cite_note-DW-39] At least four injuries were reported.[^cite_note-Guam_PDN-49]

### Northern Mariana Islands

#### Rota

The center of the typhoon passed only 2 mi (3.2 km) off the northern coast of Rota on July 6, bringing torrential rains and extremely strong Category 5 winds.[^cite_note-DW-39][^cite_note-50] The island's mayor, Aubry Hocog, reported that about half of the structures were damaged or destroyed, and that it could take up to three months to fully restore power to the island due to many, and possibly all, of Rota's wooden utility poles being damaged or destroyed.[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52] In addition, according to Senator [Donald M. Manglona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_M._Manglona), supplies of electrical equipment had already been depleted in Saipan and Tinian during recovery efforts following [Typhoon Sinlaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Sinlaku_\(2026\)) in April, further complicating electrical repairs on Rota.[^cite_note-53] A communications tower was leveled, causing cell phone services to go offline.[^cite_note-54] Hocog also described the island as destroyed "in every aspect," noting that all public facilities, including airports and seaports, were damaged.[^cite_note-\:0-55] The island's main airport, Benjamin Taisacan Manglona International Airport, was among the most heavily damaged pieces of infrastructure, with no clear timeline for when full operations could resume.[^cite_note-\:1-56] Temporary reinforced tin roofs installed for residents after the passage of Sinlaku months earlier were not able to withstand the high winds and were also easily destroyed.[^cite_note-57] Long queues formed waiting to receive clean drinking water, as many pipelines were left unusable.[^cite_note-58][^cite_note-59] [Storm chaser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stormchaser) Jordan Hall, who documented and photographed images of the damage in Rota, compared the deforestation on the island to that of a high-end [EF4](3724479.md)-[EF5](3724479.md) [tornado](37530.md).[^cite_note-60] 2 people were reportedly injured, with no fatalities.[^cite_note-\:0-55]

#### Saipan and Tinian

All 16,683 electrical customers on Saipan and Tinian lost electricity.[^cite_note-\:2-61] 439 people were relocated to shelters.[^cite_note-\:2-61] Both islands also faced a gasoline shortage, as the [United States Coast Guard](32223.md) struggled to reopen commercial ports.[^cite_note-\:2-61] Water service was almost completely interrupted on Saipan as only 0.3% of customers retained service immediately after the typhoon.[^cite_note-\:3-62] All flights were canceled at main airports and operations were suspended at several ports.[^cite_note-NYT-37] On Tinian, Francisco Manglona Borja International Airport sustained damage to its terminal building and perimeter fencing but was quickly re-cleared for operation.[^cite_note-\:1-56] Only very minor damage was reported to transportation infrastructure on Saipan.[^cite_note-\:1-56] Utility officials stated that no power poles were knocked down on Saipan, with damage largely limited to leaning poles and damaged distribution lines.[^cite_note-63] There were no reported deaths or injuries.[^cite_note-\:3-62]

### Philippines

Bavi triggered heavy rains and landslides that killed at least 23 people, injured eight others and left 18 others missing across the Philippines. Around 782,874 people were directly affected, of which 24,000 where displaced. The storm damaged 1,202 houses and 41 infrastructure facilities.[^cite_note-23dead-46] In the village of Pandaw in [Malapatan](854734.md), [Sarangani](297388.md), heavy rains attributed to Bavi, combined with weakened soil from the [2026 Mindanao earthquake](83387873.md), caused a landslide that killed 10 people.[^cite_note-64] Another landslide attributed to Bavi's rains killed seven people, injured two others and left four missing in [Calanogas](2070502.md), [Lanao del Sur](297370.md),[^cite_note-65] three deaths, five injuries and three missing in [Bukidnon](261222.md).[^cite_note-17dead-66] A [state of calamity](8251850.md) was subsequently declared in Calanogas.[^cite_note-67]

### Japan

In [Okinawa Prefecture](22477.md), Bavi resulted in 12 minor injuries and the evacuation of 82,939 people.[^cite_note-68]

### Taiwan

Bavi brought prolonged heavy rainfall and flooding to northern Taiwan.[^cite_note-69] Taiwan's fire department said on July 12 that 134 people had been injured. The transport ministry said 137 international flights and 62 domestic ones were canceled.[^cite_note-70]

### China

By the afternoon of July 12, Bavi had moved into eastern [Anhui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhui) province. The storm is expected to dump heavy to torrential rain across the provinces of [Jilin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jilin), Liaoning, Hebei, [Shandong](105032.md), [Jiangsu](166412.md) and Anhui, exacerbating flood risks in areas that have already been soaked by earlier downpours especially from [Tropical Storm Maysak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Maysak_\(2026\)) earlier in places like Jiangsu, Anhui and Shandong.[^cite_note-71] In [Shenyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenyang), the maximum cumulative rainfall was 223.8 millimetres (8.81 in) on July 13, which breaks the rainfall record in a day in the city.[^cite_note-72]

## Responses

The [Federal Emergency Management Agency](73797.md) (FEMA) stated that they will support Guam and the Northern Mariana Islands as soon as the typhoon moves through.[^cite_note-73]
