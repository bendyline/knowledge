**Dhar district** (Hindi pronunciation: [\[d̪ʱaːɾ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hindi_and_Urdu)) is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Madhya_Pradesh) of [Madhya Pradesh](47945.md) state in [central India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_India). The historic town of [Dhar](911235.md) is administrative headquarters of the district.

The district has a land area of 8,153 square kilometres (3,148 sq mi). It is bounded by the districts of [Ratlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratlam_District) to the north, [Ujjain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ujjain_District) to the northeast, [Indore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indore_District) to the east, [Khargone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khargone_District) (West Nimar) to the southeast, [Barwani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barwani_District) to the south, [Jhabua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhabua_District) and [Alirajpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alirajpur_district) to the west. It is part of the [Indore Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indore_Division) of Madhya Pradesh. The population of the district is 2,185,793 (2011 census), an increase of 25.60% from its 2001 population of 1,740,329. [Pithampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pithampur) is a large industrial area comes under Dhar District. [Kukshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kukshi) is the largest [tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehsil) of the district.

## History

Dhar district was a historical centre of the [Malwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malwa) region. It was part of the [Paramara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramara_dynasty) kingdom for four centuries, and was ruled by [Raja Bhoj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhoja), who shifted the capital from [Ujjain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ujjain) to Dhar. In 1305, [Alauddin Khilji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alauddin_Khalji) conquered Malwa from the Paramaras. In 1405, the Governor of Malwa, [Dilawar Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilawar_Khan), became independent from Delhi and founded the [Malwa Sultanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malwa_Sultanate) with the capital at Dhar. His successor [Hoshang Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoshang_Shah) moved his capital to [Mandu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandu,_Madhya_Pradesh), also in Dhar district, which he developed with many mosques and monuments. His son was poisoned by [Mahmud Khilji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmud_Khalji), who assumed the throne and began the rule of the Khiljis over Malwa. Their rule lasted until 1531, when Sher Shah Suri put Shujat Khan in charge of Malwa. His son [Baz Bahadur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Bahadur) fought the Mughal Army during their conquest of the region, and was forced to flee after he was defeated. According to legend, his beloved queen [Roopmati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roopmati) committed suicide rather than be dishonoured.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

Dhar became part of the Malwa Subah of the Mughal Empire, and was visited by Akbar and Jahangir. After the decline of Mughal power, the Marathas captured Malwa under the rule of Peshwa Bajirao. Bajirao in 1732 divided Malwa among many Maratha chiefs, and Dhar was given to Anand Rao Pawar. His dynasty continued to rule the state even after the Marathas fell to the British. In 1857, freedom fighters captured the fort at Dhar which they held until October 1857. The local people faced cruel reprisals from British forces after they retook the fort. Dhar was briefly annexed by the British after the revolt before it was returned to Anand Rao Pawar III in 1860. Dhar State joined the Indian Union in 1948, and became part of the state of [Madhya Bharat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhya_Bharat). It then became part of Madhya Pradesh in 1956.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## Geography

The [Vindhya Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vindhya_Range) runs east and west through the district. The northern part of the district lies on the [Malwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malwa) plateau. The northwestern portion of the district lies in the watershed of the [Mahi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahi_River) and its tributaries, while the northeastern part of the district lies in the watershed of the [Chambal River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chambal_River), which drains into the [Ganges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganges) via the [Yamuna River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamuna_River). The portion of the district south of the ridge of the [Vindhyas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vindhya) lies in the watershed of the [Narmada River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narmada_River), which forms the southern boundary of the district.

## Divisions

Dhar district is divided into 6 sub-divisions: [Dhar](911235.md), [Sardarpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardarpur), [Badnawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badnawar), [Manawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manawar), [Kukshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kukshi) and [Dharampuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharampuri) (Newly Added). These sub-divisions are further divided into 8 tehsils: Dhar, Badnawar, Dharampuri, Sardarpur, Manawar, Kukshi, [Dahi ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahi,_Madhya_Pradesh) and [Gandhwani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandhwani).[^cite_note-\:1-2]

There are seven [Vidhan Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vidhan_Sabha) constituencies in this district: [Sardarpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardarpur_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)), [Gandhwani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandhwani_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)), [Kukshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kukshi_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)), [Manawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manawar_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)), [Dharampuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharampuri_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)), [Dhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhar_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)) and [Badnawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badnawar_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)). All of these are part of the only [Lok Sabha](275456.md) constituency in this district : [Dhar Lok Sabha Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhar_\(Lok_Sabha_constituency\)).[^cite_note-\:1-2]

Villages in Dhar district include [Kadod Kala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadod_Kala), [Tirla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirla), and [Tornod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornod).[^cite_note-\:1-2]

According to the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India) Dhar District has a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_India) of 2,185,793,[^cite_note-districtcensus-5] roughly equal to the nation of [Latvia](17514.md)[^cite_note-cia-6] or the US state of [New Mexico](21649.md).[^cite_note-7] This gives it a ranking of 208th in India (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-districtcensus-5] The district has a population density of 268 inhabitants per square kilometre (690/sq mi) .[^cite_note-districtcensus-5] Its [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_India) over the decade 2001-2011 was 25.53%.[^cite_note-districtcensus-5] Dhar has a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) of 961 [females](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_India) for every 1000 males,[^cite_note-districtcensus-5] and a [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of 60.57%. 18.90% of the population lives in urban areas. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes made up 6.65% and 55.94% of the population respectively.[^cite_note-districtcensus-5]

At the time of the [2011 Census of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Census_of_India), 51.28% of the population in the district spoke [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi), 15.62% [Malvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malvi_language), 15.29% [Nimadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nimadi_language), 11.49% [Bhili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhili_language) and 3.87% [Bhilali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhilali_language) as their first language. The local dialect is Malvi in most of the district, with a small part in the south speaking Nimadi. Bhili is spoken by a minority of Bhils, especially in the far-western Dahi tehsil.[^cite_note-language-8]
