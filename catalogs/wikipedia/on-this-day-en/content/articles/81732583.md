**Barak MX** is an Israeli [modular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modularity) multi-mission air and missile defense system suitable for land and naval deployment, designed to defend against [aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft), [helicopters](8286923.md), [unmanned aerial vehicle](58900.md) (UAV), [anti-ship missiles](140725.md), [cruise missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruise_missiles), as well as tactical [ballistic missiles](38025.md). The original purpose of the system is to protect [vessels](301664.md) and [strategic naval installations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_platform).[^cite_note-kipa-1] The system was developed and manufactured by [Israel Aerospace Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Aerospace_Industries).[^cite_note-2] The missile's [rocket engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocket_engine) is manufactured at the "Givon" plant (formerly of [IMI Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMI_Systems) and since November 2018 of "Tomer ([Elbit Systems](1341669.md))").[^cite_note-IAI_enroute_to_Barak-8ER-3]

Unlike the [Barak 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barak_8) system, which was developed and manufactured in [cooperation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperation) between IAI, [Rafael Advanced Defense Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Advanced_Defense_Systems) and the Indian [Defence Research and Development Organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Research_and_Development_Organisation) (DRDO),[^cite_note-\:8-4][^cite_note-\:15-5] and the [Barak 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barak_1), which was developed together with Rafael, the Barak MX is an exclusive development of IAI based on the experience of the Barak 8 and Barak 1 systems.[^cite_note-IAI_enroute_to_Barak-8ER-3]

The system is installed on the [Israeli Navy](1370788.md) 's [Sa'ar 6-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sa'ar_6-class_corvette) ships, where it is called "**Barak Magen**". The land-based configuration of the system was also used by the [Israeli Air Force](177619.md) 's [air defence system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_defence_system) during [Operation Am Kalavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War).[^cite_note-IAI_enroute_to_Barak-8ER-3]

## Characteristics and mode of operation

Barak MX is a modular system that includes a fire control center,[^cite_note-6] a variety of [radar](25676.md) types, and four types of interceptors. The system components can be customized according to customer requirements, and installed on land and various sea platforms, including [warships](263960.md) such as [missile boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missile_boat), [corvettes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corvette), [frigates](11464.md) or [destroyers](8779.md).[^cite_note-7]

The system includes four models of interceptors:

- **SR** – short-range interceptor of 15 km (9.3 mi)

- **MR** – 35 km (22 mi) short and medium range interceptor (formerly known as MRAD)

  [^cite_note-8]

- **LR** – 70 km (43 mi) medium and long-range interceptor, sharing the same body with the MR with a dual-pulse rocket motor inside (formerly known as LRAD)

  [^cite_note-9]

- **ER** – A LR missile with a booster, has an extended range of 150 km (93 mi) and a maximum altitude of 30 km (98,000 ft)

  [^cite_note-10]

Another proposed interceptor was displayed at the 2025 [Paris Air Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Air_Show):

- **EA** – Variant with an extended booster, has a range of 250 km (160 mi) and a maximum altitude of more than 35 km (115,000 ft), also featuring an optical seeker

  [^cite_note-11]

## Development and testing

On 22 March 2021, it was reported that a successful series of tests of the Barak ER version, capable of intercepting threats at a range of 150 km, had been completed.[^cite_note-12]

On 30 November 2022, a successful test launch of an LR missile from an Israeli Navy [Sa'ar 6-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sa'ar_6-class_corvette) ship was conducted, intercepting a target missile that resembled a cruise missile.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

On 1 August 2024, a successful test of an LR interceptor launched from the Sa'ar 6 ship AHI *Independence* was completed.[^cite_note-15]

## Operational activity

On 16 June 2025, during the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War) between Israel and Iran, the first successful operational use of the Israeli Navy's system was reported to have intercepted UAVs launched from Iran.[^cite_note-16] A land-based version of the system, known in the IDF as **Barak**, was operated by the [Air Defense Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Defense_Command_\(Israel\)), and recorded its first interception on 19 June 2025.[^cite_note-17]

### Future users

-  [Netherlands](21148.md): In September 2024, the Netherlands revealed plans to buy two [Multifunctional Support Ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multifunctional_Support_Ship). These ships would be armed with containerized modules, with Barak ER missiles selected as one of the systems.

  [^cite_note-TWZ250924-32]

-  [Thailand](30128.md): In December 2025, the Royal Thai Air Force signed a \$108 million contract with Israel Aerospace Industries to acquire the Barak MX air and missile defense system.

  [^cite_note-33]

-  [Greece](12108.md): In August 2026, Greece ordered Barak MX, alongside [SPYDER](9830103.md) and [David's Sling](18975145.md), as part of the Achilles' Shield layered air defense system.
