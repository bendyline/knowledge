**Mandera** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Mandheer*a) is the capital of [Mandera County](7697052.md) in the former [North Eastern Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Eastern_Province,_Kenya) of [Kenya](188171.md). It is situated at around [3°55′N 41°50′E  /  3.917°N 41.833°E  / 3.917; 41.833](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Mandera\&params=3_55_N_41_50_E_), near the borders with [Somalia](27358.md) and [Ethiopia](187749.md).

## Districts

Mandera was the capital of the former [Mandera District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandera_District).[^cite_note-Districts-2]

As a whole, it used to constitute one district in previous administrations, which is now divided into six sub-counties and Constituencies; namely,&#x20;

1. [Mandera East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandera_East?action=edit\&redlink=1)

2. [Mandera Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandera_Central?action=edit\&redlink=1)

3. [Mandera West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandera_West)

4. [Mandera North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandera_north?action=edit\&redlink=1)

5. [Banissa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banissa)

6. Elwak

7. Lafey

The county occupies an area of 26,744 km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-Districts-2]

Among the notable incidents of Mandera history was the [Rhamu Incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhamu_Incident) of the 1977 in which [Somali](27358.md) military invaded [Ethiopian](187749.md) defences from the [Kenyan](188171.md) side. A force of 1500 Somali soldiers attacked a border post, and killed 30 Kenyan police officers and soldiers. The Somali army successfully crossed into the Sidamo Region of Ethiopia in the so called [Ogaden War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogaden_War).[^cite_note-3]

## Demographics

Most of Mandera's inhabitants are [ethnic Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Kenya). Economic mainstay largely remains livestock trade.

Mandera's climate is categorized as arid under the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification). Temperatures tend to be hot throughout the year. Daily temperatures are typically above 30 °C (86 °F), while at night, they can fall to 20 °C (68 °F). Precipitation is extremely low, with the area receiving a minimal amount of rain. Droughts are not unusual, often resulting in significant loss of livestock in rural areas where pastoralism is common.[^cite_note-4]
