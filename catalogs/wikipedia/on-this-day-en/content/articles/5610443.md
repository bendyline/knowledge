The **Kenya Police Service** is the national police force and law enforcement agency of the [Republic of Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Kenya). It is subordinate to the **National Police Service** which is headed by an Inspector General of Police who exercises independent command over the Service. Kenya Police is headed by Deputy Inspector General. Kenya Police is divided into Service Headquarters in Nairobi, Formations, General Duty Commands and Training Institutions.

The [Administration Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_Police) is commanded through a hierarchy separate from that of the Kenya Police.[^cite_note-aronson-8] For other state security bodies see [Law enforcement in Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_in_Kenya). Recruitment to the police service is done on yearly basis.[^cite_note-9]

## History

The Kenya police force was established as a British colonial police force in 1907. From 1887 to 1902 policing was provided by the East Africa Trading Company. After 1902 the [Kenya-Uganda Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenya-Uganda_Railway) introduced their own police units.[^cite_note-academia.edu-10]

In 1906 the Police Ordinance was established to create a new force in 1907, the Nairobi Mounted Police within the jurisdiction of the [East Africa Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Africa_Protectorate). The current force's name came into effect in 1920 with the newly created British [Kenya Colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenya_Colony).

The colonial force was made up mainly of [British](19097669.md) and [Indian](7564733.md) recruits as senior officers and Africans amongst lower ranks.[^cite_note-academia.edu-10]

Following Kenya's independence, the British officers were replaced with local Kenyan members.

## Structure

### General

The Kenya Police is structured into two main categories: General Duty and Formations. General Duty includes various levels, with regions being the highest and police patrol bases being the most basic units.

Regional Police Commander is in charge of a Region (formerly [Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Kenya)), County Police Commander is in charge of Officers in the [County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Kenya)(previously called District), Sub-County Police Commander Superintends [Sub-County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub-Counties_of_Kenya) (previously called Division). Officer Commanding Station (OCS) is in charge of a Police Station in a Ward and oversees all its Police Posts and Patrol Bases (Ward Commander).

### Formations

The Kenya Police formations/Units[^cite_note-11] are headed by [commandants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commandant)/directors, who hold the rank of Senior Assistant Inspector General, [Assistant Inspector General of Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_Inspector_General_of_Police) (AIG) or [Commissioner of Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commissioner_of_Police) (CP).

## Ongoing changes

Following the promulgation of the [new Constitution of Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proposed_Constitution_of_Kenya,_2010) on 27 August 2010, as laid down in Chapter 17 Part 4, the Kenyan police forces is undergoing a series of reforms. Hence called *The Kenya Police Service*, it is now headed by a Deputy Inspector-General and the division of its functions are organised to take into account the devolved structure of government in Kenya.

In the ongoing changes that started in 2018, police operational command was aligned police with existing administrative boundaries to create a unified command as follows:

- Regional Police Commander (RPC)

- County Police Commander (CPC)

- Sub County Police Commander (SPC)

- Officer in charge Police Station (OCS)

The following positions were therefore abolished:

- APS Regional Commander

- KPS Regional Commander

- DCI Regional Commander

- County Coordinating Commander

- KPS County Commander

- APS County Commander

- DCI County Commander

- APS Sub County Commander

- DCI Sub County Commander

- KPS Officer Commanding Police Divisions

- District Administration Police Commander

Consequently, the number of commanders were reduced from 168 to 56 Commanders.

## Societal impact

Following concerns about human right abuses by the Kenya Police, efforts are being made to reform the force.[^cite_note-17] Some Kenyan policemen face challenges with their housing that has not been expanded or renovated since the 1970s. These challenges have resulted to corruption and crime. Extortion and bribery are known practices and the Kenyan people rank the police among the most corrupt bodies in the country.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] In July 2010, the Minister, [George Saitoti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Saitoti), announced a 28% pay increase for junior officers and a 25% pay increase for senior officers. This reform means that the most junior officer, a police constable, shall receive [KSh.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenyan_shilling)21,000/= per month including allowances.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-aronson-8]
