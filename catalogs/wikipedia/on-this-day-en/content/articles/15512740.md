**Khánh Vĩnh** is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Vietnam) (*huyện*) of [Khánh Hòa province](2542623.md) in the [South Central Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Central_Coast) region of [Vietnam](202354.md).

As of 2007, the district had a population of 50,110.[^cite_note-Statoids-1] The district covers an area of 1,165 km<sup>2</sup> (450 sq mi). The district capital lies at Khánh Vĩnh.[^cite_note-Statoids-1]
