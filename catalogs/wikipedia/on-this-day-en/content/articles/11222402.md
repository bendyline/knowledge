**Law enforcement in Lithuania** is the responsibility of a national [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police) under the [jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurisdiction) of the [Interior Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Interior_\(Lithuania\)). The main institution is the **Lithuanian Police Department** ([Lithuanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_language): *Lietuvos Policijos Departamentas*) led by the Police Commissar General.

## History

The origins of the Lithuanian police could be traced back to the [Grand Duchy of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Lithuania), when the first *Boni Ordinis* Commission (literally, *Good Order Commission*) was established in [Gardinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gardinas) in 1776 (later in [Brest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brest,_Belarus), [Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minsk), [Kaunas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaunas) and other cities).[^cite_note-gdl-police-article-4][^cite_note-gdl-police-activities-history-5] These institutions were directly subordinate to the [Sejm of the Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sejm_of_the_Polish%E2%80%93Lithuanian_Commonwealth).[^cite_note-gdl-police-article-4] However, the [regional magnates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnates_of_Poland_and_Lithuania) were resisting the reforms as they saw the new institutions as a restriction of their authority.[^cite_note-gdl-police-article-4] In 1775, the [Permanent Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permanent_Council) of the [Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish%E2%80%93Lithuanian_Commonwealth) established a Police department, but it did not have the authority over the Boni Ordinis Commissions and served primarily as an advisory body.[^cite_note-gdl-police-article-4] In 1791, the [Great Sejm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Sejm) established the Police Commission of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth.[^cite_note-gdl-police-development-history-6] A separate Police Commission of the [Grand Duchy of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Lithuania) with the headquarters in [Vilnius](32597.md) was established in 1793.[^cite_note-gdl-police-development-history-6] It is considered to be the predecessor of the contemporary [Ministry of the Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Interior_\(Lithuania\)).[^cite_note-gdl-police-article-4]

The origins of the contemporary police are the volunteer [militia](20622.md) formed in 1918 after the [declaration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Act_of_Independence_of_Lithuania) of the [Republic of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Lithuania).[^cite_note-lt-police-history-3] The uniforms were approved in 1919 and the first laws regulating the police were adopted on 14 May 1920. The organisation was formally renamed to *police* on 1 January 1924.[^cite_note-lt-police-history-3] The police was divided into *public police* and *criminal police*.[^cite_note-interwar-police-7] There were several reforms and reorganizations in the following years and the *Police Department* was established on 1 January 1935, which composed of public, border, railway, private and water police divisions.[^cite_note-lt-police-history-3] In 1927, police employed 1262 officers, while in 1940 police had 2735 officers.[^cite_note-interwar-police-7]

The current form of police was established on 11 December 1990, following the adoption of the Law on Police.[^cite_note-lt-police-history-3] In 1991, Lithuania joined [Interpol](14338129.md).[^cite_note-lkbb-interpol-europol-8] Since joining the [European Union](9317.md), Lithuania is also a member of [Europol](525329.md).[^cite_note-lkbb-interpol-europol-8]

## Structure and branches

Lithuanian Police Department is established under the [Ministry of the Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Interior_\(Lithuania\)).[^cite_note-lt-police-vlk-9] It is led by the [Police Commissioner General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_Commissioner). The department consists of ten County Police Headquarters ([Lithuanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_language): *apskrities vyriausiasis policijos komisariatas*, literally *county chief police commissariat*).[^cite_note-lt-police-vlk-9] They are further divided into sub-units of [local police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_police).[^cite_note-osce-lt-police-structure-10]

Additionally, the Police Department consists of the following specialized institutions:[^cite_note-lt-police-vlk-9][^cite_note-osce-lt-police-structure-10]

-  Traffic Police Service

-  Criminal Police Bureau

-  Police Anti-terrorist Operations Unit [ARAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARAS_\(Lithuania\))

-  Forensic Science Centre

-  Police School
