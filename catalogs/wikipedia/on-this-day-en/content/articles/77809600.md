**Marta Kos** (Slovene pronunciation: [\[ˈmáːrta ˈkóːs\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Slovene); born 28 June 1965) is a Slovenian diplomat and politician. She served as ambassador to Germany from 2013 to 2017, and as ambassador to Switzerland from 2017 to 2020.[^cite_note-1] Since December 2024, she is the [European Commissioner for Enlargement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Commissioner_for_Neighbourhood_and_Enlargement) in the [second von der Leyen Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_der_Leyen_Commission_II).

From April to September 2022, she was one of the vice presidents of the [Freedom Movement](68011973.md).[^cite_note-2] Ahead of the [2022 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Slovenian_presidential_election), she was the candidate of the Freedom Movement for [president of Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Slovenia) until her withdrawal in August.[^cite_note-3] Following the withdrawal she resigned as Freedom Movement vice president. Following resignation of [Tatjana Bobnar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatjana_Bobnar) as Minister of the Interior she also resigned from the Freedom Movement stating that it is not her party anymore.[^cite_note-rtvslo.si-4]

## Early life and career

In her youth, Kos was a record-holding Yugoslav and Slovenian swimming champion.[^cite_note-Sobotainfo-5] She studied journalism and joined the sports division of [RTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiotelevizija_Slovenija), and later moved to Germany where she worked for [Deutsche Welle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Welle) and as [Bonn](3295.md) correspondent for RTV starting in 1993.[^cite_note-Sobotainfo-5] From 1997 to 1999, she served as director of the Slovenian government information office and as spokesperson of the government.[^cite_note-6] In 2000, she was appointed vice president of the [Chamber of Commerce and Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Commerce_and_Industry_of_Slovenia).[^cite_note-Sobotainfo-5]

In 2013, the government of [Alenka Bratušek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alenka_Bratu%C5%A1ek) appointed her Ambassador to Berlin, Germany, as part of a political quota (non-career diplomats).[^cite_note-7] In 2017, she was appointed Ambassador to Bern, Switzerland.[^cite_note-8] In 2020, she resigned as ambassador due to disagreement with the government's foreign policy, as well as accusations of mobbing and consequential internal control over the work of the embassy.[^cite_note-9]

In 2022, she announced her candidacy for President of the Republic.[^cite_note-10] In unexplained circumstances, she resigned from her candidacy and resigned as vice-president of the Freedom Movement.[^cite_note-11] After the resignation of [Tatjana Bobnar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatjana_Bobnar) as Minister of the Interior, due to a conflict with Prime Minister [Robert Golob](70490527.md), she also resigned from the Freedom Movement party in 2023, explaining that it was no longer her party, thus supporting Tatjana Bobnar.[^cite_note-rtvslo.si-4]

In September 2024, she was proposed as a candidate for a member of [European commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_commissioner) for Enlargement, responsible for overseeing relations with Eastern neighbors replacing [Tomaž Vesel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toma%C5%BE_Vesel), who resigned from the candidacy for European Commissioner.[^cite_note-12] On 17 September 2024, [Ursula von der Leyen](2991932.md) announced Kos would be in charge of the [European enlargement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlargement_of_the_European_Union), pending approval of the [European Parliament](9581.md), thus assessing [membership applications of Ukraine](36830143.md) and [Moldova](54111451.md) and other countries to join the European Union.[^cite_note-13] In September 2024, Kos was accused then by the right-wing [Slovenian Democratic Party](425720.md) over her alleged involvement with the former [Yugoslav Communist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Yugoslavia) secret service, [UDBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UDBA).[^cite_note-14] In March 2026 Kos faced renewed allegations of having collaborated with the UDBA.[^cite_note-15]

## Personal life

Kos is married to Henri Gétaz, who was the secretary general of the [European Free Trade Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Free_Trade_Association) until 2023.[^cite_note-16]
