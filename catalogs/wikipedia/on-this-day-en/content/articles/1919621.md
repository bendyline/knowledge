# Gas explosion



A balloon filled with gaseous [hydrogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen) exploding.

A **gas explosion** is the [ignition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combustion) of a mixture of air and flammable gas, typically from a [gas leak](1919614.md).[^cite_note-1] In household accidents, the principal explosive gases are those used for heating or cooking purposes such as [natural gas](22131.md), [methane](18582230.md), [propane](23643.md), [butane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butane). In industrial explosions, many other gases, like [hydrogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen), as well as evaporated (gaseous) [gasoline](23639.md) or [ethanol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethanol) play an important role. Industrial gas [explosions](18993857.md) can be prevented with the use of [intrinsic safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intrinsic_safety) barriers to prevent ignition, or use of alternative energy.

## Lower and upper explosive limits

Whether a mixture of air and gas is combustible depends on the [air-to-fuel ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air%E2%80%93fuel_ratio). For each fuel, ignition occurs only within a certain range of concentration, known as the [upper and lower flammability limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flammability_limit). For example, for [methane](18582230.md) and [gasoline](23639.md) vapor, this range is 5-15% and 1.4-7.6% gas to air, respectively. An explosion can only occur when fuel concentration is within these limits.

### 1900–1950

- Australian federal MP [George Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Edwards_\(Australian_politician\)) and a gas mechanic were killed on February 4, 1911, when a gasometer exploded on Edwards' property in [Turramurra, New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turramurra,_New_South_Wales).

  [^cite_note-2]

  [^cite_note-3]

- The [Pittsburgh gasometer explosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh_gasometer_explosion) occurred during the morning of November 14, 1927, in the [north side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Side_\(Pittsburgh\)) of [Pittsburgh](25101.md). A large [gasometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gasometer) exploded, killing 28 people and injuring hundreds.

- The [Mather Mine disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mather_Mine_disaster) occurred on May 19, 1928, in [Mather, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mather,_Pennsylvania). A methane gas and dust explosion resulted in the deaths of 195 men.

  [^cite_note-USBM-4]

  [^cite_note-UNT-5]

- The [New London School explosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_London_School_explosion) occurred on March 18, 1937, when a [natural gas leak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_gas_leak) caused an explosion, destroying the New London School of the city of [New London, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_London,_Texas). The disaster killed three hundred students and teachers.

- On January 27, 1943, 43 people in the Sydney suburb of [Chippendale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chippendale,_New_South_Wales) inhaled fumes after a chlorine cylinder leaked.

  [^cite_note-6]

- The [Cleveland East Ohio Gas Explosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland_East_Ohio_Gas_Explosion) occurred on the afternoon of Friday, October 20, 1944. The resulting gas leak, explosion and fires killed 130 people and destroyed a one-square-mile area on [Cleveland, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland,_Ohio)’s east side.

  &#x20;The cause was an explosion following a propane tank leak.

- On March 1, 1965, the [LaSalle Heights Disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaSalle_Heights_Disaster) occurred when a gas line fractured in a low-cost residential neighborhood near [Montreal, Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal,_Quebec), killing 28 people and injuring dozens.

- On January 9, 1968, in [Reading, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reading,_Pennsylvania), an explosion killed nine people and demolished two houses. A gas company spokesman said Reading workmen digging in the street to repair a water main had hit a gas line shortly before the explosion.

  [^cite_note-9]

- On Saturday, April 6, 1968, the [Richmond, Indiana explosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Indiana_explosion) occurred in the middle of downtown [Richmond, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Indiana). There were two explosions; the first was caused by a natural gas leak, while the second by gunpowder and ammunition inside a sporting goods store. 41 people were killed and more than 150 injured, and four squares of downtown Richmond, Indiana, were heavily damaged by the explosion or subsequent fire.

- [Ronan Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronan_Point) was a 23-story [council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_house) [tower block](118767.md) in [Newham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Borough_of_Newham), east London. - In July 1988, 167 people died when [Occidental Petroleum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occidental_Petroleum)'s [Alpha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piper_Alpha) offshore production platform, on the Piper field in the [North Sea](21179.md), exploded after a gas leak.
