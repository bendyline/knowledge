The **Public Institution For Social Security** (PIFSS; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): المؤسسة العامة للتأمينات الاجتماعية‎) is the public [pension fund](43615.md) of the state of [Kuwait](7515890.md). It was founded in 1976 and counts 11 subsidiaries and 45 documented transactions.[^cite_note-\:0-1] In 2021, its cash reserves accounted to 4% of its investments down from 11.5% in 2020.[^cite_note-2]

The current worth of the assets of the PIFSS is estimated at \$134 billion.[^cite_note-\:0-1] It recorded its best ever annual performance year in 2021, recording a 20.9% growth in its assets.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

In 2026 the main branch of PIFSS has been [struck by Iran](82554211.md) in its retaliation strikes.

## Investments

The pension fund owns 25% of the U.S. private equity firm Stone Point Capital, 25% of Oak Hill Advisors and 10% of [TowerBrook Capital Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TowerBrook_Capital_Partners).[^cite_note-5]
