The **eighty-first session of the United Nations General Assembly** is the current session of the [United Nations General Assembly](31957.md), which began on 8 September 2026.[^cite_note-1] The [president of the General Assembly](780080.md) is [Khalilur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalilur_Rahman) from the [Asia-Pacific Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia-Pacific_Group).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Organisation for the session

### President

On 2 June 2026, [Bangladesh](3454.md)'s [Foreign Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Bangladesh\)) [Khalilur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalilur_Rahman) was elected to the position of [President of the General Assembly](780080.md), defeating [Cyprus](5593.md)'s Special Envoy for Multilateralism, Ambassador Andreas Kakouris.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The election was conducted under Rule 30 of the Rules of Procedure in the General Assembly Hall at the United Nations Headquarters in New York.[^cite_note-7] The result was announced by outgoing President [Annalena Baerbock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annalena_Baerbock) in the presence of [UN Secretary-General](162415.md) [António Guterres](471806.md).[^cite_note-8] With his election, Bangladesh holds the UNGA presidency for the second time, the first being in 1986–87 when [Humayun Rashid Choudhury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humayun_Rashid_Choudhury) served as President of the [41st session](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forty-first_session_of_the_United_Nations_General_Assembly).[^cite_note-9]

### General debate

Main article: [General debate of the eighty-first session of the United Nations General Assembly](83962647.md)



[Pro-Palestine protesters](75347645.md) in [Times Square](31389.md) in front of a state sponsored pro-Israel ad on the day of Netanyahu's speech, September 24

Protesters march through [Manhattan](45470.md) on the day of Netanyahu's speech, September 24

The high-level week of the 81st session of the United Nations General Assembly began in New York on 22 September 2026, continuing through Saturday, 26 September, and concluding on Monday, 28 September 2026.[^cite_note-18] The programme included the general debate as well as high-level meetings on climate action, sea-level rise, pandemic prevention and preparedness, and development issues.[^cite_note-19] Discussions focused on international conflicts, artificial intelligence, humanitarian crises and reform of the international system.&#x20;

Each member of the General Assembly will have a representative speaking about issues concerning their country and the hopes for the coming year as to what the UNGA will do. This is an opportunity for the member states to opine on international issues of their concern.

The order of speakers is given first to member states, then observer states and supranational bodies. Any other observer entities will have a chance to speak at the end of the debate, if they so choose. Speakers will be put on the list in the order of their request, with special consideration for ministers and other government officials of similar or higher rank. According to the rules in place for the General Debate, the statements should be in one of the United Nations official languages of [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic), [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language), [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language), [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language) or [Spanish](26825.md), and will be translated by the [United Nations translators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_translators). Each speaker is requested to provide 350 advance copies of their statements to the conference officers to facilitate translation and to be presented at the podium.[^cite_note-20]
