**Ardamata** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): اردمتا) is a neighborhood located in the northeastern part of [Geneina](1501534.md), [Sudan](27421.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The neighborhood is near the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md)'s 15th Infantry Division camp.[^cite_note-5]

## History

On 8 November 2023, the [Janjaweed](683270.md) and [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) [massacred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardamata_massacre) about 800 civilians in Ardamata, [West Darfur](767645.md), Sudan.[^cite_note-6]

## Demographics

The village is home to mainly ethnic [Masalit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masalit_people) residents.[^cite_note-7]
