**Vardan Kolya Ghukasyan** ([Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_language): Վարդան Կոլյայի Ղուկասյան; born 20 January 1961), is an Armenian politician and businessman, member of the [Armenian Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_Communist_Party), deputy of the [National Assembly](4014302.md) of the Republic of Armenia (2017–2019) and Mayor of [Gyumri](1529357.md) from 2000 to 2012, and later from 16 April 2025.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] He sparked controversy when he called for a [transgender](19904525.md) activist to be burned alive. He has been repeatedly condemned for [transphobic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transphobia) comments.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Arrest

Police arrested Ghukasyan on 20 October 2025 on bribery charges. The country's Anti-Corruption Committee claims it has evidence that he accepted bribes to prevent the demolition of an illegal building in the city.[^cite_note-6]

The ACC says Ghukasyan demanded 4 million AMD (\$10,400) to let the building stand. The city's chief architect was also arrested as an accomplice in the case.[^cite_note-Police_Arrest_Gyumri_Mayor_for_Bribery-7]
