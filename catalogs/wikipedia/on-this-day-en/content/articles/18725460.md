# Tourism in Chile

Promotional videos on tourism in [Chile](5489.md) and [Easter Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easter_Island) of [Sernatur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Tourism_Service_of_Chile?action=edit\&redlink=1).

Since the mid-1990s, **tourism in Chile** has become one of the main sources of income for the country, especially in its most extreme areas. In 2005, this sector grew by 13.6%, generating more than US\$500 million, equivalent to 1.33% of the national [GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_Domestic_Product).

According to the [World Tourism Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Tourism_Organization) (WTO), [Chile](5489.md) was the eighth most popular destination for foreign [tourists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism) within the [Americas](29833.md) in 2010, after the [United States](577095.md), [Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_Mexico), [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_Canada), [Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_Argentina), [Brazil](8794264.md), the [Dominican Republic](46180040.md), and [Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_Puerto_Rico).[^cite_note-wto-1] That year, 2,766,000 tourists entered the country, generating a revenue of US\$1,636 million.[^cite_note-wto-1] The majority of these visitors came from American countries, mainly [Argentina](18951905.md); however, the biggest growth in recent years has been in visitors from [Europe](9239.md), especially [Germany](11867.md).[^cite_note-estadisticas2011-2]

In 2017, a record total of 6,449,993 international tourists visited Chile, a 13.3% increase from 2016. Argentina remained the most common country of origin, followed by Brazil. European tourists were third in terms of total numbers. The average tourist stayed for 10 nights. The Chilean government attributes the rise in tourism to "promotional campaigns, the development of new products and tourist destinations and a renewed diversification of experiences."[^cite_note-3]

Tourism for the year 2018 was projected to continue the increase in visitors, with more than 7 million international tourists estimated to travel to Chile.[^cite_note-4] Online guidebook [Lonely Planet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lonely_Planet) has listed Chile as its number 1 destination to visit in 2018. Lonely Planet emphasizes visiting the city of [Valparaíso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valpara%C3%ADso), the northern [Atacama Desert](18952975.md), and [Patagonia](65433.md) to the south.[^cite_note-5] However, in 2018 and 2019, the Chilean tourism industry was plunged into a deep crisis by the adverse effects of [internal unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%932022_Chilean_protests) and the [Argentine monetary crisis](44816333.md). As a consequence, Chile saw international tourism arrivals fall by more than 20% in 2019, to about 4.5 million.[^cite_note-6] The following year, the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md) caused further disruption to the Chilean tourism sector,[^cite_note-7] with a fall of 85.7% on 2019 and total visitors estimated at 1,122,858.[^cite_note-8] In 2021, just 161,000 foreigners visited Chile.[^cite_note-9]

## Geographic overview

The [latitudinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latitude) width of the country, which spans over 39° (over 72° if the Chilean Antarctic Territory is included) its elevation and influence of the Pacific Ocean are the main factors behind the [climatic variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Chile) and landscape of Chile,[^cite_note-ce2006-12] which determines the development of the formation of different [ecosystems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecosystem) in the country.

The main Chilean [attractions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourist_attraction) are the 6,435 km long [coast](5236.md),[^cite_note-coast-18] the [Andean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andean) [Ski resorts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ski_resorts), the [mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mountains_in_Chile) and [volcanoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_volcanoes_in_Chile), and the [islands and archipelagos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_islands_of_Chile), including Easter Island.

The country has 14 natural monuments, 36 National Parks, 10 biosphere reserves, 52 natural reserves, 39 [sanctuaries of Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Monuments_of_Chile#Sanctuaries_of_Nature), and 12 [Ramsar wetlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Ramsar_wetlands_of_international_importance#), mainly in the extreme parts of the country.[^cite_note-19]

The [Atacama Desert](18952975.md), the [driest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_climate) desert in the world[^cite_note-21] was picked in October 2014 by [Lonely Planet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lonely_Planet) to be among the 10 top travel regions of 2015.[^cite_note-22] The desert, which has been inhabited for several thousand years, makes up the main portion of the [Norte Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norte_Grande,_Chile). A myriad of [geoglyphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoglyph), [petroglyphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroglyph) and [pictographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pictograph) attest the presence of ancient cultures in the area. Among the most remarkable are those of [Azapa Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azapa_Valley), [Lluta Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lluta_River), the [Atacama Giant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atacama_Giant) and Pintados Geoglyphs, these latter are protected within the [Pampa del Tamarugal National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pampa_del_Tamarugal_National_Reserve). [R. P. Gustavo Le Paige Archaeological Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._P._Gustavo_Le_Paige_Archaeological_Museum) and the Archaeological Museum of San Miguel de Azapa are home to the most important collections of artifacts and mummies in Chile, including the famous [Chinchorro mummies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinchorro_mummies), the oldest mummies in the world,[^cite_note-23] of the [Chinchorro culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinchorro_culture) buried between 5000 and 1700. C. The extreme aridity of the Atacama has been a key factor in the preservation of such archaeological remains.

The [Chuquicamata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuquicamata) copper deposit boasts the largest [open pit mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_pit_mine) in the world[^cite_note-24] and the remains of extractions made from 12,000 and 10,000 years ago in an [iron oxide mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SR15_Mine?action=edit\&redlink=1), the oldest mine in the continent.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

[Arica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arica,_Chile) is a coastal city with beaches and is also a starting point for travelers heading east to [Putre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putre) for enjoying the majestic [Puna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Andean_dry_puna) landscape of [Lauca National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauca_National_Park). Southward from Lauca, [Volcán Isluga National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volc%C3%A1n_Isluga_National_Park) includes ceremonial sites for the [Aymara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aymara_people) people.

Inland from [Iquique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iquique), the desolate pampas are home to a few scattered towns and villages. Among those, the most interesting is the ghost town of [Humberstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humberstone_and_Santa_Laura_Saltpeter_Works), declared [World Heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Heritage) by [UNESCO](21786641.md) in 2005. In this area the [tamarugo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamarugo) forests of the [Pampa del Tamarugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pampa_del_Tamarugal) break the barren monotony of the landscape.

The altiplano of Norte Grande has a wide variety of natural scenery including salt flats, volcanoes, lakes and geysers. [San Pedro de Atacama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro_de_Atacama) and surrounding area offer one of the most spectacular combinations of archeology and awe-inspiring natural sights. San Pedro was the cradle of the [Atacameño](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atacame%C3%B1o) Culture. Villages with interesting cultural past are [Caspana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caspana), [Toconao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toconao), [Socaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socaire) and [Chiu Chiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiu_Chiu). Notable are also the pre-Hispanic fortresses of [Quitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quitor), [Lasana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lasana) and Turi. [Antofagasta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antofagasta) is the largest city in Norte Grande. [La Portada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Portada), a stunning [natural arch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_arch), is located a short drive from Antofagasta.

[Pan de Azúcar National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_de_Az%C3%BAcar_National_Park) is a park where sea and desert meet.

Also, [there more than a dozen of astronomical facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy_in_Chile), including optical observatories and radio observatories. Among others, the most important are: [Paranal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paranal_Observatory) ([VLT](151648.md)), at an altitude of 2635 meters above sea level, is the world's most advanced and powerful astronomical observatory.[^cite_note-27] the ALMA ([Atacama Large Millimeter Array](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atacama_Large_Millimeter_Array)), to date the largest astronomical project in the world;[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29] and [La Silla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Silla_Observatory),[^cite_note-30] all dependent of ESO ([European Southern Observatory](175184.md)).

In this area can be seen the [flowering desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flowering_desert), a phenomenon that occurs between September and November with normal range rainfall in the desert.[^cite_note-31] In the end of the Norte Grande, near [Putre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putre), is the [Lake Chungará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chungar%C3%A1_Lake), one of the highest lakes in the world at 4500 [m](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metre) above sea level,[^cite_note-32] and [Parinacota volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parinacota_\(volcano\)). [San Pedro de Atacama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro_de_Atacama) is frequently visited by foreign tourists who go to appreciate the architecture of the town, the highland lakes, and the [Moon Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valle_de_la_Luna_\(Chile\)), so named because its landscape resembles the moon.

The coastal resorts of the Norte Grande, like [Arica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arica), [Iquique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iquique), [Antofagasta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antofagasta), see an increase in national tourism during the summer months.

## Norte Chico

See also: [Norte Chico, Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norte_Chico,_Chile)

The Andes of [Norte Chico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norte_Chico,_Chile) are home to many of the highest volcanoes in the world, the highlight being the [Ojos del Salado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ojos_del_Salado), the highest active [volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcano) in the world at 6,891.3 meters tall and the highest peak in Chile. It is also the second highest peak in the [Southern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Hemisphere) and [Western](73162.md) hemispheres.[^cite_note-33] Apart from Ojos del Salado and other volcanoes, attractions in this portion of the Andes include [Nevado Tres Cruces National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevado_Tres_Cruces_National_Park) and [Laguna Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_Verde_\(lake_of_Chile\)).

Norte Chico boasts some of the best beaches and exclusive coastal resorts in the country, such as [Bahía Inglesa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bah%C3%ADa_Inglesa) and those of [La Serena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Serena,_Chile), and [Coquimbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coquimbo). These also see an increase in national tourism during the summer months.

The clear skies of Norte Chico and its world-renowned infrastructure have made it ideal for astronomical tourism.

[Elqui Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elqui_Valley_\(wine_region\)) is a very popular destination. In January 2015, the travel section of *[The New York Times](30680.md)* said the [Elqui Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elqui_Valley_\(wine_region\)) as the 5th of the 52 places it had to visit in 2015.[^cite_note-34] Here, the dazzling green of the valley floor contrasts nicely with the barren brown slopes of the mountains that flank it.

Also found within Norte Chico is [El Tatio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Tatio) [Geyser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geyser) Field, the largest [geyser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geyser) fields in the [Southern Hemisphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Hemisphere) and the third largest in the world.[^cite_note-35]

The [syncretism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syncretism) between [Aymara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aymara_people) tradition and [Catholicism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholicism) has produced festivals and religious traditions, such as those dedicated to [Our Lady of Mount Carmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Mount_Carmel) in [La Tirana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Tirana) and to [Virgin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Andacollo) of [Andacollo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andacollo), and [carnivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnival). The [Baile chino](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baile%20chino), one of the manifestations of religious fervor, was declared [Intangible Cultural Heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intangible_Cultural_Heritage) by UNESCO in 2014.[^cite_note-36]

This area is home to the main ski resorts in Chile, which attract a significant number of tourists from the northern hemisphere because the reversed [alpine skiing](39576.md) season. They are Chapa Verde, [Portillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portillo,_Chile), [Valle Nevado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valle_Nevado) and [Termas de Chillán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Termas_de_Chill%C3%A1n). Furthermore, [Wine tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wine_tourism) is common in [Zona Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zona_Central,_Chile), and the Wine Routes of the [Casablanca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casablanca,_Chile), [Cachapoal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cachapoal_Valley) and [Colchagua Valley](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valle%20de%20Colchagua) valleys are some of the finest in Chile.

Apart from the national capital itself and its many attractions, the metropolitan region surrounding [Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago,_Chile) includes several attractions such as the [Cajón del Maipo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caj%C3%B3n_del_Maipo), [Pomaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomaire), and some of the oldest vineyards in the country, as well as various archaeological sites.[^cite_note-37]

Due to its proximity to the capital, the coast of the [Valparaíso Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valpara%C3%ADso_Region) has the largest number of tourists during the summer months, primarily on the central coast and in [Viña del Mar](299067.md). The latter city is considered the *tourist capital of Chile*[^cite_note-vvm-38] thanks to its thirteen [beaches](40630.md),[^cite_note-vvm-38] its various entertainment centers, [one of major casinos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vi%C3%B1a_del_Mar_Casino?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the country,[^cite_note-39] and the [International Song Festival of Viña del Mar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Song_Festival_of_Vi%C3%B1a_del_Mar), the largest and best-known festival of [Latin America](18524.md).[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42]

[Valparaíso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valpara%C3%ADso) is a wonderful, amphitheater-shaped city, whose historic quarter has been recognized as a [World Heritage Site](44940.md).

[Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago,_Chile), the capital and main urban center of Chile, home to many historic sites and heritage. Within the [Greater Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Santiago), there are 174 [heritage sites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Monuments_of_Chile) under the custody of [National Monuments Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Monuments_Council_\(Chile\)) (CMN),[^cite_note-43] among which are archaeological, architectural and historical monuments, in addition to typical or picturesque areas. Of these, 93 are located within the [commune of Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_\(commune\)), considered the *historic center* of the city[^cite_note-44] Although no Santiagan monument was even declared [World Heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Heritage) by [UNESCO](21786641.md), three have already been proposed by the Chilean government: the Incan sanctuary [cerro El Plomo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerro_El_Plomo), the [church and convent of San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Church,_Santiago_de_Chile) and [Palace of La Moneda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Moneda_Palace)[^cite_note-45] Meanwhile, in June 2012, the magazine *[National Geographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Geographic_\(magazine\))* listed the [Central Market of Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercado_Central_de_Santiago) as fifth out of the ten best in the world.[^cite_note-46] In January 2011, the travel section of *The New York Times* listed the city of Santiago as the first of the 41 places it had to visit that year,[^cite_note-47] while [TripAdvisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TripAdvisor) considered it the sixth best destination of [South America](26769.md) in 2012.[^cite_note-tacom-48]

The O'Higgins Region, south of Santiago, is known as "[Huaso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huaso) country" for its rural *[criollo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criollo_people)* culture.[^cite_note-49] [Pichilemu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pichilemu), in the O'Higgins Region, is reputed to have the best beaches for [Surfing in Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfing_in_Chile), if not the world.[^cite_note-50] Other tourist sites in the O'Higgins region include the historically significant city of [Rancagua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rancagua), and the [Rio de los Cipreses nature reserve.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%ADo_de_Los_Cipreses_National_Reserve)

Furthermore, in this area of the country are [El Teniente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Teniente), the most [underground mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underground_mine) of [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper) in the world,[^cite_note-51] and the [mining town of Sewell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sewell,_Chile), declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO in 2006.

Further south in the Maule and Bio Bio regions are the [Radal Siete Tazas National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radal_Siete_Tazas_National_Reserve) and [Laguna del Laja National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_del_Laja_National_Park).

## Zona Sur

See also: [Zona Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zona_Sur)

[Araucanía Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Araucan%C3%ADa_Region) is the heartland of the [Mapuche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapuche) people. This area exhibits an incipient development of Indigenous tourism, in different sectors. in the coastal area of Araucania Ethnological tourism is developed on the banks Mapuche [Budi Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budi_Lake).

The [Chilean Patagonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilean_Patagonia), the circuit of the [Seven Lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Lakes_\(Chile\)) and various national parks like [Conguillío](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conguill%C3%ADo_National_Park), [Vicente Pérez Rosales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_P%C3%A9rez_Rosales_National_Park), the oldest in Chile and most visited of the country in 2011;[^cite_note-52] [Puyehue National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puyehue_National_Park) between others.

The Andean sector of Araucania, highlight the great national parks, such as [Conguillio National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conguillio_National_Park) the forest of [araucarias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Araucarias), [Villarrica National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarrica_National_Park), [huerquehue National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huerquehue_National_Park), [Tolhuaca National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolhuaca_National_Park) besides the national reserves as [Alto Biobio National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alto_Biobio_National_Reserve), [Malalcahuello National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malalcahuello_National_Reserve), [Nalcas National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nalcas_National_Reserve), [Malleco National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malleco_National_Reserve), [China Muerta National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Muerta_National_Reserve).

The capital of Araucania is [Temuco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temuco), city of different service, hotels, tourist circuits, airport, markets, and the symbolic and historic sectors, the [Cerro Ñielol Natural Monument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerro_%C3%91ielol_Natural_Monument) is an icon of this city, and the most important tourist point.

[Valdivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valdivia,_Chile) is one of Chile's most beautiful cities, recognition received mainly because of its lush natural surrounding. South from the city lies the colonial [Valdivian Fort System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valdivian_Fort_System).

Also in this area of the country are the [Lake O'Higgins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O'Higgins/San_Mart%C3%ADn_Lake), the deepest lake of the [Americas](29833.md) and fifth in the world with 836 meters;[^cite_note-53] On the other hand, several studies have located the oldest [archaeological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archeology) remains of the current Chilean continental territory in [Monte Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Verde),[^cite_note-54] [Los Lagos Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Lagos_Region), about 14,800 [B.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Before_Christ),[^cite_note-55] at the end of [Upper Paleolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Paleolithic), making it the first known human settlement in the [Americas](29833.md).[^cite_note-56]

The Lake District is characterized by piedmont lakes crowned by snowcapped volcanoes, whose lower flanks are covered with lush forests of native trees. Lovely lake-resort towns and villages dot the lakesides, chiefly [Pucón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puc%C3%B3n), [Villarrica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarrica,_Chile), [Frutillar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frutillar) and [Puerto Varas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Varas). [Puerto Montt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Montt) is the tourist hub of the region; two of them, were considered among the best destinations in South America in 2012 by the specialized tourism site [TripAdvisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TripAdvisor).

The almost perfect cone of [Osorno Volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osorno_\(volcano\)) is an iconic landmark in the region, as is [Villarrica Volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarrica_\(volcano\)), which is one of the few volcanoes in the world that has an active [Lava lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lava_lake).

As an added bonus, this zone includes the granite domes of the [Cochamó Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocham%C3%B3_Valley).

[Zona Austral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zona_Austral) is a premier destination for [Ecotourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecotourism) and [Adventure travel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventure_travel). It features countless islands and islets, a maze of steep-sided fjords and channels, large glaciers and icefields and exuberant unspoilt temperate rainforests. Turbulent rivers descend from the Andes, chief of those being the [Futaleufú River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futaleuf%C3%BA_River), which is widely considered one of the world's most challenging rivers for [whitewater kayaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitewater_kayaking) and [rafting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafting). Other opportunities for outdoor recreation, include hiking, [cycling](5931.md), [sea kayaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_kayaking) and [flyfishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flyfishing).

[Chiloé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilo%C3%A9) is characterized by having a rolling green countryside, but is better known for its peculiar wooden architecture and for its culture, which is full of myths and traditions. Also is remarkable its temperate rainforests.

The scenic [Carretera Austral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carretera_Austral) crosses or is nearby to many of the most popular destinations in this portion of Chile. Those include [Pumalín Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumal%C3%ADn_Park), [Yelcho Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yelcho_Lake), [Queulat National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queulat_National_Park), [Cerro Castillo National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerro_Castillo_National_Reserve), [General Carrera Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Carrera_Lake) and [Caleta Tortel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caleta_Tortel).

[Laguna San Rafael National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_San_Rafael_National_Park) is the only park where visitors can contemplate at the same time the huge [glaciers](12463.md) of Patagonia and the rich diversity of plants of the [Valdivian Ecoregion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valdivian_Ecoregion).

[Torres del Paine](5268605.md) was selected as the [eighth wonder of the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eighth_wonder_of_the_world) in 2013;,[^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58] one of the most beautiful national parks in the world.

In the [ice fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_fields), the [Glacier Pío XI (or Brüggen)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Br%C3%BCggen_Glacier), the longest glacier in the southern hemisphere outside the [Antarctic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antarctic),[^cite_note-59] and one of the few advancing glaciers in the world; and [Puerto Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Williams), the southernmost city in the world[^cite_note-60][^cite_note-61]

The sprawling glaciers of the [Cordillera Darwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordillera_Darwin), which is contained within [Alberto de Agostini National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_de_Agostini_National_Park), are reachable only by boat. Other typical destinations are [Puerto Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Williams) and [Cape Horn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Horn).

## Chile insular

See also: [Easter Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easter_Island)



Easter Island-Rapa Nui paintings of Tangata Manu (Birdmen) Frigate Birds in the "cave of the maneaters"

In the middle of the Pacific Ocean, [Easter Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easter_Island), is world-renowned for its unique cultural and natural heritage. Traditionally called *Rapa Nui* and previously known as *Te pito o te henua* ("The navel of the world") and *Mata ki te rangi* ('eyes looking at the sky'), it is one of the main tourist destinations in Chile because of its natural landscapes and mysterious ancient culture of [Rapa Nui people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapa_Nui_people) that since time immemorial, developed completely isolated for centuries until it almost became extinct in the mid-19th century.[^cite_note-62][^cite_note-63] Its most notable feature is the over 1,000[^cite_note-64] enormous and mysterious statues known as [moai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moai), which have been shrouded in mystery since their discovery. To preserve these characteristics, the [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Chile) has designated, through the [National Forestry Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Forestry_Corporation) (CONAF), the island as [Rapa Nui National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapa_Nui_National_Park), while UNESCO declared this park as a World Heritage Site in 1995.[^cite_note-pdH-65] It is named Easter Island because it was discovered on Easter Sunday in 1722.

Among the activities that can be carried out in *Rapa Nui* are different excursions and tours, visiting the craft market, the [Father Sebastian Englert Anthropological Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Father_Sebastian_Englert_Anthropological_Museum), the ceremonial center Tahai, the quarries of Puna Pau and [Rano Raraku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rano_Raraku), visiting the different *[ahus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marae)* on the beaches of Anakena and Ovahe, seeing the archaeological site of [Orongo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orongo),[^cite_note-66] witnessing shows with music and island dances, and the annual festival *[Tapati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapati) Rapa Nui*, the main artistic and cultural activity of the island, where a series of ceremonies and traditions are performed.[^cite_note-67]

The [Juan Fernández archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Fern%C3%A1ndez_archipelago) is also well known for its jungles and [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemism) flora, formed by the islands [Robinson Crusoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robinson_Crusoe_Island), [Santa Clara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara_Island) and [Alexander Selkirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Selkirk_Island). The geological origin of these islands is volcanic and their subtropical weather is rainy. The activities that can be performed are varied, among them hiking, horseback riding, snorkeling, spearfishing, fishing, and observation of flora and fauna, which is mostly endemic. The Juan Fernández Islands are also known for being inhabited by [Alexander Selkirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Selkirk), the man who inspired the novel [Robinson Crusoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robinson_Crusoe). The islands were previously named Island Mas Afuera, and Island Mas a Tierra, but in 1966 the Chilean government renamed the Island Más Afuera to Alejandro Selkirk Island and Island Más a Tierra to Robinson Crusoe Island, in order to promote tourism. Incidentally, Selkirk never set foot on Alejandro Selkirk Island, only on Robinson Crusoe Island. The islands can be accessed by air and sea routes.
