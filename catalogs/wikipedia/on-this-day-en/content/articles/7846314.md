**Thusis** ([Italian](14708.md): *Tosana*, *[Romansh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romansh_language): Tusàn*) is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Switzerland) in the [Viamala Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viamala_Region) in the Swiss [canton](180752.md) of the [Grisons](169850.md). On 1 January 2018 the former municipality of [Mutten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutten) merged into the municipality of Thusis.

## History

Thusis is first mentioned in 1156 as *Tosana*.[^cite_note-HDS-2] The town was devastated by more than 10 fires[^cite_note-\:0-3] and after the one of 1845, it had to be rebuilt.[^cite_note-4]

## Geography

Thusis has an area, as of 2006, of 6.8 km<sup>2</sup> (2.6 sq mi). Of this area, 18% is used for agricultural purposes, while 58.2% is forested. Of the rest of the land, 15.2% is settled (buildings or roads) and the remainder (8.6%) is non-productive (rivers, glaciers or mountains).[^cite_note-SFSO-5] The municipality is the capital of the Thusis sub-district, of the Hinterrhein district, after 2017 it was part of the Viamala Region.[^cite_note-6] It is the center of the [Hinterrhein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinterrhein_\(river\)) valley and is located at the [confluence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confluence) of the Hinterrhein and Nolla rivers. Thusis is also at the end of the Viamala canyon. It consists of the village of Thusis and, since 1875, includes Übernolla.

## Demographics

Thusis has a population (as of 31 December 2020) of 9,453.[^cite_note-Stat2020_GR-7] As of 2008, 23.6% of the population was made up of foreign nationals. Over the last 10 years the population has grown at a rate of 0.7%.[^cite_note-SFSO-5] In the town, the [Swiss German dialect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_German) is predominant.[^cite_note-\:1-8] Only 5% speak the local [Romansh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romansh_language).[^cite_note-HDS-2] The germanization is assumed to have begun in the 15th century, with people from the southern parts of Germany and the [Wallis](198789.md).[^cite_note-\:1-8]

As of 2000, the gender distribution of the population was 50.4% male and 49.6% female.[^cite_note-GR_Numbers-9] The age distribution, as of 2000, in Thusis is; 304 people or 11.2% of the population are between 0 and 9 years old. 142 people or 5.2% are 10 to 14, and 141 people or 5.2% are 15 to 19. Of the adult population, 354 people or 13.0% of the population are between 20 and 29 years old. 460 people or 16.9% are 30 to 39, 402 people or 14.8% are 40 to 49, and 333 people or 12.3% are 50 to 59. The senior population distribution is 255 people or 9.4% of the population are between 60 and 69 years old, 195 people or 7.2% are 70 to 79, there are 104 people or 3.8% who are 80 to 89, and there are 27 people or 1.0% who are 90 to 99.[^cite_note-Population-10] Thusis has an unemployment rate of 1.65%. As of 2005, there were 15 people employed in the [primary economic sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_sector_of_the_economy) and about 5 businesses involved in this sector. 525 people are employed in the [secondary sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_sector_of_the_economy) and there are 36 businesses in this sector. 1,325 people are employed in the [tertiary sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tertiary_sector_of_the_economy), with 190 businesses in this sector.[^cite_note-SFSO-5]

## Politics

In the [2023 federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Swiss_federal_election) the most popular party was the [SVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_People's_Party) which received 28.8% of the vote. The next three most popular parties were the [SPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_of_Switzerland) (21.5%), [die Mitte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Centre_\(political_party\)) (19.9%) [FDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Democratic_Party_of_Switzerland) (17.2%).[^cite_note-11]

The Mayor is [Remi Crameri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remi_Crameri?action=edit\&redlink=1) from the party [die Mitte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Centre_\(political_party\)). He was elected in a by-election in April 2026.[^cite_note-12]

## Education

In Thusis about 63.5% of the population (between age 25–64) have completed either non-mandatory [upper secondary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Switzerland#Secondary) or additional higher education (either university or a *[Fachhochschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fachhochschule)*).[^cite_note-SFSO-5]

## Religion



Protestant church in Thusis

From the 2000 census, 1,085 or 39.9% are [Roman Catholic](606848.md), while 1,096 or 40.3% belonged to the [Swiss Reformed Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Reformed_Church). Of the rest of the population, there are 134 individuals (or about 4.93% of the population) who belong to the Orthodox Church, and there are 46 individuals (or about 1.69% of the population) who belong to another Christian church. There are less than 5 individuals who are Jewish, and 96 (or about 3.53% of the population) who are [Islamic](6037917.md). There are 64 individuals (or about 2.36% of the population) who belong to another church (not listed on the census), 101 (or about 3.72% of the population) belong to no church, are agnostic or [atheist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheism), and 95 individuals (or about 3.50% of the population) did not answer the question.[^cite_note-Population-10]

## Weather

Thusis has an average of 101.6 days of rain per year and on average receives 892 mm (35.1 in) of [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation_\(meteorology\)). The wettest month is August during which time Thusis receives an average of 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation. During this month there is precipitation for an average of 10.7 days. The month with the most days of precipitation is June, with an average of 10.7, but with only 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation. The driest month of the year is February with an average of 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation over 10.7 days.[^cite_note-MeteoSchweiz-13]

## Transportation



Thusis train station

Located a short distance to the southwest of [Chur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chur), Thusis is accessible using the [A13 Autobahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A13_\(Switzerland\)) (Junctions 20 and 21). The [Rhaetian Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhaetian_Railway) operates services to the [Thusis railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thusis_railway_station).
