**Sintiki** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Σιντική) is a municipality in the [Serres](1802297.md) regional unit of [Central Macedonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Macedonia), [Greece](12108.md). It covers an area of 1,103.431 km<sup>2</sup>[^cite_note-stat01-3] and has a population of 18,544 according to the 2021 census. The seat of the municipality is the town of [Sidirokastro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidirokastro).[^cite_note-Kallikratis-4]

## Etymology

Sintiki is named after [Sintike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sintice), an ancient region and later district of the [Kingdom of Macedon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Macedon) that was inhabited by the [Sintians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sintians), a [Thracian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thracians) or possibly [Pelasgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pelasgians) people.[^cite_note-5]

## Municipality

The municipality was formed after the administrative reform in 2010 ([Kallikratis plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kallikratis_plan)) from the merger of the former municipalities of [Kerkini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerkini), [Petritsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petritsi) and [Sidirokastro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidirokastro) and the rural municipalities of [Agkistro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agkistro), [Achladochori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achladochori,_Serres) and [Promachonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promachonas). The administrative seat of the municipality is [Sidirokastro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidirokastro).[^cite_note-Kallikratis-4] The former municipalities and rural municipalities have since formed the six municipal districts. The community is further subdivided into 3 city districts and 23 local communities.

## Province

The province of Sintiki ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Επαρχία Σιντικής) was one of the [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Greece) of the Serres Prefecture. Its territory corresponded with that of the current municipality Sintiki, and a small part of the municipality [Irakleia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irakleia,_Serres).[^cite_note-census91-6] It was abolished in 2006.
