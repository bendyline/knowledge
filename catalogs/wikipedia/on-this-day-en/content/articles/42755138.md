The **Shah gas field** is a [natural gas field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_gas_field) in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) that was discovered in 1967. It began production in 2014 and produces [natural gas](22131.md) and [condensates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_gas_condensate). The total [proven reserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proven_reserves) of the Shah gas field are around 17 trillion cubic feet (4.8×10<sup>11</sup> m<sup>3</sup>) and production is slated to be around 1 billion cubic feet/day (2.8×10<sup>7</sup> m<sup>3</sup>/d).[^cite_note-1]

The gas deposits consist of 23% H<sub>2</sub>S and 10% CO<sub>2</sub>, making it one of the most sour operational gas fields in the world.[^cite_note-2] [Sour gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sour_gas) is defined as gas containing a high proportion of hydrogen sulphide. This environmental pollutant is required to be removed either as solid sulphur for commercial sale, or concentrated sulphide solutions are extracted and re-injected underground.
