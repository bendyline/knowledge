The **Governor of Kursk Oblast** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Губернатор Курской области) is the [head of government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_\(Russia\)) of [Kursk Oblast](480916.md), a [federal subject](701438.md) of Russia.

The position was introduced in 1991 as *[Head of Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_Administration) of Kursk Oblast*. The Governor is elected by direct popular vote for a term of five years and can hold the position for two consecutive terms.
