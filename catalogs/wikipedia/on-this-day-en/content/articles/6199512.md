# Roger Lumbala

&#x20; **Roger Lumbala** (born 1958) is an [MP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament) in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md), representing the [Rally of Congolese Democrats and Nationalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rally_of_Congolese_Democrats_and_Nationalists).[^cite_note-1] He is a former rebel leader who was backed by [Uganda](31816.md) during the 1998–2002 [Congolese civil war](877492.md).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

He was arrested in Paris, and is suspected of torture and cannibalism.[^cite_note-3] He was indicted on 6 November 2023.[^cite_note-4] On 28 February 2024, the [Paris Court of Appeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Court_of_Appeal) confirmed the indictment of Roger Lumbala "for complicity in crimes against humanity."[^cite_note-5]

On 14 November 2025 he started a [hunger strike](222292.md) to protest his ongoing trial over atrocities committed during the [Second Congo War](877492.md) in DR Congo. Lumbala argued that the French court which judges him does not have legitimacy to try him.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

On 15 December 2025, Lumbala was found guilty of complicity in multiple [crimes](61099.md), such as torture, rape, summary executions, pillaging, etc. He was sentenced to a term of 30 years of imprisonment. This verdict marks the first time a national court has prosecuted someone for crimes committed during the [Second Congo War](877492.md).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
