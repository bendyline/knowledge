**Castel d'Azzano** is a *[comune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comune)* (municipality) in the [Province of Verona](990199.md) in the [Italian](14532.md) region [Veneto](43780.md), located about 110 km (68 mi) west of [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice) and about 6 kilometres (4 miles) southwest of [Verona](263991.md). As of 1 September 2011, it had a population of 11.865 and an area of 10.0 square kilometres (3.9 sq mi).[^cite_note-istat-3]

The municipality of Castel d'Azzano contains the *[frazioni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazione)* (subdivisions, mainly villages and hamlets) La Rizza, San Martino, and Forette.

Castel d'Azzano borders the following municipalities: [Buttapietra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buttapietra), [Verona](263991.md), [Vigasio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vigasio), and [Villafranca di Verona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villafranca_di_Verona).

## History

On 14 October 2025, the deliberate explosion of the Ramponi family farmhouse killed three [Carabinieri](45173.md) officers and injured 25 others people, including the man who triggered the gas explosion. The municipality proclaimed five days of mourning,[^cite_note-4] the Veneto Region proclaimed a regional mourning day, and a national mourning day was instituted with a state funeral for the three Carabinieri.[^cite_note-5] There also was a minute of silence held in all Italian schools on Friday 17 October.
