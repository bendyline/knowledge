The **Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan** (**MoFA**)[^cite_note-1] ([Dari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dari_language): وزارت خارجه افغانستان, [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): د افغانستان د بهرنیو چارو وزارت) is the [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Afghanistan) [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) responsible for managing the [foreign relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Afghanistan) of [Afghanistan](737.md).

On 11 January 2023, the [ministry was bombed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_of_Afghanistan_bombing).[^cite_note-2] It was again bombed on 27 March 2023.[^cite_note-3]
