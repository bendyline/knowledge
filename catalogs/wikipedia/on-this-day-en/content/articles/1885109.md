The **prime minister of Somalia** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Ra'iisul wasaaraha Soomaaliya*) is the [head of government](37570.md) of [Somalia](27358.md). There have been 22 official prime ministers since the office was created in 1956. The first prime minister was [Abdullahi Issa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullahi_Issa), who served prior to independence in the [Trust Territory of Somaliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trust_Territory_of_Somaliland). The current prime minister of the [Federal Republic of Somalia](36817379.md) is [Hamza Abdi Barre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamza_Abdi_Barre),[^cite_note-1] approved by the House of the People on 25 June 2022.[^cite_note-Barre-2]

Somali prime ministers are given a new number even when they are reappointed. For example, former prime minister Omar Sharmarke wrote on his Twitter page, "13th and 18th Prime Minister of Somalia."[^cite_note-4] The seat had long been vacant since Umar Ghalib stepped down as prime minister, but Ali Khalif Galaydh became the eighth in August 2000.[^cite_note-go20201010-5] The acting prime minister is not numbered. For example, Osman Jama Ali was acting prime minister between Ali Khalif Galaydh, the eighth, and Hassan Abshir Farah, the ninth,[^cite_note-go20201010-5] he didn't get a number. In June 2022, Hamza Abdi Barre succeeded the 20th[^cite_note-go0917-6] Mohamed Hussein Roble as the 21st Prime Minister.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
