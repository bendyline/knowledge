**Geoje** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 거제; Korean pronunciation: [\[kʌ.dʑe\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Korean)) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_South_Korea) located in [South Gyeongsang Province](319775.md), South Korea. It is located near [Busan](68115.md). [Hanwha Ocean](16742825.md) in Okpo and [Samsung Heavy Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung_Heavy_Industries) (SHI) in Gohyeon are both located on Geoje Island. The city also offers a wide range of tourist sights. The city is made up of a number of islands, of which by far the largest is [Geoje Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoje_Island). There are multiple [dong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dong_\(neighbourhood\)) in the city: [Jangpyeongdong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jangpyeongdong), [Okpo-dong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okpo-dong) and [Gohyeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gohyeon).

## Etymology

**Geoje** is [Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language) for "great rescue", from the syllables *geo* ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 거; [Hanja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanja): 巨) meaning great, and *je* (제; 濟) meaning rescue.

## History

Geoje has a history stretching back thousands of years. Various artifacts dating back to the Neolithic era have been found at archaeological digs on Naedo, Sandaldo, and Isudo. While no written history can be found from this era, the digs show evidence of numerous small establishments along the coasts. The first written mention of Geoje appears as, Geoje being one of two main divisions of the Dokro kingdom, a member of the Byeonhan confederacy (circa 0-300 AD). The Byeonhan confederacy slowly gave way to the Gaya confederacy(42-562 AD). The Gaya eventually submitted to the Kingdom of Silla.

In 757, [Gyeongdeok of Silla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyeongdeok_of_Silla) classified the island as "Geoje-gun" "거제[군](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_South_Korea)" (Geoje [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_South_Korea)). Modern Geoje is classified as a "si" ([시](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_South_Korea)) ([city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_South_Korea)). In 1170, [Uijong of Goryeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uijong_of_Goryeo) secretly escaped to Geoje when [Chŏng Chung-bu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C5%8Fng_Chung-bu) raised a rebellion against him, instating his brother, [Myeongjong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myeongjong_of_Goryeo) as king. Geoje served as a strategic location in preparation of the naval forces for the [Gihae Eastern Expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gihae_Eastern_Expedition) (기해동정) of [Tsushima Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsushima_Island) in 1419 by the Korean general [Yi Chongmu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yi_Chongmu). At the time of the [Seven-Year War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_invasions_of_Korea_\(1592%E2%80%931598\)) in 1592, admiral [Yi Sun-sin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yi_Sun-sin) gained a great naval victory against Japanese invaders near the town of Okpo in Geoje. Since then. Yi was referred to by his noble title, "Chung Mu Gong," which roughly translates into English as "Earner of Great National Respect."

In the [Korean War](16772.md) (1950–53), the military government of [General Douglas MacArthur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Douglas_MacArthur) used the island as a place for the settlement for 170,000 POWs, and a [Prisoner of war camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoje_POW_camp) measuring at 11.8 square kilometres (4.6 mi<sup>2</sup>) was established during these years. The prison was rife with human rights abuses, including summary execution and the widespread use of torture against prisoners by Canadian and American troops. British journalist [Alan Winnington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Winnington) and Australian journalist [Wilfred Burchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilfred_Burchett) conducted extensive research to document these human rights abuses, and jointly published their findings in 1952.[^cite_note-2] After the war, a portion (23,000 m<sup>2</sup>) was converted into a tourist attraction.[^cite_note-3]

During the last few decades Geoje has emerged as leader in the shipbuilding industries. Due to the continued industry, Geoje largely escaped the negative impacts of the Asian market crash of 1997.

## Demographics

According to the whitebook published by Geoje City in May 2014, Geoje had a population of 243,736, 12,240 of which are foreigners (non-Koreans). A significant portion of the population depends on shipbuilding to support their livelihood, and this can be evidenced by the fact that nearly 33% of workers are employed in the shipbuilding industry.

The population of Geoje Island is 232,807 as of October 2024.

## Tourism

Geoje offers a variety of attractions, including Haegeumgang island, [Oedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oedo), a marine western-style botanical garden in Hallyeo-Haesang National Park, built by Lee Chang-ho and his wife when they settled on the island in 1969. Visitors visit by ferry boat from the harbor neighboring the islands.

Tourists also visit Jisim-do for trekking. Jisim is known for its Camellia vegetation. However, the Maemi typhoon in 2003 severely affected the island's plant life. Another popular tourist attraction at Haegeumgang-do (located southeast of Geoje) is the Sipja Donggul Cave. Buddhist and Shamanist sculptures can be found in the cave.

Geoje is the second largest island in Korea, so discovering these locations is most easily done using a tourist map.

### Historic and cultural Sites

#### Geoje POW Camp

Geoje POW Camp (거제도포로수용소) was a prisoner of war detention camp for captured North Koreans established during the [Korean War](16772.md) from (1951). It was the site of a prison riot on May 8, 1952, which is the topic of Chinese-American writer [Ha Jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ha_Jin)'s fictional *War Trash*.[^cite_note-4]

#### Gohyeon Castle



Geoje Gohyeon Castle

Gohyeon Castle is named the 46th national treasure of [South Gyeongsang Province](319775.md). Gohyeon Castle (고현성) and is located in Gohyeondong, Geoje. It was established during the [Joseon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseon) period in 1451.

In the famous [Joseon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseon) era geography book, [Donggukyeojiseungram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donggukyeojiseungram) (동국여지승람), it was recorded that the Gohyeon castle had a circumference of 3,038 Cheok (척) (equivalent to about 9.2 km or 5.7 mi), and had a height of 13 Cheok (3.93 m (12.9 ft)). It was considered the largest castle in the South Sea.[^cite_note-5]

#### Geoje Township Government Office



Geoje Government Office

The Geoje Township Government Office is regarded as Korea's 84th cultural treasure. During the time of the Japanese invasions, the office was established in 1470 to administer all military and shipbuilding plans.

#### Oksangeum Fortress Area



Oksangeum Fortress

The Oksangeum Fortress Area (옥산금성지) was an unsanctioned fortress built on the Sujeong peak to defend against Japanese invasion. Due to the strain it placed on the people during the building, the magistrate responsible was dismissed. Oksangeum Fortress is regarded as the South Gyeongsang Province's 10th cultural treasure.

#### Pyewang Fortress



Pyewangseong in Dundeok is the oldest fortress in Geoje.

Pyewang Fortress (폐왕성) is the oldest surviving fortress in Geoje. First built during the [Silla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silla) Dynasty, Pyewang fortress later served the fortress refuge for the King [Uijong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uijong_of_Goryeo) the 18th king of the [Goryeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goryeo) dynasty, while escaping from a military coup. Pyewang fortress is located in Dundeok-myeon. Pyewang fortress is regarded as the South Gyeongsang Province's 11th cultural treasure, and National Historical Site no. 509.

#### Okpo Great Victory Park

Okpo Great Victory Park is located in Okpo2Dong within Geoje. It commemorates the great victory over the invading Japanese navy by the honored general [Yi Sun-sin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yi_Sunshin) on May 7, 1592.[^cite_note-6]

#### Blue Horse (Poet) Museum

The Blue Horse ([Cheongma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yu_Chi-hwan), 청마) is a cultural exhibition and preserved birthplace museum of the poet "Blue Horse". It is located in Bangha village, Dundeokmyeon.

#### Jisimdo

Jisimdo (지심도) is an island of the east coast of Geoje. It is visited primarily for its vast amount of Camellia vegetation. Although damaged by Maemi typhoon, the camellias have mostly returned. It is also a site of a Japanese military installation, from the occupation of the early 20th century.[^cite_note-7]

### Museums



Geoje Themepark Museum

#### Haegeumgang Theme Museum

[Haegeumgang Theme Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haegeumgang_Theme_Museum) (해금강테마박물관) is a museum of travel during the 1950s to the 1970s. It is located in Galgot village, Nambumyeon (거제시 남부면 갈곶리).

#### Geoje Shipbuilding Marine Cultural Center

The Geoje Shipbuilding Marine Cultural Center is an independently established combination of a Fishing Village Folk Museum and Shipbuilding Theme park museum located in Jisepo village, Ilunmyeon (지세포리, 일운면).

The Fishing Village Folk Museum covers two floors, including 4 exhibits: "The living sea/Traditions of the sea" and "Ocean experience/Renaissance of the sea".

Shipbuilding Theme park museum also covers two floors, including 5 exhibits: "Children's Shipbuilding yard/Dock 1" The history of sailing vessels/Dock 2 Shipbuilding technology/Dock 3 The future of the oceans/ Movie theatre

#### Geoje Museum

Geoje Museum (거제박물관) is located in Okpo. It features local artifacts used over the centuries by the people of Geoje.

### Natural Attractions

#### Hill of Wind



Hill of Wind overlooking South Sea

The Hill of Wind (바람의 언덕) is located in Galgot village, Nambumyeon (거제시 남부면 갈곶리). It overlooks [South Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Sea_\(Korea\)) and there is a strong wind. A windmill is set on top of hill. It became famous after 2 Days & 1 Night, where one of the most popular Korean TV shows was filmed.[^cite_note-8]

#### Haegeumgang



Haegeumgang (해금강)

Haegeumgang (해금강) is named for resemblance of the Haegeum mountain in North Korea. It is a primary tourist destinations in all of Geoje.[^cite_note-9]

#### Oedo and Naedo



Oedo Island (외도)

Oedo (외도)[^cite_note-10] and Naedo (내도)[^cite_note-11] are two islands located in the southeast of Geoje island and of the two, Oedo hosts the second most visited attraction in all of Geoje, the expansive botanical gardens of Botania.

#### Sanbangsan Botanical Garden

Sanbangsan Botanical Garden (산방산비원) is a botanical garden in Sanbang-ri. This garden has many flower and tree varieties. Due to its remote location, it is much less busy than other botanical gardens.[^cite_note-12]

#### Mundong Waterfall

Mundong Waterfall (문동폭포) is a small waterfall located above the Mundong Reservoir. It features trails and picnic areas at this location.[^cite_note-13]

#### Gonggoji

Gonggoji (공곶이) is a mountain side garden located opposite Naedo island. It features Camellia trees, and daffodil fields. Due to there being no road access, you must walk to get to this location.[^cite_note-14]

Yugyo Fishing port

## Geography

Geoje covers an area of 401.63 square kilometres (155.07 sq mi), encompassing Geoje-do (383.44 square kilometres (148.05 sq mi)) and 60 other small islets, of which 10 are inhabited.[^cite_note-19] The main island is joined to land by two bridges from nearby [Tongyeong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tongyeong). It is also connected via a series of bridges and underwater tunnels to [Gadeokdo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gadeokdo), then [Busan](68115.md), named the [Busan–Geoje Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Busan%E2%80%93Geoje_Bridge). A proposal has been made to connect Geoje to Masan by another set of bridges and tunnels named Yi Sun-sin Great Bridge (이순신대교).

### Climate

Geoje experiences a [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Cfa/Cwa*), and temperatures reaching below zero degrees Celsius in winter are frequent during December to February. However, the island is kept relatively warm by the influence of warm ocean currents.

[Typhoon Hinnamnor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Hinnamnor) made landfall over the city. The eye centre directly passed over the city as a category 2 at 5\:50 AM on September 6, 2022, leaving severe destruction around, including flooding.

## Produce

Due to Geoje's coastal sub-tropical location, the area grows and harvests several different crops, some of which are uncommon in most of Korea. Some of these crops include yuja, pineapples, and mushrooms.

Being an island also provides an abundance of seafood that is collected locally.
