**Volgograd Oblast**[^cite_note-8] is a [federal subject](701438.md) ([oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblast)) of [Russia](25391.md), located in the [Lower Volga region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volga_region) of [Southern Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Russia). Its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) is [Volgograd](32585.md). The population of the oblast was 2,500,781 in the [2021 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census).&#x20;

Formerly known as Stalingrad Oblast, it was given its present name in 1961, when the city of Stalingrad was renamed Volgograd as part of the process of [de-Stalinization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De-Stalinization). Volgograd Oblast borders [Rostov Oblast](478744.md) in the southwest, [Voronezh Oblast](473401.md) in the northwest, [Saratov Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratov_Oblast) in the north, [Astrakhan Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrakhan_Oblast) and the [Republic of Kalmykia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Kalmykia) in the southeast, and has an [international border with Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan%E2%80%93Russia_border) in the east. The two main rivers in [European Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Russia), the [Don](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_River_\(Russia\)) and the [Volga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volga_River), run through the oblast and are connected by the [Volga–Don Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volga%E2%80%93Don_Canal). Volgograd Oblast's strategic waterways have made it a popular route for [shipping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shipping) and for the generation of [hydroelectricity](381399.md).

Volgograd Oblast was the primary site of the [Battle of Stalingrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Stalingrad) during [World War II](32927.md), regarded as the single [bloodiest battle in the history of warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_battles_by_casualties).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-\:4-10][^cite_note-11]

## Geography

- Borders length: 2,221.9 kilometers (1,380.6 mi)

Volgograd Oblast borders [Saratov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratov_Oblast), [Rostov](478744.md), [Astrakhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrakhan_Oblast), and [Voronezh Oblasts](473401.md), as well as the [Russian republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republics_of_Russia) of [Kalmykia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalmykia) and [Kazakhstan](16642.md). Most of Volgograd Oblast is located in the [Pontic–Caspian steppe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontic%E2%80%93Caspian_steppe). The [Yergeni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yergeni) hills are located to the southeast. Forests cover 4% of the territory. The major rivers are:

- The [Volga River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volga_River)

- The [Don River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_River_\(Russia\))

- The [Medveditsa River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medveditsa_River_\(Don_basin\))

- The [Khopyor River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khopyor_River)

## History

Since the [Middle Ages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Ages), the territory was ruled by [Khazars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khazars), [Cumania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumania), the [Golden Horde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Horde) and Russia.

Stalingrad Oblast (Сталинградская область) was established on December 5, 1936 on the territory of former [Stalingrad Krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stalingrad_Krai?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-ATSBook-1] It was the scene of the [Battle of Stalingrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Stalingrad) during [World War II](32927.md) in 1942–1943. The oblast was given its present name on November 10, 1961.[^cite_note-ATSBook-1]

## Politics



Building of the Oblast Duma and Oblast Government

During the [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) period, three people exercised oblast-level authority:

1. The first secretary of the Stalingrad Committee of the Communist Party (who in reality had the most power)

2. The chairman of the Oblast Soviet (legislative power)

3. The chairman of the Oblast Executive Committee (executive power)

In 1991 the CPSU lost *de facto* power, and the head of the oblast administration, and eventually the governor, was appointed and elected alongside the [regional parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_parliaments_of_Russia).

The Charter of Volgograd Oblast provides the fundamental law of the region. The Legislative Assembly of Volgograd Oblast is the province's standing legislative (representative) body. The Legislative Assembly exercises its authority by passing laws, resolutions, and other legal acts and by supervising the implementation and observance of the laws and other legal acts passed by it. The highest executive body, the oblast government, includes territorial executive bodies such as district administrations, committees, and commissions that facilitate development and run the day-to-day matters of the province. The oblast administration supports the activities of the governor, who is the highest official and acts as guarantor of the observance of the Oblast Charter in accordance with the [Constitution of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Russia).

According to a 2012 survey,[^cite_note-2012ArenaAtlas-20] 54.5% of the population of Volgograd Oblast adheres to the [Russian Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Orthodox_Church), 4% are [unaffiliated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nondenominational_Christianity) generic [Christians](18337522.md), 2% are Eastern Orthodox Christian believers who don't belong to any church or are members of non-Russian [Eastern Orthodox churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_church), and 3% are [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim). ## Government

Governor of Volgograd Oblast is Андрей Бочаров (since 2014)

Both the flag and the coat of arms of Volgograd Oblast include an image of *[The Motherland Calls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Motherland_Calls)*, an 85-meter-tall (279 ft) statue located in [Volgograd](32585.md).

## Economy

See also: [Category\:Companies based in Volgograd Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category\:Companies_based_in_Volgograd_Oblast)

Primary branches of economics are agriculture, food production, [heavy industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_industry), gas and petroleum refining. The [Volga Hydroelectric Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volga_Hydroelectric_Station) operates on the Volga River.[^cite_note-22]

The largest companies in the region include [Volzhsky Pipe Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volzhsky_Pipe_Plant?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Volgogradenergosbyt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volgogradenergosbyt?action=edit\&redlink=1) (a local electric power distribution company), [OJSC Kaustik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OJSC_Kaustik?action=edit\&redlink=1) (caustic soda manufacturer), [Volzhsky Orgsintez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volzhsky_Orgsintez?action=edit\&redlink=1) (a chemical plant).[^cite_note-regioncompanies-23]
