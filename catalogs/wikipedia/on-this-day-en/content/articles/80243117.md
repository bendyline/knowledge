# List of aviation shootdowns and accidents during the Twelve-Day War

&#x20; This is a **list of aviation shootdowns and accidents during the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War)** based on visual evidence or official self-admision from involved parties. It includes proven [helicopters](8286923.md), [fixed-wing aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixed-wing_aircraft) and military [drones](337755.md) (UCAVs).

## Timeline of aviation shootdowns and accidents

See also: [List of attacks during the Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_attacks_during_the_Twelve-Day_War)

- Israeli Air Force Roundel 14 June 2025 - The wreckage of an Israeli [IAI Eitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Eitan) drone was recorded in [Buin Zahra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buin_Zahra), western Iran.

  [^cite_note-1]

- Iranian Air Force Roundel 15 June 2025 - An [Iranian Air Force](412792.md) (IRIAF) [Boeing 707](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_707) tanker aircraft was destroyed on the ground at [Mashhad Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashhad_Shahid_Hasheminejad_International_Airport) in northeast Iran.

  [^cite_note-2]

  [^cite_note-3]

  [^cite_note-4]

  [^cite_note-5]

- Iranian Air Force Roundel **(2)** 16 June 2025 - Two non-operational Iranian Air Force [Grumman F-14 Tomcats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_F-14_Tomcat) were destroyed on the ground at [Mehrabad International Airport](2025041.md) in [Tehran](57654.md) by Israeli strikes.

  [^cite_note-TWZTomcats-6]

- Iranian Army Aviation Roundel **(8)** 18 June 2025 - Eight [Iranian Army Aviation](45381390.md) [Bell AH-1 SuperCobra](574557.md) helicopters were destroyed on the ground at a military airbase in [Kermanshah](1489244.md), western Iran.

  [^cite_note-7]

  [^cite_note-8]

- Israeli Air Force Roundel 18 June 2025 - An Israeli [Elbit Hermes 900](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Hermes_900) drone, serial *997*, was shot down by a surface-to-air missile in [Isfahan](223149.md), central Iran.

  [^cite_note-hermesdowned-9]

  [^cite_note-10]

  [^cite_note-11]

- Israeli Air Force Roundel **(2)** 19 June 2025 - Two Israeli drones; an [IAI Eitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Eitan) and an [IAI Heron](1141476.md) were shot down in western Iran including one in [Lorestan province](433072.md) by Iranian surface-to-air defenses, the interception was recorded in IR mode. The remains of the Heron-1 (Machatz), serial number *298,* was found at the [Iraq-Iran border](10282111.md).

  [^cite_note-12]

- Iranian Air Force Roundel **(3)** 21 June 2025 - The IDF published footage of the destruction of three Iranian Air Force [Grumman F-14 Tomcats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_F-14_Tomcat) in central Iran.

  [^cite_note-13]

- Iranian Air Force Roundel 21 June 2025 - An [Iranian Red Crescent Society](20826914.md) Air Rescue [Mil Mi-17](1493374.md) helicopter was damaged by an Israeli strike.

  [^cite_note-14]

- Iranian Air Force Roundel 22 June 2025 - The IDF published footage of the destruction of an Iranian Air Force [Northrop F-5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_F-5) at [Dezful Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dezful_Airport) in [Khuzestan province](448079.md).

  [^cite_note-15]

- Israeli Air Force Roundel 23 June 2025 - An Israeli [Elbit Hermes 900](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Hermes_900) drone was shot down near [Khorramabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khorramabad), western Iran. Israeli officials acknowledged the loss.

  [^cite_note-16]

  [^cite_note-17]

- Israeli Air Force Roundel 23 June 2025 - An Israeli [Elbit Hermes 900](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbit_Hermes_900) drone was shot down in [Arak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arak,_Iran), [Markazi Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markazi_province), Iran. Iranian media showed the wreckage of the drone with the serial number *939*.

  [^cite_note-18]

  [^cite_note-19]

- Israeli Air Force Roundel 26 June 2025 - The wreckage of an Israeli [IAI Eitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Eitan) or [IAI Heron](1141476.md) drone, serial number *248*, was discovered and recorded by locals in Khorramabad, Lorestan Province.

  [^cite_note-20]

  [^cite_note-21]
