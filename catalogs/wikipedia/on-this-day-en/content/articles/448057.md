**Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari Province** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): استان چهارمحال و بختیاری)[^cite_note-6] is one of the 31 [provinces of Iran](448160.md). Its capital is the city of [Shahr-e Kord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahr-e_Kord).[^cite_note-Chaharmahal_and_Bakhtiari_Province_Structure-7] The province lies in the southwest of the country, with an area of 16,332 square kilometers.

The province was classified as part of [Region 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Iran) upon the division of the provinces into 5 regions solely for coordination and development purposes on June 22, 2014.[^cite_note-hamshahrionline.ir-3]

Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari Province lies in the heart of the [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains), a mountain range that stretches across north-western [Iran](14653.md) and forms a natural barrier between Iran and [Iraq](7515928.md).[^cite_note-8]

## Demographics

### Languages

[Bakhtiāri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakhtiari_dialect), which belongs to the [Luri language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luri_language) of the [Iranian language family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_languages), is the province's main language. Bakhtiāri is primarily spoken in the valleys of the higher areas in the western half of the province. It is also spoken in the lower areas around [Lordegān](3739011.md) in the south, and by speakers who have moved into the cities in the north-east.[^cite_note-\:0-9]



Linguistic map of Chahar Mahal va Bakhtiari province

In the north-east quarter of the province, people in most cities and villages speak either [Chārmahāli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charmahali_Persian?action=edit\&redlink=1) (also in the Southwestern branch of Iranian) or [Chaharmahali Turkic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaharmahali_Turkic). Chārmahāli is transitional between [Bakhtiari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakhtiari_dialect) and [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) varieties of [Isfahan province](448061.md), but more similar to the latter. Chārmahāli varieties spoken in cities include Dehkordi (in [Shahr-e Kord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahr-e_Kord)), Ghafarrokhi (in [Farrokh Shahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farrokh_Shahr)), Heysheguni (in [Hafshejān](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafshejan)), and Borujeni (in [Borujen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borujen)). There are also many other varieties of Chārmahāli spoken in rural areas.

Most types of Turkic spoken in this province are similar to [Qashqa'i](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qashqai_language) of [Fars province](448049.md), but they are transitional to the [Āzarbāyjāni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language) (Azerbaijani) language of north-west [Iran](14653.md). The Chārmahāli and [Turkic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaharmahali_Turkic) language areas overlap with one another, and in the foothills of the [Zagros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains) and in the larger cities, they intermingle with Bakhtiāri as well.[^cite_note-\:0-9]

[Tehrani-type Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehrani_accent) is now being taught by parents to children as a first language in some parts of the province, with the highest concentrations in the cities.[^cite_note-\:0-9] This statistic is based on the population statistics of 2013.

Written descriptions of some of the Bakhtiāri varieties in the province.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] and a lexicon of the Bakhtiāri language[^cite_note-13] have also been published.

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the province's population was 843,784 in 194,171 households.[^cite_note-2006_Chaharmahal_and_Bakhtiari_Province-14] The following census in 2011 counted 895,263 people in 234,416 households.[^cite_note-2011_Chaharmahal_and_Bakhtiari_Province-15] The 2016 census measured the population of the province as 947,763 in 270,434 households.[^cite_note-2016_Chaharmahal_and_Bakhtiari_Province-4]

## Economy

The province is mainly active in the agriculture sector. Most of the industrial sector is clustered around the center of the province.

## Natural attractions

Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari province is the source and birthplace of many springs, rivers and waterfalls that supply the water of the two great and vital rivers of Zayanderud and Karoon and cause the development of many cities in the country. Natural attractions include [Zayanderud river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zayanderud), Zamankhan bridge, [Kouhrang 1 Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kouhrang_1_Dam), Dime spring, Sheik Ali Khan waterfall, Chama Ice cave, Darkesh varkesh canyon, [Rostam Abad Sardab spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostamabad,_Chaharmahal_and_Bakhtiari), [Do Polan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Do_Polan), Karoon-4 dam, Darreh Esgh (love valley), [Atashgah waterfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atashgah,_Chaharmahal_and_Bakhtiari), [Choghakhor Wetland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choghakhor_Wetland), Pire Ghar cave, Siasard spring, Helen protected area, Barm spring, Sendegan spring, Tang Sayyad national Park, Parvaz national park, Sarab cave, [Gandoman wetland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandoman), [Zard lime waterfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zardlimeh), Kordikon waterfall.[^cite_note-26]
