# Kivu

For other uses, see [Kivu (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kivu_\(disambiguation\)).



Map of eastern Democratic Republic of Congo



[North Kivu](446958.md) and [South Kivu](446959.md) ("the Kivus")

**Kivu** is the name for a large region in the eastern [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md) that borders [Lake Kivu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Kivu). It was a *Région* (read 'province') of the country under the rule of [Mobutu Sese Seko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobutu_Sese_Seko) from 1966 to 1988. As an official *Région* in 1986 it was divided into the three "Sub-Regions" (*Sous-Régions* in French) of: [Nord-Kivu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nord-Kivu), [Sud-Kivu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sud-Kivu) and [Maniema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maniema),[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] Those three became the [current provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) in the reorganization of 1988.[^cite_note-3] The capital of the Kivu Region was in [Bukavu](448570.md), and the capitals of the three Sub-Regions were in [Goma](1210483.md), [Uvira](2774222.md) and [Kindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kindu).

## History

Kivu has been repeatedly subjected to major conflicts since the early 20th century. Under [Belgian colonial rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgian_Congo), it was the site of several religious revolts such as the [1944 Kivu uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1944_Kivu_uprising).[^cite_note-4] Following independence, it was a battleground of the [Simba rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simba_rebellion), [First Congo War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Congo_War), and [Second Congo War](877492.md), and has been the site of an [ongoing military conflict since the early 2000s](14687290.md). In 2025 substantial parts of the area came under the control of the [March 23 Movement](35878650.md).&#x20;

From August 2018 to June 2020, an [Ebola epidemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kivu_Ebola_epidemic) affected the region.

## Geography

Kivu is also the name for the entire region surrounding [Lake Kivu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Kivu), including the portions in [Rwanda](25645.md) which contain the vast majority of the lake area's population (the contiguous towns of [Goma](1210483.md) in the [Democratic Republic of Congo](76762.md) and [Gisenyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gisenyi) in [Rwanda](25645.md), with a combined population approaching 1,000,000, form the largest urbanised area in the [Lake Kivu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Kivu) area).[^cite_note-5] The area is characterized by lush vegetation and an extended growing season due in part to its high elevation (1500 m or 4900 ft at the lakeshore) and the volcanic nature of its soil. The Kivu region represents the high point of the [East African Rift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_African_Rift) Valley.

The lake itself contains a massive amount of [carbon dioxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_dioxide) in its depths, and there is some concern that tectonic activity ([rifting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rift)) and/or [volcanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcano) activity might cause a [limnic eruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limnic_eruption), a sudden release of this captured carbon dioxide. If this were to happen it would devastate the population around the lake; however, the likelihood of this occurring is in dispute.
