**Ganderkesee** ([Northern Low Saxon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Low_Saxon): *Gannerseer*) is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Germany) in [Oldenburg district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oldenburg_\(district\)), in [Lower Saxony](18435.md), [Germany](11867.md).

## Geography

Ganderkesee is located on the northern edge of a nature park called "Wildeshauser Geest". The northernmost part of the community is in the glacial valley of the [Weser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weser). The sandy, higher and hilly terrain of Geest joins south.

The municipality Ganderkesee bordered to the east by the city of [Delmenhorst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delmenhorst), in the north of the communities [Lemwerder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemwerder) and [Berne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berne,_Germany) in the district [Wesermarsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesermarsch) and to the municipality [Hude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hude,_Lower_Saxony), to the west of the town [Hatten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatten) and on the south by the municipalities [Dötlingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%B6tlingen) and [Harpstedt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harpstedt).

### Divisions

Ganderkesee includes 25 hamlets with about 31,200 residents: Ganderkesee, Almsloh, Bergedorf, Bookholzberg, Bookhorn, Bürstel, Elmeloh, Falkenburg, Grüppenbühren, Habbrügge, Havekost, Heide, Hengsterholz, Hohenböken, Holzkamp, Hoyerswege, Hoykenkamp, Immer, Neuenlande, Rethorn, Schierbrok, Schlutter, Schönemoor, Steinkimmen and Stenum.

## History

Ganderkesee was mentioned in 860 AD for the first time under the name of "Gandrikesarde".
