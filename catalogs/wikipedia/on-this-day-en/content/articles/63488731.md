**Aziz Nasirzadeh** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): عزیز نصیرزاده;‎ 25 May 1964 – 28 February 2026) was an Iranian military officer who served as the [Minister of Defence of Iran](18379483.md) from 2024 to 2026. He previously served as the Deputy of Chief of Staff for the [Iranian Armed Forces](14305018.md) from September 2021 to August 2024, and was the [commander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander) of the [Iranian Air Force](412792.md) (IRIAF) from August 2018 to September 2021, prior to that position.[^cite_note-1] A veteran of the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War), he graduated as a certified [F-14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_F-14_Tomcat) pilot, but never saw any combat.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] On 28 February 2026, the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) announced that he had been killed during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Biography

Aziz Nasirzadeh was born in the year 1964 in the city of [Sarab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarab,_East_Azerbaijan) and completed his [Secondary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_education) in [Tehran](57654.md). Nasirzadeh began his military career in 1982 and after graduating from the Air Force Academy and completing training courses in Iran and [Pakistan](23235.md), he joined a tactical squadron as an [F-14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_F-14_Tomcat) Tomcat [pilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_pilot) During the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War), Over the years, he served in key positions in the [Islamic Republic of Iran Air Force](412792.md), such as chief of staff and acting commander before his appointment as full commander in 2018 to 2021.[^cite_note-auto1-8]

From September 2021 to 2024, Nasirzadeh was Deputy Chief of Staff of the [Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran](14305018.md), a position that reflected significant strategic responsibility across Iran's entire military establishment.[^cite_note-auto1-8][^cite_note-9] In August 2024, he was appointed the [ Iranian Minister of Defense](18379483.md).

After becoming [defence minister](9473138.md) in August 2024, Nasirzadeh made a number of public statements on Iran's military posture amid rising tensions with the United States and Israel. In May 2025, he said that Iran would target U.S. and Israeli interests, bases and forces if either country attacked Iran, adding that U.S. bases in the region would also be considered targets. Ahead of the sixth round of Iran–United States nuclear talks in June 2025, he said that if the negotiations failed and military conflict was imposed on Iran, U.S. bases across the region would be within Iran's reach. During the same press briefing, he also said that Iran had recently tested a missile with a two-ton warhead and would not accept limitations on its missile programme.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

Nasirzadeh was involved in national-level security planning, including chairing Iran's [National Organization for Passive Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Organization_for_Passive_Defense), responsible for safeguarding Iran's critical civilian, military, and nuclear infrastructure from potential U.S. or Israeli strikes. His international security engagements included defense cooperation with [Armenia](10918072.md) and joint military exercises with [Azerbaijan](746.md), aimed at countering Israeli, Turkish, and Western influence in the [South Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Caucasus).[^cite_note-12]

Following [June 2025 Israeli strikes on Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2025_Israeli_strikes_on_Iran), several opposition and foreign media outlets claimed that Nasirzadeh had been killed in the attacks targeting the Iranian Ministry of Defence headquarters in Tehran. However, pro-government journalist Dara Nassari said that "Amir Nasirzadeh is in full health and continues to serve the honorable people of Iran."[^cite_note-auto-13][^cite_note-14]

## Death

Nasirzadeh was killed during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), on 28 February 2026, by the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md). The Iranian government reported his death in official news websites and television channels on [IRIB](819903.md).[^cite_note-15]
