Mohamed Ali Yousfi

**Mohamed Ali Yousfi** ([Tunisian Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_Arabic_language): محمد علي اليوسفي) (born March 3, 1950, in [Béja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A9ja), [Tunisia](30188.md)) is a Tunisian writer and translator.[^cite_note-1]

## Life

After obtaining his master's degree in philosophy and social sciences, he completed his postgraduate studies at the [Lebanese University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_University).

His first novel, published in 1992, was titled *The Time for Elves*, and won the prize for best Arabic novel [Tawkit Al Binka-Al Naked Prize](https://web.archive.org/web/20110721024113/http://aan.mmsh.univ-aix.fr/volumes/1992/Documents/litt-magh-express-ara.pdf). His second novel, *[Sun Tiles](https://web.archive.org/web/20121022023307/http://weekly.ahram.org.eg/1998/379/cu3.htm)*, was published five years later, and won the prize for best novel of [Tunisia](30188.md) 1997[COMAR D'OR Edition 1998](https://web.archive.org/web/20101021190638/http://www.comar.com.tn/www/doc.asp?mcat=3\&mrub=8).
