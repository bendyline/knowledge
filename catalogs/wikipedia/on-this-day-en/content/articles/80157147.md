The **2026 Stanley Cup Final** was the [championship series](3314237.md) for the [National Hockey League](21809.md)'s (NHL) [2025–26 season](79321758.md) and the culmination of the [2026 Stanley Cup playoffs](80157146.md) between the [Eastern Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Conference_\(NHL\)) champion [Carolina Hurricanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Carolina_Hurricanes_season) and the [Western Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Conference_\(NHL\)) champion [Vegas Golden Knights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Vegas_Golden_Knights_season). The series began on June 2 and ended on June 14, with the Hurricanes defeating the Golden Knights four games to two, winning their second [Stanley Cup](66968.md) championship and first since [2006](5444393.md).

Carolina had [home ice advantage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_ice_advantage) in the series due to having a better regular season record. This was the first Final since [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Stanley_Cup_Final) to not feature a team from the state of [Florida](18933066.md), as the two-time defending champion [Florida Panthers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Panthers) failed to qualify for the playoffs, and the [Tampa Bay Lightning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa_Bay_Lightning) were eliminated by the [Montreal Canadiens](42966.md) in the first round.[^cite_note-1]

## Paths to the Final

### Carolina Hurricanes

Main article: [2025–26 Carolina Hurricanes season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Carolina_Hurricanes_season)

This was the third Stanley Cup Final for Carolina. Their first appearance in [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Stanley_Cup_Final) was a loss to the [Detroit Red Wings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_Red_Wings) in five games. They won the Stanley Cup during their second appearance in [2006](5444393.md), where they defeated the [Edmonton Oilers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmonton_Oilers) in seven games.[^cite_note-2]

During the offseason, Carolina traded [Scott Morrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Morrow_\(ice_hockey,_born_2002\)) and two picks to the [New York Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Rangers) for defenceman [K'Andre Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K'Andre_Miller).[^cite_note-3] They also picked up forward [Nikolaj Ehlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolaj_Ehlers) in free agency.[^cite_note-4] Finally, they re-signed [Logan Stankoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logan_Stankoven) and [Jackson Blake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Blake) each to eight-year contracts.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Prior to the start of the season, they picked up goaltender [Brandon Bussi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Bussi) on waivers from the [Florida Panthers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Panthers), signing him to a three-year contract later in the season.[^cite_note-7] [Sebastian Aho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Aho) led the team in scoring with 80 points.[^cite_note-8]

The Hurricanes finished first in the [Metropolitan Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Division_\(NHL\)) and [Eastern Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Conference_\(NHL\)) with 113 points via a 53–22–7 record. In the playoffs, the Hurricanes completed back-to-back four-game sweeps against the [Ottawa Senators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa_Senators) and [Philadelphia Flyers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_Flyers) in the first and second rounds.[^cite_note-9] In their second consecutive Eastern Conference final, they defeated the [Montreal Canadiens](42966.md) in five games. The Hurricanes entered the Final with one loss in the postseason, the fewest since the [1975–76 Montreal Canadiens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975%E2%80%9376_Montreal_Canadiens_season).[^cite_note-10]

### Vegas Golden Knights

Main article: [2025–26 Vegas Golden Knights season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Vegas_Golden_Knights_season)

This was the third Stanley Cup Final appearance for the Golden Knights. Their first appearance came during their inaugural season in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Stanley_Cup_Final), in which they lost to the [Washington Capitals](73125.md) in five games.[^cite_note-11] Their only Stanley Cup victory comes in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Stanley_Cup_Final), where they defeated the [Florida Panthers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Panthers) in five games.[^cite_note-12]

During the offseason, the Golden Knights traded for defenceman [Jérémy Lauzon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%A9r%C3%A9my_Lauzon) and forward [Colton Sissons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colton_Sissons) from the [Nashville Predators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_Predators).[^cite_note-13] They also acquired forward [Mitch Marner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_Marner) from the [Toronto Maple Leafs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_Maple_Leafs), who was signed to an eight-year contract prior to his trade.[^cite_note-14] After three seasons away from the Golden Knights, including two with Carolina, defenseman [Dylan Coghlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_Coghlan) returned for a second stint on a one-year contract.[^cite_note-15] They additionally re-signed forwards [Brandon Saad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Saad) and [Reilly Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reilly_Smith) to one-year contracts.[^cite_note-16] Forward [Jack Eichel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Eichel) was then signed to an eight-year extension closer to the start of the season.[^cite_note-17] At the start of the season, they signed free agent goaltender [Carter Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Hart), who had been acquitted of his charges in the [Hockey Canada sexual assault scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockey_Canada_sexual_assault_scandal).[^cite_note-18] During the season, the team traded for defenceman [Rasmus Andersson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rasmus_Andersson) from the [Calgary Flames](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calgary_Flames).[^cite_note-19] At the trade deadline, they acquired forwards [Cole Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Smith_\(ice_hockey\)) and [Nic Dowd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Dowd) from the Predators and [Washington Capitals](73125.md), respectively.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] On March 29, they fired head coach [Bruce Cassidy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Cassidy) with eight games remaining in the season, replacing him with [John Tortorella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Tortorella).[^cite_note-22] Jack Eichel led the team in scoring with 90 points.[^cite_note-23]

The Golden Knights finished first in the [Pacific Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Division_\(NHL\)) with 95 points via a 39–26–17 record. In the playoffs, they defeated the [Utah Mammoth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Mammoth) in six games in the first round.[^cite_note-24] They then defeated the [Anaheim Ducks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaheim_Ducks) in six games in the second round to reach their fifth Western Conference final in nine seasons.[^cite_note-25] In the Western Conference final, they defeated the [Presidents' Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidents'_Trophy)-winning [Colorado Avalanche](70948.md) in a four game-sweep.[^cite_note-26]

| June 4 | Vegas Golden Knights |  | **3–4** | **OT** | **Carolina Hurricanes** | [Lenovo Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenovo_Center) | [Recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/vegas-golden-knights-carolina-hurricanes-stanley-cup-final-game-2-recap-june-4-2026) |  |

[Seth Jarvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Jarvis) scored the overtime game-winning goal for Carolina in game 2.

In the first period of game two, [Mitch Marner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_Marner) lobbed the puck out of the Vegas defensive zone and [Brett Howden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Howden) picked up the puck and scored to give the Golden Knights a 1–0 lead.[^cite_note-30] After an unsuccessful power play for the Golden Knights in the second period, Howden scored again receiving assists from [Ivan Barbashev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Barbashev) and [Noah Hanifin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Hanifin).[^cite_note-31] In the third period, the Hurricanes cut Vegas's lead to one as [Logan Stankoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logan_Stankoven) scored an unassisted goal, shooting the puck and deflecting past goaltender [Carter Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Hart).[^cite_note-32] They tied the game just over two minutes later as [William Carrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Carrier) passed the puck to [Mark Jankowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Jankowski) whose wrist shot got past Hart.[^cite_note-33] With five minutes remaining in the game, the referee blew the whistle to stop play though the puck went into the Carolina net. Vegas challenged the play that no goaltender interference occurred to cause the whistle stoppage. The challenge was unsuccessful. On the ensuing power play, Carolina defenceman [Shayne Gostisbehere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shayne_Gostisbehere) shot the puck through traffic and [Jordan Staal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Staal) deflected the puck into the net, erasing the two-goal deficit to lead 3–2.[^cite_note-34] With an empty net and an extra attacker for Vegas, [Mark Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Stone) scored to tie the game 3–3, sending it to overtime.[^cite_note-35] In overtime, [Tomáš Hertl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom%C3%A1%C5%A1_Hertl) took a tripping penalty and on the Carolina power play, [Seth Jarvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Jarvis) scored to give the Hurricanes a 4–3 victory.[^cite_note-36]

### Game three

| June 6 | Carolina Hurricanes |  | **4–5** | **2OT** | **Vegas Golden Knights** | [T-Mobile Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-Mobile_Arena) | [Recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/carolina-hurricanes-vegas-golden-knights-stanley-cup-final-game-3-recap-june-6-2026) |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Mitch Marner

Shea Theodore

[Mitch Marner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_Marner) (left), shown with Toronto, scored a hat trick, while [Shea Theodore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shea_Theodore) (right) scored the double-overtime game-winning goal for Vegas in game three.

The first period of game three remained scoreless with the shots favoring Carolina 7–2. In the second period, two goals by Vegas were challenged, first for an offside call and the second on goaltender interference. Both resulted in the goals being called back. The Hurricanes took a "too many on the ice" penalty, and on the power play for Vegas, [Tomáš Hertl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom%C3%A1%C5%A1_Hertl) scored. [Mitch Marner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_Marner) then scored as his shot was deflected off of a Carolina player's stick.[^cite_note-38] Marner scored his second goal less than four minutes later, shooting the puck on his backhand past goaltender [Frederik Andersen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederik_Andersen).[^cite_note-39] Marner completed the [hat trick](411541.md#Hockey) as his third goal came from a slap shot.[^cite_note-40] His hat trick goal was the fastest in Stanley Cup Final history, scoring all three goals within 6\:10.[^cite_note-41] The Hurricanes pulled Andersen after the end of the second period, opting to put in backup goaltender [Brandon Bussi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Bussi). Marner had a chance to make it 5–0 on a penalty shot, but was stopped by Bussi. The Hurricanes broke the shutout as they scored three goals within 39 seconds to cut the deficit to one goal. [Jordan Martinook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Martinook) scored first firing a wrist shot past [Carter Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Hart).[^cite_note-42] [Taylor Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Hall) scored second as [Sebastian Aho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Aho) sent a backhand pass to Hall scoring to put it to 4–2.[^cite_note-43] Captain [Jordan Staal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Staal) then scored off a face-off, making the score 4–3 for Vegas.[^cite_note-44] With 1\:42 left in the game, on the power play, [Andrei Svechnikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Svechnikov) scored to tie the game 4–4. The game went into the double-overtime period, wherein [Shea Theodore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shea_Theodore) scored to give Vegas a 5–4 victory and spoil Carolina's comeback.[^cite_note-45]

### Game four

| June 9 | **Carolina Hurricanes** |  | **5–3** |  | Vegas Golden Knights | [T-Mobile Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-Mobile_Arena) | [Recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/carolina-hurricanes-vegas-golden-knights-stanley-cup-final-game-4-recap-june-9-2026) |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |



Hurricanes captain [Jordan Staal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Staal) scored two goals, including the game-winner, in game 4.

Carolina started the scoring in game four, with [Logan Stankoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logan_Stankoven) scoring 1\:02 into the first period. [Jackson Blake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Blake) made it 2–0 for Carolina just as the Golden Knights' penalty expired, accepting a pass from [Taylor Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Hall) and firing the puck into the net.[^cite_note-47] Vegas captain [Mark Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Stone) put Vegas on the board, faking a slap shot and tucking the puck behind goaltender [Brandon Bussi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Bussi).[^cite_note-48] Later in the first period, Vegas was given a penalty for too many men on the ice; Carolina captain [Jordan Staal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Staal) then scored to make it 3–1.[^cite_note-49] [Brayden McNabb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brayden_McNabb) attempted a buzzer-beater, putting the puck into the net just as the first period ended, but on video replay the goal was waived off as it had not completely crossed the line prior to the time expiration.[^cite_note-50] In the second period, [William Karlsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Karlsson) scored Vegas's second goal of the game, taking a pass from [Rasmus Andersson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rasmus_Andersson) firing a wrist shot past Bussi.[^cite_note-51] The Golden Knights tied up the score 3–3 during a two-on-one rush led by [Colton Sissons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colton_Sissons) and [Brett Howden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Howden), the latter of which scored.[^cite_note-52] In the third period, Vegas gave up the puck in their defensive zone, and while falling down, Staal scored to give the Hurricanes the lead once more, making it 4–3.[^cite_note-53] [Nikolaj Ehlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolaj_Ehlers) made it 5–3 with an [empty net goal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empty_net_goal), tying up the series 2–2.[^cite_note-54]

### Game five

| June 11 | Vegas Golden Knights |  | **2–4** |  | **Carolina Hurricanes** | [Lenovo Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenovo_Center) | [Recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/vegas-golden-knights-carolina-hurricanes-stanley-cup-final-game-5-recap-june-11-2026) |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |



[Andrei Svechnikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Svechnikov) scored two goals in game 5.

In game five, Vegas started the scoring on the power play. As Carolina forward [Nikolaj Ehlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolaj_Ehlers) was in the penalty box for shooting the puck over the glass, [Pavel Dorofeyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Dorofeyev) scored for the Golden Knights to make it 1–0. Carolina tied it 1–1 later in the first period as Ehlers passed the puck to captain [Jordan Staal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Staal) who shot the puck past goaltender [Carter Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Hart).[^cite_note-56] Carolina gained the lead in the second period. As the Hurricanes were on the power play, [Andrei Svechnikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Svechnikov) scored to make it 2–1. [Sebastian Aho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Aho) then made it 3–1 with [Sean Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Walker_\(ice_hockey\)) and [Jordan Martinook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Martinook) both assisting. In the third period, the Hurricanes made it 4–1 on the power play as [Shayne Gostisbehere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shayne_Gostisbehere) passed to an open Ehlers who spun around and backhanded the puck to Svechnikov who scored.[^cite_note-57] The Golden Knights cut the deficit to two goals as Dorofeyev scored his second of the game off of a rebound created by [Shea Theodore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shea_Theodore). However, the Hurricanes stopped the remaining chances by Vegas to go up 3–2 in the series.[^cite_note-58]

### Game six

| June 14 | **Carolina Hurricanes** |  | **3–0** |  | Vegas Golden Knights | [T-Mobile Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-Mobile_Arena) | [Recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/carolina-hurricanes-vegas-golden-knights-stanley-cup-final-game-6-recap-june-14-2026) |  |

Brandon Bussi

Taylor Hall

[Brandon Bussi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Bussi) (left), shown with the [Providence Bruins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence_Bruins), recorded a shutout, while [Taylor Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Hall) (right), shown with Edmonton, scored the Stanley Cup-clinching goal for Carolina in game six.

In game six, Carolina defenceman [Jaccob Slavin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaccob_Slavin) dished a long pass to [Taylor Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Hall) who scored to give the Hurricanes a 1–0 lead.[^cite_note-60] Goaltender [Brandon Bussi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Bussi) kept the Hurricanes at a 1–0 lead through the first period, stopping 11 shots from the Golden Knights. In the second period, the Hurricanes made it 2–0 with [Jackson Blake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Blake) scoring. The Golden Knights were held to three shots for the remainder of the period. In the third period, the Golden Knights pulled their goaltender, but [Nikolaj Ehlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolaj_Ehlers) sealed Carolina's Stanley Cup victory with an empty net goal to make it 3–0.[^cite_note-G6-61]

Hurricanes captain [Jordan Staal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Staal) was awarded the [Conn Smythe Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conn_Smythe_Trophy) as most valuable player of the playoffs.[^cite_note-G6-61] [Rod Brind'Amour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rod_Brind'Amour) also became the first person to win the Stanley Cup as both a former player and coach for the same team since [Toe Blake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toe_Blake) in [1956](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_Stanley_Cup_Final).[^cite_note-62]

## Media rights

In Canada, this was the final year of a 12-year agreement in which the Stanley Cup Final was televised in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) by [Sportsnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_Sportsnet), with sub-licensing agreements with [CBC Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_Television) to simulcast the broadcasts, and with [TVA Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TVA_Sports) airing the series in [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language). The series was also streamed on [Sportsnet+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportsnet+).[^cite_note-71][^cite_note-72] Sportsnet will begin a new 12-year deal next season, but as of the playing of the Final it remained uncertain whether the sub-licensing relationships with CBC and TVA will also continue.[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74] Shortly after the Final concluded, the CBC announced that it would no longer sublicense NHL games from Rogers, making game six the last NHL game to air on CBC television.[^cite_note-75] This also marked the final NHL broadcasts on over-the-air television in Canada, as all games on Sportsnet from 2026 onward would be exclusive to pay television.[^cite_note-76]

In the United States, the series was televised on [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_ABC) and streamed on [ESPN+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN+) via the [ESPN app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN_\(streaming_service\)). This was the fifth year of a seven-year deal in which ABC airs the Final in even years and [TNT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_TNT) televises the series in odd years.[^cite_note-77][^cite_note-78]
