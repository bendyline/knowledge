**Zuwarah**, **Zuwara**, or **Zwara** ([Berber languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berber_languages): ⴰⵣⵡⴰⵔ; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): زوارة) is a coastal city in north-western [Libya](17633.md).

Zuwara is primarily inhabited by indigenous [Berber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berbers) people of Libya. The local Berber dialect, known locally as [Awal m at-willul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zuwara_Berber), is commonly spoken as a first language by the inhabitants of Zuwara.

Zuwara is famous for its beaches and seafood. It is situated 102 km (63 mi) west of [Tripoli](31232.md) and 60 km (37 mi) from the [Tunisian](30188.md) border. It is the capital of the [Nuqat al Khams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuqat_al_Khams) [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Libya). Zuwarah consists of 49 districts.

## History

The settlement was first mentioned by the traveller [Abdallah al-Tijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdallah_al-Tijani) in the years 1306-1309 as composed of two parts: *Zwara al-soughra* ("Little Zwarah") and *Zwara al-koubra* ("Greater Zwarah").[^cite_note-3] In the [Catalan Atlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_Atlas) (1375) it was called as Punta dar Zoyara. The town is mentioned by [Leo Africanus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Africanus) in the 16th century. It later served as the western outpost of [Italian Libya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Libya) (1912–43), being the terminus of the now-defunct [Italian Libya Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Libya_Railways) from Tripoli 105 kilometres (65 mi) to the east. Its artificial harbour shelters a motorized fishing fleet. Cereals, dates, and [esparto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esparto) grass (used to make cordage, shoes, and paper) are local products.

It was in 1973 in Zuwara that [Muammar Gaddafi](53029.md) first proclaimed the Libyan "Cultural Revolution".

### 2011 Libyan civil war

Further information: [Libyan Crisis (2011–present)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_Crisis_\(2011%E2%80%93present\))

During the [2011 Libyan Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Libyan_Civil_War), the city was reported by [Al Jazeera](30204052.md) to be under control of the local [anti-Gaddafi forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Gaddafi_forces) on 23 February 2011, and lost by the government of [Muammar Gaddafi](53029.md).[^cite_note-4] Thousands of anti-government protesters, gathered in the Zuwara town square on 24 February, repulsed another [Libyan Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_Army_\(1951-2011\)) attempt to retake the city. Loyalist forces used the pro-government towns of [Jumayl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jumayl,_Libya) and [Riqdalin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riqdalin?action=edit\&redlink=1) to the south as bases for their attacks on the city.[^cite_note-5] However, from March onwards, the city was under the control of loyalist forces.[^cite_note-6] Amidst the [August rebel coastal offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Libyan_rebel_coastal_offensive), rebels took Zuwara on 18 August.[^cite_note-7]

In September 2011, and following the fall of the Gaddafi government, Zuwara was the first City in Libya to democratically [elect its local council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Libyan_local_elections).[^cite_note-8]

## Economy

The city's [Port of Zwara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Zwara) is used as a transfer point for smuggled fuel by the militias who fight the government. Often these vessels fly the flag of [Palau](23372.md) and are operated from a mailbox in the [Marshall Islands](19147.md).[^cite_note-hud1-9]

## Climate
