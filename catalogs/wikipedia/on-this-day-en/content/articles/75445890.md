**Arsenio Antonio Domínguez Velasco**[^cite_note-1] (born 18 November 1970) is a Panamanian [naval architect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_architect) and international civil servant. Since 1 January 2024, he is serving an initial four-year term as the Secretary-General of the [International Maritime Organization](14986.md) (IMO), a [specialized agency of the United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specialized_agency_of_the_United_Nations).

## Education

Arsenio Domínguez was born in [Panama City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panama_City) on 18 November 1970.[^cite_note-2] He trained as a [naval architect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_architect) at the [University of Veracruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Veracruzana) in Mexico.[^cite_note-MarExec-3][^cite_note-RINA-4] He also has an [MBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MBA) from the [University of Hull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hull) and a Certificate of Higher Education in International Law and European Politics from [Birkbeck University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkbeck_University) in the UK.[^cite_note-IndiaNews-5]

## Career

Domínguez worked as a port engineer in Panama before becoming a Drydock Assistant Manager at the Braswell Shipyard.[^cite_note-IMOBriefing-6]

From 2004 to 2017, he represented [Panama](22997.md) as technical advisor and then Ambassador and Permanent Representative to the International Maritime Organization.[^cite_note-WMUConf-7] During this time he also served as Chair of the Marine Environment Protection Committee (MEPC) from 2014 to 2017.[^cite_note-WMUConf-7]

In 2017, Domínguez assumed the role of Chief of Staff in the Office of the Secretary-General at the International Maritime Organization. Subsequently, he transitioned to the positions of Director of the Administrative Division and Director of the Marine Environment Division.[^cite_note-WMUConf-7]

Domínguez was elected to be the new Secretary-General of the IMO on 18 July 2023.[^cite_note-IMOElect-8][^cite_note-SeaTrade-9] In September 2023, Dominguez informed the London International Shipping Week conference that the IMO had begun reviewing the Carbon Intensity Indicator regulations for shipping.[^cite_note-Tradewinds-10]

The new Secretary-General was approved by the IMO Assembly unanimously on 30 November 2023.[^cite_note-Safety4Sea-11]

### Secretary-General



Dominguez speaking at the IMO Sub-Committee on Ship Design and Construction in 2024

Domínguez assumed office as the 10th Secretary-General of the IMO on 1 January 2024 and released a New Year's Message to the shipping industry.[^cite_note-ShipMgmt1-12][^cite_note-Safety4Sea1-13][^cite_note-MarExec1-14]

On 3 January 2024, in a speech to the [United Nations Security Council](31956.md), he condemned the [Houthi attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_involvement_in_the_Israel%E2%80%93Hamas_war#Attacks_on_shipping_in_the_Red_Sea_and_Indian_Ocean_\(2023%E2%80%932024\)) against international shipping in the [Red Sea](26191.md).[^cite_note-SeaNews-15][^cite_note-marinelink-16] On 10 January, he announced a new senior leadership at the IMO.[^cite_note-HS102024-17] On 22 January, Domínguez gave an opening address to the 10th Session of the IMO Sub-Committee on Ship Design and Construction (SDC), calling for the immediate release of the *[Galaxy Leader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaxy_Leader)* and its crew.[^cite_note-STM2024-18][^cite_note-TW2024-19] In February 2024, he called for a de-escalation of tensions which was causing the ongoing attacks in the Red Sea and stated that the IMO were working to facilitate talks on maritime security between relevant parties.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

In April 2024, at Singapore Maritime Week, Domínguez stated that the IMO was on track to reach its stated goals in reducing greenhouse emissions according to its planned timeline.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] In May 2024, at the 108th session of the Maritime Safety Committee (MSC 108), Domínguez opened the session and highlighted the ongoing attacks on shipping and the need to protect seafarers.[^cite_note-24]

In September 2024, he spoke at the launch of the [DNV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNV) Maritime Forecast event at SMM [Hamburg](13467.md).[^cite_note-WCN-25] He called on the maritime industry to maintain faith in the IMO as it continues to lead shipping towards requirements for decarbonisation and environmental protection.[^cite_note-WCN-25] Also, in September 2024, he delivered the opening remarks of the 10th session of the IMO Sub-Committee on Carriage of Cargoes and Containers.[^cite_note-Safety2024-26] He urged that ships not be targeted because of politics as conflict endangers the lives of seafarers, as well as threaten international [food security](216361.md).[^cite_note-Safety2024-26]

## Personal life

Domínguez enjoys playing [tennis](29773.md) and watching [horror movies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horror_movies).[^cite_note-Riviera1-27] He states that living near the [Panama Canal](24849.md) and the influences of his parents were key factors that encouraged him to seek challenges from a career in the maritime industry.[^cite_note-Riviera1-27]
