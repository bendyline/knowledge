A **provisional constitution**, **interim constitution** or **transitional constitution** is a [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution) intended to serve during a transitional period until a permanent constitution is adopted. The following countries currently have, or had in the past, such a constitution.

## Currently in force

- [Azad Kashmir](2745.md) – [Interim Constitution of Azad Jammu and Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interim_Constitution_of_Azad_Jammu_and_Kashmir_\(1974\)) (since 31 August 1974)

- [Libya](17633.md) – [Libyan interim Constitutional Declaration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_interim_Constitutional_Declaration) (since 3 August 2011)

- [Somalia](27358.md) – [Provisional Constitution of the Federal Republic of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Constitution_of_the_Federal_Republic_of_Somalia) (since 1 August 2012)

- [South Sudan](32350676.md) – [Transitional Constitution of South Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_South_Sudan) (since 9 July 2011)

- [Sudan](27421.md) – [Constitutional Charter of Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Sudan) (since 4 August 2019)

- [Syria](7515849.md) – [Constitutional Declaration of the Syrian Arab Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Declaration_of_the_Syrian_Arab_Republic) (since 13 March 2025)

- [Taiwan](25734.md) – [Additional Articles of the Constitution of the Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Additional_Articles_of_the_Constitution_of_the_Republic_of_China) (since 1 May 1991)

## In the past

- [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia) – [Provisional Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Czechoslovakia_\(1918%E2%80%9338\)) (from 13 November 1918 to 6 March 1920)

- [Egypt](8087628.md) – [2011 Provisional Constitution of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Provisional_Constitution_of_Egypt) (from 30 March 2011 to 26 December 2012)

- [Indonesia](14579.md) – [Provisional Constitution of 1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Constitution_of_1950) (from 17 August 1950 to 5 July 1959)

- [Iraq](7515928.md) – [Law of Administration for the State of Iraq for the Transitional Period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_Administration_for_the_State_of_Iraq_for_the_Transitional_Period) (from 28 June 2004 to 15 October 2005)

- [Ireland](147575.md) – [Dáil Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A1il_Constitution) (from 21 January 1919 to 6 December 1922)

- [Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Administered_Kosovo) – [Constitutional Framework for Provisional Self-Government in Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Framework_for_Provisional_Self-Government_in_Kosovo) (from 15 May 2001 to 16 February 2008)

- [Nepal](171166.md) – [Interim Constitution of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal#The_Interim_constitution_of_Nepal,_2007) (from 2007– 20 September 2015)

- [Pakistan](23235.md) – [Government of India Act 1935](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India_Act_1935) (August 1947 to March 11)

- [Philippines](23440.md) – [1986 Freedom Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_the_Philippines_\(1986%E2%80%931987\)) (from 1986 to 1987)

- [South Africa](17416221.md) – [Interim Constitution of South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interim_Constitution_of_South_Africa) (from 27 April 1994 to 4 February 1997)

- [Taiwan](25734.md) – [Provisional Constitution of the Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Constitution_of_the_Republic_of_China) (from 11 March 1912 to 1 June 1931)

- [Tanzania](30118.md) – [Interim Constitution of Tanzania](29547627.md#Interim_Constitution_\(1964-1977\)) (from 1964 to 1977)

- [Thailand](30128.md) – [2006 Interim Constitution of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Interim_Constitution_of_Thailand) (from 1 October 2006 to 24 August 2007)

- [Thailand](30128.md) – [2014 Interim Constitution of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_interim_constitution_of_Thailand) (from 22 July 2014 to 6 April 2017)

- [United States](3434750.md) – [Provisional Constitution (John Brown)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Constitution_\(John_Brown\)) (never in effect)

- [United States](3434750.md) – [Provisional Constitution of the Confederate States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Constitution_of_the_Confederate_States) (from 8 February 1861 to 22 February 1862)
