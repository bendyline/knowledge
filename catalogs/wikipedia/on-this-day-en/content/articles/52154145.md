**Chala** is a Peruvian town located in the [Arequipa Region](713212.md), [Caraveli Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caraveli_Province), and is the capital of **district of Chala**. It borders the Pacific Ocean. It lies approximately 170 km south of [Nazca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazca) en route to the Panamerican Highway between [Nazca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazca) and [Camana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caman%C3%A1). It is notable for the nearby [Incan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incan) ruins of Puerto Inca.[^cite_note-1] According to the [2017 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Peru_Census), it had a population of 9,240 inhabitants.[^cite_note-2]
