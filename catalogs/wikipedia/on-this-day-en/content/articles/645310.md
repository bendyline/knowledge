**UTC−05\:00** is an identifier for a [time offset from UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC_offset) of −05\:00. In North America, it is observed in the [Eastern Time Zone](496028.md) during [standard time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_time), and in the [Central Time Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Time_Zone) during the other eight months (see [Daylight saving time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daylight_saving_time)). The western [Caribbean](7822.md) uses it year round.
