**Vaavu Atoll** ([Dhivehi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhivehi_language): ފެލިދެ އަތޮޅު) is an [administrative division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_the_Maldives) of the [Maldives](19117.md), comprising the natural atolls of **Felidhu Atoll** and the [Vattaru Reef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vattaru_Reef?action=edit\&redlink=1).

It is the smallest administrative atoll in the Maldives in terms of population.

This atoll is located 40 miles (64 km) from the capital [Malé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mal%C3%A9), 60 minutes by speedboat, and 3 hours by [dhoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhoni_\(fishing_vessel\)).

The Vaavu Atoll [administrative division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_the_Maldives) includes two geographical atolls.

**1 - Felidhoo Atoll**

The main atoll, where all islands except one are located. The atoll is shaped like a diamond (or a 45° square) which the reef of fottheyo stretching eastward

**2 - Vattaru Atoll**

The only atoll which is not inhabited, it contains only one island, Vattaru. Its geographical location coupled with the fact that it is uninhabited makes it a very isolated area that does not get many visitors.

Vaavu Atoll has the largest reef in Maldives, that is the reef of Foththeyo. This is thought to be the least visited island in the whole of Maldives being at the far eastern extremity and 17 mi (15 nmi; 27 km) from the nearest inhabited island of [Keyodhoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keyodhoo_\(Vaavu_Atoll\)).[^cite_note-1]

The easternmost geographical point of the Maldives is located at Fottheyo Muli, close to Foththeyo-bodufushi Island.[^cite_note-2]

No remains from the [Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist) period have been found on this atoll.

## Incidents

Main article: [2026 Dhekunu Kandu cave diving incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Dhekunu_Kandu_cave_diving_incident)

On 14 May 2026 five Italian citizens from the [University of Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Genoa) died while cave diving at a depth of 50 meters (164 feet). It was the worst diving accident in the Maldives' history.[^cite_note-11] Maldivian military officer Sgt. Mohamed Mahudhee died during an attempted recovery of the bodies of the deceased. A Finnish team arrived to aid in their recovery on May 17, 2026.[^cite_note-12] Their bodies were found the following day.[^cite_note-13]

## Notes

Haa Alifu, Haa Dhaalu, Shaviyani, Noonu, Raa, Baa, Kaafu, etc. (including Vaavu) are code letters assigned to the present administrative divisions of the Maldives. They are not the proper names of the [natural atolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atolls_of_the_Maldives) that make up these divisions. Some atolls are divided into two administrative divisions while other divisions are made up of two or more natural atolls. The order followed by the code letters is from North to South, beginning with the first letters of the [Thaana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thaana) alphabet used in [Dhivehi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhivehi_language). These code letters are not accurate from the geographical and cultural point of view. However, they have become popular among tourists and foreigners in the Maldives who find them easier to pronounce than the true atoll names in Dhivehi, (save a few exceptions, like [Ari Atoll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Atoll)).[^cite_note-14]
