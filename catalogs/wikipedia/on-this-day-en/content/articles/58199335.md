In [Sri Lanka](26750.md) a **Member of Parliament** (**MP**), is the title given to any one of the 225 individuals elected or appointed to serve in the [Parliament of Sri Lanka](571660.md).[^cite_note-1]

## Electoral system

Of the 225 members, 196 are elected from [22 electoral districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_districts_in_Sri_Lanka), which are [multi-member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_district). The remaining 29 MPs are [elected from National Lists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_List_Member_of_Parliament) allocated to the [parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_political_parties_in_Sri_Lanka) (and [independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_\(politician\)) groups) in proportion to their share of the national vote.

## Elections

All MP positions become simultaneously vacant for [elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election) held on a five-year cycle. If a [vacancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacancy_\(economics\)) arises at another time, due to death or resignation, then an electoral district vacancy may be filled by the second highest scoring candidate in the last election from that electoral district.

## Eligibility

Any Sri Lankan citizen may be elected to parliament unless disqualified to do so under the Article 90 of the Constitution, which includes any individual who is;

- President of Sri Lanka,

- A [public officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_officer), [judicial officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_officer) or officer of a public corporation,

- A member of the Regular Force of the [Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka_Army), [Navy](6186841.md), or [Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka_Air_Force), with the exception of holders of the rank of [Field Marshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_Marshal_\(Sri_Lanka\)), Admiral of the Fleet, or Marshal of the Air Force.

- A [police officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_officer) or a public officer exercising police function,

- Declared as an undischarged [bankrupt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bankrupt) or [insolvent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insolvency) in terms of the applicable law,

- Nominated as a candidate for more than one electoral district or as a candidate for more than one [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) or group in the same district,

- A citizen of any foreign country,

- Adjudged [guilty](8033188.md) by a competent [court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court) or by a [Special Presidential Commission of Inquiry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Commission_of_Inquiry).

## Title

An MP is known as [The Honourable (The Hon. or Hon.)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Honourable) *Name* MP or simply as the *Name* MP, during their term in office. For instance, [Eran Wickramaratne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eran_Wickramaratne) is generally known to be entitled as the Hon. Eran Wickramaratne MP, and can be also referred to as just Eran Wickramaratne MP. By tradition, former MPs continued to use the [courtesy title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courtesy_title) The Hon. or Hon..
