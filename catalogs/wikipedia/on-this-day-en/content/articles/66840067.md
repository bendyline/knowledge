**KwaMakhutha** is a township in [KwaZulu-Natal](193596.md), [South Africa](17416221.md), situated inland from [eManzimtoti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EManzimtoti) and means "at the Makhutha location", Makhutha is the clan name of the local king Makhanya and the word Makhutha means stale or rot, Kwa means place of. Also known as Malagazi " Rejecter of Blood"

## Geography

KwaMakhutha is situated approximately 29 kilometres (18 mi) south-west of [Durban](313650.md) and forms part of the [eThekwini Metropolitan Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EThekwini_Metropolitan_Municipality), governing the Greater Durban [metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_area). It is bordered by eZimbokodweni to the north, [eManzimtoti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EManzimtoti) to the east, Bhekulwandle to the south and [Adams Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adams_Mission) to the west.[^cite_note-2]

## Infrastructure

KwaMakhutha has a police station, library, a few churches and a masjid. The nearest shopping centres are [Galleria Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galleria_Shopping_Mall) and Arbour Crossing in [Amanzimtoti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanzimtoti), Philani Valley Mall and KwaMuthwa Shopping Centre in [Umlazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umlazi).

#### Roads

The [M37](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M37_\(Durban\)) (Moss Kolnik Drive) is the main road passing through KwaMakhutha, connecting to [Adams Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adams_Mission) in the west and [Amanzimtoti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanzimtoti) in the east. Furthermore, Gugu Mkhize Drive and Mfundi Mngadi Drive connect KwaMakhutha to [Umbogintwini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umbogintwini) in the north-east.&#x20;

## KwaMakhutha Massacre

On 21 January 1987, 13 people, mostly women and children, were killed when gunmen opened fire with [AK47s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AK-47) on the home of [UDF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Democratic_Front_\(South_Africa\)) activist and the treasurer and area organiser of the KwaMakhutha Youth League (KYL), [Mr. Bheki Ntuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bheki_Ntuli), at KwaMakhutha. Mr Ntuli was not at home at the time. Twenty people, including the former Minister of Defence, General [Magnus Malan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnus_Malan), and the [IFP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inkatha_Freedom_Party) leader, Mr MZ Khumalo, were acquitted in the Durban Supreme Court in 1996 for their part in an alleged conspiracy between former state structures and the IFP to carry out the attack. Two former IFP members were granted amnesty for their role in Operation Marion, in which the SADF MI's Special Tasks provided paramilitary training and support to the IFP in a joint effort to combat the revolutionary threat posed by the ANC.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] Until today most Politicians are afraid to go to this area since the locals are known for being an independent Militia with significant amount of heavy duty weaponry.

## 2026 crime

On the night of 22 September 2026, eleven people, including a pregnant woman, were shot and killed when three men opened fire on a house party in KwaMakhutha.[^cite_note-6]
