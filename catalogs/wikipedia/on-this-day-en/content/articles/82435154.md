The **2026 CONCACAF Under-20 Championship** was the ninth edition of the [CONCACAF Under-20 Championship](18650041.md), the biennial men's [under-20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under-20) international [association football](10568.md) tournament organized by [CONCACAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF). It was held in Mexico from 24 July to 9 August 2026.

As with the [2024 edition](76191100.md), 12 teams competed in three groups of four teams each followed by an eight-team knockout round. The tournament results determined CONCACAF's representatives at the [2027 FIFA U-20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup) in Azerbaijan and Uzbekistan, [2027 Pan American Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_Pan_American_Games) in Lima, and [the men's football tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_2028_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_tournament) at the [2028 Summer Olympics](12695048.md) in Los Angeles.[^cite_note-Regulations-1]

## Format

CONCACAF announced the tournament format in November 2025 (Sections 12.5–12.10):[^cite_note-Regulations-1]

- **Group stage:** Twelve teams were drawn into three groups of four teams to play single [round-robin](939742.md) matches. The winners and runners-up from each group and the two highest-ranked third-place teams advance to the knockout stage.

- **Knockout stage:** The eight teams advancing from the group stage are seeded with group winners ranked 1–3, runners-up 4–6, and third-place teams 7 and 8. The first- and eighth-seeded teams, the second- and seventh-seeded teams, the third- and sixth-seeded teams, and the fourth- and fifth-seeded teams will face off in quarter-finals 1–4, respectively. The winners of quarter-finals 1 and 4, and the winners of quarter-finals 2 and 3 will meet in the semi-finals, with the winners of those matches contesting the final.

The tournament determines the CONCACAF participants in the following tournaments (Sections 5.2–5.3):[^cite_note-Regulations-1]

- The four highest-placed teams affiliated with [FIFA](11049.md) will qualify for the [2027 FIFA U-20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup).

  [\[note 1\]](#cite_note-8)

- The tournament champion and the highest-placed teams from each of the [Caribbean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caribbean_Football_Union), [Central American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_American_Football_Union), and [North American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_Football_Union) regions will qualify for the [2027 Pan American Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_Pan_American_Games).

- The highest-placed team affiliated with the [IOC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IOC) will qualify for the [2028 Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_2028_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_tournament). If that team is the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_national_under-20_soccer_team), which has qualified as the host nation, then the second-highest placed team will qualify.

  [\[note 2\]](#cite_note-9)

## Draw

On 7 May 2026, CONCACAF held the group stage draw. The 12 qualified teams were divided into four pots of three teams each based on the CONCACAF ranking prior to qualification. Teams were drawn from Pot 1 into Groups A, B and C, in order, followed by teams from Pots 2, 3 and 4.

## Knockout stage

The knockout stage will use a [single-elimination format](939680.md), starting with the quarter-finals on 4 August and culminating with the final on 9 August. If the score is even after regulation time in a match, 30 minutes of [extra time](633242.md#Association_football) were added. If still tied after this, a [penalty shootout](511302.md) determined the winner.[^cite_note-Regulations-1]

All match times are [UTC-6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC-6)
