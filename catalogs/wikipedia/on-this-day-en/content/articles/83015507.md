On April 22, 2026, a chemical release of [hydrogen sulfide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_sulfide) occurred at a silver [catalysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalysis) plant owned by Ames Goldsmith Corporation in [Institute, West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute,_West_Virginia), killing 2 workers and injuring 30, including one in critical condition.[^cite_note-2] A chemical reaction during the decommissioning of a tank created hydrogen sulfide.[^cite_note-NYTimes-3]

## Background

Institute is home to the [historically-Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historically_Black_colleges_and_universities) [West Virginia State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia_State_University) and multiple chemical plants, which have had a number of chemical incidents in their history and has caused concern from [environmental justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_justice) advocates.[^cite_note-4]

A previous chemical explosion in Institute in 2008 killed 2 and injured 8.[^cite_note-5]

The plant the disaster occurred at had a history of safety incidents, including two spills of [nitric acid](21655.md) within a month in 2013, a federal workplace safety violation in 2018, and had released 1,310 pounds of nitric acid into the air from 2014 to 2024.[^cite_note-6]

## Incident

Workers were decommissioning a tank and [hydrogen sulfide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_sulfide) was created when the chemicals M2000A and [nitric acid](21655.md) were mixed, causing a "violent reaction" that "instantaneously overcame" the workers. 2 were killed and 30 others were sent to the hospital, including 1 in critical condition.[^cite_note-NBC-1][^cite_note-7]

## Aftermath

The [Kanawha County](91038.md) school district ordered a shelter-in-place for multiple schools in the area, as did [West Virginia State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia_State_University). They were later lifted.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-NBC-1] Local officials said the site was in the process of shutting down due to the incident.[^cite_note-NYTimes-3]
