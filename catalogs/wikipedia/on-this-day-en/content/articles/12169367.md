The **Nabatieh District** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): قضاء النبطية) is a district in the [Nabatieh Governorate](4981449.md) of [Lebanon](17771.md). The capital of the district is [Nabatieh](43794798.md).&#x20;
