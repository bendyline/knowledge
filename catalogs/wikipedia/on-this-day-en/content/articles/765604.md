**USS *Truxtun* (DDG-103)** is an [*Arleigh Burke*-class](197341.md) ([Flight IIA](197341.md#Characteristics)) [Aegis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aegis_combat_system) [guided missile destroyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_missile_destroyer) currently in service with the [United States Navy](20518076.md). She is named for American Naval hero Commodore [Thomas Truxtun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Truxtun) (1755–1822), one of the first six commanders appointed by [George Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington) to the newly formed U.S. Navy. She is the [sixth U.S. naval warship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Truxtun) to bear his name.

## Construction

*Truxtun*'s [keel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel) was laid down on 11 April 2005. During construction at [Ingalls Shipbuilding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingalls_Shipbuilding), [Pascagoula, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascagoula,_Mississippi), she suffered a major electrical fire on 20 May 2006, engulfing two levels and causing damage estimated to be worth millions of dollars.[^cite_note-3] She was [launched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_naming_and_launching) on 17 April 2007,[^cite_note-NVR-1] then christened on 2 June 2007, in Pascagoula,[^cite_note-4] with Truxtun's descendants, Susan Scott Martin and Carol Leigh Roelker, serving as sponsors,[^cite_note-5] and [commissioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning) on 25 April 2009, in [Charleston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charleston,_South_Carolina), South Carolina. As of July 2020 the ship is part of [Destroyer Squadron 26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destroyer_Squadron_26) based out of [Naval Station Norfolk](896196.md).[^cite_note-6]

## Ship history

In 2012, the US Navy contracted with [L3 Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L3_Technologies) to develop a fuel-efficient [hybrid electric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_electric) drive train for the [Flight IIA Arleigh Burke](197341.md#Ships_in_class) guided missile destroyers. The system proposed used a pre-existing [quill drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quill_drive) on the reduction gearbox, allowing an electric motor to drive the ships up to 13 kn (24 km/h; 15 mph). *Truxtun* was fitted with the permanent magnet motor system in 2012, under a research and development contract with [General Atomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Atomics).[^cite_note-7] In March 2018, the US Navy announced that the trial program to install hybrid electric drives in 34 destroyers would be cancelled leaving *Truxtun* as the only ship so fitted.[^cite_note-8]

In March 2014, *Truxtun* sailed to the [Black Sea](3386.md), to conduct training with the [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Navy) and [Bulgarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Navy) navies. The deployment of *Truxtun*, along with [sister ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sister_ship) [*Donald Cook*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Donald_Cook), to the Black Sea, was intended as a "strategic reassurance" for former Soviet republics and satellite states concerned about [the annexation of Crimea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Russian_military_intervention_in_Ukraine) by the [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

On 10 August 2020, *Truxtun* completed a deployment with the [*Dwight D. Eisenhower*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Dwight_D._Eisenhower) Carrier Strike Group, without any port calls, that lasted for almost seven months.[^cite_note-11]

At a Pentagon press briefing on 24 April 2023, a spokesperson stated that *Truxtun* was "... off the coast of Sudan, near the Port of Sudan. It will stay there awaiting further orders should it be needed to support. Also, en route is the [USS *Lewis B. Puller* (ESB-3)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Lewis_B._Puller_\(ESB-3\))".[^cite_note-12]

On 12 June 2024, USS *Truxtun* along with the [USS *Donald Cook*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Donald_Cook), [USS *Delbert D. Black*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Delbert_D._Black), Coast Guard cutter [*Stone*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USCGC_Stone), and a [Boeing P-8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_P-8) maritime patrol aircraft were deployed to track a Russian naval flotilla sailing less than 30 miles off the coast of Key Largo, Florida. The flotilla was expected to arrive the same day in Havana, Cuba for naval and air exercises.[^cite_note-13]

In September 2025, *Truxtun* participated in BRIGHT STAR 25, an exercise in [Egypt](8087628.md).[^cite_note-14]

On 11 February 2026, *Truxtun* collided with [USNS *Supply*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USNS_Supply) during sea resupplying in the Caribbean Sea. Two sailors were injured and both ships sailed safely after the collision.[^cite_note-15]

On 4 May 2026, American forces reportedly destroyed six or seven small Iranian boats in the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md) in a clash during the [Operation Project Freedom](83096724.md) as part of the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md). The boats were destroyed after they allegedly tried to interfere with the transit of two US flagged commercial vessels and USS Truxtun and [USS Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Mason).[^cite_note-16]
