The Venezuelan **Ministry of People's Power for Defense** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Ministerio del Poder Popular para la Defensa*), abbreviated M.P.P.D., is the federal-level organization responsible for maintaining the [Venezuelan armed forces](32460.md). Since March 2026, the ministry has been headed by General [Gustavo González López](46270291.md). The ministry coordinates numerous counter-narcotics operations (*e.g.*, Operation Sierra through the CUFAN organ), helps perform various civil support and social development programs, and oversees the conventional military capabilities of [Venezuela](32374.md).

## History

- 1810 Established as War and Navy Bureau

- 1863 Ministry of War

- 1874 Ministry of War and Navy

- 1946 Became Ministry of National Defense

- 1951 Name amended to Ministry of Defense

- 2007 Renamed Ministry of People's Power for Defense by presidential decree
