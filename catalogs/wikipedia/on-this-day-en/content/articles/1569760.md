The **president of Kyrgyzstan**, officially the **president of the Kyrgyz Republic**,[^cite_note-2] is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [government](37570.md) of the [Kyrgyz Republic](170131.md). The president directs the executive branch of the [national government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Kyrgyzstan), is the commander-in-chief of the [Armed Forces of the Kyrgyz Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_the_Kyrgyz_Republic) and also heads the [Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_Council_of_Kyrgyzstan).

The president, according to the [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Kyrgyzstan), "is the symbol of the unity of people and state power, and is the guarantor of the Constitution of the Kyrgyz Republic, and of an individual and citizen."

## History

The office of president was established in 1990 replacing the [chairman of the Supreme Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairmen_of_the_Supreme_Soviet_of_the_Kirghiz_Soviet_Socialist_Republic) that existed, in different forms, from 1936 whilst the country was known as the [Kirghiz Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirghiz_Soviet_Socialist_Republic).

The first popularly elected officeholder was [Askar Akayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Askar_Akayev), who served from 27 October 1990 until 24 March 2005. In July 2005 after the [Tulip Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulip_Revolution), [Kurmanbek Bakiyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurmanbek_Bakiyev) was [elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Kyrgyzstani_presidential_election). He was [re-elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Kyrgyzstani_presidential_election) in 2009, but [large riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Kyrgyzstani_riots) in April 2010 forced him to resign and flee the country. [Roza Otunbayeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roza_Otunbayeva) was selected in April 2010 to head the interim government. She was officially inaugurated on 3 July 2010, as president for a limited term, until elections could be organised. Her successor, [Almazbek Atambayev](7837715.md), was elected in the [2011 Kyrgyzstani presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Kyrgyzstani_presidential_election) and subsequently succeeded Otunbayeva on 1 December that year.

On 16 October 2017, [Sooronbay Jeenbekov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sooronbay_Jeenbekov), the former [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Kyrgyzstan), was elected president of the country. He took office on 24 November 2017. On 15 October 2020, Jeenbekov resigned following [unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Kyrgyzstani_protests) over the [parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_2020_Kyrgyz_parliamentary_election) on 4 October. Jeenbekov was succeeded by Prime Minister [Sadyr Japarov](65513403.md), who became acting president before being confirmed as the 6th and current president on 16 October, and officially held the post on 28 January 2021.[^cite_note-Resignation-3][^cite_note-4]

## Oath of office

On assuming office, the president raises his/her right arm and puts it on the [Kyrgyz Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Kyrgyzstan) while reciting the following oath:

> Мен, (аты-жөнү), Кыргыз Республикасынын Президентинин кызматына киришип жатып, өз элимдин алдында жана Ала-Тоо ыйык Ата, мен ант Кыргыз Республикасынын Башмыйзамынын сыйлоого жана коргоого жана анын мыйзамдарын, Кыргыз Республикасынын мамлекеттик, урмат-сый эгемендигин жана көз карандысыздыгын коргоого жана урмат-сый менен Кыргыз Республикасынын бардык жарандарынын эркиндиги менен укуктарын камсыз кылуу жана талыкпай жогорку милдеттерин жүзөгө ашыруу үчүн Кыргыз Республикасынын президенти, бүткүл эл ишенип мага жүктөгөн! Кудай бизди коргой берсин!

Russian Translation:

> Я (полное имя), вступая в должность Президента Кыргызской Республики, перед своим народом и священным отечеством Ала-Тоо, клянусь уважать и защищать Конституцию Кыргызской Республики и ее законы, защищать суверенитет и независимость Кыргызского государства, уважать и обеспечивать права и свободы всех граждан Кыргызской Республики с честью и неустанно выполнять высокие обязанности Президент Кыргызской Республики, доверил мне доверие всего народа! Да защитит нас Бог!

English Translation:

> I, (full name), in assuming the office of President of the Kyrgyz Republic, before my people and the sacred fatherland of Ala-Too, I swear to respect and protect the Constitution of the Kyrgyz Republic and its laws, to protect the sovereignty and independence of the Kyrgyz state, respect and ensure the rights and freedoms of all citizens of the Kyrgyz Republic with honor and tirelessly carry out the high duties of the President of the Kyrgyz Republic, entrusted to me by the confidence of the whole people! May God protect us!

## Election

### Qualifications

The office of president is open to all [citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens) of Kyrgyzstan who are no younger than 35 years of age, and no older than 65 years of age. A candidate must have a command of the [state language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_language), and have resided in the republic for no less than 15 years before the nomination of his or her candidacy for the position. The president cannot be a deputy of the [Jogorku Kenesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jogorku_Kenesh) (Parliament), occupy other positions, or carry out entrepreneurial activities, and must suspend activity in [political parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_parties) and organizations for their period in office.

On taking office the president must take the following [oath](273418.md), stipulated by Article 45 of the constitution, within 30 days of election before the assembled members of the [legislative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative) chamber:

> "I, ..., assuming the office of the President of the Kyrgyz Republic, before my People and the Sacred homeland Ala-Too do swear:
>
> to sacredly observe and defend the Constitution and laws of the Kyrgyz Republic; to defend the sovereignty and independence of the Kyrgyz State;
>
> &#x20;to respect and guarantee the rights and freedoms of all citizens of the Kyrgyz Republic; with honor and tirelessly to perform the great responsibility of the President of the Kyrgyz Republic entrusted to me by the confidence of all the People!".[^cite_note-5]

### Electoral law

The president is elected by the citizens of Kyrgyzstan by a majority of votes cast. These elections are held on the basis of [universal suffrage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_suffrage), and by secret ballot. To become a candidate a person must obtain the [signatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signatures) of fifty thousand registered voters.

For an election to be considered valid the turnout must not be lower than fifty percent. Similarly, if a candidate wins the backing of more than fifty percent of the voters who participated they are the winner. Should no candidate win an outright majority in the first round, the two candidates with the highest number of votes face each other in a second ballot.

## Duties and functions

The head of state holds significant power as provided for in the constitution. This states that they have the authority to:

- Appoint the Prime Minister and other members of [government](12229.md)

- Present candidates to Parliament for positions within the [Supreme Court](4913027.md)

- Direct [foreign policy](16703.md)

- Present and sign law

- Announce all [elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections) and enact [decrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decrees)

- Act as the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Armed Forces of Kyrgyzstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_Kyrgyzstan)

## Impeachment

The president may be dismissed from office by Parliament only on the basis of a charge made by the Legislative Assembly of state [treason](31292.md) or another grievous crime supported by a ruling of the [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court). Such a decision requires the support of two-thirds of the Jogorku Kenesh who are immediately dismissed should the president be found innocent.

## Succession

If the president becomes unable to carry out their duties for reasons such as death, illness or [impeachment](15328.md), the prime minister shall carry out their duties until the election of a new head of state. This must take place within three months of the termination of their Presidency. Kyrgyzstan has only had one [peaceful transition of power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peaceful_transition_of_power) from president to president (in 2017). [Askar Akayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Askar_Akayev) was forced from office by the [Tulip Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulip_Revolution) of 2005 and [Kurmanbek Bakiyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurmanbek_Bakiyev) was forced from office by the [Kyrgyz Revolution of 2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_Revolution_of_2010). [Almazbek Atambayev](7837715.md) was peacefully succeeded by [Sooronbay Jeenbekov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sooronbay_Jeenbekov) in 2017. However, Jeenbekov resigned from office due to the [2020 Kyrgyzstani protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Kyrgyzstani_protests) and was succeeded by [Sadyr Japarov](65513403.md).
