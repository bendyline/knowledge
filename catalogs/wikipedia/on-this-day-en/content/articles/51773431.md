**Al-Jumayjimah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الجميجمة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *al-Jumayjimah*) is a municipality in the [Bint Jbeil District](12027489.md) in southern [Lebanon](17771.md), located north-east of [Tibnin](6787186.md).&#x20;

## Etymology

According to [E. H. Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Henry_Palmer), the name El Jumeijmeh comes from "The little sculls".[^cite_note-1]

## History

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described it: "A small village of stone and mud, containing about 100 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh), situated on hill-top, with a few olives and figs around. Water supply from [cisterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cisterns)."[^cite_note-2]

On 29 September 1992, following an attack on a [South Lebanon Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Lebanon_Army) outpost in [Rachaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachaf), [Hizbullah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hizbullah) fighters shot their way through a [Unifil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unifil) checkpoint at Al-Jumayjimah. One [Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Army) soldier was killed, bring the total number of Unifil soldiers killed to 187, 33 of them from [Ireland](147575.md).[^cite_note-3]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.75% of registered voters in Al-Jumayjimah. 98.68% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-4]
