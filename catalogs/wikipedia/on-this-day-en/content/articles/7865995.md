# Section 123 Agreement



123 Agreement countries as of February 2025&#x20;

| Current    Euratom members | Former    United States |
| --- | --- |

Section 123 of the United States [Atomic Energy Act of 1954](4280305.md), titled "Cooperation With Other Nations", establishes an agreement for cooperation as a prerequisite for nuclear deals between the US and any other nation.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] Such an agreement is called a **123 Agreement**.[^cite_note-3] To date, the U.S. has entered into roughly twenty-six 123 Agreements with 52 countries.[^cite_note-4]
