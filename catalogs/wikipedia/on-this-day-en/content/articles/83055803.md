The **2026 [European Aquatics Championships](657021.md)** were held in [Paris](22989.md), France, from 31 July to 16 August 2026. [^cite_note-schedule-1][^cite_note-2]

## Schedule

A total of 74 medal events are held across 5 disciplines. [Para Swimming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_swimming) makes its European Aquatics debut with 4 multi-class medal events added to the swimming schedule. Competition dates by discipline are:[^cite_note-schedule-1]

- [Swimming](#Swimming): 10–16 August

- [Open water swimming](#Open_water_swimming): 4–8 August

- [Artistic swimming](#Artistic_swimming): 31 July–5 August

- [Diving](#Diving): 31 July–6 August

- [High diving](#High_diving): 7–8 August

## Venues

The main venue of the competition is the [Centre Aquatique Olympique Métropole du Grand Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Aquatic_Centre) in [Saint-Denis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Denis,_Seine-Saint-Denis), which hosts the swimming, diving and artistic swimming events. The venue, built for the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics), has a spectator capacity of up to 5,000.

The open water swimming competitions are held in the [Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine) at the **Bras de Grenelle**, while the high diving events take place at **Port Debilly**, also on the Seine in central [Paris](22989.md).
