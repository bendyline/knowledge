**Mirzapur** (pronounced [\[mɪ̯r.zɐ.pʊɾ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hindi_and_Urdu) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/a/aa/Mirzapur.ogg/Mirzapur.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/a/aa/Mirzapur.ogg/Mirzapur.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a city in [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md), [India](14533.md). It is known for its [carpets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carpet) and [brassware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brassware) industries, and the tradition of [kajari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kajari) and [birha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biraha) music. Straddled by the [Kaimur extension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaimur_Range) of [Vindhya mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vindhya_Range), it served as the headquarters of the [Mirzapur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirzapur_district). In the district, [Vindhya mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vindhya_Range) meet with [Indo-Gangetic Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Gangetic_Plain).[^cite_note-9]

## History

Mirzapur is significant in prehistoric art history for its painted rock shelters.[^cite_note-10]

## Geography

Mirzapur is located at [25°09′N 82°35′E  /  25.15°N 82.58°E  / 25.15; 82.58](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Mirzapur\&params=25.15_N_82.58_E_).[^cite_note-11] It has an average elevation of 80 metres (265 feet). Mirzapur lies between the parallels of 23.52 & 25.32 North latitude and 82.7 and 83.33 East longitude. It forms a portion of the [Varanasi district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varanasi_district). On the north and north-east it is bounded by Varanasi district, on the south by [Sonbhadra district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonbhadra_district) and on the north-west by [Prayagraj district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prayagraj_district).&#x20;

[Indian Standard Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Standard_Time) is calculated on the basis of 82.5° E longitude, from the [Mirzapur Clock Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirzapur_Clock_Tower).[^cite_note-12]

## Demographics

As of the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India), Mirzapur municipality had a population of 233,691[^cite_note-office_of_the_registrar_general_census_commissioner_india-6] and the urban agglomeration had a population of 245,817.[^cite_note-office_of_the_registrar_general_census_commissioner_india-2-7] The municipality had a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_sex_ratio) of 869 females per 1,000 males and 11.9% of the population were under six years old.[^cite_note-office_of_the_registrar_general_census_commissioner_india-6] Effective literacy was 78.25%; male literacy was 83.85% and female literacy was 71.80%.[^cite_note-office_of_the_registrar_general_census_commissioner_india-6]

## Indian Standard Time calculation



Ghanta Ghar

[Indian Standard Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Standard_Time) is calculated from the clock tower in Mirzapur nearly exactly on the reference longitude of Indian Standard Time at 82°30'E, within 4 angular minutes, a property shared by [Tuni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuni), a town in [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md).[^cite_note-13]

## In popular culture

The Indian web series [*Mirzapur*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirzapur_\(TV_series\)) takes place in the city and is also inspired by and references its [carpet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carpet)-making industry.[^cite_note-14]
