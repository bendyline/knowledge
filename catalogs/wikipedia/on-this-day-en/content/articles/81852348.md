The **2025 Palmyra attack** was an ambush carried out on 13 December 2025 against [Syrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian) and [US](3434750.md) troops [stationed](43683196.md#2024%E2%80%93present:_Post-Assad_drawdown) near the [Syrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian) city of [Palmyra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmyra_\(modern\)). The attack was conducted by a lone [Syrian security officer](34679004.md),[^cite_note-AlMonitor_US_retaliation_Palmyra-1][^cite_note-Politico_attacker_recent_recruit-2] affiliated with the [Islamic State](9087364.md). The attack resulted in the deaths of two US soldiers (Sergeant Edgar Brian Torres-Tovar and Sergeant William Nathaniel "Nate" Howard, both posthumously promoted to the rank of staff sergeant) and Turkish-US linguist Ayad “Eddie” Mansoor Sakat, which were the first US military casualties in Syria since the [fall of the Assad regime](78548066.md).[^cite_note-AlMonitor_US_retaliation_Palmyra-1][^cite_note-Politico_attacker_recent_recruit-2]

## Attack

On 13 December 2025, a [Syrian police officer](34679004.md), suspected of being affiliated with the [Islamic State](9087364.md),[^cite_note-AlMonitor_US_retaliation_Palmyra-1][^cite_note-Politico_attacker_recent_recruit-2] carried out an ambush attack on [Syrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian) and US troops stationed in [Palmyra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmyra_\(modern\)). The attack occurred a month after Syrian president [Ahmed al-Sharaa](44007699.md) signed a political cooperation agreement with the [United States](3434750.md)-led coalition against the Islamic State, which coincided with al-Sharaa's visit to the [White House](33057.md).[^cite_note-3]

The soldiers were stationed for counter-terrorist measures when they came under gunfire by a lone gunman. The gunman was shot and killed by US troops of B Troop 1-113th Cavalry Division. Two Syrian troops were also reported to be wounded. The injured troops were evacuated to the [Al-Tanf](55833665.md) military base controlled by the United States.[^cite_note-4]

The [Syrian Observatory for Human Rights](34029669.md) stated that the attacker was a member of the security forces of Syria.[^cite_note-5] Syrian interior minister Noureddine al-Baba in a broadcast interview on the public television of Syria stated that the gunman had been a member of the security forces, whose dismissal for his "extremist" views had been planned for 14 December. He stated that the gunman had infiltrated a meeting between a delegation from the United States to combat the Islamic State and the [Syrian Armed Forces](27479.md).[^cite_note-6]

## Responses

Syria arrested five suspects in the shooting of American troops at Palmyra. [Syria's Interior Ministry](34679004.md) stated that arrests took place in coordination with the international coalition forces and special Syrian units. On 14 December, [U.S. Secretary of State](32293.md) [Marco Rubio](5502549.md) held a discussion about the issue with the Syrian foreign minister [Asaad al-Shibani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asaad_al-Shibani) on Sunday.[^cite_note-7]

### US/Jordanian military response

This section is an excerpt from [2025–2026 United States airstrikes in Syria](81885282.md).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=2025%E2%80%932026_United_States_airstrikes_in_Syria\&action=edit)]

[Operation Hawkeye Strike](81885282.md)[^cite_note-8] was a retaliatory military action by the [United States](3434750.md) against the [Islamic State](9087364.md), following the [December 2025 Palmyra attack](81852348.md) by a [Syrian police officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Security_Service_\(Syria\)), suspected of being affiliated with the Islamic State, that killed two U.S. soldiers and a civilian interpreter.[^cite_note-2025–2026_United_States_airstrikes_in_Syria_AlMonitor_US_retaliation_Palmyra-9][^cite_note-2025–2026_United_States_airstrikes_in_Syria_Politico_attacker_recent_recruit-10]

The US carried out an airstrike northwest Syria on Jan. 16, that resulted in the death of a leader affiliated with [Al-Qaeda](1921.md) who had direct ties to the terrorist responsible for an ambush which killed two U.S. service members and an American interpreter on Dec. 13, 2025.[^cite_note-11]

## Reactions

- [Syria](7515849.md) [Syria](7515849.md): President [Ahmed al-Sharaa](44007699.md) expressed condolences for the victims and vowed to promote stability in the region.

  [^cite_note-12]

- [United States](3434750.md) [United States](3434750.md): President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) blamed [ISIL](9087364.md) for the attack and vowed retaliation.

  [^cite_note-13]

- [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) [Saudi Arabia](349303.md): [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) expressed strong condemnation of the attack on [US](3434750.md) and [Syrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian) troops in [Palmyra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmyra_\(modern\)).

  [^cite_note-14]

- [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) [United Arab Emirates](69328.md): The [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) expressed strong condemnation of the incident and called for undermining safety and stability in the region.

  [^cite_note-15]

- [Bahrain](18933277.md) [Bahrain](18933277.md): In a statement issued by the Foreign ministry of Bahrain, [Bahrain](18933277.md) condemned the [ISIS](9087364.md) attack and expressed solidarity with [Syria](7515849.md) and the [United States](3434750.md).

  [^cite_note-16]

- [Iraq](7515928.md) [Iraq](7515928.md): The foreign ministry of [Iraq](7515928.md) condemned the attack and stated that "The Ministry of Foreign Affairs condemns the terrorist attack that targeted a joint patrol of Syrian internal security forces and U.S. forces near the city of Palmyra, which resulted in a number of casualties".

  [^cite_note-17]

- [Jordan](7515964.md) [Jordan](7515964.md): The foreign ministry spokesperson Ambassador Fouad al Majali affirmed [Syria](7515849.md) and the [United States](3434750.md) with full solidarity of [Jordan](7515964.md) and condemned the attack.

  [^cite_note-18]

- [Turkey](11125639.md) [Turkey](11125639.md): In an official statement on Sunday, Turkey condemned the attack and expressed condolences for the victims of the Palmyra attack.

  [^cite_note-19]
