**Nan Ni Ni Aye** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): နန်းနီနီအေး; born 12 October 1969) is a Burmese politician and former educator who has served as the fourth [Second Vice President of Myanmar](30762522.md) since April 2026. A member of the [Union Solidarity and Development Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Solidarity_and_Development_Party) (USDP), she previously served in the [Amyotha Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amyotha_Hluttaw) as the representative for Kayin State Constituency No. 6 for two consecutive terms.

## Early life and education

Nan Ni Ni Aye was born on 12 October 1969 in [Hpa-an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hpa-an), [Kayin State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayin_State).[^cite_note-Profile-1] Her father, Saw Boe Ni, was also a member of parliament from Kayin State during the era of the [Revolutionary Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_Council_of_the_Union_of_Burma).[^cite_note-BBC-2] She studied zoology at Hap-an College and [Mawlamyine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mawlamyine_University)[^cite_note-Profile-1] and later pursued a master's degree in [information science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_science) at the [University of Computer Studies, Yangon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Computer_Studies,_Yangon).[^cite_note-BBC2-3] Before entering politics, she served as a state school teacher and later as an assistant lecturer at her alma mater, the computer university, for a total of 17 years in public service.[^cite_note-BBC-2]

## Political career

After the [2010 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Myanmar_general_election), Nan Ni Ni Aye became an MP in the [Amyotha Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amyotha_Hluttaw) (Upper House) for Kayin State Constituency No. 6, which comprised [Hpapun Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hpapun_Township), a position to which she was re-elected in the [2015 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Myanmar_general_election).[^cite_note-BBC-2] She is the chair of the [Union Solidarity and Development Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Solidarity_and_Development_Party) in Kayin State,[^cite_note-4] and was a member of the Amyotha Hluttaw's Bill Committee for two terms.[^cite_note-BBC2-3] She competed for the [Kayin State Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayin_State_Hluttaw) in the [2020 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Myanmar_general_election) but lost;[^cite_note-BBC-2] however, she won a seat in the [2025–26 general election](69631013.md) for the State Hluttaw for Hpa-an Constituency No. 2 as a [proportional representative](23298.md).[^cite_note-5]

On 30 March 2026, Nan Ni Ni Aye was nominated for the vice presidency by the Amyotha Hluttaw[^cite_note-6] and was elected on 31 March with 117 votes.[^cite_note-7] At the [presidential election](82700906.md) held at the [Pyidaungsu Hluttaw](29739324.md) on 3 April 2026, she was elected as the second vice-president—after [Min Aung Hlaing](38278640.md) and [Nyo Saw](72867108.md) became the president and the first vice-president respectively—[^cite_note-8] the first woman to hold the office in the country's history.[^cite_note-BBC2-3]
