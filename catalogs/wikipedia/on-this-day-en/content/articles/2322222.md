**Tessalit** is a [rural commune](16112417.md) and village in the [Kidal Region](2214311.md) of [Mali](19127.md). The village is the administrative centre of [Tessalit Cercle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessalit_Cercle) (district). The village lies 85 kilometres (53 mi) north of [Adjelhoc](2322283.md) and about 70 kilometres (43 mi) from the Algerian border. The *commune* extends over an area of 30,000 square kilometres (12,000 sq mi) that is almost entirely desert. In the 2009 census the *commune* had a population of 5,739.[^cite_note-pop2009-2] It is currently under FLA and JNIM control, following the [2026 Mali offensives](83033878.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

It is served by [Tessalit Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessalit_Airport).

Tessalit is an [oasis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oasis) in the [Sahara](325363.md) desert and a stop for trans-Saharan travellers. A [gypsum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gypsum) deposit and a [plaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaster) factory also contribute to the local economy, though these activities have been disrupted in recent decades by the [Tuareg Rebellions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuareg_rebellion_\(disambiguation\)) and [terrorism](30636.md) in neighboring [Algeria](358.md).

The Malian government have a military base at the village of Tessalit.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Which was captured by JNIM and the FLA on May 1, 2026.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

Tessalit is situated in the mountain range of *[Adrar des Ifoghas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrar_des_Ifoghas)*. It is primarily populated by [Tuaregs](103054.md) and is the home of the musical group [Tinariwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinariwen) as well as the poet [Souéloum Diagho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sou%C3%A9loum_Diagho). The village is twinned with [Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne), France.

The cercle of Tessalit contains the [communes](16112417.md) of Tessalit, Adjelhoc, and [Timtaghene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timtaghene).

## During the Mali War

Main article: [Battle of Tessalit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Tessalit)

**2010s**

Tuareg rebels defeated Malian government forces to take the village in 2012. Attempts by the Malian Air Force, using Ukrainian mercenaries, were unable to prevent the defeat.

French paratroopers took the local airport and then were joined by French and African Forces landing at the airport. They recaptured Tessalit on Feb. 8, 2013, according to General Staff spokesman Colonel Thierry Burkhard.[^cite_note-9]

### 2020s

French forces killed the head of [Al-Qaeda in the Islamic Maghreb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qaeda_in_the_Islamic_Maghreb) (AQIM), [Abdelmalek Droukdel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdelmalek_Droukdel) on June 5, [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_in_West_Africa), near Tessalit. While France is concentrating its anti-terrorist efforts on the [Islamic State in the Greater Sahara (IS-GS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_State_in_the_Greater_Sahara), a spokesperson indicated that does not preclude other activities. Droukdel was buried at the scene.[^cite_note-10]

On May 1, 2026, the village was captured by the FLA and JNIM as part of the [2026 Mali offensives](83033878.md).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]
