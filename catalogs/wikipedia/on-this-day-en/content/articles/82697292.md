**Harakat Ashab al-Yamin al-Islamia** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): حركة أصحاب اليمين الإسلامية, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Islamic Movement of the People of the Right Hand'), commonly known as **Ashab al-Yamin** or **HAYI**, is a pro-Iranian [Islamist](15012.md) group that has taken credit for attacks against Jewish schools, [synagogues](48559.md) and charities in Europe, as well as American, Israeli and [Iranian opposition](80207658.md) targets, since March 2026. The group's activities have primarily focused on [attacks on London's Jewish community](83012228.md), with additional attacks across Europe.

The attacks have been investigated by [counter-terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-terrorism) police, with 37 arrests made, and the UK proscribed HAYI as a national security threat. Analysts from the [International Centre for Counter-Terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Counter-Terrorism) (ICCT) and European authorities have questioned the group's authenticity as an independent terrorist organization, positing it may be a [front group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front_organization) for the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC), who outsource the attacks to local criminals and use the group to create [plausible deniability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plausible_deniability).

## History and operations

HAYI's [Telegram](41501305.md) was established in 2024 but laid dormant until early March 2026, following [US-Israeli strikes on Iran on February 28](82533582.md#First_week_\(28_February_%E2%80%93_6_March\)), after which [Europol](525329.md) warned of elevated risk of attacks in Europe targeting Jewish and US-linked sites.[^cite_note-Sabbagh-1][^cite_note-RFERL-2] It became known through videos posted on the Telegram channels of [Iran-allied militant groups](36658251.md). It claims to be a [Khomeneist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khomeinism) group, in that it embraces the beliefs of [Ruhollah Khomeini](26232.md), the first [supreme leader of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_leader_of_Iran). Its name refers to the depiction of [Judgement Day in Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judgement_Day_in_Islam), where the people of the right hand will go to Paradise. The group's logo, with a hand holding a rifle against a globe, invokes similar logos of Iran-backed Shia militias in [Lebanon](17771.md) and [Iraq](7515928.md).[^cite_note-jt-3] The organization claims operations by distributing "proof-of-crime" videos via Telegram channels.[^cite_note-jpost-4][^cite_note-ht-5]

## Claimed activities

See also: [2026 London antisemitic attacks](83012228.md) and [2026 Iranian diaspora protests § Violence towards diaspora protesters](82164645.md#Violence_towards_diaspora_protesters)

HAYI has taken credit for attacks across Europe that have targeted Jewish schools, [synagogues](48559.md) and charities, as well as American, Israeli and [Iranian opposition](80207658.md) establishments. The attacks have generally utilized [arson](184695.md), [explosive devices](265112.md) and [chemicals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_terrorism), with the incidents taking place at night and with buildings as targets. HAYI has claimed responsibility for some incidents, while other incidents were successfully foiled by authorities. These include the following:

## Organization and structure

Western security services, European authorities, and specialist bodies such as the [International Centre for Counter-Terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Counter-Terrorism) (ICCT) and [Tech Against Terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tech_Against_Terrorism) believe HAYI to be a [front group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front_organization) for the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC), who outsource attacks to local 'thugs-for-hire' and use the group to create [plausible deniability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plausible_deniability).[^cite_note-RFERL-2][^cite_note-ht-5][^cite_note-35] In that regard, HAYI can be seen as an [astroturfed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astroturfing) brand rather than a [grassroots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grassroots) organization.[^cite_note-jt-3][^cite_note-Stocker-36] According to the ICCT, four channels—"two linked to Iraqi militias including [Asaib Ahl al-Haq](26106154.md), which maintains ties to the IRGC's [Quds Force](4028355.md), and two pro-Iranian content aggregators with links to sanctioned pro-Russian networks"—anchor every dissemination cycle.[^cite_note-RFERL-2] The establishment of HAYI sits within a larger pattern of [Iranian state-sponsored terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_and_state-sponsored_terrorism) and [hybrid warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_warfare).[^cite_note-jt-3]

The individuals who carried out the attacks are described as potentially amateurs, disaffected teenagers and petty criminals, who have been recruited to conduct the acts for small fees.[^cite_note-jt-3][^cite_note-Stocker-36][^cite_note-Nerozzi240326-37] Arrests in London and Rotterdam have largely been of minors, and in France have been three minors and one adult, all recruited via social media.[^cite_note-RFERL-2][^cite_note-ht-5][^cite_note-Reuters-IranLink-8] A researcher at the ICCT said "There is a controller, a multilayered system, a cell leader who is supposed to carry something out but is being guided. He is on the phone with someone—we still don't know who—and receives materials almost ready for the attack".[^cite_note-RFERL-2] Despite the possibility that some of these perpetrators may not be acting based on the ideology of the IRGC, they still face severe prosecution under the [National Security Act](74400260.md), according to terrorism expert Roger Macmillan.[^cite_note-38]

The Israeli [Ministry of Diaspora Affairs and Combating Antisemitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Diaspora_Affairs_and_Combating_Antisemitism) noted that the group's name shares nomenclature with an Iraqi militia previously designated as a terrorist organization by the United States. Israeli officials suggested that the timing of the group's activation may be linked to reports of the death of a high-ranking pro-Iranian militia leader in Iraq, raising the possibility that the European attacks were intended as retaliation.[^cite_note-nltimes-11][^cite_note-ynet-39] Experts have highlighted several "unsophisticated" errors that deviate from standard Islamist rhetoric and indicate involvement from individuals outside of Iran. The group's Arabic logo misspells the word "Islamic" (al-Islamia), and its imagery features a [Soviet SVD Dragunov sniper rifle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SVD_\(rifle\)) instead of the [AK-47](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AK-47) motifs standard among Iran-backed proxies.[^cite_note-ht2-40][^cite_note-Nerozzi2403262-41][^cite_note-RFERL2-42] Analysts also noted a total absence of [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) in the group's communications.[^cite_note-Stocker2-43]

Following the arrest of [Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood al-Saadi](83247935.md), a commander of the Iran-backed Iraqi paramilitary group [Kata'ib Hezbollah](23626646.md), court documents showed that al-Saadi coordinated the HAYI attacks from Baghdad, Iraq. Messages were initially posted on Telegram and Snapchat to send secret activation instructions to terrorist networks in Europe. Al-Saadi was seen on video calls with the individuals carrying out attacks for HAYI. Julian Lanchès of the ICCT said that al-Saadi was likely not running the social media channels himself or coordinating the attacks alone. [^cite_note-44]

## Investigation and response

In the United Kingdom, by 27 April a total of 26 individuals had been arrested for planning and conducting a [series of attacks](83012228.md), most linked to HAYI.[^cite_note-BBC_26_arrests-45] In response to the attacks, police resources were significantly stepped up in northwest London: additional uniformed and plainclothed officers have been placed in areas with Jewish populations, and extra stop and search powers have been introduced in Barnet. Additionally, armed response vehicles and Counter Terrorism Policing resources have been deployed to the area to support the increased local policing plan.[^cite_note-46]

On 15 May, the [US Justice Department](52563.md) charged Iraqi national [Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood al-Saadi](83247935.md) for his involvement in several of the incidents, tied to his alleged role as an operative for the Iran-backed Iraqi paramilitary group [Kata'ib Hezbollah](23626646.md) and Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC). They linked him to 18 European attacks (particularly the 15 March Amsterdam incident and the 12 April Skopje attack) and two in Canada, as well as planned incidents targeting synagogues in the US.[^cite_note-y488-24][^cite_note-b588-23]

In June 2026, a group of Western and allied states, including Albania, Australia, Belgium, Bulgaria, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, France, Finland, Germany, Ireland, Latvia, Lithuania, the Netherlands, New Zealand, North Macedonia, Norway, Portugal, Sweden, United Kingdom and the United States, issued a joint statement condemning Iran's usage of international and local criminal networks to carry out attacks across North America, Australia and Europe, such as the recent HAYI claimed-attacks in Europe.[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49] In July 2026, the British government designated the organization under the [National Security (State Threats) Act 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_\(State_Threats\)_Act_2026).[^cite_note-50]
