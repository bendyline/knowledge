# 2026 FIFA World Cup Group K

&#x20; Group K of the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) took place from June 17 to 27, 2026.[^cite_note-match_schedule-1] The group consisted of [Portugal](576328.md), [DR Congo](1030243.md), [Uzbekistan](1032413.md), and [Colombia](680175.md).

Colombia secured their advancement to the [round of 32](81667005.md#Round_of_32).[^cite_note-regulations-2] after two group game wins, and would top the group after earning a draw against Portugal. Portugal also advanced as group runners-up, after a crucial victory against Uzbekistan.

Although DR Congo failed to win their first two matches, a victory over Uzbekistan allowed them to finish third in the group and advance as one of the best third-place teams.[^cite_note-regulations-2] Uzbekistan finished fourth and were eliminated, losing all three of their group games.

### Portugal vs DR Congo

The two teams had never met before.

This match marked the country's first time competing under the name DR Congo, having previously appeared as *Zaire* in [1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_FIFA_World_Cup). It was also the first World Cup match to be played within the vicinity of a tropical cyclone ([Tropical Storm Arthur](83471857.md)), but it was unaffected.

Portugal captain [Cristiano Ronaldo](623737.md) matched Argentina's [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) by playing in a sixth World Cup finals, and also become the oldest outfield player to start a World Cup match at the age of 41 years and 132 days.[^cite_note-8]

Portugal went in front in the 6th minute when [João Neves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Neves) headed to the right corner after a cross from the left by [Pedro Neto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Neto). In time added on in the first half, it was 1–1 when [Yoane Wissa](52748696.md) headed to the left of the net from six yards out after a cross from the right by [Arthur Masuaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Masuaku), the goal was DR Congo's first-ever World Cup final's goal.[^cite_note-9]

### Uzbekistan vs Colombia

The two teams had never met before. It was the first time since May 12, 2002 that Colombia returned to the pitch of the Azteca having lost a friendly match there to [Mexico](808402.md) 2–1. The fixture marked Uzbekistan's debut at the FIFA World Cup.

In the 40th minute, [Daniel Muñoz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Mu%C3%B1oz_\(footballer\)) put Colombia in front when he scored with a volley from six yards out, connecting with a pass over the top from [Luis Díaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_D%C3%ADaz_\(footballer,_born_1997\)). [Abbosbek Fayzullaev](73363973.md) made it 1–1 in the 60th minute when he headed into an empty net from close range after Colombian goalkeeper had parried a shot from [Eldor Shomurodov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eldor_Shomurodov) on the right. The goal was Uzbekistan's first-ever goal at a World Cup finals.[^cite_note-13] Colombia went back in front five minutes later when Luis Díaz scored with a low finish to the right corner. In the 9th minute of added time, substitute [Jaminton Campaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaminton_Campaz) made it 3–1 when he headed to the left corner of the net from six yards out after a cross from [Cucho Hernández](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cucho_Hern%C3%A1ndez) on the right.[^cite_note-14]

[Abdukodir Khusanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdukodir_Khusanov) received a yellow card for a tackle on [Luis Díaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_D%C3%ADaz_\(footballer,_born_1997\)) that caused him to collide with a pitch-side camera operator. The match was briefly paused while medical staff attended to the cameraman, who was taken to the hospital but escaped serious injury. Following the match, Khusanov sent a signed match jersey with a written apology to the operator.[^cite_note-15]

### Portugal vs Uzbekistan

[Cristiano Ronaldo](623737.md) scored twice in the first half, becoming the first player to score in six FIFA World Cup tournaments.[^cite_note-18]

## Discipline

The team conduct ("fair play") score would have been used as a tiebreaker if the head-to-head and overall records of teams were tied. It would also be used as a tiebreaker for the [third-place ranking](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams) between groups if the overall records of teams were tied. The score was calculated based on yellow and red cards received by players and team officials in all group matches as follows:[^cite_note-regulations-2]

- yellow card: −1 point;

- indirect red card (second yellow card): −3 points;

- direct red card: −4 points;

- yellow card and direct red card: −5 points;

Only one of the above deductions could be applied to a player or team official in a single match.
