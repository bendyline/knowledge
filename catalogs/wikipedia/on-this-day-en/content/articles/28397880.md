***Carcinogenesis*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [medical journal](29500858.md) in the field of [cancer biology](105219.md). It was established in 1980 and is published monthly by [Oxford University Press](48518.md). As of 2024, the [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Sharon R. Pine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharon_R._Pine?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([University of Colorado School of Medicine, Aurora, CO, USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_School_of_Medicine,_Aurora,_CO,_USA?action=edit\&redlink=1)).[^cite_note-1] *Carcinogenesis* publishes articles in four sections: *cancer biology* covers the cell and molecular biology of cancer, as well as [mutation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutation) and [DNA repair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA_repair); *molecular epidemiology* includes [genetic predisposition to cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetics_of_cancer); *cancer prevention* covers [chemoprophylaxis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemoprophylaxis) as well as dietary factors; and *carcinogenesis* covers all forms of [carcinogens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carcinogen), including their metabolism and detection in the environment.[^cite_note-About-2] Authors can pay to have their articles released freely online as part of a [hybrid open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_open_access_journal) scheme.[^cite_note-3] Free or reduced-rate online access is available to educational institutions in low-income countries.[^cite_note-Harris-4][^cite_note-5]

## History

The journal was established in 1980 by [R. Colin Garner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Colin_Garner?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([University of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_York)) and [Anthony Dipple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Dipple?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute)).[^cite_note-Harris-4][^cite_note-front_1-6] The original scope of *Carcinogenesis* was defined in the first issue as research relating to "the prevention of cancer in man", and the journal was conceived from the outset as a [multidisciplinary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interdisciplinary_studies) journal, with the intention of encouraging the "cross-fertilization of ideas" across the "very broad spectrum of scientific endeavour" of cancer research.[^cite_note-Garner-7] In 2008, the journal added the subtitle "*Integrative Cancer Research*" to reflect its multidisciplinary scope.[^cite_note-8]

The journal was originally published by [IRL Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRL_Press?action=edit\&redlink=1),[^cite_note-front_1-6][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] which merged with Oxford University Press in 1989.[^cite_note-Harris-4][^cite_note-11]

## Abstracting and indexing

*Carcinogenesis* is abstracted and indexed in [Biological Abstracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_Abstracts), [BIOSIS Previews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BIOSIS_Previews), [CAB Abstracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAB_Direct_\(database\)), [Chemical Abstracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_Abstracts_Service), [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Life Sciences, [BIOBASE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier_BIOBASE) – Current Awareness in Biological Sciences, [EMBASE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMBASE), [Excerpta Medica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excerpta_Medica), [Global Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAB_Direct_\(database\)), [MEDLINE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MEDLINE), [ProQuest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ProQuest), and the [Science Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index).[^cite_note-About-2] According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2020 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 4.944.[^cite_note-WoS-12]
