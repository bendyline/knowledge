The **83rd annual [Venice International Film Festival](32497.md)** took place from 2 to 12 September 2026, at [Venice Lido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice_Lido) in [Italy](14532.md). American actress and filmmaker [Maggie Gyllenhaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maggie_Gyllenhaal) served as Jury President for the main competition.[^cite_note-1] Italian actors [Greta Scarano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greta_Scarano) and [Nicolas Maupas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Maupas) hosted the opening and closing ceremonies.[^cite_note-2]

The [Golden Lion](2386128.md) was awarded to *[Woman Unknown](83824249.md)* by [May el-Toukhy](62654474.md).[^cite_note-\:8-3] American actress [Ellen Burstyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellen_Burstyn) and American actor and filmmaker [George Clooney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Clooney) received the [Golden Lion for Lifetime Achievement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Lion_for_Lifetime_Achievement) during the festival.[^cite_note-\:4-4][^cite_note-\:2-5]

The festival's full line-up was announced on 23 July,[^cite_note-\:0-6] but [Yuval Abraham](76256808.md) and [Rachel Szor](79369018.md)'s *[NAZA](84051935.md)* was added to the main competition only on 24 August,[^cite_note-\:02-7] following safety concerns. While the selection of *[DAU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dau_\(film\))*, directed by Russian [dissident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissident) filmmaker [Ilya Khrzhanovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Khrzhanovsky), was condemned by Ukrainian official authorities for supposedly ties with the [Kremlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremlin).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] The statement was rebuffed by the festival's organization,[^cite_note-10] which included a strong response from its artistic director [Alberto Barbera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Barbera), who called an unacceptable manipulation of reality.[^cite_note-11]

Shortly before the festival's opening week, the main competition jury's press conference was cancelled without further explanation in an unprecedented decision. According to [Deadline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood), the move came after highly charged press conferences at the [76th Berlin International Film Festival](81825397.md#Controversies), where jurors and talent were repeatedly inquired by journalists about global politics, especially the [Gaza war](74998519.md).[^cite_note-12] Ultimately, the press conference was rescheduled to 2 September, the first day of the festival.[^cite_note-13] Meanwhile, [Roger Waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Waters), [Annie Ernaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Ernaux), [Matteo Garrone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matteo_Garrone), [Jasmine Trinca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jasmine_Trinca), [Saleh Bakri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saleh_Bakri), [Céline Sciamma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9line_Sciamma), among many other industry talents, signed an open letter organized by the Venice4Palestine collective calling the festival to take concrete action in support of [Palestine](241405.md), in the wake of the selection of two Palestinian films (*[Citizen Osama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizen_Osama)* and *[Conversation with the Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conversation_with_the_Sea)*) about the ongoing conflict.[^cite_note-14]

The festival opened with the biographical-drama film *[Ink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ink_\(2026_film\))* by [Danny Boyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Boyle),[^cite_note-\:6-15] and closed with the period-drama film *[God Laughs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/God_Laughs_\(film\)?action=edit\&redlink=1)* by [Giovanni Veronesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Veronesi).[^cite_note-16]
