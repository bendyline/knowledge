**Hassan Ghashghavi** or **Qashqavi** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): حسن قشقاوی) is the current member of [Iranian Parliament](383276.md) representing [Robat Karim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robat_Karim) and [Baharestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baharestan_County) districts since 2024.[^cite_note-1]

He is former Iranian Ambassador to Spain and former Deputy Foreign Minister of [Iran](14653.md) from 2009[^cite_note-2] to 2018.[^cite_note-3] He had previously been Iran's ambassador to Sweden from 2004 to 2008.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] He previously served as Iran's foreign ministry spokesman.[^cite_note-7]
