The **Ministry of Interior and Narcotics Control** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): **وزارتِ داخلہ**, abbreviated as **MoI**) is a [Cabinet-level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Pakistan) ministry of the [Government of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Government), tasked and primarily responsible for implementing the internal policies, state security, administration of internal affairs involving the state and affairs related to narcotics control.

The ministry is led by the [Interior Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior_Minister_of_Pakistan), the [Minister of State for Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior_Minister_of_Pakistan) and the [Interior Secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior_Secretary_of_Pakistan). The Interior Secretary is a [Grade 22 officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grade_22) whereas the Interior Minister is a leading member of the [federal cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Pakistan).

On 11 February 2025, Narcotics Control ministry was merged in to Ministry of Interior as an attached department/wing of ministry and now it is called Ministry of Interior and Narcotics Control.

The minister is required to be a member of [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Pakistan). During the martial regimes of Generals [Ayub Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayub_Khan_\(Field_Marshal\)) and [Yahya Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahya_Khan), from 1962 until 1971, the Interior Minister was called Home Affairs Minister.
