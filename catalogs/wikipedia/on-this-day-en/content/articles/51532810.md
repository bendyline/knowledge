**Sir el Gharbiyeh** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): سير الغربية, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Sīr al-Gharbiyeh*) is a municipality just north of the [Litani River](1920925.md), in the [Nabatieh District](12169367.md) in southern [Lebanon](17771.md).

## History

In the 1596 [tax records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daftar), it was named a village, *Sir*, in the [Ottoman](22278.md) *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya)* (subdistrict) of [Sagif](6853436.md) under the *[liwa'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liwa_\(Arabic\))* (district) of [Safad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safed_Sanjak). It has an all-Muslim population of 10 households. The villagers paid a fixed tax rate of 25% on agricultural products, such as [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), olive trees, goats, and beehives, in addition to "occasional revenues"; a total of 2,000 *[akçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e)*.[^cite_note-Hutteroth-1][^cite_note-2]

On 23 February 1985, during [its invasion of Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_Lebanon_War), the [Israeli Army](39237.md) shot dead seven young men from the village. Six of the dead were aged between fifteen and twenty. They were chosen after a round-up of all the village men and machine-gunned in the legs, two were bayoneted in the abdomen and one was held underwater until he drowned.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Demographics

In 2014, [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.63% of registered voters in Sir el Gharbiyeh. 98.76% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-5]

## Notable people

[Hassan Maatouk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Maatouk), the [Lebanon national football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanon_national_football_team) all-time goalscorer and most-capped player, is native to Sir el Gharbiyeh.[^cite_note-6]
