**Bright Angel Creek** is a creek located at the bottom of the [Grand Canyon National Park](46986.md) flowing into the [Colorado River](180968.md) at the end of the [North Kaibab Trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Kaibab_Trail) on the north side of the river. The creek originates from Roaring Springs that emerge from a cliff along the North Kaibab Trail, and drains to the Colorado.[^cite_note-usgs1926-1] The North Kaibab Trail follows the creek, including through "the Box," a narrow, high-walled part of the trail that becomes very hot in daylight during the summer months (April to October).
