**First Lady** or **First Gentleman of Gabon**[^cite_note-whitehouse-1] is the title held by the spouse of the [president of Gabon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Gabon). The current [first lady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_lady) is Zita Nyangue Oligui Nguema, wife of Brice Oligui, the Transitional President of Gabon.
