**Das Island** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): جزيرة داس) is an Emirati island in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md). It lies about 100 miles (160 km) north-west of the UAE mainland; Qatar is also close by. It covers approximately 0.75 miles (1.21 km) by 1.5 miles (2.4 km), and was almost rectangular in shape, but was significantly expanded to the south by [artificial additions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_reclamation) for a new runway and new accommodation in 2016.[^cite_note-1]

Das Island is inhabited by about 6,000 oil and gas industry personnel on a rotational schedule. It exports [crude oil](23195.md) and [liquefied natural gas](832128.md) by tankers as far as Japan and Europe. Oil production began after prospecting during 1956-1960 by Abu Dhabi Marine Areas (ADMA), a [joint venture](538782.md) between [BP](18998720.md) and Compagnie Française des Pétroles (later known as [Total](804161.md)). Local exporting companies now all fall under the [Abu Dhabi National Oil Company](7992255.md) (ADNOC) brand, including ADNOC Offshore (Oil), ADNOC LNG (LNG) and other companies. Das Island airport is the only airport on the island, and the runway was expanded and relocated in 2018.

Das Island is a noted breeding site for [turtles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turtle) and [seabirds](147019.md). Despite oil and gas production, turtles still feed safely in the area and Das Island has remained an important landfall for migrant animals.

## History

On 22 November 1976, a [Gulf Air](377403.md) [Short SC.7 Skyvan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_SC.7_Skyvan) cargo plane travelling from [Bahrain](18933277.md) to [Abu Dhabi-Al Bateen Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Bateen_Executive_Airport) experienced an engine failure. There were no passengers on board. The crew abandoned the aircraft off Das Island. The two occupants were rescued while the plane sank.[^cite_note-2]
