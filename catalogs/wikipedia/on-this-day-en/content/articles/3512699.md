**Téra** is a city and capital of the [Tera Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tera_Department), in the [Tillabéri Region](2050923.md) of southwestern [Niger](21373.md). It is situated 175 km north-west of the capital [Niamey](57061.md), close to the border with [Burkina Faso](3470.md). It is mainly inhabited by [Songhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songhai_people_\(subgroup\)), [Fulani](102383.md), [Gourmantche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gourmantche) and [Bouzou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikelan) ethnic groups. The majority of the population are farmers.

## History

The place name Téra comes from the [Gourmanchéma language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gourmanch%C3%A9_language) and means “frog”.[^cite_note-Cadernos_192-1]

After the fall of the [Songhai Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songhai_Empire) in 1591, the area of what later became Téra was one of the places in today's Niger where [Songhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songhai_people) refugees settled under a descendant of the former [Askiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Askiya) ruling dynasty. Abaza, son of Alazi, founded the independent state of Tougana here. Under Abaza's son Marounfa, Tougana was defeated by Gorouol. Marounfa's nephew Ama Kassa eventually founded Téra. The reigns of Ama Kassa's successors Ali Ama, Tienda and Gabélinga were marked by military conflicts with neighboring groups.[^cite_note-2]

Around 1839, Téra and Kokorou were attacked by Silanké, a subgroup of the [Fulani](102383.md) who came from Dori in [Liptako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liptako). Téra called [Tuareg](103054.md) from the left bank of the Niger for help. The Silanké, allied with other Fulani groups and [Kurtey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurtey_people), were defeated in two battles. However, Téra could not get rid of the Tuareg, who caused insecurity in the area around the city. A new war soon broke out: Téra, still in alliance with the Tuareg and the [Mossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossi_people) of Diagourou, lost to Liptako, which took around 2,000 prisoners from Téra. The Tuareg and Mossi soon turned against Téra and drove out the ruler Gabélinga after murdering his nephew Sidi. Gabélinga was able to regain power in Téra one last time in 1878, but was murdered in 1885.[^cite_note-3]

In 1899, Téra came under French military administration as part of the newly created Sinder district (cercle de Sinder). The place was annexed to the new military territory of Niger in 1905. The French set up the first school in town in 1917.[^cite_note-4]

The [Dakar Rally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakar_Rally) passed through Téra in 1986. In 1988, Téra, along with nine other Nigerien towns, received the status of an independent municipality.&#x20;

## Geography

Téra is the capital of the Téra department of the same name, which belongs to the Tillabéri region. The municipality is located on the [Dargol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dargol), a tributary of the [Niger River](21437.md). It borders the neighboring state of [Burkina Faso](3470.md) to the west. The neighboring communities in Niger are [Bankilaré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bankilare) in the north, [Dargol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dargol) and [Kokorou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kokorou) in the east and [Diagourou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diagourou,_Niger) in the south. The northern half of the municipality of Téra is included in the [Sahel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahel), while the southern half is part of the transition zone between the Sahel and [Sudan regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_\(region\)). The municipality consists of an urban and a rural area.

### Climate

Téra has a dry desert climate. The temperature is between 18 °C and 30 °C from November to February and between 36 °C and 44 °C from March to April. Annual rainfall varies from 250 mm to 400 mm.

## Demographics

In the 2012 census, the municipality had 71,201 inhabitants living in 9157 households. Around 30,000 people lived in the urban area.[^cite_note-\:0-5] The municipality is a settlement area of the [Songhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songhai_people), [Tuareg](103054.md) and [Mossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossi_people).

## Economy

Thursday is market day in Téra. Animals – mainly cattle, goats, sheep and domestic birds – are traded by farmers from all corners of the Department. A 2009 US report stated that the Téra and Tillabery regions in Western Niger "are two of the most food-insecure areas of the country".[^cite_note-6]
