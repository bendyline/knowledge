**Garissa County** is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Kenya) in [Kenya](188171.md) located in Eastern Kenya bordering [Somalia](27358.md) to the East, [Wajir County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wajir_County) and [Isiolo County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isiolo_County) to the North, [Tana River County](3378017.md) to the West, [Lamu County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamu_County) to the South and the Indian Ocean. Its capital and largest urban area is [Garissa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garissa).

The county was created by the 2010 [Constitution of Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Kenya) as a new unit of the [devolved government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Kenya). Prior to the 2010 constitution, Garissa County was part of the [North Eastern Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Eastern_Province_\(Kenya\)) which was one of the former provinces of Kenya. The North Eastern Province was carved out of the then [Northern Frontier District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Frontier_District) (NFD) prior to [independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Kenya).

The county had a population of 841,353 at the 2019 Census, and a land area of about 44,753 km<sup>2</sup> (17,279 sq mi).[^cite_note-auto-2] The majority of the population in Garissa County is of Somali ethnic background. This includes subgroups like the Ogaden, Degodia, Ajuran, and others. There are also smaller populations of other ethnic groups.[^cite_note-auto-2]

## Administrative and political units

### Political leadership

[Nathif Jama Adam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathif_Jama_Adam) was elected as the pioneer governor of Garissa in March [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Garissa_local_elections). [Ali Bunow Korane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Bunow_Korane) became the second governor in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Kenyan_general_election) however Nathif was re-elected in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Kenyan_general_election).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Nathif is currently deputised by Abdi Dagane Muhumed.[^cite_note-5]

The current elected [Senator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Kenya) of the county is Sen. Abdul Mohammed Haji[^cite_note-6] while the current [County woman representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_woman_representative) is Hon. Amina Udgoon Siyad.[^cite_note-7]

Every County Government in Kenya has a County Executive Committee (CEC) who are persons the Governor appoints, with the approval of the assembly. ## Demographics

Garissa county had a total population of 841,353 persons of which 458,975 were males, 382,344 females and 34 intersex person. There was a total of 141,394 household with an average size of 5.9 persons per house hold. It has a population density of 19 persons per square kilometre based on the 2019 Kenya Population and Housing Census.[^cite_note-16]

## Education

The county has 347 ECD centres, 224 primary schools, 41 secondary schools, 2 teachers training colleges and 2 public universities.

39.7 per cent of the population can read and write and 57.9 cannot read and write. There is an average of 8.2 per cent literacy level and 74 per cent are illiterate.[^cite_note-\:2-19]

## Transport and communication

A total of 2,700.6 km is classified as road network coverage comprising 1,637.84 km under county government and 1,062.76 km under national government. Of the total road network 420 km is covered by gravelled surface, 2,245.1 km earth surface and .5 km of bitumen surface.[^cite_note-\:2-19]

There are 6 postal services with 2,600 installed letter boxes, 2,496 rented letter boxes, and 104 vacant letter boxes.[^cite_note-\:3-22]

## Trade and commerce

The main crops grown are maize, greengrams, sorghum, rice, cowpeas, bananas, mangoes, pawpaw, water melon, tomatoes, capsicum and onions. Farmers have an average size of 1.5 hectares for small scale and 20 hectares for large scale. cattle (boran), goats (galla), sheep (black headed Persian) and camel (dromedary one humped) are kept as the main livestock for producing key products of meat, milk, hides and skins.[^cite_note-\:3-22][^cite_note-\:1-21]
