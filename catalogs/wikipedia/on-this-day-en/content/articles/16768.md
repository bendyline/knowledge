**Kevin Spacey Fowler** (born July 26, 1959)[^cite_note-1] is an American actor. Known for [his work on stage and screen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Spacey_on_screen_and_stage), he [has received numerous accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Kevin_Spacey), including two [Academy Awards](324.md), a [BAFTA Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award), a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award), a [Tony Award](54741.md), and two [Laurence Olivier Awards](16432264.md) as well as nominations for twelve [Primetime Emmy Awards](6579025.md). He was named an honorary [Knight Commander of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) in 2015.

Spacey began his film career with small roles in [Mike Nichols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Nichols)'s comedy-drama films *[Heartburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heartburn_\(film\))* (1986) and *[Working Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_Girl)* (1988). He won two Academy Awards: [Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor) for playing a con man in *[The Usual Suspects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Usual_Suspects)* (1995) and [Best Actor](23245410.md) for playing a suburban husband and father going through a [midlife crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midlife_crisis) in *[American Beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Beauty_\(1999_film\))* (1999). His other films include *[Glengarry Glen Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glengarry_Glen_Ross_\(film\))* (1992), *[Outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outbreak_\(1995_film\))* (1995), *[Se7en](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Se7en)* (1995), *[A Time to Kill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Time_to_Kill_\(1996_film\))* (1996), *[L.A. Confidential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.A._Confidential_\(film\))* (1997), *[Pay It Forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pay_It_Forward_\(film\))* (2000), *[Superman Returns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superman_Returns)* (2006), *[21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21_\(2008_film\))* (2008), *[Margin Call](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margin_Call)* (2011), *[Horrible Bosses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horrible_Bosses)* (2011), and *[Baby Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_Driver)* (2017). He has also directed the films *[Albino Alligator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albino_Alligator)* (1996) and *[Beyond the Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyond_the_Sea_\(2004_film\))* (2004).

In Broadway theatre, Spacey starred in a revival of [Eugene O'Neill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_O'Neill)'s *[Long Day's Journey into Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Day's_Journey_into_Night)* in 1986. He won a Tony Award in 1991 for his role in *[Lost in Yonkers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_in_Yonkers)*. He won a [Laurence Olivier Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award) for his performance in a revival of *[The Iceman Cometh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Iceman_Cometh)* in 1999. Spacey portrayed the [title role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_III_of_England) in *[Richard III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_III_\(play\))* in 2011 and [Clarence Darrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarence_Darrow) in a [West End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_\(theatre\)) production of *Darrow* in 2015. He was the artistic director of the [Old Vic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Vic) theatre in London from 2004 to 2015, for which he received the [Society of London Theatre Special Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_London_Theatre_Special_Award). In 2017, he hosted the [71st Tony Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/71st_Tony_Awards).

In television, Spacey portrayed [Ron Klain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Klain) in *[Recount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recount_\(film\))* (2008) and produced *[Bernard and Doris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_and_Doris)* (2008), both for [HBO Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO_Films). From 2013 to 2017, he starred as [Frank Underwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Underwood_\(House_of_Cards\)) in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix) political drama series *[House of Cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Cards_\(American_TV_series\))*, which won him a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Television_Series_Drama) and two consecutive [Screen Actors Guild Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Male_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series) for Best Actor.[^cite_note-2] Both Spacey and the show itself were nominated for five consecutive [Primetime Emmy Awards](6579025.md) for [Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series) and [Outstanding Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Drama_Series), respectively.

In 2017, Spacey faced various [allegations of sexual misconduct](57139383.md). During 2018 and 2019, he faced multiple criminal investigations. However, in each case Spacey was either not charged or the charges against him were dropped, due to reasons such as death, refusal to testify, or statutes of limitations.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] He has denied the accusations and was found not liable in a 2022 lawsuit in New York. In a separate case in London, he was acquitted by a jury of sexual assault charges in 2023.[^cite_note-nbcnews.com-6][^cite_note-cnn.com-7]

## Early life, family and education



Spacey's 1977 high school yearbook photo

Kevin Spacey Fowler was born in [South Orange, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Orange,_New_Jersey), to Kathleen Ann (*née* Knutson), a secretary, and Thomas Geoffrey Fowler, a [technical writer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_writer) and data consultant.[^cite_note-name-8][^cite_note-dlrehioho-9] His family relocated to [Southern California](62520.md) when he was four years old.[^cite_note-bio-10] Spacey has a sister and an older brother, Randy Fowler, from whom Spacey is [estranged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_estrangement).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-Randy_Fowler_Memoir-12][^cite_note-Estranged_Brothers-13] His brother has stated that their father, whom he described as a racist "[Nazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi) supporter", was [sexually](18721790.md) and [physically abusive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physically_abusive), and that Spacey shut down emotionally and became "very sly and smart" to avoid beatings.[^cite_note-independent-14] Spacey first addressed the matter in October 2022, saying that his father was "a [white supremacist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_supremacist) and a [neo-Nazi](54361.md)" who would call him "an [F-word](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faggot_\(slang\)) that is very derogatory to the gay community".[^cite_note-LangFerme-15][^cite_note-16] He stated that, as a result, he became extremely private about his personal life and did not [come out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_out) as gay earlier in his life.[^cite_note-LangFerme-15] Spacey had previously described his father as "a very normal, middle-class man".[^cite_note-17]

Spacey attended Northridge Military Academy, [Canoga Park High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canoga_Park_High_School) in the 10th and 11th grades. He graduated [co-valedictorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valedictorian) (along with [Mare Winningham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mare_Winningham)) of the class of 1977 of [Chatsworth High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatsworth_High_School) in [Chatsworth, Los Angeles](336125.md), California.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] At Chatsworth, Spacey starred in the school's senior production of *[The Sound of Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sound_of_Music)*, playing the part of [Captain Georg von Trapp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Ludwig_von_Trapp), with Winningham as [Maria von Trapp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_von_Trapp). He started using his middle name "Spacey", which was his paternal grandmother's maiden name.[^cite_note-dlrehioho-9][^cite_note-soroff-21] Spacey had tried to succeed as a comedian for several years before attending the [Juilliard School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juilliard_School) in New York City, as a member of [Group 12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Juilliard_School_people), where he studied drama with teacher [Marian Seldes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marian_Seldes)[^cite_note-22] between 1979 and 1981, though he did not graduate.[^cite_note-23] During this time period, he performed comedy in [bowling alley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowling_alley) talent contests.[^cite_note-abc-24]

## Career

### 1981–1989: Early roles and breakthrough



Spacey and [Liv Ullmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liv_Ullmann) on the set of *[Ghosts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghosts_\(play\))* at the [Eisenhower Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisenhower_Theater) in 1982

Spacey's first professional stage appearance was as a [spear carrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spear_carrier) in a [New York Shakespeare Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Shakespeare_Festival) performance of *[Henry VI, Part 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_VI,_Part_1)* in 1981.[^cite_note-25] The following year, he made his first [Broadway](725252.md) appearance, as Oswald in a production of [Henrik Ibsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henrik_Ibsen)'s *[Ghosts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghosts_\(play\))*, starring [Liv Ullmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liv_Ullmann) and director [John Neville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Neville_\(actor\)), which opened at the [Eisenhower Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisenhower_Theater) in Washington's [Kennedy Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Center).[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] He then portrayed Philinte in [Molière](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moli%C3%A8re)'s *[The Misanthrope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Misanthrope)*. In 1984, Spacey appeared in a production of [David Rabe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Rabe)'s *[Hurlyburly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurlyburly)*, in which he rotated through each of the male parts (he would later play Mickey in the [film version](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurlyburly_\(film\))). Next came [Anton Chekhov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Chekhov)'s *[The Seagull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Seagull)* alongside [David Strathairn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Strathairn) and [Colleen Dewhurst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colleen_Dewhurst). In 1986, Spacey appeared in a production of *[Sleuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleuth_\(play\))* in a [New Jersey](21648.md) [dinner theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinner_theatre).[^cite_note-cornucopia-28]

Spacey's prominence as a stage actor began in 1986, when he was cast opposite [Jack Lemmon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lemmon), [Peter Gallagher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Gallagher), and [Bethel Leslie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethel_Leslie), as Jamie, the eldest Tyrone son, in [Jonathan Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Miller)'s lauded production of [Eugene O'Neill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_O'Neill)'s *[Long Day's Journey into Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Day's_Journey_into_Night)*. Lemmon in particular would become a mentor to Spacey[^cite_note-parade-29] and was invited, along with Spacey's high school drama teacher, to be present when Spacey received his star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame) in 1999.[^cite_note-30] In 1986, Spacey made his first film appearance in [Mike Nichols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Nichols)'s *[Heartburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heartburn_\(film\))* starring [Meryl Streep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meryl_Streep) and [Jack Nicholson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Nicholson). Spacey plays a small role credited as a subway thief. In 1988, Spacey also briefly appeared in another Nichols film, *[Working Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_Girl)*, as businessman Bob Speck. Some of Spacey's other early roles include a widowed millionaire on *[L.A. Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.A._Law)*; the television miniseries *[The Murder of Mary Phagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Murder_of_Mary_Phagan)* (1988), opposite Lemmon; and the comedy *[See No Evil, Hear No Evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/See_No_Evil,_Hear_No_Evil_\(film\))* (1989). In 1987, Spacey made his first major television appearance in the second-season premiere of *[Crime Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_Story_\(U.S._TV_series\))*, playing a [Kennedy-esque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_family) American senator. That same year he appeared in spy thriller series *[The Equalizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Equalizer_\(1985_TV_series\))* as Detective Sergeant Cole in the episode "Solo". He earned a fan base after playing the [manic depressive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manic_depressive) arms dealer Mel Profitt on the television series *[Wiseguy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiseguy_\(TV_series\))* (1988).[^cite_note-31]

### 1990–1999: Stardom and acclaim



Spacey in 1996

Spacey remained actively involved in the live theatre community. In 1991, he won a [Tony Award](54741.md) for his portrayal of Uncle Louie in [Neil Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Simon)'s Broadway hit *[Lost in Yonkers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_in_Yonkers)*. His father was unconvinced that Spacey could make a career for himself as an actor and did not change his mind until Spacey became well known.[^cite_note-abc-24] Spacey quickly developed a reputation as a [character actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character_actor), and was cast in bigger roles, including the malevolent office manager in the ensemble film adaptation of the [David Mamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Mamet) play *[Glengarry Glen Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glengarry_Glen_Ross_\(film\))* (1992), as well as news reporter Harry Kingsley in a film based on a 1917 [dog sled race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dog_sled_race) *[Iron Will](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Will)* (1994) directed by [Charles Haid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Haid). Spacey played one-half of a bickering [Connecticut](6466.md) couple alongside [Judy Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judy_Davis) in the [dark comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_humor) Christmas film *[The Ref](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ref)* which also starred [Denis Leary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Leary).(1994),[^cite_note-James-32] and a malicious Hollywood studio boss Buddy Ackerman in the satire *[Swimming with Sharks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_with_Sharks)* (1995).[^cite_note-NYT-33]

Spacey gained prominence for his role as the enigmatic criminal [Verbal Kint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbal_Kint) in [Bryan Singer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Singer)'s [neo-noir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-noir) film *[The Usual Suspects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Usual_Suspects)* (1995), in an ensemble cast which included [Gabriel Byrne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Byrne), [Benicio del Toro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benicio_del_Toro), and [Chazz Palminteri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chazz_Palminteri). Todd McCarthy of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\))* wrote, "Spacey is terrific as the mysterious weak link in the chain of thieves".[^cite_note-34] The role won him the [Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor). He was also nominated for the [Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actor – Motion Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture) and [Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Actor in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Male_Actor_in_a_Supporting_Role).[^cite_note-tca-35] That same year, Spacey also appeared in the [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher) detective thriller *[Seven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_\(1995_film\))* (1995) starring [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) and [Morgan Freeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_Freeman). Spacey made a sudden entrance late in the film as [serial killer](42626.md) John Doe after going uncredited and unmentioned in the film's advertisements and opening credits. His work in *Seven*, *The Usual Suspects* and *[Outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outbreak_\(1995_film\))* earned him Best Supporting Actor honors at the 1995 [Society of Texas Film Critics Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Texas_Film_Critics_Awards_1995).[^cite_note-aas951230-36][^cite_note-ac960105-37]

Spacey played an egomaniacal [district attorney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_attorney) in *[A Time to Kill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Time_to_Kill_\(1996_film\))* (1996) and founded [Trigger Street Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigger_Street_Productions) in 1997 with the purpose of producing and developing entertainment across various media. Spacey made his directorial debut with the film *[Albino Alligator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albino_Alligator)* (1996). The film was a [box-office bomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box-office_bomb), grossing \$339,379 with a budget of \$6 million, but critics praised Spacey's direction. He starred in the [Curtis Hanson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_Hanson)-directed neo-noir crime film *[L.A. Confidential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.A._Confidential_\(film\))* (1997) acting alongside [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe), [Guy Pearce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Pearce), and [Kim Basinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Basinger). Spacey played the Detective Sergeant Jack Vincennes, an officer and technical advisor for a fictional TV police drama series. The ensemble cast received praise with Jack Matthews of *[Newsday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsday)* citing Spacey as the highlight writing, "Best of all are Spacey striking an impossible balance of smarminess and charm".[^cite_note-38] The role landed Spacey a nomination for the [BAFTA Award for Best Actor in a Leading Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role). The following year he voiced the intimidating grasshopper, Hopper in the animated [Pixar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixar) film *[A Bug's Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Bug's_Life)* (1998).[^cite_note-cornucopia-28] Throughout his career, Spacey has been well known for playing villains; he remarked in 2013: "I think people just like me evil for some reason. They want me to be a son of a bitch."[^cite_note-39] Spacey hosted *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live)* twice, first in 1997 with musical guest [Beck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beck) and special guests [Michael Palin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Palin) and [John Cleese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cleese) from *[Monty Python's Flying Circus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monty_Python's_Flying_Circus)*.[^cite_note-C-40]

In 1999, Spacey acted alongside [Annette Bening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annette_Bening) in [Sam Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Mendes)'s *[American Beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Beauty_\(1999_film\))*. In the film he played the role of Lester Burnham, a depressed suburban father and advertising executive who becomes attracted to his teenage daughter's best friend. The film and its performance received widespread acclaim, particularly Spacey, with critic [Roger Ebert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Ebert) declaring, "Spacey, an actor who embodies intelligence in his eyes and voice, is the right choice for Lester Burnham. He does reckless and foolish things in this movie, but he doesn't deceive himself; he knows he's running wild--and chooses to, burning up the future years of an empty lifetime for a few flashes of freedom".[^cite_note-41] For this role, Spacey won his second [Academy Award](324.md). In his acceptance speech, he dedicated his Oscar to Jack Lemmon, praising him as an influence, mentor, and father figure. He also stated, "\[Lemmon's] performance in *[The Apartment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Apartment)* stands as one of the finest we've ever had".[^cite_note-academy-42][^cite_note-43] For his role, he also won the [BAFTA Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role) and [Screen Actors Guild Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award) and received a [Golden Globe Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Drama) nomination. That same year, he was honored with a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame.[^cite_note-44] Also in 1999, Spacey won the [Laurence Olivier Award for Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Actor) and received his second [Tony Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Play) nomination in 1999 for *[The Iceman Cometh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Iceman_Cometh)*.[^cite_note-45]

### 2000–2009: Established actor and work at the Old Vic

Spacey played a physically and emotionally scarred grade school teacher in *[Pay It Forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pay_It_Forward_\(film\))* (2000), a patient in a mental institution who may or may not be an [alien](9588.md) in *[K-Pax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-Pax)* (2001), and singer [Bobby Darin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Darin) in *[Beyond the Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyond_the_Sea_\(2004_film\))* (2004). The latter was a lifelong dream project for Spacey, who took on co-writing, directing, co-producing and starring duties in the biography/musical about Darin's life, career and relationship with actress [Sandra Dee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Dee). Facing little interest for backing in the U.S., Spacey went to the United Kingdom and Germany for funding. Almost all of the film was made in Berlin.[^cite_note-parade-29] Spacey provided his own vocals on the film's soundtrack and appeared in several tribute concerts around the time of its release. Spacey received mostly positive reviews for his singing, as well as a [Golden Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe) nomination for his performance. However, reviewers debated the age disparity between Spacey and Darin, noting that Spacey was too old to convincingly portray Darin, particularly during the early stages of the singer's life depicted in the film.[^cite_note-46]



Spacey portrayed singer [Bobby Darrin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Darrin) in *[Beyond the Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyond_the_Sea_\(2004_film\))* (2004) which he also directed

In February 2003, Spacey announced that he was to become the artistic director of the [Old Vic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Vic), one of London's oldest theatres.[^cite_note-bbc20030203-47] Appearing at a press conference with [Judi Dench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judi_Dench) and [Elton John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_John), Spacey promised both to appear on stage and to bring in big-name talent.[^cite_note-bbc20030206-48] He undertook to remain in the post for a full ten years. The Old Vic Theatre Company staged shows eight months out of the year. Spacey's first season started in September 2004, opening with the British premiere of the play *[Cloaca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloaca_\(film\))* by [Maria Goos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Goos), directed by Spacey, which opened to mixed reviews.[^cite_note-firstseason-49] In the 2005 season, Spacey made his UK Shakespearean debut, to good notices, in the [title role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_II_of_England) of *[Richard II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_II_\(play\))*, directed by [Trevor Nunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trevor_Nunn).[^cite_note-firstseason-49]

In 2006, Spacey played [Lex Luthor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lex_Luthor) in the Bryan Singer [superhero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superhero) film *[Superman Returns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superman_Returns)* starring [Brandon Routh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Routh). He was to return for its 2009 sequel, but the series was instead rebooted with the 2013 film *[Man of Steel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_of_Steel_\(film\))*.[^cite_note-50] Spacey also appeared in *[Edison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edison_\(film\))*, which received a [direct-to-video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct-to-video) release in 2006.[^cite_note-51] The film was released in theaters in [Netherlands](21148.md) on March 12, 2006. In December 2007, Spacey co-hosted the [Nobel Peace Prize Concert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Peace_Prize_Concert) along with [Uma Thurman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uma_Thurman).[^cite_note-56] In 2008 Spacey starred as [Ron Klain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Klain) in the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO) original political drama film *[Recount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recount_\(film\))* revolving around [Florida's vote recount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_United_States_presidential_election_recount_in_Florida) during the [2000 United States presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_United_States_presidential_election) between [George W. Bush](3414021.md) and [Al Gore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Gore). The television film won three [Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award), including [Outstanding Television Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Television_Movie). ### 2010–2017: *House of Cards* and other roles



Spacey at the [Berlin Film Festival](18948039.md) 2011

In early 2010, Spacey went to China to star in writer-director [Dayyan Eng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayyan_Eng)'s [black comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_comedy) film *[Inseparable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inseparable_\(film\))*, becoming the first Hollywood actor to star in a fully Chinese-funded film.[^cite_note-60] In 2011, Spacey starred in [J.C. Chandor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.C._Chandor)'s financial thriller *[Margin Call](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margin_Call)* alongside [Paul Bettany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Bettany), [Jeremy Irons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Irons), [Zachary Quinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zachary_Quinto), [Demi Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Moore), and [Stanley Tucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Tucci). The story at large takes place over a 24-hour period at a large [Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street) [investment bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investment_bank) during the initial stages of the [2008 financial crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_financial_crisis). The film, which focuses on the actions taken by a group of employees during the subsequent financial collapse, made its debut at the [Sundance Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival) to critical acclaim. Spacey received the [Independent Spirit Robert Altman Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Spirit_Robert_Altman_Award) along with the cast. That same year, Spacey co-starred in the black comedy film *[Horrible Bosses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horrible_Bosses)*, which grossed over \$209.6 million at the box office.[^cite_note-61]

On March 18, 2011, it was announced that Spacey was cast as [Frank Underwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Underwood_\(House_of_Cards\)) in the [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix) series *[House of Cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Cards_\(American_TV_series\))*, adapted from a 1990 [BBC](19344654.md) [political drama of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Cards_\(British_TV_series\)).[^cite_note-62] He was nominated for the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series) at the [65th Primetime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/65th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards) in 2013, becoming the first lead actor to be Primetime Emmy-nominated from a [web television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_television) series.[^cite_note-NDWi2PEN-63] Spacey went on to win the [Golden Globe award for Best Actor in a Television Series Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Television_Series_Drama) at the [72nd Golden Globe Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/72nd_Golden_Globe_Awards) and [Screen Actors Guild nomination for Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Drama Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Male_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series) at the [21st Screen Actors Guild Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_Screen_Actors_Guild_Awards) for his [season 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Cards_\(season_2\)) performance.[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65] He was fired from the series after the fifth season following multiple allegations of sexual misconduct.[^cite_note-66]



Spacey showing [Maryland governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Maryland) [Martin O'Malley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_O'Malley) around the set of *[House of Cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Cards_\(American_TV_series\))*, May 2013

In 2013 he executive-produced the biographical survival thriller film *[Captain Phillips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_Phillips_\(film\))*, which was nominated for the [Academy Award for Best Picture](61702.md).[^cite_note-oscarnom-67][^cite_note-oscarwin-68] Spacey portrayed the [antagonist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antagonist) Jonathan Irons in the 2014 video game *[Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_of_Duty:_Advanced_Warfare)* through [motion capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_capture).[^cite_note-CoDAW-69] He starred as President [Richard Nixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Nixon) in the comedy-drama *[Elvis & Nixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_&_Nixon)* (2016), which is based on the meeting that took place between Nixon and singer [Elvis Presley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley) ([Michael Shannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Shannon)) in December 1970 wherein Presley requested that Nixon swear him in as an undercover agent in the [Bureau of Narcotics and Dangerous Drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Narcotics_and_Dangerous_Drugs).[^cite_note-70] He next starred in the comedy film *[Nine Lives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Lives_\(2016_film\))*, as a man trapped in the body of a cat.[^cite_note-71] The film was released on August 5, 2016.[^cite_note-72] Spacey is well known in Hollywood for his [impressions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impressionist_\(entertainment\)). In January 2016 it was announced that [Relativity Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relativity_Media), which was just emerging from [Chapter 11 bankruptcy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapter_11_bankruptcy),[^cite_note-74] had acquired Trigger Street Productions and that Spacey would become chairman of [Relativity Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relativity_Media#Relativity_Studios), while Dana Brunetti would become the studio's president.[^cite_note-75] Spacey called the move "an incredible opportunity to make great entertainment" and said he considered it the "next evolution in my career".[^cite_note-76] However, when the paperwork for the studio was filed for the court, it emerged that Spacey had opted out of assuming the chairmanship of the studios,[^cite_note-77] and by the end of 2016 Brunetti had also left Relativity, while both remained executive producers on *House of Cards* and *[Manifesto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhunt:_Unabomber)*.[^cite_note-78]

On June 16, 2016, Spacey was awarded an [honorary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Special\:Search/honorary) [knighthood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knighthood) for his services to theatre, arts education, and international culture in the [2015 Queen's Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Queen's_Birthday_Honours).[^cite_note-79] The honour, [Knight Commander of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire), was given at [Clarence House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarence_House) by then-[Prince Charles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Charles). As a non-[Commonwealth Realm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Realm) citizen, the award is honorary and he is not entitled to the [honorific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorific) "Sir".[^cite_note-80] Spacey had previously been awarded the lesser rank of honorary Commander of the Order of the British Empire (CBE) for services to drama in 2010.[^cite_note-81] Spacey was a patron of the [Shakespeare Schools Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakespeare_Schools_Festival), a charity that enables school children across the UK to perform Shakespeare in professional theatres. He also sits on the board of directors of the [Motion Picture and Television Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_and_Television_Fund).[^cite_note-82]

### 2017–present: Career controversy and comeback attempts

In March 2017, it was announced that Spacey would portray [J. But because of the [sexual assault](218407.md) allegations against Spacey, it was announced on November 8, 2017, that all of his footage would be excised,[^cite_note-84] and that [Christopher Plummer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Plummer) would replace him in [reshoots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reshoots).[^cite_note-DeadlineA_081117-85][^cite_note-86] Despite the very tight schedule, [TriStar Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TriStar_Pictures) completed the new version of the film in time for a December 25 release.[^cite_note-DeadlineA_081117-85][^cite_note-87] Spacey appeared in the film *[Billionaire Boys Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billionaire_Boys_Club_\(2018_film\))*, which had a [limited release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_release) on August 17, 2018.[^cite_note-88][^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90] [Vertical Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_Entertainment) stated that it would take no action to remove Spacey from the film, as it had been completed in late 2016, before the allegations made in October 2017.[^cite_note-91] Following the allegations leveled against him, Spacey maintained a lower profile and his career stalled.[^cite_note-92][^cite_note-93]

In November 2025, Spacey performed a cabaret show titled *Songs & Stories* at the Parklane Resort in [Limassol](57577.md), Cyprus.[^cite_note-107] In an interview with *[The Daily Telegraph](23797577.md)* regarding the performance, Spacey stated that he was "living in hotels" and performing where he could find work due to his financial situation following his legal battles.[^cite_note-108] In December 2025, Spacey starred in an Italian comedy television series titled *Minimarket* on [RaiPlay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RaiPlay).[^cite_note-109]

## Personal life

### Sexuality and relationships

&#x20;Spacey briefly dated American actress [April Winchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_Winchell) after she graduated from high school in the early 1980s.[^cite_note-113][^cite_note-114] An article in *[The Sunday Times Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine)* in 1999 stated that Spacey's "love affair with acting, and the absence of a visible partner in the life of an attractive 40-year-old, has resulted in *[Esquire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esquire_\(magazine\))* magazine asserting two years ago that he must be gay".[^cite_note-Personal-115] Spacey responded to the rumors by telling *[Playboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playboy)* and other interviewers that he was not gay,[^cite_note-116] and by telling Lesley White of *[The Sunday Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sunday_Times)*:

> I chose for a long time not to answer these questions because of the manner in which they were asked, and because I was never talking to someone I trusted, so why should I? Recently I chose to participate because it's a little hard on the people I love.[^cite_note-Personal-115]

&#x20;In 1999, reports suggested that Spacey was dating a [script supervisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Script_supervisor) named Dianne Dreyer, with their relationship possibly dating back to 1992.[^cite_note-Gotham-117][^cite_note-118][^cite_note-119] In 2000, Spacey brought Dreyer to the Academy Awards;[^cite_note-Gotham-117] during the acceptance speech for his Best Actor award, Spacey said, "Dianne, thank you for teaching me about caring about the right things, and I love you."[^cite_note-academy-42] In 2007, *[Gotham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gotham_\(magazine\))* magazine quoted Spacey saying:

> I've never believed in pimping my personal life out for publicity. Although I might be interested in doing it, I will never do it. People can gossip all they want; they can speculate all they want. I just happen to believe that there's a separation between the public life and the private life. Everybody has the right to a private life no matter what their professions are.[^cite_note-Personal-115]

In 2017, Spacey [came out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Came_out) as gay in a statement denying a sexual misconduct allegation.[^cite_note-120][^cite_note-121][^cite_note-122]

### Political views and activism



Spacey speaking during the 2010 [Clinton Global Citizen Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton_Foundation#Clinton_Global_Citizen_Awards)

Spacey is a [Democrat](5043544.md) and has been described as [left-leaning](18499.md).[^cite_note-123][^cite_note-124] Spacey worked on [Jimmy Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Carter)'s [1976 presidential campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Carter_1976_presidential_campaign).[^cite_note-125] He is a friend of former President [Bill Clinton](3356.md), having met Clinton before his presidency began. Spacey once described Clinton as "one of the shining lights" in the political process.[^cite_note-abc-24] He additionally made a [cameo appearance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameo_appearance) in the short film *President Clinton: Final Days*, a light-hearted [political satire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_satire) produced by the [Clinton Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton_Administration) for the 2000 [White House Correspondents Dinner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Correspondents_Dinner).[^cite_note-newyorktimes-126]

Spacey has undertaken activism in the domain of [HIV/AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV/AIDS). In 2002, he and fellow actor [Chris Tucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Tucker) accompanied Bill Clinton on a trip throughout several African countries to promote AIDS awareness on the continent.[^cite_note-127] The trip gained renewed attention in 2019 amidst the [sex trafficking charges](6253522.md#Legal_issues) against [Jeffrey Epstein](6253522.md), whose plane was used.[^cite_note-128] Epstein's pilot Larry Visoski confirmed that Spacey flew on Epstein's jet.[^cite_note-129] Spacey also participated in several fundraisers for HIV/AIDS healthcare, including [amfAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AmfAR) Cinema Against AIDS in 2016[^cite_note-130] and the 25th Annual [Elton John AIDS Foundation Academy Award Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_John_AIDS_Foundation_Academy_Award_Party) in 2017.[^cite_note-131]

In September 2006, Spacey said that he intended to take up [British citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_citizenship) when it is offered to him.[^cite_note-telegraph-132] When asked about the UK's [2016 European Union membership referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_European_Union_membership_referendum), Spacey replied, "I appreciate you asking me the question, but I am not a British citizen; I am a resident of Great Britain. And I have never in my twelve years ever gotten involved in politics in Great Britain. I think it's inappropriate for me as a, really as a guest, in Great Britain, so I'll leave that to the British people."[^cite_note-133]

Spacey met Venezuelan president [Hugo Chávez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Ch%C3%A1vez) in September 2007 but never spoke to the press about their encounter. During the trip, he visited the Venezuelan film studio [Villa del Cine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villa_del_Cine).[^cite_note-134] In March 2011, following Belarusian president [Alexander Lukashenko](156589.md)'s crackdown on the [Belarusian democracy movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusian_democracy_movement), Spacey joined [Jude Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Law) in a street protest in London against Lukashenko's regime.[^cite_note-135]

In October 2008, Spacey started the Kevin Spacey Foundation in the UK to encourage youth involvement in the arts.[^cite_note-136] Headquartered in [England and Wales](275187.md), its purpose was to provide grants to individuals and organizations to help young people study the arts, particularly theatre.[^cite_note-137] The charity shut down in February 2018 following sexual misconduct allegations against Spacey.[^cite_note-138]

In May 2024, Spacey endorsed [Robert F. Kennedy Jr.](522298.md), who was [running as an independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_F._Kennedy_Jr._2024_presidential_campaign), in the [2024 United States presidential election](59568546.md).[^cite_note-139] In December 2024, Spacey visited the [Israel–Gaza border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93Gaza_border) and toured areas impacted by [Hamas](13913.md)' 2023 [October 7 attacks](74998317.md). He was accompanied by pro-Israel journalist [Douglas Murray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Murray_\(author\)). Spacey was reportedly visiting the daughter of a friend, who is serving in the [Israel Defence Forces](39237.md).[^cite_note-140][^cite_note-141]

In July 2025, Spacey called for the release of the [Epstein files](80415168.md), stating, "For those of us with nothing to fear, the truth can't come soon enough." When some of the files were released in December 2025, Spacey himself was present in some photographs.[^cite_note-142]

## Sexual misconduct allegations

Main article: [Kevin Spacey sexual misconduct allegations](57139383.md)

In October 2017, Spacey faced various allegations of sexual misconduct.[^cite_note-143][^cite_note-144][^cite_note-145][^cite_note-146] He was immediately fired from his role of Frank Underwood in Netflix’s hit series *[House of Cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Cards_\(American_TV_series\))* and removed from the film *[All the Money in the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_the_Money_in_the_World)* by director [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott).[^cite_note-147][^cite_note-148]

Over the course of the next several years, Spacey faced a variety of civil and criminal cases, none of which resulted in civil liability or a criminal conviction.[^cite_note-kevin_spacey_found-149][^cite_note-actor_kevin_spacey-150]

In 2019, the state of Massachusetts brought charges for indecent assault and battery against Spacey. The case was later dropped by the prosecutor after the complainant’s mother—former Boston news anchor Heather Unruh—admitted to tampering with evidence by deleting content from her son’s phone.[^cite_note-151]

In 2022, Spacey was found not liable in a civil suit brought by Anthony Rapp in New York, leading a judge to order Rapp to pay Spacey’s legal bills.[^cite_note-kevin_spacey_found-149]

In a criminal case in London in 2023, Spacey faced nine sexual assault charges stemming from four separate men. The jury found Spacey not guilty on all counts.[^cite_note-actor_kevin_spacey-150]

Spacey denied all charges and has never been found guilty of a crime.[^cite_note-kevin_spacey_found-149][^cite_note-actor_kevin_spacey-150]
