**Omar Bravo Tordecillas** (born 4 March 1980) is a Mexican [football manager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manager_\(association_football\)) and former [footballer](10568.md) who played as a [striker](428681.md). Over the course of three spells with [Guadalajara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.D._Guadalajara), he made more than 400 appearances and scored 160 goals in all competitions, to become the club's all-time leading scorer.[^cite_note-2]&#x20;

## Club career

### First spell with Guadalajara

Bravo was brought to the Guadalajara youth system by [José Luis Real](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Luis_Real). He made his debut with the senior team under manager [Jorge Dávalos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_D%C3%A1valos) on 17 February 2001 against [Tigres UANL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tigres_UANL) in a 0–0 draw.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Bravo became a regular starter for the team during the 2002 Apertura,[^cite_note-5] playing 15 games and scoring 6 goals. He won the tournament's Best Rookie award.[^cite_note-6]&#x20;

Bravo finished the [2005 Clausura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primera_Divisi%C3%B3n_de_M%C3%A9xico_Clausura_2005) as joint-second top goalscorer of the league with 12 goals. His performances helped him win the tournament's Best Forward award.[^cite_note-7]&#x20;

In 2006, Bravo participated in a successful season for Guadalajara, helping the team win the [ Apertura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primera_Divisi%C3%B3n_de_M%C3%A9xico_Apertura_2006) championship title, Bravo scored the leading goal in the first leg match of the Apertura finals, which ended in a 1–1 draw against [Toluca](1361541.md), winning 3–2 on aggregate.[^cite_note-8] Bravo finished as the club's top scorer for the tournament with 8 goals, helping him win the tournament's Best Forward award.[^cite_note-ESPN-9]&#x20;

Bravo scored in his first appearance in the [CONCACAF Champions' Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_CONCACAF_Champions%27_Cup) against [W Connection F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_Connection_F.C.) as well as in the first match of the semifinals against [D.C. United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.C._United). In the first leg of final, on 8 April 2007, he scored a brace against [Pachuca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CF_Pachuca) to a 2–2 draw. Guadalajara lost the finals following a penalty shootout. He finished as joint-top scorer with four goals in the tournament. For the [2007 Clausura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primera_Divisi%C3%B3n_de_M%C3%A9xico_Clausura_2007), he won the Golden Boot for most goals in the league with 11 goals in 17 matches,[^cite_note-10] winning the Best Forward award again.[^cite_note-ESPN-9]&#x20;

On 26 April 2008, Bravo scored his 100th goal in a 4–0 victory over [Puebla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Puebla), becoming the 2nd player in the club's history to reach 100 goals, only behind [Salvador Reyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvador_Reyes_Monte%C3%B3n), who has 122 goals.[^cite_note-11] On 17 May, he played his last match of his first stint with Guadalajara scoring a goal against [Monterrey](547412.md) in the [Clausura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primera_Divisi%C3%B3n_de_M%C3%A9xico_Clausura_2008) quarter-finals, which they tied 4–4, losing 8–5 on aggregate.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]&#x20;

### Deportivo de La Coruña



Bravo playing for Deportivo la Coruña

On 22 May 2008, [Deportivo de La Coruña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deportivo_de_La_Coru%C3%B1a) announced an agreement with Bravo for the next three years as he left as a free agent.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] Bravo became the second Mexican on Deportivo's squad at that time, after [Andrés Guardado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Guardado). Bravo made his [league](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga) debut on 31 August in a 2–1 victory over [Real Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_C.F.). On 8 December he scored his first league goal against [Málaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1laga_CF) as a [penalty kick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_kick_\(association_football\)) in a 2–0 victory.[^cite_note-16] With only nine league appearances (only two of them as a starter), it was evident that his stint at the Spanish club was not as successful as expected.&#x20;

#### Loan to Tigres UANL

On 5 March 2009, he was loaned out to Mexico side [Tigres UANL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tigres_UANL) for the remainder of the [2009 Clausura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primera_Divisi%C3%B3n_de_M%C3%A9xico_Clausura_2009). He made 6 appearances and scored no goals.&#x20;

### Second spell with Guadalajara

Following his loan stint with Tigres UANL, on 6 August, it was announced Bravo would return to Guadalajara, where he signed a 1-year contract with the club.[^cite_note-17] The following month, Bravo was named [captain](633462.md).[^cite_note-18]&#x20;

On 8 August 2010, [Kansas City Wizards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_Kansas_City) announced that Bravo was signed as the squad's first [Designated Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Designated_Player_Rule) since [Claudio López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudio_L%C3%B3pez_\(footballer\)). Bravo would join Kansas beginning with the [2011 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Major_League_Soccer_season).[^cite_note-19] Bravo remained on loan with *Chivas* for the remainder of the [year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010%E2%80%9311_Mexican_Primera_Divisi%C3%B3n_season#Torneo_Apertura), disputing the [2010 Copa Libertadores finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Copa_Libertadores_finals) against Brazilian club [Internacional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SC_Internacional), where *Chivas* was runner-up.&#x20;

### Sporting Kansas City

Upon arrival in Kansas City, Bravo mentioned that he was very happy to join the team, and that he believed the level of play in MLS was among the best in the world. In his debut for the club against [Chivas USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chivas_USA), he showed off his skilled form and managed to score two goals in a 3–2 victory.[^cite_note-20] He scored nine goals for Kansas and was named MLS Latino Player of the Year.[^cite_note-MLS-21]&#x20;

### Cruz Azul



Bravo with [Cruz Azul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruz_Azul)

On 12 December 2011, Sporting Kansas City announced that Bravo would not be returning in 2012 and would instead be signing with [Cruz Azul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruz_Azul) in his native Mexico.[^cite_note-22] He scored a brace against [San Luis F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Luis_F.C.) in a 3–1 home win. After underperforming with [Cruz Azul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruz_Azul), the club later transferred Bravo to [Atlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlas_F.C.) on a loan. Later, he revealed that he felt he failed with Cruz Azul.[^cite_note-23]&#x20;

#### Loan to Atlas



Bravo with [Atlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlas_F.C.)

In December 2012, Bravo joined [Atlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlas_F.C.), [rivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cl%C3%A1sico_Tapat%C3%ADo) of his original club, C.D. Guadalajara, on loan for the entirety of the following year (for the [2013 Clausura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012%E2%80%9313_Liga_MX_season#Torneo_Clausura) and [2013 Apertura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013%E2%80%9314_Liga_MX_season#Torneo_Apertura)).[^cite_note-24]&#x20;

### Third spell with Guadalajara



Bravo in 2015

On 4 June 2013, it was announced that Bravo would return to Guadalajara in a trade deal that saw [Xavier Báez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavier_B%C3%A1ez) go the other way to Cruz Azul at the conclusion of his loan stint with Atlas.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] In December, Bravo was named captain of the team.[^cite_note-27] On 4 January 2014, on his first match on his third stint with the club, he scored on the 3rd minute against [Santos Laguna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santos_Laguna) in a 1–1 draw.[^cite_note-28]&#x20;

On 12 August 2015, Bravo scored a brace at home against [Morelia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarcas_Morelia) that converted him into Guadalajara's all-time top scorer in league matches, surpassing club legend [Salvador Reyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvador_Reyes_Monte%C3%B3n) with 123 goals.[^cite_note-29] On 26 September, he scored a brace against [rivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_S%C3%BAper_Cl%C3%A1sico_\(Mexico\)) [América](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Am%C3%A9rica) in a 2–1 away victory, surpassing record-holder Salvador Reyes in all goals scored at all competitions.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31]&#x20;

On 28 October, he scored the only goal in the [Apertura Copa MX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apertura_2015_Copa_MX) semi-final against Toluca.[^cite_note-32] The following month, he led the team to win the [Copa MX final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apertura_2015_Copa_MX_final) on 4 November against [León](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Le%C3%B3n) in a 1–0 away victory, attaining their first major title in nine years.[^cite_note-33] Bravo finished the [2015–2016 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_Liga_MX_season) scoring 11 goals in 34 games. On 10 July 2016, Bravo scored his last goal for *Chivas* playing against [Veracruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.D._Veracruz) in the last minute of the game ending in 2–0 win giving them their first [Supercopa MX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercopa_MX) title their second in nine months and clinched Guadalajara a spot for the [2017 Copa Libertadores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Copa_Libertadores).[^cite_note-34]&#x20;

#### Loan to Carolina RailHawks



Bravo with [Carolina Railhawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_FC) in 2016

On 13 July 2016, it was announced that Bravo had signed with NASL club [Carolina RailHawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolina_RailHawks) during halftime of a match against [West Ham United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham_United), becoming the first Mexican player to join the club.[^cite_note-35]&#x20;

#### Loan to Phoenix Rising FC

On 9 February 2017, it was announced that Bravo had signed with recently re-branded [USL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USL_Championship) club [Phoenix Rising FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Rising_FC) (formerly Arizona United).[^cite_note-36] In September, Bravo would no longer be an active player for the club.&#x20;

### Leones Negros UdeG

On July 4, 2019, Leones Negros announced that Bravo would be a guest player for the 45th anniversary game against Monterrey on July 13 and a friendly against [USL League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USL_League_One) side [Forward Madison FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_Madison_FC) on July 16.[^cite_note-37] On July 24 it was announced that Bravo would come out of retirement to play for the [following season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Ascenso_MX_season).[^cite_note-38]&#x20;

## International career

Bravo played for various levels of the Mexico youth national system and in several amateur tournaments, including the [2004 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Summer_Olympics) in [Athens](1216.md).&#x20;

On 11 June 2006, playing at the [2006 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup) in Germany, Bravo scored two goals in Mexico's 3–1 victory over [Iran](272865.md) and was named the [Man of the Match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_of_the_Match).[^cite_note-39] Two matches later, he missed a penalty against [Portugal](576328.md) in a 2–1 loss for Mexico,[^cite_note-40] and did not play in the round-of-16 match against [Argentina](454699.md).&#x20;

In 2007, Bravo was selected by former coach [Hugo Sánchez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_S%C3%A1nchez) to represent his country in a series of friendly international matches and to take part in the [2007 Gold Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Gold_Cup) and [2007 Copa América](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_Am%C3%A9rica_2007), where he scored three goals.&#x20;

Bravo also played in World Cup Qualifiers and in the [2009 CONCACAF Gold Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_CONCACAF_Gold_Cup). He was recalled to the national team in March 2013.&#x20;

## Managerial career

In January 2024, Bravo became manager for [National Independent Soccer Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Independent_Soccer_Association) club [Arizona Monsoon FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_Monsoon_FC).[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42] He left the club during the offseason.[^cite_note-43]&#x20;

## Personal life

Bravo appears on the North American cover of *[FIFA 06](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_06)* alongside [Ronaldinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronaldinho) and [Freddy Adu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freddy_Adu).[^cite_note-44]&#x20;

### Allegations of sexual abuse of a minor

Bravo was arrested on 4 October 2025 in [Zapopan](2107760.md), Jalisco by police on allegations of [sexual abuse](18721790.md) of a minor. He was formally indicted the following day,[^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46] accused of abusing the 17-year-old daughter of his girlfriend over the past six years.[^cite_note-trial-47]&#x20;

On 10 October 2025, a judge ruled that Bravo will stand trial for the charge of child sexual abuse, and ordered a minimum of six-month [pre-trial detention](14632360.md) due to the risks involving bail in a case of sexual abuse. If convicted, Bravo faces a sentence between five and 10 years in prison.[^cite_note-trial-47]&#x20;
