**Kioumars Heydari** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): کیومرث حیدری; born 1964) is an Iranian [Brigadier general](206220.md) who is currently serving as the deputy commander of the [Islamic Republic of Iran Armed Forces](14305018.md) since 2026 and the [Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters](51128846.md) since 2025.[^cite_note-ustr-1] He was Commander of the [Islamic Republic of Iran Ground Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Republic_of_Iran_Ground_Forces).[^cite_note-2]

## Biography

Kioumars Heydari was born in 1964 in the city of [Sahneh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahneh), [Kermanshah](448078.md), and completed his primary and secondary education in Kermanshah. Before joining the [Army](48259216.md), he began his military activities in 1982 during the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War) as a volunteer force in the [Basij](2892082.md). Heydari joined the [Army Ground Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Republic_of_Iran_Ground_Forces) in 1984, starting his official career in the infantry branch of the 81st Armored Division of Kermanshah. Between 1984 and 1998, he gained experience commanding platoons, companies, and battalions within this unit.

Heydari was appointed commander of the South West Headquarters of the Ground Forces (NEZAJA) in 1998 and served in this position until 2005. He received the rank of [Brigadier General](206220.md) in 2005. He briefly held the position of operations deputy for the Army Ground Forces in 2005, and served as the coordinating deputy of the Army Ground Forces (Chief of Staff of NEZAJA) from 2005 to 2008. Heydari was appointed deputy commander of the Army Ground Forces in 2008 and remained in this role until November 2016.

Heydari was appointed as the Commander of the [Islamic Republic of Iran Ground Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Republic_of_Iran_Ground_Forces) in 19 November 2016 serving in this position until 22 November 2025.

In 2022, Heydari was added to the sanctions list of the [OFAC](231045.md) and the [European Union](9317.md), alongside other senior figures in the [Iranian army](48259216.md). In 2023, he was also placed on the UK sanctions list. The sanctions against him and other senior figures stemmed mainly from [violations of human rights](13831.md) and the repression of Iranian citizens in [anti-regime protests of 2018-2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%932019_Iranian_general_strikes_and_protests).

On 13 June 2025, during the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War), Israel reportedly carried out an assassination attempt against Heydari, which he survived. In November 2025, he was appointed deputy commander of the [Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters](51128846.md).[^cite_note-deputy-3]

In August 2026, he was appointed by Supreme Leader [Mojtaba Khamenei](23324096.md) as deputy commander of the [General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran](50548383.md).[^cite_note-4]
