**USS *Frank E. Petersen Jr.* (DDG-121)** is an [*Arleigh Burke*-class](197341.md) ([Flight IIA Technology Insertion](197341.md#Characteristics)) [Aegis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aegis_combat_system) [guided missile destroyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_missile_destroyer) in the [United States Navy](20518076.md).[^cite_note-Flight_IIA_Technology_Insertion-6] The ship was named for [United States Marine Corps](17349325.md) [Lieutenant General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_General) [Frank E. Petersen Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_E._Petersen_Jr.)[^cite_note-7] the first [African-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African-American) Marine Corps [aviator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviator)[^cite_note-8] and the first African-American Marine Corps [general](148537.md).[^cite_note-9] The contract for the ship, along with the name, was first announced in a press release from [Huntington Ingalls Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntington_Ingalls_Industries) on 30 March 2016.[^cite_note-10]

## Construction and career

The first "cutting of steel" took place in April 2016[^cite_note-11] and her [keel was laid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel_laying) on 21 February 2017.[^cite_note-12] She was launched on 13 July 2018.[^cite_note-13] and [christened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_christening) on 6 October 2018.[^cite_note-christen-3] The ship was [commissioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning) on 14 May 2022 at [Charleston, South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charleston,_South_Carolina) by Ms. Gayle Petersen, Lt. Gen. Petersen's daughter.[^cite_note-14]

USS *Frank E. Petersen Jr* returned to their home port, [Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_Pearl_Harbor-Hickam), 12 December 2024, after a five-month maiden deployment assigned to the [USS *Abraham Lincoln*](199106.md) Carrier Strike Group (ABECSG). Throughout deployment, *Frank E. Petersen Jr.* completed over 475 hours of flight quarters, 10 hours of small boat operations, conducted 17 replenishments-at-sea, 15 sea and anchor details, qualified 60 sailors in small craft action team (SCAT) and 17 sailors in Security Reaction Force Basic (SRF-B). *Frank E. Petersen Jr.* also welcomed six new chief petty officers, six new first class petty officers and 18 new second class petty officers.[^cite_note-15]

On 12 April during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md) [peace talks](24903.md) she and her sister ship [USS *Michael Murphy*](17314378.md) allegedly transited through the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md) into the [Persian Gulf](24761.md) to "begin setting the conditions" to remove [sea mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_mines) placed by the [Iran Revolutionary Guard Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps).[^cite_note-16]
