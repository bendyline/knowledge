**Rif Dimashq Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة ريف دمشق, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Muḥāfaẓat Rīf Dimašq*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Damascus Countryside') is one of the fourteen [governorates](690220.md) (provinces) of [Syria](7515849.md). It is situated in the southwestern part of the country. It borders the governorates of [Quneitra](7445647.md), [Daraa](5317030.md) and [Suwayda](3096933.md) in the southwest, [Homs](7445154.md) in the north, [Lebanon](17771.md) in the west and [Jordan](7515964.md) in the south. The capital is the city of [Damascus](8914.md).

The Governorate completely surrounds the city and [governorate](17429884.md) of [Damascus](8914.md) and it has an area of 18,032 km² and a population of 2,273,074 (2004 census).[^cite_note-1]

The Governorate was a major site of fighting in the [Syrian civil war](30741795.md) in the [Rif Dimashq Governorate campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rif_Dimashq_Governorate_campaign).

In January 2023, after rumors in social media that Douma have been renamed Al-Fayhaa and became the capital of Rif Dimashq Governorate, Ibrahim Jumaa, head of Rif Dimashq Governorate Council, said that such move should be taken by presidential decret. He said that Al-Fayhaa will be the new name of the ensemble composed by Douma and Harasta, which will be named Al-Fayhaa, and a new building is under construction in Harasta to serve as governorate headquarters, which will be finished by two years.[^cite_note-2]

## Districts



Districts of Rif Dimashq in four colours

The governorate is divided into ten [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Syria) ([manatiq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mintaqah)). The districts are further divided into 37 sub-districts ([nawahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiyah)). There were nine districts until February 2009, when Qudsaya District was created from parts of Markaz Rif Dimashq and Al-Zabadani districts. There is a small village belonging to the Damascus countryside (Ghouta) called Aqraba, which is characterized by its fields and agricultural orchards ([nawahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiyah)). The governorate's total population (as of the [2004 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Syrian_census)) is 2,273,074.

At the end of 2011, the population of Rif Dimashq reached 2,836,000, accounting for 13% of Syria’s population. [Sunni Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam) made up 87% of the governorate, while [Christians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity_in_Syria) made up 5%, [Alawites](436616.md) made up 4%, [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze) made up 4%, [Twelver Shia Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelver_Shi'ism) made up 0.9%, and [Ismaili Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismailism) made up 0.1%.[^cite_note-3]
