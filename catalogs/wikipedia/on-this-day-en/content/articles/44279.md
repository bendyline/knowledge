The **United States secretary of health and human services** is the head of the [United States Department of Health and Human Services](58250.md), and serves as the principal advisor to the [president of the United States](24113.md) on health matters. The secretary is a member of the [United States Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Cabinet). [Robert F. Kennedy Jr.](522298.md) is the incumbent secretary of health and human services, having held the position since February 13, 2025.[^cite_note-2]

The office was formerly known as the secretary of health, education, and welfare. In 1980, the [Department of Health, Education, and Welfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Health,_Education,_and_Welfare) was renamed to Department of Health and Human Services, and its education functions and [Rehabilitation Services Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehabilitation_Services_Administration) were transferred to the new [United States Department of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Education).[^cite_note-3] [Patricia Roberts Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patricia_Roberts_Harris) headed the department before and after it was renamed.[^cite_note-4]

Nominations to the office are referred to the [Health, Education, Labor and Pensions Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Senate_Committee_on_Health,_Education,_Labor,_and_Pensions) and the [United States Senate Committee on Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Senate_Committee_on_Finance), which has jurisdiction over Medicare and Medicaid,[^cite_note-5] before confirmation is considered by the full [United States Senate](24909346.md). The secretary of health and human services is a [level I position in the Executive Schedule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Schedule#Level_I),[^cite_note-6] thus earning a salary of [US\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Dollar)250,600, as of January 2025.[^cite_note-Salary-7]

## Duties



The flag of the secretary of health, education, and welfare, the predecessor to the current office

The duties of the secretary revolve around human conditions and concerns in the [United States](3434750.md). This includes advising the [president](24113.md) on matters of [health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health), [welfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_security), and income security programs. The secretary strives to administer the Department of Health and Human Services to carry out approved programs and make the public aware of the objectives of the department.[^cite_note-8]

The [Department of Health, Education and Welfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Health,_Education,_and_Welfare) (HEW) was reorganized into a [Department of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Education) and a [Department of Health and Human Services](58250.md) (HHS).

The Department of Health and Human Services oversees 11 agencies including the [Food and Drug Administration](11632.md) (FDA), [Centers for Disease Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Disease_Control) (CDC), [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health) (NIH), [Administration for Children and Families](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_for_Children_and_Families) (ACF) and [Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Medicare_and_Medicaid_Services) (CMS).[^cite_note-9]

## Line of succession

The line of succession for the secretary of health and human services is as follows:[^cite_note-11]

1. [Deputy Secretary of Health and Human Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Deputy_Secretary_of_Health_and_Human_Services).

2. General Counsel of the Department of Health and Human Services

3. Assistant Secretary for Administration

4. [Assistant Secretary for Planning and Evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_Secretary_for_Planning_and_Evaluation)

6. [Commissioner of Food and Drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commissioner_of_Food_and_Drugs)

7. Director of the [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health)

8. Assistant Secretary for [Children and Families](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_for_Children_and_Families)

   3. Assistant Secretary for Legislation
