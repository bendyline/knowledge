The **Jetten cabinet** is the current [cabinet of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_Netherlands). The cabinet was sworn in on 23 February 2026. It is a [minority government](271434.md), formed after the [2025 general election](74282122.md), and consisting of the [social liberal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_liberal) [Democrats 66](172352.md) (D66), the [conservative liberal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_liberal) [People's Party for Freedom and Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Party_for_Freedom_and_Democracy) (VVD) and the [Christian democratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_democracy_in_the_Netherlands) and [conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatism_in_the_Netherlands) [Christian Democratic Appeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Democratic_Appeal) (CDA). It is led by [Rob Jetten](58754918.md), who serves as [Prime Minister of the Netherlands](273726.md) since the formation of the cabinet, and also has served as the leader of the D66 since 12 August 2023. It is the first minority cabinet in the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_\(Netherlands\)) without [confidence and supply](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confidence_and_supply) since 1918.
