The **Ministry of Public Security** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Ministério da Segurança Pública*) was a ministry of [Brazil](3383.md), responsible for managing federal police offices and penitentiary departments. It was headed by the Minister of Public Security.

It is a branch from the [Ministry of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_\(Brazil\)), then called Ministry of Justice and Public Security. After an increase in violence ratings in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro_\(city\)) and a military intervention evoked by the Brazilian federal government,[^cite_note-1] President [Michel Temer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Temer) signed a Provisional Measure, creating the ministry temporarily.[^cite_note-2] President Temer nominated [Raul Jungmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raul_Jungmann) as Extraordinary Minister, moving him from the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(Brazil\)).[^cite_note-3] In January 2019, President [Jair Bolsonaro](46603496.md) merged the Ministry of Public Security and the [Ministry of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_\(Brazil\)), and appointed former Federal Judge [Sergio Moro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Moro) as the Minister of Justice and Public Security.[^cite_note-4]
