The **Orenburg gas field** is a [natural gas field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_gas_field) located in the [Orenburg Oblast](481957.md), Russia. It was discovered in 1966 and developed by [Gazprom](320489.md). It began production in 1972 and produces [natural gas](22131.md) and [natural gas condensates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_gas_condensate).[^cite_note-1] The total [proven reserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proven_reserves) of the Orenburg gas field are around 62 trillion cubic feet (1770 km<sup>3</sup>). As of 2013 production was expected to be around 1.7 billion cubic feet per day (49×10<sup>5</sup> m<sup>3</sup>) in 2013.[^cite_note-2]

Gas from the field is exported via the [Soyuz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sojus_\(Pipeline\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) (Orenburg–Western border) pipeline.
