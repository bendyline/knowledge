**Tosc**[^cite_note-1] (2,275 metres or 7,464 feet), formerly also known as **Tolstec**,[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] is a [mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain) of the central [Julian Alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_Alps), located in [Upper Carniola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Carniola), western [Slovenia](27338.md).&#x20;

The mountain stands south of [Triglav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triglav), rising above the Voje Valley to the south and Big Field Pasture (*planina Velo polje*) to the west. There is a steep [gorge](78478.md) in it facing the [Krma Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krma) to the northeast.&#x20;

## Recreation

Tosc is protected within [Triglav National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triglav_National_Park). Its southern slope is frequently used by [ski tourers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ski_touring).&#x20;
