**Kebbi State** [Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/2/2a/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Kebbi_state.wav/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Kebbi_state.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> ([Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language): *Jihar Kebbi*; [Fulfulde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fula_language): Leydi Kebbi 𞤤𞤫𞤴𞤣𞤭 𞤳𞤫𞤦𞥆𞤭) is a [state](226734.md) in [northwestern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_West_\(Nigeria\)) [Nigeria](21383.md), bordered to the east and north by the states of [Sokoto](2227660.md) and [Zamfara](2227664.md), and to the south by [Niger State](1723431.md) while its western border forms part of the national borders with [Benin Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benin%E2%80%93Nigeria_border) and [Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger%E2%80%93Nigeria_border). Named for the city of [Birnin Kebbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birnin_Kebbi)—the state's capital and largest city—Kebbi State was formed from a part of [Sokoto State](2227660.md) on 27 August 1991.[^cite_note-Creation-5] Of the 36 [states of Nigeria](226734.md), Kebbi is the [tenth largest in area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_area) and [17th most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population) with an estimated population of about 6 million as of 2023.

Geographically, the state is within the [tropical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_and_subtropical_grasslands,_savannas,_and_shrublands) [West Sudanian savanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Sudanian_savanna) [ecoregion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ecoregions_in_Nigeria). Important geographic features include the [Sokoto River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_River) which flows through Kebbi into the [River Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Niger), which continues south before reaching [Kainji Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainji_Lake). Among the state's nature are a number of fish species exhibited during the massive [Argungu Fishing Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argungu_Fishing_Festival) along with [hippopotamus](37764.md), [West African manatee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_African_manatee), and transient [African bush elephant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_bush_elephant) populations.[^cite_note-6]

Ethnically, Kebbi is inhabited by various ethnic groups, with the [Fulani](102383.md), [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people), and [Zarma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarma_people) peoples living throughout the state while the Achipa (Achipawa), [Boko-Bala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boko_language), [Dendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendi_people), [Dukawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dukawa_language), [Kambari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kambari_languages), [Kamuku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamuku_people), [Lela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lela_language), [Puku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ut-Ma'in_language), and [Shanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanga_language) peoples live along the state's diverse western and southern borders. Religiously, the majority of the state's population (\~84%) are [Muslims](19541.md) while the remaining are followers of [Christianity](5211.md) and traditional religions like [Bori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_animism).[^cite_note-7]

In the pre-colonial period, the area that is now Kebbi State was mainly controlled by the [Kebbi Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kebbi_Kingdom), a [Hausa Banza bakwai state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banza_bakwai), until the early 1800s when [Fulani jihadists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulani_jihad) seized part of the area and attempted to incorporate it into the [Gwandu Emirate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwandu_Emirate) under the [Sokoto Caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_Caliphate). Over the next century, Kebbi rulers fought Sokoto on-and-off until the 1900s and 1910s, when the British seized control of the area as a part of the [Northern Nigeria Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nigeria_Protectorate) which later merged into [British Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Nigeria) before becoming independent as [Nigeria](21383.md) in 1960.[^cite_note-8] Originally, modern-day Kebbi State was a part of the post-independence [Northern Region](2292852.md) until 1967 when the region was split and the area became part of the [North-Western State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-Western_State). After the North-Eastern State was split, [Sokoto State](2227660.md) was formed in 1976 alongside ten other states. Twenty years afterward, a group of LGAs in Sokoto State's west and south was broken off to form the new Kebbi State.

Economically, Kebbi State is largely based around fishing and agriculture, mainly of [sorghum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorghum), [groundnuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut), [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet), onions, and rice crops.[^cite_note-9] Other key industries are trading, especially in the city of [Birnin Kebbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birnin_Kebbi), and the [livestock herding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herding) of [camels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camels), [cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle), [goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goats), and [sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheep). Kebbi has the [lowest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_Human_Development_Index#2019) [Human Development Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Development_Index) and [sixth lowest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_GDP) [GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP) in the country.[^cite_note-10]

## History

See also: [Kebbi Emirate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kebbi_Emirate)

Kebbi state is traditionally considered by [Sarki mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_Kingdoms#Mythology) as the homeland of the ƴan uwa bakwai states and [Hausa kingdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_states).

Kebbi resisted the [Fulani jihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulani_jihad) of the early 19th-century, but in the later 19th-century, the area largely converted to [Islam](6037917.md) through peaceful means.[^cite_note-11]

## History of Zuru

Zuru emirate is divided into five administrative chiefdoms: [Dabai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dabai), [Danko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danko,_Nigeria), [Fakai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fakai), [Sakaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakaba), and [Wasagu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasagu). The third class chief, who is also member of the [Zuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zuru) town in Dabai chiefdom, where the emirate headquarters is located, heads each of the towns. [Zuru Emirate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zuru_Emirate) is located in the southern part of Kebbi state Nigeria, occupying an area of about 9000 sq km. It is bordered by [Gummi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gummi) in [Zamfara state](2227664.md) in the North. To the south is [Niger state](1723431.md), this borderline extends also arbitrarily on land to the west, to a point where it ends a few kilometers to the west of large tributary of the Dan Zari River, a northwest ward protrusion of [Yauri Emirate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yauri_Emirate) of Kebbi.[^cite_note-12]

In fact, going by history, Zuru people being multi-ethnic are grouped into categories. The first category is of those that claim long term settlement and the second category is of the much more recent settlers, who in fact regard themselves – and are also regarded by the others as recent immigrants or even as temporary strangers. In the first category are the *Achifawa, Kambari, Dukkawa Fakkawa,'Dankawa, Worawa, Katsinawa* and *Lelna ([Dakarkari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakarkari_people)' such as sindawa').*

It is characteristic to find that some of them lay claim to have originated from the Hausas. Zuru as was said, is a result of upheaval, resulting from events such as Kanta's breakaway from [Songhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songhai_people) and [Nupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_people)-Kororofa control. Moreover, the *Katsinawa,* who in fact see themselves as immigrants from the old state of [Katsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_\(city\)), which had made political in road Zuru region, especially from the 16th century A.D. onward, and had enabled them to settle and to area's indigenous population.[^cite_note-13]

Kebbi state was created out of the former Sokoto state on 17 August 1991. The state has a total population of 3,137,989 people as projected from the 1991 census, within 21 local government areas.[^cite_note-14]

The state has Sudan and [Sahel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahel)-[savannah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savannah). The southern part is generally rocky with the [Niger River](21437.md) traversing the state from Benin to [Ngaski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngaski) LGA. The northern part of the state is sandy with the [Rima River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rima_River) passing through Argungu to Bagudo LGA, where it empties into the Niger. Agriculture is the main occupation of the people, especially in the rural areas. Crops produced are mainly grains. Animal rearing and fishing are also common. [Christianity](5211.md) and [Islam](6037917.md) are the dominant religions of the people. There are 225 political wards, 3,000 settlements and 1,036 hard to reach settlements in the 21 local government areas in the state.[^cite_note-15]

### Climate

Like other [Nigerian](13008384.md) states, Kebbi state is also characterised with the tropical weather conditions of coldness, wetness and [harmattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmattan). The annual rainfall of Kebbi state has the average of 787.53 and 112.21 millimetres (31.005 and 4.418 inches), since rainfall is a climatic [resource](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resource) in the state, which aids [agricultural production](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture).[^cite_note-16] The rainy season in the state is between mid-May and mid-September, while the dry season constitutes a period of seven months. The temperature of Kebbi state has an annual variation between 18 °C (64 °F) and 40 °C (104 °F). The [cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud) of Kebbi state is clearer around November to March of the succeeding month, while the state is usually cloudy between March and November at 68% annually. There is a relatively high [humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humidity) between seven months, April and November of every year, with November to July being the windiest.[^cite_note-17]

Kebbi state is mainly populated by Hausa and Fulani people. with some members of [Zarma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarma_people), [Lelna (Dakarkari)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakarkari_people), [Bussawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bissa_people) (generally speakers of [Busa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Busa_language_\(Mande\))), [Dukawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duka_language), [Kambari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kambari), [Gungawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reshe_language) and [Kamuku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamuku_people) ethnic communities.[^cite_note-19]

Most people who live in Kebbi are [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Nigeria).[^cite_note-20]

Kebbi state is one of the major producers of [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice) in Nigeria. The current participation of more than 70,000 farmers in the Anchor Borrowers Rice and wheat farming, is heading towards making Kebbi state a new destination and hub for agro-based commodities of the country.[^cite_note-23]

To reiterate his commitment to ensure that Kebbi state is not only dependent on federal government allocation, the active governor of the state, [Abubakar Atiku Bagudu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abubakar_Atiku_Bagudu) paid a visit to [Benin Republic](3459.md) last year and while on the trip, signed a number of bilateral trade protocols with the government and business community of [Benin Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benin_Republic) with a view to fostering trade, industrial and [tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism) relationships with the country.

Abubakar Atiku Bagudu is also the Chairman, National Task Force on Rice and Wheat Production in Nigeria.[^cite_note-24]

## Transport

### Federal highways

[A1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_highway_\(Nigeria\)) north from Niger State at Makirin via Yelwa, Koko, [Sokoto State](2227660.md) for 19 km from Tungan Ilo via Kuchi in [Kebbe LGA](2026634.md) to Maishaika, Jega, Sokoto State again for nine km from Illela via Tambawel in Kebbe LGA to Ungan-Bawa, and northeast to Sokoto State at Barkeji.

### Road to [Niger](21373.md)

Southwest from [Bunza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunza) at Kamba to RN8.

### Other major roads

- the Birnin-Kebbi-Anmawa Rd northwest from [A1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_highway_\(Nigeria\)) at Jega to [Kalgo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalgo),

- the Birnin-Kebbu-Anmawa Rd north from Kalgo via [Birnin Kebbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birnin_Kebbi) and Zauro to Argungu as the Birnin Kebbi-Argungu Rd,

- the Dagawa-Argungu Rd east to [Sokoto State](2227660.md) near Fakwon Sarki as the Arugungu-Iyabo-Sokoto Rd,

- the Maidahin-Daringari-Kalgo Junction Rd south from Kalgo to [Bunza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunza),

- the Dakingari-Bunza Rd south from Bunza via Dakingari and Zagga,

- the Bin Yauri-Rijau Rd east from A1 at Bin Yauri via Birnin Yauri and Gwanda to [Niger State](1723431.md) at Kambuwa as the Maburo Rijau Rd,

- the Fakai-Iri-Rijau Rd north from Niger State via Bajida,

- the Zuru-Mahuta Rd east from Fakai to Dabai, where one branch goes north via Donko to [Zamfara State](2227664.md), and another branch goes south to Niger State as the Rijau-Zuru Rd.

### Bandit attacks

Kebbi state is badly affected by the [Nigerian bandit conflict](68366617.md). Banditry attacks in the state include the [2021 Kebbi massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Kebbi_massacre) on 3 June 2021, the [Kebbi kidnapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kebbi_kidnapping) on 24 June 2021, the [Dankade massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dankade_massacre) on 14-15 January 2022 and the [2022 Kebbi massacres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_2022_Kebbi_massacres) on 8 March of the same year.[^cite_note-32]

On 17 November 2025, unidentified gunmen killed the vice principal of a [secondary school](554992.md) and kidnapped 25 female students in Maga town.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34]
