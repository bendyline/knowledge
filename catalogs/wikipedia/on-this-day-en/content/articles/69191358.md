The **al-Hajj Radwan Force** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): فوج الحاج رضوان, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Fawj al-Ḥajj Raḍwān*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'al-Hajj Radwan Regiment', also spelled **Redwan** or **Ridwan**) is a [special forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_forces) unit of [Hezbollah](13919.md). According to Alma Center, its main mission is to infiltrate the territory of [Israel](9282173.md), with specific attention to [Galilee](12639.md) and [northern Israel](324281.md).

Hezbollah has trained special forces fighters since the 1990s, which are today part of the Radwan Force. Originally known as the "Rapid Intervention Force" or the "Intervention Unit", the unit was renamed in 2008 to honor [Imad Fayez Mughniyeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imad_Mughniyeh), a senior Hezbollah leader also known as "Hajj Radwan." The Radwan Forces have particular experience in raids and [small unit tactics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_unit_tactics).

The unit has been active in various conflicts, including the [Syrian civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hezbollah_involvement_in_the_Syrian_civil_war) and the [ongoing skirmishes](75005425.md) along the Lebanon–Israel border. The unit has been involved in the fighting since [October 7, 2023](74998317.md), and is expected by Israeli officials to lead any future Hezbollah incursions into Israel.

## Mission

The Radwan Force is a special operations unit in [Hezbollah’s armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hezbollah_armed_forces). According to Tal Beeri, it is tasked with launching offensive attacks into Israeli territory and capturing civilian communities in the [Galilee](12639.md).[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7] Israeli officials expect the Radwan Force to lead any future Hezbollah infiltration into Israel, similar to the [October 7 attacks](74998317.md) by [Hamas](13913.md).[^cite_note-NYTprofile-8] In addition, the Radwan Force conducts [reconnaissance](146911.md) and intelligence operations against Israeli targets using [UAVs](58900.md).[^cite_note-ALWAGHT11Jun23-9][^cite_note-Wapo-10] The unit's sophistication and advanced weaponry explain how Hezbollah's has increasingly been viewed as a hybrid actor, especially sophisticated for a non-state actor, since the [2006 Lebanon War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Lebanon_War).[^cite_note-Wapo-10][^cite_note-seinfeld-11] and according to Hezbollah, perform "ambushes, assassinations, or operations that require deep infiltration."[^cite_note-iver-12]

## History

The Radwan Force was founded in 2006 as the Intervention Unit, a special offensive force to assist Hezbollah’s territorial units, and established with the assistance of the [IRGC](1285472.md) [Quds Force](4028355.md).[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7] Under the command of senior Hezbollah militant leader [Imad Mughniyeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imad_Mughniyeh), the unit was responsible for the [2006 Hezbollah cross-border raid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Hezbollah_cross-border_raid) that led to the outbreak of the [2006 Lebanon War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Lebanon_War).[^cite_note-NYTprofile-8][^cite_note-Wapo-10] After Mughniyeh's killing, the unit was renamed for his operational alias Hajj Radwan (الحاج رضوان) in April 2008.[^cite_note-NYTprofile-8][^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7][^cite_note-Ynetnews9Jun23-13]

The existence of the Radwan Force was revealed during [Ashura](488563.md) ceremonies in 2014 in the Dahiya neighborhood of [Beirut](37428.md), when militants of a distinct unit were photographed while providing security. At this time, the Radwan Force was allegedly commanded by a man named Muhammad Ali Hamadi.[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7][^cite_note-cf2r7Nov14-14]

As of 2023, the unit's commander was Haytham Ali Tabataba’i, who the United States added to its list of [Specially Designated Global Terrorists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specially_Designated_Global_Terrorists) (SDGT) in 2016.[^cite_note-Wapo-10] After the death of [Ibrahim Aqil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Aqil) in September 2024, Abu Ali Reda Abbas[^cite_note-aka-5] was appointed head of the unit.[^cite_note-15]

### Involvement in the Syrian civil war

The Radwan Unit gained prominence as an elite reputation in its involvement in brutal fighting in Lebanon and Syria as part of [Hezbollah's involvement in the Syrian civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hezbollah's_involvement_in_the_Syrian_civil_war) to support the government of Syrian President [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md).[^cite_note-Wapo-10] According to analysts, Hezbollah in general, and the Radwan Force in particular, gained significant battlefield experience and combat arms coordination.[^cite_note-QuitazToI20Apr23-16] According to Israeli researcher Dima Adamsky, the cooperation with advanced regular armed forces in Syria enabled the Radwan Force to transition from advanced infantry to a commando force, capable to achieve significant operational and strategic effects in a war against Israel.[^cite_note-DefenseNews7Nov16-17]

Early in the war, the unit was stationed in [Aleppo](159244.md) area and contributed to [battles of al-Qusayr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qusayr_offensive) and of [al-Qalamoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qalamoun_offensive_\(2017\)), being decisive for the Syrian government's victories. Troops were later garrisoned in northern Syria and [Saraqib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraqib) in [Idlib Governorate](7445043.md), operating with [Harakat Hezbollah al-Nujaba](48027962.md) and [Liwa Fatemiyoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liwa_Fatemiyoun)[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7] and suffering significant casualties against regular [Turkish Armed Forces](30205.md).[^cite_note-TJP21May20-18]

The Radwan Force area of operations also includes the [Quneitra Governorate](7445647.md) and [Daraa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daraa), integrated with the Syrian Arab Army and Shiite militias. In the South, the Radwan Force cooperates with the [4th Armoured Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4th_Armoured_Division_\(Syria\)),[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7] and with the [Russian Naval Infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Naval_Infantry) [Unit 810](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/810th_Guards_Naval_Infantry_Brigade).[^cite_note-WahabJan19-19]

In May 2017, Radwan Unit was allegedly withdrawn from Syria and redeployed on in southern Lebanon.[^cite_note-HezbOrgChart-20]

In February 2020, Radwan Unit was targeted by the [Turkish Air Force](1799517.md) as part of [Operation Spring Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Spring_Shield), suffering over two dozen of casualties.[^cite_note-TJP21May20-18]

In the spring of 2023, the unit took part in rare public military exercises by Hezbollah, displaying its arsenal and simulating an infiltration into Israeli territory.[^cite_note-NYTprofile-8]

### Gaza war

After the outbreak of the [Gaza war](74998519.md), Hezbollah transferred medical teams and Radwan Force militants to the [Israel–Syria border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93Syria_border).[^cite_note-ISWIran2Nov23-21] The Radwan Force led Hezbollah's participation in the [Israel–Hezbollah cross-border attacks](75005425.md) during the war and took the lead for Hezbollah's operations in the broader, longstanding [Hezbollah–Israel conflict](62111540.md). After the October 7 attacks, Israeli security officials cited the Radwan Unit as a force it could no longer accept on the [Israel–Lebanon border](987817.md). According to IDF Spokesman [Daniel Hagari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Hagari), the focus of Israel's actions in southern Lebanon was to force the Radwan Unit away from the border, either via a diplomatic solution that moved the force north of the [Litani River](1920925.md) or a major Israeli military offensive.[^cite_note-NYTprofile-8]

On 8 January 2024, in the village of [Majdel Selm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majdel_Selm), an Israeli airstrike killed [Wissam al-Tawil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wissam_al-Tawil), a deputy head within the force with another Hezbollah fighter, the first death of a commander announced by Hezbollah since the October 7 attacks. As of February 2024, about 50 Radwan fighters had been killed in the previous 4 months, including Abbas Raad, the son of [Lebanese Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Parliament) member [Mohammed Raad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Raad).[^cite_note-Wapo-10][^cite_note-22]

On 20 September 2024, an Israeli air strike in [Dahieh](6082788.md) in southern Beirut killed commander [Ibrahim Aqil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Aqil), head of the training unit Ahmed Wehbe, and 13 other Radwan Force commanders, who were at a meeting of Radwan Force leaders.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

## Organisation

According to Tal Beeri, the Radwan Force consists of around 2,500 troops,[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7][^cite_note-Ynetnews9Jun23-13] with most of them having fought in Syria by 2024. The unit has an independent chain of command and order of battle.[^cite_note-Wapo-10] Under the Radwan Force command, several small, squad-level subunits ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شُعبة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Shu’aba*, lit. 'Section') operate autonomously. Each sub-unit amounts to 7-10 militants based in Shiite villages in Lebanon. These villages function as logisitical reference points for squads. Squads are garrisoned in the vicinity of villages, with ammunition and self-sustainment supplies stored for emergencies.[^cite_note-ALWAGHT11Jun23-9] While being garrisoned near amicable villages, militants are kept separated from civilian population.[^cite_note-Ynetnews9Jun23-13]

This organisation allows squad commanders to operate with significant tactical autonomy, without being dependent on external (and insecure) logistical assistance.[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7]

Additionally, the unit consists of [combat engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_engineer) who, apart from military training, are also trained in cyber and information gathering fields.[^cite_note-ALWAGHT11Jun23-9]

According to Einav Halabi, three psychological care facilities operate in southern Lebanon in order to assist Radwan Force members.[^cite_note-Ynetnews9Jun23-13]

According to American-Israeli news enterprise All Israel News, the commander up to 23 November 2023 was Khalil Shahimi.[^cite_note-AIN23Nov23-26]

## Selection and training

Militants are admitted into the Radwan Force after a careful vetting process. Training begins only after the screening procedure.[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7]

Training includes sniper training, [anti-tank warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-tank_warfare), [hand-to-hand combat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hand-to-hand_combat), explosives training, tactical driving, as well as training for special forces. This latter variety of training includes a “captivity workshop”, in order to teach the trainee to behave in case of capture, and operation of intelligence-gathering UAVs.[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7]

The Radwan Force’s training also emphasizes physical fitness, long-distance running, mountain navigation, and tactical warfare. The unit’s operatives receive training directly from the [IRGC-GF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps_Ground_Forces) [Saberin Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saberin_Unit), as well as specialised tactical and tunnel warfare training with North Korea.[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7][^cite_note-27]

## Insignia

The al-Hajj Radwan Force insignia consists of a roaring lion holding the [Zulfiqar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zulfiqar) sword.[^cite_note-MeirAmit16JUn23-28]

## Equipment

According to Tal Beeri, the Radwan Force has access to all weapons in Hezbollah's arsenal which may be relevant for its operations, including every weapon pertaining to infantry and commando warfare available on the arms market. Combat accessories, including of Western and Russian origin,[^cite_note-cf2r7Nov14-14] are also used.[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7]

The Radwan Force employs small, highly mobile units on motorcycles, quad bikes, and light all-terrain vehicles equipped with Russian-made [Kornet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9M133_Kornet) [ATGMs](37517.md).[^cite_note-HezbEntry16Oct23-29]

### Uniforms

On the occasion of their exposure in 2014, militants of the Radwan Force wore black uniforms, helmets, balaclavas, and dark goggles concealing their features and were armed with assault rifles typical of commando units.[^cite_note-AlmaRes5Jan23-7]

According to the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md)-linked [Meir Amit Intelligence and Terrorism Information Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_and_Terrorism_Information_Center), a 2023 video shows a Radwan Force member in a red beret and with an al-Radwan badge on his sleeve.[^cite_note-MeirAmit16JUn23-28]
