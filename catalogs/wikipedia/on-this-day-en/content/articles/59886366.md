**Bhumika Shrestha** (Nepali: भूमिका श्रेष्ठ, born 1988/1989 in [Kathmandu](17168.md), [Nepal](171166.md)) is a Nepalese activist and actress who has served as [member of parliament](2707214.md) of Nepal since March 2026.[^cite_note-sm-16mar2026-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Shrestha is a [third gender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_gender) activist and is also working with the [Blue Diamond Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Diamond_Society).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Personal life

Shrestha was born in [Kathmandu](17168.md), Nepal. Shrestha was assigned male but does not identify as either a man or a woman;[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] rather, Shrestha prefers to identify as "third gender".

## Awards

In 2022, Shrestha received the [International Women of Courage Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Women_of_Courage_Award) from the [United States Department of State](31975.md).[^cite_note-10]

## Political career

In the [2026 Nepalese general election](69672716.md), Shrestha ran as a candidate for the [Janajati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janajati_people) proportional representation constituency under the [Rastriya Swatantra Party](72312845.md), and was subsequently elected.[^cite_note-sm-16mar2026-1][^cite_note-f24-16mar2026-11]
