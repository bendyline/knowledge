The men's [handball](13730.md) tournament at the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics) was the 15th edition of the [handball event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handball_at_the_Summer_Olympics) for men at the [Summer Olympic Games](27566.md). It was held from 27 July to 11 August 2024. The preliminary round games were played at the [South Paris Arena 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Expo_Porte_de_Versailles) in [Paris](22989.md) and the final games at the [Pierre Mauroy Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stade_Pierre-Mauroy) in [Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille), France.[^cite_note-1]

Denmark won their second title with a win over Germany.[^cite_note-2] It was Denmark's third final in a row and the biggest win in an olympic final.[^cite_note-3] Spain captured the bronze medal after a victory against Slovenia.[^cite_note-4]
