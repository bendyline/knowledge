**Salalah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): صَلَالَة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Ṣalālah*) is the capital and largest city of the southern [Omani](22316.md) [governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorates_of_Oman) of [Dhofar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhofar_Governorate). It has a population close to 331,949.[^cite_note-1]

Salalah is the third-largest city in the [Sultanate of Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Oman), and the largest city in the Dhofar province. Salalah is the birthplace of former Omani [Sultan](405325.md) [Qaboos bin Said](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qaboos_bin_Said_al_Said).[^cite_note-2] Salalah attracts many tourists from other parts of Oman and from abroad during the [monsoon](57630.md)/*[khareef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khareef)* season, from June to September. The [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) of the region and the [monsoon](57630.md) allow the city to grow some vegetables and fruits like coconut and bananas. There are many gardens within the city where these vegetables and fruits grow.

## History

Salalah currently is and was the traditional capital of [Dhofar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhofar), which reached the peak of prosperity in the 13th century thanks to the [incense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incense) trade. Later it decayed, and in the 19th century it was absorbed by the Sultanate of [Muscat and Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscat_and_Oman).

Between 1932 and 1970, Salalah was the residence of Sultan [Said bin Taimur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Said_bin_Taimur). His son, [Qaboos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qaboos_bin_Said_al_Said), who acceded to his father's throne in 1970, decided to move his [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_\(political\)) to Muscat, the largest city in Oman, where he lived until he quietly left for treatment in Germany. Sultan Qaboos's absence was much regretted in Salalah, where his palace was always kept ready to receive him. His last visits were in 2006 to meet influential [tribal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribe) and local leaders, and in 2010 to celebrate the 40th anniversary of his accession with a massive parade watched for several hours by an estimated 100,000 spectators.

Despite the Sultan's generally benevolent government running Oman without national taxation, thanks to its vast mineral resources, and with all menial work done by Asian labourers, Salalah saw peaceful protests for a few months in 2011 in the [domino effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domino_effect) of the [Arab Spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Spring). Some Omani protesters demanded the dismissal of current ministers, better job opportunities, salary increases, a solution to the increasing cost of living, or the establishment of [Islamic banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_banking).

## Demographics



Men at the al-Mughsayl coast

### Religion

The city, like many other in Arab states of the [Arabian Peninsula](47858.md), has a relatively large [expatriate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expatriate) community, mainly from [India](14533.md), [Pakistan](23235.md), [Bangladesh](3454.md), and the [Philippines](23440.md).

The majority of the Omani population (98.9%) in Salalah is [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim). Unlike Omanis on the eastern coast of Oman in and around [Muscat](57053.md), most of whom are [Ibadis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibadism), most Muslims in Salalah follow the [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni) sect of Islam.[^cite_note-3] There is also a considerable population of [Hindus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism), [Christians](5211.md), [Buddhists](3267529.md), and [Sikhs](27964.md) in the expatriate community.

### Language

[Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) is the official language. The unofficial, unwritten non-Arabic language known as [Shehri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shehri_language) is the second most spoken language and the [mother tongue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_tongue) of many in Salalah and its surrounding areas, with 25,000 estimated speakers as of 1993.[^cite_note-4]

[English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) is the official foreign language and the most spoken language of the [expats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expatriate). [Malayalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam) is another popular language and together with [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language), [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language), along with [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu), [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi), [European Spanish](26825.md), [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language), [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language) and [Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language) are widely spoken language among expatriates.

## Economy



Fruit stalls sell local fruits and [tender coconut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tender_coconut)

[APM Terminals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APM_Terminals), part of the [A. P. Moller-Maersk Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._P._Moller-Maersk_Group) of Denmark, manages the [Port of Salalah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Salalah); one of the largest ports on the [Arabian Peninsula](47858.md) which is an important transshipment hub for container shipping in the area. The [Port of Salalah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Salalah) is also one of the most vital ports on the peninsula connecting together [Africa](5334607.md), the [Middle East](19323.md), and [Asia](689.md). But the port is outside the city, to the south. It is also the largest private employer in the [Dhofar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhofar) region. The Salalah Free Zone, situated right beside the port, is emerging as a new center for heavy industries in the Middle East.

### Tourism



Wadi Darbat

Salalah has an international tourist economy with many [Middle Eastern](19323.md) tourists during the Khareef season from June to September. In this season the [mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhofar_Mountains) turn green, and there are many waterfalls to visit, such as at [Wadi Darbat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadi_Darbat?action=edit\&redlink=1), Ain Athum, Ain Tubrook, and Ain Khor.[^cite_note-5] There are four reputed tombs of Islamic prophets: Nabi Imran, possibly the Virgin Mary's father but more likely a local prophet; [Nabi Ayoob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomb_of_Job), the biblical [Job](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_\(biblical_figure\)); Nabi Houd; and Nabi Salih.[^cite_note-6] The city received more than 600,000 tourists during the khareef season in 2017.[^cite_note-7] Amongst the Dhofar Governorate's cultural attractions is the Salalah carnival, which includes the usual rides, family activities, concerts, cultural events and vendors.

One of [Joachim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joachim)'s or [Al Imran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Imran)'s believed graves is in Salalah; which is claimed to be the "longest grave in the world" (12 metres).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

## Climate



Salalah during Khareef Season (June to September)

The city has a hot [desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BWh*), although summers are cooler than in more northern or inland parts of Oman. Salalah is very cloudy and foggy during the [Monsoon of South Asia](31096097.md) months of July and August, even though it receives relatively little rainfall. [Khareef, خريف](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khareef) means "autumn" in Arabic, but it refers to monsoon when describing the region around Salalah. During this time, the brown landscape of Salalah and its surroundings are completely transformed to beautiful and lush green scenes. This provides picturesque scenes for photos.&#x20;

[Cyclone Mekunu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Mekunu), which originated over the Arabian Sea, became an extremely severe cyclone before hitting the Salalah city on 25 May 2018. 200 km/h was the recorded windspeed, and the city of Salalah was pounded with over 617 mm (24.3 in) of rainfall, which is almost 5 years of Oman's average rainfall.[^cite_note-Weather_Salalah-10]

## Sports

Al-Saada Stadium

Al-Saada Stadium

Salalah is known as the home of some of the best football clubs in Oman. In total, Salalah has four [sport clubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sport_club) based in the city: [Salalah Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salalah_Club), [Al-Ittihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ittihad_Salalah), [Al-Nasr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Nasr_Salalah), and [Dhofar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhofar_\(football_club\)) (the most successful club in the League with 11 trophies).

[Dhofar F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhofar_\(football_club\)) have been nicknamed as "*Al-Zaeem*", or "The Leader(s)", due to their enormous success in both the [Omani League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omani_League), and in the [Sultan Qaboos Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Qaboos_Cup). Dhofar also have an adequate number of trophies in sports like [volleyball](32558.md), and [handball](13730.md). [Al-Nasr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Nasr_Salalah) have also been known for their great success in [football](10568.md), winning the Omani League five times, and the Sultan Qaboos Cup 4 times. Al-Nasr, like Dhofar, have also been successful in other sports such as [hockey](10886.md), [basketball](3921.md), volleyball, and handball.

Salalah currently has two stadiums, the [Salalah Sports Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salalah_Sports_Complex) (also known as the "Youth Sports Complex"), which is the only [multi-purpose stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-purpose_stadium) in Salalah. The newer, [Al-Saadah Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Saada_Sports_Complex) is the newly built stadium in Al-Saada district of Salalah devoted to football. Incorporated in the walls of the sports complex apart from the football stadium is a hockey field, [tennis court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_court), [Olympic swimming pool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_swimming_pool), and indoor volleyball/basketball court. Al-Saada Stadium is the venue where [Saudi national football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_arabia_national_football_team), and the [Omani national team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oman_national_football_team) first met in Salalah on August 12, 2009.

The most popular sport played among the youth is by far football. It is very normal to see a group of boys and young men from around the area playing in makeshift fields in parking lots, or in a large open area. Beach football is also a common sight to see along the beach in the Al-Haffa district. Another popular sport in Salalah is volleyball. Although not as popular as football the game is frequently played, special in the beach of Salalah.

In December 2026, Salalah will host the finish of the Budapest–Baghdad–Salalah Rally, a transcontinental endurance rally spanning over 10,000 kilometers. The route starts in [Budapest](36787.md) and passes through numerous countries across Europe and the Middle East – including [Iraq](7515928.md) and [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) – before reaching its final destination in southern Oman. The event is a unique blend of adventure travel and motorsport, where participants compete or tour in various vehicle categories such as motorcycles, cars, quads, and side-by-sides. Designed in the spirit of minimalist assistance, the rally combines challenging navigation with cultural interaction, aiming to showcase the natural beauty and hospitality of the regions along the route. Salalah, as the endpoint, plays a symbolic and celebratory role, highlighting its geographical and cultural significance.

## Education



[Museum of the Land of Frankincense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_of_the_Land_of_Frankincense)

Currently Salalah has two colleges, the Salalah College of Technology and Salalah College of Applied Science, both of which are government owned and sponsored.

The Salalah College of Applied Sciences incorporates an English Department. Its aim is to offer students a solid grasp of the English language so that they may go on to complete further studies in important sectors such as I.T. and Communication and Design.

Salalah is also home to a private university, Dhofar University which is one of the largest in the region. It has significant shares owned by Mustahil Al-Mashani, uncle of Sultan Qaboos bin Said. Recently the university has constructed a new campus worth 25 million [OR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omani_Rial).[^cite_note-15] [British School Salalah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_School_Salalah?action=edit\&redlink=1) was founded in 1970. The school follows the National Curriculum of England and Wales, and offers schooling to children from Reception to Year 10 (Year 11 from August 2021). It is also situated in Dahariz next to the Indian School and Pakistani School.

The [Indian School Salalah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_School,_Salalah), established in 1981, is an Indian-run, self-financing, co-educational institution, primarily established to meet the academic needs of children of Indian expatriates working in Salalah.

## Transportation

### Air transport

See also: [Salalah International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salalah_International_Airport)

[Salalah International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salalah_International_Airport) mainly caters to domestic flights from Muscat and some International flights from [India](14533.md) and regional Arab countries such as [Qatar](8238258.md), [U.A.E](69328.md), and [Saudi Arabia](349303.md). [Oman Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oman_Air), the national airline operates five flights daily from Salalah to [Muscat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscat,_Oman), the capital city and also two flights to [Dubai](211583.md) weekly.

Oman Air introduced Oman Air Pass for regular travelers between Salalah and Muscat.[^cite_note-16] [Qatar Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_Airways) has daily flights from Salalah to Doha connecting to over 130 destinations worldwide.

Very convenient connections are available to destinations in Europe, the Americas, Africa, Asia and Australia. There is also a direct weekly flight from and to [Kochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kochi), [Kozhikode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kozhikode) (Calicut) for the [Malayalee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalee) expatriates. During the Khareef Season (Monsoons) there are weekly flights to other international destinations including [Sweden](5058739.md) and [Turkey](11125639.md). There are also transit flights to almost all countries.

The new International airport opened on 15 June 2015[^cite_note-17] and the old Airport has since then been converted into a Domestic and emergency Airport.

### Bus

There is a daily bus service from [Deira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deira,_Dubai), [Dubai](211583.md), [UAE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UAE) to Salalah at 3pm.[^cite_note-18] There is daily bus service between [Muscat](57053.md) and Salalah from many bus service providers.

### Public transport

Salalah did not have a public transportation system within the city limits until 2018, but in 2019 Sultan Qaboos announced a bus service within the city from Salalah airport to city center and city center to Salalah port.

Oman National Transport Company (Mwasalat) has started daily public bus service in Salalah from December 2018.[^cite_note-19]

Long-distance air-conditioned buses are operated daily from Salalah to [Haima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haima,_Oman), Muscat, [Nizwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizwa), [Al-Buraimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buraimi), [Dubai](211583.md), [Al-Ain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ain), [Al-Ghaydah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Ghaydah), [Mukalla](2414800.md), and [Seiyun](438897.md), as well as [PDO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleum_Development_Oman) locations such as Marmul.

Other forms of public transport popular in Salalah are taxis. Generally fares vary from half a Rial to 2 Rials depending on the distance to destination. Taxis are color-coded orange and white and provide semi-personal transportation in the form of both individual hire and the same opportunistic roadway service as Baisa buses, which are not as popular in the city.

Minibuses, colour-coded orange and white, are unmetered like taxis, after several government initiatives to introduce meters were rejected. The fare is set by way of negotiation, although drivers usually adhere to certain unwritten rules for fares within the city. One should always find out the normally accepted fare for one's journey from one's hotel or host before looking for a taxi.

### Water transport

See also: [Port of Salalah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Salalah)

The [Port of Salalah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Salalah) is one of the deepwater ports in Oman and also eleventh-busiest transshipment port in the world, second-busiest port in the Middle East, which is located at Port Raysut (Salalah). It can accommodate large vessels up to 18m draft. It is the main container tran-shipment terminal of the region. This port is operated and managed by Salalah Port Services Company (S.A.O.G.). The port also welcomes cruise liners and luxury ships.
