**Daniel Bueno Vorcaro** (born 6 October 1983) is a Brazilian former banker and businessman. He is involved in numerous [frauds](58790.md) in the financial, real estate, healthcare, and retail sectors.[^cite_note-g1-profile-1] He gained prominence as the majority shareholder and president of [Banco Master](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banco_Master) (formerly Banco Máxima), guiding the institution through phases of expansion and investment diversification.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4] Vorcaro's career encompassed investments in companies such as Biomm and [Veste S.A. Estilo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veste_S.A._Estilo), participation in the investor group of [Clube Atlético Mineiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clube_Atl%C3%A9tico_Mineiro)'s [Sociedade Anônima do Futebol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociedade_An%C3%B4nima_do_Futebol), and a notable presence in real estate and large-scale ventures operated via Viking Participações.[^cite_note-\:3-5]

Vorcaro gained public attention due to his involvement in [Federal Police of Brazil](2361410.md) investigations into a [multi-billion–dollar financial scheme](82553059.md), which led to the extrajudicial liquidation of Banco Master in November 2025,[^cite_note-valor-international-nov25-6][^cite_note-g1-arrest-7][^cite_note-cnn-arrest-8] and an estimated R\$41 billion loss to the Credit Guarantee Fund (FGC), an amount reported by the fund and the trade press as the highest ever in its history.[^cite_note-veja-perfil-nov25-9][^cite_note-master-fgc-nov25-10]

## Life and career

### Early life and career beginnings

Daniel Vorcaro was born in [Belo Horizonte](82198.md), [Minas Gerais](222651.md), the son of Henrique Vorcaro, a real estate broker, and grandson of Serafim Vorcaro, an Italian immigrant and [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism) pastor.[^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4] He holds a degree in [Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_administration) and an [MBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MBA) from the [Brazilian Institute of Capital Markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibmec), completed in 2007.[^cite_note-g1-profile-1][^cite_note-piaui-out24-11] The family is associated with the [Lagoinha Baptist Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagoinha_Church).[^cite_note-veja-perfil-nov25-9][^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4] Between 2008 and 2009, he hosted a gospel music program called *Supersônica* on [Rede Super](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rede_Super), a television station acquired by his father and linked to the church.[^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4] His sister, Natalia Vorcaro Zettel, is a pastor at one of the church's branches in the state capital.[^cite_note-veja-perfil-nov25-9][^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4]

Vorcaro's first business venture occurred at age 19, when he began managing PQS Empreendimentos Educacionais, an educational course in [Nova Lima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nova_Lima), and a textbook publisher, both acquired by his father.[^cite_note-12] The business was subsequently sold following reports of management conflicts.[^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4] Starting in 2004, he entered the real estate sector through family companies such as Multipar and Mercatto.[^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4]

### Involvement in the financial and investment industry

In 2018, Vorcaro entered an agreement to acquire Banco Máxima from Saul Sabbá, who had been restricted from banking operations by the [Central Bank of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Brazil).[^cite_note-g1-profile-1][^cite_note-13] In partnership with the Conte brothers, he obtained regulatory approval for the transfer of control in 2019, and in 2021, the institution was rebranded as Banco Master.[^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4] Beyond the financial sector, Vorcaro expanded his investments with stakes in the Minas Gerais-based pharmaceutical company Biomm and the São Paulo-based retail group [Veste S.A. Estilo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veste_S.A._Estilo) (formerly Restoque).[^cite_note-14] In 2023, he invested in the [Football Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociedade_An%C3%B4nima_do_Futebol) (SAF) of [Clube Atlético Mineiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clube_Atl%C3%A9tico_Mineiro).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

### Operational strategy and Banco Master expansion

During Vorcaro's management, Banco Master saw significant growth: between 2019 and 2024, its net worth increased from R\$200 million to R\$4.7 billion, and its credit portfolio grew from R\$1.4 billion to R\$40 billion.[^cite_note-veja-perfil-nov25-9] This growth was supported by the extensive issuance of [Bank Deposit Certificates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Certificate_of_deposit) (CDBs) offering returns above the market average, reaching up to 130% of the CDI.[^cite_note-veja-perfil-nov25-9][^cite_note-piaui-out24-11][^cite_note-reuters-nov25-17] The capital raised was often allocated to funds managed by the bank, which included assets from companies undergoing financial restructuring, such as [Oi](1441156.md), [Gafisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gafisa), [Light S.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_S.A.), [CVC Brasil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CVC_Brasil), and Ambipar.[^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4][^cite_note-reuters-nov25-17]

### Allegations of misconduct

To address liquidity requirements, investigations by the [Public Prosecutor's Office](13013461.md) (MPF) allege that the bank used a company called 'Tirreno'–allegedly established as a front–to create fictitious credits.[^cite_note-veja-perfil-nov25-9][^cite_note-g1-master-18][^cite_note-g1-brb-19] Vorcaro initially stated that these credits came from civil servant associations in Bahia.[^cite_note-veja-perfil-nov25-9] The assets, which lacked independent backing, were subsequently sold to [Banco de Brasília](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banco_de_Bras%C3%ADlia) (BRB).[^cite_note-g1-master-18] Investigations by the MPF indicate that BRB injected around R\$16.7 billion into Banco Master between 2024 and 2025,[^cite_note-g1-brb-19] of which at least R\$12.2 billion involved these particular unsecured transactions, described as a way to support the bank's financial position.[^cite_note-valor-international-nov25-6][^cite_note-g1-brb-19][^cite_note-valor-arrest-20]

In December 2024, the then-President of the [Central Bank of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Brazil), [Roberto Campos Neto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Campos_Neto), called Vorcaro to an emergency meeting, requesting a capital injection to address [liquidity](565034.md) concerns.[^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4] Under pressure from the Central Bank, Vorcaro explored the possibility of selling Banco Master to BRB and engaged prominent legal and political figures, including former President [Michel Temer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Temer) and former Supreme Court Justice [Ricardo Lewandowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Lewandowski) (as a consultant), to advise and represent his interests.[^cite_note-veja-perfil-nov25-9] The proposed transaction, which had local political backing, was blocked by the Central Bank in September 2025 due to concerns over financial viability.[^cite_note-piaui-nov25-4][^cite_note-g1-arrest-7][^cite_note-reuters-nov25-17][^cite_note-21] Following this decision, BRB President Paulo Henrique Costa was removed from his position.[^cite_note-g1-brb-19]

### Investigations and judicial developments

Main article: [Banco Master scandal](82553059.md)

On 17 November 2025, Vorcaro was taken into custody by the Federal Police of Brazil at [São Paulo/Guarulhos International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Paulo/Guarulhos_International_Airport) while preparing to board his private [Falcon 7X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dassault_Falcon_7X) jet to Malta.[^cite_note-g1-arrest-7][^cite_note-veja-perfil-nov25-9] Hours earlier, the Central Bank of Brazil had ordered the extrajudicial liquidation of [Banco Master](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banco_Master), overturning the announcement made the previous day about its sale to a consortium led by Fictor Holding Financeira.[^cite_note-g1-profile-1][^cite_note-reuters-nov25-17][^cite_note-valor-arrest-20] Vorcaro's defense stated that his intended destination was Dubai for a meeting with investors and that the travel had been communicated to the Central Bank; however, Brazilian judicial authorities interpreted the trip as an attempt to flee.[^cite_note-g1-profile-1][^cite_note-g1-arrest-7][^cite_note-cnn-arrest-8][^cite_note-22] On 28 November 2025, the [Regional Federal Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Federal_Courts) ordered Vorcaro's release subject to precautionary measures.[^cite_note-23] On 2 December 2025, [Supreme Federal Court](3359076.md) Minister [Dias Toffoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dias_Toffoli) imposed maximum secrecy on Vorcaro's defense request.[^cite_note-24] On 4 March 2026, the Supreme Federal Court ordered Vorcaro's arrest.[^cite_note-25]

## Personal life and wealth

Vorcaro accumulated considerable wealth. His holdings included private jets through Viking Participações and a property in [Trancoso, Bahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trancoso,_Bahia), valued at R\$280 million.[^cite_note-g1-gospel-26][^cite_note-piaui-sapucai-27] In 2023, he organized a 15th birthday celebration for his daughter in Nova Lima, which included international performers and accommodations for nearby residents in hotels to minimize disturbances.[^cite_note-g1-gospel-26] In March 2025, he acquired a property in Trancoso, Bahia, and participated in financing the Café de La Musique Alma Rio VIP box during the [Rio Carnival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Carnival).[^cite_note-piaui-sapucai-27][^cite_note-28] From September 2025, Vorcaro faced significant legal and commercial problems.&#x20;

In March 2026, materials seized from Vorcaro's phone by the Federal Police of Brazil during an investigation revealed a prior personal relationship with Russian model and influencer Maryia Lubimova, who was saved in his contacts list under the name "My Future Wife".[^cite_note-29] Lubimova publicly acknowledged the past personal relationship while stating that she had no involvement in Vorcaro's financial or business activities.[^cite_note-30]
