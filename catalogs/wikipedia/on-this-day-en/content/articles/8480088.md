# 50 metres freestyle

The **50 metres freestyle** is an event in [competitive swimming](22425760.md). World records are ratified by [World Aquatics](317029.md) (formerly FINA). The current long course world record holders are Australia's [Cameron McEvoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_McEvoy) and the US' [Kate Douglass](67964882.md), while the current short course world record holders are the Cayman Islands' [Jordan Crooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Crooks) and the US' [Gretchen Walsh](69355059.md).

The event was first contested at the [Olympic Games](22576.md) in 1988; the current Olympic champions are McEvoy, and Sweden's [Sarah Sjöström](16493334.md). It was first contested at the [World Aquatics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Aquatics_Championships) in 1986; the current world champions (long course) are McEvoy, and Australia's [Meg Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Harris). It was first contested at the [World Aquatics Swimming Championships (25m)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Aquatics_Swimming_Championships_\(25m\)) in 1993; the current world champions (short course) are Crooks and Walsh.
