# List of Formula One World Drivers' Champions









[Michael Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Schumacher) (top left) and [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) (top right) have each won the championship a record seven times during their careers, while four-time consecutive champion [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel) (bottom left) holds the record for being the youngest World Drivers' Champion, having won the [2010 Formula One World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Formula_One_World_Championship) at 23 years and 134 days old. [Lando Norris](48978813.md) (bottom right) is the current World Drivers' Champion, having won the [2025](72768531.md) title.

[Alain Prost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Prost), [Niki Lauda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niki_Lauda) and [Ayrton Senna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayrton_Senna), three World Drivers' Champions, commemorating the podium of the [1985 Dutch Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Dutch_Grand_Prix)

The World Drivers' Championship is presented by the [Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile](248019.md) (FIA), [motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorsport)'s world governing body, to the most successful driver over the course of the [season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_seasons) of [Formula One](10854.md) races, through a [points system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_points_systems) based on individual race results.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The World Championship is awarded at the [FIA Prize Giving Ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIA_Prize_Giving_Ceremony) after the season.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

[Michael Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Schumacher) and [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) hold the record for the most World Drivers' championships—seven—trailed by [Juan Manuel Fangio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Manuel_Fangio) with five.[^cite_note-8WChamps-7][^cite_note-CF1Champs-8] Schumacher holds the record for the most consecutive titles: five, from the [2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Formula_One_World_Championship) to [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Formula_One_World_Championship) seasons.[^cite_note-9] [Nigel Mansell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigel_Mansell) competed in the most seasons before winning the title: 13; he entered Formula One in [1980](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980_Formula_One_season) and won the title in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Formula_One_World_Championship).[^cite_note-10] [Nico Rosberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_Rosberg) has the highest number of Grand Prix starts before winning his first title: 206, from the [2006 Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Bahrain_Grand_Prix) and the [2016 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Abu_Dhabi_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel) is the youngest champion; he was 23 years and 134 days old when he won in [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Formula_One_World_Championship).[^cite_note-13] [Juan Manuel Fangio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Manuel_Fangio) is the oldest; he was 46 years and 41 days old when he won the [1957](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1957_Formula_One_season) title.[^cite_note-14]

As of the [2026](71681684.md) season, the 76 titles have been won by 35 of the 782 drivers who have started a Grand Prix.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-8WChamps-7][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-CF1Champs-8] The first champion was [Giuseppe Farina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Farina), in [1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_Formula_One_season); the current title holder is [Lando Norris](48978813.md) in the [2025](72768531.md) season.[^cite_note-8WChamps-7][^cite_note-CF1Champs-8] The title has been won by 11 drivers from the United Kingdom a total of 21 times, more than any other nation, followed by Brazil, Finland, and Germany with three drivers each. The title has been won by drivers from [Scuderia Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) 15 times between 9 drivers, more than any other team, followed by [McLaren](20994.md) with 13 titles between 8 drivers.[^cite_note-8WChamps-7] The championship has been won in the final race of the season 31 times in the 76 seasons it has been awarded.[^cite_note-\:1-17] Schumacher won the championship with six races left in a season, a record: the [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Formula_One_World_Championship) title at that year's [French Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_French_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-18] The points system has twice crowned a champion—[John Surtees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Surtees) in [1964](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964_Formula_One_season) and [Ayrton Senna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayrton_Senna) in [1988](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Formula_One_World_Championship)—who scored fewer points overall than the driver who finished second.[^cite_note-19] [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) won the 2023 title with 290 points more than the second-place [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez), a record,[^cite_note-20] while [Niki Lauda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niki_Lauda) won the [1984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984_Formula_One_World_Championship) title with the closest gap: 0.5 points over [Alain Prost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Prost).[^cite_note-21]

## By driver



[Michael Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Schumacher) has won the World Drivers' Championship a record seven times – twice with Benetton and five times with Ferrari.



[Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) equaled Schumacher's record in 2020, winning one with [McLaren](20994.md) and six with [Mercedes](25098794.md).



[Juan Manuel Fangio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Manuel_Fangio) won the World Drivers' Championship five times with Alfa Romeo, Maserati, Mercedes and Ferrari. He held the record from 1955 until 2003.



[Alain Prost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Prost) has four titles, three for McLaren and one for Williams. He also just missed winning the title for Renault (finished second, by two points) and for Ferrari (finished second, by seven points).



[Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel) has four titles to his name, all of which he won consecutively with [Red Bull Racing](1173193.md).



[Max Verstappen](41758713.md) won four consecutive World Drivers' Championships in 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024 with Red Bull Racing.

Drivers in **bold** have competed in the 2026 World Championship.
