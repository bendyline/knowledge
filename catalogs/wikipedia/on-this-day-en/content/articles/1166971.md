The **Ministry of Foreign Affairs** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): وزارت امور خارجه, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Vezārat-e Omūr-e Khārejeh*) is an [Iranian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_government) ministry headed by the [minister of foreign affairs](24704012.md), who is a member of cabinet. The office is currently held by [Abbas Araghchi](21951274.md) after the death of [Hossein Amir-Abdollahian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hossein_Amir-Abdollahian).

Foreign policy decisions are made in the [Supreme National Security Council](6295948.md) and according to [Mojtaba Khamenei](23324096.md)'s high courts.[^cite_note-4]

## Ministers and officials

The first minister of foreign affairs of [Iran](14653.md) was [Mirza Abdulvahab Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirza_Abdulvahab_Khan?action=edit\&redlink=1), who served from 1821 to 1823.

The current officials of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs are:

- Minister of Foreign Affairs – [Abbas Araghchi](21951274.md)

- Deputy for Political Affairs – [Majid Takht-Ravanchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majid_Takht-Ravanchi)

- Deputy for Legal & International Affairs – [Kazem Gharibabadi](78777113.md)

- Deputy for Economic Diplomacy – [Hamid Ghanbari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamid_Ghanbari?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- Deputy for Consular, Parliamentary and Iranian Expatriates Affairs – [Vahid Jalalzadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vahid_Jalalzadeh)

- Deputy for Administrative and Financial Affairs – [Mehdi Sobhani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehdi_Sobhani?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Spokesman & Head of the Center for Public and Media Diplomacy](51080171.md) – [Esmaeil Baghaee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esmaeil_Baghaee)

- Head of the [Center for Political and International Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Political_and_International_Studies) – [Saeed Khatibzadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saeed_Khatibzadeh?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- Foreign Minister's Senior Adviser for Special Political Affairs – [Ali Asghar Khaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Asghar_Khaji?action=edit\&redlink=1)

## Assigned activities

Since 5 September 2013, the ministry has been responsible for the negotiation of the [Comprehensive agreement on Iranian nuclear program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comprehensive_agreement_on_Iranian_nuclear_program), which had previously been carried out by the [Supreme National Security Council](6295948.md).[^cite_note-bbc-5]

In 2023 ministry and president began an Africa tour of three states to boost relations.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
