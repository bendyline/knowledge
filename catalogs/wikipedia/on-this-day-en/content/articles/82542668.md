## Impact

Main article: [2026 Iranian supreme leader election](59042456.md)

On 18 March, the [Hengaw Organization for Human Rights](72014392.md) estimated that 5,300+ members of the Iranian military forces had been killed in the attacks.[^cite_note-56]

Iranian state media declared a period of 40 days of mourning (the [Arba'in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arba'in)) and seven days of public holidays in the wake of Ali Khamenei's death, and an [Interim Leadership Council](82544455.md) was appointed pending the election of a new Supreme Leader. On 7 March, the Assembly of Experts announced [Mojtaba Khamenei](23324096.md), son of Ali Khamenei, as the new Supreme Leader.[^cite_note-57] International observers including [France](5843419.md), [Russia](25391.md), and the [United Nations](31769.md) said that regime collapse or escalation of the fighting could occur.[^cite_note-58] U.S. and Israeli officials said that the operation is a step toward [regime change](154074.md), while Iran has retaliated.[^cite_note-auto2-4]
