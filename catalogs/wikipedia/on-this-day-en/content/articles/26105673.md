The following is a **list of ministers of External affairs of Venezuela** since 1830,[^cite_note-1] when [Venezuela](32374.md) achieved independence after the dissolution of [Gran Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Colombia). The founding minister was [Diego Bautista Urbaneja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Bautista_Urbaneja), who held multiple terms. The current minister is [Félix Plasencia](81332725.md), who assumed office on 14 July 2026.
