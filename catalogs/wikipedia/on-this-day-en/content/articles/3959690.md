[Aleksandr Koreshkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Koreshkov_\(ice_hockey\)), captain of Kazakhstan's [national ice hockey team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan_men's_national_ice_hockey_team)

Kazakhstan's former long-term President, [Nursultan Nazarbayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nursultan_Nazarbayev), has challenged sports organizers to engage 30 percent of the country's population in sports. The state has numerous sports clubs where people participate in various types of sports; sport facilities are available to the general public. Kazakhstan currently hosts major international tournaments; Astana and Almaty hosted the VII Asian Winter Games 2011, which drew teams from 27 countries.[^cite_note-1]

[Kazakhstan](16642.md) consistently performs in Olympic competitions. It is especially successful in [boxing](4243.md). This has brought some attention to the [Central Asian](6742.md) nation, and increased world awareness of its athletes. Kazakhstan's city of [Almaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almaty) submitted twice bid for the [Winter Olympics](34069.md): In [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Winter_Olympics) and again for the [2022 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Winter_Olympics). [Astana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astana) and [Almaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almaty) hosted the [2011 Asian Winter Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Asian_Winter_Games).

## Football

Main article: [Football in Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_in_Kazakhstan)

[Football](10568.md) is the most popular sport in Kazakhstan. The [Football Federation of Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Federation_of_Kazakhstan) ([Kazakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakh_language): **Қазақстанның Футбол Федерациясы**, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Kazakh): *Qazaqstannıñ fwtbol federacïyası*) is the sport's national governing body. The FFK organises the [men's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan_national_football_team), [women's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan_women's_national_football_team), and [futsal](188197.md) national teams.

## Cycling



[Assan Bazayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assan_Bazayev), [Astana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XDS_Astana_Team) cycling team

Cycling is Kazakhstan's most successful sport. [Alexander Vinokourov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Vinokourov) represented Kazakhstan in his cycling career for the [Astana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XDS_Astana_Team) team. Vinokourov had an impressive cycling record finishing third overall in the [2003 Tour de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_Tour_de_France). Vinokourov finished 5th in the [2005 Tour de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Tour_de_France), while two other young Kazakhstanis, [Andrey Kashechkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Kashechkin), who later finished 3rd in the [2006 Vuelta a España](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Vuelta_a_Espa%C3%B1a), and [Maxim Iglinsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_Iglinsky), winner of [2012 Liege–Bastogne–Liege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Liege%E2%80%93Bastogne%E2%80%93Liege) and now one of the world's best punchers, finished 19th and 37th, respectively. In 2006, Vinokourov's team became known as [Astana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ONCE_\(cycling_team\)), after a drug doping scandal forced his team Liberty Seguros from the [2006 Tour de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Tour_de_France). Vinokourov helped form a new team funded by a conglomeration of Kazakhstan businesses and adopted the color of the Kazakhstani flag for its uniforms. That same year, Vinokourov and Kashechkin took first and third places in general classification at [Vuelta a España](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Vuelta_a_Espa%C3%B1a) in Spain. Vinokourov won the gold medal in the Cycling Road Race at the 2012 London Olympic Games.

In September 2006, Vinokourov won the 61st Vuelta a España by attacking [Alejandro Valverde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Valverde) in dramatic fashion with 23 km to go in Stage 17. He then held on to his lead over the next four stages, including the final individual time trial to win the Vuelta. Vinokourov and Kashechkin would go on in 2007 to form a new team, backed by the same sponsors as their team from 2006, but with its own lineage. This team is also known as [XDS Astana Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XDS_Astana_Team).

Another famous professional cyclist, [Andrey Kivilev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Kivilev), died after a crash in the 2003 edition of the [Paris–Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris%E2%80%93Nice) race.[^cite_note-2]

## Boxing

[Kazakh boxers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan_national_amateur_boxing_athletes) are generally well known in the world. In the last three Olympic Games, their performance was assessed as one of the best and they had more medals than any country in the world, except [Cuba](5042481.md) and Russia (in all three games). In 1996 and 2004, three Kazakhstani boxers ([Vassiliy Jirov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vassiliy_Jirov) in 1996, [Bakhtiyar Artayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakhtiyar_Artayev) in 2004 and [Serik Sapiyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serik_Sapiyev) in 2012)) were recognized as the best boxers for their techniques with the [Val Barker Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val_Barker_Trophy), awarded to the best boxer of the tournament.

[Oleg Maskaev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleg_Maskaev), born in [Zhambyl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taraz), representing [Russia](25391.md), was the [WBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Boxing_Council) Heavyweight Champion after knocking out [Hasim Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasim_Rahman) on 12 August 2006. The reigning [WBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Boxing_Association), WBC, IBF and [IBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Boxing_Organization) [middleweight champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_middleweight_boxing_champions) is Kazakh boxer [Gennady Golovkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gennady_Golovkin). [Natascha Ragosina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natascha_Ragosina), representing Russia, but from [Karaganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaganda) held seven versions of the women's super middleweight title, and two heavyweight titles during her boxing career. She holds the record as the longest-reigning WBA female super middleweight champion, and the longest-reigning WBC female super middleweight champion.

Kazakh boxers have medaled in each Olympic Games in which they have competed. Kazakhstan performed well in the [2000 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Summer_Olympics) in [Sydney](27862.md), Australia. Two boxers, [Bekzat Sattarkhanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bekzat_Sattarkhanov) and [Yermakhan Ibraimov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yermakhan_Ibraimov), earned [gold medals](526488.md). Another two boxers, [Bulat Zhumadilov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulat_Zhumadilov) and [Mukhtarkhan Dildabekov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukhtarkhan_Dildabekov), earned [silver medals](526647.md). The Tokyo [2020 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Summer_Olympics) was the first in which a Kazakh boxer did not won a gold medal. ([Kamshybek Kunkabayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamshybek_Kunkabayev) and [Saken Bibossinov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saken_Bibossinov) won bronze.)[^cite_note-olympicboxing-3] In the Paris [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics), [Nurbek Oralbay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nurbek_Oralbay) won silver and [Nazym Kyzaibay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazym_Kyzaibay) won bronze.

## Ice hockey



[Evgeni Nabokov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgeni_Nabokov), a Kazakhstani ice hockey player

The [Kazakh national ice hockey team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan_men's_national_ice_hockey_team) have competed in [ice hockey](14790.md) in the 1998 and [2006 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Winter_Olympics), as well as in the [2006 Men's World Ice Hockey Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Men's_World_Ice_Hockey_Championships).

The [Kazakhstan Hockey Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan_Hockey_Championship) is held since 1992. [Barys Astana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barys_Astana) is the main domestic Kazakhstani ice hockey professional team, and having played in the Kazakhstani national league until the 2008–09 season, when they were transferred to play in the [Kontinental Hockey League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kontinental_Hockey_League). Meanwhile, the [Kazzinc-Torpedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazzinc-Torpedo) and play in the [Supreme Hockey League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Hockey_League) since 1996 and the [Saryarka Karagandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saryarka_Karagandy) since 2012.

Top Kazakhstani ice hockey players include [Nik Antropov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nik_Antropov), [Ivan Kulshov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Kulshov?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Evgeni Nabokov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgeni_Nabokov). [Bulbul Kartanbay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulbul_Kartanbay) is a top female ice hockey player and currently plays for the NWHL Metropolitan Riveters in New Jersey.

## Athletics

[Dmitry Karpov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Karpov) is a distinguished decathlete, taking bronze in both the [2004 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Summer_Olympics), and the [2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_World_Championships_in_Athletics) and [2007 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_World_Athletics_Championships). [Olga Rypakova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Rypakova) is an athlete, specialized in [triple jump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_jump) (women's), taking silver in the [2011 World Championships in Athletics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_World_Championships_in_Athletics) and Gold in the [2012 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-4] She was called "Princess of Olympic games" by her fans.[^cite_note-5] Yeldos Smetov, 28 year old, of Bulgaria, won the bronze medal in the under-60-kilogram weight division after defeating Tornike Tsjakadoea of the Netherlands. Smetov won a silver medal at the 2016 Olympic Games in Rio de Janeiro.[^cite_note-6]

## Basketball

Kazakhstan's most famous basketball player was [Alzhan Zharmukhamedov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alzhan_Zharmukhamedov), who played for [CSKA Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PBC_CSKA_Moscow) and the [Soviet Union's national basketball team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union's_national_basketball_team) in the 1960s and 1970s. Throughout his career, he won multiple titles and medals at some of the world's most prestigious basketball competitions, including the [Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_Summer_Olympics), the [Basketball World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_World_Cup), the [EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket) (the European Basketball Championship), and the [EuroLeague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroLeague). In 1971 he earned the title [Master of Sports of the USSR, International Class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_Sports_Classification_System_of_the_USSR) and a year later he was awarded the [Order of the Badge of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Badge_of_Honor).

[Kazakhstan's national basketball team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan's_national_basketball_team) was established in 1992, after the dissolution of the Soviet Union. Since its foundation, it has been competitive at the continental level. Its greatest accomplishment was at the [2002 Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_2002_Asian_Games_%E2%80%93_Men), where it defeated the [Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines_men's_national_basketball_team) in its last game to win the bronze medal. At the official [Asian Basketball Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Basketball_Championship), now called *FIBA Asia Cup*, the Kazakhs' best finish was 4th place in 2007.

## Cross-country skiing



Stamp depicting [Vladimir Smirnov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Smirnov_\(skier\))

Skier [Vladimir Smirnov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Smirnov_\(skier\)) won seven medals in total in the [1988](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Winter_Olympics), [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Winter_Olympics) and [1998 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Winter_Olympics), including a gold in the [50 km competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-country_skiing_at_the_1994_Winter_Olympics) in 1994. He also won 11 medals in total in the [World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIS_Nordic_World_Ski_Championships) from 1987 to 1995, including four gold medals. During the first part of his active career, he represented the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), in the later part Kazakhstan.

## Rhythmic gymnastics

[Aliya Yussupova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliya_Yussupova) was placed 4th in the [Summer Olympic Games](27566.md) in [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Summer_Olympics). She has been among the medalist winners in several previous tournaments. Currently, she is considered to be one of the top rhythmic gymnasts in the world. After the retiring of [Aliya Yussupova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliya_Yussupova) in the end of 2009, [Kazakhstan](16642.md) maintained its top place in the world thanks to the emerge of [Anna Alyabyeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Alyabyeva) who now represents the nation at the international tournaments, grand prix, and world championship. As of 2015 [Sabina Ashirbayeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabina_Ashirbayeva) started her senior career and is growing and achieving good results, she is following the footsteps of former fellow rhythmic gymnast [Aliya Yussupova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliya_Yussupova).

## Rugby union

Main article: [Rugby union in Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_union_in_Kazakhstan)

[Rugby union](25405.md) is a popular sport in Kazakhstan. The [Kazakhstan Rugby Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan_Rugby_Union) was founded in 1993. It develops traditions of rugby existing in Kazakhstan since 1966, when the first [men's national rugby team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan_national_rugby_union_team) of Kazakhstani Soviet Republic was formed. In 2002, the [national women's team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhstan_women's_national_rugby_union_team) became the [Asian Champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARFU_Women's_Rugby_Championship) for the third time. The men's team is becoming stronger and participating in major international tournaments. It is now ranked 32nd (out of 95) position in the [IRB World Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRB_World_Rankings).

## Polo

Since 2012 there is the [Polo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polo) Federation of Kazakhstan, which in 2014 has been a full member of the [Federation of International Polo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_International_Polo) in the General Assembly, which took place in [Buenos Aires](255919.md), [Argentina](18951905.md). The country has a national polo team.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## Tennis

Tennis is a growing sport in the country. Nur-Sultan has hosted the [Astana Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astana_Open) in men and women's singles and doubles since 2020, at the [ATP 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour_500) and [WTA 250](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_250_tournaments) levels respectively. In 2022 the men's event was upgraded from an [ATP 250](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour_250) to an ATP 500.[^cite_note-9] In 2022 [Elena Rybakina](55541443.md) became the first Kazakh player to win a major title and [Grand Slam](197638.md), winning the women's singles title at the [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) championships.[^cite_note-10]

## Weightlifting

[Ilya Ilyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Ilyin) is a notable Kazakh [weightlifter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_weightlifting). In his first international competition, the [2005 World Weightlifting Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_World_Weightlifting_Championships), he came first in the total (386 kg) and [clean and jerk](537948.md) (216 kg) at the age of 17 in the 85 kg men's category.[^cite_note-11] He is one of the few weightlifters who have won two consecutive gold medals at the Olympics, the first at the [Beijing 2008 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_2008_Summer_Olympics), and then second in the 94 kg men's category at the [London 2012 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_2012_Summer_Olympics) in the 94 kg men's category. He set a new world record in the 94 kg category for clean & jerk (233 kg) and the total (418 kg) at London. He has been undefeated[^cite_note-12] in all [World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Weightlifting_Championships) and [Olympic](22576.md) competitions that he has entered in. Recently, in the [2014 World Weightlifting Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_World_Weightlifting_Championships), competing in the 105 kg men's category, he won gold in the total (432 kg) and in the clean and jerk with the world record (242 kg).[^cite_note-13]

## Equestrian sports



Horse riders in traditional dress playing *[Kyz Kuu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyz_Kuu)*.

[^cite_note-14]

Kazakhstan competed in the [2014 World Nomad Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_World_Nomad_Games), winning gold and silver in the *Kynan Chabysh*, silver in the *Alaman Baige*, and two bronze medals in the *[Er Enish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Er_Enish)*.[^cite_note-Lioy-Sep2014-15] The traditional sport of *[Kyz Kuu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyz_Kuu)* ("chase the girl") features [horse riders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_riding) chasing each other.[^cite_note-16] In October 2021, a tournament of traditional nomadic Kazakh games was held in Turkistan, Kazakhstan. This event featured horse racing, archery, Kazaksha kures, [togyzkumalak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Togyzkumalak), asyk atu, and audaryspak.[^cite_note-17]
