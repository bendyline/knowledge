**William Francis Sullivan**[^cite_note-1] (born June 15, 1957) is an American jurist and lawyer. He is an associate justice for the [Massachusetts Superior Court](30702547.md).[^cite_note-2]

## Early life and education

He was born on June 15, 1957, in [Boston](24437894.md), Massachusetts.[^cite_note-3] Sullivan graduated from [Xaverian Brothers High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xaverian_Brothers_High_School) in [Westwood, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westwood,_Massachusetts). He then received his undergraduate degree from the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) and his [J.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juris_Doctor) from the [Boston College Law School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_College_Law_School).[^cite_note-appt-4]

## Career

Prior to his judicial career, Sullivan was a partner at his firm of *Sullivan and Sweeney, LLP* in [Quincy, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy,_Massachusetts). He also previously served as a special assistant city solicitor for Quincy from 2006 to 2008 and as a prosecutor for the [Norfolk County, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk_County,_Massachusetts), District Attorney’s Office from 1982 to 1984.[^cite_note-appt-4][^cite_note-5] He was nominated to the state superior court by former governor [Deval Patrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deval_Patrick) (D) in April 2014, and his nomination was confirmed by the [Governor's Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Governor's_Council) on May 21, 2014.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-appt-4] Sullivan may serve on the court until he reaches the [mandatory retirement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_retirement) age of 70.[^cite_note-7] In 2026, he presided over the trial of [Lindsay Clancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsay_Clancy), who was accused of first-degree murder by the Commonwealth of Massachusetts in relation to [the strangling of her three children](83895822.md) in 2023.[^cite_note-8]

## Personal life

He and his wife, Mary Lou, have three children.[^cite_note-NA-9] Will, Conor and Brennan Sullivan.
