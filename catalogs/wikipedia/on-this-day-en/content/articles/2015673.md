The **Swedish Market Court** ([Swedish](26689.md): *Marknadsdomstolen*) is a [Swedish government agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_agencies_in_Sweden) that answers to the [Ministry of Integration and Gender Equality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Integration_and_Gender_Equality_\(Sweden\)). It is located in [Stockholm](26741.md).

The Market Court is a specialized [court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court) that tries cases related to the Swedish Competition Act as well as cases involving the Swedish Marketing Act and other [consumer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumer) and [marketing](59252.md) legislation.[^cite_note-1]
