# Aframax



Aframax tanker *Mitera Marigo*

An **Aframax** vessel is an [oil tanker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleum_tanker) with a deadweight between 80,000 and 120,000 metric tonnes. [^cite_note-1] The term is based on the **Average Freight Rate Assessment** (**AFRA**), a [tanker](2506366.md) rate system created in 1954 by [Shell Oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shell_Oil) to standardize shipping contract terms.[^cite_note-2]

Due to their favorable size, Aframax tankers can serve most ports in the world. These vessels serve regions that do not have very large ports or offshore oil terminals to accommodate [Very Large Crude Carriers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Very_Large_Crude_Carriers) and [Ultra-Large Crude Carriers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra-Large_Crude_Carriers). Aframax tankers are optimal for short- to medium-haul crude oil transportation. Aframax class tankers are largely used in the basins of the [Black Sea](3386.md), the [North Sea](21179.md), the [Caribbean Sea](7822.md), the [South](74209.md) and [East China Seas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_China_Sea), and the [Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean). Non–[OPEC](166346.md) exporting countries may require the use of this type of vessel because the harbors and canals through which these countries export their oil are too small to accommodate the [Suezmax](2858546.md) or the larger categories.

An Aframax tanker carries 600,000 barrels of oil as compared to larger tankers that may carry up to 2 million barrels of oil.&#x20;
