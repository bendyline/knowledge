**Zebdin** ([Levantine Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levantine_Arabic_language): زبدين, romanized: *Zebdīn*) is a municipality in the [Nabatieh District](12169367.md) in [Lebanon](17771.md).

## History

In 1875, [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) noted on his travels in the region: "I arrived at Zebdin, a village situated on a hill whose slopes are cultivated with fig trees; it contains 600 [Metualis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metualis). There is a small mosque fairly well built. At the bottom of the village is a fountain surmounted by an arched arcade of antique appearance. Two neighbouring [wells](15105978.md) must also date from antiquity."[^cite_note-1]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.51% of registered voters in Zebdin. 97.21% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-2]
