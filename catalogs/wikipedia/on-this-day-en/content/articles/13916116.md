The **Royal Cambodian Army** ([Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language): កងទ័ពជើងគោក, *Kâng Toăp Cheung Koŭk*; lit. 'Land Force') is a part of the [Royal Cambodian Armed Forces](2867590.md). It has ground forces which numbered 85,000 divided into eleven divisions of [infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infantry), with integrated [armour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armoured_warfare) and [artillery](2508.md) support. The Royal Army is under the jurisdiction of the [Ministry of National Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Defence,_Cambodia).

## Military organisation



Royal Cambodian Army soldiers

Under the current military plan and divisions, every military region has a full division size. Each division will be supplemented by a mobile reinforcement division in [Phnom Penh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phnom_Penh). The country is divided into six, until recently five, military regions, each comprising three or four provinces. There are garrisons in major cities and major army bases.

Lieutenant General [Mao Sophan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mao_Sophan?action=edit\&redlink=1) is the commander of the Royal Cambodian Army.

The forces are deployed as required across the country and in operations, with bases as follows:

- **1st Military Region** (យោធភូមិភាគទី១): Headquarters are in [Stung Treng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stung_Treng_Province) and the region covers the provinces of Stung Treng, [Kratié](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krati%C3%A9_Province), [Ratanakiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratanakiri_Province) and [Mondulkiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mondulkiri_Province).

- **2nd Military Region** (យោធភូមិភាគទី២): Headquarters are in [Kampong Cham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampong_Cham_Province) and the region covers the provinces of Kampong Cham, [Prey Veng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prey_Veng_Province), and [Svay Rieng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svay_Rieng_Province).

- **3rd Military Region** (យោធភូមិភាគទី៣): Headquarters are in [Kampong Speu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampong_Speu_Province) and the region covers the provinces of Kampong Speu, [Takéo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tak%C3%A9o_Province), [Kampot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampot_Province), [Sihanoukville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sihanoukville_Province), [Koh Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koh_Kong_Province) and [Kep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kep_Province).

- **4th Military Region** (យោធភូមិភាគទី៤): Headquarters are in [Siem Reap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siem_Reap_Province) and the region covers the provinces of Siem Reap, [Oddar Meanchey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oddar_Meanchey_Province), [Preah Vihear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preah_Vihear_Province), and [Kampong Thom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampong_Thom_Province).

- **5th Military Region** (យោធភូមិភាគទី៥): Headquarters are in [Battambang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battambang_Province) and the region covers the provinces of Battambang, [Pursat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pursat_Province), [Banteay Meanchey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banteay_Meanchey_Province) and [Pailin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pailin_Province).

- **Special Military Region** (យោធភូមិភាគពិសេស): Headquarters are in the capital, [Phnom Penh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phnom_Penh) and the region covers the provinces of [Kampong Chhnang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampong_Chhnang_Province), [Kandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandal_Province) and the greater municipality of Phnom Penh.

Every military region is under the command of a major general, assisted by a chief of staff with a rank of a brigadier general. In every province, there is a military base called a military operation zone under the command of a colonel.

## Peacekeeping operations

The RCAF has sent RCAF personnel to various hotspots as part of the Kingdom of Cambodia's role as a member of the [United Nations](31769.md). Mostly engineers and logistical units, a total of 6,822 soldiers, of them 369 are women, have been so far sent to 10 peacekeeping missions in nine countries such as:[^cite_note-3]

-  [Central African Republic](5478.md)

-  [Chad](5488.md)

-  [Cyprus](5593.md)

-  [Lebanon](17771.md)

-  [Mali](19127.md)

-  [South Sudan](32350676.md)

-  [Sudan](27421.md)

-  [Syria](7515849.md)

-  [Yemen](350939.md)
