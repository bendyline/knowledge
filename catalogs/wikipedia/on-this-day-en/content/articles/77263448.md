Presidential elections are scheduled to be held in the [Maldives](19117.md) in September 2028.[^cite_note-1] Voters will elect a [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Maldives) and [vice president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_the_Maldives) for a five-year term. Incumbent president and leader of the [People's National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_National_Congress_\(Maldives\)), [Mohamed Muizzu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Muizzu), is eligible for a second term.

## Electoral system

The president is elected using the [two-round system](25932.md); if no candidate receives a majority of the vote in the first round, a second round is held with just the top two candidates.[^cite_note-S-2]

In November 2024, Muizzu announced plans to hold a referendum on replacing the two-round system with [instant runoff voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instant_runoff_voting), allowing the elections to be held in a single round of voting.[^cite_note-S-2] He also proposed holding the presidential and parliamentary elections on the same day.[^cite_note-S-2] A campaign was launched by government officials and supporters to propose preferential voting.[^cite_note-3] Opposition parties allege it as a plot to meddle with the election boxes and to rig the votes.[^cite_note-4]

## Candidates

In November 2024, [Abdul Raheem Abdulla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Raheem_Abdulla), chairman of the [People's National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_National_Congress_\(Maldives\)) (PNC), stated that Muizzu would be automatically selected as the party's nominee for the 2028 elections rather than holding party primaries to determine the candidate.[^cite_note-E-5] In 2026, the PNC Senate awarded him the party ticket without a primary.[^cite_note-6]

[Maldivian Democratic Party](673147.md) (MDP) chair [Fayyaz Ismail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fayyaz_Ismail) stated that he would enter the contest to be the party's nominee, [Abdulla Shahid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulla_Shahid), the president of the party said that he'd also compete.[^cite_note-E-5][^cite_note-7] Former president [Abdulla Yameen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulla_Yameen), leader of the [People's National Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_National_Front) (PNF) has also stated he would run for the presidency,[^cite_note-8] while former president [Mohamed Nasheed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Nasheed), member of [The Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Democrats_\(Maldives\)), stated that he was intending to run for president although it's unclear whether he would run as the nominee for MDP or The Democrats.[^cite_note-9] Nasheed later joined the MDP in June 2025.[^cite_note-10]
