# Interlocutor (politics)

An **interlocutor** is someone who informally explains the views of a [government](12229.md) and also can relay messages back to a government.[^cite_note-ET_Radha-1]

## Examples

- [Narinder Nath Vohra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narinder_Nath_Vohra), [Government of India](553883.md)'s former Special Representative for carrying out the [Jammu and Kashmir Dialogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir_dispute).

  [^cite_note-\:1-3]

- [Dineshwar Sharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dineshwar_Sharma), [Government of India](553883.md)'s former Special Representative for carrying out the Jammu and Kashmir Dialogue.

  [^cite_note-4]

  [^cite_note-5]

- [R. N. Ravi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._N._Ravi), [Government of India](553883.md)'s former Special Representative for carrying out the [Nagaland Peace Accord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagaland_Peace_Accord).
