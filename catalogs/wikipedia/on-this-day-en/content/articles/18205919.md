**Konotopie** [\[kɔnɔˈtɔpjɛ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Polish) is a village in the administrative district of [Gmina Kikół](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gmina_Kik%C3%B3%C5%82), within [Lipno County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipno_County), [Kuyavian-Pomeranian Voivodeship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuyavian-Pomeranian_Voivodeship), in north-central Poland.[^cite_note-TERYT-1]

In March 2025, construction began on the [Our Lady of Mercy monument](83998742.md), dedicated to the [Virgin Mary](73513.md), in the vicinity of the village. The structure, consecrated on 15 August 2026, is Europe's tallest statue of [Mary](73513.md) (55 meters, 182 feet). The work was funded by millionaires living in the area.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
