**Pegasus Airlines** ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *Pegasus Hava Taşımacılığı A.Ş.*; [BİST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borsa_Istanbul): [PGSUS](https://www.borsaistanbul.com/en/)), sometimes stylized as **flypgs**, is a Turkish [low-cost airline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-cost_airline) headquartered in the Kurtköy area of [Pendik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pendik), [Istanbul](3391396.md). Its primary operating base is [Sabiha Gökçen International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabiha_G%C3%B6k%C3%A7en_International_Airport), with additional bases across Turkey. As of October 2026, the airline serves 161 destinations in 57 countries across Europe, Asia and North Africa.[^cite_note-Smartwings2026-1]

Established in 1989 as an inclusive-tour [charter airline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charter_airline) in partnership with [Aer Lingus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aer_Lingus), Pegasus commenced flight operations in 1990. The airline was acquired by [Esas Holding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esas_Holding) in 2005 and subsequently adopted a low-cost business model. As of June 2026, Pegasus operated a fleet of 131 aircraft, primarily comprising members of the [Airbus A320neo family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A320neo_family).[^cite_note-PegasusFleet2026-2]

In December 2025, Pegasus announced an agreement to acquire [Czech Airlines](335703.md) and its subsidiary [Smartwings](1597347.md) for €154 million, including related receivables. The acquisition was completed on 1 October 2026 following the receipt of the necessary regulatory approvals. The combined airline group has more than 175 aircraft, with Smartwings continuing to operate under its existing brand.[^cite_note-Smartwings2026-1]

### Foundation and early years

On 1 December 1989, two businesses, Net and Silkar, partnered with [Aer Lingus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aer_Lingus) to create an inclusive tour [charter airline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charter_airline) called Pegasus Airlines; services were inaugurated on 15 April 1990 with two [Boeing 737-400s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737). In Greek mythology, [Pegasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus) (Greek: Πήγασος *Pégasos*, 'strong') was a winged horse sired by [Poseidon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poseidon), in his role as horse-god, and foaled by the [Gorgon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorgon) [Medusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medusa). However, four months after the launch, [Iraq invaded Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_of_Kuwait), and the seven-month occupation that followed had a serious effect on Turkish tourism.[^cite_note-AW-4] By 1992, tourists began returning to the country, and Pegasus grew with the acquisition of a third 737-400. The airline leased a further two [Airbus A320s](164933.md) to meet the summer demand.[^cite_note-AW-4]

After two positive years, Aer Lingus and Net sold their shares in the company in 1994 to Istanbul-based *[Yapı Kredi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yap%C4%B1_Kredi)* bank, making Pegasus a purely Turkish company.[^cite_note-AW-4]

On 4 September 1997, Pegasus placed an order for one 737-400 and one 737-800 from [Boeing Commercial Airplanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Commercial_Airplanes), making it the first Turkish carrier to place an order for the [Boeing 737 Next Generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737_Next_Generation). The airline also signed lease agreements for a further 10 737-800s from the [ILFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ILFC).[^cite_note-AW-4]

In January 2005, [ESAS Holdings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9Eevket_Sabanc%C4%B1#Esas_Holding) purchased Pegasus Airlines and placed Ali Sabanci as the chairman. Two months later, he changed the airline from a charter airline to a low-cost airline. In November 2005, Pegasus placed an order for 12 new 737-800s from Boeing, which was backed up with an order for a further 12 737-800s in November 2008. The latter order had flexibility, as the orders could be changed to the 149-seat 737-700 or the 215-seat 737-900 depending on market demand. In 2018, Pegasus tried to acquire an A380, but later cancelled the order.[^cite_note-AW-4]

In 2007, Pegasus had reached a domestic market share of 15%, which grew to 27% in 2013.[^cite_note-5] In 2019, it carried a total of 29.87 million passengers.

### Development since 2010

In November 2011, [Air Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Berlin) and Pegasus Airlines launched *Air Berlin Turkey*, which was aimed at the charter market between Germany and Turkey.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The new airline, however, was absorbed into Pegasus Airlines on 31 March 2013.

In 2012, Pegasus Airlines, the second-largest airline in Turkey, signed for up to 100 [A320neo family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A320neo_family) aircraft, of which 75, 57 A320neos and 18 A321neos, were firm orders. Pegasus became a new Airbus customer and the first Turkish airline to order the A320neo. This was the largest single commercial aircraft order ever placed by an airline in Turkey at that time and was announced on 18 December 2012 at a ceremony attended by [Binali Yıldırım](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binali_Y%C4%B1ld%C4%B1r%C4%B1m), the [Turkish Minister of Transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Transport_and_Communication_\(Turkey\)).[^cite_note-9] In June 2012, Pegasus Airlines bought 49% of the Kyrgyz air company [Air Manas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Manas). On 22 March 2013, the air company operated its first flight under the brand name Pegasus Asia.[^cite_note-10]

The company [offered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_offering) 34.5% of its shares of stock to the public. The shares began to be traded at the [Borsa Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borsa_Istanbul) as [BİST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borsa_Istanbul): [PGSUS](https://www.borsaistanbul.com/en/) on 26 April 2013.[^cite_note-11]

In October 2016, Pegasus Airlines announced it was offering three of its aircraft on the [ACMI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_lease#Wet_lease) and leasing markets, stating severely decreasing passenger numbers.[^cite_note-12]

On 5 February 2020 [ a runway excursion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_Airlines_Flight_2193) would turn into the first fatal incident in the history of the airlines. After the incident some former employees from the airlines stated that the airlines would prioritize sales over safety, putting staff under pressure firing them without reasons and [ severance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severance_package). Flights would spend little time on ground and exceed legal limits in air, performing hasty [ safety checks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_maintenance_checks) and compromising on [hygiene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hygiene). Former employees called the CEO's press statement on the grievance "[Crocodile tears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crocodile_tears)".[^cite_note-13]

In August 2022, Pegasus Airlines experienced a data breach that exposed over 6.5TB of data, including a number of internal passwords and the personal information of flight crew. The breach occurred after Pegasus accidentally configured one of the [Amazon S3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_S3) storage buckets used for PegasusEFB, its [electronic flight bag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_flight_bag) system, to be without any security, exposing its contents to the public Internet. The breach affected [Turkish Airlines](167925.md) and [Air Manas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Manas), who also used PegasusEFB.[^cite_note-14] The breach was caused by [ Corporate negligence](21436.md) and a \$183,000 [ fine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_crime) could have been imposed on the airlines by the [Turkish Data Protection Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Data_Protection_Authority).[^cite_note-15]

The airlines ranked fifth worst for flight delays from [UK](31717.md) airports in 2022.[^cite_note-16] According to [AirHelp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AirHelp), Pegasus airlines was ranked the sixth worst airline in 2024.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] This ranking stems from the fact that the airlines is inferior to its competitors in all key parameters. Particularly long [ flight delays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_cancellation_and_delay) and difficulty in receiving [ compensation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Passengers_Rights_Regulation#Compensation_and_assistance) has been the major cause of inconvenience according to customers.[^cite_note-19]

In March 2022 Pegasus announced that Güliz Öztürk would become chief executive officer with effect from 1 May 2022, succeeding Mehmet T. Nane, who became vice-chairperson of the board; Öztürk was the first woman to serve as CEO of an airline in Turkish civil aviation.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] Esas Holding has remained the airline's controlling shareholder since the flotation; H. Çağatay Özdoğru, Esas Holding's chief executive, served as vice-chairman of the Pegasus board from December 2006 to September 2022 and rejoined the board in August 2023.[^cite_note-22]

Pegasus Airlines continues to operate flights to Russia despite its [invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) and the resulting [international sanctions](70323386.md).[^cite_note-23] While most Western airlines have ceased operations in or over Russian airspace, Pegasus maintains its routes, aligning with Turkey’s close diplomatic and economic ties to Moscow. This includes contributing to government-backed efforts to support [Russian tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_tourism) by allocating significant resources, such as 500,000 seats, for Russian passengers.[^cite_note-24]

In December 2025, it was announced Pegasus had signed an agreement to acquire [Smartwings](1597347.md) and its parent company [Czech Airlines](335703.md) in a deal worth €154 million.[^cite_note-25]

### Cabin

Pegasus Airlines operates a one-class interior configuration on all of its aircraft. A "Flying Cafe" is available to all passengers, whereby food and beverages are provided for an additional charge. Pegasus is also considering installing [in-flight entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In-flight_entertainment) and charging for headphones (currently, only overhead screens are available on selected 737-800s, and they only display a computer-generated map showing the flight's progress).[^cite_note-AW-4] All new Boeing 737-800s which arrived after November 2011 have Boeing Sky Interior.

### Training and maintenance

Unlike most low-cost carriers, Pegasus runs its own flight crew training centre and maintenance organisation, Pegasus Technic. Both centres are fully licensed and are used to train new staff members both on the ground and in the air.[^cite_note-AW-4][^cite_note-47]

### Sponsorships

Pegasus Airlines was one of the official sponsors of [Nef Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nef_Stadium), the stadium of Turkish Football Club [Galatasaray S.K.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galatasaray_S.K.)[^cite_note-48] from 2011 to 2013.

## Incidents and accidents

- On 10 March 2010, Pegasus Airlines Flight 361, an [Airbus A319](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A319) operated by [IZair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IZair) on a ferry flight, made an emergency landing at [Frankfurt Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt_Airport), [Germany](11867.md) after a malfunction in the nose gear. The flight landed safely but blew both front nose gear tires. The airport closed runway 07R/25L for 3 hours to allow recovery.

  [^cite_note-66]

  &#x20;The nose gear suffered the same problem as [JetBlue Flight 292](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JetBlue_Flight_292).

- On 7 February 2014, [Pegasus Airlines Flight 751](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_Airlines_Flight_751), a [Boeing 737](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737), was the victim of an attempted hijacking by Ukrainian passenger Artem Kozlov

  [^cite_note-67]

  &#x20;who claimed he had a bomb on board. The passenger demanded to be flown to Sochi, the host city of the [2014 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Winter_Olympics), where the Opening Ceremony was taking place. The plane landed safely in [Istanbul](3391396.md).

  [^cite_note-68]

- On 13 January 2018, [Pegasus Airlines Flight 8622](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_Airlines_Flight_8622), a [Boeing 737-800](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737-800) (registration *TC-CPF*) from [Ankara Esenboğa Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara_Esenbo%C4%9Fa_Airport), [Ankara](802.md) to [Trabzon Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trabzon_Airport), veered off the wet runway at Trabzon, slid into the ground of an acutely angled cliff, and got stuck in the mud, which prevented the 41-tonne fuselage from skidding into the [Black Sea](3386.md).

  [^cite_note-two_cranes-69]

  &#x20;All 168 people on board survived and there were no reported injuries. The aircraft sustained substantial damage.

  [^cite_note-AH4b39bd2e-70]

- On 7 January 2020, Pegasus Airlines Flight 747, a [Boeing 737-800](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737-800) (registration *TC-CCK*), [overran the runway](8731144.md) on landing at [Istanbul Sabiha Gökçen International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul_Sabiha_G%C3%B6k%C3%A7en_International_Airport) ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA): **SAW**) causing substantial damage to the aircraft.

  [^cite_note-ASN747accident-71]

  &#x20;All 164 people on board

  [^cite_note-ASN747accident-71]

  &#x20;evacuated via slides. There were no injuries,

  [^cite_note-AH4d1a5fd9-72]

  &#x20;and the aircraft was repaired.

  [^cite_note-ASN747accident-71]

- On 5 February 2020, [Pegasus Airlines Flight 2193](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_Airlines_Flight_2193), a Boeing 737-800 (registration *TC-IZK*), [overran the runway](8731144.md) on landing at [Istanbul Sabiha Gökçen International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul_Sabiha_G%C3%B6k%C3%A7en_International_Airport) ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA): **SAW**). The fuselage broke into three segments and an engine caught fire. Three passengers were killed and 179 occupants were injured.
