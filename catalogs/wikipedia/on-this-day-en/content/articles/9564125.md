**Injil District** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): ولسوالی انجیل) is one of the 20 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Afghanistan) of [Herat Province](312559.md) in northwestern [Afghanistan](737.md).[^cite_note-3] It has an estimated population of 301,320 people,[^cite_note-population-2] mostly [Tajiks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajiks) with smaller communities of [Pashtuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns) and others.[^cite_note-ReliefWeb-1] Almost all inhabitants of Jebrael locality are ethnic [Hazaras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazaras).

Injil District is administratively divided into 3 [nahias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya) and 65 gozars, covering an area of about 1,814 km<sup>2</sup> (700 sq mi).[^cite_note-ReliefWeb-1] It surrounds [Herat District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herat_District) and borders [Karukh District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karukh_District) in the east. The [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of Injil District is also known as [Injil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injil,_Afghanistan). The [Hari River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hari_River,_Afghanistan) flows on the southern border of the district, shared with [Guzara District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guzara_District).

## Economy

Further information: [Economy of Afghanistan](9895.md)

The people of Injil District are involved in [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture), [trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade) and [transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_in_Afghanistan). Most parts of the district are plains and low mountains. Water is not as much of a problem as in other regions. The [arable land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arable_land) is in use and irrigated. The area is famous for its [grapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grapes).[^cite_note-4]

A 100-day project in 2011 repaired the Ordokhan, Qala Farahiha, and Sawa roads and cleared and renovated 12 km of irrigation canals. The canal work improved irrigation to an estimated 400 hectares of agricultural land around Ordokhan Village.[^cite_note-USAID-2011-5]
