The **Marjayoun District** is a district in the [Nabatieh Governorate](4981449.md) of [Lebanon](17771.md). The capital of the district is [Marjayoun](6236310.md).

[Marjayoun](6236310.md) stands at a hill facing Mount Hermon to the East, with [Beaufort Castle](6853436.md). The Litani River and Mount Amel are to the West, The Rihan, Niha and the Lebanon Mountain Range to the North and the fertile plains of Sahil Marjayoun that extends into Northern Israel between the Galilee finger and plains immediately underneath the Golan Heights.
