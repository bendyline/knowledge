On 20 April 2026 at 16\:53 [JST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Standard_Time) (07\:53 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)), a [M<sub>JMA</sub>](6207163.md#Mjma) 7.7 ([M<sub>w</sub>](6207163.md#Mw) 7.4–7.5) earthquake struck off the [Sanriku Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanriku_Coast) of [Honshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honshu), Japan, along the [Japan Trench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Trench). The earthquake caused strong shaking in Iwate and Aomori, with a maximum intensity of 5-upper (5+) on the [Japan Meteorological Agency seismic intensity scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Meteorological_Agency_seismic_intensity_scale), recorded in [Hashikami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hashikami), Aomori. Additionally, it generated [long-period ground motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-period_ground_motion) of class 3 in southern Akita and northern Miyagi prefectures.[^cite_note-1]

## Tectonic setting

The northern parts of [Honshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honshu) and the island of [Hokkaido](58092.md) lie above the [convergent plate boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convergent_boundary) where the [Pacific plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_plate) is [subducting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subduction) beneath the [Okhotsk microplate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okhotsk_microplate). The convergence rate across this boundary lies in the range of 7.9–9.2 cm per year.[^cite_note-Nishimura_2000-2] The plate interface in the area of the earthquake epicenter shows an abrupt increase in dip from about 5° to about 15°, 80 km landward of the [Japan Trench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Trench).[^cite_note-Ito-3] This part of the plate boundary has been the location of many powerful historical earthquakes, dating back to the [869 Sanriku earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/869_Jogan_Sanriku_earthquake) and the devastating [2011 Tōhoku earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_T%C5%8Dhoku_earthquake_and_tsunami). Most of these events relate to rupture along the plate interface, but some, such as the [1933 Sanriku earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1933_Sanriku_earthquake),[^cite_note-Kanamori-4] involved deformation within the subducting plate. According to the studies of past great earthquakes, a weak seismic coupling is suggested in the offshore [Sanriku](31176917.md) region.[^cite_note-Takahashi-5] From the distribution of past seismicity, the width of coupling at 40°N was assumed to be about 150 km.[^cite_note-Kawasaki_2001-6]

## Earthquake

The [United States Geological Survey](23814944.md) (USGS) said it measured the earthquake at M<sub>w</sub> 7.4 and was caused by a [east-northeast-dipping, southwest–northeast-striking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike_and_dip) [thrust fault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrust_fault) at a depth of 35 km (22 mi),[^cite_note-anss-7] while the [Japan Meteorological Agency](457886.md) (JMA) put the preliminary magnitude at M<sub>JMA</sub> 7.5[^cite_note-8] and later updated to M<sub>JMA</sub> 7.7.[^cite_note-jmaintensities-9] Tremors were felt from [Hokkaido](58092.md) to [Aichi](524618.md).[^cite_note-jmaintensities-9] The [Global Centroid Moment Tensor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Centroid_Moment_Tensor) (GCMT) assigned the earthquake a moment magnitude of M<sub>w</sub> 7.5.[^cite_note-10] A USGS finite fault model estimates a [rupture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthquake_rupture) area of 70 km × 65 km (43 mi × 40 mi), with a maximum slip of 4.735 m (15.53 ft) near the [hypocenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypocenter). The observed source time function gives a 12-second duration for the earthquake, with the greatest phase of seismic moment release occurring less than 10 seconds after initiation. A USGS seismic installation at [Shingō, Aomori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shing%C5%8D,_Aomori) recorded 15.01 cm/s (5.91 in/s) in ground velocity (pgv), while another installation at [Morioka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morioka), Iwate recorded a [peak ground acceleration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak_ground_acceleration) (pga) of 0.404 *g*.[^cite_note-anss-7]

A [foreshock](1160429.md) sequence preceding the mainshock began in November 2025; 162 foreshocks exceeding M<sub>w</sub> 4.0 occurred, including five M<sub>w</sub> 6.0+ events,[^cite_note-foreshocksandaftershocks-11] the strongest of which measured M<sub>w</sub> 6.8.[^cite_note-12] By 23 April, 17 aftershocks measuring M<sub>w</sub> 4.0 or higher occurred,[^cite_note-foreshocksandaftershocks-11] the strongest measuring M<sub>w</sub> 5.6.[^cite_note-13]

### Tsunami

The JMA issued a tsunami warning for waves reaching up to 3 metres (9.8 ft) high in parts of Iwate Prefecture, [Aomori](61732.md), and [Hokkaido](58092.md) Prefectures. These warnings were later downgraded to advisories,[^cite_note-14] before being lifted at 23\:50 JST.[^cite_note-Hokkaido-NP-Breaking-15] An 80-centimeter-high (31 in) tsunami struck [Kuji, Iwate](328212.md).[^cite_note-Yomiuri-Breaking-16] At [Miyako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyako,_Iwate)[^cite_note-Iwate_Nippo-17] and [Urakawa, Hokkaido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urakawa,_Hokkaido), the tsunami was 40 centimeters (16 in) high,[^cite_note-Iwate_Nippo-17] while 30-centimeter (12 in) waves were observed in [Hiroo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiroo,_Hokkaido) and [Erimo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erimo,_Hokkaido) in Hokkaido and [Sendai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sendai), [Miyagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyagi_Prefecture).[^cite_note-Yomiuri-Sanriku-18][^cite_note-Hokkaido-NP-Breaking-15]

## Impact

Ten people were injured, two in [Hokkaido](58092.md), and four each in [Aomori](61732.md) and [Iwate](183477.md) prefectures. Two of the victims were seriously injured.[^cite_note-19] At least 232 buildings were damaged in Aomori Prefecture.[^cite_note-20] A road was damaged in [Ishinomaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ishinomaki), [Miyagi Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyagi_Prefecture).[^cite_note-21] Roughly 200 households lost power in [Hiraizumi, Iwate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiraizumi,_Iwate).[^cite_note-Yomiuri-Breaking-16] Evacuation orders were issued to 82,811 households and 175,957 people from a total of 13 cities, 21 towns and 6 villages in Hokkaido, Aomori, Iwate, Miyagi, and Fukushima Prefectures.[^cite_note-Yomiuri-Breaking-16] Schools were closed for April 21 in Urakawa,[^cite_note-Hokkaido-NP-Breaking-15] and several schools in Aomori Prefecture.[^cite_note-22] The earthquake resulted in suspension of [Shinkansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinkansen) lines including the [Hokkaido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hokkaido_Shinkansen), [Tokaido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokaido_Shinkansen) and [Tōhoku Shinkansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C5%8Dhoku_Shinkansen).[^cite_note-Hokkaido-NP-Breaking-15][^cite_note-Yomiuri-Breaking-16] Ferry services between [Aomori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aomori) and [Hakodate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakodate) were also cancelled.[^cite_note-Hokkaido-NP-Breaking-15]

## Response

The JMA issued an Off the coast of Hokkaido and Sanriku subsequent earthquake advisory until April 27, warning about a possibility of a stronger earthquake.[^cite_note-23] Along with the [Cabinet Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_Office_\(Japan\)), the Japan Meteorological Agency stated that the chance of a "[megaquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megaquake)" exceeding magnitude eight would be increased tenfold over during this period, up to 1% following the quake from a background risk of 0.1%.[^cite_note-24]

## Misinformation

Following the earthquake, social media posts circulating on [X](9988187.md) uploaded by various accounts in and outside Japan claimed to show footage of the earthquake using videos and images of the [2024 Noto earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Noto_earthquake) and the [2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_T%C5%8Dhoku_earthquake_and_tsunami) as well as [AI-generated content](73291755.md). As a result, the [Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Internal_Affairs_and_Communications) requested X and other major social media sites "to take appropriate measures based on the terms of use".[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

## 25 June aftershock

On 25 June, a M<sub>w</sub> 6.9 earthquake struck off the coast of Iwate Prefecture along the at a depth of about 34 km (21 mi).[^cite_note-27] It caused strong shaking across coastal northeastern Honshu, particularly in Iwate and [Aomori](61732.md), but no tsunami warning was issued and damage remained limited.[^cite_note-28] This was later confirmed as aftershock.

In [Aomori Prefecture](61732.md), 10 people were injured,[^cite_note-29] some walls collapsed, and 366 buildings were damaged.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31] Another two people were injured, and the ceiling of an office building collapsed in Iwate Prefecture.[^cite_note-32]
