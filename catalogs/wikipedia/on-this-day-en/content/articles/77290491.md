[Colonel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonel_\(United_Kingdom\)) **Alistair Scott Carns** (born 27 March 1980) is a British [Labour Party](19279158.md) politician. He has been the [member of Parliament](3776360.md) (MP) for [Birmingham Selly Oak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham_Selly_Oak) since 2024, and was a junior government minister between 2024 and 2026. He is a reserve [Royal Marines](93985.md) officer and a former regular officer.&#x20;

He was the [parliamentary under-secretary of state for veterans and people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_Under-Secretary_of_State_for_Veterans_and_People) from 2024 to 2025,[^cite_note-need-1] and was the parliamentary under-secretary for the armed forces from September 2025 until his resignation on 11 June 2026 as part of the [2026 Labour Party leadership crisis](83157483.md).

## Early life and education

Carns was born on 27 March 1980, in [Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen), Scotland.[^cite_note-need-1][^cite_note-Times_erupts-2] One of five siblings, he shared a bedroom with his two brothers, and was raised by their single mother, in a three-bedroom home in Heatheryfold, next to a council estate.[^cite_note-preparing-3][^cite_note-heart-4][^cite_note-\:1-5] He was educated at [Hazlehead Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazlehead_Academy), a comprehensive secondary school in Aberdeen, with an interest in military history, described by his History teacher as sporty, eclipsed only by his "accident proneness". He suffered a broken neck and leg and in a separate barbecue incident, third degree burns over 11% of his body.[^cite_note-\:2-6] He returned to college to improve his maths grades, completing a [HND](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_National_Diploma) in physical fitness and sports science at [Aberdeen College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen_College) to increase his chance of enlisting in the [Armed Forces](31730.md).[^cite_note-\:2-6]

He developed an early interest in [deep sea diving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep-sea_diving) from visits to his father at work in [Dyce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyce), inspiring him with an ambition to become a [combat diver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_diver).[^cite_note-Times_erupts-2][^cite_note-\:2-6]

## Military career

Carns enlisted as a [Royal Marine Commando](93985.md) in 1999.[^cite_note-need-1][^cite_note-7] He was commissioned as an officer in September 2002, and appointed to the trained strength as a [captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_\(United_Kingdom\)) in September 2003.[^cite_note-LG_26_October_2004-8] He was promoted to [major](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_\(United_Kingdom\)) on 1 October 2010,[^cite_note-LG_2_November_2010-9] to [lieutenant colonel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_colonel_\(United_Kingdom\)) on 30 June 2016,[^cite_note-LG_2_August_2016-10] and to [colonel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonel_\(United_Kingdom\)) on 26 July 2021.[^cite_note-LG_21_September_2021-11]

Carns served with the [Special Boat Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Boat_Service) (SBS), part of [the UK's special forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom_Special_Forces).[^cite_note-guardian-20251213-12][^cite_note-sky-20251212-13][^cite_note-heart-4] *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent)* reported that he had "served in every major conflict this country has been engaged in for the last two dozen years", but much of the detail could not be made public for security reasons.[^cite_note-independent-20240626-14] He has stated he came close to death more than four times during his service.[^cite_note-Times_erupts-2]

Carns served five operational tours [in Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Herrick),[^cite_note-standard-20260123-15] and was [mentioned in dispatches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mentioned_in_dispatches) "in recognition of gallant and distinguished services in Afghanistan during the period 1st October 2006 to 31st March 2007".[^cite_note-LG_19_July_2007-16] In September 2011, he was awarded the [Military Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Cross) (MC) "in recognition of gallant and distinguished services in Afghanistan during the period 1st October 2010 to 31st March 2011".[^cite_note-LG_30_September_2011-17] In the [2022 Queen's Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Queen's_Birthday_Honours), he was appointed [Officer of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Officer_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (OBE).[^cite_note-LG_2_June_2022-18]

Carns was a [military adviser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_adviser) to three [Defence Secretaries](302332.md): [Michael Fallon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fallon), [Gavin Williamson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gavin_Williamson) and [Penny Mordaunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penny_Mordaunt).[^cite_note-Times_erupts-2]

Carns was expecting to be promoted to [brigadier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigadier_\(United_Kingdom\)) in June 2024, but in May 2024 he resigned his commission in order to stand for the [Labour Party](19279158.md) in the [2024 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_Kingdom_general_election).[^cite_note-need-1][^cite_note-19]

Carns re-enlisted in the [Royal Marines](93985.md) as a [reservist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reservist) in November 2024.[^cite_note-Times_erupts-2]

In the [2025 New Year Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_New_Year_Honours), Carns was appointed a [Companion of the Distinguished Service Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Companion_of_the_Distinguished_Service_Order) (DSO) "in recognition of gallant and distinguished services in the field during the period 1 October 2023 to 31 March 2024".[^cite_note-\:0-20] He received the insignia during a ceremony in July 2025; his was the first to feature [King Charles III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Charles_III)'s cypher.[^cite_note-21]

## Political career



Carns with Navy personnel, 2024

Carns left the Royal Marines in 2024 so that he could stand as a candidate in the general election.[^cite_note-independent-20240626-14] He had previously voted for the [Conservative Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_Party_\(UK\)), and surprised his colleagues when he intended to stand as a Labour Party candidate.[^cite_note-heart-4]

In the [2024 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_Kingdom_general_election), he was elected [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament) (MP) for [Birmingham Selly Oak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham_Selly_Oak) with 17,371 votes (45.2%) and a majority of 11,537.[^cite_note-22] On 24 July 2024, Carns made his [maiden speech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maiden_speech) in the [House of Commons](13828.md) during a debate on education and opportunity.[^cite_note-maiden-23]

On 9 July 2024, he was appointed [Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Veterans and People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_Under-Secretary_of_State) in the [Ministry of Defence](203793.md).[^cite_note-bio_GOV-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] This ministerial post is also simply known as the Minister for Veterans and People.[^cite_note-bio_GOV-24] In September 2025, Carns became the [Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for the Armed Forces](24079896.md), also known as the Minister for the Armed Forces.[^cite_note-27]

During the [2026 Labour Party leadership crisis](83157483.md), he was named as a possible [dark horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_horse) candidate for the Labour Party leadership after [Keir Starmer](18681491.md) stood down; following media speculation in the weeks that followed Starmer's resignation, Carns ruled out running and endorsed [Andy Burnham](414818.md).[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-preparing-3][^cite_note-heart-4][^cite_note-sky8jul-29] He resigned as minister on 11 June 2026, after Defence Secretary [John Healey](434625.md) resigned earlier in the day.[^cite_note-30] In his resignation letter, Carns stated that "the character of conflict is changing faster than our procurement can keep up with", and criticised the defence investment plan agreed by [Chancellor of the Exchequer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellor_of_the_Exchequer) [Rachel Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Reeves), arguing that it was "not built for the threat we face" and was "neither transformative enough nor sufficiently funded".[^cite_note-31]

## Personal life

Carns is divorced and has three children, two sons and a daughter (who graduated in pharmacy in 2025).[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-\:2-6]

Carns set a record by reaching the summit of [Mount Everest](42179.md) in under five days, without the usual acclimatisation processes, on 21 May 2025, as part of a team of four British former-special forces members, trialling [the use of xenon gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xenon#Erythropoietin) to speed up [altitude acclimatisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altitude_acclimatisation).[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-\:1-5] He said, "The reality is if I had six to eight weeks to climb Everest, I would, but I'm a government minister and I don't have time."[^cite_note-34]
