The **2026 [Xiaomi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaomi) CHINA GT Championship** is the tenth season of the [China GT Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_GT_Championship?action=edit\&redlink=1), an [auto racing](1022.md) series for spec [GT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_tourer) cars promoted by Top Speed. The five-event season starts at the [Shanghai International Circuit](952143.md) on 17 April and will conclude at [Sepang International Circuit](944336.md) on 8 November.[^cite_note-Calendar2026-1]



Official new logo of the China GT Championship (from 2026)

## Championship standings

Scoring system

Championship points are awarded for the first ten positions in each race. Entries are required to complete 75% of the winning car's race distance in order to be classified and earn points. Individual drivers are required to participate for a minimum of 25 minutes in order to earn championship points in any race.[^cite_note-32]
