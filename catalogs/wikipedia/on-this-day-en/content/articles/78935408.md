**SpaceX Crew-11** was the eleventh operational NASA [Commercial Crew Program](64072812.md) flight and the 19th crewed orbital flight of a [Crew Dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crew_Dragon) spacecraft. The mission transported four crew members, [NASA](18426568.md) astronauts [Zena Cardman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zena_Cardman) and [Michael Fincke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fincke), [JAXA](716184.md) astronaut [Kimiya Yui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimiya_Yui), and [Roscosmos](406573.md) cosmonaut [Oleg Platonov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleg_Platonov_\(cosmonaut\)), to the [International Space Station](15043.md) (ISS). The mission launched on August 1, 2025 and docked with the ISS the next day. On January 8, 2026, NASA announced that the mission would end about a month earlier than planned due to a medical situation involving Fincke.

## Crew

[Zena Cardman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zena_Cardman) was originally assigned to [SpaceX Crew-9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpaceX_Crew-9),[^cite_note-3] but she and [Stephanie Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephanie_Wilson) were removed from that flight,[^cite_note-4] which launched with only two crew members and returned with the crew of the [Boeing Crew Flight Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Crew_Flight_Test) due to issues with the [Boeing Starliner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Starliner) [*Calypso*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Starliner_Calypso),[^cite_note-5] while [Michael Fincke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fincke) and [Kimiya Yui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimiya_Yui) were initially assigned to [Boeing Starliner-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Starliner-1), but they were reassigned to Crew-11 due to testing with the [Boeing Starliner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Starliner) capsule.[^cite_note-6]

The eleventh SpaceX operational mission in the [Commercial Crew Program](64072812.md) was scheduled for launch on July 31, 2025, but was scrubbed due to weather.[^cite_note-Robinson-Smith_2025-07-31-9] After a quick turn around, SpaceX was able to launch the mission the next day on August 1, 2025.[^cite_note-LZ1_Retired-11][^cite_note-LZ1_Retired-11] At 14 hours, 43 minutes, and 10 seconds, it is the fastest Crew Dragon rendezvous to date.[^cite_note-Space.com-12]

### Medical incident

On January 7, 2026, NASA announced the mission would be shortened due to an undisclosed medical situation experienced by one of its crew members.[^cite_note-Medical_Issues_NYT-13][^cite_note-NASA_2026-02-25-14] The incident led to the cancellation of two planned [spacewalks](9792.md) on January 8 and 15, including preparations for installation of [ISS Roll-Out Solar Arrays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roll_Out_Solar_Array) (iROSAs) and other maintenance tasks on the [Harmony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmony_\(ISS_module\)) module and the station’s S6 and S4 truss.[^cite_note-EVA_95_Cancelled-15] At a news conference on January 8, 2026, NASA administrator [Jared Isaacman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jared_Isaacman) announced that Crew-11 would return to Earth earlier than planned due to the medical situation.[^cite_note-ARS_2026-01-08_Decision-16] Crew-11 ultimately splashed down on January 15, 2026 about a month earlier than planned.[^cite_note-NSF_Crew_11_Return_Early-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

In a statement on February 25, 2026, Fincke revealed that he experienced the medical event, in which he was rendered unable to speak for a period of 20 minutes.[^cite_note-20] He said that the event required immediate attention from his crewmates, and thanks to their actions under the guidance of NASA [flight surgeons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_surgeon), he was quickly stabilized. He said that the decision to return home early was driven by a desire to take advantage of advanced [medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging) not available on the space station. After splashdown, Fincke was treated at [Scripps Memorial Hospital La Jolla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripps_Health) and stated that he is subsequently doing "very well".[^cite_note-NASA_2026-02-25-14]
