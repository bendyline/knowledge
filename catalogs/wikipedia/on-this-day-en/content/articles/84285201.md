The **Canada men's national flag football team** represents [Canada](5042916.md) in men's international [flag football](224471.md) matches. The team is governed by [Football Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Canada).

Team Canada has won the [IFAF Flag Football World Championship](43253761.md) in 2008 and won a bronze medal in [2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_IFAF_Men's_Flag_Football_World_Championship). The team also won a bronze medal at the [IFAF Americas Continental Flag Football Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFAF_Americas_Continental_Flag_Football_Championship) in 2025.

## History

The Canadian men's team has received two medals at the [IFAF Flag Football World Championship](43253761.md). They won gold in 2008 and bronze in 2026. At the 2008 game, they defeated [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark_men's_national_flag_football_team) in the finals.[^cite_note-results-1] They lost to [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_men's_national_flag_football_team) in the bronze medal game in the 2010 and 2014 editions.[^cite_note-silver2026world-2][^cite_note-touchdown-3] Canada later qualified for the [flag football tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_football_at_the_2028_Summer_Olympics) of the [2028 Summer Olympics](12695048.md) in Los Angeles by virtue of being a finalist at the [2026 IFAF Flag Football World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_IFAF_Men's_Flag_Football_World_Championship), in which they defeated [Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_men's_national_flag_football_team) in the bronze medal match.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

[IFAF Americas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFAF_Americas) organizes flag football continental championships every two years to fall in between the world championships dubbed the [IFAF Americas Continental Flag Football Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFAF_Americas_Continental_Flag_Football_Championship). The men's team competed in the [2023 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_IFAF_Americas_Continental_Men's_Flag_Football_Championship), where they lost to [Panama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panama_men's_national_flag_football_team?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the bronze medal match.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-2023_IFAF_Americas-7] They competed in the [2025 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_IFAF_Americas_Continental_Men's_Flag_Football_Championship), where they defeated Panama in the bronze medal match.[^cite_note-8]
