# Ambassadors of Brazil



Map of the countries with Brazilian embassies.

[Brazil](3383.md) has diplomatic representation with almost all other recognized states, with the exception of 56 of the 194 countries, being some of them [Afghanistan](737.md), [Bhutan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan), [Cambodia](334751.md), [Fiji](10707.md), [Kiribati](16674.md), [Iceland](14531.md), [Marshall Islands](19147.md), [Micronesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micronesia), [Monaco](19261.md), [Nauru](21302.md), [Palau](23372.md), [Samoa](27238.md), [San Marino](27248.md), [Solomon Islands](265083.md), [South Sudan](32350676.md), [Taiwan](25734.md), [Tuvalu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuvalu) and [Yemen](350939.md),[^cite_note-1] totalizing a relation with 138 countries in all of the continents and with the [Palestinian National Authority](241405.md). Currently, Brazil has strength its relations with the [BRICS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRICS) ([Russia](25391.md), [India](14533.md), [China](5405.md) and [South Africa](17416221.md)) and the [next eleven countries under development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Next_Eleven).
