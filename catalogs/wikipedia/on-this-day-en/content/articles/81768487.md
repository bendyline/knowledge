**MGI All Stars** is a beauty pageant run by [Miss Grand International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Grand_International) (MGI) for women who have already competed in an international pageant, whether under MGI or another organisation. It was announced in December 2025 and first held in 2026.[^cite_note-Inquirer-1][^cite_note-IBT-2]

The first winner was [Vanessa Pulgarin](54205059.md) of Colombia, crowned at MGI Hall in [Bangkok](56615.md) on May 30, 2026 from a field of 56.

## History

[Miss Grand International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Grand_International) is an international beauty pageant founded in [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Grand_International_2013) and run from Thailand. It judges on four criteria it calls beauty, body, brain and business, and campaigns under the slogan "Stop the War and Violence".[^cite_note-3]

MGI announced All Stars on December 4, 2025 as a competition separate from its annual edition, open to "beauty queens from any pageant" rather than to former MGI contestants alone.[^cite_note-Inquirer-1][^cite_note-IBT-2][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] Entry is open to women and trans women aged 20 to 40, married or single, with or without children, who have competed in an international pageant before, whether or not they placed.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

The winner receives the All Stars Crown and a cash prize of between US\$100,000 and US\$1,000,000.[^cite_note-\:0-9] The organisation gave as its purposes the recognition of what returning contestants had gained since their earlier attempts, and a second chance for those who had underperformed or withdrawn.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Format

Preliminary competition runs over two days, the first for swimsuit and evening gown, the second for cocktail dress and a bare-face round. On the final night the field is cut to a top 25, then to a top 10, and the top five take a question-and-answer round.[^cite_note-13] MGI did not publish full judging criteria before the first edition.[^cite_note-14]

The All Stars title is separate from the main Miss Grand International crown, and its holder does not succeed the reigning Miss Grand International.[^cite_note-15]
