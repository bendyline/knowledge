**Narta Lagoon** ([Albanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_language): *Laguna e Nartës*) is a [lagoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagoon) of the [Adriatic Sea](23275478.md) in the central [coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Adriatic_Sea_Coast) of [Albania](738.md). The lagoon extends north of the [Bay of Vlorë](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Vlor%C3%AB) on the eastern shore of the [Strait of Otranto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Otranto) and is separated from the sea by a narrow [littoral strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Littoral_zone), consisting of an [alluvial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alluvium) [dune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune_\(geology\)). It has a surface area of 41.8 km<sup>2</sup> (16.1 sq mi) with a maximal depth of 1.5 m (4.9 ft).

It is situated within the boundaries of the [Vjosa Wild River National Park](73303962.md), and has been recognised as an important [Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Important_Bird_Area) and [Plant Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Important_Plant_Areas) of international importance.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] As of May 2020, it is home to 3,000 [flamingos](165927.md).[^cite_note-4] It is formed by the constant accumulation of solid flow of the [Vjosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vjosa) River, which originates within the [Pindus Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pindus_Mountains) close to the border between Albania and Greece.[^cite_note-5]

The lagoon is named after the village of [Nartë](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nart%C3%AB), which is found on the lagoon's southern shores. Within the lagoon are two islands, the largest being [Zvërnec Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zv%C3%ABrnec_Island). A wooden footbridge connects mainland to the island, where a 13th-century monastery is located. At least 34,800 wintering birds can be counted on the lagoon.[^cite_note-6]
