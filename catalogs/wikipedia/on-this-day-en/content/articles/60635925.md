**King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Center (KSRelief)** was established by King [Salman bin Abdulaziz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salman_of_Saudi_Arabia) in 2015.[^cite_note-1] The Supervisor General of the KSRelief is [Abdullah bin Abdulaziz Al Rabeeah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_bin_Abdulaziz_Al_Rabeeah).[^cite_note-2] The center was established in the framework of the Saudi’s efforts to alleviate the suffering of those in need worldwide.[^cite_note-3]

## Key emergency relief projects

### Supporting the Global Polio Eradication Initiative

On May 1, 2024, the King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Center announced a contribution of over \$600 million to protect 370 million children annually from polio and lift millions out of poverty in 33 member countries of the [Islamic Development Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Development_Bank). [Bill Gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Gates) stated in an interview with [Al-Arabiya](321645.md).net that Saudi Arabia had allocated \$500 million to eradicate [polio](25107.md), and that the largest donors to polio eradication efforts were the [Gates Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gates_Foundation), the United States, and now [Saudi Arabia](349303.md).[^cite_note-4]
