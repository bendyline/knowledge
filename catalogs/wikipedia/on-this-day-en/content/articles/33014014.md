**Dehloran** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): دهلران)[^cite_note-4] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Dehloran_County\)) of [Dehloran County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehloran_County), [Ilam province, Iran](448068.md), serving as capital of both the county[^cite_note-Dehloran_County_Info-5] and of the district.

## Demographics

### Language and ethnicity

The city is populated by [Lurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lurs).[^cite_note-6]

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 27,602 in 5,787 households.[^cite_note-2006_Ilam_Province-7] The following census in 2011 counted 30,989 people in 7,623 households.[^cite_note-2011_Ilam_Province-8] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 32,941 people in 9,204 households.[^cite_note-2016_Ilam_Province-2]

### Location

The city lies along [Road 64](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_64_\(Iran\)), near the Iraqi border. A national natural monument lies in the north-eastern part of the city. Dehloran is located in the northern part of Dehloran Plain.

### Dehloran plain

The ancient site of [Chogha Sefid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chogha_Sefid?action=edit\&redlink=1) is just to the west of the village. Culturally, the plain is known as a part of "Greater Susiana."

&#x20;Dehloran Plain in the south of Ilam province, along with the [Susiana Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susa) in [Khuzestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan) to the southwest, has been the subject of the longest and most extensive archaeological research in Iran. These two areas had been culturally very similar during the [Chalcolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalcolithic) age. The important sites on Dehloran Plain are [Ali Kosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Kosh), [Tepe Farukhabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tepe_Farukhabad), Chogha Sefid, and Tepe Sabz.
