The **State Great Khural**[^cite_note-1] is the [unicameral](204304.md) [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament) of Mongolia,[^cite_note-mont40-2] located in the [Government Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Palace_\(Mongolia\)) in the capital [Ulaanbaatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulaanbaatar). Elections to the State Great Khural are held every four years, the latest election was held in [June 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Mongolian_parliamentary_election).[^cite_note-3]

The State Great Khural currently consists of 126 seats, 78 of which were elected by [multiple non-transferable vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_non-transferable_vote) in multi-member constituencies whilst the other 48 were elected by [closed list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_list) [proportional representation](23298.md). Prior to the 2023 constitutional amendment, the State Great Khural was made up of 76 seats, all elected by plurality block voting.[^cite_note-4]

After the ratification of the [1992 Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Mongolia), which abolished the bicameral [People's Great Khural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Great_Khural) and the [State Little Khural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Khural), a unicameral parliament was established and first convened on 20 July 1992.[^cite_note-sanders2-5]

## History

### 1914–1919

&#x20;Prince and khan [Tögs-Ochiryn Namnansüren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%B6gs-Ochiryn_Namnans%C3%BCren) became the chairman of the State Great Khural in February 1914, and served until his death in April 1919.[^cite_note-parliament.mn-first-6]



The first session of the State Great Khural in 1924

### 1924–1960

The first State Great Khural was called to session in November 1924. This body was the legislature of the [Mongolian People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Republic). It delegated much of its powers to an executive committee—the [Little Khural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Khural). The Great Khural held nine sessions between November 1924 and February 1949. Following electoral reforms in 1951, the numbering of its sessions began again. The first was held in July 1951 and the third in July 1957.[^cite_note-sanders-7]

### 1960–1992

Main article: [People's Great Khural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Great_Khural)

In 1960, a new constitution was adopted and the body was renamed the "People's Great Khural",[^cite_note-8] but the sessions were not renumbered. The fourth took place in July 1960 and the last in September 1992. In Russian and Mongolian historiography, the term "People's Great Khural" is frequently extended back to refer to the 1924–60 Khural to distinguish it from the post-1992 State Great Khural.[^cite_note-sanders-7]

#### Democratic transition

In the aftermath of the [1990 Democratic Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_Revolution_of_1990), the first free, democratic and multi-party election in Mongolia was held in [July 1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Mongolian_parliamentary_election). Subsequently, the newly elected parliament amended the [Constitution of the Mongolian People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutions_of_the_Mongolian_People's_Republic), established the [State Baga Khural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Khural) which replaced the People's Great Khural as the highest legislative body, effectively making the People's Great Khural a representative [upper house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_house) and the State Baga Khural a legislative [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

The State Little Khural was established with a composition of 50 members and 5 standing committees. The [Mongolian People's Revolutionary Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Party) (33), The [Mongolian Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Mongolia\)) (13), The [Mongolian Social Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_Social_Democratic_Party) (4), The [Mongolian National Progress Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_National_Progress_Party) (3) won seats in the parliament proportionally to the party vote in the 1990 elections.[^cite_note-\:1-11] [Radnaasümbereliyn Gonchigdorj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radnaas%C3%BCmbereliyn_Gonchigdorj) (MSDP) was elected the Little Khural's first chairman and Byaraagiin Chimed (MPRP) the first Chairman of the Secretariat.[^cite_note-\:1-11]

From 1990 to 1992, the Little Khural adopted 27 new laws, ratified 17 international treaties and conventions as well as made amendments to 19 laws.[^cite_note-\:1-11]

After a [new constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Mongolia) was ratified on 13 January 1992, the Little Khural was dissolved and a new unicameral parliament called the State Great Khural was established.[^cite_note-sanders2-5]

### 1992–1996

The 1st State Great Khural had 10 standing committees (reduced to 6 in 1995). In the [1992 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Mongolian_parliamentary_election), the [Mongolian People's Revolutionary Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Party) (70), the [Democratic Union Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Union_Coalition_\(1996%E2%80%932000\)) of the [Mongolian Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Mongolia\)), the [Mongolian National Progress Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_National_Progress_Party) and the [Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_Green_Party) (4), the [Mongolian Social Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_Social_Democratic_Party) (1) and one [independent politician](2172378.md) won seats. The elected chairman was [Natsagiin Bagabandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natsagiin_Bagabandi) (MPRP), and the Chairman of the Secretariat was Namsrain Rechnindorj (MPRP).[^cite_note-\:2-12]

The 1st State Great Khural adopted 137 laws, made amendments to 142 laws, and repealed 46 laws. The parliament also ratified 40 international treaties and conventions during its term.[^cite_note-\:2-12]

### 1996–2000

The 2nd State Great Khural had 5 standing committees in 1996–1997. This increased to 7 standing committees in 1997–2000. The [Democratic Union Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Union_Coalition_\(1996%E2%80%932000\)) (50), The [Mongolian People's Revolutionary Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Party) (25), The Mongolian Conservative United Party (1) won seats in the parliament. The elected chairman was [Radnaasümbereliyn Gonchigdorj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radnaas%C3%BCmbereliyn_Gonchigdorj) (for the second time), and the Chairman of the Secretariat was Logiin Tsog until 1999. The next Chairman of the Secretariat was Baasanganobyn Enebish.[^cite_note-\:3-13]

The 2nd State Great Khural adopted 173 new laws, made amendments to 255 laws and repealed 32 laws. The parliament also ratified 71 international treaties and conventions.[^cite_note-\:3-13]

### 2000–2004

The 3rd State Great Khural had 7 standing committees. The [Mongolian People's Revolutionary Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Party) (72), The [Democratic Union Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Union_Coalition_\(1996%E2%80%932000\)) (1), The [Civil Will Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Will%E2%80%93Green_Party) – The [Mongolian Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_Green_Party) (1), The [Motherland – The Mongolian Democratic New Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motherland_Party_\(Mongolia\)) (1), one independent won a seat in the parliament. The elected chairman was [Lhamsürem Enebish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lhams%C3%BCrem_Enebish?action=edit\&redlink=1) till 2001, and the Chairman of the Secretariat was Baasanganobo Enebish till 2001. The next chairman was [Sanjbegz Tömör-Ochir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjbegz_T%C3%B6m%C3%B6r-Ochir?action=edit\&redlink=1), and the next Chairman of the Secretariat was Dagdankhuu Batbaatar until 2003. The third and final Chairman of the Secretariat during this term was Namsraijav Luvsanjav.[^cite_note-14]

The 3rd State Great Khural adopted 140 new laws, made amendments to 443 laws, and repealed 51 laws. The parliament also ratified 110 international treaties and conventions.

### 2004–2008

The 4th State Great Khural had 11 standing committees as well as 8 subcommittees from 2004 to 2006. The number of standing committees was reduced to 7 in 2006. The [Mongolian People's Revolutionary Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Party) (37), the [Motherland and Democracy Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motherland_Democratic_Coalition) (35), The [Republican Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_\(Mongolia\)) (1) and 3 independents won seats in the parliament. The elected chairman was [Nambaryn Enkhbayar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nambar_Enkhbayar) (MPRP) until 2005, and the Chairman of the Secretariat continued to be Namsraijaviin Luvsanjav (MPRP). The next chairman was [Tsendiin Nyamdorj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsendiin_Nyamdorj) (MPRP) until 2007. The third and final chairman during this term was [Danzangiin Lundeejantsan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danzangiin_Lundeejantsan) (MPRP).[^cite_note-15]

The fourth State Great Khural adopted 89 new laws, made amendments to 336 laws. The parliament also ratified 38 international treaties and conventions as well as repealed 50 laws.

### 2008–2012

The 5th State Great Khural had 7 standing committees and 11 subcommittees. The [Mongolian People's Revolutionary Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Party) (46), The [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Mongolia\)) (27), The [Civil Will Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Will%E2%80%93Green_Party) (1) 8 The [Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_Green_Party) (1) and 1 independent won the seats in the parliament. For 5 months (May to September, 2008), Danzangiin Sandang-Ochir was the Chairman of the Secretariat. The elected chairman was [Damdiny Demberel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damdiny_Demberel) (MPRP), and the second Chairman of the Secretariat was Tserenkhuugiin Sharavdorj (MPRP).[^cite_note-\:4-16]

The 5th State Great Khural adopted 111 new laws and made amendments to 485 laws. The parliament also ratified 59 international treaties and conventions as well as repealed 70 laws.[^cite_note-\:4-16]

### 2012–2016



Parliamentary session in March 2014.

For the first time, the [2012 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Mongolian_parliamentary_election) was held using a [mixed electoral system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_voting), which combined [plurality block voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurality_block_voting) and [proportional representation](23298.md). 48 seats were elected directly from 26 multi-member constituencies and 28 seats by [closed list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_list) [proportional representation](23298.md).

The 6th State Great Khural had 8 standing committees and 10 subcommittees. The [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Mongolia\)) (34), The [Mongolian People's Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Party) (26), The [Justice Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_Coalition_\(Mongolia\)) of the [Mongolian People's Revolutionary Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Revolutionary_Party_\(2010\)) – The [Mongolian National Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_National_Democratic_Party_\(2005\)) (11), The [Civil Will–Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Will%E2%80%93Green_Party)(2), and 3 independents won seats in the parliament. The elected chairman was [Zandaakhuugiin Enkhbold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zandaakh%C3%BC%C3%BCgiin_Enkhbold) (DP), and the Chairman of the Secretariat was Byambadorjiin Boldbaatar (DP).[^cite_note-17]

Also for the first time in Mongolia, electronic voting machines were used for voter registration, vote counting and monitoring purposes.

### 2016–2020

The law on the amendments to the [election law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election_law) was adopted by the parliament in May 2016 and as a result, the [2016 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Mongolian_parliamentary_election) was held under a [first-past-the-post system](261709.md) in 76 [single-mandate constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_district).&#x20;

The 7th State Great Khural on its first plenary session approved the composition of standing committees which maintained the previous parliamentary structure of 8 standing committees and 10 subcommittees.[^cite_note-18] The [Mongolian People's Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Party) (65), The [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Mongolia\)) (9), The [Mongolian People's Revolutionary Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Revolutionary_Party_\(2010\)) (1), and 1 independent won seats in the parliament. [Miyeegombyn Enkhbold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyeegombyn_Enkhbold) (MPP) was elected as chairman.[^cite_note-19]

### 2020–2024

The 8th State Great Khural had 11 standing committees, 11 subcommittees, and 3 temporary committees, one of which was established due to the [COVID-19 pandemic in Mongolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Mongolia). The [Mongolian People's Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Party) (62), The [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Mongolia\)) (11), The [Our Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Coalition) (1), The [Right Person Electorate Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_Person_Electorate_Coalition) (1) and 1 independent won seats in the parliament. Incumbent [Gombojavyn Zandanshatar](31495583.md) (MPP) was re-elected as parliamentary speaker.[^cite_note-20]

### 2024–present

Following a constitutional amendment made in July 2023, the number of [member of parliament](98066.md) increased from 76 to 126. The [2024 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Mongolian_parliamentary_election) was held using a [parallel system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_voting), with 78 seats elected from multi-member constituencies and 48 seats elected from [closed list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_list) [proportional representation](23298.md).[^cite_note-\:5-21]

The 9th State Great Khural, the first five-party parliament convened in Mongolia, has 11 standing committees and 5 subcommittees. The [Mongolian People's Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People's_Party) (68), The [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Mongolia\)) (42), The [HUN Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HUN_Party) (8), The [National Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Coalition_\(Mongolia\)) (4) and The [Civil Will–Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Will%E2%80%93Green_Party) (4) won seats in the parliament. [Dashzegviin Amarbayasgalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dashzegviin_Amarbayasgalan) (MPP) was elected as chairman[^cite_note-\:5-21] and Barsurengiin Baasandorj (MPP) as Chairman of the Secretariat.[^cite_note-22]

## Structure



The structure of the main positions in the State Great Khural.

Main article: [List of members of the State Great Khural, 2024–2028](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_members_of_the_State_Great_Khural,_2024%E2%80%932028)

The State Great Khural is unicameral, and consists of 126 members. With mandate of no less than 57 of total members of the State Great Khural, the parliament shall be considered in existence of its powers. A member of the State Great Khural shall be an envoy of the people and shall represent and uphold the interests of all the citizens and the people. The mandate of a member of the State Great Khural shall begin with an oath taken before the State Emblem and expire when newly elected members of the State Great Khural are sworn in.

### Chairman of the State Great Khural

Main articles: [Chairman of the State Great Khural](60646912.md) and [List of chairmen of the State Great Khural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_chairmen_of_the_State_Great_Khural)

The Chairman is the [presiding officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_\(politics\)) of the State Great Khural.

### Vice-Chairman of the State Great Khural

The vice-chairman is elected by each caucus formed by the result of an election. They serve a four-year term, but may be relieved or removed of the position, on grounds defined by law, before their term expiration.

### Chairman's Council

The Chairman's Council consists of the vice-chairman of the State Great Khural, Chairmen of the party caucuses, leaders of parliamentary parties, and Chairmen of standing/temporary committees.

## Functions



The [Government Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Palace_\(Mongolia\)) hosts the State Great Khural

The State Great Khural has both legislative and oversight power in Mongolia.

### Legislative functions

The State Great Kural's principal legislative functions include preparing and carrying-out plenary sessions or standing-committee sittings, discussing drafts of laws or other decisions of the State Great Khural, and adopting, passing and resolving processes.

### Oversight functions

The State Great Khural implements its oversight powers by:

- hearing reports, presentations and briefings from the Cabinet and other organizations directly accountable to the State Great Khural

- questioning the Prime Minister, cabinet members and executives of other organizations directly accountable to the State Great Khural, demanding responses of said enquiries, and generating debate of the responses in its sessions

- assessing the implementation of laws and other resolutions of the State Great Khural at the cabinet and other organizations directly accountable to the State Great Khural; and generating debates at the Standing-Committee sittings or if necessary at the plenary sessions

- delivering verdicts on professional or ethical offences committed by the Prime Minister, by cabinet members, by those who have been appointed by the State Great Khural, or by executives and members of other organizations directly accountable to the State Great Khural

## Elections

Elections are held every four years to elect all members of the State Great Khural. Before 2023, the election used [plurality-at-large voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurality-at-large_voting) in all 26 multi-member constituencies to elect 76 members.

On 31 May 2023, the parliament approved a [constitutional amendment](261586.md) that increased the number of seats from 76 to 126. The method of election was also changed to [parallel voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_voting)[^cite_note-23] with 78 seats elected by [multiple non-transferable vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_non-transferable_vote) in 13 multi-member constituencies and 48 by [closed list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_list) [proportional representation](23298.md) at the national level with an electoral threshold of 4% for individual parties, 5% for a two-party coalition and 7% for coalitions of three or more parties.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-\:0-25] To qualify for proportional seats, parties and coalitions must also have candidates running in at least half of the seats in each constituency. Party lists must adhere to the [zipper system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zipper_system), while the overall gender ratio of candidates for a party must not be greater than 70\:30 or less than 30\:70. A voter turnout of 50% is required for the result in a constituency to be considered valid, or another round of voting must be held for that constituency.[^cite_note-\:0-25][^cite_note-26]

To vote, a Mongolian citizen must be 18 years or older, and live in Mongolia. Any person over 25 is eligible to be elected. New elections are held if the Khural is dissolved, if two-thirds of members vote for dissolution, if the President dissolves the Khural, or if the President or half the Cabinet resigns.

## Sessions

The main organizational form of the State Great Khural is the session. According to the Article 27 of the Constitution, regular sessions of the State Great Khural convene in every six months for not less than 50 working days. Session consists of plenary sessions, exclusive or joint Standing Committee sittings and caucus meetings. The four types of sessions are:

- **Inauguration session** – Convenes, by the President's convocation, within 30 days after the election.

- **Regular session** – Autumn session commences on 1 October, and the spring session commences on 5 April each year.

- **Extra ordinary session** – Convenes at the demand of greater than one third of the members of the State Great Khural, or on the initiative of either the President or the Chairman of the State Great Khural.

- **Emergency session** – Convenes within 72 hours of the President's proclamation of state of emergency or war.

### Committees

The State Great Khural shall have standing committees dealing with specific fields of public policy. The Standing committees are composed of 10–19 members and shall be convened on Tuesday and Wednesday each week. The subcommittee is affiliated with and deals with specific issues within the standing committee. The State Great Khural shall set up temporary committees for reviewing specific issues, making proposals and submitting reports to the plenary sessions.

### Party caucuses

A coalition with 8 or more seats must establish a party caucus. Independents and members of several parties may choose to join a caucus, but may not establish their own. Each caucus must elect a leader, which is then reported to the chairman. The decision to establish a caucus, along with its membership roster, must be submitted within 24 hours of the Chairman's election. The Chairman will then announce these decisions at the plenary session of the State Great Khural.

## Legislation

### Stage 1

The party caucuses or standing committees shall make draft legislation and decide whether to submit it for discussion at the plenary sessions of the State Great Khural. The State Great Khural will then decide whether to discuss the draft legislation. If they decide to discuss it, it shall send the drafts to standing committees to prepare for the first discussion. If they decide not to discuss legislation drafts, then it shall be sent back to its initiators.

### Stage 2

The standing committee shall prepare a legislation draft for the first discussion and shall submit its proposal and conclusion to the State Great Khural. At this stage, party groups are allowed to make comments about the draft. Opinions and proposals of all parties shall be included in the standing committee conclusions. After the voting at the plenary session, drafts shall be sent back to the standing committees for the preparation of the final discussion.

### Stage 3

The standing committees shall include conclusions from the first discussion to the original draft and shall make presentations about the first discussion. They shall also make the introduction of a draft to the State Great Khural. At this stage, the standing committees are allowed to request a re-voting regarding the draft. The draft shall be discussed, voted and fully adopted by the State Great Khural. If the draft is not fully adopted, it shall be sent back to the law initiators.

The standing committees shall prepare the final versions of the legislation drafts and other resolutions of the State Great Khural. The standing committees shall make the introduction of the final versions of the legislation and other resolutions to the State Great Khural. After the introduction of the final versions of the legislations and other resolutions at the State Great Khural, the Chairman of the State Great Khural shall sign the final versions within three working days. The signed legislations and other resolutions of the State Great shall be submitted to the President of Mongolia within 24 hours. If the president vetoes the legislations, the issue shall be discussed again at the State Great Khural. The legislation shall be considered valid after the publication of the legislations in the 'State Information' bulletin.
