**Senatorial elections** were held in France on 27 September 2026[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] to select 178 members of the [French Senate](567323.md) in [indirect elections](652583.md).[^cite_note-4]

## Background

### 2023 Senate elections

See also: [2023 French Senate election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_French_Senate_election)

The 2023 Senate election saw a weakened majority of the [right-wing](56522.md) and [centre](39619035.md) groups, accompanied with the rise of minor groups, such as [the greens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecologist_group_\(Senate\)) and [the independents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independents_%E2%80%93_Republic_and_Territories_group). Prior to the 2026 election, the Senate is composed of three main groups: the [Republicans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_Republicans_\(France\)), the [Socialists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_group_in_the_Senate), and the [Centrist Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrist_Union_group), as well as six minor groups (including unaffiliated senators). Currently the Senate is dominated by the right-wing and centre-right with the Republicans and the Centrist Union holding 189 seats alone between them, with the main opposition coming from the Socialists with their 65 seats.

## Electoral system

All 348 senators in the [Senate](567323.md) are elected by [indirect election](652583.md) through an [electoral college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_college) for six-year terms, however [elections are held every three years to renew the terms of half the senators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staggered_election). The 2026 election will renew the 178 seats previously elected in 2020, also called Series 2 under French electoral law. Senators are elected in [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency) corresponding to the [departments of France](38523.md), and the electoral system depends on the number of seats up for election in the department. If the constituency elects 1 or 2 senators, a modified [two-round system](25932.md) is used to elect the senators. A second round is held if the winning candidate does not secure a majority of the vote and at least a quarter of all registered eligible voters. A [simple plurality](261709.md) is needed to win the 2nd round. Should a tie occur in the second round, the older candidate shall be declared the winner.[^cite_note-5] If the constituency elects 3 or more senators, [party-list proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party-list_proportional_representation) with the highest averages method is used to elect the senators.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] The Electoral Code and a Ministry of the Interior circular require mayors to comply with several obligations and mandatory rules that they must fully understand.[^cite_note-8]
