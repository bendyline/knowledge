**Park Sung-jae** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 박성재) is a South Korean [lawyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawyer) and politician. An [independent](2172378.md), he served as the [minister of justice of South Korea](38952613.md) from 2024 to 2025.

On 12 December 2024, the [National Assembly](601423.md) passed impeachment motions against Park and [KNP Commissioner](11760674.md) Cho Ji-ho,[^cite_note-2] accusing them of their alleged roles in the controversial declaration of [emergency martial law](50477.md)[^cite_note-3] by [President](98630.md) [Yoon Suk Yeol](62877952.md). The passed impeachment motions led to Park and Cho being suspended immediately from their office duties.[^cite_note-4]

Preparatory hearings on whether to uphold or lift Park's removal were held by the [Constitutional Court of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Korea) beginning on 24 February 2025.[^cite_note-5] On 10 April, the court dismissed the impeachment motion in an 8-0 vote, thereby reinstating him immediately.[^cite_note-6]

After [Lee Jae-myung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Jae-myung) was sworn in as president of South Korea on 4 June 2025, the entirety of Yoon's cabinet expressed their will of resignation. Lee refused the resignations except for Park's.[^cite_note-7]

## Role in martial law

On 22 June 2026, Park was found guilty of playing a key role in an insurrection and abusing his power by calling a meeting of senior ministry officials following Yoon's declaration of martial law, to review dispatching prosecutors to the martial law command, checking the capacity of correctional facilities, allegedly to hold politicians and key figures expected to be arrested under the martial law, and order ministry officials in charge of imposing travel bans to report for work. He was sentenced to 25 years in prison and taken to custody immediately after the verdict.[^cite_note-jail_sentence-1]
