# 2026 FIFA World Cup Group G

Group G of the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) took place from June 15 to 26, 2026.[^cite_note-match_schedule-1] The group consisted of [Belgium](679716.md), [Egypt](1022953.md), [Iran](272865.md), and [New Zealand](1023035.md). All of the matches in Group G were played at three venues on the [West Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Coast_of_the_United_States) of the United States and Canada: [SoFi Stadium](45525802.md) in the [Greater Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Los_Angeles) area; [Lumen Field](206812.md) in [Seattle](11388236.md); and [BC Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BC_Place) in [Vancouver](32706.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

Belgium topped the group with a crucial win against New Zealand (by five goals to one), with Egypt finishing as group runners-up based on goal difference. Both teams advanced to the [round of 32](81667005.md#Round_of_32).&#x20;

Iran finished in third place, and were eliminated as they were out-performed by other teams in third place within their groups, failing to finish as one of the [eight best third-place teams](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams).[^cite_note-regulations-4] New Zealand finished fourth after a decisive defeat against Belgium, and were also eliminated.

### Iran vs New Zealand

The two teams have faced each other twice, most recently in the [2003 AFC–OFC Challenge Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_AFC%E2%80%93OFC_Challenge_Cup), which Iran won by 3–0.[^cite_note-14]

[Elijah Just](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elijah_Just) earned his team's lead by making two consecutive goals, but throughout the second half, [Mohammad Mohebi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Mohebi) leveled the Iranian team in the 64th minute as New Zealand struggled to break through the tie. Ultimately, the match ended with a 2–2 draw.

This marked New Zealand having their fourth draw since their last appearance in 2010, marred by their inconsistencies throughout the match. [Ramin Rezaeian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramin_Rezaeian) was chosen as Man of the Match for the Iranian team.

### Egypt vs Iran



Pre-match ceremony between Egypt and Iran

The two teams had faced each other twice, most recently in the [2000 LG Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_LG_Cup_\(Iran\)); Egypt won 8–7 on penalties following a 1–1 draw.[^cite_note-23]

A "[Pride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pride_\(LGBTQ_culture\)) Match" was planned in advance to take place in Seattle, organized by the local committee to tie into the [local celebration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Pride) and without endorsement by FIFA. After the draw and release of the schedule, it was determined that this match would feature Egypt and Iran, two countries where LGBT rights are severely restricted (see [LGBT rights in Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT_rights_in_Egypt) and [Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT_rights_in_Iran)) and homosexuality is [punishable by law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_rights_by_country_or_territory), including the [death penalty](5170842.md) in Iran.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] Seattle's Pride Match Advisory Committee spokesperson said "The Pride Match has been scheduled to celebrate and elevate Pride events in Seattle and across the country and it was planned well in advance."[^cite_note-26] [Iranian Football Federation](1262861.md) President [Mehdi Taj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehdi_Taj) said both countries had "objections" and that the branding was an "irrational move that supports a certain group." Iran was planning to appeal the decision.[^cite_note-27] The [Egyptian Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_Football_Association) also sent a formal letter to FIFA Secretary General [Mattias Grafström](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattias_Grafstr%C3%B6m) rejecting "in absolute terms" LGBTQ connections to the game.[^cite_note-28] A day before the match, Iran requested FIFA to ban [pride flags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pride_flag) inside the stadium, as well as preventing any "ceremonies or promotional activities" in support of the community, which was rejected.[^cite_note-29] Demonstrators against the Iranian government and the criminalization of homosexuality in both competing countries were present around the match, many sporting Pride rainbow.[^cite_note-30]

Following a goal for each team early on, [Shojae Khalilzadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shojae_Khalilzadeh) scored three minutes into stoppage time to seemingly secure Iran's first-ever World Cup knockout berth. However, VAR disallowed the strike for offside, forcing Iran to settle for a 1–1 draw. Following a [3–3 draw](81660202.md#Algeria_vs_Austria) between Algeria and Austria in [Group J](81660202.md) and Senegal's [5–0 victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_I#Senegal_vs_Iraq) over Iraq in [Group I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_I), Iran was eliminated in the group stage. They dropped to 9th place below Senegal in the third-place rankings, marking their seventh overall failure to advance.[^cite_note-31]

With an estimated 107.4 million viewers, this match became the most-watched broadcast in [Middle East and North Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East_and_North_Africa) (MENA) history and the most-viewed match of the FIFA World Cup.[^cite_note-32]

## Discipline

The team conduct ("fair play") score would have been used as a tiebreaker if the head-to-head and overall records of teams were tied. It would also be used as a tiebreaker for the [third-place ranking](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams) between groups if the overall records of teams were tied. The score was calculated based on yellow and red cards received by players and team officials in all group matches as follows:[^cite_note-regulations-4]

- yellow card: −1 point;

- indirect red card (second yellow card): −3 points;

- direct red card: −4 points;

- yellow card and direct red card: −5 points;

Only one of the above deductions could be applied to a player or team official in a single match.
