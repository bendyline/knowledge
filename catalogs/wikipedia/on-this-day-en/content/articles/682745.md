**Commewijne** is a district of [Suriname](26828.md), located on the right bank of the [Suriname River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suriname_River). Commewijne's [capital city](181337.md) is [Nieuw Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nieuw_Amsterdam,_Suriname). [Tamanredjo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamanredjo) is another major town, while [Meerzorg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meerzorg) is the most populated.[^cite_note-census-1]

The district has a population of 31,420 and an [area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area) of 2,353 km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-census-1]

One of the most populated of Suriname's districts, Commewijne relies primarily on [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) for its income, with [plantations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plantation) dating back to the first [Dutch](21148.md) colonisation of the area in the 17th century.[^cite_note-nu1-2] In the 21st century, Meerzorg has been designated as a regional centre, and suburban area for Paramaribo, and is the site of many building projects. Nieuw Amsterdam has been steadily growing as well.[^cite_note-planning-3]

## History

In 1621, the [Dutch West India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_West_India_Company) started to explore [the Guianas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guianas), and traded with the [indigenous people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_people). When a force reporting to [Francis Willoughby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Willoughby,_5th_Baron_Willoughby_of_Parham) landed on Commewijne, there was already a small Dutch settlement who were trading with the indigenous people, and a group of [Jews](25955086.md) had arrived in Suriname in 1632. [Surinam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surinam_\(English_colony\)) was conquered in 1667 by [Abraham Crijnssen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Crijnssen) for the Dutch West India Company.[^cite_note-4] Commewijne became the main location for plantations during the 18th century.[^cite_note-nu1-2] A monument to the [independence of Suriname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Suriname#Independence) is located in Fort Nieuw-Amsterdam.[^cite_note-5]

## Forts

Main article: [Fort Nieuw-Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Nieuw-Amsterdam)

Fort Nieuw-Amsterdam is located in Nieuw Amsterdam. On 10 October 1712, [Jacques Cassard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Cassard) captured the plantation Meerzorg for [France](5843419.md), and threatened [Paramaribo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramaribo) across the Suriname river.[^cite_note-6] To protect Paramaribo and Commewijne from future attacks, Fort Nieuw-Amsterdam was constructed, and opened in 1747.[^cite_note-7]

Several other small colonial forts were constructed. The [redoubts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redoubt) Frederiksdorp, [Braamspunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braamspunt), and Leiden were the first towns connected to a [telephone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telephone) line after Paramaribo to Nieuw-Amsterdam in 1888.[^cite_note-8] The scale of the other forts was small, because when [August Kappler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Kappler) visited Frederiksdorp in 1854, three soldiers were stationed there.[^cite_note-9]

## Nature and tourism

Main article: [Braamspunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braamspunt)

The [Braamspunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braamspunt) Nature Reserve is located in Commewijne, because it is an important nesting ground for the [leatherback sea turtles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leatherback_sea_turtle), and the [green sea turtles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_sea_turtle).[^cite_note-10]

The former plantation of [Frederiksdorp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederiksdorp) is located near [Johan & Margaretha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johan_&_Margaretha), and has in 2004 been designated at as a [monument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument).[^cite_note-11]

## Transport

Commewijne used to be isolated from Paramaribo, In 1931, a [ferry](50771.md) opened between Meerzorg and Paramaribo,[^cite_note-jagtlust-12] but to improve access to the eastern part of Suriname, the [Jules Wijdenbosch Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jules_Wijdenbosch_Bridge) was opened on 20 May 2000 replacing the ferry. The bridge is part of the [East-West Link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East-West_Link_\(Suriname\)).[^cite_note-13]

In 2020, plans were announced to build a bridge between [Laarwijk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laarwijk) and [Domburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domburg) in order to develop the area, and relieve traffic congestion between [Wanica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanica_District) and Commewijne.[^cite_note-14]

## Economy

The economy is still largely built around agriculture, and the industry in the district are mainly related to agriculture as well.[^cite_note-planning-3] The closure of the sugarcane factory [Mariënburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mari%C3%ABnburg) in 1986 caused a regional decline.[^cite_note-15] Meerzorg is becoming a centre for trade. Companies and banks have started to open offices or moved to the town.[^cite_note-planning-3]
