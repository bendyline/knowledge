The **Ferrocarril del Istmo de Tehuantepec**[^cite_note-2] is a government-owned railway system in Mexico that has three lines. It seeks to become a global logistics network focused on the manufacture and movement of goods between the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md) and the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md) through the [Isthmus of Tehuantepec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isthmus_of_Tehuantepec).[^cite_note-3]

The project consists of the renovation of the railroad, which is expected to increase the speed of the freight train from 20 km/h (12 mph) to 70 km/h (43 mph), more than three times the current one. Meanwhile, the passenger train will be able to reach speeds of up to 100 km/h (62 mph).[^cite_note-4]

## History

The state-owned company Ferrocarril del Istmo de Tehuantepec was founded in 1999, to operate the railway that connects Salina Cruz with the locality of [Medias Aguas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medias_Aguas?action=edit\&redlink=1), Veracruz.[^cite_note-5] Mayab and Chiapas lines were formerly leased to [Ferrocarriles Chiapas-Mayab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrocarriles_Chiapas-Mayab) until [Genesee & Wyoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesee_&_Wyoming) gave up its concession in 2007.[^cite_note-6]

### Redevelopment

On 7 June 2020, President [Andrés Manuel López Obrador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Manuel_L%C3%B3pez_Obrador) began the renovation of railway tracks and rehabilitation as passenger transport, the work will have an investment of more than 3 billion pesos to start, but that in total it is planned to allocate about 20 billion pesos in this project.[^cite_note-\:3-7]

On 17 September 2023, President Andrés Manuel López Obrador inaugurated [Line Z](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_Z_\(Tren_Interoce%C3%A1nico\)) for freight transport.[^cite_note-\:1-8] On 22 December, passenger service began on Line Z.[^cite_note-\:2-9] Later, on 13 September 2024, passenger service on [Line FA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_FA_\(Tren_Interoce%C3%A1nico\)) opened.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

A [derailment of Line Z](81973174.md) near [Asunción Ixtaltepec](23203010.md), Oaxaca, on 28 December 2025 left 13 people dead and around 100 more injured.[^cite_note-14]

## Features

As of December 2023, [Line Z](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_Z_\(Tren_Interoce%C3%A1nico\)) served seven stations in 303 kilometres (188 mi) of [single track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-track_railway), traveling between Coatzacoalcos and Salina Cruz for seven hours.[^cite_note-15] The old stations that were on the route and that were chosen to form the project, were completely remodeled, while retaining the original architectural style.[^cite_note-16]

## Lines and routes

- [Line FA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_FA_\(Tren_Interoce%C3%A1nico\)): connects [Coatzacoalcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coatzacoalcos) with [Pakal Ná (Palenque)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakal_N%C3%A1_railway_station) (transfer to [Tren Maya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tren_Maya)), with a branch to Dos Bocas.&#x20;

  - Branch DB (freight only): [Roberto Ayala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Ayala_railway_station) – [Olmeca Refinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olmeca_Refinery?action=edit\&redlink=1) Station of the Port of Dos Bocas in [Paraíso](22113122.md).

- [Line K](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_K_\(Tren_Interoce%C3%A1nico\)): connects [Ixtepec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ixtepec_railway_station) with [Ciudad Hidalgo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciudad_Hidalgo,_Chiapas#Transportation), with a connection to the [Guatemalan rail network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_transport_in_Guatemala).&#x20;

  - Branch KA: [Tapachula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapachula_railway_station) – [Port Chiapas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Chiapas).

- [Line Z](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_Z_\(Tren_Interoce%C3%A1nico\)): connects [Coatzacoalcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coatzacoalcos_railway_station) with [Salina Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salina_Cruz_railway_station), as well as industrial parks.&#x20;

  - ZA Branch: Hibueras – [Minatitlán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minatitl%C3%A1n,_Veracruz) [Refinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A1zaro_C%C3%A1rdenas_del_R%C3%ADo_Refinery?action=edit\&redlink=1).

## Rolling stock

See also: [Line Z (Tren Interoceánico) § Rolling stock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_Z_\(Tren_Interoce%C3%A1nico\)#Rolling_stock)



Map of the various lines of the Tren Interoceánico

In August 2023, the website Railtech.com reported a number of [InterCity 125](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InterCity_125) high-speed train sets, originally built for [British Rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Rail), had been seen at the Port of [Great Yarmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Yarmouth) being loaded on to a ship bound for Mexico. Unconfirmed reports suggested this second-hand rolling stock was bound for the Ferrocarril del Istmo de Tehuantepec.[^cite_note-17]

On 4 September 2023, the news website *Diario del Istmo* posted a story containing a powerpoint slide from the Sistema Portuario Nacional, confirming that a group of three [Class 43](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Rail_Class_43_\(HST\)) locomotives and 11 [Mark 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Rail_Mark_3) coaches had been exported to Mexico for use on Ferrocarril del Istmo de Tehuantepec.[^cite_note-18] In Great Britain, the Class 43 locomotives were numbered [GWR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Western_Railway_\(train_operating_company\)) 43022, 43158, and 43170; while the coaches were numbered [LNER](57431689.md) 41091, 41100, 42026, 42179, 42401, 42402, 42405, 44034, 44061, 44063, 44094.[^cite_note-Formula912-19]

&#x20;In addition to these InterCity 125 trainsets, several other types of rolling stock will be used. The *Ferrocarril Transístmico* purchased three [EMD SD70M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMD_SD70_series#SD70M_\(1992%E2%80%932004\)) locomotives from the [Union Pacific Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Pacific_Railroad), former UP 4378, 4671, and 4674.[^cite_note-Centro_Urbano-20][^cite_note-MXCity-21] and 12 passenger cars (including 3 [Amfleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amfleet) cars and the *Stampede Pass*, a [dome car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dome_car) built by the [Budd Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budd_Company) in 1954) from the Railway Excursion Management Company (Railexco).[^cite_note-22] The first of the SD70Ms, numbered 4378,[^cite_note-23] were presented on 13 August 2023.[^cite_note-Centro_Urbano-20][^cite_note-MXCity-21]



Transport routes in the Isthmus of Tehuantepec. This map shows roads and ports, as well as the railroad; the map legend is in Spanish.

Meanwhile, in September 2023, an [EMD F59PH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMD_F59PH) locomotive was seen in [Mobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile,_Alabama), [Alabama](303.md), reportedly on its way to the *Tren Interoceánico*. Later, an F59PH, now numbered FIT 3005 would, alongside an SD70M numbered FIT 4671,[^cite_note-24] be used on the inaugural run of Line Z on 22 December that year.[^cite_note-25] FIT 3005 was originally [GO Transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GO_Transit) 520, and during the late-2010s, this unit (then numbered LTEX 18520) was one of three ex-GO Transit F59PHs leased to [Metrolink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrolink_\(California\)) in the late-2010s. After Metrolink retired the ex-GO units in 2022, 18520 became one of two FIT F59PHs, with the other being the former LTEX 18522 (ex-GOT 522).[^cite_note-Trainweb_Metrolink-26]

Furthermore, five [Budd SPV-2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budd_SPV-2000) cars were purchased from Railexco for use on the *Tren Interoceánico*.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] According to Railexco's official Facebook account, these cars were once owned by [Amtrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amtrak), and later, by the [Connecticut Department of Transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut_Department_of_Transportation).[^cite_note-29]

In addition to these, on 26 August 2023, the two [Stadler Citylink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadler_Citylink) sets from the former [Puebla–Cholula Tourist Train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puebla%E2%80%93Cholula_Tourist_Train) were brought to [Coatzacoalcos railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coatzacoalcos_railway_station) for use on the Ferrocarril Transístmico.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31]
