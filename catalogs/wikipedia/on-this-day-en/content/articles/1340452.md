The **Sabaragamuwa Province** (IPA: [\[səbəɾəɡəmuʋə\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA), [Sinhala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhala_language): සබරගමුව පළාත, romanised: *Sabaragamuwa Paḷāta*, [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language): சபரகமுவ மாகாணம், romanised: *Sabaragamuva Mākāṇam*) is one of the nine [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Sri_Lanka) of [Sri Lanka](26750.md). [Ratnapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratnapura) is the capital of the province.

## History

The provinces of Sri Lanka were created by the [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) in the 19th century, but did not have any legal status until 1987 when the [13th Amendment to the Constitution of Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13th_Amendment_to_the_Constitution_of_Sri_Lanka) established [provincial councils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_councils_of_Sri_Lanka).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

The province is named after its former indigenous inhabitants, namely the Sabara (शबर *Śabara*), an Indic term for hunter-gatherers (*[mleccha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mleccha)*) and the Sinhala *gamuwa*, "gathering place."[^cite_note-7]

The [Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabaragamuwa_University_of_Sri_Lanka) is located in the town of [Belihuloya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belihuloya), and was founded in 1991.

The province has an area of 4,968 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 1,918,880. Major towns include [Ratnapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratnapura) and [Kegalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kegalle).

### Ethnic groups

Ethnicity in Sabaragamuwa Province (2024)

The [Sinhalese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhalese_people) are the majority ethnic group of the Sabaragamuwa province. Additionally, there are sizeable minority populations of [Indian Tamils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Tamils_of_Sri_Lanka), [Moors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_Moors) and [Sri Lankan Tamils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_Tamils).
