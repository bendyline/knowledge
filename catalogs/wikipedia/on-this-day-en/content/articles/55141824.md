**First Lady** ([Kyrgyz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_language): биринчи айым, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Kyrgyz): *birinçi ayım*) or **First Gentleman of Kyrgyzstan** is the title attributed to the spouse of the [president of Kyrgyzstan](1569760.md).[^cite_note-akipress-1][^cite_note-novastan-2] The current first lady is [Aigul Japarova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aigul_Japarova), the wife of President [Sadyr Japarov](65513403.md). To date, there has been no official first gentleman of Kyrgyzstan. The country's first female president, [Roza Otunbayeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roza_Otunbayeva), was divorced during her presidency.
