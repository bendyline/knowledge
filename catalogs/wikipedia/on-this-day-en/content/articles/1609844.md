The **National Assembly** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Asamblea Nacional*) is the [legislative branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) of the government of [Nicaragua](21362.md) founded in January 1985.

## History

The unicameral National Assembly replaced the bicameral [National Congress of Nicaragua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Congress_of_Nicaragua) which was disbanded following the overthrow of [Somoza government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somoza_family) in 1979. There was an interim Council of State with 47 and later 51 appointed members from 1980 to 1984.[^cite_note-3] First elections to the National Assembly took place in [November 1984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984_Nicaraguan_general_election), and the first National Assembly took legislative functions from the [Junta of National Reconstruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junta_of_National_Reconstruction) on 10 January 1985.[^cite_note-leghistory-1]

## Composition



The Nicaraguan legislature is a [unicameral](204304.md) body. It is made up of 92 [deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies), 90 of whom are elected by popular vote on a [proportional representation](23298.md) basis from party lists: 20 nationally, and 70 representing the country's [departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Nicaragua) and autonomous regions. In addition, the [President of the Republic](302862.md) who served the immediately previous presidential term is entitled to sit in the Assembly as a deputy, as is the runner-up in the most recent presidential election. The President and the National Assembly serve concurrent five-year terms.

To be eligible for election to the assembly, candidates must be (Art. 134, Constitution):

- Nicaraguan nationals. If they have held any other nationality, it must have been renounced at least four years prior to the election.

- In full enjoyment of their political and civic rights.

- At least 21 years old.

- Residents of the country for at least four years prior to the election (exemptions are granted to members of the diplomatic corps and employees of international organisations, and to those recently returned from studying abroad).

- Born in the department or autonomous region they hope to represent, or having resided there for at least two years prior to the election.

The following are disqualified from serving in the Assembly:

- Ministers, vice-ministers, and other members of the cabinet, magistrates and judges, and mayors, unless they resign from those positions at least 12 months prior to the election.

- Ministers of any church or religious organization, unless they have relinquished their ministry at least 12 months in advance of the election.

Four months before the [2016 Nicaraguan general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Nicaraguan_general_election), the Nicaraguan Supreme Court removed PLI leader [Eduardo Montealegre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Montealegre), decreeing that Pedro Reyes was the new leader of the PLI. After PLI and allied [Sandinista Renovation Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandinista_Renovation_Movement) deputies objected, Nicaragua's Supreme Electoral Council ordered them removed from the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Nicaragua) and empowered Reyes to select their replacements.[^cite_note-4]

## Parliamentary groups

The deputies are organized in Parliamentary Groups (*bancadas*). The current number of deputies of the parliamentary political parties is:

- [Sandinista National Liberation Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandinista_National_Liberation_Front) (FSLN): 71 deputies

- [Constitutionalist Liberal Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutionalist_Liberal_Party) (PLC): 14 deputies

- [Independent Liberal Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Liberal_Party_\(Nicaragua\)) (PLI): 2 deputies

- [Nicaraguan Liberal Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicaraguan_Liberal_Alliance) (ALN): 2 deputies

- [Conservative Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_Party_\(Nicaragua\)) (PC): 1 deputy

- [Alliance for the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_for_the_Republic_\(Nicaragua\)) (APRE): 1 deputy

- [YATAMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YATAMA): 1 deputy
