**Butambala District** is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Uganda) in the [Central Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Region,_Uganda) of [Uganda](31816.md).

## Location

This district is bordered by [Gomba District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gomba_District) to the west and north-west, [Mityana District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mityana_District) to the north-east, [Mpigi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mpigi_District) to the east and south, and [Kalungu District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalungu_District) to the south-west. The district headquarters at [Gombe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gombe,_Butambala,_Uganda) are approximately 31 kilometres (19 mi), by road, west of the town of [Mpigi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mpigi), the largest metropolitan area in the sub-region.[^cite_note-1] This is approximately 68 kilometres (42 mi), by road, south-west of [Kampala](56689.md), [Uganda](31816.md)'s capital and largest city.[^cite_note-2]

## History

Created by an act of parliament, the district became operational on 1 July 2010, having been split off of [Mpigi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mpigi_District), together with neighboring [Gomba District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gomba_District). Before becoming district, the area was Butambala County, one of the eighteen counties that constitute the [Kingdom of Buganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Buganda).[^cite_note-3]

## Population

The national population census in 1991 estimated the district population at 74,100. The 2002 census put the population at about 86,800. In 2012, the population was estimated a 99,400.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Economic activity

[Subsistence agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsistence_agriculture) and small-scale animal husbandry are the backbone of Butambala District's economy. The main food crops are:[^cite_note-6]

- [Beans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beans)

- [Groundnuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambara_groundnut)

- [Sugar cane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_cane)

- [Bananas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bananas)

- [Matooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matooke)

- [Maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize)

- [Mangoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangoes)
