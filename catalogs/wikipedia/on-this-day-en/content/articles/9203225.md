**Kolwezi International Airport** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **KWZ**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **FZQM**) is an [airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport) serving [Kolwezi](1331550.md), the capital of [Lualaba District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lualaba_District) in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md). The airport is 6 kilometres (3.7 mi) south of Kolwezi.

The Kolwezi [non-directional beacon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-directional_beacon) (Ident: **KWZ**) is located 0.7 nautical miles (1.3 km) west of the airport.[^cite_note-3]

## History

On 11 June 2025, [Lualaba](4225850.md) Vice-Governor [Fifi Masuka Saini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifi_Masuka_Saini) and President [Félix Tshisekedi](57511508.md) inaugurated the international terminal of the airport in a ribbon-cutting ceremony.[^cite_note-4] It was broadcast on national television and YouTube by [RTNC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTNC).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The terminal is fully compliant with ICAO standards and is updated with new amenities.[^cite_note-7]

## Accidents and incidents

On 17 November 2025 an [Embraer ERJ145LR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embraer_ERJ145LR), operating as [AirJet Angola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airjet) flight MBC100, carrying about 20 people, including the Minerals Minister, Louis Watum Kabamba, [overshot the runway](9203225.md) while attempting to land. The aircraft then ignited. All passengers were able to evacuate without any injuries. The aircraft was responding to an earlier bridge collapse at a mine which had killed 49 people.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]
