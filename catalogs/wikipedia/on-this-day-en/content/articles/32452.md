[Vanuatu](32443.md) maintains diplomatic relations with many countries, and it has a small network of [diplomatic missions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_missions_of_Vanuatu). [Australia](4689264.md), [France](5843419.md), [Japan](15573.md), [New Zealand](4913064.md), the [People's Republic of China](5405.md), [South Korea](27019.md) and the [United Kingdom](31717.md) maintain embassies, High Commissions, or missions in [Port Vila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Vila). The [British High Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_High_Commission) maintained a continued presence for almost a century, though closed from 2005 until reopening in 2019.[^cite_note-1]

The government's main concern has been to bolster the economy. In keeping with its need for financial assistance, Vanuatu has joined the [Asian Development Bank](2512.md), the [World Bank](36752.md), the [International Monetary Fund](15251.md) (IMF). According to [ABC Radio Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABC_Radio_Australia), "Foreign policy issues that feature in Vanuatu include wide support for the [Free West Papua Movement](541409.md) and broadly for independence throughout [Melanesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanesia), the [One China Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_China_Policy) and relations with Australia and New Zealand." On the latter topic, [guest worker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guest_worker) programmes feature prominently.[^cite_note-2]

## History

### 1980s: the Lini policies

Vanuatu (formerly the [New Hebrides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hebrides)) obtained independence from France and the [United Kingdom](31717.md) in 1980. The country's first elected leader, Prime Minister Father [Walter Lini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Lini), governed Vanuatu from 1980 to 1991, and shaped its initial foreign policy in distinct ways. The key bases of Lini's foreign policy were [non-alignment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutrality_\(international_relations\)) and [anti-colonialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-colonialism), support for independence movements around the world - from faraway [Western Sahara](33220.md) to neighbouring [New Caledonia](21342.md), as well as [East Timor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Timor) and [West Papua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Papua_\(region\)), who all received Vanuatu's support at the [United Nations](31769.md).[^cite_note-HUFFER,_Elise_1993,_pp._272–282-3]

Vanuatu notably angered [Indonesia](14579.md) by allowing the [Free Papua Movement](541409.md) to open an office in [Port-Vila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port-Vila). Vanuatu in the 1980s was the only country in Oceania not to align with the [Western bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_bloc) in the dying stages of the [Cold War](325329.md). Rejecting support either for the West or for the [East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_bloc), Vanuatu joined the [Non-Aligned Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Aligned_Movement) in 1983, and only established diplomatic relations with the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union%E2%80%93Vanuatu_relations) and the [United States](3434750.md) in June and September 1986, respectively.[^cite_note-HUFFER,_Elise_1993,_pp._272–282-3]

In keeping with this policy, Vanuatu established [diplomatic relations with Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanuatu%E2%80%93Cuba_relations) in 1983, and [with Libya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanuatu%E2%80%93Libya_relations) in 1986. Lini openly condemned the [1986 bombing of Libya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_bombing_of_Libya) by the United States, sending a message of condoleances to Colonel [Muammar Gaddafi](53029.md), while [Barak Sopé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barak_Sop%C3%A9) accused the United States of being a [State sponsor of terrorism](317645.md). The same accusation was levelled by Vanuatu against France after the sinking of the *[Rainbow Warrior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainbow_Warrior_\(1955\))*. [Relations with the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States%E2%80%93Vanuatu_relations) were tense until the late 1980s, when a State visit to [Washington](108956.md) by Lini and Foreign Affairs Minister [Sela Molisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sela_Molisa) contributed to a lessening of tensions. [Relations with France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France%E2%80%93Vanuatu_relations) remained strained throughout the 1980s for [a variety of reasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France%E2%80%93Vanuatu_relations#1980s:_off_to_a_rocky_start).[^cite_note-4]

Lini's government opposed [French nuclear tests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_nuclear_tests) at [Mururoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mururoa), and spoke out repeatedly against [apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid) in [South Africa](17416221.md). Vanuatu was a member of the [United Nations Special Committee against Apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Special_Committee_against_Apartheid). In 1990, Vanuatu's ambassador to the United Nations [Robert Van Lierop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Van_Lierop) remarked proudly: "I think that Vanuatu's contribution to the United Nations is somewhat disproportionate in relation to its size. \[...] When the Prime Minister met [Nelson Mandela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_Mandela) in [Namibia](21292.md), Mandela knew about Vanuatu because it has always been among the countries in the region that have most clearly spoken out on the problem of apartheid".[^cite_note-5]

Vanuatu under Walter Lini also sought to create solid relations with [Asia](689.md), and, by the end of the decade, had established official diplomatic relations with the [People's Republic of China](5405.md), [Japan](15573.md), [South Korea](27019.md), [North Korea](21255.md), [Thailand](30128.md), [Malaysia](3607937.md), [Singapore](27318.md), [Vietnam](202354.md) and the [Philippines](23440.md).[^cite_note-6]

### 1990s: the Carlot Korman and Vohor years

Following the [1991 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Vanuatuan_general_election), the [francophone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language) [Union of Moderate Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Moderate_Parties) became the dominant party in Parliament, and [Maxime Carlot Korman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxime_Carlot_Korman) became the country's first francophone Prime Minister. He "reversed \[the country's] unequivocal support for the [Kanak National Liberation Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanak_and_Socialist_National_Liberation_Front) in New Caledonia, its systematic enmity towards France, its flirting with radical regimes, and its openly anti-American nuclear-free Pacific stance." Francophones held power, under Carlot Korman or [Serge Vohor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serge_Vohor), until 1998.[^cite_note-7]

## Foreign policy issues

### Aid

Since 1980, Australia, the United Kingdom, France, and New Zealand have provided the bulk of Vanuatu's development aid. As of March 2008, Australia was Vanuatu's biggest aid provider, followed by France.[^cite_note-8] A number of other countries, including [Japan](15573.md), [Canada](5042916.md), [Germany](11867.md), and various multilateral organizations, such as the [Economic and Social Council for Asia and the Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_and_Social_Council_for_Asia_and_the_Pacific?action=edit\&redlink=1), the [UN Development Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Development_Programme), the Asian Development Bank, the [European Economic Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Economic_Community), and the [Commonwealth Development Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Development_Corporation) also provide developmental aid. The [United States](3434750.md), Canada, [Australia](4689264.md), [New Zealand](4913064.md), the [United Kingdom](31717.md), South Korea and Japan also send volunteers. Since the mid-2000s, Cuba has been a noted provider of medical aid.

### Support to the right of self-determination

Vanuatu continues to promote the right to [self-determination](29269.md). In the 1980s, the [SADR](38762388.md) and [Palestine](241405.md) was recognized. Later, Vanuatu recognized [Kosovo](17391.md) in 2010 and [Abkhazia](18933375.md) in 2011. Vanuatu is the only country in the world that recognizes all four of these states. This makes Vanuatu the only country in Oceania to officially recognize each of Palestine, the Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic and Abkhazia as of August 2025. In addition, Vanuatu strongly supports the [Free Papua Movement](541409.md) and its program of self-determination of [West Papua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Papua_\(region\)), a region in [Indonesia](14579.md), in the midst of [Papua conflict](21657843.md). Vanuatu's bringing up the issue at international forums has brought sharp rebukes from Indonesia; according to its diplomats, "it is shameful that \[Vanuatu has an] excessive and unhealthy obsession about how Indonesia should govern itself" and states that Vanuatu is "not a representation of the people of Papua, and stop fantasising of being one."[^cite_note-9]

#### *Wantok Blong Yumi Bill*

In June 2010, the [Parliament of Vanuatu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Vanuatu) unanimously gave its support to a motion – the [Wantok Blong Yumi Bill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wantok_Blong_Yumi_Bill) – clarifying Vanuatu's foreign policy with regards to West Papuan independence claims from Indonesia. The bill, tabled by Independent MP [Ralph Regenvanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Regenvanu) and supported by Prime Minister [Edward Natapei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Natapei) and opposition leader [Maxime Carlot Korman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxime_Carlot_Korman), committed Vanuatu to recognising West Papua's independence; to seeking observer status for West Papua in the [Melanesian Spearhead Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanesian_Spearhead_Group) and in the [Pacific Islands Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islands_Forum); and to "request\[ing] \[United Nations] [General Assembly](31957.md) support for the [International Court of Justice](14918.md) to provide an advisory opinion on the process in which the former [Dutch New Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_New_Guinea) was ceded to Indonesia in the 1960s".[^cite_note-RNZI_54332-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## International organizational participation

### Regional relations

Vanuatu maintains strong regional ties in the Pacific. It is a full member of the [Pacific Islands Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islands_Forum), the [South Pacific Applied Geoscience Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Pacific_Applied_Geoscience_Commission), the [South Pacific Tourism Organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Pacific_Tourism_Organisation), the [Pacific Regional Environment Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Regional_Environment_Programme) and the [Secretariat of the Pacific Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretariat_of_the_Pacific_Community). Vanuatu is one of the eight signatories of the [Nauru Agreement Concerning Cooperation in the Management of Fisheries of Common Interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nauru_Agreement) which collectively controls 25-30% of the world's tuna supply and approximately 60% of the western and central Pacific tuna supply.[^cite_note-13] Vanuatu endorsed the [Treaty of Rarotonga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Rarotonga) (the South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone Treaty) in 1995.[^cite_note-14]

Vanuatu has been a member of [The Forum of Small States (FOSS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Forum_of_Small_States_\(FOSS\)) since the group's founding in 1992.[^cite_note-singaporebook-15]

### Extra-regional organizational relations

Vanuatu has been a member of the [Organisation internationale de la Francophonie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organisation_internationale_de_la_Francophonie) since 1979 (the year before it gained independence from France). Vanuatu was admitted to the [Commonwealth of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations) in 1980 and to the [United Nations](31769.md) in 1981. Vanuatu is currently the only Pacific nation that belongs to the [Non-Aligned Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Aligned_Movement),

Additionally outside the region, Vanuatu is a member or participant of the [ACP (Lomé Convention)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACP_\(Lom%C3%A9_Convention\)), the [Alliance of Small Island States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_of_Small_Island_States), [Asian Development Bank](2512.md), [Economic and Social Commission for Asia and the Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_and_Social_Commission_for_Asia_and_the_Pacific) (ESCAP), the [Food and Agriculture Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_and_Agriculture_Organization) (FAO), the [G-77](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_of_77), the [International Bank for Reconstruction and Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Bank_for_Reconstruction_and_Development), the [International Civil Aviation Organization](14985.md), the [International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement](15487.md), the [International Development Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Development_Association), the [International Finance Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Finance_Corporation), the [IMF](15251.md), the [International Maritime Organization](14986.md), the [International Olympic Committee](15147.md), the [International Telecommunication Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Telecommunication_Union) (ITU), the [Universal Postal Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Postal_Union) and the [World Meteorological Organization](33584.md). Vanuatu became a member of the WTO in 2012. Vanuatu is also a non-signatory user of [Intelsat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelsat).

Vanuatu became a member of [Interpol](14338129.md) in 2018 and is currently a suspended member of the International Hydrographic Organization - since 2019.

On 4 December 2020, Vanuatu became the sixth ever nation to graduate from the United Nations official list of [Least Developed Countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Least_developed_countries) (LDC). After meeting graduation thresholds in the Human Assets Index and income in 2006, 2009 and 2012, the UN [Committee for Development Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_for_Development_Policy) recommended graduation. The UN Economic and Social Council and UN General Assembly approved of the recommendation in 2012 and 2013, but after [Cyclone Pam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Pam) Vanuatu was granted an extension until 2020. The country lost exclusive access to certain international support measures such as in the areas of development assistance and trade.[^cite_note-16]

| [Abkhazia](18933375.md) | Main article: [Abkhazia–Vanuatu relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abkhazia%E2%80%93Vanuatu_relations) On 23 May 2011, Vanuatu became the fifth [UN member state](31969.md) (after [Russia](25391.md), [Nicaragua](21362.md), [Venezuela](32374.md) and [Nauru](21302.md)) to recognise [Abkhazia](18933375.md). On this day joint statement on establishment of diplomatic relations was signed.[^cite_note-37] At the same time of signing a visa-free travel regime between the two countries was established.[^cite_note-mfaabkhazpacific-38][^cite_note-39] Foreign Affairs Minister [Alfred Carlot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Carlot?action=edit\&redlink=1) said that "Vanuatu's foreign policy aims at eradicating colonialism from the face of the earth." In a press release, Carlot stated: "Vanuatu is neutral; our recognition of Abkhazia does not in any way mean that we cannot have diplomatic relations with the Republic of [Georgia](48768.md)."[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41] The following month, however, the Vanuatuan government of Prime Minister [Sato Kilman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sato_Kilman) was voided by the [Supreme Court of Vanuatu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Vanuatu), on the grounds that Kilman's election in December 2010 had not conformed to constitutional requirements. Former Prime Minister [Edward Natapei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Natapei) became interim prime minister until a new leader could be elected. Natapei promptly withdrew Vanuatu's recognition of Abkhazia, arguing that it had been granted by an illegitimate government, and announced that he would seek to establish diplomatic relations with Georgia, recognising its sovereignty over Abkhazia.[^cite_note-42] On 26 June 2011, [Sato Kilman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sato_Kilman) was re-elected Prime Minister[^cite_note-RNZI_61422-43] and on 12 July 2011 Vanuatu's Foreign Minister Alfred Carlot re-confirmed Vanuatu's recognition of Abkhazia.[^cite_note-44] On 12 July 2011 the Ambassador of Abkhazia in the Asia-Pacific region [Juris Gulbis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juris_Gulbis?action=edit\&redlink=1) stated, that Abkhazia and Vanuatu plans to sign a framework agreement on cooperation in the field of culture, trade and banking sector. According to him, the Government of Vanuatu twice confirmed the establishment of diplomatic relations with Abkhazia and of their intention to contribute to the development of friendly ties between the two States.[^cite_note-45] In 2013, Vanuatu established diplomatic relations with [Georgia](48768.md) and recognized Abkhazia as a part of that country. |

Furthermore, the delegation made a tour around the Memory Wall, got familiar with its meaning and significance. | [Australia](4689264.md) | Australia and Vanuatu have very strong ties. Australia has provided the bulk of Vanuatu's military assistance, training its [paramilitary](146730.md) mobile force and also providing patrol boats to patrol Vanuatu's waters. In 1983, Vanuatu and Australia entered into a Defence Cooperation Program together. As part of this program, two [Royal Australian Navy](26327.md) advisers are stationed in Vanuatu Australia to assist Vanuatu in maintaining and operating the [RVS Tukoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RVS_Tukoro), the [Pacific class patrol boat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_class_patrol_boat) donated to Vanuatu by Australia in 1987. The [RVS Tukoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RVS_Tukoro) is Vanuatu's primarily maritime police vessel. Australia also provides assistance to the Mobile Force element of the [Vanuatu Police Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanuatu_Police_Force) (VPF), as well as providing support to exercises and infrastructure projects.[^cite_note-dfat.gov.au-48] Australia is Vanuatu's largest source of foreign direct investment, mostly directed in the areas of tourist development, agriculture and construction. Australia is Vanuatu's largest source of tourists, with Australians making up 2/3 of all long-term tourist visitors and virtually all cruise ship visits. Since 2008, Vanuatu (along with Tonga, Kiribati and Papua New Guinea) has sent seasonal workers to Australia through the Australian government-funded Pacific Seasonal Workers Pilot Scheme (PSWPS).[^cite_note-dfat.gov.au-48] Additionally, Australia is Vanuatu's main source of foreign aid, with the Australian providing A\$66 million(US\$70.4 million) in 2010–11.[^cite_note-dfat.gov.au-48] In 2004, Australia threatened to cut its aid to Vanuatu when then-Prime Minister [Serge Vohor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serge_Vohor) reappointed officials who had been dismissed after being charged with criminal activity.[^cite_note-49] Vanuatu agreed to Australian demands for more transparent government and anti-corruption steps. The result was that from 2005 to 2010, Australian aid was governed through the Australia–Vanuatu Joint Development Cooperation Strategy. This was superseded with the signing in May 2009 of the Australia-Vanuatu Partnership for Development. The central focus of the Partnership is for Australia to assist Vanuatu in achieving its UN [Millennium Development Goals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennium_Development_Goals) (MDG's). The priorities stated in the Partnership are to •support increased access and quality of education for boys and girls and equip them with relevant skills and knowledge •strengthen health services and accelerate progress towards health MDGs •develop essential infrastructure to support economic growth and service delivery •progress reform on economic governance •address equality of opportunity for all men and women and include the needs and priorities of people with disability in development activities[^cite_note-50] ([full text of Partnership agreement](https://web.archive.org/web/20110602010019/http://www.ausaid.gov.au/hottopics/pdf/Australia_Vanuatu_Partnership_for_Development.pdf)) On 29 June 2026, Australia and Vanuatu signed a [bilateral economic and security agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakamal_Agreement) barring the establishment of any foreign military base on the island state.[^cite_note-Blackburn_29_Jun_2026-51] |
| [China](5405.md) | See [China–Vanuatu relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China%E2%80%93Vanuatu_relations) China established an embassy in Vanuatu in 1989, while Vanuatu established an honorary consulate in China in 1999; it officially became an embassy in 2005.[^cite_note-52] Vanuatu briefly recognized the [Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China) (Taiwan) in late 2004 when on 3 November Prime Minister [Serge Vohor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serge_Vohor) signed a communiqué in [Taipei](57648.md) with ROC Foreign Minister [Mark Chen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Chen). Taipei had offered \$30 million in aid in return (compared with the \$10 million given by the PRC). Under the [One-China policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-China_policy), this would result in the severing of ties with the People's Republic of China. Vohor did so without consultations with his cabinet and the PRC Foreign Ministry, quoting the Vanuatu Foreign Minister, denied ties with the ROC had been established. The Vanuatu Council of Ministers, in the Prime Minister's absence, announced on 11 November that the communiqué had been withdrawn. A spokesman for the Prime Minister denied this a day later. There were reports that previous attempts by Vohor to travel to Taipei were thwarted amid pressure from Beijing so his latest visit was done secretly on purpose. For a period of few weeks, both the PRC and ROC had diplomatic missions posted in Vanuatu while the Vanuatu government was in internal disagreement. At one point Prime Minister Serge Vohor punched the PRC ambassador when approached to explain why the [flag of the Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_the_Republic_of_China) was flying over the hotel where the Taiwanese representative was posted. The standoff ended on 11 December 2004 when the parliament passed a motion of no-confidence against Vohor and replaced him with [Ham Lini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ham_Lini). In May 2009, Vanuatu appointed its first ever ambassador to China, former Minister of Finance [Willie Jimmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Jimmy).[^cite_note-Minister_confirms_appointment-53] Jimmy "call\[ed] \[...] for China to have a foot firmly planted in the Pacific through [Port Vila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Vila)", which -the *[Vanuatu Daily Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanuatu_Daily_Post)* remarked- "no doubt caused ruffled feathers among other foreign diplomatic partners".[^cite_note-dailypost.vu-54] On the Chinese parade due to the 70th Anniversary of the end of [World War II](32927.md), policemen from Vanuatu participated.[^cite_note-55] On 27 February 2017, the Chinese ambassador to Vanuatu, handed over the keys for 14 military vehicles to the [Vanuatu Mobile Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Vanuatu). This was done as an effort to safeguard Vanuatu's sovereignty.[^cite_note-56] In 2023, China and Vanuatu established policing ties, with China donating drones, patrol boats and vehicles to Vanuatu's police.[^cite_note-Blackburn_29_Jun_2026-51] |
