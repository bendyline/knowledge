The **Seoul Central District Court** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 서울중앙지방법원) is a district court with jurisdiction over [Gangnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangnam), [Gwanak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwanak_District), [Dongjak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongjak_District), [Seocho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seocho_District), [Jongno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jongno), and [Jung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jung_District,_Seoul) in [Seoul](19159283.md), South Korea.[^cite_note-1]

## History

The court was established on March 25, 1895, when the Court Constitution Act was revised.[^cite_note-2]
