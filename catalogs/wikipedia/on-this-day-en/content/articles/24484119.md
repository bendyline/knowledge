**Filippo Grandi** (born 30 March 1957) is an Italian humanitarian and [United Nations](31769.md) official, who has served as [United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees](31964.md) from 2016 to 2025.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] He previously served as Commissioner-General of the [United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees in the Near East (UNRWA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Relief_and_Works_Agency_for_Palestine_Refugees_in_the_Near_East)[^cite_note-3] and [United Nations](31769.md) Deputy Special Representative for [Afghanistan](737.md). He received the Olympic Laureate award during the Paris 2024 Olympics Opening Ceremony.

## Education

Grandi graduated with a degree in modern history from the [University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan) in 1981, and with a bachelor's degree in philosophy from the [Gregorian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_University) in Rome in 1987.[^cite_note-4]

## Career

Grandi started his career in the office of the [United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees](31964.md) (UNHCR) in 1988, and has served in a variety of countries, including [Sudan](27421.md), [Syria](7515849.md), [Turkey](11125639.md), and [Iraq](7515928.md) after the [Gulf War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War). He also headed a number of emergency operations including in [Kenya](188171.md), [Benin](3459.md), [Ghana](12067.md), [Liberia](17791.md), the [Great Lakes Region of Central Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Great_Lakes#African_Great_Lakes_Region), [Yemen](350939.md) and [Afghanistan](737.md). Between 1996 and 1997, he was Field Coordinator for UNHCR and United Nations humanitarian activities in the [Democratic Republic of Congo](76762.md) during the [civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Congo_War). From 1997 to 2001, he worked in the Executive Office of the UNHCR in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva), as Special Assistant and then Chief of Staff. From 2001 to 2004, he served as the UNHCR's Chief of Mission.



Grandi in [Djibouti](17207794.md) with [Penny Mordaunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penny_Mordaunt)

Grandi then moved to the [United Nations Assistance Mission in Afghanistan](1234277.md) (UNAMA) in 2004, where he served as Deputy Special Representative of the Secretary-General responsible for political affairs from 2004 to 2005. In 2005 he moved to UNRWA, first as Deputy Commissioner-General and then from 2010 as Commissioner-General until 29 March 2014.

On 11 November 2015 UN Secretary-General [Ban Ki-moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ban_Ki-moon) announced his intention to appoint Grandi as the next [United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees](31964.md) to take office in 2016.[^cite_note-5] On 23 November 2020, the [United Nations General Assembly](31957.md) re-elected Grandi for an additional 2.5 year mandate as High Commissioner for Refugees.[^cite_note-6]

Since 2019, Grandi has been a member of the [World Economic Forum](172185.md) High-Level Group on Humanitarian Investing, co-chaired by [Børge Brende](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%B8rge_Brende), [Kristalina Georgieva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristalina_Georgieva) and [Peter Maurer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Maurer).[^cite_note-7] He participated in the [2026 Winter Olympics opening ceremony](75430691.md).

## Awards

- During the [2024 Summer Olympics opening ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics_opening_ceremony), Grandi was presented with an [Olympic Laurel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_Laurel), the third person to be honored, for his work in helping refugee athletes.

- In July 2025, the Spanish government awarded him the Grand Cross of the [Order of Isabella the Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Isabella_the_Catholic).

  [^cite_note-9]

- In January 2026, the Ukraine government awarded him the Recipients of the [Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Merit_\(Ukraine\)), 2nd class.

  [^cite_note-10]
