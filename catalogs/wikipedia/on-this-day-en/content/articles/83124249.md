# The Civil Commission

**The Civil Commission** (formally: The Civil Commission on October 7th Crimes by Hamas Against Women, Children and Families) is an [Israeli](9282173.md) [non-profit organization](72487.md) founded in late 2023.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-\:1-3][^cite_note-\:6-4][^cite_note-\:7-5] It collects, researches and archives material relating to the [Hamas-led attacks of October 7, 2023](74998317.md), and the subsequent period of [hostage captivity](75025451.md), with a particular focus on [sexual](731637.md) and [gender-based violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender-related_violence).[^cite_note-\:7-5][^cite_note-\:2-6][^cite_note-\:3-7] The organization was founded by [Cochav Elkayam-Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochav_Elkayam-Levy), a [legal scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurist) and [human rights](13831.md) [researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research).[^cite_note-\:1-3][^cite_note-8]

## History

### Background

The Civil Commission was founded by Cochav Elkayam-Levy, Merav Israeli-Amarant, and Nirit Samocha in the weeks following the [October 7 attacks](74998317.md) on [Israel](9282173.md).[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-\:4-9][^cite_note-\:6-4][^cite_note-10] According to the organization, its establishment was a response to what its founders characterized as an absence of immediate engagement by international institutions on the subject of [sexual violence and other crimes against women and children committed during the attacks](75436869.md).[^cite_note-\:4-9][^cite_note-\:5-11] Elkayam-Levy is a lecturer in international relations at [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) and an expert specializing in international law, gender and human rights.[^cite_note-\:9-12][^cite_note-\:10-13][^cite_note-\:14-14] After serving in the IDF spokesperson unit, she studied law at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University).[^cite_note-\:14-14] While a doctoral student at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), she published a paper providing legal guidance for force feeding prisoners on [hunger strike](222292.md).[^cite_note-\:9-12][^cite_note-\:12-15][^cite_note-16] According to *[The Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times)*, Elkayam-Levy is "the most prominent public voice on the sexual violence of October 7."[^cite_note-\:9-12]

### Early work

In October 2023, Elkayam-Levy and other researchers submitted a letter to [UN Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Women)[^cite_note-\:3-7][^cite_note-17] and, on October 20, 2023, submitted a preliminary legal report to the [United Nations](31769.md).[^cite_note-18] That report was updated on November 1, 2023. In the weeks after the October 7 attacks, the Commission began creating an archive of videos, photographs, and other materials documenting allegations of violence against women and children on October 7. The commission also collected testimonies of survivors, [ZAKA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZAKA) emergency responders, and confessions of Palestinian militants interrogated by the Israeli military. Based on the evidence gathered, the Commission concluded that Hamas systematically committed widespread sexual violence on October 7. Elkayam-Levy told *[Haaretz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haaretz)*: "The torture of women was weaponized in the destruction of communities, in sowing general horror and in breaking the spirit of the Israelis."[^cite_note-\:14-14]

In the beginning, the commission consisted of 15 volunteers, including legal scholar [Yifat Biton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yifat_Bitton).[^cite_note-\:14-14] Legal scholar [Ruth Halperin-Kaddari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_Halperin-Kaddari) was initially involved in the Commission but later left.[^cite_note-\:9-12] In response to allegations of spreading misinformation, a spokesman for the Commission referred to the challenges of working in wartime conditions.[^cite_note-\:9-12][^cite_note-\:13-19]

In March 2024, Elkayam-Levy was awarded the [Israel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Prize) for her work with the Civil Commission.[^cite_note-\:9-12][^cite_note-\:10-13] The award announcement mentioned that she wrote a report about Hamas atrocities.[^cite_note-20] [Channel 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_13_\(Israel\)) journalist [Raviv Drucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raviv_Drucker) published an article alleging that Elkayam-Levy had not in fact published a report on the subject but only a brief letter.[^cite_note-\:11-21][^cite_note-\:10-13] The [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_\(Israel\)) stated that Elkayam-Levy received the award for her work to raise awareness and not for writing a report.[^cite_note-\:10-13] Elkayam-Levy stated that a comprehensive report documenting the crimes committed by Palestinian militants was in progress.[^cite_note-\:11-21][^cite_note-\:10-13]

## Activities

The Commission maintains a [digital archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archive) that, according to the organization, contains [testimonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Testimony), [photographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photograph), [videos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video), and other materials related to the [October 7 attacks](74998317.md) and subsequent [hostage captivity](75025451.md).[^cite_note-\:6-4][^cite_note-\:3-7][^cite_note-\:5-11] The organization states the archive is intended for use in [legal proceedings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_proceeding), [academic research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research), and [historical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History) [record-keeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Records_management).[^cite_note-\:2-6][^cite_note-\:3-7][^cite_note-\:8-22]

The Commission has submitted legal analyses and reports to [governmental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_agency) and international bodies.[^cite_note-\:1-3][^cite_note-23] Submissions have been directed to entities in [Israel](9282173.md), the [United States](3434750.md), and forums associated with the [United Nations](31769.md).[^cite_note-\:2-6][^cite_note-\:3-7][^cite_note-\:8-22]
