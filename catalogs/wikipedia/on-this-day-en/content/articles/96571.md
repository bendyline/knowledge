**Upolu** is an [island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island) in [Samoa](27238.md), formed by a massive [basaltic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basalt) [shield volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shield_volcano) which rises from the [seafloor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seafloor) of the [western Pacific Ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Pacific_Ocean). The island is 75 kilometres (47 miles) long and 1,125 square kilometres (434 square miles) in area, making it the second largest of the [Samoan Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samoan_Islands) by area. With approximately 145,000 inhabitants, it is by far the most populous of the Samoan Islands. Upolu is situated to the southeast of [Savai'i](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savaii), the "big island". [Apia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apia), the [capital](181337.md), is in the middle of the north coast, and [Faleolo International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faleolo_International_Airport) at the western end of the island. The island has not had any historically recorded eruptions, although there is evidence of three lava flows, dating back only to between a few hundred and a few thousand years ago.&#x20;

In the [Samoan](27238.md) branch of [Polynesian mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynesian_mythology), Upolu was the first woman on the island.&#x20;

[James Michener](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Michener) based his character Bloody Mary in *[Tales of the South Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tales_of_the_South_Pacific)* (later a major character in the [Rodgers and Hammerstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodgers_and_Hammerstein) musical, *[South Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Pacific_\(musical\))*) on the owner of Aggie Grey's Hotel on the south end of the island. She was still running the hotel in 1960. A branch was later opened in Apia, overlooking the harbor.&#x20;

## History

In 1841, the island was the site of the [Bombardment of Upolu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardment_of_Upolu), an incident during the [United States Exploring Expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Exploring_Expedition).&#x20;

In the late 19th century, the Scottish writer [Robert Louis Stevenson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Louis_Stevenson) owned a 400-acre (160-hectare) [estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estate_\(land\)) in the village of [Vailima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vailima,_Samoa) in Upolu. He died there in 1894 and is buried at the top of [Mount Vaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Vaea) overlooking his former estate. The Vailima estate was purchased in 1900 to serve as the official residence for the [German](11867.md) governor of [German Samoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Samoa). When the [British](31717.md)/[Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion) took over governance of the islands, they [confiscated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confiscation) the estate and put it to the same use. It later served as the residence for the [New Zealand](4913064.md) administrator and, after independence, for the Samoan head of state. During [World War II](32927.md), the US Navy built [Naval Base Upolu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Base_Upolu) on the island.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-auto-3]&#x20;

### 2009 Samoa tsunami

Main article: [2009 Samoa earthquake and tsunami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Samoa_earthquake_and_tsunami)

The island of Upolu was hit by a [tsunami](31161.md) at 06\:48\:11 local time on 29 September 2009 (17\:48\:11 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)).[^cite_note-4] Twenty villages on Upolu's south side were reportedly destroyed, including [Lepā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lep%C4%81,_Samoa), the home of Samoa's [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister),[^cite_note-5] [Tuila'epa Sa'ilele Malielegaoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuila%27epa_Sa%27ilele_Malielegaoi). In Lepā, only the [church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Place_of_worship) and the village's welcome sign remained standing after the disaster.[^cite_note-dt-6][^cite_note-7]&#x20;

### 2024 HMNZS *Manawanui* sinking

Main article: [HMNZS Manawanui (2019) § Sinking](58330089.md#Sinking)

On 5 October 2024 the [Royal New Zealand Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_New_Zealand_Navy) ship [HMNZS *Manawanui*](58330089.md) ran aground and sank off the coast of [Siumu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siumu) on the southern coast of Upolu. The *Manawanui* had been surveying a nearby reef and was caught up in a storm.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] The sinking of the *Manawanui* polluted the surrounding sea and disrupted the livelihoods of local communities in the Safata district, who were unable to fish due to restrictions around the wreckage site.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] The Samoan and New Zealand authorities were criticised by local villagers for a lack of compensation and engagement with local communities.[^cite_note-12] This led local villagers to seek financial assistance from the Chinese Embassy in [Apia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apia).[^cite_note-13]&#x20;

On 12 February 2025 the Samoan Government lifted a "precautionary zone" around the wreckage of HMNZS *Manawanui* following testing by the Scientific Research Organisation of Samoa (SROS). However, a two [km](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometer) prohibition zone around the sunken ship remained in force.[^cite_note-14]&#x20;

## Wildlife

The Samoan moss spider (*[Patu marplesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patu_marplesi)*), an extremely small species of [spider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider), lives on Upolu. According to the [Guinness Book of World Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_Book_of_World_Records), the spider is the size of a [period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_stop) (full stop) on a printed page.[^cite_note-15]&#x20;

## Depictions in popular culture

Upolu was the filming location for the 1953 [South Seas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Seas_\(genre\)) film *[Return to Paradise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return_to_Paradise_\(1953_film\))*, starring [Gary Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Cooper).&#x20;

The island was also the filming location for several seasons of several editions of the competition [reality television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reality_television) series, *[Survivor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_\(franchise\))*. This included:&#x20;

- Seven seasons of *[Australian Survivor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Survivor)*: the [3rd season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Survivor_\(season_3\)) in 2016 and the [4th season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Survivor_\(season_4\)) in 2017; *[Australian Survivor: Heroes vs Villains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Survivor_\(season_10\))* in 2023 (10th season); *[Australian Survivor: Titans vs Rebels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Survivor_\(season_11\))* in 2024 (11th season); *[Australian Survivor: Brains vs Brawn II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Survivor_\(season_12\))* in 2025 (12th season); *[Australian Survivor: Australia V The World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Survivor:_Australia_V_The_World)* in 2025 (13th season); *[Australian Survivor: Redemption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Survivor:_Redemption)* in 2026 (14th season).

- Four seasons of the *[American version](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_\(American_TV_series\))* of *Survivor*, *[Survivor: Samoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor:_Samoa)* (19th season); *[Survivor: Heroes vs. Villains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor:_Heroes_vs._Villains)* (20th season); *[Survivor: South Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor:_South_Pacific)* (23rd season); and *[Survivor: One World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor:_One_World)* (24th season).

- One season of *[Survivor South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_South_Africa)*: *[Survivor South Africa: Island of Secrets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_South_Africa:_Island_of_Secrets)* (7th season) in 2019.
