**Bushehr Province** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): استان بوشهر)[^cite_note-7] is one of the 31 [provinces](448160.md) of Iran. It is in the south of the country, with a long coastline on the [Persian Gulf](24761.md). Its capital is the city of [Bushehr](205669.md).[^cite_note-Bushehr_Province_Structure-8]

The province was made a part of [Region 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Iran) upon the division of the provinces into five regions, solely for coordination and development purposes, on 22 June 2014.[^cite_note-autogenerated1-3]

## History



Political map showing different local tribal regions and alliances in the Bushehr area, 1915

The [Portuguese Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Empire) invaded the city of Bushehr in 1506 and remained there until [Shah Abbas Safavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_I_of_Safavid) defeated them and liberated the Persian Gulf region.

### 2013 Bushehr earthquake

Main article: [2013 Bushehr earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Bushehr_earthquake)

A strong earthquake measuring 6.1 on the Richter magnitude scale struck the town of [Shonbeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shonbeh) and villages of [Shonbeh and Tasuj District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shonbeh_and_Tasuj_District) in [Dashti County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dashti_County) of Bushehr province on 9 April 2013, killing at least 37 people.[^cite_note-9]

## Demographics

### Language

A vast majority of the population speak [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) (mostly the [Fars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dialects_of_Fars) dialect) as a [first language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_language). There is a minority of [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) speakers.[^cite_note-languages-10][^cite_note-11]

During the [Safavid period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safavid), some people from the [Kurdish](17068.md) [Zanganeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zangana_\(tribe\)) tribe moved from [Kermanshah](1489244.md) to the villages between [Ahram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahram,_Iran) and [Borazjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borazjan) and formed a local government in this area, and from that time until the beginning of [Qajar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qajar), this part of Bushehr province was called Zanganeh bloc.[^cite_note-12]

### Religion

The vast majority of Bushehr's population are [Shia Muslims](26961.md), with a minority of [Sunnis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam) comprising 8.5% of the province's population.[^cite_note-14]

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the province's population was 886,490 in 188,762 households.[^cite_note-2006_Bushehr_Province-15] The following census in 2011 counted 1,032,949 people in 246,742 households.[^cite_note-2011_Bushehr_Province-16] The 2016 census measured the population of the province as 1,163,400 inhabitants in 321,826 households.[^cite_note-2016_Bushehr_Province-4]

## 21st century



The coast in Bushehr by the Persian Gulf.



Topological and bathmetery map of Bushehr province

The city of [Bushehr](205669.md) is the second main naval port of Iran after [Bandar Abbas](464348.md),:

### Kharg Island (Khark Island)

Main article: [Kharg Island](2210066.md)

As of March 2026, during the [2026 Iran War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_War), Kharg Island was of great strategic significance, carrying up to 90 per cent of Iran's oil exports through its terminal.[^cite_note-clark2026-19]

### Bushehr Nuclear Reactor

Main article: [Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant](18522794.md)

See also: [The Bushehr project of Iran's nuclear program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran's_nuclear_program#The_Bushehr_project)

The Bushehr Light water PWR [Nuclear Reactor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_Reactor), designed by [Siemens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siemens), built by the Russians, is Iran's first Nuclear Power Plant reactor.
