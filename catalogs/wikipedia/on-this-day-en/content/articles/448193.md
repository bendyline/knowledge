**Cabo Delgado** is the northernmost [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Mozambique) of [Mozambique](19301.md). It has an area of 82,625 km<sup>2</sup> (31,902 mi<sup>2</sup>) and a population of 2,320,261 (2017). It borders the [Mtwara Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mtwara_Region) in the neighbouring country of [Tanzania](30118.md), and the provinces of [Nampula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nampula_Province) and [Niassa](448242.md).

The Province is rich in natural resources; such as [natural gas](22131.md), [graphite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphite), [rare-earth elements](145440.md), and [vanadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanadium).

The region is an ethnic stronghold of the [Makonde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makonde_people) tribe, with the [Makua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makua_people) and [Mwani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mwani_people) as leading [ethnic minorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minority_group).[^cite_note-2]

## Geography

Cabo Delgado is the northernmost [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Mozambique) of [Mozambique](19301.md). It has an area of 82,625 km<sup>2</sup> (31,902 mi<sup>2</sup>) and a population of 2,320,261 (2017).[^cite_note-3] It borders the provinces of [Nampula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nampula_Province) in the South and [Niassa](448242.md) and borders the [Mtwara Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mtwara_Region) in the neighboring country of [Tanzania](30118.md) in the North. Cabo Delgado Province has a shoreline to the East.

The offshore Rovuma Basin was found to have large [natural gas](22131.md) reserves at the Afungi peninsula; as of 2020 three reservoirs were being developed to produce [liquefied natural gas](832128.md) and market gas by the Rovuma LNG project ([ExxonMobil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ExxonMobil), [Eni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eni) and [China National Petroleum Corp.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Petroleum_Corp.))[^cite_note-ns-4] and the Mozambique LNG (TotalEnergies)[^cite_note-mo-5]

[Pemba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pemba,_Mozambique) is the capital of the province; other important cities include [Montepuez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montepuez) and [Mocímboa da Praia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moc%C3%ADmboa_da_Praia).

## History



Provincial map

The province shares its name with [Cape Delgado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Delgado) ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Cabo Delgado*), a coastal headland on the border between Mozambique and Tanzania, which forms the northernmost point in Mozambique.

On 25 September 1964, [FRELIMO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRELIMO) [guerrillas](12720.md) arrived from [Tanzania](30118.md) and, with help from some individuals of the surrounding population, attacked a [Portuguese](23033.md) administrative post in the province. This raid marked the beginning of the [Mozambican War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozambican_War_of_Independence), part of the [Portuguese Colonial War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Colonial_War), the former of which was an armed struggle between the Portuguese colonial authorities in the then-Portuguese [Overseas Province of Mozambique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Mozambique) and the independence movement. This province was the focus of [Operation Gordian Knot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Gordian_Knot), where the Portuguese forces attempted to wipe out the guerrilla bases in the province.[^cite_note-6]

### Jihadist insurgency

Main article: [Insurgency in Cabo Delgado](56119804.md)

Beginning in October 2017, armed [Islamist extremists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamist_extremism) linked to the [Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant](9087364.md) (ISIL) launched a [jihadist](7826589.md) insurgency in the Cabo Delgado region.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The militants launched attacks and committed mass beheadings,[^cite_note-Bearak-9] and in August 2020 seized the port town of [Mocimboa da Praia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mocimboa_da_Praia).[^cite_note-ArmedAttack-10][^cite_note-Meldrum-11] The group sometimes refers to itself as *al-Shabaab*,[^cite_note-Bearak-9][^cite_note-Meldrum-11] although they do not have known links with the [Somali](27358.md) [al-Shabaab](8726463.md), a different [jihadist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jihadist) group.[^cite_note-Meldrum-11] The [International Crisis Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Crisis_Group) reported in March 2021 that while ISIL has contact with the jihadists in Mozambique and has given some level of financial assistance, ISIL likely does not exert command and control authority over the group.[^cite_note-12]

[Mozambique Defence Armed Forces](19309.md) have been battling the extremists. Many civilians have been displaced by the fighting.[^cite_note-13] In September 2020, ISIL insurgents captured [Vamizi Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vamizi_Island) in the Indian Ocean.[^cite_note-14] Over fifty people were beheaded by terrorists in the province in April 2020 and a similar number in November 2020.[^cite_note-15] In March 2021, the [NGO](46539.md) [Save the Children](436885.md) reported that Islamist militants were beheading children, some as young as 11.[^cite_note-16]

On March 24, 2021, [the militants seized Palma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Palma), murdering dozens of civilians and [displacing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Displaced_person) more than 35,000 of the town's 75,000 residents.[^cite_note-ArmedAttack-10][^cite_note-newyorktimes-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-Dozens-19] Many fled to the provincial capital, [Pemba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pemba,_Mozambique).[^cite_note-Bearak-9][^cite_note-Meldrum-11] In July 2021 the [Southern African Development Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_African_Development_Community) deployed its [military mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_African_Development_Community_Mission_in_Mozambique) to the province.

As of February 2022, there were still a few civilians being killed due to the lingering insurgency and several insurgent camps were found by the Mozambican authorities.[^cite_note-7_Feb_-_13_Feb_22-20][^cite_note-21]

### Religion

Mozambique is a majority-Christian country; however two northern provinces have an Islamic majority – Niassa (61 percent) and Cabo Delgado (54 percent). In the north of the province, Islam has few adherents in the Mueda Plateau, a region inhabited mostly by the [Makonde people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makonde_people); the coastal districts of the northeast, inhabited by the [Mwani people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mwani_people) are instead overwhelming Muslim. The Makhuwe are nominal Catholic or Muslim adherents, forming Muslim majorities in the central strip of the province. In Cabo Delgado, only three districts have a Catholic majority – Muidumbe (67 percent) and Mueda (54 percent) in the north and Namuno (61 percent) in the south. Two other districts have significant Catholic populations – Nangade (42 percent Catholic, 36 percent Muslim) in the north and Chiure (44 percent Muslim, 42 percent Catholic) in the South, whilst twelve have Muslim majorities, including Pemba; four are more than 90 percent Muslim. Coastal administrative posts are all over 75 percent Muslim.[^cite_note-25]

The province has been economically depressed.[^cite_note-gm-26]

### Natural gas

In 2010, large natural gas reserves were discovered in the offshore Rovuma Basin, 85 trillion cubic feet (Tcf) in the so called *Area 4*, and south of Palma, 5 Tcf in the so called *Area 1* alone, the equivalent of a 12 billion barrel oil field.[^cite_note-360mo-27] Area 1 has been developed by [TotalEnergies](804161.md).[^cite_note-360mo-27]

In June 2014, the Rovuma [liquefied natural gas](832128.md) (LNG) project [environmental impact assessment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_impact_assessment) was approved to exploit area 4 and the government approved the development in 2019. The 3 owners, oil companies [ExxonMobil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ExxonMobil) (40%), [Eni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eni) (40%) and [CNPC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNPC) (20%), delayed a final investment decision for years due to COVID 19 pandemic related market conditions, disruption to construction workers and decreased LNG demand.[^cite_note-ns-4]

In 2021, both Exxon and TotalEnergies invoked [force majeure](709128.md), because the project was attacked by Jihadist militants.[^cite_note-360mo-27]

On March 3, 2025, TotalEnergies suspended operations, because their financing remained unclear.[^cite_note-360mo-27] Less than 2 weeks later the Trump administration promised a 4.7 billion \$ loan to TotalEnergies through the [US Export-Import Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Export-Import_Bank) (EXIM).[^cite_note-lemonde-28]

### Mining

The [Balama mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balama_mine) owned by Australian Syrah Resources has been producing high grade [graphite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphite) since 2019. As of 2025, resettlemet grievances of farmers were ongoing and it is unclear if the mine has "contributed to the improvement of the economy and living conditions of the residents of Cabo Delgado province or Mozambique in general".[^cite_note-gm-26]
