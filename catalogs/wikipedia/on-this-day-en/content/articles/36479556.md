**National Police Service** (NPS) is the armed [national police force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_agency) in [South Sudan](32350676.md).

## Human rights

[Amnesty International](18947898.md) accused it of torture in its 2012 Annual Report.[^cite_note-3]
