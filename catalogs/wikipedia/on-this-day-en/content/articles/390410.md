# Salt flat

This article is about the geological feature. For the ghost town in Texas, see [Salt Flat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Flat). For the tidal feature, see [Salt pannes and pools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_pannes_and_pools).



The [Salar de Uyuni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salar_de_Uyuni), in [Bolivia](3462.md), the world's largest salt pan.

Natural **salt flats** or **salt pans** are flat, open expanses of ground covered with [salt](1605200.md) and other [minerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mineral), usually shining white under the [sun](26751.md). They are found in [deserts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert) and are natural formations (unlike [salt evaporation ponds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_evaporation_pond), which are artificial).

A salt flat forms by evaporation of a [water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water) pool, such as a [lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake) or [pond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pond). This happens in climates where the rate of water [evaporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evaporation) exceeds the rate of [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation_\(meteorology\)) — that is, in a desert. If the water cannot drain into the ground, it remains on the surface until it evaporates, leaving behind minerals precipitated from the salt ions [dissolved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solution_\(chemistry\)) in the water. Over thousands of [years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Year), the minerals (usually salts) accumulate on the surface.[^cite_note-1] These minerals reflect the sun's rays and often appear as white areas.

Salt flats can be dangerous. The crust of salt can conceal a [quagmire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quagmire) of [mud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mud) that can engulf a truck. The [Qattara Depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qattara_Depression) in the eastern [Sahara Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahara_Desert) contains many such traps which served as strategic barriers during [World War II](32927.md).[^cite_note-2]

## Examples



The [Bonneville Salt Flats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonneville_Salt_Flats), [Utah](31716.md)&#x20;

The [Bonneville Salt Flats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonneville_Salt_Flats) in [Utah](31716.md), where many [land speed records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_speed_record) have been set, are a well-known salt pan in the arid regions of the [western United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_United_States).

The [Etosha pan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etosha_pan), in the [Etosha National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etosha_National_Park) in [Namibia](21292.md), is another prominent example of a salt pan.

The [Salar de Uyuni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salar_de_Uyuni) in [Bolivia](3462.md) is the largest salt pan in the world. As of 2024, with an estimated 23 million [tons](31185.md), Bolivia holds about 22% of the world's known lithium resources (105 million tons); most of those are in the Salar de Uyuni.[^cite_note-3] The large area, clear skies, and exceptional flatness of the surface make the Salar an ideal object for calibrating the [altimeters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altimeter) of Earth observation satellites.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-ieee-5]

Parts of [Rann of Kutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rann_of_Kutch) (India) are [salt marsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_marsh) in the wet season and salt pan in the dry season.[^cite_note-CNN2018-6]

Salt flats can also be found in Kenya. A place called [Chalbi Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalbi_Desert) in North Horr Sub County, Marsabit County in Kenya. In the language of the [Gabra people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabra_people), "Chalbi" means "bare, salty area".[^cite_note-\:0-7]
