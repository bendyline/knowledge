The **Second Cabinet of Kyriakos Mitsotakis** was sworn in on 27 June 2023, following the [parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2023_Greek_parliamentary_election) held two days earlier.[^cite_note-1] [Kyriakos Mitsotakis](2780816.md), leader of [New Democracy](424225.md), was sworn in for a second term as [Prime Minister of Greece](273647.md) on 26 June.[^cite_note-2]

The government consists of a total of 61 members, including 22 ministers, 4 alternate ministers and 34 deputy ministers.[^cite_note-Ανακοίνωση-3] Of these, 19 are not elected members of the [Hellenic Parliament](2449826.md), but rather selected based on their political, scientific or technocratic experience.[^cite_note-4] Thirteen members of the government are women.

### Ministers

**Full ministers** (in bold in the table below) are responsible for:

- the identification of ministerial policy in the cabinet

- the representation in bodies of the [European Union](9317.md)

- the appointment of administrative agencies, public services and personnel

**Alternate Ministers** are directly assigned special responsibilities and powers by the prime minister, including:

- full parliamentary powers and, in conjunction with the minister, the [right of (legislative) initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_of_initiative_\(legislative\))

- the right to issue individual and normative acts, and to propose individual and normative decrees

**Deputy ministers** are assigned with responsibilities and powers by the prime minister and the full minister they report to. Deputy ministers may attend cabinet meetings.

## Controversies

Following an investigation into alleged fraud involving [European Union agricultural subsidies](141924.md), two ministers and two deputy ministers resigned from their cabinet positions:[^cite_note-April_2026_reshuffle-21] [rural development minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Rural_Development_and_Food_\(Greece\)) [Kostas Tsiaras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konstantinos_Tsiaras) and his deputy Christos Kellas, civil protection minister [Giannis Kefalogiannis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ioannis_A._Kefalogiannis), and [deputy health minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_\(Greece\)) Dimitris Vartzopoulos. They denied any wrongdoing, saying their resignations were intended to assist the investigation.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35]
