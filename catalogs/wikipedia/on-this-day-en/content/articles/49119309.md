The **Ruweng Administrative Area** is an [administrative area](30852056.md) in [South Sudan](32350676.md).[^cite_note-3] The area was known as **Ruweng State** between 2 October 2015 and 22 February 2020 when it was a state of South Sudan.[^cite_note-4]

## History

On 1 October 2015, President [Salva Kiir](2363203.md) issued a decree establishing 28 states instead of the 10 constitutionally established states.[^cite_note-5] The decree established the new states largely along ethnic lines. Several opposition parties and civil society groups challenged the decree's constitutionality. Kiir later resolved to take it to parliament for approval as a constitutional amendment.[^cite_note-6] In November the South Sudanese parliament empowered President Kiir to create new states.[^cite_note-7] [Mayol Kur Akuei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayol_Kur_Akuei) was appointed governor in 2015.[^cite_note-8]

## Geography

The Ruweng Administrative Area is located in the northern part of South Sudan and its headquarters is at [Pariang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pariang). It borders the former [Upper Nile State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Nile_\(state\)) in the east and [Jonglei State](767655.md) in the southeast, [Unity State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unity_\(state\)) in the south, [Warrap State](767678.md) in the southwest, [Abyei](13885196.md) to the northwest, and [Sudan](27421.md) in the north.

## Economy

It is the most oil-producing area in South Sudan. About 80% of South Sudanese oil is produced here, mainly in the [Unity / Darbim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unity_oilfield) oil field (in the southern part), [Heglig / Panthou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heglig_oil_field) oil field (in the north-western part), Tomasouth/Kaloj oilfield (in the western part), Toor / Athony oil field or, Labob / Miading and Munga / Wanhe Danluel oilfield, Maan Awal, and other fields.

Ruweng is rich in animal resources and fish resources, and it also is the home of two lakes: [Lake Jau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Jau?action=edit\&redlink=1) (in the northern part) and [Lake No](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_No) locally known as Dhoo (in the southern part), where [Bahr el Ghazal River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahr_el_Ghazal_River) ends and joins the [White Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Nile). Ruweng is rich in agricultural land and wildlife.

## Demographics

Ruweng is the home of [Ruweng Dinkas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruweng_people), who are Panaruu Dinka with 12 sub-tribes, and Aloor or Ruweng Biemnom Dinka with six sub-tribes.

## Administrative divisions

The area consists of eight counties: [Jau County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jau_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Aliny County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliny_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Wunkur County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wunkur_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Lake No county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_No_county?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Jamjang County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamjang_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Tuoch County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuoch_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Abiemnom East County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abiemnom_East_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Abiemnom West County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abiemnom_West_County?action=edit\&redlink=1).
