The **minister of foreign affairs** is the head of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Italy\)) in [Italy](14532.md). The office was one of the positions which Italy inherited from the [Kingdom of Sardinia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Sardinia) where it was the most ancient ministry of the government: this origin gives to the office a ceremonial primacy in the Italian cabinet.[^cite_note-1]

The current minister is [Antonio Tajani](1767298.md), a member of [Forza Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forza_Italia_\(2013\)), who is serving in the government of [Giorgia Meloni](29228976.md) since 22 October 2022.[^cite_note-2]
