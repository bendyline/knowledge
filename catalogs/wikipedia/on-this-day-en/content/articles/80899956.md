This page documents notable [tornadoes](37530.md) and [tornado outbreaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak) worldwide in 2026. Strong and destructive tornadoes most frequently form in the [United States](3434750.md), [China](5405.md), the [La Plata Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Plata_Basin), the [European Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Plain), [South Africa](17416221.md), and [Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal), but they can occur almost anywhere under the right conditions. Tornadoes also develop occasionally in southern [Canada](5042916.md) during the [Northern Hemisphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Hemisphere)'s summer and somewhat regularly at other times of the year across [Europe](9239.md), [Asia](689.md), and [Australia](4689264.md). Tornadic events are often accompanied by other forms of [severe weather](11593538.md), including strong [thunderstorms](70807.md), winds, and [hail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hail). Worldwide, 33 tornado-related deaths have been confirmed – 15 in the United States, 13 in [China](5405.md), two in [India](14533.md), and one each in [Argentina](18951905.md), [Israel](9282173.md) and [Italy](14532.md).

## January

### January 7 (Greece)

In the evening hours of January 7, a strong tornado tracked through [Kalpaki, Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalpaki), significantly damaging numerous structures. A large poultry farm was destroyed, killing 30,000–40,000 chickens inside. A church and a military camp were also damaged.[^cite_note-ESWD-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The tornado was rated IF2 on the [International Fujita Scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Fujita_scale).[^cite_note-ESWD-5]

### January 8–10 (United States)

A small tornado event occurred in [Oklahoma](22489.md) early on January 8, with five tornadoes being confirmed, one of which was rated a low-end EF2 after it tore the roof off of a house southwest of [Purcell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purcell,_Oklahoma).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] This tornado caused one injury along its path when it rolled a semi-truck as it crossed [I-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_35_in_Oklahoma), and also caused considerable damage in the town itself.[^cite_note-OklahomaTornadoDatabase-11][^cite_note-12] The storms also brought an 88 mph (142 km/h) wind gust to [Wynona, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wynona,_Oklahoma) and an 81 mph (130 km/h) wind gust to [Independence, Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence,_Kansas). There were five tornadoes each on January 9 and 10.[^cite_note-13]

### January 8 (Turkey)

On the early evening of January 8, an IF2-rated tornado tracked through the villages of [Kızılağaç](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C4%B1z%C4%B1la%C4%9Fa%C3%A7,_Manavgat), [Karacalar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karacalar,_Manavgat), and [Sülek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%BClek,_Manavgat) in the [Mediterranean region](8318424.md) of [Turkey](11125639.md). The roofs of six buildings were ripped away and numerous greenhouses were destroyed. The walls of various barns collapsed and trees along the tornado's path were uprooted and splintered. Several vehicles were damaged by the tornado, including a [Fiat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat) that was thrown into a nearby tractor, which struck a 20-year-old woman who sustained non-life-threatening injuries from the impact.[^cite_note-ESWD-5][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

### January 9 (Israel)

A waterspout off the coast of [Bat Yam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bat_Yam) caught three [kitesurfers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitesurfing) and expelled them at high speeds towards the shore. One kitesurfer died after sustaining severe injuries, and another was lightly injured. The waterspout went on to briefly make landfall on the beach but caused no further damage.[^cite_note-ESWD-5]

### January 10 (Brazil)

On the afternoon of January 10, an F2 tornado struck the city of [São José dos Pinhais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Jos%C3%A9_dos_Pinhais) in the Metropolitan Region of [Curitiba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curitiba), [Paraná](229411.md), [Brazil](3383.md), with peak winds estimated at 180 km/h (110 mph).[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-G1PinhaisClassif-17] An estimated 1,200 people were affected, including two people sustaining minor injuries and two families displaced. At least 350 homes were impacted, several trees were overturned, and a warehouse was wrecked. In addition, multiple utility poles were toppled by the winds, causing some damage to the electrical grid.[^cite_note-G1PinhaisClassif-17][^cite_note-18]

### January 26–27 (Turkey)

A small tornado outbreak occurred across two days in the [Mediterranean Region](8318424.md) of [Turkey](11125639.md). Two of these tornadoes, one IF2 in [Aksu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aksu,_Antalya) [District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Turkey) and the other IF1 [Manavgat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manavgat) District each caused one injury.[^cite_note-ESWD-5][^cite_note-19] Infrastructural and tree damage was also reported and some boats were damaged or destroyed. A few other tornadoes were confirmed, mostly damaging roofs, greenhouses, and farms, although one, rated IF1.5, collapsed a Mosque tower in [Denizyaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denizyaka,_Manavgat).[^cite_note-ESWD-5]

### January 30 (Spain)

A strong IF2 tornado struck the city of [Plasencia, Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasencia,_Spain) in [Province of Cáceres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_C%C3%A1ceres). In the vicinity of the Virgen del Puerto Hospital, the roof of the parking structure was torn off, and more than 50 cars were damaged.[^cite_note-ESWD-5][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] Damage was also reported on Dolores Ibárruri Avenue and in the Ciudad Jardín neighborhood. No injuries were reported.[^cite_note-ESWD-5]

### January 31 (France)

A strong IF2 tornado affected [Mios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mios) in [Gironde](64654.md) [Department](38523.md), damaging around 200–300 homes. The tornado ripped roofs off houses, collapsed walls, and uprooted power poles. It also downed many trees along its 20-kilometre (12-mile) path.[^cite_note-ESWD-5] No injuries were reported, but more than 100 firefighters were called in to assist in clearing debris, including on several lanes of the [A63 autoroute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A63_autoroute) that were closed after trees blocked the road.[^cite_note-22]

## February

### February 19 (United States)

| EFU | EF0 | EF1 | EF2 | EF3 | EF4 | EF5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 4 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |



EF2 damage to a home in [Bloomington, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomington,_Indiana)

On the morning of February 19, the [Storm Prediction Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Prediction_Center) issued an enhanced risk for severe weather due to the overlap of unseasonably rich moisture with a pronounced low-pressure system over southern [Illinois](14849.md) and southern [Indiana](21883857.md), resulting in a chance for strong EF2+ tornadoes.[^cite_note-23] During the evening hours, several tornadoes touched down across the [Ohio River Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_River). A high-end EF1 tornado in [Crawford County, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crawford_County,_Illinois) damaged 12–15 homes and injured multiple people.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] One [mobile home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_home) was rolled over by the tornado, trapping a woman inside.[^cite_note-26] An EF2 tornado in [Bloomington, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomington,_Indiana) prompted a "[particularly dangerous situation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particularly_dangerous_situation)" tornado warning for the city, with widespread damage being reported.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28][^cite_note-DAT-29] Both damaging tornadoes were produced by the same cyclic supercell, which tracked more than 150 miles through the risk area. The storm system also led to two fatalities in [Nebraska](21647.md) due to a car crash from the wintry conditions it brought.[^cite_note-30]

## March

### March 5–7 (United States)

Main articles: [Tornado outbreak of March 5–7, 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak_of_March_5%E2%80%937,_2026) and [2026 Union City tornado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Union_City_tornado)

| EFU | EF0 | EF1 | EF2 | EF3 | EF4 | EF5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 11 | 9 | 7 | 2 | 0 | 0 |



A deadly EF3 tornado approaching Union City, Michigan

A small but significant and deadly [tornado outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak) occurred between March 5–7, fueled by a large storm system. During the evening hours of March 5, a supercell tracked for several hours through northwestern Oklahoma into Kansas, producing multiple tornadoes. A mother and daughter were killed in [Major County, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_County,_Oklahoma) when the first significant tornado, rated EF2, touched down west of [Fairview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairview,_Oklahoma). The intense winds struck their vehicle as they were driving near [US 60](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_60_in_Oklahoma), throwing their car into a nearby field.[^cite_note-31] Just a few minutes after the first tornado lifted, an EF1 tornado touched down and moved northwest of [Cleo Springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleo_Springs,_Oklahoma). It was followed by another long-tracked EF2 tornado that prompted multiple [PDS tornado warnings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particularly_dangerous_situation), and caused damage near [Jet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet,_Oklahoma) and [Helena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helena,_Oklahoma). An EF1 tornado also impacted areas near [Wakita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakita,_Oklahoma) later that night. Elsewhere, trees were downed, and homes and power lines were damaged.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-OklahomaTornadoDatabase-11] Two EF1 tornadoes were reported in southern Kansas east of [Bluff City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluff_City,_Kansas).[^cite_note-DAT-29] The next day, an isolated supercell produced four tornadoes in Michigan,[^cite_note-33] with an EF1 tornado tracking over 13 miles in [Cass County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_County,_Michigan), causing one fatality after the front of a home was destroyed.



An EF3 tornado visible through a power flash near [Beggs, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beggs,_Oklahoma)

A strong EF2 tornado hit [Three Rivers, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Rivers,_Michigan), prompting a [PDS tornado warning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particularly_dangerous_situation). The tornado injured 10 people and caused significant damage to numerous businesses, including at a [Menards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menards).[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35] The cyclic supercell continued moving to the east-northeast and produced a destructive high-end EF3 tornado near [Union City, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_City,_Michigan), killing three people and injuring 12 others.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37] The worst damage occurred near Union Lake, where a couple of homes were swept off their foundations. Michigan governor [Gretchen Whitmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gretchen_Whitmer) issued a state of emergency in [Branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branch_County,_Michigan), St. Joseph, and Cass counties to coordinate an all-hands-on-deck response to the severe weather.[^cite_note-38] Back in [Oklahoma](22489.md), several tornadoes were reported in the east central and northeastern parts of the state.[^cite_note-39] An EF2 tornado tore through communities north of [Tulsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa,_Oklahoma) and two people were killed after a low-end EF3 tornado struck the city of [Beggs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beggs,_Oklahoma), which damaged multiple homes and businesses as well.[^cite_note-40] Tornadic activity continued overnight, spreading into Texas and Arkansas.[^cite_note-41] A low-end EF2 tornado struck near [Prospect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prospect,_Marion_County,_Texas) in [Marion County, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_County,_Texas), injuring two people.[^cite_note-42] A mid-range EF2 tornado touched down near [Willisville, Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willisville,_Arkansas) which damaged trees, lofted and destroyed a trailer and left one person injured.[^cite_note-43]

Overall, 32 tornadoes were confirmed during the outbreak, along with eight fatalities and 29 injuries.[^cite_note-DAT-29] According to federal weather officials, neither a tornado watch nor a severe thunderstorm watch was put into effect in Michigan during the outbreak due to the storm being primarily concentrated in a small three-county area, which made it difficult to detect in advance; the highest risk level on that day in the area was also only a Level 1 Marginal risk.[^cite_note-44] Governor Whitmer's office called for a probe into the absence of a tornado watch alert and questioned if it could be attributed to President [Donald Trump](4848272.md)'s [funding cutbacks to the National Weather Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NOAA_in_the_second_Trump_administration).[^cite_note-45]

An EF3 tornado near [Kankakee, Illinois](111393.md), as seen from [Kankakee Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankakee_Community_College) on March 10

Another significant tornado outbreak occurred across much of the central United States, highlighted by a moderate risk for severe weather for parts of northern [Illinois](14849.md) and northwest [Indiana](21883857.md).[^cite_note-46] Tornado watches were issued from Texas to Indiana during the late afternoon and early evening due to the risk of severe weather.[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48] A large and long-tracked EF3[^cite_note-k845-49] tornado passed through the southern portions of [Kankakee, Illinois](111393.md) and the suburb of [Aroma Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aroma_Park,_Illinois), damaging over 500 buildings and killing one person, before crossing into [Indiana](21883857.md) and striking [Lake Village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Village,_Indiana), where it caused two fatalities and damaged an additional 106 buildings.[^cite_note-DAT-29][^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51] The [supercell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercell) also produced potentially record-breaking hail, with reports of 5–6-inch (13–15 cm) hailstones in the Kankakee area, which, if confirmed, would break the former Illinois state record of 4.75-inch (12.1 cm) diameter hail.[^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53]

The supercell continued producing tornadoes, including a large EF1 tornado near [Wheatfield, Indiana](112365.md) that caused three injuries,[^cite_note-k845-49] and an EF2 tornado which had a [tornado emergency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_emergency) issued for it in [Knox, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knox,_Indiana).[^cite_note-KnoxTORE-54][^cite_note-55][^cite_note-DAT-29] Tornadic activity continued overnight further south as a strongly forced [QLCS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squall_line) pushed its way off to the east.[^cite_note-56] In addition to the tornadoes, a group of hailstorms impacted parts of the [Kansas City metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City_metropolitan_area), producing baseball-sized hailstones in [Parkville, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkville,_Missouri).[^cite_note-57] Overall, 106 tornadoes were confirmed during the outbreak, along with three fatalities and 16 injuries.[^cite_note-DAT-29]

### March 14 (Italy)

A brief tornado struck an airfield in [Serdiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serdiana) late in the evening on March 14. The tornado damaged and overturned fixed and mobile units, including a camper with a skydiver inside, who suffered a minor injury to his hand.[^cite_note-58]

### March 15 (India)

On the afternoon of March 15, an EF2[^cite_note-59] tornado struck parts of the [Mayurbhanj district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayurbhanj_district) in [Odisha](250724.md), eastern India, causing significant damage. More than 100 homes were damaged, many of them traditional mud-and-thatch structures. Roofs were flattened, and debris was scattered across the area in Kia village, [Karanjia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karanjia). Strong winds knocked down electric poles, uprooted large trees, cut power, blocked roads in several locations, and swept vehicles off the road. Two people were killed, and at least 29 others were injured, including six critically.[^cite_note-60][^cite_note-61][^cite_note-62]

## April

### April 7 (Turkey)

A tornado struck [Hilvan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilvan) in [Şanlıurfa Province](1353138.md), causing heavy damage, including ripping the roof off of multiple houses and downing trees in the area. The tornado was rated IF1.5 and injured two people.[^cite_note-ESWD-5][^cite_note-63]

### April 13–16 (United States)

| EFU | EF0 | EF1 | EF2 | EF3 | EF4 | EF5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 13 | 28 | 4 | 1 | 0 | 0 |



A rainwrapped EF2 tornado near [Ottawa, Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa,_Kansas), on April 13&#x20;

A tornado outbreak occurred across much of the Midwestern and Central United States, with an enhanced risk for severe weather issued for April 13 and April 14.[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65] A slight risk for severe weather was also issued for April 15.[^cite_note-66] On April 13, a strong EF2 tornado impacted southern portions of [Ottawa, Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa,_Kansas), inflicting significant damage and injuring three people after a house just east of the city was impacted.[^cite_note-67][^cite_note-DAT-29] Another EF2 tornado from the same supercell tracked through portions of [Miami County, Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_County,_Kansas), causing significant damage near [Hillsdale Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillsdale_Lake) and in the town of [Hillsdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillsdale,_Kansas). The tornado injured one person and impacted around 100 structures, with 50 being significantly damaged.[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69][^cite_note-DAT-29] An EF1 tornado tracked just south of [Mound City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mound_City,_Kansas) and [Pleasanton, Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleasanton,_Kansas), with two people being injured after a mobile home was destroyed.[^cite_note-DAT-29] Following these tornadoes in Kansas, Representative [Sharice Davids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharice_Davids) sent a letter to the National Weather Service over concerns of missing data due to skipped weather balloon launches. Davids stated that the storms in Kansas revealed gaps in the National Weather Services's forecasting and warning operations and that tornado watches went out later than usual.[^cite_note-70]



The home north of Union Center, Wisconsin with all of its walls collapsed at low-end EF3 intensity

On April 14, tornado activity resumed as another round of tornadoes took place across Iowa and Wisconsin. In the afternoon, several tornadoes touched down to the south of Chicago, including a long-tracked EF1 tornado to the south of [Tinley Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinley_Park,_Illinois). From [Monee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monee,_Illinois) to [Crown Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Point,_Indiana), a low-end EF2 tornado traveled over 26 miles (42 km) and inflicted damage to trees and structures, with one home in a [St. John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John,_Indiana) neighborhood sustaining EF2 damage. Another long-tracked EF1 tornado traveled near [Champaign, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champaign,_Illinois), and several tornadoes touched down in Iowa. Overall, 32 tornadoes were confirmed during the outbreak.[^cite_note-DAT-29]

### August 17 (Austria)

An isolated IF2 tornado moved through the village of [Stieglhofen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stieglhofen?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Austria](26964606.md), damaging many homes and trees, the tornado caused narrow swaths of damage through the village.[^cite_note-242]

### August 19-20 (Europe)

A significant tornado outbreak affected multiple countries in Northern and Central Europe, including an IF1 that moved through towns and villages to the southeast of [Berlin](3354.md), doing damage to roofs and trees, an IF1.5 did considerable damage to the town of [Höhr-Grenzhausen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%B6hr-Grenzhausen) and an IF2 that impacted [Braunfels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braunfels) and [Solms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solms). An unrated tornado caused significant damage to a home in eastern Belgium and a well documented IF1 tornado moved through the coastal town of [Dragør](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag%C3%B8r), to the south of Copenhagen, Denmark, in town, the tornado damaged many homes and vehicles, ripped a flag from a flag pole, items inside homes were sucked out through open windows, and an umbrella attached to a 50 kilogram block was displaced into a house. A wide IF3 tornado moved through dense forest near [Ohrdruf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohrdruf), denuding areas of forest and completely debranching trees. Two other IF1s also occurred in Germany. An IF1.5+ tornado moved through forested areas near [Dipperz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dipperz) and a cyclic supercell produced an IF1 and two unrated tornadoes in [Kaliningrad Oblast](17311.md). Other tornadoes occurred in [Lithuania](17675.md) and [Poland](22936.md). Overall, 31 tornadoes occurred during this outbreak.[^cite_note-243]

### August 20 (United States)

A classroom at Eastern York High School that had its roof torn away

A small tornado outbreak took place on August 20, with an EF2 tornado tracking near [Wrightsville, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrightsville,_Pennsylvania), severely damaging the Eastern York High School where dozens of student athletes were sheltering and impacting several homes, injuring one person after a window was blown inward.[^cite_note-DAT-29][^cite_note-244] An EF1 tornado directly struck [Columbia, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia,_Pennsylvania), damaging an old firehouse and several other buildings.[^cite_note-245] A waterspout formed off the [Long Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Island) coast and moved ashore onto [Atlantic Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Beach,_New_York) as an EF1 tornado, shattering the windows of around 30 vehicles and damaging or destroying around 30 beach [bungalows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bungalow) at the Sun and Surf Beach Club, with one worker sustaining a minor injury.[^cite_note-246] This was the first tornado to touch down in [Nassau County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nassau_County,_New_York) in five years.[^cite_note-247]

In the evening hours, a string of tornadoes was detected by the [National Weather Service](286989.md) across the [Delaware Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_metropolitan_area). Among them was an EF1 tornado that tracked through [Dover, Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dover,_Delaware), inflicting significant damage to numerous trees, two motels, and a warehouse, prompting governor [Matt Meyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Meyer) to issue a [state of emergency](298608.md) for the city.[^cite_note-248][^cite_note-249] It was later confirmed that three other tornadoes made landfall nearby; an EF1 tornado in [Barclay, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barclay,_Maryland), an EF0 tornado in [East Caln Township, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Caln_Township,_Pennsylvania), and an EFU tornado in [Estell Manor, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estell_Manor,_New_Jersey).[^cite_note-250][^cite_note-251]

### August 23 (Italy)

A weak tornado hit a beach in [Ligo degli Estensi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ligo_degli_Estensi?action=edit\&redlink=1), Italy, where it picked up many light objects including Umbrellas and Lawn chairs. Two people were injured when they were struck by debris. [ESSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESSL) rated the tornado IF0.5.[^cite_note-253]

### August 24 (France)



A security camera still of the tornado near Pomas

An intense IF3[^cite_note-254] tornado struck near [Carcassonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carcassonne), knocking down trees, damaging cars, affecting nearly 100 homes and causing power outages to 2,800 residences. Multiple roofs were torn off in [Pomas](15434981.md), with search and rescue operations ongoing. Two people were reported missing, a baby was reported to be in unknown condition and 41 others sustained injuries.[^cite_note-255][^cite_note-256] French severe storm and tornado observatory [KERAUNOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keraunos) rated the tornado EF3.[^cite_note-257][^cite_note-258][^cite_note-259] The storms subsequently restructured into a [derecho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derecho) that affected Southern France.[^cite_note-260]

### August 29 (The Netherlands)

An IF2 tornado moved to the northwest of [Tholen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tholen) in [Zeeland](52229.md). The tornado did major debranching to trees and also damaged three sheds.[^cite_note-261]

### August 30 – September 1 (Philippines)

| FU | F0 | F1 | F2 | F3 | F4 | F5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |



A torn roof from a classroom in Cabugao, Ilocos Sur

A small tornado outbreak occurred across [Ilocos Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocos_Sur) between August 30–September 1 amid the enhanced [southwest monsoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_monsoon). On August 30, a tornado struck two villages in [Burgos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burgos,_Ilocos_Sur) where it damaged at least ten homes and uprooted several trees.[^cite_note-262] On the morning of August 31, a waterspout made landfall in [Narvacan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narvacan), damaging beach cottages and a street light, and causing up to ₱500,000 in damages. One person was reported injured.[^cite_note-263] A waterspout was also observed in [Magsingal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magsingal).[^cite_note-264] In [Bantay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bantay), another tornado unroofed homes and toppled trees.[^cite_note-\:72-265] In the afternoon hours, a strong tornado swept through [Cabugao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabugao). It was seen travelling through the residential area, ripping away roofs of several homes and a school building. Trees and electrical poles were toppled, causing a widespread power outage. No injuries were reported.[^cite_note-266][^cite_note-\:92-267][^cite_note-268] Another tornado was struck [Sinait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinait), damaging a few roofs.[^cite_note-269]

On September 1, a tornado struck three villages in [Santa Catalina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Catalina,_Ilocos_Sur) where it flipped a utility vehicle, and caused partial roof loss on several homes. Another tornado struck [San Vicente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Vicente,_Ilocos_Sur) where it destroyed at least eight homes, and damaged several others.[^cite_note-\:92-267][^cite_note-\:72-265] A tornado also formed in [Santo Domingo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santo_Domingo,_Ilocos_Sur) where it snapped tree branches and tore parts of roofs.[^cite_note-\:82-270] A few hours later, a tornado touched down on a farm in [Caoayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caoayan) with no major damages reported.[^cite_note-271]

Overall, the outbreak affected a total of 5,026 families across 168 villages in two cities and 16 municipalities. Initial assessments from multiple government agencies confirmed at least 60 homes were damaged.[^cite_note-272] Classes and work are suspended following the effects of the enhanced southwest monsoon.[^cite_note-273] The Rapid Damage Assessment and Needs Analysis (RDANA) were deployed to examine the full extent of the damage.[^cite_note-\:82-270]

## September

### September 2–3 (United States and Canada)

| EFU | EF0 | EF1 | EF2 | EF3 | EF4 | EF5 |

| 0 | 3 | 9 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 |



A home that was completely collapsed by an EF2 tornado in Detroit, Michigan

Severe thunderstorms in the [Great Lakes region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Lakes_region) of the United States and Canada caused a small tornado outbreak from September 2–3. On September 2, an enhanced risk for severe thunderstorms was forecast, with all severe hazards possible, including the risk of an isolated significant tornado.[^cite_note-274] That evening, severe thunderstorms developed in [Ontario](22218.md), with two tornadoes being confirmed by the [Northern Tornadoes Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Tornadoes_Project) (NTP) in the province, one rated EF0 in [Tavistock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Zorra-Tavistock), and one rated EF1 in [Shakespeare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakespeare,_Ontario).[^cite_note-a193-275] In addition, a [downburst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downburst) was also confirmed in Ontario by the NTP.[^cite_note-a193-275] The storms continued into [Western New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_New_York), with several tornadoes being confirmed in the region, including an EF2 in [Seneca County, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seneca_County,_New_York).[^cite_note-DAT-29][^cite_note-276] Two tornadoes struck [Letchworth State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letchworth_State_Park), causing the closure of part of the park.[^cite_note-Letchworth-277]

The following day, an enhanced risk was again outlined, with the primary risk being damaging winds across parts of [Northeast Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_Ohio) and [Western Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Pennsylvania), as while initial supercells were possible, upscale growth into a squall line was expected to occur quickly.[^cite_note-278] Despite the lower tornado threat, an EF2 tornado struck the eastern side of [Detroit](8687.md), inflicting significant damage to homes and industrial buildings. The tornado first touched down to the northeast of [Comerica Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comerica_Park), damaging the roof of a facility and knocking over an outbuilding before strengthening and growing in width. Several industrial buildings suffered extensive damage, with walls collapsing and parts of the roof being torn away. Along its path, two vacant buildings were collapsed, numerous trees were damaged, and one person was injured. A local state of emergency was declared, and over 250,000 customers were left without electricity.[^cite_note-279][^cite_note-280][^cite_note-281] It was the first tornado to impact the city of Detroit since [1997](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak_of_July_1%E2%80%933,_1997).[^cite_note-282] The tornado continued across the [Detroit River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_River) into [Windsor, Ontario](33596.md), with EF1 damage being confirmed there by the NTP.[^cite_note-g039-283] In addition, another EF1 tornado was confirmed by the NTP in [Wheatley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheatley,_Ontario), along with a downburst.[^cite_note-g039-283] Overall, 14 tornadoes occurred during this outbreak.[^cite_note-DAT-29][^cite_note-a193-275][^cite_note-g039-283]

### September 4 (Poland)

An IF2 tornado caused significant damage as it moved through the vicinity of [Gorzuchowo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorzuchowo,_Greater_Poland_Voivodeship) and [Kłecko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C5%82ecko), [Poland](22936.md). Several homes sustained roof damage or partial roof removal and numerous trees were snapped and/or uprooted. A section of a church facade was blown off and injured a child.[^cite_note-284] In Gorzuchowo, a mobile home was heavily damaged and a roughly 1,500 kg (3,300 lb) hot tub was displaced about 20 m (22 yd), while tree branches were thrown as far as 150–180 m (160–200 yd). Additional damage included broken windows from projectiles due to the tornado and numerous downed rooftop antennas.[^cite_note-ESWD-5]

An hour later, an IF0.5 tornado in [Gruzdžiai, Šiauliai County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gruzd%C5%BEiai) partially damaged farming equipment, and garden related equipment near a house.[^cite_note-ESWD-5] More storms occurred in the area, and likely more tornadoes occurred. An IF1 tornado tracked through [Sinalunga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinalunga), [Italy](14532.md), killing a woman after she bled out after the tornado shattered a window in her house.[^cite_note-285] A church and a sports center were damaged later on.[^cite_note-286]

A strong tornado moved through areas near [San Pedro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro,_Misiones) and [Cruce Caballero, Misiones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruce_Caballero,_Misiones?action=edit\&redlink=1), damaging or destroying multiple homes and killing one man after his house was lifted from its foundation and thrown into a nearby [plantation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plantation). ### September 18 (Utah)

&#x20;A significant and rare EF2 tornado with a width of 1.1 miles was seen near [Fruit Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit_Heights,_Utah) and Middle Smith Lake, [Utah](31716.md). This became the widest tornado in Utah state history.[^cite_note-288] Several videos captured the tornado traversing through highly elevated terrain of the [Wasatch Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasatch_Range), with its touchdown point being at 7,500 feet above sea-levels.[^cite_note-289] The tornado caused intense and widespread tree damage, with mature trees being toppled and snapped.[^cite_note-290][^cite_note-DAT-29][^cite_note-291] This is the first officially documented tornado in Morgan County since reliable records began in 1950.[^cite_note-292] A [landspout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landspout) was also reported near [Naples, Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples,_Utah).[^cite_note-293]

### September 28 (Vietnam)

A significant tornado occurred in [Nguyễn Phích](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Ph%C3%ADch) district, [Cà Mau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A0_Mau_province), damaging or destroying 12 homes, one of which completely collapsed. No injuries were reported, although damages totaled around VND\$ 1 billion (\$38,502).[^cite_note-294]
