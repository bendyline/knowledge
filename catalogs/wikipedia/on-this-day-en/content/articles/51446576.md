**Aleson Shipping Lines, Inc.** is a [shipping company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shipping_company) based in [Zamboanga City](25167165.md), Philippines.[^cite_note-\:0-1] Their services include routes to [Sandakan](1732138.md), Malaysia and over [Dapitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dapitan) to [Dumaguete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumaguete).[^cite_note-\:1-2][^cite_note-\:2-3] They also ship cargo from Zamboanga City to Manila with 12 of their container carriers.



Aleson MV Antonia (Flagship)

## History

The company was founded on October 1, 1976,[^cite_note-\:1-2] and its first vessel MV Estrella del Mar[^cite_note-4] transported passengers and cargo, mainly rice, to the neighboring islands. In 1980, the company acquired its first cargo vessel, thus the debut of MV Aleson.

The company expanded in the 1980s with more cargo ships, along with voyages to Singapore for trade and commerce purposes.

During the 1990s the company concentrated on the passenger and [roll-on/roll-off](1457484.md) [ferry](50771.md) sector. Additional vessels were added to carry more passengers and travel on more routes. In 1994, a new route between Zamboanga Port and [Sandakan](1732138.md) Port, Malaysia, was opened[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] in response to the development of [BIMP-EAGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BIMP-EAGA) routes.[^cite_note-7] Aleson's [MV *Danica Joy*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_Danica_Joy) was one of the first vessels that sailed on this route.

In 1999, the company expanded into containerizing cargo, with the addition of new container carriers. The first fast craft of the corporation was bought on 2004 and was named MV Seajet.

As of 2026, Aleson operates a fleet of 37 vessels and is the largest shipping operator in [Western Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Mindanao).[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:3-8][^cite_note-\:4-9][^cite_note-\:5-10][^cite_note-11]

## Ports of call

Aleson Shipping Lines Inc. main port of call is Zamboanga City, but some of their ships are actively in service for [Manila](184334.md), [Dapitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dapitan), [Dumaguete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumaguete), [Bongao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bongao), [Isabela, Basilan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabela,_Basilan) and [Lamitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamitan).[^cite_note-\:2-3][^cite_note-\:3-8]

## Notable incidents

- On July 14, 2002, MV *Aleson Container Carrier 5* collided with Candano Shipping's MV *Romeo* off Apo Cement Corporation's wharf in [Naga, Cebu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naga,_Cebu). MV Aleson's front hull hit the side of MV Romeo which sunk immediately along with 31,250 bags of cement worth ₱3,427,500. One person, the skipper of MV Romeo, was found dead in the water by rescuers.

  [^cite_note-\:5-10]

  [^cite_note-15]

- On March 8, 2003, MV *Ciara Joie,* a [ro-ro vessel](1457484.md) en route to [Iloilo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iloilo) with 87 passengers and 19 crew members, capsized off [Bacolod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacolod). The ship tilted when a forklift slipped off the gangplank and dropped a container van it was loading. It sank 40 minutes later and spilled 2,000 liters of diesel fuel. There were no casualties, however it was reported that a leg of a ship apprentice was broken during the accident.

  [^cite_note-16]

- On February 18, 2016, MV *Lady Mary Joy 1* with 308 passengers and 38 crew members grounded off Pampat Point at [Bongao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bongao,_Tawi-Tawi) island, Philippines. The ship was en route from [Zamboanga City](25167165.md) to [Bongao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bongao,_Tawi-Tawi), but due to human mistake and strong winds grounded in a rocky shallow. The ship hardly stuck and was unable to refloat by own power, requesting assistance from the local authorities. At the scene of the accident were dispatched several rescue boats, which evacuated all the passengers to the shore. According to preliminary information there are no injured people from the crew and guests on board. The authorities started investigation for the root cause of the accident.

  [^cite_note-17]

- On September 22, 2016, [MV *Danica Joy*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_Danica_Joy) arrived at 4\:00 pm from Sandakan. But by around 9\:30 pm, she went off-balance while moored at the Zamboanga International Seaport. The ship was carrying 799 passengers, including of which 11 Malaysians and one Australian, and 603 deportees. All passengers were unloaded earlier on, resulting to no casualties.

  [^cite_note-18]

- On March 29, 2023, [MV *Lady Mary Joy 3*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_Lady_Mary_Joy_3) caught fire while plying the waters off Baluk-Baluk Island in [Hadji Muhtamad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadji_Muhtamad), [Basilan](297003.md).
