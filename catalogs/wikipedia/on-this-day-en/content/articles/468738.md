The **provinces of Saudi Arabia**, also referred to as **regions**, are the 13 first-level [administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Saudi_Arabia) of the [Kingdom of Saudi Arabia](349303.md). Each province is governed by a governor ([emir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir)).[^cite_note-statoids.com-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3]

## History

Prior to the [unification of Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unification_of_Saudi_Arabia) in 1932, the [Arabian Peninsula](47858.md) was composed of several distinct regions, many of which had been part of or influenced by the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md) (1299–1922), including the [Hejaz Vilayet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hejaz_Vilayet) and the [Lahsa Eyalet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahsa_Eyalet). Earlier in the region’s history, the [First Saudi State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Saudi_state) (1727–1818) and the [Second Saudi State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Saudi_state) (1824–1891) had established Saudi rule over much of [Najd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Najd) and its surroundings, laying the groundwork for later unification efforts. After unification, these historical regions — such as [Najd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Najd) and [Asir](468723.md) — along with former Ottoman territories like [Hejaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hejaz), formed the basis for the kingdom’s evolving administrative divisions, which were later reorganized into modern provinces.[^cite_note-statoids.com-1]

[King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Saudi_Arabia) [Fahd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahd_of_Saudi_Arabia) issued Royal Order A/92 on March 2, 1992, known as Law of the Provinces,[^cite_note-4] which provided for the division of the kingdom into 13 provinces. Subsequently, the five previous provinces were divided into thirteen provinces, called provinces (*manātiq*), each governed by administrative bodies called the emirates of the provinces (*imārāt al-manātiq*). The provinces form the first-level administrative division of the [Organization of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Saudi_Arabia) and are further divided into 136 [governorates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_governorates_of_Saudi_Arabia) (*muḥāfaẓāt*), which are the second-level division,[^cite_note-\:0-3] which are further subdivided into 1,347 municipal-level units (*marakiz*), and further subdivided into villages (*qura*) and neighborhoods (*ahya*).

## Government

Each province is governed by an *[Emir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir)* (provincial governor), who is assisted by the deputy *emir,* called *nā'ib.* The persons holding these positions are appointed by the [King of Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Saudi_Arabia). The *emir* is given the rank of minister, while the deputy *emir* is given the rank of [excellence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excellency).[^cite_note-\:0-3]
