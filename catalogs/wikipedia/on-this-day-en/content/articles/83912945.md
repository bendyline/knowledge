The **Ministry of Industry and Trade** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الصناعة والتجارة) is a government ministry of [Sudan](27421.md) responsible for industrial and trade policy. The ministry forms part of the economic sector of the [government of Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Sudan) and is responsible for policies concerning industrial production, value-added manufacturing, exports and trade.[^cite_note-SUNA-structure-1]

## History

Before the 2025 cabinet restructuring, Sudan had a separate trade portfolio called the **Ministry of Trade and Supply** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة التجارة والتموين) and, before that, the **Ministry of** **Commerce** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة التجارة), responsible for regulating imports and exports and promoting Sudanese exports. The current combined Ministry of Industry and Trade was included in the 22-ministry structure of the [Government of Hope](79211943.md) announced by Prime Minister [Kamil Idris](5065735.md) in June 2025.[^cite_note-SUNA-structure-1] The restructuring combined the previously separate industry and trade portfolios.[^cite_note-2] On 10 July 2025, Idris appointed Mahasin Ali Yaqoub as Minister of Industry and Trade.[^cite_note-SUNA-appointment-3] Yaqoub had previously worked in the ministry and had served as acting Minister of Industry from 2023.[^cite_note-4]

## Responsibilities

The ministry's stated responsibilities include promoting integrated and value-added manufacturing and increasing Sudanese exports.[^cite_note-SUNA-structure-1] Its activities also include industrial rehabilitation, management of strategic commodity supplies and the development of infrastructure for storage and food production.[^cite_note-5]

The ministry has participated in the reconstruction of Sudan's industrial sector following damage caused by the [civil war](73556699.md). In 2026, government programmes included the restoration of factories and proposals for integrated vegetable-oil, livestock-product and garment manufacturing projects.[^cite_note-6]

The ministry is also involved in the digitalisation of Sudan's foreign-trade procedures. In December 2025, the government began processing export transactions electronically through the *Baladna* platform, linking the ministry with the [Central Bank of Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Sudan), customs and other government agencies.[^cite_note-7]
