The **2026 World Baseball Classic championship game**, also commonly known as the **2026 WBC Final,** was the deciding game of the [2026 World Baseball Classic](73255749.md). This was the sixth edition of the [World Baseball Classic](1877416.md), a men's international [baseball](3850.md) tournament sanctioned by the [World Baseball Softball Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Baseball_Softball_Confederation) (WBSC) in partnership with [Major League Baseball](38776.md) (MLB) and [Major League Baseball Players Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Baseball_Players_Association) (MLBPA).[^cite_note-1]

The game was played on March 17, 2026, at [LoanDepot Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LoanDepot_Park) in [Miami](53846.md), Florida, between [Venezuela](4264306.md) and host [United States](4119270.md).[^cite_note-MLB-2] This was the first WBC final appearance by Venezuela, and the third consecutive by the United States.[^cite_note-3] Venezuela won the game 3–2, earning its first WBC title. It was the first baseball world championship and major international sporting competition won by Venezuela since the [1945 Amateur World Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945_Amateur_World_Series).

## Background

### Road to the championship

Further information: [2026 World Baseball Classic](73255749.md)

The [United States](4119270.md) won the [2017 WBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_World_Baseball_Classic) championship and reached the final in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_World_Baseball_Classic_championship_game), losing to [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_national_baseball_team).[^cite_note-4] [Venezuela](4264306.md) advanced to the final game for the first time; at the previous tournament in 2023, it lost to the United States in the quarterfinals.[^cite_note-5] The two nations had met five times before, in all five previous editions of the [World Baseball Classic](1877416.md), with the U.S. winning three games and Venezuela winning two.[^cite_note-usaven-6] Prior to the start of the tournament, the United States team was among the favorites to win the championship.[^cite_note-7] It went 3–1 in [Pool B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_World_Baseball_Classic_Pool_B), advancing as the pool's runner-up. Its only loss was an 8–6 [upset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upset_\(sports\)) against [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_national_baseball_team).[^cite_note-8] The U.S. defeated [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_national_baseball_team) in the quarterfinals,[^cite_note-9] and [Dominican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Republic_national_baseball_team) in the semifinals.[^cite_note-10]

Venezuela had not won a major international sporting competition since the [1945 Amateur World Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945_Amateur_World_Series).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] It had a 3–1 record in [Pool D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_World_Baseball_Classic_Pool_D), losing to the Dominican Republic in the final game, advancing as the pool runner-up.[^cite_note-15] It defeated defending champion Japan in the quarterfinals and [dark horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_horse) team Italy in the semifinals.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] Because Venezuela used six [relief pitchers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relief_pitcher) in its semifinal game against Italy, Venezuela's manager [Omar López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_L%C3%B3pez_\(baseball\)) said that three different [Major League Baseball](38776.md) (MLB) teams contacted him before the final requesting that their relief pitchers on their teams not pitch on back-to-back nights.[^cite_note-ESPN1-18] Because both teams had identical records in the earlier stages, the home team was determined by a coin toss, which went to the United States.[^cite_note-19]

### United States–Venezuela relations

See also: [United States–Venezuela relations](2071275.md)

The game took place against the backdrop of the [January 2026 raid](82021123.md) in which U.S. military forces captured Venezuelan president [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md) and his wife, [Cilia Flores](9530834.md).[^cite_note-maduro-21] Many Venezuelan expatriates living in Miami attended the games, bringing drums and dancing in the stands; some also yelled chants against Maduro during the tournament.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-\:0-23][^cite_note-24] Being considered a supporter of the government by many expats, [Miguel Cabrera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_Cabrera) was also heckled by some Venezuelans during the tournament.[^cite_note-\:0-23] Venezuelan players came to the U.S. for [spring training](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_training) earlier than in previous years to ensure their safety. Coaches and players on the Venezuelan national team refused to comment on the political situation due to fears of reprisals against their families and friends.[^cite_note-maduro-21][^cite_note-25]

## Game

### Details



[Maikel García](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maikel_Garc%C3%ADa), the [most valuable player](235000.md) of the tournament

The [starting pitchers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starting_pitcher) for the game were [Eduardo Rodríguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Rodr%C3%ADguez_\(left-handed_pitcher\)) for Venezuela and [Nolan McLean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nolan_McLean) for the United States.[^cite_note-26] After two scoreless innings, Venezuela scored the first run of the game in the top of the third inning. [Salvador Perez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvador_Perez) hit a [single](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single_\(baseball\)), followed by a [walk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Base_on_balls) for [Ronald Acuña Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Acu%C3%B1a_Jr.), the baserunners advanced on a [wild pitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_pitch) by McLean, and then [Maikel García](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maikel_Garc%C3%ADa) hit a [run batted in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Run_batted_in) (RBI) [sacrifice fly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacrifice_fly) that allowed Perez to reach home plate and score. [Wilyer Abreu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilyer_Abreu) led off the top of the fifth inning with a solo [home run](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_run), extending the lead to 2–0.[^cite_note-recap-27]

The United States was held scoreless by Rodríguez for 4+1⁄3 innings, then also by relief pitchers [Eduard Bazardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduard_Bazardo), [José Buttó](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Butt%C3%B3), and [Ángel Zerpa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81ngel_Zerpa). In the bottom of the eighth inning, pitched by Venezuela's [Andrés Machado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Machado), the United States' [Bobby Witt Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Witt_Jr.) drew a walk, then [Bryce Harper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryce_Harper) hit a two-run home run, tying the game with one inning left to play.[^cite_note-recap-27]

In the top of the ninth inning, [Luis Arráez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Arr%C3%A1ez) drew a walk and [Javier Sanoja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Sanoja) substituted for him as a [pinch runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinch_runner). Sanoja then [stole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stolen_base) second base and [Eugenio Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugenio_Su%C3%A1rez) drove Sanoja in with an RBI [double](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_\(baseball\)) to give Venezuela a 3–2 lead. In the bottom of the ninth inning, another Venezuela relief pitcher, [Daniel Palencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Palencia), retired the United States without scoring, to secure the victory.[^cite_note-recap-27] During the [gold medal](526488.md) presentation at the end of the game, García was named the tournament's [most valuable player](235000.md).[^cite_note-28]

## Broadcasting

The WBC championship game was televised in the United States on [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLB_on_Fox) in English and on [Fox Deportes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Deportes) in Spanish.[^cite_note-30] [Play-by-play](1662121.md#Main/play-by-play_commentator) announcer [Joe Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Davis_\(sportscaster\)) and [color analyst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_commentator) [John Smoltz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Smoltz) called the English language broadcast of the games for Fox, and were joined by [Ken Rosenthal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Rosenthal) and [Tom Verducci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Verducci) as [field reporters](1662121.md#Sideline_reporter).[^cite_note-31] The game averaged 10.8 million viewers on Fox and Fox Deportes combined, peaking at 12.1 million during the later stages of the game.[^cite_note-viewership1-32] The broadcasters in Venezuela were a [pool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_pool) of TV channels, that included: [ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN)/[Disney+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney+), [1Baseball Network](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_Baseball_Network), [Televen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Televen), [IVC](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/IVC_\(canal_de_televisi%C3%B3n\)), ByM Sport and [Venevisión](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venevisi%C3%B3n).[^cite_note-33]

## Aftermath

Following Venezuela's victory, acting president [Delcy Rodríguez](44199228.md) declared a national holiday for March 18, with the exception of essential workers. She also received the trophy at the presidential palace and declared that it should tour around Venezuela.[^cite_note-34] For their victory, the Venezuelan team will reportedly split US\$6.75 million of the US\$37 million prize pool among the players and staff, for approximately US\$112,500 per person.[^cite_note-35]

On September 16, 2026, Venezuela's championship-winning team was inducted into the [Venezuelan Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_Baseball_Hall_of_Fame_and_Museum), with a unanimous vote from the Hall of Fame's historical committee.[^cite_note-VBHoF2026-36]
