The **Pivdennyi Bridge** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Південний міст [\[piu̯ˈdɛnːɪj ˈmist\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Ukrainian), lit. '**Southern bridge**') is a bridge on the [Dnieper River](380138.md) in [Kyiv](585629.md), [Ukraine](31750.md).

## History

The bridge was designed by the architect A. Gavrilov ("Mostobud") and a group of engineers headed by G. Fux. The 7 km (4 mi) long metro/automobile bridge was built in 1990.&#x20;

It is the second [metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_Metro) bridge in Kyiv, serving both the [Syretsko-Pecherska metro line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syretsko%E2%80%93Pecherska_line) and road traffic.

Due to insufficient funding of the object during the 1990s, as well as because of a significant increase in traffic, which the engineers responsible for the project were unable to predict, starting from the 2010s sections of the bridge have been frequently closed for traffic due to constant repair works.[^cite_note-tex-1]

In 2021 the bridge was determined to be in a limited operational state, resulting in emergency repair work. The bridge reopened on December 1, 2024, but was soon closed again from March 28, 2025 to August 1, 2025 for necessary repairs on the right-bank overpass.[^cite_note-2]

On October 1 and 2, 2026, drones twice struck the bridge near the support structure. As a result, both the Pivdennyi Bridge and Paton Bridge were closed by the city of Kiev pending a technical evaluation.[^cite_note-3]

## Construction

The main span of the bridge consists of metal sheets suspended on steel cables. Parts of the bridge beyond the main span are made of [prefabricated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefabrication) concrete elements, which were transported by water and assembled on the spot. The whole construction project cost 112 million [Soviet rubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_ruble), which was equal to [\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Dollar)200 million at the time.[^cite_note-tex-1]

## Design



Traffic on Southern Bridge in Kyiv

The cables holding the spans on the bridge are supported by a [ferroconcrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferroconcrete) double-column pylon 135 m (443 ft) in height. The bridge currently has three traffic lanes and one metro track in both directions (total of six vehicle lanes and two rail tracks).

## Location

The bridge is part of the [E40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_route_E40)/[M03](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highway_M03_\(Ukraine\)) and is formally an extension of the local Promyslova Street.
