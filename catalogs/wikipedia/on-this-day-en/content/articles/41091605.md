**Ziba Kenar** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): زيبا کنار)[^cite_note-4] is a village in, and the capital of, [Aliabad-e Ziba Kenar Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliabad-e_Ziba_Kenar_Rural_District)[^cite_note-Rasht_County_Rural_Districts-5] in [Lasht-e Nesha District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lasht-e_Nesha_District) of [Rasht County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rasht_County), [Gilan](210689.md) province, [Iran](14653.md).

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population, as Aliabad, was 2,050 in 604 households.[^cite_note-2006_Gilan_Province-6] The following census in 2011 counted 2,133 people in 692 households, by which time the village was listed as Ziba Kenar.[^cite_note-2011_Gilan_Province-7] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 1,522 people in 530 households. It was the most populous village in its rural district.[^cite_note-2016_Gilan_Province-2]
