The **Federal Court of Australia** is an Australian [superior court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superior_court) which has jurisdiction to deal with most [civil disputes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_dispute) governed by federal law (with the exception of [family law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_law) matters), along with some summary (less serious) and indictable (more serious) [criminal matters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_law).[^cite_note-2] Cases are heard at first instance mostly by single judges. In cases of importance, a [full court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_court) comprising three judges can be convened upon determination by the Chief Justice. The Court also has [appellate](643.md) jurisdiction, which is mostly exercised by a Full Court comprising three judges (although sometimes by a panel of five judges and sometimes by a single judge), the only avenue of appeal from which lies to the [High Court of Australia](314201.md). In the [Australian court hierarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_court_hierarchy), the Federal Court occupies a position equivalent to the supreme courts of each of the states and territories. In relation to the other courts in the federal stream, it is superior to the [Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Circuit_and_Family_Court_of_Australia) for all jurisdictions except [family law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_family_law). It was established in 1976 by the Federal Court of Australia Act.

The Chief Justice of the Federal Court is [Debra Mortimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debra_Mortimer).

## Jurisdiction

The Federal Court has no constitutional jurisdiction; its jurisdiction is provided by statute.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The Court's original jurisdiction include matters arising from Commonwealth legislation such as, for example, matters relating to taxation, trade practices, native title, intellectual property, industrial relations, corporations, immigration and bankruptcy.[^cite_note-FCA-5][^cite_note-Kenny-6]

The Federal Court of Australia also has [appellate jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appellate_jurisdiction) from Division 2 of the [Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Circuit_and_Family_Court_of_Australia) on all general federal law matters ([family law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_law) matters are appealed to Division 1 of that Court).[^cite_note-7] The Court also exercises general appellate jurisdiction in criminal and civil matters on appeal from the [Supreme Court of Norfolk Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Norfolk_Island);[^cite_note-FCA-5][^cite_note-Kenny-6] and exercises appellate jurisdiction in appeals from state supreme courts in some federal matters.[^cite_note-8] Other federal courts and tribunals where the Court exercises appellate jurisdiction include the [Australian Sports Anti-Doping Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Sports_Anti-Doping_Authority)[^cite_note-9] and the [Australian Human Rights and Equal Opportunity Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Human_Rights_and_Equal_Opportunity_Commission).[^cite_note-10]

The Court has concurrent jurisdiction with the [Australian Capital Territory Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_the_Australian_Capital_Territory) and [Northern Territory Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_the_Northern_Territory) over civil matters arising under those Territories' laws.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

## Related courts

The jurisdiction of the Federal Court of Australia includes the jurisdiction previously exercised by three former federal courts, the [Federal Court of Bankruptcy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Court_of_Bankruptcy), [Commonwealth Industrial Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Industrial_Court) and [Industrial Relations Court of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_Relations_Court_of_Australia).

### Federal Court of Bankruptcy

The Federal Court of Bankruptcy had jurisdiction in bankruptcy matters and was created in 1930.[^cite_note-14] The jurisdiction in bankruptcy was transferred to the Federal Court of Australia on its establishment in 1977.[^cite_note-15]

### Commonwealth Industrial Court

The Commonwealth Industrial Court was established in 1956 as a result of the *[Boilermaker's case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R_v_Kirby;_Ex_parte_Boilermakers'_Society_of_Australia)*,[^cite_note-Boilermakers-16] where the High Court held that a [Chapter III Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapter_III_Court) could not exercise a non-judicial power, the arbitral function, because of the constitutional [separation of powers in Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separation_of_powers_in_Australia).[^cite_note-Boilermakers-16] The judicial functions were given to the newly created Commonwealth Industrial Court and the arbitral functions were given to [Commonwealth Conciliation and Arbitration Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Conciliation_and_Arbitration_Commission).[^cite_note-17]

The court was renamed the Australian Industrial Court in 1973.[^cite_note-18] In 1977 the jurisdiction of the Australian Industrial Court was transferred to the Federal Court of Australia.[^cite_note-19]

### Industrial Relations Court of Australia

In 1993 the industrial relations jurisdiction of the Federal Court of Australia was transferred to the Industrial Relations Court of Australia,[^cite_note-20] and transferred back to the Federal Court of Australia in 1996.[^cite_note-21] The last judge of the Industrial Relations Court, [Anthony North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_North), retired in September 2018.[^cite_note-22] The court was formally abolished on 1 March 2021.[^cite_note-Workplace_Relations_and_Other_Legislation_Amendment_(Abolishment_of_Industrial_Relations_Court)_Proclamation_2020-23]
