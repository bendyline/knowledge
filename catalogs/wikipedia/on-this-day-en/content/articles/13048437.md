The **Ministry of Foreign Affairs** (abbreviated as **MoFA**; [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): وزارتِ خارجہ, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Urdu): *Wazārat-ē-Xārjā*) is a [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) of the [Government of Pakistan](1897833.md) tasked in managing Pakistan's diplomatic and consular relations as well as its [foreign policy](233404.md). Headed by the [Minister of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Pakistan\)), the ministry is also responsible for maintaining Pakistani governmental offices and missions abroad with diplomatic and consular status.

## Minister

Main article: [Minister of Foreign Affairs (Pakistan)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Pakistan\))

The Minister of Foreign Affairs is [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Pakistan) member who responsible maintaining Pakistan's [foreign policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_policy_of_Pakistan) as well as its [diplomatic missions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_missions_of_Pakistan) abroad. [Muhammad Ishaq Dar](988764.md) is the current Minister of Foreign Affairs.
