**Lucas Alejandro Vignale** (23 September 1997 – 14 June 2026) was an Argentine director and screenwriter.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] He was best known for co-writing and co-directing the 2026 [coming-of-age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming-of-age_story) drama film *[The River Train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_River_Train)* along with [Lorenzo Ferro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Ferro).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-b-6][^cite_note-7]

## Life and career

Vignale was born in [Buenos Aires](255919.md) on 23 September 1997.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] He attended and graduated from [Fundación Universidad del Cine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundaci%C3%B3n_Universidad_del_Cine). He collaborated with [Trueno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trueno_\(rapper\)), [Nicki Nicole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicki_Nicole), [Duki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duki_\(rapper\)), [J Balvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J_Balvin), [Wos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wos_\(musician\)), and [Bizarrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bizarrap),[^cite_note-10] and won a [Gardel Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premios_Gardel) in 2022,[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] and was nominated for another one in 2024.[^cite_note-13]

In 2023, Vignale signed with Underwonder Content, a content production company.[^cite_note-14] In 2024, he co-wrote and co-directed the [short film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_film) *La Pasión* along with Ferro,[^cite_note-15] for which he and Ferro received the [Stimulus Award for Argentine Cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_Argentina) from the [Buenos Aires International Festival of Independent Cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires_International_Festival_of_Independent_Cinema) for the short film.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] In 2026, his feature [coming-of-age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming-of-age_story) drama debut, *[The River Train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_River_Train)*, also co-directed with Ferro, premiered at the [76th Berlin International Film Festival](81825397.md).[^cite_note-b-6]

## Death

Main article: [2026 Rio de Janeiro mid-air collision](83453101.md)

In June 2026, Vignale and Argentine YouTuber [Gaspi](83453165.md) were in Brazil participating in events supporting American musician [Oliver Tree](58040727.md)'s first world tour, promoting Tree's album *[Love You Madly Hate You Badly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_You_Madly_Hate_You_Badly)*. On 14 June, Vignale, Gaspi, Tree and Brazilian music producer [Lucas Frota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_Frota) were killed in an [helicopter collision](83453101.md) over [Recreio dos Bandeirantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recreio_dos_Bandeirantes), in [Rio de Janeiro](25936.md).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-heraldo-19] The helicopter the group were flying in collided with a second aircraft in mid-air, resulting in their deaths along with the helicopters' two pilots.[^cite_note-20] Vignale was 28 years old.[^cite_note-heraldo-19]
