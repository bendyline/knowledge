**Middle Shabelle** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Shabeellaha Dhexe*,[^cite_note-5] [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شبيلي الوسطى, [Italian](14708.md): *Medio Scebeli*) is an administrative region (*[gobol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Somalia)*) in southern [Somalia](27358.md).[^cite_note-Cwfsom-6]

It is bordered by [Hiran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiran,_Somalia) to the north, [Galgaduud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galgaduud) to the east, the Indian Ocean to the south and the region of [Lower Shabelle](185139.md) and the capital [Mogadishu](60154.md) to the west. The [Shabelle River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shabelle_River) also passes through this region.&#x20;

The region of Middle Shabelle formed the [Hirshabelle State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hirshabelle_State) by 2016, which considers itself an autonomous state within the larger [Federal Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Republic) of Somalia, as defined by the [Provisional Constitution of the Federal Republic of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Constitution_of_the_Federal_Republic_of_Somalia).[^cite_note-allafrica.com-7]

## Overview

As part of the former [Benadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banaadir) region, Middle Shabelle's capital was [Mogadishu](60154.md) up until the mid-1980s, when the town of [Jowhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jowhar) became the capital. It is named after the [Shebelle River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shebelle_River) that passes through this region.

The region is regarded as one of the most fertile regions in Somalia and was the first region to have multiple factories opened up in it, such as a sugar and clothes factory. It was also the main source of local grown food before the civil war.&#x20;

Today, the region supports livestock production, rain-fed and irrigated agriculture and fisheries, with an annual rainfall between 150 and 500 millimeters. Covering an area of approximately 60,000 square kilometres, the region has a 400 kilometre coastline on the Indian Ocean. The Shabelle river runs for 150 kilometres through the region.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

## Demographics

A pre-war census estimated the population at 2 million and today the Regional Council claims that the region's population is around 1.5 million. It is also estimated that 60% of the regions population are nomads or farmers.[^cite_note-10]

However, the FSNAU population estimate survey in 2022 estimated the population as being 1,618,324, which is highly disputed.[^cite_note-11]

The majority clans in the region Are the Abgaal and Galjecel. Other clans that live in the region include other Mudulood clans.[^cite_note-12]
