**Hanwha Ocean Co., Ltd.** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 한화오션), formerly known as **Daewoo Shipbuilding & Marine Engineering** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 대우조선해양), is one of the "Big Three" shipbuilders of [South Korea](27019.md), along with [Hyundai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Heavy_Industries) and [Samsung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung_Heavy_Industries).

In 1978, [Daewoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daewoo) acquired what would become DSME.[^cite_note-3]

On 21 February 2011, the [A. P. Moller-Maersk Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._P._Moller-Maersk_Group) (Maersk) ordered 10 large [container ships](262084.md) from DSME, each with a capacity of 18,000 containers, surpassing the then record holder; the [Mærsk E-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A6rsk_E-class) at 15,200 containers.[^cite_note-4] The contract is worth \$1.9bn.[^cite_note-\:0-5] The first was to be delivered in 2014. In June 2011, Maersk ordered ten more, for another \$1.9bn.[^cite_note-\:0-5] The new class is called the [Triple E class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maersk_Triple_E_class).

On December 20, 2011, Daewoo Shipbuilding Marine Engineering won the largest single defense contract by a Korean firm; valued at \$1.07 billion to build three [Indonesian submarines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang_Bogo-class_submarine).[^cite_note-6] It also would mark the first exports of submarines from South Korea.[^cite_note-7]

On 22 February 2012, a £452 million order was placed with DSME for four 37,000 tonne [double hulled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_hull) '[MARS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MARS_tanker)' [fast fleet tankers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oiler_\(ship\)) by Britain's [Ministry of Defence](203793.md) for the [Royal Fleet Auxiliary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Fleet_Auxiliary). The ships entered service in 2017.

The firm built 15 [icebreaking](483060.md) [LNG carriers](12378960.md) commissioned by [Yamal LNG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamal_LNG) which are used to export [liquefied natural gas](832128.md) from the Russian Arctic. The [ice class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_class) Arc7 [Yamalmax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamalmax) ships are designed to operate year-round from the Yamal Peninsula and to break ice up to 1.5 m (4.9 ft) thick when sailing bow first and up to 2.1 m (6.9 ft) thick in astern direction. The tankers were designed in Finland by [Aker Arctic Technology Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aker_Arctic)[^cite_note-Bloomberg7918-8][^cite_note-AA318-9]

The South Korean Board of Audit and Inspection found [₩](298884.md)1.5 trillion (equivalent to ₩1.83 trillion or [US\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USD)1.28 billion in 2025)[^cite_note-inflation-KRW-10] of accounting fraud in DSME's books on 15 June 2016.[^cite_note-11] In July, 2016, shares in DSME were suspended from trading, and were announced to be suspended until at least September 28, 2017.[^cite_note-12] After suffering losses of Won3.3tn in 2015 and Won2.7tn in 2016, it was given a [₩](298884.md)2.9 trillion (equivalent to ₩3.46 trillion or [US\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USD)2.44 billion in 2025)[^cite_note-inflation-KRW-10] government loan in March 2017 to prevent bankruptcy.[^cite_note-13]

In 2017, it was uncovered that North Korea may have hacked the company and stole company's blueprints in April 2016.[^cite_note-14]

In December 2022, [Hanwha Group](32047031.md) announced that it would acquire a controlling 49.3 percent stake in Daewoo Shipbuilding & Marine Engineering worth 2 trillion won (US\$1.5 billion). The deal was supported by the [Korea Development Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea_Development_Bank) in an attempt to improve competition in the Korean shipbuilding industry.[^cite_note-15]

In July 2024, Hanwha Ocean became the second Korean shipbuilder after [Hyundai Heavy Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HD_Hyundai_Heavy_Industries) to sign a master ship repair agreement (MSRA) with [Naval Supply Systems Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Supply_Systems_Command) (NAVSUP), qualifying it to participate in the [U.S. Navy](20518076.md)'s [MRO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maintenance) business.[^cite_note-16]

On 29 August 2024, Hanwha Ocean won an MRO contract for the [USNS Wally Schirra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USNS_Wally_Schirra), the US Navy's [auxiliary ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auxiliary_ship), and 3 months of maintenance work will take place at the [Geoje](968842.md) Shipyard from 2 September 2024.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

In July 2026, Canadian Prime Minister [Mark Carney](13571049.md) selected Germany's [TKMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TKMS) over Hanwha Ocean for the country's multi-billion dollar submarine fleet construction contract.[^cite_note-19]
