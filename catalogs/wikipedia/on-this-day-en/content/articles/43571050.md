**Alexey Sergeyevich Bugaev** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Алексей Сергеевич Бугаев, also [transliterated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transliterated) Aleksei; born 30 March 1997) is a Russian [Para alpine skier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing) who competed at the [2014 Winter Paralympics](2311504.md), winning five medals.[^cite_note-ipc-1] He won the [International Sports Prize World Athlete of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Sciacca_International_Awards_Sports_Prize) award in 2018.[^cite_note-2]

## Early life

Bugaev was born in [Krasnoyarsk](72233.md), Russia. He was born missing four fingers and the top of his thumb on his right hand.[^cite_note-ipc-1][^cite_note-aap-3] he took up skiing after his parents took him to a ski resort to help him integrate better into society.[^cite_note-ipc-1]

## Career

Bugaev competes in the [LW6/8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LW6/8) [para-alpine skiing classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para-alpine_skiing_classification), for athletes with an impairment in one arm, using two skis and a single ski pole.[^cite_note-ipc-1][^cite_note-aap-3]

At the 2013 [IPC Alpine Skiing World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPC_Alpine_Skiing_World_Championships) held in [La Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Molina_\(ski_resort\)), Spain, he broke his arm whilst finishing 5th in the slalom.[^cite_note-ipc-1] Competing with his arm in plaster he won silver medals in the standing super combined and giant slalom events.[^cite_note-ipc-1] He also placed 6th in the super-G and 11th in the downhill.[^cite_note-ipc-1]

He was selected to compete at the [2014 Winter Paralympics](2311504.md) held in [Sochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sochi), Russia. In his first event, the [standing downhill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_skiing_at_the_2014_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_downhill), he won a silver medal behind [Markus Salcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markus_Salcher) of Austria.[^cite_note-4] He won a second medal a day later, taking the bronze in the [standing super-G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_skiing_at_the_2014_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_super-G) as Salcher again won the gold medal.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Bugaev won his first Paralympic gold medal in his third event of the Games, the [standing slalom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_skiing_at_the_2014_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_slalom). He led the field after the first run and extended his lead on the second to take the gold medal in a combined time of 1 minute 38.97 seconds, 1.27 seconds of second placed [Vincent Gauthier-Manuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Gauthier-Manuel).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] He won a second gold medal in the [super-combined](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_skiing_at_the_2014_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_combined); he completed the course in 50.30 seconds to lead the field after the slalom portio of the competition and eventually won the event by 1.10 seconds over [Matthias Lanzinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthias_Lanzinger) following the super-G portion.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] Bugaev won his second silver medal, and fifth medal overall, in the [giant slalom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_skiing_at_the_2014_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_giant_slalom), finishing 1.43 seconds behind gold medalist Gauthier-Manuel on the first run and 0.57 seconds behind him on the second run.[^cite_note-11]
