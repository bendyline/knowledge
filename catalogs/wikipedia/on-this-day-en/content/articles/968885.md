**Yangsan** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 양산; Korean pronunciation: [\[jaŋ.san\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Korean)) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_South_Korea) in [Gyeongsangnam-do Province](319775.md), [South Korea](27019.md).

It borders Ulsan to the northeast, Gijang-gun and Geumjeong District in Busan to the southeast, Gimhae to the southwest, and Miryang to the northwest. City Hall is located in Nambu-dong, Yangsan.

## Administrative divisions

Currently, Yangsan is made up of 1 Eup (administrative division), 4 Myeon (administrative division) and 8 Dong.

## Flag

According to the city's website, the flag of Yangsan depicts a "forward-looking city" which, like the [magnolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnolia), the city flower, "aims for a vibrant and hope-filled future in the 21st century".[^cite_note-\:0-2]

The [negative space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negative_space) in the center depicts the letter "Y" and represents the intersection of the [Gyeongbu Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyeongbu_Expressway) and [Namhae Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namhae_Expressway) within the city, while the blue depicts the letter "S" while representing the Yangsancheon Stream and the [Nakdong River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakdong_River). The green symbolizes the "strong spirit of the Yangsan people", while the yellow represents hope or the future inspired by the sun.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

## Transportation



Yangsan station (Busan Subway Line 2)

### Rail

Yangsan has two stations on the [Gyeongbu Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyeongbu_Line): [Mulgeum station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulgeum_station) (물금역) and [Wondong station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wondong_station) (원동역). The nearest [KTX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KTX) stations are [Ulsan station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulsan_station) in Ulsan and [Gupo station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gupo_station_\(Korail\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Busan.

### Subway

[Busan Subway Line 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Busan_Subway_Line_2) serves the city, with five stations currently operating: [Yangsan Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangsan_Station), [Namyangsan Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namyangsan_Station), [Busan National University Yangsan Campus station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Busan_National_University_Yangsan_Campus_station?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Jeungsan station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeungsan_station_\(Yangsan\)) and [Hopo station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopo_station). An additional station that will connect with the [Yangsan Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangsan_Metro) is currently under construction.

[Yangsan Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangsan_Metro) is under construction and is scheduled to open in 2024.

### Bus

The city of Yangsan is served by a local bus system. In addition to serving the city itself, there are long-distance local buses to [Busan](68115.md), as well as inter-city lines connecting to [Ulsan](323111.md), [Changwon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changwon), [Gyeongju](426047.md), and several other cities.

### Highways

The [Gyeongbu Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyeongbu_Expressway) (#1) and the [Jungang Expressway Branch Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jungang_Expressway_Branch) (#551) run through the city of Yangsan.

In addition, [National Route 35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Route_35_\(South_Korea\)) bisects the city center.

## Government and politics

### Politics

The city's mayor is [Na Dong-yeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Na_Dong-yeon?action=edit\&redlink=1), a member of the [People Power Party](63143730.md) who was first elected in [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_South_Korean_local_elections). He was subsequently re-elected in [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_South_Korean_local_elections), [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_South_Korean_local_elections), and [2026](82662328.md), becoming the city's first four-term mayor.[^cite_note-5]
