**Cal Miskaad** (also **Calmiskaad**, Al Miskad; [Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Buuraha Cal Miskaad*; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): علمسكاد, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *ʿAlamiskad*) is a [mountain range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_range) in [Puntland](233569.md). It stretches across an area between the east of [Karin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karin,_Bari), to the tip of the [Horn of Africa](62142.md). Its peak sits at almost 2,000 m (6,600 ft) on [Mount Bahaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Bahaya), east of [Bosaso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosaso). [Islamic State](53150866.md) (IS) has bases in the range, consisting mainly of former [Al-Shabaab](8726463.md) fighters.[^cite_note-2] Places in Cal Miskaad include [Biyo Kulule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biyo_Kulule), [Hantaara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hantaara), [Habeeno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habeeno), [Balade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balade_\(Somalia\)), Guri awr, Marraagade, Jidin, Elalo, Buqo Caleed, Daldheer, Mareero, Casaasa,and [El Dhurre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Dhurre).&#x20;



[Yalho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yalho) valley is an instrumental for the separation of Golis ranges of Cal Miskaad and Cal Madow.



[Karin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karin,_Bari) is an interval spot that divides between Mountains of Cal Madow and Cal Miskaad respectively.
