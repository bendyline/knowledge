**Nakuru County** is one of the 47 [counties of Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Kenya). The county is host to Kenya's fifth urban area that was [assigned the status of "city"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_and_towns_in_Kenya_by_population), [Nakuru City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakuru_City) (which also serves as the county capital)[^cite_note-6] by President [Uhuru Kenyatta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uhuru_Kenyatta), ranking it alongside the other [Kenyan cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Kenya) of [Nairobi](21482.md), [Mombasa](200152.md), [Kisumu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kisumu), and [Eldoret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eldoret). With a population of 453,000 as per the 2025 census,[^cite_note-7] it is the third most-populous county in Kenya after [Nairobi County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nairobi_County) and [Kiambu County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiambu_County), in that order. With an area of 7,496.5 km<sup>2</sup>, it is Kenya's 19th largest county in size.&#x20;

Nakuru County formed part of the [Rift Valley Province](72874.md) until 2013 when [Kenyan provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Kenya) were disbanded in-favor of [devolved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devolved) counties as per the [2010 Kenyan Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Kenya). The county is now variously considered to be part of either the [Mount Kenya region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Kenya_region)[^cite_note-Paper-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] or the [Great Rift Valley region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Rift_Valley,_Kenya), although neither region is an official administrative nor governmental subdivision of Kenya.

## Sites of interest

Nakuru County is home to [Lake Nakuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Nakuru), [Lake Elmenteita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Elmenteita) and [Lake Naivasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Naivasha), which are some of the Rift Valley soda lakes. Lake Nakuru is best known for its thousands, sometimes millions of flamingos nesting along the shores. The surface of the shallow lake is often hardly recognizable due to the continually shifting mass of the beautiful pink birds. The number of flamingos on the lake varies with water and food conditions and the best vantage point is from Baboon Cliff. Also of interest, an area of 188 km around the lake fenced off as a sanctuary to protect Rothschild giraffe and black rhinos.

Other sites of interest around Nakuru include [Menengai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menengai) Crater, an extinct volcano 2,490 m (8,167 ft) high, and the [Nakuru National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakuru_National_Park?action=edit\&redlink=1) which is a wildlife zone. The views of the crater itself, as well as the surrounding countryside, are very spectacular.

[Hyrax Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyrax_Hill) Prehistoric Site, discovered by the Leakey's in 1926, is considered a major [Neolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic) and [Iron Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Age) site. The adjoining museum features are from various nearby excavations.[^cite_note-13]

The second-largest surviving volcanic crater in the world, the [Menengai Crater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menengai_Crater) is 2500 meters above sea level at its highest point. The crater plunges 500 m down from the rim and the summit is accessible by foot or vehicle 8 km from the main road.[^cite_note-14] The mountain is also surrounded by the nature's reserve.

The [Rift Valley Institute of Science and Technology](https://www.rvist.ac.ke/) is a tourist attraction. The institute was established in 1979 by the leaders and people of Rift Valley.

## Urban areas

Surrounding towns include Lanet, which lies approximately 10 km from Nakuru. Lanet is predominantly a residential town and is home to an army base. [Njoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Njoro) is another urban town that lies 20 km in the outskirts of Nakuru, and is a small agricultural town with a local university aimed at promoting agricultural development in [Kenya](188171.md), namely [Egerton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egerton_University) (est. 1934). [Naivasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naivasha) is another major significant urban Centre in Nakuru County, which sits at the floor of the Great Rift Valley and serves as a major transit of goods. Travelers passing through Naivasha usually make stopovers along the Naivasha – Nakuru Highway to enjoy the beautiful and scenic escarpments of the Great Rift Valley.

### Nakuru County Peace Accord

The [Nakuru County Peace Accord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakuru_County_Peace_Accord) (or "Rift Valley Peace Accord") refers to the peace agreement signed on 19 August 2012 between elders of the Agikuyu (see also [Kikuyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kikuyu,_Kenya)) and [Kalenjin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalenjin_people) communities as well as other ethnic groups of Kenya.[^cite_note-18] which was designed to address sources of ethnic conflict and a history of violence in the Rift Valley region of Kenya.[^cite_note-19] It was signed following a 16-month-long peace process led by the [National Cohesion and Integration Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cohesion_and_Integration_Commission) and the National Steering Committee on Peace Building and Conflict Management.

## Politics

The county has eleven [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituencies_of_Kenya) which are represented in the [Parliament of Kenya](10769176.md):&#x20;

- [Naivasha Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naivasha_Constituency) represented by Hon. [Jayne Wanjiru Njeri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayne_Njeri_Wanjiru_Kihara) – United Democratic Alliance (UDA)

- [Nakuru Town West Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakuru_Town_West_Constituency) represented by Hon. [Samuel Arama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Arama) – Jubilee Party

- [Nakuru Town East Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakuru_Town_East_Constituency) represented by Hon. [David Gikaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Gikaria) – United Democratic Alliance (UDA)

- [Kuresoi South Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuresoi_South_Constituency) Hon. Joseph Kipkosgei Tonui – United Democratic Alliance (UDA)

- [Kuresoi North Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuresoi_North_Constituency) Hon. Alfred Kiprono Mutai – United Democratic Alliance (UDA)

- [Molo Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molo_Constituency) represented by Hon. Francis Kuria Kimani – United Democratic Alliance (UDA)

- [Rongai Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rongai_Constituency) represented by Hon. Paul Chebor – United Democratic Alliance (UDA)

- [Subukia Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subukia_Constituency) represented by Hon. Samuel Kinuthia Gachobe – United Democratic Alliance (UDA)

- [Njoro Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Njoro_Constituency) represented by Hon. [Charity Kathambi Chepkwony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charity_Kathambi_Chepkwony) – United Democratic Alliance (UDA)

- [Gilgil Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgil_Constituency) represented by Hon. [Martha Wangari Wanjira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_Wangari_Wanjira) – United Democratic Alliance (UDA)

- [Bahati Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahati_Constituency) represented by Hon. [Irene Njoki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irene_Mrembo_Njoki) – Jubilee Party

Nakuru County was seen as the epicenter of violence in the aftermath of the disputed [2007 Presidential Elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenyan_presidential_election,_2007) which left over 1,100 people dead and over 300,000 displaced nationwide.[^cite_note-20]
