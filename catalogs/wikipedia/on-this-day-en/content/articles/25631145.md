# Magħtab



The landfill, commonly known as 'Mount Magħtab'

**Magħtab** is a [Maltese](19137.md) village. It lies in the valley of the same name in the [local council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_councils_of_Malta) of [Naxxar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naxxar).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] It is the site of the largest landfill in the island, commonly known as 'Mount Magħtab'.[^cite_note-tom-3] The landfill was closed when [Malta joined the EU in 2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maltese_European_Union_membership_referendum,_2003),[^cite_note-4] but two others were subsequently opened.[^cite_note-shift-5]

The *[Il-Widna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il-Widna)* [acoustic mirror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustic_mirror) is located 1,410 metres (1,540 yd) away. The hill of [Ġebel San Pietru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%A0ebel_San_Pietru) also lies within the confines of Magħtab.

&#x20;In December 2021, residents noticed new excavated rock and construction waste, forming a new 'waste mountain'.[^cite_note-shift-5]&#x20;



The 'Ear' or acoustic mirror (centre)

On June 1, 2026, a fireworks factory near the village exploded, injuring two farmers and reportedly rattling buildings across Malta.[^cite_note-6]
