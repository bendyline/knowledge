The following is a list of health ministers of Jamaica since adult suffrage (1944).[^cite_note-observer2017-1]

1. [Rose Leon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose_Leon) (1953–1955)

2. C. L. A. Stuart (1955–1959)

3. [Ivan Lloyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Lloyd) (1959–1962)

4. [Herbert Eldemire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Eldemire) (1962–1972)

5. [Kenneth McNeill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_McNeill) (1972–1977)

6. [Douglas Manley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Manley) (1977–1980)

7. [Kenneth Baugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Baugh) (1980–1989)

8. [Easton Douglas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easton_Douglas?action=edit\&redlink=1) (1989–1993)

9. [Desmond Leakey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desmond_Leakey?action=edit\&redlink=1) (1993–1995)

10. [Peter Phillips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Phillips_\(Jamaican_politician\)) (1995–1997)

11. [John Junor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Junor_\(politician\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) (1998–2006)

12. [Horace Dalley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horace_Dalley) (2006–2007)

13. [Rudyard Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudyard_Spencer) (2007–2012)

14. [Fenton Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenton_Ferguson) (2012–2015)

15. [Horace Dalley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horace_Dalley) (2015–2016)

16. [Christopher Tufton](22506208.md) (2016–present)
