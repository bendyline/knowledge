# Igor Gromyko



Igor Gromyko

**Igor Anatolyevich Gromyko** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): И́горь Анато́льевич Громы́ко; born 25 June 1954) is a Russian diplomat.&#x20;

## Career

Since June 17, 2019, he had been the [Russian ambassador to Mali](81739314.md).[^cite_note-1] From August 28, 2019 to August 20, 2025 he was the [ambassador to Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ambassadors_of_Russia_to_Niger), on a part-time basis.[^cite_note-2]

## Awards

- [Medal of the Order "For Merit to the Fatherland"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_of_the_Order_%22For_Merit_to_the_Fatherland%22) II degree (July 17, 2019) - *for a great contribution to the implementation of the foreign policy course of the Russian Federation and many years of conscientious service.*

  [^cite_note-3]

- [Order of Friendship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Friendship) (November 21, 2024) - *for a great contribution to the implementation of the foreign policy of the Russian Federation and many years of conscientious diplomatic service.*

  [^cite_note-4]
