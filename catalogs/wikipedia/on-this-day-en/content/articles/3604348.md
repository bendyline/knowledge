**Aden** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): عَدَنْ) is a [governorate](690277.md) of [Yemen](350939.md), including the city of [Aden](38050.md). At the 2004 census, it had a population of 589,419.[^cite_note-2] The ancient capital, the port city of [Crater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crater_\(Aden\)), was located here.

Aden was under [British](31717.md) rule in the period from 1839 to 1967. In 1967, after years of struggle, Aden and other southern regions gained their independence. The city of Aden became the capital of [South Yemen](718749.md) between 1967 and 1990. In 1990, both South Yemen and [North Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemen_Arab_Republic) combined to form the present Republic of Yemen. Aden is now the commercial capital of the Republic of Yemen.

The archipelago of [Socotra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socotra) was part of the Governorate of Aden, but it was attached to Hadhramaut Governorate in 2004.

On 6 December 2015, the governor of Aden, General [Jaafar Mohammed Saad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaafar_Mohammed_Saad), was killed in a [car bomb](155282.md) attack that also killed six members of his entourage. Several others were wounded. After the attack, the [Islamic State](9087364.md) claimed responsibility.[^cite_note-3] On July 29, 2020, Ahmed Hamed, the STC secretary general was sent by the Yemeni president as new governor of Aden as a result of the modified version of the [Riyadh agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riyadh_Agreement) signed between the Yemeni government and the southern transitional council representatives.

## Geography

At 1,114 square kilometers, Aden is the second-smallest governorate in Yemen by area. However, its population density is the second-highest, after Sanaa Governorate.
