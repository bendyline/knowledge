**Kati** is an [urban commune](16112417.md) and the largest town in [Mali](19127.md)'s [Koulikoro Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koulikoro_Region). The town is situated 15 km northwest of [Bamako](56606.md), Mali's capital, on the [Dakar-Niger Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakar-Niger_Railway). In the 2009 census, the commune had a population of 114,983.

## History

In the early 17th century, Kati was part of a kingdom ruled by the Diara family, based in [Nyamina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyamina). Shortly after the *[kafo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kafo)* of [Bamako](56606.md) was founded, the Niare clan attacked Kati and forced the town to pay yearly tribute in cattle and [cowries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowries). Kati launched a failed rebellion against Bamako's dominance shortly before the arrival of the French in 1880, and was at that time nearly uninhabited.[^cite_note-2]

Kati was the site of Camp [Gallieni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Gallieni), where the [2nd Regiment of Senegalese Tirailleurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Regiment_of_Senegalese_Tirailleurs) was garrisoned. On 13 May 1934 a war memorial was dedicated to dead from the [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_World_War) and the conquest of [Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Sudan). After Mali became independent, the [French Armed Forces](10724.md) left Kati on 8 June 1961. The [Malian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Mali) founded a military school at the base.[^cite_note-3]

### 2026 attacks

On April 25, 2026, fighters affiliated with [Jama'at Nusrat al-Islam wal-Muslimin](53401088.md) (JNIM) and [Azawad Liberation Front](78996050.md) (FLA) launched [a series of attacks across the country](83033878.md), including on the residence of [Sadio Camara](67924702.md), the defense minister of Mali, killing him.[^cite_note-4]

## Economy



Street vendors in Kati.

Kati is the capital of the [cercle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cercles_of_Mali) of Kati. It is also a garrison city. The town has both a military hospital and a civil hospital. The town has several teaching facilities (many fundamental schools and a college). A youth club and arts centre were created with the support of the French co-operation.

Kati is a thriving market town. An important cattle market takes place every week. Kati is located on the [Dakar-Niger Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakar-Niger_Railway) and on the road [Bamako](56606.md)-[Kolokani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolokani) and Kati-[Négéla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A9g%C3%A9la?action=edit\&redlink=1)-[Kita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kita,_Mali).

## Demographics



Kati Mosque.

The population is mainly [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim), but with the presence of a Roman Catholic mission the [Roman-Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman-Catholic) community is also well established.

The population of Kati speaks primarily [Bambara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambara_language) locally called [Bamanankan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambara_language).

## Society and government

Since 2009, the mayor of Kati has been Hamalla Haidara. It is represented in the [National Assembly of Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Mali) by [Gouagnon Coulibaly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gouagnon_Coulibaly).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

It appears to have the headquarters of the 3rd Military Region of the [Military of Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Mali).

### 2004 CAOMJ Meetings

From December 27, 2004, to December 30, 2004, the *"Coordination des associations, organisations et mouvements de la jeunesse de Kati"* organinized a meeting for young people from [Mali](19127.md), [Burkina Faso](3470.md), [Guinea](12176.md), [Côte d'Ivoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%B4te_d'Ivoire), [Senegal](27278.md) and [Togo](30138.md). The previous meeting took place in December 2003 in [Bobo-Dioulasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobo-Dioulasso) in Burkina Faso. The young people had committed themselves to contribute their share to find a solution for the problems their countries are facing, like health of the reproduction of young people and teenagers, [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDS), advancing of the desert, wars, [illiteracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illiteracy) etcetera. The meeting of Kati focused on the role of young people in the reinforcement of African integration and their role in the fight against the turning of their countries into a desert.

## Climate

[Köppen-Geiger climate classification system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen-Geiger_climate_classification_system) classifies its climate as [tropical wet and dry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_wet_and_dry_climate) (Aw). The [rainy season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainy_season) takes place in mid-year, from June to September. From July to September, it is the rainiest and the daytime temperatures are the least hot and nighttime temperatures are the least cold. Its hottest months are from February to May with average maximum temperatures above 37 °C (99 °F).
