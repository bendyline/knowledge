**Till Backhaus** (born [Neuhaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amt_Neuhaus) 13 March 1959) is a German politician. As the [German Democratic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Democratic_Republic) approached [its end](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Wende) as a standalone state, Backhaus was one of those who in December 1989 (re-)founded the [Social Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_in_the_GDR), more than four decades after its suppression.[^cite_note-BiographischeDatenbankenTB-2][^cite_note-BackhauslautBackhaus-3]

More recently, in 1998 he was appointed regional Minister for Agriculture in [Mecklenburg-Vorpommern](38168.md),[^cite_note-BiographischeDatenbankenTB-2] a post he retains, making him the longest serving regional minister currently (2025) in office in Germany.

## Early life and career

Till Backhaus was born at [Neuhaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amt_Neuhaus), roughly 3 km (2 miles) to the east of [Elbe River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbe_River) which at that time marked the [Inner German border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inner_German_border) dividing [East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Germany) from [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany), and which through the 1950s was being increasingly fortified along its eastern bank in order to prevent people escaping from the east to the west. In 1978 Backhaus successfully completed his secondary schooling at nearby [Pritzier-Schwechow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pritzier) with a vocational qualification in [agro-technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_machinery).[^cite_note-BiographischeDatenbankenTB-2] He spent two years undertaking his [Military service](938728.md) with the [National People's Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_People's_Army)[^cite_note-BackhauslautBackhaus-3] before moving on, in 1980, to the [University of Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock) where he concluded his studies, in 1985, with a degree in [Agricultural engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_engineering).[^cite_note-BiographischeDatenbankenTB-2]

In 1980 he also started working for the [Neuhaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amt_Neuhaus) [Agricultural Production Co-operative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landwirtschaftliche_Produktionsgenossenschaft), later transferring to the nearby [Lübtheen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%BCbtheen) Agricultural Production Co-operative, where he worked as a department head till 1990.[^cite_note-BackhauslautBackhaus-3]

## Political career

In December 1989, the month in which even the country's monolithic SED felt the need to participate with the Social Democrats in "round table talks", Backhaus, who hitherto had not engaged in politics, joined the SDP[^cite_note-4] and was a co-founder of its [Neuhaus (Elbe)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amt_Neuhaus) district branch.[^cite_note-MVReg-5] When, after [German reunification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_reunification) in October 1990, the eastern and western branches of the [SPD](60585.md) came together into a single national party, Backhaus remained an active party leader in the locality.[^cite_note-BackhauslautBackhaus-3] Reflecting administrative boundary changes, he was district party chairman for the SPD in the [Hagenow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagenow) district from 1991 till 1994, and for the [Hagenow-Ludwigslust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwigslust_\(district\)) district from 1994 till 2003.[^cite_note-BackhauslautBackhaus-3]

At the [regional level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Germany), between 2003 and 2007[^cite_note-MVReg-5] he served as Chairman for [Mecklenburg-Vorpommern](38168.md) region SPD group and a member of the leadership team between 2005 and 2007. Reflecting his agricultural qualifications and subsequent ministerial responsibilities (see below) he has for many years been a leading spokesman for the regional party on consumer affairs, food and agriculture.



Till Backhaus (1990)

Between March and October 1990 Backhaus sat as one of the 91 [SPD](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste%20der%20Mitglieder%20der%20Volkskammer%20der%20DDR%20\(10.%20Wahlperiode\)) members of the [German Democratic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Democratic_Republic)'s first (and, as thing turned out, last) [freely elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_East_German_general_election) [National legislative Assembly (*Volkskammer*)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Chamber).[^cite_note-MVReg-5]

At the [regional level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Germany) he has since 1990 sat as a member of the [Mecklenburg-Vorpommern regional legislative assembly](17706695.md). Between 1990 and 1994 he was elected from the SPD Mecklenburg-Vorpommern regional list, and since 1994 he has represented the "Ludwigslust I" subdivision electoral district within the Mecklenburg-Vorpommernregion).[^cite_note-BackhauslautBackhaus-3] Within the assembly, between 1992 and 1998 he was chairman of the Agriculture Committee, and from 1994 till 1998 of the Agriculture and Nature Protection committee.[^cite_note-MVReg-5]

At the local level he was, between 2004 and 2006, a member of the [local council](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kreistag) for the [Ludwigslust district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwigslust_\(district\)).[^cite_note-BiographischeDatenbankenTB-2]

### State Minister of Agriculture, 1998–present

After the 1998 regional election Backhaus was offered the position of State Minister for Food, Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries for [Mecklenburg-Vorpommern](38168.md) in the regional administration headed up, at that time, by his party colleague [Harald Ringstorff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Ringstorff). Backhaus accepted the post, and retained it (now with Consumer protection and environmental protection added to the portfolio) it after 7 November 2006[^cite_note-BackhauslautBackhaus-3] when, controversially, the Mecklenburg-Vorpommern SPD entered into a ruling coalition with the [CDU (party)](60584.md), following regional election results that had been disappointing for both parties.

As one of the state’s representatives at the [Bundesrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundesrat_of_Germany), Backhaus is a member of the Committee on Agricultural Policy and Consumer Protection and of the Committee on Environment, Nature Conservation and Nuclear Safety.

Together with [Elke Ferner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elke_Ferner), [Wolfgang Jüttner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_J%C3%BCttner), [Ute Vogt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ute_Vogt) and [Klaus Wowereit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klaus_Wowereit), Backhaus co-chaired the SPD’s 2006 national convention in Berlin.[^cite_note-6]

## *The long word*

[Mecklenburg-Vorpommern](38168.md) is a relatively rural state and the Agriculture ministry has given Backhaus a high ministerial profile in the region. In 1999 his department came up with a legislative proposal which broke records for an unusual reason.

The [Rindfleischetikettierungsüberwachungsaufgabenübertragungsgesetz (a law on the supervision of beef labelling)](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rindfleischetikettierungs%C3%BCberwachungsaufgaben%C3%BCbertragungsgesetz) is defined using a grammatically valid and usable [compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_\(linguistics\)) [noun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noun) of 63 letters.[^cite_note-9] The word featured in the *[Guinness Book of Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_Book_of_Records)* until 1996 when it was controversially challenged for the top spot by the creation of a longer word.[^cite_note-10] But for many the word created by Backhaus's ministry was toppled from its position as Germany's longest official word only in 2013 when it was withdrawn from use by the regional assembly because the law it defined had been repealed.[^cite_note-spiegelundRindfleich-11][^cite_note-Telegraphbeeflaw-12]

## Controversies

In 2015, as the minister responsible, Backhaus was called upon to make a ministerial statement after the (presumably temporary) closure of [Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostock) [zoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoo) in the wake of a [bird flu](442916.md) scare which had necessitated the precautionary killing of [storks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storks), [ducks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ducks) and a [red ibis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibis).[^cite_note-Spiegelbirdflu2015-13] Nor was this the first bird flu scare in the region to necessitate ministerial intervention.[^cite_note-Spiegelbirdflu2005-14] In the preceding fifteen years his intervention has frequently been invoked in other food, agriculture and environment related scares.

A more personal media controversy arose in respect of the [Doctorate in Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) which Backhaus received from [Berlin's Humboldt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) in 2001.[^cite_note-Spiegel452003-15] His dissertation was entitled "Reflections on Grain Production in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern between 1900 and 2000".[^cite_note-16] Sources are silent over when the work was started, but public reaction may have been triggered by the fact that by the time he actually received the doctorate Backhaus had already been the regional Minister for Agriculture for more than two years. The Agriculture Scientist [Wilhelm Römer](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm%20R%C3%B6mer%20\(Agronom\)) went public with his opinion that the dissertation was an "insubstantial compilation" without "formal scientific substance" (*"substanzlos\[es] Machwerk ... \[der] ... weder inhaltlich noch formal wissenschaftlichen Ansprüchen \[entspräche]"*). There was also criticism that the dissertation had been supervised in 2000 and 2001 by [Norbert Makowski](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norbert%20Makowski), a distinguished plant-scientist who at the time was alleged to have been undertaking paid consultancy work indirectly involving the ministry.[^cite_note-spiegel322003-17][^cite_note-focus162003-18]

## Personal life

Till Backhaus has three children from two of his three marriages.[^cite_note-superilluTBIM-19] He married his third wife, Ivonne Menzel, in June 2012. Press attention focused not on her career as a [dentist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dentist),[^cite_note-weltTBIM-20] but on her youth and her recent celebrity as a [beauty queen](95198.md).[^cite_note-spiegelTBIM)-21]
