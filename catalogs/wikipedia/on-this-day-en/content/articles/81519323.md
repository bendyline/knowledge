**Kenta Suzuki** ([Japanese](15606.md): 鈴木健太, [Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepburn_romanization): *Suzuki Kenta*; born 24 August 1975)[^cite_note-jpn1-1] is a Japanese politician currently serving as [Governor](24009688.md) of [Akita Prefecture](61729.md) as an [independent politician](2172378.md).[^cite_note-akitapref-2]

He previously served as a member of the Akita Prefectural Assembly as the 73rd Vice-Speaker and was a member of the [Japan Ground Self-Defense Force](2978818.md).[^cite_note-akitapref-2]

## Biography

Suzuki was born in [Osaka Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_Prefecture)[^cite_note-akitapref-2] and grew up in [Kobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe), [Hyōgo Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hy%C5%8Dgo_Prefecture)[^cite_note-nikkei-3][^cite_note-jpn1-1] His family ran a newspaper shop,[^cite_note-shimpo2-4] and he played [baseball](3850.md) from [elementary school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_schools_in_Japan) through [high school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_education_in_Japan).[^cite_note-shimpo2-4] While retaking his university entrance exams, he survived the [Kobe earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_earthquake), aiding in the rescue of elderly people from the rubble.[^cite_note-shimpo2-4]

He graduated from the [Faculty of Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_School_of_Law_and_Faculty_of_Law,_Kyoto_University) at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) in 2000 and joined the [Japan Ground Self-Defense Force](2978818.md) (JGSDF).[^cite_note-nikkei-3] Suzuki later attended the Officer Candidate School and was assigned to the 16th Infantry Regiment. He was also deployed to the [United Nations Peacekeeping Operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Peacekeeping_Operations) in [Timor-Leste](19159399.md) and [Iraq](7515928.md).[^cite_note-nippon1-5]

In 2006, Suzuki retired from the JGSDF and moved to [Akita City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akita_\(city\)), the hometown of his wife, whom he met at the Officer Candidate School.[^cite_note-sankei-6] Subsequently, he studied at the prefectural library and obtained a judicial scrivener's licence.[^cite_note-shimpo2-4] In 2015, Suzuki ran for the Akita City constituency in the Akita Prefectural Assembly election as a member of the [conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatism_in_Japan) [Liberal Democratic Party](164231.md),[^cite_note-sankei-6] and won two of his three political races.[^cite_note-sankei-6]

In February 2021, it was reported that he was considering running in the Akita Prefectural Gubernatorial election,[^cite_note-sankei-6] but he declined to run in a later statement.[^cite_note-jpn3-7]

He also served as Vice-Speaker of the Prefectural Assembly between 2023 and 2024.[^cite_note-nikkei-3][^cite_note-8]

In November 2024, Suzuki announced his candidacy for the 2025 Akita Prefectural Gubernatorial election upon the expiration of his term at the prefectural assembly.[^cite_note-9] He resigned from his seat after winning the election, and left the Liberal Democratic Party in January 2025.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

In the election, he defeated former Akita Vice Governor Kazuzo Saruta and others,[^cite_note-jpn1-1] winning his first election for Governor of Akita.[^cite_note-sankei-6]
