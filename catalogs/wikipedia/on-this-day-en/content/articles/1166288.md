**Crowne Plaza by IHG** is a multinational chain of full service, upscale [hotels](14276.md) headquartered in the [United Kingdom](31717.md). It caters to the business, leisure and blended travel market usually located in city centers, resorts, coastal towns or near major airports. The brand is part of [IHG Hotels & Resorts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHG_Hotels_&_Resorts) family of brands, which include [InterContinental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InterContinental) Hotels & Resorts and [Holiday Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holiday_Inn) Hotels & Resorts, and operates in nearly 100 countries with more than 400 hotels and 110,000 bedrooms.[^cite_note-1]

## History



Crowne Plaza at [Singapore Changi Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Changi_Airport) voted "World's Best Airport Hotel" for seven times by [Skytrax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skytrax).

Holiday Inn Crowne Plaza was established in 1983 as a breakout brand, the upscale sister brand to [Holiday Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holiday_Inn).[^cite_note-2] The first Holiday Inn Crowne Plaza hotel opened that year in [Rockville, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockville,_Maryland).[^cite_note-\:0-3] In 1988, Bass PLC (today [InterContinental Hotels Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InterContinental_Hotels_Group)) purchased Holiday Corporation and in 1994 the brand was renamed Crowne Plaza Hotels.[^cite_note-\:0-3] In 1999, Crowne Plaza opened its first European property, in [Madeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeira), [Portugal](23033.md).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Notable properties

- The Crowne Plaza [Changi Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changi_Airport), located within [Singapore](27318.md)'s [Changi Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changi_Airport), was voted the world's best airport hotel for seven times (2020-2025, and 2022 to 2024) by [Skytrax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skytrax).

  [^cite_note-4]

- The [Crowne Plaza Liverpool John Lennon Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowne_Plaza_Liverpool_John_Lennon_Airport_Hotel) is the former terminal building of [Liverpool Speke Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_John_Lennon_Airport), constructed in the 1930s and used until 1986. Its notable [art deco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_deco) features led to its listing as a heritage building, and subsequent conversion to a hotel.

- The [Crowne Plaza Copenhagen Towers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowne_Plaza_Copenhagen_Towers) is the first hotel in Denmark to generate all of its power from renewable sources, including [solar panels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_panel) and [geothermal heating and cooling pumps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geothermal_heat_pump). Its [stationary bicycles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stationary_bicycle) are connected to generators, which provide electricity to the hotel.
