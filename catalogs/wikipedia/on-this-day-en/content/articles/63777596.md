**Shaun Edward Byneveldt** is a South African diplomat and politician who is the current South African ambassador to the [State of Palestine](241405.md). Previously, he served as the South African Ambassador to Syria from 2010 to 2018. He was the Speaker of the [Western Cape Provincial Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Cape_Provincial_Parliament) from 2004 to 2009. Byneveldt is a member of the [African National Congress](2503.md) (ANC).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

On 30 January 2026, Byneveldt was declared *[persona non grata](521241.md)* and given 72 hours to leave the country by the [Israeli government](74576.md), in retaliation for the South African [Department of International Relations and Cooperation](14036979.md) declaring Israel’s [Chargé d'affaires](856252.md) to South Africa, Ariel Shiedman, *persona non grata*.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Despite Byneveldt being the ambassador to the [State of Palestine](241405.md), his accreditation required coordination via Israel due to the latter's [ongoing occupation of the West Bank](59076253.md).[^cite_note-5]
