**Maasim**, officially the **Municipality of Maasim** ([Cebuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_language): *Lungsod sa Maasim*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Maasim*; [Maguindanaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanaon_language): *Inged nu Maasim*, [Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_Alphabet): ايڠد نو ماسم), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](165100.md) of [Sarangani](297388.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to 2024 census, it had a population of 67,907 people.

## History

**Maasim** was first created as a municipality of [the then-undivided Cotabato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotabato_\(historical_province\)) through Executive Order No. 113, signed by President [Diosdado Macapagal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diosdado_Macapagal) on October 1, 1964, consisting of 20 *[barrios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrio)* of [Kiamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiamba,_Sarangani) and 2 *[sitios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitio)* of then-municipality of [General Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Santos); with [the seat of government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poblacion) being designated at Barrio Maasim.[^cite_note-5] However, its creation, among others, was declared [void](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Void_\(law\)) *[ab initio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ab_initio)* by the [Supreme Court](1335534.md) in its decision dated December 24, 1965.[^cite_note-6]

It was eventually recreated through Republic Act No. 5866, enacted without executive approval on June 21, 1969, which constituted the same entities. At that time, both Kiamba and General Santos (having been converted into [a city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_the_Philippines)) had been included in the separate province of [South Cotabato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Cotabato).[^cite_note-7] Maasim, Kiamba, and five other municipalities of South Cotabato ultimately became part of [Sarangani](297388.md) upon its establishment in 1992.[^cite_note-8]

On September 9, 2026, several parcels of land at Barangay Malbang, with a total area of 142,569 square meters, was designated as a Special Economic Zones, to be known as Malbang Industrial Ecozone, through Proclamation No. 1428.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

The economy of Maasim is largely based on agriculture with a high level production of [copra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copra) (dried coconut meat). Animal husbandry is the second biggest income earner, notably cattle farming. Other agricultural products are [coconuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coconut), [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [sugarcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugarcane), [bananas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana), [pineapples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pineapple), [mangoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango), [eggs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg_\(food\)), [beef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beef), and [fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish).

The economy has accelerated in the past decade, driven by advances in global communication technology and the finishing of a modern highway that greatly improved trade and transport.

### Kamanga Power Plant

KPP announced on June 3, 2008, that Alcantara-controlled Conal Holdings Co. would build in mid-2009 the \$450 million 200-megawatt clean [coal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal) Kamanga Power Plant, in Maasim, [Saranggani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saranggani). The plant was expected to supply part of [Mindanao](20546.md)'s baseload power requirements when it is operational in 2012.[^cite_note-26]
