**Field marshal** (**FM**)[^cite_note-1] is the highest rank in the [Pakistan Army](1965925.md) awarded by the [government of Pakistan](1897833.md) to officers of the Pakistan Army in recognition of distinguished service. Although it is a current and authorized rank with a [pay grade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pay_grade) of "Apex Scale,"[^cite_note-2] it has been conferred only thrice in history—on [Ayub Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayub_Khan) in 1959,[^cite_note-4] [King Mahendra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahendra_of_Nepal) of [Nepal](171166.md) on 12 May 1963 (as an honorary conferment),[^cite_note-\:2-5] and [Asim Munir](58712552.md) in 2025. It is an honorary rank and comes with no additional powers and pay grade. It is equivalent to [admiral of the fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiral_of_the_fleet) in the [Pakistan Navy](1183789.md) and [marshal of the air force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshal_of_the_air_force) in the [Pakistan Air Force](377433.md), and while it is an ordinarily senior rank in the Pakistan Army, it can be referred to as a five-star general "standard rank scale" to distinguish it from other military insignias.[^cite_note-6]

## History

Since gaining independence in 1947, Pakistan has awarded the rank of Field Marshal only three times — first to [Ayub Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayub_Khan) in October 1959.[^cite_note-7]

The second instance was the honorary conferment of the rank upon King [Mahendra of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahendra_of_Nepal) by Field Marshal and President [Mohammad Ayub Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayub_Khan), on 12 May 1963.[^cite_note-\:2-5]

The third instance occurred in May 2025, when [Asim Munir](58712552.md), the [Chief of the Army Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Army_Staff_\(Pakistan\)), was promoted to the rank of Field Marshal for his contributions in the [2025 India–Pakistan conflict](79896622.md)[^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-\:1-9] when Pakistan, under Munir's leadership, retaliated against India, shooting down multiple Indian jets.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

## Appointment

The appointment of field marshal is made through an [appeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appeal) submitted for review request by the [Prime Minister of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Pakistan) in a joint effort with the [President](419460.md) and [Defence Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(Pakistan\)) to the [Supreme Court](843857.md) or high court for [constitutional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Pakistan) orders under certain rules and regulations, although the president exercises the power of the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) and prime minister leads the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Pakistan).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]
