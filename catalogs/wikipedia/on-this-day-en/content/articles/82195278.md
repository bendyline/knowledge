The **2026 Iowa Hawkeyes football team** represents the [University of Iowa](241119.md) as a member of the [Big Ten Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Ten_Conference) during the [2026 NCAA Division I FBS football season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NCAA_Division_I_FBS_football_season). The Hawkeyes are led by [Kirk Ferentz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirk_Ferentz) who is in his 28th year as head coach. Iowa plays their home games at [Kinnick Stadium](1873811.md) located in [Iowa City, Iowa](113933.md).

Their Week 5 home loss to [Ohio State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Ohio_State_Buckeyes_football_team) marked the end of a 66-game streak going back to [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Iowa_Hawkeyes_football_team) in which Iowa prevented their opponents from scoring 30 points or more.

### vs Northern Illinois

See also: [2026 Northern Illinois Huskies football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Northern_Illinois_Huskies_football_team)

Over the years, Iowa has played some tightly contested games with the Huskies, but that wasn't the case in this one. The Hawkeyes, powered by 132 rushing yards and a pair of touchdowns from [Kamari Moulton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamari_Moulton), jumped out to a three touchdown lead in the first quarter. In the second half, Evan James returned a punt 77 yards for a touchdown to put the game well out of reach. Moulton finished the day with a career-high 183 yards on the ground. Defensively, Iowa limited Northern Illinois to 120 yards (7 yards rushing) and has shut out at least one opponent in each of the last five seasons.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

### vs Iowa State (Cy–Hawk Trophy)

See also: [2026 Iowa State Cyclones football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iowa_State_Cyclones_football_team) and [Iowa–Iowa State football rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa%E2%80%93Iowa_State_football_rivalry)

After dropping three of the last four in this in-state series, the Hawkeyes leaned heavily on defense and the kicking game to regain the Cy-Hawk Trophy. Caden Buhr kicked a 42-yard field goal with 15 seconds remaining to give Iowa its first lead of the game and secure the victory. This was the fifth consecutive matchup between the teams decided by a single possession.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
