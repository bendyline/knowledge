# List of mass shootings in South Africa

&#x20;  This is a list of [mass shootings](31883778.md) that have occurred in [South Africa](17416221.md). A mass shooting has various definitions, but is defined by [South African police](1228194.md) as an incident in which three or more people are shot with a firearm.[^cite_note-Lamb2022-1]

## Dynamics

Main article: [Crime in South Africa](6551455.md)

South Africa has one of the highest murder rates in the world, at 45 people per 100,000 according to 2023–24 figures from the [United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Office_on_Drugs_and_Crime). In 2024 alone, South Africa recorded more than 26,000 homicides—an average of more than 70 a day. Firearms are the leading cause of death in homicides. 63 people were killed every day between April and September 2024, according to police data.[^cite_note-BBC25-2]

Mass shootings in South Africa are mostly perpetrated by [gangs](80408.md), and are usually motivated by competition for turf and resources, as well as [illegal mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zama_zama).[^cite_note-Lamb2022-1][^cite_note-Bhengu2022-3] Another cause are the long-standing conflicts between [minibus taxi associations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxi_wars_in_South_Africa) in the country.[^cite_note-Lamb2022-1] They often result in revenge shootings.[^cite_note-Lamb2022-1][^cite_note-Bhengu2022-3] [Taverns](272204.md) are often targeted.[^cite_note-Bhengu2022-3][^cite_note-Nyathi2023-4] Several fatal mass shootings have also occurred in bars in recent years,[^cite_note-CBS-5] with a majority taking place in [shebeens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shebeens), or illegal [bars](272207.md). In a major crackdown between April and September 2025, police shut down 12,000 such outlets and arrested 18,000 people.[^cite_note-BBC25-2]

Shootings, often relating to organized crime, are said to be fueled by alcohol sold at shebeens.[^cite_note-6] Two of the most violent recent mass shootings; the [Soweto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Soweto_shooting) and [Pietermaritzburg shootings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Pietermaritzburg_shooting), which combined killed 20 people and injured 15 people, occurred at shebeens. Mass shootings in the country are not just confined to shebeens however, with [two related mass shootings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Lusikisiki_shootings) in the town of [Lusikisiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusikisiki), Eastern Cape, killing 24 people and injuring 9.[^cite_note-7]

A lack of essential police resources has been discussed as a cause of the slowed response to mass murders in South Africa, and a lack of detectives preventing proactive analysis of crimes.[^cite_note-Bhengu2022-3][^cite_note-Nyathi2023-4] A disconnect between policing and the communities they service has been pointed out as a possible influencing factor,[^cite_note-Bhengu2022-3] as has the rate of certified firearm theft.[^cite_note-Nyathi2023-4]
