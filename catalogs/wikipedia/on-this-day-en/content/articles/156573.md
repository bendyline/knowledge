**USS *George H. W. Bush* (CVN-77)** is the tenth and final [*Nimitz*-class](181160.md) [supercarrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercarrier) of the [United States Navy](20518076.md). She is named for the 41st [president of the United States](24113.md) and 11th [director of central intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Director_of_central_intelligence) [George H. W. Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_H._W._Bush), who was a [naval aviator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_aviator) during [World War II](32927.md). The vessel's [callsign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callsign) is *Avenger*, named after the [TBF Avenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_TBF_Avenger) [aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixed-wing_aircraft) flown by then-Lieutenant Bush in World War II. Construction began in 2003 at [Northrop Grumman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman), in [Newport News, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport_News,_Virginia), and was completed in 2009 at a cost of \$6.2 billion. Her home port is [Naval Station Norfolk](896196.md), Virginia.

## Naming



[George H. W. Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_H._W._Bush) in a [Grumman TBM Avenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_TBM_Avenger) during [World War II](32927.md)

[George H. W. Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_H._W._Bush) became one of the U.S. Navy's youngest pilots when he received his Naval Aviator wings and naval commission on 9 June 1943, three days before turning 19.[^cite_note-navy-10] He flew torpedo bombers off [USS *San Jacinto*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_San_Jacinto_\(CVL-30\)) on active duty from August 1943 to September 1945 during World War II. On 2 September 1944, during a mission over the Pacific, Japanese anti-aircraft fire hit his plane. The Navy submarine [USS *Finback*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Finback_\(SS-230\)) rescued him. He was awarded the [Distinguished Flying Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_Flying_Cross_\(United_States\)) and three [Air Medals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Medal) for courageous service in the [Pacific Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_War).

USS *George H. W. Bush* is the second United States [aircraft carrier](2219.md) to be named after a naval aviator ([*Forrestal*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Forrestal) was the first) and the second, following [*Ronald Reagan*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Ronald_Reagan), to be named after a then living former president (*Ronald Reagan* was christened in 2001 while Reagan was still alive).

## Ship's seal

Each element of the seal is significant for its relevance to the ship's namesake, naval aviation, naval service, and the nation. There are six prominent features of the seal, beginning with the 41 white stars, symbolizing the ship's namesake (the 41st president). The rays of light that appear on the seal's horizon represent Bush's concept of a "[thousand points of light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thousand_points_of_light)", wherein he urged Americans to find meaning and reward by serving a purpose higher than themselves. The graphic depiction of the aircraft carrier reflects the carrier, as both a symbol and instrument of American strength as a force for freedom. Above the carrier are the overhead profiles of a [TBM Avenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_TBF_Avenger) torpedo bomber (representing Bush's days as a Navy pilot), an [F/A-18 Hornet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F/A-18_Hornet) strike fighter, and an [F-35C Lightning II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F-35C_Lightning_II), superimposed one upon the other in reverse chronological order of the individual aircraft's service entry date, and in diminishing scale so each outline is contained within that of the newer aircraft.[^cite_note-up-www01.ffc.navy.mil-11]

Fouled anchors and shields, centered on naval aviators wings, honor the ship's namesake's aviation history. Finally, the motto "Freedom at Work" is adapted from Bush's inaugural speech, during which he said, "We know what works: Freedom works. We know what's right: Freedom is right."[^cite_note-up-www01.ffc.navy.mil-11]

## Description

*George H. W. Bush* measures 1,092 feet (333 m) and displaces over 100,000 tons, making her one of the world's largest warships (though she is slightly shorter than [USS *Enterprise*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Enterprise_\(CVN-65\))). Her top speed exceeds 30 knots (56 km/h; 35 mph); powered with two nuclear reactors, she can operate for more than 20 years without refueling.

Several features differentiate CVN-77 from other ships in the *Nimitz* class.[^cite_note-Design_Enhancements-12]

### Hull

New features include a [bulbous bow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulbous_bow) design that provides more buoyancy to the forward end of the ship and improves hull efficiency, curved [flight deck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_deck) edges to reduce radar signature, a new underwater hull coating system, deck modernized coverings to reduce ship weight by 100 tons, low solar absorptive and anti-stain paint, a less cluttered [hangar](1011776.md) bay, and a new [propeller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propeller) design.[^cite_note-13]

### Island



Placement of the 700-ton island onto the ship's flight deck in 2006

*George H. W. Bush* is the second carrier to have a modernized island, which includes a new radar tower (enclosed to reduce radar signature), navigation system upgrades, communication systems enhancements, and armored windows. The island is smaller and has been repositioned further aft to improve flight deck access and reduce signature and electronic self-interference.[^cite_note-GS1-14]

### Air operations

New air operations design features include an updated aviation fuel storage and distribution system, semi-automated refueling and servicing with new deck locations to provide faster, more efficient aircraft pit stops, requiring fewer people, modernized aircraft launch, and recovery equipment, and redesigned [jet blast deflectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_blast_deflector).[^cite_note-15]

### Environmental

Environmental upgrades have also been designed into the ship, including a vacuum collection/marine sanitation device (VC/MSD), a new marine sewage system that uses sea water in lieu of fresh water for lower maintenance costs. Many older ships in the U.S. Navy utilize a gravity-driven collection holding and transfer (CHT) system to handle sewage waste. Newer U.S. Navy ships, including now CVN-77, collect sewage waste by vacuum, allowing for greater flexibility in piping installation, smaller pipe sizes overall and reducing water consumption. The collection tanks of *George H. W. Bush* were modified to accommodate both the VCHT (Vacuum CHT) equipment and the elements of a marine sanitization device to treat the waste prior to discharge. *George H. W. Bush* is the only aircraft carrier in the U.S. Navy to combine the two technologies.

This new VC/MSD driven waste management system has, however, not been without problems. Reports began surfacing immediately after delivery in May 2009 of issues with the ship's toilet system. As of November 2011, the entire system has gone down at least twice, rendering all 423 commodes in the ship's 130 heads inoperable, with many more incidents that have rendered either half of the ship, or sections of the ship, without operating sanitary facilities. In one ship-wide incident, a repair crew spent 35 non-stop hours attempting to return the system to working order. The system is said to suffer breakdowns when inappropriate materials such as feminine hygiene products are flushed down the toilets.[^cite_note-16] During a four-month maintenance period in the dock in 2012, anti-clog measures were installed in the ship's toilet disposal systems.[^cite_note-17]

### Electronics and communications

New electronics and communications technology, space rearrangement, operational procedure changes, advanced sensor technologies and maintenance systems have been incorporated to reduce manning costs. A new zonal electrical distribution system will keep problems from affecting other parts of the ship. Automated material movement devices, semi-autonomous, gravity compensated weapons handling devices, damage control automation systems and components have also been installed. Medical and dental equipment have been upgraded, integrated display screens in Damage Control Central have been modernized to improve data integration and display, and equipment in general shops has been modernized to improve productivity.[^cite_note-GS1-14]

## History



Pentagon naming ceremony in December 2002

The contract to build CVN-77 was awarded to [Northrop Grumman Shipbuilding Newport News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_Shipbuilding_Newport_News) on 26 January 2001. A naming ceremony was held on 9 December 2002 at [Northrop Grumman Newport News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_Newport_News), with former president George H. W. Bush attending.[^cite_note-NavSource-18][^cite_note-19] [Secretary of the Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_of_the_Navy) [Gordon R. England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_R._England), presided at the ceremony.

### Construction

Construction took place at the [Northrop Grumman Newport News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_Newport_News) shipyard's [Dry Dock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drydock#) 12, the largest in the western hemisphere.[^cite_note-NavSource-18][^cite_note-NavTech1-20] The [keel laying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel_laying) ceremony was held on 6 September 2003,[^cite_note-NavSource-18] with former president George H. W. Bush serving as the keynote speaker. Former First Lady [Barbara Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Bush) also attended with their daughter, Dorothy Bush Koch, the ship's sponsor. The former president authenticated the keel by chalking his initials onto a metal plate. His initials were then welded onto the plate, which was permanently affixed to the ship.



USS *George H. W. Bush* shortly after being released from dry dock for the first time

The ship was modularly constructed, where large sections are assembled and then lifted into place using a large crane. Major milestones in the construction include the bow placement in March 2005, followed by the island placement on 8 July 2006.[^cite_note-NavSource-18] The 700-short-ton (640 t; 620-long-ton) island was lifted onto the flight deck in a ceremony called "[stepping the mast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mast_stepping)" which dates from antiquity and consists of placing coins or other items of significance under the step or bottom of a ship's mast during construction. Since at least the construction of [USS *Constitution*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Constitution) in the 1790s, this tradition has been passed on as a symbol of good luck for U.S. Navy ships. George H. W. Bush participated in the event, placing his naval aviator wings underneath the island during the ceremony.[^cite_note-21]

*George H. W. Bush* was [christened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_naming_and_launching) on 7 October 2006.[^cite_note-NavyNews12182008-1] Former president George H. W. Bush attended the ceremony and became the first president in history to participate in the christening of his namesake ship. President [George W. Bush](3414021.md) also attended and honored his father during the ceremony as a special guest speaker. Other officials participating in the ceremony included [Secretary of Defense](43998.md) [Donald Rumsfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Rumsfeld), Secretary of the Navy [Donald Winter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Winter); Virginia Senators [John Warner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Warner) and [George Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Allen_\(U.S._politician\)), Virginia Governor [Tim Kaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Kaine), and [Chief of Naval Operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Naval_Operations) Admiral [Michael Mullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Mullen).[^cite_note-22]

Other construction milestones included catapult system testing on the ship's flight deck on 25 January 2008. Former president George H. W. Bush signaled the launch of two "dead loads" off the deck of the carrier. Dead loads are large, wheeled, steel vessels weighing up to 80,000 pounds (36,000 kg) simulating the weight of actual aircraft.[^cite_note-23]

On 11 August 2008, the [Pre-Commissioning Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-Commissioning_Unit) (PCU) crew moved aboard the ship, the first meals were served in the galley, the U.S. flag was raised on the fantail for the first time, and the first watches were set.[^cite_note-24] *George H. W. Bush* left Northrop Grumman Ship Building for the first time on 23 December 2008, proceeding a few miles down river to [Norfolk Naval Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk_Naval_Station).

### Commissioning



Guests and U.S. Navy personnel at the commissioning ceremony on 10 January 2009



George H. W. and Barbara Bush depart the ship following the commissioning ceremony

*George H. W. Bush* was commissioned 10 January 2009 at Norfolk Naval Station[^cite_note-NewCarrier-25] prior to her official delivery to the Navy, in a ceremony attended by approximately 15,000 people. Veterans of [USS *San Jacinto*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_San_Jacinto_\(CVL-30\)), the ship George H. W. Bush served on during World War II, were also present. President George W. Bush delivered the principal address, George H. W. Bush set the first watch, and ship's sponsor Dorothy "Doro" Bush Koch gave the order to "man our ship and bring her to life!" A [GM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Motors)-built [Grumman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman) [TBM Avenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_TBF_Avenger) like the one then-Lieutenant junior grade George Bush flew in World War II performed a fly-over.

Northrop Grumman Corporation Builder's sea trials were completed on 16 February 2009, providing an opportunity to test systems, components and compartments at sea for the first time. The trials included high-speed runs and a demonstration of the carrier's other capabilities.[^cite_note-26] Following builder's trials, the ship underwent acceptance trials on 10 April 2009,[^cite_note-27] conducted by representatives of the U.S. Navy Board of Inspection and Survey, to test and evaluate the ship's systems and performance.

### Delivery and shakedown

*George H. W. Bush* was officially delivered to the Navy on 11 May 2009.[^cite_note-28]

The first fixed-wing flights were conducted on 19 May 2009 when [F/A-18 Super Hornets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F/A-18_Super_Hornet) from [Air Test and Evaluation Squadron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VX-23) at [Naval Air Station Patuxent River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Air_Station_Patuxent_River), Maryland began [flight deck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_deck) certification, which tests a carrier's ability to conduct [air operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_US_Navy_carrier_air_operations).[^cite_note-29]

On 26 May 2009, former president George H. W. Bush and his daughter, Dorothy Bush Koch, flew aboard the carrier to observe flight operations during the ship's underway period in the Atlantic Ocean. USS *George H. W. Bush* successfully completed her first flight deck certification on that day.[^cite_note-30]

*George H. W. Bush* returned to Northrop Grumman Newport News shipyard on 18 June 2009 for post-delivery maintenance work, also known as the ship's post shakedown availability (PSA). A PSA is a typical availability in the early life of a carrier that allows the Navy and builder to resolve any items that came up during trials and delivery and make any last-minute changes and upgrades. Work includes the installation of a [Rigid Hull Inflatable Boat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigid_Hull_Inflatable_Boat) (RHIB) handling system and a new fresh water purification system. Other changes include compartment reconfigurations, combat system and radar equipment upgrades, and minor repairs. The work was scheduled to last through early 2010.[^cite_note-31]

### Maiden 2011 deployment



*George H. W. Bush* (top) conducts an ordnance transfer with [*Harry S Truman*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Harry_S._Truman) off the East Coast in 2011

The ship was assigned to [Carrier Strike Group Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_Strike_Group_Two) for her first deployment. Under the command of Rear Admiral [Nora Tyson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nora_Tyson), *George H. W. Bush*, [Carrier Air Wing Eight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_Air_Wing_Eight) and the four ships of her group departed on her first deployment on 11 May 2011.[^cite_note-32] They sailed across the Atlantic to Britain to participate in [Exercise Saxon Warrior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exercise_Saxon_Warrior?action=edit\&redlink=1), held in the [Western Approaches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Approaches) and culminating in a so-called '[Thursday War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thursday_War)'.[^cite_note-navynews.co.uk-33] She then moved towards [Portsmouth](24207.md), United Kingdom, on 27 May, anchoring adjacent to [Stokes Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stokes_Bay) through 31 May, because she was too large to enter the harbor, and the naval base did not have sufficient nuclear berths for the carrier to moor alongside.[^cite_note-navynews.co.uk-33][^cite_note-34] The carrier arrived at Naples, Italy on 10 June 2011.[^cite_note-35]

The carrier returned to Norfolk on 10 December 2011, following a seven-month deployment supporting operations with the U.S. Navy's 5th and 6th fleets.[^cite_note-36]

### 2012



*George H. W. Bush* undertakes carrier qualifications in the Atlantic in May 2013; an [F/A-18F Super Hornet](845012.md) is on the catapult; another F/A-18F and an [X-47B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_X-47B) are parked forward of the island superstructure; two more F/A-18Fs and four [C-2 Greyhounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_C-2_Greyhound) are parked aft.

On 25 July 2012, *George H. W. Bush* began her four-month overhaul at [Norfolk Naval Shipyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk_Naval_Shipyard) at [Portsmouth, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portsmouth,_Virginia), included scheduled short-term technical upgrades.[^cite_note-37] On 1 December 2012, *George H. W. Bush* completed her PIA maintenance cycle and began sea trials on 3 December 2012. After completing sea trials on 4 December 2012, the carrier started her training and qualification cycle in preparation for the group's 2013 deployment.[^cite_note-38]

### 2013

During a two-week underway period beginning 14 January 2013, *George H. W. Bush* tested the [MV-22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV-22) tilt-rotor aircraft from squadron [VMX-22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VMX-22) as a potential [carrier onboard delivery aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_onboard_delivery) as well as operating mine-sweeping [MH-53E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MH-53E) helicopters from squadron [HM-14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HM-14).[^cite_note-39]



An [X-47B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_X-47B) is catapulted from *George H. W. Bush* in May 2013, the first time a UCAV had been launched from an aircraft carrier at sea, during carrier qualifications in the Atlantic

During another underway period, *George H. W. Bush* conducted at-sea tests for [X-47B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-47B) unmanned drone in the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md), including the first time that an unmanned drone has been [catapulted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steam_catapult#Steam_catapult) off an aircraft carrier on the morning of 14 May 2013 (*pictured*).[^cite_note-40] On 17 May 2013, another first was achieved when the X-47B performed [touch-and-go landings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touch-and-go_landing) and take-offs on the flight deck of *Bush* while underway in the Atlantic Ocean.[^cite_note-41] Also during this two-week underway period, the aircraft carrier tested a new torpedo self-defense system, as well as completed more than 115 launches and landings in assessing a new precision landing system, before returning to Norfolk on 24 May 2013.[^cite_note-42]

On 10 July 2013, an unmanned X-47B drone completed an arrested landing on the flight deck of *George H. W. Bush*. The landing marks the first time any unmanned aircraft had completed an arrested landing on board an aircraft carrier operating at sea.[^cite_note-43] The drone completed a second successful arrested landing on *George H. W. Bush*, but it was diverted to the [Wallops Flight Facility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallops_Flight_Facility) in Virginia after an issue was detected, requiring that a planned third landing to be aborted.[^cite_note-44] One of the drone's three navigational sub-systems failed, which was identified by the other two sub-systems. The anomaly was indicated to the mission operator, who followed test plan procedures to abort the landing. The Navy stated that the aircraft's detection of a problem demonstrated its reliability and ability to operate autonomously.[^cite_note-45] On 15 July 2013, in a fourth attempt, an X-47B drone failed to make a successful flight deck landing on board the vessel due to "technical issues."[^cite_note-46]

### 2014 deployment



*George H.W. Bush* transits the [Straits of Gibraltar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straits_of_Gibraltar) in February 2014

In late February 2014, *George H. W. Bush* transited the [Strait of Gibraltar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Gibraltar) on the way to a stop in [Piraeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus), Greece, for a scheduled port visit.[^cite_note-47]

On 5 March 2014, *George H. W. Bush* arrived off southern Turkey, which is under 500 miles away from [Crimea](163045.md), amid developing tensions over [Ukraine](31750.md) with [Russia](25391.md).[^cite_note-48] On 9 March 2014, the carrier entered port in [Antalya](361527.md), in southern [Turkey](11125639.md).[^cite_note-49] Some news sources had speculated that the ship's stay in the Mediterranean Sea would be extended as a result of the [annexation of Crimea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Crimea_by_the_Russian_Federation), but this proved to not be the case with [Carrier Strike Group 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_Strike_Group_2) proceeding through the [Suez Canal](29323.md).[^cite_note-50]



[*Carl Vinson*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Carl_Vinson) (foreground) relieves *George H. W. Bush* as the 5th Fleet on station carrier in October 2014.

The carrier was transiting the Suez Canal on 18 March 2014. On 23 March [USS *Harry S. Truman*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Harry_S._Truman) officially turned over the watch to *George H. W. Bush* in the U.S. 5th Fleet area of responsibility conducting maritime security operations and supporting theater security cooperation efforts.

On 14 June 2014, *George H. W. Bush* was ordered to the [Persian Gulf](24761.md) to protect US interests in Iraq in light of the militant group [Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant](9087364.md)'s (ISIL) [offensive and takeover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Iraq_offensive_\(June_2014\)) of several major cities in that country.

On 8 August 2014, two F/A-18F Super Hornets launched from the ship and dropped 500-pound (230 kg) laser-guided bombs on an ISIL mobile artillery cannon shelling [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshmerga) forces outside their capital city, [Erbil](24534207.md).[^cite_note-51] The mission was launched in accordance with President Obama's announcement on the evening of 7 August that the US would begin airstrikes to protect US personnel and the [Yazidis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yazidis) in the region from ISIL attacks.

On 23 September 2014 F/A-18 Hornets and Super Hornets from Carrier Air Wing Eight launched from *George H. W. Bush* in the Persian Gulf to strike at specific targets in [Syria](7515849.md) such as command-and-control centers, training camps, and weapons depots.[^cite_note-52]

On 15 November 2014, *George H. W. Bush* returned to her homeport in Norfolk, Virginia after a nine-month deployment.

### 2017 deployment



*George H. W. Bush* in company with [HMS *Queen Elizabeth*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Queen_Elizabeth_\(R08\)) and escorts



A pair of [Super Hornets](845012.md) from USS *George H. W. Bush* overfly [HMS *Queen Elizabeth*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Queen_Elizabeth_\(R08\)) during Exercise *Saxon Warrior* in 2017

Following a 14-month shipyard availability at Norfolk Naval Shipyard and a compressed training cycle, *George H. W. Bush* and Carrier Air Wing Eight departed Norfolk on 21 January for her third deployment. She transited the Strait of Gibraltar on 2 February and after a port visit to [Souda Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Souda_Bay), [Crete](6591.md), she again participated in strikes against ISIL in support of [Operation Inherent Resolve](44120613.md).[^cite_note-53]

In July 2017, *George H. W. Bush* arrived in the UK to take part in Exercise *Saxon Warrior*, a joint exercise involving Carrier Strike Group 2 plus elements of the [Royal Navy](26061.md), [German Navy](12960.md), [Royal Norwegian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Navy) and [Swedish Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Navy). This included the staff of the Royal Navy's Carrier Strike Group embarking *Bush* as part of their preparation for the entry into service of [HMS *Queen Elizabeth*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Queen_Elizabeth_\(R08\)), the first of Britain's new aircraft carriers.[^cite_note-54]

### 2018

In May 2018, *George H. W. Bush* took part in the [Franco](5843419.md)-American exercise Chesapeake Mission, with twelve [Dassault Aviation Rafale M](33731380.md) and a [Grumman E-2C Hawkeye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_E-2_Hawkeye) from the [French Navy](383256.md) embarked on board.[^cite_note-55]

### 2019

In February 2019, *George H. W. Bush* arrived at Norfolk Naval Shipyard for a planned 28-month docking planned incremental availability (DPIA). This was the ship's first DPIA.[^cite_note-56]

The United States Navy announced on 24 September that there had been three suicides among the crew serving aboard *George H. W. Bush* in one week. Two previous ship suicides had taken place in November 2017 and July 2019.[^cite_note-57]

### 2020

On 30 July 2020, *[Navy Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navy_Times)* reported that a spokesperson for [Naval Air Force Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Air_Force_Atlantic) confirmed that a "small number" of sailors assigned to *George H. W. Bush* had tested positive for [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronavirus_disease_2019) during the summer.[^cite_note-bush.navytimes-58] The spokesperson declined to provide an exact number of sailors or a more precise date regarding when the virus was detected, citing US Defense Department policy, but added that the carrier was not deployed at the time, and that those infected "remain\[ed] in isolation at their private residences in Virginia and receive\[d] daily medical supportive care".[^cite_note-bush.navytimes-58][^cite_note-59]

In August 2020, *George H. W. Bush* left dry dock at Norfolk Naval Shipyard, after 18 months of the planned two-year overhaul. It was the vessel's most extensive maintenance period and marked the first time out of water since she was built. The overhaul was the most complex undertaken at the shipyard to date, according to the Navy.[^cite_note-Left_Drydock'-60]

### 2022–2023 deployment

On 10 August 2022, *George H. W. Bush*, with [Carrier Air Wing 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_Air_Wing_7) (CVW-7) embarked, began a deployment as part of [Carrier Strike Group 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_Strike_Group_10) (CSG-10). This marks the carrier's first deployment since her 2017 deployment, after which she underwent major maintenance.[^cite_note-61] CSG-10 was reportedly set to relieve [Carrier Strike Group 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_Strike_Group_8) (CSG-8) and its flagship, [USS *Harry S. Truman*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Harry_S._Truman), in the [Mediterranean Sea](19006.md).[^cite_note-62] On 25 August 2022, *George H. W. Bush* and CSG-10 transited the [Strait of Gibraltar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Gibraltar) and entered the Mediterranean Sea.[^cite_note-63] *George H. W. Bush* returned to [Norfolk](896196.md) on 23 April 2023.[^cite_note-64]

### 2025



LOCUST high-energy laser system onboard the USS George H. W. Bush

During 2025, the *George H. W. Bush* had tested the [Locust Laser Weapon System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locust_Laser_Weapon_System) (LWS) onboard as part of a live-fire test. During the live-fire, the LWS had successfully managed to detect, engaged, and destroyed multiple unmanned aerial vehicles. [^cite_note-65]

### 2026

According to reports in January, after tensions between Iran and the US over [Iran's nuclear program](721807.md) began to grew in January 2026 amid Iran's [massacres of Iranian civilians](82036668.md) during their crackdown of the [2025–2026 anti-government protests](81976180.md), the *George H. W. Bush* was expected to be deployed as part of the [US military buildup in the Middle East](82251337.md),[^cite_note-66][^cite_note-67] but the [Abraham Lincoln](199106.md) and [Gerald R. Ford](4050542.md) carriers were sent instead. On March 7th, during the second week of the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), [Fox News](11121.md) reported that the US also expected to deploy the *George H. W. Bush* to the Middle East.[^cite_note-68] On March 31st, during the fifth week of the 2026 Iran war, the *George H. W. Bush* was deployed to the Middle East.[^cite_note-69]

On April 23, 2026, the *George H. W. Bush* and her [carrier strike group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_Strike_Group_10) had successfully sailed in the [Indian Ocean](14580.md) entering the [CENTCOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CENTCOM) area of responsibility, following the transit through [Cape Agulhas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Agulhas) then [Mozambique Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozambique_Channel). The arrival of the *George H. W. Bush&#x20;*&#x20;brings the number of aircraft carriers deployed in the region to 3 carriers.[^cite_note-70] With the *George H. W. Bush* in the region, this is the first time in decades where there are three aircraft carriers operating the Middle East since 2003.[^cite_note-71]
