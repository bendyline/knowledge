The **Free State**[^cite_note-6] is a [province of South Africa](53215.md). Its capital is [Bloemfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein), which is also [South Africa](17416221.md)'s judicial capital. Its historical origins lie in the [Boer republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boer_republic) called the [Orange Free State](180208.md) and later the [Orange Free State Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Free_State_\(province\)).

## History

Further information: [Orange Free State](180208.md)

The current borders of the province date from 1994 when the [Bantustans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bantustan) were abolished and reincorporated into South Africa.[^cite_note-\:0-7] It is also the only one of the four original provinces of South Africa not to undergo border changes, apart from the reincorporation of Bantustans, and its borders date from before the outbreak of the [Boer War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boer_War).[^cite_note-\:0-7]

## Geography



[Cornelia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornelia,_Free_State) in the Riemland region

The Free State is situated on a succession of flat grassy plains sprinkled with pastureland, resting on a general elevation of 3,800 feet only broken by the occasional hill or [kopje](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kopje). The rich soil and pleasant climate allow for a thriving agricultural industry.

The province is high-lying, with almost all land being 1,000 metres above sea level. The [Drakensberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drakensberg) and [Maloti Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maloti_Mountains) foothills raise the terrain to over 2,000 m in the east. The Free State lies in the heart of the [Karoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karoo) Sequence of rocks, containing [shales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shales), [mudstones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mudstones), [sandstones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandstones) and the Drakensberg [Basalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basalt) forming the youngest capping rocks. Mineral deposits are plentiful, with gold and [diamonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamonds) being of particular importance, mostly found in the north and west of the province.

### Fauna and flora

The flats in the south of the reserve provide ideal conditions for large herds of plain game such as [black wildebeest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_wildebeest) and [springbok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springbok). The ridges, koppies and plains typical of the northern section are home to [kudu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kudu), [red hartebeest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_hartebeest), [southern white rhinoceros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_white_rhinoceros) and [buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_buffalo). The [Southern African wildcat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_African_wildcat), [black wildebeest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_wildebeest), [zebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zebra), [eland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_eland), white rhinoceros and [wild dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_wild_dog) can be seen at the [Soetdoring Nature Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soetdoring_Nature_Reserve) near [Bloemfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein). The [South African cheetahs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_cheetah) were reintroduced in the Free State for the first time in June 2013 after a hundred years of regional extinction, at [Laohu Valley Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laohu_Valley_Reserve) near [Philippolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippolis).[^cite_note-8] Following the reintroduction of an adult female South African cheetah in early 2016, three wild cheetah cubs were born for the first time in Laohu Valley Reserve in February 2017, making the three new cubs the first cheetahs born in the wild since their disappearance from the Free State province in over a century.[^cite_note-9]

### Climate

The Free State experiences a [continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_climate), characterised by warm to hot summers and cool to cold winters. Areas in the east experience frequent snowfalls, especially on the higher ranges, whilst the west can be extremely hot in summer. Almost all [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation_\(meteorology\)) falls in the summer months as brief afternoon [thunderstorms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunderstorms), with aridity increasing towards the west. Areas in the east around [Harrismith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrismith), [Bethlehem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem,_Free_State) and [Ficksburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ficksburg) are well watered. The capital, [Bloemfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein), experiences hot, moist summers and cold, dry winters frequented by severe frost.

- [Bloemfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein) averages: January maximum: 31 °C (min: 15 °C), July maximum: 17 °C (min: -2 °C), annual precipitation: 559 mm

- [Bethlehem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem,_Free_State) averages: January maximum: 27 °C (min: 13 °C), July maximum: 16 °C (min: -2 °C), annual precipitation: 680 mm

### Borders



Mafahlaneng township at [Tweeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tweeling)

In the southeast, the Free State borders seven [districts of Lesotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Lesotho):

- [Mokhotlong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mokhotlong_District) – farthest to the east

- [Butha-Buthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butha-Buthe_District) – northwest of Mokhotlong and northeast of Leribe

- [Leribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leribe_District) – southwest of Butha-Buthe and northeast of Berea

- [Berea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berea_District) – southwest of Leribe and north of Maseru

- [Maseru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maseru_District) – south of Berea and northeast of Mafeteng

- [Mafeteng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mafeteng_District) – southwest of Maseru and northwest of Mohale's Hoek

- [Mohale's Hoek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohale's_Hoek_District) – southeast of Mafeteng

Domestically, it borders the following provinces:

- [KwaZulu-Natal](193596.md) – east

- [Eastern Cape](313645.md) – south

- [Northern Cape](447008.md) – west

- [North West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_West_\(South_African_province\)) – northwest

- [Gauteng](309661.md) – north

- [Mpumalanga](313647.md) – northeast

The Free State borders more districts of Lesotho and more provinces of South Africa than any other province.

It is traversed by the northwesterly line of equal latitude and longitude.

As of the [2022 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_South_African_census), the Free State had a population of 2,961,864, an increase of 8.0% from the prior census in [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_South_African_census). It recorded the slowest population growth among South Africa's nine provinces between 2011 and 2022. It is also the second-least populous and second-least densely populated province. The median age is 28, an increase of 3 years from 2011.[^cite_note-2022_census-3]

### Race/Ethnicity

In the 2022 census, 88.9% of the population described themselves as [Black African](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bantu_peoples_of_South_Africa), 8.0% as [White](4734206.md), 2.6% as [Coloured](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coloureds), and 0.4% as [Indian/Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_South_Africans).[^cite_note-2022_census-3] The majority of the population are black Africans who speak [Sotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sotho_language) as a first language. The vast majority of White people in the Free State are [Afrikaners](18947965.md).&#x20;

The proportion of whites in the province has shrunk considerably since the nineteenth century. In 1880 the white population made up 45.7% of the total population. In 1904 this had fallen to 36.8%.[^cite_note-10] Of the 142,679 people in 1904, only 60% were born in the province. Of the 2,726 [European](9239.md) immigrants born in non-[British](31717.md) states, 1,025 came from the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire), mainly [Jews](25955086.md). In 1904 whites made up a majority in most settlements, namely [Ficksburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ficksburg) (52.3%), [Wepener](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wepener) (60.2%), [Ladybrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladybrand) (60.0%), and [Kroonstad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kroonstad) (51.6%), and made up a substantial minority in [Bloemfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein) (45.7%) and [Winburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winburg) (36.3%).

### Languages

In the 2022 census, 72.3% of the population reported their [first language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_language) as [Sotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sotho_language), 10.3% as [Afrikaans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrikaans), 5.5% as [Xhosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xhosa_language), 5.3% as [Setswana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Setswana), and 1.5% as [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_English). The Free State is the only province in which native Sotho-speakers form a majority of the population. It is also the province with the third-highest proportion of Afrikaans speakers, after the [Western Cape](313646.md) and the [Northern Cape](447008.md).[^cite_note-2022_census-3]

[Sesotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesotho) is the dominant home language in most of the province. [Zulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zulu_language) is the major language in the far eastern municipality of [Phumelela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phumelela_Local_Municipality). [Setswana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Setswana) is the main language in [Tokologo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokologo_Local_Municipality) in the northwest, and in and around the area of [Thaba Nchu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thaba_Nchu). [Afrikaans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrikaans) is widely spoken throughout the province, as a first language for the majority of whites and coloureds (who constitute a minority) and as a second or third language by Sesotho, Setswana and Xhosa speakers. Although there are relatively few native English speakers, English is becoming increasingly important as the language of business and government. This is evidenced by the shift of tertiary institutions such as the [University of the Free State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Free_State) from solely using Afrikaans as the medium of instruction to using both Afrikaans and English, and eventually only English.

### Religion

The population of the Free State is overwhelmingly [Christian](5211.md). As of the 2022 census, 92.7% of the population described themselves as [Christians](5211.md), the third-highest proportion among South Africa's provinces. Among other religions, 4.9% of the population stated that they practiced [Traditional African](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_African_religions) religions, and 0.5% of the population described themselves as [Muslim](6037917.md). 1.4% of the population described themselves as being atheist, agnostic, or having no religious affiliation.[^cite_note-2022_census-3]

### Education

Approximately 34.3% of learners aged 0     −   {\displaystyle -}  {\displaystyle -} 4 did not attend any type of [Early Childhood Development(ECD)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_childhood_development) programme. According to the 2022 census, 76.8% of persons aged 5     −   {\displaystyle -}  {\displaystyle -} 24 were attending an educational institution. The overall percentage shows an increase in the percentage of attendance from the 2011 census, increasing from 73.1%. ## Government and politics

### Government

See also: [Executive Council of the Free State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Council_of_the_Free_State)

The Free State provincial government is based in [Bloemfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein), the provincial capital. The [Free State Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_State_Division) of the [High Court of South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Court_of_South_Africa) also sits in Bloemfontein.

Like South Africa's other provinces, the Free State has a parliamentary system of government, with the [provincial premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_the_Free_State) elected by the [Free State Provincial Legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_State_Provincial_Legislature). The premier then selects the members of the provincial [Executive Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Council_of_the_Free_State) (cabinet). The current premier is [Maqueen Letsoha-Mathae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maqueen_Letsoha-Mathae) of the [African National Congress](2503.md) (ANC), who has held the position since 2024.[^cite_note-11]

The provincial legislature is elected every five years by a system of [party-list proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party-list_proportional_representation) and meets at the [Vierde Raadsaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vierde_Raadsaal) in [Bloemfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein).

### Politics

See also: [2024 South African provincial elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_South_African_provincial_elections)

The politics of the Free State has been dominated by the ANC since the end of Apartheid. The ANC has won a majority of the vote in every provincial election since 1994.

The most recent provincial election was held in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_South_African_provincial_elections). The ANC again won the election, albeit with a sharply reduced majority. The [Democratic Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Alliance_\(South_Africa\)) (DA) retained its position as the province's second largest party and [official opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_opposition), with a significant increase in its share of the vote.

The Free State is the only province in South Africa that operates a free 24-hour dedicated rotor-wing aeromedical service from a public hospital. They are able to reach far-flung areas in only 45 minutes and deliver a high level of care on scene. On 31 October 2018, Free State Emergency Medical Service launched an additional 65 road ambulances to augment the fleet.

The Free State has many public and private hospitals.

## Economy

The province is the granary of South Africa, with agriculture central to its economy, while mining on the rich goldfields reef is its largest employer.

### Agriculture



Cattle grazing near [Winburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winburg)

Agriculture dominates the Free State landscape, with cultivated land covering 32,000 square kilometres, and natural veld and grazing a further 87,000 square kilometres of the province. It is also South Africa's leader in the production of biofuels, or fuel from agricultural crops, with a number of ethanol plants under construction in the grain-producing western region. South Africa is one of the top ten [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize) producers in the world (12,365,000 tons as of 2013).

Field crops yield almost two-thirds of the gross agricultural income of the province. Animal products contribute a further 30%, with the balance generated by horticulture. Ninety percent of the country's cherry crop is produced in the Ficksburg district, which is also home to the country's two largest asparagus canning factories. Soya, sorghum, sunflowers and wheat are cultivated in the eastern Free State, where farmers specialise in seed production. About 40% of the country's potato yield comes from the province's high-lying areas.

The main vegetable crop is asparagus, both white and green varieties. Although horticulture is expanding and becoming increasingly export-orientated, most produce leaves the province unprocessed.

The Free State's advantage in floriculture is the opposing seasons of the southern and northern hemispheres.

### Mining

The Free State is also rich in mineral wealth, gold representing 20% of the world's total gold production. Mining is the province's major employer. The province has 12 gold mines, producing 30% of South Africa's output and making it the fifth-largest producer of gold in the world. The Harmony Gold Refinery and Rand Refinery are the only two gold refineries in South Africa.

Gold mines in the Free State also supply a substantial portion of the total silver produced in the country, while considerable concentrations of uranium occurring in the gold-bearing conglomerates of the goldfields are extracted as a byproduct.

Bituminous coal is also mined, and converted to petrochemicals at Sasolburg. The Free State also produces high-quality diamonds from its kimberlite pipes and fissures, and the country's largest deposit of bentonite is found in the [Koppies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koppies) district.

### Industry

Since 1989, the Free State economy has moved from dependence on primary sectors such as mining and agriculture to an economy increasingly oriented towards manufacturing and export. Some 14% of the province's manufacturing is classified as being in high-technology industries – the highest of all provincial economies. The northern Free State's chemicals sector is one of the most important in the southern hemisphere. Petrochemicals company [Sasol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasol), based in the town of [Sasolburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasolburg), is a world leader in the production of fuels, waxes, chemicals and low-cost feedstock from coal.

### Tourism



On top of Koranaberg

In the northeastern Free State, nestled in the rolling foothills of the Maluti mountains, the [Golden Gate Highlands National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Gate_Highlands_National_Park) is the province's prime tourist attraction. The park gets its name from the brilliant shades of gold cast by the sun on the spectacular sandstone cliffs, especially the imposing Brandwag or Sentinel Rock, which keeps vigil over the park.



Brandwag (The Sentinel)

The sandstone of this region has been used for the dressed-stone buildings found on the Eastern Highlands, while decoratively painted [Sotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sotho_people) houses dot the grasslands. Some of South Africa's most valued San (Bushman) rock art is found in the Free State, particularly in the regions around [Clarens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarens,_Free_State), [Bethlehem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem,_Free_State), [Ficksburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ficksburg), [Ladybrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladybrand) and [Wepener](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wepener).

Not only is the Vredefort Dome the oldest and largest meteorite impact known in earth's history, but it is also the most corroded.[^cite_note-12]
