The **2023–24 Columbus Blue Jackets season** was the [24th season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Columbus_Blue_Jackets_seasons) for the [National Hockey League](21809.md) (NHL) franchise that was established on June 25, 1997.

In the off-season, on July 1, 2023, the Blue Jackets hired [Mike Babcock](644418.md) as head coach.[^cite_note-2] However, Babcock resigned after the conclusion of an investigation about improper behavior with players on September 17,[^cite_note-3] and was replaced by [Pascal Vincent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascal_Vincent).[^cite_note-4]

On March 24, 2024, the Blue Jackets were eliminated from playoff contention for the fourth consecutive season following the [Washington Capitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Washington_Capitals_season)' 3–0 win against the [Winnipeg Jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Winnipeg_Jets_season).[^cite_note-5]
