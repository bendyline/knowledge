# Huione Group



Huione Pay logo

**Huione Group** ([Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language): ហួយវ័ន គ្រុប, [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): [汇](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%B1%87) [旺](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%97%BA) [集团](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E9%9B%86%E5%9B%A2), [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Chinese): *Huìwàng jítuán*) is a Cambodian financial [conglomerate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conglomerate_\(company\)),[^cite_note-\:0-1] headquartered in [Phnom Penh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phnom_Penh).[^cite_note-takeaways-2] Some of its products and subsidiaries include Huione Pay ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 汇旺[支付](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%94%AF%E4%BB%98), [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Chinese): *Huìwàng zhīfù*) (banking),[^cite_note-\:0-1] Huione Guarantee ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 汇旺[担保](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%8B%85%E4%BF%9D), [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Chinese): *Huìwàng dānbǎo*) (an [escrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escrow) and deposit service sometimes used as a [grey market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grey_market)),[^cite_note-\:1-3] and Huione Crypto ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 汇旺([加密货币](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%8A%A0%E5%AF%86%E8%B4%A7%E5%B8%81))[交易所](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E4%BA%A4%E6%98%93%E6%89%80), [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Chinese): *Huìwàng (jiāmì huòbì) jiāoyìsuǒ*) (a [cryptocurrency exchange](3330391.md)).[^cite_note-\:1-3] The company is linked to Cambodia's ruling [Hun family](393779.md), which includes the current prime minister, [Hun Manet](42929019.md).[^cite_note-\:2-4] His cousin [Hun To](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hun_To) is a major shareholder and director of Huione Pay.[^cite_note-5]

## Money laundering charges

In July 2024, the blockchain analytics firm [Elliptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_\(company\)) linked Huione Guarantee to the sale of stolen data and [money laundering](19390.md) services used to conduct illegal activities,[^cite_note-6] such as the so-called [pig butchering scam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pig_butchering_scam).[^cite_note-\:1-3][^cite_note-\:2-4][^cite_note-takeaways-2] In March 2025, the [National Bank of Cambodia](9514099.md) revoked Huione Pay's banking license.[^cite_note-\:0-1] In May, the [U.S. Department of the Treasury](53667.md)'s Financial Crimes Enforcement Network found Huione Group to be a financial institution of primary money laundering concern and proposed to sever its access to the U.S. financial system.[^cite_note-7] FinCEN reported that Huione Group laundered over US\$4 billion in illegal funds from August 2021 to January 2025, including US\$37 million from North Korean cyber heists and US\$36 million from fraud scams.[^cite_note-8] Shortly after, [Telegram](41501305.md) deleted thousands of channels used by Huione Guarantee and banned several accounts that had operated the marketplace.[^cite_note-9] It has grown to become the largest criminal marketplace of all time, with transactions totalling more than \$27 billion.[^cite_note-10]

In April 2026, Huione's former chairman Li Xiong was extradited to China to face money laundering charges. Chinese authorities described Huione as a subsidiary of [Prince Group](13543532.md) and Li as a key member of Prince founder [Chen Zhi](81436620.md)'s "criminal gang".[^cite_note-11]
