**Herintsalama Andriamiasy Rajaonarivelo** is a Malagasy politician, businessman, and [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) who served as the [Prime Minister of Madagascar](30863461.md) from October 2025 until March 2026. Prior to his appointment as prime minister, Rajaonarivelo served as Chairman of the Board of Directors of BNI Madagascar and was thus a prominent figure in the Malagasy economic and banking sector.

## Education

Herintsalama Rajaonarivelo holds a postgraduate degree in industrial economics and business economics from the [Toulouse Capitole University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse_Capitole_University) in France.

## Career

### Professional career

Trained as an international consultant, he has carried out numerous missions for international organizations including the [World Bank](36752.md), the [European Union](9317.md), the Secretariat General of the ACP Countries, the [COMESA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Market_for_Eastern_and_Southern_Africa), the [SADC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_African_Development_Community), the [IOC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean_Commission) and the [African Development Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Development_Bank).

He has been chairman and chief executive officer of the Banque nationale de l'industrie|National Industrial Bank] since November 2020.[^cite_note-Midi-1] He has been chairman of the International Trade Board of Madagascar since November 2011.

He is the founding president of FOM (Fikambanan'ny Orinasa Malagasy), a network of businesses working for the formalization and competitiveness of the private sector. In an interview published in May 2025, he stated that "SMEs constitute the tax base," arguing for a reform of the fiscal and administrative framework to encourage formalization and economic growth.[^cite_note-LExpress-2]

He has spoken at economic conferences, including the forum "Innovative Growth Strategy and Leadership in the Banking Sector in Madagascar," organized by Altitude Madagascar in November 2024.[^cite_note-Altitude-3]

### Political career

He began his government career as a technical advisor at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs from February 2019 to April 2020.

On 20 October 2025, following the [mass demonstrations](81184334.md) that began on 25 September 2025 and evolved into the [2025 Malagasy coup d'état](81321149.md), he was appointed [Prime Minister of Madagascar](30863461.md) by President [Michael Randrianirina](81340595.md). [Ruphin Zafisambo](81277906.md) had been appointed a fortnight earlier by [Andry Rajoelina](21298487.md), prior to the coup d'état.[^cite_note-Reuters-4]

On 9 March 2026, President [Michael Randrianirina](81340595.md) dismissed multiple cabinet members and Rajaonarivelo himself, removing him as [Prime Minister](30863461.md).[^cite_note-5]
