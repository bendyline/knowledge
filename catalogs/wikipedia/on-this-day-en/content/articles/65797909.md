This increased the Sayyad-1 missile range to 80–100 km, from the previous 60 km range. **Missile specification:**  Range: 80–100 km  Altitude: 27 km  Speed: Mach 3.6  Warhead: 195 kg **Sayyad-2:** Mass production began in November 2013.[^cite_note-10] **Missile specification:**  Range: 100–120 km  Altitude: 27 km  Speed: Mach 3.6–4  Warhead: 200 kg **Sayyad-3:** The Sayyad-3 is an upgrade to the Sayyad-2 Missile. The Sayyad-3 missile, used by the SAM system, has a range of 200 kilometres (120 mi). The system can detect stealth targets from a distance of 85 kilometres (53 mi) and can intercept and destroy them from a range of 45 kilometres (28 mi).[^cite_note-\:1-5] **Missile system specification:**[^cite_note-12]  Detection Range: 150 km  Operational Range: 500 km |

The S-200 Ghareh missile uses the Talash-3 system and the Bavar-373 system. The S-200 Ghareh missiles speed is Mach 6. Its maximum flight altitude is 40 km, equipped with 200 kg high explosive warhead. |
