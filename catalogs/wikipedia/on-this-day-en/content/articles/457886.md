The **Japan Meteorological Agency** ([Japanese](15606.md): 気象庁, [Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepburn_romanization): *Kishō-chō*; **JMA**) is a division of the [Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Land,_Infrastructure,_Transport_and_Tourism) dedicated to the [scientific observation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific) and research of [natural phenomena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_phenomena). Headquartered in [Minato, Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minato,_Tokyo), the [agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_agency) collects data on [meteorology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteorology), [hydrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrology), [seismology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seismology), [volcanology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanology), and other related fields.

The JMA is responsible for collecting and disseminating [weather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather) data and [forecasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forecasting) to the public, as well as providing specialized information for [aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviation) and [marine sectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_weather_forecasting). Additionally, the JMA issues warnings for [volcanic eruptions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanic_eruptions) and is integral to the nationwide [Earthquake Early Warning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthquake_Early_Warning_\(Japan\)) (EEW) system. As one of the [Regional Specialized Meteorological Centers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Specialized_Meteorological_Center) designated by the [World Meteorological Organization](33584.md) (WMO), the JMA also [forecasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forecasting), [names](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclone_naming), and distributes warnings for [tropical cyclones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclones) in the [Northwestern Pacific](23070.md) region. This includes areas such as the [Celebes Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celebes_Sea), the [Sulu Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulu_Sea), the [South China Sea](74209.md), the [East China Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_China_Sea), the [Yellow Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_Sea), the [Sea of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_of_Japan), and the [Sea of Okhotsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_of_Okhotsk).

## History

Meteorological organizations in Japan have their origins in the 1870s, when the first weather stations started being established in the country.[^cite_note-JMA-brochure-2010-1] One of these was the Tokyo Meteorological Observatory (東京気象台, *Tōkyō Kishō-dai*), which since 1956 has been known as the **Japan Meteorological Agency** (気象庁, *Kishō-chō*). It was originally formed within the Survey Division of the Geography Bureau of the [Home Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Ministry_\(Japan\)) (内務省地理寮量地課, *Naimu-shō Chiri-ryō Ryōchi-ka*).[^cite_note-JMA-brochure-2010-1][^cite_note-\:0-4] However, jurisdiction over the agency has changed several times over the years, and since the [Japanese government reformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Central_Government_Reform) in 2001, it has been an agency of the [Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Land,_Infrastructure,_Transport_and_Tourism) (国土交通省, *Kokudo-kōtsū-shō*). Its headquarters have also changed several times, and as of November 24, 2020, they are now located in [Toranomon, Minato, Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toranomon,_Minato,_Tokyo).[^cite_note-toranomon-5]

## Services

### Overview

The JMA is responsible for observing, gathering and reporting weather data and [forecasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_forecast), and warning for [earthquakes](10106.md), [tsunamis](31161.md), [typhoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon) and [volcanic eruptions](8921015.md).[^cite_note-JMABrochure-9]

The agency has six regional administrative offices (including five [DMOs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_Meteorological_Observatory) and [Okinawa Meteorological Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okinawa_Meteorological_Observatory)), four Marine Observatories, five auxiliary facilities, four Aviation Weather Service Centers and 47 local offices composed of the [LMOs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Meteorological_Observatory). These are also used to gather data, supplemented by [weather satellites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_satellite) such as *[Himawari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himawari_\(satellite\))*, and other research institutes.[^cite_note-JMABrochure-9]

In 1968, the [World Meteorological Organization](33584.md) (WMO) designated the JMA as a [Regional Specialized Meteorological Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Specialized_Meteorological_Centre) (RSMC) for Asia.[^cite_note-intl-coop-10] In June 1988, the WMO also assigned the JMA as a RSMC for the [Northwestern Pacific](23070.md) under its Tropical Cyclone programme.[^cite_note-intl-coop-10] In July 1989, the RSMC Tokyo – Typhoon Center was established within the headquarters office, which dealt with the forecasting and dissemination of active [tropical cyclones](8282374.md), as well as preparing a summary of each year's cyclone activity.[^cite_note-11]

#### Weather

##### Land weather

Each [DMO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_Meteorological_Observatory) and [LMO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Meteorological_Observatory) issues weather forecasts and warnings or advisories to the general public live in its own area. Weather data used to these forecasts are acquired from the Surface Observation (represented by the [AMeDAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_Meteorological_Data_Acquisition_System)), the [Radar Observation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_radar), the Observation and the [Satellite Observation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_satellite) mainly using the *[Himawari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himawari_\(satellite\))* series of satellites.

##### Marine weather

See also: [Marine weather forecasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_weather_forecasting)

The Marine Observatories are seated in [Hakodate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakodate,_Hokkaid%C5%8D), [Maizuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maizuru,_Kyoto), [Kobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe), [Nagasaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagasaki). These stations observe [ocean waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_wave), [tide levels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tide), [sea surface temperatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_surface_temperature) and [ocean currents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean_current) etc. in the [Northwestern Pacific](23070.md) basin, as well as the [Sea of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_of_Japan), the [Sea of Okhotsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_of_Okhotsk), and provide [marine meteorological forecasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_weather_forecasting) in cooperation with the Hydrographic and Oceanographic Department, [Japan Coast Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Coast_Guard).

##### Aviation weather

See also: [METAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/METAR) and [SIGMET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIGMET)

In 2005, in accordance with the [ICAO](14985.md)'s new CNS/[ATM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_traffic_management) system, the [Civil Aviation Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Aviation_Bureau) of the [Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Land,_Infrastructure,_Transport_and_Tourism) set up the [Air Traffic Management Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Traffic_Management_Center?action=edit\&redlink=1) (ATMC) in [Fukuoka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukuoka), where the [FIR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_information_region) is fixed. Along with this establishment, JMA placed the [Air Traffic Mateorology Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Traffic_Mateorology_Center?action=edit\&redlink=1) (ATMetC) inside the ATMC.

The agency forecasts [SIGMET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIGMET) for aircraft in flight within the Fukuoka [FIR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_information_region) airspace, while [VOLMET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VOLMET) is broadcast by each [Aviation Weather Service Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviation_Weather_Service_Center?action=edit\&redlink=1) at the airports of [Haneda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haneda_International_Airport), [Narita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narita_International_Airport), [Centrair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chubu_Centrair_International_Airport), and [Kansai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansai_International_Airport).

##### Tropical cyclones

See also: [Typhoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon) and [2026 Pacific typhoon season](80781992.md)

In the Northwestern Pacific area, the typhoon season ordinarily comes almost from May to November. The JMA forecasts and warns or advises on tropical cyclones to the public in Japan and its surrounding countries as the [RSMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Specialized_Meteorological_Centre) Tokyo – Typhoon Center.[^cite_note-rsmc-tc-12]

#### Earthquakes

See also: [Japan Meteorological Agency seismic intensity scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Meteorological_Agency_seismic_intensity_scale) and [Earthquake Early Warning (Japan)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthquake_Early_Warning_\(Japan\))

The JMA has 624 observation stations across Japan[^cite_note-observ-seismo-13] which are set up at intervals of 20 km approximately[^cite_note-14] in order to measure the [seismic intensity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Meteorological_Agency_seismic_intensity_scale) of earthquakes precisely. The agency also utilizes about 2,900 seismographs[^cite_note-observ-seismo-13] owned by the [National Research Institute for Earth Science and Disaster Resilience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Institute_for_Earth_Science_and_Disaster_Resilience) (NIED) and [local governments](74517.md). A 24-hour office is housed within the JMA headquarters in Tokyo for monitoring and tracking seismic events in the vicinity of Japan to collect and process their data, which distributes observed earthquake information on its [hypocenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypocenter), [magnitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richter_magnitude_scale), seismic intensity and possibility of [tsunami](31161.md) occurrence after quakes quickly to the public through the [Earthquake Phenomena Observation System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthquake_Phenomena_Observation_System?action=edit\&redlink=1) (EPOS).[^cite_note-15] The [Earthquake Early Warning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthquake_Early_Warning_\(Japan\)) (EEW) system began to work fully for the general public on October 1, 2007.

The agency is one of the representatives of the national [Coordinating Committee for Earthquake Prediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinating_Committee_for_Earthquake_Prediction).[^cite_note-ccep-orgs-16]

#### Tsunamis

See also: [Tsunami warning system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsunami_warning_system)

In case of a possibility of [tsunami](31161.md) after an earthquake, JMA issues Tsunami Warning or Advisory for each region in Japan with information of estimated tsunami heights and arrival times within 2 to 3 minutes of the quake.

#### Volcanoes

See also: [Volcanic Ash Advisory Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanic_Ash_Advisory_Center)

The agency four [Volcanic Observations and Information Centers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanic_Observations_and_Information_Center?action=edit\&redlink=1) within DMOs in [Sapporo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapporo), [Sendai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sendai), [Tokyo](30057.md) and [Fukuoka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukuoka). These centers monitor volcanic events on 110 active volcanos in Japan. 47 of these volcanos selected by the [Coordinating Committee for Prediction of Volcanic Eruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinating_Committee_for_Prediction_of_Volcanic_Eruption?action=edit\&redlink=1) are under 24-hour observation with [seismographs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seismograph), [accelerometers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accelerometer), [GPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Positioning_System), air-shock recorders, fixed point observation cameras and other equipment. If it is predicted that a volcanic eruption will affect inhabited areas or around a crater, Volcanic Warnings are issued and supplemented by Volcanic Alert Levels.
