**May el-Thouky** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مي الطوخي) is a Danish-Egyptian filmmaker. Most known for her drama film *[Queen of Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_of_Hearts_\(2019_film\))* (2019). For *[Woman Unknown](83824249.md)* (2026), she won the [Golden Lion](2386128.md) at the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md).&#x20;

## Early life and education

El-Thouky was born in 1977 to a Danish mother and Egyptian father, and has said that her father's experience as a first generation immigrant has influenced her film making. She graduated from the [Danish National School of Performing Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_National_School_of_Performing_Arts) in 2002, and later as a film director from the [National Film School of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Film_School_of_Denmark) in 2009.[^cite_note-1]

## Career



El-Toukhy with her [Golden Lion](2386128.md) for [*Woman Unknown*](83824249.md) at the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md)

She creates films featuring complicated protagonists, like the 2019 film *[Queen of Hearts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_of_Hearts_\(2019_film\))*[^cite_note-2] and 2026 [*Woman Unknown*](83824249.md). *Woman Unknown* was the only film by a female director initially included in the 2026 [Venice Film Festival](32497.md) main competition.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

She was a signing member of the Dogma 25 movement.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Personal life

El-Thouky is in a relationship with Danish producer Mikael Rieks. She also has a daughter from a previous relationship.[^cite_note-\:5-4]
