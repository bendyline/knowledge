The **Mexican Navy** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Armada de México*) is the [maritime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_warfare) [service branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_branch) of the [Mexican Armed Forces](23384265.md). The [Secretariat of the Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretariat_of_the_Navy) is in charge of administration of the navy. The commander of the navy is the Secretary of the Navy, who is both a cabinet minister and a career naval officer.

The Mexican Navy's stated mission is "to use the naval force of the federation for external defense, and to help with internal order".[^cite_note-2] As of 2020, the Navy consisted of about 68,200 personnel plus reserves,[^cite_note-3] over 189 ships, and about 130 aircraft.[^cite_note-autogenerated1-4][^cite_note-5] The Navy attempts to maintain a constant modernization program to upgrade its response capability.

Given Mexico's large area of water (3,149,920 km<sup>2</sup> (1,216,190 sq mi)) and extensive coastline (11,122 km (6,911 mi)), the Navy's duties are of great importance. Perhaps its most important on-going missions are fighting the [Mexican drug war](8442323.md), which includes protecting [Pemex](286846.md)'s [oil wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_well) in [Campeche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campeche) in the [Gulf of Mexico](21076367.md). Another important task of the Mexican Navy is to help people in hurricane relief operations and other natural disasters.

The Mexican navy is the second largest navy in Latin America and North America, and the third largest in the Americas after the United States and Brazil.

The Mexican Navy has its origins in the creation of the Ministry of War in 1821. From that year until 1939 it existed jointly with the [Mexican Army](2948082.md) in the organic ministry. Since its declaration of independence from Spain in September 1810, through the mid decades of the 19th century, Mexico found itself in a constant state of war, mostly against Spain which had not recognized its independence. Therefore, its priority was to purchase its first fleet from the U.S. to displace the last remaining Spanish forces from its coasts.[^cite_note-6]

The Mexican Navy has participated in many naval battles to protect and defend Mexico's interests. Some of the most important battles were:

## Organization

The [President of Mexico](24356889.md) is commander in chief of all military forces. Day-to-day control of the Navy lies with the Navy Secretary, [José Rafael Ojeda Durán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Rafael_Ojeda_Dur%C3%A1n).[^cite_note-7] In Mexico there is no joint force command structure with the army, so the Secretary reports directly to the President. The Navy has a General Headquarters and three naval forces. There are furthermore eight regions (four on the Pacific coast, three on the Mexican Gulf coast and the Región Naval Central, grouping the naval forces, based in and around the capital [Mexico City](18987.md), such as the 7th Naval Infantry Brigade, the Central Special Operations Group and the Air Transport Squadron), thirteen zones, and fourteen naval sectors.

The Navy is divided into three main services designated as "forces":

- [Gulf and Caribbean Sea Naval Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_and_Caribbean_Sea_Naval_Force)

- [Pacific Naval Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Naval_Force)

- [Naval Infantry Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Infantry_Force)

Other notable services include:

- [Naval Aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Naval_Aviation)

- [Search and Rescue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_Search_and_Rescue_\(Mexico\))

Officers are trained at the Mexican Naval Academy, called the "[Heroica Escuela Naval Militar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroica_Escuela_Naval_Militar)" ("Heroic Military Naval School"), located in [Antón Lizardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant%C3%B3n_Lizardo), [Veracruz](63125.md).

### Naval Infantry

Main article: [Mexican Naval Infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Naval_Infantry)

The [Mexican Naval Infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Naval_Infantry) Corps was reorganized in 2007–2009 into 30 Naval Infantry Battalions (Batallones de Infantería de Marina – BIM), a paratroop battalion, a battalion attached to the Presidential Guard Brigade, two Fast Reaction Forces with six battalions each, and three [Special Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuerzas_Especiales) groups.[^cite_note-8] The Naval Infantry are responsible for port security, protection of the ten-kilometer coastal fringe, and patrolling major waterways.

The Naval Infantry also is responsible for 23 National Service Training Units under the responsibility of the Navy Secretary, enforcing the National Service obligation for Mexicans of teenage and young adult age.

Main article: [Mexican Naval Aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Naval_Aviation)

### Search and rescue units

Main article: [Maritime Search and Rescue (Mexico)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_Search_and_Rescue_\(Mexico\))

In 2008, the Mexican Navy created its new search and rescue system, allocated in strategic ports at Pacific and Gulf of Mexico ports, to provide assistance to any ships which are in jeopardy or at risk due to mechanical failure, weather conditions or life risk to the crew. To provide such support, the Navy has ordered Coast Guard Defender class ships (two per station, and one [47-Foot Motor Lifeboat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/47-Foot_Motor_Lifeboat) coast guard vessel). Other stations will be provided only with [Defender-class boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defender-class_boat).[^cite_note-9]

### Maritime role

On April 1, 2014, SEMAR officially announced the creation of Port Protection Naval Units (*Unidades Navales de Protección Portuaria: UNAPROP*) which will include a marine section.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] The main task of UNAPROPs is to ensure maritime surveillance and inspection.[^cite_note-12]

The Navy offers several options for graduate studies in their educational institutions:

[Heroica Escuela Naval Militar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroica_Escuela_Naval_Militar)

It is the school where future officers are trained for the General Corps of the Navy. Candidates can enter upon completing high school. Upon completion of studies, graduates obtain the degree of Sub-Lieutenant and the title of Naval Science Engineer.

Naval Medical School

This school Located in Mexico City, offers a career in medicine. Officers are trained with skills for the prevention and health care of naval personnel. By adopting a professional examination, graduates are commissioned Sub-Lieutenants.

Naval Engineering School

In the Naval Engineering School, officers are responsible for the preventive and corrective maintenance of systems and electronic equipment installed on ships and installations of the Mexican Navy. This school offers career of Electronic Engineering and Naval Communications. It is located between the town of Mata Grape and Anton Lizardo, 32 km (20 mi) from the port of Veracruz.

Naval Nursing School

Here the time to achieve a nursing degree lasts eight semesters. Officers are trained with the knowledge and skills necessary to enable them to assist medical personnel in caring for patients in hospitals, sanatoriums, clinics, health sections on land, aboard ships and at The Naval Medical Center.

Naval Aviation School

The Naval Aviation School trains pilots for the [Mexican Naval Aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Naval_Aviation) as well as National Guard aviators and Naval personnel from various countries of Central America. This school is located on [La Paz, Baja California Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Paz,_Baja_California_Sur).[^cite_note-13]

Search, Rescue and Diving School

Located in [Acapulco](1446.md), members of The Navy are trained for marine search, rescue and diving. It also trains state police officers and firefighters.

## Modernization and budget

The annual Navy's budget is in a one to three proportion of the national budget relative to the Mexican Army and [Mexican Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Air_Force). The Navy has a reputation for being well-run and well-organized. This reputation allows for a close relationship with the [United States Navy](20518076.md) (USN), as evidenced by the procurement of numerous former USN ships.

### Ships

The Secretary of the Navy, Admiral [Mariano Francisco Saynez Mendoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariano_Francisco_Saynez_Mendoza), announced on October 1, 2007, detailed plans to upgrade and modernize the country's naval capabilities. On the following day, *[La Jornada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Jornada)* newspaper from Mexico City, disclosed the Mexican Navy plans, which are among others, to build six [offshore patrol vessels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offshore_patrol_vessel) (OPVs) with a length of 86 metres (282 ft), 1,680 tons and each housing a [Eurocopter Panther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocopter_Panther) helicopter as well as small high-speed interception boats. The budget for this project is above US\$200 million.

Another project is to build 12 [CB 90 HMN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_Boat_90) high speed (50 [knots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (93 km/h; 58 mph)) interception boats under license from a Swedish boat company [Dockstavarvet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dockstavarvet) to the Mexican Navy. Also, a number of fully equipped planes for surveillance and maritime patrol are being considered. Combinations of options and development are being defined.

### Missiles

The Mexican Navy initiated studies to develop and construct its first [missile](19594.md), according to a May 2005 interview with the undersecretary of the Navy, Armando Sanchez, the missile was to have an average range of 12 to 15 kilometres (7.5 to 9.3 mi) and be able to target enemy ships and aircraft. The undersecretary added that they already had the solid [propellant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propellant), and the basic design of the missile. All aspects relative to their fuselage were solved as well as the launch platforms. The Mexican Navy was developing the software to direct the missile to its target. In July 2008, the project was reported to be 80% complete. Despite this effort, the missile development was canceled in 2009 due to "problems with the propulsion system".[^cite_note-15]

### Radar modernization

In 2009, the Mexican Navy began operating a batch of new [MPQ-64 Sentinel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MPQ-64_Sentinel) radars in the oil-rich Gulf of Mexico. The radar network was installed in 2007 for a trial phase while military personnel were trained to get familiar with the system. The new installations will work together with combat surface vessels that patrol the area.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

The Mexican Navy includes 60 smaller patrol boats and 32 auxiliary ships. It acquired 40 fast military assault crafts, designated [CB 90 HMN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_Boat_90), between 1999 and 2001 and obtained a production license in 2002, enabling further units to be manufactured in Mexico.

## Future

|  | This section needs to be **updated**. Please help update this article to reflect recent events or newly available information. *(June 2018)* |
| --- | --- |

For the year 2008 budget, the Mexican Congress approved a US\$15 million fund to build only 17 out of the 60 combat boats requested. These ships, designated [CB 90 HMN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_Boat_90), are to increase the Mexican Navy's fast boat fleet. Additional budgets will be awarded each passing year.[^cite_note-43] In total, the Mexican Navy has over 189 operational ships.[^cite_note-autogenerated1-4]

In January 2013, the 112th Session of US Congress authorized the transfer of the [*Oliver Hazard Perry*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Hazard_Perry-class_frigate) [frigates](11464.md) [USS *Curts*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Curts) and [USS *McClusky*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_McClusky) to the Mexican Navy,[^cite_note-H.R._6649-44] but due to the cost of overhauling the vessels and the removal of all the weapons systems and most of the electronics and radar gear by the USN prior to transfer, this is still undecided by Mexico. The offer expired on January 1, 2016.[^cite_note-H.R._6649-44]

### 2014

On March 25, 2014 [Beechcraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beechcraft) Corporation received an order of 2 T-6C+ military trainers from the Mexican Navy.[^cite_note-45]

On June 24, 2014, the Mexican Government requested the purchase of 5 UH-60Ms in USG configuration from the U.S.; its estimated cost is \$225 million.[^cite_note-46] Also on June 24, [BAE Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAE_Systems) announced it was awarded a contract by the Mexican Government to supply the navy with 4 [Mk 3 57mm naval guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bofors_57_mm_L/70_naval_artillery_gun), for the ships of the Reformador class.[^cite_note-47]
