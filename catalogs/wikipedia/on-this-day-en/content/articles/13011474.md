**Chřibská** (Czech pronunciation: [\[ˈxr̝̊ɪpskaː\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Czech); [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): *Kreibitz*) is a town in [Děčín District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C4%9B%C4%8D%C3%ADn_District) in the [Ústí nad Labem Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%9Ast%C3%AD_nad_Labem_Region) of the [Czech Republic](5321.md). It has about 1,300 inhabitants. The town is located on the Chřibská Kamenice River, on the border between the [Lusatian Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusatian_Mountains) and [Elbe Sandstone Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbe_Sandstone_Mountains). The main landmark of Chřibská is the Church of Saint George.

## Administrative division

Chřibská consists of four municipal parts (in brackets population according to the 2021 census):[^cite_note-2]

- Chřibská (620)

- Dolní Chřibská (382)

- Horní Chřibská (210)

- Krásné Pole (141)

## Etymology

The initial name of Chřibská was Křípčice (in Old Czech written as Křiepčice). The name has probably its origin in the personal name Chřáp. From 1363, the forms Křípská and Křiepská began to appear, from which the current name later evolved.[^cite_note-3]

## Geography

Chřibská is located about 21 kilometres (13 mi) northeast of [Děčín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C4%9B%C4%8D%C3%ADn) and 38 km (24 mi) northeast of [Ústí nad Labem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%9Ast%C3%AD_nad_Labem). Most of the municipal territory lies in the [Lusatian Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusatian_Mountains) and in the eponymous protected landscape area. The western part of the territory extends into the [Elbe Sandstone Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbe_Sandstone_Mountains) and a small part of Chřibská also lies in the [Bohemian Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohemian_Switzerland) National Park. The highest point is the hill [Spravedlnost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spravedlnost) at 533 m (1,749 ft) above sea level. The Chřibská Kamenice River flows through the town.

## History

The first written mention of Chřibská is from 1352.[^cite_note-history-4] It was part of the [Kamenice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%8Cesk%C3%A1_Kamenice) estate, owned by the Wartenberg family until 1614, when it was bought by the [Kinsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinsky) family.[^cite_note-5] Town rights were granted in 1570.[^cite_note-history-4]

Along with other parts of the former Austrian Empire, Chřibská became part of [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia) in 1919. From 1938 to 1945, after the [Munich Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich_Agreement), Chřibská was annexed by [Nazi Germany](21212.md) and administered as part of the [Reichsgau Sudetenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reichsgau_Sudetenland). The [expulsion of Germans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expulsion_of_Germans_from_Czechoslovakia) after World War II in 1945–1946 reduced the population.

In 2006, the town status was returned to Chřibská.[^cite_note-6]

## Economy



Waterfront of the Chřibská Kamenice

The oldest running [glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass) production in Europe is located in Horní Chřibská. It was founded shortly after 1500 by the German-Bohemian glass-maker family [Friedrich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_\(Glass-Maker_Family\)).[^cite_note-9]

## Transport

The train station *Chřibská* on the railway line Děčín–[Rumburk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumburk), which serves the town, is located east of the town in the territory in neighbouring [Rybniště](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rybni%C5%A1t%C4%9B).[^cite_note-10]

## Sights

The main landmark of Chřibská is the Church of Saint George. The original Gothic church was rebuilt in the Renaissance style in 1596. Later it was modified in the Gothic style again, and two Baroque chapels were added.[^cite_note-11]

## Notable people

- [Thaddäus Haenke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thadd%C3%A4us_Haenke) (1761–1816), botanist and explorer
