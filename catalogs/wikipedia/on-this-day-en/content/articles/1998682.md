**Balashikha** (Russian: Балашиха, IPA: [\[bəlɐˈʂɨxə\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Russian)) is the most populous [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) in [Moscow Oblast](372315.md), Russia. It is located on the [Pekhorka River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pekhorka_River) 1 kilometer (0.62 mi) east of the [Moscow Ring Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Ring_Road). Population: 520,962 ([2021 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2021Census-7] 215,494 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2010Census-4] 147,909 ([2002 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Russian_census));[^cite_note-PopCensus-8] 135,841 ([1989 Soviet census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Soviet_census)).[^cite_note-Census1989-9]

## Etymology

In [Finno-Ugric languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finno-Ugric_languages), *Bala-shika* means *land of celebrations, land of laughter and fun.*[^cite_note-10] [Finnic peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnic_peoples) lived in this area before [Slavs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavs).

## Geography

The city is known for its unique river and waterway system. The [Pekhorka River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pekhorka_River) system covers an area of 40 kilometers (25 mi) from north to south and 20 kilometers (12 mi) from east to west, and many small lakes and ponds were created by damming to provide water power for the [cotton mills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_mill) in the 19th century.

## History

Balashikha was established in 1830.[^cite_note-gr-2] It was granted town status in 1939.[^cite_note-11] Several rural hamlets had existed long before on the site of the modern city.

The city stands on the famous [Vladimir Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Highway), which led out of Moscow to the east. This was the route along which convicted criminals were marched to forced labor camps in [Siberia](29639.md). The road was renamed [Gorky Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M7_highway_\(Russia\)) in the Soviet era. The failure of the [Decembrist Revolt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decembrist_Revolt) against [Tsar Nicholas I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsar_Nicholas_I) led to the execution of its ringleaders and the exile of many nobles to Siberia. Soviet-era schoolchildren were told that the prisoners were marched in chains along this road followed by their wives. In truth, the Decembrist prisoners were sent from [St. Petersburg](24320051.md), then the capital of Russia, through Yaroslavl, and not through Moscow and Balashikha, and the story was invented as part of celebrations of the 100th anniversary of the event in 1925.

Between 1830 and 1870, a cotton factory was in operation in the area, with its fabric called Balashikha. A railway station was built at the end of the 19th century, again called Balashikha Station.

As it grew, Balashikha absorbed other villages, including Gorenki, a suburban estate of Count [Andreas Razumovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreas_Razumovsky), and Pekhra-Yakovlevskoye, an estate of the Princes [Galitzine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Golitsyn), the latter being in use for 250 years from 1591 to 1828. This is the site of a stone church, built from 1777 to 1782.

Saltykovka, a part of Balashikha, has long been known for its attractions to the artistic community. [Isaak Levitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaak_Levitan), the famous landscape painter, lived there in 1879. [Lev Tolstoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lev_Tolstoy) was another frequent visitor.

### Soviet period

Several institutions were founded in Balashikha after the [October Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_Revolution), including one dedicated to the production of fur.

During the Soviet era, Balashikha became a major industrial center with industries in metallurgy, aviation industry, [cryogenic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryogenics) technology, machinery, and other fields.

Balashikha sent many of its sons to the front to fight the Germans during World War II. Among those who fought and died was [Ivan Flerov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Flerov) who commanded a [Katyusha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katyusha_rocket_launcher) rocket division and is remembered by several monuments and museums in the area.

Along with many other [Russian Orthodox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Orthodox) Churches, the Cathedral of Saint [Alexander Nevsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Nevsky) was demolished by the government. The cathedral was blown up in the 1960s but was rebuilt, on its original site, in 2002. Additionally, Afghan operatives of the [KhAD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KHAD) agency were sent to Balashikha for training under the [Democratic Republic of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Afghanistan).[^cite_note-12]

### Modernity

The Balashikha Maternity House was designated on July 1, 2003, to be the Moscow Oblast Perinatal Center. This facility will now function as a regional perinatal care facility for high-risk mothers and infants and a perinatal health education center for Moscow Oblast.

Although not part of the extensive Moscow subway system, Balashikha is home to many office workers who commute to Moscow each day. It has several markets and retail centers and is quickly modernizing, and is surrounded by woodlands and countryside.

In January 2015, the city of [Zheleznodorozhny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheleznodorozhny,_Moscow_Oblast) was abolished with its territory merged into Balashikha.

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), it is, together with twelve [rural localities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia), incorporated as **Balashikha [City Under Oblast Jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance)**—an administrative unit with the status equal to that of the [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Moscow_Oblast).[^cite_note-Ref1406-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), Balashikha City Under Oblast Jurisdiction is incorporated as **Balashikha Urban Okrug**.[^cite_note-Ref1569-3]

In the past, Balashikha served as the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of [Balashikhinsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balashikhinsky_District). On January 1, 2011, the district was abolished.

## Sport

The 2017 national [rink bandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rink_bandy) cup took place in Balashikha.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

Balashikha is a home to [FC Balashikha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Balashikha), [Meteor Balashikha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteor_Balashikha?action=edit\&redlink=1)[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] and [Olimp-SKOPA Zheleznodorozhny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olimp-SKOPA_Zheleznodorozhny?action=edit\&redlink=1)[^cite_note-18] football teams.&#x20;

Ice Palace [Balashikha Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balashikha_Arena) was opened in 2007. From 2007 to 2010 it was a home venue for [HC MVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HC_MVD),[^cite_note-19] that took part in [Kontinental Hockey League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kontinental_Hockey_League) and from 2019 to 2022 it hosted [Avangard Omsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avangard_Omsk)[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] that won [Gagarin Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gagarin_Cup) in 2020-21 season.[^cite_note-22]

## Culture



[Balashikha radio tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Radio_Centre_13) - 300-meters mast left and 176-meters tower on the right

The city is home to several music schools, including the [Sviridov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgy_Sviridov) School of Arts. Attractions include the [Balashikha Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balashikha_Arena) and [Moscow Radio Center 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Radio_Centre_13).

## Military

Balashikha is the site of a large [Russian Army](1094752.md) base and was closed to foreigners during the Soviet era, a ban which, in theory, remains to the present day. It was the headquarters of the 1st Corps of the [Soviet Air Defense Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Air_Defense_Forces) and is now to become the headquarters of the Operational-Strategic Command for Missile-Space Defense. Balashikha is also a base for [ODON](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ODON) ([Internal security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_security) division). Balashikha is home to Military Unit 35690, which is a training facility used by the [Federal Security Service](385990.md).[^cite_note-23]
