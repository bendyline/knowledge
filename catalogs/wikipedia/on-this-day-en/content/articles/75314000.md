A significant number of attacks on healthcare facilities occurred during the [Gaza war](74998519.md) and [genocide](75668427.md). During the first week of the war, there were 94 attacks on health care facilities in Israel and Gaza, killing 29 healthcare workers and injuring 24.[^cite_note-nbcnews20231019-1] The attacks on healthcare facilities contributed to a severe [humanitarian crisis in Gaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_humanitarian_crisis_\(2023%E2%80%93present\)).[^cite_note-2] By 30 November, the [World Health Organization](33583.md) documented 427 attacks on healthcare in the West Bank and Gaza Strip, resulting in 566 fatalities and 758 injuries.[^cite_note-3] By February 2024, it was reported that "every hospital in Gaza is either damaged, destroyed, or out of service due to lack of fuel."[^cite_note-4] By April, WHO had verified 906 attacks on healthcare in Gaza, the West Bank, Israel, and Lebanon.[^cite_note-5] As of June 2024, according to [WHO](33583.md), Israel has carried out 464 attacks on health care facilities, resulting in the death of 727 health care workers, injury of 933 health care workers, and damaging or destroying 113 ambulances.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

Each side has been accused of [committing war crimes](75113830.md) in their attacks. [CNN](62028.md) quoted the [ICRC](5023832.md) saying that "hospitals are given special protection under international humanitarian law in a time of war, but if militants store weapons there, or use them as a base of fire, then that protection falls away".[^cite_note-cnn1411-8] [Human Rights Watch](46530.md) stated, "The Israeli government has put forward no evidence that would justify stripping hospitals of their special protections."[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] In December 2024, Andrew Cayley of the [International Criminal Court](14880.md) said that Israeli claims about Hamas use of hospitals are "grossly exaggerated".[^cite_note-theguardian20241211-11][^cite_note-newarab20241211-12] On 13 March 2025, a [United Nations](31769.md) investigation concluded that Israel has committed genocidal acts in Gaza by systematically destroying its reproductive healthcare facilities.[^cite_note-ndtv20250313-13]

## Background

Israel is alleged to have broken [medical neutrality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_neutrality), a war crime under the [Geneva Conventions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_Conventions).[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] According to Gaza officials, the IDF deliberately targeted ambulances and health facilities with airstrikes.[^cite_note-CBC_HEALTH-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-ajmedic-18] In a statement, the [Palestine Red Crescent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Red_Crescent) demanded "accountability for this war crime."[^cite_note-ajmedic-18] The [International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies](9720597.md), UNRWA, and [Medecins Sans Frontieres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medecins_Sans_Frontieres) (MSF) reported the deaths of their medical personnel.[^cite_note-ifrc-gaza-19][^cite_note-ajmedic-18]

The Physicians for Human Rights organization stated that a prominent aspect of the [October 7 attacks](74998317.md), was the assault on medical personnel and facilities, during which emergency teams were prevented from reaching the wounded, while some of those attempting to provide care were killed. They also stated that the crimes of those who knowingly murdered them must not be diminished.[^cite_note-PHR1611-20]

The World Health Organization (WHO) reported in December 2023 that there have been 164 attacks against healthcare in Palestine—in the Gaza Strip—since 7 October 2023. As a result of those attacks, 198 Palestinian medical staff, 12 members of the Palestinian Civil Defense, and 103 UNRWA staff have been killed. At the time of the report, 26 hospitals and 52 healthcare centers in Gaza were out of service, with 55 ambulances damaged.[^cite_note-21]

The WHO has provided an updated report that states between 7 October 2023 and 19 September 2024, there have been 492 attacks on healthcare in Gaza with 492 individuals killed. No hospital in Gaza is fully functioning, only 17 of 36 hospitals are partially functioning and the remaining 19 are out of service.[^cite_note-22] In May 2024, *The Washington Post* reported that only 4 of the 36 hospitals in Gaza have not been damaged by munitions, raided by Israeli forces, or ceased operations.[^cite_note-23]

## Attacks in Israel

### Attacks on ambulances and medical crews

Israel's national emergency service reported three employees were killed, four volunteers remain injured, nine ambulances were put out of use due to gunshot and fire damage on the October 7 attacks, and a patient was shot inside an ambulance.[^cite_note-PHR1611-20] Director General of the service said that paramedics' bodies were found with their medical gloves still on, and claimed that Hamas had killed them in the midst of treating the wounded; ambulance drivers were killed while sitting in the drivers' seats, and ambulances were intentionally torched and blown up by Hamas militants to prevent any attempt to save potential victims.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] Several people that were hiding in an abandoned ambulance were killed when it was bombed by Hamas gunmen at the [Nova music festival massacre](75007485.md).[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29] A [Battalion Aid Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battalion_Aid_Station) team took cover under an armored vehicle due to [AK-47](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AK-47) fire on them from the front and from its right side, while they were providing medical care, near [Sufa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sufa,_Israel).[^cite_note-30]

### Medical helicopter hit by rocket debris

On 7 October 2023, an ambulance helicopter was transporting three critically wounded people while rockets were flying overhead. Debris hit its rotors. The helicopter lost its balance and started the helicopter spinning. No casualties were reported.[^cite_note-31]

### Be'eri Clinic

During [Be'eri massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Be'eri_massacre) on 7 October 2023, the local dental clinic became the site of a standoff between attacking militants and the kibbutz's security teams.[^cite_note-Sky-32] Militants eventually stormed the clinic, reportedly killing all of the staff and patients.[^cite_note-WCFWH-33][^cite_note-TOI1410-34]

## Attacks in the Gaza Strip

Further information: [Gaza humanitarian crisis § Healthcare](75277791.md#Healthcare)

See also: [Timeline of the Gaza Strip healthcare collapse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_the_Gaza_Strip_healthcare_collapse) and [Israeli bombing of the Gaza Strip](76139149.md)



Israeli destruction of UNRWA el-Sheikh Radwan health center, February 2024

On 12 October 2023, the [ICRC](5023832.md) stated: "Hospitals in Gaza risk turning into morgues". On 15 October 2023, WHO data showed there had been 48 reported attacks on healthcare facilities in the Gaza Strip, resulting in damage to approximately 24 hospitals and healthcare facilities, including six hospitals.[^cite_note-52] According to the [World Health Organization](33583.md), at least 521 people, including 16 medical workers, had been killed in 137 "attacks on health care" in Gaza by 12 November.[^cite_note-Massive_and_systematic_bombing-53] By 14 November 2023, only one hospital was still operating in the north of Gaza.[^cite_note-Massive_and_systematic_bombing-53] On 7 February 2024, the UN stated that only 4 of its 22 health facilities in Gaza remained operational.[^cite_note-54]



[Al-Shifa Hospital](21218880.md) in Gaza City, destroyed by Israeli bombing, December 21, 2024

The IDF accused Hamas of military operations inside hospitals, including alleged attacks on IDF soldiers, weapon storage, fighters taking shelter, providing support for Hamas tunnels, human shielding, and holding hostages[^cite_note-55] Many of these claims, however, have been debunked under scrutiny from journalists.[^cite_note-56][^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58] According to *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)*: "It's almost impossible to refute every accusation that has been made, but it's clear the intended and actual result of this campaign has been the systematic destruction of the healthcare infrastructure for Palestinians in Gaza".[^cite_note-59] In December 2024, Andrew Cayley, leading the [International Criminal Court investigation in Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Criminal_Court_investigation_in_Palestine) said that Israeli claims about Hamas use of hospitals in Gaza used to justify Israeli attacks on them are "grossly exaggerated".[^cite_note-theguardian20241211-11][^cite_note-newarab20241211-12]

[Doctors Without Borders](20498.md) stated Israel had shown "complete disregard for the protection and safety of medical and humanitarian missions and their staff".[^cite_note-60] As of October 2024, the World Health Organization stated it had verified 516 attacks on healthcare in the Gaza Strip since October 2023.[^cite_note-61]

On 13 March 2025, a [United Nations](31769.md) investigation concluded that Israel has committed genocidal acts in Gaza by systematically destroying its reproductive healthcare facilities while at the same time imposing a siege preventing necessary medications for deliveries, pregnancies and neonatal care, causing an "irreversible" harm to the reproductive prospects of Palestinians in Gaza. The investigation said the destruction "was a measure intended to prevent births among Palestinians in Gaza, which is a genocidal act".[^cite_note-ndtv20250313-13]

On the same day, Israel bombed the [Gaza European Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_European_Hospital), killing Hamas leader [Muhammed Sinwar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammed_Sinwar) and his companions who were holding a meeting in a bunker. Sources from Gaza-based factions reported that "the location did in fact contain a tunnel system".[^cite_note-Aawsat20250515-62]

### Al-Ahli Arab Hospital

Main article: [Al-Ahli Arab Hospital explosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ahli_Arab_Hospital_explosion)



Aftermath of the Al-Ahli Arab hospital explosion, 18 October 2023

On 14 October 2023, according to a statement by the [Archbishop of Canterbury](2345.md), [Justin Welby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Welby), an Israeli rocket had damaged the upper two floors of the hospital's cancer treatment center, which contained the ultrasound and mammography wards, and injured four staff members.[^cite_note-anglicannews-63][^cite_note-Boorstein-64][^cite_note-archbishopofcanterbury-65][^cite_note-GuardianLiveBlog-66]

On 17 October 2023, an explosion took place in the parking lot of the courtyard of [al-Ahli Arab Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ahli_Arab_Hospital) in Gaza City during the war, resulting in a large number of fatalities and injuries among displaced Palestinians seeking shelter there.

Initial report suggested that an errant rocket launched from within Gaza hitting a parking lot near a hospital was the most probable cause.[^cite_note-67] However, investigations from [Channel 4 News](273621.md), [Le Monde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Monde), and [The New York Times](30680.md) called this explanation into question and showed that multiple videos used as evidence were unlikely to depict the supposed rocket or even the attack itself.[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69][^cite_note-70][^cite_note-71][^cite_note-72] In its investigation on 20 October 2023, [Forensic Architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forensic_Architecture) concluded that the blast was the result of a "munition fired from the direction of Israel", and in subsequent visual investigations published on 15 February 2024 and 17 October 2024, with the latter including situated testimony from doctors, it cast further doubt on the errant rocket launch theory.[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-FA23-74][^cite_note-75]

On 18 December 2023, the [al-Ahli Arab Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ahli_Arab_Hospital) was attacked by Israeli soldiers, with displaced people forced out and two doctors arrested.[^cite_note-76] The following day, the al-Ahli Hospital director stated Israeli troops arrested doctors, patients, and medical staff, partially destroyed the building's grounds, leaving the hospital unable to receive patients. Four people from the 18 December attacks on the hospital died.[^cite_note-77]

On 13 April 2025, Israel bombed a part of the hospital, taking out its emergency department.[^cite_note-78]

### Al-Shifa Hospital siege

Main articles: [Alleged military use of al-Shifa hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alleged_military_use_of_al-Shifa_hospital) and [Al-Shifa Hospital siege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Shifa_Hospital_siege)

Al-Shifa Hospital was under siege and bombing during the war. The hospital has been completely cut off from the rest of Gaza City, trapping 15,000 people who are either patients in need of treatment or have taken refuge in the hospital grounds.[^cite_note-79][^cite_note-80] Israel [claims it is used as a military base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alleged_military_use_of_al-Shifa_hospital) by [Hamas](13913.md), a claim that Hamas denies.[^cite_note-81][^cite_note-82] Analysis from *[BBC](19344654.md)*, *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent)*, and *[The Washington Post](102226.md)* found Israel's claims of a Hamas base at al-Shifa to be "unconvincing".[^cite_note-BBC_2023-11-17-83][^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85] But later examination by *[The New York Times](30680.md)* suggest Hamas did store weapons and took cover at the hospital, using tunnels 213 meters long, twice the size previously known. The tunnels included bunkers, living areas, and computer and communications rooms, and established documents showed that Hamas masked its activities using the hospital. The Times also verified that the tunnel was under the surgery center. The analysis confirmed that "The Israeli military, however, has struggled to prove that Hamas maintained a command-and-control center under the facility. Critics of the Israeli military say the evidence does not support its early claims, noting that it had distributed material before the raid showing five underground complexes and also had said the tunnel network could be reached from wards inside a hospital building."[^cite_note-86] In November 2023 the tunnels were destroyed by IDF while humanitarian activities continued in the hospital without interruption.[^cite_note-87]

On 4 February 2024, Israeli surveillance and [quadcopter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadcopter) drones fired on people sheltering inside the hospital.[^cite_note-88]

### Indonesian Hospital

On 20 November 2023, as part of the [Israeli invasion of the Gaza Strip](74998519.md), the IDF completely surrounded and besieged the hospital.[^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90] A report by [Al-Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Haq) found that the [Indonesia Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia_Hospital) had been the one targeted most persistently by the IDF.[^cite_note-91] In June 2024, footage showed Israeli forces had destroyed the Noura al-Kaabi specialised dialysis centre at the Indonesian Hospital.[^cite_note-92] On 18 May 2025, the hospital was attacked by Israeli drones.[^cite_note-93]

### Al-Quds Hospital

The Palestinian Red Crescent reported that Al-Quds Hospital in Gaza City was warned by IDF to immediately evacuate. The [World Health Organization](33583.md) has expressed its deep concern over the report.[^cite_note-94] On 19 January 2024, the Palestinian Red Crescent reported they were working to clean and repair Al-Quds after it sustained damage from Israeli attacks, stating, "The hospital suffered a fire, and a destruction of all its medical equipments and contents."[^cite_note-95] On 9 February, Palestine Red Crescent Society stated the hospital had sustained severe damage from strikes by Israeli tanks.[^cite_note-96]

### Al-Amal Hospital

#### January 2024

On 26 January 2024, snipers were reported around the vicinity of the hospital.[^cite_note-97] Two brothers were shot by the snipers despite carrying a white flag.[^cite_note-98] A 28-year-old was reportedly killed in the hospital compound on 28 January.[^cite_note-99] Three people were killed and four injured on 29 January when they tried to move the body of a woman from the hospital.[^cite_note-100] On 30 January, the [Palestinian Red Crescent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Red_Crescent) reported that IDF tanks had raided the yard at al-Amal Hospital, forced displaced people to evacuate immediately.[^cite_note-101] On 31 January, the Palestinian Red Crescent reported Israeli forces had raided the hospital square and were firing heavily.[^cite_note-102] Satellite analysis showed extensive damage to the area around the hospital, and 20 Israeli army vehicles located 500 metres (1,640 feet) west of the hospital building.[^cite_note-103] Israeli forces again raided the hospital on 31 January, and a [quadcopters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadcopter) shot and killed a security guard.[^cite_note-104]

#### February 2024

### Al Basma IVF center

In December 2023, a single strike on Gaza City's [Al Basma IVF center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Basma_IVF_center?action=edit\&redlink=1), Gaza's largest IVF centre, destroyed 4000 human embryos, this was most of the frozen [IVF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IVF) Embryos in the Gaza Strip.[^cite_note-Haaretz_-_embryos-232][^cite_note-PET_IVF-233][^cite_note-SMH_IVF-234] Even though the strike occurred in December 2023 it was mostly ignored until the story was published by [Reuters](18998750.md) in late April 2024.[^cite_note-235][^cite_note-Reuters_-_embryos-236][^cite_note-Guardian_mourn_miracle-237]

The bombing destroyed 4000 human embryos and an additional 1000 samples of frozen sperms and eggs.[^cite_note-Reuters_-_embryos-236][^cite_note-Telly_India_-_5000-238] The embryos were stored in liquid nitrogen, which did not require electricity to maintain; they only needed to be topped up once per month.[^cite_note-Haaretz_-_embryos-232] But the blast blew the lid off 5 tanks, causing the liquid to quickly evaporate, which caused the embryos to defrost and die.[^cite_note-Reuters_-_embryos-236] There are 9 or more clinics in the Gaza Strip that perform IVF related procedures, but most of the embryos were stored in 5 liquid nitrogen tanks at the Al Basma center.[^cite_note-Haaretz_-_embryos-232] The clinic was established in 1997 by obstetrician and gynecologist Bahaeldeen Ghalayini.[^cite_note-Haaretz_-_IVF_1_shell-239] Bahaeldeen Ghalayini said a single Israeli shell struck the part of the centre where there embryos were stored on the ground floor, but he said he did not know if the lab had been specifically targeted by the strike.[^cite_note-Haaretz_-_IVF_1_shell-239]

Dr Bahaeldeen Ghalayini told Reuters that, *"At least half of the couples – those who can no longer produce sperm or eggs to make viable embryos – will not have another chance to get pregnant"*.[^cite_note-PET_IVF-233] One of these people was a 45-year-old woman, whose 19-year-old only child died in a bombing near [Jabalia refugee camp](18169472.md) in 2022.[^cite_note-ABC_IVF-240]

Gaza's government health ministry funded IVF treatment for a limited number of couples.[^cite_note-241] In 2017 [The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian) described this as a, "goodwill gesture by the ruling Hamas party".[^cite_note-242]

Since 2004, fertility clinics in both Gaza and the [West Bank](33209.md) have been having [Palestinian prisoners of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_prisoners_of_Israel) become parents by using sperm smuggled out of prisons, for [security prisoners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_prisoners) who are denied conjugal visits with their wives.[^cite_note-IVF_Ethics_paper-243] Palestinians viewed this as a victory over the “oppressing Israeli authorities”.[^cite_note-IVF_Ethics_paper-243]

On 13 March 2025, a UN investigation found that Israeli forces intentionally attacked and destroyed Al-Basma [IVF Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IVF), the main in-vitro fertility clinic in Gaza which served 2,000-3,000 patients a month, including all its reproductive materials stored for future Palestinian babies. No evidence that the building was used for military purposes was found.[^cite_note-ndtv20250313-13] According to [Navi Pillay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navi_Pillay), the clinic was a standalone building not connected to other hospital buildings. Pillay cited the attack as one piece of evidence that the [UNHRC Commission of Inquiry on Gaza genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNHRC_Commission_of_Inquiry_on_Gaza_genocide) used to find that Israel was committing genocide in Gaza.[^cite_note-244]

## UN Aid workers and other medical personnel



On 27 October, the [United Nations General Assembly](31957.md) passed [Resolution ES-10/21 calling for](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_General_Assembly_Resolution_ES-10/21) an "immediate and sustained" humanitarian truce and cessation of hostilities.

Further information: [Killing of health workers in the Gaza war](75657910.md)

Medical personnel have taken a heavy toll in the conflict, as many doctors have been killed by Israeli airstrikes,[^cite_note-254] and attacks by the [Israeli army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_army).[^cite_note-255]

According to an update from the UN World Health Organization (WHO), doctors in Gaza have been conducting surgeries without the use of anesthesia or other essential surgical resources. The report highlights that fuel has now become the most crucial commodity in the region. Dr. Rick Brennan, the Emergencies Director for the Eastern Mediterranean Region at the UN health agency (WHO), expressed the urgent need for continuous, expanded, and safeguarded humanitarian operations.[^cite_note-256]

### UN health agency

The World Health Organization (WHO) has reported a significant increase in cases of diarrhoea in Gaza, with a 66 percent rise among children under five and a 55 percent increase in the rest of the population; although these figures may not accurately reflect the full extent of the situation due to incomplete information and the collapsing health system in the region. Doctors and aid workers have expressed concerns about the potential for epidemics in this dire humanitarian situation. The inhabitants of the Gaza Strip, who are currently enduring the onslaught of Israel's bombings and gunfire, are now confronted with the escalation of illnesses due to the torrential winter rains that have inundated their temporary dwellings. Moreover, they are grappling with a severe scarcity of nourishment and safe drinking water.[^cite_note-AJ1512-257]

In its latest report on conditions in Gaza, the UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs mentioned the WHO has reported diverse cases of the items such as: meningitis, chickenpox, jaundice and upper respiratory tract infections.[^cite_note-AJ1512-257]

## Impact

Attacks on healthcare facilities have had devastating consequences for the population.[^cite_note-nbcnews20231019-1] Hospitals in Gaza are facing severe shortages of medical supplies amid Israel's [blockade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Israeli_blockade_of_the_Gaza_Strip). Hospitals are running on external generators – a backup that is heading into its last few hours. Israel has cut off water, electricity and fuel supplies, leaving 2.3 million people stranded.[^cite_note-258] The [World Health Organization](33583.md) has stressed the urgent need to end the violence and create a humanitarian corridor in the [Gaza Strip](12047.md).[^cite_note-259][^cite_note-260]

Because of the fuel shortage, Gaza's health facilities are not able to run generators to deliver lifesaving healthcare, it is impossible for ambulances to run, water desalination plants cannot function, as well as for garbage disposal, and other problems.[^cite_note-261]

According to a recent report by the United Nations, water consumption in Gaza has plummeted by 92% compared to pre-conflict levels. This decline is alarming considering the already dire state of the water supply in Gaza, which has been catastrophic since before 7 October 2023. The situation worsened on 4 November and 5 November 2023, when seven water desalination facilities across the Gaza Strip were directly targeted and suffered significant damage. Among the affected infrastructure were three sewage pipelines in Gaza City, two water reservoirs in Gaza City, Rafah, and Jabalia refugee camp, as well as two water wells in Rafah. Given that it is currently winter in Gaza and heavy rainfall is not uncommon, the Gaza municipality has issued a warning about the imminent risk of sewage flooding.[^cite_note-262]

## Reactions

Health officials and medical organizations in the Gaza Strip accused Israel of deliberately bombing ambulances and health facilities in the besieged enclave. And said that these attacks are considered a violation of international laws that include such attacks in the list of war crimes.[^cite_note-nation20231112-263]

The [Joint Arab-Islamic Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Islamic_extraordinary_summit_2023) urged the [International Criminal Court](14880.md)'s prosecutor to "thoroughly investigate the war crimes and crimes against humanity committed by Israel against the Palestinians."[^cite_note-nation20231112-263]

[Human Rights Watch](46530.md) (HRW) said the attack on an ambulance near al-Shafa Hospital should be investigated as a possible war crime.[^cite_note-264] The UN Special Rapporteur on the right to health, [Tlaleng Mofokeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tlaleng_Mofokeng), stated "Protected sites under international human rights law and international humanitarian law have been bombed and attacked".[^cite_note-265]

President [Recep Tayyip Erdogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recep_Tayyip_Erdogan) has said that Turkey has "crossed out" Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) and will refer Israel's human rights abuses and war crimes to the [International Criminal Court](14880.md) (ICC).[^cite_note-266][^cite_note-267][^cite_note-268]

The [Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_International_Commission_of_Inquiry_on_the_Occupied_Palestinian_Territory,_including_East_Jerusalem,_and_Israel) found that Israel's destruction of Gaza's healthcare system constituted war crimes and the [crime against humanity](61099.md) of [extermination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extermination_\(crime\)).[^cite_note-269] Adele Khodr, the Regional Director of [UNICEF](19698110.md) Middle East and North Africa, emphasized that the rights of children to life and health are being violated. She stressed that the laws of war require the protection of hospitals and the provision of life-saving medical supplies, both of which are urgently needed. In addition to the already burdened medical facilities in the middle and southern areas of the Gaza Strip, there is now the added challenge of providing healthcare to the hundreds of thousands of people who have recently arrived and are residing in densely populated areas. It is crucial to provide the necessary support and reinforcement to these existing services in order to effectively address the growing challenges they are facing.[^cite_note-unicef.org-218]

[Mohamed Elmasry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Elmasry), a Canadian engineering professor at the [Doha Institute for Graduate Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doha_Institute_for_Graduate_Studies), stated, "For four and a half months Israel has been seeking to make life unlivable for Palestinians, and part of that operation is attacking hospitals... What Israel has been doing is part of the '[Greater Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Israel)' plan. They will not finish until they ethnically cleanse Gaza."[^cite_note-270]

Doctors from the US, UK and France who were involved in humanitarian missions in Gaza in recent months accused Israeli Defense Forces of systematically targeting healthcare facilities in Gaza and "dismantling the whole healthcare system". Professor [Nick Maynard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Maynard), formerly director for cancer services at [Oxford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University), said: "It's not just about targeting the buildings, it's about systematically destroying the infrastructure of the hospitals. Destroying the oxygen tanks at the [al-Shifa hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Shifa_hospital), deliberately destroying the [CT scanners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CT_scanner) and making it much more difficult to rebuild that infrastructure. If it was just targeting Hamas militants, why are they deliberately destroying the infrastructure of these institutions?" Maynard said he had witnessed the indiscriminate killing of countless innocent civilians and had performed operations continuously for a fortnight, far more often on women than on men. He recalled seeing appalling burns and amputations on children, including a girl who had such severe burns that her facial bones were visible: "We knew there was no chance of her surviving that but there was no morphine to give her. So not only was she inevitably going to die but she would die in agony." In fact, Maynard added, the girl died lying on the hospital floor, as there was not even a bed available for her.[^cite_note-271][^cite_note-272][^cite_note-273] In May 2024, more than 30 states condemned Israel's attacks on Gaza's health system.[^cite_note-274]
