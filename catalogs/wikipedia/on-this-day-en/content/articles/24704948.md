**Donghai County** ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): [东海](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E4%B8%9C%E6%B5%B7)[县](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%8E%BF); [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): [東海](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%9D%B1%E6%B5%B7)[縣](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E7%B8%A3); [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Dōnghǎi Xiàn*; lit. 'east sea') is under the administration of [Lianyungang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lianyungang), [Jiangsu](166412.md) province, China. It borders the [prefecture-level cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefecture-level_cities) of [Linyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linyi) ([Shandong](105032.md)) to the north and [Xuzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xuzhou) to the west.

The county has 300 million tons of quartz and 300,000 tons of rock crystal reserves, which is the highest in China, so it is nicknamed "the county of rock crystal".[^cite_note-2]

&#x20;The crystal coffin of pre-president of China, [Mao Zedong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mao_Zedong) was unearthed here.[^cite_note-3]
