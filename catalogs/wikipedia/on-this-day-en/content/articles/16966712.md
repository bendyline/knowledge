# Argentina at the FIFA World Cup

For the women's team, see [Argentina at the FIFA Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina_at_the_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup).



Argentina's starting lineup against [Egypt](1022953.md) at the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md)

This is a record of [Argentina](454699.md)'s results at the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md). Argentina is one of the most successful teams in the tournament's history, having won three World Cups. Argentina won its first title as a host country in [1978](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_FIFA_World_Cup), defeating the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_national_football_team) 3–1 in the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_FIFA_World_Cup_final). In [1986](157231.md), Argentina won their second World Cup title after defeating [West Germany](250204.md) 3–2 in the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_FIFA_World_Cup_final). In 2022, Argentina won their third World Cup, after winning a penalty shoot-out against France following a 3–3 draw in the [final](70432805.md). Argentina has also been runner-up four times, in [1930](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930_FIFA_World_Cup), [1990](157232.md), [2014](656933.md), and [2026](36581929.md).

In nineteen World Cup tournaments, Argentina has 54 victories in 96 matches. The team has been present in all but four of the World Cups, ranking behind only [Brazil](149286.md) and [Germany](250204.md) in total number of appearances.

### By confederation

[FIFA](11049.md) delegates regional qualification and other organizational affairs to continental conferences, with six different groups separated by geographical location. Argentina plays in [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL), the confederation for South American teams. Due to World Cup rules that state two teams from the same confederation cannot be in the same group for the first round (with the exception of European [UEFA](32332.md) sides), Argentina commonly faces teams from outside their region, including Europe, Africa and Asia. However, the Argentine national team has never played against an Oceanian ([OFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceania_Football_Confederation)) side at the World Cup.

Main article: [1930 FIFA World Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930_FIFA_World_Cup_final)

### 1978 v Netherlands

Main article: [1978 FIFA World Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_FIFA_World_Cup_final)

Argentina hosted the 1978 edition of the World Cup and reached the final for a second time. Their opponents [the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_national_football_team) had already played the previous [1974 final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_FIFA_World_Cup_final) in West Germany, and also lost to the hosts.

[Diego Maradona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Maradona) was 17 years old at this point and already a star in his home country, but did not make the squad as coach [César Luis Menotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9sar_Luis_Menotti) felt he was too inexperienced to handle the pressure of a major tournament. The playmaker position was instead filled by [Mario Kempes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Kempes), who became the first Argentinian to win the [Golden Ball](3482503.md#Golden_Ball) in addition to being the tournament's top scorer with six goals. The Dutch side was missing a superstar of their own: [Johan Cruyff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johan_Cruyff) did not join the 1978 World Cup squad due to the aftermath of a kidnapping attempt which occurred in 1977; he only disclosed this information 30 years later.[^cite_note-7]

The closely contested match was influenced by a hostile atmosphere and ended with the Dutch players refusing to attend the award ceremony after Argentina won the title in extra time.

### 2022 v France

Main article: [2022 FIFA World Cup final](70432805.md)

After reaching their sixth World Cup final, Argentina took the lead against [France](250197.md) through captain [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) from the penalty spot, and doubled their advantage with an [Ángel Di María](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81ngel_Di_Mar%C3%ADa) goal in the 36th minute.[^cite_note-10] France made two substitutions in the first half, but went into half-time trailing 0–2.[^cite_note-auto-11] Despite not having a shot until after the 80th minute, France were awarded a penalty after [Randal Kolo Muani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randal_Kolo_Muani) was brought down in the area. [Kylian Mbappé](48711857.md) would convert the penalty for France, and added a second goal less than two minutes later to level the score.[^cite_note-auto-11] Since regulation time ended with the score 2–2, the match went to extra time. Messi again scored for Argentina in 108th minute to restore Argentina's lead. However, Mbappé was then awarded a second penalty in the 118th minute after his initial shot hit the arm of Argentine defender [Gonzalo Montiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonzalo_Montiel). Mbappé converted the penalty to again tie the game, becoming the second ever player to score a hat-trick in the final of a men's World Cup.[^cite_note-auto-11] With extra time concluding and the score at 3–3, the match went to a penalty shoot-out; Argentina emerged victorious after scoring all of their penalties, winning the shoot-out 4–2.[^cite_note-auto-11]

## Head coaches

Throughout the team's history, several coaches have managed the Argentina national side with different football styles and ranges in offensive and defensive tactics. There were two predominant "football schools" taught by Argentina's World Cup-winning coaches: [César Menotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9sar_Menotti), who propagated possession and well-look play, and [Carlos Bilardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Bilardo), who prioritized results and tactical order. These head coaching styles divided Argentine fans kindly.[^cite_note-Bilardo_o_Menotti-20] Other managers such as [Marcelo Bielsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcelo_Bielsa) had their own football style. ## Miscellaneous

Argentina's match against West Germany in 1958 featured [a yellow jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFK_Malm%C3%B6) instead of the traditional light blue and white colors or blue as alternate. This was because both teams wore white jerseys, creating confusion. As the South American side forgot to bring a second jersey, they decided to borrow jerseys from local team club Malmö. Germany won 3–1.[^cite_note-Malmö-21]

In the [1978 France–Hungary match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_1#France_vs_Hungary) something similar occurred. Both teams arrived to the match with white jerseys, so France was forced to borrow jerseys from [Mar del Plata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mar_del_Plata)'s local club [Kimberley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimberley_de_Mar_del_Plata). France wore striped green and white keeping the traditional blue shorts and red socks. France won 3–1.[^cite_note-White_jersey-22]

The stadium [José María Minella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_Jos%C3%A9_Mar%C3%ADa_Minella) is the southernmost World Cup venue located at [38°0′0″S 57°33′0″W  /  38.00000°S 57.55000°W  / -38.00000; -57.55000](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Argentina_at_the_FIFA_World_Cup\&params=38_0_0_S_57_33_0_W_). Including France vs. Hungary, several matches of the 1978 World Cup were played in this stadium. Mar del Plata is in the south of [Buenos Aires Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires_Province).[^cite_note-Southernmost-23]

## Referees

As a major [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL) member, Argentina has been represented by match officials in nearly every tournament. In 2006, [Horacio Elizondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horacio_Elizondo) refereed the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup_final) between [France](250197.md) and [Italy](362466.md), where he sent off [Zinedine Zidane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinedine_Zidane) after a headbutt to an opponent. In 2018, [Néstor Pitana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A9stor_Pitana) did it when France played versus [Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatia_national_football_team). They are also the only two who refereed the opening game in the same tournament.
