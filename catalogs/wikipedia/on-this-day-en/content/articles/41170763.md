The **National Electoral Council** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Consejo Nacional Electoral*) is the highest electoral authority, both at the administrative and jurisdictional level in the Republic of [Honduras](13394.md). It was created through constitutional reforms in January 2019, supplanting the previous **Supreme Electoral Tribunal** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Tribunal Supremo Electoral*).

The Council alongside the **Electoral Justice Tribunal** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Tribunal de Justicia Electoral*) deal with everything related to electoral acts and procedures. Integration, organization and operation are governed by the precepts of the [Constitution of Honduras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Honduras) and the Electoral and Political Organizations.[^cite_note-1]
