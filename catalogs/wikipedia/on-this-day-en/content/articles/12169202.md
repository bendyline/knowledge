The **Jezzine District** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): قضاء جزين) is a district in the [South Governorate](1283570.md) of [Lebanon](17771.md). The capital is [Jezzine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jezzine).
