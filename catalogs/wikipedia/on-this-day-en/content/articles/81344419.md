# 2025 Dhaka garment factory fire

| Time | c. 11\:00 a.m. – 12\:00 p.m. [BST](9544593.md) |
| --- | --- |
| Location | [Mirpur Thana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirpur_Thana), [Dhaka](56656.md), Bangladesh |
| Type | [Fire](11145.md), [chemical accident](1916689.md) |
| Cause | Under investigation |
| Deaths | 16 |
| Injuries | Multiple |
| Property damage | [Garment factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garment_factory) Chemical [warehouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warehouse) |

On 14 October 2025, a fire broke out on the third floor of a [garment factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garment_factory) nearby a chemical [warehouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warehouse) located in the [Mirpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirpur_Thana) area of [Dhaka](56656.md), killing at least 16 people and injuring several others. The blaze was the deadliest since 2022, when a [container depot caught fire and exploded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Sitakunda_fire) in [Sitakunda Upazila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitakunda_Upazila), [Chittagong District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chittagong_District), killing at least 47 people.[^cite_note-r-1][^cite_note-b-2][^cite_note-a-3][^cite_note-n-4]

## Background

Bangladesh has the world's second largest garment industry after [China](5405.md), making up over 10% of the [country's gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_of_Bangladesh), earning about \$40 billion a year from exports, mainly to the [United States](3434750.md) and [Europe](9239.md), and employing approximately 4 million workers, mostly women.[^cite_note-r-1][^cite_note-a-3][^cite_note-n-4] Since the [Rana Plaza collapse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rana_Plaza_collapse) in 2013, the most fatal industrial accident in the [history of Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Bangladesh), which caused 1,134 deaths and whose building maintained several factories, the working conditions have been improved.[^cite_note-n-4]

Large fires are relatively common in the [densely populated country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Bangladesh), frequently attributed to lax safety standards and inadequate infrastructure.[^cite_note-a-3] Hundreds of people have been lost their lives in similar incidents over the decades.[^cite_note-b-2]

## Fire

According to eyewitness, the fire occurred between 11 a.m. and midday [local time](9544593.md), on the third floor of a four-storey factory, spreading to a close by chemical warehouse storing [chlorine powder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlorine_powder), [plastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic) and [hydrogen peroxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_peroxide). All 16 bodies were found on the second and third floors and burned beyond recognition.[^cite_note-r-1][^cite_note-b-2]

Local media reported that up until 9 p.m., the warehouse continued to burn, despite the blaze at the factory had been extinguished after three hours.[^cite_note-b-2] Fire service director Mohammad Tajul Islam Chowdhury said the victims probably died "instantly" after inhaling "[highly toxic gas](22387613.md)"."The chemical explosion caused a flashover that released toxic gas, leaving many [unconscious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unconsciousness) and trapping them inside. They couldn't escape either upward or downward," he said. It was unclear what chemicals were housed in the warehouse.[^cite_note-r-1][^cite_note-b-2] He added that neither the factory nor the warehouse had any [fire safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_safety) plan.[^cite_note-r-1]

Preliminary analysis showed the factory had a tin roof with a locked grilled door, impeding the workers to reach the upper level.[^cite_note-r-1]

## Reactions

Outside the buildings, family members were seen gathered waiting for information, with many of them holding photos of the relatives.[^cite_note-r-1][^cite_note-b-2][^cite_note-a-3][^cite_note-n-4]

The [chief adviser](9219750.md) [Muhammad Yunus](548105.md)-led interim [government of Bangladesh](18420484.md) expressed its condolences and urged authorities to investigate and support victims and families.[^cite_note-r-1]

As part of the investigation, police and military officers have begun searches to locate the owners of the factory and the warehouse.[^cite_note-b-2][^cite_note-r-1]
