# Ministry of Economy and Finance (Benin)



Ministry building in Cotonou

The **Ministry of Economy and Finance** (French: *Ministère de l'Économie et des Finances*) is the [government ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_ministry) responsible for governing and managing the [economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy) and the [financial activity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance) of [Benin](3459.md).

The current Minister of Economy and Finance is [Aristide Medenou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristide_Medenou?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-1]
