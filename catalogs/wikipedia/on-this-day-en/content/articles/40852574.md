**Jared Genser** (born June 17, 1972) is an international human rights lawyer who serves as managing director of the law firm Perseus Strategies, LLC, Special Advisor on the [Responsibility to Protect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_to_Protect) to the [Organization of American States](22788.md), and Co-Founder and General Counsel to the Neurorights Foundation.[^cite_note-perseus-strategies-1] Genser is U.S. Chair of the [Raoul Wallenberg Centre for Human Rights](57845825.md), where he was previously a Senior Fellow.[^cite_note-perseus-strategies-1] Referred to by the *New York Times* as "The Extractor",[^cite_note-2] he has served as pro bono counsel to five Nobel Peace Prize Laureates, including the last three Laureates who won their Prize while imprisoned- [Aung San Suu Kyi](2847.md) (Burma, 2006-2010), [Liu Xiaobo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liu_Xiaobo) (China, 2010-2017), and [Ales Bialiatski](31971174.md) (Belaruse, 2023-Present) -- as well as [Archbishop Desmond Tutu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archbishop_Desmond_Tutu) and [Elie Wiesel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elie_Wiesel).[^cite_note-perseus-strategies-1] Other former clients have included former Czech Republic President [Václav Havel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A1clav_Havel), Malaysia Prime Minister [Anwar Ibrahim](19264815.md), Venezuelan politician [Leopoldo López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leopoldo_L%C3%B3pez), and former Maldives President [Mohamed Nasheed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Nasheed). He was previously an associate of the [Carr Center for Human Rights Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carr_Center_for_Human_Rights_Policy) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) from 2014 to 2016 and a visiting fellow with the [National Endowment for Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Endowment_for_Democracy) from 2006 to 2007.[^cite_note-3] Coming from his experience freeing a political prisoner as a law student in 2001, he founded the nonprofit [Freedom Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_Now)[^cite_note-4] and earlier in his career was named by the *National Law Journal* as one of "40 Under 40: Washington's Rising Stars".[^cite_note-5]

## Early life and education

Genser was born in [New Haven, Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Haven,_Connecticut), and grew up in suburban Maryland. He was a participant in the Raoul Wallenberg Scholar program at [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem).[^cite_note-\:0-6][^cite_note-7] Genser received a B.S. from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).[^cite_note-\:0-6] He subsequently earned a Master in Public Policy degree from the [John F. Kennedy School of Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_F._Kennedy_School_of_Government) at Harvard University, where he was an Alumni Public Service Fellow and a J.D. *cum laude* from the [University of Michigan Law School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan_Law_School).[^cite_note-perseus-strategies-1]

## Career

Genser began his career as a management consultant with [McKinsey & Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McKinsey_&_Company).[^cite_note-perseus-strategies-1] He later joined the global law firm [DLA Piper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DLA_Piper), where he was elected a partner.[^cite_note-8] In 2011, he left DLA Piper to found Perseus Strategies, a human-rights law firm.[^cite_note-abajournal-9] Genser has previously been an adjunct professor of law at [Georgetown University Law Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University_Law_Center),[^cite_note-10] the [University of Michigan Law School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan_Law_School) and [University of Pennsylvania Law School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania_Law_School).[^cite_note-perseus-strategies-1] In October 2020, Genser was appointed by OAS Secretary General [Luis Almagro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Almagro) as Special Advisor on the Responsibility to Protect for the Organization of American States.[^cite_note-11]

## Writings

### Books

Genser is the author of *The UN Working Group on Arbitrary Detention: Commentary and Guide to Practice* (Cambridge University Press, 2019; [ISBN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISBN_\(identifier\)) [9781107034457](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special\:BookSources/9781107034457)). He was also co-editor with Canadian Member of Parliament [Irwin Cotler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irwin_Cotler) of *The Responsibility to Protect: The Promise of Stopping Mass Atrocities in Our Time* (Cambridge University Press, 2012; [ISBN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISBN_\(identifier\)) [978-0199797769](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special\:BookSources/978-0199797769)) and he is co-editor with former Minister of Foreign Affairs of Costa Rica [Bruno Stagno Ugarte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Stagno_Ugarte) for *The UN Security Council in the Age of Human Rights* (Cambridge University Press, 2013; [ISBN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISBN_\(identifier\)) [978-1107040076](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special\:BookSources/978-1107040076)).

### Law review articles and op-eds

Genser has published numerous law review articles and more than 130 op-eds on human rights topics in major newspapers around the world. Among law review articles, he has published them on a diverse array of topics including the UN Security Council's application on the responsibility to protect,[^cite_note-12] the Future of the UN Human Rights System,[^cite_note-13] and the EU trade sanctions on Burma, among other topics.&#x20;

He has published op-eds in such newspapers as [The Baltimore Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Baltimore_Sun), [The Boston Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Boston_Globe), [The Chosun Ilbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chosun_Ilbo) (Seoul), [Far Eastern Economic Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_Eastern_Economic_Review), [The Huffington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Huffington_Post),[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] [The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent) (UK), [International Herald Tribune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Herald_Tribune),[^cite_note-17] [The Jakarta Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jakarta_Post), [Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times),[^cite_note-18] [South China Morning Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_China_Morning_Post),[^cite_note-19] [The Sydney Morning Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sydney_Morning_Herald),[^cite_note-20] [The Nation (Thailand)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nation_\(Thailand\)), [The Star (South Africa)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Star_\(South_Africa\)), [The Times (UK)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times_\(UK\)), [The Wall Street Journal](173070.md), [The New York Times](30680.md), and [The Washington Post](102226.md).&#x20;

## Other activities and awards

Genser is a life member of the [Council on Foreign Relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_on_Foreign_Relations) and a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Arts). He was elected in 2008 as a Fellow to the British American Project. In 2009 he was elected as a delegate to the Asia Society's Asia 21 Young Leaders Summit and also as a U.S.-Japan Young Leadership Fellow.[^cite_note-perseus-strategies-1] Genser was a Young Global Leader of the [World Economic Forum](172185.md) from 2008 to 2013 and a Member of the World Economic Forum's Global Agenda Council for Human Rights (2010–2011). In 2011, he was selected among the *Young Leaders* of the [French-American Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French-American_Foundation).[^cite_note-21] He is a recipient of the Charles Bronfman Prize and [Liberty in North Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_in_North_Korea)'s Freedom Fighter Award.[^cite_note-perseus-strategies-1] In addition to being qualified to practice law in Maryland and the District of Columbia, he is also a solicitor of England & Wales.[^cite_note-perseus-strategies-1] Gesner co-founded the [Neurorights Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neurorights_Foundation?action=edit\&redlink=1) with neuroscientist [Rafael Yuste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Yuste) after both won Global Leadership Prizes from the [Tällberg Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%A4llberg_Foundation?action=edit\&redlink=1). [^cite_note-22] Genser is also an avid ice hockey player, playing on both the Titans and the Crunch through Hockey North America.[^cite_note-23]
