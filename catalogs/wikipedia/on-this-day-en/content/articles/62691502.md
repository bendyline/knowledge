**Lee Jong-sup** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 이종섭; [Hanja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanja): 李鐘燮; 20 August 1960) is a South Korean retired [army](1515574.md) [lieutenant general](201916.md) and former vice chairman of the [Joint Chiefs of Staff](14470718.md) who served as the [minister of national defense](5985967.md) from May 2022 to October 2023.

## Ambassador

### Appointment

On March 12, 2024, he was officially inaugurated as the South Korean ambassador to Australia. Lee Jong-sup said in Korean:[^cite_note-2]

> "Since the establishment of their diplomatic relations in 1961, the two countries have developed friendly and cooperative relations in various fields, including in diplomacy, military, defense, economy, culture and people-to-people exchanges, and have cooperated closely together in upholding peace, stability and prosperity on the Korean Peninsula and in the region."

### Resignation

Lee resigned himself as ambassador to Australia on March 29, 2024, and [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korean_President) [Yoon Suk Yeol](62877952.md) approved his resignation the same afternoon.[^cite_note-3]
