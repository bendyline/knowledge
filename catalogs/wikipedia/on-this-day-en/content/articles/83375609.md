On 3 June 2026, fighting erupted in the [Howlwadaag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howlwadaag,_Mogadishu) district of [Mogadishu](60154.md), ahead of planned protests against the extension of disputed president [Hassan Sheikh Mohamud](36973989.md)'s term. Shooting took place near the homes of former prime minister [Hassan Khaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Khaire) and former president [Sharif Sheikh Ahmed](5442210.md), both opposition leaders involved in planning the protests.[^cite_note-1] Khaire stated that government forces attempted to kill him, while police labeled the event as a "large-scale security operation", blaming armed militias for starting the hostilities.[^cite_note-france24-2] Fighting later spread to the districts of [Abdiaziz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdiaziz,_Mogadishu), [Hodan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodan,_Mogadishu), [Warta Nabadda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warta_Nabada,_Mogadishu), and [Kaaraan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karan,_Mogadishu).[^cite_note-3] The [UNHCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNHCR) estimated that the clashes were responsible for 13 deaths, 189 injuries and 12,500 displaced households.[^cite_note-aljazeera5june-4]

Fighting renewed in the morning of 4 June.[^cite_note-france24-2] An agreement to end the fighting in Howlwadaag was reached in the afternoon, following talks between Hassan Khaire and the director of the [National Intelligence and Security Agency](40417792.md).[^cite_note-5] Clashes continued near Sharif Sheikh Ahmed's residence in the Abdiaziz district, ending the next day through mediation by clan elders.[^cite_note-aljazeera5june-4]

## Reactions

United Nations General Secretary [António Guterres](471806.md) condemned the violence, calling all parties to exercise restraint and protect civilians.[^cite_note-6] The [United States diplomatic mission in Mogadishu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_the_United_States,_Mogadishu) called the violence "reckless", requesting peaceful dialogue between the parties.[^cite_note-7]
