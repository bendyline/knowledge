**Yehiel Curry** (born July 26, 1972) is an American [Mainline Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainline_Protestant) prelate who has served as [presiding bishop](1468007.md) of the [Evangelical Lutheran Church in America](248255.md) (ELCA) since October 2025.[^cite_note-1]&#x20;

## Ministry

Before his election as presiding bishop, he served as bishop of the Metropolitan Chicago Synod and prior to that he served as a pastor of Shekinah Chapel Lutheran Church. He was first elected synod bishop in 2019 and was re-elected for a second term in June 2025.&#x20;

In July 2025, he was elected for a six-year term as Presiding Bishop, beginning on October 1, to succeed [Elizabeth Eaton](40278297.md), at the age of 53.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-4][^cite_note-\:2-5] He is the first African-American and person of color to be Presiding Bishop,[^cite_note-\:1-4] as well as being the ELCA's fifth presiding bishop.[^cite_note-6]&#x20;

His [installation](48795115.md), which ceremonially marked the beginning of his term as presiding bishop, took place on October 4, 2025 at Central Lutheran Church in Minneapolis.[^cite_note-\:2-5]&#x20;

## Education

In 1995, Curry graduated from [Lewis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_University), in [Romeoville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romeoville,_Illinois), [Illinois](14849.md), with a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts). In 2013, some years later, he graduated from the [Lutheran School of Theology at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutheran_School_of_Theology_at_Chicago) with a [Master of Divinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Divinity).[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-4]&#x20;

## Personal life

Curry was raised in the [South Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Side,_Chicago) of Chicago as a Roman Catholic. He worked as a public school teacher and was subsequently a social worker.[^cite_note-\:1-4]&#x20;

He is married to LaShonda Curry, an educator in the [Chicago Public Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Public_Schools), and together they have three adult daughters.[^cite_note-\:1-4][^cite_note-7]&#x20;
