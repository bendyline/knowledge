**Child murder**, also known as **pedicide**, **child manslaughter** or **child homicide**, is the [homicide](123451.md) of an individual who is a [minor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_majority). In many legal jurisdictions, it is considered an [aggravated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aggravation_\(law\)) form of homicide. The age of the victim may constitute an aggravated factor for homicide offenses, or child murder may be a stand-alone criminal offense.

## Punishment by jurisdiction



[Albert Fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Fish), a [serial killer](42626.md) known for committing pedicides from the 1920s until his arrest in 1932

### United States

In 2008, there were 1,494 pedicides in the [United States](3434750.md). Among the victims, 1,035 were male and 452 were female.[^cite_note-1]

About half of the states that maintain the [death penalty](412425.md) have included pedicide to their list of aggravating factors that may make a murder punishable by death. The victim's age under which the crime is a capital crime varies between states. The ages are set between 10 and 17, with 12 being the most common age.[^cite_note-2]

Child [manslaughter](7397035.md) can result in an aggravated charge in some jurisdictions such as in the [State of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Florida).[^cite_note-3]

### United Kingdom

Any murder in the [United Kingdom](31717.md) carries a [mandatory life sentence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_imprisonment_in_the_United_Kingdom). In [England](9316.md) and [Wales](69894.md), pedicides involving sexual or sadistic conduct or abduction of the victim can result in a whole life order (i.e. life imprisonment without the possibility of parole) being imposed where the offender is at least 21 years of age. Sentencing guidelines state that where the offender is under 21, the starting point is a minimum term of 30 years.

## By other children



18th century illustration of William York (aged 10) murdering Susan Matthew (aged 5) on 13 May 1748, from [The Newgate Calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Newgate_Calendar)

See also: [List of youngest killers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_youngest_killers)

In most countries, there are very few cases where children are killed by other children. According to the U.S. Department of Justice statistics for 1996, one in five pedicides were committed by other children. Several pedicides have gained prominent media exposure.

One was the killing on 12 February 1993 of the 2-year-old boy [James Bulger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_James_Bulger) by Robert Thompson and Jon Venables who were both 10-years-old in [Bootle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bootle), [Merseyside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merseyside), [England](9316.md). He was beaten and stoned before his corpse was left on train tracks in order to give the impression that a train had hit him. Bulger's killers became the youngest convicted murderers in the modern history of the United Kingdom.

Another notable case in the UK occurred in 1968 in [Newcastle upon Tyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_upon_Tyne), when 10-year-old [Mary Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Bell) was convicted of manslaughter due to diminished responsibility in the deaths of toddlers Martin Brown and Brian Howe. She was released in 1980 at the age of 23.[^cite_note-4]

In 1998, 8-year-old Madelyn Clifton was killed by 14-year-old [Josh Phillips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Phillips_\(murderer\)).

One of the most recent cases occurred in May 2021 in [St. Johns County, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Johns_County,_Florida), when 14-year-old Aiden Fucci fatally stabbed his schoolmate, 13-year-old [Tristyn Bailey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Tristyn_Bailey). Bailey sustained 114 stab wounds in total. In 2023, Fucci pleaded guilty to the murder and was sentenced to life in prison.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## By medical professionals

Children, especially infants, have been killed on purpose by medical professionals. [Katherine Ramsland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Ramsland), an expert in serial killers, believes predatory offenders may view healthcare agencies as "places of trust" where they have advantages. While other killers may decide to reduce the workload, or set someone up out of spite, or get [God complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/God_complex) and think they are [helping their victims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_of_mercy_\(criminology\)), or be seeking attention, self-empowerment, and thrill.[^cite_note-8] In one such case, [neonatal nurse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neonatal_nurse) [Lucy Letby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Letby) was accused of killing at least 7 infants between June 2015 and 2016 at the [Countess of Chester Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countess_of_Chester_Hospital) and attempting to kill a further 10 more.[^cite_note-9] In another case, French anaesthetist Frédéric Péchier was sentenced for murdering or attempting to murder patients as young as 4 years old, from 2008 to 2017, out of jealousy and disdain for his peers.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

## Genocide and child soldiers

Main article: [Children in the military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Children_in_the_military)

The military use of children refers to children being placed in harm's way in military actions, in order to protect a location or provide propaganda.[^cite_note-Children_at_war-14][^cite_note-UNICEF-2007-15] This is sometimes referred to as child sacrifice, though not equivalent to the religious variety. It may also refer to the use of children as child soldiers or saboteurs.

[Red Hand Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Hand_Day) on February 12 is an annual commemoration day to draw public attention to the practice of using children as soldiers in wars and armed conflicts.

## Muti killings

[Medicine murder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine_murder), often referred to as *muti killing*, is a practice of [human sacrifice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_sacrifice) and [mutilation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disfigurement) associated with traditional medicinal practices, such as [muti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muti). Victims of muti killings are often children. Organs and/or body parts are usually taken while the child is still alive. An unknown child (referred to as *[Adam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_\(unsolved_Thames_murder_case\))*), whose decapitated torso was found in the [River Thames](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Thames) in [London](17867.md) in 2002 is believed to have been the victim of a muti killing.[^cite_note-16]
