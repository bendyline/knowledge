[Leicester City F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester_City_F.C.), an [association football](10568.md) club, won the [Premier League](11250.md) in the [2015–16 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_Premier_League) despite being 5000/1 underdogs, an example of an upset.

[^cite_note-1]

An **upset** occurs in a [competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competition), frequently in [electoral politics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_politics) or [sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports), when the party popularly expected to win (the "favorite") is defeated by (or, in the case of sports, ties with) an [underdog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underdog) whom the majority expects to lose, defying the [conventional wisdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conventional_wisdom). It is often used in reference to beating the [betting odds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odds#Gambling_usage) in sports, or beating the [opinion polls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_poll) in electoral politics.

## Origin

The meaning of the word "upset" has long included "an overthrowing or overturn of ideas, plans, etc." (see [OED](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary) definition 6b), from which the sports definition almost surely derived. "Upset" also once referred to "a curved part of a bridle-bit, fitting over the tongue of the horse", (now the port of a [curb bit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curb_bit)), but even though the modern sports meaning of "upset" was first used far more for horse races than for any other competition, there is no evidence of a connection.

In 2002, George Thompson, a lexicographic researcher, used the full-text online search capabilities of *[The New York Times](30680.md)* databases to trace the usage of the verb *to upset* and the noun *upset*. The latter was seen in usage as early as 1877.[^cite_note-2]

## Examples of upsets

### Sports



The [2004 Detroit Pistons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003%E2%80%9304_Detroit_Pistons_season) were considered [underdogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underdog) due to the team entering the [2004 NBA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_NBA_Finals) without a single [NBA All-Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NBA_All-Stars) on their roster. The Pistons defeated [a Los Angeles Lakers team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003%E2%80%9304_Los_Angeles_Lakers_season) that was heavily favored in the series. It remains one of the biggest upsets in [NBA](22093.md) history.

Below is a selection of major upsets from a variety of popular sports around the world. It is not meant to be comprehensive, merely representative.&#x20;

#### American football

- Heading into the [2007 college football season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_NCAA_Division_I_FBS_football_season), the [Michigan Wolverines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Michigan_Wolverines_football_team) were ranked as the pre-season Number 5 team, and among the favorites for that year's [BCS National Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowl_Championship_Series). As an early season tune-up game, Michigan had booked the lower division [Appalachian State Mountaineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Appalachian_State_Mountaineers_football_team) for their [first game of the season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Appalachian_State_vs._Michigan_football_game). The Mountaineers surprised the football world by leading 28–17 at the half. Though Michigan clawed their way back to lead 32–31 late into the fourth quarter, the Mountaineers kicked a field goal with 26 seconds left in the game to take the lead 34–32. Michigan managed to use only 20 seconds of game time to drive the ball down to the App State 27-yard line, and as time was expiring the Mountaineers' [Corey Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corey_Lynch) blocked a Michigan field goal attempt to secure the upset for App State. The game marked only the second time, to that point, that a lower-division school had beaten a top-division AP-ranked team.

  [^cite_note-3]

- [Howard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_University), a [FCS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_I_Football_Championship_Subdivision) team, entered as a 45-point road underdog against [UNLV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nevada,_Las_Vegas) and stunned the Rebels 43–40 in Las Vegas. By point-spread standards, Howard pulled off the biggest upset in modern college football history.

  [^cite_note-4]

- In [Super Bowl III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_III), the senior [National Football League](21211.md) was playing their third interleague championship game against the upstart [American Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Football_League). The NFL had won the prior two matchups without much difficulty, and it looked poised to do so again, as the [Baltimore Colts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_Baltimore_Colts_season), with a 13–1 record, behind [quarterback](25277.md) [Earl Morrall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_Morrall), who led the league in touchdown passes that season and was named NFL Most Valuable Player. The team also had several future Hall of Fame players on the roster, including quarterback [Johnny Unitas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Unitas), relegated to a back-up role following an early-season injury, [tight end](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tight_end) [John Mackey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Mackey_\(American_football\)), as well as a defense led by perennial all-pro [Bubba Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubba_Smith). The [New York Jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Jets) were led by [Joe Namath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Namath) at quarterback, who earlier in the week had "guaranteed" victory against the Colts. Namath's top target, future Hall of Fame wide receiver [Don Maynard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Maynard), was hobbled by an injury, but Namath led the Jets on a run-focused attack that leaned heavily on fullback [Matt Snell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Snell), who ran for 121 yards and scored the Jets' only touchdown. The Jets defense confounded Morrall, who had only six completions on 17 attempts, with three interceptions in the first half, including an interception to the Jets' [Jim Hudson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Hudson) while Colts' star receiver [Jimmy Orr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Orr), uncovered near the end zone, waved his hands to no avail. Colts' head coach [Don Shula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Shula) put the hobbled Unitas in the game in the second half, and despite a late game touchdown, lost the game 16–7.

#### Association football

- The English [FA Cup](11237.md) – a [knockout tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knockout_tournament) which is randomly drawn for each round – is mythologised for frequent occurrences of "giant-killing"; on occasion, "[non-League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-League_football)" teams (i.e. teams not in the top four tiers of the [league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system)) have even beaten teams in the [Premier League](11250.md), or its predecessor, the [First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League_First_Division).

  [^cite_note-times_analysis-6]

  &#x20;As of January 2026, the largest upset in terms of league position was the [National League North](645482.md)'s [Macclesfield](66155008.md) beating the Premier League team and [title holders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_FA_Cup_final) [Crystal Palace](385313.md) [2–1](82078681.md) in the [third round of the 2025–26 FA Cup](79604093.md#Third_round); the teams were separated by five leagues and 117 league positions at the time of the match.

  [^cite_note-7]

  &#x20;Seven second-tier teams have won the FA Cup since the founding of the [Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_League) in 1888, the most recent being [West Ham United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham_United_F.C.) in [1980](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980_FA_Cup_final). One non-league team, [Tottenham Hotspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.) in [1901](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1901_FA_Cup_final), has also won the FA Cup during this time.

  [^cite_note-8]

- [Leicester City F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester_City_F.C.), an English football club, were crowned champions of the [2015–16 Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_Premier_League). The club had narrowly avoided the relegation the previous season by finishing 14th and being in the bottom of the table the most part of the season. At the beginning of the season, bookmakers had given Leicester City odds of 5000/1 against them winning the league. It was the first [English title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_English_football_champions) in their history.

- A major upset in Spanish football was the [Alcorconazo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcorconazo), when in the first leg of a [2009–10 Copa del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009%E2%80%9310_Copa_del_Rey) [AD Alcorcón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_Alcorc%C3%B3n) won over [Real Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_C.F.) 4–0.

  [^cite_note-Alcorconazo-9]

  &#x20;Real Madrid is one of the largest clubs in Spanish football and the world while Alcorcón team played in the third-tier [Segunda División B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segunda_Divisi%C3%B3n_B). Because Real Madrid won the second leg only 1–0, Alcorcón advanced victorious to the next round. The half-time substitution of [Guti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guti) when the score was 3–0 and when he was booked before was another topic in the Spanish press because of words exchanged between the player and his coach, [Manuel Pellegrini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Pellegrini).

First awarded in 1976, it was known as the "Thunder (fear-strike) of the elites" or "Danger of the elites".

#### Baseball

- The [1906 World Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1906_World_Series) looked to be one of the most lopsided matchups in [World Series](23489161.md) history, as the [National League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_\(baseball\)) powerhouse [Chicago Cubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1906_Chicago_Cubs_season), with a record of 116–36, represented the best winning percentage in modern [Major League Baseball](38776.md) history. They faced off against their cross-town rivals, the [Chicago White Sox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1906_Chicago_White_Sox_season), who finished with the [American League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_League) pennant having a record of 93–58. The White Sox were dubbed the "hitless wonders" as their .230 team [batting average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batting_average_\(baseball\)) was not only the worst batting average by a team to win their league pennant, it was the worst overall batting average in all of Major League Baseball that season. Buoyed by a pitching staff that held the Cubs to a [below .200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendoza_Line) batting average for the series, the White Sox showed an uncharacteristic surge of batting prowess in games 5 and 6 with 16 runs on 26 hits over the two games to claim the World Series crown four games to two in what has been called the biggest upset in MLB history.

#### Basketball

- In the [1985 NCAA Division I men's basketball tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_NCAA_Division_I_men's_basketball_tournament), the [Georgetown Hoyas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984%E2%80%9385_Georgetown_Hoyas_men's_basketball_team) had won the [previous national championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984_NCAA_Division_I_men's_basketball_tournament), and looked poised to win their second straight, as they entered the [1985 national championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_NCAA_Division_I_Men's_Basketball_Championship_Game) as the defending national champion with the Number 1 overall seed, as well as the Number 1 seed in the East Region, posting a record of 35–2, including a [Big East tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Big_East_men's_basketball_tournament) title. Their opponent and Big East rival, the Number 8 seed [Villanova Wildcats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984%E2%80%9385_Villanova_Wildcats_men's_basketball_team), entered the national championship with a 24–10 record. Villanova defeated Georgetown, 66–64, in what has been considered one of the biggest upsets in NCAA Division I men's basketball tournament history. As of [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NCAA_Division_I_men's_basketball_tournament), this is the only time an 8th-seeded team has won the NCAA tournament and the 1984–85 Villanova team remains the lowest-seeded team to win the NCAA tournament.

- The [Stanford Cardinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_Cardinal_women's_basketball) entered the [1998 NCAA Division I women's basketball tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament) with the Number 1 seed in the West Region, posting a record of 21–5, including a Pac-10 regular season title. Their opponent, the Number 16 seed [Harvard Crimson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Crimson_women's_basketball), entered the tournament with a 22–4 record and an [Ivy League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivy_League) regular season title. Before this match, no Number 1 seed had ever fallen to a Number 16 seed since the field expanded to 64 teams in 1994. [Harvard defeated Stanford, 71–67](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Harvard_vs._Stanford_women's_basketball_game), in what has been considered the biggest upset in NCAA Division I women's basketball tournament history. 20 years later, a Number 16 seed had beaten a Number 1 seed in men's tournament (see below).

- The [Virginia Cavaliers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017%E2%80%9318_Virginia_Cavaliers_men's_basketball_team) entered the [2018 NCAA Division I men's basketball tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_NCAA_Division_I_men's_basketball_tournament) with the Number 1 overall seed, as well as the Number 1 seed in the South Region, posting a record of 31–2, including both an ACC regular season title and [ACC tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_ACC_men's_basketball_tournament) title. Their opponent, Number 16 seed [UMBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017%E2%80%9318_UMBC_Retrievers_men's_basketball_team), entered the tournament with a 24–10 record and an [America East tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_America_East_men's_basketball_tournament) title. Before this match, no Number 1 seed had ever fallen to a Number 16 seed since the field expanded to 64 teams in 1985. [UMBC defeated Virginia, 74–54](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_UMBC_vs._Virginia_men's_basketball_game), in what has been considered the biggest upset in NCAA Tournament history.

  [^cite_note-13]

- The [Purdue Boilermakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Purdue_Boilermakers_men's_basketball_team) entered the [2023 NCAA Division I men's basketball tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_NCAA_Division_I_men's_basketball_tournament) with the Number 1 seed in the East Region, posting a record of 29–5, including both a [Big Ten regular season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Big_Ten_Conference_men's_basketball_season) and [tournament title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Big_Ten_men's_basketball_tournament). Their opponent, Number 16 seed [Fairleigh Dickinson Knights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Fairleigh_Dickinson_Knights_men's_basketball_team), entered the tournament with a 20–15 record after an 84–61 win over [Texas Southern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Texas_Southern_Tigers_basketball_team) in the First Four. Despite a 67–66 loss in the [Northeast Conference tournament championship game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Northeast_Conference_men's_basketball_tournament) to the NEC regular season champion [Merrimack Warriors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Merrimack_Warriors_men's_basketball_team), Fairleigh Dickinson received an automatic bid to the NCAA tournament as the NEC tournament runners-up, while the NEC tournament champion Merrimack was ineligible for the NCAA tournament due to a 4-year transition from Division II. Closed as 23.5-point underdogs, [Fairleigh Dickinson shocked Purdue 63–58](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Fairleigh_Dickinson_vs._Purdue_men's_basketball_game) to become the second Number 16 seed to beat a Number 1 seed in NCAA Division I men's basketball tournament history.

- Entering the [first round of the 2007 NBA Playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_NBA_playoffs#\(1\)_Dallas_Mavericks_vs._\(8\)_Golden_State_Warriors), the [Dallas Mavericks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006%E2%80%9307_Dallas_Mavericks_season) had the best record in all of [NBA](22093.md) at 67–15, six games up on second place, while their first round opponent, the [Golden State Warriors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006%E2%80%9307_Golden_State_Warriors_season), had a 42–40 record and had only qualified for the tournament on the last day of the regular season, having needed to win all of their last five games just to qualify. Dallas was captained by [power forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_forward_\(basketball\)) [Dirk Nowitzki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirk_Nowitzki), who was in the midst of a Hall-of-Fame career that would feature 14 all star appearances, and supported by other star players such as [Jason Terry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Terry), [Jerry Stackhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Stackhouse), [Devin Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devin_Harris), and [Josh Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Howard). Golden State had completely revamped their team mid-season, including two starters ([Stephen Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Jackson) and [Al Harrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Harrington)), who arrived in January in a blockbuster 8-player deal with the [Indiana Pacers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_Pacers). [Baron Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baron_Davis) was the unquestioned star of the playoff run, as he dominated the Mavericks, averaging 25.3 points, 6.5 assists, and 2.9 steals per game, as the Warriors would knock off the Mavericks four games to two.

  [^cite_note-14]

- Entering the [second round of the 2020 NBA Playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_NBA_playoffs#\(2\)_Los_Angeles_Clippers_vs._\(3\)_Denver_Nuggets), the [Los Angeles Clippers](72892.md), under the guidance by championship head coach [Doc Rivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doc_Rivers), were widely regarded as frontrunners for the championship. Armed with star talents like [Kawhi Leonard](31163849.md), who came off a championship win with the [Toronto Raptors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_Raptors), and [Paul George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_George), a previous MVP candidate and first-team All-Defensive player, and defensive guard [Patrick Beverley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Beverley), the Clippers had high expectations. Their deep bench, featuring [Montrezl Harrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montrezl_Harrell) and [Lou Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lou_Williams), added to their championship aspirations, while their strong defensive prowess further solidified their position. However, the [Denver Nuggets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver_Nuggets), guided by the dynamic duo of [Jamal Murray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamal_Murray) and [Nikola Jokić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikola_Joki%C4%87), emerged as formidable challengers. Murray's explosive scoring prowess, highlighted by a remarkable 40-point performance in Game 7, combined with Jokić's extraordinary playmaking and versatility as a center, were instrumental in fueling the Nuggets' remarkable comeback. This unforeseen development showcased the Nuggets' remarkable resilience and their strategic acumen in exploiting the Clippers' defensive weaknesses. In contrast, the Clippers' star players faltered significantly in the crucial Game 7, with Leonard managing only 14 points and a 6-of-22 shooting performance, and George contributing a mere 10 points at a shooting efficiency of 25%, including a notable miss that hit the side of the backboard during a 3-point attempt. Notably, both stars were unable to score in the 4th quarter. This series of events culminated in a stunning reversal, as the Clippers lost their dominant 3–1 series lead, becoming the 12th team in NBA history to do so. The Nuggets, driven by the exceptional performances of Murray and Jokić, clinched a victory in what became one of the most unexpected and memorable upsets in recent NBA playoff history.

#### Cricket

- In the [1983 Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_Cricket_World_Cup), the third edition of the tournament, the [West Indies cricket team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Indies_cricket_team) had won both of the previous two World Cups and looked poised to win their third. Their opponent in the finals, [India](407754.md), had never made it out of the group stage before 1983. India went to bat first, and managed 183 before being dismissed with five overs left. West Indies star batsman, [Viv Richards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viv_Richards), hit a hook towards the leg-side boundary, where Indian captain [Kapil Dev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapil_Dev) made a running catch to get out the West Indies best batsman. Among the remaining batsman, only [Jeff Dujon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Dujon) managed more than 20 runs, and West Indies were bowled out at 140, giving India their first World Cup.

#### Ice hockey

- Entering the [medal round of the 1980 Winter Olympics](1400841.md#Final_round), the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union_men's_national_ice_hockey_team) had won four consecutive gold medals of the previous four Winter Olympics and were heavily favored to win another gold medal. Their opponent, the [United States](945923.md), consisted entirely of collegiate and amateur players and under head coach [Herb Brooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herb_Brooks), had finished second in Blue Division behind [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_men's_national_ice_hockey_team) (based on goal difference) and qualified for the medal round. The U.S. pulled off a 4–3 upset over the Soviets, in what has since become known as the "[Miracle on Ice](295721.md)". After that loss, the Soviets pummeled Sweden to settle for silver, while the U.S. went on to win gold after defeating [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland_men's_national_ice_hockey_team).

- Entering the [first round of the 2019 Stanley Cup playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Stanley_Cup_playoffs#\(A1\)_Tampa_Bay_Lightning_vs._\(WC2\)_Columbus_Blue_Jackets), the [Tampa Bay Lightning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_Tampa_Bay_Lightning_season) earned the [Presidents' Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidents'_Trophy) as the [NHL](21809.md)'s best regular season team with a 62–16–4 record (128 points), while their first round opponent, the [Columbus Blue Jackets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_Columbus_Blue_Jackets_season), had a 47–31–4 record (98 points), had qualified for the playoffs for the third straight year and earned the second wild card spot in the [Eastern Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Conference_\(NHL\)). The [Blue Jackets swept the Lightning in four games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Tampa_Bay_Lightning%E2%80%93Columbus_Blue_Jackets_playoff_series) and won the playoff series for the first time in franchise history, in what has been considered the biggest upset in Stanley Cup playoffs history. This marks the first time in Stanley Cup playoffs history that the Presidents' Trophy winners were swept in the opening round.

- Entering the [2023 Stanley Cup playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Stanley_Cup_playoffs#\(A1\)_Boston_Bruins_vs._\(WC2\)_Florida_Panthers), the [Boston Bruins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Boston_Bruins_season) had set new NHL records for single-season wins and points, finishing with a 65–12–5 record (135 points), and earning the President's Trophy. By contrast, their first round opponent, the [Florida Panthers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Florida_Panthers_season), had narrowly qualified for the playoffs, finishing with a 42–32–8 record (92 points), and clinching an Eastern Conference wild card berth one day after their penultimate regular-season game. The Panthers defeated the Bruins in seven games, rallying from a 3–1 series deficit and winning three straight games to eliminate Boston; additionally, the team advanced to the second round of the playoffs for just the third time in franchise history. The 43-point difference between the two teams represented the largest upset by standings position of any best-of-seven playoff series in NHL history.

- Entering the [2025 IIHF World Championship playoff round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_IIHF_World_Championship_playoff_round), 1-seed and 28-time champion [Canada](744439.md) was the strongest team in the tournament, and was favored to make the final round.
