**Iraqi Oil Tanker Company** (IOTC) ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شركة ناقلات النفط العراقية) is the state owned Iraqi company specializing in the ocean transport of crude oil and refined products. It was established in 1972.
