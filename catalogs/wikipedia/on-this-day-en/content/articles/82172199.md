***Possible Love*** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 가능한 사랑) is a 2026 [drama film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drama_film) directed by [Lee Chang-dong](285217.md), and co-written with Oh Jung-mi. A co-production of South Korea and the United States, starring [Jeon Do-yeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeon_Do-yeon), [Sul Kyung-gu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sul_Kyung-gu), [Zo In-sung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zo_In-sung), and [Cho Yeo-jeong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cho_Yeo-jeong), it follows the intertwined lives of two married couples leading completely opposite lives.

The film premiered in the main competition of the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md) on September 6, 2026, where it won the [Grand Jury Prize](16581726.md) and the [FIPRESCI Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIPRESCI_Prize). It was theatrically released in South Korea on September 23, followed by the United States on October 23, and a worldwide release by [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix) on November 6.

It was also selected as the [South Korean entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_South_Korean_submissions_for_the_Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film) for the [Best International Feature Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film) category for the [99th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/99th_Academy_Awards).

## Premise

A story of two married couples — a laid-off worker and his spouse, and a documentary filmmaker and her spouse — crossing paths to film a documentary and facing their different lives and hidden desires.[^cite_note-VIFF-2]

## Production

A co-production by South Korea and United States, it's produced by Pinehouse Film, [Anonymous Content](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anonymous_Content) and NOWFILM.[^cite_note-3]

The film was originally part of [Maciej Musiał](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maciej_Musia%C5%82)'s project to remake all ten episodes of [Krzysztof Kieślowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krzysztof_Kie%C5%9Blowski)'s *[Dekalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dekalog)* as feature films, alongside [Asghar Farhadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asghar_Farhadi)'s *[Parallel Tales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_Tales)*.[^cite_note-4] According to Lee, once Netflix joined the project, the "project fell into an ambiguous state. Although this film originated from there, it does not adapt an episode of *Dekalog* and is a completely different story. However, it is related to one of the commandments."[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-\:1-6] Lee's first draft of what became *Possible Love* had been written over a decade prior. Upon reading the finished script, Netflix immediately signed on and "guaranteed \[Lee] artistic autonomy."[^cite_note-\:1-6]

[Jung Jae-il](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jung_Jae-il) was hired to compose the score.[^cite_note-7]

## Release

The film had its world premiere in the main competition of the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md) on September 6, 2026,[^cite_note-VIFF-2] where it won the FIPRESCI Prize.[^cite_note-\:2-8] It will also screen at the [2026 Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Toronto_International_Film_Festival),[^cite_note-9] and in the Main Slate of the [2026 New York Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_York_Film_Festival).[^cite_note-10]

*Possible Love* was released first in South Korean cinemas on September 23, it will be followed by select U.S. theaters on October 23, and will be released worldwide by [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix) on November 6.[^cite_note-11]

It was also selected as the [South Korean entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_South_Korean_submissions_for_the_Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film) for the [Best International Feature Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film) category for the [99th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/99th_Academy_Awards), marking director Lee's fourth film selected, following *[Oasis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oasis_\(2002_film\))* (2002), *[Secret Sunshine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_Sunshine)* (2007), and *[Burning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burning_\(2018_film\))* (2018).[^cite_note-12]

## Reception

### Critical response

On the [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator) website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), [100%](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_films_with_a_100%25_rating_on_Rotten_Tomatoes) of 38 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 8.7/10.[^cite_note-Rotten_Tomatoes-13] [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic), which uses a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean), assigned the film a score of 93 out of 100, based on 19 critics, indicating "universal acclaim".[^cite_note-Metacritic-14]

[Peter Bradshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Bradshaw) of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* rated the film 4 out of 5 stars, describing it as "a finely judged, beautifully acted and rather novelistic work".[^cite_note-15]
