The **FIFA U-20 World Cup** is the biennial [football](10568.md) world championship tournament for [FIFA](11049.md) members' men's national teams with players up to the age of 20. The competition has been staged every two years since the inaugural tournament in [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_FIFA_World_Youth_Championship) when it was hosted by [Tunisia](30188.md)[^cite_note-1] under the tournament name of **FIFA World Youth Championship** until [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_FIFA_World_Youth_Championship).[^cite_note-2] In [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup) the name was changed to its present form. FIFA bills the men's Under-20 World Cup as "the tournament of tomorrow's superstars."[^cite_note-3] [Diego Maradona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Maradona), [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) and [Paul Pogba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Pogba) are previous winners of the official player of the tournament award, and [Erling Haaland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erling_Haaland) was the top scorer at the [2019 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup). The reigning champions are [Morocco](15604185.md), which won their first title at the [2025 tournament](73421401.md) in Chile.

## History

In the twenty-three editions of the tournament held, twelve nations have won the title. [Argentina U20](12504513.md) is the most successful team with six titles, followed by [Brazil U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_national_under-20_football_team) with five titles. [Portugal U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal_national_under-20_football_team) and [Serbia U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia_national_under-20_football_team) have both won two titles (with the latter winning once as [Yugoslavia U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugoslavia_national_under-20_football_team)), while [Ghana U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghana_national_under-20_football_team), [Germany U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany_national_under-20_football_team), [Spain U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_national_under-20_football_team), [France U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_national_under-20_football_team), [England U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-20_football_team), [Ukraine U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine_national_under-20_football_team), [Russia U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia_national_under-20_football_team) (as the [USSR U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union_national_under-20_football_team)), [Uruguay U20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguay_national_under-20_football_team), and [Morocco U20](15604185.md) have won the title once each.

A corresponding event for women's teams, the [FIFA U-20 Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_U-20_Women's_World_Cup), began in 2002 with the name "FIFA U-19 Women's World Championship" and an age limit of 19. The age limit for the women's competition was changed to 20 beginning with the [2006 FIFA U-20 Women's World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_U-20_Women's_World_Championship), and the competition was renamed as a "World Cup" in 2007 in preparation for the [2008 event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_FIFA_U-20_Women's_World_Cup). The next edition is planned to be held in 2027 in [Azerbaijan](746.md) and [Uzbekistan](31853.md).

### Performances by continental zones

All continental confederations except for the [OFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceania_Football_Confederation) (Oceania) have made an appearance in the final match of the tournament. To date, [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL) (South America) leads with twelve titles, followed by [UEFA](32332.md) (Europe) with ten titles and the [CAF](715265.md) (Africa) with two titles. Teams reprensenting the [AFC](715249.md) (Asia) have made the final three times, but were defeated by strong UEFA teams. Mexico is the only team from [CONCACAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF) (North, Central America and the Caribbean) which has made the final. Mexico lost to the Soviet Union in the final of the inaugural tournament in 1977, which is also their only appearance in a final. No current OFC member has ever made the semi-finals; Australia reached the last four as an OFC member in 1991 and 1993, finishing fourth on both occasions, before the country joined the AFC in 2006.

## Awards

The following awards are now presented:

- The **Golden Ball** is awarded to the most valuable player of the tournament;

- The **Golden Boot** is awarded to the top goalscorer of the tournament;

- The **Golden Glove** is awarded to the most valuable goalkeeper of the tournament;

- The **FIFA Fair Play Trophy** is presented to the team with the best disciplinary record in the tournament.
