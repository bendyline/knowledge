**Benjamin Todd Shelton** (born October 9, 2002) is an American professional [tennis](29773.md) player. He has been [ranked](13480983.md#Current_rankings) world No. 4 in men's singles by the [Association of Tennis Professionals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) (ATP), achieved in September 2026. Shelton has won seven [ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour) singles titles, including two [Masters 1000](30872553.md) trophies at the [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_National_Bank_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) and [2026 Canadian Opens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_National_Bank_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles). His best result at the [majors](197638.md#Tournaments) is reaching the final of the [2026 US Open](84011000.md). He is currently the American No. 1 player in singles.[^cite_note-6]

Shelton has won one doubles title, at the [2026 U.S. Men's Clay Court Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_U.S._Men's_Clay_Court_Championships_%E2%80%93_Doubles) with [Andrés Andrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Andrade_\(tennis\)), and has a career-high doubles ranking of world No. 68, attained in May 2024.

## Early life

Shelton was born on October 9, 2002, in [Atlanta](3138.md), Georgia. Shelton is the son of former professional tennis player [Bryan Shelton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Shelton).[^cite_note-7] His mother, Lisa Witsken Shelton, was a highly ranked junior tennis player, and his uncle, [Todd Witsken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Witsken), was a professional tennis player. His sister Emma played college tennis at Florida.[^cite_note-NYT-8] Shelton was born in [Atlanta](3138.md) as his father was the then-coach of [Georgia Tech's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech_Yellow_Jackets) women's tennis team.[^cite_note-USAToday-9] He grew up in [Gainesville, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gainesville,_Florida), after his father became the coach of [Florida Gators men's tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Gators_men's_tennis) team.&#x20;

Shelton played [American football](18951490.md) in his early childhood,[^cite_note-NYT-8] and his parents did not pressure him to play tennis.[^cite_note-NYT-8] At age 12 he began playing tennis regularly, coached by his father.[^cite_note-ESPN-3]

He graduated from [Buchholz High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buchholz_High_School).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

## Juniors

Shelton peaked at No. 3 in the USTA Boys' 18s division rankings.[^cite_note-Florida-12] He was a runner-up at the 2020 USTA Boys 18s Winter Nationals and won the 2019 USTA Boys 16s tournament in doubles.[^cite_note-Florida-12]

Shelton had mixed results on the [ITF junior circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITF_junior_circuit) and reached a junior combined ranking of No. 306 in December 2020.[^cite_note-overview-13]

## College years

From 2020 to 2022, he played collegiate tennis for the [Florida Gators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Gators_men's_tennis).[^cite_note-14] A finance major, Shelton mostly played No. 5 singles as a freshman in 2020–21 and had an overall record of 28–5.[^cite_note-Florida-12] He helped the Gators win the [Southeastern Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_Conference) (SEC) regular season title, and he won the championship-clinching match at the [2021 NCAA Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_NCAA_Division_I_Tennis_Championships), securing Florida's first national title.[^cite_note-Florida-12]

In 2021–22, Shelton had a record of 37–5 in singles matches (including 14–2 in the No. 1 spot).[^cite_note-Florida-12] He won the [Intercollegiate Tennis Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercollegiate_Tennis_Association) (ITA) All-American Championships in the fall without dropping a set.[^cite_note-Florida-12] He helped Florida defend their SEC title and win the [SEC Tournament Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Southeastern_Conference_champions#Men's_tennis).[^cite_note-Florida-12] Shelton won the [2022 NCAA Singles Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NCAA_Division_I_Men's_Tennis_Championships) over [August Holmgren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Holmgren_\(tennis\)) and finished the season as the nation's top-ranked player.[^cite_note-Florida-12][^cite_note-USAToday-9] He was named SEC Player of the Year and National Player of the Year.[^cite_note-Florida-12][^cite_note-15]

Shelton returned to Florida for his junior year in 2022–23 to complete his finance degree,[^cite_note-USAToday-9] but soon continued his college education online.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-ESPN-3]

## Professional career

### 2022: ATP debut, Challenger titles, Top 5 win

At the [Georgia's Rome Challenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Georgia's_Rome_Challenger_%E2%80%93_Singles) in July, Shelton reached his first [Challenger final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Challenger_Tour), losing to [Wu Yibing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wu_Yibing).[^cite_note-17] The next week at the [Indy Challenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Indy_Challenger_%E2%80%93_Singles), he reached the semi-finals, highlighted by a win over world No. 103 [Tim van Rijthoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_van_Rijthoven).

Shelton made his ATP Tour debut at the [Atlanta Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Atlanta_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) as a [wildcard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_card_\(tennis\)), and in the first round he defeated [Ramkumar Ramanathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramkumar_Ramanathan) for his first ATP win.[^cite_note-18] He lost his next match to the No. 2 seed [John Isner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Isner) in three sets.[^cite_note-19] Shelton received a wildcard entry into the [Cincinnati Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Western_&_Southern_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles). In the first round, he defeated world No. 56 [Lorenzo Sonego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Sonego) in three sets for his first win over a top-100 player.[^cite_note-20] In the 2nd round, Shelton faced world No. 5 [Casper Ruud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casper_Ruud) in his first matchup against a top-10 opponent. Shelton defeated Ruud, in straight sets for his first top-10 win.[^cite_note-21]



Shelton at the [2022 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_\(tennis\))

On August 23, 2022, Shelton announced he would not return to college and would turn professional.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] He announced that he would be represented by agent Alessandro Sant Albano, who is a part of Roger Federer's TEAM8 management firm.[^cite_note-ESPN-3]

Shelton also received a wildcard to appear in the main draw for his Grand Slam debut at the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) on August 14.[^cite_note-24] He lost in the first round, in five sets, to [Nuno Borges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuno_Borges_\(tennis\)) while also recording the second fastest serve of the US Open tournament at 149 miles per hour (240 km/h) in this match.[^cite_note-25] He also competed in [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) having paired with fellow American [Christopher Eubanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Eubanks). They were eliminated in the second round after defeating [Stefanos Tsitsipas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefanos_Tsitsipas) and [Petros Tsitsipas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petros_Tsitsipas) in the first round.

Shelton reached his third Challenger final of the year at the [2022 Tiburon Challenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Tiburon_Challenger) after defeating top seed [Denis Kudla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Kudla). As a result, he moved into the top 160 in the rankings on October 10, 2022. He advanced to his fourth Challenger final at the [Charlottesville Men's Pro Challenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Charlottesville_Men's_Pro_Challenger_%E2%80%93_Singles) where he defeated his doubles partner Christopher Eubanks to secure his first Challenger title. As a result, he moved into the top 150 in the rankings at world No. 128 on November 7.[^cite_note-26]

His fifth Challenger final featured a repeat of his last with a win against Christopher Eubanks in the [Knoxville Challenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Knoxville_Challenger_%E2%80%93_Singles) which lifted him another 20 positions up to No. 108 in the rankings on November 14. After winning his third straight title at the [Champaign–Urbana Challenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Champaign%E2%80%93Urbana_Challenger_%E2%80%93_Singles), he debuted in the top 100 of the rankings ending the year at world No. 97 on November 21, 2022, and became the youngest player in ATP Challenger Tour history to win three titles in three weeks. He was also the youngest American in the Top 250.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

### 2023: First Major semifinal, first ATP title, top 15



Shelton at the [2023 Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Miami_Open)

On his debut at the [2023 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), Shelton reached the fourth round, with wins against [Zhang Zhizhen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Zhizhen), [Nicolas Jarry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Jarry), and [Alexei Popyrin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexei_Popyrin).[^cite_note-29] This was only Shelton's second Grand Slam after the [2022 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-30] Next he defeated compatriot [J. J. Wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._Wolf) to reach the quarterfinal of a Major for the first time in his career.[^cite_note-31] His run concluded with a four-set loss to fellow American [Tommy Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Paul_\(tennis\)). As a result, he moved 53 positions up into the top 50, at world No. 44, for the first time in his career.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33]

In the beginning of the American hard court season for 2023, Shelton faced a tough start, experiencing defeat in the opening round at the [2023 Delray Beach Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Delray_Beach_Open), losing in straight sets to [Marcos Giron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcos_Giron). His struggles continued at the [Mexican Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Abierto_Mexicano_Telcel), where he once again fell in the first round, this time to the fourth-seeded [Holger Rune](60992715.md), in a match that stretched to three sets. However, Shelton managed to bounce back at [Indian Wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Wells_Masters), securing a victory over [Fabio Fognini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabio_Fognini) in the initial round, only to be bested by fellow American [Taylor Fritz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Fritz) in the second round.

At the [2023 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_US_Open_\(tennis\)) Shelton made it to his first Grand Slam semifinal,[^cite_note-34] where he lost in straight sets to second seed and eventual champion [Novak Djokovic](16100029.md). As a result, he reached the top 20 in the rankings at world No. 19 on September 11, 2023.[^cite_note-35]

At the [2023 Rolex Shanghai Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Rolex_Shanghai_Masters) he reached the quarterfinals of a Masters 1000 for the first time defeating fourth seed [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md) for the second biggest win of his career.[^cite_note-36] He reached his maiden final and won his maiden title at the [2023 Japan Open Tennis Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Japan_Open_Tennis_Championships) defeating qualifier [Taro Daniel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taro_Daniel), [Jordan Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Thompson_\(tennis\)), fifth seed [Tommy Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Paul_\(tennis\)), qualifier [Marcos Giron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcos_Giron) and [Aslan Karatsev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aslan_Karatsev).[^cite_note-37] He became the sixth first-time ATP champion in the season and as a result reached the top 15 in the singles rankings on October 23, 2023.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40]

### 2024: First title on clay, American No. 1

At the [U.S. Clay Court Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_U.S._Men's_Clay_Court_Championships) he reached his first quarterfinal on clay courts defeating [Zizou Bergs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zizou_Bergs),[^cite_note-41] and his first semifinal defeating compatriot [Brandon Nakashima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Nakashima). Next he defeated fourth seed [Tomás Martín Etcheverry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom%C3%A1s_Mart%C3%ADn_Etcheverry) to reach his first clay court final. He won his second career title and first on clay becoming the youngest champion since [Andy Roddick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Roddick) in 2002, defeating third seed [Frances Tiafoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_Tiafoe) in the first African-American men's singles tennis final in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Era).[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43] As a result, he reached a new career-high singles ranking of world No. 14 on 8 April 2024, and became the American No. 1 player ahead of [Taylor Fritz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Fritz) a week later.[^cite_note-44] He became the youngest American No. 1 player since Andy Roddick achieved the feat in March 2004.[^cite_note-45] Shelton would go on to reach a new ranking of world No. 13 on 19 August 2024.

Ben Shelton in Basel 2024

Ben Shelton, ATP Basel 2024

At the [Cincinnati Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Cincinnati_Open) he reached the round of 16 for the second time at the tournament, saving a match point against Tomás Martín Etcheverry.[^cite_note-46] he reached his second Masters career quarterfinal with a win over [Fábián Marozsán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A1bi%C3%A1n_Marozs%C3%A1n).[^cite_note-47] Shelton reached the third round of the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_-_Men's_singles), defeating former 2020 champion [Dominic Thiem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_Thiem) in his final US Open, and [Roberto Carballes Baena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Carballes_Baena) in the second round, before losing to eventual semifinalist Francis Tiafoe in a repeat of the previous year's quarter-final.[^cite_note-48]

At the [2024 Laver Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Laver_Cup), Shelton earned 6 of Team World's 11 points in a 13–11 loss to Team Europe. On day one, Shelton partnered Taylor Fritz to defeat [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md) and [Carlos Alcaraz](63121147.md) of Team Europe. On day 2, Shelton was defeated by Alcaraz in singles, while partnering with [Alejandro Tabilo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Tabilo) to defeat the doubles team of Casper Ruud and Stefanos Tsitsipas. On the third day, Shelton defeated [Daniil Medvedev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniil_Medvedev), before losing to the doubles team of Alcaraz and Ruud. Shelton played a total of 5 matches, more than any other Laver Cup participant, and earned 6 points, more than anyone on Team World.[^cite_note-49]

Shelton defeated [Ugo Humbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ugo_Humbert) in four quarters to win his maiden [UTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultimate_Tennis_Showdown#Past_results) title in Frankfurt.[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52] Shelton reached the final of the [Swiss Indoors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Indoors) in October, but was defeated by [Giovanni Mpetshi Perricard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Mpetshi_Perricard).[^cite_note-53] On his way to the final, Shelton defeated, among others, [Arthur Fils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Fils) in the semifinals.[^cite_note-54]

### 2025: World No. 5, Masters title, ATP Finals debut



Shelton at the 2025 Australian Open

Shelton, seeded first at the [Auckland Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_ASB_Classic_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) lost to [Jakub Menšík](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakub_Men%C5%A1%C3%ADk) in the round of 16.[^cite_note-55] In the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), Shelton advanced past the first week after defeating compatriot [Brandon Nakashima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Nakashima) in straight sets in round 1, [Pablo Carreño Busta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_Carre%C3%B1o_Busta) in round 2, [Lorenzo Musetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Musetti) in round 3, and advanced past [Gaël Monfils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ga%C3%ABl_Monfils) after the Frenchman retired in the fourth set due to injury. In the quarterfinals, Shelton defeated [Lorenzo Sonego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Sonego) in four sets. In his final match of the tournament, Shelton lost to defending champion [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md) in the semifinals in three sets.[^cite_note-56][^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58][^cite_note-59]

At the [Indian Wells Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_BNP_Paribas_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), Shelton reached the quarterfinals for the first time at the tournament becoming the youngest American to accomplish the feat since [Andy Roddick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Roddick) in 2004.[^cite_note-60] Shelton made his top 10 debut following his quarterfinal win over [Jiří Lehečka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ji%C5%99%C3%AD_Lehe%C4%8Dka) at the [Stuttgart Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_BOSS_Open).[^cite_note-61][^cite_note-62][^cite_note-top10-63] He lost in the semifinals to top seed and eventual champion [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md). By reaching the quarterfinals at the [Washington Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Mubadala_Citi_DC_Open), Shelton secured a new career high ranking of world No. 7 on July 28, 2025. He defeated hometown favorite and sixth seed [Frances Tiafoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_Tiafoe) to reach a consecutive semifinal in Washington, the third American to accomplish the feat.[^cite_note-64]

He reached his first ATP 1000 semifinal at the [Canadian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_National_Bank_Open), defeating [Flavio Cobolli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavio_Cobolli) en route and recording his 100th career win,[^cite_note-65] and [Alex de Minaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_de_Minaur). He became the youngest American to reach the last four at an ATP 1000, since [Andy Roddick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Roddick) in 2005 in Indian Wells.[^cite_note-66] He defeated [Karen Khachanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Khachanov) in the final in three sets to win his third career title, first ATP Masters title, and a new ranking of world No. 6.

At the [2025 Cincinnati Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cincinnati_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), Shelton made it to the quarterfinals after beating [Camilo Ugo Carabelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camilo_Ugo_Carabelli), [Roberto Bautista Agut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Bautista_Agut), and [Jiří Lehečka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ji%C5%99%C3%AD_Lehe%C4%8Dka) all in straight sets, before losing to [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md) in straight sets.[^cite_note-67][^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69][^cite_note-70] At the [Paris Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Masters), Shelton defeated [Andrey Rublev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Rublev) to reach the quarterfinals. In doing so, Shelton rose to a new career high ranking of world No. 5 in November and qualified for the [ATP Finals](1209231.md), marking his debut appearance.[^cite_note-71][^cite_note-72]

### 2026: World No. 4, First Major Final

Starting his season at the [ASB Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_ASB_Classic_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), Shelton reached the quarterfinals where he lost against [Sebastián Báez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebasti%C3%A1n_B%C3%A1ez).[^cite_note-73] He made another quarterfinal run at the [Australian Open](81715568.md), defeating [Ugo Humbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ugo_Humbert) in the first round.[^cite_note-74] In the fourth round, he defeated [Casper Ruud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casper_Ruud)[^cite_note-75] before losing to two-time defending champion [Jannik Sinner.](60061043.md)[^cite_note-76]

In February, Shelton won his fourth career title and first title of the season at [Dallas Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Dallas_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), with wins against defending champion [Denis Shapovalov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Shapovalov)[^cite_note-77] and after saving 3 championship points in the final against [Taylor Fritz.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Fritz)[^cite_note-78]

At the [US Men's Clay Court Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_U.S._Men's_Clay_Court_Championships) Shelton lifted his first ATP doubles title with his University of Florida men's tennis college teammate [Andrés Andrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Andrade_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-79][^cite_note-80] In singles he once again became the American No. 1 player in the ATP Rankings on 6 April 2026.

Ben Shelton won the [BMW Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_BMW_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) in Munich defeating [Flavio Cobolli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavio_Cobolli).[^cite_note-81] It was his fifth career [ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour) title, first on European clay, and made history as the first American man to win a title on clay at the [ATP 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_500) level or higher since [Andre Agassi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andre_Agassi) at the [2002 Italian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Italian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles).[^cite_note-82]

For the first time in 71 years, two American men faced off in the [Stuttgart Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart_Open) final.[^cite_note-83] During the tournament, Shelton went three sets in each match, saved nine break points, and two match points en route to the title.[^cite_note-84] In defeating Taylor Fritz to win the [Boss Open 250](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_BOSS_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) event, Shelton claimed his first grass title and became the sixth American champion of the event.[^cite_note-85] Shelton also became the first American male since 2010 and fourth male player during the 2020s, to win titles on all three surfaces in the same year (hard, clay, and grass).[^cite_note-86][^cite_note-87][^cite_note-88]

At [Wimbledon](80434323.md), for the first time at a major since the 2023 French Open, Shelton lost in the first round to qualifier [Otto Virtanen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Virtanen), despite having a match point in the 5th set tiebreak.[^cite_note-89]

Shelton successfully defended his title at the [Canadian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_National_Bank_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), highlighted by wins over [João Fonseca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Fonseca_\(tennis\)) and [Jakub Menšík](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakub_Men%C5%A1%C3%ADk).[^cite_note-90] He became the first player to go back to back at this tournament since [Rafael Nadal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Nadal) in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Rogers_Cup_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles)–[2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Rogers_Cup_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), first player to win without dropping a set since [Novak Djokovic](16100029.md) in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Rogers_Cup_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles),[^cite_note-91] and eighth player in history to win titles on every surface (outdoor hard, indoor hard, clay and grass) within the same season.[^cite_note-92]

Shelton made his first Grand Slam final at the 2026 US Open, losing in four sets to [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md).

## National representation

### Davis Cup

Shelton made his Davis Cup debut in the quarterfinals of the [2024 Davis Cup Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Davis_Cup_Finals) against [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_Davis_Cup_team).[^cite_note-93] Shelton lost his singles match to [Thanasi Kokkinakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanasi_Kokkinakis) after failing to convert four match points, and lost his doubles match partnered with Tommy Paul to [Matthew Ebden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Ebden) and [Jordan Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Thompson_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-94][^cite_note-95] He was announced as part of the team for the [2025 Davis Cup Qualifiers second round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Davis_Cup_Qualifiers_second_round), but withdrew due to a shoulder injury.[^cite_note-96]

## Playing style

Shelton is able to hit a big first serve, which averages 126 miles per hour (203 km/h).[^cite_note-USAToday-9] At the 2023 US Open, he hit two service aces at 149.0 miles per hour (239.8 km/h) in the same game; he has hit serves at 150.0 miles per hour (241.4 km/h) on multiple separate occasions. He also can produce a significant amount of kick on both first and second serves. A left-handed player, Shelton is powerful and consistent on the forehand and backhand side.[^cite_note-USAToday-9]

He is comfortable moving to the net off his powerful groundstrokes to finish points. Shelton has said "I love to get to net, be able to use some of my hand skills, athletic skills and going up to get the ball (to put away overheads) is one of my favorite things to do..."[^cite_note-USAToday-9] He has said that the serve-and-volley is an important part of his game and an area he wants to further develop.[^cite_note-USAToday-9]

Shelton's preferred court is the hard court but he has achieved success on clay, being the first American to win an [ATP 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_500_tournaments) or above clay court title since [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Italian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles).[^cite_note-108][^cite_note-109]

Shelton's serve is not his only ability, as he's developed a wider variety of shots to use within his games after some criticisms that he relied too heavily on his serve. He is now able to sustain longer rallies, utilizing his stamina rather than aiming for quick winners.[^cite_note-110]

He is coached by his father [Bryan Shelton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Shelton) as of June 2023,[^cite_note-111] and formerly [Dean Goldfine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_Goldfine).[^cite_note-112] His tennis idol is [Roger Federer](262376.md).[^cite_note-113]

## Personal life

Shelton is based in [Orlando, Florida.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida)[^cite_note-NYMag-4] He was in a relationship with [heptathlete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heptathlon) [Anna Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Hall_\(heptathlete\)) from 2021 to 2023.[^cite_note-114][^cite_note-115] Shelton went public with his relationship with soccer player [Trinity Rodman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Rodman) in March 2025.[^cite_note-116]

A fan of soccer player [Michael Olise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Olise), Shelton has recreated Olise's trademark goal celebration at the [2025 Munich Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_BMW_Open).[^cite_note-117][^cite_note-118]
