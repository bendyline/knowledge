The **2026 Masters Tournament** was the 90th edition of the [Masters Tournament](157211.md) and the first of the four [men's major golf championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men's_major_golf_championships) held in 2026. The tournament was played April 9–12 at [Augusta National Golf Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augusta_National_Golf_Club) in [Augusta, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augusta,_Georgia), United States.&#x20;

[Rory McIlroy](3949844.md) won the tournament for the second consecutive year, one stroke ahead of runner-up [Scottie Scheffler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottie_Scheffler). He joined [Jack Nicklaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Nicklaus), [Nick Faldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Faldo) and [Tiger Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_Woods) as the only players to win two years consecutively.

## Field

The field included 91 players, including six amateurs.

Going into the tournament, [Scottie Scheffler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottie_Scheffler) was the betting favorite to win.[^cite_note-ap-26-how-watch-4] Scheffler and [Rory McIlroy](3949844.md) were returning to play after three-week breaks. Twenty-two golfers made their Masters debut, including [Jacob Bridgeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Bridgeman), [Ben Griffin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Griffin_\(golfer\)), [Chris Gotterup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Gotterup) and [Kristoffer Reitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristoffer_Reitan),[^cite_note-ap-26-how-watch-4] all but Reitan were ranked in the top 20 in the Official World Golf Ranking.[^cite_note-ap-asia-break-26-5][^cite_note-tap-edge-26-6] There were eight [Nordic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordic_countries) players in the field, the most of any year.[^cite_note-ap-asia-break-26-5] Neither [Phil Mickelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Mickelson) nor [Tiger Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_Woods) played, which had not happened since the [1994 tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Masters_Tournament).[^cite_note-ap-asia-break-26-5]

Participation in the Masters Tournament is by invitation only,[^cite_note-7] and the tournament has the smallest field of the major championships.[^cite_note-8] Invitations are issued to all past winners, recent major champions, leading finishers in the previous year's majors, leading players from the [PGA Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PGA_Tour) season, winners of full-point regular-season tournaments on the PGA Tour during the previous 12 months, leading players in the [Official World Golf Ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_World_Golf_Ranking), and some leading amateurs.&#x20;

In August 2025, Augusta National announced that invitations would also go to winners of several national opens, and no longer to winners during the PGA Tour's [FedEx Cup Fall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FedEx_Cup_Fall).[^cite_note-9]

## Pre-tournament events

### Champions Dinner

In keeping with tradition, defending champion [Rory McIlroy](3949844.md) chose the menu for the Masters Club Dinner, which took place on April 7. The dinner tradition, which began in 1952 when [Ben Hogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Hogan) invited past Masters champions to dine together, is attended by former champions as members of the Masters Club.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-mcilroy-dinner-26-16]

The appetizers included bacon-wrapped dates with goat cheese; elk sliders with caramelized onion jam and garlic aioli; peach and ricotta flatbread; and rock shrimp tempura. Yellowfin tuna carpaccio with foie gras, a toasted baguette and chives were followed by a choice of either wagyu filet mignon or salmon, each served with champ, Brussels sprouts, glazed carrots with brown butter, and Vidalia onion rings. Dessert was sticky toffee pudding with sauce and ice cream.[^cite_note-mcilroy-dinner-26-16]

The wine selection included a 2015 [Champagne Salon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champagne_Salon) brut; a 2022 [Domaine Leflaive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domaine_Leflaive) Burgundy; a 1990 [Château Lafite Rothschild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C3%A2teau_Lafite_Rothschild) Bordeaux; and a 1989 [Château d'Yquem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C3%A2teau_d'Yquem) Bordeaux.[^cite_note-mcilroy-dinner-26-16]

### Par 3 Contest

[Aaron Rai](53603874.md) won the [Par 3 Contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masters_Tournament_Par_3_Contest) with a score of 21 (−6), finishing one stroke ahead of [Jacob Bridgeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Bridgeman) and [John Keefer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Keefer), who tied for second at 22 (−5), followed by [J. J. Spaun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._Spaun) and [Mason Howell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason_Howell) at 24 (−3).[^cite_note-17] Seventeen players completed their own rounds without allowing family members or guests to hit shots, out of a field of 80 competitors. Four holes-in-one were recorded, including one by [Keegan Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keegan_Bradley), who became the first player to make a hole-in-one in consecutive years.[^cite_note-18] [Tommy Fleetwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Fleetwood), [Justin Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Thomas) and [Wyndham Clark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyndham_Clark) also recorded aces.[^cite_note-19] Comedian and actor [Kevin Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Hart) caddied for [Bryson DeChambeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryson_DeChambeau).[^cite_note-usa-hoefling-hart-20]

## Round summaries

### First round

*Thursday, April 9, 2026*

Honorary starters [Jack Nicklaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Nicklaus), [Tom Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Watson_\(golfer\)) and [Gary Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Player) opened the 90th edition of the Masters Tournament.[^cite_note-23] [Sam Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Burns) and defending champion [Rory McIlroy](3949844.md) shared the first-round lead after both posted 67 (−5). McIlroy recovered from an inconsistent start to record three consecutive birdies on holes 13 through 15.[^cite_note-24] Burns, who played in the morning, recorded an eagle on the par-5 2nd hole and added birdies on holes 12, 13 and 15.[^cite_note-25]

A group of three players, [Patrick Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Reed), [Jason Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Day) and [Kurt Kitayama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Kitayama), were tied for third at 69 (−3). [Justin Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Rose), runner-up in the previous tournament, reached 4-under late in his round but finished with consecutive bogeys to card a 70 (−2). ### Third round

*Saturday, April 11, 2026*

[Rory McIlroy](3949844.md) started with a six-shot advantage, but his lead was erased over [moving day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_golf#M). McIlroy's round was 73 (+1), including being 3 over par around [Amen Corner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amen_Corner_\(golf\)).[^cite_note-ap1day3-34] [Cameron Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Young), seeking to be the third consecutive golfer to win the Players Championship and the Masters Tournament in the same year, turned in the joint-lowest round of the day, a 65 (−7). This brought Young from eight shots back into a share of the lead with McIlroy, at 205 (−11), securing a place in the final pairing on Sunday.

[Sam Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Burns) scored a bogey-free 68 (−4) to leave him one shot behind the leaders. [Jason Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Day) produced a mid-round charge featuring four consecutive birdies, finishing with a 68 (−4), to move into contention at 8-under. [Justin Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Rose) turned in a second consecutive 69 (−3), leaving him also 8 under. [Shane Lowry](22834302.md) achieved a hole-in-one at the par-three 6th on his way to a 68, climbing into solo fourth at 9-under.[^cite_note-ap1day3-34] World number one [Scottie Scheffler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottie_Scheffler) recorded a bogey-free 65 to surge back into contention.[^cite_note-ap2day3-35]

The third-round scoring average of 70.63 was the lowest in third-round Masters history.[^cite_note-ap2day3-35]

### Final round

*Sunday, April 12, 2026*



[Rory McIlroy](3949844.md) won his second Masters title [wire-to-wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wire-to-wire) and became the fourth to defend the title.

Third-round co-leader [Rory McIlroy](3949844.md) shot a 71 (−1) to finish at 276 (−12) and win by one stroke over [Scottie Scheffler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottie_Scheffler), who shot a 68 (−4) for the round to finish alone in second at 277 (−11).[^cite_note-ref34_ap_final-36]

McIlroy immediately lost his share of the lead early in his round after a double bogey at the par-three 4th, where he pulled his tee shot and proceeded to 3-putt, falling behind the lead. He dropped another shot shortly after at the 6th, while playing partner [Cameron Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Young) and [Justin Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Rose), who was playing in the group ahead, both moved into contention.[^cite_note-ref35_guardian_live-37]

Rose, starting the day three shots back, produced a charge through the middle of the first nine with a run of three consecutive birdies to take the outright lead at −12, which afforded him a 2-stroke lead as he headed for the turn. McIlroy, meanwhile, steadied himself and responded with consecutive birdies on 7 and 8 to move back within one shot of the lead heading into the turn.[^cite_note-ref35_guardian_live-37]

On the 11th, Rose bogeyed after missing the green with his approach, allowing McIlroy to draw level. McIlroy then regained the outright lead on the second nine, capitalizing on scoring opportunities and avoiding further mistakes.[^cite_note-ref35_guardian_live-37] Rose then faltered through Amen Corner and the back nine, including a chunked chip on the 12th and a three-putt on the 13th after reaching the green in two, while McIlroy made key birdies at the 12th and 13th to take control with a two-shot lead.[^cite_note-ref35_guardian_live-37]

Scheffler, playing ahead, steadily climbed into contention with a bogey-free round and set the clubhouse lead at −11 after posting a 68, becoming the first player since 1942 to complete the weekend without a bogey at the Masters.[^cite_note-ref36_ap_scheffler-38]

Rose remained in contention after Amen Corner but failed to convert further birdie opportunities, and despite a late birdie on the 15th, a costly bogey on the 17th, missing a 3-foot par putt left him tied for third alongside [Tyrrell Hatton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrrell_Hatton), [Russell Henley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Henley) and Young at −10.[^cite_note-ref35_guardian_live-37]

After maintaining control through pars on the closing stretch, McIlroy took a two-shot lead to the 18th tee over Scheffler knowing a bogey would be enough. His drive found trouble in the right trees, but he recovered into the greenside bunker to save a closing bogey, securing victory by a single stroke.[^cite_note-ref37_reuters_final-39] With this victory, he joined [Jack Nicklaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Nicklaus), [Nick Faldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Faldo) and [Tiger Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_Woods) as the only players to repeat as champion.[^cite_note-40]

[Collin Morikawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collin_Morikawa) and [Sam Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Burns) finished tied for seventh at −9, while [Max Homa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Homa) and [Xander Schauffele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xander_Schauffele) rounded out the top ten at −8.[^cite_note-ref33_ap_overview-41]
