The [Republic of Korea Navy](2284275.md) has about 160 ships in [commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning) (a total displacement of about 232,285 tonnes[^cite_note-1]).

The vessels are categorized into four levels. A first-rate ship (*Sohn Wonyil-*&#x63;lass SS, DDG, DDH, LPH, MLS, and AOE) is commanded by a captain; a second-rate ship (SS, FFG, FF, PCC, LST, ATS, and ASR) by a commander; a third-rate ship (PKG, MSH, and MHC) by a lieutenant commander; and a fourth-rate craft (PKMR, PKM, and LSF) is commanded by a lieutenant or a warrant officer.

The ROK Navy employs the U.S. Navy-style letter based [hull classification symbols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hull_classification_symbols) to designate the types of its ships and [hull numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hull_number) to uniquely identify its vessels (e.g. DDH 975). The names are that of the historical figures, provinces, cities, counties, peaks, lakes, islands, and birds. The Chief of Naval Operations selects the names of ships.[^cite_note-2]

The [ship prefix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_prefix) for all the commissioned ROK Navy ship is **ROKS** (Republic of Korea Ship) when the names of ships are written in English.

## Notes

a. Hull number: The ROK Navy does not use the number '4' when assigning hull numbers to their ships since ROKS *Jirisan* (PC 704; formerly USS *PC-810*), during the [Korean War](16772.md), struck a mine and sank in December 1951, resulting in death of all sailors aboard. Currently only the hull numbers of the mine layers and submarine tender end with number '0'. The hull numbers of the submarines start with the number '0'.\
&#x20;b. Romanization of Ship names: Romanization is according to [Revised Romanization of Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revised_Romanization_of_Korean) (adopted in 2000), with exceptions of personal names. Names of ships commissioned before 2000 might have been romanized according to [McCune–Reischauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McCune%E2%80%93Reischauer). Examples of changes ([M-R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McCune%E2%80%93Reischauer) → [RR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revised_Romanization)): Chinhae → [Jinhae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinhae-gu); Kangnung → [Gangneung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangneung); Kimpo → [Gimpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gimpo); Kyongju → [Gyeongju](426047.md); Pusan → [Busan](68115.md); Taegu → [Daegu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daegu).\
&#x20;c. Delivery date: The date when the ROK Navy acquires a ship from the shipbuilder.
