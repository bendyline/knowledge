**Kahramanmaraş Province** ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *Kahramanmaraş ili*) is a [province](428896.md) and [metropolitan municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_municipalities_in_Turkey) of [Turkey](11125639.md). Its area is 14,520 km<sup>2</sup>,[^cite_note-2] and its population is 1,177,436 (2022).[^cite_note-tuik-1] Its provincial capital is the city of [Kahramanmaraş](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kahramanmara%C5%9F), and the [traffic code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_car_number_plates#Location_codes) is 46. Its Pazarcık and [Elbistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbistan) districts were impacted by the [2023 Turkey–Syria earthquakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Turkey%E2%80%93Syria_earthquakes), causing major damage to the city with more than 50,000 deaths.

## History

More than 12,600 residents of Kahramanmaraş province were killed during the [6 February 2023 earthquakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Turkey%E2%80%93Syria_earthquakes). Both earthquakes had epicenters in the province.[^cite_note-3] Many large towns and cities including [Elbistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbistan) had buildings collapse.[^cite_note-4]

Making up 1.83 % of Turkey's land area, Kahramanmaraş is traversed by the northeasterly line of equal latitude and longitude.[^cite_note-7] The majority of the province with around 60% of the total area is covered with mountains, being followed by plateaus and plains. Most of the mountains in the province is part of the Southeastern [Taurus Mountain range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taurus_Mountains). Due to the low [tree line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tree_line) most of the highest areas are bare, but lower regions have forests.[^cite_note-\:0-8]

Southern parts of the province has a hot-summer type [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate) (*Csa*), including the administrative center of Kahramanmaraş. Northern parts of the province show the characteristics of a [continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_climate) (*Dsa* and *Dsb*).[^cite_note-\:0-8] At the northernmost parts of the province lays the Elbistan Plain, where the town and district of [Elbistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbistan) is located. It has a [cold semi arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-arid_climate).

[Ceyhan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceyhan_River) is the most important river in the province. [Lake Kumaşır](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Kuma%C5%9F%C4%B1r?action=edit\&redlink=1), along with some small [crater lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crater_Lake) on the [Nurhak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nurhak_Mountains) and Ahir mountains makes the only natural body of water in the province, though there are multiple [dam reservoirs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reservoir) such as [Dam Lake Menzelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menzelet_Dam).[^cite_note-\:0-8]

## Health

[Air pollution in Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pollution_in_Turkey) is a persistent issue and has been linked to the usage of coal-fired power plants, such as the [Afşin-Elbistan power stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Af%C5%9Fin-Elbistan_power_stations).[^cite_note-10]

The province's most famous culinary specialty is Dondurma, also called "[Maraş ice cream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dondurma)", available in specialist Ice Cream shops throughout Turkey. The ice cream has an elastic consistency and is served with knife and fork, often accompanied by a slice of [baklava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baklava). It melts in the mouth while chewed, with a consistency similar to bubble gum.

Maraş [red peppers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capsicum) ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *kırmızı biber*) are also well known for its aroma, [vitamin C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitamin_C) content, and moderate hotness. Maraş pepper is different from far-east chilies as dried with support of Scientific Investigations and Universities which contains no [aflatoxin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aflatoxin) and no diseases.
