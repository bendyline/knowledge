# List of attacks during the 2026 Iran war

|  | This list is [incomplete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia\:WikiProject_Lists#Incomplete_lists); you can help by [adding missing items](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=List_of_attacks_during_the_2026_Iran_war\&action=edit). *(March 2026)* |

This is a list of [airstrikes](323167.md) and [bombardments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardment) carried out during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md). The strikes began on 28 February 2026, when [Israel](9282173.md) and the [United States](3434750.md) launched attacks on targets across [Iran](14653.md), codenamed Operation Roaring Lion[^cite_note-1] in Israel and Operation Epic Fury in the United States.

During the conflict, Iran reportedly carried out attacks or hostile actions against multiple countries and targets across the region, including [Israel](9282173.md), [Bahrain](18933277.md), [Kuwait](7515890.md), [Oman](22316.md)—where specific incidents involved [ports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port) and [ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship)—[Qatar](8238258.md), the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md), [Jordan](7515964.md), [Saudi Arabia](349303.md), [Syria](7515849.md), [Iraq](7515928.md), [Turkey](11125639.md), the British overseas territory of [Akrotiri and Dhekelia](1025144.md), and the [West Bank](33209.md). Additional alleged activity was reported in [Azerbaijan](746.md), while maritime targets included vessels of the [United States Navy](20518076.md) and Thai-flagged ships operating in the region.

### Thailand ships

Twenty crew members were evacuated by the Omani Navy, while three others remained on the damaged vessel as the minimum crew member awaiting rescue.[^cite_note-200]
