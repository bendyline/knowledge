# Primary ticket outlet

A **primary ticket outlet** is an organization that contracts directly with venues and promoters to sell event tickets on its behalf.[^cite_note-sports-1]

Primary ticket outlets have a direct relationship with the owner of a venue or event. They will often use software to manage the sale of tickets for their clients.[^cite_note-sports-1]

## Booking fees

Primary ticket outlets charge a booking fee that is paid by the consumer to help pay for services.[^cite_note-2]
