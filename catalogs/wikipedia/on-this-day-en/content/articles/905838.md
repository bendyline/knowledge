**Anambra** ([Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/2/29/LL-Q33578_%28ibo%29-Chinwoke_01-Anambra_State.wav/LL-Q33578_%28ibo%29-Chinwoke_01-Anambra_State.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> [Igbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_language): *Alaọha Anambra*) is a [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states) in the [Southeastern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_East_\(Nigeria\)) region of [Nigeria](21383.md) bordered by [Delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_State) to the west, [Imo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imo_State) and [Rivers](1966992.md) to the south, [Abia](905817.md) to the southeast, [Enugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu_State) to the east and [Kogi](299399.md) to the north.[^cite_note-5] [Awka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awka) is the state's capital[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] while [Onitsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onitsha) is its most populous city.[^cite_note-8]

The state name was inherited from the former Anambra State, a territory that consisted of the present-day Enugu State, Anambra State and parts of Ebonyi State. The old Anambra State was formed in 1976 from the former [East Central State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Central_State). The state is named after [Omambala River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omambala_River), a river that runs through the state. Anambra is the anglicized form of Omambala.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] The State capital is [Awka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awka). The city of [Onitsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onitsha), a historic port city from the pre-colonial era, remains an important centre of commerce within the state, while [Nnewi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nnewi) is the second largest commercial and industrial city in the state.[^cite_note-11]

Nicknamed the "Light of the Nation", Anambra State is the [fourteenth most populous state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population)[^cite_note-12] in the nation, although that has seriously been argued against as [Onitsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onitsha), the state's largest and most populous urban area was discovered to be over 8.5 million in population in 2022 by Africapolis which makes Onitsha one of the largest urban areas in [Nigeria](21383.md) by population.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] The area currently known as Anambra State has been the site of numerous civilizations since at least the 9th century AD, including the ancient [Kingdom of Nri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Nri), whose capital was the historic town of [Igbo-Ukwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo-Ukwu) within the state. Residents of Anambra State are primarily [Igbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_people),[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] with the [Igbo language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_language) serving as a [lingua franca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingua_franca) throughout the state.[^cite_note-19]

During the [Nigerian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Civil_War) (1967–1970), Anambra State was part of the secessionist [Republic of Biafra](44431.md) formed by Igbo nationalists. Anambra was severely affected by the war.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] Today, Anambra State is one of the most [urbanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanization) states in Nigeria.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

## Etymology

The name Anambra is the merging of [Anam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anam,_Nigeria) and the English word 'branch'. Anam is a clan in the [Omambala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omambala_River) region[^cite_note-24] and the last Igbo speaking community the British colonialists encountered while heading up to Northern Nigeria from across the riverine areas. They usually described present day Anambra as 'Anam branch' to their colleagues up North. Reason Anam together with some neighboring clans was Anambra LGA when the state was created. It is now Anambra-West LGA with Olumbanasa.

## History

Anambra's history stretches to the 9th century AD, as revealed by archaeological excavations at [Igbo-Ukwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaeology_of_Igbo-Ukwu) and [Ezira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezira). It has great works of art in [iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron), [bronze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze), [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper), and [pottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pottery). These have revealed a sophisticated divine Kingship administrative system, which held sway in the area of Anambra from c. 948 AD to 1911. In some towns, such as [Ogidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogidi,_Anambra_State) and others, local families had hereditary rights to kingship for centuries.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

Great Britain recognised some of these traditional kings and leaders in their system of indirect rule of the Protectorate of South [Nigeria](21383.md). Beginning in the 19th century, they appointed some noble leaders as Warrant Chiefs, authorizing them to collect taxes, among other duties.

Anambra is in the Igbo-dominated area that seceded as part of an independent [Biafra](44431.md) in 1967, following rising tensions with Northern Nigeria. During the [Nigerian/Biafran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Civil_War) war (1967–1970), Biafran engineers constructed a relief airstrip in the town of Uli/Amorka (code named "Annabelle"). Extremely dangerous relief flights took off from [Sao Tome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sao_Tome) and other sites loaded with tons of food and medicine for the distressed Biafran population. Uli/Amorka airstrip was the site where American pilots such as Alex Nicoll, and scores of others, delivered tons of relief supplies to the Biafran population.[^cite_note-27]

Disgusted by the suffering and mounting death toll in [Biafra](44431.md) from starvation, as well as the continuous harassment of the relief planes by the [Nigerian Airforce](538869.md), [Carl Gustaf von Rosen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Gustaf_von_Rosen) resigned as a [Red Cross](15487.md) relief pilot. He helped Biafra to form an Airforce of five Minicoin planes [Malmö MFI-9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malm%C3%B6_MFI-9) stationed at the Uga airstrip. He named his tiny but effective air force "[Babies of Biafra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biafran_airlift)" in honour of the babies who died from starvation inside Biafra.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

Old Anambra State was created in 1976 from part of [East Central State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Central_State), and its capital was [Enugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu_\(city\)). In 1991, a re-organisation divided Anambra into two states, Anambra and [Enugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu_State,_Nigeria). The capital of the New Anambra is [Awka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awka).[^cite_note-31]

## Geography



Agulu Lake

### Location

Boundaries are formed by [Delta State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_State) and [Edo State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_State) to the west for about 97 km across the [River Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Niger), [Imo State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imo_State) and [Rivers State](1966992.md) (for four km) to the south, [Abia State](905817.md) to the southeast for 18 km, [Enugu State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu_State) to the east for 139 km, and [Kogi State](299399.md) to the north for about 40 km. The name was derived from the [Anambra River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anambra_River) (Omambala) which flows through the area and is a tributary of the River Niger.

Anambra is the eighth-most populated state in the [Federal Republic of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Republic_of_Nigeria) and the second-most densely populated state in [Nigeria](21383.md) after [Lagos State](626168.md). The stretch of more than 45 km between the towns of [Oba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oba,_Anambra) and [Amorka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amorka) contains a cluster of numerous thickly populated villages and small towns, giving the area an estimated average density of 1,500–2,000 persons per square kilometre.[^cite_note-32]

### Climate

The state has a Tropical wet and dry or [savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savanna) with yearly temperature of 28.99 °C (84.18 °F) and it is -0.47% lower than Nigeria's averages. Anambra typically receives about 212.36 millimetres (8.36 inches) of precipitation and has 243.38 rainy days (66.68% of the time) annually.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34]

The state has been described as one of the worst places affected by the [climate change](5042951.md) in Nigeria and sub-Saharan Africa in 2022 according to the Climate Change Education and Action Programme, (CLEAP).[^cite_note-35]

With an annual population growth rate of 2.21 percent per annum, Anambra State has over 60% of its people living in urban areas. It is one of the most urbanized states in Nigeria.[^cite_note-36]

The major urban centres of Anambra State are [Onitsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onitsha), Nnewi, [Ekwulobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekwulobia), Ihiala, [Aguleri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguleri) and [Awka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awka), the state capital. Awka and Onitsha had developed as pre-colonial urban centres: Awka was the craft industrial centre of the Nri hegemony. Onitsha is a city state on the Niger, having developed as a river port and commercial centre.

Onitsha is a fast-growing commercial city and has developed to become a huge conurbation extending to [Idemili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idemili_North), Oyi and Anambra East LGAs, with one of the largest markets in West Africa.

In 2012 the tri-city area was dubbed the Onitsha-Nnewi-Awka (ONA) Industrial Axis, in recognition of the expanding industrial capacity. [Nnewi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nnewi) (sometimes called the [Taiwan](25734.md) of Nigeria)[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38] is a rapidly developing industrial and commercial centre. Designated as the state capital, Awka has regained its precolonial administrative eminence.

## Economy



Innoson Vehicles in Nnewi

Anambra houses the first Nigerian vehicle manufacturer, [Innoson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innoson_Vehicle_Manufacturing), an automobile manufacturer, located in [Nnewi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nnewi).[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

People all over [West Africa](67393.md) travel to [Onitsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onitsha) and [Nnewi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nnewi) to trade, which boost the internal revenue of the state. Onitsha is among the top commercial cities in Africa.[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43]

Agriculture is an important economic sector in Anambra. [Oil palms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elaeis_guineensis), [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice), [yams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yam_\(vegetable\)) and [cassava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassava) are among the crops grown. [Fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing) is also part of the economic mainstay of Anambra State more especially for communities along the riverine areas.

Anambra is a home of innovation, inventions and creativity. There have been several innovations in Anambra, due to its appreciation of education, which has added to the state's GDP. One of those was in 2018, when two brothers, Atikpo Chukwuebuka and Ubaka Chukwuebuka, were the first to invent a machine, that could wash off the bitterness from bitter-leaf (a popular vegetable staple in Nigeria).[^cite_note-techecon_bitter-44] This machine will greatly improve the productivity of vegetable farming in the state.

The [export](307592.md) of agricultural items is a continual boost to Anambra Internal Generated Revenue. In 2017, the state generated \$5 million from the export of washed bitter leaf.[^cite_note-techecon_bitter-44]

There is a huge deposit of oil and gas in Nigeria, with the [Anambra Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anambra_Basin) having a great potential of 1000 trillion cubic feet of gas reserves untapped. Having more than 13 oil wells situated in Anambra, the state has the capacity to produce more than 100,000 barrels of crude oil per day, with indigenous companies like Orient Petroleum, and Sterling Oil Exploration and Production Co. LTD (SEEPCO), already leading the way.[^cite_note-45]

### Natural resources

Anambra is rich in [natural gas](22131.md), [crude oil](23195.md), [bauxite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bauxite), and [ceramic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceramic).[^cite_note-\:0-46] It has an almost 100 percent [arable soil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arable_land).

Anambra state has many other resources in terms of agro-based activities such as fisheries and farming, as well as land cultivated for [pasturing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasturing) and [animal husbandry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_husbandry).

#### Oil and gas

In the year 2006, a foundation-laying ceremony for the first Nigerian private refinery, Orient Petroleum Refinery (OPR), was made at [Aguleri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguleri) area.[^cite_note-\:1-47] The Orient Petroleum Resource Ltd (OPRL), owner of OPR, was licensed in June 2002, by the Federal Government to construct a private refinery with a capacity of 55,000 barrels per day (\~7,500 t/d).

In 2012, following the efforts of Governor [Peter Obi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Obi) and other stakeholders of Orient Petroleum, Anambra State became an oil-producing state. The indigenous company struck oil in the [Anambra River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anambra_River) basin.[^cite_note-\:2-48]

On 2 August 2015, the management of Orient Petroleum Resources Plc said the company planned to increase its crude oil production to 3,000 barrels per day by September 2015, as it stepped up production activities in two new oil wells in its [Aguleri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguleri) oil fields. An indigenous company, Nails and Stanley Ltd, was to establish a [gas plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gasplant) at Umueje in [Ayamelum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayamelum) Local Government Area to support economic activities in the oil and gas industry in the state.[^cite_note-\:3-49]

Following the attribution of eleven (11) oil wells by the [Revenue Mobilization Allocation and Fiscal Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revenue_Mobilisation_Allocation_and_Fiscal_Commission) (RMAFC) in August 2021, Anambra State joined the league of oil producing States in Nigeria.[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52] The process to amend the law that set up the [Niger Delta Development Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger_Delta_Development_Commission) (NDDC) through which infrastructure is provided to oil producing States has started. The purpose is to include Anambra State in the list of oil producing States as stated in the NDDC law through the amendment of Section 2(1)(b) of the NDDC Establishment ACT CAP N 86 LFN 2004.[^cite_note-53]

## Infrastructure and urbanization



&#x20;Niger Bridge

Since the late 1990s, there has been a migration from rural to urban areas in the state, resulting in Anambra becoming a highly urbanized state: 62% of its population lives in urban areas. In October 2015, the [APGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Progressives_Grand_Alliance)-led state government of [Willie Obiano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Obiano), signed a memorandum of understanding with [Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway) modular housing company, Affordable Building Concepts International, for 10,000 housing units to be built in the state.[^cite_note-54]

Given decades of neglect of infrastructure and bad governance, the shift in human migration has posed problems for the state. Infrastructure improvements, both physical and social, have lagged behind the growth in population. There are problems in environmental sanitation, erosion control, and provision of social services. Major cities have become characterized by inadequate and deteriorated road networks and walkways, unregulated building patterns, poor sanitation, uncontrolled street trading, mountains of garbage, and chaotic transport systems, creating congestion, noise pollution, and overcrowding.[^cite_note-55]

The government of Peter Obi, with the assistance of the [UN-HABITAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Human_Settlements_Programme), produced 20-year structural plans (2009–2028) for three major cities in the State: [Onitsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onitsha), [Nnewi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nnewi) and [Awka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awka) – the Capital Territory, to restore urban planning and guide their growth into the future.

The plans contain policies and proposals for land use, city beautification, road infrastructure, industrial development, housing, waste disposal, water supply and health and educational facilities to turn the cities into successful urban areas that can generate employment and wealth, and provide high living standards for their residents.[^cite_note-56]

Anambra became the first state in Nigeria to adopt structural plans for its cities.[^cite_note-Britanica1-57] With effective implementation, it should systematically grow as a major economic center in Nigeria, and West Africa.

The process of [urbanization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanization) is fairly contributed by population growth, immigration, migration, and infrastructure initiatives like good road, water, power, and gardens, resulting in the growth of villages into towns, town into cities and cities into metros. To have ecologically feasible development, planning requires an understanding of the growth dynamics. There is a fear that if too many people leave the villages, only the aged men and women will be left to farm. This pattern has been seen in Amesi, Akpo, and Achina towns in Aguata local government area. They have been important in the production of yam, cocoyam, and cassava through consistent agriculture, but such activities have suffered due to the out-migration of youth to the urban centres. There has been both food scarcity in the region and over-population in [urban areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_area).

To upgrade the state capital and improve traffic, [Awka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awka), Governor [Willie Obiano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Obiano) signed off on construction of three flyovers between the [Amawbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amawbia) and Arroma end of the Enugu-Onitsha Expressway, a distance of about three kilometres within the city.[^cite_note-58] [Anambra International Cargo Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinua_Achebe_International_Airport), [Umueri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umuleri), opened on 7 December 2021.[^cite_note-59]

## Culture and tourism

[Ogbunike Caves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogbunike_Caves), listed by [UNESCO](21786641.md)[^cite_note-61] as a [World Heritage Site](44940.md),[^cite_note-62] is one of the most visited tourist sites in Anambra State. It is classified as a sandstone cave (Lateritic sandstones of Campanian-Miocene age).[^cite_note-63] The [Owerre Ezukala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owerre_Ezukala) caves and waterfalls are great tourist attractions in the state.[^cite_note-64] Largely unexplored, the caves are said to be the largest in West Africa.[^cite_note-65][^cite_note-66]

The indigenous ethnic groups in Anambra state are the [Igbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_people) (99% of the population) and a small population of people who are bilingual[^cite_note-67]



&#x20;Igbo Ukwu 9th century bronzes monuments

[Igbo Ukwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_Ukwu) Museum:[^cite_note-68] Igbo Ukwu is an ancient town known for its astonishing metal crafts; it continues to attract tourists to see its bronze artifacts. First noticed in 1938, the bronzes were later excavated by [Thurstan Shaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurstan_Shaw) (an English archaeologist). They have been dated to the 9th century, and are of high value and historic relevance.[^cite_note-69]

Other places of interest in the state includes:

- Ogba Waterfalls; considered to be one of the largest cave in West Africa, which will take upwards to two hours to explore. It is located in Owerre Ezeukala, that borders three other states: Abia, Enugu and Imo.

- Agulu Lake; it is very wide and lengthy with habitats that support several marine species, including crocodile and water turtle. Fishing is prohibited there.

- Rojenny Tourist Village; located in Oba and has small games reserves that are populated with a variety of animals such as Baboons, Chimpanzees, Alligators etc. It also has African bar-restaurant, children's park, Olympic size swimming pool, etc.

- Odinani Museum Nri; located in Anaocha Local Government, it houses lots of archaeological discoveries, cultural and traditional religious artifacts.

- Ikenga Virgin Forest; the only virgin forest in Nigeria as the trees have never been deforested nor its land cultivated. It is home to species of animals that have gone extinct in other parts of the country.

- The palaces of [Obi of Onitsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obi_of_Onitsha) and [Eze Nri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_rulers_of_Nri).

  [^cite_note-70]

Traditional music in Anambra includes a number of genres such as [Igbo Highlife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_highlife), [Ogene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogene), Igbo Gospel, Ekpili, etc. Some notable traditional musicians from Anambra includes: Chief [Stephen Osita Osadebe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Stephen_Osita_Osadebe), Chief [Oliver De Coque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_De_Coque), Maduka Morocco, [Umu Obiligbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umu_Obiligbo), [Flavour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavour_\(musician\)), etc.

Dance is one of the best ways of self-expression in the Igboland. The most popular dance from the state is Mkpokiti dance. Some of the most popular dances in Anambra include: [Atilogwu Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atilogwu_Dance), Egedege dance, Igbo Ndi Eze, etc.[^cite_note-74]

The Anglican Cathedral is All Saints Cathedral, [Onitsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onitsha).[The Anglican Province on the Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_on_the_Niger) in Onitsha was the original diocese in Nigeria, under Archbishop [Owen Chidozie Nwokolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Chidozie_Nwokolo), and includes eight other [Dioceses of Awka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Awka) under Archbishop [Alexander Chibuzo Ibezim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Chibuzo_Ibezim), [Nnewi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Nnewi) under Bishop [Ndubuisi Obi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ndubuisi_Obi), [Aguata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Aguata) under Archbishop [Samuel Ezeofor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Ezeofor), [Ogbaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Ogbaru) under Bishop [Prosper Afam Amah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prosper_Afam_Amah), [Ihiala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Ihiala) under Bishop [Israel Kelue Okoye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Kelue_Okoye), [Niger West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Niger_West) under Bishop [Johnson Ekwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnson_Ekwe) in Umueri, [Mbamili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Mbamili) under Bishop [Obiora Uzochukwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obiora_Uzochukwu), [Amichi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Amichi) under Bishop [Ephraim Ikeakor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ephraim_Ikeakor).

## Government and politics

Main article: [Government of Anambra State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Anambra_State)

### Executive

Further information: [Governor of Anambra State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Anambra_State) and [Deputy Governor of Anambra State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Governor_of_Anambra_State)

The position of Governor of Anambra State is one of the most powerful in Nigeria. The governor is elected on a ticket with their deputy governor as the only statewide elected executive officials in the state; the governor then appoints the entire executive cabinet and judges of the state's Supreme Court. [Charles Soludo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Soludo) ([APGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Progressives_Grand_Alliance)) is the [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Anambra_State). The governor's house is called "Light House", located in Agu-Awka, [Awka South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awka_South), [Awka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awka), the state's capital.

The office of the Deputy Governor was established when the state was created. Roy Umenyi was elected the first [deputy governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Governor_of_Anambra_State) on the ticket with Governor [Jim Nwobodo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Nwobodo) and took office in October 1979. [Onyeka Ibezim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onyeka_Ibezim) ([APGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Progressives_Grand_Alliance)) is the deputy governor and assumed office in 2022.

### Legislative

The State has a three-tier administrative structure: State, Local and Autonomous community levels. The three arms at state level are the [Executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_functions), the [Legislative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) and the [Judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary). The executive arm is headed by an elected governor, who is assisted by a deputy governor, commissioners and executive advisers.

Anambra's political history can be described as varied. Until the early 21st century, it was marked by considerable unrest. Having a long list of "firsts" in Nigerian history, it has been known by the sobriquet as, "The Light of The Nation". On 29 May 1999, [Chinwoke Mbadinuju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinwoke_Mbadinuju) was sworn in as civilian governor of Anambra state, after many years of military rule. His administration was plagued by deep problems: the most notable was withholding of teachers' salaries in the school. The teachers finally conducted a ten-month strike in all the government secondary schools in the state.[^cite_note-114]

Before Mbadinuju's rule, secondary education had been free of charge. His administration imposed a tuition fee of 3,000 [Naira](178686.md) per term, for all secondary schools, which led to an unprecedented massive demonstration by secondary school students from all over the state. Many people attribute Mbadinuju's failure to political godfathers; his successor also struggled. On 26 May 2003, [Chris Ngige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Ngige) was sworn in as the new governor of the state, but he was removed in March 2006 after Peter Obi of APGA filed charges against him of electoral malpractice. The Court of Appeal in Enugu asserted that Ngige's apparent victory in the 2003 election was fraudulent and ordered him to leave the seat.[^cite_note-gua-115]

Obi was ousted by a faction of the [Anambra State House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anambra_State_House_of_Assembly) on 2 November 2006 and replaced by [Virginia Etiaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Etiaba), his deputy.[^cite_note-thd-116] On 9 February 2007, Etiaba handed power back to Obi after the Court of Appeal had nullified Obi's removal.[^cite_note-tri-117]

On 14 April 2007, [Andy Uba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Uba) of PDP was "elected" as the new governor of the state and, on 29 May, was sworn in.[^cite_note-118] Reported to be massively rigged, the election was widely criticised. On 14 June 2007 the [Supreme Court of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Nigeria) ruled that Peter Obi's tenure had not ended; therefore, there was no vacancy in the governorship. It removed Andy Uba from office and replaced him with his predecessor Obi.[^cite_note-119]

On 6 February 2010, Peter Obi was re-elected governor for a second term of four years, after a hot contest with [Chris Ngige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Ngige), a former governor of the state; Prof. [Charles Soludo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Soludo), a former governor of the [Central Bank of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Nigeria); and [Andy Uba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Uba), who was a strong voice in the state's politics.[^cite_note-120][^cite_note-121] Other contenders included Mrs [Uche Ekwunife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uche_Ekwunife), Prince Nicholas Ukachukwu, and many others. Twenty-five contestants ran for the office. Obi was affirmed as the winner of the election, having more than 30% votes above the immediate runner-up. Chief [Willie Obiano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Obiano) was sworn in on 17 March 2014 after winning the 16 November 2013 election. Governor Willie Obiano of All Progressives Grand Alliance (APGA) was sworn in for a second term in office on 17 March 2018 after the victory at 18 November 2017 elections.[^cite_note-122] He handed over to Charles Soludo, winner of the 9 November 2021 gubernatorial election in Anambra state on 17 March 2022.[^cite_note-123][^cite_note-124]

## Electoral system

The electoral system of each state is selected using a modified two-round system. To be elected in the first round, a candidate must receive the plurality of the vote and over 25% of the vote in at least two -third of the State local government Areas. If no candidate passes threshold, a second round will be held between the top candidate and the next candidate to have received a plurality of votes in the highest number of local government areas.[^cite_note-127]
