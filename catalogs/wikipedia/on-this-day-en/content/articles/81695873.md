The **High Military Command for the Restoration of National Security and Public Order**[^cite_note-13] ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Alto Comando Militar para a Restauração da Segurança Nacional e Ordem Pública*) is the current [military junta](216815.md) of [Guinea-Bissau](12186.md), announced on 26 November 2025 by its Spokesperson, [Brigadier General](206220.md) [Dinis Incanha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinis_Incanha), during the [2025 coup d'état](81695397.md).[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] After claiming control, they ordered the closure of all [borders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category\:Borders_of_Guinea-Bissau), established an overnight curfew, and ordered the suspension of the [electoral process](76446266.md) until further notice.[^cite_note-16]

On 27 November 2025, Incanha presided over the investiture ceremony of General [Horta Inta-A Na Man](81702722.md) as [Transitional President of Guinea-Bissau](392259.md) and Head of the High Military Command for the Restoration of National Security and Public Order.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]
