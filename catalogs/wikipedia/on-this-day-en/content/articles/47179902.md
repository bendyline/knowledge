# Visa requirements for Somali citizens

**Visa requirements for Somali citizens** are administrative entry restrictions imposed on citizens of [Somalia](27358.md) by the authorities of other states.

As of 2026, [Somali citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_citizenship) had visa-free or visa on arrival access to 32 countries and territories, ranking the [Somali passport](21445500.md) 97th in terms of travel freedom according to the [Henley Passport Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henley_Passport_Index).[^cite_note-1]

## Vaccination

Many African countries, including [Angola](701.md), [Benin](3459.md), [Burkina Faso](3470.md), [Cameroon](5447.md), [Central African Republic](5478.md), [Chad](5488.md), [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md), [Republic of the Congo](19599929.md), [Côte d'Ivoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%B4te_d'Ivoire), [Equatorial Guinea](9366.md), [Gabon](12027.md), [Ghana](12067.md), [Guinea](12176.md), [Liberia](17791.md), [Mali](19127.md), [Mauritania](18778516.md), [Niger](21373.md), [Rwanda](25645.md), [São Tomé and Príncipe](20026306.md), [Senegal](27278.md), [Sierra Leone](27308.md), [Uganda](31816.md), [Zambia](34415.md) require all incoming passengers to have a current [International Certificate of Vaccination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Certificate_of_Vaccination). Some other countries require vaccination only if the passenger is coming from an infected area.[^cite_note-348]

## Passport validity

Many countries require passport validity of no less than 6 months and one or two blank pages.[^cite_note-349]
