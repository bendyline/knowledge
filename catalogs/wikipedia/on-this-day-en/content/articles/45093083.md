The **governor of Odesa Oblast** is the [head of the executive branch of government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Local_State_Administration_\(Ukraine\)) for the [Odesa Oblast](61438266.md) of [Ukraine](31750.md). The office of [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor) is an appointed position, with officeholders being appointed by the [president of Ukraine](20688479.md), on recommendation from the [prime minister](1256678.md), to serve a four-year term.

The official residence for the governor is located in [Odesa](55545.md). Due to the current [Russo-Ukrainian war](42085878.md), since 24 February 2022, the governor of Odesa Oblast is officially called *Head of the Odesa Regional Military Administration*.
