**Samburu County** is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Kenya) in the former [Rift Valley Province](72874.md), [Kenya](188171.md) which covers an area of roughly 21,000 km<sup>2</sup> (8,000 mi<sup>2</sup>) in northern [Kenya](188171.md) where the [Samburu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samburu_people), [Turkana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkana_people) and many other tribes live. It stretches north from the [Wuaso Ng'iro River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuaso_Ng'iro_River) to the south of [Lake Turkana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Turkana). According to the 2019 census, the county had a population of 310,327.[^cite_note-1]

Within Samburu County are the towns of [Maralal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maralal) (the capital and largest town),[^cite_note-2] [Baragoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baragoi), [Archers Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archers_Post), [South Horr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Horr), [Wamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wamba,_Kenya), [Lodosoit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodosoit), and [Kisima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kisima).

It also includes the [Samburu National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samburu_National_Reserve), [Bisanadi National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bisanadi_National_Reserve), and [Buffalo Springs National Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo_Springs_National_Reserve), [Mount Ng'iro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Ng'iro), [Ndoto Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ndoto_Mountains), [Mathews Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathews_Range) (Ol Doinyo Lenkiyo), [Kirisia Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirisia_Hills?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Loroki Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loroki_Forest?action=edit\&redlink=1).

The main access road to Samburu County is the [A4 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A4_road_\(Kenya\)) from [Rumuruti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumuruti)-Maralal-Baragoi, which as of 2019 was being tarmacked from Rumuruti up to Maralal.[^cite_note-3]

There is a town named [Samburu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samburu,_Kenya) in [Kwale County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwale_County), Kenya, but it is not related to Samburu County or Samburu people.

## County subdivisions

The county has three constituencies: [Samburu East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samburu_East_Constituency), [Samburu West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samburu_West_Constituency), and [Samburu North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samburu_North_Constituency). The headquarters of Samburu Central is Maralal, Samburu North is Baragoi and headquarters for Samburu East is Wamba although there has been controversy about the headquarters of Samburu East. Some schools of thought proposed Archers Post, a rapidly growing town, while others proposed Wamba town a less rapidly growing town.[^cite_note-6]

## Government

The Samburu County Government is headed by Governor Jonathan Lati Leleliit who won the Samburu County's gubernatorial race in the 2022 general elections under the [United Democratic Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Democratic_Alliance_\(Kenya\)) (UDA). He garnered 40,740 votes against Dr. Richard Lesiyampe of the Jubilee Party who got 26,834 votes, and Daniel Lekupe an independent candidate, who garnered 1,847 votes, coming in third, and Richard Leiyagu who came last with 771 votes. He succeeded the former governor, [Moses Lenolkulal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moses_Kasaine), who retired after two terms in office.[^cite_note-9] A member of the national Jubilee party, Lenolkulal was the first governor following Kenya's devolution to county administrations.[^cite_note-10]
