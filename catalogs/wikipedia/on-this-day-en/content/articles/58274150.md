**Alireza Tangsiri** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): علیرضا تنگسیری; 27 August 1962 – 26 March 2026) was an Iranian naval officer who served as the commander of the [IRGC Navy](4676345.md).[^cite_note-mehr-1][^cite_note-2] He was killed by the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).

## Military career

Tangsiri was a [naval brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_brigade) commander during the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War). He later headed the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy](4676345.md) (IRGCN) 1st Naval District in [Bandar Abbas](464348.md).[^cite_note-band-3]

In September 2007, Tangsiri said that if the United States were "to clash with us, we would chase them even to the [Gulf of Mexico](21076367.md)."[^cite_note-\:gom-4]

In January 2016, after Iran detained 10 US sailors in the [2016 United States–Iran naval incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_United_States%E2%80%93Iran_naval_incident) when they had engine failure and entered Iranian waters, Tangsiri described it as "U.S. forces surrendering to Iran."[^cite_note-\:gom-4]

On 23 August 2018, Iran's Supreme Leader, [Ali Khamenei](385653.md), appointed Tangsiri as commander of the IRGCN.[^cite_note-band-3]

In November 2021, Tangsiri said that the United States had "come to realize the Islamic Republic’s superiority at sea."[^cite_note-se-5] In February 2025, he unveiled Iran's first [drone carrier](76996952.md), a commercial vessel that had been repurposed, which he described as the “largest naval military project” in the history of Iran.[^cite_note-dro-6] In March 2025, Tangsiri warned that "If the enemies make a mistake, we will send them to the depths of hell," after U.S. President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) wrote Iran's supreme leader Khamenei urging Iran to reach a nuclear agreement with the U.S. or else face military action.[^cite_note-sar-7]

In April 2025, Tangsiri said that the [United States Navy](20518076.md) was "incapable" of challenging Iran's maritime dominance.[^cite_note-\:dom-8]

## Political views

In 2013, Tangsiri said: "the Israelis are [Jews](25955086.md) and the Americans are [Christians](18337522.md). Our [Quran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quran) stresses that they are not our friends."[^cite_note-\:dysy-9][^cite_note-10]

In 2022, Tangsiri claimed that [Saudi rulers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Saud) descend from the [Jews of Medina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jews_of_Medina) and [Khaybar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Khaybar), enemies of the Prophet [Muhammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad).[^cite_note-\:6-11] He has previously threatened American and British shipping in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md).[^cite_note-\:0-12]

## U.S. sanctions

On 24 June 2019, the [U.S. Treasury Department](53667.md) sanctioned Tangsiri as a [Specially Designated Global Terrorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specially_Designated_Global_Terrorist), freezing any of his U.S. assets and banning U.S. persons from doing business with him, under [Executive Order 13224](1296167.md).[^cite_note-13] He was sanctioned with secondary sanctions in 2024 due to his position on the board of [Paravar Pars Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paravar_Pars_Company?action=edit\&redlink=1), an Iranian military company which develops [unmanned aerial vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_aerial_vehicles) and is sanctioned by the United States.[^cite_note-\:0-12]

## Assassination

On 26 March 2026, Israel's defense minister [Israel Katz](19041378.md) and the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) announced the assassination of Tangsiri.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-\:1-15][^cite_note-\:2-16] The strike took place in the port city of [Bandar Abbas](464348.md).[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] Katz claimed that Tangsiri had overseen the [blockade of the Strait of Hormuz](82550763.md) and was directly responsible for attacks in the area.[^cite_note-19] The IDF also claimed that other senior IRGC Navy commanders were killed in the strike.[^cite_note-20] Tangsiri's death was later confirmed by Iranian authorities.[^cite_note-reuters-21]

According to [Al Jazeera](30204052.md), Ali Hashem described Tangsiri as a well-known IRGC Navy commander who helped shape Iran’s naval doctrine in the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md) and was involved in developing maritime drone capabilities. He also reported that Tangsiri’s social media accounts had posted updates on vessel movements through the strait, which he said were linked to fluctuations in global energy prices. Hashem stated that, according to Israeli reports, Tangsiri survived two assassination attempts in 2025 and 2026 before being killed in Bandar Abbas.[^cite_note-22]
