**Andrey Atanasov Gurov**[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-4] (also **Gyurov**) ([Bulgarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_language): Андрей Атанасов Гюров, born 31 December 1975) is a Bulgarian [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist), [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor), and [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician). On 19 February 2026, he was appointed caretaker [prime minister](335071.md) of the [Republic of Bulgaria.](3415.md) Currently a [political independent](2172378.md), he previously served as Deputy Governor of the [Bulgarian National Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_National_Bank) from 2023 to 2026 and Member of the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Bulgaria) from 2021 to 2023.

## Biography

Gurov has been serving as a deputy governor of the [Bulgarian National Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_National_Bank) since 2023.[^cite_note-5] From 2021 to 2023, he was a member of the [National Assembly](2122384.md).[^cite_note-6] Prior to becoming Prime Minister, Gyurov worked as a Business Professor in the [American University in Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_University_in_Bulgaria).[^cite_note-7] On 11 February 2026, President [Iliana Iotova](34677899.md) nominated Gyurov as caretaker prime minister pending the [2026 Bulgarian parliamentary election](76476389.md).[^cite_note-8] He was formally appointed the next week.[^cite_note-9]

Before assuming office, he served as a [Member of Parliament](98066.md) in the [47th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/47th_National_Assembly_of_Bulgaria)[^cite_note-\:1-4], [48th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/48th_National_Assembly_of_Bulgaria)[^cite_note-10], and [49th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/49th_National_Assembly_of_Bulgaria)[^cite_note-11] [National Assemblies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Bulgaria), chairman of the parliamentary group[^cite_note-\:1-4] of [We Continue the Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Continue_the_Change), and deputy governor[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-12] of the [Bulgarian National Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_National_Bank). In his political work, he emphasises transparency in [governance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governance), the modernisation of the [economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy), and improving the effectiveness of state institutions.
