**Moneta** ([/məniːtə/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA) or [/moʊniːtə/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English)) is a [census-designated place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census-designated_place) (CDP) in southern [Bedford County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford_County,_Virginia), [Virginia](32432.md), United States. The community is located along [Route 122](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Route_122) between the towns of [Bedford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford,_Virginia) and [Rocky Mount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Mount,_Virginia). It is part of the [Lynchburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynchburg,_Virginia) [Metropolitan Statistical Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynchburg_metropolitan_area). As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), Moneta had a population of 450.[^cite_note-Census2020PLLede-1]

## History

[Olive Branch Missionary Baptist Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_Branch_Missionary_Baptist_Church) and the [Holland-Duncan House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holland-Duncan_House) are listed on the [National Register of Historic Places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places).[^cite_note-nris-2]

On August 26, 2015, the community made news when journalists [Alison Parker and Adam Ward were murdered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murders_of_Alison_Parker_and_Adam_Ward) on live television by former colleague Vester Flanagan at a nearby shopping center and marina.[^cite_note-WDBJ-3]

## Geography

According to the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the CDP has a total area of 3.846 square miles (9.96 km²).

Moneta first appeared as a [census designated place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_designated_place) in the [2020 U.S. census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_U.S._census).[^cite_note-4]

### 2020 census

The community was delineated by the United States Census Bureau for the first time during the 2020 census.

As of the [census](6889.md) of 2020, there were 450 people residing in the CDP. There were 223 housing units. The racial makeup of the CDP was 81.8% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Hispanic_whites), 6.9% [African American or Black](2154.md), 0.0% [American Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 0.7% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans), 0.0% [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander_Americans), 2.4% from [other races](273285.md), and 8.2% from [two or more races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial_Americans). [Hispanic or Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) of any race were 6.7% of the population.

## Parks and recreation

Moneta Park, a 119-acre public park maintained by the Bedford County Parks and Recreation Department, is located in the CDP. The park features two multi-use ball fields, a football field, an 18-hole [disc golf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disc_golf) course, picnic shelters, and walking trails.[^cite_note-5]

Waterwheel Marina, Bridgewater Marina, and Crystal Shores Marina Resort offer waterfront access to [Smith Mountain Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_Mountain_Lake).

## Government

The [United States Postal Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Postal_Service) operates the Moneta Post Office within the CDP. The Moneta [ZIP Code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZIP_Code) extends to both sides of Smith Mountain Lake, including parts of [Franklin County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_County,_Virginia), since there is no Smith Mountain Lake postal address.

## Education

The CDP is served by [Bedford County Public Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford_County_Public_Schools). Public school students residing in Moneta are zoned to attend Moneta Elementary School, Staunton River Middle School, and [Staunton River High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staunton_River_High_School).

The closest higher education institutions are located in Bedford, Lynchburg, and Roanoke.

## Infrastructure

The Bedford Regional Water Authority operates a public water and wastewater system within the CDP.

### Public safety

Law enforcement is provided by the Bedford County Sheriff's Office, which maintains a field office within the CDP. Fire protection is provided by Moneta Volunteer Fire Department, which operates three fire stations, including one within the CDP. [Emergency medical services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_medical_services) are provided by the Moneta Volunteer Rescue Squad and Bedford County Department of Fire and Rescue.

### Transportation

#### Air

The [Smith Mountain Lake Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_Mountain_Lake_Airport) is the closest [general aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_aviation) airport to Moneta. The [Roanoke-Blacksburg Regional Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roanoke-Blacksburg_Regional_Airport) and [Lynchburg Regional Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynchburg_Regional_Airport) are the closest airports with commercial service.

#### Highway

-  [Virginia Route 122](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Route_122) (Moneta Road)

#### Rail

The [Norfolk Southern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk_Southern_Railway) operated Altavista District runs through the CDP. The closest passenger rail service is located in [Roanoke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roanoke_station_\(Virginia\)) and [Lynchburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kemper_Street_station).

## In popular culture

Moneta was popularized as the filming location for scenes in the 1991 movie *[What About Bob?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_About_Bob%3F)*. In the movie, Bob arrived by bus with his goldfish and went into a local general store, which still stands, albeit in a disheveled state because it is no longer open.
