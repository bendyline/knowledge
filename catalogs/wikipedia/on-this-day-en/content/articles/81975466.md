**Oriol Cardona Coll** (born 7 October 1994) is a Spanish [ski mountaineer](424477.md) and [skyrunner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyrunning). He represented Spain at the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md) and won a gold medal in the [sprint race](82409752.md), the first gold medal for Spain at the [Winter Olympic Games](34069.md) since [1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Winter_Olympics).

## Career

Cardona made his [European Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Championships_of_Ski_Mountaineering) debut in 2022 and won a gold medal in the sprint race with a time of 3\:17.988.[^cite_note-1] He then competed at the [2023 World Championship of Ski Mountaineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_World_Championship_of_Ski_Mountaineering) and won a gold medal in the sprint race with a time of 2\:35.218.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] He again competed at the 2024 European Championships and won a gold medal in the mixed relay along with [Ana Alonso Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_Alonso_Rodriguez).[^cite_note-4]

On 26 January 2025, during the first mixed relay of the season, he won a gold medal with a time of 30\:23.6.[^cite_note-5] He finished the [2024–25 ISMF Ski Mountaineering World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_ISMF_Ski_Mountaineering_World_Cup) reaching the podium in all five relay events, including three golds and two silvers, to win the mixed relay World Cup title with 480 points. He also finished first in the men's sprint World Cup standings with 575 points.[^cite_note-6]

He again competed at the [2025 World Championship of Ski Mountaineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Championship_of_Ski_Mountaineering) and won a gold medal in the sprint race with a time of 2\:45.40.[^cite_note-7] With the win, Spain earned a quota spot for the discipline at the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md).[^cite_note-8] He also won a silver medal in the mixed relay, along with Rodriguez, with a time of 32\:45.0, losing to France's team of [Emily Harrop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_Harrop) and [Thibault Anselmet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thibault_Anselmet) by 0.9 seconds.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] He earned a scholarship for the 2026 Winter Olympics.[^cite_note-11]

On 19 February 2026, Cardona won the first-ever men's Olympic gold medal in [ski mountaineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ski_mountaineering_at_the_Winter_Olympics), finishing in first place in the [sprint race](82409752.md) with a time of 2\:34.03.[^cite_note-12] This marked Spain's first Winter Olympic gold medal since [1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Winter_Olympics).[^cite_note-13] On 21 February 2026, he won a bronze medal in the [mixed relay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ski_mountaineering_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Mixed_relay), along with Alonso, with a time of 27\:23.94.[^cite_note-14]

## Personal life

Since 2023, he has been in a relationship with actress [Amaia Aberasturi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amaia_Aberasturi).[^cite_note-15]
