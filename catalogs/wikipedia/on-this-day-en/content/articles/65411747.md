# List of earthquakes in Poland

**Earthquakes in Poland** are a rare phenomenon. Most often they are caused by [rock bursts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_burst) in coal or copper mines. Natural ones appear in the [Carpathian Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carpathian_Mountains), [Sudetes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudetes), or in the [Trans-European Suture Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-European_Suture_Zone). Sometimes events from other countries are felt in Poland.

## Mining-induced earthquakes

Between 2015 and 2019, in Polish mines, 23 strong earthquakes occurred, killing 24 miners and damaging buildings on the surface.[^cite_note-Forsal-11]

| Date | Location | Magnitude | Damage/notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 29 November 1980 | [Bełchatów Coal Mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Be%C5%82chat%C3%B3w_Coal_Mine) | 4.7 | Cracks in the ground; damaged chimney[^cite_note-GJ-12] |
| 20 July 1987 | [Lubin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubin), Copper Mine | 4.8-4.9 | Four miners killed,[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] |
