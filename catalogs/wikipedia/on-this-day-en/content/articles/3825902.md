Over 40 different [Lutheran](23371382.md) denominations currently exist in North America. However, most North American Lutherans belong to one of the three largest denominations, the [Evangelical Lutheran Church in America](248255.md), the [Lutheran Church – Missouri Synod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutheran_Church_%E2%80%93_Missouri_Synod), or the [Wisconsin Evangelical Lutheran Synod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin_Evangelical_Lutheran_Synod).&#x20;
