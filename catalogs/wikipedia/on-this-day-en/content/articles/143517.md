The **Province of Ávila** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Provincia de Ávila* [\[ˈaβila\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/c/c7/Es-%C3%81vila.ogg/Es-%C3%81vila.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/c/c7/Es-%C3%81vila.ogg/Es-%C3%81vila.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Spain) of central-western [Spain](26667.md), in the southern part of the [autonomous community](55002.md) of [Castile and León](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castile_and_Le%C3%B3n). Its capital is the city of [Ávila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81vila). It is bordered on the south by the provinces of [Toledo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo_Province) and [Cáceres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A1ceres_Province), on the west by [Salamanca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salamanca_Province), on the north by [Valladolid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valladolid_Province), and on the east by [Segovia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segovia_Province) and [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid_Province).

It has a population of 160,738 in an area of 8,049.04 square kilometres (3,107.75 sq mi) across its 248 municipalities.[^cite_note-population-2][^cite_note-area-1]

The first recorded inhabitants of Ávila were the [Celts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celts), who left behind a number of large stone statues of bulls called Verracos, with the largest number of surviving examples in Ávila. The area of the province was conquered by the Romans around 192 B.C. After the Roman Empire fell, the area became part of the Visigothic Kingdom, and it is from this period we have many of the earliest records of the towns of Ávila.

The population of the territory comprising the current-day province of Ávila greatly decreased since the late 16th century and the first half of the 17th century, taking over nearly two centuries to recover the late 16th century population.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELlopis_AgellánCuervo_Fuente200439-3]

Ávila is naturally divided into two sections, differing completely in soil and climate. The northern portion is generally level; the soil is of indifferent quality, strong and [marly](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/marl#Etymology) in a few places, but rocky in all the valleys of the Sierra de Ávila; and the climate alternates from severe cold in winter to extreme heat in summer. The population of this part is mainly agricultural. The southern division, however, is one mass of rugged granitic sierras, interspersed with sheltered and well-watered valleys, abounding with rich vegetation.

The winter, especially in the elevated region of the Paramera and the wastelands of Ávila, is long and severe, but the climate is not unhealthy.

The principal mountain chains are the Guadarrama, separating this province from Madrid; the Paramera and [Sierra de Ávila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_de_%C3%81vila), west of the Guadarrama; and the vast wall of the [Sierra de Gredos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_de_Gredos) along the southern frontier, where its outstanding peaks rise from around 6,000 to 8,000 feet (1,800 to 2,400 m). [Pico Almanzor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pico_Almanzor) is the highest point. The ridges which [ramify](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ramify#English) from the Paramera are covered with valuable forests of beeches, oaks and firs, presenting a striking contrast to the bare peaks of the Sierra de Gredos.

The main rivers stemming from the [Sierra de Gredos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_de_Gredos) are the [Alberche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberche), the [Tiétar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti%C3%A9tar_River), and the [Tormes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tormes).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKlemm200839-4] The first two belong to the [Tagus basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagus_basin), while the latter belongs to the [Douro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douro) catchment area. Meanwhile, the [Adaja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaja), another Douro tributary whose source lies at the cut-off point of the [Sierra de Ávila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_de_%C3%81vila) and La Serrota, carries little water in the Summer.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKlemm200839-4]

Altitude ranges from the 400 metres above sea level in the Tiétar river trough to the 2592 metres of the [Pico Almanzor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pico_Almanzor).[^cite_note-5]

## Demographics

As of 2025, the population is 160,738, of which 50.3% are male, and 49.7% are female. Minors make up 14.2% of the population, and seniors make up 27.2%.[^cite_note-population-2]

### Immigration

As of 2025, immigrants make up 12.7% of the population. The 5 largest foreign countries of birth are [Morocco](19291.md), [Colombia](5222.md), [Peru](170691.md), [Romania](25445.md), and [Venezuela](32374.md).[^cite_note-population-2]
