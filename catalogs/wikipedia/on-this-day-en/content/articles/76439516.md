# Basmanny District Court



Basmanny District Court, pictured in 2023

**Basmanny District Court** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Басманный районный суд) is a district court in the [Basmanny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basmanny_District) district of [Moscow, Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow,_Russia), known for its high-profile and controversial trials, which have caused many-sided criticism of the Russian judicial system, in particular, in the case of [Mikhail Khodorkovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Khodorkovsky) and [Yukos shareholders v. Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yukos_shareholders_v._Russia).[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:1-3][^cite_note-\:2-4][^cite_note-5] The name of the court has led to this sort of trial being known as [Basmanny Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basmanny_Justice).

In 2024, the court issued an [arrest warrant](146746.md) for [Yulia Navalnaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yulia_Navalnaya), the widow of [Alexei Navalny](29174356.md).[^cite_note-6]
