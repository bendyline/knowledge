The **Cabinet of Israel** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): ממשלת ישראל, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hebrew): *Memshelet Yisra'el*; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مجلس وزراء إسرائيل, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Majlis Wuzaraʾ Israʾil*) is the cabinet which exercises [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) authority in the [State of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Israel). It consists of [ministers](739237.md) who are chosen and led by the [prime minister](24898.md). The composition of the government must be approved by a [vote of confidence](208101.md) in the [Knesset](74656.md) (the Israeli parliament). Under Israeli law, the prime minister may dismiss members of the government but must do so in writing, and new appointees must be approved by the Knesset. Most ministers lead [ministries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)), though some are [ministers without portfolio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_without_Portfolio). Most ministers are members of the Knesset, though only the Prime Minister and the "[designated acting prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_leaders_of_Israel)" are required to be Knesset members. Some ministers are also called deputy and vice-prime ministers. Unlike the designated acting prime minister, these roles have no statutory meanings. The government operates in accordance with the [Basic Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Laws_of_Israel). It meets on Sundays weekly in [Jerusalem](16043.md). There may be additional meetings if circumstances require it. The current head of government, the prime minister, is [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md).

The Israeli cabinet–officially described in the Basic Laws as the "Government"–is both the *de jure* and *de facto* executive authority in Israel. The Basic Laws explicitly vest executive power in the cabinet/Government, not the [President](153049.md).

## Use of terms

"Government of Israel" redirects here. For the branches of government in Israel, see [Israeli system of government](74576.md).

The body discussed in this article is referred to in Israeli official documents as the *Government of Israel*. This is in accordance with the normal translation of its Hebrew name, ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): ממשלה, *Memshala*). In Israel, the term *cabinet* ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): קבינט) is generally used for the [State-Security Cabinet](22256400.md) ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): הקבינט המדיני-ביטחוני *HaKabinet haMedini-Bitachoni*), a smaller forum of cabinet members that decides on defense and foreign policy issues and may consist of up to half of the (full) cabinet members. Another term in use is the [Kitchen Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitchen_Cabinet_of_Israel) ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): המטבחון, *HaMitbahon*, lit. "The kitchenette"), a collection of senior officials, or unofficial advisers to the Security Cabinet of Israel.

## Provisional and first governments of Israel

Main article: [Provisional government of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_government_of_Israel)

The first government was the [provisional government of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_government_of_Israel) (*HaMemshala HaZmanit*) which governed from shortly before [independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Declaration_of_Independence_\(Israel\)) until the formation of the [first formal government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_government_of_Israel) in March 1949 following the [first Knesset elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1949_Israeli_legislative_election) in January that year. It was formed as the People's Administration (*Minhelet HaAm*) on 12 April 1948, in preparation for independence just over a month later. All its thirteen members were taken from [Moetzet HaAm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_State_Council), the temporary legislative body set up at the same time.

## Current government

The [thirty-seventh government of Israel](72545811.md) ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): ממשלת ישראל השלושים ושבע) is the current government of Israel, which was sworn in on 29 December 2022.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-auto-2]
