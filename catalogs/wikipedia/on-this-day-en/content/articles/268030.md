**Shantou**, [alternately](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_postal_romanization) [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Chinese) as **Swatow**[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] and sometimes known as **Santow**,[^cite_note-5] is a [prefecture-level city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefecture-level_city) on the eastern coast of [Guangdong](65109.md), [China](5405.md), with a total population of 5,502,031 as of the 2020 census (5,391,028 in 2010) and an administrative area of 2,248.39 square kilometres (868.11 sq mi). However, its built-up (or metro) area is much bigger with 12,543,024 inhabitants including Rongcheng and Jiedong districts, Jiexi county and Puning city in [Jieyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jieyang) plus all of [Chaozhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaozhou) city largely conurbated. This is de facto the 5th built-up area in mainland China between [Hangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou)-[Shaoxing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaoxing) (13,035,026 inhabitants), [Xi'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi'an)-[Xianyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xianyang) (12,283,922 inhabitants) and Tianjin (11,165,706 inhabitants).

Shantou, a city significant in 19th-century Chinese history as one of the [treaty ports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_ports) established for Western trade and contact, was one of the original [special economic zones of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_economic_zones_of_China) established in the 1980s, but did not blossom in the manner that cities such as [Shenzhen](83368.md), [Xiamen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiamen) and [Zhuhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhuhai) did. However, it remains eastern Guangdong's economic centre, and is home to [Shantou University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantou_University), which is under the provincial Project 211 program in Guangdong.

## History

Shantou was a fishing village part of Tuojiang Du (鮀江都), [Jieyang County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jieyang) during the [Song dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_dynasty). It came to be known as Xialing (廈嶺) during the [Yuan dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuan_dynasty). In 1563, Shantou became a part of [Chenghai County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chenghai_District) in Chao Prefecture ([Chaozhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaozhou)). As early as 1574, Shantou had been called Shashanping (沙汕坪). In the seventeenth century, a cannon platform called Shashantou Cannon (沙汕頭炮臺) was made here, and the place name later was shortened to "Shantou". Locally it has been referred to as Kialat.

Connecting to Shantou across the [Queshi Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queshi_Bridge) is Queshi (礐石) which had been known by the local people through the 19th century as Kakchio. It was the main site for the American and British consulates. Today the area is a scenic park but some of the structures from its earlier history are somewhat preserved. In 1860, Shantou was opened for foreigners and became a trading port according to [Treaty of Tientsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Tientsin).[^cite_note-6]

It became a city in 1919, and was separated from Chenghai in 1921. 1922 saw the devastating [Swatow Typhoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1922_Swatow_Typhoon), which killed 5,000 out of the 65,000 people then inhabiting the city.[^cite_note-MWR-7] Some nearby villages were totally destroyed.[^cite_note-Other_parts-8] Several ships near the coast were totally wrecked.[^cite_note-Selga-9] Other ones were blown as far as two miles inland.[^cite_note-Other_parts-8] The area around the city had around another 50,000 casualties.[^cite_note-Selga-9] The total death toll was above 60,000,[^cite_note-NOAA-10] and may have been higher than 100,000.[^cite_note-Selga-9]

In the 1930s, as a transport hub and merchandise distribution centre in Southeast China, Shantou Port's cargo throughput ranked third in the country. A brief account of a visit to the city in English during this period is the English accountant Max Relton's *A Man in the East: A Journey through French Indo-China* (Michael Joseph Ltd., London, 1939). On 21 June 1939, [Japanese troops invaded Shantou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swatow_Operation).[^cite_note-11] Japanese forces occupied Shantou until 15 August 1945.[^cite_note-12] The Communist [People's Liberation Army](66890.md) captured Shantou on 24 October 1949, 23 days after the People's Republic of China was founded.[^cite_note-13]

With higher-level administrative authority, Shantou governed [Chaozhou City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaozhou_City) and [Jieyang City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jieyang) from 1983 to 1989.[^cite_note-14]

## Geography

Shantou is located in eastern Guangdong with latitude spanning 23°02′33″ – 23°38′50″ N and longitude 116°14′40″ – 117°19′35″ E; the [Tropic of Cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropic_of_Cancer) passes through the northern part of the city, and along it there is a monument, in fact the easternmost in mainland China, at [23°26′33″N 116°35′20″E  /  23.44240°N 116.58885°E  / 23.44240; 116.58885](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Shantou\&params=23.4424_N_116.58885_E_type\:landmark_region\:CN-44).[^cite_note-stxcb-15] The highest peak in the city's administration is Mount Dajian (大尖山) on [Nan'ao Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nan'ao_Island), at 587 m (1,926 ft); the highest peak on the geographic mainland is [Mount Lianhua](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%8E%B2%E8%8A%B1%E5%B1%B1) (莲花山), at 562 m (1,844 ft) in [Chenghai District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chenghai_District). The city is located at the mouths of the [Han](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_River_\(Guangdong\)), [Rong](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%A6%95%E6%B1%9F%20\(%E5%B9%BF%E4%B8%9C\)) (榕江), and [Lian Rivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lian_River_\(South_China_Sea\)).

Shantou is 301 km (187 mi) northeast of [Hong Kong](13404.md).[^cite_note-16]

### Climate

Shantou has a monsoon-influenced [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Cwa*), with short, mild to warm winters, and long, hot, humid summers. Winter begins sunny and dry but becomes progressively wetter and cloudier. Spring is generally overcast, while summer brings the heaviest rains of the year though is much sunnier; there are 8.2 days annually with 50 mm (1.97 in) of rainfall. Autumn is sunny and dry. The monthly 24-hour average temperature ranges from 14.7 °C (58.5 °F) in January to 29.1 °C (84.4 °F) in July, and the annual mean is 22.58 °C (72.6 °F). The annual rainfall is around 1,618 mm (64 in), about 60% of which occurs from May to August. With monthly percent possible sunshine ranging from 28% in March to 58% in July and October, the city receives 1,979 hours of bright sunshine annually. Extremes since 1951 have ranged from 0.3 °C (33 °F) on 29 December 1991 (unofficial record of −0.6 °C (31 °F) was set on 18 January 1893) to 38.9 °C (102 °F) on 11 July and August 8, 2026.[^cite_note-17]

## Economy

Shantou's economy is medium by [Guangdong](65109.md) standards. Manufacturing accounts for a large and increasing share of employment, with [canning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canning), garments, lithography, plastic, and toys being some of the principal products. Toy manufacturing is the city's leading export industry, with 400 million U.S. dollars' worth of exports each year. Canaton Calculator Co. is a multinational electronic devices manufacturing company.

[Guiyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guiyu_\(town\)), a populous town in [Chaoyang District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaoyang_District,_Shantou), is the biggest [electronic waste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_waste) site on earth.[^cite_note-29] Health-environmental issues incurred have concerned international organizations such as [Greenpeace](12233.md).

In 2000, the biggest tax fraud in the history of the People's Republic of China was uncovered, estimated worthy of 32.3 billion yuan. In 2017, the analyzed data of Shantou GDP is approximately 230 billion yuan(US\$35.4 billion).

### Development zone

With an area of 2.34 km<sup>2</sup> (0.90 sq mi), Shantou Free Trade Zone lies at the south part of Shantou city. It was ratified by the [State Council of the People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Council_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) and founded in January 1993, and it formally came into use on December of the same year after its supervision installations were checked and accepted by the [General Administration of Customs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Administration_of_Customs). It has been comprehensively developing export processing, storage, international trade, finance and information industry. Its goal is to establish a modernized international zone that is open to overseas by drawing experience from international free trade zones.[^cite_note-30]

## Demographics

Shantou is one of the most densely populated regions in China. Former Chaoyang City was China's most populous county-level administrative region, with 2.4 million inhabitants. Shantou has direct jurisdiction over six districts and one county, and the six urban districts of Shantou have a population of 5,330,764.

### Metro area

With it and the surrounding cities of [Jieyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jieyang) and [Chaozhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaozhou), the administrative [metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_area) known as [Chaoshan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaoshan) covers an area of 10,404 km<sup>2</sup> (4,017 sq mi), and had a permanent population of 13,648,232 as of the 2020 census. Nevertheless, its built-up area spread on 11 districts, [Puning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puning) city and [Raoping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raoping) county was home to 12,543,024 inhabitants as of 2020 census.[^cite_note-citypopulation.de-1] This is de facto the fifth built-up area of China after Guangzhou-Shenzhen Pearl River Delta megacity, Shanghai-Suzhou-Wuxi-Changzhou conurbation, Beijing and Hangzhou-Shaoxing agglomeration.

### Languages

Most residents are linguistically [Teochew people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_people). [Teochew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_Min) is a variant of [Southern Min](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Min) (Hokkien-Taiwanese) spoken in the neighbouring Southern Fujian and Taiwan. There are also [Hakka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakka_people), popularly known as Half-[Hakka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakka_people) (半山客), living mainly in Chaoyang District and Chaonan District, although they speak Teochew on a daily basis and practise Teochew culture. The Mandarin-medium education system, widely promoted throughout China, has made most people, especially younger generations, speak Mandarin fluently. Cantonese language TV and labor migrations to the Pearl River Delta has also made Cantonese widely spoken as a third language by the younger generations.

Governmental statistics show that 2.16 million [overseas Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_Chinese) have roots in Shantou, with significant populations of [Teochew people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_people) residing in [Thailand](30128.md) and [Cambodia](334751.md), which constitute a majority of [Thai Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_Chinese) and a majority of [Chinese Cambodians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Cambodians). This is demonstrated by the unusually high number of international direct flights between [Bangkok](56615.md) and Shantou. In addition, there are at least two [Teochew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_dialect)-speaking air hostesses on board each [China Southern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Southern) flight between Shantou and Bangkok.[^cite_note-31] The Teochew presence, furthermore, is evident in Singapore and [Malaysia](3607937.md); [Johor Bahru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johor_Bahru), a coastal city situated at the latter's southernmost tip, is known as 'Little Swatow', due to the majority local [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysian_Chinese) populace is dominantly Teochew and as well as the second largest group of the local [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Singaporeans) population in Singapore.

## Culture and lifestyle

Main article: [Teochew people § Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_people#Culture)

Shantou people share the same culture with other [Teochew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teochew_people). The tea-drinking tradition widely practised in town is a classic instance. According to *[China Daily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Daily)*, Shantou people "drink more tea than anyone else in China, in total 700 million yuan (US\$87.5 million) each year".[^cite_note-32]

## Religions



St. Joseph's Cathedral of Shantou

Most of the population in Shantou is non-religious or practices [traditional folk religions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_folk_religion), [Buddhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Buddhism), [Taoism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taoism), or worship of [Chinese deities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shen_\(Chinese_religion\)) and [ancestors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancestor_veneration_in_China). About 2% of the population belongs to an organised religion, with 40,000 [Protestants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism_in_China), 20,000 [Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholicism_in_China) and 500 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_China).[^cite_note-33] [Saint Joseph's Cathedral (Shantou)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Joseph's_Cathedral_\(Shantou\)) is the cathedral of the [Roman Catholic Diocese of Shantou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Shantou).

## Infrastructure

### Health

The public hospitals in the Shantou metropolitan area are operated by the Government of Shantou. Management of these hospitals and other specialist health facilities are coordinated by Shantou Board of Health.

### Utilities

Shantou's electricity is provided entirely by [China Southern Power Grid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Grid_Corporation), [postal service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mail) operated by [China Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Post).

### Telecommunications

Shantou is one of the most important international telecommunications ports in China. Four [international submarine communications cables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_international_submarine_communications_cables) land at Shantou submarine cable landing station, including [APCN 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APCN_2), [China-US Cable Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China-US_Cable_Network), [SMW3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMW3) and South-East Asia Japan Cable System (SJC).[^cite_note-34]

[China Telecom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Telecom), [China Unicom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Unicom) and [China Mobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Mobile) provide fixed lines, broadband internet access and mobile telecommunications services there.

### Transport



Shantou [Queshi Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queshi_Bridge) during sunset

#### Urban transport

Public transportation is provided by bus, ferry, bike sharing system and taxi. Residents also travel by private car and [motorbikes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorbike). There are two bridges throughout the city that cross the Shantou Harbor: the [Queshi Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queshi_Bridge) and [Shantou Bay Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantou_Bay_Bridge). A [metro system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantou_Metro) is planned with construction of three lines (Lines 1, 2, and 3) commencing in 2018 and opening of the system planned in 2020.



The new CBD of Shantou on the east coast, facing the Taiwan strait, as viewed from Dahao Island.

#### Air

Shantou previously had its own civil airport, [Shantou Waisha Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantou_Waisha_Airport). It was formerly the main airport serving the Shantou until nearby [Jieyang Chaoshan Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jieyang_Chaoshan_Airport) was opened on 15 December 2011. Shantou Waisha Airport became a military airbase since then and all civilian flights were transferred to the newly built airport in Jieyang.[^cite_note-35] Taxi is the usual way to travel between the airport and the city proper. The taxi fare is around 60 [RMB](66958.md). Airport-Downtown Shantou shuttle charter is also suggested. Based in Shantou, Shantou Airlines Co. operated by China Southern Airlines has a 15 aircraft fleet in service.

#### Railways



[Shantou Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantou_Railway_Station), 2019



A high-speed train leaving Shantou for Guangzhou, 2022

There are three railway stations which serve Shantou: [Chaoshan Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaoshan_Railway_Station) and [Chaoyang railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaoyang_railway_station_\(Guangdong\)) which lie on the [Xiamen-Shenzhen Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiamen-Shenzhen_Railway) line, and [Shantou Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantou_railway_station) which lies on the [Guangzhou–Meizhou–Shantou Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangzhou%E2%80%93Meizhou%E2%80%93Shantou_Railway) and is under construction for the Guangzhou-Shanwei-Shantou 350 km/h high speed railway.
