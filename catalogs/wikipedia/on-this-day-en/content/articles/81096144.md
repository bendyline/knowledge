The **2025 Barbadian presidential election** was held on 7 October 2025 to choose the second [president of Barbados](68909900.md). Dame [Sandra Mason](48704234.md), the inaugural president elected to replace [Queen Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_II) as [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of [Barbados](3455.md) in 2021, completed her term on 29 November 2025, and chose to retire rather than run for a second term.&#x20;

Lt. Col. [Jeffrey Bostic](69934601.md) was nominated jointly on 16 September 2025, thereby making him the only candidate to be presented for this election.[^cite_note-\:1-1] His candidacy was not objected to in the joint sitting of Parliament in which the election took place.[^cite_note-\:3-2] Thus, he was [acclaimed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acclamation) as President-elect, in accordance with the Constitution, in a walkover and was sworn in on 30 November 2025.&#x20;

## Electoral system

The president is elected [indirectly](652583.md) by the [Parliament of Barbados](2555282.md) in the context of a joint sitting of Parliament.[^cite_note-\:0-3]&#x20;

The [prime minister](450163.md) and the [leader of the opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(Barbados\)) jointly nominate a consensus candidate 90 days before the incumbent's term is due to expire, who is then elected in a [walkover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walkover) without a vote unless any MP lodges their objection. If an objection is lodged, the joint sitting is suspended and the two Houses of Parliament, the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Barbados) and the [House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Assembly_\(Barbados\)), meet separately and each vote on accepting or rejecting the nominee. A [two-thirds majority](638313.md) of valid votes in each house separately is then required to elect a candidate on all rounds of balloting.[^cite_note-\:0-3]&#x20;

If no consensus candidate is nominated by the 60th day before the end of the incumbent's term, the election is opened to other candidates. To gain [ballot access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballot_access) in such an open election, a candidate must be nominated either by the prime minister, the leader of the Opposition, or at least ten members of the House of Assembly. The requirement for a two-thirds majority of valid votes in each house separately also applies in an open election; this means that if only one candidate has been nominated, the voting system is the same as when a consensus candidate has been objected to.[^cite_note-\:0-3]&#x20;

## Candidate

Lt. Col. [Jeffrey Bostic](69934601.md), the former Minister of Health and Wellness, military officer and member of parliament for the [City of Bridgetown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Bridgetown), was the only candidate for the office; he was nominated jointly by the prime minister, [Mia Mottley](15337866.md), and the leader of the opposition, [Ralph Thorne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Thorne).[^cite_note-\:1-1]&#x20;

Bostic was widely commended by the Barbadian public for his work in leading the island's response to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), and for that reason, was awarded the [Order of Freedom of Barbados](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Freedom_of_Barbados) on 28 April 2022, the island's National Heroes Day.[^cite_note-\:2-4]&#x20;

Given there were no objections at the joint sitting of Parliament, Bostic was declared [elected without a vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walkover) on 7 October 2025. Due to his election, Bostic was sworn in 30 November, which was the 59th anniversary of Barbadian independence.[^cite_note-\:3-2]&#x20;
