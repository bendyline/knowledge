**East Flanders**[^cite_note-5] is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Belgium) of [Belgium](3343.md). It borders (clockwise from the North) the [Dutch](21148.md) province of [Zeeland](52229.md) and the Belgian provinces of [Antwerp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antwerp_\(province\)), [Flemish Brabant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_Brabant), [Hainaut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainaut_\(province\)) and [West Flanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Flanders). It has an area of 3,007 km<sup>2</sup> (1,161 sq mi), divided into six administrative districts containing 60 municipalities, and a population of over 1.57 million as of January 2024.[^cite_note-6] The capital is [Ghent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent), home to the [Ghent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent_University) and the [Port of Ghent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Ghent).

## History

During the short-lived [Napoleonic Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleonic_Empire), most of the area of the modern province was part of the [Department of Escaut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escaut_\(department\)), named after the [River Scheldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Scheldt). Following the defeat of [Napoleon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon), the entity was [renamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographical_renaming) after its geographical location in the eastern part of the historic [County of Flanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_of_Flanders) (now in the western portion of the current [Flemish Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_Region)).

The provincial flag has a black lion with red tongue and claws, on a background of horizontal white and green stripes. This is a recent adaptation; formerly, East Flanders used the [Flemish flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Flanders), a black lion on a yellow background, as in the current coat of arms. The old flag is still publicly used, e.g. for road signs.

## Geography

The province has several geographic or tourist regions:

- [Denderstreek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denderstreek)

- [Meetjesland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meetjesland)

- [Waasland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waasland)

- [Flemish Ardennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_Ardennes)

Important rivers are the [Scheldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheldt) and the [Leie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leie) which merge in [Ghent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent). The [Dender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dender) merges into the [Scheldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheldt) in the city of [Dendermonde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendermonde).

## Demographics

The province has a population of almost 1.5 million. It had 734,000 inhabitants in 1830, when it was the most populated province of Belgium, and about a million in 1900. Population growth halted around the 1980s, but has increased again in the 21st century. Population figures in recent years is as follows:

| Year | Population |
| --- | --- |
| 1 January 1990 | 1,331,608 |
| 1 January 1995 | 1,349,382 |
| 1 January 2000 | 1,361,623 |
| 1 January 2005 | 1,380,072 |
| 1 January 2010 | 1,432,326 |
| 1 January 2015 | 1,477,346 |
| 1 January 2017 | 1,496,187 |

The capital and biggest city is [Ghent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent), also the second largest city in the [Flemish Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_Region). Other smaller cities are [Aalst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalst,_Belgium), [Sint-Niklaas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sint-Niklaas) and [Dendermonde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendermonde) in the east of the province. The eastern part of the province, part of the [Flemish Diamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_Diamond), is more densely populated than the western part.

## Economy

The [Gross domestic product](12594.md) (GDP) of the province was 56.4 billion € in 2018. GDP per capita adjusted for purchasing power was 33,000 € or 109% of the EU27 average in the same year.[^cite_note-7]

## Government



The *[Gravensteen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravensteen)* in [Ghent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent), the provincial capital



The *[Muur van Geraardsbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muur_van_Geraardsbergen)*

The provincial council (*provincieraad*) consists of 36 members which were last elected in the [2018 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Belgian_local_elections#Provincial_elections). Previously it consisted of 72 members. The council currently consists of the following political parties:

- [N-VA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-VA) (Flemish nationalists): 8 members

- [CD\&V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CD\&V) (Christian democrats): 7 members

- [Open VLD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_VLD) (liberals): 7 members

- [sp.a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sp.a) (social democrats): 3 members

- [Vlaams Belang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vlaams_Belang) (far-right nationalists): 6 members

- [Groen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groen!) (greens): 5 members

Four people chosen by and from the council form the daily government, called the deputation (*deputatie*). The deputation of East Flanders is a coalition of the [N-VA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-VA), [CD\&V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CD\&V), and [Groen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Vld).

The daily government is led by the governor, who is appointed by the [Flemish Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_Government). [Carina Van Cauter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carina_Van_Cauter) ([VLD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Vlaamse_Liberalen_en_Democraten)) has been the governor of East Flanders since 1 September 2020.[^cite_note-8]

The province has a yearly budget of approximately 300 million euro.
