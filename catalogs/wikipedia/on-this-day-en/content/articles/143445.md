The **Province of Córdoba** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Provincia de Córdoba*, [\[ˈkoɾðoβa\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish); also called **Cordova** in English[^cite_note-3]) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Spain) of [Spain](26667.md), in the north-central part of the [autonomous community](55002.md) of [Andalusia](2736.md). It is bordered by the [Andalusian](2736.md) provinces of [Málaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1laga_\(province\)), [Seville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seville_\(province\)), [Jaén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Ja%C3%A9n_\(Spain\)), and [Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granada_\(province\)), the [Extremaduran](68204.md) province of [Badajoz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badajoz_\(province\)) and the province of [Ciudad Real](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciudad_Real_\(province\)), which is part of the autonomous community of [Castile-La Mancha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castile-La_Mancha).

It has a population of 773,163 in an area of 13,772.22 square kilometres (5,317.48 sq mi) across its 77 municipalities.[^cite_note-population-2][^cite_note-area-1]

## History

A royal decree of 30 November 1833, created the Province of Córdoba ([along with 48 other provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1833_territorial_division_of_Spain)),[^cite_note-Ford2011-4] which was formed by joining the towns of the [Kingdom of Córdoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_C%C3%B3rdoba) and the following towns until then located in [Badajoz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Badajoz): [Belalcázar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belalc%C3%A1zar,_Spain), [Fuente la Lancha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuente_la_Lancha), [Hinojosa del Duque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinojosa_del_Duque), and [Villanueva del Duque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villanueva_del_Duque).

## Geography

The province is mainly divided into three geographical areas: the [Sierra Morena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Morena) to the north, the [Baetic Depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baetic_Depression) in the center and [La Campiña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campi%C3%B1a_Sur_\(C%C3%B3rdoba\)) in the south.[^cite_note-Today-5] The climate is continental Mediterranean with temperatures in the capital ranging from 9.2 °C in January and 27.2 °C in July and August, which often exceed 40 °C. Rainfall in the capital is recorded from 600 to 750 mm per year. It is concentrated from October to April. The Province of Córdoba ranks 11th in Spain in which the entire population is concentrated in the capital. On average, 31.96% of a Spanish province's population inhabits its capital. The province consists of 75 municipalities. They are further grouped into 8 "comarcas".[^cite_note-Today-5]

Its population is 799,402 (2014), of whom more than 40% live in the capital, [Córdoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%B3rdoba,_Spain), and its population density is 58.06/km<sup>2</sup>. The Province of Córdoba contains [75 municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_municipalities_in_C%C3%B3rdoba). The province has three natural parks: [Sierra de Cardeña y Montoro Natural Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_de_Carde%C3%B1a_y_Montoro_Natural_Park?action=edit\&redlink=1),[^cite_note-6] [Sierra de Hornachuelos Natural Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_de_Hornachuelos_Natural_Park),[^cite_note-7] and [Sierras Subbéticas Natural Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierras_Subb%C3%A9ticas_Natural_Park?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-8] The [University of Córdoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_C%C3%B3rdoba_\(Spain\)) was founded in 1972.[^cite_note-Piñal1991-9]

The province's capital city is a well-known tourist destination. The [Mezquita of Cordoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosque%E2%80%93Cathedral_of_C%C3%B3rdoba) was built using pillars of uneven heights.[^cite_note-10] The city was declared a [world heritage site](44940.md) in 1984.[^cite_note-11]

## Demographics

As of 2025, the population is 773,163, of which 48.9% are male, and 51.1% are female. Minors make up 16.3% of the population, and seniors make up 21.6%.[^cite_note-population-2]

### Immigration

As of 2025, immigrants make up 5.4% of the population. The 5 largest foreign countries of birth are [Morocco](19291.md), [Colombia](5222.md), [Romania](25445.md), [Venezuela](32374.md), and [Honduras](13394.md).[^cite_note-population-2]
