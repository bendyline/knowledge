The **Catholicos-Patriarch of All Georgia** ([Georgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_language): სრულიად საქართველოს კათოლიკოს პატრიარქი, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Georgian): sruliad sakartvelos k'atolik'os p'at'riarki) is the Archbishop of [Mtskheta and Tbilisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mtskheta_and_Tbilisi?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the head of the [Georgian Orthodox Church](433340.md).

The official full title is ***His Holiness and Beatitude, Catholicos-Patriarch of All Georgia and the Archbishop of Mtskheta and Tbilisi, Metropolitan Bishop of Bichvinta and Tskhum-Abkhazia***.

The incumbent [Catholicos-Patriarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholicos-Patriarch) of the church is Patriarch [Shio III](55912934.md) since his [election](82725870.md) in 2026, who is also the Metropolitan Bishop of [Bichvinta and Tskhum-Abkhazia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eparchy_of_Bichvinta_and_Tskhum-Abkhazia).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

*Catholicos-Patriarch* has been the title of the heads of the Georgian Orthodox Church since 1010, shortly after the [unification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unification_of_the_Georgian_realm) of the [Kingdom of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Georgia). The first Catholicos-Patriarch of All Georgia was [Melkisedek I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melchizedek_I_of_Georgia) (1010–1033). In the 15th century, with the [collapse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collapse_of_the_Georgian_realm) of the Kingdom, the Georgian Orthodox Church was divided into the East and the West parts and accordingly they were ruled by the *Catholicos-Patriarch of East Georgia* and the *Catholicos-Patriarch of West Georgia*.&#x20;

In 1801, the [Kingdom of Kartli-Kakheti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Kartli-Kakheti) (Eastern Georgia) was [occupied and annexed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_within_the_Russian_Empire) by the [Tsarist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsarist_autocracy) [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire). In 1811, the [autocephalous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autocephaly) status (independence) of the Georgian Orthodox Church was abolished by Russia and the [Russian Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Orthodox_Church) took over its administration.

In 1917, the autocephaly of the Georgian Orthodox Church was restored. The first *Catholicos-Patriarch of All Georgia* since the restoration of autocephaly was [Kyrion II Sadzaglishvili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrion_II_of_Georgia) (1917–1918).

To date there have been 82 Catholicos-Patriarchs, of whom 7 have been formally [glorified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glorification#Eastern_Orthodox_Church) by the Georgian Orthodox Church.
