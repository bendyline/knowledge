The **Asian Football Confederation** (**AFC**) is the [governing body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_governing_body) of [association football](10568.md), [beach soccer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beach_soccer), and [futsal](188197.md) in most countries and territories in [Asia](689.md).[^cite_note-Outside_Asia-1] It is one of the six continental confederations under [FIFA](11049.md) and is responsible for organising international competitions for national teams and clubs within its jurisdiction, including the [AFC Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Asian_Cup) and [AFC Champions League Elite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Champions_League_Elite). Founded in 1954 and headquartered in [Kuala Lumpur](16854.md), Malaysia, the AFC comprises [47 member associations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_men's_national_association_football_teams#AFC_\(Asia\)).

## History

The idea of an Asian Football Confederation was first brought up during the [1952 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1952_Summer_Olympics) in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki), Finland.[^cite_note-\:0-5] On 8 May 1954, the AFC was officially formed at the [1954 Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1954_Asian_Games) in [Manila](184334.md), Philippines.[^cite_note-\:1-6][^cite_note-7] The first president was [Lo Man-kam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lo_Man-kam) of Hong Kong, and the first office was based in Hong Kong.[^cite_note-\:1-6] The 13 founding members included [Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghanistan_Football_Federation), [Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar_Football_Federation), [Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Association_of_Hong_Kong,_China), [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_India_Football_Federation), [Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Association_of_Indonesia), [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Football_Association), [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Football_Association), [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Football_Federation), the [Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Football_Federation), [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Association_of_Singapore), [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea_Football_Association), [South Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Football_Federation), and [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Taipei_Football_Association).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

Of the AFC's original 13 member associations, only seven entered the [qualifying tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_AFC_Asian_Cup_qualification) for the inaugural [AFC Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Asian_Cup) in [1956](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_AFC_Asian_Cup).[^cite_note-\:0-5] Four teams ultimately qualified for the final tournament, which was held in then-British Hong Kong.[^cite_note-\:0-5] The 1956 tournament was the first edition of the AFC Asian Cup, the world's second-oldest continental national-team football competition.[^cite_note-11]

Arab countries entered the Asian Cup qualifiers for the first time in the [1972 AFC Asian Cup qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_AFC_Asian_Cup_qualification),[^cite_note-\:0-5] following [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_national_football_team)'s withdrawal.[^cite_note-12] Israel was eventually [expelled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boycotts_of_Israel_in_sports) from the AFC in 1974, due to political pressure from Arab and Muslim members that [refused to play against Israel](7960202.md).[^cite_note-JTA2-13]

In 1958, the office relocated from Hong Kong to Malaysia.[^cite_note-\:1-6] In 2000, the AFC House was inaugurated as the AFC's headquarters; based in [Bukit Jalil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bukit_Jalil), [Kuala Lumpur](16854.md), the construction cost [RM](360278.md) 18 million ([US\$](18717338.md) 8.18 million).[^cite_note-\:1-6]

## Women's football

The Asian Ladies Football Confederation (ALFC) was the section of AFC that managed [women's association football](191466.md) in Asia. The group was independently founded in April 1968 in a meeting involving [Taiwan](25734.md), [British Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Hong_Kong), [Malaysia](3607937.md), and [Singapore](27318.md). In 1986, ALFC merged with AFC.[^cite_note-14]

## Members



AFC regional federations

Due to the geographical size of Asia, the AFC is subdivided into five sub-confederations:

- [ASEAN Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASEAN_Football_Federation) (AFF) – represents countries in [Southeast Asia](28741.md), plus Australia.

- [Central Asian Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Asian_Football_Association) (CAFA) – represents countries in [Central Asia](6742.md), comprising Afghanistan, Iran and most of [Central Asia](6742.md), except Kazakhstan.

- [East Asian Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Asian_Football_Federation) (EAFF) – represents nations in [East Asia](19605700.md), plus Guam and the Northern Mariana Islands.

- [South Asian Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Asian_Football_Federation) (SAFF) – represents countries in [South Asia](21566765.md).

- [West Asian Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Asian_Football_Federation) (WAFF) – represents countries in [West Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Asia), except Israel.

### AFC competitions

The AFC organises the [AFC Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Asian_Cup) and the [AFC Women's Asian Cup](2428648.md), both held every four years. It also runs the [AFC Futsal Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Futsal_Asian_Cup), [AFC Women's Futsal Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Women's_Futsal_Asian_Cup), and the [AFC Beach Soccer Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Beach_Soccer_Asian_Cup), along with various youth and age-group international football and [futsal](188197.md) tournaments. In addition, the AFC oversees the [Asian qualifying tournaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_World_Cup_qualifiers) for the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) and for [football at the Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_Summer_Olympics).

The premier men's club competition is the [AFC Champions League Elite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Champions_League_Elite), which is an amalgamation of the [Asian Club Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Club_Championship), the [Asian Cup Winners' Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Cup_Winners'_Cup), and the [Asian Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Super_Cup). It features leading clubs from AFC member associations, with the number of participating teams determined by the ranking of each association, allowing for promotion or relegation of slots based on performance. The top-tier women's club competition is the [AFC Women's Champions League](64565584.md), introduced in the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_AFC_Women's_Champions_League).[^cite_note-27]

The second-tier men's club competition is the [AFC Champions League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Champions_League_Two), while the third tier is the [AFC Challenge League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Challenge_League), both of which were restructured and rebranded in 2024.[^cite_note-28]

&#x20;The AFC also organises the annual [AFC Futsal Club Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Futsal_Club_Championship), the continent’s premier club futsal competition.[^cite_note-29]
