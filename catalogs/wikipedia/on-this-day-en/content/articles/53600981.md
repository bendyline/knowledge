The **president of the Kurdistan Region** is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of the semi-autonomous [Kurdistan Region](3949386.md) in northern [Iraq](7515928.md). They are part of the [Kurdistan Presidency Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdistan_Presidency_Council?action=edit\&redlink=1)[^cite_note-1] and commander-in-chief of the [Peshmerga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshmerga).[^cite_note-2] The current president of Kurdistan Region is [Nechirvan Barzani](1110441.md), who assumed office on 10 June 2019.[^cite_note-3]

## History

After the results of the [1992 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Kurdistan_Region_parliamentary_election), the [Kurdistan Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdistan_Democratic_Party) (KDP) and the [Patriotic Union of Kurdistan](70789.md) (PUK) with both parties holding 50 out of 100 seats, chose to create a unity government unrecognized by [Ba'athist Iraq](26215470.md).[^cite_note-End_of_Iraq-4]



Kurdistan Region after the 1998 cease-fire. Green area controlled by the PUK, Yellow area controlled by the KDP

The unity government collapsed in 1994 and caused the [Iraqi Kurdish Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Kurdish_Civil_War) to break out that ended in 1998. This resulted in the establishment of two Kurdistan Regional Governments, a KDP-controlled one in [Erbil](24534207.md) and a PUK-controlled one in [Sulaymaniyah](653537.md), each with their own president.[^cite_note-End_of_Iraq-4]

After an official reconciliation between the KDP and PUK in October 2002,[^cite_note-5] [parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Kurdistan_Region_parliamentary_election) were then held on 30 January 2005 and on 14 June 2005 the KDP-leader [Masoud Barzani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masoud_Barzani) was sworn in by the parliament in as new president and [Kosrat Rasul Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosrat_Rasul_Ali) as the new [vice president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Kurdistan_Region).[^cite_note-6] In 2009, the system was then changed to elect the president and on 25 July 2009 [presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Kurdistan_Region_presidential_election) were held resulting in Barzani's re-election.[^cite_note-7] On 29 October 2017, amidst the [2017 Iraqi–Kurdish conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Iraqi%E2%80%93Kurdish_conflict), Barzani announced his intentions to step down as president on November 1st.[^cite_note-8]
