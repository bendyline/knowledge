**Czech Republic–Turkey relations** are foreign relations between [Czech Republic](5321.md) and [Turkey](11125639.md).

In 1993, Turkey formally recognized both the [Slovakia](26830.md) and [Czech Republic](5321.md) as separate, sovereign states.[^cite_note-auto1-1] Diplomatic relations and the Turkish Embassy in [Prague](23844.md) were established on January 4, 1993.[^cite_note-auto1-1] Both countries are full members of [Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Europe) and of [NATO](21133.md).

## History

Relations between [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia) and Turkey had been excellent until 1948,[^cite_note-auto2-2] when relations rapidly deteriorated[^cite_note-auto2-2] because of disagreements over the compensation[^cite_note-auto4-3] for the nationalization[^cite_note-auto2-2] of the property owned by Turkish businesses.

Trade relations were modest but limited because of Turkey's refusal[^cite_note-auto5-4] to participate in a trade agreement[^cite_note-auto4-3] until the claims[^cite_note-5] of nationalized businesses were settled.

Relations were also tense because of Czechoslovakia's alignment against [Israel](9282173.md)[^cite_note-6] in the [Middle East](19323.md), which was [Turkey](11125639.md)’s closest ally[^cite_note-auto5-4] in the region.

In 1993, Turkey formally recognized both the Slovak Republic and the Czech Republic as separate, sovereign states.[^cite_note-auto1-1] Diplomatic relations and the Turkish Embassy in Bratislava were established on January 4, 1993.[^cite_note-auto1-1]

Czech Republic and Turkey have strong diplomatic ties and cooperate in the military and law enforcement areas since [Czech Republic](5321.md) joined the [NATO](21133.md) Alliance.[^cite_note-7]

## Economic relations

- Trade volume between the two countries was US\$3.65 billion in 2018 (Turkish exports/imports: 1/2.65 billion USD).

  [^cite_note-auto-11]

- Over 228 thousand Czech tourists visited Turkey in 2018, an increase by 181% compared to the previous year.

  [^cite_note-auto-11]

## Resident diplomatic missions

- Czech Republic has an embassy in [Ankara](802.md) and an consulate-general in [Istanbul](3391396.md).
