The **Freedom 250 Grand Prix of Washington D.C.** was an [NTT IndyCar Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series) [motor race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorsport) held on August 23, 2026, at the 1.7 mi (2.7 km) [Washington Street Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Street_Circuit) in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) It was the fifteenth round of the [2026 NTT IndyCar Series](80635394.md).[^cite_note-3] The race consisted of 147 laps, extended from the originally scheduled 125 laps.

[Kyle Kirkwood](57671103.md) won the race, followed by [Christian Lundgaard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Lundgaard) and [Will Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Power) on the podium.

## Background



The [National Mall](167796.md), the area where the race was held. The [Lincoln Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Memorial) and [its reflecting pool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Memorial_Reflecting_Pool), the [Washington Monument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Monument) behind it, and the [United States Capitol](31979.md) in the background

On January 23, 2026, months after the [NTT IndyCar Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series)' [2026](80635394.md) schedule was released,[^cite_note-4] [United States President](24113.md) [Donald Trump](4848272.md) posted an [AI-generated](73291755.md) video of a proposed 'D.C. Grand Prix' [motor race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorsport) to [Truth Social](69077351.md) as part of the [Department of Transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Transportation)'s push to host an IndyCar Series race on the streets of [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) for the [United States Semiquincentennial](57473130.md).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The race was officially announced on January 30, with Trump signing an [executive order](210541.md) authorizing the race.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] It was the first major motor race to be held at Washington, D.C. since the [2002 Grand Prix of Washington D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Grand_Prix_of_Washington_D.C.) [American Le Mans Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Le_Mans_Series) race[^cite_note-9] and the second event held in proximity to the [White House](33057.md) along with the [UFC Freedom 250](80931969.md).[^cite_note-10] The order instructed the [Secretary of the Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_the_Interior) and the Secretary of Transportation to design the course layout within fourteen days and to "ensure that all permits, approvals, and other authorizations ... are issued and granted as expeditiously as possible". The executive order also instructed the two Secretaries, in coordination with Washington, D.C. mayor [Muriel Bowser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muriel_Bowser), to "ensure that any roads, trails, or bridges to be used as part of the race course are properly maintained and capable of being used in such a manner".[^cite_note-11] The street circuit was composed around the [National Mall](167796.md).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

### Race promotion



Grandstands being built near 7th Street.

In February 2026, [IndyCar](30446232.md) (the NTT IndyCar Series' governing body) announced a partnership with [Monumental Sports & Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monumental_Sports_&_Entertainment) to support the promotion and commercial development of the race.[^cite_note-14] Under the agreement, Monumental was appointed as the event’s official agency for marketing, sponsorship sales, and corporate hospitality. Its role includes securing commercial partners and coordinating hospitality activities associated with the race weekend. IndyCar stated that the collaboration was intended to draw on Monumental’s experience in hosting large-scale sporting and entertainment events in the [Washington metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_metropolitan_area).[^cite_note-15]

In May 2026, IndyCar launched an officially licensed T-shirt with the slogan "One Nation, One Race" around an IndyCar Series driver posed in a manner similar to the [Lincoln Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Memorial).[^cite_note-16] In an email to The Athletic, IndyCar said: "A shirt was removed from IndyCar’s online store following feedback from customers. We understand that some individuals found its phrasing concerning and therefore have remedied the situation."[^cite_note-17] Reuters summarized the internet backlash as "fans objected to language they said could be interpreted as white-supremacist" [^cite_note-18]

### Freedom 250 Grand Prix showcase

On July 13, President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) invited drivers ([David Malukas](58540280.md), [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou) and [Felix Rosenqvist](25501259.md)) and personnel (including [Roger Penske](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Penske)) to the [White House](33057.md) for the **Freedom 250 Grand Prix showcase**. Malukas and his [Team Penske](2321219.md) crew performed a [pit stop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pit_stop) demonstration in front of the [West Wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Wing).

## Entry list

Further information: [2026 IndyCar Series § Confirmed entries](80635394.md#Confirmed_entries)

The entry list includes the 25 chartered, full-time IndyCar Series entries.[^cite_note-racer-01-06-2026-19] There were no part-time or "one-off" entries.[^cite_note-20] All entries used a [spec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-design_racing#Motorsport) [Dallara DW12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallara_DW12) chassis with the [UAK-18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallara_DW12#IR%E2%80%9318_Universal_Aero_Kit_\(UAK-18\)_\(2018%E2%80%932027\)) universal aero kit and the 2020-adopted aeroscreen. [Honda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Indy_V6) ([HRC US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Racing_Corporation_USA)) and [Chevrolet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet_Indy_V6) ([Ilmor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilmor)) are the engine providers. [Firestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firestone_Tire_and_Rubber_Company) is the exclusive tire supplier. This is the third season of [hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_electric_vehicle) [energy recovery system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_energy_recovery_system) powertrains in IndyCar.[^cite_note-21]

Going into the race, [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou) led the [2026 IndyCar Series](80635394.md) points championship, with a 133-point lead over [Kyle Kirkwood](57671103.md). Palou led the drivers this season with six victories and six pole positions before the race [Josef Newgarden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Newgarden) and [Christian Lundgaard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Lundgaard) had two wins each going into the race. [Kyle Kirkwood](57671103.md), [Pato O'Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pato_O'Ward), [Marcus Ericsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Ericsson), and [Indy 500 winner](80783201.md) [Felix Rosenqvist](25501259.md) each had one win.



Four-time IndyCar Series champion [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou) (*pictured in 2024*) led the points standings entering the Freedom 250 Grand Prix.

### Practice 1

The first practice session was held Saturday August 22, beginning at 9\:00 a.m. [ET](496028.md). The session was divided into three groups. Due to overnight rain, the first session was declared "wet", with cars utilizing rain tires. [Christian Lundgaard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Lundgaard), [Will Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Power), and [Christian Rasmussen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Rasmussen_\(racing_driver\)), all made light clipping contact with walls, but no major incidents were reported. About 25 minutes into the session, the track surface had dried sufficiently, and competitors switched to slick tires.[^cite_note-ICS-08-21-26-a-22]

## Broadcasting

### Television

The race was televised live on [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series_on_Fox) in the second year of a multi-year contract with the series. Practice and qualifying was aired on [FS1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Sports_1) and [FS2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Sports_2). [Will Buxton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Buxton) served as the lead commentator with driver analysts [James Hinchcliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Hinchcliffe) and [Townsend Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townsend_Bell).[^cite_note-24] Pre-race host [Chris Myers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Myers) and analyst [Danica Patrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danica_Patrick) returned to the crew after serving in those roles at the [Indianapolis 500](80783201.md). They were joined by Fox newcomer [Hélio Castroneves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9lio_Castroneves).[^cite_note-25]
