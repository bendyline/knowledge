The **Ministry of Ecological Transition** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Ministère de la Transition écologique*),[^cite_note-Gouvernement.fr_2018-1] commonly just referred to as **Ministry of Ecology**, is a department of the [Government of France](3398091.md). It is responsible for preparing and implementing the government's policy in the fields of sustainable development, climate, [energy transition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_transition) and biodiversity. [Monique Barbut](40678821.md) was appointed Minister of Ecological Transition on 12 October 2025 in the [Second Lecornu government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Lecornu_government).

The cabinet ministry is in [Paris](22989.md), while the ministry's administration is in the following places: [Grande Arche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grande_Arche) Paroi Sud and [Tour Sequoia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tour_Sequoia), both in [La Défense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_D%C3%A9fense).[^cite_note-2]

## History

On 8 January 1971, under [President](24899.md) [Georges Pompidou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Pompidou), the Ministry of the Environment (*Ministère de l'Environnement*) was created as a ministry subordinate to the [Prime Minister of France](218751.md). The first Minister of the Environment was [Robert Poujade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Poujade). From 1974 to 1977, the position was renamed Minister of Quality of Life; in 1978 it became Minister of the Environment and Way of Life. [Sustainable development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_development) was added in 2002.

The ministry's administration is headquartered in [Tour Sequoia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tour_Sequoia) and [La Grande Arche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Grande_Arche), both in [La Défense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_D%C3%A9fense). The cabinet of the minister is within the Hôtel de Roquelaure, [Boulevard Saint-Germain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boulevard_Saint-Germain), Paris.

## Competencies

See also: [List of national parks of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_national_parks_of_France)

The ministry is responsible for the country's environmental policy (preservation of biodiversity, Climate Kyoto Protocol application, environmental control of industries), transportation (air, road, railway and sea regulation departments), national parks and housing policy. The ministry distributes funds to research agencies or councils.[^cite_note-3] As of 2017, the ministry is also responsible for energy policy.[^cite_note-reuters-20170517-4]

### Transports

Main article: [Minister of Transport (France)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Transport_\(France\))

### Housing

Main article: [Minister in charge of Housing (France)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_in_charge_of_Housing_\(France\))

The Minister delegate of Housing, currently [Vincent Jeanbrun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Jeanbrun), is in charge of housing policy.[^cite_note-Gouvernement.fr_2018-1]

### Biodiversity

The Secretary of State in charge of Biodiversity is in charge of biodiversity policy.[^cite_note-Gouvernement.fr_2018-1] There is currently no such officeholder.

## Former names

- 1971: Ministry of the Protection of Nature and of the Environment (*Ministère de la Protection de la nature et de l'environnement*)

- 2007: Ministry of Ecology and Sustainable Development (*Ministère de l'Écologie et du Développement durable*)

- 2012: Ministry of the Environment, Sustainable Development and Energy (*Ministère de l'Écologie, du Développement durable et de l'Énergie*)

- 2016: Ministry of the Environment, Energy and the Sea (*Ministère de l'Environnement, de l'Énergie et de la Mer*)

- 2017: Ministry of Ecological and Solidarity Transition (*Ministère de la Transition écologique et solidaire*)

- 2020: Ministry of Ecological Transition (*Ministère de la Transition écologique*)

- 2024: Ministry of Ecological Transition, Energy, Climate and Risk Prevention (*Ministère de la Transition écologique, de l'Énergie, du Climat et de la Prévention des risques*)

- 2025: Ministry of Ecological Transition, Biodiversity, Forest, Sea and Fishing

- 2025: Ministry of Ecological Transition (*Ministère de la Transition écologique*)
