The **Ministry of Funds and Regional Policy** ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *Ministerstwo Funduszy i Polityki Regionalnej* is a ministry in the [Polish government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Poland), established on 15 November 2019.[^cite_note-isap-1][^cite_note-establishment-2] It is headed by [Katarzyna Pełczyńska-Nałęcz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katarzyna_Pe%C5%82czy%C5%84ska-Na%C5%82%C4%99cz), who has held the position of **Minister of Funds and Regional Policy** since 13 December 2023.[^cite_note-site-3][^cite_note-prezydent-5][^cite_note-site.about-4] It was created from the former [Ministry of Investment and Economic Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Investment_and_Economic_Development), which was disestablished simultaneously.[^cite_note-isap-1]

The ministry seeks sources of development funding and helps determine how these funds will be used in Poland.[^cite_note-prezydent-5][^cite_note-site.robimy-6] In particular, the ministry is responsible for the handling of development funds from the European Union.[^cite_note-prezydent-5][^cite_note-site.robimy-6] The ministry is also involved in promoting sustainable urban development.[^cite_note-site.robimy-6][^cite_note-unhabitat-7][^cite_note-worldbank-8]
