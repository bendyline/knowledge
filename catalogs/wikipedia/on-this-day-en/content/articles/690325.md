Since 2016, [Mali](19127.md) has been divided into ten [regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Region) and one [capital district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District). A reorganization of the country from eight to nineteen regions was passed into law in 2012,[^cite_note-1] but of the new regions, only [Taoudénit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taoud%C3%A9nit_Region) (partitioned from Tombouctou Region) and [Ménaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A9naka_Region) (formerly [Ménaka Cercle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A9naka_Cercle) in Gao Region) have begun to be implemented.[^cite_note-malijet-2][^cite_note-3] Each of the regions bears the name of its capital. The regions are divided into 56 [cercles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cercles_of_Mali). The cercles and the capital district are divided into 703 [communes](16112417.md).[^cite_note-4]

In 2023, Mali has added nine new regions to its administrative structure, bringing the total to 19 regions plus the district of Bamako. This reorganization aims to improve governance and bring public services closer to local populations. This initiative continues the decentralization efforts that began with the creation of the Taoudénit and Ménaka regions in 2016. The nineteen regions in turn are subdivided into 159 [*cercle*s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cercle_\(Mali\)), 466 *districts*, 819 [*communes*](16112417.md) and 12.712 *villages*[^cite_note-5]

## Demographics

The most populated region in 2023 is [Ségou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9gou) with 2.455 million people, and the least populated is [Kidal](2214311.md) with just 83 thousand people.

## Geography

Though five regions are composed of mainly desert, they also have half the country's land mass. The largest region is [Taoudénit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taoud%C3%A9nit_Region) and the smallest is Dioila, excluding [Bamako](56606.md).

## Regions

The regions are numbered, originally west to east, with [Roman numerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals).[^cite_note-6] The numbers were fixed by order in 1977.[^cite_note-7] The numbering after 2023 is stipulated by law 2023-006[^cite_note-8] and 2023-007[^cite_note-9] The capital [Bamako](56606.md) is administered separately and is in its own district.[^cite_note-10] The regions and the Bamako District are listed below. The population figures are from the 1998 and 2009 censuses.[^cite_note-11]
