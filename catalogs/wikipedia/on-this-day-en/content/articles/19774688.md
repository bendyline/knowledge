The **Ministry of Home Affairs**,[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] is the [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Government) [government ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government\)) responsible for [public security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_security), [civil defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_defense), [electoral administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections), [road traffic safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_traffic_safety), and [immigration](4599312.md) and [refugee](45547.md) affairs.

The minister is [Luís Neves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lu%C3%ADs_Neves)[^cite_note-3], who had previously served as National Director of the [Polícia Judiciária](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pol%C3%ADcia_Judici%C3%A1ria). He replaced [Maria Lúcia Amaral](68016804.md), who resigned in February 2026 in the wake of [Storm Kristin](82263504.md).[^cite_note-4]

## History

The ministry was created in 1736 by [King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Portugal) [John V of Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_V_of_Portugal) as the **Secretariat of State for the Interior Affairs of the Kingdom**,[^cite_note-5] later being known simply as the **Ministry of the Kingdom**.[^cite_note-6]

In 1910, after the establishment of the [Portuguese First Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_First_Republic), the ministry was renamed **Ministry of the Interior**.[^cite_note-7] In 1974, after the [Carnation Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnation_Revolution), it was again renamed, becoming the **Ministry of Home Affairs**.
