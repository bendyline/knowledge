# List of American military installations



Countries containing foreign bases and military installations of the United States.



Map of US military bases within the [continental United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_United_States).



Foreign bases and military installations of the United States.

This is a **list of military installations** owned or used by the [Department of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Defense_\(United_States\)) both in the [United States](3434750.md) and around the world. This list details only current or recently closed facilities; some defunct facilities are found at [Category\:Former military installations of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category\:Former_military_installations_of_the_United_States).

A military installation is the basic administrative unit into which the U.S. Department of Defense groups its infrastructure, and is statutorily defined as any "base, camp, post, station, yard, center, or other activity under the jurisdiction ... \[or] operational control of the Secretary of a military department or the Secretary of Defense".[^cite_note-1] An installation or group of installations may, in turn, serve as a base, which DOD defines as "a locality from which operations are projected or supported".[^cite_note-\:0-2]

The U.S. military maintains hundreds of installations, both inside the United States and overseas (with at least 128 military bases located outside of its national territory as of July 2024).[^cite_note-\:0-2] According to the U.S. Army, Camp Humphreys in South Korea is the largest overseas base in terms of area.[^cite_note-3] Most foreign military installations are located in [NATO](21133.md) countries, [Middle East](19323.md) countries, [South Korea](27019.md), [Australia](4689264.md), and [Japan](15573.md).

U.S. officials have been accused of collaborating with oppressive regimes and anti-democratic governments to secure their military bases, from Central America to the Middle East, Africa, and Asia.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEChirico201470-4] The *[Democracy Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy_Index)* classifies many of the forty-five current non-democratic U.S. base hosts as fully "authoritarian governments".[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEChirico201470-4] Military bases in non-democratic states were often rationalized during the [Cold War](325329.md) by the U.S. as a necessary if undesirable condition in defending against the communist threat posed by the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). Few of these bases have been abandoned since the [end of the Cold War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_War_\(1985%E2%80%931991\)).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEVine2017-5]

Several rounds of closures and mergers have occurred since the [end of World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftermath_of_World_War_II), a procedure most recently known as [Base Realignment and Closure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Base_Realignment_and_Closure).

## Domestic bases

### Domestic joint bases

- [The Pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pentagon) – located in [Arlington County, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_County,_Virginia), across the [Potomac River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potomac_River) from [Washington, D.C.](108956.md)

- [Joint Base Elmendorf–Richardson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_Elmendorf%E2%80%93Richardson) – located 12 kilometers (8 miles) north of [Anchorage, Alaska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anchorage,_Alaska)

- [Joint Base Pearl Harbor–Hickam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_Pearl_Harbor%E2%80%93Hickam) – located 11 kilometers (7 miles) northwest of [Honolulu, Hawaii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honolulu,_Hawaii)

- [Joint Base McGuire–Dix–Lakehurst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_McGuire%E2%80%93Dix%E2%80%93Lakehurst) – located 29 kilometers (18 miles) south of [Trenton, New Jersey](57843.md)

- [Joint Base Lindsey Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_Charleston) (formally Joint Base Charleston) – located 8 kilometers (5 miles) east of [North Charleston, South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Charleston,_South_Carolina)

- [Joint Base San Antonio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_San_Antonio) – located 8 kilometers (5 miles) north of [San Antonio, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Antonio,_Texas)

- [Joint Base Langley–Eustis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_Langley%E2%80%93Eustis) – located 12 kilometers (8 miles) east of [Newport News, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport_News,_Virginia)

- [Joint Region Marianas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Region_Marianas) – combines [Naval Base Guam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Base_Guam), [Andersen Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andersen_Air_Force_Base) and [Marine Corps Base Camp Blaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_Corps_Base_Camp_Blaz) all located on the U.S. territory of Guam

- [Joint Base Myer–Henderson Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_Myer%E2%80%93Henderson_Hall) – located 1 kilometer (1 mile) northwest of [Arlington County, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_County,_Virginia)

- [Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek–Fort Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Expeditionary_Base_Little_Creek%E2%80%93Fort_Story) – located in and around [Virginia Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Beach)

- [Joint Base Lewis–McChord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_Lewis%E2%80%93McChord) – located 17 kilometers (11 miles) southwest of [Tacoma, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tacoma,_Washington)

- [Joint Base Anacostia–Bolling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_Anacostia%E2%80%93Bolling) – located in [Southeast Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_\(Washington,_D.C.\))

- [Joint Base Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Base_Andrews) – located 22 kilometers (14 miles) south of [Washington, D.C.](108956.md)

- [Joint Forces Training Base – Los Alamitos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Forces_Training_Base_%E2%80%93_Los_Alamitos) – located in [Los Alamitos, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamitos,_California) (ARNG/[USAR](1187028.md))
