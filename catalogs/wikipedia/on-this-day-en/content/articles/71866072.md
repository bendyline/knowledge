**Karpivka** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Карпівка; Russian: Карповка) is a village in [Kramatorsk Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kramatorsk_Raion) in [Donetsk Oblast](494889.md) of eastern [Ukraine](31750.md).

## History

### Russian invasion of Ukraine

The settlement came under attack by [Russian forces](25709.md) during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md) in 2022.[^cite_note-ISW_2022-09-22-2]

## Local government

It belongs to [Lyman Urban Hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyman_urban_hromada), one of the [hromadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hromada) of Ukraine.
