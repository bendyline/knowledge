The **home minister of Bangladesh** is the minister in charge of the [Ministry of Home Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Home_Affairs_\(Bangladesh\)) of the [government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_People's_Republic_of_Bangladesh). He is also the minister of all departments and agencies under the Ministry of Home Affairs. During the [Caretaker government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caretaker_government_of_Bangladesh) period the minister of Home affairs is known as **Adviser of Home affairs**.

[Abul Hasnat Muhammad Qamaruzzaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abul_Hasnat_Muhammad_Qamaruzzaman) was the first person appointed to this office by the then [Mujibnagar government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Government_of_Bangladesh) formed during the [Bangladesh War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_War_of_Independence) in 1971.[^cite_note-Ex_Ministers-1]
