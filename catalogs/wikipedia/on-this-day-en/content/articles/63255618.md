# List of organizations designated as terrorist by Canada

&#x20;**Organizations designated as terrorist by Canada** are groups that have been listed by the [Canadian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_government) as [terrorist organisations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist_organisation).

Since 18 December 2001, the *[Anti-terrorism Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Terrorism_Act_\(Canada\))* has allowed for section 83.05 of the [Canadian *Criminal Code*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_Code_\(Canada\)) to be invoked by the [Governor in Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_in_Council) to maintain a list of "entities" that are engaged in [terrorism](936572.md), facilitating it, or acting on behalf of such an entity.[^cite_note-canada-law-1][^cite_note-cntr-trrrsm-2]

Matters relevant to overall [counterterrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-terrorism) and [national security](240468.md) within the Government of Canada fall under the jurisdiction of the [Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Public_Safety_and_Emergency_Preparedness).[^cite_note-cntr-trrrsm-2] A review is conducted by the minister every five years to determine whether an entity should remain listed. Entities may apply for a judicial review by the [Chief Justice of the Federal Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Court_of_Canada#Judges). Both ministerial and judicial reviews are published in the *[Canada Gazette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Gazette)*.[^cite_note-canada-law-1] The list is also published on the website of [Public Safety Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Safety_Canada).[^cite_note-canada-list-3]
