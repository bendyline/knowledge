**Aali en Nahri** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): علي النهري, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *ʿAlī an-Nahrī*) is a village located in the [Zahlé District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahl%C3%A9_District) of the [Beqaa Governorate](866204.md) in eastern [Lebanon](17771.md).

## History

In 1838, [Eli Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Smith) noted '*Aly en-Nahry* as a [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh) village in the [Baalbek](58207.md) area.[^cite_note-1] As of 2014, the town had 6,626 register voters, 98.41% of whom were [Shiites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-2]
