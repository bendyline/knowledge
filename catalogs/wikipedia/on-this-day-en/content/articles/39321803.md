The **Ministry of Environment and Sustainable Development** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Ministerio de Ambiente y Desarrollo Sostenible*) is the [national executive ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministries_of_Colombia) of the [Government of Colombia](76741.md) in charge of formulating, implementing, and orienting [environmental policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_policy) to ensure the [sustainable development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_development) of the country.[^cite_note-4]
