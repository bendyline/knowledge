**Canindeyú** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[kanindeˈʝu\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a [department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_\(subnational_entity\)) in [Paraguay](23420.md). The capital is the city of [Salto del Guairá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salto_del_Guair%C3%A1).[^cite_note-1] Canindeyú comes from the Guarani words Kaninde - macaw; ju - yellow, [blue-and-yellow macaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue-and-yellow_macaw) (*Ara ararauna*).

## Districts

The department is divided into 16 districts:

1. [Corpus Christi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpus_Christi,_Paraguay?action=edit\&redlink=1)

2. [Curuguaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curuguaty)

3. [General Francisco Caballero Álvarez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Francisco_Caballero_%C3%81lvarez) (Puente Kyhá)

4. [Itanará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itanar%C3%A1)

5. [Katueté](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katuet%C3%A9)

6. [La Paloma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Paloma,_Paraguay)

7. [Laurel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurel,_Paraguay?action=edit\&redlink=1)

8. [Maracaná](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maracan%C3%A1,_Paraguay?action=edit\&redlink=1)

9. [Nueva Esperanza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nueva_Esperanza,_Paraguay?action=edit\&redlink=1)

10. [Puerto Adela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Adela?action=edit\&redlink=1)

11. [Salto del Guairá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salto_del_Guair%C3%A1)

12. [Villa Ygatimí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ygatim%C3%AD)

13. [Yasy Cañy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasy_Ca%C3%B1y?action=edit\&redlink=1)

Map of Canindeyú showing tree-cover loss years, with forest remaining since 2000 in green and loss years shaded yellow through purple.

The eastern part of Canindeyu is very green; mostly rolling hills and soy bean farms. A fair portion of the population consists of Brazilian immigrants and Portuguese is widely spoken, along with Spanish and Guaraní.
