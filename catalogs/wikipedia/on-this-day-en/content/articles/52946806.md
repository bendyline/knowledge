# Economic zone

**Economic zone** may refer to:

- [Exclusive economic zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclusive_economic_zone), an area of sea over which a sovereign state has special rights

- [Special economic zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_economic_zone), an area within a country in which the business and trade laws differ from elsewhere
