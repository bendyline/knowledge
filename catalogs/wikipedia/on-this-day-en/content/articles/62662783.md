**The Game Award for Game of the Year** is an award presented annually by [The Game Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards). It is given to a video game judged to deliver the best experience across creative and technical fields. The award is traditionally accepted by the game's directors or studio executives. The process begins with over 100 [video game publications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_publication) and websites, which collectively name six games as nominees. After the nominees are selected, the winner is chosen by a combined vote between the jury (90%) and public voting (10%).

Since its inception, the award has been given to eleven video games. Publisher [Sony Interactive Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Interactive_Entertainment) has won the award three times and been nominated a record fourteen times, while [FromSoftware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FromSoftware) is the only developer with more than one win. [Bethesda Softworks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethesda_Softworks) and [Capcom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capcom) are the most nominated companies without a win at four. The most recent winner is *[Clair Obscur: Expedition 33](77750771.md)* by [Sandfall Interactive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandfall_Interactive).

## Process and history

[The Game Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards) has a voting jury consisting of over 100 [video game media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_journalism) and influencer outlets, which have been specifically selected for their work in critically evaluating [video games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_games). Each outlet completes an unranked ballot listing its top five choices; games with the most appearances across the ballots are selected as the nominees. The winners are determined between the jury (90%) and public voting (10%).[^cite_note-FAQ-1] The public vote is held via the official website and social media platforms such as [Discord](49271581.md), [Facebook](7529378.md), and [Twitter](9988187.md);[^cite_note-GameSpot_Discord-2][^cite_note-2020_PCGamesN-3] in China, fan voting is held via [Bilibili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilibili).[^cite_note-FAQ-1]

Any game released before a certain date in November prior to the ceremony is eligible for award consideration.[^cite_note-2020_Polygon-4] As a result, any game released after the cutoff date is eligible in the following year's ceremony,[^cite_note-FAQ-1] such as 2018's *[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Smash_Bros._Ultimate)* nominations at [the Game Awards 2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2019);[^cite_note-2019_TheGamer-5] similarly, games released between the ballot due date in early November and the cutoff date in mid-to-late November are often overlooked, such as *[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars_Jedi:_Fallen_Order)* in both 2019 and [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2020)[^cite_note-9] and *[Demon's Souls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demon's_Souls_\(2020_video_game\))* and *[Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel's_Spider-Man:_Miles_Morales)* in 2020.[^cite_note-2020_Polygon-4] [Early access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_access) games available before the cutoff date are eligible, as are [live service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_service) games regardless of their release year:[^cite_note-FAQ-1] 2018's *[Among Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Among_Us)* received several nominations in 2020.[^cite_note-2020_Game_Rant-10]



[Josef Fares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Fares) presenting Game of the Year at [The Game Awards 2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2022)

The Game Award for Game of the Year is given to a video game judged to deliver the best experience across creative and technical fields.[^cite_note-2022_Gematsu-11] It is presented as the final award of the ceremony and is widely considered its most prestigious honor.[^cite_note-2014_Daily_Dot-12][^cite_note-2021_Push_Square-13][^cite_note-2021_Game_Developer-14] Since 2017, the announcement of the winner has been preceded by a medley performance of the scores of each nominee by the Game Awards Orchestra, conducted by [Lorne Balfe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorne_Balfe).[^cite_note-2017_GameSpot-15][^cite_note-2023_BBC-16] The category was expanded from five to six nominees in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2018).[^cite_note-2018_Nominees-17]

The award is traditionally accepted by the director of the winning game or an executive from the studio; the first award in [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2014) was accepted by *[Dragon Age: Inquisition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Age:_Inquisition)* executive producer Mark Darrah and [BioWare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BioWare) general manager Aaryn Flynn.[^cite_note-2014_Global_News-18] The Game Awards host and producer [Geoff Keighley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoff_Keighley) presented the award for the first four ceremonies.[^cite_note-2014_Daily_Dot-12][^cite_note-2015_DarkZero-19][^cite_note-2016_Twitter-20][^cite_note-2017_Polygon-21] Other presenters include directors of past winners—such as [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2016) winner *[Overwatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overwatch_\(2016_video_game\))*'s lead director [Jeff Kaplan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Kaplan) in 2018,[^cite_note-2018_USgamer-22] 2020 winner *[The Last of Us Part II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_Part_II)*'s creative director [Neil Druckmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Druckmann) in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2021),[^cite_note-2021_Push_Square-13] 2021 winner *[It Takes Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It_Takes_Two_\(video_game\))*'s director [Josef Fares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Fares) in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2022),[^cite_note-2022_VentureBeat-23] 2023 winner *[Baldur's Gate 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur's_Gate_3)*'s director [Swen Vincke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swen_Vincke) in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2024),[^cite_note-2024_IGN-24] and 2024 winner *[Astro Bot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astro_Bot)*'s director Nicolas Doucet in [2025](81382602.md)[^cite_note-2025_GamesRadar-25]—and celebrity guests like [Vin Diesel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vin_Diesel) and [Michelle Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Rodriguez) in 2019,[^cite_note-2019_CNET-26] [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan) in 2020,[^cite_note-2020_Eurogamer-27] and [Timothée Chalamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timoth%C3%A9e_Chalamet) in 2023.[^cite_note-2023_Polygon-28]
