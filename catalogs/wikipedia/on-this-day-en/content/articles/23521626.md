**Kevin Krawietz** (born 24 January 1992) is a German professional [tennis](29773.md) player who specializes in doubles. He achieved his career-high doubles ranking of world No. 5 on 10 February 2025. Krawietz has won twelve doubles titles on the [ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour), including the [2024 ATP Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_ATP_Finals) with [Tim Pütz](41501290.md), becoming the first all-German pair to win the title. He is a two-time [Grand Slam](197638.md) champion at the French Open in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) and [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) with [Andreas Mies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreas_Mies).[^cite_note-1]

Krawietz was also a finalist with Tim Pütz at the [2024 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) and reached the mixed doubles semifinals at the [2021 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles) and at the [2022 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles), partnering [Květa Peschke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kv%C4%9Bta_Peschke) and [Nicole Melichar-Martinez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Melichar-Martinez) respectively.

In singles, Krawietz reached his highest ranking of world No. 211 in December 2018. He has represented Germany in the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup) since 2019, and also competed at the [2020 Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics) in men's and mixed doubles and [2024 Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) in men's doubles.

## Junior career

In Juniors, Krawietz won one junior [Grand Slam](197638.md) title, the [2009 Wimbledon Championships – Boys' doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Boys'_doubles) tournament.[^cite_note-2]

## Professional career

### 2009–2017: ATP debut

Krawietz made his ATP debut at the [2009 German Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_International_German_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) as a wildcard. He lost to [Jan Hernych](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Hernych) in the first round in three sets.[^cite_note-3] In 2010 Krawietz received a wildcard for the [Bavarian Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_BMW_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) in Munich, where he lost to [Tomáš Berdych](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom%C3%A1%C5%A1_Berdych) in the first round in straight sets.[^cite_note-4] Through 2017 Krawietz played mainly on the [ITF Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITF_World_Tennis_Tour) and the [ATP Challenger Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Challenger_Tour). In 2015, he won his first Challenger doubles title at the [Morocco Challenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Morocco_Tennis_Tour_%E2%80%93_Meknes) in Meknes, partnering with [Maximilian Marterer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximilian_Marterer).[^cite_note-5]

### 2018–2020: Consecutive French Open titles, world No. 7

Krawietz reached the third round at the [2018 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) in doubles with partner [Andreas Mies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreas_Mies) as a qualifier, where they lost to the later champions [Mike Bryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Bryan) and [Jack Sock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Sock) despite having two match points.[^cite_note-6]

Krawietz won his first doubles title on the ATP Tour at the [2019 New York Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_New_York_Open_%E2%80%93_Doubles), again with Mies.[^cite_note-7] He and Mies then won the [2019 French Open doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) title as unseeded players, defeating the French duo [Jérémy Chardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%A9r%C3%A9my_Chardy) and [Fabrice Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabrice_Martin) in the final.[^cite_note-8] This victory made them the first all-German team in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Era) to win a Grand Slam title, and the first since [Gottfried von Cramm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_von_Cramm) and [Henner Henkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henner_Henkel) in 1937.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

He won his first main draw singles match on the ATP Tour as a qualifier at the [Antalya Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Antalya_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), defeating wildcard [Cem İlkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cem_%C4%B0lkel) in the first round.[^cite_note-11] At the [2019 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), he and Mies reached the semifinals.[^cite_note-12] They won their third title at the [2019 European Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_European_Open) in Antwerp.[^cite_note-13]

In 2020, Krawietz and Mies successfully defended their [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) title, defeating [Mate Pavić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mate_Pavi%C4%87) and [Bruno Soares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Soares) in straight sets in the final. After winning the title twice, they have not yet lost a match at the French Open as a pair.[^cite_note-14]

### 2021–2022: Four doubles titles, partnership changes

In 2021 Krawietz won his fifth doubles title at the [Bavarian Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_BMW_Open_%E2%80%93_Doubles) in Munich, partnering [Wesley Koolhof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_Koolhof).[^cite_note-15] For the French Open, he teamed up with [Horia Tecău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horia_Tec%C4%83u). As a twice defending champion he extended his unbeaten run to 15 wins before finally suffering his first French Open defeat in a quarterfinal loss.[^cite_note-16] He and Tecău won the [2021 Halle Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Halle_Open_%E2%80%93_Doubles), which was his first title at an [ATP 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour_500) tournament, and his first on grass.[^cite_note-17]

At the [2022 Barcelona Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Barcelona_Open_Banc_Sabadell_%E2%80%93_Doubles), Krawietz won the title, joining up with Mies. Within a week, they also won the title on home soil in [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_BMW_Open).[^cite_note-18] At the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), he lost his first doubles match at the tournament when he and Mies suffered an upset loss in the first round.

### 2023: New partnership with Putz, Wimbledon semifinal

In March, partnering with [Fabrice Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabrice_Martin), he reached the semifinals of the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Miami_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles).[^cite_note-19]



Krawietz on his way to a Monte-Carlo semifinal in 2023.

He reached the semifinals of the [2023 Monte-Carlo Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Monte-Carlo_Masters_%E2%80%93_Doubles)[^cite_note-20] and for the fourth time the quarterfinals of the [2023 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) with his new partner [Tim Pütz](41501290.md), with a win over [Romain Arneodo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Arneodo) and [Sam Weissborn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Weissborn), getting revenge for their loss to the same duo in Monte-Carlo six weeks earlier.[^cite_note-21] They lost to eventual champions [Ivan Dodig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Dodig) and [Austin Krajicek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Krajicek) in three sets.[^cite_note-22]

He reached the semifinals for the first time at the [Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) with Pütz. The same month, they won their first title together at the [Hamburg European Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Hamburg_European_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles).[^cite_note-23]

### 2024: US Open finalist and ATP Finals champion

At the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), he reached the quarterfinals for the first time at this tournament with Pütz.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] The pair also reached the semifinals at the [Indian Wells Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_BNP_Paribas_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) where they lost to unseeded pair and eventual champions Wesley Koolhof/Nikola Mektić. At the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Miami_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) they lost also in the semifinals to second seeds Austin Krajicek/Ivan Dodig.[^cite_note-26] Next they reached the quarterfinals at the [Monte-Carlo Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Monte-Carlo_Masters_%E2%80%93_Doubles) where they lost again to the eventual champions and also unseeded pair of [Joran Vliegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joran_Vliegen) and [Sander Gillé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sander_Gill%C3%A9).[^cite_note-27]

He reached his third major final with Pütz at the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) defeating fifth seeds [Andrea Vavassori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Vavassori) and [Simone Bolelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Bolelli), 16th seeds [Máximo González](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1ximo_Gonz%C3%A1lez) and [Andrés Molteni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Molteni) and fourth seeds [Marcelo Arévalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcelo_Ar%C3%A9valo) and [Mate Pavić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mate_Pavi%C4%87) in the semifinals.[^cite_note-28] At the [2024 ATP Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_ATP_Finals) with Pütz, he reached the semifinals defeating [Simone Bolelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Bolelli) and [Andrea Vavassori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Vavassori).[^cite_note-29] They became the first all-German duo to reach the semifinals[^cite_note-30] and then the final in ATP Finals history. They also became the first No. 8 seeds to reach the doubles final,[^cite_note-31] which they won in straight sets against Marcelo Arévalo and Mate Pavić.[^cite_note-32]

### 2025: Masters title, world No. 5

Krawietz and Pütz reached the final at the [Adelaide International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Adelaide_International_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), losing to [Simone Bolelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Bolelli) and [Andrea Vavassori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Vavassori) in a deciding champions tiebreak.[^cite_note-33] He won his first [ATP Masters 1000](30872553.md) title at the [Shanghai Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Rolex_Shanghai_Masters_%E2%80%93_Doubles), again with Pütz.[^cite_note-34]
