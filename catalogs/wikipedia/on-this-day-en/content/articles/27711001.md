# Ivory Coast at the FIFA World Cup

For the women's team, see [Ivory Coast at the FIFA Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory_Coast_at_the_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup).

[Ivory Coast](1039856.md), recognized by [FIFA](11049.md) as Côte d'Ivoire, have appeared in the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) on four occasions in [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup), [2010](168079.md), [2014](656933.md), and [2026](36581929.md).

## Player records

### Most appearances



Yaya Touré holds the national record for most FIFA World Cup matches played

[Yaya Touré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaya_Tour%C3%A9) was the only Ivorian player to feature in all nine of the nation's World Cup matches from 2006 to 2014, giving him the sole appearance record at the tournament for his country.
