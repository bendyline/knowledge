This is a **list of [destroyers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destroyers) of the United States Navy**, sorted by [hull number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hull_number). It includes all of the series DD, [DL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destroyer_leader), [DDG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_missile_destroyer), DLG, and DLGN.

CG-47 *Ticonderoga* and CG-48 *Yorktown* were approved as destroyers (DDG-47 and DDG-48) and redesignated cruisers before being laid down; it is uncertain whether CG-49 *Vincennes* and CG-50 *Valley Forge* were ever authorized as destroyers by the United States Congress (though the fact that the DDG sequence resumes with DDG-51 *Arleigh Burke* argues that they were).

Main article: [List of United States Navy destroyer leaders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_States_Navy_destroyer_leaders)

The DL category was established in 1951, with the abolition of the experimental [CLK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cruisers_of_the_United_States_Navy#Hunter-Killer_cruisers_\(CLK\)) category. CLK-1 became [DL-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Norfolk_\(DL-1\)) and DDs 927–930 became [DLs 2–5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitscher-class_destroyer). By the mid-1950s the term destroyer leader had been dropped in favor of [frigate](11464.md). The DLG sequence was deactivated in the 1975 fleet realignment, most DLGs and DLGNs were reclassified as [CGs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruiser) and CGNs, 30 June 1975. However, DLG 6–15 became DDG 37–46. DL-1 through DL-5 had been decommissioned prior to this time; DLG-6 *Farragut* through DLG-15 *Preble* became DDG-37 through DDG-46. DLG-16 *Leahy* through DLGN-40 *Mississippi* became CG-16 through CGN-40.
