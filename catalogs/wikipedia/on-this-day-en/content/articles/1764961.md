**Federal University of Paraíba** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Universidade Federal da Paraíba*, **UFPB**) is a [public university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_university) located in the city of [João Pessoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Pessoa,_Para%C3%ADba), [Paraíba](229523.md), [Brazil](3383.md). Along with [Federal University of Campina Grande](8791967.md), it is the main university in the state of [Paraíba](229523.md), Brazil.

UFPB offers degrees (Bachelor's, Master's and Doctoral) in areas such as the liberal arts (including Law), health sciences (including Medicine and Dentistry), engineering and technology (Computer Science and Computer Engineering), business, education and the fine arts (Music, Theater, Art).

There are also campuses in [Areia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Areia,_Para%C3%ADba), [Bananeiras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bananeiras) and North Coast ([Rio Tinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Tinto_Group) and [Mamanguape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamanguape)).

## 2002 campus detachment

In 2002 the UFPB, at the time formed by the campuses in [João Pessoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Pessoa,_Para%C3%ADba), [Areia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Areia,_Para%C3%ADba), [Campina Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campina_Grande), [Patos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patos,_Brazil), [Cajazeiras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cajazeiras) and [Sousa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sousa,_Para%C3%ADba), had four of them detached due to the creation of the [Federal University of Campina Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ufcg).
