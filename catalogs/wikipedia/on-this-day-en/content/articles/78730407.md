**Anas Hasan Khattab** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): أنس حسن خطاب; born 7 April 1986), *nom de guerre*, **Abu Ahmad Hudood**[^cite_note-fn1-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] is a Syrian politician and former rebel commander who has served as the incumbent [minister of Interior](34679004.md) in the [Syrian transitional government](79584988.md) since 29 March 2025 and current director of the National Security Office since 19 July 2026.[^cite_note-5]

He was the deputy commander of [Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levant_Liberation_Committee) under [Ahmed al-Sharaa](44007699.md), and the head of the General Security Apparatus within the group.[^cite_note-syria-tv-6][^cite_note-7]

## Early life and education

Anas Hasan Khattab,[^cite_note-8] was born in April 1986 in the town of [Jayrud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayrud), located in the western Qalamoun region of [Rif Dimashq Governorate](4521946.md), Syria. He initially studied architectural engineering at the [University of Damascus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damascus_University) before leaving Syria for [Iraq](7515928.md) in 2008. His departure was reportedly motivated by his intent to "fight against the [American occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Iraq_\(2003%E2%80%932011\))" during that time.[^cite_note-syria-tv-6]

Khattab got the nickname "Abu Ahmad Hudoud"[^cite_note-fn1-2] (Hudoud meaning Borders in Arabic) from his days in [Al-Qaeda in Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qaeda_in_Iraq) under the leadership of [Abu Musab al-Zarqawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Musab_al-Zarqawi), which eventually evolved into the [Islamic State of Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_State_of_Iraq). Khattab was entrusted with managing a guesthouse on the [Syrian-Iraqi border](46975552.md) that prepared fighters crossing the border.[^cite_note-9]

## Career

### Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham

Khattab joined [Al-Nusra Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Nusra_Front) (later [Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham](53015755.md)) in 2012 and quickly rose through its ranks. He became known for his strategic and intelligence capabilities, playing a critical role in securing the group's dominance in the Idlib region. He was instrumental in dismantling rival factions, including [Hurras al-Din](56718004.md), by penetrating their ranks and orchestrating precision operations.[^cite_note-syria-tv-6]

Khattab was reportedly recruited by [Turkey](11125639.md)'s [National Intelligence Organization](575957.md) (**MİT**) and acted as a liaison between them and Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham after the latter took control of [Idlib](2528679.md).[^cite_note-\:0-10]

As the head of the General Security Apparatus, Khattab oversaw efforts to eliminate extremist factions such as [ISIL](9087364.md) in Idlib, employing sophisticated infiltration and rapid-response tactics. His leadership has been credited with maintaining relative stability in HTS-controlled areas.[^cite_note-syria-tv-6]

Khattab's career within Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham included several high-profile positions:[^cite_note-syria-tv-6]

- Former General Administrator of Jabhat al-Nusra

- Deputy to Abu Mohammad al-Julani

- Head of the General Security Apparatus

### Syrian government

Khattab served as the first Director of the newly formed [General Intelligence Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Intelligence_Service_\(Syria\)) during the [Syrian caretaker government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_caretaker_government) following the [fall of the Assad regime](78548066.md).[^cite_note-syria-tv-6] In March 2025, Khattab, along with Syrian President [al-Shaara](44007699.md) and other senior ministers met with a Turkish delegation which included the head of Turkish intelligence.[^cite_note-11] The meeting came immediately following a deal between the Syrian government and Kurdish militias in North East Syria who have clashed with Turkish forces in the past.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

On 29 March 2025, Khattab was appointed [Minister of Interior](34679004.md) in the [Syrian transitional government](79584988.md).[^cite_note-14]

## Personal life

Khattab is reportedly multilingual and is known for his preference for operating behind the scenes. Despite his prominence, no public images of him have been released until his appointment on 26 December 2024. His professional background in architecture contrasts with the political and economic studies pursued by many of his contemporaries in [Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham](53015755.md).[^cite_note-syria-tv-6]

In January 2026, media reports indicated that his sister was released from detention at the [Al-Hol camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hol_camp) in northeastern Syria, where she had allegedly been held on suspicion of links to the Islamic State. Khattab reportedly attempted to secure her release by offering a ransom through intermediaries.[^cite_note-15]

On 12 February 2026, the [United Nations](31769.md), along with its secretary-general, said that Khattab was a target of 5 ISIL and [Saraya Ansar al-Sunnah](79264370.md) backed assassination attempts, along with foreign minister [Asaad al-Shaibani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asaad_al-Shaibani) and president al-Sharaa.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

## Controversies

Khattab had faced allegations of involvement in the assassination of rival jihadist leaders via U.S. drone strikes, accusations frequently leveled by factions such as [Hurras al-Din](56718004.md).[^cite_note-syria-tv-6] He was designated a terrorist by the [United States](3434750.md) in 2012,[^cite_note-syria-tv-6] and by the [United Nations](31769.md) in 2014.[^cite_note-18] The UN's designation included targeted sanctions against Khattab.[^cite_note-19] After Bashar al-Assad fled Syria in December 2024, the new Syrian government of which Khattab is Interior Minister has requested that these sanctions be lifted.[^cite_note-20] In November 2025, with the support of the United States and other countries, the UN Security Council [lifted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_2799) the sanctions against him, along with Syrian President [Ahmed al-Sharaa](44007699.md). On 7 November, the United Kingdom announced the removal of Khattab and al-Sharaa from its sanctions list, stating that he was no longer subject to asset freezes.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

The Turkish government resisted these designations until November 2016, when they too designated Khattab a terrorist, but intelligence cooperation continued and even expanded as HTS solidified its control over Idlib.[^cite_note-\:0-10] Despite their intelligence cooperation, in 2021, the [Turkish Interior Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Interior_\(Turkey\)) released a report which designated Khattab as affiliated with ISIL.[^cite_note-\:0-10]
