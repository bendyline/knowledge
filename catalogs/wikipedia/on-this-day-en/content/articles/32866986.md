**Ghana–South Africa** relations refers to the [historical and current relationship](429154.md) of the [Republic of Ghana](12067.md) and the [Republic of South Africa](17416221.md). Both countries are former [British colonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_colonies) and members of the [African Union](10634933.md) and [Commonwealth of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations).

Following the end of the [apartheid regime in South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa_under_apartheid) in 1994 and the first democratic elections in Ghana in 1992, trade grew extensively with the establishment of bilateral relations. In 2005, the [South African Institute of International Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Institute_of_International_Affairs) reported that South African investors called Ghana a "beacon of hope" in West Africa and the volume of trade between the two economies was growing extensively.[^cite_note-SouthAfrica.info-1]
