**Zamfara**[Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/0/0f/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Zamfara.wav/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Zamfara.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> ([Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language): *Jihar Zamfara*; [Fula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fula_language): *Leydi Zamfara*; [Adlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adlam_script): 𞤤𞤫𞤴𞤣𞤭 𞤶𞤢𞤥𞤬𞤢𞤪𞤢) is a [state](226734.md) in northwestern [Nigeria](21383.md). The capital of Zamfara state is [Gusau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gusau) and its current [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Governors_of_Zamfara_State) is [Dauda Lawal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dauda_Lawal). The state is named after the [Zamfara River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamfara_River) which flows through the state. Until 1996, the area was part of [Sokoto State](2227660.md).

Zamfara is a densely populated area with the [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people). The Zamfarawa, a section of the Hausa ethnic nationality, cluster mainly in [Anka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anka,_Nigeria), [Gummi](17946147.md), [Bukkuyum](24894595.md) and [Talata Mafara](24894653.md) [Local Governments areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria). The [Gobirawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gobir), another Hausa sub-group, populate [Shinkafi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinkafi) Local Government.[^cite_note-\:1-4] The Gobirawa actually migrated from the [Gobir Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gobir_Kingdom). The Burmawa sub-group are found in Bakura, while the Fulani are scattered all over the State, with significant concentrations in [Bungudu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bungudu), [Maradun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maradun), and [Gusau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gusau). Hausa sub-groups in [Chafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chafe,_Nigeria), [Bungudu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bungudu) and [Maru](24894504.md), are mainly Katsinawa, Garewatawa and Hadejawa, while the Alibawa are located at Kaura Namoda and Zurmi. The Alawan Shehu Usmanu Fulani's are found in Birninmagaji.[^cite_note-\:3-5]

It is bordered to the north by the Republic of the [Niger](21373.md) for 32 km (20 miles), to the south by [Kaduna State](2227637.md) for 117 km (73 miles) and [Niger](1723431.md) for 38 km (24 miles), to the east by [Katsina State](2227638.md), and to the west by the states of [Sokoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_\(city\)) and [Kebbi](2227643.md). It has a population of 3,278,873 according to the 2006 census and contains fourteen local government areas.

## History

Main article: [History of Zamfara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Zamfara)



Zamfara State Museum.

The people of Zamfara have over the years struggled for autonomy, but it was not until 1996 that the then military administration of the late [General Sani Abacha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sani_Abacha) detached the Zamfara State from [Sokoto State](2227660.md) with an area of 38,418 square kilometres. The first Governor was [Jibril Yakubu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jibril_Yakubu).[^cite_note-\:3-5]

The area today called Zamfara state was one of the old [Hausa Kingdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_Kingdoms) like Kano, Katsina, Gobir, Kabi and Zazzau. The earliest inhabitants of Zamfara were said to have been hunters and giants.[^cite_note-6] They established their first settlement at Dutsi, which was the first capital of Zamfara. It extends up to the bend of [River Rima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Rima) to the north west and River Ka in the south west. Zamfara Kingdom was established in the 11th century and flourished up to 16th century as a city-state. Its capital has shifted with the fortunes of the kingdom from place to place like Dutsi and Birnin Zamfara.

In the first half of the 18th century, its then capital Birnin Zamfara, was destroyed by the [Gobir Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gobir) and a new capital was established in [Anka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anka,_Nigeria) by the second half of the 19th century. Zamfara had many centres of commerce and scholarship that attracted many scholars like the Yandoto city. It became part of the [Sokoto Caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_Caliphate) after the 1804 [jihad](16203.md) by [Usman dan Fodio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usman_dan_Fodio). In fact, Usman Danfodiyo settled in [Sabon Gari](11800566.md) where Sarkin Zamfara Abarshi had already established a garrison headquarters during the early days of his Jihad as a base from where he fought Gobir and Kabi.[^cite_note-\:0-7]

At the wake of British colonialism, the emerging town of Gusau became an important commercial and administrative centre with road and rail networks passing through it. With the creation of states during the [Gowon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakubu_Gowon) Administration, Zamfara Kingdom became part of the then North West state and later the Sokoto State.[^cite_note-\:0-7]

## Geography

### Climate

The climate condition of Zamfara is tropical with temperatures rising up to 38 °C (100.4 °F) and above between March and May. Rainy season starts in late May to September while the mild season known as [Harmattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmattan) lasts from December to April. The hottest months in Zamfara are March and April, and that is just before the first rains. the onset of the rains bring a cooling effect with temperature dropping.[^cite_note-8]

## Pollution

Significant health risks are posed by dust and particulate matter in Zamfara, especially to the deepest parts of the lungs, where they may exacerbate lungs diseases like bronchitis and asthma attacks while weakening the body defences against infections.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

## Demographics



A household in Zamfara State

Zamfara State is mainly populated by [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people).[^cite_note-11]

The state capital is an important commercial centre with a heterogeneous population of people from all over Nigeria. As in all major towns in Nigeria, all the major towns in Zamfara have a large population of other peoples from different parts of Nigeria.[^cite_note-\:3-5]

## Languages

English is spoken by the Zamfara state government as an official language, Hausa and Arabic language is spoken by the Muslim ummah and Islamic clerics.[^cite_note-e22-12]

## Economy



Gathering firewood in rural Zamfara



A camel being transported home from a market

Agriculture and gold mining are the state's main occupations and the central source of income. Irrigation is required for [cereals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cereals) and [legumes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legumes), hence the slogan "farming is our pride".[^cite_note-13]

Over 80 percent of the population is engaged in agriculture. Major products include millet, [guinea corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinea_corn), maize, rice, groundnut, cotton, tobacco and beans.[^cite_note-auto-14] The State is known as farming is our pride because Agriculture provides foodstuffs, raw materials and employment opportunities for young people in the State.[^cite_note-\:3-5]

The state is [one of the poorest in Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_poverty_rate) and has one of the highest incidences of [extreme poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_poverty) (over 60% of the population) according to [World Bank](36752.md) data from 2018.[^cite_note-15]

## Transport

### Federal Highways

[A126](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A126_highway_\(Nigeria\)) northwest from [Katsina State](2227638.md) near Kutcheri as the Gusau Rd via [Tsafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chafe,_Nigeria), Unguwar Audu, [Gusau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gusau), [Bungundu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bungudu), Kabai, and [Talata Mafara](24894653.md) to Sokoto State at Bimasa as the Sokoto-Gusau Rd.

### Other major roads

- the Katsina-Gusau Rd north from [Gusau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gusau) via [Kaura Namoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaura_Namoda) to [Katsina State](2227638.md) as the Jibiya-Zurmi-Barakeji Rd at Gidan Baure,

- the Kaura Namoda-Shinkafi Rd north from Kaura Namoda to [Sokoto State](2227660.md) at Kwanar Isa as the Shinkafi-Kafi Rd,

- the Dansadau Rd south from Gusau to Dan Sadau where the road continues west to [Kebbi State](2227643.md) at Kaboro,

- the Anka-Malichi Rd west from [A126](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A126_highway_\(Nigeria\)) at Malinchi to Anka where the Gayawa-Anka Rd continues to Gayawa, where the Gummi-Gayawa Rd continues to Sokoto State at Tashar-Bundun,

- the Zuru-Mahuta Rd south from Gayawa via Zugu to [Kebbi State](2227643.md) near Sakke.

## Incidents

Main article: [Zamfara State lead poisoning outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamfara_State_lead_poisoning_outbreak)

In 2009, gold mining became a greater source of income in Zamfara State as worldwide gold prices rose drastically.[^cite_note-23] High concentrations of Lead in the ore from which gold was being extracted led to [a Lead poisoning outbreak in the state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamfara_State_lead_poisoning_outbreak), requiring national and international intervention to remediate affected areas and provide medical care to children with severe Lead poisoning.[^cite_note-\:1-4]

### Bandit attacks

Main articles: [Nigerian bandit conflict](68366617.md), [Zamfara kidnapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamfara_kidnapping), [Zurmi massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zurmi_massacre), and [2022 Zamfara massacres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Zamfara_massacres)

Zamfara is known to experience several recent attacks from bandits. Some among them are:

On 26 February 2021, 279 girls were abducted from their boarding school located in Jangebe, Zamfara during the [Zamfara kidnapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamfara_kidnapping). They have since been released.[^cite_note-24]

On 11–12 June 2021, motorcycle-riding bandits killed over 53 villagers, mostly farmers.[^cite_note-25]

On 4–6 January 2022, over 200 people were killed by [bandits](68366617.md) in Zamfara State.[^cite_note-A-26][^cite_note-27]

## Religion

Main article: [Religion in Zamfara State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion_in_Zamfara_State)

[Islam](6037917.md) is the principal and major religion of the state. [Christianity](5211.md) also has many adherents. The original native religions also remain but they have the fewest followers. These religions are mainly practiced in ancient settlements like Dutsi and Kwatarkwashi. Zamfara was the first state in Nigeria to introduce [Sharia](28840.md) law during the tenure of [Ahmad Sani Yerima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_Sani_Yerima), the former Governor of the state.
