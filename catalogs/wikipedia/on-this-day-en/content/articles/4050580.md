**Refunding**[^cite_note-1] occurs when an entity that has issued [callable bonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callable_bond) calls those [debt securities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debt_securities) from the debt holders with the express purpose of reissuing new debt at a lower [coupon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coupon_\(bond\)) [rate](163115.md). In essence, the issue of new, lower-interest debt allows the company to prematurely refund the older, higher-interest debt.&#x20;

On the contrary, non-refundable bonds may be callable but they cannot be re-issued with a lower coupon rate—they cannot be refunded.
