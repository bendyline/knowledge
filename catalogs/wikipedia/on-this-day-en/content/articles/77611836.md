The **women's tournament** in [ice hockey at the 2026 Winter Olympics](75053831.md) took place in [Milan](36511.md), Italy, between 5 and 19 February 2026.[^cite_note-1] Ten countries [qualified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_hockey_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_qualification) for the tournament; six via [ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIHF_World_Ranking) by the [IIHF](16760449.md), three via qualification tournaments, and [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_women's_national_ice_hockey_team) as hosts. [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia_women's_national_ice_hockey_team) and [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus_women's_national_ice_hockey_team) were banned from competing due to the countries' [invasion of Ukraine](70149799.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

[Canada](1329942.md) were the defending champions, having won the record-extending fifth Olympic title in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_hockey_at_the_2022_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_tournament).[^cite_note-4] The [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_women's_national_ice_hockey_team) won the gold medal by defeating Canada 2–1 in [OT](633242.md#Ice_hockey) in the gold medal game.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland_women's_national_ice_hockey_team) won the bronze medal for the second time in history.[^cite_note-7]

## Format

The ten teams were split into two groups of five teams each, in which they played against each team once. All teams from Group A and the top-three ranked teams from Group B advanced to the quarter-finals. A knockout system was used after the group stage.
