The **president of the Republic of Lithuania** ([Lithuanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_language): *Lietuvos Respublikos Prezidentas*) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of the [Republic of Lithuania](17675.md). The president directs and appoints the executive branch of the [Government of Lithuania](16769373.md), represents the nation internationally and is the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Lithuanian Armed Forces](17827.md). The president is directly elected by the [citizens of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanians) for a five-year term, with the office holder limited to serving two terms consecutively. The current president is [Gitanas Nausėda](58519561.md) who assumed office on July 12, 2019.

## Eligibility

To be eligible for election, candidates must be at least 40 years old on the election day and reside in Lithuania for at least three years, in addition to satisfying the eligibility criteria for a member of the parliament. A person who has been removed from office through impeachment for breach of constitution or an oath of office may not be elected as a president.[^cite_note-PresidentLaw-2]

## Powers

The Lithuanian president has somewhat more executive authority than their counterparts in neighboring [Estonia](1581712.md) and [Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Latvia); the Lithuanian president's function is very similar to that of the presidents of [France](24899.md) and [Romania](273659.md). Similarly to them, but unlike presidents in a fully presidential system such as [the United States](24113.md), the Lithuanian president generally has the most authority in foreign affairs. In addition to the customary diplomatic powers of Heads of State, namely receiving the [letters of credence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letters_of_credence) of foreign ambassadors and signing [treaties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaties), the president determines Lithuania's basic foreign policy guidelines. The president is also the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Lithuanian Armed Forces](17827.md), and accordingly heads the State Defense Council and has the right to appoint the [Chief of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Defence_\(Lithuania\)) (subject to Seimas consent).[^cite_note-Powers-3]

The president also has a significant role in domestic policy, possessing the right to submit bills to the [Seimas](571739.md) and to [veto](74661.md) laws passed by it, appointing the [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Lithuania) and approving the government formed by them, and also having the right to [dissolve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_Parliament) the Seimas and call [snap elections](462915.md) following a successful [motion of no confidence](208101.md) or if the Seimas refuses to approve the government's budget within sixty days. However, the next elected Seimas may retaliate by calling for an earlier presidential election.[^cite_note-Constitution-4]

Finally, the president ensures an effective judiciary, being responsible for nominating one third of the judges of the [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Lithuania), and the entirety of the [Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Lithuania), for appointment by the Seimas; the president also has the right to directly appoint all other judges.

## Election

Under the [Constitution of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Lithuania) adopted in 1992, the president is elected to a five-year term under a modified [two-round system](25932.md): a candidate requires an absolute majority of the vote and either voter turnout to be above 50% or for their vote share to be equivalent to at least one-third of the number of registered voters to win the election in the first round.[^cite_note-5] If no candidate does so, the two candidates with the most votes face each other in a second round held two weeks later. Upon taking office, the president must suspend any formal membership in a political party.[^cite_note-Constitution-4]

If the president dies or becomes incapacitated while in office, the Speaker of the Seimas assumes the office until a new president can be inaugurated following fresh elections.
