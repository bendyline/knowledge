**Kuhestak** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): کوهستک)[^cite_note-4] is a small coastal city in, and the capital of, [Bemani District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bemani_District) of [Sirik County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirik_County), [Hormozgan](448064.md) province, [Iran](14653.md).[^cite_note-Sirik_County_Establishment-5] It also serves as the administrative center for [Bemani Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bemani_Rural_District).[^cite_note-Minab_County_Rural_Districts-6] The city has a harbour facing onto the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md).

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, Kuhestak's population was 2,449 in 430 households, when it was a village in [Bemani Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bemani_Rural_District) of the former [Byaban District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byaban_District) of [Minab County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minab_County).[^cite_note-2006_Hormozgan_Province-7] The following census in 2011 counted 2,548 people in 591 households,[^cite_note-2011_Hormozgan_Province-8] by which time the district had been separated from the county in the establishment of Sirik County. The rural district was transferred to the new Bemani District.[^cite_note-Sirik_County_Establishment-5] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 3,060 people in 746 households,[^cite_note-2016_Hormozgan_Province-2] when Kuhestak had been elevated to the status of a city.[^cite_note-Tazian-e_Pain,_Kuhestak,_Tirur_and_Lamazan_Cities-9]

## History

### IRGC Navy

In 1986 the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy](4676345.md) deployed [HY-2 Silkworm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silkworm_\(missile\)) [cruse missiles](6590.md) near Kuhestak.[^cite_note-10] By 2009, the Iranian Navy had Hudong fast missile-carrying boats equipped with Silkworm missiles based in Kuhestak.[^cite_note-11] By 2014, the Kuhestak Missile Battery, with missile facilities and engaging in missile technical design and engineering, was located in Kuhestak.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

### 2026 wedding attack

On 1 September 2026, amidst the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), the [United States military](32212.md) allegedly [launched an airstrike](84122275.md) on a home in the area hosting a wedding party. Five people were killed and over 60 injured.[^cite_note-telegraph-20260904-14][^cite_note-scmp-20260903-15][^cite_note-nyt-20260902-16]
