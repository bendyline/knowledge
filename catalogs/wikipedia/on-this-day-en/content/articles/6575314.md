**Situbondo Regency** ([Madurese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madurese_language): *Kabhupatèn Situbânḍâ*) is a [regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regencies_of_Indonesia) *(kabupaten)* in the east of [East Java](320443.md) province, [Indonesia](14579.md). It covers an area of 1,655.05 km<sup>2</sup>, and had a population of 647,619 at the 2010 Census[^cite_note-Biro_Pusat_Statistik_2011-2] and 685,967 at the 2020 Census;[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] the official estimate as at mid 2025 was 698,375 (comprising 338,331 males and 353,304 females).[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1] It is located towards the eastern end of Java, before [Banyuwangi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banyuwangi_Regency). Its administrative centre is Situbondo, a small town within the regency. One of its famous tourist sites is [Baluran National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baluran_National_Park).

## Climate

Situbondo has a [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) (Aw) with heavy rainfall from December to March and moderate to little rainfall from April to November.

### Languages

Further information: [Situbondo Madurese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situbondo_Madurese?action=edit\&redlink=1)

[Situbondo Madurese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situbondo_Madurese?action=edit\&redlink=1) is a [Madurese dialect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madurese_language) spoken mainly in Situbondo Regency, in the [eastern salient of Java](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_salient_of_Java). This dialect is especially closely related or very close to the Sumenep dialect spoken on the easternmost island of [Madura](183981.md).[^cite_note-6] Situbondo, where this language is spoken, although separated from the island of Madura, most or almost all of its population are Madurese, mainly migrating from [Sumenep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumenep). [Madurese people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madurese_people) really dominates all the places there, this can be seen from the fact that in all the districts there the dominant language is Madurese.[^cite_note-7]
