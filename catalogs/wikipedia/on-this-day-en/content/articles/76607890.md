**Statesville Regional Airport** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **SVH**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **KSVH**, [FAA](11186.md) [LID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Location_identifier): **SVH**), formerly known as Statesville Municipal Airport, is a public, city-owned [general aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_aviation) [airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport) located 3 miles (5 km) southwest of downtown [Statesville](127986.md), a city in [Iredell County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iredell_County,_North_Carolina), [North Carolina](21650.md).

In 2023, Statesville airport had 41,200 aircraft operations, with 76% being transient general aviation, 20% being local general aviation, 3% being [air taxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_taxi) services, and <1% being military activity.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2]

## Services

Statesville airport features a single 7,003-foot [asphalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphalt_concrete) [runway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway) and a parallel [taxiway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxiway). It is also home to seventeen total aircraft hangars as well as two aircraft maintenance facilities for [Cessna Citation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_Citation_family) and [Dornier 328](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dornier_328) jets.[^cite_note-\:1-2]

Statesville Regional Airport is known for being home to several aircraft hangars owned by NASCAR teams. The crews of notable NASCAR drivers including [Dale Earnhardt Sr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Earnhardt), [Dale Earnhardt Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Earnhardt_Jr.), [Ryan Newman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Newman_\(racing_driver\)), [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski), [Greg Biffle](1637067.md), and [Martin Truex Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Truex_Jr.) have owned hangars at the airport.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:2-3]

The [Lowe's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowe's) company owns a 40,000-square foot hangar at Statesville that it built in 2004 after moving its headquarters from [North Wilkesboro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Wilkesboro,_North_Carolina) to [Mooresville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mooresville,_North_Carolina). The hangar is home to five [Dassault Falcon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dassault_Falcon) jets and one [Sikorsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_Aircraft) helicopter.[^cite_note-\:2-3]

## History

Statesville Regional Airport was opened around 1935 as a grass [airstrip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerodrome) called Statesville Municipal Airport. The main runway was paved in the 1950s followed by the paving of another in the 1960s. The second runway was later repaved in 1999 to function as the taxiway. Dale Earnhardt Sr. was the first NASCAR driver to build a hangar at Statesville airport, where he operated a small private [carrier](1942.md) called Champion Air to provide flights for him and his team on a fleet of three [Embraer ERJ-145 ERs](273890.md). In the early 2000s, the main runway was extended from 5,000 feet to 7,000 feet.[^cite_note-\:1-2][^cite_note-\:2-3]
