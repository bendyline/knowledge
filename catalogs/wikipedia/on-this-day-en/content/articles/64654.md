**Gironde** ([/ʒɪˈrɒnd/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [*zhih-ROND*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key),[^cite_note-3] [US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_English) usually [/dʒɪˈ-/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [*jih-*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key);[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] French: [\[ʒiʁɔ̃d\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/54/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Gironde.wav/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Gironde.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/54/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Gironde.wav/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Gironde.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>; [Occitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occitan_language): *Gironda*, pronounced [\[dʒiˈɾundɔ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Occitan)) is the largest [department](38523.md) in the southwestern French [region](58846.md) of [Nouvelle-Aquitaine](45092904.md). Named after the [Gironde estuary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gironde_estuary), a major waterway, its [prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_in_France) is [Bordeaux](4097.md). In 2023, it had a population of 1,690,493.[^cite_note-pophist-6] The famous [Bordeaux wine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bordeaux_wine) region is in Gironde. It has [six arrondissements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrondissements_of_the_Gironde_department), making it one of the [departments](38523.md) with the most arrondissements ([Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrondissements_of_the_Nord_department_\(France\)) also has six, while [Pas-de-Calais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrondissements_of_the_Pas-de-Calais_department) has the most of any department, with seven).

## History

Gironde is one of the original 83 departments created during the [French Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolution) on 4 March 1790. It was created from parts of the [former provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_France) of [Guyenne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guyenne) and [Gascony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gascony).

From 1793 to 1795, the department's name was changed to *Bec-d'Ambès* to avoid the association with the [Girondist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girondists) political party of the French Revolution.

In July 2022 and July 2026, Gironde was affected by [large wildfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Gironde_wildfires).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## Geography

Gironde is part of the current region of [Nouvelle-Aquitaine](45092904.md) and is surrounded by the departments of [Landes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landes_\(department\)), [Lot-et-Garonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lot-et-Garonne), [Dordogne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dordogne) and [Charente-Maritime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charente-Maritime) and the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md) on the west. With an area of 10,000 km<sup>2</sup>, Gironde is the largest department in [metropolitan France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_France), and the second-largest in entire [France](5843419.md). Its size is larger than [Lebanon](17771.md) or the province of [Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten). If overseas departments are included, however, Gironde's land area is dwarfed by the 83,846 km<sup>2</sup> of [French Guiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Guiana).

Gironde is well known for the [Côte d'Argent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%B4te_d'Argent) beach which is Europe's longest, attracting many [surfers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfer) to [Lacanau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lacanau) each year. It is also the birthplace of [Jacques-Yves Cousteau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques-Yves_Cousteau) who studied the sea and all forms of life in water.

[The Great Dune of Pyla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_Dune_of_Pyla) in [Arcachon Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcachon_Bay) near Bordeaux is the tallest sand dune in Europe.[^cite_note-9]
