The **Four Seasons Hotel Damascus** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): فُنْدُق فَوْر سِيزُون دِمَشْق, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Funduq Fawr Sīzūn Dimašq*) is a 297-room [five-star hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_rating)[^cite_note-2] and was part of the [Toronto](64646.md)-based [Four Seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Seasons_Hotels_and_Resorts) luxury hotels and resorts from 2005 to 2019. It is located in the central district neighborhood of [Damascus](8914.md), [Syria](7515849.md), near the [Barada River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barada_River), on [Shukri al-Quwatli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shukri_al-Quwatli) Street.[^cite_note-fivestar-3]

The hotel was financed by Saudi prince [Al-Waleed bin Talal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Waleed_bin_Talal) and was constructed by the local Fouad Takla Company. It opened on 1 December 2005.[^cite_note-NYTimes-4] Al-Waleed later sold his stake in the hotel to [Syrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_people) businessman [Samer Foz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samer_Foz). The Syrian government is the second largest stakeholder in the hotel.[^cite_note-Hotel_Management-5][^cite_note-Bloomberg-6]

Four Seasons Hotels severed its management contract with the property effective 19 June 2019, due to US sanctions on Foz. The US government accused Foz of war-profiteering and doing business with the Syrian President, [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md).[^cite_note-The_National-7]

The United Nations and several of its agencies used the hotel, which had the best security rating by UN's standards in Damascus, for their principal residences and offices during the civil war. In 2021, the United Nations spent \$11.5 million at the hotel.[^cite_note-8]

As of 2023, the hotel continues to operate using the Four Seasons name and logo, though it is no longer recognized as part of the chain.[^cite_note-The_National-7]

### Damascus bomb attack

Main article: [2026 Damascus bombings](83637437.md)

On 7 July 2026, explosions occurred near the hotel during French President [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md)'s visit to Damascus, killing one and injuring 36 others. Macron was staying at the hotel, but was not there when the explosions occurred.[^cite_note-9]
