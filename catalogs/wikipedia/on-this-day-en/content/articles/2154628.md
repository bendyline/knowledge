**Sarangani**, officially the **Municipality of Sarangani** ([Cebuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_language): *Lungsod sa Sarangani*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Sarangani*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Davao Occidental](38297169.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 22,515 people, making it the least populated municipality in the province.[^cite_note-5]

## History

On June 11, 1978, the islands of Sarangani and [Balut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balut_island?action=edit\&redlink=1) were carved out from the municipality of [Jose Abad Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jose_Abad_Santos,_Davao_Occidental) to form the new municipality of Sarangani, then in [Davao del Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davao_del_Sur), through Presidential Decree No. 1550 signed by [President](271126.md) [Ferdinand Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Marcos).[^cite_note-6] On October 28, 2013, the municipality was ceded to [Davao Occidental](38297169.md) as a result of a [plebiscite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Davao_Occidental_creation_plebiscite), in which the majority of voters approved the creation of the new province.[^cite_note-7]

## Geography



Map of the Sarangani islands

The municipality consists of two major islands (the eponymous Sarangani Island and [Balut Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balut_Island)) and one minor islet (Olanivan Island), collectively called the Sarangani Islands, located just south of mainland [Mindanao](20546.md) island in the [Celebes Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celebes_Sea). The municipality shares a maritime border with [Sangihe Islands Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangihe_Islands_Regency) of [Indonesia](14579.md).[^cite_note-8]

### Climate

Sarangani has a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) (Af) with moderate to heavy rainfall year-round.

Due to its proximity to Indonesia, the town also has a significant population of migrants from the Sangihe-Talaud region, including several generations of Indonesian families. As early as 1918, Indonesians extensively planted coconuts in the municipality. Sarangani is also used by Indonesians as a transit point on their way to mainland Mindanao.[^cite_note-15]
