**Zernez** (Swiss Standard German: [\[tsɛrˈnɛts\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Standard_German)) is a village and a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Switzerland) in the [Engiadina Bassa/Val Müstair Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engiadina_Bassa/Val_M%C3%BCstair_Region) in the [Swiss](26748.md) [canton](180752.md) of the [Grisons](169850.md). On 1 January 2015 the former municipalities of [Lavin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavin) and [Susch](4299431.md) merged into the municipality of Zernez.[^cite_note-2]

Zernez is a major node in the regional road network, with roads leading into the Upper Engadine and towards [Italy](14532.md), the Lower Engadine and towards [Austria](26964606.md), over the [Fuorn Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuorn_Pass) into the [Val Mustair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val_Mustair) and over the [Flüela Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fl%C3%BCela_Pass). Zernez is the center for visitors of the [Swiss National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_National_Park) ([Romansh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romansh_language): Parc Naziunal).

## History

Zernez is first mentioned in 1131 as *Gumpo de Ernece*. In 1161-64 it was mentioned as *Zarnetz* though both references are found in a copy of the document from 1365.[^cite_note-HDS-3]

### Topography

After the 2015 merger Zernez had an area of 344.04 km<sup>2</sup> (132.83 sq mi).[^cite_note-Arealstatistik-4] Before the merger Zernez had an area, (as of the 2004/09 survey) of 203.91 square kilometers (78.73 sq mi). Of this area, about 7.8% is used for agricultural purposes, while 35.6% is forested. Of the rest of the land, 0.7% is settled (buildings or roads) and 55.9% is unproductive land. Over the past two decades (1979/85-2004/09) the amount of land that is settled has increased by 8 ha (20 acres) and the agricultural land has decreased by 130 ha (320 acres).[^cite_note-SFSO2016-5]

Until 2017 the municipality was located in the Sur Tasna sub-district of the Inn district, after 2017 it was part of the Engiadina Bassa/Val Müstair Region.[^cite_note-6] It is located along the [Ofen Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ofen_Pass) road into the [Engadine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engadin). It consists of the village of Zernez and the section of Brail.

[Lai da Ova Spin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai_da_Ova_Spin) is a compensation reservoir on the river [Spöl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sp%C3%B6l). It is located in the upper areas of the Lower Engadine valley (Romansh: Engiadina Bassa). It is located at an elevation of 1,473 m (4,833 ft) and is connected to the railway network of the [Rhätische Bahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rh%C3%A4tische_Bahn). In Zernez, the stream [Spöl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sp%C3%B6l) empties into the [Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inn_River) (Romansh: En).

### Climate

Zernez has an average of 102.7 days of rain per year and on average receives 752 mm (29.6 in) of [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation_\(meteorology\)). The wettest month is August during which time Zernez receives an average of 103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation. During this month there is precipitation for an average of 11.4 days. The driest month of the year is February with an average of 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation over 11.4 days.[^cite_note-MeteoSchweiz-7]

## Demographics

### Population

Zernez has a population (as of December 2020) of 1,506.[^cite_note-Stat2020_GR-8] As of 2013, 18.6% of the population are resident foreign nationals. Over the last 3 years (2010-2013) the population has changed at a rate of 1.14%. The [birth rate](515898.md) in the municipality, in 2013, was 8.7 while the [death rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_rate) was 6.9 per thousand residents.[^cite_note-SFSO2016-5]

As of 2013, children and teenagers (0–19 years old) make up 20.6% of the population, while adults (20–64 years old) are 59.4% and seniors (over 64 years old) make up 19.9%.[^cite_note-SFSO2016-5]

In 2013 there were 508 private households in Zernez. Of the 282 inhabited buildings in the municipality, in 2000, about 29.4% were single family homes and 38.3% were multiple family buildings. Additionally, about 43.6% of the buildings were built before 1919, while 10.6% were built between 1991 and 2000.[^cite_note-Housing-9] In 2012 the rate of construction of new housing units per 1000 residents was 5.22. The vacancy rate for the municipality, in 2014, was 4.03%.[^cite_note-SFSO2016-5]

### Languages

Most of the population (as of 2000) speaks (Rhaeto-)Romansh (61.1%), with German being second most common (31.3%) and Italian being third ( 4.4%).[^cite_note-SFSO-11] The Romansh speaking majority speak the [Vallader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vallader) dialect. In 1880 there were 84% of the population spoke Romansh as their first language, in 1900 it was 79% and in 1941 it was 78%. In 1990 about 81% understood Romansh and in 2000 it was 80%.

### Education

In Zernez about 72.5% of the population (between age 25-64) have completed either non-mandatory [upper secondary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Switzerland#Secondary) or additional higher education (either university or a *[Fachhochschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fachhochschule)*).[^cite_note-SFSO-11]

### Economy

As of  2012, there were a total of 653 people employed in the municipality. Of these, a total of 63 people worked in 18 businesses in the [primary economic sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_sector_of_the_economy). The [secondary sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_sector_of_the_economy) employed 208 workers in 27 separate businesses. Finally, the [tertiary sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tertiary_sector_of_the_economy) provided 382 jobs in 88 businesses. In 2013 a total of 5.2% of the population received social assistance.[^cite_note-SFSO2016-5]

### Criminality

In 2014 the crime rate, of the over 200 crimes listed in the [Swiss Criminal Code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strafgesetzbuch_\(Switzerland\)) (running from murder, robbery and assault to accepting bribes and election fraud), in Zernez was 52 per thousand residents, slightly lower than the national average (64.6 per thousand). During the same period, the rate of drug crimes was 7.8 per thousand residents. The rate of violations of immigration, visa and work permit laws was 0.9 per thousand residents, which is only 18.4% of the rate for the entire country.[^cite_note-12]

## Politics

In the [2011 federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Swiss_federal_election) the most popular party was the [SVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_People's_Party) with 45.8% of the vote. The next three most popular parties were the BDP (25.6%), the [SP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_of_Switzerland) (9.6%) and the [FDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FDP.The_Liberals) (8.0%). In the federal election, a total of 443 votes were cast, and the [voter turnout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voter_turnout) was 58.2%.[^cite_note-13]

In the [2007 federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Swiss_federal_election) the most popular party was the [SVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_People's_Party) which received 45.9% of the vote. The next three most popular parties were the [SPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_of_Switzerland) (23.4%), the [FDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Democratic_Party_of_Switzerland) (20.6%) and the [CVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Democratic_People's_Party_of_Switzerland) (7.2%).[^cite_note-SFSO-11]

## Sights

### Heritage sites of national significance

The abandoned village of *Gonda*, the *[Reformed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Reformed_Church) Church in Zernez*, the *Schloss Wildenberg ([Wildenberg Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildenberg_Castle_\(Zernez\)))*, the *Palazzo Bezzola* and the *Richtstätte/Galgen Fuorcha* are listed as Swiss [heritage sites of national significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_inventory_of_cultural_property_of_national_and_regional_significance).[^cite_note-14]

The church was first built in 1367. When the [Protestant Reformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant_Reformation) entered Zernez in 1553, the church was stripped of all the statues and paintings. Then, in 1609 the old church was replaced with a new [baroque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baroque_architecture) building, though the [romanesque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanesque_architecture) bell tower of the old church was retained.

The castle was built in the 12th Century but was rebuilt into a baroque castle later. Since 1956 it has served as the town hall.

### Other sights

In addition to the four Swiss Heritage Sites of National Significance, Zernez has several other notable sites. These include the [gothic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_architecture) [catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church) chapel of St. Sebastian, the *Morenturm*, the *Swiss National Park* and park house, the abandoned tower of *La Serra* and in Brail, a [medieval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Ages) Reformed church.

The chapel of St. Sebastian stopped being used as a catholic chapel following the Reformation. In 1952 it was put back into service as a chapel. The *Morenturm* is a medieval [tower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_house) in the village. The Swiss National Park, as of 2009, is the only National Park in Switzerland. It has an area of 174.2 km<sup>2</sup> (67.3 sq mi) and is the largest protected area of the country. It was one of the earliest national parks in Europe, being founded on 1 August 1914. The *Nationalparkhaus* in Zernez is a museum for the National Park. The tower of *La Serra* was built either in the 13th or 14th Century, though it could have been built as late as just before the [Swabian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swabian_War) in 1495. During the *[Bündner Wirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%BCndner_Wirren)* (or Confusion of the Leagues) it was reinforced and a regiment from Schauenstein was stationed there. After the Confusion, it was abandoned and fell into disrepair. In 1996–98 it was repaired and reinforced.

## Transportation

The municipality has four railway stations: [Zernez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zernez_railway_station), [Susch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susch_railway_station), [Sagliains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagliains_railway_station), and [Lavin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavin_railway_station). All four are located on the [Bever–Scuol-Tarasp line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bever%E2%80%93Scuol-Tarasp_railway) and between them have with regular service to [Disentis/Mustér](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disentis/Must%C3%A9r_railway_station), [St. Moritz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Moritz_railway_station), [Landquart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landquart_railway_station), [Scuol-Tarasp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuol-Tarasp_railway_station), and [Pontresina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontresina_railway_station).
