The **director-general of the World Health Organization** is the [chief executive officer](52234.md) of the [World Health Organization](33583.md) (WHO) and the principal advisor to the [United Nations](31769.md) on matters pertaining to global health. The director general is elected by and answers to the [World Health Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Health_Assembly) (WHA).[^cite_note-1] The current director-general is [Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus](39712205.md), who was appointed on 1 July 2017,[^cite_note-2] and re-appointed on 24 May 2022.[^cite_note-tagrenew-3] The director-general also leads the [World Health Organization Secretariat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Health_Organization_Secretariat?action=edit\&redlink=1) and is also the *[ex officio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex_officio)* secretary of the [World Health Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Health_Assembly), the World Health Organization Executive Board, and of all commissions and committees, and conferences convened by the organization.[^cite_note-4]

&#x20;\


## Selection process

Candidates for director-general can be proposed by Member States, then nominated by the executive board and appointed by the [World Health Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Health_Assembly).[^cite_note-5]

The appointment process begins more than one year prior to the May vote, when the World Health Organization sends out a letter informing Member States that the nomination process has begun. The nomination period ends in mid-September, and candidates are announced at the end of October. If there are multiple candidates, the [executive board of the World Health Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_board_of_the_World_Health_Organization?action=edit\&redlink=1) – a panel of members from 34 member countries representing the various World Health Organization regions – interviews the nominees.[^cite_note-hbnew-6]

The term of the director-general lasts for five years. A director-general can be re-appointed only once.[^cite_note-7] The director-general is typically appointed in May, when the World Health Assembly meets.
