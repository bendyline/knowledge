**Gaziantep Province** ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *Gaziantep ili*) is a [province](428896.md) and [metropolitan municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_municipalities_in_Turkey) in south-central [Turkey](11125639.md). It is located in the westernmost part of [Turkey](11125639.md)'s [Southeastern Anatolia Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_Anatolia_Region) and partially in the [Mediterranean Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_Region,_Turkey). Its area is 6,803 km<sup>2</sup>,[^cite_note-3] and its population is 2,154,051 (2022).[^cite_note-tuik-2] Its capital is the city of [Gaziantep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaziantep). It neighbours [Adıyaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad%C4%B1yaman_Province) to the northeast, [Şanlıurfa](1353138.md) to the east, [Syria](7515849.md) and [Kilis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilis_Province) to the south, [Hatay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatay_Province) to the southwest, [Osmaniye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmaniye_Province) to the west and [Kahramanmaraş](727324.md) to the northwest.

An important trading center since ancient times, the province is also one of Turkey's major manufacturing zones, and its agriculture is dominated by the cultivation of [pistachio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pistachio) nuts.

In ancient times, first under the power of [Yamhad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamhad), then the [Hittites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hittites) and later the [Assyrians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assyrian_people) controlled the region. It saw much fighting during the [Crusades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crusades), and [Saladin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saladin) won a key battle there in 1183. After [World War I](4764461.md) and the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md)'s disintegration, it was invaded by the forces of the [French Third Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Third_Republic) during the [Turkish War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_War_of_Independence). It was returned to Turkish control after the [Treaty of Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Lausanne) was signed, formally ending hostilities between Turkey and the [Allies of World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allies_of_World_War_I).

Originally known as Antep, the title *gazi* (meaning *veteran* in Turkish) was added to the province's and the provincial capital's name in 1921, due to its population's actions during the Turkish War of Independence.

[Kilis Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilis_Province) was part of Gaziantep Province until it separated in 1994. [Turks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people) are the majority in the province although there is a substantial [Kurdish](17068.md) population.[^cite_note-4]

## Geography



Landscape of the province of Gaziantep

Gaziantep is traversed by the northeasterly lines of equal latitude and longitude.

### Geology

Two major active [geological faults](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geological_fault) meet in western Gaziantep near the border with adjoining [Osmaniye Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmaniye_Province): the [Dead Sea Transform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Sea_Transform) and the [East Anatolian Fault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Anatolian_Fault). These represent the [tectonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tectonic) boundary between the northward-moving [Arabian Plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabian_Plate) to the east, and the converging [African](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Plate) and [Eurasian Plates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasian_Plate) to the west.

On February 6, 2023, at 04\:17 [TRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Turkey) (01\:17 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)), a [moment magnitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_magnitude) ( [M<sub>w</sub>](6207163.md#Mw)) 7.8 [earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Turkey%E2%80%93Syria_earthquake) occurred in Gaziantep Province on the [East Anatolian Fault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Anatolian_Fault), which caused widespread damage and loss of life. The [epicenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicenter) was 37 km (23 mi) west-northwest of the city of [Gaziantep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaziantep). At 13\:24, it was followed by an M<sub>w</sub>7.7 earthquake.[^cite_note-anss7.8-5]

- [Nurdağı](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nurda%C4%9F%C4%B1)

- [Oğuzeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O%C4%9Fuzeli)

- [Şahinbey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9Eahinbey)

- [Şehitkamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9Eehitkamil)

- [Yavuzeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yavuzeli)

The city of Gaziantep encompasses Şahinbey and Şehitkamil districts; İslahiye and Nurdağı districts form the western part of the province, while the remaining districts lie to the east of Gaziantep city.
