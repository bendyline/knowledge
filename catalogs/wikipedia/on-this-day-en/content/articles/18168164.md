**Nuseirat Camp** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مخيّم النصيرات) is a [Palestinian refugee camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_refugee_camp) located in the middle of the [Gaza Strip](12047.md),[^cite_note-2] five kilometers north-east of [Deir al-Balah](1470463.md). The [refugee camp](438046.md) is in the [Deir al-Balah Governorate](14704596.md), Gaza Strip. According to the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics), the refugee camp had a population of 31,747 and the surrounding Nuseirat municipality had a population of 54,851 in 2017.[^cite_note-PrelimCensus2017-1] The camp was established after the [1948 Palestinian expulsion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Palestinian_expulsion) during the [1948 Palestine war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Palestine_war).

## History

### Ancient history

The Nuseirat camp was named after the local Nuseirat tribe, part of the larger [Hanajira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanajira) confederation, that historically dominated the area between Deir al-Balah and Gaza. Excavations in the area have revealed remains of a large [coenobium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cenobium), including a church with a crypt, a bathhouse and a hospice, reminiscent of the [monastery of Martyrius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monastery_of_Martyrius) at [Ma'ale Adummim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma'ale_Adumim). The side has been tentatively identified as that of the [monastery of Seridus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monastery_of_Seridus).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

### Origin of the refugee camp

The camp was established after the [1948 Palestinian expulsion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Palestinian_expulsion) during the [1948 Palestine war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Palestine_war). Most refugees of this camp came from the southern areas of [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_\(region\)) such as [Beersheba](312755.md) and the coastal plain. Prior to the camp's establishment by [UNRWA](32270.md), the roughly 16,000 original refugees settled in the grounds of a former British military prison at the site.[^cite_note-5]

### 2000s

In May 2018 a mosque, the Grand Nuseirat Mosque, was opened by the [Qatari](8238258.md) envoy to Gaza, Mohammed Al Emadi.[^cite_note-6]

#### Gaza war

Main articles: [Attacks on refugee camps in the Gaza war § Nuseirat](75393092.md#Nuseirat), and [Nuseirat rescue and massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuseirat_rescue_and_massacre)

See also: [Al-Sardi school attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Sardi_school_attack)



A resident of the camp carries his grandchildren injured by Israeli bombing. 29 October 2023

The Nuseirat refugee camp has been bombed repeatedly since the start of the [Gaza war](74998519.md). On 18 October 2023 the Grand Nuseirat Mosque was bombed and destroyed by Israeli airstrikes,[^cite_note-7] subsequently the attacks continued causing more than a hundred victims.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

On 8 June 2024, the [IDF](39237.md) and [Yamam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamam) rescued four [Israeli hostages](75025451.md) held by [Hamas](13913.md) in Nuseirat, including [Noa Argamani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidnapping_of_Noa_Argamani), during [a rescue operation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Nuseirat_rescue_operation).[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] An IDF spokesperson claimed that the hostages were guarded by armed militants and concealed among Gazan civilians. The [Gaza Health Ministry](52186562.md) reported that at least 274 Palestinians were killed and 698 wounded in the Israeli airstrikes on the refugee camp, causing international outrage and the attack deemed a [massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuseirat_refugee_camp_massacre).[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] The operation took place in two sites in Nuseirat, where the hostages were kept, a school and a marketplace.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

The IDF killed at least 10 civilians and injured at least 16 in a missile strike on a water distribution point on July 13, 2025.[^cite_note-r565-31][^cite_note-b056-32] Six of the dead and seven of the wounded were children.[^cite_note-r565-31][^cite_note-b056-32] The Israeli military stated the missile missed its intended target by dozens of meters because of a "technical error".[^cite_note-b056-32]
