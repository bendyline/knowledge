&#x20;The **National Committee of Reconciliation and Development** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Comité national du rassemblement pour le développement*, CNRD) was the ruling [military junta](216815.md) of [Guinea](12176.md) from 5 September 2021 to 17 January 2026.

## Historical background

The CNRD seized power in the [2021 Guinean coup d'état](68651369.md) on 5 September 2021. Colonel [Mamady Doumbouya](68651894.md), leader of the coup, stated that the CNRD would steer the country for an 18-month transition period.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-\:4-2]
