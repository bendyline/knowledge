# List of aviation shootdowns and accidents during the 2026 Iran war

This is a list of aviation shootdowns, incidents and accidents during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md) based on visual evidence or official self-admission from involved parties. It includes proven [helicopters](8286923.md), [fixed-wing aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixed-wing_aircraft) and [military drones (UCAVs)](337755.md) losses.
