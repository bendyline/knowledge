**Chatuchak** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): จตุจักร, pronounced [\[t͡ɕàʔ.tùʔ.t͡ɕàk\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Thai)) is one of the [50 districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Bangkok) (*khet*) of [Bangkok](56615.md), [Thailand](30128.md). The district is bounded by seven other districts (from the north clockwise): [Lak Si](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lak_Si_district), [Bang Khen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Khen_district), [Lat Phrao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lat_Phrao_district), [Huai Khwang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huai_Khwang_district), [Din Daeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Din_Daeng_district), [Phaya Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phaya_Thai_district), and [Bang Sue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Sue_district).

## History

Chatuchak was originally part of [Bang Khen district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Khen_district). It became a separate district in 1989.[^cite_note-2] The name of the district came from its two major landmarks, [Chatuchak Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatuchak_Park) and [Chatuchak Weekend Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatuchak_Weekend_Market).

## Government and infrastructure

The district is divided into five sub-districts (*[khwaeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khwaeng)*).

The [Department of National Parks, Wildlife and Plant Conservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_National_Parks,_Wildlife_and_Plant_Conservation), [Royal Forest Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Forest_Department) and [Department of Fisheries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Fisheries_\(Thailand\)) with [Criminal Court of Thailand](43063176.md) as well as [Department of Land Transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Land_Transport_\(Thailand\)) have their headquarters in the district. Central Juvenile and Family Court is located on [Kamphaeng Phet Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamphaeng_Phet_Road) near [Bang Sue Grand Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Sue_Grand_Station). [Klong Prem Central Prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klong_Prem_Central_Prison), also known as "Lat Yao Prison", is in the district.

## Markets

The best-known site in the district is the [Chatuchak Weekend Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatuchak_Weekend_Market), the largest market in Thailand. Northwest of the weekend market is another market, Chatuchak Plaza, which sells clothes and many other products. Across Kamphang Phet Road is [Or Tor Kor Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Or_Tor_Kor_Market) (ตลาด อ.ต.ก.) belonging to The Marketing Organization for Farmers (องค์การตลาดเพื่อการเกษตร), offering fresh agricultural products and food. Just north of the market along Kamphang Phet Road is an area selling plants and garden products.[^cite_note-3]

## Parks



Chatuchak Park seen from [Mo Chit BTS Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mo_Chit_BTS_Station)

Adjoining the Chatuchak Weekend Market to the north is the Chatuchak Park complex, covering 1.20 km<sup>2</sup> of a former [State Railway of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Railway_of_Thailand) golf course consisting of [Chatuchak Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatuchak_Park) (สวนจตุจักร), [Queen Sirikit Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Sirikit_Park) (สวนสมเด็จพระนางเจ้าสิริกิติ์ฯ), [Wachirabenchathat Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wachirabenchathat_Park) (สวนวชิรเบญจทัศ), and [Chak Phupha Su Maha Nathee Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chak_Phupha_Su_Maha_Nathee_Park) (สวนจากภูผาสู่มหานที), the newest addition to the area.

Chatuchak Park (0.304 km<sup>2</sup>) is the first park in the complex. It opened in December 1980 and is on [Phahonyothin Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phahonyothin_Road) next to the [Mo Chit BTS Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mo_Chit_BTS_Station). It is the most accessible park of the three. Also included in this park is the [Train Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hall_of_Railway_Heritage).



[Queen Sirikit Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Sirikit_Park)

[Queen Sirikit Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Sirikit_Park) (0.224 km<sup>2</sup>) is a botanical garden built to honor the queen's 60th birthday in 1992. It was formally opened in December 1996. Plants collected in the garden include [hibiscus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hibiscus), [plumeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plumeria), and [palms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_tree). Within the park compound is the Children's Museum. The park is behind the Chatuchak Weekend Market parking lot.

The [Wachirabenchathat Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wachirabenchathat_Park) (0.600 km<sup>2</sup>) is the biggest park of the complex. It was called State Railway Public Park (สวนรถไฟ, *Suan Rot Fai*) but was renamed in July 2002 in honor of Prince [Maha Vajiralongkorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajiralongkorn,_Crown_Prince_of_Thailand)'s 50th birthday. It contains a butterfly park and the tallest fountain in Thailand.

[Chak Phupha Su Maha Nathee Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chak_Phupha_Su_Maha_Nathee_Park) (0.040 km<sup>2</sup>), is the newest and smallest park within the Chatuchak Park complex. It was officially opened in early 2025 and serves as a continuation of adjacent Queen Sirikit Park. The park's design and layout are inspired by the royal initiative of [Queen Sirikit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Sirikit), The Queen Mother, focusing on forest conservation. It is intended as a learning centre to educate visitors about sustainable living and the vital relationship between people and forests.

## Locations

The [Elephant Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephant_Tower) ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): ตึกช้าง) is one of the most distinctive buildings in Bangkok. Shaped like an elephant, it consists of three towers, A, B, and C, joined at the top; it includes condominiums and office space.

On Vibhavadi Road, opposite [Kasetsart University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasetsart_University), is the [Museum of Contemporary Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_of_Contemporary_Art_\(Bangkok\)) (MOCA). One of the main artists featured is [Thawan Duchanee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thawan_Duchanee) (ถวัลย์ ดัชนี, also spelled Tawan Datchanee), a late artist who is still regarded as one of Thailand's most renowned modern artists.

[Central Ladprao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Ladprao) is the largest shopping center in the district; it contains Central Department Store, Bangkok Convention Centre, the first convention hall in Thailand, cinemas, and many retail shops. Other shopping centers in Chatuchak District include [Major Cineplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_Cineplex) Ratchayothin, featuring a 14-screen multiplex cinema; and [Union Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Mall), an eight-storey shopping mall popular with young shoppers. Mixt Chatuchak and [JJ Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JJ_Mall) are two shopping malls located near Chatuchak Weekend Market; opposite JJ Mall is Bangsue Junction (affectionately known as *Tuek Daeng*; "the red building"), a shopping mall known for home decorations and antiques.

The [Bangkok Mass Transit System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok_Mass_Transit_System) (BTSC), the operator of the [BTS Skytrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BTS_Skytrain), has its headquarters in the district on Phahonyothin Road, opposite Chatuchak Park.

[Channel 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBTV_Channel_7), [Thai Rath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_Rath), and [Matichon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matichon), three of Thailand's leading mass media organizations, also have their headquarters in this district.

[Wat Samian Nari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wat_Samian_Nari) and Wat Thewasunthorn are the only two [Buddhist temples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wat) located in Chatuchak.

[RS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RS_Promotion), one of Thailand's leading entertainment companies, is also headquartered in the district.

The renowned amusement park [Dan Neramit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Neramit), also known as Magic Land, was located on Phahonyothin Road near [Central Ladprao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Ladprao) from 1976 to 2010; it has since been turned into a [go-kart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Go-kart) racing track.

## Education



The [Kasetsart University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasetsart_University) administration building

[Kasetsart University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasetsart_University) is one of the top [universities in Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Thailand#Universities). Originally focused on agricultural sciences, it now includes many fields including business and engineering. The university is on a large block bounded by [Vibhavadi Rangsit Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vibhavadi_Rangsit_Road), [Ngamwongwan Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngamwongwan_Road) and [Phahonyothin Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phahonyothin_Road).

[Sripatum University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sripatum_University) is a private university adjacent to 11th Royal Infantry Regiment.

[Saint John's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_John's_Group_of_Schools_and_University) and [Saint John's International School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_John's_International_School_\(Thailand\)) are also in Chatuchak District.

[Chandrakasem Rajabhat University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandrakasem_Rajabhat_University) is one of Thailand’s [Rajabhat Universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajabhat_University), located on Ratchadaphisek Road near the Criminal Court of Thailand.

## Transport

Five [metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_Rapid_Transit_Master_Plan_in_Bangkok_Metropolitan_Region) lines run through the district: the [MRT Blue Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRT_Blue_Line), [BTS Sukhumvit Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhumvit_Line), [SRT Dark Red Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SRT_Dark_Red_Line), [SRT Light Red Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SRT_Light_Red_Line), and [MRT Yellow Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_Line_\(Bangkok\)).

The MRT Blue Line crosses the district with four stations: [Kamphaeng Phet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamphaeng_Phet_Station), [Chatuchak Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatuchak_Park_MRT_Station), [Phahon Yothin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phahon_Yothin_MRT_Station), and [Lat Phrao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lat_Phrao_MRT_Station); [Bang Sue MRT Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Sue_MRT_Station) sits at the boundary between Chatuchak and Bang Sue districts.

The BTS Sukhumvit Line serves the district with the following stations: [Mo Chit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mo_Chit_BTS_station), [Ha Yaek Lat Phrao station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ha_Yaek_Lat_Phrao_BTS_station), [Phahon Yothin 24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phahon_Yothin_24_BTS_station), [Ratchayothin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratchayothin_BTS_station), [Sena Nikhom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sena_Nikhom_BTS_station), [Kasetsart University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasetsart_University_BTS_station), [Royal Forest Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Forest_Department_BTS_station) and [Bang Bua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Bua_BTS_station).

The SRT Dark Red Line also passes through the area with four stations: [Bang Sue (Krung Thep Aphiwat)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krung_Thep_Aphiwat_Central_Terminal), [Chatuchak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatuchak_station), [Wat Samian Nari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wat_Samian_Nari_station) and [Bang Khen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Khen_station).

Chatuchak district marks the northern terminus of the MRT Yellow Line, with its final stop at [Lat Phrao MRT Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lat_Phrao_MRT_Station).&#x20;

The area is served by [Bang Sue Junction railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Sue_Junction_railway_station) under the [State Railway of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Railway_of_Thailand) (SRT), where the [Southern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Line_\(Thailand\)), [Northern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Line_\(Thailand\)), and [Northeastern Lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_Line_\(Thailand\)) pass through.

[Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krung_Thep_Aphiwat_Central_Terminal), located just behind Chatuchak Weekend Market, officially replaced Bang Sue 1 railway station after its completion on November 29, 2021. It now functions as Thailand’s main railway hub, replacing [Bangkok railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok_railway_station) (Hua Lamphong).

The [Northern Bus Terminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Bus_Terminal_\(Bangkok\)) (commonly called "Mo Chit Mai" or "Mo Chit II", หมอชิตใหม่ or หมอชิต 2) is also located in this district, providing bus services to the northern and northeastern provinces.

Lat Phrao Intersection, also known as [Lat Phrao Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lat_Phrao_Square), is one of the most important junctions in both the district and Bangkok. It connects five routes: Phahonyothin Road, Vibhavadi Rangsit Road, [Lat Phrao Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lat_Phrao_Road), and [Don Mueang Tollway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Mueang_Tollway); and is considered the starting point of Lat Phrao Road.

## District council

The district council for Chatuchak has eight members, who serve four-year terms. [Elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Thailand_local_elections) were last held on 30 April 2006. The results were:

- [Thai Rak Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_Rak_Thai) Party, eight seats.

## Economy

[Thai Airways International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_Airways_International) and [Bangkok Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok_Airways) have their head offices in Chatuchak.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

[TMBThanachart Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TMBThanachart_Bank) and [Siam Commercial Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siam_Commercial_Bank) (SCB) with [Bank for Agriculture and Agricultural Co-operatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_for_Agriculture_and_Agricultural_Co-operatives) (BAAC) have their head offices in the district as well. The head office building of SCB is known as [SCB Park Plaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCB_Park_Plaza).

## Organisations

The [International Civil Aviation Organization](14985.md) (ICAO) has its Asia and Pacific (APAC) Office in Chatuchak district.[^cite_note-6]
