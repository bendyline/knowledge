**Khartoum North**, or **Khartoum Bahri** or simply **Bahri** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الخرطوم بحري, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *al-Kharṭūm Baḥrī*), is a city in [Khartoum State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khartoum_\(state\)), lying to the north of [Khartoum city](17320.md), the capital of [Sudan](27421.md). It has a population of 1,012,211 people, making it the third-largest city proper in Sudan, behind the neighbouring cities of [Omdurman](100414.md) and Khartoum.&#x20;

## Geography

It is located on the north bank of the [Blue Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Nile), and the east bank of the River Nile, near the confluence of the Blue Nile with the [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Nile). Until 2023, the [Shambat Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shambat_Bridge) connected it with [Omdurman](100414.md) to its west. Another major bridge links it with [Khartoum](17320.md) to the south.

It was part of a three-city agglomeration (with [Khartoum](17320.md) proper and [Omdurman](100414.md)) with a combined population of 4,272,728 in 2008.

## History

The original settlement was the largest in the area of the Nile confluence before the Egyptians established [Khartoum](17320.md) as their military garrison and administrative centre in the 1820s.[^cite_note-EBK-2] It was eclipsed by Egyptian Khartoum, its [Mahdist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahdist_Sudan) replacement [Omdurman](100414.md), and the [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Egyptian_Sudan) refounding of Khartoum following their reconquest of the country in 1898. However, Khartoum North began to grow again as the southern terminus of the [Sudan Military Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_Military_Railroad), completed in 1899.[^cite_note-EBK-2] The Blue Nile was bridged in 1910, and the line extended to [Sennar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sennar), but Khartoum North continued to serve as the central railroad station and yard.

On August 20, 1998, the [Al-Shifa pharmaceutical factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Shifa_pharmaceutical_factory) was destroyed by a [cruise missile](6590.md) after the United States accused the factory of making [VX (nerve agent)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VX_\(nerve_agent\)) for [al-Qaeda](1921.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

The city was captured by the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) in 2023 during the opening stages of the [Sudanese civil war (2023–present)](73556699.md) before being retaken by the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) in February 2025 as part of the [2024–2025 Bahri offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%932025_Bahri_offensive).

## Economy

The area is the industrial centre of the region and the country. It contains dockyards, marine and rail workshops, and sawmills. Khartoum North trades in cotton, grains, fruit, and livestock; industries include tanning, brewing, brickmaking, textile weaving, and food processing. After 2000, [chemical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical) plants supplying household products to the rest of the country were built there.&#x20;

A wealthy [suburb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suburb) emerged towards the eastern part of the neighbourhood, along the [Blue Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Nile).
