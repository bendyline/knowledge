# List of members of the Philippine House of Representatives expelled, removed, or suspended

&#x20;The following is a list of members of the [Philippine House of Representatives](293605.md) who were expelled, censured, removed,[^cite_note-1] and suspended.

## Expelled representatives

The House of Representatives can remove an erring member through a two-thirds majority vote according to Section 143 of its rules. [Arnolfo Teves Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnolfo_Teves_Jr.) is the first representative to have been removed through this manner. On June 2, 2026, the House also ousted [Kiko Barzaga](80329195.md). [^cite_note-historic-first-2]

| Date | Portrait | Representative | Expulsion vote[^cite_note-3] | Notes |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| February 1, 1908 |  |  | **[Dominador Gómez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominador_G%C3%B3mez)** ([Nacionalista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nacionalista_Party), [Manila's 1st district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila's_1st_congressional_district)) | **40**–35[^cite_note-Ingles2008p37-4] | Legal controversies including a contested citizenship status and past labor-related sedition charges, heavily fueled by a political power struggle with the [Philippine Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Assembly) leadership. At the time, only a majority of members were needed to expel a member. |
| August 16, 2023 |  |  | **[Arnolfo Teves Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnolfo_Teves_Jr.)** ([NPC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalist_People's_Coalition), [Negros Oriental's 3rd district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negros_Oriental's_3rd_congressional_district)) | **265**–0–3[^cite_note-5] | Abandonment of his role as representative through persistent absence and seeking political asylum in [East Timor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Timor); designation as terrorist by the government; posting of a video of himself dancing in a tank top and boxer shorts in social media.[^cite_note-historic-first-2] Teves was implicated in the [Pamplona massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamplona_massacre) which included the assassination of [Negros Oriental](261980.md) governor [Roel Degamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roel_Degamo). |
| June 2, 2026 |  |  | **[Kiko Barzaga](80329195.md)** ([PDP–Laban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PDP%E2%80%93Laban), [Cavite's 4th district](63851522.md)) | **265**–14–8[^cite_note-6] | Expelled due to double suspensions, and for disorderly conduct. Many ethics complaint were filed against him. He was suspended two times, so he was subjected to expulsion. |

## Censured representatives

The House of Representatives can censure or reprimand an erring member through a majority vote through Section 143 of its rules. [Pantaleon Alvarez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantaleon_Alvarez) is the sole representative to have been censured through this manner.[^cite_note-7]

| Date | Portrait | Representative | Censure vote[^cite_note-8] | Notes |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| May 22, 2024 |  |  | **[Pantaleon Alvarez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantaleon_Alvarez)** ([Reporma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partido_para_sa_Demokratikong_Reporma), [Davao del Norte’s 1st district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davao_del_Norte's_1st_congressional_district)) | **186**–5-7 | For alleged seditious remarks in calling on the [Armed Forces of the Philippines](23448.md) and the [Philippine National Police](1313055.md) to withdraw their support for President [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md). |
