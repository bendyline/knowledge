**Huánuco** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˈwanuko\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish); [Quechua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechua_language): *Wanuku*) is a [department of Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Peru).[^cite_note-1] It is bordered by the [La Libertad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Libertad_Region), [San Martín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Mart%C3%ADn_Region), [Loreto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loreto_Region) and [Ucayali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ucayali_Region) departments in the north, Ucayali to the east, [Pasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasco_Region) to the south and [Lima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lima_Region) and [Ancash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancash_Region) to the west. It is administered by a [regional government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Government_of_Hu%C3%A1nuco). Its capital is the city of [Huánuco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hu%C3%A1nuco).

Huánuco has a rugged topography, comprising parts of the [Sierra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_\(Peru\)) and the High Jungle (mountain rim) regions. Being equidistant from the north and the south of the country, it has the privilege of having a mild weather with an average annual temperature of 20 °C (68 °F).

This region is important for its geographical location, history, and for the richness of its land, where the presence of man goes back to ancient times. *El Hombre de Lauricocha* (Man of [Lauricocha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauricocha_Culture)) is among the most distinctive examples, dating from 10,000 BC, as well as Kotosh, where vestiges of the oldest settlement in the [Americas](29833.md) (4200 BC) took place.

## History

Several ethnic groups inhabited this region. However, after a severe resistance, they started to incorporate as part of the [Inca Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inca_Empire). Huánuco then became part of the Cusco-Cajamarca-Cusco route.

In the beginning of the 19th century, during the emancipation process, Huánuco was one of the first cities to promote Peru's independence. Moreover, a first oath took place in this city on December 15, 1820, after several uprisings in Huamalíes, Huallanca and Ambo.

### Languages

According to the [2007 Peru Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Peru_Census), the language learned first by most of the residents was [Spanish](26825.md) (70.92%) followed by [Quechua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechua_language) (28.56%).
