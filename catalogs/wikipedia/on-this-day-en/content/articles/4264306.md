The **Venezuela national baseball team** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Selección de béisbol de Venezuela*[^cite_note-2] or *Selección de Venezuela*)[^cite_note-3] also known as **"Arepa Power"**[^cite_note-4] is the national [baseball](3850.md) team of [Venezuela](32374.md).[^cite_note-5] It is currently [ranked fifth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WBSC_World_Rankings) in the world by the [World Baseball Softball Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Baseball_Softball_Confederation).[^cite_note-WBSC_World_Rankings-1]

Venezuela has competed in every edition of the [World Baseball Classic](1877416.md) since 2006, with its best result before 2026 being a semifinal appearance in [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_World_Baseball_Classic). It won its first major international title in the [1941 Amateur World Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1941_Amateur_World_Series), defeating a heavily favored [Team Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuba_national_baseball_team), and won two more world championships in the 1940s. It has also historically dominated [baseball at the Bolivarian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball_at_the_Bolivarian_Games). In 2026, it qualified for the [Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball_at_the_Summer_Olympics) for the first time via its performance in the World Baseball Classic.

Venezuela competed against [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_national_baseball_team), [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_national_baseball_team), [Dominican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Republic_national_baseball_team), and [Nicaragua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicaragua_national_baseball_team) in the [2026 World Baseball Classic](73255749.md) from March 6–11, 2026, at [LoanDepot Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LoanDepot_Park) in Miami, Florida. After advancing from the group stage, Team Venezuela was undefeated in the knockout stage, facing [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_national_baseball_team), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_national_baseball_team), and finally [United States](4119270.md), notching their first World Baseball Classic win, 3–2.&#x20;

## History

Venezuela debuted in international competition at the [1938 Central American and Caribbean Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball_at_the_1938_Central_American_and_Caribbean_Games) in Panama City, finishing sixth. The country first participated in what was then the top level of international baseball competition, the [Amateur World Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amateur_World_Series) (later to be known as the [Baseball World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball_World_Cup)), in [1940](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1940_Amateur_World_Series).



The "Heroes of '41"

Venezuela won its first international baseball title the following year, defeating defending champions [Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuba_national_baseball_team) at the [1941 Amateur World Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1941_Amateur_World_Series) in Havana. The championship team, with illustrious names from the country's amateur circuits like [Héctor Benítez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9ctor_Ben%C3%ADtez), [Daniel "Chino" Canónico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Can%C3%B3nico), and [José Antonio Casanova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Antonio_Casanova), was hailed as "*Los Heroes del '41*" ("Heroes of '41"). The victory of the 1941 AWS is cited as the catalyst for [baseball's exploding popularity in Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball_in_Venezuela),[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Venezuelan President [Isaías Medina Angarita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isa%C3%ADas_Medina_Angarita) declared October 22, the day of the championship game, a national holiday.

### World Baseball Classic era

#### 2009-23

Venezuela competed in the [2009 World Baseball Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_World_Baseball_Classic), playing its opening games in Pool C, in [Toronto, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto,_Ontario). The Venezuelans secured advancement out of Pool C with a pair of resounding wins over [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_national_baseball_team). Along the way, Venezuela also lost a 15–6 slugfest against the [United States](4119270.md), but won twice against the same team.[^cite_note-8]

In the [2013 World Baseball Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_World_Baseball_Classic), Venezuela was eliminated by [Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico_national_baseball_team), who went on to eliminate Italy, the United States and two-time defending champion Japan. Their placing as the third team in [Pool C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_World_Baseball_Classic_Pool_C) after defeating newcomers [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_national_baseball_team) was sufficient to secure direct qualification to the [2017 World Baseball Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_World_Baseball_Classic).

The team competed against [Team Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_national_baseball_team), [Team Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico_national_baseball_team), [Team Dominican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Republic_national_baseball_team), and [Team Nicaragua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicaragua_national_baseball_team) in the [2023 World Baseball Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_World_Baseball_Classic) in Miami, Florida, coming in first in the pool, but losing to the U.S. in the next round.[^cite_note-auto1as-9]

#### 2026

Team Venezuela competed against [Team Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_national_baseball_team), [Team Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_national_baseball_team), [Team Dominican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Republic_national_baseball_team), and [Team Nicaragua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicaragua_national_baseball_team) in pool play of the [2026 World Baseball Classic](73255749.md). They played from March 6–17, 2026, at [LoanDepot Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LoanDepot_Park) in Miami, Florida, in [Pool D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_World_Baseball_Classic_Pool_D).[^cite_note-10]

Houston Astros [bench coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bench_coach) [Omar López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_L%C3%B3pez_\(baseball\)) managed Team Venezuela at the 2026 World Baseball Classic.[^cite_note-aut-11]

After an upset victory against [Team Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_national_baseball_team) in the quarterfinals,[^cite_note-12] a victory against the insurgent [Team Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_national_baseball_team) in the semifinals,[^cite_note-13] the Venezuela national team won their first World Baseball Classic [final](82034770.md) against [Team USA](4119270.md), 3–2.[^cite_note-14]

### Olympics era

As one of the Latin American baseball powerhouses, Venezuela remarkably has not competed in the Olympics tournament which began from [1992 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Summer_Olympics) in [Barcelona](4443.md), unlike more famous teams such as [Dominican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Republic_national_baseball_team) and [Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico_national_baseball_team).[^cite_note-15]

#### 2028

The [2026 World Baseball Classic](73255749.md) is the first tournament in any sport to have [2028 Summer Olympics](12695048.md) qualification quota spots directly available, in this case for the top-two-performing Americas teams excluding Olympic hosts the [United States](4119270.md), who have already qualified automatically.[^cite_note-16]

On 14 March 2026, after securing a vital victory against Japan in the quarterfinals, Venezuela qualified for the historically first time in the six-team baseball tournament for [LA28](12695048.md).[^cite_note-17]

## Tournament record

### World Baseball Classic

In June 2005, [Major League Baseball](38776.md) announced the formation of the [World Baseball Classic](1877416.md), a sixteen nation international competition to be held in March 2006 for the first time. The team Venezuela sent to the [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_World_Baseball_Classic) and [2009 World Baseball Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_World_Baseball_Classic) included Venezuelan players from [Major League Baseball](38776.md). A player is eligible to participate on a WBC national team if:

- The player is a citizen of the nation the team represents.

- The player is qualified for citizenship or to hold a passport under the laws of a nation represented by a team, but has not been granted citizenship or been issued a passport, then the player may be made eligible by WBCI upon petition by the player or team.

- The player is a permanent legal resident of the nation or territory the team represents.

- The player was born in the nation or territory the team represents.

- The player has one parent who is, or if deceased was, a citizen of the nation the team represents.

- The player has one parent who was born in the nation or territory the team represents.
