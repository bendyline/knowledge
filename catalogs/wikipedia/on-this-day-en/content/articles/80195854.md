# FIFA World Cup 2026 Task Force

&#x20; The **FIFA World Cup 2026 Task Force** was a [United States government](195149.md) [task force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Task_force) created to coordinate federal efforts in support of the [2025 FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_FIFA_Club_World_Cup) and the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).

## Background

The 2026 World Cup drew millions of tourists to the United States, Canada, and Mexico. The tournament featured 48 teams playing 104 matches, with 78 in the United States and 13 each in Canada and Mexico.[^cite_note-\:0-1] The task force was announced on March 7, 2025 to oversee the logistics and security for the whole event over three countries.[^cite_note-2]

## Structure

The task force was chaired by US President [Donald Trump](4848272.md), with Vice President [JD Vance](53396477.md) serving as its Vice Chair.[^cite_note-3] In May 2025, Trump announced that [Andrew Giuliani](10194394.md), the son of former New York City Mayor [Rudy Giuliani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudy_Giuliani), would lead the task force, with [Carlos Cordeiro](56549173.md), a senior advisor to [FIFA](11049.md), also serving on the task force.[^cite_note-4]

A [congressional](31756.md) task force, initially led by [Michael McCaul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_McCaul) and [Mark Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Green_\(Tennessee_politician\)), worked in parallel to support the White House Task Force through legislative means and oversight.[^cite_note-McCaul-5][^cite_note-6] The task force collaborated with multiple federal agencies, such as the [Department of Homeland Security](58236.md) (DHS), the [Federal Emergency Management Agency](73797.md) (FEMA), the [Transportation Security Administration](172933.md) (TSA), and the [Department of State](31975.md).[^cite_note-McCaul-5]

## Events

The first meeting of the task force was held in public on May 6, 2025, where Trump and [FIFA President](11052.md) [Gianni Infantino](9286117.md) stated that the World Cups would be the "biggest, safest, and most extraordinary soccer tournament in history while contributing almost \$50 billion in economic output. While Infantino welcomed all visitors stating that "America will welcome the world," numerous comments were made by members such as Vance, that highlighted the [Trump administration's](78298108.md) [aggressive deportation policies](78314324.md). Concerns were also raised about the ability for the administration to process and approve the large number of travel visas for foreign visitors and fans, which were brushed off by Homeland Secretary [Kristi Noem](27663933.md).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

In October 2025, it was announced that the Task Force was being designated \$500 million USD by the Trump administration to support state and local governments to develop an anti-drone security strategy ahead of the tournaments. While currently only federal agencies can disrupt or intercept drones, the laws were reviewed by the administration and Justice Department.[^cite_note-9]

## Response

[FIFA President](11052.md) [Gianni Infantino](9286117.md) expressed support for the task force, and said that it would cause each visitor to feel safe, happy, and that they were "creating something special".[^cite_note-\:0-1]
