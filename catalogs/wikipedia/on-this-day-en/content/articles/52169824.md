The **Régional 1**, formerly known as the **Division d'Honneur**, is the generic name for the sixth-tier [football](10568.md) league competitions run by each of the 13 [regional leagues](3480022.md#Regional_Leagues) in France. Clubs may be promoted from these leagues into the [Championnat National 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Championnat_National_3), subject to certain criteria. The name is also used for the highest level of football in the [French overseas departments and regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_departments_and_regions_of_France) of [Guadeloupe](12343.md), [French Guiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Guiana), [Martinique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martinique), [Mayotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayotte) and [Réunion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A9union).

Each region administers its own league under its own rules and may give it a distinct name. Some clubs are [semi-professional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-professional), and qualifying [reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reserve_team) and [academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_system) teams of clubs competing at higher levels are also eligible to enter.
