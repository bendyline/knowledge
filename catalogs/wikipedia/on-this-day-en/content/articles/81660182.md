# 2026 FIFA World Cup Group B

&#x20; Group B of the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) took place from June 12 to 24, 2026.[^cite_note-match_schedule-1] The group consisted of [Canada](723688.md) (co-host), [Bosnia and Herzegovina](1008974.md), [Qatar](1111350.md), and [Switzerland](681862.md). This also marked the return of Bosnia and Herzegovina to the World Cup finals, with the Bosnians making their first appearance since [2014](656933.md).

Switzerland began their World Cup campaign with a draw, and would go on to top the group after winning their next two group games. Canada finished as runners-up, thanks to their first ever World Cup win against Qatar, and both teams advanced to the [round of 32](81667005.md).

Bosnia and Herzegovina finished in third based on goal difference, and their performance secured them as one of the [eight best third-place teams](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams),[^cite_note-regulations-2] allowing them to progress to the round of 32. Qatar finished fourth after losing their second and third group games, which resulted in their elimination.

### Canada vs Bosnia and Herzegovina

The two teams had never met before. This was the first senior men's World Cup match played in Canada.[^cite_note-11]

Bosnia took the lead in the 21st minute when [Jovo Lukić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jovo_Luki%C4%87) headed into the net from close range after [Sead Kolašinac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sead_Kola%C5%A1inac) had flicked on [Ivan Bašić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Ba%C5%A1i%C4%87)'s corner from the right. In the 78th minute it was 1–1 when substitute [Cyle Larin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyle_Larin) scored with a deflected shot to the right of the net.

With a 1–1 draw, this marked the first time that Canada won a point at a World Cup, after they had been beaten in all three games in 1986 and then again in 2022.[^cite_note-12]

### Qatar vs Switzerland

The teams previously met in a 2018 friendly, which ended in a 1–0 win for Qatar.[^cite_note-16]

In the 17th minute, Switzerland were awarded a penalty when [Remo Freuler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remo_Freuler) was fouled by the diving Qatar goalkeeper [Mahmud Abunada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmud_Abunada). Television replays appeared to show Freuler had strayed marginally offside in the build-up but the penalty was awarded and was scored by [Breel Embolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breel_Embolo) with a low finish to the left. In the 94th minute, Qatar scored an equalizer when Switzerland defender [Miro Muheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miro_Muheim) under pressure from [Boualem Khoukhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boualem_Khoukhi), headed a Homam Ahmed cross from the left past his own goalkeeper to the right of the net with the match finishing 1–1.[^cite_note-17]

This was the first time Qatar earned a point at the World Cup, after losing three consecutive matches as the host nation in 2022.[^cite_note-18]

### Canada vs Qatar



[Jonathan David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_David) scoring one of his three goals, becoming the second North American player to score a World Cup [hat-trick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIFA_World_Cup_hat-tricks).

The teams previously faced each other once in 2022, a friendly won 2–0 by Canada.[^cite_note-24]

This was the first World Cup match won by Canada in its history, and the biggest winning margin by a nation outside of [UEFA](32332.md) or [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL) in World Cup history.

Additionally, [Jonathan David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_David) became the first North American player to score a World Cup [hat-trick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIFA_World_Cup_hat-tricks) since [Bert Patenaude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bert_Patenaude) did so for the [United States](582488.md) against [Paraguay](804025.md) in [1930](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930_FIFA_World_Cup), and the first player to score a World Cup hat-trick on home soil since [Geoff Hurst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoff_Hurst) did so for [England](9904.md) against West Germany in the [1966 final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966_FIFA_World_Cup_Final).[^cite_note-25] He also became the second player to score a hat-trick in the tournament, after [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) did so in [Argentina's opening match against Algeria](81660202.md#Argentina_vs_Algeria).[^cite_note-26]

### Switzerland vs Canada



Pre-match ceremony between [Switzerland](681862.md) and [Canada](723688.md).

The teams' only meeting took place in 2002, a friendly won 3–1 by Canada.[^cite_note-29]

With this fixture, co-hosts Canada finished as Group B runners-up behind Switzerland, who secured the top spot after handing the Canadians their only defeat. A day later, fellow co-hosts the United States also suffered a setback, [losing 3–2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_D#Turkey_vs_United_States) to already-eliminated Turkey in Group D in Los Angeles. This leaves Mexico as the only co-host to remain undefeated, having won all of their group stage matches.

The loss forced Canada to play against [Group A](81660180.md) runners-up [South Africa](1018598.md) outside of its borders for the [round of 32](81667041.md#South_Africa_vs_Canada) in Los Angeles, as failing to top the group cost them a guaranteed spot on [home soil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_advantage), a match which was also played in Vancouver.

## Discipline

The team conduct ("fair play") score would have been used as a tiebreaker if the head-to-head and overall records of teams were tied. It would also be used as a tiebreaker for the [third-place ranking](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams) between groups if the overall records of teams were tied. The score was calculated based on yellow and red cards received by players and team officials in all group matches as follows:[^cite_note-regulations-2]

- yellow card: −1 point;

- indirect red card (second yellow card): −3 points;

- direct red card: −4 points;

- yellow card and direct red card: −5 points;

Only one of the above deductions could be applied to a player or team official in a single match.
