**Jama Canton** is a [canton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantons_of_Ecuador) of [Ecuador](9334.md), located in the [Manabí Province](728376.md). Its capital is the town of [Jama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jama,_Ecuador). Its population at the 2001 census was 20,230.[^cite_note-1]

## Demographics

Ethnic groups as of the [Ecuadorian census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_census) of 2010:[^cite_note-2]

- [Mestizo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestizo) 72.5%

- [Montubio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montubio) 21.0%

- [Afro-Ecuadorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Ecuadorian) 4.5%

- [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Latin_American) 1.7%

- [Indigenous](18429324.md) 0.2%

- Other 0.1%
