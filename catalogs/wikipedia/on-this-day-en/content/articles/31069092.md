**Al-Ba'aj District** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بعاج) is a district in [Nineveh Governorate](200568.md), [Iraq](7515928.md). Its administrative center is the city of [Al-Ba'aj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ba'aj). Other settlements include Qaryat al-Sakar and Al-Jughaifi. The district is predominantly populated by [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni) [Arabs](2185.md).
