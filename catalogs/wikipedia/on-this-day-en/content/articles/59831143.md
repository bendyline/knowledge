The **State Minister of Artsakh** was a political post in the government of [Republic of Artsakh](1000530.md). The position was introduced after constitutional amendments, which were approved in a [constitutional referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Nagorno-Karabakh_constitutional_referendum) in 2017, and after the [Prime Minister of Artsakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Artsakh) post was abolished.

Following the [Azerbaijani offensive](74857564.md) on 19 September 2023, Artsakh agreed to dissolve itself by 1 January 2024,[^cite_note-Reuters-2023-09-28-1] however instead of dissolving, they established a [government-in-exile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government-in-exile) in [Yerevan](34352.md), Armenia.[^cite_note-2] The [Prime Minister of Armenia](483914.md), [Nikol Pashinyan](17897961.md), has since severely opposed the government-in-exile's existence in Armenia.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
