**Attica** ([/ˈætɪkə/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [*AT-ih-kə*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key); [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Περιφέρεια Αττικής, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Greek): *Periféria Attikís*, [\[periˈferi.a atiˈcis\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Greek)) is an [administrative region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_regions_of_Greece) of [Greece](12108.md) that encompasses the entire [Athens metropolitan area](72650858.md), the core city of which is the country's [capital](181337.md) and [largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_Greece), [Athens](1216.md). The region is coextensive with the former [Attica Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attica_Prefecture) of [Central Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Greece_\(geographic_region\)) and covers a greater area than the [historical region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_region) of [Attica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attica).

## Overview

Located on the eastern edge of [Central Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Greece_\(geographic_region\)), Attica covers about 3,808 square kilometres. In addition to Athens, it contains within its area the cities of [Elefsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elefsina), [Megara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megara), [Laurium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurium), and [Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathon,_Greece), as well as a small part of the [Peloponnese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peloponnese) peninsula and the islands of [Salamis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salamis_Island), [Aegina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aegina), [Angistri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angistri), [Poros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poros), [Hydra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydra,_Saronic_Islands), [Spetses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spetses), [Kythira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kythira), and [Antikythera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antikythera). About 3,790,000 people live in the region, of whom more than 95% are inhabitants of the [Athens metropolitan area](72650858.md). In 2019, Attica had the HDI of 0.912, the highest in Greece.

## Administration



Head office of the Attica region

The region was established in the 1987 administrative reform, and until 2010 it comprised the 4 [prefectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_of_Greece) of [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_Prefecture), [East Attica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Attica), [Piraeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus_Prefecture) and [West Attica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Attica).

With the 2010 [Kallikratis plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kallikratis_plan), the region's powers and authority were completely redefined and extended. Since 1 January 2011, the region represents the second-level local administration. While being supervised by the [Decentralized Administration of Attica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decentralized_Administration_of_Attica), it is now an independent [self-governing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-governance) body with powers and a budget comparable to the former [prefectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_of_Greece).

The region is subdivided into eight subordinate [regional units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_units_of_Greece):[^cite_note-Kallikratis-4]

- [Central Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Athens_\(regional_unit\))

## Climate

Due to its large area Attica has a variety of climates. The largest part of the peninsula has a hot-summer [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Csa*). Some areas of the [Athens Riviera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_Riviera), areas of the [Thriasio Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thriasio_Plain), [Aigina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aigina), [Hydra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydra_\(island\)) and some areas in west Attica have a [hot semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_semi-arid_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *BSh*). According to the meteorological stations of the [National Observatory of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Observatory_of_Athens) and the [Hellenic National Meteorological Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hellenic_National_Meteorological_Service) areas in the north have wetter and colder winters. [Parnitha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnitha) mountain has a January average high of around 4 °C and an average low below 0 °C.[^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-6][^cite_note-Dionysos-7][^cite_note-Parnitha_2-8] Areas in the south mainland have very mild winters with [Nea Smyrni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nea_Smyrni) recording a January average high close to 15 °C and an average low of around 9 °C.[^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-6][^cite_note-Nea_Smyrni-9] The islands of the Attica region have even milder winters with [Hydra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydra_\(island\)) recording a January average low of over 10 °C and falling in 11a [hardiness zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardiness_zone).[^cite_note-meteo.gr-10][^cite_note-11]

The highest July and August temperatures are recorded in the interior of the Athens Basin and also in west areas with [Nea Filadelfeia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nea_Filadelfeia), [Harokopio University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harokopio_University) and [Salamina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salamis_Island) registering average summer highs over 35 °C.[^cite_note-Meteoclub-12][^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-6][^cite_note-Salamina-13] Due to land breezes, summer nights can be particularly hot in the south and especially around [Piraeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus) which records an average August low of around 27 °C.[^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-6][^cite_note-Piraeus-14] The highest average annual temperatures are recorded in [Nea Smyrni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nea_Smyrni) registering around 20.5 °C, while the lowest is recorded in [Parnitha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnitha) mountain with less than 11.0 °C.[^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-6][^cite_note-Parnitha_2-8][^cite_note-15] [Neos Kosmos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neos_Kosmos) is the warmest area of [Downtown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown) [Athens](1216.md) with an average annual temperature of 20.2 °C and a [hot semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_semi-arid_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *BSh*) closely bordering a [hot-summer mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot-summer_mediterranean) (*Csa*) climate. During July 2024 minimum temperatures remained over 30 °C (86 °F) for 12 consecutive days in metropolitan [Athens](1216.md), breaking all known records for any area in the country.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

[Anavysos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anavysos) in the [Athens Riviera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_Riviera) records the lowest average annual precipitation in mainland [Greece](12108.md) with around 295 mm, while the highest average annual precipitation is found in [Avlonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avlonas,_Attica) with around 930 mm.[^cite_note-Anavysos-18] [Lavrio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavrio) is the only area in mainland Attica that has never recorded an air frost according to the [National Observatory of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Observatory_of_Athens) station which operates since 2008.[^cite_note-Lavrio-19][^cite_note-Monthly_Bulletins-6]

The highest temperature ever recorded in Attica was 48.0 °C in [Elefsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elefsina) and [Tatoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatoi) on July 10, 1977, which according to the [World Meteorological Organization](33584.md) was also the highest official temperature ever recorded in [Europe](9239.md) until 2021.[^cite_note-wmo.asu.edu-20] The lowest temperature ever recorded was −12.0 °C in [Parnitha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnitha) mountain on January 9, 2017.[^cite_note-Parnitha_4-21] The highest minimum temperature in mainland Attica was 32.9 °C (91 °F) and it was recorded on July 26, 2023, in [Hellinikon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hellinikon).[^cite_note-22] The highest minimum temperature in the Attica region was 37.0 °C (99 °F) in [Kythira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kythira).[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] Furthermore, Attica has experienced temperatures of [47.5 °C and over in four different locations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_extreme_temperatures_in_Greece).

## Economy

The [Gross domestic product](12594.md) (GDP) of the region was 87.4 billion € in 2018, accounting for about 47% of the Greek economic output. GDP per capita adjusted for purchasing power was €28,000 or 93% of the EU27 average in the same year. The GDP per employee was 99% of the EU average. Attica is the region in Greece with the highest GDP per capita.[^cite_note-33] Despite that, the unemployment rate stood at 21.6% in 2017.[^cite_note-34]
