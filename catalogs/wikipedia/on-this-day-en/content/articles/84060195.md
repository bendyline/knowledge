# Swimming at the 2026 Asian Games

The **[swimming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_\(sport\))** competitions at the **[2026 Asian Games](51790406.md)** was held between 20 and 25 September 2026 at the [Tokyo Aquatics Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Aquatics_Centre).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-schedule-2][^cite_note-events-3]

[China](83783500.md) topped the swimming medal table winning a record 30 gold medals, matching its gold-medal tally from the [2022 Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Asian_Games). [Japan](81296528.md) finished second with seven gold medals, improving on its third-place finish and medal tally from the previous edition.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-\:1-5]

The competition produced one world record, seven Asian records and 24 Asian Games records. [Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_at_the_2026_Asian_Games) and [Kyrgyzstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyzstan_at_the_2026_Asian_Games) won their first Asian Games swimming medals.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-\:1-5]
