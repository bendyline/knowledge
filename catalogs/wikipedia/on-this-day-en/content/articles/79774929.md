The **2026 Spanish Grand Prix** (officially known as the **Formula 1 TAG Heuer Gran Premio de España 2026**) was a [Formula One](10854.md) motor race that was held on 13 September 2026, at the [Madring](80044756.md) in [Madrid](41188263.md), [Spain](26667.md), as the first running of the [Spanish Grand Prix](526356.md) at the recently-constructed Madring track. It was the fourteenth round of the [2026 Formula One World Championship](71681684.md). [Lando Norris](48978813.md) ([McLaren](20994.md)) took pole position for the race, but lost out to [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)). [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) ([Red Bull](1173193.md)) completed the podium alongside Norris.

## Background

The event was held at the [Madring](80044756.md) in [Madrid](41188263.md) for the first time in the circuit's history, and for the first time as a host for the [Spanish Grand Prix](526356.md), across the weekend of 11–13 September.[^cite_note-3] The most recent venue to hold the Spanish Grand Prix was the [Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_Barcelona-Catalunya) at [Montmeló](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montmel%C3%B3), Spain, which currently hosts the [Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona-Catalunya_Grand_Prix) on rotation with the [Belgian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgian_Grand_Prix). The Grand Prix was the fourteenth round of the [2026 Formula One World Championship](71681684.md) and the 56th running of the Spanish Grand Prix as a round of the Formula One World Championship.[^cite_note-4]

### Championship standings before the race

Going into the weekend, [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) led the [Drivers' Championship](3437295.md) with 267 points, 66 points ahead of teammate [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) in second and 76 ahead of [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) in third. [Mercedes](25098794.md), with 468 points, led the [Constructors' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Constructors'_Championship) from [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) and [McLaren](20994.md), who were second and third with 346 and 287 points, respectively.[^cite_note-5]

### Entrants

See also: [2026 Formula One World Championship § Entries](71681684.md#Entries)

The drivers and teams were the same as published in the season entry list, with the exception of [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) at [Red Bull](1173193.md), who injured his wrist leading into the [Dutch Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Dutch_Grand_Prix) and was again unable to compete. Like the previous two Grands Prix, he was again replaced by [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson). Red Bull reserve driver [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda) replaced Lawson at [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

### Tyre choices

Further information: [Formula One tyres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_tyres)

Tyre supplier [Pirelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirelli) selected its C2, C3, and C4 compounds—the middle three in its range—for the event, designating them as hard, medium, and soft, respectively.[^cite_note-8]

## Practice

Three free [practice sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_racing#Free_practice) were held for the event. The first free practice session was held on 11 September 2026, at 13\:30 local time ([UTC+2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+2)), and was topped by [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)) ahead of his teammate [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) and [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) ([Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari)). The second free practice session was held on the same day, at 17\:00 local time, and was topped by Antonelli ahead of Leclerc and his teammate [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton). A red flag was observed due to [Arvid Lindblad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvid_Lindblad) ([Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls)) hitting the wall at turn 13. The third free practice session was held on 12 September 2026, at 12\:30 local time, and was topped by Antonelli ahead of Leclerc and [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) ([McLaren](20994.md)). The session was red-flagged twice; the first came after Hamilton crashed into the wall at turn 22, while the second was observed in the closing stages due to [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman) ([Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team)) hitting the wall at turns 10.[^cite_note-Grand_Prix-1]

## Qualifying

[Qualifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualifying_\(Formula_One\)) was held on 12 September 2026, at 16\:00 local time ([UTC+2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+2)), and determined the starting grid order for the race.[^cite_note-Grand_Prix-1]

### Qualifying report

Q1, the opening part of qualifying, began with the [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One) drivers leading the field out of the pit lane. [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)) set the initial pace on soft tyres, while [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) ([Red Bull](1173193.md)) and the [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) drivers, [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) and [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc), used the medium compound. [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) (Red Bull) subsequently moved to the top, while Antonelli abandoned another attempt after locking up and narrowly avoiding the barrier. [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) (Mercedes) brushed the wall but improved on his final run to lead Antonelli, Lawson and Verstappen. [Alexander Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Albon) ([Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing)) secured the final place in Q2 (the second part of qualifying) by 0.005 seconds over team-mate [Carlos Sainz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.), who was eliminated alongside [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) ([Aston Martin](63473867.md)) and [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez) and [Valtteri Bottas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valtteri_Bottas) (both Cadillac). [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman) ([Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team)) did not participate because his car could not be repaired in time following his crash in final practice, while [Lance Stroll](42274711.md) (Aston Martin) was unable to run owing to a suspected water-pressure problem. Sainz subsequently received a three-place grid penalty for impeding Bottas.[^cite_note-F1_Spanish_qualifying_report-9][^cite_note-Motorsport_Spanish_qualifying_report-10][^cite_note-Sky_Sports_Spanish_qualifying_report-11]

Antonelli again established the early benchmark in Q2, before Verstappen moved ahead by 0.046 seconds. [Franco Colapinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Colapinto) ([Alpine](65240620.md)) abandoned his opening attempt after a slide through the banked La Monumental corner, leaving both Alpine drivers without a time until the closing minutes. Colapinto recovered to advance in eighth, while Verstappen finished fastest, 0.160 seconds ahead of Antonelli, followed by Hamilton, Leclerc, Lawson, Russell and [Lando Norris](48978813.md) ([McLaren](20994.md)). [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) (McLaren) and [Arvid Lindblad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvid_Lindblad) ([Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls)) took the final two places in Q3 (the final part of qualifying) with identical times of 1\:33.204. [Nico Hülkenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_H%C3%BClkenberg) ([Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_in_Formula_One)) was the fastest driver eliminated, missing the cutoff by 0.019 seconds; he was followed by team-mate [Gabriel Bortoleto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto), [Esteban Ocon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Ocon) (Haas), [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md) (Alpine), [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda) (Racing Bulls) and Albon. Gasly lost an earlier attempt to double yellow flags, while Tsunoda had a lap deleted for exceeding track limits.[^cite_note-F1_Spanish_qualifying_report-9][^cite_note-The_Race_Spanish_qualifying_report-12][^cite_note-F1_Spanish_qualifying_results-13]

Hamilton held provisional pole after the opening Q3 runs with a time of 1\:32.079, ahead of Antonelli and Russell; Leclerc was fourth, followed by Verstappen and the two McLarens, with Norris sixth. Antonelli moved to the top on his final attempt with a 1\:31.835, while Hamilton improved but was unable to reclaim first place. Norris then set a 1\:31.824 despite a slide at the final corner, beating Antonelli by 0.011 seconds. Verstappen improved to third, 0.140 seconds behind Norris, while Hamilton and Leclerc qualified fourth and fifth, separated by 0.006 seconds. Russell finished sixth, ahead of Piastri and Lawson, with Colapinto and Lindblad completing the top ten. Despite the crashes during practice, qualifying passed without a red-flag interruption. Norris's pole was his third of the season and the 19th Grand Prix pole of his career.[^cite_note-Sky_Sports_Spanish_qualifying_report-11][^cite_note-RacingNews365_Spanish_qualifying_report-14][^cite_note-F1_Spanish_qualifying_report-9][^cite_note-F1_Spanish_qualifying_results-13]

## Race

The race was held on 13 September 2026, at 15\:00 local time ([UTC+2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+2)), and was run for 57 laps.[^cite_note-Grand_Prix-1]

### Race report

#### Pre-race

[Lando Norris](48978813.md) ([McLaren](20994.md)) started from pole position, with [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)) alongside him and [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) ([Red Bull](1173193.md)) and [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) ([Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari)) on the second row. [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) (Ferrari) and [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) (Mercedes) occupied the third row.[^cite_note-ESP26-grid-21] [Carlos Sainz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.) ([Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing)) started 20th following a three-place penalty for impeding in qualifying. [Lance Stroll](42274711.md) ([Aston Martin](63473867.md)) was 21st after a power unit penalty, while [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman) ([Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team)) started from the pit lane following changes to his car and power unit components under [parc fermé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parc_ferm%C3%A9) conditions.[^cite_note-ESP26-f1-22]

The race began in hot, dry conditions, with the track temperature around 52 °C before the start. All three dry-weather tyre compounds were represented on the grid.[^cite_note-ESP26-live-23] Norris and Antonelli chose medium tyres, while Verstappen and Hamilton started on softs. Leclerc, Russell, [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) (McLaren) and [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) (Red Bull) selected hards, giving the leading teams differing options for the opening stint. [Nico Hülkenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_H%C3%BClkenberg) ([Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_in_Formula_One)) was the other soft-tyre starter. [Franco Colapinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Colapinto) ([Alpine](65240620.md)) and [Arvid Lindblad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvid_Lindblad) ([Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls)) joined the front-row starters on mediums, while most of the remaining field chose hards. Tyre graining and degradation were expected to influence the choice between one and two stops.[^cite_note-ESP26-f1-22]

#### Start and opening laps

Antonelli challenged Norris at the start but cut the opening chicane and returned the lead. He then attempted another move at turns 5 and 6, again leaving the track and conceding the position. Verstappen took advantage of the second incident to pass Antonelli at turn 7. Norris emerged from the opening exchanges ahead of Verstappen, Antonelli and Hamilton and began to pull away. Verstappen had also gone off the circuit during his opening-corner contest with Hamilton, however, leaving his position ahead of the Ferrari in question.[^cite_note-ESP26-sky-24]

Red Bull instructed Verstappen to restore the position to Hamilton to avoid a possible penalty. With Antonelli between them, this required Verstappen to surrender two places. He complied on lap 4, dropping from second to fourth despite disputing the instruction over the radio. Hamilton was briefly third but soon began losing pace because of braking problems. Verstappen recovered the place, and Hamilton continued to fall through the field before retiring, ending his run of finishes in every Grand Prix in the season.[^cite_note-ESP26-espn-25] Hamilton later described the brake pedal losing its normal response as he approached turn 5, followed by contact with a barrier and a further deterioration a few corners later. The failure forced him to abandon the race despite having qualified within two tenths of pole position. His retirement left Leclerc as Ferrari's sole remaining driver.[^cite_note-ESP26-rn365-26]

Piastri's race was also compromised immediately; contact with Russell damaged his front wing and cost him positions. Further back, Sainz and [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda) (Racing Bulls) made contact at turn 1. As the opening order settled, Colapinto ran ahead of Lawson, but the Red Bull driver passed the Alpine at turn 5 on lap 10 to take sixth.[^cite_note-ESP26-live-23]

Norris continued to extend his advantage over Antonelli, while Verstappen lost further ground behind the Mercedes. Leclerc and Russell followed, with Russell discussing a possible early stop to gain track position over the Ferrari. Antonelli had another excursion, and McLaren informed Norris that his rival was struggling more with his tyres. These developments allowed Norris to maintain control of the opening stint as the leading drivers considered their first stops.[^cite_note-ESP26-mw-27]

Sainz and [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) (Aston Martin) subsequently collided while contesting 17th place. Alonso's front wing was damaged, and Sainz received a five-second time penalty. The incident further complicated the home drivers' races, with both outside the points.[^cite_note-ESP26-motorsport-28]

#### Virtual safety car and first pit stops

Stroll stopped at turn 20 with a brake problem, prompting a [virtual safety car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_safety_car) period. Norris had already passed the pit entry when the intervention began, while Antonelli was still able to enter. Mercedes used the opportunity to change the Italian's tyres with less time lost relative to cars circulating at the reduced speed. Verstappen also stopped, leaving Norris dependent on the caution lasting until he reached the pits again.[^cite_note-ESP26-reaction-29] Antonelli, Verstappen and Russell made their first stops at the end of lap 14; Colapinto, Lindblad and Hülkenberg were among those who also stopped on that lap. Norris entered at the end of lap 15. Leclerc stayed out.[^cite_note-ESP26-pits-30]

The virtual safety car ended before Norris reached the pit entry, so he had to make his stop with his rivals back at racing speed. A slow tyre change then kept him stationary for approximately seven seconds, compounding the time lost through the timing of the caution. Before the intervention, he had held a lead of around six seconds over Antonelli; afterwards, he faced a recovery through traffic on a circuit with few overtaking opportunities.[^cite_note-ESP26-sky-24]

Norris rejoined fifth, behind Leclerc, Antonelli, Verstappen and Russell. Leclerc inherited the lead without stopping, while Antonelli headed the drivers who had already changed tyres. The outcome therefore remained dependent on the different strategies: Ferrari still had a stop to make, while Norris had lost the clear track he had enjoyed at the front. Russell was ahead of the McLaren but would subsequently make a second stop, leaving Antonelli better placed to benefit from the early intervention.[^cite_note-ESP26-gulf-31]

Antonelli and Verstappen had changed to hard tyres, whereas Russell had replaced his starting hards with mediums. Antonelli initially drew closer to Leclerc before the gap widened again. During the middle stages, he reported an unusual feeling through the steering, although Mercedes told him that its data showed no apparent problem. Leclerc continued on his original tyres, with Verstappen, Russell and Norris following the leading pair.[^cite_note-ESP26-newsgp-32]

#### Middle stages

Russell made his second stop at the end of lap 28, returning to hard tyres and releasing Norris into fourth.[^cite_note-ESP26-pits-30][^cite_note-ESP26-newsgp-32] Russell rejoined behind Lawson and began closing on the Red Bull. He regained fifth after Lawson ran deep at the chicane, but remained well behind the leading group. Russell questioned the decision to stop again, arguing over the radio that he could have kept Norris behind him. Meanwhile, Norris used the clear track to reduce his deficit to Verstappen. By the final ten laps, he was within a second of the Red Bull, bringing the McLaren back into contention for a podium position.[^cite_note-ESP26-espn-25]

[Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez) ([Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One)) retired during the middle stages with a suspected problem with the car's water system. His withdrawal followed those of Hamilton and Stroll, while Leclerc remained in the lead on his starting set of hard tyres.[^cite_note-ESP26-f1-22]

Piastri continued his opening stint until lap 42, while Lawson stayed out until lap 44. Their delayed stops contrasted with those of the drivers who had changed tyres during the early virtual safety car.[^cite_note-ESP26-pits-30] Piastri continued with the damaged front wing, and McLaren adjusted it during his stop rather than replacing it. He rejoined behind Lindblad and subsequently passed the Racing Bulls driver at turn 1.[^cite_note-ESP26-live-23]

McLaren's attempt to bring Piastri ahead of Lawson through an earlier stop was unsuccessful. Lawson retained the position when he pitted, leaving the Australian behind him. Piastri also became involved in the contest between [Gabriel Bortoleto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto) (Audi) and Colapinto, where the Alpine driver attempted a move at the turn 5 chicane. The close running produced further pressure for positions but did not allow Piastri to move ahead of Colapinto.[^cite_note-ESP26-mw-27]

Sainz was subsequently called into the pits to retire, completing an unsuccessful home race for the Williams driver. Leclerc remained at the head of the field, while Norris continued closing on Verstappen behind Antonelli.[^cite_note-ESP26-newsgp-32]

Ferrari extended Leclerc's opening stint in the hope that another safety car intervention would reduce the cost of his stop. Antonelli remained behind him, with the gap varying as he managed his tyres. No further caution arrived to assist Ferrari. As the race approached its closing stages, Verstappen and Norris joined the leading battle, but Leclerc still needed to change tyres. Ferrari eventually abandoned the attempt to gain from an interruption and brought him in.[^cite_note-ESP26-bbc-33]

#### Closing stages and finish

Bortoleto made his stop on lap 47, followed by Leclerc at the end of lap 48.[^cite_note-ESP26-pits-30] Leclerc fitted soft tyres and returned in fourth, ahead of Russell but behind the three drivers contesting victory. Antonelli now held the outright lead from Verstappen and Norris. His advantage was initially small, with the first three separated by less than two seconds, but he increased the gap once released into clear track. Verstappen was left defending second from Norris, whose recovery had brought him back to the Red Bull without providing a clear opportunity to pass.[^cite_note-ESP26-yahoo-34][^cite_note-ESP26-motorsport-28]

Bortoleto received a warning for moving under braking during the midfield contest. At the front, Antonelli reported brushing the wall at turn 7 during the closing laps but continued in the lead. Norris also lost time with a late lock-up while pursuing Verstappen.[^cite_note-ESP26-live-23]

On the penultimate lap, Verstappen encountered lapped traffic and cut the turn 5 chicane. Norris challenged him and argued that the Red Bull had retained an advantage by leaving the track. The incident was reviewed without further action, and Verstappen kept second. Despite remaining close in the final stages, Norris was unable to complete a pass.[^cite_note-ESP26-bbc-33]

[Pierre Gasly](40843850.md) (Alpine) had extended his original hard-tyre stint in the hope of a late neutralisation, but ultimately had to stop under green-flag conditions. His late visit to the pits removed him from the points. Colapinto, who had changed from mediums to hards during the early virtual safety car, finished seventh, ahead of Piastri. Lindblad and Hülkenberg completed the points scorers, while [Esteban Ocon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Ocon) (Haas) finished just outside the top ten.[^cite_note-ESP26-motorsport-28]

Antonelli won the race ahead of Verstappen. Norris was third abd Leclerc held fourth over Russell, with Lawson sixth. Gasly was classified 12th and received a five-second penalty for speeding in the pit lane. Bortoleto, Tsunoda, [Alexander Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Albon) (Williams), Bearman, Alonso and [Valtteri Bottas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valtteri_Bottas) (Cadillac) were the remaining finishers. Sainz, Pérez, Stroll and Hamilton retired.[^cite_note-ESP26-result-35]

#### Post-race

Antonelli acknowledged afterwards that Norris had deserved to win and that the circumstances made his own victory less satisfying. He nevertheless highlighted Mercedes' pace on the hard tyres and stressed that he intended to approach the remaining races individually rather than regard the championship as decided. The result was his eighth Grand Prix victory of the season.[^cite_note-ESP26-reaction-29]

Norris attributed the result to the unfortunate timing of the virtual safety car, observing that his early speed had put him beyond the pit entry when the intervention began. He believed McLaren should have won.[^cite_note-ESP26-sky-24] The circuit's limited overtaking opportunities also drew criticism from drivers following its first Grand Prix.[^cite_note-ESP26-guardian-36]

Antonelli left Madrid with 292 points, extending his championship lead over Russell to 81 points and his advantage over Norris to 106.[^cite_note-Stats_F1-37] Mercedes reached 503 points in the Constructors' Championship, ahead of Ferrari on 358.[^cite_note-Stats_F1-37]
