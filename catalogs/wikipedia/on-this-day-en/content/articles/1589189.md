The **Chamber of Deputies** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Cámara de Diputados*) is the [lower chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_chamber) of the [Plurinational Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurinational_Legislative_Assembly), the [bicameral legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameral_legislature) of Bolivia. Like the [upper chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_chamber), the [Senate](1589188.md), it meets in [La Paz](42821.md), the seat of government. It is an elected body consisting of 130 members and their [substitutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitute_\(elections\)), known as deputies.

## Deputies

Deputies serve five-year terms, and must be aged at least 25 on the day of the election.&#x20;

### Electoral system

The Chamber of Deputies comprises 130 seats (including the seven special seats), elected using a [seat linkage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seat_linkage?action=edit\&redlink=1) based mixed [compensatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compensation_\(electoral_systems\)) system using a two votes: 63 deputies are elected by [first-preference plurality](261709.md) to represent single-member [electoral districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_district), 60 are elected by [closed list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_list) [party-list proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party-list_proportional_representation) from party lists on a departmental basis (in districts of varying sizes corresponding to Bolivia's nine departments with a threshold of 3%).[^cite_note-RegimenElectoral-1] The list seats in each region are awarded proportionally based on the vote for the presidential candidates, subtracting the number of single-member districts won (to provide [mixed-member proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed-member_proportional_representation) via [additional member system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Additional_member_system)). The remaining seven seats are reserved indigenous seats elected by the *[usos y costumbres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usos_y_costumbres)*. A voter can only vote in one of either the normal constituencies or special constituencies ([coexistence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coexistence_\(electoral_systems\))).

The election uses the same votes as the votes for the President and the Senate, making it a [double (triple) simultaneous vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_simultaneous_vote). Voters may therefore not [split their ticket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split-ticket_voting) between these elections, but they may vote for a candidate of a different list in the election of the Chamber as the deputies from the single-member districts are elected using separate votes.

Party lists are required to alternate between men and women, and in the single-member districts, men are required to run with a female alternate, and vice versa. At least 50% of the deputies from single-member districts are required to be women.
