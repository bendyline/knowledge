The **monarchy of Cambodia** is the [constitutional monarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_monarchy) of the [Kingdom of Cambodia](334751.md). The **king of Cambodia** ([Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language): ព្រះមហាក្សត្រកម្ពុជា) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and head of the ruling Royal [House of Norodom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Norodom). In the contemporary period, the king's power has been limited to that of a symbolic figurehead. The monarchy had been in existence since at least 50 AD except during its abolition from 1970 to 1993. Since 1993, the king of Cambodia has been an elected monarch, making Cambodia one of the few [elective monarchies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elective_monarchy) of the world. The king is elected for life by the [Royal Council of the Throne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Council_of_the_Throne), which consists of several senior political and religious figures. Candidates are chosen from among male descendants of King [Ang Duong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ang_Duong) who are at least 30 years old, from the two royal houses of Cambodia (the [House of Norodom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Norodom) and the [House of Sisowath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Sisowath)).

## Role

Cambodia's constitution, promulgated in 1993, stipulates the king's role as a mainly ceremonial one. It declares that the king "shall reign, but not govern"[^cite_note-1] as well as being the "symbol of national unity and continuity".[^cite_note-2]

The king performs important functions of state as required by the constitution. These include but are not limited to:

- Appointing the [prime minister](3971840.md) and the [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Cambodia).

  [^cite_note-3]

- Convening over the opening of the two legislative bodies, the [National Assembly of Cambodia](4497389.md) and the [Senate of Cambodia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_\(Cambodia\)).

  [^cite_note-4]

  [^cite_note-5]

- Serving as the supreme commander of the [Royal Cambodian Armed Forces](2867590.md).

  [^cite_note-6]

- Presiding over the [Supreme Council of the National Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Council_of_the_National_Defense?action=edit\&redlink=1).

  [^cite_note-7]

- Meeting with the prime minister on a semi-monthly basis in which the king is briefed on matters of state.

  [^cite_note-8]

- Signing the royal code/decree that gives effect to laws enacted by the legislature and proposals by the cabinet.

  [^cite_note-9]

- Acting as "supreme arbiter" to enable the functioning of state institutions.

  [^cite_note-10]

- Receiving credentials from ambassadors.

  [^cite_note-11]

- Possessing the power of commutation and pardon.

  [^cite_note-12]

- Presiding over the [Supreme Council of the Magistracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Council_of_the_Magistracy).

  [^cite_note-13]

  [^cite_note-14]

- Appointing a fixed number of members to serve on state institutions such as the Senate and the Constitutional Council.

  [^cite_note-15]

  [^cite_note-16]

- Awarding of [national honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders,_decorations,_and_medals_of_Cambodia).

  [^cite_note-17]

The king also fulfils other roles not explicitly mentioned in the constitution in his capacity as head of state, for example, presiding over events of national significance[^cite_note-18] including religious ceremonies and traditions integral to the Khmer nation,[^cite_note-19] supporting humanitarian and philanthropic causes,[^cite_note-20] and representing Cambodia abroad when undertaking official visits overseas.[^cite_note-21] Although there have been female rulers in the past, the 1993 constitution currently forbids women from succeeding to the throne.[^cite_note-22]

## Ministry of the Royal Palace

The Ministry of the [Royal Palace](2830605.md), currently overseen by Minister [Kuy Sophal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuy_Sophal) in conjunction with the Supreme Privy Advisory Council, formerly headed by the King's half-brother Prince [Norodom Ranariddh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norodom_Ranariddh) and now headed by former Prime Minister [Hun Sen](393779.md) assists and advises the king accordingly in carrying out his duties as monarch.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]
