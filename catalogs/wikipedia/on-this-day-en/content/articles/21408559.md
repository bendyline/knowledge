**San Cercle** is an administrative subdivision of the [Ségou Region](1382091.md) of [Mali](19127.md). The administrative center (*[chef-lieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chef-lieu)*) is the town of [San](1727971.md).

The [cercle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cercles_of_Mali) is divided into 25 [communes](16112417.md):[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

- [Baramandougou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baramandougou)

- [Dah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dah,_Mali)

- [Diakourouna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diakourouna)

- [Diéli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dieli)

- [Djéguena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djeguena)

- [Fion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fion)

- [Kaniegué](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaniegue)

- [Karaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaba,_Mali)

- [Kassorola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kassorola)

- [Kava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kava,_Mali)

- [Moribila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moribila)

- [N'Goa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N'Goa)

- [N'Torosso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N'Torosso)

- [Niamana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niamana,_S%C3%A9gou)

- [Niasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niasso)
