**Ramat Gan** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): רָמַת גַּן, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Height of Garden', pronounced [\[raˈmat ˈɡan\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hebrew) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/4/46/Ramat_gan.ogg/Ramat_gan.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/4/46/Ramat_gan.ogg/Ramat_gan.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a city in the [Tel Aviv District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_District) of [Israel](9282173.md), located east of the municipality of [Tel Aviv](31453.md), and is part of the [Gush Dan metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gush_Dan). It is home to a [Diamond Exchange District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Exchange_District) (one of the world's major [diamond](8082.md) exchanges), [Sheba Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheba_Medical_Center) (the largest hospital in Israel) and many high-tech industries.

Ramat Gan was established in 1921 as a [moshava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshava), a communal farming settlement. In 2024 it had a population of 172,242.[^cite_note-Israelpopulations-1]

## History



Ramat Gan in 1936



Ramat Gan in 1945



Ramat Gan in 1948

Ramat Gan was established by the *Ir Ganim*[^cite_note-2] association in 1921 as a [satellite town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_town) of [Tel Aviv](31453.md). The first plots of land were purchased between 1914 and 1918.[^cite_note-JVL-3] It stood just south of the [Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab) village of [Jarisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarisha).[^cite_note-4] The settlement was initially a [moshava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshava), a [Zionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zionist) agricultural colony that grew [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley) and [watermelons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watermelon).[^cite_note-profile-5] The name of the settlement was changed to Ramat Gan (lit: *Garden Height*) in 1923. The settlement continued to operate as a moshava until 1933, although it achieved [local council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_council_\(Israel\)) status in 1926. At this time it had 450 residents. In the 1940s, Ramat Gan became a battleground in the country's language war: A [Yiddish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yiddish) language printing press in Ramat Gan was blown up by Hebrew-language extremists.[^cite_note-6]

Over the years, the economy shifted from agriculture to commerce and industry. By 1946, the population had grown to 12,000.[^cite_note-JVL-3] In 1950, Ramat Gan was recognized as a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_council_\(Israel\)). The city's population was greatly boosted by an influx of [Iraqi Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Iraq) into Israel during [Operation Ezra and Nehemiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Ezra_and_Nehemiah). So many Iraqi immigrants settled in Ramat Gan that it became known as "Little [Baghdad](4492.md)."[^cite_note-7] The popular Israeli dish [Sabich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabich), based on a traditional Iraqi Jewish meal, was created in Ramat Gan by Iraqi immigrants. In 1955, it had a population of 55,000. The first mayor was [Avraham Krinitzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avraham_Krinitzi) who remained in office for 43 years. In 1961, the municipal area of Ramat Gan expanded eastward, to encompass the area that includes the [Sheba Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheba_Medical_Center) in [Tel Hashomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Hashomer) and [Bar Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar_Ilan_University). In 1968, the world's largest [diamond exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Exchange_District) opened in Ramat Gan.[^cite_note-JVL-3] The [Sheba Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheba_Medical_Center) and the [Israel Diamond Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Diamond_Exchange) are located in Ramat Gan.

In June 2025, during the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War), the city was targeted by Iran. On June 14, a ballistic missile struck a residential building, killing a woman.[^cite_note-8] Nine buildings were destroyed, while hundreds of others were damaged at some level; approximately 100 residents were displaced from their homes due to the bombardment.[^cite_note-9] On June 19, 22 people were injured due to a hit on the financial district with considerable material damage, with two of them seriously wounded.[^cite_note-10]

On 17 March, during the [2026 Iran War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_War), a [cluster munition](79745.md) from the warhead of an Iranian missile hit an apartment building killed two residents in their 70s, who were found just outside their safe room.[^cite_note-11] A statement from the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) called the barrage that killed the two civilians "revenge for the blood of martyr Dr. [Ali Larijani](531848.md) and his companions," who had been killed a day before as the target of an Israeli airstrike.[^cite_note-12]

## Geography and climate

Ramat Gan is located in the [Gush Dan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gush_Dan) metropolitan area east of [Tel Aviv](31453.md). It is bounded in the north by the [Yarkon River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarkon_River) and in the east by [Bnei Brak](314671.md). [Giv'atayim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giv'atayim) lies to the southwest.[^cite_note-profile-5]

Ramat Gan experiences an average of 560 mm (22 in) of rainfall per year and is located, on average 80 m (260 ft) [above sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Above_mean_sea_level).[^cite_note-profile-5] It is built on [limestone hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone).[^cite_note-13] Ramat Gan parks include [The National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_National_Park_\(Ramat_Gan\)) (Park Leumi) which covers some 1,900 dunams, and David Park in the Merom Naveh neighborhood.[^cite_note-gardens-14] 25% of Ramat Gan is covered by public parkland.

Ramat Gan neighborhoods include: Shchunat Hageffen, City Center, Nachalat Ganim, Kiryat Krinitzi, Ramat Shikma, Ramat Yitzhak, Shchunat Rishonim, Tel Yehuda, Givat Geula, Neve Yehoshua, Kiryat Borochov, Merom Naveh, Ramat Amidar, Ramat Chen, Shikun Vatikim, Shchunat Hillel, Elite and Diamond Exchange District and Tel Binyamin.[^cite_note-JVL-3]

According to the [1931 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1931_census_of_Palestine), Ramat Gan had 975 inhabitants, in 253 houses.[^cite_note-Census31-16] As of 2006, Ramat Gan had 129,700 residents, in an area of 12,000 [dunams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunam) (12 km<sup>2</sup>).[^cite_note-grow-19] The population was growing at a rate of 1.0% per annum with 90% of this growth coming through [natural increase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_increase).[^cite_note-grow-19] The population density of the city is 9,822.6 per square kilometer, one of the highest in Israel.[^cite_note-density-17] In terms of the origin of Ramat Gan's residents, 42,900 originate from Europe and America, 10,200 from Africa, 29,200 from Asia, and 40,600 from Israel.[^cite_note-origin-20] 86,200 of the residents of Ramat Gan were born in Israel, whilst 36,600 were born abroad.[^cite_note-origin-20]

According to the [Israel Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics), as of 2001, Ramat Gan's [socioeconomic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socioeconomic) ranking stood at 8 out of 10. 70.9% of twelfth grade students received a [matriculation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matriculation) certificate in 2000. That year, the average wages in Ramat Gan were 6,995 NIS. As of 2006, 32,100 of the city's households had people who were not in the [labour force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_force), with 23,300 of these retired.[^cite_note-status-21] 1,900 of the households had unemployed household members.[^cite_note-status-21] 43,000 households were fully employed.[^cite_note-status-21] The largest sectors of jobs for those employed in Ramat Gan were business activities, which accounted for 18.1% of jobs; education (15.1%); wholesale and retail trade and repairs (14.2%); manufacturing (10.8%); and health, welfare, and social work services (10.0%).[^cite_note-22]

## Economy



[Diamond Exchange District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Exchange_District) also known as "The Bursa Area" of Ramat Gan

Ramat Gan's economy is dominated by the [Diamond Exchange District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Exchange_District) in the northwest of the city, home to a large concentration of [skyscrapers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyscrapers), including [Moshe Aviv Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshe_Aviv_Tower) (also known as City Gate), Israel's second tallest building at 235 metres (771 ft), the [Israel Diamond Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Diamond_Exchange) (a world leader in [diamonds](8082.md)), a large Sheraton hotel, and many [high-tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-tech) businesses, among them [Mobileye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobileye), Mazebolt and [ArticlesBase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArticlesBase).

Also located in the Diamond Exchange District is the [State Bank of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Bank_of_India)'s Israeli headquarters and the headquarters of [Bank Mizrachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_Mizrachi), whilst the embassies of [Ghana](12067.md), [Kenya](188171.md), [Ivory Coast](57744.md), [Jordan](7515964.md), [Eritrea](17238590.md), [Norway](21241.md), [Belgium](3343.md), [the Netherlands](21148.md), and the [European Economic Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Economic_Community), are located in the area.[^cite_note-23] Several other international embassies are also located in the city, as is the [British Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Council). Also headquartered in the city is the [Histadrut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Histadrut) trade union. Located to the south of Ramat Gan is [Hiriya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiriya), the largest [waste transfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_station_\(waste_management\)) site in the Middle East.

Ramat Gan is also an important center for industry and manufacturing with major fruit and vegetable canning plants, textile mills, metal production plants, electrical manufacturers, furniture makers, and food producers based there.[^cite_note-24] Currently, the [Elite Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elite_Tower), set to exceed the [Moshe Aviv Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshe_Aviv_Tower) in height, is being built on the site of the historic [Elite Candy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elite_Candy) factory. As a tribute to the history of the site, the lower floors of the tower will house a chocolate museum.[^cite_note-25] At the end of 2006, Ramat Gan had three hotels, with a total of 408 rooms and 150,000 person-nights over the course of the year, which represented a 64% room occupancy rate.[^cite_note-26]

## Education



[Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University)

Ramat Gan is home to Israel's [second largest university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_universities_in_Israel), [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University), with 24,000 students. The city is also the location of the [Shenkar College of Engineering and Design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenkar_College_of_Engineering_and_Design), [Ramat Gan College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat_Gan_College?action=edit\&redlink=1), the [College of Law and Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_Law_and_Business), [Beit Zvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_Zvi) acting college.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] Ramat Gan is home to the [Israel Bible Center](https://israelbiblecenter.com/about-us) as well.&#x20;

## Healthcare

The [Sheba Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheba_Medical_Center), located in southeastern Ramat Gan and [Tel HaShomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_HaShomer), is Israel's largest hospital.[^cite_note-sheba-31] It includes the Safra Children's Hospital and Padeh Geriatric Rehabilitation Center. The city has 32 medical centers run by health authorities and 10 child-care clinics operated by the municipality.[^cite_note-medical-32] The city is also served by [Mayanei Hayeshua Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayanei_Hayeshua_Medical_Center), a [Haredi](53835.md) hospital in nearby Bnei Brak.[^cite_note-33]

## Archaeology

Northwest of the city is the archaeological site of [Tel Gerisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Gerisa), with its main occupation phases dating back to the [Middle and Late Bronze Ages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_archaeological_periods_\(Levant\)) and declining through [Iron Age I and II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_archaeological_periods_\(Levant\)).[^cite_note-Negev-34]

In 2026, a 1,300-year-old industrial and commercial complex was discovered in the parking lot of the [Winter Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_Stadium), revealing ancient streets, pottery kilns, and industrial buildings. The [Israel Antiquities Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Antiquities_Authority) stated that the site dates to the [Umayyad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umayyad), [Abbasid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbasid), and [Fatimid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatimid) periods, and proposed that it could have served as a major trade and industrial station or been part of a nearby undiscovered settlement.[^cite_note-35]

## Culture



[Ramat Gan National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat_Gan_National_Park)

Cultural venues in Ramat Gan include the [Ramat Gan Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat_Gan_Theater?action=edit\&redlink=1), the [Diamond Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Theater?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the [Russell Cultural Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Cultural_Center?action=edit\&redlink=1). The [Beit Zvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_Zvi) School of Performing Arts is based in Ramat Gan. Ramat Gan operates two cinema complexes: the Lev-Elram Cinema and the "[Yes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yes_\(Israel\)) Planet" [megaplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplex_\(movie_theater\)). Ramat Gan also has a [safari park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safari_park). The 250-acre site consists of both a drive-through African safari area and a modern outdoor zoo.[^cite_note-perform-36]

### Museums

Beit Avraham Krinitzi, home of the first mayor, is now a museum of the history of Ramat Gan. [Man and the Living World Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_and_the_Living_World_Museum?action=edit\&redlink=1) is a [natural history museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_history_museum) and the Maccabi Museum is a museum which focuses on the history of Jewish sports since 1898.[^cite_note-museums-37] The [Ramat Gan Safari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat_Gan_Safari), a 250-acre (1.0 km<sup>2</sup>) zoo housing 1,600 animals, is the largest animal collection in the Middle East.[^cite_note-gardens-14] Other museums in the city include the [Museum of Israeli Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat_Gan_Museum_of_Israeli_Art), Kiryat Omanut, which houses sculpture galleries and a ceramics studio, the Museum of [Russian Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_art), the Museum of [Jewish Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_culture), and the Yehiel Nahari Museum of Far Eastern Art.

## Sports



[Ramat Gan Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat_Gan_Stadium)

The [Maccabiah Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maccabiah_Games) are held in Ramat Gan every four years. [Ramat Gan Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat_Gan_Stadium) was Israel's national football stadium until 2014. The stadium can seat up to 41,583 in practice (the official capacity is 13,370 seats). [Hakoah Amidar Ramat Gan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakoah_Amidar_Ramat_Gan_F.C.) and [Hapoel Ramat Gan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hapoel_Ramat_Gan_Giv'atayim_F.C.), who both play at the [Winter Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_Stadium), are the city's main football clubs, both having won the championship at some point in their history. [Beitar Ramat Gan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beitar_Ramat_Gan_F.C.) plays in the South A Division of [Liga Bet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liga_Bet) (fourth tier league), whilst F.C. Mahanaim Ramat Gan, [Maccabi Hashikma Hen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maccabi_HaShikma_Ramat_Hen_F.C.), Maccabi Spartak Ramat Gan, and [Shikun Vatikim Ramat Gan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shikun_Vatikim_Ramat_Gan_F.C.) are all playing in the Tel Aviv Division of [Liga Gimel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liga_Gimel) (fifth tier league). The now-defunct clubs [Maccabi Ramat Gan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maccabi_Ramat_Gan_F.C.) and [Maccabi Ramat Amidar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maccabi_Ramat_Amidar_F.C.) were both involved in mergers which formed Hakoah Amidar. In basketball, [Ironi Ramat Gan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ironi_Ramat_Gan) plays in [Ligat HaAl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ligat_HaAl_\(basketball\)), the top division.

## Voting Patterns

In the [2022 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Israeli_legislative_election), 62 percent of voters in Ramat Gan voted for the parties of the center-left coalition led by [Yair Lapid](2448072.md), while 34 percent voted for the parties of the right-wing coalition led by [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) and 0.5 percent voted for the Arab parties.[^cite_note-38]
