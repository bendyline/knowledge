The **Ministry of Defense**[^cite_note-3] is the [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Afghanistan) [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) of [Afghanistan](737.md) responsible for overseeing the [Afghan Armed Forces](57836785.md) (currently referred to as the Islamic Emirate Armed Forces). The ministry is located in [Kabul](16826.md).[^cite_note-4]

## The Democratic Republic period



The Ministry of Defense emblem from 1987 to 1992

From the 30th of April until 9 August 1978, [Abdul Qadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Qadir_\(Afghan_communist\)) succeeded the slain [Ghulam Haidar Rasuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghulam_Haidar_Rasuli) as Defense Minister of the DRA, responsible for the [Armed Forces of the Democratic Republic of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_Afghanistan), until being succeeded by [General](148537.md) [Aslam Watanjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Aslam_Watanjar). In 1990 forces loyal to Minister of Defense Shahnawaz Tanai and [Hezbi Islami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hezb-e_Islami_Gulbuddin) leader [Gulbuddin Hekmatyar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulbuddin_Hekmatyar) attempted a [fail coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Afghan_coup_d'%C3%A9tat_attempt) against then [President Najibullah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Najibullah). His forces were thwarted by General [Aslam Watanjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Aslam_Watanjar) who was rewarded the post of Minister of Defence. Watanjar would be the last Minister of Defense of the DRA/ROA.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-Los_Angeles_Times-7] The government collapsed in 1992.

Additionally, the Ministry of Defense also had their own annual publication titled “The Military Magazine” ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): د اردو مجله, [Dari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dari_language): مجله ارتش) which began in 1967, under the [Kingdom of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Afghanistan). This was continued under the [Democratic Republic of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Afghanistan).[^cite_note-8]

## The Islamic Republic period



Former logo from 2018.

During the [Islamic Republic of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Republic_of_Afghanistan) (2004–2021), the defense minister was nominated by the [President of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Afghanistan) and the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Afghanistan\)) made the final approval.

One of the functions of the Defense Ministry during that period was the continuance of [disarming](57979.md) insurgent groups, through programmes such as the [Afghan New Beginnings Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_New_Beginnings_Programme) (which included the [rehabilitation and reintegration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehabilitation_and_reintegration_of_child_soldiers) of [child soldiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_use_of_children#Afghanistan)). These militant groups coalesced from warlords and former army personnel after the collapse of the [Najibullah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Najibullah) [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Afghanistan) in 1992.[^cite_note-BhatiaSedra-9]
