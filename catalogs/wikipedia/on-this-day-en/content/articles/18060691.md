**Kafr Rumman** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): كفر رمّان) is a [Palestinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_people) town in the [Tulkarm Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulkarm_Governorate) in the eastern [West Bank](33209.md), located 11 kilometers East of [Tulkarm](851834.md). According to the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics), Kafr Rumman had a population of approximately 869 inhabitants in mid-year 2007.[^cite_note-2]

## History

[Potsherd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherd) from the [Middle Bronze Age IIB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Age#Near_East_timeline),[^cite_note-Zertal-3] [Iron Age II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Age#Near_East_timeline),[^cite_note-Zertal-3] [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaemenid_Empire),[^cite_note-Zertal-3] [Hellenistic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hellenistic_period),[^cite_note-Zertal-3] [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire),[^cite_note-Zertal-3][^cite_note-Dauphin761-4] [Byzantine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Empire)[^cite_note-Zertal-3][^cite_note-Dauphin761-4] and early [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim) eras have been found here.[^cite_note-Zertal-3]

### Ottoman era

Kafr Rumman, like all of [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_\(region\)) was incorporated into the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md) in 1517. In the 1596 [tax registers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daftar), it was part of the *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya)* ("subdistrict") of Jabal Sami, part of the larger [Sanjak of Nablus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjak_of_Nablus). It had a population of 20 households, all [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim). The inhabitants paid a fixed tax rate of 33,3% on agricultural products, including wheat, barley, summer crops, olive trees, goats and beehives, in addition to occasional revenues and a press for olive oil or grape syrup, and a fixed tax for people of Nablus area; a total of 3,022 [akçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e).[^cite_note-5]

The old core of the village is presumed to have been built in the 16th-17th century CE, and contains high, fortified buildings.[^cite_note-Zertal-3]

In 1838 *Kefr Rumman* was placed in the *Wady esh-Sha'ir* administrative region, west of [Nablus](233369.md).[^cite_note-Robinson129-6]

In 1870 [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) noted it from nearby [Ramin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramin,_Tulkarm).[^cite_note-7]

In 1870/1871 (1288 [AH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anno_Hegirae)), an Ottoman census listed the village in the *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiyah)* (sub-district) of Wadi al-Sha'ir.[^cite_note-8]

In 1882 the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s *[Survey of Western Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine)* (SWP) described *Kefr Rumman* as: "a small [hamlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlet_\(place\)) on the side of the mountain, with a [well](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_well) to the north and olives."[^cite_note-9]

### British Mandate era

In the [1922 census of Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1922_census_of_Palestine) conducted by the [British Mandate authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine), *Kufr Rumman* had a population of 161 Muslims,[^cite_note-Census1922-10] increasing in the [1931 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1931_census_of_Palestine) to 189 Muslims, in 48 houses.[^cite_note-Census1931-11]

In the [1945 statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_Statistics,_1945) the population of *Kafr Rumman* was 270 Muslims,[^cite_note-DoS1945-12] with 3,933 [dunams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunam) of land according to an official land and population survey.[^cite_note-13] Of this, 625 dunams were plantations and irrigable land, 352 were used for cereals,[^cite_note-14] while 5 dunams were built-up (urban) land.[^cite_note-15]

### Jordanian era

In the wake of the [1948 Arab–Israeli War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Arab%E2%80%93Israeli_War), and after the [1949 Armistice Agreements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1949_Armistice_Agreements), Kafr Rumman came under [Jordanian rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordanian_annexation_of_the_West_Bank).

In 1961, the population was 466.[^cite_note-16]

### Post 1967

Since the [Six-Day War](29328.md) in 1967, Kafr Rumman has been under [Israeli occupation](59076253.md). In 1997, Kafr Rumman had a population of 649, of whom 45.5% were registered refugees.[^cite_note-17]
