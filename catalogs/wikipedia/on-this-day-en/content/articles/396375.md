This is a list of [political parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Latvia](17514.md). Latvia has a [multi-party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party) system, where often no one party has a chance of gaining power alone, and parties must work with each other to form [coalition governments](6036.md).
