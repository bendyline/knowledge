**Tahoua** is one of seven [regions of Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Niger). The capital of the region is the [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Niger) of [Tahoua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahoua). The region covers 106,677 km².

## Geography

Tahoua borders [Agadez Region](2000287.md) to the northeast, [Maradi Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maradi_Region) to the southeast, [Nigeria](21383.md)'s [Sokoto State](2227660.md) to the south, and [Mali](19127.md) ([Gao](2349862.md) and [Kidal](2214311.md) regions), [Dosso Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dosso_Region) and [Tillabéri Region](2050923.md) to the west. Much of the region lies with the [Sahel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahel), merging into the [Sahara](325363.md) desert in the north.

### Settlements

[Tahoua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahoua) is the regional capital; other major settlements include [Abalak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abalak), [Bagaroua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagaroua), [Birni-N'Konni](1378749.md), [Bouza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bouza), [Illela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illela,_Niger), [Keita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keita,_Niger), [Madaoua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madaoua) and [Tchintabaraden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tchintabaraden).[^cite_note-2]

## Demographics

As of 2011 the population of Tahoua Region was 2,741,922.[^cite_note-3] The main ethnolinguistic groups are the [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people), [Fulani](102383.md), [Arabs](2185.md) (Azawakh Arabs) and [Tuareg](103054.md).[^cite_note-4]
