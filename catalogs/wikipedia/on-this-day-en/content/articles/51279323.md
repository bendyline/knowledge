The **Supreme Political Council** (**SPC**; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): المجلس السياسي الأعلى, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *al-Majlis as-Siyāsiyy al-ʾAʿlā*) is an extraconstitutional[^cite_note-1] [rival](80144294.md) [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) established in 2016 in [Sanaa](18691665.md) by the [Houthis](17240332.md) and the pro-Houthi faction of the [General People's Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_People's_Congress_\(Yemen\)) (GPC) to rule [areas of Yemen under their control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_Yemen) opposed to the internationally recognized [Presidential Leadership Council](70488867.md) (PLC) in [Aden](38050.md).[^cite_note-2] The SPC carries out the functions of head of state in Yemen, appointing the [country's cabinet](31167279.md) and managing the Yemen's state affairs in a bid to fill in political vacuum during the [Yemeni Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_Civil_War_\(2015%E2%80%93present\)).[^cite_note-3] The Council aims to outline a basis for running the country and managing state affairs on the basis of the constitution.[^cite_note-aljazeera.com-4][^cite_note-sabanews435947-5] Since 2018, the SPC has been headed by [Mahdi al-Mashat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahdi_al-Mashat) as [Chairman of the Council](444090.md).[^cite_note-sabanews435947-5]

The SPC was formed on 28 July 2016 with an initial ten members and was headed by [Saleh Ali al-Sammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saleh_Ali_al-Sammad) as president and Qassem Labozah as vice-president. The members were sworn in on 14 August 2016,[^cite_note-6] and the next day the [Supreme Revolutionary Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Revolutionary_Committee) (SRC) handed power to the Supreme Political Council.[^cite_note-7] After al-Sammad was killed in a [drone strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drone_strikes_in_Yemen) on 19 April 2018, chairmanship of the SPC passed to Mahdi al-Mashat. Under the SPC is subordinate the [Cabinet of Yemen](31167279.md), which it appoints and supervises government officials who oversee relevant ministries; since 2016 two governments have been established under the council. In 2016 the SPC appointed former [Aden](3604348.md) governor [Abdel-Aziz bin Habtour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdel-Aziz_bin_Habtour) to form a ministry known as the National Salvation Government (NSG).[^cite_note-reuters.com-8] In September 2023 the NSG was dismissed and replaced by a caretaker administration which continued to be headed by Habtour. In August 2024 a Presidential resolution appointed [Ahmad al-Rahawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_al-Rahawi) as the head of a new ministry known as the 'Government of Change and Construction' (GCC).[^cite_note-middleeastmonitor20240812-9][^cite_note-sabanews3357644-10] Eight days later the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_\(Yemen\)) (in Sanaa) approved the cabinet and called for a 36-point 'general program'.[^cite_note-sabanews3360123-11] However, on August 28, 2025 [Israel](9282173.md) [launched Operation Lucky Drop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/28_August_2025_Israeli_attack_on_Yemen), assassinating the Prime Minister amongst other government officials of the SPC. On August 30, 2025, after confirming the assassinations, the Houthis appointed [Muhammad Ahmed Miftah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Ahmed_Miftah) as the new Prime Minister of the SPC. The assassination of the SPC's Defense Minister has not yet been confirmed by the Houthis.

The SPC remains internationally unrecognized compared to the PLC in Aden and is acknowledged only by [Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Yemen_relations), with the Houthis placing an ambassador to Iran in August 2019.[^cite_note-aljazeera.com-4][^cite_note-Hatem2019-12][^cite_note-13] The formation of the SPC has been condemned by the [international community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_community); with the [United Nations](31769.md) (UN) officially describing the act as "a clear violation of the [Yemeni constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_constitution)" and denounced the council for sabotaging the [Yemeni peace process](34801006.md).[^cite_note-aljazeera.com-4][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

## Background



[Territory](79583620.md) controlled by the [Houthis](17240332.md) shown in blue.

&#x20;In the wake of president [Abdrabbuh Mansur Hadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdrabbuh_Mansur_Hadi) and prime minister [Khaled Bahah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khaled_Bahah) resignations over Houthi rebels takeover of presidential palace in January 2015, Houthi leader [Abdul Malik Al Houthi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Malik_Al_Houthi) reportedly proposed a six-member "transitional presidential council" which would have equal representation from north and south, although this proposal was rejected by the [Southern Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Movement).[^cite_note-16] Nevertheless, on 1 February, the Houthis gave an ultimatum to Yemen's political factions warning that if they did not reach a solution to the current political crisis, then the Houthi "revolutionary leadership" would assume formal authority over the state.[^cite_note-houthiultimatum-17] According to [Reuters](18998750.md), political factions have agreed to form an interim presidential council to manage the country for up to one year. Former [South Yemen](718749.md) president [Ali Nasser Mohammed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Nasser_Mohammed) was originally being considered as a prospective interim leader, but Mohammed later declined the post.[^cite_note-18]

On 6 February 2015, the Houthis formally assumed over authority in [Sanaa](18691665.md), declaring the dissolution of House of Representatives and announced that a "presidential council" would be formed to lead Yemen for two years, while a "revolutionary committee" would be put in charge of forming a new, 551-member national council.[^cite_note-19] This governance plan was later affirmed by Houthi Ansarullah politburo leader [Saleh Ali al-Sammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saleh_Ali_al-Sammad) as he said that national council would choose a five-member presidential council to govern the country.[^cite_note-20]

## Cabinets

### Government of Change and Construction (2024–present)

On 10 August 2024, [Ahmed al-Rahawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_al-Rahawi) was appointed as prime minister of Yemen and tasked with forming a ministry known as the 'Government of Change and Construction' by the President of the Supreme Political Council [Mahdi al-Mashat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahdi_al-Mashat).[^cite_note-sabanews3357644-10] Two days later the composition of the new government was announced in a presidential resolution.[^cite_note-yemeneco87574-22] On 18 August 2024 the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_\(Yemen\)) (San'aa) gave [confidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confidence_and_supply) to the government and called on it to follow a 36-point 'general program' prepared by a house of representatives special committee.[^cite_note-sabanews3360123-11] The government and SPC continues to be internationally unrecognised in comparison with the [Presidential Leadership Council](70488867.md) (PLC).[^cite_note-23]

On 28 August 2025, [a series of Israeli airstrikes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/28_August_2025_Israeli_attack_on_Yemen) killed Rahawi and nine other ministers.[^cite_note-24]

### National Salvation Government (2016–2023)

On 2 October 2016, [Abdel-Aziz bin Habtour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdel-Aziz_bin_Habtour) was appointed as prime minister by the Houthis.[^cite_note-reuters.com-8] On 4 October, he formed his cabinet.[^cite_note-RFI_Rival-26] The cabinet was composed of members of the [Southern Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Movement).[^cite_note-RFI_Rival-26] However, the cabinet is not internationally recognized.[^cite_note-27]

On 28 November 2016, a new cabinet was formed.[^cite_note-28] It is only composed of members of the pro-Saleh GPC and the Houthis.

However, the UN Special Envoy for Yemen [Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismail_Ould_Cheikh_Ahmed) said the move was "a new and unnecessary obstacle. Yemen is at a critical juncture. The actions recently taken by Ansarullah and the [General People's Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_People's_Congress_\(Yemen\)) will only complicate the search for a peaceful solution. The parties must hold Yemen’s national interests above narrow partisan ambitions and take immediate steps to end political divisions and address the country’s security, humanitarian and economic challenges." He further claimed that such an action could harm peace talks.[^cite_note-29]

On 27 October 2020, the Minister of Youth and Sport Hassan Mohammed Zaid was shot dead by unknown gunmen in the capital Sanaa.[^cite_note-reutersidUKKBN27C1E9-30]

On 27 September 2023, the National Salvation Government was dismissed and replaced by a caretaker government. Habtour continued as caretaker prime minister before being elevated to the SPC in 2024.[^cite_note-middleeastmonitor20240812-9]

The Supreme Political Council currently only has [diplomatic relations with Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Yemen_relations). [Relations with Ba'athist Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria%E2%80%93Yemen_relations) existed between 2015 and 2023. Additionally, the government has diplomatic contacts with [Russia](25391.md), [Abkhazia](18933375.md) and [North Korea](21255.md). In 2015, *[The Wall Street Journal](173070.md)* reported that the government would seek contacts with [China](5405.md), Iran and Russia.[^cite_note-34] In 2016, a National Salvation government official had invited several North Korean diplomats to [Damascus](8914.md).[^cite_note-35] The same year, a delegation of the National Salvation government visited Russia and met the Russian Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs.[^cite_note-36] In 2017, Foreign Minister Hisham Sharaf Abdullah met with the chargés d’affaires of the Russian and the Syrian Embassy in Sanaa.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38] In August 2019, the National Salvation government appointed an Ambassador to [Tehran](57654.md).[^cite_note-Hatem2019-12][^cite_note-39] In early 2020, the [Saba News Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saba_News_Agency) reported that Hisham Sharaf Abdullah called China a "friendly government".[^cite_note-40] In October 2020, Iran appointed [Hasan Irlu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasan_Irlu) as its ambassador to Sanaa.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43] Two months later, the [United States](3434750.md) sanctioned Irlu.[^cite_note-44] In August 2020, the National Salvation government appointed an ambassador to [Damascus](8914.md).[^cite_note-45] In March 2021, the National Salvation government appointed Ambassador in Damascus met with the Abkhaz Ambassador to Syria.[^cite_note-46] In October 2023, Syria expelled representatives of the National Salvation government from the Yemeni embassy in Damascus.[^cite_note-47]

The President, the Parliament Speaker and the Foreign Minister of the National Salvation government have sent numerous diplomatic notes to a wide range of countries such as [Algeria](358.md),[^cite_note-48] [Bahamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahamas),[^cite_note-49] [Comoros](5403.md),[^cite_note-50] [Kiribati](16674.md),[^cite_note-51] [Malawi](19088.md),[^cite_note-52] [Malta](19137.md),[^cite_note-53] [Mongolia](19271.md),[^cite_note-54] [Montenegro](20760.md),[^cite_note-55] [Slovenia](27338.md),[^cite_note-56] [South Sudan](32350676.md),[^cite_note-57] [Venezuela](32374.md)[^cite_note-58] and [Uzbekistan](31853.md).[^cite_note-59]
