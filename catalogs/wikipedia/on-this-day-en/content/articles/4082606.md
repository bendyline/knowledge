**Bargi Dam** is one of the first completed [dams](51518.md) out of the chain of 30 major dams to be constructed on [Narmada River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narmada_River) in [Madhya Pradesh](47945.md), [India](14533.md). It has 21 gates. Two major irrigation projects, named Bargi Diversion Project and Rani [Avantibai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avantibai) Lodhi Sagar Project, have been developed by the Bargi Dam administration.[^cite_note-2]
