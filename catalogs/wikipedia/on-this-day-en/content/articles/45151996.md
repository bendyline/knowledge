The **governor of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast** is the [head of executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Local_State_Administration_\(Ukraine\)) for the [Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](494909.md).

The office of [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor) is an appointed position, with officeholders being appointed by the [president of Ukraine](20688479.md), on recommendation from the [prime minister of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Ukraine), to serve a four-year term.

Due to the current [Russo-Ukrainian War](42085878.md), since 24 February 2022, the governor of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast is officially called *Head of the Dnipropetrovsk Regional Military Civil Administration*. The official residence for the governor is located in [Dnipro](989795.md).
