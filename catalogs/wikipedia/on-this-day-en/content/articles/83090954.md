**Cherie DeVaux** (born December 2, 1981)[^cite_note-someinfo-1] is an American [thoroughbred racehorse](67745.md) trainer. In 2026, she won the [Kentucky Derby](82792534.md) and the [Belmont Stakes](62398.md) as the trainer of [Golden Tempo](83090873.md), becoming the first woman to train a Derby winner and the second to train a Belmont winner, after [Jena Antonucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jena_Antonucci).

## Early life

DeVaux was born to Janet and Adrian DeVaux in [Saratoga Springs, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratoga_Springs,_New_York). The family moved when she was young to [Englewood, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Englewood,_Florida).[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-\:1-3] She has nine siblings (seven brothers and two sisters).[^cite_note-4] Her father and several siblings are also involved in the horse world, training horses for flat and harness racing.[^cite_note-\:1-3] She attended [Lemon Bay High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemon_Bay_High_School) and [Florida Gulf Coast University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Gulf_Coast_University). She also rode horses and competed in barrel racing.[^cite_note-\:1-3]

## Career

DeVaux in her early years worked as an assistant trainer for Chuck Simon and [Chad Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chad_Brown_\(horse_trainer\)).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

DeVaux started her horse training business in 2018 with eight horses. She won her first race on her 29th start.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] She trained several horses for [Breeders' Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breeders'_Cup) races in 2021, 2023, and 2024, winning the 2024 [Breeders' Cup Mile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breeders'_Cup_Mile) as the trainer of [More Than Looks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/More_Than_Looks?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-someinfo-1][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

She also trained [She Feels Pretty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_Feels_Pretty) to 2025 [American National Champion Female Turf Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eclipse_Award) honors, the first for herself and for owners Roy and Gretchen Jackson.[^cite_note-9] During her racing career, She Feels Pretty has won multiple Grade 1 races including the [Natalma Stakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalma_Stakes) at 2, [Queen Elizabeth II Challenge Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_II_Challenge_Cup_Stakes) at 3 and the [E.P. Taylor Stakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.P._Taylor_Stakes) at 4.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

On May 2, 2026, DeVaux became the first woman trainer to win the Kentucky Derby when her horse, Golden Tempo, closed from last to win.[^cite_note-12] She later won the [2026 Belmont Stakes](80257691.md), again as the trainer of Golden Tempo, who came from behind to earn a second [Triple Crown](19116721.md) win in 2026. She is the second woman trainer to win the Belmont.[^cite_note-13]
