A political **demarcation line** is a [geopolitical](163225.md) [border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border), often agreed upon as part of an [armistice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armistice) or [ceasefire](592591.md).

## Africa

- [Moroccan Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moroccan_Western_Sahara_Wall), delimiting the [Moroccan-controlled part of Western Sahara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moroccan-occupied_Western_Sahara) from the [Sahrawi-controlled part](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Zone_\(region\))

## Americas

- During European imperialism overseas, the [lines of amity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lines_of_amity) were drawn to differentiate Europe from the rest of the world. The Line of Demarcation was one specific line drawn along a meridian in the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md) as part of the [Treaty of Tordesillas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Tordesillas) in 1494 to divide new lands claimed by [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Portugal_\(1415%E2%80%931578\)) from those of [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Empire). This line was drawn in 1493 after [Christopher Columbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus) returned from his [maiden voyage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voyages_of_Christopher_Columbus#First_voyage) to the [Americas](29833.md).

- The [Mason–Dixon line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason%E2%80%93Dixon_line) (or "Mason and Dixon's Line") is a demarcation line between four [U.S. states](18618239.md), forming part of the borders of [Pennsylvania](23332.md), [Maryland](18858.md), [Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware), and [West Virginia](32905.md) (then part of [Virginia](32432.md)). It was surveyed between 1763 and 1767 by [Charles Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Mason) and [Jeremiah Dixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremiah_Dixon) in the resolution of a border dispute between [British colonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_colonies) in [Colonial America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonial_America).

## Asia

### Middle East

- The [Blue Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Line_\(Lebanon\)) is a border demarcation between [Lebanon](17771.md) and [Israel](9282173.md) published by the [United Nations](31769.md) on 7 June 2000 for the purposes of determining whether Israel had fully withdrawn from Lebanon.

- The term [Green Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Line_\(Israel\)) is used to refer to the [1949 Armistice lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1949_Armistice_lines) established between [Israel](9282173.md) and its neighbours ([Egypt](8087628.md), [Jordan](7515964.md), [Lebanon](17771.md) and [Syria](7515849.md)) after the [1948 Arab–Israeli War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Arab%E2%80%93Israeli_War).

- The [Purple Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_Line_\(border\)) was the [ceasefire](592591.md) line between [Israel](9282173.md) and [Syria](7515849.md) after the 1967 [Six-Day War](29328.md).

- The [Green Line (Lebanon)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Line_\(Lebanon\)) refers a line of demarcation in [Beirut](37428.md), [Lebanon](17771.md) during the [Lebanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Civil_War) from 1975 to 1990. It separated the mainly Muslim factions in West Beirut from the predominantly Christian East Beirut controlled by the Lebanese Front.

### South and East Asia

- The [McMahon Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMahon_Line) is a line dividing China and India, drawn on a map attached to the [Simla Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simla_Convention), a treaty negotiated between the [British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire), [China](5405.md), and [Tibet](31516.md) in 1914.

- The [Military Demarcation Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Demarcation_Line), sometimes referred to as the *Armistice Line*, is the border between [North Korea](21255.md) and [South Korea](27019.md). The Military Demarcation Line was established by the [Korean Armistice Agreement](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Korean%20Armistice%20Agreement) as the line between the two Koreas at the end of [Korean War](16772.md) in 1953.

  [^cite_note-1]

- The [Northern Limit Line](3013261.md) or *North Limit Line* (NLL) is a disputed maritime demarcation line in the [Yellow Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_Sea) between [North Korea](21255.md) and [South Korea](27019.md).

- The [Line of Actual Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_of_Actual_Control) established by [India](14533.md) and the [People's Republic of China](5405.md) between [Aksai Chin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aksai_Chin) and [Ladakh](303611.md) after the [Sino-Indian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sino-Indian_War) of 1962.

- The [Line of Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_of_Control) established by [India](14533.md) and [Pakistan](23235.md) over the disputed region of [Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir).

- The [nine-dash line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine-dash_line) appears on maps used by the [People's Republic of China](5405.md) and the [Republic of China (Taiwan)](25734.md) accompanying their [South China Sea](74209.md) claims, which are challenged by [Malaysia](3607937.md), the [Philippines](23440.md), and [Vietnam](202354.md).

## Europe



The [Line of Contact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_of_Contact), final positions of the armies of the Western Allies and Soviets, May 8, 1945.

- The [Curzon Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curzon_Line) was a demarcation line proposed in 1920 by [British](31717.md) [Foreign Secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_of_State_for_Foreign_and_Commonwealth_Affairs) [Lord Curzon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Nathaniel_Curzon,_1st_Marquess_Curzon_of_Kedleston) as a possible [armistice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armistice) line between [Poland](22936.md) to the west and the [Soviet republics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) to the east during the [Polish-Soviet War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish-Soviet_War) of 1919–21. The modern [Poland–Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus%E2%80%93Poland_border) and [Poland–Ukraine borders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland%E2%80%93Ukraine_border) mostly follow the Curzon line, as this was used as the border with the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) after WWII. These are the borders of [Ukraine](31750.md) and [Belarus](3457.md) after the collapse of the [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union).

* The [Foch Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foch_Line) was a temporary demarcation line between [Poland](22936.md) and [Lithuania](17675.md) proposed by the [Entente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allies_of_World_War_I) in the aftermath of [World War I](4764461.md).

* The [demarcation line in France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demarcation_line_\(France\)) in [Vichy France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vichy_France) imposed by [Nazi Germany](21212.md) from 1940 to 1942, with the [German-occupied zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zone_occup%C3%A9e) in the north and a [free zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zone_libre) in the south.

* The [Line of Contact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_of_Contact) was a demarcation line between Soviet-aligned forces and forces aligned with the Western allies, marking where Soviet-aligned forces and Western-aligned forces met as they advanced into Germany, Austria and Czechoslovakia at the end of World War II in Europe.

* The Bosnian [Inter-Entity Boundary Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-Entity_Boundary_Line) is an ethno-administrative border established by the [Dayton Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton_Agreement) that followed the end of the [Bosnian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnian_War).
