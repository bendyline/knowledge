The **2026 [WTA Tour](1398173.md)** (branded as the **2026 WTA Tour Driven by [Mercedes-Benz](18564164.md)** for sponsorship reasons)[^cite_note-1] is the global elite women's professional [tennis](29773.md) circuit organized by the [Women's Tennis Association](57768.md) (WTA) for the 2026 tennis season.[^cite_note-\:1-2] The calendar comprises the [Grand Slam](197638.md) tournaments (supervised by [World Tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Tennis)), the [WTA 1000 tournaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_1000_tournaments), the [WTA 500 tournaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_500_tournaments), the [WTA 250 tournaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_250_tournaments), the [Billie Jean King Cup](1108893.md) (organized by World Tennis), the year-end championships ([WTA Finals](1356185.md)), and the team event [United Cup](72152971.md) (combined event with [ATP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals)).[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-4]



[Elena Rybakina](55541443.md) won her second and third [major](197638.md#Tournaments) titles at the [Australian Open](81715708.md) and the [US Open](84044135.md), defeating [Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md) in both finals.



[Mirra Andreeva](71915009.md) won her first major title at the [French Open](83033049.md), defeating [Maja Chwalińska](53209114.md) in the final.



[Linda Nosková](67929086.md) won her first major title at the [Wimbledon Championships](83354904.md), defeating [Karolína Muchová](55266410.md) in the final.

## Statistical information

These tables present the number of [singles](4543024.md) (S), [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubles_\(tennis\)) (D), and [mixed doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_doubles_tennis) (X) titles won by each player and each nation during the season, within all the tournament categories of the 2025 WTA Tour: the [Grand Slam](197638.md) tournaments, the year-end championships (the [WTA Finals](1356185.md)), the [WTA Premier tournaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_Premier_tournaments) (WTA 1000 and WTA 500), and the [WTA 250](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_International_tournaments). The players/nations are sorted by:

1. total number of titles (a doubles title won by two players representing the same nation counts as only one win for the nation);

2. cumulated point value of those titles (one Grand Slam tournament win equaling two WTA 1000 wins, one year-end championships win equaling one-and-a-half WTA 1000 win, one WTA 1000 win equaling two WTA 500 wins, one WTA 500 win equaling two WTA 250 wins);

3. a singles > doubles > mixed doubles hierarchy;

4. alphabetical order (by family names for players).

## Retirements

The following is a list of notable players (winners of a main tour title, and/or part of the WTA rankings top 100 in singles, or top 100 in doubles, for at least one week) who announced their retirement from professional tennis, became inactive (after not playing for more than 52 weeks), or were banned from competition for a minimum of 12 months, during the 2026 season:

- [Hungary](13275.md) **[Tímea Babos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%ADmea_Babos)** joined the professional tour in 2011 and reached career-high rankings of No. 25 in singles in September 2016 and No. 1 in doubles in July 2018. She won three singles and 29 doubles titles, including four [major](197638.md#Tournaments) doubles titles at the [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles) and [2020 Australian Opens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles) and [2020 French Opens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), and three consecutive titles at the [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_WTA_Finals_%E2%80%93_Doubles), [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_WTA_Finals_%E2%80%93_Doubles) and [2019 WTA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_WTA_Finals_%E2%80%93_Doubles). Babos announced in November 2025, that she is going to take a break from tennis, wanting to focus on starting a family, but later would like to return to competitions and play at the [2028 Summer Olympics](12695048.md). Her final appearance before starting her hiatus was at the [2026 Australian Open](81711108.md).
