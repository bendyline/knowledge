The **2025 [IHF World Men's Handball Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHF_World_Men's_Handball_Championship)** was the 29th such event hosted by the [International Handball Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Handball_Federation). It was held in Croatia, Denmark and Norway from 14 January to 2 February 2025.[^cite_note-1] This edition marked the first time the [world championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHF_World_Men's_Handball_Championship) was held in three countries.

Co-host [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark_men's_national_handball_team) were the triple defending champions, having won the [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_World_Men's_Handball_Championship), [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_World_Men's_Handball_Championship) and [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_World_Men's_Handball_Championship) editions, and successfully defended their title by defeating co-host [Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatia_men's_national_handball_team) 32–26 in the final. In doing so, Denmark became the first men's national handball team to win four consecutive world titles.[^cite_note-records-2] [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_men's_national_handball_team) beat [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal_men's_national_handball_team) to win the bronze medals, while third co-host [Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway_men's_national_handball_team) finished 10th.

Denmark beat the record for the highest average win margin after 7 matches at a World Championship played with +13.4 goals. They also extended their record of most World Championship matches in a row without defeat to 31.[^cite_note-records-2]

## Bidding process

On 11 October 2018, five nations expressed interest in hosting the tournament.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

-  [Croatia](5573.md)

-  [Denmark](76972.md)/ [Norway](21241.md)/ [Switzerland](26748.md)

-  [Serbia](29265.md)

The deadline elapsed on 25 September 2019.[^cite_note-6] Switzerland became a solo bid, while Croatia took their place in the triple bid.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Serbia withdrew their bid, while Hungary filed both an application and deferment for a later tournament. These were the applicants:

- ** [Croatia](5573.md)/ [Denmark](76972.md)/ [Norway](21241.md)**

-  [Hungary](13275.md) (withdrew)

-  [Switzerland](26748.md) (withdrew)

Shortly before the vote, Hungary and Switzerland both withdrew, leaving the bid of Croatia, Denmark and Norway unopposed.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] Croatia, Denmark and Norway were chosen as the hosts on 28 February 2020 at the IHF Council meeting in [Cairo, Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo,_Egypt).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] This edition marks the first time Norway hosts the World Men's Handball Championship, the second for Croatia, and the third for Denmark.

## Venues

The bid contained 11 cities: [Zagreb](55906.md), [Split](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split,_Croatia), [Varaždin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vara%C5%BEdin), [Poreč](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pore%C4%8D) and [Dubrovnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubrovnik) in Croatia;[^cite_note-14] [Copenhagen](5166.md) and [Herning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herning) in Denmark; and [Trondheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trondheim), [Stavanger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavanger), [Drammen](178144.md) and [Bærum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A6rum) in Norway; with the opening match and final taking place in Bærum.[^cite_note-15]

On 25 January 2024, Denmark announced that the [Jyske Bank Boxen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jyske_Bank_Boxen) would be their only venue for the competition.[^cite_note-danskhåndbold-16] Then, two days later, Norway announced the [Telenor Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unity_Arena) would be their only venue for the tournament, after negotiations with [Trondheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trondheim) fell through due to financial reasons.[^cite_note-17]

On 8 April 2024, the [IHF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Handball_Federation) announced the venues, with [Bærum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A6rum), [Herning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herning), [Zagreb](55906.md), [Varaždin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vara%C5%BEdin) and [Poreč](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pore%C4%8D). being confirmed as hosts cities. The opening game will be in [Herning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herning) and the final will be in [Bærum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A6rum).[^cite_note-Schedule-18][^cite_note-auto2-19][^cite_note-auto1-20][^cite_note-auto-21] During the preparations for the tournament, the [Telenor Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unity_Arena) was renamed the Unity Arena for the tournament.[^cite_note-22]

## Qualification

Main article: [2025 World Men's Handball Championship qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Men's_Handball_Championship_qualification)

The World Championship hosts were directly qualified, along with the reigning world champions. Since there were three organisers, all from Europe, the number of compulsory places for Europe was reduced by two: 2 instead of default 4. The number of compulsory places awarded to other continental confederation remained unchanged. In regards to the 12 performance spots, and based on the results of the [2023 Men's World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_World_Men's_Handball_Championship), [Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Handball_Federation) received 11 more spots, while [Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Handball_Confederation) took 1.

The slot allocation was as follows:

- [AHF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Handball_Federation) (Asia): 4 slots

- [CAHB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Handball_Confederation) (Africa): 5 slots

- [EHF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Handball_Federation) (Europe): 17 slots (including the three co-hosts)

- [NACHC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_America_and_Caribbean_Handball_Confederation) (North America and the Caribbean): 1 slot

- [OCHF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceania_Continent_Handball_Federation) (Oceania): 0 slots

- [SCAHC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_and_Central_America_Handball_Confederation) (South and Central America): 3 slots

- Wildcards: 2 slots

With the exception of [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus_men's_national_handball_team) and [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia_men's_national_handball_team), who are banned of the IHF due to the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine), all remaining [IHF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Handball_Federation) member associations were eligible to enter qualification. In total, 83 nations have entered the continental championships and European qualifiers, which act as world championship qualification.

The [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_men's_national_handball_team) made back to back appearances for the first time in 30 years. ### Wildcards

After no Oceanian team achieved a top 5 finish in the [Asian Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Asian_Men's_Handball_Championship), two wildcards were given out by the [IHF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Handball_Federation).

- **  [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland_men's_national_handball_team)**

- ** [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_men's_national_handball_team)**

As the host country of the [2028 Summer Olympics](12695048.md), the first wildcard was given to [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_men's_national_handball_team) on 18 October 2018.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29] Regarding the second wildcard, 12 non-classificated nations reportedly applied.[^cite_note-30] On 23 May 2024, it was announced that the second wildcard was given to Switzerland due to multiple reasons: their sporting merit, their commercial reach, their sporting project and also due to the fact that the proposal made by the Swiss broadcast partner was the most commercially interesting for the IHF.[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33] Prior to their selection, [Switzerland's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland_men's_national_handball_team) coach, [Andy Schmid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Schmid), stated that after their narrow penalty shootout loss to [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia_men's_national_handball_team), the Swiss' performance merited a wildcard.[^cite_note-34]

## Draw



The [Vatroslav Lisinski Concert Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatroslav_Lisinski_Concert_Hall) in [Zagreb](55906.md) hosted the draw.

The draw took place at 19\:30 [CET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Time) in [Zagreb](55906.md), Croatia on 29 May 2024 at the [Vatroslav Lisinski Concert Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatroslav_Lisinski_Concert_Hall).[^cite_note-auto3-35][^cite_note-36] Originally scheduled for the 1 June,[^cite_note-37] it was changed a few weeks later to 29 May.[^cite_note-auto2-19] The guests for the draw were Croatian goalkeeper [Dominik Kuzmanović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominik_Kuzmanovi%C4%87), head coach of the Croatian national team, [Dagur Sigurðsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dagur_Sigur%C3%B0sson), Norwegian national team player, [Alexander Blonz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Blonz) and former Danish international player, [Morten Stig Christensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morten_Stig_Christensen), who all assisted the draw.[^cite_note-38]

## Final ranking and awards

Places 1 to 4 and 25 to 32 were decided by play-off or knock-out. The losers of the quarter finals were ranked 5th to 8th according to the places in the main round, points gained and goal difference. Teams finishing third in the main round were ranked 9th to 12th, teams finishing fourth in the main round were ranked 13th to 16th, teams finishing fifth in the main round were ranked 17th to 20th and teams ranked sixth were ranked 21st to 24th. In case of a tie in points gained, the goal difference of the main round was taken into account, then number of goals scored. If teams were still equal, number of points gained in the preliminary round was considered followed by the goal difference and then number of goals scored in the preliminary round.

## Preparations

Croatian host city, [Varaždin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vara%C5%BEdin) signed their hosting contract on 26 June 2024.[^cite_note-52] In November 2024, host city [Poreč](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pore%C4%8D) also signed their contract.[^cite_note-53][^cite_note-54] In December 2024, [Croatian Handball Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatian_Handball_Federation), [Varaždin County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vara%C5%BEdin_County), [Međimurje County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Me%C4%91imurje_County) and [Čakovec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%8Cakovec) signed a joint co-hosting contract.[^cite_note-Ugovor-55]

On 22 October 2024, the official ball was released.[^cite_note-56]

On 22 November 2024, the Norwegian government allotted 10 million [NOK](64149.md) for the tournament.[^cite_note-57]

### Tickets

The first phase of ticket sales started on 15 March 2024 in [Croatia](5573.md),[^cite_note-58][^cite_note-danskhåndbold-16][^cite_note-auto1-20][^cite_note-59][^cite_note-60] while the sales started on 8 April 2024 in [Denmark](76972.md) for people who made a federation account.[^cite_note-auto2-19] General sales in Denmark commenced on 12 April 2024. Norway started their ticket sales on 12 April 2024.[^cite_note-auto-21]

Another round of sales in Croatia started on 5 June 2024.[^cite_note-61]
