**Abdolrahim Mousavi** (23 June 1960 – 28 February 2026) was an Iranian military officer who served as [Chief of Staff](50548383.md) of the [Iranian Armed Forces](14305018.md) from June 2025 until his death. He assumed the position following the death of his predecessor, [Mohammad Bagheri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Bagheri_\(general\)), during the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War). Previously, he served as commander-in-chief of the [Islamic Republic of Iran Army](48259216.md) from 2017 to 2025.

## Early life

Abdolrahim Mousavi was born in 1960 in an [underprivileged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty) area of [Qom](638742.md). His father owned a spinning workshop, where Mousavi began working at the age of six. He married at the age of 23 and had two sons.

Mousavi held a professional [football](10568.md) refereeing license and had coaching experience. However, despite his deep passion for football and the national team, severe stress prevented him from watching the [Iranian national football team's](272865.md) matches.

## Career

### Early years

Mousavi's military career began following the [Islamic Revolution](347268.md) in 1979.[^cite_note-chief-1] During the 1980–88 [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War), he served in the [Iranian Army](48259216.md)'s [artillery](2508.md) units on various fronts, including with the [28th Kurdistan Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/28th_Infantry_Division_of_Kordestan) in [Kurdistan province](448112.md), and with the [33rd Artillery Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_of_the_Iranian_Army) in [Khuzestan province](448079.md).[^cite_note-chief-1]

From 1999 to 2005, he served as the Chief of the Joint Staff of the Ground Forces.[^cite_note-chief-1] From 2008 to 2016, he was Deputy Commander-in-Chief of the Iranian Army.[^cite_note-chief-1] From 2016 to 2017, he was deputy chief of staff of the [Iranian Armed Forces](14305018.md).[^cite_note-chief-1][^cite_note-aftabir.com-2] In 2017, he was promoted from Brigadier General to Major General, and from August 2017 to June 2025, he served as the chief of staff of the Ground Forces.[^cite_note-chief-1]

### Commander-in-Chief of the Iranian Army

In April 2017, Mousavi was promoted from Brigadier General to Major General and made the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of Iran's ground forces.[^cite_note-chief-1] In August 2017, Mousavi said that the "[martyrdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martyr)-seeking and [jihadi](16203.md) spirit of the Iranian people" made it impossible for the United States and its allies to attack Iran militarily.[^cite_note-imp-3] In January 2018, he referred to the United States as the "[Great Satan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Satan)", and stated that his “greatest wish is the [annihilation of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destruction_of_Israel_in_Iranian_policy)".[^cite_note-wish-4]

In September 2019, Mousavi said that the United States is "like an animal that would chase after you if you run away, and would run away if you attack them".[^cite_note-abde-5] Following the [assassination of Qasem Soleimani](62726668.md) in January 2020, Mousavi opined that the United States lacked the “courage to initiate” a conflict with Iran.[^cite_note-dni-6]

Mousavi stated in April 2023 that [Israel](9282173.md) was "too small to be considered a threat to the Islamic Republic of Iran,” as reported by *Middle East Monitor*.[^cite_note-small-7] In May 2025 Mousavi dismissed comments by Israeli officials on Iran as "bluster", and said that "the Israeli leadership lacks the capacity to harm the greatness of Iran."[^cite_note-bluster-8]

### Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces

On 13 June 2025, [Supreme Leader of Iran](36814344.md) [Ali Khamenei](385653.md) appointed Mousavi as the [chief of staff](50548383.md) of the armed forces, following the killing of Mousavi's predecessor [Mohammad Bagheri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Bagheri_\(general\)) in the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War).[^cite_note-generalstaff-9]

### Sanctions

In March 2023, Mousavi was sanctioned by the [U.S. Department of the Treasury](53667.md)'s [Office of Foreign Assets Control](231045.md) (OFAC) for serious [human rights abuses](80259436.md). It said that, in both the [2019–2020 Iranian protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%932020_Iranian_protests) and the [2022–2023 Mahsa Amini protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahsa_Amini_protests), troops under Mousavi's command "used [machine guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_gun) to fire indiscriminately into crowds of protestors".[^cite_note-cro-10] He was also listed on sanctions lists of the European Union, United Kingdom, and Australia for serious human rights abuses.[^cite_note-turns-11]

## Death

On 1 March 2026, Iranian state television announced that Mousavi had been killed during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-12] It was reported that he had been killed along with the Ayatollah and the defense minister [Aziz Nasirzadeh](63488731.md) in the Ayatollah's office.[^cite_note-13]

## Awards and honours

On 10 March 2024, Mousavi was awarded an [Order of Fath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Fath) medal by Supreme Leader [Ali Khamenei](385653.md) for "improving the defence, combat, and deterrence power" of the Iranian armed forces.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-medals2024-15]
