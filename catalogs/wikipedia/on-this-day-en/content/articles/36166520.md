The **Secretary of national defense** ([Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language): *Kalihim ng Tanggulang Pambansa*) is the head of the [Department of National Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_National_Defense_\(Philippines\)) of the Philippines and a member of the [president](271126.md)'s [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_Philippines).[^cite_note-3]

The current secretary is former [Philippine House representative](293605.md) of [Tarlac's 1st congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarlac's_1st_congressional_district), [Gilbert Teodoro](21940341.md). He assumed office on June 5, 2023[^cite_note-TeodoroDNDSecretaryAppointment-1][^cite_note-revamp-2] and has previously served the same position under the [presidency of Gloria Macapagal Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Gloria_Macapagal_Arroyo) from August 2007 to November 2009.

Historically, this cabinet position has been a stepping stone to the Philippine presidency. Two previous secretaries of national defense were elected president: [Ramon Magsaysay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramon_Magsaysay) and [Fidel Ramos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidel_V._Ramos). Several others ran for the presidency: [Alejo Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejo_Santos), [Juan Ponce Enrile](3322486.md), [Renato de Villa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renato_de_Villa), [Norberto Gonzales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norberto_Gonzales), and [Gilbert Teodoro](21940341.md).

## Powers and functions

The 1987 Executive Order No. 292 gives the national defense secretary executive supervision of the [Armed Forces of the Philippines](23448.md), the [Office of Civil Defense](51079847.md), the [Philippine Veterans Affairs Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Veterans_Affairs_Office), the [National Defense College of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Defense_College_of_the_Philippines), and the [Government Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Arsenal).
