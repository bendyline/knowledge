The **Ministry of Defence of Bosnia and Herzegovina** ([Serbo-Croatian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbo-Croatian_language): Ministarstvo odbrane Bosne i Hercegovine / Министарство одбране Босне и Херцеговине) is the [government ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_ministry) responsible for the [Armed Forces of Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) and defence affairs in [Bosnia and Herzegovina](3463.md).

## History

Prior to the creation of the Ministry of Defence of Bosnia and Herzegovina, from 1945 to 1992, the [Ministry of Defence of Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(Yugoslavia\)) was responsible for the defence of the [Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia).

Following the [independence of Bosnia and Herzegovina from Yugoslavia in 1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Bosnian_independence_referendum), the Ministry of Defence of the [Republic of Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) was formed, with [Munib Bisić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munib_Bisi%C4%87) appointed as minister. The ministry had the newly formed [Army of the Republic of Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_the_Republic_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) at its disposal. In addition to the Ministry of Defence of the Republic of Bosnia and Herzegovina, there were two other ministries of defence in Bosnia and Herzegovina: the [Ministry of Defence of the Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(Republika_Srpska\)) and the Ministry of Defence of [Herzeg-Bosnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatian_Republic_of_Herzeg-Bosnia).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

After the signing of the [Dayton Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton_Agreement) in December 1995 and the end of the [Bosnian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnian_War), the [Army of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_the_Federation_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) was formed by merging the [Croatian Defence Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatian_Defence_Council) with the [Army of the Republic of Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_the_Republic_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina).[^cite_note-4]

On 1 December 2005, the [Armed Forces of Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) were formed by merging the Army of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina and the [Army of Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_Republika_Srpska) under one ministry.

Since 25 January 2023, [Zukan Helez](72759206.md) ([SDP BiH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina)) has served as Minister of Defence of Bosnia and Herzegovina.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Organization

The Ministry of Defence of Bosnia and Herzegovina consists of eight sectors and six offices, three of which are religious services.

- Inspectorate General

- Policy and Plans Sector

- Sector for International Cooperation

- Sector for Intelligence and Security Affairs

- Sector for Command, Control and Communications, Computers and Information Management

- Personnel Management Department

- Procurement and Logistics Sector

- Finance and Budget Sector

- Internal Audit Office

- Public Relations Office

- Department for General and Joint Affairs

- Office of the Military Mufti

- Office of Orthodox Pastoral Care

- Office of Catholic Pastoral Care
