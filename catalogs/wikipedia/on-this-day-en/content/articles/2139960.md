**Yobe**([Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/9/9c/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Yobe.wav/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Yobe.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a [state](226734.md) located in northeastern [Nigeria](21383.md). A mainly [agricultural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural) state, Yobe as created on 27 August 1991. Yobe State was carved out of [Borno State](2227628.md). The capital of Yobe State is [Damaturu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damaturu), and its largest city by population is [Potiskum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potiskum), while the largest by land area is [Fune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fune). The Potiskum [local government area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_areas_of_Nigeria) is a place of farmers and marketers; one of the largest cattle markets in Africa is located in Potiskum.

## Geography

The state borders four states: [Bauchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bauchi_\(city\)), [Borno](2227628.md), [Gombe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gombe_State), and [Jigawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jigawa_State). Yobe State shares borders with [Borno State](2227628.md) to the east for about 421 km, [Gombe State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gombe_State) to the south for 140 km (in the vicinity of [Gongola River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gongola_River)), Bauchi State for 188 km (117 miles) and [Jigawa State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jigawa_State) for 193 km (120 miles) to the west and [the Republic of Niger](21373.md) to the north for about 352 km.[^cite_note-5] It [borders to the north](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger-Nigeria_border) the [Diffa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffa_Region) and [Zinder Regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinder_Region) of [Niger](21373.md). Because the state lies mainly in the dry [savanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savanna) belt, conditions are hot and dry for most of the year, except in the southern part of the state which has more annual rainfall.

### Climate

The [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) condition of Yobe is warm with daily temperature of 37 °C (98.6 °F). November being the sunniest month and rainy day is between August and December.[^cite_note-6]

## History

Yobe State came into being on 27 August 1991. It was carved out of the old Borno State by the [Babangida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Babangida) administration. Yobe State was created because the old Borno State was one of Nigeria's largest states in terms of land area and was therefore considered to be too large for easy administration and meaningful development. [Ethnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_group) rivalries within the old Borno State also contributed to the decision.[^cite_note-7]

On 3 September 2024, [Islamic State](9087364.md) militants [attacked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarmuwa_massacre) a village in Yobe State, killing at least 130 villagers accused of collaborating with the Nigerian military.[^cite_note-8]

## Boko Haram issues

On 14 May 2013, [President](516088.md) [Goodluck Jonathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodluck_Jonathan) declared a [state of emergency](298608.md) in Yobe State along with nearby Borno and [Adamawa States](905832.md), due to the [jihadist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jihadist) [terrorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist) network [Boko Haram](23769768.md)'s [insurgency](27868253.md).[^cite_note-9] Boko Haram's leader [Abubakar Shekau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abubakar_Shekau) was born in Shekau village of Yobe.[^cite_note-10]

Boko Haram's attacks in Yobe include those in [Damaturu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damaturu) [in November 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Damaturu_attacks), [December 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/December_2011_Nigeria_clashes) [and June 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damaturu_prison_break), [Gadaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gadaka) [in December 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/December_2011_Northern_Nigeria_attacks), Potiskum [in December 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/December_2012_shootings_in_Northern_Nigeria), [November 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Yobe_State_attacks) [and July 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5_July_2015_Nigeria_attacks), [Mamudo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamudo) [in July 2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yobe_State_school_shooting), [Gujba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujba) [in September 2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujba_college_massacre), [Buni Yadi](74426701.md) [in February](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Government_College_attack) [and May 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_2014_Buni_Yadi_attack) and [Dapchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dapchi) [in 2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dapchi_schoolgirls_kidnapping).

## Economy

While Yobe state is an agricultural state, it also has rich mineral deposits, including [gypsum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gypsum) in [Fika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fika,_Nigeria) and [kaolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaolin) in [Fune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fune) local government and very rich agricultural resources as well. The State's agricultural produce include [gum arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gum_arabic), [groundnuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambara_groundnut), [beans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beans), and [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton). The State also has one of the largest [cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle) [markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_\(place\)) in [West Africa](67393.md), located in Potiskum.[^cite_note-11]

## Religion



Central Mosque Damaturu

The inhabitants of Yobe are mainly [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim) and [Sharia](28840.md) law was established in the state in 2000 by Governor [Bukar Abba Ibrahim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bukar_Abba_Ibrahim). However, there are [Christians](18337522.md) in the State although no Roman Catholic diocese has its seat in the state.[^cite_note-zodml.org-14]

## Politics

The state government is headed by a Governor who is elected through a democratically process. The Governor works closely with the [State House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yobe_State_House_of_Assembly), which is the law-making arm in the state.

Just like every other state in the [Federal Republic of Nigeria](21383.md), the electoral system of Yobe State is that of a modified two-round system. To be elected in the first round, a candidate must receive the plurality of the vote and over 25% of the vote in at least two -third of the State local government Areas. If no candidate passes this threshold, a second round will be held between the top candidate and the next candidate to have received a plurality of votes in the highest number of local government areas.[^cite_note-24]

## Natural resources

Yobe State has deposits of [trona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trona) and [gypsum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gypsum).[^cite_note-thenationonlineng.net-25]

## Healthcare

The state's administration has improved healthcare delivery lifted a moratorium on hiring and provided incentives for staff retention. The free-drugs policy and decentralised drug supply have increased access to healthcare, underscoring the state's dedication to renovating and building new primary healthcare facilities to make healthcare more widely available and reasonably priced.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

## Desertification

Residents of Yobe State in Northern Nigeria are worried about [desertification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desertification) because it could have a negative impact on their quality of life.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31]
