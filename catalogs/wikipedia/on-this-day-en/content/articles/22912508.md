**Motoarena Toruń im. Mariana Rosego** is a multi-use stadium in [Toruń](31258.md), Poland. It is currently used mostly for [motorcycle speedway](230182.md) matches and it is used for car racing and concerts and it is the home stadium of [KS Toruń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KS_Toru%C5%84) in the [Speedway Ekstraliga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedway_Ekstraliga). The stadium has a capacity of 15,500 people [^cite_note-1] and was opened on 3 May 2009. It is named after [Marian Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marian_Rose), former speedway rider from Toruń.&#x20;

The speedway track is 318 metres (348 yards) long. Since 2010, a round of the [Speedway Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedway_Grand_Prix) has been held at the Motoarena.[^cite_note-2]&#x20;
