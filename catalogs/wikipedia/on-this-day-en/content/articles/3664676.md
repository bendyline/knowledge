The **Parliament of Eswatini** ([Swazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swazi_language): *Libandla*) consists of two chambers:

- The [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Eswatini) (Indlu yeTimphunga, [Upper Chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_house))

- The [House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Assembly_of_Eswatini) (Indlu Yemhlangano, [Lower Chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house))

The Houses of Parliament are located in [Lobamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobamba).



Outside the Eswatini Parliament in [ Lobamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobamba)

It was established in 1967 when the [Legislative Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Council_of_Swaziland) was disbanded and the bicameral parliament was established in the new [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Swaziland).[^cite_note-1]
