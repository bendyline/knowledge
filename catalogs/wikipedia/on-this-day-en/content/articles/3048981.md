**Dorogobuzh** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Дорогобуж; [Belarusian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusian_language): Дарагабуж) is a historic [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) and the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of [Dorogobuzhsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorogobuzhsky_District) in [Smolensk Oblast](480938.md), Russia, straddling the [Dnieper River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnieper_River) and located 125 kilometers (78 mi) east of [Smolensk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smolensk), the administrative center of the [oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblast). Population:  10,720 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2010Census-2] 12,250 ([2002 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Russian_census));[^cite_note-PopCensus-7] 12,254 ([1989 Soviet census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Soviet_census)).[^cite_note-Census1989-8]

## History



Pre-1917 view of the [Dnieper River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnieper_River) in Dorogobuzh

First mentioned in 1150, it was established as a fortress defending eastern approaches to [Smolensk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smolensk). It was located in the [Principality of Smolensk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Smolensk), which in 1404 became part of [Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Lithuania).[^cite_note-sgk-9] It passed to the [Grand Duchy of Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Moscow) after the [Battle of Vedrosha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Vedrosha) in 1500.[^cite_note-sgk-9] In 1508, [Vasily III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasily_III_of_Russia) sent Italian masters to build a wooden fort there.

During the [Time of Troubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_of_Troubles), Dorogobuzh passed between Lithuania and various Muscovite factions several times,[^cite_note-sgk-9] and was ravaged, with its population reduced to ten people in 1614. It was captured by [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsardom_of_Russia) in 1613, and then re-captured by Polish Prince [Władysław IV Vasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W%C5%82adys%C5%82aw_IV_Vasa) in 1617.[^cite_note-sgk-9] Within the [Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish%E2%80%93Lithuanian_Commonwealth), it was part of the [Smolensk Voivodeship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smolensk_Voivodeship). During the [Smolensk War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smolensk_War), in 1632, it was [besieged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Dorogobuzh) and captured by Russia, however, it was restored to the Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth by the [Treaty of Polyanovka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Polyanovka) in 1634.[^cite_note-sgk-9] In 1667, it passed to Russia.[^cite_note-sgk-9]

During the [Napoleonic Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleonic_Wars), it was captured by [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_French_Empire) troops in August 1812, and re-captured by Russia in October 1812.[^cite_note-sgk-9] In the late 19th century, the town held an annual [fair](504805.md) for grain, horses and cattle.[^cite_note-sgk-9]

Dorogobuzh was occupied by the [German Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht) during [World War II](32927.md) first from October 5, 1941 to February 15, 1942 (when it was liberated by three partisan detachments and some Soviet troops), then again from June 7, 1942 to September 1, 1943. From 1941 to 1943, the Germans operated a [forced labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forced_labour_under_German_rule_during_World_War_II) camp in the town,[^cite_note-10] and in April-May 1942 also the Dulag 184 [prisoner-of-war camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_prisoner-of-war_camps_in_World_War_II).[^cite_note-11]

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), Dorogobuzh serves as the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of [Dorogobuzhsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorogobuzhsky_District).[^cite_note-SmolenskO_admlist-1] As an administrative division, it is incorporated within Dorogobuzhsky District as **Dorogobuzhskoye [Urban Settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_of_district_significance)**.[^cite_note-SmolenskO_admlist-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), this administrative unit also has urban settlement status and is a part of Dorogobuzhsky Municipal District.[^cite_note-SmolenskODorogobuzhskyD_mun-3]
