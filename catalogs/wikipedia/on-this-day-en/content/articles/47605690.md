**Miao Hua** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 苗华; born November 1955) is a former admiral of the Chinese [People's Liberation Army Navy](24918.md) (PLAN). He served as the director of the [Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission](49138500.md) from October 2017 to June 2025. Previously, he served as [political commissar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_commissar) of the PLA Navy from December 2014 to September 2017, and political commissar of the [Lanzhou Military Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanzhou_Military_Region) in 2014.

Miao had also served as a member of the [18th Central Commission for Discipline Inspection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/18th_Central_Commission_for_Discipline_Inspection), the [19th Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19th_Central_Committee_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party)[^cite_note-ifeng-2] and the [20th Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_Central_Committee_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party).[^cite_note-3] He was considered to have been a part of the [Fujian clique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fujian_clique) of the [Xi Jinping faction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi_Jinping_faction) in the CCP.[^cite_note-4]

## Biography

Miao Hua was born in November 1955 in [Fuzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuzhou), [Fujian](88585.md) Province. He is of [Rugao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugao), [Jiangsu](166412.md) ancestry.[^cite_note-ifeng-2][^cite_note-china-5]

He enlisted in the PLA in December 1969, serving as a soldier in the 274th regiment of the 92nd division of the [31st Group Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/31st_Group_Army), in the [Nanjing Military Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_Military_Region). He joined the [Chinese Communist Party](7175.md) in September 1973. In the 1980s, he served as a [political commissar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_commissar) in several regiments. In the 1990s, he rose to director of the Political Department of the 93rd division, and then political commissar of the 91st division. In August 1999, Miao became director of the Political Department of the 31st Group Army, and attained the rank of major general in July 2001. He was made Political Commissar of the [12th Group Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_Group_Army) in July 2005.[^cite_note-ifeng-2][^cite_note-6]

Miao was appointed director of the Political Department of the [Lanzhou Military Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanzhou_Military_Region) in December 2010. In July 2012, he became deputy political commissar of the Lanzhou MR, and attained the rank of lieutenant general. In July 2014, he was promoted to political commissar of the Lanzhou MR, replacing General [Li Changcai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Changcai), who had retired.[^cite_note-ifeng-2][^cite_note-china-5]

Five months later, Miao was transferred from the [army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Liberation_Army_Ground_Force) to the [People's Liberation Army Navy](24918.md), and appointed political commissar of the navy.[^cite_note-ifeng-2] It was a highly unusual move, as navy political commissars, including his predecessor [Liu Xiaojiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liu_Xiaojiang), were normally promoted internally. Observers have interpreted the move as related to the fall of General [Xu Caihou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xu_Caihou), the former vice chairman of the [Central Military Commission](214345.md).[^cite_note-china-5] Miao spent most of his career in the [Nanjing Military Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_Military_Region), and was based in [Xiamen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiamen), Fujian Province around the same time when [Xi Jinping](2017814.md) served as deputy party secretary of Fujian. He worked for many years alongside Xi, who later became [CCP general secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Secretary_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party), the [top leader](262766.md) in 2012.[^cite_note-china-5]

On 31 July 2015, Miao Hua was promoted to admiral, the highest rank for Chinese military officers in active service, together with nine other officers.[^cite_note-7]

## Suspension

On 28 November 2024, the [Ministry of National Defense](33632648.md) announced the suspension of Miao, while he was being investigated for "serious violations of discipline."[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] On 30 April 2025, the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress announced that Miao was dismissed from the NPC.[^cite_note-10] In late May, a party official stated that Miao was suspected of "legal violations".[^cite_note-11] The Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission held a military representative conference on 14 March 2025 and decided to remove him as a representative in the 14th National People's Congress. On 27 June, the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress voted to remove Miao from the Central Military Commission.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] On October 17, 2025, the [Ministry of National Defense](33632648.md) announced that Miao was expelled from the CCP and the PLA for "serious violations of discipline and law".[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]
