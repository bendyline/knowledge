The **Ministry of Defense** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الدفاع, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Wizārat al-Difāʿ*) is a [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_\(Syria\)) ministry of [Syria](7515849.md), responsible for [defense affairs](9473138.md) and national security.[^cite_note-1] [Murhaf Abu Qasra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murhaf_Abu_Qasra), Minister of Defense holds the position of Deputy Commander-in-Chief of the [Army](3423927.md) and [Armed Forces](27479.md).[^cite_note-2]

## History

### Contemporary Syria

Following the [fall of the regime](78548066.md) of [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md) in December 2024, caretaker prime minister [Mohammed al-Bashir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_al-Bashir) has said the defense ministry would be restructured using former rebel factions and officers who defected from the former [Ba'athist](57134800.md) [armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Arab_Armed_Forces).[^cite_note-3]

On 16 July 2025, the [Israeli Air Force](177619.md) bombed the entrance and two of its facades to the [defense ministry headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hay'at_al-Arkan) in [Damascus](8914.md), amid the [July 2025 southern Syria clashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Syria_clashes_\(July_2025%E2%80%93present\)).[^cite_note-4]

### Incorporation of rebel groups

At the [Syrian Revolution Victory Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Revolution_Victory_Conference), which was held in late January 2025, the [Syrian caretaker government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_caretaker_government) announced the dissolution of all security agencies of the Ba'athist regime and all militias established by it. In addition, the [Syrian opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_opposition) groups are expected to be dissolved and merged into the ministry.[^cite_note-memri29jan-5] The Ministry of Defense later reported that over 70 armed factions across six regions in total agreed to participate in the new administration.[^cite_note-6]

However, one leader from the [Syrian National Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_National_Army) indicated that the dissolution of different groups will be done "gradually", while a rebel leader from [Daraa Governorate](5317030.md) confirmed that the rebel groups had been ordered to disband, but that the "implementation" needs more time until more clarity is provided on how the ministry will ultimately be structured.[^cite_note-7]

As of February 2025, the ministry has formed a committee focused on restructuring and managing assets, as well as a "sub-committee" whose purpose is to determine a new "internal structure" for the armed forces.[^cite_note-8]
