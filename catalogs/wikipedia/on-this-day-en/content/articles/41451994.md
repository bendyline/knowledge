**Ahmed Moallim Fiqi** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Axmed Macallin Fiqi*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): أحمد معلم الفقهي) is a politician from Somalia who currently serves as defense minister and has served as [foreign minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Somalia\)) from 2024 to 2025.[^cite_note-1] Formerly a member of the [Islamic Courts Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Courts_Union), Fiqi is currently a member of the Somali Federal Parliament. From May 2011 to March 2013, he served as Director of the [National Intelligence and Security Agency](81014.md) (NISA). After resigning from the position, he was provisionally replaced by [Abdikarin Dahir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdikarin_Dahir).[^cite_note-Snicsd-2][^cite_note-Gsnibto-3][^cite_note-4]

In 2015, Ahmed Moallim Fiqi ran for the office of [President of Galmudug State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_presidents_of_Galmudug).[^cite_note-\:0-5] Ahmed lost to his contender [Abdikarim Hussein Guled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdikarim_Hussein_Guled).[^cite_note-\:1-6] Guled won by 49 to 40 votes over former Intel. Chief Ahmed Moallim Fiqi.

## Early life and education

Fiqi was born in 1971 in [Galkayo, Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galkayo,_Somalia), His family hailed from the [Sa'ad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacad) [Habargidir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habargidir) clan. Fiqi took his primary and secondary school in [Mogadishu](60154.md). In 1988, after his two-year [Military service](938728.md) he was admitted to the College of Law at the [Somali National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_National_University). Fiqi didn't complete his studies due to the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md). He then went to [Sudan](27421.md) and studied agriculture at the [University of Khartoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Khartoum).[^cite_note-7] Details regarding the completion of formal degrees have not been widely documented in Publicly available sources.

Fiqi speaks [Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language), Arabic, and English.

## Political career

Ahmed Fiqi started his political career during the [Islamic Courts Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Courts_Union). He was very close friend of former ICU leader [Sharif Sheikh Ahmed](5442210.md). Later when [Sharif Sheikh Ahmed](5442210.md) become president,[^cite_note-8] Fiqi served as the Somali ambassador to Sudan from 2010 to 2011. He served as Director General of the [National Intelligence and Security Agency](81014.md) and Presidential Candidate for the [Galmudug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galmudug) State of Somalia.

In early 2015, Ahmed Fiqi co-founded [Daljir Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daljir_Party) with [Sharif Sheikh Ahmed](5442210.md) and [Hassan Moalim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Moalim).[^cite_note-9] Later Ahmed Fiqi ran for the office of [President of Galmudug State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_presidents_of_Galmudug).[^cite_note-\:0-5] He lost to his contender [Abdikarim Hussein Guled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdikarim_Hussein_Guled).[^cite_note-\:1-6] Guled won by 49 to 40 votes over former Intelligence Chief Ambassador Ahmed Moallim Fiqi.

On 28 November 2016, Ahmed Moalim Fiqi was elected[^cite_note-10] as member of the [Federal Parliament of Somalia](36817385.md) from [Galmudug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galmudug) in [Adado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adado), Somalia.

Fiqi's [Daljir Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daljir_Party) formed a coalition with former Somali President [Hassan Mohamud](36973989.md)'s [Peace and Development Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_and_Development_Party). They launched[^cite_note-11] [Union for Peace and Development Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_for_Peace_and_Development_Party) in October 2018.

Fiqi has been a prominent and sometimes polarizing figure in Somali politics. During his tenure as Minister of Interior (2022–2024), he played a central role in federal-state relations and reconciliation processes, including engagements with federal member states such as Puntland. His approach drew both support and criticism from political stakeholders.[^cite_note-12]

In 2024, he was appointed Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, where he oversaw Somalia’s diplomatic engagement at the United Nations, including its successful bid for a non-permanent seat on the UN Security Council. He also took a firm position on regional issues, including Somalia’s response to Ethiopia’s memorandum of understanding with Somaliland.[^cite_note-13]

Fiqi’s frequent reassignment across key ministries has been widely noted in Somali political discourse. Analysts have described his appointments as reflecting his perceived reliability within the executive, particularly by President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud. At the same time, critics argue that repeated redeployments may point to shifting political alignments and raise questions about continuity within government institutions.[^cite_note-14]

### Foreign minister (2024–2025)

On 7 April 2024, Fiqi was appointed [Minister of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Somalia\)) of Somalia.[^cite_note-15]
