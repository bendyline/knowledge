**Pénjamo** ([Purepecha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purepecha_language): *Penlamu*, "place of [ahuehuetes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahuehuete)") is a city in [Guanajuato](412827.md), Mexico. It is the seat of Pénjamo municipality. It was cofounded in 1549 by [Guamares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guamare), [Purépechas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pur%C3%A9pecha), and [Otomis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otomi) prior to the outbreak of the [Chichimeca war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chichimeca_War).[^cite_note-1]

The city is located in the southwest of Guanajuato, and accounts for a total of 164,261.27 hectares of land, or 5.20% of the total land in the state. It borders the municipalities of [Abasolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abasolo,_Guanajuato), [Cuerámaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuer%C3%A1maro), and [Manuel Doblado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Doblado,_Guanajuato) and the states of [Jalisco](16020434.md) and [Michoacán](195805.md). According to the 2000 census, the total population of the municipality was 144,426; however, in the last census, conducted in 2010, the total population of the city was near 41,000, the majority of which devotes themselves to the services, trade, tourist services. The motto of the city is "Cradle of Hidalgo" (*"Cuna de Hidalgo"*), because in 1753, [Miguel Hidalgo y Costilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_Hidalgo_y_Costilla), known as the [Father of the Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Father_of_the_Nation), was born in the [Hacienda de Corralejo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacienda_de_Corralejo?action=edit\&redlink=1) near the city.

The [locality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Localities_of_Mexico) of Santa Ana Pacueco, which is part of Pénjamo, is linked to [La Piedad](1491749.md) in Michoacán by seven bridges over the [Lerma River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lerma_River). Because of this, the two cities have common needs and problems. To address this, the [Mexican Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Congress) created the Pénjamo-[La Piedad](1491749.md) Metropolitan Area in 2010. The area had a population of 229,289 people according to the 2005 [INEGI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INEGI) census. The [locality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Localities_of_Mexico) of Santa Ana Pacueco is linked to La Piedad by seven bridges over the [Lerma River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lerma_River).

## Geography

Pénjamo has an elevation in approximately 1,700 meters above sea level, but in some places, like [Sierra de Pénjamo](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra%20de%20P%C3%A9njamo), it reaches an elevation of 2,400 meters above sea level.

The city is located near [Mexican Federal Highway 90](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Federal_Highway_90). It is approximately 40 minutes from the city of [Irapuato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irapuato) and 130 kilometres (81 mi) from [León](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le%C3%B3n,_Guanajuato).

A large part of the city lies on the slopes of Sierra de Pénjamo. The rest of the city is divided between hills and flat land. For this reason, some of Pénjamo's streets are very steep while others are straight.

## History

The territory that is now the municipality of Pénjamo was first settled by the [Guachichil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guachichil) and [Purépecha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pur%C3%A9pecha_people) peoples. The archeological site [Plazuelas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plazuelas) is 11 kilometres (6.8 mi) from the city of Pénjamo.

The city and the municipality were founded on November 12, 1542 under the name of San Francisco de Penxamo by Diego Tomás de Jesuchihua, who was authorized by the Spanish king [Carlos V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_V,_Holy_Roman_Emperor). The [Purépecha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pur%C3%A9pecha) name for the city was Penlamu, meaning "place of [ahuehuetes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahuehuete)".

Miguel Hidalgo, recognized as the father of Mexico, was born in the former [Hacienda de Corralejo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacienda_de_Corralejo?action=edit\&redlink=1), which was under Pénjamo's jurisdiction. Today the estate is used to distill [Tequila Corralejo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tequila_Corralejo).[^cite_note-2]

In 1811, during the [Mexican War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_War_of_Independence), the guerilla leader [Albino García Ramos](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albino%20Garc%C3%ADa%20Ramos) fought a battle against royalist forces in Pénjamo. In 1817, General [Martín Francisco Javier Mina y Larrea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mart%C3%ADn_Francisco_Javier_Mina_y_Larrea) was executed by Spanish soldiers in Sierra de Pénjamo.

Pénjamo was burned down completely in 1815, and rebuilding began in 1830. On May 22, 1857, Pénjamo was categorized as a town. It was recognized as a city in 1906 by decree of the governor [Joaquín Obregón González](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joaqu%C3%ADn%20Obreg%C3%B3n%20Gonz%C3%A1lez).

During the [Second World War](32927.md), the city experienced [blackouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackout_\(wartime\)) to avoid bombardments.

seal of Pénjamo

The seal of the city is a shield with an [ahuehuete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxodium_mucronatum) tree with its roots extending below the ground. Underneath the tree is written 1542, the year of the city's foundation. In the background is a drawing of Cerro Grande, one of the mountains in Sierra de Pénjamo against a blue sky with clouds and a five-pointed star. The year 1753 is written above the star, representing the year Miguel Hidalgo was born. At the top of the shield, above the image, are the words "Cuna de Hidalgo San Francisco de Penxamo". At the bottom of the shield are the latin words "Nobilis, Fidelis, Fortis" ("Noble, Faithful, Strong").

Pénjamo is a tourist center. The city is part of [Route 2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celebration_of_Mexican_political_anniversaries_in_2010), which links important geographic locations related to the Mexican War of Independence and Revolution.

The following tourist attractions exist in or near Pénjamo:&#x20;

- Corralejo: The birthplace of Miguel Hidalgo. Celebrations are held at the estate during the first week of May, when Hidalgo's birth is celebrated.

* Plazuelas: An archeological site

  [^cite_note-3]

  &#x20;and museum that is open to the public. The site has a large, rectangular plaza and several [pyramids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramids), as well as a [ball court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesoamerican_ball_game). Dances and events are held in spring.

- Sierra de Pénjamo

* *La Feria de Mayo* or *Fiestas de Mayo* (May Festival): The fair occurs from the last days of April until the first third of May every year, in commemoration of Hidalgo's birthday. The fair includes art exhibitions, [jaripeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaripeo) events, and [bullfighting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullfighting).

- *Festival Cultural de la Fundación* (Founding Festival): The festival lasts from November 6 to 13 and celebrates the day the city was founded, November 12, 1542. The festival features art and photograph exhibitions, live music and theatrical shows, a parade and fireworks, [motocross racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motocross_racing), and jaripeo events.

## Notable people

- Don [Miguel Hidalgo y Costilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_Hidalgo_y_Costilla) - leader of the Mexican War of Independence (1753-1811)

- [Joaquín Pardavé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joaqu%C3%ADn_Pardav%C3%A9) - cinematographic and theatrical director, composer and actor (1900-1955)

- [Luis Navarro Origel](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis%20Navarro%20Origel) - prominent [cristero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristero) during the Cristero War (1897-1928)

- [Ignacio Vázquez Torres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ignacio_V%C3%A1zquez_Torres) - Mexican congressman (1939-2023)
