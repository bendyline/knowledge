**Neuves-Maisons** (French pronunciation: [\[nœv mɛzɔ̃\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French), literally *New Houses*) is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in the [Meurthe-et-Moselle](90583.md) [department](38523.md) in [Grand Est](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Est) in North-Eastern [France](5843419.md), on the banks of the [Moselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moselle). It is the seat of the [Canton of Neuves-Maisons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Neuves-Maisons) and neighbored by the commune [Pont-Saint-Vincent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pont-Saint-Vincent) on the other side of the Moselle.

## History

The city had a great steel industry during the 19th and 20th century.

Neuves-Maisons erected a plaque in the memory of [Émilie Busquant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89milie_Busquant), a [feminist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feminism), [anarcho-syndicalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anarcho-syndicalist) and an [anti-colonial](302084.md) activist born in the area, on the fiftieth anniversary of her death in 2003. A 2015 documentary by director Rabah Zanoun introduced a French audience to her forgotten story.[^cite_note-3]
