# Office of the Special Envoy of the Secretary-General for Yemen

The **Office of the Special Envoy of the Secretary-General for Yemen** is a [Special Envoy of the Secretary-General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Envoy_of_the_Secretary-General) first appointed in 2012 to assist the [Secretary-General](162415.md) for [Yemen](350939.md).[^cite_note-1]

## List of envoys

**[Jamal Benomar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamal_Benomar)**: The first Special Envoy to Yemen was Jamal Benomar who began representing the Secretary General in Yemen during the summer of 2011 but was only formally appointed as a Special Envoy on 1 August 2012.[^cite_note-2] Benomar is from [Morocco](19291.md). Benomar was credited with helping Yemen avert civil war in 2011 and for helping organise the National Dialogue Conference. By the end of 2014 and the start of 2015, a full blown conflict had started in Yemen, which Benomar tried to mediate. Benomar resigned in April 2015 after failing to prevent the conflict.[^cite_note-3]

**[Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismail_Ould_Cheikh_Ahmed)**: On 25 April 2015, Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed was appointed Special Envoy to Yemen.[^cite_note-4] Ahmed is from [Mauritania](18778516.md). Ahmed studied economics at Maastricht University and Montpellier University and also studied human resources at Manchester University.[^cite_note-5] Between 2008 and 2012, Ahmed was a UN resident humanitarian and development co-ordinator in Syria. From 2012 to 2014, he occupied the same position in Yemen. In June 2017, the [Houthis](17240332.md) accused Mr. Ahmed of having abandoned his neutrality and warned him never to come to Yemen again. Ahmed left his post on 22 January 2018.[^cite_note-6] In June 2018, he was appointed Minister of Foreign Affairs in Mauritania.

**[Martin Griffiths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Griffiths)**: On 16 February 2018, Martin Griffiths was appointed Special Envoy to Yemen.[^cite_note-7] Griffiths is [British](31717.md) and is a career diplomat. Griffiths previously served as the first executive director of the [European Institute of Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Institute_of_Peace) from 2016 to September 2018.[^cite_note-8] In 1999, he helped launch the [Centre for Humanitarian Dialogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Humanitarian_Dialogue) in Geneva.[^cite_note-9] On 12 May 2021, United Nations Secretary-General [António Guterres](471806.md) announced that he had appointed Griffiths as Under-Secretary-General for Humanitarian Affairs and Emergency Relief Coordinator, Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA).[^cite_note-10]

**[Hans Grundberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Grundberg)**: On 6 August 2021, Hans Grundberg was appointment by António Guterres as his Special Envoy for Yemen. Grundberg who is from [Sweden](5058739.md) has served as ambassador of the [European Union](9317.md) to Yemen since 2019.[^cite_note-11]
