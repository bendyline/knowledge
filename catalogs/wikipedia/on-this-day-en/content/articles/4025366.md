The **Ministry of National Economy and Finance** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Υπουργείο Εθνικής Οικονομίας και Οικονομικών) is the [government department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) responsible for overseeing Greece's [public finances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_finance). The current minister is [Kyriakos Pierrakakis](61389979.md), a member of the [Hellenic Parliament](2449826.md) representing the [Athens A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_A) constituency for the [New Democracy party](424225.md).

The ministry was established in August 2023 through a renaming and reorganization of the former **Ministry of Finance** under the [Mitsotakis government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Cabinet_of_Kyriakos_Mitsotakis). The restructured ministry now incorporates entities previously managed by the [Ministry of Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Development_\(Greece\)).

## Minister's role



View of the Hellenic Ministry of Finance from Othonos Street, Athens

According to Article 73 of the [Constitution of Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Greece), any bills relating to pensions must be submitted by the Minister of Finance. According to Article 75 of the Constitution, any bill relating to expenditure or a reduction in revenue must not be introduced unless accompanied by a special report on the bill, signed by the Minister of Finance. Finally, according to Article 79 of the Constitution, the Minister of Finance must bring the budget before the [Hellenic Parliament](2449826.md) at least one month before the start of the fiscal year, for it to be voted on.[^cite_note-cecl-3]
