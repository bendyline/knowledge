# Crore

A **crore** ([/krɔːr/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English); abbreviated **cr**) is a quantity of [ten million](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10,000,000) (10,000,000 or 10<sup>7</sup>) in the [Indian numbering system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_numbering_system), equal to 100 [lakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakh). In the [Indian convention of digit grouping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_numbering_system#Decimal_formatting), it is formatted 1,00,00,000.[^cite_note-nroer-1] The crore is widely used both in official and other contexts in [Bangladesh](3454.md), [Bhutan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan), [India](14533.md), [Myanmar](19457.md), [Nepal](171166.md), and [Pakistan](23235.md). The next named numbers after it are *arab* (a billion), and *kharab* (a hundred billion), although they are not widely used in the modern-day Indian subcontinent.

## Etymology and regional variants

The word *crore* derives from the [Prakrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prakrit) word *kroḍi*, which in turn comes from the [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit) *koṭi* (कोटि),[^cite_note-2] denoting ten million in the [Indian number system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_number_system), which has separate terms for most powers of ten from 10<sup>0</sup> up to 10<sup>19</sup>.&#x20;

## Money

Large amounts of money in India, Bangladesh and Pakistan are often written in terms of *crore*. For example 150,000,000 (one hundred and fifty million) rupees is written as "fifteen *crore* [rupees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupee)", "₹ 15 crore".[^cite_note-nroer-1] In the abbreviated form, usage such as "₹ 15 cr" is common.[^cite_note-3]

[Trillions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders_of_magnitude_\(numbers\)#1012) (in the [short scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_and_short_scales)) of money are often written or spoken of in terms of *lakh crore*. For example, *one trillion rupees* is equivalent to:

- ₹1 lakh crore

- ₹10

  12

- ₹10,00,00,00,00,000 in [Indian notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_numbering_system#Decimal_formatting)

- ₹1,000,000,000,000 in metric notation
