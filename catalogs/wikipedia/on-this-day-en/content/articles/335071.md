The **prime minister of Bulgaria** ([Bulgarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_language): Министър-председател на България) is the [head of government](37570.md) of [Bulgaria](3415.md). They are oftentimes the leader of a [political coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_coalition) in the Bulgarian [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament), known as the [National Assembly](2122384.md), and the leader of the [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Bulgaria). At times, the prime minister has been appointed by the [president of Bulgaria](335070.md) in order to lead a [caretaker government](240683.md).[^cite_note-1] As of May 2026, [Rumen Radev](51575780.md) is serving as prime minister, following the victory of [Progressive Bulgaria](82553504.md) in the [2026 election](76476389.md).

## Selection

Following a [general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Bulgaria), after commencement of the term of the newly-elected [National Assembly](2122384.md) the president appoints the representative of the party a [plurality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurality_\(voting\)) of seats in the assembly (i.e. the largest party). Factions of the National Assembly are entitled to nominate candidates for prime minister within a period of seven days.
