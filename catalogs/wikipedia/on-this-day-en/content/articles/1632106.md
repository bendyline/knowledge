**Shaheen Sehbai** is a [Pakistani-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani-American) journalist and former Group Editor of daily English newspaper *[The News International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_News_International)*.[^cite_note-1]

## Career

Shaheen Sehbai was born and raised in Peshawar. He graduated in MSc Geology from [University of Peshawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Peshawar) before starting his career in journalism following in the footsteps of his father. Sehbai resigned from *[The News International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_News_International)* in March 2002 after receiving a memorandum from his publisher admonishing him for publishing libelous material, alienating advertisers, and generally avoiding senior government officials as well his own staff, among other complaints; Sehbai claimed that the criticism was because he failed to support the [Musharraf government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musharraf_government).[^cite_note-2] In August 2002, the [Committee to Protect Journalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_to_Protect_Journalists) sent a public letter to then-President [Pervez Musharraf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pervez_Musharraf) to draw his attention to alleged harassment of Sehbai's family.[^cite_note-3]

In July 2010, Sehbai filed a defamation notice against [Azeem Daultana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azeem_Daultana), a member of the [National Assembly of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Pakistan) and two newspapers for publishing a column written by Daultana.[^cite_note-4] The notice complains that the column alleges that Sehbai had undertaken a "revenge mission" against then President [Asif Ali Zardari](993655.md) because Sehbai had been "denied" an Ambassadorship, a claim that was originally made in an [Aftab Iqbal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftab_Iqbal) column published in [Nawa-i-Waqt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nawa-i-Waqt) on December 29, 2008.[^cite_note-5]

In April 2016, Shaheen Sehbai resigned from *[The News International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_News_International)*, citing concerns about the newspaper's political leaning and claiming that the paper "unnecessarily engaged in a dangerous conflict with national institutions."[^cite_note-6]

On June 12, 2023, [Islamabad Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_Police) filed a [First Information Report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_information_report) (FIR) against Sehbai, alongside fellow journalist [Wajahat Saeed Khan](46265547.md) and others, accusing them of "abetting mutiny" and inciting attacks on the Pakistani military, under [Pakistan Army Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Army_Act,_1952) Section 131 and related terrorism and sedition charges under the [Anti-Terrorism Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Terrorism_Act_1997), for their coverage of the [May 9 riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_9_riots). [Reporters Without Borders](526054.md) and the [Committee to Protect Journalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_to_Protect_Journalists), condemned the FIR as [transnational repression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnational_repression) of journalists.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] On January 1, 2026, an [Anti-Terrorism Court](4988272.md) in Islamabad convicted Sehbai in absentia and sentenced him to two consecutive life terms, along with Khan, [Adil Raja](81766027.md) and other journalists.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]
