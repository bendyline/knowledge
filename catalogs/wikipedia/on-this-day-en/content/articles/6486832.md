Even before the outbreak of the [Al-Aqsa Intifada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Aqsa_Intifada), various [insurgent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurgent) [Palestinian](47246228.md) ([Palestinian resistance](2646043.md)) groups built domestically-produced weapons for [attacks](2646043.md) against [Israel](9282173.md). Most of the effort has been in the production of unguided [artillery rockets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocket_artillery), though [Hamas](13913.md) has built its own versions of [anti-tank missiles](37517.md) and [rocket-propelled grenades](26286.md) (RPGs). Though usually smuggled across the [Egyptian](8087628.md) border into the [Gaza Strip](12047.md) and to a lesser extent [Jordanian](7515964.md) border into the [West Bank](33209.md), various small arms are believed to be produced in the [Palestinian territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories).

On 14 August 2008 the [Popular Resistance Committees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Resistance_Committees) showed off the [Nasser-4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasser-4?action=edit\&redlink=1) missile, an upgraded version of the existing [Nasser-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasser-3?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-1]
