**Nørresundby** (Danish pronunciation: [\[nɶɐ̯ɐˈsɔnˌpyˀ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Danish)) is a city in [Aalborg Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_Municipality), north of [Limfjorden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limfjorden), in [Vendsyssel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vendsyssel), in [Denmark](76972.md). The urban area has a population of 24,831 (1 January 2026).[^cite_note-1] It is located just north of [Aalborg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg), which lies south of Limfjorden. Since 2006, it has been a statistically independent urban area, but it is often still considered part of Aalborg; sometimes the name Greater Aalborg (*Stor-Aalborg*) is used to describe the concept.

The city is connected to Aalborg by the [Limfjordsbroen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limfjordsbroen) road bridge, and an iron railway bridge, as well as a motorway (E45) passing it to the east and running under the [Limfjord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limfjord).

Nørresundby is the site of the [Lindholm Høje](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindholm_H%C3%B8je) settlement and burial ground from the Germanic Iron Age and [Viking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viking) times. There is also a museum on the site.

Nørresundby has many sports clubs, including football club [Nørresundby FB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%B8rresundby_FB).[^cite_note-2]

During the [Torstenson War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torstenson_War), the city was captured by Swedish forces following [a battle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_N%C3%B8rresundby) in January 1644.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEØrnbjerg201021–23-5]

In 1865 a [pontoon bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontoon_bridge) by the name of [Christian IX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_IX)'s pontoon bridge was built. Fourteen years later in 1879 a [railroad bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jernbanebroen_over_Limfjorden) was built, which, although it was replaced in 1938, still stands today. In 1933, the pontoon bridge was removed and a new bridge, called the Limfjords bridge (Limfjordsbroen), was built in its place. Five years later, on 29 May 1938, [Aalborg Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_Airport) opened.

Through 1958–1960 the Limfjords bridge was expanded and in 1969 the Limfjords tunnel (Limfjordstunnelen) east of Nørresundby was opened. As a supplement to these bridges and the railway bridge, adding a fourth connection over the Limfjord has been spoken about for several years, although no direct action has been taken.

## Transportation

### Airport

[Aalborg Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_Airport) is located west of Nørresundby. [North Flying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Flying?action=edit\&redlink=1) has its head office in the North Flying Terminal at [Aalborg Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_Airport) in Nørresundby.[^cite_note-6]

### Rail

Nørresundby is served by [Lindholm railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindholm_railway_station), located in the [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_district) of [Lindholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindholm,_Aalborg?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the western part of the city. Lindholm station is located on the [Vendsyssel railway line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vendsyssel_railway_line) between [Aalborg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg) and [Frederikshavn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederikshavn). North of the station the [Aalborg Airport railway line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_Airport_railway_line) branches west to [Aalborg Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_Airport). The station offers direct [InterCity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InterCity) services to [Copenhagen](5166.md), [regional train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_train) services to [Aalborg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg) and [Frederikshavn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederikshavn), and [commuter rail](6286.md) services to [Aalborg Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_Airport_railway_station) and [Skørping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sk%C3%B8rping) as part of the [Aalborg Commuter Rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalborg_Commuter_Rail) service.

### Road

The city is connected to Aalborg by a road bridge [Limfjordsbroen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limfjordsbroen), as well as a motorway (E45) passing it to the east and running under the [Limfjord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limfjord).

## Nørresundby Gymnasium & HF

Nørresundby Gymnasium & HF is an upper secondary school in the city of Nørresundby, in North Jutland in Denmark. The school offers the traditional three-year program but also the so-called Higher Preparatory Examination (HF), which takes two years to complete. Nørresundby Gymnasium & HF is a certified Cambridge & Science school and it is furthermore open for exchange students. The school offers 12 different streams of studies. It is one of the only ones offering Greek as a subject.[^cite_note-8]
