**Myawaddy District** ([S'gaw Karen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S'gaw_Karen_language): ရါမတံၤကီၢ်ရ့ၣ်, [Phlone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Pwo_language)\:မေဝ်ပ္တီခြိုင့်,[Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): မြဝတီခရိုင်) is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District) of the [Kayin State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayin_State) in Myanmar (Burma). It consists of a single township composed of 50 villages.
