**Mandera County** is a county in northeastern [Kenya](188171.md), bordering [Ethiopia](187749.md) to the north, [Somalia](27358.md) to the east, and [Wajir County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wajir_County) to the south. It is predominantly home to the [Somali](1571696.md) community, with a population of about 983,000 (2024)-([nullified](https://ntvkenya.co.ke/news/court-orders-fresh-census-in-mandera-wajir-and-garissa-quashes-2019-count/)), making it the 28th largest county in Kenya by population. Covering an area of approximately 25,797 square kilometers, Mandera County features natural landscapes, including the [Dawa River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawa_River) and vast deserts.

The primary economic activities include [pastoralism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pastoralism) and [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture), with a focus on livestock. The capital and largest town is [Mandera](4521231.md).

## Economy

Mandera County, while facing persistent developmental and [insecurity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_insecurity) challenges, has shown signs of economic progress in recent years. In 2024, the county's economy grew by 3.5%,[^cite_note-knbs2025-4] reflecting gradual recovery in key sectors such as [livestock](25160767.md) and informal trade. Despite this growth, Mandera remains the poorest [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County) in [Kenya](188171.md) by GDP per capita, estimated at US\$500 as of 2024, according to the [Kenya National Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenya_National_Bureau_of_Statistics) (KNBS).[^cite_note-knbs2025-4]

With a population of approximately 936,000, Mandera ranked 42nd out of 47 counties in total economic output, recording a GDP of US\$140 million in 2022.[^cite_note-knbs2025-4] The county's economy is primarily [pastoralist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pastoralism), heavily dependent on [livestock](25160767.md) production and trade. Recurrent insecurity, especially along its borders with [Somalia](27358.md) and [Ethiopia](187749.md), continues to undermine private investment, public service delivery, and broader economic diversification.

## Climate

The county has a temperature range between 24C – 42C. Rainfall is very low and unreliable with annual average of 91.7mm annually. Long rains normally occur in April and May averaging 69.1mm while short rains in October and November averaging 122mm.[^cite_note-5]

## Demographics

Religion in Mandera County (2019)

[^cite_note-Census2019-6]

1. [Islam](6037917.md) (99.3%)

2. Others (0.70%)

The Kenya Population and Housing Survey report (KPHC) 2009 showed that Mandera County had a population of 1,025,756 persons. This comprised 559,943 males (54.6%) and 465,813 females (45.4%). The county's population was projected to be 1,399,503 persons, comprising 763,966 males and 635,537 females in 2017.[^cite_note-7]

The county has a total population of 867,457 of which 434,976 are males, 432,444 females and 37 intersex persons. There are 125,763 household with an average household size of 6.9 persons per household and a population density 33 people per square kilometre 2020.[^cite_note-8]

It was also projected that the population would be 1,699,437 persons comprising 927,695 males and 771,742 females in the year 2022.

Like in other areas of North Eastern counties, Mandera is predominantly inhabited by ethnic [Somalis](1571696.md).

## Health

There is a total of 116 health facilities in the county and 1520 health personnel of different cadre.[^cite_note-33]

HIV prevalence is at 1.8%, below the national average of 5.3%.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35]

## Transport and communication

The county is covered by 1,884.5 km of road network. Of this 1,390 km is covered by earth surface, 494.5 km is murram surface.[^cite_note-36]

There are 13 cyber cafes, 5 post offices installed letter boxes, 1150 rented letter boxes and 1100 rented letter boxes, and 50 vacant letter boxes.[^cite_note-37]

## Trade and commerce

There are 32 trading centers, 127 licensed retail traders, 212 licensed hawkers and 10 petrol stations.[^cite_note-38]

[Mandera Town](4521231.md) is the most densely populated urban area of the county, while [Takaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takaba) is the least populated.

The town is the oldest and main urban section of the county and its high population is attributed to the residents' diverse economic activities. The town has small-scale traders; retailers, artisans, hoteliers, grocers and even cross-border traders.
