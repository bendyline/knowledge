The **North Gaza Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة شمال غزة) is a [governorate](5811262.md) of [Palestine](241405.md) in the [Gaza Strip](12047.md). According to the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics), the governorate had a population of 270,245 (7.2% of the Palestinian population) with 40,262 households in mid-year 2007 encompassing three municipalities, two rural districts and one refugee camp.[^cite_note-2]

North Gaza has seen numerous strikes during the [Gaza war](74998519.md), including attacks on the [Shadia Abu Ghazala School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacks_on_schools_during_the_Israeli_invasion_of_Gaza#Shadia_Abu_Ghazala_School) and [Beit Lahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19_October_2024_Beit_Lahia_attacks).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
