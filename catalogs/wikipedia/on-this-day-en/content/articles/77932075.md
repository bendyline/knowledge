**Congressman Andres Acop Cosalan Road**, signed as **National Route 207 (N207)**, is a 64.77-kilometer (40.25 mi),[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] two-lane, secondary national road that forms part of the [Philippine highway network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_highway_network).

## History

Previously called the **Gurel–Bokod–Kabayan–Buguias–Abatan Road**, the road's current name honors the former Representative of Baguio and Benguet [Andres Cosalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andres_Cosalan) who served for two terms from 1965 to 1973 and again from 1978 to 1984.[^cite_note-4] Cosalan was instrumental in the development of Benguet and Baguio, and was mostly known for co-authoring Republic Act 4695 in 1966 that would divide the old Mountain Province to four different provinces, namely, Kalinga–Apayao (which was further split in 1995[^cite_note-5]), Benguet, Ifugao, and Mountain Province.[^cite_note-6] The road was changed to its present name upon the passing of Republic Act No. 11061 in January 16, 2017.[^cite_note-7]

## Route description

Cong. Cosalan Road starts at the junction with [Benguet–Nueva Vizcaya Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benguet%E2%80%93Nueva_Vizcaya_Road) at Sitio Gurel in at the southern tip of [Bokod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bokod,_Benguet) town proper. It continues to weave through mountainous and landslide-prone terrain towards Sitio Ambangeg, which harbors the a beginner-friendly hiking route going up to [Mount Pulag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Pulag). From there, it continues upwards the Upper Agno River Basin through more zigzag roads towards the municipality of [Kabayan](2137467.md) where it runs alongside the mainstem of the [Agno River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agno_River) towards the Akiki Trail Jump-off Point, and towards the municipal center of Kabayan, into Kabayan Barrio which serves as a backroad towards [Halsema Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halsema_Highway). The road then continues to zigzag its way upwards and crossing several tributaries until it reaches the municipality of [Buguias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buguias), where it crosses the main river once more to reach the Halsema Highway junction at the border of [Mankayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mankayan) and Buguias.



Gurel Junction, southern terminus of Cong. Cosalan Road.
