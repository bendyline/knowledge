**Flamingo Air** is a small airline in the Bahamas. Its base of operations is the [Grand Bahama International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Bahama_International_Airport) in [Freeport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeport,_Bahamas). It also has offices in Marsh Harbour Abaco Airport, in Bimini International Airport, and in the [Lynden Pindling International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynden_Pindling_International_Airport), [Nassau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nassau,_Bahamas). It provides scheduled service to several islands, as well as [Air Charter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Charter) service to the Bahamas and south [Florida](18933066.md).

Another airline that was based in the Bahamas with a similar name was Flamingo Airlines which operated scheduled passenger service during the early 1970s with [British Aircraft Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Aircraft_Corporation) [BAC One-Eleven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAC_One-Eleven) jet aircraft, [Convair 340](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convair_CV-240_family), [Lockheed L-188](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_L-188), and [Douglas DC-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-3) prop aircraft from its base in [Nassau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nassau,_Bahamas).[^cite_note-1] Flamingo Air/s air operator certificate has been suspended following the [ July 10, 2026 incidents](#Accidents_and_incidents), and the airline is now currently undergoing investigation by the Bahamian Ministry of Transport.[^cite_note-2] The Civil Aviation Authority of the Bahamas (CAAB) has temporarily suspended Flamingo Air’s [ AOC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Operator_Certificate), not a complete cease of operations.[^cite_note-3]

## Accidents and incidents

- 31 July 2012 - [Piper Aztec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piper_Aztec) experienced mechanical problem departing Freeport.

  [^cite_note-6]

- 18 August 2012 - [Cessna 402C](1781827.md) hit trees while attempting to abort a landing in heavy rain.

  [^cite_note-7]

- 4 October 2013 - [Cessna 402](1781827.md) landing gear collapsed during landing at Mayaguana on a charter flight.

  [^cite_note-8]

- 16 August 2016 - [Beech 99](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beech_99) A Flamingo Air plane crash landed at the airport in South Bimini on Monday afternoon after the wheel under the right wing collapsed.

  [^cite_note-9]

- 3 June 2016 - [Beech 99](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beech_99) landing gear failed during landing at Staniel Cay.

  [^cite_note-10]

- 12 June 2020 - [Beech 99](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beech_99) Nose Landing Gear collapsed during landing at Lynden Pindling International Airport.

- 17 October 2022 - [Beech 99](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beechcraft_Model_99) Left landing gear failed during landing at Black Point.

  [^cite_note-11]

- 2 October 2023 - [Beech 99](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beech_99) Boarding door opened mid flight en route to Staniel Cay.

  [^cite_note-12]

- 10 July 2026 - A [Beech 99](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beechcraft_Model_99) returned back to [Lynden Pindling International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynden_Pindling_International_Airport) whilst en route to [Mayaguana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayaguana) due to an unknown concern, which was likely an engine fire.&#x20;

  [^cite_note-13]

- 10 July 2026 - A [Cessna 402C](1781827.md) crashed in [North Andros](4124230.md), killing all nine passengers and the pilot, becoming the deadliest aviation accident in Bahamas domestic aviation.

  [^cite_note-14]
