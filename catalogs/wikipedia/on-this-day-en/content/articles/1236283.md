**Swat District** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): سوات اولسوالۍ, [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضلع سوات) was an administrative [district](639402.md) in the [Malakand Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malakand_Division) of [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md), [Pakistan](23235.md) from 1969 to 2025. The district was known for its natural sites and was a popular tourist destination. It had a population of 2,687,384 per the [2023 national census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), and was the 15th-largest district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa at the time.

Swat District was centred on the [Swat Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swat_Valley), usually referred to simply as Swat, which is a natural geographic region surrounding the [Swat River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swat_River).&#x20;

## History

In 1947, following the [Partition of British India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_British_India) and subsequent [independence of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_\(Pakistan\)), [Swat princely state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swat_\(princely_state\)) acceded to the [Dominion of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_Pakistan) continuing as a self-governing [princely state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princely_states_of_Pakistan) until it was officially annexed and merged into [West Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Pakistan) and later became a part of [North-West Frontier Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-West_Frontier_Province) (later Khyber Pakhtunkhwa) in 1969, forming the Swat district. The former [tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehsil) of [Buner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buner_District) was granted the status of a separate [district](639402.md) in 1991.[^cite_note-1] [Shangla](988627.md) was separated into a district in 1995.[^cite_note-2]

In October 2025, the government divided the Swat District into two, the [Upper Swat District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Swat_District) and (lower) Swat District.[^cite_note-\:03-3]

## Geography

Swat's total area was 5,337 square kilometres (2,061 mi<sup>2</sup>). Swat District consisted of two well-defined geographic regions, [Swat Kohistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swat_Kohistan) and Swat Proper. Swat Kohistan formed the larger, northern part of the district, while Swat Proper formed the lower portion of the district.[^cite_note-4] In terms of administrative divisions, Swat was surrounded by [Chitral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitral), [Upper Dir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Dir_District) and [Lower Dir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Dir_District) to the west, [Gilgit-Baltistan](18807336.md) to the north, and [Kohistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohistan_District,_Pakistan), [Buner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buner_District) and [Shangla](988627.md) to the east and southeast, respectively.&#x20;

As of the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), Swat district had 381,212 households and a population of 2,687,384. The district had a sex ratio of 104.83 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 48.13%: 61.83% for males and 33.95% for females. 808,888 (30.11% of the surveyed population) were under 10 years of age. 794,368 (29.56%) lived in urban areas.[^cite_note-2023_census-6]

## Economy

Approximately 38% of economy of Swat depended on [tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism)[^cite_note-11] and 31% on [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture).[^cite_note-12]

### Agriculture

Gwalerai, a village located near [Mingora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mingora), is one of those few villages which produces 18 varieties of apples due to its agriculturally favourable temperate climate in summer. The apple produced here is consumed in Pakistan as well as exported to other countries. It is known as 'the apple of Swat'.[^cite_note-13]

## Education

According to the *Alif Ailaan Pakistan Education Rankings for 2017*, Swat with a score of 53.1, was ranked 86 out of 155 districts in terms of education. Furthermore, the Swat schools infrastructure scored 90.26 which ranked it on number 31 out of 155.[^cite_note-14]

According to the [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Local Government Act, 2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa_Local_Government_Act,_2013), a new *local governments* system was introduced, in which Swat was included. This system has 67 [wards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_\(Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa\)), in which the total number of [village councils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_Council_\(Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa\)) is around 170, while neighbourhood councils number around 44.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20]

### Politics

The region elects three male members of the [National Assembly of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Pakistan) (MNAs), one female MNA, seven male members of the [Provincial Assembly of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_Assembly_of_Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa) (MPAs)[^cite_note-21] and two female MPAs. In the 2002 National and Provincial elections, the [Muttahida Majlis-e-Amal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muttahida_Majlis-e-Amal) (MMA), an alliance of religious political parties, won all the seats.
