**Dan Musa** is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Katsina State](2227638.md), [Nigeria](21383.md).

It has an area of 792 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 113,691 at the 2006 census.

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 821.[^cite_note-1]

## Climate

With an average yearly temperature of 30.48 °C (86.86 °F), 1,293 mm (50.92 in) of precipitation, and 53.9 rainy days, Dan Musa has a subtropical [steppe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steppe) climate.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3]

### Average Temperature

With an average daily high temperature of 36 °C (97 °F), the hot season spans 2.2 months, from March 14 to May 21. April is the hottest month of the year in Dan Musa, with typical high temperatures of 38 °C (100 °F) and low temperatures of 24 °C (75 °F). With an average daily maximum temperature below 31 °C (87 °F), the cool season spans 1.6 months, from December 7 to January 28. January is the coldest month of the year in Dan Musa, with an average high temperature of 29 °C (85 °F) and low temperature of 15 °C (59 °F).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Population

The population of Dan musa local government area as of 2006 [population census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_census) made is 113,691.

## Economy

Dan Musa Local Government Area is home to various crops including [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton), groundnuts, soybeans, [sugarcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugarcane), [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet), and beans. Cattle rearing and commerce are also significant economic aspects of the Local Government Area. Moreover, the region boasts a considerable presence of mineral resources like kaolin, iron-ore, and silica. Trade thrives in the area, with the Local Government Area hosting numerous markets.[^cite_note-4]
