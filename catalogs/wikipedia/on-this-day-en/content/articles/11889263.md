# Tani District

**Tani District** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): تڼي ولسوالۍ, [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): ولسوالی تنی شيتک تني) is situated in the southern part of [Khost Province](457249.md), [Afghanistan](737.md). Where most of Shitakzai Taniwal or [Tani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanai) live. It borders [Spera District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spera_District) to the west, [Nadir Shah Kot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadir_Shah_Kot_District) and [Mando Zayi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mando_Zayi_District) to the north, [Gurbuz District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurbuz_District) to the east and [Pakistan](23235.md) to the south. The population is 52,800 (2006) people.  The district center is the village of [Tani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tani,_Khost), situated in the northeastern part of the district.&#x20;

Taniwal or [Tani tribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanai) of Shitak or Shitakzai is settled here. Shitak (Tani) is the Brother Tribe of Shitakzai (Bannuchi now live in Bannu & Dawar or Dour of Tochi Valley Waziristan) The language spoken in Taniwal or Tani District is Pashto language close relation with Dawari or Baniswola Pashto.
