**New Iberia** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *La Nouvelle-Ibérie*;[^cite_note-4] [Spanish](26825.md): *Nueva Iberia*;[^cite_note-5] [Louisiana Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Creole_language): *Nouvèl Ibéri*) is the largest city in and the [parish seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parish_seat) of [Iberia Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberia_Parish,_Louisiana) in the [U.S. state](18618239.md) of [Louisiana](18130.md).[^cite_note-GR6-6] The city of New Iberia is located about 21 mi (34 km) southeast of [Lafayette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette,_Louisiana) and forms part of the [Lafayette metropolitan statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette_metropolitan_area,_Louisiana) in the region of [Acadiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acadiana). The [2020 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census) reported a population of 28,555.[^cite_note-\:3-7] New Iberia is served by [Amtrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Iberia_station)'s *[Sunset Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunset_Limited)*, operating between [Los Angeles](18110.md) and [New Orleans](53842.md). New Iberia has a major four-lane highway, [U.S. 90](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._90) (future [Interstate 49](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_49)), and has its own general-aviation airfield, [Acadiana Regional Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acadiana_Regional_Airport). Scheduled passenger and cargo airline service is available via the nearby [Lafayette Regional Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette_Regional_Airport) located adjacent to U.S. 90 in Lafayette.

## History

&#x20;New Iberia dates its founding to the spring of 1779, when a group of some 500 colonists (*[Malagueños](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_M%C3%A1laga)*) from Spain, led by Lieutenant Colonel [Don](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_\(honorific\)) [Francisco Bouligny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Bouligny), came up [Bayou Teche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayou_Teche) and settled around what became known as [Spanish Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Lake_\(Iberia_Parish\)).[^cite_note-8]



Flooding in New Iberia due to [Hurricane Ike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Ike), September 2008

The Spanish settlers called the town *Nueva Iberia* in honor of the Iberian Peninsula; French-speakers referred to the town as *Nouvelle Ibérie*, while the English settlers arriving after the [Louisiana Purchase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Purchase) called it "New Town." In 1814, the U.S. government opened a post office in the town, officially recognizing the name as New Iberia, but postmarks from 1802 show the town being called "Nova Iberia" (Latin for "new").[^cite_note-9] The town was incorporated in 1839[^cite_note-EB1911-1] as the "Town of Iberia", but the state legislature amended the town's charter in 1847, recognizing New Iberia as the town's name.[^cite_note-10]

In 1868, Iberia Parish was established, and New Iberia became the seat of parish government. At first, only rented space served for the courthouse. By 1884, a new courthouse was completed on a landscaped lot in downtown New Iberia, at the present-day site of Bouligny Plaza. In 1889, the Wakefields, an African-American family from New Iberia, fled to New Orleans after their son was lynched by a white mob; days after the lynching, the family patriarch and former state senator [Samuel Wakefield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Wakefield) committed suicide on Howard Street.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-sui-12] That courthouse served Iberia Parish until 1940. That year, the current courthouse was built along Iberia Street, two blocks from the New Iberia downtown commercial district.

In September 2008, New Iberia was struck by [Hurricane Ike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Ike). The lakes overflowed and filled the city, flooding it under several feet of dirty, brown water.[^cite_note-13] On [December 14, 2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak_of_December_12%E2%80%9315,_2022), the southeastern part of the city was heavily damaged by a high-end [EF2](3724479.md#Parameters) tornado, injuring 16 people.[^cite_note-14]

## Geography

New Iberia is located in southern Louisiana, in the [Acadiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acadiana) region. The city of New Iberia is a part of the [Lafayette metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette_metropolitan_area,_Louisiana). According to the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the city has a total area of 10.6 square miles (27.4 km<sup>2</sup>), all land. In 2000, the population density was 3,088.8 inhabitants per square mile (1,192.6/km<sup>2</sup>). The 12,880 housing units had an average density of 1,219.5 per square mile (470.9/km<sup>2</sup>). New Iberia lies roughly 16 to 20 feet above mean sea level.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

Among the lakes near the city is [Lake Peigneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Peigneur), which was formerly a 10-foot (3.0 m) deep freshwater lake until a 1980 disaster involving oil drilling and a salt mine. The lake is now a 1,300-foot (400 m) deep saltwater lake, having been refilled by the [Gulf of Mexico](21076367.md) via the [Delcambre Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delcambre_Canal).[^cite_note-17] Also, Lake Tasse is better known as [Spanish Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Lake_\(Iberia_Parish\)). This region has many natural features of interest, such as [Avery Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avery_Island), famous for its [Tabasco sauce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabasco_sauce) factory, deposits of [rock salt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_salt), and [Jungle Gardens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jungle_Gardens).

As of the [2020 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), 28,555 people, 11,030 households, and 7,338 families were residing in the city. The 2019 [American Community Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Community_Survey) estimated 29,456 people resided in the city limits.[^cite_note-acs_pop-23] At the [2010 U.S. census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_census), the population of New Iberia was 30,617. At the [census](6889.md) of 2000,[^cite_note-GR2-24] 32,623 people, 11,756 households, and 8,335 families resided in the city.

Of the population in 2019, New Iberians lived in 13,455 housing unit with 11,030 households.[^cite_note-2019_census_newiberia-25] New Iberia's population had a sex ratio of 96.2 males per 100 females.[^cite_note-acs_pop-23] The city had a median age of 36.2 and 7,671 were under 18, 2,609 were under 5, and 21,785 were 18 or older. An estimated 4,268 households were married couples living together, 784 were cohabiting households, 2,273 were male households with no female present, and 3,705 were female households with no male present.[^cite_note-2019_census_newiberia-25] The average household size was 2.63, and the average family size was 3.24.

In 2000 of the 11,756 households, 36.6% had children under 18 living with them, 45.1% were married couples living together, 20.5% had a female householder with no husband present, and 29.1% were not families. About 25.2% of all households were made up of individuals, and 10.8% had someone living alone who was 65 or older. The average household size was 2.70, and the average family size was 3.24. In the city, the age distribution was 29.8% under 18, 9.7% from 18 to 24, 26.8% from 25 to 44, 20.4% from 45 to 64, and 13.3% who were 65 or older. The median age was 34 years. For every 100 females, there were 87.8 males. For every 100 females 18 and over, there were 83.6 males.

The 2019 census estimates determined New Iberia a median income of \$38,221 and mean income of \$54,126.[^cite_note-26] At the 2000 census, the median income in the city for a household was \$26,079 and for a family was \$30,828. Males had a median income of \$30,289 versus \$16,980 for females. The [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income) for the city was \$13,084. About 24.9% of families and 29.5% of the population were below the [poverty line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_line), including 40.8% of those under 18 and 20.8% of those 65 or over.

### Race and ethnicity

New Iberia had a racial and ethnic makeup of 51.6% [non-Hispanic Whites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Hispanic_Whites), 40.7% [Blacks or African Americans](2154.md), 0.1% [American Indians and Alaska Natives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 1.4% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans), 2.1% some other race, and 2.1% [multiracial Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial_Americans). [Hispanics or Latino Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) of any race made up 3.8% of the total population in 2019.[^cite_note-acs_pop-23] After the 2020 census, its racial and ethnic makeup was 44.47% non-Hispanic White, 44.04% Black or African American, 0.26% American Indian and Alaska Native, 2.8% Asian, 3.37% from two or more races, and 5.06% Hispanic or Latino American of any race.[^cite_note-\:0-22] At the 2000 census, the racial makeup of the city was 56.99% White, 38.42% African American, 0.21% Native American, 2.64% Asian, 0.02% Pacific Islander, 0.51% from [other races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), and 1.20% from two or more races; 1.49% of the population were Hispanic or Latino American of any race.

### Religion

In common with most of Louisiana, most New Iberians profess a religion.[^cite_note-27] New Iberia is dominated by [Christianity](5211.md), and the single largest Christian denomination in the city is the Roman [Catholic Church](606848.md), owing in part to the [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_colonization_of_the_Americas) and [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_colonization_of_the_Americas) heritage of its residents. Catholics in New Iberia and the surrounding area are served by the [Roman Catholic Diocese of Lafayette in Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Lafayette_in_Louisiana).[^cite_note-28] Of the religious population, 0.1% each practice [Judaism](15624.md) or an [eastern religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_religions).

## Economy

The city of New Iberia was the founding headquarters for [Bruce Foods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Foods) before their relocation to [Lafayette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette,_Louisiana); it was also the birthplace of [Trappey's Hot Sauce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trappey's_Hot_Sauce). Currently, the economy is stimulated by small businesses, agriculture, [New Iberia station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Iberia_station), [Louisiana Hot Sauce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Hot_Sauce), and [Acadiana Regional Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acadiana_Regional_Airport).

### Festivals

New Iberia hosts the Louisiana Sugar Cane Festival in September.[^cite_note-37] The Sugar Cane Festival celebrates the commencement of the sugar cane harvest, locally referred to as *grinding.* [Sugar cane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_cane) is a principal crop grown by New Iberia farmers. The city also hosts El Festival Español de Nueva Iberia, which honors the area's Spanish heritage.[^cite_note-38] Other notable festivals include the World Championship Gumbo Cook-Off, on the second full weekend in October;[^cite_note-39] and the Books Along the Teche Literary Festival, in April, which celebrates James Lee Burke and South Louisiana literature.[^cite_note-40]

### Popular culture

New Iberia is home to fictional detective [Dave Robicheaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Robicheaux) and home to his creator, author [James Lee Burke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Lee_Burke).[^cite_note-41]

*[The Air-Conditioned Nightmare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Air-Conditioned_Nightmare)* (1945) travelogue book mentions [Henry Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Miller)'s stay in New Iberia.[^cite_note-42] *[In the Electric Mist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Electric_Mist)*, a movie based on one of Burke's novels, was filmed in New Iberia in 2009 and starred [Tommy Lee Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Lee_Jones).
