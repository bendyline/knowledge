The **Supreme Council of National Defence** ([Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language): *Consiliul Suprem de Apărare a Țării*; **CSAT**) is the autonomous administrative authority in [Romania](25445.md), invested by the [Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Romania) with the task of organising and coordinating, by unanimous decisions, the activities related to the country's defence and national security.

## Control

The activity of the Council is examined by the [Parliament](4462971.md). Annually, not later than the first trimester of the following year, if and when asked by the special committees of the [Parliament](4462971.md), and also whenever necessary, the Council presents, in a [joint session](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_session) of the [Chamber of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies_of_Romania) and the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Romania), its report on activities it has developed.
