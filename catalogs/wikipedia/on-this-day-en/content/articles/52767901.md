**Luis Andrés** "**Toto**" **Caputo** (born 21 April 1965) is an Argentine economist who is the current [Minister of Economy](8308783.md) under [Javier Milei](56599840.md)'s presidency, having assumed office on 10 December 2023.[^cite_note-3] He previously served as [Minister of Public Finances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economy_and_Public_Finances_\(Argentina\)) from 2017 to 2018 and [President of the Central Bank of Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Argentina) in 2018.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Early life and education

Caputo completed his primary and secondary studies at the catholic and bilingual [La Salle College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Salle_College_\(Buenos_Aires\)). He graduated with a degree in [Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) from the [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires).

His cousin, [Nicolás Caputo](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicol%C3%A1s%20Caputo), is a construction industry entrepreneur close to [Mauricio Macri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauricio_Macri).[^cite_note-Clarín-6]

## Academics

Caputo was a professor of [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) and [finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance) in the postgraduate course of the [Pontifical Catholic University of Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontifical_Catholic_University_of_Argentina).

## Finances

Caputo served as Chief of Trading for [Latin America](18524.md) at [JP Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.P._Morgan_&_Co.) between 1994 and 1998, and he held the same position for [Eastern Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Europe) and [Latin America](18524.md) at [Deutsche Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Bank) between 1998 and 2003. From then until 2008, he was chairman of the Argentine branch of [Deutsche Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Bank).

## Secretary and Minister of Finances

In December 2015, Caputo assumed the position of [Secretary of Finances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Economy_\(Argentina\)) of the [Macri's administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Mauricio_Macri).

During his tenure he played a key role together with [Alfonso Prat Gay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Prat_Gay) in the [negotiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentine_debt_restructuring) with the [hedge funds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedge_funds), where the country agreed to pay 9.352 million US dollars and Argentina returned to [international capital markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_markets) and guaranteed [external financing](10672313.md) in a difficult context as a result of the [default](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereign_default) inherited from [Cristina Fernández de Kirchner](1644780.md).[^cite_note-Clarín-6]

On 26 December 2016, after the dismissal of [Alfonso Prat-Gay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Prat-Gay) by President Macri, the Ministry of Treasury and Public Finance was split in two, with Caputo being appointed [Minister of Finance](8308783.md) in January 2017.[^cite_note-7]

He continued to take on the task of ensuring the arrival of the necessary [funds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funds) to face the [fiscal deficit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiscal_deficit). His deep knowledge of [international finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_finance) and direct contact with interlocutors on [Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street) are highlighted.[^cite_note-8]

In 2017, a government branch of the Fiscal Ministry opened a judicial case accusing Caputo of embezzling money from a Social Security fund by paying fees of at least 540,000 AR\$ to a company he had owned and directed until 2015, in lieu of unnecessary services.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

On 5 November 2017, the [Paradise Papers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradise_Papers), a set of confidential [electronic documents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_document) relating to [offshore investment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offshore_investment), revealed that Caputo had managed at least two [offshore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offshore_bank) [wealth funds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wealth_management).

## President of Central Bank

On 14 June 2018, Caputo became President of the [Central Bank of Argentina (BCRA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Argentina). His main challenge was to contain [inflation](38286.md), a result that the [Sturzenegger administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federico_Sturzenegger) did not achieve in 2018.[^cite_note-12]

Caputo was in the midst of the [2018 Argentine monetary crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Argentine_monetary_crisis). He increased the [interest rate](163115.md) from 20% and the [Argentine peso](407877.md) was devalued by 40% in three months.[^cite_note-lanacion.com.ar-13]

He oversaw a dramatic reduction in BRCA debt in the form of Letras del Banco Central (LEBAC) bills, a move considered to be an important achievement.[^cite_note-lanacion.com.ar-13][^cite_note-14]

Meanwhile, the BCRA's reserves in June were \$45 billion and, upon announcing its resignation, it reached \$49 billion (considering here the [IMF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMF)'s money inflow).[^cite_note-lanacion.com.ar-13]

He resigned on 25 September 2018 "for personal reasons", during a negotiation with the [International Monetary Fund](15251.md) and internal policies, and was replaced by the Secretary of Economic Policy of the Ministry of Treasury, [Guido Sandleris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guido_Sandleris).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

Argentine [stocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock_\(finance\)) and [bonds](60737.md) fell after his departure from the Central Bank.[^cite_note-17]
