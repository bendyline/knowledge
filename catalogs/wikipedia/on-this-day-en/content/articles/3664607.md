# Minister of Foreign Affairs (Egypt)

The [Egyptian](8087628.md) Minister of Foreign Affairs is the head of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Egypt\)).

## History

In the mid-2020s, the title has been changed to *Minister of Foreign Affairs, Emigration and Egyptian Expatriates*.
