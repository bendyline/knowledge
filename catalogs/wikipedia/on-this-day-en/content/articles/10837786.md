**Yongchuan** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 永川区; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Yǒngchuān Qū*) is a district of [Chongqing](171752.md), China, located by the north side of upper reach of [Yangtze River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangtze_River), with a history of 1200 years. Yongchuan borders [Sichuan](65185.md) province to the southwest and is 63 km (39 mi) away from [Yuzhong District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuzhong_District) of central Chongqing and 276 km (171 mi) from [Chengdu](170375.md).

## History

Yongchuan enjoys a history of 1200 years.

- In 776, Yongchuan county was established.

- In 1983, Yongchuan county was put under the administration of [Chongqing](171752.md) city.

- In 1992, Yongchuan county was promoted to a city level, called [Yongchuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yongchuan) city.

- In 2006, Yongchuan city was changed into Yongchuan district, a [county-level division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County-level_division) of Chongqing.

## Geographic condition

Yongchuan is in west Chongqing, in border with [Bishan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishan_District) and [Jiangjin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiangjin) in east, with [Dazu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dazu_District) and [Rongchang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rongchang) in west, with [Tongliang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tongliang) in north, and with [Luxiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxiang?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Hejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hejiang_County). Yongchuan is located in 105°37′31″－106°5′7″ of longitude and 28°56′16″－29°34′30″ of north latitude. It is 70.65 km (43.90 mi) long from south end to north end and 44.85 km (27.87 mi) wide from east end to north end, total area is 1,572.66 km<sup>2</sup> (607.21 sq mi).

## Climate

The climate of Yongchuan belongs to subtropic [monsoon](57630.md) weather style, with an annual average temperature of 17.7°C, monthly average temperature of 7.2°C in January, 27.3°C in July, total annual radiation of 1273.6 hrs, total annual rainfall of 1015 mm, frost-free period of 317 days .

## Transportation

The Yangtze river passes through Jiangjin, making up a 17.6 km long waterway. Both the Chengdu-Chongqing freeway and Chengdu-Chongqing railway have their ways across Yongchuan, which make up a very convenient traffic condition.

## Economy

- Yongchuan is one of important agricultural production areas in China, with a products include of rice, wheat, sweet potato, tea, vegetables, fruits, silk and bamboo. Its animal husbandry include pig raising etc.

- In 2006, its total annual [GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP) was [CNY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNY)12.194 billion.

## Tourism

The famous tourism place in Yongchuan is the Tea Mountain and Bamboo Sea.
