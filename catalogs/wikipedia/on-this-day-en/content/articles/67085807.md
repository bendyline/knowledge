**Alar Karis** (Estonian pronunciation: [\[ˈɑlɑr ˈkɑris\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Estonian); born 26 March 1958) is the sixth [president of Estonia](1581712.md), having taken office on 11 October 2021. He previously worked as [molecular geneticist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_genetics), [developmental biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Developmental_biology), and [Estonian](28222445.md) civil servant.[^cite_note-ETIS-1][^cite_note-\:1-2]

## Prior to presidency

Karis was born in [Tartu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tartu) on 26 March 1958.[^cite_note-ETIS-1][^cite_note-\:02-3] He was raised there by his mother and grandmother after his parents divorced in 1960.[^cite_note-4]

In 1981, he graduated from the [Estonian Agricultural Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_Agricultural_Academy) (now renamed [Estonian University of Life Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_University_of_Life_Sciences)) in Tartu.[^cite_note-\:02-3] In 1987, he received the degree of [Candidate of Veterinary Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) from the [Academy of Sciences of the Byelorussian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences_of_Belarus) in [Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minsk).[^cite_note-5] In 1999, he became a professor at the [University of Tartu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tartu).[^cite_note-\:02-3]

Karis served as [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_\(academia\)) of the Estonian University of Life Sciences from 2003 to 2007, as [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_of_Tartu_University) of the University of Tartu from 2007 to 2012, as the [Auditor General of Estonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auditor_General_of_Estonia) from 2013 to 2018, and as director of the [Estonian National Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_National_Museum) from 2018 to 2021.[^cite_note-ETIS-1][^cite_note-\:0-6]

## President of Estonia (2021–present)



Karis meets with Ukrainian President [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) in Kyiv, on 22 February 2022

In August 2021, he was approached by the speaker of the parliament ([Riigikogu](794927.md)) [Jüri Ratas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%BCri_Ratas) with a prospect of nomination for the position of the president of Estonia in the [upcoming election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Estonian_presidential_election) in the autumn.[^cite_note-7] He accepted the nomination[^cite_note-8] and his candidacy was subsequently endorsed by both coalition parties, the [Reform Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_Reform_Party)[^cite_note-9] and the [Centre Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_Centre_Party).[^cite_note-10] On 31 August 2021, Karis was elected as the president of Estonia with a two thirds majority of 72 votes in the Riigikogu.[^cite_note-\:1-2][^cite_note-11] He assumed the office on 11 October 2021.[^cite_note-\:1-2]



Karis with King [Carl XVI Gustaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_XVI_Gustaf) of Sweden in Tallinn, 2 May 2023

The election received criticism and calls for electoral reform due to the absence of opposing candidates.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] In his acceptance speech Karis referenced the "turmoil that surrounded the presidential selection process" and called for changes of the system to be examined by the Riigikogu, such as using an enlarged electoral college, facilitating the nomination of candidates or even using direct election. He promised to be a balancer and a mediator during his presidential term.[^cite_note-15]



Karis at the 2025 [AI for Good Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITU_AI_for_Good) in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva)

Karis started his presidency with state visits to Latvia and Finland.[^cite_note-16] The visit to Latvia was hosted by the [speaker of the Saeima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_Saeima) [Ināra Mūrniece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In%C4%81ra_M%C5%ABrniece) as President of Latvia [Egils Levits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egils_Levits) had tested positive for [COVID-19](63030231.md) the previous evening.[^cite_note-17]

Karis on several occasions has used his right not to promulgate laws passed by the parliament. In December 2023 Karis refused to promulgate a law due to finding that the rules of proceedings were violated by the government by tying the bill to confidence vote. Karis found in his decision that all-out obstruction in the Riigikogu, that may render the parliament unable to work, does not give the government the right to keep tying all bills to confidence votes and therefore pushing them through the parliament.[^cite_note-18] The Riigikogu however adopted the bill unchanged, Karis refused to promulgate the second time and sent the bill to the [Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Estonia).[^cite_note-19] Supreme Court sided with Karis.[^cite_note-20]

In July 2024 Karis did not proclaim the Vehicle Tax Act finding it to be unconstitutional in violating the principle of equal treatment in the part that deals with persons with disabilities.[^cite_note-21] He approved it when the law was amended by the parliament.[^cite_note-22]

On 23 June 2026, Karis announced during his [Estonian Victory Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%B5idup%C3%BCha) address in [Rapla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapla) that he would not seek a second term in the [2026 Estonian presidential election](69822115.md). Stating that his decision was for personal reasons, he cited a prior agreement made with his family to serve only a single term.[^cite_note-23] Opposition leaders from the [Centre Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_Centre_Party) and [Isamaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isamaa) subsequently claimed that Karis was pressured into the decision by the governing coalition despite high public approval ratings.[^cite_note-24]

## Personal life

His father was the [botanist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botanist) Harry Karis (1930–2018)[^cite_note-25] and his mother Virve Karis (1928–2020), an agriculture specialist in animal nutrition.[^cite_note-26] He grew up with his mother and grandmother Vilhelmine after his parents divorced in 1960.

He has been married to [Sirje Karis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirje_Karis) (née Jädal) since 1977, with whom he has 3 children and 5 grandchildren.[^cite_note-27] Besides [Estonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_language), Karis is fluent in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language). Although he is fluent in Russian, he has admitted that it needs some practice since he has not had to speak the language in a long time.[^cite_note-28]
