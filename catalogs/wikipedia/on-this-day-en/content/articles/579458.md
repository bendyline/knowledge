Below is a list of the 18 **states of the [Sudan](27421.md)** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) names are in parentheses). Prior to 9 July 2011, the Republic of the Sudan was composed of 25 states. The ten southern states now form part of the independent country of [South Sudan](32350676.md). Two additional states were created in 2012 within the [Darfur](605837.md) region, and one in 2013 in [Kordofan](1127161.md), bringing the total to 18.

## History

[Anglo-Egyptian Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Egyptian_Sudan) had eight *[mudiriyat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mudiriyah)*, or [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province), which were ambiguous when created but became well defined by the beginning of [World War II](32927.md). The eight provinces were: [Blue Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Nile_\(state\)), [Darfur](605837.md), [Equatoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equatoria), [Kassala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kassala_\(state\)), [Khartoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khartoum_\(state\)), [Kordofan](1127161.md), [Northern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_state,_Sudan), and [Upper Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Nile_\(state\)). In 1948, [Bahr al Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahr_el_Ghazal_\(region_of_South_Sudan\)) split from Equatoria.[^cite_note-1]

There were numerous new provinces created on 1 July 1973. [North](767666.md) and [South Darfur](767650.md) were created from Darfur, while Kurdufan divided into [North](767669.md) and [South Kordofan](767653.md). [Gezira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gezira_\(state\)) and [White Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Nile_\(state\)) were split off from Blue Nile. [River Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Nile_\(state\)) split off from Northern. [Red Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Sea_\(state\)) was split off from Kassala.

A further fracturing of provinces occurred in 1976. [Lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakes_State) split from Bahr al Ghazal, and [Jonglei](767655.md) split off from Upper Nile. Equatoria divided into [Eastern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Equatoria) and [Western Equatoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Equatoria). There were thus eighteen provinces. In 1991, the government reorganized the administrative regions into nine federal states, matching the nine provinces that had existed from 1948 to 1973. On 14 February 1994, the government reorganized yet again, creating twenty-six *[wilayat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilayah)* (states). The majority of the wilayat were either the old provinces or administrative subregions of a province. As part of the new government structure in [South Sudan](32350676.md) in 2005, Bahr al Jabal was renamed [Central Equatoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Equatoria). In 2006, West Kurdufan was split and merged with North Kurdufan and South Kordofan.

In January 2012, the new states of Central Darfur and East Darfur were created in the Darfur region, bringing the total number of states to 17.[^cite_note-2] In July 2013, West Kurdufan was reestablished.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

Since the [2019 Sudanese coup d'état](60469131.md), the states in Sudan have been without state governments and legislative councils.

### Former states now part of South Sudan

See also: [States of South Sudan](30852056.md)

On 9 July 2011, the [ten southern states](30852056.md) became the independent country of South Sudan. They were further divided into [86 counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_South_Sudan).

### Special administrative areas

- The [Abyei Area](13885196.md), located on the border between South Sudan and the Republic of the Sudan, currently has a special administrative status and is governed by an [Abyei Area Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abyei_Area_Administration). It was due to hold a [referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abyei_status_referendum) in 2011 on whether to be part of [South Sudan](32350676.md) or part of the Republic of Sudan.

### Regional bodies

In addition to the states, there also exist regional administrative bodies established by peace agreements between the central government and rebel groups:

- The [Darfur Regional Government](14497212.md) was established by the [Darfur Peace Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darfur_Peace_Agreement) to act as a co-ordinating body for the states that make up the region of [Darfur](605837.md).

- The [Eastern Sudan States Coordinating Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Sudan_States_Coordinating_Council) was established by the [Eastern Sudan Peace Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Sudan_Peace_Agreement) between the Sudanese Government and the rebel [Eastern Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Front_\(Sudan\)) to act as a coordinating body for the three eastern states of [Red Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Sea_\(state\)), [Kassala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kassala_\(state\)), and [Al Qadarif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Qadarif_\(state\)).
