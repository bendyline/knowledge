**Kauda** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): كاودا) is a town in southern [Sudan](27421.md), located in the [South Kordofan](767653.md) [state](579458.md) which currently serves as headquarters of rebels from the [Sudan People's Liberation Movement–North (al-Hilu)](30884032.md) group.

## History

On 19 December 2013, the [Sudanese army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_army) bombed Kauda, killing one person and wounding another.[^cite_note-2]

In 2020, Sudanese Prime Minister [Abdallah Hamdok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdallah_Hamdok) visited Kauda for the first time since [war broke out in 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_conflict_in_South_Kordofan_and_Blue_Nile).[^cite_note-3]

As of January 2025, the town is under the control of the [Sudan People's Liberation Movement–North (al-Hilu)](30884032.md) rebel group during the [Sudanese civil war (2023–present)](73556699.md).[^cite_note-4]

## Religion

There is a mosque in the town. A [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic) community also exists.[^cite_note-5]
