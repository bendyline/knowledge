The **Minister for Migration**, ([Swedish](26689.md): *migrationsminister*), formally **[cabinet minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_minister) of the [Ministry of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_\(Sweden\))**, is a [member and minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_minister) of the [Government of Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Sweden) and is appointed by the [Prime Minister](195846.md). The minister is responsible for policies related to [migration](4599312.md) and [asylum policies](1621432.md).
