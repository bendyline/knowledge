**Mapanuepe Lake** is a freshwater [lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake) located in the [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality) of [San Marcelino](2117884.md), [Zambales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambales) in the [Philippines](23440.md). The lake was created after the cataclysmic eruption of [Mount Pinatubo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Pinatubo) in 1991. [Lahars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahar) following the eruption [blocked the drainage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landslide_dam) of Mapanuepe River, south of the [volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcano), flooding Mapanuepe Valley together with its settlements. Only the [steeple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steeple_\(architecture\)) of a local village [chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapel) protrudes from the lake’s surface.[^cite_note-Umbal-2]

## History

Mapanuepe lake is located at the [confluence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confluence) of Marella and Mapanuepe Rivers as the two rivers merge to become the Santo Tomas River. The rains after the 1991 eruption of [Mount Pinatubo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Pinatubo) produced [lahar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahar) that dumped volcanic debris into the Marella River, one of the major drainages of the mountain, aggrading the river and eventually damming the Mapanuepe River.&#x20;

Rising waters drowned the Mapanuepe Valley including the [barangays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barangays) of Aglao (lower), Buhawen and Pili of [San Marcelino](2117884.md), [Zambales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambales). During the formation of the lake, breaching and reforming of the debris dam occurred after each lahar flow. At its maximum extent, the lake grew to an area of 670 hectares (1,700 acres) and had a stored water volume of 75 × 10<sup>6</sup> cubic meters (98,000,000 cu yd) before reaching its current size.&#x20;

Presently, the only structure visible above the lake’s surface is the steeple and large metal cross of Saint Barbara’s Chapel, a *[visita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapel_of_ease)* which once served Barangay Buhawen.[^cite_note-3] Prior to the 1991 eruption, studies of [geologic formations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geologic_formations) and [sediments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sediments) of Mapanuepe Valley showed that the area was the site of a similar, lahar-formed lake from much older eruptions.[^cite_note-Umbal-2]

## Water quality

The lake’s water is reportedly contaminated with [mercury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_\(element\)) leaking from the abandoned Dizon [Copper Mine](828834.md) on the eastern shore. However, the harmful chemicals are believed to have since been diluted and the water safe for diving and fishing.[^cite_note-4]
