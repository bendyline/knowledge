**Viacha** is a city in [Bolivia](3462.md), situated in the [Ingavi Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingavi_Province) in the [La Paz Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Paz_Department,_Bolivia). Viacha lies in the [Altiplano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altiplano), 22 km southwest of La Paz. Transportation to and from the city includes cars, buses, and a train.[^cite_note-1]

## Economy

Viacha is home to one of the largest [cement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cement) factories in Bolivia, '*Cemento Viacha*,' which is part of SOBOCE <https://web.archive.org/web/20090512015007/http://www.soboce.com/WebSoboce/principal.htm>. Other sources of income for the city include [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture), mainly consisting of [potatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potatoes).

## Culture

The location of Viacha, which lies close to [Lake Titicaca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Titicaca) and [Tiawanaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiawanaku), makes it a cultural center for the Irohito-Urus. These descendants of the [Incas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incas) have lived in this area for hundreds of years.[^cite_note-2]
