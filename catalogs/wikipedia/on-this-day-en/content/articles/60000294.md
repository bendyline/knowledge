The **2026 FIFA World Cup qualification (CAF)** was the African section of the [qualification tournament](49459509.md) for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md), held in the United States, Canada and Mexico. A total of nine slots in the final tournament and one inter-confederation play-off slot were reserved for [Confederation of African Football](715265.md) (CAF) teams.[^cite_note-1]

## Format

Because nine CAF teams directly qualified instead of five, the CAF Executive Committee announced a new qualification format on 19 May 2023. Teams were drawn into nine groups of six teams. The winner of each group directly qualified to the World Cup, while the four best group runners-up participated in play-offs to determine which team would advance to the [inter-confederation play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\)).[^cite_note-ExCon-2][^cite_note-format-3]

- **First round (group stage)**: Nine groups of six teams played in a home-and-away [round-robin](939742.md) tournament. Group winners qualified for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).

- **Second round (play-off)**: The four best group runners-up were seeded into play-offs to determine the CAF representative at the [inter-confederation play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\)).

## First round

Teams were drawn into nine groups of six teams to play home-and-away [round robin matches](939742.md) from November 2023 to October 2025. The winners of each group qualified directly to the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md), and the four best group runners-up advanced to a play-off to determine which team would advance to the [inter-confederation play-offs](49459509.md#Inter-confederation_play-offs).

### Draw

The draw was held on 13 July 2023 at 15\:00 [GMT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Ivory_Coast) ([UTC±0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC%C2%B100\:00)) in [Abidjan](21371767.md), Ivory Coast.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-draw-5] All 54 FIFA-affiliated football associations from CAF entered qualification. However, Eritrea withdrew after the draw.[^cite_note-ERI_withdrawal-6][^cite_note-7]

## Second round

Main article: [2026 FIFA World Cup qualification – CAF second round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_%E2%80%93_CAF_second_round)

The four best group runners-up ([Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon_national_football_team), [DR Congo](1030243.md), [Gabon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabon_national_football_team), and [Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria_national_football_team)) advanced to play-offs to determine the sole CAF representative at the [inter-confederation play-offs](49459509.md#Inter-confederation_play-offs). The matchups for the semi-final were based on the 17 October 2025 [FIFA rankings](719095.md), with the highest-ranked side taking on the lowest and the second-highest taking on the third-highest.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] A total of three matches were played, all hosted in Morocco.[^cite_note-35]

## Inter-confederation play-offs

Main article: [2026 FIFA World Cup qualification (inter-confederation play-offs)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\))

The winner of the second round, [DR Congo](1030243.md), joined [Bolivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivia_national_football_team) (from [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL)), [Iraq](1039652.md) (from [AFC](715249.md)), [Jamaica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamaica_national_football_team) and [Suriname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suriname_national_football_team) (both from [CONCACAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF)), and [New Caledonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Caledonia_national_football_team) (from [OFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceania_Football_Confederation)) in the inter-confederation play-offs.

The teams were ranked according to the November 2025 [FIFA Men's World Ranking](719095.md), with the four lowest-ranked teams playing in two single-elimination matches. The winners met the two highest-ranked teams in another set of single-elimination matches, with the winners of these matches qualifying for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).[^cite_note-36]
