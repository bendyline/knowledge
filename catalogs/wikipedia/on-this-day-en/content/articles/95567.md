**Madison County** is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_\(United_States\)) located in the central part of the [U.S. state](18618239.md) of [Kentucky](16846.md). At the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), its population was 92,701.[^cite_note-QF-1] Its [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) is [Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Kentucky).[^cite_note-GR6-2] The county is named for [Virginia](32432.md) [statesman](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/statesman) [James Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Madison), who later became the fourth [President of the United States](24113.md).[^cite_note-3]

The county is part of the [Richmond-Berea, KY Micropolitan Statistical Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond-Berea,_KY_Micropolitan_Statistical_Area) which is also included in the [Lexington-Fayette–Richmond–Frankfort, KY combined statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexington%E2%80%93Fayette%E2%80%93Richmond%E2%80%93Frankfort_combined_statistical_area).

Madison County was considered a [moist county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moist_county), meaning that although the county prohibited the sale of alcoholic beverages (and is thus a [dry county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_county)), it contained a city where retail alcohol sales are allowed. Nevertheless, two of the county's 19 precincts are completely dry.[^cite_note-4] Alcohol can also be sold by the drink in Berea,[^cite_note-5] Richmond, and at Arlington and The Bull golf clubs. In 2023, the county voted to become [wet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_county).[^cite_note-6]

Madison County is home to [Eastern Kentucky University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Kentucky_University), [Berea College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berea_College), and historic [Boone Tavern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boone_Tavern).[^cite_note-7] Famous pioneer [Daniel Boone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Boone) lived in Madison County and built [Fort Boonesborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Boonesborough), now a state historic site.

## History

Indian trader John Findley, Daniel Boone, and four others first came into the area that is now Madison County in 1769 on a hunting and exploring expedition. In 1774, the Transylvania Company, led by Judge Richard Henderson of North Carolina, purchased 20,000,000 acres (8,100,000 ha) of land west of the Appalachians (including present-day Madison County) from the [Cherokee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherokee) Nation. Daniel Boone was hired to cut a trail through the Cumberland Gap and establish a settlement on the Kentucky River. The settlement at [Fort Boonesborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Boonesborough_State_Park) began in April 1775.

In 1785, Madison County was established from a portion of [Lincoln County, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_County,_Virginia).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

### 2020 census

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), the county had a population of 92,701. The median age was 35.6 years. 20.9% of residents were under the age of 18 and 14.9% of residents were 65 years of age or older. For every 100 females there were 93.9 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 91.5 males age 18 and over.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-16][^cite_note-Census2020PL-17]

The racial makeup of the county was 86.9% White, 4.3% [Black or African American](2154.md), 0.3% [American Indian and Alaska Native](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 1.1% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans), 0.1% [Native Hawaiian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Hawaiians) and [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander), 1.3% from some other race, and 6.1% from [two or more races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial_Americans). [Hispanic or Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) residents of any race comprised 3.3% of the population.[^cite_note-Census2020PL-17]

63.8% of residents lived in urban areas, while 36.2% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-Census2020DHC-18]

There were 36,148 households in the county, of which 29.6% had children under the age of 18 living with them and 26.3% had a female householder with no spouse or partner present. About 28.1% of all households were made up of individuals and 9.7% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-16]

There were 39,319 housing units, of which 8.1% were vacant. Among occupied housing units, 59.5% were owner-occupied and 40.5% were renter-occupied. The homeowner vacancy rate was 1.6% and the rental vacancy rate was 9.0%.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-16]

### 2000 census

As of the [census](6889.md)[^cite_note-GR2-19] of 2000, there were 70,872 people, 27,152 households, and 18,218 families residing in the county. The [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) was 161 per square mile (62/km<sup>2</sup>). There were 29,595 housing units at an average density of 67 per square mile (26/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of the county was 93.01% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_American), 4.44% [Black or African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American), 0.28% [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 0.72% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_American), 0.02% [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander_American), 0.34% from [other races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_and_ethnicity_in_the_United_States_Census), and 1.19% from two or more races. 0.97% of the population were [Hispanics or Latinos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanics_in_the_United_States) of any race.

There were 27,152 households, out of which 31.50% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 53.10% were [married couples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage) living together, 10.70% had a female householder with no husband present, and 32.90% were non-families. 25.20% of all households were made up of individuals, and 7.60% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older. The average household size was 2.42 and the average family size was 2.90.

By age, 21.90% were under 18, 18.80% from 18 to 24, 29.40% from 25 to 44, 20.10% from 45 to 64, and 9.80% 65 or older. The median age was 31 years. Both the relatively large 18-to-24 population and the relatively low median age can be explained by the presence of [Eastern Kentucky University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Kentucky_University), and to a considerably lesser extent [Berea College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berea_College). For every 100 females, there were 93.30 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 90.20 males.

The median income for a household in the county was \$32,861, and the median income for a family was \$41,383. Males had a median income of \$31,974 versus \$22,487 for females. The [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income) for the county was \$16,790. About 12.00% of families and 16.80% of the population were below the [poverty line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_line), including 17.80% of those under age 18 and 17.10% of those age 65 or over.

## Economy

### Military

The [Blue Grass Army Depot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Grass_Army_Depot) (BGAD) is located just south of [Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Kentucky). [^cite_note-20]

In February 2026, U.S. Senator Mitch McConnell announced a nearly \$1 billion in federal funding for BGAD as part of the FY 2026 Defense Appropriations bill. Energetics Manufacturing received \$903 million to expand manufacturing capacity for energetics materials, (explosives materials). Plus an additional \$64+ million towards other BGAD improvements.[^cite_note-21]

### Civilian

Hitachi Astemo Americas Inc., has been making major automotive investments in [Berea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berea,_Kentucky), since 2023.

## Education

### Schools

Madison County is served by two school districts:[^cite_note-26]

- [Madison County Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_County_Schools_\(Kentucky\)), currently consisting of 10 elementary, 5 middle, and 2 high schools.

- [Berea Independent Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berea_Independent_Schools), currently consisting of 1 elementary, 1 middle, and 1 high school.

The county is also served by [Model Laboratory Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_Laboratory_Schools) which is part of [Eastern Kentucky University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Kentucky_University).
