**Sudan News Agency**, also known as **SUNA**, is the official [news agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_agency) of [Sudan](27421.md). It provides news to other organizations in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language), and [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic).[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## History and profile

The Sudan News Agency was established in 1970.[^cite_note-\:0-1] It was officially inaugurated on the second anniversary of the May Revolution in 1971.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-2] Abdul Karim Mehdi was the first director of the SUNA. Then Mustafa Amin became the director of the agency.[^cite_note-ssig-3] Amin served as the director until 1985.[^cite_note-ssig-3]

In June 2023 a SUNA journalist, Abdel Rahman Warab, was reportedly abducted from his home by the paramilitary group [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) (RSF).[^cite_note-4] He still remained missing in April 2025.[^cite_note-5] In November 2025 RSF assassinated Taj Al-Sir Ahmed Suleiman, director of the SUNA office in the city of [Al Fasher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Fasher).[^cite_note-6]
