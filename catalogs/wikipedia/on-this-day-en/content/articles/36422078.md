# Ruwenzori (commune)

**Ruwenzori** is a commune of the city of [Beni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beni,_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) in [North Kivu](446958.md), [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md).[^cite_note-1]
