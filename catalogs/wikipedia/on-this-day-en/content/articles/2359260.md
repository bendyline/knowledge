A **military hospital** is a [hospital](18994221.md) owned or operated by a [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military). They are often reserved for the use of [military personnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_personnel) and their [dependents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dependant), but in some countries are made available to [civilians](145804.md) as well. They may or may not be located on a [military base](843566.md); many are not.



A former military hospital in [Tampere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampere), [Finland](10577.md) in 1940.

In the United Kingdom and Germany, [British military hospitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_military_hospital) have been closed; military personnel are usually treated in a special wing of a designated civilian hospital, in the UK, these are referred to as a [Ministry of Defence Hospital Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_Hospital_Unit). Service personnel injured in [combat operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_operations) are normally treated at the [Royal Centre for Defence Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Centre_for_Defence_Medicine).
