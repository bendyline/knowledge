The **House of Representatives** ([Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): प्रतिनिधि सभा, romanized: *Pratinidhi Sabhā*) is the [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house) of the [bicameral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameralism) [Federal Parliament of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Parliament_of_Nepal), with the [upper house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_house) being the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Nepal\)).[^cite_note-1] Members of the House of Representatives are elected through a [parallel voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_voting) system. They hold their seats for five years or until the body is dissolved by the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Nepal) on the advice of the [council of ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Nepal). The House meets in the under construction [Parliament house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_House,_Singha_Durbar) at [Singha Durbar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singha_Durbar); before the [2025 Gen Z protests](81013780.md), it met at the [International Convention Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Convention_Centre,_Nepal).

The House has 275 members; 165 elected from [single-member constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_Nepal) by [first-past-the-post voting](261709.md) and 110 elected through [proportional electoral system](23298.md) where voters vote for political parties, considering the whole country as a single election constituency.[^cite_note-2] The House of Representatives, unless dissolved, continues to operate for five years from the date appointed for its first meeting. However, in a [state of emergency](298608.md), the term of the House of Representatives may be extended, not exceeding one year in accordance with federal law.

The [current](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7th_House_of_Representatives_\(Nepal\)) House of Representatives was elected by the [general election held on 5 March 2026](69672716.md), following the dissolution of the previous parliament by the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Nepal) on 12 September 2025, as a result of the [political unrest and Gen Z protest](81013780.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-KP_Address-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The inaugural session of the current house convened on 2 April 2026.[^cite_note-7]

## History

### Parliament of Kingdom of Nepal, 1959–1962

The 1959 constitution of the Kingdom of Nepal, proclaimed on 12 February 1959, first mentions the Pratinidhi Sabha first as follows: "There shall be a Parliament which shall consist of His Majesty and two Houses, to be known respectively as the Senate ([Maha Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Nepal\)#Maha_Sabha)) and the House of Representatives (Pratinidhi Sabha)" *(Article No. 18, Constitution of the Kingdom of Nepal, 1959).*[^cite_note-8]

The 1959 constitution was abrogated on 16 December 1962 when the new [Constitution of the Kingdom of Nepal, 1962](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Kingdom_of_Nepal,_1962) was proclaimed and the parliament of the kingdom became unicameral.[^cite_note-9]

### Post-Panchayat, 1990–2002

The House of Representatives was first provided for by the "Constitution of the Kingdom of Nepal 1990", which replaced the former [panchayat system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panchayat_\(Nepal\)) of parliament with a bicameral parliament.[^cite_note-10] It consisted of 205 members directly elected from single-member constituencies. It had five-year terms, but it could be dissolved by the [King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Nepal) on the advice of the Prime Minister before the end of its term.[^cite_note-11]

### Dissolution, 2002–2007



Front face of Pratinidhi Sabha Building



Pratinidhi Sabha building of Nepal (moderation of ancient Gallery Hall)

In May 2002, the House of Representatives was dissolved by [King Gyanendra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyanendra_of_Nepal) on advice of the then prime minister, [Sher Bahadur Deuba](623301.md), in order to hold new elections.[^cite_note-12] Elections could not take place due to the ongoing [civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepalese_Civil_War), which eventually led King Gyanendra to stage a royal coup. Following the democracy movement of 2006, the King reinstated the earlier legislature.[^cite_note-13] On 15 January 2007, the House of Representatives was transformed into an [Interim legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interim_legislature_of_Nepal). This consisted of members appointed under an agreement between the [Seven Party Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Party_Alliance) and the Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist) (known by the name [Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist Centre)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Nepal_\(Maoist_Centre\)), since 2009).[^cite_note-14]

### Federal Parliament of Nepal, 2015–present

The [Constitution of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal) was drafted by the [2nd Constituent Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Nepalese_Constituent_Assembly) and the provision for a [bicameral legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameralism) was re-adopted.[^cite_note-15] The House of Representatives became the lower house of the [Federal Parliament of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Parliament_of_Nepal) and its first election was held [in 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Nepalese_general_election).[^cite_note-16]

## Members

The composition and powers of the house are established by Parts 8 and 9 of the [Constitution of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal). The qualifications for becoming a member of the House are laid out in Article 87 of the Constitution and House of Representatives Election Act, 2017. Members must be:

- a citizen of Nepal

- twenty five years or older on date of nomination

- without a criminal offense conviction involving moral turpitude

- not disqualified by any federal law

- not hold any office of profit (paid by the government).

  [^cite_note-17]

In addition to this, no member can be a member of both the House of Representatives and the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Nepal\)).

### Vacation of seat

The seat of a member of House of Representatives may be considered vacant in the following circumstances:[^cite_note-18]

- Written resignation to the Speaker

- Unable to comply to Article 91

- Expired term of office / house

- Unclarified / uninformed absence for ten consecutive house sessions

- Resignation / removal from the party to which the candidate was associated during election

- Death
