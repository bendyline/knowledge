# National Centre for Information Defence

The **National Centre for Information Defence** is a division of the [Government of the United Kingdom](25318118.md), aimed at combating [misinformation](2203174.md) attempts, including [AI-generated content](62008969.md).[^cite_note-1]

## History

The [Prime minister of the United Kingdom](24150.md), [Andy Burnham](414818.md) announced the creation of the agency on 23 September 2026, at the [United Nations General Assembly](83962647.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
