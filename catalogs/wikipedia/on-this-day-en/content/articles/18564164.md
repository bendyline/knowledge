**Mercedes-Benz** (German pronunciation: [\[mɛʁˌtseːdəs ˈbɛnts, -dɛs -\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Standard_German) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/b/b0/De-Mercedes-Benz.ogg/De-Mercedes-Benz.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/b/b0/De-Mercedes-Benz.ogg/De-Mercedes-Benz.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>),[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] commonly referred to simply as **Mercedes** and occasionally as **Benz**, is a German luxury [automotive brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automotive_brand) that was founded in 1926. **Mercedes-Benz AG** (a subsidiary of the [Mercedes-Benz Group](42977.md), established in 2019) is based in [Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart), Baden-Württemberg, Germany.[^cite_note-Daimler_structure-1] Mercedes-Benz AG manufactures [luxury vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxury_vehicle) and [light commercial vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_commercial_vehicle), all branded under the Mercedes-Benz name. From November 2019 onwards, the production of Mercedes-Benz-branded heavy commercial vehicles (trucks and buses) has been managed by [Daimler Truck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daimler_Truck), which separated from the Mercedes-Benz Group to form an independent entity at the end of 2021.

In 2018, Mercedes-Benz became the world's largest premium vehicle brand, with a sales volume of 2.31 million passenger cars.[^cite_note-9]

The roots of the brand trace back to the 1901 [Mercedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_\(marque\)) by [Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft) and the 1886 [Benz Patent-Motorwagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benz_Patent-Motorwagen) and 1894 [Benz Velo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benz_Velo) by [Carl Benz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Benz), which is widely recognised as the first automobile powered by an internal combustion engine. The brand’s slogan is "The Best or Nothing".[^cite_note-10]

## History

See also: [List of companies involved in the Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_companies_involved_in_the_Holocaust), [Diesel emissions scandal](53648498.md), and [History of the automobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_automobile)



[Carl Benz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Benz) (1844–1929) made the 1886 [Benz Patent Motorwagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benz_Patent_Motorwagen), which is widely regarded as the first automobile.

Mercedes-Benz traces its origins to [Carl Benz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Benz)'s first internal combustion engine in a car, seen in the Benz Patent-Motorwagen – financed by [Bertha Benz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertha_Benz)'s dowry[^cite_note-EAHF-11] and patented in January 1886[^cite_note-MBoverview-12] – and [Gottlieb Daimler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottlieb_Daimler) and its engineer [Wilhelm Maybach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Maybach)'s conversion of a stagecoach, with the addition of a petrol engine, introduced later that year. The [Mercedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_\(car_brand\)) automobile was first marketed in 1901 by [Daimler Motoren Gesellschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daimler_Motoren_Gesellschaft) (DMG).

[Emil Jellinek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emil_Jellinek), an Austrian automobile entrepreneur who worked with DMG, registered the trademark in 1902, naming the 1901 Mercedes 35 hp after his daughter [Mercédès Jellinek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merc%C3%A9d%C3%A8s_Jellinek). Jellinek was a businessman and marketing strategist who promoted "horseless" Daimler automobiles among the highest circles of society. At the time, it was a meeting place for the *haute volée* of France and Europe, especially in winter. His customers included the [Rothschild family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rothschild_family) and other wealthy clients, but as early as 1901, he was selling Mercedes cars in the "[New World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_World)" as well, including to billionaires [Rockefeller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockefeller_family), [Astor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astor_family), [Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_family), and Taylor. At the Nice race he attended in 1899, Jellinek drove under the pseudonym "Monsieur Mercédès". Many consider that race the birth of Mercedes-Benz as a brand. In 1901, the name "Mercedes" was re-registered by DMG worldwide as a protected trademark. The first Mercedes-Benz branded vehicles were produced in 1926, following the merger of Karl Benz and Gottlieb Daimler's companies into the Daimler-Benz company on 28 June of the same year.[^cite_note-MBoverview-12][^cite_note-Markenregister-13]



[Gottlieb Daimler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottlieb_Daimler) (1834–1900) – founder of [Daimler Motoren Gesellschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daimler_Motoren_Gesellschaft)

Gottlieb Daimler was born on 17 March 1834 in Schorndorf. After training as a gunsmith and working in France, he attended the Polytechnic School in Stuttgart from 1857 to 1859. After completing various technical activities in France and England, he started working as a draftsman in Geislingen in 1862. At the end of 1863, he was appointed workshop inspector at a machine-tool factory in Reutlingen, where he met Wilhelm Maybach in 1865.[^cite_note-14]

Throughout the 1930s, Mercedes-Benz produced the [770](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_770) model, a car that was notably popular throughout Germany's [Nazi](21212.md) period. [Adolf Hitler](2731583.md) was known to have driven in a model of this car during his time in power, with modified custom [bulletproof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulletproof) windshields.[^cite_note-15] However, most of the currently surviving 770 models were sold at auctions due to private buyers. One of the cars is currently on display at the [War Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_War_Museum) in Ottawa, [Ontario](22218.md).[^cite_note-Junk_Jet_n°2-16]

From 1937 onward, Daimler Benz focused increasingly on military products such as the [LG3000 lorry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_L_3000) and the [DB600](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daimler-Benz_DB_600) and the [DB601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daimler-Benz_DB_601) aero engines. To build the latter, in 1936, it built a factory hidden in the forest at [Genshagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwigsfelde#Economy_and_infrastructure) around 10 km south of [Berlin](3354.md). By 1942, the company had mostly stopped producing cars, and was now devoted to war production. According to its own statement, in 1944, almost half of its 63,610 employees were forced labourers, prisoners of war, or concentration-camp detainees.[^cite_note-17] Another source quotes this figure at 46,000. The company later paid \$12 million in reparations to the labourers' families.[^cite_note-18]

In 1958, the two companies began a partnership to sell its cars in the United States with [Studebaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studebaker#Contract_with_Curtiss-Wright). A few American-based Daimler Benz dealerships were converted into Mercedes-Benz dealerships when Daimler's non-Mercedes-partnered company closed in 1966.

Over the decades, Mercedes-Benz has introduced many electronic and mechanical [innovations](#Innovations) and safety features that later became common.[^cite_note-autogenerated2-19] Currently, Mercedes-Benz is one of the best-known and longest-standing automotive brands in the world. The pontiff's [Popemobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popemobile) has often been sourced from Mercedes-Benz.[^cite_note-Junk_Jet_n°2-16]

In November 2019, Daimler AG announced that Mercedes-Benz, until that point a company marque, would be spun off into a separate, wholly owned subsidiary called Mercedes-Benz AG. The new subsidiary would manage the Mercedes-Benz car and van business. Mercedes-Benz-badged trucks and buses would be part of the Daimler Truck AG subsidiary.[^cite_note-Daimler_structure-1]

For information relating to the three-pointed star symbol of the brand, see under the title [Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft), including the merger into Daimler-Benz.

In May 2022, Mercedes-Benz announced that it had recently sold the most expensive car at the price of \$142 million (€135 million).[^cite_note-20] The car is a very rare 1955 Mercedes-Benz SLR that has been kept in the German automaker's collection and bought by a private owner. Mercedes in an announcement said that the sale will be used to establish the Mercedes-Benz Fund.[^cite_note-21]

In June 2022, Mercedes-Benz recalled almost one million vehicles built between 2004 and 2015, due to potential problems with their braking system, caused by possible "advanced corrosion".[^cite_note-22]

## Subsidiaries and alliances

As of the Daimler AG company split, the Mercedes-Benz Cars division now handled the Mercedes-Benz- and the [Smart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_\(marque\))-branded cars' production.[^cite_note-23]

### Mercedes-AMG

[Mercedes-AMG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-AMG) became a majority-owned division of Mercedes-Benz in 1999.[^cite_note-24] The company was integrated into DaimlerChrysler in 1999,[^cite_note-25] and became Mercedes-Benz AMG on 1 January 1999.[^cite_note-26]

### Mercedes-Maybach

Daimler's ultra-luxury [Maybach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maybach) brand was under the Mercedes-Benz Cars division until December 2012, when production was stopped due to decreased sales.[^cite_note-27] It now exists under the [Mercedes-Maybach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Maybach) name, with the models being luxury-focused, enhanced models of Mercedes-Benz cars, such as the 2016 [Mercedes-Maybach S600](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Maybach_S600).[^cite_note-28] The [Mercedes-Maybach GLS 600](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_GLS_\(X167\)) SUV debuted in November 2019.[^cite_note-29]

### China

Daimler partnered with [BYD Auto](1461760.md) to make and sell a battery-electric car called [Denza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denza) in China.[^cite_note-30] However, after reducing its stake in 2021, the Stuttgart-based company has now exited Denza completely: the Mercedes-Benz Group is no longer involved in the Chinese car brand Denza, as it sold its last ten per cent stake in Denza in July 2024.[^cite_note-31] In 2016, Daimler announced plans to sell Mercedes-Benz-badged fully electric battery cars in China.[^cite_note-32] [Beijing Benz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Benz) is a joint venture with the [BAIC Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAIC_Group) to produce the Mercedes-Benz branded cars in China.[^cite_note-dameofficial-33]

### Vietnam

Established in 1995, Mercedes-Benz Vietnam became a joint venture between Saigon Mechanical Engineering Corporation (SAMCO) and [Daimler AG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daimler_AG).[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35] Daimler AG assembles various Mercedes-Benz models from CKD kits at its factory in [Ho Chi Minh City](66956.md).[^cite_note-\:2-36][^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38] The factory is a joint venture with [Saigon Mechanical Engineering Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Gon_Mechanical_Engineering_Corporation).[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41] In 2021–2022, Mercedes-Benz Vietnam invested approximately 33 million USD to upgrade six departments at this factory in preparation for the assembly of AMG models.[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43] The 1996 Mercedes E230 was the first model produced by the Mercedes-Benz Vietnam assembly plant and was part of the initial batch assembled locally.[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46][^cite_note-\:2-36]

### Quality rankings



Mercedes-Benz dealer in Munich, Germany

Before the mid 1990s, Mercedes-Benz had a very strong reputation for quality and durability. Mercedes-Benz's passenger cars were considered "tanks" or "indestructible" by owners who had their own Mercedes for very long periods. Some objective reviewers looking at passenger vehicles, such as [J. D. Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._D._Power) surveys, demonstrated a downturn in reputation by these criteria in the late 1990s and early 2000s. By mid-2005, Mercedes temporarily returned to the industry average for initial quality, a measure of problems after the first 90 days of ownership, according to J. D. Power.[^cite_note-70] In J. D. Power's Initial Quality Study for the first quarter of 2007, Mercedes showed dramatic improvement by climbing from 25th to 5th place and earning several awards for its newer models.[^cite_note-71] For 2008, Mercedes-Benz's initial quality rating improved by yet another mark, to 4th place.[^cite_note-2008jdpower-72] On top of this accolade, it also received the [Platinum Plant Quality Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platinum_Plant_Quality_Award?action=edit\&redlink=1) for its Mercedes branded Sindelfingen, Germany body assembly plant.[^cite_note-2008jdpower-72] J. D. Power's 2011 US Initial Quality and Vehicle Dependability Studies both ranked Mercedes-Benz vehicles above average in build quality and reliability.[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74] In J. D. Power's United Kingdom Survey in 2011, Mercedes cars were rated above average.[^cite_note-75] Additionally, [iSeeCars.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISeeCars.com) study for [Reuters](18998750.md) in 2014 found Mercedes to have the lowest vehicle recall rate out of competitors.[^cite_note-76]

### Car nomenclature

#### Pre-1994

Until 1994, Mercedes-Benz utilized an alphanumeric system for categorizing its vehicles, consisting of a number sequence approximately equal to the engine's displacement in liters multiplied by 100, followed by an arrangement of alphabetical suffixes, indicating body style and engine type.

- "C" indicates a coupe or cabriolet body style (for example, the CL and CLK models, though the C-Class is an exception, since it is also available as a sedan or station wagon).

- "D" indicates the vehicle is equipped with a diesel engine.

- "E" (for "Einspritzung") indicates the vehicle's engine is equipped with petrol [fuel injection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuel_injection). Also used for electric models and plug-in hybrids.

- "G" was originally used for the [Geländewagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_G-Class) off-road vehicle, but is now applied to Mercedes SUVs in general (G, GLA, GLC, GLE, GLK, and GLS).

- "K" was used in the 1930s, indicating a supercharger ("Kompressor") equipped engine. Three exceptions : the SLK, SSK and CLK, where K indicates "Kurz" (short-wheelbase) (though the SLK

  [^cite_note-80]

  &#x20;and SSK had a supercharger).

- "L" indicates "Leicht" (lightweight) for sporting models and "Lang" (long-wheelbase) for sedan models.

- "R" indicates "Rennen" (racing), used for racing cars (for example, the [300SLR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_300SLR)).

- "S" Sonderklasse "Special class" for flagship models, including the S-Class, SLR McLaren, and SLS sports cars.

- "T" indicates "Touring" and an estate (or station wagon) body style.

Some models in the 1950s also had lower-case letters (b, c, and d) to indicate specific trim levels. For other models, the numeric part of the designation does not match the engine displacement. This was done to show the model's position in the model range independent of displacement or in the price matrix. For these vehicles, the actual displacement in litres is suffixed to the model designation. An exception was the [190-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_W201) with the numeric designation of "190" as to denote its entry-level in the model along with the displacement label on the right side of the boot (190E 2.3 for 2.3-litre 4-cylinder petrol motor, 190D 2.5 for 2.5-litre 5-cylinder diesel motor, and so forth). Some older models (such as the SS and SSK) did not have a number as part of the designation at all.

#### 1994 to 2014

For the 1994 model year, Mercedes-Benz revised the naming system. Models were divided into "classes" denoted by an arrangement of up to three letters (see "Current model range" above. This in turn is followed by a three-digit (two-digit for AMG models) number related to the engine displacement as before. Variants of the same model such as an estate version or a vehicle with a diesel engine are no longer given a separate letter. The SLR and SLS supercars do not carry a numerical designation.

Some models carry further designations indicating special features:

- "[4MATIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4MATIC)" indicates the vehicle is equipped with [all-wheel-drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-wheel_drive).

- "[BlueTEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BlueTec)" indicates a diesel engine with [selective catalytic reduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selective_catalytic_reduction) exhaust after-treatment.

- "BlueEFFICIENCY" indicates special fuel economy features (direct injection, [start-stop system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Start-stop_system), aerodynamic modifications, etc.)

- "CGI" (Charged Gasoline Injection) indicates [direct petrol injection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_fuel_injection).

- "CDI" (Common-rail Direct Injection) indicates a [common-rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_rail) diesel engine.

- "Hybrid" indicates a petrol- or diesel-electric [hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_vehicle).

- "NGT" indicates a natural gas-fuelled engine.

- "Kompressor" indicates a [supercharged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercharger) engine.

- "Turbo" indicates a [turbocharged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turbocharger) engine, only used on A-, B-, E- and GLK-Class models.

- "AMG Line" indicates the interior or engine, depending which car, has been fitted with the luxuries of its AMG sports cars.

Model designation badges can be removed at the request of the customer.

#### 2015 and beyond

Rationalisation of the model nomenclature was announced in November 2014 for future models.[^cite_note-81][^cite_note-82] The changes consolidate many confusing nomenclature and their placements in the model range.

The new nomenclature was originally divided into four segments: core models; off-road vehicles and SUVs; 4-door coupés, and roadsters. Other segments were unaffected, and the [Mercedes-EQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-EQ) line was introduced in 2016.&#x20;

| Segment | Nomenclature | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core models | **A**, **B**, **C**, **E**, or **S** | E-Class |
| Off/road vehicles/SUVs | **"GL" + the core equivalent** | GLE |
| 4-door coupés | **"CL" + the core equivalent** | CLE |
| Roadsters | **the core equivalent + "L"** | SL |
| Electric vehicles | **"EQ" + the core equivalent** | EQE |

Under this new nomenclature, the GLK-Class became the GLC, the M-Class became the GLE, and the GL-Class became the GLS. The CL-Class became the S-Class Coupé, which has since been discontinued. Another model, the X-Class (based on the [Nissan Navara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nissan_Navara) pickup truck) was produced from 2017 to 2020.&#x20;

The numerical trim designation was kept from the previous nomenclature. However, many numerical designations no longer reflect the engine's actual displacement, but more of the relative performance and marketing position. For example, the GLC 300 actually has a 2.0-litre engine. Recent AMG models use the "63" designation (in honour of the 1960s 6.3-litre [M100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_M100_engine) engine) despite being equipped with either a 6.2-litre ([M156](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_M156_engine)), a 5.5-litre ([M157](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_M278_engine)) or even a 4.0-litre engine.

In addition to the revised nomenclature, Mercedes-Benz has new suffix nomenclature for the drive systems:

| Previous | New | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural Gas Drive | **c for "compressed natural gas"** | B 200 c |
| BlueTec CDI | **d for "diesel"** | E 350 d GLA 200 d |
| PLUG-IN HYBRID Electric Drive | **e for "electric"** | S 500 e B 250 e |
| Fuel Cell | **f for "fuel cell"** | B 200 f |
| HYBRID BlueTEC HYBRID | **h for "hybrid"** | S 400 h E 300 h |
| 4MATIC | **4MATIC (all-wheel drive)** | E 400 4MATIC |

The revised A45 AMG for the 2016 model year on has shifted the model designation to the right side while AMG is on the left side.[^cite_note-83] This trend commenced with Mercedes-Maybach with MAYBACH on the left and S500/S600 on the right.[^cite_note-84]

### Environmental record

Mercedes-Benz has developed multi-[concept cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concept_vehicle) with alternative propulsion, such as hybrid-electric, fully electric, and fuel-cell [powertrains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powertrain). ## Motorsport

Main article: [Mercedes-Benz in motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_in_motorsport)



A DMG [Mercedes Simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_Simplex) 1906 in the [Deutsches Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsches_Museum)

The two companies which were merged to form the *Mercedes-Benz* brand in 1926 had both already enjoyed success in the new sport of [motor racing](1022.md) throughout their separate histories. A single Benz competed in the world's first motor race, the [1894 Paris–Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris%E2%80%93Rouen_\(motor_race\)), where Émile Roger finished 14th in 10 hours 1 minute. Throughout its long history, the company has been involved in a range of motorsport activities, including [sports car racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_car_racing) and [rallying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rallying). On several occasions, Mercedes-Benz has withdrawn completely from motorsport for a significant period, notably in the late 1930s, and after the [1955 Le Mans disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1955_Le_Mans_disaster), where a [Mercedes-Benz 300 SLR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_300_SLR) rammed another car (an Austin-Healey), took off into the stands, and killed more than 80 spectators. [Stirling Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stirling_Moss) and co-driver [Denis Jenkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Jenkinson) won the 1955 [Mille Miglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mille_Miglia) road race in Italy during a record-breaking drive with an average speed of almost 98 mph in a [Mercedes-Benz 300 SLR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_300_SLR).[^cite_note-115]

Although there was some activity in the intervening years, it was not until 1987 that Mercedes-Benz returned to front line competition, returning to Le Mans, [Deutsche Tourenwagen Meisterschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Tourenwagen_Meisterschaft) (DTM), and [Formula One](10854.md) with [Sauber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauber). The 1990s saw Mercedes-Benz partner with British engine builder [Ilmor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilmor) (now [Mercedes-Benz High Performance Engines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_High_Performance_Engines)), and campaign [IndyCars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series) under the [USAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Automobile_Club)/[CART](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Championship_Auto_Racing_Teams) rules, eventually winning the [1994 Indianapolis 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Indianapolis_500) and [1994 CART IndyCar World Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_CART_World_Series_season) Championship with [Al Unser Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Unser_Jr.) at the wheel. The 1990s also saw the return of Mercedes-Benz to GT racing with the [Mercedes-Benz CLK GTR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_CLK_GTR), which took the company to two titles in FIA's GT1 class.

Mercedes-Benz is currently active in two motorsport categories, [Formula One](10854.md) and [GT racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GT_racing).

### Formula One

Main article: [Mercedes-Benz in Formula One](25098794.md)



Both [Mercedes-AMG](25098794.md) Formula One cars at the [2013 Malaysian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Malaysian_Grand_Prix)

Mercedes-Benz took part in the world championship in 1954 and 1955, but despite being successful with two championship titles for [Juan-Manuel Fangio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan-Manuel_Fangio),[^cite_note-116] the company left the sport after just two seasons.

Mercedes-Benz returned as an engine manufacturer in 1994, with the engines being designed and manufactured by [Ilmor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilmor) in [Brixworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brixworth).[^cite_note-117] It initially partnered with [Sauber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauber_Motorsport), before switching to [McLaren](20994.md) in 1995. Although the Mercedes engines were not successful at first, it later won drivers' championships for [Mika Häkkinen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mika_H%C3%A4kkinen) in 1998 and 1999, and for [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) in 2008, as well as a constructors' championship in 1998. Mercedes part-owned McLaren, and the collaboration had been extended into the production of road-going cars such as the [Mercedes-Benz SLR McLaren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_SLR_McLaren).

In 2007, McLaren-Mercedes was fined a record US\$100 million for stealing confidential Ferrari technical data.[^cite_note-118]

In 2009, [Ross Brawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Brawn)'s newly conceived Formula One team, [Brawn GP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brawn_GP) used Mercedes engines to win the titles. At the end of the season, Mercedes-Benz sold its 40 percent stake in McLaren to the McLaren Group and bought 70% of the Brawn GP team jointly with an [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md)-based investment consortium. Brawn GP was renamed [Mercedes GP](25098794.md) for the 2010 season and became the main team for Mercedes-Benz. The company continued providing engines to other teams under customer relationships.[^cite_note-119]

After major rule changes in [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Formula_One_World_Championship), Mercedes clinched the drivers' and constructors' titles with drivers [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) and [Nico Rosberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_Rosberg), after dominating much of the season. Mercedes repeated the feat in [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Formula_One_World_Championship), winning 16 out of 19 races, and again in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Formula_One_World_Championship), winning 19 of the 21 races. In the following years, Mercedes continued its success by winning the drivers' championships from [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Formula_One_World_Championship) to [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Formula_One_World_Championship) and the constructors' championships from [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Formula_One_World_Championship) to [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Formula_One_World_Championship), becoming the first team to win seven consecutive "double-championships".[^cite_note-\:1-120][^cite_note-\:0-121][^cite_note-122] In these years, Hamilton was the champion in 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020, while Rosberg won in 2016.[^cite_note-\:1-120] Their unbeaten streak was broken in 2021, when [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) of [Red Bull](1173193.md)-[Honda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_in_Formula_One) won the drivers' championship.

## Innovations

Numerous technological innovations have been introduced on Mercedes-Benz automobiles throughout the many years of their production, including:

- The [internal combustion engine](41228673.md) automobile was developed independently by Benz and Daimler & Maybach in 1886.

- Daimler invented the honeycomb radiator of the type still used on all water-cooled vehicles today.

- Daimler invented the float [carburetor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carburetor) which was used until replaced by [fuel injection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuel_injection).

- The "drop chassis" is the car originally designated the "Mercedes" by Daimler; it was also the first car with a modern configuration, having the carriage lowered and set between the front and rear wheels, with a [front engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front-engine,_rear-wheel-drive_layout) and [powered rear wheels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rear-wheel_drive). All earlier cars were "horseless carriages", which had high centres of gravity and various engine/drive-train configurations.

- The first passenger road car to have [brakes](65423.md) on all four wheels (1924).

  [^cite_note-autogenerated1-133]

- In 1936, the [Mercedes-Benz 260 D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_260_D) was the first diesel-powered passenger car.

- Mercedes-Benz were the first to offer direct fuel injection on the [Mercedes-Benz 300SL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_300SL) Gullwing.

- The "safety cage" or "[safety cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile_safety)" construction with front and rear [crumple zones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crumple_zones) was first developed by Mercedes-Benz in 1951. This revolutionised the automotive industry with passive safety.

  [^cite_note-134]

  [^cite_note-autogenerated2-19]

- In 1959, Mercedes-Benz patented a device that prevents drive wheels from spinning by intervening at the engine, transmission, or brakes. In 1987, Mercedes-Benz applied for its patent by introducing a [traction control system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traction_control_system) that worked under both braking and acceleration.

- An [anti-lock braking system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-lock_braking_system) (ABS) was first offered on the W116 450SEL 6.9. They became standard on the W126 S-Class starting production in 1979 and first sold in most markets in 1980.

- [Airbags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbag) were first introduced in the European market, beginning with the model year 1981 S-Class.

- Mercedes-Benz was the first to introduce pre-tensioners to [seat belts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seat_belt) on the 1981 S-Class. In the event of a crash, a pre-tensioner will tighten the belt instantaneously, removing any 'slack' in the belt, which prevents the occupant from jerking forward in a crash.

- In September 2003, Mercedes-Benz introduced the world's first seven-speed [automatic transmission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automatic_transmission) called '[7G-Tronic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7G-Tronic)'.

### Robot cars

Main article: [Driverless car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Driverless_car)

In the 1980s, Mercedes built the world's first robot car, together with the team of Professor [Ernst Dickmanns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_Dickmanns) at the [University of the Bundeswehr Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Bundeswehr_Munich).[^cite_note-140] Encouraged in part by Dickmanns' success, in 1987 the European Union's [EUREKA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EUREKA) programme initiated the [Prometheus Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EUREKA_Prometheus_Project) on autonomous vehicles, funded to the tune of nearly €800 million. In 1995 Dickmanns' re-engineered autonomous [S-Class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_S-Class) Mercedes took a long trip from [Munich](19058.md) in Bavaria to [Copenhagen](5166.md) in Denmark, and back. On highways, the robot achieved speeds exceeding 175 km/h (109 mph) (permissible in some areas of the German [Autobahn](1764276.md)).

In October 2015, the company introduced the Vision Tokyo, a five-seat self-driving electric van powered by a hybrid hydrogen fuel-cell system. The super-sleek van is touted as "a chill-out zone in the midst of megacity traffic mayhem."[^cite_note-141]

## Tuners

Mercedes-Benz sponsors [Bundesliga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundesliga) club [VfB Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VfB_Stuttgart) and provided until June 2023 the naming rights for its stadium, the former [Mercedes-Benz Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_Arena_\(Stuttgart\)).[^cite_note-144] The company formerly held a ten-year naming rights contract to the [Caesars Superdome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesars_Superdome), an [American football](18951490.md) stadium in [New Orleans](53842.md), from 2011 to 2021.[^cite_note-145] On 24 August 2015, Mercedes-Benz was announced as the naming rights sponsor for the [Atlanta Falcons](2103.md)' new home, [Mercedes-Benz Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_Stadium), (Mercedes-Benz's US headquarters are in [Greater Atlanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Atlanta)) which opened in August 2017.[^cite_note-MB_Stadium_Atlanta-146]

Mercedes-Benz worked with English magician Steven Frayne, also known as [Dynamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamo_\(magician\)), to create a video called Dynamo vs Coulthard.[^cite_note-147] [Formula One](10854.md) driver [David Coulthard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Coulthard) drove Dynamo around a track at race-speed in a [Mercedes-Benz SL63 AMG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_SL-Class), before Dynamo successfully drove around the same track in the same car whilst blindfolded and surrounded by pyrotechnics.[^cite_note-148] The stunt was part of the finale for Series 3 of [Dynamo: Magician Impossible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamo:_Magician_Impossible), screened on UK television channel [Watch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_\(UK_TV_channel\)).

To promote the release of *[Mario Kart 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Kart_8)*, [Nintendo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo) and Mercedes-Benz partnered up to have three of its cars in its history, the [2014 Mercedes-Benz GLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_GLA-Class), the [1957 SL 300 Roadster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_300_SL), and the [1934 W25 Silver Arrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_W25) as karts in the game. As part of the partnership, [Mario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario), [Luigi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_\(character\)), and [Peach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Peach) appeared in Japanese commercials to promote the 2014 Mercedes-Benz GLA.[^cite_note-149]

The Mercedes-Benz was chosen by [Mitsubishi Electric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Electric) as an official sponsor of [AFF Mitsubishi Electric Cup 2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_AFF_Championship), along with [Maspion Holdings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maspion), [Wuling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuling_Motors), [Gree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gree_Electric), [Yanmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yanmar) and [Tiger Brokers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_Brokers).
