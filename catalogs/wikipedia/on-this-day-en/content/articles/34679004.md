The **Ministry of Interior** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الداخلية, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Wizārat al-Dākhilīyah*) is a [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_\(Syria\)) ministry of [Syria](7515849.md), responsible for [internal affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior_ministry). Its headquarters were located on [Kafr Sousa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kafr_Sousa) in [Damascus](8914.md).[^cite_note-1] According to the Ministry official website, its tasks are limited to the protection and enforcement of security.[^cite_note-SARMOITasks-2]

The former headquarters of the Ministry of Interior was located at the Grand Serail which was established in 1900 near [Marjeh Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marjeh_Square).[^cite_note-3] The building was transferred to the [Ministry of Tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Tourism_\(Syria\)) in 2011 for use as a hotel.[^cite_note-4]

## Organization

The Ministry of Interior is responsible for the administration of several law enforcement agencies in Syria, including:

- [Internal Security Forces](78776643.md)

- [General Intelligence Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Intelligence_Service_\(Syria\))

- [General Authority of Borders and Customs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Authority_for_Land_and_Sea_Ports)

- [Criminal Investigations Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_Investigations_Department_\(Syria\)?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Traffic Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_Department_\(Syria\)?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Drug Enforcement Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_Enforcement_Department_\(Syria\))

- [Tourist Police Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourist_Police_Department_\(Syria\))

- [Immigration and Passport Directorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immigration_and_Passport_Directorate?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Directorate of Airport and Border Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directorate_of_Airport_and_Border_Security_\(Syria\)?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Special Missions Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Missions_Department_\(Syria\))

- [Desert Security Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_Security_Forces_\(Syria\)?action=edit\&redlink=1)
