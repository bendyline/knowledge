The **2026–27 NBA season** is the 81st season of the [National Basketball Association](22093.md) (NBA). The regular season is scheduled to run from October 20, 2026, to April 11, 2027. The fourth edition of the in-season [NBA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Cup) tournament is planned to run from October 30 to December 11, 2026. The [2027 NBA All-Star Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_NBA_All-Star_Game) is scheduled for February 21, 2027, at the [Mortgage Matchup Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortgage_Matchup_Center) in [Phoenix, Arizona](49121.md). The play-in tournament is then planned for April 13–16, following with the playoffs, and ending with the NBA Finals in June.[^cite_note-1]

- On July 4, 2026, [Garrett Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garrett_Temple) was hired as an assistant coach for the [Dallas Mavericks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Mavericks). He played for 12 teams over a 17-year career.

Lowry was the last active player from the [2006 NBA draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_NBA_draft).

### NBA Cup

Main article: [2026 NBA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NBA_Cup)

The [NBA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Cup) is expected to have the same basic structure as previous seasons:[^cite_note-19]

- All games except the championship final count towards the regular-season standings.

- Six intraconference pools of five (three pools per conference).

- Group games against each of the other teams in their pool (two at home and two on the road) will be held on Fridays and other select days in November.

- The winners of each pool (three teams per conference) and two wild-card teams (one team per conference) advance to a single-elimination tournament in early December.

- To compensate, only 80 regular-season games for each team is initially announced during the offseason. The first two rounds of the tournament count as regular-season games 81 and 82. The championship game is an extra 83rd game that does not count toward the regular season. Teams that do not qualify for knockout round or is eliminated in the quarterfinals will be scheduled additional games against other teams eliminated in the same conference (if possible) and round to reach 82 games.

- The semifinals will move to the home arenas of the higher-seeded teams in each conference.

- The championship game for this edition will be held at [Hinkle Fieldhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinkle_Fieldhouse) in [Indianapolis](57707.md), [Indiana](21883857.md) as part of a new vision to host the game at more distinct and iconic basketball arenas. It will replace [T-Mobile Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-Mobile_Arena) in [Paradise, Nevada](124384.md), which hosted the championship game during the first three editions of the Cup.

## Uniform and logo changes

- The [Houston Rockets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston_Rockets) unveiled new uniforms resembling the "ketchup and mustard" look that was used during their back-to-back championships in [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_NBA_Finals) and [1995](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_NBA_Finals). The "R" logo remains as the team's primary mark, but with an added yellow accent to reflect its reintroduction into the Rockets' color scheme.

  [^cite_note-22]

- The [Minnesota Timberwolves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Timberwolves) unveiled a new logo and uniforms. The new logo resembles their previous mark introduced in 2017, but using a color scheme similar to the one used from the franchise's inception in 1989 until 1996. The Icon and Association uniforms also resemble the original look of the Timberwolves while the Statement uniform resembles their black uniforms from the [Kevin Garnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Garnett) era.

##### Postseason

Amazon Prime Video will stream all [play-in tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_play-in_tournament) games. The first two playoff rounds will then be split between Amazon Prime Video, ESPN/ABC and NBC/Peacock. Amazon Prime Video will have between nine and 17 first-round games, and between five and nine second-round games. ESPN and ABC will air approximately 18 games in the first two rounds. NBC and/or Peacock will air between 15 and 23 first-round games, and between seven and 11 second-round games, with at least half of all of them airing on NBC.[^cite_note-Karangu-38]

Per a rotation, ESPN/ABC will air a conference final, with this season being the Western Conference Finals, and Amazon Prime Video will exclusively stream the Eastern Conference Finals this season.[^cite_note-39] ABC will exclusively air the [NBA Finals](890793.md) for its 25th consecutive season.[^cite_note-Karangu-38]

## Notable occurrences

- The NBA resumed its tradition of not scheduling any games on [Election Day in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election_Day_in_the_United_States), which falls on November 3 in [2026](70357296.md). The league began this practice for three consecutive years in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_NBA_season#Notable_occurrences), [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_NBA_season#Notable_occurrences) and [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_NBA_season#Notable_occurrences) to encourage fans to vote, but then held Election Day games on November 4, [2025](79260681.md).

  [^cite_note-77]

- On September 2, the NBA announced punishments related to the [Los Angeles Clippers salary cap scandal](84132049.md). The Clippers were stripped of five first-round picks, one each from 2029 to 2033, given a \$30 million fine, and owner [Steve Ballmer](29656.md) was suspended from league and team activities for one year. [Kawhi Leonard](31163849.md) was required to pay the league \$700,000, and two executives were also suspended without pay.
