**Arpora** is a coastal village close to the [North Goa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Goa) beach belt.

## Role in the traditional salt industry

Traditionally, it has been a coastal village, known for its traditional salt-making industry. This aspect of the village has been studied in a book, *As Dear as Salt*, which studies four traditional salt-making villages of [Goa](40010153.md) (including Arpora).[^cite_note-1]

## Night market

In recent times, it is more known for its night market, "The Saturday Night Market". This is open during the fair-weather tourist season (around September to March). Items sold range from musical [mouth harps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonica) or varieties of food.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] It also has live musical performances.[^cite_note-4]

## History

Arpora is known for St Joseph's, a school that was the first in Goa to offer education in the [English language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language). It was set up by William Robert Lyons, a British priest who inculcated sports into the curriculum, introducing [Goans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goan) to football in 1883.[^cite_note-5]

On 6 December 2025, a [fire at a nightclub](81780663.md) in Arpora killed 25 people.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
