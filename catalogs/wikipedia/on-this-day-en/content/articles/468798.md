**Asyūṭ** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة أسيوط) is one of the 27 [governorates of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorates_of_Egypt). It stretches across a section of the [Nile](21244.md) River.[^cite_note-Google_Maps_Asyut_Gov-5] The capital of the governorate is the city of [Asyut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asyut).[^cite_note-6]

## Etymology

The name of Asyut is derived from [early Egyptian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_language) *Zawty* (*Z3JW\.TJ*), [late Egyptian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_language) *Səyáwt*, into [Coptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coptic_language) *Syowt*. An *A* was added to the beginning of the name *Syowt* to become Asyut.[^cite_note-Co1905-7]

## Overview

The rate of poverty in Asyut is more than 60%. Recently, some social safety networks have been provided, in the form of financial assistance and job opportunities. The funding has been coordinated by [Egypt's Ministry of Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Finance_\(Egypt\)), with assistance from international organizations.[^cite_note-Takaful_and_Karama-8]

## Population

According to 2024 estimates, the majority of residents in the governorate live in rural areas, with an urbanization rate of only 26.5%. Out of an estimated 5,169,423 people residing in the governorate, 3,799,525 live in rural areas, as opposed to only 1,369,898 in urban areas.[^cite_note-pop-9]

## Demographics

Asyut has a population of over 4 million people, with a significant [Coptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copts_in_Egypt) presence. Muslims and Christians have lived together in Asyut and at times there have been clashes. In July 2013, a large number of Christians took to the streets to protest Muslim extremism in Asyut.[^cite_note-Protests-10]

## Projects and programs

In 2016, Switzerland committed to funding a solid waste management program in Asyut, a project with the [Egyptian Ministry of Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Environment_\(Egypt\)) that will conclude in 2021. The *National Solid Waste Management Programme* (NSWMP) involves the construction of infrastructure for new as well as the expansion and improvement of existing waste treatment, landfill, and recycling facilities.[^cite_note-Swiss_waste_mgmt_2016-12]

## Important sites

Ancient quarries are an important feature of Asyut. There are about 500 rock-cut tombs and limestone quarries all around Asyut.[^cite_note-geomorphology-13] The governorate of Asyut includes the [Ancient Egyptian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Egyptian) tombs of [Meir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meir,_Egypt),[^cite_note-Meir-14] and the town of [Durunka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durunka),[^cite_note-Dronka-15] which is a pilgrimage site for many [Copts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copts) who come to visit a monastery dedicated to the [Virgin Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_Mary).[^cite_note-Convent-16][^cite_note-CopticBook-17][^cite_note-Convent2016-18]
