The **vice president of South Sudan** is the second highest political position obtainable in [South Sudan](32350676.md). Additionally, a temporary position called *first vice president* was created in August 2015. In 2020, a coalition agreement was reached in 2020 creating five vice presidents.[^cite_note-theeastafrican.co.ke-1] The vice presidents of this [unity government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revitalised_Transitional_Government_of_National_Unity) were dismissed in September 2026 ahead of the [2026 elections](40095629.md).[^cite_note-2]

### First vice presidents of the Republic of South Sudan (2016–2020)

Following the signing of the Agreement on the Resolution of the Conflict in the Republic of South Sudan (ARCSS) in August 2015 a new position of 'first vice president' was established alongside the pre-existing positions of President of South Sudan and Vice President of South Sudan, with the incumbents in both these positions continuing in office.[^cite_note-4] Unlike the positions of president and vice president, which are permanent features of the Constitution, the office of first vice president will cease to exist following the end of the transitional period stipulated in the ARCSS unless otherwise decided in the permanent Constitution.

## Vice presidents in the Revitalised Government of National Unity (2020-2026)

The [Revitalised Transitional Government of National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revitalised_Transitional_Government_of_National_Unity) (RTGoNU) was formed in February 2020 and included five vice presidents.[^cite_note-theeastafrican.co.ke-1] Two new vice-presidents were appointed during a cabinet reshuffle on 10 February 2025.[^cite_note-sudantribune2-5] The vice presidents of the RTGoNU were dismissed in September 2026 ahead of the [2026 elections](40095629.md).[^cite_note-6]
