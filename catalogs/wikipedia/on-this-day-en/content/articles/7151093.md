**Kotovsk** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Кото́вск) is a [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) in [Tambov Oblast](480933.md), Russia, located on the [Tsna River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsna_River_\(Moksha_basin\)) 13 kilometers (8.1 mi) south of [Tambov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambov). Population:  31,850 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2010Census-2] 34,054 ([2002 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Russian_census));[^cite_note-PopCensus-6] 38,510 ([1989 Soviet census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Soviet_census)).[^cite_note-Census1989-7]

## History

Kotovsk was founded before [World War I](4764461.md) under the initiative of [Tsar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsar) [Nicholas II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_II_of_Russia) as a settlement for workers who were engaged in the construction of the [gunpowder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunpowder) factory (commissioned in 1912). The settlement was initially called **Porokhovoy Zavod** (Порохово́й Заво́д, lit. *gunpowder factory*). It was later renamed **Krasny Boyevik** (Кра́сный Боеви́к, lit. *red fighter*), because it was used by the [Bolsheviks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolshevik) as a lodgement for the establishment of the Soviet government in the region.[^cite_note-8] In 1940, it was granted town status and renamed Kotovsk after [Grigory Kotovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grigory_Kotovsky) (1881–1925), who had suppressed an anti-[Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) peasant rebellion in [Tambov Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambov_Governorate) in 1921.

The railway station was closed till 2022, when it became available for passenger transportation again.[^cite_note-9]

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), it is incorporated as the **[town of oblast significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance) of Kotovsk**—an administrative unit with the status equal to that of the [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Tambov_Oblast).[^cite_note-Ref142-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), the town of oblast significance of Kotovsk is incorporated as **Kotovsk Urban Okrug**.[^cite_note-Ref143-3]
