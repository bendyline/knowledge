As of January 2026, [Somaliland](51425.md) is [recognised](524626.md) as a [sovereign state](23604120.md) by one [UN member state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_member_state) – [Israel](9282173.md). The Republic of Somaliland was proclaimed on 18 May 1991 claiming to be the legal successor to the [State of Somaliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Somaliland) which gained independence from the United Kingdom on 26 June 1960 and merged into the [Somali Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_Republic) five days later. [Somalia](27358.md) continues to claim Somaliland as an integral part of its territory, considering it to be one of [its federal member states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Somalia).

In 2020, Taiwan and Somaliland began [engaging in ambiguous, high-level relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somaliland%E2%80%93Taiwan_relations). On 26 December 2025, Israel became the first country and UN member state to formally recognise Somaliland as an independent country.

## History

The region of modern day Somaliland was ruled by various groups including most recently the [Isaaq Sultanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaaq_Sultanate) before coming under British rule in 1884. While under British rule, [British Somaliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Somaliland) defined the borders that are today claimed by the Republic of Somaliland.[^cite_note-1]

In June 1960, British Somaliland gained its independence from the United Kingdom as the State of Somaliland.[^cite_note-\:0-2] The following month, in July 1960, the State of Somaliland merged with the [Trust Territory of Somaliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trust_Territory_of_Somaliland) to form Somalia.[^cite_note-\:0-2] In May 1991, Somaliland [declared independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somaliland_Declaration_of_Independence) from Somalia.[^cite_note-3]

In September 2017, Somaliland and the micronation [Liberland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberland)[^cite_note-5] signed a memorandum of understanding that "begun the process of mutual recognition".[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] In January 2024, Somaliland and Ethiopia signed a [memorandum of understanding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Ethiopia%E2%80%93Somaliland_memorandum_of_understanding) which include a provision that the latter would recognise the former as an independent state. However, Ethiopia later signed the [Ankara Declaration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara_Declaration) in December 2024 that reaffirmed support for Somalia's territorial integrity.

On 26 December 2025, Israel became the first UN member state to recognise Somaliland.[^cite_note-8] At the request of Algeria, Sierra Leone, Somalia, and Guyana, the [United Nations Security Council](31956.md) convened for an emergency session on 29 December following Israel's recognition of Somaliland.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]
