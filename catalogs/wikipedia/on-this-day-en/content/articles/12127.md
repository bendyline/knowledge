[Hans Egede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Egede) (1686–1758), [Lutheran](23371382.md) [missionary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missionary), credited with revitalising Denmark's relationship with Greenland

Being part of the [Kingdom of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Denmark), the **foreign relations of Greenland** are handled in cooperation between the [government of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Denmark) and the [government of Greenland](23389805.md). Unlike [Denmark proper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark_proper), [Greenland](12118.md) is not part of the [European Union](9317.md) (EU). The country's status was changed to an [Overseas Country and Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_member_state_territories_and_the_European_Union#Overseas_countries_and_territories) (OCT) associated with the EU, a dependent territory that has a special relationship with a member state of the EU. However, Greenland remains a part of the [Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Europe) and [NATO](21133.md) as part of Denmark.

## General aspects of diplomatic relations

Main article: [List of diplomatic missions in Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_missions_in_Greenland)

With the Kingdom of Denmark having the responsibility for Greenland's international affairs, other countries often do not have direct [diplomatic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatics) representation in Greenland—their embassies or consulates in Denmark are responsible for their relations with Greenland and their citizens in Greenland. Greenland is represented internationally by both the Greenland Representations and the embassies and consulates of Denmark. Further Greenland participates in the parliamentary Nordic Council and the Nordic Council of Ministers, and organisations as the [West Nordic Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Nordic_Council) and the EU-based [Overseas Countries and Territories Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_Countries_and_Territories_Association), the latter being former colonies of member states of the EU (dependent countries/territories).

The [United States](3434750.md) reopened its consulate in June 2020, in Nuuk, after it closed in 1953.[^cite_note-np20200611-1]

## Diplomatic representations



Greenlandic, Danish and U.S. politicians meeting at [Christiansborg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christiansborg_Palace) in Copenhagen during the [Greenland crisis](82044223.md), January 2026

The [Premier of Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_Greenland) is unable to speak to a foreign leader without a Danish ambassador present.[^cite_note-jungersen20250110-2] Greenland has representative offices in several countries and otherwise is represented by [embassies of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_missions_of_Denmark) worldwide. The Self-Government Act of 2009 allows the government of Greenland to open diplomatic offices, mainly within areas of full jurisdiction of Greenland, this being foreign trade, industry, fisheries, education, science, mining etc. Further Greenlandic [diplomats](82433.md), representing the government of Greenland, participate in areas of shared responsibilities between Denmark and Greenland, this mainly being observed in the defence cooperation between the Kingdom of Denmark and the United States.[^cite_note-berlingske20191104-3]
