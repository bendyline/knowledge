**Burmese in Malaysia** mostly comprise [manual labourers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manual_labour) and refugees. The [2014 Myanmar Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Myanmar_Census) enumerated 303,996 [Burmese individuals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_people) living in Malaysia.[^cite_note-cens-1] As of November 2014, there are around 139,200 Burmese refugees registered under [UNHCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNHCR) for which 50,620 are [Chins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuki_people), 40,070 are [Rohingyas](1918632.md), 12,160 [Panthays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panthays) and 7,440 others are [Rakhines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rakhine_people).[^cite_note-un-2] However, the [Malaysian government](9660028.md) does not officially recognise all newly arrived refugees as it may encourage more to enter Malaysia as Malaysian officials noted they may become a threat to national security.[^cite_note-3] Many of the new workers and refugees have frequently fallen into the hands of human traffickers and have been killed by other criminals.[^cite_note-4]
