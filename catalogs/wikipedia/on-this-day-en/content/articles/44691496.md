The **United States men's national ice sledge hockey team** represents the United States at the [World Para Ice Hockey Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Para_Ice_Hockey_Championships) and [Paralympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_ice_hockey_at_the_Winter_Paralympics). The team is overseen by [USA Hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Hockey). The team is one of the most successful in international para ice hockey, having won a record five Paralympic gold medals and a record seven World Championship titles.[^cite_note-1]
