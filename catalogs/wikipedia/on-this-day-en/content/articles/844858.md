**Manado** (Indonesian pronunciation: [\[maˈnado\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Malay), [Minahasan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minahasan_language): *Wenang*) is the capital [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_status_in_Indonesia) of the [Indonesian](14579.md) [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Indonesia) of [North Sulawesi](453688.md). It is the second largest city in [Sulawesi](28740.md) after [Makassar](389464.md), with the 2020 census giving a population of 451,916,[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-2] and the official estimates for mid 2025 showing 462,658 inhabitants (232,553 males and 230,105 females),[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1] distributed over a land area of 162.35 km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2024-3][^cite_note-4] The [Manado metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manado_metropolitan_area) had a population of 1,377,815 as of mid 2023.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2024-3] The city is situated on the Bay of Manado, and is surrounded by a mountainous area.[^cite_note-5]

Manado is among Indonesia's top-five tourism priorities[^cite_note-6] and [Bunaken National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunaken_National_Park) is one of the city's most famous tourist attractions. Tunan Waterfall in Talawaan village and Mount Tumpa are some of the many attractions for visitors who like to take Manado city tour especially to natural places.[^cite_note-7] The city is served by [Sam Ratulangi International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Ratulangi_International_Airport), which connects Manado with various domestic destinations, as well as international destinations in [East Asia](19605700.md) and [Southeast Asia](28741.md). The city is also known for its [Christian](18337522.md)-majority population, and holds the country's biggest [Christmas](6237.md) celebration annually. It is also recognised as one of the most tolerant and peaceful cities in Indonesia.[^cite_note-8]

## Etymology

The name Manado is derived from the [Sangir language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangir_language) word *manaro*, meaning 'on the far coast' or 'in the distance', and originally referred to the further of two islands which can be seen from the mainland. When the settlement on this island was relocated to the mainland, the name Manado was brought with it, after which the island itself became referred to as [Manado Tua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manado_Tua) (Old Manado).[^cite_note-9] The name for Manado in the [Sangir language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangir_language) is *Manaro*, while in [Gorontalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorontalo_language) is *Moladu*.[^cite_note-10]

## History

The first mention of Manado comes from a world map by French cartographer [Nicolas Desliens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Desliens?action=edit\&redlink=1), which shows the island of Manarow (today's Manado Tua). Before Europeans arrived in [North Sulawesi](453688.md), the area was under the rule of the [Sultan of Ternate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Ternate), who exacted tribute and introduced [Islam](6037917.md) to its inhabitants. The Portuguese made the Sultan their vassal, ruling over the [Minahasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minahasa) people, and establishing a factory in [Wenang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wenang).

Meanwhile, the Spanish had already set themselves up in the [Philippines](23440.md) and Minahasa was used to plant coffee because of its rich soil. Manado was further developed by Spain as a centre of commerce for the Chinese traders who traded the coffee in China. With the help of native allies, the Spanish took over the Portuguese fortress in Amurang in the 1550s, and Spanish settlers also established a fort at Manado, so that eventually, Spain controlled all of the Minahasa. It was in Manado where one of the first [Indo-Eurasian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo_people) (Mestizo) communities in the archipelago developed during the 16th century.[^cite_note-11] The first King of Manado (1630) named Muntu Untu was in fact the son of a Spanish Mestizo.[^cite_note-12]



Map of Manado in 1679

Spain renounced its possessions in Minahasa by means of a treaty with the Portuguese in return for a payment of 350,000 ducats.[^cite_note-13] Minahasan natives made an alliance treaty with the Dutch, and expelled the last of the Portuguese from Manado a few years later.

The [Dutch East India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_East_India_Company) built a fortress in Manado named Fort Amsterdam in 1658. As with regions in eastern Indonesia, Manado underwent Christianisation by missionaries such as [Johann Friedrich Riedel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Friedrich_Riedel) and [Johann Gottlieb Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Gottlieb_Schwarz). The Dutch missionaries built the first Christian church in Manado called Oude Kerk (Old church), which still stands, and is now called Gereja Sentrum. [HMS *Dover*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carron_\(1792_ship\)) captured Manado in June 1810. The Javanese prince [Diponegoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diponegoro) was exiled to Manado by the Dutch government in 1830 for leading a war of rebellion against the Dutch. In 1859, the English biologist [Alfred Wallace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Wallace) visited Manado and praised the town for its beauty.



Coat of Arms of Manado during Dutch colonial era, granted in 1931.

In 1919, the Apostolic Prefecture of Celebes was established in the [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_status_in_Indonesia). In 1961, it was promoted to the [Diocese of Manado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Manado).

The Japanese captured Manado in the [Battle of Manado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manado) in January 1942.[^cite_note-14] The city was heavily damaged by [Allied](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allies_of_World_War_II) bombing during [World War II](32927.md).

In 1958, the headquarters of the rebel movement [Permesta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permesta) was moved to Manado. When Permesta confronted the central government with demands for political, economic and regional reform, Jakarta responded by bombing the city in February 1958, and then invading in June 1958.

In 1962, the People's Representative Council declared Manado as the official capital city of North Sulawesi Province.

## Climate

Manado has a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) (*Af*) according to the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification), as there is no real dry season. The wettest month is January, with an average rainfall of 465 millimetres (18.3 in), while the driest is September with an average rainfall of 121 millimetres (4.8 in).[^cite_note-DWD_precip-15] The abundance of rain seems to be influenced by the [monsoon](57630.md). As its location is near the equator, the temperature is essentially constant throughout the year. The hottest month is August with an average temperature of 26.6 °C (79.9 °F), while the coolest months are January and February with an average temperature of 25.4 °C (77.7 °F).[^cite_note-DWD_mean-16]

Currently, the majority of Manado [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_status_in_Indonesia) residents are from the [Minahasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minahasa_people) ethnic group. The indigenous people of Manado are from the Tombulu sub-tribe. The [Tombulu language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tombulu_language) is considered one of the Minahasa languages and is spoken widely in several urban villages within Manado, for example: Wenang (Wenang / Mahawenang - [kolintang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolintang)), Tumumpa (down), Mahakeret (yelling), Tikala Ares (Walak Ares Tombulu, where the word 'ares' means punishable), Ranotana (ground water), Winangun (built), Wawonasa (wawoinasa - sharpened above), Pinaesaan (unity place), Pakowa (Tree of Treasure), Teling (fur / bamboo to make equipment), Titiwungen (excavated), Tuminting (from the word Ting-Ting: a bell, the inserted syllable -um- changing the noun to a verb, so Tuminting: ringing bell), Pondol (Edge), Wanea (from the word Wanua: meaning the country), etc. While the Malalayang area has residents mainly from the Bantik people, other indigenous groups in Manado today are from the Sangir, Gorontalo, Mongondow, Babontehu, Talaud, Tionudese, Siau, and Borgo peoples. There are also Arabian [peranakan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_Peranakan) communities, mainly in the *Kampung Arab* area which is near Pasar '45 and has become a destination for religious tourism. Other ethnicities represented include [Javanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javanese_people), [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Indonesian), [Batak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batak_people), [Makassar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makassar_people), and [Moluccans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moluccans). A small [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Indonesia) community also exists.

[Manado Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manado_Malay) is the main language spoken in Manado. It is a Malay-based creole. Some of the loan words in the Minahasan vernacular are derived from [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language), [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language), and other foreign languages.

### Religion

As of 2023, [Protestant Christianity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant_Christianity) is the major religion in Manado, constituting around 62.89 percent of all residents, [Islam](6037917.md) comes second forming about 30.93 percent, [Catholicism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholicism) comes in third forming around 5.32 percent, and the rest follow [Buddhism](3267529.md), [Hinduism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism), and [Confucianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confucianism), each coming in at less than 1 percent each, according to North Sulawesi regional office of [Statistics Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Indonesia).[^cite_note-22] In addition, about 20 [Indonesian Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_Jews) live in Manado.[^cite_note-23] The people of Manado identify as tolerant, harmonious, open and dynamic. Therefore, the city of Manado has a relatively conducive social environment, and is known as one of the safest cities in Indonesia. When [Indonesia](14579.md) was vulnerable to political upheaval around 1999, and there were riots in other Indonesian cities, Manado was little affected. This is shown through the slogan of the people of Manado: *Torang samua basudara*, which means *We are all family*. And also through the words of Dr. [Sam Ratulangi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Ratulangi): "Sitou, Timou, Tumou, Tou", which roughly translates to 'Man lives to educate others'.

[Sam Ratulangi International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Ratulangi_International_Airport) of Manado is one of the main ports of entry to [Indonesia](14579.md). In 2005, more than 15,000 international passengers entered [Indonesia](14579.md) via Manado airport, connected with other major cities like [Jakarta](16275.md), [Surabaya](212635.md) and [Makassar](389464.md), etc. Manado is also connected with several major cities in Asia for international routes.

Other public transportation in Manado are:

1. Local [share taxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_taxi) services, known as [*mikrolet*s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_taxi#Indonesia).

2. [Perum DAMRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perum_DAMRI) buses serving airport to Manado

3. Other buses serving Manado to other cities in [North Sulawesi](453688.md) as well as other cities in the island of [Sulawesi](28740.md)

[Manado–Bitung Toll Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manado%E2%80%93Bitung_Toll_Road) connects the city with [Bitung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitung). Terminal Malalayang, or Malalayang Bus Terminal serves as the main gateway for long-distance buses in Manado.

### Cuisine

See also: [Minahasan cuisine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minahasan_cuisine)



Fish *Woku*

Food typical of Manado include *[tinutuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinutuan)*, a [porridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porridge) consisting of various kinds of vegetables. In addition to tinutuan, there is *[cakalang fufu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cakalang_fufu)*, a smoked skipjack ([Katsuwonus pelamis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsuwonus_pelamis)), *roa fish* (exocoetidae or torani) [Parexocoetus brachypterus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parexocoetus_brachypterus), *kawok* which is based on the meat of the forest rodent white rat [Maxomys hellwandii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxomys_hellwandii?action=edit\&redlink=1); *paniki*, [bat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bat) meat-based dishes; such as ([Pteropus pumilus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pteropus_pumilus)) and *rinte wuuk* (abbreviated to RW) which is a local name of [dog meat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dog_meat), pork (a pig is cooked rotating over embers, usually served at parties), and *babi putar* (made from pork mixed with Manado spices, rolled and burned in bamboo).

There is also a typical drink from the area of Manado and its surroundings are *saguer* which is a kind of wine or palm wine derived from enau / aren tree (*[Arenga pinnata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arenga_pinnata)*), which is then fermented. Saguer is a *cap tikus* (spirits with an average of 40% alcohol content). The exact amount of alcohol depends on the technique of distillation, which varies among different Minahasa villages).



Manado styled *Nasi Kuning*

*[Woku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woku)* is a type of *[bumbu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bumbu_\(seasoning\))* ([spice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spice) mixture) found in [Manado cuisine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manado_cuisine) of [North Sulawesi](453688.md), [Indonesia](14579.md). It has rich aroma and spicy taste. Woku consist of ground spices paste; red [ginger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ginger), [turmeric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turmeric), [candlenut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candlenut), and red [chili pepper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chili_pepper), mixed with chopped [shallot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shallot), [scallion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scallion), tomato, lemon or citrus leaf, and turmeric leaf, [lemon basil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemon_basil) leaf, and bruised [lemongrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemongrass). Rub main ingredients (chicken or fish) with salt and lime juices, and marinate for 30 minutes. All spices are cooked in [coconut oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coconut_oil) until the aroma came up and mixed together with the main ingredients, water, and a pinch of salt, well until all cooked well.

Other typical food of Manado city which is also quite famous is *nasi kuning* which taste and looks different from yellow rice in other area because it is spiked with abon of cakalang rica fish and presented in a parcel using sugar palm leaves. There is also grilled fish roasted head. *[Dabu-dabu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dabu-dabu)* is a very popular typical Manado sauce, made from a mixture of red chilies, cayenne pepper, sliced red onion, and freshly diced tomatoes, and finally given a mixture of soy sauce.

## Language

| icon | This section **does not [cite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia\:Citing_sources) any [sources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia\:Verifiability)**. Please help [improve this section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special\:EditPage/Manado) by [adding citations to reliable sources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Referencing_for_beginners). Unsourced material may be challenged and [removed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia\:Verifiability#Burden_of_evidence). *(September 2020)*_&#x20;([Learn how and when to remove this message](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Maintenance_template_removal))_ |
| --- | --- |

The local language spoken in Manado and the surrounding area is a [creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creole_language) of the [Malay language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language) called [Manado Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manado_Malay). It exhibits significant influence of Portuguese, Spanish and Dutch, for example:

- "But" in Indonesian is "tapi", in Manado it is called "mar" (maar- Dutch word for but).

- Chair in Indonesian is "kursi", in Minahasa it is called "kadera" (cadeira - Portuguese for chair).

- Horse in Indonesian is "kuda", a word of Sanskrit origin. In the town of Tomohon, a horse is called "kafalio" ("cavalo" - Portuguese, "caballo" - Spanish).

While there is not much known about the origin of ideogramatical Minahasa writing system, currently the orthography used for indigenous Minahasan languages closely matches that used for Indonesian.
