The [Laurence Olivier Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award) for **Best New Play** is an annual award presented by the [Society of London Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_London_Theatre) in recognition of the "world-class status of London theatre."[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] The awards were established as the Society of [West End Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_theatre) Awards in [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_Laurence_Olivier_Awards), and renamed in [1984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984_Laurence_Olivier_Awards) in honour of English actor and director [Laurence Olivier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier).

The award was titled **Play of the Year** from its establishment in 1976, and was first retitled to its current name for the [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Laurence_Olivier_Awards) Olivier Awards.
