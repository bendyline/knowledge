This video was taken by the crew of [Expedition 29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expedition_29) on board the [ISS](15043.md). The pass begins over Canada and ends in the [Caribbean Sea](7822.md), covering the entire Central United States.

The **Central United States** is sometimes conceived as between the [Eastern](509486.md) and [Western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_United_States) as part of a three-region model, roughly coincident with the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau)'s definition of the [Midwestern United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midwestern_United_States) plus the western and central portions of the U.S. Census's definition of the [Southern United States](179553.md). The Central States are typically considered to consist of [North Dakota](21651.md), [South Dakota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Dakota), [Nebraska](21647.md), [Kansas](16716.md), [Oklahoma](22489.md), [Texas](29810.md), [Minnesota](19590.md), [Iowa](26810748.md), [Missouri](19571.md), [Arkansas](1930.md), [Louisiana](18130.md), [Wisconsin](33127.md), [Illinois](14849.md), [Michigan](18859.md), [Indiana](21883857.md), [Ohio](22199.md), [Kentucky](16846.md), [Tennessee](30395.md), [West Virginia](32905.md), [Mississippi](16949861.md) and [Alabama](303.md).

## Geography

[Chicago](6886.md) is the area's largest city and metropolitan area; other large cities with large metropolitan areas include [New Orleans](53842.md), [Houston](13774.md), [Dallas](53838.md), [Fort Worth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Worth,_Texas), [San Antonio](53848.md), [Austin](1998.md), [Oklahoma City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_City), [Tulsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa,_Oklahoma), [Kansas City](115009.md), [Kansas](16716.md) and [Kansas City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City,_Missouri), [Missouri](19571.md), [Topeka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topeka,_Kansas), [Wichita](114932.md), [Omaha, Nebraska](46159.md) and [Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln,_Nebraska), [Minneapolis](6097240.md) and [St. Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Paul,_Minnesota), [Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison,_Wisconsin) and [Milwaukee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee), [St. Louis](27687.md), [Louisville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisville,_Kentucky), [Lexington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexington,_Kentucky), [Detroit](8687.md) and [Grand Rapids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Rapids), [Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati), [Cleveland](5951.md), [Columbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus,_Ohio), [Toledo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo,_Ohio), [Dayton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton,_Ohio), [Rockford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockford,_Illinois), [Peoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peoria,_Illinois), [Indianapolis](57707.md), [Evansville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evansville,_Indiana), [Fort Wayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Wayne,_Indiana) and [South Bend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Bend,_Indiana).

Four of nine [Census Bureau Divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_regions_of_the_United_States#Census_Bureau-designated_regions_and_divisions) have names containing "Central", though they are not grouped as a region. They include 20 states and 39.45% of the U.S. population as of July 1, 2007.[^cite_note-1]

Almost all of the area is in the [Gulf of Mexico](21076367.md) [drainage basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drainage_basin) and most of that is in the [Mississippi basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_basin). Small waterways near the Great Lakes drain into the [Great Lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Lakes), and eventually the [St. Lawrence River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Lawrence_River). The [Red River Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_River_Valley) is centered on the [North Dakota](21651.md)-[Minnesota](19590.md) border and drains to [Hudson Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Bay). Floods have been a problem for the region during the 20th and early 21st century.[^cite_note-2]

The [Central Time Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Time_Zone) includes portions of the [Florida panhandle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_panhandle), upper portions of [Michigan](18859.md), parts of [Indiana](21883857.md), western [Kentucky](16846.md), western [Tennessee](30395.md), all of [Texas](29810.md) except [El Paso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Paso,_Texas), and extends to the westernmost fringes of [Great Plains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Plains) states.

## Geographic center of the United States

This section is an excerpt from [Geographic center of the United States § Contiguous United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographic_center_of_the_United_States#Contiguous_United_States).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Geographic_center_of_the_United_States\&action=edit)]

The geographic center of the 48 [contiguous or conterminous United States](83759.md), determined in a 1918 survey, is located at , about 2.6 miles (4.2 km) northwest of the center of [Lebanon, Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanon,_Kansas), approximately 12 miles (19 km) south of the [Kansas](16716.md)–[Nebraska](21647.md) border.[^cite_note-Geographic_center_of_the_United_States_doigs-4] The determination is accurate to about 20 miles (32 km).[^cite_note-Geographic_center_of_the_United_States_CLUI-5]

The NGS coordinates are recognized in a historical marker in a small park at the intersection of AA Road and [K-191](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-191_\(Kansas_highway\)), although again, this will always be slightly off due changing shorelines and other factors. It is accessible by a turn-off from [U.S. Route 281](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_281).
