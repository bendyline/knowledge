**Naegohyang Women's Football Club** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 내고향 녀자축구단) is a North Korean professional [women's football](191466.md) club from [Pyongyang](47948.md).[^cite_note-1] The club was founded in 2012 and competes in the [DPR Korea Women's Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPR_Korea_Women's_Premier_League), the top division of women's football in [North Korea](21255.md).

## History

Naegohyang was founded in 2012 and won its first championship in the 2021–22 season.[^cite_note-2] The team won its second title in the 2023–24 season, which qualified them for the inaugural [2024–25 AFC Women's Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_AFC_Women's_Champions_League) as direct entrants to the group stage; however, they withdrew before the draw.[^cite_note-3] Their first international appearance resulted in an 11–0 victory over Laos's [Master FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_FC).[^cite_note-afc-4] They are currently coached by Ri Yu-il, who has also coached the [North Korea women's national team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korea_women's_national_football_team).[^cite_note-afc-4][^cite_note-tough-5]

Naegohyang participated in the [2025–26 AFC Women's Champions League](78265767.md). One of their fixtures was the semi-final match against [Suwon FC Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwon_FC_Women), the first South Korea-hosted sport event that North Korean athletes participated after over seven years.[^cite_note-6] On 23 May 2026, Naegohyang faced [Tokyo Verdy Beleza](19930804.md) for the [final](82944499.md) and won 1–0, qualifying to the [2027 FIFA Women's Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_Champions_Cup) as the Asian representative.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-xinhua-8] In total, Naegohyang scored 36 goals throughout the tournament.[^cite_note-9]
