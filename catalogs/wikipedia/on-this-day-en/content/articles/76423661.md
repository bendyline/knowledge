The election of the president and the vice president of the Maldives is [presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_election) that determine who will serve as the [President of the Maldives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Maldives) for the next 5 years (previously four terms in 1968).[^cite_note-1]

The presidential elections has taken place thirteen times; [1952](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1952_Maldivian_presidential_election), [1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_Maldivian_presidential_election), [1973](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973_Maldivian_presidential_election), [1978](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_Maldivian_presidential_election), [1983](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_Maldivian_presidential_election), [1988](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Maldivian_presidential_election), [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Maldivian_presidential_election), [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Maldivian_presidential_election), [2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_Maldivian_presidential_election), [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Maldivian_presidential_election), [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Maldivian_presidential_election), [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Maldivian_presidential_election), [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Maldivian_presidential_election). Out of the 13 elections, the 2008 election was the first democratic elections of the country and after 2008, all elections were democratic.[^cite_note-2] The next presidential election is scheduled for [2028](77263448.md).

## Eligibility

The [Constitution of the Maldives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Maldives) lays out the requirements for presidential candidates:[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

- Be a [Maldivian Citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maldivian_nationality_law) born to Maldivian parents;

- Not a citizen of another country;

- Be a [Sunni Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam);

- Be at least 35 years of age;

- Never been convicted of an offence for which [Hadd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudud) is prescribed in [Islam](6037917.md);

- Not been convicted of a criminal offence and sentenced to more than 12 months, unless been more than three years after release or [pardoned](288593.md).

## Procedure

### Nomination of candidates

Candidates can be nominated by a [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_political_parties_in_the_Maldives) or run as [independent](2172378.md).

### Political party

Political parties hold primaries. In these primaries, the members of the party, supporters participate in voting to determine who will represent the party in the Presidential Election.[^cite_note-5]

### Popular Vote

The president is elected by a [direct vote](652563.md). If no candidate receives more than 50% of a vote in the first round, the two candidates with the most votes head to a [run-off](25932.md) which are mandated to be less than 21 days before the first round. The candidate who receives the highest in the second round is then elected as president.[^cite_note-6]

### Election calendar

These dates are corresponding the 2023 election:

- May 2 - June 2: Publishing voters registry.

  [^cite_note-\:0-7]

- July 23 - August 3: Submitting presidential candidacy.

  [^cite_note-\:0-7]

- July 25 - August 13: Nominating election observers and [monitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election_monitoring).

  [^cite_note-\:0-7]

- 10 August - September 3: Presidential campaign.

  [^cite_note-\:0-7]

- September 9 - Voting Day (Round One)

  [^cite_note-8]

- September 30 - Round Two (if neither candidate got 50% in Round One).

  [^cite_note-9]

- November 17 - President Inauguration Day.
