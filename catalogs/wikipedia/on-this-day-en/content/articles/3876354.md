The **City of Ekurhuleni Metropolitan Municipality** ([Zulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zulu_language): *UMasipala weDolobhakazi laseKurhuleni*; [Afrikaans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrikaans_language): *Ekurhuleni Metropolitaanse Munisipaliteit*; [Northern Sotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Sotho_language): *Mmasepala wa Toropokgolo ya Ekurhuleni*; [Sotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sotho_language): *Masepala wa Toropohadi ya Ekurhuleni*) is a [metropolitan municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_municipality_\(South_Africa\)) that forms the local government of the [East Rand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Rand) region of [Gauteng](309661.md), a large suburban region east of [Johannesburg](58893.md). *Ekurhuleni* means "place of peace" in [XiTsonga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsonga_language). Ekurhuleni is one of the five [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_South_Africa) of Gauteng and one of the eight metropolitan municipalities of [South Africa](17416221.md). The seat of Ekurhuleni is [Germiston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germiston) and the most spoken language is [Zulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zulu_language) at 28.6%. The city is home to South Africa's busiest airport, [OR Tambo International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OR_Tambo_International_Airport), which is in the [Kempton Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kempton_Park,_Gauteng) area of Ekurhuleni.[^cite_note-Airportconfirm-5]

## History

The municipality was established in 2000, the result of a merger between the Eastern Gauteng Services Council, the Khayalami Metropolitan Council, and the previous municipalities of Alberton, Benoni, Boksburg, Brakpan, Edenvale/Lethabong, Germiston, Kempton Park/Tembisa, Nigel and Springs.[^cite_note-6] The planned expansion of Ekurhuleni through the abolition of the [Lesedi Local Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesedi_Local_Municipality), which includes [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg,_Gauteng), and its absorption into Ekurhuleni after the 2016 municipal elections, were blocked by the [High Court of South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Court_of_South_Africa) in 2015.[^cite_note-7]

The name *Ekurhuleni* meaning *place of peace* alludes to the fact that the East Rand townships were the site of severe political violence between supporters of the [Inkatha Freedom Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inkatha_Freedom_Party) and the [ANC](2503.md) in the early 1990s, prior to South Africa's first non-racial elections in 1994.[^cite_note-8]

## Geography

Ekurhuleni lies on the [highveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highveld) plateau at about 1600 metres in altitude above sea level (asl). The highest elevation is at the Gillooly's Ridge – Bill Stewart Side – Fisher's Hill at 1772 metres asl.[^cite_note-9] The lowest point is at 1552 metres asl. The city is rich in wetlands, pans, streams and rivers.[^cite_note-10] The city is highly urbanised; however, the north especially has high-potential agricultural land.[^cite_note-11]

## Climate

Ekurhuleni has a [subtropical highland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical_highland) climate ([Cwb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical_highland)) according to the [Köppen-Geiger Climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification).[^cite_note-12] This is a temperate climate of warm summers and dry winters. In the winter months (May - October) the city has a diurnal temperature pattern with a high amplitude, as temperatures can range from freezing at night to mid 20s °C in the day.

The main precipitation falls between October and March with a monthly average precipitation of up to 148 mm which falls usually in convective thundershowers.[^cite_note-13] The daily maximum temperatures in October–March are on average 27 °C. The Winter is mostly dry with only a few [cold fronts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_front) reaching the highveld from the southern Atlantic Ocean.[^cite_note-climate_charts_on_meteoblue-14]

## Parks and nature

Germiston Lake is a natural pan in the Elsburgspruit system and has a catchment area of 1174ha. It is fed mostly by [surface runoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_runoff) water and storm water drains. The lake is one of the cleanest bodies of water in South Africa. It has a size of 57.4 ha and a maximum capacity of 2839×     10  6    m  3     {\displaystyle 10^{6}m^{3}}  {\displaystyle 10^{6}m^{3}}. Its depth is approximately 8 metres.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] On the banks of the lake are a recreational park with playground equipment and [braai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braai) areas as well as a rowing club and golf club.

A wetland of note is the Blesbokriver wetland which stretches over an area of 1858ha.[^cite_note-20]

The Bullfrog Pan is almost 10 hectares and is home to more than 150 bird species.[^cite_note-arcgis.com-21]

### Landmarks

In 2017, the [Chris Hani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hani) memorial at Thomas Nkobi Memorial Park in Boksburg was revealed during the 22nd commemoration of his death. The Chris Hani memorial site consists of the Monument, the Walk of Remembrance and the Wall of Remembrance which recognizes the historical events, places and people associated with the liberation struggle of South Africa.[^cite_note-arcgis.com-21]

## Demographics

In 2016, 3,379,104 people lived in Ekurhuleni, of whom 22.7% were under 15 years old, 71.2% were between the ages of 15-64 and 6.1% were older than 65 years old. Ekurhuleni had 1,299,490 households with an average people per household number of 2.6. 80.2% of people lived in formal dwellings and 52.9% owned housing.[^cite_note-25]

## Education

Ekurhuleni has two school districts: Ekurhuleni North and Ekurhuleni South.[^cite_note-27] There are 671 schools in Ekurhuleni, of which 137 are independent.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29] Ekurhuleni has two colleges of further education and training (FET)[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31] as well as two centres of adult education and training (AET).[^cite_note-32] The municipal government under Executive Mayor Mzwandile Masina conducted a feasibility study and lobbied the national government to establish a university in Ekurhuleni [^cite_note-symposium_University_of_Ekurhuleni-33] which was formally announced by President [Cyril Ramaphosa](1089194.md) in the 2020 State of the Nation Address.

## Controversies

Police are investigating the R5.2 million paid for consultation work that was never done.[^cite_note-34]

### Ekurhuleni Metropolitan Police Department Crimes and Corruption

On 11 November 2025, acting [Ekurhuleni Metropolitan Police Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekurhuleni_Metropolitan_Police_Department) head [Julius Mkhwanazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Mkhwanazi) was suspended following damning allegations at the [Madlanga Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madlanga_Commission).[^cite_note-35] Witnesses from the EMPD testified and gave evidence that linked Mkhwanazi to a murder case and accused him of running a rogue unit allegedly involved in extortion, kidnapping, theft, and truck hijackings in and around Gauteng. Mkhwanazi was also mentioned to have played a major part in having [Vusimuzi Matlala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vusimuzi_Matlala), a controversial businessman who is currently behind bars, sign an agreement between the EMPD and Matlala's security company, which did not follow the correct protocols.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37]

Among others, allegations levelled against Mkhwanazi include being an accessory to a murder. This murder happened on 15 April 2022 in the [Brakpan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brakpan) area.[^cite_note-38] It is alleged that the victim was tortured on the scene was body was dumped in a dam on Mkhwananzi's instruction.[^cite_note-39]

During the commission hearings, a video was shown of Mkhwananzi and the rogue unit raiding a factory in [Meyerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meyerton,_Gauteng), outside their operation boundaries. Mkhwanazi and these EMPD officers stole copper at a workshop in August 2022. Jaco Hanekom, a supervisor and whistleblower who exposed the EMPD operation at the said workshop, was later killed in March 2023 after the EMPD officers who were arrested were granted bail.[^cite_note-40]

EMPD's specialised services unit, under Mkhwanazi's command is linked to a theft of a truck and its load in Putfontein, [Benoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benoni,_South_Africa), and the kidnapping of the driver, in March 2023.[^cite_note-41]

It is also alleged that Mkhwananzi and his rogue unit was involved in the Precious Stones case, which occurred in the [Hillbrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillbrow)-Kensington area in 2024, where police are accused of stealing precious stones worth R45-million.[^cite_note-42]

## Governance and politics

Main article: [City of Ekurhuleni elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Ekurhuleni_elections)

The governance of Ekurhuleni is set out by Chapter 7 of the [Constitution of South Africa](441610.md) which governs the operation and function of local government in South Africa. The Local Government: Municipal Structures Act 117 of 1998 states that the minister responsible for local government, namely the [Minister of Cooperative Governance and Traditional Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Cooperative_Governance_and_Traditional_Affairs), is responsible for the designation of municipalities. As per the act and regulations issued by the Minister, Ekurhuleni is designated as a category A municipality and is thus a [metropolitan municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_municipality_\(South_Africa\)). The external boundaries of the municipality and subsequently the remit of the jurisdiction of the metropolitan government is regulated by the [Municipal Demarcation Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_Demarcation_Board).[^cite_note-43]

As of 11 April 2011, the Municipal Council approved the separation of the legislative and executive functions of the metropolitan government.[^cite_note-\:0-44] Under Section 56 of the Municipal Structures Act, the Executive Mayor is responsible for directing and monitoring, and presiding over Mayoral Committee.[^cite_note-45] The Mayoral Committee comprises the administrative branch of the municipality and is responsible for bringing to effect the by-laws of the Municipal Council, administering the responsibilities designated by national and provincial legislation, as well as providing for the political oversight for the functioning of the city structures.[^cite_note-\:0-44] A professional civil servant known as the City Manager,[^cite_note-46] is responsible for the technical and managerial direction and oversight of the municipality.[^cite_note-47]

In 2014/2015, the city ranked highest out of all metros in South Africa in the Business Day Index, measuring how well cities and towns spend resident's money.[^cite_note-48]

The municipal council consists of 224 members elected by [mixed-member proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed-member_proportional_representation). Of these 224, 112 are elected by [first-past-the-post voting](261709.md) in 112 [wards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_\(South_Africa\)), while the remaining 112 are chosen from [party lists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_list) so that the total number of party representatives is proportional to the number of votes received. In the [2021 South African municipal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_South_African_municipal_election), no party obtained a majority of seats on the council.

## Economy

As of 2014, the [GDP](12594.md) of Ekurhuleni was estimated at over US\$55 billion ([PPP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purchasing_power_parity)), being US\$17,361 per person.[^cite_note-Brookings-4] Ekurhuleni has an active workforce of 1.6 million people of whom 28.8% are unemployed.[^cite_note-statssa.gov.za-26] Ekurhuleni makes up 6.2% of national production.

Ekurhuleni is home to the [Rand Refinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rand_Refinery), the largest integrated single-site precious metals refining and smelting complex in the world and [^cite_note-50]



Airways Park, the head office of [South African Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Airways)



Professor [John D. Kasarda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_D._Kasarda).

### Manufacturing

The city is an important [manufacturing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacturing) centre in South Africa, contributing 32% of manufacturing production.[^cite_note-nwu.ac.za-51] The city has been described as "the workshop of the country".[^cite_note-52] It contributes 11% to the GVA of South Africa.[^cite_note-53]

Ekurhuleni has held the "Manufacturing Indaba" Conference every year since 2014. This two-day conference provides contacts and networking between business owners, industry owners, capital providers, experts and the government.[^cite_note-54]

## Infrastructure

### Road network

Ekurhuleni is connected to the main motorways in South Africa via the [M2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M2_\(Johannesburg\)), [N3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N3_\(South_Africa\)), [N17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N17_\(South_Africa\)), [R21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R21_\(South_Africa\)), [R24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R24_\(South_Africa\)) and [R59](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R59_\(South_Africa\)) highways. As Ekurhuleni is part of the Johannesburg [Conurbation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conurbation), Transport routes in Ekurhuleni share the same [metropolitan route numbering system as Johannesburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Routes_in_Johannesburg).

The road network in Ekurhuleni spans 8,024 km of paved roads and approximately 1,200 km of gravel road.[^cite_note-mfma.treasury.gov.za-55]

### Public transport

Ekurhuleni is one of 13 cities and towns in South Africa to implement the bus rapid transit (BRT) system. Phase 1 of the project runs from [Tembisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tembisa) to Vosloorus via Kempton Park and the [OR Tambo International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OR_Tambo_International_Airport). New routes have been integrated from [Katlehong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katlehong) to [Rhodesfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodesfield), from [Vosloorus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vosloorus) to Rhodesfield, and from [Reiger Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reiger_Park) to Rhodesfield.[^cite_note-56] Passengers are served by [Metrorail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrorail_\(South_Africa\)) which sustains the passenger rail network in the Western Cape, Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal and the Eastern Cape. Seven train lines serve Ekurhuleni.[^cite_note-57]

### Rail

Ekurhuleni has the largest rail hub in South Africa used by [Transnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnet).[^cite_note-nwu.ac.za-51]

### Aviation



Ekurhuleni aerotropolis

Several airline companies are headquartered in the [Kempton Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kempton_Park,_Gauteng) area of Ekurhuleni.

Ekurhuleni is Gauteng's first aerotropolis.[^cite_note-58] This is a metropolis with an airport at its centre. One should be able to get to the airport from anywhere in Ekurhuleni in 25 min. The major aim will be logistics and connecting the local industry and agriculture to the world markets.

[O.R. Tambo International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O.R._Tambo_International_Airport) has two terminals handling domestic and international flights. Terminal A handles international traffic and Terminal B domestic flights. The airport services airlines from all five continents and plays a vital role in serving the local, regional, intra- and inter-continental air transport needs of South Africa and sub-Saharan Africa. It is the biggest and busiest airport in Africa. OR Tambo International handles more than 20 million passengers per year and employs more than 18,000 people.[^cite_note-59]

[South African Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Airways), the flagship air carrier of South Africa, had its head office in Ekurhuleni.[^cite_note-Airportconfirm-5][^cite_note-60][^cite_note-61]

[Federal Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Air) has its headquarters on the OR Tambo International Airport grounds.[^cite_note-62] [Safair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safair)'s head office is in Kempton Park.[^cite_note-63]

[Airports Company South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airports_Company_South_Africa) has its head office in [Bedfordview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedfordview), Ekurhuleni.[^cite_note-64] [TAAG Angola Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TAAG_Angola_Airlines) has an office in Bedfordview.[^cite_note-65]

### Stormwater systems

There are 11,318 storm water systems and a total lengths of pipes and channels of more than 3800 km.[^cite_note-mfma.treasury.gov.za-55]

## Sports and recreation

Ekurhuleni has at least 16 golf courses and is home to the international Golf Tournament the South African Open held at [Glendower Golf Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendower_Golf_Club).[^cite_note-66]

There is an over 100 year old rowing club at the bank of Germiston Lake,[^cite_note-67] and over 30 swimming pools can be found in the city.[^cite_note-68]

Ekurhuleni United FC play in the fourth division of South African Football.[^cite_note-69]

Ekurhuleni has the Ekurhuleni masters league, a soccer league of retired pros and talented players who did not make the cut.[^cite_note-70]

38 eco "outdoor gyms" can be found in Ekurhuleni.

## Management

In 2021, [Eskom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eskom) identified Ekurhuleni as a municipality with a poor payment record, with its debt at the time amounting to R8 million.[^cite_note-khu1-71]

In July 2025, a new taxi rank completed three months earlier for R17 million had not yet been used, with taxi operators claiming they had not been consulted.[^cite_note-72]
