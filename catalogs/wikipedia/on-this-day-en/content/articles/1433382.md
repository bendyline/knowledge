**Pyatigorsk** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Пятиго́рск; [Circassian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circassian_languages): Псыхуабэ, *Psıxwabæ*) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) in [Stavropol Krai](474074.md), Russia, located on the [Podkumok River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podkumok_River), about 20 kilometers (12 mi) from the town of [Mineralnye Vody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mineralnye_Vody), which has an international airport, and about 45 kilometers (28 mi) from [Kislovodsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kislovodsk). Since January 19, 2010, it has been the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of the [North Caucasian Federal District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Caucasian_Federal_District) of Russia. Population:  142,511 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2010Census-4] 140,559 ([2002 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Russian_census));[^cite_note-PopCensus-8] 129,499 ([1989 Soviet census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Soviet_census)).[^cite_note-Census1989-9]

## Overview

The name is derived from the fused Russian words "пять гор" (*five mountains*) and the city is so called because of the five peaks of the [Beshtau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beshtau) (which also means *five mountains* in [Turkic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkic_languages)) of the [Caucasus Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus_Mountains) overlooking the city. Founded in 1780,[^cite_note-megabook-2] it has been a health spa with [mineral springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mineral_spring) since 1803, as part of the [Caucasian Mineral Waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasian_Mineral_Waters).&#x20;

Russian poet [Mikhail Lermontov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Lermontov) was shot dead by Nikolai Martynov in a [duel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duel) at Pyatigorsk on July 27, 1841. There is a museum in the city devoted to his memory. [Zionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zionist) activist [Joseph Trumpeldor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Trumpeldor) was born in Pyatigorsk.

The writings of the 14th-century Arabian traveler [Ibn Battuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_Battuta) included the earliest known mention of the mineral springs. [Peter the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_the_Great) (reigned 1682–1725) fostered the earliest scientific study of them, but the information collected on his expedition has not survived. Interest revived at the end of the 18th century with the foundation of the first Russian settlement (Konstantinogorskaya fortress), erected at Mt. Mashuk in 1780.[^cite_note-megabook-2][^cite_note-10]

The value of the Caucasian mineral waters led to the construction of a resort in 1803, and studies of their medical properties began thereafter: on April 24, [Alexander I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_I_of_Russia) signed a decree which made the mineral waters state property. Many settlements developed near the springs: first Goryachevodsk (now part of Pyatigorsk) at the bottom of Mt. Mashuk, then [Kislovodsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kislovodsk), [Yessentuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yessentuki), and [Zheleznovodsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheleznovodsk).[^cite_note-11] During the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire), the settlement was the administrative capital of the [Pyatigorsky Otdel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyatigorsky_Otdel) of the [Terek Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terek_Oblast).

During [World War II](32927.md), the German [Wehrmacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht) temporarily occupied Pyatigorsk. The [Einsatzkommando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einsatzkommando) 12 of [Einsatzgruppe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einsatzgruppen) D had its headquarters in Pyatigorsk in 1942.[^cite_note-12] The German occupation resulted in the killing of many Jewish inhabitants of the region, including medical workers.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

## Geography

The city is situated on a small plateau, 512 meters (1,680 ft) above [sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_level), at the foot of [Beshtau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beshtau), Mashuk, and three other outliers of the [Caucasus Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus_Mountains), which protect it on the north. The snow-covered summits of [Mount Elbrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Elbrus) are visible to the south.

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), it is, together with two [urban-type settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban-type_settlement) ([Goryachevodsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goryachevodsky) and [Svobody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svobody)) and five [rural localities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia), incorporated as the **[city of krai significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance) of Pyatigorsk**—an administrative unit with the status equal to that of the [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Stavropol_Krai).[^cite_note-Ref372-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), the city of krai significance of Pyatigorsk is incorporated as **Pyatigorsk Urban Okrug** Daria Kuznetsova is a local leader.[^cite_note-Ref893-5]

## Economy

The industry of Pyatigorsk is primarily oriented towards service of the health resort. There is also food industry (a meat-processing plant, a [winery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winery), a [dairy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy), a [brewery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brewery), a [confectionery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confectionery)), textiles ([clothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clothing), shoe plant, carpet factories), machine industry and metal working (PО *Pyatigorskselmash* specializes in machines and equipment for [aviculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviculture); a special automobile equipment works, an electromechanical plant, etc.); mining, a chemical factory and a ceramics factory who specialize in porcelain and ceramic gifts such as [samovars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samovar), figurines, vases, and decorative ceramic wall hanging panels. In 1991, the Pyatigorsk health resort had ten [sanatoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanatorium), four boarding houses and five sanatoria-preventoria.&#x20;

### Climate

The climate of Pyatigorsk falls within [humid continental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate) (Dfb) classification under the [Köppen-Geiger climate classification system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen-Geiger_climate_classification_system) and is characterized by absence of sharp fluctuations of annual and daily temperatures. Summers are warm with the average July temperature of +21 °C (70 °F) while winters, lasting two to three months, are cold, with the average January temperature of −4 °C (25 °F). Spring is cool, with a sharp transition by the summer, and a warm, dry, and long fall. There are an average of ninety-eight sunny days in a year.

This monument marks the place of [Lermontov's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Lermontov) duel

The state memorial estate of Mikhail Lermontov was founded in 1973. It unites all of the Lermontov memorial places in the region: the place where he fought his duel and was killed, a [necropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necropolis), Lermontov's small house, [Verzilin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verzilin?action=edit\&redlink=1)'s houses, the house of [Alexander Alyabyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Alyabyev), the Lermontov square, and monument.

The [Aeolian harp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeolian_harp) is a small stone pavilion in the [classical style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_style), constructed by the [brothers Bernardacci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brothers_Bernardacci) in 1828.

Diana's grotto was built in 1830, in honor of the first ascent of [Mount Elbrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Elbrus).

Pyatigorsk features in [Jonathan Littell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Littell)'s 2009 novel, [*The Kindly Ones*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kindly_Ones_\(Littell_novel\)).
