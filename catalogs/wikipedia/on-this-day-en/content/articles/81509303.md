The **[English Football League play-offs](6043445.md)** for the [2025–26 season](79698535.md) (referred to as the **[Sky Bet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Betting_and_Gaming) Play-Offs** for sponsorship reasons) were held in May 2026 with all finals staged at [Wembley Stadium](8913012.md) in [London](17867.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

The play-offs began in each league with two semi-finals which were played over two legs. The teams who finished in 3rd to 6th place in the [Championship](79604105.md) and [League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_EFL_League_One) and the 4th to 7th-placed teams in [League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_EFL_League_Two) competed. The winners of the semi-finals advanced to the finals, with the finals winners gaining promotion for the following season.

## Background

The [English Football League](14419588.md) play-offs have been held every year since 1987. They take place for each division following the conclusion of the regular season and are contested by the four clubs finishing below the automatic promotion places. The fixtures are determined by final league position – in the Championship and League One this is 3rd v 6th and 4th v 5th, while in League Two it is 4th v 7th and 5th v 6th.

## Championship

Main article: [2025–26 EFL Championship](79604105.md)

### Championship semi-finals

The final table was confirmed after the final matchday on 2 May 2026. Going into the final day, [Southampton](81576.md) had already confirmed a place in the play-offs at fifth place. Southampton leapfrogged [Middlesbrough](298603.md) after the Saints beat [Preston North End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preston_North_End_F.C.) and Middlesbrough drew 2–2 against [Wrexham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrexham_A.F.C.). [Ipswich](352843.md), [Millwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millwall_F.C.) and Middlesbrough all had the opportunity to finish in second place with Ipswich occupying it at the start of the day. Ipswich won their match which meant Millwall and Middlesbrough had their playoff places confirmed. [Hull City](451358.md), Wrexham and [Derby County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derby_County_F.C.) were all in the hunt for the last spot. Hull City leapfrogged Wrexham after their win against ninth place [Norwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich_City_F.C.), and help from fourth place Middlesbrough who held Wrexham to a 2–2 draw. Derby County lost 2–1 to 13th place [Sheffield United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_United_F.C.), eliminating them from play-off contention.

## League One

Main article: [2025–26 EFL League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_EFL_League_One)

### League One semi-finals

The final table was confirmed after the final matchday on 3 May 2026. Going into the final day, [Bolton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolton_Wanderers_F.C.) had already qualified for the play-offs. On the final day [Stockport County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockport_County_F.C.), [Bradford City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradford_City_A.F.C.), [Stevenage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevenage_F.C.), [Luton Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luton_Town_F.C.), and [Plymouth Argyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth_Argyle_F.C.) were all chasing a play-off spot. Stockport and Bradford both won and leapfrogged 3rd place Bolton, who lost to play-off hopefuls Luton. Plymouth won as well, but as Stevenage also won, they qualified in the final spot at the expense of Luton and Plymouth. On the final day, everyone except the already qualified Bolton won their match, which caused them to drop from 3rd to 5th and lose the home advantage.

## League Two

Main article: [2025–26 EFL League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_EFL_League_Two)

### League Two semi-finals

The final table was confirmed after the final matchday on 2 May 2026. Going into the final day, [Cambridge United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_United_F.C.), [Salford City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salford_City_F.C.), [Notts County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notts_County_F.C.), and [Grimsby Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grimsby_Town_F.C.) had already secured a play-off place with all but Grimsby Town still capable of automatic promotion. Cambridge United clinched the last automatic spot as both teams drew, consigning Salford City to the playoffs. [Chesterfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesterfield_F.C.), [Swindon Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swindon_Town_F.C.), and [Barnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnet_F.C.) were all in contention for the last play-off spot. Barnet won against relegation-threatened [Harrogate Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrogate_Town_A.F.C.) condemning them to relegation, but Chesterfield beat Swindon Town eliminating them both in the process and leapfrogging Grimsby Town who had already qualified with Chesterfield and Swindon Town playing each other on the final matchday.
