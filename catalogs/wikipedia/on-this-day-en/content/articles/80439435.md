The **Liberal Democratic Party–Komeito coalition** was an [alliance](1898891.md) in [Japan](15573.md) between the [Liberal Democratic Party](164231.md) (LDP) and [Komeito](163859.md). A [coalition government](6036.md) between the parties has been established twice: the first from 5 October 1999 to 16 September 2009, and the second from 26 December 2012 to 10 October 2025, when Komeito announced their exit from the coalition.

## History

The two parties formed a coalition in October 1999 during the premiership of [Keizō Obuchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keiz%C5%8D_Obuchi).[^cite_note-1]

The coalition was roundly defeated and lost power in the [2009 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Japanese_general_election). In the [general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Japanese_general_election) on 16 December 2012, the LDP/Komeito coalition secured a [supermajority](638313.md) and came back into government.[^cite_note-LAtimes-analysis-2][^cite_note-BBC-analysis-3]

In the [2024 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Japanese_general_election), the LDP–Komeito coalition lost its majority for the first time since [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Japanese_general_election). Although the LDP still remained the largest party, the coalition fell short of the 233 seats needed for a majority, securing only 215 and they formed the [minority government](271434.md).[^cite_note-losesmajority-4][^cite_note-losesmajority2-5][^cite_note-losesmajority3-6]

On 10 October 2025, Komeito chief representative [Tetsuo Saito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetsuo_Saito) announced that it would leave the ruling coalition, over disagreements with new LDP president [Sanae Takaichi](8125532.md).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] Following Komeito's withdrawal, the LDP formed [a coalition agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Democratic_Party%E2%80%93Japan_Innovation_Party_coalition) with the [Japan Innovation Party](48459506.md).[^cite_note-9]
