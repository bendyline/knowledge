The **8th Division** (第8師団) is one of nine active [divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_\(military\)) of the [Japan Ground Self-Defense Force](2978818.md). The division is subordinated to the [Western Army](32283031.md) and is headquartered in [Kumamoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumamoto_City). Its responsibility is the defense of the [Kagoshima](217439.md), [Kumamoto](220713.md) and [Miyazaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyazaki_Prefecture) prefectures.

The division was raised on 18 January 1962.

## Organization



JGSDF 8th Division organization

- **8th Division**, in [Kumamoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumamoto_City)&#x20;

  - 8th Division HQ, in Kumamoto

  - 12th Infantry Regiment, in [Kirishima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirishima,_Kagoshima), with 1 × headquarters, four [Type 73](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_73_armored_personnel_carrier) [armored personnel carrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armored_personnel_carrier), and 1 × [120 mm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortier_120mm_Ray%C3%A9_Tract%C3%A9_Mod%C3%A8le_F1) mortar company

  - 42nd Rapid Deployment Regiment, in Kumamoto, with 1 × headquarters, three [Type 96](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_96_armored_personnel_carrier) [armored personnel carrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armored_personnel_carrier), 1 × [Type 16 maneuver combat vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_16_maneuver_combat_vehicle), and 1 × [120 mm F1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortier_120mm_Ray%C3%A9_Tract%C3%A9_Mod%C3%A8le_F1) mortar company

  - 43rd Infantry Regiment, in [Miyakonojō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyakonoj%C5%8D,_Miyazaki), with 1 × headquarters, four Type 73 armored personnel carrier, and 1 × 120 mm mortar company

  - Amami Security Unit, [Amami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amami,_Kagoshima), with 1 × headquarters, 1 × infantry, and 1 × logistic support company

  - 8th Anti-Aircraft Artillery Battalion, in Kumamoto, with [Type 81](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_81_surface-to-air_missile) and [Type 93](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_93_surface-to-air_missile) [surface-to-air missile](182664.md) systems

  - 8th Engineer Battalion (Combat), in [Satsumasendai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satsumasendai,_Kagoshima)

  - 8th Signal Battalion, in Kumamoto

  - 8th Reconnaissance Company, in Kumamoto, with [Type 87 reconnaissance vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_87_RCV)

  - 8th Intelligence Company, in Kumamoto, with [ScanEagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Insitu_ScanEagle)

  - 8th Aviation Squadron, in [Mashiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashiki,_Kumamoto), flying [UH-1J](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_UH-1_Iroquois) and [OH-6D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hughes_OH-6_Cayuse) helicopters

  - 8th [NBC-defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapon_of_mass_destruction) Company, in Kumamoto

  - Western Army Tank Battalion, in [Kusu](459858.md), with [Type 10](14207001.md) [main battle tanks](26595097.md) and [Type 96](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_96_armored_personnel_carrier) [armored personnel carriers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armored_personnel_carrier) (administrative control during peacetime)

  - Western Army Anti-tank/Anti-ship Company, in Kusu, with [Type 96 multi-purpose missile system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_96_multi-purpose_missile_system) (administrative control during peacetime)

  - 8th Logistic Support Regiment, in Kumamoto&#x20;

    - 1st Maintenance Battalion

    - 2nd Maintenance Battalion

    - Supply Company

    - Medical Company
