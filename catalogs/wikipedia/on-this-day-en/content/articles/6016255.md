**Al-Nabi Shayth** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ٱلنَّبِي شَيْت, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *An-Nabī Shayth*, [lit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation). [Prophet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prophets_and_messengers_in_Islam) [Seth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth)) is a village in eastern [Lebanon](17771.md), located in the [Beqaa Valley](2162385.md)[^cite_note-LeStrange1890-2] and [Baalbek District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baalbek_District).[^cite_note-SmithRobinson1841-1] The village's name comes from the claim that it is the burial site of [Seth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth), the son of [Adam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam). A mosque on the burial site contains the grave of Seth (though rival traditions place Seth's tomb in the [Palestinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_\(region\)) village of [Bashsheet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashsheet) and in the [Iraqi](7515928.md) city of [Mosul](200475.md)).[^cite_note-Al-Arabiya_07-2014-3] The village is also the home town of [Abbas al-Musawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_al-Musawi), the second leader and co-founder of [Hezbollah](13919.md), as well as other senior Hezbollah leaders such as [Fuad Shukr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuad_Shukr). The village of is predominantly inhabited by people with the surnames Helbawi, Al-Moussawi, Hazimeh and Chokr.[^cite_note-4]

## History

[Ibn Jubayr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_Jubayr) (1145–1217 [CE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era)) noted:

> the two graves of Seth and [Noah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah) \[..] are in the [Bika'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bika'), and two days' journey from [Damascus](8914.md). One who measured the tomb of Sheeth (Seth), reported to us that it was 40 [fathoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fathom) (ba') long, and the [tomb of Nuh (Noah)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karak_Nuh) was 30. The tomb of Noah's son lies side-by-side with that of [Noah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_in_Islam). There is a building over the tombs, and an [endowment for charitable purposes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waqf).

— Ibn Jubayr[^cite_note-LeStrange1890-2]

In 1838, [Eli Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Smith) noted *En-Neby Sheeth* as a "[Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh)" village in the district of Baalbek.[^cite_note-SmithRobinson1841-1]

On 22 December 1998 the [Israeli Air Force](177619.md) bombed a farm house in al-Nabi Shayth killing a woman and six children. The target had been an antenna belonging to Hizbollah's *Voice of the Oppressed* radio station. Hizbollah responded by firing rockets into Northern Israel, injuring sixteen people. Two weeks later the IAF again tried to destroy the radio mast, injuring seven villagers.[^cite_note-5] On April 14, 2024, the IDF carried out an airstrike on the settlement, claiming that they were targeting a [Hezbollah](13919.md) run weapons manufacturing site.[^cite_note-6]
