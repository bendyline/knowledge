**As Silw District** is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Yemen) of the [Taiz Governorate](3604497.md), [Yemen](350939.md). As of 2003, the district had a population of 152,486 inhabitants.[^cite_note-1]
