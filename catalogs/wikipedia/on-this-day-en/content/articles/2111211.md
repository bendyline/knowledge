# National day of mourning

"Day of mourning" redirects here. For the album, see [Day of Mourning (album)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_of_Mourning_\(album\)).

A **national day of mourning** is a day, or one of several days, marked by [mourning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mourning) and memorial activities observed among the majority of a country's populace. They are designated by the national government. Such days include those marking the death or funeral of a renowned individual or individuals from that country or elsewhere or the anniversary of such a death or deaths, wartime commemorations, or the occurrence or anniversary of a significant [disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disaster) either in the country or in another country. Flying a national or military [flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag) of that country at [half-mast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-mast) is a common symbol.

Sociologically, period of national mourning are understood "as instituting states of social exception during which state authorities enact ritual actions consisting in a sequence of choreographically staged performative acts meant to create a national community of grief in the face of what is framed as a socially meaningful loss."[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Selected list of figures recognized

### State officials

- National days of mourning are typically declared for [presidents of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidents_of_the_United_States), usually on the day of their funerals. Beginning with the November 25, 1963, mourning of [John F. Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_F._Kennedy) following his [assassination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_John_F._Kennedy), these days are also considered federal holidays.

  [^cite_note-4]

  &#x20;There was no official day of mourning for [Herbert Hoover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Hoover).

  [^cite_note-USStateFunerals-5]

- In the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), an official mourning period was reserved for the deaths of leaders or former leaders, with [Alexei Rykov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexei_Rykov), [Nikita Khrushchev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Khrushchev) and [Georgy Malenkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgy_Malenkov) being notable exceptions after Rykov was executed during the [Great Purge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Purge) and the other two were relegated to obscurity. Khrushchev's death was announced only hours before he was buried without full state honors,

  [^cite_note-6]

  &#x20;while Malenkov's death was publicly announced more than 2 weeks after he died.

  [^cite_note-7]

  &#x20;This custom changed in 1968 when a national day of mourning was declared for Soviet cosmonaut [Yuri Gagarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Gagarin), the first human to journey into outer space.

  [^cite_note-Gagarin-8]

  [^cite_note-9]

  &#x20;In the final years of the Soviet Union, official mourning was declared for 2 disasters: the [1988 Armenian earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Armenian_earthquake) and the [Ufa train disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ufa_train_disaster).

  [^cite_note-10]

- [Presidents of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidents_of_Mexico), usually on the day of their funerals. These days are usually considered municipal and religious holidays in [Mexico City](18987.md) and federal holidays in the rest of [the republic](3966054.md). [Miguel de la Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_de_la_Madrid) in 2012 was the most recent.

## International days of mourning

Further information: [International day of mourning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_day_of_mourning)

A similar but rarely-used concept exists at the [European Union](9317.md)-level and [Arab League](52625.md)-level and are called a [European Day of Mourning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Day_of_Mourning) and [Arab League Day of Mourning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_League_Day_of_Mourning). The [European Commission](9974.md) first introduced the concept on 12 September 2001, when a day of mourning was declared across EU member states for the victims of the [September 11 terrorist attacks in the United States](5058690.md).[^cite_note-11] A second and third day of mourning was held in April 2010 for the victims of [Smolensk air disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smolensk_air_disaster) in [Russia](25391.md) and in November 2015 for the victims of the [Paris attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/November_2015_Paris_attacks).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] Arab League has declared days of mourning for four times until October 2023.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] Other organizations such as the [African Union](10634933.md), [Union of South American Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_South_American_Nations), etc., have also announced [international days of mourning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_day_of_mourning).[^cite_note-16]

## Remembrance events

While not the same as a national day of mourning, some remembrance events and protests are called a "day of mourning".

- [National Day of Mourning (Bangladesh)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Day_of_Mourning_\(Bangladesh\)), held 15 August. In 1975 [Sheikh Mujibur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Mujibur_Rahman) was killed by a group of army personnel, along with his family.

- [Circassian Day of Mourning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circassian_Day_of_Mourning), held May 21, commemorating the Circassian genocide and the Circassian defeat in the [Russian-Circassian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Circassian_War)

- [Day of Mourning (Australia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_of_Mourning_\(Australia\)), annual protest of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Australians held on Australia Day, 26 January

- [National Day of Mourning (United States protest)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Day_of_Mourning_\(United_States_protest\)), an American Indian protest held on the fourth Thursday of November

- [National Day of Mourning (Canadian observance)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Day_of_Mourning_\(Canadian_observance\)), held 28 April, a commemoration of workers killed or injured on the job

- [Nakba Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakba_Day), annual commemoration of the [1948 Palestinian expulsion and flight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Palestinian_expulsion_and_flight) on 15 May by [Palestinians](23267.md) worldwide.
