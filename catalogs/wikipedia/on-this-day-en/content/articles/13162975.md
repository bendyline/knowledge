The **Ministry of Health** is a [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) in Zambia. Its head offices are located in [Lusaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusaka).

## Operations

Subsidiary organizations include the Central Board of Health and the National Malaria Control Centre.

[Healthcare facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_facility) run by the Ministry are categorised into Urban Health Centers and Rural Health Centres (or Health Posts).

[Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School) operated by the Ministry include the Mansa School of Nursing in [Mansa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansa,_Zambia) and the Ndola School of Nursing and Midwifery in [Ndola District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ndola_District).
