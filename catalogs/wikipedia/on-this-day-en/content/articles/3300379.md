The **Theban Necropolis** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مدينة طيبة الجنائزية, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Madīnat Ṭaybah al-Janāʼizīyah*) is a [necropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necropolis) on the west bank of the [Nile](21244.md), opposite [Thebes](65811.md) ([Luxor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxor)) in [Upper Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Egypt). It was used for ritual burials for much of the [Pharaonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Egypt) period, especially during the [New Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Kingdom_of_Egypt).[^cite_note-1]
