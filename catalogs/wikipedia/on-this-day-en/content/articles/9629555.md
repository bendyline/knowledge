**Jewel Cianeh Taylor** (née **Howard**; born 17 January 1963) is a [Liberian](17791.md) politician who served as the 30th [vice president of Liberia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_president_of_Liberia) from 2018 to 2024. She was married to convicted warlord and former president [Charles Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Taylor_\(Liberian_politician\)) from 1997 to 2006 and was first lady of Liberia during his presidency.[^cite_note-2] In 2005, Jewel Taylor was elected to the [Senate of Liberia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Liberia) for [Bong County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bong_County) as a member of the [National Patriotic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Patriotic_Party). She was Chair of the Senate Health and Social Welfare Committee on Gender, Women and Children.[^cite_note-3]

## Life and career

Jewel Taylor is a member of Liberia’s [Kpelle people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kpelle_people). While her husband was president, Taylor held several official posts in the Liberian government, including Deputy Governor of the National Bank of Liberia (forerunner to the current [Central Bank of Liberia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Liberia)), President of the Agriculture Cooperative and Development Bank (ACDB), and Mortgage Financing Underwriter of the First Union National Bank. In addition, she focused on educational, health, and social projects.

Taylor holds a [graduate degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_degree) in [banking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banking) and two [bachelors'](188874.md) in banking and [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics). She is currently enrolled in the [MBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MBA) program at [Cuttington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuttington_University) in Liberia. On 21 December 2011, she graduated from the Louise Arthur Grimes School of Law of the state-owned [University of Liberia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liberia).[^cite_note-4] Two days later, a public dispute arose in Bong County regarding honors allegedly given her; she was announced to be the new holder of the title "Madam Suakoko",[^cite_note-5] an honorary Bong County title memorializing the namesake of the [Suakoko District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suakoko_District), but members of the group that had supposedly awarded her the title soon began denying that the award had been given by their group, saying that the meeting at which she was awarded the title was a meeting to help county residents overcome political differences.[^cite_note-6]

In February 2012, Taylor attempted to introduce legislation into the Liberian parliament that would have made [homosexual](5488304.md) activity a first-degree [felony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felony) carrying the [death penalty](5902.md) as the maximum punishment. The legislation was not passed after President [Ellen Johnson Sirleaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellen_Johnson_Sirleaf) said she would not sign any such bill.[^cite_note-7]

In 2017, Taylor was chosen by [George Weah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Weah) as his running mate on the newly-formed [Coalition for Democratic Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalition_for_Democratic_Change) ticket. Following a runoff in late 2017, she became the first female Vice President of [Liberia](17791.md) when her party won the elections.

In 2020, she was infected with [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronavirus_disease_2019) and was flown to Ghana for treatment.[^cite_note-8] Taylor's son, Philip Taylor, died on 20 January 2026.[^cite_note-9]

In August 2026, Taylor was arrested by the [Liberian National Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberian_National_Police) and charged in connection to a cocaine shipment seized by law enforcement estimated to be worth \$370,000,000 USD bound for Europe. She has been indicted for drug trafficking and money laundering.[^cite_note-10]
