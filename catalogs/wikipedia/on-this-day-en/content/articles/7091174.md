The **Spain men's national field hockey team** represents [Spain](26667.md) in international men's [field hockey](10886.md) competitions. It is controlled by the Royal Spanish Hockey Association, the governing body for field hockey in Spain.

Spain has competed in every [European Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men's_EuroHockey_Championship) or [World Cup](951198.md), and has qualified consistently since 1960 for the [Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_hockey_at_the_Summer_Olympics). They also have won medals in these three main international competitions: six at [European Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men's_EuroHockey_Championship) (two-time winners), four at [World Cups](951198.md) (three-time finalists) and four at [Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_hockey_at_the_Summer_Olympics) (three silvers and a bronze).
