[Turkey](11125639.md) is divided into 81 provinces ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *il*). Each province is divided into a number of [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Turkey) (*ilçe*). Each provincial government is seated in the central district (*merkez ilçe*). For non-[metropolitan municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_municipalities_in_Turkey) designated provinces, the central district bears the name of the province (e.g. the city/district of [Rize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rize) is the central district of [Rize Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rize_Province)). In the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md), the corresponding unit was the *[vilayet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilayet)*.

Each province is administered by an appointed governor (*[vali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W%C4%81li)*) from the [Ministry of the Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Interior_\(Turkey\)).

## Background

After the collapse of the Ottoman Empire and the [official establishment of the Republic of Turkey on 29 October 1923](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_Day_\(Turkey\)), changes were made to the administrative system. Two years later, [Ardahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardahan_Province), [Beyoğlu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyo%C4%9Flu), [Çatalca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%87atalca), [Tunceli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunceli), [Ergani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ergani), [Gelibolu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelibolu), [Genç](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gen%C3%A7_\(il\)), [Kozan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kozan,_Adana), [Oltu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oltu), [Muş](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu%C5%9F_Province), [Siverek](4116477.md) and [Üsküdar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%9Csk%C3%BCdar) provinces were transformed into districts. In 1927, [Doğubayazıt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Do%C4%9Fubayaz%C4%B1t) was transformed into a district and was made a part of [Ağrı](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C4%9Fr%C4%B1). In 1929, Muş became a province again and [Bitlis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitlis) became a district. Four years later, [Aksaray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aksaray_Province), [Cebelibereket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmaniye_Province), [Hakkâri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakk%C3%A2ri_Province) and [Şebinkarahisar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9Eebinkarahisar_District) became districts, [Mersin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mersin_Province) and [Silifke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silifke) were merged to form a new province called [İçel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mersin_Province), and [Artvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artvin_Province) and [Rize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rize_Province) were merged to form a new province called [Çoruh](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%87oruh_vilayeti), bringing the number down to fifty-six. In 1936, Rize, [Tunceli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunceli_Province) and Hakkâri became provinces again, in the same year Dersim was renamed Tunceli; 3 years later in 1939, [Hatay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatay_Province) was [annexed to Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Hatay) and became a province. In 1953, it was decided that [Uşak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U%C5%9Fak_Province) would become a province and that [Kırşehir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C4%B1r%C5%9Fehir_Province) would be transformed into a district, one year later in 1954 [Adıyaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad%C4%B1yaman_Province), [Nevşehir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nev%C5%9Fehir_Province) and Sakarya gained province status. In 1956, the name of Çoruh province was changed to [Artvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artvin_Province), and in 1957 Kırşehir's province status was restored. After this year, there were no changes in the number of provinces for the next 32 years until Aksaray, [Bayburt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayburt_Province), [Karaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaman_Province) and [Kırıkkale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C4%B1r%C4%B1kkale_Province) became provinces in 1989 along with [Batman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_Province) and [Şırnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9E%C4%B1rnak_Province) in 1990; [Bartın](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bart%C4%B1n_Province) in 1991; [Ardahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardahan_Province) and [Iğdır](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%C4%9Fd%C4%B1r_Province) in 1992; [Yalova](888009.md), [Karabük](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karab%C3%BCk_Province) and [Kilis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilis_Province) in 1995; [Osmaniye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmaniye_Province) in 1996, and [Düzce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%BCzce_Province) in 1999.
