**Sir David Tiva Kapu**, [GCMG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_St_Michael_and_St_George) is a [Solomon Islands](265083.md) Anglican priest and the [governor-general of Solomon Islands](1017894.md) since 2024.[^cite_note-rulersdate-1][^cite_note-YTTavuli-2][^cite_note-3]

## Education

Kapu received his [Master of Theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Theology) degree from the [Pacific Theological College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Theological_College). His thesis titled, "What has the Church to do with the State?" analyzed the role of church and state relationship in [Nggela Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nggela_Islands), Solomon Islands from 1932 to 2007.[^cite_note-4]

## Career

Reverend Kapu has served as the academic dean of studies [Bishop Patteson Theological College at Kohimarama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_Patteson_Theological_College), in Solomon Islands.[^cite_note-YTTavuli-2]

In June 2024, he was the sole candidate put forward for nomination as the next [Governor-General of Solomon Islands](1017894.md), the [monarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Solomon_Islands)'s viceregal representative in the country. He was formally nominated as governor-general on 17 June 2024.[^cite_note-rulersdate-1][^cite_note-5] A Royal Commission to appoint Kapu as Governor General was issued by [King Charles III](125248.md) on June 27, 2024, which came into effect on 7 July 2024 following the expiry of [Sir David Vunagi's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Vunagi) term.

Kapu was appointed a [Knight Grand Cross of the Order of St Michael and St George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Grand_Cross_of_the_Order_of_St_Michael_and_St_George) (GCMG) on 1 October 2024,[^cite_note-6] and was invested as such by [King Charles III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Charles_III) during an audience at Buckingham Palace on 25 February 2025.[^cite_note-7]
