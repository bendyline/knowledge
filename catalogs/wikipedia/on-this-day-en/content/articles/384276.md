This is a **list of presidents of Djibouti**. Since the establishment of the office of [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)) in 1977, there have been two presidents. The president is both [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [head of government](37570.md) of [Djibouti](17207794.md) and the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Djibouti Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djibouti_Armed_Forces). The current president is [Ismaïl Omar Guelleh](392341.md), since 1999.

## Overview

The first president of Djibouti was [Hassan Gouled Aptidon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Gouled_Aptidon), one of the leaders of the Ligue Populaire Africaine pour l'Indépendance (LPAI), who took office on 27 June 1977, the day on which Djibouti was declared a republic.

## Term limits

There was a two-term limit for the president in the [Constitution of Djibouti](51114502.md). This limit was lifted in 2010.[^cite_note-termlimits-2]

## Latest election

Main article: [2026 Djiboutian presidential election](78451207.md)

When 6% of all votes were counted,[^cite_note-PartialResults1-4] an [AFP](471981.md) tally of early partial results covering 38 polling stations in [Djibouti City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djibouti_City) indicated that incumbent president [Ismaïl Omar Guelleh](392341.md) was leading with 96.47% of the vote, while CDU candidate Mohamed Farah Samatar followed second with 3.52% of the vote.[^cite_note-PartialResults2-5]

Later preliminary figures published by the Ministry of the Interior revealed that Guelleh won re-election to a sixth term in office, winning 97.81% of the vote, while Samatar took 2.19% of the vote.[^cite_note-PreliminaryResults1-6] Turnout in the election was reportedly 80.33%.[^cite_note-PreliminaryResults2-7] The results were confirmed by the Constitutional Council on 21 April, which showed that Guelleh was reelected with 97.01% of the vote, followed by Samatar with 2.99%.[^cite_note-8]
