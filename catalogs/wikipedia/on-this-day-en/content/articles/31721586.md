The **Norm Goss Memorial Medal** is an [Australian rules football](2403.md) award presented annually to the player adjudged the [best on ground](906698.md#Australian_rules_football) in the [grand final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_final) of the [Victorian Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victorian_Football_League) (VFL). Prior to 1996, the competition was known as the [Victorian Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victorian_Football_Association) (VFA). The award is named after [Norm Goss Sr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norm_Goss_Sr.), who was heavily involved with the [Port Melbourne Football Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Melbourne_Football_Club) as a player and administrator.

[Tim Rieniets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Rieniets) is the only player to have won the award twice, while two players have received the award as members of the losing teams. The club with the most Norm Goss Memorial Medal wins is [Sandringham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandringham_Football_Club), with eight awards won by players representing the club. When the competition was split into two divisions before the [1989 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_VFA_season), it was only awarded for the Division 1 grand final.

The most recent recipient of the award is [Box Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box_Hill_Hawks)'s [Ollie Greeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ollie_Greeves), who received ten votes for his performance in the [2026 VFL Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_VFL_season) against [Geelong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geelong_Football_Club#Reserves_team).

## Voting and presentation

As at 2022, the winner is voted on by a four-member panel consisting of former players, journalists and media personalities, with one member designated as the chair. Each panellist independently awards 3 votes, 2 votes and 1 vote to the players they regard as the best, second best and third best in the match respectively. These votes are tallied, and the highest number of combined votes wins the medal.

There is no chance of a tie for the medal; if two players are tied for votes, the following countbacks will apply in order:

- the player with the higher number of three-votes;

- the player with the higher number of two-votes;

- the player deemed best by the panel chair.
