A two-part referendum was held in [Slovakia](26830.md) on 4 July 2026.[^cite_note-1] The questions were on whether to cancel life payments for leaders after their terms expire,[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] and whether to reestablish the [Special Prosecutor's Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Prosecutor's_Office?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the [National Criminal Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Criminal_Agency?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-statistical-office-5]

Although a very large majority of those that voted supported the proposals, it, nevertheless, was not adopted as less than 50% of all eligible voters in the country exercised their right to vote in the referendum, a requirement under current Slovak law.

With a turnout of 16%, it was the second lowest in a Slovak referendum since the [1997 Slovak referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_Slovak_referendum).&#x20;

## Background

The referendum came from a petition organised by the [Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democrats_\(Slovakia\)) party which was signed by more than 350,000 citizens.[^cite_note-6]

## Questions

The two questions on the referendum paper was posed as:

> 1\. *Súhlasíte so zrušením tzv. doživotnej renty, napríklad, pre Roberta Fica, ustanovenej v § 24a ods. 1 písm. b) zákona č. 120/1993 Z. z. o platových pomeroch niektorých ústavných činiteľov Slovenskej republiky v aktuálnom znení?*\
> "Do you agree with the abolition of the so-called lifetime pension—for example, for [Robert Fico](4127226.md)—as provided for in Section 24a(1)(b) of Act No. 120/1993 Coll. on the Salaries of Certain Constitutional Officials of the Slovak Republic, as currently in force?"\
> 2\. *Súhlasíte s tým, aby boli obnovené Úrad špeciálnej prokuratúry a Národná kriminálna agentúra?*\
> "Do you agree that the Special Prosecutor's Office and the National Criminal Agency should be reinstated?"[^cite_note-statistical-office-5]

## Results

Polling stations were opened from 07\:00 to 22\:00.[^cite_note-7] As only 16% of the electorate turned out to vote while a majority of votes and a minimum of 50% turnout was required to pass the proposals, the referendum failed.[^cite_note-8]
