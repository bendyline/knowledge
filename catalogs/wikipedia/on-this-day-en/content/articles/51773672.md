**Burj Qalaouiyah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): برج قلويه, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Burj Qalawiyah*) is a municipality in the [Bint Jbeil District](12027489.md) in southern [Lebanon](17771.md).&#x20;

## Etymology

According to [E. H. Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Henry_Palmer), the name *Burj el Alawei* means "the tower of the Alawei".[^cite_note-1]

## History

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described Burj Alawei as: “A village, built of stone, containing about 300 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh), situated on upland, with a few olives, figs, and some arable land round. The water supply is from a well and spring.”[^cite_note-2]

On 13 March 2026, during the [2026 Lebanon war](82549840.md), 12 people were killed in an [Israeli](9282173.md) airstrike on a medical centre, killing several paramedics and doctors.[^cite_note-3]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 98.97% of registered voters in Burj Qalaouiyah. 97.71% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-4]
