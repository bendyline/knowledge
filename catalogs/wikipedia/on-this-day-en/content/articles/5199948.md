**Daniel John Greaney**[^cite_note-\:1-1] (born September 28, 1964[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-3]) is an American [television](29831.md) [writer](45353.md) and political candidate. A member of the [Republican Party](32070.md), he is a declared candidate for the [2028 Republican Party nomination](76305246.md) to succeed [Donald Trump](4848272.md) as [President](24113.md). He has written for *[The Simpsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons)* and [*The Office*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Office_\(American_TV_series\)). He was hired during *[The Simpsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons)*' [seventh season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons_season_7) after writing the first draft of the episode "[King-Size Homer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King-Size_Homer)",[^cite_note-Geaney-4] and left after [season eleven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons_season_11). He returned to the *Simpsons* staff during the [thirteenth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons_season_13), and remains involved with the series (as a consulting producer and occasional writer) into the present day.

On May 26, 2026, Greaney announced that he would run for president of the United States in 2028 as a Republican to succeed term-limited Republican incumbent president Donald Trump, making him one of the first declared candidates for president in 2028.[^cite_note-5]

## Life and career

Greaney attended [Harvard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_College), where he was president of *[Harvard Lampoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Lampoon)* and editor of the *Harvard Lampoon*'s nationally distributed parody of *[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today)*. He also worked as an editorial assistant at *[The Boston Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Boston_Globe)*.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] He graduated from Harvard in 1987.[^cite_note-10]

After college, he worked as a [reporter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reporter) for *[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today)* and co-authored a book entitled *Truly One Nation* with *USA Today* founder [Allen H. Neuharth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_H._Neuharth). He subsequently attended [Harvard Law School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Law_School). While at Harvard Law, he was an editor of the [Harvard Law Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Law_Record).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] Upon graduation, he practiced law in New York for two years,[^cite_note-crim2-13] during which time he co-founded PME, a television and media company operating in [Ukraine](31750.md) and several other former Soviet republics.

Greaney coined the word *[embiggen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_the_Iconoclast#Embiggen_and_cromulent)* for the 1996 "[Lisa the Iconoclast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_the_Iconoclast)" episode from season seven of *[The Simpsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons)*.[^cite_note-Oakley-14]

Greaney has worked on numerous film projects, most notably as composer on *[Borat: Cultural Learnings of America for Make Benefit Glorious Nation of Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borat:_Cultural_Learnings_of_America_for_Make_Benefit_Glorious_Nation_of_Kazakhstan)*.

Greaney is credited with writing "[Bart to the Future](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bart_to_the_Future)", an episode of *The Simpsons* from 2000 based on Donald Trump's [run for president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_2000_presidential_campaign) under the [Reform party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_Party_of_the_United_States_of_America) that presented the possibility of a [Donald Trump](4848272.md) presidency, which would be realized sixteen years later.[^cite_note-15]

On May 26, 2026, Greaney announced his intention to run for [president of the United States](24113.md) in the [2028 election](77541080.md) in a video posted to his [Instagram](31591547.md) account. In a press release, his campaign stated he would run as a "progressive [Republican](32070.md) in the tradition of [Abraham Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lincoln) and [Teddy Roosevelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_Roosevelt)", naming [universal health care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_health_care) and the [Green New Deal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_New_Deal) as policy planks.[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-17] Greaney has also called for the [expansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packing_the_Supreme_Court) of the [Supreme Court](31737.md) to 13 justices.[^cite_note-klee-16] Greaney filed to run for president in the [Republican primary](76305246.md) with the [Federal Election Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Election_Commission) on April 19, 2026.[^cite_note-\:1-1]

Greaney held his first campaign event on June 5, 2026, in [Los Angeles](18110.md).[^cite_note-klee-16] On June 23, Greaney joined “The Hill on [NewsNation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NewsNation)” to discuss his candidacy for president with news correspondent [Laura Ingle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Ingle).[^cite_note-18] Greaney has stated on running that "I see a future where [Trumpism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trumpism) has been defeated, polarization is moderated, and we create an America that works for all."[^cite_note-19]
