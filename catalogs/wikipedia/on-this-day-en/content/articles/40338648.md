**Thomas Teye Partey** (born 13 June 1993) is a Ghanaian professional [footballer](10568.md) who plays as a [defensive midfielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensive_midfielder) for [Saudi Pro League](4525043.md) club [Al-Shabab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Shabab_Club) and the [Ghana national team](1029620.md).

Partey began his professional career at Spanish club [Atlético Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid) in 2013, going on loan to [Mallorca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCD_Mallorca) and [Almería](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UD_Almer%C3%ADa), and returned to Atlético in 2015, with whom he won the [UEFA Europa League](232175.md) and [UEFA Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Super_Cup) in 2018, as well as appearing in the [2016 UEFA Champions League final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_UEFA_Champions_League_final). In 2020, he joined [Arsenal](2174.md) in a [transfer](2643292.md) worth £45 million (€50 million), becoming [the most expensive Ghanaian player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_expensive_association_football_transfers) of all time. He played 167 games over five seasons at Arsenal, winning the [Community Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield) in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_FA_Community_Shield).

A Ghanaian international, Partey represented his nation at three [Africa Cup of Nations](250680.md) ([2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Africa_Cup_of_Nations), [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Africa_Cup_of_Nations), and [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Africa_Cup_of_Nations)) and two [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) ([2022](17742072.md) and [2026](36581929.md)). He was named into the [CAF Team of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAF_Team_of_the_Year) in 2018, and won [Ghana Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghana_Player_of_the_Year) in 2018 and 2019.

In July 2025, Partey was charged by the [Crown Prosecution Service](383064.md) with five counts of [rape](7980471.md) and one count of [sexual assault](218407.md), relating to alleged incidents involving three women in London between 2021 and 2022, and in February 2026 was further charged with two additional counts of rape, relating to alleged incidents involving a fourth woman in London in 2020.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-BBC_News-6]

## Club career

### Atlético Madrid



Partey with [Atlético Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid) in 2018.

Born in [Krobo Odumase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krobo_Odumase), Partey was a product of local club Odometah's youth ranks.[^cite_note-auto-4] He signed with [Atlético Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid) in 2012, after a short spell with [Leganés](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CD_Legan%C3%A9s),[^cite_note-7] and was subsequently moved to [the reserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid_B) a year later. On 10 March 2013, Partey was called up to the main squad for the match against [Real Sociedad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Sociedad).[^cite_note-8] However, he remained unused in the eventual 0–1 home defeat.[^cite_note-9]

#### Mallorca and Almería

On 12 July, Partey was loaned to [Mallorca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCD_Mallorca), [freshly relegated to the second level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012%E2%80%9313_La_Liga).[^cite_note-10] On 18 August, he made his professional debut, in a 0–4 away defeat against [Sabadell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CE_Sabadell_FC).[^cite_note-11] Partey scored his first professional goal on 15 September, netting his side's second of a 2–2 draw at [Hércules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9rcules_CF).[^cite_note-12]

On 27 July 2014, Partey joined [La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga) side [Almería](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UD_Almer%C3%ADa) on a loan.[^cite_note-13] He made his debut in the competition on 23 August, starting in a 1–1 home draw against [Espanyol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCD_Espanyol).[^cite_note-14] Partey scored his first goals in [the main category of Spanish football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_football_league_system) on 11 April 2015, netting a brace in a 3–0 home win against [Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granada_CF).[^cite_note-15]

#### Return to Atlético Madrid

Partey made his first team debut for Atlético Madrid on 28 November 2015, replacing [Luciano Vietto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luciano_Vietto) in a 1–0 home win against Espanyol.[^cite_note-16] On 2 January of the following year, he scored his first league goal for the club, netting the game's only strike in a home success over [Levante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levante_UD).[^cite_note-17] On 28 May, he played in the [UEFA Champions League Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_UEFA_Champions_League_Final) against [Real Madrid](26413.md), replacing [Koke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koke_\(footballer,_born_1992\)) in the 116th minute as his side lost on penalties.[^cite_note-final-18]

Partey signed a contract extension with Atlético Madrid through 2022 on 14 February 2017.[^cite_note-19] On 31 October, he scored his first European goal with a long-range strike to equalise at home to [Qarabağ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qaraba%C4%9F_FK) in a 1–1 draw in the Champions League group game; he became the first African to score in the competition for Atlético.[^cite_note-20] On 1 March 2018, Partey signed a new contract with Atlético Madrid, extending his deal until 2023.[^cite_note-21] On 16 May, he played in the [2018 UEFA Europa League Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_UEFA_Europa_League_Final), as his side won 3–0 against [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympique_de_Marseille).[^cite_note-europa-22]

On 1 September 2019, Partey came on as a late substitute and netted the match's winner in the last minute of the game, as Atlético came back from 2–0 down to win the game by 3–2 against [Eibar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SD_Eibar).[^cite_note-23] He marked his 100th [La Liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Liga) appearance for *Los Rojiblancos* with a [man-of-the-match](906698.md) performance in a 0–0 draw against [Real Madrid](26413.md) in the [Madrid derby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid_derby) four weeks later.[^cite_note-24] Despite leaving Atlético at the beginning of the 2020–21 season, Partey made enough appearances at the start of the season to become eligible for a winner's medal as Atlético won [La Liga that year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_La_Liga).[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-\:0-26]

### Arsenal

On 5 October 2020, [Premier League](11250.md) club [Arsenal](2174.md) announced the signing of Partey on a long-term contract, after activating his £45 million (€50 million) [release clause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buyout_clause) with [Atlético Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Madrid). He was given the number 18 shirt, which had been vacated by [Nacho Monreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nacho_Monreal) the previous season.[^cite_note-27] Upon signing, Partey stated his desire to help Arsenal "back where \[they] belong", describing his decision to move being based on "\[wanting] to experience new challenges", while also crediting the transfer to manager [Mikel Arteta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikel_Arteta) and technical director [Edu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edu_Gaspar).[^cite_note-28]

On 17 October 2020, Partey made his debut for Arsenal as a substitute for [Granit Xhaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granit_Xhaka) in a 1–0 away defeat in the league against [Manchester City](165813.md).[^cite_note-29] Five days later, he started his first match for Arsenal in a 2–1 away win over [Rapid Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SK_Rapid_Wien) in the [UEFA Europa League](232175.md).[^cite_note-30] Midway through a match against [Aston Villa](42173.md) on 8 November, he suffered a thigh injury which saw him miss the rest of the month's games.[^cite_note-31] He returned on 6 December in the [North London derby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_London_derby), but suffered another injury at half-time as Arsenal lost 2–0 to [Tottenham Hotspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.).[^cite_note-32] He did not play again until a month later, which he came off the bench in a 0–0 draw against [Crystal Palace](385313.md).[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] On 22 October 2021, Partey scored his first goal for Arsenal in a 3–1 win against [Aston Villa](42173.md).[^cite_note-35] In February 2022, Partey was named Arsenal's player of the month.[^cite_note-36]

In the [2022–23 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Premier_League), he scored three goals in 33 appearances, including his club's Goal of the Season in a 3–1 victory over Tottenham Hotspur.[^cite_note-37] On 6 August 2023, he won his only honour for Arsenal, the [FA Community Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FA_Community_Shield) on penalties against Manchester City; he played the entire game and was booked after eight minutes for kicking the ball away after fouling [Julián Alvarez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juli%C3%A1n_Alvarez).[^cite_note-shield-38][^cite_note-39] In November 2023, he sustained a hamstring injury which kept him sidelined for most of the [2023–24 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Premier_League).[^cite_note-40]

On 24 August 2024, Partey returned to Arsenal's starting line-up in their second game of the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Arsenal_F.C._season) and scored in a 2–0 victory over Aston Villa. The goal was the player's first goal for the club since March 2023.[^cite_note-41] He scored an outside-the-box shot against [Nottingham Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_Forest_F.C.) on 23 November 2024.[^cite_note-42] In a post-game interview against [Sporting C.P.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_C.P.), Arteta praised his performances in the past weeks, stating, "This year, he’s been so consistent...that’s a big thing, he’s a massive player for us".[^cite_note-43] On 2 February 2025, Partey scored a deflected shot off [John Stones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Stones) against Manchester City in a 5–1 rout at the [Emirates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emirates_Stadium).[^cite_note-44] His contract expired at the end of the season and negotiations for a new deal were not agreed.[^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47]

### Villarreal

On 7 August 2025, La Liga club [Villarreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarreal_CF) announced the signing of Partey on a free transfer.[^cite_note-villarreal-48] The club stated that it was aware of the criminal charges against Partey and that it respected the [presumption of innocence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presumption_of_innocence).[^cite_note-villarreal-48] He made his debut eight days later as the [season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_La_Liga) began with a 3–0 home win over [Real Oviedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Oviedo), as a substitute for the last eight minutes; according to journalist [Sid Lowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sid_Lowe), there was no opposition to Partey's involvement in the game.[^cite_note-49]

Partey made an appearance as a 78th-minute substitute against Arsenal's rivals [Tottenham Hotspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.) in the [2025–26 UEFA Champions League](74127997.md) league phase, where his introduction to the match was met with loud boos from home fans at the [Tottenham Hotspur Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_Stadium).[^cite_note-ESPNsept1-50][^cite_note-IndeSept1-51][^cite_note-NYTsept1-52] Villarreal lost the match 1–0.[^cite_note-ESPNsept2-53]

On 17 June 2026, Villarreal announced it would not intend to renew Partey's contract for a further season, and becoming a [free agent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_agent) since the end of June.[^cite_note-54]

### Al-Shabab

On 21 August 2026, Partey signed with [Saudi Pro League](4525043.md) club [Al-Shabab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Shabab_FC_\(Riyadh\)) on a free transfer.[^cite_note-55]

## International career

In May 2016, Partey was called up for the first time to the [Ghana national team](1029620.md) by manager [Avram Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avram_Grant), ahead of a [2017 Africa Cup of Nations qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Africa_Cup_of_Nations_qualification) match against [Mauritius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauritius_national_football_team).[^cite_note-56] He made his debut on 5 June, replacing [Frank Acheampong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Acheampong) for the final 11 minutes of a 2–0 away win that booked the Black Stars' position in the [finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Africa_Cup_of_Nations).[^cite_note-57] On 5 September 2017, Partey scored his first international hat-trick in a 5–1 win against [Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congo_national_football_team) in [2018 FIFA World Cup qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification).[^cite_note-58]

Partey was chosen in [Kwesi Appiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Kwesi_Appiah)'s 23-man squad for the [2019 Africa Cup of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Africa_Cup_of_Nations) in Egypt.[^cite_note-59] In their last group game, he scored in a 2–0 win over [Guinea-Bissau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinea-Bissau_national_football_team) at the [Suez Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suez_Stadium) as the Black Stars topped their group.[^cite_note-60] He netted in the penalty shootout at the end of the last-16 game against [Tunisia](1029953.md) on 8 July, though his team was eliminated.[^cite_note-61]

Partey won [Ghana Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghana_Player_of_the_Year) in 2018 and 2019.[^cite_note-ghana18-62][^cite_note-ghana19-63] Ahead of the [2021 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Africa_Cup_of_Nations_qualification), as well as for the [2022 FIFA World Cup](17742072.md) qualifiers, Partey was named Ghana's vice-captain.[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65]

In November 2022, Partey was called up to the 26-man Ghana squad that would compete in [Qatar](8238258.md) for the [2022 FIFA World Cup](17742072.md).[^cite_note-66]

In May 2026, Partey was included in Ghana's squad for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md), as vice-captain.[^cite_note-67] On 12 June, it was announced Partey had been denied entry to Canada, and would be unable to feature in Ghana's opening World Cup match against [Panama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panama_national_football_team) in [Toronto](64646.md) as a result.[^cite_note-68] On 16 June, Partey's appeal against his denial of entry to Canada was dismissed by [The Canadian Federal Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Court_\(Canada\)),the court documents revealed his application to enter Canada contained the answer "no" to the question of whether or not he had "ever been charged for any [criminal offence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_offence) in any country".[^cite_note-69][^cite_note-70][^cite_note-71] He was given a visa by the United States for Ghana's two other games, with officials saying that they were aware of his upcoming trial, but he had not been convicted of any crime.[^cite_note-72] On 21 June, Partey returned to Ghana's squad for their FIFA World Cup group-stage match against [England](9904.md) at the [Gillette Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillette_Stadium).[^cite_note-73]

Partey was involved in the [Amazon Original](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Amazon_Prime_Video_original_programming) sports [docuseries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docuseries) *[All or Nothing: Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_or_Nothing:_Arsenal)*, which documented the club by spending time with the coaching staff and players behind the scenes both on and off the field throughout their [2021–22 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_Arsenal_F.C._season).[^cite_note-74][^cite_note-75]

## Personal life

Partey [converted to Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Converted_to_Islam) in March 2022. He said that his decision was influenced by his relationship with his Moroccan girlfriend, Sara Bella.[^cite_note-76] In June 2022, it was reported that he had changed his first name to Yakubu as part of his [religious conversion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religious_conversion), while remaining Thomas Partey both legally and professionally;[^cite_note-77] he said days later that he had changed his name back to Thomas.[^cite_note-78]

In June 2022, Partey was made a chief of the [Manya Krobo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manya_Krobo_District) people of the [Eastern Region of Ghana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Region_\(Ghana\)) by the paramount chief, Nene [Sakite II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakite_II), following Ghana's qualification for the [2022 FIFA World Cup](17742072.md).[^cite_note-79]

### Legal issues

In July 2025, Partey was charged with five counts of [rape](7980471.md) and one count of [sexual assault](218407.md), relating to alleged incidents involving three women between 2021 and 2022. He appeared at [Westminster Magistrates' Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_Magistrates'_Court) on 5 August 2025.[^cite_note-80][^cite_note-81][^cite_note-82] Partey was the previously unnamed footballer who news reports had said had been arrested on suspicion of rape in July 2022.[^cite_note-83][^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85] He was not named at this point due to legal reasons and continued to play for Arsenal while under investigation. However, he was named as the footballer under investigation on social media, was subject to jeers related to the investigation while playing, and [MPs](3776360.md) and women's groups called for his suspension from the club.[^cite_note-86][^cite_note-87][^cite_note-88]

The [Metropolitan Police](192450.md) submitted an evidence file on Partey to the [Crown Prosecution Service](383064.md) to assess possible criminal charges in January 2025.[^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90][^cite_note-91] Arsenal's handling of the case while Partey was under investigation was criticized by some commentators, politicians, and advocacy groups. In this period, they had continued to play him regularly despite being aware of the allegations and investigation. Furthermore, one of Partey's alleged victims contacted the club and said they did not show enough sympathy to her.[^cite_note-92][^cite_note-93][^cite_note-94][^cite_note-95] Arsenal have stated that "The player's contract ended on June 30. Due to ongoing legal proceedings, the club is unable to comment on the case."[^cite_note-skysports1-96] With head coach [Mikel Arteta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikel_Arteta) stating he was "100 per cent" sure the club had followed all the correct processes around the arrest and investigation.[^cite_note-skysports1-96]

Partey appeared at [Southwark Crown Court](26120657.md) on 17 September 2025, where he entered a plea of not guilty to the charges of rape.[^cite_note-skysports1-96][^cite_note-NYTsept2-97][^cite_note-AJsept1-98][^cite_note-skynewssept1-99] He was granted bail on the same conditions as before his appearance.[^cite_note-skysports1-96] A trial date of 2 November 2026 was set for the case to be heard at Southwark Crown Court.[^cite_note-NYTsept2-97] On 12 February 2026, Partey was charged with an additional two counts of rape against a new complainant.[^cite_note-BBC_News-6] On 13 April 2026, Partey pleaded not guilty to the two counts at Southwark Crown Court. The judge ordered all seven counts to be heard together, and the trial date was likely to be pushed back to January 2027.[^cite_note-100]

On 8 April 2026, Partey was put under investigation by a judge in [Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra) for allegations of illegal watch smuggling.[^cite_note-101][^cite_note-102]
