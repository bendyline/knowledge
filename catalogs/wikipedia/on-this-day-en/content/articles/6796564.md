# List of diplomatic missions of India



Diplomatic missions of India&#x20;

&#x20; Countries that host an Indian embassy or high commission

&#x20; Countries that do not host Indian missions

&#x20; Republic of India

The [Republic of India](14533.md) has one of the largest diplomatic networks, reflecting its links in the world and particularly in neighbouring regions: [Central Asia](6742.md), the [Middle East](19323.md), [East Africa](260987.md), [Europe](9239.md), [Southeast Asia](28741.md), and the rest of the [Indian subcontinent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_subcontinent). There are also far-flung missions in the [Caribbean](18956035.md) and the [Pacific](23070.md), locations of historical [Indian diaspora](26998691.md) communities.

As a member of the [Commonwealth of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations), Indian diplomatic missions in the capitals of other Commonwealth members are known as [High Commissions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Commission). In other cities of Commonwealth countries, the consular missions are known as "Consulates-General",[^cite_note-1] except those in Bangladesh, which are known as "Assistant High Commission(s)".

## Current missions

As of 2026, India has 220 resident missions operating in various countries worldwide, not including honorary missions.&#x20;
