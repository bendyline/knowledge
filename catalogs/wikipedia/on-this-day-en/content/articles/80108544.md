The **2025–26 New York Knicks season** was the 80th season of the franchise in the [National Basketball Association](22093.md) (NBA). On June 3, 2025, the Knicks dismissed head coach [Tom Thibodeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Thibodeau) after five seasons with the team.[^cite_note-1] On July 7, the Knicks hired [Mike Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Brown_\(basketball,_born_1970\)) as their head coach.[^cite_note-2] The Knicks won the [NBA Championship](82427430.md) during this season, for the first time since [1973](4875070.md). It was their third title in franchise history, and their first in which their Finals opponent was not the [Los Angeles Lakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Lakers).

In the [2025 NBA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NBA_Cup), the Knicks won East Group C with a 3–1 group stage record to advance to the knockout stage for the third straight year, also doing so as a group winner for the second straight year, by clinching the tiebreaker over the [Miami Heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Miami_Heat_season) via their head-to-head victory on November 14. In the knockout stage, they defeated the [Toronto Raptors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Toronto_Raptors_season) in the quarterfinals, the [Orlando Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Orlando_Magic_season) in the semifinals, and finally the [San Antonio Spurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_San_Antonio_Spurs_season) in the championship round, winning the Cup title. The Knicks finished the tournament with a 6–1 record, becoming the first Cup champions to win the tournament title without a perfect record.

In a span from December 31 to January 19, the Knicks had a 2–9 stretch that featured two separate four-game losing streaks, their longest since the [2023–24 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_New_York_Knicks_season). Following a loss to the [Dallas Mavericks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Dallas_Mavericks_season), the Knicks closed out the month with a five-game winning streak, which included a 120–66 victory over their [rival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knicks%E2%80%93Nets_rivalry), the [Brooklyn Nets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Brooklyn_Nets_season), which also broke a franchise record for the largest victory margin in a regular season game.[^cite_note-3]

On March 30, the Knicks clinched the playoffs for the fourth consecutive season following a [Philadelphia 76ers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Philadelphia_76ers_season) loss to the [Miami Heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Miami_Heat_season). On April 9, the Knicks improved over their 51–31 record from the [previous season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_New_York_Knicks_season) with a win over the [Boston Celtics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Boston_Celtics_season). They finished the regular season with a 53–29 record, placing third in the East.



Temporary "Champions Way" street sign after the Knicks' championship win at [Broadway](4626.md) in [Financial District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_District,_Manhattan), [Manhattan](45470.md).

In the first round of the playoffs, the Knicks defeated the [Atlanta Hawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Atlanta_Hawks_season) in six games, advancing to the conference semifinals for the fourth consecutive year. The series concluded with a record-breaking game where the Knicks set an NBA playoff record for the largest halftime lead (47 points) and a franchise record for the largest playoff margin of victory (51 points).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] They would later sweep the Philadelphia 76ers in the conference semifinals and the [Cleveland Cavaliers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Cleveland_Cavaliers_season) in the [Eastern Conference finals](809525.md#Eastern_Conference_finals) (New York's second straight appearance) to clinch their first [NBA Finals](890793.md) berth since [1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_NBA_Finals).&#x20;

The Knicks faced the [San Antonio Spurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_San_Antonio_Spurs_season) in the [2026 NBA Finals](82427430.md), which was a rematch of the 1999 Finals and this season's NBA Cup championship game. The Knicks had also won a franchise-record 13 straight playoff games, which stands as the [second-longest single-postseason winning streak in NBA history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NBA_longest_winning_streaks#Playoffs) behind only the [2016–17 Golden State Warriors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016%E2%80%9317_Golden_State_Warriors_season), spanning from a first-round game 4 victory following a 2–1 deficit against the Hawks to a game 3 Finals loss to the Spurs.[^cite_note-6] In game 4 of the Finals, New York achieved the largest comeback in NBA Finals history, in which they overcame a 29-point deficit to beat San Antonio 107–106.[^cite_note-7] The Knicks defeated the Spurs in five games in the NBA Finals to win their first championship since [1973](4875070.md). They also became the first team to win the [NBA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NBA_Cup), win a playoff series, make the Finals, and win the NBA championship in the same season.

The Knicks entered the draft with a single second-round pick, which originally belonged to the [Memphis Grizzlies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis_Grizzlies). The Knicks acquired the selection from the [Oklahoma City Thunder](18256220.md) via a 2024 trade; it conveyed as a more favorable pick because Memphis finished with a worse record in the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_NBA_season) than the [Boston Celtics](43376.md), the pick's first trade destination before such condition was added.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] The team had originally held another second-round selection, but it was forfeited due to tampering violations related to the free agency signing of [Jalen Brunson](43132078.md) in 2022, following three consecutive drafts that each featured only 58 total picks due to various teams—including the Knicks in prior years—being penalized for free agency violations.[^cite_note-10] They had also traded their original 2025 first-round pick to the [Brooklyn Nets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_Nets) in 2024 as an exchange for [Mikal Bridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikal_Bridges).[^cite_note-11]

With the 50th overall pick, the Knicks selected [Kobe Sanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_Sanders), but immediately traded his draft rights to the [Los Angeles Clippers](72892.md) in exchange for the draft rights to the 51st pick, [Mohamed Diawara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Diawara), and to [Luka Mitrović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luka_Mitrovi%C4%87).[^cite_note-12]
