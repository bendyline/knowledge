The **Minister of Defence** (Urdu: 'وزیرِ دفاع') heads the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(Pakistan\)). The minister serves in the cabinet of the [Prime Minister](461896.md) and is required to be a member of [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Pakistan).

In the history of the country, the defence portfolio has usually been headed by the head of the government, be that the President or Prime Minister of the country. The first defence minister was [Liaquat Ali Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liaquat_Ali_Khan).
