# Tennis in Germany

&#x20;**Tennis** is one of the most popular sports in [Germany](11867.md) with more than five million active players. The [German Tennis Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Tennis_Federation) is the largest tennis federation in the world with ca. 1.4 million members.[^cite_note-1]

World No. 1 ranked tennis players from Germany



[Steffi Graf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steffi_Graf)



[Boris Becker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Becker)



[Angelique Kerber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelique_Kerber)

All statistics below are based on the data from the [ATP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) and the [WTA](57768.md) in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_tennis#Open_Era) (since 1968).

Germany won the [5th most singles Grand Slam titles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Grand_Slam_singles_champions_by_country#Total_Grand_Slam_titles_by_country) and is among the [10 most successful nations regarding all Grand Slam titles combined (singles, doubles and mixed)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Open_Era_Grand_Slam_champions_by_country). German female tennis players won the 2nd most singles titles on the [WTA Tour](1398173.md) while German male tennis players have won the 7nd most singles titles on the [ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

Singles Grand Slam champions from Germany before the Open Era were [Gottfried von Cramm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_von_Cramm), who became the first non American, British, Australian or French player to win a singles slam title at the [1934 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1934_French_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles),[^cite_note-4] [Henner Henkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henner_Henkel), [Cilly Aussem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cilly_Aussem) and [Hilde Krahwinkel Sperling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilde_Krahwinkel_Sperling), with von Cramm and Krahwinkel Sperling becoming world number 1.

Active players are in boldface.&#x20;

*All lists are correct as of 3 October 2026.*
