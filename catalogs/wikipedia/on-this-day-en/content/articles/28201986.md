**Tamacine** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ﺗﻤﺎﺳﻴﻦ) (sometimes written **Témacine**) is a town and [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Algeria), and capital of [Témacine District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%A9macine_District), in [Touggourt Province](63749221.md), [Algeria](358.md). According to the 2008 census it has a population of 20,067,[^cite_note-census2008-2] up from 15,933 in 1998,[^cite_note-3] and an annual growth rate of 2.4%.[^cite_note-census2008-2] It is around 10 kilometres (6.2 mi) south of [Touggourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touggourt). The [Tugurt language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tugurt_language) is spoken by some residents of Tamacine.[^cite_note-Ethnologue-4]

## History

The mosque in the town of Témacine, the Mosque of Si al-Hajj, dates to 1431.[^cite_note-5]

## Geography



Great Mosque Street in January 1913

Tamacine lies at an elevation of 82 metres (269 ft) within the system of [oases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oases) near Touggourt. These oases feature extensive [palm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_\(plant\)) groves and other agriculture. Beyond this is the arid and barren landscape of the Sahara, featuring areas of [sand dunes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sand_dunes) ([ergs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erg_\(landform\))) and flat rocky plains ([regs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert#Desert_features)). Lake Tamacine, a small lake covering 2 hectares (4.9 acres) and 402 metres (440 yd) long, lies along the eastern side of the town, and is notable for its variety of birds and fish.[^cite_note-OfficialWebsite-1]

## Climate

See also: [Climate of Touggourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touggourt#Climate)

Tamacine, like other towns in the [Saharan](325363.md) region, has a hot [desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_climate) with very hot summers and mild winters, and very little precipitation.

## Economy

The most significant sector in Tamacine's economy is agriculture, in particular the farming of [date palms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Date_palms); over 200,000 palms are planted across the commune. Other fruit trees are also cultivated, including [olives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive), [figs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_fig), [apricots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apricot), [pomegranates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomegranate), [apples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple), [grapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grape) and [plums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plum). Livestock reared in the commune are mainly [goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goat) and [sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheep).[^cite_note-OfficialWebsite-1]

Tamacine also has various traditional industries, such as [pottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pottery), [jewellery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewellery), [textile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile) industries including [embroidery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embroidery) and [knitting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knitting), and [blacksmithing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blacksmithing).[^cite_note-OfficialWebsite-1]

Tourism is another important industry for Tamacine. The most notable attractions include Lake Tamacine, as well as the various mosques and palaces in the town.[^cite_note-OfficialWebsite-1]

## Infrastructure

Drinking water is available to 99% of the population, and access to the sewerage network is available to 95%.[^cite_note-OfficialWebsite-1]

## Health

Tamacine has two municipal health clinics, a private health clinic, and several pharmacies.[^cite_note-OfficialWebsite-1]

## Education

Tamacine's educational facilities include 8 municipal elementary schools, a vocational training center, apprenticeships, and an educational center for mentally handicapped children.[^cite_note-OfficialWebsite-1] 8.3% of the population has a tertiary education, and another 20.9% has completed secondary education.[^cite_note-6] The overall literacy rate is 83.4%, and is 88.5% among males and 78.5% among females.[^cite_note-7]

## Transportation

Tamacine is connected by local roads to [Touggourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touggourt) to the north and the N3 national highway to the west. From Touggourt, major highways lead north to [Biskra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biskra), east to [El Oued](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Oued), and south to [Ouargla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouargla) and [Illizi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illizi). The town is served by the [Sidi Madhi Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidi_Madhi_Airport) near Touggourt.[^cite_note-OfficialWebsite-1]

The road network of Tamacine covers 300 kilometres (190 mi).

## Localities

The commune is composed of five localities:[^cite_note-8]

- Temacine

- El Behour

- Sidi Ameur

- El Ksour

- [Tamellaht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamellaht)
