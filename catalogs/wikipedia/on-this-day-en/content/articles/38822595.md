**Jorge David Glas Espinel** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˈxoɾ.xe ðaˈβið ɣlas espiˈnel\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish); born 13 September 1969) is an Ecuadorian politician and electrical engineer. He served as [Vice President of Ecuador](17370343.md) from 24 May 2013 to 13 December 2017. Then Ecuadorian president [Lenín Moreno](8187373.md) suspended Glas of his official duties as vice president on 3 August 2017. In December 2017, Glas was sentenced to six years imprisonment by a Criminal Tribunal of the [National Court of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Ecuador), for receiving over \$13.5 million in bribes in the [Odebrecht scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odebrecht_case).[^cite_note-3] In April 2024 during a [raid on the Mexican embassy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_raid_on_the_Mexican_embassy_in_Ecuador) by Ecuadorian police, Glas was arrested and held in prison, resulting in Mexico severing relations with Ecuador.

## Early life and education

Jorge Glas was born 13 September 1969 in Guayaquil. His paternal grandfather was a German Jewish immigrant named Heriberto Glas Cohen[^cite_note-german_jewish-4] who escaped the Nazi regime. For this reason, he holds German citizenship.[^cite_note-german_citizenship-5] He has known and been friends with [Rafael Correa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Correa), the former [president of Ecuador](840787.md), since their time as [boy scouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asociaci%C3%B3n_de_Scouts_del_Ecuador).[^cite_note-contratos-6]

On 22 September 2008, Glas graduated in electrical and electronics engineering from the [ESPOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escuela_Superior_Politecnica_del_Litoral).[^cite_note-bachiller-7] National Assembly member for the [Patriotic Society Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriotic_Society_Party), [Galo Lara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galo_Lara), reported Glas on 3 January 2013 to the police for plagiarism in his thesis and for holding public office while not being qualified to do so. Lara said that Glas during his term as General Manager of the Solidarity Fund needed an academic title of the third degree, which Glas did not have.[^cite_note-bachiller-7] Glas's thesis was reviewed by [Genove Gneco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genove_Gneco?action=edit\&redlink=1), a professor from the [Dominican Republic](8060.md) who found suspected plagiarism in four theses by top governmental officials in his own country, including then-President [Danilo Medina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danilo_Medina). Gneco found 35% of Glas's thesis to be suspected plagiarism.[^cite_note-plagiarism-8] An investigation by a commission set up by [ESPOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escuela_Superior_Politecnica_del_Litoral) acquitted Glas of plagiarism. Glas however recognized that he should have cited his sources better.[^cite_note-9]

## Political career

Starting in 2007, Glas held several political appointments in the government of [Rafael Correa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Correa). He was general manager of the now-closed [Solidarity Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fondo_de_Solidaridad?action=edit\&redlink=1) between 2007 and 2009. The company was an amalgamation of several public companies of telecommunications and electricity generation and distribution. He was also president of the [National Telecommunications Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Telecommunications_Council?action=edit\&redlink=1). Afterwards, Glas was Minister of Telecommunications and later became Coordinating Minister of Strategic Sectors. He directed the merger of [Andinatel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andinatel?action=edit\&redlink=1) S.A. and [Pacifitel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacifitel?action=edit\&redlink=1) S.A. into the [National Corporation of Telecommunications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNT_EP), a corporation of which he became the first chairperson.[^cite_note-candidato-10] As Coordinating Minister of Strategic Sectors, he was responsible for building several hydroelectric stations.[^cite_note-contratos-6]

In the [2013 Ecuadorian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Ecuadorian_general_election), Glas was elected [Vice President of Ecuador](17370343.md). He commenced his term as vice president on 24 May 2013.[^cite_note-11] On 4 August 2017, Glas was suspended from his duties as vice president by new president [Lenín Moreno](8187373.md).[^cite_note-12]

## Prison sentences

On 15 December 2017, Glas was sentenced to six years in prison for taking \$13.5 million from Brazilian conglomerate Odebrecht.[^cite_note-13] On 2 January 2018, under Ecuador's Constitution's Article 146, as Glas was unable to exercise his office for 90 days, he was stripped of his office.[^cite_note-14] On 6 January 2018, [María Alejandra Vicuña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mar%C3%ADa_Alejandra_Vicu%C3%B1a) was formally sworn in as [Vice President of Ecuador](17370343.md) after the National Assembly voted to approve her for the position.[^cite_note-15] In October 2019, The National Supreme Court of Justice reaffirmed his six-year prison sentence. Glas was imprisoned in Latacunga, south of Quito, in 2017.[^cite_note-16] In April 2020, Glas was sentenced to 8 years in prison for aggravated bribery by a Court of the National Court of Justice. In addition, he lost his political rights for 25 years.[^cite_note-17] In January 2021, Ecuador's Superior Court added another sentence of eight years in prison for misuse of public funds in an oil contract.[^cite_note-18]

Glas was granted [Habeas corpus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habeas_corpus) on 10 April 2022, which allowed him to be released on parole for 40 days since his legal guarantees had been allegedly violated and also due to the inability to provide in prison the care that he needed for a health condition,[^cite_note-19] which was revoked by a court in the province of [Santa Elena](17572552.md), with which Jorge Glas was imprisoned again.[^cite_note-20] In November 2022, Glas was released again, but could not leave the country during the remainder of his sentences. Then the attorney general's office said in a statement it was insisting on charging Glas with respect to the case involving public funds collected to aid the reconstruction of [Manabí Province](728376.md) after [a 2016 earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Ecuador_earthquake). In December 2023 Jorge Glas entered the [Mexican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexica) embassy in [Quito](209982.md) and asked for [political asylum](1621432.md) on the grounds he was being politically persecuted.[^cite_note-21]

On 5 April 2024, Ecuadorian police [raided the Mexican embassy in Quito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_attack_on_the_Mexican_embassy_in_Ecuador) and arrested Glas.[^cite_note-22] Mexico had granted Glas asylum just prior to the raid.[^cite_note-aj130424-23] After the raid, Mexico severed diplomatic relations with Ecuador.[^cite_note-24] The next day, [Nicaragua](21362.md) broke ties with Ecuador in solidarity with Mexico.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

On 9 April 2024, prison authorities said that Glas had been hospitalized in the Guayaquil naval hospital after not eating for 24 hours, adding that his condition was stable.[^cite_note-27]

On 9 October 2024 the U.S. State Department banned Glas from entering the United States on charges of corruption.[^cite_note-28]

On 5 January 2025, Glas was evacuated from La Roca prison in Guayaquil, after an alleged assassination attempt against him.[^cite_note-29] On 30 June 2025, Glas was sentenced to another 13 years in prison for misusing public funds meant for reconstruction following the [2016 Ecuador earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Ecuador_earthquake).[^cite_note-30] Glas was transferred to the [El Encuentro](81582900.md) maximum security prison on 11 November 2025.[^cite_note-31]
