**Harald V** (21 February 1937 – 28 August 2026) was [King of Norway](4154892.md) from 1991 until [his death](84083326.md) in 2026.

Born in [Skaugum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skaugum), Asker, during the reign of his paternal grandfather, King [Haakon VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haakon_VII), Harald was the third child and only son of Crown Prince Olav (later King [Olav V](66068.md)) and [Crown Princess Märtha of Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Princess_M%C3%A4rtha_of_Norway). Harald was second in the [line of succession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Succession_to_the_Norwegian_throne) at the time of his birth, behind his father. In 1940, as a result of the [German occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_occupation_of_Norway) during [World War II](32927.md), the royal family went into exile where Harald spent parts of his childhood in Sweden and subsequently the United States.&#x20;

After World War II ended in 1945, Harald returned to Norway, and later studied for periods at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo), the [Norwegian Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Military_Academy), and [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford). An active sportsman, Harald represented Norway in sailing at the [1964](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_at_the_1964_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_5.5_Metre), [1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_at_the_1968_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_5.5_Metre), and [1972 Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_at_the_1972_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Soling). In 1970, Harald was named the founding president of the [Norwegian Sailing Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Sailing_Federation). He went on to serve as President of Honour for [World Sailing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Sailing) starting in 1994, a position he held until his death, while remaining an active competitor in World Championships until 2022.

Following King Haakon's death in 1957, Harald became crown prince as his father became King Olav V. He married [Sonja Haraldsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonja_Haraldsen) in 1968; their relationship was initially controversial due to her status as a [commoner](24710172.md). They had two children, [Märtha Louise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_M%C3%A4rtha_Louise_of_Norway) and [Haakon](98258.md). In 1990, when his father's health declined, Harald became [regent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regent) and took most of the powers of the monarchy while Olav remained the *de jure* monarch.&#x20;

Following his father's death in 1991, Harald acceded to the throne. He was the first Norwegian-born monarch to inherit the throne since [Olav IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olav_IV) in 1380. Harald's leadership during major Norwegian national crises earned him widespread acclaim both domestically and internationally. His presence during events such as the [1992 New Year's Day Storm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_New_Year's_Day_Storm), the [2011 Norway attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Norway_attacks), the [2020 Gjerdrum landslide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Gjerdrum_landslide), and the [COVID-19 pandemic in Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Norway) earned him the popular nickname "grandfather of the nation" (nasjonens bestefar).

During his reign, Harald suffered from several health issues, with his son acting as regent several times between 2003 and 2026. He became Norway's oldest reigning monarch in 2024, surpassing his father. Following the [death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_state_funeral_of_Elizabeth_II) of [Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II), his paternal second cousin, in 2022, he became Europe's oldest reigning monarch. Harald died in 2026 in [Oslo](22309.md), aged 89, and was succeeded by his son [Haakon VIII](98258.md).

## Early life and education

### Birth



Harald with his mother [Crown Princess Märtha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_M%C3%A4rtha_of_Sweden) in 1937

Harald was born on 21 February 1937 at the [Skaugum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skaugum) estate[^cite_note-HaraldVdeath-2] during the reign of his grandfather, King [Haakon VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haakon_VII). His parents were [Crown Prince Olav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Prince_Olav) (later Olav V) and [Princess Märtha of Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_M%C3%A4rtha_of_Sweden). He had two older sisters, [Princess Ragnhild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Ragnhild) and [Princess Astrid](439166.md).[^cite_note-3] Harald was baptised in the [Royal Chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slottskapellet_\(Oslo\)) of the [Royal Palace](1489319.md) in [Oslo](22309.md) on 31 March by bishop Johan Lunde.[^cite_note-Lange-Nielsen-4]<sup>: 17 </sup>

His godparents were his paternal grandparents, King Haakon VII and [Queen Maud of Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Maud_of_Norway); his maternal grandparents [Prince Carl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Carl,_Duke_of_V%C3%A4sterg%C3%B6tland) and [Princess Ingeborg of Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Ingeborg_of_Denmark); his maternal uncle, King [Leopold III of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leopold_III_of_Belgium); his paternal grandaunt [Queen Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_of_Teck) and King [George VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_VI) of the United Kingdom; and [Crown Princess Ingrid of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Princess_Ingrid_of_Denmark).[^cite_note-yahooline-5]

At the time of Harald's birth, he was second in the line of [succession to the Norwegian throne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Succession_to_the_Norwegian_throne), after his father.[^cite_note-Haralddeathtatler-6]

### Second World War



Harald as [mascot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mascot) for the [Royal Norwegian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Air_Force) at its [Little Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Norway) airfield in Canada, 1943

In 1940, the entire royal family fled Oslo because of the [German invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Weser%C3%BCbung). It was deemed safer for the family to split up. The King and Olav remained in Norway, and Märtha made her way to Sweden with Ragnhild, Astrid, and Harald. They reached Sweden on the night of 10 April, but although Märtha was Swedish-born, they encountered problems at the border station. According to Astrid and others who were present, they were admitted only after the driver threatened to ram the border gate. Another account does not describe the escape so dramatically.[^cite_note-Hegge-7] When the King and Olav asked Swedish foreign minister [Christian Günther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_G%C3%BCnther) whether they could sleep one night in Sweden without being interned, their request was refused.[^cite_note-Hegge-7]

Harald spent the following days in [Sälen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A4len) before moving to his maternal uncle [Prince Carl Bernadotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Carl_Bernadotte)'s home in [Frötuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fr%C3%B6tuna) on 16 April. On 26 April, the group moved to [Drottningholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drottningholm) in [Stockholm](26741.md). King [Gustaf V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustaf_V), Märtha's paternal uncle, is accounted to have had an amicable relationship with his Norwegian guests, but the topic of the war in Norway was not to be raised. Influential Swedish politicians, including Minister of Justice [Karl Gustaf Westman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Gustaf_Westman), wanted Märtha and Harald to be sent back to Norway so he could be proclaimed King by the Germans.[^cite_note-Hegge-7][^cite_note-8]

After the King and Olav had to leave Norway on 7 June, they felt Sweden might not be the best place for the rest of the family, and began planning for them to go to the United States. On 17 August, Märtha and her children left for the United States from [Petsamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pechengsky_District), Finland, aboard the [United States Army](32087.md) transport ship [*American Legion*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_American_Legion).[^cite_note-Hegge-7]

Harald, his mother, and his sisters lived in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md), during the war,[^cite_note-9] while his father, Olav, and his grandfather, King Haakon, stayed in London with the Norwegian government-in-exile. One of the notable events he remembers from that time is standing behind [Franklin D. Roosevelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_D._Roosevelt) when he was [sworn in for his fourth term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_inauguration_of_Franklin_D._Roosevelt) on the South Portico of the [White House](33057.md) in 1945. Such childhood experiences were reflected in a trace of an American accent when he spoke English.[^cite_note-nyt1971-10]

Harald visited Norwegian servicemen training in the United States. He also made visits outside the United States, travelling north to visit Norwegian personnel at the training base [Little Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Norway) in Ontario, Canada.[^cite_note-11] He attended The White Hall Country School from 1943 with his sisters. Harald returned to Norway with his family after the war's end in 1945.[^cite_note-12]

### Return

In the autumn of 1945, Harald was enrolled at [Smestad School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smestad,_Oslo).[^cite_note-13] He was [confirmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confirmed) in May 1953 by bishop [Eivind Berggrav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eivind_Berggrav) at [Slottskapellet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slottskapellet_\(Oslo\)), the royal chapel.[^cite_note-Lange-Nielsen-4]<sup>: 108 </sup>

Harald's mother died in 1954,[^cite_note-14] and his maternal grandmother in 1958.[^cite_note-15]

## Crown Prince



Harald as a student in the Cavalry Officers' Candidate School, Trandum, 1956/1957

Harald studied at [Oslo katedralskole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo_katedralskole) from 1950 to 1955.[^cite_note-16] He attended [Befalsskolen for kavaleriet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Befalsskolen_for_kavaleriet?action=edit\&redlink=1) at [Trandum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trandum) from January 1956 to May 1957.[^cite_note-Frem_til_i_dag-17]<sup>: 34–35 </sup> He studied at the [Norwegian Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Military_Academy) from 1957 to 1959.[^cite_note-Frem_til_i_dag-17]<sup>: 35 </sup> On the death of his grandfather on 21 September 1957,[^cite_note-18] Harald became crown prince at the age of 20. He attended the [Council of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_State_\(Norway\)) for the first time on 27 September.[^cite_note-Frem_til_i_dag-17]<sup>: 35 </sup> Harald reached the [age of majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_majority) on 21 February 1958 when he became 21, and took the oath to the Constitution of Norway on the same day. In March, he served as regent in the King's absence for the first time.[^cite_note-Frem_til_i_dag-17]<sup>: 36 </sup>

In 1960, Harald entered [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford), where he studied history, economics, and politics.[^cite_note-nyt1971-10] He was a keen [rower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rower) during his student days and was taught by fellow student and friend [Nick Bevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Bevan), later a leading British school rowing coach. In 1960, he also made his first official journey abroad, visiting the United States for the 50th anniversary of the [American Scandinavian Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_American-Scandinavian_Foundation). An avid sailor,[^cite_note-19] Harald represented Norway in the [yachting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yachting) events of the [Summer Olympics](27566.md) in [Tokyo in 1964](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_at_the_1964_Summer_Olympics),[^cite_note-20] [Mexico City in 1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_at_the_1968_Summer_Olympics),[^cite_note-nyt1971-10] and [Munich in 1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_at_the_1972_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-HaralddeathCNN-21] He carried the Norwegian flag at the [1964 Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-22]

### Marriage

Main article: [Wedding of Harald, Crown Prince of Norway, and Sonja Haraldsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedding_of_Harald,_Crown_Prince_of_Norway,_and_Sonja_Haraldsen)



Harald with [Sonja Haraldsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Sonja_of_Norway), 1968

Harald married [Sonja Haraldsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonja_Haraldsen) at [Oslo Domkirke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo_Domkirke) in Oslo on 29 August 1968. The pair had dated for nine years, but Olav was reluctant to allow his son to marry a [commoner](24710172.md). He relented only when Harald told his father that if he was not allowed to marry Sonja he would not marry at all.[^cite_note-23] The couple had two children, [Princess Märtha Louise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_M%C3%A4rtha_Louise) (born 1971) and [Haakon](98258.md) (born 1973).[^cite_note-24]

## Reign

On the death of his father on 17 January 1991, Harald [succeeded to the Norwegian throne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Succession_to_the_Norwegian_throne). He became the first Norwegian-born monarch since [Olav IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olav_IV) died in 1387, a gap of 604 years. Harald was the sixth King of Norway to bear that name, and the first in 855 years. The five earlier kings were [Harald Fairhair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Fairhair), [Harald Greycloak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Greycloak), [Harald Bluetooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Bluetooth), [Harald Hardrada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Hardrada), and [Harald Gille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Gille). Harald Bluetooth is usually not given a number in the [list of Norwegian monarchs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Norwegian_monarchs), so Harald was numbered as *Harald V*. He chose to use his grandfather and father's royal motto, "[Alt for Norge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Norwegian_monarchs'_mottos)". He also chose to continue the tradition of royal [benediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benediction), introduced with his [father](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olav_V_of_Norway), and was consecrated with Queen Sonja in the [Nidaros Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nidaros_Cathedral) on 23 June 1991.[^cite_note-25]

Harald's reign was marked by modernisation and reform for the [Norwegian Royal family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Royal_family). The King cooperated closely with Queen Sonja and Haakon in making the [royal household](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_household) more open to the Norwegian public and the [Norwegian media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_media). Harald's decision to accept two more commoners into the royal family, [Mette-Marit Tjessem Høiby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mette-Marit_Tjessem_H%C3%B8iby) and [Ari Behn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Behn), was interpreted as a sign of modernisation and adjustment.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] Under Harald and Sonja's leadership, comprehensive renovation projects took place at the [Bygdøy Royal Estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bygd%C3%B8y_Royal_Estate), the [Royal Palace](1489319.md), [the royal stables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Queen_Sonja_Art_Stable), and [Oscarshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscarshall). The latter three were also opened to the public.[^cite_note-28] Together with Queen Sonja, the king for decades attempted to establish a [palace museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palace_museum) in [Oslo](22309.md).[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

### Constitutional role

Main article: [Monarchy of Norway](4154892.md)

Although the [Constitution of Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Norway) vested the King with executive authority, Harald exercised it only in accordance with parliamentary conventions established since 1884. His acts required the countersignature of a member of the [Council of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_State_\(Norway\)), usually the [prime minister](220458.md), and the Council's proceedings were signed by all ministers. Since 1905, no Norwegian monarch has refused assent to an act of the [Storting](172897.md).[^cite_note-kjell-31]

By convention, Harald appointed as prime minister the leader of the parliamentary bloc able to command a majority in the Storting. When the parliamentary situation was unclear, he relied on the advice of the President of the Storting and the sitting prime minister. Unlike many constitutional monarchs, he did not have the power to dissolve Parliament, as the Constitution does not allow for snap elections.[^cite_note-kjell-31]



Harald, [Laura Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Bush), [George W. Bush](3414021.md), and Queen Sonja, 2005

The King met the Council of State at the [Royal Palace](1489319.md) every Friday. He also had weekly meetings with the Prime Minister and the [Minister of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Norway\)). He received foreign envoys and formally opened parliament every October, delivering a [speech from the throne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech_from_the_throne) during each opening. Harald travelled extensively throughout Norway[^cite_note-HaralddeathCNN-21] and made official [state visits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_state_visits_made_and_received_by_Harald_V_of_Norway) to other countries,[^cite_note-32] as well as receiving and hosting guests.[^cite_note-33]

Until a constitutional amendment in 2012, the King of Norway was the formal head of the [Church of Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Norway).[^cite_note-34] Harald wished that the monarch still be required by the constitution to be of [Evangelical-Lutheran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelical-Lutheran) faith, which the parliament accepted.[^cite_note-35]

On 8 May 2018, the King's constitutional status as [holy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred) was dissolved, while leaving his [sovereign immunity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereign_immunity) intact.[^cite_note-36]

### 1990s and 2000s

In 1994, both the King and Haakon played roles during the opening ceremony of the [1994 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Winter_Olympics) in [Lillehammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lillehammer). Harald opened the games, while Haakon [lit the cauldron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_people_who_have_lit_the_Olympic_Cauldron).[^cite_note-37]

In 1997, Harald apologized to the [Sámi people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A1mi_people) for the government trying to erase their culture for over two centuries.[^cite_note-n196-38]

### 2010s



Estonian ambassador Janne Jõesaar-Ruusalu presents her credentials to the King in 2016.

In 2015, Harald became the world's first reigning monarch to visit [Antarctica](18959138.md), specifically the Norwegian dependency [Queen Maud Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Maud_Land).[^cite_note-39]

&#x20;In 2016, in a speech marking 25 years on the throne, Harald sought to unify Norwegians on religious tolerance, diversity, and LGBTQ rights:&#x20;

> Norwegians are enthusiastic young people — and wise old people. Norwegians are single, divorced, families with children and old married couples. Norwegians are girls who love girls, boys who love boys, and girls and boys who love each other. Norwegians believe in God, Allah, the Universe and nothing... In other words... Norway is you.

— Harald V

[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-n196-38]

When the King and Queen turned 80 years old in 2017, Harald decided to open the former royal stables to the public as a gift to his wife. The new venue was named [The Queen Sonja Art Stable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Queen_Sonja_Art_Stable) and is the first institution owned by the royal family which is permanently open to the public.[^cite_note-41] Harald was named [Name of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Name_of_the_Year) by the newspaper *[VG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verdens_Gang)* in 2017.[^cite_note-42]

### 2020s



Harald in October 2021 at the opening of [Sámi Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A1mi_Parliament) [session](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_session) of 2021–22

On 17 January 2021, Harald celebrated 30 years on the Norwegian throne.[^cite_note-43] On 11 September 2022, he visited Denmark to celebrate the [Golden Jubilee of Margrethe II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Jubilee_of_Margrethe_II).[^cite_note-44] On 15 September 2023, he attended the celebrations of the [Golden Jubilee of Carl XVI Gustaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Jubilee_of_Carl_XVI_Gustaf) in [Stockholm](26741.md), Sweden. Since the [death of Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_Elizabeth_II) in 2022, Harald had been Europe's oldest hereditary reigning monarch, at the age of 89.[^cite_note-45]

During periods when Harald was unable to perform his duties as sovereign, Haakon served as regent.[^cite_note-46] Despite his health issues, Harald never considered abdicating. Following the announcement of the impending [abdication of his Danish counterpart Margrethe II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdication_of_Margrethe_II), he told the press, "No, I don't really have \[a plan to abdicate]. I stand by what I have said all along. I have taken an oath to the Storting, and it lasts for life."[^cite_note-47]

#### Rebel Royals

In the 2025 [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix) documentary *[Rebel Royals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Royals:_An_Unlikely_Love_Story)* featuring his daughter Märtha Louise and son-in-law [Durek Verrett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durek_Verrett), Verrett accused Harald, Sonja, and Haakon of not "knowing what [racism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racism) was" and said the King and Queen hated everything he did and "would always let Märtha know" he was the wrong boyfriend for her.[^cite_note-nrk-netflix-48][^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50] Verrett said the first time Harald asked about his experiences of racism was when *[Oprah with Meghan and Harry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oprah_with_Meghan_and_Harry)* aired, and that "they didn't want a family discussion because they didn't want to treat me that way. They wanted to have a family discussion because they were afraid I might be the next one on Oprah."[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52] Norwegian media called the documentary and Verrett's statements a "character assassination of the king and queen".[^cite_note-53] Norwegian commentators said Verrett and Märtha Louise "are making fools of themselves".[^cite_note-54]

In a speech during the 2025 [Stortingsmiddag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stortingsmiddag) (dinner with members of the Storting) at the Royal Palace, Harald responded to the documentary by jokingly proposing a sequel titled "*Kongen minutt for minutt*" (The King minute by minute).[^cite_note-55][^cite_note-56]

#### Høiby and Epstein affairs

Harald refused to answer questions about the [relationship of Mette-Marit and Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relationship_of_Mette-Marit,_Crown_Princess_of_Norway,_and_Jeffrey_Epstein) and the criminal trial of [Marius Borg Høiby](1281851.md), Mette-Marit's son by an earlier relationship, for rape.[^cite_note-utenkommentar-57]

## Health and death

Main article: [Death and state funeral of Harald V](84083326.md)

Harald experienced several periods of ill health following the start of the twenty‑first century. He was diagnosed with [bladder cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bladder_cancer) in 2003, and in 2005, he underwent heart surgery. In 2020, he underwent another heart surgery to replace a [heart valve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_valve).[^cite_note-58]

He had a leg operation in January 2022.[^cite_note-59] Harald was hospitalised in August with a fever and again in December for an infection, and was admitted once more in May 2023, shortly before [Constitution Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_Day_\(Norway\)). Harald contracted [COVID-19](63030231.md) in March 2022 and again in October 2023.[^cite_note-Health-60] In January 2024, the royal palace announced that he was on sick leave due to a respiratory infection.[^cite_note-61]

While on holiday in Malaysia in February 2024, Harald was hospitalised on [Langkawi](422472.md) with a low heart rate and had a temporary [pacemaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacemaker) implanted.[^cite_note-62][^cite_note-63] He was discharged on 3 March and returned to Norway on a medical evacuation flight, after which he was placed on two weeks' sick leave.[^cite_note-64] He received a permanent pacemaker on 12 March[^cite_note-65] and was discharged two days later.[^cite_note-66]

In February 2026, he was hospitalised during a vacation in [Tenerife](269223.md) and treated for a skin infection on his leg.[^cite_note-67]



Commemoration of Harald at [The Blue Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blue_Stone) monument in [Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergen) on the day of his death

On 17 August 2026, Harald was admitted to [Oslo University Hospital, Rikshospitalet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo_University_Hospital,_Rikshospitalet) with fluid retention caused by [cortisone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cortisone) treatment for [hemolytic anemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemolytic_anemia) and was placed on two weeks' sick leave.[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69] On 24 August, the palace announced that his condition had worsened and that he was receiving antibiotics for a bloodstream infection.[^cite_note-70] On 27 August, the palace announced that his condition had become "extremely serious",[^cite_note-71] with family members visiting the hospital to be by his bedside.[^cite_note-72] Harald died at 06\:35 (UTC+2) on 28 August, aged 89.[^cite_note-73]

On the evening of 28 August, when Harald's coffin was taken from the National Hospital to the [Royal Palace](1489319.md), more than an estimated 10,000 people lined the streets of Oslo with flowers, [Norwegian flags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Norway), and cards.[^cite_note-74] His [state funeral](84083326.md) was held in Oslo on 9 September.[^cite_note-75]

## Public image

See also: [List of state visits made and received by Harald V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_state_visits_made_and_received_by_Harald_V)

Harald's leadership during Norwegian national crises, such as the [New Year's Day Storm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Year's_Day_Storm) (1992), the [July 2011 massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Norway_attacks), the [2020 Gjerdrum landslide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Gjerdrum_landslide), and particularly the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Norway) (January 2020 to March 2022), was met with both national and international acclaim.[^cite_note-76][^cite_note-77][^cite_note-78][^cite_note-79] He was popularly known among the Norwegian public as a "grandfather of the nation".[^cite_note-HaraldVdeath-2]

He was also known for being down-to-earth and his sense of [humour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humour), as well as his use of informal language with somewhat archaic pronunciations. He was an [outdoorsman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outdoorsman) who participated in skiing, hunting, and fishing.[^cite_note-r671-80] Harald described his life in Norway as "normal", attending movies, travelling without paparazzi surrounding him, and wearing casual attire while sailing, with citizens who were equally laid back about having a monarchy.[^cite_note-NPTO-81]

Harald was an environmentalist, acting as an initiator of the Norwegian branch of the [World Wide Fund for Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Wide_Fund_for_Nature) and held the title of president of the Norwegian chapter for 20 years.[^cite_note-HaraldVdeath-2] He went to visit the Amazon region in 2013.[^cite_note-o827-82]

## Sports career

Harald was engaged in [sailing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_\(sport\)), and participated in the Olympics: in 1964 and 1968 in the [5.5 Metre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5.5_Metre) class, and in 1972 in the [Soling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soling) class.[^cite_note-snl-harald-83] With his sailing crew he won World Championship medals: gold [^cite_note-86] in 1987 and bronze [^cite_note-87] in 1988, both in the [One Ton class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Ton_class), and silver in 1982.[^cite_note-worsail-88]

In July 2005, Harald and his crew aboard the royal sailboat *Fram XV* won the gold medal at the European Championships in Sweden. In the 2007 World Championship, the King came in sixth place.[^cite_note-89] In 2016, Harald competed with a team at the sailing World Championships on [Lake Ontario](17947.md), in [Toronto](64646.md), finishing second in the classic fleet category.[^cite_note-90] He was dubbed a "sailor king" by Canada's *[National Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Post)* as he slept on board his yacht *Sira*.[^cite_note-NPTO-81] In 2018 he placed third at the World Championships and in 2022 finished 10th in the 8 Metre World Championship.[^cite_note-worsail-88]

Harald was the first president of the [Norwegian Sailing Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Sailing_Federation) when it was founded in 1970 and became the President of Honour for [World Sailing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Sailing) in 1994 succeeding his father; both maintained the role until their deaths.[^cite_note-worsail-88] He also organized the inclusion of sailing under the purview of the [Norwegian Olympic and Paralympic Committee and Confederation of Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Olympic_and_Paralympic_Committee_and_Confederation_of_Sports), and participated in the organization of the [1994 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Winter_Olympics) in [Lillehammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lillehammer).[^cite_note-snl-harald-83]

### Foreign honours

In the [British Army](4887.md), Harald V was appointed an honorary [general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_\(United_Kingdom\)) in 1994,[^cite_note-93] and he was the final [Colonel-in-Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonel-in-Chief) of the [Green Howards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Howards).[^cite_note-94] He was also an honorary Colonel in the British [Royal Marines](93985.md).[^cite_note-95] He was patron of the [Anglo-Norse Society in London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Norse_Society_in_London), formerly together with Queen [Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II), his second cousin.[^cite_note-96] Harald was in the line of [succession to the British throne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Succession_to_the_British_throne), because of his descent from King [Edward VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_VII).[^cite_note-yahooline-5] He was a Stranger Knight Companion of the [Order of the Garter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Garter), an Honorary Knight Grand Cross of the [Royal Victorian Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Victorian_Order), and a Recipient of the [Royal Victorian Chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Victorian_Chain), as well as numerous other orders of chivalry.[^cite_note-97]
