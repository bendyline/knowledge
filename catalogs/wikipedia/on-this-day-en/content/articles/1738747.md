**Sargodha**[^cite_note-15] is the [sixth-largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Punjab,_Pakistan_by_population) in the [Pakistani](23235.md) province of [Punjab](24751.md). Located along the [Kirana Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirana_Hills_Site) in [central Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Punjab), it serves as the headquarters of its [eponymous district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_District) and [division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_Division). Sargodha is the [11th-most populous city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_populous_cities_in_Pakistan) in Pakistan, being one of the fastest-growing cities of the country.[^cite_note-citypopulation.de-16][^cite_note-17] It is also known as the *City of Eagles*.[^cite_note-visitpak_coe-1]

&#x20;It is one of the few planned cities of Pakistan (others include [Faisalabad](401880.md), [Islamabad](51153.md) and [Gwadar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwadar)).[^cite_note-ReferenceA-18]

## History

Sargodha was established by the British as a canal-colony in 1903 (originally spelled Sargoda) as a tehsil of Shahpur district.[^cite_note-19] Sargodha was badly affected by an outbreak of the [bubonic plague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubonic_plague) in 1903, and experienced a milder outbreak in 1904.[^cite_note-Cell_Cell_2002_p.-20] Although it was a small town in the beginning, the British [Royal Air Force](25679.md) built an airport there due to its strategic location.[^cite_note-sargodha.dc.lhc.gov.pk-21] The term "Sargodha" has its origin in the words "Sar" (from "sarowar") meaning "pond" and "Godha" meaning "[Sadhu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadhu)", which means "Pond of Godha".[^cite_note-22] The city was founded by Lady Trooper by the supervision of Sir [Charles Montgomery Rivaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Montgomery_Rivaz) KCSI (1845 – 7 October 1926), a colonial administrator in British India and Lieutenant-Governor of the Punjab from 1902 to 1907.[^cite_note-ReferenceA-18] Being the District Headquarters since 1940, Sargodha was given the status of Divisional Headquarters in 1960.[^cite_note-23]

Sargodha is situated in Pakistani province of [Punjab](24751.md). It is located 187 kilometres (116 mi) northwest of [Lahore](125315.md), in [Sargodha District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_District).[^cite_note-Distance_from_Lahore_to_Sargodha-25] It lies about 48 kilometres (30 miles) from the M-2 motorway, which connects [Lahore](125315.md) and [Islamabad](51153.md). It is connected to the M-2 by several interchanges at different locations. Sargodha is roughly 91 km (57 mi) from [Faisalabad](401880.md), due southeast.[^cite_note-toponavi_distance-27] Directly east connected by the M-2 motorway are Lahore and the route to [Rawalpindi](343524.md) and [Islamabad](51153.md). Due east is the city of [Jhang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhang); toward the west are the city of [Mianwali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mianwali) and the Chashma Barrage.

### Topography

Sargodha mainly comprises flat, fertile plains, although here are a few small hills along the Sargodha-Faisalabad Road. The River Jhelum flows on the western and northern sides, and the River [Chenab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chenab_River) on the eastern side of the city.[^cite_note-29] The city is located 190 metres above sea level.[^cite_note-30]

### Climate

The city has a climate of extreme heat in the summers and moderate cold in the winters. The maximum temperature reaches 50 °C (122 °F) in the summer while the minimum temperature recorded is as low as freezing point in the winter.

## Economy

The majority of Sargodha's economy is based on agriculture. Sargodha is considered the best citrus-producing area of Pakistan and therefore is also known as the *[California](5407.md)* *of Pakistan*.[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47] Sargodha is largest [kinnow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinnow)-producing district of the world. It produces oranges that are considered high-quality, and supplies them to the different parts of the country. These oranges are also exported to other countries.[^cite_note-48] Sargodha produces a large amount of wheat, cotton, rice and vegetables that are transported to other parts of country and exported to other countries.

There are also [textile mills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile_mills), rice processing plants and [Nestle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nestle) and [Shezan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shezan) juice factories.

The Sargodha Chamber of Commerce and Industry monitors industrial activity in the city and reports their findings to the [Federation of Pakistan Chambers of Commerce & Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Pakistan_Chambers_of_Commerce_&_Industry) and provincial government.[^cite_note-49] A [dry port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_port) is also under construction in Sargodha.[^cite_note-50]

## Administration

**Divisional and District Administration**

Sargodha is the capital of [Sargodha Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_Tehsil). As of October 2012, the division system in Punjab Province has been restored and Sargodha became the divisional headquarters of districts [Sargodha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_District), [Khushab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khushab_District), [Mianwali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mianwali_District) and [Bhakkar](4592127.md). Sargodha became the administrative capital of the [Sargodha Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_Division), handling over a population of about 8.1 million.[^cite_note-DISTRICT_WISE_CENSUS_RESULTS_CENSUS_2017-34]

**Local Government**

The Municipal Committee, Sargodha city was upgraded into Municipal Corporation in 1981 when the population of city was increased over half a million.[^cite_note-60] In December 2019, Sargodha Municipal Corporation was upgraded into [Metropolitan Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Corporation_\(Pakistan\)) under Punjab Local Government Act, 2019.[^cite_note-61] Metropolitan Corporation Sargodha is currently headed by the [Divisional commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Administrative_Service)[^cite_note-62] whereas the seat of mayor is vacant.

Sargodha Development Authority (SDA) was established by the government of Punjab[^cite_note-63] in 2017 which replaced the Sargodha Improvement Trust. SDA is responsible for new planned development in Sargodha. It also regulates and issues permits for new construction and housing projects, private houses, and commercial development.

Sargodha also has a Parks and Horticulture Authority ([PHA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parks_and_Horticulture_Authority)) which maintains all the parks and horticulture in the city. Moreover, Sargodha Water and Sanitation Agency ([WASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_and_Sanitation_Agency)) and Sargodha Waste Management Company (SWMC) are currently under planning phase.

### Safe City Authority Project

In 2017, Sargodha was announced as one of six cities in Punjab whose security would be improved by the [Punjab Safe Cities Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Safe_Cities_Authority). 5.5 billion Rupees were allocated for the project,[^cite_note-64] which was to be modeled along the lines of the Lahore Safe City project in which 8,000 [CCTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CCTV) cameras were installed throughout the city at a cost of 12 billion rupees to record and send images to Integrated Command and Control Centres.[^cite_note-65] In October 2024, the project was finally launched and in the project's first phase, the installation of cameras begin along the main roads of the city.[^cite_note-66]

## Education



Main Gate to University of Sargodha



PAF Public School

According to the census of 1998, the literacy rate of Sargodha was 67.8%, while in 2017 the literacy rate was recorded as 80.5%.[^cite_note-68] The city is considered the educational hub of Sargodha Division, handling a population of about 8.1 million.[^cite_note-DISTRICT_WISE_CENSUS_RESULTS_CENSUS_2017-34] [Sargodha Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_Division) has its own Education Board for Secondary School Certificate (SSC) and Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSSC) Examinations. There are about four universities and numerous colleges of Medical Sciences, Law, IT, Commerce, Engineering and Intermediate Courses. Quaid-e- Azam Law College is famous for law education.

### Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education

The Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Sargodha[^cite_note-69] handles the whole Sargodha Division. It was established in 1968 under the West Pakistan Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education ([Multan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Intermediate_and_Secondary_Education,_Multan) and [Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Sargodha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Intermediate_and_Secondary_Education,_Sargodha)) Ordinance No. VII of 1968, with the jurisdiction of Sargodha and Rawalpindi Civil Divisions.

## Transportation

### Road

#### Roads and flyovers

The 4 km (2.5 mi) long [University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sargodha) Road runs from 47 Pull to Khayyam Chowk. Other main roads include 6 km (3.7 mi) long [Fatima Jinnah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatima_Jinnah) Road & City Road, Canal Road, Katchery Road, [Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_Cricket_Stadium) Road, Club Road, Jail Road, Queens Road, [Mushaf Ali Mir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mushaf_Ali_Mir) Road, [Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_Junction_railway_station) Road, [Lahore](125315.md) Road, [Faisalabad](401880.md) Road, Eid Gah Road, [Mianwali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mianwali) Road, [Sillanwali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sillanwali) Road, [Bhalwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhalwal) Road, and Shaheenabad Road.

There are 3 flyovers located in the city area to regulate the flow of traffic:&#x20;

- Khayyam Chowk's flyover has a length of 1 km (0.62 mi) that leads to [Mianwali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mianwali) Road.

- [Fatima Jinnah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatima_Jinnah) Road's flyover also has a length of 1 km (0.62 mi).

- A 0.4 km (0.25 mi) long flyover is located on Kachehry Road.

#### Motorways and highways



Motorway M2 exit to Sargodha

Sargodha lies about 45 km (28 mi) from the [M-2 Motorway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-2_motorway_\(Pakistan\)), which connects Lahore and [Islamabad](51153.md). The [N-60 National Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-60_National_Highway) runs from Sargodha to Lahore through [Pindi Bhattian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pindi_Bhattian) and [Sheikhupura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikhupura). It is also connected to Faisalabad by a highway.[^cite_note-87]

### Buses

General Bus Stand is located on Fatima Jinnah Road that provides bus service from Sargodha to almost every part of the country. [Daewoo Bus Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daewoo_Express) drives regular routes from Sargodha to the rest of the country.[^cite_note-88] Others include Niazi Express, Faisal Movers, Bilal Travels, Islamabad Express and Skyways etc.

### Taxi and rickshaws

The online cab service [inDrive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/InDrive) and some locally owned cab services such as Apni-Sawari are available in the city. Many [auto rickshaws](593765.md) are also available throughout the city, which are often used by those who cannot afford the cab services.

### Rail



[Sargodha Junction railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_Junction_railway_station)

Sargodha is also connected by the rest of the country through the rail.[^cite_note-89] Sargodha Junction railway station is located on Shorkot-Lala Musa branch railway line. [Hazara Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazara_Express), [Millat Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millat_Express), Sandal Express and Sargodha Express provide daily transport to Rawalpindi, Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan, Karachi and other major cities.

## Air base

Pakistan's largest [airbase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbase), [PAF Base Mushaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PAF_Base_Mushaf) (formerly PAF Base Sargodha), is situated in Sargodha and hosts the headquarters of the [Pakistan Air Force](377433.md)'s Central Air Command. The airbase is also home to the [Combat Commanders School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_Commanders_School) (CCS), formerly the Fighter Leader's School.[^cite_note-90]

#### Kirana Hills

The [Kirana Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirana_Hills) are a small and extensive rocky [mountain range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_range) located in Sargodha.[^cite_note-91][^cite_note-92][^cite_note-93][^cite_note-Wikimaps-94] They are a commonly-visited tourist attraction in Sargodha City. Locally known as the *Black Mountains*[^cite_note-Bahria_University_press-95] due to their [brownish landscape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bistre), their highest peak is about 980 feet (300 m).[^cite_note-96]

#### Jinnah Hall

Jinnah Hall is a historical landmark in Sargodha.[^cite_note-97] Jinnah Hall was built in 1949. It was named for [Muhammad Ali Jinnah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Ali_Jinnah), the founder of Pakistan. Jinnah Hall was originally built as a town hall and library in Company Bagh, Sargodha; now it is currently used as an exhibition hall, event space, and library.

## Sports



[Sargodha Astroturf Hockey Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_Hockey_Stadium)

Sargodha is home to the [Sargodha Cricket Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_Cricket_Stadium).[^cite_note-98] Sargodha's cricket team was a first-class cricket team that represented Sargodha Division. They competed in Pakistan's first-class tournaments in 1961–62 and 2002–03. There's a sports complex adjacent to the stadium that includes gym as well as basketball, badminton and table tennis courts. Moreover, karate classes also take place regularly at the complex.

[Mela Mandi Ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mela_Mandi_Ground) is a multi-use historic stadium in Sargodha. It is mostly used for staging [cricket](25675557.md) and [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_\(soccer\)) matches.[^cite_note-99] The Mela Mandi Ground was constructed to provide a vast ground for outdoor sports and activities. During March, every year (the country's spring season), several competitions are held here, attracting considerable spectator numbers.[^cite_note-100]

Sargodha is also home to [Sargodha Astroturf Hockey Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargodha_Hockey_Stadium), located near Mela Mandi Ground. It is home to the Sargodha District Hockey Team, and provides a reliable platform for the city's youth.[^cite_note-101] Galaxy Sports Complex is a renowned Sports Academy of the city.[^cite_note-102]

A [flood-lit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floodlight) football ground is under construction in Company Bagh, Sargodha.

### Traditional food and drink

The cuisine of Sargodha is similar to that of other regions of South Asia, with some of it originating from the royal kitchens of 16th-century Mughal emperors.[^cite_note-Goodwin2002-103] Most of those dishes have their roots in [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_cuisine), [Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_cuisine), [Central Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Asian_cuisine) and [Middle Eastern cuisine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Eastern_cuisine).[^cite_note-LeeNadeau2011-104] Pakistani cooking uses large quantities of spices, herbs, and seasoning. Garlic, [ginger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ginger), [turmeric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turmeric), red [chili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chili_powder), and [garam masala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garam_masala) are used in most dishes, and home cooking regularly includes curry. [*Roti*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pita_bread), a thin flatbread made from wheat, is a staple food, usually served with curry, meat, vegetables, and lentils. Rice is also common; it is served plain, fried with spices, and in sweet dishes.[^cite_note-yasmeen-105][^cite_note-106][^cite_note-107]

[Lassi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lassi) is a traditional drink in [Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_region), including Sargodha. [Black tea with milk and sugar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_tea_culture) is also popular throughout the city and is consumed daily by most of the population.[^cite_note-taxila-108][^cite_note-109]

### Fast food

Fast food is also very popular across the city. Along with local fast food restaurants, there are also several international fast food outlets in the city, including [KFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KFC),[^cite_note-110] [McDonald's](2480627.md), [Subway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subway_\(restaurant\)) and [Domino's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domino's).[^cite_note-111] The world's biggest [Gloria Jean's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Jean's_Coffees) store is also located in Sargodha.

## Awards

In 1966, the Government of Pakistan awarded a special flag, the [Hilal-e-Istaqlal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilal-e-Istaqlal) to Sargodha (also to [Lahore](125315.md) and [Sialkot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sialkot)) for showing severe resistance to the enemy during the [Indo-Pakistani War of 1965](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Pakistani_War_of_1965) as these cities were targets of the Indian advance.[^cite_note-112] Every year on [Defence Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Day) (6 September), this flag is hoisted in these cities in recognition of the will, courage and perseverance of their people.[^cite_note-113]
