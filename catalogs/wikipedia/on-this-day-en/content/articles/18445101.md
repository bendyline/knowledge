**Aramoun** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): عرمون) is a [village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village) in the [Aley District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aley_District) of [Mount Lebanon](3028437.md), Lebanon, lying to the east of [Khalde](17284436.md) and 22 kilometres away from [Beirut](37428.md). Aramoun's altitude ranges between 250 meters to 600 meters further east. The village has a [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze) and [Christian](18337522.md) community, major families are Yehia, Jawhari, Halabi, Mohtar, Abu Ghanam and Dakdouk.[^cite_note-BDS-2]

## Nature

The village is primarily known for its [olives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olives) and [olive oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_oil) as it enjoys a mountain [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate). It is also fairly [forested](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forested).

## Education

Aramoun contains 18 schools, of which two are public serving 699 students, and 16 are private serving 2,372 students.
