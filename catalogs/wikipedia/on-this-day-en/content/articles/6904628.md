**Omealca** is a town and its surrounding [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Veracruz) in the [Mexican state](8196151.md) of [Veracruz](63125.md).[^cite_note-1]

In the [2020 INEGI census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_INEGI_census), the municipality reported a total population of 23,773.[^cite_note-2]

## Etymology

Omealca means place with two rivers in [Nahuatl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahuatl),[^cite_note-3] due to it being next to Blanco river and above a subterranean one.&#x20;

## Geography

### Climate

Omealca has a very diverse range of climates, as it is next to the state of [Puebla](413436.md) and [Oaxaca](156896.md), meaning that besides having [Veracruz](63125.md)'s tropical climate, it also has Puebla's mountainous climate and Oaxaca's [arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arid_climate).
