**Lâm Đồng** is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Vietnam) of [Vietnam](202354.md) located in the south of the [Central Highlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Highlands_\(Vietnam\)). It borders [Khánh Hòa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kh%C3%A1nh_H%C3%B2a_Province) to the east, [Đồng Nai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%90%E1%BB%93ng_Nai) and [Hồ Chí Minh City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%E1%BB%93_Ch%C3%AD_Minh_City) to the southwest, the [South China Sea](74209.md) to the southeast, [Đắk Lắk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%90%E1%BA%AFk_L%E1%BA%AFk) to the north, and [Cambodia](334751.md) to the northwest.

The three highest plateaus of the Central Highlands: [Lâm Viên](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A2m_Vi%C3%AAn), [Di Linh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Di_Linh), and [Bảo Lộc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%E1%BA%A3o_L%E1%BB%99c) (formerly known as B'Lao), are located in Lâm Đồng province at an elevation of 1,500 meters above sea level.[^cite_note-\:0-5] The provincial capital, [Đà Lạt City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Da_Lat), is situated 300 km northeast of [Ho Chi Minh City](66956.md), 658 km south of [Đà Nẵng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%90%C3%A0_N%E1%BA%B5ng), and 1,414 km from the capital, Hanoi, via National Route 1.[^cite_note-\:0-5] In 2010, Lâm Đồng became the first province in the Central Highlands to have two provincial-level cities: [Đà Lạt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%90%C3%A0_L%E1%BA%A1t) and [Bảo Lộc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%E1%BA%A3o_L%E1%BB%99c).[^cite_note-6]

The economy is based largely on agriculture, with tea, coffee and vegetables being the main agricultural products. Lâm Đồng attracted 8.65 million tourists in 2023.[^cite_note-7]
