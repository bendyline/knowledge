**Tula Oblast** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Ту́льская о́бласть, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Russian): *Tulʹskaya oblastʹ*) is a [federal subject](701438.md) (an [oblast](646268.md)) of [Russia](25391.md). It is geographically located in [European Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Russia) and is administratively part of the [Central Federal District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Federal_District), covering an area of 25,700 square kilometers (9,900 sq mi). It has a population of 1,553,925 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census)).[^cite_note-2010Census-4] [Tula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tula,_Russia) is the largest [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) and the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of the oblast.

Tula Oblast borders [Moscow Oblast](372315.md) in the north, [Ryazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryazan_Oblast) in the east, [Lipetsk](480918.md) in the southeast, [Oryol](480945.md) in the southwest, and [Kaluga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaluga_Oblast) in the west. Tula Oblast is one of the most developed and urbanized territories in Russia, and the majority of the territory forms the Tula-[Novomoskovsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novomoskovsk,_Russia) Agglomeration, an [urban area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_area) with a population of over 1 million.

## History

See also: [Upper Oka Principalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Oka_Principalities)

The Tula Oblast area has been inhabited since the [Stone Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_Age), as shown by the discoveries of burial mounds ([kurgans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurgan)) and old settlements.[^cite_note-5] By the eighth century, these lands were occupied by the [Vyatichi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyatichi), an [East Slavic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Slavic_people) tribe who cultivated the land, traded, and worked at crafts, confirmed by records in property registers which mention an "ancient settlement" located at the [confluence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confluence) of the [Upa River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upa_River) and Tulitsa River. The first mention of the city of [Tula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tula,_Russia) in 1146 is found in the [Nikon Chronicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikon_Chronicle), in reference to the campaign of Prince [Svyatoslav Olgovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svyatoslav_Olgovich) of [Chernigov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernigov). At the time the lands belonged to the [Ryazan Principality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryazan_Principality), and Prince Sviatoslav passed through a number of settlements, including Tula, while heading for [Ryazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryazan).[^cite_note-TulaRegion-6]

## Geography

Tula Oblast is located in Russia's [Central Federal District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Federal_District) and borders [Moscow](372315.md), [Ryazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryazan_Oblast), [Lipetsk](480918.md), [Oryol](480945.md), and [Kaluga Oblasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaluga_Oblast).

### Rivers

Tula Oblast contains more than 1,600 rivers and streams. Major rivers include:

- [Don River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_River_\(Russia\))

- [Oka River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oka_River)

- [Upa River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upa_River)

### Natural resources

The oblast is rich in [iron ore](145352.md), [clay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay), [limestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone), and deposits of [lignite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lignite) (coal).[^cite_note-7] The lignite deposit is part of the [Moscow coal basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_coal_basin?action=edit\&redlink=1).

### Climate

Tula Oblast has a moderate [continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_climate), with warm summers and cold winters. Average January temperature is −10 °C (14 °F) in the north and −9 °C (16 °F) in the south. Average July temperature is about +19 °C (66 °F) to +20 °C (68 °F). Annual precipitation is 470 millimetres (19 in) in the southeast and 575 millimetres (22.6 in) in the northwest.

## Politics



Oblast administration building

During the [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) period, the high authority in the oblast was shared between three persons: The first secretary of the Tula CPSU Committee (who in reality had the biggest authority), the chairman of the oblast Soviet (legislative power), and the Chairman of the oblast Executive Committee (executive power). Since 1991, CPSU lost all the power, and the head of the Oblast administration, and eventually the governor was appointed/elected alongside elected [regional parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_parliaments_of_Russia).

The Charter of Tula Oblast is the fundamental law of the region. The Tula Oblast Duma is the province's standing legislative (representative) body. The Oblast Duma exercises its authority by passing laws, resolutions, and other legal acts and by supervising the implementation and observance of the laws and other legal acts passed by it. The highest executive body is the Oblast Government, which includes territorial executive bodies such as district administrations, committees, and commissions that facilitate development and run the day to day matters of the province. The Oblast administration supports the activities of the Governor who is the highest official and acts as guarantor of the observance of the oblast Charter in accordance with the [Constitution of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Russia).

### Local government

Representative bodies of urban and rural settlements and urban districts consist of deputies elected in municipal elections. The representative body of the municipal district consists of the heads of settlements that are part of the municipal district, and of deputies of the representative bodies of these settlements, elected by the representative bodies of the settlements from among their members in accordance with the same norm of representation, regardless of the population of the settlement. The term of office of representative bodies of cities, rural settlements, city districts, and municipal districts is 5 years, with some exceptions when the term is 3 years.[^cite_note-8] Local government bodies in the Tula Oblast are headed by 103 heads of municipalities and 84 heads of municipal administrations.[^cite_note-Report-9]

In accordance with the regional law of 2017, village elders carry out activities to organize interaction between local government bodies and residents of rural settlements when resolving issues of local importance.[^cite_note-10] As of 1 November 2022, 1,071 village elders operate in 23 municipal districts and urban districts. Their powers extend to the territory of more than 1,700 settlements, home to about 160,000 inhabitants.[^cite_note-Report-9]

In the Tula Oblast, regional branches of dozens of political parties are registered, the largest in terms of membership being [United Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Russia), the [Communist Party of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_the_Russian_Federation) (CPRF), the [Liberal Democratic Party of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Democratic_Party_of_Russia) (LDPR), [Patriots of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriots_of_Russia), the [Party of Growth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_of_Growth), [A Just Russia – For Truth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Just_Russia_%E2%80%93_For_Truth), [Yabloko](429579.md), and the [Party of Pensioners of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_of_Pensioners_of_Russia).[^cite_note-11]

Since 2011, the "People's Budget" project has been implemented in the Tula Oblast, aimed at identifying and solving socially significant problems in the territories of the region’s municipalities. Project activities are financed from the budget of the Tula Oblast, the budgets of municipalities, and personal funds of residents and sponsors. The target orientation of the "People's Budget" project is to enhance the participation of residents of the Tula Oblast in solving local problems, creating comfortable living conditions, and supporting the initiatives of residents.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

In 2022, an annual survey of the population was conducted in the Tula Oblast on the effectiveness of the activities of heads of local government bodies and enterprises operating at the regional and municipal levels, in which 20,141 people participated. Efficiency assessment was carried out according to the following criteria: population satisfaction with the organization of transport services and the quality of roads, housing, and communal services. The overall percentage of satisfaction in municipal areas and urban districts was 88.13% (2020 – 81.01%, 2021 – 83.14%). The highest overall satisfaction rate in the municipalities is [Kamensky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamensky_District,_Tula_Oblast) – 99.75, [Tyoplo-Ogaryovsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyoplo-Ogaryovsky_District) – 99.65%, [Chernsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernsky_District) – 99.12%, [Donskoy city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donskoy,_Tula_Oblast) – 98.86%. Low satisfaction rates were noted in the municipalities of [Kireyevsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kireyevsky_District) – 73.15%, [Zaoksky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaoksky_District) – 76.34%, [Shchyokinsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shchyokinsky_District) – 77.19%, [Tula Urban Okrug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tula_Urban_Okrug) – 77.29%, [Aleksin city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksin) – 77.63%.[^cite_note-14]

## Economy

Tula Oblast is part of the [Central economic region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_economic_region). It is a prominent industrial center with [metalworking](266443.md), [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering), [coalmining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalmining), and chemical industries.[^cite_note-24] Major industrial cities include [Novomoskovsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novomoskovsk,_Russia) and [Aleksin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksin). Historical industries, such as [firearm](11966.md), [samovar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samovar), and [accordion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accordion) manufacturing, still play an important role in the region.

The oblast also has a developed agricultural sector, which ranks 33rd in Russia in agricultural production.[^cite_note-TulaRegion-6] The sector includes farming [grain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grain) ([wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat) and [rye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rye)), [potatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potato), [sugar beets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_beet), and [vegetable growing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetable_growing), [livestock](25160767.md) raising, and [dairying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairying).

## Culture



Leo Tolstoy's estate in Yasnaya Polyana

Tula Oblast has more than 100 museums. Several are located in the administrative center of the oblast, the city of [Tula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tula,_Russia), notably the *Tula State Arms Museum*, the *Tula Kremlin*, and the *Tula Samovar Museum*. Another important cultural tourist attractions is the home and country estate of [Leo Tolstoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Tolstoy), [Yasnaya Polyana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasnaya_Polyana), located 12 kilometres (7.5 miles) outside of the city of Tula.

The oblast also has four professional theaters, a [philharmonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philharmonic) orchestra, and a [circus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circus).

The cuisine of Tula Oblast use the same ingredients as [Russian cuisine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_cuisine). [Tula pryanik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tula_pryanik) is the most famous dish from the oblast.
