**Marina Ferrari** (born 10 October 1973) is a French politician of the [Democratic Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Movement_\(France\)) (MoDem) who has served as the [Minister of Sports, Youth and Community Life](3374119.md) in the [Lecornu government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Lecornu_government) since 2025. She has served as a [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament_\(France\)) for [Savoie's 1st constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savoie's_1st_constituency) from [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_legislative_election) to 2024 and in 2025.[^cite_note-1]

Ferrari served as the Secretary of State for Digital Affairs in the [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attal_government) of [Prime Minister](218751.md) [Gabriel Attal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Attal), and then Delegated Minister for Tourism Economy in the [government of Michel Barnier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnier_government) in 2024.

## Early life

Ferrari was born in [Aix-les-Bains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aix-les-Bains) to a French mother and Italian father. She is also of Austrian descent.[^cite_note-2]

## Political career

In parliament, Ferrari served on the [Finance Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance_Committee_\(French_National_Assembly\)) from 2022 to 2023.[^cite_note-3] In addition to her committee assignment, she was part of the French-Italian Parliamentary Friendship Group.[^cite_note-4]

Marina Ferrari was appointed Minister of Sports, Youth and Associative Life on 5 October 2025 in the [first Lecornu government](81029757.md). However, her tenure—along with that of the entire Lecornu cabinet—ended very shortly thereafter when the government resigned on 6 October 2025, making her effectively a minister in title only.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] She was reappointed for Minister of Sports in the [second Lecornu government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Lecornu_government).

The French government called for emergency talks among European football authorities amid the [FIFA Forward Enterprise](83820435.md) proposal, with Ferrari warning that the plan represented a major shift in football governance and stating that "football is not for sale".[^cite_note-7]
