The **Ministry of Defence** ([Swedish](26689.md): *Försvarsdepartementet*) is a [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) in the [Government of Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Sweden) responsible for policies related to [national defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_\(military\)) and [civil defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_defence).

The ministry is currently headed by the **Minister for Defence**, [Pål Jonson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A5l_Jonson) of the [Moderate Party](202985.md).[^cite_note-4]

## History

The ministry was established on 1 July 1920[^cite_note-5] when the [Ministry of Land Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Land_Defence) and the [Ministry for Naval Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_for_Naval_Affairs_\(Sweden\)) was merged into one ministry.

It's located at [Jakobsgatan 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakobsgatan) at [Gustaf Adolfs torg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Adolfs_torg,_Stockholm) in [Stockholm](26741.md), where it has been located since 1966.

## Government agencies

Source:[^cite_note-Agencies,_companies_and_funds_of_the_Ministry_of_Defence-6]

The Ministry of Defence is principal for 12 government agencies, one partly state-owned company (SOS Alarm) and one fund.

- [Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Armed_Forces) (*Försvarsmakten*)

- [Defence Materiel Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Defence_Materiel_Administration) (*Försvarets materielverk*)

- [Defence Conscription and Assessment Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Defence_Conscription_and_Assessment_Agency) (*Totalförsvarets plikt- och prövningsverk*)

- [National Defence Radio Establishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Defence_Radio_Establishment) (*Försvarets radioanstalt*)

- [Defence Research Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Defence_Research_Agency) (*Totalförsvarets forskningsinstitut*)

- [Foreign Intelligence Inspectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Foreign_Intelligence_Inspectorate?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Statens inspektion för försvarsunderrättelseverksamheten*)

- [Foreign Intelligence Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Intelligence_Court_\(Sweden\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Försvarsunderrättelsedomstolen*)

- [Agency for Defence Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Agency_for_Defence_Analysis?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Myndigheten för totalförsvarsanalys*)

- [Coast Guard](7640363.md) (*Kustbevakningen*)

- [Psychological Defence Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Psychological_Defence_Agency?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Myndigheten för psykologiskt försvar*)

- [Civil Defence and Resilience Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Civil_Defence_and_Resilience_Agency) (*Myndigheten för civil försvar*)

- [Accident Investigation Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Accident_Investigation_Authority) (*Statens haverikommission*)
