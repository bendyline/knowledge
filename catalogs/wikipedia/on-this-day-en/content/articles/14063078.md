**Rabak** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ربك) is a city in south-eastern [Sudan](27421.md) and the capital of the Sudanese state of [White Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Nile_\(state\)). Numerous factories are based in the industrial-oriented city, such as the [Kenana Sugar Company](83012015.md).

## Geography

The city is located on the eastern bank of the [White Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Nile), facing [Kosti](6671707.md) on the western bank. It lies some 362 meters above sea level. Rabak is approximately 260 kilometres (160 mi) south of [Khartoum](17320.md) and 340 kilometres (210 mi) west of the [Ethiopian](187749.md) border. It is linked to the north of Sudan via the Khartoum–Rabak road; and it is linked by road eastward to [Sennar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sennar) and westward to [Al-Ubayyid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ubayyid).

### Climate

Rabak has a [hot arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_climate#Hot_desert_climates) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BWh*), despite receiving over 350 millimetres or 14 inches of rainfall annually, owing to the extremely high potential [evapotranspiration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evapotranspiration).

## History

### Prehistory

The Rabak locality contains a [Neolithic settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic#Northeastern_Africa) on a riverbank terrace approximately 3 km (1.9 mi) east of the present White Nile river. The site has cultural deposits 60–80 cm (24–31 in) in depth, including fish bones and other food waste. The radiocarbon dates sur veyed hints to possible human settlement of the area around c. 6000 and 4500 [BP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Before_Present), with the earliest levels contemporary with [Khartoum](17320.md) Neolithic sites.[^cite_note-4]

### 20th century

In 1910, the construction of a one-way bridge near the city, likely on the Nile, began. It was mainly a railway line, with only one lane for vehicles.

By the 1980s, a newer bridge was started, being built to connect the town to the rest of the country, with the bridge carrying more vehicles and pedestrians. In a survey conducted in the city from 2007 to 2010, 3.5% of the respondents said that the implementation of the new bridge likely contributed in its rapid urban growth.[^cite_note-ya-5]

Rabak was made the capital of the White Nile State[^cite_note-6] in 1994.[^cite_note-7]

### 21st century

In June 2026, during the [Sudanese civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_civil_war_\(2023-present\)), RSF launched a drone strike on Rabak, killing 2 civilians, injuring 7, and damaging a fuel station.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

**Rabak Airport** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **KST**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **HSKI**) was an airport formerly serving the cities of [Kosti](6671707.md) and [Rabak](14063078.md) in the [White Nile State](767622.md) of [Sudan](27421.md). The dirt runways have been overbuilt with housing.[^cite_note-10]

## Economy

Rabak is one of major commercial cities in Sudan owing to its unique location in the country and its transportation links to the other major Sudanese states. In the city, there is a [cement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cement_in_Africa) processing company named [Nile Cement Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nile_Cement_Company?action=edit\&redlink=1), which produced 50,200 tons in 2001 and 41,000 tons in 2002. There is a calcite mine nearby the locality, as well as industrial facilities including the Kenana Sugar Factory, Asalaya Sugar Factory, oil refineries, and other manufacturing plants.

The city is built on a major highway where the settlements of [Sennar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sennar) and [Kosti](6671707.md) are also connected to.[^cite_note-ya-5]
