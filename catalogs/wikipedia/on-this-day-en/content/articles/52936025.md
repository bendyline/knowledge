**Godwin Elliot Loraine Friday**[^cite_note-3] (born 28 September[^cite_note-Birthday-4] 1959) is a [Vincentian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Vincent_\(Saint_Vincent_and_the_Grenadines\)) politician who has served as [Prime Minister of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Saint_Vincent_and_the_Grenadines) since 2025 and as leader of the [New Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Democratic_Party_\(Saint_Vincent\)) (NDP) since 2016. He has been the [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament) (MP) for the Northern Grenadines constituency since 2001 and served as the [leader of the opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(Saint_Vincent_and_the_Grenadines\)) from 2016 to 2025.

In the [2001 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Vincentian_general_election), Friday was elected to the [House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Assembly_of_Saint_Vincent_and_the_Grenadines) for the first time. He retained his seat in the [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Vincentian_general_election), [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Vincentian_general_election), [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Vincentian_general_election), [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Vincentian_general_election), and 2025 elections.[^cite_note-5] In the [2025 general election](76606789.md), Friday's NDP defeated [Ralph Gonsalves](393744.md)' long-governing [Unity Labour Party](1836153.md), forming a supermajority government and becoming prime minister.

## Early life and education

He was born on 28 September[^cite_note-Birthday-4] 1959 in [Bequia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bequia), the second-largest island in Saint Vincent and the [Grenadines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenadines) and the largest island of the Grenadines. Friday earned a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in history and political science from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), and a [Bachelor of Laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Laws) from [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) in [Ontario](22218.md), Canada.[^cite_note-\:0-6] He graduated with a [master's degree](170346.md) in history from the University of Waterloo in 1985.[^cite_note-\:1-7] In 1989, he graduated with a [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in [political studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_studies) from Queen's University.[^cite_note-\:2-8]

## Political career

In 2016, [Arnhim Eustace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnhim_Eustace), the party leader and opposition leader of [Saint Vincent and the Grenadines](27228.md), resigned, which left Friday with the position as party leader and [leader of the opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(Saint_Vincent_and_the_Grenadines\)).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-\:0-6][^cite_note-10] He led the party into the [2020 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Vincentian_general_election), winning the popular vote but losing 1 seat. At the [2025 general election](76606789.md), the NDP won 14 of 15 seats in the [House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Assembly_of_Saint_Vincent_and_the_Grenadines), forming government for the first time since 1998.[^cite_note-11] Friday took additional portfolios of [Minister of Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Finance_of_Saint_Vincent_and_the_Grenadines) and [Minister of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Legal_Affairs_\(Saint_Vincent_and_the_Grenadines\)).[^cite_note-12]
