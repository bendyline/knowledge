**Haibat Hamad Abbas al-Halbousi al-Dulaimi** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): هيبت حمد عباس الحلبوسي الدليمي; born January 13, 1980) is an Iraqi politician serving as the [Speaker of the Council of Representatives of Iraq](22405991.md) since December 2025. A member of the [Progress Party](68979365.md), he served as Chairman of the Oil and Energy Committee in the [Council of Representatives](1445450.md) from 2019 to 2025.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]
