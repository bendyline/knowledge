**Eastern Province** ( in [Kinyarwanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinyarwanda_language): *Intara y'Iburasirazuba*; [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Province de l'Est*; [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *Oostelijke Provincie*) is the largest, the most populous and the least densely populated of [Rwanda](25645.md)'s five [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Rwanda). It was created in early January 2006 as part of a government decentralization program that re-organized the country's local government structures.

It has seven districts: [Bugesera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bugesera_District), [Gatsibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gatsibo), [Kayonza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayonza_District), [Ngoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngoma_District), [Kirehe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirehe), [Nyagatare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyagatare_District) and [Rwamagana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rwamagana_District). The capital city of the Eastern Province is [Rwamagana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rwamagana).

The Eastern Province comprises the former provinces of [Kibungo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kibungo_Province) and [Umutara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umutara), most of [Kigali Rural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kigali_Rural), and part of [Byumba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byumba_Province).

The [Akagera National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akagera_National_Park) is situated is this province.

## History

It is not known when the territory of present day Rwanda was first inhabited, but it is thought that humans moved into the area following the [last ice age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_glacial_period) either in the [Neolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic) period, around ten thousand years ago, or in the [long humid period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_humid_period) which followed, up to around 3000 BC.[^cite_note-Bradt2006p6-4][^cite_note-5] Archaeological excavations have revealed evidence of sparse settlement by [hunter gatherers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_gatherer) in the late [Stone Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_Age), followed by a larger population of early [Iron Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Age) settlers, who produced dimpled [pottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pottery) and iron tools.[^cite_note-Bradt2006p6-4][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] By the 17th century it is thought that most of Rwanda was inhabited, with a fairly even spread of population across the hills of the country.[^cite_note-8]
