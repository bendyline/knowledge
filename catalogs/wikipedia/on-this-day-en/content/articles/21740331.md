The **deputy chief minister of Maharashtra** is the deputy head of the executive branch of the [Government of Maharashtra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Maharashtra) and the second highest ranking minister of the Council of Ministers.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]The position of deputy chief minister is not explicitly defined or mentioned in the [Constitution of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India). However, the [Supreme Court of India](395852.md) has stated that the appointment of deputy chief ministers is not unconstitutional. The court has clarified that a deputy chief minister, for all practical purposes, remains a minister in the council of ministers headed by the chief minister and does not draw a higher salary or perks compared to other ministers.[^cite_note-5]During the absence of the chief minister, the deputy-chief minister may chair cabinet meetings and lead the assembly majority. Various deputy chief ministers have also taken the oath of secrecy in line with the one that chief minister takes. This oath has also sparked controversies.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
