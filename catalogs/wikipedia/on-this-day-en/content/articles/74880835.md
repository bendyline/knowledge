The **DIO Arash**, **Arash 2** is an Iranian long-range [one-way attack drone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-way_attack_drone) in Islamic Republic of Iran military service from 2020.&#x20;

&#x20;\
&#x20;It has a 2,000 km strike range and carries a 150 kg warhead.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## Operator



Iranian drone exercise in 2022 - Day 1 (39)

This drone is operated by the [Islamic Republic of Iran Army](48259216.md) Ground Forces and is an upgraded version of the [Kian 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kian_\(UAV\)) drone, featuring a piston engine instead of a jet engine.[^cite_note-3]

Arash-2 drones may be, or may have been, supplied to Russia.[^cite_note-4]
