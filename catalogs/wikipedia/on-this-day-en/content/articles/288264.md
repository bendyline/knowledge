**Zhengzhou,**[^cite_note-6] [previously romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_postal_romanization) as **Chengchow**,[^cite_note-9] is the [capital](181337.md) and the largest [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefecture-level_city) of [Henan Province](166408.md), [China](5405.md). With a population of 12.6 million people in 2021, it is China's 11th largest city by population.[^cite_note-2021年郑州市人口发展报告_-_郑州市统计局-10] Zhengzhou is situated in north-central Henan Province at the eastern foothills of the Xiong'er Mountains and on the southern bank of the [Yellow River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_River);[^cite_note-11] where its valley widens into the North China Plain.[^cite_note-\:0-12] Zhengzhou is a major city within the [Zhongyuan region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongyuan),[^cite_note-13] an area that has been continuously inhabited for over 4,000 years, and is considered a cradle of [Chinese civilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_culture).[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] It is one of China's nine [national central cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_central_city), serving as a major political, economic and transport hub.[^cite_note-16] The Zhengzhou [metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_area), including Zhengzhou and [Kaifeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaifeng), is the core area of the [Central Plains Economic Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Plains_Economic_Zone).[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

Originally an early [Shang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shang_dynasty) urban center, the area that is now Zhengzhou was the site of the [Shang capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Shang_City) *Áo* (隞) or *Bó* (亳) (c. 1600-1046 BC).[^cite_note-19] Following the Shang dynasty, Zhengzhou remained an important regional city due to its strategic location on the North China Plains, and the city first became the seat of a prefectural administration in 587 CE in the [Sui dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sui_dynasty).[^cite_note-\:0-12] In the [Republican period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China_\(1912%E2%80%931949\)), [Nationalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalist_government) forces breached the [dikes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levee) at [Huayuankou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huayuankou,_Henan) in June 1938, north of Zhengzhou, to hinder the Japanese advance during the [Second Sino-Japanese War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sino-Japanese_War).[^cite_note-20] The [resulting flood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938_Yellow_River_flood) inundated large areas of Henan, [Anhui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhui) and [Jiangsu](166412.md) provinces; the Yellow River did not return to its original course until 1947.[^cite_note-21]

In the 1990s, following China's [reform and opening up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_and_opening_up) supported by [Deng Xiaoping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deng_Xiaoping), Zhengzhou's economy expanded rapidly: its [gross domestic product](12594.md) grew from ¥2.03 billion in 1978 to over ¥100 billion by 2003.[^cite_note-22] As state monopoly reforms progressed throughout this decade, Zhengzhou pioneered commercial reforms among inland regions, with numerous [wholesale markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wholesale_marketing_of_food) forming near [railway freight stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goods_station) to facilitate the growth of [private enterprises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_sector).[^cite_note-23] The city had a total GDP of 1.524 trillion (RMB) in 2025.[^cite_note-24] It is the home of the [Zhengzhou Commodity Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Commodity_Exchange), the first [futures exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futures_exchange) approved by the [State Council of China](44498.md) in October 1990.[^cite_note-25] The first Airport Economy Zone in China was established in [Zhengzhou Airport Economy Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Airport_Economy_Zone).[^cite_note-26] Zhengzhou has been classified as a Beta ([global second-tier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_city)) city together with nine other cities in China, including [Chongqing](171752.md), [Xiamen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiamen), [Nanjing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing) and [Jinan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinan) by the [Globalization and World Cities Research Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Globalization_and_World_Cities_Research_Network).[^cite_note-27] Greater Zhengzhou was named as one of the 13 emerging [megacities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megacity) in China in a July 2012 report by the [Economist Intelligence Unit](15303116.md),[^cite_note-28] and was officially named as the eighth National Central City[^cite_note-29] in 2017 by the central government in Beijing.[^cite_note-30] The city holds the title of [National Civilized City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Civilized_City), and is also a [National Famous Historical and Cultural City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Famous_Historical_and_Cultural_City).[^cite_note-\:1-31]

&#x20;Zhengzhou is also a major city for scientific research, often appearing among the world's top 60 cities as tracked by the [Nature Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_Index).[^cite_note-nature.com-32] The city is home to [multiple institutes of higher education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_universities_and_colleges_in_Henan), notably [Zhengzhou University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_University), [Henan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_University), [Henan Agricultural University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_Agricultural_University), [Henan University of Chinese Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_University_of_Chinese_Medicine), and [Henan University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_University_of_Technology). There are two [World Heritage Sites](44940.md) in Zhengzhou [^cite_note-33] and one [UNESCO Global Geopark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO_Global_Geoparks) in [Songshan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Song), [Dengfeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dengfeng).[^cite_note-34] The city is home to the headquarters of [Yutong Bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yutong), the largest bus manufacturer in the world by sales volume.[^cite_note-35]

## Etymology

The settlement was originally known as "[Guancheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guan_\(state\))", meaning "City of the Guan", named after the Guan family who held the area as a city-state.[^cite_note-\:2-36]

In 583 AD, it became the seat of a prefecture and was renamed "Guanzhou", with "zhou" indicating a prefectural administrative division.[^cite_note-\:2-36] The character Zheng (郑) refers to the [Zheng state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheng_\(state\)), which once governed the region.[^cite_note-\:0-12]

## History

Main article: [History of Zhengzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Zhengzhou)

Several city ruins dating back to the early [Shang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shang_dynasty) have been discovered in the Zhengzhou area,[^cite_note-37] including candidate locations of the early Shang capitals Ao (隞), Bo (亳) or Yan (奄). Since 1950, archaeological finds in the [Zhengzhou Shang City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Shang_City), a Shang capital city located near the historical center of Zhengzhou, have provided evidence of Shang settlements in the area around 1600 BC.[^cite_note-Xu-38][^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40] Outside of the city, remains of large public buildings and a complex of small settlements have been discovered at a site 20 kilometres (12 mi) to the northwest. This site is identified by some as the Shang capital of Ao.[^cite_note-shangduw-41]

The Shang, who continually moved their capital, left the area around the 13th century BC. The Zhengzhou site, nevertheless, remained occupied; [Zhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhou_dynasty) (post-1050 BC) tombs have also been discovered.[^cite_note-zzst-42] After defeating the Shang, the [King Wu of Zhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Wu_of_Zhou) named three of his brothers as vassal rulers over territories around the core of the Shang kingdom. One of them was [Shu Xian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shu_Xian_of_Guan), who founded the city-state of [Guan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guan_\(state\)) in what is now Zhengzhou. However, the three princes allied with Shang remnants and [rebelled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebellion_of_the_Three_Guards) a few years later, although the rebellion was soon put down.[^cite_note-43] From this derives the name borne by the county (*[xian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_\(People's_Republic_of_China\))*) since the late 6th century BC—Guancheng (Guan City). The city first became the seat of a prefectural (*[zhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhou_\(administrative_division\))*) administration in AD 587, when it was named *Guanzhou*.&#x20;

The name Zhengzhou ([Zheng Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheng_Prefecture)) dates back to the [Sui dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sui_dynasty) (AD 582). During Sui times, the prefectural seat was located in Chenggao, another town. The prefecture moved to Guancheng during the [Tang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tang_dynasty), and the city has been known as Zhengzhou ever since. It achieved great importance under the [Sui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sui_dynasty) (AD 581–618), [Tang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tang_dynasty) (618–907), and early [Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_dynasty) (960–1127) dynasties, when it was the terminus of the [New Bian Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Bian_Canal?action=edit\&redlink=1), which joined the [Yellow River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_River) to the northwest. There, at a place called Heyin, a vast granary complex was established to supply the capitals at [Luoyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luoyang) and [Chang'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang'an) to the west and the frontier armies to the north.&#x20;

In 1903 the [Beijing–Hankou Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing%E2%80%93Hankou_Railway) arrived at Zhengzhou, and in 1909 the first stage of the [Longhai Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longhai_Railway) gave it an east–west link to Kaifeng and Luoyang; it later was extended eastward to the coast at [Lianyungang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lianyungang), [Jiangsu](166412.md), and westward to [Xi'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi'an) ([Chang'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang'an)), [Shaanxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaanxi), as well as to western Shaanxi. Zhengzhou thus became a major rail junction and a regional center for cotton, grain, [peanuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut), and other agricultural produce. Early in 1923 a workers' strike began in Zhengzhou and spread along the rail line before it was suppressed; [Erqi Memorial Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erqi_Memorial_Tower), a 14-story double tower in the center of the city, commemorates the strike. On 10 June 1938, Chiang Kai-shek's [National Revolutionary Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Revolutionary_Army) opened up the dikes retaining the Yellow River at Huayuankou between Zhengzhou and Kaifeng, in an effort to stem the [tide of invading Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sino-Japanese_War); however, the ensuing [1938 Yellow River flood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938_Yellow_River_flood) also killed hundreds of thousands of Chinese.[^cite_note-44]



Cover illustration of *Liberation of Zhengzhou* (解放鄭州), published by the Political Department of the Second Field Army, People's Liberation Army, 1949.

Zhengzhou also has a [locomotive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locomotive) and [rolling-stock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-stock) repair plant, a tractor-assembly plant, and a thermal generating station. The city's industrial growth has resulted in a large increase in the population, coming predominantly from industrial workers from the north. A water diversion project and pumping station, built in 1972, has provided [irrigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrigation) for the surrounding countryside.[^cite_note-45] The city has an agricultural university.

In 1954, the [Henan Provincial Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_Provincial_People's_Government) relocated from [Kaifeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaifeng) to Zhengzhou, making Zhengzhou the provincial capital, as a result of its railway transportation importance.[^cite_note-46]



The Zhengzhou Yellow River Railway Bridge on the day of its reopening following restoration, August 2, 1958.

[Li Keqiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Keqiang) became the Communist Party Secretary of Henan province in 1998.[^cite_note-\:12-47]<sup>: 265 </sup> As part of his advocacy for [urbanisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanization_in_China), Li sought to position Zhengzhou as an engine for regional and national growth.[^cite_note-\:12-47]<sup>: 265 </sup> During the 2010s, Zhengzhou's GDP averaged a 13.2% yearly increase.[^cite_note-\:12-47]<sup>: 273 </sup> Between 2010 and 2018, its population doubled.[^cite_note-\:12-47]<sup>: 273 </sup>

In July 2021, [record breaking floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Henan_floods) left over a million people displaced[^cite_note-48] and at least 300 people dead.[^cite_note-hndaily0802-49]

## Geography

Located just north of the province's centre and south of the [Yellow River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_River), Zhengzhou borders [Luoyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luoyang) to the west, [Jiaozuo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiaozuo) to the northwest, [Xinxiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinxiang) to the northeast, [Kaifeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaifeng) to the east, [Xuchang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xuchang) to the southeast, and [Pingdingshan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pingdingshan) to the southwest. With the land within its administrative borders generally sloping down from west to east, Zhengzhou is situated at the transitional zone between the [North China Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_China_Plain) to the east and the [Song Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_Mountains) and [Xionger Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xionger_Mountains?action=edit\&redlink=1) to the west, which are part of the greater [Qinling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qin_Mountains) range. The city centre is situated to the south of the middle reach of the Yellow River, where its valley broadens into the great plain. Zhengzhou is at the crossing point of the north–south route skirting the [Taihang Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taihang_Mountains) and the mountains of western Henan. The prefecture spans 34° 16' \~ 34° 58 N latitude and 112° 42' \~ 114° 14' E longitude, covering a total area of 7,567 square kilometres (2,922 sq mi), including the metropolitan area, which covers 1,284.89 km<sup>2</sup> (496.1 sq mi), and the city centre, which occupies 709.69 square kilometres (274.0 sq mi).[^cite_note-50]



[Jialu River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jialu_River), a tributary of the [Huai River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huai_River), flows through Zhengzhou

A section of the Yellow River passes by the northern edges of the urban area, extending 150.4 km (93.5 mi) within Zhengzhou prefecture. However, [Jialu River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jialu_River), a secondary tributary of the [Huai River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huai_River), is Zhengzhou's main urban river and flood channel.[^cite_note-51] The Jialu enters Zhengzhou from [Xinmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinmi) to the southwest, and turns to the southeast within the city. Mountains loom over the western counties of [Gongyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gongyi) and [Dengfeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dengfeng) while the easternmost county of [Zhongmu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongmu_County) is a vast, fertile floodplain, with the counties in between being hilly transitions.

### Climate

Zhengzhou experiences a monsoon-influenced, four-season [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Cwa*), with cool, dry winters and hot, humid summers. Spring and autumn are dry and somewhat abbreviated transition periods. The city has an annual mean temperature of 15.4 °C (59.7 °F), with the monthly 24-hour average temperature ranging from 1.0 °C (33.8 °F) in January to 27.8 °C (82.0 °F) in July.[^cite_note-cma_new-52] The frost-free period lasts on average 220 days. Extremes since 1951 have ranged from −17.9 °C (0 °F) on 2 January 1955, 27 December 1971 and 1 February 1990 to 43.0 °C (109 °F) on 19 July 1966.[^cite_note-53][^cite_note-zzdl-54]

Rainfall is primarily produced by the [monsoonal low](4399268.md) during summer; in winter, when the vast [Siberian High](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siberian_High) dominates due to radiative cooling from further north, the area receives little precipitation. During the summer season, the city is also often affected by tropical depressions, which bring additional amounts of rain. The annual precipitation is about 630 millimetres (25 in). With monthly percent possible sunshine ranging from 37 percent in January to 49 percent in April and May, the city receives 1,905 hours of sunshine per year, which is around 43% of the possible total.[^cite_note-cma_new-52]

### 2021 flood

On 20 July 2021, "The heaviest hour of rainfall ever reliably recorded in China crashed like a miles-wide waterfall over the city of Zhengzhou on 20 July, killing at least 300 people, including 14 who drowned in a subway tunnel." Although an emergency alert was issued the day before the flood, businesses and subways remained open.[^cite_note-60] From 4pm to 5pm on 21 July, 7.95 inches of rain fell. A collapsed retaining wall allowed water to pour into subway tunnels. "The Chinese government now appears to be acknowledging missteps by local officials, as well as the possibility that severe weather events will become increasingly common."[^cite_note-61]

Zhengzhou was the capital of China during the [Shang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shang_dynasty). Parts of the Shang-era capital city wall that were built 3,600 years ago still remain in Downtown Zhengzhou (see [Zhengzhou Shang City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Shang_City)).[^cite_note-shangduw-41] Zhengzhou maintains abundant cultural heritages that reflect its history as well as the culture of Henan Province. [Zhengzhou Confucius Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Confucius_Temple?action=edit\&redlink=1), initially built during the Eastern Han dynasty 1900 years ago, is one of the oldest Confucian Temples in China. Other important architectural heritage sites in the city center include Town God Temple and [Erqi Memorial Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erqi_Memorial_Tower).

One internationally known tourist attraction is the [Shaolin Monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaolin_Monastery) (少林寺), which is in [Dengfeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dengfeng), about 90 kilometres (56 miles) southwest of downtown Zhengzhou (1.5 hours by coach). The Shaolin Monastery is not only known as one of China's most important Buddhist shrines, but also as the ancient centre of Chinese [Kung-fu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kung-fu). Shaolin Monastery and its famed [Pagoda Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pagoda_Forest_at_Shaolin_Temple) were inscribed as a [UNESCO World Heritage Site](44940.md) in 2010.[^cite_note-79]

Zhengzhou City God Temple is one of the historic temples in Zhengzhou and is located in the old part of the city. It was originally built during the Ming Dynasty and has been rebuilt several times throughout its history. The temple is known for its traditional Chinese architecture, including its wooden buildings, courtyards, and decorative roofs. Today, it is a popular place for people to learn about Zhengzhou’s traditional culture and local history. During traditional festivals, the temple area can become especially lively, with cultural activities and local events.

The [Henan Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_Museum) is one of China's most important museums. It has a collection of more than 130,000 cultural objects includes exhibitions from prehistoric times (such as [dinosaur](8311.md) fossils and prehistoric human remains) through to the modern era.

Zhengzhou's most developed and modern area is the [Zhengdong New Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengdong_New_Area), which is in the eastern part of the city. It is home to some of the tallest skyscrapers in Zhengzhou, including the 280 metres (918.6 ft) tall [Zhengzhou Greenland Plaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Greenland_Plaza) ("Big Corn"), which is one of the most prominent landmarks in Zhengzhou, and the twin towers of [Zhengzhou Greenland Central Plaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Greenland_Central_Plaza) (285 metres (935.0 ft)), which are currently the tallest skyscrapers in the city. The tallest structure in Zhengzhou is the 388-meter height [Zhongyuan Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongyuan_Tower), located on Hanghai East Road in the south of [Zhengdong New Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengdong_New_Area). It is used as a television tower, with a revolving restaurant and an observation deck. The tower is among [the tallest towers in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tallest_towers).

[Zhengzhou Zoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Zoo?action=edit\&redlink=1) (郑州动物园) is located on Huayuan Road (花园路).

The newly built [Zhengzhou Botanic Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Botanic_Garden?action=edit\&redlink=1) is at the western edge of Zhengzhou city.

## Economy

See also: [Central Plains Economic Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Plains_Economic_Zone) and [Zhengdong New Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengdong_New_Area)

Zhengzhou, along with [Xi'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi'an), [Chengdu](170375.md), [Chongqing](171752.md) and [Wuhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuhan), are some of the most economically important cities in inland China. Zhengzhou is the economic center of the province and the surrounding areas such as southeastern [Shanxi](81262.md) and southwestern [Shandong](105032.md). Due to its strategic location in one of the most populous areas in the world (nearly 100 million people in Henan alone) and in China's railway, road and aviation transport networks, Zhengzhou is increasingly attracting domestic and international investment as well as migrants from other areas, transforming the city into one of the largest economic centers in China. In 2018, total GDP of Zhengzhou was ¥1020 billion,[^cite_note-80][^cite_note-81] ranked 17th in China. And in 2021, total GDP was ¥1269.1 billion, ranked 16th in China.[^cite_note-82] In the first half of 2026, the GDP of city was reached 777.27 billion [RMB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMB)[^cite_note-83].

### Agriculture

By the end of 2006, Zhengzhou had a total population of over 7 million, of which 2.88 million lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-84] Its main products include [apples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple), [paulownia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paulownia), [tobacco](30942.md), [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton), and [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat). In addition, Zhengzhou also produces Yellow River [carp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carp), Zhengzhou [watermelons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watermelon), Xinzheng [jujube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jujube), [Xingyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xingyang) dried [persimmons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persimmon), Guangwu [Pomegranate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomegranate) and Zhongmu [garlic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garlic), all of which are specialties that are rarely found outside the region.

Zhengzhou and the surrounding area have large reserves of coal and other minerals. Coal mining and electricity generation are traditionally important in the local economy.

Zhengzhou has been one of the major industrial cities in The People's Republic of China since 1949. The city's staple industry is [textiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile). Others [manufactured items](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacture) include [tractors](152692.md), [locomotives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locomotive), [cigarettes](38327.md), [fertilizer](37401.md), [processed meats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Processed_meat), [agricultural machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_machinery), and [electrical equipment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_equipment). Some high-tech companies in new material, electronics and biotechnology are also growing rapidly during the recently years, especially in the high-tech industrial park in the northwest of the city.[^cite_note-85]

### Economic development zones



Longzihu area, located in [Jinshui District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinshui_District)

The [Zhengdong New Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengdong_New_Area) ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 郑东新区), literally Eastern Zhengzhou New Area, similar to [Hangzhou Bay New Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou_Bay_New_Area) in [Ningbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ningbo) and [Hangzhou Bay New Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou_Bay_New_Area) in [Ningbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ningbo), is one of dozens of major economic zones that are currently developing in various regions of China.[^cite_note-90] Established in 2003 by the provincial and municipal governments, it has become the financial center of [Henan province](166408.md) and one of the most rapidly growing areas of China.

[Kisho Kurokawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kisho_Kurokawa), a Japanese world-renowned planner and architect, was appointed to design the overall planning scheme for Zhengdong New Area.[^cite_note-91] He brought in advanced ideas including ecological city, co-existing city, metabolic city and ring city ideas. The scheme won the "Prominent Award for City Planning Design" at the first session of Annual Meeting of the World Architects Alliance in 2002.[^cite_note-92] Zhengdong New Area is mainly constituted by the CBD area, the Longhu commercial and residential area, the Longzihu college area, and the [Zhengzhou East railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_East_railway_station) commercial area.

### Industrial zones

- Zhengzhou New & Hi-Tech Industries Development Zone

Zhengzhou High & New Technology Industries Development Zone was established in 1988, and approved by the state Council of PRC to be a state development zone on Mar.6,1991. It was appraised to be advanced high tech zone of China respectively in 1993, 1998 and 2002. The Zone currently covers a total area of 18.6 square kilometres (7.2 square miles). An extension plan was approved by Zhengzhou Municipal Government, the various construction work started in 2004. Under the development strategy of "multiple parks in one zone", the Zone has been making great efforts to promote the development of software, information technologies, new materials, bio-pharmaceutical and photo-machinery-electronic industries.[^cite_note-93]

- Zhengzhou Economic and Technological Development Zone

Zhengzhou Economic and Technological Development Zone was approved as state-level development zone on 13 February 2000. The zone has a developed area of 7 square kilometres (3 square miles) Industries encouraged include Electronics Assembly & Manufacturing, Telecommunications Equipment, Trading and Distribution, Biotechnology/Pharmaceuticals, Instruments & Industrial Equipment Production, Medical Equipment and Supplies, Shipping/Warehousing/Logistics and Heavy Industry.[^cite_note-94]

- Zhengzhou Export Processing Area

Zhengzhou (Henan) Export Processing Zone was established on 21 June 2002 with approval by the state council. Its planned area is 2.7 square kilometres (1.0 square mile). Zone A is located in Zhengzhou National Economic & technological Development Area and began to operate on 1 June 2004. The area of land developed is 0.893 square kilometres (0.345 square miles) at present. Zone B is located in Zhengzhou Airport Area and is adjacent to Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport on the north and it covers a planned area of 5 square km with bonded logistics zone, bonded processing zone and supporting industry zone, etc.[^cite_note-95]

## Transportation

Zhengzhou is located in the central part of China and is a main national [transport hub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_hub).

### Public transit

#### Metro

Main article: [Zhengzhou Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Metro)



A Zhengzhou Metro Line 12 train

The [Zhengzhou Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Metro) is a rapid transit metro rail network serving urban and suburban districts of the Zhengzhou metropolitan area. The system started operation on 28 December 2013. As of 2026, it currently has 13 lines in operation, creating a 450 km (280 mi) long network. The first two lines ([Line 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_1_\(Zhengzhou_Metro\)) and [Line 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_2_\(Zhengzhou_Metro\))) were approved by the [National Development and Reform Commission](10465505.md) in Feb. 2009.[^cite_note-96] Construction of the two lines started in 2009 and 2010, and were finished in 2013 and 2015 respectively.[^cite_note-97] The [Chengjiao Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengjiao_Line) (planned to be part of Line 9), which is now in through operations with Line 2, allows the system to serve the [Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Xinzheng_International_Airport). A total of 21 metro lines have been planned to connect all areas in Great Zhengzhou Metropolitan Area.[^cite_note-98]

As of 2026, Zhengzhou Metro operates 13 lines with a total operating length of 450 km, namely Lines 1–8, 10, 12 and 14, the Suburban Line, and the Zhengxu Line.[^cite_note-ZZ-network-2025-99] Line 6 Phase I became fully operational on 30 November 2024, when its northeast section entered initial operation, while Line 7 Phase I and Line 8 Phase I opened on 29 December 2024.[^cite_note-ZZ-line6-2024-100][^cite_note-ZZ-678-2024-101] Zhengzhou's updated rail transit network plan, approved by the municipal government in May 2025, envisions 22 lines totaling about 1,260 km by 2035 and 30 lines totaling about 1,600 km by 2050.[^cite_note-ZZ-plan-approved-102][^cite_note-ZZ-plan-targets-103]

The Zhengzhou subway fares has a segmented pricing system. The starting price is 2 yuan (\$0.28) for a ride of up to 6 kilometers. If the mileage exceeds 6 kilometers, the principle of "decreasing for further distances" will apply. Within the mileage of 6 to 13 kilometers, 1 yuan (\$0.14) will be added for every 7 kilometers, and for 8 kilometers between 13 and 21 kilometers, 1 yuan (\$0.14) will be added. For each additional 9 kilometers above 21 kilometers, an additional 1 yuan (\$0.14) is added. Riders can use cash, a physical metro card, or QR code payment available on Alipay or WeChat apps to pay for the ride.

Zhengzhou is the junction of the [Longhai Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longhai_Railway) ([Lianyungang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lianyungang), [Jiangsu](166412.md)–[Lanzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanzhou), [Gansu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gansu)) and the [Beijing–Guangzhou Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing%E2%80%93Guangzhou_Railway) as well as a major national [railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Railway) hub.[^cite_note-105] The main railway station for these conventional services is [Zhengzhou railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_railway_station), opened in 1904.

Zhengzhou is also on the [Beijing–Guangzhou–Shenzhen–Hong Kong high-speed railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing%E2%80%93Guangzhou%E2%80%93Shenzhen%E2%80%93Hong_Kong_high-speed_railway) and the [Xuzhou–Lanzhou High-Speed Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xuzhou%E2%80%93Lanzhou_High-Speed_Railway). The [high-speed rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-speed_rail_in_China) network provides fast train services to most major cities in China, including [Beijing](18603746.md) (2.5 hours), [Guangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangzhou) (6 hours), [Xi'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi'an) (2 hours), [Wuhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuhan) (2 hours), [Shanghai](27643.md) (4 hours), [Nanjing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing) (3 hours), [Hangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou) (5 hours), and [Hong Kong](13404.md) (6.5 hours). Proposed high-speed railways from Zhengzhou to [Chongqing](171752.md), [Hefei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hefei), [Jinan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinan) and [Taiyuan](401079.md) are under construction.

The completion of the [Zhengzhou–Jinan high-speed railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou%E2%80%93Jinan_high-speed_railway), planned for 2023, will complete a star-shaped (referred to as a "米"-shaped) network of eight high-speed lines radiating out from the city.[^cite_note-106]

Zhengzhou is also the hub of [intercity railways in Henan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Plains_Metropolitan_Region_intercity_railway). Currently there are three intercity railways from Zhengzhou: [Zhengzhou–Kaifeng intercity railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou%E2%80%93Kaifeng_intercity_railway), [Zhengzhou–Jiaozuo intercity railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou%E2%80%93Jiaozuo_intercity_railway) and [Zhengzhou–Xinzheng Airport intercity railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou%E2%80%93Xinzheng_Airport_intercity_railway) are in operation.

[Zhengzhou East railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_East_railway_station) is dedicated to high-speed trains and is one of the largest in Asia and [Zhengzhou Hangkonggang railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Hangkonggang_railway_station) is a new high-speed railway hub dedicated to [Zhengzhou Airport Economy Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Airport_Economy_Zone).

[Zhengzhou North railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_North_railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1), over 6,000 metres (20,000 ft) long and over 800 metres (2,600 ft) wide, has been described as Asia's largest [classification yard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classification_yard).[^cite_note-107]

Zhongzhou Avenue and Nongye Expressway interchange

### Air



The sign of Zhengzhou Airport Economy Zone (ZAEZ) at Yingbin Elevated Road

Zhengzhou is primarily served by [Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Xinzheng_International_Airport) ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Air_Transport_Association_airport_code): **CGO**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Civil_Aviation_Organization_airport_code): **ZHCC**), which is 37 km (23 mi) southeast of the city center.

In 2017, it was the busiest airport in [central China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_China) in both passenger and cargo traffic.[^cite_note-108] It is also one of the eight air hubs nominated by the [Civil Aviation Administration of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Aviation_Administration_of_China).

Other airports in Zhengzhou include [Shangjie Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shangjie_Airport?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Air_Transport_Association_airport_code): **HSJ**) which is for general aviation, and Matougang Airbase which is for military use.
