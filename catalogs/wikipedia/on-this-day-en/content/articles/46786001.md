The **Special Jury Prize** is an official award given at the [Venice Film Festival](32497.md) to one of the feature films in competition slate since 2013.&#x20;

Israeli filmmakers [Yuval Abraham](76256808.md) and [Rachel Szor](79369018.md) were the most recent winners for *[NAZA](84051935.md)* (2026), at the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md) .&#x20;

## History

German filmmaker [Philip Gröning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Gr%C3%B6ning) was the first winner, for *[The Police Officer's Wife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Police_Officer's_Wife)* (2013) at the [70th Venice International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70th_Venice_International_Film_Festival). Iranian filmmaker Ana Lily Amirpour was the first woman to win the award, for *[The Bad Batch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bad_Batch_\(film\))* (2016) at the [73rd Venice International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/73rd_Venice_International_Film_Festival).

*[Below the Clouds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Below_the_Clouds)* (2025), directed by [Gianfranco Rosi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gianfranco_Rosi), was the first documentary to win the award, at the [82nd Venice International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/82nd_Venice_International_Film_Festival).

## Winners



[Warwick Thornton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warwick_Thornton) won for *[Sweet Country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_Country_\(2017_film\))* (2017)



[Jafar Panahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jafar_Panahi) won for *[No Bears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Bears)* (2022)



[Agnieszka Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnieszka_Holland) won for [*Green Border*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Border) (2023)



[Gianfranco Rosi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gianfranco_Rosi) won for *[Below the Clouds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Below_the_Clouds)* (2025)
