Since [the takeover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gaza_\(2007\)) of the [Gaza Strip](12047.md) by the [Hamas](13913.md) militant group in 2007, the **Palestinian Civil Police** in the territory, also known as the **Palestinian Police** or the **Gazan Police**, has been under the control of a&#x20;*&#x20;de facto* Hamas-led [administration](38626632.md) rather than the *de jure* [Palestinian Authority](24093.md) based in the [West Bank](33209.md).[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2]

## History

### Establishment of a Palestinian Civil Police Force

Following the 1993 [Oslo Accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo_Accords), which lead to the establishment of the [Palestinian Authority](24093.md), a [Palestinian Civil Police Force](34888928.md) assumed responsibility for policing in the Gaza Strip.[^cite_note-mfa-3]

### Hamas takeover of the Gaza Strip

Hamas won a majority of seats in the [2006 Palestinian legislative election](38945340.md), and [Ismail Haniyeh](41207463.md) was appointed prime minister of the Palestinian Authority. A [conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatah%E2%80%93Hamas_conflict) between forces loyal to Hamas and forces loyal to Palestinian Authority president [Mahmoud Abbas](232595.md)'s [Fatah](11104.md) movement resulted in Hamas [gaining control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gaza_\(2007\)) of the Gaza Strip in June 2007.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2]

In 2007, the [Hamas](13913.md) militant group [took over](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gaza_\(2007\)) the Gaza Strip and assumed control over its police force.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2] On 16 June 2007, Haniyeh declared [Said Fanuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Said_Fanuna) (officially a Fatah general who, in reality, distanced himself from Abbas) as the new security chief in the Gaza Strip, stating him as a "higher police command" than the West Bank-based police chief Kamal el-Sheikh of the Fatah.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-orf070616-5][^cite_note-6]

### 2023 Gaza War

[Hamas](13913.md) and several other Palestinian militant groups carried out a series of [coordinated armed attacks](74998317.md) in [southern Israel](844338.md) on 7 October 2023, leading to the start of the [Gaza War](74998519.md).

During the Gaza war, Hamas members assigned in the civilian police force in the Gaza Strip were systemically targeted and killed by the Israeli military.[^cite_note-7] The attacks impacted the ability for [humanitarian aid](76066918.md) to be delivered, as well as the rise of other armed gangs who would frequently raid humanitarian aid trucks in absence of Hamas forces.[^cite_note-8]

Israel and Hamas accepted a [Gaza peace plan](81215079.md) in October 2025, which was endorsed by the [United Nations](31769.md) through the adoption of [United Nations Security Council Resolution 2803](81623997.md) on 17 November 2025. Under the plan around half of the Gaza Strip remains under Hamas control and the other half under Israeli control. Resolution 2803 allows an international peacekeeping force to be deployed in the Gaza Strip and a [new civil police force](81634311.md) to be raised.[^cite_note-9]

## Structure

### Arrow Unit

The [Arrow Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrow_Unit) is the [plain-clothed](613453.md#Plainclothes_law_enforcement) division of the Hamas controlled Palestinian Civil Police in Gaza.
