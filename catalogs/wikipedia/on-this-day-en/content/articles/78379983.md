# Timeline of crimes involving the Order of Nine Angles

This page is a chronological list of criminal cases and incidents involving individuals or groups allegedly associated with the [Order of Nine Angles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Nine_Angles) (ONA or O9A), a secretive and extremist group with an [esoteric neo-Nazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esoteric_neo-Nazism), and [Satanist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satanism) ideology. ONA has been proscribed as a terrorist organization in [New Zealand](4913064.md).

According to a report by the civil rights group [Southern Poverty Law Center](27164.md), the ONA "holds an important position in the niche, international nexus of occult, esoteric, and/or satanic neo-Nazi groups."[^cite_note-1] Several newspapers have reported that the ONA is linked to a number of high-profile figures from the far right[^cite_note-members-2][^cite_note-aljareeza-3] and that the group is affiliated and shares affiliates with neo-Nazi terrorist groups such as the [Atomwaffen Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomwaffen_Division), proscribed [National Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Action_\(UK\)), [Sonnenkrieg Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonnenkrieg_Division), [Combat 18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_18) and [Nordic Resistance Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordic_Resistance_Movement) (NRM).[^cite_note-Upchurch_2021-4][^cite_note-kids-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-Anti-Defamation_League-7] Additionally, the leader of the eco-extremist group [Individualists Tending to the Wild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualists_Tending_to_the_Wild) has claimed to have been influenced by the ONA.[^cite_note-8]

Allegations have been made by antifascist organisations, several British politicians and the media that the ONA condones and encourages [sexual abuse](7397019.md), and this has been given as one of the reasons why the ONA should be [proscribed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proscription) by the British government. Many ONA adherents openly advocate rape as an effective way to undermine society by transgressing against its norms. White Star Acception commits rapes by their own admission, and ONA texts such as "The Dreccian Way", "Iron Gates", "Bluebird" and "The Rape Anthology" recommend and praise rape and pedophilia, even suggesting rape is necessary for "ascension of the [Ubermensch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%9Cbermensch)". To advance in rank, ONA member must perform assaults, with lynchings and sexual assaults being the most recommended.[^cite_note-GNET-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] Material promoting pedophilia has also appeared in ONA's in-house magazines *Fenrir* and *Drums of Tophet*, the latter of which also contained "borderline \[child porn]".[^cite_note-Wired-12][^cite_note-13] According to [BBC News](1139893.md), "the authorities are concerned by the number of paedophiles associated with the ONA".[^cite_note-terrorism-14]

## Timeline of crimes involving the Order of Nine Angles

### 2011

Ryan Fleming of [Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire)-based ONA nexion Drakon Covenant was sentenced to 26 months in prison for false imprisonment and sexual assault of a vulnerable teenager.[^cite_note-15]

### 2017

Ryan Fleming was again sentenced to three years in jail for the rape of a 14-year-old girl.[^cite_note-16]

### 2019

In 2024, the FBI released files stating that a string of 2019 [arson attacks against Black churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_black_church_fires) were connected to the ONA.[^cite_note-17]

A US [paratrooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paratrooper) named Ethan Melzer in the [173rd Airborne Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/173rd_Airborne_Brigade)'s Sky Soldiers, who was assigned to the [1st Battalion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Battalion,_503rd_Infantry_Regiment), [503rd Infantry Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/503rd_Infantry_Regiment), in [Vicenza, Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicenza,_Italy), in 2019 until 2020, plotted an [ambush](1905.md) on his unit, "to result in the deaths of as many of his fellow service members as possible." He was charged in June 2020 with [conspiring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conspiring) and attempting to murder military service members, and providing and attempting to provide [material support](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Material_support) to terrorists. The paratrooper was charged with leaking [classified information](252857.md) (including the unit's location and security) to his co-conspirators in the RapeWaffen nexion and the Order of the Nine Angles (O9A).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-doj-pr-june-2020-19] In June 2022 he pleaded guilty to three charges and on March 3, 2023, Melzer was sentenced to 45 years in prison.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

In November 2019, a [Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham,_England) teen who, according to the BBC News, adheres to "occult Nazism" and, after being "influenced by the ONA," sought "to alter himself in line with their literature," was found guilty of preparing a terrorist attack. In addition to the terror offences, he is charged with sexually assaulting a 12-year-old girl.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] He was eventually convicted of five sexual assaults in addition to the terror offenses.[^cite_note-25]

### 2020



Simpson in Ohio Army National Guard uniform

After Melzer was exposed, several other active members of the US military were also discovered to be adherents of the ONA. Corwyn Storm Carver was found to be another in communication with the group and in possession of ONA paraphernalia and literature while stationed in Kuwait. Shandon Simpson, member of the [Ohio Army National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_Army_National_Guard) sent to quell the [George Floyd protests in Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Floyd_protests_in_Washington,_D.C.) openly espoused neo-Nazi views and was also found to be in the RapeWaffen. Simpson told that he was planning to shoot the protesters as part of "[racial holy war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racial_holy_war)" and was intercepted by the FBI but only after he had already been deployed.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]

In January 2020, ONA followers Luke Austin Lane, Jacob Kaderli and Michael Helterbrand were arrested for and later convicted of stockpiling weapons and plotting to kill an antifascist couple and their young children. In preparation Lane, along with a dozen other people, had engaged in paramilitary training and sacrificed a ram, drank its blood and consumed psychedelic drugs in an occult ritual on his property.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

The British political advocacy group [Hope not Hate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hope_not_Hate) reported in March 2020 that there were six cases of neo-Nazis connected to ONA being prosecuted for terrorist offenses during the year alone.[^cite_note-31]

In March 2020, Hope not Hate began a campaign to have the Order of Nine Angles banned, proscribed, as a terrorist group, a campaign supported by several British members of [Parliament](13964.md) including the [Labour Party](19279158.md)'s [Yvette Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yvette_Cooper), Chair of the [Home Affairs Select Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Affairs_Select_Committee).[^cite_note-terrorism-14][^cite_note-Karelia-32][^cite_note-33]

On 18 September 2020, Toronto Police arrested 34-year-old Guilherme "William" Von Neutegem and charged him with the murder of Mohamed-Aslim Zafis. Zafis was the caretaker of a local mosque who was found dead with his throat cut. The [Toronto Police Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_Police_Service) said the killing is possibly connected to the stabbing murder of Rampreet Singh a few days prior a short distance from the spot where Zafis' murder took place. Von Neutegem is an ONA adherent and social media accounts established as to belonging to him promote the group and included recordings of Von Neutegem performing satanic chants. In his home there was also an altar with the symbol of the ONA adorning a monolith.[^cite_note-34] According to Evan Balgord of the [Canadian Anti-Hate Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Anti-Hate_Network), they are aware of more ONA members in Canada, and their affiliated organization [Northern Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Order). CAHN reported previously of the Northern Order when a member in [Canadian Armed Forces](182792.md) was caught selling firearms and explosives to other neo-Nazis.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

Canadian Armed Forces launched an internal investigation in October 2020 after a special forces soldier with the [CJIRU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CJIRU) was identified as a member of the Northern Order and Order of Nine Angles. According to the SPLC, the man is among "some pretty well-known, high-up people in these organizations" and an acquaintance of [James Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Mason_\(neo-Nazi\)) and the former Master Corporal Patrik Mathews who was previously exposed as a recruiter for the Northern Order and Base in Canada.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

15-year-old Rhianan Rudd allegedly planned blowing up a synagogue and was arrested in October 2020 in the United Kingdom. Home office however dropped the charges after concluding "she had been a victim of trafficking who had been groomed and sexually exploited". Rudd was allegedly radicalized by Atomwaffen and ONA. She died by suicide on 19 May 2022.[^cite_note-42]

Danyal Hussein who killed two sisters, [Bibaa Henry and Nicole Smallman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murders_of_Bibaa_Henry_and_Nicole_Smallman), in a Wembley park in London was "closely associated" with the Order of Nine Angles and took part in an ONA internet forum. Two of them were also accused of large-scale drug trafficking as a large amount of narcotics was found in their home.[^cite_note-45][^cite_note-Fontaka-46][^cite_note-Fontaka2-47][^cite_note-48]

On 11 December 2020, the UK based video hosting service [BitChute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BitChute) removed all ONA material for violating the site's anti-terror policy, citing ONA's "close connections with other proscribed organisations".[^cite_note-49] The same month Yahoo! News acquired a report by the US [National Counterterrorism Center](3231506.md), the FBI and the [Department of Homeland Security](58236.md) circulated to U.S. intelligence agencies, assessing that the ONA poses a violent threat and that it plays an influential role among right-wing terror groups. ## Nexions of the ONA

### RapeWaffen

"RapeWaffen" is a faction the ideas of which have roots in Atomwaffen and in the Order of Nine Angles, and which encourages its adherents to rape white women in order to increase the number of white births. The ideology came to public attention following the arrest of a former U.S. Marine who had been plotting to rape women and attack a synagogue.[^cite_note-rape-102]

### Legion Ave Satan offshoot

Russian [Federal Security Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Security_Services) arrested a group of satanists in April 2020 in [Krasnodar](474128.md) suspected of "public calls to carry out extremist activities", incitement to murder due to religious and racial hatred, and criminal activities against women. The police also seized occult "extremist material" during the raids. They belonged to a group called *Legion Ave Satan*, a chapter of the ONA,[^cite_note-103][^cite_note-104] using the Nazi [Reichsadler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reichsadler) grasping a pentagram and sword as its symbol. On their now-banned [VKontakte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VKontakte) page, they claimed nexions all over the [CIS](36870.md) countries, promoted ONA, and identified as followers of "Traditional Satanism" and "pre-christian faith".[^cite_note-105][^cite_note-106] They appeared in Russian media in 2018 when a teenager burned down a church in [Republic of Karelia](199243.md). The teenager had expressed his support for the Legion Ave Satan in VKontakte and posted pictures wearing a skull mask associated with Atomwaffen and ONA. He was sent to involuntary psychiatric treatment. The local nexion used an old poultry farm in [Kondopoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kondopoga) for gatherings, and the group had lured children to prostitution according to [Moskovskij Komsomolets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moskovskij_Komsomolets).[^cite_note-107][^cite_note-108][^cite_note-109] As is the case elsewhere, the group is connected to the [local Atomwaffen chapter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomwaffen_Division#Russia_\(AWD_Russland\)).[^cite_note-110]

ONA literature has been banned on the territory of the Russian Federation as "extremist material".[^cite_note-115]

### Finland

### Maniac Murder Cult

The **[Maniac Murder Cult](78379983.md)** (MKU; [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Маньяки: культ убийства, М.К.У., МКУ) is an [accelerationist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accelerationist) group affiliated with the ONA/764 founded in [Dnipro](989795.md) by Egor Krasnov when he was 16 years old, active in Russia and Ukraine. Krasnov allegedly splintered from another far right group along with 15 other members after they were expelled for committing extreme violent acts.[^cite_note-155] According to MKU's own texts, the group adheres to "nihilistic [national socialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazism)" and ONA Satanism.[^cite_note-MKY-156] According to the Terrorism Research & Analysis Consortium, "members \[are] taking pride in \[...] attacking homeless people, immigrants, and [LGBTQ communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_communities), often filming themselves in beat-downs or even murdering these groups of people in Russia." In 2022 187 MKU members were detained by the [Federal Security Service](385990.md), and it is alleged that they were preparing "terrorist" attacks on civilians and government buildings. Russian officials have made the claim MKU is supported by Ukraine and is linked to [arson attacks in Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mystery_fires_\(2022%E2%80%93present\)). Ukraine has denied the claims. Krasnov was in fact detained in Ukraine and he had reportedly boasted that he had killed at least 15 people.[^cite_note-MKU-163] In July 2024, a leader of MKU, Michail "Butcher" Chkhikvishvili, native of [Georgia](48768.md), was arrested and is facing 50 years in prison for a plot to poison Jewish people. Under him MKU co-operated with another Eastern European Nazi group [Feuerkrieg Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feuerkrieg_Division), based in Estonia.[^cite_note-164] According to the [Combating Terrorism Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combating_Terrorism_Center) MKU is responsible for at least 50 murders and 150 assaults with possibly more that have not been made public. MKU "allegedly made specific attempts to recruit individuals with experience/expertise in demolitions and/or chemical/biological weapons" to "graduate" from simple assault and murder to mass casualty event. MKU also implemented a system where aspiring members need to upload videos of assault or murder to be considered for membership. Members are graded with a system of "murder points", where members need to perpetrate acts of violence to be considered an active member.[^cite_note-MKY-156]

Alina Afanaskina, perpetrator of the [2023 Bryansk school shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryansk_school_shooting), was suspected of having communicated online with "curators" from MKU prior to the attack.[^cite_note-165] The organization claimed responsibility for June 23, 2024 bombing of a mosque in [Fryazino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fryazino), Russia.[^cite_note-166] Maniac Murder Cult was designated a terrorist group in the United Kingdom on July 2, 2025.[^cite_note-167] On December 10, 2025, Maniac Murder Cult was designated as a terror group by Canada.[^cite_note-168]

The MKU has some affiliated extremists in the United States that have allegedly been tied to a school shooting and five arson attacks.[^cite_note-169][^cite_note-170]

In February 2026, the Swedish news outlet [Expressen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expressen) reported MKU has poached accelerationist NRM members and followers to form a Nordic affiliate for the group for those to whom even NRM's level of militancy is not enough. There previously existed a [local Atomwaffen chapter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomwaffen_Division_Finland) formed by NRM members who embraced accelerationism and occultism, but which went defunct in 2024 at the latest.[^cite_note-171][^cite_note-172][^cite_note-173] A Swedish municipal official from [Greater Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Stockholm) was charged with participating in a terrorist organization for allegedly recruiting for the group.[^cite_note-174] On June 26, 2026, the [Attunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attundaland) Court ruled that MKU was a terrorist organization and the man was sentenced to a year and a half in prison.[^cite_note-175]
