[Gustavo Petro](13391962.md) assumed office as [President of Colombia](251302.md) on August 7, 2022. The President has the power to nominate members of his [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Colombia) at his own discretion, in accordance with the [Constitution of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Colombia).

After the confirmation and ratification of the ruling coalition, along with the other Majority Parties in [Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_Congress_of_Colombia), the presidents of the three respective [Conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_Conservative_Party), [Liberal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_Liberal_Party) and [U](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_of_the_U) parties met to obtain cabinet representation through one, two or three departments' executives. The creation of the Cabinet was part of the [transition of power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peaceful_transition_of_power) after the [2022 Colombian presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Colombian_presidential_election).

As a result of the political alliance between Petro and the majority parties, [Néstor Osuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A9stor_Osuna), [Minister of Justice and Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_and_Law_\(Colombia\)), and [Catalina Velasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalina_Velasco), [Minister of Housing, City, and Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Housing,_City_and_Territory_\(Colombia\)), were confirmed as political representatives of the Liberal Party. This came after Petro's rejection of other candidates proposed by the party as in their opinion, they did not meet the requirements for the post. The Liberals considered withdrawing from the government, but a meeting between the two ministers with parliamentarians and party president [César Gaviria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9sar_Gaviria) consolidated support.[^cite_note-1]

The [Minister of Transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Transport_\(Colombia\)) [Guillermo Reyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_Reyes_Gonz%C3%A1lez) is the representation in the cabinet of the Conservative Party, as confirmed by Reyes himself in several interviews.[^cite_note-2] For her part, the [Minister of Information Technology and Communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Information_Technologies_and_Communications_\(Colombia\)), [Sandra Urrutia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Urrutia), is the representation in the cabinet of the Party of the U, as confirmed by different media.

In addition to the 6 heads of executive departments and the 5 members attached to the Administrative Department of the presidency, there are eleven cabinet-level officials.

This page documents the confirmation process for cabinet candidates in the [Gustavo Petro administration](71877268.md). They are listed according to the [order of precedence of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_order_of_precedence).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-FT_Election_2022-5][^cite_note-reuters-6][^cite_note-7]

## Elected officials

### President

On 4 May 2022, it was announced that leftist leader Gustavo Petro had defeated incumbent Anti-Corruption candidate [Rodolfo Hernandez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodolfo_Hern%C3%A1ndez_Su%C3%A1rez) in the 2022 presidential election. Gustavo Petro received 11,292,758 electoral votes compared to 10,04,656 electoral votes for Hernandez; more than 10 million was needed to win the presidency. He took office on 7 August 2022.

## Nominated candidates for Cabinet-level positions

Cabinet-level officials hold positions that are considered cabinet-level but are not heads of executive departments. The exact positions that are considered Cabinet-level vary with each president. In 2019 it was raised to seven positions at the cabinet level with the creation of the [Chief of Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Staff_of_the_Presidency_\(Colombia\)) being the most important position at the cabinet level and the third most important at the Government level.

### Chief of Staff

The Chief of Staff is traditionally the highest-ranking employee of the [Casa de Nariño](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_de_Nari%C3%B1o). The chief of staff's responsibilities are both management and advisory on the president's official affairs. The chief of staff is appointed by the President and serves at his discretion. The third member appointed to a cabinet or cabinet-level position announced by Petro was the chief of staff of the presidency, Laura Sarabia.[^cite_note-57] She resigned in June 2023, being nominated [Cielo Rusinque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cielo_Rusinque), who would later fail to take office.[^cite_note-58]
