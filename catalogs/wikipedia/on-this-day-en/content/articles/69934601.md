**Jeffrey Davidson Bostic** is a Barbadian politician and former army officer who has served as the first male [president of Barbados](68909900.md) since 2025. He was a cabinet minister in the cabinet of [Mia Mottley](15337866.md).[^cite_note-1] Bostic was the Minister of Health and Wellness of Barbados and a Member of the [House of Assembly](3629496.md) of [Barbados](3455.md).[^cite_note-well-2] On 7 October 2025, Bostic was [elected](81096144.md) by the Parliament of Barbados to become the country's second president and first male president, taking office on 30 November 2025.[^cite_note-3]&#x20;

## Early life and career

### Military career

Bostic served in several positions in the [Barbados Defence Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbados_Defence_Force). In 1989, during his time in the military, Bostic served as [equerry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equerry) to [Queen Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_II) during her last visit to Barbados. He also worked with Governor-General Sir [Hugh Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Springer) as an aide de camp. In 2006, he had retired as a Commanding Officer of the [Barbados Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbados_Regiment) and Director of Operations at the [RSS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Security_System) from the [Barbados Defence Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbados_Defence_Force) after serving over 30 years in the army.[^cite_note-4]&#x20;

### Political career

Bostic was first elected to the [Barbados House of Assembly](3629496.md) in 2013, after successfully securing the majority vote in the [2013 Barbadian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Barbadian_general_election).[^cite_note-5] He was re-elected in 2018 and was subsequently appointed Minister of Health.[^cite_note-6]&#x20;

He resigned as Minister of Health on 18 January 2022 after serving for almost four years.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-well-2]&#x20;

## President of Barbados (2025–present)

Bostic was nominated jointly, by the Prime Minister [Mia Mottley](15337866.md) and Leader of the Opposition [Ralph Thorne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Thorne) on 16 September 2025, to be the second [President of Barbados](68909900.md).[^cite_note-8]&#x20;

Given there were no objections at the joint sitting of Parliament, Bostic was declared [elected without a vote](5051448.md) on 7 October 2025. Bostic was sworn in on 30 November, the 59th anniversary of Barbadian independence, succeeding Dame [Sandra Mason](48704234.md).[^cite_note-9]&#x20;

## Honours

In 1989, Bostic was made a Member of the [Royal Victorian Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Victorian_Order) (MVO) for services as an equerry to Queen Elizabeth II.&#x20;

In 2022, Bostic was conferred the [Order of Freedom of Barbados](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Freedom_of_Barbados) (FB).&#x20;
