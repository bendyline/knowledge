**Los Ríos** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[los ˈri.os\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a [province](576127.md) in [Ecuador](9334.md). The province's capital is [Babahoyo](2306909.md). The province was founded on October 6, 1860. Under legislative decree.[^cite_note-Info_on_Los_Rios-3] Babahoyo was made its capital on September 30, 1948.

## Demographics

Ethnic groups as of the [Ecuadorian census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_census) of 2010:[^cite_note-4]

- [Mestizo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestizo) 52.9%

- [Montubio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montubio) 35.1%

- [Afro-Ecuadorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Ecuadorian) 6.2%

- [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Latin_American) 5.0%

- [Indigenous](18429324.md) 0.6%

- Other 0.3%

## Governance

In July 2023, and despite a curfew, four gunmen fired on the home of the Governor of the province, [Connie Jiménez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connie_Jim%C3%A9nez).[^cite_note-5]

## Economy

The province's economy is largely based on its agriculture: coffee, [cacao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_bean), [bananas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bananas), rice, tobacco, etc. Its small industrial sector produces paper, sugar, and [wood crafts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodworking). Recently developed tourist attractions include [fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing) and [native rituals](18429324.md#Modern_times).
