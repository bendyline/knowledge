**Mbaïki** (also spelt **Mbaki** or **M'Baiki**) is the capital of [Lobaye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobaye), one of the 14 [prefectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_of_the_Central_African_Republic) of the [Central African Republic](5478.md). It is situated in the southwest of the country, 107 km from the capital [Bangui](56617.md). Lobaye people and [Pygmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pygmy) people live in the area. There is also a [waterfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterfall) near the town.

## History

Mbaïki was ceded by France to [Germany](11867.md) under the terms of the 1911 [Morocco-Congo Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morocco-Congo_Treaty), becoming part of the German colony of [Neukamerun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neukamerun) until it was reconquered by the French during [World War I](4764461.md). Mbaiki received electricity in 1969.[^cite_note-2] In 1995, the [Roman Catholic Diocese of Mbaïki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Mba%C3%AFki) was established in the city.

As a consequence of the [Central African Republic conflict (2012–present)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_African_Republic_conflict_\(2012%E2%80%93present\)), its previously large Muslim population has been emptied from the town.[^cite_note-3]

## Economy

The economy is based on the [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee) and [timber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timber) industries.&#x20;
