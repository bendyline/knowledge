**Rodney Smith** (born 1974)[^cite_note-LawDiary-1] is a [United States district judge](834368.md) of the [United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_District_Court_for_the_Southern_District_of_Florida).

## Biography

Smith received a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) from [Florida A\&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_A\&M_University) in 1996, *cum laude*. He earned his [Juris Doctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juris_Doctor) in 1999 from [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University), also *cum laude*.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

Smith served as an Assistant [State Attorney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Attorney) for the [Miami-Dade County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami-Dade_County,_Florida) State Attorney's office from 1999 to 2003. He then was in private practice from 2003 to 2007. From 2007 to 2008, he served as a Senior Assistant City Attorney for the city of [Miami Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Beach,_Florida). Governor [Charlie Crist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Crist) appointed him in 2008 to preside as a judge of the [Miami-Dade County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami-Dade_County) [Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_court_\(Florida\)), where he served until 2012. Governor [Rick Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Scott) appointed him to the [Eleventh Judicial Circuit Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleventh_Judicial_Circuit_Court_of_Florida) in 2012, where he served until his elevation to the federal bench in 2019.[^cite_note-4]

## Federal judicial service

Smith was mentioned as a potential judicial nominee in February 2018.[^cite_note-5] On April 26, 2018, President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) announced his intent to nominate Smith to serve as a United States District Judge of the [United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_District_Court_for_the_Southern_District_of_Florida).[^cite_note-WHBio-6] On May 7, 2018, his nomination was sent to the Senate. He was nominated to the seat vacated by Judge [Robin S. Rosenbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_S._Rosenbaum), who was elevated to the [United States Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Court_of_Appeals_for_the_Eleventh_Circuit) on June 2, 2014.[^cite_note-7] On October 17, 2018, a hearing on his nomination was held before the [Senate Judiciary Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Senate_Committee_on_the_Judiciary).[^cite_note-8]

On January 3, 2019, his nomination was returned to the President under [Rule XXXI, Paragraph 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standing_Rules_of_the_United_States_Senate,_Rule_XXXI) of the [United States Senate](24909346.md). On January 23, 2019, President Trump announced his intent to renominate Smith for a federal judgeship.[^cite_note-9] His nomination was sent to the Senate later that day.[^cite_note-10] On February 7, 2019, his nomination was reported out of committee by a 17–5 vote.[^cite_note-11] On June 11, 2019, the Senate invoked [cloture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloture) on his nomination by a 77–19 vote.[^cite_note-12] On June 12, 2019, his nomination was confirmed by a 78–18 vote.[^cite_note-13] He received his judicial commission on June 14, 2019.[^cite_note-14]
