**Kanuma** (鹿沼市, *Kanuma-shi*) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_Japan) located in [Tochigi Prefecture](180960.md), Japan. As of 1 August 2020, the city had an estimated [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_\(human_biology\)) of 94,926 in 36,795 households,[^cite_note-Kanuma-hp-1] and a [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) of 190 persons per km<sup>2</sup>. The total area of the town is 490.64 km<sup>2</sup> (189.44 sq mi).

## Geography

Kanuma is located in the central area of [Tochigi Prefecture](180960.md). The city is located in the northern part of the [Kanto region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanto_region). The western half of the city area is occupied by the Ashio Mountains, and the eastern half, by a diluvial plateau called Kanuma Plateau. The city is bordered by the prefectural capital of [Utsunomiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utsunomiya) to the east.

### Climate

Kanuma has a [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) (Köppen *Cfa*) characterized by warm summers and cold winters with heavy snowfall. The average annual temperature in Kanuma is 13.1 °C (55.6 °F). The average annual rainfall is 1,677.2 mm (66.03 in) with September as the wettest month. The temperatures are highest on average in August, at around 24.9 °C (76.8 °F), and lowest in January, at around 1.5 °C (34.7 °F).[^cite_note-normals-2]

## History

Kanuma was the [castle town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C5%8Dkamachi) for a 25,000 *[koku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koku)* [feudal domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_system) in the early [Edo period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_period). Even after the domain was suppressed by the [Tokugawa shogunate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokugawa_shogunate), the area continued to prosper from its location with a number of [post stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shukuba) on the [Nikkō Reiheishi Kaidō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikk%C5%8D_Reiheishi_Kaid%C5%8D). The town of Kanuma was created on April 1, 1889, with the establishment of then modern municipalities system. It was raised to city status on October 10, 1948. On January 1, 2006, the town of [Awano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awano,_Tochigi) (from [Kamitsuga District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamitsuga_District,_Tochigi)) was merged into Kanuma.

## Government

Kanuma has a [mayor-council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor-council) form of government with a directly elected mayor and a [unicameral](204304.md) city legislature of 24 members. Kanuma contributes three members to the Tochigi Prefectural Assembly. In terms of national politics, the city is part of [Tochigi 2nd district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tochigi_2nd_district) of the [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_Japan) of the [Diet of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diet_of_Japan).

## Economy

The economy of Kanuma is primarily agricultural. [Hemp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemp) was once a major cash crop, but has been supplanted by [*soba*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckwheat) and [strawberries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strawberries).

## Education

Kanuma has 24 [primary schools](25058.md) and ten [middle schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_school) operated by the city government. The city has four public [high schools](554992.md) operated by the Tochigi Prefectural Board of Education.[^cite_note-5]
