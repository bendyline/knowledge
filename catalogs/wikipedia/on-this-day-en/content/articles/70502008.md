**Rashad Muhammad al-Alimi** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): رشاد محمد العليمي, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Rashād Muḥammad al-ʿAlīmī*; born 15 January 1954) is a Yemeni politician who is serving as the president of the [Presidential Leadership Council](70488867.md) since April 2022.[^cite_note-\:2-2]

## Early life and education

Rashad al-Alimi was born on 15 January 1954[^cite_note-Biography-1] in Al-Aloom, a village in the [Taiz Governorate](3604497.md),[^cite_note-\:2-2] and is the son of judge Mohammed ben Ali al-Alimi. He graduated from [Gamal Abdel Nasser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamal_Abdel_Nasser) High School in [Sanaa](18691665.md) in 1969.[^cite_note-\:0-3] He subsequently obtained a [bachelor's degree](188874.md) in [military science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_science) from the [Kuwait Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_Police) College in 1975, and another university degree in arts from [Sanaa University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanaa_University) in 1977, then a [master's degree](170346.md) and a [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in [sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology) from [Ain Shams University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ain_Shams_University) in [Egypt](8087628.md) between 1984 and 1988.[^cite_note-\:1-4]

## Career



&#x20;al-Alimi with Russian president [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) on 28 May 2025

A member of the [General People's Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_People's_Congress_\(Yemen\)), he was [Minister of the Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_\(Yemen\)) from 2001 to 2008.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-Biography-1] He then became Chairman of the Supreme Security Committee and [Deputy Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Yemen) in charge of Defense and Security Affairs in May 2008, subsequently becoming a member of the [Yemeni National Dialogue Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_National_Dialogue_Conference), then adviser to President [Abdrabbuh Mansur Hadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdrabbuh_Mansur_Hadi) in 2014.[^cite_note-\:1-4]

On 3 June 2011, during the [Battle of Sanaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Sanaa_\(2011\)), al-Alimi was wounded along with [Ali Abdullah Saleh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Abdullah_Saleh) during an attack on the Al-Nahdin Mosque in the [Presidential Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Palace,_Yemen).[^cite_note-6] He was subsequently transferred to [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) and [Germany](11867.md) for treatment, before returning to Sanaa on 13 June 2012. He left the city again as a result of the [Houthi takeover in Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_takeover_in_Yemen)[^cite_note-\:2-2] and began living in Saudi Arabia in 2015.[^cite_note-7]

## President of Yemen

Al-Alimi became Chairman of the Presidential Leadership Council, a body given the powers of the [President of Yemen](444090.md), on 7 April 2022, through a decree by President Hadi, who irreversibly transferred his powers to the council. Multiple sources in the [Yemeni](78626.md) and [Saudi governments](27272.md) stated that [Saudi Arabia](349303.md), where Hadi was living, forced him to cede power to al-Alimi.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

On 27 August 2024, al-Alimi made his first official visit to [Taiz](545270.md), the third largest city in Yemen, pledging to liberate the Houthi-controlled areas of the city and end the nine-year long [Houthi siege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Taiz) affecting it. He also promised to restore or improve basic services in the city such as power supplies and announced several planned projects with funding from the [Saudi Development and Reconstruction Program for Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Development_and_Reconstruction_Program_for_Yemen) such as a 30 megawatt power plant, a medical school and educational complex at [Taiz University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiz_University) and improved roads along the Heijat Al-Abed route.[^cite_note-11]

Al-Alimi and prime minister [Salem Saleh bin Braik](79873699.md) were reported to have left Aden for Riyadh, Saudi Arabia on 5 December 2025[^cite_note-12] following an [offensive](81757459.md) by the [Southern Transitional Council](54251566.md) which saw it rapidly take control of most of the former South Yemen.[^cite_note-13] He chaired a meeting of Yemen's National Defence Council in Riyadh on 26 December 2025.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

### Relationship with the Houthis

During a briefing with journalists in Riyadh in January 2024, al-Alimi stated that the [airstrike campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_missile_strikes_in_Yemen) launched earlier in the month by the [United States](3434750.md) and the [United Kingdom](31717.md) against the Houthis was "defensive", saying that the solution to the Red Sea crisis "is to eliminate the Houthis’ military capabilities".[^cite_note-16] Al-Alimi hailed US President [Donald Trump](4848272.md)'s re-designation of the Houthis as a [foreign terrorist organization](421835.md) on 23 January 2025, calling it "key to accountability and a step toward peace and stability in Yemen and the region."[^cite_note-17]
