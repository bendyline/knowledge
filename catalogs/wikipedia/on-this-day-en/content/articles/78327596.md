**Zawtar al-Gharbiyah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): زوطر الغربية, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Zawṭar al-Gharbiyah*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Western Zawtar') is a municipality in the [Nabatieh District](12169367.md) in [Lebanon](17771.md), paired with [Zawtar al-Sharqiyah](34789989.md) (Eastern Zawtar).

## History

In the 1596 [tax records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daftar), it was named as a village, *Sarqiyya*, in the [Ottoman](22278.md) *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya)* (subdistrict) of [Sagif](6853436.md) under the *[liwa'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liwa_\(Arabic\))* (district) of [Safad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safed_Sanjak), with a population of 24 households, all [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim). The villagers paid a fixed tax-rate of 25% on agricultural products, such as [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), fruit trees, goats and beehives, in addition to "occasional revenues"; a total of 1,240 *[akçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e)*.[^cite_note-Hutteroth-1][^cite_note-2]

## Demographics

In 2014, [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.47% of registered voters in Zawtar al-Gharbiyah. 98.78% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-3]
