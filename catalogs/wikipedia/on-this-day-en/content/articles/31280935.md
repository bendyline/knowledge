**Pakistan–Qatar relations** refer to the bilateral links between the [Islamic Republic of Pakistan](23235.md) and the [State of Qatar](8238258.md). Pakistan has an embassy in [Doha](26214389.md); Qatar maintains an embassy in [Islamabad](51153.md)[^cite_note-1] and a consulate-general in [Karachi](17123.md). Relations between the two are shaped by Pakistan's generally [close relations with the Arab world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan%E2%80%93Arab_relations). Like other nearby gulf states, there is a large [Pakistani community in Qatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistanis_in_Qatar) which numbers over 150 000. They work in diverse fields and send remittances each year.[^cite_note-thefreelibrary-2] During the [2010 Pakistan floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Pakistan_floods), Qatar provided timely assistance to the country. After the British left, Pakistan originally called for the territory to be merged into with the [Trucial States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trucial_States) of the United Arab Emirates, which it had recognized in the same year. Despite this, diplomatic relations between the two were established.

## Economic relations

A deal was signed by the two countries' governments in December 2015 in which Qatar agreed to supply Pakistan with \$16 billion of [LNG](832128.md) over a lengthy period.[^cite_note-3]

Despite the ongoing [economic and diplomatic embargo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017%E2%80%9319_Qatar_diplomatic_crisis) imposed on Qatar, its trade relations with Pakistan remained unaffected. In 2018, trade between the two countries flourished and grew over 230 percent to \$2.6 billion.[^cite_note-4]

In June 2019, Qatar announced to invest \$3 billion in Pakistan, in the form of deposits and direct investments. The economic partnership between both the countries is expected to reach \$9 billion once the investment is done.[^cite_note-5]

In 2021, the Qatari government announced conditional arrival visas for Pakistani nationals.[^cite_note-6] A number of prerequisites that matched the one month arrival visa retirements allows Pakistani citizens entry to Qatar on arrival visas.[^cite_note-7]

### Participation at 2022 World Cup

In 2022, the Pakistani government announced its agreement with the royal family of Qatar for the possible hiring and deployment of [Pakistan Army](1965925.md) troops to provide security for the [2022 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_World_Cup).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

There was widely news reported labour abuse in the construction of the stadium and no workers rights many in other countries.[^cite_note-10]

## COVID-19 pandemic

During the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), Qatar provided urgent medical assistance to Pakistan including [testing kits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_testing), oxygen devices and food.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

## Event of the Arab countries cutting ties

On 5 June 2017, 5 Arab countries: Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Yemen, UAE and Bahrain cut ties with Qatar. Pakistan has stated that "At the moment there is nothing on Qatar issue, (we) will issue a statement if some development takes place," -Nafees Zakaria, spokesman of Pakistan's Foreign Ministry. Pakistan was unsure who to support in the issue.[^cite_note-14]

## 2022 Pakistan floods

After the [2022 Pakistan floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Pakistan_floods), Qatar established an [air bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbridge_\(logistics\)) with the [Qatari Emiri Air Force](4014890.md) and sent urgent aid to Pakistan including food, tents and hygiene kits.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

## Resident diplomatic missions

- Pakistan has an embassy in [Doha](26214389.md).

- Qatar has an embassy in [Islamabad](51153.md) and a consulate-general in [Karachi](17123.md).
