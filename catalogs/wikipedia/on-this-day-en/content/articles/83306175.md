# Enrique Riquelme

**Enrique Riquelme** (born 9 January 1989) is a Spanish businessman. He is the founder of COX Energy, a company in the renewable energy field. In 2026, he announced his candidacy for the [Real Madrid Presidential Elections](83280606.md) against current president [Florentino Pérez](2919782.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] He was born in [Cox, Alicante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cox,_Spain).

## Real Madrid presidential candidacy

Riquelme announced his candidacy to run for [President of Real Madrid](28911295.md) on 21 May 2026[^cite_note-3] following current president Florentino Pérez's call for new board of directors elections. This was following a [trophy-less season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Real_Madrid_CF_season) which saw the departure of manager [Xabi Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xabi_Alonso) midway through January and the appointment of [Real Madrid Castilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid_Castilla) manager [Álvaro Arbeloa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lvaro_Arbeloa).[^cite_note-4] This was the first contested [Real Madrid presidential election](83280606.md) since 2006 with incumbent president Pérez being unopposed in the last five electoral cycles.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

During a television appearance on 3 June, Riquelme presented a Real Madrid jersey which bore the last name of [Erling Haaland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erling_Haaland) on the back with Riquelme claiming that the Norwegian striker would join the club if he were elected president.[^cite_note-7] Riquelme further claimed that Haaland expressed desire to play for Real Madrid and had a [release clause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buyout_clause) available despite his current contract tying him with [Manchester City](165813.md) through 2034.[^cite_note-8] Both Haaland's father [Alfie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alf-Inge_Haaland) and his agent [Rafaela Pimenta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafaela_Pimenta) have denied the claims alongside Manchester City who expressed consideration for legal action.[^cite_note-9]

Riquelme revealed that [Raúl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C3%BAl_Gonz%C3%A1lez) would assume the position of Sporting Director, and [Fernando Hierro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Hierro) would become Director of the Real Madrid academy, [La Fábrica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_F%C3%A1brica_\(Real_Madrid\)). On 5 June, Riquelme pledged to bring the German coach [Jürgen Klopp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%BCrgen_Klopp), who he believes suits Real's status and ambitions.[^cite_note-10]

Riquelme would go on to lose the election against Pérez receiving 11,814 votes representing 35% of the total.[^cite_note-11] Enrique conceded the loss and congratulated Pérez while affirming that Real shall not remain anymore 20 years without elections.[^cite_note-12] He requested that the members be responsible for taking decisions and all matters connected to the club.[^cite_note-13]
