**Arzu Rana Deuba** ([Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): आरजु राणा देउवा) (born 26 January 1962)[^cite_note-Profile_of_Dr._Arzu_Rana_Deuba-4] is a Nepali [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician), who previously served as the [Minister of Foreign Affairs](69917115.md).[^cite_note-5] She served as the Chairperson of the Parliamentary Committee on Agriculture, Cooperatives and Natural Resources from August 2023 to July 2024.[^cite_note-6]

Dr. [Deuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deopa) joined the Nepali Congress in 1996 and was elected Central Committee member of the party in [14th general convention of Nepali Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_general_convention_of_Nepali_Congress). In the [2022 Nepalese general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Nepalese_general_election) she was elected as a proportional representative from the [Khas people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khas_people) category.[^cite_note-7] She is married to former Prime Minister of Nepal, [Sher Bahadur Deuba](623301.md).

[Deuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deopa) has pursued issues such as women's rights, reproductive rights, equal citizenship rights, property rights, violence against women, and equitable political representation while writing the [Constitution of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal). She has also highlighted the disproportionate impact of climate change on Nepali women, particularly those in rural and mountainous communities who face increased burdens from water scarcity, food insecurity, agricultural disruption, and climate-induced disasters.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

She was a member of the Constitution Drafting Committee and served as Chair of the Women Parliamentarians Coordination Committee. [Deuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deopa) has established a number of NGOs in Nepal, including RUWDUC,[^cite_note-10] Saathi,[^cite_note-11] SMNF,[^cite_note-12] and SAMANTA, a research-based institute for social and gender equality. She has also served as an elected regional councilor for South and East Asia for [IUCN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IUCN) and sits on boards including [Ipas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipas_\(organization\)) and the [Mahatma Gandhi Institute of Education for Peace and Sustainable Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahatma_Gandhi_Institute_of_Education_for_Peace_and_Sustainable_Development).

## Education

[Deuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deopa) graduated from Padmakanya College, [Bagmati Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagmati_Province). She obtained her master's degree from [Punjab University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panjab_University). She also obtained her PhD in Organizational Psychology in 1990 from [Punjab University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panjab_University,_Chandigarh) in [India](14533.md).[^cite_note-13]

## Political career

Arzu Rana Deuba with [Prime Minister of India](24452.md) [Narendra Modi](444222.md)

[Deuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deopa) has been a member of the Nepali Congress Party since 1996.[^cite_note-\:0-14] Since 2000, she has been a member of the Nepali Congress Party convention and Central Leadership Voting Committee.[^cite_note-15] She was a member of the Constituent Assembly and Parliament of Nepal for ten years (2008–2017). She worked extensively on constitutional provisions for women's rights and representation.

## Social work

Deuba has established several NGOs in Nepal focused on women and children, including Saathi (1992), the Rural Women's Development and Unity Centre (1995), the Safe Motherhood Network Federation (1996), and Samanata – Institute for Social & Gender Equality (1997).[^cite_note-16]

## Controversies and Allegations

Deuba has faced allegations of corruption and abuse of authority. Reports in *The Kathmandu Post* claimed she benefited from public contracts and exerted influence over government transfers and appointments, and her name has been linked to the Bhutanese refugee scam and land irregularities in Tikapur, though she has not been formally charged.[^cite_note-17] In August 2024, activist Yubraj Safal filed a complaint against her at the Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority (CIAA).[^cite_note-18]

## Personal life

She is married to [Sher Bahadur Deuba](623301.md), former Prime Minister of Nepal and former president of the Nepali Congress Party.[^cite_note-\:0-14] During the 2025 Gen Z protests, the couple were injured and taken into police custody after their house was attacked and set on fire on 9 September.[^cite_note-19]

## Electoral History

In the 2017 general election, Deuba contested the House of Representatives seat from Kailali 5 as a candidate of the Nepali Congress. The election was widely viewed as a test of her individual political standing, as it marked her first direct electoral contest after previously serving in the Constituent Assembly through the proportional representation system.[^cite_note-20] She faced CPN-UML candidate Narad Muni Rana, who was considered to have a strong local support base in the constituency.[^cite_note-21] The election was also influenced by the broader left alliance formed between the CPN-UML led by K.P. Sharma Oli and the CPN (Maoist Centre) led by Pushpa Kamal Dahal, which had consolidated communist votes in several constituencies.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] Deuba was defeated by Rana by a margin of 4,880 votes, receiving 22,322 votes against Rana’s 27,202 votes.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]
