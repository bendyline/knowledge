**Andriy Borysovych Yermak** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Андрій Борисович Єрмак; born 21 November 1971)[^cite_note-yermakreplaces-3] is a [Ukrainian](31750.md) politician and former [film producer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_producer) who served as the head of the [Office of the President of Ukraine](19362024.md) from 2020 to 2025.[^cite_note-640533BohdanB-1]

As head of the presidential office, Yermak was a member of the [Headquarters of the Supreme Commander-in-Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headquarters_of_the_Supreme_Commander-in-Chief), the highest command and control body for the [Armed Forces of Ukraine](42799055.md). He was described as [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md)'s right-hand man and a "real power broker" in Ukraine.[^cite_note-4] In 2023, *[Politico](9172097.md)* called him Kyiv's "Green Cardinal".[^cite_note-\:0-5] In 2024, [*Time* magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_\(magazine\)) named Yermak to the [Time 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100) list of the most influential people in the world.[^cite_note-\:1-6]

On 28 November 2025, Yermak resigned from his position as the head of the Office of the President of Ukraine after his home was raided by anti-corruption agents.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-bbc-20251128-8] He was formally removed from the Headquarters of the Supreme Commander-in-Chief and the [National Security and Defense Council of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_and_Defense_Council_of_Ukraine) by presidential decree on 6 December 2025.[^cite_note-hromadske_out-9]

On 2 January 2026, [Kyrylo Budanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrylo_Budanov) was appointed to replace Andriy Yermak as the [Head of the Office of the President of Ukraine](19362024.md).[^cite_note-10]

## Biography

Andriy Yermak was born on 21 November 1971 in [Kyiv](585629.md), Ukraine (then part of the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)).[^cite_note-yermakreplaces-3] Yermak's Russian mother Maria met his Kyiv native father Borys on a school trip of a [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad) school to Kyiv.[^cite_note-7240284Yermak-11] Yermak's father is [Jewish](25955086.md).[^cite_note-12] They met through mutual acquaintances.[^cite_note-7240284Yermak-11] The couple married in 1971, and she moved to Kyiv.[^cite_note-7240284Yermak-11] Yermak has a brother Denys, who is eight years younger.[^cite_note-7240284Yermak-11]

In 1995, Yermak graduated from Kyiv's [Taras Shevchenko National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taras_Shevchenko_National_University)'s Institute of International Relations with a master's degree in international private law.[^cite_note-yermakreplaces-3][^cite_note-7240284Yermak-11] The same year he received his license for law practice.[^cite_note-yermakreplaces-3] In his second year of university, at the request of one of his professors, he started working for the "Proxen" law firm.[^cite_note-7240284Yermak-11]

In 1997, Yermak founded his own law firm and worked in the field of intellectual property and commercial law.[^cite_note-640533BohdanB-1] He also became a member of the Association of Lawyers of Ukraine during that time.[^cite_note-\:2-13] Between 2006 and 2014 he, in his profession as lawyer, aided [Party of Regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_of_Regions) MP [Elbrus Tedeyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbrus_Tedeyev).[^cite_note-glavcom658860-14][^cite_note-640533BohdanB-1] In 2009, he founded and headed the public organization "Association of Entrepreneurs of the City of Kyiv".[^cite_note-\:2-13] In the [2010 Ukrainian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Ukrainian_presidential_election), he was a proxy of candidate [Arseniy Yatsenyuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arseniy_Yatsenyuk) in Kyiv's 216th constituency.[^cite_note-7240284Yermak-11] In 2011 he became the head of the “Association of Small Business Owners”.[^cite_note-15]

He also founded the Garnet International Media Group in 2012[^cite_note-16] and is the producer of such films as *[Rules of the Fight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rules_of_the_Fight?action=edit\&redlink=1)* and *[The Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Line_\(2017_film\))*.[^cite_note-17] He is a member of the Ukrainian Film Academy, and a member of the [European Film Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Film_Academy) (2017).

Yermak became acquainted with [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) in 2011, when Zelenskyy was the general producer of the TV channel [Inter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter_\(TV_channel\)).[^cite_note-glavcom658860-14] The two became friends.[^cite_note-glavcom658860-14] He worked in Zelenskyy's election campaign team in the [2019 Ukrainian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Ukrainian_presidential_election).[^cite_note-7240284Yermak-11] On 21 May 2019, newly elected President Zelenskyy appointed Yermak as Presidential Aide for Foreign Policy Issues.[^cite_note-640533BohdanB-1] In this role he negotiated major prison exchanges with Russia during the [war in Donbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Donbas_\(2014%E2%80%932022\)).[^cite_note-7240133BohdanAY-18] He became the main point of contact between Ukraine and US [President Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_Trump)'s circle,[^cite_note-ft-20250724-19] and was the point of contact for [Kurt Volker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Volker) and [Rudy Giuliani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudy_Giuliani) on behalf of Zelenskyy during the buildup of the [Trump–Ukraine scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump%E2%80%93Ukraine_scandal).[^cite_note-7240133BohAY-20] Yermak promised that, in response to their request, Zelensky would publicly pledge [to investigate Burisma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biden%E2%80%93Ukraine_conspiracy_theory), a company that employed [Hunter Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Biden). However, Zelenskyy never made such a public statement.[^cite_note-7240133BohAY-20]



Yermak at a meeting of [President Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_Trump) and President Zelenskyy in the [White House](33057.md) on 18 August 2025

President Zelenskyy appointed Yermak as Head of the [Office of the President of Ukraine](19362024.md) on 11 February 2020.[^cite_note-640533BohdanB-1] He became a member of the [National Security and Defense Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_and_Defense_Council_of_Ukraine) the following day.[^cite_note-21] As the Head of the President's Office, he provides administrative, legal, consultative, advisory, media, analytical and other assistance and support to the [President](20688479.md). Yermak was appointed chairman of the [Coordination Headquarters for Humanitarian and Social Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordination_Headquarters_for_Humanitarian_and_Social_Affairs_\(Ukraine\)) on 2 March 2022.[^cite_note-22]

Citing unnamed Ukrainian officials involved in military planning, the *[Financial Times](136566.md)* reported that he was a major proponent of the [defence of Bakhmut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Bakhmut) in 2022, against frontline commanders who urged a strategic retreat. On 20 December, Zelenskyy and Yermak made a risky visit to the Bakhmut frontline. By June 2023, Russian forces had captured Bakhmut following a major battle.[^cite_note-ft-20250724-19]

In 2023, *[Politico](9172097.md)* called him Kyiv's "Green Cardinal", commanding both private and public arenas in his olive-colored combat fatigues.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

In 2024, the *[Financial Times](136566.md)* wrote that he had a major influence on Ukraine's foreign relations and drafted peace plans, often acting with presidential authority, and stated "among Ukrainians, foreign leaders and diplomats, he is a person of immense and polarising influence." Detractors stated that Yermak was eroding democratic checks and balances.[^cite_note-ft-20250724-19] [*Time*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_\(magazine\)) named Yermak to their 2024 annual list of the 100 most influential people in the world.[^cite_note-\:1-6] In 2025, *[The Kyiv Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kyiv_Independent)* reported that officials said Yermak was regarded unfavorably in EU and U.S. political circles, and that in Ukraine his reputation was even worse.[^cite_note-ki-20251119-23]

In summer 2025, as anti-corruption watchdogs began targeting presidential insiders, Yermak and Zelenskyy's office supported legislation to place [NABU](33096574.md) and the [Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office](57303521.md) under the politically appointed prosecutor general; the effort sparked the first major anti-government street protests since Russia's full-scale invasion began in 2022, and Zelenskyy reversed course amid EU pressure.[^cite_note-politico-20251128-24] Two weeks before Yermak's dismissal, the anti-corruption agencies dismantled an alleged criminal organization involving current and former energy officials, government ministers, and a former deputy prime minister in a widening of the ongoing [\$100 million energy sector corruption scandal](33085862.md#Energoatom).[^cite_note-politico-20251128-24]

On 28 November 2025, Yermak's apartment was raided by agents from NABU and the Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's Office, amid the ongoing [Operation Midas](81589122.md).[^cite_note-bbc-20251128-8][^cite_note-guardian-20251128-25] Later that day, Zelenskyy dismissed Yermak as head of the presidential office.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-politico-20251128-24] In May 2026, Yermak was formally named a suspect in a corruption investigation.[^cite_note-27]

## Personal life

Yermak has lived in [Kyiv](585629.md) his whole life. He is single and has no children.[^cite_note-7240284Yermak-11]
