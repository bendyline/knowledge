**Simbai Rural LLG** is a [local-level government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local-level_governments_of_Papua_New_Guinea) (LLG) of [Madang Province](1240623.md), [Papua New Guinea](22943.md).[^cite_note-Momase-2011-2][^cite_note-Final-2011-3] The [Kalam language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalam_language) is spoken in the LLG.[^cite_note-Pawley-TNG-4]
