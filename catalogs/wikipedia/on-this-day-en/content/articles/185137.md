**Bakool** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Bakool*, [Maay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maay_language): *Bokool*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بكول) is a region (*[gobol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Somalia)*) in southwestern [Somalia](27358.md).[^cite_note-Cwfsom-4]

## Overview

It is bordered by the Somali regions of [Hiiraan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiiraan), [Bay](185141.md) and [Gedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gedo).

Bakool, like Gedo, Bay and most parts of the [Jubbada Dhexe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jubbada_Dhexe) (Middle Juba) region, used to be a part of the old Upper Region, which was subdivided in the mid-1980s. The town of [Hudur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudur) is the administrative capital of the region.

In March 2014, [Somali Armed Forces](27366.md) assisted by an Ethiopian battalion with [AMISOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMISOM) re-captured the Bakool province's capital [Hudur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudur) from the [Al-Shabaab](8726463.md) militant group.[^cite_note-Gsfgeflstoh-5] The offensive was part of an intensified military operation by the allied forces to remove the insurgent group from the remaining areas in southern Somalia under its control.[^cite_note-Gsfgatcwa-6]
