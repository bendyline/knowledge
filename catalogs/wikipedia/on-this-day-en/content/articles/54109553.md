The **Ministry of Infrastructure and Energy** ([Albanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_language): *Ministria e Infrastrukturës dhe Energjisë*) is a department of the [Albanian Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_\(Albania\)), responsible for national climate policy and international cooperation on [climate change](5042951.md), as well as energy issues, meteorology and national geological surveys, electricity, water, wastewater services and industry in Albania.[^cite_note-1]
