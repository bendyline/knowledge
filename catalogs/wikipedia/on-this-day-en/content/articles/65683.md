# Drive-by shooting

"Drive-by" redirects here. For other uses, see [Drive-by (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drive-by_\(disambiguation\)).

For the album by Henry Rollins, see [Drive by Shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drive_by_Shooting).

&#x20;A **drive-by shooting** is a type of assault that usually involves the perpetrator(s) firing a weapon (typically a [firearm](11966.md)) from within a [motor vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motor_vehicle) and then fleeing.[^cite_note-hutson-1] Drive-by shootings allow the perpetrators to quickly strike their targets and flee the scene before [law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement) is able to respond. The protection, anonymity, sense of power, and ease of escape provided by the getaway vehicle lead some perpetrators to feel safe expressing their hostility toward others.[^cite_note-asu-2]

## Historical conception



[Nestor Makhno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nestor_Makhno) (pictured in 1921) is attributed with inventing the tactics of drive-by shooting using horses, before motor vehicles became common.

The invention of the drive-by shooting is attributed to [Nestor Makhno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nestor_Makhno),[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] commander of the [Revolutionary Insurgent Army of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_Insurgent_Army_of_Ukraine) at the beginning of the 20th century. He attached a machine gun to a [horse-drawn carriage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carriage), enabling his forces to quickly assault targets then flee before they could properly react. This vehicle, called the [tachanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tachanka), would be used by multiple factions during the [Russian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War).

Motor vehicles offer similar concealment for transport of weapons to [crime scenes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_scene) in situations like the [2015 San Bernardino attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_San_Bernardino_attack), and can simultaneously serve as [getaway vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_scene_getaway). Using a vehicle allows the shooter to approach the intended target without being noticed and then to speed away before anyone reacts. Besides [gang](80408.md)-related attacks, drive-by shootings may result from [road rage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_rage) or personal disputes between neighbors, acquaintances, or strangers unrelated to gang membership.[^cite_note-asu-2]

## United States

There is no national data on the volume of drive-by shootings. National [statistical databases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_database) such as the [Uniform Crime Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_Crime_Reports) record the shooting outcome rather than the method. Non-gang-related drive-by shootings are not well researched, but journalistic accounts and police reports suggest that these constitute a significant proportion of the drive-by shootings to which police respond. Drive-by shootings that occur as an extreme form of [road rage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_rage) appear to be rather unpredictable in terms of the times and locations, but often occur in reaction to seemingly trivial events, although the underlying motivation usually appears to be a series of unrelated stressors in the perpetrator's life. Recent legislation has focused on transfer of guns rather than vehicles, so those who carry out drive-by shootings may use their own vehicle or one that has been borrowed, rented, or stolen.[^cite_note-asu-2]

The primary motivations for a gang-involved drive-by include intimidation, terrorisation, and assassination of rival street gang members.[^cite_note-gang-epidemic-9] Such shootings are associated with gang violence in urban areas of the United States but also occur in other contexts. The tactic is also called simply a "drive-by".[^cite_note-drive-by-wkyc-10]

### History

Motorcycle ride-by killings were a common form of murder used by drug lord [Griselda Blanco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griselda_Blanco) during her years controlling the Miami cocaine trade routes in the late 1970s and early 1980s.[^cite_note-11] Blanco herself died by this method after having been shot twice in the head by a motorcyclist in a drive-by shooting in [Medellín, Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medell%C3%ADn).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] Drive-by shootings are also included in the [Ku Klux Klan](16779.md)'s *[modus operandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modus_operandi)*.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

Chicago [Prohibition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prohibition)-era gangster and [North Side Gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Side_Gang) boss [Bugs Moran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bugs_Moran) was considered a "pioneer" of the drive-by shooting, with the [Tommy gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thompson_submachine_gun) being the weapon of choice. The gang utilized this tactic against multiple rivals, including the [South Side gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Outfit) led by [Al Capone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Capone) and the [Genna brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genna_crime_family). Other Chicago based boot legging gangs, such as the [Saltis-McErlane Gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Saltis), the [Sheldon Gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheldon_Gang), and the [Southside O'Donnell's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myles_O'Donnell), also committed drive-bys on one another. Al Capone had a Cadillac painted in Chicago police colors (police lights included) with armored rear windshield and a small hatch to fire machine guns with the car moving.

During the [Second Philadelphia Mafia War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_crime_family), two warring factions fought for control of the family; one led by former alleged boss, [John Stanfa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Stanfa); and the "Young Turks", led by future [Philadelphia crime family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_crime_family) boss [Joey Merlino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Merlino). On 5 August 1993, Merlino survived a drive-by shooting assassination attempt by two Stanfa gunmen, taking four bullets in the leg and buttocks, while his friend and associate Michael Ciancaglini was shot in the chest and killed.[^cite_note-deseretnews.com-17] On 31 August 1993, a drive by shooting was performed on Stanfa and his son while they were driving on the [Schuylkill Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schuylkill_Expressway). Stanfa escaped uninjured and his son survived being shot in the jaw.

In 1992, the [Mexican Mafia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Mafia) prison gang announced an edict prohibiting [Sureno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sure%C3%B1os) gangs from committing drive-by shootings, in order to avoid police crackdowns throughout neighborhoods in southern [California](5407.md). Those who broke the edict were to be greenlighted for assault or even death in the California prison system.[^cite_note-18]

Numerous [hip-hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip-hop) artists have been targeted in drive-bys;[^cite_note-rappers-19] prominent rappers who were killed in such incidents include [Tupac Shakur](31341.md),[^cite_note-pac-20] [The Notorious B.I.G.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Notorious_B.I.G.),[^cite_note-biggie-21] [Big L](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_L), and [Mac Dre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mac_Dre).[^cite_note-dre-22] Other rappers, such as [Obie Trice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obie_Trice)[^cite_note-23] and [50 Cent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/50_Cent) have survived being assaulted in drive-by shootings.

In 2015, Jorja Leap, an UCLA anthropologist studying gang culture, pointed out how drive-by shooting tactics are being replaced by the "walk-up shooting" method, because murders have become more targeted and while driving, there is low accuracy in aiming.[^cite_note-24]

## Italy

In [Italy](14532.md), the circulation of firearms is low compared to that in the United States, and just over a million civilians own a firearm license,[^cite_note-25] so face-to-face shooting or threats with firearms are rare. Drive-by shootings, on the other hand, are common, especially in professional criminal contexts, as the statistical incidence says that almost all assaults with firearms are carried out from cars, [motorcycles](19876.md), or [scooters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scooter_\(motorcycle\)). These kinds of vehicles are used since they provide better mobility in the narrow city districts. From the 1970s into the 21st century, [Cosa Nostra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosa_Nostra) and the [Camorra](44945.md) have both been known to perform drive-by shootings during clan or mafia wars, or to [assassinate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassinate) targets. One notable example of such, is [Carabiniere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carabiniere) general [Carlo Alberto dalla Chiesa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlo_Alberto_dalla_Chiesa), who was killed with an [AK-47](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AK-47) in a drive by shooting in September 1982 in [Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palermo).[^cite_note-ambrose-26]

One of the most striking episodes of a drive-by shooting in Italy was the [Macerata shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macerata_shooting), conducted against six Africans by a far-right extremist, Luca Traini, using an [Alfa Romeo 147](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfa_Romeo_147). However, the attack caused no deaths.[^cite_note-27]

## Iraqi civil wars

In the first decade of the 21st century, drive-by shootings were also used for assassinations by militants in [Iraq](7515928.md),[^cite_note-urlISIS_Claims_Drive-by_Shooting_that_Killed_8_in_Iraq_|_Asharq_AL-awsat-28] including that of [Waldemar Milewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waldemar_Milewicz), [^cite_note-29] [Hatem Kamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatem_Kamil), [^cite_note-30] [Noor Alsaffar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Noor_Alsaffar) and [Umm Fahad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Umm_Fahad).
