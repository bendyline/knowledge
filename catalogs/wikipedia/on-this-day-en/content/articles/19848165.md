**Bahamians** [/bəˈheɪmiənz/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) are people originating or having roots from [The Bahamas](3451.md). One can also become a Bahamian by acquiring citizenship.

## History

Main articles: [Battle of the Bahamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Bahamas_\(disambiguation\)) and [History of the Bahamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Bahamas)

The [Lucayan people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucayan_people), the original inhabitants, migrated from [Hispaniola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispaniola) and [Cuba](5042481.md) to settle in the southern islands of the Bahamas around the 11th century CE.[^cite_note-8]

## List

Main article: [List of Bahamians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Bahamians)

- [Sidney Poitier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidney_Poitier), first black man to win an Oscar, first black Bahamian actor to win Best Actor, first black Bahamian actor to be nominated for best actor.

- [Shaunae Miller-Uibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaunae_Miller-Uibo), sprinter, 2016 Olympic 400m champion, and world record holder in 200m straight

- [Buddy Hield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddy_Hield), basketball player

- [Klay Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klay_Thompson), basketball player

- [Rick Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Fox), three-time NBA champion, owner of [Echo Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echo_Fox)

- [Lynden Oscar Pindling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynden_Oscar_Pindling), first Prime Minister of the Bahamas.

- [Joseph Robert Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Robert_Love), important pan-African leader of the 19th and 20th century who influenced [Marcus Garvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Garvey)

- [Allan Glaisyer Minns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allan_Glaisyer_Minns), first black British mayor

- [Bert Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bert_Williams), first black lead actor on Broadway

- [Deandre Ayton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deandre_Ayton), was ranked number-one high-school basketball player in the USA by scout in 2015

- [Mychal Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mychal_Thompson), two-time NBA champion and first foreign-born player to be selected as 1st pick in the NBA draft history.

- [Angela Palacious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Palacious), first woman deacon and priest of the [Diocese of The Bahamas and the Turks and Caicos Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_The_Bahamas_and_the_Turks_and_Caicos_Islands)

- [Yves Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yves_Edwards), mixed martial artist

- [Kimbo Slice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimbo_Slice), streetfighter, boxer and mixed martial artist

- [Helen Singer Kaplan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Singer_Kaplan), important psychiatrist and sex therapist
