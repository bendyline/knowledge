The **[constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution) of the [Republic of Naoero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Naoero)** was adopted following national independence on 31 January 1968.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

In 2007 there were political debates in progress with a view to amend aspects of the Constitution, owing to the challenge of widely acknowledged political instability. One notable issue being discussed was the possibility of making the office of [President of Nauru](300806.md) directly elected by the population, instead of the office being indirectly elected by the [Parliament of Nauru](4503121.md). Owing to frequent recourse to the [votes of no confidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vote_of_no_confidence), changes of President have been frequent (i.e.: [Bernard Dowiyogo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_Dowiyogo) was appointed President of Nauru a total of seven times, prior to his death in 2003). Supporters of making the office of President independent of a Parliamentary vote hope that increased stability will ensure from the adoption of such a measure. Skeptics are concerned that attempts may arise to set aside the elected President through the courts; bearing in mind also that under the [Nauruan](21302.md) court system some appeals are actually heard in [Australia](4689264.md).

## Arrangement of Parts

The Constitution of [Nauru](21302.md) is divided into 11 parts and 6 schedules:

**Part I** (Articles 1 and 2) - The Republic of Nauru and the Supreme Law of Nauru\
&#x20;**Part II** (Articles 3-15) - Protection of Fundamental Rights and Freedoms\
&#x20;**Part III** (Articles 16-25) - The President and the Executive\
&#x20;**Part IV** (Articles 26-47) - The Legislature\
&#x20;**Part V** (Articles 48-57) - The Judicature\
&#x20;**Part VI** (Articles 58-67) - Finance\
&#x20;**Part VII** (Articles 68-70) - The Public Service\
&#x20;**Part VIII** (Articles 71-76) - Citizenship\
&#x20;**Part IX** (Articles 77-79) - Emergency Powers\
&#x20;**Part X** (Articles 80-84) - General Provisions\
&#x20;**Part XI** (Articles 85-100) - Transitional Provisions
