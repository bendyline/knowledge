On 22 October 2025, an [explosion](18993857.md) and fire occurred at the Plastmass Factory in [Kopeysk](1015783.md), [Chelyabinsk Oblast](475230.md), Russia, killing 23 people.[^cite_note-\:1-1]

## Background



Satellite imagery of the factory prior to the explosion

The Plastmass Factory is located in [Kopeysk](1015783.md) in the [Chelyabinsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelyabinsk) region and is owned by [Technodinamika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technodinamika), itself a holding company within the [Rostec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostec) state corporation. The factory produces larger caliber munitions from 76 to 152 mm, Russian artillery shell calibers used by the [Russian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Army) in the [Russo-Ukrainian war](42085878.md).[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-3]

## Explosion

On 22 October 2025 at around 22\:30 one and then at around midnight a second explosion occurred. Local residents reportedly testified that at least 3 drones attacked the factory.[^cite_note-4] Investigative outlet [ASTRA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astra_\(news_outlet\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) reported that [Russia's Investigative Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investigative_Committee_of_Russia) opened a [criminal case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_case) into 'alleged violations of [industrial safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_safety) rules at a [hazardous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazardous) facility resulting in multiple deaths" – stating the blast was caused by breaches of safety during a technological process in a workshop.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

Governor [Aleksey Teksler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksey_Teksler) stated that the cause of the explosion was not related to a [drone attack](38212231.md).[^cite_note-\:1-1][^cite_note-\:2-5] He also noted that the fire had been extinguished by the morning of 23 October but that there were still 10 people unaccounted for.[^cite_note-\:1-1]

Later on 29 October 2025 Teksler announced that 23 people had died in the explosion.[^cite_note-6]

## Reaction

Hundreds of residents gathered at Kopeysk central square to pay tribute to the victims and demanded news about the missing workers. They forced the head of a local labor union to read out the names of the 12 employees whose deaths have been confirmed. Governor Teksler was later seen laying flowers at a makeshift memorial, while Plastmass CEO Alexander Balashovtold residents, "It's hard to understand" how the tragedy occurred.[^cite_note-\:2-5]
