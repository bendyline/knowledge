The **government of Moldova** ([Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language): *Guvernul Republicii Moldova*) is the [government](12229.md) of the [Republic of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Moldova). It is housed in the [Government House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_House,_Chi%C8%99in%C4%83u) at the [Great National Assembly Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_National_Assembly_Square,_Chi%C8%99in%C4%83u) in [Chișinău](56635.md), the [capital](181337.md) of Moldova.[^cite_note-1] Currently, the [president of Moldova](1118398.md) is [Maia Sandu](40796149.md), while the [prime minister of Moldova](2174189.md) is [Vasile Tofan](83660480.md). The current ruling [cabinet of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Moldova) is the [Tofan Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tofan_Cabinet), incumbent since 22 July 2026.
