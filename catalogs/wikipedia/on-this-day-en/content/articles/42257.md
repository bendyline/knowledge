**Hyundai Motor Company**, often referred to as **Hyundai Motors** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 현대자동차) and commonly known as **Hyundai** (현대; [\[çəːndɛ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Korean)[^cite_note-8]; 'modernity'), is a South Korean [multinational](214491.md) [automotive](163778.md) manufacturer established in 1967. Currently, the company owns 33.88 percent of [Kia Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kia),[^cite_note-\:1-9] and owns a luxury cars subsidiary, [Genesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_Motor).[^cite_note-10] The three brands altogether make up the [Hyundai Motor Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Motor_Group).

Headquartered at the Hyundai Motor Group complex in [Seoul](19159283.md), Hyundai operates the world's largest single integrated automobile manufacturing facility in [Ulsan](323111.md), South Korea. Established in 1968, the Ulsan plant spans approximately 5,000,000 m²—about 670 [football fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_field)—and employs around 31,000 workers, with an annual completed-vehicle production capacity of 1.52 million units.[^cite_note-money.cnn.com-11] The company employs approximately 75,000 people worldwide. Hyundai vehicles are sold in 193 countries through 5,000 [dealerships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Car_dealership) and [showrooms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Showroom).[^cite_note-12] As of November 2024, Hyundai is the world's [third-largest carmaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_automotive_manufacturers_by_production) in terms of production, behind competitors [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota) and [Volkswagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen_Group).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-auto-14]



A Hyundai dealership in [‎⁨Burlington, Vermont](67258.md)

Hyundai's product portfolio includes passenger cars, [SUVs](47696.md), [commercial vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commercial_vehicle) and [hydrogen fuel-cell vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuel_cell_vehicle) sold under the Hyundai marque alongside its Genesis luxury division and the Ioniq and [N sub-brands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_N). Recent technology and product initiatives include [battery-electric vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_electric_vehicle) based on the Electric-Global Modular Platform (E-GMP) and continued hydrogen fuel-cell development. In 2025, Hyundai sold about 4.14 million vehicles worldwide and reported record annual revenue of ₩186.3 trillion, while operating profit declined to ₩11.47 trillion amid tariff and competitive pressures.[^cite_note-15]

## History

### Early years



1982 [Hyundai Pony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Pony)

[Chung Ju-yung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chung_Ju-yung) (1915–2001) founded the [Hyundai Engineering and Construction Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Group) in 1947. Hyundai Motor Company was founded in 1967 with his brother [Chung Se-yung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chung_Se-yung).[^cite_note-16] and the company's first model, the [Cortina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Cortina), was released in cooperation with [Ford Motor Company](30433662.md) in 1968.[^cite_note-17] When Hyundai wanted to develop its own car, it hired [George Turnbull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Turnbull_\(businessman\)) in February 1974, the former managing director of Austin Morris at [British Leyland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Leyland). He in turn hired five other top British car engineers. They were body designer Kenneth Barnett, engineers John Simpson and Edward Chapman, [John Crosthwaite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Crosthwaite), formerly of [BRM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRM), as chassis engineer and Peter Slater as chief development engineer.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] In 1975, the [Pony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Pony), the first South Korean car, was released, with styling by Giorgio Giugiaro of [Italdesign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italdesign) and powertrain technology provided by Japan's [Mitsubishi Motors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Motors). Exports began in the following year to Ecuador and soon thereafter to the [Benelux countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benelux_countries). Hyundai entered the British market in 1982, selling 2993 cars in its first year there.[^cite_note-nouv-22]

In 1984, Hyundai began exporting the [Pony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Pony) to [Canada](5042916.md), but not to the [United States](3434750.md), as the Pony would not pass emissions standards there. Canadian sales greatly exceeded expectations, and it was at one point the top-selling car on the Canadian market. In 1985, the one millionth Hyundai car was built.[^cite_note-TV991-23] Until the 1986 introduction of the larger [Hyundai Grandeur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Grandeur), Hyundai offered a locally assembled [Ford Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Granada_\(Europe\)#MkII) for the South Korean executive market. The import of these knocked down kits was permitted as long as Hyundai exported five cars for every single Granada brought in (the same demands were placed on [Kia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kia_Motors)).[^cite_note-usitc-24]

### Overseas expansion

In 1986, Hyundai began to sell cars in the United States, and the [Excel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Excel) was nominated as "Best Product #10" by *[Fortune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortune_\(magazine\))* magazine, largely because of its affordability. The company began to produce models with its own technology in 1988, beginning with the midsize [Sonata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Sonata). In the spring of 1990, aggregate production of Hyundai automobiles reached the four million mark.[^cite_note-TV991-23] In 1991, the company succeeded in developing its first proprietary gasoline engine, the four-cylinder [Alpha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Alpha_engine), and also its own transmission, thus paving the way for technological independence.

In 1996, [Hyundai Motor India Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Motor_India_Limited) was established with a production plant in [Irungattukottai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irungattukottai) near [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai), India.[^cite_note-25]

In 1998, after a shake-up in the South Korean auto industry caused by overambitious expansion and the [Asian financial crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_financial_crisis), Hyundai acquired the majority of rival Kia Motors.[^cite_note-\:1-9]

### Design and quality improvements

In 1998, Hyundai began to overhaul its image in an attempt to establish itself as a world-class brand. Chung Ju-yung transferred leadership of Hyundai Motor to his son, [Chung Mong-koo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chung_Mong-koo), in 1999.[^cite_note-bw-26] Hyundai's parent company, [Hyundai Motor Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Motor_Group), invested heavily in the quality, design, manufacturing, and long-term research of its vehicles. It added a 10-year or 100,000-mile (160,000 km) warranty to cars sold in the United States and launched an aggressive marketing campaign.

&#x20;In 2004, Hyundai was ranked second in "initial quality" in a survey/study by [J.D. Power and Associates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.D._Power_and_Associates) in North America.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] Hyundai is now one of the top 100 most valuable brands worldwide according to Interbrand.[^cite_note-29] Since 2002, Hyundai has also been one of the worldwide official sponsors of the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md).



The world's largest automobile manufacturing plant in [Ulsan](323111.md), South Korea, produces over 1.6 million vehicles annually.

In 2004, Hyundai Motor Company had \$57.2 billion in sales in South Korea making it the country's second largest corporation, or [chaebol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaebol), after [Samsung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung). Worldwide sales in 2005 reached 2,533,695 units, an 11% increase over the previous year.&#x20;

In 2006, the South Korean government initiated an investigation of [Chung Mong-koo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chung_Mong-koo)'s practices as head of Hyundai, suspecting him of [corruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_crime). On 28 April 2006, Chung was arrested, and charged for [embezzlement](209411.md) of 100 billion [South Korean won](298884.md) (US\$106 million).[^cite_note-30] As a result, Hyundai vice chairman and CEO, Kim Dong-jin, replaced him as head of the company.

In 2011, Hyundai sold 4.05 million cars worldwide and the Hyundai Motor Group was the world's fourth largest automaker behind [GM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Motors), [Volkswagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen) and [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota).[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32] On 30 September 2011, Yang Seung-suk announced his retirement as CEO of Hyundai Motor Co. In the interim replacement period, Chung Mong-koo and Kim Eok-jo divided the duties of the CEO position.[^cite_note-33]

In 2014, Hyundai started an initiative to focus on improving [vehicle dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_dynamics) in its vehicles and hired Albert Biermann, former Vice President of Engineering at [BMW M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMW_M), to direct chassis development for Hyundai vehicles, stating: "The company intends to become a technical leader in ride and handling, producing vehicles that lead their respective segments for driver engagement."[^cite_note-34]

### 2020–present

On 14 October 2020, [Euisun Chung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chung_Eui-sun) was inaugurated as the new chairman of the Hyundai Motor Group. His father, Chung Mong-koo, has been made Honorary Chairman.[^cite_note-35]

In April 2021, the company said that its profits rose by 187%, the highest rise in four years. The company recorded a profit of \$1.16 billion from the beginning of 2021 until March.[^cite_note-36]

In May 2023, Hyundai established a joint venture with [LG Energy Solution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LG_Energy_Solution) to manufacture electric vehicle batteries in the US.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38]

In October 2024, Hyundai Motor said it had signed a 610-gigawatt-hour (GWh) power purchase agreement (PPA) with SK E\&S and other partners, the latest in its renewable energy transition efforts.[^cite_note-39] In November 2024, the company announced that its [Global Chief Operating Officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_operating_officer), Jose Munoz will hold the new role of global [co-CEO](52234.md) beginning 1 January 2025.[^cite_note-40] Munoz will be the first foreigner to hold such a high executive post in a giant South Korean conglomerate. The promotion is said to be credited to Munoz's resilience and strategies in pushing for record sales in the North America region.[^cite_note-auto-14]

## Design emphasis



Hyundai Le Fil Rouge concept car

Hyundai's *Fluidic Sculpture* design philosophy,[^cite_note-41] heavily inspired by nature, was introduced in the early 2000s.[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43] In 2006, Hyundai hired Thomas Bürkle as head of the company's design center in [Rüsselsheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%BCsselsheim), Germany. Bürkle had previously worked for [BMW](3772.md), having designed the [BMW 3 Series (E46)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMW_3_Series_\(E46\)), and the [BMW 6 Series (E63)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMW_6_Series_\(E63\)).[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-Burkle-45]

In 2018, the company announced the *Sensuous Sportiness* next-generation design direction. The design philosophy was unveiled at the 2018 [Geneva Motor Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_Motor_Show) demonstrated by the Le Fil Rouge concept car, and has been rolled out into its recent models, ranging from sedans to SUVs. The front grille was first introduced with the 2022 NX4 Generation Tucson.[^cite_note-46] The *Sensuous Sportiness* design identity and strategy has won Design Management Institute's (DMI) Design Value Award 2020.[^cite_note-47]

In 2024, Hyundai showcased a new design language called 'Art of Steel' with its [Intium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Initium) hydrogen fuel cell electric concept vehicle.[^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49] Under this philosophy, Hyundai unveiled the Concept THREE in 2025, which featured sharper lines.[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51]

## Research and development

Hyundai operates research and development centres in South Korea, Germany, the United States (California), Japan, India and China.[^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53]

Hyundai established the Hyundai Design Center in [Fountain Valley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountain_Valley,_California) in 1990. The center moved to a new \$30 million facility in [Irvine, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irvine,_California) in 2003, and was renamed the Hyundai Kia Motors Design and Technical Center. The facility also housed Hyundai America Technical Center, Inc, a subsidiary responsible for all engineering activities in the U.S. for Hyundai. Hyundai America Technical Center moved to its new 200,000-square-foot (19,000 m<sup>2</sup>), \$117 million headquarters in [Superior Township, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superior_Township,_Washtenaw_County,_Michigan) (near [Ann Arbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Arbor,_Michigan)) in 2005.[^cite_note-54]

In 2004, Hyundai America Technical Center completed construction of its Hyundai/Kia proving ground in [California City, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_City,_California). The 4,300-acre (17 km<sup>2</sup>) facility is located in the [Mojave Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojave_Desert) and features a 6.4-mile (10.3 km) oval track,[^cite_note-money.cnn.com-11] a Vehicle Dynamics Area, a vehicle-handling course inside the oval track, a paved hill road, and several special surface roads. A 30,000-square-foot (2,800 m<sup>2</sup>) complex featuring offices and indoor testing areas is located on the premises as well. The facility was built at a cost of \$50 million.

In the 2021 review of [WIPO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Intellectual_Property_Organization)'s annual [World Intellectual Property Indicators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Intellectual_Property_Indicators) Hyundai ranked as 4th in the world for its 141 industrial design registrations being published under the [Hague System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hague_Agreement_Concerning_the_International_Deposit_of_Industrial_Designs) during 2020.[^cite_note-wipopub941-55] This position is up on its previous 7th-place ranking for 57 industrial design registrations being published in 2019.[^cite_note-wipo-56]

#### United States



Hyundai Motor America headquarters in [Fountain Valley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountain_Valley,_California)

Hyundai Motor America began selling cars in the United States on 20 February 1986, with a single model, the [Hyundai Excel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Excel). That year, Hyundai set a record of selling the most automobiles in its first year of business in the [United States](3434750.md) compared to any other car brand; total sales in 1986 were 168,882.[^cite_note-58]

Initially well received, the Excel's faults soon became apparent; cost-cutting measures caused reliability to suffer. With an increasingly poor reputation for quality, Hyundai sales plummeted, and many dealerships either earned their profits on repairs or abandoned the product. At one point, Hyundai became the butt of many jokes (i.e. Hyundai stands for "Hope you understand nothing's driveable and inexpensive").[^cite_note-59]

In response, Hyundai began investing heavily in the quality, design, manufacturing, and long-term research of its vehicles. The company added free maintenance for the first 2 years or 24,000 miles for all its new cars sold, starting with the 1992 model year. It also added a 10-year or 100,000-mile (160,000 km) powertrain warranty (known as the Hyundai Advantage) to its vehicles sold in the United States.[^cite_note-60]

Hyundai incorporated a new manufacturing facility, [Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Motor_Manufacturing_Alabama), in April 2002. The new plant in [Montgomery, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montgomery,_Alabama), was completed during 2004, at a cost of \$1.7 billion. Production started in May 2005. It employed more than 3,000 workers in 2012.[^cite_note-61]

By 2004, sales had dramatically increased, and the reputation of Hyundai cars improved. In 2004, Hyundai tied with Honda for initial brand quality in a survey/study from [J.D. Power and Associates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.D._Power_and_Associates), for having 102 problems per 1000 vehicles. This made Hyundai second in the industry, only behind [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota), for initial vehicle quality. The company continued this tradition by placing third overall in [J.D. Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.D._Power)'s 2006 Initial Quality Survey, behind only [Porsche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche) and [Lexus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexus).[^cite_note-62]

In 2009, the [Hyundai Genesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Genesis) luxury sedan was named 2009 [North American Car of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_Car_of_the_Year), the first for Hyundai.[^cite_note-63] It also won the 2009 [Canadian Car of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Car_of_the_Year) after winning its category of Best New Luxury Car under \$50,000.[^cite_note-64] The Hyundai's V8 Tau engine in the Genesis received 2009 [Ward's 10 Best Engines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward's_10_Best_Engines) award.[^cite_note-65]

In January 2012, the [Hyundai Elantra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Elantra) was named the [North American Car of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_Car_of_the_Year) at the [North American International Auto Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_International_Auto_Show), selling more than 200,000 cars since the model's redesigned debut.[^cite_note-66]

#### Canada



Hyundai Auto Canada office in Markham

In 1989, Hyundai Auto Canada Inc. opened a stamping and assembly plant in [Bromont, Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromont,_Quebec), employing 800. The plant cost \$387.7 million, with Quebec and Canadian federal government subsidies of \$131 million.[^cite_note-nyt01-76] The plant was designed to manufacture approximately 2000 [Hyundai Sonatas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Sonata) per week.[^cite_note-nyt01-76] Subsequently, [Chrysler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrysler) and Hyundai considered a joint venture that would have Chrysler rebranding the Sonata manufactured at Bromont – only to later announce the deal had failed.[^cite_note-nyt02-77] The Bromont plant was operational for four years before it closed – with Hyundai's sales unable to support the plant.

In 2009, Hyundai Auto Canada Inc. planned to open a new plant in Canada and resume production in Canada.[^cite_note-torstar-78] Hyundai subsequently sold the plant,[^cite_note-torstar-78] which was eventually purchased by Olymbec inc, a Quebec real estate developer.

Hyundai is the No. 1 import car brand in Canada without a local plant. Sales over 100,000 cars-per-year mark in 2012.[^cite_note-79]

### Asia

#### China

Main article: [Beijing Hyundai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Hyundai)

Hyundai formed a 50-50 joint venture with [Beijing Automotive Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Automotive_Group) since 2002 to produce cars in China.[^cite_note-86] The joint venture is called Beijing Hyundai, which also manufactures several models which are exclusive to the Chinese market.[^cite_note-87] It began operations in China by producing Sonata in December 2002.[^cite_note-\:0-88] The joint venture sold 700,000 passenger cars in 2010, 855,995 car sales in 2012, and 2014 saw the company sell 1,120,000 vehicles.[^cite_note-2010700k-89][^cite_note-90][^cite_note-91] At its peak, the company had five plants: three located in Beijing, one in Hubei, and one in Chongqing.[^cite_note-92][^cite_note-ChinaElectric-93]

Hyundai cars sold well in China until 2016, when sales fell by half because of its over-reliance on sedans, poor brand images and local Chinese automakers compete with price-competitive SUVs.[^cite_note-94] It marked the start of a slump that continued into 2019, when the company announced plans to cut jobs in the region.[^cite_note-95]

##### Hawtai partnership

Between 2002 and 2010,[^cite_note-terra-96][^cite_note-tt2-97] [Hawtai Motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawtai_Motor) had produced Chinese-market versions of the [Hyundai Matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Matrix),[^cite_note-shan-98] the [Hyundai Santa Fe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Santa_Fe)[^cite_note-terra-96][^cite_note-WOC-99] and the [Hyundai Terracan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Terracan).[^cite_note-terra-96][^cite_note-WOC-99] The Santa Fe was the fifth most-purchased SUV in China in 2010,[^cite_note-100] and some of Hawtai's versions may greatly differ from those sold in other markets.[^cite_note-101] Hyundai ended its partnership with Hawtai in 2010.[^cite_note-tt2-97]

#### India

Main article: [Hyundai Motor India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Motor_India)



Hyundai's manufacturing plant at Irungattukottai near Chennai, India

Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) was formed on 6 May 1996. During the entry of Hyundai in 1996, fellow South Korean [Daewoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daewoo_Motors) had entered the Indian automobile market just three years before, while [Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_India_Private_Limited), [Opel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opel) and [Honda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Siel_Cars_India) had entered less than a year back. Hyundai's first car in the country, the [Hyundai Santro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Santro) was launched on 23 September 1998 and was considered a success. It became the second best-selling car in the country from 2000.[^cite_note-103]

Hyundai has two manufacturing plants in India located at Sriperumbudur in the Indian state of [Tamil Nadu](29918.md). Both plants have a combined annual capacity of 600,000 units. In the year 2007, Hyundai opened its R\&D facility in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_India), employing now nearly 450 engineers from different parts of the country. Hyundai Motor India Engineering (HMIE) gives technical & engineering support in vehicle development to Hyundai's main R\&D centre in Namyang, Korea. Hyundai is currently the second largest auto exporter from India.[^cite_note-104][^cite_note-105] It is making India the global manufacturing base for small cars.[^cite_note-106]



A stamp released to commemorate the 20th anniversary of Hyundai in India

In June 2017, The [Competition Commission of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competition_Commission_of_India) imposed ₹87 crore (\$13.6 million) penalty for unfair business practices with respect to providing discounts for cars.[^cite_note-107]

#### Bangladesh

Hyundai vehicles are assembled in Bangladesh with local partner, Fair Technology. Its manufacturing plant in Bangabandhu Hi-Tech Park was opened on 19 January 2023. #### Japan



Hyundai Customer Experience Center in [Yokohama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yokohama), Japan

Hyundai started to market its vehicles in 2001.[^cite_note-R-109] Despite having growing sales worldwide, Hyundai struggled in Japan, having sold only 15,095 passenger cars from 2001 to 2009.[^cite_note-Japan-110][^cite_note-Japan_2-111] Following an announcement in November 2009, Hyundai pulled its passenger car division out of the Japanese market and focused on its commercial vehicle division instead.[^cite_note-Japan-110]

According to the newspaper *The Chosun Ilbo*, the reason for Hyundai's failure in the car market was due to the company's inability to recognize the value of small cars due to parking spaces. For instance, actor [Bae Yong-joon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bae_Yong-joon) was hired to endorse the mid-sized [Sonata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Sonata) in Japan to appeal to housewives who watched the drama *[Winter Sonata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_Sonata)* in 2003.[^cite_note-112] In addition, the Sonata was priced too similarly to its Japanese rivals, which resulted in poor sales.[^cite_note-english.chosun.com-113][^cite_note-Japan_2-111] The company said that it is possible for them to come back to Japan fully if market conditions continue to improve.[^cite_note-114]

On 9 February 2022, Hyundai announced a comeback to Japan by marketing eco-friendly vehicles like the Ioniq 5 and Nexo with pre-orders from May 2022 for delivery beginning in July 2022 under the company name *Hyundai Mobility Japan*.[^cite_note-KN-115] As of 2022, Hyundai Mobility Japan operates an office and R\&D center in Yokohoma.[^cite_note-116] In addition, it only operates its sales through online means only.[^cite_note-R-109]

In early 2022, Hyundai Motor Philippines, Inc. (HMPH) became the official distributor of Hyundai passenger cars in the country after the company took over its passenger car operations from HARI,[^cite_note-118] It started its operations on 1 June 2022.[^cite_note-119]

### Crossovers/SUVs



The [Tucson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Tucson) is currently the best-selling Hyundai vehicle.

Hyundai entered the [crossover SUV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossover_SUV) market early with the [Santa Fe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Santa_Fe) in 2000, followed by the smaller [Tucson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Tucson) in 2004. The Santa Fe was a huge hit with American and European markets, despite receiving criticism in the past for Hyundai's obscure looks. It quickly became Hyundai's best seller and was one of the reasons Hyundai survived despite having its sales declined.[^cite_note-brief-152] As of 2020, Hyundai has sold more than 5,260,000 units of Santa Fe globally.[^cite_note-153]

The first-generation Tucson shared its Elantra-based platform with the [Kia Sportage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kia_Sportage). In most countries apart from South Korea and the United States, the Tucson was retired for the Hyundai ix35 from 2009 to 2015. However, the Tucson name was restored for the third generation, where it was to be used across all markets. It was unveiled at the Geneva Motor Show in 2015.[^cite_note-brief-152] The Tucson is the fourth best-selling SUV in the world in 2020, with a total sales of 451,703 units, below the Toyota RAV4, Honda CR-V, and Volkswagen Tiguan.[^cite_note-154][^cite_note-155]

By mid-2010s, Hyundai moved into developing smaller crossover SUV models, starting from the [Creta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Creta) (ix25 in China) from 2014, and the [Kona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Kona) in 2017. The Kona is also consisted of a hybrid electric and a pure battery electric variant. By 2019, both model became the third and fourth-best selling vehicle of the brand, while the Creta has been the best-selling SUV in Russia since 2016,[^cite_note-156][^cite_note-top10ofbest-157][^cite_note-\:2-158] and India in 2020.[^cite_note-159] In 2021, Hyundai released its first crossover SUV model in the South Korean light car segment, the [Casper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Casper). It is the first venture of the brand in the segment in 15 years, and also the smallest automobile the brand produces of any kind.[^cite_note-160]

Currently, Hyundai produces 12 crossover SUV models for different markets.

### Hybrid and electric vehicles

See also: [Hyundai E-GMP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_E-GMP) and [Ioniq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ioniq)

Hyundai Motor Company began developing [flexible-fuel vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flexible-fuel_vehicle) (FFVs) in 1988. The test vehicle was 1991 MY [Scoupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Scoupe) FFV.[^cite_note-Lee-161] Since March 1992, in Seoul, Korea, through at least November 1993, field trials of several FFVs had been performed over more than 30,000 miles.[^cite_note-Lee-161]



The [Hyundai Sonata Hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Sonata_Hybrid) uses a lightweight [lithium polymer battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_polymer_battery).

The new [hybrid-electric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_electric_vehicle) FGV-1 was unveiled at the [Seoul Motor Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_Motor_Show) in 1995 which featured full-time electric drive technology.[^cite_note-hyundainews.com-162] The 1995 FGV-1 was the result of Hyundai's first experiments with hybrid propulsion systems in 1994. The FGV-2 was the second vehicle to be produced. The company is using the "[parallel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_vehicle_drivetrain)" type design, which utilizes either the [internal combustion engine](41228673.md) or the electric motor.[^cite_note-hyundainews.com-162] Others are the [Elantra HEV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Elantra) and the [Hyundai Accent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Accent) HEV, which were unveiled in 1999 and 2000, respectively.[^cite_note-hyundainews.com-162]

The first [pure electric car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_car) developed by Hyundai was the Sonata Electric Vehicle in 1991. The car started as a [Sonata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Sonata) sedan-based model.[^cite_note-163] Hyundai planned to have six electric vehicles available for testing by the end of 1992.[^cite_note-164]

Hyundai began mass-producing hybrid electric vehicles in 2008. The company is using Hybrid Blue Drive, which includes lithium polymer batteries, as opposed to [lithium-ion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium-ion).[^cite_note-165][^cite_note-166][^cite_note-167] The new hybrid Sonata made its debut at the [Los Angeles International Auto Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_International_Auto_Show) in November 2008.[^cite_note-168] The 2011 Sonata Hybrid sales in the U.S. began in February 2011.

In 2009, Hyundai released the [Avante LPI Hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Elantra#LPI_Hybrid) in the South Korean domestic market in July.[^cite_note-169] Hyundai showcased also the [Hyundai BlueOn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_BlueOn), an electric prototype of [i10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_i10), was first unveiled at the [Frankfurt Motor Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt_Motor_Show) in 2009. At the 2010 [Geneva Motor Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_Motor_Show), Hyundai unveiled the i-flow, a concept car using a variant of the BLUE-WILL hybrid system.[^cite_note-170]

As of March 2014, cumulative global sales of hybrid models totaled 200,000 units, including both Hyundai Motors and Kia Motors hybrid models.[^cite_note-Hyundai200K-171]

In 2016, Hyundai revealed the Ioniq five-door liftback to rival the Toyota Prius. The nameplate *Ioniq* is a [portmanteau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portmanteau) of *[ion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ion)* and *unique*.[^cite_note-hybridcars-172] It is the first automobile to be offered in [hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_electric_vehicle), [plug-in hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plug-in_hybrid), and [all-electric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_car) variants with no "standard" [internal combustion engine](41228673.md) only version.[^cite_note-ExtremeTech-173][^cite_note-SKhybrid-174][^cite_note-KoreaTimes-175] The hybrid variant launched in its home market in February 2016, followed by the electric model in July 2016.[^cite_note-SKhybrid-174][^cite_note-176] The plug-in hybrid version followed in February 2017.[^cite_note-177]



The [Hyundai Ioniq 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Ioniq_5) is the first Hyundai model developed on the [E-GMP platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Electric_Global_Modular_Platform).

In August 2020, the company announced the launch of [Ioniq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ioniq) as its own new electric brand and confirmed three new electric cars that will be sold under the sub-brand. Ioniq is Hyundai's second stand-alone brand after the Genesis. The new brand is going to utilise Hyundai's [Electric Global Modular Platform (E-GMP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_E-GMP), which it claims will enable "fast charging capability and plentiful driving range."[^cite_note-178] Hyundai Motor Group's dedicated battery-electric vehicle platform, E-GMP, underpins models including the [Ioniq 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Ioniq_5), [Ioniq 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Ioniq_6) and [Ioniq 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Ioniq_9) and supports both 400-volt and 800-volt charging.&#x20;

In December 2020, Hyundai Motor Group announced details about its [E-GMP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_E-GMP) platform that will be the underpinning of new Hyundai and Kia electric vehicles starting in 2021. The platform's main components is a battery pack under the cabin and an all-in-one motor, transmission, and inverter designed and developed by Hyundai. By bundling the components, Hyundai said, it raised the maximum speed of the motor by up to 70 percent compared to existing motors, despite its small size. The company claimed that it is capable to handle power output up to 600 hp (608 PS; 447 kW) from the system.[^cite_note-179]

In February 2021, Hyundai launched its first vehicle built on the E-GMP platform, the [Ioniq 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Ioniq_5), following the platform's detailed unveiling in December 2020.[^cite_note-180] It is the first product to be marketed under the [Ioniq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ioniq) sub-brand.[^cite_note-181] At its introduction, it is the most advanced electric vehicle produced by Hyundai. Its battery can be charged from 10 to 80 percent in 18 minutes with its 800 V charging capabilities by using a 350-kW charger. A five minutes charge will add 100 km (62 mi) to its range by [WLTP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WLTP_\(driving_cycle\)) standards.[^cite_note-182] Its claimed maximum range is 480 km (298 mi) for the rear-wheel-drive 72.6 kWh variant.[^cite_note-183]

In July 2022, Hyundai announced its new automotive factory in South Korea, solely dedicated for electric vehicles and with production set to begin in 2025. It will be the first Hyundai plant to open in South Korea since 1996.[^cite_note-None-184]

The Ioniq 5 was followed by the Ioniq 6 (2022; with a facelift unveiled in 2025) and the three-row flagship Ioniq 9, unveiled in Los Angeles ahead of the 2024 Los Angeles Auto Show and produced from 2025 at Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America in Georgia. In 2025, Hyundai sold 961,812 electrified vehicles globally, including 275,669 EVs and 634,990 hybrids, a 27% year-on-year increase.[^cite_note-185]

## Scandals and controversies

### Inflated fuel economy numbers

After an investigation in 2012, the [EPA](58666.md) found that 35% of all 2011–2013 Hyundai and [Kia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kia_Motors) vehicles had inflated fuel economy numbers; by as much as six miles per gallon. Currently, Hyundai and Kia have started a reimbursement program for the owners of the 2011–2013 affected vehicles.[^cite_note-236][^cite_note-237][^cite_note-238] In 2014 the company was issued \$350 million in penalties by the US government, agreed to pay \$395 million in 2013 to resolve claims from vehicles owners, and agreed to pay \$41.2 million to cover the "investigative costs" of 33 US state attorneys general.[^cite_note-239]

### Wrongly advertised engine and horsepower

Several consumers complained that the engine delivered with the Hyundai Veloster in Brazil was inferior to the one advertised.[^cite_note-240] Independent tests confirmed that it was not the same engine, and it delivered only 121 CV (119 hp) instead of the advertised 140 CV (140 hp), with the car earning derogatory nicknames like Slowster in the Brazilian market.[^cite_note-241]

### Marketing controversy

In April 2013, Hyundai Motors UK released a commercial depicting a man attempting to [commit suicide via carbon monoxide poisoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide_by_carbon_monoxide_poisoning) in an ix35, only to fail to do so because of the vehicle's non-toxic emissions. The advert, produced by Hyundai's in-house agency Innocean Worldwide, received widespread criticism for promoting suicide.[^cite_note-AdAge-242] Hyundai has since taken down the video and issued a formal apology.[^cite_note-AdAge-242][^cite_note-243]

### Vehicle security vulnerability

See also: [Kia Challenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kia_Challenge)

Since October 2022, following a [TikTok](56822861.md) trend, Hyundai vehicles especially in North America are highly targeted for [theft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motor_vehicle_theft) due to their weaker security systems. Hyundai vehicles manufactured from 2016 to 2021 for the U.S. market that are not equipped with optional [key fob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key_fob) and a [push-button start](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Push-button_start) mechanism lack [immobilizers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immobilizers), a system that prevent the engine from being started unless a proper key is inserted. A video posted on [TikTok](56822861.md) depicted the theft process, which involves inserting a [USB connector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USB_connector) on a naked key slot and successfully starts a car.[^cite_note-Herald_responses-244] The vulnerability also affects vehicles produced by sister company Kia.

Hyundai and Kia acknowledged the vulnerability. Hyundai introduced a free anti-theft software patch starting 14 February 2023, with all eligible vehicles patched by 23 June 2023.[^cite_note-Herald_responses-244] Models that cannot be upgraded will have [steering wheel lock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steering_wheel_lock) reimbursements.[^cite_note-245] Both companies also agreed to pay about \$200 million to settle a U.S. [class-action lawsuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class-action_lawsuit), including up to \$145 million for car owners' out-of-pocket losses. The settlement, announced on 18 May 2023, was rejected by a federal judge.[^cite_note-246]

According to U.S. authority [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](989530.md), the trend has led to eight fatalities in the country.[^cite_note-247]

### U.S. child labor allegations



Hyundai Alabama child labor allegations HQ

See also: [Hyundai Alabama child labor allegations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Alabama_child_labor_allegations)

In 2022, Hyundai and its [parts suppliers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supply_chain) in the U.S. were discovered to be illegally employing [child labor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_labor), primarily involving [refugees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refugees) from [Central America](6121.md).[^cite_note-\:3-248][^cite_note-\:04-249] This issue involved parts suppliers, including Hyundai subsidiaries [Hyundai Glovis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Glovis) and SMART, SL, Hwashin, and AJIN, many of which used temporary work agencies to recruit underage workers. These suppliers hired minors, some as young as 12, to operate heavy machinery, in direct violation of child labor laws. Following a [Reuters](18998750.md) report in July 2022, investigations confirmed these illegal practices, leading to fines and sanctions against several suppliers.[^cite_note-250] In response to the scandal, Hyundai announced plans to divest its controlling stake in SMART.[^cite_note-251]
