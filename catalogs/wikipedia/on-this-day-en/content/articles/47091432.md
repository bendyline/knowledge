The **National Defence and Security Council** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): အမျိုးသား ကာကွယ်ရေးနှင့် လုံခြုံရေး ကောင်စီ, Burmese pronunciation: [\[əmjóðá kàkwɛ̀jé n̥ḭɴ lòʊɴdʑòʊɴjé kàʊɴsì\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Burmese); abbreviated **NDSC**) is an eleven-member [national security council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_security_council) responsible for security and defence affairs in [Myanmar](19457.md).[^cite_note-Slow-1] Though the [president](11195083.md) is the [chief executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_executive_\(gubernatorial\)) of the [government of Myanmar](20390.md), the [2008 Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Constitution_of_Myanmar) reserves certain important executive powers for the NDSC and certain actions of the president and the [Tatmadaw](20394.md) (military) require approval by the NDSC. Of the 11 members, the [commander-in-chief of defence services](56636725.md) appoints 5 members and controls 6 votes.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-gyaw-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The NDSC's existence is enshrined in Chapter V of the [2008 Constitution of Myanmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Constitution_of_Myanmar),[^cite_note-com-7] and it was formed on 31 March 2011.[^cite_note-pd-8][^cite_note-gyaw-3]

A meeting of the NDSC can only be called by the president, which was never done during the [National League for Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_for_Democracy)'s 2016–2021 time in power.[^cite_note-Slow-1] After President [Win Myint](37228839.md) refused to call a meeting of the NDSC to address the Tatmadaw's baseless allegations of massive voter fraud in the [2020 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Myanmar_general_election), the Tatmadaw under the leadership of Commander-in-Chief [Min Aung Hlaing](38278640.md) executed a [coup on 1 February 2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Myanmar_coup), removing Win Myint and installing [Myint Swe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myint_Swe) as Acting President so he could call a meeting of the NDSC and transfer state power to Min Aung Hlaing. The declaration of a state of emergency by the NDSC served as the purported legal basis for all powers exercised by Min Aung Hlaing during his military rule.[^cite_note-9] After the state of emergency expired in 2025 and the [State Administration Council](66589972.md) was dissolved, Min Aung Hlaing ruled directly through the NDSC until being elected president in 2026.[^cite_note-AP-10]

The opposition [National Unity Government](67413816.md) designated the NDSC a terrorist organization in November 2025.[^cite_note-11]

## Powers

The NDSC's constitutionally enshrined roles include expansive powers:

- Recommend pardonees for the President to grant amnesty

  [^cite_note-com-7]

  [^cite_note-12]

- Approve the President's act of severing foreign diplomatic relations

  [^cite_note-com-7]

- Coordinate with the President to take military action against aggressors

  [^cite_note-com-7]

- Approve the [Tatmadaw](20394.md)'s ability to conscript citizens

  [^cite_note-com-7]

- Nominate a candidate for [Commander-in-Chief of Defence Services](56636725.md), to be appointed by the president

  [^cite_note-com-7]

- Coordinate with the President in declaring a state of emergency

  [^cite_note-com-7]

- Exercise legislative, executive and judiciary powers during a state of emergency

  [^cite_note-com-7]

- Exercise sovereign power during a state of emergency

  [^cite_note-com-7]

#### Office of the National Defence and Security Council

National Defense and Security Council did not have an office body. However, it was announced on 31 July 2025 via Order No. 2/2025 that an office body has now been formed with two new positions: Chief Executive and Joint Chief Executive, with former SAC secretary [Aung Lwin Dwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aung_Lin_Dwe) as Chief Executive and General [Ye Win Oo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ye_Win_Oo) as Joint Chief Executive.[^cite_note-14] On April 10, 2026, following the formation of Min Aung Hlaing’s second cabinet after his election as president, the establishments that had been created by NDSC were abolished.
