**Concordia** is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Sinaloa) in the [Mexican state](8196151.md) of [Sinaloa](222251.md) in northwestern [Mexico](3966054.md).[^cite_note-2]

According to the 2010 census it had a population of 28,493 inhabitants.

## Political subdivision

Concordia Municipality is subdivided in 8 *sindicaturas*:

- El Verde

- [Santa Lucía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Luc%C3%ADa,_Sinaloa)

- Mesillas

- Zavala

- [Agua Caliente de Gárate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agua_Caliente_de_G%C3%A1rate)

- Pánuco

- Tepuxtla

- [Copala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copala,_Sinaloa)
