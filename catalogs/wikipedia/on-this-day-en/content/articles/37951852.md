**Ciudad de la Paz** (Spanish: [\[θjuˈðað ðe la ˈpaθ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish), lit. 'City of Peace'), previously known as **Djibloho&#x20;**&#x20;or **Oyala**,[^cite_note-3] commonly known as **La Paz**, is the official capital city of [Equatorial Guinea](9366.md), though still under construction.[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Established as an [urban district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Equatorial_Guinea) in [Wele-Nzas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wele-Nzas) in 2015,[^cite_note-inauguration-8] it is also the administrative headquarters of [Djibloho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djibloho), Equatorial Guinea's newest province created in 2017, and is located near the town of [Mengomeyén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mengomey%C3%A9n).[^cite_note-9] In 2017, the city was officially named *Ciudad de la Paz* ("City of Peace").[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

The [planned city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planned_city)'s location was chosen for its easy access, milder climate, and inland location, as the [2004 Equatorial Guinea coup attempt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Equatorial_Guinea_coup_attempt) raised fears about maritime access to the capital.[^cite_note-12] It was designed by the Portuguese Studio for [Architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture) and [Urbanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanism) *FAT – Future Architecture Thinking*. It is projected to have around 200,000 inhabitants,[^cite_note-ABC-13] a new [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Equatorial_Guinea) building, a number of presidential villas and an area of 8150 hectares.[^cite_note-piniweb.com.br-14][^cite_note-greensavers.pt-15] The construction of this new capital has been criticized by the political opposition to President [Teodoro Obiang](263622.md), who was the driving force behind the initiative. The government of Equatorial Guinea began to move to the city in early 2017.[^cite_note-16]

## Geography

### Location

La Paz is located near the center of [Río Muni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%ADo_Muni), the continental part of Equatorial Guinea. It is located between the cities of [Bata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bata,_Equatorial_Guinea) and [Mongomo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongomo) and 20 km from the [airport of Mengomeyén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_Obiang_Nguema_International_Airport). The power supply relies upon the 120 MW [Djibloho Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djibloho_Dam) in the Djibloho Evinayong district.[^cite_note-17]

### Climate

Ciudad de la Paz has a tropical climate that borders between a [tropical monsoon climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate) and a [tropical savannah climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savannah_climate). Despite not being classified as having a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate), it is nonetheless located within a region of [tropical rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest). It has high overall rainfall, averaging 2142 mm a year. There is an extensive wet season, spanning 10 months of the year from September to June, and a brief and slightly cooler dry season covering the remaining two months, July and August. There is also a noticeably drier, though still wet, stretch in December and January. Temperatures remain very warm throughout the course of the year, albeit lower than one might expect in other places with the same climate, especially considering its proximity to the [equator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equator).

## Planning and construction

In the middle of the undeveloped [rain forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest), the government planned to build a new city as the future seat of government.[^cite_note-ABC-13][^cite_note-BBC171212-18] It would be the [headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headquarters) of the president, government, administration, police and military leadership[^cite_note-BBC171212-18] and replace the then capital [Malabo](48823.md). The city was designed to house 160,000–200,000 people, living in an area of 81.5 km<sup>2</sup>.

A golf course, a university, a luxury hotel, and a six-lane highway were finished in 2013.[^cite_note-BBC171212-18] Other buildings planned include government buildings, a financial district and residential areas. Three bridges and highways have been completed or are under construction. A connection between the city and the new [airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_Obiang_Nguema_International_Airport) in [Mengomeyen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mengomeyen) was constructed.

Funding for construction was provided through [AICEP Portugal Global](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AICEP_Portugal_Global). The plans came from a Portuguese architectural office. The construction work was supported by [China](5405.md), [Poland](22936.md), [Brazil](3383.md) and [North Korea](21255.md).[^cite_note-BBC171212-18] Construction faced delays such as, according to unconfirmed reports, President Obiang ordering a building to be moved because he did not like the view.[^cite_note-BBC171212-18] Additionally, while many specialized components were imported, the city’s foundational construction relied heavily on locally sourced materials. This was made possible by the establishment of domestic quarrying operations, which provided high-quality stone and aggregates essential for infrastructure and large-scale development within the Oyala region.[^cite_note-\:0-4]

## Design

The city was designed by the Portuguese urban architecture firm FAT – Future Architecture Thinking. Estimates of its ultimate population size have been revised from 65,000 initially[^cite_note-19] to between 160,000 and 200,000.[^cite_note-area-1] The city was built on an area of 8,150 hectares (81.5 km<sup>2</sup> or 20,100 acres).[^cite_note-piniweb.com.br-14][^cite_note-greensavers.pt-15]

In 2021, a video sponsored by the [China State Construction group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_State_Construction_Engineering), which is part of the building consortium, showed what appeared to be an overpass of completed, but not yet functioning, highways to Ciudad de la Paz, Bata, Mongomo and Mengomeyén airport.[^cite_note-20] The video continued showing viewers around parts of the new city, where work on a six-story cylindrical tower-building for the ministry of infrastructure remained partly built, and most other structures appeared far less advanced.

However, 2022 videos show two glass towers, the gateway to the campus of the [Afro-American University of Central Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-American_University_of_Central_Africa),[^cite_note-21] several administrative style buildings and the Grand Hotel Djibloho all appearing functionally complete.[^cite_note-22] The 380-room hotel has a golf course, wellness center and facilities for conferences as large as 1200 people, though its own website describes itself as being surrounded by tropical forest, which suggests that it is not in the heart of a city.[^cite_note-23]
