# Court of Appeal of Paris

[48°51′22″N 2°20′42″E  /  48.856°N 2.345°E  / 48.856; 2.345](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Court_of_Appeal_of_Paris\&params=48.856_N_2.345_E_)



The [Palais de Justice, Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palais_de_Justice,_Paris), seat of the Court of Appeal of Paris

The **Court of Appeal of Paris** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Cour d'appel de Paris*, [\[kuʁ dapɛl də paʁi\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French)) is the largest [appeals court in France](51310988.md) in terms of the number of [cases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_case) brought before it. Its jurisdiction covers the [departments](38523.md) of Paris, Essonne, Yonne, Seine-et-Marne, Seine-Saint-Denis, and Val-de-Marne.[^cite_note-1]

The Court is housed in the [Palais de Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palais_de_Justice,_Paris) of [Paris](22989.md). Jacques Degrandi has been the president of the Court since 2010.[^cite_note-2]
