**Domingos Inácio Brazão** (born 7 March 1965),[^cite_note-2023.0059871-SR/PF/RJ-1] [mononymously](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mononymously) known as **Brazão**,[^cite_note-2023.0059871-SR/PF/RJ-1] is a Brazilian politician, former state congressman and adviser to [Rio de Janeiro's court of auditors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro's_court_of_auditors?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-24_March_2024_The_Guardian-2]

## Assassination of Marielle Franco

Main article: [Assassination of Marielle Franco](59758264.md)

On 24 March 2024, Brazão was arrested, alongside his brother [Chiquinho Brazão](71090450.md) and chief of police [Rivaldo Barbosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivaldo_Barbosa_\(chief_of_police\)), on suspicions of involvement in the [assassination of councilwoman Marielle Franco and driver Anderson Gomes](59758264.md).[^cite_note-24_March_2024_The_Guardian-2][^cite_note-24_March_2024_The_New_York_Times-3][^cite_note-24_March_2024_Reuters-4][^cite_note-24_March_2024_France_24-5]

On 25 February 2026, a panel of the [Supreme Federal Court](3359076.md) convicted five people, including the Brazão brothers, for Franco's assassination and sentenced them to up to 76 years' imprisonment.[^cite_note-6]
