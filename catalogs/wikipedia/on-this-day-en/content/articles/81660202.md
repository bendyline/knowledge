# 2026 FIFA World Cup Group J

&#x20; Group J of the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) took place from June 16 to 27, 2026.[^cite_note-match_schedule-1] The group consisted of [defending champions](17742072.md) [Argentina](454699.md), [Algeria](1029528.md), [Austria](695226.md), and [Jordan](1090065.md).

Argentina topped the group with three wins out of three, and Austria finished as runners-up, gaining the edge over Algeria on goal difference. Both teams thus advanced to the [round of 32](81667005.md#Round_of_32).[^cite_note-regulations-2] In the [knockout stage](81667005.md), Argentina defeated multiple teams but lost to Spain in [the final](75845879.md).

Algeria's four points were nonetheless sufficient enough to also qualify for the knockout stage as one of the best third-placed teams. World Cup debutants Jordan finished last with no points and were eliminated.

On June 22, [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) scored his 17th World Cup goal in Argentina's win against Austria, surpassing [Miroslav Klose](201840.md) (16) as the FIFA World Cup's [all-time top goalscorer](43255516.md). Messi's six goals across the three group matches brought his overall tally to 19 across the six World Cups he had participated in.[^cite_note-3]

### Argentina vs Algeria

Argentina had faced Algeria previously only once, a 4–3 friendly win in 2007.[^cite_note-9] This fixture marked Algeria's first return to the FIFA World Cup since [2014](656933.md), when they reached the round of 16.

With his [hat-trick](411541.md), [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) equalled [Miroslav Klose](201840.md) as the [all-time top-scorer](43255516.md) of the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md), with 16 goals.[^cite_note-10]

Messi also became [the first male player to play in six World Cups](26472070.md#Tournaments) and [the second player to score in five World Cups](43255516.md#Goalscorers_at_multiple_tournaments).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] At 38 years and 357 days old, he also became the oldest player to score a World Cup hat-trick.[^cite_note-13]

### Argentina vs Austria

The teams had met on two occasions, both friendlies, with the most recent being a 1–1 draw in 1990.[^cite_note-19]

In the 8th minute, Argentina were awarded a penalty after [Stefan Posch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Posch) fouled [Lautaro Martinez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lautaro_Martinez) in the box, [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) took the penalty but fired wide low to the right of the post. In the 38th minute, Messi scored with a first-time left-foot finish from just inside the penalty area to the left corner after a low cross from the left by [Facundo Medina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facundo_Medina). In the 95th minute, Messi scored again to make it 2–0 when his initial shot was blocked he followed up to hit low to the net from a tight angle on the left past two Austrian defenders.[^cite_note-20]

In scoring both of Argentina's goals, Messi surpassed [Miroslav Klose](201840.md) to become the [all-time leading World Cup goalscorer](43255516.md) with 18 goals.[^cite_note-21] He was named Man of the Match and became the third player to score in six consecutive World Cup matches.

Algeria and Austria warming up before the match

### Jordan vs Argentina

The two teams had never met before. The fixture featured two teams that had both reached finals in FIFA-organized tournaments at the [Lusail Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusail_Stadium) in Qatar. This was the same venue where Argentina won their [last World Cup](70432805.md) on penalties against France, and Jordan competed in the [2025 FIFA Arab Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_FIFA_Arab_Cup_final), losing 3–2 to [Morocco](1024865.md).

Argentina came out strong despite having already secured first place, with two goals scored [Giovani Lo Celso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovani_Lo_Celso) and [Lautaro Martínez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lautaro_Mart%C3%ADnez) in the first half. [Musa Al-Taamari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musa_Al-Taamari) then made it 1–2 in the 55th minute. However, with [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) subbed in, he scored from a free kick, marking his sixth goal overall at this World Cup.

With this result, defending champions Argentina completed Group J with a perfect 3–0–0 record. In contrast, tournament debutants Jordan suffered three straight defeats, making them, along with [Uzbekistan](1032413.md) in [group K](81660204.md), the only debutant teams to finish without a single point.

## Discipline

The team conduct ("fair play") score would have been used as a tiebreaker if the head-to-head and overall records of teams were tied. It would also be used as a tiebreaker for the [third-place ranking](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams) between groups if the overall records of teams were tied. The score was calculated based on yellow and red cards received by players and team officials in all group matches as follows:[^cite_note-regulations-2]

- yellow card: −1 point;

- indirect red card (second yellow card): −3 points;

- direct red card: −4 points;

- yellow card and direct red card: −5 points;

Only one of the above deductions could be applied to a player or team official in a single match.
