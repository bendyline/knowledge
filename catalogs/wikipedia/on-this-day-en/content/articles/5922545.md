The **Holy Trinity Cathedral of Tbilisi** ([Georgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_language): თბილისის წმინდა სამების საკათედრო ტაძარი *Tbilisis tsminda samebis sakatedro tadzari*), commonly known as **Sameba** ([Georgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_language): სამების ლავრა for [Trinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Trinity)), is the main [cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_church_architecture) of the [Georgian Orthodox Church](433340.md) located in [Tbilisi](42298.md), the capital of [Georgia](48768.md). Constructed between 1995 and 2004, it is the [third-tallest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tallest_Orthodox_churches) Eastern Orthodox cathedral in the world and one of the [largest religious buildings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_largest_church_buildings_in_the_world) in the world by total area. Sameba is a synthesis of traditional styles dominating the Georgian church architecture at various stages in history and has some [Byzantine undertones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_architecture).

## History and construction



Sameba seen in the Elia neighbourhood along with the [Ceremonial Palace of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceremonial_Palace_of_Georgia)

The idea to build a new cathedral to commemorate 1,500 years of the [autocephaly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autocephaly) of the [Georgian Orthodox Church](433340.md) and 2,000 years from the birth of [Jesus](1095706.md) emerged as early as 1989, a crucial year for the national awakening of the then-[Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [Republic of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic). In May 1989, the Georgian Orthodox Church and the authorities of Tbilisi announced an international contest for the "Holy Trinity Cathedral" project. No winner was chosen at the first round of the contest when more than a hundred projects were submitted. Finally, the design by architect [Archil Mindiashvili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archil_Mindiashvili?action=edit\&redlink=1) won the contest. The subsequent turbulent years of [civil unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_civil_war_of_1991%E2%80%931993) in Georgia deferred this grandiose plan for six years, and it was not until 23 November 1995, that the foundation of the new cathedral was laid.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-Shevardnadze2018-2]

The construction of the cathedral was proclaimed as a "symbol of the Georgian national and spiritual revival" and was sponsored mostly by anonymous donations from several businessmen and common citizens. On 23 November 2004, on [St. George's Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_George's_Day), the cathedral was consecrated by [Catholicos Patriarch of Georgia](435047.md) [Ilia II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilia_II) and high-ranking representatives of fellow Orthodox Churches of the world. The ceremony was also attended by leaders of other religious and confessional communities in Georgia as well as by political leaders.[^cite_note-Sameba.-4][^cite_note-Shevardnadze2018-2]

At least part[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] of the site chosen for the new cathedral complex included land within what had once been an old Armenian cemetery called [Khojavank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_Pantheon_of_Tbilisi).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-Burford-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-armenianow_2007-10][^cite_note-noravank-11] The cemetery once had an Armenian church that was destroyed during the Soviet period by the orders of [Lavrenti Beria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavrenti_Beria). Most of the cemetery's gravestones and monuments were also destroyed and the cemetery was turned into a recreational park. However, the cemetery still contained many of its graves when construction of the Sameba Cathedral commenced.[^cite_note-12] The cemetery was treated with a "scandalous lack of respect" according to one author,[^cite_note-Burford-8] after bones and gravestones appeared scattered all over the construction site.[^cite_note-armenianow_2007-10][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

## Architecture



[Dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dome) of the cathedral

The Sameba Cathedral is erected on the [Elia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elijah) Hill, which rises above the left bank of the [Kura River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kura_\(Caspian_Sea\)) (Mtkvari) in the historic neighborhood of [Avlabari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avlabari) in [Old Tbilisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Tbilisi).

Designed in a traditional Georgian style but with a greater vertical emphasis, and "regarded as an eyesore by many people, it is equally venerated by as many others".[^cite_note-15] The cathedral has a [cruciform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruciform) plan with a [dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dome) over a [crossing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossing_\(architecture\)) resting on eight columns. At the same time, the parameters of the dome is independent from the [apses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apse), imparting a more monumental look to the dome and the cathedral in general. The dome is surmounted by a 7.5-meter-tall (25 ft) gilded gold cross.



Cathedral interior

The cathedral consists of nine [chapels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapel) (chapels of the [Archangels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archangel), [John the Baptist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_the_Baptist), [Saint Nino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Nino), [Saint George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_George), [Saint Nicholas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Nicholas), the [Twelve Apostles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve_Apostles), and [All Saints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glorification#Eastern_Orthodox_Church)); five of them are situated in a large, underground compartment. The cathedral mainly consists of two parts — above-ground and underground sections. The overall height from the zero level to the top of the cross is 86.1 metres (the cross itself is 7.5 metres tall). The underground section of the Lavra has two floors. The foundation of the cathedral is at a depth of 14.07 metres, and the floor of the lower level is at 13.07 metres (plus 1 metre for the foundation). According to these measurements, the total height of the Holy Trinity Cathedral is 100.17 metres (86.10 + 14.07). The cathedral's length is 70.45 metres, and its width is 64.68 metres. The total area of the cathedral is approximately 5,000 square metres.[^cite_note-Shevardnadze2018-2]

Natural materials are used for construction. The floor is made of marble tiles and the [altar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altar) will also be decorated with [mosaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosaic). The painting of the murals is being executed by a group of artists guided by [Amiran Goglidze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amiran_Goglidze?action=edit\&redlink=1).

The Sameba complex, the construction of which is already completed, consists of the main cathedral church, a free-standing bell-tower, the residence of the Patriarch, a monastery, a clerical [seminary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seminary) and theological academy, several workshops, places for rest, etc.

## Historic events



Sameba Cathedral at dusk

In early January 2024, the discovery of an icon featuring Saint [Matrona of Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrona_of_Moscow), a Russian Orthodox saint, blessing [Stalin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stalin) sparked uproar.[^cite_note-SovLab-RFE-16] The presence of Stalin on an icon has been described as unusual by theologians.[^cite_note-MythDetect-17] The icon featuring Stalin has been donated by members of the conservative [Alliance of Patriots of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_of_Patriots_of_Georgia).[^cite_note-Politico-18] On 10 January, the icon featuring Stalin was defaced with blue paint, in an incident that drew press attention, and subsequent protests.[^cite_note-defacingRFE-19]
