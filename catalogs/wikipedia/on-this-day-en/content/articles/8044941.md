**Shamsiddin Shohin District** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Район Шамсиддин Шохин; [Tajik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajik_language): Ноҳияи Шамсиддин Шоҳин *Nohiyai Shamsiddin Shohin*, before 2016 *Shuroobod District* [Tajik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajik_language): Ноҳияи Шуро-обод),[^cite_note-2] is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Tajikistan) in [Khatlon Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khatlon_Region), southeastern [Tajikistan](30108.md). Its capital is the village [Shuroobod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuroobod).

Shuroobod District was renamed [Shamsiddin Shohin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamsiddin_Shohin) in memory of this Tajik poet.

## Geography

Shamsiddin Shohin District is bordered in the north by [Darvoz District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darvoz_District) and [Khovaling District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khovaling_District), in the west by [Mu'minobod District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu'minobod_District) and [Hamadoni District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamadoni_District), all in [Khatlon Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khatlon_Region). In the east and south, across the river [Panj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panj_\(river\)), it borders Afghanistan. It is located in the foothills of the Hazrati Shoh mountains.
