**Nariño** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[naˈɾiɲo\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a [department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Colombia) of [Colombia](5222.md) named after independence leader [Antonio Nariño](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Nari%C3%B1o). Its capital is [Pasto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Juan_de_Pasto). It is in the west of the country, bordering [Ecuador](9334.md) and the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md).

Nariño has a diverse geography and varied climate according to altitude: hot in the plains of the Pacific and cold in the mountains, where most of the population resides, a situation that is repeated in a north-south direction. Other important cities include [Tumaco](4513756.md) and [Ipiales](4513789.md).

The territory was occupied during the [Pre-Columbian era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-columbian_America) by numerous Indian tribes, including Quillacingas, [Awá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awa-Kwaiker), Pasto, and Tumas.&#x20;

The first European [conquistador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conquistador) who entered the territory was [Andagoya Pascual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andagoya_Pascual?action=edit\&redlink=1) in 1522, who traveled from the Colombian Pacific coast and then used information obtained by [Francisco Pizarro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Pizarro) to organize the expedition that culminated in the conquest of [Peru](170691.md).

[Juan de Ampudia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_de_Ampudia) and [Pedro de Añazco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_de_A%C3%B1azco) first explored the mountainous part of the department, commissioned by [Sebastián de Belalcázar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebasti%C3%A1n_de_Belalc%C3%A1zar) in 1535, who then toured the territory in 1536 and reached Popayán and remained for some time before leaving for Spain.

### Major municipalities

#### Municipal population position

According to the latest census conducted in 2018, 1,335,521 people live in Nariño.

The city of Pasto and the municipalities of [Chachagüí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chachag%C3%BC%C3%AD), [Nariño](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nari%C3%B1o,_Nari%C3%B1o), [Tangua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangua), [Chía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C3%ADa,_Cundinamarca), [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid,_Cundinamarca) and [Yacuanquer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yacuanquer) form a single metropolitan area.

## Administrative divisions



Nariño Subregions

Nariño is composed of thirteen subregions and 64 municipalities organized among them:

- [Centro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subregi%C3%B3n_del_Centro_\(Nari%C3%B1o\)?action=edit\&redlink=1): [Pasto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasto), [Chachagüí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chachag%C3%BC%C3%AD), [La Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Florida,_Nari%C3%B1o), [Nariño](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nari%C3%B1o,_Nari%C3%B1o), [Tangua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangua,_Nari%C3%B1o) and [Yacuanquer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yacuanquer).

- [Juanambú](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subregi%C3%B3n_de_Juanamb%C3%BA?action=edit\&redlink=1): [Arboleda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arboleda), [Buesaco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buesaco), [La Unión](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Uni%C3%B3n,_Nari%C3%B1o), [San Pedro de Cartago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro_de_Cartago) and [San Lorenzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Lorenzo,_Nari%C3%B1o).
