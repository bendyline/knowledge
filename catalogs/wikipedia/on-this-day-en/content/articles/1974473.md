The **minister of home affairs** or the **home minister** is the head of the [Ministry of Home Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Home_Affairs_\(India\)) of the [Government of India](553883.md). One of the senior-most portfolios in the [Central Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_India), the chief responsibility of the home minister is the maintenance of the [internal security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_security) of [India](14533.md); the country's large police force comes under its jurisdiction. Occasionally, they are assisted by the minister of state of home affairs and the lower-ranked deputy minister of home affairs.

Ever since the time of independent India's first home minister [Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardar_Vallabhbhai_Patel), the office has been seen as second in seniority only to the [prime minister](24452.md) in the [Central cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_cabinet). Like Patel, home ministers [Yashwantrao Chavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yashwantrao_Chavan) and [L. K. Advani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L._K._Advani) have since held the additional portfolio of [deputy prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_India). As of today, three home ministers have gone on to become the prime minister: [Lal Bahadur Shastri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lal_Bahadur_Shastri), [Charan Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charan_Singh) and [P. V. Narasimha Rao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._V._Narasimha_Rao). [Amit Shah](28165549.md) is the current and the longest serving home minister in [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_India_\(1947%E2%80%93present\)) from 30 May 2019 till date.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

On several occasions, the prime minister also held the additional portfolio of the minister of home affairs. [Jawaharlal Nehru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawaharlal_Nehru) briefly acted as home minister in 1950 following the demise of Vallabhbhai Patel, [Indira Gandhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indira_Gandhi) briefly served as home minister in 1966 following resignation of [Gulzarilal Nanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulzarilal_Nanda), [Morarji Desai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morarji_Desai) held the portfolio from 1978 till 1979, [Chandra Shekhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandra_Shekhar) throughout his prime ministerial tenure and [H. D. Deve Gowda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._D._Deve_Gowda) briefly served as home minister in 1996.

[P. Chidambaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._Chidambaram) who served as the home minister from 2008 till 2012 has the rare distinction of serving as both cabinet minister and minister of state in the ministry. He served as minister of state from 1986 till 1989. The current minister is [Amit Shah](28165549.md) of the [Bharatiya Janata Party](149330.md) who has been in office since 30 May 2019 and succeeded his party colleague [Rajnath Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajnath_Singh) as the home minister.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]
