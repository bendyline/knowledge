The **Legislative Assembly of Rio de Janeiro** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Assembleia Legislativa do Estado do Rio de Janeiro* or *ALERJ*) is the [unicameral](204304.md) legislature of the state of [Rio de Janeiro](229419.md) in [Brazil](3383.md). It has 70 state deputies elected by [proportional representation](23298.md).

After the 1834 Additional Act, the Provincial Assembly was founded in the city of Vila Real de Praia Grande (now [Niterói](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niter%C3%B3i)), after the unification of the states of Guanabara and Rio de Janeiro on March 15, 1975 the assembly was moved to the [Tiradentes Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiradentes_Palace) in the city of [Rio de Janeiro](25936.md) (site of the [Chamber of Deputies of Brazil](1589485.md) until 1960).
