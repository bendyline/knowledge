**Mamou Region** ([Pular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pular_language): 𞤁𞤭𞥅𞤱𞤢𞤤 𞤃𞤢𞥄𞤥𞤵𞤲) is located in central [Guinea](12176.md). It is bordered by the country of [Sierra Leone](27308.md) and the Guinean regions of [Faranah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faranah_Region), [Labé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lab%C3%A9_Region), and [Kindia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kindia_Region).

## Administrative divisions

Mamou Region is divided into three prefectures; which are further sub-divided into 36 sub-prefectures:

- [Dalaba Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalaba_Prefecture) (10 sub-prefectures)

- [Mamou Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamou_Prefecture) (14 sub-prefectures)

- [Pita Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pita_Prefecture) (12 sub-prefectures)
