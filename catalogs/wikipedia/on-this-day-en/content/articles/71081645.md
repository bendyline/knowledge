The **2026 Women's FIH Hockey World Cup** was the 16th edition of the [Women's FIH Hockey World Cup](6516384.md), the quadrennial world championship for women's national [field hockey](10886.md) teams organized by the [International Hockey Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Hockey_Federation). It was held from 15 to 29 August 2026 in [Wavre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavre), Belgium and [Amsterdam](844.md), Netherlands.

Belgium and Netherlands were given the hosting rights in November 2022, beating bids from [Australia](4689264.md), [South Africa](17416221.md) and [Uruguay](31843.md) in the process. This was the second time the women's event was co-hosted after [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Women's_FIH_Hockey_World_Cup). This also marked Belgium's first and Netherlands' fifth time hosting. This was also the first time the men's and women's world cup were held as one joint tournament.

16 teams participated for the third time after the expansion in 2018. Qualification was an amalgamation of the [FIH Pro League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_FIH_Pro_League), continental championships and the [World Cup Qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Women's_FIH_Hockey_World_Cup_Qualifiers). Qualification took place from December 2023 to March 2026 to decide the qualifiers.

[Netherlands](7325406.md) were the defending champions after defeating the [Argentina](3642950.md) 3–1 at the 2022 final in [Terrassa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrassa). Argentina returned the favour and won after penalties against the Netherlands to win their third title. [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium_women's_national_field_hockey_team) beat [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_women's_national_field_hockey_team) 1–0 to win their second bronze medal, the first since the [1978 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_Women's_Hockey_World_Cup).[^cite_note-Final-1]

## Host selection

Main article: [2026 Women's FIH Hockey World Cup bidding process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Women's_FIH_Hockey_World_Cup_bidding_process)

The [International Hockey Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Hockey_Federation) announced on 15 June 2022 that they had received the following three bids for holding only women's 2026 World Cup:[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

-  [Australia](4689264.md)

-  [South Africa](17416221.md)

-  [Uruguay](31843.md)

The following two bids were received for holding combined women's and [men's world cup](71081638.md) simultaneously:

-  [Belgium](3343.md) and  [Netherlands](21148.md)

-  [South Africa](17416221.md)

Wavre, Belgium and Amstelveen, Netherlands were awarded the hosting rights on 3 November 2022.[^cite_note-4] This was the Netherlands' fifth time hosting after [1986](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Women's_Hockey_World_Cup), [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Women's_Hockey_World_Cup), [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Women's_Hockey_World_Cup), and [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Women's_FIH_Hockey_World_Cup). This was Belgium's first time hosting.

Just as in 2018 and 2023, 16 teams competed in the tournament. Alongside hosts, [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium_women's_national_field_hockey_team) and [Netherlands](7325406.md), the top-ranked team in the 2023–24 and 2024–25 seasons in the [FIH Pro League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_FIH_Pro_League) and the five continental champions receive an automatic berth. The other 16 teams will compete for the last seven places in the World Cup Qualifiers. Of the qualified teams, 14 took part in the previous edition.

**Returnees**

- [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland_women's_national_field_hockey_team) returns after last taking part in [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Women's_Hockey_World_Cup).

- [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_women's_national_field_hockey_team) qualified after missing out in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Women's_FIH_Hockey_World_Cup).

**Absentees**

- [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea_women's_national_field_hockey_team) failed to qualify for the first time since [1986](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Women's_Hockey_World_Cup), having made the previous nine editions.

- [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_women's_national_field_hockey_team) didn't advance after making their first appearance since 1994 in 2022.

The highest ranked team to not qualify was [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_women's_national_field_hockey_team), ranked 16th, while the lowest ranked team to qualify was [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa_women's_national_field_hockey_team), ranked 18th.

## Draw



The [Wagener Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagener_Stadium) in [Amstelveen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amstelveen) hosted the draw.

The final draw took place at 15\:00 [CET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Time) on 17 March 2026 at the [Wagener Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagener_Stadium) in [Amstelveen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amstelveen), Netherlands.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] Model and presenter, [Joyce Colfs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyce_Colfs?action=edit\&redlink=1), hosted the draw. Field Hockey players, [Naomi van As](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_van_As), [Barbara Nelen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Nelen) and [Teun de Nooijer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teun_de_Nooijer), alongside Dutch rapper [La Fuente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Fuente), were the guests who assisted the draw. The draw started with the co-hosts being placed into their respective groups held in their country, as well as the two remaining pot 1 teams, [Argentina](3642950.md) and [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_women's_national_field_hockey_team), being pre-drawn into groups B and D respectively. The draw continued with, in order, pots 4, 3 and 2 being drawn, with each team selected then allocated into the first available group alphabetically. The position for the team within the group would then be drawn (for the purpose of the schedule). There were no restrictions.
