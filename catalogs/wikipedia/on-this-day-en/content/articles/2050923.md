**Tillabéri** (var. *Tillabéry*) is one of the seven [regions of Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Niger). The capital of the region is [Tillabéri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tillab%C3%A9ri). Tillabéri Region was created in 1992, when [Niamey](57061.md) Region was split, with Niamey and its immediate hinterland becoming a new *capital district* enclaved within Tillabéri Region.[^cite_note-3]

## Geography

Tillabéri borders [Mali](19127.md) ([Gao Region](2349862.md)) to the north, [Tahoua Region](2000318.md) to the east, [Dosso Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dosso_Region) to the southeast, [Benin](3459.md) ([Alibori Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alibori_Department)) to the south, and [Burkina Faso](3470.md) ([Sahel Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahel_Region) and [Est Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Est_Region_\(Burkina_Faso\))) to the west. The [Niamey Capital District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niamey_Capital_District) forms an [enclave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enclave) within the region. Tillabéri contains almost all of Niger's share of the [Niger River](21437.md), as well as several seasonal (known as Gorouol, Sirba) and permanent (known as Mékrou, Tapoa) watercourses. The [W National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_National_Park) is located in the extreme south of the region and extends into Burkina Faso and Benin. The northwestern areas of the region (Ouallam and Filingué) have a [savannah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savannah) type flora and fauna.

### Settlements

Tillabéri is the regional capital; other major settlements include [Abala](25547392.md), [Ayourou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayourou), [Banibangou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banibangou), [Bankilare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bankilare), [Filingué](14102366.md), [Ouallam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouallam), [Say](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Say,_Niger), [Téra](3512699.md) and [Torodi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torodi).[^cite_note-4]

## Climate

Tillabéri has a [hot arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_climate#Hot_desert_climates) (*BWh* in the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification)) despite receiving almost 400 millimetres or 16 inches of rainfall per year, due to the extreme heat and high evaporation.

## Economy

The economy of the region of Tillabéri is based primary on agriculture, livestock and fishery production. However, Tillabéri is rich in mineral resources (gold and iron ore) and increasingly becoming attractive for future mining investments. In 2004, the first gold mine in [Téra](3512699.md) began operation. In addition, the region of Tillabéri has great touristic potential with [W National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_National_Park), the Niger river and many more attractions.

### Agriculture, livestock and fishery

Based on data from the National Statistics Institute of Niger, The region of Tillaberi is 1st producer of rice (5,700 tonnes), 5th for sorghum (40,900 tonnes), 5th for millet (39,940 tonnes), 3rd for corn (1,100 tonnes), 5th for black-eyed peas (15,300 tonnes) and 5th in peanut (2,400 tonnes) in 2011 among regions.[^cite_note-9] It is also an important livestock producer and the 1st producer of cattle with recorded 2087 thousand cattle heads in 2011.[^cite_note-10] Although the Niger river is crossing through this region, it is only the 3rd producer of fishery products with 637 thousand tonnes in 2011.[^cite_note-11]

### Mining

The region is home to the [Samira Hill Gold Mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samira_Hill_Gold_Mine) in [Téra](3512699.md), which opened in 2004.[^cite_note-12] In addition to [gold](12240.md), the region is rich in [iron ore](145352.md) with estimated reserves of 650 million tonnes in Say.[^cite_note-13]

## Tourism



Elephants in W Park

The region of Tillabéri has many tourist sites. The [W National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_National_Park), which straddles the [tri-border area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripoint) of Benin-Burkina Faso-Niger, is classified as a [World Heritage Site](44940.md) by [UNESCO](21786641.md).[^cite_note-list-14] The park contains a wide variety of fauna and flora on side located in Niger. The region has a modest hospitality infrastructure with only two 4-star hotels and 137 rooms (42 rooms for the 4 star hotels).[^cite_note-15]

## Crime

Tillabéri is badly affected by the [insurgency in the Maghreb](13153771.md). Major attacks occurred in [January 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Chinagodrar), [May 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_2020_Tillab%C3%A9ri_attacks), [August 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kour%C3%A9_shooting), [January 2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Tillab%C3%A9ri_attacks) and [March 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fambita_mosque_attack).

In September 2025, twenty-two people were killed in the Tillabéri region, during and after a baptism.[^cite_note-16]
