**Mucuri** is a city in the state of [Bahia](199070.md) in Brazil. The estimated population in 2020 is 42,251 inhabitants. It is the southernmost city in Bahia as well as Brazil's [Northeast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_Region,_Brazil) region, and the only one from that state to border [Espírito Santo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esp%C3%ADrito_Santo).

The city contains part of the 1,503.75-hectare (3,715.8-acre) [Córrego Grande Biological Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%B3rrego_Grande_Biological_Reserve). The climate is tropical, warm and mostly humid, with one or two dry months. Average annual temperature is 22 to 24 °C (72 to 75 °F), and average annual rainfall is 1,250 to 1,500 millimetres (49 to 59 in).[^cite_note-4]

Mucuri is served by [Max Feffer Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mucuri_Airport).
