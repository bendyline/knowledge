**Nation Media Group** (**NMG**), formerly known as **East African Newspapers (Nation Series) Ltd**, is an [East African](260987.md) media group based in Kenya and listed on the [Nairobi Stock Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nairobi_Stock_Exchange). It is owned by [Rostam Azizi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostam_Azizi).

## History

NMG was founded in 1959 by [Aga Khan IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aga_Khan_IV) as East African Newspapers (Nation Series) Ltd,[^cite_note-2] under the [Aga Khan Fund for Economic Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aga_Khan_Fund_for_Economic_Development). In 1999, NMG launched [NTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NTV_\(Kenya\)), a news channel in Kenya, and [Easy FM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nation_FM_\(Kenya\)) (now known as Nation FM).[^cite_note-akfed-3]

## Media outlets

As of 2007 the group was the largest private media house in [East](260987.md) and [Central Africa](539606.md), with offices in Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania.[^cite_note-akfed-3]

The group publications include:

- *[The EastAfrican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_EastAfrican)*

- *[Daily Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Nation)*

- *[Business Daily Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_Daily_Africa)*

- *[Daily Monitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Monitor)*

- *[The Citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Citizen_\(Tanzania\))*

- *NMG Investor Briefing*

- *[Taifa Leo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taifa_Leo)*

- *MyNetwork*.

The *Daily Nation* and its Sunday edition, the *Sunday Nation*, celebrated their [50th anniversaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Jubilee) in 2010, branded by the Nation Media Group as "50 Golden Years".[^cite_note-4]In 2016, *MyNetwork* replaced *Zuqka*, with both having the youth as their target audience.[^cite_note-5]

**Regional Presence**

As of 2016 NMG owned a 76.5% stake in the [Monitor Publications Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Monitor) and 93.3 KFM, a [Kampala](56689.md)-based radio station in Uganda. It also owns two television stations in the country, [NTV Uganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NTV_Uganda) and Spark TV.[^cite_note-Grow-6] NMG also has a 60% shareholding in *Mwananchi Communications Limited'* in Tanzania. The group's East African subsidiaries, especially the Tanzanian business, contribute significantly to growth in the group.[^cite_note-Grow-6]

In March 2016, NMG commissioned a new state-of-the art printing press on Mombasa Road in Nairobi. The new facility has capacity to print 86,000 newspapers per hour. It cost KSh2 billion (about US\$20 million) and will print the dailies *Daily Nation*, *Business Daily*, *Taifa Leo* and the weekly *The EastAfrican*.[^cite_note-7]

## Shareholding

The shares of [stock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_capital) of the Group are listed at the Nairobi Stock Exchange[^cite_note-8] and are cross-listed at the [Uganda Securities Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uganda_Securities_Exchange) as well as the [Rwanda Stock Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rwanda_Stock_Exchange), trading at all three bourses under the symbol "NMG".[^cite_note-9] As of December 2014, there were a total of 10,436 shareholders in the company. Of these, 4,135, owned 1 to 500 shares each, with a cumulative total of 782,157 shares, controlling 0.41 percent of the company. The seven largest shareholders controlled 62.57 percent of the total shareholding. These included:

- [Rostam Azizi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostam_Azizi) with 44.66 percent.

- Alpine Investments Limited with 10.15 percent.

- [National Social Security Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Social_Security_Fund_\(Kenya\)) with 3.44 percent.

- John Kibunga Kimani who had 1.54 percent shareholding.

- [Jubilee Insurance Company of Kenya Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jubilee_Insurance_Company_Limited) with 1.07 percent.

- Standard Chartered Nominees A/c KE17984, who controlled 0.91 percent

- CfC Stanbic Nominee Limited with 0.81 percent shareholding.
