**Mali–Russia relations** are the [bilateral relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilateral_relations) between [Mali](19127.md) and [Russia](25391.md).

## History

### Soviet-era relations (1960–1991)



Ambassador Bréhima Coulibaly presenting his [letter of credence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_of_credence) to President [Dmitry Medvedev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Medvedev), 16 January 2009

The [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) recognised the independence of Mali on 7 July 1960, and [diplomatic relations](28165911.md) between the two states were established on 14 October 1960.[^cite_note-1] Following the collapse of the [Mali Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mali_Federation), and due to [French](5843419.md) support for [Senegal](27278.md), [Modibo Keïta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modibo_Ke%C3%AFta), the first [President of Mali](430390.md), sought closer ties with the Soviet Union.[^cite_note-bingen-2] In 1961 the two countries signed trade and cultural pacts,[^cite_note-3] and the Soviet Union granted Mali loans and other aid,[^cite_note-bingen-2] which included the acquisition of two [Ilyushin Il-18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilyushin_Il-18) [passenger aircraft](51215.md) for [Air Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Mali_\(1960%E2%80%931985\)).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] Under the cultural agreement Russia sent circus performers, sports coaches and a soccer team to Mali.[^cite_note-6]

When Keïta was overthrown by [Moussa Traoré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moussa_Traor%C3%A9) by a [coup d'état](25914154.md) in 1968, Traoré improved relations with [France](5843419.md) and other Western countries, but Mali remained dependent on the Soviet Union for the arming and training of the [Malian Armed Forces](19135.md). Approximately 50 Soviet [military advisors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_advisor) provided armour, artillery and parachute training to Mali's military, and trained all of Mali's pilots.[^cite_note-state-7] The Soviets also improved the Malian Air Force base in [Mopti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mopti), and occasionally used Malian airfields to stage supply flights for groups it supported in [Angola](701.md).[^cite_note-state-7]

On 16 January 1992, Mali recognised the [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation) as the [successor state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Successor_state) of the Soviet Union, after the latter's [dissolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Soviet_Union).[^cite_note-malimid-8]

### Military rule in Mali (2020–present)



[Assimi Goïta](65000306.md) with [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) in St. Petersburg, Russia, 29 July 2023



[Malick Diaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malick_Diaw) with Putin's close associate [Vyacheslav Volodin](32671558.md) in Moscow, Russia, 19 March 2023

In 2020, a [coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Malian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) in Mali deposed President [Ibrahim Boubacar Keïta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Boubacar_Ke%C3%AFta) and replaced his government with a [junta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Committee_for_the_Salvation_of_the_People) led by Colonel [Assimi Goïta](65000306.md). Russian involvement in the coup has been speculated.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] The Malian government has increasingly allied with Russia since Goïta [assumed direct control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Malian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) in 2021.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

Mercenaries from the Russian [private military company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_military_company) [Wagner Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagner_Group) began arriving in Mali in late 2021,[^cite_note-14] followed by Russian military equipment donations and advisors throughout 2022 and 2023.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] In November 2022, Minister of Economy and Finance [Alousséni Sanou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alouss%C3%A9ni_Sanou?action=edit\&redlink=1) said Russia was committed to send 60,000 tonnes of petroleum products, 30,000 tonnes of fertiliser and 25,000 tonnes of wheat, for a total worth of around \$100 million.[^cite_note-17]

In February 2023, Russia's foreign minister [Sergey Lavrov](541346.md) met Malian military junta leaders to ensure his country's support to Mali against the [Islamist insurgency in the Sahel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamist_insurgency_in_the_Sahel).[^cite_note-18] Goïta was one of the few heads of state present at the [2023 Russia–Africa Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Russia%E2%80%93Africa_Summit).[^cite_note-\:0-19]

According to the [United States Department of State](31975.md), the Malian junta is suspected of routing foreign military purchases to Wagner, which were "intended for use in Ukraine" during the [ongoing invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine).[^cite_note-\:0-19][^cite_note-20]

On September 27, 2024, Russian Foreign Minister [Sergey Lavrov](541346.md) and Foreign Minister of Mali [Abdoulaye Diop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdoulaye_Diop_\(diplomat\)) signed a statement on non-deployment of weapons in [space](177602.md).[^cite_note-21]

## Diplomatic relations

In 2003, meetings were held by Alexander Makarenko, Director of the Africa Department of the [Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs), with N. L. Traoré, Secretary General of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Mali\)).[^cite_note-22] In 2005 Anatoly Safonov, the Special Presidential Representative for International Cooperation in the Fight Against Terrorism and Cross-Border Organized Crime, met from 25 January 2005 through 28 January 2005 in [Bamako](56606.md) with his Malian counterpart.[^cite_note-23]

## Diplomatic missions



Embassy of Mali, Moscow

Russia has an [embassy](8970.md) in [Bamako](56606.md), and Mali has an embassy in [Moscow](19004.md). The current [Russian ambassador to Mali](81739314.md) is [Igor Gromyko](83061253.md), since 17 June 2019.[^cite_note-24] The current Malian ambassador to Russia is Seydou Kamissoko, who was nominated on 24 May 2023.[^cite_note-25]
