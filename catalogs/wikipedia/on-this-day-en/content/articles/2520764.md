# Foreign hostages in Iraq

|  | This article needs to be **updated**. Please help update this article to reflect recent events or newly available information. *(June 2017)* |
| --- | --- |

&#x20;Members of the [Iraqi insurgency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_insurgency_\(2003%E2%80%932011\)) began taking foreign hostages beginning in April 2004. Since then, they have captured more than 200 foreigners and thousands of [Iraqis](4184636.md); among them, dozens of hostages were killed and others rescued or freed. In 2004, executions of captives were often filmed, and many were [beheaded](70802.md).[^cite_note-SRB_Nov_30-1] The number of recorded killings later decreased significantly, but many hostages remain missing. In the summer of 2004, the [United States Department of State](31975.md) set up the [Hostage Working Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hostage_Working_Group), organized by the [U.S. Embassy in Baghdad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Embassy_in_Baghdad), to monitor foreign hostages in Iraq.

The motives for these kidnappings include:

- influencing foreign governments with troops in Iraq to withdraw

- influencing foreign companies with workers in Iraq to leave the country

- demanding [ransom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ransom) money

- creating terror in Iraq

- discouraging travel to Iraq

- [prisoner exchange](17209653.md)

The following is a list of known civilian foreign nationals who have been taken hostage in Iraq.
