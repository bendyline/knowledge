The **2025 Nobel Peace Prize**, an international peace prize established according to the will of [Alfred Nobel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Nobel),[^cite_note-nobelwill-2] was announced on 10 October 2025 by the [Norwegian Nobel Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Nobel_Committee) in [Oslo](22309.md), Norway. It was awarded to [María Corina Machado](5791389.md) of [Venezuela](32374.md). The prize was presented at the ceremony on 10 December 2025 in Oslo, given to her daughter on her behalf.[^cite_note-announcement-3][^cite_note-4] The Norwegian Nobel Committee praised her as "one of the most extraordinary examples of civilian courage in [Latin America](18524.md) in recent times".[^cite_note-UPI-5]

At the time of the prize's announcement, Machado was in hiding inside Venezuela, fearing repression from the government of [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md). She secretly escaped the country to reach Oslo with the help of international allies, but vowed to return to Venezuela to continue opposing the Maduro regime.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Machado said she dedicated her award to [Donald Trump](4848272.md) and after the [2026 United States intervention in Venezuela](82021123.md) she gave him her Nobel medal as a recognition of his commitment to Venezuela's freedom, according to Machado.[^cite_note-\:1-8]

## María Corina Machado

Main article: [María Corina Machado](5791389.md)

Machado was born in [Caracas](55289.md), Venezuela, in 1967. She established the Atenea Foundation in 1992 to help children in Caracas. In 2002, she was one of the founders of [Súmate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%BAmate), an electoral monitoring group. Machado was an elected member of the [National Assembly of Venezuela](510768.md) from 2010 to 2014 when she was expelled by [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md)'s government. In 2023, she announced her candidacy for the [2024 presidential election](60825396.md) but she was blocked from running and supported the alternative candidacy of [Edmundo González](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmundo_Gonz%C3%A1lez). Opposition parties mobilized to systematically document and monitor the election. Their results showed González as the winner, but Maduro's administration declared victory instead.[^cite_note-9]

Machado is the [second Venezuelan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Venezuelan_Nobel_laureates) to receive a Nobel Prize after Venezuelan-born American [Baruj Benacerraf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baruj_Benacerraf), who shared the [Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine](52502.md) with [Jean Dausset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Dausset) and [George Davis Snell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Davis_Snell) in 1980.

## Award ceremony

### Before the ceremony and extraction from Venezuela

[Kristian Berg Harpviken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristian_Berg_Harpviken), director of the [Norwegian Nobel Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Nobel_Institute), confirmed with Maria Corina Machado on 5 December that she would attempt to attend the prize award ceremony on 10 December.[^cite_note-10]

According to a later account reported by *[The Wall Street Journal](173070.md)*, on 5 December, an operation funded by private donors contacted the Grey Bull Rescue Foundation based in the U.S. state of Florida, specialized in evacuation missions from warzones like [Afghanistan](737.md) and the [Gaza Strip](12047.md), to carry out Machado's extraction from Venezuela.[^cite_note-Leary-2025-11] The extraction mission was reportedly code-named Operation Golden Dynamite in reference to [Alfred Nobel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Nobel), inventor of dynamite and founder of the Nobel Prize. The final extraction plan consisted on transporting Machado through the country, to a beach, and then by boat reach [Curaçao](65205.md), in order to take a flight to Oslo, but the expedition faced several complications.[^cite_note-Leary-2025-11]

According to the same account, on 8 December Machado travelled by land through Venezuela in disguise, wearing a wig, crossing 10 military checkpoints without getting caught.\[^cite_note-[www.bbc.com-2025-12](http://www.bbc.com-2025-12/)] Operatives took her to a fishing village to catch a rundown fishing boat. On 9 December, the boat had mechanical issues which delayed departure for 12 hours, leaving at sunset. Due to severe weather, Machado and the operatives encountered waves reported to be approximately 3 m (10 ft) high, and members of the crew suffered from [motion sickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_sickness). During the trip communications were reportedly lost, the [Global Positioning System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Positioning_System) (GPS) was thrown overboard and the backup systems malfunctioned. The meeting point with the Grey Bull rescue vessel was in the middle of the [Gulf of Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Venezuela), which the boat finally reached after reestablishing communications at 23\:00.[^cite_note-13] From there, Machado was taken to [Curaçao](65205.md). Grey Bull contacted the authorities but the Dutch embassy refused to take part in the operation and Machado was only allowed to remain for 24 hours on the island.[^cite_note-Leary-2025-11] The Norwegian newspaper *[Aftenposten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftenposten)* reported later that Machado had fractured a [vertebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertebra) during the trip.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

On 9 December, the Nobel Institute canceled that evening's scheduled press conference with Machado, as her whereabouts were unknown at the time.[^cite_note-guard-9dec2025-16]

Machado reportedly went through customs in Curaçao and took a private jet to [Miami](53846.md) on the morning of 10 December, and at 6\:42[^cite_note-17] took a plane to Oslo.[^cite_note-Leary-2025-11] Most details of the operation became public only after the ceremony, when *The Wall Street Journal* published a detailed account of the extraction.[^cite_note-Fouche-2025-18] [Jørgen Watne Frydnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%B8rgen_Watne_Frydnes) from the Norwegian Nobel Committee described the journey as "a situation of extreme danger".\[^cite_note-[www.bbc.com-2025-12](http://www.bbc.com-2025-12/)]

### During the ceremony



Ana Corina Sosa Machado accepting the Prize on behalf of her mother, 10 December 2025

On the day of the ceremony, because of concerns regarding her safety, at first it was revealed that Machado would not have been able to attend the award ceremony,[^cite_note-19] but hours later it was announced then that she would have arrived later in Oslo anyway, skipping the ceremony.[^cite_note-20] Her daughter, Ana Corina Sosa Machado, was present at the award ceremony and accepted the award on behalf of her mother, also delivering a speech written by the latter.[^cite_note-Fouche-2025-18]

The speech covered the history of Venezuela. From independence, to the oil industry in the 20th century to the present day. Focus was given to the dismantling of democracy by [Hugo Chávez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Ch%C3%A1vez) and Nicolás Maduro, and the [Venezuelan refugee crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_refugee_crisis). The speech also recounted the events of the [2024 Venezuelan presidential election](60825396.md) where González won with 67 percent of the vote yet Maduro responded with "[state terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_terrorism)". The speech ends naming those who shared the prize with her according to Machado, including the political prisoners, persecuted families, journalists, and human rights defenders.[^cite_note-21]

The ceremony was attended by [Edmundo González](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmundo_Gonz%C3%A1lez) and by Argentinian president [Javier Milei](56599840.md), Ecuadorian president [Daniel Noboa](73980059.md), Panamanian president [José Raúl Mulino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Ra%C3%BAl_Mulino) and Paraguayan president [Santiago Peña](58414156.md).[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] This was the largest number of heads of state to attend a Nobel ceremony since the [2012 Nobel Peace Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Nobel_Peace_Prize) awarded to the [European Union](9317.md).[^cite_note-guard-9dec2025-16]

The Venezuelan singer [Danny Ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Ocean_\(singer\)) and Venezuelan pianist [Gabriela Montero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriela_Montero) performed during the award ceremony.[^cite_note-Civita-2025-24] Ocean played a song that blended "[Alma llanera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alma_llanera)", considered popularly as the second anthem of Venezuela, and "[Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuela_\(song\)?action=edit\&redlink=1)", which has become popular with the Venezuelan diaspora.[^cite_note-Civita-2025-24] Montero played the national anthem "[Gloria al Bravo Pueblo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_al_Bravo_Pueblo)", as well as [Simón Díaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sim%C3%B3n_D%C3%ADaz)'s "Mi querencia".[^cite_note-Civita-2025-24]

### After the ceremony



Machado arriving at Oslo

Machado arrived to Oslo late at night after the award ceremony. She was received by crowd at the [Grand Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Hotel_\(Oslo\)), where Nobel laureates are hosted.[^cite_note-Fouche-2025-18] She addressed the crowd with a brief speech.[^cite_note-Fouche-2025-18]

On 11 December, the Nobel Committee held a press conference with Machado.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-France_24-2025-26] She vowed to return to Venezuela and "to end with this tyranny very soon and have a free Venezuela".[^cite_note-France_24-2025-26][^cite_note-27] Machado also expressed support for the [Trump administration](78298108.md)'s push to oust Maduro.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

The same day Machado opened the 2025 Nobel Peace Prize Exhibition, exposing pictures of the [Venezuelan refugee crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_refugee_crisis) in Colombia during 2025, taken by Turkish photographer [Emin Özmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emin_%C3%96zmen).[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31] Venezuelan-born Swedish singer [Omar Rudberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Rudberg) performed the song "Venezuela" during the opening.[^cite_note-32]

## Nobel medal aftermath



U.S. president [Donald Trump](4848272.md) is gifted the Nobel Peace Prize medal awarded to [María Corina Machado](5791389.md) at the White House Oval Office on 15 January 2026.

Following the [United States intervention in Venezuela](82021123.md) on 3 January 2026, Machado offered to share her Nobel prize with U.S. president Donald Trump, to thank him on behalf of the Venezuelan people. She said in an interview "What he has done is historic. It's a huge step towards a democratic transition." The Nobel Prize Committee rejected her request and clarified that the prize "cannot be revoked, shared or transferred".[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] The Nobel Committee has reiterated that while ownership of the physical award can change, the title does not; therefore Trump is not a Nobel Peace Prize laureate.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]

On 15 January 2026, Machado had a lunch meeting with Trump in which she presented her Nobel Prize medal as a gift in recognition of what she called his commitment to the freedom of the Venezuelan people.[^cite_note-ReutersMedal-37][^cite_note-38] Trump confirmed he would keep the medal, writing in [Truth Social](69077351.md) "Maria presented me with her Nobel Peace Prize for the work I have done. Such a wonderful gesture of mutual respect. Thank you Maria!"[^cite_note-ReutersMedal-37] The White House posted a photo with Machado and Trump holding a frame containing the medal with the legends "To President Donald J. Trump In Gratitude for Your Extraordinary Leadership in Promoting Peace through Strength," and "Personal Symbol of Gratitude on behalf of the Venezuelan People."[^cite_note-ReutersMedal-37] Speaking to reporters, Machado compared the handover of her medal to [Marquis de Lafayette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marquis_de_Lafayette) handing over a gold medallion with the face of [George Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington) to Venezuelan independence hero [Simón Bolívar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sim%C3%B3n_Bol%C3%ADvar) in 1825, as "a sign of the brotherhood between the people of the U.S. and the people of Venezuela in their fight for freedom against tyranny".[^cite_note-\:1-8]

## Reactions

### To the announcement

#### Venezuela

On [Twitter](9988187.md), Machado dedicated the prize to the "suffering people of Venezuela" and to U.S. president [Donald Trump](4848272.md) "for his decisive support of our cause".[^cite_note-39] In Venezuela, state-run media ignored the Nobel Peace Prize announcement or downplayed its significance, stating that the Norwegian Nobel Foundation was controlled by the "international right" and alleging Machado had plotted a coup d'état.[^cite_note-40]

On 13 October, the Venezuelan foreign ministry announced the closure of the embassy of Venezuela in Norway. Oslo called the decision "regrettable".[^cite_note-41]

In November, Venezuela's attorney general [Tarek William Saab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarek_William_Saab) expressed that Machado would become a fugitive if she attended the award ceremony in Norway.[^cite_note-guard-9dec2025-16]

#### International

See also: [Dear Jonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dear_Jonas)

In the United States, officials of the [second Trump administration](78298108.md) were strongly critical of the award not being given to Trump, who for months had expressed his desire to win the prize.[^cite_note-42] [Steven Cheung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Cheung), the [White House Communications Director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Communications_Director), stated, "The Nobel Committee proved they place politics over peace."[^cite_note-Guardian_WhiteHouse_slams_snub-43] [Richard Grenell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Grenell), Trump's envoy for Venezuela, declared that the "Nobel Prize died years ago".[^cite_note-44] President Trump himself praised Machado following her selection,[^cite_note-45] and Machado said that Trump was a worthy winner for the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize.[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48] Sources close to Trump later told *[The Washington Post](102226.md)* that her decision to accept the award effectively destroyed Trump's support for her possible presidency; after the [U.S. capture of Nicolás Maduro](82021123.md), one of those sources said that, if Machado had "turned it down and said, 'I can't accept it because it's Donald Trump's,' she'd be the president of Venezuela today."[^cite_note-49] Trump cited his perceived snub as a factor in [his escalation of efforts to acquire Greenland on behalf of the United States](82044223.md), writing in [a letter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dear_Jonas) to Norwegian prime minister [Jonas Gahr Støre](2926932.md) on 18 January 2026 that "Considering your Country decided not to give me the Nobel Peace Prize for having stopped 8 Wars PLUS, I no longer feel an obligation to think purely of Peace, although it will always be predominant *\[emphasis in original]*."[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52]

Former U.S. president and Nobel Peace laureate [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama) congratulated Machado on X and said that the award should remind the U.S. of the "responsibility to constantly preserve and defend our own hard-won democratic traditions".[^cite_note-53] According to *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)*, the Nobel Prize committee's references to [backsliding democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backsliding_democracy) in its announcement, such as "When authoritarians seize power, it is crucial to recognise courageous defenders of freedom who rise and resist", while contextually referring to [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md) and his actions in the [2024 Venezuelan presidential election](60825396.md), were also seen by critics of Trump as "a not-too-subtle dig at the U.S. president's [use of the military in its cities](80838047.md) and [pressure on his political enemies at home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Targeting_of_political_opponents_and_civil_society_under_the_second_Trump_administration)".[^cite_note-Guardian_WhiteHouse_slams_snub-43]

Argentine president [Javier Milei](56599840.md) celebrated the recognition for Machado and congratulated her for her "enormous fight for the brave defense of freedom and democracy", adding "you \[Machado] light up the world by fighting the narcodictatorship in Venezuela".[^cite_note-54] French president [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md) called Machado to congratulate her for the award.[^cite_note-55][^cite_note-56] German chancellor [Friedrich Merz](1967626.md) congratulated Machado on as "democracy thrives on the courage of individuals".[^cite_note-DW101025-57]

The [Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Europe) congratulated Machado for being one of the "most extraordinary examples of civilian courage in Latin America in recent times".[^cite_note-58] President of the [European Commission](9974.md) [Ursula von der Leyen](2991932.md) celebrated Machado's recognition, saying that the award recognises her courage as well as "every voice that refuses to be silenced".[^cite_note-DW101025-57][^cite_note-59] The [Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights](22463.md) and [António Guterres](471806.md), secretary-general of the [United Nations](31769.md), congratulated Machado, stating that "this recognition reflects the clear aspirations of the people of Venezuela for free and fair elections, for civil and political rights and for the rule of law".[^cite_note-60]

The [Council on American–Islamic Relations](159138.md) (CAIR) released a statement on [X](9988187.md) condemning the decision as "unconscionable", citing Machado's support for [Likud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Likud) and association with far-right European politicians such as [Geert Wilders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geert_Wilders) and [Marine Le Pen](572897.md), while adding, "The Nobel Peace Prize committee should instead recognize an honoree who has shown moral consistency by bravely pursuing justice for all people, such as one of the students, journalists, activists, medical professionals who have risked their careers and even their lives to oppose the crime of our time: the [genocide in Gaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genocide_in_Gaza)."[^cite_note-61][^cite_note-62] Colombian president [Gustavo Petro](13391962.md) also questioned the awarding of the Nobel to Machado, citing her past outreach to then Argentinian president [Mauricio Macri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauricio_Macri) and Israeli prime minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) in seeking support for her campaign to oust Maduro.[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64]

On 17 December 2025, founder of [Wikileaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikileaks), [Julian Assange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_Assange) announced he had filed a lawsuit against the Nobel Foundation to prevent the disbursement of funds to Machado, stating that her support for [U.S. military actions against Venezuela](80987786.md) contradicts the principles of the Peace Prize. He said that the award represents "gross misappropriation" and the "facilitation of war crimes" under Swedish law.[^cite_note-65]

### Demonstrations

Supporters of Maria Corina Machado gathered in various demonstrations worldwide on 6 December 2025 in support to her award and demanding a "Free Venezuela".[^cite_note-Cano-2025-66] Thousands of people gathered in [Buenos Aires](255919.md), [Bogotá](211271.md), [Lima](85423.md), [Madrid](41188263.md) and [Utrecht](50333.md).[^cite_note-Cano-2025-66]

## Alleged internal leak

On 10 October 2025, the Nobel Institute began an investigation into a potential internal leak of the prize-winner's name after two Norwegian newspapers, *[Aftenposten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftenposten)* and *[Finansavisen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finansavisen)*, reported on suspicious surges in the online betting markets the night before that led to Machado's odds of winning rising from 3.75% to 72.8% in a matter of hours. Institute director [Kristian Berg Harpviken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristian_Berg_Harpviken) said, "It seems we have been prey to a criminal actor who wants to earn money on our information."[^cite_note-67][^cite_note-68] Bets raised to US\$2.2 million but no media published the name of the candidate ahead of time.[^cite_note-digitalspy-69]

The Norwegian Nobel Committee attributed the leak to digital hacking on 30 January 2026. The secretary Harpviken said "We have not been able to determine exactly what happened or who was behind the breach but we do believe that the digital domain still is the main suspect".[^cite_note-digitalspy-69]

## Other candidates

Although nominations are strictly kept a secret by the committee itself for 50 years, it is not uncommon for candidacies to be announced publicly by either nominators or nominees.[^cite_note-70] On 5 March 2025, the [Norwegian Nobel Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Nobel_Committee) announced that 338 candidates had been nominated for the 2025 Nobel Peace Prize: 244 individuals and 94 organizations.[^cite_note-71]
