**Isfahan province** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): استان اصفهان)[^cite_note-5] is one of the 31 [provinces of Iran](448160.md). Its capital is the city of [Isfahan](223149.md).[^cite_note-Isfahan_Province_Structure-6]

The province is in the center of the country in Iran's [Region 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Iran),[^cite_note-7] whose secretariat is located in Isfahan.

Isfahan Province is located along the eastern foothills of the [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains). It covers both mountainous terrain and arid plains, contributing to its diverse climate and natural resources.[^cite_note-8]

## History

Historians have recorded *Espahan*, *Sepahan* or *Isfahan* initially as a defense and military base. Historical castles were *Atashgah*, *Sarooyieh*, *Tabarok*, *Kohan Dej*, and *Gard Dej*. The oldest is *Ghal'eh Sefeed* and the grounds at *Tamijan* from prehistoric times. The historic village of [Abyaneh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abyaneh), a nationwide attraction, also has [Sasanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasanian_Empire) ruins and [fire temples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_temple) among other historical relics.

During the 17th and 18th centuries, Isfahan province became the capital of [Safavid Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safavid_Iran). While the city of Sepahan (Esfahan) was their seat of monarchical, [Kashan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashan) was their place of vacation and leisure.

A majority of the population speak [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) as first language with a minority of Luri, Turkic, Georgian, Biyabanaki and Armenian speakers.[^cite_note-language-9]

During the Pahlavi era, a large group of Kurds from the Gulbaghi tribe were moved from the north of [Kurdistan province](448112.md) to the city of Isfahan and the cities of Yazd, Kashan, and Nayin. Today, the Gulbaghi tribe are mostly assimilated elements in the population of these cities.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the province's population was 4,499,327 in 1,223,684 households, with a literacy rate of 88.65 percent.[^cite_note-2006_Isfahan_Province-12] The following census in 2011 counted 4,879,312 people in 1,454,162 households.[^cite_note-2011_Isfahan_Province-13] The 2016 census measured the population of the province as 5,120,850 in 1,607,482 households.[^cite_note-2016_Isfahan_Province-2]

## Geography

The Isfahan province covers an area of approximately 107,018 square km and is situated in the center of Iran. To its north, stand the [Markazi (Central) Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markazi_province) and the provinces of [Qom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qom_province) and [Semnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semnan_province). To its south, it is bordered by the provinces of [Fars](448049.md), and [Kohgiluyeh and Boyer-Ahmad province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohgiluyeh_and_Boyer-Ahmad_province). [Aminabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aminabad,_Shahreza) is the most southern city of Isfahan province just 2 km north of the border. To the east, it is bordered by the province of [Yazd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yazd_province). To the west, it shares its border with the province of [Lurestan](433072.md) and to the southwest lies the province of [Chahar Mahal and Bakhtiyari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chahar_Mahal_and_Bakhtiyari).

The province experiences a moderate and dry climate on the whole, ranging between 40.6 °C (105.08 °F) and 10.6 °C (51 °F) on a cold day in the winter season. The average annual temperature has been recorded as 16.7 °C (62 °F) and the annual rainfall on average has been reported as 116.9 mm. The city of Sepahan (Esfahan) however experiences an excellent climate, with four distinct seasons.

With an elevation of 4,040 metres, the [Shahankuh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahankuh) is the highest peak in Isfahan province. This mountain is located about 20 kilometres southwest of the city of [Fereydunshahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fereydunshahr) in the western part of the province.

Isfahan province has 52 rivers, mostly small and temporary, with the exception of the [Zāyanderud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zayandeh_Rud), which totals 405 km in length a basin area of 27,100 km2.[^cite_note-29]

### Biodiversity

*Esmaeilius isfahanensis* (Farsi: کپوردندان_اصفهان) is a species of [Cyprinodontid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprinodontiformes) fish endemic to the [Zayandehrud river basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zayanderud) (see genus *[Esmaeilius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esmaeilius)*). It is known only from three sites across the province.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33] *Allium chlorotepalum* is an endemic species of Isfahan province, *Astragalus vernaculus* is also common in the west of the province.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35]
