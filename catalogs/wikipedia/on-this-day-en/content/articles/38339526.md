The **1994 Oklahoma Sooners baseball team** represented the [University of Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma) in the [1994 NCAA Division I baseball season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_NCAA_Division_I_baseball_season). The Sooners played their home games at [L. Dale Mitchell Baseball Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L._Dale_Mitchell_Baseball_Park). The team was coached by [Larry Cochell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Cochell) in his 4th season at Oklahoma.

The Sooners won the [College World Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_NCAA_Division_I_baseball_tournament#College_World_Series), defeating the [Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Georgia_Tech_Yellow_Jackets_baseball_team) in the championship game.
