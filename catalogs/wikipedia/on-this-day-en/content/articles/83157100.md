An election for the [president](8108148.md) and [president pro tempore of the Senate of the Philippines](8750570.md) was suddenly held on May 11, 2026. It was the third leadership election of the [Senate](299521.md) in the [20th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_Congress_of_the_Philippines) and the 56th election for the Senate presidency in the chamber's history.

The election removed incumbent Senate president [Tito Sotto](3873709.md), who was replaced by [Senate Minority Leader](8767721.md) [Alan Peter Cayetano](10220741.md), triggering a [leadership crisis and dispute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Philippine_Senate_leadership_dispute) that would later last for a month.

## Background

Further information: [Second impeachment of Sara Duterte](82338655.md)

On September 8, 2025, Senate Minority Leader Sotto replaced incumbent [Francis Escudero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Escudero), and senators [Panfilo Lacson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panfilo_Lacson) and [Juan Miguel Zubiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Miguel_Zubiri) were elected as [president pro tempore](8750570.md) and [majority floor leader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majority_Floor_Leader_of_the_Senate_of_the_Philippines), respectively.[^cite_note-1] Escudero was removed due to links with [flood control projects](80804811.md).[^cite_note-2] The senators of the minority bloc after that election were called the "[Duterte bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diehard_Duterte_Supporters)".[^cite_note-3] As early as February 2026, Sotto said that the majority had blocked an alleged attempt by the minority to remove him as the Senate President.[^cite_note-4] Sotto confirmed that senator [Loren Legarda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loren_Legarda) would be the next Senate president before the 20th Congress ends in 2028.[^cite_note-5] In May 2026, rumors were rising that the minority was seeking to replace Sotto as the [House of Representatives](293605.md) was going to vote on the articles of impeachment against [Vice President](2271639.md) [Sara Duterte](42504861.md) and send the case for trial.[^cite_note-6] On May 10, 2026, Sotto confirmed that there was a plan to remove him as president of the Senate.[^cite_note-7]

## Electoral system

This section is an excerpt from [President of the Senate of the Philippines § Election](8108148.md#Election).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=President_of_the_Senate_of_the_Philippines\&action=edit)]

The Senate president is elected by a majority vote of the members of the Senate from among themselves.[^cite_note-President_of_the_Senate_of_the_Philippines_\:12-8] Since there are 24 senators, 13 votes are needed to win the Senate presidency, including any vacant seats or senators not attending the session. Although Senate presidents are elected at the start of each Congress, there had been numerous instances of Senate leadership elections in which a sitting Senate president has been unseated in the middle of a session.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] Term-sharing agreements among senators who are both eyeing the position of the Senate president also played a role in changing the leadership of the Senate, but in a smooth manner, through the [peaceful transition of power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peaceful_transition_of_power). Three known instances were in 1999, 2006, and 2018.[^cite_note-President_of_the_Senate_of_the_Philippines_\:3-11][^cite_note-President_of_the_Senate_of_the_Philippines_\:4-12]

Newly elected Senate presidents are traditionally sworn in by the youngest senator, known as the "[Benjamin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_of_the_House#Philippines)" of the chamber, usually one belonging to the majority bloc, as in the cases of [Bam Aquino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bam_Aquino) administering the oath of office to [Franklin Drilon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Drilon) in 2013, [Manny Pacquiao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manny_Pacquiao) to [Koko Pimentel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koko_Pimentel) in 2016, [Mark Villar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Villar) to [Francis Escudero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Escudero) in 2024, and [Camille Villar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camille_Villar) to [Alan Peter Cayetano](10220741.md) in 2026.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

Unlike the symbolic presiding officers of upper houses in some other governments, the Senate president of the Philippines has considerable power to influence the legislative agenda, and has a normal vote rather than acting only as a tie-breaker, though the tradition that the presiding officer is the last to vote has been maintained. A tied vote in the Senate therefore means that the motion is lost; the Senate president, having already voted in the normal way, cannot also [cast a tie-breaking vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casting_vote).

### Return of Ronald dela Rosa

Main article: [Attempts to arrest Ronald dela Rosa](83172418.md)

On May 11, 2026, after months long of absence, senator [Ronald dela Rosa](50966529.md), who suddenly showed up in the Senate, arrived at the plenary hall and was furious due to him being prevented by [National Bureau of Investigation](2489044.md) (NBI) agents from entering the ongoing session.[^cite_note-16] This came after rumors that the [International Criminal Court](14880.md) (ICC) had filed an arrest warrant due to dela Rosa's links to the [Philippine drug war](51032591.md).[^cite_note-17] dela Rosa was also set to be [subpoenaed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subpoena) by the [Criminal Investigation and Detection Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_Investigation_and_Detection_Group) (CIDG) on the same day as part of an ongoing investigation.[^cite_note-18]

### Leadership election

On May 11, 2026, Cayetano manifested that there were enough votes to support a leadership change which were thirteen "Duterte-aligned" senators led by Cayetano.[^cite_note-auto-19] Immediately after, session was suspended. When it was later resumed, Senate Deputy Minority Leader [Joel Villanueva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Villanueva) moved to declare all electable seats in the Senate to be vacant. The motion was approved after the voting with 13 senators in favor, 10 against, and one abstaining.[^cite_note-\:0-20] Senator [Imee Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imee_Marcos) then nominated Cayetano as the new Senate president.[^cite_note-21] Villanueva who was designated as acting majority leader attempted to move to close the nomination but Lacson stood and nominated Sotto for Senate president, causing a two-way race.[^cite_note-\:0-20]

## Aftermath

| \[icon] | This section **needs expansion**. You can help by [adding missing information](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=May_2026_President_of_the_Senate_of_the_Philippines_election\&action=edit\&section=). *(May 2026)* |
| --- | --- |

After Cayetano's victory, Senator Legarda was then elected as Senate president pro tempore, a position she previously held in the [19th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19th_Congress_of_the_Philippines).[^cite_note-26] Dela Rosa was then placed under Senate protection against NBI agents trying to serve his warrant of arrest allegedly issued by the ICC,[^cite_note-27] which were later confirmed to be true.[^cite_note-28] CCTV footage of dela Rosa running away from NBI agents was then revealed, resulting in dela Rosa spending the night at the Senate office.[^cite_note-29] Senators Sotto, Hontiveros, Aquino, Pangilinan, and Lacson signed a resolution on May 12, 2025, urging dela Rosa to surrender to authorities.[^cite_note-30] Alan Peter Cayetano then said that the senate will only accept arrest warrants from Philippine courts.[^cite_note-31] On May 13, 2026, the CIDG withdrew its subpoena on dela Rosa.[^cite_note-32] On the same day, the Senate was placed under lockdown after gunshots were heard when there were [attempts to arrest dela Rosa](83172418.md).[^cite_note-33] Senate President Cayetano later confirmed dela Rosa had escaped the premises of the Senate.[^cite_note-34] Senators [Ejercito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JV_Ejercito) and [Zubiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Miguel_Zubiri) later joined the minority on May 18, 2026.[^cite_note-35] On May 20, 2026, Senator [Pia Cayetano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pia_Cayetano), sister of Alan Peter, turned emotional during a plenary session and confronted her fellow senators of the current minority bloc that they did not ask how members of the majority were doing during the shooting incident.[^cite_note-36] On May 23, 2026, the minority bloc, now called the "Solid Bloc 11" or "SB−11" officially endorsed senator [Sherwin Gatchalian](22207546.md) for Senate president.[^cite_note-37]
