[Albania](738.md) is a [unitary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitary_state) [parliamentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system) [constitutional republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic), in which the [president of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Albania) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and the [prime minister of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Albania) is the [head of government](37570.md) in a [multi-party system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party_system). The executive power is exercised by the [Government](67576.md) and the prime minister with its [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Albania). Legislative power is vested in the [Parliament of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania). The judiciary is independent of the executive and the legislature. The political system of Albania is laid out in the [1998 constitution](7878011.md).[^cite_note-1] The Parliament adopted the current [constitution](7878011.md) on 28 November 1998. Historically Albania has had many constitutions. Initially constituted as a [monarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Albania) in 1913, Albania became briefly a [republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Republic) in 1925, and then a [authoritarian monarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Kingdom_\(1928%E2%80%9339\)) in 1928. In 1939 Albania was invaded by [Fascist Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II_in_Albania) forces, imposing a [puppet state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puppet_state), and later occupied by [Nazi German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_occupation_of_Albania) forces. Following the partisan liberation from the Nazis in 1944 a [provisional government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Government_of_Albania) was formed, which by 1946 had transformed into a [communist one-party state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Socialist_Republic_of_Albania).[^cite_note-2] In March 1991 democracy was restored with multi-party elections.[^cite_note-3]

The [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Albania) represents the unity of the [Albanian people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanians) in the country and abroad as the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and is also the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Albania).[^cite_note-4] The president is nominated through a [secret vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_vote) and without debate by the [Parliament of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania) by a majority of three-fifths of all its members and is in every case elected for five years.[^cite_note-5] The president maintains regular and coordinated operation and stability of the national government system, safeguards the independence and territorial integrity of Albania and appoints prime ministers on the basis of the balance of power in the Parliament. The [prime minister](259136.md) is appointed by the president after each [parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Albania) and must have the confidence of the Parliament to stay in office. The prime minister is elected on the basis of [universal suffrage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_suffrage), through a [secret ballot](479210.md), for a four-year [term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_of_office). The constitution sets no limit as to office terms of the prime minister. The prime minister is *[de facto](43594.md)* the most powerful and influential person in Albanian politics. However, in the absence of the prime minister, the [deputy prime minister](11930399.md) takes over his functions, such as chairing the [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Albania) and the [council of ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Albania) of Albania.

The [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania) is a [unicameral legislative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicameral_legislature) body of Albania. It is vested in both the government as well as in the parliament. The number of representatives is 140, which are elected by popular vote to serve four-year terms. The oldest parliament with extant records was held on 2 March 1444, forged in [Lezhë](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lezh%C3%AB) under [Gjergj Kastrioti Skënderbeu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gjergj_Kastrioti) as the leader against the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md).[^cite_note-6] Since 1991, the introduction of pluralism, the party system is dominated by the [Democratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_of_Albania) and the post-communist [Socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Party_of_Albania). [Parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Albania) are held every four years, the most recent in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Albanian_parliamentary_election). In April 2021 parliamentary [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Albanian_parliamentary_election), ruling Socialist Party, led by Prime Minister [Edi Rama](277805.md), secured its third consecutive victory, winning nearly half of votes and enough seats in parliament to govern alone.[^cite_note-7] In February 2022, Albania's Constitutional Court overturned parliament's impeachment of President [Ilir Meta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilir_Meta), opponent of the ruling Socialist Party.[^cite_note-8]

Albania is a member state of the [North Atlantic Treaty Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Atlantic_Treaty_Organization) and also an [official candidate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accession_of_Albania_to_the_European_Union) for membership in the [European Union](9317.md).[^cite_note-9]

The [Economist Intelligence Unit](15303116.md) rated Albania a "[flawed democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy_Index#Definitions)" in 2024,[^cite_note-democracy-index-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] in conjunction with the electoral democracy score of over 0.5 given by [V-Dem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V-Dem_Democracy_Indices) in 2024.

The [Kryeministria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister's_Office_\(Albania\)) serves as the office and residence of the [Prime Minister](259136.md). It is also the seat of the [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_\(Albania\)) in [Tirana](31075.md).



The [Presidential Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Office_Building,_Tirana), the official workplace of the [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Albania).

Since the [Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Declaration_of_Independence) in 1912, [Albania](738.md) has experienced different models of governance, including an international protectorate, a monarchy, a state-party regime and the parliamentary republic as well. Throughout all this period, the function of the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) has been exercised in various forms.

Nowadays, the [President of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Albania) *(Presidenti)* is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state), [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Armed_Forces) and the representative of the unity of the [Albanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanians) people.[^cite_note-13] The president is [indirectly elected](652583.md) to a five-year [term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_of_office) by the [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania) by [secret vote](479210.md), requiring a three-fifths majority of the votes of all members. The president has the power to guarantee observation of the [Constitution](7878011.md) and all laws, act as [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Albanian Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Armed_Forces), exercise the duties of the [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania), when the Parliament is not in session, and appoint the [Prime Minister](259136.md). The president has the power to declare war, to grant pardon and to conclude agreements of peace, alliance, and participation in international organizations; upon the request of the government a simple parliamentary majority is required to ratify such actions, agreements, or treaties.

The [prime minister of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Albania) *(Kryeministri)* is the [head of government](37570.md). According to the [Constitution](7878011.md), the prime minister is the most senior minister of cabinet in the executive branch of government in the Albanian [parliamentary system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system). The prime minister is appointed by the president; ministers are nominated by the president as well on the basis of the prime minister's recommendation.[^cite_note-14] The [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_ministers_Albania) is responsible for carrying out both [foreign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Albania) and domestic policies. It directs and controls the activities of the ministries and other state organs. The [deputy prime minister of Albania](11930399.md) *(Zëvendëskryeministri)* is the [deputy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_prime_minister) [head of government](37570.md). In the absence of the prime minister, the deputy prime minister takes over his functions, such as chairing the [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Albania) and the Council of Ministers.

## Legislative

Further information: [Parliament of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania)



The Kuvendi serves as the seat of the [Parliament of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania).

The [Parliament of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania) *(Kuvendi i Shqipërisë)* is a [unicameral](204304.md) legislative body. It is composed of not less than 140 members [elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election) to a four-year [term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_of_office) on the basis of direct, universal, periodic and equal [suffrage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffrage) by [secret ballot](479210.md). As of Article 45 of the [Constitution](7878011.md), which guarantees the [right to vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_to_vote), the people of Albania exercise their power through their elected [representatives](739237.md) in the Parliament. When the Parliament is elected, the first session shall be held no later than 20 days after the completion of elections with the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Albania) as the speaker.[^cite_note-15] There are 15 permanent committees. Extraordinary meets can be called by the president, by the [Chairperson of the Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Parliament_of_Albania), by the [prime minister](259136.md) or by the one fifth of the members. Decisions are made based on a majority vote if more than half of the members are present, except in cases where the Constitution provides for a special majority. As specified by the current electoral legislation in [Albania](738.md), 140 members of the Parliament are elected in multi-seat [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Albania). Within any constituency, parties must meet a threshold of 3 percent of votes, and pre-election coalitions must meet a threshold of 5 percent of votes.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-Albania_electoral_code-17] All laws passed by the Parliament are published through the [Fletorja Zyrtare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Official_Journal), the [official journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_journal) of the government.[^cite_note-18]

The [chairperson of the Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Parliament_of_Albania) *(Kryetar i Kuvendit)* is the presiding officer of the Parliament and is elected by the members of the Parliament. The official function is to moderate [debate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debate), make rulings on procedure, announce the results of votes, decides who may speak and has the powers to discipline members who break the procedures of the chamber or house. When the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Albania) is temporarily absent or incapable of exercising its powers, the chairperson takes over his functions.[^cite_note-19]

## Judiciary

Further information: [Judiciary of Albania](49669048.md) and [Constitution of Albania](7878011.md)

Albania follows the civil law tradition, which is based on the [French law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleonic_Code) system. It has a three-tiered, independent judicial system governed by the [constitution](7878011.md) and national legislation enacted by the [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania). The judicial branch of Albania is divided into three main institutions; the 'Highest Courts', which includes the [Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Albania) (Gjykata e Lartë) and the [Constitutional Court](29207004.md) (Gjykata Kushtetuese), the 'Intermediate Courts' such as the [Courts of Appeal](49668987.md) (Gjykata e Apelit), Court of Appeals against corruption and organized crime (Gjykata e Apelit kundër Korrupsionit dhe Krimit të Organizuar), and the Administrative Court of Appeals (Gjykata Administrative e Apelit) as well as the 'First Instance Courts' such as the [District Courts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_courts_in_Albania) (Gjykata e Juridksionit të Përgjithshëm) and the Court of First Instance against corruption and organized crime (Gjykata e Shkallës së Parë kundër Korrupsionit dhe Krimit të Organizuar).

The [Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Albania) is the highest court of appeal in Albania; its hearings are open and judgments are made publicly, except in cases where the privacy of the accused is to be protected. It is composed of nineteen judges such as the president of the Supreme Court and eighteen members of the Supreme Court. The judges are selected and appointed amongst judges with more than 10 years of seniority or prominent lawyers, who have exercised their activity for more than 15 years. The members can serve for a single term of nine years, without the right of reappointment. The [Constitutional Court](29207004.md) is the final authority for the interpretation of the Constitution and the compliance of laws in accordance with the constitution.

## Elections

Further information: [Elections in Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Albania)

Following the collapse of the communist regime in the nation, the [first multi-party elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Constitutional_Assembly_election,_1991) were held in 1991, simultaneously with the [1991 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Constitutional_Assembly_election,_1991).[^cite_note-20] The 1998 [Constitution of Albania](7878011.md) guarantees the right to vote without restriction due to sex, race, social status, education level, or wealth.[^cite_note-21] Every [Albanian citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanians) who has reached the age of 18 has the right to vote.[^cite_note-22] Regular elections take place for the office of the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Albania), [parliamentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_elections_in_Albania), county prefects, county assemblies, city and municipal mayors and city and municipal councils.

The president is elected through a [secret vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_vote) and without debate by the [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania) by a majority of three-fifths majority of all its members, for five years.[^cite_note-23] The [Constitution](7878011.md) sets a limit to a maximum of two terms in office.[^cite_note-24] The Parliament is elected to a four-year term in twelve multi-seat constituencies analogous to the 12 [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Albania) borders, with amendments to achieve a uniform number of eligible voters in each constituency to within 3%. Within the constituencies, seats are elected by [closed list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_list) [proportional representation](23298.md), with an [electoral threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_threshold) of 3% for parties and 5% for alliances.[^cite_note-25] Seats were allocated to alliances using the [d'Hondt system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D'Hondt_system), then to political parties using the [Sainte-Laguë method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sainte-Lagu%C3%AB_method).[^cite_note-ipu-26]

The county prefects and city and municipal mayors are elected to four-year terms by majority of votes cast within applicable local government units. A runoff election is held if no candidate achieves a majority in the first round of voting. Members of county, city, and municipal councils are also elected to four-year terms through [proportional representation](23298.md).

## Foreign policy

Further information: [Foreign relations of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Albania)



The countries with [diplomatic missions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_missions_of_Albania) of Albania.

Foreign policy is conducted through the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Albania\)) in [Tirana](31075.md). Albania maintains a network of 50 diplomatic missions abroad and holds relations with more than 115 countries.[^cite_note-27] It is a member of the [North Atlantic Treaty Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Atlantic_Treaty_Organization) (NATO), [United Nations](31769.md) (UN), [Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Europe), [International Monetary Fund](15251.md) (IMF), [World Bank](36752.md) and several others. Citizens of the country have visa-free or visa on arrival access to 98 countries and territories, with the [Albanian passport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_passport) ranking 55th in terms of travel freedom according to the [Henley visa restrictions index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_\(document\)#Visa_restrictions).[^cite_note-28] Today, Albania maintains strong diplomatic relations with such countries as [the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania-United_States_relations), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania-Italy_relations), [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania-Brazil_relations), [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania_Greece_relations), [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania-Austria_relations), [Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania_Croatia_relations), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania_China_relations), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania-Germany_relations), [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania-Portugal_relations), [the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania_-_United_Kingdom_relations), [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albania_Turkey_relations), [Kuwait](7515890.md) and [Switzerland](26748.md).

Since the [collapse of communism](17545990.md) in 1990, the country has extended its responsibilities and position in European and international affairs, supporting and establishing friendly relations with other nations. The main objectives are the [accession of Albania to the European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accession_of_Albania_to_the_European_Union); the [international recognition of Kosovo](15808914.md); the recognition of the [expulsion of Cham Albanians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expulsion_of_Cham_Albanians);[^cite_note-29] and helping and protecting the rights of Albanians in [Montenegro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanians_in_Montenegro), [Macedonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanians_in_Macedonia), [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanians_in_greece), [southern Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanians_in_south_Serbia), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arb%C3%ABresh%C3%AB_people) and the [Albanian diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_diaspora).

Furthermore, the country became one of the first [eastern European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Europe) countries to join the [North Atlantic Treaty Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Atlantic_Treaty_Organization). Politicians of Albania considered admission to NATO as a top priority for the country. Since 1992, it has been extensively engaged with NATO, and has maintained its position as a stability factor and a strong ally of the [United States](3434750.md) and [European Union](9317.md) in the Balkans. At the April 2008 summit in [Bucharest](36877.md), [NATO](21133.md) agreed to the accession of Albania and Croatia. One year later in April 2009, both countries joined the alliance. Although, the country received candidate status for the [European Union](9317.md) membership in 2014, based on its 2009 application.[^cite_note-30] Today, Albania plays a central role in the negotiation of the [Berlin Process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_Process).

## Administrative divisions

Main articles: [Counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Albania) and [Municipalities of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Albania)

With a total area of 28,748 square kilometres (11,100 square miles), Albania is divided into twelve [administrative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Albania) counties (*[Albanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_language): *Qarqe**). The counties are further subdivided into 61 [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Albania) (*_Bashkia_*). In addition, the counties were further subdivided in 36 districts (*_Rrethe_*), which became defunct in 2000.[^cite_note-terref-31]

The counties were created on 31 July 2000 to replace the thirty-six former [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Albania).[^cite_note-32] The government introduced a new administrative division to be implemented in 2015, whereby municipalities were reduced to 61, while rural ones called (*_Komuna_*) are abolished. The defunct municipalities are known as Neighborhoods or Villages (*_Lagje or Fshat_*).[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] There are overall 2980 villages or communities in the entire country, formerly known as localities (*_lokalitete_*). The municipalities are the first level of local governance, responsible for local needs and [law enforcement](11802631.md).[^cite_note-35] As part of the reform, major town centers in the country were physically redesigned and façades painted to reflect a more Mediterranean look.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37]
