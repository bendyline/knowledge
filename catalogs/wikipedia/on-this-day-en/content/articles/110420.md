**Davisboro** is a city in [Washington County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_County,_Georgia), [Georgia](48830.md), United States. Per the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), the population was 1,832.[^cite_note-4]

## History

A post office called Davisboro(ugh) has been in operation since 1821.[^cite_note-5] The community most likely was named after a pioneer settler with the surname Davis.[^cite_note-6] The [Georgia General Assembly](426277.md) incorporated the place in 1894 as the "Town of Davisboro".[^cite_note-7]

On May 18, 1922, [Charles Atkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynching_of_Charles_Atkins), a 15-year-old [African-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African-American) boy, was tortured and burnt alive by a white mob some 2,000 people strong after killing a white woman.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTE''New_York_Times'',_May_19,19221-8]

## Geography

Davisboro is located at [32°58′48″N 82°36′32″W  /  32.98000°N 82.60889°W  / 32.98000; -82.60889](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Davisboro,_Georgia\&params=32_58_48_N_82_36_32_W_type\:city) (32.980128, -82.608892).[^cite_note-GR1-9]

According to the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the city has a total area of 3.1 square miles (8.0 km<sup>2</sup>), all land.

### 2020 census

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), Davisboro had a population of 1,832.[^cite_note-Census2020PL-25] The median age was 38.7 years. 5.7% of residents were under the age of 18 and 6.0% of residents were 65 years of age or older. For every 100 females there were 700.0 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 886.9 males age 18 and over.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-26]

0.0% of residents lived in urban areas, while 100.0% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-Census2020DHC-27]

There were 151 households in Davisboro, of which 36.4% had children under the age of 18 living in them. Of all households, 29.8% were married-couple households, 19.9% were households with a male householder and no spouse or partner present, and 49.0% were households with a female householder and no spouse or partner present. About 26.5% of all households were made up of individuals and 14.6% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-26]

There were 177 housing units, of which 14.7% were vacant. The homeowner vacancy rate was 2.7% and the rental vacancy rate was 0.0%.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-26]

## Business

Built by [Jacoby Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacoby_Development?action=edit\&redlink=1), the Azalea Solar Park was the largest of its kind in [Georgia](48830.md) and opened for production in November 2013. The 80 acres (32 ha) [Photovoltaic system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photovoltaic_system) produces 7.7 [megawatts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megawatts) of power using ground-mounted [photovoltaic panels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photovoltaic_panels).[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29][^cite_note-JD-30]
