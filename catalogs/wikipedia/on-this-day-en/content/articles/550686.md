**Évian-les-Bains** ([\[evjɑ̃ le bɛ̃\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/c/c9/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-Mecanautes-%C3%89vian-les-Bains.wav/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-Mecanautes-%C3%89vian-les-Bains.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/c/c9/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-Mecanautes-%C3%89vian-les-Bains.wav/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-Mecanautes-%C3%89vian-les-Bains.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>; lit. 'Évian-the-Baths'; [Arpitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arpitan_language): *Èvian*, *Évyan*, or *L'Èvian*), or simply **Évian**, is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in Eastern [France](5843419.md), by the [Swiss border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France%E2%80%93Switzerland_border). It is located in the northern part of the [Haute-Savoie](90605.md) department in the [Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes](45093325.md) region.

An upmarket holiday resort and [spa town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spa_town) on the shores of [Lake Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Geneva) ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Lac Léman*), it has been visited, over two centuries, by royalty such as Kings [Edward VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_VII) and [George V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_V) of the [United Kingdom](31717.md) and King [Farouk of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farouk_of_Egypt), and [celebrities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celebrities) such as Countess [Anna de Noailles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_de_Noailles) and [Marcel Proust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Proust).

In international relations, it is the namesake of the 1962 [Évian Accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89vian_Accords), which ended the [Algerian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algerian_War), and its Hôtel Royal hosted the [29th G8 summit](2069846.md) in 2003 and [52nd G7 summit](77292926.md) in 2026.

## History

### Birth of an elite spa town

The springs of Évian were still rather unknown at the time of the [French Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolution), but the First Empire's interest for spa towns inspired a scientist to analyse them in 1807 and 1808.



Postcard of Évian, 1907

A lakeside port and a new road (RN5) connecting the town to [Milan](36511.md) and [Paris](22989.md) were constructed in 1809. In 1823, Genevan entrepreneur M. Fauconnet launched the Évian mineral water company (*Société des Eaux Minérales d'Évian*) and in March 1827 purchased the city's two main springs, the better-known being the *Cachat* spring named after the family that sold it. M. Fauconnet's company eventually went bankrupt, and the springs were bought by the Hôtel des Bains.

In the 1818 novel *[Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankenstein)*, by [Mary Shelley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Shelley), Victor Frankenstein spends the first night of his honeymoon in Évian, but cannot prevent his monster from strangling his young wife Elizabeth.[^cite_note-3] Mary Shelley travelled through Évian in 1817 with her partner, the famous English poet [Percy Bysshe Shelley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percy_Bysshe_Shelley). In a letter, the impression the town leaves on him is rather bad: "The appearance of the inhabitants of Evian is more wretched, diseased and poor, than I ever recollect to have seen."[^cite_note-4]

In the following years, many hotels (Hôtel des Quatre saisons, Hôtel de France, Hôtel des Alpes) were built, helping the town's popularity as a holiday resort.

### Golden Age of the Belle Époque and the Roaring Twenties



The Cachat spring



Entrance of the Lumière brothers' villa

The Cachat mineral waters SA (*Société Anonyme des Eaux Minérales de Cachat*) was created as an [anonymous company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.A._\(corporation\)) in December 1859 by Parisian investors, selling Évian water, and in 1865 the small town changed its name to Évian-les-Bains to promote its rise as a spa town. Three other springs joined the Cachat (Guillot, Bonnevie, Corporau). Improvement in transportation (a railroad station) helped make the town a more famous spa.

In the late 19th century, the city contained more than 20 hotels. The hills and the lakeshore were covered with noble houses and luxurious villas and a [theatre](20913771.md) and a [casino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casino) were built on the lakeside. In 1902, the baths were constructed and in 1909, the architect Hébrard built one of the most luxurious hotels: the Royal Hotel. Évian considers itself one of the top European spas, claiming popularity with high society figures such as Countess [Anna de Noailles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_de_Noailles), [Frédéric Mistral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric_Mistral), the [Lumière brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumi%C3%A8re_brothers)[^cite_note-5] and [Marcel Proust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Proust).

After [World War I](4764461.md), the city maintained its status as a high-class spa town, hosting socialites and royalty such as [Aga Khan III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aga_Khan_III), the Maharaja of [Kapurthala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapurthala), King [George V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_V) of United Kingdom, King [Fuad I of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuad_I_of_Egypt), as well as French president [Albert Lebrun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Lebrun).

The [Évian Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89vian_Conference) was convened in Évian in July 1938 to discuss the problem of [Jewish refugees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_refugees). During [World War II](32927.md), German forces occupied Évian and the town's statue of [General Dupas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Louis_Dupas) was removed.

### Contemporary



Lakeside view of Évian

The [Évian Accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89vian_Accords), which ended the Algerian War and recognised an independent Algeria, were signed there on 18 March 1962.[^cite_note-6] The [G8](59755.md) began its [29th summit meeting](2069846.md) in Évian from 1 to 3 June 2003.[^cite_note-7] Évian hosted the [52nd G7 summit](77292926.md) from 15 to 17 June 2026, making it the first French town to host a G7 or G8 leaders' summit twice.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-ElyseeFrMain-9]

Évian still derives funding as a holiday resort and spa town.

## Geography

### Climate

The climate is continental mountainous, characterized by a marked humidity. Winters are cold and snowy and the summer season is mild with occasional stormy episodes. The off-seasons (April and October) are also on average relatively humid.

## Economy

The town is home to [Évian mineral water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evian), which adds significantly to the economy, together with the Casino d'Évian, the largest themed casino in Europe, and the Évian Royal Resort, the reported favorite holiday destination of former French President [François Mitterrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Mitterrand) and King [Farouk of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farouk_of_Egypt). Many of the inhabitants of Évian work in Lausanne and other Swiss cities nearby.

The two largest hotels in Évian are the Hotel Royal (where the G8 summit was held) and the Hilton.

## Transport



The funicular



[CGN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compagnie_G%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale_de_Navigation_sur_le_lac_L%C3%A9man) [paddle steamer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddle_steamer) *Montreux* leaving Évian-les-Bains in July 2002.

Évian is served locally by a [funicular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funicular), the [Funiculaire d'Évian-les-Bains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funiculaire_d'%C3%89vian-les-Bains?action=edit\&redlink=1),[^cite_note-remonteesmecaniques-10] which has six stations, as well as a bus network. [Évian-les-Bains station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89vian-les-Bains_station) offers regular train services to [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva), Switzerland, [Annemasse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annemasse), and [Bellegarde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellegarde-sur-Valserine), as well as less frequent services to Paris and [Lyon](8638634.md). There is also a very busy ferry service running between the town and [Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausanne) across the lake, as well as a more tourist-centred service that runs to [Yvoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yvoire). The closest international airport to Évian-les-Bains is [Geneva Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_Airport).

## Education

Public nursery/preschools and primary schools serving the town include: École du Centre, École de la Détanche, Écoles du Mur Blanc, and École des Hauts d'Évian.[^cite_note-ES-11] Collège des Rives du Léman is the public junior high school,[^cite_note-JHS-12] and [Lycée Anna de Noailles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyc%C3%A9e_Anna_de_Noailles_\(%C3%89vian-les-Bains\)) is the senior high school.[^cite_note-13]

École Saint-Bruno is a private nursery/preschool, primary school,[^cite_note-ES-11] and junior high school.[^cite_note-JHS-12]

## Sport

The town has some golf courses, and hosts [The Evian Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Evian_Championship) women's professional golf tournament, founded in 1994, every summer. Now it is one of the five women's major championships.

The [Evian Thonon Gaillard F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evian_Thonon_Gaillard_F.C.) football club was located here before relocating to [Thonon-les-Bains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thonon-les-Bains).

The *Club de l'Aviron d'Évian* is the local [rowing club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rowing_club).
