## Temporary base

For the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), [US Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Forces) relocated their armed general and members to a temporary base at [Bubiyan Island](1550688.md) named **Camp Al-Adiri** from [Camp Arifjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Arifjan).

## No longer existent

- [Camp Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Maine?action=edit\&redlink=1) (closed in 2003)

- [Camp Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Pennsylvania?action=edit\&redlink=1) (closed in 2004)

- [Camp New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_New_Jersey?action=edit\&redlink=1) (closed in 2004, combined to become part of Camp Virginia)

- [Camp New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_New_York) (closed in 2004, reactivated and deactivated several times since)

- [Camp Wolverine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Wolverine?action=edit\&redlink=1) (closed in 2005)

- Camp Victory (closed in 2006)

- [Camp Doha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Doha) (closed in 2006, forces and equipment distributed among Camps Arifjan and Buehring)

- [Camp Navistar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Navistar?action=edit\&redlink=1) (closed in 2007)

- [Camp LSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_LSA?action=edit\&redlink=1) (closed in 2013, U.S. Army administered part of Ali Al Salem Air Base)

- [Camp Amber SEMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Amber_SEMC?action=edit\&redlink=1) (unofficial closed in 2013 TS, S, C)

- [Khabari Crossing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khabari_Crossing?action=edit\&redlink=1) (unofficial closed 2011)

- [KCIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_City_International_Airport) [Camp Wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Wolf) (closed in 2005)

- [TAA Thunder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TAA_Thunder?action=edit\&redlink=1) (closed in 2003)
