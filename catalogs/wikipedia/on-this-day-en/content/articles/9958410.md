Samara

**Samara** ([Afar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afar_language): *Samara*; [Amharic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amharic_language): ሰመራ) is the capital of [Afar Region](2483633.md), [Ethiopia](187749.md). It is a town on the [Awash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awash,_Ethiopia)–[Assab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asseb) highway in north-east Ethiopia, having been planned and built to replace [Asaita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asaita). Located in [Administrative Zone 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_Zone_1_\(Afar\)), Samara has a latitude and longitude of [11°47′32″N 41°0′31″E  /  11.79222°N 41.00861°E  / 11.79222; 41.00861](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Semera\&params=11_47_32_N_41_0_31_E_). One of the completed buildings is [Samara University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samara_University_\(Ethiopia\)), which began holding classes in 2007.[^cite_note-1]

The 2006 [Lonely Planet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lonely_Planet) guide to Ethiopia had this description of Samara:

With its quirky mix of barracks, modern apartment blocks, and soulless administrative buildings, it looks like a microscopic version of [Brasília](4752.md) emerging incongruously in the middle of the desert – except that it's a completely botched attempt at creating a new town.

[^cite_note-2]

The 2009 Lonely Planet guide omitted the final phrase following the dash.[^cite_note-3] The 2002 edition of *Ethiopia: the Bradt travel guide* described Semera as consisting of "one active filling station (complete with fridge) and a cluster of modern offices and tall apartment blocks in various states of construction – all in mad isolation from any existing settlement!"[^cite_note-4]

The area is served by [Sultan Ali Mirah Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semera_Airport_\(Ethiopia\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), which has scheduled service to [Addis Ababa](52593.md).

## History

Radio Ethiopia reported that the inaugural meeting of the Afar Regional Council was held in Semera on 20 July 1995. Six days later, the Council decided to make Semera its capital city and [Amharic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amharic_language) its temporary working language. The three top officials would be President [Alimirah Hanfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alimirah_Hanfadhe), Vice-president [Osman Ainet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osman_Ainet?action=edit\&redlink=1) and Secretary [Mohammed Seid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_Seid?action=edit\&redlink=1); the Regional president at the time, [Habib Alimirah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habib_Alimirah?action=edit\&redlink=1), was not present.[^cite_note-5]

The Eighth meeting of the Afar Regional Council was held in Semera 4–5 March 2009, and items scheduled to be discussed included a bill to eliminate [female genital mutilation](11408.md) in the Region.[^cite_note-6]

## Demographics

The town had a population of 2,625 in 2007.[^cite_note-7] It is one of five towns in [Dubti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubti_\(woreda\)) [woreda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woreda).

## Climate

Semera has a [hot desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_desert_climate) (*BWh*) in the [Köppen-Geiger system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen-Geiger_climate_classification_system).
