**2031** ([MMXXXI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals)) will be a [common year starting on Wednesday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_year_starting_on_Wednesday) of the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar), the 2031st year of the [Common Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era) (CE) and *[Anno Domini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anno_Domini)* (AD) designations, the 31st year of the [3rd millennium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_millennium) and the [21st century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_century), and the 2nd year of the [2030s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2030s) decade.&#x20;
