**Stellantis N.V.** is a multinational [automotive manufacturing](163778.md) corporation formed in 2021 through the merger of the French [PSA Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PSA_Group) and Italian-American [Fiat Chrysler Automobiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat_Chrysler_Automobiles) (FCA), which was itself created by the merger of Italy's [Fiat S.p.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat_S.p.A.) and the US-based [Chrysler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrysler), completed in stages between 2009 and 2014. Stellantis is headquartered in [Hoofddorp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoofddorp), Netherlands, while the CEO now operates from [Auburn Hills, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_Hills,_Michigan), United States.[^cite_note-9]

The company designs, manufactures, and markets vehicles under 14 brands: [Abarth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abarth), [Alfa Romeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfa_Romeo), [Chrysler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrysler_\(brand\)), [Citroën](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citro%C3%ABn), [Dodge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dodge), [DS Automobiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DS_Automobiles), [Fiat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat), [Jeep](15658.md), [Lancia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancia), [Maserati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maserati), [Opel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opel), [Peugeot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peugeot), [Ram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ram_Trucks), and [Vauxhall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vauxhall_Motors). At the time of the merger, Stellantis employed approximately 300,000 people, with manufacturing operations in 30 countries and a commercial presence in over 130 markets worldwide.[^cite_note-10]

## Etymology

The company says the name *Stellantis* is derived from the Latin verb *stello* 'to brighten with stars'. The present participle *stellans* means "starry" or "starlike".[^cite_note-Fox-11][^cite_note-12] "N.V." stands for *[naamloze vennootschap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naamloze_vennootschap)*, a [publicly traded company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_company), equivalent to the British "[PLC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_limited_company)" or French "[S.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.A._\(corporation\))".

## History

In early 2019, [Fiat Chrysler Automobiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat_Chrysler_Automobiles) (FCA) pursued a merger with French automaker [Renault](162292.md), reaching a provisional agreement.[^cite_note-13] However, the deal collapsed due to political concerns from the French government and resistance from [Renault's alliance partner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault%E2%80%93Nissan%E2%80%93Mitsubishi_Alliance), [Nissan](21240.md).[^cite_note-economist-14] FCA subsequently turned to another French automaker, the [PSA Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PSA_Group), and in December 2019, the two companies agreed to merge, forming the world's fourth-largest automaker by global vehicle sales, with projected annual cost savings of €3.7 billion, or about US\$4.22 billion.[^cite_note-Fox-11]

The [European Commission](9974.md) approved the merger on 21 December 2020 with minimal conditions.[^cite_note-15] Shareholders of both companies approved the deal on 4 January 2021, and the merger was completed on 16 January 2021. The combined entity was renamed Stellantis N.V. on 17 January, with shares beginning to trade under the symbol "STLA" on the [Milan Stock Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan_Stock_Exchange) and [Euronext Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euronext_Paris) on 18 January and on the [New York Stock Exchange](21560.md) on 19 January.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] For accounting purposes, PSA was designated the acquirer, and financial statements reflect PSA's historical performance.[^cite_note-Fox-11][^cite_note-2020AR-p6-19][^cite_note-thedetroitbureau-20]

Following the merger, the largest shareholders of Stellantis were [EXOR NV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exor_\(company\)), the investment company of the [Agnelli family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnelli_family), the founders of [Fiat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat), with a 14.2% stake; [Peugeot Invest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peugeot_Invest), the Peugeot family's holding company, with 7%; and [Bpifrance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bpifrance_\(public_entity\)), the French state-owned investment bank, with 6%.[^cite_note-thedetroitbureau-20][^cite_note-euronext-21]

[Carlos Tavares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Tavares), the CEO of PSA Group, was appointed CEO of Stellantis with a five-year term. Immediately following the merger, Tavares announced plans to offer 29 electrified vehicle models by the end of 2021 and to invest over €30 billion in electrification and software by the end of the decade.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

In response to the [global semiconductor shortage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%932023_global_supply_chain_crisis) that forced the company to slow or stop production at its plants,[^cite_note-27] Stellantis signed a partnership with [Foxconn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxconn) in 2021 to co-develop automotive chips,[^cite_note-28] and in 2023, the two companies launched a joint venture called SiliconAuto, based in the Netherlands.[^cite_note-29]

In 2022, Stellantis halted Russian operations due to [sanctions related to the invasion of Ukraine](70323386.md). In early 2024, Russian partners began producing Citroën-branded vehicles at the Kaluga plant, prompting Stellantis to state it had "lost control" of its assets in Russia.[^cite_note-30]

In 2022, Stellantis established the electric vehicle battery joint ventures NextStar Energy with [LG Energy Solution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LG_Energy_Solution) in Canada and StarPlus Energy with [Samsung SDI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung_SDI) in the US.[^cite_note-31] NextStar was dissolved in February 2026.[^cite_note-32]

Stellantis also faced legal and regulatory scrutiny. In May 2022, the company pleaded guilty to criminal charges and paid \$300 million to settle a U.S. Department of Justice investigation into diesel emissions fraud affecting over 100,000 vehicles.[^cite_note-DieselSettlement-33]

The company continued expanding its mobility and autonomous technology portfolio, acquiring [carsharing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carsharing) platform [Share Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_Now) in July 2022 and autonomous driving startup [aiMotive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AiMotive) in November 2022.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]

In October 2023, Stellantis acquired a 20% stake in Chinese EV manufacturer [Leapmotor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leapmotor) for €1.5 billion,[^cite_note-37] forming a joint venture, Leapmotor International, to handle global sales and production outside China.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

The company announced a €3 billion (US\$3.2 billion) share [buyback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Share_repurchase) in 2024.[^cite_note-40]

Under the leadership of Tavares, Stellantis faced mounting criticism over its cost-cutting strategy, declining sales, and strained relationships with key stakeholders. Tavares implemented aggressive restructuring measures, including workforce reductions and tight control over product development, which some analysts blamed for delays in new model launches and weakening brand performance, particularly in North America.[^cite_note-ReutersTavaresClash-41] U.S. dealers expressed concern about rising inventories and brand mismanagement, while the United Auto Workers criticized the company over job cuts and halted investment plans.[^cite_note-AutoNewsDealerBacklash-42][^cite_note-PBSUAW-43] Stellantis reported a 70% drop in net profit in 2024, with global shipments and U.S. market share declining significantly.[^cite_note-ElPaisTavares-44] Amid internal friction with the board and worsening financial performance, Tavares resigned in December 2024, two years before his contract was set to expire.[^cite_note-WSJExit-45]

On 28 May 2025, the board unanimously appointed Antonio Filosa, a longtime executive who had led Stellantis operations in North and South America, as CEO, effective 23 June 2025.[^cite_note-46] Filosa is expected to place renewed emphasis on the North American market.[^cite_note-47]

In May 2026, the company announced it will manufacture Peugeot and Jeep vehicles in China following a deal with [Dongfeng Motor Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongfeng_Motor_Corporation).[^cite_note-48] In July 2026, within the framework of the Dieselgate scandal, the British courts rejected the accusations against five manufacturers, including Peugeot-Citroën, finding in their favor.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51] In August 2026, Stellantis returned to operating profit in the first half of 2026, but profitability per car still remains quite low.[^cite_note-52]

## Positions on environmental regulations

Stellantis stated that it supports the Trump administration's proposal to repeal the 2009 Endangerment Finding and all greenhouse gas emissions standards for motor vehicles,[^cite_note-58] which would also repeal all federal regulation of GHGs across all sectors of the economy. Stellantis endorsed the Transportation Freedom Act in March 2025,[^cite_note-59] a bill that would weaken and repeal many emissions regulations governing passenger vehicles in the United States.[^cite_note-60] The bill sought to strip California of its [authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_emission_standards) to enforce the [Advanced Clean Cars II](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/our-work/programs/drive-forward-light-duty-vehicle-program/advanced-clean-cars) policy, which required manufacturers to achieve 100% ZEV sales in participating states by 2035. According to the Alliance for Automotive Innovation, about 30% of the US vehicle market was regulated by ACC II,[^cite_note-61] which is significantly lower than the company's stated decarbonization target for the US.[^cite_note-\:0-62]

The company opposed Canada's Electric Vehicle Availability Standard in a December 2025 letter to PM Mark Carney and an October 2025 testimony in the House of Commons.[^cite_note-63]

Although Stellantis CEO Carlos Tavares supported a phase-out date of internal combustion engine vehicles by 2035 in statements made in February 2024,[^cite_note-64] the company advocated for flexibilities for meeting the 2025 targets in EU's CO<sub>2</sub> emissions standards for cars and vans in early 2025.[^cite_note-65]

## Motorsport

Stellantis Motorsport is a department led by director and senior vice president Jean-Marc Finot.[^cite_note-66] It is responsible for the [motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorsport) activities of the corporation's brands, divisions, and subsidiaries:

- [Citroën Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citro%C3%ABn_Racing)

  [^cite_note-citroenracing-67]

  - Commercial operation focusing on customer racing with the [Citroën C3 Rally2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citro%C3%ABn_C3_Rally2), although known to be supporting entries in WRC2

    [^cite_note-68]

  - Entrant in [FIA Formula E World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_E)

- [DS Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DS_Performance)

  [^cite_note-69]

  - Entrant in FIA Formula E World Championship

- [Opel Motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opel_Motorsport?action=edit\&redlink=1)

  [^cite_note-70]

  - Manufacturer of the electric rally car [Opel Corsa-e Rally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opel_Corsa-e_Rally?action=edit\&redlink=1) and organiser of its Opel e-Rally Cup

  - Commercial operation focusing on customer racing with the [Opel Corsa Rally4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opel_Corsa_Rally4)

- [Peugeot Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peugeot_Sport)

  [^cite_note-peugeot-sport-71]

  - Entrant of the [FIA World Endurance Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIA_World_Endurance_Championship)

  - Commercial operation focusing on customer racing with the [Peugeot 208 Rally4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peugeot_208_Rally4)

The *Stellantis Motorsport Racing Shop* combines Citroën Racing, Peugeot Sport and Opel Motorsports' customer racing distribution and retail sales operations.[^cite_note-peugeot-sport-71] It is also known as Peugeot Citroën Racing Shop[^cite_note-72] and Peugeot Citroën Opel Racing Shop.[^cite_note-73]

The *Stellantis Motorsport Cup* is a rally competition run in Belgium, France and Spain using Peugeot 208 and Opel Corsa Rally4 cars.[^cite_note-citroenracing-67] The cup originated in Belgium and Luxembourg as *PSA Motorsport Cup Belux*.[^cite_note-74]

## Technology

### Electrification strategy

In 2021, Stellantis released its "Dare Forward" strategic plan, which aims to invest €50 billion before 2030 to fund electrification of vehicles and sets global sales and sustainability targets. Goals include achieving 100% battery electric vehicle (BEV) sales for passenger cars in Europe and 50% of passenger car and light-duty truck sales in the United States by 2030, across 75 electric vehicle models.[^cite_note-\:0-62] Stellantis is using a mix of nickel-based (NMC) and cobalt-free battery packs, and [lithium iron phosphate (LFP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate_battery) packs for cheaper BEV variants, to power vehicles with front-, rear-, and all-wheel-drive systems, for driving ranges between 500 and 800 kilometers (300–500 miles).[^cite_note-St-Electrification-75]

## Sustainability

In 2023, Stellantis introduced SUSTAINera, a hub for recycling and sourcing used parts to improve [circular economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_economy) sustainability goals, reduce waste, and offset emissions.[^cite_note-76][^cite_note-77] The initiative aims to contribute to the goal of achieving carbon net zero by 2038.[^cite_note-78] Stellantis hopes to also further these goals by integrating its B-Parts aftermarket platform, acquired by PSA in 2020.[^cite_note-79]

On 12 December 2024, it was reported that Stellantis have agreed to establish a joint-venture with [CATL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CATL) to invest €4.1 billion in building a large-scale [lithium iron phosphate battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate_battery) plant in [Zaragoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaragoza), Spain. This is in line with its 2038 zero carbon target.[^cite_note-80]
