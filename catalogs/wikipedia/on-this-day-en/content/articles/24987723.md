**Da Qaidam** is a [county-level administrative zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_China) in [Haixi Prefecture](3472790.md) in northwestern [Qinghai province](173818.md), China. It borders [Gansu province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gansu) to the north.

## Etymology



Map including Da Qaidam (labeled as Ta-ch'ai-tan) and surrounding region from the [International Map of the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Map_of_the_World) (1975)

*Da Qaidam* is a combination of the [Hanyu Pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanyu_Pinyin) [romanization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Chinese) of the [Mandarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandarin_Chinese) pronunciation of [大](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%A4%A7) (*dà*), the [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language) word meaning "big" or "greater", and the [Zangwen Pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zangwen_Pinyin) [romanization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Tibetan) of the Tibetan name [ཚྭ](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E0%BD%9A%E0%BE%AD)་[འདམ](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E0%BD%A0%E0%BD%91%E0%BD%98) (*qaidam*), meaning "[salt](1605200.md) marsh" and referencing the surrounding [Qaidam Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qaidam_Basin). The Mandarin pinyin romanization of the Chinese [transcription](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcription_into_Chinese_characters) of Qaidam is "*Cháidàn*".

## Administrative divisions

The area administered as Da Qaidam is divided into two [towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towns_of_China). The seat of Da Qaidam's administration is Qaidam Town, which lies at an altitude of 3,174 m (10,413 ft) above [sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_level).[^cite_note-地方志-2]

## Geography and climate

Da Qaidam borders [Delingha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delingha) to the east, [Lenghu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenghu) to the west, [Golmud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golmud) across the [Qarhan Playa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qarhan_Playa) to the south, and [Jiuquan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiuquan) ([Gansu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gansu)) to the north, and is part of the northern [Qaidam Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qaidam_Basin).

Similar to neighbouring Golmud, Da Qaidam has a [cool arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cool_arid_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BWk*), with long, cold winters, and warm summers, although due to its location further north and elevation more than 350 metres (1,150 ft) higher, Da Qaidam is substantially cooler. The monthly 24-hour average temperature drops to −12.2 °C (10.0 °F) in January and rises to 16.6 °C (61.9 °F) in July, while the annual mean is 2.9 °C (37.2 °F). Precipitation is very low, totaling only 98 millimetres or 3.86 inches per annum, falling on 35 days, most of which are during the summer. With monthly percent possible sunshine ranging from 65% in July to 82% in October, the area receives 3,257 hours of bright sunshine annually.

## Economy

As elsewhere in the Qaidam Basin, mining industries are of major importance in Da Qaidam. There is a lead mine in Xitieshan, the Xitieshan Lead Mine ([锡铁山](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E9%94%A1%E9%93%81%E5%B1%B1)[铅](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E9%93%85)[矿](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E7%9F%BF)),[^cite_note-6] as well as a number of salt lakes where potash and related products are extracted.

## Transportation

[China National Highway 215](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Highway_215) and [China National Highway 315](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Highway_315) pass through Da Qaidam and meet here. [Yinmaxia railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yinmaxia_railway_station) and [Xitieshan railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xitieshan_railway_station) on the [Qinghai–Tibet Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qinghai%E2%80%93Tibet_Railway) are both located within Da Qaidam.[^cite_note-地方志-2]
