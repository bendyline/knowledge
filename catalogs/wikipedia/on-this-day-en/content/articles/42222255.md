**Apalachee High School** is a [public](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_High_School) [high school](554992.md) located near [Winder](109875.md), [Georgia](48830.md), United States.[^cite_note-6] The school mascot is a [wildcat](269357.md).[^cite_note-7] Apalachee is a part of [Barrow County Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrow_County_Schools), a [Title I district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_and_Secondary_Education_Act#Title_I). Feeder schools include Haymon-Morris Middle School and Westside Middle School.

The school is [Barrow County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrow_County,_Georgia)'s second high school and opened in 2000.[^cite_note-8] It is in an [unincorporated area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unincorporated_area) in the county.[^cite_note-9] Its attendance boundary includes southern Winder, [Bethlehem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem,_Georgia), [Carl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl,_Georgia), and the Barrow County portion of [Auburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn,_Georgia).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-BarrowSDmap-12]

As of 2024 it has about 1,900 students in grades 9–12. The student body is a mix of White, Hispanic, and Black students with a smaller percentage of Asian students. Almost half the students come from families documented as economically disadvantaged.[^cite_note-usn-5]

## 2024 shooting

Main article: [2024 Apalachee High School shooting](77791441.md)

On September 4, 2024, a [mass shooting](77791441.md) occurred at the school, resulting in the deaths of two students and two teachers, as well as another nine injuries. A 14-year-old student, Colt Gray, was arrested along with his father, Colin Gray.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]
