The **Abqaiq oil field** is an [oil field](2910801.md) located in [Eastern Province](468721.md). It was discovered in 1940 and developed by [Saudi Aramco](290527.md). It began production in 1940 and produces [oil](4207510.md). The total [proven reserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proven_reserves) of the Abqaiq oil field are around 22.5 billion barrels (3.020 billion tonnes), and production is centered on 400,000 barrels per day (64,000 m<sup>3</sup>/d).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] Much of its production is carried through the [East-West Crude Oil Pipeline](54166263.md).
