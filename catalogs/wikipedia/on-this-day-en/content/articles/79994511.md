On May 16, 2025, ten inmates of the [Orleans Parish Prison](40287582.md), located in [New Orleans, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans,_Louisiana), escaped through a hole in the wall behind a toilet. Six of the ten inmates were charged with murder or attempted murder, and all inmates have since been recaptured as of October 8.[^cite_note-7] Nine of the inmates are pre-trial.&#x20;

## Escape

At 12\:23 a.m., inmates at the Orleans Parish Prison, who managed to escape their cells due to some faulty locks, began tampering with a locked sliding cell door for about 20 minutes before pulling it off its tracks and gaining entry into the cell. Once inside, the group ripped off a toilet and sink unit in the cell, and cut off horizontal bars from a hole behind the toilet that was used to connect pipes.[^cite_note-8] By doing this, they were successfully able to escape. Above the hole where the inmates escaped from, the inmates scribbled graffiti and various obscene messages, among them "To Easy LoL" and "We Innocent."[^cite_note-9] Authorities stated the guard assigned with watching their cell pod was away getting food at the time of the escape.[^cite_note-\:0-10]&#x20;

After squeezing through the hole, the group fled through a [loading dock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loading_dock), scaled a wall, and then ran across the [Interstate 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_10_in_Louisiana) highway.[^cite_note-11] By 1\:30 a.m., the group was outside the jail.[^cite_note-\:0-10] At 8\:30 a.m., roughly seven hours after the escape, the inmates were discovered missing during a routine head count.[^cite_note-\:1-12]&#x20;

Orleans Parish Sheriff [Susan Hutson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Hutson) noted that there was a chance that someone who worked at the jail had helped the men escape, saying “It’s almost impossible, not completely, but almost impossible for anybody to get out of this facility without help".[^cite_note-\:0-10] A jail employee who was monitoring surveillance had reportedly witnessed the escape but did not report it to deputies. Three employees at the jail have been suspended without pay while officials investigate.[^cite_note-\:1-12]&#x20;

Prison officials early on suspected the escapees had inside help to escape. On May 20, 2025, a jail maintenance worker was arrested and accused of turning off the water in certain cells in order to aid the escapees in removing the toilet. Three additional jail employees were suspended without pay pending the outcome of the investigation.[^cite_note-ABC-1][^cite_note-BBC-13] On May 21, 2025, two women were arrested for aiding the escapees and charged with being accessories after the fact. At least one of the women was in communication with an escapee prior to the escape.[^cite_note-two_women-2] Late on May 22, 2025, another woman was arrested for being an accomplice.[^cite_note-one_woman-3] As of Friday, May 23, 2025, seven people had been arrested for aiding the escapees: one jail employee, one jail inmate, and five acquaintances.[^cite_note-abc7-4] On the weekend of May 24–25, 2025, four people who were family or acquaintances of escapee Lenton Vanburen were arrested for assisting him.[^cite_note-vanburen4-5]&#x20;

## Capture

Three of the men were captured on May 16, 2025, and were taken to the [Louisiana State Penitentiary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_Penitentiary) via helicopter.[^cite_note-\:0-10] Gary Price was captured on May 19, 2025,[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] and Cory Boyd was captured a day later.[^cite_note-fifth-16] Lenton Vanburen was captured on May 26, 2025, in [Baton Rouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baton_Rouge).[^cite_note-vanburen6-17] Leo Tate and Jermaine Donald were also captured on May 26 in [Walker County, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walker_County,_Texas).[^cite_note-3day-18][^cite_note-2day-19]&#x20;

[Louisiana State Police](5875801.md) said in a statement that one of the inmates, Kendall Myles, was found under a car at a hotel garage in the [French Quarter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Quarter).[^cite_note-\:0-10] This was the third time inmate Antoine Massey had escaped from a jail since 2007, and he was also ineligible for electronic ankle monitoring due to a history of cutting the devices off.[^cite_note-20] Massey was captured on June 27, 2025.[^cite_note-21] During his time on the run, Massey published media on [Instagram](31591547.md) in which he claimed innocence. Though police were able to locate and raid the home that Massey was shown in, the convict was gone on arrival.&#x20;

The final inmate, Derrick Groves, was captured on October 8, nearly five months after the escape, in [Atlanta](3138.md), Georgia.[^cite_note-22]&#x20;

## Perpetrators

In total, 26 individuals were charged with crimes related to the escape. In addition to the 10 inmates, 16 accessories were charged with assisting the escapees. Many of the accessories were either employees of the prison or relatives of the inmates.&#x20;

The escapees' ages ranged from 20 to 43. Several have been charged with murder. Others were charged with aggravated assault with a firearm, false imprisonment with a weapon, armed robbery with a firearm, and domestic abuse.[^cite_note-23]&#x20;
