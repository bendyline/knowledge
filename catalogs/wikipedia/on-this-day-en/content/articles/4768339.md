The **minister of foreign affairs** ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *minister van Buitenlandse Zaken*) is a member of the [cabinet of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_Netherlands) and the head of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Netherlands\)). The officeholder is in charge of the [Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_the_Netherlands)'s [foreign policy](233404.md) and [international relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_relations). The current minister, [Tom Berendsen](62485937.md), has served since 23 February 2026.&#x20;

Alongside the minister, a [minister without portfolio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_without_portfolio_\(Netherlands\)) is usually attached to the ministry to oversee [development cooperation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Development_cooperation) and [trade promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_promotion_\(international_trade\)). Multiple cabinets have also included a [state secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_secretary_\(Netherlands\)) responisble for specific sub-portfolios, such as [European affairs](683704.md).
