**Al-Qa'im District** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): قضاء القائم) is a district in [Al Anbar Governorate](467036.md), [Iraq](7515928.md), on the border with [Syria](7515849.md). It is centred on the town of [Al-Qa'im](1831738.md). The [Euphrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphrates) river flows through it. At its western end, in the city of [Husaybah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Husaybah), is the [Al-Qa'im border crossing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qa'im_border_crossing) to [Abu Kamal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Kamal) in Syria's [Deir ez-Zor Governorate](7445749.md).[^cite_note-1]
