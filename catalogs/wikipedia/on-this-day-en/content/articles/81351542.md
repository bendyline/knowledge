The **Council of the Presidency for the Re-Foundation of the Republic of Madagascar** is the acting governing body of [Madagascar](18964.md) and [Military junta](216815.md),[^cite_note-1] established by the [Madagascar Armed Forces](50881.md) unit [CAPSAT](81340651.md) immediately following the [2025 Malagasy coup d'état](81321149.md) of 14 October.[^cite_note-\:0-2] It is headed by Colonel [Michael Randrianirina](81340595.md), who consequently serves as the [President of Madagascar](30876601.md). Currently composed of officers from the armed forces and the national gendarmerie, the council’s first decree stated that civilians would likely be included as well.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

## Background

### Protests and seizure of power

Main articles: [2025 Malagasy protests](81184334.md) and [2025 Malagasy coup d'état](81321149.md)

After [weeks of protests](81184334.md) in Madagascar triggered by frequent power and water outages, the [Madagascar Armed Forces](50881.md), led by [CAPSAT](81340651.md), began to disobey orders to quell protesters from then-President [Andry Rajoelina](21298487.md) and urged other members of the armed forces and the [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Gendarmerie_\(Madagascar\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) to support the protesters. By the morning of 12 October, CAPSAT partially seized the capital Antananarivo with little resistance and declared the seizure of every Armed Forces chain of commands.[^cite_note-3]

After Antananarivo was partially seized and Rajoelina fled to France, General [Demosthene Pikulas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demosthene_Pikulas?action=edit\&redlink=1) was appointed as the new chief of staff of the Madagascar Armed Forces. On 14 October, [Iavoloha Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iavoloha_Palace) fell to the armed forces and Colonel [Michael Randrianirina](81340595.md) was named as the country's interim leader shortly after by the High Constitutional Court and made his first presidential instruction to dissolve all national institutions except the [National Assembly](4503558.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] Randrianirina promised an election within two years and said a committee comprising officers from the army, the paramilitary [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie), and the national police would govern the nation. Addressing reporters and the protesters gathered nearby, he said: “The [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Madagascar) is suspended. We will organize a [referendum](51582.md) within two years to establish a new [democratic order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratization).”[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-\:02-7]

On 15 October, Malagasy state television announced that Randrianirina would be inaugurated on 17 October as "President of the Refoundation of the Republic of Madagascar" during a session of the Constitutional Court.[^cite_note-8] He was sworn in on that date as planned.[^cite_note-9]

### Leadership

On 20 October 2025, Randrianirina appointed businessman [Herintsalama Rajaonarivelo](81386406.md) as the nation's prime minister, succeeding general [Ruphin Zafisambo](81277906.md).[^cite_note-10] On 25 October, the new government revoked Rajoelina's Malagasy citizenship, citing laws against [dual nationality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_citizenship).[^cite_note-11] In an interview with *[Sky News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_News)* on 10 November, he promised elections within 18 to 24 months following a period of “refoundation and recovery,” saying that [Generation Z](4015968.md) and the broader population wanted change.[^cite_note-12] On 9 March 2026, Randrianirina dismissed several cabinet members and Prime Minister Rajaonarivelo. On 15 March 2026, he appointed [Mamitiana Rajaonarison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamitiana_Rajaonarison) as Prime Minister.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

### 24-month transitional plan

On 10 December 2025, the National Consultation of Madagascar was officially launched in the capital to discuss constitutional reforms aimed at establishing the country’s Fifth Republic. According to Minister of State for Refoundation Hanitriniaina Razafimanantsoa, the consultation will last six months.[^cite_note-15]

On 28 February 2026, the Council of the Presidency for the Re-Foundation of the Republic of Madagascar released the national Refoundation, outlining a 24-month transition in 2026–2027.[^cite_note-\:03-16] The first phase, from March 2026 to January 2027, will hold nationwide consultations to gather citizens’ proposals and ideas on institutional reform.[^cite_note-\:03-16] From June 2026 to May 2027, authorities will revise the legal and structural framework to prepare for a constitutional or [referendum](51582.md) vote and a presidential election that meets international standards.[^cite_note-\:03-16] Between January and April 2027, they will draft the Fifth Republic constitution, which will be submitted to the electorate for approval.[^cite_note-\:03-16] A [presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_Malagasy_presidential_election) is scheduled for September 2027.[^cite_note-\:03-16] From May to December 2027, the Refoundation will organize the constitutional referendum and the subsequent presidential and territorial elections, depending on the electoral model chosen.[^cite_note-\:03-16]
