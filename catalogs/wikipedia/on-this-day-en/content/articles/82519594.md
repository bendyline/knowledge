**Beinir Johannesen** (born 12 February 1997) is a Faroese politician serving as the [Prime Minister of the Faroe Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_the_Faroe_Islands) since 2026 and as a member of the [Løgting](535570.md) since 2019.[^cite_note-1] He has served as chairman of the [People's Party](1896640.md) since 2022.[^cite_note-2] Johannesen became prime minister after his party finished first in the [2026 election](82433896.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
