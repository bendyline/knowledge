**Flora Pili** (born 21 July 1997) is a French [professional boxer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_boxer). She held the [IBO super lightweight title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_IBO_female_world_champions#Super_lightweight) from December 5, 2025 to September 5, 2026[^cite_note-1] and holds a professional record of 12–1.[^cite_note-2] She is ranked as the second best super lightweight by the [WBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Boxing_Council)[^cite_note-3] and the [IBF](241038.md).[^cite_note-4]

As of 2026, Pili is ranked the world’s sixth best super lightweight by [BoxRec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BoxRec).[^cite_note-5]

## Early life

Pili was born on 21 July 1997 in [Saint Avold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Avold), [Moselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moselle), France.&#x20;

## Amateur career

As an amateur Pili became French Youth champion and won a Senior National title in 2015 and 2019, respectively, in 2017 she faced off against future 2 time Olympic gold medalist Kellie Harrington in a losing effort.[^cite_note-6]

## Professional career

Pili made her professional debut in 2019 and amassed 12 straight wins capturing the French, European and [WBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WBO) International titles.[^cite_note-7]

### IBO super lightweight champion

#### Pili vs. Janicijevic

On December 5, 2025 Pili challenged [Jelena Janićijević](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jelena_Jani%C4%87ijevi%C4%87) for the [IBO super lightweight title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_IBO_female_world_champions#Super_lightweight) in her hometown of [Saint-Avold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Avold), [Moselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moselle), France. Pili secured the victory via [majority decision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majority_decision) (MD) with one judge scoring the bout a draw at 95–95 while the other two scored it 98–92 and 97–93 respectively.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

#### Pili vs. Taylor

Main article: [Katie Taylor vs. Flora Pili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Taylor_vs._Flora_Pili)

On September 5, 2026 Pili faced [Katie Taylor](12426364.md) for the [Undisputed super lightweight title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_female_undisputed_world_boxing_champions#super_lightweight),[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] but lost by a unanimous decision.
