**Sincan** is a municipality and [metropolitan district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Turkey) of [Ankara Province](370068.md), [Turkey](11125639.md).[^cite_note-2] Its area is 880 km<sup>2</sup>,[^cite_note-3] and its population is 572,609 (2022).[^cite_note-tuik-1] It is a large town 27 km from the city of [Ankara](802.md). Its elevation is 789 m (2,589 ft).[^cite_note-4] Sincan has friendly relations with the municipality of [Doboj Jug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doboj_Jug) from [Bosnia and Herzegovina](3463.md).



Osmanlı Stadium in Sincan, Ankara.

Sincan District hosts [ASO 1. Organize Sanayi Bölgesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASO_1._Organize_Sanayi_B%C3%B6lgesi?action=edit\&redlink=1), the biggest Organized Industrial Zone in Ankara, operated by Ankara Chamber of Industry.[^cite_note-5]

## Geography

Sincan stands on a plain surrounded by hills and watered by the Ankara River, a tributary of the [Sakarya River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakarya_River). There is some agriculture and light industry in Sincan, but the majority of people commute to Ankara by rail.

The symbol of the municipality is the [tulip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulip). The central square is called *Lale Meydanı* ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language) for "tulip square"), and every year a tulip festival is held where plastic tulips are handed out in the streets.

## History

Even prior to the period of the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md), a village stood in this location, which subsequently grew to when [Mustafa Kemal Atatürk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_Kemal_Atat%C3%BCrk) commissioned a housing project here for [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people) refugees from [Bulgaria](3415.md). The battle saw fighting during the [Battle of the Sakarya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Sakarya) in the [Turkish War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_War_of_Independence). This was the furthest spot in [Anatolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatolia) in which the [Greek Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_Army) had advanced to.
