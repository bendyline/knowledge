**Karabudakhkentsky District** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Карабудахкентский райо́н, [Kumyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumyk_language): Къарабудагъгентли якъ, *Qarabudağgentli yaq*) is an administrative[^cite_note-RDagestan_admlaw-1] and municipal[^cite_note-RDagestan_mun-6] district ([raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raion)), one of the [forty-one](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_the_Republic_of_Dagestan) in the [Republic of Dagestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Dagestan), [Russia](25391.md). It is located in the east of the republic. The area of the district is 1,460 square kilometers (560 sq mi).[^cite_note-about-2] Its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) is the [rural locality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) (a *[selo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village#Russia)*) of [Karabudakhkent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karabudakhkent).[^cite_note-RDagestan_admlaw-1] As of the [2010 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Census_\(2010\)), the total population of the district was 73,016, with the population of Karabudakhkent accounting for 21.0% of that number.[^cite_note-2010Census-3]

## History

The district was established by the Central Executive Committee decree on January 20, 1921.[^cite_note-history-11] It underwent numerous changes during the following years and was established in its present borders in 1965 as **Leninsky District** (Ле́нинский райо́н).[^cite_note-history-11] In 1992, it was renamed Karabudakhkentsky District.[^cite_note-history-11]

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), Karabudakhkentsky District is one of the [forty-one](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_the_Republic_of_Dagestan) in the [Republic of Dagestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Dagestan).[^cite_note-RDagestan_admlaw-1] It is divided into two [settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban-type_settlement#Administrative_divisions) (administrative divisions with the [administrative centers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) in the [urban-type settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban-type_settlement) (inhabited localities) of [Achi-Su](45313972.md) and [Manas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manas_\(urban-type_settlement\))) and three [selsoviets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selsoviet), which comprise sixteen [rural localities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia).[^cite_note-OKATO2-4] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), the district is incorporated as **Karabudakhkentsky Municipal District**.[^cite_note-RDagestan_mun-6] The settlements are incorporated as urban settlements, and the three selsoviets are incorporated as twelve rural settlements within the municipal district.[^cite_note-RDagestan_mun-6] The *[selo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village#Russia)* of [Karabudakhkent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karabudakhkent) serves as the administrative center of both the administrative[^cite_note-RDagestan_admlaw-1] and municipal[^cite_note-OKTMO-12] district.
