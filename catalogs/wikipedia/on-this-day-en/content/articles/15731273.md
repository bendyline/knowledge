**Bruno Eduardo Rodríguez Parrilla** (born 22 January 1958) is a [Cuban](5042481.md) [diplomat](82433.md) and [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician). He is a member of the [Politburo of the Communist Party of Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politburo_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba), and has served as Cuba's [Minister of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Foreign_Ministers_of_Cuba) since 2009.

## Biography and career

Rodríguez was born in [Mexico City](18987.md) to engineer José María Rodríguez Padilla who held high positions in the Cuban government.[^cite_note-1]

He was President of the Federation of High School Students (FEEM) and leader of the Federation of University Students (FEU). Graduated with a Law Degree, he was a professor of Public International Law at the [University of Havana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Havana). In 1986, he was elected Secretary of International Affairs of the National Committee of the [Young Communist League (UJC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Communist_League_\(Cuba\)), and in 1991, he was appointed director of the newspaper *[Juventud Rebelde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juventud_Rebelde)*.

He served in the [Republic of Angola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Angola) as an officer in the [Revolutionary Armed Forces](73251.md).

## Involvement in Cuban diplomacy

Rodríguez Parrilla served as Cuba's [Permanent Representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permanent_Representative) to the [United Nations](31769.md) from 1995 to 2003. He was appointed Minister of Foreign Affairs on March 2, 2009, replacing [Felipe Pérez Roque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felipe_P%C3%A9rez_Roque), after serving as the Vice-Minister. This was a result of the [2009 shake-up by Raúl Castro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Cuban_government_dismissals).



Bruno Rodriguez Parrilla

On 25 October 2011, Rodríguez Parrilla addressed the [United Nations General Assembly](31957.md) right before the annual non-binding vote calling for the [United States](3434750.md) to end its [embargo](1105954.md) against Cuba.[^cite_note-2]

On 20 July 2015, Rodríguez attended the reinauguration of the [Cuban Embassy in Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Cuba_in_Washington,_D.C.), making him the first Cuban Minister of Foreign Affairs to visit the United States on a diplomatic mission since 1958.[^cite_note-3]
