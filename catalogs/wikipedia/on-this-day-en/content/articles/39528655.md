**Mwigulu Lameck Nchemba** (born 7 January 1975) is a Tanzanian economist and [CCM](38207770.md) politician, who has served as the elected [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament) for [Iramba West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iramba) Constituency since 2010. On 13 November 2025, President [Samia Suluhu Hassan](38577619.md) appointed him as the [Prime Minister of Tanzania](1897249.md), succeeding [Kassim Majaliwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kassim_Majaliwa). Before his current appointment, he served as the Minister of Finance in the Tanzanian Cabinet, since March 2021.[^cite_note-1R-1][^cite_note-2R-2]

## Background and education

Nchemba was born on 7 January 1975 in [Iramba District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iramba_District), in the [Singida Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singida_Region) of Tanzania. He attended Makunda Primary School, then transferred to Ilboru Secondary School for his middle school education. He completed his A-Level schooling at Mazengo Secondary School, graduating in 2000 with the equivalent of a [High School Diploma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_School_Diploma).[^cite_note-3R-3]

In 2001 he was admitted to the [University of Dar es Salaam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dar_es_Salaam), graduating with a [Bachelor of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Economics) degree in 2004. He went on to obtain a [Master of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Economics) degree in 2006, also from Dar es Salaam University.[^cite_note-3R-3] He later obtained a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree in [Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) from the same university.[^cite_note-4R-4]

## Career

Before joining elective Tanzanian politics, Nchemba worked as an economist at the [Bank of Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_Tanzania). Later, President [Jakaya Kikwete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakaya_Kikwete) appointed him as Minister of Finance and Economic Planning. His tenure at the finance ministry coincided with the "[Tegeta escrow scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tegeta_escrow_scandal)", which involved the embezzlement of hundreds of million of dollars by high-ranking government officials. Nchemba, who was not implicated, maintained that the full extent of the law should apply to those involved.[^cite_note-4R-4]

He was appointed the [Minister of Agriculture, Livestock and Fisheries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Agriculture,_Livestock_and_Fisheries_\(Tanzania\)) in December 2015, by the then incoming president [John Magufuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Magufuli). Following the death of [Augustine Mahiga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustine_Mahiga), he was appointed the Minister of Constitutional and Legal Affairs.[^cite_note-5R-5]

Following the formation of the [6th Cabinet of Tanzania](67278077.md), on 31 March 2021, [Philip Mpango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Mpango) was promoted to [Vice-President of Tanzania](16997743.md), Nchemba took over the post of Minister of Finance and Planning.[^cite_note-6R-6] On 4 July 2023, he retained his position of Minister of Finance, however, the Ministry of planning was moved under the president's office.[^cite_note-7] He was nominated as [Prime Minister of Tanzania](1897249.md) by Suluhu on 13 November 2025 following the [2025 Tanzanian general election](76587785.md).[^cite_note-8]
