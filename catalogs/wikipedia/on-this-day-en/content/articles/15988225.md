Hungary and the United States are [NATO](21133.md) allies and security partners.[^cite_note-p610-1] The U.S. Embassy in Hungary is located in [Budapest](36787.md), and the Hungarian Embassy in the U.S. is located in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md)

After first recognizing the Hungarian state in 1848, the United States established diplomatic relations with the [Kingdom of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hungary) in 1921, following the dissolution of [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) after [World War I](4764461.md).[^cite_note-p610-1] Relations were severed during [World War II](32927.md) and were reestablished in 1945.[^cite_note-p610-1] Following the collapse of the [Hungarian People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_People's_Republic) in 1989, the United States supported Hungary's transition to democracy and a market economy.[^cite_note-p610-1] Hungary acceded to NATO in 1999 and the [European Union](9317.md) in 2004. Since then, the two countries have cooperated on security, trade, and transatlantic issues.[^cite_note-p610-1]

## History

The [Hungarian State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_State) produced by the [Hungarian Revolution of 1848](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Revolution_of_1848) was recognised by the United States. Hungarian revolutionary [Lajos Kossuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lajos_Kossuth) was brought to the United States by an American warship in 1851. An American diplomatic post to the [Kingdom of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hungary) was established in 1869, and upgraded to a [consulate](6770059.md) in 1904.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEHungary-U.S._Diplomatic_Relations-2]

After the dissolution of [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary), President [Warren G. Harding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_G._Harding) called for the [United States Congress](31756.md) to create a peace treaty between the United States and Hungary on 2 July 1921. Negotiations started in Budapest on 9 July 1921, and there were no difficulties as there was no significant conflict between the two sides during the war nor were any Americans held as prisoners of war. The [U.S.–Hungarian Peace Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S.%E2%80%93Hungarian_Peace_Treaty) was signed on 29 August.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMajor1974174-3] The United States' legation was established in [Budapest](36787.md) on 26 December 1921. Hungary opened its first consulates in [New York City](645042.md), [Pittsburgh](25101.md), [Chicago](6886.md), and [Cleveland](5951.md) in 1922. [László Széchenyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A1szl%C3%B3_Sz%C3%A9chenyi), the first Hungarian minister to the United States, presented his credentials on 11 January 1922, and served until 31 March 1933.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEHungary-U.S._Diplomatic_Relations-2]

In the 1930s [Standard Oil of New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ExxonMobil) started exploring for oil in Hungary.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEFenyo197293-4] The Hungarian-American Oil Corporation (MAORT) started oil production in 1937. This rose from 1.3 million tons of oil in 1937, to 37.3 million tons in 1938, and 141.8 million tons in 1939.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEFenyo197294-5]

The United States represented the British after the United Kingdom and Hungary ended diplomatic relations on 7 April 1941. The British ultimatum to Hungary requesting that it end its participation in the war against the Soviet Union was delivered by Americans on 29 November. The United States was opposed to the United Kingdom declaring war on Hungary as it was believed that it would strengthen the pro-war faction in Hungary. The ultimatum expired and the British declared war on 7 December.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEFenyo197245–47-6]

Germany's [declaration of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_declaration_of_war_against_the_United_States) against the United States resulted in [László Bárdossy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A1szl%C3%B3_B%C3%A1rdossy) ending diplomatic relations with the United States on 11 December. Bárdossy was opposed to war with the United States as he felt it would be similar to Panama and Costa Rica declarations of war against Japan, as neither side would be greatly involved in the conflict against the subject of the declaration.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEFenyo197251-7] However, Bárdossy informed [Herbert Pell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Pell) on 13 December that a state of war existed between the United States and Hungary.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEFenyo197252-8]

U.S. Secretary of State [Cordell Hull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordell_Hull) opposed declaring war on Hungary as he viewed it as a [puppet state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puppet_state) of Germany.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEFenyo197253-9] On 2 June 1942, President [Franklin D. Roosevelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_D._Roosevelt) requested the United States Congress to declare war on Bulgaria, Hungary, and Romania.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMessage_to_Congress_on_a_State_of_War_Between_the_United_States_and_Hungary,_Rumania,_and_Bulgaria.-10]

Tibor Eckhardt left Hungary on 7 March 1941, and travelled to the United States. Germany's [Federal Foreign Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Foreign_Office) was suspicious of Eckhardt, but Horthy denied sending him on a mission. Eckhardt made contact with the [United States Department of State](31975.md) as the leader of an independent Hungary movement. Eckhardt was stripped of his Hungarian citizenship in October. His independent Hungary movement disbanded in July 1942.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEFenyo1972111–112-11]

## Trade

In 2024, the United States' exported \$2.64 billion to Hungary and Hungary exported \$10.9 billion to the United States[^cite_note-12].&#x20;

## Public opinion

According to the 2012 U.S. Global Leadership Report, 38% of Hungarians approve of U.S. leadership, with 20% disapproving and 42% uncertain, a decrease from 53% approval in 2011.[^cite_note-13] In a 2018 Eurobarometer poll, 68% of Hungarians viewed the United States favorably.[^cite_note-Eurobarometer-14] According to a 2026 [Pew Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pew_Research_Center) survey, 58% of Hungarians had a favorable view of the United States, while 38% had a negative view.[^cite_note-\:62-15]
