**&#x20;Ananda Wijepala** is a Sri Lankan politician currently serving as the Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs. He was elected to the Sri Lankan Parliament from [Kurunegala Electoral District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurunegala_Electoral_District) as a member of the [National People's Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_People's_Power).[^cite_note-k574-1][^cite_note-m971-2][^cite_note-p003-3][^cite_note-d659-4][^cite_note-PoSL-AW-5]

He previously served as the private secretary to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and currently serves as his chief of staff.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
