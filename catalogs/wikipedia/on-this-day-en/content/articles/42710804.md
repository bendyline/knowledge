**Greater Beirut** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بيروت الكبرى) is the [urban agglomeration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_agglomeration) comprising the [city of Beirut](37428.md) ([Beirut Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beirut_Governorate)) and the adjacent municipalities over the [Mount Lebanon Governorate](3028437.md). It does not constitute a single administrative unit. Greater Beirut geographically stretches south to the [Damour River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damour_River) in the [Chouf District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chouf_District) until it reaches the "[Nahr al-Kalb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahr_al-Kalb)" river in the [Keserwan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keserwan_District) in the north. It also comprises many towns and cities in the mountains in the [Aley District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aley), [Baabda District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baabda) and [Metn District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metn) Districts, most notably being the cities of [Baabda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baabda), [Aley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aley), [Choueifat](2991980.md), [Hazmieh](17303821.md), [Sin el-Fil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sin_el-Fil), [Jdeideh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jdeideh,_Matn), [Bchamoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bchamoun) and [Dekwaneh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dekwaneh). The [conurbation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conurbation) spreads south, east, and north of Beirut city. To the west, the [Eastern Mediterranean Sea](19006.md) serves as a natural boundary.

## Demographics

Greater Beirut is equally split between Christians and Muslims:

- **West Beirut** is predominantly inhabited by [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Sunni_Muslims) (70%) and a minority of [Shia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims) Muslims, with small but substantial numbers of [Christians](4796145.md) and [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Druze).

- **East and North Beirut** is predominantly [Christian](4796145.md), of which 65% are [Maronites and other Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maronite_Church_in_Lebanon) and 35% are [Orthodox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Greek_Orthodox_Christians).

- **South Beirut Suburbs** also known as [Dahieh](6082788.md), is predominantly [Shia Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims) (85%) with small co-existing minorities of Sunnis and Christians.
