**North Gyeongsang Province** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 경상북도; Korean pronunciation: [\[kjʌŋ.saŋ.buk̚.t͈o\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Korean)), also known as **Gyeongbuk** (경북), is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_South_Korea) in eastern [South Korea](27019.md). With an area of 18,420 km<sup>2</sup> (7,110 sq mi), North Gyeongsang is the [largest province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Korea) in the [Korean peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea). The province was formed in 1896 from the northern half of the former [Gyeongsang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyeongsang) province, and remained a province of [Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea_under_Japanese_rule) (as [Keishōhoku-dō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keish%C5%8Dhoku-d%C5%8D) during Japanese rule) until the country's [division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_Korea) in 1945, then became part of South Korea.

[Daegu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daegu) was the capital of North Gyeongsang Province between 1896 to 2016, but has not been a part of the province since 1981. In 2016, the provincial capital moved from Daegu to [Andong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andong).[^cite_note-4]

## History

See also: [Gyeongsang Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyeongsang_Province)

The 1,000-year-old [Silla Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silla_Kingdom) flourished and unified the previous [three kingdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Kingdoms_of_Korea). The area was named Gyeongsang-do in 1314 during the [Goryeo Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goryeo_Dynasty). In 1896, during the [Joseon Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseon_Dynasty), the area was renamed Gyeongsangbuk-do as 13 provinces were reorganized. The current administrative district was reorganized with the reorganization of Bu, Gun, and Myeon in 1914.[^cite_note-5]

## Geography and climate

The province is part of the [Yeongnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeongnam) [region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Korea), bordered to the south by [South Gyeongsang Province](319775.md), to the west by [North Jeolla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Jeolla_Province) and [North Chungcheong Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Chungcheong_Province), and to the north by [Gangwon Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangwon_Province_\(South_Korea\)). It is largely surrounded by mountains: the [Taebaek Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taebaek_Mountains) in the east and the [Sobaek Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobaek_Mountains) in the west. The province also contains the island of [Ulleungdo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulleungdo), the seventh largest island in South Korea, located in the [Sea of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_of_Japan).[^cite_note-6]

## Culture

### Cuisine

Andong [Sikhye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikhye), a local food from [Andong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andong) and northern North Gyeongsang Province, is essential for entertaining guests on [Seollal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seollal) or feast days. Unlike ordinary Sikhye, it is red with [Chili powder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chili_powder), has more grains of rice, and less soup.[^cite_note-7]

### Tourism

North Gyeongsang Province is the homeland of the former kingdom of [Silla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silla) and has retained much of its cultural tradition. A number of artists, political leaders and scholars have come from the province.

## Economy

In October 2022, the province announced its plan to make the province a leading area centered on the domestic metaverse. It is also a specialized complex for [semiconductor](612813.md) and [secondary battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_battery) national high-tech strategic industries.[^cite_note-13]

### Industry

The service industry accounts for 58.3%, mining manufacturing industry account for 30.6%, construction account for 10.5%, and agriculture and fishing account for 0.6%. Mobile convergence, digital device parts, and energy material parts are major specialized industries.[^cite_note-\:0-14]

## Recent discoveries

In September 2021, archaeologists announced the discovery of 1500 years-old woman skeleton with a [necklace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necklace) and a [bracelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bracelet) in North Gyeongsang Province. The remains of a 135-centimeter-tall woman, who is estimated to have died in her 20s, were discovered along with the bones of animals such as horses and cows, as well as [earthenware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthenware).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]
