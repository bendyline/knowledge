**Typhoon Dolphin**[^cite_note-2] was a powerful, large, very long-lived and costly [tropical cyclone](8282374.md) that affected East China. The thirteenth [named storm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclone_naming), fifth typhoon and third [violent typhoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclone_intensity_scales) of the [2026 Pacific typhoon season](80781992.md), Dolphin originated from a disturbance near the [International Date Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Date_Line) in the Central Pacific in mid-July. Initially monitored by the [Central Pacific Hurricane Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Pacific_Hurricane_Center) (CPHC), it crossed the International Date Line a week later, with the [Japan Meteorological Agency](457886.md) (JMA) recognizing it as a [tropical depression](8282374.md) during the late hours of July 26. The United States' [Joint Typhoon Warning Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Typhoon_Warning_Center) (JTWC) followed suit the following day, designating it as *12W*; a few hours later, both agencies upgraded it to a tropical storm, with the former giving it the name *Dolphin*.

[^cite_note-3]

On July 19, the [Central Pacific Hurricane Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Pacific_Hurricane_Center) began monitoring a disturbance south of the [Hawaiian Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaiian_Islands). When the disturbance had a high 90% chance of development in the next two days, it was designated [Invest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invest_\(meteorology\)) 92C.[^cite_note-4]



Dolphin in the [East Pacific basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclone_basins) as [Invest 92C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invest_\(meteorology\))

Later on July 26, the disturbance crossed into the Western Pacific, and the JMA classified the system as a tropical depression upon crossover. The following day at midnight, The JTWC upgraded the disturbance to a tropical depression, designating it as *12W,*[^cite_note-5] upgraded by the JMA to a tropical storm shortly after, and received its current name *Dolphin*.[^cite_note-6] Almost immediately after its nomenclature, Dolphin began a period of [rapid intensification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_intensification), to an extent not seen since [Typhoon Noru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Noru),[^cite_note-7] quickly becoming a typhoon and reaching a Category 4-equivalent initial peak intensity on the [Saffir–Simpson scale](255313.md) on July 28, before its intensification was stunted by an [eyewall replacement cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyewall_replacement_cycle). After finishing, Dolphin resumed intensifying once again, reaching [super typhoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_typhoon) status the following day. Soon after reaching Category 5 strength on the [Saffir–Simpson scale](255313.md), Dolphin started undergoing a second eyewall replacement cycle, temporarily halting further development of the storm.[^cite_note-8] Dolphin continued moving westward which it started slowly weakening to a Category 2 equivalent typhoon after 3 days. Next, Dolphin regained intensity as Category 3 typhoon. On August 5, Dolphin started weakening again shortly to a Category-1 equivalent typhoon with 1-minute sustained winds of 90mph. After that, Dolphin passed the [Ryukyu Islands](8458939.md) and made a direct landfall over [Kume Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kume_Island). Dolphin soon made landfall over [Yuhuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuhuan) in China's [Zhejiang](96602.md) province at 17\:30 [CST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_China) on August 9, followed by a second landfall over [Wenzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wenzhou) an hour later.[^cite_note-bbc-9]

## Preparations

### Japan

Satellite loop of Typhoon Dolphin approaching Okinawa on August 6.

The [Japan Meteorological Agency](457886.md) (JMA) warned of strong winds, heavy rainfall, landslides, and flooding due to the effects of Dolphin.[^cite_note-10] Heavy rainfall warnings were issued for the [Daitō Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dait%C5%8D_Islands) as the typhoon closely approached.[^cite_note-11] Level 4 heavy rainfall warnings were also issued for [Ogimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogimi), [Higashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higashi,_Okinawa), and [Nakijin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakijin). Level 4 landslide warnings were issued for the same areas as well as [Kunigami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunigami) and [Motobu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motobu). A Level 4 storm surge warning was posted for [Kumejima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumejima), as well.[^cite_note-Watchers1-12]



Three storms active on August 9 as labeled Typhoon Dolphin (Left), Tropical Storm Peilou (Middle), and Tropical Storm Chan-hom (Top) Circulation to the south and to the East of Chan-hom later become TD 15W

More than 260,000 people in [Kagoshima](217439.md) and [Okinawa](22477.md) prefectures were advised to evacuate. The typhoon was expected to affect parts of [Kumamoto Prefecture](220713.md) impacted by [a magnitude 7.1 earthquake](83804277.md) the previous week. More than 6,700 residents remained in shelters at the time of Dolphin's arrival.[^cite_note-13] Residents in Kumamoto Prefecture covered roofs damaged by the earthquake in plastic sheets. More than 500 flights were cancelled due to the storm, with [Naha Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naha_Airport) cancelling all its flights on August 7.[^cite_note-\:0-14] [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota) ceased operations at nine factories across Japan over concerns about shipping disruptions.[^cite_note-AOL1-15] All bus companies on the island of Okinawa suspended operations as the typhoon approached.[^cite_note-16] The [Okinawa Urban Monorail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okinawa_Urban_Monorail) also suspended operations for the duration of the typhoon.[^cite_note-17] The city government of [Naha](6792923.md) opened seven temporary storm shelters.[^cite_note-18] About 240 people evacuated to storm shelters prior to the storm across Okinawa Prefecture.[^cite_note-Watchers1-12]

### China

[China Meteorological Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Meteorological_Administration) (CMA) issued a yellow typhoon warning for part of the country's east coast as Dolphin approached, and updated it to red on the noon of August 9.[^cite_note-19] Gale-force winds were expected to impact the waters of the [East China Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_China_Sea) and the [Bashi Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashi_Channel), as well as coastal areas of [Zhejiang](96602.md) and [Fujian](88585.md).[^cite_note-20] More than 1 million people were evacuated across eastern China in preparation for the storm.[^cite_note-bbc-9]

The provincial government of Zhejiang prepared 15 tow boats for emergencies. A total of 162 ferry routes and 193 water construction projects were halted for the duration of the typhoon. A total of 474 ships were also docked for the storm. Seaside tourists and sports activities in [Ningbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ningbo) were cancelled. The government of Fujian Province declared a Level 4 emergency response which advised all communities to dock fishing vessels and ferries and to secure offshore wind power platforms. Safety inspections, evacuations of high-risk zones, and the gathering of emergency resources were also required through the order.[^cite_note-SCMP1-21] Major delays occurred as ports in China closed in preparation for Dolphin. Container operations were suspended at the [Port of Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Shanghai). Temporary port suspensions were also expected to take place in Ningbo.[^cite_note-22] China's insurance industry had received nearly 130,000 claims related to Typhoon Dolphin as of August 12, with estimated losses of 1.75 billion yuan (\$259.5 million).[^cite_note-23]

The government of China used the arrival of Dolphin to test new technology for typhoons. This included the use of [artificial intelligence](1164.md) in forecasting systems at the National Marine Environmental Forecasting Centre which were used to give long-range forecasts for the storm. Drones were also deployed to aid in preparations in coastal provinces.[^cite_note-SCMP1-21]

### Philippines

Despite the system remaining outside the [Philippine Area of Responsibility](4272946.md) (PAR), rainfall warnings were put in place as the system enhanced the southwest monsoon.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] Heavy rainfall warnings were put in place over [Bataan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bataan), [Occidental Mindoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occidental_Mindoro), and [Zambales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambales), while multiple provinces plus [Metro Manila](72340.md) were put under an orange warning.[^cite_note-26]

## Impact

### Japan

In [Japan](15573.md), Dolphin reached maximum wind speeds of 162 km/h (101 mph), with [Okinawa](2113477.md) and [Amami Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amami_Islands) among the most affected regions; [Naha Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naha_Airport) of Okinawa suspended all flights on August 7.[^cite_note-\:0-14] Dolphin created havoc in parts of southern Japan, paralyzing traffic and sending hundreds to take shelter.[^cite_note-27] Preliminary damage caused by the storm was assessed at JP¥800 million (US\$5.02 million).[^cite_note-28]

### Taiwan

Taiwan was hit with heavy [rainstorms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainstorm) on August 7-8, with citizens of [Taipei](57648.md) reporting "toppled trees, downed scaffolding and collapsed construction fencing", and [Tamsui District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamsui_District) reporting blown-off roofs.[^cite_note-29] One the morning of August 9, [Taiwan](25734.md) suspended all [ferry routes](50771.md) and issued about 200 [flight cancellations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_cancellation_and_delay); its [Central Weather Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Weather_Administration) reported a maximum sustained wind speed of 119 km/h (74 mph) on 11 a.m., stating it to be about 350 kilometers north-northeast of Taipei and moving west.[^cite_note-30]

### China

In [Shanghai](27643.md), the [Shanghai Ferry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Ferry) suspended all services on August 8[^cite_note-a653-31], and all coastal tourist destinations in [Fengxian District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fengxian_District)[^cite_note-p008-32] and a number of parks[^cite_note-33] were temporarily closed. More than 1,300 flights were cancelled on August 9, and around 300,000 people in surrounding regions were evacuated in preparation.[^cite_note-34] [Shanghai Disney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Disney) and Disneytown also closed beginning that night.[^cite_note-35] Numerous metro lines either completely or partially stopped operation, with more closing down on August 10.[^cite_note-a653-31][^cite_note-36] Flooding occurred in the [French Concession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Concession).[^cite_note-bbc-9]

In Beijing, intense rain from the storm flooded key roads and stranded vehicles.[^cite_note-37]

In [Zhejiang](96602.md), [Ningbo Lishe International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ningbo_Lishe_International_Airport) suspended all flights.[^cite_note-\:0-14] A nine-year-old boy in [Wenling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wenling) was reported missing after he was swept into the sea. Two people were detained on suspicion of spreading misinformation related to the storm.[^cite_note-bbc-9] Total economic damage in China from Dolphin and other subsequent storms added up to 28.26 billion yuan (US\$4.21 billion). [^cite_note-38]

### Philippines

Further information: [2026 Philippine floods § Typhoon Dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Philippine_floods?action=edit\&redlink=1#Typhoon_Dolphin)

More than 500 passengers were stranded across 28 ports due to rough conditions brought on by the southwest monsoon that was intensified by Dolphin.[^cite_note-39] Torrential rains caused waist-deep flooding in low-lying communities and areas near creeks in [Quezon City](284223.md). Several other neighborhoods in [Manila](184334.md) and western Philippines also experienced flooding.[^cite_note-40] [Malacañang Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaca%C3%B1ang_Palace) announced sweeping suspensions on classes and work in government offices in [Metro Manila](72340.md) and 15 other provinces due to continuing heavy rains by the typhoon's enhancement of the southwest monsoon.[^cite_note-41]

### Hong Kong

[Hong Kong](13404.md) recorded its hottest ever temperature of 36.9 °C because of subsiding hot air from outside the typhoon, breaking the previous record set on 22 August 2017, before [Typhoon Hato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Hato) hit. A weather station in [Sheung Shui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheung_Shui) recorded 39.8 °C, the highest ever temperature recorded in Hong Kong since automatic weather stations were introduced in 1984.[^cite_note-42]
