# Southern Lebanon

"South Lebanon" redirects here. For other uses, see [South Lebanon (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Lebanon_\(disambiguation\)).



Southern Lebanon

**Southern Lebanon** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): جنوب لبنان, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Janūb Lubnān*) is the area of [Lebanon](17771.md) comprising the [South Governorate](1283570.md) and the [Nabatiye Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabatiye_Governorate). The two entities were divided from the same province in the early 1990s. The term may also comprise [Rashaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashaya_District) and [Western Beqaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Beqaa_District) districts, the southernmost districts of the [Beqaa Governorate](866204.md).

The main cities of the region are [Sidon](28366.md) and [Tyre](30796.md) on the coast, with [Jezzine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jezzine) and [Nabatiyeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabatiyeh) more inland. The destroyed [caza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaza) of [Bint Jbeil](6124393.md), and the cazas of Tyre, and Nabatieh in Southern Lebanon are known for their large [Shi'a Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islam_in_Lebanon) population with a minority of [Christians](4796145.md). Sidon is predominantly [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam_in_Lebanon), with the rest of the caza of Sidon having a Shi'a Muslim majority, with a considerable Christian minority, mainly [Melkite Greek Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melkite_Christianity_in_Lebanon). The cazas of Jezzine and [Marjeyoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marjeyoun) have a Christian majority and also Shia Muslims. The villages of [Ain Ebel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ain_Ebel), [Debel](9814180.md), [Qaouzah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qaouzah), and [Rmaich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rmaich) are entirely [Christian Maronite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maronite_Christianity_in_Lebanon). The caza of [Hasbaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasbaya) has a [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze_in_Lebanon) majority.

## History

### Free Lebanon State and South Lebanon security belt

Southern Lebanon became the location of the self-proclaimed [Free Lebanon State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Lebanon_State), announced in 1979 by [Saad Haddad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saad_Haddad).[^cite_note-1] The state failed to gain international recognition, and its authority deteriorated with the death of Saad Haddad in 1984.

Southern Lebanon has also featured prominently in the [Israel-Lebanon conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel-Lebanon_conflict).

### Ahmadinejad's state visit

In October 2010, [Iranian President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_President) [Mahmoud Ahmadinejad](7907317.md) visited South Lebanon. This was his first visit to Lebanon since he first assumed office in [Tehran](57654.md) in 2005. Both [Israel](9282173.md) and the [United States](3434750.md) condemned the trip as being "provocative." Ahmadinejad was welcomed by tens of thousands of supporters of [Hezbollah](13919.md), Iran's Shiite Muslim ally in Lebanon which has been [branded a terrorist organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hezbollah_foreign_relations#Designation_as_a_terrorist_organization) in part or whole by much of South America, the EU, the Arab League, the United States and Israel. This is despite its participation in Lebanon's fragile government.

### 2026 Lebanon war

In March 2026, during the [2026 Lebanon war](82549840.md), Israeli Finance Minister [Bezalel Smotrich](46196834.md) stated that Israel, following the war, should annex territories up to the [Litani River](1920925.md).[^cite_note-2]

Israeli Defense minister [Israel Katz](19041378.md) states that the military will control a "security zone" up to the [Litani River](1920925.md) in southern Lebanon until the threat of [Hezbollah](13919.md) is removed.[^cite_note-3]
