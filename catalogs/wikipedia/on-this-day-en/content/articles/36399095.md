**The Directorate General of Shipping, India&#x20;**&#x69;s an attached office under the [Ministry of Shipping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Shipping_\(India\)), [Government of India](553883.md), responsible for [life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life), [health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health), [vessel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_vessel) and the [environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_environment) for Indian registered [ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship) and ships at Indian [ports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port). The [Directorate](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/directorate), is located in [Mumbai](19189.md) and led by Director General of Shipping Shri Shyam Jagannathan, IAS[^cite_note-1] and Additional Secretary to the Government of India.

## Duties

The main job for the directorate is to ensure that Indian ships and [shipping companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shipping_company) meet high safety- and environmental standards, to ensure that Indian [seamen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailor) have high qualifications and good working- and living conditions, and to ensure that foreign ships in Indian [territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territorial_sea) and ports meet international rules.

The Director General of Shipping is vested with statutory powers under Section 7 of the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958, and is responsible for implementation of the provisions of the Act.

It ensures implementation of various international Conventions, relating to safely SOLAS, ([International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Convention_for_the_Safety_of_Life_at_Sea)) requirements for prevention of pollution, [MARPOL 73/78](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MARPOL_73/78) and other mandatory requirements of International Maritime Organization.

Shri Shyam Jagannathan,[^cite_note-2] is the current director of DG Shipping India, an IAS officer of the 1997 Assam-Meghalaya cadre. He is on central deputation since May 2021 and will serve as director at DG Shipping for three years.
