The **2026 NCAA Division I women's basketball tournament** was a 68 team [single-elimination tournament](939680.md) to determine the [National Collegiate Athletic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA) (NCAA) [Division I](669931.md) [college basketball](768856.md) national champion for the [2025–26 NCAA Division I women's basketball season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_season). The 44th edition of the [tournament](536018.md) began on March 18, 2026, with the First Four, and concluded with the [championship game](79646889.md) on April 5, at the [Mortgage Matchup Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortgage_Matchup_Center) in [Phoenix, Arizona](49121.md).[^cite_note-1] The [UCLA Bruins](80436779.md) won their first national championship in the NCAA era by defeating the [South Carolina Gamecocks](81087913.md) 79–51.

[Coastal Athletic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_Athletic_Association) (CAA) champion [Charleston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Charleston_Cougars_women's_basketball_team) was the only program in the field to make its NCAA tournament debut. With just one tournament debutant, the 2026 NCAA Tournament set the record for the fewest tournament debutants (the previous low was two, in the [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament) and [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament) editions).

[Atlantic 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_10_Conference) champion [Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Rhode_Island_Rams_women's_basketball_team) made its second-ever appearance and its first since [1996](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament). [MAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid-American_Conference) champion [Miami (OH)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Miami_RedHawks_women's_basketball_team) made its second-ever appearance and its first since [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament). [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Conference_\(NCAA\)) champion [UTSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_UTSA_Roadrunners_women's_basketball_team) made its third-ever appearance and its first time since [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament). [Southern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Conference) champion [Samford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Samford_Bulldogs_women's_basketball_team) made its third-ever appearance, its first since [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament). [Atlantic Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Sun_Conference) champion [Jacksonville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Jacksonville_Dolphins_women's_basketball_team) made its third-ever appearance, its first since [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament). [Ohio Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_Valley_Conference) champion [Western Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Western_Illinois_Leathernecks_women's_basketball_team) made its third-ever appearance, its first since [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament).

The Final Four featured the same four teams as the [2025 tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament), [UConn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_UConn_Huskies_women's_basketball_team) (who went in undefeated), South Carolina, [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Texas_Longhorns_women's_basketball_team), and UCLA. For only the fifth time in the tournament's history, all four of the number one seeds made it to the Final Four ([1989](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament), [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament), [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament), [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament)).

## Tournament procedure

Further information: [NCAA Division I women's basketball tournament § Current tournament format](536018.md#Current_tournament_format)

A total of 68 teams were invited to participate in the 2026 tournament, consisting of the 31 [conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NCAA_conferences) champions and 37 "at-large" bids that were determined by the NCAA Selection Committee. The last four at-large teams and teams seeded 65 through 68 overall competed in First Four games, whose winners advanced to the 64-team first round.[^cite_note-2]

The top 16 seeds hosted the First Four, first round, and second round at their home arenas. In a procedural change, new for this tournament, the top 16 seeds were announced in alphabetical order on Saturday, March 15, ahead of the full bracket reveal that was announced on Sunday, March 16. This was done to give host sites more time to prepare and organize the logistics required to host.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
