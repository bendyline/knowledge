**Siem Pang Wildlife Sanctuary** ([Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language): ដែនជម្រកសត្វព្រៃសៀមប៉ាង) is a 133,707.73 ha (516.2484 sq mi) wildlife sanctuary located in Santepheap, Thma Keo, and Prek Meas communes, [Siem Pang District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siem_Pang_District), [Stung Treng Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stung_Treng_Province), created on November 6, 2019, according to Sub-decree no.161 ANKr.BK.[^cite_note-SubDecree161-2019-1] This combined the two wildlife sanctuaries: Siem Pang and Western Siem Pang, which were established in 2016.

## History

- May 9, 2016: **Siem Pang Wildlife Sanctuary** ([Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language): ដែនជម្រកសត្វព្រៃសៀមប៉ាង), 66,932 ha (258.43 sq mi), created according to Sub-decree no.86 ANKr.BK.

  [^cite_note-2]

* May 9, 2016: **Western Siem Pang Wildlife Sanctuary** ([Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language): ដែនជម្រកសត្វព្រៃសៀមប៉ាងខាងលិច), 65,389 ha (252.47 sq mi), created according to Sub-decree no.76 ANKr.BK.

  [^cite_note-3]

- February 25, 2014: **Siem Pang Protected Forest** ([Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language): តំបន់ព្រៃការពារសម្រាប់អភិរក្សធនធានសេនេទិច រុក្ខជាតិ និងសត្វព្រៃសៀមប៉ាង ខេត្តស្ទឹងត្រែង, known as ព្រៃការពារសៀមប៉ាង), 66,932 ha (258.43 sq mi), created according to Sub-decree no.77 ANKr.BK, administered by [Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Agriculture,_Forestry_and_Fisheries_\(Cambodia\)).

  [^cite_note-4]

  &#x20;It was part of a large protected area complex that includes the [Xe Pian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xe_Pian) protected area in [Laos](17752.md) and the [Virachey National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virachey_National_Park) in Cambodia, and [Chư Mom Ray National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C6%B0_Mom_Ray_National_Park) in [Vietnam](202354.md). The area is important for several critically endangered bird species including the [giant ibis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_ibis) and [white-shouldered ibis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White-shouldered_ibis).

  [^cite_note-5]
