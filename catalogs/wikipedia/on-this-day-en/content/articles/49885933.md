**Madhesh Province** ([Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): मधेश प्रदेश, romanized: *Madhēś pradēś*) is a [province of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Nepal) in the [Terai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terai) region with an area of 9,661 km<sup>2</sup> (3,730 sq mi) covering about 6.5% of the country's total area. It has a population of 6,114,600 as per the [2021 Nepal census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Nepal_census), making it Nepal's most densely populated province and the smallest province by area.[^cite_note-Nepal_Census_Report-6][^cite_note-7] It borders [Koshi Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koshi_Pradesh) to the east and the north, [Bagmati Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagmati_Province) to the north, and [India](14533.md)’s [Bihar](442482.md) state to the south and the west. The border between [Chitwan National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitwan_National_Park) and [Parsa National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsa_National_Park) acts as the provincial boundary in the west, and the [Kosi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosi_River) forms the provincial border in the east. The province includes eight districts, from [Parsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsa_District) in the west to [Saptari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saptari_District) in the east. It is a centre for religious and cultural tourism.[^cite_note-Samiti-8]

## Etymology

The word *madhesh* is thought to be derived from the [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit) *madhya desh* (मध्य देश), literally the middle country, which refers to "the central region, the country lying between the Himalaya and the [Vindhya mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vindhya_Range)".[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKabir201311-9][^cite_note-10] However, in the context of Nepal, *Madhesh* refers to the region in the Nepal Terai located south of the [Siwalik Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siwalik_Hills).[^cite_note-Mishra2007-11][^cite_note-12] Madhesh has also been defined as the cultural and linguistic space existing as a basis for identity among the people of the Terai.[^cite_note-13]

## History

Madhesh Province was founded with the provisional name Province No. 2 in September 2015 in accordance with Schedule 4 of the [Constitution of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal).[^cite_note-14] It received its current name on 17 January 2022, when the provincial assembly declared [Janakpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janakpur) as its capital.[^cite_note-15]

## Geography

As per Central Bureau of Statistics, Madhesh Province covers about 9,661 km<sup>2</sup> (3,730 sq mi) of Nepal's total area of 147,516 km<sup>2</sup> (56,956 sq mi). With 6,114,600 inhabitants as of 2021, it is Nepal's second most populous province.[^cite_note-2021_Nepal_census-3][^cite_note-statoids-16] Madhesh Province is surrounded by the [Chitwan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitwan_District) to the west, [Makwanpur District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makwanpur_District) and [Sindhuli District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhuli_District) and [Udayapur District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udayapur_District) to the north, [Sunsari District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunsari_District) to the east, and [India](14533.md) to the south.[^cite_note-17] The province has 574,360 ha (1,419,300 acres) of [arable land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arable_land), making it the country's most agriculture-dominated province. The west of the province contains part of [Parsa National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsa_National_Park), while part of [Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koshi_Tappu_Wildlife_Reserve) lies within the east. The province is biodiverse, and is crossed by migrating elephants.[^cite_note-18]

## Demographics

According to the 2021 Nepal census, the province has a population of 6,114,600: 3,065,751 males and 3,048,849 females. The province with 20.97% of the country's population has the second-highest population after [Bagmati Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagmati_Province) in the country, and is the densest province in the country with a density of 633 people per square kilometre.[^cite_note-2021_Nepal_census-3]

### Ethnic groups

[Maithils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maithils) are the largest [ethnolinguistic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnolinguistic_group). [Yadav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yadav) is the largest group among the Madhesi people in the province making up around 15.2% of the population. [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Nepal) are the second largest group making up 13% of the population. [Teli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teli) (5.3%), [Kushwaha(koeri)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kushwaha) (4.5%), [Chamar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamar) (4.4%), [Kurmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurmi) (3.0%), [Musahar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musahar) (2.9%), [Dusadh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dusadh) (2.79%), [Mallaah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mallaah) (2.26%), [Maithil Brahmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maithil_Brahmin) (2.2%), and [Karan Kayastha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karan_Kayastha) (1.5%) are other Madhesi pandit (8.0) groups in the province. [Bahun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahun) and [Chhetri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chhetri) are the largest [Khas Arya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khas_Arya) groups in the province making up 2.34% and 1.99% of the population, respectively. [Tharu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tharu_people) (5.27%) and [Dhanuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhanuk) (3.49%) is the largest non-Madhesi, non-Khas Arya group followed by [Tamang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamang_people) (2.17%).[^cite_note-nationaldata-5]

### Languages

The [Maithili language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maithili_language) is spoken by 45.36% of the total population of the province. [Bhojpuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhojpuri_language) is spoken by 18.81% and [Bajjika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bajjika) is spoken by 18.44% of the population. [Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language) is the province's official language, but is spoken as mother tongue by only 5.76% of the population. [Tharu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tharu_languages) (4.17%), [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language) (4.08%), and [Tamang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamang_language) (1.65%) are other languages spoken by a minority of the population.[^cite_note-mothertongue-20]

The [Language Commission of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_Commission_\(Nepal\)) has recommended Maithili, Bhojpuri and Bajjika as official languages of the province, and Urdu, Tharu and Tamang as additional official languages for specific regions and purposes in the province.[^cite_note-langcomreport-4]

## Government and administration

Main article: [Government of Madhesh Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Madhesh_Province)

### Executive

Main article: [Saroj Kumar Yadav cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saroj_Kumar_Yadav_cabinet)

The Governor acts as the head of the province while the Chief Minister is the head of the provincial government. The present Governor and Chief Minister are [Surendra Labh Karn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surendra_Labh_Karn?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Krishna Prasad Yadav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishna_Prasad_Yadav_\(Nepali_politician\)) respectively.

### Legislative

Main articles: [Madhesh Provincial Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhesh_Provincial_Assembly) and [2nd Madhesh Provincial Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Madhesh_Provincial_Assembly)

The province has 107 [provincial assembly constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pradesh_Sabha) and 32 constituencies.[^cite_note-22]

Madhesh Province has a [unicameral](204304.md) legislature, like all of the other provinces in Nepal. The term length of provincial assembly is five years. The [Provincial Assembly of Madhesh Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_Assembly_of_Madhesh_Province) is temporarily housed at the District Education Office in [Janakpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janakpur,_Nepal).[^cite_note-23]

### Judiciary

The Chief Judge of the Janakpur High Court is the head of the judiciary.[^cite_note-24] The acting chief justice is Binod Sharma.[^cite_note-25]

### Mithila Paag

Main article: [Paag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paag)



Mithila Paag

The **Paag** is a headdress in the [Mithila region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mithila,_India) of [Nepal](171166.md) and [India](14533.md) worn by [Maithil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maithil) people. It is a symbol of honour and respect and a significant part of [Maithil culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mithila_culture). The Paag dates back to pre-historic times when it was made of plant leaves. It exists today in a modified form. The Paag is wore by the whole Maithil community. The colour of the Paag also carries a lot of significance. The red Paag is worn by the [bridegroom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridegroom) and by those who are undergoing the [sacred thread rituals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upanayana). Paag of mustard colour is donned by those attending wedding ceremonies and the elders wear a white Paag.

Madhubani_art

### Dances

#### Jhijhiya

Main article: [Jhijhiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhijhiya)

Jhijhiya is a cultural dance from the [Mithila region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mithila_\(region\)).[^cite_note-33]



Women performing Jhijhiya dance

Jhijhiya is mostly performed at time of [Dusshera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayadashami), in dedication to Durga [Bhairavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhairavi), the goddess of victory.[^cite_note-Kumari1999-34] While performing jhijhiya, women put lanterns made of clay on their head and they balance it while they dance.[^cite_note-35]

#### Domkach

Main article: [Domkach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domkach)

Domkach is a folk dance performed in [Mithila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mithila_\(region\)) and [Bhojpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhojpuri_region) regions of Madhesh Province.[^cite_note-36]

**Jat Jatin**

Jat Jatin is based on folk songs of Mithila and Tharu community which they perform from Shrawan Purnima to Bhadra Purnima. It is based on the love story of hero jat and heroine Jatin and their lives.[^cite_note-37]

**Gauna dance**

The Dance is based in Mithila Tradition and popular in Janakpur region of Nepal. This dance is performed in the religious occasions.

## Politics

As a political center of the country, Madhesh Province is home to prominent leaders like [Ram Baran Yadav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ram_Baran_Yadav), [Bimalendra Nidhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bimalendra_Nidhi) and [Pradeep Giri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pradip_Giri) from the [Nepali Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_Congress), [Mahantha Thakur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahantha_Thakur) from [Loktantrik Samajwadi Party, Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loktantrik_Samajwadi_Party,_Nepal), [Matrika Yadav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrika_Prasad_Yadav) from [CPN (Maoist Centre)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Nepal_\(Maoist_Centre\)) and [Dharmanath Prasad Sah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharmanath_Prasad_Sah), [Bansidhar Mishra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bansidhar_Mishra), [Ram Chandra Jha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ram_Chandra_Jha) from the [CPN (Unified Socialist)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Nepal_\(Unified_Socialist\)) who have been ministers at various point of time and are still active at National level.[^cite_note-38] In local level, [Nepali congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_Congress,_Madhesh_Province) remains the single largest party.[^cite_note-39]
