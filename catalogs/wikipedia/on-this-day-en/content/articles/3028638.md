The **Southwest Region** or **South-West Region** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Région du Sud-Ouest*) is a [region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Cameroon) with [special status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_region) in [Cameroon](5447.md). Its capital is [Buea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buea).[^cite_note-3] As of 2015, its population was 1,553,320. Along with the [Northwest Region](646814.md), it is one of the two [Anglophone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglophone) (English-speaking) regions of Cameroon. Various [Ambazonian](841514.md) nationalist and separatist factions regard the South-West region as being distinct as a polity from Cameroon.[^cite_note-4]

## History

In 1884, the region was colonized by [Germany](11867.md) under the [Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protectorate) regime until 1916 when it became a [condominium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condominium_\(international_law\)) administered jointly by the [United Kingdom](31717.md) and [France](5843419.md).[^cite_note-5] In 1919, the administration of the South West region became solely British. In 1961, the region joined Cameroon as part of the federated state of [West Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Cameroon).[^cite_note-6]

At the end of 2017, an [Ambazonian separatist](841514.md) movement in the two English-speaking regions of the [North-West](646814.md) and South-West initiated a wave of violence affecting the military, police, business leaders and workers. The separatist militiamen are trying in particular to prevent children from returning to school. Between 2016 and 2019, separatists reportedly ransacked, destroyed or burned more than 174 schools[^cite_note-7] Separatists from the [Ambazonia](841514.md) administration regard both the Northwest and Southwest regions as being constituent components of their envisaged breakaway state.[^cite_note-8]

## Administration



Map of Southwest

The region is divided into six [divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_\(country_subdivision\)) or [departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Cameroon): [Fako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fako_\(department\)), [Koupé-Manengouba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koup%C3%A9-Manengouba), [Lebialem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebialem), [Manyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manyu_\(department\)), [Meme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meme_\(department\)) and [Ndian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ndian). These are in turn broken down into subdivisions.[^cite_note-9] Presidentially appointed senior divisional officers (*prefets*) and subdivisional officers (*sous-prefets*) govern each respectively.

### Special status

As part of the [Major National Dialogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_National_Dialogue), the region was given a "special status" in December 2019, granting additional rights and responsibilities in relation to economic, health, social, educational, sports and cultural development.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] Under the special status the region has a bicameral Regional Assembly, made up of a 20-member House of Chiefs composed of traditional leaders and a 70-member House of Divisional Representatives nominated by municipal councils. The Regional Assembly appoints a Regional Executive Council.[^cite_note-\:0-12] Additional powers over health and education were also granted to municipalities.[^cite_note-13]

## Geography

The region was notable for having the first English-speaking university in Cameroon (the University of Buea). Towns include the capital [Buea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buea), [Limbe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limbe,_Cameroon), [Tiko](1978638.md), [Ekondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekondo_Titti?action=edit\&redlink=1) , [Mundemba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mundemba), [Kumba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumba,_Cameroon) and [Mamfe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamfe). Limbe in particular is a popular tourist resort notable for its fine beaches. [Korup National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korup_National_Park) situated in the Ndian Division is also a major attraction. Buea itself, meanwhile, sits at the foot of [Mount Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Cameroon), and possesses an almost temperate climate markedly different from the rest of the province.
