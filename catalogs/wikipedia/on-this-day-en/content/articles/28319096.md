# List of county councils in England



Two-tier non-metropolitan county councils in England (red).

There are currently 23 [county councils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_council) in England, which serve areas known as [non-metropolitan counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-metropolitan_county). 21 of the county councils serve as the upper-tier authorities in a [two-tier arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-tier_local_government_in_England); their areas are divided into a number of [non-metropolitan districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-metropolitan_district), which each have a district council.[^cite_note-Municipal-1] [Durham County Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_County_Council) and [Northumberland County Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northumberland_County_Council) are [unitary authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitary_authorities_of_England), which combine county and district functions.

The first county councils were created in 1889 under the [Local Government Act 1888](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Act_1888). There was a significant reform of the number, powers and jurisdictions of county councils [in 1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Act_1972). County councils were also established for the [metropolitan counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_county) created in 1974, but the metropolitan county councils were all abolished in 1986. From 1995 onwards numerous unitary authorities have been created.
