- [Rumour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumour_\(restaurant\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), one of Hungary's seven Michelin-1-star-rated (in 2023) restaurants

  [^cite_note-1-star-3]

- [Salt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_\(restaurant\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), one of Hungary's seven Michelin-1-star-rated (in 2023) restaurants

  [^cite_note-1-star-3]

- [Stand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stand_\(restaurant\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), Hungarian cuisine,

  [^cite_note-2-star-8]

  [^cite_note-9]

  [^cite_note-10]

  &#x20;and they won the Károly Gundel Prize in 2019.

  [^cite_note-GundelPrize-1]

  [^cite_note-11]

  &#x20;he was previously the first Hungarian to reach the world finals, as winner of the European-level Bocuse d'Or in 2012 and 2016.
