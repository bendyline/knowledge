# Dangam District



Dangam District shown in dark pink in the east

**Dangam District** is situated in the eastern end of [Kunar Province](2159600.md) in [Afghanistan](737.md). It borders [Marawara district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marawara_district) to the southwest, [Asadabad district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asadabad_district) to the west, [Bar Kunar district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar_Kunar_district) to the north, [Nari district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nari_district) to the northeast and [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) in [Pakistan](23235.md) to the east. The district is mountainous, but it is also economically underdeveloped.[^cite_note-1]

The population of Dangam is approximately 18,803 (2019).[^cite_note-2] Of this, around 98% are [Pashtuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns) and the remaining 2% are [Tajiks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajiks).[^cite_note-3] There are 50 sub-villages and the district center is **Dangam** ([34°59′33″N 71°25′08″E  /  34.9925°N 71.4189°E  / 34.9925; 71.4189](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Dangam_District\&params=34.9925_N_71.4189_E_)) at 1356 m altitude, located in the central part of the district.

Dangam and the region in general has been known for [military](57836785.md) actions against [militant](30635.md) groups.[^cite_note-4] It was reported in 2018 that [Pakistani military](23369.md) entered Dangam District in helicopters to conduct air strikes.[^cite_note-5]
