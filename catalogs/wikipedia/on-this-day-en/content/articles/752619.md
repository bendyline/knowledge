**Sulaymaniyah Governorate** ([Sorani Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorani_Kurdish_language): پارێزگای سلێمانی; [Kurmanji Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurmanji_Kurdish_language): *Parêzgeha Silêmaniyê*;[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة السليمانية) or **Sulaymaniyah Province** is a [governorate](222800.md) in the [Kurdistan Region](3949386.md) of [Iraq](7515928.md). Its capital and largest city is [Sulaymaniyah](653537.md). [Halabja Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halabja_Governorate) was formerly the [Halabja District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halabja_District,_Sulaymaniyah_Governorate) of Sulaymaniyah, until it became a separate governorate in 2014.

## Geography

Sulaymaniyah Governorate lies in the southeastern part of the [Kurdistan Region of Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdistan_Region_of_Iraq), bordered by [Iranian Kurdistan](61733686.md) to the east, [Kirkuk](199292.md) to the west, [Halabja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halabja) to the southeast, and [Erbil](24534207.md) to the northwest. It is one of the most culturally and geographically diverse areas of Kurdistan, known for its rolling green mountains, fertile plains, deep valleys, and a climate that shifts dramatically between the lowlands and the high ranges.

The governorate is the largest by area in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, and its landscape can be divided broadly into three zones:

1. The northern and eastern highlands marked by rugged mountain ranges. Stretching from [Pishdar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pishdar?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Sharbazher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharbazher) regions in the north to [Penjwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penjwen?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Hawraman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawraman) in the east and southeast along the Iranian border to outlier ranges such as [Piramagrun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piramagrun?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Qaradagh mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qaradagh_mountains?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the west and southwest. Part of the great [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains), the highlands are known for their steep mountain chains, deep valleys, cool climate and rich biodiversity. Elevations here range from 1200 m to over 3000 meters above sea level, creating dramatic contrasts in both temperature and vegetation.

2. The central hills and valleys zone as two somewhat disjointed enclaves separated by a narrow valley between the Piramagrun and [Haibat Sultan mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haibat_Sultan_mountain?action=edit\&redlink=1) chains. The northern zone is the basin of [Lake Dukan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Dukan) including the [Ranya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranya) and Pishdar plains in the north-northeast, to the town of [Dukan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dukan_District) in the south, and the [Little Zab river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Zab_river) valley. The southern zone is the heartland of the governorate and the most populated area, stretching from the Azmar-Goizha mountain chain in the north to the [Sirwan river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirwan_river?action=edit\&redlink=1) basin in the south, and from [Darbandikhan Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darbandikhan_Lake?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the west to Penjwen's foothills in the east, including most of the fertile [Sharazur plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharazur_plain?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the cultural center of the governorate itself: [Sulaymaniyah](653537.md). It's a transitional landscape between the mountains and the plains, characterized by elevations from 400 to 1200 m above sea level, moderate climate, fertile soil and human settlement.

3. The western and southern plains, representing part of the wide open plains beyond the mountains, known to Kurds as [Garmian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garmian), meaning "the warm place". This zone borders [Diyala](377806.md) and [Kirkuk](199292.md) to the south and west. It includes the districts of [Kalar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalar,_Iraq), [Kifri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kifri), [Khanaqin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khanaqin) (disputed) and parts of [Chamchamal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamchamal) and [Darbandikhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darbandikhan), forming a broad, low-lying landscape along the Sirwan river basin. This Garmian region is the frontier separating the Zagros Mountains in the northeast from the Mesopotamian plains in the southwest. Characterized by warm temperature, agricultural flat land, low ridges, river valleys, and distant horizons. The main urban center here is Kalar, situated along the Sirwan river.
