**Meurthe-et-Moselle** (French pronunciation: [\[mœʁt e mɔzɛl\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/f/f1/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Meurthe-et-Moselle.wav/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Meurthe-et-Moselle.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/f/f1/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Meurthe-et-Moselle.wav/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Meurthe-et-Moselle.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>, lit. 'Meurthe and Moselle') is a [department](38523.md) in the [Grand Est](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Est) [region](58846.md) of [France](5843419.md), named after the rivers [Meurthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meurthe_\(river\)) and [Moselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moselle). Its prefecture and largest city is [Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy,_France) and it borders the departments of [Meuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meuse_\(department\)) to the west, [Vosges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vosges_\(department\)) to the south, and [Moselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moselle_\(department\)) and [Bas-Rhin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bas-Rhin) to the east, and it also borders the [Belgian](3343.md) province of [Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg_Province) and the country of [Luxembourg](17515.md) by the [canton of Esch-sur-Alzette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Esch-sur-Alzette) to the north. It had a population of 732,236 in 2023.[^cite_note-pophist-3]

## History

Meurthe-et-Moselle was created in 1871 at the end of the [Franco-Prussian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco-Prussian_War) from the parts of the former departments of [Moselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moselle_\(department\)) and [Meurthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meurthe_\(department\)) which remained French territory.

The current boundary between Meurthe-et-Moselle and Moselle was the border between France and [Germany](11867.md) from 1871 to 1919 and again between 1940 and 1944. The only subsequent change took place in 1997 and involved the incorporation, for administrative reasons, of the little commune of [Han-devant-Pierrepont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han-devant-Pierrepont) which had previously fallen within the [Meuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meuse_\(department\)) department.[^cite_note-4]

## Geography

Meurthe-et-Moselle is part of the [administrative region](58846.md) of [Grand Est](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Est) and the traditional region of [Lorraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorraine_\(region\)) and is surrounded by the departments of [Meuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meuse_\(department\)), [Vosges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vosges_\(department\)), [Bas-Rhin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bas-Rhin), and [Moselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moselle_\(department\)), and by the nations of [Luxembourg](17515.md) and [Belgium](3343.md) by the [salient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salient_\(geography\)) of the [arrondissement of Val-de-Briey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrondissement_of_Val-de-Briey). It is one of two departments in France which border with Luxembourg. Parts of Meurthe-et-Moselle belong to the [Lorraine Regional Natural Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorraine_Regional_Natural_Park).

The department extends for 130 km from north to south and is between 7 and 103 km wide.

Its chief rivers are the [Moselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moselle), the [Meurthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meurthe_\(river\)), the [Chiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiers), and the [Vezouze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vezouze).

## Economy

The economy was highly dependent on mining until the 1960s. There are iron, salt, and lime extraction sites. The urban area around [Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy,_France) has an economy based largely on services, research, and higher education.

## Transport

The department is served by [Metz–Nancy–Lorraine Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metz%E2%80%93Nancy%E2%80%93Lorraine_Airport) which provides scheduled air service to several cities in France and some North African countries. However, other airports such as [Luxembourg Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg_Airport) and [Strasbourg Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg_Airport) are also used by air travellers from the department.

## Demographics

The inhabitants of the department [are known as](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demonym) *Meurthe-et-Mosellans*. The area around Nancy has become highly urbanized, whereas the Saintois in the south is quite rural.
