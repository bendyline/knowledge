On 9 December 2025, a fire occurred at a seven-story [shophouse](1499523.md), the Terra Drone Building, on Jalan Letjen Suprapto, [Cempaka Baru, Kemayoran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cempaka_Baru,_Kemayoran), [Jakarta](16275.md), Indonesia.[^cite_note-1] The incident resulted in 22 fatalities and the massive deployment of [firefighters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_firefighting#Indonesia).

## Fire

Wikinews logo

Wikinews has related news:&#x20;

- [ Battery explosion triggers fatal office fire in Jakarta’s Kemayoran district](https://en.wikinews.org/wiki/Battery%20explosion%20triggers%20fatal%20office%20fire%20in%20Jakarta%E2%80%99s%20Kemayoran%20district)

The fire was suspected to have started in a warehouse on the first floor of the building, with strong suspicions that it originated from a burning [lithium battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium-ion_battery).[^cite_note-2] A statement from the Central [Jakarta Metro Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Jakarta_Metropolitan_Regional_Police#Inside_Jakarta) Chief, Senior Commissioner Susatyo Purnomo Condro, confirmed this suspicion, although there are other suspicions regarding an electrical [short circuit](235898.md) as the initial trigger.[^cite_note-3] An employee also reported that a loud explosion was heard before the fire grew.[^cite_note-4] Although employees attempted to extinguish the fire on the first floor, the fire spread rapidly because the floor functioned as a storage [warehouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warehouse).[^cite_note-5]

The growing fire then produced thick, dense smoke that quickly filled the second and third floors and spread up to the sixth floor.[^cite_note-6] At the time of the incident, employees were scattered; most were outside the building for lunch, while the rest were resting on floors 2 to 6.[^cite_note-7] Employees trapped on the upper floors immediately panicked and many tried to save themselves by running to the roof of the building.[^cite_note-8]

[Firefighters (Damkar)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_firefighting#Indonesia) arrived at the location at around 12.50 WIB and deployed a total of 28 [fire trucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_truck) and more than 100 personnel to handle the situation.[^cite_note-9] The evacuation process was dramatic; trapped employees shouted for help from the highest point of the building.[^cite_note-10] Officers evacuated victims who were on the rooftop using emergency stairs from the fire trucks installed on the side of the building.[^cite_note-11] A total of 19 people were successfully rescued, including those who jumped to the next building, although some experienced [shortness of breath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shortness_of_breath).[^cite_note-12]

The extinguishing process faced serious obstacles due to the very thick smoke, making it difficult for officers to penetrate and comb through each floor, especially the sixth floor.[^cite_note-13] This condition forced officers to break through the building's windows, which resulted in several police officers being injured.[^cite_note-14] The fire was successfully tamed in the afternoon, and continued with the cooling process and final combing.[^cite_note-15]

## Victims

This incident claimed a total of 22 lives, as confirmed by the police.[^cite_note-16] The victims consisted of 7 men and 15 women, one of them being a pregnant woman.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-\:0-18] The majority of the victims who died were found not with burns, but were suspected to have died from suffocation due to inhaling thick smoke and lack of oxygen.[^cite_note-19] All the bodies of the victims were evacuated to the Kramat Jati Police Hospital, [East Jakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Jakarta), for further identification.[^cite_note-20]

## Investigation and impact

The Terra Drone Building fire caused severe traffic jams on Jalan Letjen Suprapto due to the deployment of emergency units.[^cite_note-21] Until now, the exact cause of the fire is still under investigation by the [Indonesian National Police](18251498.md) Forensic Laboratory (Labfor) team, while material losses due to this incident are also still being recorded.[^cite_note-22] The [Greater Jakarta Metropolitan Regional Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Jakarta_Metropolitan_Regional_Police) has stated the main director of Terra Drone with the initial MW as a suspect.[^cite_note-\:0-18]
