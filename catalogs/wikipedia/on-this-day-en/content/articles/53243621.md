# Half marathon world record progression



[Abraham Kiptum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Kiptum), ran a half marathon in 2018 in a time of 58\:18, which would have been a world record (world record 58\:23), but in 2019 this world record was nullified because of a doping violation verdict.

[^cite_note-1]

The **[world record](3443107.md) in the [half marathon](1019031.md)** has been officially recognized since 1 January 2004 by [World Athletics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Athletics), the international [governing body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sport_governing_body) for the [sport of athletics](43719.md). A total of five men's world records and six women's world records have been officially ratified since that date. The IAAF officially recognized the fastest times before that date as a "world best" from 1 January 2003 onwards. Before that date, the IAAF did not recognize any [road running](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_running) world records,[^cite_note-IAAF-2] though the concept of a world record was recognized by other organizations, such as the [Association of Road Racing Statisticians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Road_Racing_Statisticians) (ARRS).[^cite_note-ARRS1-3]

The men's world record in the half marathon was set at a time of 56\:51 by [Yomif Kejelcha](46932262.md) of Ethiopia on August 23, 2026, at the [Buenos Aires Half Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires_Half_Marathon)[^cite_note-4]. [Yomif Kejelcha](46932262.md) reclaimed the record from [Jacob Kiplimo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Kiplimo) of Uganda who previously set the record at 57\:20 during the March 8, 2026 EDP Lisbon Half Marathon. [Yomif Kejelcha](46932262.md)'s previous 57\:30 world record was set at the 27 October 2024 [Valencia Half Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia_Half_Marathon). A time of 56\:42, set by [Jacob Kiplimo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Kiplimo) of Uganda on 16 February 2025 in the [Barcelona Half Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona_Half_Marathon), was not ratified due to illegal pacing.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

The women's record is 1\:02\:52, set by [Letesenbet Gidey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letesenbet_Gidey) on 24 October 2021, at the [Valencia Half Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia_Half_Marathon). The previous record of 1\:04\:02 was set by Ruth Chepngetich of Kenya on 4 April 2021, in Istanbul, Turkey. On 8 September 2019, [Brigid Kosgei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigid_Kosgei) ran a time of 1\:04\:28 at the 2019 [Great North Run](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_North_Run) in [Newcastle, England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_on_Tyne). This was 23 seconds faster than the previous best, but the Great North Run was not eligible for record purposes.[^cite_note-7] The IAAF has since 2011 also kept records for the fastest time run by women in women-only races (i.e. without male [pacemakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacemaker_\(running\))). The best time for that category is held by [Peres Jepchirchir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peres_Jepchirchir), who ran 1\:05\:16 in [Gdynia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gdynia) on 17 October 2020. There was some criticism of this change, as the IAAF originally intended to downgrade world records set in mixed-gender races to "world best" status. Still, in response the organization agreed to maintain historic marks as official.[^cite_note-8]

Races close to the official half marathon distance of 21.0975 kilometers (13.1094 mi) had taken place throughout the early 20th century, and athletes had also been timed at the midpoint of full [marathons](49132.md), but the first half marathon races proper emerged in the 1960s. Some of that era, such as the [Route du Vin Half Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Route_du_Vin_Half_Marathon) and [San Blas Half Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Blas_Half_Marathon?action=edit\&redlink=1) (which both took the official distance in 1966) are extant today.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] The earliest half marathon world record accepted by the [Association of Track and Field Statisticians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Track_and_Field_Statisticians) is that of 67\:01 minutes run by Englishman [Brian Hill-Cottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Hill-Cottingham?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Romford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romford) in 1960.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] For women, the earliest ARRS-recognised time is that of American [Kathy Gibbons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathy_Gibbons), who finished the distance in 83\:56 on 7 March 1971 in [Phoenix, Arizona](49121.md).[^cite_note-ARRS1-3] The earliest men's and women's marks recognized as world records by the IAAF are 65\:44 set by [Ron Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Hill) in 1965 and 75\:04 set by [Marty Cooksey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marty_Cooksey) in 1978.[^cite_note-IAAF-2]

On 30 March 1991, [Arturo Barrios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arturo_Barrios) ran a world record distance of 21.101 km in [one hour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hour_run), becoming the first man to run the half marathon distance in under one hour.[^cite_note-13] On 3 April 1993, [Moses Tanui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moses_Tanui) became the first man to run a half marathon race in under one hour, with a time of 59\:47.[^cite_note-14]
