The **Portugal women's national futsal team** represents [Portugal](23033.md) in international [futsal](188197.md) competitions and is controlled by the [Federação Portuguesa de Futebol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federa%C3%A7%C3%A3o_Portuguesa_de_Futebol).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
