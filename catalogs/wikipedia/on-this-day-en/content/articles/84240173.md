**Pratkūnai** is a village in [Kaišiadorys District Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai%C5%A1iadorys_District_Municipality), [Lithuania](17675.md), 3 km from [Kruonis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kruonis).

On 15 September 2026 during the [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md), a drone was being shot down by a [NATO](21133.md) fighter jet over the village that had crossed from [Belarusian](3457.md) [airspace](353557.md) into Lithuania.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
