**Phthiotis** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Φθιώτιδα, *Fthiótida* [\[fθi'otiða\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Greek); [ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek) and [Katharevousa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katharevousa): Φθιῶτις) is one of the [regional units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_units_of_Greece) of [Roumeli, Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Greece_\(geographic_region\)). It is part of the administrative region of [Central Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Greece_\(administrative_region\)). The capital is the city of [Lamia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamia_\(city\)). It is bordered by the [Malian Gulf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malian_Gulf) to the east, [Boeotia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeotia) in the south, [Phocis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phocis) in the south, [Aetolia-Acarnania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aetolia-Acarnania) in the southwest, [Evrytania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evrytania) in the west, [Karditsa regional unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karditsa_\(regional_unit\)) in the north, [Larissa regional unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larissa_\(regional_unit\)) in the north, and [Magnesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnesia_\(regional_unit\)) in the northeast. The name dates back to ancient [Achaea Phthiotis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaea_Phthiotis) and Homeric [Phthia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phthia), which is best known as the home of [Achilles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achilles).

## Geography



[Thermopylae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermopylae) pass



[Mount Oeta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Oeta)

Phthiotis covers the northern and southern shorelines of the [Malian Gulf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malian_Gulf), an inlet of the [Aegean Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aegean_Sea). It stretches inland towards the west along the valley of the river [Spercheios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spercheios). In the south it covers the upper part of the [Cephissus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cephissus_\(Boeotia\)) valley. There are several mountain ranges in Phthiotis, including the [Othrys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Othrys) in the northeast, the [Tymfristos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tymfristos) in the west, the [Vardousia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vardousia) in the southwest, [Oeta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Oeta) in the south and the [Kallidromo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kallidromo) in the southeast.

### Mythology

"Phthiotis" derives its name from "the region of [Phthia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phthia)", the Homeric home of [Achilles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achilles), possibly located in the southernmost region of [ancient Thessaly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Thessaly) around [Pharsalus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farsala). The [Catalogue of Ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalogue_of_Ships) in Iliad speaks of Achilles' kingdom (Hom. Il. 2.680-5):

> Now again all those who dwelt in [Pelasgic Argos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argos_Pelasgikon):\
> &#x20;those who dwelt in [Alos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halos_\(Thessaly\)) and [Alope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alope_\(Thessaly\)) and [Trachis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trachis)\
> &#x20;and those who held Phthia and Hellas with its fair women,\
> &#x20;and who were called [Myrmidons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myrmidons) and Hellenes and [Achaians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaeans_\(Homer\));\
> &#x20;of those fifty ships the leader was [Achilles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achilles).

These names are generally believed to have referred to places mostly in the [Spercheios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spercheios) valley in what is now Phthiotis in Central Greece.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

In [Classical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_Greece) times, Phthiotis referred to the region of [Achaea Phthiotis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaea_Phthiotis), which bordered Thessalian Phthiotis from the south and east. Achaea Phthiotis covered the northern part of the present-day regional unit of Phthiotis and the southern part of present-day [Magnesia (regional unit)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnesia_\(regional_unit\)).&#x20;

The southeastern part of present-day Phthiotis covered the ancient region of [Opuntian Locris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opuntian_Locris). The southwestern part was ancient [Malis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malians_\(Greek_tribe\)) and [Ainis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ainis).

## Transport

The [Piraeus–Platy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus%E2%80%93Platy_railway) and [Leianokladi–Stylida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leianokladi%E2%80%93Stylida_railway) railways pass through Phthiotis, with both lines meeting at [Leianokladi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leianokladi_railway_station). Other main train stations in the regional unit include [Lamia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamia_railway_station) and [Stylida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stylida_railway_station), both on the Leianokladi–Stylida railway.

Phthiotis also has two motorways, the [A1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_motorway_\(Greece\)) (part of European routes [E65](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_route_E65) and [E75](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_route_E75)) and [A3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A3_motorway_\(Greece\)), with the A3 branching off the A1 near [Anthili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthili?action=edit\&redlink=1). Many national roads pass through the regional unit as well, including the [EO1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_1), [EO1a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_1a), [EO3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_3), [EO27](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_27), [EO38](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_38), the [Lamia Eastern Bypass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamia_Eastern_Bypass) and the [Thermopylae–Antirrio National Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermopylae%E2%80%93Antirrio_National_Road).

### Prefecture

The prefecture [Phthiotis and Phocis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phthiotis_and_Phocis_Prefecture) was created in 1845. In 1947 this prefecture was split into the southern part [Phocis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phocis) and the northern part Phthiotis. As a part of the 2011 Kallikratis government reform, the regional unit Phthiotis was created out of the former prefecture Phthiotis ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Νομός Φθιώτιδας). The prefecture had the same territory as the present regional unit. At the same time, the municipalities were reorganised, according to the table below.[^cite_note-Kallikratis-4]
