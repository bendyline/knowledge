The **war on cartels**, also known as **Operation Southern Spear**, is a United States military and surveillance campaign launched in 2025 by the [administration](78298108.md) of US president [Donald Trump](4848272.md) with the declared aims of "detecting, disrupting, and degrading transnational criminal and illicit maritime networks".[^cite_note-53] Some analysts say the operation is a hybridization of the [war on terror](13425800.md) and the [war on drugs](1181646.md). Others have said that [removal](82021123.md) of [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md) from power in [Venezuela](32374.md) was an objective of the operation.

In mid-August 2025, the US began [deploying military forces to the Caribbean Sea](80987786.md), amid [heightened tensions](2071275.md) between the US and Venezuela during the [crisis in Venezuela](52966258.md) and as the Trump administration sought to slow the flow of illicit drugs. In September, the U.S. military began conducting [airstrikes on vessels](80956668.md) the Trump administration alleged were controlled by [narco-terrorists](221350.md). By late November, with the [Cartel of the Suns](45272133.md) designated by the US as a [Foreign Terrorist Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Terrorist_Organization) and a [Specially Designated Global Terrorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specially_Designated_Global_Terrorist) organization, a new phase of operations began. In December, the Trump administration declared the Maduro government a Foreign Terrorist Organization, and began [boarding and seizing sanctioned crude tankers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_naval_quarantine_during_Operation_Southern_Spear).[^cite_note-54] In late December, the US made its [first strike on a land target within Venezuela](80956668.md#Land_targets_in_Venezuela). On 3 January 2026, the US [bombed targets in Caracas](82021123.md) and other locations, and captured Maduro and flew him out of the country.

Later in January, NPR reported that "drug-seizure data suggest that the attacks are doing little to stop the flow of illegal narcotics into the United States."[^cite_note-55]

On March 6, 2026, the operation expanded to Ecuador when U.S. forces bombed [Comandos de la Frontera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comandos_de_la_Frontera) as part of the [Ecuadorian conflict (2024–present)](75768010.md).[^cite_note-\:0-56][^cite_note-\:1-57][^cite_note-\:2-22]

The campaign was formally named on 13 November 2025 by [Pete Hegseth](34076202.md), US [secretary of defense](43998.md), as an expansion of an operation bearing the same name announced by the [United States Navy](20518076.md) on 28 January 2025. It uses a hybrid fleet of vessels with [robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotics) and autonomous systems to detect and combat alleged [drug trafficking](60850852.md) networks in the [Western Hemisphere](73162.md).

According to an assessment by the [Drug Enforcement Administration](146720.md) (DEA), the military strikes against suspected drug-trafficking vessels did not reduce the volume of [cocaine entering the United States](37389158.md). Instead, the operations forced criminal organizations to change their smuggling techniques and at the same time eroded traditional law enforcement investigative techniques.[^cite_note-wp26-58]

## Origins and history

See also: [Proposed United States invasion of Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proposed_United_States_invasion_of_Venezuela)

The [United States Navy](20518076.md) announced an operation by the same name in January 2025, using the [Navy's Fourth Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Fourth_Fleet), focused on integrating "robotic and autonomous systems with traditional naval forces".[^cite_note-MHSSouthernSpear-59][^cite_note-NavyPR28Jan-60] According to the *[Miami Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Herald)*, Foster Edwards, the 4th Fleet's Hybrid Fleet Director, "described Southern Spear as a significant step in the Navy's evolving Hybrid Fleet Campaign" that would "operationalize a heterogeneous mix of Robotic and Autonomous Systems to support the detection and monitoring of illicit trafficking while learning lessons for other theaters" and "help develop critical techniques and procedures for integrating RAS into the maritime environment."[^cite_note-MHSSouthernSpear-59] The Operation would use unmanned surface vessels supplied by [Saildrone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saildrone_\(company\)) "to monitor illegal activity in the Caribbean and Pacific Ocean", according to Axios.[^cite_note-61][^cite_note-62] Uncrewed boats for high-risk missions were being used to gather intelligence by June 2025.[^cite_note-63]

The [United States Southern Command](1590932.md) (USSOUTHCOM), whose [area of responsibility](9452512.md) covers Central and South America and the Caribbean,[^cite_note-TheHillWhatToKnow-64] created a [joint task force](12632543.md) in October 2025 around the headquarters of [II Marine Expeditionary Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/II_Marine_Expeditionary_Force) (II MEF) tasked with "crushing" the [drug cartels](197015.md) operating in the region.[^cite_note-65][^cite_note-66] Later referred to as the Joint Task Force Southern Spear, its operations are coordinated with the 4th Fleet, the US Navy's component of USSOUTHCOM.[^cite_note-MHSSouthernSpear-59][^cite_note-TheHillFordArrives-67]

According to *[The Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hill_\(magazine\))*, after the Navy's 4th Fleet Operation Southern Spear envisioned "deploying robotic surface vessels, small robotic interceptor boats and vertical take-off and landing robotic air vessels to the Southcom region", in November 2025, Hegseth formally unveiled the Joint Task Force Operation Southern Spear, "made up of ground, air and logistics troops, to 'synchronize and augment counter-narcotics efforts across the Western Hemisphere'."[^cite_note-TheHillWhatToKnow-64] The campaign was formally named on 13 November 2025 by Hegseth on orders from Trump,[^cite_note-MHSSouthernSpear-59][^cite_note-68] bearing the same name as the operation announced by the Navy on 28 January 2025.[^cite_note-MHSSouthernSpear-59][^cite_note-NavyPR28Jan-60] The Joint Task Force headquarters are at [Naval Station Mayport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Station_Mayport) in Florida, with operations to begin later in November.[^cite_note-MHSSouthernSpear-59]

*[The New York Times](30680.md)* described the [Chairman of the US Joint Chiefs of Staff](364458.md), [Dan Caine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Caine), as a "major architect" of the operation.[^cite_note-NYT23Nov-24]

On 6 August 2026, [U.S. Southern Command](1590932.md) announced the creation [Joint Task Force Western Hemisphere](84044719.md) (JTF-WHEM) which will replace Operation Southern Spear.[^cite_note-69] It will be a unified command of the 18 partner nations of the [Americas Counter Cartel Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americas_Counter-Cartel_Coalition) (A3C). Marine Corps Maj. Gen. Kevin Jarrard will command the task force.&#x20;

## Terrorist and other designations

Three days after Hegseth unveiled Operation Southern Spear,[^cite_note-TheHillWhatToKnow-64][^cite_note-TheHillFordArrives-67] [Marco Rubio](5502549.md), the [US Secretary of State](32293.md), announced the US would designate [Cartel of the Suns](45272133.md)—which the US alleges is headed by Venezuelan president [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md)—as a [Foreign Terrorist Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Terrorist_Organization).[^cite_note-CBS16Nov-70][^cite_note-71] Rubio stated that cartels are "responsible for terrorist violence throughout our hemisphere as well as for trafficking drugs into the United States and Europe"; Maduro denies the US allegation that he is "complicit with armed criminal gangs that smuggle drugs" to the US.[^cite_note-CBS16Nov-70] The *Miami Herald* wrote: "The designation would effectively label Maduro and senior officials, including Interior Minister [Diosdado Cabello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diosdado_Cabello) and Defense Minister [Vladimir Padrino López](45272341.md), as terrorists."[^cite_note-72]

The US listed the [Gulf Clan](34249730.md) (*Clan del Golfo*), described by *The Guardian* as "Colombia's largest and most powerful illegal armed criminal group" as a [Foreign Terrorist Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Terrorist_Organization) on 16 December 2025.[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74]

On 15 December 2025, Trump signed an [executive order](210541.md) designating fentanyl as a [weapon of mass destruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapon_of_mass_destruction).[^cite_note-75] Two days later, Trump stated he would designate the "Maduro regime" a [Foreign Terrorist Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Terrorist_Organization).[^cite_note-76]

## Campaign

Main articles: [US military buildup in the Caribbean](80987786.md), [strikes on alleged drug traffickers](80956668.md), and [sanctioned oil blockade and oil tanker seizures](81814844.md)

Further information: [Operation Absolute Resolve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Absolute_Resolve)

### Strikes on alleged drug traffickers

The US began [deploying troops to the Caribbean](80987786.md) in mid-August.[^cite_note-77] By late November 2025, the US had amassed the largest military presence in the region since the [Cuban Missile Crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_Missile_Crisis) in October 1962,[^cite_note-Altman-78] and was [striking and sinking vessels](80956668.md) allegedly operated by drug traffickers in the Caribbean and Eastern Pacific.[^cite_note-79] A US official told a reporter that Hegseth's 13 November unveiling was "a formal operation naming for what the Joint Task Force Southern Spear ... and Southcom have already been doing in theater".[^cite_note-80]

Within days of the unveiling of the operation, cocaine-smuggling boats were captured in joint operations between the Dominican Republic and the US.[^cite_note-CNN19Nov-4][^cite_note-81]

As of September 2026, at least 234 people had been killed in the operation.[^cite_note-82] Experts, human rights groups and international organizations including the United Nations have said the killings violate US and [international law](8195726.md).[^cite_note-83][^cite_note-84] In September 2026, UN Special Rapporteur on human rights and counterterrorism [Ben Saul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Saul) concluded that there were "reasonable grounds to believe that the reported 68 attacks that killed 223 people constituted crimes against humanity of murder under customary international law." The report rejected the U.S. claim of self-defence, finding that drug trafficking does not meet the threshold of an "armed attack" and that less-lethal alternatives such as maritime interception were available.[^cite_note-85][^cite_note-86]

### Venezuela intervention

Several US [B-52 Stratofortress](18933037.md) long-range bombers flew from [Minot Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minot_Air_Force_Base) in [North Dakota](21651.md)[^cite_note-87] within several miles of the coast of Venezuela on 20 November in what SOUTHCOM described as a "bomber [attack demo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Show_of_force)".[^cite_note-Altman-78]

On 22 November, senior Trump administration officials told Reuters that the US was prepared to launch a new phase of operations against Venezuela.[^cite_note-88][^cite_note-Altman-78] Dan Caine visited Puerto Rico as the "Trump Administration weigh\[ed] the possibility of a broader military campaign against Venezuela".[^cite_note-NYT23Nov-24][^cite_note-Altman-78] According to *The New York Times*, he was "expected to consult with commanders"; sources said that Trump had approved covert CIA activity in Venezuela, and authorized additional negotiations with Maduro, but had not yet authorized the use of land forces.[^cite_note-NYT23Nov-24]

The US [began seizing, intercepting or pursuing tankers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_naval_quarantine_during_Operation_Southern_Spear) transporting Venezuelan oil on 11 December,[^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90][^cite_note-91] and on 17 December, Trump ordered a "blockade" of sanctioned oil tankers heading to and from Venezuela.[^cite_note-92]

The first strike on a [land target in Venezuela](80956668.md#Land_targets_in_Venezuela) was announced by Trump on 26 December 2025[^cite_note-NYTPort29Dec-93][^cite_note-94]—a marine facility the US said had been used for loading drug boats had been hit in a drone strike by the [CIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA), with no casualties, likely on 24 December.[^cite_note-NYTPort29Dec-93]

On 3 January 2026, explosions and low-flying aircraft were reported in [Caracas](55289.md) and other locations and [Maduro was captured in Operation Absolute Resolve](82021123.md) by the US.[^cite_note-95]

After the operation, [Delcy Rodríguez](44199228.md) was sworn in as President of Venezuela and has effectively functioned as a puppet state of the US with the US exercising de facto financial and foreign policy control over Venezuela[^cite_note-96]

### Ecuador intervention

On March 6, 2026, the Operation was expanded militarily in Ecuador when the United States participated in bombing [Comandos de la Frontera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comandos_de_la_Frontera), during the [Ecuadorian conflict (2024–present)](75768010.md).[^cite_note-\:0-56][^cite_note-\:1-57][^cite_note-\:2-22]

### Possible expansion into other Latin American nations

In August of 2026, while visiting Panama, Hegseth proclaimed that the United States has made a military agreement with Colombia, [Guatemala](17238567.md) and [Honduras](13394.md) to combat criminal drug organizations. Hegseth added that like the attacks on boats that the U.S. had been conducting in the East Pacific and the Caribbean, the attacks on drug traffickers on land would be the same. In addition, Hegseth stated that the U.S. would continue to pursue military cooperation with Ecuador. However, Guatemala later publicly repudiated Hegseth's claims.[^cite_note-97]

#### Dominican Republic

Collaborative efforts between the [Dominican Republic](8060.md) and US anti-drug agencies have been conducted, recovering numerous packages of cocaine.[^cite_note-98][^cite_note-99] On 26 November, during a meeting with Hegseth, Dominican president [Luis Abinader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Abinader) granted the US temporary authorization to use [San Isidro Air Base](19402966.md) and the [Las Americas International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Americas_International_Airport) for its counter-narcotics operations.[^cite_note-100][^cite_note-101]

By mid-December, the US had moved more specialized military assets to Puerto Rico and the Dominican Republic that would support targeted land strikes, seizures of oil tankers or enforcement of an oil embargo.[^cite_note-102] TheWarZone.com described the addition of refueling and search and rescue aircraft as "moving into a posture ... for tactical air combat operations over hostile territory";[^cite_note-103] the deployment of [EA–18G Growlers](833647.md) as "indicative of what one would see in the lead-up to a kinetic operation centered heavily on strikes on targets in inland areas" with "electronic warfare support for platforms launching standoff attacks or penetrating through enemy air defenses";[^cite_note-104] and deployment of F-35s as preparing for airstrikes "on targets deep inside Venezuelan airspace".[^cite_note-105]

#### El Salvador

According to a visual investigation report by *The New York Times*, satellite imagery and other data revealed the US began operating aircraft out of El Salvador in mid-October; the three aircraft identified included the Air Force's [AC-130J Ghostrider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_AC-130), which is "designed to destroy targets on the ground or at sea using missiles or barrages from its cannons and machine guns" along with a [P8–A Poseidon](731169.md) operated by the Navy, and a [C-40 Clipper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_C-40_Clipper), about which little is known.[^cite_note-NYTElSalvador-5] The report stated that "the deployment ... is likely to be the first time a foreign country has hosted US planes that may be involved in military strikes in the region".[^cite_note-NYTElSalvador-5] According to *The War Zone*, the P-8 is the "most advanced maritime patrol aircraft in the world and is specifically capable of collecting multiple types of intelligence to find small targets in vast bodies of water".[^cite_note-106]

#### Trinidad and Tobago

The destroyer *Gravely* arrived on 26 October 2025 to spend four days in Trinidad and Tobago, where their country's forces would jointly train with US Marines.[^cite_note-107][^cite_note-108] Trinidad and Tobago announced additional joint training drills with US Marines in November.[^cite_note-109]

Caine would travel to Trinidad and Tobago after the November 2025 Puerto Rico visit to meet with Trinidad and Tobago Prime Minister [Kamla Persad-Bissessar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamla_Persad-Bissessar); the *Caribbean National Weekly* stated that the visit came "as the two nations deepen their security collaboration to strengthen regional stability and combat illicit trafficking and transnational criminal organizations".[^cite_note-110]

In late November, the US installed a radar system in the island nation, which is useful for aerial surveillance.[^cite_note-111] In December, Trinidad and Tobago approved military aircraft from the US to use the country's airports for operations that are "logistical in nature, facilitating supply replenishment and routing personnel rotations", according to its foreign ministry.[^cite_note-112][^cite_note-113]

#### United Kingdom

Further information: [United States oil blockade during Operation Southern Spear § Marinera (Bella 1)](81814844.md#Marinera_\(Bella_1\))

The [Royal Air Force](25679.md) (RAF) provided surveillance and reconnaissance support, with an RAF surveillance plane tracked over the area where a Russian-linked tanker, *[Bella 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_1)*, was seized by US forces in January 2026.[^cite_note-ReutersUKsupport-6][^cite_note-114]

The UK provided one of its largest naval supply ships, the [RFA *Tideforce*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RFA_Tideforce) to support the US seizure of *Bella 1* (renamed *Marinera*) in the North Atlantic,[^cite_note-ReutersUKsupport-6][^cite_note-115] and UK military bases in the Atlantic were used by US planes and military assets involved in the operation.[^cite_note-116]

### Allied with Venezuela

*The Washington Post* wrote in October that Maduro had written to Russia's [Vladimir Putin](32817.md), asking for help with "defensive radars, military aircraft repairs and potentially missiles", and that Maduro had contacted "China and Iran, soliciting military assistance and equipment to strengthen the country's defenses".[^cite_note-WAPO31Oct-117] According to *The Wall Street Journal*, China and Russia are "Maduro's two most powerful allies" and they have "previously provided military equipment, maintenance and training, say analysts, along with economic assistance",[^cite_note-WSJ29Nov2025-118] but along with Maduro's other traditional allies of Cuba and Iran, they are unlikely to lend significant support help beyond words of support.[^cite_note-NYT18Nov2025-19][^cite_note-WSJ29Nov2025-118]

#### Cuba

Cuba has contributed security and counterintelligence to Venezuela's Maduro since before the US military buildup; that support has expanded during the buildup to include more bodyguards, security and counterintelligence officers, who are considered less likely to rise against Maduro.[^cite_note-NYT18Nov2025-19][^cite_note-119] The government of Cuba said 32 citizens were killed engaging in "direct combat" in Venezuela during the 3 January [capture of Maduro](82021123.md).[^cite_note-AJ04-50]

#### China

China has historically supported the Maduro government with financial and military assistance, and most of Venezuela's oil exports go to China. Analysts state that its relationship with China is significant for Venezuela, but China had "stepped back" in new lending, was frustrated by corruption in Venezuela, and unlikely to lend considerable military support in the face of its own problems with the US.[^cite_note-NYT18Nov2025-19][^cite_note-WSJ29Nov2025-118][^cite_note-120]

#### Iran

Iran has provided technical assistance to Venezuela's oil industry during the [crisis in Venezuela](52966258.md), but is considered weakened by internal issues with limited ability to support abroad, according to *The New York Times*.[^cite_note-NYT18Nov2025-19]

#### Russia

On 26 October, a Russian [Il-76](229943.md) transport aircraft [under US sanctions](70323386.md) landed in Venezuela "after a circuitous route over Africa to avoid Western airspace, according to Flightradar24".[^cite_note-WAPO31Oct-117][^cite_note-NYT18Nov2025-19] The aircraft carried [Pantsir-S1](8645085.md) and [Buk-M2E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buk_missile_system) air defense systems, according to a Russian official.[^cite_note-WSJ29Nov2025-118][^cite_note-TWZ4Nov-121]

### Others

In December 2025, Maduro asked Colombian society, including Colombia's army, to join in defending against any aggression from the US. [Gustavo Petro](13391962.md), the president of Colombia, denied the request, stating that no one can order Colombia's armed forces, and later adding that Maduro was a dictator but not a narco-trafficker.[^cite_note-122][^cite_note-Newsweek19Nov-123] Petro has called for a negotiated settlement.[^cite_note-Newsweek19Nov-123]

## Analysis

Some analysts have identified this operation as a hybridization of the [war on terror](13425800.md) and the [war on drugs](1181646.md).[^cite_note-124][^cite_note-125][^cite_note-126]

According to [Pino Arlacchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pino_Arlacchi), the former head of the [UN Office on Drugs and Crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Office_on_Drugs_and_Crime), the portrayal of Venezuela as a "drug state" is a "geopolitically motivated smear campaign" by the US government; he refers to the [World Drug Report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Drug_Report) 2025[^cite_note-127] and his own experience, according to which the Venezuelan government's cooperation in the fight against drug trafficking is among the best in South America. According to Arlacchi, the US has taken an interest in [Venezuelan oil reserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_oil_reserves), which are among the largest in the world.[^cite_note-128]

An article published in December 2025 by the [Council on Foreign Relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_on_Foreign_Relations) stated that some experts believe that the "scope and intensity" of the Operation indicate that the Trump administrations's goals extend beyond combatting drug trafficking to a "broader plan to oust Maduro".[^cite_note-129]

An analysis by the [Costs of War Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costs_of_War_Project) estimated that Operation Southern Spear and [Operation Absolute Resolve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Absolute_Resolve), from August 1, 2025 to March 31, 2026, cost at least \$4.7 billion in government funds.[^cite_note-130]

## Reactions

*[Foreign Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Policy_\(magazine\))* magazine stated that a poll of Latin Americans living across the Americas, including the US and Canada, in October 2025 showed "fairly strong levels of support among people in the region for a US military intervention in Venezuela to depose Maduro and his government", adding that people in Latin America were more "likely to support such a scenario" than US respondents.[^cite_note-131][^cite_note-132]
