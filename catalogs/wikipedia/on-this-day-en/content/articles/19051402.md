**Benighat** was a formal [village development committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_development_committee_\(Nepal\)) in [Dhading District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhading_District) in the [Bagmati Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagmati_Zone) of central [Nepal](171166.md). At the time of the [1991 Nepal census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Nepal_census) it had a population of 6264 and had 1196 houses in it.[^cite_note-1] It was later merged with several other [VDCs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_development_committee_\(Nepal\)) to form the [Benighat Rorang Rural Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benighat_Rorang_Rural_Municipality).[^cite_note-2]
