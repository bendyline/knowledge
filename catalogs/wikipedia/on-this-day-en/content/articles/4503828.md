The **Parliament of Saint Lucia** is the [bicameral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameral) [legislative branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_branch) of the [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Saint_Lucia) of [Saint Lucia](27208.md). It consists of the [King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Saint_Lucia), who is represented by the [governor general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-General_of_Saint_Lucia), and the 2 parts of the [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature), the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Saint_Lucia) and the [House of Assembly](4388564.md).

The House of Assembly, the [lower chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_chamber), has 17 [elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Saint_Lucia) members who each represent a [constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Saint_Lucia#Political_divisions) with terms lasting no more than 5 years. The Senate, the [upper chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_chamber), has 11 members who are appointed by the governor general based on their own judgement, as well based on the advice of the [prime minister](466569.md) and the [leader of the opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(Saint_Lucia\)).

## Composition

Parliament consists of three different elements:

- The [monarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Saint_Lucia), represented by a [viceroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viceroy), the [Governor General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-General_of_Saint_Lucia)

- The [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Saint_Lucia), the [upper chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_house)

- The [House of Assembly](4388564.md), the [lower chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house)

### Monarch

Main article: [Monarchy of Saint Lucia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Saint_Lucia)

Although the sovereign constitutes part of parliament, neither the sovereign nor his viceroy (the governor general), participates in the legislative process. However, [Royal Assent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Assent) is still necessary for a bill to become law.

### Senate

Main article: [Senate of Saint Lucia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Saint_Lucia)

The Senate is the upper house of parliament, consisting of 11 members appointed by the governor general. Six members are appointed based on the advice of the prime minister, whilst three members are appointed based on the advice of the leader of the opposition. The other two members are appointed by the governor general. To be appointed as a senator, one must be at least 21 years old.

### House of Assembly

Main article: [House of Assembly of Saint Lucia](4388564.md)

The House of Assembly is the lower house of parliament, with each member being elected via a plurality of voters in each of the country's 17 [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Saint_Lucia#Political_divisions). Members of the House must be at least 21 years old. A parliamentary term lasts no more than 5 years, but Parliament may be [dissolved](652293.md) at any time.
