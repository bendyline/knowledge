An election for the position of [president](28911295.md) of [Real Madrid CF](26413.md) was held on 7 June 2026, for a term until 2030.[^cite_note-auto1-3] On 12 May 2026, after the Board of Directors Meeting, the current president [Florentino Pérez](2919782.md) called for a new board of directors elections, following a [trophy-less season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Real_Madrid_CF_season) for the club.[^cite_note-4] The disappointment was deepened by back-to-back seasons without a single La Liga, Copa del Rey, or Champions League title, a drought unprecedented since the period from 2008 to 2010.

These are expected to be the first contested elections since 2006, and the third in the 21st century.[^cite_note-auto-5] On 21 May 2026, Spanish businessman [Enrique Riquelme](83306175.md) announced his candidacy.[^cite_note-auto-5] Pérez launched his re-election campaign officially on 27 May.[^cite_note-6] During his campaign announcement, Pérez asserted that the opposition campaign was led by individuals from the administration of former president [Ramón Calderón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ram%C3%B3n_Calder%C3%B3n) (2006–2009), referring to that era as the club's "darkest period."[^cite_note-7]

On 24 May, the candidacy of Riquelme was accepted.[^cite_note-8] Riquelme earlier ran in the 2021 elections, yet chose to withdraw promptly owing to narrow time limits and rigorous institutional mandates.[^cite_note-9]

Riquelme has clarified that the club is suffering from an economic crisis and there is an intention to sell club shares, thereby opening the door to privatization,[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] which he considers dangerous to the club’s identity, and that his election will prevent this; furthermore, the team's failure to win titles is due to Pérez focusing excessively on the [European Super League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Super_League), and had he been president, Barcelona would not have dared to carry out the [Negreira "scandal"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negreira_case). Riquelme also added that the club spends heavily on non-sporting activities, three times what any other club in Europe spends, questioning among other things, "Why do we spend 80 million dollars on financial consulting and third-party expenses?"[^cite_note-12] Riquelme stated his determination to revive the team’s Spanish identity through a strategic mission to boost Real Madrid's representation in the [national team](609825.md), re-establishing the club as a key feeder for *La Roja*.[^cite_note-13]

On 3 June, Riquelme revealed that [Raúl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C3%BAl_Gonz%C3%A1lez) would assume the position of Sporting Director, and [Fernando Hierro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Hierro) would become Director of the Real Madrid academy, [La Fábrica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_F%C3%A1brica_\(Real_Madrid\)). On 5 June, Riquelme pledged to bring the German coach [Jürgen Klopp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%BCrgen_Klopp), who he believes suits Real's status and ambitions.[^cite_note-14]

On 4 June, Florentino Perez pledged that if elected, [José Mourinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mourinho) will be Real Madrid coach,[^cite_note-15] as well as announcing that [Ibrahima Konaté](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahima_Konat%C3%A9) and [Denzel Dumfries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Dumfries) would be his first signings.[^cite_note-16] Outside of that, he also promised that on Tuesday, 9 June, he would bid a club-record €150 million for a mystery player.[^cite_note-17]

Incumbent president Florentino Pérez won the election with 21,741 votes, representing 65% of the turnout, while also winning all voting tables.[^cite_note-auto2-18] Enrique conceded the loss, congratulated Pérez, and affirmed that Real shall not remain anymore 20 years without elections.[^cite_note-19] He requested that the members be responsible for taking decisions and all matters connected to the club.[^cite_note-20]

Voting was conducted from 9\:00 AM to 8\:00 PM within the Real Madrid City Basketball Pavilion.[^cite_note-auto1-3]
