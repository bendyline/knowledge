[Para alpine skiing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing) was one of the competitions at the [2026 Winter Paralympics](41045653.md) in Milan and Cortina d'Ampezzo, Italy.[^cite_note-qualification_criteria_paralympic_org-1][^cite_note-cortina_venues_2026-2] In total, 30 medal events were held.[^cite_note-qualification_criteria_paralympic_org-1]

## Events

The competition events are:

- [Downhill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downhill_\(ski_competition\)) (sitting, standing, visually impaired): [women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_downhill) – [men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_downhill)

- [Super-G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super-G) (sitting, standing, visually impaired): [women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_super-G) – [men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_super-G)

- [Giant slalom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_slalom) (sitting, standing, visually impaired): [women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_giant_slalom) – [men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_giant_slalom)

- [Slalom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slalom_skiing) (sitting, standing, visually impaired): [women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_slalom) – [men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_slalom)

- [Super combined](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_combined) (sitting, standing, visually impaired): [women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_super_combined) – [men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_super_combined)
