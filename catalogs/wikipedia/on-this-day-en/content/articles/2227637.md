**Kaduna** [Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/4/42/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-kaduna.wav/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-kaduna.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> ([Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language): *Jihar Kaduna*, جىِهَر كَدُنا; مدينة كدونا; [Fula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fula_language): *Leydi Kaduna*, 𞤤𞤫𞤴𞤣𞤭 𞤳𞤢𞤣𞤵𞤲𞤢; [Tyap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyap_language): *Si̱tet Ka̱duna*) is a [state](226734.md) in the northwest geopolitical zone of [Nigeria](21383.md). The state capital is its namesake, the city of [Kaduna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_\(city\)), which was the [8th largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_cities_by_population) in the country as of 2006. Created in 1967 as **North-Central State**, which also encompassed the modern [Katsina State](2227638.md), Kaduna State achieved its current borders in 1987. Kaduna State is the [fourth largest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_area) and [third most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population) state in the country, Kaduna State is nicknamed the *Centre of Learning*, owing to the presence of numerous educational [institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_education) of importance within the state such as [Ahmadu Bello University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadu_Bello_University),[^cite_note-\:1-6] Nigerian Defence Academy, Air Force Institute of Technology (AFIT), Kaduna Polytechnic, etc.

Modern Kaduna State is home to the sites of some of Africa's oldest civilizations, including the [Nok civilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nok_culture) that prospered from c. [1500 BC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1500s_BC_\(decade\)) to c. [500 AD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_500).[^cite_note-PB_2014-7][^cite_note-FB_1969-8] In the 9th century, [geographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographer) and historian [Ya'qubi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ya'qubi) documented the existence of the [Hausa Kingdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_Kingdoms), which existed until the region was incorporated into the [Sokoto Caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_Caliphate) in the early 1800s. During the [colonial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonial_Nigeria) era, the city of Kaduna was made the capital of [Northern Nigeria Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nigeria_Protectorate) by British leadership.

The state economy is dependent on [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture), especially [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton) and [groundnut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut) production.[^cite_note-\:1-6][^cite_note-\:3-9][^cite_note-\:4-10] In the modern era, Kaduna State has been the site of violent [ethnic](21386.md) and [religious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion_in_Nigeria) conflict,[^cite_note-11] with the 2002 [Miss World riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_World_riots) in the state capital over purported [blasphemy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blasphemy) leading to around 250 deaths and the loss of homes for around 30,000.[^cite_note-HRWimpunity-12]

## Etymology

The most widespread etymology for the word *Kaduna* is that it is a corruption of the [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language) plural for crocodile, *kadduna*, as there used to be many crocodiles in the [Kaduna River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_River).[^cite_note-\:1-6]

Another version of the etymology of the name is a narrative linked to the [Gbagyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gbagyi_language) word/name 'Odna' for the Kaduna River.[^cite_note-13]

## History

[Zazzau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Zazzau), a [traditional state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_traditional_states) which lies within the province's capital, is said to have been founded in 1536. It would later be renamed to [Zaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria) after the younger sister of [Queen Amina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amina_\(Queen_of_Zazzau\)).[^cite_note-14] The [Hausa people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people) of [Zaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria) & the [Ham people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ham_people) of [Jaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaba,_Nigeria), are said to be the old ancestral of the region's north & south respectively.[^cite_note-\:0-15][^cite_note-16]

It is indicative that the name, Kaduna, was taken up by Lord [Frederick Lugard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Lugard,_1st_Baron_Lugard) and his colonial colleagues when they moved the capital of the then Northern Region from [Zungeru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zungeru) to Kaduna City in 1916. This move of the colonial office to Kaduna city started in 1912–1918/20, with the initial effort having been made in 1902 from Jebba to Zungeru.[^cite_note-17]

At the start of British colonial rule in northern Nigeria, the people groups who lived in the area became 'Northern Nigerians'- a construct which continues even today. By 1967 these people groups were again carved into 'North Central State'; this was the case until 1975 when 'Kaduna State' was formerly created by the then military leader, Gen. [Murtala Mohammed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murtala_Mohammed), with all distinct identities amalgamated into one state without a referendum. The state hence is the successor of the old [Northern Region](2292852.md) of Nigeria, which had its capital at Kaduna which is now the state capital of about 6.3 million people (Nigerian census figure, 2006).

In 1967, the old [Northern Region](2292852.md) was divided into six states in the north, leaving Kaduna as the capital of North-Central State, whose name was changed to Kaduna State in 1976. Meanwhile, Kaduna State was further divided in 1987, creating [Katsina State](2227638.md). Under the governance of Kaduna are the ancient cities of [Zaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria), [Kafanchan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kafanchan), and [Nok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nok). The most intriguing aspect of this area is that the colonial construction and its post-colonial successor called 'Nigeria' hardly documented the history or the method of how Kaduna State's people groups encompassed in these constructs define and identify themselves. As such, the people groups who populate the area have lived in near oblivion or obscurity as they are often thought of as [Hausa people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people).[^cite_note-18] In 2019 Kaduna State celebrated its 100th anniversary, making it one of the oldest states in Nigeria.[^cite_note-19]

In 2021, Kaduna State was the site of several major attacks done by bandits involved in the [Nigerian bandit conflict](68366617.md). On 24 February, at least 34 were killed in [attacks in Kaduna and neighboring Katsina state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_2021_Kaduna_and_Katsina_attacks).[^cite_note-20] On 11 March, [39 students were kidnapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afaka_kidnapping) when gunmen attacked the Federal College of Forestry Mechanization.[^cite_note-21] On 20 April, in another raid by bandits on [Greenfield University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenfield_University) students and staff, [22 were kidnapped and 6 of them were killed.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenfield_University_kidnapping)[^cite_note-22] On 5 July, a further [140 students were kidnapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikun_kidnapping) from Bethel Baptist High School.[^cite_note-23] Attacks have continued into 2022,[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] 2023,[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] and 2024.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

## Geography

Kaduna River

The state is located in the Northern part of Nigeria's high plains. The [vegetation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetation) cover is Sudan Savannah type, characterized by scattered short trees, shrubs and grasses. The soil is mostly loamy to sandy. A substantial amount of clay is found also.

Its northern half became [Katsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_\(city\)) state in 1987. The state is bordered by seven states: Zamfara for 117 km (73 miles) and Katsina for 161 km (100 miles) to the north, Kano to the north-east for 255 km, Bauchi and Plateau to the east, Nasarawa and Abuja Federal Capital Territory (for 45 km) to the south, and Niger to the west.[^cite_note-30] Kaduna state is located between latitude 10°38'58" N and 10°25'36" N and to longitude 7°22'14" E and 7°32'00" E.[^cite_note-31]

The state was ranked number four by the total area of land and number three by population.[^cite_note-32]

The [Kaduna River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_River), a tributary of the [Niger River](21437.md), flows through the state. There are rocky stones in [Zaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria) and Kogoro Hill. Many communities are prone to seasonal flooding during the rainy season.[^cite_note-33]

### Climate

The rainy season in Kaduna is hot, humid, and cloudy, while the dry season is hot and partly cloudy. Throughout the year, the temperature rarely falls below 50 °F or rises above 102 °F, usually ranging between 55 °F and 95 °F.[^cite_note-34]

## Government

The current governor of Kaduna State is legally under the control of [Kaduna State Executives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_State_Governor), [Kaduna State House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_State_House_of_Assembly) and [Kaduna State Judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_State_Judiciary). The current elected governor of the state is [Senator Uba Sani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uba_Sani) and his deputy is [Hadiza Sabuwa Balarabe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadiza_Sabuwa_Balarabe).[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] In the state there 14 ministries that operate with the state government to improve the state: [Ministries of Kaduna State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministries_of_Kaduna_State). Within each Ministry, there are multiple agencies with regulatory authority, such as the [Kaduna State Environmental Protection Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_State_Environmental_Protection_Authority) which oversees waste, water, and other environmental quality issues.

## Economics



Sun-dried tomato and pepper farm, Hunkuyi, Kaduna State

The Kaduna State economy was ranked 15th largest state in the Nigerian economy from 2002 to 2008, and it made up 3.3% of Nigerian GDP. While agriculture contributed 30% of SGDP in Kaduna.[^cite_note-38] Kaduna state cultivate cotton and [peanuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut) (groundnuts) for exporting and domestic extraction of [Peanut oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut_oil). ## Services

### Water

**Kaduna State Rural Water Supply and Sanitation Agency** was created by the state government to supply clean water to the rural parts of Kaduna State.[^cite_note-auto-41] It was created to address the need for clean water in rural areas of Kaduna State.[^cite_note-auto-41] Having being in existence for over 15 years, RUWASSA has always maintained its commitment of providing a clean water supply to the 23 local governments of the state.[^cite_note-auto-41] RUWASSA also encourages and welcomes partnerships from various local communities, traditional leaders, public and private sectors, NGOs, etc., to ensure that each project meets the specific needs of the host community. With an about 85% project success rate, RUWASSA has continued to demonstrate excellence in its projects, thereby delivering jobs with accountability and transparency.[^cite_note-auto-41] Also, it has about 1,200 water points with 24/7support availability.[^cite_note-auto-41]

## Religion

The main [religions in Kaduna State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion_in_Kaduna_State) are [Islam](6037917.md) and [Christianity](5211.md) while some minority ethnic groups practice traditional worshipping, mostly in the southern area of the state. The people of Kaduna are very religious, causing two religious crises in 2001 and 2002, the [Miss World riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_World_riots).

## Education

Kaduna is one of the largest centres of education in Nigeria. The slogan of the state is *Center of Learning* because of the presence of many institution like [Ahmadu Bello University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadu_Bello_University) (established 1962). There are many government schools, include primary schools and secondary schools. All secondary schools in Kaduna are owned by the state government, federal government or private organisations. there are many tertiary institutions in the state. The state also has colleges for transportation and agriculture.[^cite_note-\:1-6]

## Architecture



The entrance gate of [Zazzau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria)

Architecture includes the National Museum which was built in 1975 with archaeological and ethnographic exhibitions, the [Kajuru Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kajuru_Castle), Lugard Hall, [Zaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria) walls and gates and [Nok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nok) settlements. In the state there are many architectural buildings like [Ahmadu Bello Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadu_Bello_Stadium), [Murtala Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murtala_Square), Investment house, [Kaduna central market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abubakar_Mahmud_Gumi_Market), and [Sultan Bello Mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Bello_Mosque). The palace of empire of [Zazzau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Zazzau) is one of the oldest traditional buildings in Kaduna state.[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52]

### Traditional architecture



[Kajuru castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kajuru_castle)

In Kaduna State there are a lot of ethnic groups, which lead to the variation of culture and [architectural style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architectural_style), this include the city wall of Zaria. The walls constructed during the reigns of [Queen Amina of Zazzau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amina_\(Queen_of_Zazzau\)) protected the city and they are between 14 and 16 km long, and are closed by eight gates, Also the Emir's Palace of Zaria is an important traditional heritage. The palace has luxurious interiors. The St. Bartholomew's Church Zaria, built by the Church Missionary Society in 1929, still stands in Zaria, the church was built based on Hausa traditional architecture.

### Modern architecture



Throneroom's Salama radio 14-storey tower, [Kafanchan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kafanchan)

Modern architecture is present in the state as a result of civilization and development. Most of these architectural buildings were built by the federal or state government, while the residential ones are mostly built by individuals; these buildings include [Ahmadu Bello Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadu_Bello_Stadium), Ten storey building, Investment house, federal secretary, [Ranchers Bees Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranchers_Bees_Stadium), and [Murtala square.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murtala_Square)

## Health

Kaduna State has over 1,000 primary healthcare facilities to cater to every resident, even in the most remote village or ward of the state. To further improve on healthcare delivery, in 2016, the Kaduna State Government partnered with the [UK Department for International Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_for_International_Development) (DFID) to install over 1.3 megawatts of solar power in primary healthcare facilities across the state.[^cite_note-53]

## Entertainment and tourism

In Kaduna State an annual festival is organized by Ministry of Culture and Tourism (Kaduna State). The festival exposes folklore talent and through these process, to promote unity and encourage tourism and build culture in the State. It takes place every November or December.[^cite_note-\:2-59] Cultural activities include [Eid al-Fitr](154944.md) and [Eid al-Adha](358226.md) performed by the Muslims in the state; the Tuk Ham; the [Afan National Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afan_Festival); the Christmas and Easter celebrations, by Christians, and the Kallan-Kowa celebrations. Kaduna has a museum and a park, the Kofar Gamji park and Zoo. Lord Lugard's Residence, is also a tourist attraction and it currently houses the state assembly.[^cite_note-\:2-59]

### Festivals

- [Eid al-Fitr](154944.md) and [Eid al-Adha](358226.md): This celebration is scheduled on the 1st of [Shawwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawwal) and the 10th of [Dhu al-Hijjah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhu_al-Hijjah) respectively according to the [Islamic Calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Calendar) for three days usually in Kaduna, Usually, Muslims all over the world celebrate the end of the Ramadan fasting period and the conclusion of the hajj (pilgrimage) rites. Most of the emirates in Kaduna State and other parts of the northern Emirates celebrate it with a colourful [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people) traditional dressing, horse decoration and Durbar.

  [^cite_note-\:2-59]

- [Christmas celebrations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas_celebrations): This takes place in every 25/26 December in Kaduna State to celebrate the birth of Jesus Christ by Christians all over the state.

  [^cite_note-\:2-59]

- [Easter](9325.md) celebrations: It takes place every March/April to remember the death and resurrection of [Jesus Christ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_Christ) by all Christians in Kaduna State.

  [^cite_note-\:2-59]

- [Afan National Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afan_Festival): This is a celebration on every 1 January, in [Kagoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kagoro). The festival has assumed an international standard with the sons and daughters of Agworok land coming together to discuss issues that required their attention and to show their extreme cultural heritage.

  [^cite_note-\:2-59]

- Kalankuwa Cultural festival is a purely cultural festival that is celebrated in northern part of the state. It is a celebration to give thanks for good farm crops and to celebrate the season. It is celebrated in November/December. Young men and women come together in a peaceful manner to entertain themselves. It is celebrated in Bomo Village, Samara, in [Sabon Gari](11800566.md) local government area.

## Politics

The state government is led by a democratical elected governor who works closely with members of the state's house of assembly. The capital city of the state is [Kaduna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_\(city\))[^cite_note-64]

### Electoral system

The governor of each state is selected using a modified two-round system. To be elected in the first round, a candidate must receive the plurality of the vote and over 25% of the vote in at least two -third of the State local government Areas. If no candidate passes threshold, a second round will be held between the top candidate and the next candidate to have received a plurality of votes in the highest number of local government Areas.[^cite_note-65]
