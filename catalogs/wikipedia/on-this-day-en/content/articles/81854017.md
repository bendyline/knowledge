A global [computer memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_memory) supply shortage started in 2025 due to supply constraints and rapid price escalation in the [semiconductor memory](1313007.md) market, particularly affecting [DRAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_random-access_memory) and [NAND flash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAND_flash_memory) integrated circuits. This shortage has been referred to in media outlets as "**RAMmageddon**"[^cite_note-2] or the "**RAMpocalypse**".[^cite_note-3] Unlike the [2020–2023 global chip shortage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%932023_global_chip_shortage), which stemmed primarily from [pandemic](62750956.md)-related supply chain disruptions from [COVID-19](63030231.md), this shortage is driven by the intentional reallocation of manufacturing capacity toward highly profitable products intended for [AI data center](81802552.md) development,[^cite_note-4] creating a scarcity of [integrated circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_circuit) intended for the consumer and enterprise markets.[^cite_note-TH_RAM-5][^cite_note-Reuters-6] According to a 2026 [Kearney's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kearney_\(consulting_firm\)) PERLab analysis, the shortage is expected to last at least until 2030.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] In June 2026, [Micron Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micron_Technology) CEO [Sanjay Mehrotra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjay_Mehrotra) expected the shortage to last through 2027, and supply to gradually improve by 2028.[^cite_note-9] In August 2026, Jaejune Kim of [Samsung Electronics](46445426.md) expects the shortage to continue through 2028,[^cite_note-10] while Kwak Noh-Jung of [SK Hynix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SK_Hynix) expects it to persist until 2030.[^cite_note-11]

## Background

Following a severe market downturn during 2022–2023, major memory manufacturers—[Samsung Electronics](46445426.md), [SK Hynix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SK_Hynix), and [Micron Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micron_Technology)—implemented strategic production cuts to stabilize pricing.[^cite_note-Micron_Q4-12] By mid-2024, the rapid expansion of [generative AI](73291755.md) services triggered unprecedented demand for specialized memory products, particularly [high bandwidth memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Bandwidth_Memory) (HBM) used in AI accelerators and data center [GPUs](390214.md).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] Specialized components of semiconductor technology are also experiencing supply constraints due to high demand in AI applications. For example, glass cloth, a high-performance [glass fiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_fiber) substrate used for power-efficient high speed data transfer and a crucial component of semiconductor manufacturing, is experiencing a supply crisis. [Nitto Boseki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitto_Boseki), a Japanese firm having overwhelming monopoly in its production, is not able to meet increased demands, making chip-makers such as [Qualcomm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualcomm), [Apple](856.md), [Nvidia](39120.md), and [AMD](2400.md) compete for securing supply.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] There are also reports of smaller electronics companies struggling to find suppliers for components such as [NAND flash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAND_flash). Memory suppliers are adapting to increased demands and market unpredictability by requiring prepayment or shorter time-frame of payment, which makes it more difficult for smaller firms to acquire capital to survive.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] By 2026, due to steadily increased demand on resources, [CPUs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_processing_unit) are also experiencing shortage issues due to low fabrication capacity, prioritization of server CPUs, and increased demand, with CPU prices also being forecast to increase by as much as 15%.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

The demand on memory has also increased strain on other electronic components such as [hard disk devices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_disk_drive), with reports such as [Western Digital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Digital)'s hard disk supply for 2026 being booked for enterprise applications before February 2026.[^cite_note-22]

A 2024 [McKinsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McKinsey) analysis projected that global demand for AI-ready data center capacity would grow at approximately 33% annually through 2030, with AI workloads consuming roughly 70% of total data center capacity by the decade's end.[^cite_note-McKinsey-23] In addition, according to [Kearney's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kearney_\(consulting_firm\)) State of Semiconductor 2025 Report, executives were already expecting a shortage in the sub–8 nm feature size with memory chips being mentioned as an acute source of concern. Multiple companies mentioned being prepared for it through long-term agreements with RAM suppliers or amassing additional inventory.[^cite_note-24]

On March 24, 2026, [Google](1092923.md) announced [TurboQuant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TurboQuant),[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] a memory compression technology focused on [large language models](73248112.md) (LLM) and vector search engines, which it claimed achieves 6 times lower memory consumption in tested local LLMs, and 8-fold performance enhancement in tests running on [H100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H100_GPU) accelerators. The technology is also a drop-in enhancement for existing inference pipeline. Amid speculation about memory demand trends, memory manufacturers, [Sandisk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandisk), [Micron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micron_Technology), [Western Digital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Digital), and [Seagate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seagate_Technology), among other companies involved in memory manufacture experienced stock price declines.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] Prices of memory kits also reduced in the following months, although still at inflated prices.[^cite_note-29]

In August 2026, Intel hinted it was investigating a new memory architecture.[^cite_note-30]

## Development

On July 8, 2026, [Micron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micron_Technology) announced it had broken ground on a new \$9.3 billion fab for memory production.[^cite_note-31] It is expected to start production in Q3 2028.

## Causes

### HBM production displacement

HBM manufacturing requires significantly more wafer capacity per bit than standard DRAM modules. Industry sources reported that as manufacturers allocated increasing wafer capacity to HBM production to meet contracts with AI infrastructure providers, the supply of conventional DDR4 and DDR5 modules for consumer PCs and smartphones contracted sharply.[^cite_note-TH_RAM-5][^cite_note-Intuition-32]

By September 2025, Samsung Electronics had reportedly expanded its 1c DRAM capacity to target 60,000 wafers per month specifically for [HBM4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBM4) production, further diverting resources from consumer memory lines.[^cite_note-33]

### Geopolitical and trade barriers

The supply chain was further constrained by escalating trade tensions between the United States and China. Throughout 2025, fears of U.S. regulatory backlash and new tariff structures led major manufacturers like Samsung and SK Hynix to halt sales of older semiconductor manufacturing equipment to Chinese entities, effectively capping production capacity in the region.[^cite_note-34] Additionally, proposed tariff policies by the U.S. administration in late 2025 prompted supply chain realignments, with [Apple](856.md) reportedly accelerating plans to source all U.S.-bound iPhones from India to avoid potential levies.[^cite_note-35]

### NAND flash capacity constraints

In the NAND flash segment, manufacturers prioritized higher-margin enterprise SSDs for data center applications while phasing out older process nodes more rapidly than anticipated. In November 2025, contract prices for NAND wafers increased by more than 60% month-over-month for certain product categories, with 512 GB TLC experiencing the steepest rise as legacy manufacturing capacity was retired.[^cite_note-TH_NAND-36]

## Impact on other industries

### Memory manufacturer responses

Samsung is concerned about the possibility of a demand shortfall by 2028.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38] Chey Tae-won, Chairman of SK Group, estimated the memory shortage will last until 2030.[^cite_note-39] In 2026, Kye-hyun Kyung, advisor to Samsung and former head of Samsung's chip group, suggested memory prices could drop within a year.[^cite_note-40]

### PC manufacturers

Major PC manufacturers responded to component cost increases with significant price raises and supply chain strategies. [Dell Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dell_Technologies) Chief Operating Officer Jeff Clarke stated during a November 2025 analyst call that the company had "never witnessed costs escalating at the current pace", describing tighter availability across DRAM, hard drives, and NAND flash memory.[^cite_note-Bloomberg-41][^cite_note-Straits-42]

Analysts at [Morgan Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_Stanley) downgraded [Dell Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dell_Technologies) stock from "Overweight" to "Underweight" in late 2025, citing the company's heavy exposure to rising server memory costs. The firm warned that skyrocketing memory prices could erode margins for server and PC [OEMs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Original_equipment_manufacturer).[^cite_note-TH_MS_Downgrade-43] Conversely, [Apple Inc.](856.md) was reportedly less affected than its competitors, having secured long-term supply agreements for DRAM through the first quarter of 2026.[^cite_note-TH_MS_Downgrade-43] However, on June 25, 2026, Apple announced price hikes effective immediately on all iPads, Macs, HomePods, Apple Vision Pro, and Apple TV, causing Apple shares to decline by more than 6%, the worst day for the stock since [the April 2025 stock market crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_stock_market_crash).[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45]

[Lenovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenovo) chief financial officer Winston Cheng described the cost surge as "unprecedented" and disclosed that the company's memory inventories were approximately 50% above normal levels in anticipation of further price increases.[^cite_note-Bloomberg-41][^cite_note-Straits-42]

### Consumer electronics sector

The shortage particularly affected smartphone manufacturers and other consumer electronics producers. DRAM prices reportedly rose by 172% throughout 2025, leading manufacturers like [Samsung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung) to halt new orders for DDR5 modules to reassess pricing structures and [Micron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micron_Technology) to exit its 'Crucial' brand of consumer products.[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47]

In Tokyo's [Akihabara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akihabara) electronics district, retailers began limiting purchases of memory products to prevent hoarding, with prices for popular DDR5 memory modules more than doubling in some cases.[^cite_note-Reuters-6] Despite the broad trend of rising hardware costs, some companies engaged in aggressive pricing strategies to maintain market share; for example, [Sony](26989.md) reduced the price of the [PlayStation 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_5) by US\$100 for [Black Friday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Friday_\(shopping\)) 2025, potentially absorbing increased component costs to stimulate software ecosystem growth.[^cite_note-48]

Due to memory prices more than doubling in a single quarter, [HP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP_Inc.) revealed in its Q1 2026 earnings call that memory costs accounted for 35% of PC build materials up from 15 to 18% previous quarter. Despite showing strong Q1 2026 earnings driven by [Windows 11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_11) upgrade cycle and AI PC adoption, HP warned investors of low operating margins and up to double digit percentage decline for the coming quarter.[^cite_note-49] Trendforce, an IT analytics company, updated its forecast from 1.7% year-over-year growth in PC market to 2.6% year-over-year decline for 2026, amid backdrop of steadily increasing prices and supply crisis.[^cite_note-50] Research and analytics firms, [Gartner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gartner) and IDC, expected the worldwide PC market to decline 10–11%, and the smartphone market to decline 8–9% in 2026.[^cite_note-51] Gartner also projected that rising memory prices would make low-margin entry level laptops under US\$500 financially unviable in two years.[^cite_note-52]

[Valve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valve_Corporation)'s [second Steam Machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steam_Machine), originally set to launch in early 2026, was delayed because of the shortage.[^cite_note-53][^cite_note-54] When it released in June, the company said "our original goal for the price of Steam Machine is no longer viable" and retailed it at US\$1,049. Analyst Piers Harding-Rolls noted the price was 75% more than the PlayStation 5 at the time, calling it a "niche offering".[^cite_note-55]

### AI infrastructure competition

Technology companies including [Google](1092923.md), [Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon.com), [Microsoft](19001.md), and [Meta Platforms](62420226.md) placed open-ended orders with memory suppliers, indicating they would accept as much supply as available regardless of cost, according to Reuters sources.[^cite_note-Reuters-6]

The limited supply of AI chips has been cited as a reason for the slow down in [compute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compute_\(machine_learning\)) growth.[^cite_note-\:1-56]

In October 2025, [OpenAI](48795986.md) formally announced a strategic partnership using [letters of intent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_of_intent) with [Samsung Electronics](46445426.md) and [SK Hynix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SK_Hynix) to secure supply for its [Stargate Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stargate_LLC) AI infrastructure.[^cite_note-57] Reports indicated that the Stargate initiative alone would consume up to 40% of global DRAM output, requiring approximately 900,000 wafers per month.[^cite_note-58][^cite_note-59] The deal involved the supply of undiced wafers rather than packaged chips to streamline logistics for the massive data center build-out.

[Nvidia](39120.md), whose AI processors require substantial amounts of high-bandwidth memory, acknowledged significant price increases but stated it had secured adequate supply.[^cite_note-Reuters-6] CNBC reported that Nvidia's increasing adoption of LPDDR memory for its AI products added additional pressure to supply chains, as this memory type is also used in premium consumer electronics.[^cite_note-CNBC_Nvidia-60]

## Legal issues

Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron—the three largest manufacturers of DRAM—were sued in an [antitrust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antitrust) class-action lawsuit over potentially taking advantage of the supply shortage by [price-fixing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price-fixing).[^cite_note-61] The complaint alleges they conspired to manipulate the commodity DRAM market through price-fixing and artificial supply constraints. Plaintiffs assert that the manufacturers used the transition to HBM as a pretext to simultaneously cut production of conventional DRAM and exit legacy product lines like DDR3.[^cite_note-62]
