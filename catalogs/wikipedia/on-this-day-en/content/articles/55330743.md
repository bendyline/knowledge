**Marlen Reusser** (born 20 September 1991) is a Swiss [racing cyclist](857311.md), who currently rides for [UCI Women's WorldTeam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCI_Women's_Team) [Movistar Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movistar_Team_\(women's_team\)).[^cite_note-rouleur-4]

## Career

She took up competitive cycling whilst at university, riding in the cycling legs of mixed relay [triathlons](31524.md) as an amateur. She entered the road events of the Swiss national cycling championships in 2017, a few months after receiving a racing licence and whilst in the final year of studies for a degree in medicine: she won the [time trial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_National_Time_Trial_Championships) and was runner up in the [road race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_National_Road_Race_Championships). Her success earned her selection for that year's [European Road Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_European_Road_Championships).[^cite_note-rouleur-4] She also rode in the [women's time trial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_UCI_Road_World_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_time_trial) event at the [2017 UCI Road World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_UCI_Road_World_Championships).[^cite_note-5] In 2019 Reusser took up a place at the [World Cycling Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Cycling_Centre) in [Aigle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aigle), deciding to ride full-time. During her year with the WCC she finished third at the [BeNe Ladies Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BeNe_Ladies_Tour) and sixth in the [World Championship individual time trial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_UCI_Road_World_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_time_trial).[^cite_note-rouleur-4]

The following year she took a top 10 finish at [Liège–Bastogne–Liège](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Li%C3%A8ge%E2%80%93Bastogne%E2%80%93Li%C3%A8ge_Femmes).[^cite_note-rouleur-4] She also rode in the [individual time trial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_UCI_Road_World_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_time_trial) at the [2020 UCI Road World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_UCI_Road_World_Championships) in [Imola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imola), where she won the silver medal.[^cite_note-6]

For the 2021 season, Reusser joined the [Alé BTC Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UAE_Team_L'Imad) team, following the collapse of [Équipe Paule Ka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89quipe_Paule_Ka).[^cite_note-7] She rode in the [individual time trial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_UCI_Road_World_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_time_trial) at the [2021 UCI Road World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_UCI_Road_World_Championships) in [Brugge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruges), where she won the bronze medal.

After one season there, she joined [SD Worx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_SD_Worx%E2%80%93Protime) in 2022.[^cite_note-rouleur-4] She won stage 4 of the [2022 Tour de France Femmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Tour_de_France_Femmes) in a solo breakaway. She was also named most combative rider for the day. She rode in the [individual time trial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_UCI_Road_World_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_time_trial) at the [2022 UCI Road World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_UCI_Road_World_Championships) in [Wollongong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wollongong), where she won the bronze medal - as well as the [mixed team relay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_UCI_Road_World_Championships_%E2%80%93_Mixed_team_relay) where she won the gold medal.

In 2023, Reusser won [Gent–Wevelgem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Gent%E2%80%93Wevelgem_\(women's_race\)), as well as the [Itzulia Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Itzulia_Women) and [Tour de Suisse Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Tour_de_Suisse_Women) stage races. At the [Tour de France Femmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Tour_de_France_Femmes), she won the stage 8 time trial in Pau.[^cite_note-8]

In September, 2025, Reusser won her first UCI Women 's Elite Individual Trial (ITT ) title at the [2025 UCI Road World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_UCI_Road_World_Championships) in [Kigali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kigali). [^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

In September 2026, she won this title again at the 2026 UCI Road World Championships in [Montreal](7954681.md).[^cite_note-11]

## Personal life

Marlen Reusser grew up in a farming family in the Swiss [Emmental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmental). Until 16 she played violin, and participated at an arts support program at the [University of the Arts Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Arts_Bern). In school she started running, after a couple of ankle injuries she switched to swimming and cycling. From 2008 to 2009 she was president of the [Bernese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bern) Young [Greens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Party_of_Switzerland). After [matura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matura) she studied [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine) and worked as assistant doctor for surgery. From 2017 to 2018 she was in the board of the Emmental Green Party. While preparing for the 2018 World Championship in [Innsbruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innsbruck), she concurrently worked part time as a doctor in the [Langnau hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langnau_im_Emmental).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]
