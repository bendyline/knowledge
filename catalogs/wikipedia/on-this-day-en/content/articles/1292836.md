The **Southern Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): المحافظة الجنوبية, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Al-Muḥāfaẓat al-Janūbīyah*) has the largest area of the four [governorates of Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorates_of_Bahrain). It includes parts of [Bahrain](18933277.md)'s old municipalities – [Al Mintaqah al Gharbiyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Mintaqah_al_Gharbiyah), [Ar Rifa' wa al Mintaqah al Janubiyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ar_Rifa'_wa_al_Mintaqah_al_Janubiyah), and [Juzur Hawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawar_Islands) (the Hawar Islands). It has the second smallest population of the governorates on Bahrain after [Al Muharraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Muharraq_Governorate).

The Governor of the Southern Governorate is Shaikh [Khalifa bin Ali Al Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalifa_bin_Ali_Al_Khalifa?action=edit\&redlink=1) (born 1993), the grandson of former Prime Minister Prince [Khalifa bin Salman Al Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalifa_bin_Salman_Al_Khalifa) and the son of Shaikh [Ali bin Khalifa Al Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_bin_Khalifa_Al_Khalifa), the deputy prime minister.
