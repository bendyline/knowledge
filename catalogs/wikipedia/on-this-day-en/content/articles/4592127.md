**Bhakkar District** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضِلع بھكّر), is a [district](639402.md) in the province of [Punjab](24751.md), [Pakistan](23235.md). The district was created out of parts of [Mianwali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mianwali_District) in 1982,[^cite_note-4] and has the city of [Bhakkar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhakkar) as its headquarters. Part of its area consists of a riverine tract along the [Indus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus), called Kaccha, while most of the district area lies in the desolate plain of the [Thal Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thal_Desert).[^cite_note-IGI-5]

Located in the west of the Punjab province, Bhakkar district is bordered by [Layyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Layyah_District) to the south, [Jhang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhang_District) to the southeast, [Dera Ismail Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dera_Ismail_Khan_District) to the west, [Khushab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khushab_District) to the northeast, and [Mianwali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mianwali_District) to the north.

As of the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), Bhakkar district has 313,311 households and a population of 1,957,470. The district has a sex ratio of 108.00 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 55.68%: 66.81% for males and 43.87% for females.[^cite_note-2023table1-11][^cite_note-2023table12-12] 593,924 (30.35% of the surveyed population) are under 10 years of age.[^cite_note-2023table5-13] 352,434 (18.00%) live in urban areas.[^cite_note-2023table1-11]

### Languages

Languages of Bhakkar district (2023)

[^cite_note-2023_census-19]

1. [Saraiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraiki_language) (79.4%)

2. [Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language) (9.73%)

3. [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu) (7.50%)

4. [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) (2.41%)

5. Others (0.94%)

At the time of the 2023 census, 79.42% of the population spoke [Saraiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraiki_language), 9.73% [Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language), 7.5% [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu) and 2.41% [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) as their first language.[^cite_note-20]

## Education

According to the 2017 census, the literacy rate of Bhakkar is about 55%. There are 19 colleges, and 1300 primary, elementary, secondary, and higher secondary schools.
