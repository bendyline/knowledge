The **2026 FIA World Endurance Championship** is the fourteenth season of the [FIA World Endurance Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIA_World_Endurance_Championship), a [sports car racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_car_racing) series organised by the [Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile](248019.md) (FIA) and the [Automobile Club de l'Ouest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile_Club_de_l'Ouest) (ACO). The series is open to Hypercars (built under [Le Mans Hypercar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans_Hypercar) (LMH) or [Le Mans Daytona h](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans_Daytona_h) (LMDh) regulations) and [LMGT3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_GT3) racing cars.

The [FIA](248019.md) and [ACO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile_Club_de_l'Ouest) decided to stop publishing the [balance of performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balance_of_performance) (BoP) tables for the WEC starting in 2026. The decision aimed to prevent speculation and misinterpretation of the performance balancing process. The BoP tables, which previously detailed the weight, power, and energy allocation for each car, will now be shared only with the teams involved.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]





[Sébastien Buemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9bastien_Buemi), [Brendon Hartley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendon_Hartley), and [Ryo Hirakawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryo_Hirakawa) of the No. 8 [Toyota Racing](3796361.md) currently lead the Hypercar World Endurance Drivers' Championship, with [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota) leading the Manufacturers' Championship. [Jonny Edgar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonny_Edgar) currently leads the LMGT3 Drivers' Championship, with his No. 33 [TF Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TF_Sport) team leading the LMGT3 Endurance Trophy.

## Calendar

The calendar was announced on 13 June 2025, during the [2025 24 Hours of Le Mans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans) weekend. The calendar was unchanged from 2025, with the only notable change that the [Qatar race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_1812_km) was planned to move from February to March.[^cite_note-3] In March 2026, the Qatar 1812 km was postponed to October due to the evolving geopolitical situation in the Middle East caused by the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] As a result, the 6 Hours of Imola became the opening round of the season.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] The Qatar 1812 km and [8 Hours of Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8_Hours_of_Bahrain) were ultimately cancelled in July 2026. They were replaced with a pair of 6-hour races in Europe: a first trip to [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6_Hours_of_Barcelona), which previously hosted the Prologue in 2019, and a return to [Monza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6_Hours_of_Monza) for the first time since 2023.[^cite_note-8]
