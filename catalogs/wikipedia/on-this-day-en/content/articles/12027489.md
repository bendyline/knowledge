The **Bint Jbeil District** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): قضاء بنت جبيل, *_Qaḍāʾ Bint Jubayl_*) is a district in the [Nabatiyeh Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabatiyeh_Governorate) of [Lebanon](17771.md). The capital of the district is [Bint Jbeil](6124393.md), which was demolished by Israel in April of 2026.
