***Ophiocordyceps*** is a [genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genus) of [fungi](19178965.md) within the [family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_\(taxonomy\)) [Ophiocordycipitaceae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophiocordycipitaceae).[^cite_note-2] The widespread genus, first described scientifically by British [mycologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mycology) [Tom Petch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Petch) in 1931,[^cite_note-Petch1931-3] contains about 140 species that grow on insects.[^cite_note-Kirk2008-4] [Anamorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teleomorph,_anamorph_and_holomorph) genera that correspond with *Ophiocordyceps* species are *[Hirsutella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hirsutella)*, *[Hymenostilbe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hymenostilbe)*, *[Isaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaria)*, *[Paraisaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraisaria)*, and *[Syngliocladium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syngliocladium)*.[^cite_note-Sung2007-5]

One [species complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Species_complex), *[Ophiocordyceps unilateralis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophiocordyceps_unilateralis)*, is known for its [parasitism](43937.md) on [ants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant), in which it alters the behavior of the ants in such a way as to propagate itself more effectively. The parasite starts on the ground and comes in contact with an ant, killing it and then growing its fruiting bodies from the ant's head and releasing its [spores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spore).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] To accomplish this, infected ants are stripped of their instinctive fear of heights, and leaving the relative safety of their nests, climb up the nearest plant—a syndrome known as "summit disease".[^cite_note-\:0-10] The ant then clamps its jaws around the plant in a "death grip" and mycelia grow from the ant's feet and stitch them to the surface of the plant.[^cite_note-\:0-10] The spores released from the ant carcass fall to the ground and infect other ants that come in contact with the spores so that this cycle continues.[^cite_note-ReferenceA-11] Areas with high densities of ants that have this fungus growing out of them are known as graveyards.[^cite_note-ReferenceA-11]

A 48-million-year-old fossil of an ant in the death-grip of *Ophiocordyceps unilateralis* was discovered in Germany.[^cite_note-12]

Not all fungi of this genus are harmful to host insects. A few act as yeast-like symbioants of cicadas, having retired from the parasitic lifestyle of *O. sobolifera* to take on the role of the degraded *[Hodgkinia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodgkinia?action=edit\&redlink=1)* (bacterial) symbioant.[^cite_note-13]

## Sources and uses



Moth larvae infested by *[Ophiocordyceps sinensis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophiocordyceps_sinensis)* sold as herbal medicine

*[Ophiocordyceps sinensis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophiocordyceps_sinensis)* is a species that infects the larvae of [Tibetan ghost moths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepialidae), and is used extensively in [traditional Chinese medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_medicine).[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-drugs-15][^cite_note-16] There is currently no [scientific evidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evidence-based_medicine) that use of this species has any clinically detectable effect on human diseases.[^cite_note-drugs-15]

*[Ophiocordyceps robertsii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophiocordyceps_robertsii)* is used by the [Māori people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%81ori_people) of New Zealand as food and a source of ink for tattoos. The charred insect-fungus complex was mixed with tree sap to make an almost black ink.[^cite_note-17]

*[Ophiocordyceps nutans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophiocordyceps_nutans)* in its anamorphic form *[Hymenostilbe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hymenostilbe) nutans* is used as a biological control for stinkbugs.[^cite_note-Sasaki_2008-18]

## Non-insect hosts

One not-yet-named fungus that falls into *Ophiocordyceps* infects juvenile [edible crabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edible_crab). Infection is fatal once the fungus becomes established in the [hemocoel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemocoel).[^cite_note-19]

## Phylogeny

A relatively broad phylogeny of the genus was published in 2024 as part of the effort to distinguish more species from inside the *O. sinensis* [complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Species_complex).[^cite_note-22]

## In popular culture

Simply referred to as "cordyceps", an unspecified species in this genus is the cause of a worldwide pandemic and the zombie-like "infected" in the 2013 video game *[The Last of Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_\(video_game\))*, its 2020 [sequel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_Part_II), and the 2023 [television adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_\(TV_series\)).

In the 2014 novel *[The Girl with All the Gifts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Girl_with_All_the_Gifts)* and its 2016 [film adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Girl_with_All_the_Gifts_\(film\)), a mutation of *[Ophiocordyceps unilateralis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophiocordyceps_unilateralis)* is responsible for an infection that causes the collapse of civilization.

In the 2022 comic-book series *[Poison Ivy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poison_Ivy_\(2022_comic_book\))* written by [G. Willow Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._Willow_Wilson), the titular character makes use of a fictitious species belonging to this genus.

The [Pokémon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon) [Paras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paras_\(Pok%C3%A9mon\)) and [Parasect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parasect) are based on insects parasitized by *Ophiocordyceps*.
