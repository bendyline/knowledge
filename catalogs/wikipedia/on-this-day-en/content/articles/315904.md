**Israeli security forces** (also known as **Israel security establishment**; [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): מערכת הבטחון, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hebrew): *Ma'arechet haBitachon*) include a variety of organizations, including [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military), [law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_agency), [paramilitary](146730.md), [governmental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_agency), and [intelligence agencies](81014.md).

## Military

- [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) (IDF): Israel's military, comprising the [Ground Forces](64121347.md), [Air Force](177619.md) and [Navy](1370788.md).

## Police



Israeli police officers and a patrol car

- [Israel Police](745595.md): A civilian police force responsible for law enforcement and maintaining [public safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_safety).&#x20;

  - [Border Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Border_Police): A [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie) responsible for [border security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_security).

  - [Civil Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Guard_\(Israel\)): A volunteer organization of [Israeli citizens](181296.md) which assists in daily police work.

  - [National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Guard_of_Israel): An operational body which assists the police activity in routine and emergency situations.

## Intelligence

Main article: [Israeli intelligence community](4990721.md)

- [Shin Bet](311752.md) (Israel Security Agency): The organization responsible for [internal security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_security), including in the [Israeli-occupied territories](2781576.md).

- [Mossad](27619007.md) (Institute for Intelligence and Special Operations): The agency responsible for [foreign intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_intelligence).

- [Aman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Intelligence_Directorate_\(Israel\)) (Directorate of Military Intelligence): The agency responsible for [military intelligence](146678.md). It produces comprehensive national intelligence estimates for the prime minister and cabinet, daily intelligence reports, risk of war estimates, target studies on nearby countries, and communications intercepts. Aman also conducts across-border agent operations. Aman is an independent service, co-equal with the army, navy and air force. Aman has an estimated staff of 7,000.

  [^cite_note-1]

## Emergency services

- [Magen David Adom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magen_David_Adom) (Red Shield of David): Made up of volunteer and professional medical responders, they provide emergency medical, disaster, ambulance, and blood services.

  [^cite_note-2]

- [Israel Fire and Rescue Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Fire_and_Rescue_Services): Responsible for extinguishing fires and civilian rescue operations.

- [Home Front Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Front_Command): An IDF military rescue team, which handles large-scale civilian disasters such as earthquakes, collapsed buildings and missile attacks on cities.

- [Unit 669](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_669): The [Israeli Air Force](177619.md) [medevac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medevac) extraction unit.

- 11 Local Rescue teams in the [Golan Heights](7515865.md), [Galilee](12639.md)-[Carmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Carmel), [Jezreel Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jezreel_Valley), [Samaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samaria), [Ein Gedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ein_Gedi_\(kibbutz\)), [Megilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megilot_Regional_Council), [Gush Etzion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gush_Etzion), [Arad](310435.md), [Negev](142273.md), [Arava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabah), and [Eilat](66162.md)-[Eilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eilot).

## Other organizations

- [Israel Prison Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Prison_Service): A security organization responsible for the holding of prisoners and detainees. The IPS coordinates with relevant national, regional and municipal authorities and organizations.

  [^cite_note-3]

- [Knesset Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knesset_Guard): Responsible for security in the [Knesset](74656.md) compound and [building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knesset_building), headed by the sergeant-at-arms.
