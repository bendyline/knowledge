**Kings County** is a county located in the [U.S. state](18618239.md) of [California](5407.md). The population was 152,486 at the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census).[^cite_note-5] The [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) is [Hanford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanford,_California).[^cite_note-GR6-6]

Kings County comprises the [Hanford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanford,_California)-[Corcoran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corcoran,_California), CA [metropolitan statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_statistical_area), which is also included in the [Visalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visalia,_California)-[Porterville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porterville,_California)-Hanford, CA [combined statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combined_statistical_area). It is in the [San Joaquin Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Joaquin_Valley), a rich agricultural region.

## History

The area was inhabited for thousands of years by [Native Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States) including the Tachi [Yokuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yokuts) tribe.[^cite_note-7] They continue to live in the area on the [Santa Rosa Rancheria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Rosa_Rancheria). It was colonized by Spain, Mexico and the United States.

An 1805 expedition probably led by Spanish Army Lieutenant [Gabriel Moraga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Moraga) recorded discovering the river, which they named *El Rio de los Santos Reyes* (River of the Holy Kings) after the [Three Wise Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Wise_Men) of the [Bible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bible).[^cite_note-Hoover-8] At the time of the United States conquest of California in 1848, the new government changed the name to [Kings River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kings_River_\(California\)) after which the county was named.[^cite_note-Hoover-8]

In 1880, a dispute over land titles between settlers and the [Southern Pacific Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Pacific_Railroad) resulted in a bloody gun battle on a farm 5.6 mi (9.0 km) northwest of Hanford; seven men died. This event became known as the [Mussel Slough Tragedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mussel_Slough_Tragedy).

Kings County was formed in 1893 from the western part of [Tulare County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulare_County,_California).[^cite_note-Hoover-8] In 1909, by an act of the state legislature, 208 square miles (540 km<sup>2</sup>) of Fresno County territory was added to the northwest portion of Kings County.[^cite_note-9]

Settlers reclaimed [Tulare Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulare_Lake) and its [wetlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wetlands) for agricultural development. In surface area, it was formerly the largest body of freshwater west of the [Great Lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Lakes), and supported a large population of migratory birds as well as local birds and wildlife. [Monoculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monoculture) has sharply reduced habitat for many species.

In 1928, oil was discovered in the [Kettleman Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kettleman_Hills) located in the southwestern part of Kings County. The [Kettleman North Dome Oil Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kettleman_North_Dome_Oil_Field) became one of the most productive oil fields in the United States.[^cite_note-10]

During the [Great Depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Depression), over 18,000 cotton pickers in the southern San Joaquin Valley, mostly migrant [Mexican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Americans) workers, went on strike in the [California agricultural strikes of 1933](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_agricultural_strikes_of_1933). During the strike, 3,500 striking farm workers lived in a four-acre camp on the land of a small farmer on the outskirts of Corcoran. Ultimately, the federal government intervened to force both sides to negotiate a settlement.[^cite_note-11]

[Lemoore Army Airfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemoore_Army_Airfield) was established for training and defense during [World War II](32927.md). In 1961, the [U.S. Navy](20518076.md) opened [NAS Lemoore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAS_Lemoore) 9 miles (14 km) west of [Lemoore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemoore,_California), not far from the earlier site.

The completion of the [California Aqueduct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Aqueduct) in the early 1970s brought needed water for agriculture and domestic use to the west side of the county.

## Geography

According to the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Census_Bureau), the county has a total area of 1,392 square miles (3,610 km<sup>2</sup>), of which 1,389 square miles (3,600 km<sup>2</sup>) is land and 2.1 square miles (5.4 km<sup>2</sup>) (0.2%) is water.[^cite_note-GR1-12]

Kings County is bordered on the north and northwest by [Fresno County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fresno_County), on the east by [Tulare County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulare_County), on the south by [Kern County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_County) and a small part of [San Luis Obispo County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Luis_Obispo_County) and on the west by [Monterey County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterey_County).

[Table Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Table_Mountain_\(Kings_County,_California\)) is the highest point in Kings County at an elevation of 3,476 feet (1,059 m). It is located in the [Diablo Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diablo_Range) in southwestern Kings County on the boundary between Kings and Monterey counties. [Tent Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tent_Hills) is a ridge in Kings County.

Most of the historic [Tulare Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulare_Lake) was within Kings County. Although reclaimed for farming late in the 19th century, it was the largest freshwater lake west of the Great Lakes.

### 2020 census

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), the county had a population of 152,486. The median age was 32.9 years, with 26.6% of residents under the age of 18 and 10.6% aged 65 or older. For every 100 females there were 118.1 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 124.0 males age 18 and over.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-20]

The racial makeup of the county was 40.2% White, 5.9% [Black or African American](2154.md), 2.4% [American Indian and Alaska Native](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 3.9% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans), 0.3% [Native Hawaiian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Hawaiians) and [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander), 31.5% from some other race, and 15.9% from [two or more races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial_Americans). [Hispanic or Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) residents of any race comprised 56.8% of the population.[^cite_note-Census2020PL-21]

89.1% of residents lived in urban areas, while 10.9% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-Census2020DHC-22]

There were 44,100 households in the county, of which 44.4% had children under the age of 18 living with them and 24.9% had a female householder with no spouse or partner present. About 18.7% of all households were made up of individuals and 7.5% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-20]

There were 46,287 housing units, of which 4.7% were vacant. Among occupied housing units, 54.1% were owner-occupied and 45.9% were renter-occupied. The homeowner vacancy rate was 1.1% and the rental vacancy rate was 3.9%.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-20]

### 2010 census

The [2010 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_census) reported that Kings County had a population of 152,982. The census included inmates of the three men's state prisons. According to the [California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Department_of_Corrections_and_Rehabilitation), there were 18,640 inmates in Kings County prisons on March 31, 2010, which was 12.2% of the population.[^cite_note-cdcr.ca.gov-30] The inmate population had been reduced to 13,894 on December 31, 2013.[^cite_note-cdcr.ca.gov-30] The racial makeup of Kings County was 83,027 (54.3%) [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_\(U.S._Census\)), 11,014 (7.2%) [African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American_\(U.S._census\)), 2,562 (1.7%) [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 5,620 (3.7%) [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_\(U.S._Census\)), 271 (0.2%) [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander_\(U.S._Census\)), 42,996 (28.1%) from [other races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), and 7,492 (4.9%) from two or more races. [Hispanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_\(U.S._Census\)) or [Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latino_\(U.S._Census\)) of any race were 77,866 persons (50.9%).[^cite_note-31]

The U.S. Census does not identify how many residents are undocumented immigrants. However, the [Public Policy Institute of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Policy_Institute_of_California) issued a report in July 2011, which estimated there were 9,000 illegal immigrants living in Kings County in 2008, which would be 5.8% of the county's population.[^cite_note-32]

According to the California Board of State and Community Corrections, Kings County had the highest incarceration rate of California's 58 counties in 2014 at 1,384 per 100,000 population. Statewide, the rate was 567 per 100,000.[^cite_note-33]

### 2000

As of the 2000 census, there were 129,461 people, 34,418 households, and 26,983 families residing in the county. However, the California Department of Finance estimates that the population had grown 154,434 as of January 1, 2008.[^cite_note-34] The [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) based on the 2000 census was 36/km<sup>2</sup> (93/sq mi). There were 36,563 housing units at an average density of 26 per square mile (10/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of the county was 53.7% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), 8.3% [Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)) or [African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), 1.7% [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), 3.1% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), 0.2% [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), 28.3% from [other races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), and 4.8% from two or more races. 43.6% of the population were [Hispanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)) or [Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)) of any race. 7.1% were of [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Americans), 6.2% [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Americans), 5.3% [Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Americans) and 5.1% [American ancestry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_ancestry) according to [Census 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_2000). 63.6% spoke [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), 30.9% [Spanish](26825.md), 1.4% [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language), 1.4% [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language) and 1.3% [Samoan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samoan_language) as their first language.

There were 34,418 households, out of which 46.4% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 58.0% were [married couples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage) living together, 14.3% had a female householder with no husband present, and 21.6% were non-families. 17.0% of all households were made up of individuals, and 6.8% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older. The average household size was 3.18 and the average family size was 3.56.

In the county, the population was spread out, with 29.0% under the age of 18, 11.8% from 18 to 24, 35.0% from 25 to 44, 16.8% from 45 to 64, and 7.4% who were 65 years of age or older. The median age was 30 years in 2000, which had increased to 31.1 by the time of the 2010 census.[^cite_note-Fresno_Bee,_May_15,_2011-35] For every 100 females there were 134.8 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 148.8 males. The ratio may be attributed to the presence of three men's state prisons in the county.

## Government and policing

### Administration

The government of Kings County operates as a general law county defined and authorized under the [California Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Constitution) and [law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_law). That is, it does not have a county [charter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charter). The county is governed by a five-member [Board of Supervisors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Supervisors). Supervisors are elected by districts for four-year terms. There are no term limits in effect. The chairman and vice-chairman are elected annually by the Board of Supervisors from among its members. On January 7, 2020, the Board elected Supervisor Doug Verboon as chairman and Supervisor Craig Pederson as vice-chairman to serve during 2020. Other Supervisors include Richard Valle, Joe Neves and Richard Fagundes.[^cite_note-36] The Board of Supervisors appoints a County Administrative Officer. Rebecca Campbell was appointed to that office effective on January 1, 2018. She succeeded Larry Spikes who held the post from 1993 through 2017.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

### District Attorney

The Kings County District Attorney is Sarah Hacker. The District Attorney of each of the 58 counties in California is responsible for prosecuting the violation of California state and local laws and ordinances. The District Attorney is the sole criminal prosecuting entity in the county. In addition to the prosecution duties of the District Attorney, the Kings County District Attorney also has civil and criminal investigative authority with its own Bureau of Investigations composed of sworn peace officers with arresting authority. The Kings County District Attorney's Victim-Witness Assistance Program also provides assistance to victims of crime through federal, state, and local resources.

### City police departments

Within Kings County, there are four city police departments: Hanford (the county seat), Corcoran, Lemoore, and Avenal.

### Sheriff

As of November 2025, the Kings County Sheriff-Coroner-Public Administrator is Dave Putnam.[^cite_note-40] The Kings County Sheriff's Office provides court protection, county jail operation, and coroner services for the entire county. It provides patrol and detective services to the unincorporated areas of the county.[^cite_note-41]

### State and federal representation

In the [California State Legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Legislature), Kings County is in [the 16th senatorial district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California's_16th_senatorial_district), represented by [Democrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Democratic_Party) [Melissa Hurtado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_Hurtado), and in [the 33rd Assembly district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California's_33rd_State_Assembly_district), represented by [Republican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Republican_Party) [Alexandra Macedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandra_Macedo).[^cite_note-42]

In the [United States House of Representatives](19468510.md), Kings County is split between [California's 20th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California's_20th_congressional_district), represented by [Republican](32070.md) [Vince Fong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vince_Fong),[^cite_note-43] and [California's 22nd congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California's_22nd_congressional_district), represented by [Republican](32070.md) [David Valadao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Valadao).[^cite_note-44]

## Economy

The economy is based on agriculture. About 87% of Kings County's 890,000 acres is agricultural farmland. The gross value of all agricultural crops and products produced during 2018 in Kings County was \$2,351,983,000. Kings County is 10th among California counties in agricultural production. The top commodity in 2018 was milk with a value of \$676.7 million. Other major agricultural commodities included pistachios, cotton, cattle and calves, almonds and processing tomatoes.[^cite_note-56][^cite_note-57] Another major employer is [NAS Lemoore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAS_Lemoore), the [U.S. Navy](20518076.md)'s newest and largest master jet base. According to a Navy study in 2008, payroll, base spending, retiree and veterans' checks total more than \$1 billion in annual economic impact to the region.[^cite_note-58] Other important employers include a [Del Monte Foods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Del_Monte_Foods) tomato processing plant, [Adventist Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventist_Health), the [J. G. Boswell Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._G._Boswell_Company), an [Olam International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olam_International) tomato processing facility, [Leprino Foods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leprino_Foods), the largest [mozzarella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozzarella) cheese maker in the world, the Kings County Government and the [California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Department_of_Corrections_and_Rehabilitation) which operates three state prisons in Kings County.[^cite_note-59]

In 2011–2013, the U.S. Census Bureau estimated that the median household income in the county was \$47,035 and that 17.6% of the population was below the [poverty line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_line).[^cite_note-60] In 2018, according to the U.S. [Bureau of Economic Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Economic_Analysis), average per capita income was \$35,306 in Kings County, which ranked it last of California's 58 counties.[^cite_note-61] Per capita personal income is calculated by dividing the population by the total personal income of the area's residents. In Kings County's case, the U.S. Census Bureau's estimated population of 151,366 was used for that calculation. However, the population estimate includes incarcerated persons with little or no income.

The homeownership rate was 54.2% at the time of the 2010 census.[^cite_note-Fresno_Bee,_May_15,_2011-35] According to Zillow Real Estate Research, an estimated 24% of homeowners in the county owned their homes free and clear in the third quarter of 2012.[^cite_note-62]

Taxable sales in 2015 totaled about \$1.7 billion.[^cite_note-63]

Kings County did not escape the effects of the [Great Recession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Recession). The unemployment rate in May 2012 was 14.9%, up from 10.1% in July 2008. However, the rate had dropped to 9.8% in February 2020 at the eve of the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md). The unemployment rate had risen to 16.8% in April of that year.[^cite_note-64] According to the California Employment Development Department, as of December 2012, civilian employment totaled 53,100 and an additional 8,900 people were unemployed. Many residents of Kings County were employed in services (31,900 persons, including 14,800 government employees) and agriculture (5,500 employees) as well as in some manufacturing enterprises (4,300 employees) and construction (1,000 employees).[^cite_note-65] Jeffrey Michael, director of the Business Forecasting Center at the [University of the Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Pacific_\(United_States\)), stated in an October 2010 newspaper interview that nearly half of Kings County's personal earnings come from government jobs, which pay more than agricultural employment.

Kings County's dairy industry dropped from \$670 million in milk sold in 2008 to \$411 million in 2009 - a 39% drop.[^cite_note-66] By mid-2009, the price paid to milk producers had dropped to a point that was far below the cost of production according to a July 2009 quote from Bill Van Dam, [CEO](52234.md) of the Alliance of Western Milk Producers.[^cite_note-67] By December 2010, milk prices had increased to about \$13 per hundredweight from a low of below \$10 in 2009. However, the price of corn used for feed had increased because of its use by the ethanol industry. Van Dam was quoted that month as saying that at current prices, dairy operators are at or close to the break-even point.[^cite_note-68] By the summer of 2012, it was reported that despite a milk price of about \$15 per hundredweight, the rising cost of cattle feed had caused many dairy farmers to sell all or part of their herds and even file for bankruptcy.[^cite_note-69] In 2014, milk prices were topping \$22 per hundredweight and the value of milk sold rose to \$970 million in that year.[^cite_note-70] However, by March 2016 milk was reportedly selling closer to \$13 per hundredweight.[^cite_note-71]

## Culture

Kings County has a large annual celebration held each May called Kings County Homecoming Week.[^cite_note-72] In 2015, the event was scaled back to one day and renamed Pioneer Days.[^cite_note-73] The event returned as Kings County Homecoming Week in 2016 but without the traditional parade.[^cite_note-74]
