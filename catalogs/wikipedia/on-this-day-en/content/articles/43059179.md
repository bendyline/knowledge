**Palghar district** ([Marathi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathi_language) pronunciation: [\[paːlɡʱəɾ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Marathi)) is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Maharashtra) in the [Konkan division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konkan_division) of the [Indian state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_state) of [Maharashtra](20629.md).[^cite_note-1] The headquarters of the district is the town of [Palghar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palghar). Other major cities in the district are [Vasai-Virar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasai-Virar), [Talasari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talasari), [Jawhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawhar) and [Dahanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahanu).

On 1 Aug 2014, the [Government of Maharashtra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Maharashtra) announced the formation of Palghar as the 36th district of Maharashtra. It was carved out of [Thane district](638817.md). Palghar District starts from [Dahanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahanu) in the north and ends at [Naigaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naigaon). It comprises the talukas of [Palghar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palghar), [Vada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vada,_Palghar), [Vikramgad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikramgad), [Jawhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawhar), [Mokhada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mokhada), [Dahanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahanu), [Talasari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talasari) and [Vasai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasai). At the [2011 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Census_of_India), the talukas now comprising the district had a population of 2,990,116.[^cite_note-Collector-2]

Palghar has an urban population of 1,435,210, that is 48% of total population is living in Urbanized Area. Palghar has all the three categories of the lifestyles, Urban (Nagari), Coastal (Sagari) and Mountains-Hilly Region (Dongari). Palghar District is blessed by beautiful beaches as it is the part of konkan such as "Shirgaon Beach", "Kelve Beach", "Vadrai Beach", "Dahanu Beach", "Bordi Beach", "Chinchani Beach", "Arnala Beach", "Rajodi Beach", "Suruchi Beach", "Kalamb Beach", "Bhuigaon Beach" etc. The district is bounded by Thane and [Nashik districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashik_district) in the east and northeast, and by the [Valsad district](3129172.md) of [Gujarat](53707.md) state and the [Dadra and Nagar Haveli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli) district of the [Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli_and_Daman_and_Diu) union territory in the north. The [Arabian Sea](2747.md) forms the western boundary, while the whole district falls under the fastest developing northern part of [Mumbai Metropolitan Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Metropolitan_Region). Basically people of Palghar District population belong to Tribal-Konkni, Warli, koli, Agri..

## History

Until 1 August 2014, the talukas now comprising Palghar District formed a part of Thane District. After a struggle and demand for almost 25 years, for bifurcation, the creation of the new district was approved by the Cabinet of Maharashtra on 13 June 2014 and the new district of Palghar came into existence on 1 August.

## Geography

The district is the northernmost part of the Konkan lowlands of Maharashtra. It comprises the wide, amphitheater-like [Ulhas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulhas) basin on the south and the hilly [Vaitarna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaitarna) valley on the north, together with plateaus and the slopes of [Sahyadri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Ghats). From the steep slopes of the Sahyadri in the east, the land falls through a succession of plateaus in the north and centre of the district to the Ulhas valley in the south. The distance from the parts of different palaces to headquarters Palghar, by road is as follows: Khodala 138 km, Mokhada 112 km, Jawhar 75 km, Vikramgad 60 km.

Palghar can be separated into three geographical regions.[^cite_note-\:03-3] The Eastern part of the [Western Ghats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Ghats), or Sahyadri mountains, is called *jangalapatti*. This mainly encompasses the Jawhar, Mokhada, and Vikramgad talukas. The western part of the mountains district is called *bandarpatti* which includes Vasai, Palghar, Dahanu. The third section is characterized by broad and flat land, including the Wada taluka.

The main river flowing through the district is the Vaitarna. The river has many tributaries; the most important of them are Barvi and [Bhatsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhatsa), Pinjal, [Surya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surya), Daherja and [Tansa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tansa_River). Vaitarna, the largest of Konkan Rivers rises in the Trimbakeshwar hills in Nashik district, opposite to the source of [Godavari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godavari). The river flows across [Shahapur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahapur_taluka), Vada and Palghar talukas and enter the Arabian Sea through a wide estuary off Arnala. Vaitarna River is 154 km long and has a drainage area that practically covers the entire northern part of the district. In Palghar many students come for studying from outer areas. Ulhas river which flows to Arabian sea is Vasai creek, district's southern border. Arnala Island is located in Vasai taluka, at the entrance to the Vaitarna estuary.

The Palghar district gets some of the most rainfall in all of Maharashtra, an average 3000 mm (118 in) every year.[^cite_note-\:03-3] Given its proximity to Mumbai, Palghar serves as a water catchment area to Mumbai.[^cite_note-\:13-4] There are many dams, such as the [Tansa Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tansa_Dam), which collect water and deliver to Mumbai through pipelines. There are various debates on if this is damaging to Palghar. Some scholars argue believe this is necessary to meet the water demands of a large-scale city, but others argue that this is depriving Palghar residents of water, many of whom are farmers and have high water needs for agriculture.[^cite_note-\:13-4]

## Demographics

At the time of the 2011 census, Palghar district had a population of 2,990,116, of which 1,561,438 (52.22%) lived in urban areas. Palghar had a sex ratio of 934 females per 1000 males. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes made up 2.91% and 37.39% of the population respectively.[^cite_note-census2011-5]

## Economy

In the mountainous regions of the Palghar district, such as in [Jawhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawhar) and [Mokhada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mokhada), agriculture is the primary business.[^cite_note-\:1-8] These areas mainly cultivate rice, millet, and black gram, as well as a variety of fruits and vegetables.[^cite_note-9] Due to Palghar district's proximity to Mumbai, the region is primary source of produce and grains to the [Mumbai Metropolitan Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Metropolitan_Region). The dependency on the district has led the Government of Maharashtra to encourage [organic farming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organic_farming) in Palghar in order to ensure agricultural output and work towards sustainable options. The western 'bandarpatti' region's economy relies on in agricultural and fishing businesses. Dahanu is known for their [chickoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chickoo) production, which is distributed throughout all of India. A special Chickoo festival is held every year at Bordi beach in Dahanu. [Satpati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satpati), also in 'bandarpatti, is Maharashtra's largest fishing port. Dahanu, Arnala, Vasai and Datiware *a*re also major fishing ports in the region.[^cite_note-\:1-8]

Palghar has India's first [atomic power plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarapur_Atomic_Power_Station) located at [Tarapur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarapur,_Maharashtra). The industrial town of Boisar is also home to one of Maharashtra's largest industrial areas at Tarapur MIDC. [MSEDCL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSEDCL) supplies electricity to all urban and rural areas across the district. [Gujarat Gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujarat_Gas) has been authorized in April 2015 to supply [compressed natural gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compressed_natural_gas) and [piped natural gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piped_natural_gas) to all urban and rural areas across the district.
