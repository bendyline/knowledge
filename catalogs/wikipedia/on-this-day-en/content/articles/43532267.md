The **Government of Puntland** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Dowladda Buntilaan*; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): حكومة أرض بنط) is the supreme governing authority of the [semi-autonomous state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Somalia#Federal_Member_States) of [Puntland](233569.md). The legal structure of Puntland consists of the [judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary) ([Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Puntland)), [legislative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) ([House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_Puntland)) and the [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) (the [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Puntland), [Vice president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Puntland) and his nominated council of Ministries) branches of government.[^cite_note-MOPIC-1] Puntland has conducted seven [presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puntland_presidential_election) ([1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Puntland_presidential_election?action=edit\&redlink=1), [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Puntland_presidential_election?action=edit\&redlink=1), [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Puntland_presidential_election), [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Puntland_presidential_election), [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Puntland_presidential_election), [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Puntland_presidential_election), [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Puntland_presidential_election)). Five Presidents are elected by a 66-member unicameral parliament.[^cite_note-2] Since 1998, the governing bodies have been located in the administrative capital of [Garowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garowe).

## Establishment

Following the outbreak of the [civil war](3070332.md) in 1991, a homegrown constitutional conference was held in [Garowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garowe) in 1998 over a period of three months. Attended by the area's political elite, traditional elders (*Issims*), members of the business community, intellectuals and other civil society representatives, the autonomous *Puntland State of Somalia* was subsequently officially established so as to deliver services to the population, offer security, facilitate trade, and interact with both domestic and international partners.[^cite_note-Speipbasb-3]

## Competencies

Since 31 March 2024, following changes due to the [constitutional crisis in Somalia](76521890.md#Actions_of_Puntland), the government of Puntland no longer recognizes the authority of the [Somali federal government](36817379.md) and would no longer participate in [Somali federal institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Member_State_of_Somalia). It has declared that it will "act independently", or "exercise powers of an independent state", until there is a federal government with a constitution agreed upon in a referendum in which Puntland participates.
