**Jizan** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): جيزان, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Jīzān*) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_and_towns_in_Saudi_Arabia) and the capital of [Jazan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazan_Province), which lies in the southwest corner of [Saudi Arabia](349303.md). Jizan is situated on the coast of the [Red Sea](26191.md) and serves a large agricultural heartland that has a population of 173,919 as of 2022 and over 1.4 million, in the whole Jizan region. The city has a regional airport as well as the Jizan seaport. The area is noted for its high-quality production of fruits such as [mangoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango), [figs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fig), [papayas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papaya), [plums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plum), and [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee).[^cite_note-3]

Jizan has one of the largest megaprojects market in the kingdom with significant infrastructure projects worth many billions of dollars. [Saudi Aramco](290527.md) is building the 400,000-[bpd](594043.md) **Jazan Refinery** with associated terminal facilities on the [Red Sea](26191.md) near the city, scheduled for completion in late 2018.[^cite_note-4]

## Economy

In the early 20th century, Jizan was a major site for [pearl fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl_hunting). When World War I began, trade declined, due to ships moving to [Al Hudaydah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Hudaydah).[^cite_note-Prothero75-5]

Ambitious projects in transport, renewable energy, tourism, and agricultural development are taking place in the Jizan region, particularly in [Jazan Economic City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazan_Economic_City), including a US\$3 billion [aluminum smelter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminum_smelter).[^cite_note-6]

### Jazan Refinery

Jazan Refinery is a megaproject under construction and owned by [Saudi Aramco](290527.md). The refinery will process heavy and medium crude oil to create [liquefied petroleum gas](361384.md), sulfur, asphalt, benzene, and paraxylene. It is expected to produce 400,000 barrels per day.[^cite_note-7]

In July 2026, it was attacked by Yemen’s [Houthis](17240332.md). Several missiles and drones struck the plant resulting in plumes of black smoke and flames rising from the site.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] On July 27, it was shut down.[^cite_note-10]

## Transport

[Jazan Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazan_Port?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Jizan Regional Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jizan_Regional_Airport) serve the city of Jizan.

## Ethnography

The inhabitants of Jizan are [Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab) who mainly follow [Sunni Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam) with a small [Zaydi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaydism) minority.[^cite_note-11]

## Climate

Jizan has a hot [desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen-Geiger_climate_classification_system): *BWh*) with a rainfall below 200 mm per year and an average annual temperature above 30 °C (86 °F). The weather is very hot all year round and its average annual temperature is one of the hottest in the world, as daily lows average over 20 °C (68 °F) and highs over 30 °C (86 °F) even in the mildest month of the year, while unlike a lot of tropical cities the summer is even noticeably warmer. It is very humid but parched in some months, making the weather volatile. Sandstorms are quite common in areas further from the sea.

The highest recorded temperature was 46.3 °C (115.3 °F) on 15 September 2006, while the lowest was 11.8 °C (53.2 °F) on 7 February 1993.[^cite_note-Jeddah_Regional_Climate_Center-12]
