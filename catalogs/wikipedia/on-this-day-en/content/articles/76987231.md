The **2026 UEFA Europa League final** was the final match of the [2025–26 UEFA Europa League](78224540.md), the 55th season of Europe's secondary club [football](10568.md) tournament organised by [UEFA](32332.md), and the 17th season since it was renamed from the UEFA Cup to the [UEFA Europa League](232175.md). It was played at [Beşiktaş Stadium](40309652.md) in [Istanbul](3391396.md), Turkey,[^cite_note-appointment-5] on 20 May 2026 between German club [SC Freiburg](1438835.md) and English club [Aston Villa](42173.md).

Aston Villa won the match 3–0 for their first UEFA Europa League title, their second European title after winning the [1982 European Cup final](9979118.md) and their first overall title since winning the [1996 Football League Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996_Football_League_Cup_final).[^cite_note-summary-6] As winners, they earned the right to play against the winners of the [2025–26 UEFA Champions League](74127997.md), [Paris Saint-Germain](357488.md), in the [2026 UEFA Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_UEFA_Super_Cup). Since Aston Villa had already qualified to enter the league phase of the [2026–27 UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_UEFA_Champions_League) through their league performance, the league phase spot reserved for the Europa League title holders was vacated and the access list was rebalanced.

## Background

Freiburg reached their first ever European final. They were aiming for a first major title. Freiburg's previous best European run was reaching the Europa League round of 16 twice, in [2022–23](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_UEFA_Europa_League#Round_of_16) and [2023–24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_UEFA_Europa_League#Round_of_16).

Aston Villa reached their first UEFA Cup/Europa League final and a third European final, having won the [1982 European Cup final](9979118.md) and [1982 European Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_European_Super_Cup). They were seeking a first major trophy since winning the [League Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) in [1996](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995%E2%80%9396_Football_League_Cup), thus discounting them being one of the three winners of the [2001 UEFA Intertoto Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_UEFA_Intertoto_Cup). This was their best [European run](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Villa_F.C._in_international_football) across three successive seasons after a 13-year absence, having lost the semi-finals of the [2023–24 UEFA Europa Conference League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_UEFA_Europa_Conference_League) to eventual winners [Olympiacos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympiacos_F.C.) before elimination in the [2024–25 UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UEFA_Champions_League) quarter-finals to another eventual winner in [Paris Saint-Germain](357488.md). Their previous furthest run in the UEFA Cup/Europa League was the quarter-finals in [1977–78](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977%E2%80%9378_UEFA_Cup) and [1997–98](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997%E2%80%9398_UEFA_Cup). Villa head coach [Unai Emery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unai_Emery) was looking for a record-extending [fifth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UEFA_Cup_and_Europa_League%E2%80%93winning_managers) UEFA Cup/Europa League title and the first with a non-Spanish side, having won three with [Sevilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sevilla_FC) ([2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_UEFA_Europa_League_final), [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_UEFA_Europa_League_final), [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_UEFA_Europa_League_final)) and one with [Villarreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarreal_CF) ([2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_UEFA_Europa_League_final)) sandwiching his defeat with [Arsenal](2174.md) in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_UEFA_Europa_League_final). Their striker [Tammy Abraham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tammy_Abraham) was aiming to become the 11th player to have [won all three major European trophies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_club_competition_records_and_statistics#List_of_players_to_have_won_the_three_main_European_club_competitions) and the first to have achieved that with three different clubs, having won the [2020–21 UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_UEFA_Champions_League) with [Chelsea](7473.md) and the [2021–22 UEFA Europa Conference League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_UEFA_Europa_Conference_League) with [Roma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AS_Roma).

## Venue



[Beşiktaş Stadium](40309652.md) in [Istanbul](3391396.md) hosted the final.

### Host selection

On 17 May 2023, UEFA opened the bidding process for the final, which was held in parallel with that of the [2027 final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_UEFA_Europa_League_final). Interested bidders could bid for either one or both of the finals. Additionally, bidding associations could only be appointed one UEFA final in a given year. The proposed venues had to include natural grass and be ranked as a [UEFA category four stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_stadium_categories), with a gross capacity of 40,000 to 60,000 preferred. The bidding timeline was as follows:[^cite_note-invitation_to_bid-7]

- 17 May 2023: Applications formally invited

- 17 July 2023: Closing date for registering intention to bid

- 26 July 2023: Bid requirements made available to bidders

- 15 November 2023: Submission of preliminary bid dossier

- 21 February 2024: Submission of final bid dossier

- 22 May 2024: Appointment of host

UEFA announced on 18 July 2023 that four associations had expressed interest in hosting the 2026 and 2027 finals during the first bidding process.[^cite_note-8]

## Match

### Summary

In the 41st minute, Aston Villa went in front when [Youri Tielemans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youri_Tielemans) scored with a right foot volley low to the left corner of the net after a short corner routine from the left ended with the ball being crossed into him. It was 2–0, three minutes into added on time in the first half when [Emiliano Buendía](44612006.md) curled a shot into the top left corner of the net from 18 yards out on the right.[^cite_note-10] [Morgan Rogers](59876948.md) got a third goal for Aston Villa in the 58th minute when he diverted a low cross from Emiliano Buendía on the left in at the near post past Freiburg goalkeeper [Noah Atubolu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Atubolu). Aston Villa went on to win the game 3–0 and win their first trophy in 30 years.[^cite_note-11]
