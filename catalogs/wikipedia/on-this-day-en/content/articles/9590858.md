# Short-term rental

**Short-term rentals** (**STR**) are furnished [lodging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodging) rented for short periods of time.[^cite_note-sas-1] They can be used as alternatives to [hotels](14276.md) for [vacation rentals](1822425.md) and housing during relocation.[^cite_note-2] They are usually reserved via [online marketplaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_marketplace).

## Regulations

Regulations have been imposed on short-term rental platforms after proponents argued that short-term rental has reduced [affordable housing](3531662.md) and contributed to the increase of long-term rental prices due to changes in housing [supply and demand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supply_and_demand); short-term rentals can be more lucrative than long-term rentals for landlords and also attract investors who bid up housing prices.[^cite_note-3] Supporters of short-term rentals argue that the impact on housing prices has been minimal and that the way to increase [affordable housing](3531662.md) is to increase the [housing supply](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_supply).[^cite_note-conflict-4][^cite_note-5] Examples include Lisbon, where a moratorium on short-term rentals did not reduce housing costs; it only increased hotel prices.[^cite_note-failed-6]

In rare cases, landlords have been accused of illegally [evicting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evict) tenants to convert properties into short-term rentals to obtain higher rents.[^cite_note-7]

The hotel industry has lobbied for additional regulations on short-term rentals, which compete with hotels.[^cite_note-8] Short-term rental platforms also use lobbying to promote their interests.[^cite_note-9]

In addition to government-imposed restrictions, many [homeowner associations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeowner_association) also limit short-term rentals.[^cite_note-10] Neighborhood community groups have voiced concern that short-term residents can cause issues with congestion, noise, and safety.[^cite_note-conflict-4]

Regulation of short-term rentals can include requirements for hosts to have business licenses, payment of [hotel taxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_tax), limitations on the number of days that a space is rented, requirements for hosts to occupy the space, and compliance with building, city and zoning standards.

### Regulations by jurisdiction

#### Europe

- [Amsterdam](844.md): Hosts can rent their properties for up to 30 nights per year to a group of no more than four at a time.

  [^cite_note-11]

  &#x20;Short-term rentals are banned in certain areas.

  [^cite_note-12]

- [Barcelona](4443.md): Licenses and higher property taxes are required.

  [^cite_note-13]

  [^cite_note-14]

  [^cite_note-15]

  &#x20;All short-term rentals will be banned in November 2028.

  [^cite_note-16]

- [Berlin](3354.md): Licenses are required. Hosts must live in most of the property.

  [^cite_note-17]

- [Budapest](36787.md): Licenses and taxation are required; short-term rentals are banned in certain areas.

  [^cite_note-18]

- [Ireland](147575.md): Licenses are required. Short-term rentals are restricted to a maximum of 90 days per year for primary residences.

  [^cite_note-ODD23-19]

  [^cite_note-20]

  [^cite_note-21]

- [Lisbon](18091.md): Licenses are required. Short-term rentals are limited to 10% of the housing stock.

  [^cite_note-failed-6]

- [London](17867.md): Short-term rentals are limited to 90 days per year.

  [^cite_note-22]

  [^cite_note-23]

- [Lucerne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucerne): Short-term rentals are limited to 90 days per year.

  [^cite_note-24]

  [^cite_note-25]

- [Madrid](41188263.md): Listings without private entrances are banned.

  [^cite_note-26]

- [Palma de Mallorca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palma_de_Mallorca): Short-term rentals are banned.

  [^cite_note-27]

- [Paris](22989.md): Hosts can rent their homes for no more than 120 days per year.

  [^cite_note-28]

  &#x20;Licenses are required.

  [^cite_note-29]

- [Rome](25458.md): Short-term rental sites are required to withhold a 21% rental income tax.

  [^cite_note-30]

- [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice): Hosts must collect and remit tourist taxes.

  [^cite_note-31]

  [^cite_note-32]

- [Vienna](55866.md): Short-term rentals are banned in specific "residential zones" within the city, with the exemption of apartments occupied by the host.

  [^cite_note-33]

#### United States

- [Boston, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts): Short-term rentals are only allowed in owner-occupied condominiums, single-family, two-family, and three-family buildings. Registration is required.

  [^cite_note-34]

- [Chicago, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago,_Illinois): Licenses are required. Single-night stays are prohibited.

  [^cite_note-35]

- [Jersey City, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jersey_City,_New_Jersey): Hosts are only allowed to rent for 60 days per year.

  [^cite_note-36]

  [^cite_note-37]

- [Las Vegas, Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas,_Nevada): Owners must occupy the property; must be away from casinos, licenses are required, limited to 1% of housing stock.

  [^cite_note-38]

- [Los Angeles, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles,_California): Hosts must register with the city planning department and pay an \$89 fee and cannot home-share for more than 120 days in a calendar year.

  [^cite_note-39]

- [Miami, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami,_Florida): Short-term rentals are banned in most neighborhoods, in part due to lobbying efforts of the hotel industry.

  [^cite_note-40]

  [^cite_note-41]

- [New York City, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City,_New_York): Licenses are required. Rentals under 30 days are prohibited unless the host is present on the property.

  [^cite_note-42]

- [Phoenix, Arizona](49121.md): Most regulations are not allowed since municipalities are prohibited from interfering in property rights.

  [^cite_note-43]

- [Portland, Oregon](23503.md): The number of bedrooms in a single unit that may be listed is limited.

  [^cite_note-44]

- [San Diego, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego,_California): Units for short-term rental are limited to 1% of the housing stock and licenses are required.

  [^cite_note-45]

- [San Francisco, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco,_California): Registration by hosts is required.

  [^cite_note-46]

- [Santa Cruz, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Cruz,_California): Local [Board of supervisors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_supervisors) discussed parking restrictions in the areas where there are many short-term rentals, in order to discourage people from staying there, and prioritize the local residents' ability to park.

  [^cite_note-47]

- [Santa Monica, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica,_California): Licenses are required. Hosts are prohibited from listing multiple properties.

  [^cite_note-48]

- [Seattle, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle,_Washington): Licenses are required. Hosts cannot rent more than two units.

  [^cite_note-49]

- [Washington, D.C.](108956.md): Licenses are required. If the host does not live on the property, rentals are limited to 90 days each year.

  [^cite_note-50]

- [Weehawken, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weehawken,_New_Jersey): Short-term rentals under 30 days are banned.

  [^cite_note-51]

- [West New York, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_New_York,_New_Jersey): Short-term rentals under 30 days are banned.

  [^cite_note-52]

#### Canada

- [Montreal](7954681.md): Short-term rentals must be in a host's primary residence. Only allowed between June 10 and September 10. Prohibited in certain districts.

  [^cite_note-53]

- [Toronto](64646.md): Licenses are required. Short-term rentals must be in a host's primary residence. Taxes are required.

  [^cite_note-54]

- [Vancouver](32706.md): Licenses are required; cost C\$1,108/year. Short-term rentals must be in a host's primary residence.

  [^cite_note-55]

#### Asia

- [Japan](15573.md): Licenses are required; a home can be rented for a maximum of 180 days per year.

  [^cite_note-56]

- [Singapore](27318.md): Short-term home rentals of less than three months for private properties or less than six months for [Housing and Development Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_and_Development_Board) flats are illegal.

  [^cite_note-57]

- [Taiwan](25734.md): Short-term rentals require approval, a hotel business license, and must pass safety inspections and provide public accident insurance; many short-term rentals are illegal.

  [^cite_note-58]

#### South America

- [Brazil](3383.md): Under the Brazilian Tenancy Law, a residential property may be rented for temporary purposes for a period not exceeding 90 days, whether or not it is furnished.

  [^cite_note-59]

  &#x20;In May 2026, the [Superior Court of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superior_Court_of_Justice_\(Brazil\)) ruled that the use of units in residential condominiums for short stays offered through digital platforms requires a change in the designated use of the property approved by at least two-thirds of the condominium owners.

  [^cite_note-60]

  &#x20;Short- and medium-term furnished rentals are also offered by Brazilian services such as [Shortstay](https://www.shortstay.com.br/en), which provides furnished apartments with flexible rental periods and digital contracts.

  [^cite_note-61]
