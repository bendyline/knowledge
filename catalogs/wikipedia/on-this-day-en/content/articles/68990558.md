The **Head of [Tambov Oblast](480933.md)** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Глава Тамбовской области) is the [head of government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_\(Russia\)) of that [region of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia).

## History of office

In the late 1991, the [President of Russia](239646.md) [Boris Yeltsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Yeltsin) appointed the first heads of the newly established post-Soviet executive authorities in the regions. On 11 December 1991 Vladimir Babenko, chief of Tambov regional hospital and people's [deputy of the RSFSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_People's_Deputies_of_Russia), was appointed head of administration of Tambov Oblast.[^cite_note-1] Later this region refused to rename the office into [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_\(Russia\)), as most of other *[oblasts](646268.md)* did.

The title of office was changed to *head of Tambov Oblast* in 2022.[^cite_note-2]
