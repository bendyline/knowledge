**Al-Rayyan** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الريان) located in the similarly named [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Qatar) of [Al Rayyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Rayyan), is the second-largest city in [Qatar](8238258.md).[^cite_note-2015census-3] The city and its suburbs comprise the largest population center in Qatar outside of [Doha proper](26214389.md), which is immediately to the east of Al Rayyan.[^cite_note-4] Consisting of all of the districts in the municipality's easternmost section, its western boundary is roughly where the [Al Majd Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Majd_Road) runs through the municipality.[^cite_note-mme1-5] The city is a part of the [Doha Metropolitan Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doha_Metropolitan_Area).[^cite_note-6] It was one of the venues for the [2022 FIFA World Cup](17742072.md).[^cite_note-alexia-7]

## Etymology

The city's name derives from the [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) word *ray*, which translates to "irrigation". It was given this name due to its low elevation, allowing it to act as a [flood plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flood_plain) during the rainy season and provide a prolonged supply of water to the numerous wild plants and crops that grew in the area.[^cite_note-gis-8]

## History



An aerial photograph of Al Rayyan taken by the Royal Air Force during a reconnaissance on 9 May 1934.

Before the massive expansion of Al Rayyan City, the two main areas in Al Rayyan were *Old Al Rayyan* and *New Al Rayyan*. Old Al Rayyan consisted of many traditional villages, while New Al Rayyan contained many large villas occupied by members of Qatar's [ruling family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Thani).[^cite_note-9] As Al Rayyan City grew in the later 20th century, all of the municipality's eastern settlements were incorporated as districts in the city.[^cite_note-mme1-5]

Al Rayyan joined the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities in 2022.[^cite_note-UNESCO_UIL-GNLC-10]

### Central Municipal Council

When free elections of the [Central Municipal Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Municipal_Council) first took place in Qatar during 1999,[^cite_note-11] Al Rayyan City was divided into two constituencies: no. 18, which had New Al Rayyan as its seat, and no. 19, which had Old Al Rayyan as its seat.[^cite_note-firstelec-12] These two constituencies would remain independent of each other for the next three consecutive elections until the [fifth municipal elections in 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatari_municipal_elections,_2015), when they were merged under constituency no. 14, with New Al Rayyan as the seat. Also included in its constituency is [Al Luqta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Luqta), [Lebday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebday), and [Al Shagub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Shagub).[^cite_note-moi2015-13]

In the inaugural municipal elections in 1999, voter turnout was 78.3% in constituency no. 18 where Mohammed Hamoud Al Shafi was elected, receiving 37.9%, or 256 votes. In constituency no. 19, Mohammed Saleh Al-Marri won, receiving 66%, or 264 votes. Voter turnout was low, at 37.7%[^cite_note-firstelec-12] At the next elections, held in 2002, Al Shafi retained his seat in constituency no. 19 while Faraj Saeed Al Aweer was elected in no. 18.[^cite_note-14] In the next election in [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatari_municipal_elections,_2007), Al Shafi once again retained his post in constituency no. 19 whereas Hamad Hamad Al Haoul won the elections in no. 18.[^cite_note-15] The [2011 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatari_municipal_elections,_2011) saw both Al Shafi and Haoul retain their seats in their respective constituencies.[^cite_note-16] For the 2015 elections, after the two constituencies were merged into one, long-time representative of constituency no. 19 Mohammed Hamoud Al Shafi emerged as the election winner.[^cite_note-moi2015-13]

## Landmarks

### City proper

[Khalifa International Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalifa_International_Stadium), one of the first large-scale stadiums in Qatar, was built in Al Rayyan City in 1976. It was later incorporated in the [Aspire Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspire_Zone).[^cite_note-17] The Aspire Zone is located in the [Al Waab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Waab) district and accommodates [Aspire Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspire_Academy). Also found within the Al Waab area are [Villaggio Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villaggio_Mall) and Doha Zoo.[^cite_note-mme2-18]

[Education City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_City), [Qatar Science & Technology Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_Science_&_Technology_Park) and other [Qatar Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_Foundation) facilities are located in the [Al Gharrafa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Gharrafa), [Gharrafat Al Rayyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gharrafat_Al_Rayyan) and [Al Shagub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Shagub) districts of Al Rayyan.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-mme2-18]

### Old Al Rayyan

The Municipal Headquarters is based out of the Old Al Rayyan district, as is the Al Rayyan Security Department and the Al Rayyan Pediatric Emergency Center.[^cite_note-gis-8] On 4 October, 1982, the Al Rayyan Public Library was officially inaugurated in Old Al Rayyan by the Minister of Education, [Mohammed bin Hamad bin Abdullah Al Thani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_bin_Hamad_bin_Abdullah_Al_Thani).[^cite_note-20]

### New Al Rayyan



Qatar Equestrian Federation headquarters in New Al Rayyan.

In the New Al Rayyan district, the Qatar Equestrian Federation (QEF) maintains its stables and outdoor arena.[^cite_note-21] Founded in 1975 and boasting a spectator capacity of 1,500, the QEF arena is considered to be the country's main horse racing venue.[^cite_note-22] The New Al Rayyan Park was opened in 2004 in a residential zone on Al Atouriya Street. Covering an area of 8,827 m<sup>2</sup>, facilities in the park include a children's play area and a cafeteria while plants featured include numerous [date palms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Date_palm).[^cite_note-23]

## Sports

[Education City Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_City_Stadium), a [2022 FIFA World Cup](17742072.md) venue, has been built in the city. It has a seating capacity of 40,000 spectators and has built in solar panels on its roof.[^cite_note-alexia-7][^cite_note-24] It hosted 8 games of the 2022 FIFA World Cup[^cite_note-25] and was opened on 15 June 2020.[^cite_note-26]

[Ahmad bin Ali Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_bin_Ali_Stadium) and [Khalifa International Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalifa_International_Stadium) were rebuilt in the city to host matches for the [2022 FIFA World Cup](17742072.md). Their capacity during the tournament were 45,032 and 45,857 spectators. Ahmad bin Ali Stadium hosted 7 games of the 2022 FIFA World Cup and Khalifa International Stadium hosted 8 games.

In the city there is also [Jassim bin Hamad Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jassim_bin_Hamad_Stadium) with the 15,000 capacity. All four stadiums were used for the [2023 AFC Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_AFC_Asian_Cup).

The city will host matches for the [2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIBA_Basketball_World_Cup).

## Transport

The underground [Al Shaqab station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Shaqab_station) currently serves the [Green Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Line_\(Doha_Metro\)) of the [Doha Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doha_Metro).[^cite_note-27] It is located on Huwar Street in Old Al Rayyan, near its border with [Al Shagub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Shagub).[^cite_note-journeymap-28] The station was opened to the public on 10 December, 2019 along with the other [Green Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Line_\(Doha_Metro\)) stations.[^cite_note-29] The station currently has no metrolinks.[^cite_note-30] Facilities on the premises include restrooms and a prayer room.[^cite_note-journeymap-28]

Another station which serves Al Rayyan and is also a part of the Green Line is the [Al Rayyan Al Qadeem station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Rayyan_Al_Qadeem_station). It is situated in the neighboring [Lebday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebday) district near the border with Old Al Rayyan.[^cite_note-journeymap-28]
