## Transport authorities



Catalan transport authorities map.

Transport authorities are inter-administrative voluntary corporations responsible for coordinating the public transportation system. There are currently four transport authorities in Catalonia:

- [Autoritat del Transport Metropolità](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoritat_del_Transport_Metropolit%C3%A0) (ATM) in [Àmbit metropolità de Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%80mbit_metropolit%C3%A0_de_Barcelona) ([Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona_\(province\)) and part of [Tarragona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarragona_\(province\)) and [Girona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girona_\(province\))).

- [Autoritat Territorial de la Mobilitat del Camp de Tarragona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoritat_Territorial_de_la_Mobilitat_del_Camp_de_Tarragona) (ATM Camp de Tarragona) in [Camp de Tarragona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_de_Tarragona).

- [Autoritat Territorial de la Mobilitat de l'Àrea de Lleida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoritat_Territorial_de_la_Mobilitat_de_l'%C3%80rea_de_Lleida) (ATM Àrea de Lleida) in some parts of [Lleida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponent).

- [Autoritat Territorial de la Mobilitat de l'Àrea de Girona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoritat_Territorial_de_la_Mobilitat_de_l'%C3%80rea_de_Girona) (ATM Àrea de Girona) ) in some parts of [Comarques Gironines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comarques_Gironines).

- In project:&#x20;

  - [Autoritat Territorial de la Mobilitat de les Comarques Centrals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoritat_Territorial_de_la_Mobilitat_de_les_Comarques_Centrals) (ATM Comarques Centrals) in [Comarques Centrals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comarques_Centrals).

## Rodalies de Tarragona

[Renfe Operadora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renfe_Operadora) [Tarragona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Tarragona)'s lines:

- S3 [Tarragona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarragona_railway_station) - [Reus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reus) *(service suspended due to Covid pandemic and not reactivated yet as of May 2023. The route is covered by Regional Exprés' lines R14 and R15)*

- S3 [Salou- PortAventura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salou_railway_station) - [Tarragona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarragona_railway_station) - L'Arboç *(service suspended due to Covid pandemic and not reactivated yet as of may 2023. The route is covered by Barcelona's R4 from L'Arboç to Sant Vicenç de Calders and by Regional Exprés' R17 from there to Salou)*

The [Generalitat de Catalunya](294461.md) is building a tramway system connecting several cities of the [Camp de Tarragona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_de_Tarragona), those being [Reus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reus), [Tarragona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarragona), [Vila-seca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vila-seca), [Cambrils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambrils) and [Salou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salou). This service, the *Tramvia del Camp de Tarragona* (also called *TramCamp*) will comprise around 46km of tracks and 47 stations, with a first part of 14km (between Cambrils, Salou and Vila-seca) being finished in 2026 and the Vila-seca - Tarragona and Vila-seca - Reus sections finishing in no longer than 10 years from the initial announcement in 2023. The whole system will be built in [Standard-gauge railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard-gauge_railway) and operated by [Ferrocarrils de la Generalitat de Catalunya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrocarrils_de_la_Generalitat_de_Catalunya).

### Railway gauge



Most of Catalan's railway gauge is [broad gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broad_gauge), as in other parts of [Spain](26667.md), and new railways are built in [standard gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_gauge) to be able to connect with European railways; therefore, some stations exist where trains can change gauges. Currently, there are only two stations in [Catalonia](6822.md) with that capability:

- [Portbou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portbou_railway_station): [Talgo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talgo) system.

- [Roda de Barà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roda_de_Bar%C3%A0_railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1): [Talgo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talgo) and [Brava-CAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brava-CAF?action=edit\&redlink=1) systems.
