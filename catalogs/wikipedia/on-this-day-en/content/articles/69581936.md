**Karol Tadeusz Nawrock&#x69;*[^cite_note-1]*** (born 3 March 1983) is a Polish historian and politician who has served as the 7th [president of Poland](43581.md) since 2025. He served as the director of the [Institute of National Remembrance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_National_Remembrance) (IPN) from 2021 to 2025, and was the director of the [Museum of the Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_of_the_Second_World_War) in [Gdańsk](12099.md) from 2017 to 2021.

Born in Gdańsk, Nawrocki studied history at the [University of Gdańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gda%C5%84sk), earning a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 2013 with a dissertation on [anti-communist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-communist) activities in the former [Polish People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_People's_Republic). His academic work centres on themes such as [anti-communist resistance in Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-communist_resistance_in_Poland_\(1944%E2%80%931989\)), [organised crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organised_crime), and the [history of sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_sports), a subject related to his own background as an active youth athlete, particularly in [football](10568.md) and [boxing](4243.md). Nawrocki's early professional career was closely aligned with institutions dedicated to preserving and promoting Poland's historical memory. He joined the Institute of National Remembrance (IPN) in 2009 and gained recognition for reorienting Poland's historical institutions toward a patriotic and anti-communist narrative. In the [2025 Polish presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Polish_presidential_election), Nawrocki was chosen as the presidential candidate of the right-wing populist [Law and Justice](520309.md) (PiS) party, ran as a nonpartisan "citizens' candidate" and defeated the liberal [Rafał Trzaskowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafa%C5%82_Trzaskowski).

As president, Nawrocki has reflected a broader [anti-liberal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-liberal) realignment in Poland's political landscape, aligning with [populist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Populist) and [right-wing](56522.md) cultural shifts.[^cite_note-prawica-2] However, he holds a pro-welfare and [protectionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protectionist) economic stance described by some as [left-wing](18499.md).[^cite_note-3] He pledged to implement new policies that will combat "unfair competition" in the Polish economy.[^cite_note-money-4] Since his inauguration on 6 August 2025, Nawrocki has been following a confrontational course with the [15 October Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15_October_Coalition) and professed opposition to its leader, [Donald Tusk](648724.md). Nawrocki strives to strengthen power around the presidency, supporting [constitutional reform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_reform) into a [semi-presidential system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-presidential_system). His [chancellery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellery_of_the_President_of_the_Republic_of_Poland) is made up of PiS politicians and his IPN affiliates.

## Early life and education

Karol Nawrocki was born on 3 March 1983 in [Gdańsk](12099.md). His father, Ryszard (1949–2008), was a turner and a member of [Solidarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solidarity_\(Polish_trade_union\)). His mother, Elżbieta, was a bookbinder. He has a younger sister named Nina who is a pastry cook and works in a restaurant in Kraków. He completed primary school and the 4th High School in his home city.[^cite_note-\:1-5] After passing his final exams ([matura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matura)) in 2002, he graduated in 2003 from the Post-Secondary School of Business and Administration in Gdańsk with the title of Personnel Management Specialist.[^cite_note-\:1-5] In the same year, he began his university studies at Faculty of History at the [University of Gdańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gda%C5%84sk), which he completed in 2008 with a master's degree.[^cite_note-6]

At the same institute, he obtained a PhD in the humanities based on his dissertation titled: *Social Resistance to Communist Rule in the Elbląg Voivodeship, 1976–1989*.[^cite_note-ipn-pres-7][^cite_note-\:1-5] In 2023, he completed the International MBA in Strategy, Programme and Project Management postgraduate studies at the [Gdańsk University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gda%C5%84sk_University_of_Technology).[^cite_note-8] In his youth, Nawrocki developed a strong interest in physical fitness, spending time working out at the gym, and participating in [football](10568.md) and [boxing](4243.md).[^cite_note-9]

## Early career



Nawrocki as the director of the Museum of the Second World War during a meeting with [Polish diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_diaspora) in [New Zealand](4913064.md), February 2020

Nawrocki worked at the Institute of National Remembrance in the years 2009–2017, heading its Branch Public Education Office in Gdańsk from 2013 to 2017. He also served as the chairman of the [Siedlce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siedlce,_Gda%C5%84sk) District Council in Gdańsk between 2011 and 2017.[^cite_note-ipn-pres-7]

In 2017, Nawrocki was appointed the director of the [Museum of the Second World War in Gdańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_of_the_Second_World_War),[^cite_note-10] a job he held until 2021. He then returned to the Institute of National Remembrance, becoming its deputy president in June 2021.[^cite_note-11] In July 2021, he took office as the head of the Institute of National Remembrance after being elected by the [Sejm](28167.md) and approved by the [Senate of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Poland).[^cite_note-12] Nawrocki is the author or co-author of several books as well as numerous scientific and popular science papers on anti-communist opposition, organised crime in the [Polish People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_People's_Republic) and the history of sports.

Nawrocki used the [pen name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pen_name) "Tadeusz Batyr" to write a book about a gangster living in 1980s [Communist Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Poland).[^cite_note-Vernon-13] Nawrocki in 2018 went on [television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_in_Poland) as Tadeusz Batyr, wearing a hat and having the television broadcast blur his face, where he said that Nawrocki "inspired me" and highlighted how Nawrocki "was the first person to examine organised crime in communist Poland".[^cite_note-Vernon-13] Meanwhile, on social media, Nawrocki wrote that "Tadeusz Batyr contacted me for some guidance" and "thanked me for my help with an interesting book, which I recommend".[^cite_note-Vernon-13]

### Rise to candidacy

Nawrocki was in consideration among speculative candidates such as PiS parliamentary leader [Mariusz Błaszczak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariusz_B%C5%82aszczak), [MEP](258909.md) [Tobiasz Bocheński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobiasz_Boche%C5%84ski), [poseł](28167.md) [Zbigniew Bogucki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zbigniew_Bogucki), former [Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Education_\(Poland\)) [Przemysław Czarnek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Przemys%C5%82aw_Czarnek), MEP [Patryk Jaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patryk_Jaki), former [Prime Minister](40543.md) [Mateusz Morawiecki](48505487.md), poseł [Arkadiusz Mularczyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkadiusz_Mularczyk), poseł [Kacper Płażyński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacper_P%C5%82a%C5%BCy%C5%84ski), former [Deputy Minister](7425373.md) [Tomasz Szatkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomasz_Szatkowski) and MEP [Dominik Tarczyński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominik_Tarczy%C5%84ski),[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] among others. President of the [World Anti-Doping Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Anti-Doping_Agency), [Witold Bańka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witold_Ba%C5%84ka), was also considered Kaczyński's favoured candidate, but he refused to run in the election.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

Because of the centralised nature of the party, the choice for selecting who the party's candidate would be fell primarily to the party chairman, [Jarosław Kaczyński](637440.md), based on social research[^cite_note-18] and the balance of factional power within the party.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] In an August [Radio Maryja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Maryja) interview, Kaczyński stated that the selection of possible candidates was narrowing, and described that the PiS candidate must be a "young, tall, imposing, handsome" male "\[with] a family".[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] A United Surveys poll in September 2024 suggested 29% of PiS voters supported Błaszczak, 21% supported Morawiecki, 11% supported Czarnek, 7% supported Nawrocki, 6% supported Bocheński and 6% supported Tarczyński.[^cite_note-23] The possibility of a primary election for the party was rumored, but ultimately, never announced.[^cite_note-24]

By November 2024, media speculation regarding the candidates narrowed down to, most frequently, Bocheński, Czarnek and Nawrocki.[^cite_note-25] According to *[Newsweek Polska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsweek_Polska)*, Nawrocki was supported internally by party activists such as [Adam Bielan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Bielan), [Joachim Brudziński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joachim_Brudzi%C5%84ski), [Sławomir Cenckiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C5%82awomir_Cenckiewicz), [Marta Kaczyńska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marta_Kaczy%C5%84ska?action=edit\&redlink=1), and Mateusz Morawiecki (despite initially desiring to be the candidate himself).[^cite_note-newsweekkulisy-26] Ultimately, at 13.15 on 22 November, Nawrocki was informed that he had been selected as the party's presidential candidate in a meeting with Kaczyński and his opponent, Bocheński.[^cite_note-27]

Speculation began on whether PiS would replace Nawrocki when it was revealed that he had contact with a future criminal during his time as a boxer two decades prior, for which he was attacked by opposing politicians.[^cite_note-28] Polling showed that the vast plurality of people did not expect Nawrocki to be replaced.[^cite_note-29] Nawrocki continued being PiS' presidential candidate going into the first round.

The selection of Nawrocki without consultation with outgoing president [Andrzej Duda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrzej_Duda) (the previous PiS candidate) strained relations between him and Nawrocki,[^cite_note-30] although Duda eventually endorsed Nawrocki at his election convention in [Łódź](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%81%C3%B3d%C5%BA) on 26 April.[^cite_note-31]

### First round of the presidential election

Days before the first round, Nawrocki and Romanian presidential candidate [George Simion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Simion) campaigned together in the city of [Zabrze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zabrze)[^cite_note-32], with Nawrocki praising Simion as the "future president of Romania".[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35] Donald Tusk criticised cooperation between the two, stating that “Russia rejoices” at such political ties. Critics say this refers to nationalist [Călin Georgescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C4%83lin_Georgescu) whose victory in the [2024 Romanian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Romanian_presidential_election) got cancelled citing Russian interference.[^cite_note-36]

On 24 November 2024, at the [Sokół Gymnastics Society Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sok%C3%B3%C5%82_Gymnastics_Society_Building?action=edit\&redlink=1), during a civic congress organised by [Law and Justice](520309.md), Nawrocki was announced by [Andrzej Nowak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrzej_Nowak_\(historian\)) as an [independent](2172378.md) candidate supported by the party in the [2025 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Polish_presidential_election).[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39] Nawrocki's campaign's platform was described as "patriotic, pro-Christian, pro-[NATO](21133.md), pro-Western, and pro-Donald Trump".[^cite_note-\:0-40] On 1 May 2025, Nawrocki met with [Donald Trump](4848272.md) in the [Oval Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oval_Office).[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42] He finished second in the first round held on 18 May 2025 with 29.54% of the vote, and faced [Civic Platform](23998.md) candidate [Rafał Trzaskowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafa%C5%82_Trzaskowski) in a runoff on 1 June.[^cite_note-43]

During the course of the campaign, public criticism emerged over Nawrocki's acquisition of a second apartment from an elderly man in pre-trial detention. As Nawrocki had declared to own just one apartment during a debate, [Onet.pl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onet.pl) publicised information about him owning a second one. The candidate proceeded to declare that he had acquired the second apartment from the elderly man for pledging lifelong care in exchange; it was revealed the man was placed in a state care facility without Nawrocki's involvement.[^cite_note-44] Amid accusations of exploitation, Nawrocki defended the deal's legality and donated the property to charity, after which some journalists said the man regained access to the property. He also stated he had regularly tried to visit the elderly man, but without success.[^cite_note-45]

### Second round of the presidential election

On 22 May 2025, Nawrocki met with [Sławomir Mentzen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C5%82awomir_Mentzen), who placed third in the first round of the election. They reportedly discussed possible concessions that could lead to Mentzen endorsing Nawrocki.[^cite_note-\:4-46] Mentzen presented a set of eight points[^cite_note-47] for a candidate to sign onto,[^cite_note-48] which were signed by Nawrocki.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51] Former candidates [Marek Jakubiak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marek_Jakubiak), [Marek Woch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marek_Woch), [Grzegorz Braun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grzegorz_Braun) and [Artur Bartoszewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artur_Bartoszewicz) endorsed Nawrocki in the [runoff](25932.md).[^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53][^cite_note-braunpoparcie-54][^cite_note-55] On 1 June 2025, Nawrocki won the runoff election with 50.9% of votes, beating Trzaskowski, who received 49.1% of votes.[^cite_note-56][^cite_note-57]

Throughout the second round, Nawrocki encountered a set of new incidents involving his personal life, beginning with the revelation that he had previously participated in a 70 vs 70 [football hooligans' fight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_hooliganism_in_Poland) ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *ustawka*) between fans of [Lechia Gdańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lechia_Gda%C5%84sk) and [Lech Poznań](677622.md).[^cite_note-58] Facing criticism, he said that Prime Minister Tusk likewise participated in football hooliganism in his youth, and called the fights "noble battles".[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-Gądek-60] It also came to light that Nawrocki had [Chelsea](7473.md) and Lechia Gdańsk tattoos on his torso.[^cite_note-61] There were allegations of him having worked as a bodyguard for sex workers at the five-star [Grand Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Hotel,_Sopot) in [Sopot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sopot) in his youth[^cite_note-62] and using a [nicotine pouch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicotine_pouch) while on air during a presidential debate,[^cite_note-63] which raised concerns about a possible nicotine addiction.[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65]

Karol Nawrocki giving a speech at his inauguration on 6 August 2025

Nawrocki was inaugurated as president of Poland on 6 August 2025, succeeding [Andrzej Duda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrzej_Duda). On the same day, he participated in the ceremony of accepting the authority of the President of Poland on the [Polish Armed Forces](40153898.md).[^cite_note-66]

### Domestic policy

#### Relations with the Prime Minister and government

Nawrocki's presidency was marked by a confrontational stance against [Prime Minister](40543.md) [Donald Tusk](648724.md) and his [ruling coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Tusk_cabinet),[^cite_note-67] citing his large and recent [social mandate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandate_\(politics\)) after the recent presidential election.[^cite_note-68] Nawrocki views Tusk as the worst Prime Minister of the [Third Polish Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Poland_\(1989%E2%80%93present\)), but expresses willingness to work with him regardless.[^cite_note-69] Analysts consider the downfall of Tusk's coalition and early [parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Next_Polish_parliamentary_election) as a goal for Nawrocki,[^cite_note-70] with him also seen as competing with Tusk's government in matters of lawmaking and governance despite the president having a primarily representative role,[^cite_note-governance-71] announcing he is seeking a "new formula of cooperation" between the President and [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Poland).[^cite_note-72] Nawrocki held his first meeting with Tusk on 14 August,[^cite_note-73] and on 27 August convened the first [Cabinet Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_Council?action=edit\&redlink=1), oriented on finances, investments and agriculture.[^cite_note-74]

#### Partisan affiliations

Further information: [Opinion polling on the Karol Nawrocki presidency § Will Nawrocki be/is Nawrocki an independent president?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_on_the_Karol_Nawrocki_presidency#Will_Nawrocki_be/is_Nawrocki_an_independent_president?)

Karol Nawrocki was elected primarily with the support of the Law and Justice (PiS) party and, despite being officially independent, was considered the PiS candidate in the [2025 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Polish_presidential_election).[^cite_note-75] However, Nawrocki's affiliation as a "PiS president" is debated, with some suggesting he could be a more independent president, or could cooperate with the far-right [Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederation_Liberty_and_Independence) as well, despite its [libertarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_liberalism) economic policies conflicting with PiS.[^cite_note-76] Alongside seeking to empower his role in governance of the country[^cite_note-governance-71] and reform Poland into a [semi-presidential republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-presidential_republic),[^cite_note-Adamski-Konstytucja-77] Nawrocki is perceived as aspiring to lead the "right wing" of politics,[^cite_note-prawica-2] possibly threatening the position of established political leaders such as [Jarosław Kaczyński](637440.md) of the economically interventionist Law and Justice party.[^cite_note-78]

#### Constitutional reform

Further information: [Proposed Fourth Polish Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proposed_Fourth_Polish_Republic) and [Opinion polling on the Karol Nawrocki presidency § Constitutional reform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_on_the_Karol_Nawrocki_presidency#Constitutional_reform)

At his inaugural speech, Nawrocki announced his intention to create a new [Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Poland) by 2030,[^cite_note-79] announcing the creation of a Council for the Reform of the System of the State[^cite_note-80] ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *Rada ds. Naprawy Ustroju Państwa*).[^cite_note-81] Soon after, Paweł Szefernaker, Nawrocki's appointed cabinet chief, elaborated that Nawrocki aims to empower the presidency.[^cite_note-82] According to [RMF FM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMF_FM), the Council is to be chaired by presidential advisor [Dariusz Dudek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dariusz_Waldemar_Dudek?action=edit\&redlink=1), and seek to reform, among others, the form of government from the current [parliamentary-cabinet system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary-cabinet_system?action=edit\&redlink=1) to a [semi-presidential system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-presidential_system).[^cite_note-Adamski-Konstytucja-77] In November 2025, Nawrocki stated that if the [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Cabinet_of_Donald_Tusk) continued what he described as the "process of destabilisation of Poland" by [non-recognition of judicial authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_constitutional_crisis), he would work to initiate a constitutional referendum in the subsequent year.[^cite_note-83]

On 3 May 2026, Nawrocki created a new Council for a New Constitution[^cite_note-84] ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *Rada Nowej Konstytucji*).[^cite_note-85] Members of the council as of founding were: [Julia Przyłębska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Przy%C5%82%C4%99bska), [Ryszard Piotrowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryszard_J%C3%B3zef_Jan_Piotrowski?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Barbara Piwnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Piwnik), [Józef Zych](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%B3zef_Zych), [Marek Jurek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marek_Jurek), [Ryszard Legutko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryszard_Legutko), [Anna Łabno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_%C5%81abno?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Piotr Andrzejewski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piotr_Andrzejewski?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Zdzisław Krasnodębski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zdzis%C5%82aw_Krasnod%C4%99bski_\(sociologist\)) and [Jacek Majchrowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacek_Majchrowski).[^cite_note-86]

#### Legislative initiatives and vetoes

Further information: [Opinion polling on the Karol Nawrocki presidency § Legislative proposals and other initiatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_on_the_Karol_Nawrocki_presidency#Legislative_proposals_and_other_initiatives)



Nawrocki signing the 0% PIT for families bill on 8 August 2025

Part of Nawrocki's conflict strategy against the Tusk cabinet is to use his [privilege as president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_of_initiative_\(legislative\)) to introduce laws to parliament and to reshape coalition legislation by vetoing laws and re-submitting them with the disputed provisions removed.[^cite_note-governance-71] In the first weeks of Nawrocki's presidency, Nawrocki introduced legislation that would restore the old project of the [Central Communication Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Communication_Port),[^cite_note-87] abolish PIT for families with more than one child, increase the [income tax threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Income_tax_threshold) to an annual income of 140,000 PLN,[^cite_note-88] extend the prohibition of sale of Polish land to foreigners.[^cite_note-89] On 21 August, Nawrocki for the first time used the presidential [veto](74661.md) against an [omnibus bill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnibus_bill) regarding [wind turbine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_turbine) deregulation and the extension of energy [price freeze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_freeze) period, criticising the deregulation as pushed by a wind turbine [lobby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobbying) and unsafe, stating he would propose his own price freeze bill.[^cite_note-90]

On 3 November 2025, Nawrocki introduced a proposed bill titled "Retiring with Dignity", which would raise the minimum wage to 2,000 PLN (up from 1,878 PLN), and introduce the [indexation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indexation) of social pensions both as a percentage and by a fixed amount, with a guaranteed increase of at least 150 PLN for the lowest pensions.[^cite_note-91] Dual pension indexation was one of Tusk's 100 pledges in the 2023 election.[^cite_note-92]

#### Historical policy

During his presidency, Nawrocki has been opposed to [Ukrainian ultranationalist and Nazi collaborationist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far-right_politics_in_Ukraine) symbolism and activities. Nawrocki pursued the criminalisation of [Banderite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banderite) ideology in Poland, including the equalisation of Banderite symbols with [Nazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazism) and [communist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communism) symbolism.[^cite_note-93] On 31 August 2025, the 45th anniversary of the [August Agreements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gda%C5%84sk_Agreement) which created the [Solidarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solidarity_\(Polish_trade_union\)) trade union, Nawrocki criticized former union leader and president [Lech Wałęsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lech_Wa%C5%82%C4%99sa), stating that while Wałęsa cannot be forgotten, he must be correctly and truthfully remembered.[^cite_note-94]

Nawrocki advocates for pursuing [World War II reparations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II_reparations#Poland) from [Germany](11867.md), which Poland never received. On a World War II anniversary on 1 September 2025, Nawrocki "unequivocally" demanded Germany pay Poland war reparations worth over 6 trillion [PLN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_z%C5%82oty) (1.4 trillion [Euros](9472.md)),[^cite_note-95][^cite_note-96] continuing the course set by the [Law and Justice](520309.md) party.[^cite_note-97] During a visit to Germany in September 2025, Nawrocki attempted to discuss the topic of war reparations, but was rejected, with Germany stating that the matter of war reparations is "definitively regulated".[^cite_note-98] An alternative solution to paying war reparations proposed by Nawrocki was for Poland to receive financial aid for its military industry.[^cite_note-99]

### Foreign policy

Further information: [Opinion polling on the Karol Nawrocki presidency § Foreign policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_on_the_Karol_Nawrocki_presidency#Foreign_policy)



Nawrocki at a bilateral meeting with U.S. President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) on 3 September 2025



Meeting between Karol Nawrocki and Ukrainian President [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) on 19 December 2025

According to analysts, the dispute over whether the President or Prime Minister should represent Poland at international meetings is an axis of conflict between Tusk and Nawrocki, titled as a "war for the seat".[^cite_note-100] On 13 August, Nawrocki represented Poland at a [teleconference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teleconference) of Trump and European leaders regarding the upcoming [2025 Russia–United States summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Russia%E2%80%93United_States_summit) in [Alaska](624.md), a change from Tusk previously representing the country in international conferences.[^cite_note-101] However, the lack of any Polish representation at an [18 August White house meeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_2025_European-White_House_crisis_meeting) caused controversy, with some blaming Nawrocki for not representing the country at the meeting.[^cite_note-102]

During his presidency, Nawrocki attended many international meetings, and in September 2025, he began travelling abroad. In September alone, he visited: 3 September — [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) for a meeting with [Donald Trump](4848272.md), 5 September — a meeting with [Pope Leo XIV](54520372.md) and [Italian Prime Minister](273741.md) [Giorgia Meloni](29228976.md) and [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Italy) [Sergio Mattarella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Mattarella) in [Rome](25458.md);[^cite_note-103] 8 September — in [Vilnius](32597.md) with [President of Lithuania](273654.md) [Gitanas Nauseda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gitanas_Nauseda);[^cite_note-104] 9 September — in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki) with [President of Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Finland) [Alexander Stubb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Stubb);[^cite_note-105] 16 September — a meeting in [Berlin](3354.md) with [Chancellor of Germany](20890626.md) [Friedrich Merz](1967626.md) and [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Germany) [Frank-Walter Steinmeier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank-Walter_Steinmeier);[^cite_note-106] and with [President of France](24899.md) [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md) in [Paris](22989.md);[^cite_note-107] 21–24 September — a visit to the United States in [Doylestown, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doylestown,_Pennsylvania), to meet with the local [Polish diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_diaspora),[^cite_note-108] and [New York](645042.md) for the 80th session of the [United Nations General Assembly](31957.md).[^cite_note-109]

Soon after his inauguration, Nawrocki was invited to the [White House](33057.md) by [President of the United States](24113.md) [Donald Trump](4848272.md).[^cite_note-110] With Nawrocki bringing no [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](1161355.md) delegates to the meeting, foreign minister [Radosław Sikorski](3199016.md) met separately with United States [Secretary of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_of_State_\(United_States\)) [Marco Rubio](5502549.md).[^cite_note-111] On 3 September, Nawrocki arrived to the meeting, where Trump affirmed his intention to maintain the presence of American troops in Poland and invited Nawrocki to the upcoming [G20 summit](81302119.md) in [Florida](18933066.md).[^cite_note-112]

In 2025, Nawrocki cancelled a planned meeting with Hungarian Prime Minister [Viktor Orbán](750582.md). One of Nawrocki's Chancellery members, [Marcin Przydacz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcin_Przydacz), explained how the president disapproved of Orbán's relationship with Russian President [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) and the fact that the Hungarian leader had recently visited the Russian president. Przydacz added that President Nawrocki will seek to limit his diplomatic contacts with Orbán solely to [Visegrád Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visegr%C3%A1d_Group) meetings.[^cite_note-113][^cite_note-114]

#### Foreign policy stances toward the European Union

As president, Nawrocki opposes the [EU–Mercosur Association Agreement](61184322.md), promising to uphold the slogan "Polish farmer, Polish field, Polish bread on the Polish table" ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *Polski rolnik, polskie pole, polski chleb na polskim stole*).[^cite_note-115][^cite_note-116] Nawrocki opposes the implementation of the EU Digital Services Act as proposed by the Tusk government, with the members of his Chancellery calling the proposed ability of the President of the Office of Electronic Communications to block content "too far-reaching".[^cite_note-117]

#### Foreign policy stances toward Ukraine

Nawrocki has presided over a significant deterioration in bilateral [Poland-Ukraine relations](16367528.md).[^cite_note-118]

In August 2025, Nawrocki vetoed a bill that would have extended financial support for Ukrainian refugees of the [Russo-Ukrainian war](42085878.md) not employed in Poland as well as funding for [Starlink](45111627.md) systems to maintain Ukraine's internet connectivity.[^cite_note-119] Nawrocki was invited to meet with [president](20688479.md) [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) of [Ukraine](31750.md) in its capital city, [Kyiv](585629.md), but Nawrocki rejected the invitation, suggesting that Zelenskyy should come to Poland instead.[^cite_note-120] Nawrocki's response was supported by public opinion.[^cite_note-121]

Main article: [Polish Order of the White Eagle controversy](83538418.md)

In May 2026, Nawrocki announced that he was considering stripping Zelenskyy of the [Order of the White Eagle](719178.md), Poland's highest state honour, after Zelenskyy honored a special operations forces unit with the name "Heroes of the [Ukrainian Insurgent Army](1045217.md)", a [Nazi-collaborating organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_collaboration_with_Nazi_Germany) that [conducted a genocide against Poles in Volhynia](464698.md). Nawrocki further stated that Ukraine is not ready to "be part of the [European family](9317.md)" due to their mentality of "glorifying the bandits and murderers of the Ukrainian Insurgent Army." Zelesnkyy was previously conferred the honour in 2023 by Nawrocki's predecessor Andrzej Duda.[^cite_note-122] On 19 June, Nawrocki officially revoked Zelenskyy's state decoration.[^cite_note-123] On 20 June, Zelenskyy sent the order back to Nawrocki's chancellery via [Nova Poshta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nova_Poshta),[^cite_note-124] while the three former Ukrainian presidents, who were also the order's recipients - [Leonid Kuchma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Kuchma), [Viktor Yushchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Yushchenko) and [Petro Poroshenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petro_Poroshenko) - declared they would also hand their orders back on the grounds that the row was harming the attempts they had made to improve ties with Poland.[^cite_note-125]

## Political views

Further information: [Conservatism in Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatism_in_Poland)



Nawrocki greeting US President [Donald Trump](4848272.md), 1 May 2025

Nawrocki is considered a nonpartisan, but with a conservative outlook.[^cite_note-140] Nawrocki describes himself as a "representative of the broadly defined patriotic camp", and stresses that he has never belonged to a political party.[^cite_note-kot-141] He considers himself a "citizens' candidate" that will end the "Polish-Polish war".[^cite_note-142] He declared that he is ready to support "any Polish government that demands the exhumation of [Polish victims in Volhynia](464698.md)", and describes issues of history and social accountability as his "demarcation lines".[^cite_note-143] [Euronews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euronews) describes the political alignment of Nawrocki's campaign as "patriotic, pro-Christian, pro-[NATO](21133.md) and favourable to president [Donald Trump](4848272.md)".[^cite_note-\:0-40] The *[Financial Times](136566.md)* described Nawrocki as "anti-liberal, anti-German, anti-EU".[^cite_note-144] [Xinhua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinhua_News_Agency) described Nawrocki's values as "prioritising national sovereignty, [Catholic social teaching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_social_teaching), and skepticism toward the EU".[^cite_note-145]

### Social issues

Nawrocki is a [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic) with [culturally conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_conservatism) views; he accused [the ruling coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15_October_Coalition) and [LGBT groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_grooming_conspiracy_theory) of "sexualising children".[^cite_note-BBC-146] He stated that he strongly opposes "reinterpreting gender identity",[^cite_note-147] and would allow "no compromises",[^cite_note-148] demonstratively throwing a copy of the cover of *[Gender Queer: A Memoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_Queer:_A_Memoir)* into a [paper shredder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_shredder) in 2025.[^cite_note-angryLGBT-149] Nawrocki supports maintaining close ties between the [Catholic Church in Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church_in_Poland) and the [Polish government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Poland), the [broad criminalisation of abortion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abortion_in_Poland), and opposes the legalisation of [same-sex marriage](92656.md), [civil unions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_union) or extending legal rights to people in same-sex relationships, citing [Catholic sexual ethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_theology_of_sexuality).[^cite_note-BBC-146][^cite_note-150]

During the campaign, Nawrocki stated: "There is a male sex and a female sex. And if anyone defies that, they don't understand the present, the past, or the future", and "marriage is a union between a man and a woman".[^cite_note-legrand-151] Nawrocki also opposes euthanasia, and his position on abortion has been described as "even more conservative than that of PiS itself";[^cite_note-legrand-151] he stated that he would not allow the return of pre-2021 abortion laws in Poland, which allowed abortion in case of serious diseases, arguing: "I would not sign a law that would reinstate the agreement on abortion. As a future president, I cannot allow the abortion of children with [Down syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Down_syndrome)."[^cite_note-elentir-152]

He stresses his commitment to Polish patriotism, Christian values and national sovereignty, and has declared the need to defend traditional social values.[^cite_note-salon24-153] He strongly opposes the removal of crosses from state buildings.[^cite_note-154] He holds [anti-communist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-communism) views, and has criticised the Polish education system, claiming that it is controlled by the "post-communist party environment". In February 2024, he was listed as one of the persons wanted by the [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation) on criminal charges for the [removal of monuments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_monuments_and_memorials_removed_following_the_Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) commemorating the [Red Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Army) erected in the [Polish People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_People's_Republic) in the years 1944–1989.[^cite_note-155] He has described [cursed soldiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cursed_soldiers) as national heroes of Poland and praised PiS for implementing a National Remembrance Day in their honour.[^cite_note-salon24-153] He is an opponent of the [EU's migration policy](62251245.md) and wishes to terminate the EU's [migration pact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Pact_on_Migration_and_Asylum).[^cite_note-156]

### Economics

Nawrocki's program has been described as [economically interventionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economically_interventionist),[^cite_note-157] [economically nationalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economically_nationalist),[^cite_note-158] and [economically left-wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economically_left-wing).[^cite_note-159] He pledged to implement new policies that will combat "unfair competition" in the Polish economy.[^cite_note-money-4] Nawrocki describes himself as a strong supporter of armaments and social investment programmes. In a speech in which he accepted Law and Justice's endorsement for his candidacy, Nawrocki pledged to abolish all [overtime](341254.md) labor tax and to focus on large economic investments; he strongly supports the [Central Communication Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Communication_Port) project, and praised the [Central Industrial Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Industrial_Region_\(Poland\)) and [Stocznia Gdynia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stocznia_Gdynia) schemes developed in the interwar [Second Polish Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Polish_Republic).[^cite_note-onet_eko-160] Nawrocki expressed his fascination with other large investments such as the [Vistula Spit canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vistula_Spit_canal) and [Świnoujście LNG terminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9Awinouj%C5%9Bcie_LNG_terminal), and wants to pursue similar undertakings.[^cite_note-161]

Nawrocki calls for "a welfare state with zero VAT on food".[^cite_note-162] He promised to increase social spending, welfare benefits and pensions;[^cite_note-163] he also supports welfare programmes and opposes adopting the [Euro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro_currency) as Poland's currency.[^cite_note-164] He stresses the lack of transport in Poland and has pledged to develop rail infrastructure in underdeveloped regions of the country.[^cite_note-165] Nawrocki proposes to enact an additional tax on the owners of three or more apartments, with an exemption for families with children.[^cite_note-166] His other proposals include abolishing tax on savings and increasing the annual indexation of pensions.[^cite_note-Bloomberg-167] He also expressed [welfare chauvinist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welfare_chauvinism) views - believing that Poles are "treated worse in their own country than immigrants", Nawrocki argues that social benefits in Poland should be for Poles only, and pensions for Ukrainian immigrants should be eliminated; additionally, Polish citizens should have a priority in healthcare, school and kindergarten queues.[^cite_note-168][^cite_note-169] Nawrocki is also considered to align with "Trumpian tariffs and [protectionism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protectionism)".[^cite_note-170]

He objected to the [Tusk government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Cabinet_of_Donald_Tusk)'s cut in healthcare contributions for businesses and stated that he opposes any attempt to reduce [healthcare funding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_care_in_Poland).[^cite_note-171] Nawrocki also signed a list of 11 pledges, including promises to not raise the retirement age, and to uphold worker protections, defend minimum wages, retain the ban on Sunday business activity, to promote [economic patriotism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_patriotism), and to increase funding for the public health service and [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture_in_Poland). Based on these pledges, he was endorsed by Poland's [Solidarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solidarity_\(Polish_trade_union\)) trade union.[^cite_note-172] He supports additional taxes on "digital giants operating online and targeting Polish users" and the expansion of [social housing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_housing).[^cite_note-173]

He argues that Poland needs to achieve "full energy sovereignty". He supports [nuclear energy](22153.md), describing it as "the most secure and stable one". He also criticised the [European Green Deal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Green_Deal), stating that while he supports environmental protection, he opposes "climate madness at the expense of Polish homes, workers and entrepreneurs". Nawrocki also believes that Poland needs to ensure its food security and respect "the sovereignty of the Polish countryside"; he has described rural Poland as "the mainstay of Polish culture, traditions and social values".[^cite_note-onet_eko-160] He supports selective tax cuts, but is considered to not be [economically liberal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economically_liberal).[^cite_note-elentir-152] He advocated for reducing the [value-added tax](52177473.md) rate from 23% to 22% during the 2025 campaign, as well as an exemption from [personal income tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_income_tax) for families with two or more children. He also pledged to veto blanket tax increases.[^cite_note-Bloomberg-167][^cite_note-174]

### Foreign policy



Nawrocki with [DHS](58236.md) Secretary [Kristi Noem](27663933.md), May 2025

Karol Nawrocki has emphasised in his statements that the geopolitical interest of Poland lies in pushing back the [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation),[^cite_note-\:1-5] stating that "Russia is imperialist in its foundation whether it is [white terror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Terror_\(Russia\)), [red terror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_terror) or modern terror".[^cite_note-wm.pl-nawrocki-175] According to him, the foundation of Poland's security is a strong position within [NATO](21133.md) and a close alliance with the [United States](3434750.md).[^cite_note-\:1-5] He also supports regional alliances such as the [Bucharest Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest_Nine) and favours expanding this format to include [Sweden](5058739.md) and [Finland](10577.md).[^cite_note-\:1-5] He advocates increasing defense spending and expanding the army to 300,000 troops, but opposes conscription or mandatory military training for young men.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

Geopolitically, Nawrocki supports further [strengthening Polish ties to the United States](10858944.md) and [NATO](21133.md) while opposing [European integration](683704.md).[^cite_note-\:0-40] He opposes the [federalisation of the European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federalisation_of_the_European_Union) and stresses the need to maintain Polish national identity within the EU, adding that "Poland does not need a centralised state populated by EU citizens of Polish origin".[^cite_note-money-4] Nawrocki accused the [Donald Tusk's government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Cabinet_of_Donald_Tusk) of being submissive toward [Germany](11867.md), and advocated for the restoration of a balanced relationship between the two countries.[^cite_note-\:1-5] He demands [World War II reparations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II_reparations) from Germany for [material losses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_material_losses_during_World_War_II) and [war crimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_war_crimes_in_occupied_Poland_during_World_War_II) committed in [occupied Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Poland_\(1939%E2%80%931945\)).[^cite_note-\:1-5]

He supports ending the [Russo-Ukrainian war](42085878.md) by a [peace agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_negotiations_in_the_Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) but argues that the issue of territorial cessions should be decided by the European community as well as Ukraine itself.[^cite_note-wm.pl-nawrocki-175] Nawrocki opposed the deployment of Polish troops to Ukraine. He supported the normalisation of relations with Ukraine on a basis of partnership, as well as the recognition and dignified burial of Polish victims in [Volhynia](464698.md).[^cite_note-\:1-5] Nawrocki is opposed to Ukrainian membership in [NATO](21133.md) or the [European Union](9317.md) until Ukraine accepts responsibility for the genocide of Poles in Volhynia. He has previously denounced attempts to downplay the 1943–1945 [massacres of Poles in Volhynia and Eastern Galicia](464698.md) for the sake of improving [Polish-Ukrainian relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish-Ukrainian_relations).[^cite_note-176]

Nawrocki distanced himself from the former pro-Ukrainian policy of PiS and criticised Ukraine for acting against Polish interests;[^cite_note-177] to this end, Nawrocki calls for limiting military exports to Ukraine and distancing Poland from Ukraine in order to prioritize Polish interests over Ukrainian ones.[^cite_note-178] He stated that he would punish the glorification of Ukrainian nationalist leader [Stepan Bandera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stepan_Bandera).[^cite_note-179] He was accused of anti-Ukrainian sentiment,[^cite_note-180] and "echoing Kremlin talking points about Ukraine".[^cite_note-181] Responding to the accusations of speaking "Putin's language" on Ukraine, Nawrocki stated: "I speak the language of Polish people. Millions want to say that Zelensky mistreats us, but they're silenced with 'you're spreading Putin's propaganda'."[^cite_note-182]

He was criticised in [Israel](9282173.md) for downplaying the role of Poles in [the Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Holocaust).[^cite_note-183] Nawrocki said he would defend Poland "against all disgusting attacks" by Holocaust scholars.[^cite_note-184] Poland's foreign minister, [Radosław Sikorski](3199016.md), publicly defended Nawrocki in response to the criticism.[^cite_note-185] During the election campaign, Nawrocki defended the [2018 amendment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amendment_to_the_Act_on_the_Institute_of_National_Remembrance) that criminalized attributing responsibility for the Holocaust to the Polish nation, stating: "I am fighting for this truth. Poles are not antisemites. Many Poles lost their lives during World War II saving Jews." When asked about Israel, Nawrocki expressed his belief that "there is no state that is a chosen nation to live out its history".[^cite_note-186] He also promised to end the tradition of lighting [Hanukkah candles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanukkah_menorah) in the Presidential Palace,[^cite_note-187] stating that commitment to Christian values would exclude celebrating Jewish holidays.[^cite_note-legrand-151]

## Personal life

Karol Nawrocki is married to [Marta Nawrocka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marta_Nawrocka) (born in 1986), a graduate of the Faculty of Law and Administration at the [University of Gdańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gda%C5%84sk) and an employee of the National Revenue Administration.[^cite_note-\:2-188] She specialises in the control of the oil industry and combating illegal trade. Together, they have two children: a son, Antoni and a daughter, Katarzyna. They also raised Daniel (born in 2003), Marta Nawrocka's son from a previous relationship.[^cite_note-\:2-188] The couple married in 2010, and Karol Nawrocki adopted Daniel.[^cite_note-\:2-188] The pair signed their son, Antoni, out of "health education" classes introduced by the [Ministry of National Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Education_\(Poland\)) under [Barbara Nowacka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Nowacka).[^cite_note-189] After Nawrocki's election in 2025, he initially moved into the [Belweder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belweder), but in October of the same year moved to the [Presidential Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Palace,_Warsaw).[^cite_note-190]

Daniel Nawrocki studies at the Faculty of Law and Administration at the University of Gdańsk. He is involved in journalism, working for Gazeta Morska and previously for Dziennik Bałtycki. In 2023, he was appointed a member of the Youth Sports Council under the Minister of Sport. He was also a member of the Youth Council of the City of Gdańsk and now serves as a neighbourhood councillor. In 2024, he ran unsuccessfully for a seat on the [Gdańsk City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gda%C5%84sk_City_Council) as a candidate of the Law and Justice party.[^cite_note-191]

## Public image



Graphical summary of Nawrocki's approval ratings

### Public approval

Further information: [Opinion polling on the Karol Nawrocki presidency § Approval rating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_on_the_Karol_Nawrocki_presidency#Approval_rating)

During the first months of Nawrocki's presidency, he maintained a positive approval rating, being one of Poland's most popular politicians. His popularity was highest among Polish youth, with a United Surveys poll showing 73% approval among respondents aged 18–29, and lowest among those over 70, where approval stood at 42%.[^cite_note-192]

### Sports

Nawrocki's image is tied to sports, especially [football](10568.md) and [boxing](4243.md). During the early stages of [his 2025 presidential campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Polish_presidential_election#Nawrocki's_campaign), Nawrocki appeared in workout videos,[^cite_note-193] and compared himself to *[Rocky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_\(franchise\))*, with his campaign [merchandise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merchandise) portraying catchphrase "Now Rocky", a play on his surname.[^cite_note-194] Nawrocki's participation in a [football hooligans' fight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_hooliganism_in_Poland) ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *ustawka*) between fans of [Lechia Gdańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lechia_Gda%C5%84sk) and [Lech Poznań](677622.md) was controversial, attracting praise[^cite_note-195] and criticism.[^cite_note-196] Since his inauguration, Nawrocki maintained his athletic and [football-fan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football_culture) image, seen working out[^cite_note-197] or attending football matches in Poland and on foreign visits.[^cite_note-198] *[Polityka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polityka)* argues Nawrocki wants to be a "patron of football fans".[^cite_note-199]

### Public engagement



Karol Nawrocki at [Polish Armed Forces Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_Day_\(Poland\)) on 15 August 2025

Nawrocki's image as president has been compared to President of the United States [Donald Trump](4848272.md).[^cite_note-200] Nawrocki was described as a president close to the people, a "people's president" or [tribune of the people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribune_of_the_plebs) ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *trybun ludowy*). He was also applauded for actively representing Poland in [international visits](327283.md).[^cite_note-peoplespresident-201] Besides exercising in public, Nawrocki also appeared in public with various members of the public, including eating a [kebab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kebab) with youth from a remote village as part of a casual campaign promise[^cite_note-202] and speaking with Polish citizens who called out for him during a visit to the [United States](3434750.md).[^cite_note-203]

During his 2025 presidential campaign, Nawrocki portrayed himself as a nonpartisan "citizens' candidate" ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *kandydat obywatelski*),[^cite_note-204] and as an authentic "flesh and bone man" ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *człowiek z krwi i kości*).[^cite_note-205]

### Independence March appearance

Nawrocki appeared at the 2025 [Independence March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_March_\(Poland\)) on 11 November.[^cite_note-206]
