**Ituri Province** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Province de l'Ituri*; [Swahili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swahili_language): *Mkoa wa Ituri*) is one of the 26 [provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo](3984115.md) created in the [2015 repartitioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_the_DR_Congo#New_provinces). Ituri, [Bas-Uele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bas-Uele), [Haut-Uele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haut-Uele), and [Tshopo](33835756.md) provinces are the result of the subdividing of the former [Orientale province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientale_Province).[^cite_note-\:0-2] Ituri was formed from the [Ituri district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ituri_district), whose town of [Bunia](1925095.md) was elevated to capital city of the new province.[^cite_note-3]

## Geography



[Ituri Rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ituri_Rainforest)

Ituri is a region of high plateau (2,000–5,000 meters) that has a large tropical forest but also the landscape of savannah. The province has rare fauna, including the [okapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okapi), the national animal of the Congo. As for flora, an important species is [Mongongo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongongo), whose leaves are used by the [Mbuti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbuti) to build their homes.

Map of Ituri showing tree-cover loss years, with forest remaining since 2000 in green and loss years shaded yellow through purple.

A map of tree-cover loss in Ituri, 2001-2024, from the [Global Forest Change dataset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Forest_Change_dataset).

The [Ituri Rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ituri_Rainforest) is in this area, and is located northeast of the [Ituri River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ituri_River) and on the western side of [Lake Albert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Albert_\(Uganda\)). It has borders with [Uganda](31816.md) and [South Sudan](32350676.md).

### Legal status in dispute (1998–2006)

Prior to the adoption of the 2006 [Constitution of the Democratic Republic of the Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo), the legal status of Ituri was a topic of some dispute. From the beginning of the [Second Congo War](877492.md) in 1998, it was held by soldiers of the [Uganda People's Defense Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uganda_People's_Defense_Force) (UPDF) and the [Ugandan](31816.md)-backed Movement for Liberation faction of the [Rally for Congolese Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rally_for_Congolese_Democracy) (RCD-ML). In June 1999, the commander of UPDF forces in the DRC, Brig. Gen. [James Kazini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Kazini), ignored the protests of RCD-ML leaders and re-created the province of Kibali-Ituri out of the eastern section of the northeastern [Orientale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientale,_Congo) province.[^cite_note-4]

The province is almost always referred to simply as Ituri. The creation of the new province under the political rivalry contributed to the start of the current [Ituri conflict](1585788.md), which has caused thousands of deaths. Most official cartographers did not include the new province, and those referring to it as a "province" rather than a "region" were sometimes viewed as having a pro-Uganda bias. With the new constitution, Ituri's status as a province was finally settled.

### Creation as a province (2015)

In 2015, the [Orientale Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientale_Province) was divided into 4 new provinces, one of which was Ituri.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

### Ebola outbreak (2026–present)

Main article: [2026 Central Africa Ebola epidemic](83191582.md)

The earliest known case of [ebola](40817590.md) in Ituri in the 2026 epidemic was a 59-year old man. He became symptomatic on 24 April 2026 and died three days later on 27 April.[^cite_note-5] As of 15 May 2026, the epidemic had reached 3 health zones, [Bunia](1925095.md), [Mongbwalu](34360729.md), and [Rwampara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rwampara,_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-6] As of 26 May 2026, the ebola epidemic reached 4 more health zones, [Aru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aru,_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo), [Kilo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilo,_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Nizi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizi,_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Nyankunde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyankunde?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-7]

## Government

In 2003, an [Ituri Interim Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ituri_Interim_Administration) was formed through the efforts of the [Ituri Pacification Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ituri_Pacification_Commission?action=edit\&redlink=1), a commission sponsored by the United Nations Organization Mission in Democratic Republic of the Congo ([MONUC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MONUC), abbreviation of the French name "Mission de l'Organisation des Nations Unies en République Démocratique du Congo") that was set up, after much initial delay, after the pull-out of Ugandan troops from the district.[^cite_note-8] It led to the creation of the [Ituri Interim Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ituri_Interim_Assembly), which elected an administrator and an assembly chairperson. [Petronille Vaweka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petronille_Vaweka), served as chairperson,[^cite_note-9] and was also the sole deputy for the district to the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) in [Kinshasa](45773.md).

The Interim Assembly was dissolved two years after its establishment.[^cite_note-10]

## Economy

[Coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee_bean) and [cocoa beans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_bean) are major agricultural exports from Ituri. The cultivation of cocoa beans is growing in popularity among farmers.[^cite_note-11]

The [Kilo-Moto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilo-Moto) gold mines are partly located in Ituri. In the beginning of the 21st century, petroleum reserves were found by [Heritage Oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heritage_Oil) and [Tullow Oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tullow_Oil) on the shores of [Lake Albert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Albert_\(Africa\)).

## Demographics



[Bunia](1925095.md) from the air

The 2020 population was estimated to be 4,392,200.[^cite_note-12]

### Ethnic groups

The population is composed primarily of [Alur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alur_people), [Hema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hema_people), [Lendu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lendu), [Ngiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngiti), [Bira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bira_\(ethnic_group\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Ndo-Okebo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ndo-Okebo?action=edit\&redlink=1). Figures differ on which one of the groups constitutes the largest percentage of the population in the province. The [Mbuti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbuti), a pygmy ethnic group, primarily live in the [Ituri forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ituri_forest) near the [Okapi Wildlife Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okapi_Wildlife_Reserve). Some Mbuti have been forced into urban areas by [deforestation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deforestation), over-hunting and [violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effacer_le_Tableau).
