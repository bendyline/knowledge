The **2026 World Rugby Pacific Nations Cup**[^cite_note-3] was the eighteenth [Pacific Nations Cup](3709127.md) (PNC) tournament. The tournament featured a condensed version, hosted over two matchdays in [Japan](15573.md) between four teams with semi-finals, a third-place game and a final.[^cite_note-format-2]

[Fiji](1074616.md) were the defending champions, going into the tournament having won the previous two editions back-to-back ([2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_World_Rugby_Pacific_Nations_Cup), [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Rugby_Pacific_Nations_Cup)).[^cite_note-4] [Japan](759561.md) won the tournament for the fourth time after defeating [Fiji](1074616.md) 20–15 in the [Grand Final](#Grand_Final).[^cite_note-5] [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_national_rugby_union_team) defeated the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_national_rugby_union_team) 19–16 for third place.[^cite_note-6]

## Background

The 2026 edition of the PNC tournament featured a new four-team format with [Japan](759561.md), [Fiji](1074616.md), [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_national_rugby_union_team), and the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_national_rugby_union_team).[^cite_note-format-2] Semi-finals were held in [Ōsaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_Prefecture) on 12 September, followed by the third-place play-off and final in [Tokyo](30057.md) on 19 September.[^cite_note-format-2] Defending champions Fiji were aiming for a third consecutive title.[^cite_note-format-2]

The competition formed an important part of preparations for the [2027 Rugby World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_Rugby_World_Cup) in [Australia](4689264.md) and aligns with World Rugby's revamped international calendar.[^cite_note-format-2] Although [Samoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samoa_national_rugby_union_team) and [Tonga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonga_national_rugby_union_team) did not compete in this edition, they instead played in the new [Nations Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Rugby_Nations_Cup) in July and continue to receive development support.[^cite_note-format-2][^cite_note-7]
