**HMS *Dragon*** is the fourth ship of the [Type 45](466618.md) or *Daring*-class [air-defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air-defence) [destroyers](8779.md) built for the [Royal Navy](26061.md). She was launched in November 2008 and commissioned on 20 April 2012.[^cite_note-32]

## Construction

*Dragon*'s construction began at the then [BAE Systems Naval Ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAE_Systems_Naval_Ships) (later [BAE Systems Surface Fleet Solutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAE_Systems_Surface_Fleet_Solutions)) yard at [Scotstoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotstoun) on the [River Clyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Clyde) in December 2005, and by December 2007 the bow section was in place on the [Govan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Govan) slipway for mating with the other modules. *Dragon* launched from the slipway at [Govan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Govan) on 17 November 2008 at 3\:00 pm.[^cite_note-Launch1-33] Her sponsor was Mrs Susie Boissier, wife of Vice Admiral [Paul Boissier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Boissier_\(Royal_Navy_officer\)), Deputy Commander-in-Chief Fleet and Chief of Staff.[^cite_note-34] The ship was fitted out at Scotstoun.

## Sea trials

*Dragon* commenced her first set of contractor's sea trials on 5 November 2010.[^cite_note-35]

*Dragon* entered her home port of Portsmouth for the first time on 31 August 2011.[^cite_note-36]

## Operational service



*Dragon* off Gibraltar in 2013



*Dragon* operating with [HMS *Queen Elizabeth*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Queen_Elizabeth_\(R08\)) in 2019

*Dragon* joined the Royal Navy Surface Fleet on Friday, 20 April 2012.[^cite_note-37] On Friday 27 April, she made her maiden visit to [Liverpool](18081.md), staying for three days. She opened to the public on Saturday 28 April, with visitors able to see the inside of the ship, including the operations room.

In August 2013, it was reported *Dragon* was sailing with the [USS *Nimitz*](186973.md) carrier group in the Arabian Sea, acting as the main point ship for aircraft control.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39] In August 2013, several [Typhoons](167667.md) from [No. 6 Squadron RAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No._6_Squadron_RAF) were exercising with *Dragon* and US fighters in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md).[^cite_note-40] It sailed westward to the Eastern [Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean).[^cite_note-41]

In April 2014, *Dragon* was deployed to waters north of Scotland, after sailing from [Portsmouth](24207.md) to track the Russian warship [*Vice-Admiral Kulakov*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_destroyer_Vice-Admiral_Kulakov).[^cite_note-42] She was part of the Royal Navy's Atlantic [Patrol Tasking in late 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Royal_Navy_deployments), visiting places such as the [South Georgia Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Georgia_Islands), the [Falkland Islands](7550182.md), and a transit through the [Panama Canal](24849.md).[^cite_note-43]

In October 2016, *Dragon* tracked two Russian corvettes in the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md) and [Bay of Biscay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Biscay) during a major deployment of Russian naval forces near the United Kingdom.[^cite_note-44]

On 11 February 2017, *Dragon* rescued the fourteen crew of the dismasted and adrift British yacht *Clyde Challenger* in the Atlantic Ocean 610 nautical miles (1,130 km) south west of [Land's End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land's_End), [Cornwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornwall).[^cite_note-ITN110217-45] *Clyde Challenger* was subsequently scuttled.[^cite_note-Scuttled-46]

A 26 November 2018 press-release claimed *Dragon* discovered a suspicious boat while on operation in the Middle East. Sailors and Royal Marines boarded the vessel, and found 148 bags containing 3,048 kg of [hashish](14415.md).[^cite_note-47]

On 15 March 2019, *Dragon* made its seventh drug seizure, involving 224 kg of heroin from a fishing vessel in the [Arabian Sea](2747.md).[^cite_note-48] During her time in the Arabian Sea, *Dragon* made eight drug busts and seized over eighteen tons of narcotics, a record for the number of successful busts and the total weight of drugs seized by a Royal Navy ship in the Middle East.

As of March 2019, [HMS *Montrose*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Montrose_\(F236\)) took over the *Dragon*'s patrol role in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md).[^cite_note-49]

HMS *Dragon* set sail in May 2025 to take part in NATO's Formidable Shield; the largest at-sea live-fire exercise in Europe.[^cite_note-50]

On 3 March 2026, Prime Minister [Keir Starmer](18681491.md) announced that HMS *Dragon* would be deployed to the eastern Mediterranean to reinforce security around [RAF Akrotiri](432784.md) in [Cyprus](5595.md).[^cite_note-51] The deployment followed a [Shahed-136](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahed-136) drone strike shortly after midnight on 2 March 2026 that caused minor damage to a hangar during the [Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53][^cite_note-54] Accompanied by three [Wildcat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AgustaWestland_AW159_Wildcat) helicopters, armed with [Martlet missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martlet_\(missile\)), and a [Merlin](505833.md) helicopter that were airlifted separately, the destroyer was tasked with strengthening air defences against drone threats.[^cite_note-\:1-55]

HMS *Dragon* departed Portsmouth for Cyprus on 10 March 2026 after rapid preparations following a period in [dry dock](213739.md), with the crew recalled and the usual six weeks of maintenance, weapons loading, and system checks completed in six days.[^cite_note-\:1-55][^cite_note-56]

### 2026 Iran conflict

*Dragon* was deployed to the Eastern [Mediterranean](19006.md) after rising tensions as a result of strikes on [RAF Akrotiri](432784.md) on [Cyprus](5593.md). HMS *Dragon* was to help "defending Cyprus and the wider Eastern Mediterranean."[^cite_note-57]

As is routine when such ships are deployed, *Dragon* embarked two [AgustaWestland AW159 Wildcat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AgustaWestland_AW159_Wildcat) helicopters from [815 Naval Air Squadron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/815_Naval_Air_Squadron). These will be fitted with [Martlet Multirole missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martlet_\(missile\)), which were added with the intention of strengthening anti-drone defences in the area. [Lockheed Martin F-35 Lightning II](11812.md) [fighter aircraft](10929.md) are also in the area and "are already conducting air defence sorties."[^cite_note-58] HMS Dragon was forced to dock shortly after arriving in Cyprus due to what the Ministry of Defence described as a "minor technical issue". The incident occurred amid criticism that the United Kingdom government had responded too slowly to the escalating conflict in the Middle East.[^cite_note-59]

On 9 May 2026 the UK and France announced a plan, where *Dragon* would be deployed to the Middle East to facilitate shipping through the Straight of Hormuz once a ceasefire is established.[^cite_note-\:2-60]

## Characteristics

Main article: [Type 45 destroyer § Characteristics](466618.md#Characteristics)

At launch *Dragon* displayed a red [Welsh Dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_Dragon) on each side of her bow, the only Royal Navy ship to be adorned in this way. The dragons, which had been privately funded, were removed in a 2011 refit and repaint, and restored in 2016, funded by a campaign led by the [British Warships Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Warships_Association?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-61]

## In popular culture

*Dragon* appeared in *[No Time to Die](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Time_to_Die)*, the 25th film of the [*James Bond* series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_James_Bond_films).[^cite_note-67] The film inaccurately depicts the Type 45's [Aster Surface-to-Air missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aster_surface-to-air_missiles)[^cite_note-68] as land attack missiles. Shooting for *Dragon*'s role in the film's finale took place off the [Faroe Islands](17238607.md) in September 2019.[^cite_note-69]
