The **Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Expatriates** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الخارجية والمغتربين, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Wizārat al-Khārijīyah wa-al-Mughtaribīn*) is a [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_\(Syria\)) ministry of [Syria](7515849.md), responsible for conducting [foreign relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Syria) of the country. The ministry oversees the expression of Syrian foreign policy, the protection of the interests of Syria and the interests of Syrian citizens abroad, as well as the representation of the country to other countries and [international organizations](18201224.md). The responsibilities of the [Ministry of Expatriates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Expatriates_\(Syria\)) were merged on 14 April 2011.

The current minister is [Asaad al-Shaibani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asaad_al-Shaibani) who was appointed on 21 December 2024 following the [collapse](78548066.md) of the [Ba'athist regime](57134800.md).
