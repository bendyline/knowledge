The **Nikko Line** (日光線, *Nikkō-sen*) is a 94.5-kilometre (58.7 mi) railway line in Japan operated by the [private railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_railway) company [Tobu Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobu_Railway). It branches from [Tōbu Dōbutsu Kōen Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C5%8Dbu_D%C5%8Dbutsu_K%C5%8Den_Station) in [Miyashiro, Saitama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyashiro,_Saitama) on the [Skytree Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobu_Skytree_Line), extending north to [Tōbu Nikkō Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C5%8Dbu_Nikk%C5%8D_Station) in [Nikkō, Tochigi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikk%C5%8D,_Tochigi).

The line has two branch lines: the [Utsunomiya Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobu_Utsunomiya_Line) at [Shin-Tochigi Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shin-Tochigi_Station) in [Tochigi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tochigi_\(city\)), Tochigi, and the [Kinugawa Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobu_Kinugawa_Line) at [Shimo-Imaichi Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimo-Imaichi_Station) in Nikkō.

## Service patterns

As of March 2013, stops and service patterns are

- **Local** (普通, *Futsū*): Stops at all stations on the Nikko Line. Through to/from [Naka-Meguro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naka-Meguro_Station) on the [Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metro_Hibiya_Line);

- **Section Semi-Express** (区間準急, *Kukan Junkyū*): Stops at all stations on the Nikko Line, limited-stop service on the [Skytree Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C5%8Dbu_Skytree_Line);

- **Semi-Express** (準急, *Junkyū*): Stops at all stations on the Nikko Line, limited-stop service on the Skytree Line. Through to/from [Chūō-Rinkan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C5%AB%C5%8D-Rinkan_Station) on the [Tokyu Den-en-toshi Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyu_Den-en-toshi_Line) via the [Tokyo Metro Hanzomon Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metro_Hanzomon_Line);

- **Section Express** (区間急行, *Kukan Kyūkō*): Stops at all stations on the Nikko Line, limited-stop service on the Skytree Line;

- **Express** (急行, *Kyūkō*): Stops at all stations on the Nikko Line, express service on the Skytree Line. Through to/from [Chūō-Rinkan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C5%AB%C5%8D-Rinkan_Station) on the [Tokyu Den-en-toshi Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyu_Den-en-toshi_Line) via the [Tokyo Metro Hanzomon Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metro_Hanzomon_Line).

- **&#x20;Limited Express** (特急, *Tokkyū*): Stops at partly stations on the Nikko Line, Limited express also services on the Skytree Line. There are Limited Express which are 4 types of **KN/KG** (スペーシアきぬ・けごん, *SPACIA KINU・KEGON*)・**RV** (リバティきぬ・けごん・会津, *REVATY KINU・KEGON・AIZU*)・**KF** (きりふり, *KIRIFURI*)・**JR** ([日光](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikk%C5%8D_\(train\))・[きぬがわ・スペーシアきぬがわ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinugawa_\(train\)), *JR NIKKO・KINUGAWA*). Trains which are named **KINU** or **KINUGAWA** bound to [Kinugawaonsen Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinugawaonsen_Station). Trains which are named **KEGON** or **NIKKO**, **KIRIFURI** bound to [Tobu-Nikko Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobu-Nikko_Station). Trains which are named **AIZU** bound to [Aizu-Tajima Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aizu-Tajima_Station).

- **SL Taiju Futara** (SL大樹ふたら, *Esueru Taiju Futara*):

  [^cite_note-1]

  &#x20;Stops at Tobu-Nikko and Shimo-Imaichi. Passengers riding on this train must purchase the package

  [^cite_note-2]

  &#x20;on Tobu Top Tours and so on. Runs once a month.

## History

The Nikko Line opened (dual track and electrified) on 1 October 1929.[^cite_note-terada2002-3]

In 1943, the section north of Kassemba was reduced to single track and the recovered rails were used to build the [Tobu Kumagaya Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobu_Kumagaya_Line).[^cite_note-asahi2010-4]

Electric limited express services first started operating in February 1949.[^cite_note-terada2002-3] From October 1960, new 1720 series "Deluxe Romance car" EMUs were introduced on limited express services to and from Nikko.[^cite_note-terada2002-3]

From 1 June 1990, new [100 series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobu_100_series) "Spacia" EMUs were introduced on limited express services, and the maximum speed of these was raised to 120 km/h in 1992.[^cite_note-terada2002-3]

A connecting track at [Kurihashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurihashi_Station) was built to the [JR-East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Japan_Railway_Company) [Tōhoku Main Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C5%8Dhoku_Main_Line) ([Utsunomiya Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utsunomiya_Line)) from 18 March 2006, allowing through-operations of *[Nikkō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikk%C5%8D_\(train\))* and *[Kinugawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinugawa_\(train\))* limited express services to/from [Ōmiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Cmiya_Station_\(Saitama\)), [Ikebukuro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikebukuro_Station), and [Shinjuku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinjuku_Station).

From 17 March 2012, station numbering was introduced on all Tobu lines, with Tobu Nikko Line stations adopting the prefix "TN" in orange.[^cite_note-tobu20120209-5]

The former *Skytree Train* and [300 series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobu_300_series) limited express trains, *Rapid*, and *Section Rapid* services were discontinued from the start of the revised timetable on 21 April 2017.[^cite_note-6]

## Incidents

On 20 August 2026, four Tobu Railway workers were struck and killed by a limited express train outside of [Shin-Kanuma Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shin-Kanuma_Station) as they were spraying herbicide to remove weeds from the tracks. Police began an investigation into whether relevant safety regulations were followed at the time of the accident.[^cite_note-7]
