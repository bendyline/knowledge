[Nigeria](21383.md) has 36 [states](226734.md) that each elect a [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor) to serve as chief executive of the state government. The sole [federal district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_district), the [Federal Capital Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Capital_Territory_\(Nigeria\)), is headed by a minister appointed by the [president](516088.md) to oversee the administration. In the event of a vacancy, the governor is succeeded by the second-highest-ranking state official; the [deputy governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_governor).

As of 14 May 2026, there are 31 states with [APC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Progressives_Congress) governors, 2 states with [APM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allied_Peoples_Movement) governors and 1 state each with [Accord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accord_\(Nigeria\)), [APGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Progressives_Grand_Alliance), and [Labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Party_\(Nigeria\)) governors.

The current gubernatorial term ends and new term begins in May for most states, two to three months after their election. Governors are elected for a term of four years (maximum of two terms).

All 36 governors are members of the [Nigeria Governors' Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria_Governors'_Forum), a non-partisan organization which represents states in discussions with the federal government. Other organizations for governors include the partisan [Progressive Governors Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_Governors_Forum) and [PDP Governors Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PDP_Governors_Forum).

## State governors

Citizen of [Nigeria](21383.md) by birth, at least 35 years of age, is a member of a political [party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_political_parties_in_Nigeria) and is sponsored by that political party are eligible for the office of Governor. The [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nigeria) limits state governors to only two four-year terms in office.
