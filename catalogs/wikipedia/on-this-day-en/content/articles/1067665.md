The **Kamov Ka-27** ([NATO reporting name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO_reporting_name) '**Helix'**) is a [coaxial rotor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coaxial_rotor) [military helicopter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_helicopter) developed for the [Soviet Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Navy), and as of 2024 is in service in various countries including [Russia](25391.md), [Ukraine](31750.md), [Vietnam](202354.md), [China](5405.md), [South Korea](27019.md), and [India](14533.md). Variants include the **Ka-29** assault transport, the **Ka-28** downgraded export version, and the **Ka-32** for civilian use.

## Design and development

The helicopter was developed for ferrying and [anti-submarine warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-submarine_warfare). Design work began in 1969 and the first prototype flew in 1973. It was intended to replace the decade-old [Kamov Ka-25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamov_Ka-25), and had to have identical or smaller external dimensions than its predecessor. Like other Kamov military helicopters it has [coaxial rotors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coaxial_rotors), removing the need for a [tail rotor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tail_rotor). In total, five prototypes and pre-series helicopters were built. Series production started at [Kumertau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumertau) in July 1979, and the new helicopter officially entered service with the [Soviet Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Navy) in April 1981.[^cite_note-modernisation_red_samovar-1]

The Ka-27 has a crew of three with a pilot and navigator both stationed in the cockpit, and a sonar operator seated behind them. It has a four-leg fixed landing gear. The Ka-27 is equipped with two lateral buoys, that can be inflated in the case of a forced landing on water.[^cite_note-modernisation_red_samovar-1]

The Ka-27PL anti-submarine version is equipped with a radar, and either a [dipping sonar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dipping_sonar) or a [magnetic anomaly detector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_anomaly_detector). It can also carry either up to 36 [sonobuoys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonobuoys), or a torpedo, or between six and eight conventional [depth charges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depth_charge), or a single nuclear one. Ka-27PLs generally operate in pairs as [hunter-killer teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter-killer_team).[^cite_note-modernisation_red_samovar-1]

The Ka-27PS [search and rescue](172599.md) helicopter can carry 12 folding seats or four stretchers in its cabin, and is equipped with a 300 kg (660 lb) winch. Its fuel capacity of 3,450 litres (910 US gal; 760 imp gal) is greater than the 2,940-litre (780 US gal; 650 imp gal) capacity of the Ka-27PL.[^cite_note-modernisation_red_samovar-1]

Ka-32 variants, e.g. the Klimov-powered Ka-32A11BC, have been certified for commercial operations throughout the world, notably in Canada and Europe. The Ka-32 has been certified for the newer [Klimov VK-2500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klimov_VK-2500)PS-02 engine.[^cite_note-3]

The Ka-32A11BC features a high [power-to-weight ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power-to-weight_ratio) and ease of handling, owing to its coaxial rotor design. The rotors' diameters are not restricted by the presence of a tail rotor and associated tail boom; this facilitates maneuvering near obstacles and helps assure exceptional accuracy when hovering in heavy smoke and dust conditions. The Ka-32A11BC may be equipped with the [Bambi Bucket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambi_Bucket) suspended fire-fighting system of up to five tons capacity. The service life has been extended to up to 32,000 flight hours.[^cite_note-4]

## Operational history



Ka-27s aboard the aircraft carrier [*Novorossiysk*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_aircraft_carrier_Novorossiysk) in 1984

A Russian Navy Ka-27 helicopter from the Russian [*Udaloy*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udaloy-class_destroyer) [destroyer](8779.md) *Severomorsk* conducted interoperability deck landing training on board the US command ship [USS *Mount Whitney*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Mount_Whitney) on 22 July 2010.[^cite_note-rhKa27-5]

Ka-32A11BC multipurpose helicopters have been successfully operated in Portugal for over five years. In 2006, [Kamov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamov) won the tender for the supply of Ka-32A11BC firefighting helicopters, to replace [Aérospatiale SA 330 Pumas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%A9rospatiale_SA_330_Puma).

Over 240 Ka-32 have been built as of 2019 and have been exported to more than 30 countries;[^cite_note-6] South Korea operates some 60 Ka-32s.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] In the mid-1990s Russia offset debt to South Korea through supplies of weapons.[^cite_note-9]

Since the 1990s, China has purchased the Ka-28 export version and Ka-31 radar warning version for the [PLAN](24918.md) fleet. Ka-31 purchases were first revealed in 2010. It is believed that Chinese Ka-28s have been equipped with more enhanced avionics compared to Ka-28s exported to other countries.[^cite_note-10]

In 2013, Russia tested the new Kamov Ka-27M with an [active electronically scanned array](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_electronically_scanned_array) radar. The basis of the modernization of the Ka-27M is installed on the helicopter airborne radar with an active phased array antenna FH-A. This radar is part of the command and tactical radar system that combines several other systems: acoustic, magnetometric, signals intelligence and radar. All the information on them is displayed on the display instrumentation.[^cite_note-11]

Ka-32s are used for construction of [transmission towers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transmission_tower) for [overhead power lines](1355057.md), as it has somewhat higher lift capacity than the [Vertol 107](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Vertol_CH-46_Sea_Knight#Civilian).[^cite_note-12] In Canada, the Ka-32 is used for selective logging as it is able to lift selective species vertically.[^cite_note-13]

In August 2013, a Kamov Ka-32, C-GKHL operating in [Bella Coola, British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Coola,_British_Columbia), Canada, experienced failure of one of its Klimov TV3-117BMA engines (manufactured by Motor Sich in Ukraine). The subsequent technical investigation indicated that there was poor quality control in the assembly of the compressor turbine, leading to failure of the complete unit after several compressor blades separated.[^cite_note-14]

Ka-27s were used by the Ba'athist [Syrian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Navy) during the [Syrian Civil War](30741795.md).[^cite_note-15]

On 21 June 2024, a [Ka-29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ka-29) was reportedly shot down over Crimea, by a Russian [Pantsir-S1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantsir-S1), during a Ukrainian drone attack involving both air and naval drones, killing the crew of four.[^cite_note-16]

On 15 May 2026 a Ka-27 was destroyed during a Ukrainian drone attack on the Yeysk airport, Krasnodar oblast. [^cite_note-17]

**General characteristics**

- **Crew:** one-three, plus two-three specialists (Ka-27)

  

- **Capacity:** 4,000 kg (8,818 lb) payload (Ka-32), or up to 16 troops (Ka-29).

- **Length:** 11.3 m (37 ft 1 in)

- **Height:** 5.5 m (18 ft 1 in)

- **Empty weight:** 6,500 kg (14,330 lb)

- **Gross weight:** 11,000 kg (24,251 lb)

- **Max takeoff weight:** 12,000 kg (26,455 lb)

- **Powerplant:** 2 × [Isotov TV3-117V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isotov_TV3-117V) turboshaft engines, 1,660 kW (2,230 hp) each

- **Main rotor diameter:** 2 × 15.8 m (51 ft 10 in)

- **Main rotor area:** 392.2 m

  2

  &#x20;(4,222 sq ft) 3-bladed contra-rotating rotors

**Performance**

- **Maximum speed:** 270 km/h (170 mph, 150 kn)

- **Cruise speed:** 205 km/h (127 mph, 111 kn)

- **Range:** 980 km (610 mi, 530 nmi)

- **Service ceiling:** 5,000 m (16,000 ft)

**Armament**\
&#x20;***Ka-27***

- 1 × torpedoes ([AT-1M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT-1M?action=edit\&redlink=1), [VTT-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VTT-1?action=edit\&redlink=1), [UMGT-1 Orlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UMGT-1_Orlan?action=edit\&redlink=1), [APR-2 Yastreb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APR-2_Yastreb?action=edit\&redlink=1)) or 36 [RGB-NM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RGB-NM?action=edit\&redlink=1) & [RGB-NM-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RGB-NM-1?action=edit\&redlink=1) sonobuoys

***Ka-29TB***

- 1 × mobile forward firing [GShG-7.62 machine gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GShG-7.62_machine_gun) with 1800 rounds,

- 1 × 30 mm [2A42](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2A42) cannon with 250 rounds (flexible semi-rigid mount, optional/removable with ammunition carried in cabin)

- four external hardpoints for bombs, rockets, gunpods, munitions dispensers, special four round missile launchers for the [9K114 Shturm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9K114_Shturm)
