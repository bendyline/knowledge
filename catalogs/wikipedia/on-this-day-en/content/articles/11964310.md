**&#x20;Ubaté Province** is one of the 15 provinces in the [Cundinamarca Department](189405.md), [Colombia](5222.md).

## Etymology

The name Ubaté comes from the native name "Ebate" meaning "Bloodied land" or "Sower of the mouth".[^cite_note-websiteUbate-1]
