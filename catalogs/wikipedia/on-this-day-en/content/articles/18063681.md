**Florești** (Romanian pronunciation: [\[floˈreʃtʲ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Romanian)) is the capital city and industrial and commercial center of [Floreşti District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flore%C5%9Fti_District) of [Moldova](19260.md). It is located on the river [Răut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C4%83ut_River).

## Name

The name comes from the [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language) word *floare* ("flower"). The old name of the settlement was *Rădiul Florilor*, which is also a derivative from the Romanian word for "flower".

## Geography

The city is located in the north of [the country](19260.md), on the river [Răut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C4%83ut_River), a tributary of the [Dniester](376633.md).

## Politics and administration

Florești is governed by the City Council and the City Mayor ([Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language): *Primar*), both elected once every four years. The current mayor is [Iurie Ţap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iurie_%C5%A2ap).

## Demographics

According to the [2024 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Moldovan_census), 10,925 inhabitants lived in Florești, a decrease compared to the previous census in 2014, when 11,998 inhabitants were registered.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
