The **2027 NCAA Division I women's basketball tournament** will involve a total of 76 teams in a [single-elimination tournament](939680.md) to determine the [National Collegiate Athletic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA) (NCAA) [Division I](669931.md) [college basketball](768856.md) national champion for the [2026–27 NCAA Division I women's basketball season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_season). The 45th edition of the [tournament](536018.md) is scheduled to begin on March 17, 2027, and will conclude with the [championship game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_championship_game?action=edit\&redlink=1) on April 4, at the [Nationwide Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationwide_Arena) in [Columbus, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus,_Ohio).[^cite_note-1] This tournament will mark the introduction of the Opening Round and an expansion of the field of participants from 68 teams to 76.[^cite_note-2]

## Tournament procedure

Further information: [NCAA Division I Women's Basketball Tournament § Current tournament format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_I_Women's_Basketball_Tournament#Current_tournament_format)

A total of 76 teams will enter the 2027 tournament, a change from 68 in previous years. 32 automatic bids are awarded to each program that won its conference's tournament. The remaining 44 teams will receive at-large bids, with selections extended by the [NCAA selection committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_basketball_tournament_selection_process) on Selection Sunday scheduled for March 14. The selection committee will also [seed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seeding_\(sports\)) the entire field from 1 to 76.

In a change from past practice, the top 16 teams in the committee's ranking will be bracketed solely by their ranking, without regard to conference affiliation. This change affects only the women's tournament.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

In all, 24 teams (the 12 lowest-seeded automatic qualifiers and the 12 lowest-seeded at-large teams) play in the March Madness Opening Round. The winners of these games will advance to the main tournament [bracket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bracket_\(tournament\)).

## Media coverage

### Television

[ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN_College_Basketball) broadcast each game of the tournament across either [ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN), [ESPN2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN2), [ESPNU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPNU), [ESPNEWS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPNEWS), or [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN_on_ABC). For the 5th consecutive season, the national championship game will air on ABC.[^cite_note-8]
