**MV *Mayuree Naree*** is a Thailand-[flagged](6162348.md) [bulk carrier](1324895.md) built in India as ***Good Princess*** in 2008. Since 2012 the vessel has been owned and operated by the Thai shipping company [Precious Shipping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precious_Shipping?action=edit\&redlink=1).

On 11 March 2026, the vessel was struck by Iranian projectiles near the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md), causing a fire and forcing most of the crew to abandon ship. Nearly a month later on 8 April, three crew members of the vessel were confirmed to have died.

## Description

*Mayuree Naree* is a double-hulled, single-deck, self-trimming<sup>[A](#endnote_selftrim)</sup> [bulk carrier](1324895.md) built in 2008 by [Hindustan Shipyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan_Shipyard) in [Visakhapatnam](238760.md), India as *Good Princess* to [Indian Register of Shipping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Register_of_Shipping) [class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_classification_society).[^cite_note-6] It is designed to transport dry bulk cargo such as grain, coal, and iron ore in five cargo holds with a total capacity of 40,975 cubic metres (1,447,000 cu ft). For cargo handling, the ship has four 30-tonne (30-long-ton; 33-short-ton) cranes.[^cite_note-seaweb-1][^cite_note-trader-5]

It has an overall length of 178.7 metres (586 ft) and a beam of 28 metres (92 ft) metres, and fully laden draws 9.75 metres (32.0 ft) of water with a displacement of 38,154 tonnes (37,551 long tons). Its [gross tonnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_tonnage) is 30,193; [net tonnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_tonnage) 10,297; and [deadweight tonnage](1346384.md) 30,193 tonnes.[^cite_note-seaweb-1]

The ship is propelled by a single fixed-pitch propeller driven by a [six-cylinder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straight-six_engine) [MAN B\&W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everllence) 6S42MC [two-stroke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-stroke_engine) low-speed [crosshead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crosshead) [diesel engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel_engine), with a maximum continuous rating of 6,480 kW (8,690 hp). Its service speed is 14.3 knots (26.5 km/h; 16.5 mph).[^cite_note-seaweb-1]

## Career

The ship is one of four Trader-class bulk carriers originally built for Goodearth Maritime of [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai), India.[^cite_note-trader-5][^cite_note-seaweb-1] It was [laid down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel_laying) at [Hindustan Shipyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan_Shipyard) in [Visakhapatnam](238760.md), India on 18 January 2006, [launched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceremonial_ship_launching) on 16 May 2007 as *Good Princess*, and completed on 23 January 2008.[^cite_note-seaweb-1]

In 2012, *Good Princess* was sold to [Precious Shipping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precious_Shipping?action=edit\&redlink=1) of [Bangkok](56615.md), renamed *Mayuree Naree*, and reflagged from India to Thailand.[^cite_note-equasis-2][^cite_note-precious-7]

### Attack near the Strait of Hormuz

On 11 March 2026, *Mayuree Naree* was struck by two projectiles while sailing near the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md),[^cite_note-bkpost-8][^cite_note-Dahir-9] one of several vessels struck during the [Strait of Hormuz crisis](82550763.md).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] The vessel had departed [Khalifa Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalifa_Port) in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) and was en route to [Kandla Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandla_Port) in [Gujarat](53707.md), India.[^cite_note-bkpost-8]

According to the [Royal Thai Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Thai_Navy), the attack occurred at about 11\:10 a.m. Thailand time shortly after the vessel transited the strait.[^cite_note-bkpost-8] Two projectiles struck the ship above the waterline, damaging the stern and the engine room and causing a fire on board.[^cite_note-bkpost-8][^cite_note-Dahir-9] All 23 crew members aboard the vessel were Thai nationals.[^cite_note-bkpost-8] Twenty crew members abandoned ship in liferafts and were rescued by the [Royal Navy of Oman](13921289.md), who brought them ashore at [Khasab](1658322.md), [Oman](22316.md).[^cite_note-bkpost-8][^cite_note-12] Three crew members were reported missing and believed to be trapped in the engine room following the explosions.[^cite_note-13] The vessel was sailing in ballast at the time of the attack, meaning it was not carrying cargo.[^cite_note-bkpost-8] The ship was reported to have sustained the heaviest damage among several vessels targeted in the region that day.[^cite_note-bkpost-8]

Iran's [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy](4676345.md) later claimed responsibility for the attack.[^cite_note-bkpost-8][^cite_note-Dahir-9] Its naval commander, [Alireza Tangsiri](58274150.md), said in a social media post that the vessel had ignored warnings issued by Iranian forces while passing through the area.[^cite_note-Dahir-9] The attack occurred amid escalating conflict in the region and a series of incidents targeting shipping in and around the Strait of Hormuz, one of the world's most important maritime chokepoints for oil and natural gas transportation.[^cite_note-Dahir-9]

She drifted ashore near the village of [Ramchah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramchah) on [Qeshm Island](2252168.md) in the Strait of Hormuz on 27 March.[^cite_note-14] On 8 April, Thai Foreign Minister [Sihasak Phuangketkeow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sihasak_Phuangketkeow) confirmed during a press conference that three crew members of the vessel had died.[^cite_note-15]
