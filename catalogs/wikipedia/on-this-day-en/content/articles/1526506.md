**Fishlake National Forest** is a [U.S. national forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Forest) located in south central [Utah](31716.md). The namesake for the forest is [Fish Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish_Lake_\(Utah\)), the largest freshwater mountain lake in the state.[^cite_note-6]

## Wildlife

Animals that inhabit this forest are [elk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elk), [shrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrew), [deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deer), [black bears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_black_bear), [coyotes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coyote), various species of [bats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bat), [moose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moose), [raccoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon), two species of [skunks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skunk), [badgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_badger), [turkey vultures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey_vulture), two species of [eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eagle), [pika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pika), [snowshoe hares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowshoe_hare), various species of [woodpeckers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodpecker), [pine marten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_marten), [porcupines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_porcupine), four species of [hummingbirds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hummingbird), [beavers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_beaver), [kestrels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_kestrel), [pronghorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pronghorn), various species of [owls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owl), [bobcats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobcat), [minks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_mink), three species of [fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox), [cougars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_cougar), [mountain lions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_lions), [bighorn sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bighorn_sheep), [wild turkeys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_turkey), and [mountain goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_goat).

## Geography

Established in 1908 from the merging of [Fish Lake National Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish_Lake_National_Forest) and [Glenwood National Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenwood_National_Forest), the forest covers 1.5 million acres (6,100 km<sup>2</sup>) and is split into four districts. The forest lies in parts of nine counties. In descending order of forestland area, they are [Sevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sevier_County,_Utah), [Millard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millard_County,_Utah), [Piute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piute_County,_Utah), [Beaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaver_County,_Utah), [Wayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_County,_Utah), [Juab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juab_County,_Utah), [Garfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garfield_County,_Utah), [Iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_County,_Utah), and [Sanpete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanpete_County,_Utah) counties. Forest headquarters are located in [Richfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richfield,_Utah) with local [ranger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Park_Ranger) district offices in [Beaver](136864.md), [Fillmore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fillmore,_Utah), [Loa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loa,_Utah), and Richfield.[^cite_note-7] The national forest is the headwaters of Otter Creek, a tributary of the [East Fork Sevier River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Fork_Sevier_River). It is also home to the [Chalk Creek Hieroglyphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalk_Creek_Hieroglyphics).

[Pando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pando_\(tree\)), a [quaking aspen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaking_aspen) [clonal colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clonal_colony), that is according to some sources, the oldest (80,000 years) and largest (106 acres, 13 million pounds) organism on Earth, is located in the Fremont River Ranger District of the National Forest, 1 mile southwest of Fish Lake on Utah route 25.[^cite_note-8]

## Acquisition

[Native](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_of_the_Americas) water rights to Fish Lake were sold to the [Fremont Irrigation Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fremont_Irrigation_Company?action=edit\&redlink=1) on March 10, 1889, for nine horses, 500 pounds of flour, one steer, and a suit of clothes. Ten years later President [William McKinley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_McKinley) created a Forest Reserve which included Fish Lake.[^cite_note-USDA-9]
