**Barmal District** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): برمل ولسوالۍ, [Dari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dari_language): ولسوالی برمل) is a district of [Paktika Province](457294.md), [Afghanistan](737.md). It shares a border with [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) and [Balochistan, Pakistan](4687.md). The [Angur Ada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angur_Ada) is the official [border checkpoint](288329.md) and [border crossing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_crossing) between the people of Paktika and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The government office of Barmal District is located in Newai Ada.

## History

Barmal was one of the districts most affected by the [2022 Afghanistan earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2022_Afghanistan_earthquake), which killed at least 500 persons and injured a thousand others in the district.[^cite_note-1] Many houses constructed primarily of mud and wood were razed to the ground.[^cite_note-2] Heavy rain and the earthquake contributed to landslides that destroyed entire [hamlets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlet_\(place\)).[^cite_note-3]

### Construction of new earthquake-resistant houses

In August 2022, new "[earthquake-resistant houses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthquake-resistant_structures)" began to be constructed in the district for victims of the earthquake.[^cite_note-Saqalain_Eqbal-4] The project involves the establishment of 2,000 homes in both [Gayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gayan_District) and Barmal districts of Paktika Province, including 300 houses in [Spera District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spera_District) of neighboring [Khost Province](457249.md).[^cite_note-Saqalain_Eqbal-4]

## Demographics

Further information: [Demographics of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Afghanistan)

The population of the district in 2004 was 88,028 people.[^cite_note-mrrd-7][^cite_note-All-Paktika-8] Majority of them are ethnic [Pashtuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns), mainly from the [Wazir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wazir_\(Pashtun_tribe\)) and [Kharoti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharoti) tribes.
