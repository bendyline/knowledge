**Kupiansk Raion** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Куп'янський район) is a [raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raion) (district) in [Kharkiv Oblast](494901.md), [Ukraine](31750.md). The administrative center of the raion is the city of [Kupiansk](5156384.md). Population: 130,111 (2022 estimate).[^cite_note-ua2022estimate-2]

On 18 July 2020, as part of the administrative reform of Ukraine, the number of raions of Kharkiv Oblast was reduced to seven, and the area of Kupiansk Raion was significantly expanded. Three abolished raions, [Dvorichna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dvorichna_Raion), [Shevchenkove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shevchenkove_Raion), and [Velykyi Burluk Raions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velykyi_Burluk_Raion), as well as the city of Kupiansk, which was previously incorporated as a [city of oblast significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_regional_significance_\(Ukraine\)) and did not belong to the raion, were merged into Kupiansk Raion.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-2020_reform_maps-4] The January 2020 estimate of the raion population was 23,180 (2020 est.).[^cite_note-ua2020estimate-5]

It is approximately 120 kilometers to the east of Kharkiv and 40 kilometers south of the border with [Russia](25391.md).

## Subdivisions

### Current



Map of Kupiansk Raion Hromadas after July 2020.

After the reform since 17 July 2020, the [Raion](6719452.md) consists of 8 territorial [hromadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hromada),[^cite_note-2020_reform_maps-4][^cite_note-6] including 1 city, 3 rural settlements, and 4 villages as administrative centers:

- [Dvorichna settlement hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dvorichna_settlement_hromada), with the administration in rural settlement of [Dvorichna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dvorichna), transferred from Dvorichna Raion;

  [^cite_note-admreform_2020_dvorichna-7]

- [Kindrashivka rural hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kindrashivka_rural_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), village of [Kindrashivka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kindrashivka,_Kharkiv_Oblast?action=edit\&redlink=1), retained from Kupiansk Raion;

- [Kupiansk urban hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kupiansk_urban_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), city of [Kupiansk](5156384.md), transferred from [Kupiansk Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kupiansk_Municipality);

  [^cite_note-admreform_2020_kupiansk-8]

- [Kurylivka rural hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurylivka_rural_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), village of [Kurylivka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurylivka,_Kupiansk_Raion), retained from Kupiansk Raion;

- [Petropavlivka rural hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petropavlivka_rural_hromada,_Kharkiv_Oblast?action=edit\&redlink=1), village of [Petropavlivka](72311698.md), retained from Kupiansk Raion.

- [Shevchenkove settlement hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shevchenkove_settlement_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), rural settlement of [Shevchenkove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shevchenkove,_Shevchenkove_settlement_hromada,_Kupiansk_Raion,_Kharkiv_Oblast), transferred from Shevchenkove Raion;

  [^cite_note-admreform_2020_shevchenkove_kharkiv-9]

- [Velykyi Burluk settlement hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velykyi_Burluk_settlement_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), rural settlement of [Velykyi Burluk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velykyi_Burluk), transferred from Velykyi Burluk Raion;

  [^cite_note-admreform_2020_velykyi_burluk-10]

- [Vilkhuvatka rural hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilkhuvatka_rural_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), village of [Vilkhuvatka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilkhuvatka,_Kupiansk_Raion,_Kharkiv_Oblast?action=edit\&redlink=1), transferred from Velykyi Burluk Raion;

  [^cite_note-admreform_2020_velykyi_burluk-10]

### Before 2020



Kupiansk Raion in Kharkiv Oblast before 2020

Before the 2020 reform, the raion consisted of three hromadas:[^cite_note-11]

- Kindrashivka rural hromada with the administration in Kindrashivka;

- Kurylivka rural hromada with the administration in Kurylivka;

- Petropavlivka rural hromada with the administration in Petropavlivka.
