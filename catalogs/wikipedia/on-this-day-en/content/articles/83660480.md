**Vasile Tofan** (born 12 July 1982) is a Moldovan investor and businessman.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] He has served as [Prime Minister of Moldova](2174189.md) since 22 July 2026,[^cite_note-3] having been nominated by president [Maia Sandu](40796149.md) on 11 July.[^cite_note-4]

## Early life

Tofan was born on 12 July 1982. After completing both a bachelor's and master's degree from [Erasmus University Rotterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_University_Rotterdam) in public management, he worked as a consultant at [Monitor Deloitte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monitor_Deloitte) and as a senior manager at [Philips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philips) in the [Netherlands](21148.md).[^cite_note-ad-5] He then returned to education, completing another master's degree in business administration from [Harvard Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Business_School) in 2012.[^cite_note-ad-5] From 2011 until July 2026, he worked as a senior partner and member of the investment committee of the [Kyiv](585629.md)-based company Horizon Capital.[^cite_note-ad-5]
