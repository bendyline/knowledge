# List of diplomatic missions of the Netherlands



Countries with Dutch diplomatic missions.

This is a **list of diplomatic missions of the [Kingdom of the Netherlands](21148.md)**, excluding honorary consulates. [Ambassador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambassadors) of the Netherlands are officially known as His Majesty's Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary, with their [Diplomatic Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassies) called His Majesty's Embassy accordingly. Neither [consuls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consuls) nor [consulates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consulates) receive an official title.
