**Au Kam San** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 區錦新) (born 30 April 1957 in [Macau](19068.md)) is a former member of [Legislative Assembly of Macau](1620997.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] He was a member of [New Macau Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Macau_Association) until 2016[^cite_note-3] and was one of the three pro-democracy [lawmakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawmakers) in Macau.[^cite_note-4]

## Career

Au stepped down from the Legislative Assembly at the [2021 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Macanese_legislative_election).[^cite_note-5]

### 2025 arrest

On 31 July 2025, Au was arrested by the Judiciary Police for alleged collusion with foreign forces to endanger national security, a crime under the revised [Macau National Security Law](19892280.md).[^cite_note-6] In 2026, Macau passed a law that allows judges to hold national security trials in secret behind closed doors.[^cite_note-\:1-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Au was the first person to be arrested under the law, charged with "subversion of state power" and building ties with foreign media outlets and groups that threaten China.[^cite_note-\:1-7] His trial was held on September 16.[^cite_note-10]

The European Union issued a statement condemning Au's arrest.[^cite_note-\:1-7]
