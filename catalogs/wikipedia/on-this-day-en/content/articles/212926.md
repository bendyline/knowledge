The **United States secretary of homeland security** is the head of the [United States Department of Homeland Security](58236.md), the [federal department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_federal_executive_departments) tasked with [border control](247927.md), [counterterrorism](83392.md) and other aspects of [public safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_safety) in the [United States](3434750.md). The secretary is a member of the [Cabinet of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_United_States). The position was created by the [Homeland Security Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeland_Security_Act) following the [terrorist attacks of September 11, 2001](5058690.md).

The new department consisted primarily of components transferred from other Cabinet departments because of their role in [homeland security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeland_security), such as the [Coast Guard](32223.md), the [Federal Protective Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Protective_Service_\(United_States\)), [U.S. Customs and Border Protection](757040.md) (which includes the [United States Border Patrol](567453.md)), [U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Immigration_and_Customs_Enforcement) (which includes Homeland Security Investigations), the [United States Secret Service](58221.md), the [Transportation Security Administration](172933.md) and the [Federal Emergency Management Agency](73797.md).

The current homeland security secretary is [Markwayne Mullin](37205890.md), who was sworn in on March 24, 2026.

## Order of succession

While appointment of acting officials is generally governed by the [Federal Vacancies Reform Act of 1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Vacancies_Reform_Act_of_1998) (FVRA), the [Homeland Security Act of 2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeland_Security_Act) creates exceptions to FVRA, mandating that the [under secretary of homeland security for management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_Secretary_of_Homeland_Security_for_Management) is third in the line of succession for Secretary of Homeland Security,[^cite_note-2] and establishes an alternate process by which the secretary can directly establish a line of succession outside the provisions of the FVRA.[^cite_note-\:8-3]

As of November 8, 2019, the order of succession is as follows.[^cite_note-\:0-4] However, the [legality of this update was challenged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%932021_Department_of_Homeland_Security_appointment_disputes).[^cite_note-\:8-3][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Office of the Secretary of Homeland Security

Main article: [Office of the Secretary of Homeland Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_the_Secretary_of_Homeland_Security)

The Office of the Secretary (OS) oversees the execution of the duties of the Department of Homeland Security.[^cite_note-\:1-8] Certain elements also aid the secretary of homeland security and senior officials of the Department of Homeland Security, as well as private sector and government partners in their duties.

### Composition

The Office of the Secretary contains several offices and other elements of the DHS.[^cite_note-\:1-8] Most of the heads of these elements report directly to the secretary or deputy secretary, but the military advisor and executive secretary report to the **DHS chief of staff**, a position that is currently vacant since January 2025.

While DHS secretary [Kristi Noem](27663933.md) was participating in an immigration raid on April 8, 2025, she was accompanied by former Trump campaign manager and senior adviser [Corey Lewandowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corey_Lewandowski), who introduced himself to the federal agents as “chief of staff.” DHS later clarified that he is an adviser to DHS and a special government employee.[^cite_note-9]
