The **Constitution of the Arab Republic of Egypt** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): دستور مصر, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Dustūr Miṣr*) is the fundamental law of [Egypt](8087628.md). The Egyptian Constitution of 2014 was passed in a [referendum in January 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Egyptian_constitutional_referendum).[^cite_note-1] The constitution took effect after the results were announced on 18 January 2014. A [constitutional amendments referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Egyptian_constitutional_referendum) was held from 20 to 22 April 2019.[^cite_note-2]

## Background

In July 2013, after the [ousting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Egyptian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) of former President [Mohammed Morsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_Morsi), the [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_Armed_Forces) announced the schedule for the development of the constitution, with the vote to occur around the end of November 2013.[^cite_note-3] Two different committees were involved in amending the [2012 constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_Constitution_of_2012).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The constitution replaces the [Egyptian Constitution of 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_Constitution_of_2012) which came into effect under Morsi.[^cite_note-BBC-6]

## Contents

The constitution adopted in 2014, like the constitution drafted under Morsi, is based on the [Egyptian Constitution of 1971](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_Constitution_of_1971).[^cite_note-7]

The 2014 constitution sets up a president and parliament.[^cite_note-BBC-6] The president is elected to a six-year term and may serve one term.[^cite_note-BBC-6] The parliament may impeach the president.[^cite_note-BBC-6] Under the constitution, there is a guarantee of equality between the sexes and an absolute freedom of belief, but Islam is the state religion.[^cite_note-BBC-6] The military retains the ability to appoint the national Minister of Defense for the next 8 years.[^cite_note-BBC-6] Under the constitution, political parties may not be based on "religion, race, gender or geography";[^cite_note-BBC-6] the law regarding Egyptian political parties that regulated the [2011–2012 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011%E2%80%9312_Egyptian_parliamentary_election) included a similar clause prohibiting religious parties, though it was not enforced.[^cite_note-8] The document, whilst it does proclaim an absolute [freedom of expression](6184863.md), that freedom is often subject to exceptions leading to legal consequences often targeting public supporters of the [LGBTQ community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_rights_in_Egypt).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-NYT17Jan-11] The constitution protects texts pertaining to presidency terms, freedoms and equality from being amended in an [entrenched clause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrenched_clause) in article 226, except with more guarantees.[^cite_note-12]

## Reception

In 2014, the constitution was criticized by the [Revolutionary Socialists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_Socialists_\(Egypt\))[^cite_note-13] and the Road of the Revolution Front,[^cite_note-14] who perceived it as leaving too much power in the hands of the military.
