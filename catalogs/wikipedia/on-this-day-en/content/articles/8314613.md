# Parental consent

Main article: [Consent](215934.md)

**Parental consent** [laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislation) (also known as **parental involvement** laws)[^cite_note-1] in some countries require that one or more [parents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parent) [consent](215934.md) to or be notified before their [minor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_\(law\)) [child](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child)[^cite_note-2] can legally engage in certain activities.

**Parental consent** may refer to:

- A parent's right to give consent, or be informed, before their minor child undergoes [medical treatment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine).

  [^cite_note-3]

  &#x20;See [informed consent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Informed_consent) for such legislation in general, or [minors and abortion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minors_and_abortion)

  [^cite_note-4]

  &#x20;for legislation relating specifically to [abortion](765.md).

  [^cite_note-5]

* Some jurisdictions stop short of requiring parental consent for abortion but require **parental notification**.

  [^cite_note-6]

- The [parental rights movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parental_rights_movement), which seeks to require parents to either be notified or give consent if their minor child wishes to go by a different [name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_name) or different [gender pronouns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_pronouns) at school.

- A parent's right to give consent before their minor child undergoes [body modification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_modification) such as [piercing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_piercing) or [tattooing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tattoo).

  [^cite_note-7]

- A parent's right to consent to their minor child [marrying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage) before they reach [marriageable age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriageable_age).

- A parent's right to be involved in their minor child's [education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education), including the right to approve or disapprove of certain [curricula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curriculum), or to consent to [extracurricular activities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extracurricular_activities) and [field trips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_trip).

  [^cite_note-8]
