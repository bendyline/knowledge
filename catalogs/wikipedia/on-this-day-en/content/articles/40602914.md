This is a **list of [unmanned aerial vehicles](58900.md) of [China](5405.md)**.

Chinese companies are leaders in the global civilian drone industry and China is the second largest drone market in the world, after the United States.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] In late of 2010, there were more than a hundred Chinese UAV developers/manufacturers.[^cite_note-3] Chinese manufacturer [DJI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJI) held three-quarters of consumer market share in 2017.[^cite_note-4] In 2018, DJI was dominant, followed by Chinese company [Yuneec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuneec_International), US company [3D Robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_Robotics) and French company [Parrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parrot_SA).[^cite_note-5]
