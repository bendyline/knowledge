**Abu Zabad** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): أبو زبد) is a locality in [West Kordofan](767647.md), [Sudan](27421.md).[^cite_note-1]

## History

On 7 June 2025, eight people were killed in an SAF drone attack on a school sheltering refugees in Abu Zabad.[^cite_note-2]
