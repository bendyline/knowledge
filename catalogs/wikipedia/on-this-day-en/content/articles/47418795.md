# Trillionaire

For other uses, see [Trillionaire (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trillionaire_\(disambiguation\)).



In June 2026, businessman and entrepreneur [Elon Musk](909036.md) became the only person to accumulate a net worth of US\$1 trillion, but his net worth has been below \$1 trillion since July 2026.

A **trillionaire** is a person whose [net worth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_worth) is at least one [trillion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1,000,000,000,000) units of a given [currency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currency),[^cite_note-1] typically the [US dollar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_dollar) due to its status as a [world currency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_currency).[^cite_note-2] A trillionaire is the highest tier of [ultra-high-net-worth individuals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra-high-net-worth_individual).[^cite_note-3] In June 2026, [Elon Musk](909036.md) became the only US dollar trillionaire in history upon the [initial public offering of his company](82660746.md), [SpaceX](832774.md), but Musk's net worth has been under \$1 trillion since early July 2026, after a SpaceX stock collapse wiped hundreds of billions from his net worth.[^cite_note-\:3-4]

## Etymology

The earliest evidence of the word *trillionaire*, according to the *[Oxford English Dictionary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary)*, is from a [letter to the editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_to_the_editor) published in an 1861 issue of the *Huddersfield Chronicle and West Yorkshire Advertiser*.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] *[The New York Times](30680.md)* first used the word in 1922, in reference to "trillionaires in [rubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_ruble)" in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union).[^cite_note-7]

### Predictions before 2026

In 1999, Evan L. Marcus of *[Wired](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wired_\(magazine\))* provided four scenarios in which [Microsoft](19001.md) founder [Bill Gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Gates) becomes not only the first trillionaire in US dollars, but also the first quadrillionaire (having a net worth of one thousand trillions or one [quadrillion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrillion)).[^cite_note-10] In 2005, astrophysicist [Neil deGrasse Tyson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_deGrasse_Tyson) also said that Gates would become the first trillionaire.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTETyson200522-11] In 2017, a report from international charity [Oxfam](19698439.md) predicted that the first trillionaire would emerge within 25 years, pointing to increasing global [wealth inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wealth_inequality) and individuals such as Gates.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEHardoon20175,_20-12]

In 2008, [Peter Diamandis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Diamandis), founder of the [Xprize foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xprize_Foundation), predicted that the first trillionaire would be made in the [space industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_industry) by [asteroid mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asteroid_mining).[^cite_note-\:1-13] In 2015, Tyson predicted the same,[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-\:1-13] as did US senator [Ted Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Cruz) in 2018.[^cite_note-\:1-13] Musk said in 2003 that asteroid mining was "bogus", and has not publicly updated that view since then,[^cite_note-\:1-13] and in 2018 Andrew Glester of *[Physics World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics_World)* questioned the feasibility of precious metal extraction as well as of the effect on prices that a sudden influx of a supply of precious metals might have.[^cite_note-\:1-13]

Entrepreneur [Mark Cuban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Cuban) predicted in 2017 that the first trillionaire would make their fortune from [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_in_industry).[^cite_note-15]

A study by Approve, a platform for finance teams, predicted in 2022 that Musk would become the world's first trillionaire by 2024.[^cite_note-\:0-16] The same study also predicted nine other individuals would become trillionaires after Musk: [Gautam Adani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautam_Adani) in 2025, [Zhang Yiming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Yiming) in 2026, [Bernard Arnault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_Arnault) in 2029, [Mukesh Ambani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukesh_Ambani) in 2029, [Jeff Bezos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Bezos) in 2030, [Larry Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Page) in 2032, [Sergey Brin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Brin) in 2032, [Steve Ballmer](29656.md) in 2032, and [Michael Dell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Dell) in 2033.[^cite_note-\:0-16]

A study by wealth-tracking service [Informa Connect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Informa) in 2024 predicted Musk would become a trillionaire in 2027, Adani in 2028, [Prajogo Pangestu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prajogo_Pangestu) in 2028, [Jensen Huang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang) in 2028, [Mark Zuckerberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Zuckerberg) in 2030, Arnault (and his family) in 2030, and [Phil Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Knight) (and his family) in 2030.[^cite_note-17]

### First US dollar trillionaire

Main article: [Wealth of Elon Musk](76211626.md)

On June 12, 2026, with the [initial public offering of Musk's company SpaceX](82660746.md) at a record valuation of nearly US\$1.8 trillion, he became the only person whose [net worth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_worth) has exceeded \$1 trillion.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] His ownership of 42% of SpaceX shares, combined with his multi-billion-dollar holdings in [Tesla, Inc.](5533631.md) and [other companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_career_of_Elon_Musk), brought him over the threshold upon the IPO.[^cite_note-22] His net worth reached an intraday peak of \$1.45 trillion in the week after the IPO,[^cite_note-23] but by late July 2026, a SpaceX stock collapse had reduced his net worth by \$750 billion, the largest loss in history.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-\:3-4][^cite_note-25]

Musk's status as the first US dollar trillionaire drew historical comparisons, acclaim, and criticism. The *[New York Times](30680.md)* drew the connection between Musk and [John D. Rockefeller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_D._Rockefeller), who became the first US dollar billionaire in 1916.[^cite_note-Wealth_of_Elon_Musk_\:0-27] Rockefeller's wealth was estimated at \$900 million in 1913, which was 2.3% of the US gross domestic product (GDP);[^cite_note-28] when Musk's wealth was \$970 billion on June 11, 2026 due to the pricing of SpaceX at \$135 per share, it was 3% of the US GDP.[^cite_note-29] When Musk became a trillionaire, he was nearly four times as wealthy as the second richest person, [Larry Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Page).[^cite_note-30] Left leaning American political figures such as [Elizabeth Warren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Warren)[^cite_note-Wealth_of_Elon_Musk_\:1-31] and [Zohran Mamdani](64618347.md)[^cite_note-32] used the milestone to call for the implementation of a [wealth tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wealth_tax).

The emergence of a trillionaire has prompted criticism from groups concerned with [global wealth inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_of_wealth#Wealth_inequality).[^cite_note-33] In June 2026, Oxfam said that a \$1 trillion fortune would make Musk wealthier than the combined net worth of the poorest 46% of the [world's population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World's_population), or 3.8 billion people.[^cite_note-\:2-34] Oxfam described the milestone as an example of extreme wealth concentration and called for higher taxation of ultra-high-net-worth individuals.[^cite_note-\:2-34] Economist [Gabriel Zucman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Zucman) argued that extreme wealth will always lead to extreme power and threatens the very possibility of democracy.[^cite_note-35]

## In other currencies



100 trillion [Zimbabwean dollars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwean_dollar_\(1980%E2%80%932009\)), an example of [hyperinflation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperinflation)

People have been considered trillionaires in other currencies, often due to [hyperinflation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperinflation), such as the [Hungarian pengő](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_peng%C5%91) in 1946 and the [Zimbabwean dollar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwean_dollar_\(1980%E2%80%932009\)) in 2008.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37]

The net worth of people outside the US is often calculated in their respective currencies, creating [dong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_%C4%91%E1%BB%93ng) trillionaires,[^cite_note-38] [ruble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_ruble) trillionaires,[^cite_note-39] [peso](147253.md) trillionaires,[^cite_note-40] [yen](34392.md) trillionaires,[^cite_note-41] and others.
