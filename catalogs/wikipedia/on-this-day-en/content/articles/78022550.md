***Music by John Williams*** is a 2024 American documentary film about the composer and conductor [John Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Williams), produced and directed by [Laurent Bouzereau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurent_Bouzereau).&#x20;

## Background and synopsis

The film explores the life and career of John Williams. Williams composed music for many films throughout his career, including the *[Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars)* series, the *[Indiana Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_Jones)* series, *[E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.T._the_Extra-Terrestrial)*. The film contains interviews with many of the directors and cast members Williams has collaborated with, including [Steven Spielberg](26940.md), [George Lucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lucas), [Ron Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Howard), [ Chris Columbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Columbus_\(filmmaker\)), [Kate Capshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Capshaw), [Ke Huy Quan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ke_Huy_Quan), [Kathleen Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathleen_Kennedy_\(producer\)), [Itzhak Perlman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itzhak_Perlman), and [J. J. Abrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._Abrams), alongside with Williams's close colleagues composers, such as [David Newman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Newman_\(composer\)), [Thomas Newman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Newman) and [Alan Silvestri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Silvestri), who also worked on Steven Spielberg's various projects.

## Release

Interviews given at the film's premiere

*Music by John Williams* opened the 2024 [AFI Fest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFI_Fest) on October 23. It was given a [limited release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_theatrical_release) and premiered on [Disney+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney+) on November 1.[^cite_note-IW-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Reception

### Critical response

On the [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator) website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), [100%](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_films_with_a_100%25_rating_on_Rotten_Tomatoes) of 49 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 8.1/10. The website's consensus reads: "A warm retrospective on a modern master, this documentary is a symphonic treat for anyone who loves music in the movies -- or just plain great music."[^cite_note-4] [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic), which uses a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean), assigned the film a score of 74 out of 100, based on 12 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.[^cite_note-Metacritic-5]

Peter Debruge of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\))* wrote, "It is not a documentary so much as a tribute, a tool for fans designed to celebrate Williams' legacy without getting too personal or technical in the process."[^cite_note-6]

Matt Goldberg of *[TheWrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TheWrap)* wrote, "While I wish the film got more into the weeds of where Williams and his work exists in comparison to those who preceded and those who followed him, this is still the kind of inoffensive celebratory piece that will have you eager to revisit his most beloved scores while gaining a bit more insight into their creation."[^cite_note-7]

### Accolades

With his Grammy win for [Best Music Film](586170.md) at the [68th Grammy Awards](79128292.md), [Steven Spielberg](26940.md) became the 22nd competitive [EGOT](361388.md) winner.[^cite_note-8]
