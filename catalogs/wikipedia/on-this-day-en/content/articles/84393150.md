The announcement of [President](33730924.md) [Guy Parmelin](48772769.md)'s resignation from the [Federal Council](218299.md) on 2 October, effective on 31 December, triggered a by-election that will take place on 9 December 2026. The [People's Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_People's_Party) announced that candidates have until 23 October to nominate themselves as a candidate.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

As per an informal agreement between the major political parties known as the [magic formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_formula_\(Swiss_politics\)), the only candidates will be from Parmelin's party, the People's Party. His successor will take over the leadership of the [Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research (EAER)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Department_of_Economic_Affairs,_Education_and_Research).
