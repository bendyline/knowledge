**Al Hadath** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الحدث, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'The Event') is a Saudi Arabian [state-owned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_ownership) Arabic-language interactive television [news channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_broadcasting) that focuses primarily on political developments in the [Arab world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_world). It operates as a sister channel to [Al Arabiya](321645.md) and serves as an extension of its coverage, offering in-depth reports and live updates on breaking news events.[^cite_note-1]

## Overview

The channel is accessible internationally and is available in the United Kingdom via the [Vision TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vision_TV_Network) streaming platform on the British [Freeview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeview_\(UK\)) service. From 28 March 2022, it became available alongside Al Arabiya on Freeview channel 273.[^cite_note-2] Al Hadath also operates country-specific social media pages such as Al Hadath Yemen and Al Hadath Egypt on platforms including X (formerly [Twitter](9988187.md)) and [Facebook](7529378.md), offering localized news and updates.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

The [Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boycott,_Divestment_and_Sanctions) (BDS) movement's Arabic-language account published a call to boycott al-Hadath along with its sister channel [Al Arabiya](321645.md) for being what they called "the mouthpieces of the Israeli enemy that speak Arabic".[^cite_note-5]
