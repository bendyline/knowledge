A **Spanish passport** ([Spanish](26825.md): *pasaporte español*) is an identity document issued to Spanish citizens with right of abode in the [Iberian mainland](42178382.md), [Ceuta](6443.md), [Melilla](20226.md), [Balearic Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balearic_Islands) and [Canary Islands](5717.md), for travel outside [Spain](26667.md). Every Spanish citizen is also a [citizen of the European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_of_the_European_Union). The [passport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passport), along with the [national identity card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Documento_Nacional_de_Identidad_\(Spain\)), allows for free rights of movement and residence in any of the states of the [European Union](9317.md), [European Economic Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Economic_Area), and [Switzerland](26748.md).

Spanish citizens have visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 185 countries and territories, which places Spanish passport holders is in the 4th-ranking group in the world according to February 2026 [Henley Passport Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henley_Passport_Index).[^cite_note-2]

## Types

- *Ordinary Passport* ([Spanish](26825.md): *Pasaporte ordinario*) – issued for ordinary travel, such as vacations and business trips

- *Collective Passport* ([Spanish](26825.md): *Pasaporte colectivo*) – issued for the occasion of pilgrimages, excursions and other acts of analogous nature, whenever reciprocity with the destination country exists; its validity is limited a single trip, whose duration will not be able to exceed three months.

- *Diplomatic Passport* ([Spanish](26825.md): *Pasaporte diplomático*) – issued to Spanish diplomats, top-ranking government officials, and [diplomatic couriers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_courier).

- *Official and Service Passports* ([Spanish](26825.md): *Pasaportes oficiales y de servicio*) – issued to individuals representing the Spanish government on official business

As of 3 February 2026, Spanish passport holders had visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 185 (compared to 192 as the most) countries and territories, placing the ordinary Spanish passport in the fourth ranking group according to the number of destinations that their holders can access without a prior visa.[^cite_note-3]

Spanish citizens can live and work in any country within the EU due to the right of free movement and residence granted in [Article 21 of the EU Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaties_of_the_European_Union).[^cite_note-4]
