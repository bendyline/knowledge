**Shane Lowry** (born 2 April 1987) is an Irish [professional golfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_golfer) who plays on the [European Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Tour) and the [PGA Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PGA_Tour). He has won one [major championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_championship_\(golf\)), the [2019 Open Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Open_Championship).&#x20;

Lowry won the [Irish Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Open_\(golf\)) in 2009 as an amateur. He turned professional and accepted European Tour membership afterwards. Lowry earned PGA Tour status by winning the [2015 WGC-Bridgestone Invitational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_WGC-Bridgestone_Invitational) and began to play primarily in the United States. He claimed his first major title at the 2019 Open Championship. Lowry won the [BMW PGA Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMW_PGA_Championship), the European Tour's flagship event, in 2022. He added a second Irish Open title in 2026, with a record eleven-shot margin.

Lowry played for the Irish teams which won the [European Amateur Team Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Amateur_Team_Championship) in both [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_European_Amateur_Team_Championship) and [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_European_Amateur_Team_Championship). He has represented Europe at the [Ryder Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryder_Cup) in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Ryder_Cup), [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Ryder_Cup), and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Ryder_Cup); Lowry holed the decisive putt at the latter event.

## Early life

Born in the [Midland Regional Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midland_Regional_Hospital,_Mullingar), [Mullingar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mullingar), County Westmeath,[^cite_note-et-1] Ireland, Lowry grew up in [Clara, County Offaly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clara,_County_Offaly). He is the son of [Brendan Lowry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Lowry), who won the [1982 All-Ireland Senior Football Championship Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship_Final) with [Offaly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offaly_county_football_team).[^cite_note-3] Shane Lowry maintained his links with Offaly as his golfing career advanced, saying in 2021: "But any time I get the chance to go to [O'Connor Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O'Connor_Park) and watch Offaly play, I do and I am the first to give out if they lose and I am sitting in the stand."[^cite_note-4]

Lowry learned his early golf at Esker Hills Golf Club, where he began his amateur career.[^cite_note-5] He attended [Athlone Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athlone_Institute_of_Technology) as a scholarship student on the Higher Certificate in Sport and Recreation.

## Amateur career

Lowry was the 2007 Irish Amateur Close Champion, defeating Niall Turner 4 and 3 in the final.[^cite_note-6]

Lowry was part of the Irish teams that won the [European Amateur Team Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Amateur_Team_Championship) in [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_European_Amateur_Team_Championship) and [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_European_Amateur_Team_Championship), on the first occasion in the same team as [Rory McIlroy](3949844.md).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

While still an amateur in May [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_European_Tour), he won the [Irish Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Open_\(golf\)) at [County Louth Golf Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Louth_Golf_Club) on the [European Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Tour), defeating [Robert Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Rock) on the third hole of a sudden death playoff. The win, on his tour debut, made him just the third amateur to win on the European Tour,[^cite_note-9] following [Danny Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Lee_\(golfer\)) earlier in the [2009 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_European_Tour), and [Pablo Martín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_Mart%C3%ADn_\(golfer\)) in [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_European_Tour). Lowry shot a 62 to equal the lowest ever by an amateur on the circuit and led from the second round onwards.[^cite_note-Lowry_ponders_his_options-10]



Lowry celebrating his win at the 2009 [Irish Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Open_\(golf\)) at [County Louth Golf Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Louth_Golf_Club)&#x20;

With this win, he entered the [Official World Golf Ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_World_Golf_Ranking) as an amateur at No. 168.[^cite_note-11] His highest position in the [World Amateur Golf Ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Amateur_Golf_Ranking) was No. 3.[^cite_note-12] [Walker Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walker_Cup) captain Colin Dagleish described the win as "fairytale stuff", adding: "You'd have to say that Shane's victory was the biggest (of the three amateur wins). To win your own national Open is quite something. It was unbelievable, it really was."[^cite_note-Lowry_ponders_his_options-10] The win was only the second home victory since 1982 and the first since [Pádraig Harrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A1draig_Harrington) in 2007.[^cite_note-Lowry_ponders_his_options-10] Harrington himself was also full of praise: "It's fabulous for Irish golf. You only have to look at the fact it is such a rarity for an amateur to win, such a rarity for an Irish player to win the Irish Open. So, on a lot of fronts, it is a big deal. It was very impressive."[^cite_note-13]

## Professional career

### European Tour

Lowry elected to turn professional the week after his Irish Open triumph, meaning that he would miss out on the opportunity to play in the [Walker Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walker_Cup) in September 2009.[^cite_note-14] He made his professional début on 28 May at the [European Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Open_\(golf\)), where he shot 78 in the first round to end the opening day almost at the back of the field, and went on to miss the cut following a second round 73.[^cite_note-15]

Lowry missed the first three cuts of his professional career, but had his first payday on 5 July, when he finished 50th at the [Open de France Alstom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_de_France_Alstom).[^cite_note-16] He had his best professional result in 2009 in November, when he finished 3rd in the [Dunlop Phoenix Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunlop_Phoenix_Tournament) in Japan.[^cite_note-17] In January [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_European_Tour), Lowry finished in fourth place in the [Abu Dhabi Golf Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Dhabi_Golf_Championship),[^cite_note-18] moving him into the top-100 of the [Official World Golf Ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_World_Golf_Ranking).[^cite_note-19]



Lowry at the 2010 [KLM Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KLM_Open)

In June 2010, Lowry qualified for his first [major championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men's_major_golf_championships), the [Open Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Open_Championship) at [St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Course_at_St_Andrews). During the qualification, Lowry equalled [Nick Faldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Faldo)'s course record at [Sunningdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunningdale_Golf_Club) when he posted a 62. Lowry went on to make the cut at St Andrews and eventually finished in a tie for 37th place.

Lowry won for the first time as a professional in [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_European_Tour), at the [Portugal Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal_Masters) in October. He shot a 67 to tie for the lead with the Englishman [Ross Fisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Fisher), who made bogey on the final hole after missing a four-foot putt. The victory lifted Lowry back into the Official World Golf Ranking top 100 and made him eligible to compete in the [WGC-HSBC Champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WGC-HSBC_Champions).

In [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_European_Tour), Lowry was runner-up at the [BMW PGA Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_BMW_PGA_Championship) in May and later tied for ninth at the [Open Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Open_Championship) at [Royal Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Liverpool_Golf_Club) (Hoylake), his best finish at a major.[^cite_note-20]

In August [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_European_Tour), Lowry won the [WGC-Bridgestone Invitational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_WGC-Bridgestone_Invitational) in the [United States](3434750.md) at [Firestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firestone_Country_Club) in [Akron, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akron,_Ohio), two strokes ahead of runner-up [Bubba Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubba_Watson).[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

### PGA Tour

Lowry accepted PGA Tour membership for the [2015–16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_PGA_Tour) season.

At the [2016 U.S. Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_U.S._Open_\(golf\)) at [Oakmont Country Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakmont_Country_Club) near [Pittsburgh](25101.md), Lowry shot 65 (−5) in the third round for 203 (−7) and a four-stroke advantage over [Dustin Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dustin_Johnson) and [Andrew Landry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Landry) at the 54-hole stage. The 65 was Lowry's joint career best round at a major championship. Due to weather delays on Thursday, the third round was not completed until early on Sunday, and Lowry birdied two of his remaining four holes to extend his two stroke overnight lead to four. In the final round he was in contention for the title, within one shot of Dustin Johnson after 12 holes, before three consecutive bogeys on holes 14, 15 and 16 ended his chances. He finished the tournament tied for second place, with a final round score of 76.

Lowry did not win on the [European Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Tour) from 2016 to 2018 but was joint runner-up in the 2017 [DP World Tour Championship, Dubai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DP_World_Tour_Championship,_Dubai) and runner-up in the 2018 [Andalucía Valderrama Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaluc%C3%ADa_Valderrama_Masters).

Lowry started the 2018–19 season with partial status after finishing 140th in the FedEx Cup.

In January 2019, Lowry won the [Abu Dhabi HSBC Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Dhabi_HSBC_Championship) by one shot over [Richard Sterne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Sterne_\(golfer\)). He led by 3 strokes after three rounds but was two-over-par after 11 holes of his final round and dropped 4 shots behind Sterne. However he finished with 3 birdies in the final 7 holes, while Sterne had two bogeys, giving Lowry the victory.[^cite_note-23]

In July, Lowry moved into a share of the 36-hole lead at the [2019 Open Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Open_Championship) at [Royal Portrush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Portrush), after successive opening rounds of 67. He was tied at eight under with [J. B. Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._B._Holmes). Lowry then shot a 63 for a new course record to lead by four strokes at the end of the third round.[^cite_note-24] He won the tournament by six shots ahead of [Tommy Fleetwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Fleetwood).[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] Lowry held the Claret Jug for two years due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md) postponing the 2020 edition of the Open Championship.

In September 2021, Lowry played on the European team in the [2021 Ryder Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Ryder_Cup) at [Whistling Straits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whistling_Straits) in [Kohler, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohler,_Wisconsin). The U.S. team won 19–9 and Lowry went 1–2–0 including a loss in his Sunday singles match against [Patrick Cantlay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Cantlay).

In February 2022, Lowry finished runner-up at [The Honda Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Honda_Classic). [Sepp Straka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sepp_Straka) birdied the final hole to finish one shot ahead of him.[^cite_note-28] In April, Lowry held the lead during the final round of the [RBC Heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RBC_Heritage), however a double-bogey on the back nine, saw him finish one shot short of the playoff between [Patrick Cantlay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Cantlay) and [Jordan Spieth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Spieth).[^cite_note-29] In September, Lowry won the [BMW PGA Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMW_PGA_Championship) at the [Wentworth Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wentworth_Club). He shot a final-round 65 after the event had been shortened to 54 holes. He beat [Rory McIlroy](3949844.md) and [Jon Rahm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Rahm) (who shot a 62 in the final round) by one stroke for his sixth European Tour victory.[^cite_note-30]

In September 2023, Lowry played on the European team in the [2023 Ryder Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Ryder_Cup) at [Marco Simone Golf and Country Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Simone_Golf_and_Country_Club) in [Guidonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guidonia_Montecelio), [Rome](25458.md), [Italy](14532.md). The European team won 16.5–11.5 and Lowry went 1–1–1 including a half in his Sunday singles match against [Jordan Spieth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Spieth).

In the third round of the [2024 PGA Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_PGA_Championship), Lowry shot a record-tying 62, only the fifth in men's major history.[^cite_note-31]

In September 2025, Lowry was part of the European team in the [2025 Ryder Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Ryder_Cup) at [Bethpage Black Course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethpage_Black_Course) in [Long Island, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Island,_New_York). The European team won 15–13. Lowry birdied a six-and-a-half footer at the 18th hole in his singles match against [Russell Henley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Henley) to win the half point that ensured that Europe would retain the trophy, which they went on to win.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33]

Lowry held the lead at the [Dubai Invitational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_Invitational) in January 2026. On the final hole, he hit his third shot from a greenside bunker into the water. He made double bogey and finished two shots behind the winner [Nacho Elvira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nacho_Elvira).[^cite_note-34] He said afterwards: "A disaster, that's what that was. ... It's not good enough. It's been a few years since I've won. It's hard to get over the line and I should have, but I didn't."[^cite_note-35] In March, Lowry had a three-shot lead with three holes remaining at the [Cognizant Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognizant_Classic). He made double bogey on both the 16th and 17th holes to finish runner-up, two strokes behind [Nico Echavarria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_Echavarria). Lowry said: "I had the tournament in my hands, and I threw it away. What more can I say? That's twice this year now so far. 'm getting good at it."[^cite_note-36]

Lowry made a hole in one during the third round of the [2026 Masters Tournament](82215987.md), at the 190-yard (170 m), par-3 6th hole.[^cite_note-37] He entered the final round in fourth place, two shots behind the co-leaders [Rory McIlroy](3949844.md) and [Cameron Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Young). Lowry faded with a final-round 80 to finish tied-30th.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

At the [Amgen Irish Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amgen_Irish_Open) in September 2026, Lowry shot 23-under 257 at [Trump Doonbeg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_Doonbeg) to win his national open for the second time. He finished eleven shots ahead of runners-up [Joaquín Niemann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joaqu%C3%ADn_Niemann) and [Patrick Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Reed) and broke the tournament record for largest margin of victory, previously set by [Bernhard Langer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernhard_Langer)'s ten-shot win in 1987.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

## Personal life

Lowry married Wendy Honner in 2016 and has two daughters.[^cite_note-42] He is Catholic.[^cite_note-43]

During tournaments he often wears black and white clothes, which are the colours of his [hometown's Gaelic sports club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clara_GAA_\(Offaly\)).[^cite_note-44]

Lowry announced a five-year partnership with [Offaly GAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offaly_GAA) in April 2021.[^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46]

Lowry is a lifelong supporter of [Manchester United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_Football_Club).[^cite_note-47]
