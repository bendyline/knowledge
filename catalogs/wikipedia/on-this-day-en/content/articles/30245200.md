**Ntabankulu Municipality** ([Xhosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xhosa_language): *uMasipala wase Ntabankulu*) is a [local municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_municipality_\(South_Africa\)) within the [Alfred Nzo District Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Nzo_District_Municipality), in the [Wild Coast Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Coast_Region,_Eastern_Cape) of the [Eastern Cape](313645.md) province of [South Africa](17416221.md). *Ntabankulu* is an [isiXhosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xhosa_language) word meaning "great or big mountain", since the municipal area is mountainous in character.[^cite_note-3]

It was formerly part of the [OR Tambo District Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OR_Tambo_District_Municipality), but was transferred to the Alfred Nzo District Municipality after the [2011 municipal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_South_African_municipal_election).[^cite_note-4]

## Politics

Main article: [Ntabankulu Local Municipality elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ntabankulu_Local_Municipality_elections)

The municipal council consists of thirty-eight members elected by [mixed-member proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed-member_proportional_representation). Nineteen councillors are elected by [first-past-the-post voting](261709.md) in nineteen [wards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_\(South_Africa\)), while the remaining nineteen are chosen from [party lists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_list) so that the total number of party representatives is proportional to the number of votes received. In the [election of 1 November 2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_South_African_municipal_elections) the [African National Congress](2503.md) (ANC) won a majority of thirty-one seats on the council.
