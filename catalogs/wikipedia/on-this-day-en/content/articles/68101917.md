**Adelante Andalucía** (English: "Forward Andalusia") is an [Andalusian nationalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andalusian_nationalism) and [regionalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regionalism_\(politics\)) political party founded by former [Podemos Andalusia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podemos_Andalusia) leader [Teresa Rodríguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teresa_Rodr%C3%ADguez). The party was established as a refoundation from the late [Adelante Andalucía electoral alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelante_Andaluc%C3%ADa_\(2018\)) formed by Podemos and [United Left/The Greens–Assembly for Andalusia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Left/The_Greens%E2%80%93Assembly_for_Andalusia).[^cite_note-2]

After announcing their intention to leave Podemos in February 2020, Rodríguez and the rest of the ex-Podemos parliamentarians affiliated with [Anti-capitalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anticapitalistas) (Anticapitalistas) were subject to an expulsion from their parliamentary group in October 2020.[^cite_note-3] The party was eventually refounded as a left-wing nationalist organisation in June 2021,[^cite_note-4] with the participation of Anticapitalistas, Andalusian Spring (Primavera Andaluza), the Party of the Andalusian Left (IzAnd) and Defend Andalusia (Defender Andalucía).[^cite_note-5]

## Ideology

### Andalusian nationalism & regionalism

Adelante Andalucía's core identity is rooted in [Andalusian nationalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andalusian_nationalism) and [regionalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regionalism_\(politics\)), advocating for the self-determination, distinct cultural identity, and political autonomy of Andalusia.[^cite_note-IdeologíaPartidista2026-6][^cite_note-IV-Ideology-7] It views Andalusian nationalism as a tool for social emancipation and [regional economic equity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Spanish_autonomous_communities_by_median_income). While Andalusia is the [most populated autonomous region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranked_lists_of_Spanish_autonomous_communities) in Spain,[^cite_note-8] it is 13th in median income.[^cite_note-9]

### Anti-capitalism

The party maintains a staunch anti-capitalist stance, criticizing standard [neoliberal economic policies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neoliberalism). It focuses on addressing [regional unemployment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unemployment_in_Spain), precarious [labor conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labor_movement_in_Spain), and the historical economic marginalization of Andalusia relative to wealthier northern Spanish regions.[^cite_note-10] As of 2024, Andalusia had the 3rd highest unemployment rate in Spain.[^cite_note-11] Its platform champions workers' rights, public ownership of key infrastructure, and strong public services.[^cite_note-IV-Ideology-7]

### Eco-socialism

Adelante Andalucía integrates [environmentalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmentalism) with the [socialist economic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_economics). The party highlights the vulnerability of the Andalusian geography to [climate change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change_in_Spain), [drought](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drought_in_Spain), and [desertification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desertification). Its platform advocates for a [green transition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renewable_energy_in_Spain) that does not penalize the working class, psuhing for [sustainable agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_agriculture), [renewable energy](25784.md) under public control, and pivoting away from an economy overly reliant on [seasonal tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_Spain#Autonomous_Community_by_nights_spent_in_hotel),[^cite_note-IV-Ideology-7][^cite_note-IdeologíaPartidista2026-6] where it ranked 3rd in total tourist hotel nights.[^cite_note-12]

### Feminism

[Feminism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth-wave_feminism_in_Spain) is also structurally embedded into the party's principles. Adelante Andalucía promotes [intersectional feminist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersectional_feminism) policies aimed at closing the [gender wage gap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_wage_gap), fighting [gender-based violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender-based_violence), and ensuring equitable representation both within public institutions and its own internal party governance.[^cite_note-IdeologíaPartidista2026-6]
