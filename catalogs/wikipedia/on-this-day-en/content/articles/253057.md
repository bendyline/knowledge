The **[Tony Award](54741.md) for Best Revival of a Play** has only been awarded since [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/48th_Tony_Awards). Prior to that, plays and musicals were considered together for the [Tony Award for Best Revival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Revival). The award is given to the best non-musical play that has appeared on [Broadway](725252.md) in a previous production, or which is determined to be a "classic" or in the historical or popular repertoire despite not having been produced on Broadway. The award goes to the producers of the play. In addition, as of 2026, authors (if living)[^cite_note-1] are also eligible for the award for shows that are considered revivals but that have not previously appeared on Broadway.[^cite_note-2]
