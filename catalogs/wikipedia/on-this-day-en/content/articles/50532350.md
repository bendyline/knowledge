**No Kwang-chol** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 노광철; [Hanja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanja): 努光鐵; born 1956) is a North Korean soldier, a four-star general, and a member of the political bureau,[^cite_note-1] who served as [Minister of Defence of North Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(North_Korea\)) from 2024 to 2026. He previously held the same office from 2018 to 2019.

## Biography

No was born in 1956.[^cite_note-2]

He was elected as a member of the Supreme People's Assembly following the [2003 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_North_Korean_parliamentary_election) and [2009 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_North_Korean_parliamentary_election). In the [September 2010 Representative Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_Conference_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea), he was elected as an alternate (candidate) member of the [Central Committee of the Workers' Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea). In December 2011, Kim Jong Il died and he was included as one of the members of [his funeral committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_state_funeral_of_Kim_Jong_Il#Funeral_committee).

In June 2018, he was promoted from first vice minister to [Minister of People's Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_People's_Armed_Forces).[^cite_note-3]

On June 12, 2018, [Trump](4848272.md) and [Kim Jong Un](5962511.md) [were meeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_North_Korea%E2%80%93United_States_Singapore_Summit) in [Singapore](27318.md). At the meeting, Trump wanted to shake hands with No, who was accompanying Kim Jong Un in the meeting, but No did not shake hands but saluted Trump. After Trump hesitated, he saluted him and the two shook hands.

On 21 December 2019 he was replaced by [Kim Jong-gwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Jong-gwan). From 12 April 2019 to 12 April 2020 he served as a member of the [State Affairs Commission](50991731.md). Since his dismissal as minister, No reportedly headed a research institute under the [General Staff Department of the Korean People's Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff_Department_of_the_Korean_People's_Army), and was observed attending meetings including the enlarged meetings of the [WPK Central Military Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Military_Commission_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea).[^cite_note-4]

On 8 October 2024, during 11th Session of the 14th Supreme People's Assembly, No Kwang-Chol was reappointed as the minister of Defence.[^cite_note-5]

In November 2024, No Kwang-Chol held talks with his [Russian](25391.md) counterpart, [Andrei Belousov](36245360.md), mere [months](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Month) after North Korean troops entered the [Russo-Ukrainian war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine).[^cite_note-\:0-6]

On August 29, 2026, at a meeting of the Ninth Central Military Commission of the [Workers' Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers'_Party_of_Korea), No Kwang-chol was dismissed from his post.[^cite_note-7] He was succeeded by Kim Song-gi.[^cite_note-8]

## Sanctions

As North Korea's defence minister, No Kwang-Chol has faced multiple [international sanctions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_sanctions) for a variety of reasons, from the [country's nuclear programme](106424.md) to its [human rights abuses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rights_in_North_Korea) to, most notably, [his role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korean_involvement_in_the_Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) in the [Russo-Ukrainian war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine).

On December 16, 2024, No Kwang-Chol was sanctioned by the United States, European Union, and South Korea over the role of North Korean troops in the Russo-Ukrainian war.[^cite_note-\:0-6]

On February 24, 2025, the third anniversary of the 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine, the [British](31717.md) [Foreign Office](59653.md) placed him on a list of UK sanctions over his role in the sending of North Korean troops to fight on Russia's behalf in Ukraine.[^cite_note-\:0-6][^cite_note-9]
