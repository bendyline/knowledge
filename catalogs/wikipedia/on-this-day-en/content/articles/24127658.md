**Doksany** ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): *Doxan*) is a municipality and village in [Litoměřice District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litom%C4%9B%C5%99ice_District) in the [Ústí nad Labem Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%9Ast%C3%AD_nad_Labem_Region) of the [Czech Republic](5321.md). It has about 400 inhabitants. The municipality is located on the [Ohře](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oh%C5%99e) River in the [Lower Ohře Table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Oh%C5%99e_Table). It is known for a [Premonstratensian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premonstratensians) convent.

## Etymology

The origin of the name Doksany is uncertain. There is a theory that the initial name of the settlement was Dokze/Doksy (of pre-Slavic origin, from the [Old English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_English) word *dox* = 'dark') and then it changed to Doksany, modeled after Brozany (now [Brozany nad Ohří](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brozany_nad_Oh%C5%99%C3%AD)), which is located on the opposite bank of the Ohře River.[^cite_note-2]

## Geography

Doksany is located about 9 kilometres (6 mi) south of [Litoměřice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litom%C4%9B%C5%99ice), 24 km (15 mi) south of [Ústí nad Labem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%9Ast%C3%AD_nad_Labem) and 42 km (26 mi) north of [Prague](23844.md). It lies in a flat agricultural landscape in the [Lower Ohře Table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Oh%C5%99e_Table). The municipality is situated on the right bank of the [Ohře](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oh%C5%99e) River, which forms the western municipal border.

### Climate

Doksany's climate is classified as [oceanic climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanic_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Cfb*). Among them, the annual average temperature is 9.7 °C (49.5 °F), the hottest month is 19.9 °C (67.8 °F) in July, and the coldest month is 0.0 °C (32.0 °F) in January. The annual precipitation is 480.6 millimetres (18.92 in), of which July is the wettest with 70.8 millimetres (2.79 in), while February is the driest with only 18.9 millimetres (0.74 in).

The extreme temperature throughout the year ranged from −27.0 °C (−16.6 °F) on 22 December 1969 to 41.9 °C (107.4 °F) on 28 June 2026, which was at this time the highest temperature recorded in the Czech Republic.[^cite_note-3]

## History



Courtyard of the convent complex

The oldest settlement dates back between 4500 and 2500 BC. In 1993, archaeologists discovered more ancient buildings and settlements that dated back to the same period of time. The first written mention of Doksany is from 1151 by chronicler Vincencius, who wrote about the Doksany Convent.[^cite_note-history-8] It was probably founded in 1144 by [Gertrude of Babenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gertrude_of_Babenberg,_Duchess_of_Bohemia) and [Vladislaus II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladislaus_II,_Duke_and_King_of_Bohemia) as the second convent in the kingdom.[^cite_note-9]

The village of Doksany was specifically mentioned in a deed of King [Ottokar I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottokar_I_of_Bohemia) from 1226. The convent owned the village until 1421, when the convent was damaged as a result of the [Hussite Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hussite_Wars). Properties of the convert were then lent by the Bohemian kings to various nobles. During the reign of King [Vladislaus II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladislaus_II_of_Hungary) (1471–1516), the village returned to the ownership of the convent.[^cite_note-history-8]

Doksany was owned by the Doksany Convent until 1782, when the convent was abolished. From 1782, its properties were managed by the Religious Fund. In 1790, Countess Terezie Kinsky acquired Doksany and began the reconstruction of the monastery into a castle. From 1797 to 1804, the estate was owned by Jakub Wiemmer. From 1804 until the establishment of an independent municipality in 1850, the estate was owned by the Lex of Aehrenthal family.[^cite_note-history-8]

In 2003, the convent was restored with the help of [Premonstratensians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premonstratensians) from Poland.[^cite_note-history-8]

## Economy

There is a small hydropower plant on the [Ohře](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oh%C5%99e) River. It is part of a historic [weir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weir) from 1937.[^cite_note-12]

## Transport

There are no railways or major roads passing through Doksany, but the municipality is located near the [D8 motorway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D8_motorway_\(Czech_Republic\)) from Prague to Ústí nad Labem.

## Sights

Doksany is known for a [Premonstratensian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premonstratensians) convent with the Church of the Nativity of Mary. The religious community still lives in it, but under certain conditions it is open to the public.[^cite_note-13]
