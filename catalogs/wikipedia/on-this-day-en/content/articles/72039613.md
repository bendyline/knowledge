**Carl-Oskar Simon Bohlin** (born 8 January 1986) is a Swedish politician who has served as [Minister for Civil Defence](9463365.md#Ministers_for_Civil_Defence) in the [cabinet of Ulf Kristersson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristersson_cabinet) since 2022.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] He is a member of the [Moderate Party](202985.md).

## Early life and education

Bohlin is the son of Magnus Bohlin, professor emeritus at [Dalarna University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalarna_University), and Ingrid Lindberg Bohlin. He went to junior high school in the [Jakobsgårdarna](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakobsg%C3%A5rdarna) area in [Borlänge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borl%C3%A4nge) and then at [Falu Frigymnasium](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falu%20frigymnasium) in [Falun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falun).[^cite_note-daladem20100909-3]

He studied law at [Uppsala University](31801.md) between 2007 and 2012, but does not have a [candidate degree in law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_\(degree\)).[^cite_note-4]

## Political career

Bohlin got involved early in municipal politics in [Borlänge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borl%C3%A4nge) and was elected to the municipality's children's and school board at the age of 18. In February 2005, Bohlin was elected chairman of the [Moderate Youth League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moderate_Youth_League) in Dalarna.[^cite_note-5]

He was elected to the [Riksdag](158651.md) in the [2010 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_general_election,_2010).[^cite_note-6] He was a candidate for the [European Parliament](9581.md) in the [2014 European Parliament election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_European_Parliament_election_in_Sweden) but was not elected.

### Minister for Civil Defence

Following [2022 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_general_election,_2022), Bohlin was appointed [Minister for Civil Defence](9463365.md#Ministers_for_Civil_Defence) (the first since 1947) and [cabinet minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_minister) in the [Ministry of Defence](9463365.md) in the [cabinet of Ulf Kristersson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristersson_cabinet). The ministerial portfolio also includes responsibility for [crisis management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crisis_management) and [civil protection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_protection).

## Personal life

He is married to Sara Kristoffersson (b. 1986) and has two children.[^cite_note-7]

## Politics

In February 2019, the Moderate Party appointed Bohlin acting chairman of the Riksdag Committee on Industry and Trade after the regular chairman, Lars Hjälmered, went on paternity leave. In May 2019, Bohlin became the Moderate Party's new spokesperson for housing policy. In January 2022, it was reported that Bohlin would leave housing policy in mid-February to become chairman of the Committee on Industry and Trade, with responsibility for industry and energy policy.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]
