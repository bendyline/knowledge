# Sex trafficking in Cambodia



Women and girls are sex trafficked in and out of all the [provinces of Cambodia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Cambodia). They are raped and physically and psychologically harmed in brothels, homes, hotels, and other locations throughout these administrative divisions.

[^cite_note-1]

**Sex trafficking in Cambodia** is [human trafficking](19333662.md) for the purpose of [sexual exploitation and slavery](173634.md) that occurs in the [Kingdom of Cambodia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Cambodia). Cambodia is a country of origin, destination and transit for sex trafficked persons.[^cite_note-2]

Sex trafficking victims in the country are from all [ethnic groups in Cambodia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_groups_in_Cambodia) and foreigners. Cambodian citizens, primarily women and girls, have been sex trafficked within the country and throughout the world.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] They are threatened and forced into prostitution, marriages, and or pregnancies. Sex trafficked victims experience physically and psychologically trauma. Some are tortured[^cite_note-5] and murdered.

Sex trafficking and exploitation occur at all levels of Cambodian society. Male and female perpetrators in Cambodia come from many backgrounds. A number of traffickers are members of or facilitated by gangs. [Pedophiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedophiles) travel to Cambodia for [sex tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_tourism).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] The extent of sex trafficking in Cambodia is not known because of the lack data, the underground nature of sex trafficking crimes,[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] and other factors. Anti-sex trafficking initiatives have been impeded by corruption, apathy, border management problems, fractured social institutions, weak law enforcement, and more.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] UNICEF states that some 37 % of the victims trafficked for sexual exploitation in Cambodia are children.

## Victims

Cambodian women and girls are sex trafficked into [China](5405.md), [Hong Kong](13404.md),[^cite_note-12] [Vietnam](202354.md), [Japan](15573.md),[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] and other countries throughout the world. They are imposed by coercion into marriages or brothels,[^cite_note-15] including phony massage parlors and karaoke bars,[^cite_note-16] and [unfree labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unfree_labour) in homes or on farms.[^cite_note-17] Many are tied or locked up in dark rooms without restrooms and abused.[^cite_note-18]

Many trafficked victims are taken into hotels, which are harder to detect than brothels. [^cite_note-19] Survivors recall being terrified and crying often.[^cite_note-20] Some attempt suicide. Many victims are drugged.[^cite_note-21] Victims are sometimes forced by traffickers to get [virginity certificates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginity_test) at hospitals.[^cite_note-22] Women and girls from families in poverty [^cite_note-23] and that lack education, as well as children and minorities, are vulnerable to sex trafficking.[^cite_note-24]

## Perpetrators

The traffickers are often part of gangs. The perpetrators are sometimes the victims' family parents, neighbors, or friends.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]

## Online forced prostitution

There has been a rise in online prostitution and cybersex trafficking in twenty-first century Cambodia. The global spread of high-speed internet and increase in computer, tablet, and smart phone ownership has fueled online sex abuse.[^cite_note-28] Some sex trafficked women and girls in Cambodia are forced to be on live-streaming pornography sharing and [dark web](40712897.md) sites via webcams that are sometimes paid with [cryptocurrency](36662188.md) and or use encrypted technology by clients worldwide..[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

## Anti-sex trafficking efforts

The government has taken some initiatives to reduce sex trafficking, but these are inadequate.

## Corruption

Further information: [Corruption in Cambodia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corruption_in_Cambodia)

Corruption and impunity have hindered anti-sex trafficking efforts in Cambodia. Some officials and police have colluded with the traffickers.[^cite_note-31]

## Non-governmental organizations

[Friends-International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friends-International)[^cite_note-32] and [Agape International Missions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agape_International_Missions) (AIM) conduct anti-sex trafficking efforts in Cambodia.[^cite_note-33] AIM created its own brothel-raiding SWAT team that works alongside the Cambodian police to capture traffickers and rescue girls.[^cite_note-34] Other organizations fighting against sex trafficking include the International Organisation for Migration, APLE Cambodia, the International Justice Mission, and Chab Dai Cambodia.[^cite_note-35]
