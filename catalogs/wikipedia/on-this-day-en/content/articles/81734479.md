**Emilly Micaela Marcondes** (born 6 September 1994), commonly known as **Emilly**, is a Brazilian professional [futsal](188197.md) player who plays as a winger for Torreblanca Melilla and the [Brazil women's national team](44894373.md). She was awarded the [best female futsal player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futsal_Awards#Female) in 2024 and 2025.

## Club career

Marcondes started playing football at the age of five in the street with the boys. At eleven, training became part of her routine. At 14, she went to play for the city club in Telêmaco Borba where she stayed for five years. She also played for Estrela de Guarulhos, in [São Paulo](390875.md), UNIFOR in Ceará, São José in São Paulo and Torreblanca in Spain.[^cite_note-zonafutsal-1]

In 2022, Emilly joined Burela, based in Galicia, Spain.[^cite_note-Xanxerê-2] With this club, she won two league titles, two Queen's Cups, three Spanish Super Cups and one Galician Cup. She played a key role in these victories and was twice elected best player in the league.[^cite_note-3]

## International career

In 2023, Emilly was part of the [Brazil women's national team](44894373.md) that won the 1st International Women's Futsal Tournament in Xanxerê. She scored one of the three goals in the final of the competition.[^cite_note-Xanxerê-2][^cite_note-4]

Emilly was named to the Brazilian squad for the [2025 FIFA Futsal Women's World Cup](76913115.md) on 25 September 2025.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Brazil won the tournament, having beaten [Portugal](59976997.md) 3–0 in the final, with Emilly scoring the opening goal. She was awarded the [Golden Ball](3482503.md) and the Golden Boot, finishing as the tournament's top scorer with six goals, tied with [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain_women's_national_futsal_team)'s Irene Córdoba, but registering more assists than Córdoba.[^cite_note-7]
