The **Ford Bronco Sport** is a [compact crossover SUV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_crossover_SUV) sold by the [Ford Motor Company](30433662.md) and marketed under the [Ford Bronco](1307386.md) nameplate.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] It was released alongside the [sixth generation Bronco](1307386.md#Sixth_generation_\(U725;_2021\)) [body-on-frame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body-on-frame) [SUV](47696.md), featuring a similar [retro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retro-style_automobile) and [off-road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-road_vehicle) styling in a smaller footprint.[^cite_note-9] The vehicle is based on the [front-wheel drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front-wheel_drive)-based, [unibody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unibody) [C2 platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_C2_platform), which is also used by the [Ford Escape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Escape#fourth) crossover and [Maverick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Maverick_\(2022\)) pickup.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

### Engine

The Bronco Sport has two engine options. The base engine is a [turbocharged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turbocharged) 1.5-liter *[Ecoboost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_EcoBoost_engine)* [I3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straight-three_engine) engine producing 181 hp (184 PS; 135 kW) at 6,000 rpm, and 190 lb⋅ft (258 N⋅m; 26.3 kg⋅m) of torque at 3,000 rpm. It is also available with a turbocharged 2.0-liter *Ecoboost* [I4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inline-four_engine) engine producing 245 hp (248 PS; 183 kW) at 6,000 rpm, and 275 lb⋅ft (373 N⋅m; 38.0 kg⋅m) of torque at 3,000 rpm. Both engines are mated to an 8-speed automatic transmission.[^cite_note-\:0-13][^cite_note-\:1-14]

### 4x4 drive system

The Bronco Sport comes equipped with an [all-wheel drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-wheel_drive) system with "G.O.A.T. Modes" (Goes Over Any Terrain).[^cite_note-15] The G.O.A.T. modes allow the driver to select different terrains for the 4x4 system. The Bronco Sport has four-wheel [independent suspension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_suspension). Only the Badlands model has a twin-clutch rear differential that can act as a rear differential locker and has torque vectoring. Unlike the Bronco, the Bronco Sport does not have a low-range [transfer case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_case). Instead of having traditional front bump stops, the Badlands model Bronco Sport has hydraulic bump stops. The Base, Big Bend, and Outer Banks models share the same AWD system as the Escape, with proprietary programming for off-road use.[^cite_note-\:0-13] The Bronco Sport has the tow capability of 2,200–2,700 lb (998–1,225 kg) depending on the engine.[^cite_note-16]

## Trims



Bronco Sport Badlands



Bronco Sport Outer Banks

For launch, the Bronco Sport was available in five trims, but the First Edition trim was limited to only 2,000 units.[^cite_note-\:1-14] All trims came standard with G.O.A.T mode, a flip-up rear glass and rear flood lights in the tailgate. The 8-inch infotainment system with [SYNC 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Sync) was standard across all trims.[^cite_note-17]

Base

The Base is the entry-level trim of the Bronco Sport lineup. It is equipped with the 1.5-litre three-cylinder Ecoboost gasoline engine mated to a rotary-controlled eight-speed automatic transmission, aluminum-alloy wheels, the SYNC 3 touchscreen infotainment system with SiriusXM Satellite Radio (SiriusXM was deleted for the 2022 [model year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_year)) and wired [Apple CarPlay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_CarPlay) and [Android Auto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Android_Auto) smartphone integration, cloth seating surfaces, a six-speaker audio system, 4.2-inch full-color LCD instrument cluster display screen and Ford's proprietary [door-mounted keypad system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remote_keyless_system#Keypad_entry_system), marketed as its *Securicode*.

Big Bend

The Big Bend trim level is the mid-level model of the Bronco Sport lineup. It adds convenience features onto the Base trim, such as SiriusXM Satellite Radio (starting with the 2022 model year), a keyless access system with a push-button ignition, a [MOLLE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOLLE) Strap System, and a rear seat back protector.&#x20;

Outer Banks

The Outer Banks trim level is the luxury-oriented model of the Bronco Sport lineup. It adds luxury amenities onto the Big Bend trim level, such as eighteen-inch tires and aluminum-alloy wheels, combination leather-and-cloth-trimmed seating surfaces, and a Shadow Black-painted front grille with white 'BRONCO' lettering.

Badlands

The Badlands trim level is the off-road oriented model of the Bronco Sport lineup. It is powered by the 2.0-litre four-cylinder Ecoboost gasoline engine mated to a rotary-controlled eight-speed automatic transmission (the Badlands is the only trim level in the Bronco Sport lineup to receive the higher-output engine), and adds features such as two additional "G.O.A.T. Modes" for the [four wheel drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_wheel_drive) system and all-terrain tires and unique aluminum-alloy wheels onto the mid-level Big Bend trim level.

First Edition

The First Edition trim level, only available for the 2021 model year, was based on the off-road oriented Badlands trim, and included almost all of its available options and packages (such as luxury leather-trimmed seating surfaces, dual heated front bucket seats, and a Bang and Olufsen premium amplified audio system). Ford's CoPilot 360 Assist+ was the only available option not offered on the First Edition. Production was limited to 2,000 units.&#x20;

Heritage Edition

The Heritage Edition trim level, only available beginning with the 2023 model year, and was based on the Big Bend trim. Several options are exclusive to the Heritage Edition trim and in honor of the original 1966 Bronco.&#x20;

Heritage Limited Edition

The Heritage Limited Edition trim level, only available to the 2023 model year, and was based on the off-road oriented Badlands trim, and includes almost all available options and packages. Several options are exclusive to the Heritage Limited Edition trim and in honor of the original 1966 Bronco, this retro production was limited to 1,966 units.&#x20;

Along with the regular options, Ford is also offering bundles on all trims with certain accessories. The four bundles offered are: Bike, Camp, Snow, and Water.[^cite_note-\:0-13]
