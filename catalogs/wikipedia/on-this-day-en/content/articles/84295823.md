On 22 September 2026, a [school shooting](59628.md) occurred near the İnci Üzmez Vocational and Technical Anatolian High School ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *İnci Üzmez Mesleki ve Teknik Anadolu Lisesi*) in [Turgutlu](4116768.md), Manisa Province, Turkey. Eleven students were injured, three of them critically.

This is the third school shooting in Turkey in 2026, after the [Siverek](82950772.md) and [Onikişubat](82958189.md) shootings.[^cite_note-BBC1-1]

## Shooting

Minutes prior to the attack the perpetrator, later identified as a 15-year-old ninth-grade student from the school, Halil Orhan,[^cite_note-ORR-2][^cite_note-UU-3] was spotted on security cameras carrying a [shotgun](26838.md) on the street outside the school.[^cite_note-BBC1-1] The attack happened around 8\:00 a.m. local time, when withnesses reported gunshots coming from school direction.[^cite_note-4] Minister [Mustafa Çiftçi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_%C3%87ift%C3%A7i) stated that a security guard and a gardener were on duty at the school during the attack. Orhan shot multiple shots from a shotgun that was later identified as his grandfather's. The gardener said that the gunman opened fire on the students from a distance of '80-100 meters' from the school.[^cite_note-5] Following the attack, Orhan fled the street with the weapon and was again captured on surveillance footage.[^cite_note-BBC1-1] As news spread, worried parents rushed to the school and clashed with law enforcement trying to get inside.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-\:2-7]

### Victims

A total of 11 people were wounded; three of them were in critical condition and receiving intensive care. One person was discharged from the hospital the same day.[^cite_note-\:2-7]

## Suspect

The suspect was identified as **Halil Orhan** (born 2011), a 15-year-old ninth-grade student from the aforementioned school, and was detained by law enforcement along with his mother, father and grandfather who owned the shotgun that was used in the attack.[^cite_note-ORR-2][^cite_note-UU-3][^cite_note-LL-8][^cite_note-AB-9][^cite_note-10]

## Reactions

President [Recep Tayyip Erdoğan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recep_Tayyip_Erdo%C4%9Fan) and Minister of Justice [Akın Gürlek](82385461.md) released statements on their social media accounts condemning the attack.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

Minister of Interior Mustafa Çiftçi and education minister [Yusuf Tekin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yusuf_Tekin) visited students who were injured in the attack.[^cite_note-13]
