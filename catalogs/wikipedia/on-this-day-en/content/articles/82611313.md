# 2026 Iran war regional mobilizations

During the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), amidst [joint US-Israel strikes on Iran](82546969.md#Operations_Roaring_Lion_and_Epic_Fury), [Iranian strikes against American and Israeli assets across the region](82546969.md#Iranian_response), and the [economic impact of the conflict](82562375.md), several surrounding nations began [military mobilizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_mobilization) to secure their borders and maritime trade across the [Arabian Sea](14580.md), [Red Sea](26191.md) and the [Eastern Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Mediterranean), among others.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## Western Bloc

See also: [United Kingdom involvement in the 2026 Iran war](82581844.md), [Canada and the 2026 Iran war](82593114.md), and [Australian involvement in the 2026 Iran war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_involvement_in_the_2026_Iran_war)

Many Western countries have deployed ships to the [Gulf of Oman](12896.md) with the goal of ensuring maritime security in the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md), also serving as observers in the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-3] [France](5843419.md) deployed the aircraft carrier [*Charles de Gaulle*](639127.md) to defend Cyprus, with the intention of protecting French citizens affected by the regional instability, and to prevent a [broader regional conflict](75895273.md) that could threaten energy supplies to Europe.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The [United Kingdom](31717.md) deployed forces to reinforce its [Overseas military bases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_military_bases_of_the_United_Kingdom) in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md), while blocking US access to them for aggressive purposes.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] The [Netherlands](21148.md) expressed interest in potentially joining the French and British mobilization in the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-Verdú-11] Reports by Israeli sources suggest that [Germany](11867.md) is planning for a possible military deployment in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md) against Iran.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] [Italy](14532.md) deployed naval vessels and air defense systems (potentially including [SAMP/T](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAMP/T) batteries) to help protect the Gulf region against Iranian airstrikes, and also to protect Italian personnel and regional energy supply, while refusing to participate in offensive actions in support of the US and Israel against Iran.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] [Ukraine](31750.md)[^cite_note-16] has sent drone warfare specialists to US bases in Jordan, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Qatar to help counter Iranian [Shahed drones](75153890.md).[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] The [Australian Government](1222540.md) pledged to not send any ground troops to Iran and said that [Australia would only take purely defensive action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_involvement_in_the_2026_Iran_war). Australia deployed forces to the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) (UAE) to defend the [Gulf states](3088555.md) and Australian military bases at the request of the UAE.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20]

The [European Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Council) and the [Gulf Cooperation Council](220950.md) made a joint declaration to mobilize forces with the intention to "work together on de-escalation, safeguarding regional security and respect for international law". France formed their own plans to assemble a [multinational force](25406585.md) (including Non-EU states) on a "purely defensive, purely support" mission to secure [freedom of navigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_of_navigation) in the [Middle East](19323.md) and allow commercial vessels to continue transit through the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md) and the [Gulf of Oman](12896.md).[^cite_note-Verdú-11][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-Mahadzir-22] Similar projects for [multilateral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilateralism) mobilization have been planned by [Italy](14532.md).[^cite_note-23] Iran warned [NATO](21133.md) and the [European Union](9317.md) against military activities in support of the US and Israel.[^cite_note-24]

Regarding US efforts to re-open the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md) with the aid of [NATO](21133.md) and other US allies, the [United Kingdom](31717.md) and [France](5843419.md) have backed coordinated action to restore shipping without supporting US and Israeli attacks on Iran, while [Spain](26667.md), [Greece](12108.md), and [Japan](15573.md) have ruled out military intervention.[^cite_note-25] On March 19, [France](5843419.md), [Germany](11867.md), [Italy](14532.md), [Japan](15573.md), [Netherlands](21148.md) and [United Kingdom](31717.md) declared their support to contribute in the efforts to open the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md).[^cite_note-26] Also did the [European Union](9317.md).[^cite_note-27]

On 11 April, US Navy destroyers crossed the Strait of Hormuz, as they were challenged by IRGC naval forces. The incident was reportedly intended to show that the US will not recognize Iranian control of the Strait.[^cite_note-28]

## Eastern Mediterranean

### In Cyprus and the Balkans

See also: [2026 drone strikes on Akrotiri and Dhekelia](82553346.md)

#### European Union and Britain



[Eastern Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Mediterranean), main region of European mobilization through the territory of [Cyprus](5593.md), [Lebanon](17771.md), [Turkey](11125639.md), [Greece](12108.md), [Bulgaria](3415.md)

Following strikes on the island of Cyprus and the threats of the [IRGC](1285472.md) against the island, numerous nations[^cite_note-29]—including [France](5843419.md),[^cite_note-30] [Germany](11867.md),[^cite_note-31] [Greece](12108.md), [Italy](14532.md),[^cite_note-32] [Spain](26667.md),[^cite_note-33] the [Netherlands](21148.md) and the [United Kingdom](31717.md)—decided to mobilize their armed forces in the surrounding waters[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35] and onto the island,[^cite_note-36] vowing to protect it in case of an attack. The [European Commission](9974.md) Chief Spokesperson [Paula Pinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paula_Pinho) stated that in the coming days following the attack, the [EU](9317.md) "mutual defence clause will be up for discussion".[^cite_note-37] On 6 March, [Bulgaria](3415.md) allowed the [United States](3434750.md) and Greece[^cite_note-38] to use its territory to counter potential Iranian attacks.[^cite_note-39] The president of the [European People's Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_People's_Party) (EPP), [Manfred Weber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manfred_Weber), called Iran a "criminal regime" and vowed that an attack on the Republic of Cyprus would be considered an attack on Europe.[^cite_note-40] On 2 March, [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md) ([President of France](24899.md)) announced that simultaneous mobilizations were being conducted, which will include the temporary deployment of its nuclear-armed aircraft to 8 European countries (Germany, Britain, [Poland](22936.md), the Netherlands, [Belgium](3343.md), Greece, [Sweden](5058739.md) and [Denmark](76972.md)) to bolster the security of the continent against spillovers from the 2026 Iran War and the [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md).[^cite_note-41]

Iran's response was to state that such acts of involvement in the conflict could be considered "acts of war";[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43] however, analysts note that the European mobilization in Cyprus was not the reaction Iran expected and seems to have worked as a deterrent.[^cite_note-44] Observers in the countries of Cyprus and Greece noted how the war was perceived pretty vividly and fears of further escalation involving both nations are prevalent.[^cite_note-45] Cyprus expressed worries of possible terrorist cells that could act within the nation on Iran's behalf from [Northern Cyprus](271409.md), where 10,000 pro‑regime Iranians live and where, allegedly, Hamas and the [Muslim Brotherhood](20742.md) operate.[^cite_note-46]

Italy's [intelligence agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_intelligence_agencies) reported that it is highly possible that the entire [Eastern Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Mediterranean) region will be affected by the growing instability in the [Levant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levant) and the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md), mainly suffering impacts on energy markets and supply security, which will affect [Mediterranean countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_countries) and Europe as a whole if the [Indo-Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Mediterranean) trade route through the [Suez Canal](29323.md)-[Red Sea](26191.md) is damaged by the conflict. Such [geopolitical](163225.md) possibility of maritime insecurity was declared as the main reason for various Western countries to deploy troops, with the main goal of protecting the Euro-Atlantic trade routes.[^cite_note-47] Italian Prime Minister [Giorgia Meloni](29228976.md) has been developing a rapprochement between the European Union and the [Gulf Cooperation Council](220950.md) for a common diplomatic strategy and maintaining their neutrality while condemning Iranian attacks.[^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49]

##### Opposition to aggressive actions

See also: [Spain in the 2026 Iran war](82654191.md)

Despite the mobilizations, there was no unified European posture in the conflict, with political divisions between an energic support to the Anti-Iranian military activities (e.g., Germany) and the development of a defensive strategy accompanied by distancing from—and sometimes condemnation of (e.g., Spain)—US and Israeli activities perceived as legally dubious.[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53] Protests started in Cyprus against the existence of [British Cyprus bases](1025144.md), as the [Cyprus–United Kingdom relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus%E2%80%93United_Kingdom_relations) worsened, due to [Cypriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cypriots) fearing that the country could be dragged into the Iranian conflict if the bases are used for pro-US/Israel offensive activities instead of their declared defensive purposes.[^cite_note-54][^cite_note-55][^cite_note-56] Similarly the [Government of Spain](1306113.md) protested and hardened its position against a more active European intervention in the [Middle Eastern crisis](75895273.md), which damaged the [Spain–United States relations](15420327.md) after [Pedro Sánchez](43297512.md) condemned US-Israel actions against Iran as a violation of [International law](8195726.md),[^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58] declaring such actions as a "big error" which only developed a conflict that is foreign to Europe and is dragging the [Eastern Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Mediterranean) to instability, while denying the use of Spanish bases to the US and Israel armies.[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-60][^cite_note-61] [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md) and the [European Commission](9974.md) expressed solidarity with Spain against the threats of US economic coercion against European military independence.[^cite_note-62] [Germany](11867.md) and [Belgium](3343.md) also declared their opposition to deploying forces in support for the US-Israel aggressions.[^cite_note-63] The EU has officially been calling for de-escalation and to prefer diplomatic initiative with the help of the [Gulf Countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_Countries), adjacent [regions in Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_Africa) and the [Indo-Pacific](330456.md) that are also affected by the War.[^cite_note-EU-64]

#### Turkey

See also: [Cyprus problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus_problem)

[Turkey](11125639.md) also expressed its worries about the insecurity for [Turkish Cypriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Cypriots), planning to deploy air forces to [North Cyprus](271409.md) with the mission of provide more security to [Cyprus island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus_\(island\)) and avoid merging the [Cypriot Conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cypriot_Conflict) with the Iranian War.[^cite_note-65] Also the [Greece–Turkey relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece%E2%80%93Turkey_relations) entered in tensions due to their conflicting interests as both countries have taken advantage of the Iranian menace to deploy their armies near the [Greece–Turkey border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece%E2%80%93Turkey_border) and to secure positions to aid the conflictive [Greek Cypriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_Cypriots) and Turkish Cypriots, as also both countries protesting against the other military movements while being reluctant to cooperate as [NATO](21133.md) partners (even being denounced by [Greeks](42056.md) that the [Turkish government](3680197.md) is secretly helping Iranians during the [2026 Kurdish–Iranian crisis](82575260.md) and also giving help to [Hamas](13913.md) against Israel through North Cyprus).[^cite_note-66][^cite_note-67]

### In the Levant

See also: [2026 Lebanon war](82549840.md) and [Syrian conflict (2024–present)](81373641.md)

#### Syria

See also: [Syria in the 2026 Iran war](82679864.md)

By Wednesday 4 March, the [Syrian Army](3423927.md) (already mobilized in the region before the 2026 Iran War, due to the Spillover of the Gaza war by the [Israeli invasion of Syria](78549398.md) and the Iran-backed [Assad loyalist insurgency](78735576.md)) has bolstered its troop deployments on the country's borders with [Lebanon](17771.md) and [Iraq](7515928.md), as also for giving support to the [Lebanese government](17775.md) in the [Disarmament of Hezbollah](80353105.md).[^cite_note-68]

> "We have reinforced our defensive forces along the border as a precaution to prevent the repercussions of the conflict from spilling over onto Syrian territory, and to combat cross-border organizations and prevent them from using Syrian soil"

— Syrian President [Ahmed al-Sharaa](44007699.md)

#### European Union

At the start of the war, a projected 5 March International Conference in Paris to mobilize support to the [Lebanese Armed Forces](17779.md) had been postponed.[^cite_note-69] Despite, France mobilized forces in [Southern Lebanon](4205968.md) to aid in the [peace process](24903.md) in the context of the [Israeli–Lebanese conflict](6020082.md) and redirect the efforts of the [Lebanese government](17775.md) in the common goal of the [Disarmament of Hezbollah](80353105.md), while also serving as a countermeasure to protect European interests against the non-agreed US-Israel military operations.[^cite_note-Verdú-11] The [European Union](9317.md) itself on 10 March deployed emergency [humanitarian aid](434679.md) for the civilian population harmed by the crossfire of the [2026 Lebanon war](82549840.md) and its large scale displacements, with the help of the [World Food Programme](33582.md) (WFP) and [UNICEF](19698110.md).[^cite_note-70] A day before, during the announcement of the mobilisation, the EU stated its desire for the respect of the sovereignty and [territorial integrity](31335.md) of Lebanon by Iran-backed Hezbollah and Israel.[^cite_note-71] [Lebanon](17771.md) urged to European allies of Israel to send military aid for the stabilization of the region.[^cite_note-72]

#### United Nations

Since 2006, the [United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon](787766.md) has been mobilized at the [Israel Lebanon border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Lebanon_border) as a peacekeeping force, aiming to support the Lebanese army's operations against [Iran's sponsored terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_and_state-sponsored_terrorism) by [Hezbollah](13919.md) insurgencies, and to secure the [Israeli](9282173.md) withdrawal from [Lebanon](17771.md). Although UNIFIL's operations are scheduled to end on 31 December 2026, countries like Spain have declared that they will maintain their presence in the region in support of the Lebanese government and the Lebanese Armed Forces until conditions allow, given the escalating crossfire between Israel and Iran backed Hezbollah. Currently, [UN](31769.md) forces have had to reinforce security measures to the maximum and remain "bunked" for most of the day to avoid being hit at the [2026 Lebanon war](82549840.md).[^cite_note-Defensa-73] Despite, recently have been proposals for an evacuation of the UNIFIL, with help of the European mobilized forces in Cyprus, due to the intensification of the hostilities in the region and the fears of institutional collapse in Lebanon.[^cite_note-74]

## Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

See also: [Red Sea crisis](75207766.md)

### Houthi Yemen

See also: [Yemeni civil war (2014–present)](46215853.md), [Houthi–Saudi Arabian conflict](47695166.md), and [Iranian support for the Houthis](78962952.md)

The militarization of the [Houthis](17240332.md) (already engaged in the [Yemeni civil war](46215853.md)) has intensified as the Axis of Resistance links the Iran war to their ongoing [Red Sea blockade](75207766.md) by Israel, threatening to resume their shipping attacks if tensions escalate,[^cite_note-75] while focusing to mobilize its forces to defeat the pro-US/Israel [Saudi-led intervention in Yemen](46230181.md) (not wanting to violate the [2025 United States–Houthi ceasefire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_United_States%E2%80%93Houthi_ceasefire)).[^cite_note-76][^cite_note-77]

By March 2026, has been reported new mobilizations by the Saudi-backed [Presidential Leadership Council](70488867.md) to repress the remnants of the UAE-backed [Southern Transitional Council](54251566.md) (dissolved after the [2025–2026 Southern Yemen campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_Southern_Yemen_campaign)) for the total [stabilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stabilization_\(warfare\)) of [Aden](38050.md), being accelerated such mobilizations for unifying the security and military apparatus under [Yemen Republic](350939.md)'s command due to the menace of the increased tensions with the Iran-backed [Supreme Political Council](51279323.md) after the 2026 Iran War and the risks of being broken the Iranian-Saudi normalization.[^cite_note-78][^cite_note-79] In that context of increased tensions in Yemen, the neutral commercial vessels from [Afro-Eurasia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Eurasia) have been avoiding the [Bab al-Mandab Strait](62138.md) and the [Suez Canal](29323.md) [Indo-Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Mediterranean) route in preference to the [Cape of Good Hope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_of_Good_Hope) one to reach the [Indian Ocean trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean_trade).[^cite_note-80][^cite_note-81][^cite_note-82][^cite_note-83]

In reaction to the Houthi threat, some Western countries like [France](5843419.md) announced the deployment of forces in the [Red Sea](26191.md) to secure the trade routes in the region from potential spillovers of the 2026 Iran War.[^cite_note-Mahadzir-22]

### Egypt

Even before the 2026 US-Israel War on Iran, [Egypt](8087628.md) already mobilized tens of thousands of troops, tanks, armored vehicles, and combat helicopters to the northern [Sinai Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinai_Peninsula) due to the [Middle Eastern crisis (2023–present)](75895273.md) and the risk of spillover of the [Gaza war](74998519.md) by the [Hezbollah–Israel conflict (2023–present)](75005425.md), desiring to avoid the expansion of the [Iran–Israel proxy conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Israel_proxy_conflict) in its territory. Also Egypt has heavily fortified the [Israel-Egypt border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt%E2%80%93Israel_barrier) with cement obstacles and buffer zones to physically block this, viewing any forced displacement of [Palestinians](23267.md) as a direct threat to its national security and the [1979 peace treaty with Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt%E2%80%93Israel_peace_treaty). Some of these mobilizations has been coordinated with Israel against the common menace of [Shia Islamism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islamism) backed by the [Axis of Resistance](36658251.md), and also as independent deployments which has coldened its relations with Israel due to accusations of "excessive buildup".[^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85]

Also the [Egyptian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_government) has been aggressively expanding its military footprint further south in the [Horn of Africa](62142.md), signing military cooperation agreements with [Somalia](27358.md) to deploy troops there and secure the [Bab el-Mandeb strait](62138.md), countering the expanding Iranian/Houthi influence, and push back against Israel's recent controversial security maneuvers in the region (such as Israel's recognition of Somaliland).[^cite_note-86] Also Egypt has been proposing the development of a neutral Pan-Arab force to counter the [Iran–Israel proxy conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Israel_proxy_conflict) spillovers in the Red Sea and avoid a dependence of the American-Israeli security umbrella, without success.[^cite_note-87]

### Northeast Africa

See also: [Anti-piracy measures in Somalia](44635581.md)

The [Horn of Africa](62142.md), alike [North Africa](21714.md), have been historically linked to the Middle East due to the existence of military, political and economic networks across the [Gulf of Aden](58716.md) and [Red Sea](26191.md) interactions (provoking that a crisis on one shore to quickly affect the other). Due to this, the [Axis of Resistance](36658251.md) have expanded its [Sphere of influence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_of_influence) in the region even before the US-Israel War, as also did [Saudi Arabia](349303.md), the [UAE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UAE) and [Israel](9282173.md) to secure their interests (in the context of the [Iran–Saudi Arabia proxy war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Saudi_Arabia_proxy_war)). The [Iranian vessel Saviz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_Saviz) is deployed at the [Red Sea](26191.md) (close to the [Eritrean](17238590.md) coast) from [Houthi Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_Yemen) as a logistical and intelligence platform low-cost tools, alongside military advisers and arms networks to transport weaponry, as also have been happening a tiny [Iranian mobilization in Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_intervention_in_Sudan) and Eritrea since the 2000s to secure its interests in the [Strait of Mandab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Mandab).[^cite_note-88][^cite_note-89] Some analysts have showed serious preoccupations that the worst-case scenario for the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md) would be an expansion of the conflict towards [Northeast Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_Africa) due to the related [Red Sea crisis](75207766.md) and the adjacent Horn of Africa conflict, in which could be merged the [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md), the [Eritrea–Ethiopia crisis](57858711.md), the [Ethiopia-Egypt and Sudan crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Ethiopian_Renaissance_Dam#Impact_on_Sudan_and_Egypt), the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md), the [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md), among others (as also a rupture of [Saudi Arabia with the UAE](30267249.md), and of [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93Turkey_relations) and [Egypt](21337265.md) with [Israel](9282173.md)) due to the already mobilization of various countries as a consequence of regional tensions since before the war.[^cite_note-EU-64][^cite_note-90][^cite_note-91]

Some security sources claim that Houthis are considering to do incursions in the [Arabian Sea](2747.md) and also use bases from the African side of the Red Sea, helped by their presence and some cells established in [East Africa](260987.md) (including the involvement of the [Al-Shabaab](8726463.md) [Islamic terrorist](566821.md) group, currently participating in the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md)).[^cite_note-92] Those projected bases have been hinted as the Eritrean ports (mainly [Assab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assab), [Massawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massawa)) and Somali ones, due to Iranian logistical access to Eritrean infrastructure and proxy relationships between Houthi and Somali militant networks.[^cite_note-93] The [Gulf states](3088555.md) and [Turkey](11125639.md) also have been mobilizing towards [Africa](5334607.md) in case of increase of tensions with the [Axis of Resistance](36658251.md), expecting a total militarization of the Red Sea corridor if the conflicts evolves to Total War.[^cite_note-94][^cite_note-95][^cite_note-96]

Due to the [Piracy in Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piracy_in_Somalia), a lot of countries have deployed forces in the [Gulf of Aden](58716.md), like the [European Union](9317.md) in the [Operation Atalanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Atalanta), the US in the [Operation Prosperity Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Prosperity_Guardian), among others. Recently the [Spanish frigate Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_frigate_Victoria) withdraw from the region and was replaced by the [Spanish frigate Canarias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_frigate_Canarias), as also has been considerations about ordering the escort vessel to remain further south, in [Somali](27358.md) waters, to avoid becoming involved in military actions between the US and Iran in [South Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Arabia) coasts.[^cite_note-97][^cite_note-Defensa-73] The [French frigate Forbin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_frigate_Forbin) has been deployed at the [Bab al-Mandab Strait](62138.md).[^cite_note-98]

#### Tensions in East Africa

It has been developed a [Turkey](11125639.md)–[Saudi Arabia](349303.md)–[Somalia](27358.md) militar bloc to counter the one of the [UAE](69328.md)–[Somaliland](51425.md)–[Israel](9282173.md) and also the [Iran](14653.md)-[Houthi](17240332.md)-[Al Shabaab](8726463.md) (the last ones have also developed approachments to the [Somali pirates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_piracy)). This current cold war in [East Africa](260987.md) between Iran, Israel and Saudi Arabia also has been dragging [Ethiopia](187749.md) (who seeks to maximize its strategic gains without signalling preference for either side) and [Egypt](8087628.md) (who have mobilized forces in [Mogadishu](60154.md) to counter any competitive influence in the [Red Sea](26191.md)).[^cite_note-99][^cite_note-100] Geopolitical analysts like [Mohamed Marshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Marshal?action=edit\&redlink=1) have stated that "The Horn is no longer peripheral. It is tied to Gulf rivalries, Sudan’s war, Nile politics and global shipping routes. Ethiopia’s sea access discourse is part of that structural shift",[^cite_note-101] while [Abdel Latif El Menawy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdel_Latif_El_Menawy) declared that "The growing overlap between African and Middle Eastern dynamics is reshaping the character of conflicts" and that Egypt is trying to develop an equilibrium in the region (considering to use military force if it's necessary).[^cite_note-102] This Horn of Africa conflict is a consequence of the [Indo-Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Mediterranean) being increasingly congested with naval deployments, foreign bases, proxy competition, and infrastructure rivalries.[^cite_note-103] Despite the Saudi-UAE rivalry in Africa, both states have made a temporarily Rapprochement due to being times of regional instability after the US-Israel War on Iran, not wanting dangerous power vacuums to emerge.[^cite_note-104]

The [Port of Berbera](41240501.md) in [Somaliland](51425.md) (an ally of [Israel](9282173.md) and of the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md), who both have a military presence in the area and already has mobilized there)[^cite_note-105] and [Camp Lemonnier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Lemonnier) in [Djibouti](17207794.md) (a US base in the country)[^cite_note-106] has been considered military target by Iran and the [Houthis](17240332.md) (and its Al-Shabaab allies) due to its strategic importance.[^cite_note-107] The Israel recognition of Somaliland included discussions of Israel establishing a military base in Berbera, with Houthis warning after the recognition that "Israeli assets in Somaliland would be legitimate military targets", risking regional stability in the [Horn of Africa](62142.md).[^cite_note-108]

## Civilian evacuations

Tens of thousands of people within the [Gulf region](3088555.md) are stuck with few ways to leave.[^cite_note-Al_Jazeera_English-194] The United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, China, and Poland urged citizens to leave Iran and the Gulf states (Middle East).[^cite_note-195] On 6 March, the [United Nations](31769.md) declared that the Iran war had evolved into a "[major humanitarian emergency](1251700.md)" due to the affected regions currently hosting nearly 25 million people as [refugees](45547.md). This high number of refugees is a consequence of the significant population movements in reaction to the increased insecurity by the military activities in the [Indian Ocean](14580.md) and the [Middle East](19323.md)-[South Asia](21566765.md).[^cite_note-196] Due to that humanitarian crisis, the [United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees](31964.md) mobilized in the [Middle East](19323.md) to help people from [Lebanon](17771.md) to [Afghanistan](737.md) that are trying to leave from the regional conflicts.[^cite_note-197]

The [US Department of State](31975.md) urged Americans in the Middle East to leave such countries due to "safety risks" as the war could last from four to five weeks or even more, recommending its population to use the available [commercial transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transit_privatization).[^cite_note-198] The US Department of State subsequently announced it was working on bringing Americans in the Middle East home, and stated they are in contact with more than 3,000 Americans there. According to Trump and Rubio, almost 9,000 Americans have evacuated from the Middle East since the start of the strikes.[^cite_note-Al_Jazeera_English-194] A US state department official stated many more Americans are expected to evacuate,[^cite_note-199] and on 9 March reported that a total of 36,000 Americans have returned from the middle east since 28 February.[^cite_note-200]



Map of emergency evacuation of persons to Europe

[Kazakhstan](16642.md),[^cite_note-Bland-2026-201] [Turkey](11125639.md),[^cite_note-202] the [New Zealand Defence Force](21359.md)[^cite_note-203] and the United Kingdom's [Foreign Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Office) said they were developing plans to evacuate their nationals in the region.[^cite_note-204] [Russia](25391.md), through its [embassy](6882725.md), made facilitations for Russians unable to leave Iran by liberalizing the route in the [Iran–Turkmenistan border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Turkmenistan_border).[^cite_note-205] The [Foreign Ministry of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Ministry_of_Italy) is expecting to evacuate 30,000 Italians from the [UAE](69328.md),[^cite_note-206] receiving on 3 March around 300 people from [Oman](22316.md) and other 200 from the UAE.[^cite_note-207] Italy, [Austria](26964606.md), [Slovakia](26830.md) and other European countries solicited the help of resources from the [European Union](9317.md) in the evacuation.[^cite_note-208] By 6 March, German troops of the [Bundeswehr](288188.md) stationed in Bahrain and Kuwait, as also the ones within the [UNIFIL](787766.md) mission in Lebanon, were successfully evacuated.[^cite_note-209] The [Spanish foreign ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_foreign_ministry) evacuated the Spanish diplomatic personnel in the [Arab states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_state) and [Iran](14653.md) by 7 March.[^cite_note-210]

[Asia-Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia-Pacific) countries—including China, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Indonesia, Pakistan, India and Australia—also developed operations to evacuate their citizens trapped in the Persian Gulf by 3 March, and to secure energy supplies and monitor financial markets.[^cite_note-211] According to the [Indian Ministry of Civil Aviation](10257026.md), [Indian airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_airlines) brought at least 52,000 passengers, including 32,107 onboard Indian carriers and the others by foreign airlines, according to the [Indian Ministry of External Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ministry_of_External_Affairs).[^cite_note-212][^cite_note-213] These included passengers stranded in UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Oman to India.[^cite_note-214][^cite_note-215] Besides commercial repatriation flights,[^cite_note-216][^cite_note-217] the [Singapore](27318.md) government will also deploy a [Republic of Singapore Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Singapore_Air_Force) [A330 MRTT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A330_MRTT) aircraft to repatriate Singaporeans in the region on two separate flights from Saudi Arabia.[^cite_note-218][^cite_note-219]

The [Iran–Turkey border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Turkey_border) day-trip passenger crossings was closed by 2 March to avoid a [Refugee crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refugee_crisis).[^cite_note-220] People started to leave Kazakhstan to reach the Caucasus through [Azerbaijan](746.md)'s [Astara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astara_District) crossing,[^cite_note-Bland-2026-201][^cite_note-221] and by 6 March both Azerbaijan (who successfully evacuated its diplomatic personnel from [Tehran](57654.md))[^cite_note-222] and [Armenia](10918072.md) have received 1500 refugees from Iran (mostly [Chinese](372983.md), [Russians](59510.md) and other foreigners)[^cite_note-223] while [Georgia](48768.md) became a refugee for [Iranian people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_peoples) related to the [opposition](80207658.md).[^cite_note-224] However, the UN refugee agency stated there has been no major exodus from Iran.[^cite_note-225][^cite_note-226] The UAE announced its intent to repatriate 44,000 travelers.[^cite_note-Al_Jazeera_English-194] The Israeli military issued warnings to evacuate almost the entire southern Lebanon, causing tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands of people to flee. The entire region that Israel warned to evacuate is said to house around 500,000 people.[^cite_note-227]
