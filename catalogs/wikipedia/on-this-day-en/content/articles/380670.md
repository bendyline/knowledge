The **President of Cameroon** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Président de la République du Cameroun*) is the executive [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and *de facto* [head of government](37570.md) of [Cameroon](5447.md) and is the commander in chief of the [Cameroon Armed Forces](5455.md). The authority of the state is exercised both by the president and by the [Parliament](7164302.md).[^cite_note-3]

## History

The office of president of Cameroon was established in 1960, following the country's independence from [France](5843419.md). Only two people have held the office of president—[Ahmadou Ahidjo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadou_Ahidjo), who served from 1960 to 1982, and [Paul Biya](392319.md), who has served since Ahidjo's resignation in 1982.

## Term limits

Term limits for the president were lifted for Biya in 2008.[^cite_note-termlimits-4]
