The **Landtag of Rhineland-Palatinate** is the state [diet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diet_\(assembly\)) of the German state of [Rhineland-Palatinate](26239.md).

Article 79, Section 1 of the Rhineland-Palatinate [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution) provides: "The [Landtag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landtag) is the supreme organ of political decision-making, elected by the people. It represents the people, elects the [Minister-President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister-President_of_Rhineland-Palatinate) and confirms the cabinet, passes the laws and the budget, controls the executive and enunciates the popular will in the conduct of public affairs, in questions of European policy and according to the agreements between the Landtag and the cabinet."

The Landtag consists of 101 members.

The Landtag convenes in the [Deutschhaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutschhaus,_Mainz) building,[^cite_note-Zeit-1] where also the first democratically elected parliament German history had convened, the *Rhenish-German national convention* of the [Mainz Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainz_Republic). Parts of its administration are located in the [old arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal,_Mainz).

The German flag used in the Landtag is a historical one used during the [Hambacher Fest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hambacher_Fest).

## Electoral system

The Landtag is elected via [mixed-member proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed-member_proportional_representation). 52 members are elected in [single-member constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-member_constituencies) via [first-past-the-post voting](261709.md). 49 members are then allocated using compensatory proportional representation, distributed in four multi-member districts.[^cite_note-2] Voters have two votes: the "first vote" for candidates in single-member constituencies, and the "second vote" for [party lists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_list), which are used to fill the proportional seats. The minimum size of the Landtag is 101 members, but if [overhang seats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overhang_seat) are present, proportional [leveling seats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leveling_seat) will be added to ensure proportionality. An [electoral threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_threshold) of 5% of valid votes is applied to the Landtag; parties that fall below this threshold are excluded.[^cite_note-3]

## Composition

After the elections of March 22, 2026, the composition of the Landtag is as follows[^cite_note-4]:

| Party | Seats |
| --- | --- |
| **[Christian Democratic Union (CDU)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Democratic_Union_\(Germany\))** | 39 |
| [Social Democratic Party (SPD)](60585.md) | 32 |
| [Alliance '90/The Greens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_'90/The_Greens) | 10 |
| [Alternative for Germany (AfD)](38708229.md) | 24 |

Political groups in **bold** support the state's [coalition government](6036.md). Since 2011 the [Webster/Sainte-Laguë method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webster/Sainte-Lagu%C3%AB_method) is used for allocating seats in party-list proportional representation.[^cite_note-5]

The parliamentary groups are chaired by [Alexander Schweitzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Schweitzer_\(politician\)) (SPD), [Christoph Gensch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_Gensch) (CDU), [Michael Büge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_B%C3%BCge) (AfD) and [Katrin Eder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katrin_Eder) (Greens).[^cite_note-6]

## Presidents of the Landtag

- 1947–1948 Jakob Diel, [CDU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Democratic_Union_\(Germany\))

- 1948–1959 [August Wolters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Wolters?action=edit\&redlink=1), CDU

- 1959–1971 [Otto van Volxem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_van_Volxem?action=edit\&redlink=1), CDU

- 1971–1974 [Johannes Baptist Rösler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Baptist_R%C3%B6sler?action=edit\&redlink=1), CDU

- 1974–1985 [Albrecht Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albrecht_Martin?action=edit\&redlink=1), CDU

- 1985–1991 [Heinz Peter Volkert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinz_Peter_Volkert?action=edit\&redlink=1), CDU

- 1991–2006 [Christoph Grimm](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_Grimm_\(Politiker,_1943\)), [SPD](60585.md)

- 2006–2016 [Joachim Mertes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joachim_Mertes?action=edit\&redlink=1), SPD

- 2016–2026 [Hendrik Hering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrik_Hering), SPD

- since 2026 [Matthias Lammert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthias_Lammert), CDU
