# List of active United States military aircraft



Lifecycles of U.S. aircraft weapon systems showing notional projected lifetime and extended life.

The [United States Armed Forces](32212.md) uses a wide variety of [military aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_aircraft) across the respective aviation arms of its various [service branches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service_branch). The numbers of specific aircraft listed in the following entries are estimates from published sources and may not be exhaustive.
