The [legal status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_status) of [cryptocurrencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrencies) varies substantially from one jurisdiction to another, and is still undefined or changing in many of them.[^cite_note-1] While in the majority of countries the usage of cryptocurrency is not in itself illegal, its status and usability as a means of payment (or a commodity) varies, with differing regulatory implications.[^cite_note-2]
