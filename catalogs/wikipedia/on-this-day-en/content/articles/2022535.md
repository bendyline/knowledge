**Omukama wa Tooro** (translates to the **King of Tooro**) is the official title given to the king of [Tooro](1506178.md) in [Uganda](31816.md). Ugandan kingdoms currently function "more as cultural institutions than political powers".[^cite_note-1]

## History

The history of Omukama wa Tooro starts with the kingdom's founding around 1830s by Rukirabasaija Omukama Kaboyo Olimi l Amooti who was the son of Rukirabasaija Kyebambe lll Nyamutukura Amooti, the king of Bunyoro.[^cite_note-\:0-2] He broke away and formed his own independent kingdom, establishing the Tooro monarchy, continuing the ancient [Babiito dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babiito_dynasty) linked to the legendary [Kitara empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_of_Kitara) with earlier legendary rulers like the [Tembuzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tembuzi_dynasty) and Chwezi.[^cite_note-\:0-2] In the late 1880s the Bunyoro King, Kabarenga temporarily reconquered the [Tooro](1506178.md). However, with help from the British colonizers the Tooro prince restored his throne in the 1890s.&#x20;

In the 1950s, the [Uganda National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uganda_National_Congress) supported the Omukama as he resisted the Central Government's efforts to "rule" Tooro as a district.[^cite_note-3]

Tooro existed as an independent kingdom until 1967 when [Apollo Milton Obote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_Obote) banned all kingdoms in Uganda. The kingdoms were reinstated as cultural institutions in 1993.

## Election of kings

Tooro kingdom runs a hereditary system of succession, and the successor is determined by royal lineage.[^cite_note-4]

## Current kingship

The most recent Omukama of Tooro is [Edward Rukidi Nyabongo I](84244857.md), who ascended the throne in 2026.[^cite_note-5] He is the 14th ruler of this kingdom, which originated as a secession from [Bunyoro Kingdom.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunyoro)[^cite_note-6]
