The **2026 NCAA Division I women's basketball championship game** was the final game of the [2026 NCAA Division I women's basketball tournament](76476380.md). It determined the champion of the [2025–26 NCAA Division I women's basketball season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_season) and was contested by the No. 1 seed [South Carolina Gamecocks](81087913.md) from the [Southeastern Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_Conference) (SEC) and the No. 1 seed [UCLA Bruins](80436779.md) from the [Big Ten Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Ten_Conference). The game was played on April 5, 2026, at [Mortgage Matchup Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortgage_Matchup_Center) in [Phoenix, Arizona](49121.md). This was the first time that Phoenix hosted the national championship game.[^cite_note-1] The Bruins won the game 79–51 for the program's first NCAA title, and [Lauren Betts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Betts) was named the [Most Outstanding Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_I_basketball_tournament_Most_Outstanding_Player). The Gamecocks lost their second straight title game.



UCLA's Gabriela Jaquez shoots a free throw.

## Participants

### South Carolina Gamecocks

Main article: [2025–26 South Carolina Gamecocks women's basketball team](81087913.md)

Coming off their 82–59 loss in the [2025 national championship game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_championship_game) to [UConn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UConn_Huskies_women's_basketball_team),[^cite_note-teague_04052026-2] South Carolina won the [2025–26 SEC regular season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Southeastern_Conference_women's_basketball_season?action=edit\&redlink=1) for the 10th time and advanced to the [SEC tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_SEC_women's_basketball_tournament) championship, which they lost to [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Texas_Longhorns_women's_basketball_team).[^cite_note-creme_03152026-3] A No. 1 seed in the [NCAA tournament](76476380.md),[^cite_note-creme_03152026-3] the Gamecocks advanced to the championship with a 62–48 win in the semifinals over [the Huskies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_UConn_Huskies_women's_basketball_team), ending their 52-game winning streak.[^cite_note-marshall_04042026-4] It was South Carolina's third consecutive national championship game,[^cite_note-5] and their fourth in five seasons.[^cite_note-6] They were seeking their fourth national title in nine seasons. The Gamecocks were led by [Joyce Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyce_Edwards), who was named a second-team [All-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NCAA_Women's_Basketball_All-Americans),[^cite_note-marshall_04042026-4] and [Raven Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raven_Johnson), a third-team All-American selection and the [SEC Defensive Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_Conference_Women's_Basketball_Defensive_Player_of_the_Year).[^cite_note-7]

### UCLA Bruins

Main article: [2025–26 UCLA Bruins women's basketball team](80436779.md)

After exiting the Final Four of the [2025 NCAA tournament](76476380.md) with a 85–51 loss in the semifinals to UConn,[^cite_note-teague_04052026-2] UCLA went undefeated in 2025–26 against all Big Ten opponents, winning the [Big Ten regular season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Big_Ten_Conference_women's_basketball_season) and [conference tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Big_Ten_women's_basketball_tournament) titles. It was their second consecutive Big Ten tournament championship. Senior center [Lauren Betts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Betts) earned first-team All-American honors for the second consecutive season,[^cite_note-8] and she was named both the [Big Ten Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Ten_Conference_Women's_Basketball_Player_of_the_Year) and [Defensive Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Ten_Conference_Women's_Basketball_Defensive_Player_of_the_Year).[^cite_note-creme_03152026-3] Senior guard [Kiki Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiki_Rice) was named a third-team All-American.[^cite_note-9] The Bruins were also seeded No. 1 in the NCAA tournament,[^cite_note-creme_03152026-3] and they returned to the Final Four after their first under head coach [Cori Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cori_Close) in 2025.[^cite_note-mcmanaman_04052026-10] UCLA reached the national championship game after a 51–44 win over Texas, who had handed them their only loss of the season.[^cite_note-marshall_04042026-4] It was the Bruins' first national championship game in the NCAA era.[^cite_note-11] They won a national championship in [1978](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_AIAW_National_Large_College_Basketball_Championship), when the [Association for Intercollegiate Athletics for Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Intercollegiate_Athletics_for_Women) was overseeing women's basketball,[^cite_note-marshall_04042026-4] until the NCAA took over in 1982.[^cite_note-feinberg_04052026-12]

## Game summary



[Lauren Betts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Betts) was named the [NCAA tournament Most Outstanding Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_I_basketball_tournament_Most_Outstanding_Player).

UCLA never trailed in the game and defeated South Carolina 79–51 for their 31st consecutive win. The 28-point margin was the third largest in a Division I women's championship game.[^cite_note-13] The Bruins' [Lauren Betts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Betts) had 14 points and 11 [rebounds](3521142.md) and was named the [tournament's Most Outstanding Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_I_basketball_tournament_Most_Outstanding_Player).[^cite_note-14] Fellow [senior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_\(education\)) teammate [Gabriela Jaquez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriela_Jaquez) added a game-high 21 points, 10 rebounds and five [assists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assist_\(basketball\)).[^cite_note-mcmanaman_04052026-10] All five of UCLA's [starters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starting_lineup) scored in double figures. The Gamecocks' 51 points was their season low.[^cite_note-ingemi_04052026-15]

The Bruins led 14–3 after five minutes while coaxing the Gamecocks' [post players](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post_player) into settling for [jump shots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jump_shot_\(basketball\)).[^cite_note-teague_04052026-2] UCLA's [Kiki Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiki_Rice) made a buzzer-beating [three-pointer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-point_field_goal) to end the first quarter, giving them a 21–10 lead.[^cite_note-ingemi_04052026-15] South Carolina shot 17% for its worst quarter of the season,[^cite_note-feinberg_04052026-12] with their leading scorer, [Joyce Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyce_Edwards), going 0-for-3.[^cite_note-teague_04052026-2] The Bruins led 36–23 at halftime after holding the Gamecocks to 25.7% shooting for the half.[^cite_note-ingemi_04052026-15] UCLA opened the second half with a 12–3 burst, with Jacquez scoring five points.[^cite_note-feinberg_04052026-12] South Carolina was outscored 25–9 in the third quarter, as the Gamecocks scored the second-fewest points ever in one quarter of the championship game.[^cite_note-mcmanaman_04052026-10] The Bruins extended their lead to 61–32 after three quarters, the largest lead entering the fourth quarter in the history of the championship game.[^cite_note-ingemi_04052026-15]

[Tessa Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessa_Johnson) led South Carolina in scoring with 14 points, and [Agot Makeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agot_Makeer) added 11 points off the [bench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution_\(sport\)).[^cite_note-teague_04052026-2] It was the Gamecocks' second consecutive championship game loss after winning the title in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_championship_game).[^cite_note-feinberg_04052026-12]
