# Ministry of Mines and Petroleum (Afghanistan)

The **Ministry of Mines and Petroleum** is responsible for the management of [natural resources in Afghanistan](17967521.md).[^cite_note-1] The Ministry is responsible for developing the [national economy](9895.md) and encouraging [private sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_sector) investment in minerals and hydrocarbon extraction to maximize income and boost national income.[^cite_note-2] As of 2024, the Minister of Mines and Petroleum is [Gul Agha Ishakzai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gul_Agha_Ishakzai)[^cite_note-3]

## Projects

Main article: [Mes Aynak mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mes_Aynak_mine)

One of the notable projects under the Ministry of Mines and Petroleum is [Mes Aynak mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mes_Aynak_mine), which is a major copper mine within Afghanistan.[^cite_note-4]
