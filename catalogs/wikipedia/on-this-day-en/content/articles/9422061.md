**Nakhchivan International Airport** ([Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language): *Naxçıvan Beynəlxalq Hava Limanı*) ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **NAJ**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **UBBN**) is a civilian airport and [Azerbaijani](746.md) military [airbase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbase) located in [Nakhchivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakhchivan_City),[^cite_note-WAD-1] the capital of the [Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic](385358.md), a [landlocked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landlocked_country) [exclave](4848945.md) of Azerbaijan. The airport was built between 1974-1976.[^cite_note-3]

## Facilities



Main Terminal

The airport is at an elevation of 2,863 feet (873 m) above [mean sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_sea_level). It has two [runways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway): 14R/32L with a concrete surface measuring 3,300 by 45 metres (10,827 ft × 148 ft) and 14L/32R with an [asphalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphalt_concrete) surface measuring 3,300 by 42 metres (10,827 ft × 138 ft).

## History

Main article: [2026 Iranian strikes on Azerbaijan](82580859.md)

On 5 March 2026, it was reported that Iranian missiles and drones struck the airport area, due to the ongoing [2026 Iran War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_War).[^cite_note-4] In the aftermath of the event, the air traffic was diverted to the [Iğdır Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%C4%9Fd%C4%B1r_Airport) in neighboring [Iğdır](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%C4%9Fd%C4%B1r), [Turkey](11125639.md) during the airport's temporary closure.[^cite_note-5]
