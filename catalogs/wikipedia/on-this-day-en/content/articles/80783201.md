The **2026 Indianapolis 500** (branded as the **110th Running of the Indianapolis 500 presented by Gainbridge** for sponsorship reasons) was an [IndyCar Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series) race that took place on May 24, 2026, at the [Indianapolis Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_Motor_Speedway) in [Speedway, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedway,_Indiana).[^cite_note-Schedule-7][^cite_note-8] The race was the seventh round of the [NTT IndyCar Series](80635394.md) season. The [month of May](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_500_traditions#Month_of_May) activities formally began with the [Sonsio Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Sonsio_Grand_Prix) on the [combined road course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oval_track_racing#Combined_road_course) on May 9. The race was televised on [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series_on_Fox).[^cite_note-Schedule-7] This was the first "500" since the [Fox Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Corporation) acquired a one-third stake in [IndyCar](30446232.md) and Indianapolis Motor Speedway parent company [Penske Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penske_Corporation) in August 2025.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

Practice began on May 12, and Time Trials were scheduled for May 16–17. The first day of qualifying, however, was rained out. Carb Day, the traditional final day of practice took place on May 22. The annual [Pit Stop Challenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pit_Stop_Challenge), however, was halted and cancelled due to rain.[^cite_note-Schedule-7] A full-field open test was held April 28–29, which included rookie orientation. [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou) was the [defending winner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indianapolis_500), and three-time defending [IndyCar Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series) champion. Palou qualified for the [pole position](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Indianapolis_500_pole-sitters), his second career Indy pole.[^cite_note-11] The Carb Day practice event on Friday, May 22 honored the late [NASCAR](21114.md) driver [Kyle Busch](1467805.md) – a two-time winner of the [Brickyard 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brickyard_400) – who died from complications with [pneumonia](52135.md) on Thursday, May 21.[^cite_note-12] [Dale Coyne Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Coyne_Racing) further honored Busch by decorating [Romain Grosjean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Grosjean)'s No. 18 car with the same number font Busch used throughout his NASCAR career.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] Later that evening, Busch was also remembered at the [Coca-Cola 600](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Coca-Cola_600).[^cite_note-15]

[Felix Rosenqvist](25501259.md) of [Meyer Shank Racing](30697656.md) started 4th and ran in the top ten nearly all day. Executing an alternate fuel strategy, he took the lead for the first time on lap 154 (of 200). Two late-race cautions set up a one-lap shootout to the finish. With the green and white flag waving for the final lap, [David Malukas](58540280.md) of [Team Penske](2321219.md) grabbed the lead from [Marcus Armstrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Armstrong), with Rosenqvist third. Rosenqvist and Armstrong raced side-by-side for nearly the entire final lap, while Malukas pulled out to a small lead. Coming out of turn four, Felix Rosenqvist made a strong run and beat David Malukas to the finish line by 0.0233 seconds, the closest margin of victory in Indy 500 history. It was Rosenqvist's first Indy 500 victory, and the second for [Meyer Shank Racing](30697656.md) ([2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Indianapolis_500), 2026).[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-\:0-17]

Several race records were broken, including 70 lead changes, the most in Indy history. The previous record was 68, set in [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Indianapolis_500). The previous closest finish came in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Indianapolis_500) when [Al Unser Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Unser_Jr.) beat [Scott Goodyear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Goodyear) by 0.043 seconds. [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou) and [Scott Dixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Dixon) traded the lead a record-breaking 26 times. The previous record of lead changes between the same two drivers was 18, between [James Hinchcliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Hinchcliffe) and [Ryan Hunter-Reay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Hunter-Reay) ([2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Indianapolis_500)). [Santino Ferrucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santino_Ferrucci) also completed the full 500-mile distance for a record-breaking eighth consecutive year. 2026 marked the fifth time in Indy 500 history that the lap leader on the final lap ([David Malukas](58540280.md)) failed to win. [Felix Rosenqvist](25501259.md) became the first 34-year-old to win an Indy 500 since [Helio Castroneves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helio_Castroneves) did so in [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Indianapolis_500).[^cite_note-18]

For the third year in a row, a driver attempted to complete "[Double Duty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_Duty)", as [Katherine Legge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Legge) attempted to compete in both the Indianapolis 500 and Coca-Cola 600 on the same day.[^cite_note-19] After a crash attempting to avoid a spinning [Ryan Hunter-Reay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Hunter-Reay) on Lap 18, Legge reached Charlotte and raced the Coca-Cola 600, finishing the latter 12 laps behind the winner and in 31st place, last among cars still in the race.[^cite_note-calamityoferrors-20]

## Race background

Main articles: [Indianapolis 500](159018.md) and [Indianapolis Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_Motor_Speedway)



Turn one at the Indianapolis Motor Speedway (*pictured in 2018*).

The [Indianapolis 500](159018.md), commonly called the Indy 500, is held at the [Indianapolis Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_Motor_Speedway), a 2.5-mile (4.02 km) paved [oval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oval_track_racing). First held in [1911](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1911_Indianapolis_500), it is currently a points-paying race of the [NTT IndyCar Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_IndyCar_Series). The event is contested by ["Indy cars"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_open-wheel_car_racing), a [formula](1618495.md) of professional-level, single-seat, open [cockpit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cockpit), [open-wheel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-wheel_car), purpose-built race cars. The race is the most prestigious event of the [IndyCar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series) calendar, and one of the oldest and [most important](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_Crown_of_Motorsport) automobile races in the world.[^cite_note-21] The race [traditionally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_500_traditions) has a field of 33 cars.[^cite_note-22]

### Track improvements

According to Indianapolis Motor Speedway president [Doug Boles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doug_Boles), track maintenance crews have been investigating bumps in Turn 2 since [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Indianapolis_500). During track evaluations prior to the [2025 race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indianapolis_500), the Turn 2 bump in question was considerably higher than in the past. The pavement was pressed in an attempt to reduce the severity. In July 2025, during the [Brickyard 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Brickyard_400), the severity of the bumps in Turn 2 were reported by drivers and crew chiefs during the track walk and all sessions.[^cite_note-23]

On September 23, 2025, construction crews excavated the section of track in question. It was determined that bricks originally installed in 1909 (that had been paved over by asphalt in 1937) had expanded and split due to moisture, which raised the pavement. The damaged bricks were removed and a new base was applied. The section of the track was repaved, followed by diamond grinding to blend the new section with the adjacent asphalt. The track was allowed to cure for four weeks before the late October Speedway tests for Firestone.[^cite_note-24]

### Rule changes

- A new entity, titled IndyCar Officiating, Inc. has been created, tasked with governing over the [IndyCar Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series) and [Indy NXT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indy_NXT). It includes a three-person Independent Officiating Board (IOB) consisting of: [Ray Evernham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Evernham), Raj Nair ([Ford](30433662.md)), and Ronan Morgan ([FIA](248019.md)).

- A modified qualifying format was announced on May 11, due to a lack of entries to necessitate the Last Chance Qualifying session that had been featured in previous years. During Saturday qualifying, those qualifying positions 1–15 would advance to Sunday qualifying sessions, while positions 16–33 would be locked in to their positions. During Sunday qualifying, the drivers qualified positions 10–15 on Saturday would participate in a "Final 15" session, in which the fastest three cars advance to the Top 12 session later in the day, while positions 13–15 would be locked in to their spots. The remaining session would proceed as in previous years; the fastest six cars in the Top 12 session moved on to the Fast Six, while positions 7–12 were locked. The Fast Six session would then determine starting positions 1–6.

Previously, pitting off of turn four (during practice) was only allowed during Post-Qualifying Practice (Monday) and on Carb Day.

### Sponsorship

Online financial services company Gainbridge signed an initial four-year deal for [presenting sponsorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naming_rights) of the Indianapolis 500 from 2019 to 2022.[^cite_note-36] A multi-year extension was signed in 2022.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38] On November 6, 2025, Gainbridge signed another multi-year contract extension. Terms of the deal were undisclosed.[^cite_note-39]

### 2026 IndyCar Series

Main article: [2026 IndyCar Series](80635394.md)

The 2026 Indianapolis 500 was the seventh race of the [2026 NTT IndyCar Series season](80635394.md).[^cite_note-40] Four different drivers won the first six races of the season. [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou) won the season opener at [St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Firestone_Grand_Prix_of_St._Petersburg). [Josef Newgarden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Newgarden) won at [Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Good_Ranchers_250), and [Kyle Kirkwood](57671103.md) won at [Arlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Java_House_Grand_Prix_of_Arlington). Palou won for the second and third time this season at [Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Children's_of_Alabama_Indy_Grand_Prix) and [Long Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Acura_Grand_Prix_of_Long_Beach). The [Sonsio Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Sonsio_Grand_Prix) on the Indianapolis Motor Speedway road course was won by [Christian Lundgaard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Lundgaard). It was the final race before the "500". Palou held a 27-point margin over Kirkwood in the championship points standings going into the Indianapolis 500 - the fourth year in a row Palou has led the points standings going into the "500".[^cite_note-\:2-41]

## Entry list

Further information: [2026 IndyCar Series § Confirmed entries](80635394.md#Confirmed_entries)



Four-time IndyCar Series champion [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou) (*pictured in 2024*) entered as the [defending](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indianapolis_500) Indy 500 winner.



Four-time Indy 500 winner [Hélio Castroneves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9lio_Castroneves) (*pictured in 2022*) had the most previous starts in the field with 25.

All entries utilized a [spec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-design_racing#Motorsport) [Dallara DW12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallara_DW12) chassis with the [UAK-18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallara_DW12#IR%E2%80%9318_Universal_Aero_Kit_\(UAK-18\)_\(2018%E2%80%932027\)) universal aero kit and the 2020-adopted aeroscreen. [Honda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Indy_V6) ([HRC US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Racing_Corporation_USA)) and [Chevrolet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet_Indy_V6) ([Ilmor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilmor)) are the engine providers. [Firestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firestone_Tire_and_Rubber_Company) was the exclusive tire supplier. This was the second year of [hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_electric_vehicle) [energy recovery system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_energy_recovery_system) powertrains at Indianapolis.[^cite_note-43]

The official entry list was released on May 11, with 33 confirmed entries.[^cite_note-44] The list included 25 full-time IndyCar Series entries,[^cite_note-racer-01-06-2026-45] and eight one-off or "Indy-only" entries.[^cite_note-Racer-Abel-46][^cite_note-racer-03-17-2026-HC-47][^cite_note-48][^cite_note-e.l.f._x_Katherine_legge-49][^cite_note-racer-03-17-2026-ts-50][^cite_note-51][^cite_note-Sato-52]

[Andretti Global](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andretti_Global) was planning to run a fourth car, in an "Indy-only" entry, with the #98. [Colton Herta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colton_Herta), who is competing full-time in [Formula 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIA_Formula_2_Championship) in [2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Formula_2_Championship), was the likely driver. The original Formula 2 calendar had no races scheduled for May, which would have allowed Herta to compete at Indy.[^cite_note-53] However, due to the [Iran war](82533582.md), which resulted in the cancellation of two races in [Bahrain](18933277.md) and [Saudi Arabia](349303.md), the Formula 2 schedule was revised with the addition of races in [Miami](53846.md) and [Canada](5042916.md), the latter of which was scheduled for the same day as the 500.[^cite_note-54][^cite_note-55] Herta was therefore no longer available due to the scheduling conflict, and Andretti Global thus elected to not run the car.[^cite_note-56]

[PREMA Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PREMA_Racing) had two Chevrolet engine leases for the 2026 IndyCar Series season, but was undergoing an ownership shake-up which left the team in limbo regarding competing in any of the 2026 events.[^cite_note-57] On April 10, it was announced PREMA would not enter at Indianapolis. As a result, their two engines were released to other teams.[^cite_note-58]

This was the first time since [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Indianapolis_500) that no bumping took place. [Devlin DeFrancesco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devlin_DeFrancesco), who raced full-time for [RLL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahal_Letterman_Lanigan_Racing) in 2025, and [Stefan Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Wilson), who last competed in 2023, were two drivers who aggressively pursued rides before the last entry was filled by Legge.[^cite_note-racer-02-28-2026-59]

## Testing and Rookie Orientation



[Mick Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mick_Schumacher) is one of four rookies entered. His father [Michael Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Schumacher) won the [U.S. Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Grand_Prix) at Indy five times.

Rookie rules for the Indianapolis 500 included the mandate that a driver pass a supervised high-speed oval test before he/she is allowed to participate in the official Rookie Orientation Program (ROP) at the Indianapolis Motor Speedway.[^cite_note-ICS-rulebook-2024-1-95] Approved venues for the initial high-speed oval track test included [Texas Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Motor_Speedway), [Homestead–Miami Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homestead%E2%80%93Miami_Speedway), and a few other select venues.[^cite_note-ICS-rulebook-2024-2-96]

The Rookie Orientation Program at Indianapolis consisted of three phases. For phase 1, each driver was required to complete ten laps between 205–210 mph (330–338 km/h), while demonstrating satisfactory car control, proper racing line, and safe interaction with other cars on the circuit. The laps did not have to be consecutive. Phase 2 was fifteen laps between 210–215 mph (338–346 km/h), and phase 3 was fifteen laps over 215 mph (346 km/h). Veteran drivers that had not competed in an IndyCar oval race since the [previous year's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indianapolis_500) Indy 500 were required to take a refresher test. The refresher test consisted of phase 2 and phase 3 of the aforementioned rookie test.[^cite_note-ICS-10-9-23-97]

### Testing – October 2025

On October 20–21, 2025, the Speedway organized private testing. [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou) ([Ganassi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chip_Ganassi_Racing)) and [Pato O'Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pato_O'Ward) ([Arrow McLaren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrow_McLaren)) participated in tire tests for [Firestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firestone_Tire_and_Rubber_Company), including evaluating the newly repaved section of track to remove bumps in turn 2. A new tire compound was developed in order to address weight balance issues stemming from the hybrid system first used at the Speedway in 2025. [Alexander Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Rossi) ([ECR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Carpenter_Racing)) and [Takuma Sato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takuma_Sato) ([RLLR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahal_Letterman_Lanigan_Racing)) tested [brake](65423.md) and [damper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shock_absorber) components for speedway-specific braking systems (used at Indianapolis and [Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series_at_Nashville_Superspeedway)).[^cite_note-racer-10-21-2025-98]

### Rookie oval tests

The series hosted a high-speed oval rookie evaluation test at [Nashville Superspeedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_Superspeedway) on November 6, 2025. Two drivers participated: [Dennis Hauger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Hauger) ([Coyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Coyne_Racing)) and [Caio Collet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caio_Collet) ([Foyt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._J._Foyt_Racing)). No incidents were reported. Both drivers completed their evaluations and were approved to take the Rookie Orientation Program at the April Open Test at Indianapolis.[^cite_note-99]

[Rahal Letterman Lanigan Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahal_Letterman_Lanigan_Racing) conducted a private oval evaluation test for rookie driver [Mick Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mick_Schumacher) at [Homestead–Miami Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homestead%E2%80%93Miami_Speedway) on February 4, 2026. Schumacher, guided by driver coach [Ryan Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Briscoe), took his first laps on an oval, completing 97 laps without incident. He posted a top lap of 194 mph.[^cite_note-racer-02-04-2026-100][^cite_note-101]

### Open Test Day 1 — Tuesday April 28



[Conor Daly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_Daly) (*pictured in 2023*) was fastest on Day 1 of the Open Test.

The first day of open testing was scheduled for April 28.[^cite_note-102] After a five-minute period for install laps, the track was first made available for veterans from 10\:05 a.m. to 12 p.m. Rookie Orientation and Refresher Tests took place from 12 p.m. to 2 p.m. The track was then open to all cars from 2 p.m. to 6 p.m.[^cite_note-OpenTestIMS-103]

- **Weather**: 64 °F (18 °C); Mostly cloudy early, fair late

- **Summary**: The first veteran session began as scheduled at 10\:05 a.m. A total of 24 drivers turned laps during the session, with [Josef Newgarden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Newgarden) running fastest at 223.415 mph (359.552 km/h).

  [^cite_note-104]

  &#x20;One minor incident occurred during the session, when [Romain Grosjean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Grosjean) collided with a bird, bringing out a caution for debris, and causing some clean up of his car to be needed.

  [^cite_note-test1res-105]

The Rookie Orientation and Refresher Test session began immediately following the veteran's test, with nine drivers scheduled to participate. [Jack Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Harvey_\(racing_driver\)), [Ed Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Carpenter_\(racing_driver\)), [Ryan Hunter-Reay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Hunter-Reay), and [Hélio Castroneves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9lio_Castroneves) completed their veteran refresher tests, while [Caio Collet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caio_Collet), [Jacob Abel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Abel_\(racing_driver\)), and [Mick Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mick_Schumacher) completed all three phases of their rookie tests. [Dennis Hauger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Hauger) completed phases 1 and 2 of his rookie test, and was given permission to complete the final phase during the full-field session later in the afternoon.

[^cite_note-106]

&#x20;[Katherine Legge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Legge), the final driver required to complete a refresher test, experienced clutch problems and was unable to complete any laps at speed. As a result, she was not permitted to participate in the full-field session.

[^cite_note-107]

The full-field test session began at 2\:00 p.m., with the track open until 6\:00 p.m. [Conor Daly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_Daly) set the fastest speed of the day, running a lap at 225.394 mph (362.736 km/h) late in the day. His teammate [Jack Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Harvey_\(racing_driver\)) ran the fastest "no-tow" lap — a lap without the assistance of [aerodynamic drafting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drafting_\(aerodynamics\)) — at 220.318 mph (354.567 km/h). Only one incident was reported. At 5\:34 p.m., [Christian Rasmussen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Rasmussen_\(racing_driver\)) came to a stop on track at the exit of turn 2 with a loose right-rear wheel. The car was undamaged, but needed to be towed off of the race track.

The scheduled was revised, shifting the early session to 11\:15 a.m. to 1\:15 p.m. Legge's ROP session was moved to 1\:15 p.m., and the afternoon session was changed to 2\:15 p.m. to 5 p.m.

- **Weather**: 63 °F (17 °C); cloudy to mostly cloudy

- **Summary**: Day two of testing began at 11\:15 a.m. after track drying from overnight rain was completed. Rookie driver [Caio Collet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caio_Collet) was fastest during the morning session, running a lap at 226.381 mph (364.325 km/h).

  [^cite_note-test2morn-111]

  &#x20;This lap would be the fastest of the day, as it was not matched during the afternoon session.   [^cite_note-112]

[^cite_note-113]

## Practice

### Opening Day — Tuesday May 12

## Time Trials

### Saturday, May 16

- **Weather**: 69 °F (21 °C), Scattered thunderstorms

- **Summary**: Persistent rain throughout the day prevented any track running on the scheduled first day of qualification. All qualifications were shifted to Sunday, and the scheduled "Fast 15" session was discarded in the revised condensed schedule.

  [^cite_note-qualday1-126]

### Sunday, May 17

- **Weather**: 83 °F (28 °C), Fair

- **Summary**: With the first day of qualification rained out, a revised schedule was announced for Sunday, with the scheduled "Top 15" session discarded. During the first session, which began at 12 p.m., all entries were given one guaranteed attempt. The cars qualifying in positions 1–12 in the first session advanced to the Top 12 session later in the day, while those in positions 13–33 were locked in to their positions. At 4 p.m., the Top 12 session was held, where the top six qualifiers advanced to the final Fast Six qualifying, while positions 7–12 were locked in to their positions. At 6\:00 p.m., the Top Six session started, which determined the top six starting positions.

  [^cite_note-qualday1-126]

#### Pre-qualifying practice

A one-hour practice session was held before qualifying, beginning at 9\:30 a.m. Only 14 entries turned laps during the session. One minor incident occurred when [Takuma Sato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takuma_Sato) drifted high in turn 3 and brushed the outside wall. The incident brought out a caution period, but Sato's car was not significantly damaged. [Christian Rasmussen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Rasmussen_\(racing_driver\)) was the fastest in the session, turning a lap at 232.255 mph (373.778 km/h).[^cite_note-127]

#### Full field qualifying

Full field qualifying began at noon. [Felix Rosenqvist](25501259.md) was the fastest in the session, running a four-lap average at 232.599 miles per hour (374.332 km/h). Rosenqvist was the only qualifier whose run was above 232 mph (373 km/h). [Caio Collet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caio_Collet) was the fastest rookie qualifier at 231.419 mph (372.433 km/h), good enough for seventh fastest in the session. Collet was the only rookie to advance to the Top 12 session. Also advancing to the Top 12 session were [David Malukas](58540280.md), [Conor Daly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_Daly), [Scott McLaughlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_McLaughlin), [Rinus VeeKay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rinus_VeeKay), [Alexander Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Rossi), [Scott Dixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Dixon), [Pato O'Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pato_O'Ward), [Santino Ferrucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santino_Ferrucci), [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou), and [Kyffin Simpson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyffin_Simpson).[^cite_note-128]

### Post-qualifying penalties

Hours after qualifying ended, Indycar announced that the cars of [Caio Collet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caio_Collet) and [Jack Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Harvey_\(racing_driver\)) had failed technical inspection due to using unapproved hardware for the Dallara-supplied energy management system. ## Post-qualifying practice

### Post-qualifying practice — Monday May 18

- **Weather**: 81 °F (27 °C); Mostly cloudy turning to thunderstorms late

- **Summary**: A two-hour session was scheduled from 1 p.m. to 3 p.m. At 1\:29 p.m., the first major incident of the month occurred. [Alexander Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Rossi) lost control and spun in turn two, and made heavy contact with the outside wall. [Pato O'Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pato_O'Ward) spun in avoidance, but collided with Rossi's car. Behind them, [Romain Grosjean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Grosjean) lost control of his car while trying to slow for the incident, and also impacted the wall. All three drivers climbed from their cars under their own power, but Rossi was held for extended evaluation at the infield care center and was later transported to [IU Methodist Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Health_Methodist_Hospital) for further evaluation.

## Carb Day events

### Wienie 500

The second annual Wienie 500 was held as part of Carb Day festivities. Six [Oscar Mayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Mayer) [Wienermobiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wienermobile), each representing a different regional [hot dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_dog) [variation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_dog_variations), took part in a two-lap, 5-mile race from a standing start. For the 2026 event, [Sonoran Dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonoran_hot_dog) was replaced by [Corn Dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corn_dog).[^cite_note-135] [Andy Richter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Richter) served as the "Commander in Beef" and gave the command to start engines.[^cite_note-136]

The #01 Chicago Dog took the lead at the start and led into turn one. As the field exited turn two and started down the backstretch, Chicago Dog threw a hard block on the #05 Seattle Dog, forcing it to the inside apron. The #03 Slaw Dog took the lead going into turn three, and led the first lap. Down the backstretch on lap 2, Chili Dog, Chicago Dog, and New York Dog went three-wide. The #02 New York Dog emerged as the leader. Down the frontstretch, Chili Dog attempted a slingshot pass on the outside, but came up short. New York Dog held on to win by about 3⁄4 length.[^cite_note-137]

### Pit Stop Challenge

The 47th annual [Pit Stop Challenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pit_Stop_Challenge) was scheduled for Friday May 22, after the completion of Carb Day practice. For 2026, the event once again picked up sponsorship from [Oscar Mayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Mayer) and was officially the "Oscar Mayer \$150,000 Pit Stop Challenge" for sponsorship reasons. The bracket was expanded back to 16 participants (with no byes), up from 14 the previous two years. The eligibility for participation in the event was as follows:[^cite_note-138]

- Best average finish of the top five in each race's pit stop performance ([2025 Indianapolis 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indianapolis_500) through the [2026 Sonsio Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Sonsio_Grand_Prix)).

- Each full-time team is permitted to enter their highest-ranked entrant.

- Indy-only teams.

- Full-time teams may be invited to enter their next highest-ranked entrant.

The final round was scheduled to be a best-of-three series, for a \$50,000 first prize. After five of the first-round matchups were completed, a light rain began to fall. The competition was halted to wait out the shower, but the rain began to fall harder, and the event was cancelled; it was the first Pit Stop Challenge to be cancelled due to rain since [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Indianapolis_500).[^cite_note-139]

## Race report

- Weather 72 °F (22 °C), Cloudy with occasional light rain.

### First half

At the start of the race, [Alexander Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Rossi) moved in to the lead ahead of pole sitter [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou). The two then exchanged the lead between themselves for the opening several laps.[^cite_note-summary-141] At lap 18, the Fox broadcast did a silent lap for Kyle Busch, but it was interrupted by the first caution of the race when [Ryan Hunter-Reay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Hunter-Reay) spun in turn two and made contact with the wall. [Katherine Legge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Legge) also spun to try to avoid Hunter-Reay and impacted the inside wall on the backstraight.[^cite_note-142] During the yellow, most of the field made their first pit stops. Palou emerged first of those that pitted, while Rossi suffered a slow pitstop and dropped farther down in the running order. [Rinus VeeKay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rinus_VeeKay), [Romain Grosjean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Grosjean), and [Caio Collet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caio_Collet) did not pit, and moved to the lead of the race. Racing resumed at lap 26, with Grosjean moving to the lead of the race. Behind them, [Ed Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Carpenter_\(racing_driver\)) lost control of his car in turn one after being three-wide with [Takuma Sato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takuma_Sato), resulting in him impacting the outside wall and bringing out the race's second caution period.[^cite_note-summary-141]

Racing resumed at lap 31, with Collet briefly taking the lead before those that had not stopped at the first caution needed to pit. Palou moved back in to the lead, and began to cycle with [Conor Daly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_Daly). At the next round of stops, [Felix Rosenqvist](25501259.md) moved in to second place, while Palou held the lead. A few laps later, [Scott Dixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Dixon) passed Rosenqvist for second, and Palou and Dixon began to exchange the lead between each other, working as teammates to try to hold the lead.[^cite_note-summary-141] At lap 92, the third caution of the race came as [Will Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Power) suffered a mechanical failure and spun on the pit lane exit lane on the fluid left by the car. Power did not make contact with any walls, but was out of the race.[^cite_note-143] At the same time as the caution came, [Alexander Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Rossi) came to a stop on pit lane with the car briefly catching fire. Rossi was also out of the race.[^cite_note-summary-141] The entire field made pit stops during the caution period, with Dixon emerging in the lead ahead of Palou and [Josef Newgarden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Newgarden), while [David Malukas](58540280.md) was fourth. The caution period was extended due to small amounts of rain at the north end of the track. At lap 105, the [race was briefly stopped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_red-flagged_IndyCar_Series_races) to allow the rain to pass.[^cite_note-summary-141]

### Second half

Once the rain passed, the race resumed under yellow. The green flag was shown again at lap 110, with Palou and Malukas jumping ahead of Dixon at the restart. [Conor Daly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_Daly) moved up to third position, while Palou and Malukas exchanged the lead ahead. At lap 116, the caution came out again for a brief rain shower, but no race stoppage was needed.[^cite_note-summary-141] Racing resumed at lap 124, but almost immediately the caution was needed again, as [Josef Newgarden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Newgarden) spun in turn four and heavily impacted the outside wall.[^cite_note-144] During this caution, several drivers elected to pit, most notably [Felix Rosenqvist](25501259.md), [Pato O'Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pato_O'Ward), and [Marcus Armstrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Armstrong), which put them on an alternate strategy to the other leaders.[^cite_note-summary-141]

Racing resumed at lap 132, with the leaders going four-wide in to turn one passing Malukas. Conor Daly emerged as the leader, with [Scott McLaughlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_McLaughlin) and Palou behind. Both McLaughlin and Palou passed Daly later and began to exchange the lead, but at lap 147 Malukas moved his way back in to the lead.[^cite_note-summary-141] Soon after, all leaders pitted, which moved Rosenqvist and O'Ward on the alternate strategy to the lead. O'Ward and Rosenqvist pitted with 36 and 34 laps to go respectively, handing the lead back to the battling Malukas and Palou, but eliminating their final pit stops while Malukas and Palou would still need to pit. Beginning at lap 175, the leading cars of the primary strategy pitted, giving the lead initially to O'Ward, but on lap 185 Rosenqvist moved in to the lead and began pulling away, while Malukas and Palou remained over 16 seconds behind after their final stops.[^cite_note-Malsher-145]

#### Finish



[Felix Rosenqvist](25501259.md) (no. 60) passing [David Malukas](58540280.md) on the final straightaway to take victory.

On lap 192, the sixth caution of the race came as [Caio Collet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caio_Collet) collided with the outside wall in turn two. IndyCar officials chose to stop the race to allow Collet's car to be removed and attempt to ensure a green-flag finish. After clearing Collet's car, the race resumed under caution. On lap 196, the green flag came out again, with [Marcus Armstrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Armstrong), who had been running third on the same strategy as Rosenqvist, moving in to the lead, while [David Malukas](58540280.md) moved in the second ahead of Rosenqvist. Behind them, [Mick Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mick_Schumacher) drifted wide in turn one and brushed the outside wall, bringing out the race's seventh and final caution. Schumacher's car was not severely damaged and he continued driving, allowing IndyCar to clear the track quickly and have a one-lap shootout to finish. The green and white came out on lap 200, with Malukas jumping to the lead, while Rosenqvist ran around the outside of Armstrong. Rosenqvist and Armstrong remained side-by-side through the whole lap until turn four, when Rosenqvist moved ahead. Despite having been side-by-side, Rosenqvist was still able to generate a run on Malukas, and in a photo-finish Rosenqvist pushed ahead to take victory. The final margin of victory was 0.0233 seconds, setting a new record for closest finish in Indianapolis 500, surpassing the old mark of 0.043 seconds that had been set in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Indianapolis_500).[^cite_note-Malsher-145][^cite_note-\:0-17] The race also saw 70 lead changes, setting a new record for most lead-changes in a single running of the race.[^cite_note-\:0-17] For winning, Rosenqvist earned \$4.34 million from a record purse of \$30,906,400.[^cite_note-postrace-146] Mick Schumacher was voted [Rookie of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_500_Rookie_of_the_Year) for his performance in the race.[^cite_note-postrace-146]

#### Post-race penalties

During post race technical inspection, the car of [Álex Palou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Palou) was found to have violated rules regarding maximum height for wing end plates. The team maintained their finishing position in the race, but were penalized five points in both the driver's and owner's championship and also given a \$10,000 fine.[^cite_note-147]

## Broadcasting

### Television

The race was [televised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Indianapolis_500_broadcasters) live by [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series_on_Fox) in the second year of a multi-year contract. On February 18, 2026, Fox announced their primary broadcast team for the IndyCar season, with [Will Buxton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Buxton) serving as lead commentator and driver analysts [James Hinchcliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Hinchcliffe) and [Townsend Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townsend_Bell) each returning from 2025.[^cite_note-148]

On May 13, 2026, Indianapolis Motor Speedway president Doug Boles announced that due to an sellout of grandstand tickets, the traditional [blackout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackout_\(broadcasting\)) in the Indianapolis media market would be lifted. Therefore, local Fox affiliate [WXIN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WXIN) carried the live telecast; this was the sixth time the blackout had been lifted since live flag-to-flag coverage began in [1986](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Indianapolis_500).[^cite_note-149]

On May 28, Fox announced that the race broadcast drew an average audience of 6.6 million viewers, with a peak of 8.349 million viewers between 4 p.m. and 4\:15 p.m.[^cite_note-ratings-6]

### Radio

The race was broadcast by the [Indianapolis Motor Speedway Radio Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_Motor_Speedway_Radio_Network). The chief announcer was Mark Jaynes in his eleventh year as chief announcer, and 31st year overall with the network. [Zach Veach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Veach) served as the driver expert, replacing regular analyst [Davey Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davey_Hamilton), who served as the strategist for [Jack Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Harvey_\(racing_driver\))'s entry for [Dreyer & Reinbold Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreyer_&_Reinbold_Racing).[^cite_note-150] Gone was Chris Denari who retired after last years race and taking his place in turn 4 was Ryan Myrehn.
