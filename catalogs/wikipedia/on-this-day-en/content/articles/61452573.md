**Yanbu Commercial Port** is among the ancient ports on the [Red Sea](26191.md). Its strategic importance comes from the fact that the port is a main gateway to the Muslim holy city of [Medinah](36636.md), as well as being a minor alternative to oil exports through the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md).[^cite_note-2]. It is a natural port surrounded by land and [coral reef](87410.md).[^cite_note-3]

The port, like many other Saudi ports, has undergone many development projects since the establishment of [Saudi Arabia](349303.md). The port sector, including Yanbu Commercial Port, is administered by the Saudi Ports Authority.[^cite_note-4]

Yanbu Commercial Port's operations grow faster as in 2018 only the port operated 3.33 million tons of cargo.[^cite_note-5] The port includes a 7,200-square-metre (78,000 sq ft) terminal to receive [pilgrims](23763.md#Islam).[^cite_note-6]

As of 2025, the port has three deepwater piers available for oil and gas condensate shipping. The [Saudi Aramco](290527.md) Yanbu Refinery is located adjacent to the port as well as the western terminus of the [East–West Crude Oil Pipeline](54166263.md) transporting oil from oil fields in eastern Saudi Arabia.
