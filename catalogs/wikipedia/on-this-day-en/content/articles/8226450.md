**São José da Tapera** (Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: \[ˈsɐ̃w ʒuˈɐ̃w ˈda taˈpɛɾɐ]) is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Brazil) located in the western of the [Brazilian state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Brazil) of [Alagoas](165082.md). Its population was 32,405 (2020)[^cite_note-6] and its area is 520 km<sup>2</sup> (200 sq mi).[^cite_note-7]
