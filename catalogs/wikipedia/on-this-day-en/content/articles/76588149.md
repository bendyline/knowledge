General elections were held in [Seychelles](27288.md) on 27 September 2025 to elect a [president](450100.md) and members of the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Seychelles\)),[^cite_note-1] with a second round of the presidential election held from 9 to 11 October after no candidate received a majority of the vote in the first round.[^cite_note-2]

The presidential election resulted in a victory for [Patrick Herminie](20114965.md) of the [United Seychelles Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Seychelles_Party), who received 53% of the vote. Incumbent president [Wavel Ramkalawan](2501053.md) of the [Linyon Demokratik Seselwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linyon_Demokratik_Seselwa) finished second with 47% of the vote.

## Electoral system

The [president of Seychelles](450100.md) is elected using the [two-round system](25932.md);[^cite_note-3] if no candidate receives a majority of the vote in the first round, a second round is held between the top two candidates.

Members of the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Seychelles\)) are elected by two methods; 26 (with a new constituency established in 2020) are elected from single-member constituencies using [first-past-the-post voting](261709.md), and up to a further ten are elected based on the percentage of votes received by each party; for each 10% of the total national vote received, a party gets one additional seat.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Campaign

Herminie and his United Seychelles party pledged to revert the retirement age down to 63, reduce bus fares by 40% and increase social security benefits. Ralph Volcère pledged to decriminalize recreational [cannabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis) as part of efforts to reduce the rate of drug addiction and put drug lords out of business.[^cite_note-10] Another key issue in the election was the Ramkalawan administration's decision to lease large areas of [Assumption Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assumption_Island), near the [World Heritage Site](44940.md) and marine reserve of [Aldabra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldabra), for 70 years to a Qatari-built luxury hotel chain, which triggered protests over environmental damage. This led Herminie to accuse Qatar of financing Ramkalawan's campaign and pledging to cancel the lease agreement.[^cite_note-11]

## Conduct

Early voting for elderly and essential workers was held on 25 September.[^cite_note-12] More than 77,000 people were eligible to vote.[^cite_note-13] For the second round, voting was held for outlying islands and some essential workers on 9 October, while voting in the country's three main islands was held on 11 October.[^cite_note-14]
