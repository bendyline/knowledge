This article lists the **heads of state of Ivory Coast**, officially the [Republic of Côte d'Ivoire](57744.md), since the country gained independence from [France](5843419.md) in 1960. [Alassane Ouattara](1173624.md) has been serving as President of Ivory Coast since 4 December 2010.

## Term limits

There is a two-term limit for the president in the new 2016 [Constitution of Ivory Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Ivory_Coast). This limit has been lifted for [Alassane Ouattara](1173624.md), allowing him to run for third and fourth terms.[^cite_note-termlimits-2]
