The **2026 Women's FIH Hockey World Cup final** was the final match of the [2026 Women's FIH Hockey World Cup](71081645.md), the 16th edition of the quadrennial world championship for women's national [field hockey](10886.md) teams organized by the [International Hockey Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Hockey_Federation). The match was played at [Wagener Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagener_Stadium) in [Amstelveen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amstelveen), Netherlands, on 29 Augudt 2026, and was contested by co-hosts [Netherlands](7325406.md) and [Argentina](3642950.md)[^cite_note-1].

The tournament involved co-hosts [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium_women's_national_field_hockey_team) and Netherlands alongside 14 other teams. The 16 teams competed in a group stage, from which 8 teams qualified for the second round and 4 later advanced to the semifinals.

For the Netherlands, it was their fourteenth final, while it was the sixth final for Argentina. This was the fifth time the two teams met in a World Cup final after [1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_Women's_Hockey_World_Cup), [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Women's_Hockey_World_Cup)[^cite_note-2], [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Women's_Hockey_World_Cup)[^cite_note-3], and [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Women's_FIH_Hockey_World_Cup)[^cite_note-4]. Both teams have won two finals against each other. This was a rematch of the 2022 final where the Dutch won 3–1.

Argentina won their third title after beating the Netherlands 3–1 on [penalties](42538249.md) after a 1–1 draw in [Amstelveen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amstelveen).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Background

The 2026 Women's FIH Hockey World Cup was the 16th edition of the [Women's FIH Hockey World Cup](6516384.md), the quadrennial world championship for women's national [field hockey](10886.md) teams organized by the [International Hockey Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Hockey_Federation). It is being held from 15 to 30 August 2026 in [Wavre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavre), Belgium and [Amstelveen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amstelveen), Netherlands.

Belgium and Netherlands were given the hosting rights in November 2022, beating bids from [Australia](4689264.md), [South Africa](17416221.md) and [Uruguay](31843.md) in the process. This is the second time the women's event is co-hosted after [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Women's_FIH_Hockey_World_Cup). This also marks Belgium's first and Netherlands' fifth time hosting. This is also the first time the men's and women's world cup are held as one joint tournament.

16 teams are participating for the third time after the expansion in 2018. Qualification was an amalgamation of the [FIH Pro League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_FIH_Pro_League), continental championships and the [World Cup Qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Women's_FIH_Hockey_World_Cup_Qualifiers). Qualification took place in December 2023 to March 2026 to decide the qualifiers.

Netherlands were the defending champions after defeating the Argentina 3–1 at the 2022 final in [Terrassa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrassa).
