**Iranshahr Airport** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **IHR**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **OIZI**) ([Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language): ایرانشہربالی پَٹ) is an [airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport) serving [Iranshahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranshahr_\(city\)), in Iran's [Sistan and Baluchestan](448105.md) province.[^cite_note-2]

On 16 July 2026, Iranian state media reported that the airport was hit by [U.S.](3434750.md) airstrikes as part of the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-3]
