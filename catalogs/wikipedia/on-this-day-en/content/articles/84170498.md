The men's [200 metres](1833981.md) was held over two rounds at the [2026 World Athletics Ultimate Championship](81879269.md) at the [National Athletics Centre](59274354.md) in [Budapest](36787.md), Hungary on 13 September 2026. It was the first time the World Ultimate Championship is held.[^cite_note-1] Athletes qualified by [wildcard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildcard_\(sports\)) or by their [world ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Athletics_Rankings).

## Qualification

For the 200 metres, sixteen athletes qualified, up to three by [wildcard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildcard_\(sports\)) for winners of the event at the [2024 Summer Olympic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_200_metres), the [2025 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Athletics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_200_metres), or the [2026 Diamond League final](84136543.md) and the others by their position at the [World Athletics Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Athletics_Rankings) for the event on 3 September 2026.[^cite_note-3]
