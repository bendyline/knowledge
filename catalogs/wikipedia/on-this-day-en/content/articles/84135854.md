**GPT-6** is a family of [large language models](73248112.md) (LLM) developed by American [artificial intelligence](1164.md) firm [OpenAI](48795986.md). GPT-6 Astra was released to the general public on September 4, 2026, while GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna were released on September 22, 2026. OpenAI is yet to release GPT-6 Luna to free-tier users.

## History

Following [a series of unsanctioned cyberattacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI%E2%80%93HuggingFace_incident) by OpenAI agents in July 2026, the company delayed the release of their next model to add more safeguards.[^cite_note-\:0-1] GPT-6 Astra was released as a limited preview on September 3, 2026, the same day it was unveiled.[^cite_note-2] The company publicly released the model to paid users the following day, in a restricted version that rejects certain prompts in areas such as [cybersecurity](7398.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-\:1-4]

## Capabilities

OpenAI called the model a "generational leap" for areas such as cybersecurity, professional work, software engineering, and science, with the company's president [Greg Brockman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Brockman) claiming that it could eventually be seen as the arrival of [artificial general intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_general_intelligence) (AGI).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The company also stated that GPT-6 Astra "is faster and capable of performing more tasks than any prior iteration"[^cite_note-7] and better at staying focused, adhering to task boundaries, understanding user intent, handling tedious tasks, and completing multi-step workflows.[^cite_note-\:2-8] OpenAI has mentioned several examples of tasks GPT-6 Astra could perform, including filling out tax returns, building video game scenes, ordering food, and conducting job searches.[^cite_note-9] It described the model as state of the art in coding, math, and navigating computers and web browsers.[^cite_note-\:02-10]

OpenAI's vice president of research, Aidan Clark, told reporters that GPT-6 Astra's development involved "by far" their largest training run: "It's the first time we've pretrained on more than 100,000 [GPUs](390214.md) at our [Stargate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stargate_LLC) site in Texas."[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## Safety

OpenAI described Astra as its most [aligned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_alignment) model yet,[^cite_note-\:02-10] but warned about its cybersecurity capabilities.[^cite_note-\:2-8]

GPT-6 Astra uses a new [reasoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reasoning_model) technique called "recurrent depth" or "looped [transformers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformer_\(deep_learning\))", which increases efficiency but "works in a way that obscures some or all of the AI's reasoning, otherwise known as its 'chain of thought'".[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] Despite limited use, this has been met with concerns regarding the model's monitorability.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] OpenAI's chief scientist [Jakub Pachocki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakub_Pachocki) said that preventing unintended harm from AI is increasingly difficult and may be a bottleneck to further [AI progress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progress_in_artificial_intelligence).[^cite_note-\:02-10]
