**MV *Hondius*** is a Dutch expedition cruise ship owned by [Oceanwide Expeditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanwide_Expeditions). Built by [Brodosplit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brodosplit) in [Split, Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split,_Croatia), the vessel entered service in 2019 and operates primarily in the Arctic and Antarctic regions. According to the owner, the *Hondius* is the world's first-registered [Polar Class 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_Class_6) ice-strengthened cruise ship.[^cite_note-3]

The ship received worldwide news coverage in May 2026 following [reports of eleven hantavirus infections among passengers and crew aboard](83111976.md), and several more cases were suspected.[^cite_note-4]

*Hondius* is 107.6 metres (353 ft 0 in) [long overall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length_overall) and 94.275 metres (309 ft 3.6 in) [between perpendiculars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length_between_perpendiculars), has a beam of 17.6 metres (57 ft 9 in), and draws 5.36 metres (17 ft 7 in) of water with a displacement of 5,633 tonnes (5,544 long tons).[^cite_note-seaweb-5] Her [gross tonnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_tonnage) is 6,603; [net tonnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_tonnage) 2,057; and [deadweight tonnage](1346384.md) 1,154 tonnes. Her hull and propulsion system are strengthened for navigation in ice-covered waters according to [Polar Class 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_Class_6), which is intended for summer and autumn operation in medium first-year ice, which may include old ice inclusions.[^cite_note-lr-2][^cite_note-6]

Unlike bigger and more luxurious cruise ships,[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-qc-8] *Hondius* is built to support expedition-style voyages and activity-based shore excursions in polar regions.[^cite_note-rotterdam-9][^cite_note-order-10] The ship can accommodate up to 170 passengers in 80 outside passenger cabins across eight categories from four-berth staterooms to grand suites with private balcony. The public spaces include a dining room, a lecture room, and an observation lounge, as well as two gangways and an indoor platform to embark [Zodiac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zodiac_\(boat\)) [inflatable boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inflatable_boat).[^cite_note-oceanwide_deckplans-11] The ship has a crew of 57 with an additional 13 guides and a doctor.[^cite_note-oceanwide_hondius-12]

The ship's propulsion system consists of two 2,130-kilowatt (2,860 hp) [12-cylinder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V12_engine) [Anglo Belgian Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo_Belgian_Corporation) (ABC) 12DZC four-stroke medium-speed [diesel engines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel_engines) geared to a single [controllable pitch propeller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controllable_pitch_propeller). This gives *Hondius* a service speed of 15 knots (28 km/h; 17 mph). Onboard electricity can be generated either with a shaft generator driven by [power take-off](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_take-off) from the ship's main propulsion or two 750-kilowatt (1,010 hp) auxiliary [diesel generators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel_generator).[^cite_note-seaweb-5][^cite_note-13] For maneuvering in ports as well as [maintaining station without dropping an anchor to seafloor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_positioning) during shore excursions, *Hondius* has two [transverse thrusters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bow_thruster): one in the bow and another in the stern.[^cite_note-riviera-14]

## History

### 2016–2019: Order, construction, delivery

In late 2016, Oceanwide Expeditions ordered an ice-strengthened expedition vessel from [Brodosplit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brodosplit) in [Split, Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split,_Croatia).[^cite_note-order-10][^cite_note-riviera-14][^cite_note-15] The ship, named *Hondius* after the Flemish [cartographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartographer) [Jodocus Hondius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodocus_Hondius),[^cite_note-rotterdam-9][^cite_note-Namesake-16] would be the first newly built ship for the company that had previously operated chartered or converted second-hand tonnage.[^cite_note-qc-8][^cite_note-17]

First steel was cut on 22 August 2017,[^cite_note-18] followed by [keel laying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel_laying) on 11 December 2017[^cite_note-19] and [launching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceremonial_ship_launching) on 10 June 2018.[^cite_note-20] The completed vessel was delivered on 22 May 2019 and on the same day Oceanwide Expeditions signed a new contract with the shipyard for the construction of a sister ship, [*Janssonius*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_Janssonius?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-obdelivery-1]

### 2019–2026: Expedition cruises

In June 2019, *Hondius* was off on her first excursion to [Spitsbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spitsbergen).[^cite_note-21]

In December 2019, *Hondius* participated in the search for the [Chilean Air Force C-130 airplane '990'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Chilean_Air_Force_C-130_crash) following her disappearance.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-viegtuig-23][^cite_note-24] She was the closest ship in the area, being 11 nautical miles (13 mi; 20 km) away from where the airplane disappeared from the radar.[^cite_note-viegtuig-23]

In March 2026, Oceanwide Expeditions signed a Letter of Intent for the construction and delivery of two new *Hondius*-class vessels. These eco-sail vessels are an iteration of the *Hondius* design, featuring additional sustainability-focused features including a hybrid [sail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sail) propulsion system. The first is planned for launch in 2029, with the second planned in 2030.[^cite_note-25]

Video footage of *Hondius* during the [hantavirus outbreak](83111976.md).

### 2026: Hantavirus outbreak

Main article: [MV Hondius hantavirus outbreak](83111976.md)

In April 2026, an outbreak of [hantavirus](416945.md) on board *Hondius* killed three people and left another seriously ill.[^cite_note-26] Thereafter, the ship was placed under isolation. She was anchored off the coast of the [Cape Verde Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verde_Islands) in early May 2026, with passengers unable to disembark.[^cite_note-BBCcy0294829ndo-27] *Hondius* arrived at [Tenerife](269223.md), Canary Islands, on 10 May. Her passengers were flown home.[^cite_note-BBCcn7pzmg8zeno-28] *Hondius* arrived at [Rotterdam](26049.md) on 18 May. The 27 people on board were quarantined and the ship was disinfected.[^cite_note-DN180526-29]
