**Nineveh Governorate**[^cite_note-8] is a [governorate](222800.md) in northern [Iraq](7515928.md). It has an area of 37,323 km<sup>2</sup> (14,410 sq mi) and a population of 4,261,980 people as of 2024.[^cite_note-9] Its largest city and provincial capital is [Mosul](200475.md), which lies across the [Tigris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tigris) river from the ruins of ancient [Nineveh](21699.md). Before 1976, it was called *Mosul Province* and included the present-day [Dohuk Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dohuk_Governorate).[^cite_note-10]

The region is home to many historical sites including the ancient Assyrian city of [Nineveh](21699.md), and the ruins of [Hatra](200437.md), a UNESCO [World Heritage Site](44940.md) which was part of the 2nd-century Arab [Kingdom of Hatra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hatra). An ethnically, religiously and culturally diverse region, where the second largest city, [Tal Afar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tal_Afar), has an almost exclusively [Turkmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Turkmen) population.[^cite_note-11]

## Recent history and administration



Former governor [Atheel al-Nujaifi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheel_al-Nujaifi) in the [Yezidian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yezidian_Academy), [Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover), Germany, 2014

Its two cities endured the [2003 U.S.-led invasion of Iraq](201936.md) and emerged unscathed. In 2004, however, Mosul and Tal Afar were the scenes of fierce battles between [US](3434750.md)-led troops and [Iraqi insurgents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_insurgency_\(2003%E2%80%932011\)). The insurgents moved to Nineveh after the [Battle of Fallujah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Battle_of_Fallujah) in 2004.

After the invasion, the military of the province was led by (then Major General) [David Petraeus](3223944.md) of the [101st Airborne Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/101st_Airborne_Division) and later by (then Brigadier General) [Carter Ham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Ham) as the multi-national brigade for Iraq. During the time, the American civil head of the local office of the [Coalition Provisional Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalition_Provisional_Authority) was US Foreign Service Officer and former Kurdish refugee to the States, [Herro Mustafa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herro_Mustafa). Mustafa administered her nominees on the provincial council and through members of the Kashmoula family.

In June 2004, [Osama Kashmoula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osama_Kashmoula) became the interim governor of the province and in September of the same year he was assassinated en route to Baghdad. He was succeeded as interim Governor by [Duraid Kashmoula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duraid_Kashmoula), who was elected governor in January 2005. Duraid Kashmoula resigned in 2009.[^cite_note-12] In April 2009, [Atheel al-Nujaifi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheel_al-Nujaifi), a hardline Arab nationalist and member of [Al-Hadba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hadba), became governor.[^cite_note-13] While al-Nujaifi's Arab [Muttahidoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muttahidoon) bloc lost its majority to the Kurdish [Brotherhood and Coexistence Alliance List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdistan_List) in the [2013 provincial election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Ninawa_governorate_election), al-Nujaifi was reelected as governor by a larger Sunni Arab coalition[^cite_note-14] that was later formalized as the [Nahda Bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahda_Bloc?action=edit\&redlink=1).

In June 2014, [insurgents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurgents) from the [Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant](9087364.md) (known as ISIS or ISIL) [overran the capital Mosul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Mosul_offensive), forcing an estimated 500,000 refugees to flee the area,[^cite_note-15] including governor al-Nujaifi,[^cite_note-16] who was subsequently deposed by the [Iraqi Parliament](1445450.md).[^cite_note-17]

While the Kurdish list proposed Hassan al-Allaf, an Arab affiliated with the [Islamic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Islamic_Party),[^cite_note-18] the provincial council elected [Nofal Hammadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nofal_Hammadi_al-Sultan?action=edit\&redlink=1) (formerly Loyalty to Nineveh List) with the votes of the Nahdha bloc.[^cite_note-19]

An [offensive to retake Mosul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Mosul_\(2016\)) from ISIL control began in October 2016, with Iraqi and Kurdish soldiers supported by a U.S.-led coalition of 60 nations.[^cite_note-atlantic1020-20]

## Geography

### Borders

The province borders the governorates of [Dohuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duhok_Governorate), [Kirkuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirkuk_Governorate), [Erbil](463860.md), [Saladin](200537.md), and [Anbar](467036.md). It also shares a border with [Syria](7515849.md), mostly [Al-Hasakah Governorate](1429110.md), and [Deir ez-Zor Governorate](7445749.md).

Nineveh Province is multiethnic, with [Arabs](2185.md) constituting the majority,[^cite_note-24] while [Assyrians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assyrian_people), [Turkmens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Turkmen), [Kurds](17068.md), and [Yazidis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yazidis) who live in both in towns and cities, and in their own specific villages and regions, constitute the minority. There are also many [Armenians](387816.md), [Kawliya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romani_people_in_Iraq), [Mandeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandaeans), and [Shabaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shabaks).

The majority are [Sunni Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam), with 80% of the [Arabs](2185.md) and [Turkmens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Turkmen) being Sunni Muslim, as well [Kurds](17068.md) also being Sunni Muslim. About 5–10% of the population is [Shia Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Muslim). Generally, [Yazidis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yazidis), [Shabaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shabaks) and [Mandeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandaeans) are followers of their respective heritage religions, [Yazidism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yazidism), [Shabakism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shabakism), and [Mandaeism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandaeism).

The primary spoken language is [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic). Minority languages include [Turkmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Turkmen), [Neo-Aramaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Aramaic_languages) dialects, [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_languages) (predominantly [Kurmanji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurmanji)) and [Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_language).

### Proposed Assyrian autonomous region

Main article: [Proposals for Assyrian autonomy in Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proposals_for_Assyrian_autonomy_in_Iraq)

Many Assyrian leaders advocate an autonomous [Assyrian homeland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assyrian_homeland) within the Nineveh Province (mostly in the [Nineveh Plains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nineveh_Plains) region) for the [Assyrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assyrians_in_Iraq) population.[^cite_note-25]
