The **Ministry of Defence** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Ministerio de Defensa*, **MINDEF**) is the government ministry responsible for safeguarding [national security](240468.md) on land, sea and air. It exercises command over the [Peruvian Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Peru) composed of the [Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peruvian_Army), the [Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peruvian_Navy) and the [Air Force](1040948.md).

## History



Quiñones Building, the ministry's headquarters.

In 1821, [José de San Martín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_de_San_Mart%C3%ADn) created three ministries of the [Protectorate of Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protectorate_of_Peru): those of [Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economy_and_Finance_\(Peru\)) (*Hacienda*), War and Navy (*Guerra y Marina*) and [Foreign Affairs](17829557.md) (*Relaciones Exteriores*). They were again reestablished in 1844 after a series of changes in government.[^cite_note-1]

On March 21, 1824, [Simón Bolívar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sim%C3%B3n_Bol%C3%ADvar) issued a decree in [Trujillo](476649.md) reducing the three ministries to a single one, which would become the **General Secretariat of Affairs of the Peruvian Republic** (*Secretaría General de los Negocios de la República Peruana*), under Colonel José Gabriel Pérez. However, shortly thereafter, the Governing Junta repealed the decree, reestablishing the three ministries.[^cite_note-2]

On October 13, 1920, during the [second government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Peru_\(1919%E2%80%931930\)) of President [Augusto B. Leguía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augusto_B._Legu%C3%ADa), the Ministry of War and Navy was separated into two ministries through law N° 4003. The *[Navy Aviator Corps](1040948.md)* ([Spanish](26825.md): *Cuerpo de Aviadores de la Armada*) was created the same year as part of the [Peruvian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peruvian_Navy), with the Navy ministry being reorganised as the *Ministry of Navy and Aviation* ([Spanish](26825.md): *Ministerio de Marina y Aviación*), combining both military entities until 1941, when the *Ministry of Aviation* (also known as the "Ministry of Aeronautics" [Spanish](26825.md): *Ministerio de Aviación; Ministerio de Aeronáutica*)[^cite_note-3] was created.[^cite_note-4]

The Ministry of Defence was created by Law No. 24654 on 1 April 1987, during the government of [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Presidents_of_Peru) [Alan García](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Garc%C3%ADa). It absorbed the aforementioned Ministries of War, Navy and Aeronautics as well as the [Joint Command of the Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Command_of_the_Armed_Forces_of_Peru) and the National Defence Secretariat.[^cite_note-5]

## Organisation

The ministry is organised as follows:

- *Secretaría General* (General Secretariat)

- *Viceministerio de Asuntos Administrativos y Económicos* (Viceministry of Administrative and Economic Matters)

- *Viceministerio de Asuntos Logísticos y de Personal* (Viceministry of Logistics and Personnel Matters)

- *Dirección Nacional de Política y Estrategia* (National Directorate of Politics and Strategy)

- *Comando Conjunto de las Fuerzas Armadas* ([Joint Command of the Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Command_of_the_Armed_Forces_of_Peru))

- *Ejército del Perú* ([Peruvian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peruvian_Army))

- *Marina de Guerra del Perú* ([Peruvian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peruvian_Navy))

- *Fuerza Aérea del Perú* ([Peruvian Air Force](1040948.md))

Entities under the ministry include:

- *[Benemérita Sociedad Fundadores de la Independencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Founders_of_Independence_Society)*

- *Centro Nacional de Estimación, Prevención y Reducción del Riesgo de Desastres* (CENEPRED)

- *[Comisión Nacional de Investigación y Desarrollo Aeroespacial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Commission_for_Aerospace_Research_and_Development)* (CONIDA)

- *Escuela Nacional de Marina Mercante* (ENAMM)

- *[Instituto Geográfico Nacional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Geographic_Institute_\(Peru\))* (IGN)

- *[Servicios Industriales de la Marina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Servicios_Industriales_de_la_Marina)* (SIMA)

- *Fábrica de Armas y Municiones del Ejército* (FAME S.A.C.)

- *Servicio de Mantenimiento* (SEMAN)

- *Secretaría de Defensa Nacional*

- *[Centro de Altos Estudios Nacionales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Higher_National_Studies)* (CAEN)

- *[Instituto Nacional de Defensa Civil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Civil_Defence)* (INDECI)
