**Ebrahim Zolfaghari** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): ابراهیم ذوالفقاری) is an Iranian military officer and spokesperson for the [Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters](51128846.md), the joint operational command of the [Islamic Republic of Iran Armed Forces](14305018.md).[^cite_note-reuters-1][^cite_note-ht-2]

He is known for representing Iran’s military leadership in official communications and for his public statements during regional tensions.[^cite_note-ht-2]

## Career

Zolfaghari holds the rank of Brigadier General within Iran's military structure and is affiliated with the country's defense establishment.[^cite_note-ht-2]

He serves as spokesperson for the [Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters](51128846.md), which coordinates operations between the [Islamic Republic of Iran Army](48259216.md) and the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC).[^cite_note-reuters-1][^cite_note-aj2-3]

## Public profile

Zolfaghari gained international attention in 2026 during heightened tensions involving Iran, the [United States](3434750.md), and [Israel](9282173.md), especially in the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-reuters-1][^cite_note-ht2-4]

He stated that the United States was "negotiating with itself" while rejecting diplomatic efforts.[^cite_note-reuters-1] He also said that wars are decided on the battlefield rather than on social media.[^cite_note-aj-5] He referenced U.S. President [Donald Trump](4848272.md)'s phrase "You're fired" in a televised message, which was widely reported.[^cite_note-ht-2][^cite_note-ht2-4]

## Public image

Zolfaghari is regarded as part of Iran’s strategic communications apparatus, delivering official narratives on behalf of military leadership.[^cite_note-ht-2]
