**Muraikh** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مريخ) is a district of [Al Rayyan City](57488319.md) in the municipality of [Al Rayyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Rayyan) in [Qatar](8238258.md).[^cite_note-3]

## Etymology

The district derives its name from *markh* (*[Leptadenia pyrotechnica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leptadenia_pyrotechnica)*), the local term used for a tree that grows abundantly in the area.[^cite_note-dalila-4]

## History

In 1908, [J.G. Lorimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Gordon_Lorimer_\(1870-1914\)) documented Muraikh in the *[Gazetteer of the Persian Gulf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gazetteer_of_the_Persian_Gulf,_Oman_and_Central_Arabia)*, giving its location as "9 miles west by south of [Dohah](26214389.md)". He mentioned the presence of a masonry well, 2 fathoms deep, which yielded good water.[^cite_note-5]

## Administration

When free elections of the [Central Municipal Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Municipal_Council) first took place in Qatar during 1999,[^cite_note-6] Muraikh was designated the constituency seat of constituency no. 16.[^cite_note-firstelec-7] It would remain constituency seat in the next three consecutive elections until the [fifth municipal elections in 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatari_municipal_elections,_2015), when it was made part of constituency no. 5.[^cite_note-8] In the inaugural municipal elections in 1999, Nasser Fallah Al Dosari won the elections, receiving 40.4%, or 201 votes. Trailing behind Al Dosari was Ali Salem Al Suwaidi, who attained 34.5%, or 172 votes.[^cite_note-firstelec-7] Salem Mohammed Al Nabat won the 2002 elections.[^cite_note-9] In the [third municipal elections in 2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatari_municipal_elections,_2007), Mohammed Salem Al Qamra was elected constituency representative.[^cite_note-10] Al Qamra successfully retained his seat in the [2011 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatari_municipal_elections,_2011).[^cite_note-11]
