The **Regional Government of Moquegua** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Gobierno Regional de Moquegua*; **GORE Moquegua**)[^cite_note-1] is the [regional government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Governments_of_Peru) that represents the [Department of Moquegua](714958.md). It is the body with [legal identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_identity) in [public law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_Peru) and its own assets, which is in charge of the administration of [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Peru) of the department in [Peru](170691.md). Its purpose is the social, cultural and economic development of its constituency. It is based in the city of [Moquegua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moquegua).
