The **Uganda Police Force** is the national [police force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police) of [Uganda](31816.md). The head of the force is called the [Inspector General of Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector_General_of_Police) (IGP). The current IGP is Abbas Byakagaba. Byakagaba replaced former IGP, Martins Okoth Ochola on 18 May 2024.[^cite_note-18mayIGP-2] Recruitment to the forces is done annually.[^cite_note-3]

## History

The Uganda Police Force was established in 1906 by the British administration.[^cite_note-\:0-4] At that time, it was referred to as the Uganda Armed Constabulary with the primary responsibility of quelling "riots and unrest."[^cite_note-\:0-4]

On 25 May 1906, then Captain (later Brigadier General) William F.S Edwards, DSO, arrived in Uganda and became the first Inspector General of the Uganda Protectorate Police.[^cite_note-5] [Brigadier General William FS Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigadier_General_William_FS_Edwards) was regarded as a "stern disciplinarian and an excellent administrator." He held the IGP appointment until 1908, but held a position in administration up to the time of his retirement in 1922.[^cite_note-\:1-6][^cite_note-\:2-7]

The size of the force was reduced from 8,000 to 3,000 in 1986.[^cite_note-\:0-4] Up until April 2014, the official name of the government agency was **Uganda Police Force**. On that day, the IGP publicly announced the name change to Uganda National Police.[^cite_note-8]

## Controversies

Uganda police force has had a number of incidences that have been cited as controversial for example use of force against the opposition political parties like [FDC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forum_for_Democratic_Change) and [NUP](64618028.md). It has also brutally manhandled leaders like [Kiiza Besigye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kizza_Besigye), [Erias Lukwago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erias_Lukwago), [Betty Nambooze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betty_Nambooze) and [Bobi Wine](25321945.md).

These incidents have led to the organisation being identified as partisan and intolerant to dissent.
