In April 2026, an outbreak of [hantavirus infection](21180942.md) caused by the [Andes virus](6944726.md) was identified on the Dutch cruise ship [MV *Hondius*](83110814.md). There were ten confirmed cases and two suspected cases directly linked to the [outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disease_outbreak) as of 22 May.[^cite_note-ECDC-4][^cite_note-Burke-22May-3] There have been three deaths, two of which have been confirmed as caused by the Andes virus,[^cite_note-Van_Campenhout-2026-7][^cite_note-ECDPC-2026-8] the last on 2 May.[^cite_note-Stump-2026-9] By 11 May, all passengers had disembarked and been evacuated, many to their home countries for [quarantine](25237.md).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-Oceanwide-2026b-11] On 2 July 2026, the [World Health Organization](33583.md) officially declared the outbreak to be over, after the last close contact was released from quarantine.[^cite_note-Guardian-2_July-12]

The Andes virus is the only known [hantavirus](416945.md) to [spread between humans](63917263.md). It spreads through close, sustained contact between people, and it may be airborne.[^cite_note-ISH-2026-13][^cite_note-UK2021-14] The [World Health Organization](33583.md) has emphasized that the risk of an epidemic is low, as previous outbreaks have only involved transmission in close-contact settings.[^cite_note-15]

The ship departed from [Ushuaia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ushuaia), Argentina, on 1 April with plans to visit Antarctica and several islands in the South Atlantic. A passenger died on board on 11 April. His body was taken ashore in [Saint Helena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Helena) on 24 April, where 30 passengers disembarked, including his wife, who died two days later in a [Johannesburg](58893.md) hospital. Another passenger died on board on 28 April. A British passenger was sent to Johannesburg on 2 May for treatment in critical but still stable condition. Although the ship was docked at [Praia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praia), Cape Verde, for three days, no one disembarked, as local facilities were unable to handle a safe evacuation. After receiving approval from [Spanish health authorities](33528060.md), the ship departed for [Tenerife](269223.md), Canary Islands, on 6 May with additional medical resources and 147 individuals on board, arriving on 10 May. Passengers disembarked and evacuation flights repatriated passengers to six European countries and Canada. As of 15 May 2026, former passengers were hospitalized or quarantined in Australia, Canada, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Saint Helena, Singapore, South Africa, Spain, Switzerland, Turkey, and the United States.&#x20;

On 18 May, MV *Hondius* arrived in Rotterdam where everyone was retested and then disembarked. The two Dutch medical officers returned home for self-quarantine. The 23 crew from four countries entered quarantine in Rotterdam. The body of a deceased passenger was removed for cremation, and the vessel began disinfection in preparation for returning to service.[^cite_note-Van_Campenhout-17May-16][^cite_note-Oceanwide-18May-17]

## Background

### Hantavirus

Main article: [Andes virus infection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andes_virus_infection)



Locations the Andes virus usually occurs in red, orange, and yellow.

[^cite_note-ArgenLoc-18]

[^cite_note-ChileLoc-19]

[Hantaviruses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hantaviruses) are a group of over fifty types of viruses that infect [rodents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodent) and sometimes humans.[^cite_note-douglas-2021-20][^cite_note-hesman-saey-2026-21] In rodents, [Hantavirus infection](21180942.md), while usually persistent, does not result in symptoms. Hantaviruses may transmit through rodent populations via aerosols or droplets from infected rodent [feces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feces), [urine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urine), [saliva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saliva), and [blood](3997.md);[^cite_note-douglas-2021-20][^cite_note-aikman-2026-22] through the consumption of [contaminated food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contaminated_food); or from virus particles shed from skin or fur.[^cite_note-23]

[Andes virus](6944726.md) is a species of the *[Hantaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hantaviridae)* taxonomic family of viruses and the only known hantavirus to spread via [human-to-human transmission](63917263.md). Past human outbreaks have indicated that transmission of the Andes virus is possible under specific close-contact conditions.[^cite_note-ISH-2026-13][^cite_note-UK2021-14] The routes and mechanisms of transmission have not been definitively characterized, but [airborne transmission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airborne_transmission) is considered a possibility.[^cite_note-ISH-2026-13][^cite_note-UK2021-14] Transmission typically occurs in cases of close sustained contact, such as household exposure, caregiving without [personal protective equipment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_protective_equipment), and prolonged exposure in poorly ventilated or crowded settings.[^cite_note-ISH-2026-13][^cite_note-UK2021-14] In humans, Andes virus usually causes [hantavirus pulmonary syndrome](39903079.md) – a severe illness affecting the heart and lungs with an [incubation period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incubation_period) of one to seven weeks.[^cite_note-24] Andes virus outbreaks are associated with high case fatality rates of 20 to 40 percent.[^cite_note-ISH-2026-13]

On 6 May, it was confirmed that the Andes virus, which is normally found in the [Andes mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andes_mountains) of Argentina and Chile,[^cite_note-25] was responsible for the outbreak on the MV *Hondius*.[^cite_note-aikman-2026-22][^cite_note-26] The spread of the virus on board has been at least partially attributed to human-to-human transmission.[^cite_note-roberts-2026-27] Early reports identified [Ushuaia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ushuaia), Argentina, as a possible site of infection. However, no cases of the virus have ever been recorded there or in the region of [Tierra del Fuego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_del_Fuego), and the city lies 1,500 km south of the endemic range of the [long-tailed mouse subspecies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oligoryzomys_longicaudatus) known to carry the virus.[^cite_note-28]

### MV *Hondius*

Main article: [MV *Hondius*](83110814.md)

The MV *Hondius* is owned by the Dutch company [Oceanwide Expeditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanwide_Expeditions).[^cite_note-asadu-risemberg-2026-29] The ship has accommodation for 196 passengers across 95 cabins, and a crew of 72.[^cite_note-30] The ship departed from [Ushuaia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ushuaia), Argentina,[^cite_note-asadu-risemberg-2026-29] on 1 April with 114 passengers and 61 crew.[^cite_note-oceanwide_8may-31] The first people infected had been travelling farther north in Argentina and into Chile before arrival in Ushuaia.[^cite_note-32] The ship had plans to visit [Antarctica](18959138.md) and "several isolated islands in the South Atlantic".[^cite_note-asadu-risemberg-2026-29]

After disembarkation at St. Helena, 149 people of 23 nationalities remained on board *Hondius*.[^cite_note-oceanwide_8may-31][^cite_note-hesman-saey-2026-21] Passengers mostly came from Spain, France, the United Kingdom, and the United States, while the bulk of the crew came from the Philippines.[^cite_note-33] Berth prices on the cruise ranged from €14,000 to €22,000.[^cite_note-reuters_roy-34]

## Timeline

### April

The ship departed from [Ushuaia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ushuaia) on 1 April with 175 passengers and crew.[^cite_note-oceanwide_8may-31] On 6 April, a 70-year-old Dutch man began showing symptoms,[^cite_note-carroll-2026-35] and he died on board on 11 April,[^cite_note-asadu-risemberg-2026-29][^cite_note-carroll-2026-35] initially attributed to generic [natural causes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_causes).[^cite_note-carroll-2026-35] The ship stopped at the [British Overseas Territory](207114.md) of [Tristan da Cunha](31361.md) on 13–15 April.[^cite_note-green-36][^cite_note-kendall-2026a-37] On 24 April, the Dutchman's body was taken off the ship[^cite_note-aikman-2026-22][^cite_note-asadu-risemberg-2026-29] upon the ship's arrival at the British Overseas Territory of [Saint Helena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Helena). At this time, 29 passengers disembarked the ship; among them was the man's 69-year-old widow, who later took a plane to South Africa.[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-Oceanwide-2026a-64] The disembarked passengers were from 12 countries and returned home before [contact tracing](1724851.md) began.[^cite_note-ctv_7may-65]

On 25 April, the widow, who had unknowingly contracted the virus, boarded [KLM](16882.md) flight KL592 from [Johannesburg](58893.md) to [Amsterdam](844.md), but she was removed from the plane before takeoff due to her medical condition,[^cite_note-66] and she died in a hospital in South Africa on 26 April.[^cite_note-aikman-2026-22] The widow was on the plane for 45 minutes.[^cite_note-rtl_7may-67] After stopping in Saint Helena, the ship continued on to the British Overseas Territory of [Ascension Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascension_Island),[^cite_note-68] where an ill British passenger was removed and flown to South Africa for hospital care. On 27 April, *Hondius* left Ascension Island.[^cite_note-69]

### May

#### 1–5 May

On 2 May, a German woman died on board,[^cite_note-asadu-risemberg-2026-29][^cite_note-Edwards-2026-47] and, as of 8 May, her body remains on board the ship.[^cite_note-sample-2026-70] Following the identification of the outbreak on 4 May,[^cite_note-Oceanwide-2026a-64] passengers were told to limit close contact and frequently use [hand sanitizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hand_sanitizer).[^cite_note-parakozov-2026b-71] On 2 May, the [World Health Organization](33583.md) (WHO) first received a report of the hantavirus outbreak.[^cite_note-CNN/tenerife-arrive-72]

On 3 May, the ship docked in [Praia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praia), the capital of [Cape Verde](18962637.md).[^cite_note-aikman-2026-22][^cite_note-asadu-risemberg-2026-29] The authorities said they were sending medical supplies and officials to support the ship, and officials in Praia also expanded safety protocols near the port as a precautionary measure.[^cite_note-asadu-risemberg-2026-29] The Cape Verdean government announced the creation of an isolation area and the coordination of a multidisciplinary team to assist the ship's passengers and crew.[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74] Around the same time, South African officials began precautionary contact tracing.[^cite_note-asadu-risemberg-2026-29] By 4 May, gene sequencing had identified the Andes virus in at least one infected person.[^cite_note-75]

#### 6 May



The first evacuation flight was carried out by an air ambulance (LX-RHC) of [Luxembourg Air Rescue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg_Air_Rescue).

[^cite_note-76]

On 6 May, the ship was anchored outside [Cape Verde](18962637.md), a country not deemed capable of dealing with the medical emergency on board.[^cite_note-aikman-2026-22] Despite the objections of the [Canary Islands' regional president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Canary_Islands), Spain approved the plan for the *Hondius* to dock in [Tenerife](269223.md), citing its moral and legal obligation to the passengers.[^cite_note-the-independent-2026-77]

The Swiss government confirmed that a man infected with hantavirus was being treated in [Zurich](40334603.md), Switzerland. Swiss authorities confirmed that the patient was a passenger on the cruise ship, bringing the total number of infections to eight.[^cite_note-SWI-6_May-52] The man was one of the 30 passengers who disembarked on 24 April.[^cite_note-McArthur-2026-78] On the same day, it was announced that three more people experiencing symptoms had been sent to the Netherlands by two [air ambulances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_ambulance) for treatment; these were a 56-year-old Briton, a 41-year-old Dutch national, and a 65-year-old German, one of which was the ship's doctor.[^cite_note-aikman-2026-22][^cite_note-79][^cite_note-sample-2026-70]

The first plane carrying two patients landed in the Netherlands on 6 May; the German national was later transferred to [University Hospital of Düsseldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Hospital_of_D%C3%BCsseldorf) and the Briton to [Leiden University Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University_Medical_Center).[^cite_note-80][^cite_note-81] The second plane carrying the Dutch national diverted to [Gran Canaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Canaria) and awaited a replacement aircraft after experiencing technical issues in its life support systems.[^cite_note-82][^cite_note-83] On 6 May, the ship left Cape Verde for [Spain](26667.md).[^cite_note-84] Four medical experts embarked while the ship was leaving Cape Verde. Two of these were medical specialists from [Amsterdam University Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_University_Medical_Center) and Central Military Hospital, a military hospital in [Utrecht](50333.md). The two others were epidemiologists from Italy and the Netherlands, meant to investigate the scope of the virus on the ship.[^cite_note-oceanwide_8may-31] The selection, transport, and coordination of these experts was assisted by the WHO and [European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Centre_for_Disease_Prevention_and_Control).[^cite_note-overheid.nl-85]

Two anonymous Argentine investigators claimed that the leading hypothesis was that the [index case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_case)—the Dutch citizen who showed the first symptoms—had contracted the virus.[^cite_note-Debre-AP-86][^cite_note-grierson-2026a-87] On 6 May, the [Argentine health ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_\(Argentina\)) published a report detailing the movements of the index case, the Dutch citizen who presented the first symptoms, prior to the ship's departure; the report showed he had gone on a four-month road trip between 27 November 2025 and 1 April 2026, spanning Chile, Uruguay, and Argentina. Meanwhile, the National Ministry of Health and the [Malbrán Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malbr%C3%A1n_Institute) are advancing the epidemiological investigation at the local level, capturing and testing rodents along the route the Dutch passenger travelled, as well as conducting [contact tracing](1724851.md). The index case had returned to Argentina from Uruguay only four days before departure.[^cite_note-88]

#### 7 May

On 7 May, one of the flight attendants on the flight from [Johannesburg](58893.md) to [Amsterdam](844.md) on 26 April was admitted to [Amsterdam University Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_University_Medical_Center) on suspicion of infection, having been in contact with the deceased Dutch woman.[^cite_note-nederlandse-omroep-stichting-2026-89] On 7 May, the diverted flight carrying the third patient from the ship landed in the Netherlands, and that patient was hospitalized at [Radboud University Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Medical_Center).[^cite_note-nederlandse-omroep-stichting-2026-89] Later that day, the patient tested positive for the virus.[^cite_note-90] The same day, the Singapore Communicable Diseases Agency announced that two of its residents who had been on the cruise and on the same flight as the Dutch woman from [Saint Helena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Helena) to Johannesburg—one of them symptomatic—were being tested.[^cite_note-91] They tested negative on 8 May.[^cite_note-CNA-92]

A comprehensive risk analysis of the individuals on the two flights the deceased woman had taken was completed by [GGD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_Health_Service) Kennemerland, the Dutch municipal health service responsible for [Amsterdam Airport Schiphol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_Airport_Schiphol). Five individuals were categorized as high risk for having assisted the woman in leaving [KLM](16882.md) flight KL592; the hospitalized flight attendant is one of them. An additional 50 individuals were deemed lower risk, as they had been seated within two rows of the woman. The GGD is still attempting to contact these individuals.[^cite_note-rtl_7may-67] The remaining individuals have been informed and are considered to be at the lowest risk.[^cite_note-93] From the first flight from Saint Helena to Johannesburg, three individuals have been tested due to symptoms; two of those tests have since come back negative.[^cite_note-94]

It was reported that two patients had been identified as having the Andes virus in lab work by the [National Institute for Communicable Diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_for_Communicable_Diseases) of South Africa.[^cite_note-95] The same day, the [UK Health Security Agency](64959819.md) (UKHSA) told the [BBC](19344654.md) that all thirty people who left the ship during its stop in Saint Helena had been identified and contact traced.[^cite_note-roberts-2026-27] Two of these were Britons who are currently isolating without symptoms.[^cite_note-sample-2026-70] The Quebec Health Minister confirmed that three Canadians are isolating in [Quebec](7954867.md) and [Ontario](22218.md). Two of them disembarked in Saint Helena on 24 April, and the third flew on the same flight as the deceased woman to Johannesburg.[^cite_note-ctv_7may-65] On 7 May, [Oceanwide Expeditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanwide_Expeditions) said the ship was expected to arrive at the [Port of Granadilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Granadilla), Tenerife, in the early hours of 10 May.[^cite_note-OE/update-7-may-2026-17-15-96][^cite_note-cnn/2026/05/08/ship-hanta-97][^cite_note-scmp/3352939-98]

#### 8 May

On 8 May, it was confirmed that the second British national in a health facility in South Africa, who had been on the same flight as the deceased Dutch woman, tested positive. Another Briton was suspected by the UKHSA of having contracted the virus on the island of [Tristan da Cunha](31361.md), where the ship was docked between 13 and 15 April.[^cite_note-Rawlinson-Guardian-53][^cite_note-99] This individual was currently hospitalized on the island, and their spouse was isolating.[^cite_note-kendall-2026b-100] The KLM stewardess tested negative for the virus on 8 May.[^cite_note-101] On this day, GGD Kennemerland announced that everyone on flight KL592 was being monitored and in contact with the GGD, an escalation of the prior risk analysis which only deemed the two rows around the deceased to be at risk. Secretary of State for Health [Javier Padilla Bernáldez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Padilla_Bern%C3%A1ldez) also confirmed that a Spanish passenger on KL592 was symptomatic and hospitalized in [Alicante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicante). The individual sat two rows behind the deceased, and was thus classified as lower risk.[^cite_note-102] The US [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](6811.md) (CDC) classified the outbreak as 'Level 3' emergency response, the lowest on the CDC classification scale. The US administration announced the publication of a full report on the outbreak later that day.[^cite_note-Watson-ABC-103] In a press conference, the Director of Epidemiology and Environmental Health for [Tierra del Fuego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_del_Fuego_Province,_Argentina)'s Ministry of Health stated that the patients on the *Hondius* were unlikely to have been infected in the province.[^cite_note-104]

Evacuation plans for the arrival of the ship on the [Canary Islands](5717.md) were being finalized. Spain was coordinating the arrival of the ship with 22 countries, and the WHO. Almost all countries were sending evacuation aircraft for their citizens, and the EU provided two aircraft for those who did not have an assigned flight.[^cite_note-105] Countries had differing quarantine plans for their nationals once evacuated.[^cite_note-106][^cite_note-tg_vinter_jones-107]

There had been three deaths, with two confirmed to have been caused by hantavirus.[^cite_note-WHO-2026a-45] The other one remained under investigation.[^cite_note-WHO-2026a-45] As of 8 May, the WHO reported two probable cases and six confirmed cases, for a total of eight.[^cite_note-WHO-2026a-45] The UKHSA reported one additional suspected case, with two Britons being confirmed infected.[^cite_note-108][^cite_note-109]

The sequences of the virus isolated from a Swiss resident was published on [virological.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virological.org) on 8 May. The isolate was designated "ANDV/Switzerland/Hu-3337/2026". [Nextstrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nextstrain) pages were set up for this outbreak using these sequences on the same day.[^cite_note-hu3337-110]

#### 9–10 May

On 9 May, the ship was en route to the Canary Islands and expected to arrive early on 10 May.[^cite_note-*Vintner-2026*-111]



[Edinburgh of the Seven Seas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh_of_the_Seven_Seas), the only settlement on Tristan da Cunha. One inhabitant is suspected of having the virus.

On 10 May, British military personnel from the [16 Air Assault Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16_Air_Assault_Brigade) parachuted down to [Tristan da Cunha](31361.md) from a [RAF A400M Atlas](371265.md), where one of its 220 residents, an earlier passenger on the ship, was suspected of having contracted the virus. As the island has no airstrip, parachuting was the only option given the time-sensitive nature of the situation – the nearest port (in South Africa) is over a week sailing time away. In total six paratroopers, a [Royal Air Force](25679.md) (RAF) consultant and an army nurse arrived to the island while oxygen supplies and medical aid were also dropped.[^cite_note-112] This comes after the island reported their oxygen reserves being at a critical level, ruling out sending equipment by ship from [Ascension Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascension_Island).[^cite_note-113]

The ship arrived in the [Port of Granadilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Granadilla), around 05\:30 [WET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_European_Time) on 10 May and began disembarkation.[^cite_note-nypost/hondius-in-canary-114][^cite_note-115] Spanish health minister [Mónica García](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B3nica_Garc%C3%ADa) described the planning to prevent hantavirus spread as "unprecedented".[^cite_note-bbc/cn7pzmg8zeno-116][^cite_note-CNN/tenerife-arrive-72] At Spain's request, an [EU](9317.md) air ambulance stationed in Norway and crewed by Norwegian doctors was sent to Tenerife to be on stand-by in case of any serious illness found during disembarkation.[^cite_note-117] The passengers disembarked in order of their homeward-bound flight departure time, with the Spanish nationals disembarking first. The first evacuation flight took off at 13\:31 WET.[^cite_note-118] When the French evacuees arrived at a French hospital, Prime Minister [Sébastien Lecornu](54364868.md) announced that one of the five French nationals showed symptoms fitting the virus during the flight.[^cite_note-119] As of late 10 May WET, seven evacuation flights had taken off, transporting 94 passengers to six European countries and Canada.[^cite_note-120][^cite_note-121]

Some of the ship's crew stayed on board to take it to [Rotterdam](26049.md) in the Netherlands after its planned departure from Tenerife on 11 May for the disinfection of the entire ship.[^cite_note-122] The body of a passenger that died on board will also remain on the ship until its arrival in Rotterdam.[^cite_note-Sullivan-2026-123] The rest of the crew were to be evacuated by a Dutch government plane on 11 May.[^cite_note-124]

A flight transporting evacuated passengers landed in Manchester around 21\:00 on 10 May. The passengers—consisting of twenty UK citizens, one German national residing in the UK, and one Japanese man (who was evacuated at Japan's request)—were hospitalized at [Arrowe Park Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrowe_Park_Hospital), the location of UK's initial quarantine during the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-Rawlinson-2026-125]

#### 11 May

It was announced that the symptomatic French national had tested positive, with her health worsening overnight. Additionally, one asymptomatic American tested positive, and another had mild symptoms.[^cite_note-Funk-2026-126] The French government increased its public health alertness level, and had identified 22 French nationals who were high risk contact cases with infected individuals.[^cite_note-127] Later, the WHO reported that the woman was in "very critical condition", and that she had already shown symptoms while on the ship. In the evening of 11 May local time, one of the Spanish nationals who had arrived in Spain the day before tested positive. The 13 other Spanish nationals tested negative.[^cite_note-128]

During the evacuation of the final passengers aboard the MV *Hondius*, a Spanish officer died of a heart attack at the port.[^cite_note-129] Around 20\:00 WET, the ship left Tenerife for Rotterdam with 25 crew members and two medics from the [RIVM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIVM) after all passengers and 35 crew members successfully disembarked and were repatriated.[^cite_note-130] The body of the deceased German national was still on board.[^cite_note-Oceanwide-2026b-11] The journey is expected to take six days, with the ship arriving in the evening of 17 May.[^cite_note-131] In total, 122 individuals disembarked the ship in Tenerife and were repatriated to their home countries, or the Netherlands for those nationalities that did not provide their own evacuation flights.[^cite_note-Oceanwide-2026b-11]

#### 12 May

A Spanish passenger was confirmed positive, exhibiting symptoms, and was isolated in hospital.&#x20;

Twelve hospital workers at the Radboud University Medical Center in [Nijmegen](21713.md), Netherlands, failed to comply with protocols and were placed in isolation. The hospital staff followed standard protocols rather than strict protocols while drawing the patient's blood during admission on 7 May, and did not follow appropriate safety protocols when draining the patient's urine on 9 May.[^cite_note-Radboudumc-132][^cite_note-Voskamp-12May-133]

#### 13 May

Since the hospitalization of the French passenger on 10 May, her condition has worsened. On 13 May, French authorities stated that she is on life-support. They also said that this is "the final stage of supportive care".[^cite_note-Iribarren-2026-56] That same day, the WHO posted its third official update regarding the outbreak, and adjusted one of the previously confirmed cases to inconclusive after a second test came back negative, awaiting a third test from the American national who had shown no symptoms.[^cite_note-WHO-don601-134]

#### 15 May

On 15 May, the CDC announced that an American passenger who had developed flu-like symptoms on board and who had initially been listed as having "inconclusive" test results was retested with negative result and showed no antibodies, meaning he was never infected.[^cite_note-Stump-2026-9]

A Canadian passenger isolating in British Columbia tested "presumptively positive", with the patient hospitalized and three others under medical observation. While awaiting national laboratory confirmation, health officials are treating the case as positive during the 21-day isolation period.[^cite_note-Schmunk-16_May-40]

#### 18 May



The *Hondius* arriving in Rotterdam. On the left is a tour boat from where the press could follow the arrival.

On 18 May, MV *Hondius* docked in Rotterdam. Disinfection of the vessel began, and is estimated to take 3 to 4 days. Five crew members, including the captain, will stay on board during the disinfection.[^cite_note-Oceanwide-18May-17] The remaining on-board were tested again and disembarked. Of those, the two Dutch medical workers went home, where they would self-quarantine. The 20 non-Dutch crew (17 from the Philippines and 3 from Ukraine) entered into quarantine for six weeks in separate cabins. The body of the deceased German woman was taken off board to be cremated in the Netherlands.[^cite_note-Oceanwide-18May-17][^cite_note-Van_Campenhout-17May-16] The status of the Canadian passenger was updated by the national lab and "confirmed" positive.[^cite_note-Joannou-18_May-61]

## On board health care and monitoring

The *Hondius* is equipped with a cabin for the ship's doctor, normally only one,[^cite_note-overheid.nl-85] and an infirmary.[^cite_note-Ajay-2017-144][^cite_note-145] It is intended more for the minor health problems typically arising at sea, and stocks [anti-inflammatory drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-inflammatory_drugs), some [over-the-counter medications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Over-the-counter_drug), and [oxygen tanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxygen_tank). It lacks the kind of sophisticated equipment such as scanners or ventilators needed to diagnose or treat serious [respiratory illness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respiratory_illness).[^cite_note-Wu-2026-146]

By the end of April, the ship's doctor was among the two crew members who were very ill and confined to quarters. On 1 May, one of the passengers, who is a doctor, began rendering medical assistance to other passengers and crew. As the situation escalated, he also provided health care information resources for passengers on board, as well as became the central point of contact on board for health authorities.[^cite_note-Wu-2026-146][^cite_note-Goodman-19May-147] While docked outside [Cape Verde](18962637.md), two doctors and one nurse made three trips to the ship to provide additional care to passengers.[^cite_note-ap_cape-148]

On 6 May, four medical experts embarked while the ship was leaving Cape Verde. Two of these were medical specialists from [Amsterdam University Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_University_Medical_Center) and [Central Military Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Military_Hospital?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Utrecht](50333.md). The two others were epidemiologists from Italy and the Netherlands, meant to investigate the scope of the virus on the ship.[^cite_note-oceanwide_8may-31] The selection, transport and coordination of these experts was assisted by the WHO and [European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Centre_for_Disease_Prevention_and_Control).[^cite_note-overheid.nl-85]

## International response

The [World Health Organization](33583.md) (WHO) issued guidance aimed at port facilities and health authorities and agencies handling the disembarkation and monitoring of individuals on the MV *Hondius*.[^cite_note-WHO-Tech_Note-149]

### Passenger monitoring and contact tracing

The WHO has been coordinating monitoring of those who disembarked or were evacuated with health care agencies in twelve countries, including Canada, Denmark, Germany, the Netherlands, New Zealand, [Saint Kitts and Nevis](27198.md), Singapore, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, the United Kingdom, and the United States.[^cite_note-McArthur-2026-78]

### Canary Islands arrival

On 6 May, the ship was anchored near Cape Verde, which was not considered able to handle the scale of operation needed for the ship's safe evacuation.[^cite_note-aikman-2026-22] Instead, it intended to travel to [Tenerife](269223.md) and have its passengers disembark there. However president of the Canary Islands [Fernando Clavijo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Clavijo_\(politician\)) said he "cannot allow \[*Hondius*] to enter the Canaries" and refused to receive the ship in Tenerife.[^cite_note-aikman-2026-22][^cite_note-the-independent-2026-77] Clavijo's refusal stemmed from his concern that the ship's arrival would endanger the people of the Canary Islands, a view shared by many islanders, particularly in light of [their experiences during the COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_Canary_Islands).[^cite_note-the-independent-2026-77] The cruise company said that "Spain has a moral and legal obligation to assist these people, among whom are several Spanish citizens".[^cite_note-aikman-2026-22]

Despite the objections of the Canary Islands' president, Spain approved the plan for the *Hondius* to dock in Tenerife, with its health agency stating it was "in accordance with international law and humanitarian principles".[^cite_note-grierson-2026a-87] The ship set sail for the islands on 6 May.[^cite_note-grierson-2026a-87][^cite_note-192]

### Rodent investigation

The index case couple had been traveling through Argentina prior to boarding the MV *Hondius*, leading experts to believe the couple may have contracted the virus in the country. Argentinian officials initially believed that the couple may have contracted the virus in [Ushaia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ushaia) in the [Tierra del Fuego Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_del_Fuego_Province,_Argentina) — the same city that the MV *Hondius* departed from — while visiting a landfill for birdwatching. Hantavirus had never been detected in the [Tierra del Fuego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_del_Fuego) archipelago before.[^cite_note-Zibell-10_May-193]

From 18 to 22 May, Argentine scientists from the [Malbrán Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malbr%C3%A1n_Institute) trapped and tested rodents for hantavirus in the forests around Ushuaia.[^cite_note-194][^cite_note-Buenos_Aires_Times-195] From 8 to 12 June, a team of scientists from the Malbrán Institute, working with scientists from the United States' [Centers for Disease Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Disease_Control), trapped and tested rodents in [Malargüe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malarg%C3%BCe) in the [Mendoza Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendoza_Province) for hantavirus, a city that had been visited by the deceased couple.[^cite_note-196] No *[Oligoryzomys longicaudatus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oligoryzomys_longicaudatus)* specimens — the species considered the primary reservoir of the Andes virus — were captured in either location.[^cite_note-197]

Hantavirus was detected in 5 of the 144 rodents captured near Ushuaia, and was determined to be a previously undescribed viral variant related to the Andes virus; however, it was a different variant of the virus to the one that spread on the MV *Hondius*.[^cite_note-Buenos_Aires_Times-195] Prior to this investigation, hantavirus had never been detected in the [Tierra del Fuego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_del_Fuego) archipelago.[^cite_note-Zibell-10_May-193]

## Misinformation and disinformation

Several conspiracy theories emerged online following the outbreak. Some made the false claim that the outbreak was caused by Israel, and that the name of the virus is derived from the Hebrew word for *scam* or *fake* ([hantavirus](416945.md) takes its name from the [Hantan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hantan_River) in Korea).[^cite_note-Euro_News_2026-198] Other false claims portray the hantavirus outbreak as a side-effect of the [COVID-19 vaccine](63319438.md)[^cite_note-\:0-199][^cite_note-200] and as a scheme by pharmaceutical companies to sell vaccines, citing [Moderna](39743243.md)'s development of a [hantavirus vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hantavirus_vaccine) in 2024.[^cite_note-Euro_News_2026-198][^cite_note-\:1-201] Several [AI-generated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI-generated) images with false information about the outbreak have circulated on social media.[^cite_note-\:0-199] Unsupported claims that [Ivermectin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivermectin) can cure hantavirus were endorsed by [far-right](221220.md) former US Congress member [Marjorie Taylor Greene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marjorie_Taylor_Greene).[^cite_note-Euro_News_2026-198][^cite_note-202]

The [World Health Organization](33583.md) addressed the misinformation regarding the outbreak in Q\&A sessions on social media.[^cite_note-\:1-201]

## Legacy and impact

### Impetus for further study

There is little evidence about how hantavirus is transmitted because outbreaks are rare and mostly limited to Latin America. The Andes strain is the only one which can spread person-to-person, and little is known about it; no vaccine is available.[^cite_note-Cullinan-21May-203]

The [UK Health Security Agency](64959819.md) (UKHSA) is planning a study to learn more about Andes virus based on the cruise ship outbreak. Twenty British nationals who were on board and potentially exposed to the virus have volunteered to participate. None of the Britons isolating in the UK tested positive.[^cite_note-Cullinan-21May-203]

The UKHSA study is being run by the [International Severe Acute Respiratory and Emergency Infection Consortium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Severe_Acute_Respiratory_and_Emergency_Infection_Consortium) (ISARIC), known for gathering evidence about infectious diseases. It seeks evidence about how and when individuals become infectious and for how long; who gets sick, the progression of the disease, and what the risk factors are. A key question is whether individuals can become infected without having symptoms, as this would impact how health agencies monitor the disease and implement contact-tracing.[^cite_note-Cullinan-21May-203]
