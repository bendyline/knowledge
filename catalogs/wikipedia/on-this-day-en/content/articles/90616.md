**Val-d'Oise** (French: [\[val dwaz\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/9/90/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Val-d%27Oise.wav/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Val-d%27Oise.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/9/90/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Val-d%27Oise.wav/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-GrandCelinien-Val-d%27Oise.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>; lit. 'Vale of [Oise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oise_\(river\))') is a [department](38523.md) in the [Île-de-France](1716427.md) [region](58846.md), Northern France. It was created in 1968 following the split of the [Seine-et-Oise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine-et-Oise) department. In 2023, Val-d'Oise had a population of 1,281,653.[^cite_note-pophist-4]

It is named after the river [Oise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oise_\(river\)), a major tributary of the [Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine), which crosses the region after having started in Belgium and flowed through Northeastern France. Val-d'Oise is Île-de-France's northernmost department. [Charles de Gaulle Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_de_Gaulle_Airport), France's main international airport, is partially located in [Roissy-en-France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roissy-en-France), a commune of Val-d'Oise.

Its [INSEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_national_de_la_statistique_et_des_%C3%A9tudes_%C3%A9conomiques) and postcode number is 95.

## History

The original departments of France were established in 1790 when the French National Assembly split the country into 83 departments of roughly the same size and population. They were designed as sets of communes, and when better maps became available, certain revisions had to be made. In 1964, it was determined to divide up the departments of [Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine_\(department\)) and [Seine-et-Oise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine-et-Oise). Val-d'Oise was one of the new departments so formed, and was created entirely from the previous department of Seine-et-Oise.[^cite_note-5]

## Geography

Val-d'Oise is a department in north central France and is part of the region of [Île-de-France](1716427.md). To the south of the department lies the department of [Hauts-de-Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hauts-de-Seine), to the southwest lies [Yvelines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yvelines), to the west lies [Eure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eure), to the north lies [Oise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oise), to the east lies [Seine-et-Marne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine-et-Marne) and to the southeast lies [Seine-Saint-Denis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine-Saint-Denis).[^cite_note-Philips-6] The official *préfecture* (capital) of the department is the commune of [Pontoise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontoise), situated in the suburbs of [Paris](22989.md) some 28 kilometres (17 mi) northwest of the centre of the city, but the *préfecture* building and administrative offices are in the neighbouring commune of [Cergy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cergy). The [River Oise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oise_\(river\)) is a right tributary of the [River Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine), and flows through the province from northeast to southwest.[^cite_note-Philips-6]

The eastern part of the department is part of the [Pays de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pays_de_France), an area of fertile plain traditionally used for agriculture (particularly cereals and sugar beet) based on its fine silty soils. This part is progressively diminishing in size as Paris expands. Part of [Charles de Gaulle Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_de_Gaulle_Airport) falls in this eastern region, while other parts are in the departments of Seine-et-Marne and Seine-Saint-Denis. The southernmost region of the department forms part of the [Seine Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine) and occupies the whole of the small [Vallée de Montmorency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vall%C3%A9e_de_Montmorency?action=edit\&redlink=1). These parts are heavily urbanised, but the ancient Roman road, the [Chaussée Jules César](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauss%C3%A9e_Jules_C%C3%A9sar), which linked Paris and [Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouen), passes through the latter. The central and southwestern parts of the department are also largely urbanised and part of the greater Paris sprawl. The western part of the department forms part of the historic county of [Vexin français](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vexin), a verdant, largely agricultural plateau. Its capital was [Pontoise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontoise) on the eastern extremity of the county. This commune is now combining with the neighbouring commune of [Cergy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cergy) to form the new town of [Cergy-Pontoise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cergy-Pontoise). The Vexin area remains largely rural, and across the whole department, one fifth is covered with trees.[^cite_note-Philips-6]

## Economy

The economy of Val-d'Oise relies on two different themes. The northern, eastern and western parts are fertile areas of agricultural land producing large quantities of corn, sugar beet, and other crops. The urban parts to the south are dormitory towns, used by people working in the greater metropolitan area of Paris. The presence of Charles de Gaulle Airport and its associated [TGV](57212.md) station provides access by rail to all parts of France. The department has nine business zones designated for high-tech industries.[^cite_note-8]

## Tourism

The department has a rich archaeological and historical heritage, but is not a region visited much by tourists, perhaps being overshadowed by the French capital. Places of interest include the following sites;[^cite_note-Agence-13] [La Roche-Guyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Roche-Guyon) with a castle on top of a rocky hill and a twelfth century château; [L'Isle-Adam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Isle-Adam,_Val-d'Oise), a historic small town on the bank of the River Oise; [Auvers-sur-Oise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auvers-sur-Oise), which owes its international fame to its landscapes and the impressionist painters such as [Charles-François Daubigny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles-Fran%C3%A7ois_Daubigny), [Paul Cézanne](24472.md), [Jean-Baptiste-Camille Corot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Baptiste-Camille_Corot), [Camille Pissarro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camille_Pissarro) and [Vincent van Gogh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_van_Gogh) who immortalised them; [Enghien-les-Bains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enghien-les-Bains), a spa resort with a hot, sulphurous spring, on the site of what was originally Lake Enghien; [Écouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89couen) with a fine château which houses the Museum of the Renaissance; Cergy-Pontoise, the new administrative capital which has been created out of thirteen communes and has quadrupled in population since the 1960s. There is a branch of the Académie de Versailles in the city which provides tertiary education; [Théméricourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Th%C3%A9m%C3%A9ricourt), where as well as a fine château, there is the historic church of Notre-Dame, and the twelfth or thirteenth century Croix de l'Ormeteau-Marie.[^cite_note-14] [Royaumont Abbey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royaumont_Abbey), founded by St. Louis in the thirteenth century, is another important site. There are two protected nature areas in the department: the [Parc naturel régional du Vexin français](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parc_naturel_r%C3%A9gional_du_Vexin_fran%C3%A7ais) and the [Parc naturel régional Oise-Pays de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parc_naturel_r%C3%A9gional_Oise-Pays_de_France?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-Agence-13]

[Argenteuil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argenteuil) is a sub-prefecture and is the second most populous of Paris' suburbs. It is in a scenic location by the River Seine and has been much-painted by [Claude Monet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Monet), [Eugène Delacroix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eug%C3%A8ne_Delacroix), [Pierre-Auguste Renoir](24546.md), [Gustave Caillebotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustave_Caillebotte), [Alfred Sisley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Sisley) and [Georges Braque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Braque). It has several historic buildings and a local museum.[^cite_note-15]
