# Energy in Turkmenistan

&#x20; [Turkmenistan](198149.md) had a total primary energy supply ([TPES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TPES)) of 26.75 [Mtoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonne_of_oil_equivalent) in 2014.[^cite_note-iea2014-1] Electricity consumption was 14.64 [TWh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TWh). Most of this primary energy came from [fossil fuels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fossil_fuels).[^cite_note-iea2014-1] All of the electricity is generated with natural gas.
