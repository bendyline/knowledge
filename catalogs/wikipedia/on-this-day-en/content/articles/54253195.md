**Francisco** "**Frank**" **Carlos** **Rubio** (born December 11, 1975) is a Salvadoran-American [US Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Army) [flight surgeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_surgeon), aviator, and [NASA](18426568.md) [astronaut](664.md). He holds the American [record for the longest single spaceflight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_spaceflight_records#Ten_longest_human_spaceflights) at nearly 371 days. In June 2026, he was announced as a mission specialist on [Artemis III](50493439.md).[^cite_note-artemisIII-1]

## Early life and education

Francisco Rubio was born on December 11, 1975 to Salvadoran parents.[^cite_note-nasabio-2][^cite_note-timestory-3] As a child, he lived in [El Salvador](9356.md) for the first six years of his life, after which his family moved to [Miami](53846.md), Florida, where he attended [Miami Sunset Senior High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Sunset_Senior_High_School). He attended the [United States Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Military_Academy) and earned a bachelor's degree in [international relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_relations).[^cite_note-nasabio-2][^cite_note-spacefacts-4]

## Military career

After commissioning as a second lieutenant in the [US Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Army), Rubio became a [UH-60 Blackhawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UH-60_Blackhawk) pilot. Rubio was a platoon leader in [A Company, 2nd Battalion, 82nd Aviation Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/82nd_Aviation_Regiment_\(United_States\)), and a company commander for [A Company, 2nd Battalion, 3rd Aviation Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_Aviation_Regiment_\(United_States\)). As a pilot, Rubio had over 1,100 hours of flying time, including 600 combat hours during operations in [Bosnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnian_War), [Iraq](5043324.md), and [Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Afghanistan_\(2001%E2%80%93present\)).[^cite_note-nasabio-2]

Rubio received a Doctorate of Medicine from the [Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniformed_Services_University_of_the_Health_Sciences), and completed a family medicine residency at [Fort Benning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Benning). He served as a clinic supervisor and flight surgeon at [Redstone Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redstone_Arsenal). At the time of his selection as an astronaut candidate, Rubio was the surgeon for 3rd Battalion, [10th Special Forces Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10th_Special_Forces_Group_\(United_States\)) at [Fort Carson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Carson).[^cite_note-nasabio-2]

## NASA career

In 2017, Rubio was selected as a member of [NASA Astronaut Group 22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA_Astronaut_Group_22) and began his two-year training.[^cite_note-nasabio-2][^cite_note-CBS_News_-_7_June_2017_-_NASA_introduces_12_new_astronauts-5]

### Expedition 67/68/69

On July 15, 2022, NASA announced he would fly onboard [Soyuz MS-22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soyuz_MS-22) on a mission to the [International Space Station](15043.md).[^cite_note-exp67-68-69-6]

Rubio launched aboard Soyuz MS-22 alongside [Roscosmos](406573.md) cosmonauts [Sergey Prokopyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Prokopyev_\(cosmonaut\)) and [Dmitry Petelin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Petelin) on September 21, 2022. His mission was originally planned to last around six months with a return to Earth in early 2023. However, damage to the spacecraft extended the mission, and Rubio returned to Earth on [Soyuz MS-23](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soyuz_MS-23) on September 27, 2023, breaking [Mark T. Vande Hei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_T._Vande_Hei)'s previous [record for the longest spaceflight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_spaceflight_records#Ten_longest_human_spaceflights) by an American astronaut with 371 days.[^cite_note-7]

### Artemis III

On June 9, 2026, NASA announced Rubio as a mission specialist of the [Artemis III](50493439.md) mission to low Earth orbit, along with NASA astronauts [Randy Bresnik](6926275.md) and [Andre Douglas](69448162.md) and [ESA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESA) astronaut [Luca Parmitano](22874956.md).[^cite_note-artemisIII-1]

## Personal life

Rubio and his wife, Deborah, have four children.[^cite_note-nasabio-2][^cite_note-timestory-3]

## Awards and honors

Rubio has received the [Defense Superior Service Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_Superior_Service_Medal), [Legion of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Merit), [Bronze Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Star_Medal) with two oak leaf clusters, [Meritorious Service Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meritorious_Service_Medal_\(United_States\)) with three oak leaf clusters, [Air Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Medal), [Army Commendation Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_Commendation_Medal) with four oak leaf clusters, [Army Achievement Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_Achievement_Medal) with four oak leaf clusters, [NATO Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO_Medal), and the [Armed Forces Expeditionary Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_Expeditionary_Medal).[^cite_note-8]

His other decorations include the [Combat Action Badge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_Action_Badge), Senior [Aviator Badge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_aviator_badges) with Astronaut Device, [Flight Surgeon Badge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_Surgeon_Badge_\(United_States\)), [Parachutist Badge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parachutist_Badge_\(United_States\)), Senior [Space Badge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Operations_Badge), [Pathfinder Badge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pathfinder_Badge_\(United_States\)), and [Air Assault Badge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Assault_Badge). He is a graduate of the [US Army Command and General Staff College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Army_Command_and_General_Staff_College).[^cite_note-nasabio-2][^cite_note-9]
