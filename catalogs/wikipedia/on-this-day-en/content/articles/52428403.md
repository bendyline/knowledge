# Cree Nation of Mistissini



The Cree village of [Mistissini](35713519.md) in 2012

The **Cree Nation of Mistissini** ([Cree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cree_language): ᒥᔅᑎᓯᓃ) is a [Cree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cree) [First Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Nations_in_Canada) of [Canada](5042916.md). It is headquartered at the [Cree village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cree_village_municipality_\(Quebec\)) of [Mistissini](35713519.md) and also has a *terre réservée crie* of the [same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mistissini,_Quebec), both in [Northern Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nord-du-Qu%C3%A9bec). The nation is in negotiation with the [government of Canada](616255.md) to obtain its [self-governance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-governance).[^cite_note-1] In 2016, it has a registered population of 3,964 members.[^cite_note-2]
