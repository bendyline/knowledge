The **2026 US Open** was the 146th edition of [tennis](29773.md)' [US Open](82729.md), and the fourth and final [Grand Slam](197638.md) event of the year. It was held on the outdoor [hard courts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_court) at the [USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USTA_Billie_Jean_King_National_Tennis_Center) in New York City. [Carlos Alcaraz](63121147.md)[^cite_note-2] and [Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md)[^cite_note-3] were the men's and women's singles defending champions, but could not defend their titles.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Notable stories

### Special events

On August 23, 2026 to start fan week was the annual [Arthur Ashe Kids' Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Ashe_Kids'_Day) event, an event that aims to encourage future generations to play tennis and identify the sport's future stars.[^cite_note-7]

The induction ceremony for the [International Tennis Hall of Fame](532503.md) "Class of 2026" took place on Saturday August 29, 2026, featuring Swiss former professional tennis player [Roger Federer](262376.md) and retired producer [Mary Carillo](2303019.md) as the headliners.[^cite_note-8]

### Singles highlights

In men's singles play, [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md) defeated US player [Ben Shelton](71310148.md) 6–3, 7–6<sup>(7–2)</sup>, 5–7, 6–2, which marked the first time since 1989 the US Open men's singles was won by a German. It was Zverev's second major championship.

Four-time champion [Novak Djokovic](16100029.md) was eliminated in the first round. This was only the third time in his career that he lost in the opening round of a major.[^cite_note-9] [Botic van de Zandschulp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botic_van_de_Zandschulp) took 5 hours 13 minutes to defeat [Arthur Gea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Gea) in the 4th round. This was the fourth longest match in US Open history.[^cite_note-10] In the match of the tournament, [Ben Shelton](71310148.md) defeated Carlos Alcaraz in a fifth-set tiebreak in men's quarterfinal action.[^cite_note-11] The match didn't finish until 3\:33am local time.&#x20;

On the women's side of singles, [Elena Rybakina](55541443.md) defeated defending champion [Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md) 6-4, 5-7, 6-2 to win her first US Open women's singles title. The victory was Rybakina's third Grand Slam title and her second major championship of 2026.[^cite_note-12] A notable highlight was that she received the trophy from the Russian retired tennis player [Maria Sharapova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Sharapova). The tournament organizers allowed Sharapova to present the title to the winner as a tribute to her own victory there [20 years ago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_US_Open_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-13]

In other singles highlights, [Zheng Qinwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheng_Qinwen) came from 0–5 down in a set in consecutive matches to eventually win the sets and the matches.[^cite_note-14] All four women's quarterfinal matches were very competitive and went the three-set distance.

### Doubles highlights

In women's doubles, [Kateřina Siniaková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate%C5%99ina_Siniakov%C3%A1) and [Taylor Townsend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Townsend) defeated [Ashlyn Krueger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashlyn_Krueger) and [Robin Montgomery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Montgomery) in the final and completed a team [Career Grand Slam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Grand_Slam_women's_doubles_champions#Career_Grand_Slam); Siniaková's second and Townsend's first.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

A separate mixed doubles event began on August 24 with four rounds and four seeded teams, and all but one seeded team was eliminated in the first round.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] In the final, [Karolína Muchová](55266410.md) and [Jakub Menšík](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakub_Men%C5%A1%C3%ADk) defeated [Belinda Bencic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belinda_Bencic) and [Flavio Cobolli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavio_Cobolli) to win the title.[^cite_note-20]

### Other highlights

Before the US Open semifinal match between Americans [Ben Shelton](71310148.md) and [Frances Tiafoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_Tiafoe)—won by the first—there were tributes and commemorations marking the 25th anniversary of the [September 11, 2001 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/September_11,_2001_attacks), featuring a musical performance and the unfurling of a giant flag on the Arthur Ashe court.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

### Prize money

The 2026 US Open offered a record \$108 million in total player compensation, the largest package in tennis history and an increase of 21% from 2025.[^cite_note-23]

The men's and women's singles champions each received \$5,500,000, a 10% increase from the \$5,000,000 awarded in 2025, while runners-up received \$2,800,000. First-round singles players received \$140,000, an increase of 27.3% from 2025.[^cite_note-24]
