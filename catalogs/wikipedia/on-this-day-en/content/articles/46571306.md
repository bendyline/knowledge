**Dalton Emani Makamau Tagelagi** (born 5 June 1968) is a [Niuean](21228.md) politician who has served as the sixth and current [Prime Minister](15284988.md) of Niue since June 2020. He was elected premier by the [Niue Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niue_Legislative_Assembly) on 11 June 2020, defeating [O'Love Jacobsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O'Love_Jacobsen) by 13 votes to 7.[^cite_note-rnz1-1]

Tagelagi is the son of [Sam Pata Emani Tagelagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Pata_Emani_Tagelagi), who served as Speaker of the [Niue Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niue_Legislative_Assembly) from 1972 to 1996.

## Early life

Dalton Tagelagi was born on 5 June 1968 in Alofi, Niue.[^cite_note-birmingham2022-2] His father, [Sam Pata Emani Tagelagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Pata_Emani_Tagelagi), served as the Speaker of the [Niue Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niue_Legislative_Assembly) from 1972 until 1996. Growing up, he attended the Halamahaga Primary School, followed by [Niue High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niue_High_School).[^cite_note-3] He is the half-brother of [Sonya Talagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonya_Talagi).[^cite_note-4]

## Political career

Tagelagi was first elected to the Niue Assembly at the [2008 Niuean general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Niuean_general_election). Following the [2014 Niuean general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Niuean_general_election) he was appointed Minister of Infrastructure.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] He was re-elected at the [2017 Niuean general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Niuean_general_election),[^cite_note-Government_of_Niue-7][^cite_note-Tala-8] and subsequently served as Minister for the Environment, Natural Resources, Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.[^cite_note-rnz1-1][^cite_note-9] As Environment Minister he represented Niue at the [2019 United Nations Climate Change Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_United_Nations_Climate_Change_Conference), calling for rich nations to show greater ambition.[^cite_note-10]

He was re-elected in the [2020 Niuean general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Niuean_general_election) and subsequently elected premier. After being elected he announced that his first priority would be investigating government finances.[^cite_note-11] As Premier his government ratified the [PACER Plus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PACER_Plus) regional trade agreement.[^cite_note-12] During the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md) he negotiated a one-way travel-bubble allowing Niueans to travel to [New Zealand](4913064.md),[^cite_note-13] and oversaw a vaccination program which saw Niue gain full herd immunity to the virus.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] In November 2021 he began a one-year term as Chancellor of the [University of the South Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_South_Pacific).[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] His tenure concluded in June 2022, and he was succeeded by [Tuimalealiʻifano Vaʻaletoʻa Sualauvi II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuimaleali%CA%BBifano_Va%CA%BBaleto%CA%BBa_Sualauvi_II).[^cite_note-18]

He was re-elected unopposed to the Assembly at the [2023 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Niuean_general_election).[^cite_note-RNZUnop-19] He was subsequently re-elected as Premier, defeating [O'Love Jacobsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O'Love_Jacobsen) 16 votes to 4.[^cite_note-20] He appointed his cabinet on 12 May 2023, the first gender-balanced cabinet in Niuean history.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

In the [2026 election](82777114.md), he narrowly retained his seat in Alofi South by one vote, defeating his challenger Alana Rex.[^cite_note-PreliminaryPMN-23][^cite_note-PreliminaryRNZ-24] He was also re-elected for a third term as prime minister after defeating [Emani Fakaotimanava-Lui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emani_Fakaotimanava-Lui) in a close vote of 11–9.[^cite_note-NewAssembly1-25][^cite_note-NewAssembly2-26]

## Bowls career

Tagelagi has competed in [bowls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowls) for Niue, at the [2014 Commonwealth Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Commonwealth_Games) in Glasgow[^cite_note-27] and the [2018 Commonwealth Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Commonwealth_Games) on the [Gold Coast](59251.md).[^cite_note-28] In 2022, he competed in the [men's pairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawn_bowls_at_the_2022_Commonwealth_Games_%E2%80%93_Men's_pairs) and the [men's fours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawn_bowls_at_the_2022_Commonwealth_Games_%E2%80%93_Men's_fours) at the [2022 Commonwealth Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Commonwealth_Games) in Birmingham.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]
