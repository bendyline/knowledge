Compared to other European nations, the French are not avid newspaper readers, citing only 164 adults out of every 1000 as newspaper readers.

A year after the end of [World War II](32927.md), 28 papers had a combined circulation of about 7 million. However, seven years later that figure had been nearly halved. This decline was principally due to the greater popularity of the [broadcast media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadcast_media) and the subsequent diversion of [advertising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advertising) revenues. Since 2000 newly produced free papers have further weakened the established press. Still, 80 daily papers remain, and there is a wide range of weeklies, many of which now feature [internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet) sites.[^cite_note-1]

Regional papers have remained relatively unaffected by the decline, with provincial newspapers commanding a higher degree of reader loyalty. For example, *[Ouest-France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouest-France)*, sells almost twice as many copies as any of the national dailies.[^cite_note-2]

## Newspapers

Main articles: [History of French journalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_French_journalism) and [List of French newspapers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_French_newspapers)

In the early 21st century, the best-selling daily was the regional *[Ouest-France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouest-France)* in 47 local editions, followed by *[Le Progres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Progres)* of Lyon, *[La Voix du Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Voix_du_Nord_\(daily\))* in Lille, and *[Provençal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Provence)* in Marseille. In Paris the Communists published *[l'Humanite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Humanite)* while *[Le Monde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Monde)* and *[Figaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Figaro)* had local rivals in *[Le Parisien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Parisien)*, *[L'Aurore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Aurore)* and the leftist *[Libération](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lib%C3%A9ration).*
