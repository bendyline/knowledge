**Santon Downham** is a village and [civil parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_parish) in the [West Suffolk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Suffolk_\(district\)) district of [Suffolk](27886.md) in eastern [England](9316.md). In 2005 it had a population of 240.[^cite_note-population-2] The village is located within [Thetford Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thetford_Forest) on a meander of the [River Little Ouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Little_Ouse) on the [Norfolk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk)-Suffolk border. [Thetford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thetford) is 4 miles (6 km) southeast and the nearest railway station is in [Brandon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon,_Suffolk) 2 miles (3 km) west.

## History



East of England Forestry Commission offices

The village was the administrative centre for the Eastern [Forestry Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forestry_Commission) for many years, following the planting of tens of thousands of trees in the [Breckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breckland) to form the [Thetford Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thetford_Forest). The District office and staff were housed in a 1960s office building close to the site of the now-demolished Downham Hall. The Hall garden formed the base for the nursery department of the district. The Forestry Commission repair depot was built close by in an award-winning building, next to which stood the Forestry Commission fire station. A [Bedford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford_Vehicles) '[Green Goddess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Goddess)' fire tender and a water tanker were kept in the fire station due to the high fire risk in the area.

In 1972 the bridge over the [River Little Ouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Little_Ouse) appeared in programme 50 of the fifth series of BBC's sitcom *[Dad's Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dad's_Army)*, when [Captain Mainwaring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_Mainwaring) and his troops attempt to demolish it.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Climate

Santon Downham has an [oceanic climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanic_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Cfb*).

The highest temperature since records began in 1959 was 39.0 °C (102.2 °F) on 19 July 2022 and the lowest was −18.9 °C (−2.0 °F) on 23 January 1963.[^cite_note-RoostWeather-5]

Santon Downham is a known frost hollow and holds the record for the coldest temperature ever recorded in the UK in June, a low of −5.6 °C (21.9 °F) on 1 and 3 June 1962, a record it also shares with Dalwhinnie in the highlands of Scotland. Temperatures also dipped to −5.6 °C (21.9 °F) on 30 September 1969, which is one of the coldest September nights recorded in the UK.[^cite_note-6] The temperature can go below freezing at any time of the year.

## Transport

The [Santon Downham Tramway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santon_Downham_Tramway) was a 3.7-mile (6 km) long [narrow-gauge railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrow-gauge_railway), which was operated around 1918 to 1922.[^cite_note-Manning-8][^cite_note-Timber-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]
