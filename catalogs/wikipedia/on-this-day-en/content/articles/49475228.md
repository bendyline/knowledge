**San Vicente** is a town in the [Manabí](728376.md) [province](576127.md) of Ecuador and the capital of [San Vicente Canton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Vicente_Canton,_Ecuador).

On 23 March 2024, Ecuador's youngest mayor, 27-year-old [Brigitte García](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigitte_Garc%C3%ADa), mayor of San Vicente for the opposition [Citizen Revolution Movement](60837451.md), was shot dead.[^cite_note-2]
