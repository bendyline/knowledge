The **2026–27 NHL season** is the [110th season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NHL_seasons) of operation (109th season of play) of the [National Hockey League](21809.md) (NHL). The season features an 84-game regular season schedule for the first time since [1993–94](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993%E2%80%9394_NHL_season). The regular season began on September 29, 2026.[^cite_note-startdate-1] The [Stanley Cup playoffs](10490910.md) are then planned to begin in April 2027, ending with the [Stanley Cup Final](3314237.md) in June.

## League business

### Collective bargaining agreement

On June 27, 2025, the league and the [NHL Players' Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_Players'_Association) agreed on a new four-year [collective bargaining agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_Collective_Bargaining_Agreement) (CBA) that will take effect from the 2026–27 to 2029–30 seasons. Among the changes under the agreement, the 2026–27 regular season expands from 82 to 84 games, featuring two more divisional games; the additional regular season games will come with a reduction in the number of preseason games played, down from 6 to 4.[^cite_note-2026CBA-2][^cite_note-3]

Additionally, as part of the new CBA, the 2026–27 season will see the end of the use of amateur [emergency back-up goaltenders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_back-up_goaltender) (EBUGs) supplied by the home team for each game; instead, teams must hire their own emergency goaltender who will travel with the team and is permitted to have other non-player team duties. To prevent the hiring of "[ringers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sports_idioms#R)", each team's new EBUG must fulfill the following conditions: they must not have played professional hockey at any level in the previous three seasons, cannot have ever played a single game in the NHL under a standard player contract, cannot have played more than 80 professional games in their careers, and are not on the reserve list or restricted free agent list of any NHL teams.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] If a team signs three or more goaltenders, the team is still required to hire a permanent EBUG but is not required to have their EBUG present for a game.[^cite_note-8] EBUGs will be required to wear 00 as their uniform number.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

### Winnipeg Jets franchise consolidation

On September 24, 2026, the NHL announced that the franchise records and statistics of the [original Winnipeg Jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnipeg_Jets_\(1972%E2%80%931996\)) from 1972 to 1996 would be moved from the inactive [Arizona Coyotes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_Coyotes) franchise to the current [Winnipeg Jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnipeg_Jets) franchise. As a result, the Jets' franchise records include both its original and current incarnations as well as the [Atlanta Thrashers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Thrashers).[^cite_note-JetsHistoryUpdate-11]

### Entry draft

The [2026 NHL entry draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NHL_entry_draft) took place on June 26–27 at [KeyBank Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KeyBank_Center) in [Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo,_New_York), [New York](8210131.md).[^cite_note-12]

## Regular season

The regular season began on September 29, 2026, and will end on April 10, 2027.[^cite_note-startdate-1][^cite_note-41]

### International games

The [Seattle Kraken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Kraken) and [Carolina Hurricanes](70523.md) are scheduled to play two games against each other on November 12 and 14, 2026 at [Veikkaus Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veikkaus_Arena) in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki), Finland.[^cite_note-42] The [Chicago Blackhawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Blackhawks) and [Ottawa Senators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa_Senators) are scheduled to play two games against each other on December 18 and 20, 2026 at [PSD Bank Dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PSD_Bank_Dome) in [Düsseldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%BCsseldorf), Germany.[^cite_note-43]

### Outdoor games

The league has scheduled three outdoor games this season:

- The [2026 Heritage Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Heritage_Classic) is scheduled for October 25, at [Princess Auto Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Auto_Stadium) in [Winnipeg](100730.md), with the [Winnipeg Jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnipeg_Jets) hosting the [Montreal Canadiens](42966.md).

- The [2027 NHL Winter Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_NHL_Winter_Classic) is scheduled for December 31, at [Rice–Eccles Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice%E2%80%93Eccles_Stadium) in [Salt Lake City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Lake_City), with the [Utah Mammoth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Mammoth) hosting the [Colorado Avalanche](70948.md).

  [^cite_note-46]

### All-Star Game

The [2027 All-Star Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_National_Hockey_League_All-Star_Game) is scheduled for February 7 at [UBS Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UBS_Arena) at [Elmont, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elmont,_New_York), the home of the [New York Islanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Islanders). The All-Star Game would be played for the first time since [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_National_Hockey_League_All-Star_Game). The league instead held the [4 Nations Face-Off](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4_Nations_Face-Off) in 2025, then the players participated in the [2026 Winter Olympics](77611835.md). The league originally planned a 2026 All-Star Game at UBS Arena as a "send-off" to the Olympics, but decided against it.[^cite_note-2027ASG-2-47]

## Media rights

### National

#### Canada

This will be the first season of a 12-year renewal to the NHL's [Canadian broadcast rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_Sportsnet) with [Rogers Sports & Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogers_Sports_&_Media).[^cite_note-SN_new_2026_deal-58] Both [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_Prime_Video) and [TVA Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TVA_Sports) renewed their sub-licensing agreements with Rogers for the entire 12 years.[^cite_note-Prime_new_2026_deal-59][^cite_note-TVA_new_2026_deal-60]

Rogers did not renew its sub-licensing agreement with [CBC Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_Television) for *[Hockey Night in Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockey_Night_in_Canada)*, ending the CBC's relationship with the NHL after 74 years. The CBC maintains ownership of the *HNIC* brand,[^cite_note-61] and will instead use it for Saturday night [Professional Women's Hockey League](74699992.md) (PWHL) games as part of its new *CBC Sports Primetime* anthology.[^cite_note-62]

Linear television

- Saturday night games were rebranded as *[Saturday Night Hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Hockey)*, and will continue to air across the [four regional Sportsnet feeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportsnet#Regional_feeds), [SN1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportsnet_One), and [SN360](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportsnet_360), with weekly doubleheaders featuring at least one Canadian team in each matchup.

  [^cite_note-SNSatNightHky-63]

  &#x20;Sportsnet would also be able to [flex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flexible_scheduling_\(sports\)) more regional games from its networks to national telecasts.

  [^cite_note-SN_new_2026_deal-58]

  [^cite_note-Prime_new_2026_deal-59]

  [^cite_note-globemail-64]

  &#x20;For the first time since the [2021–22 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_NHL_season), Sportsnet will have its own Tuesday opening-night doubleheader featuring Canadian teams (in previous seasons, the Tuesday opening-night only featured American teams for [ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_ESPN)'s U.S. media deal).

  [^cite_note-65]

  &#x20;[Citytv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citytv) and [Omni Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omni_Television) will also no longer carry broadcasts, ending all free-to-air television coverage of the NHL in Canada;

  [^cite_note-66]

  &#x20;Sportsnet confirmed the cancellation of Omni's *Hockey Night in Canada: Punjabi Edition* on August 14, 2026.

  [^cite_note-67]

- TVA Sports will offer French-language broadcasts of up to 350 regular season games per season, including regular season events as the Heritage Classic, Winter Classic, and All-Star Game. Out of these games, TVA will televise at least 32 Montreal Canadiens games per-season, an increase from 22.

  [^cite_note-TVA_new_2026_deal-60]

  [^cite_note-MON-Gazette_TVA_deal-68]

Streaming

- [Sportsnet+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportsnet+)'s Standard plan takes over *[Monday Night Hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monday_Night_Hockey_\(Sportsnet\))* from Amazon Prime Video. The Standard plan will also continue to simulcast all national games and in-market regional games aired on Sportsnet's linear channels, while Sportsnet+'s higher Premium plan will continue to include out-of-market games. Sportsnet+'s add-on plan [via television provider authentication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_Everywhere) includes *Monday Night Hockey* and over 100 U.S.–U.S. games.

  [^cite_note-SN_new_2026_deal-58]

  [^cite_note-SNSatNightHky-63]

  [^cite_note-Prime_new_2026_deal-59]

Postseason

Under the new sub-licensing deal, first- and second-round games will be split between Sportsnet and Amazon Prime Video. During the first round, Sportsnet will have the first three choices of which series to televise, Amazon Prime Video will have the fourth and fifth choices, and Sportsnet will have the remaining three series. Sportsnet will then have the first two picks of which second-round series to air, and Amazon Prime Video will pick third, and Sportsnet will air the fourth and final series. Sportsnet will exclusively televise the conference finals and the Stanley Cup Final.[^cite_note-MON-Gazette_Prime_deal-71]

Amazon Prime Video will hold French-language rights to all playoff series that it selects, aside from those featuring the Montreal Canadiens. TVA Sports will hold French-language rights to all playoff series that are televised by Sportsnet, as well as exclusive French-language rights to any series featuring the Montreal Canadiens (even if they are picked by Amazon).[^cite_note-MON-Gazette_TVA_deal-68]

#### United States

This will be the sixth season of the league's seven-year U.S. national broadcast rights deals with the [ESPN family of networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_ESPN) and [TNT Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_TNT).[^cite_note-cnbc-72]

Linear television

- ESPN continues to have the opening day tripleheader before airing games on select days throughout the regular season as its schedule permits, primarily on Tuesdays and Thursdays. ESPN also has Sunday night doubleheaders on four select weekends between late February and April. [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_ABC)'s *Hockey Saturday* has 16 regular season games on free-to-air television across select weekends between January and April, including one doubleheader, three tripleheaders, a Saturday afternoon game on January 16 prior to the NFL Wild Card games, and a special Sunday afternoon edition on January 31 prior to the NFL Conference Championship games, and the Stadium Series, marking the first time since [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_NHL_Stadium_Series) that the outdoor game will be on ABC instead of ESPN. ESPN and ABC will also have the All-Star skills competition and the All-Star Game, respectively.

  [^cite_note-NHL_US-TV_schedule-74]

  [^cite_note-ESPN_schedule-75]

This season, the network will also have a Friday doubleheader on opening week, and one Monday game on November 16. TNT's Sunday schedule this season includes the Heritage Classic in October, five consecutive weekends in February and March, and the last Sunday of the regular season in April. The March 21 game in particular would mark the first time under its contract that TNT will have the NHL instead of two of the Sunday night Round of 32 [NCAA March Madness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_March_Madness_\(TV_program\)) games (TNT will still avoid conflicting with the March Madness Regional Finals on March 28). Some of TNT's games may be simulcast on [TruTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TruTV). [TBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TBS_\(American_TV_channel\)) may be used as an overflow channel. Not all of TNT Sports' regular season games will be exclusive broadcasts and are subject to [blackout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackout_\(broadcasting\)) in local markets.

  [^cite_note-TNT_schedule-77]

- [NHL Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_Network_\(American_TV_channel\)) will continue to televise games when the other national broadcasters are not airing games and will again primarily simulcast local coverage.

Streaming

- The [ESPN app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN_app) will continue to stream games depending on the tier. [ESPN+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN+) (the ESPN app's Select plan) will have 47 exclusive games on select days throughout this season, primarily on Tuesdays and Thursdays when the linear ESPN network does not have games. Postseason

First- and second-round games will be split between ESPN-produced telecasts (either on ESPN, ABC, or ESPN2) and TNT Sports-produced telecasts (either on TNT or TBS, with selected simulcasts on TruTV). Each U.S. team's regional broadcaster will also televise local coverage of first-round games, except for any game on ABC.[^cite_note-cnbc-72][^cite_note-ESPN_announcement-73][^cite_note-TNT_announcement-76]
