**Riyadh Province**,[^cite_note-1] is a [province](468738.md) in central [Saudi Arabia](349303.md), covering much of the historic [Najd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Najd) region in the heart of the [Arabian Peninsula](47858.md). Its administrative seat is [Riyadh](51554.md), the capital and largest city of Saudi Arabia. With a population of 8,591,748 in 2022, it is the most populous province in the Kingdom and the second-largest by area after the [Eastern Province](468721.md).

## Transportation

### Airport



[King Khalid International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Khalid_International_Airport)

Riyadh Province is served by [King Khalid International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Khalid_International_Airport), an international and domestic hub located north of the capital city. Plans are underway to develop [King Salman International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Salman_International_Airport), which will encompass the existing airport facility and serve as the base for [Riyadh Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riyadh_Air) to expand international aviation connections.[^cite_note-dezeen.com-4]

### Inland Port



A freight train on the line near [Dhahran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhahran)

As an inland territory, the province uses the [Riyadh Dry Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riyadh_Dry_Port) as its central logistics and trade connection point. Linked by the [Dammam–Riyadh railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dammam%E2%80%93Riyadh_railway) line to the [Arabian Gulf coast](24761.md), this facility handles customs, container processing, and distribution for goods entering local industrial markets.[^cite_note-SAP-5]

### Road and rail



[KAFD Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAFD_metro_station) on the [Riyadh Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riyadh_Metro)

The province is a node in the national highway network, centered on [Highway 40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highway_40_\(Saudi_Arabia\)) which connects Mecca to the west and Dammam to the east. Urban transit includes the [Riyadh Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riyadh_Metro) network, which operates driverless trains across six lines to service Riyadh city. Regionally, the [railway network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riyadh%E2%80%93Qurayyat_railway) links the province to the [Al-Jouf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Jouf_Province), [Hail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hail_Province,_Saudi_Arabia), and [Al-Qassim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qassim_Province) provinces, while the planned [Saudi Landbridge Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Landbridge_Project) is designed to establish a rail link west to Jeddah.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
