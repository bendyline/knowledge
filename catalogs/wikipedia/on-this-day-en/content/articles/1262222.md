**Alofi** is the [capital](181337.md) of the island nation of [Niue](21228.md). With a population of 610 in 2022,[^cite_note-Census-4] Alofi is the [second-smallest national capital by population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_national_capitals_by_population) (after [Ngerulmud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngerulmud), capital of [Palau](23372.md)). It consists of two villages: Alofi North and Alofi South. They collectively serve as the capital, but of the two, Alofi South hosts more government buildings, as many were moved to the southern part after [Cyclone Heta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Heta).

## History

In 1922 the first hospital in Niue opened in Alofi.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

In January 2004, Niue was hit by the fierce tropical storm [Cyclone Heta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Heta), which killed two people and did extensive damage to the entire island. Many of Alofi's buildings were destroyed, including the hospital. Government buildings were shifted to a less exposed site 3 km (≈ 2 mi) inland from the west coast,[^cite_note-8] named Fonuakula, after the storm.[^cite_note-heta-9] This site is within the village boundaries of Alofi South.

## Geography

### Overview

It is located at the centre of [Alofi Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alofi_Bay) on the west coast of the island, close to the only break in the [coral reef](87410.md) that surrounds Niue. The bay stretches for 30% of the island's length (about seven kilometres) from [Halagigie Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halagigie_Point) in the south to Makapu Point in the north.

The council's territory borders with [Avatele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatele), [Hakupu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakupu), [Lakepa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakepa), [Liku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liku,_Niue), [Makefu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makefu), [Mutalau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutalau), [Tamakautoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamakautoga) and [Tuapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuapa).[^cite_note-10]

Alofi features a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) under the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification), with no discernible [dry season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season). The city has a noticeably drier stretch from June through September. However, all of these months average more than 60 mm (2.4 in) of rain, the limit for a dry season month. Average temperatures vary slightly throughout the course of the year in Alofi hovering at around 27 °C (81 °F), during the warmest month (February) and at around 23 °C (73 °F) during the coolest months (July and August).

## Transport

The town is serviced by [Niue International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niue_International_Airport). There are also numerous roads, both [paved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_surface) and [dirt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirt_road), that crisscross the town.[^cite_note-12]

The island's main seaport is [Sir Roberts Wharf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Roberts_Wharf). It is close to the centre of Alofi, within 200 metres of the capital's post office and just to the north of the Chamber of Commerce building. It is a small wharf, capable—due to the island's topography—of only handling smaller flat-bottomed boats. Larger cargo vessels and fishing boats moor near the reef, and barges are used to offload cargo. Major strengthening, extension work, and renovations to the wharf were commissioned in 2020, after cyclone damage earlier in the year.[^cite_note-13]
