The **King of the Kingdom of Bahrain** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ملك مملكة البحرين *Malik Mamlakat al-Baḥrayn*) is the [monarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarch) and [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of the [Kingdom of Bahrain](18933277.md). The [House of Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Khalifa) has been the ruling family of the country since the [1783 Arab invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bani_Utbah_invasion_of_Bahrain) that led to the end of [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zand_dynasty) rule in Bahrain. Between 1783 and 1971, the Bahraini monarch held the title of [hakim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakim_\(title\)), and, from 1971 until 2002, the title of [emir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir). On 14 February 2002, the emir of Bahrain, [Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa](392300.md), declared Bahrain a kingdom and proclaimed himself the first king.[^cite_note-kingdom-1] The king enjoys wide-ranging powers, which include appointing the [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Bahrain) and the [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Bahrain), holding supreme command over the [Defence Force](67658.md), chairing the [Higher Judicial Council](33282951.md), appointing the [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Bahrain\))'s [upper house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consultative_Council_\(Bahrain\)) and dissolving its elected [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Representatives_\(Bahrain\)).[^cite_note-Fr_15.2.2011-2]
