The **Southeastern United States**, also known as the **American Southeast** or simply **the Southeast**, is a geographical [region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_regions_in_the_United_States) of the [United States](3434750.md) located in the eastern portion of the [Southern United States](179553.md) and the southern portion of the [Eastern United States](89126.md). The region includes a core of states that reaches north to [Maryland](18858.md) and [West Virginia](32905.md), bordering the [Ohio River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_River) and [Mason–Dixon line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason%E2%80%93Dixon_line), and stretches west to [Arkansas](1930.md) and [Louisiana](18130.md).[^cite_note-2]

There is no official [U.S. government](195149.md) definition for the region, and it is defined variably among agencies and organizations.

The history of the present-day Southeastern United States dates to the dawn of civilization in approximately 11,000 BC or 13,000 BC. The earliest artifacts from the region were from the [Clovis culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clovis_culture). Prior to the arrival of [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonial_history_of_the_United_States) colonialists, [Native Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States) occupied the region for several hundred years during the [Woodland period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodland_period).

The first Europeans to arrive in the region were [conquistadors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conquistador) associated with the [Spanish Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Empire). In 1541, [Hernando de Soto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hernando_de_Soto) journeyed through the southeast and crossed the [Mississippi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_River). The region hosted the first permanent European settlement in the [United States](3434750.md), with the [Kingdom of Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Spain) establishing [St. Augustine, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Augustine,_Florida) in 1565. Four southeast states, [Georgia](48830.md), [North Carolina](21650.md), [South Carolina](27956.md), and [Virginia](32432.md), were among the [Thirteen Colonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteen_Colonies) who sent delegates to the [Second Continental Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Continental_Congress) in [Philadelphia](50585.md), which declared [independence](31874.md) from the [British Crown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Crown), which was established with the colonies' victory over the [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Army_during_the_American_Revolutionary_War) in the [American Revolutionary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War).

During the [American Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War), the [Confederate States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_America) consisted of the southeastern states of [Florida](18933066.md), [Alabama](303.md), Georgia, [Mississippi](16949861.md), [Tennessee](30395.md), South Carolina, North Carolina, Virginia, Louisiana, and Arkansas. [Texas](29810.md) was a Confederate State that isn't defined as part of the southeast region. [Missouri](19571.md) and [Kentucky](16846.md) had dual competing Unionist and Confederate state governments in the [Confederate government of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_government_of_Missouri) and [Confederate government of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_government_of_Kentucky), while Maryland and [Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware) were neutral southern [border states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_states_\(American_Civil_War\)) that remained with the [Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_\(American_Civil_War\)). [West Virginia](32905.md) split from Virginia during 1863, and also served as a border state that remained with the Union while being contested by the Confederacy and [Confederate government of West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_government_of_West_Virginia). Following the [Reconstruction era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reconstruction_era) in the 1870s, many Southeast state legislatures passed [Jim Crow laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Crow_laws). The [American segregation era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segregation_in_the_United_States) spanned from the late 19th century to the mid-1960s.

In the mid-to-late 20th century, the Southeast saw many shifting changes take place population wise and economically. The Southeast started to see a spur of new economic growth in the 1930s, which stemmed from [New Deal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Deal) policies such as the [Tennessee Valley Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_Valley_Authority) and the Fair Labor Standards Act instituting a minimum wage for the entire nation. [World War II](32927.md) further helped growth within the Southeast, as [military bases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_bases) and military production drew workers from farming or low-wage industries into a new economy. With farming [mechanization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanization) picking up to speed in the 1940s, promotion of different industries, and federal spending on [defense](608296.md) and [space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace) programs, the Southeast saw further economic transformation in the ensuing years and decades of the 20th century.[^cite_note-3]

The Southeast benefitted from its business and warm climate in its population growth during the late 20th century, as it helped in attracting job seekers and retirees from other U.S. regions. Florida in particular went from being the 27th most populated U.S. state in [1940](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1940_United_States_census) with 1.9 million residents, to being the 4th most populated U.S. state and having nearly 13 million residents in [2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_United_States_census). Southeast states such as North Carolina and Georgia saw large population growth increases as well during the late 20th century.[^cite_note-4]

## Geography

The [United States Geological Survey](23814944.md) defines Southeastern United States as including the states of [Alabama](303.md), [Arkansas](1930.md), [Florida](18933066.md), [Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_\(U.S._State\)), [Kentucky](16846.md), [Louisiana](18130.md), [Mississippi](16949861.md), [North Carolina](21650.md), [South Carolina](27956.md), [Tennessee](30395.md) and [Virginia](32432.md).

There is no official [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau) definition of the Southeastern United States. They instead divide a larger region which includes [Texas](29810.md), [Oklahoma](22489.md), [Maryland](18858.md), [Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware), Washington, D.C., and [West Virginia](32905.md), designated as "the South", into three separate subregions, none of which are conventionally considered to define the Southeast.

The nonprofit [American Association of Geographers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_of_Geographers) defines the Southeastern United States as including Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Maryland, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Virginia, and West Virginia.[^cite_note-5] The OSBO (Organization Supporting Business Owners) uses the same states, but includes Arkansas and Louisiana. The states of Delaware and [Missouri](19571.md), along with the U.S. capital of Washington, D.C., are also sometimes added in definitions of the term.

### Combined statistical areas

*Beyond Megalopolis* by [Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech)'s Metropolitan Institute, an attempt to update [Jean Gottmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Gottmann)'s work with current trends, defines two "[megapolitan areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megapolitan_area)" contained within the Southeast, out of a total of ten such areas in the United States:

- "Piedmont" extending from North Carolina to Alabama

- "Peninsula" covering [South Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Florida) and [Central Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Florida)

- "Gulf Coast" extending as far east as the western tip of Florida

- "Northeast" including much of Maryland, Central Virginia, and Eastern Virginia.

## Culture

Main article: [Culture of the Southern United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_the_Southern_United_States)

The predominant culture of the Southeast U.S. has its origins with the settlement of the region by [European colonists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_colonization_of_the_Americas) and [African slaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavery_in_the_United_States) during the 17th to 19th centuries, as large groups of [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Americans), [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Americans), [Scotch-Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotch-Irish_Americans), [Germans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Americans), [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Americas), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Americans), and [Acadians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acadians) migrated to the region. Since the late 20th century, the "[New South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_South)" has emerged as the fastest-growing area of the United States economically. Multiculturalism has become more mainstream in the Southeastern states. African Americans remain a dominant demographic, at around 30% of the total population of the Southeast. Cities along this corridor from north to south include the Raleigh-Durham area, Greensboro, Charlotte, Spartanburg, Greenville, Atlanta, and Montgomery.

## Climate

Most of the southeastern part of the United States is dominated by the [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) (Cfa/Cwa). As one nears the southern portion of Florida, the climate gradually becomes tropical, as the winter season and all months have a mean temperature above 64.4 °F (18.0 °C) (the defined coldest monthly mean temperature of tropical climates).

Seasonally, summers are generally hot and humid throughout the entire region. The [Bermuda High](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bermuda_High) pumps hot and moist air mass from the tropical Atlantic Ocean and eastern [Gulf of Mexico](21076367.md) westward toward the southeast United States, creating the typical sultry tropical summers. Daytime highs are often in the upper 80s to lower 90s F.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] Rainfall is summer concentrated along the Gulf Coast and the South Atlantic coast from [Norfolk, Virginia](57898.md) southward, reaching a sharp summer [monsoon](57630.md)-like pattern over peninsular Florida, with dry winters and wet summers. Sunshine is abundant across the southeastern United States in summer, as the rainfall often comes in quick, but intense downpours. The mid-South, especially Tennessee, and the northern halves of Mississippi, Alabama, and Georgia, have maximum monthly rainfall amounts in winter and spring, owing to copious Gulf moisture and clashes between warm, moist air from the Gulf of Mexico and cold, dry air from Canada during the cold season. In this area, December, March, or April are typically the wettest months; August to October, the driest months (for example, in [Tupelo, MS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tupelo,_MS), [Huntsville, AL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntsville,_AL) and [Memphis, TN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis,_TN)). The region is also part of [Dixie Alley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dixie_Alley) and are prone to [Tornadoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornadoes) particularly in states such as Alabama, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi and Tennessee.

Winters are cool in the northern areas like [Tennessee](30395.md), [Virginia](32432.md), [Maryland](18858.md), and western [North Carolina](21650.md), with average high temperatures in the 45 °F (7 °C) range in January. Farther south, winters become milder across interior eastern North and South Carolina, Georgia, and Alabama, with average January highs in the 53 °F (12 °C) range. As one nears the Gulf of Mexico, coastal plain, and coastal areas of Georgia and the Carolinas, winters become warm, with daytime highs near or over 60 °F (16 °C). Once in Florida, daytime highs are above 70 °F (21 °C) from just south of St. Augustine on the Atlantic coast and Clearwater on the Gulf Of Mexico. Extreme southern Florida has the warmest winter temperatures (mid 70's F) in the United States. Winters tend to be very dry and sunny across Florida, with a gradual increase in winter rainfall with increasing latitude, especially west of the Appalachian Mountains.

The Southeast economically has changed dramatically since the late 20th century. There has been a [boom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_boom) in its [service economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service_economy), manufacturing base, high technology industries, and the financial sector. Examples of this include the surge in tourism in Florida and along the [Gulf Coast](11969.md); the numerous new automobile production plants such as [Mercedes-Benz](18564164.md) in [Tuscaloosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuscaloosa,_Alabama);[^cite_note-20] [Hyundai](42257.md) in [Montgomery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montgomery,_Alabama);[^cite_note-21] [Toyota Motors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_Motors) in [Blue Springs, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Springs,_Mississippi);[^cite_note-22] [Kia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kia_Motors) in [West Point, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Point,_Georgia); [BMW](3772.md) production plant in [Greer, South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greer,_South_Carolina); [Volkswagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen) in [Chattanooga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chattanooga,_Tennessee); [GM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Motors) manufacturing plant in [Spring Hill, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_Hill,_Tennessee); with the [Nissan](21240.md) North American headquarters in [Franklin, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin,_Tennessee); [Mercedes-Benz USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_USA); and [Porsche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche) North American headquarters in the Atlanta area; the two largest research parks in the country: [Research Triangle Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Triangle_Park) in the Triangle area of North Carolina (the world's largest) and the [Cummings Research Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cummings_Research_Park) in [Huntsville, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntsville,_Alabama) (the world's fourth largest); corporate headquarters of [Bank of America](347756.md), [Truist Financial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truist_Financial), and [Wells Fargo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wells_Fargo) in [Charlotte, North Carolina](57447.md), the corporate headquarters of [International Paper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Paper), [AutoZone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AutoZone), and [FedEx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FedEx) in [Memphis, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis,_Tennessee) as well as the corporate headquarters of [the Coca-Cola Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Coca-Cola_Company), [Delta Air Lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Air_Lines), [the Home Depot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Home_Depot) and [United Parcel Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Parcel_Service) in [Atlanta](3138.md), Georgia. [Dillards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dillard's), along with [Heifer International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heifer_International), is headquartered in [Little Rock, Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Rock,_Arkansas). [Walmart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walmart) is also headquartered in [Arkansas](1930.md).&#x20;

In 2020, Fortune 500 companies having headquarters in the Southeast region include: 22 in Virginia, 18 in Georgia, 18 in Florida, 13 in North Carolina, and 10 in Tennessee.[^cite_note-23] This economic expansion has enabled parts of the South to have some of the lowest unemployment rates in the United States.[^cite_note-24] In Alabama, there is the large-scale manufacturing project owned by the German steel megacorporation [ThyssenKrupp](944221.md), which operates a massive, state-of-the-art facility in [Mobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile,_Alabama).

Even with certain states and areas in the Southeast doing well economically, many Southeast states and areas still have a high poverty rate when compared to the U.S. nationally. In 2017, seven Southeast states were in the top ten nationwide when it came to having the highest poverty rate.[^cite_note-25]

### Research and development areas

[Research Triangle Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Triangle_Park) in the [Raleigh–Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Triangle) urban area of North Carolina, has emerged as the largest in the nation, and a major hub of technology, governmental, and biotechnological research and development. The [Cummings Research Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cummings_Research_Park) in the [Huntsville, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntsville,_Alabama) area, is the second-largest research complex in the nation. Located in Huntsville is the [Redstone Arsenal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redstone_Arsenal), [United States Army Missile Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Missile_Command), the [U.S. Space & Rocket Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Space_&_Rocket_Center), [NASA](18426568.md)'s [Marshall Space Flight Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_Space_Flight_Center) and other key government, military, and aerospace agencies. [Tullahoma, TN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tullahoma,_Tennessee) contains the Arnold Air Force Base. The base is home to the Arnold Engineering Development Complex (AEDC), the most advanced and largest complex of flight simulation test facilities in the world.&#x20;

The [National High Magnetic Field Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_High_Magnetic_Field_Laboratory) in [Tallahassee, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallahassee,_Florida), is the largest laboratory in the world devoted to the study of magnetism.[^cite_note-26] The [University of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina) is currently constructing a research campus in downtown [Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia,_South_Carolina), and the university is the nation's only [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation)-funded Industry/University Cooperative Research Center for [Fuel Cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuel_Cell).[^cite_note-27]

## Sports

### Professional



[Atlanta Braves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Braves)



[Miami Heat](72850.md)



[New Orleans Saints](21720.md)

There are nine [National Football League (NFL)](21211.md) franchises across the region: the [Atlanta Falcons](2103.md), [Baltimore Ravens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore_Ravens), [Carolina Panthers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolina_Panthers), [Jacksonville Jaguars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacksonville_Jaguars), [Miami Dolphins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Dolphins), [New Orleans Saints](21720.md), [Tampa Bay Buccaneers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa_Bay_Buccaneers), [Tennessee Titans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_Titans), and [Washington Commanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Commanders).&#x20;

The Southeast has seven [National Basketball Association (NBA)](22093.md) franchises: the [Atlanta Hawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Hawks), [Charlotte Hornets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Hornets), [Memphis Grizzlies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis_Grizzlies), [Miami Heat](72850.md), [New Orleans Pelicans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans_Pelicans), [Orlando Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Magic), and [Washington Wizards](72858.md).

[Major League Baseball (MLB)](38776.md) maintains five teams in the Southeast: the [Atlanta Braves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Braves), [Baltimore Orioles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore_Orioles), [Miami Marlins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Marlins), [Tampa Bay Rays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa_Bay_Rays), and [Washington Nationals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Nationals).

The Southeast has five [National Hockey League (NHL)](21809.md) franchises: the [Carolina Hurricanes](70523.md), [Florida Panthers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Panthers), [Nashville Predators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_Predators), [Tampa Bay Lightning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa_Bay_Lightning), and [Washington Capitals](73125.md).

[Major League Soccer](71802.md) currently holds six clubs: [Atlanta United FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_United_FC), [Charlotte FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_FC), [DC United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.C._United), [Inter Miami CF](41853326.md), [Nashville SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_SC) and [Orlando City SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_City_SC).

The majority of [NASCAR](21114.md) teams are headquartered in the Charlotte area along with the sports operations headquarters and media outlets. Tracks in the region include [Atlanta Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Motor_Speedway),[^cite_note-56][Bristol Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Motor_Speedway),[^cite_note-57][Charlotte Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Motor_Speedway),[^cite_note-58][Darlington Raceway](685467.md), [Daytona International Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytona_International_Speedway), [Homestead-Miami Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homestead-Miami_Speedway), [Martinsville Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martinsville_Speedway), [Nashville Superspeedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_Superspeedway), [North Wilkesboro Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Wilkesboro_Speedway), [Richmond Raceway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Raceway), and [Talladega Superspeedway](681674.md).

The southeast also hosts two of the three legs of the American [Triple Crown](19116721.md): the [Kentucky Derby](17135.md) in [Louisville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisville,_Kentucky), and the [Preakness Stakes](62370.md) in [Baltimore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore). The Derby is considered the western leg of the crown and the Preakness is traditionally considered the southern leg.

The [Southeastern Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_Conference) is also an [NCAA Division I](669931.md) conference made up of Southern college teams, including the: [Alabama Crimson Tide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alabama_Crimson_Tide), [Arkansas Razorbacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Razorbacks), [Auburn Tigers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_Tigers), [Florida Gators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Gators), [Georgia Bulldogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Bulldogs), [Kentucky Wildcats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_Wildcats), [LSU Tigers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LSU_Tigers), [Oklahoma Sooners](965018.md), [Ole Miss Rebels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ole_Miss_Rebels), [Mississippi State Bulldogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_State_Bulldogs), [Missouri Tigers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_Tigers), [South Carolina Gamecocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Carolina_Gamecocks), [Tennessee Volunteers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_Volunteers), [Texas A\&M Aggies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A\&M_Aggies), [Texas Longhorns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Longhorns), and [Vanderbilt Commodores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_Commodores).[^cite_note-60]
