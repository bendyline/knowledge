# List of organisations banned in Pakistan

The [Ministry of Interior](26905116.md) of [Pakistan](23235.md) has banned a number of organizations that have been proscribed as [terrorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist) organizations under the [Anti-Terrorism Act 1997](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Terrorism_Act_1997).

## List

Groups that are banned As of 7 September 2021.[^cite_note-1]
