The **India women's national cricket team** represents [India](14533.md) in [international cricket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_cricket).[^cite_note-9] It is governed by the [Board of Control for Cricket in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Control_for_Cricket_in_India) (BCCI) and is a [full member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_International_Cricket_Council_members#Full_Members) of the [International Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) with [Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Test), [One Day International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_One_Day_International) and [Twenty20 International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Twenty20_International) status. India are the current holders of the [World Cup](3977980.md), the [Asia Cup](11395320.md), and the [Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_at_the_Asian_Games).

The team has played 43 Test matches, winning 9, losing 7, and with 27 ending in draws. Their first international match, on 31 October 1976, was a [Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Test_cricket) against the [West Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Indies_women's_cricket_team) at the [M. Chinnaswamy Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Chinnaswamy_Stadium) in [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore) which ended in a draw.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

The team has played 345 ODI matches, winning 188, losing 150, [tying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tied_Women's_One_Day_Internationals) 2, and with 5 ending in a [no-result](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No-result). India has won the [World Cup](3977980.md) once in [2025](70509671.md). India has reached the [World Cup](3977980.md) final on three occasions, losing to [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_women's_national_cricket_team) by 98 runs in [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Women's_Cricket_World_Cup_Final), and to England by 9 runs in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Women's_Cricket_World_Cup_Final), and winning against [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_women's_national_cricket_team) by 52 runs in [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Women's_Cricket_World_Cup_final).[^cite_note-nytimes.com-13] India have also won the [ODI Asia Cup](11395320.md#Results), a record four times in [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Women's_Asia_Cup), [2005-06](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005-06_Women's_Asia_Cup), [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Women's_Asia_Cup), and [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Women's_Asia_Cup).

The team has played 233 T20I matches, winning 131, losing 95, [tying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tied_Women's_One_Day_Internationals) 1, and with 6 ending in a [no-result](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No-result). India has reached the finals of the [T20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Women's_World_Twenty20) once, losing to Australia by 85 runs in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_ICC_Women's_World_Twenty20). India have also won the [T20I Asia Cup](11395320.md#Results), a record four times in [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Women's_Twenty20_Asia_Cup), [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Women's_Twenty20_Asia_Cup), [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Women's_Twenty20_Asia_Cup), and [2026](82523320.md). In addition, India have also won golds at the [Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_at_the_Asian_Games) in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_at_the_2022_Asian_Games_%E2%80%93_Women's_tournament) and [2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_at_the_2026_Asian_Games_%E2%80%93_Women's_tournament), and won a silver at the [Commonwealth Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_at_the_Commonwealth_Games) in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_at_the_2022_Commonwealth_Games).

## History

The British brought cricket to India in the early 1700s, with the first cricket match played in 1721.[^cite_note-14] It was played and adopted by [Kolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koli_people) of [Gujarat](53707.md) because they were sea [pirates](50715.md) and [outlaws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlaw) who always looted the British ships, so the [East India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_Company) tried to manage the Kolis in cricket.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] The first Indian cricket club was established by the [Parsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsis) community in Bombay, in 1848; the club played their first match against the Europeans in 1877.[^cite_note-18] In 1911, an Indian men's cricket team was formed and toured [England](9316.md), where they played English county teams.[^cite_note-19] The [India men's team](407754.md) made their [Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_cricket) debut against [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_cricket_team) in 1932.[^cite_note-20] The first women's Test was played between [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_women's_cricket_team) and [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_national_women's_cricket_team) in 1934.[^cite_note-21]

Women's cricket arrived in India much later; the [Women's Cricket Association of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Cricket_Association_of_India) (WCAI) was formed in 1973.[^cite_note-22] The Indian women's team played their first Test match in [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Indies_women's_cricket_team_in_India_in_1976%E2%80%9377), against the [West Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Indies_women's_cricket_team).[^cite_note-23] India recorded its first-ever Test win in November 1976 against West Indies under [Shantha Rangaswamy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantha_Rangaswamy)'s captaincy at the [Moin-ul-Haq Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moin-ul-Haq_Stadium) in [Patna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patna).[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

The WCAI, the governing body for women's cricket, was affiliated to the [International Women's Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Women's_Cricket_Council). As part of the [International Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council)'s initiative to develop women's cricket, the Women's Cricket Association of India was merged with the [Board of Control for Cricket in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Control_for_Cricket_in_India) in 2006/07.[^cite_note-26]

In 2021, the BCCI announced that [Ramesh Powar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramesh_Powar) would become the Head Coach of the Indian Women's Cricket Team.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] In 2022, Indian Women script history by winning 1st series on England soil in 23 years.[^cite_note-29]

In July 2025, India clinched their first-ever Women's T20I series win against [England](9316.md), securing an unassailable 3–1 lead in the five-match series. The landmark victory came in the fourth T20I at Worcester, where Indian spinners [Radha Yadav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radha_Yadav), [Deepti Sharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepti_Sharma), and newcomer [Shree Charani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shree_Charani) restricted England to 126/7. Openers [Shafali Verma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shafali_Verma) and [Smriti Mandhana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smriti_Mandhana) set up a comfortable six-wicket chase, finished with 18 balls to spare. The win marked a historic breakthrough, as India had never previously won a T20I series against England, either home or away. The performance, highlighted by disciplined bowling and sharp fielding, also served as vital preparation ahead of the 2026 [Women's T20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_T20_World_Cup) in England.[^cite_note-30]

In November 2025, India won their first [Women's Cricket World Cup](3977980.md), defeating South Africa by 52 runs in the final at the [DY Patil Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DY_Patil_Stadium) in [Navi Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navi_Mumbai). In the semi-final, they chased a huge target of 339 runs against Australia, which is one also the highest successful run chases in the history of Women's ODI.[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32] The victory was widely celebrated and recognized across India as a landmark moment for women's cricket in the country.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-nytimes.com-13][^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35]

## Governing body

Main article: [Board of Control for Cricket in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Control_for_Cricket_in_India)

The [Board of Control for Cricket in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Control_for_Cricket_in_India) (BCCI) is the governing body for the Indian cricket team and first-class cricket in India. The Board has been operating since 1928 and represents India at the [International Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council). It is amongst the richest sporting organisations in the world. It sold media rights for India's matches from 2006 to 2010 for US\$612,000,000.[^cite_note-36] It manages the Indian team's sponsorships, its future tours and team selection. The [International Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) (ICC) determines India's upcoming matches through its future tours program.
