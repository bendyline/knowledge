**Baniyas** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بَانِيَاس *_Bāniyās_*) is a [Mediterranean](19006.md) coastal city in [Tartus Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tartus_Governorate), western [Syria](7515849.md), located 55 km (34 mi) south of [Latakia](195594.md) and 35 km (22 mi) north of [Tartus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tartus). Its ancient name was Balaneais, Balanaea or Balanea but it was also called Leucas or Leucas-Claudia.

It is known for its citrus fruit orchards and its export of wood. North of the city is an [oil refinery](78649690.md), the largest in Syria, and a [power station](212141.md). The oil refinery is connected with Iraq by the [Kirkuk–Baniyas pipeline](15219021.md) (defunct since the US-led [2003 invasion of Iraq](201936.md)).

On a nearby hill stands the [Crusader castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crusader_castle) of [Margat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margat) (Qalʻat al-Marqab), a huge [Knights Hospitaller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knights_Hospitaller) fortress built with black [basalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basalt) stone.

## History

### Ancient

In [Phoenician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenicia) and Hellenistic times, it was an important seaport. Some have identified it with the Hellenistic city of **Leucas** (from colonists from the island [Lefkada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lefkada)), in Greece, mentioned by [Stephanus of Byzantium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephanus_of_Byzantium). It was a colony of [Aradus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arwad),[^cite_note-\:1-1] and was placed by Stephanus in the late [Roman province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_province) of [Phoenicia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenice_\(Roman_province\)), though it belonged rather to the province of [Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria_\(Roman_province\)).[^cite_note-CE-2] In [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language) and [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin), it is known as **Balanaea** or **Balanea** (Βαλανέαι).

### Modern

See also: [Siege of Baniyas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Baniyas), [2025 massacres of Syrian Alawites](79404646.md), and [March 2025 Western Syria clashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_2025_Western_Syria_clashes)

During the early 21st century [Syrian civil war](30741795.md), rebel sources reported that a [massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayda_and_Baniyas_massacres) took place on 2 May 2013, perpetrated by regime forces.[^cite_note-3] On 3 May,[^cite_note-4] another massacre was, according to [SOHR](34029669.md), perpetrated in the Ras al-Nabaa district of Baniyas causing hundreds of [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni) residents to flee their homes.[^cite_note-5] According to one opposition report, a total of 77 civilians, including 14 children, were killed.[^cite_note-massacres-6] Another two opposition groups documented, by name, 96–145 people who are thought to have been executed in the district.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] Four pro-government militiamen and two soldiers were also killed in the area in clashes with rebel fighters.[^cite_note-9]

#### 2025 massacres

After the [overthrow of the Assad regime](78548066.md) in late 2024, several massacres were occurring throughout the country with the most notable recent incident on the coast affecting several areas, including Baniyas city.[^cite_note-10] From 6 March 2025, according to the UK-based [Syrian Observatory for Human Rights](34029669.md) (SOHR), over 1,000 people were killed in clashes between militias loyal to the [HTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahrir_al-Sham) and militias loyal to the deposed president [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md), including more than 745 confirmed civilian deaths caused by the current government forces and the Syrian security forces. The massacres occurring negatively affected the [Alawite](436616.md) population in the area causing many to flee.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Climate

Baniyas has a [hot-summer Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot-summer_Mediterranean_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Csa*). Rainfall is higher in winter than in summer. The average annual temperature in Baniyas is 19.3 °C (66.7 °F). About 862 mm (33.94 in) of precipitation falls annually.

## Bishopric

The bishopric of Balanea was a [suffragan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffragan) of [Apamea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apamea,_Syria), the capital of the [Roman province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_province) of [Syria Secunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria_Secunda), as is attested in a 6th-century *[Notitiae Episcopatuum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notitiae_Episcopatuum)*.[^cite_note-13] When Justinian established a new civil province, Theodorias, with Laodicea as metropolis, Balanea was incorporated into it, but continued to depend ecclesiastically on Apamea, till it obtained the status of an exempt bishopric directly subject to the Patriarch of Antioch.[^cite_note-CE-2]

Its first known bishop, Euphration, took part in the [Council of Nicaea](11118.md) in 325 and was exiled by the [Arians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arianism) in 335 later Timotheus was at both the [Robber Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robber_Council) of Ephesus in 449 and the [Council of Chalcedon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Chalcedon) in 451. In 536, Theodorus was one of the signatories of a letter to the emperor [Justinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justinian) against [Severus of Antioch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severus_of_Antioch) and other non-Chalcedonians. Stephanus participated in the [Second Council of Constantinople](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Council_of_Constantinople) in 553.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

During the [Crusades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crusades), Balanea became an [episcopal see](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Episcopal_see) of the [Latin Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_Church), called **Valenia** or **Valania** in the West. It was situated within the [Principality of Antioch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Antioch) and was [suffragan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffragan) to the Latin [metropolitan see](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_see) of Apamea, whose archbishop intervened in the nomination of bishops of the suffragan see in 1198 and 1215.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] For reasons of security, the bishop lived in Margat Castle.[^cite_note-CE-2]

No longer a residential bishopric, Balanea is today listed by the [Catholic Church](606848.md) as a [titular see](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titular_see).[^cite_note-19]
