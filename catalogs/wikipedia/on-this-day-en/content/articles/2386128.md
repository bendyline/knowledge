The **Golden Lion** ([Italian](14708.md): *Leone d'oro*) is the highest prize given to a film at the [Venice Film Festival](32497.md). The prize was introduced in [1949](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10th_Venice_International_Film_Festival) by the organizing committee, as the successor to the earlier **Gran Premio Internazionale di Venezia** (Grand International Prize of Venice), awarded in 1947 and 1948. The prize was introduced as the Golden [Golden Lion of Saint Mark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lion_of_Saint_Mark), which was one of the best known symbols of the ancient [Republic of Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Venice). In [1954](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15th_Venice_International_Film_Festival), the prize was permanently named the Golden Lion.

The Golden Lion is widely regarded as one of the [film industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_industry)'s most prestigious and distinguished prizes. In [1971](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/32nd_Venice_International_Film_Festival), the [Golden Lion for Lifetime Achievement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Lion_for_Lifetime_Achievement) was introduced, an honorary prize for people who have made an important contribution to cinema.

### Background

From 1934 until 1942, the highest award of the festival was the [Coppa Mussolini](32497.md#Mussolini_Cup_\(Coppa_Mussolini\)) for Best Italian Film and Best Foreign Film. Even though other awards were attributed to [Nazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazism) propaganda films, such as *[Jud Süß](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jud_S%C3%BC%C3%9F)* (*Suss, the Jew*), an [antisemitic](1078.md) production made at the behest of Nazi Propaganda Minister [Joseph Goebbels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Goebbels), won the festival's *Golden Crown*[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] award in 1940.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-Etlin2002_143-5]

After the end of the WWII during the reestablishment of the festival, *[The Southerner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Southerner_\(film\))*, directed by [Jean Renoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Renoir), won the main prize at the 1946 edition. In 1947 and 1948, the equivalent prize for the Golden Lion was the *Gran Premio Internazionale di Venezia* (Grand International Prize of Venice), awarded to [Karel Steklý](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karel_Stekl%C3%BD)'s *[The Strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Strike_\(1947_film\))* in 1947 and [Laurence Olivier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier)'s *[Hamlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlet_\(1948_film\))* in 1948.

### Golden Lions

&#x20;The first Golden Lion was awarded in 1949.[^cite_note-6] Previously, the equivalent prize was the Gran Premio Internazionale di Venezia (Grand International Prize of Venice), awarded in 1947 and 1948. The prize was introduced in 1949 as the Golden [Golden Lion of Saint Mark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lion_of_Saint_Mark), which was one of the best known symbols of the ancient [Republic of Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Venice).



[Roberto Rossellini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Rossellini) and [Mario Monicelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Monicelli) winning the Golden Lion in 1959 for *[General Della Rovere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Della_Rovere)* and *[The Great War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_War_\(1959_film\)),* respectively

No Golden Lions were awarded between 1969 and 1979. According to the Biennale's official website, the hiatus was a result of the 1968 Lion being given to the radically experimental *[Die Artisten in der Zirkuskuppel: Ratlos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artists_Under_the_Big_Top:_Perplexed)*; the website says that the awards "still had a statute dating back to the fascist era and could not side-step the general political climate. Sixty-eight produced a dramatic fracture with the past".[^cite_note-7]

More French films have been awarded the Golden Lion, more than to any other nation. However, there is considerable geographical diversity in the winners. American recipients include [John Cassavetes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cassavetes) and [Robert Altman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Altman) (tied), [Ang Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ang_Lee) (*[Brokeback Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brokeback_Mountain)* was the first winning U.S. film not to tie), [Darren Aronofsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darren_Aronofsky), [Sofia Coppola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Coppola), [Todd Phillips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Phillips), [Chloé Zhao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlo%C3%A9_Zhao), [Laura Poitras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Poitras), and [Jim Jarmusch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Jarmusch). Prior to 1980, only three of 21 winners were of non-European origin. Since the 1980s, the Golden Lion has been presented to a number of Asian filmmakers, particularly in comparison to the [Cannes Film Festival](101460.md)'s top prize, the [Palme d'Or](612946.md).

Ang Lee won the Golden Lion twice within three years in the 2000s, once for an American film and once for a Chinese-language film. [Zhang Yimou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Yimou) has also won twice. Other Asians to win the Golden Lion since 1980 include [Jia Zhangke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jia_Zhangke), [Hou Hsiao-hsien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hou_Hsiao-hsien), [Tsai Ming-liang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsai_Ming-liang), [Trần Anh Hùng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tran_Anh_Hung), [Takeshi Kitano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takeshi_Kitano), [Kim Ki-duk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Ki-duk), [Jafar Panahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jafar_Panahi), [Mira Nair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mira_Nair), and [Lav Diaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lav_Diaz). Russian filmmakers have won the Golden Lion several times, including since the end of the USSR.

To date, 33 of the 54 winners have been European men, including Soviet/Russian winners. Since 1949, seven women have won the Golden Lion for directing: [Margarethe von Trotta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margarethe_von_Trotta), [Agnès Varda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agn%C3%A8s_Varda), [Mira Nair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mira_Nair), [Sofia Coppola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Coppola), [Chloé Zhao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlo%C3%A9_Zhao), [Audrey Diwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audrey_Diwan), and [Laura Poitras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Poitras). In 1938, German director [Leni Riefenstahl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leni_Riefenstahl) won the Festival when its highest award was the Coppa Mussolini. In 2019, *[Joker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joker_\(2019_film\))* became the first movie based on original comic book characters to win the prize.[^cite_note-8]

From 2026, the non-English language winners will also be automatically eligible for the [Academy Award for Best International Feature Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film).[^cite_note-9]

The prize is the highest prize given to a film at the Venice Film Festival, and is widely regarded as one of the [film industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_industry)'s most prestigious and distinguished prizes.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

## Golden Lion for Lifetime Achievement

Main article: [Golden Lion for Lifetime Achievement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Lion_for_Lifetime_Achievement)

The Golden Lion for Lifetime Achievement is awarded to directors, actors, and other personalities from the world of cinema who have distinguished themselves in the art. It joins the Golden Lion, the festival's highest prize, which is instead awarded to a film in competition.[^cite_note-20]
