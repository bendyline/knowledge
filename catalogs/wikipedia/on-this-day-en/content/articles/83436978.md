The **Bangsamoro Federalist Party** (**BFP**) is a regional [parliamentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament) [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) based in the [Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao](37260549.md) (BARMM), [Philippines](23440.md). The party was formed for the [2026 Bangsamoro Parliament election](68697708.md), and serves as the regional organization of the [Partido Federal ng Pilipinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partido_Federal_ng_Pilipinas).[^cite_note-1]

## History

### 2026 Bangsamoro elections

The BFP was established on March 2, 2026 in [Cotabato City](298955.md).[^cite_note-2] On April 7, 2026, the BFP filed a “Petition for Registration with Manifestation to Participate in the 2026 BARMM Elections” at the Bangsamoro Electoral Office, the regional arm of the [Commission on Elections](1847922.md) (COMELEC), in the petition, the party claimed to have 14,879 members.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The president of the BFP, Tomanda Antok claims that the party has the support of the overwhelming majority of mayors in four provinces.[^cite_note-5] On May 28, 2026, over 2000 members of the [Moro Islamic Liberation Front](51673.md) joined the BFP in separate ceremonies.[^cite_note-6] On June 11, 2026, the mayor of Cotabato City, Bruce Matabalao along with other officials of the city announced their resignation from the [United Bangsamoro Justice Party](46652799.md) and took oath as members of the BFP.[^cite_note-7]

On August 15, 2026, Interim Chief Minister [Abdulraof Macacua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulraof_Macacua) joined the party and became the Party President and Chairman, replacing Tomanda Antok and became Party Executive Vice President.[^cite_note-8] On the same day, the Interim Chief Minister's convoy was ambushed on the way back from the oathtaking held at his hometown Gambar, [Kabuntalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabuntalan) at [Datu Odin Sinsuat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datu_Odin_Sinsuat) en route towards Cotabato City.[^cite_note-9] The following day on August 16, 2026, three governors from [Maguindanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_del_Norte), [Maguindanao del Sur](67871248.md), and [Tawi-Tawi](297097.md), as well as the Vice Governor of [Basilan](297003.md) joined the Party following the Interim Chief Minister. The governor of Lanao del Sur was also present at the event and pledged support.[^cite_note-10]

The BFP condemned a disinformation campaign against them alleging that they are linked to [Israel](9282173.md) based largely on the similarities of its logo's color scheme with the [Israeli flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_flag), with the party emphasizing its members having been supporters of the [Bangsamoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangsamoro_peace_process) and [Palestinian causes](47246228.md).[^cite_note-11]
