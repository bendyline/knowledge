# Fauna of Colombia

See also: [Flora of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flora_of_Colombia)



The [pink dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_river_dolphin) of the [Amazon River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_River) is an endangered species.

The **fauna of [Colombia](5222.md)** is characterized by a high [biodiversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biodiversity), with the highest rate of species by area unit worldwide.[^cite_note-1]

## Endemic animals

Colombia has the largest number of [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemic) species (species that are not found naturally anywhere else) worldwide. About 10% of the species in the world live in Colombia.[^cite_note-DIVERSITY_OF_SPECIES_IN_COLOMBIA-2] Some determinant factors in the distribution range of the species are the [weather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather) conditions, [temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature), [humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humidity) and [sunlight](27979.md) availability.

Endemics can easily become [endangered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endangered) or [extinct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extinct_species) due to their restricted habitat and vulnerability to the actions of man, including the [introduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Introduced_species) of new organisms.

See also: [Endemic birds of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemic_birds_of_Colombia)

### Ecoregions with high endemism

According to the Colombian Ministry of Environment, the following [ecoregions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecoregions) have the highest percentage of endemic species:

- [Cocora valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocora_valley) ([Quindío](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quind%C3%ADo))

- [Serranía de la Macarena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serran%C3%ADa_de_la_Macarena) ([Meta Department](549697.md))

- [Gorgona, Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorgona,_Colombia) ([island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island) in the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md))

- [Amacayacu National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amacayacu_National_Park) ([Amazonas Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonas_\(Colombian_department\)))

### The national bird



The *Vultur gryphus* also known as the [Andean condor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andean_condor) is the [national bird of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_symbols_of_Colombia).

The [Andean condor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andean_condor) inhabits the [Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andes) mountain range. Although it is primarily a [scavenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scavenger), feeding on [carrion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrion), this species belongs to the [New World vulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_World_vulture) family [Cathartidae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathartidae).

The condor is one of the largest birds on earth with a wingspan ranging from 274 to 310 cm (108–122 in) and weighing up to 11–15 kg (24–33 lb) for males and 7.5–11 kg (16–24 lb) for females, but overall length can range from 117 to 135 cm (46 to 53 inches).

The adult [plumage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plumage) is of a uniform black, with the exception of a frill of white feathers nearly surrounding the base of the neck and, especially in the male, large patches or bands of white on the wings which do not appear until the completion of the first molting.

## Mammals

See also: [List of mammals in Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mammals_in_Colombia) and [Primates of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primates_of_Colombia)

There are 456 reported species of mammals in Colombia.[^cite_note-3] Of these, about 22% are [endangered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endangered) or [critically endangered](7451605.md). Most of the threatened species status are due to human activities, in particular destruction of plant and animal [habitats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habitat_\(ecology\)) driven by local [consumption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingestion) of organic resources, especially related to [tropical forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_forest) destruction.[^cite_note-4]

While most of the species that are becoming extinct are not food species, their [biomass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomass_\(ecology\)) is converted into human food when their habitat is transformed into [pasture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasture) and [cropland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cropland).

## Amphibians

Colombia has the largest number of amphibians in the world (including [frogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frog), [toads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toad), [salamanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salamander) and [caecilians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caecilian)) with 589 species, 208 of them being endangered, being the zoological group with the highest rate of endangerment.
