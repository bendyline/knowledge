The **Naalakkersuisut** or the **Government of Greenland** ([Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_language): *Grønlands Regering*) is the chief executive body, the [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_\(government\)) and the government of [Greenland](12118.md) since the island became self-governing in 1979. As an autonomous territory of the [Kingdom of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Denmark), Greenland is a [parliamentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system) [representative democratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representative_democracy) [territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territory), in which the [premier](273648.md) (*Naalakkersuisut Siulittaasuat*) leads the [cabinet](37570.md), and of a [multi-party system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party_system).

There are currently 10 members of the Cabinet,[^cite_note-1] known as "Ministers" (*Naalakkersuisoq*), all of whom are also heads of specific government ministries. The ministers are appointed by the Prime Minister. The Greenlandic government currently consists of 10 ministers including the Prime Minister.[^cite_note-2]

## Executive power

Executive power rests with a high commissioner, and a [prime minister](273648.md) heads the Cabinet. The [high commissioner of Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Commission_of_Denmark,_Nuuk#List_of_High_Commissioners) since 2011 is appointed by the [monarch](20714373.md) ([King Frederik X](339155.md)), and the prime minister is elected indirectly by parliament elections results for four-year terms.

## Current cabinet

Further information: [Nielsen cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen_cabinet)

The Naalakkersuisut is divided into a number of areas of responsibility each led by a *Naalakkersuisoq* (English: Minister) with powers corresponding to that of a [minister](739237.md) or secretary of government. ## Legislative power

Legislative power is shared by the government and the legislature. The legislature or [Self-rule of Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Greenland#Home_rule) ([Greenlandic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenlandic_language): *Namminersorlutik Oqartussat*, [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_language): *Selvstyre*) is made up of 31 members in the *[Inatsisartut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Greenland)* elected by direct, popular vote to serve four-year terms.

## Judicial power

The [judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary) is independent of the executive and the legislature. Greenland has full autonomy on most matters, except on policies and decisions affecting the region including negotiations with the devolved legislatures and the Kingdom Parliament *[Folketing](429743.md)*. Greenland's judicial system has mainly been derived from the Danish civil law system. It has one court of first instance: the [Court of Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Greenland), and an appeal court the [High Court of Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Court_of_Greenland). No appeal is possible to decisions of the Joint Court of Justice, but fundamental "questions of law" may be submitted to the [Østre Landsret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%98stre_Landsret) and the [Supreme Court of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Denmark) in [cassation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassation). Verdicts by those institutions may lead to a new decision of the Joint Court, taking into account the results of the cassation.

## History

### Naalakkersuisut 2013

Parliamentary elections were held on March 12, 2013, and [Aleqa Hammond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleqa_Hammond), leader of the [Siumut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siumut) party, was designated as prime minister by a coalition of the parties [Siumut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siumut), [Partii Inuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inuit_Party), and [Atassut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atassut).

### Naalakkersuisut 2014

Parliamentary elections were held on 28 November 2014 and [Kim Kielsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Kielsen), leader of the [Siumut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siumut) party, was designated as prime minister by a coalition of the parties [Siumut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siumut), [Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democrats_\(Greenland\)), and [Atassut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atassut). The coalition was formed on 4 December 2014, Siumut has five ministers, Democrats 2 and Attasut 2.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
