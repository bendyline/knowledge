**Michael Prescott** is a British communications and corporate affairs professional and former journalist. He spent more than a decade at *[The Sunday Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sunday_Times)*, where he served as chief political correspondent and later political editor, before moving into public relations and senior corporate roles, including corporate affairs director at [BT Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BT_Group).[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2] Prescott is Managing Director of Hanover Communications and a member of the UK government's [Advisory Committee on Business Appointments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advisory_Committee_on_Business_Appointments).[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-3]

He came to wider public attention in 2025 after a leaked memo raised [concerns about editorial standards](81564054.md) at the [BBC](19344654.md), where he had served as an independent adviser to the Editorial Guidelines and Standards Board.[^cite_note-\:1-2][^cite_note-4]
