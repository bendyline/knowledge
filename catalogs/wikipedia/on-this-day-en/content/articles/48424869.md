The **People's Party of Andalusia** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Partido Popular de Andalucía*, PP), informally also known as the **Andalusian People's Party** (Spanish: *Partido Popular Andaluz*, PPA), is the regional wing of the Spanish [People's Party](404727.md) operating in [Andalusia](2736.md). It is the largest political party in the [Parliament of Andalusia](12181806.md), and has an outright majority following the elections in 2022.

## Party leaders

- Gabino Puche, 1989–1993

- [Javier Arenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Arenas_\(Spanish_politician\)), 1993–1999

- [Teófila Martínez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Te%C3%B3fila_Mart%C3%ADnez), 1999–2004

- Javier Arenas, 2004–2012

- [Juan Ignacio Zoido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Ignacio_Zoido), 2012–2014

- [Juan Manuel Moreno Bonilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Manuel_Moreno_Bonilla), 2014–present
