The **2026 Chinese Grand Prix** (officially known as the **Formula 1 Heineken Chinese Grand Prix 2026**) was a [Formula One](10854.md) motor race that was held on 15 March 2026 at the [Shanghai International Circuit](952143.md) in [Shanghai](27643.md), [China](5405.md). It was the second round of the [2026 Formula One World Championship](71681684.md) and the first of six Grands Prix in the season to utilise the [sprint format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_racing#Sprint).&#x20;

[George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)) converted pole position in the sprint to a victory. His teammate, 19-year-old [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md), became the [youngest Formula One polesitter](13599687.md#Youngest_polesitters), [second-youngest Grand Prix winner](13599687.md#Youngest_winners) and the first [Italian to win a Grand Prix](37543516.md) since [Giancarlo Fisichella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giancarlo_Fisichella) at the [2006 Malaysian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Malaysian_Grand_Prix). Antonelli led a Mercedes 1–2 ahead of Russell and [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton), who achieved his maiden podium with [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari).

## Background

The event was held at the [Shanghai International Circuit](952143.md) in [Shanghai](27643.md) for the 19th time in the circuit's history, across the weekend of 13–15 March.[^cite_note-5] The Grand Prix was the second round of the [2026 Formula One World Championship](71681684.md) and the 19th running of the [Chinese Grand Prix](339308.md).[^cite_note-6] It was also the first of six Grands Prix in the season to utilise the [sprint format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_racing#Sprint) and the third time overall that the Chinese Grand Prix featured it.[^cite_note-sprint-7]

### Championship standings before the race

Going into the event, [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) led the [Drivers' Championship](3437295.md) with 25 points, 7 points ahead of his teammate [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) in second, and 10 ahead of [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) ([Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari)) in third. [Mercedes](25098794.md) entered this round as the leaders of the [Constructors' Championship](3437393.md) with 43 points, 16 ahead of Ferrari and 33 ahead of [McLaren](20994.md), who were second and third, respectively.[^cite_note-8]

### Entrants

See also: [2026 Formula One World Championship § Entries](71681684.md#Entries)

The drivers and teams were the same as published in the season entry list, with no additional stand-in drivers for the race.[^cite_note-9] The Grand Prix marked the 1,000th entry for [McLaren](20994.md).[^cite_note-10]

### Tyre choices

Further information: [Formula One tyres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_tyres)

Tyre supplier [Pirelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirelli) brought the C2, C3, and C4 tyre compounds designated hard, medium, and soft, respectively, for teams to use at the event.[^cite_note-11]

## Practice

The only [free practice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_practice) session was held on 13 March 2026, at 11\:30 local time ([UTC+8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+8)), and was topped by [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)), ahead of teammate [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) and [Lando Norris](48978813.md) ([McLaren](20994.md)).[^cite_note-Chinese_GP-3][^cite_note-12]

## Sprint qualifying

[Sprint qualifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sprint_qualifying) was held on 13 March 2026, at 15\:30 local time ([UTC+8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+8)), and determined the starting grid order for the [sprint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_racing#Sprint).[^cite_note-Chinese_GP-3]

### Sprint qualifying report

Sprint qualifying consisted of three sessions, with the six drivers with the slowest lap times being eliminated following each of the first two sessions. For the first two sessions, new medium tyres were mandated, while the third and final session allowed teams to use any set of soft tyres.[^cite_note-\:0-13]

During the twelve-minute long first session (SQ1), [Carlos Sainz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.) and [Alexander Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Albon) (both [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing)) were eliminated, alongside [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) and [Lance Stroll](42274711.md) (both [Aston Martin](63473867.md)), and [Valtteri Bottas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valtteri_Bottas) and [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez) (both [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One)), Pérez did not set a time during the session.[^cite_note-\:0-13][^cite_note-\:1-14][^cite_note-\:2-15] The second session (SQ2), which lasted for ten minutes, saw the eliminations of [Nico Hülkenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_H%C3%BClkenberg) and [Gabriel Bortoleto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto) (both [Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_in_Formula_One)), [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) and [Arvid Lindblad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvid_Lindblad) (both [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls)), [Esteban Ocon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Ocon) ([Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team)), and [Franco Colapinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Colapinto) ([Alpine](65240620.md)).[^cite_note-\:0-13][^cite_note-\:1-14][^cite_note-\:2-15]

The third session (SQ3) lasted for eight minutes, and determined the final order of the remaining ten drivers. [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)) took pole position ahead of teammate [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md), while [Lando Norris](48978813.md) ([McLaren](20994.md)) finished third. [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) ([Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari)) and [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) (McLaren) finished in fourth and fifth, respectively. [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc), [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md), [Max Verstappen](41758713.md), [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman), and [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) completed the rest of the top ten.[^cite_note-\:0-13][^cite_note-\:1-14][^cite_note-\:2-15]

## Sprint

The [sprint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_racing#Sprint) was held on 14 March 2026, at 11\:00 local time ([UTC+8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+8)), and was run for 19 laps.[^cite_note-Chinese_GP-3]

### Sprint report

The sprint began with [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) holding first into the first turn, while [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) had move up to second from fourth, having passed [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) and [Lando Norris](48978813.md) down the main straight. Hamilton then passed Russell into turn 14, and was in the lead at the end of lap one. At turn 4, Antonelli made contact with Isack Hadjar, and received a 10-second penalty as a result. By lap 5, Russell had overtaken Hamilton, and was increasing his lead. Behind, [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) had overtaken his teammate Hamilton on lap 8, with Antonelli getting past [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) and Norris. Antonelli then overtook Hamilton on lap 11, and Leclerc two laps later. [Nico Hulkenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_H%C3%BClkenberg) then came to a stop, prompting a safety car and most of the front runners fitted the soft compound tyre. Antonelli served his penalty, coming out just ahead of Piastri in seventh. The sprint resumed on lap 14, with Russell holding the lead until the end. Leclerc finished second, with Hamilton two seconds behind in third. Norris held off Antonelli to take fourth. Piastri, [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) and [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman) collected the remaining points, in sixth to eighth respectively. ## Qualifying

## Race

The race was held on 15 March 2026, at 15\:00 local time ([UTC+8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+8)), and was run for 56 laps.[^cite_note-Chinese_GP-3]

### Race report

[Lando Norris](48978813.md), [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md), [Gabriel Bortoleto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto), and [Alexander Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Albon) all failed to make the race start. Norris and Piastri both suffered power unit problems. Bortoleto and Albon failed to start the race due to hydraulic problems.[^cite_note-24]

Starting from pole position, Antonelli took his maiden Grand Prix win, Championship leader [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) finished second. It was the first win for an Italian driver in Formula One since [Giancarlo Fisichella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giancarlo_Fisichella) at the [2006 Malaysian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Malaysian_Grand_Prix). With his win, Antonelli also became the second-youngest Formula One winner and the youngest driver to secure a [hat trick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hat-trick_\(Formula_One\)). Lewis Hamilton took his first podium in a full Grand Prix for Ferrari after duelling with teammate [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) for third for much of the race.[^cite_note-25]
