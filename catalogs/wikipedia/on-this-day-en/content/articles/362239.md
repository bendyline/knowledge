**Zabul Province**[^cite_note-6] is one of the 34 [provinces of Afghanistan](412343.md), located in the south of the country. It has a population of 249,000.[^cite_note-nsia-5] Zabul became a separate province from neighbouring [Kandahar](457241.md) in 1963. Historically, it was part of the [Zabulistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zabulistan) region. [Qalat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qalat,_Zabul_Province) serves as the capital of the province. The major ethnic group are [Pashtuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns). Primary occupations within Zabul are [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) and [animal husbandry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_husbandry).

According to [Iranic legend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_mythology), Zabul is the birthplace of the hero [Rostam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostam).[^cite_note-7]

## Geography

Further information: [Geography of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Afghanistan)



A bull walks at the foot of a mountain near Mizan in the southeast of Zabul Province.

Zabul borders [Uruzgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruzgan_Province) in the north, [Kandahar](457241.md) in the west and in the south, [Ghazni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghazni_Province) and [Paktika](457294.md) in the east. It borders [Pakistan](23235.md) in the east.

The province covers an area of 17293 km2. Two-fifths of the province is mountainous or semi mountainous terrain (41%) while more than one quarter of the area is made up of flat land (28%).

The primary ecoregion of the province is the central Afghan mountains xeric woodlands. Common vegetation is listed as dry shrub-land and [pistachio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pistachio). The high mountains of the northern portion of the province are in the [Ghor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghor_Province)-[Hazarajat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazarajat) alpine meadow ecoregion, which is characterized by meadows, willows, and [sea buckthorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_buckthorn).[^cite_note-8]

## Economy

Further information: [Transport in Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_in_Afghanistan)

In 2006, the province's first airstrip was opened near [Qalat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qalat,_Zabul_Province), to be operated by the [Afghan National Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_National_Army), but also for use by commercial aviation. Twice weekly service was scheduled by PRT Air between Qalat and [Kabul](16826.md). The airstrip is not paved.[^cite_note-21] The [ANA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_National_Army) Chief in Zabul is Major General Jamaluddin Sayed[^cite_note-22]

Zabul Province is bisected by [Highway 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highway_1_\(Afghanistan\)) and travelers going between [Kandahar and Kabul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabul%E2%80%93Kandahar_Highway) via road typically pass through the province.[^cite_note-23]

On 4 September 2016, at least 38 people were killed and 28 were injured during the September 2016 Afghanistan road crash.

## Demographics

### Population

As of 2021, the total population of the province is about 391,150,[^cite_note-nsia-5] which is mostly a rural tribal society. 60.8% of the population lived below the national [poverty line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_line), one of the highest figures of all of Afghanistan's provinces.[^cite_note-24]

### Ethnicity, languages and religion

According to the [Naval Postgraduate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Postgraduate_School), the population is primarily [Pashtuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns), sprinkled throughout around 2,500 remote villages. Major tribal groups include the Tokhi, Hotak, Nasar, Kharoti, Taraki, [Ghilji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghilji) and the [Noorzai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noorzai) and Panjpai [Durrani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durrani).

[Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language) is the dominant language in the area. The people of Zabul are overwhelmingly [Sunni Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam). Primary occupations within Zabul are agriculture and animal husbandry.[^cite_note-nps-25]

Zabul is by many indications one of Afghanistan's most religious conservative provinces.[^cite_note-26]

### Education

Further information: [Education in Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Afghanistan)



Bibi Khala School in Qalat

The overall literacy rate (6+ years of age) increased from 1% in 2005 to 19% in 2011. The overall net enrollment rate (6–13 years of age) fell from 31.3% in 2005 to 5% in 2011.

### Health

Further information: [Health in Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_in_Afghanistan)



An Afghan National Police officer, right, stands watch over food before distributing it to families Feb. 5, 2012, in Pinzo village, Zabul province, Afghanistan. The food distribution was intended to supplement the villagers winter food reserves from the fall harvest.

The percentage of households with clean drinking water increased from 0% in 2005 to 32% in 2011. The percentage of births attended to by a skilled birth attendant increased from 1% in 2005 to 5% in 2011.
