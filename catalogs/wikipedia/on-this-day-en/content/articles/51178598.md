The **Congolese National Police** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Police nationale congolaise*, PNC) is the [national police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_in_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) force of the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md). The national police consists of 110,000–150,000 officers and operates on the provincial level, answering to the Interior Ministry.[^cite_note-1] It has gained notoriety in the Congo for corruption, repression of political dissidents, and other human rights abuses.[^cite_note-brutal-2] It is currently undergoing reforms, and a police academy is being built.[^cite_note-3]

The current Police Commissioner General is [Dieudonne Amuli Bahigwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dieudonne_Amuli_Bahigwa?action=edit\&redlink=1), a former [Congolese army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_Forces_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) officer who replaced [Charles Bisengimana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Bisengimana) in July 2017.[^cite_note-4]

The former Kinshasa Police Commissioner, General [Celestin Kanyama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestin_Kanyama), was sanctioned by the United States in 2016 for his role in the violent crackdown of protestors during [anti-government protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Congolese_protests), and was consequently removed from his post in 2017.[^cite_note-5]

The organisation's budget is approximately \$257,130,643 (2015).

The PNC operates alongside the military police (*police militaire* or *PM*) to police the city of Kinshasa.

## History

Main article: [Law enforcement in the Democratic Republic of the Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_in_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo)

The legislation "Decree-Law N° 002-2002 On institution, organization and functioning of the Congolese national police" from 26 January 2002 establishes the role of the National Police (PNC) of the DRC.[^cite_note-6]

Starting in 2014, about 150 police officers part of the "Formed Police Unit" were deployed to the neighboring [Central African Republic](5478.md) as part of a peacekeeping contingent, along with 850 [FARDC](8029.md) troops.[^cite_note-7]

In February 2019 [Human Rights Watch](46530.md) accused the Congolese police of extrajudicially executing dozens of people during a crackdown on gangs in Kinshasa.[^cite_note-8]



Denise Tshisekedi with MONUSCO

In March 2022, first lady [Denise Nyakéru Tshisekedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denise_Nyak%C3%A9ru_Tshisekedi) visited the Congolese National Police in Kinshasa during training. They were working with [MONUSCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MONUSCO) police concerning gender mainstreaming in peacekeeping operations.[^cite_note-9]

## Weapons and Uniforms

Congolese police officers wear a blue uniform. Their service weapons are the [Kalashnikov (7.62mm)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AK-47) though the large majority of officers on the streets do not carry weapons.

## Vehicles

The National Police often use Toyota Land Cruisers and 4x4s as well as trucks, some more specialised vehicles and they use smaller Toyota Yaris type vehicles in the city of Kinshasa.
