**&#x20;Tecoanapa&#x20;**&#x20;is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Guerrero) in the [Mexican](3966054.md) [state](8196151.md) of [Guerrero](195816.md). The municipal seat lies at [Tecoanapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tecoanapa). The municipality covers an area of 776.9 square kilometres (300.0 mi<sup>2</sup>).

In 2005, the municipality had a population of 42,619.[^cite_note-1]
