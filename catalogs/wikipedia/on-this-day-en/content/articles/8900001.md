The **judiciary of Peru** is a branch of the [government of Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Peru) that interprets and applies the laws of Peru to ensure [equal justice under law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equal_justice_under_law) and provide a mechanism for [dispute resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dispute_resolution).[^cite_note-1]

Its headquarters are located at the [Palace of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palace_of_Justice,_Lima) in Lima.[^cite_note-2]

## Organization

The Peruvian judiciary is a hierarchical system of courts, with the [Supreme Court of Peru](8801058.md) at the top. The second level is composed of 28 [superior courts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superior_Courts_of_Peru), each of which has jurisdiction over a [judicial district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_Districts_of_Peru) which are more or less synonymous to the 25 [regions of Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Peru). The third level is formed by 195 [courts of first instance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courts_of_First_Instance_of_Peru) (trial court), each of which has jurisdiction over a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Peru). The fourth and lowest level is composed of 1,838 [courts of peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courts_of_Peace_of_Peru), each with jurisdiction over a single [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Peru).[^cite_note-3]

## History

The predecessor to the judiciary of Peru was the [Royal Audiencia of Lima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Audiencia_of_Lima), in the [Viceroyalty of Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viceroyalty_of_Peru). When Peru achieved independence, [José de San Martín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_de_San_Mart%C3%ADn) resolved that the Audencia of Lima would be used as a national court until a permanent judicial system was established. Later, [Simón Bolívar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sim%C3%B3n_Bol%C3%ADvar) established the makings of the current Judicial system, with the creation of the Superior Courts of Justice of [Lima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_District_of_Lima), [Cusco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_District_of_Cusco), [La Libertad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_District_of_La_Libertad), and [Huamanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_District_of_Ayacucho).
