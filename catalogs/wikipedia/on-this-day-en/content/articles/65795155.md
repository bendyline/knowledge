**Bekasi Timur Station** (**BKST**, also known as **Ampera Station** or **Bulak Kapal Station**) is a class III [railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_railway_stations_in_Indonesia) located in Duren Jaya, East Bekasi, [Bekasi](1915107.md), West Java. The station, which is located at an altitude of +19 meters, is included in the Jakarta Operational Area I and only serves the [KRL Commuterline](15039992.md) route.

## History



The signage of Bekasi Timur Station

Bekasi Timur Station was inaugurated on 7 October 2017 by the [Minister of Transportation of Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Transportation_\(Indonesia\)), [Budi Karya Sumadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budi_Karya_Sumadi), along with the inauguration of the KRL Commuterline [Blue Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KA_Commuter_Line_Jakarta_Kota%E2%80%93Cikarang) for [Cikarang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cikarang_railway_station) Station.[^cite_note-BKST-2]

The reason for the creation of this station is because [Bekasi station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bekasi_railway_station) has exceeded its passenger capacity by almost 190% from morning and evening trips to and from work, so this station was built to break down the density of passengers at the station.[^cite_note-3]

## Incidents

Main article: [2026 Bekasi train crash](83050707.md)

On 27 April 2026, the [KRL Commuterline](15039992.md) train running as PLB 5568A, had an emergency stop due to a railway crossing accident that happened to a Cikarang-bound [CLI-125](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SFC120-V_Series) an unmarked crossing. PLB 5568A was then hit from behind by [Argo Bromo Anggrek](36507072.md) running as KAI 4 from [Gambir station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambir_railway_station). The [CC206 Locomotive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GE_CM20EMP) pulling KAI 4 penetrated half the length of the rearmost car of PLB 5568A, a [Tokyo Metro 6000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metro_6000_series). At least 81 people were injured and 16 people died as a result of this accident.[^cite_note-CNA-5]
