**Naim Qassem**[^cite_note-3] (born February 1953) is a [Lebanese Shia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia) cleric and politician who is serving as the 4th [secretary-general of Hezbollah](9364927.md) since 29 October 2024.[^cite_note-4] He participated in the founding of [Hezbollah](13919.md) in 1982, and previously served as the first [deputy secretary-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Secretary-General_of_Hezbollah) from 1991 to 2024.[^cite_note-5]

Born in [Kfar Kila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kafr_Kila,_Lebanon), Qassem received a master's degree in chemistry from the [Lebanese University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_University) in 1977. He worked as a chemistry teacher, before joining [Amal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amal_Movement), a political movement led by [Musa al-Sadr](2432466.md). He studied theology under [Mohammad Hussein Fadlallah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Hussein_Fadlallah).

Following the [Iranian Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_revolution), he helped found Hezbollah. In 1991, he was appointed as deputy secretary-general under secretary-general [Abbas al-Musawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_al-Musawi), a role he retained under Al-Musawi's successor, [Hassan Nasrallah](313436.md). He led the party's electoral campaigns, and held a leading intellectual and ideological role.[^cite_note-6]

Following the [assassination of Hassan Nasrallah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Hezbollah_headquarters_strike) in September 2024, he was appointed acting secretary-general. A month later in October, Qassem was elected as secretary-general after Nasrallah's possible successor [Hashem Safieddine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hashem_Safieddine) was [assassinated by Israeli strikes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Hashem_Safieddine). On 7 March 2026, it was reported that Lebanese Minister of Justice is considering the option of prosecuting him.[^cite_note-7]

## Early life and education

Qassem was born in February 1953 in [Kfar Fila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kfar_Fila), into a [Shiite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islam_in_Lebanon) family with origins in the town.[^cite_note-npr-8][^cite_note-reuters-9][^cite_note-wmer5dec-10][^cite_note-11] He grew up in [Beirut](37428.md).[^cite_note-guardian-12] He studied [theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theology) and his teacher was [Mohammad Hussein Fadlallah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Hussein_Fadlallah).[^cite_note-wmer5dec-10] He received a [bachelors](188874.md) and a [master](170346.md)'s degree in [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry) from the [Lebanese University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_University), completing his studies in 1977.[^cite_note-wmer5dec-10][^cite_note-npr-8][^cite_note-13] He had studied the subject in [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language).[^cite_note-14]

## Career



Qassem as Deputy Secretary-General of [Hezbollah](13919.md) in 2009

Qassem began his career employed as a chemistry teacher in public schools for some six years.[^cite_note-wmer5dec-10][^cite_note-npr-8]

Qassem was one of the founders of the Lebanese Union of Muslim Students that was established in the 1970s.[^cite_note-AvonK2012-15] He joined the [Amal Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amal_Movement) when it was led by [Musa al-Sadr](2432466.md),[^cite_note-wmer5dec-10][^cite_note-reuters-9] but left in 1979.[^cite_note-reuters-9] Qassem was the head of the Association for Islamic Religious Education from 1974 to 1988.[^cite_note-AvonK2012-15] He also served as the advisor for al-Mustafa schools.[^cite_note-AvonK2012-15] Qassem participated in the foundational activities of [Hezbollah](13919.md).[^cite_note-wmer5dec-10]

In 1991, he became the deputy secretary-general of Hezbollah.[^cite_note-mer20nov-16] [Abbas al-Musawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_al-Musawi) appointed him to this role, which he retained when al-Musawi was succeeded by [Hassan Nasrallah](313436.md) in 1992.[^cite_note-reuters-9]

Qassem has handled Hezbollah's political campaigns for parliamentary elections since the [1992 Lebanese general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Lebanese_general_election), which was their first time participating.[^cite_note-reuters-9] In the years leading up to 2024, Qassem has "long been one of Hezbollah's leading spokesmen".[^cite_note-17]

### Secretary-General of Hezbollah

Following the [2024 Hezbollah headquarters strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Hezbollah_headquarters_strike), Qassem gave his first statement, claiming that “[Hezbollah will go on with its goals and its battle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideology_of_Hezbollah),”, assuring that even after Nasrallah's death, the organization and its fighters remain strong.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

Qassem was elected [secretary-general](9364927.md) of Hezbollah on 29 October 2024, following the Israeli assassination of the previous leader [Hassan Nasrallah](313436.md) in the [2024 Hezbollah headquarters strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Hezbollah_headquarters_strike) and his assumed successor [Hashem Safieddine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hashem_Safieddine) on 3 October.[^cite_note-guardian-12]

On 7 August 2025, the [Lebanese Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Lebanon) approved the restriction of weapons to the exclusive possession of the state.[^cite_note-20] In an appearance on 15 August, Qasim threatened that “there will be no life in Lebanon if the government tries to confront his party,” and expressed his readiness to wage a “[Karbala-like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Karbala) battle” to challenge the decision to limit arms to the state and to [disarm](80353105.md) the defensive resistance.[^cite_note-21]

On 7 March 2026, following his controversial remarks accusing the government of carrying out Israeli orders, [Adel Nassar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adel_Nassar), Lebanon's Minister of Justice said he is considering taking legal action against him.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] On 18 March, he repeated that Hezbollah is loyal to Iran and supports it.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

On 25 May 2026, the Al-Hadath news channel reported that Israel recently made two attempts to target Qassem.[^cite_note-26] In a speech delivered on 4 August 2026, Qassem stated for the first time that he did not object to meeting with the [new Syrian leadership](79584988.md).[^cite_note-27]

## Works and views

In 2002, Qassem published a book, *Hizbullah: The Story from Within*, which was revised and updated four times, mostly recently in 2010.[^cite_note-AvonK2012-15] In August 2011, Qassem attended a ceremony for the eighth edition of his book, where he made the statement that "Billions of dollars have been offered to us to rebuild the deprived south Lebanon and in return to surrender our arms and stop the work of the resistance. But we told them we're not in need \[of their money] and the resistance will go on regardless of the consequences."[^cite_note-tds1aug-28]

In 2009, [Mustafa Badreddine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_Badreddine) replaced [Imad Mughniyeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imad_Mughniyeh) as the head of Hezbollah's military activities.[^cite_note-zvi25feb-29] Qassem did not support the move, favoring his relative Samir Shehade.[^cite_note-zvi25feb-29]

In June 2013, he maintained that [Hezbollah’s combat in Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hezbollah_involvement_in_the_Syrian_civil_war) was 'to defend the resistance project and constitutes an integral part of the resistance against Israel, protecting our rear and the resistance's supply hub, preventing the Israeli threat from spreading to our homes, and severing links between terrorists.' He added that his party fights for no one else.[^cite_note-30]

## Personal life

Qassem is married and has six children.[^cite_note-reuters-9]
