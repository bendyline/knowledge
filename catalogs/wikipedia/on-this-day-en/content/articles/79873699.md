**Salem Saleh Salem bin Braik** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): سالم صالح سالم بن بريك; born 3 April 1965) is a Yemeni politician who has served as the internationally recognised [Prime Minister of Yemen](718737.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Early life

Salem Saleh Salem bin Braik was born on 3 April 1965 in [Mukalla](2414800.md), a coastal city in eastern [Yemen](350939.md) along the [Gulf of Aden](58716.md).[^cite_note-4]

## Career

Before bin Braik was appointed Prime Minister, he held the position of Finance Minister. But following the resignation of [Ahmad Awad bin Mubarak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_Awad_bin_Mubarak), who stepped down citing difficulties in executing his role, including being blocked from implementing a cabinet reshuffle. Mubarak's tenure was marked by clashes with [Rashad al-Alimi](70502008.md), head of Yemen's presidential council, over the dismissal of several government ministers.[^cite_note-5] Bin Braik's leadership came at a time when Yemen continued to grapple with a prolonged civil war, with the [Houthi controlled rival government](51279323.md) who control significant portions of the country, including the capital, [Sanaa](18691665.md). The [U.S.](3434750.md) has intensified airstrikes against Houthi positions to deter their attacks on commercial shipping in the [Red Sea](26191.md).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] On 5 December 2025 Salem Saleh bin Braik left Aden for Riyadh, Saudi Arabia following an [offensive](81757459.md) by the [Southern Transitional Council](54251566.md) which saw it rapidly take control of most of the former South Yemen.[^cite_note-9] He attended a meeting of Yemen's National Defence Council in Riyadh on 26 December 2025.[^cite_note-10]
