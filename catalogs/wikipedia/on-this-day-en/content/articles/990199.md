The **province of Verona** ([Italian](14708.md): *Provincia di Verona*) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Italy) of the region of [Veneto](43780.md) in [northern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Italy) [Italy](14532.md). On its northwestern border, [Lake Garda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Garda)—Italy's largest—is divided between Verona and the provinces of [Brescia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Brescia) ([Lombardy](43807.md) region) and [Trentino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trentino) ([Trentino-Alto Adige/Südtirol](246766.md) region). Its capital is the city of [Verona](263991.md). The city is a [UNESCO World Heritage Site](44940.md).[^cite_note-Italia-4]

The province has a population of 930,842 in an area of 3,096.39 square kilometres (1,195.52 sq mi) across its 98 municipalities.[^cite_note-population-2][^cite_note-area-1]

The province is cosmopolitan in nature. It is bordered by Italian [Tyrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrol) in the north, [province of Vicenza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Vicenza) and [province of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Padua) in the east, [province of Rovigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Rovigo) and [province of Mantua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Mantua) in the south, and Lake Garda in the west. From north to south the maximum extent of the province is 50 miles while it is 25 miles from east to west.[^cite_note-Penny-5]



[Juliet Capulet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliet)'s villa in Verona

[William Shakespeare](32897.md)'s play *[Romeo and Juliet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romeo_and_Juliet)* takes place in Verona, as do some scenes in his play *[The Two Gentlemen of Verona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Two_Gentlemen_of_Verona)*. Verona attracts many tourists, and the Casa di Giulietta ([Juliet Capulet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliet)'s villa in the play) is an important local visitor attraction.[^cite_note-Italia-4]

## Demographics

As of 2026, the population is 930,842, of which 49.4% are male, and 50.6% are female. Minors make up 15.2% of the population, and seniors make up 23.9%.[^cite_note-population-2]

### Immigration

As of 2025, immigrants make up 14.9% of the population. The 5 largest foreign countries of birth are [Romania](25445.md), [Morocco](19291.md), [Moldova](19260.md), [Sri Lanka](26750.md), and [Albania](738.md).[^cite_note-foreignborn-8]

## Sights

Due to its historic importance, the province boasts a large number of castles, towers, hermitages, monasteries, sanctuaries, and old Romanesque parishes. A [regional park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lessinia_Regional_Park) is located in [Lessinia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lessinia). [Valpolicella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valpolicella) is popular for its wines which are made from indigenous techniques. Europe's biggest natural bridge-Ponte di Veja is located in the province. The northern part of the province is mostly hilly, with several rivers, including [Tartaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tartaro-Canalbianco-Po_di_Levante), Caslagnaro and [Adige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adige).[^cite_note-Penny-5]
