**Tshopo** is one of the 21 [provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo](3984115.md) created in the [2015 repartitioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_the_DR_Congo#New_provinces). It is situated in the north central part of the country on the [Tshopo River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tshopo_River), for which it is named.

Tshopo, [Bas-Uele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bas-Uele), [Haut-Uele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haut-Uele), and [Ituri](1545860.md) provinces are the result of the dismemberment of the former [Orientale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientale_Province) province.[^cite_note-2] Tshopo was formed from the **Tshopo district** and the independently administered city of [Kisangani](588207.md) which retained its status as a provincial capital. The 2020 population was estimated to be 2,829,700.[^cite_note-3]

## Geography

Tshopo is located in the north central Democratic Republic of the Congo. It borders the provinces of [Tshuapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tshuapa) to the west; [Mongala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongala), [Bas-Uele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bas-Uele), and [Haut-Uele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haut-Uele) to the north; [Ituri](1545860.md) and [North Kivu](446958.md) to the east; and [Maniema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maniema_Province) and [Sankuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sankuru) to the south. Most of Tshopo has a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) by the [Köppen-Geiger climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen-Geiger_climate_classification), except for parts of the north that have a [tropical monsoon climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate).[^cite_note-4]

## History

Map of Tshopo showing tree-cover loss years, with forest remaining since 2000 in green and loss years shaded yellow through purple.

Tree-cover loss year in Tshopo, 2001-2024, from the [Global Forest Change dataset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Forest_Change_dataset).

From 1963 to 1966, the area was constituted as the province of **Haut-Congo** (Upper Congo). It was merged into [Orientale Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientale_Province) in 1966 as, separately, the **District of Tshopo** and the city of Kisangani. The Presidents (later governors) of Haut-Congo were:

- 1963 – 26 June 1963: Georges Grenfell (b. 1908)

- 26 June 1963 – 1964: Paul Isombuma

- 1964 – August 1964: François Aradjabu

- August 1964 – 5 Nov 1966: Jean Marie Alamazani

Provincial status was re-instated to Tshopo in 2015, being formed from Tshopo District and the city of Kisangani.[^cite_note-6]
