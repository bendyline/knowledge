The number of registered crimes per [Ukrainian Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Oblast) in 2008

The fight against **crime in [Ukraine](31750.md)** is led by the [Ukrainian Police](44656231.md) and certain other agencies. Due to the hard economic situation in the 1990s, the crime rate rose steadily to a peak in 2000. Following this peak, the crime rate declined, until 2009. In that year, the [2008 financial crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_financial_crisis) reached Ukraine.

## Crime by type

### Murder

Further information: [List of countries by intentional homicide rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_intentional_homicide_rate)

In 2010, Ukraine had a murder rate of 4.3 per 100,000 of population.[^cite_note-UNODC-2] There were a total of 1,988 murders in Ukraine in 2010.[^cite_note-UNODC-2] In 2017, 0.3% of Ukrainian crime was homicide.[^cite_note-UNIAN2324289-3] In 2016 the Ukrainian police investigated 1,707 murders and, in 2017, 1,397.[^cite_note-UNIAN2324289-3]

### Corruption

Further information: [Corruption in Ukraine](33085862.md)

Corruption is a widespread and growing problem in [Ukrainian](31750.md) society.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] In 2014's [Transparency International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transparency_International) [Corruption Perceptions Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corruption_Perceptions_Index), Ukraine was ranked 142nd out of the 175 countries investigated (tied with [Uganda](31816.md) and [the Comoros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Comoros)).[^cite_note-6]

Bribes are given to ensure that public services are delivered either in time or at all.[^cite_note-corupt-7] Ukrainians have stated they give bribes because they think it is customary and expected.[^cite_note-corupt-7][^cite_note-Gfk2001-8] According to a 2008 Management Systems International (MSI) sociological survey, the highest corruption levels were found in vehicle inspection (57.5%), the police (54.2%), health care (54%), the courts (49%) and higher education (43.6%).[^cite_note-TI-GCP-2008-p225-9] On 8 June 2011, Ukrainian President [Viktor Yanukovych](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Yanukovych) stated that corruption costs the state budget US\$2.5 billion in revenues annually and that, through corrupt dealings in public procurement, 10 to 15% (US\$7.4 billion) of the state budget "ends up in the pockets of officials".[^cite_note-Yanusays4June2011-10]

According to the [United States Agency for International Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Agency_for_International_Development) (USAID), the main causes of corruption in Ukraine are a weak [justice system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_system_of_Ukraine) and an over-controlling, non-transparent government combined with [business-political ties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_oligarchs) and a weak [civil society](209443.md).[^cite_note-USAID2006-11] Corruption is regularly discussed in the [Ukrainian media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_media).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-KPJune252009-13]

In May 2018, Estonian President [Kersti Kaljulaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kersti_Kaljulaid) stated that corruption is the primary factor holding back the development of Ukraine and that it can only be resolved with a strong political will, after a meeting with the head of the [National Anti-Corruption Bureau of Ukraine](33096574.md) (NABU), Artem Sytnyk.[^cite_note-14]

### Theft



Kyiv police investigating a burglary in May 2014

In 2017, the main segment of crime was theft, representing a 52% proportion of the total crimes in Ukraine.[^cite_note-UNIAN2324289-3]

### Terrorism

According to official statistics there were 126 acts of terror on Ukrainian soil every month in 2014, 108 in 2015, 155 in 2016 and 124 in 2017.[^cite_note-UNIAN2324289b-15]

From 2014 until late 2017, 5,804 criminal cases were registered as 'acts of terror', or 129 terrorist attacks per month, on average. In this time-frame, 15 persons were convicted on charges of terrorism.[^cite_note-UNIAN2324289b-15]
