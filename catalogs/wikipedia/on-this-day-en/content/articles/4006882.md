# Indiana's congressional districts



Indiana's congressional districts since 2023\
[Interactive map version](4006882.md)

[Indiana](21883857.md) has nine [congressional districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congressional_districts). They were last redrawn after the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census) and took effect in 2023, following the [2022 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Indiana).

## Current districts and representatives

This is a list of United States representatives from Indiana, their terms in office, district boundaries, and the district political ratings according to the [CPVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CPVI). The delegation in the [119th United States Congress](70645797.md) has nine members: seven [Republicans](32070.md) and two [Democrats](5043544.md).
