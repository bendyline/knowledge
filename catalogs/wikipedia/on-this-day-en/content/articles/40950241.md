**John John Mnyika** is a Tanzanian [CHADEMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chama_cha_Demokrasia_na_Maendeleo) politician and his party Secretary General.

He was [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament) for Ubungo constituency from 2010 to 2015 and Kibamba constituency from 2015 to 2020.[^cite_note-1]
