**Sulima** is a [chiefdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiefdoms_of_sierra_leone) that is located in the [Falaba District](57997417.md) of [Sierra Leone](27308.md), with a population of 20,062.[^cite_note-census-1] Its principal town is [Falaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falaba).
