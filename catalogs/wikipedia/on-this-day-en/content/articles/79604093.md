The **2025–26 FA Cup** was the 145th season of the [Football Association Challenge Cup](11237.md), the oldest [football](10568.md) tournament in the world. It is sponsored by [Emirates](142715.md) and known as the **Emirates FA Cup** for sponsorship reasons. The FA Cup is the main domestic cup competition for men's football teams in England. The qualifying competition began on 1 August 2025, with the tournament proper starting on 31 October 2025. The [final](80461614.md) was played at [Wembley Stadium](8913012.md), [London](17867.md), on 16 May 2026.&#x20;

Defending champions [Crystal Palace](385313.md), having beaten [Manchester City](165813.md) in the [2025 final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_FA_Cup_final) to win their first ever title, were eliminated in the third round after an [upset victory](82078681.md) by sixth-tier [National League North](645482.md) side [Macclesfield](66155008.md).[^cite_note-3]

Manchester City, the previous year's finalists, won their 8th FA Cup, defeating [Premier League](11250.md) side [Chelsea](7473.md) in what was their fourth consecutive FA Cup Final.

## Teams, dates and regulations



Teams in each round.

The FA Cup is a [knockout](939680.md) competition with 124 teams taking part from the first round proper, and all trying to reach the final at Wembley Stadium on 16 May 2026.[^cite_note-Teams-2] The competition consists of the 92 teams from the Football League system (20 teams from the [Premier League](11250.md) and the 72 in total from the [EFL Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship), [EFL League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_One) and [EFL League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_Two)) plus the 32 surviving teams out of 655 teams from the [National League System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_System) that started the competition in the [qualifying rounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_FA_Cup_qualifying_rounds). The total 747 entrants was an increase of 2 from the previous season,[^cite_note-Teams-2] and the most since 758 entrants were accepted in [2012–13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012%E2%80%9313_FA_Cup).

## Rule changes

This is the second consecutive (and third overall) edition of the tournament to be played without replays in the proper rounds since [2020–21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_FA_Cup), following a six-year agreement beginning in [2024–25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_FA_Cup) between the FA and the [Premier League](11250.md), and the second edition of the tournament to have the final played one week before the end of the Premier League season.[^cite_note-4]

## Qualifying

For all qualifying round results, see [2025–26 FA Cup qualifying rounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_FA_Cup_qualifying_rounds).

## First round

A total of 80 teams played in the first round: 32 winners from the fourth qualifying round, 24 from [League Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_Two) (tier 4), and 24 from [League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_League_One) (tier 3). Eighth tier side [Maldon & Tiptree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maldon_&_Tiptree_F.C.) (Isthmian League North Division) were the lowest-ranked team in the draw.

The 40 winners from the first round played in the second round. Sixth tier sides [Buxton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buxton_F.C.) and [Macclesfield](66155008.md) from National League North, [Chelmsford City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelmsford_City_F.C.), [Slough Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slough_Town_F.C.) and [Weston-super-Mare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weston-super-Mare_A.F.C.) from National League South were the lowest-ranked teams in the draw.

## Third round

A total of 64 clubs played in the third round: 20 winners from the second round, 20 from the [Premier League](11250.md) (tier 1), and 24 from the [Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship) (tier 2). Sixth tier sides [Macclesfield](66155008.md) (National League North) and [Weston-super-Mare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weston-super-Mare_A.F.C.) (National League South) were the lowest-ranked teams in the draw.

## Fourth round

The 32 winners from the third round played in the fourth round. National League North (tier 6) side [Macclesfield](66155008.md) was again the lowest-ranked team in the draw. In reaching this stage, Macclesfield equalled the best-ever Cup performance of Macclesfield Town, who had competed in the fourth round of the [2012–13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012%E2%80%9313_FA_Cup) tournament. Elsewhere, Mansfield Town's surprise victory over Premier League outfit Burnley was the Stags' first Cup win over top-tier opponents since 1969.

The draw was made on 12 January 2026 by [Joleon Lescott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joleon_Lescott) and [Steve McManaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_McManaman), and took place at [Anfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anfield), [Liverpool](18081.md), prior to the third round match between [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.) and [Barnsley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnsley_F.C.).[^cite_note-11]
