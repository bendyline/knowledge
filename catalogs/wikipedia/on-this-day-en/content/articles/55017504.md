**Ahmad Donyamali** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): احمد دنیامالی; born 1960) is an Iranian politician, who currently is the Minister of Sport and Youth since August 2024. He represented [Bandar-e Anzali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandar-e_Anzali) constituency in the [Parliament of Iran](383276.md) from 2020 to 2024.

He was formerly chairman of Ports and Maritime Organization of Iran[^cite_note-1] and president of Canoeing, Rowing, and Sailing Federation.[^cite_note-2]

Donyamali, along with fellow councillor [Elaheh Rastgou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elaheh_Rastgou), entered the council endorsed by the [reformist](491098.md) faction, but [crossed the floor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossing_the_floor) to vote in favor of the conservative [Mehdi Chamran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehdi_Chamran).[^cite_note-3]

**Educational Background**

- Master’s degree in Architecture and Urban Development
