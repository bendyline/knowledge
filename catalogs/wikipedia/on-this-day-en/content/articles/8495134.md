# Habshan

**Habshan** is an area in the [southwestern part](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Gharbia,_Abu_Dhabi) of the [Emirate of Abu Dhabi](8821238.md) in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md).

## History

It is currently a major [oil](2910801.md) and [gas field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_gas_field) for the [Abu Dhabi National Oil Company](7992255.md) (ADNOC).[^cite_note-1] It contains oil field and camps for oil workers. The major part of this region is marked as a [Red Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclusion_zone) due to the presence of [hydrogen sulphide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_sulfide) gas.



View of the [Wadi Ham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadi_Ham) from the top of the Habshan Pipeline cutting

The area is a major production area for [sulphur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulphur), a byproduct of the oil industry,[^cite_note-national2015-1-2] and is the terminal station of a new railway network in the United Arab Emirates developed by [Etihad Rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etihad_Rail). Commercial operations on this railway commenced in December 2015.[^cite_note-national2015-2-3]

The gas facility was damaged by falling debris of an intercepted aerial vehicle on 3 April 2026 during the [2026 Iran War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_War).[^cite_note-4]
