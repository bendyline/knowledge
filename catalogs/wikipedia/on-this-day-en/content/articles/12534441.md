**Jiang** ([formerly romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wade%E2%80%93Giles) **chiang** and usually translated **general** or, for navies, **admiral**) is a [general officer](148537.md) (or [flag officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_officer) for navies) rank used by [China](5405.md) and [Taiwan](25734.md). It is also used as **jang** in [North](21255.md) and [South Korea](27019.md), **shō** in [Japan](15573.md), and **tướng** in [Vietnam](202354.md).

Under the rank system in place in the PLA in the era 1955–1965, there existed the rank of *[dajiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Da_jiang)* ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 大将; lit. 'Grand commander'). This rank was awarded to 10 of the veteran leaders of the PLA in 1955 and never conferred again. It was considered equivalent to the Soviet rank of [army general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_General_\(Soviet_rank\)). The decision to name the equivalent rank *yiji shangjiang* ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 一级上将; lit. 'Upper commander first class') when it was briefly re-established in 1988-1994 was likely due to a desire to keep the rank of *dajiang* an honorary one awarded after a war, much as [General of the Armies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_of_the_Armies) in the [United States Army](32087.md). It was offered to Deng Xiaoping who declined the new rank. Thus it was never conferred and scrapped in 1994.

## Japanese variant

The same rank names are used for all services, prefixed by *riku* ([Japanese](15606.md): 陸, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'land'), *kai* ([Japanese](15606.md): 海, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'sea') or *kū* ([Japanese](15606.md): 空, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'air'). The rank insignia of the *Bakuryōchō-taru-shō* ([Japanese](15606.md): 幕僚長たる将) was enacted on December 1, 1962, and before that, it was the same three-star as other *Shō* ([Japanese](15606.md): 将). The English notation of the *Shō* in the English version of the Defense White Paper until 2024 is as follows.\


- *Tōgōbakuryōchō-taru-Rikusho, Kaishõ,mata-ha Kũshō* ([Japanese](15606.md): 統合幕僚長たる陸将、海将または空将) GEN, ADM or Gen serving as Chief of Staff Joint Staff Office.

- *Rikujōbakuryōchō-taru-Rikushō* ([Japanese](15606.md): 陸上幕僚長たる陸将) General serving as JGSDF Chief of Staff.

- *Rikushō* ([Japanese](15606.md): 陸将) General.

- *Kaijōbakuryōchō-taru-Kaishō* ([Japanese](15606.md): 海上幕僚長たる海将) Admiral serving as JMSDF Chief of Staff.

- *Kaishō* ([Japanese](15606.md): 海将) Vice Admiral.

- *Kōkūbakuryōchō-taru-Kũshō* ([Japanese](15606.md): 航空幕僚長たる空将) General serving as JASDF Chief of Staff.

- *Kūshō* ([Japanese](15606.md): 空将) General.
