**Homs Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مُحافظة حمص, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Muḥāfaẓat Ḥimṣ*) is one of the fourteen [governorates](690220.md) of [Syria](7515849.md). It is situated in central Syria. It has 42,226 km<sup>2</sup> (16,304 sq mi).It is geographically the largest governorate in Syria. Homs Governorate has a population of 1,763,000 as of 2010. The Homs governorate is divided into six administrative districts (*[mantiqah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantiqah)*), with the city of Homs as a separate district. [Homs](77533.md) is the [capital city](181337.md) of the [district of Homs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homs_District). Its governor is Namir Habib Makhlouf.[^cite_note-Zwaan-2]

## History

A Homs Governorate also formed part of [Ottoman Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Syria), when it was also known as the Sanjak of Homs.

During the [Syrian civil war](30741795.md), the governorate witnessed several major military operations, including the [Homs offensive of November–December 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homs_offensive_\(November%E2%80%93December_2015\)), which marked one of the key campaigns in the region.

In late 2011, the population of Homs reached 1,803,000. This amounted to 8% of Syria’s total population. [Sunni Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Muslim) made up 64% of the governorate, while [Alawite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alawite_Muslim) made up 25%, [Christians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity_in_Syria) made up 8%, [Twelver Shia Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelver_Shia_Muslim) made up 2%, and [Ismaili Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismaili_Muslim) made up 0.2%.[^cite_note-3]
