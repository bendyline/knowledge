**Myriam Spiteri Debono**[^cite_note-1] ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A9e) **Zammit**; born 25 October 1952)[^cite_note-2] is a Maltese politician who is the 11th and current [president of Malta](348075.md). She is the first [Gozitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gozo) woman to be elected to the position. She was also the [Speaker of the House of Representatives of Malta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_House_of_Representatives_of_Malta)[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] from 1996 to 1998, the first woman to hold that position.[^cite_note-\:3-5]

## Early life and career

Spiteri Debono was born Miriam Zammit in [Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria,_Malta), [Gozo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gozo), to Pawlu Zammit and his wife.[^cite_note-6] She received her formal childhood education in Gozo and later attended the [University of Malta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Malta) where she studied English Literature and Linguistics, graduating in 1973, and founded the Socialist Students Union.[^cite_note-\:5-7][^cite_note-\:2-8] She also studied law and graduated as a [Notary Public](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notary_public) in 1980.[^cite_note-\:0-9][^cite_note-\:5-7]

Her first public office appointments included that of chair of the Cooperatives Board, and as a founding member of the Gender Equality Commission.[^cite_note-\:0-9]

She was a candidate for the [Malta Labour Party](404834.md) in five general elections (1981, 1987, 1992, 1996, 2003), but was never elected as a member of parliament.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-Redemption,_representation,_maturity-11] However, she was elected to the party's national executive in 1982 and served as the party's propaganda secretary.[^cite_note-\:5-7][^cite_note-12]

Spiteri Debono served as president of the [Malta Labour Party](404834.md)'s women section between 1993 and 1996.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-\:4-14]

In September 1996, she was appointed Malta's [Speaker of the House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_House_of_Representatives_of_Malta) by Prime Minister [Alfred Sant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Sant).[^cite_note-\:2-8] She was the first woman to serve in this role in Malta and was replaced by [Anton Tabone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Tabone) in October 1998.[^cite_note-\:3-5][^cite_note-\:2-8]

In January 2023, she was proposed for the role of Standards Commissioner by the [Nationalist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalist_Party_\(Malta\)), however, she declined to be formally considered for the role claiming that she was not suited for it nor was she interested in taking the position.[^cite_note-15]

## Presidency

On 21 March 2024, she was reported to be the front runner to be appointed [President of the Republic of Malta](348075.md).[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] The following week, this was confirmed in a joint statement issued from the offices of the Prime Minister and the Leader of the Opposition.[^cite_note-\:1-18] Malta's House of Representatives voted unanimously to [elect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Maltese_presidential_election) Spiteri Debono as Malta's 11th President on 27 March 2024, becoming the first president to be elected using the new constitutional reforms of 2020 including the new requirement of two-thirds of the parliament's approval for the presidential nominee.[^cite_note-\:0-9]

Spiteri Debono is the third woman to serve as President of Malta, after [Agatha Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agatha_Barbara) and [Marie-Louise Coleiro Preca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie-Louise_Coleiro_Preca).[^cite_note-19] She is also the third person from Gozo to be appointed to this office, following [Anton Buttigieg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Buttigieg) and [Ċensu Tabone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%8Aensu_Tabone).[^cite_note-\:0-9]

She was inaugurated on 4 April 2024.[^cite_note-20]

At the time of her appointment she admitted to being a [technophobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technophobe); owning a [flip phone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flip_phone) and preferring to dictate her letters and other correspondence to an assistant, leaving emails to an email address in her husband's name.[^cite_note-21]

## Personal life

Spiteri Debono lived most of her adult life in [Birkirkara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkirkara), Malta.[^cite_note-\:4-14] She was married to notary Dr. Anthony Spiteri Debono, who died on 24 July 2025, at the age of 78.[^cite_note-22] They have three children and four grandchildren.[^cite_note-23]

## Honours

### National honours

-  Grand Master and Companion of Honour of the [National Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Merit_\(Malta\)), Malta, *by right as a President of Malta*
