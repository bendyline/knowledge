[Abelardo de la Espriella](81665680.md)'s inauguration as the 36th [President of Colombia](251302.md) took place on Friday, August 7, 2026. It was the 40th Colombian presidential inauguration marking the beginning of his four-year term as president and [José Manuel Restrepo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Manuel_Restrepo) as vice president.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] The ceremony was held in the Auditorium of the [Santiago de Cali University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_de_Cali_University) in [Cali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cali), Valle del Cauca.&#x20;

This was the third presidential inauguration held outside the [National Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Capitol_of_Colombia) in [Bogotá, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogot%C3%A1,_D.C.). The last time was the presidential inauguration of [Manuel Antonio Sanclemente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Antonio_Sanclemente) in 1898 in [Villeta, Cundinamarca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villeta,_Cundinamarca).[^cite_note-4]

## Context

The ceremony marked the end of the presidential transition period for [Abelardo de la Espriella](81665680.md), who became president-elect after defeating the candidate of the Historic Pact, [Iván Cepeda](66799376.md), on June 21, 2026.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The victory of De La Espriella and his running mate, [José Manuel Restrepo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Manuel_Restrepo), was confirmed by the [National Electoral Council](13500564.md), which later issued the official certification on June 25, 2026, formalizing the election.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

The election saw the highest voter turnout in the history of Colombian presidential elections. De La Espriella will become the second president from [Córdoba](549670.md), after [Gustavo Petro](13391962.md) (his predecessor). [José Manuel Restrepo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Manuel_Restrepo) becomes the second Rector of the Universidad del Rosario to become vice president; the last was [José Manuel Marroquín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Manuel_Marroqu%C3%ADn) (1898-1900).

## Planning

Following De La Espriella's election as president in the [2026 presidential election](79603998.md), rumors began circulating about the location of his [presidential inauguration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_presidential_inauguration). During his presidential campaign, he had announced that it would take place at a [garrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garrison) in [Popayán, Cauca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popay%C3%A1n,_Cauca).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] This was later confirmed by De La Espriella himself but subsequently rejected by President [Gustavo Petro](13391962.md). Constitutional experts argued that it was erroneous and even [unconstitutional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unconstitutional) for De La Espriella to be sworn in at a military garrison.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] Senator [Agmeth Escaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agmeth_Escaf) explained, The President of the Republic is sworn in before Congress, not before an armed force, not by a rank; he is sworn in as a civilian to a government position.&#x20;

On July 22, the [Senate](645242.md), and subsequently the [Chamber of Representatives](645347.md) on July 28, approved that his inauguration would be held in Popayán, Cauca, without specifying a garrison. President Gustavo Petro subsequently issued strict orders to the [Military Forces of Colombia](5843.md) not to lend any military installations for the inauguration.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] Petro would later state, The president obeys the people, not the military. On Wednesday, July 29, [Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_Colombia) unanimously ratified the decision to move the inauguration to Cali.[^cite_note-21] The vote was 58 in favor and 30 against.

Subsequently, the inauguration ceremony would be moved to the [Arena USC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_USC?action=edit\&redlink=1) of the [Santiago de Cali University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_de_Cali_University) in [Cali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cali), [Cauca Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauca_Valley).[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

### Oaths of office

Senate President [Honorio Henríquez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorio_Henr%C3%ADquez) administered the [presidential oath of office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oath_of_office) to President [Abelardo de la Espriella](81665680.md). It was the first time the president received a [military baton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baton_\(law_enforcement\)). This is neither regulated nor a tradition or obligation; presidents do not have a baton to represent their command as [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the armed forces. President Abelardo de la Espriella then administered the vicepresidential oath of office to Vice President [José Manuel Restrepo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Manuel_Restrepo).

## Boycott

Several members of the [Historic Pact](70336228.md) Party at the [10th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10th_Congress_of_Colombia) decided to boycott the inauguration.[^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58] This boycott was perceived as an initial show of opposition to the incoming administration and its policies.[^cite_note-59]

Several reasons were given for the boycott, including the increased cost of the inauguration, which cost twice as much as the previous [presidential inauguration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inauguration_of_Gustavo_Petro).
