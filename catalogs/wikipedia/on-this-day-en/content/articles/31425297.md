The following is a **list of speakers of the Parliament of [Somalia](27358.md)**. There have been 12 official [speakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_\(politics\)) of the Parliament since the office was created in 1956 .[^cite_note-1] The first Speaker of the Parliament was [Aden Abdulle Osman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aden_Abdullah_Osman_Daar), who served prior to independence in the [Trust Territory of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trust_Territory_of_Somalia), Succeeded by [Hagi Bashir Ismail Yusuf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagi_Bashir_Ismail_Yusuf) on July 1, 1960, Somali National Assembly. The Speakership is currently vacant after the latest speaker, [Aden Madobe](9322839.md), became the [President of the South West State](65910790.md).[^cite_note-2]

## Acting President

In the event of a temporary absence of the [President of Somalia](516101.md) on account of illness, travel abroad or similar circumstances, the speaker of the parliament serves as acting president, and exercises the powers of the state president until the president resumes his functions, and in the event that the presidency falls vacant as a result of death or resignation or for any other reason, until the election of a new president.
