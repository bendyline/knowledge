**Kaura** ([Tyap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyap_language): *Watyap*) is a town and a [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [southern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Kaduna) [Kaduna State](2227637.md), [Nigeria](21383.md). The Local Government Council is chaired by Matthias Siman.[^cite_note-2] Other towns include: Takad (Attakar) [Manchok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchok) and [Kagoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kagoro). It has an area of 461 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 174,626 at the 2006 census.[^cite_note-3] The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 801.[^cite_note-4]

## Boundaries

Kaura (*Watyap*) [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area) (LGA) shares boundaries with [Zangon Kataf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zangon_Kataf) LGA to the west, [Kauru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kauru) LGA to the north, [Jema'a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jema'a) LGA to the south; and [Riyom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riyom) LGA of [Plateau State](1723458.md) to the east, respectively.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Administrative subdivisions

Kaura [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area) consists of 10 subdivisions (second-order administrative divisions) or electoral wards, namely:[^cite_note-7]

3. Fada (Ucyio)

6. Kpak

## Population

Kaura [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area) according to the March 21, 2006 national population census was put at 174,626. Its population was projected by the [*National Population Commission of Nigeria and National Bureau of Statistics*](https://citypopulation.de/php/nigeria-admin.php?adm1id=NGA019,) to be 235,700 by March 21, 2016.

## Climate

The rainy season, which is warm and humid, and the dry season, which is hot and partially cloudy, have different climates throughout the year.[^cite_note-\:0-8]

### Average Temperature

The average daily maximum temperature during the 2.7-month hot season, which runs from January 29 to April 18, is above 33 °C (91 °F). March is the hottest month in Kaura, with an average high temperature of 34 °C (94 °F) and low temperature of 19 °C (67 °F). The average daily high temperature during the 3.3-month chilly season, which runs from June 28 to October 4, is below 27 °C (81 °F). December is the coldest month of the year in Kaura, with an average high temperature of 31 °C (87 °F) and low temperature of 14 °C (58 °F).[^cite_note-\:0-8]

### Geography

Kaura Local Government Area has an average temperature of 32 degrees Celsius or 89.6 degrees Fahrenheit and a total area of 485 square kilometres. The dry and wet seasons are the two distinct seasons that the area experiences. Kaura's average wind speed is believed to be 11 km/h (6.8 mph), and the area receives 1,000 millimetres (39 inches) of precipitation year.[^cite_note-9]

## People

The people of Kaura [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area) are homogenous, belonging to the larger [Atyap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atyap_people) (Nienzit) Ethno-Linguistic Cluster. These people include the A̱sholyia̱ (also spelt *Osholio*, *Æsholio*, *A̠sholyio*), 'Moro'a' in [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language) language, A̱gworok (also spelt *Œgworok*), also known as 'Kagoro' in [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language) language, Takad as well as the A̱tyeca̱rak also known as 'Kachechere' in [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language) language, and [Atyap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atyap) Proper, also known as 'Kataf' in [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language) language.

## Languages

The people of Kaura [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area) speak five inter-related dialectical varieties of the [Tyap language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyap_language), namely: Sholyia̱ (also spelt Sholio), Gworok, Takad (also spelt *Takat*), Tyeca̱rak and [Tyap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyap) Proper. Kaura Local government was carved out from Jema'a local government area by the past [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military) government of [Kaduna state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_state) and it shares boundary with [Plateau](538005.md) state, blessed with beautiful [mountainous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountainous) environment and good weather.

## Religion

The people are mostly [Christians](18337522.md) (above 95%) and a very minute percent are [Abwoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abwoi_religion) adherents and [Muslims](19541.md).

## Traditional States

There are three [Nigerian traditional states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_traditional_states) headed by three *Agwams* (monarchs):

1. The Gworok (Kagoro) Chiefdom, headed by [Agwam Ufuwai Bonet (OON)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ufuwai_Bonet), Agwam Agworok (also Oegwam Oegworok).

2. The Asholyio (Moroa) Chiefdom, headed by [A̠gwam Tagwai Sambo (OFR)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagwai_Sambo), Agwam Asholyio.

3. The Takad Chiefdom, headed by Agwam Tobias Nkom Wada, Agwam Takad.
