**Zarqa Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة الزرقاء, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Muħāfazat az-Zarqāʔ*, local dialects *ez-Zergā* or *ez-Zer'a*) is the third largest governorate in [Jordan](7515964.md) by population. The capital of Zarqa governorate is [Zarqa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarqa) City, which is the largest city in the governorate. It is located 25 kilometres (16 mi) east of the Jordanian capital [Amman](45007.md). The second largest city in the governorate is [Russeifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russeifa).

Zarqa Governorate hosts the largest military and air bases of the Jordanian armed forces.

## History



[Nabatean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabatean) Palace [Qasr al Hallabat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qasr_al_Hallabat)

The land of Zarqa Governorate has been inhabited since the [Bronze Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Age), most prominent were the [Ammonite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammon) kingdom and the [Nabateans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabateans), who constructed the fort known as [Qasr al Hallabat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qasr_al_Hallabat), which then was used as a fort by the Romans, and then as a desert palace by the [Umayyads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umayyad_Caliphate).

The most significant historical remains are the Umayyad desert palaces, such as [Qasr Amra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qasr_Amra), a [World Heritage Site](44940.md), [Qasr al Hallabat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qasr_al_Hallabat), Qasr Shabib in the center of the city of Zarqa, as well as the Castle of Azraq.

After the construction of the [Hedjaz railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedjaz_railway) by the Ottoman Turks at the beginning of the 1900s, Zarqa became a strategically important hub that connects [Damascus](8914.md) to [Medina](36636.md), the cities along the railway started flourishing. Later, Jordan's [Arab Legion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Legion) led by [Glubb Pasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glubb_Pasha) had its main bases in Zarqa.

## Geography



The wetlands in [Azraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azraq)

Zarqa Governorate borders [Mafraq Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mafraq_Governorate) from the north and north east, [Amman Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amman_Governorate) from the south and south west, [Jerash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerash_Governorate) and [Balqa Governorates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balqa_Governorate) from the west. It also shares an international border with [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) in its south eastern edge.



[Qasr Amra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qasr_Amra), A World Heritage Site

Most of the area covered by the governorate constitutes part of the Syrian desert plateau. the heavily populated western regions of the governorate constitute part of the [Zarqa River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarqa_River) basin. The two cities of [Zarqa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarqa) and [Russeifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russeifa) are the second and fourth largest cities in Jordan.

## Demographics

The census of 2004 shows that the population of Zarqa Governorate for that year was 764,650 of whom 94.5% is considered urban population and 5.5% is a rural population. Jordanian citizens made up 97% of the population. The female to male ratio was 46% to 54%. The Jordanian Department of Statistics population estimate for the year 2010 is 910,800 with a female to male ratio of 48.25 to 51.75 and a population density of 191.3 persons per Km<sup>2</sup>

## Economy



A Jordanian F-16 in the [Muwaffaq Salti Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaheed_Mwaffaq_Air_Base) in [Azraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azraq)

Due to its close location to the populated cities in Jordan, Zarqa Governorate has the largest number of factories in Jordan, [Jordan's only oil refinery plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Petroleum_Refinery_Company) is located in Zarqa.

[Muwaffaq Salti Air Base](32592197.md), the largest in the country, is located in [Azraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azraq). Zarqa Governorate hosts three universities, [Hashemite University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hashemite_University),[^cite_note-6] [Al-Balqa\` Applied University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Balqa%60_Applied_University)-Zarqa College,[^cite_note-7] and [Zarqa Private University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarqa_Private_University).
