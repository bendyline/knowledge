The **Ministry of Defence and Armed Forces Logistics** (**MODAFL**; [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): وزارت دفاع و پشتیبانی نیروهای مسلح, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *vezârat-e defa' va poštibâni-ye niruhâ-ye mosallah*) is the [defence ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_ministry) of [Iran](14653.md) and part of the country's [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Iran). It thus reports to the [President of Iran](233913.md), not to the [Commander-in-Chief of the Iranian Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-Chief_of_the_Iranian_Armed_Forces).

Unlike many countries, the ministry is not involved with in-the-field military operational command of the armed forces. Instead it is responsible for planning, logistics and funding of the [Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran](14305018.md) while the [General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran](50548383.md), a separate institution under command of the [supreme leader of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_leader_of_Iran), has control over the forces.[^cite_note-2] The MODAFL is also the major player in the [defence industry of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_industry_of_Iran), with multiple conglomerates and subordinates active in [research and development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_and_development), [maintenance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maintenance_\(technical\)) and manufacturing of military equipment. It annually exports [military equipment manufactured in Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_military_equipment_manufactured_in_Iran) to forces of countries such as [Syria](57134800.md) ([until 2024](78548066.md)), [Iraq](7515928.md), [Venezuela](32374.md), and [Sudan](27421.md) (the latter ceased in 2019), as well as non-state actors like [Hezbollah](13919.md) in Lebanon.[^cite_note-3]

The ministry is considered one of the three "sovereign" ministerial bodies of Iran due to nature of its work at home and abroad.[^cite_note-4]

## History



Emblem of the Former Ministry of War

From the mid-1940s, U.S. Army and later Air Force officers of the Army Mission Iran and later [Military Assistance Advisory Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Assistance_Advisory_Group) Iran (ARMISH-MAAG) were closely involved in providing a range of advice, support, and reorganizational assistance.

### 1952–53: Reforms under Mossadegh

When [Mohammad Mossadegh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Mossadegh) took over the ministry on 21 July 1952, he initiated a series of reforms in the ministry. He named General [Ahmad Vossough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_Vossough?action=edit\&redlink=1) as his deputy and renamed the ministry from 'War' to 'National Defence', cut the [military budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_budget) by 15%, and vowed to only purchase defensive military equipment.[^cite_note-Abrahamian-5] Two investigatory commissions were formed, one for examining previous [promotions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_\(rank\)) and the other for materiel procurement.[^cite_note-Abrahamian-5] Under Mossadegh, some 15,000 personnel were transferred from the army to the [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Gendarmerie) and 136 officers, including 15 general officers, were purged.[^cite_note-Abrahamian-5]

### 1982–89: Two ministries

See also: [Ministry of Revolutionary Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Revolutionary_Guards)

The [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) had between 1982 and 1989 its own dedicated defence ministry, mirroring the existing ministry of defence which solely supplied the [Islamic Republic of Iran Army](48259216.md) during this period. Under President [Akbar Hashemi Rafsanjani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akbar_Hashemi_Rafsanjani) in 1989, the two ministries were merged into one in order to cease parallel work and reduce [interservice rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interservice_rivalry).

## Subordinates

See also: [Defense industry of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_industry_of_Iran)

Iranian military industry, under the command of the Ministry of Defence, is composed of the following main components:[^cite_note-IW-6]

| Organization | Field of activity |
| --- | --- |
| [Iran Electronics Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_Electronics_Industries) (SAIRAN) | Electronics, communications, e-warfare, radars, satellites, etc. |
| [Defence Industries Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_Industries_Organization) (SASAD) | Tanks, rockets, bombs, guns, armored vehicles, etc. |
| [Aerospace Industries Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_Industries_Organization) (AIO) | Guided missiles systems, etc. |
| [Aviation Industries Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_Aviation_Industries_Organization) (IAIO) | Aircraft, UAV, helicopters, etc. |
| [Marine Industries Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_Industries_Organization) (MIO) | Ships, hovercraft, submarines, etc. |
| [Organization of Defensive Innovation and Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization_of_Defensive_Innovation_and_Research) (SPND) | Defence related research and development. |
| [National Geographical Organization of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Geographical_Organization_of_Iran) (NGO) | Matters related to military maps, national borders and geographical services required by the Armed Forces. |
| [Malek-Ashtar University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malek-Ashtar_University_of_Technology) (MUT) | The ministry's educational institution |

In August 2018, the Iranian Ministry of Defence declared it had offloaded its shares in [Wagon Pars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagon_Pars) and [Iran Airtour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_Airtour).[^cite_note-7] In November 2020, the head of the Research and Innovation Organisation of the defence ministry, the nuclear physicist [Mohsen Fakhrizadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohsen_Fakhrizadeh), was assassinated in an ambush near Tehran.[^cite_note-8]

### Aerospace Industries Organization

**Aerospace Industries Organization** (**AIO**, [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): سازمان صنایع هوافضا) is a subordinate of the Iranian defence ministry and a leading high-tech complex headquartered in [Tehran](57654.md).[^cite_note-Tarikhi-9] It is a key player in development and production of space assets of Iran, and has some research center and factory under its control.[^cite_note-Tarikhi-9] [Shahab ballistic missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahab_\(missile\)) are one of notable products manufactured by the organization.[^cite_note-Tarikhi-9]
