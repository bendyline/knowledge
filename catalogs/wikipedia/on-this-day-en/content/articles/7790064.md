The **Chief of the Army Staff** (**COAS**) is a statutory position in the [Pakistan Army](1965925.md) held by its professional head, which is usually a [four-star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-star_rank) [general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_\(Pakistan\)), appointed by the [President](419460.md) on the [Prime Minister](461896.md)'s nomination. Since 2025, the COAS serves *[ex officio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex_officio_member)* as the [Chief of Defence Forces](81566574.md) (CDF), the head of the [Pakistan Armed Forces](23369.md). As the highest-ranking officer, it is the most powerful position in the land army; and due to the [influence of the military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Establishment_\(Pakistan\)), the position is seen as the most powerful office in the country.[^cite_note-7]

Until 2025, the chief served as a member of the [Joint Chiefs of Staff Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Chiefs_of_Staff_Committee,_Pakistan), usually consulting the chiefs of the [aerial](377433.md) and [naval branches](1183789.md) to act as a [military adviser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_adviser) to the Prime Minister and the [federal government](1897833.md) in the line of defending the [land borders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Template\:Borders_of_Pakistan) of the country.[^cite_note-PakDef_Military_Consortium-8] The Chief of the Army Staff exercises responsibility of [command and control](7092305.md) of the operational, combatant, logistics, and training commands within the army.[^cite_note-PakDef_Military_Consortium-8]

The appointment, in principle, is constitutionally subjected to be for 5 years after the approval by the President on the recommendations of the Prime Minister.[^cite_note-Tenure-9] The COAS is based in the [General Headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Headquarters_\(Pakistan_Army\)) within the [Rawalpindi Cantonment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawalpindi_Cantonment) in [Rawalpindi, Punjab](343524.md). The incumbent COAS is [Asim Munir](58712552.md), serving in this capacity since 29 November 2022.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

## Office of the Chief of the Army Staff

The designation of the Chief of the Army Staff was created from the previous title [Commander-in-Chief of the Pakistan Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-Chief_of_the_Pakistan_Army) in 1972. The [Prime Minister](461896.md) approves the nomination and appointment of the Chief of Army Staff, with [President](419460.md) confirming the Prime Minister's appointed choosing and nomination.[^cite_note-12]

The army leadership is based in the [GHQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GHQ_\(Pakistan_Army\)) whose functions are supervised by the Chief of Army Staff, assisted by the civilians from the Army Secretariat of the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(Pakistan\)) (MoD).[^cite_note-Ministry_of_Defence_Press-13] The Chief of Army Staff exercises the responsibility of complete operational, training and logistics commands.<sup>: 131 </sup>[^cite_note-Lulu.com_INC-14]

Typically, the Chief of Army Staff is a four-star full general. [Asim Munir](58712552.md) is the first army chief to have been promoted to the five-star field marshal rank.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

There are several principle [staff officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staff_Officer) (PSO) who assist in running the operations of the Army GHQ:

- [Chief of General Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_General_Staff_\(Pakistan\))

- [Chief of Logistics Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_serving_Generals_of_the_Pakistan_Army)

- [Inspector-General of Training and Evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_serving_Generals_of_the_Pakistan_Army) (IGT\&E)

- [Inspector-General Communications and IT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_serving_Generals_of_the_Pakistan_Army) (IGC\&IT)

- [Inspector-General Arms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_serving_Generals_of_the_Pakistan_Army) (IG Arms)

- [Engineer-in-Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer-in-Chief_\(Pakistan_Army\)) (E-in-C)

- [Military Secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Secretary_\(Pakistan\)) (Mil Secy)

- [Adjutant-General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjutant_general#Pakistan)

- [Quartermaster General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartermaster_general) (QMG)
