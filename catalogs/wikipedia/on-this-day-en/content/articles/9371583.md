**Law enforcement in Pakistan** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): پاکستان میں نفاذِ قانون) is one of the three main components of the [criminal justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Penal_Code) system of [Pakistan](23235.md), alongside the [judiciary](4988685.md) and the [prisons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category\:Prisons_in_Pakistan).[^cite_note-usip-1] The country has a mix of federal, provincial and territorial police forces with both general and specialised functions, but the senior ranks of all the provincial forces and most of the federal ones are manned by members of the Police Service of Pakistan (PSP). The PSP is one of the most prestigious part of the [Central Superior Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Superior_Services), Pakistan's main civil service organisation.[^cite_note-usip-1][^cite_note-2] Federal law enforcement agencies are generally overseen by the [Ministry of Interior](26905116.md) of the [Government of Pakistan](1897833.md), while provincial police forces are overseen by a department of the government of that province.

## Provincial and territorial police



An officer of [Elite Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elite_Police) of the [Punjab Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Police_\(Pakistan\))

The four [provinces of Pakistan](446017.md) ([Punjab](24751.md), [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md), [Sindh](28972.md) and [Balochistan](4687.md)) each have their own police force, organised to suit the challenges of that locality, with their own specialised and elite units. Each police force has a [Commissioner of Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commissioner_of_Police) appointed as [Inspector-General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector_General_of_Police) who is a senior officer from the Police Service of Pakistan.[^cite_note-3] Some provincial police forces are routinely supported by [federal paramilitary units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Armed_Forces) operating in that area. All provincial police forces contain [Counter Terrorism Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter_Terrorism_Department_\(Pakistan\)) and [Special Branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Branch_\(Pakistan\)).&#x20;

The traditional uniform of Pakistani provincial police officers is a black shirt with tan trousers. In 2017, police in Punjab transitioned to an olive green uniform, but reverted to the traditional uniform in 2019.[^cite_note-4] In 2020, all provinces decided to adopt the uniform worn in Islamabad - light blue or white shirts with dark blue trousers.[^cite_note-5]

The Police Service of Pakistan (PSP) replaced the [Indian Imperial Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Imperial_Police) in 1948, a year after Pakistan became [independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_\(Pakistan\)) from the [British Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj). The service commands and provides leadership to federal, provincial, and territorial police forces. Its officers are assigned to different districts, provinces and stations across Pakistan. Many of the country's highest-profile law enforcement posts are held by PSP officers, including Inspectors General of provinces, Director Generals of the [Intelligence Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Bureau_\(Pakistan\)) and [Federal Investigation Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Investigation_Agency), and superintendents of the [Federal Constabulary](25964521.md) and [National Highways and Motorway Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highways_&_Motorway_Police). Officers are recruited through an extremely competitive examination held once a year by the [Federal Public Service Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Public_Service_Commission). Those selected then must undergo a six month training programme known as CTP at the [Civil Services Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Services_Academy_Lahore) (CSA) in [Lahore](125315.md), and a further 18 months of specialised training occurs at the [National Police Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Police_Academy_of_Pakistan) Islamabad.&#x20;

Primarily operated through the [four provincial governments](446017.md) and the [Islamabad Capital Territory](15294.md),[^cite_note-17] each police service has a jurisdiction extending only within the relevant province or territory.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

The law enforcement agencies are also involved in providing first response to emergencies and other threats to [public safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_safety) as well as [protecting the infrastructure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rescue_operation) and maintaining order in the country. Apart from investigating crime scenes, criminal acts, suspected unlawful activities, and detention of suspected criminals pending judicial action, the law enforcement agencies (primarily police) also perform duties that include the service and enforcement of warrants, writs, and other orders of the courts.
