**Prince Hassan Air Base** ([ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **OJPH**;[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): قاعدة الأمير حسن الجوية; formerly **H-5**) is a [Royal Jordanian Air Force](377729.md) base, located near the town of [Safawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safawi,_Jordan), [Mafraq Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mafraq_Governorate), Jordan, 72.4 miles (100 km) east-northeast of the country's capital [Amman](45007.md).

The airfield was established as a landing strip associated with the [Kirkuk–Haifa oil pipeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirkuk%E2%80%93Haifa_oil_pipeline)'s H-5 pumping station, being used by [Royal Air Force](25679.md) and [Iraq Petroleum Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq_Petroleum_Company) mail aircraft operating between [Baghdad](4492.md), [Amman](45007.md), and [Cairo](6293.md).[^cite_note-rjaf-4]

In 1969, the airfield was opened as a military base, being named after [Prince Hassan bin Talal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Hassan_bin_Talal), then [Crown Prince of Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Prince_of_Jordan).[^cite_note-rjaf-4] [No. 9 Squadron RJAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No._9_Squadron_RJAF) was established at the base, operating [Lockheed F-104A/B Starfighters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_F-104_Starfighter).[^cite_note-rjaf-4]

In 1994, the Fighter Weapons Instructor School was moved to the base.[^cite_note-rjaf-4]

No. 6 Fighter Reconnaissance Squadron RJAF has been based at the airfield in the past,[^cite_note-rjaf-4] but has since moved to [Muwaffaq Salti Air Base](32592197.md).

## Current use

As of 2015, [No. 17 Squadron RJAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No._17_Squadron_RJAF) with [Northrop F-5E/F Tiger IIs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_F-5) was stationed at the base.[^cite_note-5]

The [United States Air Force](32090.md) has used the base occasionally since the 1980s, with a 2017 report noting that U.S. Air Force [C-17s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-17) are transiting the airfield regularly.[^cite_note-6]

The [French Air and Space Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Air_and_Space_Force) has been using Prince Hassan Air Base as a base of operation since 2014, deploying six [Mirage 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirage_2000) fighter jets there, which were replaced in 2017 by four [Rafales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafale).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
