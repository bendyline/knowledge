**Rachel Szor** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): רחל שור) is an Israeli creator, [investigative journalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investigative_journalism), film director, editor, [cinematographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinematographer), and activist. She rose to international prominence when she co-directed *[No Other Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Other_Land)* (2024), an [Oscar-winning documentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Documentary_Feature_Film#2020s) about the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) and [settler violence in the West Bank](47246246.md).

Her second documentary feature, *[NAZA](84051935.md)* (2026), follows the Israeli Government involvement in the [mass killing of Palestinians in Gaza](75668427.md), and won the [Special Jury Prize](46786001.md) at the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md).

## Biography

Born in [Tel Aviv](31453.md), Szor attended the [Thelma Yellin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thelma_Yellin_High_School_for_the_Arts) High School of the Arts. In 2017, she participated in a four-month program at the [Arava Institute for Environmental Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arava_Institute_for_Environmental_Studies).

## Career

### *No Other Land*

Main article: [No Other Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Other_Land)

Szor co-directed, co-filmed and featured in the documentary *[No Other Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Other_Land)* (2024) with [Basel Adra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basel_Adra), [Yuval Abraham](76256808.md), and Hamdan Ballal, about Adra's long-term attempts to resist [Israeli settler violence](47246246.md) and displacement from his home in [Masafer Yatta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masafer_Yatta). The group had no experience in documentary filmmaking and initially approached the story as activists and journalists.[^cite_note-ce_feb_24-1] Working together for over five years, with Adra and Ballal having footage including home videos from Adra's family, the documentary depicts Adra befriending Abraham and juxtaposes Adra's life under [occupation](2781576.md) with Abraham's life of freedom.[^cite_note-variety_feb_24-2][^cite_note-3] *No Other Land* opened in February 2024 to critical acclaim at the [74th Berlin International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_Berlin_International_Film_Festival) (Berlinale), where it won two awards: The Panorama Audience Award for Best Documentary Film[^cite_note-4] and the Berlinale Documentary Film Award.[^cite_note-5]

### *NAZA*

Main article: [NAZA (film)](84051935.md)

Szor co-wrote, co-directed, co-filmed and featured in the documentary *[NAZA](84051935.md)* (2026) with [Yuval Abraham](76256808.md). It was shot in [Tel Aviv](31453.md) during the ongoing [Gaza war](74998519.md), and reveals the [Israeli Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Government)’s role in the [mass killing of Palestinian civilians](75668427.md) during the conflict.[^cite_note-\:0-6] Produced by *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* and British producer [James Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Wilson_\(producer\))'s JW Films, the film was selected for the main competition of the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md),[^cite_note-\:0-6] where it won the [Special Jury Prize](46786001.md).[^cite_note-7]

On 14 September 2026, shortly after the Venice awards ceremony, *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* reported that Israeli culture minister, [Miki Zohar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miki_Zohar), had used the social media platform [X](9988187.md) to declare that he would "act immediately to revoke the Israeli citizenship of these despicable creators \[Abraham and Szor] for treason against the state".[^cite_note-8] [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md), the [Prime Minister of Israel](24898.md), subsequently announced that his government would seek to pass legislation to revoke the citizenship of Israelis who "defame" Israeli soldiers abroad, citing Abraham and Szor in the statement.[^cite_note-9]
