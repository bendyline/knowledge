The **Chief of Army** ([Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language): *Panglima Tentera Darat — PTD*, [Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_alphabet): ڤڠليما تنترا دارت) is the most senior appointment in the [Malaysian Army](1966311.md) and has been held by a [four-star officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-star_officer) in the rank of [General](148537.md) since 1977. The Chief of Army is a member of the [Malaysian Armed Forces Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysian_Armed_Forces_Council) and directly reports to the [Chief of the Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Defence_Forces_\(Malaysia\)).

The current Chief of Army ([Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language): *Panglima Tentera Darat — PTD*) is General [Datuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#Datuk) [Azhan Md Othman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azhan_Md_Othman) , who succeeded General [Datuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#Datuk) [Muhammad Hafizuddeain bin Jantan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Hafizuddeain_bin_Jantan) .[^cite_note-Panglima-1]
