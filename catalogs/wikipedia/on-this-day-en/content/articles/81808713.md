# 2025 Fez buildings collapse

| Date | 9 December 2025 |
| --- | --- |
| Location | [Fez](39760328.md), [Morocco](19291.md) |
| Deaths | 22 |
| Injuries | 16 |

On 9 December 2025, after 23\:00[^cite_note-national-1] two buildings collapsed in [Fez](39760328.md), [Morocco](19291.md), killing 22 people and injuring 16 others.[^cite_note-2]

The buildings were two adjacent four-storey residential buildings in the Al Mustaqbal neighbourhood. At the time of the disaster, there was a [Aqiqah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aqiqah), a family newborn celebration, in one of the buildings, with the other being unoccupied. A search and rescue operation was started to recover other people possibly trapped under the debris.[^cite_note-national-1][^cite_note-3]

Residents were evacuated from the disaster area. The injured were brought to the University Hospital Centre.[^cite_note-national-1]

An investigation has been started into the causes of the accident.[^cite_note-national-1] According to the Moroccan news outlet *[Hespress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hespress)*, the collapse may have been caused by illegally constructed additional floors on the buildings. A source reported that the owners had received a permit for only two floors, but allegedly violated the applicable building regulations.[^cite_note-4]
