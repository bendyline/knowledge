The **minister of foreign affairs of Hungary** ([Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_language): *Magyarország külügyminisztere*) is a member of the [Government of Hungary](17048864.md) and the head of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The current foreign minister is [Anita Orbán](82941631.md).

The position was called **People's Commissar of Foreign Affairs** ([Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_language): *külügyi népbiztos*) during the [Hungarian Soviet Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Soviet_Republic) in 1919[^cite_note-1] and **Minister besides the King** ([Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_language): *a király személye körüli miniszter*) between 1848 and 1918,[^cite_note-2] except in 1849 when Hungary [declared its independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Declaration_of_Independence) from the [Austrian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Empire). During the existence of [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) (1867–1918) the two countries also had a [joint Minister of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Ministry_of_Austria-Hungary).
