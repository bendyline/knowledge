[Demographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demography) features of the [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_\(human_biology\)) of the [Republic of the Congo](19599929.md) include [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density), [ethnicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_group), education level, health of the populace, economic status, [ religious affiliations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion_in_the_Republic_of_the_Congo) and other aspects of the population.

The Republic of the Congo's sparse population is concentrated in the southwestern portion of the country, leaving the vast areas of tropical [jungle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jungle) in the north virtually uninhabited. Thus, Congo is one of the most urbanized countries in Africa, with 85% of its total population living in a few urban areas, namely in [Brazzaville](52710.md), [Pointe-Noire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointe-Noire), or one of the small cities or villages lining the 534-kilometre (332 mi) [railway which connects the two cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congo-Ocean_Railway). In rural areas, industrial and commercial activity has declined rapidly in recent years, leaving rural economies dependent on the government for support and subsistence. Before the [1997 civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_the_Congo_Civil_War_\(1997%E2%80%931999\)), about 15,000 Europeans and other non-Africans lived in Congo, most of whom were French. Presently, only about 9,500 remain. [Pygmies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pygmies) make up 2% of Congo's population.[^cite_note-1]

## Population



Population of the Republic of the Congo, Data of [FAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAO), 2005

The proportion of children below the age of 15 in 2010 was 40.6%, 55.7% was between 15 and 65 years of age, while 3.7% was 65 years or older.[^cite_note-WPP_2010-4]

## Languages



[Kikongo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kongo_language)/[Kituba language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kituba_language) area



[Lingala language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingala_language) area

Languages spoken here include [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language) (official), [Kituba or Monokutuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kituba_language) ([lingua franca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingua_franca)), [Kongo or Kikongo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kongo_language), [Lingala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingala_language), and many local languages and dialects.

The majority of the population is concentrated along the railroad between [Pointe-Noire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointe-Noire) and [Brazzaville](52710.md) in the south where Kituba (a creole language based on Kikongo) is the primary language. Lingala is influential in the sparsely inhabited northern half of the country.

## Health

Life expectancy for the population was estimated at 54.91 years in 2011: 53.62 for males and 56.25 for females. The adult prevalence rate for [HIV](14170.md)/[AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDS) was 3.4% as of 2009, representing 77,000 people living with the disease and 5,100 deaths. The Republic of the Congo is considered to have a high degree of risk of infectious diseases, particularly bacterial [diarrhea](53951.md), [hepatitis A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepatitis_A) and [typhoid fever](31596.md) (all food- or waterborne), and [malaria](20423.md) (vectorborne).
