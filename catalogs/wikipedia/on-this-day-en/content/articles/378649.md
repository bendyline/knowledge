The **president of Chad** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): رئيس جمهورية تشاد, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *rayiys jumhuriat tshad*, [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Président de la République du Tchad*) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of [Chad](5488.md), and the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Chadian National Army](5336.md). The presidency is the highest [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) position in the country. The president is [directly elected](652563.md) for a double five-year term.

A total of seven people have served as head of state of Chad (not counting two Interim Heads of State). Additionally, one person, [Goukouni Oueddei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goukouni_Oueddei), has served on two non-consecutive occasions.

The current head of state is President [Mahamat Déby](39073394.md), who was inaugurated on 23 May 2024 following the [2024 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Chadian_presidential_election).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Prior to that, Déby has served as [transitional president](598010.md) from 2022,[^cite_note-The_East_African-4] and Chad's *de facto* head of state as president of the [Transitional Military Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Military_Council_\(Chad\)) from 2021, after his father, the previous president [Idriss Déby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idriss_D%C3%A9by), was killed in the [Northern Chad offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Northern_Chad_offensive).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Term limits

In 2018, the new [Constitution of Chad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Chad) reinstated two-term limits for the president. It also changed the length of the term from five years to six years.[^cite_note-termlimits-7] However, in 2023, a [constitutional referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Chadian_constitutional_referendum) was held which changed the length of the term from six years back to five years and which also lowered the minimum age for the presidency from 40 down to 35.[^cite_note-8]
