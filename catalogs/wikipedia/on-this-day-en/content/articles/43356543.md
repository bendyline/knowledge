**Anadolu Agency** ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *Anadolu Ajansı*, lit. 'Anatolia Agency'; abbreviated **AA**) is a [state-run](20803507.md)[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] [news agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_agency) headquartered in [Ankara](802.md), [Turkey](11125639.md).[^cite_note-3] It operates in 13 languages in over 100 countries.[^cite_note-4]

## History

The Anadolu Agency was founded in 1920 during the [Turkish War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_War_of_Independence) by the order of [Mustafa Kemal Pasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_Kemal_Pasha). As the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md)'s capital, [Constantinople](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantinople), was under the [Ottoman sultans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_sultan)' control, all newspapers were also under the sultan's rule along with [British occupiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Istanbul), and it was necessary for the [revolutionary government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Grand_National_Assembly) to establish a communication and news network for [Anatolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatolia) and [Rumelia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumelia).[^cite_note-5] Journalist [Yunus Nadi Abalıoğlu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yunus_Nadi_Abal%C4%B1o%C4%9Flu) and writer [Halide Edip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halide_Edip), fleeing the [occupied capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Istanbul), met in [Geyve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geyve) and concluded that a new Turkish press agency was needed. The agency was officially launched on April 6, 1920, 17 days before the [Turkish Grand National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Grand_National_Assembly) convened for the first time. It announced the first legislation passed by the Assembly, which established the Republic of Turkey.[^cite_note-6] In 1925, an act of parliament declared it an autonomous agency, but it continued to serve as a "quasi-governmental entity".[^cite_note-7]

After the [Justice and Development Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_and_Development_Party_\(Turkey\)) (AKP) took power, AA and the [Turkish Radio and Television Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Radio_and_Television_Corporation) (TRT) were both restructured to more closely reflect the administration line. According to a 2016 academic article, "these public news producers, especially during the most recent term of the AKP government, have been controlled by officials from a small network close to the party leadership."[^cite_note-8]

According to the former general director of AA, [Kemal Öztürk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kemal_%C3%96zt%C3%BCrk?action=edit\&redlink=1), it has the largest broadcaster of Arabic news in the Middle East and produces 70 percent of all news content in Turkey.[^cite_note-9] Political scholar Oğuz Güner says that "AA conveys the vision of Turkish foreign policy, Türkiye's viewpoint towards the world, and Turkish culture and history to the international community."[^cite_note-10]

Anadolu Agency is an active member of the [European Alliance of News Agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Alliance_of_News_Agencies) (EANA).[^cite_note-11] Since 1978, AA has been a member of the [Organization of Asia-Pacific News Agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization_of_Asia-Pacific_News_Agencies) (OANA).[^cite_note-12]
