The **United States deputy attorney general** is the second-highest-ranking official in the [United States Department of Justice](52563.md) and oversees the day-to-day operation of the department. The deputy attorney general acts as [attorney general](44002.md) during the absence of the attorney general.&#x20;

The deputy attorney general is a [political appointee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_appointments_in_the_United_States) of the [president of the United States](24113.md) and takes office after confirmation by the [United States Senate](24909346.md). The position was created in 1950.[^cite_note-1]
