On 3 November 2025, at 00\:59 [AFT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Afghanistan) (20\:29 UTC, 2 November), an earthquake with a magnitude of 6.2 on the [moment magnitude scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_magnitude_scale) (M<sub>w</sub>) struck northern [Afghanistan](737.md).[^cite_note-anss-1] Around 31 people were killed, 1,172 were injured and many homes collapsed in [Balkh](457218.md) and [Samangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samangan_Province) Provinces.

## Tectonic setting

Much of Afghanistan is situated in a broad zone of [continental deformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intraplate_deformation) within the [Eurasian Plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasian_Plate). [Seismic activity in Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_earthquakes_in_Afghanistan) is influenced by the [subduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subduction) of the [Arabian Plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabian_Plate) to the west and the [oblique subduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblique_subduction) of the [Indian Plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Plate) in the east. The subduction rate of the Indian Plate along the [continental convergent boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_Himalayan_Thrust) is estimated to be 39 mm/yr or higher. [Transpression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transpression) due to the plates interacting is associated with high [seismicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seismicity) within the shallow [crust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crust_\(geology\)). Seismicity is detectable to a depth of 300 km (190 mi) beneath Afghanistan due to plate subduction.[^cite_note-Wheeler05-2] These earthquakes beneath the [Hindu Kush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_Kush) are the result of movement on faults accommodating [detachment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slab_detachment) of the subducted crust.[^cite_note-Sofia-Katerina-3] Within the shallow crust, the [Chaman Fault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaman_Fault) represents a major [transform fault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transform_fault) associated with large shallow earthquakes that forms the [transpressional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transpression) boundary between the Eurasian and Indian Plates. This zone consists of seismically active [thrust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrust_fault) and [strike-slip faults](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike-slip_fault) that have accommodated crustal deformation since the beginning of the formation of the [Himalayan orogeny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geology_of_the_Himalayas). The Northern Afghanistan Platform has one prominent active fault zone, the 220 km long west-east trending Alburz–Mormul fault that runs to the south of Mazar-e Sharif and is formed of thrust faults.[^cite_note-Shnizai-4]

## Earthquake



USGS Shakemap

The earthquake had a moment magnitude (M<sub>ww</sub>) of 6.2, according to the [United States Geological Survey](23814944.md) (USGS),[^cite_note-anss-1] while [Uzbekistan's Ministry of Emergency Situations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Emergency_Situations_\(Uzbekistan\)) reported a magnitude of 6.8.[^cite_note-5] Its [epicenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicenter) was located in [Nahri Shahi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahri_Shahi_District), [Balkh Province](457218.md), near the border with [Samangan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samangan_Province), with a [hypocenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypocenter) 28 km (17 mi) beneath the surface. It had a maximum [Modified Mercalli intensity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modified_Mercalli_intensity) of VII (*Very strong*). According to the USGS' [Prompt Assessment of Global Earthquakes for Response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PAGER) service, approximately 80,000 people were within the zone of intensity VII, and 1.49 million people were exposed to VI (*Strong*) shaking. The MMI of the earthquake was estimated to have reached VII in [Kholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kholm,_Afghanistan), VI in [Mazar-i-Sharif](20517.md) and [Balkh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balkh), and V (*Moderate*) in [Termez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Termez), [Uzbekistan](31853.md), the districts of [Nosiri Khusrav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nosiri_Khusrav_District) and [Shahritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahritus_District) in [Tajikistan](30108.md), and [Köýtendag District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6%C3%BDtendag_District), [Turkmenistan](198149.md).[^cite_note-anss-1] Tremors were also felt in [Mashhad](406512.md), Iran, [Islamabad](51153.md), Pakistan, and areas along the [India–Pakistan border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India%E2%80%93Pakistan_border).[^cite_note-bno-6] The shaking was estimated to have lasted for around 20 seconds.[^cite_note-7]

## Impact

The earthquake resulted in at least 31 fatalities,[^cite_note-8] 1,172 injuries, 305 homes destroyed and 1,004 others damaged. [^cite_note-reliefweb-9] There were 12 deaths and 616 injuries in [Balkh Province](457218.md) while 11 died and 329 were injured in [Samangan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samangan_Province).[^cite_note-pajhwok-10][^cite_note-rw-11] In Balkh Province, all districts reported structural damage.[^cite_note-bbc-12] Four fatalities and many injuries were reported in [Sholgara District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sholgara_District).[^cite_note-13] In [Mazar-i-Sharif](20517.md), dozens of people were injured by mass panic, broken windows and collapsing walls, many of them seriously,[^cite_note-14] and at least one house was badly damaged.[^cite_note-bno-6] In a nearby village, many homes and walls reportedly collapsed.[^cite_note-15] The [Mausoleum of Imam Ali](17910844.md)[^cite_note-bbc-12] and the Bagh-e Jehan Nama Palace in Baghlan were damaged.[^cite_note-ap2-16]

In [Samangan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samangan_Province), a landslide buried a section of [Asian Highway 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AH7) in [Khulm District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khulm_District), killing 10 people and injuring 43 others.[^cite_note-17] More than 200 houses in the district were destroyed while more than 1,000 others were damaged.[^cite_note-18] One death also occurred in each of [Aybak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aybak,_Samangan) and [Hazrati Sultan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazrati_Sultan_District).[^cite_note-8am-19] The laboratory of Samangan Provincial Hospital collapsed, destroying critical medical equipment.[^cite_note-ap2-16] At least two other medical facilities were also damaged.[^cite_note-20] Power lines connecting Afghanistan with Tajikistan and Uzbekistan were damaged,[^cite_note-bbc2-21] causing outages in nine provinces of Afghanistan including [Kabul](16826.md).[^cite_note-22] About 800 homes were partially destroyed in [Badakhshan Province](1282198.md), with the worst damage occurring in the Chogani area.[^cite_note-23] Over 500 homes were damaged or destroyed in other parts of the country.[^cite_note-24] Some damage was reported in [Faryab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faryab), [Sar-e-Pol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sar-e-Pol_Province), [Jowzjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jowzjan)[^cite_note-25] and [Baghlan Provinces](457216.md).[^cite_note-aa-26]

## Aftermath

The [United Nations Assistance Mission in Afghanistan](1234277.md) (UNAMA), the European Union Delegation to Afghanistan, the [Norwegian Refugee Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Refugee_Council), the [World Health Organization](33583.md)[^cite_note-27] and the [Bayat Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehsan_Bayat)[^cite_note-28] deployed emergency teams and humanitarian aid to the affected areas. The UN warned that only one-third of its \$2.4 billion Humanitarian Response Plan for Afghanistan had been funded so far.[^cite_note-29] The [Afghan defence ministry](1289906.md) deployed the [209 Al-Fatah Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/209_Al-Fatah_Corps) to conduct rescue and relief operations.[^cite_note-30] The [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) sent six shipments of relief supplies to help people affected by the earthquake.[^cite_note-31]
