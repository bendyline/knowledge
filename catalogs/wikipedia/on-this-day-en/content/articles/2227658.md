**Oyo** ([Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/8/82/Oyo_town.wav/Oyo_town.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>)([Yoruba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoruba_language): *Ìpínlẹ̀ Ọ̀yọ́* [\[ìk͡pĩ́lɛ̀ ɔ̀jɔ́\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Yoruba)) is a [state](226734.md) in [southwestern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_West_\(Nigeria\)) [Nigeria](21383.md). Its capital is [Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibadan), the [third most populous city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_cities_by_population) in the country and formerly the second most populous city in [Africa](5334607.md).[^cite_note-8] Oyo State is [bordered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border) to the north by [Kwara State](2227645.md) for 337 km, to the southeast by [Osun State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osun_State) for 187 km, partly across the River Osun, and to the south by [Ogun State](1436438.md), and to the west by the [Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic) of [Benin](3459.md) for 98 km. With a projected population of 7,976,100 in 2022, Oyo State is the [sixth most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population) in [Nigeria](21383.md).[^cite_note-\:0-9]

The vast majority of Oyo State residents are [Yoruba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoruba_people).[^cite_note-\:1-10] Nicknamed the "Pace Setter State", present-day Oyo State sits on territory formerly ruled by the [Oyo Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyo_Empire). The Oyo Empire was a powerful[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] [Yoruba empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorubaland) that ruled much of Oyo state and by extension major parts of [Yoruba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoruba_people) lands from c. 1300 to 1896.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] Built in the 1830s, the modern city of Oyo "New Ọyọ" (*Ọ̀yọ́ Àtìbà*) is considered a remnant of the imperial Oyo era to distinguish itself from the former capital to the north, '[Old Oyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyo_Empire#Ibadan)' (*Ọ̀yọ́-Ilé*). Although the medieval great Oyo empire collapsed in 1835, The [Alaafin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaafin) (owner and custodian of the palace) continues to serve a [ceremonial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceremony) role in the new city of Oyo in present Oyo state.[^cite_note-15]

Oyo State is the biggest state in Southern Nigeria in terms of landmass and is the second most populated state in Southern Nigeria after Lagos. According to the 2006 census the state is ranked the 4th most populous state in Nigeria with a population of 5,580,894. The recent estimate in 2022 projected the state population to be around 7,976,100 making it the [sixth most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population) in the [Nigeria](21383.md).[^cite_note-\:0-9] Noted for being the site of the first university in Nigeria,[^cite_note-16] the [University of Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ibadan),[^cite_note-17] founded in 1948, the state is acclaimed to be the state of many first in Nigeria including the First television station, first road in Nigeria, first stadium in Nigeria, First railway line in Nigeria, first teaching hospital in Nigeria amongst others.

The capital city of Ibadan, is the 3rd most populated city in Nigeria according to the 2006 official census. The state has the highest number of towns and cities mentioned in the top 50 most populous towns in Nigeria with Ibadan, Ogbomoso, Oyo, Saki and Iseyin all featuring in the list. The state [economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy) remains largely [agrarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrarian_society), with the western city of [Shaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaki,_Oyo) being described as the state's [breadbasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breadbasket). [Cassava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassava), [cocoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_bean), and [tobacco](30942.md) are among the most important [crops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop) to Oyo State's economy.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

## Geography



Ijio Hill, Iwajowa LGA, Oyo State, Nigeria

Oyo State covers approximately an area of 28,454 square kilometres (7,031,000 acres; 10,986 square miles) and is ranked 14th by size.[^cite_note-Unveiling_Nigeria_-_state-20] The [landscape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landscape) consists of old hard rocks and dome shaped hills, which rise gently from about 500 metres (550 yards; 1,600 feet) in the southern part and reaching a height of about 1,200 metres (1,300 yards; 3,900 feet) above sea level in the northern part.[^cite_note-21] Some principal rivers such as [Ogun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogun_River), [Oba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oba_River), [Oyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyan), [Otin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otin_River), Ofiki, Sasa, [Oni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oni), [Erinle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erinle_River) and [Osun river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osun_river) originate in this highland. The southern and southeastern parts of the state form a continuous plain.[^cite_note-Unveiling_Nigeria_-_state-20]

Oyo State contains a number of natural features including the [Old Oyo National Park, Agodi parks and gardens](4562292.md). In this location there was earlier [habitat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habitat) for the endangered [African wild dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_wild_dog), *[Lycaon pictus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycaon_pictus)*;[^cite_note-22] however, this [canid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canidae) is thought to have been locally [extirpated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_extinction) at the present.

### Climate

The climate is [equatorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equatorial_climate), notably with [dry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season) and [wet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_season) seasons with relatively high [humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humidity). The [dry season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season) lasts from November to March while the wet season starts from April and ends in October. Average daily temperature ranges between 25 °C (77 °F) and 35 °C (95 °F), almost throughout the year.[^cite_note-23]

## History

It was formed in 1976[^cite_note-24] from [Western State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_State_\(Nigeria\)), and included [Ọsun State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osun_State), which was split off in 1991. Oyo State is [homogenous](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Homogenous), mainly inhabited by the [Yoruba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoruba_people) ethnic group who are primarily agrarian but have a predilection for living in high-density urban centres.[^cite_note-25] The indigenes mainly comprise the Oyos, the [Oke-Oguns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onko), the Ibadans[^cite_note-26] and the [Ibarapas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibarapa_people), all belonging to the Yoruba family. Ibadan had been the centre of [administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_\(government\)) of the old [Western Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Region,_Nigeria) since the days of [British colonial rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_colonial_rule).[^cite_note-27]

Apart from Ibadan, other notable cities and towns in Ọyọ State include [Ọyọ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyo,_Nigeria), [Ogbomọsọ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogbomoso), Isẹyín-Okeogun, Ipapo-Okeogun, Kíṣì-Okeogun, Okeho-Okeogun, Saki-Okeogun, Igbeti-Okeogun, Igboho-okeogun \[Igboho], Eruwa-Ibarapa, [Iroko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iroko), Lanlate, OjeOwode-Okeogun, Sepeteri-Okeogun, Ilora-Oyo, Jobele-Oyo, [Awe-Oyo](21383.md), Ilérò-Okeogun, Okaka-Okeogun, Igbo Ora-Ibarapa, [Idere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idere)[^cite_note-28]

In 2024, Yoruba [separatists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separatist_movements_of_Nigeria) unsuccessfully [attempted to overthrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyo_State_coup_attempt) the state government in Ibadan.[^cite_note-29]

## Records and landmarks



Bower towers, Ibadan

The first degree awarding institution in Nigeria is the [University of Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ibadan) (established as a college of the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London)[^cite_note-30] when it was founded in 1948, and later converted into an [autonomous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_entity) university in 1962).[^cite_note-31] The other universities in the state are: [Lead City University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lead_City_University), Ibadan,[^cite_note-32] [Ajayi Crowther University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajayi_Crowther_University), [Oyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyo,_Nigeria),[^cite_note-acu.edu.ng-33] [Koladaisi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koladaisi_University),[^cite_note-34] Dominican University, Ibadan, and [Ladoke Akintola University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladoke_Akintola_University_of_Technology), [Ogbomoso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogbomoso).[^cite_note-lautech.edu.ng-35] [The Polytechnic, Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Polytechnic,_Ibadan),[^cite_note-Home-36] Oyo State College of Agriculture and Technology, Igbo Ora,[^cite_note-37] Adeseun Ogundoyin Polytechnic, Eruwa are located in Oyo State.[^cite_note-38]

There are 324 [secondary schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_schools) and 1,576 public [primary schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_schools) in the state. Other noteworthy institutions in the city include the [University College Hospital, Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_Hospital,_Ibadan);[^cite_note-39] the first teaching hospital in Nigeria and the [International Institute of Tropical Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Institute_of_Tropical_Agriculture) (IITA).[^cite_note-40] [Cocoa House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_House) located in Ibadan was the first [skyscraper](29485.md) built in Africa.[^cite_note-41]

The state is home to [NTA Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Television_Authority),[^cite_note-42] the first television station in Africa, and the Obafemi Awolowo (formerly, Liberty) Stadium, a stadium with a capacity of 35,000.[^cite_note-43]

Other major tourist attractions located in the state include: [Agodi Botanical Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agodi_Gardens), Ado-Awaye Suspended lake, [Mapo Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapo_Hall), University of Ibadan [Zoological Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoological_Garden), Ido [Cenotaph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cenotaph), [Trans-Wonderland Amusement Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans_Wonderland), [Old Oyo National Park](4562292.md) located in the historical site of the ancient capital of the famous [old Oyo Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyo_Empire), Iyamopo and Agbele Hill in Igbeti, [Bower's Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bower's_Tower) and the Cultural Centre, Mokola. The state hosts the first FM radio, and the first private television station, [Galaxy Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaxy_Television_\(Nigeria\))[^cite_note-44] in the country.[^cite_note-45]

## Government and politics

Under the Nigerian 1999 constitution[^cite_note-46] the government of Oyo State, and those of the other 35 [Nigerian States](226734.md), is divided into three branches to be in line with the government of the [Federal Republic of Nigeria](21383.md) which is also three tier: the [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_branch), the [legislative branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) and the [judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary).[^cite_note-47] The executive branch of Oyo State government is headed by an elected [executive governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Oyo_State) who presides over the [State Executive Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Council_of_Oyo_State) made up of appointed cabinet members. The present governor of Oyo State is [Seyi Makinde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seyi_Makinde) with [Bayo Lawal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayo_Lawal) serving as deputy governor.[^cite_note-48] The legislative branch is headed by an elected Speaker of the House of Assembly. The current Speaker is Hon. [Debo Ogundoyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debo_Ogundoyin).[^cite_note-49] And lastly, the judiciary is headed by the Chief Judge of Oyo State High Court.[^cite_note-50] The present Chief Judge of the state is Hon. Justice Iyabo Yerima[^cite_note-51]

## Education

Presently, Oyo State has 2,004 [public schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_school_\(government_funded\)) (primary), 971 private nursery/primary schools, 969 public secondary schools including 7 schools of Science and 57 private secondary schools. The state also has five government [technical colleges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_school) at [Oyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyo,_Nigeria), [Ogbomoso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogbomoso), [Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibadan),[^cite_note-52] Shaki-Okeogun[^cite_note-53] and Iseyin-Okeogun with enrolment of 2,829 students in the 2000/2001 academic session. The National Youth Service Corp[^cite_note-54] (NYSC) permanent orientation camp is located in Iseyin.[^cite_note-55]

The Ebedi Writers' Residency situated at the hill-side of barracks area of Iseyin is an international residency that has brought great writers, journalists and authors all over the world including Africa's first Nobel Laureate, Prof. [Wole Soyinka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wole_Soyinka), Jumoke Verissiomo, Funmi Aluko, Richard Ali, Paul Liam and others.[^cite_note-56]

Historically prominent secondary schools include [St Anne's School Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Anne's_School_Ibadan) (1869),[^cite_note-57] [Wesley College, Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_College,_Ibadan) (1905), [Ibadan Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibadan_Grammar_School) (1913), [Government College, Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_College,_Ibadan) (1927),[^cite_note-58] St Theresa's College Ibadan (1932), Ibadan Boys' High School (1938), [Olivet Heights Oyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivet_Baptist_High_School) (1945), [Queen's School, Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_School,_Ibadan) (1952), [Loyola College, Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_College,_Ibadan) (1954), St. Bernadine's Oyo (1957), Lagelu Grammar School Ibadan (1958), Iseyin District Grammar School Iseyin (1964), Methodist High School, Ibadan (1961) St Patrick's Grammar School Ibadan (1962) and several others. It is also home to Africa's leading fountain of knowledge, the iconic University of Ibadan (The university was originally instituted as an independent external college of the University of London, then it was called the University College, Ibadan[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-60]).

Two new technical colleges located at Iseyin, Iseyin Local Government area and Ikija in Oluyole Local Government area were established in the 2001/2002 academic session. A college of education, Oyo State College of Education, Oyo.[^cite_note-61] There is a Polytechnic, [The Polytechnic, Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Polytechnic,_Ibadan)[^cite_note-Home-36] with 2 satellite campuses at [Eruwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eruwa)[^cite_note-62] and Shaki-Okeogun, (now known as The Oke-Ogun Polytechnic) and a State-owned University, The [Ladoke Akintola University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladoke_Akintola_University_of_Technology) (LAUTECH), Ogbomoso[^cite_note-lautech.edu.ng-35][^cite_note-63] which is jointly owned by Oyo and [Osun State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osun_State) Governments. The federal premier university The [University of Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ibadan)[^cite_note-64] is also located in the State capital. A private Polytechnic (SAF Polytechnic, Iseyin) is located in Iseyin. There is a vocational Institute in Saki West Local Government named: The Kings Poly, Shaki-Okeogun.[^cite_note-65]

There are also the [Federal College of Animal Health and Production Technology, Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_College_of_Animal_Health_and_Production_Technology,_Vom);[^cite_note-66] [Federal College of Education (Special), Oyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_College_of_Education_\(Special\),_Oyo),[^cite_note-67] the Federal School of Surveying, Oyo; [Cocoa Research Institute of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_Research_Institute_of_Nigeria) (CRIN), Institute of Agricultural Research and Training (IAR\&T), the Nigerian Institute of Science Laboratory Technology (NISLT), the Federal College of Forestry, Ibadan (FEDCOFOR),[^cite_note-68] a subsidiary of [Forestry Research Institute of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forestry_Research_Institute_of_Nigeria) (FRIN)[^cite_note-69] and the [Nigerian Institute Of Social And Economic Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Institute_Of_Social_And_Economic_Research) (NISER), all in Ibadan.[^cite_note-70][^cite_note-71]

Similarly, there are 15 Nomadic schools in the State. They are Gaa Jooro and Gaa Baale, both in Kisi (Irepo Local Government); Baochilu Government; Arin-Oye, Abiogun, Okaka and Baba-Ode (Itesiwaju Local Government); Iganna (Iwajowa Local Government); Igangan and Ayete (Ibarapa North Local Government); Gaa Kondo and Igbo-Ora, (Ibarapa Central Local Government) and Sepeteri (Saki East Local Government). There are 213 [continuing education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuing_education) centres spread all over the State.[^cite_note-72]

15 special primary schools and 8 special units in secondary schools cater for handicapped children. There are 11,732 teaching staff in the state public secondary schools and 2,789 non-teaching staff.

The Agency for Adult and Non-formal Education (AANFE) caters for illiterate adults who had no opportunity of formal education. The agency has 455 classes in existence in the 33 Local Government areas of the State, while 200,000 illiterate adults and over 80,000 post-illiterate adults have been trained recently.[^cite_note-73]

## Agriculture

[Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) is the main occupation of the people of Oyo State. The climate in the state favours the cultivation of crops like [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [yam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yam_\(vegetable\)), [cassava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassava), [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet), [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice), [plantains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plantain_\(cooking\)), [cocoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_bean), [palm produce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_tree), [cashew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cashew) etc. There are a number of government farm settlements in Iseyin/Ipapo, Ilora, [Eruwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eruwa), [Ogbomosho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogbomosho), Iresaadu, Ijaiye, Akufo and [Lalupon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalupon). There is abundance of clay, [kaolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaolinite) and aquamarine. There are also vast cattle ranches at Saki, Fasola and Ibadan, a dairy farm at Monatan in Ibadan and the statewide Oyo State Agricultural Development Programme with headquarters at Saki. A number of international and federal agricultural establishments are located in the state.

The state government is led by a democratical elected governor who works closely with members of the state's house of assembly. The capital city of the state is [Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibadan).[^cite_note-84]

## Electoral system

The governor of each state is selected using a modified two-round system. To be elected in the first round, a candidate must receive the plurality of the vote and over 25% of the vote in at least two -third of the State local government Areas. If no candidate passes threshold, a second round will be held between the top candidate and the next candidate to have received a plurality of votes in the highest number of local government Areas.[^cite_note-85]
