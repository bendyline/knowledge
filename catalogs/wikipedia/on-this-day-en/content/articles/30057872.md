**Argentina–Israel relations** are the historical and bilateral relations between the [Argentine Republic](18951905.md) and the [State of Israel](9282173.md) began shortly after the [Israeli Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Declaration_of_Independence) in 1948, with the countries establishing diplomatic relations on 31 May 1949. Both are members of the [World Trade Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Organization) and the [United Nations](31769.md).

## History

### 20th century



[Jacob Tsur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Tsur), the first [Ambassador of Israel to Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ambassadors_of_Israel_to_Argentina), speaking at the [Casa Rosada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_Rosada) during a ceremony for the signing of an agreement between Argentina and Israel, 1950. He is joined by [Juan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Per%C3%B3n) and [Eva Perón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Per%C3%B3n), [Oscar Ivanissevich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Ivanissevich), and [Héctor J. Cámpora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9ctor_J._C%C3%A1mpora), among others.

Argentina has a long history of the Jewish population dated back to the 16th century. On 29 November 1947, Argentina was among the 10 countries that abstained from voting in the [United Nations Partition Plan for Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Partition_Plan_for_Palestine). Argentina established diplomatic relations with Israel on 31 May 1949, a year after its independence.[^cite_note-3]

Relations between the two were tested during the early years of [Nazi hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_hunters) when Israel's [Mossad](27619007.md) [kidnapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Eichmann's_capture) former [Nazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi) [Adolf Eichmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Eichmann) despite Argentine protestations of a violation of its sovereignty. Immediately after [World War II](32927.md), Argentina was a safe haven for former Nazi officials because they brought badly needed capital investment and/or technical expertise.

According to declassified [British Foreign and Commonwealth Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_and_Commonwealth_Office) documents, Israel sold arms to Argentina before, during, and in the immediate aftermath of the [Falklands War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falklands_War) in 1982.[^cite_note-4] The arms sales to Argentina included [Douglas A-4 Skyhawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_A-4_Skyhawk) jets, which would later be used in the war with the [United Kingdom](31717.md).[^cite_note-5]

[Carlos Menem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Menem) was the first head of state of Argentina to make a diplomatic visit to Israel, in 1991. He proposed to mediate between Israel and [Syria](7515849.md) in their negotiations over the [Golan Heights](7515865.md).[^cite_note-6] However, the relations were further tested when [Hezbollah](13919.md) was blamed for [bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Buenos_Aires_Israeli_embassy_bombing) the [Israeli embassy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Israel,_Buenos_Aires) and a [Jewish community centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMIA_Bombing) in 1992 and 1994, respectively. Since 2013, roughly 100 Jewish organisations across Argentina have called for the government to repeal its pact with the [Islamic Republic of Iran](14653.md) over the AMIA terrorist attacks.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

### 21st century

While Argentina has the largest Jewish population in [Latin America](18524.md), there have been various cases of [antisemitism](1078.md) in Argentina,[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] such as the desecration of 58 Jewish graves in [La Tablada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Tablada) by unknown peoples in 2009,[^cite_note-11] mostly due to negative [stereotypes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereotypes_of_Jews) of Jews controlling business interests and dominating the world through capitalism, as well as Israel's affiliation with the United States.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

In 2010, Argentina announced the intention to join [Brazil](3383.md) in [recognising an independent Palestinian state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_recognition_of_the_State_of_Palestine), provoking sharp criticism from Israel.[^cite_note-kirchner-15] On 6 December 2010, Argentina subsequently recognised the [State of Palestine](241405.md) on the borders of 4 June 1967 (i.e., the [West Bank](33209.md), [Gaza](12047.md) and [East Jerusalem](343508.md)), which constituted Arab territory prior to the [Six-Day War](29328.md).[^cite_note-16] In 2012, Argentine President [Cristina Fernández de Kirchner](1644780.md) met an Israeli-Palestinian delegation and announced that Argentina would spearhead the Latin American role in reinvigorating the peace process in the [Israeli–Palestinian conflict](46216.md).[^cite_note-kirchner-15]

In September 2017, Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) paid an official visit to Argentina, becoming the first Israeli Prime Minister in office to visit Argentina and Latin America.[^cite_note-17]

In March 2019, two Iranian citizens used fake Israeli passports to leave [Spain](26667.md) and different fake Israeli passports to enter Argentina. Realising that the passports were fraudulent, Argentinian police arrested the suspects. Argentinian prosecutors discovered that the suspects had previously used fake passports to enter other countries, including [Portugal](23033.md).[^cite_note-18]

In January 2020, President [Alberto Fernández](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Fern%C3%A1ndez) travelled to Israel for his first presidential trip abroad. There he paid respects to the victims of the [Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust) and maintained a bilateral meeting with Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) who thanked him for keeping [Hezbollah](13919.md) branded as a terrorist organisation, a measure taken by Fernández's predecessor [Mauricio Macri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauricio_Macri).[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20]



President [Javier Milei](56599840.md) and Israeli President [Isaac Herzog](4645588.md) shake hands in [Beit HaNassi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_HaNassi) in Jerusalem on 6 February 2024



Milei and Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) in June 2025

In the aftermath of the [2023 Argentine general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Argentine_general_election) and [Javier Milei](56599840.md)'s victory, President-elect Milei pledged to move the Argentine Embassy from [Tel Aviv](31453.md) to [Jerusalem](16043.md),[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] and announced as part of "a spiritual trip" alongside rabbis he studies with, Milei would visit the [Western Wall](75225.md) and Israel before his inauguration on 10 December 2023.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] In December 2023, President Javier Milei announced that his government is working on designating [Hamas](13913.md) as a terrorist organization.[^cite_note-25]

In February 2024, Argentinian President Javier Milei visited Israel, making Israel his first bilateral overseas destination as president.[^cite_note-26] In May 2024, President Javier Milei's government broke with Argentina's previous position on Palestine, voting against recognition of Palestinian statehood at United Nations. The vote was in line with the country's pro-Israel shift in its foreign policy.[^cite_note-27]

In June 2025, Milei reaffirmed his pledge to move the embassy to Jerusalem, aiming for the spring of 2026,[^cite_note-28] and El Al announced plans to launch direct passenger flights between the two countries.[^cite_note-29]

Argentina has [supported Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina_in_the_2026_Iran_war) and the United States in the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), with Milei saying that he is "proud to be the most Zionist president in the world."[^cite_note-30] During his [third state visit to Israel](82990930.md), Milei and Netanyahu announced direct flights between the two countries via [El Al](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Al) and the advance on the embassy issues.[^cite_note-visitelal-31] The flights connecting [Ben Gurion Airport](113149.md) (TLV) in Israel to [Ezeiza Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezeiza_Airport) (EZE) will connect [Jerusalem](16043.md) and [Buenos Aires](255919.md) and will become the airline's longest scheduled route.[^cite_note-32] The new service is planned to start in late November 2026 and will be operated twice weekly with Boeing 787-9 aircraft configured to accommodate 271 passengers, with a flight time of 16 1/2 hours to Buenos Aires and 15 hours to Tel Aviv.[^cite_note-33]

In September 2026, Argentinian President Javier Milei met Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu at the [United Nations General Assembly](31957.md) in New York.[^cite_note-34]

#### Isaac Accords

In August 2025, the [Isaac Accords](80945706.md), which are modeled on the 2020 [Abraham Accords](64883396.md), were officially announced. The Isaac Accords are a diplomatic initiative inspired by Argentine President [Javier Milei](56599840.md)'s receipt of the [Genesis Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_Prize) that aims to strengthen political, economic, and cultural cooperation between Israel and various Latin American countries, starting with Uruguay, Panama, and Costa Rica. The nonprofit organization [American Friends of Isaac Accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Friends_of_Isaac_Accords) (AFOIA) was also launched in the same month. The organization was established with an initial US\$1 million from the Genesis Prize Foundation, in honor of Milei's acceptance of the Genesis Prize in Jerusalem on June 12, 2025.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]

#### Sea Lion Field

The involvement of the Israeli company [Navitas Petroleum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navitas_Petroleum) in the development of the [Sea Lion Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_Lion_Field) near the [Falkland Islands](7550182.md) was reported to have caused tension between the two countries amidst a heated renewal of the [Falklands issue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falkland_Islands_sovereignty_dispute).[^cite_note-\:0-37] Argentina's foreign ministry called the company's plans to develop the offshore oil field "unilateral and illegitimate."[^cite_note-\:0-37] In September 2026, Javier Milei threatened to sanction oil firms working off the Falkland Islands and said his government would file criminal charges against Navitas Petroleum, which has a 65 percent stake in the oil field.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39] The same month, a judge in Argentina ordered British and Israeli companies to halt oilfield development.[^cite_note-40]
