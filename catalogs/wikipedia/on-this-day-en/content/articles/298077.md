The [United States](3434750.md) is one of the biggest [paper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper) consumers in the world. Between 1990 and 2002, paper consumption in the United States increased from 84.9 million tons to [97.3 million tons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_E8_kg). In 2006, there were approximately 450 paper mills in the United States, accounting for \$68 billion.[^cite_note-1]
