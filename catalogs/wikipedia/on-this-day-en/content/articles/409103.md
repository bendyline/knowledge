This article lists the various **[political parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Cameroon)**.

Cameroon is a [one party dominant state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_party_dominant_state) with the [Cameroon People's Democratic Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon_People's_Democratic_Movement) in power. While opposition parties are allowed, they are widely considered to have no real chance of gaining power.
