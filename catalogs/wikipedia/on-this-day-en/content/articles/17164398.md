**Chongos Bajo District** is one of nine [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Peru) of the province [Chupaca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chupaca_Province) in [Peru](170691.md).[^cite_note-1]

## History

On 19 July 2026, a M<sub>w</sub> 5.1 earthquake struck Chongos Bajo District, killing five people and injuring 21 others.[^cite_note-2]
