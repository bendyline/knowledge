**Daniel Funke** (born 23 July 1981)[^cite_note-1] is a German journalist. Since 2019 he has been the head of the [Berlin](3354.md) office and [lobbyist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobbying) at BurdaVerlag, a division of German media company [Hubert Burda Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Burda_Media).[^cite_note-2]

From 2002 to 2009, he studied [German studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_studies) ("Germanistik") and history at [Humboldt-Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt-Universit%C3%A4t_Berlin). Between 2004 and 2007, he also worked at the private TV channel [Sat. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sat._1) and the magazine *Blitz* ("*Flash*"). In 2007, he became an editor at *[Bunte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunte)*, a German people magazine, and in 2012, the head of its Berlin office.

## Personal life

Funke has been married to German politician and former federal secretary of health [Jens Spahn](37152181.md) ([CDU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christlich_Demokratische_Union)) since 2017.[^cite_note-3] Funke and Spahn had a child via [surrogacy](1514981.md) in the United States in July 2026.[^cite_note-4]

## General

In 2018 Funke caused controversy with a story he wrote for *Bunte* magazine about the then-new US ambassador [Richard Grenell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Grenell), which presented Grenell in a very positive light. The story didn't reveal that Funke and Spahn were friends with Grenell and his partner Matt Lashey and could therefore be seen as a political favour to ease Grenell's entry into German politics.[^cite_note-5] In 2024 he left the Bunte Verlag to pursue new projects and tasks.[^cite_note-6]

## FFP 2 masks dealings

Funke again caused controversy in March 2021 when it was revealed that his employer had sold 570,000 FFP 2 masks without a formal bidding process conducted by the Federal Ministry of Health, which was led by his husband, Jens Spahn.[^cite_note-7]
