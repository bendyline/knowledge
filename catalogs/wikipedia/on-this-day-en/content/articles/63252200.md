**Economic diversity** or **economic diversification** refers to variations in the economic status or the use of a broad range of economic activities in a region or country.[^cite_note-1] Diversification is used as a strategy to encourage positive [economic growth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_growth) and development.[^cite_note-unfccc-2] Research shows that more diversified economies are associated with higher levels of [gross domestic product](12594.md).[^cite_note-3]

For example, the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) have intentionally invested in aviation, tourism, finance, logistics, real estate, renewable energy, continually from the 1970s, and is thus relatively less dependent on the [petroleum industry](994887.md), which is otherwise the principal economic driver in the [Gulf Arab states](3088555.md).[^cite_note-4]

## Diversification types

1. **Non-connected diversification** – creating a new area. The process is slow, because it is needed to create a whole infrastructure, but the profit would be higher.

2. **Connected diversification** is based on an economical mechanism for expanding the available potential. For business development it means low risks and good margin.

3. **Combined diversification** – more frequently both methods are used together.
