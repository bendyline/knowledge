# 78th Primetime Creative Arts Emmy Awards

For the main ceremony, see [78th Primetime Emmy Awards](81631496.md).

| --- | --- |
| Date | September 5–6, 2026 |
| Presented by | [Academy of Television Arts & Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Television_Arts_&_Sciences) |
| Most awards | *[Widow's Bay](79205666.md)* (8) |
| Most nominations | *[Hacks](67541962.md)* (17) |

The **78th Primetime Creative Arts Emmy Awards** honored the best in artistic and technical achievement in American prime time television programming from June 1, 2025, until May 31, 2026, as chosen by the [Academy of Television Arts & Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Television_Arts_&_Sciences).[^cite_note-Rules_and_Procedures-1] The awards were presented on September 5 and 6, 2026, at the [Peacock Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_Theater) in [Downtown Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Los_Angeles), California.[^cite_note-2] A total of 101 Creative Arts Emmy categories were presented. The nominations were announced on July 8, 2026. *[Hacks](67541962.md)* received the most nominations with 17, followed by *[Beef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beef_\(TV_series\))* and the medical drama *[The Pitt](77660135.md)* with 13 each.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Winners and nominees

Winners will be listed first, highlighted in boldface, and indicated with a double dagger (‡).[^cite_note-6] Sections are based upon the categories listed in the 2025–2026 Emmy rules and procedures.[^cite_note-Rules_and_Procedures-1] Area awards and juried awards are denoted next to the category names as applicable.[^cite_note-7] For simplicity, producers who received nominations for program awards have been omitted.
