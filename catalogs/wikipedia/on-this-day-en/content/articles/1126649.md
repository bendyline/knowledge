The **Béjaïa Province** ([Kabyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabyle_language): *Tawilayt n Bgayet*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ولاية بجاية, *Wilāyat Bijāyah*) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Algeria) of [Algeria](358.md) in the [Kabylie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabylie) region. Its population was 984,050 inhabitants in 2019, with a density of 305 km<sup>2</sup>. The province's capital city is [Béjaïa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A9ja%C3%AFa), the terminus of the [Béni Mansour–Bejaïa railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A9ni_Mansour%E2%80%93Beja%C3%AFa_railway).



Coast of Bejaia Province

[Gouraya National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gouraya_National_Park) is located in Béjaïa Province. A population of an [endangered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endangered) primate species, the [Barbary macaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbary_macaque), is found within the park; this [primate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primate) has a severely restricted and [disjunctive range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disjunctive_population).[^cite_note-2]

## History

The province was created by the [Sétif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9tif_\(d%C3%A9partement\)) in 1974.

## Administrative divisions

The province is divided into 19 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Algeria) (*[daïras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Da%C3%AFra)*), which are further divided into 52 [*communes*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Algeria) or municipalities.
