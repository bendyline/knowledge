In [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), a **duty** is a target-specific form of [tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tax) levied by a [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_\(polity\)) or other political entity. It is often associated with [customs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customs), in which context they are also known as **[tariffs](55551.md)** or **dues**. The term is often used to describe a tax on certain items purchased abroad.[^cite_note-1]

A duty is levied on specific [commodities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commodity_\(type_of_good\)), [financial transactions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_transaction), [estates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estate_\(law\)), etc. rather than being a direct imposition on individuals or corporations such [income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Income_tax) or [property taxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Property_tax). Examples include [customs duty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customs_duty), [excise](11404033.md) duty, [stamp duty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stamp_duty), [estate duty](5824627.md), and [gift duty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gift_duty).

## Customs duty

Main article: [Tariff § Customs duty](55551.md#Customs_duty)

A customs duty or due is the [indirect tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indirect_tax) levied on the import or export of goods in international trade. In [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) a duty is also a kind of [consumption tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumption_tax). A duty levied on goods being imported is referred to as an 'import duty', and one levied on exports an 'export duty'.

## Estate duty

An estate duty (in the U.S. [inheritance tax](5824627.md)) is a tax levied on the [estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estate_\(law\)) of a deceased person in many [jurisdictions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurisdiction) or on the [inheritance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inheritance) of a person. The tax is sometimes referred to, formally or informally, as a *death duty*.[^cite_note-2]

## Gift duty

A [gift tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gift_tax) on value given from one taxable entity to another.
