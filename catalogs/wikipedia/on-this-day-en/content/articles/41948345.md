# Terrorism in Norway



The aftermath of the [2011 Norway attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Norway_attacks)

**Terrorism in Norway** includes a list of major [terrorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist) incidents where organized groups and lone wolves have tried carrying out attacks. In recent years, there has been a rise mostly of [Islamic extremism](8734632.md) and [far-right](221220.md) violence and various groups have been suspected of terrorism plots.&#x20;

The [Norwegian Police Security Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Police_Security_Service) are closely monitoring [Islamist](15012.md) and [far-right](221220.md) groups in [Eastern Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Norway).[^cite_note-1]

## 21st century

The [right-wing](56522.md) terrorist [Philip Manshaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Manshaus) who was behind the [Bærum mosque shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A6rum_mosque_shooting) in August 2019, admitted to have been in contact with the [Nordic Resistance Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordic_Resistance_Movement) prior to the attack, but was never accepted as a member.[^cite_note-2]

In November 2020, the [Norwegian Police Security Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Police_Security_Service) (PST) made an announcement about the general terrorist threat against Norway as moderate. The background for this was the [2020 Nice stabbing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Nice_stabbing) and [2020 Vienna attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Vienna_attack). The Norwegian police considered it possible that someone would try to carry out terrorist acts in Norway, motivated by [Islamist](15012.md) motives. The development in both [France](5843419.md) and [Austria](26964606.md) with the republishing of the [Jyllands-Posten Muhammad cartoons controversy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jyllands-Posten_Muhammad_cartoons_controversy), and the debate this creates in Norway, may also help inspiring people to commit acts of terrorism. The police was temporarily armed for three weeks, but the arming was prolonged.[^cite_note-3]
