**Sagaing Region** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): စစ်ကိုင်းတိုင်းဒေသကြီး, pronounced [\[zəɡáɪ̯ɰ̃ táɪ̯ɰ̃ dèθa̰ dʑí\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Burmese); formerly **Sagaing Division**) is a [first-level administrative division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Myanmar) of [Myanmar](19457.md), located in the north-western part of the country between latitude 21° 30' north and longitude 94° 97' east. It is bordered by [Chin State](493366.md) and India's [Nagaland](408677.md), [Manipur](422642.md), and [Arunachal Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh) states to the west and north, [Kachin State](493369.md), [Shan State](493376.md), and [Mandalay Region](2357817.md) to the east and Mandalay Region and [Magway Region](493360.md) to the south. The [Ayeyarwady River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayeyarwady_River) forms a greater part of its eastern and also southern boundary. Sagaing Region has an area of 93,527 square kilometres (36,111 sq mi), making it the second-largest subdivision of Myanmar. In 1996, it had a population of over 5,300,000, while its population in 2012 was 6,600,000. The urban population in 2012 was 1,230,000, and the rural population was 5,360,000.[^cite_note-4] The namesake of Sagaing Region is [Sagaing](2116404.md) but the administrative capital and largest city is [Monywa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monywa).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]&#x20;

## History

### 1st to 13th centuries



Pyu city in red

The [Pyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyu_people) were the first in recorded history to populate the area of Sagaing Region by the first century CE. The [Burmans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamar) first migrated into [Upper Myanmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Myanmar) by the ninth century CE. The area came under the [Pagan Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pagan_Kingdom) certainly by the middle of the 11th century when the King [Anawrahta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anawrahta) (r. 1044–1077) founded the Pagan Empire, which encompasses the modern day Myanmar.&#x20;



Pagan Empire



Sagaing state came under territory of Mong Mao in the heyday of the [Si Kefa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Si_Kefa) period (1360)

### 13th to 19th centuries



Map of Taungoo Empire in 1580 stretched from Manipur in the west to Cambodia in the east

After the fall of Pagan in 1287, the northwestern parts of Upper Myanmar came under the [Sagaing Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagaing_Kingdom) (1315–1364) ruled by Burmanized [Shan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shan_people) kings. The area was ruled by the kings of [Ava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ava_Kingdom) from 1364 to 1555 and the kings of [Taungoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taungoo_Dynasty) from 1555 to 1752. [Konbaung Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konbaung_Dynasty) (1752–1885), founded by king [Alaungpaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaungpaya) in [Shwebo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shwebo), became the last Burmese dynasty before the [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) conquest of Upper Burma in 1885. The area became Sagaing Division after the Burmese independence in January 1948.&#x20;

### Modern era



Konbaung Empire in 1824

In the aftermath of the [2021 Myanmar coup d'état](66567997.md), Sagaing Region, which is part of the [Bamar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamar_people) homeland, emerged as a stronghold of resistance against military rule. [Myanmar Armed Forces](20394.md) has engaged in significant military offensives throughout the region to quell resistance and intimidate local villagers. Sagaing Region has since become the site of several high-profile massacres by military forces, including the 2022 [Let Yet Kone massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Let_Yet_Kone_massacre) and the 2023 [Tar Taing massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tar_Taing_massacre).[^cite_note-\:1-8][^cite_note-\:2-9]&#x20;

In March 2025, a [M<sub>w</sub>](6207163.md#Mw) 7.7–7.9 [earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Myanmar_earthquake) struck close to the capital city of Sagaing. Significant damage was recorded within the city and throughout the entire region.&#x20;

## Administrative divisions

As of 2022, Sagaing Region consists of 13 districts and 1 Self-Administered Zone, divided into 34 townships[^cite_note-MIMU001-10] with 198 wards and villages. The major cities are [Sagaing](2116404.md), [Shwebo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shwebo), [Monywa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monywa), [Ye U](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ye_U\&action=edit\&redlink=1), [Katha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katha,_Burma), [Kale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalaymyo), [Tamu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamu,_Myanmar), [Mawlaik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mawlaik) and [Hkamti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hkamti_District). [Mingun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mingun) with its famous bell is located near Sagaing but can be reached across the Ayeyarwady from [Mandalay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandalay).&#x20;

## Government

### Executive

Main article: [Sagaing Region Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagaing_Region_Government)

Sagaing Region was formerly under the control of a military junta known as the [State Administration Council](66589972.md) (SAC), led by General [Min Aung Hlaing](38278640.md). This regime seized power following a [coup on February 1, 2021](66567997.md), overthrowing the elected civilian government led by [Aung San Suu Kyi](2847.md) and the [National League for Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_for_Democracy) (NLD). Since then, the country has been under an extended state of emergency and faces an ongoing civil war between the junta, pro-democracy forces, and various ethnic armed groups. However, from late 2025 to early 2026, [sham elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025-26_Myanmar_general_election) were held for the country's national legislature, the [Union Assembly](29739324.md) and for administrative divisional legislatures, including the [Sagaing Region Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagaing_Region_Hluttaw). This has led to Sagaing Region's governmental institutions functioning again, including the [Sagaing Region Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagaing_Region_Government). The Sagaing Region Government is the government of Sagaing Region and exercises authority over the region.&#x20;

### Legislature

Main article: [Sagaing Region Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagaing_Region_Hluttaw)

The **Sagaing Region Hluttaw** is the legislature of Sagaing Region, responsible for local governance, passing regional laws, approving localized budgets, and overseeing the region cabinet. The Hluttaw is [unicameral](204304.md), with 101 seats.&#x20;

### Judiciary

The **Sagaing Region High Court** is the highest court within Sagaing Region.&#x20;

The [Bamar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamar_people) make up the majority of the region's population, living in [Anyar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_Zone_\(Myanmar\)), the country's central dry zone and along the Mandalay-Myitkyina Railroad. Other groups, like the Shan and Kachin, form small minorities. The [Shan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shan_people) live in the upper [Chindwin River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chindwin_River) valley. [Kuki people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zo_people), which includes the [Thadou people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thadou_people), live in the south and along the [Indo-Myanmar Border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India%E2%80%93Myanmar_border) from Homalin to Tamu-Namphalong axis.&#x20;

Smaller ethnic groups native to the Region include the [Kadu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadu_people) and [Ganang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganan_language), who live in the upper [Mu River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_River_\(Irrawaddy\)) valley and Meza River valley. There are also an unknown number of [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church_in_Myanmar) [Bayingyi people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayingyi_people) (at least 3,000), the descendants of 16th and 17th century Portuguese adventurers and mercenaries, who live in their ancestral villages on the expansive plains of the Mu river valley.&#x20;

After the 2014 [Census in Myanmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_in_Myanmar), the Burmese government indefinitely withheld release of detailed ethnicity data, citing concerns around political and social concerns surrounding the issue of ethnicity in Myanmar.[^cite_note-\:7-13][^cite_note-\:72-14] In 2022, researchers published an analysis of the [General Administration Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Administration_Department)'s nationwide 2018-2019 township reports to tabulate the ethnic makeup of the region.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-\:73-16]&#x20;

According to the [2014 Myanmar Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Myanmar_Census), [Buddhists](3267529.md), who make up 92.2% of Sagaing Region's population, form the largest religious community there.[^cite_note-TUR2-18] Minority religious communities include [Christians](5211.md) (6.6%), [Muslims](6037917.md) (1.1%), and [Hindus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism) (0.1%) who collectively comprise the remainder of Sagaing Region's population.[^cite_note-TUR2-18] 0.1% of the population listed no religion, other religions, or were otherwise not enumerated.[^cite_note-TUR2-18]&#x20;

According to the [State Sangha Maha Nayaka Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Sangha_Maha_Nayaka_Committee)'s 2016 statistics, 55,041 Buddhist monks were registered in Sagaing Region, comprising 10.3% of Myanmar's total [Sangha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangha) membership, which includes both novice [samanera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samanera) and fully-ordained bhikkhu.[^cite_note-mahana-19] The majority of monks belong to the [Thudhamma Nikaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thudhamma_Nikaya) (83.8%), followed by [Shwegyin Nikaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shwegyin_Nikaya) (16.1%), with the remainder of monks belonging to other small [monastic orders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nik%C4%81ya).[^cite_note-mahana-19] 9,915 [thilashin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thilashin) were registered in Sagaing Region, comprising 16.4% of Myanmar's total thilashin community.[^cite_note-mahana-19]&#x20;

## Transport



[Sagaing](2116404.md)

Hemmed in by two great rivers of Myanmar, the [Irrawaddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrawaddy_River) and the [Chindwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chindwin_River), river transport is a common way to move people and cargo. Much of the inland Sagaing Region relies on roads and rail in poor condition.&#x20;

## Economy

Agriculture is the chief occupation. The leading crop is [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice), which occupies most of the arable ground. Other crops include [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [sesame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesame), [peanut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut), [pulses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulses), [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton), and [tobacco](30942.md). The region being next to India, depends on the export import business from India. It is the gateway to India for Myanmar. Sagaing is Myanmar's leading producer of wheat, contributing more than 80% of the country's total production. Important minerals include [gold](12240.md), [coal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal), [salt](1605200.md) and small amounts of [petroleum](23195.md). Industry includes [textiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile), [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper) refining, [gold](12240.md) smelting, and a [diesel engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel_engine) plant. The Region has many rice mills, [edible oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooking_oil) mills, saw mills, cotton mills, and mechanized [weaving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weaving) factories. Local industry includes earthen [pots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pottery), [silverware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household_silver), [bronze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze)-wares, [iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron)-wares and [lacquerware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lacquerware).&#x20;

Forestry is important in the wetter upper regions along the [Chindwin River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chindwin_River), with [teak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teak) and other hardwoods extracted. As in other parts of the country, [reforestation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reforestation) is not effective enough to maintain [sustainable forestry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_forestry). Since the [2021 Myanmar coup d'état](66567997.md), [illegal logging](465780.md) of teak and tamalan trees has surged in Sagaing Region, predominantly in key contested battlegrounds, including [Kani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kani_Township), [Yinmabin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yinmabin_Township), [Kantbalu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanbalu_Township), [Indaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indaw_Township) and [Banmauk townships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banmauk_Township).[^cite_note-\:0-24] Both the Burmese military and resistance groups have profited from the illegal logging trade.[^cite_note-\:0-24] Smugglers transport the wood to India in order to circumvent [economic sanctions](411315.md), and use the [Myanma Timber Enterprise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanma_Timber_Enterprise) to license the wood as being sourced from permitted areas.[^cite_note-\:0-24][^cite_note-25]&#x20;

## Healthcare

The general state of healthcare in Myanmar is poor. The military government spends anywhere from 0.5% to 3% of the country's GDP on health care, consistently ranking among the lowest in the world.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] Although healthcare is nominally free, in reality, patients have to pay for medicine and treatment, even in public clinics and hospitals. Public hospitals lack many of the basic facilities and equipment. Moreover, the healthcare infrastructure outside of [Yangon](57404.md) and [Mandalay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandalay) is extremely poor. In 2003, Sagaing Region had less than a quarter of the number of hospital beds counted in [Yangon Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangon_Region), with a similar size of population.[^cite_note-cso-hosp-29]&#x20;
