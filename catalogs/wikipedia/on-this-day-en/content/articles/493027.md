The **minister for culture, communications and sport** ([Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_language): *An tAire Cultúir, Cumarsáide agus Spóirt*) is a senior [minister](739237.md) in the [Government of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Ireland) and leads the [Department of Culture, Communications and Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Culture,_Communications_and_Sport).

The minister since January 2025 is [Patrick O'Donovan](31018587.md), [TD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teachta_D%C3%A1la). He is assisted by one [minister of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_State_at_the_Department_of_Culture,_Communications_and_Sport):

- [Charlie McConalogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_McConalogue), TD – [Minister of State for sport and postal policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_State_at_the_Department_of_Culture,_Communications_and_Sport)
