The **Jerusalem District** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): מחוז ירושלים; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): منطقة القدس) is one of the [six administrative districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Israel) of [Israel](9282173.md). The district capital is [Jerusalem](16043.md) and its total land area is 652 km<sup>2</sup>. The population of 1,331,595 is 64.4% [Jewish](25955086.md) and 31.2% [Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab).[^cite_note-cbs-populations-4] A fifth (21%) of the Arabs in Israel live in the Jerusalem Municipality, which includes both [East](343508.md) and [West Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Jerusalem).[^cite_note-5] [Israel's annexation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem_Law) of East Jerusalem has not been recognized by the international community.[^cite_note-6]

Most Arabs in the Jerusalem District are [Palestinians](23267.md), eligible to apply for citizenship under Israeli law, but either decline to apply or are unsuccessful in doing so. [Arab citizens of Israel](492331.md) constitute a significant minority in the district, living in [Abu Ghosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Ghosh), [Beit Safafa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_Safafa) and [East Jerusalem](343508.md), where Arab professionals have settled since the late 1970s, mainly for the provision of legal and other services to the local population. The non-Jewish population is 95.2% [Muslim](19541.md), 3.5% [Christian](18337522.md) with the others unclassified by religion.[^cite_note-cbs-population-groups-7]
