# List of diplomatic missions of Argentina

&#x20; The Argentine Republic has the [15th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_number_of_diplomatic_missions) most [diplomatic missions](8970.md) of any country in the world,[^cite_note-1] with posts in 85 of the 193 [members states](31969.md) of the [United Nations](31769.md), as well as [observer states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_General_Assembly_observers) [Palestine](241405.md) and [Vatican City](32408.md), and [Taiwan](25734.md). Argentina's diplomatic network also has a very strong focus on the Americas. This list excludes honorary consulates.[^cite_note-Ar.MRECIC-Reps-2]
