**Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange** (**ADX**; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): سوق أبو ظبي للأوراق المالية) is a [stock exchange](28168.md) in [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md), [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) (UAE).

## History

It was established in November 2000 via Local Law No. (3). In January 2003, it was launched the ADX General Index.[^cite_note-1]

On March 17, 2020. According to Local Law No. (8), ADX transitioned from a public entity into a Public [Joint Stock Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint-stock_company) (PJSC). It became a subsidiary of ADQ.[^cite_note-2]

In January 2022, it transitioned to [FTSE Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FTSE_Russell)'s methodology, after which a newly branded index and sector groupings were introduced.[^cite_note-3]

### Impact of the 2026 Iran–United States conflict

Following the outbreak of the Iran conflict in March 2026, the ADX fell by approximately 9%, according to an [Al Jazeera](30204052.md) report.[^cite_note-4]
