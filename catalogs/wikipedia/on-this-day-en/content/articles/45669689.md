The **Kremlin press secretary** or officially **press secretary of the president of the Russian Federation** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Пресс-Секретарь Президента Российской Федерации) is a senior official in the [Presidential Administration of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Administration_of_Russia) whose primary responsibility is to act as [spokesperson](338047.md) for the Russian Federation [government administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_\(government\)), especially with regard to the [president](239646.md), senior executives, and policies.

## Responsibilities

The press secretary is responsible for collecting information about actions and events within the president's administration and around the world. The information includes items such as a summary of the president's schedule, whom the president has seen or had communication with, and the official position of the administration on the news. The press secretary interacts with the media, and deals with the [Kremlin pool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremlin_pool).
