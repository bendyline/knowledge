This article lists the **[prime ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister) of [Cape Verde](18962637.md)**, an [island country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_country) in the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md) off the coast of [West Africa](67393.md), since the [establishment of the office of prime minister in 1975](18962637.md#Independence_\(1975\)). [Pedro Pires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pires) was the first person to hold the office, taking effect on 8 July 1975. The incumbent is [Francisco Carvalho](83208316.md), having taken office on 19 June 2026.
