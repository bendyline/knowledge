# Dhanusha 1

**Dhanusha 1** is one of four parliamentary constituencies of [Dhanusha District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhanusha_District) in [Nepal](171166.md). This constituency came into existence on the Constituency Delimitation Commission (CDC) report submitted on 31 August 2017.[^cite_note-1]

## Incorporated areas

Dhanusha 1 incorporates [Ganeshman Charnath Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganeshman_Charanath), [Dhanushadham Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhanusadham), [Sabaila Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabaila,_Nepal), wards 1, 3, 4 and 5 of [Mithila Bihari Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mithila_Bihari), wards 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8 of [Hansapur Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hansapur,_Dhanusa), ward 4 of [Mithila Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mithila_Municipality) and wards 8 and 9 of [Chhirreshwarnath Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chhireshwarnath_Municipality)

## Assembly segments

It encompasses the following [Madhesh Provincial Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhesh_Provincial_Assembly) segment

- Dhanusha 1(A)

- Dhanusha 1(B)
