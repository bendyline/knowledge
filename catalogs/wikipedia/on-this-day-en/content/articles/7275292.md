**Remedios** is a town and [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality) in the [Colombian](5222.md) [department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Colombia) of [Antioquia](185251.md). Part of the subregion of [Northeastern Antioquia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_Antioquia), it lies at an altitude of 700 m (2,300 ft) above sea level.

## Notable people

- [Leonel Álvarez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonel_%C3%81lvarez_\(footballer,_born_1965\)), footballer.

- [Wilmar Roldán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilmar_Rold%C3%A1n), Colombian football referee.
