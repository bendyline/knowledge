**Los Gallardos** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[los ɣaˈʎaɾðos\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a [Spanish municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Spain) of the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Spain) of [Almería](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Almer%C3%ADa), in the [autonomous community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_community) of [Andalusia](2736.md).

## Wildfire

Main article: [2026 Los Gallardos wildfire](83658163.md)

In July 2026 [wildfire](56106.md) affected the area around Los Gallardos. The Spanish authorities at the time struggled to contain the fire.[^cite_note-1] This was described as one of Spain's worst-ever wildfires, with hundreds of firefighter working to extinguish remaining hotspots around the village of [Bédar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A9dar) in [Almería](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almer%C3%ADa).
