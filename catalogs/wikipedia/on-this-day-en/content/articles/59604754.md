**Jebrat al Sheikh** is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Sudan) of [North Kordofan](767669.md) state, [Sudan](27421.md).[^cite_note-\:sd-1][^cite_note-\:def-2]

In the [Sudanese civil war (2023–present)](73556699.md) the district and city centre was occupied by the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md). As part of the occupation by the RSF the main hospital was reportedly looted and turned into [barracks](1033489.md). [^cite_note-3]
