The **70th Academy Awards** ceremony, organized by the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences) (AMPAS), took place on March 23, 1998, at the [Shrine Auditorium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrine_Auditorium) in [Los Angeles](18110.md) beginning at 6\:00 p.m. [PST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Time_Zone) / 9\:00 p.m. [EST](496028.md). During the show, AMPAS presented [Academy Awards](324.md) (commonly referred to as Oscars) in 24 categories honoring [films released in 1997](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_in_film). The ceremony, which was televised in the United States by [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company), was produced by [Gil Cates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert_Cates) and directed by Louis J. Horvitz.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Actor [Billy Crystal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Crystal) hosted the show for the sixth time.[^cite_note-5] He had first hosted the [62nd ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/62nd_Academy_Awards) held in 1990, and most recently the [previous year's awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/69th_Academy_Awards).[^cite_note-6] Nearly a month earlier in an event held at the [Regent Beverly Wilshire Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverly_Wilshire_Hotel) in [Beverly Hills, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverly_Hills,_California) on February 28, the [Academy Awards for Technical Achievement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Technical_Achievement) were presented by host [Ashley Judd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Judd).[^cite_note-7]

*[Titanic](52371.md)* won 11 awards, including [Best Picture](61702.md), a number that is tied with *[Ben-Hur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Hur_\(1959_film\))* and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Other winners included *[As Good as It Gets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_Good_as_It_Gets)*, *[Good Will Hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Will_Hunting)*, and *[L.A. Confidential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.A._Confidential_\(film\))* with two awards, and *[Character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character_\(film\))*, *[The Full Monty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Full_Monty)*, *[Geri's Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geri's_Game)*, *[The Long Way Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Long_Way_Home_\(1997_film\))*, *[Men in Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\))*, *[A Story of Healing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Story_of_Healing)*, and *[Visas and Virtue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visas_and_Virtue)* with one. The telecast garnered more than 57 million viewers in the United States, making it the most-watched Oscars broadcast in history.[^cite_note-10]

## Winners and nominees

The nominees for the 70th Academy Awards were announced on February 10, 1998, at the Samuel Goldwyn Theater in Beverly Hills, California, by [Robert Rehme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Rehme), president of the academy, and actress [Geena Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geena_Davis).[^cite_note-cnnnom-11] *[Titanic](52371.md)* led all nominees with fourteen nominations, tying with *[All About Eve](41881.md)* (1950) for the most Oscar nominations in history.[^cite_note-13] *[Good Will Hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Will_Hunting)* and *[L.A. Confidential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.A._Confidential_\(film\))* came in second with nine apiece.[^cite_note-14]

The winners were announced during the awards ceremony on March 23, 1998. With eleven awards, *Titanic* tied with *[Ben-Hur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Hur_\(1959_film\))* (1959) for the most Academy Awards in Oscar history.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-Tie2-17] It also became the first film to win Best Picture without a screenwriting nomination since *[The Sound of Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sound_of_Music_\(film\))* (1965).[^cite_note-18] Due to *Titanic*, [James Cameron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron) became the first person to win as a producer, director and editor in one night.[^cite_note-Variety-19] [Jack Nicholson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Nicholson) received his eleventh acting nomination, becoming the most nominated man in acting categories.[^cite_note-cnnnom-11] He also became the fourth performer to win at least [three acting Oscars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_actors_with_two_or_more_Academy_Awards_in_acting_categories).[^cite_note-Variety-19] Both Nicholson and [Helen Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Hunt) won for their roles in *[As Good as It Gets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_Good_as_It_Gets)*, making it the seventh film to win both lead acting awards.[^cite_note-Variety-19] Nominated for their performances as Rose DeWitt Bukater in *Titanic*, Best Actress nominee [Kate Winslet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Winslet) and Best Supporting Actress nominee [Gloria Stuart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Stuart) became the first pair of performers nominated for portraying the same character in the same film.[^cite_note-cnnnom-11] At the age of 87, Stuart became the [oldest acting nominee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_oldest_and_youngest_Academy_Award_winners_and_nominees) in Oscars history.[^cite_note-20]

## Ceremony information

Photo of Billy Crystal in 2012.

[Billy Crystal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Crystal) hosted the 70th Academy Awards.

In December 1997, the academy hired veteran Oscar telecast producer [Gil Cates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gil_Cates) to oversee the 1998 ceremony.[^cite_note-Cates-26] "Gil has become the consummate Oscar show producer, consistently garnering top television ratings for the telecast," said AMPAS President Robert Rehme in a press release announcing the selection. "His shows are full of wit, charm and surprise."[^cite_note-Cates-26] A few days later, actor and comedian Billy Crystal was chosen to emcee the upcoming telecast. Cates explained his reason to bring back the veteran comedian saying, "Billy's performance last year was spectacular. There is nobody like him."[^cite_note-27] In an article published in *[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today)* he initially requested to Cates and AMPAS five months after the [previous year's ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/69th_Academy_Awards) that he would like to take a break from hosting duties. However, pressure from the academy, Cates, and several friends and family members made him reconsider his decision.[^cite_note-28] His sixth stint would make him second only to [Bob Hope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Hope) in number of ceremonies hosted.[^cite_note-29] For the first time, the show was procuded by AMPAS, not [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company), because of the failure in reaching a contract agreement with the [National Association of Broadcast Employees and Technicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Association_of_Broadcast_Employees_and_Technicians).[^cite_note-30]

To commemorate the seventieth anniversary of the Academy Awards, 70 actors who have received both competitive and honorary awards appeared seated onstage together during a segment called Oscar's Family Album.[^cite_note-31] Each former winner was acknowledged by announcer [Norman Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Rose) with the films he or she won for. At the end of the segment newly minted winners Kim Basinger, Helen Hunt, and Robin Williams joined them. This marked the largest gathering of former winners since the [50th ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/50th_Academy_Awards) held in 1978.[^cite_note-32] The Academy also commissioned musician and composer [Jerry Goldsmith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Goldsmith) to compose a new [leitmotif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leitmotif) for the Oscars telecast entitled "[Fanfare for Oscar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanfare_for_Oscar)".[^cite_note-33]

Several others participated in the production of the ceremony. [Bill Conti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Conti) served as musical director for the telecast.[^cite_note-34] Dancer [Daniel Ezralow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ezralow) choreographed a dance number showcasing the nominees for Best Original Comedy or Musical Score. [Bart the Bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bart_the_Bear) made a surprise appearance during the presentation of the [Best Sound Effects Editing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Sound_Editing) award with [Mike Myers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Myers).[^cite_note-35]

### Box office performance of nominees

At the time of the nominations announcement on February 10, the combined gross of the five Best Picture nominees was \$579 million with an average of \$116 million per film.[^cite_note-BoxOfficeOscars-36] *Titanic* was the highest earner among the Best Picture nominees with \$338.7 million in domestic box office receipts.[^cite_note-BoxOfficeOscars-36] The film was followed by *As Good as It Gets* (\$92.6 million), *Good Will Hunting* (\$68.9 million), *L.A. Confidential* (\$39.7 million), and finally *The Full Monty* (\$38.7 million).[^cite_note-BoxOfficeOscars-36]

Of the top 50 grossing movies of the year, 40 nominations went to 15 films on the list. Only *Titanic* (1st), *As Good as It Gets* (16th), *Good Will Hunting* (20th), and *In & Out* (24th) were nominated for directing, acting, screenwriting, or Best Picture.[^cite_note-boxofficemojooscars-37] The other top 50 box office hits that earned nominations were *Men in Black* (2nd), *The Lost World: Jurassic Park* (3rd), *Air Force One* (5th), *My Best Friends Wedding* (7th), *Face/Off* (9th), *Con Air* (12th), *Contact* (13th), *Hercules* (14th), *The Fifth Element* (25th), *Anastasia* (30th), and *Starship Troopers* (34th).[^cite_note-boxofficemojooscars-37]

### Critical response

The show received a positive reception from most media publications. Television critic [Howard Rosenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Rosenberg) of the *[Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times)* lauded Crystal's performance writing that he "would earn top billing as that unusual comedian as artful at doing musical comedy as jokes."[^cite_note-38] *[San Francisco Chronicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Chronicle)* columnist John Carman raved,"It was the best Oscar show in two decades." He also gave high marks for the host, commenting, "But last night, Crystal was back in razor form."[^cite_note-39] *[The Seattle Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Seattle_Times)* television editor Kay McFadden praised Crystal commenting that "he possesses nearly impeccable timing and judgment." In addition, she noted that while the ceremony dragged on, "Last night was one of television's smartest live ceremonies in recent memory."[^cite_note-40]

Some media outlets were more critical of the show. Ray Richmond of *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\))* complained that the ceremony proved to be a "Yawner of an Oscarcast." He added that Crystal's "off-the-cuff one-liners sank faster than the great ship herself."[^cite_note-41] *[Boston Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Globe)* television critic Matthew Gilbert bemoaned,"There was hardly a spontaneous moment during last night's Oscarcast."[^cite_note-42] Film critic Carrie Rickey from *[The Philadelphia Inquirer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Philadelphia_Inquirer)* lamented that the inevitable *Titanic* sweep "sank a telecast loaded with montages of previous years' Oscar highlights."[^cite_note-43]

### Ratings and reception

The American telecast on ABC drew in an average of 57.25 million people over its length, which was a 29% increase from the [previous year's ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/69th_Academy_Awards).[^cite_note-44] An estimated 87.50 million total viewers watched all or part of the awards.[^cite_note-45] The show also earned higher [Nielsen ratings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen_ratings) compared to the previous ceremony with 35.32% of households watching over a 55.77 share.[^cite_note-TVB_Oscars-46] In addition, it garnered a higher 18–49 demo rating with a 24.90 rating over a 44.30 share among viewers in that demographic.[^cite_note-TVB_Oscars-46] It was the most watched Oscar ceremony in television history since viewership figures were compiled beginning with the [46th ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/46th_Academy_Awards) in 1974.[^cite_note-47]

In July 1998, the ceremony presentation received eight nominations at the [50th Primetime Emmys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/50th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards).[^cite_note-48] Two months later, the ceremony won five of those nominations for [Outstanding Individual Performance in a Variety or Music Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Individual_Performance_in_a_Variety_or_Music_Program) (Billy Crystal), Outstanding Directing for a Variety or Music Program (Louis J Horvitz), Outstanding Lighting Direction (Electronic) for a Drama Series, Variety Series, Miniseries, or Movie (Bob Barnhart, Robert Dickinson, Matt Ford, Andy O'Reilly), Outstanding Music Direction (Bill Conti), and Outstanding Sound Mixing for a Variety Series or Special (Patrick Baltzell, Robert Douglass, Edward J. Greene, Tommy Vicari).[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50]
