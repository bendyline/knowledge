**Golestan province**[^cite_note-9] is one of the 31 [provinces of Iran](448160.md), located in the northeast of the country and southeast of the [Caspian Sea](19653787.md). Its capital is the city of [Gorgan](84622.md),[^cite_note-Golestan_Province_Structure-4] formerly called Esterabad until 1937. Golestan was split off from [Mazandaran Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazandaran_Province) in 1997.

Golestan province borders [Turkmenistan](198149.md) to the north. One of the key border points between the two countries is Incheh Borun, which serves as a vital crossing point.[^cite_note-10]

The province was made a part of Region 1 upon the division of the provinces into [5 regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Iran), solely for coordination and development purposes, on 22 June 2014.[^cite_note-ReferenceA-3] The majority of its population are Shia Muslims.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Etymology

Gulistan, Golestan, or Golastan translates to "gul-" meaning "flower" and "-stan" meaning "land" or "region." Golestan, therefore, literally means "land of flowers" in [Iranian languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_languages) (e.g., [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language), [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language), and [Mazandarani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazandarani_language)). This is a common toponym in countries with Persian linguistic roots (see [Gulistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulistan_\(disambiguation\))).

The capital of Gorgan derives its name from a wider region known historically as [*Gorgân*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyrcania) ([گرگان](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%DA%AF%D8%B1%DA%AF%D8%A7%D9%86)), [Middle Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Persian) *Gurgān*, and [Old Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Persian) *Varkāna* (in the [Behistun Inscription](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behistun_Inscription)) meaning "land of [wolves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf)".[^cite_note-Iranica-13] This is also the root of the [Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek) Ὑρκανία (*Hyrkanía*) and [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) *Hyrcania*. Wild wolves are still found in the province.

## History



Map of the Abbasid Caliphate showing Gorgan province, forerunner to modern-day Golestan province

Human settlements in this area date back to 10,000 BC. Evidence of the ancient city of *Jorjan* can still be seen near the current city of [Gonbad-e Kavus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonbad-e_Qabus_\(city\)). It was an important city of Persia located on the [Silk Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silk_Road).

Under [Achaemenid Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaemenid_Iran), it seems to have been administered as a sub-province of [Parthia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parthia) and is not named separately in the provincial lists of [Darius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darius_the_great) and [Xerxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xerxes_I_of_Persia). The Hyrcanians, however, under the leadership of Megapanus, are mentioned by [Herodotus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herodotus)[^cite_note-14] in his list of Xerxes' army during the invasion of [Greece](12108.md).[^cite_note-Iranica-13]

## Demographics

### Language and ethnicity



Linguistic Map of Golestan province

- [Turkmens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkmens): 34.20%

- [Mazandaranis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazandaranis): 30.40%

- [Sistanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sistanis) & [Persians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persians): 14.90%

- [Baluchis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baluchis): 10.90%

- [Qizilbash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qizilbash): 7.30%

- Others (including [Azeris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azeris), [Kazakhs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhs), [Kurds](17068.md), [Armenians](387816.md) and [Georgians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgians)): 2.3%

The [Mazandaranis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazandarani_people) who inhabit the foothills to [Shah Pasand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shah_Pasand) were subsumed under the rubric "Persian" by these official statistics.

The Persians/Mazandaranis are considered by nearly all inhabitants of the province to be "the old natives" while all others are considered ethnic immigrants in the past.

Most [Mazandaranis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazandarani_people) live in [Gorgan](84622.md), [Ali Abad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliabad-e_Katul), [Kordkuy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kordkuy), [Bandar-e Gaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandar-e_Gaz) and [Gonbad-e Kavus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonbad-e_Qabus_\(city\)). They speak [Mazanderani language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazanderani_language).[^cite_note-iranicaonline.org-16][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-زبان_تبری-15][^cite_note-ReferenceB-17][^cite_note-19]

The [Turkmens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkmen_people) reside in the north of the province, a plain called [Turkmen Sahra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkmen_Sahra). Since the 15th century, these formerly nomadic people have lived in this area, the main cities of which are [Gonbad-e Kavus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonbad-e_Qabus_\(city\)) and [Bandar Torkaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandar_Torkaman). [Turkmens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkmen_people) are [Sunni Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Muslim). They form a sizable minority in cities such as [Gorgan](84622.md), [Ali Abad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliabad-e_Katul), [Kalaleh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalaleh_County), and many eastern townships.

Azeris and the Qizilbash predate the Turkmen by centuries and have in time become completely [Shia](26961.md), and therefore, culturally associated with the Persians and other Shias.

The Sistani Persians and the Baluch are relatively recent arrivals and date back to the early 20th century. They are still arriving in the area in some numbers due to the lasting drought in their home areas of [Sistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sistan).

The people of Kordkuy are originally from the Kurdish areas of west Iran, [Kermanshah](448078.md) and [Kurdistan](448112.md) provinces. They belong to the Kord-rostami tribe and are also found in significant numbers in other cities like Gorgan and Bandare Gaz. Subtribes of the Kord-Rostami tribe are the Aghili and Sepanlou tribes. Moreover, the city of [Maraveh Tappeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maraveh_Tappeh) has a mixed population of Sunni [Turkmens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Turkmens) and Shia [Kurds](3821779.md).[^cite_note-20]

Other ethnic groups such as [Kazakhs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Kazakhs), [Khorasani Kurds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khorasani_Kurds), [Georgians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgians_in_Iran), and [Armenians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenians_in_Iran) also reside in this area, and have preserved their traditions and rituals.

A small minority of followers of the [Baháʼí faith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bah%C3%A1%CA%BC%C3%AD_Faith) also live in Golestan (in [Shahpasand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azadshahr)).[^cite_note-hamshahrionline.ir-7][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the province's population was 1,593,055 in 379,354 households.[^cite_note-2006_Golestan_Province-23] The following census in 2011 showed an increase in population to 1,777,014 in 482,842 households.[^cite_note-2011_Golestan_Province-24] The 2016 census measured the population of the province as 1,868,819 in 550,249 households.[^cite_note-2016_Golestan_Province-6]

## Culture

The world's tallest brick structure of its kind, the [Gonbad-e Qabus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonbad-e_Qabus_\(tower\)) tower, stands in this province.

## Climate and geography

Golestan enjoys mild weather and a temperate climate most of the year. Geographically, it is divided into two sections: The plains, and the mountains of the [Alborz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alborz) range. In the eastern Alborz section, the direction of the mountains faces northeast and gradually decreases in height. The highest point of the province is *Shavar*, with a height of 3,945 meters. Two of the main rivers are the [Gharasu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gharasu_River) and [Gorganrud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorganrud_River).

### Golestan National Park

See also: [Geography of Iran](64697.md) and [Environmental issues in Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_issues_in_Iran)

[Golestan National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golestan_National_Park) in northern Iran is faced with the construction of a road through the [forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Clouds_Forest), allegedly for the ease of traffic for the villagers and woodmen but at the expense of losing the only national park in Iran throughout which a range of different climates (humidity near the Caspian Sea and desert farther south) is spread.

Golestan National Park is Iran's biggest national park which spans three provinces, Golestan, Mazandaran, and North Khorasan. Surprisingly, the authorities ignore repeated calls by experts to construct such roads around, instead of through, the forests, which in this way would no longer threaten animal and plant life.

The park has a significant biodiversity. Its fauna includes wild deer, bears, and small deer. However, with the exception of wild boars, the ungulate population has declined by 68–89% between 1970–1978 and 2011–2014 due to poaching, according to a study published in 2017.[^cite_note-33] The park covers 5% of Iran's forests, and at least 1,362 plant species have been recorded there. The western forest zone, which is typical of the Hyrcanian mountain range, is home to numerous broad-leaved trees - including remnant species such as oak and maple - while the eastern steppe zone is mainly composed of evergreen shrubs such as juniper and artemisia.[^cite_note-Ershadi-34][^cite_note-naturalia-35]
