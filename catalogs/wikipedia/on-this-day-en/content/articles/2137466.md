**Itogon**, officially the **Municipality of Itogon**, ([Ilocano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_language): *Ili ti Itogon*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Itogon*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Benguet](274018.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 59,736 people.[^cite_note-5]

It is a [mining town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining_town), being the site of the first large-scale [mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining) operations in the country.[^cite_note-Inquirer-6][^cite_note-DILG-CAR-7][^cite_note-ItogonGov-8] The town is also the site of [Binga Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binga_Dam),[^cite_note-NAPOCOR-9] managed and operated by the SN Aboitiz Power - Benguet, Inc.

## History

### Spanish period

During the [Spanish Regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Philippines_\(1521-1898\)), a native of the historic [pueblo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pueblo) of *Itogon* (or *Itokhon*), named *Codeng*, was appointed by the Spanish authorities as *capitan* of another nearby village, *Balingway* (currently *Itogon Central/Proper*[^cite_note-OntheBasis-10]).[^cite_note-DILG-CAR-7][^cite_note-ItogonGov-8] Balingway was later established as a town site and renamed after *Codeng'*&#x73; native place.[^cite_note-DILG-CAR-7]

### American period

During the [American rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Philippines_\(1898-1946\)), Itogon was established as one of the 19 townships of the province of Benguet, upon the issuance of *Act No. 48* by the [Philippine Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Commission) on November 22, 1900.[^cite_note-DILG-CAR-7][^cite_note-NSCBGov-11][^cite_note-BenguetGov-12]

[Mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining) operations started in Itogon in 1903, after [Benguet Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benguet_Corporation), the Philippines' first mining firm, was established in the town under the name, [Benguet Consolidated Mining Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benguet_Consolidated_Mining_Company?action=edit\&redlink=1) (BCMC), by Americans *Nelson Peterson* and *Harry Clyde*.[^cite_note-Inquirer-6][^cite_note-ABS-CBNNews-13]

On August 13, 1908, Benguet was established as a sub-province of the newly created [Mountain Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_Province) with the enactment of *Act No. 1876*. As a result, six townships of Benguet were abolished, but Itogon remained a constituent town of Benguet sub-province.[^cite_note-BenguetGov-12]

### World War II

Guerrilla forces in Northern Luzon launch a successful attack on the Itogon Mining District on October 15, 1942, drawing Japanese attention. This results in the Japanese pouring more troops in Luzon Island.

### Post-war era

In 1948, plans by the [National Power Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Power_Corporation) (NAPOCOR) for a second [dam](51518.md) construction along the [Agno River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agno_River) in the province of Benguet started, after the [Ambuklao Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambuklao_Dam) construction commenced in [Bokod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bokod,_Benguet). Bulldozers started clearing the forested area of the Guissit Mountains in 1954 for the [Binga Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binga_Dam). Construction of the dam took 3 years and 9 months, from August 1956 until its formal operations opening in May 1960.[^cite_note-ItogonGov-8][^cite_note-NAPOCOR-9]

On May 15, 1951, the town was converted into a [regular municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_the_Philippines) from the former municipal district of the same name, with the enactment of *[Republic Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_Act) No. 616*.[^cite_note-PhilLaw-14]

On June 18, 1966, the sub-province of Benguet was separated from the old [Mountain Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_Province) and was converted into a [regular province](165100.md). Itogon remained to be a component municipality of the newly established province.[^cite_note-BenguetGov-12][^cite_note-RA4695-ChanRobles-15]

## Geography



A forest in Mount Ulap

The Municipality of Itogon is located at [16°22′N 120°40′E  /  16.37°N 120.67°E  / 16.37; 120.67](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Itogon\&params=16.37_N_120.67_E_), at the southeast end of the Benguet, forming a border with the provinces of [Nueva Vizcaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nueva_Vizcaya) (on the east) and [Pangasinan](1691800.md) (on the south). The town is bounded by [Baguio](162480.md) and the municipality of [Tuba](2137478.md) on the west, [La Trinidad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Trinidad,_Benguet) and [Tublay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tublay,_Benguet) on the north-west, [Bokod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bokod,_Benguet) on the north-east, [Kayapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayapa,_Nueva_Vizcaya) and [Santa Fe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Fe,_Nueva_Vizcaya) on the southeast, [San Manuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Manuel,_Pangasinan) and [San Nicolas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Nicolas,_Pangasinan) on the south, and [Sison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sison,_Pangasinan) on the south-west.

According to the [Philippine Statistics Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Statistics_Authority), the municipality has a land area of 449.73 square kilometres (173.64 sq mi) [^cite_note-nscb-area-16] constituting 16.24% of the 2,769.08-square-kilometre- (1,069.15 sq mi) total area of Benguet. It is the largest municipality in Benguet by land area. Around 80 percent of the municipality's land area is protected as part of the [Lower Agno Watershed Forest Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Agno_Watershed_Forest_Reserve) and [Upper Agno River Basin Resource Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Agno_River_Basin_Resource_Reserve). The [Agno River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agno_River) traverses the municipality and is impounded at Binga (19 kilometres or 12 miles from the [Ambuklao Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambuklao_Dam) in [Bokod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bokod,_Benguet)) forming the [Binga Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binga_Dam).[^cite_note-NAPOCOR-9]

Itogon is situated 21.74 kilometres (13.51 mi) from the provincial capital [La Trinidad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Trinidad,_Benguet), and 255.01 kilometres (158.46 mi) from the country's capital city of [Manila](184334.md).

In the 2024 census, Itogon had a population of 59,736 people.[^cite_note-25] The population density was 130 inhabitants per square kilometre (340/sq mi).

Generally inhabited by 60 percent [Ibalois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibaloi_people), 40 percent [Kankanaey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankanaey_people), and other ethnicities such as the [Ilocano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_people), Itogon was the most populous municipality in the province, with a population of 61,773 in the 1990 census. Its population abruptly declined five years after as illustrated in the census of 1995.[^cite_note-LWUA-PopulationData-24]

## Government

Itogon, belonging to the [lone congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benguet's_at-large_congressional_district) of the province of [Benguet](274018.md), is governed by a mayor designated as its local chief executive and by a municipal council as its legislative body in accordance with the Local Government Code. The mayor, vice mayor, and the councilors are elected directly by the people through an election which is being held every three years.

## Tourism

Former seal of Itogon

Known tourist destination areas in Itogon include the [Binga Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binga_Dam) in Tinongdan and Balatoc Mines Tours in Balatoc, Virac. ## Education

There are two schools districe offices which govern all educational institutions within the municipality. They oversee the management and operations of all private and public, from primary to secondary schools.[^cite_note-39] These are Itogon I Schools District Office, and Itogon II Schools District Office.

### Public schools

As of 2014, Itogon has 39 [public](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_school) [elementary schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_school) and 7 public [secondary schools](554992.md).[^cite_note-DepEdElem-40][^cite_note-DepEdSecon-41][^cite_note-DepEd2014Elem-42]
