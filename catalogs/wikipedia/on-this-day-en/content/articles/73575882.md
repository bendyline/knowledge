The **National News Agency** (**NNA**; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الوكالة الوطنية للإعلام) is the official [news agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_agency) of the [government of Lebanon](17775.md), located at the headquarters of the [Ministry of Information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Information_\(Lebanon\)) in [Hamra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamra,_Beirut), [Beirut](37428.md). Established in 1961, the domestic and international news publisher publishes in Arabic, English and French.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-server-2]

As of 2023, the NNA has about 300 employees working at more than 20 offices across Lebanon[^cite_note-server-2] and is an active member of the [Federation of Arab News Agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Arab_News_Agencies) (FANA).[^cite_note-3]

## Stolen photo archive

On 27 February 2023, a server, which contained photos of events since 1961, and five computers were reportedly stolen from the agency's archive room; the archive had pictures of the country's [1975–1990 civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Civil_War). Later, Minister [Ziad Makary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziad_Makary), who had described the theft as a "crime of national proportions", said that only part of the photo archive was taken, and the ministry still had the database.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-server-2]
