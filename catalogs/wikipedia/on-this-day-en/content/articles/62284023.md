The **2027 FIFA Women's World Cup qualification** process determines the 31 teams joining hosts [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_women's_national_football_team) in the [2027 FIFA Women's World Cup](60983910.md). It is the tenth [FIFA Women's World Cup](244862.md), the quadrennial international [women's football](191466.md) world championship tournament. The tournament is the first Women's World Cup to be hosted by a [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL) member association and the second to be held in the [Southern Hemisphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Hemisphere), after the previous edition in [2023](45271353.md).

Qualification began on 19 February 2025 with two matches of [the CAF zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Women's_Africa_Cup_of_Nations_qualification#First_round) played that day. The first goal of the qualification series was scored by [Algerian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algeria_women's_national_football_team) player [Laura Taleb Muller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Taleb_Muller) against [South Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Sudan_women's_national_football_team).

## Slot allocation

The allocation of slots for the final tournament was approved by the [FIFA Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Council) on 10 December 2024.[^cite_note-allocation-1] The slot for the host nation was taken directly from CONMEBOL's allocation.

## Qualification process

### Summary of qualification

FIFA's confederations organise their qualifications either through continental championships or separate qualifying competitions. Hosts [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_women's_national_football_team) qualified automatically for the tournament, and all eligible remaining [FIFA member associations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_women's_national_association_football_teams) could enter qualification if they chose to do so.

Qualifying matches began on 19 February 2025 and are scheduled to end in February 2027.

### Suspensions and withdrawals

[Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia_women's_national_football_team) were suspended indefinitely on 28 February 2022 from participating in UEFA and FIFA competitions due to [their country's invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine).[^cite_note-FIFA_RUS_PR-2][^cite_note-UEFA_RUS_PR-3] The suspension was still in effect when UEFA finalised its qualifying process, and Russia were excluded from European qualification.[^cite_note-4]

[Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congo_women's_national_football_team) withdrew prior to playing any matches, citing a lack of preparation.[^cite_note-5]

[Chad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chad_women's_national_football_team) withdrew prior to playing any matches, citing delays in the disbursement process of the funds needed for match preparations and a lack of funding.[^cite_note-6]

## Confederation qualification

FIFA retained the slot allocation from the [previous edition](45271353.md). [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_women's_national_football_team) automatically qualified as hosts, and they took one of the slots allocated to [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL).

### AFC

Main article: [2026 AFC Women's Asian Cup](67991141.md)

As in 2023, the AFC Women's Asian Cup served as the qualifying competition to determine the Asian representatives at the Women's World Cup. The qualifying process was as follows:

- Qualifying stage: Nations were drawn into eight groups of four or five teams to determine the eight nations joining the three top-ranked teams from the [2022 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_AFC_Women's_Asian_Cup) ([China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_women's_national_football_team), [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea_women's_national_football_team) and [Japan](3190583.md)) and hosts [Australia](30864460.md) for the final tournament. Groups B–H competed from 23 June – 5 July 2025, while Group A was postponed until 7–19 July due to the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War).

  [^cite_note-Group_A_Delay-7]

  [^cite_note-Group_A_Host-8]

- Final tournament: The final tournament took place from 1–21 March 2026. Twelve nations were drawn into groups of four teams each to play single [round-robin](939742.md) matches. The group winners and runners-up and the two best-ranked third-place teams advanced to the knockout phase. The four quarter-final winners qualified directly for the 2027 Women's World Cup, while the four quarter-final losers competed to determine the final two direct qualifiers, and the two Asian representatives in the [inter-confederation play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\)).

### CAF

Main article: [2026 Women's Africa Cup of Nations](78603473.md)

As in 2023, the Women's Africa Cup of Nations serves as the qualifying competition to determine the African representatives at the Women's World Cup. The qualifying process is as follows:

- Qualifying stage: Qualification for the 2026 Women's Africa Cup of Nations took place over two rounds during the international windows of 19–26 February 2025 and 22–28 October 2025. Eleven nations advanced to join hosts Morocco in the final tournament.

  [^cite_note-9]

  &#x20;After the conclusion of qualifying, CAF announced four teams defeated in the second round had advanced based on the [FIFA Women's World Ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Women's_World_Ranking).

  [^cite_note-10]

- Final tournament: The final tournament will be held 26 July – 16 August 2026.

  [^cite_note-WAFCON_rescheduled-11]

  &#x20;Sixteen nations were drawn into four groups of four teams each to play single [round-robin](939742.md) matches. The group winners and runners-up will advance to the knockout phase, with the four quarter-final winners qualifying directly for the 2027 Women's World Cup, while the four quarter-final losers will compete to determine the two African representatives in the [inter-confederation play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\)).

### CONCACAF

Main article: [2026 CONCACAF W Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_CONCACAF_W_Championship)

As in 2023, the CONCACAF W Championship serves as the qualifying competition to determine the North American, Central American, and Caribbean representatives at the Women's World Cup. The qualifying process is as follows:[^cite_note-12]

- Qualifying stage: Twenty-nine nations were drawn into five groups of five teams each and one group of four teams to play matches during the [international windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_International_Match_Calendar#Women's_International_Match_Calendar) in November/December 2025, and February/March and April 2026. The six group winners advanced to join the top two CONCACAF teams in the FIFA Women's World Ranking

  [^cite_note-13]

  &#x20;– [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_women's_national_soccer_team) and the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_women's_national_soccer_team) – for the final tournament.

- Final tournament: Eight nations will compete in a single-elimination knockout tournament in November 2026. The four quarter-final winners will qualify directly for the 2027 Women's World Cup, and the four quarter-final losers will compete to determine the two CONCACAF representatives in the [inter-confederation play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\)).

### OFC

Main article: [2027 FIFA Women's World Cup qualification (OFC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_qualification_\(OFC\))

The OFC held its first stand-alone qualification tournament. The qualification format was announced on 8 August 2025:[^cite_note-15]

- The four lowest-ranked teams competed in a [single-elimination tournament](939680.md) in the Cook Islands on 28 November and 1 December 2025. The winner advanced to the second round.

- The first round winner joined the seven highest-ranked teams in the second round. In August 2025, the eight teams were divided into two groups of four teams to play a single-leg [round-robin tournament](939742.md) between 27 February and 8 March 2026. The group winners and runners-up advanced to the third round.

- The four teams advancing from the second round competed in a single-elimination tournament between 12 and 15 April 2026. The winner qualified for the [2027 FIFA Women's World Cup](60983910.md), and the runner-up advanced to the [inter-confederation play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\)).

### UEFA

Main article: [2027 FIFA Women's World Cup qualification (UEFA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_qualification_\(UEFA\))

As in 2023, UEFA is holding a stand-alone qualification tournament to determine the European representatives at the Women's World Cup. The qualifying process is as follows:

- League phase: Fifty-three nations were divided into three leagues based on the [2025 UEFA Women's Nations League overall phase ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_UEFA_Women's_Nations_League#Overall_ranking). Each league consisted of groups of three or four teams playing a [league format](939742.md) from 3 March to 9 June 2026. Four teams qualified directly for the 2027 Women's World Cup and 32 advanced to the play-off phase.

- Play-off phase: Thirty-two teams will play two rounds of home-and-away elimination matches to determine the final seven European nations qualifying directly for the Women's World Cup and the European representative in the [inter-confederation play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\)). Round 1 matches will be held in October 2026 with round 2 matches in November and December 2026.

## Inter-confederation play-offs

Main article: [2027 FIFA Women's World Cup qualification (inter-confederation play-offs)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\))

Ten teams will advance to a play-off tournament to determine the final three teams to qualify for the Women's World Cup: two teams each from [AFC](715249.md), [CAF](715265.md), [CONCACAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF), and [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL), and one team each from [OFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceania_Football_Confederation) and [UEFA](32332.md). Two rounds of matches will take place, with the teams from CONCACAF and UEFA receiving a bye based on the results of the [2023 play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\)) and the CONMEBOL team ranked higher in the [FIFA Women's World Ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Women's_World_Ranking) receiving a bye based on Brazil hosting the final tournament. The lower-ranked team from CONMEBOL and the teams from AFC, CAF, and OFC will play a series of matches in November–December 2026 with the top two teams advancing to the second round. The second round will consist of three single-elimination matches in February 2027 with teams from the same confederation barred from facing each other. The winners of these matches will qualify for the final tournament.[^cite_note-allocation-1]
