# Tennis in Italy



[Jannik Sinner](60061043.md) is currently the top Italian player in the [ATP rankings](13480983.md).



[Jasmine Paolini](46663470.md) is currently the top Italian player in the [WTA rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_rankings).

**Tennis in Italy** is governed by the [Italian Tennis and Padel Federation](66675069.md) and in 2012 it was the 6th sport by number of practitioners, with 1,298,000 people.[^cite_note-1]

## History

One Italian player, [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md), has been men's singles world No. 1 ([ATP ranking](13480983.md)).

Five Italian players have won [Grand Slam](197638.md) tournaments: [Nicola Pietrangeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicola_Pietrangeli) ([1959](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1959_French_Championships_\(tennis\)) and [1960 French Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960_French_Championships_\(tennis\))), [Adriano Panatta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adriano_Panatta) ([1976 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_French_Open)), [Francesca Schiavone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesca_Schiavone) ([2010 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_French_Open)), [Flavia Pennetta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavia_Pennetta) ([2015 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_US_Open_\(tennis\))) and Jannik Sinner ([2024 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Australian_Open), [2024 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_\(tennis\)), [2025 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Australian_Open), [2025 Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Wimbledon_Championships) and [2026 Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Wimbledon?action=edit\&redlink=1)).

Four Italian players have won an Olympic medal: [Uberto De Morpurgo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uberto_De_Morpurgo) and [Lorenzo Musetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Musetti) won a bronze medal in the men's singles, respectively in [1924](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_1924_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) and [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), and the [Sara Errani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Errani)–[Jasmine Paolini](46663470.md) duo won a gold medal in the women's doubles, again in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles).

The [men's national team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_Davis_Cup_team) won the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup) four times ([1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_Davis_Cup), [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Davis_Cup), [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Davis_Cup) and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Davis_Cup)) and the [women's national team](11233225.md) won the [Billie Jean King Cup](1108893.md) six times ([2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Fed_Cup), [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Fed_Cup), [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Fed_Cup), [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Fed_Cup), [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Billie_Jean_King_Cup) and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Billie_Jean_King_Cup)).

Women's tennis in particular has seen tremendous successes in the 21st century, with the advent of [Francesca Schiavone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesca_Schiavone), Sara Errani, [Flavia Pennetta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavia_Pennetta) and [Roberta Vinci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberta_Vinci). All four of these women reached the top 10 of the WTA rankings in both singles and doubles (with Schiavone and Errani reaching the top 5 in singles and Pennetta, Errani and Vinci reaching No 1 in doubles), and reached Grand Slam finals in both disciplines. Schiavone and Pennetta won singles Grand Slam titles, while Pennetta, Errani and Vinci won doubles Grand Slam titles, with Errani and Vinci achieving the [Career Grand Slam](197638.md#Career_Grand_Slam) in doubles as a pair. They were also responsible for Italy's 4 Fed Cup victories as members of the national Fed Cup team.
