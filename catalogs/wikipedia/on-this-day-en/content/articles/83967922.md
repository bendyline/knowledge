**Johannes Wilhelm Liebmann** (born 21 March 2007) is a German [swimmer](22425760.md).

## Career

Liebmann competed at the [2024 European Junior Swimming Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_European_Junior_Swimming_Championships) and won silver medals in the 4×200 metre freestyle relay and 1500 metre freestyle, and bronze medals in the 400 metre and 800 metre freestyle events. He again competed at the [2025 European Junior Swimming Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_European_Junior_Swimming_Championships) and won silver medals in the 400 metre and 1500 metre freestyle and a bronze medal in the 800 metre freestyle.[^cite_note-1]

In December 2025, he competed at the [2025 European Short Course Swimming Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_European_Short_Course_Swimming_Championships) and set a [world junior record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_world_junior_records_in_swimming) time in the 800 metre freestyle heats with a time of 7\:30.47.[^cite_note-2] In August 2026, he competed at the [2026 European Aquatics Championships](83055803.md) and won a gold medal in the [800 metre freestyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2026_European_Aquatics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_800_metre_freestyle) with a [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_European_records_in_swimming) and [European Championships record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_European_Championships_records_in_swimming) time of 7\:37.59.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] He also won a gold medal in the [1500 metre freestyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2026_European_Aquatics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_1500_metre_freestyle) with a [world record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_world_records_in_swimming) time of 14\:26.79, taking 3.88 seconds off [Bobby Finke](54696773.md)'s world record from the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]
