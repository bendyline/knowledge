The **Buyan class** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Буян, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) '[Buyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buyan)'), Russian designation **Project 21630 *Buyan***, are a series of [corvettes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corvette) (small artillery in Russian classification) developed by [Zelenodolsk Design Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zelenodolsk_Design_Bureau\&action=edit\&redlink=1) for the [Russian Navy](383258.md)'s [Caspian Flotilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caspian_Flotilla). The Buyan-class ships are primarily designed for operations within [littoral zones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Littoral_zone) to protect Russia's vast coastal areas. Due to the small tonnage, they can operate even within shallow parts of oceans and seas and [Russia's extensive inland waterway system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_Deep_Water_System_of_European_Russia). The export variant is known as Project 21632 *Tornado*. Since 2010, all subsequent vessels are being constructed as improved [Project 21631 subclass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buyan-M_class_corvette), incorporating greater tonnage, [stealth technology](1958969.md) and the [3S14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3S-14) [vertical launching system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_launching_system).&#x20;

## Design

Project 21630, codenamed "Buyan", a class of ships of the "river-sea" type, was developed by the Zelenodolsk Design Bureau (FSUE) under the leadership of Chief Designer Yakov Evgeneyevich Kushnir. Scientific and technical support for the design and construction of the ship for the Russian Navy was provided by the 1st Central Research Institute of the [Russian Ministry of Defense](7875884.md). This ship was designed by the Zelenodolsk Design Bureau taking into account the characteristics of the Caspian Sea and the Volga Delta. The main requirements for the ships were good [seaworthiness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seakeeping) and the ability to navigate the shallow depths of the northern [Caspian Sea](19653787.md) and the [Volga Delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volga_Delta). Therefore, the ships' small size and displacement enables them to operate within inland river systems, including traversing the [Moscow Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Canal). This allows them to redeploy to various seas around [European Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Russia). Among the requirements of the tactical and technical specifications was a specified cruising range, enabling the ship to navigate the entire length of the Volga and Caspian Seas.&#x20;

The corvettes are armed with a 100 mm [A190-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AK-100_\(naval_gun\)#A190) [dual-purpose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual-purpose_gun) [naval gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_artillery), two [30 mm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30_mm_caliber) [AK-630](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AK-630) [CIWS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Close-in_weapon_system), two [14.5 mm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14.5_%C3%97_114_mm) [MPTU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KPV_heavy_machine_gun) [machine guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_gun), a [3M-47 Gibka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9K38_Igla) [anti-aircraft missile](182664.md) launcher, and one [Grad-M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BM-21_Grad) [multiple rocket launcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_rocket_launcher).[^cite_note-lenta291211-3] The propulsion system consists of two main 112-cylinder (2 × 56) [M520 Zvezda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zvezda_M503) radial diesel engines, each producing 10,000 hp, coupled via a final drive (reduction gear).&#x20;

## Construction history

The [lead ship](855210.md) of the class, Astrakhan, was [laid down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel_laying) at the [Almaz Shipbuilding Сompany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almaz_Shipbuilding_Company) in [Saint Petersburg](24320051.md) on 30 January 2004.&#x20;

The second ship of the class, Volgodonsk (previously Kaspiysk), was laid down at the same shipyard on 25 February 2005.&#x20;

Astrakhan was [launched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceremonial_ship_launching) on 7 October 2005 and, after [sea trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_trial) was [commissioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning) on 1 September 2006..&#x20;

Makhachkala, the third ship of the Project 21630 class, was laid down on 24 March 2006.&#x20;

Volgodonsk was launched 6 May 2011,[^cite_note-\:0-4] had its acceptance act signed on 20 December 2011[^cite_note-\:1-5], was commissioned on 28 December 2011,[^cite_note-\:2-6] and entered service with the Caspian Flotilla in July of 2012.[^cite_note-\:3-7]&#x20;

Makhachkala was launched on 27 April 2012[^cite_note-8] and was commissioned 4 December 2012.[^cite_note-\:4-9]&#x20;

## Operational history

On 6 November 2024, a modified [A-22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeroprakt_A-22_Foxbat) drone ([flying bomb](53703349.md)) from the [Armed Forces of Ukraine](42799055.md) struck the [Kaspiysk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaspiysk) naval base reportedly damaging a Buyan-class corvette of the Caspian Flotilla along with two [Gepard-class frigates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gepard-class_frigate).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]&#x20;
