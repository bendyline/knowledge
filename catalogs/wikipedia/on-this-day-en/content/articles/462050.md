The **Minister for Foreign Affairs** ([Swedish](26689.md): *utrikesminister*) is the [foreign minister](317153.md) of [Sweden](5058739.md) and the head of the [Ministry for Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_for_Foreign_Affairs_\(Sweden\)).

The current Minister for Foreign Affairs is [Maria Malmer Stenergard](72039473.md) of the [Moderate Party](202985.md).

## History

The office was instituted in 1809 as a result of the constitutional [Instrument of Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instrument_of_Government_\(1809\)) promulgated in the same year. Until 1876 the office was called **Prime Minister for Foreign Affairs** ([Swedish](26689.md): *statsminister för utrikes ärendena*, commonly known as *utrikesstatsminister*), similar to the office of [Prime Minister for Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Justice_\(Sweden\)) ([Swedish](26689.md): *justitiestatsminister*). The Prime Minister for Foreign Affairs initially served as head of the Cabinet of Foreign Mail Exchange at the [Royal Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Office?action=edit\&redlink=1). Following the ministry reform in 1840, the Prime Minister for Foreign Affairs became head of the newly instituted [Ministry for Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_for_Foreign_Affairs_\(Sweden\)). In 1876 the office proper of [Prime Minister of Sweden](195846.md) was created and at the same time the Prime Minister for Foreign Affairs was nominally demoted to a mere Minister. The holder of the office did however continue to be [styled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Style_\(manner_of_address\)) as "Your excellency", until 1974 when the new [Instrument of Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instrument_of_Government_\(1974\)) came into force. Before the parliamentary breakthrough in the early 20th century, that title had been granted exclusively to members of the most prominent [noble families](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Swedish_noble_families).
