**Beni Territory**, also known as **Oicha Territory**,[^cite_note-ReliefWeb_2021-1] is a [territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territories_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) of [North Kivu](446958.md), [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md).[^cite_note-2] It's administrative center is the town of [Oicha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oicha).[^cite_note-3]

It has been the site of fighting during the [Allied Democratic Forces insurgency](40780074.md) between [government troops](8029.md) and the [ADF](1258240.md) militia, which also crosses the border into the territory from [Uganda](31816.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] It was there that the [Kirindera massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirindera_massacre), perpetrated by the ADF, happened.[^cite_note-6]
