**2026** ([MMXXVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals)) is the current year, and is a [common year starting on Thursday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_year_starting_on_Thursday) of the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar), the 2026th year of the [Common Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era) (CE) and *[Anno Domini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anno_Domini)* (AD) designations, the 26th year of the [3rd millennium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_millennium) and the [21st century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_century), and the 7th year of the [2020s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020s) decade.&#x20;

The early part of the year saw escalating armed conflicts in the Americas and Middle East. In January, the United States launched [a military operation](82021123.md) in Venezuela that captured sitting [President](3329692.md) [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md), leading to [a blockade](81814844.md) of [Venezuelan](32374.md) oil exports that contributed to [a crisis](82348646.md) in [Cuba](5042481.md). In [Syria](7515849.md), [an offensive](82106178.md) by the Syrian government led to the [Syrian Democratic Forces](48205248.md) eventually being integrated into the [Syrian Army](3423927.md), ending the [Rojava Revolution](48303468.md), while in Yemen, [a counteroffensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_Southern_Yemen_campaign#Yemeni_government_counter-offensive) by the Yemeni government led to the [collapse](82075309.md) of the [Southern Transitional Council](54251566.md). In February, [Pakistan](23235.md) launched airstrikes against [Afghanistan](737.md), leading to [an open war](82482902.md) between both nations. In [Iran](14653.md), in response to [authorities cracking down on protests](81976180.md), the [United States](3434750.md) and [Israel](9282173.md) began [a war](82533582.md) in late February with the [assassination of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei](82537558.md), which resulted in [widespread economic grief](82562375.md), [fuel shortages](82827398.md) and [retaliatory strikes](82751620.md) on Gulf nations, as well as the [closure of the Strait of Hormuz](82550763.md) and [a naval blockade](82937256.md).

The continued release of the [Epstein files](80415168.md) that began the previous year has influenced scrutiny, media coverage, and in some cases arrests of high profile individuals in politics, business, and academia in the western world. Businessman [Elon Musk](909036.md) became the first [person to have a verified net worth exceeding \$1 trillion](47418795.md) upon the [initial public offering of his company SpaceX](82660746.md) in June.

In politics, a [conservative wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_wave) spread through [Latin America](18524.md), with [right-wing](56522.md) victories in [Colombia](79603998.md), [Costa Rica](78707168.md) and [Peru](69679018.md). Far-right parties in Europe continued to gain ground in the [United Kingdom](79139854.md) and across the [European Union](9317.md), though the long ruling right-wing government in [Hungary lost power after ruling since 2010](79337461.md), and [Portugal elected a centre-left social democrat over a right-wing populist in their presidential election](68734153.md).

In science and technology, the dominant headline has been [NASA](18426568.md)'s [Artemis II](41391511.md) flyby of the Moon, the first crewed flight beyond [low Earth orbit](47568.md) since [Apollo 17](1971.md) in 1972, sending the first woman, Black man, and non-American beyond low Earth orbit. [Artificial intelligence](1164.md) has continued to advance, with increasing focus on scaling models, infrastructure, and commercial applications. These developments have also brought growing scrutiny of the potential risks posed by increasingly capable AI systems, including concerns over [alignment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_alignment), misuse, loss of human control, and their wider societal and economic effects.

In sports, the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md) were the first [Olympic Games](22576.md) to be officially co-hosted by two cities ([Milan](36511.md) and [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md), [Italy](14532.md)). The [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) became the first edition to be hosted by three nations ([United States](3434750.md), [Mexico](3966054.md) and [Canada](5042916.md)) and the first to include 48 teams, in addition to setting the record for the [highest attendance in World Cup history](3602475.md#Attendance); a short time later, a crisis in FIFA erupted following a proposal of creation of a [commercial subsidiary](83820435.md), largely rejected by confederations.

In seismology, earthquakes in the [Philippines](83387873.md), [Venezuela](83547391.md), [Colombia](83931036.md) and [Indonesia](83975294.md) caused widespread destruction; in nautics, several incidents, such as [a hantavirus outbreak](83111976.md) on the cruise ship [MV *Hondius*](83110814.md), the [capsizing of the ferry *Mbuya Nehanda*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Lake_Kariba_ferry_accident) on [Lake Kariba](632358.md), [a fire on the vessel MV *June Aster*](84186736.md) and the [sinking of the ship *Virgo Transport 8*](84218139.md), resulted in large numbers of casualties; in mining, [two collapses](82291770.md) at the [Rubaya mines](71973347.md) in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md), [an explosion at a coal mine](83248010.md) in [China](5405.md) and [a landslide at a gold mine](83265364.md) in [Angola](701.md) became some of the worst [disasters of their types](954550.md) in recent years of these countries.

In health, [an epidemic](83191582.md) of [Ebola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundibugyo_virus) in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and [Uganda](31816.md) has been declared a [Public Health Emergency of International Concern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Health_Emergency_of_International_Concern) by the [World Health Organization](33583.md).
