**Finlay Luke Tarling** (2 October 2006 – 14 August 2026) was a Welsh [track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Track_cycling) and [road cyclist](1755460.md) who rode for the [UCI Continental team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCI_Continental_team) [NSN Development Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Premier_Tech_Academy). He was the younger brother of Welsh track and road cyclist [Josh Tarling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Tarling).

In 2023, Tarling won [silver medals](526647.md) in the [team pursuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_pursuit) at the [Junior European Track Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_UEC_European_Track_Championships_\(under-23_&_junior\)), and in the Junior [Chrono des Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrono_des_Nations). In 2024, he won a gold medal with Team Wales in the team pursuit at the [2024 British Cycling National Track Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_British_Cycling_National_Track_Championships), a [silver medal](526647.md) in the British national junior time trial championship, and [bronze medals](526648.md) in the junior team pursuit at the [2024 UEC European Track Championships (under-23 & junior)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_UEC_European_Track_Championships_\(under-23_&_junior\)) and in each of the men's points race, the men's [Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_\(cycling\)) relay race, and the team pursuit (with a new national record) at the [2024 UCI Junior Track Cycling World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_UCI_Junior_Track_Cycling_World_Championships).

Tarling died, aged 19, following a collision with a motor vehicle during the eighth stage of the [2026 Volta a Portugal](83836252.md).

## Cycling career

### Beginnings

In June 2019, at 12 years of age, Tarling (riding for West Wales Cycle Racing Team) won the [Hammer Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammer_Series) event in [Sittard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sittard), in the [Netherlands](21148.md), at the [Tom Dumoulin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Dumoulin) Bike Park against 80 riders, all 12 or 13 years of age.[^cite_note-2] At the 2019 Welsh [Cyclo-cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclo-cross) Championships at the [Gilwern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilwern) Outdoor Education Centre in December, Tarling (riding for WORX Factory Racing) won the under-14 boys.[^cite_note-3]

In September 2020, he won the [RTTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTTC) 13-year-old 25-mile time trial national championship, riding for WORX Factory Racing Powered by Silverstone.[^cite_note-4] In July 2021, Tarling won the [silver medal](526647.md) in the British National Circuit Time Trial Championships held in [Naseby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naseby), [Northamptonshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northamptonshire).[^cite_note-5]

In 2022, Tarling joined elder brother Joshua on the Belgian FlandersColor cycling team.[^cite_note-sixteen-6] He won his first five races in a row, including the Welsh Cyclocross Championships at [Pembrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pembrey), [Carmarthenshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmarthenshire), Wales, where he won the U16 race to become Welsh Champion.[^cite_note-sixteen-6]

### Junior career

In July 2023, he won a silver medal in the [team pursuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_pursuit) at the [Junior European Track Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_UEC_European_Track_Championships_\(under-23_&_junior\)) in [Anadia, Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anadia,_Portugal).[^cite_note-qa-7] He rode that year for Dutch team Willebrord Wil Vooruit (WWV).[^cite_note-qa-7] In October 2023 he finished runner-up in the 27.23-kilometer Junior [Chrono des Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrono_des_Nations) in [Les Herbiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Herbiers), [Vendée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vend%C3%A9e), France, finishing one second behind the champion.[^cite_note-qa-7][^cite_note-welsh-8][^cite_note-hal-9][^cite_note-pro-1] He said: "Time trials have to be my favorite discipline, but I also like to attack."[^cite_note-10]

With Team Wales he won a [gold medal](526488.md) in the [team pursuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_National_Team_Pursuit_Championships) at the [2024 British Cycling National Track Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_British_Cycling_National_Track_Championships) at the [Manchester Velodrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Velodrome) in [Manchester](20206.md) in February 2024. In June 2024, he was runner-up in the 30-kilometer British national junior time trial championship, and won a bronze medal in the 121-kilometer British national junior time road race.[^cite_note-welsh-8][^cite_note-pro-1]

Tarling won a [bronze medal](526648.md) in the junior team pursuit at the [2024 UEC European Track Championships (under-23 & junior)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_UEC_European_Track_Championships_\(under-23_&_junior\)) at the [Lausitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausitz) Velodrome in [Cottbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cottbus), Germany, in July 2024.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] He set a record (46.14) in the [Ross-On-Wye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross-On-Wye) & Dist CC 25-mile time trial at [Monmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monmouth), Wales, in July 2024, against 77 competitors.[^cite_note-14]

He won bronze medals in each of the men's points race, the men's [Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_\(cycling\)) relay race, and the team pursuit (with a new national record) at the [2024 UCI Junior Track Cycling World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_UCI_Junior_Track_Cycling_World_Championships) in [Luoyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luoyang), China, in August 2024.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] He was sick with an upset stomach for the Road World Junior Championship time trial in [Zurich](40334603.md), Switzerland, in September 2024, and placed 24th overall out of 66 riders.[^cite_note-welsh-8][^cite_note-17]

### NSN Development Team

Tarling signed a two-year contract to ride for [Sylvan Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvan_Adams)'s [Israel-Premier Tech Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Premier_Tech_Academy) team from the 2025 season.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-hal-9][^cite_note-20] Academy Manager Tim Elverson said: “Time trialling is his passion; it’s also an area in which the team is really keen to improve. He’s also got an eye on Classics racing, and that’s a big area in which we can work together.”[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-hal-9] Tarling said: "I think as it’s my first year the races that I could best fit into would be the classic-type races .... I also think with TT background that I have that it could be used for shorter stage races with TTs to carry a possible GC contention."[^cite_note-hal-9] His first race was the [Tour of Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tour_of_Britain).[^cite_note-22] In 2026, the team was renamed NSN Development.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

## Personal life

Finlay Luke Tarling was born [Gloucestershire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloucestershire), England on 2 October 2006,[^cite_note-25] but was brought up in [Ffos-y-ffin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ffos-y-ffin), [Ceredigion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceredigion), west [Wales](69894.md).[^cite_note-hal-9] He was the younger brother of Olympic track and road cyclist [Josh Tarling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Tarling), and his father is Michael Tarling,[^cite_note-welsh-8][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] a keen cyclist who used to race at [Newport Velodrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport_Velodrome).[^cite_note-QandA-28][^cite_note-SkippingU23-29] In 2012, Tarling's parents started the West Wales Cycle Racing Team, in part to address the lack of cycling clubs in the area.[^cite_note-QandA-28][^cite_note-30][^cite_note-dyl-31]

## Death

On 14 August 2026, while competing in the [2026 Volta a Portugal](83836252.md), Tarling, aged 19, was killed in a collision accident in [Arcozelo](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcozelo_\(Ponte_de_Lima\)), Ponte de Lima[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33] during the eighth stage of the race. The incident, which involved a [light goods vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_goods_vehicle) outside the race convoy that entered via a secondary road which was not blocked by warning tape or GNR officers,[^cite_note-34] occurred on the route from [Melgaço](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melga%C3%A7o,_Portugal) to [Fafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fafe).[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] Following his death, the NSN Development team left the tour.[^cite_note-37] The mens' cyclist union, [Cyclistes Professionnels Associés](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclistes_Professionnels_Associ%C3%A9s?action=edit\&redlink=1), criticised the road safety measures in the race; unlike other races, the Volta a Portugal attempts to secure the road only in the vicinity of the moving convoy rather than over the entire route.[^cite_note-38]
