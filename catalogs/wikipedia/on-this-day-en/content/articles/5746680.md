**Savar** ([Bengali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_language): সাভার) is a city in central [Bangladesh](3454.md), located in the [Dhaka District](4233263.md) in the [division of Dhaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhaka_Division). It is the closest separate city to the centre of [Dhaka](56656.md). It is also a part of the [Greater Dhaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Dhaka) conurbation which forms the [Dhaka megacity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Dhaka). About 380,000 people live here which makes this city the second most populous in [Dhaka district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhaka_district) and the [13th largest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_and_towns_in_Bangladesh) in [Bangladesh](3454.md).[^cite_note-census2022-1]

## History

The origin of the name Savar is thought to be an evolved version of the ancient 7th–8th-century township of সর্বেশ্বর *Shôrbeshshôr* ("Lord of everything") or সম্ভার *Shômbhar* situated on the banks of the river known today as the [Bangshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangshi_River). Shôrbeshshôr, in turn, is said to have been established on the site of the ancient Sambagh Kingdom. Local legends as well as archaeological finds indicate a king by the name of [Raja Harishchandra Pal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harishchandra_Pal_\(Savar_Pala_Dynasty\)), said to be of the [Pala dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pala_Empire), ruled over *Shôrbeshshôr*. There is an old [shloka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shloka) that goes বংশাবতী-পূর্বতীরে সর্বেশ্বর নগরী, বৈসে রাজা হরিশচন্দ্র জিনি সূরপুরী *Bôngshaboti-purbotire shôrbeshshôr nôgori, boishe raja Horishchôndro jini shurpuri* ("On the East banks of the Bangsabati is the city of Sharbeshvar, lives there King Harishchandra conquering Heaven"). In any case, local legend holds that the childless [Harishchandra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harishchandra_Pal_\(Savar_Pala_Dynasty\)) was succeeded to the throne by his sister Rajeswari's son, Damodar. Damodar's reign started a decline for the kingdom, culminating in the reign of one of his descendants, king Ravan, a music enthusiast. During Ravan's reign, the [Koch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koch_Dynasty?action=edit\&redlink=1) invaded and sacked the capital established by [Harishchandra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harishchandra_Pal_\(Savar_Pala_Dynasty\)). However, inscriptions on an undated burnt brick fragment indicate king Mahendra in 869 CE dedicated a [matha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matha) to his father, saint king Harishchandra, son of king Ranadhirasena, son of king Dhimantasena, son of king Bhimasena. The same inscription also states the [Buddhist king](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist_kingship) Dhimantasena invaded and captured the land between the [Bangshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangshi_River) and the [Brahmaputra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahmaputra_River) and king Ranadhirasena extended the kingdom to the [Himalayas](37966.md) and fixed his residence in the city of Shômbhar.

During the [1971 war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_War_of_Liberation), [Savar Cantonment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savar_Cantonment) (then Ansar Camp) and the then-newly founded [Jahangirnagar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jahangirnagar_University) were some of the first targets of military swoop outside the capital following [Operation Searchlight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Searchlight) of 25 March. In December of that year, Savar was the last obstacle before the freedom fighters (led by [Kader Siddiqui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kader_Siddiqui) and others) entered the capital, and the Pakistan army conceded defeat. Days before the end of the war, teenager Golam Dastagir Titu was killed in a direct encounter between the Pakistani Army and the freedom fighters. The compatriots buried him near the main gate of the Central Cattle Breeding & Dairy Farm, Savar. The Bangladeshi Army constructed a memorial monument in his honour.

On 24 November 2012, a garment factory fire killed at least 112 people. The factory-made clothes for US and European companies were faulted for negligent safety standards.[^cite_note-2] [Walmart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walmart) and [Sears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sears), two of the companies who contracted work from this factory, refused to compensate victims.[^cite_note-3]

On 24 April 2013, [a building in Savar collapsed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rana_Plaza_collapse), killing 1,129 people and injuring around 2,500.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The building housed a garment factory that exported clothing to US and European companies. Eighty percent of the workers were women aged 18–20, paid \$0.12-\$0.26 per hour.[^cite_note-6]

At the time of the 2022 census, Savar Paurashava had 113,484 households and a population of 384,105. 7.74% of the population was under 5 years of age. Savar had a sex ratio of 107.20 males per 100 females and a literacy rate of 86.00%: 87.75% for males and 84.12% for females.[^cite_note-census2022-1]

## Administration

Savar is governed by a [Paurashava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paurashava) named Savar municipality which consists of 9 [wards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wards_of_Bangladesh) and 56 [mahallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahalla_\(Bangladesh\)), which occupies an area of 14.08 km<sup>2</sup> (5.44 sq mi).[^cite_note-census2022-1]
