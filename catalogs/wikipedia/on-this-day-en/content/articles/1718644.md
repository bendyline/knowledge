The **Musandam Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مُحَافَظَة مُسَنْدَم, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Muḥāfaẓat Musandam*) is a [governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Oman) of [Oman](22316.md). Except for the [exclave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclave) of [Madha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madha),[^cite_note-Lancaster\&Lancaster2011-1] it is located on the [Musandam Peninsula](1935665.md), which juts into the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md), the narrow entry into the [Persian Gulf](24761.md), from the [Arabian Peninsula](47858.md). The governorate is also an exclave, separated from the rest of Oman by the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md). Its location gives Oman partial control, shared with [Iran](14653.md), of the strategic strait. In the northern section of Musandam, around [Kumzar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumzar), the language is [Kumzari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumzari_language), which is a [southwestern Iranian language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwestern_Iranian_language) closely related to [Larestani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luri_language) and [Luri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luri_language). The Musandam Peninsula has an area of 1,800 km<sup>2</sup> (690 sq mi) and, at the 2020 census, a population of 49,062.



Jebel Harim, highest point in Musandam

Access to the peninsula was formerly difficult, with the only options being limited flights or a ten-hour drive through four immigration posts. The [Shinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinas_\(ferry\)) fast ferry service between [Muscat](57053.md) and Musandam was launched in August 2008 to alleviate this problem and make the region more accessible.[^cite_note-2] The governorate is accessible by land only from the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md). Mainland Musandam can be accessed by [Ras Al Khaimah](61770159.md) near Al Jeer via [Route 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Route_2_\(Oman\)), and an [exclave](4848945.md) of Sharjah, [Dibba Al-Hisn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dibba_Al-Hisn). [Oman Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oman_Air) provides an air link between [Cairo](6293.md), [Dubai](211583.md), the capital of the country, Muscat, and the main administrative town of the governorate, [Khasab](1658322.md).

## History

Isolated from surrounding powers by [mountainous terrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajar_Mountains) and a rocky coast,[^cite_note-thomas-3] the Musandam peninsula was historically self-ruled by local tribes including the [Shihuh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shihuh)[^cite_note-thomas-3] and [Habus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habus).[^cite_note-4] By the early 20th century, Musandam was claimed by the [Sultan of Oman](405325.md) who stationed a representative in [Khasab](1658322.md).[^cite_note-naval-5][^cite_note-thomas-3] The Sultan did not exert effective control over the area, neither collecting tax[^cite_note-naval-5] nor establishing a military presence,[^cite_note-hughes-6] and the peninsula continued to be ruled by local tribes. In 1970–71, following the British-backed [1970 Omani coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970_Omani_coup_d'%C3%A9tat), British and [Trucial Oman Scouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trucial_Oman_Scouts)[^cite_note-7] forces invaded Musandam ending local rule[^cite_note-8] and establishing a permanent Omani military force.[^cite_note-hughes-6]

## Provinces

Musandam Governorate consists of four [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Oman):[^cite_note-Lancaster\&Lancaster2011-1]

- [Khasab](1658322.md), the regional centre of the Governorate

- [Bukha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bukha)

- [Dibba Al-Bay'ah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dibba_Al-Baya)

- [Madha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madha), itself an exclave, located halfway between the rest of Musandam and Oman proper

The area has great strategic importance owing to its proximity to the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md). The administrative head of the Governorate is the governor, who is also the Minister of State. The current governor is Sayyid Ibrahim bin Said Al Busaidi (الـسَّـیِّـد ابراهيم بن سعيد بن ابراهيم الْـبُـوسَـعِـيـدي).[^cite_note-9]

## Geography



Dhow along the coast of [Khasab](1658322.md)

The rugged coastline resembles the [glacier](12463.md)-carved coasts of polar regions, but in this case, the coast was shaped by the movement of [Earth's crust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth's_crust). The [Arabian Plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabian_Plate) is slowly pushing under the [Eurasian Plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasian_Plate), creating the earthquake-prone mountains of Iran. On the leading edge of the Arabian Plate, the [Musandam Peninsula](1935665.md) is sinking. The higher elevation mountains remain above the water, but the sea has rushed in to fill the valleys with fingers of water.[^cite_note-10] The highest point in Musandam is [Jabal Al Harim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabal_Al_Harim), with an elevation of 2,087 meters (6,847 feet) above sea level.
