**Madura**[^cite_note-1] is an [Indonesian island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_islands_of_Indonesia) off the northeastern coast of [Java](69336.md). The island has an area of about 4,471.05 square kilometers (1,726.28 mi<sup>2</sup>), but administratively Madura comprises an area of around 5,408.45 square kilometers (2,088.21 mi<sup>2</sup>) due to the inclusion of various smaller islands to the east, southeast and north that are part of Madura's easternmost [Sumenep Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumenep_Regency). Madura is part of the province of [East Java](320443.md). It is separated from Java by the narrow [Madura Strait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madura_Strait). The administered area had a density of 773 people per km<sup>2</sup> (2,003 per sq. mile) in mid-2024, while the main island had a somewhat higher figure of 862.3 per km<sup>2</sup> (2,233 per sq. mile).[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2025-2]

## History

search

Map of the [Madoera Stoomtram Maatschappij](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madoera_Stoomtram_Maatschappij), c. 1915

In 1624, [Sultan Agung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Agung) of [Mataram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mataram_Sultanate) conquered Madura, and the island's government was brought under the [Cakraningrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cakraningrat?action=edit\&redlink=1), a single princely line.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTERicklefs200847-4] The Cakraningrat family opposed Central Javanese rule and often conquered large parts of Mataram.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEAkhmad_Saiful_Ali199462-5]

Following the [First Javanese War of Succession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Javanese_War_of_Succession) between [Amangkurat III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amangkurat_III) and his uncle, [Pangeran Puger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangeran_Puger), the Dutch gained control of the eastern half of Madura in 1705. Dutch recognition of Puger was influenced by the lord of West Madura, Cakraningrat II, who is thought to have supported Puger's claims in the hope that a new war in central Java would provide the Madurese with a chance to interfere. However, while Amangkurat was arrested and exiled to Ceylon, Puger took the title of [Pakubuwono I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakubuwono_I) and signed a treaty with the Dutch that granted them East Madura.

The Cakraningrats agreed to help the Dutch quash the 1740 rebellion in Central Java after the [Chinese massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1740_Batavia_massacre) in 1740. In a 1743 treaty with the Dutch, [Pakubuwono II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakubuwono_II) ceded the full sovereignty of Madura to the Dutch, which was contested by [Cakraningrat IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cakraningrat_IV). Cakraningrat fled to [Banjarmasin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banjarmasin), took refuge with the British, was robbed and betrayed by the sultan, and captured by the Dutch and exiled to the [Cape of Good Hope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_of_Good_Hope).

The Dutch continued Madura's administrative divisions of four states each with their own regent. The island was initially important as a source of colonial troops and in the second half of the nineteenth century it became the main source of salt for Dutch-controlled territories in the archipelago. The Dutch gradually sidelined the Sultan and took over direct control of the entire island in the 1880s, governing it as the [Madoera Residency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madoera_Residency). From 1948 to 1950, under the [United States of Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_Indonesia), Madura was administered as a federative state known as the [State of Madura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Madura).

## Geography

Madura Island is a relatively flat topography and there is no significant difference in elevation, which makes Madura a [badland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badlands). Geologically, Madura is part of the northern [limestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone) mountains of [Java](69336.md). The limestone hills in Madura are lower, rougher, and rounder than the hills in northern Java.

Madura (including its offshore islands) has a population of about four million, most of whom are [ethnically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnicity) [Madurese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madurese_people). The main language of Madura is [Madurese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madurese_language), one of a family of [Austronesian languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austronesian_languages), which is also spoken in part of eastern Java and on many of the 66 outlying islands.

The Madurese are a large ethnic population in Indonesia, numbering around seven million inhabitants. They come from the island of Madura as well as surrounding islands, such as Giliraja, [Sapudi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapudi), [Raas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raas_Island), and the [Kangean Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kangean_Islands). Additionally, many Madurese live in the eastern part of [East Java](320443.md), commonly called the "Horseshoe", from [Pasuruan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasuruan) to the north of [Banyuwangi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banyuwangi). Madurese are found in Situbondo and Bondowoso, and east of Probolinggo, [Jember](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jember), and a few at most who speak Javanese, including North [Surabaya](212635.md), as well as some of [Malang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malang).

Madura has a [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni) [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim) majority and a large [Shia](26961.md) minority. However, since 2012, interfaith discord has escalated to violence, resulting in many Shia villages around the city of [Sampang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampang_\(city\)) being attacked and many residents escaping to government refugee centers.[^cite_note-7] The [United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs](1602284.md) has provided details of such attacks in 2013.[^cite_note-8]

## Economy



[Salt](1605200.md) making in Madura in 1948

On the whole, Madura is one of the poorest regions of the East Java province.[^cite_note-12] Unlike [Java](69336.md), the soil is not fertile enough to make it a major agricultural producer. Limited economic opportunities have led to chronic unemployment and poverty. These factors have led to long-term emigration from the island, such that most ethnically Madurese people now do not live on Madura. People from Madura were some of the most numerous participants in government [transmigration programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transmigration_program), moving to other parts of Indonesia.

Subsistence [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) is a mainstay of the economy. [Maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize) is a key subsistence crop, on the island's many small landholdings. [Cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle)-raising is also a critical part of the agricultural economy, providing extra income to peasant farmer families, in addition to being the basis for Madura's famous bull-racing competitions. Small-scale fishing is also important to the subsistence economy.

Among export industries, [tobacco](30942.md) farming is a major contributor to the island's economy. Madura's soil, while unable to support many food crops, helps make the island an important producer of tobacco and [cloves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clove) for the domestic *[kretek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kretek)* (clove cigarette) industry. Since the [Dutch era](23476997.md), the island has also been a major producer and exporter of [salt](1605200.md).

[Bangkalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkalan_Regency), on the western end of the island, has [industrialized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrialisation) substantially since the 1980s. This region is within a short ferry ride of [Surabaya](212635.md), Indonesia's second-largest city, and hence has gained a role as a suburb for commuters to Surabaya, and as a location for industry and services that need to be near the city.

The [Surabaya-Madura (Suramadu) Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suramadu_Bridge), opened in 2009, is expected to further increase the Bangkalan area's interaction with the regional economy.

### Bull racing



Bull racing in [Sumenep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumenep), Madura

Madura is famous for its bull-racing competition (called *[karapan sapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karapan_sapi)*), where a [jockey](284545.md), usually a young boy, rides a simple wooden sled pulled by a pair of [bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bull) over a course of about 100 meters in ten to fifteen seconds.

### Music and theater

Several forms of music and theater are popular on Madura, particularly among lower-class people for whom they provide an inexpensive form of entertainment and community-building. The *topeng* theater, which involves [masked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mask) performances of classic stories such as the [Ramayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramayana) and [Mahabharata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahabharata), is the Madurese performance art best known outside the island, due to its role as a representative Madurese art form at exhibitions of regional cultures from all over Indonesia. However, performances of it are rare on Madura and are generally restricted to entertainment at large official functions. The less formal *loddrok* theater, where performers do not wear masks and perform a wider range of themes, is more popular on the island.

The [gamelan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamelan) orchestra, best known as a classical Javanese instrument, is also played on Madura, where several of the former royal courts, such as at Bangkalan and Sumenep, possess elaborate gamelans. *Tongtong* music, more exclusive to Madura, is played on several wooden or bamboo drums, and often accompanies bull-racing competitions.

### Vessels

The Madurese are considered to be excellent sailors. Madurese vessels loaded with cargoes of wood from other islands, like [Borneo](4518.md), used to ply their trade between Indonesia and [Singapore](27318.md). Traditional vessels of Madura include the [golekan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golekan), [leti leti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leti_leti) (or leteh-leteh), [lis-alis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lis-alis), and [janggolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janggolan#Madurese_janggolan).[^cite_note-17]
