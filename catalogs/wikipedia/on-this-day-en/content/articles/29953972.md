**Sanandaj** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): سنندج; pronounced [\[sænænˈdædʒ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Persian) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/f/fb/Sanandaj.ogg/Sanandaj.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/f/fb/Sanandaj.ogg/Sanandaj.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language): سنە, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Kurdish): *Senneh*[^cite_note-4]) is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Sanandaj_County\)) of [Sanandaj County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanandaj_County), in the [Kurdistan province](448112.md) of [Iran](14653.md), serving as capital of the province, the county, and the district.[^cite_note-Kurdistan_Province_Structure-5] With a population of 850,000,[^cite_note-6] it is the second largest Kurdish city and 20th largest city overall in Iran. The city is considered a historic centre of Kurdish cultural and intellectual life, particularly noted for its tradition of Kurdish music, poetry, and carpet weaving.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## History

Sanandaj's founding is fairly recent (about 250 years ago), yet in its short existence it has grown to become one of the centers of [Kurdish culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_culture).[^cite_note-auto-9][^cite_note-10] During the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War) the city was attacked by Iraqi planes and saw disturbances.[^cite_note-11] Since 2019, UNESCO has recognized Sanandaj as Creative City of Music.[^cite_note-12]

The name "Sinna" first appears in records from the 14th century CE. Before this, the main city in the region was **[Sisar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sisar?action=edit\&redlink=1)**, whose exact location is unknown. Sisar was also called "Sisar of Sadkhaniya", or "Sisar of the hundred springs", and it has been proposed that the current name of "Sinna" is a contracted form of "Sadkhaniya".[^cite_note-Minorsky_Sisar-13]

The name "Sisar" disappears in the 14th century and the name "Sinna" replaces it, for example in the works of [Hamdallah Mustawfi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamdallah_Mustawfi) who refers to a mountain and a pass with this name.[^cite_note-Minorsky_Sisar-13] Then the Kurdish historian [Sharaf al-Din Bitlisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharaf_al-Din_Bitlisi) mentions that in 1580 an [Ardalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardalan) ruler named Timur Khan had a land grant including Sinna and the earlier Ardalan capital of [Hasanabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasanabad,_Sanandaj). However, the local historian Ali-Akbar Munshi Waqayi-Nigar wrote in 1892/3 that Sinna was founded later, by the ruler [Soleyman Khan Ardalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soleyman_Khan_Ardalan), on the site of an earlier settlement; the [chronogram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronogram) he gives for this event corresponds to 1046 [AH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hijri_year), or 1636-7 CE.[^cite_note-Minorsky_Sanandaj-14]

Sinna was developed significantly under the reign of Aman Allah "the Great" (from 1797-1825).[^cite_note-Minorsky_Sanandaj-14] 19th-century Sinna was "a lively commercial center, exporting [oak galls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oak_gall), [tragacanth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tragacanth), furs, and carpets". Its population was mostly [Kurdish](17068.md), with a significant [Jewish](25955086.md) minority and smaller numbers of [Armenian](387816.md) and [Assyrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assyrian_people) Christians (the latter of which are predominantly [Chaldean Catholic](698821.md)).[^cite_note-Minorsky_Sanandaj-14]

## Demographics

### Ethnicity

The population of Sanandaj is mainly [Kurdish](3821779.md). The city also had an [Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Armenians) minority who gradually emigrated from the city. Until the [Iranian Revolution](347268.md) (1979), the city had a small [Aramaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanandaj_Jewish_Neo-Aramaic)-speaking [Jewish](25955086.md) community of about 4,000 people.[^cite_note-auto-9] The city boasted a sizable [Assyrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assyrian_people) community that spoke a unique dialect of Aramaic called [Senaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Neo-Aramaic_dialect_of_Senaya), they are mostly members of the [Chaldean Catholic Church](698821.md).[^cite_note-15] The Kurdish character of Sanandaj has been extensively documented in urban studies scholarship. Alizadeh and colleagues (2019) characterise Sanandaj as a paradigmatic example of a traditional Kurdish city, noting that its urban morphology, architectural heritage, and social organisation have been shaped over centuries by Kurdish cultural norms.[^cite_note-16]

Persian serves primarily as the language of education, government, and inter-ethnic communication, while Kurdish is the dominant vernacular of daily life.[^cite_note-18]

### Religion

Most of the people of Sanandaj follow the [Shafi‘i](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shafi'i_school) branch of [Sunni Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam).[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20]

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 311,446 in 81,380 households.[^cite_note-2006_Kurdistan_Province-21] The following census in 2011 counted 373,987 people in 106,771 households.[^cite_note-2011_Kurdistan_Province-22] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 412,767 people in 126,240 households.[^cite_note-2016_Kurdistan_Province-3]

The city is the second largest Kurdish city in Iran, behind [Kermanshah](1489244.md).[^cite_note-23]

## Geography

### Location

The city is between the [Qishlaq river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qishlaq_river?action=edit\&redlink=1), a tributary of the [Diyala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diyala_river), and Mount Awidar, which separates it from the old [Ardalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardalan) capital of [Hasanabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasanabad,_Sanandaj). Carpet making is the biggest industry in Sanandaj.[^cite_note-Minorsky_Sanandaj-14]

### Climate



Sanandaj city center in 2021

Sanandaj has a [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate) (*Csa*) according to the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification), bordering on a [humid continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate) (*Dsa*), with cold and wet winters and very hot and dry summers.

The synoptic station of Sanandaj started working in the autumn of 1959.[^cite_note-24]

Highest recorded temperature: 44°C on 16 July 1981[^cite_note-MAX-25]

Lowest recorded temperature: -31°C on 6 February 1974[^cite_note-MIN-26]

## Economy

The economy of Sanandaj is based upon the production of carpets, processed hides and skins, milled rice, refined sugar, woodworking, cotton weaving, [metalware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metalware) and cutlery.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

In Sanandaj, there is a combined cycle power plant with a capacity of 1000 megawatts, a petrochemical plant, a tractor manufacturing plant, a tire manufacturing plant, a tile and ceramic manufacturing plant, a pasteurized milk manufacturing plant, and hundreds of other factories.

There is also an international airport in Sanandaj.

In Sanandaj, there are two 5-star hotels, Laleh Hotel and Jin Plus Hotel, two 4-star hotels, Shadi Hotel and Shahu Hotel, and two 3-star hotels, Jahangardi Hotel and Farhangian Hotel.

The world's largest open-air cinema is located in Abidar Forest Park.
