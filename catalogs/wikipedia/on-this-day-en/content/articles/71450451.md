# Atrocity crimes during the Russo-Ukrainian war

[Atrocity crimes](26684335.md) have been committed during the [Russo-Ukrainian war](42085878.md), chiefly by the [Russian Federation](25391.md) and [its proxy forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_separatist_forces_in_Ukraine) in Ukraine's [Donbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donbas) region.[^cite_note-1]

Atrocity crimes is a legally defined group of offences against [international law](8195726.md), that includes [war crimes](48626.md), [crimes against humanity](61099.md), and [genocide](12441.md), and is often considered to include the non-legally defined [ethnic cleansing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_cleansing).[^cite_note-prevention-2] Since the 2014 Russian invasion and [annexation of Crimea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Crimea_by_the_Russian_Federation), the [war in Donbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Donbas), and the 2022 [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine), numerous atrocity crimes have been identified, and some are being or have been tried in courts.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
