**Malekshahi County** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): شهرستان ملکشاهی)[^cite_note-11] is in [Ilam](448068.md) province, [Iran](14653.md). Its capital is the city of [Arkavaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkavaz).[^cite_note-Malekshahi_County_Establishment-12][^cite_note-fn1-14] The Malekshahi tribe is the largest Kurdish tribe in Ilam.

## History

In 2008, [Malekshahi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malekshahi_District)[^cite_note-15] was separated from [Mehran County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehran_County) in the establishment of Malekshahi County, which was divided into two districts of two rural districts each, with Arkavaz as its capital.[^cite_note-Malekshahi_County_Establishment-12] The village of [Delgosha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delgosha) was elevated to the status of a city.[^cite_note-Delgosha_City-16] After the 2011 National Census, the village of [Mehr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehr,_Ilam) became a city as well.[^cite_note-Mehr_City-17]

### During the 2025-2026 Iranian protests

Main article: [2026 Malekshahi massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Malekshahi_massacre)

On January 3, 2026, during the [2025-2026 Iranian protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025-2026_Iranian_protests) and [2026 Iran massacres](82036668.md), forces of the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC) and [Basij](2892082.md) opened fire on protesting civilians, killing several in the Malekshahi County.[^cite_note-Hengaw_atleast4killed_Malekshahi-18]

Among those killed was [Latif Karimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Latif_Karimi). His son confirmed on his personal social media page that Karimi was killed directly by government forces.[^cite_note-19] Following the incident, state-affiliated institutions promoted media narratives describing him as a member of government forces who had been killed by protesters.[^cite_note-20]

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2011 census, the county's population was 22,587 people in 5,626 households.[^cite_note-2011_Ilam_Province-21] The 2016 census measured the population of the county as 21,138 in 6,022 households.[^cite_note-2016_Ilam_Province-2]

## 2026 Malekshahi massacre

This section is an excerpt from [2026 Malekshahi massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Malekshahi_massacre).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=2026_Malekshahi_massacre\&action=edit)]

The [2026 Malekshahi massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Malekshahi_massacre) was committed by the forces of the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC) and [Basij](2892082.md) on 3 January 2026 in Malekshahi County, [Ilam province](448068.md), [Iran](14653.md). The massacre began at noon when the IRGC and Basij forces opened fire on protesting civilians, killing several.[^cite_note-2026_Malekshahi_massacre_Hengaw_atleast4killed_Malekshahi-22]
