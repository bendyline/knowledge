**Operation Five Stones** was a four-day [Israeli](9282173.md) military operation in the northern [West Bank](33209.md) that lasted from 26 to 29 November 2025. It focused on combatting [Palestinian](23267.md) militants in an area of [Tubas Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tubas_Governorate) dubbed the "Pentagon of Villages": [Tubas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tubas_\(city\)), [Tammun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tammun), the [al-Fara'a camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far'a), [Aqaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/'Aqqaba), and [Tayasir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tayasir).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:1-3] While some sources described the operation as an extension of Israel's large-scale "[Operation Iron Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Wall_\(Israeli_military_operation\))",[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-\:2-5][^cite_note-6] the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) (IDF) said it was not and referred to it as a new and distinct operation.[^cite_note-\:3-7]

## Timeline

### 26 November

A heavy deployment of Israeli forces in the Tubas Governorate began at midnight.[^cite_note-\:1-3] Israel announced the start of the operation, which it described as "a broad [counterterrorism](83392.md) operation" in the northern West Bank.[^cite_note-\:3-7] Israeli forces sealed off large portions of the governorate, imposing a siege in the region and obstructing roads.[^cite_note-8] Israeli helicopters firing at random were reported above Tubas.[^cite_note-\:1-3] According to Tubas mayor Mahmoud Daraghmeh, this is the first time since the [Second Intifada](71717.md) that military helicopters have been used in the city.[^cite_note-\:4-9]

According to Ahmed al-Assad, governor of Tubas Governorate, Israeli forces encircled Tubas and established positions across several of the city's neighborhoods. He said the operation "looks to be a long one" and that Israeli forces were displacing and arresting residents.[^cite_note-\:2-5]

Per Palestinian media, Tammun mayor Samir Basharat was among those detained by Israeli forces.[^cite_note-10]

The [Tubas Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tubas_Brigade) said it had "confronted the incursion of the occupation forces", detonating explosives against IDF army vehicles.[^cite_note-11]

### 27 November

Israeli forces in the Tubas Governorate detained two journalists, a Palestinian and an [Egyptian](31912046.md).[^cite_note-12]

### 28 November

The IDF withdrew from Tammun in the morning.[^cite_note-\:5-13]

The IDF reported that 40 militants had been arrested, dozens of weapons had been confiscated, dozens of kilograms of [potassium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potassium) for explosives production had been seized, an "operational apartment" in Tammun was destroyed, and tens of thousands of [shekels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_new_shekel) meant to finance militancy were confiscated.[^cite_note-14]

The IDF also carried out mass detentions in the al-Fara'a camp.[^cite_note-15] However, it withdrew from the camp that night.[^cite_note-\:5-13]

Tubas Brigade militants targeted an Israeli foot patrol with an explosive device in a river valley of the Tayasir area.[^cite_note-\:hundreds-16]

### 29 November

During the day, Israeli forces continued to operate in Tubas, Aqaba, and Tayasir.[^cite_note-\:hundreds-16][^cite_note-17] However, that evening, Israeli forces pulled out of Tubas Governorate, marking the conclusion of the operation.[^cite_note-\:6-18][^cite_note-19]

## Reported human rights violations

Per [IMEMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Middle_East_Media_Center), Palestinian medical officials reported widespread abuse during Operation Five Stones. Nidal Odeh, director of ambulance and emergency services in Tubas, said paramedics treated 204 injuries caused by beatings, with 69 victims transferred to hospitals and the remainder treated in the field. Kamal Bani Odeh, director of the Palestinian Prisoners’ Society (PPS) in Tubas, confirmed that Israeli forces detained around 200 Palestinians for field interrogations. Most were later released, except for 10 individuals. It was additionally reported that Israeli forces carried out extensive home invasions, destroying property and subjecting residents to mistreatment.[^cite_note-\:6-18]
