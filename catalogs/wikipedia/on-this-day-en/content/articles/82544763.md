Following [strikes against Iran](82546969.md) on 28 February 2026 by a joint military operation conducted by [Israel](9282173.md) and the [United States](3434750.md) against the [Government of Iran](50913538.md), including [the assassination](82537558.md) of [Ali Khamenei](385653.md), the long-time [Supreme Leader of Iran](36814344.md), protests opposing the war were held in several countries.[^cite_note-CNN-2026-1][^cite_note-The-Guardian-2026a-2] In addition to Khamenei's death, the strikes targeted Iranian military sites, nuclear facilities, and government infrastructure, resulting in the deaths of other high-ranking officials.[^cite_note-The-New-York-Times-2026-3][^cite_note-4]

Protests erupted in multiple countries, with demonstrators condemning the attacks as acts of aggression and calling for an end to foreign intervention, and some expressing support for the government of Iran.[^cite_note-The-Washington-Post-2026-5][^cite_note-NPR-2026-6] In Iran, pro-government rallies occurred in major cities such as [Tehran](57654.md), where crowds gathered to mourn Khamenei and denounce the strikes.[^cite_note-Reuters-2026-7][^cite_note-The-New-York-Times-2026-3] Internationally, anti-war groups organized demonstrations in the United States, Europe, and the Middle East.[^cite_note-The-Guardian-2026a-2] [Violence also occurred in some locations](82535697.md#Attacks_abroad), including near U.S. diplomatic facilities in Iraq and [Pakistan](82542761.md).[^cite_note-The-Economic-Times-2026-8][^cite_note-Al-Jazeera-2026b-9]

The demonstrations have varied in scale, from hundreds in U.S. cities to thousands in Tehran.[^cite_note-Mission-Local-2026-10] In contrast, [celebratory demonstrations supporting the strikes](82164645.md#Celebrations_following_the_Israeli%E2%80%93United_States_strikes_on_Iran), particularly among Iranian diaspora communities opposed to the Islamic Republic, have also occurred but are distinct from these anti-war actions.[^cite_note-CNN-2026-1]

## Background

[Operation Epic Fury](82533582.md), a coordinated Israeli–U.S. military campaign against Iran, was launched on 28 February 2026.[^cite_note-11] The operation was justified by U.S. president [Donald Trump](4848272.md) as a response to [Iran's nuclear program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran's_nuclear_program) and its suppression of domestic [protests in January 2026](81976180.md), which had resulted in thousands of deaths.[^cite_note-NPR-2026-6] Iranian state media confirmed Khamenei's death, prompting retaliatory missile strikes by Iran on Israeli and U.S. targets in the region.[^cite_note-The-New-York-Times-2026-3]

The protestors have framed the strikes as violations of international law.[^cite_note-The-Guardian-2026a-2]

## Involved parties

### Iran

In [Tehran](57654.md), pro-government demonstrators marched on 28 February, waving [Islamic Republic flags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Iran) and chanting against the U.S. and Israel.[^cite_note-Reuters-2026-7] Similar rallies occurred in [Yasuj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasuj), where crowds mourned Khamenei.[^cite_note-NPR-2026-6] State media reported gatherings in [Enqelab Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enqelab_Square) on 1 March.[^cite_note-The-New-York-Times-2026-3]

#### Pro-government rallies

Main article: [2026 Iranian pro-government rallies](83125264.md)

Since the onset of the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), Iranians have held nightly rallies in support of the Iranian government.[^cite_note-12] According to *The Guardian*, more than 850 pro-government demonstrations had taken place across Iran by March 2026.[^cite_note-13] According to Iranian media, these gatherings have continued for 198 consecutive nights so far.[^cite_note-14]

### Israel

On 28 March 2026, anti-war protests broke out across Israel after the [Supreme Court](1666510.md) ordered police to allow the protests. Police clashed with the protesters at the [Habima Square](31773044.md) in Tel Aviv despite the court order. 22 people were arrested during protests in twenty locations including [Tel Aviv](31453.md), [Haifa](55606.md), and [Jerusalem](16043.md).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

On 4 April, the [Israeli High Court of Justice](1666510.md#High_Court_of_Justice) ordered the [Israel Police](745595.md) to allow anti-war protests in Israel.[^cite_note-17] The Israel Police forcibly dispersed an anti-war protest at the [Habima Square](31773044.md) in Tel Aviv, defying the recent high court order. This followed protesters' defiance of a previous order by the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) to limit gatherings to 150 people. [^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

### United States



Protesters in [Portland, Oregon](23503.md) during the 28 March 2026 [No Kings 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Kings_3) protests

Demonstrations occurred in multiple cities in the United States on 28 February. In [Washington, D.C.](108956.md), hundreds protested near the [White House](33057.md).[^cite_note-The-Washington-Post-2026-5] In [New York City](645042.md), pro-Iranian protests took place in [Times Square](31389.md).[^cite_note-The-Guardian-2026a-2] Protests were also held in other cities including [Atlanta](3138.md), [Baltimore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore), [Boston](24437894.md), [Chicago](6886.md), [Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati), [Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver), [Las Vegas](47737.md), [Los Angeles](18110.md), [Miami](53846.md), [Minneapolis](6097240.md), [San Francisco](49728.md), and [Philadelphia](50585.md).[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-Mission-Local-2026-10][^cite_note-22]

The protests were sponsored mainly by a coalition of [left-wing](28903916.md) groups critical of the United States' involvement in the strikes.[^cite_note-Helmore-2026-23] These groups include the [A.N.S.W.E.R.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.N.S.W.E.R.) Coalition, the [50501 movement](79143789.md), [American Muslims for Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Muslims_for_Palestine), [The People's Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_People's_Forum), [Palestinian Youth Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Youth_Movement), [Code Pink](2440600.md), [Black Alliance for Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Alliance_for_Peace), [Democratic Socialists of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Socialists_of_America), and the [National Iranian American Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Iranian_American_Council), who all gathered to denounce the United States' Iran strikes as illegal and a violation of international law.[^cite_note-Helmore-2026-23]

Actress and activist [Jane Fonda](63520.md) joined anti-war demonstrators sponsored by these groups at a Los Angeles rally.[^cite_note-24] On 28 March, millions of protesters joined the [third "No Kings" protest](82478314.md), with anti-war sentiment over the conflict in Iran being one of the major issues.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

In contrast, some in the [Iranian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian-American) diaspora community held [celebration rallies across the country](82164645.md).[^cite_note-27] A poll conducted by [Zogby analytics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Zogby#In_popular_culture) and commissioned by the [National Iranian American Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Iranian_American_Council), however, has shown "nearly two-thirds" of the Iranian-American diaspora do not support the war after being "near evenly divided at the start of the conflict".[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

#### Washington D.C.



Protester on 28 February 2026, in Washington D.C., referencing the [Epstein files](80415168.md)

Hundreds of protesters have gathered in front of the [White House](33057.md) since 28 February, both in support and in opposition to the war.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31] Protests have persisted despite severe weather.[^cite_note-32]

On 4 March, former Marine Brian McGinnis interrupted a [Senate Armed Services Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_Armed_Services_Committee) hearing on Iran in the [Hart Senate Office Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hart_Senate_Office_Building) with shouts of “America does not want to send its sons and daughters to war for Israel!” US senator [Tim Sheehy](77920271.md) (R-MT) attempted to assist [Capitol police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_police) in forcibly removing him, breaking his arm in the process.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34]

On 18 March, a protest from [Win Without War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Win_Without_War) included the display of children backpacks on [Capitol Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Hill) in protest of [an airstrike](82536519.md) on an [Minab](2392946.md) elementary school that killed 160 children. Additionally, several [Democrats](5043544.md) in [Congress](31756.md) attended the protest.[^cite_note-35]

On 7 April, a protest was held outside the [White House](33057.md) despite the ceasefire announcement to call for Trump's removal from office, accusing him of [war crimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_war_crimes).[^cite_note-36]

On 21 April, over 62 veterans and their families were arrested in the [Cannon House Office Building](840257.md) after a protest organized by several veterans' group occupied the building.[^cite_note-37]

On 21 July, protesters interrupted a [hearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_congressional_hearing) in the [United States Senate](24909346.md) by [U.S Defense Secretary](43998.md) [Pete Hegseth](34076202.md) over the wars in Iran and [Palestine](241405.md), with the protesters removed by [police](1915214.md).[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

#### New York

Since 28 February in [New York City](645042.md), various protests across the city have taken place. Around 1,000 people gathered in [Times Square](31389.md) on 28 February in a "Freedom for Iran" rally.[^cite_note-60][^cite_note-61] In Manhattan, protesters gathered at [Columbus Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus_Circle) in a "Stop the War" rally.[^cite_note-62] The [New York City Police Department](255413.md) (NYPD) has increased security at "sensitive locations" across the city.[^cite_note-63]

On 7 March, an [explosive device was thrown outside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_York_City_bombing_attempt) of [Zohran Mamdani](64618347.md)'s official residence at [Gracie Mansion](1206268.md) during a counter protest.[^cite_note-64] The FBI is investigating the incident as an act of [terrorism](30636.md).[^cite_note-65]

On 14 April 90 were arrested during a protest against arms sales to Israel in [Manhattan](45470.md) organized by the [Jewish Voice for Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_Voice_for_Peace), the group says that those who were arrested included whistleblower [Chelsea Manning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_Manning), actress [Hari Nef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hari_Nef) and [New York City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_Council) Member [Alexa Avilés](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexa_Avil%C3%A9s).[^cite_note-66]

## Other countries

### Australia



Anti-war rally at [Parliament House, Adelaide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_House,_Adelaide), Australia

The group Democratic Solidarity of Iranians organized a protest outside the United States consulate in Sydney on 6 March, where they were met by counter-protesters.[^cite_note-74] The Sydney Anti-[AUKUS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AUKUS) Coalition organized an anti-war protest at [Sydney Town Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Town_Hall) on 14 March.[^cite_note-75] On 15 March, the [South Australian Greens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Australian_Greens) organized a rally attended by 300 people outside of the [South Australia](26716.md) [Parliament House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_House,_Adelaide) in [Adelaide](1148.md), demanding an end to Australian involvement with the US-Israeli war effort.[^cite_note-76] On the morning 8 April, approximately 10 activists blocked the entrance to the Adelaide headquarters of the military contractor [BAE Systems Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAE_Systems_Australia) for around three hours, with one chaining himself to the entrance gates.[^cite_note-77]

### Bangladesh

### France

### New Zealand

The [Socialist Aotearoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Aotearoa) group organised a public meeting at the [Mount Eden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Eden) Memorial Hall in [Auckland](18660332.md) on 11 March to express opposition to the war in Iran. Notable speakers at the event included Socialist Aotearoa leader [Joe Carolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Carolan), [New Zealand Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Labour_Party) Member of Parliament [Phil Twyford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Twyford), [Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Party_of_Aotearoa_New_Zealand) co-leader [Marama Davidson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marama_Davidson) and [New Zealand Council of Trade Unions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Council_of_Trade_Unions) president Sandra Grey and several Iranian New Zealander speakers.[^cite_note-Bradbury-2026-101][^cite_note-102] The meeting was picketed by Iranian New Zealander counter-protesters supporting the US and Israeli strikes as a means of regime change.[^cite_note-Small-2026-103][^cite_note-Bradbury-2026-101] Police arrested a counter-protester who was involved in a scuffle with a pro-Palestine protester.[^cite_note-Small-2026-103]

On 4 April, anti-war protesters gathered in Auckland's [Te Komititanga Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Te_Komititanga_Square?action=edit\&redlink=1) in opposition to the New Zealand government's refusal to condemn US and Israeli actions against Iran. The event was organised by the Stop Wars Aotearoa coalition and featured speakers from the Iranian New Zealand community and the Palestine Solidarity Network Aotearoa (PSNA).[^cite_note-104]

On 11 April, several "Stop Wars Aotearoa" rallies were held in several New Zealand cities including [Auckland](18660332.md) and [Christchurch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christchurch) to protest against US and Israeli military actions in Iran and Lebanon. These protests were organised by the Stop Wars Aotearoa coalition which included the Socialist Aotearoa, "Americans Abroad Against the War," Worker First Union, PSNA, and members of the Iranian and Afghan communities in New Zealand. In Auckland, protesters marched to the United States Consulate-General on [Customs Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customs_Street) where they staged a "[die in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_in)" to mark the [2026 Minab school attack](82536519.md). A smaller group of pro-Pahlavi and pro-Israel counter-protesters also gathered outside the US Consulate. Several speakers also addressed the Auckland rally including the PSNA's co-chair Maher Nazzal, Labour MP Phil Twyford, Green MP [Ricardo Menéndez March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Men%C3%A9ndez_March), [Alliance Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_\(New_Zealand_political_party\)) candidate Victor Billot, and NZCTU president Sandra Grey.[^cite_note-105]

### Pakistan

See also: [2026 attack on the United States consulate in Karachi](82542761.md)

Protests erupted across Pakistan on 1 March 2026, primarily among [Shia Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Muslim) communities, in response to the killing of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei in the joint US-Israeli strikes. The demonstrations condemned the attacks and expressed solidarity with Iran, with chants of "[Death to America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_to_America)" and "[Death to Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_to_Israel)."[^cite_note-Al-Jazeera-2026b-9][^cite_note-106] Protestors also accused the Pakistani government of siding with the US during the conflict.[^cite_note-Al-Jazeera-2026a-107] The nationwide protests lead to the deaths of 26–35 protestors and 120 injured, including 10–16 at the [US Consulate in Karachi](41909779.md), at least 3 at the [US embassy in Islamabad](32553327.md) and at least 13 in [Gilgit-Baltistan](18807336.md).[^cite_note-Sharma-2026-108][^cite_note-Junaidi-2026-109][^cite_note-Nagri-2026-110]

In [Karachi](17123.md), hundreds of protesters attempted to [storm the US Consulate](82542761.md), breaching the outer wall and clashing with police and paramilitary forces who used tear gas and live fire. As protests escalated, the [Marine Security Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_Security_Guard) opened fire, killing at least 10 and upto 16 protestors and injuring over 60 people.[^cite_note-111][^cite_note-112][^cite_note-113][^cite_note-Sharma-2026-108] Protesters smashed windows, torched a nearby police post, and set vehicles ablaze before being dispersed.[^cite_note-114]

In [Lahore](125315.md), hundreds gathered outside the [US Consulate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consulate_General_of_the_United_States,_Lahore), but were dispersed by the police after the consulate was attacked.[^cite_note-Al-Jazeera-2026b-9][^cite_note-115] Protests also occurred in [Islamabad](51153.md), authorities blocked roads to the [Red Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Zone_\(Islamabad\)) and increased security around the US Embassy amid calls for demonstrations with 3 protesters being killed near the embassy.[^cite_note-Al-Jazeera-2026a-107][^cite_note-116][^cite_note-Junaidi-2026-109] Additional rallies were reported in [Multan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan), where Shia participants mourned Khamenei and condemned the strikes, and plans were noted for further actions in [Peshawar](66230.md) and other areas.[^cite_note-117] Protests and demonstrations were also reported in [Faisalabad](401880.md).[^cite_note-Hussain-2026-118]

In [Gilgit-Baltistan](18807336.md), pro-Iranian protests erupted in [Gilgit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgit), on 1 March 2026 following the killing of Khamenei, thousands of Shia demonstrators targeted and set fire to the offices of the [United Nations Military Observer Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Military_Observer_Group_for_India_and_Pakistan) amid widespread unrest in the region.[^cite_note-119][^cite_note-120] At least 13 protesters and 1 security officer were killed and 60 were injured in [Gilgit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgit_District), [Skardu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skardu_District), and [Shigar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shigar_District) districts, and other buildings, including a school, were damaged. A three-day curfew was imposed in Gilgit-Baltistan and [Pakistan Army](1965925.md) troops were deployed in the region.[^cite_note-Al-Jazeera-2026a-107][^cite_note-Hussain-2026-118][^cite_note-Nagri-2026-110]

### Philippines



Anti-war protest in Manila on 9 April ([Day of Valor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_of_Valor))

On 6 March, several protest organized by several groups such as [Bahaghari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahaghari_Philippines), [Gabriela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriela_Women's_Party), [Anakbayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anakbayan), and the Moro-Christian People’s Alliance was held outside the [US embassy](10576426.md) in Manila denouncing the war.[^cite_note-121] On 24 April, students at the [University of the Philippines Baguio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Philippines_Baguio) in [Baguio](162480.md), [Cordillera Administrative Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordillera_Administrative_Region), [Philippines](23440.md) staged a walkout to protest against the US-Israeli strikes against [Iran](14653.md), and hold a candle vigil for slain [UP Diliman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Philippines_Diliman) student council member Alyssa Alano, who was killed in a [19 April clash](18748169.md) with the [New People's Army](1262096.md) in [Toboso](2051335.md), [Negros Occidental](266813.md).[^cite_note-122]

On [1 May](83082967.md), a [Labour Day](18051.md) rally organized by labour group [Kilusang Mayo Uno](5308660.md), was held in [Manila](184334.md), [Philippines](23440.md). During the rally, protesters attempted to storm the [U.S. Embassy](10576426.md) to demand an end to the Iran war and clashed with police, injuring seven officers.[^cite_note-Police-123]

### South Korea

On the anniversary of the [March First Movement](430481.md), civic groups such as the [People's Solidarity for Participatory Democracy](37584004.md) held an anti-war demonstration at the [US embassy in Seoul](8335869.md).[^cite_note-TRT-2026-99][^cite_note-124] On 17 March, around 60 Buddhist monks from the [Jogye Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jogye_Order) order marched around three kilometres to the embassy to protest the war, with some performing [full body prostration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prostration_\(Buddhism\)).[^cite_note-125]

### Spain

On 12 March, the Assembly Against Armament and Militarization organized a [human chain](484000.md) against the war in the Middle East in Madrid, which was attended by [Podemos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podemos_\(Spanish_political_party\)) leader [Ione Belarra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ione_Belarra). A petition signed by civil society groups and public figures called for nationwide demonstrations on 14 March.[^cite_note-126]

### Turkey

Demonstrations against the strikes were held in [İzmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0zmir) on 28 February.[^cite_note-127] On 1 March, members of the [Revolutionary Party](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devrim%20Partisi) protested against the war outside the [US embassy in Ankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_the_United_States,_Ankara).[^cite_note-128] Members of the Labour and Democracy Platform in [Adana](185243.md) protested against the attacks and called for a closure of US and NATO airbases in Turkey, including the [Incirlik](1597543.md) base hosted in the city.[^cite_note-129]

### United Kingdom

In [London](17867.md), anti-strikes protests took place on 28 February.[^cite_note-130] London's [Metropolitan Police](192450.md) imposed conditions on both the [Iranian diaspora rallies](82164645.md#Celebrations_following_the_Israeli%E2%80%93United_States_strikes_on_Iran) and the pro-Palestinian "Hands off Iran" protests in order to prevent potential clashes between the opposing groups.[^cite_note-131] On 7 March, more than 50,000 people joined protests in London against the strikes, organized by groups that includes the [Campaign for Nuclear Disarmament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campaign_for_Nuclear_Disarmament), [Stop The War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stop_the_War_Coalition), the [Palestine Solidarity Campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Solidarity_Campaign), the [Muslim Association of Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim_Association_of_Britain), the Palestinian Forum in Britain, and Friends of Al-Aqsa.[^cite_note-The-Guardian-2026b-132][^cite_note-133] That same day, four people were arrested in total on 7 March for suspicion of inciting racial hatred, possession of an offensive weapon, racially aggravated public order offences, and violent disorder in relation to an incident the day prior near the [Islamic Centre of England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Centre_of_England).[^cite_note-The-Guardian-2026b-132][^cite_note-134] Subsequently, an Iran-linked pro-Palestinian [Quds Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quds_Day) march planned for 15 March in London was banned by the [Home Secretary](149104.md) to "prevent serious public disorder" by request of the Metropolitan Police.[^cite_note-135][^cite_note-136]
