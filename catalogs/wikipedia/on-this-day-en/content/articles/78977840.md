# List of international trips made by Marco Rubio as United States Secretary of State

Main article: [List of international trips made by secretaries of state of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_international_trips_made_by_secretaries_of_state_of_the_United_States)



Official portrait of Marco Rubio as Secretary of State, 2025

This is a list of international visits undertaken by [Marco Rubio](5502549.md) (in office since 2025)[^cite_note-office_of_the_historian-1] while serving as the [72nd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_States_secretaries_of_state) and current [United States secretary of state](32293.md). The list includes both private travel and [official visits](327283.md). The list includes only foreign travel which he made during his tenure in the position.[^cite_note-office_of_the_historian-1]

## Summary

Rubio has visited 39 countries. The number of visits per country or territory where Secretary Rubio traveled are:

- **One** visit to [Armenia](10918072.md), [Bahrain](18933277.md), [Belgium](3343.md), [China](5405.md), [Colombia](5222.md), [Costa Rica](5551.md), the [Dominican Republic](8060.md), [Egypt](8087628.md), [El Salvador](9356.md), [Guatemala](17238567.md), [Guyana](17238691.md), [Hungary](13275.md), [India](14533.md), [Jamaica](15660.md), [Japan](15573.md), [Kuwait](7515890.md), [Mexico](3966054.md), the [Netherlands](21148.md), [Panama](22997.md), [Peru](170691.md), the [Philippines](23440.md), [Saint Kitts and Nevis](27198.md), [Slovakia](26830.md), [South Korea](27019.md), [Suriname](26828.md), [Sweden](5058739.md), and the [United Kingdom](31717.md)

- **Two** visits to [Ecuador](9334.md), [Germany](11867.md), [Malaysia](3607937.md), [Qatar](8238258.md), [Switzerland](26748.md), [Turkey](11125639.md), the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md), and [Vatican City](32408.md)

- **Three** visits [Canada](5042916.md), [France](5843419.md), [Italy](14532.md), [Saudi Arabia](349303.md)

- **Four** visits to [Israel](9282173.md)
