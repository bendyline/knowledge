The **College Football Playoff National Championship** is a postseason [college football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_football) [bowl game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowl_game), used to determine a [national champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_football_national_championships_in_NCAA_Division_I_FBS) of the [NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_I_Football_Bowl_Subdivision) (FBS), which began play in the [2014 college football season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_NCAA_Division_I_FBS_football_season).[^cite_note-USA_Today_April_25._2013-1] The game is held on a Monday in January and serves as the final game of the [College Football Playoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Football_Playoff) (CFP), a [bracket tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bracket_\(tournament\)) between the top five ranked conference champions, and the top 7 ranked at-large teams in the country that are selected by a playoff committee, which was established as a successor to the [Bowl Championship Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowl_Championship_Series) (BCS) and the [BCS National Championship Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BCS_National_Championship_Game).

The participating teams in the College Football Playoff National Championship are determined by two semifinal games, hosted by an annual rotation of bowls commonly known as the [New Year's Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Year's_Six). Thus, the teams to compete in the final are not directly selected by a selection committee, as had been the format used for the BCS National Championship Game.

The game is played at a neutral site, determined through bids by prospective host cities (similar to the [NCAA Final Four](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Men's_Division_I_Basketball_Championship)). When announcing it was soliciting bids for the 2016 and 2017 title games, playoff organizers noted that the bids must propose host stadiums with a capacity of at least 65,000 spectators,[^cite_note-stadium-future-2] and cities cannot host both a semifinal game and the title game in the same year.[^cite_note-future-hosting-3]

The winner of the game is awarded the [College Football Playoff National Championship Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Football_Playoff_National_Championship_Trophy), which is sponsored by [Dr Pepper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr_Pepper).[^cite_note-trophy-4] It was created as a new championship trophy, rather than the ["crystal football"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFCA_National_Championship_Trophy) that has been given by the [American Football Coaches Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Football_Coaches_Association) (AFCA) since 1986, as officials wanted a new trophy that was unconnected with the previous BCS championship system.[^cite_note-5]

The inaugural game was held at [AT\&T Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT\&T_Stadium) in [Arlington, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington,_Texas), on January 12, 2015, and was won by [Ohio State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Ohio_State_Buckeyes_football_team).[^cite_note-stadium-2015-6] A top-ranked team did not win the College Football Playoff National Championship until [LSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_LSU_Tigers_football_team) won the sixth edition of the game, in January 2020. [Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alabama_Crimson_Tide_football) has the most appearances in a College Football Playoff National Championship, with six, and also the most wins, with three.

The College Football Playoff National Championship is not awarded by the [National Collegiate Athletic Association](60706.md) (NCAA). The highest level of college football that the NCAA awards a championship in is the Division I [Football Championship Subdivision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Championship_Subdivision) (FCS).

## Venues



[AT\&T Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT\&T_Stadium), in [Arlington, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington,_Texas), hosted the first College Football Playoff National Championship game, in January 2015.

Cities across the United States can bid on the National Championship Game each year. The number of cities capable of bidding for the event is restricted by a requirement to have a stadium with at least 65,000 seats. The stadium restriction would limit the bidding to most cities with a [National Football League](21211.md) franchise, since [all but four of the stadiums in the league](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_current_National_Football_League_stadiums) meet the capacity requirements, and in practice all National Championship venues chosen for up to and including the 2026 game are also home to at least one NFL franchise. Unlike the [Super Bowl](27718.md), there is no *de jure* restriction on climate.[^cite_note-stadium-future-2][^cite_note-future-hosting-3] A venue cannot host a semifinal game and the National Championship Game in the same season.

## National anthem performers

The [national anthem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Star-Spangled_Banner) usually begins with a [drum roll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_roll) by two snare drummers, with one member representing each team.

- 2015: [Lady Antebellum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Antebellum)

- 2016: [Ciara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciara)

- 2017: [Little Big Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Big_Town)

- 2018: [Zac Brown Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zac_Brown_Band)

- 2019: [Andy Grammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Grammer)

- 2020: [Lauren Daigle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Daigle)

- 2022: [Natalie Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Grant)

- 2023: [Pentatonix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentatonix)

- 2024: [Fantasia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantasia_\(singer\))

- 2025: [Coco Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_Jones) (accompanied by [Adam Blackstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Blackstone))

- 2026: [Jamal Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamal_Roberts)

The national anthem for the 2021 game was pre-recorded due to COVID concerns.[^cite_note-25]
