**Rift Valley Province** ([Swahili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swahili_language): *Mkoa wa Bonde la Ufa*) of [Kenya](188171.md), bordering [Uganda](31816.md), was one of Kenya's eight [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Kenya), before the 2013 [Kenyan general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenyan_general_election,_2013). Rift Valley Province was the largest and one of the most economically important provinces in [Kenya](188171.md). It was dominated by the [Kenya Rift Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Rift_Valley,_Kenya) which passes through it and gave the province its name. According to the 2009 Census, the former province covered an area of 182,505.1 square kilometres (45,098,000 acres; 70,465.6 sq mi) and would have had a population of 10,006,805,[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] making it the largest and most populous province in the country. The bulk of the provincial population inhabited a strip between former [Nairobi](21482.md) and [Nyanza Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyanza_Province). The capital was the town of [Nakuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakuru).

## Geography



Map of Kenya, showing its provinces

The Great Rift Valley runs south through Kenya from [Lake Turkana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Turkana) in the north and has several unique geographical features, including the [Elgeyo escarpment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elgeyo_escarpment) which is a popular tourist attraction.

Apart from the Rift Valley itself, the area has other important geographic features such as: the extinct volcanoes [Mount Longonot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Longonot) and [Mount Suswa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Suswa) and [Lake Baringo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Baringo), [Lake Bogoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Bogoria), [Lake Magadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Magadi), [Lake Nakuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Nakuru), [Lake Naivasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Naivasha), the [Suguta Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suguta), and [Lake Turkana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Turkana).

## Geology

A large part of Kenya is underlain by [Precambrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precambrian) basement, while the Kenya rift basin (a typical extensional basin) hosts [Tertiary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tertiary_\(period\)) [volcanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanics) that cover [Mesozoic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesozoic) sediments (Recently these sediments have been considered for oil exploration).[^cite_note-4] The [sedimentary basins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedimentary_basin) evolved along the [Anza trough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anza_trough) during the Late [Paleozoic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleozoic) to Early Tertiary times through extension tectonics during the major [Gondwanaland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gondwana) breakup. In the [Miocene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miocene) Period, the region underwent intermittent uplift and subsidence along major boundary faults accompanied by a large outpouring of lava flows. The Anza trough intersects the modern rift valley in the area of Lake Turkana. Rifting still continues today; primarily in the north, where active volcanoes are more plentiful.

## Economy

A tea plantation near Kericho in the Kenyan highlands.

A tea plantation near Kericho in the Kenyan highlands.

The highlands provide adequate rainfall for farming and agriculture which is the economic base of the residents of the Rift Valley. [Tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea) from the highlands in the [Kericho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kericho) district enjoys a worldwide reputation, but [horticulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horticulture) is an important part of the district's economy and cattle raising is also practised to a large extent.

The full economic potential of the Rift Valley region is, however, far from fully exploited, though the current growth in population and improved education may change this in a near future. People in the province are still mostly rural, but [urbanisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanisation) is gradually increasing; new cities and towns contain the rural-urban migration and, provided the right policies are instituted, the Rift Valley province will be able to emerge as a national economic and cultural hub.

## Ethnicity

The Rift Valley is home to various communities. The people of the Rift Valley are a mesh work of different ethnic identities, and the [Kalenjin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalenjin_people) and the [Maasai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maasai_people) are two of the best known ethnic groups. Most of Kenya's top runners come from the [Kalenjin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalenjin_people) community. The [Maasai people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maasai_people) have the most recognizable cultural identity, both nationally and internationally, and serve as Kenya's international cultural symbol.
