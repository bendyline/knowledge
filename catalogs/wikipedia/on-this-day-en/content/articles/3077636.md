**Ștefan Vodă** (Romanian pronunciation: [\[ˈʃtefan ˈvodə\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Romanian) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/c/c5/LL-Q7913\_%28ron%29-KlaudiuMihaila-%C8%98tefan_Vod%C4%83.wav/LL-Q7913\_%28ron%29-KlaudiuMihaila-%C8%98tefan_Vod%C4%83.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/c/c5/LL-Q7913_%28ron%29-KlaudiuMihaila-%C8%98tefan_Vod%C4%83.wav/LL-Q7913_%28ron%29-KlaudiuMihaila-%C8%98tefan_Vod%C4%83.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Moldova) ([Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language): *[raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raion)*) in the south-east of [Moldova](19260.md), with the administrative center at [Ștefan Vodă](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C8%98tefan_Vod%C4%83). The district is situated 100 km from [Chișinău](56635.md) and 100 km from [Odesa](55545.md), [Ukraine](31750.md). As of the [2024 Moldovan census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Moldovan_census), its population was 42,285.[^cite_note-2024_Census-1]

## History

Localities with the earliest documentary attestation are: [Cioburciu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cioburciu,_%C8%98tefan_Vod%C4%83), [Olănești](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ol%C4%83ne%C8%99ti), [Carahasani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carahasani), first certified locations in 1405-1456. In the 16th-18th centuries, intense [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) was developed, with important wine-making industries and population growth attested. In 1812, after the [Russo-Turkish War (1806-1812)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Turkish_War_\(1806-1812\)), [Bessarabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessarabia) was incorporated into the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) during this period (1812–1917), and an intense [russification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russification) of the native population occurred. In 1918, after the [collapse of the Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Revolution_\(1917\)), [Bessarabia united with Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Bessarabia_with_Romania) (1918–1940, 1941–1944), and the district was part of the [Cetatea Albă County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cetatea_Alb%C4%83_County). In 1940, after [Molotov–Ribbentrop Pact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molotov%E2%80%93Ribbentrop_Pact), Bessarabia was occupied by the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). In 1991, as a result of the proclamation of [Independence of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_of_Moldova), the district became part and residence of the [Tighina County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tighina_County_\(Moldova\)) (1991–2003), and in 2003, it became an administrative unit of [Moldova](19260.md).

## Geography



[Palanca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palanca,_%C8%98tefan_Vod%C4%83) and [Nistru river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nistru_river)

Ștefan Vodă district is located in south-eastern part of the [Republic of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Moldova). It is bordered by [Căușeni District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C4%83u%C8%99eni_District) in the northwest, [Slobozia District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slobozia_sub-district) in the northeast, and the state [border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border) with [Ukraine](31750.md) in the south and east. The [landscape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landscape) is predominantly [plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain), slightly fractured, the highest altitude around 210–230 m in the western part of district. Minimum altitude 2–5 m in the Lower [Nistru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nistru) Plain. [Erosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erosion) processes with a low intensity.

### Climate

[Temperate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperate) continental [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) with an annual average district [temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature) 11 °C (52 °F). July average temperature 22.5 °C (73 °F), of January −4 °C (25 °F). Annual [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation_\(meteorology\)) 450–550 mm. Average [wind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind) speed 3–5 m \ s.

### Fauna

Typical [European](9239.md) [fauna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fauna), with the presence of such [mammals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mammals) such as [foxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox), [hedgehogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedgehog), [deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deer), [wild boar](52255.md), [polecat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_polecat), [wild cat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynx), [ermine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoat) and others. Of [birds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birds): [partridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partridges), [crows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crows), [eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eagle), [starling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starling), [swallow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swallow) and more.

### Flora

[Forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forests) of the district are complemented by tree species such as [oak](39696.md), [ash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ash_\(Fraxinus\)), [hornbeam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbeam), [linden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_linden), [maple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maple), [walnut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut) and others. From [plants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plants): [wormwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemisia_\(plant\)), [knotweed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knotweed), [fescue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fescue), [nettle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urtica) and many others.

### Rivers

Ștefan Vodă district is located in the basin of the [Nistru River](376633.md), which crosses the district in the north-eastern part of district. [Tributaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tributaries) of the Nistru here are usually short. The area has numerous artificial lakes.

## Economy&#x20;



[Purcari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purcari) [wine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wine) factory

Ștefan Vodă District hosts a total of 14,469 registered [businesses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Businesses), many of which are [agricultural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural). Agricultural land makes up 65,199 ha (65.3%) of total land area, while [arable land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arable_land) occupies 55,542 ha (55.6%) of the total [agricultural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural) land. The farmland in the district includes 4977 ha (5.0%) of [vines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vine), 3237 ha (3.2%) of [orchards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orchards), and 1398 ha (1.4%) of [pastures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pastures). Major [crops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crops) grown in the area include [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [oats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oats), [sunflower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunflower), [canola](71016451.md), [soybeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soybeans), and [vegetables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetables).
