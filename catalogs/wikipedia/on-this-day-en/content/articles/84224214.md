On September 13, 2026, two groups of men opened fire in a crowded area outside the Datu Usman Mampen Elementary School in [Cotabato City](298955.md), Philippines, which served as a [polling station](763947.md) for the [2026 Bangsamoro Parliament election](68697708.md), ahead of voting. Three people were killed and at least 15 others were injured. The perpetrators fled the scene and are at large.

## Background

The shooting occurred amid the first ever [2026 Bangsamoro Parliament election](68697708.md). Prior to the explosion, two explosions rocked Cotabato City in the night, killing a person.[^cite_note-PhilStar-1] A [strafing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strafing) incident also occurred in one of the villages where a bomb went off.[^cite_note-PNA-2] A separate shooting at another elementary school in Cotabato City left one person dead and three others injured, including a police officer who was shot in the foot.[^cite_note-3] The gunmen fled the scene.[^cite_note-4]

## Shooting

Just before midnight at around 11\:41 p.m. ([PHT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Standard_Time)), an incident led to a heated argument and fist fight between two groups of men outside the Datu Usman Mampen Elementary School, which was a polling center.[^cite_note-PhilStar-1][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-PNA-2] The altercation escalated into a shootout between the people, resulting in over a dozen people being struck by gunfire. The shooters fled the scene and remain unidentified.[^cite_note-ANN-6]

## Victims

The deceased victims were identified as 88-year-old Idar Kasan Andog, 43-year-old Caril Abdul Lumidsog and 48-year-old Bain Abdul Lumidsog.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] At least 15 people were transported to nearby hospitals for medical treatment following the shooting.[^cite_note-ANN-6]

## Aftermath

Security was tightened across Bangsamoro after the shootings and bombings near polling stations.[^cite_note-9]

## Investigation

Police recovered empty shells from the scene believed to belong to an [.45 ACP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.45_ACP) [pistol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pistol) and a [9 mm caliber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9_mm_caliber) pistol.[^cite_note-ANN-6] Police said that the men were members of rival political factions clashed while competing to be first in line to vote.[^cite_note-10]
