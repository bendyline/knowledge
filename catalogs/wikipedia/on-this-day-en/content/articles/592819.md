**Río Gallegos** ([\[ˈri.o ɣaˈʝeɣos\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is the capital and largest settlement of the [Patagonian](65433.md) [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Argentina) of [Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Cruz_Province_\(Argentina\)) in [Argentina](18951905.md). Located in the department of [Güer Aike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G%C3%BCer_Aike_Department), it had a population of 115,524, according to the 2022 [census](6889.md) \[[INDEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Statistics_and_Census_of_Argentina)]. The city bears the name of the [Gallegos River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallegos_River), and sits on its [estuary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estuary) 2,636 km (1,638 mi) south from the Argentine federal capital [Buenos Aires](255919.md).

Established on 19 December 1885 to increase Argentine power over southern Patagonia, Río Gallegos became the capital of the then Territory of Santa Cruz in 1888, retaining its status when the territory became a province in 1957. [Néstor Kirchner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A9stor_Kirchner), later [President of Argentina](229473.md), served as the city's mayor from 1987 to 1991 and is interred in a [mausoleum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mausoleum_of_N%C3%A9stor_Kirchner) in the city's cemetery.

## History

See also: [Patagonian sheep farming boom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patagonian_sheep_farming_boom)

In 1525 Spanish explorer [García Jofre de Loaísa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garc%C3%ADa_Jofre_de_Loa%C3%ADsa) became the first European to reach the Gallegos River, and named it *Río San Idelfonso*. [Simón de Alcazaba y Sotomayor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sim%C3%B3n_de_Alcazaba_y_Sotomayor)'s 1535 expedition was the first to name the river *"Río Gallegos"*. The area was not settled by Europeans until much later, with the settlement established 19 December 1885, when the Argentine government wished to better express its sovereignty over southern [Patagonia](65433.md) – a naval base was created which increased the development of the town. Between 1912 and 1920, the government encouraged settlers from the [Falkland Islands](7550182.md) and southern [Chile](5489.md) with preferential farming conditions. Some 3,000 arrived and boosted the town. As [sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheep)-farming increased, Río Gallegos became the principal port for exporting sheep and their products. The Pioneers Museum is a preserved old Patagonian house exhibiting the life of the early settlers.

Governor [Ramón Lista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ram%C3%B3n_Lista) decided to move the Territorial Capital from [Puerto Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Santa_Cruz) to Río Gallegos in 1888; official ratification of this decision came on 19 May 1904. The Cathedral of Our Lady of Luján was consecrated in 1900. The Territory of Santa Cruz was declared a [Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Argentina) in 1957, with Mario Cástulo Paradelo as its first Governor.

In the 1982 [Falklands War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falklands_War), the [city airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piloto_Civil_Norberto_Fern%C3%A1ndez_International_Airport) was base of [Argentine Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentine_Air_Force) [Mirage III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirage_III) interceptors and [A-4 Skyhawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-4_Skyhawk) strike aircraft.

[Néstor Kirchner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A9stor_Kirchner), President of Argentina from 2003 to 2007, was mayor of Río Gallegos between 1987 and 1991. His mausoleum is in the city and a street bears his name.

Today, Río Gallegos is an important city of the Argentine far south, with military bases and an [international airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piloto_Civil_Norberto_Fern%C3%A1ndez_International_Airport). Flights between Chile and [Mount Pleasant Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAF_Mount_Pleasant) on the Falkland Islands stop at Río Gallegos once a month.

## Wildlife

Birds in the area include the [thrush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turdus_falcklandii), [rhea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhea_\(bird\)), *chingolo* ([rufous-collared sparrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rufous-collared_sparrow)), [Chilean flamingo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilean_flamingo), [upland goose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upland_goose) and [black-necked swan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black-necked_swan). [Grey foxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_American_gray_fox) are predators on the sheep ranched in the city. Other animals found in the area include the [guanaco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanaco).

On the outskirts of Rio Gallegos there is a wide variety of native plants, including [calafates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berberis_microphylla), [anartrofilos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anarthrophyllum), [oxalis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxalis), [violets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violet_\(plant\)) and [pansies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pansy), [paper flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bougainvillea_glabra), [calceolarias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calceolaria), hipoqueris, [leucerias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marticorenia), [perezias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perezia_\(plant\)), [senecios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senecio), mata arrears, senecio miser, [amancay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alstroemeria), [lilies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilium), lily of the field.

## Sport

Rio Gallegos' main association football club is [Boca Rio Gallegos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boca_Rio_Gallegos) of the [Torneo Argentino B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torneo_Argentino_B), the fourth tier of Argentine football. Founded 12 October 1945 by meat factory workers, the side share their crest and kit with [Boca Juniors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boca_Juniors) of Buenos Aires, one of Argentina's most successful clubs, and have played in the national league system since 1988.[^cite_note-1] Rio Gallegos' oldest [association football](10568.md) club are [Boxing Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Boxing_Club) founded August 1920 and Hispano Americano founded 17 December 1925.

## Climate



Average temperatures (min., avrg., max.) and precipitations.

Rio Gallegos has a [cold steppe climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_semi-arid_climate) (*BSk*, under the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification)).[^cite_note-Climate-Data.org-2] The climate is dry, windy and cold, with occasional snow during the winter.[^cite_note-3] It is one of the windiest cities on earth, with winds above 50 km/h (31 mph) being commonplace, and over 100 km/h (62 mph) not exceptional. Winter temperatures average 5 °C (41 °F) during the day and −2 °C (28 °F) at night, but have dropped as low as −20 °C (−4 °F). Summers are extremely windy and cloudy, with days reaching a cool 19 °C (66 °F) and nights dropping to a relatively cold 7 °C (45 °F).[^cite_note-IRN-4] Cold weather can occur at any time, and despite the very low precipitation, light drizzle and cloudy days are quite common. The highest temperature recorded was 35.8 °C (96 °F) on February 5, 2019 while the record low is −20.2 °C (−4 °F) on July 12, 1982.[^cite_note-SMN3-5][^cite_note-meteoclimat-6]

## Transportation

The city is served by [Piloto Civil Norberto Fernández International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piloto_Civil_Norberto_Fern%C3%A1ndez_International_Airport), with four commercial airlines, including [Aerolineas Argentinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerolineas_Argentinas) and an international one, [LATAM Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LATAM_Chile).&#x20;
