After a [major withdrawal of combat troops in Iraq in 2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Withdrawal_of_combat_troops_in_Iraq_in_2021), Iraq and the U.S. began discussing the complete withdrawal of American troops from Iraq in September 2024.[^cite_note-2] In September 2024, President [Joe Biden](145422.md) announced that he would end the U.S. mission in Iraq by 2026.[^cite_note-3] Following the election of president [Donald Trump](4848272.md), the withdrawal continued and was completed in September 2026.[^cite_note-4]

## Background

The United States completed its [withdrawal of troops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Withdrawal_of_U.S._troops_from_Iraq_\(2007%E2%80%932011\)) in December 2011, concluding the [Iraq War](5043324.md).[^cite_note-5]

In October 2014, the United States formed [Combined Joint Task Force – Operation Inherent Resolve](44618350.md) (CJTF-OIR) and [re-intervened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American-led_intervention_in_Iraq_\(2014%E2%80%93present\)) at the request of the Iraqi government due to the rise of the [Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant](9087364.md) (ISIL).[^cite_note-6] Iran also [intervened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_intervention_in_Iraq_\(2014%E2%80%93present\)) in Iraq in June 2014.[^cite_note-7] On 9 December 2017, Iraq declared victory against ISIS, concluding the 2013–2017 [War in Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Iraq_\(2013%E2%80%932017\)) and commencing the latest [Islamic State (IS) insurgency in Iraq](58580238.md).[^cite_note-8]

In March 2020, the U.S.-led coalition, [Combined Joint Task Force – Operation Inherent Resolve](44618350.md) (CJTF–OIR), began transferring control over a number of military installations back to [Iraqi security forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_security_forces), citing developments in the [multi-year mission](44120613.md) against the [Islamic State](9087364.md) (IS). By 4 April 2020, four bases had been transferred. The base transfers and withdrawal were accelerated due to the [COVID-19 pandemic in Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Iraq) and the threat of Iranian [proxy elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violent_non-state_actor).[^cite_note-9]

In February 2021, [NATO](21133.md) announced it would expand its mission to train Iraqi forces in their [fight against the Islamic State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_State_insurgency_in_Iraq_\(2017%E2%80%93present\)), partially reversing the U.S.-led troop withdrawal.[^cite_note-10] In December 2021, [U.S. Central Command](338482.md) stated that there were no plans for a total withdrawal of U.S. forces from Iraq, citing continued threats posed by ISIS.[^cite_note-11]

In December 2021 the U.S. ended the combat mission.[^cite_note-12]

From 2023 to 2024 there were attacks on U.S. bases; like Al-Asad air base[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] and Al-Harir air base.[^cite_note-15]

## Withdrawal

### August 2025: Start of the withdrawal

U.S. troops first withdrawal from [Al-Asad Airbase](3832376.md).[^cite_note-16]

### January 2026: First base full withdrawal in Iraq

The U.S. fully transfers [Al-Asad Airbase](3832376.md),[^cite_note-17] ending presence federally in Iraq.[^cite_note-18]

### March 2026: The Iran war in Iraq

See also: [Iraq in the 2026 Iran war](82560534.md)

There have been attacks on U.S. bases in Iraq since the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md) started as Iran aligned Iraqi groups fire at American military personnel and infrastructure and is hit back by them.[^cite_note-19] Since March 2026 [NATO](21133.md) has no troops in Iraq due to the Iran war.[^cite_note-20]

### July 2026: Complete withdrawal reaffirmed by Iraq

During Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi's visit to Washington, the United States and Iraq reaffirmed that all remaining U.S. military forces would withdraw from Iraq by 30 September 2026, marking the end of the U.S.-led military mission in the country.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

### September 2026: Complete withdrawal is finalized

All remaining U.S. forces withdrew from Iraq on 30 September 2026, marking the end of the withdrawal process.[^cite_note-24] A small contingent of U.S. troops responsible for protecting diplomats remain.[^cite_note-25]

This withdrawal led to a new Iraqi holiday known as [Iraq's Sovereignty Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq's_Sovereignty_Day), which is a four-day long Holiday beginning from 30 September 2026.[^cite_note-K24Sovereignty-26] The [Iraqi security forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_security_forces) held parades in [Baghdad](4492.md) and [Najaf](200512.md) to celebrate the holiday.[^cite_note-27] Iraqis were seen celebrating including in Baghdad and Basra were civilians flew flags belonging to Shi'ite Militias and portraits of militia leaders.[^cite_note-28]
