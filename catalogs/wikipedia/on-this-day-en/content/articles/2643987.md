# Light aircraft



A typical light aircraft: the Cessna 172.



With a maximum gross takeoff weight of 12,500 lbs, the [DHC-6 Twin Otter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DHC-6_Twin_Otter) is an example of the upper limit of the light aircraft category.



A [Robinson R44](1491866.md) light helicopter

A **light aircraft** is an [aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft) that has a [maximum gross takeoff weight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_Takeoff_Weight) of 12,500 lb (5,670 kg) or less.[^cite_note-Crane-1]

Light aircraft are used as [utility aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utility_aircraft) commercially for small-scale passenger and [freight transport](2375692.md); for sightseeing, photography, [cropdusting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cropdusting), and other so-called aerial work roles of [civil aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_aviation); for the personal-use aspect of [general aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_aviation); and in certain aspects of [military aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_aviation).

Examples of aircraft that are at the maximum gross takeoff weight for this category include the [de Havilland Canada DHC-6 Twin Otter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Havilland_Canada_DHC-6_Twin_Otter) and [Beechcraft B200 Super King Air](9023483.md).[^cite_note-Viking-2][^cite_note-Janes-3]

## Uses

Uses include aerial surveying, such as monitoring pipelines,[^cite_note-4] light cargo operations, such as ["feeding"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_airline) cargo hubs,[^cite_note-5] and passenger operations. Light aircraft are used for marketing purposes, such as banner towing and [skywriting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skywriting), and [flight instruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_instruction). The majority of personal aircraft are light aircraft, the [most popular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-produced_aircraft) in history being the [Cessna 172](173462.md), and most popular in modern history being the [Cirrus SR22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirrus_SR22) and [Robinson R44](1491866.md). Larger light aircraft, such as twin [turboprops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turboprop) and [very light jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Very_light_jets), are often used as [business aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_aircraft). Most [floatplanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floatplanes) also fall into the category of light aircraft.
