**National Highway 25** (**NH 25**) is a [National Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_\(India\)) in India that connects Barmer and Beawar in the state of Rajasthan. It has recently been widened to four lanes by the [National Highways Authority of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highways_Authority_of_India).[^cite_note-renumber-1]
