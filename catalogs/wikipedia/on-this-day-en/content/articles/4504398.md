The **Legislative Assembly of the Falkland Islands** is the [unicameral legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicameral_legislature) of the [British Overseas Territory](207114.md) of the [Falkland Islands](7550182.md). The Legislative Assembly replaced the [Legislative Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Council_of_the_Falkland_Islands) (which had existed since 1845) when the new [Constitution of the Falklands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Falkland_Islands) came into force in 2009 and laid out the composition, powers and procedures of the islands' legislature.

The Legislative Assembly consists of eight elected members, the clerk of Assembly, two [*ex officio* members](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex_officio_member) (the [Chief Executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Executive_of_the_Falkland_Islands) and the [Financial Secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Director_of_Finance_of_the_Falkland_Islands)), and the [Speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_Legislative_Assembly_of_the_Falkland_Islands). Although they take part in proceedings, the *ex officio* members do not have the right to vote in the Legislative Assembly. The [Attorney General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney_General_of_the_Falkland_Islands) and the [Commander British Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_the_Falkland_Islands) also has the right to take part in the proceedings of the Legislative Assembly, though again they may not vote.[^cite_note-1]

## Powers and role

Meetings of the Legislative Assembly are normally held in the Court and Assembly Chamber in Stanley Town Hall and begin at a time appointed by the [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_the_Falkland_Islands). The constitution states there must be at least one meeting of the Legislative Assembly every year, although the Assembly normally meets every month. Meetings of the Legislative Assembly are broadcast live on the local radio station, the [Falkland Islands Radio Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falkland_Islands_Radio_Service).

The members of the Legislative Assembly (MLAs) have office facilities at Sulivan House, Stanley.[^cite_note-2] They moved there in January 2026 from their old offices at [Gilbert House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert_House_\(Falkland_Islands\)), [Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley,_Falkland_Islands).

The constitution gives the Legislative Assembly [legislative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_making) powers for "the [peace, order and good government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace,_order,_and_good_government) of the Falkland Islands." Any MLA may introduce a [bill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_\(proposed_law\)) or propose any motion for debate. However, the Legislative Assembly is not permitted to propose a bill which makes alterations to the taxes or finances on the Islands, unless given permission to do so by the Governor, as this is the remit of the Director of Finance and the Public Accounts Committee who are elected by the Legislative Assembly and whose proposals are voted on by the Legislative Assembly. Most motions in the Assembly, including bills, are passed by a [simple majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurality_\(voting\)) of the elected members with the presiding officer having the [casting vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casting_vote).

The [presiding officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_\(politics\)) of the Assembly is the Speaker (or the Deputy Speaker in the Speaker's absence) who is elected by the MLAs. Unlike the [Speaker of the House of Commons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_House_of_Commons_\(United_Kingdom\)), the Speaker of the Legislative Assembly does not need to be a member of the Assembly. The Speaker and the Deputy Speaker are elected for the life of the Legislative Assembly though can be removed via a [motion of no confidence](208101.md) voted for by six or more MLAs. During the Speaker election, the [Attorney General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney_General_of_the_Falkland_Islands) acts as presiding officer.

The Assembly is governed by a number of [standing orders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rules_of_order) which lay out the regulations for proceedings during Assembly meetings. Also, MLAs are granted [parliamentary privilege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_privilege) in the proceedings of the Legislative Assembly. There is also a Clerk of the Legislative Assembly who has a similar role to that of the [Clerk of the House of Commons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clerk_of_the_House_of_Commons).

Since the [2013 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Falkland_Islands_general_election) MLAs have been paid a salary, rather than just expenses, and are expected to work full-time, giving up whatever jobs or business interests they may have previously held.[^cite_note-3]

## Elections

There must be a [general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_election) on the Falklands at least once every four years, although there can be an election at any time. As in most [Westminster systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_system), the election campaign officially begins with the dissolution of the legislature. In the Falklands the Governor dissolves the Legislative Assembly by [proclamation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proclamation) at the request of the [Executive Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Council_of_the_Falkland_Islands). There must be an election within 70 days of the dissolution and the Governor retains the power to recall a dissolved Legislative Assembly before the election, though only in the event of an emergency.

The Falkland Islands are divided into two constituencies, [Camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_\(constituency\)) and [Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_\(constituency\)). Camp returns three elected members and Stanley returns five elected members using [block voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurality-at-large_voting). The Stanley constituency consists of the area within 3.5 mi (5.6 km) of the spire of [Christ Church Cathedral, Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church_Cathedral_\(Falkland_Islands\)), while the Camp constituency consists of the rest of the Territory.[^cite_note-4] The Constitution allows for the constituencies and their boundaries to be amended, but such an amendment must be agreed to by two-thirds of the vote in a [referendum](51582.md) of the islanders.

Anyone aged eighteen years or over on the date of the election and registered to vote in the Falklands qualifies to be elected as a member of the Legislative Assembly in the constituency where they are registered. The Legislative Assembly is elected through [universal suffrage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_suffrage), which means that a person can vote if they are eighteen years or over on the date of the election, have [Falkland Islands status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falkland_Islands_status), are a [British citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_nationality_law), a [British Overseas Territories citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Overseas_Territories_citizen) or a [British Overseas citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Overseas_citizen), and are a resident in the Falkland Islands on the date of the election.[^cite_note-5]

Someone can lose their right to vote if they have been certified [insane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insane) or of unsound mind under any law, if they are serving a sentence of imprisonment for a term of at least twelve months, if they have been convicted of an offence relating to elections, or if they have any acknowledgement of allegiance, obedience or adherence to a foreign power or state. Regular members of [His Majesty's Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/His_Majesty's_Armed_Forces) are also not allowed to vote.

If there is any dispute over the election of the Assembly, or the validity of anyone to vote or stand for election, the [Supreme Court of the Falkland Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_the_Falkland_Islands) has the jurisdiction to resolve the dispute.

### By-elections

See also: [List of Falkland Islands by-elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Falkland_Islands_by-elections)

If an elected member of the Legislative Assembly vacates their seat for any reason other than a dissolution of the Assembly, there is a [by-election](155434.md) to fill the empty seat. The by-election must be held within 70 days the vacancy occurring, unless the Assembly is due to be dissolved within 126 days.

## Oath or affirmation

Under section 42 of the constitution, before entering upon the functions of their office, the MLAs must take the [oath of allegiance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oath_of_allegiance) and the oath of office. The wording for the oath of office is specified in Annex B to the Constitution:

> "I, *name*, do swear (or solemnly affirm) that I will well and truly serve His Majesty King Charles the Third, His Heirs and Successors, and the people of the Falkland Islands, and will uphold the Constitution and other laws in force in the Falkland Islands, as a member of the Legislative Assembly. So help me God."

## Committees

The committees of the Legislative Assembly of the Falkland Islands include:

- Public Accounts Committee (PAC) – Examines government finances, public expenditure, and audit reports

- Standing Orders Committee – Reviews and recommends changes to the Assembly's rules and procedures

- House Committee – Oversees the administration and operation of the Legislative Assembly

- [Constitution Select Committee](#Constitution_Select_Committee) – Considers constitutional matters and possible constitutional reforms

- [Legislation Select Committee](#Legislation_Select_Committee) – Examines bills, draft laws, and legislative matters referred to it by the Assembly

- Privileges Committee – Deals with parliamentary privilege and matters affecting Assembly members and proceedings

- Records Committee – Oversees the management and preservation of Assembly records and official documents

- [Advisory Committee on the Prerogative of Mercy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advisory_Committee_on_the_Prerogative_of_Mercy) – Advises the Governor on pardons, sentence reductions, and other exercises of mercy

In addition, the Legislative Assembly may establish Special Select Committees to investigate specific issues or conduct inquiries when required.

### Advisory Committee on the Prerogative of Mercy

The Governor has the power to grant a [pardon](288593.md) to any person concerned in or convicted of an offence, but the Governor can only use this power after consultation with the Advisory Committee on the Prerogative of Mercy. The Committee consist of two elected members of the [Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_the_Falklands) (appointed by the Governor on the advice of the Legislative Assembly), the Chief Executive, the Attorney General and the Chief Medical Officer.

### Legislation Select Committee

The Legislation Select Committee of the Legislative Assembly of the Falkland Islands examines bills, draft laws, regulations, and other legislative matters referred to it by the Legislative Assembly. It reviews proposed legislation, considers its legal and practical implications, and makes recommendations to the Assembly. The committee helps ensure that laws are thoroughly scrutinised and improved before they are debated and enacted.

### Constitution Select Committee

The Constitution Select Committee of the Legislative Assembly of the Falkland Islands reviews constitutional matters and considers proposals for constitutional reform. It examines the territory's system of government, electoral arrangements, and the relationship between the Falkland Islands and the United Kingdom, making recommendations to the Legislative Assembly on potential constitutional changes.
