**Mohi El-Din Salem Ahmed Ibrahim** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic): محي الدين سالم أحمد إبراهيم; born 1 January 1957),[^cite_note-govsudan-1] also transliterated as **Mohieldin Salim Ahmed Ibrahim**[^cite_note-2] and **Muhi al-Din Salem Ahmed Ibrahim**,[^cite_note-3] is a Sudanese diplomat and politician.[^cite_note-4]

## Career

Mohi El-Din Salem completed a bachelor of laws at the [University of Khartoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Khartoum) in 1979, and was called to the bar in 1982. He obtained a diploma from the Institute for Diplomatic Studies in [Cairo](6293.md) the following year.[^cite_note-govsudan-1]

El-Din Salem joined the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in 1980. His diplomatic postings include serving as Sudan's ambassador to the United Arab Emirates (1999–2004), to Ethiopia and the [African Union](10634933.md) (2007–2011), and to Kuwait (2015–2019).[^cite_note-govsudan-1] He was appointed [minister of foreign affairs](18387433.md) in September 2025,[^cite_note-5] and has played a key role in attempting to end the [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md).[^cite_note-6]
