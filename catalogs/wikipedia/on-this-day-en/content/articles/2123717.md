**Kano State** [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/f/ff/LL-Q56475\_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-kano.wav/LL-Q56475\_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-kano.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/f/ff/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-kano.wav/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-kano.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> ([Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language): *Jihar Kano* جِهَرْ كَنُوَ) is one of the 36 [states](226734.md) of [Nigeria](21383.md), located in the northern region of the country.[^cite_note-\:0-5] According to the 2006 national [census](6889.md), Kano State was the [most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population) state in [Nigeria](21383.md). Created in 1967 out of the former [Northern Region](2292852.md), Kano State shares a border with [Katsina State](2227638.md) to the [northwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwest), [Jigawa State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jigawa_State) to the northeast, [Bauchi State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bauchi_State) to the southeast, and [Kaduna State](2227637.md) to the [southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest).[^cite_note-\:0-5] The state's capital city is [Kano](508782.md), the largest in Nigeria after [Lagos](85232.md).[^cite_note-\:0-5] The incumbent [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor) of the state is [Abba Kabir Yusuf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abba_Kabir_Yusuf), sworn in on 29 May 2023.[^cite_note-6]

&#x20;Two major ethnic groups, the Hausa and Fulani, make up the majority of Kano State's population.[^cite_note-\:4-7] Hausa is the dominant language in the state, as in most parts of [Northern Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nigeria).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Kano State has most recently faced challenges such as attacks by the [Islamist terrorist](566821.md) group [Boko Haram](23769768.md),[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-B-11][^cite_note-12] inter-religious violence,[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-bbc-14] and [extreme poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_poverty).[^cite_note-15] Kano State operates under [Sharia law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharia_law), within the legal framework of the [Nigerian Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Constitution).[^cite_note-16]

## History

Modern-day Kano State was the site of a number of kingdoms and empires including the [Kingdom of Kano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Kano), which was centred on [Dalla Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalla_Hill) (Hausa: *Dutsen Dala*) from to 1000CE to 1349.[^cite_note-17] In 1349, the [Sultanate of Kano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Kano) was established when [Yaji I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaji_I) converted to [Islam](6037917.md) and became its first Sultan.[^cite_note-18] In 1463, [Kurmi Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurmi_Market) was opened, which helped the city of Kano become a centre of [commercial activity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Saharan_trade) in [Hausaland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausaland).[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-\:1-20][^cite_note-21] The market remains open today and its historic importance is reflected in the state's nickname, the *Centre of Commerce*.[^cite_note-22]

During the 16th and 17th centuries, the Sultanate of Kano became one of the most powerful of the [Hausa Kingdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_Kingdoms).[^cite_note-23] After the Fulani [jihad](16203.md) of 1804-08, the [Kano Emirate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kano_Emirate) deposed the former sultanate.[^cite_note-24] Emir [Ibrahim Dabo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Dabo) made a number of administrative reforms, seeking to increase commerce in the region.[^cite_note-25]

Leaders during this time encouraged traders to move from [Katsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_\(city\)), capitalising on raids from the Hausa Sultanate of [Maradi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maradi_Region).[^cite_note-26] The leaders of the caliphate encouraged the [kola nut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kola_nut) trade, and Kano was the greatest beneficiary with an annual turnover of about \$30 million. [Craft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft) industries also evolved in the pre-colonial period contributing to the prosperity of the province.

In 1903, the [British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) [conquered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kano) the Kano Emirate, incorporating it into the [Northern Nigeria Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nigeria_Protectorate).[^cite_note-27] The major ethnic groups in pre-colonial Kano were the [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people), [Fulani](102383.md), Barebari ([Kanuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanuri_people)), [Tuareg](103054.md), [Arab](2185.md), and [Nupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_people). Most people in Kano speak [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language) as a first language, while some identify as ethnically Hausa.[^cite_note-28]

Since [Nigerian independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_\(Nigeria\)), Kano State has developed a diverse economy, establishing itself as a centre for industry,[^cite_note-\:2-29] [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture),[^cite_note-\:1-20] and [Islamic banking.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_banking_and_finance)[^cite_note-\:3-30] Kano State was created on 27 May 1967.[^cite_note-\:0-5] In 1991, part of Kano State was separated to form [Jigawa State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jigawa_State).

## Geography

### Climate

The rainfall in Kano State has variable rainfall with [drought](37335.md) and near-drought conditions between the 1970s and 1980s. Between the 1990s and the year 2015, the moisture conditions improved considerably but rainfall still fluctuated.[^cite_note-31] Since 2015, there has been a considerable increase in rainfall.[^cite_note-32] Based on a report of the [Nigerian Meteorological Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Meteorological_Agency) (NIMET), rainfall averaged by a mean of 897.7 mm (35.34 in).[^cite_note-33]

In the first quarter of 2022, NIMET sounded an early warning for floods in some states, including Kano. The agency asserted that their warning was based on the amount and distribution of rainfall that had been observed in the nation during the rainy season. According to the Kano State Emergency Management Agency (SEMA), 25 local government districts experienced flooding as a result of the extreme rain.[^cite_note-34]

### Temperature

The temperature of Kano has risen significantly since the 1960s.[^cite_note-35] The annual temperature of the state is between 26 °C to 30 °C for the diurnal temperature, which is high, and measurable between the range of 13.1%, with the [relative humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humidity) of between 17% and 90% respectively.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37]

## Economy

Many large markets exist within Kano today, such as Kurmi Market, Kantin Kwari Market,[^cite_note-38] [Sabon Gari Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabon_Gari_Market), Dawanau Market,[^cite_note-39] Kofar Wanbai Market, Galadima Market, Yankura Market and Bata Market.[^cite_note-\:1-20] Many of these markets specialise in certain products, such as [textiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile) or [grain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grain).[^cite_note-\:1-20] The dye industry beginning in the 19th century contributed greatly to Kano's economy.[^cite_note-40]

### Agriculture

[Subsistence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsistence_agriculture) and commercial agriculture are mostly practised in the outlying districts of the state. Some of the food crops cultivated are [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet), [cowpeas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowpeas), [sorghum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorghum), [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize) and [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice) for local consumption, while [groundnuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut) and [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton) are produced for export and industrial purposes. During the colonial period and several years after the country's independence, the groundnuts produced in the state constituted one of the major sources of revenue of the country. Kano State is a major producer of hides and skins, [sesame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesame), [soybeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soybean), cotton, [garlic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garlic), [gum arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gum_arabic) and [chili peppers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chili_pepper).[^cite_note-41]

A 2018 study of [Tudun Wada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tudun_Wada_\(Kano_state\)) found that both temperature and rainfall were likely to increase with climate change, causing increased stress on crops, and would require increased [climate change adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change_adaptation) for agricultural practices.[^cite_note-42]

### Industry

Kano State is the second-largest industrial centre after [Lagos State](626168.md) in Nigeria and the largest in northern Nigeria. Some of its industries include but are not limited to, textiles, [tanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanning_\(leather\)), [footwear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Footwear), [cosmetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmetics), [plastics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic), enamelware, pharmaceuticals, [ceramics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceramic), [furniture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furniture).[^cite_note-\:2-29] including [agricultural implements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_machinery), [soft drinks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_drink), food and beverages, [dairy products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy_product), [vegetable oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetable_oil), and animal feed.[^cite_note-43] Kano is also the centre of a growing [Islamic banking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_banking) industry in Nigeria.[^cite_note-\:3-30][^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48]

## Transport

### Federal highways

- [A2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A2_highway_\(Nigeria\)) (part of the [Trans-Sahara Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Sahara_Highway) or TAH2) north from [Kaduna State](2227637.md) at Gidan Mallam Idi for 166 km as Zaria Road via Kura, [Nassarawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasarawa,_Kano_State), Kano, Bankaura, Kunya, [Dambarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dambatta) and Ajumawa to [Jigawa State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jigawa_State) at Yanzaki,

- [A9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A9_highway_\(Nigeria\)) (part of the Dakar-Ndjamena [Trans-Sahelian Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Sahelian_Highway) or TAH 5) northwest from A2 at Bankaura (14 km north of Kano city) for 71 km via Bichi, Dan Zabuwa and Tsanyawa as IBB Way to [Katsina State](2227638.md) at Yan Kamaye,

- [A237](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A237_highway_\(Nigeria\)) continuing TAH5 east from A2 in Kano for 110 km across the [Hadejia River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadejia_River) at Wudil via Takai and Kachako to [Jigawa State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jigawa_State) as the Kano Rd.

## Demographics

### Population

According to the 2006 PON census figures, Kano State had a population totalling 9,401,288. Based on the official data by the [National Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Bureau_of_Statistics,_Nigeria),[^cite_note-65] Kano is the most populous state in the country followed by [Lagos State](626168.md).[^cite_note-vanguardngr.com-66] The state is mostly populated by the [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people) and [Fulani](102383.md) people.[^cite_note-\:4-7]

## Languages

The official languages of Kano State are [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language) and [Fulfulde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulfulde).[^cite_note-68] Several [Kainji languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainji_languages), namely [Moro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moro_language_\(Nigeria\)), [Kurama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurama_language), and [Map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map_language), are also spoken in the [Doguwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doguwa) local government area.

## Politics

The state government is led by a democratically elected governor who works closely with the state House of Assembly. The capital city of the state is [Kano](508782.md).[^cite_note-auto1-80]

### Electoral system

The governor of each state is selected using a modified two-round system. To be elected in the first round, a candidate must receive the plurality of the vote and over 25% of the vote in at least two-thirds of the state's [LGAs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_areas_of_Nigeria). If no candidate passes this threshold, a second round of voting is held between the two, highest-ranking candidates.[^cite_note-auto1-80]
