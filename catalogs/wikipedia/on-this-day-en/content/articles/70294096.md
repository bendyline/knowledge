**Harir Air Base** ([ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **ORBR**; [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language): بنکەی ئاسمانیی ھەریر), is a [special operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_operations) air base located near to the town of [Harir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harir,_Iraq), in the [Erbil Governorate](463860.md), [Kurdistan Region](3949386.md), [Iraq](7515928.md).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

## History

In 1983, the base, previously known as **Bashur Airfield**, was constructed to improve the deployment flexibility of the [Iraqi Air Force](1817852.md). It was also as part of a national drive to construct new airfields and renovate existing airfields. It was in the early stages of construction by June 1983. A single 2,600 meter long runway orientated NW/SE was built, equipped with two taxiways and an apron.[^cite_note-3]

The base was seized during [Operation Northern Delay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Northern_Delay) by the [United States Armed Forces](32212.md) as part of the [2003 invasion of Iraq](201936.md). During the operation to seize the base, American paratroopers of the [173rd Airborne Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/173rd_Airborne_Brigade)[^cite_note-4] reportedly believed they had landed in friendly territory after being greeted by local Kurds and encountering no resistance.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

Soon after the invasion, the [Kurdistan Regional Government](1330553.md) (KRG) encouraged the [United States](3434750.md) to take advantage of the Kurdish region's stability and offered to host a long-term U.S. military presence at the Harir Air Base, but the proposal was declined. U.S. troops later [withdrew entirely from Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Withdrawal_of_United_States_troops_from_Iraq_\(2007%E2%80%932011\)) and the base had been largerly abandoned.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

In 2014, reports emerged of negotiations between the KRG and the U.S. over the possible use of the site to conduct airstrikes against the [Islamic State](9087364.md). [Iraqi Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Arabs) politicians reacted angrily to the reports. Majid al-Gharawi, a Baghdad lawmaker, said that “U.S. intentions to build a military base in the area of Harir are unacceptable,” adding that the move was aimed at reoccupying Iraq and exerting pressure on the central government for further concessions.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

As part of [Operation Inherent Resolve](44120613.md) US military forces returned to the base in the [war against the Islamic State](43517689.md). The base hosted various American aircraft and units,[^cite_note-6] including [special operation forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_special_operations_forces), such as:

- [Pilatus U-28A Draco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilatus_U-28_Draco) from the [Air Force Special Operations Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Special_Operations_Command)

  [^cite_note-AFMAPR22-37-7]

- [Boeing MH-47G Chinook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_CH-47_Chinook#MH-47G) from the [160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment (Airborne)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/160th_Special_Operations_Aviation_Regiment_\(Airborne\)) (160th SOAR (A))

  [^cite_note-AFMAPR22-37-7]

- [Sikorsky MH-60M Black Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_UH-60_Black_Hawk#Special_purpose) from the 160th SOAR (A)

  [^cite_note-AFMAPR22-37-7]

- [General Atomics MQ-1C Gray Eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Atomics_MQ-1C_Gray_Eagle) of the [United States Army](32087.md)

  [^cite_note-AFMMAR17-78-8]

During the war against the Islamic State the base was also home to a unit of the [Delta Force](328809.md).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

After the defeat of the Islamic State, the [U.S. force at the base was gradually reduced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Withdrawal_of_United_States_troops_from_Iraq_\(2020%E2%80%932021\)), though a small contingent remained as of October 2025.[^cite_note-\:2-9]

On 30 September 2026 U.S. forces withdrew from the air base and Iraqi forces now control the air base.[^cite_note-10]
