**Nasdaq Dubai** is a [Dubai](211583.md)-based [stock exchange](28168.md) that lists regional and international shares in the [Middle East](19323.md). Through the exchange, regional issuers can access regional and international investment. International issuers can access investment from the region, through a primary or dual listing.

The NASDAQ Dubai region includes the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) and the rest of the Gulf Cooperation Council ([GCC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperation_Council_for_the_Arab_States_of_the_Gulf)), the wider Middle East and North Africa, [Turkey](11125639.md) and the [Indian sub-continent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_subcontinent).

The majority shareholder of NASDAQ Dubai is [DFM](2568867.md), which acquired two thirds of the shares in May 2010 from [Borse Dubai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borse_Dubai) and [NASDAQ OMX Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASDAQ_OMX_Group). Borse Dubai still owns one third of the shares.

NASDAQ Dubai is located in the [Dubai International Financial Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_International_Financial_Centre), a [financial free zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_economic_zone). The exchange is regulated by the [Dubai Financial Services Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_Financial_Services_Authority).

## History

The exchange launched on 26 September 2005 under the name Dubai International Financial Exchange (DIFX), which initially listed [equities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock) and [index](23408957.md) products.

In 2007, the exchange listed the largest [IPO](63879.md) in the Middle East, when [DP World](3272467.md) raised US\$4.96 billion.

In 2008, [NASDAQ OMX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASDAQ_OMX_Group) acquired a one-third stake in the DIFX and it rebranded to NASDAQ Dubai. The other two-thirds was owned by [Borse Dubai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borse_Dubai), the holding company of NASDAQ Dubai and [Dubai Financial Market](2568867.md) (DFM).[^cite_note-1]

In 2008, NASDAQ Dubai launched the first [equity derivatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equity_derivative) market in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md).[^cite_note-2]

In 2010, the exchange took a step towards creating a single regional liquidity pool by moving all its equities trading on to DFM's trading platform. The same year [DFM](2568867.md) acquired a two-thirds stake in NASDAQ Dubai, with [Borse Dubai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borse_Dubai) retaining a one-third stake.

In 2011, the exchange transferred its Listing Authority from its own control to that of the Dubai Financial Services Authority.

In 2012, the DFSA reduced the minimum [market capitalisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_capitalization) for companies to list to US\$10 million, from US\$50 million previously. This change has opened the door to listings by [small and medium-sized enterprises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_and_medium_enterprises), including family owned businesses.[^cite_note-3]

In 2013, the exchange launched a [fixed income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bond_market) trading platform, allowing investors to trade [Sukuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukuk) and conventional [bonds](60737.md) on-exchange.[^cite_note-4]

In October 2013, the [Bank of London and The Middle East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BLME) (BLME) listed its [shares](867877.md) on the exchange.[^cite_note-5]

In April 2014, Emirates REIT listed its shares following its IPO, in the first listing of a [Real Estate Investment Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_estate_investment_trust) (REIT) on an exchange in the [GCC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperation_Council_for_the_Arab_States_of_the_Gulf). Emirates REIT raised 201 million dollars and was 3.5 times oversubscribed.[^cite_note-6]

In the same month, the exchange launched an Islamic financing [Murabaha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murabaha) platform. This offers an alternative to many traditional Murabaha solutions, which can carry a risk of losses through price movements, [spreads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bid%E2%80%93offer_spread) and poor [liquidity](56121.md) as well as delays.[^cite_note-7]

In June 2014, NASDAQ Dubai had 28 current Sukuk listings with a total nominal value of US\$18.3 billion, which made Dubai (NASDAQ Dubai plus DFM) the third largest venue in the world for Sukuk listings.[^cite_note-8]

## Listed securities

Products listed on NASDAQ Dubai include ordinary shares listed by [DP World](3272467.md). DP World was the largest [IPO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPO) in the Middle East raising \$4.96 billion[^cite_note-9] until [Saudi Aramco](290527.md)'s [IPO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPO).[^cite_note-10] It was 15x oversubscribed and is one of the most valued companies in the region.[^cite_note-11]

In 2014, Emirates REIT carried out an [IPO](63879.md) on the exchange, becoming the first [Real Estate Investment Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_estate_investment_trust) to list in the GCC.[^cite_note-12]

The exchange is the third largest in the world for listed [Sukuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukuk) by value and conventional [bonds](60737.md) are also listed. [Equity derivatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equity_derivative) can be traded. The exchange operates a [Murabaha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murabaha) platform for Islamic financing.

## Trading hours

The trading hours of NASDAQ Dubai are from 10\:00 am to 3\:00 pm (06\:00 am to 11\:00 am GMT) from Monday to Friday.[^cite_note-13]

## The Dubai International Financial Centre

NASDAQ Dubai is located in the [Dubai International Financial Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_International_Financial_Centre) (DIFC), a [financial free zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_economic_zone) which opened for business in 2004. Financial activities in the DIFC are governed to international standards by an independent regulator, the Dubai Financial Services Authority. NASDAQ Dubai has a licence from the DFSA to operate an exchange. The President of the DIFC is [Sheikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh) [Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_bin_Rashid_Al_Maktoum), the UAE Vice President, Prime Minister and Ruler of Dubai.

## The Dubai Financial Services Authority

NASDAQ Dubai is regulated by the [Dubai Financial Services Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_Financial_Services_Authority), this structure falls into a number of different categories:

- Rules which regulate the process which companies must follow if they wish to have their securities admitted to trading on the market operated by NASDAQ Dubai.

  [^cite_note-14]

- Rules which regulate the obligations of companies after their securities have been admitted to trading (e.g., continuous disclosure rules) – also covered by the Issuers and Securities Rules

- Rules which regulate the process which an entity must follow in order to become a Member of NASDAQ Dubai.

  [^cite_note-15]

- Rules which regulate the behaviour of Members on the market operated by NASDAQ Dubai and the obligations which Members owe to their clients – also covered by the Business Rules.

- Rules which regulate Clearing and Settlement procedures are covered by the NASDAQ Dubai Business Rules.
