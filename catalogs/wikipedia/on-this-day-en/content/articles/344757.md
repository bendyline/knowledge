**Nonthaburi**[^cite_note-5] is one of the central [provinces](204637.md) (*changwat*) of [Thailand](30128.md), established by the [Act Establishing Changwat Samut Prakan, Changwat Nonthaburi, Changwat Samut Sakhon and Changwat Nakhon Nayok, Buddhist Era 2489 (1946)](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Translation\:Act%20Establishing%20Changwat%20Samut%20Prakan,%20Changwat%20Nonthaburi,%20Changwat%20Samut%20Sakhon%20and%20Changwat%20Nakhon%20Nayok,%20Buddhist%20Era%202489%20\(1946\)), which came into force on 9 May 1946 (Thursday).[^cite_note-6]

Neighboring provinces are (from north clockwise) [Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayutthaya_province), [Pathum Thani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pathum_Thani_province), [Bangkok](56615.md), and [Nakhon Pathom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakhon_Pathom_province). Nonthaburi is the most densely populated province after Bangkok. The [Bang Kwang Central Prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Kwang_Central_Prison) is in the province.

## Geography

The province of Nonthaburi is located on the [Chao Phraya River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chao_Phraya_River) basin in [central Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Thailand). It borders [Bangkok](56615.md) to the south and to the east, the provinces of [Pathum Thani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pathum_Thani_province) and [Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phra_Nakhon_Si_Ayutthaya_province) to the north, and [Nakhon Pathom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakhon_Pathom_province) to the west. The province is part of the greater [Bangkok Metropolitan Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok_Metropolitan_Region). With an area of 637 km<sup>2</sup> (246 sq mi), it is the third smallest province in the country by land area.&#x20;

In most parts, the provice is as urbanized as the capital,[^cite_note-7] and the boundary between the two provinces is nearly unrecognizable. There is no forest area in the province.[^cite_note-8]

## Climate

Nonthaburi province has a [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) category Aw). Winters are dry and warm. Temperatures rise until May. The monsoon season runs from May through October, with heavy rain and somewhat cooler temperatures during the day, although nights remain warm. Climate statistics: the maximum temperature is 41.1 °C (106.0 °F) in April and the lowest temperature is 18.2 °C (64.8 °F) in December. The highest average temperature is 38.2 °C (100.8 °F) and the minimum average temperature is 22.2 °C (72.0 °F). Annual total rainfall was 1,290 millimetres (51 in) with total 112 rainy days. Maximum daily rainfall was 76.6 mm (3.02 in) on September 20, 2024.[^cite_note-9]

## History

Historical affiliations

> &#x20;[Thailand](30128.md) [Kingdom of Siam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rattanakosin_Kingdom_\(1782%E2%80%931932\)) 1782–1932\
> &#x20;[Thailand](30128.md) [Kingdom of Thailand](30128.md) 1932–present

Nonthaburi was declared a city in the middle of the [16th century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_century). It was previously a village named Talat Khwan. During the reign of King [Prasat Thong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prasat_Thong), a canal was dug to create a shortcut for the flow of the Chao Phraya. The river changed its flow into the [new canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khlong_Om_Non), which is still the riverbed today. In 1665 King [Narai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narai) built a fortress, as the shorter river course was giving enemies an easier route to the capital, [Ayutthaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phra_Nakhon_Si_Ayutthaya_Province). The town was then moved near the fortress.

From 1943 to 1946 the province was incorporated into [Bangkok](56615.md).

## Symbols

The [provincial seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seals_of_the_provinces_of_Thailand) shows [earthenware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthenware), a traditional product of Nonthaburi. The [provincial tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Thai_provincial_trees) and flower is the [yellow flame tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_flame_tree) (*[Peltophorum pterocarpum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peltophorum_pterocarpum)*). Nonthaburi is a province where the Chao Phraya River crosses from north to south, and on the ground, the [Chao Phraya giant catfish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chao_Phraya_giant_catfish) (*[Pangasius sanitwongsei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangasius_sanitwongsei)*) is the provincial aquatic life.

The provincial slogan translates to "Grand royal mansion, renowned Suan Somdet, Ko Kret's pottery, famous ancient temples, tasty [durians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durian), and the beautiful government office". The royal mansion is a reference to Phra Tamnak Nonthaburi in [Mueang Nonthaburi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mueang_Nonthaburi_District), the former residence of Prince Maha [Vajiralongkorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajiralongkorn). Princess Mother Srinagarindra Garden (Suan Somdet) is a water garden with a statue of princess [Srinagarindra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srinagarindra) in the [Pak Kret District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pak_Kret_District). The provincial administration building once received an award as the most beautiful such building by the Ministry of Interior.

### Agriculture

Nonthaburi province is renowned for growing the best [durians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durian) in the country. Durian has been a well-known fruit in this province for 400 years.[^cite_note-Year_of_the_Durian-48] The fruit is known as "Durian Non" which means durian from Nonthaburi province.[^cite_note-stop-49] It is also known as the most expensive durian in the world.[^cite_note-Year_of_the_Durian-48] There are six groups of Nonthaburi durian which are Kop, Luang, Kan Yao, Kampan, Thong Yoi, and miscellaneous.[^cite_note-stop-49] Most durian orchards are near rivers such as the Chao Phraya. This is because the soil next to the river is good for planting which is also good for durian trees.[^cite_note-Year_of_the_Durian-48] Many durian orchards have disappeared due to flooding and pollution.[^cite_note-stop-49] The price of durian Non depends on its group. Kan Yao is the most expensive, starting from around 10,000 baht up to 20,000 baht (or about US\$600) for one durian. The Kan Yao itself is not easy to find in normal markets. The main reason for the high price is because it is rare. The recent flood in 2011 cleared out almost all of the durian trees in Nonthaburi, and only a few trees have been newly planted.[^cite_note-Year_of_the_Durian-48] Also, residential areas are expanding into agricultural areas.[^cite_note-stop-49]

Agriculture and fishery in Nonthaburi province, the sixteenth sector of the economy, generated 5,515 million baht (US\$145 million) or 1.4% of GPP with a workforce of 16,366 (1.3% of all employed persons).

Agricultural land use 270 km<sup>2</sup> (100 sq mi) is 43.4% of total land of Nonthaburi province 622 km<sup>2</sup> (240 sq mi). This is divided as follows: [paddy land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_field): 152 km<sup>2</sup> (59 sq mi) 24.4%, [farmland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farmland): 70 km<sup>2</sup> (27 sq mi) 11,3%, [orchard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orchard)/[perennial crop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perennial_crop): 27 km<sup>2</sup> (10 sq mi) 4.3% and [vegetable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetable)/[ornamental plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ornamental_plant): 20 km<sup>2</sup> (7.7 sq mi) 3.2%.[^cite_note-50]

Production of the [arable crop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arable_crop): [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice) 113,403 [tonnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonnes).

Production of the eight main [vegetable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetable) crops: [lettuce](57079.md) 1,346 tonnes, [Chinese kale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_kale) 1,134 tonnes, [pakchoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakchoi) 912 tonnes, [water convolvulus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_convolvulus) 749 tonnes, [holy basil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_basil) 400 tonnes, [Chinese chive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_chive) 381 tonnes, [water mimosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_mimosa) 218 tonnes and [Thai basil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_basil) 174 tonnes.

Production of the five main [fruit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit) crops: [mango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango) 1,465 tonnes, [banana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana) 1,117 tonnes; sweet young [coconut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coconut) 569 tonnes, [lime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lime_\(fruit\)) 501 tonnes and [pomelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomelo) 387 tonnes.

### Animal husbandry

[Livestock](25160767.md) produced included: [chickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chickens) 113,905, [ducks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ducks) 93,663, [goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goats) 4,428 and [beef cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beef_cattle) 2,491.

### Fisheries

Total catch from 2,053 freshwater aquaculture farms amounted to 2,746 tonnes.
