On 30 March 2026, a school shooting took place at Mariano Moreno high school in [San Cristóbal, Santa Fe](4080045.md), Argentina. A 13-year-old was killed and eight others were injured before the suspect, a 15-year-old boy, was subdued and taken into custody. The shooting was the first major school shooting in Argentina since 2004.[^cite_note-Carmen-2]

## Background

The Escuela Normal Superior Nº40 "Mariano Moreno" is a public secondary school in [San Cristóbal](4080045.md), a small city in north-west [Santa Fe Province](520560.md) with a population of around 16,000.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-BAT-4] Months prior to the shooting in January, a group of classmates attacked and attempted to murder a 15-year-old girl. Three suspects aged 18, 17, and 16 were arrested.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

Argentina rarely records school shootings. Civilian gun ownership is tightly regulated and firearms are not commonly carried by teenagers. Previous school shootings included the [Rafael Calzada school shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Calzada_school_shooting) which killed one person in 2000 and the [Carmen de Patagones school shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmen_de_Patagones_school_shooting) that killed three in 2004, both in [Buenos Aires Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires_Province).[^cite_note-BAT-4][^cite_note-Carmen-2][^cite_note-7]

## Shooting

The shooter arrived at the school at 7\:01 a.m. and allegedly concealed a [shotgun](26838.md) believed to be his grandfather's inside of his guitar case before entering the school.[^cite_note-Shotgun-1][^cite_note-8] The shooting occurred at around 7\:15 a.m. while students were waiting to raise the flag, as they do each day before classes begin. The gunman first went into the upstairs restroom, locking himself inside a toilet cubicle to assemble his gun. He then fired on several students in the restroom, injuring them. In the restroom's doorway, the gunman fired four more shots, killing a student who was running away.[^cite_note-\:0-9] One witness said the shooter yelled "surprise!" before opening fire and seemed calm.[^cite_note-Infobae-10] The gunman reloaded his weapon and fired twice more at the courtyard below without hitting anyone. The school janitor then subdued the attacker.[^cite_note-\:0-9] Several police vehicles and emergency personnel arrived at the scene ten minutes after the first call.[^cite_note-Infobae1-11][^cite_note-BAT-4]

## Victims

13-year-old Ian Cabrera was killed and eight others were injured, including six non-gunshot victims.[^cite_note-Infobae-10][^cite_note-Infobae1-11] The six students, who sustained injuries from cut glass while breaking windows to escape,[^cite_note-\:2-12] were treated at the local hospital while the two gunshot victims were transferred to the Rafaela Regional Hospital, including one in serious but stable condition.[^cite_note-BAT-4]

## Suspect

The suspect was identified as a 15-year-old student at the school who lived in [Entre Ríos Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entre_R%C3%ADos_Province). He had experience with [long guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_gun) and used to go hunting in the area with his father.[^cite_note-Shotgun-1] He did not have a previous criminal record but was undergoing psychological treatment after a history of [self-harm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-harm) and [depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depression_\(mood\)).[^cite_note-Infobae2-13][^cite_note-BAT-4] Students, teachers and friends described him as a quiet but good kid with good grades and was not being bullied.[^cite_note-Infobae-10]

The shooting was not targeted at anyone. He was initially charged with aggravated murder involving the use of a firearm,[^cite_note-\:2-12] but held not criminally responsible because of his age, he will likely be sent to a psychiatric facility.[^cite_note-Infobae2-13] His father reportedly had substance abuse problems and his mother was on leave from work due to a psychiatric condition. He had shared videos of previous school shooters on social media, including Kosta Kecmanović, the perpetrator of the 2023 [Belgrade school shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade_school_shooting) that killed 10, [Eric Harris and Dylan Klebold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Harris_and_Dylan_Klebold), the perpetrators of the [Columbine High School massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbine_High_School_massacre) in 1999 that killed 14, [Seung-Hui Cho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seung-Hui_Cho), the perpetrator of the 2007 [Virginia Tech shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech_shooting) that killed 32, and [Elliot Rodger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliot_Rodger), the perpetrator of the [2014 Isla Vista killings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Isla_Vista_killings) that killed six. He also posted a video on [TikTok](56822861.md) praising Japanese author and failed coup leader [Yukio Mishima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yukio_Mishima) who killed himself in 1970, and set his profile picture as the [The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Nicolaes Tulp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Anatomy_Lesson_of_Dr._Nicolaes_Tulp), a 1632 painting made by [Rembrandt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rembrandt).[^cite_note-14]

## Aftermath

Classes were suspended and students were dismissed.[^cite_note-BAT-4] School activity was resumed on 15 April.[^cite_note-15]

On 31 March, three students at a technical school were arrested for carrying two concealed knives and a hand axe in their backpacks.[^cite_note-\:1-16] On 4 or 5 April, a 16-year-old boy was arrested in [Sunchales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunchales) for attempting to enter Alberdi School in [Rafaela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafaela). A search of his person revealed an [air rifle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_rifle) in his bag and online, he was found to have threatened a shooting at the school. A loaded revolver was found at the boy's home.[^cite_note-\:1-16] On 5 April, a 15-year-old boy was reported by former classmates for threatening a shooting at Domingo Savio school in [Aldo Bonzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldo_Bonzi). The boy had not attended since 2024 and posted audio messages containing death threats and images of firearms in a group chat for twenty days. A search of his phone found that he was in the same [WhatsApp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WhatsApp) chat group as the perpetrator of the San Cristóbal shooting.[^cite_note-17]

## Investigation

Authorities in Santa Fe Province launched an investigation into the shooting.[^cite_note-18] The Anti-Terrorism Investigation Unit Department (DUIA) is also involved.[^cite_note-\:1-16]

Santa Fe Investigative Police (PDI) seized the shotgun, ammunition, spent casings, a cartridge container, a cartridge belt with several cartridges, shotgun pellets, a backpack and a black jumpsuit from the scene.[^cite_note-Shotgun-1] On 5 April, it was announced that the shooting was not an isolated incident with further investigations into the suspect's online activity.[^cite_note-\:0-9] On 6 April, a 16-year-old boy was arrested on suspicions of having prior knowledge of the attack and charged with obstruction of justice.[^cite_note-\:1-16]

On 8 April, [National Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Security_\(Argentina\)) Minister [Alejandra Monteoliva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandra_Monteoliva) and [Governor of Santa Fe Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Santa_Fe_Province) [Maximiliano Pullaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximiliano_Pullaro) stated that [psychosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychosis) and retaliation for bullying had been ruled out as motives. It is instead believed that the perpetrator had acted out of "[veneration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veneration) for violent crimes and murders", as he was involved in the [True Crime Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Crime_Community) (TCC), an [online community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_community) suspected of encouraging violent crime out of an admiration for the Columbine High School shooting. [Federal Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentine_Federal_Police) and the American [Federal Bureau of Investigation](11127.md) were investigating the influence of the TCC in fifteen other criminal acts committed in Argentina since 2024.[^cite_note-19] Both the suspected shooter and the boy charged with obstruction of justice were also linked to the [incel](57379245.md) subculture.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]
