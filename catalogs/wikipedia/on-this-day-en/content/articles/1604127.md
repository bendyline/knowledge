The **National Congress** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Congreso Nacional*) is the [legislative branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) of the government of [Honduras](13394.md).

## Organization

The Honduran Congress is a [unicameral](204304.md) legislature. The nominal [President of the National Congress of Honduras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_National_Congress_of_Honduras) is currently [Luis Redondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Redondo). Its members are 128 [deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies), who are elected on a [proportional representation](23298.md) basis, by [department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Honduras), to serve four-year terms.

## Meeting place

Congress meets in a purpose-built legislative palace *(Palacio Legislativo)* in the centre of [Tegucigalpa](25994100.md). Of a [modernist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_architecture) design, it is painted in an array of bright colours and rests on a series of concrete pillars that separate it from the ground.
