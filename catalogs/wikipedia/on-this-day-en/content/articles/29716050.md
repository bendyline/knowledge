The Port of Duqm is integrated in the [Special Economic Zone at Duqm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Economic_Zone_at_Duqm?action=edit\&redlink=1) (SEZAD)

The **Port of Duqm**, also known as **Duqm Port**,[^cite_note-ASYAD-2] is a [seaport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port) and road terminal[^cite_note-UNECE-1] located at [Duqm](3887884.md) in the [Al Wusta governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Wusta_Governorate_\(Oman\)) of [Oman](22316.md). Duqm Port, integrated in the [Special Economic Zone at Duqm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Economic_Zone_at_Duqm?action=edit\&redlink=1) (SEZAD), and located 550 [kilometres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometre) (342 [miles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mile)) south of capital [Muscat](57053.md), is equipped with a ship repair yard and [dry dock](213739.md) facility. Overlooking the [Arabian Sea](2747.md) and the [Indian Ocean](14580.md), the Port of Duqm is a joint-venture between ASYAD Ports and Consortium Antwerp Port.[^cite_note-ASYAD-2][^cite_note-OmanObserver-1114967-5] Although operating since 2012,[^cite_note-ASYAD-2] it was not officially opened until 4 February 2022 by Mr. [Asa'ad bin Tariq Al Said](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asa'ad_bin_Tariq_Al_Said), [Deputy Prime Minister for International Relations and Cooperation Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Oman) and Personal Representative of [His Majesty the Sultan of Oman](405325.md).[^cite_note-OmanObserver-1114967-5]

The Port of Duqm has a total area of 188 [square kilometres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_kilometre) (73 [square miles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_mile)), and includes land for industry, an oil storage terminal, commercial and government [berths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berth_\(moorings\)), a dry dock, and associated logistical lands. It is protected by a main [breakwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakwater_\(structure\)) and a secondary one; the height of the main breakwater is 11 metres (36 feet) above sea level and more than 22 metres (72 feet) on average to the sea floor, and is 4.1 kilometres (3 miles) in length. The length of the secondary breakwater is 4.6 kilometres (3 miles).[^cite_note-OmanObserver-1114967-5]

Its commercial berth has four stations, including two container terminals with a length of about 1,600 metres (5,249 feet) and an annual capacity to handle about 3.5 million standard containers, a terminal for dry bulk materials with an annual capacity of about 5,000,000 [tonnes](31185.md) (5,511,557 [tons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ton)), and a multi-use terminal with an annual capacity of about 800,000 tonnes (881,849 tons) and a [Ro-Ro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ro-Ro) capacity of 200,000 cars per annum.[^cite_note-OmanObserver-1114967-5]

## International agreements

In August 2017, the [Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Oman) and the [United Kingdom](31717.md) (UK) [governments](25318118.md) jointly established the [UK Joint Logistics Support Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Joint_Logistics_Support_Base) (UKJLSB) at Duqm Port.[^cite_note-ie-7] The port has sufficient space and depth to berth the Royal Navy's latest [Queen Elizabeth-class aircraft carriers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth-class_aircraft_carrier).[^cite_note-Maritime-Executive-6][^cite_note-ToO-108955-8] In 2018, the port was used for [Exercise Saif Sareea 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exercise_Saif_Sareea_3) (Exercise Swift Sword 3), a 10-day long, bilateral tri-service [military exercise](1225714.md).[^cite_note-triple-9] Also in 2018, the [Indian government](553883.md) announced that it would use Duqm Port for maintenance of Indian military vessels.[^cite_note-ie-7]

In March 2019, the United States embassy announced an agreement for USA access to the ports at [Salalah](1518956.md) and Duqm.[^cite_note-10] In September [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_in_Oman), the [UK Defence Secretary](302332.md) announced a plan to triple the size of its [UK Joint Logistics Support Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Joint_Logistics_Support_Base) at the Omani port, with a further £23.8 million investment.[^cite_note-triple-9]

## Dry dock

The [dry dock](213739.md) complex, originally known as Oman Drydock Company, now known as Asyad Drydock, includes ship repair and maintenance facilities.[^cite_note-ODC-11] It was opened a year before the main port in [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_in_Oman).[^cite_note-ASYAD-2][^cite_note-OmanObserver-1113643-3][^cite_note-ToO-109245-4] In 2021, the yard completed the build of its first ship, an Omani-owned logistics support ship.[^cite_note-ToO-109245-4][^cite_note-OmanObserver-1113643-3]
