***Widow's Bay*** is an American [supernatural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supernatural_fiction) [comedy horror](5866625.md) television series created by [Katie Dippold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Dippold) for [Apple TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_\(streaming_service\)), and starring [Matthew Rhys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Rhys), [Kate O'Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_O'Flynn), [Kevin Carroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Carroll_\(actor\)), [Dale Dickey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Dickey), [Kingston Rumi Southwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston_Rumi_Southwick), and [Stephen Root](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Root). The series is set in the fictional [New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England) island town of Widow's Bay, which happens to be afflicted with a centuries-old curse that brings various supernatural evils upon the island. [Hiro Murai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiro_Murai) serves as director and executive producer on the series.

The series premiered on April 29, 2026, to critical acclaim. For its first season, it received a record 14 wins at the [78th Primetime Emmy Awards](81631496.md), including for [Outstanding Comedy Series](4719642.md), acting wins for Rhys, O'Flynn, Root, and [Betty Gilpin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betty_Gilpin), as well as wins for directing and writing. In June 2026, the series was renewed for a second season.

## Cast and characters

### Main

- [Matthew Rhys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Rhys) as Tom Loftis, mayor of Widow's Bay. Originally from the mainland, he is initially skeptical of the local residents' superstitions about the island's supposed curse. He is a widower, and father to a son, Evan, from his marriage to his deceased wife, Lauren.

- [Kate O'Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_O'Flynn) as Patricia Moyer, Tom's eccentric assistant. She is a social outcast among her former classmates, who believe she lied about being targeted by a serial killer known as the Boogeyman while in high school.

- [Kevin Carroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Carroll_\(actor\)) as Bechir Clemmons, sheriff of Widow's Bay

- [Dale Dickey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Dickey) as Rosemary, an employee in the mayor's office

- [Kingston Rumi Southwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston_Rumi_Southwick) as Evan Loftis, Tom's rebellious teenage son, who was born on the island and is bored by a lack of things to do there.

- [Stephen Root](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Root) as Wyck Crawford, an outspoken believer of the town's supernatural history. He initially clashes with Tom over his refusal to believe in the island's curse.

- [Jeff Hiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Hiller) as Dale (season 2; recurring season 1), an employee of the mayor's office

## Production

The series was created by [Katie Dippold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Dippold), who is an executive producer and showrunner. It was ordered by [Apple TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_\(streaming_service\)) in September 2024, with [Hiro Murai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiro_Murai) directing and executive-producing, alongside Claudia Shin and Carver Karaszewski.[^cite_note-2] In February 2025, [Matthew Rhys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Rhys) joined the project as an executive producer; he leads the cast as Mayor Tom Loftis.[^cite_note-3] Murai directed five episodes of the first season; [Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West), Sam Donovan, and [Andrew DeYoung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_DeYoung_\(director\)) served as directors of the remaining episodes.[^cite_note-Lewis-4] Alongside Rhys, the cast includes [Kate O'Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_O'Flynn), [Stephen Root](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Root), [Kingston Rumi Southwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston_Rumi_Southwick), [Kevin Carroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Carroll_\(actor\)), and [Dale Dickey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Dickey).[^cite_note-Lewis-4]



[Berlin Town Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_Town_Hall_\(Berlin,_Massachusetts\)), one of the filming locations

Filming took place in [Massachusetts](1645518.md) in 2025, with locations including [Ayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayer,_Massachusetts) and [Worcester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester,_Massachusetts) in May, and [Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex,_Massachusetts), [Gloucester](116744.md) and [Rockport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockport,_Massachusetts) in June. Interiors were constructed and shot at [New England Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England_Studios) in [Devens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devens,_Massachusetts).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The [Berlin Town Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_Town_Hall_\(Berlin,_Massachusetts\)) stood in for the town hall of Widow's Bay while [Eastern Point Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Point_Light) was used as the town's lighthouse. The [Rebecca Nurse Homestead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Nurse_Homestead) in [Danvers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danvers,_Massachusetts), the [Martha Mary Chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_Mary_Chapel) in [Sudbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudbury,_Massachusetts), and the Lorenzo Maynard House in [Maynard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maynard,_Massachusetts) were also used as filming locations.[^cite_note-9]

After having filmed the first two episodes, Dippold contacted [David Fleming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fleming_\(composer\)) to score *Widow's Bay*.[^cite_note-mpa-10] Neither of the two wanted to use [comedy music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_music), with Fleming suggesting to use elements from the fictional town to create the soundscape. This included the use of [bass flutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_flute) and other woodwinds, as well as [conches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conch_\(instrument\)). For the sixth episode, set in 1702, instruments such as the [Baroque cello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baroque_cello) and [viola da gamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viola_da_gamba) were used.[^cite_note-mpa-10] For the eighth episode, inspiration was drawn from [John Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Carpenter)'s [film score to the 1978 film *Halloween*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halloween_\(1978_soundtrack\)) and its use of the [analog synthesizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analog_synthesizer).[^cite_note-mpa-10] The score was recorded in [Los Angeles](18110.md),[^cite_note-mpa-10] and the soundtrack album was released on May 4, 2026, via [Milan Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan_Records).[^cite_note-11]

On June 11, 2026, Apple TV renewed the series for a second season.[^cite_note-12] In August 2026, [Jeff Hiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Hiller) was promoted to a series regular for the second season.[^cite_note-13]

## Release

The first two episodes were released on Apple TV on April 29, 2026, with further episodes being made available weekly.[^cite_note-14]

## Reception

### Critical response

On the review aggregator website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), the first season holds an approval rating of 98%, based on 108 reviews, with an average rating of 8.5/10. The website's critics consensus states: "Katie Dippold successfully continues to invest in eccentricity with this outlandish horror-comedy that stokes the genre's well-worn tropes to winning effect, bringing scares, laughs, and a game cast."[^cite_note-15] [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic), which uses a weighted average, assigned a score of 78 out of 100, based on 26 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.[^cite_note-16]

[Lucy Mangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Mangan) of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* gave the series 5 out of 5 stars, calling it "an absolute blast \[...] rich and wonderful. Grownup, funny, scary, true—*[Mare of Easttown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mare_of_Easttown)* meets *[Schitt's Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt's_Creek)*, but with something else that makes it singular".[^cite_note-17] *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\))*'s Alison Herman wrote, "*Widow's Bay* may not make the best tourist destination, but I'd happily make a return trip."[^cite_note-18]
