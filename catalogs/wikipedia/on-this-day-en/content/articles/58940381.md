# World War II bomb disposal in Europe



Technicians inspecting a British bomb unearthed in [Koblenz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koblenz), Germany in 2011

The [Royal Air Force](25679.md) and [United States Army Air Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Air_Forces) dropped 2.7 million [tonnes](31185.md) of bombs on Europe during [World War II](32927.md).[^cite_note-higginbotham16-1] In the United Kingdom, the German [Luftwaffe](17885.md) dropped more than 12,000 tonnes of bombs on [London](17867.md) alone.[^cite_note-\:0-2] In 2018, the [British Ministry of Defence](203793.md) reported that 450 World War II bombs were made safe or defused since 2010 by disposal teams.[^cite_note-3] Every year, an estimated 2,000 tons of World War II munitions are found in Germany, at times requiring the evacuation of tens of thousands of residents from their homes.[^cite_note-higginbotham16-1] In [Berlin](3354.md) alone, 1.8 million pieces of ordnance have been defused between 1947 and 2018.[^cite_note-berlin18-4] Buried bombs, as well as mortars, land mines and grenades, are often found during construction work or other excavations, or by farmers tilling the land.[^cite_note-5]
