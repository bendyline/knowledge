**Los Angeles County**, sometimes abbreviated as **LA County**, is a county in the U.S. state of [California](5407.md), in the [southern region](62520.md) of the state. It is the [most populous county in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_States_counties_and_county_equivalents), with 9,694,934 residents estimated in 2025. Its population is greater than that of 40 individual [U.S. states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_U.S._states_and_territories_by_population). Comprising [88 incorporated cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Los_Angeles_County,_California) and [101 unincorporated areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_unincorporated_communities_in_Los_Angeles_County,_California) within a total area of 4,083 square miles (10,570 km<sup>2</sup>), it accommodates more than a quarter of [Californians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_California) and is one of the most ethnically diverse U.S. counties.[^cite_note-7] The [county's seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat), [Los Angeles](18110.md), is the [second-most populous city in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_States_cities_by_population), with 3,878,704 residents estimated in 2024. The county is globally known as the home of the [U.S. motion picture industry](418334.md) since its inception in the early 20th century.

The County of Los Angeles is one of the original [counties of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_counties_in_California), created at the time of statehood in 1850.[^cite_note-coy-8] The county originally included parts of what are now [Kern](80557.md), [San Bernardino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Bernardino_County,_California), [Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverside_County,_California), [Inyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inyo_County,_California), [Tulare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulare_County,_California), [Ventura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventura_County,_California), and [Orange](45330.md) counties. In 1851 and 1852, Los Angeles County stretched from the coast to the state line of [Nevada](21216.md).[^cite_note-9] As the population increased, sections were split off to organize San Bernardino County in 1853, Kern County in 1866, and Orange County in 1889.

Before the 1870s, Los Angeles County was divided into townships (many of which were amalgamations of one or more [old ranchos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranchos_of_California)):[^cite_note-10]

- [Azusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azusa,_California) – encompassed the [foothill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foothills) communities east of the [San Gabriel River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Gabriel_River_\(California\)), including present-day [Covina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covina,_California) and [Duarte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duarte,_California)

- [El Monte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Monte,_California) – encompassed communities in the [Whittier Narrows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whittier_Narrows) area, including present-day El Monte, [La Puente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Puente,_California), as well as [Monterey Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterey_Park,_California)&#x20;

  - Azusa and El Monte Townships – merged for the 1870 census

- [City of Los Angeles](18110.md) – then consisting solely of its four-league Spanish land grant

- [Los Angeles Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Township,_California) – consisted of areas surrounding the City of Los Angeles, including the [San Fernando Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Fernando_Valley) and present-day [West Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Los_Angeles) and [East Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Los_Angeles,_California). Most of this area has now been annexed to the city of Los Angeles.

- [Los Nietos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Nietos,_California) – consisted of areas south of the Whittier Narrows and [Puente Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puente_Hills) south to present-day [Long Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach,_California), centered on the early settlement at Los Nietos. Some of this area is now in Orange County

- [San Jose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose_Township,_Los_Angeles_County,_California) – consisted of the eastern portions of the county drained by [San Jose Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose_Creek), including what is now the cities of [Pomona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomona,_California), [Claremont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California) and [Walnut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut,_California)

- [San Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Gabriel,_California) – consisted of the western [San Gabriel Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Gabriel_Valley) and foothill communities, including present-day [Alhambra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alhambra,_California) and [Pasadena](92408.md). Centered on the [Mission San Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission_San_Gabriel)

- [Santa Ana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Ana,_California) – consisted of what is now northern and central Orange County, including what is now [Fullerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fullerton,_California), [Huntington Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntington_Beach,_California) and the [City of Orange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange,_California). (Centered on Santa Ana).&#x20;

  - For the 1870 census, [Anaheim district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaheim,_California) was enumerated separately

## Geography



The historical boundaries of Los Angeles County since its establishment in 1850 as defined by the [California State Legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Legislature). The solid blue line represents the original boundaries of the county, the dashed blue lines represent the changes made to the boundaries, and the red line represents the final major boundary changes of the county made by the Legislature in 1889. This map does not include minor changes to the boundary after 1922, such as the transfer of a small amount land east of [Interstate 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_5_in_California) to [Kern County](80557.md), among others. Portions or the entirety of modern-day [Inyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inyo_County,_California), Kern, [Orange](45330.md), [Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverside_County,_California), [San Bernardino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Bernardino_County,_California), [San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego_County,_California), and [Ventura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventura_County,_California) counties were formerly in Los Angeles County.

According to the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Census_Bureau), the county has an area of 4,751 square miles (12,310 km<sup>2</sup>), of which 4,058 square miles (10,510 km<sup>2</sup>) (85%) is land and 693 square miles (1,790 km<sup>2</sup>) (15%) is water.[^cite_note-GR1-14] Los Angeles County borders 70 miles (110 km) of coast on the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md) and encompasses mountain ranges, valleys, forests, islands, lakes, rivers, and desert. The [Los Angeles River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_River), [Rio Hondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Hondo_\(California\)), [Ballona Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballona_Creek), the [San Gabriel River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Gabriel_River_\(California\)) and the [Santa Clara River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara_River_\(California\)) flow in Los Angeles County, while the primary mountain ranges are the [Santa Monica Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica_Mountains) and the [San Gabriel Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Gabriel_Mountains). The western extent of the [Mojave Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojave_Desert) begins in the [Antelope Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antelope_Valley), in the northeastern part of the county. Los Angeles County is bordered on the north by [Kern County](80557.md), on the east by [San Bernardino County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Bernardino_County,_California), on the southeast by [Orange County](45330.md), on the south by the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md), and on the west by [Ventura County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventura_County,_California).

Most of the population of Los Angeles County resides in the south and southwest, with major population centers in the [Los Angeles Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Basin), [San Fernando Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Fernando_Valley), and [San Gabriel Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Gabriel_Valley). Other population centers are found in the [Santa Clarita Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clarita_Valley), [Pomona Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomona_Valley), [Crescenta Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crescenta_Valley) and [Antelope Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antelope_Valley).

The county is divided west-to-east by the [San Gabriel Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Gabriel_Mountains), which are part of the [Transverse Ranges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transverse_Ranges) of southern California, and are contained mostly within the [Angeles National Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angeles_National_Forest). Most of the county's highest peaks are in the San Gabriel Mountains, including [Mount San Antonio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_San_Antonio) 10,068 feet (3,069 m) at the Los Angeles–[San Bernardino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Bernardino_County,_California) county lines, [Mount Baden-Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Baden-Powell_\(California\)) 9,399 feet (2,865 m), [Mount Burnham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Burnham) 8,997 feet (2,742 m) and [Mount Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Wilson_\(California\)) 5,710 feet (1,740 m). Several lower mountains are in the northern, western, and southwestern parts of the county, including the [San Emigdio Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Emigdio_Mountains), the southernmost part of [Tehachapi Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehachapi_Mountains) and the [Sierra Pelona Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Pelona_Mountains).

Los Angeles County includes [San Clemente Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Clemente_Island) and [Santa Catalina Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Catalina_Island,_California), which are part of the [Channel Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_Islands_of_California) archipelago off the [Pacific Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Coast).

### Climate

The Northern part of the county has a [Desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_climate), while the rest of the county generally is a mix of [semi-arid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-arid_climate) and a hot-summer [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate). There is rainfall mostly in the wintertime, but the mountains in the north-central part of the county have snow during winter.[^cite_note-15]

### Income



Percent of households with incomes above \$150k across LA County census tracts

In 2024, the Area Median Income (AMI) for a one-person household in Los Angeles County was \$74,600 (\$6,217 per month); for a four-person household, \$106,600 (\$8,883 per month).[^cite_note-42]

As of 2024 12 12, the homeownership rate was 49.81%.[^cite_note-43] As of 2025 12 23, the median home value was \$859,958.[^cite_note-44] Multi-unit structures comprised approximately 54.2% of the total housing inventory as of late 2025.

In October 2025, the number of homeless people in the county was 72,195, of which 47,450 were unsheltered.[^cite_note-45] LA County holds the undisputed title for the largest unsheltered homeless population in the nation—in 2024, only 30% of LA's homeless population was sheltered either in an emergency shelter, transitional housing, or a safe haven program, compared to 97% of New York's unhoused.[^cite_note-46]

### Religion

In 2015, there were over two thousand [Christian churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_church), the majority of which are Catholic.[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48] Roman Catholic adherents number close to 40% of the population. There were 202 Jewish [synagogues](48559.md), 145 [Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist) temples, 38 Muslim [mosques](19894.md), 44 [Baháʼí Faith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bah%C3%A1%CA%BC%C3%AD_Faith) worship centers, 37 [Hindu temples](2247213.md), 28 [Tenrikyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenrikyo) churches and fellowships, 16 [Shinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinto) worship centers, and 14 Sikh [gurdwaras](482849.md) in the county.[^cite_note-49] The [Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Los_Angeles) has approximately 5 million members and is the largest diocese in the United States. In 2014, the county had 3,275 religious organizations, the most out of all US counties.[^cite_note-50]

## Law, government, and politics

### Government

Main article: [Government of Los Angeles County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Los_Angeles_County)



The [Kenneth Hahn Hall of Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Hahn_Hall_of_Administration) is the seat of the [government of Los Angeles County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Los_Angeles_County).

The [Government of Los Angeles County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Los_Angeles_County) is defined and authorized under the [California Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Constitution), [California law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_law) and the Charter of the County of Los Angeles.[^cite_note-51] Much of the [Government of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_California) is in practice the responsibility of local governments such as the Government of Los Angeles County.

The county's voters elect a governing five-member [Los Angeles County Board of Supervisors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Board_of_Supervisors). The small size of the board means each supervisor represents over 2 million people. The board operates in a legislative, executive, and [quasi-judicial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-judicial_body) capacity. As a legislative authority, it can pass ordinances for the unincorporated areas (ordinances that affect the whole county, like posting of restaurant ratings, must be ratified by the individual city). As an executive body, it can tell the county departments what to do, and how to do it. As a quasi-judicial body, the Board is the final venue of appeal in the local planning process, and holds public hearings on various agenda items.

As of 2020, the Board of Supervisors oversees a \$35.5 billion annual budget and over 112,000 employees.[^cite_note-52] The county government is managed on a day-to-day basis by a chief executive officer and is organized into many departments, each of which is enormous in comparison to equivalent county-level (and even many state-level) departments anywhere else in the United States. ### Legal system

Further information: [Los Angeles County Superior Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Superior_Court)

The Los Angeles County Superior Court is the county's court of general jurisdiction, while the [U.S. District Court for the Central District of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._District_Court_for_the_Central_District_of_California) may hear cases where federal jurisdiction is present. Both are headquartered in a large cluster of government buildings in the city's Civic Center.

Historically, the courthouses were county-owned buildings that were maintained at county expense, which created significant friction since the trial court judges, as officials of the state government, had to lobby the county Board of Supervisors for facility renovations and upgrades. In turn, the state judiciary successfully persuaded the state Legislature to authorize the transfer of all courthouses to the state government in 2008 and 2009 (so that judges would have direct control over their own courthouses). Courthouse security is still provided by the county government under a contract with the state.

Unlike the largest city in the United States, [New York City](645042.md), all of the city of Los Angeles and most of its important suburbs are located within a single county. As a result, both the county superior court and the federal district court are respectively the busiest courts of their type in the nation.[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65]

Many celebrities have been seen in Los Angeles courts. In 2003, the television show *Extra* (based in nearby [Glendale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendale,_California)) found itself running so many reports on the legal problems of local celebrities that it spun them off into a separate show, *Celebrity Justice*.

State cases are appealed to the Court of Appeal for the Second Appellate District, which is also headquartered in the [Civic Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civic_Center,_Los_Angeles), and then to the [California Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_California), which is headquartered in [San Francisco](49728.md) but also hears argument in Los Angeles (again, in the Civic Center). Federal cases are appealed to the [Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Court_of_Appeals_for_the_Ninth_Circuit), which hears them at its branch building in [Pasadena](92408.md). The court of last resort for federal cases is the [U.S. Supreme Court](31737.md) in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md)

## Ecology



Many introduced species, such as this [Indian peafowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_peafowl), adapt readily to urban living and Los Angeles County's mild climate.

According to the authors of *Wild L.A.*, a book about urban biodiversity, "Los Angeles is the birdiest county in the country with over 500 recorded species." LA's amenable climate supports a large number of introduced, tropical and migratory species, such as tropical parrots that snack on the local ornamental and edible fruit trees, including [dates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Date_palm), [figs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fig), and [loquats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loquat).[^cite_note-wildla-72][^cite_note-73] Because of the county's wide range of [biomes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biome) it is possible to see [desert bighorn sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_bighorn_sheep) and [green sea turtles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_sea_turtles) in the same day, without crossing the county line.[^cite_note-wildla-72] The range of habitats in the county is "greater than in many states, with mountains, wetlands, desert, ocean, meadows and chaparral, each with its own endemic species."[^cite_note-74] There are at least 100 species of trees, and 1000 species of non-native plants, in the urban areas of the county.[^cite_note-75] Charismatic biodiversity indicator species native to the area include three species of amphibian ([Baja California chorus frog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baja_California_chorus_frog), [black-bellied slender salamander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black-bellied_slender_salamander), [western toad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_toad)), 14 species of bird ([acorn woodpecker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acorn_woodpecker), [California quail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_quail), [canyon wren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canyon_wren), [cinnamon teal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinnamon_teal), [great blue heron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_blue_heron), [great horned owl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_horned_owl), [greater roadrunner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_roadrunner), [hooded merganser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hooded_merganser), [Northern harrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_harrier), [red-tailed hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-tailed_hawk), [red-winged blackbird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-winged_blackbird), [spotted towhee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotted_towhee), [western bluebird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_bluebird), [western meadowlark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_meadowlark)), nine kinds of invertebrates ([Behr's metalmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behr's_metalmark), [bramble green hairstreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bramble_green_hairstreak), [bumblebees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bumblebee), [El Segundo blue butterfly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Segundo_blue_butterfly), [harvester ants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvester_ant), [Lorquin's admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorquin's_admiral), [North American Jerusalem crickets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_Jerusalem_crickets), [Sara orangetip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_orangetip), [velvet ants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velvet_ants)), five mammals ([bobcat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobcat), [dusky footed woodrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dusky_footed_woodrat), [gray fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gray_fox), [mountain lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cougar), [mule deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mule_deer)), and six reptiles ([California kingsnake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_kingsnake), [coachwhip snake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachwhip_snake), [gopher snake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pituophis_catenifer), [side-blotched lizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Side-blotched_lizard), [western pond turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_pond_turtle), [western rattlesnake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crotalus_oreganus)).[^cite_note-76] Any observations of these species within the county are considered ecologically significant indicators of ecosystem health and may be documented using the [iNaturalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INaturalist) app.[^cite_note-77][^cite_note-78]

## Education

The Los Angeles County Office of Education provides a supporting role for school districts in the area. The county office also operates two magnet schools, the [International Polytechnic High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Polytechnic_High_School) and [Los Angeles County High School for the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_High_School_for_the_Arts). There are a number of private schools in the county, most notably those operated by the [Roman Catholic Archdiocese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Archdiocese). The county's public education sector is run by numerous school districts with the [Los Angeles Unified School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Unified_School_District) being the largest one running public schools primarily within the city of Los Angeles and its immediately neighboring cities.

## Sites of interest



[LA County Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Fair) at dusk, 2008



Photo of the [Los Angeles County Museum of Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Museum_of_Art) during its 2005 Ancient Egypt exhibit

The county's most visited park is [Griffith Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griffith_Park), owned by the city of Los Angeles. The county is also known for the annual [Rose Parade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose_Parade) in Pasadena, the annual [Los Angeles County Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Fair) in Pomona, the [Los Angeles County Museum of Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Museum_of_Art), the [Los Angeles Zoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Zoo), the [Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_History_Museum_of_Los_Angeles_County), the [La Brea Tar Pits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Brea_Tar_Pits), the [Arboretum of Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arboretum_of_Los_Angeles), and two horse racetracks and two car racetracks ([Pomona Raceway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairplex) and [Irwindale Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irwindale_Speedway)), also the [RMS *Queen Mary*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMS_Queen_Mary) located in Long Beach, and the [Long Beach Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach_Grand_Prix), and miles of beaches—from Zuma to Cabrillo.

[Venice Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice,_Los_Angeles) is a popular attraction whose [Muscle Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_Beach) used to attract throngs of tourists admiring "hardbodies". Today, it is more arts-centered. Santa Monica's pier is a well known tourist spot, famous for its [Ferris wheel](175541.md) and bumper car rides, which were featured in the introductory segment of the television sitcom *[Three's Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three's_Company)*. Further north in [Pacific Palisades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Palisades,_Los_Angeles) one finds the beaches used in the television series *[Baywatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baywatch)*.[^cite_note-86] The fabled [Malibu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu,_California), home of many film and television stars, lies west of it.

In the mountain, canyon, and desert areas one may find [Vasquez Rocks Natural Area Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasquez_Rocks_Natural_Area_Park), where many old Westerns were filmed. [Mount Wilson Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Wilson_Observatory) in the [San Gabriel Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Gabriel_Mountains) is open for the public to view astronomical stars from its telescope, now computer-assisted. Many county residents find relaxation in water skiing and swimming at [Castaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castaic,_California) Lake Recreation Area – the county's largest park by area – as well as enjoying natural surroundings and starry nights at [Saddleback Butte State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddleback_Butte_State_Park) in the eastern [Antelope Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antelope_Valley) – California State Parks' largest in area within the county. The [California Poppy Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antelope_Valley_California_Poppy_Reserve) is located in the western Antelope Valley and shows off the State's flower in great quantity on its rolling hills every spring.

### Air



[Los Angeles International Airport](18131.md)

[Los Angeles International Airport](18131.md) (LAX), located in the [Westchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westchester,_Los_Angeles) district, is the primary [commercial airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commercial_airport) for commercial airlines in the county and the Greater Los Angeles Area. LAX is operated by [Los Angeles World Airports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_World_Airports) (LAWA), an agency of the City of Los Angeles.

Other important commercial airports in Los Angeles County include:

- [Long Beach Municipal Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach_Municipal_Airport) operated by the City of Long Beach.

- [Hollywood Burbank Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Burbank_Airport) in Burbank, operated by the Burbank-Glendale-Pasadena Airport Authority.

The following [general aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_aviation) airports also are located in Los Angeles County:

- County-operated airports ([Department of Public Works, Aviation Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Department_of_Public_Works#Major_Divisions))&#x20;

  - [Compton/Woodley Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compton/Woodley_Airport) in [Compton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compton,_California).

  - [San Gabriel Valley Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Gabriel_Valley_Airport) in [El Monte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Monte,_California).

  - [Brackett Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brackett_Field) in [La Verne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Verne,_California).

  - [Whiteman Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whiteman_Airport) in [Pacoima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacoima,_Los_Angeles).

  - [General William J. Fox Airfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_William_J._Fox_Airfield) in [Lancaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster,_California).

- City-operated airports&#x20;

  - [Van Nuys Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Nuys_Airport) in [Van Nuys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Nuys), also operated by [LAWA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LAWA). Van Nuys Airport sees significant [executive jet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_jet) air traffic.

  - [LA/Palmdale Regional Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA/Palmdale_Regional_Airport) in [Palmdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmdale,_California). The airport is a separate facility on the grounds of [Air Force Plant 42](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Plant_42).

  - [Santa Monica Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica_Airport) in [Santa Monica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica,_California), which has major executive jet traffic.

  - [Hawthorne Municipal Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawthorne_Municipal_Airport_\(California\)), also known as Jack Northrop Field, in [Hawthorne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawthorne,_California).

  - [Zamperini Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamperini_Field) in [Torrance](107690.md).

The [U.S. Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Air_Force) operates three airports in Los Angeles County:

- Portions of [Edwards Air Force Base](107530.md), located at the northern edge of the county.

- [Air Force Plant 42](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Plant_42) in Palmdale, sharing runways with LA/Palmdale Regional.

- The non-flying [Los Angeles Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Air_Force_Base) in El Segundo.

### Rail

Los Angeles is a major freight-railroad transportation center, in great part due to the large volumes of freight moving in and out of the county's seaport facilities. The ports are connected to the downtown rail yards and to the main lines of [Union Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Pacific) and [Burlington Northern Santa Fe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burlington_Northern_Santa_Fe) headed east via a grade-separated freight rail corridor known as the [Alameda Corridor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alameda_Corridor).

Passenger rail service is provided in the county by [Amtrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amtrak), [Los Angeles Metro Rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Metro_Rail) and [Metrolink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrolink_\(California\)).

Amtrak has the following intercity Amtrak service at [Union Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Union_Station) in the city of Los Angeles:

- The *[Pacific Surfliner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Surfliner)* to [Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Barbara,_California), [San Luis Obispo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Luis_Obispo), and [San Diego](28504.md).

- The *[Coast Starlight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coast_Starlight)* to [San Francisco Bay Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Bay_Area), [Portland](23503.md) and [Seattle](11388236.md).

- The *[Southwest Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Chief)* to [Albuquerque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albuquerque,_New_Mexico), [Kansas City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City,_Missouri) and [Chicago](6886.md).

- The *[Sunset Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunset_Limited)* to [Tucson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucson,_Arizona), [Houston](13774.md) and [New Orleans](53842.md).

Union Station is also the primary hub for Metrolink commuter rail, which serves much of the [Greater Los Angeles Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Los_Angeles_Area).

Light rail, subway (heavy rail), and long-distance bus service are all provided by the [Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Metropolitan_Transportation_Authority) (Metro). Other smaller regional transit agencies that provide public transit to specific regions of Los Angeles County include [LADOT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LADOT), [Long Beach Transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach_Transit), [Montebello Bus Lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montebello_Bus_Lines), [Norwalk Transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwalk_Transit_\(California\)), [Santa Monica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica)'s [Big Blue Bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Blue_Bus) serving the [western LA region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westside_\(Los_Angeles_County\)), [Santa Clarita Transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clarita_Transit), [Torrance Transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torrance_Transit), [Glendale Beeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendale_Beeline), [Foothill Transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foothill_Transit) serving the [San Gabriel Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Gabriel_Valley) region, and the [Antelope Valley Transit Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antelope_Valley_Transit_Authority) serving the [Lancaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster,_California) and [Palmdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmdale,_California) area in the [Antelope Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antelope_Valley) region.

### Sea

The county's two main seaports are the [Port of Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Los_Angeles) and the [Port of Long Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Long_Beach). Together they handle over a quarter of all [container](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Containerization) traffic entering the United States, making the complex the largest and most important port in the country, and the third-largest port in the world by shipping volume.

The Port of Los Angeles is the largest cruise ship center on the [West Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Coast_of_the_United_States), handling more than 1 million passengers annually.

The Port of Long Beach is home to the [Sea Launch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_Launch) program, which uses a floating launch platform to insert payloads into orbits that would be difficult to attain from existing land-based launch sites.

[Catalina Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalina_Express) ferries link the Catalina Island city of [Avalon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalon,_California) to the mainland at [San Pedro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro,_Los_Angeles) and [Long Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach,_California), as well as [Dana Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dana_Point,_California) in [Orange County](45330.md).
