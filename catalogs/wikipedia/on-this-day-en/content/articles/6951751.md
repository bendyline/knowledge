# Terrorism in Kuwait

Kuwait has experienced various terror attacks, including those carried out by [ISIS](9087364.md), [Al-Qaeda](1921.md), and other acts of [Islamic terrorism](566821.md). Various terror attacks in Kuwait were associated with the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War), [Gulf War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War), and the subsequent American military support in Kuwait.

Kuwait currently has the largest US military presence in the entire Middle East region.[^cite_note-largest_US_miltiary_presence-1] There are over 14,000 US military personnel stationed in the country.[^cite_note-largest_US_miltiary_presence-1]

## 1970s

- [1974 attack on the Japanese Embassy in Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_attack_on_the_Japanese_Embassy_in_Kuwait)

- June 17, 1976: The [*Al-Anba*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Anba_\(Kuwait\)) newspaper building was bombed. editorial offices were destroyed. Five people, including the editor-in-chief, were wounded.

  [^cite_note-2]

## 1980s

- December 1983 – [1983 Kuwait Bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983%E2%80%931988_Kuwait_terror_attacks#1983_Kuwait_bombings) were attacks on six key installations in Kuwait.

- April 1985 – [Ahmed Al-Jarallah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Al-Jarallah) was subjected to an assassination attempt when a gunman opened fire on him outside his offices. He was shot six times.

  [^cite_note-seale-3]

  [^cite_note-\:0-4]

  [^cite_note-\:1-5]

  [^cite_note-\:2-6]

- May 1985 – A failed assassination attempt was made on Sheikh [Jaber Al-Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaber_Al-Ahmad_Al-Jaber_Al-Sabah), who was the emir of Kuwait at the time.

- July 1985 – [1985 Kuwait City bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Kuwait_City_bombings) in which two coffeehouses were bombed.

- January 1987 – Bomb exploded in a shopping district on the eve of the Organization of Islamic Conference meeting.

  [^cite_note-bomb-7]

- July 1987 – Car bombs exploded in a fashionable shopping district, killing two people and blowing the facades off several stores.

  [^cite_note-bomb-7]

- [1984–1988 Aircraft hijackings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983%E2%80%931988_Kuwait_terror_attacks#Aircraft_hijackings_\(1984%E2%80%931988\))

## 1990s

Several terror attacks associated with the [Gulf War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War) and an assassination attempt on actor [Abdulhussain Abdulredha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulhussain_Abdulredha) for an anti-Iraq theatrical play.[^cite_note-Jeff_Dunham-8]



Smoke from burning Kuwait oil fields after Saddam Hussein set fire to during Gulf war.

## 2000s

- October 8, 2002 – [Failaka Island attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faylaka_Island_attack), in which two [Kuwaiti](7515890.md) citizens with ties to [jihadists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mujahideen) in [Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Emirate_of_Afghanistan) attacked a group of unarmed [United States Marines](17349325.md) conducting a training exercise on [Failaka island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Failaka_Island), killing one before being killed themselves.

  [^cite_note-9]

- October 10, 2002 – Two gunmen with ties to [Al-Qaeda](1921.md) open fire on an American [Humvee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humvee).

  [^cite_note-NYTimes2002-10-09-10]

- November 21, 2002 – Two American contractors with Tapestry Corporation were wounded while driving a civilian vehicle when a Kuwaiti police sergeant opened fire at them at close range.

- January 31, 2005 – Kuwait Police infiltrate a terrorist cell belonging to the [Peninsula Lions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peninsula_Lions) in a [Salmiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salmiya) apartment complex. Five terrorist members and a civilian bystander were killed.

- August 11, 2009 – Kuwaiti authorities reported that they had arrested six individuals accused of planning attacks on U.S. troops stationed at [Camp Arifjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Arifjan). The six men allegedly belong to a terrorist group with ties to [Al-Qaeda](1921.md).

- December 11, 2003 – [Ahmed Al-Jarallah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Al-Jarallah)'s secretary at Al-Seyassah was injured when he opened a large envelope addressed to Jarallah, sent from [Beirut](37428.md), [Lebanon](17771.md). The envelope bomb contained the plastic explosive [Semtex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semtex).

  [^cite_note-unhcr-11]

  [^cite_note-12]

  [^cite_note-13]

## 2010s

- June 26, 2015 – [2015 Kuwait mosque bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Kuwait_mosque_bombing) – A bombing occurred during Friday prayer at masjid Imam al-Sadeq when a man was seen on surveillance cameras rushing into the masjid and then detonating himself. 27 people killed with 227 injured. ISIS has claimed responsibility for the bombing.

  [^cite_note-14]

  &#x20;It was the largest terror attack in Kuwait's history.

- August 13, 2015 – Kuwaiti security forces arrested Al-Abdali terror cell belonging to [Iran](14653.md) and [Hezbollah](13919.md).

  [^cite_note-15]

  [^cite_note-16]

- 2017 – No terrorist incidents were reported in Kuwait in 2017, despite various attempts by [ISIS](9087364.md).

  [^cite_note-17]

## 2020s

- January 25, 2024 – Kuwaiti security forces arrested 3 Tunisians over [ISIS](9087364.md) terrorist plot to bomb [Shiite](26961.md) places of worship.

  [^cite_note-18]
