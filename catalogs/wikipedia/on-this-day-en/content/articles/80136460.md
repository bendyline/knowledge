The [Para ice hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sledge_hockey) competition of the [2026 Winter Paralympics](41045653.md) was held at the [PalaItalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PalaItalia) in Milan, Italy from 7 to 15 March 2026.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] A total of eight teams competed in the mixed team tournament.[^cite_note-qualification_criteria_paralympic_org-3]

[Akari Fukunishi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akari_Fukunishi) from Japan has become the fourth female Para ice hockey player at the Paralympic Games.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
