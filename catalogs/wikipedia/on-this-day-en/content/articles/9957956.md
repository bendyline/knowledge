The **Minister for Foreign Affairs** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): وزیرِ خارجہ, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Urdu): *Wazīr-e Xārjā*), simply the **Foreign Minister**, is the head of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](13048437.md) of the [Government of Pakistan](1897833.md). The minister oversees the federal government's foreign policy and [International relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_relations). Their responsibility includes representing Pakistan and its government in the international community. This position is one of the senior-most offices in the [Cabinet of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Pakistan). The office of the foreign minister was initially held by [Liaquat Ali Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liaquat_Ali_Khan), who also served as the country's first prime minister. Subsequently, several other prime ministers have held the additional charge of the office of the foreign minister.
