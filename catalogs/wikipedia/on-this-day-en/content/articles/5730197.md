The **French Tennis Federation** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Fédération française de tennis*, **FFT**) is the governing body for [tennis](29773.md) in [France](5843419.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] It was founded in 1920, and is tasked with the organisation, co-ordination and promotion of the sport. It is recognised by the [International Tennis Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Tennis_Federation) and by the French Minister for Sports. Its headquarters are at the [Roland Garros stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stade_de_Roland_Garros). It was founded under the name *Fédération Française de Lawn Tennis* until it changed to the *Fédération Française de Tennis* in 1976. The roles of the FFT include organising tennis competitions in France, most notably the [French Open](147724.md), supporting and co-ordinating tennis clubs, and managing the French tennis teams, including their [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup) and [Billie Jean King Cup](1108893.md) teams.&#x20;
