**Jibia** (or **Jibiya**) is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_in_Nigeria) (LGA) in [Katsina State](2227638.md), northern [Nigeria](21383.md).[^cite_note-nc-1] The population of the LGA was approximately 125,000 as of 2003, and the area is 1037 km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-nc-1]

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 822.[^cite_note-2]

Jibia sits along on the Nigerian border with [Niger](21373.md), and the border post was burnt down by a mob in 2005.[^cite_note-tn-3] According to the [Daily Triumph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Triumph) newspaper, the mob was "hired" by [smugglers](28645.md) angry about the Katsina Custom Command's crackdown on [contraband](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contraband) goods.[^cite_note-tn-3] The Local Government shares borders with [Batsari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batsari), Kaita, Katsina, Batagarawa and [Zurmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zurmi) (Zamfara State) Local Government Areas.

## History

Na'eem Sanusi Isah Tsambe, a researcher on the history of Jibia, wrote that the origins of Jibia date back to the wars of conquest between the rulers of [katsina Emirate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_Emirate), with Maradi and Katsina migrations over territoriality and religion, especially after the jihad of Usman Bin Fodiyo.

## Climate

Jibia experiences oppressive wet and dry seasons, with a temperature range of 14 °C (58 °F) to 39 °C (102 °F) year-round. The wet season is cloudy, while the dry season is windy and partly cloudy.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
