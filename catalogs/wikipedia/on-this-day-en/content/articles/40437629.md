# List of extreme temperatures in Spain

The following are lists of the highest and lowest temperatures recorded in Spain.

## Highest temperatures ever recorded

On July 30, 1876 and August 4, 1881, temperatures of 51.0 °C (123.8 °F) and 50.0 °C (122.0 °F)[^cite_note-1] were both reported for [Seville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seville): these readings are unreliable, since they were measured under a standard exposure and in poor technical conditions.[^cite_note-Abc.es-2] A temperature of 48.8 °C (119.8 °F) was also recorded at [Cazalla de la Sierra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cazalla_de_la_Sierra) on August 30, 1926, but is generally not considered valid by international standards,[^cite_note-Tiempo.com-3] along with other unofficial readings measured in various locations, probably taken without proper instruments.[^cite_note-4] On July 17, 1978, an unconfirmed temperature of 47.5 °C (117.5 °F) was recorded at [Barranco de Masca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masca_Gorge), [Tenerife](269223.md).[^cite_note-AEMET1-5]

## Lowest temperatures ever recorded
