**Laconia** or **Lakonia** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Λακωνία, *Lakonía*, [\[lakoˈni.a\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Greek)) is a historical and administrative region of [Greece](12108.md) located on the southeastern part of the [Peloponnese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peloponnese) peninsula. Since 2011, it has been administered as a [regional unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_units_of_Greece), with [Sparta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparti_\(municipality\)) as its capital. The word *[laconic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laconic)*—meaning, "spoken in a blunt, concise way"—is probably derived from the name of this region,[^cite_note-2] a reference to the ancient [Spartans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparta) who were renowned for their verbal austerity and blunt, often pithy remarks.

## Geography



[Eurotas river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurotas_\(river\)) outside the city of Sparti

Laconia is bordered by [Messenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messenia) to the west and [Arcadia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcadia_\(regional_unit\)) to the north and is surrounded by the [Myrtoan Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myrtoan_Sea) to the east and by the [Laconian Gulf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laconian_Gulf) and the [Mediterranean Sea](19006.md) to the south. It encompasses [Cape Malea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Malea) and [Cape Tainaron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Tainaron) and a large part of the [Mani Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mani_Peninsula). The Mani Peninsula is in the west region of Laconia.[^cite_note-3] The islands of [Kythira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kythira) and [Antikythera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antikythera) lie to the south, but they administratively belong to the [Attica](26280929.md) regional unit of [islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islands_\(regional_unit\)). The island [Elafonisos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elafonisos), situated between the Laconian mainland and Kythira, is part of Laconia.

The [Evrotas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evrotas) is the longest river in the prefecture. The Evrotas Valley is predominantly an agricultural region that contains many citrus groves, olive groves, and pasture lands. It is the location of the largest orange production in the Peloponnese and probably in all of Greece. *Lakonia*, a brand of orange juice, is based in [Amykles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amykles).

The main mountain ranges are the [Taygetus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taygetus) 2,407 m (7,897 ft) in the west and the [Parnon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnon) 1,961 m (6,434 ft) in the northeast. Taygetus, known as Pentadaktylos (*five-fingers*) throughout the Middle Ages, is west of Sparta and the [Evrotas Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evrotas_Valley). It is the highest mountain in Laconia and the Peloponnese and is mostly covered with pine trees. Two roads join the Messenia and Laconia prefectures: one is a tortuous mountain pass through Taygetus and the other bypasses the mountain via the Mani district to the south.

The [stalactite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stalactite) cave, Dirou, a major tourist attraction, is located south of [Areopolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Areopoli) in the southwest of Laconia.

### Climate

The city of Sparta enjoys a sunny and warm [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Csa*). January highs are around 14 °C (57 °F) while July and August highs are around 36 °C (97 °F) in the city proper. Sparta records the highest summer average maximum temperatures in Greece.[^cite_note-Meteoclub.gr-4] In July 2012 the city registered an average maximum temperature of 38.3 °C (100.9 °F), making it Greece's second highest monthly average maximum temperature to date after the 38.6 °C (101.5 °F) recorded in [Stylida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stylida) in July 2023.[^cite_note-NOA-5][^cite_note-6] The highest temperature ever recorded in Sparta is 45.7 °C (114.3 °F) in August 2021.[^cite_note-7] On average, Sparta records 5 days per year with temperatures of over 40.0 °C (104.0 °F).[^cite_note-8]

## History

### Ancient

Main article: [Sparta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparta)



The theater of ancient [Sparta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparta) with modern Sparti and Taygetus in the background

Evidence of [Neolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic) settlement in southern Laconia has been found during excavations of the [Alepotrypa cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alepotrypa_cave) site.[^cite_note-13] Significant archaeological recovery exists at the [Vaphio-tomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaphio) site in Laconia. Found there is advanced [Bronze Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Age) art as well as evidence of cultural associations with the contemporaneous [Minoan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minoan) culture on [Crete](6591.md).[^cite_note-14] At the end of the Mycenean period, the population of Laconia sharply declined.[^cite_note-15] In [classical Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_Greece), Laconia was Spartan territory but from the 4th century BC onward Sparta lost control of various ports, towns and areas.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] From the mid-2nd century BC until 395 AD, Laconia was a part of the [Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire).

### Medieval



Palace of [Mystras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystras)

In the medieval period, Laconia formed part of the [Byzantine Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Empire). In the 7th century, [Slavic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_Slavs) tribes settled in the Peloponnese. Two of them, the [Melingoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melingoi) and the [Ezeritai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezeritai), who settled in parts of Laconia, survived the subsequent Byzantine reconquest and re-Hellenization of the Peloponnese, and are attested until the late Middle Ages.

Following the [Fourth Crusade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Crusade), Laconia was gradually conquered by the [Frankish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankokratia) [Principality of Achaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Achaea). In the 1260s, the Byzantines recovered [Mystras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystras) and other fortresses in the region and managed to evict the Franks from Laconia, which became the nucleus of a new Byzantine province. By the mid-14th century, this evolved into the [Despotate of the Morea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Despotate_of_the_Morea), held by the last [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Greeks) ruling dynasty, the [Palaiologoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palaiologoi). The capital of the Despotate, Mystras, was a major site of the [Palaiologan Renaissance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palaiologan_Renaissance), the last flowering of [Byzantine culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_culture). With the fall of the Despotate to the [Ottomans](22278.md) in 1460, Laconia was conquered as well.

### Modern

With the exception of a [30-year interval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_the_Morea) of [Venetian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Venice) rule, Laconia remained under Ottoman control until the outbreak of the [Greek War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_War_of_Independence) of 1821. Following independence, Sparta was selected as the capital of the modern prefecture, and its economy and agriculture expanded. With the incorporation of the British-ruled [Ionian Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_the_Ionian_Islands) into Greece in 1864, [Elafonissos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elafonissos) became part of the prefecture. After [World War II](32927.md) and the [Greek Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_Civil_War), its population began to somewhat decline, as people moved from the villages toward the larger cities of Greece and abroad.

In 1992, a devastating fire ruined the finest [olive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive) crops in the northern part of the prefecture, and affected the area of [Sellasia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sellasia) along with [Oinountas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oinountas) and its surrounding areas. Firefighters, helicopters and planes battled for days to put out the horrific fire.

In early 2006, flooding ruined olive and citrus crops as well as properties and villages along the Eurotas river. In the summer 2006, a fire devastated a part of the Mani Peninsula, ruining forests, crops, and numerous villages.

## Transport

The [A71 motorway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A71_motorway_\(Greece\)) is the only motorway in Laconia: the southern end is connected with a bypass around [Sparta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparta_\(modern_city\)), which is part of [European route E961](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_route_E961). The national roads in the regional unit are the [EO39](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_39), [EO82](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_82), [EO84](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_84) and [EO86](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_86): there are also 37 numbered provincial roads.[^cite_note-ep-list-1956-20]
