**Edgard Leblanc Fils** (born 18 August 1955)[^cite_note-3] is a Haitian civil engineer and politician who served as the 1st [chairman of the Transitional Presidential Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Transitional_Presidential_Council) from April to October 2024.[^cite_note-4] A member of the [Struggling People's Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Struggling_People's_Organization), he previously served as [president of the Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Haitian_Senate) from 1995 to 2000.[^cite_note-5] Leblanc has led the party since 2004.[^cite_note-6]

## Provisional government

Following President [Michel Martelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Martelly)'s resignation on 10 February 2016, he finished second to [Jocelerme Privert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jocelerme_Privert) in the second round of the indirect [provisional presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_2016_Haitian_presidential_election) in February 2016 (in which only the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Haiti\)) voted).[^cite_note-7]

Edgard Leblanc was one of six people named as a potential prime minister,[^cite_note-8] but [Fritz Jean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_Jean) ended up getting the nod.[^cite_note-9]

During a 2024 speech to the [United Nations General Assembly](31957.md), Leblanc called for reparations to Haiti and denounced the [Springfield pet-eating hoax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield_pet-eating_hoax). A clip of the speech went viral online, showing him drinking water directly from a pitcher, and the presidency was forced to apologize.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]
