**Carlos Alberto Francisco Díaz Dañino** is a retired Peruvian military officer with the rank of [brigadier general](206220.md). He served as [Minister of Defense of Peru](5629239.md) from 17 March to 22 April 2026, during the government of [José María Balcázar](82451696.md).

## Biography

He belongs to the 1988 "Héroes de San Juan y Miraflores" class of the [Chorrillos Military School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chorrillos_Military_School). He holds a degree in military science and a doctorate in Management and Development, as well as master's degrees in geopolitics, National Defense, and Administration.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2][^cite_note-\:2-3]

On 22 April 2026, Díaz resigned as defense minister along with foreign minister [Hugo de Zela](81354711.md) in protest against president [José María Balcázar](82451696.md)'s decision to defer a purchase agreement to buy 24 [F-16](11642.md) fighter jets from the United States pending the result of the [2026 Peruvian general election](69679018.md).[^cite_note-4]
