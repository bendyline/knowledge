On 2 April 2026, a [M<sub>w</sub>](6207163.md#Mw) 7.4 [earthquake](10106.md) struck the [Molucca Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molucca_Sea), between [North Sulawesi](453688.md) and [North Maluku](453682.md) Provinces, [Indonesia](14579.md). The offshore epicenter was located near the [Batang Dua Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batang_Dua_Islands), at a depth of 35 km (22 mi). One person was killed, four were injured and over 450 structures were damaged or destroyed, the majority of them in the Batang Dua Islands.

## Tectonic setting

The unique and complex [tectonic setting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tectonic_setting) of the [Molucca Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molucca_Sea) region is the only global example of an active arc-arc collision consuming an oceanic basin via [subduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subduction) in two directions. The [Molucca Sea plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molucca_Sea_plate) is subsumed by tectonic microplates, the [Halmahera plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halmahera_plate) and the [Sangihe plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangihe_plate). The whole complexity is now known as the [Molucca Sea Collision Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molucca_Sea_Collision_Zone).[^cite_note-Hall-2]

According to the [United States Geological Survey](23814944.md) (USGS), this tectonic region has been the source of 115 earthquakes exceeding M<sub>w</sub> 6.0 since 1908, including 18 >M<sub>w</sub> 7.0 events. The largest events to strike this zone were two M<sub>w</sub> 7.7 events in 1932 and 1968, respectively.[^cite_note-3]

## Earthquake

According to Indonesia's [Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteorology,_Climatology,_and_Geophysical_Agency) (BMKG), the earthquake's [epicenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicenter) was located some 127 kilometres (79 mi) from the city of [Bitung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitung) in the province of [North Sulawesi](453688.md), and close to the [Batang Dua Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batang_Dua_Islands) which is administratively part of the city of [Ternate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ternate) in [North Maluku](453682.md) province.[^cite_note-kompas-4][^cite_note-darilaut-5] The BMKG reported a magnitude of M<sub>w</sub> 7.3,[^cite_note-kompas-4] while the [United States Geological Survey](23814944.md) (USGS) put the magnitude at M<sub>ww</sub> 7.4. It struck at a depth of 35.0 kilometres (21.7 mi) according to the USGS. The [focal mechanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focal_mechanism) of the earthquake corresponded to [reverse faulting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_fault), moderately dipping towards either the northwest or the southeast, in line with the general trend of earthquakes in the region.[^cite_note-usgs-6] A rupture area of 100 km (62 mi) x 55 km (34 mi) was estimated, rupturing directly through the [Batang Dua Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batang_Dua_Islands), with a maximum slip of 1.0241 m (3.360 ft) west of the [hypocenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypocenter). The observed source time function gives a 30 second duration for the earthquake, with the greatest phase of seismic moment release occurring about 15 seconds after initiation.[^cite_note-7]

The [Modified Mercalli intensity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modified_Mercalli_intensity) (MMI) was estimated (according to USGS information) at VIII (*Severe)* in the Batang Dua Islands, VII (*Very Strong*) in [Bitung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitung), VI (*Strong*) at [Ternate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ternate), V (*Moderate*) at [Ibu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Halmahera_Regency) and [Manado](844858.md), and III (*Weak*) at [Gorontalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorontalo_\(city\)), [Bone Bolango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bone_Bolango_Regency), [North Gorontalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Gorontalo_Regency), [Boalemo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boalemo_Regency) and [Pohuwato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pohuwato).[^cite_note-darilaut-5] The earthquake was followed by at least 1,378 aftershocks (25 of them being felt),[^cite_note-tempo-8] with the largest having a magnitude of M<sub>wb</sub> 6.2.[^cite_note-9] A 75 cm (2.46 ft) tsunami was observed in [North Minahasa Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Minahasa_Regency),[^cite_note-10] 65 cm (2.13 ft) in [Kema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kema_District), 15 cm (0.49 ft) in [Beo](31153243.md), 13 cm (0.43 ft) in [Talengen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangihe_Islands_Regency) and 5 cm (0.16 ft) at [Davao City](489401.md), Philippines.[^cite_note-11]

## Impact



House damage in [Batang Dua Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batang_Dua_Islands)

One person died after a sports centre collapsed and two others were injured in [Manado](844858.md).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] A [Siloam Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siloam_Hospitals) was seriously damaged.[^cite_note-14] Five government buildings and a hotel were also damaged in the city.[^cite_note-detik_sumsel-15] In the [Batang Dua Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batang_Dua_Islands), near the rupture area, one person was injured,[^cite_note-16] 1,966 others were displaced,[^cite_note-ambonantara-17] 104 homes collapsed,[^cite_note-porostimur-18] 85 were moderately damaged and 79 were slightly damaged. In addition, six churches, a school and two public facilities were destroyed, with eight more churches damaged.[^cite_note-ambonantara-17] At least 23 homes on [Ternate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ternate) Island,[^cite_note-porostimur-18] and 41 structures in [Bitung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitung) were also affected.[^cite_note-19] The kitchen of a house was destroyed by a landslide and the walls of a mosque and several government offices collapsed in the city.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] Across [Halmahera](555708.md), two homes collapsed, 12 others, a bridge and a school were damaged.[^cite_note-metro-22] At least 27 homes collapsed,[^cite_note-23] 25 others, five places of worship and a public facility were damaged on [Tidore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidore).[^cite_note-metro-22] In [Minahasa Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minahasa_Regency), one person was injured,[^cite_note-metro-22] 17 homes, three buildings and a road were damaged.[^cite_note-detik_sumsel-15] Three churches were also damaged in the regency and in [Tomohon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomohon).[^cite_note-24] Several roads in [Gorontalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorontalo) were damaged or struck by landslides.[^cite_note-25]
