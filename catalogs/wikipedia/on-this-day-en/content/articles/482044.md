The **Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug**,[^cite_note-8] also known as **Yamalia**[^cite_note-9] ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Ямалия) is a [federal subject](701438.md) of [Russia](25391.md) and an [autonomous okrug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_okrugs_of_Russia) of [Tyumen Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyumen_Oblast). Its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) is the [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) of [Salekhard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salekhard), and its largest city is [Novy Urengoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novy_Urengoy). The [2021 Russian Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Census_\(2021\)) recorded its population as 510,490.[^cite_note-2021Census-10]

The autonomous okrug borders [Krasnoyarsk Krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krasnoyarsk_Krai) to the east, the [Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Okrug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khanty-Mansi_Autonomous_Okrug) to the south, and the [Nenets Autonomous Okrug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nenets_Autonomous_Okrug) and [Komi Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Komi_Republic) to the west.

## Geography

The [West Siberian petroleum basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Siberian_petroleum_basin) is the largest [hydrocarbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrocarbon) ([petroleum](23195.md) and [natural gas](22131.md)) basin in the world covering an area of about 2.2 million km<sup>2</sup>, and is also the largest oil and gas producing region in Russia.[^cite_note-11]

The [Nenets people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nenets_people) are an indigenous tribe who have long survived in this region. Their [prehistoric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prehistoric) life involved subsistence [hunting and gathering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter-gatherer), including the taking of [polar bears](24408.md); the practice of hunting polar bears (*Ursus maritimus*) continues up to the present time.[^cite_note-12]

Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug is traversed by the northeasterly line of equal latitude and longitude, that is, at the point 70°N and 70°E, with equal degrees. The [Polar Urals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_Urals) rise in the western part and the highest point of the okrug, as well as of the whole [Ural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_Mountains) mountain system, is [Mount Payer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Payer).[^cite_note-PB-13][^cite_note-GЕ-14]

The area consists of arctic tundra and taiga, with three large peninsulas – the [Yamal Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamal_Peninsula), [Taz Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taz_Peninsula?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the [Gyda Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyda_Peninsula) (itself containing the [Yavay Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yavay_Peninsula?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Mamonta Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamonta_Peninsula?action=edit\&redlink=1)). There are nearly 300,000 lakes in the okrug, some of the main ones being [Pyakuto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyakuto), [Chyortovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Chyortovo), [Neito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neito), [Yambuto the 1st](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yambuto_the_1st?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Yarroto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarroto?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Yambuto the 2nd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yambuto_the_2nd?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-STM-15]

The [Ob River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ob_River) flows through Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug to the [Kara Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kara_Sea) via the [Gulf of Ob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Ob), which dominates the geography of the Okrug (together with its sub-bay, the [Taz Estuary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taz_Estuary) and neighboring [Gydan Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gydan_Bay)).[^cite_note-npolar.no-16][^cite_note-http\://russiatrek.org/yamalo-nenets-okrug-17]

A number of islands are off the okrug's coast – from west to east, the main ones are [Torasovey Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torasovey_Island?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Bolotnyy Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolotnyy_Island?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Litke Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litke_Island?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Sharapovy Koshki Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharapovy_Koshki_Islands?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Bely Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bely_Island), [Shokalsky Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shokalsky_Island), [Petsovyye Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petsovyye_Islands?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Proklyatyye Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proklyatyye_Islands?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Oleny Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleny_Island), and [Vilkitsky Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilkitsky_Island_\(Kara_Sea\)).

## History

On December 10, 1930, **Yamal (Nenets) National [Okrug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okrug)** (Ямальский (Ненецкий) национальный округ) was formed based on [Ural Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_Oblast_\(1923%E2%80%931934\)).

**Population**: 510,490 ([2021 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2021Census-10] 522,904 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2010Census-19] 507,006 ([2002 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Russian_census));[^cite_note-PopCensus-20] 486,164 ([1989 Soviet census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Soviet_census)).[^cite_note-Census1989-21]

From 1960 to 2016, Yamal Nenets population increased from 60 000 people to more than 530 000 due to the natural resources discovered in the region. Currently, Yamal Nenets is the only Arctic Region in the Russian Federation that is not experiencing population decline. Despite the growing pressure on the regional environment, former governor Dmitry Kobylkin assured in 2016 that industrial developments are not affecting the traditional lifestyles of the native population. Official data accounts for an increment of 11 percent of the indigenous population from 2006 to 2016.[^cite_note-22]

### Ethnic groups

The [Nenets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nenets_people) make up 8.9% of the population, preceded by ethnic [Russians](59510.md) (62.9%), and followed by [Tatars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatars) (4.7%) and [Ukrainians](145144.md) (4.5%). Other prominent ethnic groups include [Khanty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khanty) (2.5%), [Azerbaijanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijanis) (1.7%), [Bashkirs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashkirs) (1.5%), [Kumyks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumyks) (1.2%), and [Nogais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nogais) (0.9%) (all figures are from the 2021 Census).[^cite_note-25]

According to a 2012 survey[^cite_note-2012ArenaAtlas-27] 42.2% of the population of Yamalia adhere to the [Russian Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Orthodox_Church), 14% are [unaffiliated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nondenominational_Christianity) generic [Christians](18337522.md), 1% are believers in Orthodox Christianity who do not belong to any church, 1% are members of the [Slavic neopaganism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavic_neopaganism) (Rodnovery) or practitioners of local [shamanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamanism) religions, and 1% are members of [Protestant churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism); [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim), mostly [Caucasian peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peoples_of_the_Caucasus) and [Tatars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatars), make up 18% of the total population. In addition, 14% of the population declare to be "spiritual but not religious", 8% are [atheist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheism), and 0.8% follow other religions or did not give an answer to the question.[^cite_note-2012ArenaAtlas-27]

## Economy



[Zapolyarnoye gas field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zapolyarnoye_gas_field)

In 2009, the Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug was Russia's most important source of [natural gas](22131.md), with more than 90% of Russia's natural gas being produced there. The region also accounted for 12% of Russia's oil production.[^cite_note-kommersant-29] The region is the most important to Russia's largest company [Gazprom](320489.md), whose main production fields are located there. [Novatek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novatek) – the country's second-largest gas producer – is also active in the region, with its headquarters located in [Tarko-Sale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarko-Sale). According to Novatek on 22 October 2019, the natural gas reserves in the Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug represent 80% of Russia's natural gas and 15% of the world's natural gas supply.[^cite_note-Novatek20191022-30]

Since the early 2010s, Gazprom has been developing [Yamal project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamal_project) in the Yamal Peninsula area. As of 2020, Yamal produces over 20% of Russia's gas, which is expected to increase to 40% by 2030. The shortest pipeline routes from Yamal to the northern EU countries are the [Yamal–Europe pipeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamal%E2%80%93Europe_pipeline) through Poland and [Nord Stream 1](3498125.md) to Germany.[^cite_note-31] The proposed gas route from Western Siberia to [China](5405.md) is known as [Power of Siberia 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altai_gas_pipeline) pipeline.[^cite_note-32]
