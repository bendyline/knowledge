This is a list of [prime ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister) of [Mali](19127.md) since the country gained independence from [France](5843419.md) in 1960 to the present day. The prime minister heads the [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Mali).

A total of seventeen people have served as Prime Minister of Mali (not counting five acting prime ministers). Additionally, one person, [Modibo Keita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modibo_Keita_\(born_1942\)), served on two non-consecutive occasions.

The current Interim Prime Minister of Mali is [Abdoulaye Maïga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdoulaye_Ma%C3%AFga_\(officer\)), since 21 November 2024. He was appointed by the National Committee for the Salvation of the People, which has governed the country since the [2021 Malian coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Malian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat).

The [2023 constitutional referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Malian_constitutional_referendum) brought a new constitution into force on July 22, 2023, which reduced the powers of the prime minister significantly. The prime minister and government are now responsible to the president rather than parliament, and the president heads and directs the policy of the government.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
