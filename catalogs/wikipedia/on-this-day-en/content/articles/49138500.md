The **Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 中央军委政治工作部) is the chief political organ under the [Central Military Commission](214345.md) (CMC). It was created in January 2016 following the [military reforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_People's_Republic_of_China_military_reform) under CMC chairman [Xi Jinping](2017814.md). Its predecessor was the [General Political Department of the People's Liberation Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Political_Department_of_the_People's_Liberation_Army).

The department leads all political and cultural activities in the [People's Liberation Army](66890.md).[^cite_note-1] Lieutenant General [Chen Demin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Demin?action=edit\&redlink=1) currently serves as the acting director.[^cite_note-2] Its deputies are [Wang Chengnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Chengnan?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Zhang Yutang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Yutang?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Xiong Zhaoyuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiong_Zhaoyuan?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

The Political Work Department's Liaison Department controls a [United Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Front_\(China\)) organization called the [China Association for International Friendly Contact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Association_for_International_Friendly_Contact) (CAIFC) that is active in [overseas intelligence gathering](2440214.md) and [influence operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influence_operations).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## History

In November 2015 the [General Political Department of the PLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Political_Department_of_the_People's_Liberation_Army) was abolished and was replaced with the CMC Political Work Department as part of Chairman Xi Jinping's [military reforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_People's_Republic_of_China_military_reform).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] Its role is to integrate the CCP and its ideology and propaganda into the People's Liberation Army. In January 2016, the Political Work Department became official.[^cite_note-\:0-11]
