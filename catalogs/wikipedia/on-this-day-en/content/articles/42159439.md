# Ministries of the Dominican Republic

The **Ministries of the Dominican Republic** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Ministerios de la República Dominicana*) are the primary units of the [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_branch) of the [government of the Dominican Republic](77006.md). These offices were formerly known as [Secretaries of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretaries_of_State) ([Spanish](26825.md): *Secretarias de Estado*), but that denominations changed on 2010, with the [Constitutional reform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Dominican_Republic). The change was effective on February 6, 2010, by Decree no. 55–10.[^cite_note-1]

The history of these offices can be traced to the first [Constitution of the Dominican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Dominican_Republic), signed at [San Cristóbal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Crist%C3%B3bal,_Dominican_Republic), on November 6, 1844. The first four institutions, known as Secretaries of State, were: Justice and Public Instruction; the Interior and Police; Finance and Commerce; and War and Navy.[^cite_note-2] As of February, 2023, the [Dominican Republic](8060.md) has a total of 24 Ministries, including the General Attorney's Office.

Ministries are analogous to [Departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) and [Secretaries of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretaries_of_State) in others nations.

## Council of Ministers

Main article: [Cabinet of the Dominican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_Dominican_Republic)

The heads of the ministries, known as [Ministers](739237.md) of their respective department, form the traditional [Cabinet of the Dominican Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_Dominican_Republic), an executive organ that serves at the disposal of the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Dominican_Republic) for the coordination of the government affairs and normally act as an advisory body to the presidency.

Out of the 24 Ministries, only 3 are women, as of February 2023.
