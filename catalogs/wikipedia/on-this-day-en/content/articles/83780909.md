Further information: [2026 Mubadala DC Open](83684794.md)

[Alexandra Eala](62712906.md) defeated [Jessica Pegula](32944701.md) in the final, 4–6, 6–4, 6–0 to win the women's singles tennis title at the [2026 Washington Open](83684794.md). It was her first [WTA Tour](1398173.md) title, becoming the first Filipino to win a tour-level singles title. Eala was also the first [Southeast Asian](28741.md) player to win a singles title at the [WTA 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_500) level or higher.[^cite_note-1]

[Leylah Fernandez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leylah_Fernandez) was the defending champion,[^cite_note-2] but lost in the second round to Eala.[^cite_note-3]
