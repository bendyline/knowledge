The **2025–26 South Carolina Gamecocks women's basketball team** represented the [University of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina) during the [2025–26 NCAA Division I women's basketball season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_season). The Gamecocks, led by eighteenth-year head coach [Dawn Staley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawn_Staley), played their home games at [Colonial Life Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonial_Life_Arena) and competed as members of the [Southeastern Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_Conference) (SEC), where they won a 5th consecutive regular season championship. For the second consecutive season, they were also the NCAA tournament runner-up.

## Previous season

The [Gamecocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_South_Carolina_Gamecocks_women's_basketball_team) finished the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_rankings) 35–4, 15–1 in SEC play, to share the SEC regular season title with [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Texas_Longhorns_women's_basketball_team). As the No. 1 seed in the [SEC tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_SEC_women's_basketball_tournament), they defeated [Vanderbilt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Vanderbilt_Commodores_women's_basketball_team), [Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Oklahoma_Sooners_women's_basketball_team) and [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Texas_Longhorns_women's_basketball_team) to win the SEC Tournament. They received an automatic bid to the [NCAA tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NCAA_Division_I_women's_basketball_tournament) as the No. 1 seed in the Birmingham 2 region. They defeated [Tennessee Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Tennessee_Tech_Golden_Eagles_women's_basketball_team) and [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Indiana_Hoosiers_women's_basketball_team) in the first and second rounds, [Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Maryland_Terrapins_women's_basketball_team) in the sweet sixteen and [Duke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Duke_Blue_Devils_women's_basketball_team) in the elite eight to advance to their 5th straight Final Four where they defeated SEC member Texas before losing in the national championship game to [UConn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UConn_Huskies_women's_basketball_team).
