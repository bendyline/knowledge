On 28 June 2026, a [Pilatus PC-6 Porter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilatus_PC-6_Porter) carrying [skydivers](19723982.md) crashed immediately after takeoff from [Nancy-Essey Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy-Essey_Airport) in [Tomblaine](15886909.md), France, killing all eleven occupants.[^cite_note-BBCJune2026-1] The accident is the deadliest skydiving plane crash in French history.[^cite_note-2]

## Background

### Aircraft

The aircraft involved was D-FIPS, a [Pilatus PC-6/B2-H4 Turbo Porter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilatus_PC-6_Porter) manufactured in 1991, operated by Classic Wings/KIAS Airlines and leased to Tandemotion Parachutisme.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-BEA-4]

### Passengers and crew

The pilot, five instructors, and five trainee parachutists were on board the aircraft. The trainees were reportedly a group of nurses. None of them survived.[^cite_note-5]

## Accident

The aircraft crashed at around 11 a.m. into a bike path in a residential area about 300 metres (330 yd) from the runway, narrowly missing nearby homes and a shopping centre.[^cite_note-6] The plane plunged vertically to the ground. A witness said the plane seemingly experienced [engine failure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engine_failure).[^cite_note-7] A resident said two bodies were thrown a few meters from the plane.[^cite_note-8] A significant emergency response was mobilized, with police units and 50 firefighters deployed to the scene.[^cite_note-9] The prefect of Meurthe-et-Moselle, Yves Séguy, brought together the relevant services for real-time monitoring of the situation. The French Interior Minister, [Laurent Nuñez](60527935.md), also visited the site.[^cite_note-10]

## Investigation

The [Bureau of Enquiry and Analysis for Civil Aviation Safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Enquiry_and_Analysis_for_Civil_Aviation_Safety) (BEA) sent four investigators and a first information investigator to the crash scene.[^cite_note-11] A judicial inquiry for involuntary manslaughter was opened on 6 July.[^cite_note-12]

The BEA's July preliminary report said two instructors shouted "there's smoke" during rotation, though none was visible on videos. The engine's gas generator speed then fell by about 10%, and the aircraft pitched up and entered an asymmetric stall.[^cite_note-BEA-4]
