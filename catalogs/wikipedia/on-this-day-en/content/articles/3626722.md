This is a list of winners and nominees of the **[Primetime Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award) for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Comedy Series**. Prior to 1988 the category was not gender specific, thus was called **Outstanding Guest Performer in a Comedy Series**. These awards, like the other "Guest" awards, are not presented at the Primetime Emmy Award ceremony, but rather at the [Creative Arts Emmy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Arts_Emmy_Award) ceremony.

Beginning with the 77th Primetime Emmy Awards, performers are no longer eligible in guest acting categories if they were previously nominated for a lead or supporting award for playing the same character role in the same series.[^cite_note-1]

[Nathan Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Lane) has received the most nominations in this category with 7, winning once for the [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu) comedy series *[Only Murders in the Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_Murders_in_the_Building)* in 2022. [Mel Brooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Brooks) has received the most wins in this category for his role in the [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC) comedy series *[Mad About You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_About_You)* in 1997, 1998, and 1999.
