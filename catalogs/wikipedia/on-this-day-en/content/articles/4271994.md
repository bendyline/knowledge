The **Department of Primary Industries and Regional Development** (**DPIRD**) is a [Western Australian](33613.md) government department responsible for regulating and advancing agricultural and food industries, fisheries and regional development within the state. It was formed by an amalgamation of the **Department of Agriculture and Food**, **Department of Fisheries** and **Department of Regional Development** in 2017.

The [Minister for Agriculture and Food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Agriculture_and_Food_\(Western_Australia\)), the [Minister for Regional Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Regional_Development_\(Western_Australia\)) and the [Minister for Fisheries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Fisheries_\(Western_Australia\)) are responsible for the department.

In 2004 the department had operating costs of \$215,000,000 approx with \$120,000,000 provided directly by the state government. The balance was from federal government grants, public operating activities and user charges and fees.[^cite_note-1]

This department was also responsible for [quarantine](25237.md) control on all plants, soil and animal products brought into the state. The Agricultural Protection Board \[needs updating] is also part of this and responsible for the eradication of pests in Western Australia; including the rainbow lorikeet (*[Trichoglossus moluccanus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trichoglossus_moluccanus)*),[^cite_note-2] skeleton weed (*[Chondrilla juncea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chondrilla_juncea)*),[^cite_note-3] and Portuguese millipede (*[Ommatoiulus moreletii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ommatoiulus_moreletii)*).[^cite_note-4]

## History

In 1894, [Premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_Western_Australia) [John Forrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Forrest), established the Bureau of Agriculture under the chairmanship of [Charles Harper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Harper_\(politician\)).[^cite_note-5] The members were A. R. Richardson, W. Paterson (the first manager of the [Agricultural Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_Western_Australia)), J. H. D. Amherst, [F. H. Piesse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Henry_Piesse) and [G. L. Throssell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Throssell). Initially, the bureau was not placed under the supervision of a minister. This changed in April 1898 when the bureau became the Agricultural Advisory Board and a new Department of Agriculture was gazetted under the control of Throssell as Commissioner for Crown Lands.

Professor William Lowrie was appointed director in 1908. He resigned in 1911. [Sir James Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Mitchell_\(Australian_politician\)) was at this time Minister for Agriculture and he expanded the senior position to three commissioners: George Lowe Sutton, as Commissioner for the Wheat Belt, James M. B. Connor as Commissioner for the South-West; and James P. Moody as Commissioner for the Fruit Industries.
