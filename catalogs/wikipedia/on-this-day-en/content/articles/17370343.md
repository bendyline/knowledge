The **vice president of Ecuador** is the second highest political position in [Ecuador](9334.md). In many instances, the vice president succeeded the [president](840787.md) in turbulent political situations. The last time was in 2005 after the resignation of president [Lucio Gutiérrez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucio_Guti%C3%A9rrez). According to the current [constitution](18864296.md), the vice president is elected on the same ticket as the president. Salary of the vice president is US\$4,869 per month.[^cite_note-1]

The current vice president is [Maria Jose Pinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Jose_Pinto) since 24 May 2025.&#x20;
