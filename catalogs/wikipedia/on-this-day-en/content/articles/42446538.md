**Ras al-Maara** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): رأس المعرة) is a [Syrian](7515849.md) village in the [Yabroud District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yabroud_District) of the [Rif Dimashq Governorate](4521946.md). According to the [Syria Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bureau_of_Statistics_\(Syria\)) (CBS), Ras al-Maara had a population of 8,520 in the 2004 census.[^cite_note-CBS-1]
