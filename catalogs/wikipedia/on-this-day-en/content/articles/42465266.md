**Maarounah** (Arabic: ***معرونة***, romanized: ***maearuwna***) is a [Syrian](7515849.md) village in the [Al-Tall District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Tall_District) of the [Rif Dimashq Governorate](4521946.md). According to the [Syria Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bureau_of_Statistics_\(Syria\)) (CBS), Maarounah had a population of 1,153 in the 2004 census.[^cite_note-CBS-1] It is a [Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity_in_Syria) village.[^cite_note-2] With rugged mountainous terrain, the residents rely on cultivating olives and figs, as well as livestock farming. Many archaeological sites overlook the village, making it a popular summer retreat for Damascus residents.

In the Syrian Civil War, the [Guardians of the Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Dawn) group played a key role in the fight for the Qalamoun Mountains, where this village is located, between 2013 and 2014.
