[Formula One](10854.md) (F1) is the highest class of [open-wheeled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_wheel_car) [motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorsport) defined by the [Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile](248019.md) (FIA), motorsport's world governing body.[^cite_note-1] The "formula" in the name refers to a set of rules to which all participants and cars must conform.[^cite_note-2] The [F1 World Championship season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_seasons) consists of a series of races, known as [Grands Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_Grands_Prix), held usually on purpose-built [circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_circuits), and in a few cases on [closed city streets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_circuit).[^cite_note-3] The World Constructors' Championship is presented by the FIA to the most successful F1 [constructor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_constructors) over the course of the season through a [points system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_points_systems) based on individual Grand Prix results. Constructors' Championship points are calculated by adding points scored in each race by any driver for that constructor.[^cite_note-4] According to FIA regulations, the constructor of a chassis and an engine of a [Formula One car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_car) is a person or corporate entity that owns the intellectual rights to the chassis and the engine of the car that a team/entrant enters into a competition.[^cite_note-\:0-5]&#x20;

From the inaugural season of the World Constructors' Championship in [1958](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958_Formula_One_season) up until the [1978](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_Formula_One_season) season only the highest-scoring driver in each race for each constructor contributed points towards the World Constructors' Championship (then officially as the *International Cup for Formula One Constructors*); since the [1979](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_Formula_One_season) season points from all cars entered by each constructor have counted towards their championship total.[^cite_note-6] The Constructors' Championship is won when it is no longer mathematically possible for another constructor to overtake another's points total regardless of the outcome of the remaining races,[^cite_note-7] although it is not officially awarded until the [FIA Prize Giving Ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIA_Prize_Giving_Ceremony) held in various cities following the conclusion of the season.[^cite_note-8]&#x20;

The Constructors' Championship was first awarded, as the *International Cup for Formula One Manufacturers*, in [1958](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958_Formula_One_season) to [Vanwall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanwall).[^cite_note-9] In [1981](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981_Formula_One_World_Championship) this name was officially changed to the *World Constructors' Championship*. The makes of both chassis and an engine are included in the constructor name.[^cite_note-10]&#x20;

Out of the 170 chassis constructors that have entered an F1 Grand Prix,[^cite_note-11] a total of 15 have won the Championship in its 68 seasons. [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) holds the record for the highest number of World Constructors' Championships victories, having won the title on 16 occasions. [McLaren](20994.md) are in second position with ten Constructors' Championships. [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing) are in third position with nine Constructors' Championships and [Mercedes](25098794.md) are in fourth with eight titles.[^cite_note-CF1Constructors-12][^cite_note-8WChamps-13] With 16 titles, Ferrari has amassed the highest number of Constructors' Championships as an engine manufacturer, followed by [Renault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_in_Formula_One), Mercedes, [Ford](30433662.md) and [Honda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_in_Formula_One) with twelve, twelve, ten and six titles, respectively.[^cite_note-14] Mercedes holds the record for the most consecutive constructors' titles with eight between the [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Formula_One_World_Championship) and the [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Formula_One_World_Championship) seasons.[^cite_note-ConsecutiveWins-15]&#x20;

All but 17 titles (16 titles won by Italian-built Ferrari chassis and 1 title won by French-built [Matra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equipe_Matra_Sports) chassis) have been won by chassis that were designed and constructed in the United Kingdom.[^cite_note-16] On twelve occasions, the World Constructors' Champion team has not contained the World Drivers' Champion for that season.[^cite_note-8WChamps-13] All titles but one (in [1969](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969_Formula_One_season) Matra cars were entered by [Ken Tyrrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Tyrrell)'s [privateer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privateer_\(motorsport\)) team [Matra International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrrell_Racing)) have been won by cars that were entered by [works teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factory-backed).[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] Among drivers that have contributed with at least a single point to the constructors' title, [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) has contributed to the most with eight constructors' titles, all of them with Mercedes.[^cite_note-19]&#x20;

## By chassis constructor

([Ferrari F2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_F2008) pictured)

([Mercedes F1 W07 Hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_F1_W07_Hybrid) pictured)



The [Red Bull Racing RB19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Bull_Racing_RB19) is the most dominant car to win a World Constructors' Championship, winning 21 of 22 Grands Prix across the [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Formula_One_World_Championship) season.



[McLaren](20994.md), second in all time championships, are the reigning World Constructors' Champions, having won back-to-back titles in [2024](72504121.md) and [2025](72768531.md). ([McLaren MCL39](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McLaren_MCL39) pictured)
