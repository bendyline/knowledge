**Flydubai Flight 1073** was a regularly scheduled international passenger flight operated by [Flydubai](18432521.md) from [Dubai International Airport](154742.md) in Dubai, the United Arab Emirates (UAE) to [Ben Gurion Airport](113149.md) near [Tel Aviv](31453.md), Israel. On 30 September 2026, while en route, the [Boeing 737 MAX 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737_MAX_8) broadcast the [squawk code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squawk_code) for a [hijacking](2075.md), diverted from its [flight path](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airway_\(aviation\)), and made an emergency landing at [Prince Sultan bin Abdulaziz Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Sultan_bin_Abdulaziz_Airport) in [Tabuk](900737.md), Saudi Arabia. UAE officials have called the incident an attempted terrorist attack.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

According to passenger reports, the [first officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_officer_\(aviation\)), **Hamam al-Hammami**,[^cite_note-Al_Lawati_etal-4][^cite_note-McCluskey-5][^cite_note-copilot-6][^cite_note-confusion-7] struck and seriously wounded the [captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilot_in_command), [Smit Machchhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smit_Machchhar), with the cockpit [crash axe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axe#Crash_axe)[^cite_note-Kipling-8] during the flight and sent the plane into a steep descent, in an apparent attempt to [hijack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_hijackings) and crash the plane, causing structural damage to the aircraft's [rudder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudder) due to [overspeed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overspeed_\(aeronautics\)).[^cite_note-toi2-9] The captain was able to open the cockpit door to let in passengers, who restrained the first officer and stabilized the plane.[^cite_note-10] Other Flydubai pilots [deadheading on board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadheading_\(employee\)) then landed the plane in Tabuk. The incident is under investigation by law enforcement agencies from Israel, Oman, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and the United States.[^cite_note-jaiswal-11][^cite_note-peled-12][^cite_note-kweller-13]

## Background

### Aircraft

The aircraft involved in the incident was a [Boeing 737 MAX 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737_MAX_8), registered A6-FKF. The plane entered service with Flydubai on 21 December 2022. At the time of the incident, the aircraft was approximately three years and nine months old.[^cite_note-14] Flydubai is owned by the [Investment Corporation of Dubai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investment_Corporation_of_Dubai).[^cite_note-15]

### Passengers and crew

There were 174 passengers, including 150 [Israeli](181296.md) citizens,[^cite_note-toi2-9] and 6 crew members onboard.[^cite_note-ynet-16][^cite_note-Klein-2026-17]

The crew consisted of two pilots: an [Indian](7564733.md) captain, [Smit Machchhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smit_Machchhar), who was the [pilot flying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilot_flying), and Hamam al-Hammami,[^cite_note-Al_Lawati_etal-4][^cite_note-McCluskey-5][^cite_note-copilot-6][^cite_note-confusion-7] an [Omani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omanis) [first officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_officer_\(aviation\)) who was the [pilot monitoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilot_monitoring).[^cite_note-Reuters-20261001-18] Machchhar, aged 38,[^cite_note-19] trained as a pilot at Mainland Air in [Dunedin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunedin), New Zealand,[^cite_note-20] and flew for [SpiceJet](1701189.md) for 11 years before joining Flydubai in 2022.[^cite_note-21] At the time of the incident he had logged about 9,750 hours on Boeing 737 aircraft, 6,400 of them as pilot-in-command.[^cite_note-22]

There were also two [deadheading pilots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadheading_\(employee\)), one of whom was [British](19097669.md),[^cite_note-Berman-23] in the passenger cabin[^cite_note-Klein-2026-17] in order to operate the return flight back to [Dubai](154742.md).[^cite_note-Berman-23]

### Middle Eastern conflict

Due to the ongoing [conflict across the Middle East](75895273.md), several Western airlines reduced flights to Israel, but the UAE airlines continued to provide multiple flights each day between Dubai and Tel Aviv.[^cite_note-WSJSep30-24]

On 29 September, the day before Flydubai Flight 1073, Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) publicly stated that he had received indications that Israel might be attacked ahead of the upcoming [legislative election](71244652.md) on 27 October. Political opponents dismissed the statement as "[fearmongering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fearmongering)".[^cite_note-Kershner-25][^cite_note-Berman-2026-26] Opposition leader [Yair Lapid](2448072.md) was briefed shortly thereafter and denounced Netanyahu's warning and insisted there was no reason to panic.[^cite_note-Berman-2026-26] Lapid later stated, "Nothing even remotely resembling the \[Flydubai Flight 1073] incident came up in the security briefing."[^cite_note-27]

## Incident



Flight telemetry showing aircraft entering a steep dive at 05\:20 ([UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC); [Gulf time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+04\:00) is 4 hours ahead)

Flight 1073 departed from Dubai at 7\:05 a.m. ([GST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_Standard_Time)) traveling west to Tel Aviv.[^cite_note-ynet-16] The flight was scheduled to be three and a half hours, and the incident started roughly an hour before its expected arrival.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29] While flying near the [Saudi Arabia–Jordan border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan%E2%80%93Saudi_Arabia_border) at 9\:21 a.m.,[^cite_note-30] it plunged from an altitude of approximately 34,000 ft (10,000 m) to 17,000 ft (5,200 m) in less than a minute,[^cite_note-zman-31][^cite_note-32] diving towards the ground at speeds estimated at 600 miles per hour (970 km/h).[^cite_note-Pereira-33] The plane briefly rose to 21,750 ft (6,600 m), before descending again to 15,000 ft (4,600 m) and turning back into Saudi [airspace](353557.md).[^cite_note-toi2-9][^cite_note-fo-34] Shortly after the initial dive, the aircraft's [transponder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transponder_\(aeronautics\)) broadcast squawk code 7700, indicating a general emergency, and shortly thereafter broadcast code 7500, indicating "unlawful interference" or a hijacking.[^cite_note-toi3-35]

Based on initial assessments by authorities and video and eyewitness accounts from passengers, al-Hammami, the [first officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_officer_\(aviation\)), stabbed and seriously wounded the captain, [Smit Machchhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smit_Machchhar), using the crash axe found in the cockpit,[^cite_note-Kipling-8] in an alleged attempt to gain control of the aircraft and crash it.[^cite_note-36] In a televised interview with Netanyahu, Machchhar said "The co-pilot wanted to pray. A lot of the locals, they do pray, which is not out of the ordinary. While he was praying, out of respect, I was just looking at the front and not at the back — and suddenly after about four, five minutes, there was a big blow to the back of my head.” [^cite_note-37] The sound of the struggle alerted Tali Manes, a mother waiting for her daughter to finish using the nearby lavatory.[^cite_note-Drori-2026-38][^cite_note-39] Tali informed her husband, Tzvika Manes, sitting in the front row, and one of the two [deadheading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadheading_\(employee\)) pilots on board.[^cite_note-Drori-2026-38] Meanwhile, Machchhar managed to click the release to open the cockpit door from the inside, which otherwise would have been difficult to penetrate.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41] Tzvika saw Machchhar fall out of the cockpit with the first officer "on top of him, stabbing him" as the plane began to dive.[^cite_note-Drori-2026-38][^cite_note-pd-42]

One of the deadheading pilots (identified by the passengers as "James"),[^cite_note-Itzhaki-2026-43] Tzvika, and other passengers, including Yaniv Hayun, Dr. Shota Musayev and Asaf Rajuan then attempted to enter the cockpit,[^cite_note-pd-42][^cite_note-Itzhaki-2026-43] which Hayun said was difficult because the captain’s body was resting against the door.[^cite_note-44] They then overpowered al-Hammami, who was allegedly attempting to disable or damage the flight controls, and removed him from the cockpit.[^cite_note-YNET_Eichner-45] Hayun stated that he pulled back on the [yoke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoke_\(aeronautics\)) to level the plane, based on having watched the Canadian documentary series [*Mayday*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayday_\(Canadian_TV_series\)).[^cite_note-46] The deadheading pilot "James" then took over,[^cite_note-Klein-2026-17][^cite_note-pd-42][^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48] later joined by the other deadheading pilot.[^cite_note-toi2-9] Al-Hammami was initially restrained with [headphone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headphones) wires,[^cite_note-The_Times_of_Israel-2026-49] and reportedly yelled "Kill me, kill me";[^cite_note-Burke-50][^cite_note-CNN-2026-51] he was later restrained with [cable ties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cable_tie).[^cite_note-News18-2026-52] Dr. Musayev, a dentist, treated the captain's injuries.[^cite_note-The_Times_of_Israel-2026-49]

Upon receiving the hijacking code and being unable to contact the flight for 30 minutes,[^cite_note-53] the [Israeli Air Force](177619.md) scrambled fighter jets.[^cite_note-toi2-9][^cite_note-fo-34] The aircraft continued west into [Jordanian](7515964.md) airspace before turning around and heading back toward Saudi Arabia.[^cite_note-54] After an hour flying at its new altitude of 15,000 ft (4,600 m), the plane landed safely at [Prince Sultan bin Abdulaziz Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Sultan_bin_Abdulaziz_Airport) in [Tabuk](900737.md), in northwestern Saudi Arabia at 10\:45 a.m. GST (9\:45 a.m. [SAST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia_Standard_Time)).[^cite_note-zman-31][^cite_note-toi3-35][^cite_note-okaz-55] Photographs taken after the landing showed that most of the aircraft's [rudder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudder#Aircraft_rudders) was missing; it had likely detached during the rapid descent when the plane exceeded its maximum design speed.[^cite_note-toi2-9]

Upon landing, al-Hammami was arrested by Saudi authorities.[^cite_note-toi2-9] Both injured pilots were taken to nearby hospitals.[^cite_note-News18-2026-52] The captain was in serious condition after sustaining deep cuts to the head and hands;[^cite_note-News18-2026-52] his condition was later reported to be stable.[^cite_note-moore-56][^cite_note-JP-57] Dr. Musayev said that he feared that if the landing had been delayed by even a few more moments, the captain would not have survived.[^cite_note-CNN-Musaev-58] One passenger was also hospitalized, and some passengers required bandages for minor injuries, but most passengers were unharmed.[^cite_note-59]

## Investigation

Local Saudi authorities questioned the passengers and cabin crew for seven hours.[^cite_note-WSJSep30-24] An initial assessment from Saudi authorities concluded that al-Hammami intended to crash the plane, though they could not determine a motive or whether he acted alone.[^cite_note-WSJSep30-24] Saudi Arabia is cooperating with Israel, Oman, the UAE, and the United States in its investigation into a motive.[^cite_note-GuardianOct1-60] Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said that UAE President [Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan](2121945.md) agreed to let Israel participate in the investigation.[^cite_note-kweller-13][^cite_note-61] Two Israeli security officials stated that Israel's initial assessment was that al-Hammami "likely acted alone" and that they had not identified any [Iranian involvement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_and_state-sponsored_terrorism). One official said they had not received all the information they had sought from Saudi Arabia. Netanyahu stated that it was "too early to say" whether Iran was involved,[^cite_note-Reuters-20261001-18][^cite_note-GuardianOct1-60] but said "it looks like a suicide attempt".[^cite_note-62]

Al-Hammami is an Omani national,[^cite_note-63] who had worked for the airline for less than a year.[^cite_note-Burke-50][^cite_note-64] According to Netanyahu, "he was suicidal, that’s not a question" and interrogation revealed he had undergone [Islamist radical indoctrination](8734632.md).[^cite_note-CNN-2026-51] According to [Al Arabiya](321645.md), he was radicalized while in Syria.[^cite_note-65] Under its agreement with Israel, Flydubai was obligated to exclude Omani pilots from any of its Israeli operations because [Oman and Israel do not have diplomatic relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93Oman_relations).[^cite_note-66]

On 1 October, the UAE launched a formal investigation into the incident, including the motive, the weapon used (undetermined as of 30 September),[^cite_note-67] and any possible link to terrorism.[^cite_note-68] Al-Hammami was also transferred from Saudi Arabia to the UAE that same day.[^cite_note-69]

On 2 October, *[The Wall Street Journal](173070.md)* reported that Oman had previously banned the first officer from piloting because he had radical ideological views.[^cite_note-Czerny_etal-70] It was unclear if Oman had shared its ban with other countries or airlines.[^cite_note-Czerny_etal-70] On 3 October, [ABC News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABC_News_\(United_States\)) reported that extremist material had been found in his possession while he worked for [Oman Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oman_Air) in 2024, yet he remained in an administrative role for the airline. The same report said that al-Hammami was cooperating with UAE authorities, and indicated that he intended to crash the airplane in Israel.[^cite_note-71]

## Aftermath

Captain [Smit Machchhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smit_Machchhar) described his actions during the flight, in a call with [Prime Minister of India](24452.md) [Narendra Modi](444222.md) as he recovered in hospital in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md).

[^cite_note-haworth-73]

[^cite_note-reuterscall-74]

In an official statement, Flydubai confirmed that an "incident" had occurred during the flight, noting that the plane landed safely in Tabuk and that all passengers were secure.[^cite_note-75][^cite_note-76] A second statement cited "an altercation" on the flight deck, called the motives unknown, and said on-duty crew travelling on the flight landed the aircraft.[^cite_note-fz-77]

Saudi Arabia, which has no [diplomatic relations with Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93Saudi_Arabia_relations), refused a request to allow [Israeli military](39237.md) aircraft to retrieve Israeli passengers from Tabuk;[^cite_note-78] instead, Flydubai sent two replacement aircraft to Tabuk to fly the passengers to Tel Aviv.[^cite_note-kan-79][^cite_note-80][^cite_note-fz-77]

Netanyahu and Defence Minister [Israel Katz](19041378.md) held a security consultation, and [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) (IDF) chief of staff [Eyal Zamir](61877756.md) held a situational assessment with commander of the Israeli Air Force [Omer Tischler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omer_Tischler) and other senior military officials.[^cite_note-ynet-16][^cite_note-toi3-35][^cite_note-sc-81] Israeli officials called it an attempted terrorist attack,[^cite_note-82] and Netanyahu said that it is too early to establish who was behind the attack.[^cite_note-83] Netanyahu and Indian prime minister [Narendra Modi](444222.md) both praised Captain Machchhar.[^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85]

Netanyahu announced new security measures in response to the incident.[^cite_note-sc-81] Following Israeli transport minister [Miri Regev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miri_Regev)'s advice, Israel suspended all Flydubai flights to the country for six days.[^cite_note-86] The UAE agreed to Israel's security demands, allowing [El Al](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Al), [Arkia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkia), and [Israir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israir) to resume flights after being restricted by [Israeli security forces](315904.md) for the preceding six months over unresolved security concerns stemming from the [Middle Eastern conflict](75895273.md).[^cite_note-87][^cite_note-88] The three airlines announced that they would resume service to Dubai on 2 October to bring back Israelis remaining there; however, El Al's landing slot was delayed until 3 October.[^cite_note-89]

On the morning of 1 October, the captain and first officer were flown from Saudi Arabia to the UAE after they were medically cleared for travel.[^cite_note-ABC_AU-90][^cite_note-91] The captain continued to receive medical treatment in the UAE.[^cite_note-ABC_AU-90] The same day, US president [Donald Trump](4848272.md) threatened to [hit Iran](82533582.md) "very hard" if it was behind the thwarted attack, suggesting that the Omani first officer was linked to [Tehran](50913538.md) "based on what I'm hearing," but did not provide further details.[^cite_note-92][^cite_note-93]

On 2 October, an adviser to UAE president [Mohammed bin Zayed](2121945.md) became the first senior Emirati official to publicly state the incident was an "act of terrorism."[^cite_note-94]

### Disinformation and conspiracy theories

[Antisemitic tropes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisemitic_trope), [anti-Zionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Zionist) [disinformation](142690.md) and [conspiracy theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conspiracy_theory) circulated online, claiming that the incident was an Israeli-planned or staged [false flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False_flag) operation.[^cite_note-95][^cite_note-96][^cite_note-97] Monitoring of social media by the [Combat Antisemitism Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_Antisemitism_Movement) and the Antisemitism Research Center showed conspiracy theories being spread as early as 67 minutes after the plane squawked an emergency signal (i.e., while the plane was still in the air). More than 4,000 posts claiming Jewish or Israeli involvement in the incident were posted in a twelve-hour period after the news was first reported, including nearly 3,000 using the term "false flag".[^cite_note-98]

## Analysis

Richard Levy, a retired airline captain, said that the wounded captain sending an emergency signal and managing to unlock the cockpit door while under attack was remarkable.[^cite_note-Adam-99][^cite_note-Guardian-2026-10-02-100] He also said it was astonishing that the aircraft was flyable after the rudder was ripped off.[^cite_note-Adam-99]

Sally Leivesley, an aviation security expert, estimated that the plane pulled out of its rapid descent with only five seconds to spare before recovery would have been impossible.[^cite_note-Adam-99]

[ABC News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABC_News_\(United_States\)) contributor and retired Col. Steve Ganyard said it was extraordinary that "a passenger who'd done nothing more than watch television shows,... knew how to recover the airplane and pull just hard enough to recover the airplane, but not so hard that it would do \[further] damage or destroy the airplane."[^cite_note-Pereira-33]
