# List of NBA single-game scoring leaders

&#x20; This is a complete listing of [National Basketball Association](22093.md) players who have scored 60 or more [points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_\(basketball\)) in a regular season game.



[Wilt Chamberlain](255645.md) scored [100 points in 1962, which is the current NBA record](9499391.md).

This feat has been accomplished 93 times in NBA history by 38 different players, while just eleven players have joined the 70-point club, with three players having scored at least 80 points. Only 10 players have scored 60 or more points on more than one occasion: [Wilt Chamberlain](255645.md) (32 times); [Kobe Bryant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_Bryant) (6 times); [Damian Lillard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damian_Lillard) (5 times); [Michael Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jordan) and [James Harden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Harden) (4 times); [Elgin Baylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elgin_Baylor) and [Luka Dončić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luka_Don%C4%8Di%C4%87) (3 times); and [Karl-Anthony Towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl-Anthony_Towns), [Devin Booker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devin_Booker), and [Stephen Curry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Curry) (2 times). Chamberlain holds the single-game scoring record, having [scored 100 points in a game in 1962](9499391.md). The youngest player to score at least 60 points in a regular season game is [Devin Booker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devin_Booker) (70 points; 20 years and 145 days),[^cite_note-1] and the oldest is Bryant (60 points; 37 years and 234 days).[^cite_note-2]
