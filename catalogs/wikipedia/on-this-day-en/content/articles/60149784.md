# Bantar Gebang



Landfill in Bantar Gebang

**Bantar Gebang** is administratively a [kelurahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelurahan) or village in [Bekasi](1915107.md) city, Indonesia, but it usually refers to the [massive trash dump](211285.md) in the area, which measures 100 hectares (250 acres).[^cite_note-theguardian-1] Between 6,000 and 20,000 people are estimated to live and work on the site, collecting [recyclables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recyclable) for resale.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-theguardian-1] The site receives approximately 8,000 tons of trash each day from the nation's capital, [Jakarta](16275.md), located about 40 kilometres (25 mi) away.[^cite_note-theguardian-1]

On 8 March 2026, heavy rainfall induced an [avalanche](73321.md) of garbage that killed seven people.[^cite_note-3]

The Indonesian government has stated that it plans to shut down the open landfill, which is significantly over capacity, by the end of 2027, and build a [waste-to-energy plant](1454584.md) on the site.[^cite_note-theguardian-1]
