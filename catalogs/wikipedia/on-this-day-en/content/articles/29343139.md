**Express FM** is a private [Tunisian](30188.md) [radio station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_station) specializing in [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) which was founded on October 21, 2010 by Mourad Gueddiche and Naoufel Ben Rayana, co-founders of Maghreb Productions Communication (MPC). Express FM is the fifth-launched private radio station in Tunisia.[^cite_note-1]
