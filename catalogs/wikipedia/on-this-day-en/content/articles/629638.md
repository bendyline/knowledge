**Yanbu** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ينبع, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Yanbuʿ*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Spring'), colloquially sometimes referred to as **Yambu** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): يمبع, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Yambuʿ*), is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_and_towns_in_Saudi_Arabia) and [governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_governorates_of_Saudi_Arabia) in the [Medina Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medina_Province,_Saudi_Arabia) of western [Saudi Arabia](349303.md), located on the [Red Sea](26191.md) coast. It is a major port and industrial center, known for its oil refineries and the Yanbu Industrial City. Yanbu is home to the province's only two ports, the [Yanbu Commercial Port](61452573.md) and the [King Fahd Industrial Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Fahd_Industrial_Port_\(Yanbu\)), and is the second most populated city in the province after [Medina](36636.md).[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-2]

## History



Old Yanbu

### Pre-modern era

Yanbu's history dates back at least 2,500 years, when it was a staging point on the [spice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spice) and [incense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incense) route from Yemen to Egypt and the Mediterranean region.

Sharm Yanbu ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شرم ينبع), historically known as **[Charmuthas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charmutha_\(Harbor\))**, which is a small peninsula located to the north of Yanbu was mentioned by the [Greek](42056.md) historian [Diodorus Siculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diodorus_Siculus).[^cite_note-3]

The [Invasion of Dul Ashir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_of_Dul_Ashir) took place in Yanbu two or three months after prophet [Muhammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_in_Islam)'s return from Buwat. He appointed [Abu Salamah Ibn Abd al-Assad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Salama_Abd_Allah_ibn_Abd_al-Asad_al-Makhzumi) to take his place in [Medina](36636.md) while he was away commanding another raid. Between 150 and 200 followers joined this operation to al-Ushayra, Yanbu in either the month of [Jumada al-awwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jumada_al-awwal) or [Jumada al-Thani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jumada_al-Thani).[^cite_note-Haykal_1976-4][^cite_note-Mubarakpuri,_The_Sealed_Nectar_p._128-5][^cite_note-Mubarakpuri_p._148-6]

In this operation, Muhammad entered into an alliance with Banu Madlaj, a tribe inhabiting the vicinity of al-Ushayra. He also concluded another treaty that was made with Banu Damrah previously.[^cite_note-Mubarakpuri_p._148-6] All those treaties established good political connections for him.[^cite_note-Mubarakpuri,_The_Sealed_Nectar_p._128-5]

### Modern era

Yanbu served as a supply and operational base for [Arab and British forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Revolt#Prelude) fighting the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md) during [World War I](4764461.md). On 27 July 1916 the Ottoman garrison surrendered in the [Capture of Yanbu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Yanbu) to the [Arab revolutionary forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hejaz).[^cite_note-Purcell-Burg-7] It remained a small port town until 1975, when the Saudi government designated it as one of the country's two new industrial centers (the other being [Al Jubayl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Jubayl) in the [Eastern Province](468721.md)). Extensive government and private development have taken place at the port and surrounding area, which is based on the [petrochemical industry](2293201.md).

During the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), an Iranian missile attacked a refinery near Yanbu, causing some parts of Yanbu to be destroyed.

## Geography

The city is divided into three primary sections, which are located about fifteen minutes (drive by car) away from each other. Yanbu is the Second-largest Saudi Arabian city by the [Red Sea](26191.md), after [Jeddah](277718.md).[^cite_note-\:0-1]

### Yanbu Al-Bahr



A Red Sea beach with turquoise waters at Yanbu



A park at Yanbu

Also known as Yanbu Al-Bahar ("spring by the sea") is the most northern part of Yanbu. The downtown area of Yanbu Al-Bahr (which is also called *Al-Balad*) contains most of the population. Most of the restaurants here cater to the various ethnic groups in the area, although some also cater to residents in the Royal Commission. The area also contains some international chain restaurants, as well as some coffee shops. There is also a shopping mall and various shopping centers located here as well. The city has many popular local brand hotels, including international brand like [Holiday Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holiday_Inn) Hotel, [ ibis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibis_\(hotel\)) Hotel, [Radisson Blu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radisson_Blu) Hotel and [Novotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novotel) Hotel is located at the edge of this area, as is [Prince Abdulmohsen Bin Abdulaziz International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Abdulmohsen_Bin_Abdulaziz_International_Airport). Arabian Homes also have a gated private residential community compound catering to the expat population, and is located next to the Holiday Inn. Most of the residents tend to be lower and middle-class Saudi citizens, and well as mostly South Asian and lower and middle-class Arab and other Middle Eastern expatriates. Every year there is a flower festival at the outskirts of Yanbu that has attracted many viewers around Saudi Arabia and [neighboring countries](220950.md).[^cite_note-8]



Yanbu flower festival

Currently, the government is attempting to improve the old, historical area in downtown Yanbu, as many historical buildings are located there. Many are still in a serious state of disrepair. While most of Yanbu Al-Balad has a rather old, worn-out feel to it, parts of the city, particularly at the edges of the city, are starting to modernize, as new shopping centers, as well as a new shopping mall, have been built. [Jarir Bookstore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarir_Bookstore) had also opened a branch in Yanbu Al-Balad.

### Yanbu Al-Nakhal

(*The Palms*) is a separate area, 47 kilometers from the city. The most notable village in Yanbu Al Nakhal is Jabriyya, where the local government office for the town is located. The village has a general hospital, a bank, three ATM machines, four well-serviced filling stations, a fire station, two notable malls, several recreational facilities among other things. The locals are friendly and helpful. There is almost everything you need for basic livelihood in the neighborhood, however, the options for residents pales in comparison to both the Royal Commission and Yanbu Al Bahr. The Radwah Mountain is very visible anywhere in the town.

However, the town has neither hotels nor international schools, and locals and expatriates seeking foreign education for their children had to send them to international schools in the main city center, Yanbu Al Bahr, or in the Royal Commission.

### Yanbu Al-Sina'iya

Yanbu Al-Sina'iya (literally "the industrial Yanbu") is the industrial city, established in 1975 by royal decree to create a Royal Commission to manage the effort to build a new modern city. It is the southernmost part of Yanbu city. Yanbu Al-Sina'iya is divided into two parts, the industrial area to the south, and a residential area (referred to as the Royal Commission by many locals) directly north and adjacent to the industrial area. The industrial area within Yanbu Al-Sina'iya is the area of all the major refineries and petrochemical installations and is still undergoing major growth. This area of Yanbu was a strip of undeveloped coastal desert land on the Red Sea and has been transformed into an industrial city.

Yanbu Al-Sina'iya's residential section is near the Royal Commission Headquarters. It has amenities such as many international chain and local restaurants, two shopping malls, various shopping centers, supermarkets of various sizes, hospitals, banks, and coffee shops. The [Movenpick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B6venpick_Hotels_&_Resorts) Hotel, Yanbu's newest hotel, is located here, as is The Cove, a gated private residential housing compound built around a large private lagoon. Yanbu Al Sinaiyah is home to [Yanbu Industrial College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yanbu_Industrial_College), created for training and imparting knowledge for the students and workforce of the industry. Yanbu International School was also located here, prior to its closing in 2018.

The majority of Yanbu's Western expatriate population is found living in the Royal Commission. The rest of the residents of Yanbu Al-Sina'iya tend to be upper-middle and upper-class Saudi citizens, upper-class Asians, and upper-class Arab and Middle Eastern residents. The residential area of Yanbu Al-Sina'iya has a more modern, clean, and organized feel to it than the downtown area. It is also greener.

A new waterfront project has been launched in the coastline of Yanbu Industrial City. The total area of this project is 12 km. The project consists of four phases. The first phase is made of a campground equipped with the needed facilities. The second phase is made up of gardens, marina and a number of islands. The third phase has a recreation area, hotel and other facilities. The fourth phase has an aquarium.[^cite_note-9]

## Economy

Yanbu is an important [petroleum](23195.md) shipping terminal and is home to three oil [refineries](339530.md), a plastics facility, and several petrochemical plants. The city hosts two major seaports: the [Yanbu Commercial Port](61452573.md) and the [King Fahd Industrial Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Fahd_Industrial_Port_\(Yanbu\)). These ports make Yanbu the country's second-largest port city after [Jeddah](277718.md) and the principal maritime gateway for the holy city of [Medina](36636.md), located 160 km (99 mi) to the east.

Yanbu's natural harbour is protected on both sides by wide [coral reefs](87410.md), which remain largely untouched and are considered popular diving areas. Three major oil pipelines cross the desert from the oilfields in eastern Saudi Arabia and terminate at the [Red Sea](26191.md) in Yanbu, supporting its role as a major hub for oil export and petrochemical industries.

### Universities and Colleges

Yanbu is home to several institutions of higher education. Among them:

- The [College of Computer Science & Engineering at Yanbu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_Computer_Science_&_Engineering_at_Yanbu) (CCSEY) is affiliated with [Taibah University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taibah_University) and offers undergraduate programs in computer science, engineering, and related fields.

- [Yanbu Industrial College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yanbu_Industrial_College) provides vocational and technical education, including programs in engineering, business, and applied sciences.

- [Yanbu University College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yanbu_University_College) offers a range of bachelor's degree programs in arts, sciences, and professional disciplines.

## Tourism



Traditional old houses in the city of Yanbu

Although Yanbu is primarily known for its industrial activities, it has been developing as a tourist destination in recent years. The city's coastline is used for scuba diving, swimming, and recreational activities along its white sandy beaches.[^cite_note-13] Among its recreational areas is China Herbal Park, a coastal park covering approximately 4 km<sup>2</sup>.

In 2020, Saudi Arabia's [Ministry of Tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Tourism_\(Saudi_Arabia\)) announced plans to renovate the former residence of [T. E. Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T._E._Lawrence) in Yanbu. Lawrence, a British officer, lived in the city during [World War I](4764461.md), and the restoration aims to preserve the site as a cultural and tourist attraction.[^cite_note-14]

## Transportation

### Airport

Yanbu is served by the [Prince Abdulmohsen Bin Abdulaziz International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Abdulmohsen_Bin_Abdulaziz_International_Airport), which was upgraded in 2009. Domestically, it offers flights to [Dammam](479214.md), [Jeddah](277718.md), and [Riyadh](51554.md). International services connect Yanbu with [Alexandria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria_International_Airport_\(Egypt\)) and [Cairo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo_International_Airport) in Egypt, [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul_Airport) in Turkey, [Dubai](154742.md) and [Sharjah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharjah_International_Airport) in the United Arab Emirates, and [Doha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamad_International_Airport) in Qatar.[^cite_note-15]



The new Yanbu Terminal and air traffic control tower (far right)

All international flights are operated by foreign carriers, while domestic services are provided by [Saudia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudia) and [Flynas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flynas).

### Ports

Yanbu has the only two ports in the [Medina Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medina_Province,_Saudi_Arabia): the [Yanbu Commercial Port](61452573.md) and the [King Fahd Industrial Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Fahd_Industrial_Port_\(Yanbu\)). Both are managed by the [Saudi Ports Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Ports_Authority).[^cite_note-16]

Since the [Strait of Hormuz crisis](82550763.md) has begun in March 2026, the Saudi company [Maaden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maaden_\(company\)) aims to resolve the supply chain bottleneck caused by this crisis. It transports goods by rail and truck to Yanbu to ship them from there.[^cite_note-17]
