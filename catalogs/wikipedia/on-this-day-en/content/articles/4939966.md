**Guéréda** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): غيريدا, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Ḡīrīdā*) is a town in the [Wadi Fira Region](2668871.md), [Chad](5488.md). It is located at around [14°30′30″N 22°5′13″E  /  14.50833°N 22.08694°E  / 14.50833; 22.08694](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Gu%C3%A9r%C3%A9da\&params=14_30_30_N_22_5_13_E_region\:TD_type\:city).

About 60% of Guéréda residents are [Tama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tama_people) but people also come from up to 60 km around.

Guéréda was the site of fighting between the [Chadian army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chadian_army) and the [Rally of Democratic Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rally_of_Forces_for_Change) (RaFD) in early December 2006. On December 1, eleven people were killed and 82 injured in the fighting. On December 13, the [FUC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Front_for_Democratic_Change) took control of the streets of Guéréda, ending almost twenty years of control by the [Zaghawa](1467480.md) minority. Power dynamics were reversed and FUC rebels organized mass interrogations of civilians who were questioned about their ethnic origins. Zaghawa civilians accused of anti-[Tama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tama_people) sentiments were arrested.

Seventeen expatriate humanitarian staff are located in Guéréda near the large [refugee camps](438046.md) at [Mile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mile,_Chad?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Kounoungo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kounoungo?action=edit\&redlink=1), where 30,000 [Sudanese](27421.md) refugees from the [Darfur conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darfur_conflict) are located.

The town is served by [Guéréda Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gu%C3%A9r%C3%A9da_Airport).
