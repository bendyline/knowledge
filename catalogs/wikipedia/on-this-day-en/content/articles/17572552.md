The **Province of Santa Elena** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˌsanta eˈlena\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a [province](576127.md) of Ecuador in the coastal region. Created in 2007 from territory that belonged to the [Guayas Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guayas_Province,_Ecuador), it is one of the two newest provinces of Ecuador, along with [Santo Domingo de Los Tsáchilas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santo_Domingo_de_Los_Ts%C3%A1chilas_Province,_Ecuador). Its capital city is [Santa Elena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Elena,_Ecuador), from which the province derives its name.[^cite_note-3]
