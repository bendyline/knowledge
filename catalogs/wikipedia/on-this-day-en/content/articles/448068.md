**Ilam Province** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): استان ایلام)[^cite_note-15] is one of the 31 [provinces of Iran](448160.md). Its capital is the city of [Ilam](482372.md).[^cite_note-Ilam_Province_Structure-16]

The province is in the western part of the country in [Region 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Iran) and covers 20,164.11 km<sup>2</sup> (7,785.41 mi<sup>2</sup>). It shares 425 km (264 mi) of the [border with Iraq](10282111.md), and also borders on the provinces of [Kermanshah](448078.md), [Lorestan](433072.md), and [Khuzestan](448079.md).

## Etymology

The name "Ilam" comes from "[Elam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elam)", the prehistoric civilisation that ruled in south western Iran and south eastern Iraq from 2700 BC to 539 BC.

### Pre-history

The name "Ilam" comes from "[Elam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elam)", the pre-historic civilization that ruled the area in modern southwest Iran and southeast Iraq from 2700 BC to 539 BC. Archaeological findings date human settlement of the area to around 5000 BC.

[Ashurbanipal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashurbanipal), the then king of [Assyria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assyria), invaded Elam in 639 BC and totally destroyed it. Although not as united as before, the Elamites survived and continued to live in the area after the invasion. Between 612 and 546 BC, Elam was incorporated into the [Median kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Median_kingdom) and later into the [Achaemenid Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaemenid_Empire) in 539 BC. During the Achaemenid era, Elam lost its independence forever and became the third province of the empire after [Persis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persis) and [Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_\(region\)).[^cite_note-17]

For a full history on Elam civilization, see [Elam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elam).

During the [Median](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Median_kingdom), [Achaemenid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaemenid_Empire), [Seleucid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seleucid_Empire), and [Parthian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parthian_Empire) eras the area and its settlements took on importance due to its proximity to the districts of Hamadan, [Kurdistan](448112.md), Kermanshah, Lorestan and, most notably, [Susa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susa).

During the [Sasanian era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasanian_Empire), present-day Ilam was composed of two states: [Mehrjanqazaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehrjankadak) in the east, which was the modern day [Darreh Shahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darreh_Shahr) and Masbasan in the west.[^cite_note-18]

### Arab reign



[Buyids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buyid_dynasty) rule over western Iran

After the [Muslim conquest of Mesopotamia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim_conquest_of_Mesopotamia) in 640 AD, the Arabs named the area the "Land of the Mountains," or "[Jibal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jibal)". During the Arabs' rule, the kingdom of Jibal, whose capital was [Seymareh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darreh_Shahr_Ancient_City), was one of the territories under the control of Baghdad and Basra, and lasted until 961 CE.

The [Rashidun Caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashidun_Caliphate) conquered [Mesopotamia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesopotamia) by 640 CE and later the whole of the [Sasanian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasanian_Empire) by 643 CE and kept control until their collapse in 661 CE. Prior to their collapse, civil war had taken over the caliphate, after which the [Muawiyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muawiyah_I) faction won and thus established the [Umayyad Caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umayyad_Caliphate). The Umayyads controlled the area until their own collapse in 750 AD, after which the [Abbasid Caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbasid_Caliphate) took over and reigned for around two hundred years until 945 CE. After conquering western Iran in 945 CE, the [Buyid Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buyid_dynasty) ruled over this area until 1055.

### Kurdish reign

For 270 years, between 1155 and 1424, the Kurds reigned in the [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains), especially areas around [Lorestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorestan_Province). [Hazaraspids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazaraspids) who are also known as Lorestan [Atabegs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atabeg) were composed of two groups, the [Little Lor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khorshidi_dynasty), who were mostly of Lor descent and Big Lor Atabegs who were of Kurdish descent. The former controlled parts of the present-day provinces of [Markazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markazi_Province), [Hamedan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamadan_Province), [Kermanshah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kermanshah_Province), [Lorestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorestan_Province), [Khuzestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan_Province) and Ilam.[^cite_note-19]

From 961 to 1015, the [Ḥasanwayhid dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasanwayhids) ruled over western Iran under the influence of the [Buyid Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buyid_dynasty). Centred at [Dinawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinavar), near present-day [Kermanshah](1489244.md), Hasanwayhids, Kurdish Muslims with ties to the Kurdish Barzikani tribe, controlled central areas of the [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains). At its greatest extent, the dynasty included the modern-day areas around [Khorramabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khorramabad), [Borujerd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borujerd), [Nahavand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahavand), [Asadabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asadabad,_Iran), [Borujerd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borujerd), [Ahwaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahwaz), [Ilam](482372.md), [Kermanshah](1489244.md), [Hulwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulwan,_Iran) and [Kirkuk](199292.md). The [Ḥasanwayhid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasanwayhids) dynasty was overthrown by the [Annazids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annazids), who, with help of the [Buyid dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buyid_dynasty), controlled the area until their collapse in 1116 CE.[^cite_note-20]

### Khorshidi dynasty



Little and Big Lorestan



[Vali Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vali_Castle?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Ilam

The capital of Little Lor was Shapourkhwast or the modern-day [Khorramabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khorramabad), especially the [Falak-ol-Aflak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falak-ol-Aflak) Castle. The Little Lor Atabegs or the [Khorshidi dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khorshidi_dynasty) ruled over the area for over 412 years, from 1184 to 1597 CE, after which [Shah Abbas the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_the_Great) executed the last ruler, Shahverdi Khan, and replaced Shahverdi's nephew, Hossein Beyg/Khan, thus ending the rule of Little Lor and beginning of the Vali/Wali dynasty also known as [Feyli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feyli_\(tribe\)) Vali.[^cite_note-21]

### Qajar era (1789–1925)

During the [Qajar era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qajar_Iran), Lorestan was divided into two regions: [Poshtkoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poshtkouh?action=edit\&redlink=1) (present-day Ilam province) with the center of Ilam city and Pishkouh (present-day Lorestan) with the center of [Khorramabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khorramabad). Poshtkhouh literally means “back of mountain” and it was due to the fact that Ilam was situated at the westernmost parts of the [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains) and to the rest of the country it was plainly considered at the back of mountains.[^cite_note-\:1-22]

From that point on, Ilam was ruled independently from Lorestan, and the governor reported directly to the central government, thereby ending Lorestan control of Ilam and Poshtkouh. The two districts were later renamed "Deh-e Bala" and "Deh-e Pa'een", meaning the upper and lower villages respectively.[^cite_note-\:1-22][^cite_note-\:2-23] During the rule of Hossein Qoli Khan over "Deh-e Bala" (1863–1900 CE), it was renamed to "Hossein-Abad Poshtkuh".

The Vali Castle of Ilam was built by the next Vali, Gholamreza Khan Feyli, in 1908. The castle is now a nationally registered [heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_National_Heritage_List) and also the Ilam Museum of Anthropology.

### 20th century

Following the establishment of the [Pahlavi dynasty](338883.md) in 1925, [Reza Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reza_Shah) suppressed the local governments to consolidate his dominance. In 1928, the government forces took over Poshtkouh and [Gholamreza Khan Feyli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gholamreza_Khan_Fili?action=edit\&redlink=1), the last Feyli governor left Poshtkouh for Iraq without any resistance, thus abolishing the rule of the Feylis in Ilam after 332 years.[^cite_note-\:2-23] A year later, in 1929, "Hossein-Abad" was renamed to "Ilam" by the suggestion of [Academy of Persian Language and Literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Persian_Language_and_Literature).[^cite_note-\:8-24]

On 7 November 1937, the parliament passed a law on the political division of the country under the title of "Act of Division of the Country and the Duties of the Governors", thereby dividing the country into 6 provinces and 50 counties.[^cite_note-25] Poshtkouh, being a county, was part of the "West Province".

The 1937 political division law did not last long and on 9 January 1938, an amendment was passed which divided the country into 10 province and 49 counties. Based on the new division, "Ilam" was a county of the "Fifth Province".

In 1964, [Darreh Shahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darreh_Shahr_County), [Abdanan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdanan_County) and [Dehloran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehloran_County) from [Lorestan province](433072.md) and [Mousian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musiyan,_Ilam) from [Khuzestan](448079.md) were annexed to Ilam, thus upping its status to "general governorate" as part of the Kermanshah Province.[^cite_note-\:8-24] Ilam subsequently became a province in March 1974.

After the 1979 [Iranian Revolution](347268.md), Ilam was one of the main battlegrounds of the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War) for years. The war began with the [Iraqi invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_invasion_of_Iran) into western and southwestern Iran on 22 September 1980. As a border province, Ilam was a constant target for the Iraqi bombing campaign and suffered heavily in terms of economic infrastructure. The war left Ilam province devastated for years, making it one of the least developed provinces in Iran.

## Geography



Topographic map of Ilam province indicating mountains and rivers.

Ilam province, with an area of 20,150 km<sup>2</sup> (7,780 mi<sup>2</sup>), is the [22nd largest province in Iran](448160.md). It is located in the southwest of the country, in Central [Zagros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains), and is bordered by [Iraq](7515928.md) to the west, [Khuzestan province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan_Province) to the south, [Lorestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorestan_Province) province to the east, and [Kermanshah province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kermanshah_Province) to the north.

Ilam province is situated at the southwestern edge of the [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains) and right at the transition between the [Arabian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabian_Plate) and the [Iranian plateaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Plateau). Because of this, it is divided into two distinct natural areas; The northern and eastern parts are mountainous, whereas the southwest is covered with low plains that extend to the Iraqi and Khuzestan borders.

The mountains in the north and east are mostly parallel, running in the northwest–southeast direction. These mountains are separated by plains and rolling hills that are mostly used to grow crops and orchards by settlers.

The most prominent mountain in Ilam province is [Kabir Kouh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabir_Kouh), which is located in the eastern part of the province, stretching 160 km from near [Pol-e Zal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pol-e_Zal) in the southeast to [Mishkhas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mishkhas_Rural_District) village near [Ilam city](482372.md) in the northwest. The highest point in the province, [Kan Seifi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kan_Seifi?action=edit\&redlink=1) peak with 2,775 m (9,104 ft) above sea level, is part of Kabir Kouh range.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

Other major mountains include:

- Manesht and Qolarang near [Ilam](482372.md)

- Sharah-Zoul and Bankoul in [Eywan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyvan_County)

- Qalajeh and Ahuran in [Chardavol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chardavol_County)

- Dinar Kouh, in [Abdanan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdanan_County)

- Anaran and Siah Kouh in central [Dehloran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehloran_County)

- Laneh and Charmin near [Lumar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumar)

- Chaman-Ghir and Sivan near [Mishkhas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mish_Khas_Rural_District).

Manesht mountain

Contrary to the north, the south and southwestern part of the province is covered with low-lying plains with elevation varying between 50 and 300 m (160 and 980 ft) above sea level. Despite receiving little precipitation, large areas of these low lands, notably [Dehloran](33014014.md), [Musian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musian) and [Dasht Abbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dasht-e_Abbas), are cultivated owing to a combination of factors such as rivers flowing from the mountains to the east, dams and irrigation networks.

The [Ghamasiab river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghamasiab_river?action=edit\&redlink=1), after running through [Kermanshah province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kermanshah_Province), flows toward Ilam. Upon arriving at the point where the borders of Ilam, [Lorestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorestan_Province) and [Kermanshah](1489244.md) provinces meet, it is named [Seymareh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seymareh_River). It then flows west and southward and somewhere near [Kal Sefid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kal-e_Sefid,_Ilam) Village, [Darreh Shahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darreh_Shahr_County), is joined by the [Kashkan river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashkan_river?action=edit\&redlink=1) and together they create the [Karkheh river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karkheh_River), the third longest river in Iran with a length of around 755 km. Seymareh and Karkheh constitute the Ilam's eastern border with Lorestan province.



Siah-Ghav Twin Lakes, near [Abdanan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdanan)

Because of the terrain, the permanent and seasonal rivers and stream that originate from Ilam highlands either fall into [Seymareh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seymareh_River), such as Chardavol, Garab, Seekan, Darreh Shahr and Majeen rivers; or, flow westward toward the low lands and into Iraq and Khuzestan, such as Ghanghir, Ghodar-Khosh, Kanjan-Cham, Roud Ghavi, Changouleh Meymeh, Murmuri, Doiraj and Siah Ghav rivers.[^cite_note-\:0-30]

Apart from some few springs here and there, such as [Siah-Ghav Twin Lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siah-Ghav_Twin_Lakes?action=edit\&redlink=1) near [Abdanan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdanan), there are no major natural bodies of water in the province. There are, however, four dam that provide important drinking water, these include [Ilam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilam_dam?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Seymareh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seimare_Dam), [Eywan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eywan_dam?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Doiraj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doiraj_dam?action=edit\&redlink=1) dams.[^cite_note-31]

#### Flora

With about 31% of its territory covered with scattered to dense forests of mostly [Persian oak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quercus_brantii), Ilam ranks 3rd in the country after [Kohgiluyeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohgiluyeh_and_Boyer-Ahmad_Province) and [Lorestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorestan_Province) provinces, excluding the northern provinces of [Mazandarn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazandaran_Province) and [Gilan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilan_Province).[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33] The highlands and the forests of the province are the habitat of many animals including the [Asian black bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_black_bear), [Caracal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caracal), [Caucasian squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasian_squirrel), [Eastern imperial eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_imperial_eagle), [Golden eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_eagle), [Black kite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_kite), [Black stork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_stork), [Blunt-nosed viper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macrovipera_lebetinus), and the endangered [Persian leopard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_leopard).[^cite_note-34] Ilam is home to the endangered [Spider-tailed horned viper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-tailed_horned_viper), a unique species only found in Ilam province in the world.[^cite_note-35]

More than 6,400 km<sup>2</sup> (2,500 mi<sup>2</sup>) of the province, around 31% of its territory, is covered with scattered to dense forests. This is about one third of the total forests in the [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains). The predominant species of these forests are [Persian oak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quercus_persica), though other trees such as [wild almond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prunus_scoparia), [hawthorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crataegus), [Persian turpentine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pistacia_atlantica), [Montpellier maple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acer_monspessulanum) and [Judas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cercis_siliquastrum) can be found.

The Arghavan Canyon near Ilam is known for the pink blooms of the [Judas-trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cercis_siliquastrum), which flower in Spring.

#### Fauna



Spider-tailed horned viper

Due to its geographical location, natural conditions and the diversity of its habitats, Ilam province is home to 32 mammals and 183 birds. The mountainous areas, specifically, host mammals such as:

- [wild goat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_goat), [Asiatic mouflon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mouflon), [Argali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argali), [goitered gazelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goitered_gazelle)

- the endangered [Persian leopard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_leopard) and [Asiatic cheetah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asiatic_cheetah)

- as well as [Caracal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caracal), [Eurasian lynx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasian_lynx), [Asian black bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_black_bear), [Brown bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_bear), [wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf), [Golden jackal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_jackal), [Red fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_fox) and [wild boar](52255.md)

- smaller species such as [Caucasian squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasian_squirrel), [rabbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbit), [Long-eared hedgehog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-eared_hedgehog), [European hedgehog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_hedgehog), [Indian crested porcupine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_crested_porcupine) and [weasels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weasel).

### Landslides



[Seymareh Landslide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seymareh_Landslide?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Darreh Shahr County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darreh_Shahr_County)

One of the largest landslides in the eastern hemisphere and world is believed to have occurred in Ilam Province, more specifically, in the *Kabir Kuh* [anticline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anticline) at [Gorz-e Langar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorz-e_Langar) in Darreh Shahr county. ### Climate



[Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) climate types of Ilam province

Due to the influence of various factors such as altitude, proximity to the [Arabian desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabian_Desert), the plains of [Khuzestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan_Province) and [Iraq](7515928.md), the climate of the province is very diverse.

Different atmospheric systems affect the province, causing fall, winter, spring, and occasionally summer precipitation. The Mediterranean and Black Sea air masses from the west, trigger autumn and winter rains in the province.[^cite_note-\:3-42]

The Red Sea and Saudi desert currents, too, bring winter and spring rains. The very same currents and the vast Iraqi plain to the west are the main causes of hot weather in summers. On the other hand, the northern air masses entering the country in winter from Siberia lower the temperatures and cause freezing in northern parts of the province.

Ilam province is considered as one of the tropical regions of the country, but due to difference in altitudes, temperature and precipitation, three different types of climate can be observed:[^cite_note-\:0-30][^cite_note-\:3-42]

- Mountainous areas of the north and northeast, which have cold climate and long winters. The minimum temperature in winter reaches −15 to −20 °C (5 to −4 °F) and the amount of precipitation exceeds 600 mm (24 in) per year.

- The plains of the west and southwest of the province have dry hot climate. The maximum temperature in these areas can exceed 52 °C (126 °F). The rainfall in these areas is about 200 mm (7.9 in) per year.

- Other areas have temperature to warm climate with hot summers and mild winters. Temperatures can go as low as −15 °C (5 °F) in higher altitudes and in summers it can go above 45 °C (113 °F) in lower altitudes. Annual precipitation in these areas varies between 200 mm (7.9 in) and 450 mm (18 in).

### Floods



Flood of Nov 2015 city of Ilam.



Flood of 31 Mar 2019 at Majeen, [Darreh Shahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darreh_Shahr_County)

Occasional heavy rainstorms in spring and autumn bring, sometimes, huge floods. The steep slopes of [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains), soil type, lack of vegetation are among some of the reasons why Ilam province is prone to flooding. Moreover, majority of communities in Ilam province are located in the floodplains, at the foothills of mountains, on river banks and near sources of water, so there is always risk of flooding with potential human and financial costs. According to the available statistics, [Mehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehran_County) and [Dehloran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehloran_County) counties are at the highest risk of flooding due to their lower elevation compared to the rest of the province and the subsequent concentration and joining of rivers and streams.

&#x20;The latest flood happened in March and April 2019, in which most of country, including Ilam province, experienced levels of flooding. On 1 April 2019, the flood destructed many bridges including the Gavmishan bridge in [Darreh Shahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darreh_Shahr_County), an important bridge over [Karkheh River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karkheh_River) which connects south of the province to [Lorestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorestan_Province) and rest of the country. The bridge was reopened months later on 6 October 2019.[^cite_note-43]

Further information: [2019 Iran floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Iran_floods)

### Dust

Ilam is occasionally affected by dust, especially in summers. It has become more frequent in the last ten years. The cities of Mehran and Dehloran are at the highest risk in the province, due to lower elevation and proximity to the deserts of Iraq and Saudi Arabia. However, in case of large dust storms, the western part of the country could be affected. The successive droughts of the recent decade in Iran and the long wars in Iraq and Syria have also exacerbated the issue.[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-\:6-45]

## Demographics

### Language and ethnicity

Ilam Province has a diverse linguistic presence, albeit having the smallest population in Iran. This diversity derives from the geographical location of the province, positioned in the transitional zone between [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_languages) and the [Southwestern Iranian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwestern_Iranian) language bloc. The predominant language is Kurdish, more specifically the [Southern Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Kurdish) dialects Kalhori, Elami, Malekshahi, [Laki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laki_language) and the Khezeli dialect closely related to Laki. Kurdish is the dominant language in the cities of [Ilam](482372.md), [Mehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehran,_Ilam), [Eyvan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyvan), [Abdanan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdanan), [Sarableh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarableh), [Lumar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumar) and [Pahleh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahleh), and [Cheshmeh-ye Shirin Shah Ahmad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheshmeh-ye_Shirin_Shah_Ahmad) is the southern-most Kurdish-speaking settlement in the province.[^cite_note-\:10-11]

[Laki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laki_language) is the predominant language of [Darreh Shahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darreh_Shahr) County. Luri and Kurdish are also spoken in parts of the county.[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47]

[Luri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luri_language) is spoken in the southern parts of the province and is the predominant language in [Murmuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murmuri), [Haft Cheshmeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haft_Cheshmeh,_Abdanan) and many smaller villages. Two linguistically distinctive Luri dialects called Hinimini and Shuhani are also spoken in the province. [Shahrak-e Changuleh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahrak-e_Changuleh) is the largest Shuhani-speaking settlement, while Hinimini is spoken in and around [Zarangush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zarangush).[^cite_note-\:10-11]

[Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) is spoken in smaller settlements near the border to [Khuzestan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan_Province) including in [Shahrak-e Zu ol Faqari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahrak-e_Zu_ol_Faqari).[^cite_note-\:10-11]

## Culture

The original inhabitants of Ilam were the [Guitians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gutian_people) and the [Kassites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kassites), and with the arrival of the [Aryans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aryan) in Iran, the [Medes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medes) settled in the Zagros region. Throughout history, many people have settled and ruled in the region and as the result, today, Ilam is inhabited by [Kurds](17068.md), [Lurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lurs), and [Arabs](2185.md). For the same reasons, the culture of the Ilamians is diverse, mostly a blend of many cultures.[^cite_note-\:6-45]

### Handicrafts



[Kilim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilim), a handicraft popular in Ilam

[Embossed kilim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilim) is a combination of simple [kilim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilim) and carpet knot in its text, which is considered as the most important and original handicraft in Ilam province.[^cite_note-\:9-64] Ilam's embossed [kilim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilim) is different from other parts of the country. Upon its creation by Sahar Chalanghar, a resident of [Zanjireh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanjireh-ye_Olya) village of [Sarableh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarableh) county, Ilam's embossed kilim became popular both in Iran and the world. Now, there are more than 4,500 weavers of this specific type of kilim in Ilam province, producing around 3,000m<sup>2</sup> annually. It is estimated that around 50% of those involved in all handicrafts work in Ilam province are in the field of kilim production.

"Ilam's embossed kilim" is different from other parts of the country because a weaver named "Sahar Chalangar", a resident of Zanjireh village (one of the functions of Sarablah city of Ilam province), succeeds in performing a prominent role in a part of his woven kilim by using a carpet knot. From this, a background is provided for the growth and promotion of embossed kilims. Ilam's embossed kilim has received a national hologram and has been registered as a souvenir and brand of Ilam province. [Ilam](482372.md) was subsequently appointed as the national capital of embossed kilim.[^cite_note-\:9-64][^cite_note-65][^cite_note-66]

## Government

The highest government authority of the province is the governor-general, who is appointed by the [Minister of Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_\(Iran\)) given cabinet approval. The governor-general, subsequently appoints county governors. ## Economy

With about 0.99% contribution to the national GDP as of 2016, Ilam is one of the least developed provinces in Iran, ranked 26th out of 31. Most economic activities in the province are focused on agriculture and animal husbandry in the rural communities; and, skilled trades, public and private services in urban areas. The industry has a small share in the economy of the province and there are few factories.

### Agriculture



Production of crops and fruits in Ilam province as of 2018.

&#x20; Crops: 700,732 tons (97.8%)

&#x20; Fruits: 15,409 tons (2.2%)

The largest sector of Ilam's economy is agriculture, though much smaller compared to other provinces of Iran. There are more than 55,000 farmers in Ilam province. It is estimated that, about 50% of all households in the province are either directly or indirectly involved in agricultural activities.[^cite_note-\:02-69]

In the 2018 harvest year, 223,228 [hectares](24919513.md) (551,610 [acres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre)) of land (11.1% of the territory of Ilam province) were cultivated for various crops such as [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), [forage maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [watermelon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watermelon), [melon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melon) and [cucumber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cucumber), producing 700,732 tons of produce in total, 14% through [dry farming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dryland_farming). Ilam is the bottom 3rd province in the country in terms of crop yield at 3.14 tons per hectare, only surpassing [Kohgiluyeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohgiluyeh_and_Boyer-Ahmad_Province) and [Kurdestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdistan). For comparison, the average yield is 9.88 tons per hectare and highest is 25.64 tons per hectare in [Alborz province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alborz_Province).[^cite_note-Ag_YB97_V1-70]

The farming lands of Ilam, like its geography, are divided; The northern and eastern parts of the province are mountainous and despite the presence of water resources, cultivable lands are limited to inter-mountain plains, foothills and river banks; On the contrary, the hot climate plains of the south and southwest such as Hojandan-Dasht, Amir-Abad, Mehran, Mosian, Dehloran and Dasht-Abbas, are large in size but limited in water resources.[^cite_note-71]

As of 2019, the total area of all orchards in Ilam province was 3,950 [ha](24919513.md) (9,800 [acres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre)), making it the last in the country. In total, these orchards produced 15,409 tons of fruits, or 0.1% of the country's production. The most grown horticultural products are: [grapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grape), [walnut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut), [pomegranate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomegranate), [peach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peach), [olive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive) and [fig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_fig).[^cite_note-Ag_YB97_V3-72]

As a comparison, the number of the same type of workshops is 6,806 for [Tehran](202698.md) and 3,892 for [Isfahan](448061.md) provinces, indicating how underdeveloped Ilam's industry sector is. According to the latest statistics, Ilam is the least industrial province in Iran.[^cite_note-74] In recent years, some efforts went into stimulating Ilam's industry by building industrial towns and areas, providing encouraging opportunities for businesses such as cheaper land, utilities and tax. Since 2005, 8 such towns and 5 areas have been built.[^cite_note-75]

### Energy

With an estimated 14 trillion m<sup>3</sup> of gas reserves and 17 billion [barrels](594043.md) of oil reserve, equal to 6% and 11% of Iran's reserves respectively, Ilam ranks 2nd and 3rd in the country in terms of gas and oil. Ilam province produces more than 230,000 [barrels](594043.md) of oil per day and 1.5 billion cubic meters of gas per year, accounting to about 90% of western Iran's oil and gas, making Ilam the energy hub of western Iran. The most important oil and gas fields in Ilam are: Dehloran, Tang-e Bijar, Chashmeh Khosh, Paydar-e Gharb, Azar and Danan. ### Mining

Ilam province is part of the Zagros [sedimentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedimentary_rock) structure and is completely covered with sedimentary rocks of different ages. In other words, there are no phenomena of [magmatism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magmatism) and [metamorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metamorphism) in the province. Therefore, the province's metal mineral reserves are negligible. There are, however, substantial non-metallic reserves of [gypsum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gypsum), [limestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone), sandstone, [dolomite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolomite_\(mineral\)), shale, [bitumen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitumen), [celestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestine_\(mineral\)), building stone and facade stone.[^cite_note-\:03-79] The province's gypsum is more than 99 percent pure and can be found in colossal amounts in Dehloran, Mehran and Bouli of Abdanan. [Dehloran Gypsum Factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehloran_Gypsum_Factory?action=edit\&redlink=1) produces a premium quality product in the country.

The limestone reserves are also very significant in size and very high in purity (93–99.9%). [Ilam Cement Factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilam_Cement_Factory?action=edit\&redlink=1), founded 1989 to use these mines, produces one of the finest cements in Iran and has won many national and international awards.[^cite_note-80] There are also large amounts of [celestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestine_\(mineral\)) discovered northwest of Dehloran. [Celestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestine_\(mineral\)) is used in oil well drilling industry.

The most valuable mineral in the province is bitumen. It is found as [gilsonite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilsonite) and has a purity of more than 70%. Bitumen is found in [Eyvan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyvan_County), [Dehloran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehloran_County) and [Malekshahi](24131946.md) counties.[^cite_note-\:03-79] Ilam province is the 2nd province in the country in terms of production and reserves of bitumen. [Eyvan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyvan_County) has the largest reserves of bitumen in the province and is considered the largest bitumen mine in the Middle East. Ilam's bitumen reserves are estimated at 465,000 tons proven and likely up to 900,000 tons.[^cite_note-81] In some places in the province, liquid bitumen seeps to the ground and creates springs. "Dehloran Bitumen Spring" is a nationally registered natural monument in Iran.[^cite_note-82]

Salt stone has been recovered in Baloutestan area of Zarin Abad. There's also veins of blue salt excavated near Dashtalak (Mimak) and Majeen (Darreh Shahr).

Given the geology of the province, building stones can also found in large amounts; White and black marble stone have been discovered near the city of Ilam.

About 2.2 million tons of mineral were excavated in 2019 and Ilam's reserves are estimated at around 1 billion tons.[^cite_note-83]

## Education

The provincial department of education, in line with the [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_\(Iran\)), supervises elementary and secondary education in the province. Higher education is supervised by the [Ministry of Science, Research and Technology (Iran)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Science,_Research_and_Technology_\(Iran\)). As of 2019, there were 1778 schools and 112,503 students and 26,804 higher education students. ## Healthcare

As of 2018, Ilam has one of the highest ratios of number of general practitioners to population in the country, ranking 2nd. There are 3.48 [GPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_practitioner) per 10,000 population in Ilam province, the average of the country being 2.37. Ilam also has the highest ratio of dentists in the country.[^cite_note-\:5-63]



Distribution of medical practitioners in Ilam province

Despite the positively looking statistics when compared with other provinces, the medical facilities and expertise are unequally distributed in Ilam province; [Ilam](482372.md), the capital, serves as the medical hub in the province, hosting majority of hospitals, medical laboratories and specialist: Six of the 12 hospitals in the province; 23 of the 60 medical laboratories; 18 out of the 19 medical nuclear institutes; 123 out of the 189 specialists; all the post-specialist and professional doctorates; 13 out of 14 pharmacologists; and, the only medical university in the province are in Ilam.[^cite_note-\:5-63][^cite_note-84]

The most prominent hospitals of the province are:[^cite_note-85]

## Transportation

### Air

Compared to the rest of the country, Ilam is under-developed in term of transportation. The [Ilam Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilam_Airport) is the only airport in the province. With 2850 sorties, 215,799 passengers and around 1,416 [metric tons](31185.md) (1,561 [short tons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_ton)), [Ilam Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilam_Airport) ranked 21st in the country and 2nd to Kermanshah Airport in the western region.[^cite_note-86] Built in 1996, Ilam Airport has regular flights to Tehran and Mashhad. In 2016, the airport was upgraded to international status ([controlled airspace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controlled_airspace)) and started a weekly service to [Najaf](200512.md).[^cite_note-87]

As of 2019, there are only 179 km (111 mi) of highway in Ilam province:

-  [Road 17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_17_\(Iran\)): Connecting [Eslamabad-e Gharb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eslamabad-e_Gharb) to [Mehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehran,_Ilam) through [Eyvan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyvan) and [Ilam](482372.md).

-  [Road 19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_19_\(Iran\)): Connecting [Eslamabad-e Gharb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eslamabad-e_Gharb) to [Road 37](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_37_\(Iran\)) near [Pol-e Dokhtar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pol-e_Dokhtar) in [Lorestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorestan_Province) with a small section of it passing through the northeastern bulge of Ilam province, the [Chardavol County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chardavol_County).

-  [Road 21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_21_\(Iran\)): Connecting [Ilam](482372.md) to [Road 19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_19_\(Iran\)) at Seymareh Bridge and then to [Kermanshah](1489244.md).

-  [Road 64](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_64_\(Iran\)): Connecting the border city of [Mehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehran,_Ilam) to [Dehloran](33014014.md) and to [Road 37](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_37_\(Iran\)) near [Andimeshk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andimeshk) in [Khuzestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan_Province).
