The following is the list of the **[Mayors](101146.md) of the [Royal Capital City of Kraków](16815.md)**, Poland; heads of local municipal government ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *Prezydent Stołecznego Królewskiego Miasta Krakowa*) according to extant records. Most have been elected by inhabitants, with notable exceptions, such as mayors mandated by foreign authorities during various types of militarised control of the city such as [Partitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partitions_of_Poland) or the Nazi German [occupation of Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Poland).
