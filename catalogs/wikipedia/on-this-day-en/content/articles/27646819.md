**Thabazimbi Municipality** ([Tswana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tswana_language): *Mmasepala wa Thabazimbi*; [Afrikaans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrikaans_language): *Thabazimbi Munisipaliteit*; [Tsonga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsonga_language): *Masipala was Thabazimbi*) is a [local municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_municipality_\(South_Africa\)) within the [Waterberg District Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterberg_District_Municipality), in the [Limpopo](365944.md) province of [South Africa](17416221.md). The seat is [Thabazimbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thabazimbi).

*Thabazimbi* is a [Sotho–Nguni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Bantu_languages) name meaning "iron ore mountain." The early [Bantu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bantu_peoples_of_South_Africa) communities ([Sotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sotho_people), [Venda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venda_people), [Kalanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalanga_people), [Nguni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguni_people) and [Nyasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyasa_languages)) worked on this mountain to mine iron.[^cite_note-5]

## Politics

Main article: [Thabazimbi Local Municipality elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thabazimbi_Local_Municipality_elections)

The municipal council consists of twenty-three members elected by [mixed-member proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed-member_proportional_representation). Twelve councillors are elected by [first-past-the-post voting](261709.md) in twelve [wards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_\(South_Africa\)), while the remaining eleven are chosen from [party lists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_list) so that the total number of party representatives is proportional to the number of votes received. In the [election of 1 November 2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_South_African_municipal_elections), the [African National Congress](2503.md) (ANC) won a plurality of 11 seats.
