The **Center for Countering Digital Hate** (**CCDH**), formerly **Brixton Endeavors**, is a British-American not-for-profit NGO[^cite_note-About_Us-3] company[^cite_note-companies-1] with offices in [London](17867.md) and [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) with the stated purpose of stopping the spread of [online hate speech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_hate_speech) and [disinformation](142690.md).[^cite_note-About_Us-3][^cite_note-4] It was started by Dr. Siobhan Marie McAndrew, Kirsty Jean McNeill, [Morgan James McSweeney](75790152.md) and David Craig Roberts,[^cite_note-5] and it campaigns to [deplatform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deplatforming) people that it believes [promote hate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hate_speech) or [misinformation](2203174.md), and campaigns to restrict media organisations such as *[The Daily Wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Wire)* from advertising.[^cite_note-Vaughan_2023-6] CCDH is a member of the [Stop Hate For Profit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stop_Hate_For_Profit) coalition.[^cite_note-7]

According to public records, the organisation was [incorporated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incorporation_\(business\)) in 2018 in London as Brixton Endeavours Limited. It changed its name to Center for Countering Digital Hate in August 2019.[^cite_note-companies-1] In 2021, its US office was registered as a [nonprofit organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/501\(c\)\(3\)) in the United States.[^cite_note-8] CCDH's current CEO is [Imran Ahmed](76552330.md).[^cite_note-About_Us-3]

## Activities

The CCDH has targeted social media platforms for what it says are insufficient efforts on their part to fight [neo-Nazis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Nazis)[^cite_note-Center_for_Countering_Digital_Hate-9] and [anti-vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-vaccine) advocates.[^cite_note-10] The CCDH wrote an article asking Google to stop running advertisements for what the CCDH called "racist disinformation and conspiracies" relating to [George Soros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Soros), claims that the U.S. [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health) funded the [Wuhan Institute of Virology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuhan_Institute_of_Virology), and the veracity of climate change claims. Advertisements from American news site *[The Daily Wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Wire)* and other sources were targeted by CCDH in a 2021 campaign.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

### Campaigns

#### Galloway and Hopkins

Further information: [Twitter suspensions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter_suspensions)

In January 2020, the CCDH campaigned against [Katie Hopkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Hopkins), a [far-right](221220.md) political commentator, and [George Galloway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Galloway), a veteran politician and broadcaster. TV presenter [Rachel Riley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Riley) and the CCDH directly lobbied "[Big Tech](57174306.md)" companies to have both journalists removed from major social media platforms. According to media reports, Riley and Imran Ahmed had a "secret meeting" with [Twitter](9988187.md)'s London based staff in January 2020, demanding the removal of Hopkins and Galloway from their platform. CCDH's attempt to remove Galloway from Twitter failed, but Hopkins had her account suspended for a week in February 2020.[^cite_note-khhp-14]

#### David Icke

In April 2020 the CCDH launched a campaign against the British conspiracy theorist [David Icke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Icke), who gained increased media attention during the [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronavirus_disease_2019)-associated [lockdown in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_United_Kingdom).[^cite_note-icke-15] In November 2020, Twitter removed Icke's account for violating the site's rules against spreading misinformation about the COVID-19 pandemic.[^cite_note-BBC-DavidIcke-Twitter-16]

#### *Stop Funding Misinformation*



"Stop Funding Misinformation" campaign logo

Originally called *Stop Funding Fake News*, the campaign asks advertisers to stop placing ads on websites it argues are spreading misinformation ("[fake news](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fake_news)").[^cite_note-\:0-17] It was inspired by the US success of [Sleeping Giants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Giants) which had convinced several advertisers not to advertise on the *[Breitbart News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breitbart_News)* website.[^cite_note-\:0-17] [Ted Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Baker), [Adobe Inc.](1955.md), [Chelsea FC](7473.md), [eBay](130495.md) and [Manchester United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United) were among the 40 brands and charities that the campaign had persuaded to stop advertising on what it called fake news sites.[^cite_note-McCarthy_2019-18][^cite_note-\:2-19] It has been accused of being set up for political purposes by political staffers.[^cite_note-20]

In March 2019, charity [Macmillan Cancer Support](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macmillan_Cancer_Support) removed an advertisement from *[The Canary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Canary_\(website\))* website after complaints from the campaign and from others.[^cite_note-21] The campaign accused *The Canary* of promoting [conspiracy theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conspiracy_theory), defending [antisemitism](1078.md), and publishing fake news.[^cite_note-politics.co.uk_2019-22] *The Canary* said changes to Google and Facebook's algorithms and the Stop Funding Fake News campaign led to *The Canary* downsizing its operations; it said that it was "against the actions of a state, not against Jewish people as an ethnic group" and that it had been "smeared with accusations of anti-Semitism by those who've [weaponised the term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weaponisation_of_anti-semitism) for political ends".[^cite_note-\:2-19][^cite_note-Tobitt_2019-23][^cite_note-24] Labour Party MP [Chris Williamson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Williamson_\(politician\)) described the campaign against *The Canary* as "sinister".[^cite_note-25]

#### Google advertisements for media organisations

In 2023, the CCDH has campaigned to restrict the [reach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reach_\(advertising\)) of the following media organisations by targeting their use of the Google advertising platform: *[Breitbart News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breitbart_News)*, [CNSNews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNSNews), [Hot Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Air), [Newsbusters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsbusters), [Newsmax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsmax), [MRCTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRCTV), [RedState](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RedState), [Twitchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitchy), *[The Daily Wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Wire)*, and [Zero Hedge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_Hedge).[^cite_note-Vaughan_2023-6]

The CCDH notified [Google](1092923.md) that the Zero Hedge website had published what it called "racist articles" about the [Black Lives Matter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Lives_Matter) protests. As a result, in June 2020, Google found that reader comments on Zero Hedge breached its policies and banned Zero Hedge from its advertising platform.[^cite_note-nbc170620-26]

#### Climate change denial

In November 2021, a report by the CCDH identified "ten fringe publishers" that together were responsible for nearly 70 percent of Facebook user interactions with content that denied climate change. Facebook said the percentage was overstated and called the study misleading.[^cite_note-Porterfield_11/2/2021-27][^cite_note-Toxic_Ten_11/2/2021-28] The "Toxic Ten" publishers were: *[Breitbart News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breitbart_News)*, *[The Western Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Western_Journal)*, [Newsmax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsmax), [Townhall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townhall), [Media Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_Research_Center), *[The Washington Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Times)*, *The Federalist Papers*, *[The Daily Wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Wire)*, [RT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RT_\(TV_network\)), and *The Patriot Post*.[^cite_note-Toxic_Ten_11/2/2021-28]

#### Vaccine disinformation

In a 2021 report, the CCDH identified what it called the top 12 spreaders of [anti-vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-vaccine) disinformation on social media platforms during the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-Dozen_3/2/2021-29][^cite_note-Jarry_3/31/2021-30][^cite_note-31] Labeled as ***The Disinformation Dozen***, they were: [Joseph Mercola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Mercola), [Robert F. Kennedy Jr.](522298.md), [Ty and Charlene Bollinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Bollinger), [Sherri Tenpenny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherri_Tenpenny), [Rizza Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rizza_Islam), [Rashid Buttar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashid_Buttar), [Erin Elizabeth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erin_Elizabeth), [Sayer Ji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayer_Ji), [Kelly Brogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Brogan), [Christiane Northrup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christiane_Northrup), Ben Tapper,[^cite_note-Catalini_Smith_Bauer_10/8/2021-32] and [Kevin Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Jenkins_\(conspiracy_theorist\)). [Meta](62420226.md), formerly Facebook, rejoined that the CCDH did not clearly explain how they surveyed the posts on the site,[^cite_note-33] countering that posts from the twelve named profiles were responsible for only about 0.05% of all views of vaccine-related content on Facebook. Nevertheless, Meta confirmed that they had removed or otherwise penalized vaccine misinformation content associated with each of the twelve accounts.[^cite_note-Bickert_8/18/2021-34]

#### TikTok

In December 2022, the CCDH claimed that the social media platform [TikTok](56822861.md) promoted self-harm and dieting content to users.[^cite_note-35]

#### Twitter/X Corp.

In June 2023, the CCDH stated that after [Elon Musk's acquisition of Twitter](70614210.md), the site "fails to act on 99% of hate posted by Twitter Blue subscribers".[^cite_note-36] Twitter's successor company, [X Corp.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_Corp.), responded to this by filing a lawsuit against the CCDH on 31 July 2023, saying that they "falsely claim it had statistical support showing the platform is overwhelmed with harmful content."[^cite_note-37] On 25 March 2024 District Judge [Charles Breyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Breyer) dismissed the case, writing that "This case is about punishing the Defendants for their speech."[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-Duffy_Fung_20240327-39][^cite_note-40] In April 2024, X Corp. appealed the ruling to the [United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Court_of_Appeals_for_the_Ninth_Circuit).[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42]

## History

### Role in the election of Keir Starmer

Paul Holden, in his book *The Fraud,*[^cite_note-43] stated that CCDH, and its sister organisation Stop Funding Fake News (SFFN, now hosted by CCDH), were incubated using resources (including late-declared donations totalling £740,000)[^cite_note-Channel4FactCheck2025-44] from the organisation [Labour Together](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Together). He stated that Labour Together was a means of organising to replace Jeremy Corbyn as leader of the [UK Labour Party](19279158.md) with a politician to his right. Labour Together's Director [Morgan McSweeney](75790152.md) went on to lead Starmer's election campaign to succeed Corbyn as Labour leader and became Starmer's Chief of Staff once he was elected as UK Prime Minister. CCDH and SFFN represent themselves as non-partisan campaigns. They have targeted some right-wing media outlets but they focused on UK left wing media outlets that were supportive of Corbyn, particularly [The Canary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Canary_\(website\)).[^cite_note-45]

### US visa sanction on CEO

In December 2025, the [US State Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_State_Department) imposed a visa sanction on CCDH CEO Ahmed for leading what US Secretary of State [Marco Rubio](5502549.md) called "organized efforts to coerce American platforms to censor, demonetize, and suppress American viewpoints they oppose".[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47] Ahmed subsequently filed an injunction order against U.S. authorities which was granted by Judge [Vernon S. Broderick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vernon_S._Broderick) from the [Southern District of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_District_of_New_York), blocking U.S. officials from "arresting, detaining or transferring" him.[^cite_note-48]

## Funding

In 2020, the BBC noted some of CCDH's funders as the [Pears Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pears_Foundation), the [Joseph Rowntree Charitable Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Rowntree_Charitable_Trust) and the [Barrow Cadbury Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrow_Cadbury_Trust).[^cite_note-49]

In August 2023, [Jim Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Jordan), the chair of the [US House of Representatives Judiciary Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_House_Committee_on_the_Judiciary), wrote to CCDH requesting the CCDH provide all documents and communications between the CCDH and the U.S. Executive branch and social media companies, a list of employees, contractors and grants received, to determine if the U.S. government "has coerced and colluded with companies and other intermediaries to censor speech".[^cite_note-Zakrzewski_2023_p308-50][^cite_note-51] Responding to *[The Washington Post](102226.md)* reporters, the CCDH denied receiving any funds from the United States government and provided documents it said showed its bipartisan approach.[^cite_note-Zakrzewski_2023_p308-50]
