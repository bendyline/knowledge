**Parinacochas** is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Peru) located in the [Ayacucho Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayacucho_Region) of [Peru](170691.md). It is one of the eleven that make up that region. The province has a population of 24,028 inhabitants as of 2002. The capital of the province is [Coracora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coracora).

## Boundaries

- **North**: [Apurímac Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apur%C3%ADmac_Region)

- **East**: province of [Paucar del Sara Sara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paucar_del_Sara_Sara_Province)

- **South**: [Arequipa Region](713212.md)

- **West**: province of [Lucanas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucanas_Province)

## Political division

The province extends over an area of 5,968.32 square kilometres (2,304.38 sq mi) and is divided into eight districts:

- [Coracora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coracora_District)

- [Chumpi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chumpi_District)

- [Coronel Castañeda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronel_Casta%C3%B1eda_District)

- [Pacapausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacapausa_District)

- [Pullo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pullo_District)

- [Puyusca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puyusca_District)

- [San Francisco de Ravacayco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_de_Ravacayco_District)

- [Upahuacho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upahuacho_District)

## Ethnic groups

The people in the province are mainly [indigenous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_of_the_Americas) citizens of [Quechua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechua_people) descent. [Quechua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechua_language) is the language which the majority of the population (54.94%) learnt to speak in childhood, 44.60% of the residents started speaking using the [Spanish](26825.md) language ([2007 Peru Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Peru_Census)).[^cite_note-2]
