**Damac Club** is a [Saudi Arabian](349303.md) professional [football](10568.md) club based in [Khamis Mushait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khamis_Mushait), that competes in the [Saudi Pro League](4525043.md), the top tier of the [Saudi football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_football_league_system).

Damac hosts its home games at two venues: the [Al-Mahalah Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Sultan_bin_Abdulaziz_Sports_City) in [Abha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abha) for high-profile matches and the [Damac Club Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damac_Club_Stadium)[^cite_note-1] in [Khamis Mushait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khamis_Mushait) for less prominent fixtures. The club takes its name from the nearby [Damac Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damac_Mountain?action=edit\&redlink=1).
