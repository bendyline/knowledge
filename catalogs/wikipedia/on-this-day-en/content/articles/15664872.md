A **game seven** is the final game of a [best-of-seven series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best-of-seven_series). This game can occur in the postseasons for [Major League Baseball](38776.md) (MLB) ([League Championship Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/League_Championship_Series) and [World Series](23489161.md)), the [National Basketball Association](22093.md) (NBA) (all rounds of the [NBA playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_playoffs)), and the [National Hockey League](21809.md) (NHL) (all rounds of the [Stanley Cup playoffs](10490910.md)). The game is generally played at the site of the team holding the [home advantage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_advantage) across the series.[^cite_note-1] It can also happen on the television game show *[Jeopardy!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeopardy!)* with its Tournament of Champions format since Season 38, where a player must win three games to win the final (2-2-2 tie leads to a Game Seven).[^cite_note-2]

The nature of a best-of-seven series requires that the series be tied 3–3 going into game seven, such that either team can take the series (advancing further in the playoffs or winning the championship) by winning the game. Because of this decisive nature, game sevens add an element of drama to their sports.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Aside from North American sports leagues, game sevens are also a fixture in many other sports around the world, mostly in [baseball](3850.md), [basketball](3921.md), and [ice hockey](14790.md) leagues. Most codes of [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football) do not employ a best-of-seven series (or any best-of-*x* series in general), hence game sevens are not played in those leagues. Some playoff rounds (such as MLB's current [Division Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_Series)) are played in a [best-of-five format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best-of-five_format), such that game five has similar qualities to those described above, though the suspense and drama have less time to build in a shorter series. Furthermore, the World Series of [1903](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1903_World_Series), [1919](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1919_World_Series), [1920](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1920_World_Series), and [1921](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1921_World_Series) were played in a [best-of-nine format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best-of-nine_format), though none of the four went to a decisive game nine.

The game seven is comparable to a [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_\(competition\)) or to a single game in a [single-elimination tournament](939680.md) or to a [one-game playoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-game_playoff). A championship series' game seven is equivalent to the [Super Bowl](27718.md) game in the [National Football League](21211.md) in that the game's winner is the league's champion for the season.

#### Major League Baseball

Main article: [List of Major League Baseball game sevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Major_League_Baseball_game_sevens)

In the [Major League Baseball postseason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Baseball_postseason), a game seven can occur in the [League Championship Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/League_Championship_Series) and the [World Series](23489161.md); a game seven cannot occur in the [Division Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_Series) of the playoff, which are played as best-of-five series nor can it occur in the [Wild Card Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Card_Series), which are played as best-of-three series.

In the World Series, there have been 41 decisive game sevens through the 2025 season; visiting teams have won 22 of those games.[^cite_note-9] Four *non-decisive* game sevens have been played, in the World Series contested as best-of-nine series (1903, 1919, 1920, 1921); none went to a game nine.

## Defunct major leagues

The [American Basketball Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Basketball_Association) and [World Hockey Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Hockey_Association), major leagues that saw teams that later merged with the [National Basketball Association](22093.md) and [National Hockey League](21809.md), respectively, each had playoff seasons that saw teams play a decisive seventh game.

### Major sports leagues



The [2004 Boston Red Sox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Boston_Red_Sox_season), seen here at the [White House](33057.md), are one of just two teams in [Major League Baseball](38776.md) to force a game seven after being down 3–0, and the only team to win a game 7 after being down 3–0.
