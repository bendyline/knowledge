# Legislative assembly

Not to be confused with [legislative chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_chamber).

"Legislative Assembly" redirects here. For legislatures with that name, see [Legislative Assembly (British Guiana)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_\(British_Guiana\)) and [Legislative Assembly (France)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_\(France\)).

**Legislative assembly** is the name given in some countries to either a [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature), or to one of its houses.

The name is used by a number of countries, including member-states of the [Commonwealth of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations) and other countries. It is also used by their sub-national divisions, such as the [Indian states and union territories](375986.md), [Australian states](4689096.md) and [Canadian provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Canada).

## Legislative assemblies in the Commonwealth

Legislative assemblies in modern-day Commonwealth countries, either as national or sub-national parliaments, are in most cases an evolution of one of the legislative chambers of the previous colonial parliaments, whether the full legislature or a [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house). In a number of jurisdictions, the name [House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Assembly) is used instead. It is one of the main names used in everyday speech for parliament in many countries.

### Australia

Three of the six [Australian](4689264.md) [states](4689096.md) style their [lower houses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house) as *Legislative Assemblies*; in [South Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Australian_House_of_Assembly) and [Tasmania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tasmanian_House_of_Assembly), they are styled as *Houses of Assembly*. The unicameral parliaments of [Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Queensland) and the two [self-governing territories](4689096.md) also style their legislature as *Legislative Assemblies*. Queensland originally had a Legislative Council as its [upper house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_house), in line with the other states, until it was abolished leaving it the only state with a sole chamber of parliament.

The previously self-governing Norfolk Island had its own *Legislative Assembly* until it was abolished and replaced with a [Regional Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk_Island_Regional_Council) in 2015.

Members of these assemblies are referred to as MLAs (WA, ACT, NT) and MPs (NSW, QLD, VIC, SA, TAS).[^cite_note-1] Previously, MLA and MHA have been used in states that now use MP.

### Canada

Main article: [Legislative assemblies of Canadian provinces and territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_assemblies_of_Canadian_provinces_and_territories)

In [Canada](5042916.md), seven of the ten [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_provinces) and all three of the [territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territories_of_Canada) style their legislatures as *legislative assemblies*. All are unicameral. Manitoba was the first to abolish [its Legislative Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Council_of_Manitoba) in 1876. British Columbia and Newfoundland and Labrador abolished their Councils before becoming provinces. There was no Council for Alberta when it was created in 1905. The [Legislative Assembly of Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Quebec) was renamed the [Quebec National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec_National_Assembly) as part of the abolition of the [Legislative Council of Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Council_of_Quebec) on December 31, 1968.

### India

In [India](14533.md), the [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house) or the only house of the State Legislatures are called [Legislative Assemblies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_legislative_assemblies_of_India). The same name is also used for the only house of the legislatures of three of the eight [union territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_territory), [Delhi](37756.md), [Jammu and Kashmir](61449812.md) and [Puducherry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puducherry_\(union_territory\)). The [upper house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_house) in states with a [bicameral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameral) legislature are called [Legislative Councils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_legislative_councils_of_India). Members of the former are called MLAs, and those of the latter are called MLCs.

## Legislative assemblies outside the Commonwealth

Legislative Assembly is the name given to some national legislatures (or one of the houses of the national legislature) of the sovereign nations of:

- [Plurinational Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurinational_Legislative_Assembly) of [Bolivia](3462.md)

- [Legislative Assembly of Costa Rica](1783211.md)

- [Legislative Assembly of El Salvador](1608500.md)

Former assemblies include:

- [National Legislative Assembly of Thailand (2014)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Legislative_Assembly_of_Thailand_\(2014\))

- [National Legislative Assembly (South Sudan)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Legislative_Assembly_\(South_Sudan\))

- [Legislative Assembly of Kyrgyzstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Kyrgyzstan)

- [Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_\(France\)) of Revolutionary France

Legislative bodies of the [United States](3434750.md) include:

- [North Dakota Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Dakota_Legislative_Assembly)

- [Oregon Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_Legislative_Assembly)

- [Legislative Assembly of Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Puerto_Rico)

The legislatures of the [States of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Brazil) are called "legislative assembly", as are the legislatures of the two insular [autonomous regions of Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_regions_of_Portugal), [Azores](3226.md) (the [Legislative Assembly of the Azores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_the_Azores)) and [Madeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeira) (the [Legislative Assembly of Madeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Madeira)), and the [regional councils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_council_\(Italy\)) of three [Italian regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Italy) ([Emilia-Romagna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Emilia-Romagna), [Marche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Marche) and [Umbria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Umbria)). In Mexico, the legislative branch of government of Mexico City is the [Legislative Assembly of Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Mexico_City).

The [Legislative Assembly of Macau](1620997.md) is the organ of the legislative branch of the Macau Special Administrative Region.
