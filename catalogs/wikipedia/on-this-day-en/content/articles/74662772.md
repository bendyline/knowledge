The **Puntland State House** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Madaxtooyada Dawladda Puntland*), ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): القصر الرئاسي في أرض البنط); is a presidential palace in [Garowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garoowe) and significant state government building located in [Government of Puntland](43532267.md), [Somalia](27358.md). Functioning as a representation of [political authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_authority) and [governance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governance), this notable palace has held a crucial role in the region's administrative matters. Since its establishment, it has observed the changes in [Puntland's political environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Puntland), reflecting the ambitions, difficulties, and accomplishments of the populace in their pursuit of [self-determination](29269.md).[^cite_note-1]

## History

The first presidential palace operated by the [Puntland State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puntland_State) after its establishment was the former home of the [local government in Garowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garowe_District). After [Somali Democratic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_Democratic_Republic) government collapsed in 1991.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
