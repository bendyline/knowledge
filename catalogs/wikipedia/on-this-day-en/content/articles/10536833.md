**Al-Tall** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): التل, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *at-Tall*, also spelled **al-Tell**) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Syria) in southern [Syria](7515849.md), administratively part of the [Rif Dimashq Governorate](4521946.md) and capital of the [al-Tall District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Tall_District). Situated in the middle of the [Anti-Lebanon Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Lebanon_Mountains), having an elevation of roughly 1,000 meters above sea level.[^cite_note-Boulanger303-2] Nearby localities include [Maaraba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maaraba,_Rif_Dimashq) to the southwest, [Damascus](8914.md) to the south, [Dahiyat al-Assad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahiyat_al-Assad) and [Douma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douma,_Syria) to the southeast, [Maarat Saidnaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maarat_Saidnaya) to the northeast, [Manin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manin,_Syria) to the north, [Ashrafiyat al-Wadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashrafiyat_al-Wadi) and [Basimah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basimah) to the northwest and [al-Hamah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hamah) and [Qudsaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qudsaya) to the west. According to the [Syria Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bureau_of_Statistics_\(Syria\)) (CBS), al-Tall had a population of 44,597 in the [2004 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Syrian_census).[^cite_note-CBS-1] Its inhabitants are predominantly [Sunni Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Muslim).[^cite_note-Boulanger303-2]

In the early 1960s al-Tall was reported to be a large village of 3,500 inhabitants. It had a large [mosque](19894.md) surrounded by several column fragments, hewn stones and burial [grottoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grotto).[^cite_note-Boulanger303-2]
