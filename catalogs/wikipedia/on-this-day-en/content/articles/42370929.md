# Temporary resident

A **temporary resident** is a [foreign national](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_national) granted the right to stay in a country for a certain length of time (e.g. with a [visa](330265.md) or [residency permit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Residency_permit)), without full [citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship). This may be for [study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student), [business](39206.md), or other reasons.

Various countries have their own rules or policies relating to temporary residency:-

  - [Temporary residency in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alien_\(law\)#Temporary_resident)
