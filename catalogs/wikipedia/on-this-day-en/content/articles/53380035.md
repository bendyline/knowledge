**Ansarul Islam** is a militant Islamist group active in [Burkina Faso](3470.md) and in [Mali](19127.md). It was founded by Boureima Dicko, also known as Ibrahim Malam Dicko, and it is the first native Jihadi group in Burkina Faso. The group cooperated closely with [Jama'at Nasr al-Islam wal Muslimin](53401088.md) (JNIM)[^cite_note-\:0-5] and later in 2017 joined them.[^cite_note-6]

## History

The group announced its existence in December 2016 in a statement claiming responsibility for an [attack in Nassoumbou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nassoumbou_attack).[^cite_note-7] It was founded and led by [Ibrahim Malam Dicko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Malam_Dicko) until his death in May 2017 from natural causes.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

The group has claimed responsibility for several attacks. These include the attack on two police stations in [Tongomayel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tongomayel_Department) and [Baraboulé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baraboul%C3%A9_Department) in February 2017[^cite_note-8] and the attack at a village in [Soum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soum_Province) in March 2017.[^cite_note-9] Later in 2017, it joined [Jama'at Nasr al-Islam wal-Muslimin](53401088.md), however it still exists as organization, while not independent anymore.[^cite_note-10] The decision to join JNIM was spurred by the death of Dicko, who was hesitant about joining the alliance.[^cite_note-11]

On 30 October 2020, about fifty jihadists on motorcycles belonging to the Ansarul Islam group were killed by the French Army in Mali, near the border with Burkina Faso. Weapons and equipment were also seized.[^cite_note-12]
