**&#x20;MV *Touska*** (Persian: توسکا, a tree) is a [Panamax](504868.md)-sized [container ship](262084.md) built in 2008 and sailed under the [flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_registration) of Iran.[^cite_note-optima-1]

In April 2026, as part of the [naval blockade of Iran](82937256.md) during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), the vessel was fired-on, disabled, and seized by the United States. It was also involved in an accident in November 2017 when it ran aground.

## Grounding

The *Touska* [ran aground](14396096.md) on 5 November 2017, at 9.15 p.m. off [Magazine Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Lamma_Channel) near [Hong Kong](13404.md) while en route from [Kaohsiung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaohsiung) to [Shenzhen](83368.md).[^cite_note-vt-3] The ship was traveling at 7.5 knots (13.9 km/h; 8.6 mph) at the time of grounding but the crew of 28 was not injured.[^cite_note-vt-3]

## 2026 Iran war

On 19 April 2026, as part of the [naval blockade of Iran](82937256.md) during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), the *Touska* was fired upon by the destroyer [USS *Spruance*](11831645.md) and seized by [US Marines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Marines) from the [31st Marine Expeditionary Unit](5885654.md) after the vessel refused to stop in the north [Arabian Sea](2747.md) en route to [Bandar Abbas, Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandar_Abbas,_Iran).[^cite_note-DVIDS2-4] While bound for Iran, it had previously stopped at a Chinese port known for the export of chemicals that could be used for [rocket propellants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocket_propellant).[^cite_note-5] *Spruance* disabled *Touska*'s propulsion by firing several rounds from the destroyer's [5-inch/54-caliber Mark 45 gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-inch/54-caliber_Mark_45_gun) into *Touska*'s engine room.[^cite_note-DVIDS2-4] The [United States Central Command](338482.md) claimed that the vessel was subject to multiple warnings; including one advising the crew to evacuate the engine room, that the *Touska*'s crew had "failed to comply with repeated warnings over a six-hour period", and released a video documenting at least part of the alleged warnings.[^cite_note-6]

&#x20;In response, the [Iranian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_government) stated:

> "'We warn that the Armed Forces of the ⁠Islamic Republic of Iran ⁠will soon respond ⁠and ⁠retaliate against this armed piracy by the U.S. military\[...]"

Iran further claimed that the United States had violated the in-place ceasefire.[^cite_note-7]

The ship was under [United States Treasury](53667.md) sanctions because of previous illegal activity according to President [Donald Trump](4848272.md).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

In May 2026, 22 crew members were transferred from the vessel into Pakistani custody as part of confidence-building measures between the United States and Iran. According to Pakistan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and international reports, the transfer involved coordination between both countries, with Pakistan acting as a facilitator. The crew were later repatriated to Iran via Pakistan.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]
