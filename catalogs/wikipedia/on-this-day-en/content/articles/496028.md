The **Eastern Time Zone** (**ET**)[^cite_note-1] is a [time zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_zone) encompassing part or all of 23 [states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._states) in the eastern [United States](3434750.md), parts of central and eastern [Canada](5042916.md), some [Caribbean](18956035.md) islands and the state of [Quintana Roo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quintana_Roo) in [Mexico](3966054.md). Most areas in the time zone observe [daylight saving time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daylight_saving_time) and thus alternate between:

- **Eastern Standard Time** (**EST**), which is five hours behind [Coordinated Universal Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time) ([UTC−05\:00](645310.md)) and observed during late autumn and winter, and

- **Eastern Daylight Time** (**EDT**), which is four hours behind Coordinated Universal Time ([UTC−04\:00](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC%E2%88%9204\:00)) and observed during spring, summer, and early autumn.

Areas in the Eastern Time Zone which do not observe daylight saving time use Eastern Standard Time, which is based on the 75th meridian west of Greenwich. Observation of daylight saving time starts on the second Sunday in March; at 2\:00 a.m. EST, clocks are advanced to 3\:00 a.m. EDT, creating a 23-hour day. On the first Sunday in November, at 2\:00 a.m. EDT, clocks are moved back to 1\:00 a.m. EST, which results in a 25-hour day.[^cite_note-statoidus-2][^cite_note-3]

The time in this zone is based on the mean solar time of the [75th meridian west](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/75th_meridian_west) of the [Royal Observatory, Greenwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Observatory,_Greenwich).

## History

The boundaries of the Eastern Time Zone have moved westward since the [Interstate Commerce Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_Commerce_Commission) (ICC) took over time-zone management from railroads in 1938. The easternmost and northernmost counties in [Kentucky](16846.md) were added to the zone in the 1940s, and in 1961, most of the state went Eastern. In 2000, [Wayne County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_County,_Kentucky), on the [Tennessee](30395.md) border, switched from Central to Eastern Time.[^cite_note-4] Within the United States, the Eastern Time Zone is the most populous region, with nearly half of the country's population.

In March 2019, the [Florida Legislature](429346.md) passed a bill requesting authorization from [Congress](31756.md) for year-round daylight saving time, which would effectively put [Florida](18933066.md) on Eastern Daylight Time year-round (except for west of the [Apalachicola River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apalachicola_River), which would be on Central Daylight Time year-round).[^cite_note-5] A similar bill was proposed for the [Canadian province](75763.md) of [Ontario](22218.md) by [its legislative assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Ontario) in late 2020,[^cite_note-6] which passed though was placed on hold until Quebec and [New York](8210131.md) agreed to make the same change, which had not happened as of 2024.[^cite_note-7]

On September 17, 2026, [Manitoba premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_Manitoba) [Wab Kinew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wab_Kinew) announced that Manitoba would be adopting permanent Central Daylight Time, effectively moving the province onto year-round Eastern Standard Time.[^cite_note-\:1-8]

## Daylight saving time

For those in the United States, daylight saving time for the Eastern Time Zone was introduced by the [Uniform Time Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_Time_Act) of 1966, which specified that it would run from the last Sunday of April until the last Sunday in October.[^cite_note-edt-9] The act was amended to make the first Sunday in April the beginning of daylight saving time beginning in 1987.[^cite_note-edt-9] Later, the [Energy Policy Act of 2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_Policy_Act_of_2005) extended daylight saving time in the United States, beginning in 2007.[^cite_note-edt-9]

## Canada

Main article: [Time in Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Canada)

In [Canada](5042916.md), the following [provinces and territories](75763.md) are part of the Eastern Time Zone:[^cite_note-nrc.canada.ca-10]

- [Manitoba](18926.md)

  [^cite_note-\:1-8]

- [Ontario](22218.md), excluding western regions

- [Quebec](7954867.md), excluding the [Magdalen Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalen_Islands) and [Le Golfe-du-Saint-Laurent Regional County Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Golfe-du-Saint-Laurent_Regional_County_Municipality)

- Eastern regions of [Nunavut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nunavut)

All of these regions observe daylight saving time with the exception of Manitoba and [Southampton Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southampton_Island) in Nunavut, which observe Eastern Standard Time year-round.[^cite_note-nrc.canada.ca-10] Eastern Standard Time (UTC–5 year-round) is officially called **Manitoba Standard Time** (**MBT**) in Manitoba.[^cite_note-11]

Within Canada, as with the United States, the Eastern Time Zone is the most populous time zone. Most of [Eastern Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Canada) and Nunavut (with exceptions) observes daylight saving time synchronously with the United States, while the rest of the country does not.

## United States

Main article: [Time in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_the_United_States)

The boundary between time zones is set forth in [Title 49](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title_49_of_the_Code_of_Federal_Regulations) of the [Code of Federal Regulations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_of_Federal_Regulations) with the boundary between the Eastern and [Central Time Zones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Time_Zone) being specifically detailed in Part 71.[^cite_note-12]

Five states are divided between the Eastern Time Zone and the [Central Time Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Time_Zone). The following locations observe Eastern Time:

- [Florida](18933066.md): The majority of the state, except the western half of the [panhandle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_panhandle). The eastern half of the panhandle, including [Big Bend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Bend_\(Florida\)) regions east of the [Apalachicola River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apalachicola_River) and portions of [Gulf County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_County,_Florida) south of the [Intracoastal Waterway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intracoastal_Waterway) observe Eastern Time.

- [Indiana](21883857.md): The majority of the state, except [Northwest Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwest_Indiana) and the [Evansville metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evansville_metropolitan_area).

- [Kentucky](16846.md): The eastern half of the state, including its three largest metropolitan areas: [Louisville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisville,_Kentucky), [Lexington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexington,_Kentucky), and [Northern Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati_metropolitan_area).

- [Michigan](18859.md): The entire state except for the four [Upper Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Peninsula_of_Michigan) counties that border [Wisconsin](33127.md): [Gogebic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gogebic_County,_Michigan), [Iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_County,_Michigan), [Dickinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dickinson_County,_Michigan), and [Menominee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menominee_County,_Michigan).

- [Tennessee](30395.md): All of [East Tennessee](646800.md) including the major cities of [Chattanooga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chattanooga,_Tennessee), [Knoxville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knoxville,_Tennessee) and the [Tri-Cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tri-Cities,_Tennessee) region, with the exception of [Bledsoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bledsoe_County,_Tennessee), [Cumberland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumberland_County,_Tennessee), and [Marion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_County,_Tennessee) counties.

Additionally, [Phenix City, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenix_City,_Alabama), and several nearby communities in [Russell County, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_County,_Alabama), unofficially observe Eastern Time.[^cite_note-14] This is due to their close proximity to [Columbus, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus,_Georgia), which is on Eastern Time. In addition [Smiths Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smiths_Station,_Alabama) in [Lee County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_County,_Alabama) along with [Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valley,_Alabama) and [Lanett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanett,_Alabama) in [Chambers County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chambers_County,_Alabama) observe Eastern Time.[^cite_note-15]

## Mexico

Main article: [Time in Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Mexico)

[Quintana Roo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quintana_Roo) is the only Mexican state to observe Eastern Standard Time, called *[Zona Sureste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Mexico)* ([lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Southeast Zone') in Mexico, after successful lobbying effort by tourism interests to move from Central Time.[^cite_note-16] Quintana Roo does not observe daylight saving time.

## Caribbean islands

The [Bahamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahamas) and [Haiti](13373.md) officially observe Eastern Time with daylight saving time. [Cuba](5042481.md) generally follows the U.S. with Eastern Standard Time in the winter, and Eastern Daylight Time in the summer, but the exact day of change varies year to year. The [Cayman Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayman_Islands), [Jamaica](15660.md), and [Navassa Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navassa_Island) use Eastern Standard Time year-round.

The [Turks and Caicos Islands](30217.md) followed Eastern Time with daylight saving until 2015, when the territory switched to the [Atlantic Time Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Time_Zone). The Turks and Caicos Islands switched back to the pre-2015 schedule in March 2018.[^cite_note-17] A 2017 consultation paper highlighted the advantage for business and tourism of being in the same time zone as the [eastern United States](509486.md) as an important factor in the decision.[^cite_note-18]
