**Yalova Province** ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *Yalova ili*) is a [province](428896.md) in northwestern [Turkey](11125639.md), on the eastern coast of the [Sea of Marmara](28863.md). Its adjacent provinces are [Bursa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bursa_Province) to the south and [Kocaeli](499408.md) to the east. Its area is 798 km<sup>2</sup> (making it the smallest province of Turkey),[^cite_note-2] and its population is 296,333 (2022).[^cite_note-tuik-1] Prior to 1930, the area around Yalova constituted a district of Kocaeli Province; from 1930 to 1995, it was part of [Istanbul Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul_Province); in 1995, the area was separated and made into the current Yalova Province.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The provincial capital is the city of [Yalova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yalova).

## Districts



Yalova Province is divided into six [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Turkey):

- [Çiftlikköy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%87iftlikk%C3%B6y_District)

- [Çınarcık](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%87%C4%B1narc%C4%B1k_District)

- **[Yalova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yalova_District)**
