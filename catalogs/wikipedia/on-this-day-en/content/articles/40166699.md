The **[petroleum industry](994887.md) in Kenya** is relatively new in terms of mining and exploration. [British](31717.md) firm [Tullow Oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tullow_Oil) began operations in Kenya in 2010 after signing agreements with Africa Oil and Centric Energy, purchasing a 50% interest in five onshore licences. In 2012, the Ngamia-1 exploration well was a success and has since been followed by further exploration in the South Lokichar Basin.[^cite_note-1] Drilling for oil at the Lokichar oil fields, Turkana county is expected to begin next year between January and February 2026[^cite_note-2]

## History



Oil Exploration Blocks in Kenya

The history of oil marketing in Kenya began in 1903, during colonial times. Initially, [kerosene](16992.md) was the main import in tins, but later gasoline was imported in tins and drums. [Royal Dutch Shell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Dutch_Shell) established the first depot on the [Mombasa Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mombasa_Island) at Shimanzi.

According to [Deloitte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deloitte) in 2013, Kenya has four prospective sedimentary basins: Anza, Lamu, Mandera, and the Tertiary Rift. The Lamu basin extends offshore.[^cite_note-3]

Oil is regulated by the Energy Regulation Commission and the [Ministry of Mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Mining_\(Kenya\)). Current traders include the [National Oil Corporation of Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Oil_Corporation_of_Kenya), [Shell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shell_Oil_Company), [Tullow Oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tullow_Oil), [KenolKobil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KenolKobil), [MOGAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOGAS_Group), Hass, Hashi Energy, [Gulf Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_Energy_SAOC), Olympic, Dalbit Petroleum, and Petrocam. In August, 2019, Kenya exported its first crude oil from the port of Mombasa. This was an experimental stage to test the country’s crude oil before full production and exportation, which was expected to begin in 2024.[^cite_note-4] Tullow oil, which was the pioneer of oil extraction in Kenya, sold its assets to Gulf Energy in September 2025[^cite_note-5]
