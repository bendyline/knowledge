The **CONCACAF Under-20 Championship** is the second longest running international [association football](10568.md) event in the [North America](21139.md), [Central America](6121.md) and the [Caribbean](18956035.md) region, [CONCACAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF), and is the qualification tournament for the [FIFA U-20 World Cup](1168189.md). The format of the tournament has undergone changes over the years. The tournament proper is currently played with a first round of four [round-robin](939742.md) groups from which the top two sides from each group advance to a [single-elimination](939680.md) championship round.

### FIFA U-20 World Cup qualifying tournament era

The format of the tournament was changed beginning with the 1998 tournament and ending with the 2007 tournament. In the five tournaments during that time the top eight teams in the region were divided into two groups of four, each group hosted by a separate nation, and the top two sides from each group qualified for the [FIFA U-20 World Cup](1168189.md). There was no championship round within the CONCACAF region. The tournament served solely to qualify the four allotments given to CONCACAF by FIFA for the U-20 World Cup.

### CONCACAF U-20 Championship era

Beginning with the [2009 FIFA U-20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup) tournament, the CONCACAF region returned to a championship-style tournament in which all four semifinalists qualify for the FIFA U-20 World Cup.[^cite_note-1] The reintroduction of a championship round was done to reinvigorate the competition.

The competition changed its format in 2015. The two group winners would secure a place in the final and the U-20 World Cup, the groups' second and third placed teams would compete in a repechage play-off to determine the qualifiers for the U-20 World Cup. The number of guaranteed games per team was increased from 2 to 5 and the semi-final stage was removed.&#x20;
