The **Tbilisi Appeal Court** is the [second instance court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_second_instance) in the Common Courts System of [Georgia](48768.md). The Court discusses [appeals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appeal) on the [judgments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judgment_\(law\)) of the [first instance courts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_first_instance), and its [jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurisdiction) covers [eastern Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Georgia_\(country\)).

## History

The Tbilisi Appeal court was established on 1 November 2005, after the previously-operating Tbilisi Regional Court was abolished.[^cite_note-1]

## Structure

The Tbilisi Appeal Court is managed by its Chairman. The present Chairman Valeri Tsertsvadze (ვალერი ცერცვაძე). The Court consists of 3 chambers and 1 board:

- Chamber for [Civil Cases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_law_\(common_law\))

- Chamber for [Administrative Cases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_law)

- Chamber for [Criminal Cases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_case)

- Investigatory Board

The Tbilisi Appeal Court's staff is overseen by a Court Manager. The structure of the Court's staff, which currently numbers 201:

- Chairman's Bureau

- Division for [Human Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Resources) and Management Matters

- [Chancellery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellor) and Court Reception

- Division for Material Maintenance and Court's [Bailiff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bailiff)

The Appeal Court's staff consists of [jurists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurist), [translators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translator), couriers, drivers, and [IT specialists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IT_Specialist).

## Court Reform

The Tbilisi Appeal Court has established many projects for [court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_law) petitioners. Reform efforts are ongoing.
