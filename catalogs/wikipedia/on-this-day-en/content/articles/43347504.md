# List of beaches in Australia

Main article: [List of beaches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_beaches)



The [Gold Coast](59251.md) consists of 70 kilometres (43 mi) of coastline with some of the most popular [surf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfing) breaks.

[^cite_note-1]

**Beaches in Australia** are in abundance due to the fact that [Australia](4689264.md) is entirely surrounded by the ocean. Beaches are popular destinations among the country's local population, and travellers alike, as over 85% of Australians live on the coast and most of Australia falls under the warm [temperate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperate) and [subtropical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical) climate zones. Australia has a prominent [beach culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_in_culture), which is often considered part of its [national identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_identity), with [television shows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Australian_television_series), [movies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_Australia), and songs being set in or relating to beaches. This page features a list of beaches in Australia.[^cite_note-2]

The longest beach in Australia is the 194 km (121 mi) long sandy beach running down the outer side of the [Younghusband Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Younghusband_Peninsula) in [South Australia](26716.md), commonly referred to as [The Coorong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Coorong). It runs from the [Murray mouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murray_mouth) to [Cape Jaffa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Jaffa).[^cite_note-coorong-3]
