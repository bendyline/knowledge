The **2025–26 UConn Huskies men's basketball team** represented the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut) in the [2025–26 NCAA Division I men's basketball season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_NCAA_Division_I_men's_basketball_season). The Huskies were led by eighth-year head coach [Dan Hurley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Hurley) in the team's sixth season since their return to the [Big East Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_East_Conference). The Huskies played their home games at [Harry A. Gampel Pavilion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_A._Gampel_Pavilion) on-campus in [Storrs, Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storrs,_Connecticut), and the [PeoplesBank Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PeoplesBank_Arena) in [Hartford, Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartford,_Connecticut). This marked the first season that [Tom Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Moore_\(basketball\)) was not on the coaching staff since his second stint started during the [2018–19 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018%E2%80%9319_UConn_Huskies_men's_basketball_team), moving to a general manager role. The Huskies finished the season as the [NCAA tournament](507399.md) runners-up after losing to [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025-26_Michigan_Wolverines_men's_basketball_team) 69–63 in the [National championship game](79949085.md).

## Previous season

The Huskies finished the season 24–11, 14–6 in Big East play, to finish 3rd in the regular season conference standings. They defeated [Villanova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Villanova_Wildcats_men's_basketball_team) 73–56 in the [Big East tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Big_East_men's_basketball_tournament) Quarterfinals before losing to [Creighton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Creighton_Bluejays_men's_basketball_team) in the semifinals, 71–62.[^cite_note-1] UConn received an at-large bid to the [NCAA tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NCAA_Division_I_men's_basketball_tournament), where they were awarded a #8 seed. The Huskies sought to become the first team to win three straight national championships since UCLA won seven straight championships from 1966–67 to 1972–73. However, the team narrowly lost to the eventual national champion [Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Florida_Gators_men's_basketball_team) in the second round, 77–75, ending their three-peat bid.[^cite_note-2]
