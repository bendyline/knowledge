**Kenge** is the capital of [Kwango Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwango_Province) in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md). As of 2012 it had an estimated population of 44,743.[^cite_note-2]

## Transport

The town is served by [Kenge Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenge_Airport).
