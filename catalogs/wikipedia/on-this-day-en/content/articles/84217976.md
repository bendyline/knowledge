The&#x20;**&#x20;Kurti IV cabinet** (**Fourth Cabinet of Albin Kurti**) was formed in [Kosovo](17391.md) on 13 September 2026, following a coalition agreement between the political parties [Vetëvendosje](3225595.md), [Guxo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guxo), [Albanian Christian Democratic Party of Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Christian_Democratic_Party_of_Kosovo), [Alternativa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternativa_\(Kosovo\)), [Progressive Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_Democratic_Party_\(Kosovo\)), [New Democratic Initiative of Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Democratic_Initiative_of_Kosovo), [Turkish Democratic Party of Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Democratic_Party_of_Kosovo), [Vakat Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vakat_Coalition) and [New Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Democratic_Party_\(Kosovo\)).[^cite_note-1]

After speculation on wether Kurti IV was legal, the constitutional court put them to rest on 23 September ruling that the government was elected in accordance with the constitution (despite it being elected well after the constitutional deadline to elect a government)[^cite_note-2]
