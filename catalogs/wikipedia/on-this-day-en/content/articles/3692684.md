**Constantine** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ولاية قسنطينة) is one of the 58 [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Algeria) (*[wilayas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilaya)*) of [Algeria](358.md), whose capital is [the city of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine,_Algeria), with 969,934 inhabitants, with a density of 424/km<sup>2</sup> (1,908/sq mi).

## History

In 1984 [Mila Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mila_Province) was carved out of its territory.

## Administrative divisions

The province is divided into 6 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Algeria) or *daïra*, which are subdivided into 12 [*communes*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Algeria) or municipalities.[^cite_note-2]
