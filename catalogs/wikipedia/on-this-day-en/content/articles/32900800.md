**Kong** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): كنگ)[^cite_note-4] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Bandar_Lengeh_County\)) of [Bandar Lengeh County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandar_Lengeh_County), [Hormozgan](448064.md) province, [Iran](14653.md).

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 14,881 in 2,908 households.[^cite_note-2006_Hormozgan_Province-5] The following census in 2011 counted 16,496 people in 4,009 households.[^cite_note-2011_Hormozgan_Province-6] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 19,213 people in 4,921 households.[^cite_note-2016_Hormozgan_Province-2]

From April 5 through 6, 2026, amidst the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), strikes hit both Kong and the nearby city of [Bandar Lengeh](1337157.md).[^cite_note-\:0-7] Combined, the strikes killed 6 people and wounded 17 others.[^cite_note-\:0-7]
