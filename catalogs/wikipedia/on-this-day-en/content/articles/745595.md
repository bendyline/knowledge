The **Israel Police** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): משטרת ישראל, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hebrew): *_Mišteret Yisra'el_*; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شرطة إسرائيل, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *_Shurtat Isrāʼīl_*) is the civilian police force of [Israel](9282173.md). As with most other [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police) forces in the world, its duties include crime fighting, [traffic control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_control_police), maintaining [public safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_safety), and [counter-terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-terrorism). It is under the jurisdiction of the [Ministry of National Security](28572000.md).[^cite_note-5] The [National Headquarters of the Israel Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Headquarters_of_the_Israel_Police) is located at [Kiryat HaMemshala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiryat_HaMemshala_\(East_Jerusalem\)) in [Jerusalem](16043.md).[^cite_note-6]

The Israel Police operates throughout Israel, [Area C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_C_\(West_Bank\)) of the [West Bank](33209.md), and the [Golan Heights](7515865.md),[^cite_note-7]—in all places in which Israel has civilian control.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] It is the sole civilian law enforcement agency in Israel: there are no municipal or regional police forces, though some municipalities employ [bylaw enforcement officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bylaw_enforcement_officer) who deal with low-level offenses and provide additional security and as such have the power to issue fines, but do not have police authority.[^cite_note-10]

In an emergency, the police can be reached by dialing **100** from any telephone in Israel.[^cite_note-11]

## History



Honor guard of the Israeli Police and [Border Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Border_Police) for Israel's Memorial Day

The Israel Police was established in 1948.[^cite_note-12] It is responsible for public security, maintaining public order, securing public events and rallies, dismantling suspicious objects and explosives ([EOD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosive_Ordnance_Disposal)), riot and crowd control, law enforcement, crime fighting, detective work, covert operations against drug networks, investigating suspects, road traffic control, operating the [Civil Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Guard_\(Israel\)), handling civilian complaints, handling youth violence, educational campaigns.[^cite_note-13]

The Israel Police is a professional force, with some 35,000 persons on the payroll. There are also 70,000 Civil Guard volunteers who carry out part-time work in helping to police their own communities.[^cite_note-14]

## Operational units

- The Security and Community Policing Branch is responsible for ordinary law enforcement tasks such as conducting patrols in public and responding to emergency calls.

- The [Israel Border Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Border_Police) ("*MAGAV*") is the combat arm of the police and mainly serves in specific areas – the borders, Jerusalem, and the West Bank. It is responsible for law enforcement in the rural countryside and for putting down civil unrest, particularly rioting. It participates in counter-terrorism operations. The Border Police has both professional officers on payroll and conscripts who serve in the Border Police as their mandatory three-year national service.

  [^cite_note-16]

  &#x20;It also has volunteer personnel. The Israel Border Police has four tactical units:&#x20;

  - [Yamam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamam) (National Counter-Terrorism Unit) - the police's counter-terrorism and hostage rescue unit. It is known as one of the most experienced and specialized units of its kind in the world. The unit has taken part in hundreds of operations in and outside the borders of Israel.

    [^cite_note-17]

  - [Yamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamas_\(Israel_Border_Police_unit\)) ([Mista'arvim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mista'arvim) Unit) - a counter-terrorist commando unit. Its operators are trained in conducting operations undercover, disguised as civilians. Although officially part of the Border Police, it is directly subordinate to [Shin Bet](311752.md), the Israeli internal security service.

  - Samag (Tactical Counter-Crime and Counter-Terror Rapid Deployment Unit)

  - Matilan (Intelligence Gathering and Infiltrations Interception Unit)

- The [Yasam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasam) (Special Patrol Unit) is a [riot police](1148542.md) and crowd control unit, and also participates in counter-terror operations. The unit, originally started as Riot Police, was called upon to assist with counter-terror operations, as well as dismantling settlements in accordance with Israeli court decisions.

  [^cite_note-18]

  &#x20;The Yasam has sub-units of Rapid Response Motorcycle Units.

  [^cite_note-19]

- The [National Traffic Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Traffic_Police) is the police's traffic enforcement arm. It is divided into five regional districts and a national patrol unit.

- The [Civil Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Guard_\(Israel\)) is the police's force of part-time volunteer officers, who comprise the majority of Israeli police officers. It is officially a division of the Security and Community Policing Branch. Civil Guard volunteers have limited police powers. They carry out patrols in public, are trained to provide the initial response to any security situation they encounter until regular police forces arrive, and partake in traffic control. The Civil Guard also has search and rescue teams.

- [Lahav 433](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahav_433) is the police's unit for investigations of serious crimes and corruption.

- The Police SIGINT Unit is responsible for [signals intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signals_intelligence) (SIGINT) activities.

- The Dog Handling Unit is responsible for operating [police dogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_dog).

- The Police Aerial Unit operates police helicopters.

- The Police Marine Unit is responsible for operating boats and has police divers.

- The [Israel Police Bomb Disposal Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Police_Bomb_Disposal_Unit) is the bomb squad of the police. It deals with operations and investigations which involve suspicious objects, explosive devices or military ordnance.

- The Seif Unit is responsible for tackling crime in the [Israeli-Arab](492331.md) population.

  [^cite_note-20]

- The Yoav Unit is responsible for enforcement of land use and construction regulations in the [Negev](142273.md), particularly among Bedouin communities.

  [^cite_note-21]

- The Division of Identification and Forensic Science is responsible for forensic science in investigations. It has laboratories dealing with latent fingerprint analysis, arson investigation, [mass spectrometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_spectrometry) and explosive analysis, digital evidence, DNA and other areas of biology, firearms, and [questioned document examination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Questioned_document_examination).

  [^cite_note-22]

- The Internal Investigations Department is responsible for investigating wrongdoing by police personnel. It is officially independent from the police and under the jurisdiction of the Justice Ministry.

  [^cite_note-23]

## Weapons and equipment



Israeli police boat on the [Sea of Galilee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_of_Galilee)



An Israeli Police [Bell 206](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_206) helicopter



[Israel Border Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Border_Police) vehicle

Each patrol officer is armed with a [pistol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pistol) (handgun) which he or she usually also carries while off duty. Also, each patrol car must have at least one long-arm (i.e. rifle). Police volunteers are usually armed with an [M1 Carbine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M1_carbine), which they return to the armory after they finish their duty (they do not take the rifle home, but may sign one out for escorting field trips, etc.). Volunteers who have a [gun license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gun_license) may use their own personal handgun as a personal defense weapon while on duty, under the condition that the gun and ammunition type is authorized by the police (9 mm). Common pistols owned and carried by volunteers include [Glock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glock) and [CZ-75](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CZ_75) designs.[^cite_note-24]

Specialized armaments such as automatic rifles, bolt-action rifles and non-lethal weapons are assigned according to activity and not on personal basis. Border Guard personnel, however, carry an [M16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M16_rifle) or [M4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M4_carbine) rifle as a standard personal weapon and can carry it home while off duty (like regular infantry in the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md)).[^cite_note-25]

## Women police

In 2015, the editorial of *[Haaretz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haaretz)* claimed "Women constitute at least 30 percent of the police force" and criticized there were no women who have reached the rank of major general.[^cite_note-31]

## Controversies

A 2014 analysis by [Yesh Din](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yesh_Din) questioned the professionalism of the police force of the [Judea and Samaria District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judea_and_Samaria_District) (also known as the [West Bank](33209.md)) as only 7.4% of reported attacks by Israeli citizens on Palestinian persons and property had led to indictments.[^cite_note-32] In 2015, several senior officers resigned due to criminal investigations or accusations of sexual harassment of employees. Five police officers at the rank of major general resigned in the preceding 18 months amid scandal.[^cite_note-33] The February 2015 announcement that another senior Israel Police officer was under investigation for sexual harassment was criticized by women's and rape victim advocacy groups, who held protests at police headquarters in a number of cities.[^cite_note-34]

An ongoing Israeli programme of inviting U.S. police groups to study Israeli policework in sponsored visits has been the object of controversy for several years.[^cite_note-35]
