The **Northern Region** is one of four [regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Uganda) in [Uganda](31816.md).[^cite_note-GeoHive-1] As of Uganda's 2014 [census](6889.md), the region's population was 7,188,139.[^cite_note-CityPopulation-2]
