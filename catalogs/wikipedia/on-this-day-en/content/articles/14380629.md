The **Government of the Czech Republic** ([Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_language): *Vláda České republiky*) exercises executive power in the [Czech Republic](5321.md). The members of the government are the [Prime Minister of the Czech Republic](410283.md) (Chairman of the Government), the deputy prime minister and other ministers. It has its legal basis in the [Constitution of the Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Czech_Republic).

## Overview

The government is led by the [Prime Minister](410283.md), who selects all the remaining ministers at hand. The Government of the Czech Republic is responsible to the [Chamber of Deputies from the Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies_of_the_Parliament_of_the_Czech_Republic).

The [Prime Minister](410283.md) is appointed by the [President of the Czech Republic](410288.md). The [Prime Minister](410283.md) as of December 2025 is [Andrej Babiš](40378134.md).
