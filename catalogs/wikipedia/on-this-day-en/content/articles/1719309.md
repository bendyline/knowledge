**Viral hemorrhagic fevers** (**VHFs**) are a diverse group of [infectious diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infection) characterized by [fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fever) and systemic damage to the [circulatory system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circulatory_system) caused by [RNA viruses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA_virus). Viral hemorrhagic fevers cause symptoms ranging from mild to life-threatening, depending on the [virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virus) involved, but generally cause [internal bleeding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_bleeding) that leads to sudden onset of [muscle pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_pain), fever, and [hypotension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypotension).[^cite_note-1] In severe cases, it can cause life-threatening [shock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shock_\(circulatory\)) and bleeding from [internal organs](152776.md).[^cite_note-\:1-2]

While some VHFs are generally mild, such as [nephropathia epidemica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nephropathia_epidemica) (caused by two species of [hantavirus](416945.md)), many are debilitating or lethal without treatment. Outbreaks of VHFs tend to have high [case fatality rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_fatality_rate) and disproportionately affect communities with poor health infrastructure.[^cite_note-3] Therefore, the emergence of VHFs is a growing public health concern.[^cite_note-\:1-2] VHFs are often [zoonoses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoonosis), meaning they can be transmitted from wild animals to human populations;[^cite_note-4] for instance, the [Lassa](17887.md) arenavirus is spread by mice. Viral hemorrhagic fevers are caused by members of seven families of single-stranded RNA viruses: *[Arenaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arenaviridae)*, *[Filoviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filoviridae)*, *[Flaviviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flaviviridae)*, *[Nairoviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nairoviridae)*, *[Phenuiviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenuiviridae)*, *[Hantaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hantaviridae)* and *[Peribunyaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peribunyaviridae)*.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

## Symptoms

[Signs and symptoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signs_and_symptoms) of most or all viral hemorrhagic fevers can be divided into "wet" and "dry" symptoms. The disease typically progresses from dry symptoms to wet symptoms.[^cite_note-\:0-10]

### Dry symptoms

- [Headache](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headache)

- [Malaise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaise) (a general feeling of sickness)

- [Fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fever) (high body temperature)

- [Hypotension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypotension) (low [blood pressure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_pressure))

- [Flushing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flushing_\(physiology\)) (redness) of the face and chest

- [Myalgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myalgia) (muscle pain)

### Wet symptoms

- [Circulatory shock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circulatory_shock)

- [Diarrhea](53951.md) (feces which resemble more liquid than solid)

- [Hemorrhage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemorrhage) (bleeding) and sometimes [bleeding diathesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleeding_diathesis) (a person loses more blood than usual from an injury – for example, getting only a little cut, and losing a lot of blood)

- [Petechiae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petechiae) (small red or purple spots)

- [Swelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edema) caused by [edema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edema)

The severity of symptoms varies with both [individual immune competence](#Pathophysiology) and the type of virus. The "VHF syndrome" causes bleeding diathesis, capillary leak, and circulatory shock. It happens to most people who have *Filoviridae* infections (such as Ebola virus or [Marburg virus](33134774.md)), [Crimean–Congo hemorrhagic fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean%E2%80%93Congo_hemorrhagic_fever) (CCHF), or the South American hemorrhagic fevers (which are caused by *Arenaviridae*). VHF syndrome only happens to a small minority of people who have [dengue fever](39669.md) or [Rift Valley fever](26378.md).

## Causes

All known viruses causing hemorrhagic fever are part of the [Orthornavirae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthornavirae) kingdom.[^cite_note-11]

- The order *[Hareavirales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hareavirales)* includes the families *[Arenaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arenaviridae)*, *[Nairoviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nairoviridae)* and *[Phenuiviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenuiviridae)*

* The family *[Arenaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arenaviridae)* includes the viruses responsible for [Lassa fever](17887.md) ([Lassa virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lassa_virus)), [Lujo virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lujo_virus), [Argentine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentine_hemorrhagic_fever) ([Junin virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junin_virus)), [Bolivian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivian_hemorrhagic_fever) ([Machupo virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machupo_virus)), [Brazilian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_hemorrhagic_fever) ([Sabiá virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabi%C3%A1_virus)), Chapare hemorrhagic fever ([Chapare virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapare_virus)), [Venezuelan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_hemorrhagic_fever) ([Guanarito virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanarito_virus)), and [Whitewater Arroyo virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitewater_Arroyo_virus) hemorrhagic fevers

* The family *[Nairoviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nairoviridae)* includes the [Crimean-Congo hemorrhagic fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean-Congo_hemorrhagic_fever) (CCHF) virus from the genus *[Orthonairovirus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthonairovirus)*.

* The family *[Phenuiviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenuiviridae)* includes the [Rift Valley fever](26378.md) (RVF) virus from the genus *[Phlebovirus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phlebovirus)*.

- The order *[Elliovirales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliovirales)* includes the families *[Peribunyaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peribunyaviridae)* and *[Hantaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hantaviridae)*.

* The family *[Hantaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hantaviridae)* includes the causative agents of [Hantavirus hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hantavirus_hemorrhagic_fever_with_renal_syndrome) (HV-HFRS).

* The family *[Peribunyaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peribunyaviridae)* includes the [Ngari virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngari_virus).

- The order *[Mononegavirales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mononegavirales)* contains the family *[Filoviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filoviridae)*.

* The family *[Filoviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filoviridae)* includes [Ebola virus](40817806.md) and [Marburg virus](33134774.md).

- The order *[Amarillovirales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amarillovirales)* includes the family *[Flaviviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flaviviridae)*.

* The family *[Flaviviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flaviviridae)* includes [dengue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dengue), [yellow fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_fever), and two viruses in the [tick-borne encephalitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tick-borne_encephalitis) group that cause VHF: [Omsk hemorrhagic fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omsk_hemorrhagic_fever) virus and [Kyasanur Forest disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyasanur_Forest_disease) virus.

In September 2012 scientists reported the discovery of a member of the *[Rhabdoviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhabdoviridae)* family potentially causing hemorrhagic fever. In a 2009 outbreak of acute hemorrhagic fever they discovered a new viral genome in one of the three people affected. The virus was named [Bas-Congo virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bas-Congo_virus).[^cite_note-Grard2012-13] The virus could not be isolated and has since then not been detected in any other patient, raising doubts on whether the virus is actually the causative agent of a disease.[^cite_note-14]

## Pathophysiology

Different hemorrhagic fever viruses act on the body differently, resulting in variable symptoms. In most VHFs, several mechanisms likely contribute to symptoms, including liver damage, [disseminated intravascular coagulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disseminated_intravascular_coagulation) (DIC), and bone marrow dysfunction. In DIC, small blood clots form in blood vessels throughout the body, removing platelets necessary for clotting from the bloodstream and reducing clotting ability. DIC is thought to cause bleeding in Rift Valley, Marburg, and Ebola fevers. For filoviral hemorrhagic fevers, there are four general mechanisms of pathogenesis. The first mechanism is the dissemination of the virus due to suppressed responses by [macrophages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macrophage) and [dendritic cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendritic_cell) (antigen-presenting cells). The second mechanism is prevention of [antigen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antigen) specific immune response. The third mechanism is [apoptosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apoptosis) of lymphocytes. The fourth mechanism is when infected macrophages interact with toxic [cytokines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cytokine), leading to [diapedesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diapedesis) and coagulation deficiency. From the vascular perspective, the virus will infect vascular endothelial cells, leading to the reorganization of the [VE-cadherin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VE-cadherin) catenin complex (a protein important in cell adhesion). This reorganization creates intercellular gaps in endothelial cells. The gaps lead to increased endothelial permeability and allow blood to escape from the vascular circulatory system.

The reasons for variation among patients infected with the same virus are unknown but stem from a complex system of virus-host interactions. Dengue fever becomes more virulent during a second infection by means of [antibody-dependent enhancement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antibody-dependent_enhancement). After the first infection, [macrophages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macrophage) display antibodies on their cell membranes specific to the dengue virus. By attaching to these antibodies, dengue viruses from a second infection are better able to infect the macrophages, thus reducing the immune system's ability to fight off infection.

## Diagnosis

Definitive diagnosis is usually made at a reference laboratory with advanced [biocontainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biocontainment) capabilities. The findings of laboratory investigation vary somewhat between the viruses but in general, there is a decrease in the total white cell count (particularly the [lymphocytes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lymphocyte)), a decrease in the [platelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platelet) count, an increase in the blood serum [liver enzymes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liver_enzyme), and reduced blood clotting ability measured as an increase in both the [prothrombin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prothrombin_time) (PT) and activated [partial thromboplastin times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_thromboplastin_time) (PTT). The [hematocrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hematocrit) may be elevated. The serum urea and creatine may be raised but this is dependent on the hydration status of the patient. The [bleeding time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleeding_time) tends to be prolonged.

Labs can use [RT-PCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_transcription_polymerase_chain_reaction) to detect the genetic material of the virus, or an [ELISA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ELISA) test in later stages to detect antibodies produced by the immune system.[^cite_note-15]

## Prevention

Main article: [Prevention of viral hemorrhagic fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prevention_of_viral_hemorrhagic_fever)

With the exception of [yellow fever vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_fever_vaccine) and [Ebola vaccines](44003094.md), vaccines for VHFs are generally not available. For someone exposed to CCHF, [ribavirin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribavirin) is available as [post-exposure prophylaxis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-exposure_prophylaxis) (PEP).[^cite_note-pmid30124196-16] Ribavirin may also help in exposure to Lassa fever.[^cite_note-pmid21122249-17]

Any person who is taking care of a patient with any VHF should take multiple precautions against exposure and infection. The precautions include hand hygiene, double gloves, gowns, shoe and leg coverings, and face shields or goggles. Treatment of any VHF should involve careful donning (putting on) and doffing (taking off) procedures for PPE. A Trained Observer, or TO, is used to monitor the donning/doffing process. Lassa, CCHF, Ebola, and Marburg viruses may be particularly prone to [nosocomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nosocomial) (hospital-based) spread. Airborne precautions should be utilized including, at a minimum, a [fit-tested](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respirator_fit_test), HEPA filter-equipped respirator (such as an [N95 mask](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N95_respirator)), a battery-powered, air-purifying respirator, or a positive pressure supplied air respirator to be worn by personnel coming within 1.8 metres (six feet) of a VHF patient. Groups of patients should be cohorted (sequestered) to a separate building or a ward with an isolated air-handling system. Environmental decontamination is typically accomplished with hypochlorite (e.g. bleach) or [phenolic disinfectants](478185.md#Phenolics).[^cite_note-18]

Some VHF patients, notably those infected with [Marburg](58743.md) and [Ebola](40817590.md), are at their most infectious after death. Strict precautions should be taken including the safe transport and disinfection of the body. [^cite_note-19]

## Management

Medical management of VHF patients may require intensive supportive care. Antiviral therapy with intravenous [ribavirin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribavirin) may be useful in Bunyaviridae and Arenaviridae infections (specifically Lassa fever, RVF, CCHF, and HFRS due to Old World Hantavirus infection) and can be used only under an experimental protocol as IND approved by the [U.S. Food and Drug Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_and_Drug_Administration_\(United_States\)) (FDA). Interferon may be effective in Argentine or Bolivian hemorrhagic fevers (also available only as IND).

## Potential therapies

A potential novel treatment, the [NMT inhibitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMP-1088), has been shown to completely inhibit [Lassa (LAS)](17887.md) and [Junín (JUN)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentinian_mammarenavirus) viral infections in cells based assays.[^cite_note-20] Another host-directed antiviral acts on [EPRS1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPAS1) which in turn acts, in human cells, as a proviral factor in mammarenaviruses infection, including [LCMV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lymphocytic_choriomeningitis), [JUNV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jun%C3%ADn_virus), and [LASV](17887.md), and its inhibition using [halofuginon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halofuginone) compound, a prolyl domain inhibitor of [EPRS1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPRS), completely abolishes the viral infection by interrupting viral assembly and budding.[^cite_note-21] PKR has been shown to act as a proviral factor [^cite_note-22] while the inhibition of its kinase activity restricted the virus replication and infectivity.[^cite_note-23]

## Biowarfare potential

The VHF viruses are spread in a variety of ways. Some may be transmitted to humans through a respiratory route. The viruses are considered by military medical planners to have a potential for aerosol dissemination, weaponization, or likelihood for confusion with similar agents that might be weaponized.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

[Scientific Research Institute of Medicine of the Ministry of Defense in Sergiyev Posad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_Research_Institute_of_Medicine_of_the_Ministry_of_Defense_in_Sergiyev_Posad) was researching the military use potential of hemorrhagic fever viruses.
