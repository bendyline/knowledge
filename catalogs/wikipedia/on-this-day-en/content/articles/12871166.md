The **98th Division "HaEsh"** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): עֻצְבַּת הָאֵשׁ, *Utzbat HaEsh,* lit. The Fire Formation), is an elite division of the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md). It is subordinate to the [Central Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Command_\(Israel\)). In October 2024 it took part in a [ground operation in Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Israeli_invasion_in_Lebanon?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
