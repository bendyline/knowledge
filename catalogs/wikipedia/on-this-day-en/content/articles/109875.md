**Winder** ([/ˈwaɪndər/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English), [*WYNE-dər*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key))[^cite_note-Census_2010-5] is a city and the [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) of [Barrow County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrow_County,_Georgia), [Georgia](48830.md), United States.[^cite_note-GR6-6] It is located east of Atlanta and is part of [Metro Atlanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Atlanta). The population was 18,338 at the 2020 census.

## History

[Creek people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creek_people) first inhabited the area.[^cite_note-7]

The [Georgia General Assembly](426277.md) incorporated Winder in 1893.[^cite_note-8]  The community was named after [John H. Winder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_H._Winder) who served as a General in the Confederate Army. Before Winder was named Winder it was originally named Jug Tavern.

The first hotel of the [Jameson Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jameson_Inn) chain opened in Winder in 1987.[^cite_note-9]

The first [Doctors’ Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Doctors'_Day) observance was March 28, 1933, in Winder. This first observance included the mailing of cards to the physicians and their wives, flowers placed on graves of deceased doctors, including Dr. [Crawford Long](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crawford_Long) (who in 1842 performed the first surgery under general anesthesia), and a formal dinner in the home of Dr. and Mrs. William T. Randolph. After the Barrow County Alliance adopted Mrs. Eudora Brown Almond's resolution to pay tribute to the doctors, the plan was presented to the Georgia State Medical Alliance in 1933 by Mrs. E. R. Harris of Winder, president of the Barrow County Alliance. On May 10, 1934, the resolution was adopted at the annual state meeting in Augusta, Georgia. The resolution was introduced to the Women's Alliance of the Southern Medical Association at its 29th annual meeting held in St. Louis, Missouri, November 19–22, 1935, by the Alliance president, Mrs. J. Bonar White. Since then, Doctors' Day has become an integral part of and synonymous with, the Southern Medical Association Alliance.

## Geography

Winder is located in central Barrow County at [33°59′47″N 83°43′15″W  /  33.99639°N 83.72083°W  / 33.99639; -83.72083](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Winder,_Georgia\&params=33_59_47_N_83_43_15_W_type\:city) (33.996495, -83.720873).[^cite_note-GR1-10] It is 20 miles (32 km) west of [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens,_Georgia) and 50 miles (80 km) northeast of downtown [Atlanta](3138.md). Winder is 140 miles northwest of the [Augusta metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augusta_metropolitan_area).&#x20;

According to the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the city has a total area of 12.9 square miles (33.5 km<sup>2</sup>), of which 12.4 square miles (32.2 km<sup>2</sup>) is land and 0.50 square miles (1.3 km<sup>2</sup>), or 3.97%, is water.[^cite_note-Census_2010-5]

### 2020 census

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), Winder had a population of 18,338. The median age was 36.3 years. 24.7% of residents were under the age of 18 and 15.2% of residents were 65 years of age or older. For every 100 females there were 91.4 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 87.8 males age 18 and over.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-16][^cite_note-Census2020PL-17]

98.8% of residents lived in urban areas, while 1.2% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-Census2020DHC-18]

There were 6,645 households and 3,885 families in Winder, of which 35.4% had children under the age of 18 living in them. Of all households, 44.1% were married-couple households, 16.1% were households with a male householder and no spouse or partner present, and 32.6% were households with a female householder and no spouse or partner present. About 25.6% of all households were made up of individuals and 12.2% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-16]

There were 6,964 housing units, of which 4.6% were vacant. The homeowner vacancy rate was 1.5% and the rental vacancy rate was 3.6%.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-16]

## Arts and culture

### National Register of Historic Places

The [Barrow County Courthouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrow_County_Courthouse) in Winder was built in 1920, and is listed under the [National Register of Historic Places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places).[^cite_note-state1-19]

### Barrow County Museum

Main article: [Barrow County Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrow_County_Museum)

The Barrow County Museum holds artifacts of Barrow County. It is the home of the Barrow County Historical Society. The museum located in the old Barrow County Jail.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

## Education

### Public schools

Public schools are part of the [Barrow County School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrow_County_Schools). The district consists of eight elementary schools, four middle schools, and two high schools.[^cite_note-22] The district has 610 full-time teachers and over 9,362 students.[^cite_note-23]

Northern portions of Winder are assigned to [Winder-Barrow High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winder-Barrow_High_School), while southern portions are zoned to [Apalachee High School](42222255.md).[^cite_note-BarrowSDmap-24] The majority of AHS-zoned parts of Winder are in the zones of Kennedy Elementary School and Westside Middle School, with one section in the zones of Bethlehem Elementary School and Haymon-Morris Middle School.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-Windermap-26] Elementary schools with attendance boundaries covering the WBHS part include Winder Elementary School, County Line Elementary School, and Holsenbeck Elementary School.[^cite_note-BarrowSDmap-24][^cite_note-Windermap-26] Richard B. Russell Middle School is in Winder,[^cite_note-27] in the Winder-Barrow feeder zone.

The Arts and Innovation Magnet Program is in Wilder.[^cite_note-28]
