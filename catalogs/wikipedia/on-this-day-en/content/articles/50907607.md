**Ri Song Gum** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 리성금; born 17 October 1997) is a North Korean [weightlifter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_weightlifting),[^cite_note-1] competing in the 49 kg category and representing North Korea at international competitions.[^cite_note-2]

She has competed at multiple World Championships and at the [Asian Games](39205.md). At the [2026 Asian Games](51790406.md) she set three world records and won gold.[^cite_note-3]
