The **sultan of the Sultanate of Oman** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): سلطان سلطنة عمان, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Sulṭān Salṭanat ʿUmān*) is the [monarchical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarch) [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [head of government](37570.md) of [Oman](22316.md). It is the most [powerful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_\(social_and_political\)) [position](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolute_monarchy) in the country. The sultans of Oman are members of the [Al Bu Said dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Bu_Said_dynasty), which has been the ruling family of Oman since the mid-18th century. [Haitham bin Tariq](46281094.md) is the current sultan, reigning since 11 January 2020.[^cite_note-Reuters-1]

Prior to the establishment of the present-day Sultanate of Oman in 1970, the [Sultanate of Muscat and Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscat_and_Oman), the [Omani Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omani_Empire), and the [Imamate of Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imamate_of_Oman) all ruled the area since 749.

## Succession



The will of Sultan Qaboos, in which he named his cousin Haitham bin Tariq his heir

Unlike the heads of other [Arab states of the Persian Gulf](3088555.md), Qaboos did not publicly name an [heir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heir_apparent). Article 6 of the [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Statute_of_Oman) says the Royal Family Council has three days to choose a new sultan from the date the position falls vacant. If the Royal Family Council fails to agree, a letter containing a name penned by Sultan Qaboos should be opened in the presence of the Defence Council of military and security officials, supreme court chiefs, and heads of the upper and lower houses of the consultative assemblies.[^cite_note-Dokoupil-19] Analysts saw the rules as an elaborate means of Qaboos securing his choice for successor without causing controversy by making it public during his lifetime, since it was considered unlikely that the royal family would be able to agree on a successor on its own.[^cite_note-Dokoupil-19]

Qaboos had no children, and only one sister, Sayyida Umaima (who predeceased him in 2002), but no male siblings; there are other male members of the Omani royal family including paternal uncles and their families. Using same-generation [primogeniture](136535.md), the successor to Qaboos would appear to be the children of his late uncle [ Sayyid Tariq bin Taimur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tariq_bin_Taimur_Al_Said), Oman's first prime minister and the Sultan's former father-in-law. Oman watchers believed the top contenders to succeed Qaboos were three of Tariq's sons: [Asa'ad bin Tariq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asa'ad_bin_Tariq), Deputy Prime Minister[^cite_note-LfL9Y-20] for International Relations and Cooperation[^cite_note-jtukv-21] and the Sultan's special representative; [Shihab bin Tariq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shihab_bin_Tariq), a retired commander of the [Royal Navy of Oman](13921289.md); and [Haitham bin Tariq](46281094.md), Minister of Heritage and National Culture.[^cite_note-Dokoupil-19][^cite_note-dugyF-22]

On 11 January 2020, Oman state TV said the Royal Family Council, in a letter to the Defense Council, had decided to defer to the choice that Qaboos named in his will, and thus had opened the letter by Qaboos naming his successor, announcing shortly that Haitham bin Tariq is the country's ruling sultan.[^cite_note-v6fty-23] Haitham has two sons and two daughters.[^cite_note-oXnU3-24][^cite_note-KfGIN-25]

On 12 January 2021, the current Sultan, [Haitham bin Tariq](46281094.md) officially changed the Basic Law of the State, stipulating the creation of the post for the [Crown Prince of Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Prince_of_Oman) and appointed his first son, [Theyazin bin Haitham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theyazin_bin_Haitham) as the apparent successor, making him the first Crown Prince of the Sultanate.[^cite_note-26]
