**Pro-Europeanism**, sometimes called **European Unionism**,[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] is a political position that favours [European integration](683704.md) and membership of the [European Union](9317.md) (EU).[^cite_note-euroscepticism-integration-3] The opposite of Pro-Europeanism is [Euroscepticism](10037.md).

## Political position



Percentage responding that their country on balance benefited from being a member of the EU at [Eurobarometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurobarometer) 2026:

[^cite_note-4]

| 91–100%   81–90%   71–80% | 61–70%   51–60% |
| --- | --- |

Pro-Europeans are mostly classified as [centrist](39619035.md) ([Renew Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renew_Europe)) in the context of [pan-European politics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_political_alliance), including [centre-right](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-right_politics) [liberal conservatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_conservatism) ([EPP Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_People's_Party_Group)) and [centre-left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-left_politics) [social democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_democracy) ([S\&D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_Alliance_of_Socialists_and_Democrats) and [Greens/EFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greens%E2%80%93European_Free_Alliance)). Pro-Europeanism is ideologically closely related to the [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberalism_in_Europe) and global [liberal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberalism) movement.[^cite_note-\:6-5][^cite_note-\:1-6][^cite_note-Edwards2-7][^cite_note-8]

Pro-Europeans often argue that EU membership has specific benefits for member nations such as that the EU encourages economic prosperity among members, that it promotes peace and stability in member states, that it encourages social progress among member states, that the EU gives countries greater leverage on the world stage compared to countries not in the EU, and that Europeans have shared values and identity. They also argue that these benefits outweigh common [criticisms and issues of the EU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticism_of_the_European_Union) for member states.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

Some pro-Europeans may also support a stronger and more [centralized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralized) EU as well as the idea of a [Federal Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Europe) and further [European integration](683704.md) among member states.[^cite_note-12]
