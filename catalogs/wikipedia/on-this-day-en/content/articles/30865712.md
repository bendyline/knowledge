**Piranshahr** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): پيرانشهر, [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language): *Pîranşar* or *Khane*)[^cite_note-5] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Piranshahr_County\)) of [Piranshahr County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piranshahr_County), [West Azerbaijan](47567329.md) province, [Iran](14653.md), serving as capital of both the county and the district.[^cite_note-West_Azerbaijan_Province_Structure-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-mefa-8]

## Etymology

According to the Arab geographer [Yaqut al-Hamawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaqut_al-Hamawi), the name of the city is derived from the famous figure of *[Shahnameh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahnameh)* [Piran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piran_Viseh).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-hamidian-11] Piran is a [Turanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turan) figure in *Shahnameh*, the [national epic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_epic) of [Greater Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Iran). Beside Shahnameh, Piran is also mentioned in other sources such as [Tabari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Prophets_and_Kings) and [Tha'ālibī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Tha'%C4%81lib%C4%AB). He is the king of [Khotan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khotan) and the [spahbed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spahbed) of [Afrasiab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrasiab), the king of Turan. He is described as a wise and intelligent man, seeking to bring peace to Iran and Turan.

In old Iranian writings, Piran and [Aghrirat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aghrirat) are the only Turanians that have been described positively. Piran plays a vital role in the story of [Siavash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siy%C3%A2vash), the story of [Kay Khosro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kay_Khosro) and the story of [Bizhan and Manizhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bizhan_and_Manizhe). In [Persian culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_culture), Piran is a symbol of wisdom.[^cite_note-rch-12] It has been said that [Karim Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karim_Khan) called [Mohammad Khan Qajar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Khan_Qajar) "Piran".[^cite_note-rch-12][^cite_note-13] Piran is often compared to [Bozorgmehr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bozorgmehr).[^cite_note-hamidian-11]

According to Djalal Khaleghi Motlagh, Piran may be the Median [Harpagus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harpagus) that saved [Cyrus the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrus_the_Great).[^cite_note-14]

## Parsua civilization

According to Minorsky, the ancient Parsua is identical with the name of the town [Pasveh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasveh) in [Lajan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lajan_District) of [Piranshahr County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piranshahr_County).[^cite_note-15]

[Pasveh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasveh) is in the vicinity of Piranshahr. Pasva is a village near Piranshahr whose name, according to the Iranist Vladimir Minorsky has existed since the 9th century BC and was built by the "Parsua tribes." It was also mentioned in the records of the Assyrian ruler [Shalmaneser III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shalmaneser_III) (reign 858-824 BC).[^cite_note-16]

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 57,692 in 12,184 households.[^cite_note-2006_West_Azerbaijan_Province-17] The following census in 2011 counted 69,049 people in 16,407 households.[^cite_note-2011_West_Azerbaijan_Province-18] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 91,515 people in 23,468 households.[^cite_note-2016_West_Azerbaijan_Province-3][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

According to Piranshahr's organization for Civil Registration, the highest average annual growth rate in West Azerbaijan province is in Piranshahr.[^cite_note-22]

## Climate

## Law and government

The city's chief administrator is the mayor, who is elected by the municipal board of the city. According to Iranian laws the municipal board is periodically elected by the city residents.

Mayor: Safer Tefakhur[^cite_note-25]

Governor: Kakderwishi[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]

Friday prayer: Saleh Hashimabadi[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

Majlis representative: Kemal Hussainpur[^cite_note-30]

## Education

The main institutions of higher education in the area are [Payame Noor University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payame_Noor_University) and [Islamic Azad University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Azad_University).[^cite_note-piranshahr-iau-31] The city is also home to a number of conservatories and art schools.
