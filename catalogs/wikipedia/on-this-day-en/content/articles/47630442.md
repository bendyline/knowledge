**Islamic Iran Nation's Union Party** or **Nation's Union Party**[^cite_note-11] ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): حزب اتحاد ملت ایران اسلامی, *Hezb-e Ettehad-e Mellat-e Iran-e Eslami*) is an Iranian [reformist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_reform_movement) [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) founded in 2015.[^cite_note-Financial_Times-8]

## Establishment

In January 2015, it was confirmed by the [Ministry of Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_\(Iran\)) that it had received a request for a permit to establish a new political party. The reformists had carried out some six similar initiatives for party formation in the past six months.[^cite_note-securitymiddleeast.com-10] It was officially given permission on April 20, 2015.[^cite_note-BBC_Persian-2] The party’s declaration mentions that "a group of the nation’s children have gathered with the intention to take a new step toward the ideals of the revolution and the demands of the reform movement based on the constitution and the nation’s rights mentioned in its third chapter."[^cite_note-Ilna-12]

## Members

Most members of the party are former members of [Islamic Iran Participation Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Iran_Participation_Front), banned in 2009.[^cite_note-Islamic_Republic_News_Agency-1] The party was accused of being a "[front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front_organization)" for the banned [Islamic Iran Participation Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Iran_Participation_Front),[^cite_note-BBC_Persian-2][^cite_note-Radio_Zamaneh-9] which was denied by the party.[^cite_note-13]

The Central Council of the party consists of 30 members, including [Gholamreza Ansari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gholamreza_Ansari_\(born_1956\)), [Hamidreza Jalaeipour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamidreza_Jalaeipour), [Fatemeh Rakeei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatemeh_Rakeei) and [Jalal Jalalizadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalal_Jalalizadeh). Emadaddin Khatami, son of [Mohammad Khatami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Khatami), is also a member.[^cite_note-14]
