**Richard Sean Parnell** is a former [United States Army](32087.md) [Captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_\(United_States\)#Captain) and author currently serving as the [assistant to the secretary of defense for public affairs](18433915.md) since February 2025.[^cite_note-1] He was the Republican nominee for [Pennsylvania's 17th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania's_17th_congressional_district) in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania#District_17).[^cite_note-PG-2]

## Early life and education

Richard Sean Parnell was born in [Pittsburgh](25101.md), Pennsylvania.[^cite_note-Bal-3] After serving in the [U.S. Army](32087.md) from 2004 to 2010, he pursued undergraduate studies at [Clarion University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarion_University), the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), and [Duquesne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duquesne_University), as well as graduate education at Duquesne University.[^cite_note-Bal-3]

## Military service

After receiving his commission through the Army [Reserve Officers' Training Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reserve_Officers'_Training_Corps) program at Duquesne University, Parnell led 3rd Platoon, B Company, [2nd Battalion, 87th Infantry Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Battalion,_87th_Infantry_Regiment), [3rd Brigade Combat Team, 10th Mountain Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_Brigade_Combat_Team,_10th_Mountain_Division_\(United_States\)).[^cite_note-Army-Mar2012-4] The company was based near the [Pakistani](23235.md) border in [Barmal District](14912368.md) of [Paktika Province](457294.md) on an extended 16-month deployment. After the deployment, Parnell was awarded two [Bronze Star Medals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Star_Medal) and a [Purple Heart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_Heart).[^cite_note-PPG-Feb2012-5] Parnell left active duty as a lieutenant and was discharged as a captain from the [Individual Ready Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individual_Ready_Reserve). He authored the book *[Outlaw Platoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlaw_Platoon)* with John R. Bruning based on his experiences.

## Political career

Parnell ran for [Pennsylvania's 17th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania's_17th_congressional_district) in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Pennsylvania#District_17), narrowly losing to [Democratic](5043544.md) incumbent [Conor Lamb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_Lamb).[^cite_note-PG-2][^cite_note-Ballot17-6]

The following year, Parnell announced he would run in the [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_Senate_election_in_Pennsylvania) [Republican](32070.md) primary for Pennsylvania's open senate seat. He was endorsed by [Donald Trump](4848272.md).[^cite_note-Cnn1-7] Parnell suspended his candidacy in November 2021, amid allegations, by his estranged wife, that he had abused her and her children[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

In February 2025, President Trump announced via [Truth Social](69077351.md) that Parnell would serve as the chief [Pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pentagon) spokesman under his administration.[^cite_note-10] In addition to his primary role as assistant to the secretary of defense for public affairs, Parnell also serves as a senior advisor to Secretary of Defense [Pete Hegseth](34076202.md).[^cite_note-11] In April 2026, *[New York Post](102227.md)* and *[The Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hill_\(newspaper\))* reported that Parnell has privately expressed interest in the position of Army Secretary [Daniel P. Driscoll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_P._Driscoll), amid rumors of tension between Driscoll and Hegseth.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]
