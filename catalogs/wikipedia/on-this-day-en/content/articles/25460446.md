**Ndu** is a town and [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Cameroon) in [Donga-Mantung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donga-Mantung), a division in the [Northwest Region](646814.md) of [Cameroon](5447.md). It lies at the northeast edge of the [Bamenda Grassfields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamenda_Grassfields), on the eastern arc of the Ring Road.[^cite_note-map-1] It is the highest elevation town in Cameroon.

About 85,000 people live in Ndu commune. Most are Wimbum - the three clans which speak the [Limbum language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limbum_language). Ndu commune contains the southeast part of Wimbum-land, including the villages of Talla, Ngarum, Taku, Ntundip, Luh, Ndu-town, Mbipgo, Njimkang, Njilah, Wowo, Sehn, Ntumbaw, Njirong, Ngulu, Nseh Macop, Sinna and Sop.[^cite_note-Ndu-2] [Nkambe Central commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nkambe) contains the rest of Wimbum-land.[^cite_note-3] Most Wimbum are farmers, raising [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [beans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beans), [potatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potato), [yams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yam_\(vegetable\)), [njama-njama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solanum_scabrum), [tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomatoes), [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee), [plantains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plantain_\(cooking\)), and [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice). Most soils are rich and the rain is generally sufficient for good crops.[^cite_note-Ndu-2] At the south end of Ndu commune is the Ndu Tea Estate, the largest tea plantation in Cameroon.[^cite_note-4] Some people raise cattle, horses, goats, sheep, and fowls.[^cite_note-Ndu-2]

Ndu town is the administrative headquarters of the commune. The town includes a large market, hotels, schools, two hospitals(Government and Baptist Hospitals), a [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie), and the Cameroon [Baptist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptist) [Theological Seminary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theological_Seminary).[^cite_note-Ndu-2]

Ndu lies in Cameroon's [Western High Plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_High_Plateau).[^cite_note-map-1] The land is quite hilly, with elevations ranging from 700 m on the Mbaw plain, to cool grassy highlands like Talla at 2,200 m. Important tree species include [mahogany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahogany), [iroko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iroko) and [sapele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapele).[^cite_note-Ndu-2] The average weather ranges from 7 °C to 20 °C throughout the year.

## Geography

### Climate

Situated at 2,057 m (6,749 ft) above sea level, the town of Ndu has a [subtropical highland climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanic_climate#Subtropical_highland_variety_\(Cfb,_Cwb\)) (*Cwb*, according to the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification)), with mild temperatures throughout the year. The rainfall is abundant, with a short dry season between December and February.
