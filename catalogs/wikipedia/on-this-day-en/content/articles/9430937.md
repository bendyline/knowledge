The **Government of the Democratic Republic of the Congo**, also known as the **Cabinet**, is the junior institution in the [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_branch) of the [central authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_government) governing the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md), the senior institution being the [President of the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo). The President is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) whereas the [Prime Minister](8699891.md) is the [head of government](37570.md).

## Description

Under Article 90 of the [Constitution of the Third Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo),[^cite_note-const-1] the government is composed of the Prime Minister, of ministers, of deputy ministers; and as the case may be, of deputy prime ministers, of ministers of state, and of minister delegates. The number of these ministers vary from one government to the next.

The government is headed by the Prime Minister, appointed by the President, from the political party, the group or the coalition that holds the majority of seats in the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo), the lower chamber of Parliament.

The government is the effective executive arm of the state, in charge of all the country's main administration, in all the domains reserved to the central government by the constitution, and in all the domains in which the central government has concurrent jurisdiction with the provinces.&#x20;

The government is accountable to the [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo). Any individual member of the government, as well the entire government, can be censored by its lower-chamber, the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo), through the vote of a motion of censure, or a motion of no-confidence. When the Prime minister is censured, the entire government is asked to step down.[^cite_note-2]

## Current government

The current government is the [Suminwa government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suminwa_cabinet), under the leadership of Prime Minister [Judith Suminwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judith_Suminwa). Suminwa assumed office on 12 June 2024,[^cite_note-3] and her cabinet was announced on 29 May 2024.[^cite_note-4]
