# List of most expensive paintings



*[Salvator Mundi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvator_Mundi_\(Leonardo\))* attributed to Italian artist [Leonardo da Vinci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_da_Vinci) (c. 1500)

This is a list of the highest known [prices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prices) paid for [paintings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paintings). The record payment for a work is approximately [US\$](18717338.md)450.3 million (which includes [commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_\(remuneration\))) for the work *[Salvator Mundi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvator_Mundi_\(Leonardo\))* (c. 1500) generally considered to be by [Leonardo da Vinci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_da_Vinci), though this is disputed. The painting was sold in November 2017,[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] through the auction house [Christie's](155688.md) in New York City.

## Background

The most famous paintings, especially [old master](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_master) works created before 1803, are generally owned or held by [museums](37585.md) for viewing by patrons. Since museums rarely sell them, they are considered priceless. *[Guinness World Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Records)* lists Leonardo da Vinci's *[Mona Lisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mona_Lisa)* as having the highest [insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurance) value for a painting. On permanent display at the [Louvre](17546.md) in Paris, the *Mona Lisa* was assessed at US\$100 million on 14 December 1962.[^cite_note-gwr82-3] Taking only [inflation](38286.md) into account, the 1962 value would be around US\$1.1 billion in 2025.[^cite_note-inflation-US-4]

The earliest sale on the list below (*[Vase with Fifteen Sunflowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunflowers_\(series_of_paintings\))* by [Vincent van Gogh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_van_Gogh)) is from March 1987; with a price of £24.75 million (£71.6 million in 2025 currency). This sale tripled the previous record, and introduced a new era in top art sales. Before this, the highest absolute price paid for a painting was £8.1 million (£24.2 million in 2025 currency) paid by the [J. Paul Getty Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._Paul_Getty_Museum) for [Andrea Mantegna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Mantegna)'s *[Adoration of the Magi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adoration_of_the_Magi_\(Mantegna\))* at [Christie's](155688.md) in London on 18 April 1985.[^cite_note-wilson-5] The sale of Vincent van Gogh's *Sunflowers* was the first time a "modern" (in this case 1888) painting became the record holder. [Old master paintings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_master_painting) had previously dominated the market.[^cite_note-gwr82-3] In contrast, there are currently only nine pre-1875 paintings among the listed top 89, and none created between 1635 and 1874.

&#x20;An exceptional case is graffiti artist [David Choe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Choe), who accepted payment in [shares](867877.md) for painting graffiti art in the headquarters of a fledgling [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook,_Inc.). His shares were of limited value when he was given them, but by the time of Facebook's [IPO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPO) they were valued at around \$200 million.[^cite_note-NYT_Facebook-6]&#x20;



*[The Red Vineyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red_Vineyard)* (1888), the only painting the Dutch painter Vincent van Gogh sold during his lifetime which is documented. It was sold for 400 francs (US\$2,765 in 2025).

The list is incomplete with respect to sales between private parties, as these are not always reported and, even if they are, details like the purchase price may remain secret. For example, on June 25, 2019, the American hedge fund manager [J. Tomilson Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._Tomilson_Hill) bought a recently rediscovered *Judith and Holofernes* (1607) attributed to [Caravaggio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caravaggio), two days before it would have been auctioned in [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse).[^cite_note-rpob-7] Though the [Louvre](17546.md) Museum had turned down the opportunity to purchase it for €100 million,[^cite_note-8] the painting was estimated to sell for \$110 to \$170 million.[^cite_note-9] The actual purchase price was not disclosed, because of a confidentiality agreement attached to the private sale.[^cite_note-rpob-7] Another example is a 2019 sale of *[The Seated Zouave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Zouave)* by Vincent van Gogh. According to some sources, the painting had been sold by Argentine art collector Nelly Arrieta de Blaquier for \$300 million,[^cite_note-10] but the price was not confirmed by any of the parties involved.[^cite_note-11]

Vincent van Gogh, [Pablo Picasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_Picasso), and [Andy Warhol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Warhol) are the best-represented artists in the list. Whereas Picasso and Warhol became wealthy men, van Gogh is known to have sold only one painting in his lifetime, *[The Red Vineyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red_Vineyard)*, for 400 [French francs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_franc) (approximately \$2,900 in 2026 U.S. dollars) in 1890, to the Belgian impressionist painter and heiress [Anna Boch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Boch).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] [Frida Kahlo](162276.md) holds the record for the highest price paid for a painting by a woman. On November 21, 2025 at Sotheby's, an unnamed buyer acquired 1940's "El Sueño" for US\$54.7 million [^cite_note-14]beating the 2014's purchase by the [Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_Bridges_Museum_of_American_Art) of the 1932 painting [*Jimson Weed/White Flower No. 1*](37644592.md) by [Georgia O'Keeffe](311615.md) for US \$44.4 million (equivalent to US\$60.4 million in 2025).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

In 2025, Frida Kahlo broke the previously held record by Georgia O'Keeffe for the highest price paid for a painting by a woman.[^cite_note-17]

Among the listed top paintings, only seven are by non-Western artists. Five are traditional [Chinese paintings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_painting) by [Qi Baishi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qi_Baishi), [Wu Bin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wu_Bin_\(painter\)), [Wang Meng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Meng_\(painter\)) and [Xu Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xu_Yang_\(Qing_dynasty\)). In particular, Qi Baishi's *[Twelve Landscape Screens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve_Landscape_Screens)* was sold for \$140.8 million in 2017. The only non-Western [modern](163543.md) artwork listed is that of the Chinese-French painter [Zao Wouki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zao_Wou-Ki)'s oil painting *Juin-Octobre 1985*, which was sold for \$65 million in 2018.[^cite_note-\:3-18] Also included in this list is Chinese painter Wang Shaofei's *The High Sun*, which was sold at an estimated price of \$74 million in 2017.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-Shaofei2-20]
