**Hamrat El Sheikh** or **Hamrat al-Sheikh** is a town in [North Kordofan](767669.md), Sudan.

## History

In October 2024, 30 people were killed and more than a hundred were injured in an airstrike.[^cite_note-1]

On 4 December 2025, a [World Food Programme](33582.md) (WFP) convoy was attacked in Hamrat El Sheikh, damaging a truck and injuring its driver.[^cite_note-2]
