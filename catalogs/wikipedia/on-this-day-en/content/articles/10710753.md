**Algeciras** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[alxeˈsiɾas\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a town and municipality in the [Huila Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huila_Department), [Colombia](5222.md).
