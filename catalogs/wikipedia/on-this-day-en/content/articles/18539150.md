Oil and gas basins of [Kazakhstan](16642.md)

The **oil and gas basins of Kazakhstan** can be grouped into four revealed or prospective oil and gas provinces in [the Republic of Kazakhstan](16642.md). Exploration in those provinces in which oil and gas has already been extracted had, by 2010, led to the discovery of more than 200 [oil](23195.md), [gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_gas_field), oil-and-gas and [condensate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_gas_condensate) hydrocarbon accumulations. Of these, the [Kashagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashagan_Field), the [Tengiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tengiz_Field) and the [Karachaganak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachaganak_Field) fields can be considered giants.[^cite_note-1]

## Oil and gas basins of Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan has a share in four oil and gas provinces:

1\. The *[Pre-Caspian Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-Caspian_Basin?action=edit\&redlink=1)* lies in the western part of the country, behind the [Mugalzhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mugalzhar) mountains. The geology of this province is made up of [Paleozoic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleozoic) sediments covering a [Proterozoic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proterozoic) basement.

- Southern Emba

- Northern Emba

- Eastern Emba

- Primorsk-Emba

- Northern Pre-Caspian

- Western Pre-Caspian

- Northern Caspian

- Central Pre-Caspian

2\. The *[Mangistau-Usturt Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangistau-Usturt_Basin?action=edit\&redlink=1)* lies in the Mangistau and [Aktobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aktobe) areas of Kazakhstan.

- [South Mangyshlak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Mangyshlak_oil_and_gas_basin?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- Northern Buzashi

- Northern Usturt

3\. The *[Central Kazakhstan Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Kazakhstan_Basin?action=edit\&redlink=1)* lies in the eastern and southern areas of Kazakhstan.

- Southern Torgay

- Eastern Aral

- Syr-Darya

- Chu-Sarysu

- Teniz

- Alakol

- Balkash

- Zaysan

- Ily

4\. The *[Western Siberian Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Siberian_Basin?action=edit\&redlink=1)* is in the northern and northeastern region of Kazakhstan, north of the Kokshetauskikh mountains. The geology is of a platform type, with a [Mesozoic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesozoic) cover overlying a Paleozoic basement.

- Northern Kazakhstan

- Irtish

The basins are located in six provinces of Kazakhstan: Aktobe, Atyrau, West Kazakhstan, Karaganda, Kyzylorda and Mangystau provinces.[^cite_note-2]
