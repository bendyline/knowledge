**MGX Fund Management Limited** (**MGX**) is an [Emirati](69328.md) state-owned investment firm for [artificial intelligence](1164.md) (AI) technologies. Established by the [government of Abu Dhabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Abu_Dhabi) in 2024, MGX aims to become a vehicle for AI-driven investments with a target of managing US\$100 billion in assets.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2][^cite_note-\:2-3]

## History

MGX was established in 2024 by the government of Abu Dhabi to channel investments into AI-driven technologies and startups and to foster innovation and drive economic growth within the region.[^cite_note-\:0-1] MGX was launched in March by two entities: global investment company [Mubadala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mubadala_Investment_Company) and an artificial intelligence firm [G42](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G42_\(company\)). MGX's board chair is Sheikh [Tahnoun bin Zayed Al Nahyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahnoun_bin_Zayed_Al_Nahyan_\(national_security_advisor\)) and [Khaldoon Al Mubarak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khaldoon_Al_Mubarak) is the vice chair.[^cite_note-\:2-3][^cite_note-4] In September 2024, MGX, [BlackRock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BlackRock), and [Microsoft](19001.md) announced the *Global AI Infrastructure Investment Partnership*, an AI investment fund that will focus on data centers and their power infrastructure.[^cite_note-5]

In January 2025, MGX partnered with [OpenAI](48795986.md), [SoftBank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SoftBank_Group), and [Oracle](22591.md) on the [Stargate Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stargate_LLC).[^cite_note-6] In March 2025, MGX made a minority investment in [Binance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binance), using [World Liberty Financial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Liberty_Financial)'s [Stablecoin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stablecoin) for the \$2 billion investment.[^cite_note-7] The transaction was announced by the [Trump family](52170275.md), with *The New York Times* calling the announcement "a public and vivid illustration of the ethical conflicts swirling around [Mr. Trump](4848272.md)’s crypto firm."[^cite_note-8]

MGX, as of July 2025, was working with [Mistral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mistral_AI) and [Nvidia](39120.md) to build the largest AI data center campus in Europe.[^cite_note-9] MGX had announced in early 2025 that it planned to invest US\$31.2 billion to US\$52 billion in the new data center campus in France, to have a one-gigawatt capacity focused on AI.[^cite_note-tech-10]

On September 25, 2025, Trump approved a deal where [TikTok](56822861.md) in the United States would be controlled by a new US-controlled entity with Oracle, Silver Lake, and MGX collectively holding around 45% of the company and ByteDance retaining less than 20%.[^cite_note-11] The new company [TikTok USDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok_USDS) was established in January 2026.

In October 2025, a consortium involving MGX, AIP, and BlackRock's GIP agreed to acquired Aligned Data Centers for \$40 billion[^cite_note-12] from Macquarie Asset Management.[^cite_note-13] It was reported as the largest global data center deal to date.[^cite_note-14]

In June 2026, MGX raised close to \$50 billion from regional and global investors for a fund to accelerate spending on AI infrastructure and technology.[^cite_note-15]

## Operations

MGX operates primarily within the global AI technology sector, focusing on identifying and investing in high-potential AI startups and established companies[^cite_note-\:1-2][^cite_note-\:2-3] and has AI ventures across North America, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.[^cite_note-\:2-3][^cite_note-16] Its website in 2025 described three related areas of focus with investments: semiconductor companies, AI infrastructure companies, and AI technology companies.[^cite_note-17]
