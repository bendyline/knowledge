[Najib Razak](523641.md) formed the **second Najib cabinet** after being invited by [Tuanku Abdul Halim Muadzam Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Halim_of_Kedah) to begin a new government following the [5 May 2013 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysian_general_election,_2013) in [Malaysia](3607937.md). In order to be the prime minister, Najib sworn in before the Yang di-Pertuan Agong on 6 May 2013.[^cite_note-1] Prior to the election, Najib led (as [prime minister](496731.md)) the [first Najib cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Najib_cabinet), a [coalition government](6036.md) that consisted of members of the component parties of [Barisan Nasional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barisan_Nasional).

A new cabinet was announced by Najib on 15 May 2013.[^cite_note-pmo-cabinet13-2] It was the 19th [cabinet of Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Malaysia#List_of_cabinets) formed since [independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_\(Malaysia\)). The ministers and deputy ministers were then sworn in before [Yang di-Pertuan Agong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_di-Pertuan_Agong) [Abdul Halim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Halim_Muadzam_Shah_ibni_al-Marhum_Sultan_Badishah) on the following day.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Notably, the two main ethnic Chinese-majority parties in Barisan Nasional, the [Malaysian Chinese Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysian_Chinese_Association) (MCA) and [Gerakan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parti_Gerakan_Rakyat_Malaysia) declined to join the cabinet due to their dismal performance in the election.[^cite_note-5]

On 25 June 2014, Najib announced a [cabinet reshuffle](845103.md), which saw the return of the MCA and Gerakan to the cabinet.[^cite_note-6]

After the [1MDB scandal](47550643.md) occurred in 2015, Deputy Prime Minister [Muhyiddin Yassin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhyiddin_Yassin), who criticized this issue, was removed from his position by Prime Minister Najib.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] Najib faced public demands for resignation amid allegations of corruption.[^cite_note-9]
