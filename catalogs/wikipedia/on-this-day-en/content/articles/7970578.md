**Bhiwandi** ([pronunciation](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/f/f9/Bhiwandi.ogg/Bhiwandi.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City) in the [Thane district](638817.md) of [Maharashtra](20629.md), India. It is located 20 kilometres (12 mi) northeast of [Mumbai](19189.md), and 15 km (9.3 mi) northeast of the city of [Thane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thane). The city is a part of the [Mumbai Metropolitan Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Metropolitan_Region).

Bhiwandi is a commercial city and a major trade centre that connects Mumbai and the rest of India through the [Mumbai–Agra Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_3_\(India,_old_numbering\)). It is known for its textile industry, though in recent years, the economic downturn has forced the closure of a large portion of the sector.[^cite_note-1]

Bhiwandi lies in the [Konkani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konkan) coastal lowland, a region known for its hills and streams. The city houses the [tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehsil) headquarters of Bhiwandi, and it is administered by the [Bhiwandi-Nizampur City Municipal Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhiwandi-Nizampur_City_Municipal_Corporation).

## Business and employment

The city of Bhiwandi has the largest number of [power looms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_loom) and handlooms in the country.[^cite_note-frontline-2][^cite_note-hinduaug-3][^cite_note-toijuly-4] The majority of the population is employed in the power loom sector.[^cite_note-5] The second-largest power loom cluster, after the one in [Surat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surat), [Gujarat](53707.md), is located in Bhiwandi. Since 2012, the city's textile industry has been in decline, partly caused by the sudden enactment of new goods and services taxes.[^cite_note-6] Another concern facing textile producers is a lack of contact with buyers, leading many to sell primarily to middlemen, mostly from Gujarat, who charge significant markup prices.[^cite_note-7]

Bhiwandi initially developed as an industrial hub for the textile industry, but more recently has hosted other industries and logistics sectors. Bhiwandi is among Asia's biggest warehousing hubs; its [godowns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godown) are considered Asia's largest.[^cite_note-economictimes.indiatimes.com-8] It is a major contributor to the logistic landscape of Mumbai and India due to its close proximity to the Nhava Seva port of Mumbai, India's financial capital. Many e-commerce companies like [Delhivery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhivery), [Amazon](90451.md), [Flipkart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flipkart), [Reliance Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reliance_Industries), [Snapdeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snapdeal), and [FedEx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FedEx) have branches in the city. The city is the next developed region for [MMRDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Metropolitan_Region_Development_Authority), which has developed other regions such as the [Bandra–Kurla Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandra_Kurla_Complex), [Mumbai Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_Metro), Monorail Project, and Eastern Freeway.[^cite_note-9]

Bhiwandi's economy is divided into three sectors: textiles, groceries, and services (supplying, logistics, and food delivery). During the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), small hotels faced significant challenges.[^cite_note-10]

## Infrastructure

Bhiwandi has a large water treatment plant at Panjrapur with 455 MLD capacity, making it one of the largest water treatment plants in Asia.[^cite_note-11]

Bhiwandi has a central railway station at Anjur Phata. The station is connected to Central Railway (Kopar) and Western Railway (Vasai) the Mumbai suburban railway network.

[Bhiwandi Metro Project (Mumbai Metro line 5)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_5_\(Mumbai_Metro\)) is proposed to be completed in 2025.[^cite_note-12]

## Municipal finance

According to financial data published on the CityFinance Portal of the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs,[^cite_note-13] the Bhiwandi Municipal Corporation reported total revenue receipts of ₹509 crore (US\$61 million) and total expenditure of ₹508 crore (US\$61 million) in 2022–23. Tax revenue accounted for about 19.3% of the total revenue, while the corporation received ₹331 crore in grants during the financial year.
