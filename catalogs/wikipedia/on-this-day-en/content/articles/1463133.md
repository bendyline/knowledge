**Kidal** ([Tuareg Berber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuareg_language): ⴾⴸⵍ, KDL, Kidal) is a town and [commune](16112417.md) in the desert region of northern [Mali](19127.md). The town lies 285 km (177 mi) northeast of [Gao](1255721.md) and is the capital of the [Kidal Cercle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidal_Cercle) and the [Kidal Region](2214311.md). The commune has an area of about 9,910 km<sup>2</sup> (3,830 sq mi) and includes the town of Kidal and 31 other settlements.[^cite_note-2]

## History

The Kidal region has been inhabited by the Tuareg for centuries. The modern town grew up around a French military post established in the early 20th century.[^cite_note-3]

On 30 March 2012, Kidal and its military base were captured by the [National Movement for the Liberation of Azawad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Movement_for_the_Liberation_of_Azawad) as part of the [Tuareg rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Tuareg_rebellion) for the independence of [Azawad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azawad). A spokesman for the [Malian military junta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Malian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) said "To preserve the life of the people of Kidal, the military command decided not to prolong the battle".[^cite_note-4] Gao and [Timbuktu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbuktu) were captured within the next 48 hours, and on 6 April, the National Movement for the Liberation of Azawad declared the independence of Azawad from [Mali](19127.md).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] In the course of the conflict the MNLA lost their control to Islamist militias.[^cite_note-7] On 30 January 2013 [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Serval) and [Malian forces](19135.md) moved into the town to bring it back under government control.[^cite_note-8]

On 14 December 2013, a [car bombing](155282.md) in Kidal killed two [United Nations peacekeepers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Multidimensional_Integrated_Stabilization_Mission_in_Mali).[^cite_note-9]

On 21 May 2014, MNLA forced government troops in Kidal to retreat after heavy fighting, capturing the military base.[^cite_note-10]

On 13 February 2020, [Mali government forces](19135.md) were sent to Kidal for the first time in six years.[^cite_note-11] In late 2023, the ruling junta carried out [an offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidal_offensive) along with [Wagner Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagner_Group) fighters. They managed to bring the city back under government control in the [Battle of Kidal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kidal_\(2023\)) in November 2023.[^cite_note-12]

In April 2026, the [Azawad Liberation Front](78996050.md) launched an assault on the city as part of a [coordinated offensive](83033878.md) across the country.[^cite_note-13] The rebels later reported that they had captured the city and posted a video of rebels in the governor's residence.[^cite_note-aj2-14]

The population of the city of Kidal has grown from 11,159 in 1998 to 25,969 in 2009, raising its percentage in the [Kidal Region](2214311.md) from 26.3% to 38.3%.

Kidal city is mainly [Tamasheq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamasheq_language) speaking with 79.13% of the city speaking it in 2009. The second most spoken language in Kidal city is [Songhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songhai_language) with 7.18% of the city speaking it. Other minority languages include [Bambara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambara_language) spoken by 5.51% of the city, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) spoken by 3.78% of the city, and other minority languages spoken by 4.4%.

## Climate

[Köppen-Geiger climate classification system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen-Geiger_climate_classification_system) classifies it climate as [hot desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_desert_climate) (BWh), with extremely hot weather most of the year.
