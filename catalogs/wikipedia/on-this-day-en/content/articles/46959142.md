The **Vice Chairmen of the Central Military Commission** are deputies to the [chairman of the Central Military Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Central_Military_Commission_\(China\)), who is the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_command_of_the_armed_forces_in_the_People's_Republic_of_China) of the [People's Liberation Army](66890.md) (PLA), the [People's Armed Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Armed_Police) (PAP) and the [Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militia_\(China\)).

There are technically two offices with the same name, including the vice chairman of the [Chinese Communist Party](7175.md) (CCP) CMC and vice chairman of the People's Republic of China (PRC) CMC. However, under the arrangement of "[one institution with two names](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_institution_with_two_names)", they function as one office.[^cite_note-\:11-1] According to military regulations, the vice chairmen are granted the rank of general by the virtue of their posts.[^cite_note-2] Since 1997, the vice chairmen have served as the only members of the [CCP Politburo](51851.md) holding military rank.[^cite_note-\:22-3]

According to the [state constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_China), the vice state CMC vice chairmen are elected by the [National People's Congress](38878740.md) after the nomination of the CMC chairman.[^cite_note-\:0-4] According to the [CCP constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party), the Party CMC vice chairmen are officially elected by the CCP's [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party).[^cite_note-\:1-5] Currently, [Zhang Shengmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Shengmin) is currently serving as the vice chairman.
