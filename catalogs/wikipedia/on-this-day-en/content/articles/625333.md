This page is a **list of [French education ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Education_\(France\))**.

A governmental position overseeing public education was first created in [France](5843419.md) in 1802. Following the various regime changes in France in the first decades of the 19th century, the position changed official status and name a number of times before the position of **Minister of Public Instruction** was created in 1828. For much of its history, the position was combined with that of [Minister of Public Worship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Public_Worship), who dealt with issues related to the [Roman Catholic Church](606848.md), except in instances where the Minister of Public Instruction was a [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism). The position has also occasionally been combined with [Minister of Sports](3374119.md) and Minister of Youth Affairs. In 1932, the office's title was changed to **Minister of National Education**, although it was briefly changed back in 1940–1941, and was renamed **Minister of Education** during the presidency of [Valéry Giscard d'Estaing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val%C3%A9ry_Giscard_d'Estaing) (1974–1981).
