The **mass media in Iran** is both privately and publicly owned but all channels are [subject to censorship](4297092.md). In 2016, Iran had [178 newspapers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_newspapers), [83 magazines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Iranian_magazines), 15,000 information sites and [two million blogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_blogs).[^cite_note-1] The [Islamic Revolutionary Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Revolutionary_Court) has authority to monitor the print media and may suspend publication or revoke the licenses of papers or journals that a jury finds guilty of publishing material deemed anti-religious, [slanderous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slanderous) or detrimental to the [national interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_interest).

The [Mass Media Regulatory Authority Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_Media_Regulatory_Authority_Organization) is one of the main regulatory controllers of mass media in Iran.

## Newspapers

Most [Iranian newspapers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Iranian_newspapers) are published in Persian, but newspapers in English and other languages also exist. The most widely circulated periodicals are based in [Tehran](57654.md). Popular daily and weekly newspapers include *[Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_\(newspaper\))*, *[Ettelaat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ettelaat)*, *[Kayhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayhan)*, *[Hamshahri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamshahri)* and *[Resalat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resalat_\(newspaper\))*. *[Iran Daily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_Daily)* and *[Tehran Times](5997270.md)* are both English language papers.[^cite_note-BYI-2] Iran’s largest media corporation is [Islamic Republic of Iran Broadcasting](819903.md) (IRIB).[^cite_note-BYI-2] The *[Financial Tribune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Tribune)* is the main (online) [English language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) [economic](64704.md) journal. Iran Front Page (IFP News) is an English News website which provides its audiences with the English version of the latest news and views from Iran published by the Iranian [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language)-language media.[^cite_note-3]

## Foreign broadcasts

A number of foreign broadcasts are transmitted into the country, including [Persian language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) programmes from [BBC Persian TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Persian_Television), [Kol Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kol_Israel) and [Radio Farda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Farda); however, these broadcasts are occasionally countered by [radio jamming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_jamming).[^cite_note-4] The government engages in [censorship programs](4297092.md) with respect to anything that diverges from national laws and regulations.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The majority of [Iranians](14653.md)—upwards of 80 percent—get their news from government-owned media.[^cite_note-7] Attempts to establish private, independent media outlets in Iran have been restricted or quashed, and [Reporters Without Borders](526054.md) has declared Iran to have the highest number of jailed journalists in the Middle East. Under the 1979 [Iranian Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Constitution), all broadcasting must exclusively be government-operated and, in 1994, the Islamic Republic banned the use of satellite television.[^cite_note-samati-8] In spite of which, over 30 percent of Iranians do in fact watch [satellite television channels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_channel).[^cite_note-9]

Iranian media include [Iranian news agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Iranian_news_agencies), [Iranian newspapers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_newspapers_in_Iran), [Iranian blogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_blogs), [Persian-language magazines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Persian-language_magazines) and [Persian-language television stations (not all of which are actually Iranian)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Persian-language_television_channels).
