The **Ukraine Billie Jean King Cup team** represents Ukraine in the [Billie Jean King Cup](1108893.md) [tennis](29773.md) competition and are governed by the [Ukrainian Tennis Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Tennis_Federation?action=edit\&redlink=1). They currently compete in the [2025 Billie Jean King Cup Europe/Africa Zone Group I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_Europe/Africa_Zone_Group_I_%E2%80%93_play-offs?action=edit\&redlink=1).

## History

&#x20;Ukraine competed in its first Fed Cup in [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Federation_Cup_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-o245-1] Their best result is reaching the World Group in [2010 Fed Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Fed_Cup) and [2012 Fed Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Fed_Cup).
