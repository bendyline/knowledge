**Shamir Medical Center**, formerly **Assaf Harofeh Medical Center**, is a [teaching hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching_hospital) in [Be'er Ya'akov](3085967.md), 15 kilometres (9.3 mi) southeast of [Tel Aviv](31453.md), [Israel](9282173.md).[^cite_note-netguide-2]

## History

The Medical Center was named after [Asaph the Jew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asaph_the_Jew), author of the [Oath of Asaph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oath_of_Asaph) and an early medical text.[^cite_note-netguide-2][^cite_note-3] The facility was established in 1918 as a [military hospital](2359260.md) of the [British Army](4887.md) in the closing days of the [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_World_War).[^cite_note-netguide-2] It was located adjacent to the sprawling British military base in [Tzrifin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tzrifin) (Sarafand). After the creation of the State of Israel, it was converted to an Israeli hospital.[^cite_note-netguide-2]

The hospital was renamed after the former Israeli prime minister [Yitzhak Shamir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yitzhak_Shamir) in April 2017.[^cite_note-4]

## Services

It is one of Israel's largest hospitals, with over 800 beds.[^cite_note-netguide-2][^cite_note-google12-5] It serves over 370,000 people in Israel's [Central District](844282.md).[^cite_note-netguide-2][^cite_note-google12-5] As a teaching facility, the hospital is part of the [Sackler Faculty of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sackler_Faculty_of_Medicine) of [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University).[^cite_note-netguide-2] On its grounds are the first and largest Israeli academic nursing school and the oldest Israeli school of [physiotherapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physiotherapy).[^cite_note-netguide-2]
