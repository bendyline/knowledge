**Jhapa 5** is one of five parliamentary constituencies of [Jhapa District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhapa_District) in [Nepal](171166.md). This constituency came into existence on the Constituency Delimitation Commission (CDC) report submitted on 31 August 2017.[^cite_note-1]

## Incorporated areas

Jhapa 5 incorporates [Damak Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damak_Municipality), [Kamal Rural Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamal_Rural_Municipality), wards 1–8 of [Gauradaha Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauradaha) and wards 3–6 of [Gauriganj Rural Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaurigunj).

## Assembly segments

It encompasses the following [Province No. 1 Provincial Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_Assembly_of_Province_No._1) segment

- Jhapa 5(A)

- Jhapa 5(B)
