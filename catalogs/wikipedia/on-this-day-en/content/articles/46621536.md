**Jon Guerry Burns**[^cite_note-1] (born September 4, 1952) is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_people) politician from the U.S. state of Georgia and the [speaker of the Georgia House of Representatives](59098819.md) since January 2023. Burns is a [Republican](32070.md) member of the [Georgia House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_House_of_Representatives) from [District 159](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia's_159th_House_of_Representatives_district). Burns previously served [District 157](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia's_157th_House_of_Representatives_district). Burns was the former Republican [majority leader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majority_leader) of [Georgia House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_House_of_Representatives), being succeeded by [Chuck Efstration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Efstration) upon his election as speaker of the House of Representatives. He was selected as the speaker in November 2022, succeeding [David Ralston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ralston) due to his death.[^cite_note-2]

## Early life

Burns was born in [Effingham County, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effingham_County,_Georgia). Burns graduated from [Effingham County High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effingham_County_High_School).[^cite_note-ga_burns-3][^cite_note-votesmart_burns-4][^cite_note-savannahnow_burns2015-5]

## Education

Burns earned a bachelor's degree in political science from [Georgia Southern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Southern_University). Burns earned a JD from [John Marshall Law School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta's_John_Marshall_Law_School).[^cite_note-votesmart_burns-4]

## Career

Burns served in the [Georgia Army National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Army_National_Guard).[^cite_note-votesmart_burns-4]

On November 2, 2004, Burns won the election and became a [Republican](32070.md) member of [Georgia House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_House_of_Representatives) for [District 157](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia's_157th_House_of_Representatives_district). Burns defeated Woodrow Lovett with 65.05% of the votes.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-savannahnow_burns2015-5] On November 7, 2006, as an incumbent, Burns won the election unopposed and continued serving [District 157](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia's_157th_House_of_Representatives_district).[^cite_note-7] On November 4, 2008, as an incumbent, Burns won the election unopposed and continued serving [District 157](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia's_157th_House_of_Representatives_district).[^cite_note-8] On November 2, 2010, as an incumbent, Burns won the election and continued serving [District 157](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia's_157th_House_of_Representatives_district). Burns defeated Elizabeth N. Johnson with 67.36% of the votes.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-ga_burns-3][^cite_note-votesmart_burns-4]

On November 6, 2012, Burns won the election unopposed and became a [Republican](32070.md) member of [Georgia House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_House_of_Representatives) for [District 159](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia's_159th_House_of_Representatives_district).[^cite_note-10] On November 4, 2014, as an incumbent, Burns won the election unopposed and continued serving [District 159](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia's_159th_House_of_Representatives_district).[^cite_note-11] On November 8, 2016, as an incumbent, Burns won the election unopposed and continued serving District 159.[^cite_note-12] On November 6, 2018, as an incumbent, Burns won the election unopposed and continued serving [District 159](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia's_159th_House_of_Representatives_district).[^cite_note-13] On November 3, 2020, as an incumbent, Burns won the election unopposed and continued serving [District 159](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia's_159th_House_of_Representatives_district).[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-ga_burns-3][^cite_note-votesmart_burns-4]

In 2015, Burns was elected as the majority leader of Georgia House of Representatives.[^cite_note-savannahnow_burns2015-5]

## Personal life

Burns's wife is Dayle Burns. They have two children. Burns and his family live in [Newington, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newington,_Georgia).[^cite_note-votesmart_burns-4]
