A **trade prohibition** is a restriction in which one country will not buy goods from another country unless certain standards are met or conditions are followed, such as [labor standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_labor_standards) and [environmental standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_standard). It is the opposite of a [trade preference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_preference). An example would be a ban on goods produced from [forced labor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forced_labor).
