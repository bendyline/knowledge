**Garuk** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): گروك)[^cite_note-4] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Sirik_County\)) of [Sirik County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirik_County), [Hormozgan](448064.md) province, [Iran](14653.md).

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 3,705 in 628 households, when it was a village in [Sirik Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirik_Rural_District) of the former [Biyaban District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biyaban_District) of [Minab County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minab_County).[^cite_note-2006_Hormozgan_Province-5] The following census in 2011 counted 4,243 people in 872 households,[^cite_note-2011_Hormozgan_Province-6] by which time the district had been separated from the county in the establishment of Sirik County. The rural district was transferred to the new Central District.[^cite_note-Sirik_County_Establishment-7] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 4,008 people in 943 households,[^cite_note-2016_Hormozgan_Province-2] when Garuk had been elevated to the status of a city.[^cite_note-Garuk_City-8]
