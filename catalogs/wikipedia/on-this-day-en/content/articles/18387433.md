The **Ministry of Foreign Affairs** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الشؤون الخارجية) is the [Sudanese cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Sudan) [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) which oversees the [foreign relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Sudan) of [Sudan](27421.md).
