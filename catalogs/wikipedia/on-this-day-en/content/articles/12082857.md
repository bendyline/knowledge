The **Minister of Defense** (防衛大臣, *Bōei Daijin*), or **Bōei-shō** (防衛相), is a member of the [Japanese cabinet](573784.md) and is the leader of the [Ministry of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defense_\(Japan\)), the executive department of the [Japanese Armed Forces](2785204.md). The minister of defense's position of command and authority over the military is second only to that of the [Prime Minister of Japan](24833.md), who is the commander-in-chief.

The minister of defense is appointed by the Prime Minister and is a member of the [National Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Council_\(Japan\)) and the [National Intelligence Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Intelligence_Council_\(Japan\)). The current Minister of Defense is [Shinjirō Koizumi](27407451.md), who took office on October 21, 2025.

## History

On 26 December 2007, the [Government of Japan](30858300.md) made the decision to reform its Defense Agency to the Ministry of Defense in the expectation to have a far-reaching effect on Japan's future military development.[^cite_note-1] The defense policy that has been pursued by Japan is based on the "Basic Policy for National Defense", which was adopted by the Cabinet in May 1957.[^cite_note-OverviewPDF-2] Japan's main goal of national defense is the prevention of indirect as well as direct aggression from outside enemies.

The Japanese government reformed the Defense Agency to the [Ministry of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defense_\(Japan\)) with a ceremony that was attended by then Prime Minister [Shinzō Abe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinz%C5%8D_Abe) and the then-new Minister of Defense [Fumio Kyuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fumio_Kyuma). The creation of the Ministry of Defense was in conjunction with Prime Minister Shinzō Abe's continued efforts to ensure a stronger image of the [Japan Self-Defense Forces](2785204.md) (JSDF). The bill in which to upgrade the Defense Agency to the Ministry of Defense was approved by the [House of Representatives](217778.md) (lower house) in November 2006, and the [House of Councillors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Councillors_\(Japan\)) (upper house) in mid-December 2006. Minister Kyuma personally attended a session in the House of Councillors and gave a speech after the bill was approved.[^cite_note-3]

On 11 September 2019, [Taro Kono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taro_Kono) became the first high-profile 'prime minister-ready' politician to head the Ministry of Defense. He has the strongest LDP factional backing of any defense minister thus far. His social media following is second only to Prime Minister [Shinzo Abe](2634434.md). He's a leading candidate for post-Abe premiership. Kono previously held the prominent role of foreign minister.[^cite_note-4]

## Chain of command

1. Prime Minister

2. Minister of Defense

3. Chief of Staff, Joint Staff

### Structure

The [Commander-in-Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-Chief) of the Japan Self-Defense Forces (JSDF), who does not formally constitute a uniformed military, is the [Prime Minister](24833.md). The [Emperor of Japan](10110.md) is a [constitutional monarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_monarch) who does not have political or military authority over the JSDF; that authority rests with the Prime Minister. However, the Emperor formally [appoints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Investiture) the Prime Minister to office. The Minister of Defense is responsible for the organization and formulating the national security policy. The budget request is drafted by the [Ministry of Finance](870389.md) and making its own legislative proposals to the [National Diet](163847.md).

The Minister of Defense is advised on every concern related to the duties of the [Japan Self-Defense Forces](2785204.md) by the [Chief of Staff, Joint Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Staff,_Joint_Staff).

## Allied occupation of Japan

Following the end of [World War II](32927.md), the [Armed Forces of the Empire of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_the_Empire_of_Japan) were disbanded and US forces took control. From 1950 to 1952, the [National Police Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Police_Reserve) led by [Minister of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_State) Takeo Ōhashi was formed. It was renamed as the National Safety Force in 1952. In 1952, the Coastal Safety Force, the waterborne counterpart of the National Police Reserve, was founded and led by the Commissioner of the Coastal Safety Force (保安庁長官) Tokutarō Kimura.

## Ministers with military experience

Although [Article 68](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Constitution%20of%20Japan#CHAPTER_V._THE_CABINET) of the Constitution states that all members of the Cabinet must be [civilians](145804.md), former military persons may be appointed Minister of Defense.

- [Raizo Matsuno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raizo_Matsuno) - served in the [Imperial Japanese Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_Navy) during World War II and rose to rank of [Lieutenant commander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_commander)

- [Yasuhiro Nakasone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasuhiro_Nakasone) - served in the [Imperial Japanese Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_Navy) during World War II and rose to rank of [Lieutenant commander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_commander) and worked as Paymaster

- [Sōsuke Uno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C5%8Dsuke_Uno) - served in the [Imperial Japanese Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_Army) during World War II and rose to rank of [Second Lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Lieutenant)

- [Soichiro Ito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soichiro_Ito_\(Japanese_politician\)) - served in the [Imperial Japanese Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_Army) during World War II and rose to rank of [Second Lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Lieutenant)

- [Shin Kanemaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shin_Kanemaru) - served briefly in the [Kwantung Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwantung_Army) as [Sergeant](219424.md) in 1937-1938 and was discharged due to illness

- [Satoshi Morimoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satoshi_Morimoto) - served in [Japan Air Self-Defense Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Air_Self-Defense_Force) and rose to rank as [Major](201920.md)

- [Gen Nakatani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gen_Nakatani) - served in [Japan Ground Self-Defense Force](2978818.md) as platoon leader and instructor with rank of [Lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant)

  - [Imperial Japanese Army Air Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_Army_Air_Service)
