**Myawaddy Township** ([S'gaw Karen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S'gaw_Karen_language): ရါမတံၤကီၢ်ဆၣ်, [Phlone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Pwo_language) မေဝ်ပ္တီကၞင့်; [Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): မြဝတီမြို့နယ်) is a [township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_Burma) of [Myawaddy District](15144411.md) in the [Kayin State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayin_State) of Burma (Myanmar). It is the only township in Myawaddy District. The administrative seat is the town of [Myawaddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myawaddy).

## Boundaries

Myawaddy Township borders on:[^cite_note-1]

- [Kyain Seikgyi Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyain_Seikgyi_Township) of [Kawkareik District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawkareik_District) to the southwest and west,

- [Kawkareik Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawkareik_Township) of [Kawkareik District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawkareik_District) to the west,

- [Hlaingbwe Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hlaingbwe_Township) of [Hpa-an District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hpa-an_District) to the northwest, and

- [Thailand](30128.md) to the northeast, east and south.
