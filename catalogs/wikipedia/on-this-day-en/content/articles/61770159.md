**Ras Al Khaimah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): رَأْس ٱلْخَيْمَة; /raʔs͜ ɪlˈxajma/, [Emirati Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emirati_Arabic) : \[räːs͜ ɪlχe̞ːmɛ] ), often referred to its initials **RAK**, is an industrial port city[^cite_note-3] and the largest city and capital[^cite_note-4] of the [Emirate of Ras Al Khaimah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emirate_of_Ras_Al_Khaimah) in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md). The city had a population of 191,753 people in 2025,[^cite_note-5] and is the [sixth-most populous city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_the_United_Arab_Emirates) in UAE after [Dubai](211583.md), [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md), [Sharjah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharjah), [Al Ain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Ain) and [Ajman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajman). The city is divided by a creek into two parts: old town in the west and [Al Nakheel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Nakheel) in the east.[^cite_note-6] The town is the successor to the Islamic era port and trading hub of [Julfar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julfar).

## Etymology

**Ras Al Khaimah** translates to "Headland of the Tent".[^cite_note-\:12-7] The Arabic word *Khaimah* (خَيْمَة) means 'tent', but also refers to the palm frond or *areesh* houses that were common in the area.[^cite_note-8]

## History

See also: [History of Ras Al Khaimah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Ras_Al_Khaimah), [Julfar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julfar), [Kush (Ras Al Khaimah)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kush_\(Ras_Al_Khaimah\)), and [History of the United Arab Emirates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_United_Arab_Emirates)

### Antiquity

Ras Al Khaimah has been the site of continuous human habitation for 7,000 years, with archaeological finds dating back to the [Neolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic).[^cite_note-9] The northern area of the city today known as Ras Al Khaimah was previously the location of the important Islamic era settlement and port of [Julfar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julfar).[^cite_note-TheNational_10-2015-10] Although Julfar and Ras Al Khaimah are often placed together, they are in fact two different settlements with distinctive histories. Julfar was abandoned in the early 17th Century as Ras Al Khaimah rose. Julfar itself had a predecessor settlement, [Kush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kush_\(Ras_Al_Khaimah\)), today located in suburbs to the north of the city.[^cite_note-\:7-11]

One of Ras Al Khaimah's most celebrated sons, [Ibn Majid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_ibn_M%C4%81jid), was a hugely influential seaman, navigator and cartographer,[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] and there is evidence in his writing that the town he came from was at that time known as Ras Al Khaimah,[^cite_note-\:12-7] that town having eclipsed Julfar as the principal port and settlement of the Shimal coast. Since 1507 the city fell under control of the [Portuguese Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Empire).[^cite_note-14]

### 18th to 19th centuries



Ras Al Khaimah during the [Persian Gulf campaign of 1809](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_Gulf_campaign_of_1809) in November 1809

In the early 18th century, the [Qawasim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qasimi) (singular Al Qasimi) established themselves in Ras Al Khaimah and [Sharjah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharjah_\(emirate\)) on the [Arabian Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Peninsula), growing to become a significant maritime force with holdings on both the Persian and Arabian coasts that frequently came into conflict with British flagged shipping.[^cite_note-auto-15]

In the aftermath of a series of attacks against shipping sailing under Omani flags and following 1809 monsoon season, the British mounted the [Persian Gulf campaign of 1809](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_Gulf_campaign_of_1809) against Ras Al Khaimah, in which the Al Qasimi fleet was largely destroyed. The British operation continued to [Lingeh](1337157.md) on the Persian coast which was, like the [Greater and Lesser Tunbs](931816.md) islands, administered by the Al Qasimi.[^cite_note-auto-15][^cite_note-16]

By the morning of 14 November, the military expedition was over and the British forces returned to their ships, having suffered light casualties of five killed and 34 wounded. Arab losses are unknown, but were probably significant, while the damage done to the Al Qasimi fleets was severe: a significant portion of their vessels had been destroyed.[^cite_note-17]

Following the 1809 campaign, an 1815 arrangement was made between the British and the Al Qasimi.[^cite_note-qdl1-18] However, by 1819 it was clear the arrangement had broken down and so in November of that year, the British embarked on a [second expedition against the Al Qasimi at Ras Al Khaimah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_Gulf_campaign_of_1819), led by Major-General [William Keir Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Grant_\(British_Army_officer\)).[^cite_note-19]

The force gathered off the coast of Ras Al Khaimah on 25 and 26 November and, on 2 and 3 December, troops were landed south of the town and set up batteries of guns and mortars and, on 5 December, the town was bombarded from both land and sea for a period of four days, until, on 9 December, the fortress and town of Ras Al Khaimah were stormed and found to be practically deserted. On the fall of Ras Al Khaimah, three cruisers were sent to blockade Rams to the North and this, too was found to be deserted and its inhabitants retired to the 'impregnable' hill-top [fort of Dhayah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhayah_Fort).[^cite_note-\:0-20]

The [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) landed a force at Rams on 18 December, which fought its way inland through date plantations to Dhayah Fort on the 19th. There, 398 men and another 400 women and children held out, without sanitation, water or effective cover from the sun, for three days under heavy fire from mortars and 12-pound cannon.[^cite_note-auto1-21]



The hilltop fort of Dhayah

The two 24-pound cannon from [HMS Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Liverpool_\(1814\)) which had been used to bombard Ras Al Khaimah from the landward side were once again pressed into use and dragged across the plain from Rams, a journey of some four miles. Each of the guns weighed over 2 tonnes. After enduring two hours of sustained fire from the big guns, which breached the fort's walls, the last of the Al Qasimi surrendered at 10.30 on the morning of 22 December.[^cite_note-auto1-21]

In January 1820, the British imposed the [General Maritime Treaty of 1820](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Maritime_Treaty_of_1820) signed by [Sheikh Sultan Bin Saqr Al Qasimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Sultan_bin_Saqr_Al_Qasimi) of Sharjah who was reinstated by the British in Ras Al Khaimah after the deposition of [Hassan bin Rahma Al Qasimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_bin_Rahma_Al_Qasimi).[^cite_note-22] The treaty stipulated the end of piracy and slavery, and laid the foundation for the [British protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_protectorate) over the [Trucial States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trucial_States) that lasted until December 1, 1971.

### British protectorate

A British protectorate from this point forward, as one of the [Trucial States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trucial_States), in 1869, Ras Al Khaimah became fully independent from neighbouring [Sharjah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharjah_\(emirate\)). From September 1900 to 7 July 1921, it was re-incorporated into Sharjah; the last governor became its next independent ruler.[^cite_note-23]

### Modern history

&#x20;The last of the Trucial States to join the newly independent United Arab Emirates, on 10 February 1972, Ras Al Khaimah, under the leadership of Sheikh [Saqr bin Mohammad Al Qasimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saqr_bin_Mohammad_Al_Qasimi), joined the United Arab Emirates following the Iranian [seizure of Abu Musa and the Greater and Lesser Tunbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seizure_of_Abu_Musa_and_the_Greater_and_Lesser_Tunbs).[^cite_note-24]

## Climate

&#x20;Ras Al Khaimah is notable for its climate that is particularly hot and humid even for the region. The highest ever recorded [wet bulb temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet-bulb_temperature) from a reputable weather station was at [Ras Al Khaimah International airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_Al_Khaimah_International_Airport) with a WT of 36.3°C. Because high humidity makes cooling down via perspiration less effective, it makes hot temperatures feel significantly hotter. Wet bulb temperatures above 35°C can prove fatal even for healthy adults.[^cite_note-26]

## Population

The population of the city is around 115,949 (2021), and it is the largest city in the [Emirate of Ras Al Khaimah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emirate_of_Ras_Al_Khaimah). It is the [6th most populous city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_the_United_Arab_Emirates) in the UAE.

## Education

Other than Arabic government-funded schools, the city is home to [Ras Al Khaimah Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_Al_Khaimah_Academy) and other Indian schools.[^cite_note-30] Higher education institutions in the city include the [Higher Colleges of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Colleges_of_Technology), [Ras Al Khaimah Medical and Health Sciences University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_Al_Khaimah_Medical_and_Health_Sciences_University), American University of Ras Al Khaimah[^cite_note-31] and many other colleges.

## Economy

The city of Ras Al Khaimah is home to the [Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_Al_Khaimah_Economic_Zone) that helps connect investors and international markets. It operates an online client portal called Portal 360.[^cite_note-32] The zone services businesses ranging from freelancers to [SMEs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_and_medium-sized_enterprises) and start-ups across 50 industries. RAKEZ is divided into six dedicated zones:[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34]

- Al Ghail Industrial Zone

- Al Hamra Industrial Zone

- RAKEZ Academic Zone

- Al Hulaila Industrial Zone

- Al Nakheel Business Zone

- Al Hamra Business Zone

### Ceramics industry

The city is the headquarters of [RAK Ceramics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAK_Ceramics), a global ceramics manufacturer. The company produces 123 million square meters of tiles and 5 million pieces of sanitaryware every year.[^cite_note-35] It has 12,000 employees and is listed on the [Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange](17443744.md) in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) and on the [Dhaka Stock Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhaka_Stock_Exchange) in [Bangladesh](3454.md).[^cite_note-36] Its annual turnover is estimated at \$1 billion.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38]

### Pharmaceutical industry

[Gulf Pharmaceutical Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julphar) is a pharmaceutical manufacturer operating in the [MENA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MENA) region and headquartered in the city of Ras Al Khaimah.[^cite_note-39] The company, also known under the name of [Julphar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julphar), employs 5,000 people and distributes its products to 50 countries.[^cite_note-40] It divides its activities between three units: Julphar Diabetes Solutions, General Medicines and Julphar Life. The company became a producer of raw ingredients for [insulin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insulin) in 2012.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42]

### Gambling industry

In 2022, Ras Al Khaimah announced a \$3.9 billion project, [Wynn Al Marjan Island](78424843.md), which is scheduled to open in 2027. The integrated resort is being established by [Wynn Resorts](1642677.md), whose CEO Craig Billings confirmed that it will include a 224,000 sq. ft. “casino component”. For its 40% stake, Wynn Resorts contributed \$900 million, while the remaining amount was financed by the Ras Al Khaimah government. The UAE has no laws to legalize gaming or gambling equipment. However, the country established the General Commercial Gaming Regulatory Authority (GCGRA), in September 2023. The GCGRA granted a “Commercial Gaming Facility Operator” license to Wynn Resorts, in October 2024. Given the country’s Sharia laws, gambling is completely prohibited for local citizens. But, the project was quietly progressing to target foreign travelers and reshape the region’s tourism.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45]
