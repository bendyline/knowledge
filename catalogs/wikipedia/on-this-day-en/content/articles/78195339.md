The **2026 Major League Soccer season** is the ongoing 31st season of [Major League Soccer](71802.md) (MLS), the top professional [soccer](10568.md) league in the United States and Canada. It is also the 48th overall season of a national first-division men's soccer league in the United States overseen by the [United States Soccer Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Soccer_Federation). The league has 30 clubs that are divided into the [Eastern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Conference_\(MLS\)) and [Western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Conference_\(MLS\)) conferences, each with 15 teams.

The regular season is scheduled to run from February 21 to November 7, with 34 matches for each team, primarily against opponents in the same conference. A six-week break was taken from May 25 to July 16 for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md), which was hosted in the United States, Canada, and Mexico. The playoffs are scheduled to be played during an uninterrupted period in November and December culminating in [MLS Cup 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_2026), the league's postseason championship,[^cite_note-2] on December 18.[^cite_note-3] The 2026 season will be the final full season played under the spring–autumn format, as MLS plans to transition to a summer–spring format beginning with the [2027–28 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027%E2%80%9328_Major_League_Soccer_season); a [shortened season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_Major_League_Soccer_season) with 14 matches is scheduled to be played in early 2027.[^cite_note-4]

All MLS matches during the regular season and playoffs are planned to be broadcast on [Apple TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_\(streaming_service\)), an online streaming service operated by [Apple Inc.](856.md) It replaces [MLS Season Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Season_Pass), which was a standalone streaming service by Apple, used from 2023 to 2025.[^cite_note-5]

[Inter Miami CF](41853326.md) enters the season as reigning [MLS Cup](78441305.md) champions, while the [Philadelphia Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_Union) enter as holders of the [Supporters' Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supporters'_Shield).

## Teams

[MLS had been expanding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expansion_of_Major_League_Soccer) at the rate of nearly one team a year since 2005, when [Real Salt Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Salt_Lake) joined the league, through 2025, with the introduction of [San Diego FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego_FC).[^cite_note-usp-6][^cite_note-7] Commissioner [Don Garber](1878607.md) stated that the league would put a pause on expansion once reaching thirty teams until after the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).[^cite_note-usp-6] As such, the 2026 MLS season represents the first season played without an expansion team added or planned in over twenty years.[^cite_note-usp-6]
