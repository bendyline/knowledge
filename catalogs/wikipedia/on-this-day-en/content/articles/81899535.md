The **2026 French municipal elections** were held on 15 and 22 March 2026 to elect the [municipal councils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_council_\(France\)) of the approximately 35,000 communes in [France](5843419.md).[^cite_note-1]

## Background

See also: [2024–2025 French political crisis](77741666.md)

During the last municipal election in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_municipal_elections), which was marked by the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md) and the highest [abstention rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstention) for local elections in French history, the [left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Left) reversed the trend of 2014. They narrowed the vote gap with the [right](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right-wing_politics_in_France) by flipping cities such as [Annecy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annecy), [Bordeaux](4097.md), [Marseille](40888948.md), and [Saint-Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Paul,_R%C3%A9union). Nevertheless, the right managed to reclaim several cities from the left, including [Metz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metz). The [National Rally](67119.md) strengthened its local presence, winning the city of [Perpignan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perpignan) while holding all the municipalities it controlled.

The [2022 French presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_presidential_election) and [legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_legislative_election) saw the power of [President of France](24899.md) [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md) and his [presidential majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_majority) weakened by the rise of both the [far-right](9701975.md) and the left. Concurrently, [The Republicans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Republicans_\(France\)) were further diminished, resulting in their smallest parliamentary group since the start of the [Fifth Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Fifth_Republic).

In the [European Parliament election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_European_Parliament_election) of June 9, 2024, the National Rally saw its support soar, while the presidential majority list lost nearly half it seats. The left entered the election divided, despite having previously united in 2022 under [NUPES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Ecological_and_Social_People's_Union). The joint list of the [Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Party_\(France\)) and [Place Publique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Place_Publique), as well as [La France Insoumise](52520313.md), gained seats, while the [French Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Communist_Party) again failed to secure representation, and [The Ecologists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ecologists) plummeted from 13 seats to just five.

On the evening of the results of the European election, Emmanuel Macron [dissolved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_dissolution_\(France\)) the [National Assembly](299616.md). [New legislative elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_legislative_election) were held on June 30 and July 7, 2024, further upending an already disrupted political landscape. The left reunited and formed the [New Popular Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Popular_Front) in just a few hours. The Republicans faced an unprecedented [crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_The_Republicans_alliance_crisis) when their president, [Éric Ciotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89ric_Ciotti), formed an alliance with the National Rally. Though the centrists, headed by [Renaissance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaissance_\(French_political_party\)), struggled with the President's decision to dissolve parliament, they maintained their [Ensemble pour la République](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ensemble_\(political_coalition\)) coalition.

Between the second round of voting, faced with the surge of the far-right, the left called for a "[Republican Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Front_\(French_Fifth_Republic\))", which most qualifying candidates joined. The final results saw the left-wing alliance NFP win a narrow plurality of 193 deputies. The National Rally became the largest single-party group in the National Assembly with 125 deputies, while Éric Ciotti formed his own group with 18 candidates. The center suffered a major collapse, losing one-thirds of its representatives.

Many mayors have resigned since 2020.[^cite_note-2]

### Campaign

The race in Paris was considered to be important for the [2027 French presidential election](72162761.md), in a fierce battle between the left and right.[^cite_note-3] The election in Nice was also competitive between centre-right and far-right. Overall, the left was successful in cities such as Paris, Marseille, and Lyon, while the right was successful in cities including Nice and Toulouse.

## Results

26,182,365 valid votes were cast in the first round, and 9,391,407 valid votes were cast in the second round.[^cite_note-4]
