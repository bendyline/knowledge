**Bekasi** (Indonesian pronunciation: [\[bəˈkasi\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Malay) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/52/LL-Q9240\_%28ind%29-Noaius_Paticus-Bekasi.wav/LL-Q9240\_%28ind%29-Noaius_Paticus-Bekasi.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/52/LL-Q9240_%28ind%29-Noaius_Paticus-Bekasi.wav/LL-Q9240_%28ind%29-Noaius_Paticus-Bekasi.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is the largest [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_status_in_Indonesia) in the province of [West Java](314356.md) and the [third-largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Indonesian_cities_by_population) in [Indonesia](14579.md). Located on the eastern border of [Jakarta](16275.md), it serves as a [commuter city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commuter_town) within the [Jakarta metropolitan area](1340014.md). According to the 2020 Census by [Statistics Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Indonesia) (BPS), Bekasi had 2,543,676 inhabitants.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] The official estimate for mid 2025 was 2,648,272 (comprising 1,328,712 males and 1,319,560 females).[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-2] The city is bordered by [Bekasi Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bekasi_Regency) (from which the city was separated administratively on 16 December 1996) to the north and the east, [Bogor Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogor_Regency) and [Depok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depok) City to the south, and the city of [East Jakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Jakarta) to the west.

Bekasi is one of the oldest cities in Indonesia, and has a history of being the capital city of the [Kingdom of Tarumanagara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarumanagara). At that time, the name of Bekasi was **Dayeuh Sundasembawa** or **Jayagiri**. The earliest evidence of its existence dates from the fifth century according to the [Tugu inscription](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tugu_inscription), which describes the name of two rivers that run through the city, i.e. Candrabhaga and Gomati and one of those rivers, i.e. **Candrabhaga** is the origin of the name Bekasi where the name Candrabhaga evolved into "Bhagasasi" – due to the Sanskrit word candra (which means moon) evolved into [Old Sundanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Sundanese_language) word 'sasi' which also means moon – and then the name Bhagasasi was mis-spelt as "Bhagasi", and then the Dutch colonial government also mis-spelt the name Bhagasi as "Bacassie", and finally it became "Bekasi".[^cite_note-4] During the [Dutch East Indies](23476997.md) period, Bekasi was a part of [Batavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batavia,_Dutch_East_Indies) residency. As a dormitory city, many middle-upper class satellite areas have been developed in Bekasi,[^cite_note-5] complete with their own shopping malls, schools, hospitals, club houses, [water park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_park), and shuttle bus services to [central Jakarta](2728760.md). The large number of [multinational companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multinational_companies) has apparently attracted many expatriates (mainly [Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_migration_to_Indonesia) and [Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koreans_in_Indonesia)) to settle in Bekasi.[^cite_note-6]

## Economy

Bekasi has already grown to become one of the main centres of growth in **Jabodetabek**. The city accounts for 2.11% of total national GDP.[^cite_note-7] Recently, many foreigners (mostly Korean, Japanese, and Chinese) have set up their business to take advantage of its dynamism and boost its economy. Bekasi's economy was mainly based on service and manufacturing. Some headquarters of big corporations are located here, such as PT Metropolitan Land Tbk and PT Kian Santang Muliatama Tbk.

### Commerce

Bekasi is one of the most promising property markets in [Jabodetabek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabodetabek), and several high-value developments have transformed its property market. Many of country's big property developers are building apartments, hotels, and shopping malls in the city. In recent years, some residences have developed along the Jatiwarna/Jatibening — East Bekasi toll highway, further to Cibatu in Bekasi Regency. These include [Kota Harapan Indah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kota_Harapan_Indah), [Summarecon Bekasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summarecon_Bekasi), Kemang Pratama and Grand Galaxy City.

Prime business and commercial centres are situated in the western part of city. There are some financial centres, restaurants, and shopping centres along Jalan Ahmad Yani, Bekasi CBD, Jalan Sudirman, Jalan K.H. Noer Alie, and Harapan Indah Boulevard.

The largest shopping centre is Grand Metropolitan with over 125,000 m<sup>2</sup> (1,350,000 sq ft) of floor space.[^cite_note-8] Other shopping centres include [Metropolitan Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Mall), Pakuwon Mall Bekasi, Summarecon Mal Bekasi, Mal Ciputra Cibubur, Mega Bekasi Hypermall, Grand Galaxy Park, Grand Mall, Blu Plaza, BTC Mall, GP Mall, Bekasi Cyber Park, Plaza Pondok Gede, and Lagoon Avenue.

Many hotels developed in Bekasi, such as [Santika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santika_Indonesia_Hotels_&_Resorts), [Fairfield by Marriott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairfield_by_Marriott), [Four Points by Sheraton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Points_by_Sheraton), [Tune Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tune_Hotels), Horison, Harris, Aston Imperial, Amaris and Amaroossa.

## Infrastructure



Bekasi commercial centers

The development of large scale residential areas and industrial parks in Bekasi has been induced by infrastructure development, especially roads, highways and railways.

### Road and highway

Bekasi is connected by the [Trans-Java Toll Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Java_Toll_Road), the main road across Java. There are three expressways connections from the [Jabodetabek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabodetabek) urban area: the [Jakarta–Cikampek Toll Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakarta%E2%80%93Cikampek_Toll_Road), which has three exits in Bekasi; the [Jakarta Outer Ring Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakarta_Outer_Ring_Road), which provides access to [Tangerang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangerang), Jakarta and [Bogor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogor); and the [Bekasi-Cawang-Kampung Melayu Toll Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bekasi-Cawang-Kampung_Melayu_Toll_Road). In 2019, Jakarta-Cikampek elevated toll road will operate to ease traffic on the current toll road between Cikunir and West Karawang with a total length of 36.4 kilometres.[^cite_note-9] Owing to its location as a satellite city of [Jakarta](16275.md), heavy [rush hour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rush_hour) traffic jams have become common on the roads between East Jakarta and Bekasi.

### Railway

A double track railway connecting Manggarai to Cikarang serves the city.[^cite_note-10] Beside a double track railway, the government also built a 17.9 km [light rail transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakarta_LRT) line connecting East Bekasi-Cawang-Dukuh Atas across Greater Jakarta.[^cite_note-11]

## Transport



[Jabodebek LRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabodebek_LRT) arriving to Jati Mulya station, Bekasi



A TransPatriot fleet in Bekasi

At present, rapid transit in Bekasi across Greater Jakarta consists of rail [KRL Commuterline](15039992.md), [Jabodebek LRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabodebek_LRT), and a bus rapid transit [TransJakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TransJakarta).

KRL Commuterline's [Blue Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KA_Commuter_line_Jakarta_Kota-Cikarang) serves from either [Bekasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bekasi_railway_station), [Bekasi Timur](65795155.md) or [Kranji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kranji_railway_station) to [Jakarta Kota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakarta_Kota_railway_station) (via [Pasar Senen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasar_Senen_railway_station)/[Manggarai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manggarai_railway_station)) and [Cikarang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cikarang_railway_station). Bekasi Station also serves intercity trains to cities across Java.

[Jabodebek LRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabodebek_LRT) serving on [Bekasi Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bekasi_Line) which serves trips from Dukuh Atas station to Jatimulya station. The distance traveled in this fully-elevated line is 29.54 km.

[Transjakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transjakarta) and the Transjabodetabek premium serves commuters from Harapan Indah, [Summarecon Bekasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summarecon_Bekasi), Bulak Kapal, Vida Bekasi, and Jatiwarna, as well as the feeder buses from Kemang Pratama and Grand Galaxy City to Jakarta city center. The [TransPatriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans_Patriot_Rapid_Bus_Service?action=edit\&redlink=1) operates a route within the city. [DAMRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perum_DAMRI) [shuttle bus service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuttle_bus_service) is available from the [Soekarno–Hatta International Airport](1203751.md) to Kayuringin, Harapan Indah and Summarecon Bekasi. Taxis are widely available. The primary means of public transportation is by minibus, called *[angkot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angkot)*. They serve certain routes throughout the city. The main bus terminal is Terminal Bekasi, located in East Bekasi. The other terminals are Pondok Gede, Harapan Indah and Kayuringin.

[Halim Perdanakusuma International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halim_Perdanakusuma_International_Airport) and [Halim high-speed train station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halim_railway_station) are located on the border of Bekasi and East Jakarta.

## Hydrology

The City of Bekasi is traversed by the main river, the [Kali Bekasi (Bekasi River)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kali_Bekasi), along with its tributaries. The Bekasi River originates from the confluence of the Cikeas and Cileungsi rivers, which have their sources in the mountains of Bogor Regency. Surface water in the Bekasi area includes the Bekasi River, several smaller rivers, and the [Tarum Barat irrigation canals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarum_Barat_irrigation_canals?action=edit\&redlink=1) used for irrigating fields. This water serves as the raw water source for the drinking water needs of the Bekasi region (both the city and the regency) and the DKI Jakarta area. Currently, the surface water condition of the Bekasi River is polluted by industrial waste originating from the southern part of the City of Bekasi (industries located in Bogor Regency).[^cite_note-\:0-12]

The City of Bekasi is a part of the [Bekasi river basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category\:Bekasi_basin), located in the downstream area of the basin with slopes ranging from 0 to 2% and elevations between 11 meters to 81 meters above sea level. This topographical condition leads to frequent flooding in many areas of Bekasi, especially during rainy seasons, such as in the districts of Jatiasih, East Bekasi, Rawalumbu, South Bekasi, Pondok Gede, and Pondok Melati.[^cite_note-\:0-12]

## Demographics

The 2020 Census of Bekasi's population was 2,543,676,[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] but according to the official estimates for mid 2025 the total had become 2,648,272, an increase of over 100,000.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-2] Bekasi is inhabited by many different ethnic groups, mostly [Betawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betawi_people), [Sundanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_people), and [Javanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javanese_people) descent. [Sundanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_people) are the largest minority as well as other minorities include [Minangkabau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minangkabau_people), [Bataks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batak), and [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Indonesians).

The regional language used by the majority of the population is the [Betawi Ora dialect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betawi_language#Dialects). Then at the southern end, [Sundanese language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_language) ([Bekasi dialect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bekasi_Sundanese?action=edit\&redlink=1)) is also spoken, precisely in districts of Jatisampurna and Bantargebang.

Most citizens in Bekasi adhere to [Islam](6037917.md). Other religions include Christianity ([Roman Catholicism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholicism) and [Protestantism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism)), [Hinduism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism), [Buddhism](3267529.md), and [Confucianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confucianism).

## Government

### Mayor

Main article: [Mayor of Bekasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Bekasi)

The [Mayor of Bekasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Bekasi) is the highest-ranking official within the Bekasi City government. He reports to the [Governor of West Java](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_West_Java). The current mayor or regional head of Bekasi City is [Tri Adhianto Tjahyono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tri_Adhianto_Tjahyono), with [Abdul Harris Bobihoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Harris_Bobihoe?action=edit\&redlink=1) as vice mayor. They took office on 20 February 2025.

## Climate

Situated in the alluvial lowland, Bekasi City has a [tropical monsoon climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Am*). The wettest month (highest rainfall) is January, with a rainfall total of 374 millimetres or 14.7 inches, while the driest month (lowest rainfall) is August, with a rainfall total of 55 millimetres or 2.2 inches.&#x20;

- [Pramoedya Ananta Toer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pramoedya_Ananta_Toer)'s novel *[Di Tepi Kali Bekasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Di_Tepi_Kali_Bekasi?action=edit\&redlink=1)* (1951) is set mainly in Bekasi.
