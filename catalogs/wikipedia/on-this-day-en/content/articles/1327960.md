The **Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries** (農林水産省, *Nōrin-suisan-shō*; **MAFF**) is a [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_\(government\)) level ministry in the [government of Japan](30858300.md) responsible for oversight of the [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture), [forestry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forestry) and [fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing_industry) industries. Its [acronym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acronym) is **MAFF**. The current [Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Agriculture,_Forestry_and_Fisheries_\(Japan\)) is [Norikazu Suzuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norikazu_Suzuki).[^cite_note-chief_MAFF-1]

## History

The [Constitution of the Empire of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_Constitution) provided for the creation of a **[Ministry of Agriculture and Commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Agriculture_and_Commerce)** (農商務省, *Nōshōmu-shō*), which was established in 1881, with [Tani Tateki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tani_Tateki) as its first minister. As an additional note, the Ministry of Agriculture and Commerce was a division that served as the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and the [Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economy,_Trade_and_Industry).

In 1925, the commerce functions were separated out into a separate **[Ministry of Commerce and Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Commerce_and_Industry_\(Japan\))** (商工省, *Shōkō-shō*), and the ministry was renamed the **Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry** (農林省, *Nōrin-shō*). The ministry was also given responsibility for oversight of the Factory Act of 1903, which provided regulations for work hours and worker safety in both industrial and agricultural industries.

From 1943 to 1945, when most of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry was merged into the **[Ministry of Munitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Munitions_\(Japan\))** (軍需省, *Gunjushō*) due to the mobilisation of industry for the war effort in [World War II](32927.md), parts of that ministry reverted to the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, which was again briefly named **Ministry of Agriculture and Commerce** (農商省, *Nōshō-shō*).

In 1978, the name of Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry was expanded to the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries to better reflect the ministry's role in guaranteeing the Japanese public a safe food supply, and to protect producers and workers in the food production industries.[^cite_note-Maff_role-2]

## Functional activities

The primary function of the ministry is to set quality standards for food products, supervise [commodity markets](48190.md) and food sales, and to undertake [land reclamation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_reclamation) and land improvement projects.[^cite_note-Maff_role-2]
