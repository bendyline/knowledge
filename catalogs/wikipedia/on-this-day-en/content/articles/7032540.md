The **Germany men's national field hockey team** is one of the most successful sides in the world, winning gold at the [Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_hockey_at_the_Summer_Olympics) four times (including once as [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany)), the [Hockey World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockey_World_Cup) 4 times, the [EuroHockey Nations Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroHockey_Nations_Championship) nine times (including twice as West Germany) and the [Hockey Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockey_Champions_Trophy) ten times (including three times as West Germany).

## History

The team caused an upset in the [2002 Men's Hockey World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Men's_Hockey_World_Cup) when they defeated [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_men's_national_field_hockey_team) 2–1 with striker [Olivier Domke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Domke?action=edit\&redlink=1) scoring the winner after Germany came back from being 1–0 down. After this period the Germans went through a transition period, finishing lowly in the [2003 Men's Hockey Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_Men's_Hockey_Champions_Trophy) and the [2004 Men's Hockey Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Men's_Hockey_Champions_Trophy) with several inexperienced players in their squad. Coach Bernhard Peters was looking to nurture the players for the [World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Men's_Hockey_World_Cup) such as [Christopher Zeller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Zeller), [Moritz Fürste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moritz_F%C3%BCrste) and [Timo Wess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timo_Wess), and was successful as the Germans won the [2006 Men's Hockey World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Men's_Hockey_World_Cup) in [Mönchengladbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B6nchengladbach), defeating Australia 4–3 in the final. Bernhard Peters left the team in order to pursue a career in [football](10568.md) and is now a staff member at [TSG 1899 Hoffenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TSG_1899_Hoffenheim).[^cite_note-2]

On 6 November 2006, Markus Wiese was appointed as the new head coach. Success at the [2007 Men's Hockey Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Men's_Hockey_Champions_Trophy) and a gold medal at the [2008 Beijing Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Beijing_Olympics) followed this. Germany headed into the [2010 Men's Hockey World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Men's_Hockey_World_Cup) with a largely young and inexperienced squad but reached the final of the World Cup after strong performances throughout the tournament. In the final, they were defeated 2–1 by [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_men's_national_field_hockey_team).

Germany has played in the annual [2011 Hockey Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Men's_Hockey_Champions_Trophy) held in [Auckland](18660332.md), [New Zealand](4913064.md). The team competed in pool B with [Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea_men's_national_field_hockey_team), [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_men's_national_field_hockey_team) and host nation [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sticks_Men). The team finished fifth in the tournament.
