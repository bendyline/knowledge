**Crime in Bangladesh** is present in various forms such as [black marketeering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_market), [contract killing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_killing), [corruption](38163102.md), [drug trafficking](218449.md), [extortion](162449.md), [fraud](58790.md), [human trafficking](19333662.md), [kidnapping](169376.md), [money laundering](19390.md), [murder](20171.md), [political violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_violence), [rape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rape_in_Bangladesh), [robbery](54849.md), [terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorism_in_Bangladesh), [wildlife trafficking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife_trafficking), etc.

## Narcotics trafficking

[Bangladesh](3454.md) is used as a transit route for [narcotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narcotics) produced in neighboring countries.[^cite_note-CIABANGLADESH-2] The Annual Report for 2007 from the [International Narcotics Control Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Narcotics_Control_Board) (INCB), reports that Bangladesh is now the main transit point for the movement and trafficking of [heroin](14034.md) from [Southeast Asia](28741.md) into the [European](9239.md) market .[^cite_note-BNBHT-3] The report noted that the porous borders between Bangladesh and [India](14533.md) contribute to the cross-border trafficking of narcotics.[^cite_note-AR2007-4]

The known means of trafficking drugs into Bangladesh are couriers from [Pakistan](23235.md), commercial vehicles and trains from [India](14533.md) or [Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma) in addition to shipments from India via the [Bay of Bengal](47892.md).[^cite_note-AR2007-4]

It is estimated that 100,000 people are involved in narcotics trafficking in Bangladesh.[^cite_note-5]

## Homicides

A total of 10,331 homicides were reported to Bangladesh authorities from 2001 to 2003, showing a significant increase in recent years.[^cite_note-WPE69-6]

## Software piracy

For the [Asia Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Pacific) region, Bangladesh is ranked as one of the main countries for [Software Piracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyright_infringement_of_software).[^cite_note-StopPiracyCrime-7] In 2008, it was estimated that the [Software Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_industry) loses nearly \$102 million (USD) every year as a result.[^cite_note-8]
