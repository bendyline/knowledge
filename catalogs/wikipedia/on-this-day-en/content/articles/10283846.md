The **Ministry of Interior**[^cite_note-official_book-1] or **Ministry of the Interior**[^cite_note-2] or **Interior Ministry**[^cite_note-3] ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *İçişleri Bakanlığı* lit. Ministry of Internal Affairs) is a [government ministry of the Republic of Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministries_of_Turkey), responsible for interior security affairs in [Turkey](11125639.md).

As of 10 February 2026, the current Minister of the Interior is [Mustafa Çiftçi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_%C3%87ift%C3%A7i).[^cite_note-4]

## Functions

The ministry is responsible for [disaster and emergency management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disaster_and_Emergency_Management_Presidency), [immigration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immigration_to_Turkey),[^cite_note-official_book-1] inspection of [local government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_districts_in_Turkey), [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie_General_Command) and [coast guard](2658218.md) (in peacetime), and [police](20141676.md). The ministry helps to combat [human trafficking](19333662.md),[^cite_note-5] smuggling[^cite_note-6] and [bootleg alcohol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rum-running).[^cite_note-7]
