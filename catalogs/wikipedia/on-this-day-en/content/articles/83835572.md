[Brazil](3383.md) had a [suicide rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide_rate) of 7.6 per 100,000 (male 11.4, female 3.9) as of 2021 and ranked 87 in the world standing.[^cite_note-1]

In 2025, the rate of attempted [self-harm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-harm) or suicide is 8,332. In the same year, there were 342 deaths by suicide.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

In Brazil, there is one attempted suicide or act of self-harm among [adolescents](83859.md) every 10 minutes.[^cite_note-3]

The reasons for suicide are multifaceted, influenced by social, cultural, biological, psychological, and environmental factors present throughout the life course.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

Every year in Brazil, throughout the month of September, there is a suicide prevention campaign, which is [Setembro Amarelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Setembro_Amarelo?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
