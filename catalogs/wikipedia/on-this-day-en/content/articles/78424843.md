**Wynn Al Marjan Island** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): منتجع وين جزيرة المرجان) is an under-construction [integrated casino and resort hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_resort) developed by [Wynn Resorts](1642677.md) in [Ras Al Khaimah](61770159.md), [United Arab Emirates](69328.md).[^cite_note-3]

## Location and features

Wynn Al Marjan Island is being built on [Al Marjan Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Marjan_Island), a new development in [Ras Al Khaimah](61770159.md).

The resort will feature 1,530 accommodations, comprising 1,217 hotel rooms, 297 suites, 2 royal apartments, 4 garden townhomes, and 10 Marina estates across 70 floors.[^cite_note-4] It also features a 20,900 square metre main casino, 22 restaurants, 12,000 square meters of retail space and 7,708 square meters of indoor convention space including a 2,633-square-meter, column-free Grand Ballroom.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] In April of 2025, Wynn Resorts revealed renderings of what the hotel rooms will look like.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

The resort will feature an additional casino on the 22nd floor, which has been described as a "sky gaming casino".[^cite_note-10]

Included in the hotel will be 297 exclusive suites called Enclave, described as an "ultra-luxury destination within a destination."[^cite_note-11] These suites will be located towards the top of the resort tower and will feature a separate and private entrance from the rest of the resort.[^cite_note-12] Enclave will also feature a private pool and a private beach.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

Connected to the resort will be a marina which will contain 98 berths that will be able to accommodate [superyachts](3652673.md) measuring as long as 85 metres. The marina will be managed by IGY Marinas.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

Restaurants planned for opening include [Delilah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delilah_\(restaurant\)), an upscale American supper club[^cite_note-17], and Ducasse, a French-American steakhouse by chef Alain Ducasse.[^cite_note-18]

In 2026, the resort's art collection was revealed to include [Jeff Koons'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Koons) "Tulips", a [triceratops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triceratops) skull, and works by both [Marc Quinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Quinn) and [Jaume Plensa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_Plensa).[^cite_note-19]

Up to 80% of the resort's planned 9,000+ employees will be accommodated at Wynn Oasis, a 15-building colleague-living residential village, including facilities for visitors and fitness.[^cite_note-20] The facility is said to meet guest hospitality standards.[^cite_note-21]

### Entertainment

In 2026, Wynn announced a partnership with British theatre company [Punchdrunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punchdrunk_\(theatre_company\)) to bring an inaugural show to Wynn Al Marjan Island.[^cite_note-\:0-22] The production will be unique to Wynn and allow guests to select between a seated experience or a "roaming immersive journey" in which spectators are able to walk below and to the backstage of the show. The wandering show concept is similar to other Punchdrunk productions such as *Sleep No More*, which ran for 14 years in New York with more than 5,000 performances.[^cite_note-23] It will be Punchdrunk's first [MENA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East_and_North_Africa) production.[^cite_note-24] The show was designed in collaboration with Craig S. Billings, CEO of [Wynn Resorts](1642677.md).[^cite_note-25] The New York Times Style Magazine describes the theater itself as "a modern interpretation of a classic theatre, with [Art Deco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Deco) references, rich fabrics, sculpted drapery and grand interior spaces."[^cite_note-\:0-22]

## History

Wynn Resorts secured the first gaming license awarded in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) in 2024 while Wynn Al Marjan Island was under construction. This will make Wynn Al Marjan Island the first casino resort in the UAE.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] Wynn Resorts stands to have a [monopoly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopoly) on gaming in the UAE for the first several years before other potential casinos may open.[^cite_note-28] Authorities in the UAE have indicated that they have no plans to award additional casino licenses in the immediate future and it is unknown when that might happen.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

By the end of 2024, construction on the property was progressing quickly. 55% of structural concrete was in place and complete up to the 26th floor. 73% of the guest rooms were also in place. Wynn Resorts expects a topping out by December 2025, with the resort opening in 2027.[^cite_note-31] Wynn has also acquired an additional 155 acres of land on Al Marjan Island for further development of Wynn Al Marjan Island.[^cite_note-32] Wynn confirmed this acquisition in September of 2025. The additional property has been described as being a “second integrated resort”. The existing plot of land will be expanded through [land reclamation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_reclamation). The addition of an adjoining resort also includes an agreement for issuing an IPO.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34]

In early 2025, Wynn Resorts acquired the [Crown London Aspinalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_London_Aspinalls) casino in [London](17867.md) from [Crown Resorts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Resorts). It was rebranded as Wynn Mayfair in June 2025.[^cite_note-35] The purpose of this acquisition is to bring British gamblers to Wynn Al Marjan Island after it opens.[^cite_note-36]

By February 2025, Wynn Resorts had completed financing for Wynn Al Marjan Island.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38] It has also been reported that there has been high demand for retail space at the new resort. [Rolex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolex) has been mentioned as a retailer likely to have a store at the resort.[^cite_note-39]

Wynn Resorts commenced the process of hiring staff for the resort in May of 2025. Multiple job openings were listed less than two years prior to the resort’s planned opening.[^cite_note-40] As June 2025 approached, the resort was reaching 60% completion.[^cite_note-41] The building topped out in December 2025 with 100% of the tower's structural concrete competed and all guest accommodation structure completed.[^cite_note-42]

In 2026, the 548-metre bridge connecting the island to the mainland was named Wynn Bridge, with completion estimated in late 2026.[^cite_note-43]
