The **Islamic State – Pakistan Province**[^cite_note-1] (**ISPP**) is the Pakistani branch of the [Islamic State](9087364.md). It was formed as a split of the [Islamic State – Khorasan Province](48029263.md) (ISKP).

## History

In May 2019, the Islamic State announced the creation of Pakistan Province on [Amaq News Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amaq_News_Agency). Prior to the creation of ISPP, the [ISKP](48029263.md) was in charge of Islamic State activities in all of Pakistan. ISKP is active in [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) (KPK), while ISPP is active in Pakistani provinces [Punjab](24751.md), [Balochistan](4687.md) and [Azad Kashmir](2745.md), as well as [Sistan and Baluchestan province](448105.md) of Iran. It is a separate group from the unofficial [ISJK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_State_in_Jammu_and_Kashmir) faction. Shortly after its creation, ISPP killed a Pakistani policeman in [Mastung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mastung,_Pakistan) and a [TTP](18953699.md) militant in [Quetta](93764.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-IslamicStatePakistanProvince-4] ISPP also killed [Shia](26961.md) [Hazara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazaras) refugees.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Immediately after the creation of ISPP, the [Government of Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Punjab,_Pakistan) called for measures against ISPP.[^cite_note-7]

ISPP has claimed responsibility for the [2024 Sibi bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Sibi_bombing) and the [2024 Balochistan bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Balochistan_bombings).[^cite_note-8]

On 15 April 2025, a bus carrying personnel from the [Pakistani security forces](1965925.md) was targeted with an [improvised explosive device](265112.md), resulting in the deaths of three soldiers and injuries to twenty others.[^cite_note-\:0-9] The Islamic State – Pakistan Province claimed responsibility for the attack.[^cite_note-10]

On 6 February 2026, a suicide bomber of the ISPP [blew up in a Shia Imambarghah](82346663.md) during Friday prayers, killing 31 people and injuring 170+ others.[^cite_note-11]
