**Gosen-Neu Zittau** is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Germany) in the [Oder-Spree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oder-Spree) district, in [Brandenburg](3765.md), [Germany](11867.md). Neu Zittau is situated in the [Spree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spree_\(river\)) valley, near the [Oder-Spree-Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oder-Spree-Canal?action=edit\&redlink=1).
