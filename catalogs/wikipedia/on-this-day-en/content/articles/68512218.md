The **90th 24 Hours of Le Mans** (French: *90<sup>e</sup> 24 Heures du Mans*) was a 24-hour [automobile endurance event](699018.md) that was held in front of 244,200 spectators on 11 and 12 June 2022 at the [Circuit de la Sarthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_la_Sarthe), near [Le Mans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans), France for [Le Mans Hypercar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans_Hypercar) (Hypercar), [Le Mans Prototypes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans_Prototype) (LMP) and [Le Mans Grand Touring Endurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LM_GTE) (LMGTE) cars that were entered by teams of three drivers each. It was the 90th edition of the event organised by the [Automobile Club de l'Ouest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile_Club_de_l'Ouest), and the third round of the [2022 FIA World Endurance Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIA_World_Endurance_Championship). A test day was held the week before the race on 5 June.

A [Toyota GR010 Hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_GR010_Hybrid) shared by [Sébastien Buemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9bastien_Buemi), [Brendon Hartley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendon_Hartley) and [Ryō Hirakawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ry%C5%8D_Hirakawa) started from [pole position](630713.md) after Hartley recorded the fastest overall lap time in the Hyperpole session. The three drivers led for 274 of the race's 380 laps and finished first, two minutes and 1.222 seconds ahead of teammates [Mike Conway](5506888.md), [Kamui Kobayashi](9537340.md) and [José María López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mar%C3%ADa_L%C3%B3pez) (who shared another Toyota GR010 Hybrid). It was Buemi's fourth Le Mans victory, Hartley's third, Hirakawa's first and Toyota's fifth in succession. A [Glickenhaus SCG 007 LMH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glickenhaus_SCG_007_LMH) shared by [Ryan Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Briscoe), [Franck Mailleux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franck_Mailleux) and [Richard Westbrook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Westbrook) completed the podium, five laps behind in third place.

[Jota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jota_Sport)'s team of [Roberto González](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Gonz%C3%A1lez_\(racing_driver\)), [António Félix da Costa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant%C3%B3nio_F%C3%A9lix_da_Costa) and [Will Stevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Stevens), sharing an [Oreca 07](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oreca_07)-[Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibson_Technology) car, won the Le Mans Prototype 2 (LMP2) category after leading for all but 15 laps. The debuting [Prema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prema_Powerteam) squad of [Lorenzo Colombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Colombo_\(racing_driver\)), [Louis Delétraz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Del%C3%A9traz) and [Robert Kubica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Kubica) followed, two minutes and 21 seconds later, and the class podium was completed by the sister Jota trio of [Jonathan Aberdein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Aberdein), [Ed Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Jones_\(racing_driver\)) and [Oliver Rasmussen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Rasmussen) in third place. In the final Le Mans Grand Touring Endurance Pro (LMGTE Pro) race at Le Mans, the No. 91 [Porsche 911 RSR-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche_911_RSR-19) of [Gianmaria Bruni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gianmaria_Bruni), [Richard Lietz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Lietz) and [Frédéric Makowiecki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric_Makowiecki) won the category; [AF Corse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AF_Corse)'s No. 51 [Ferrari 488 GTE Evo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_488_GTE), shared by [James Calado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Calado), [Alessandro Pier Guidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandro_Pier_Guidi) and [Daniel Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Serra), was second. [Aston Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Martin) won the Le Mans Grand Touring Endurance Am (LMGTE AM) class with an [TF Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TF_Sport)-run [Aston Martin Vantage GTE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Martin_Vantage_GTE_\(2018\)), shared by [Henrique Chaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henrique_Chaves), [Ben Keating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Keating) and [Marco Sørensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_S%C3%B8rensen), 44.446 seconds ahead of [WeatherTech Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_Competition)'s Porsche 911 RSR-19 driven by [Julien Andlauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julien_Andlauer), [Cooper MacNeil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_MacNeil) and [Thomas Merrill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Merrill_\(racing_driver\)).

The [Alpine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signature_Team) trio of [André Negrão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Negr%C3%A3o), [Nicolas Lapierre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Lapierre) and [Matthieu Vaxivière](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthieu_Vaxivi%C3%A8re) remained atop the [Hypercar Drivers' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIA_World_Endurance_champions#World_Endurance_Drivers'_Championship); their advantage was reduced to three points over Buemi, Hartley and Hirakawa, who passed [Glickenhaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Cameron_Glickenhaus)' [Romain Dumas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Dumas) and [Olivier Pla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Pla) for second. Bruni and Lietz took the [GTE Drivers' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIA_World_Endurance_champions#World_Endurance_GT_Drivers'_Championship) lead by three points over their [Porsche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche) teammates [Michael Christensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Christensen_\(racing_driver\)) and [Kévin Estre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%A9vin_Estre). Toyota and Porsche left Le Mans as the respective [Hypercar World Endurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIA_World_Endurance_champions#Hypercar_World_Endurance_Championship) and [GTE Manufacturers'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIA_World_Endurance_champions#World_GT_Manufacturers'_Championship) Championship leaders with three rounds remaining in the season.

## Background

Part of the track, with a Ferris wheel in the background

The [Circuit de la Sarthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_la_Sarthe), where the race was held

The [24 Hours of Le Mans](1401596.md) was first held in [1923](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1923_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans) after automotive journalist [Charles Faroux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Faroux), [Automobile Club de l'Ouest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile_Club_de_l'Ouest) (ACO) general secretary Georges Durand and industrialist Emile Coquile agreed to conduct a test of vehicle reliability and durability. Considered one of the world's most prestigious motor races, it is part of the [Triple Crown of Motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_Crown_of_Motorsport).[^cite_note-1] The 2022 race, held at the [Circuit de la Sarthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_la_Sarthe) (near [Le Mans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans), France) from 11 to 12 June 2022, was the event's 90th edition and the third round of the [2022 FIA World Endurance Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIA_World_Endurance_Championship).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] This was the first Le Mans race to host a full crowd and be run in June since restrictions were imposed in response to the [COVID-19 pandemic in France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_France).[^cite_note-4]

Before the race, [Alpine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signatech) drivers [André Negrão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Negr%C3%A3o), [Nicolas Lapierre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Lapierre) and [Matthieu Vaxivière](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthieu_Vaxivi%C3%A8re) led the [Hypercar Drivers' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIA_World_Endurance_champions#World_Endurance_Drivers'_Championship) with 57 points. [Glickenhaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Cameron_Glickenhaus)' [Romain Dumas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Dumas) and [Olivier Pla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Pla) were second with 39 points, and [Toyota](3796361.md)'s [Sébastien Buemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9bastien_Buemi), [Brendon Hartley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendon_Hartley) and [Ryō Hirakawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ry%C5%8D_Hirakawa) were third with 27.[^cite_note-Points-5] [Porsche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche_in_motorsport)'s [Michael Christensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Christensen_\(racing_driver\)) and [Kévin Estre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%A9vin_Estre) led the [GTE Drivers' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIA_World_Endurance_champions#World_Endurance_GT_Drivers'_Championship) with 57 points, ahead of [AF Corse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AF_Corse)'s [James Calado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Calado) and [Alessandro Pier Guidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandro_Pier_Guidi) (43 points) and third-place [Tommy Milner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Milner) and [Nick Tandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Tandy) of [Corvette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corvette_Racing) (39 points).[^cite_note-Points-5] Alpine led Toyota in the [Hypercar World Endurance Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIA_World_Endurance_champions#World_Manufacturers'_Championship), and Porsche led Ferrari in the [GTE Manufacturers' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIA_World_Endurance_champions#World_GT_Manufacturers'_Championship).[^cite_note-Points-5]

## Regulation and circuit changes

Following the [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans) race, where [clerk of the course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clerk_of_the_course) Patrick Morrisseau was almost struck by an approaching car at the finish line, the ACO allowed Morrisseau to wave the [checkered flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Checkered_flag) from a footbridge in the event of a close finish for safety reasons.[^cite_note-6] For the first time, all entries used [Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile](248019.md)-compliant renewable fuel produced by [TotalEnergies](804161.md) from wine residue to reduce carbon-dioxide emissions.[^cite_note-7]

A section of the [Mulsanne Straight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulsanne_Straight) from the Antares [roundabout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roundabout) to the first [chicane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicane) was resurfaced to make it more suitable for public and motor racing use after an inspection discovered that there were signs that the tarmac surface was becoming fatigued as well as damage to the asphalt covering the [shoulders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoulder_\(road\)).[^cite_note-8]

## Entries

Entry to the race was initially open from 6 December 2021 to 28 February 2022.[^cite_note-Regulations-9] In response to the Russian G-Drive Racing team's suspension of its racing programme and measures imposed on Russian drivers and entries by international sporting bodies after the country's [invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md), the ACO selection committee invited new applicants to apply for entry to the race from 7 to 9 March 2022.[^cite_note-10] The committee issued 62 invitations, and entries were placed into the [Le Mans Hypercar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans_Hypercar) (LMH), [Le Mans Prototype](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans_Prototype) 2 (LMP2), [Le Mans Grand Touring Endurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LM_GTE) Professional (LMGTE Pro) and Le Mans Grand Touring Endurance Amateur (LMGTE Am) classes.[^cite_note-Regulations-9] [Peugeot Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peugeot_Sport) withdrew their planned entries so they could continue testing their [9X8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peugeot_9X8) Hypercars before [homologation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homologation).[^cite_note-11]

### Automatic entries

Teams which won their class in the 2021 24 Hours of Le Mans or won championships in the [European Le Mans Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Le_Mans_Series) (ELMS), [Asian Le Mans Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Le_Mans_Series) (ALMS) and the [Michelin Le Mans Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelin_Le_Mans_Cup) (MLMC), received automatic entry invitations. The second-place finishers in the [2021 ELMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_European_Le_Mans_Series) in LMP2 and LMGTE championships also received an automatic invitation. Two participants from the [IMSA SportsCar Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMSA_SportsCar_Championship) (WTSC) were chosen by the ACO as automatic entries, regardless of performance or category. Teams earning automatic entries in LMP2 were allowed to change their cars from the previous year. ELMS LMGTE and ALMS GT teams earning automatic entries were allowed to use them in LMGTE Pro or LMGTE Am. The 2021 European and [2022 Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Asian_Le_Mans_Series) LMP3 (Le Mans Prototype 3) champions were eligible to field an entry in LMP2, and the [2021 MLMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Le_Mans_Cup) [Group GT3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_GT3) (GT3) champion was limited to the LMGTE Am category.[^cite_note-Regulations-9][^cite_note-S365Auto-12] The ACO announced the final list of automatic entries on 28 February 2022.[^cite_note-13]

### Entry list

Further information: [2022 FIA World Endurance Championship § Entries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIA_World_Endurance_Championship#Entries)

The ACO was scheduled to announce the 62-car list on 28 February but due to "administrative reasons", the announcement was delayed;[^cite_note-15] it was published on 10 March. In addition to the 38 guaranteed WEC entries, 14 came from the ELMS, four each from IMSA and the ALMS, and three were one-off Le Mans entries. In addition to the 62 entries invited to the race, six (three each from LMP2 and LMGTE Am) were placed on a reserve list to replace withdrawn or unaccepted invitations. Reserve entries were ordered, with the first replacing the first withdrawal from the race regardless of class. Entries were selected for their sporting and technical quality, fan, media and public interest and commitment, and loyalty to other ACO-administered series.[^cite_note-Regulations-9][^cite_note-16]

After publication of the entry list, [Racing Team Nederland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Team_Nederland) withdrew its TDS Racing-run [Oreca 07](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oreca_07) entry from third in the reserve list to enable to team to focus on the IMSA endurance races.[^cite_note-17] On 31 May, Hardpoint Motorsport (which received an automatic invitation through the IMSA for driver Rob Ferriol, who received the 2021 Akin Award as the highest-placed bronze-ranked IMSA entrant) withdrew their LMGTE Am-category [Porsche 911 RSR-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche_911_RSR-19) and relinquished the entry to [Proton Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_Competition). Proton gave it to the Absolute Racing customer squad, which raced under the Hardpoint Motorsport name in accordance with ACO regulations.[^cite_note-18] [Josh Pierson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Pierson) (driving the [United Autosports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Autosports) Oreca 07-[Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibson_Technology)) was 16 years and 117 days old when he competed at Le Mans, beating the record set by [Matt McMurry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_McMurry) in 2014.[^cite_note-pierson-19]

## Pre-race balance of performance changes

In the [balance of performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balance_of_performance) adjustments, the [Alpine A480](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebellion_R13)-Gibson received a performance increase with an extra 13 hp (9.7 kW) of power and weight; the [Toyota GR010 Hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_GR010_Hybrid) and the [Glickenhaus SCG 007 LMH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glickenhaus_SCG_007_LMH) cars remained unchanged from the preceding [6 Hours of Spa-Francorchamps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_6_Hours_of_Spa-Francorchamps). The LMGTE Pro-class [Chevrolet Corvette C8.R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet_Corvette_C8.R) had its performance slowed by an 0.8-millimetre (0.031 in) engine-air-restrictor diameter reduction. The [Ferrari 488 GTE Evo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_488_GTE) was equipped with a revised power curve it had since the WEC season began, and the Porsche 911 RSR-19 received a 3-litre (0.66 imp gal; 0.79 US gal) fuel-capacity increase in both LMGTE categories. Aston Martin's LMGTE Am-category [Vantage AMR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Martin_Vantage_GTE_\(2018\)) had its fuel capacity decreased by 1 L (0.22 imp gal; 0.26 US gal).[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

## Testing

All entrants participated in eight hours of mandatory testing on 5 June, divided into two sessions.[^cite_note-Regulations-9] The testing took place in mainly-dry weather, with a brief period of light rain. Driver [Paul di Resta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_di_Resta) helped Pierson in one of the United Autosports Oreca cars, while the team waited for [Alex Lynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Lynn) and [Oliver Jarvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Jarvis) to arrive at Le Mans from Detroit.[^cite_note-22]

A white-and red racing car

[José María López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mar%C3%ADa_L%C3%B3pez) set the pace of both sessions of testing in the No. 7 Toyota GR010 Hybrid.

The morning session was led by Toyota with a 3\:31.626 lap from [José María López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mar%C3%ADa_L%C3%B3pez)'s No. 7 entry with 17 minutes left,[^cite_note-Test1S365-23] demoting Dumas' No. 708 Glickenhaus entry to second.[^cite_note-AutosportTest1-24] Hartley made the No. 8 Toyota third-quickest, with [Franck Mailleux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franck_Mailleux)'s sister Glickenhaus No. 709 car fourth and the No. 36 Alpine of Vaxivière fifth. In LMP2, [Jota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jota_Sport)'s No. 38 car driven by [Will Stevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Stevens) lapped fastest at 3\:33.964 during the third hour; United Autosport USA's No. 22 entry of [Phil Hanson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Hanson_\(racing_driver\)) was second.[^cite_note-Test1S365-23][^cite_note-Test1DSC-25] The quickest Pro/Am sub-class entry was [Mathias Beche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathias_Beche)'s No. 13 [TDS Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TDS_Racing) car in third,[^cite_note-Test1S365-23][^cite_note-Test1DSC-25] and [Alex Brundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Brundle)'s No. 34 [Inter Europol Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter_Europol_Competition) car was fourth in class.[^cite_note-Test1S365-23][^cite_note-AutosportTest1-24] Corvette were first and second in LMGTE Pro, with Milner's No. 64 car faster than [Nicky Catsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicky_Catsburg)'s No. 63 sister entry by almost two-tenths of a second. The highest-placed Porsche was [Gianmaria Bruni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gianmaria_Bruni)'s No. 91 car in third, with the best Ferrari the privateer [Riley Motorsports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riley_Technologies) entry of [Felipe Fraga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felipe_Fraga) placing fourth.[^cite_note-AutosportTest1-24][^cite_note-Test1DSC-25] [Michelle Gatting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Gatting)'s No. 85 [Iron Dames](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Lynx) Ferrari led in LMGTE Am ahead of the No. 52 AF Corse, LMGTE Pro's slowest entry.[^cite_note-Test1DSC-25] Some cars went off the track during the session, sustaining damage; it was stopped after ten minutes when the No. 65 Panis car of [Julien Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julien_Canal) lost a wheel at the Daytona chicane on the Mulsanne Straight and was returned to the [pit lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pit_stop).[^cite_note-Test1S365-23][^cite_note-Test1DSC-25]

Dumas set the early pace in the second test session before López improved to a 3\:29.986 lap with almost 90 minutes left to go. Hirakawa's No. 8 Toyota was third, and Mailleux was fourth in Glickenhaus' No. 709 car. Late in the session, López briefly went off the circuit during a car-safety procedure at Mulsanne Corner and forced the testing to end early. ### After testing

After testing, the ACO and FIA again altered the LMGTE balance of performance.[^cite_note-29] The LMGTE Ferrari 488 GTE Evo received a 0.07-bar (7.0 kPa) decrease in turbocharger boost pressure curve across all revolutions, a 3-litre (0.66 imp gal; 0.79 US gal) fuel-capacity reduction from 87 litres (19 imp gal; 23 US gal) to 84 litres (18 imp gal; 22 US gal), and minimum rear-wing angle changes to decrease performance. The Porsche 911 RSR-19 received a minimum rear-wing angle change to increase performance. The Pro-class Chevrolet Corvette C8.R and the Am-category Aston Martin Vantage AMR had no performance changes.[^cite_note-30]

## Practice

The first three-hour practice session was held on the afternoon of 8 June.[^cite_note-LMProgramme-31] Quick laps were initially disregarded as teams tried to conduct race simulations.[^cite_note-RacerFP1-32] Light rain had fallen on the circuit, and the session was declared wet before cars on [slick tyres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_slick) removed water.[^cite_note-DSCFP1-33] Hartley's No. 8 Toyota lapped fastest at 3\:29.441 in the last 20 minutes. Mailleux's No. 709 Glickenhaus was second. Glickenhaus' No. 708 sister entry of [Pipo Derani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pipo_Derani) was third, ahead of [Kamui Kobayashi](9537340.md)'s No. 7 Toyota.[^cite_note-S365FP1-34] Lynn's No. 23 United Autosports car was quickest in LMP2 and fourth overall at 3\:30.238 in the session's final minutes. Félix da Costa put the No. 38 Jota car second just before practice ended, with [Prema Orlen Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prema_Powerteam)'s No. 9 entry of [Robert Kubica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Kubica) third.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-AutosportFP1-36] In LMGTE Pro, Corvette maintained its test-day pace with the No. 63 entry fastest after a lap by [Antonio García](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Garc%C3%ADa_\(racing_driver\)) demoted teammate Milner (in the No. 64 vehicle) to second. Porsche led in LMGTE Am, with the fastest class lap coming from the No. 79 [WeatherTech Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_Competition) car driven by [Julien Andlauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julien_Andlauer); brand mate [Ben Barker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Barker_\(racing_driver\))'s No. 86 [GR Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GR_Racing) car was second.[^cite_note-S365FP1-34][^cite_note-AutosportFP1-36] Several cars went off the track during the session. The [full-course yellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Yellow_flag) procedure was deployed for [Ryan Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Cullen)'s No. 10 Vector Sport car, which stopped in the Tertre Rouge S-curves. The session ended early when [Steven Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Thomas_\(racing_driver\)) damaged the No. 45 Algarve Pro Racing car's front left corner on the left tyre wall entering the Ford chicane.[^cite_note-RacerFP1-32][^cite_note-DSCFP1-33]

A red-and-white racing car

[Romain Dumas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Dumas) lapped fastest overall on the first day of practice in the No. 709 [Glickenhaus SCG 007 LMH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glickenhaus_SCG_007_LMH).

Algarve Pro installed a new chassis on the No. 45 car, which missed qualifying.[^cite_note-37] Dumas's No. 708 Glickenhaus car set the day's fastest time of 3\:28.900 24 minutes into the second, two-hour evening session, 0.348 seconds faster than Kobayashi's second-place No. 7 Toyota.[^cite_note-LMProgramme-31][^cite_note-S365FP2-38] Toyota's No. 8 sister car was third after a lap by Hartley, and [Ryan Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Briscoe)'s No. 709 Glickenhaus was fourth-quickest.[^cite_note-39] [René Rast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Rast) recorded the quickest LMP2 lap at 3\:33.119 in the No. 31 [WRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_Racing_Team) vehicle, with Albuquerque's No. 22 United Autosports USA entry second. [Dries Vanthoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dries_Vanthoor) was third with a late lap in Team WRT's No. 32 car.[^cite_note-S365FP2-38][^cite_note-RacerFP2-40] [Fabio Scherer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabio_Scherer) stopped the No. 43 Inter Europol car on the start-finish [straight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straight_\(racing\)) with right front [suspension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Car_suspension) damage from mounting the [sausage kerbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curb) at the Ford chicane;[^cite_note-RacerFP2-40] a [flatbed truck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flatbed_truck) returned the car to the pit lane.[^cite_note-DSCFP2-41] Catsburg kept Corvette first in LMGTE Pro, as Makowiecki moved the No. 91 Porsche to second. LMGTE Am was led by [Mikkel O. Pedersen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikkel_O._Pedersen)'s No. 46 [Project 1 Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_1_Motorsport) Porsche with a lap set with five minutes remaining, leading Heylen's No. 88 Dempsey-Proton car.[^cite_note-S365FP2-38]

Before the third practice session, a balance of performance adjustment gave the Alpine A480-Gibson 7 kW (9.4 hp) more power and 11 MJ (3.1 kWh) more energy per stint for a total power output of 427 kW (573 hp). The Toyota GR010 Hybrid and Glickenhaus SCG 007 LMH had no performance changes.[^cite_note-42]

The third practice session, for three hours on the afternoon of 9 June, saw Kobayashi lap fastest early in the No. 7 Toyota; Hartley's No. 8 car was second.[^cite_note-LMProgramme-31][^cite_note-S365FP3-43] Both Glickenhaus cars were third and fourth, with Pla's No. 708 entry ahead of Briscoe's No. 709 sister car. The final two-hour practice session took place on the night of 9 June.[^cite_note-LMProgramme-31] Toyota led from the start with López's No. 7 entry going fastest overall at 3\:28.322, 0.362 seconds quicker than Buemi's No. 8 sister car. The Glickenhaus team followed in third and fourth, with Derani's No. 708 entry faster than teammate [Richard Westbrook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Westbrook)'s No. 708 car. Lynn's No. 23 United Autosport car led in LMP2 at 3\:32.226, ahead of [Panis Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Panis)'s [Job van Uitert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_van_Uitert) and [Jonathan Aberdein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Aberdein)'s No. 28 Jota entry.[^cite_note-S365FP4-47][^cite_note-MCOMFP4-48] Graff driver [David Droux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Droux), in their No. 39 car, went across the gravel and crashed lightly against the tyre barrier at Mulsanne corner. Félix da Costa's No. 38 Jota car and [Alexandre Cougnaud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_Cougnaud)'s No. 3 [DKR Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DKR_Engineering) entry collided at the Ford chicane, sending Cougnaud sideways into the tyre wall. Cougnaud drove across the circuit to the pit lane for repairs.[^cite_note-S365FP4-47][^cite_note-49] Corvette continued their fast pace, with Catsburg lapping quickest in the No. 63 car in LMGTE Pro; the No. 64 sister C8R driven by Milner was second by 0.021 seconds. Dempsey-Proton led LMGTE Am with [Harry Tincknell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Tincknell)'s No. 77 Porsche ahead of teammate Heylen in the second-place No. 88 entry.[^cite_note-S365FP4-47][^cite_note-MCOMFP4-48]

## Qualifying

Divided into two sessions, the first one-hour qualifying session set the race's starting order except for the fastest six vehicles in each class; they qualified for a half-hour shootout, "Le Mans Hyperpole", which determined [pole position](630713.md) in all four classes. Cars were placed in starting order by category, with every Hypercar at the front of the field regardless of lap time, followed by LMP2, LMGTE Pro and LMGTE Am. The vehicles were placed with the six qualifying Hyperpole cars ordered by fastest Hyperpole-session lap time first, followed by the remaining non-qualifying class vehicles by fastest lap time set during the first qualifying session.[^cite_note-Regulations-9]

A green racing car

The No. 93 [Proton Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_Competition) [Porsche 911 RSR-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche_911_RSR-19) was crashed by actor Michael Fassbender in qualifying.

Each of the five entries in the Hypercar category progressed to Hyperpole, with the fastest lap a 3\:27.247 recorded by Kobayashi's No. 7 Toyota early before light rain fell during the second half of the session. In LMP2, the qualifiers were Frijns' No. 31 WRT car with entries from Jota's Félix da Costa, [Norman Nato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Nato) for [RealTeam by WRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_WRT), the United Autosports pair of Lynn and Albuquerque and Prema's [Louis Delétraz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Del%C3%A9traz). LMGTE Pro saw the six factory entrants advance to Hyperpole. The No. 92 Porsche driven by [Laurens Vanthoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurens_Vanthoor) was fastest, ahead of García's No. 63 Corvette, Porsche's No. 91 car driven by Makowiecki, the No. 64 sister Corvette of Tandy and the AF Corse Ferrari entries driven by Calado (No. 51) and [Antonio Fuoco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Fuoco) (No. 52). LMGTE Am saw [Nicki Thiim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicki_Thiim)'s No. 98 [NorthWest AMR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Martin_Racing), Jensen's Kessel Racing Ferrari, the No. 54 AF Corse Ferrari driven by [Nick Cassidy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Cassidy), [Rahel Frey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahel_Frey)'s No. 85 Iron Dames Ferrari, Dempsey-Proton Racing's Tincknell in its No. 77 Porsche and the No. 61 AF Corse Ferrari driven by [Vincent Abril](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Abril).[^cite_note-AutosportQ1-50][^cite_note-RacerQ1-51] Actor [Michael Fassbender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fassbender) lost control of the rear of Proton Competition's No. 93 Porsche braking for the first Mulsanne chicane due to a front puncture, and struck the Armco barrier with the front.[^cite_note-RacerQ1-51][^cite_note-52] Fassbender was unhurt, and the session was stopped for about 12 minutes to recover the damaged car.[^cite_note-AutosportQ1-50][^cite_note-53] The crash resulted in the Proton Competition mechanics replacing the chassis on the No. 93 Porsche.[^cite_note-54]

In Hyperpole, Hartley set the fastest lap (3\:24.408) in the No. 8 Toyota for Toyota's sixth consecutive pole position at Le Mans late in the session on worn tyres after being slowed by traffic in the Porsche Curves earlier on. Kobayashi qualified the No. 7 sister Toyota second after his fastest lap was deleted, reinstated and then invalidated again for a track-limits infringement at the Tertre Rouge corner.[^cite_note-MCOMHyperpole-55][^cite_note-56] The No. 36 Alpine qualified third after a lap by Lapierre, and the Glickenhaus entries of Briscoe (No. 709) and Pla (No. 708) were fourth and fifth.[^cite_note-MCOMHyperpole-55] The LMP2 pole position was taken by Frjins' No. 31 WRT entry, with a time of 3\:28.394. Nato's No. 41 Realteam entry was second, holding the category pole until Frijns' lap. Following in third to sixth were the lead United Autosports car of Albuquerque, the Jota entry of Félix da Costa, Delétraz's Prema car and Lynn's sister United Autosports car (after one of Lynn's laps was invalidated). Corvette Racing took the first two positions in LMGTE Pro with Tandy's No. 63 entry on category pole, with the only sub-3\:50 class lap from his teammate García's No. 64 car. The two Porsches were third and fourth, with Makowiecki's No. 91 car faster than Laurens Vanthoor's No. 92 vehicle. Abril took pole position in LMGTE Am in AF Corse's No. 61 Ferrari early in the session from Jensen's No. 57 Kessel entry and Tincknell's Dempsey-Proton Porsche after a lap by Frey in Iron Dames' No. 85 vehicle was invalidated for violating track limits.[^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58]

A grey, red and blue racing car

The No. 13 [TDS Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TDS_Racing) x [Vaillante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaillante) Oreca 07 was moved from the LMP2 Pro/Am subclass to the main LMP2 category after [Nyck de Vries](28892600.md) replaced the banned [Philippe Cimadomo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippe_Cimadomo).

After qualifying, stewards barred Cimadomo from racing at race director [Eduardo Freitas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Freitas)'s recommendation for his and other entrants' safety due to his "not driving to the standard required to safely participate in the remainder of the event" after his involvement in three incidents during free practice.[^cite_note-60][^cite_note-CimadomoBanned-59] Cimadomo was replaced by [Formula E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_E) champion [Nyck de Vries](28892600.md), and TDS' No. 13 car was moved from the LMP2 Pro/Am subclass to the main LMP2 category because De Vries held a platinum racing licence.[^cite_note-TDSdeVries-61] The FIA and ACO revised the balance of performance, increasing the LMGTE Ferrari 488 GTE Evo's turbocharger boost pressure and decreasing the A480's engine power by 13 hp (9.7 kW) and a loss of 32 MJ (8.9 kWh) of energy allowance per stint.[^cite_note-62][^cite_note-63]

## Warm-up

A 15-minute warm-up session was held on the morning of 11 June,[^cite_note-LMProgramme-31] and a number of drivers did not have a fast lap due to incidents during the session. The No. 7 Toyota of [Mike Conway](5506888.md) lapped fastest at 3\:31.236 seconds, ahead of the fastest LMP2 entry (driven by Rast) in WRT's No. 31 car; Buemi's No. 8 sister Toyota was 2.313 seconds slower in third, and second in Hypercar. The fastest LMGTE Pro lap was 3\:54.788, set by Garcia in the No. 63 Corvette, and [Toni Vilander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toni_Vilander)'s No. 21 AF Corse Ferrari paced LMGTE Am.[^cite_note-MCOMWarmUp-66] Mailleux appeared to lose control of the No. 709 Glickenhaus leaving the Mulsanne turn due to a malfunctioning [traction-control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traction_control_system) sensor before stopping on the right hand side of the circuit. A [driveline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drivetrain) fault slowed Tandy's No. 64 Corvette between the Mulsanne and Indianapolis corners, necessitating a pit stop. [Nico Müller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_M%C3%BCller) beaching the No. 10 Vector Sport car in the gravel at the Indianapolis corner and [Laurents Hörr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurents_H%C3%B6rr) losing control of the No. 3 DKR entry at the Dunlop Chicane prompted a full-course-yellow period.[^cite_note-MCOMWarmUp-66][^cite_note-67]

## Race

### Start and early hours

A race car being removed from the circuit after an accident by five people wearing orange for visibility

[United Autosports USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Autosports)'s No. 22 Oreca was involved in a first-lap accident with both [WRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_Racing_Team) entries.

The weather was dry at the start, with an air temperature of 13 to 26 °C (55 to 79 °F) and a track temperature of 17.5 to 57.5 °C (63.5 to 135.5 °F).[^cite_note-68] There were 244,200 spectators in attendance.[^cite_note-69] The [French tricolour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_France) was waved at 16\:00 [CEST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Summer_Time) by TotalEnergies CEO [Patrick Pouyanné](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Pouyann%C3%A9) to begin the race,[^cite_note-70] which was led by the pole-sitting Buemi.[^cite_note-EurosportLive-71] [Will Owen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Owen_\(racing_driver\))'s No. 22 United Autosports USA Oreca, pinched between the WRT cars of [Ferdinand Habsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Habsburg_\(racing_driver\)) and LMP2-class pole-sitter Rast at the first corner, was sent into the gravel trap. Owen required recovery by crane to return to the pit lane for repairs (which cost him two laps), and Habsburg's No. 41 car sustained front-end damage and a right rear puncture.[^cite_note-DSCH1-72][^cite_note-73] The stewards deemed Rast responsible for the accident, and imposed a one-minute stop-and-go penalty.[^cite_note-DSCH1-72][^cite_note-RacerH1-74] This moved Kubica's No. 9 Prema entry on the inside from fifth to the LMP2 lead.[^cite_note-RastMCOM-75] In LMGTE Am, Abril was unable to retain the No. 61 AF Corse Ferrari's class lead; Tincknell used the end of a slow zone at turn one to overtake Jensen and Abril in Dempsey's No. 77 Porsche.[^cite_note-DSCH1-72][^cite_note-RacerH1-74]

The race's first hour ended with [Alessio Picariello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessio_Picariello)'s No. 99 [Hardpoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_Competition) Porsche taking the LMGTE Am lead from Project 1's No. 46 car, driven by [Matteo Cairoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matteo_Cairoli).[^cite_note-DSCH1-72][^cite_note-RacerH1-74] Félix da Costa's No. 38 Jota entry became the new LMP2 leader after passing Prema's No. 9 Prema during the first pit-stop cycle.[^cite_note-R\&TLive-76] Buemi could not build up a large-enough lead over his teammate Conway due to an [oversteer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Understeer_and_oversteer), and relinquished his overall lead to the No. 7 sister car after their pit stops; Conway's stop was faster.[^cite_note-EurosportLive-71][^cite_note-RacerH1-74] Tandy had trouble downshifting for the Mulsanne corner and drove across the roundabout to rejoin the circuit, but forfeited the No. 64 Corvette's hold on the LMGTE Pro lead to García's No. 63 sister Corvette.[^cite_note-S365H3-77] The LMGTE Am class became a battle between the No. 99 Hardpoint Porsche and the No. 79 WeatherTech Racing Porsche, and Andlauer took the category lead by passing Picariello on the inside at the Indianapolis corner.[^cite_note-EurosportLive-71][^cite_note-R\&TLive-76] Glickenhaus' No. 709 car, driven by Mailleux, was forced to the garage for three minutes for mechanics to replace a faulty engine sensor which was detected by telemetry just before the start of the third hour and fell to 10th place.[^cite_note-R\&TLive-76][^cite_note-DSCH3-78][^cite_note-79]

[Dane Cameron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dane_Cameron) and [Felipe Nasr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felipe_Nasr) brought the No. 5 [Team Penske](2321219.md) Oreca car from ninth to second in LMP2 by the end of the third hour, ahead of [Lorenzo Colombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Colombo_\(racing_driver\))'s third-place No. 9 Prema car which drew to within a second of Nasr.[^cite_note-80] Lapierre was observed speeding in a slow zone, and the No. 36 Alpine received a drive-through penalty which dropped the car from the lead lap.[^cite_note-S365H3-77] Team Project's No. 46 Porsche, driven by Nicolas Leutwiler, was beached in the Mulsanne Corner gravel and required extraction to get to the pit lane before the car was retired with irreparable [ride height](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ride_height) damage.[^cite_note-RacerH4-81][^cite_note-82] The No. 7 Toyota lost the overall race lead to its No. 8 sister car when López went across the gravel at the second Mulsanne chicane on an [out-lap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_motorsport_terms#O). Vaxivière later brought the Alpine into the garage to repair an electronic clutch fault; the 13+1⁄2 minutes of clutch bleeding dropped the car out of contention.[^cite_note-RacerH4-81][^cite_note-S365H4-83] Porsche began closing on the Corvette LMGTE Pro leaders – who were lapping faster in the cooler conditions[^cite_note-DSCH3-78][^cite_note-RacerH4-81] – and overtook the No. 64 Corvette, which had downshifting problems after Tandy locked its brakes at the Mulsanne turn.[^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85]

Sims brought the No. 64 Corvette back to second in LMGTE Pro, passing Laurens Vanthoor's No. 92 Porsche on the inside at the second Mulsanne chicane.[^cite_note-EurosportLive-71][^cite_note-R\&TLive-76] In the sixth hour, Nasr's No. 5 Penske car lost its hold on second in LMP2 to [Ricky Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Taylor)'s No. 37 Cool Racing vehicle when debris caused left-front-tyre [delamination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delamination) which damaged the car's left front corner. An unscheduled two-minute, 15-second pit stop dropped the car to third in LMP2 after brake-duct and front-end replacements.[^cite_note-86][^cite_note-S365H6-87][^cite_note-MCOMH8-88] The No. 8 Toyota lost the overall lead to Kobayashi's No. 7 sister Toyota when Hirakawa was impeded by a GT car after exiting the pit lane.[^cite_note-S365H6-87] Corvette lost their hold on the top two places in LMGTE Pro when Tandy brought the No. 64 car into the garage for unscheduled brake repairs and García's No. 63 entry was forced slowly into the garage for a left rear suspension replacement.[^cite_note-R\&TLive-76][^cite_note-MCOMH8-88] This promoted Christensen's No. 92 Porsche to the LMGTE Pro lead and Bruni's No. 91 sister car to second.[^cite_note-DSCH9-89]

### Night to morning

As night fell, Pla lost control of the third-place No. 708 Glickenhaus and damaged its left rear suspension on the right guardrail leaving the Tertre Rouge corner after he struck the kerb. Pla drove the vehicle slowly to the pit lane for repairs, relinquishing the car's hold on third to Mailleux's sister Glickenhaus. The No. 7 Toyota had a slow pit stop for a tyre change and driver switch (from Kobayashi to Conway), and Buemi's No. 8 Toyota retook the lead. Conway then reclaimed the lead, passing Buemi into the Daytona Chicane on the Mulsanne Straight.[^cite_note-90][^cite_note-91] Buemi returned the No. 8 Toyota to the overall lead ahead of Conway's No. 7 entry to begin the ninth hour, extending the car's lead to half a minute when a slow zone was imposed to dry a coolant spill between the Tertre Rouge turn and the first Mulsanne Straight chicane.[^cite_note-R\&TLive-76] The No. 709 LMGTE Am-class D'station Racing Aston Martin of Charlie Fagg retired with chassis and wishbone damage.[^cite_note-92] The category became a two-car battle for the lead between the No. 79 WeatherTech Porsche and the No. 33 TF Sport Aston Martin, driven by [Marco Sørensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_S%C3%B8rensen), after Sørensen lapped faster than [Paul Dalla Lana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Dalla_Lana)'s No. 98 NorthWest AMR entry.[^cite_note-LeadChart-93][^cite_note-94]

The No. 31 WRT Oreca recovered from its early stop-and-go penalty to move to second in LMP2 after a quick stint by Frijns before Delétraz's No. 9 Prema passed Frijns' co-driver Gelael on the main straight by forcing Gelael towards the pit-lane barrier. Hirakawa's No. 8 Toyota led López's No. 7 car by almost 19 seconds at half distance, and both cars were two laps ahead of Briscoe's third-place No. 709 Glickenhaus. Jota's No. 38 Oreca, driven by Félix da Costa, still led LMP2 with a one-lap advantage over Prema's No. 9 entry of Delétraz due to the No. 38 team's fast pace and slow zones. Porsche's No. 92 team led the No. 64 Corvette by 100 seconds in LMGTE Pro.[^cite_note-S365H12-95][^cite_note-DSCH12-96] TF Sport's No. 33 Aston Martin, driven by Chaves, took the LMGTE Am lead from WeatherTech's No. 79 Porsche when driver [Cooper MacNeil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_MacNeil) lost a minute in one lap for a pit stop just before half distance.[^cite_note-DSCH12-96][^cite_note-97] The No. 8 Toyota lost the lead it had held for 86 laps[^cite_note-LeadChart-93] when a slow zone was deployed to recover West's retired LMGTE Am-category No. 59 Inception Ferrari from the Mulsanne Straight, catching Buemi off-guard and allowing Conway's No. 7 car to pass during a pit-stop cycle.[^cite_note-98][^cite_note-DSCH16-99] Porsche brought the No. 92 car into the garage for routine brake disc and pad switches, and lost the LMGTE Pro lead it had held for 104 laps to Pier Guidi's No. 51 AF Corse Ferrari.[^cite_note-LeadChart-93][^cite_note-DSCH16-99]

LMGTE Pro became a close battle between Milner's No. 64 Corvette and Pier Guidi's No. 51 AF Corse Ferrari, with both drivers close for multiple laps with Porsche's No. 92 RSR (driven by Christensen). Milner [drafted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drafting_\(aerodynamics\)) past Pier Guidi on the Mulsanne Straight and the outside of the Daytona chicane to return to the category lead.[^cite_note-DSCH16-99][^cite_note-100] The No. 92 Porsche left the battle for the class lead when Christensen understeered into the gravel at the Mulsanne corner. Christensen had a right-front puncture when he built up speed on the approach to the Indianapolis corner, and heavy damage to the car's front required him to drive slowly to the pit lane for repairs. This moved Sims' No. 64 Corvette back into the category lead, with [Daniel Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Serra)'s No. 51 AF Corse Ferrari second.[^cite_note-DSCH18-101] Before the 16th hour ended, López stopped the No. 7 Toyota at the exit of the right-hand Arnage corner with a front-axle [motor–generator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motor%E2%80%93generator) issue that made him perform a power cycle to restart the car and return to the pit lane for further troubleshooting; he lost a lap and the lead to Hartley's No. 8 sister Toyota.[^cite_note-102] [Brendan Iribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Iribe) was caught off-guard under braking for the Daytona chicane and struck the left-side [Armco barrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_barrier) with the front of the No. 56 Team Project 1 Porsche hard enough to retire the car, which was third in LMGTE Am. Iribe was unhurt and extricated himself from the car.[^cite_note-DSCH18-101][^cite_note-103]

Yellow race car

The No. 64 [Chevrolet Corvette C8.R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet_Corvette_C8.R) retired from the LMGTE Pro lead after being sent into the barrier by the No. 83 [AF Corse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AF_Corse) LMP2 car.

In the 18th hour, just after the No. 63 Corvette was retired with rear-end mechanical damage, [François Perrodo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Perrodo)'s No. 83 AF Corse Oreca was sandwiched between Sims' No. 64 Corvette and [John Falb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Falb)'s No. 47 Algarve Pro Oreca on the Mulsanne Straight after the first chicane when he veered left. Perrodo made slight left-side contact with Sims, who was sent into the left-side barrier at high speed; the damaged No. 64 Corvette was retired. Perrodo, who received a three-minute stop-and-go penalty for causing the crash, slowly entered the pit lane for rear-suspension repairs; Calado's No. 51 AF Corse Ferrari took the LMGTE Pro lead, with Makowiecki's No. 91 Porsche second.[^cite_note-DSCH18-101][^cite_note-104] Frijns lost control of the No. 31 WRT Oreca, going onto the gravel at the exit of Indianapolis turn and crashing into the left-side Armco barrier. Since the blue impact light came on, Frijns was transported to the medical centre; he was unhurt. The accident forced the No. 31 car's retirement and the safety car's only deployment, to enable repairs to the Armco wall.[^cite_note-105][^cite_note-DSCH21-106] The safety cars were recalled after fifteen minutes,[^cite_note-R\&TLive-76] and the first two cars in LMGTE Pro were together; Pier Guidi lost the No. 51 AF Corse Ferrari's advantage to Makowiecki's No. 91 Porsche. Pier Guidi had a right-rear puncture entering the Porsche Curves which forced an unscheduled pit stop, promoting the No. 91 Porsche to the LMGTE Pro lead.[^cite_note-DSCH21-106][^cite_note-107] The safety-car period reduced the LMP2-leading No. 38 Jota Oreca's gap from one lap to a half-lap, because it had to remain in the pit lane and wait for the nearest safety car.[^cite_note-108]

### Afternoon to finish

[Claudio Schiavoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudio_Schiavoni), in the No. 85 Iron Lynx Ferrari, collided with an LMP2 entry and struck the left tyre barrier leaving the Porsche Curves with four minutes left in the 21st hour. Schiavoni was unhurt, but the Ferrari was retired.[^cite_note-DSCH21-106][^cite_note-109] The second-place No. 9 Prema car of Colombo and (later) Kubica in LMP2 closed the gap to [Roberto González](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Gonz%C3%A1lez_\(racing_driver\))'s class-leading No. 38 Jota car, while the No. 28 sister Jota entry of Aberdein in third made an unscheduled pit stop to repair a slow puncture. [Sebastian Priaulx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Priaulx) brought the No. 77 Dempsey-Proton Porsche into the pit lane from third in LMGTE Am to repair a failed lower front wishbone, losing his position to [David Pittard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Pittard)'s No. 98 Aston Martin. Thomas Merrill miscalculated entering the Porsche Curves in WeatherTech's No. 79 Porsche and entered the gravel trap; he avoided striking the barriers, and continued without relinquishing his car's hold on second in LMGTE Am.[^cite_note-DSCH23-110][^cite_note-111]

The winning car, leading three others

[Brendon Hartley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendon_Hartley) driving the race-winning No. 8 Toyota GR010 Hybrid to the pit lane for the podium ceremony

Hartley maintained the overall lead his No. 8 Toyota had held for the previous 125 laps, and finished first after 380 laps; it was Buemi's fourth Le Mans victory, Hartley's third, Hirakawa's first and Toyota's fifth in succession.[^cite_note-LeadChart-93][^cite_note-MCOMWin-112] They finished two minutes and 1.222 seconds ahead of No. 7 sister Toyota; Glickenhaus made the overall Le Mans podium for the first time in third, with its No. 709 car five laps down.[^cite_note-MCOMWin-112][^cite_note-113] Jota's No. 38 Oreca won LMP2, leading for all but 15 laps,[^cite_note-LeadChart-93] to finish two minutes and 21 seconds ahead of the debuting second-place No. 9 Prema car. It was Jota's third category victory (their first since [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans)) and the maiden LMP2 Le Mans win for Félix da Costa, González and Stevens. The No. 28 sister Jota car completed the LMP2 podium in third.[^cite_note-DSCVictory-114][^cite_note-115] The No. 45 Algarve Pro team of [James Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Allen_\(racing_driver\)), [René Binder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Binder) and Thomas won the LMP2 Pro/Am subclass by one lap over the No. 24 Nielsen Racing car driven by [Matt Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Bell_\(British_racing_driver\)), [Ben Hanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Hanley) and [Rodrigo Sales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodrigo_Sales).[^cite_note-116] In the final LMGTE Pro race at Le Mans, Porsche had their first class victory since [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans) with the No. 91 RSR leading the No. 51 AF Corse Ferrari by 42.684 seconds.[^cite_note-S365LMGTEWinners-117] Aston Martin won LMGTE Am, with the No. 33 TF Sport car leading for the final 105 laps in the first category victory for Chaves, [Ben Keating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Keating) and Sørensen.[^cite_note-LeadChart-93] WeatherTech Racing placed second in the class, 44.446 seconds behind.[^cite_note-S365LMGTEWinners-117] A record 53 of the 62 starting cars finished the race.[^cite_note-DSCVictory-114][^cite_note-S365PostRace-118]

## Post-race

The overall winners on the podium, holding up their trophies

The post-race podium ceremony, with the overall top three teams

The top three teams in each of the four classes appeared on the podium to collect their trophies and spoke to the media at individual press conferences.[^cite_note-Regulations-9] Hirakawa said that he did not expect to win the race in his first year with Toyota, and his next objective was to win as many as possible.[^cite_note-119] Hartley admitted being emotional about winning and said it was an "amazing race because we were pushing each other so hard. We were giving it everything: there was nothing left on the table because it was so evenly matched."[^cite_note-120] López said that making a car run perfectly was difficult.[^cite_note-DSCVictory-114] Toyota's technical director, [Pascal Vasselon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascal_Vasselon), called the speed of the Glickenhaus cars "remarkable" given the team's inexperience.[^cite_note-121] Briscoe called Glickenhaus' podium finish "a massive accomplishment", but was disappointed at not matching Toyota's pace.[^cite_note-122] Westbrook said that finishing third felt like a victory, and Mailleux described a podium result as "so unreal".[^cite_note-DSCVictory-114]

Félix da Costa said about the LMP2 win, "The win is special, but the way to get there takes a lot. It's a collective victory."[^cite_note-DSCVictory-114] González said it was Jota's dream to win, something they had prepared for during the previous three years.[^cite_note-DSCVictory-114] Bruni said that luck was a factor in winning LMGTE Pro when the Corvette and the No. 92 Porsche encountered issues: "This is part of racing. Sometimes it works well, other times it works bad. For sure they were not lucky, Corvette was unlucky. In the end Le Mans chooses \[whether] you win or not. This is the special thing of Le Mans. Sometimes she recognises you, other times not."[^cite_note-123] Lietz said a lot of misfortune and difficulty in the preceding two years prevented Porsche from achieving a decent result and that it felt good for them to be lucky again.[^cite_note-124] Keating said that his LMGTE Am win gave him a "little bit of vindication" after a 2019 post-race fuel disqualification from first place:[^cite_note-125] "Somebody asked me what it feels like to win Le Mans, I told them this is my second! Everyone knows we won it in 2019, the difference is this one will be in the record books. There's something special about that".[^cite_note-126]

Perrodo and AF Corse team principal Amato Ferrari visited Corvette Racing's garage to apologise for the accident which caused the No. 64 car's retirement.[^cite_note-127] Perrodo said that he had no excuse: "I made a terrible mistake. It's like a small movement with huge consequences. So I had to apologise to Corvette. It's the least I can do. I know that nothing will make them feel better at this stage. It's horrible. It's horrible for me, for them, but of course particularly for them."[^cite_note-128] Sims said, "It's never nice when things happen outside of your control, but we're on track with 60 other cars and people make mistakes. I'm sure Perrodo didn't plan it, we were just in the wrong place, wrong time."[^cite_note-129] Owen believed that Rast was overly aggressive on the first lap: "That's how you cause accidents, honestly. I couldn't even react fast enough before I hit the blue car on my left and was just in the gravel. Of course, it took five or six minutes to get the car unstuck. That was quite a setback."[^cite_note-130] Rast thought that the cars behind him jumped the start, and he followed the race director's instructions: "Everybody who knows me knows that I'm a fair and hard driver, but I'm not taking somebody out on purpose. So yeah, it was the drivers behind me just jumping the start and I got the penalty for it."[^cite_note-RastMCOM-75]

The result kept Lapierre, Negrão and Vaxivière atop the Hypercar Drivers' Championship with 81 points; race winners Buemi, Hartley and Hirakawa moved from third to second, three points behind. With 69 points, Dumas and Pla fell from second to third. Bruni's and Lietz's LMGTE Pro win placed them atop the GTE Drivers' Championship with 84 points, ahead of teammates Christensen and Estre by three points. Toyota and Porsche left Le Mans as the Hypercar and GTE Manufacturers' Championship leaders, respectively, with three rounds of the season left.[^cite_note-Points-5]
