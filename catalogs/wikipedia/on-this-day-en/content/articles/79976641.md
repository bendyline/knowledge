The **2025–26 [Polish Cup](772047.md)** ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *Puchar Polski* [\[ˌpuxar ˈpɔlskʲi\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Polish)) was the 72nd season of the annual [Polish](22936.md) [football](10568.md) knockout tournament. The competition began on 5 August 2025 with the preliminary round and ended on 2 May 2026 with the final match at the [Stadion Narodowy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadion_Narodowy) in [Warsaw](32908.md). The Polish Cup is considered the second-most important club title in Polish football after the [Ekstraklasa competition](30871677.md). The competition is organised by the [Polish Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Football_Association) (PZPN). On 19 August 2025, PZPN announced that the Polish Cup would be sponsored by STS for next three seasons, making the official name **STS Puchar Polski**.[^cite_note-1] Winners of the competition will qualify for [2026–27 UEFA Europa League third qualifying round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_UEFA_Europa_League).

The defending champions were [Legia Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legia_Warsaw) were knocked out in the round of 32 by [Pogoń Szczecin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pogo%C5%84_Szczecin) in a rematch of last year’s final. [Górnik Zabrze](677580.md) defeated [Raków Częstochowa](8538149.md) 2–0 in the final to win their 7th cup title.

## Preliminary round

The matches were played on 5–6 August 2025. Participating in this round were the 2 lowest ranked teams from [2024–25 I liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_I_liga) (which finished 2024–25 season on positions 17–18) and 18 teams from the [2024–25 II liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_II_liga). With reference to the competition regulations, the matches were played according to the following scheme:[^cite_note-4]

- 17th team of [2024–25 I liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_I_liga) season will be a host of match against 18th team of [2024–25 II liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_II_liga) season,

- 18th team of I liga season will be a host of match against 17th team of II liga season,

- 1st team of II liga season will be a host of match against 16th team of II liga season,

- 2nd team of II liga season will be a host of match against 15th team of II liga season,

- 3rd team of II liga season will be a host of match against 14th team of II liga season,

- 4th team of II liga season will be a host of match against 13th team of II liga season,

- 5th team of II liga season will be a host of match against 12th team of II liga season,

- 6th team of II liga season will be a host of match against 11th team of II liga season,

- 7th team of II liga season will be a host of match against 10th team of II liga season,

- 8th team of II liga season will be a host of match against 9th team of II liga season.

## First round

The draw for this round was conducted in the headquarter of [Telewizja Polska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telewizja_Polska) on 19 August 2025.[^cite_note-FirstRoundDraw-6] The matches were played on 23–30 September 2025. Participating in this round were the 10 winners from the [previous round](#Preliminary_round), 14 teams from [2024–25 Ekstraklasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Ekstraklasa) which were not qualified to 2025–26 [UEFA](32332.md) club competitions, top 16 teams of [2024–25 I liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_I_liga) together with the 16 winners of the 2024–25 regional cup competitions. Games were hosted by teams playing in the lower division in the 2025–26 season or by first drawn team in a case of match between clubs from the same division.[^cite_note-2025-26SeasonRegulations-2]
