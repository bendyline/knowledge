**Iliman Cheikh Baroy Ndiaye** (born 6 March 2000) is a professional [footballer](10568.md) who plays as a [winger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winger_\(football\)) for [Premier League](11250.md) club [Manchester City](165813.md). Born in France, he plays for the [Senegal national team](1017436.md).

## Club career

### Early career

Ndiaye began playing football with the French clubs Rouen Sapins, [Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Rouen), and [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympique_de_Marseille) before moving to Senegal with [Dakar Sacré-Cœur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AS_Dakar_Sacr%C3%A9-C%C5%93ur). His father moved to England and Ndiaye joined him where he joined the youth side of [Boreham Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boreham_Wood_F.C.), also playing Sunday League matches with [Rising Ballers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rising_Ballers_Kensington_F.C.) to keep fit, in addition to representing his school [Westminster Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_Academy,_London).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-\:0-4] In November 2017, Ndiaye signed his first contract with [Boreham Wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boreham_Wood_F.C.).[^cite_note-5]

### Sheffield United

On 31 August 2019, Ndiaye signed with [Sheffield United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_United_F.C.).[^cite_note-6] He joined [Hyde United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyde_United_F.C.) on loan for the latter half of the 2019–20 season.[^cite_note-7] He made his professional debut with Sheffield United as a late substitute in a 5–0 [Premier League](11250.md) loss to [Leicester City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester_City_F.C.) on 14 March 2021.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-\:0-4] On his full Sheffield United debut, he scored twice against [Peterborough United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterborough_United_F.C.) on 11 September in a 6–2 win.[^cite_note-9] On 3 March 2023 in the [FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_FA_Cup), he scored in the 79th minute against [Tottenham Hotspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.) to secure a 1–0 victory and ensure Sheffield United's place in the quarter-finals.[^cite_note-10]

### Marseille

On 1 August 2023, Ndiaye signed for [Ligue 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ligue_1) club [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympique_de_Marseille), one of his former youth clubs.[^cite_note-11] The transfer fee was a reported £20 million.[^cite_note-12] He made his debut on 12 August in a 2–1 win against [Reims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stade_de_Reims).[^cite_note-13]

### Everton

On 3 July 2024, [Premier League](11250.md) club [Everton](91155.md) announced the signing of Ndiaye on a five-year deal for an undisclosed fee. His debut goal for the club was against [Doncaster Rovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doncaster_Rovers_F.C.) in the second round of the [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_EFL_Cup).[^cite_note-14]

On 18 May 2025, Ndiaye scored the last goals for Everton at [Goodison Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodison_Park), scoring both goals against [Southampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southampton_FC) in a 2–0 win for the Toffees.[^cite_note-15] He was also subsequently named Man of the Match.[^cite_note-16] Later that year, on 24 August, he scored the first goal for Everton at [Hill Dickinson Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everton_Stadium) in the Premier League, in a 2–0 victory against [Brighton & Hove Albion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighton_&_Hove_Albion_F.C.).[^cite_note-17]

### Manchester City

On 1 September 2026, Ndiaye signed for fellow Premier League club [Manchester City](165813.md) for a reported base fee of £60 million.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] He made his debut for the club in a 1–0 home victory against [Coventry City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coventry_City_F.C.) on 5 September.[^cite_note-20]

## International career



Ndiaye with [Senegal](1017436.md) at the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md)

Ndiaye was born in France to a Senegalese father and French mother.[^cite_note-21] He debuted with the [Senegal national team](1017436.md) in a 3–1 [2023 Africa Cup of Nations qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Africa_Cup_of_Nations_qualification) win over [Benin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benin_national_football_team) in the 67th minute when he was substituted on for [Boulaye Dia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boulaye_Dia) on 4 June 2022.[^cite_note-22]

In November 2022, Ndiaye was named in the final squad for the [2022 FIFA World Cup](17742072.md) in Qatar.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29] He appeared as a substitute in Senegal's second group match, assisting [Bamba Dieng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamba_Dieng)'s goal as the team beat hosts [Qatar](1111350.md) 3–1. He went on to start the team's third group match against [Ecuador](1016797.md) and round of 16 loss to [England](9904.md).[^cite_note-30]

On 24 March 2023, Ndiaye scored his first international goal in a 5–1 victory against [Mozambique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozambique_national_football_team) during an Africa Cup of Nations qualifier.[^cite_note-31] In December 2023, he was named in Senegal's squad for the postponed [2023 Africa Cup of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Africa_Cup_of_Nations) held in the [Ivory Coast](57744.md).[^cite_note-32] He appeared as a substitute in all four of the team's matches, assisting [Lamine Camara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamine_Camara)'s second goal in the opening 3–0 win over [The Gambia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambia_national_football_team) and scoring the second goal of a 2–0 win against [Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinea_national_football_team) in the final group match.[^cite_note-33]

In 2025, Ndiaye was named to the Senegal squad for the [2025 Africa Cup of Nations](57061770.md) held in [Morocco](19291.md). Ndiaye appeared in all seven games of Senegal's campaign, and scored the only goal in their quarter-final win against [Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mali_national_football_team).[^cite_note-34]

On 21 May 2026, Ndiaye was officially selected by Senegal's coach [Pape Thiaw](5728292.md) from his list of 28 players to participate in the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).[^cite_note-35] A month later, on 26 June, he scored his first World Cup goal and provided an assist in a 5–0 victory over [Iraq](1039652.md), helping Senegal confirm qualification to the knockout stage.[^cite_note-36]
