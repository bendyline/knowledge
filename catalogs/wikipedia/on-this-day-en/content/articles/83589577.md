The **Andris Kulbergs cabinet** ([Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language): *Kulberga Ministru kabinets*) is the 43rd [government](19235330.md) of [Latvia](17514.md), sworn in on 28 May 2026 after [Andris Kulbergs](83201443.md) was proposed as [Prime Minister](481129.md) by [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Latvia) [Edgars Rinkēvičs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgars_Rink%C4%93vi%C4%8Ds) and elected by the [Saeima](499765.md), with 66 votes "for".[^cite_note-3] The government succeeded the [Siliņa cabinet](74828536.md), led by [Evika Siliņa](74610811.md), after latter resigned as [Prime Minister](481129.md), after [Progressives](57853722.md) withdrew it support for the government after the party's defence minister, [Andris Sprūds](76461391.md) was forced to resign by Siliņa after multiple incidents involving stray drones suspected to be from Ukraine crossing into Latvian territory.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

The government is a majority coalition between the [New Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Unity), [Union of Greens and Farmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Greens_and_Farmers), [United List](71430208.md) and [National Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Alliance_\(Latvia\)).[^cite_note-6]
