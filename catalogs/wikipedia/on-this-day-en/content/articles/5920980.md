# FIFA Disciplinary Code

The **FIFA Disciplinary Code** (FDC) is a set of [codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_code) and [regulations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regulation) promulgated by [FIFA](11049.md)'s [judicial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary) bodies which are composed by its "Disciplinary Committee" and its "Appeal Committee".[^cite_note-1]

The FDC regulates almost all issues related to [doping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doping_\(sport\)), [corruption](66220.md), [arbitration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbitration), [racism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racism), [stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadium) [bans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ban_\(law\)), etc... It also details the [code of conduct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_of_conduct) of football's world governing body.

## Committees

The FDC decisions and regulations engages the following FIFA committees:

- Player Status Committee

- Disciplinary Committee

- Appeal Committee

- [Court of Arbitration for Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Arbitration_for_Sport)
