**Duivendrecht** (Dutch pronunciation: [\[ˈdœyvə(n)ˌdrɛxt\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Dutch)) is a village in the [Dutch](21148.md) province of [North Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Holland). It is a part of the municipality of [Ouder-Amstel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouder-Amstel), and lies about 6 km southeast of [Amsterdam](844.md)’s city centre. It is a suburb of Amsterdam.

## History

The village was first mentioned in 1308 as Doevendrecht, and means "ferry of Doeve (person)."[^cite_note-3] Duivendrecht started to developed in the 19th century and has become encircled by Amsterdam, [Diemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diemen) and the [Bijlmermeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bijlmermeer).[^cite_note-monument-4]

The Catholic St Urbanus Church is a single aisled cruciform church with a double tower. It was built between 1877 and 1878 as a replacement of the 1840 church.[^cite_note-monument-4]

The local [Duivendrecht railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duivendrecht_railway_station) is a major interchange point on the Dutch railway network. It opened in 1993 and is on the Amsterdam to [Arnhem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnhem) railway line, the [Weesp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weesp) to [Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden) line as well as the [Amsterdam Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_Metro).[^cite_note-5]

## Radio Stations (local)

The local radio station for Duivendrecht is Jamm fm 104.9 Smooth & Funky.[^cite_note-6]
