**Raqqa Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مُحافظة الرقة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Muḥāfaẓat ar-Raqqah*; [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language): *Parêzgeha Reqa*) is one of the fourteen [governorates](690220.md) of [Syria](7515849.md). It is situated in the north of the country and covers an area of 19,618 km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-Statoids-1] The capital is [Raqqa](858965.md).&#x20;

## History

### Modern Syria (1946–2011)

On November 17, 1957, during the presidency of [Shukri al-Quwatli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shukri_al-Quwatli), Al-Rashid Governorate, named after [Harun al-Rashid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harun_al-Rashid), was carved out from the adjacent [Deir ez-Zor Governorate](7445749.md). Subsequently, on January 1, 1970, the Governorate's name was changed to Raqqa Governorate.[^cite_note-2]&#x20;

### Contemporary Syria

[ISIS](9087364.md) claimed full control of this province as of August 24, 2014 when its fighters captured Tabqa Airbase in the southwest part of the province.[^cite_note-3] However, the [Syrian Democratic Forces](48205248.md) (SDF) would go on to control much of the province; all of the area north of the [Euphrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphrates) including the provincial capital of [Raqqa](858965.md) and the city of [al-Tabqah](10513014.md) were under SDF control,[^cite_note-4] with the government holding the southern part of the governorate. After [a successful offensive was launched](82106178.md) by the Syrian Transitional Government (STG), the SDF would lose control over all of Raqqa[^cite_note-5], with the Syrian government reestablishing full control over the Governate for the first time since [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raqqa_campaign_\(2012%E2%80%932013\)).&#x20;

### Syrian civil war (2011–2024) and Syrian conflict

As of 19 September 2012, the town of [Tell Abyad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tell_Abyad), directly across the border from the Turkish town of Akçakale, was already under rebel control.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]&#x20;

On 11 February 2013, Syrian rebels, including [al-Nusra Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Nusra_Front) fighters, took control of the [Tabqa Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabqa_Dam), and [Tabqa City](10513014.md), according to SOHR and videos posted by rebels. Tabqa Dam, on the [Euphrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphrates) river, is the largest hydroelectric dam in Syria and provides electricity to many areas, including [Aleppo](159244.md). Tabqa is also known as Thawrah.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]&#x20;

As of September 2014, governance has been fully reestablished with previous personnel who pledge allegiance to the new regime. Only the police and soldiers are ISIS fighters, who receive confiscated lodging previously owned by non-Sunnis and others who fled. ISIS asserts that it is providing welfare services, that it has established price controls, and that it has imposed taxes on the wealthy. The Raqqa Dam continued to provide electricity and water. The exportation of oil brought in tens of millions of dollars.[^cite_note-10]&#x20;

As of July 2017, the [Syrian Democratic Forces](48205248.md) control the majority of the province, including the city of Raqqa, which they [have captured from ISIS following a months-long siege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Raqqa_\(2017\)). A few months before the capture of Raqqa, the Syrian government launched the [Southern Raqqa offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Southern_Raqqa_offensive), which cleared southern Raqqa and southeastern [Aleppo](7444618.md) from [ISIS](9087364.md) militants. With the south part of the governorate under government control and the northern part under Kurd-led [AANES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Autonomous_Administration_of_North_and_East_Syria) (Rojava), the governorate has been fully cleared from ISIS. On 18 January 2026, the [Syrian transitional government](79584988.md) captured the majority of the governorate during the [2026 northeastern Syria offensive](82106178.md).&#x20;

## Geography

### Settlements

[Raqqa](858965.md) is the provincial capital; other major settlements include Abu Hamad, Abu Susah, Al Hawrah, Al Qaltah, [Al-Sabkhah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Sabkhah), [Al-Tabqah](10513014.md), Ar Ruhayyat, Ar Rusafah, Ath Thadyayn, Bash Dulki, Dulq Maghar, Fatsat ath Thayb, Hamrat Nasir, Jubb al Abyad, Kasrat Muraybit, [Ma'adan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma%27adan), Matir, Nasiriyah, [Suluk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suluk,_Syria) and [Tell Abyad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tell_Abyad).&#x20;
