The **prime minister of Peru** ([Spanish](26825.md): *primer ministro del Perú*),[^cite_note-j565-2] officially the **president of the Council of Ministers** (Spanish: *Presidente del Consejo de Ministros*), serves as head of the [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_\(government\)) and as the most senior member of the Council of Ministers, being appointed by the [president of Peru](239109.md) (pending ratification by [Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_Peru), as with all other cabinet members). The person heads the presidency of the Council of Ministers (Spanish: *Presidencia del Consejo de Ministros*, *PCM*) of [Peru](170691.md), the [governmental organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ministries_of_Peru) that oversees the [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Peru) and coordinates and monitors the multisectoral policies and programmes of the [executive branch](239109.md), as well as coordinates actions with the [legislative branch](1571007.md), the autonomous constitutional bodies, and the entities and commissions of the executive branch, aligning priorities to ensure compliance with objectives of national interest.

The prime minister is not the executive [head of government](37570.md), since the president of Peru serves both as head of state and head of government, similar to the [prime minister of South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_the_Republic_of_Korea). Peru is one of the few countries in [Latin America](18524.md) (others include [Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Cuba), and [Haiti](3972111.md)) where the position of prime minister currently exists. For alternative reasons, Peru is sometimes nonetheless considered a [semi-presidential republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-presidential_republic) where the head of government (the President) is accountable to the Congress.

## History

The office was created in 1856, when the [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Peru) was itself created through that year's constitution.[^cite_note-3] Under the governments of [Mariano Ignacio Prado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariano_Ignacio_Prado) (1865–1867) and [Nicolás de Piérola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicol%C3%A1s_de_Pi%C3%A9rola) (1879–1881), the ministries took the names of *secretariats*, and the head of state directly oversaw the functions attributed to the president of the Council of Ministers.&#x20;

[Luis Galarreta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Galarreta) serves as the incumbent prime minister since 28 July 2026.

- Senior Management&#x20;

  - President of the Council of Ministers

  - General Secretariat of the Presidency of the Council of Ministers

- Institutional Control Body

- Judicial Defense Body

- Advisory Bodies&#x20;

  - General Office of Legal Counsel

  - General Office of Planning and Budget

- Support Bodies&#x20;

  - General Office of Administration

  - General Office of Social Communication

- Line Bodies&#x20;

  - Coordination Secretariat

  - Public Management Secretariat

  - Decentralization Secretariat

- Specialized Technical Bodies&#x20;

  - National Technical Directorate of Territorial Demarcation

  - National Office of Electronic Government and Information Technology

- Vice Ministry of Territorial Governance&#x20;

  - Decentralisation Secretariat

  - Social Management and Secretariat Dialogue

  - Secretariat of Demarcation and Territorial Organization

- Permanent Coordination Committees

- Attached Committees and Collegiate Bodies

- National Commission for Development and Life without Drugs (DEVIDA)

- [National Institute of Statistics and Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_Nacional_de_Estad%C3%ADstica_e_Inform%C3%A1tica) (INEI)

- Energy and Mining Investment Supervisory Agency (OSINERGMIN)

- Public Transportation Infrastructure Investment Supervisory Agency (OSITRAN)

- National Superintendency of Services and Sanitation (SUNASS)

- Secretariat FONAVI Technical Support for the Ad Hoc Commission (FONAVI-ST)

- High-Level Anti-Corruption Commission (CAN)

- Commission Responsible for Proposing Administrative and Management Reform Actions and Measures for the Reorganisation of the Presidential Office (CRDP)

- [National Coca Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Coca_Company) (ENACO S.A)

- Citizen Service Centres (MAC)
