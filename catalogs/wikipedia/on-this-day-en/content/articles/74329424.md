**IRIS *Dena*** (75) ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): ناوشکن دنا) was a [*Moudge*-class](10946960.md) [frigate](11464.md) in the Southern Fleet of the [Islamic Republic of Iran Navy](2160969.md). She was named after Mount [Dena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dena), and was commissioned into the navy in 2021.

[She was sunk on 4 March 2026](82572206.md) during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md) by the [*Los Angeles*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles-class_submarine) [submarine](28825.md) [USS *Charlotte*](1121727.md) of the [United States Navy](20518076.md) in international waters near the southern coast of [Sri Lanka](26750.md).&#x20;

She was the only ship to be sunk in active combat by a submarine since 1982, when the Argentine Navy [cruiser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruiser) [ARA *General Belgrano*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARA_General_Belgrano) was sunk by torpedoes from the [Royal Navy](26061.md) nuclear submarine [HMS *Conqueror*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Conqueror_\(S48\)) during the [Falklands War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falklands_War), and the only to be sunk by an American submarine since the [Pacific theater of World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_War).[^cite_note-Cheonan-4]

## Description



*Dena* during her commissioning in 2021



Rear view of IRIS *Dena*

*Dena* was a [*Moudge*-class](10946960.md) [frigate](11464.md), described as both a [destroyer](8779.md) and [frigate](11464.md) by the English media, and was known as a novshekan (ناوشکن) in [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language), which translates to "destroyer". The ship was 94 m (308 ft 5 in)–long, 11 m (36 ft 1 in)–wide, and [displaced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Displacement_\(ship\)) about 1,300 to 1,500 tons. Similar to other ships of her class, she had a [landing pad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helipad) to host a [helicopter](8286923.md).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-Hutton-6]

*Dena* was the first Iranian ship equipped with the Iranian "Bonyan 4" engines. She had four engines, each generating 5,000 hp (3,700 kW) for a total power of 20,000 hp (15,000 kW). She had a [bow thruster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maneuvering_thruster) system for increased maneuverability.[^cite_note-7] She was equipped with an [Asr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asr_\(radar\)) [phased array](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phased_array) [radar](25676.md).[^cite_note-Hutton-6]

### Armament

As per [Iranian Navy Commander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Iranian_Navy) [Hossein Khanzadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hossein_Khanzadi), the ship carried a significantly greater armament compared with the frigates of the same class. According to declarations from 2019, *Dena* was to be equipped with a [vertical launching system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_launching_system), a first for an Iranian ship.[^cite_note-8] However, the ship was eventually equipped with two [Sayyad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayyad_\(missile\)) missiles[^cite_note-Hutton-6] and photographs show two container launchers before a bridge, like on earlier ships. The ship was equipped with [surface-to-air](182664.md) and [anti-ship missiles](140725.md). She had an armament of four [Ghader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghader_\(missile\)) anti-ship missiles, a 76 mm [Fajr-27](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fajr-27) naval gun, a 40 mm Fath-40 AA cannon, two 20 mm [Oerlikon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oerlikon_20_mm_cannon) cannons, two 12.7 mm heavy machine guns, and two triple 324 mm anti-submarine [torpedo](31424.md) launchers.[^cite_note-Hutton-6] At the time of her sinking, however, *Dena* was reportedly unarmed according to the protocol of a naval exercise hosted by India, which she had just visited.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

## Service history

*Dena* was launched in 2015, and was commissioned into the [Iran Navy](2160969.md) in 2021.[^cite_note-12] In 2022–2023, *Dena* and [IRIS *Makran*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRIS_Makran) of the 86th naval fleet performed [the 360-Degree mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/360-Degree_mission_of_Iran's_86th_Naval_Fleet) around the world, a historic mission for the Iranian Navy.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

In February 2026, *Dena* participated at the [International Fleet Review 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Fleet_Review_2026) held at the Indian port of [Visakhapatnam](238760.md).[^cite_note-15] Following the Fleet Review on 18 February, the ship docked at the Indian port on 20 February. On the same day, the Commander of the Iranian Navy, Commodore [Shahram Irani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahram_Irani), who was on a visit to [India](14533.md) for participating in various events including the Fleet Review, [Indian Ocean Naval Symposium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean_Naval_Symposium) and the [2026 Milan exercise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan_\(naval_exercise\)#2026), met the Indian [Chief of the Naval Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_the_Naval_Staff_\(India\)), [Admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiral_\(India\)) [Dinesh K Tripathi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinesh_K_Tripathi).[^cite_note-16]

### Sinking

Main article: [Sinking of IRIS *Dena*](82572206.md)

*Dena* being torpedoed by [USS Charlotte](1121727.md)

On 4 March 2026, amid the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), *Dena* transmitted a distress call at 05\:08 hrs (UTC+05\:30), 19 [nautical miles](21289.md) (35 km; 22 mi)[^cite_note-17] off the coast from [Galle](426566.md), [Sri Lanka](26750.md), prompting an immediate search and rescue operation by the [Sri Lanka Navy](6186841.md) and [Sri Lanka Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka_Air_Force).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] The ship sank before the Sri Lanka Navy could reach the area.[^cite_note-20] The Sri Lanka Navy rescued 32 sailors, who were sent to the [Galle National Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galle_National_Hospital).[^cite_note-21] Later, the Sri Lanka Navy recovered the bodies of 87 sailors.&#x20;

The United States confirmed on 4 March that *Dena* had been sunk by [USS *Charlotte*](1121727.md). She fired two [Mark 48 torpedoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_48_torpedo), of which one hit *Dena*.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] The ship became the only one to be sunk by an American submarine since the [Pacific theater of World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_War).[^cite_note-Cheonan-4][^cite_note-25] The warship was reportedly unarmed at the time of the incident.[^cite_note-26]
