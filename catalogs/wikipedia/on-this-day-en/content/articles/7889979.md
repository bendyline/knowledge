# Mathare



The Mathare Valley slum

**Mathare** is a collection of [slums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slum) in [Nairobi](21482.md) with a population of approximately 500,000 people;[^cite_note-NYT-1] the population of [Mathare Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathare_Valley) alone, the oldest of the slums that make up Mathare, is 180,000 people. Mathare is the home of [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_\(soccer\)) teams [Mathare United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathare_United) and Real Mathare of the [MYSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathare_Youth_Sports_Association).[^cite_note-ghetto-2] Mathare is currently part of two electoral constituencies; the titular [Mathare Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathare_Constituency_\(2013\)) and the northern part being in [Ruaraka Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruaraka_Constituency). The northern part was initially part of [Kasarani Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasarani_Constituency) up to the [2013 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Kenyan_general_election) when Kasarani was split into three electoral constituencies; Ruaraka being among them. The southern part was domiciled in [Starehe Constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starehe_Constituency).

## Gang violence

In 2006, Mathare was damaged by violence between rival gangs the [Taliban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taliban_\(gang\)) (not to be confused with the [Islamist group](30635.md) of the same name), a [Luo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luo_\(Kenya_and_Tanzania\)) group, and the [Mungiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mungiki), a [Kikuyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kikuyu_people) group.[^cite_note-NYT-1] Brewers of an illegal alcoholic drink, [chang'aa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang'aa), asked the Taliban for help after the Mungiki tried to raise their taxes on the drink; since then, fighting between the two has led to the burning of hundreds of homes and at least 10 deaths.[^cite_note-NYT-1] Police entered the slum on November 7, 2006, and the [General Service Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Service_Unit_\(Kenya\)) arrived a day later. However, many residents who fled are still afraid to return.[^cite_note-NYT-1]

On June 5, 2007, the Mungiki murdered two police officers in Mathare; the same night, police retaliated by killing 22 people and detaining around 100.[^cite_note-nyt2-3]

Following the controversial [presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenyan_presidential_election,_2007) that took place on December 27, 2007, gangs of Kikuyu and Luo youth engaged in violent fights and burned more than 100 homes.[^cite_note-Jeff-4]
