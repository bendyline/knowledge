**Anéfis** or **Anefif** is a [Saharan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahara_Desert) village and [commune](16112417.md) in the [Cercle of Kidal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidal_Cercle) in the [Kidal Region](2214311.md) of north-eastern [Mali](19127.md).

## Geography

Anefif is located on the [Tessalit Trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessalit_Trail?action=edit\&redlink=1). It lies 95 km (59 mi) southwest of [Kidal](1463133.md) on the route linking Kidal to [Bourem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourem) and [Gao](1255721.md). In 2009 the commune had a population of 5,087.[^cite_note-pop2009-3]

There are multiple rocks and stones made from volcanic material.[^cite_note-4]

## History

In 2013, the [Malian Military](19135.md) attacked [Tuareg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuareg_rebellion_\(2012\)) rebels living in the village. The war was highly criticized by the military, and the press, who claimed that it was too invasive and violent.[^cite_note-5]

## Climate

The commune has a Saharian climate and is too dry for rain-fed agriculture. The annual rainfall of around 150 mm occurs between late June and early September.[^cite_note-precaged-2]

## Kel Adagh

There are many [Kel Adagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kel_Adagh) in the area, along with multiple small [clans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clan).[^cite_note-6]
