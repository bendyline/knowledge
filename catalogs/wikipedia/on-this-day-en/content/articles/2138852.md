**Del Gallego**, officially the **Municipality of Del Gallego** ([Central Bikol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bikol_language): *Banwaan kan Del Gallego*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Del Gallego*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Camarines Sur](255883.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 26,029 people.[^cite_note-5]

## History

It is used to be called Danawin and located along Kilbay River (now Sabang River). It was established through [Executive Order No. 56 Series of 1936](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1936/10/05/executive-order-no-56-s-1936/) signed by then President [Manuel L. Quezon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_L._Quezon).

## Geography

Del Gallego is the last town in the northwestern part of Camarines Sur. This is where the Quirino Highway (Andaya Highway) road meets with the first town of [Quezon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon), [Tagkawayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagkawayan,_Quezon). It is 109 kilometres (68 mi) from [Pili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pili,_Camarines_Sur) and 287 kilometres (178 mi) from [Manila](184334.md).

## Transportation

The municipality is connected with Manila by the [Andaya Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaya_Highway) and daily rail services to and from Naga and Legazpi are provided by the [Philippine National Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_National_Railways).

In order to spur development in the municipality, the Toll Regulatory Board declared Toll Road 5 the extension of the [South Luzon Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Luzon_Expressway).[^cite_note-22] A 420-kilometer, four lane expressway starting from the terminal point of the now under construction SLEX Toll Road 4 at Barangay Mayao, [Lucena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucena), [Quezon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon) to [Matnog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matnog), [Sorsogon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorsogon), near the Matnog Ferry Terminal. On August 25, 2020, [San Miguel Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Miguel_Corporation) announced that they will invest the project which will reduce travel time from Lucena to Matnog from 9 hours to 5.5 hours.[^cite_note-23]

Another expressway that will serve Del Gallego is the [Quezon-Bicol Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon-Bicol_Expressway) (QuBEx), which will link between Lucena and [San Fernando, Camarines Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Fernando,_Camarines_Sur).[^cite_note-24]

## Education

The Del Gallego Schools District Office governs all educational institutions within the municipality. It oversees the management and operations of all private and public, from primary to secondary schools.[^cite_note-25]

Del Gallego has a total of 23 schools, twenty of which are public schools and the other three are private schools.
