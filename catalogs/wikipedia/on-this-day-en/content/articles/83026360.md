The **Eighth General Conference of Fatah**, officially known as the **Eighth General Conference of the Palestinian National Liberation Movement** (Fatah), opened on 14 May 2026,[^cite_note-4] with the attendance and participation of 2,514 members. The opening date of the conference coincided with the [Nakba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakba) anniversary, commemorating the [founding of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Declaration_of_Independence) in 1948.

The General Conference constitutes the highest legislative and movement authority within [Fatah](11104.md) and undertakes the review of the political programme and the basic statutes as well as the election of the leadership bodies. Fatah is the de facto ruling party of [Areas A and B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bank_areas_in_the_Oslo_II_Accord) in the [West Bank](33209.md). The seventh conference was held in 2016. It aims to renew the leadership structures via elections to the [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_Fatah) and the [Revolutionary Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatah_Revolutionary_Council).

On 29 April 2026, 2,514 members were approved for participation and the “Direct Link” mechanism was announced to ensure cadre involvement in the Gaza Strip, Lebanon, and Egypt.[^cite_note-5] [Mahmoud Abbas](232595.md) was unanimously re-elected Chairman of Fatah, drawing criticism from some due to his low approval ratings.[^cite_note-6]
