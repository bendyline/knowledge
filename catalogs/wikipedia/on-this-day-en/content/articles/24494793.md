**Sheikh Ijlin** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الشيخ عجلين[^cite_note-1]) is a neighborhood in southern [Gaza City](55387.md) near the coastal road.[^cite_note-2]

On 18 November 2002, the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) (IDF) attacked the neighborhood, destroying the offices of the [Preventive Security Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preventive_Security_Force) of the [Palestinian National Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_National_Authority).[^cite_note-3]

An internationally-backed sewage cleanup in 2022 saw Gaza's [sewage treatment plants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sewage_treatment) increase their operations, resulting in a reduction of waste entering the sea. This resulted in the Mediterranean Sea being clean enough to swim in and the neighborhood's beaches became popular in Gaza City.[^cite_note-4]

In December 2023 *[The New York Times](30680.md)* reported that Israel had razed cemeteries in the [Gaza Strip](12047.md) including that of Sheikh Ijlin.[^cite_note-israel-razes-cemeteries-5]
