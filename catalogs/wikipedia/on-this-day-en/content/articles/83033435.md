# Abdul Halim Aman

In this [Malay name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysian_names#Malay_names), there is no [surname or family name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname). The name *Aman* is a [patronymic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patronymic), and the person should be referred to by their [given name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Given_name), *Abdul Halim*. The word "bin"/"ibn" or "binti"/"binte" means 'son of' or 'daughter of', respectively.

**Abdul Halim bin Aman** ([Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_language): عبدالحليم بن أمان; born 10 March 1957) is a Malaysian judge who served as the [seventh chief commissioner of the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission](25431607.md) since May 2026, replacing [Azam Baki](69258517.md).[^cite_note-1] He also served as the Judge of the Penang High Court in 2009 until 2010, Judge of the Johor Bahru High Court in 2010 until 2013 and Judge of the Shah Alam High Court from 2013 until 2023.

## Early life and education

Abdul Halim bin Aman was born on 10 March 1957 at Kampung Alai, [Malacca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malacca), [Malaysia](3607937.md). He received his [Bachelor of Laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Laws) (LLB) from the [Universiti Malaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universiti_Malaya) (UM) on 1982 and [Master of Laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Laws) (LLM) from the [University of Hull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hull) on 1996.

## Legal career (1982–2023)

Abdul Halim began his legal career in 1982 as a Federal Counsel at the [Attorney General's Chambers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney_General's_Chambers_of_Malaysia). In 1983, he was appointed as a Magistrate in [Kluang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kluang), [Johor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johor). He later served as Senior Assistant Registrar of the High Court of Malaya in [Malacca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malacca) in 1985. In 1987, Abdul Halim became a Senior Magistrate in [Muar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muar_\(town\)), Johor. He was appointed as a Sessions Court Judge in [Tawau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tawau), [Sabah](28678.md) in 1990 and subsequently served in the same capacity in [Sandakan](1732138.md), [Sabah](28678.md) in 1992. That same year, he was also appointed as Deputy Registrar of the Federal Court and later served as a Sessions Court Judge in [Kuala Lumpur](16854.md).

In 1993, Abdul Halim took on the role of Senior Federal Counsel at the [Inland Revenue Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inland_Revenue_Board_of_Malaysia) before returning to the Attorney General's Chambers in 1995 in the same capacity. In 1994, he had served as a Sessions Court Judge in [George Town, Penang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Town,_Penang). In 1996, Abdul Halim was appointed as Official Assignee at the Department of Insolvency (then known as the Department of Official Assignee). The following year, in 1997, he became the [State Legal Adviser of Pahang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahang_State_Executive_Council#Ex_officio_members).

In 2003, Abdul Halim served concurrently as Deputy Public Prosecutor at the Attorney General's Chambers, Sessions Court Judge in [Ampang, Selangor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ampang,_Selangor) and [Head of Research Division at the Office of the Chief Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Justice_of_Malaysia).

In 2005, he was appointed as a Judicial Commissioner at the High Court in [Temerloh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temerloh) before being transferred to [Kuantan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuantan) in 2006. Shortly thereafter, Abdul Halim was appointed as a High Court Judge on 11 April 2007. In 2009, he began serving as a [Judge of the High Court in Penang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penang_High_Court) (2009) and [Johor Bahru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johor_Bahru) High Court (2010) before continuing a lengthy tenure at the [Shah Alam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shah_Alam) High Court from 2013 until his mandatory retirement in March 2023.

## 7th chief commissioner of MACC (2026–present)

He will be appointed as the [seventh chief commissioner of the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission](25431607.md) on 13 May 2026.
