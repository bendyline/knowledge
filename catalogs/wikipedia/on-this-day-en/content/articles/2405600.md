**Linstead** is a town in the [parish](23623.md) of [St. Catherine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Catherine_Parish), [Jamaica](15660.md), in the [West Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Indies). In 2011 its population was 15,231.[^cite_note-1] It is located 12 miles (19 km) NNW of [Spanish Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Town).&#x20;

## Description

Close to [Ewarton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ewarton) and [Windalco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WINDALCO) Ewarton works, a large aluminum plant employing many of the local population. Often employing a [job share](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_sharing) scheme as work in the area is scarce. It holds a Grand Market celebration each [Christmas Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas_Eve) when people come out and fill the streets, small shops sell toys and other Christmas gifts, and events and parties are held in the square. It was popularized in the song [Linstead Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linstead_Market).&#x20;

## Transportation

The  [A1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_road_\(Jamaica\)) road passes directly through Linstead, while the [B13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roads_in_Jamaica) also crosses the town, running through it via Main Road and the Linstead–Oracabessa Road. The town is served by the Linstead/Ewarton interchange on  [T3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T3_road_\(Jamaica\)), the Edward Seaga Highway, where a tolled access road connects the A1 to the highway, providing service to both Linstead and [Ewarton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ewarton).&#x20;
