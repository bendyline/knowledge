**Dorcheh** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): درچه)[^cite_note-6] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Khomeynishahr_County\)) of [Khomeynishahr County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khomeynishahr_County), [Isfahan](448061.md) province, [Iran](14653.md).[^cite_note-Isfahan_Province_Structure-7]

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 43,183 in 12,038 households.[^cite_note-2006_Isfahan_Province-8] The following census in 2011 counted 44,689 people in 13,888 households.[^cite_note-2011_Isfahan_Province-9] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 47,800 people in 25,411 households.[^cite_note-2016_Isfahan_Province-3]
