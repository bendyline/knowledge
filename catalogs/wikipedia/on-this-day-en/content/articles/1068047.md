**Dimitris Avramopoulos** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Δημήτρης Αβραμόπουλος; born 6 June 1953) is a Greek [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician) of the conservative [New Democracy](424225.md) party, and former career [diplomat](82433.md). He has served in various high-level cabinet posts, including [Minister for Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Foreign_Affairs_\(Greece\)) and [Minister for National Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_National_Defence_\(Greece\)), and was [Mayor of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Athens) from 1995 to 2002. He served as [EU Commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU_Commissioner) for [Migration, Home Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_European_Commission_portfolios#Home_Affairs) and [Citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Commissioner_for_Justice,_Fundamental_Rights_and_Citizenship) in the [Juncker Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juncker_Commission) between 2014 and 2019.

## Personal life

Avramopoulos was born in Athens in 1953, into a family which had originally come from [Ilia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elis_\(regional_unit\)) and [Elliniko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliniko,_Arcadia) in [Arcadia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcadia_\(regional_unit\)). He served his 26 months [military service](938728.md) from 1978 to 1980 in the [Hellenic Air Force](363254.md).[^cite_note-avramopoulos.gr-1]

He is married to Vivian, with whom he has two sons, Filippos and Iasonas. Apart from his native [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language), he speaks [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language) and [Italian](14708.md) fluently.

## Diplomatic career

In 1980, Avramopoulos joined the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Greece\)) in [Athens](1216.md), where he worked until 1993. From 1988 to 1992, he served as Greek [Consul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consul_\(representative\)) to Belgium in [Liège](28341707.md). At the same time he was a Special Adviser to [Konstantinos Mitsotakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konstantinos_Mitsotakis), President and Leader of the [New Democracy](424225.md). During this time he also represented Greece in [Vienna](55866.md) at the [Conference for Security and Co-operation in Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_for_Security_and_Co-operation_in_Europe). In 1992 he became official spokesman of the Greek Ministry of Foreign Affairs and was appointed [Consul General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consul_General) of Greece in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva). In 1993 he was promoted to director of the [Prime Minister of Greece](273647.md)'s Diplomatic Office.[^cite_note-2]

## Political career



Dimitri Avramopoulos, Foreign Minister of Greece, with President [Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama) and the [First Lady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Obama), at the 67th UN General Assembly in 2012

### Mayor of Athens

In 1993, Avramopoulos resigned from Greek diplomatic service to enter [parliamentary politics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_politics) as a member of [New Democracy](424225.md) and was elected a member of its Central Committee. From 1993 to 1994 he served as a [Member of the Hellenic Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Hellenic_Parliament). In 1994 he was elected [Mayor of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Athens), and was re-elected in October 1998 in a historic first-round landslide victory. From 1995 to 1999 he served as chairman of the *Central Union of Local Authorities* of Greece.

In 1995 he founded the “Permanent Conference of the Mayors of the Capitals of South-East Europe” and served as its first President. From 1996 to 2000 he served as vice-president of the executive committee of the [International Union of Local Authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Union_of_Local_Authorities) (IULA) and from 1997 to 2002, as a member of the [Committee of the Regions of the European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_the_Council_of_Europe). Elected as president of the [Summit Conference of the Mayors of the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Cities_Summit) in 2000, he served until 2002, during which time he founded the "*World Union of Olympic Cities*", "*Athens’ International Prize for Democracy*", and “*World Institute of Global and Cities’ Diplomacy*”, [Rome](25458.md).

### Ministerial posts

In March 2001, he split from New Democracy with his [Movement of Free Citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_of_Free_Citizens_\(Greece\)) (KEP), but in a surprising move merged back in June 2002. When in 2004 New Democracy won the [parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Greek_legislative_election), Avramopoulos was appointed [Minister of Tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Tourism_\(Greece\)), serving until 2006, and afterwards, from 2006 until 2009, as [Minister of Health and Social Solidarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_and_Social_Solidarity).[^cite_note-3]

In the [October 2009 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Greek_legislative_election), Avramopoulos was re-elected to the [Hellenic Parliament](2449826.md) representing the constituency of [Athens A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens_A). After the election of [Antonis Samaras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonis_Samaras) to the “New Democracy” Party Presidency he was appointed President of the Organizing Committee of the 8th Party Congress held at Athens in June 2010. In July 2010, he became Vice-President of “New Democracy”, serving until 1 November 2014.

On 11 November 2011 he was appointed [Minister for National Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_National_Defence_\(Greece\)) in the coalition government of [Lucas Papademos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_Papademos), resigning from his [parliamentary seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_seat), thus adhering to the New Democracy policy that no serving Opposition Party [Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Greek_Parliament) may hold [ministerial office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministerial_office).

At the 6 May 2012 election, Avramopoulos was returned as Deputy for Athens A, being re-elected in the 17 June 2012 general election. On 21 June 2012 he was appointed [Minister for Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Foreign_Affairs_\(Greece\)). After the Cabinet reshuffle of 25 June 2013 he became Greek Minister for Defence again, serving until his nomination as Greece's [European Commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Commissioner) in November 2014.

### European Commissioner for Migration and Home Affairs



Avramopoulos and Bulgarian Prime Minister [Boyko Borisov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyko_Borisov) in 2018

On 27 July 2014, Prime Minister Antonis Samaras nominated Avramopoulos as Greece's next member of the [European Commission](9974.md).[^cite_note-auto-4] Before the nomination, [Dora Bakoyiannis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dora_Bakoyiannis) was widely seen to be a strong contender for the Commission post.[^cite_note-auto-4] Samaras also defied calls from the centre-left Pasok party for [Maria Damanaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Damanaki) to continue as European Commissioner.[^cite_note-auto-4]

[Jean-Claude Juncker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Claude_Juncker) then nominated Avramopoulos as European Commissioner for Migration and Home Affairs. In this capacity, Avramopoulos shares competency over cyber-security matters with [Günther Oettinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G%C3%BCnther_Oettinger).[^cite_note-5]

In the aftermath of the victory of the left-wing [SYRIZA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SYRIZA) party in the [25 January election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_2015_Greek_legislative_election) in 2015, Avramopoulos' name was widely circulated in Greek media as the most likely choice for SYRIZA's candidate in the second round of the [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_presidential_election,_2014%E2%80%9315) for the [Presidency of Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Greece). According to the Greek media reports, the nomination would be seen both as a gesture of conciliation with the right, but would crucially also enable SYRIZA to nominate its own European Commissioner.[^cite_note-6] On 30 January Samaras phoned Avramopoulos and assured him that *New Democracy* would support his eventual nomination.[^cite_note-7]

Avramopoulos criticized the [2019 Turkish offensive into north-eastern Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Turkish_offensive_into_north-eastern_Syria). He said that "the European Union remains committed to the unity, sovereignty and territorial integrity of the Syrian state".[^cite_note-8]

### Member of Parliament

Avramopoulos was elected to the [Hellenic Parliament](2449826.md) in [Elis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elis_\(constituency\)) at the [June 2023 Greek legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2023_Greek_legislative_election).

### Qatargate

From February 2021 to February 2022, Avramopoulos was paid €5,000 per month as a member of the board of the Brussels-based [Non-governmental organization](46539.md) (NGO) Fight Impunity, having received approval from the EU ethics panel to take up the position. The founder and director of the NGO, [Antonio Panzeri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Panzeri) later confessed to orchestrating the [Qatar corruption scandal at the European Parliament](72479300.md). In June 2026, the Belgian judiciary confirmed that a [European arrest warrant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_arrest_warrant) had been issued for Avramopoulos in connection with the investigation into the scandal. Avramopoulos denied any wrongdoing.[^cite_note-9] Later that month, Avramopoulos appealed to the [Supreme Court of Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Civil_and_Criminal_Court_of_Greece) to annul the arrest warrant on the grounds that it failed to include sufficient detail of the charges against him.[^cite_note-10]

## Other positions

From the outset of his political career, Avramopoulos served for twenty years as Honorary President of the *Athens’ International Prize for Democracy* for [UNESCO](21786641.md) in [Paris](22989.md) (until 2013). He has also been Chairman of the Steering Committee on Cities´ Diplomacy, established by the Global Forum (Rome) and the World Bank Institute (Washington D.C.). He was elected President of the “World Institute of Global and Cities’ Diplomacy”, an independent NGO based in Rome as well as Executive President of the “World Union of Olympic Cities”, an [Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IOC) NGO.

## Greek-Turkish rapprochement

Avramopoulos has a friendly relation with the Turkish President [Recep Tayyip Erdoğan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recep_Tayyip_Erdo%C4%9Fan) since they were Mayors of Athens and Istanbul respectively.[^cite_note-11] He is deemed one of the main proponents of [Greek-Turkish rapprochement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek%E2%80%93Turkish_relations). Also this year the two former Mayors came together to discuss topics regarding Migration, Borders and Security on European and EU-level in [Istanbul](3391396.md) on 3 June 2019.[^cite_note-12] On the same day, Avramopoulos also met with Turkish Interior Minister [Süleyman Soylu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%BCleyman_Soylu) to discuss about the same topic. [^cite_note-13]

## Academic degrees

Avramopoulos read Public Law and Political Science at [Athens University Law School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_and_Kapodistrian_University_of_Athens), graduating with the degree of [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) (BA). He then undertook postgraduate studies receiving a [Master's degree](170346.md) in European Studies at the Institute of European Affairs, [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universit%C3%A9_libre_de_Bruxelles). He has been conferred honorary doctorates by [Adelphi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelphi_University) (Long Island, New York), [Deree College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deree_College) (Athens), [Drexel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drexel_University) (Philadelphia) and [Kingston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston_University) (London), and has been elected Honorary Professor of [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) (Beijing) and of the European College of Parma (Italy).[^cite_note-avramopoulos.gr-1]

### Distinctions and awards

Avramopoulos received, in 2006, the “Vincitore Assoluto” Award of the Premio Internazionale “Giuseppe Sciacca”, and has been presented with distinctions by many foreign Cities and Municipalities: Famagusta, [Barcelona](4443.md), [Beijing](18603746.md), [Beirut](37428.md), [Berlin](3354.md), [Boston](24437894.md), Brooklyn N.Y., Bucharest, Caracas, [Chicago](6886.md), [Crotone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crotone), [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence), [Genoa](47332321.md), [Jakarta](16275.md), [Havana](49719.md), [Istanbul](3391396.md), [Kyiv](585629.md), Ljubljana, [Los Angeles](18110.md), [Miami](53846.md) -[Florida](18933066.md), [Massachusetts](1645518.md), [Montreal](7954681.md), [Moscow](19004.md), [New Jersey](21648.md), New York, Nicosia, Paris, [Philadelphia](50585.md), Providence, Rhode Island, [Rome](25458.md), Sofia, State of Illinois, [Sydney](27862.md), [Tbilisi](42298.md), [Tirana](31075.md), [Toronto](64646.md), [Valletta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valletta), [Washington D.C.](108956.md), [Xi'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi'an), [Yerevan](34352.md). He has been honoured with the Honorary Freedom of 40 Greek and foreign cities. In November 2017, he became Honorary Member of the Propeller Club of the United States Port of Piraeus and he received the Award of Excellence of the Propeller Club of the United States Port of Piraeus for his strenuous effort and impact on developing a new European Policy on regular Migration. In December 2017, the Department of International and European Studies of the [University of Piraeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Piraeus) awarded Mr. Avramopoulos the first "Themistocles" prize for his contribution to the management of the EU migration and security crisis.[^cite_note-avramopoulos.gr-1]
