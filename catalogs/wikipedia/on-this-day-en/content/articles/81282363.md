# 2025 visit by Pope Leo XIV to Turkey and Lebanon

| Date | November 27 – December 2, 2025 |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Turkey:[Ankara](802.md)[Istanbul](3391396.md)[İznik](7493823.md)[Nicaea](77588.md) Lebanon:[Monastery of Saint Maron](37324663.md)[Bkerké](9434189.md)[Port of Beirut](2301960.md) |

[Pope Leo XIV](54520372.md) visited [Turkey](11125639.md) from November 27 to 30, and [Lebanon](17771.md) from November 30 to December 2, 2025. The trip was this pope's first [apostolic journey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_journey) abroad since his [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_conclave) in May 2025. The visit to Turkey included an [ecumenical](146656.md) commemoration of the 1,700th anniversary of the [First Council of Nicaea](11118.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-unitynicaea-3]&#x20;

## Background



Pope Francis and Patriarch Bartholomew at the [Church of the Holy Sepulchre](7810.md) in 2014.

### Turkey

Further information: [Holy See–Turkey relations](21691682.md)

[Pope Francis](1687680.md) had intended to return to Turkey after visiting in 2014, upon the invitation of [Bartholomew I of Constantinople](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartholomew_I_of_Constantinople) to commemorate the 1,700th anniversary of the First Council of Nicea in [İznik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iznik), but the pope [died](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_funeral_of_Pope_Francis) on 21 April 2025. His successor Pope Leo XIV stated from the beginning of his papacy that he intended to continue the planned visit.[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-5] Other papal visits to Turkey were by [Pope Paul VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Paul_VI) in 1967, [Pope John Paul II](23805.md), and [Pope Benedict XVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Benedict_XVI).[^cite_note-6]&#x20;

### Lebanon

Further information: [Holy See–Lebanon relations](21667630.md)

Francis also expressed his desire to visit Lebanon on multiple occasions during his papacy, but he was prevented from doing so by political instability in the nation.[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-7] [John Paul II](23805.md) was the first pope to make an extended formal visit to the nation in 1997, and Benedict XVI travelled to the country in 2012, the last apostolic visit of his papacy.[^cite_note-8] In June of 2025, [Joseph Aoun](53467128.md), the [President of Lebanon](2032981.md), formally invited Leo to visit the nation.[^cite_note-9] The journey was announced on the anniversary of the [October 7 attacks](74998317.md) by [Hamas](13913.md).[^cite_note-\:0-4] Pope Leo's advocacy of peace during the [Gaza war](74998519.md) became more vocal following the killing of three people during [Israel](9282173.md)'s missile strike on the [Holy Family Church, Gaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Family_Church,_Gaza).[^cite_note-10] Israel [invaded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Israeli_invasion_of_Lebanon) Lebanon in October 2024 in response to [Hezbollah](13919.md)'s presence in the latter which was a consequence of the ongoing conflict in the region.[^cite_note-11] In an October 15 meeting with [King Abdullah II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_II_of_Jordan) and [Queen Rania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Rania_of_Jordan) of [Jordan](7515964.md), the queen enquired about the safety of the trip, to which Leo simply responded "Well, we're going."[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]&#x20;

## Itinerary





[Pope Leo XIV](54520372.md) (left) and [Ecumenical Patriarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecumenical_Patriarch_of_Constantinople) [Bartholomew I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartholomew_I) (right) took part in the joint pilgrimage

### Turkey

On 27 November, Pope Leo XIV departed [Rome](25458.md) early in the morning and arrived in [Ankara](802.md), where his first stop was [Anıtkabir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An%C4%B1tkabir), the mausoleum of [Atatürk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atat%C3%BCrk).[^cite_note-14] From there he proceeded to the [Presidential Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Complex_\(Turkey\)), where he met with President [Recep Tayyip Erdoğan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recep_Tayyip_Erdo%C4%9Fan).[^cite_note-15] He spoke with Erdoğan about the [Russia–Ukraine war](70149799.md) as well as the [Israel–Gaza war](74998519.md).[^cite_note-16] He then met with Safi Arpaguş, the head of the [Presidency of Religious Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Religious_Affairs).[^cite_note-17] After the formal events in the capital, he left Ankara in the early evening and flew to [Istanbul](3391396.md), arriving at [Atatürk Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atat%C3%BCrk_Airport).[^cite_note-auto1-18]&#x20;

On 28 November, he began the day in Istanbul at the [Cathedral of the Holy Spirit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral_of_the_Holy_Spirit,_Istanbul), where he met the local clergy and pastoral workers.[^cite_note-19] He also visited the Nursing Home of the Little Sisters of the Poor.[^cite_note-20] Later in the afternoon, he travelled by helicopter to [İznik](7493823.md), where he joined Bartholomew for an [ecumenical](146656.md) prayer service held near the archaeological site of the ancient [Basilica of Saint Neophytos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_of_Saint_Neophytos), marking the 1,700th anniversary of the First Council of Nicaea.[^cite_note-unitynicaea-3][^cite_note-21] After the service, he returned to Istanbul for further meetings at the Apostolic Delegation.[^cite_note-auto1-18]&#x20;

On 29 November, he remained in Istanbul, beginning with a visit to the [Sultan Ahmed Mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Ahmed_Mosque), his first visit to a Muslim place of worship as pope.[^cite_note-22] He then went to the [Mor Ephrem Syriac Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mor_Ephrem_Syriac_Orthodox_Church) for a private meeting with leaders of the local churches and Christian communities.[^cite_note-23] In the afternoon, he was received at the [Patriarchal Church of Saint George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._George%27s_Cathedral,_Istanbul) in the [Phanar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phanar), where he took part in the [Doxology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doxology), met with Bartholomew, and signed a joint declaration to work towards a common date for the celebration of [Easter](9325.md).[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] The day concluded with Mass at the [Volkswagen Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen_Arena_\(Istanbul\)).[^cite_note-26]&#x20;

On 30 November, his final day in Turkey, he visited the [Armenian Apostolic Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Gregory_the_Illuminator_Church_of_Galata).[^cite_note-27] He returned once more to the Patriarchal Church of Saint George for the Divine Liturgy and an ecumenical blessing, celebrating the feast of [Andrew the Apostle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_the_Apostle).[^cite_note-28] He remained at the Ecumenical Patriarchate for lunch with Bartholomew and then departed from Atatürk Airport in the afternoon, continuing his journey to Lebanon.[^cite_note-29]&#x20;

### Lebanon

After concluding his visit to Turkey, Pope Leo XIV arrived in [Beirut](37428.md) on the afternoon of 30 November, where he was welcomed at the [Beirut International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beirut_International_Airport) before he traveled to [Baabda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baabda).[^cite_note-30] There, he paid courtesy visits to the President of the Republic, [Joseph Aoun](53467128.md), followed by holding meetings with the Speaker of Parliament, [Nabih Berri](592775.md), the Prime Minister, [Nawaf Salam](21077090.md), and the country's civil authorities, diplomatic corps, and representatives of Lebanese society.[^cite_note-31]&#x20;

On 1 December, the pope travelled north into the mountains to visit [Annaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kfar_Baal), where he prayed at the tomb of [Saint Charbel Makhlouf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Charbel) in the [Monastery of Saint Maroun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monastery_of_Saint_Maroun).[^cite_note-32] He then continued to [Harissa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harissa-Daraoun), stopping at the Shrine of [Our Lady of Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Lebanon) to meet bishops, priests, consecrated men and women, and pastoral workers.[^cite_note-33] Later, at the Apostolic Nunciature in Beirut, he held a private meeting with the Catholic Patriarchs of the East.[^cite_note-34] In the afternoon, he took part in an ecumenical and interreligious gathering in [Martyrs' Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martyrs%27_Square,_Beirut).[^cite_note-35] He then headed to [Bkerké](9434189.md), the seat of the [Maronite Patriarchate of Antioch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maronite_Patriarchate_of_Antioch), where he met with thousands of young people assembled before the patriarchal residence.[^cite_note-36]&#x20;

The final day of his journey, 2 December, began in [Jal el Dib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jal_el_Dib), where he visited the staff and patients of the "De la Croix" Hospital.[^cite_note-37] He then returned to Beirut for a moment of silent prayer at the [Port of Beirut](2301960.md), honoring the victims of the devastating [2020 explosion](64752725.md).[^cite_note-38] Afterwards, he celebrated [Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_\(liturgy\)) on the [Beirut Waterfront](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beirut_International_Exhibition_%26_Leisure_Center) for 150,000 faithful from across Lebanon.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40] That afternoon, after a farewell ceremony at the airport, Pope Leo departed Beirut and returned to Rome, concluding his three-day visit to the country.[^cite_note-41]&#x20;
