The **Memorial Van Damme** is an annual [athletics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_\(sport\)) event at the [King Baudouin Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Baudouin_Stadium) in [Brussels](3708.md), [Belgium](3343.md), that takes place in late August or early September. Previously one of the [IAAF Golden League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_Golden_League) events, it now serves as a final event of the [Diamond League](21778161.md), along with [Weltklasse Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weltklasse_Z%C3%BCrich) between 2010 and 2019. In 2024 and 2026, Memorial Van Damme will serve as the sole final of the Diamond League.[^cite_note-2]

It was first organized in 1977 by a group of journalists in honour of [Ivo Van Damme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivo_Van_Damme), a Belgian double medal winner at the [Montreal Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_Summer_Olympics) who was killed the previous year in a car accident at the age of 22.[^cite_note-3]
