# Judea and Samaria Area

Not to be confused with [Area C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_C), the administrative area defined in the [Oslo II Accord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo_II_Accord).

This article is about an Israeli administrative division that overlaps the [West Bank](33209.md). For the proposed state, see [State of Judea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Judea). For the geographic regions that serve as its namesake, see [Judea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judea) and [Samaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samaria). For the historical kingdoms, see [Kingdom of Judah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Judah) and [Kingdom of Samaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Israel_\(Samaria\)).

The **Judea and Samaria Area** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): אֵזוֹר יְהוּדָה וְשׁוֹמְרוֹן, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hebrew): *Ezor Yehuda VeShomron*;[^cite_note-2] [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): يهودا والسامرة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Yahūda wa-s-Sāmara*) is an administrative division used by [Israel](9282173.md) to refer to the entire [West Bank](33209.md), which has been [occupied by Israel since 1967](59076253.md), but excludes [East Jerusalem](343508.md) (see [Jerusalem Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem_Law)).[^cite_note-GalnoorBlander2018-3][^cite_note-SAI2012-4] Its area is split into 165 [Palestinian "islands"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_enclaves) that are under total or partial civil administration by the [Palestinian National Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_National_Authority) (PNA), and a contiguous territory of [Area C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_C) containing 230 [Israeli settlements](15123.md) into which [Israeli law is "pipelined"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_law_in_the_West_Bank_settlements).

While the area is widely recognized internationally as a part of [Palestine](241405.md), some Israeli authorities group it together with the [districts of Israel proper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Israel), largely for statistical purposes.[^cite_note-SAI2012-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-Otto2011-6]

## Terminology

### Historical and biblical significance

The Judea and Samaria Area covers a portion of the territory designated by the historical and [biblical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bible) names of [Judea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judea) and [Samaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samaria). Both names are tied to the ancient [Israelite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelites) kingdoms: the former corresponds to part of the [Kingdom of Judah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Judah), also known as the Southern Kingdom; and the latter corresponds to part of the [Kingdom of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Israel_\(Samaria\)), also known as the Northern Kingdom. In 1947, the terminology was noted by the [United Nations](31769.md) in the [Partition Plan for Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Partition_Plan_for_Palestine) with the statement: "the boundary of the hill country of Samaria and Judea starts on the [Jordan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_River)..."[^cite_note-7] The modern term used by the Israeli government does not map precisely with the geography of the historical/biblical areas, which in tradition extended beyond the West Bank to include [Beersheba](312755.md) and Caesarea.[^cite_note-8]

### 1967 Arab–Israeli War

The [Six-Day War](29328.md) in 1967 saw Israeli forces capture the [Jordanian-annexed West Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordanian_annexation_of_the_West_Bank), marking the beginning of the [ongoing Israeli occupation of the territory](59076253.md). Following its capture, right-wing Israelis began to refer to the territories by their [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language)-language names and argued for [their integration into Israel](60995835.md) on historical, religious, nationalist, and security grounds.[^cite_note-Caplan2011-9][^cite_note-Dowty2012-10] In December 1967, the [Israeli Military Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Military_Governorate) issued an order that stated: "the term 'Judea and Samaria region' shall be identical in meaning for all purposes to the term 'the West Bank Region'".[^cite_note-Judea_and_Samaria4-11] By early 1968, "Judea and Samaria" had been formally adopted in official usage.[^cite_note-Judea_and_Samaria3-12] However, the phrase was rarely used until 1977, when [Menachem Begin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menachem_Begin), a proponent of extending Israel's sovereignty to the region, was elected as Israel's sixth prime minister.[^cite_note-Judea_and_Samaria1-13][^cite_note-Judea_and_Samaria3-12][^cite_note-Judea_and_Samaria2-14][^cite_note-Judea_and_Samaria5-15]

The name *Judea*, when used in *Judea and Samaria*, refers to all of the area to the south of [Jerusalem](16043.md), including [Gush Etzion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gush_Etzion) and [Har Hevron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Har_Hevron_Regional_Council). The name *Samaria*, on the other hand, refers to all of the area to the north of Jerusalem. In 1980, [East Jerusalem](343508.md) (a part of the [West Bank](33209.md)) was [effectively annexed by Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_annexation_of_East_Jerusalem) and has since been under civilian administration; it is thus excluded from the administrative structure of the Judea and Samaria Area.

The names "West Bank" (הַגָּדָה הַמַּעֲרָבִית, *HaGadah HaMaʽaravit*) or, alternatively, "[the territories](2781576.md)" (השטחים, *HaShtahim*) are also current in Israeli usage. Generally, preference for one term over the other indicates the speaker's position on the [Israeli political spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Israel). Left-wingers, who view that the territory should fall under Palestinian control and evacuated of [settlements](15123.md) through [a peace agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli%E2%80%93Palestinian_peace_process), prefer "West Bank"; conversely, right-wingers, who view that the territory should come under Israeli administration permanently, advocate the usage of "Judea and Samaria" (similar to the [Derry/Londonderry name dispute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derry/Londonderry_name_dispute) in [Northern Ireland](21265.md)).

## Status

Main article: [Israeli occupation of the West Bank](59076253.md)



[Area C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_C_\(West_Bank\)), controlled by Israel [under Oslo Accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bank_Areas_in_the_Oslo_II_Accord), in blue and red

The Judea and Samaria Area is administered by the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) [Central Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Command_\(Israel\)), and [military law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_law) is applied. The incumbent chief of Central Command is [Aluf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluf) [Avi Bluth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avi_Bluth). The civil administrative body is the [Israeli Civil Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Civil_Administration).

The future status of the region is a key factor in the ongoing [Israeli–Palestinian conflict](46216.md).

[United Nations Security Council Resolution 242](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_242), adopted in November 1967, after Israel captured the region from [Jordan](7515964.md) in the [Six-Day War](29328.md), lists as its first principle "the inadmissibility of the acquisition of territory by war and the need to work for a just and lasting peace in which every State in the area can live in security" and called for the "withdrawal of Israel armed forces from territories occupied in the recent conflict" in conjunction with the "termination of all claims or states of belligerency and respect for and acknowledgment of the sovereignty, territorial integrity and political independence of every State in the area and their right to live in peace within secure and recognized boundaries free from threats or acts of force".[^cite_note-16]

The West Bank (including East Jerusalem) and Gaza Strip are considered occupied [Palestinian territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories) by the United Nations, the United States,[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] the [International Court of Justice](14918.md), the [European Union](9317.md),[^cite_note-19] and by non-governmental organizations such as [Amnesty International](18947898.md),[^cite_note-20] [Human Rights Watch](46530.md),[^cite_note-21] and [B'Tselem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B'Tselem).[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] The [Supreme Court of Israel](1666510.md) has considered the section of the West Bank which excludes East Jerusalem to be [Israeli-occupied territories](2781576.md).[^cite_note-h169920-24]

On 13 May 2012, a bill to extend Israeli law to the [Israeli settlements](15123.md) in the Judea and Samaria Area initiated by [Knesset](74656.md) member [Miri Regev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miri_Regev) (Likud) first approved by the majority of the Ministerial Committee for Legislation was rejected in a second round of votes after prime minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) had instructed his ministers to vote against the bill. Extending Israeli law to the settlements would mean a de facto annexation of the settlements to Israel.[^cite_note-25] In July 2012, a government-commissioned report from a three-member committee, called [Levy Report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levy_Report), asserted, based on a number of reasons, that there is no legal basis under international law to refer to Judea and Samaria as "occupied territory". Article 43 of the [Fourth Hague Convention of 1907](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hague_Conventions_of_1899_and_1907#Hague_Convention_of_1907) is the basis of the Levy committee's opinion.[^cite_note-26]

In April 2026, the [Tennessee General Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_General_Assembly) passed House Bill 1446 and Senate Bill 1663, requiring state agencies to use the term "Judea and Samaria" instead of "[West Bank](33209.md)" in official materials. The legislation became Chapter 877 of the Tennessee Public Acts of 2026 and will be effective after July 1 (2026).[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

## Administrative local authorities

The area is further divided into eight military administrative regions: Menashe ([Jenin](50997.md) area), HaBik'a ([Jordan Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Valley_\(Middle_East\))), [Shomron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samaria) ([Shechem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shechem) area, known in Arabic as [Nablus](233369.md)), Efrayim ([Tulkarm](851834.md) area), Binyamin ([Ramallah](44132.md)/[al-Bireh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Bireh) area), Maccabim ([Maccabim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modi'in-Maccabim-Re'ut) area), [Etzion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gush_Etzion) ([Bethlehem](4312.md) area) and [Yehuda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judea) ([Hebron](38577.md) area).
