The **closing ceremony** of the [2026 Winter Paralympics](41045653.md) took place on 15 March 2026. Entitled "Italian Souvenir", the ceremony was held in the [Stadio olimpico del ghiaccio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadio_olimpico_del_ghiaccio) (named Cortina Curling Olympic Stadium during the Games). The city of [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md) was chosen to host the ceremony, which previously did so during the [1956 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_Winter_Olympics).[^cite_note-1]

## Preparations

The ceremony was led by artistic director Angelo Bonello and was produced by G2 Eventi – Casta Diva Group.[^cite_note-2] The theme of the ceremony was "Italian Souvenir", following the dream-like journey of Sofia Tansella, a young Italian girl with [spinal muscular atrophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinal_muscular_atrophy).[^cite_note-Guardian-MacInnes-3] The ceremony was described as a postcard of memories, highlighting the sporting success and the beauty of the Italian mountainous regions of [Milan](36511.md) and [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md). The Cortina Curling Olympic Stadium was transformed for the event, with a stage built on its curling sheets.[^cite_note-4] Several nations, led by [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics), boycotted the ceremony due to the participation of [Russian](82446138.md) and [Belarusian](82538958.md) athletes at the Games.[^cite_note-Guardian-MacInnes-3][^cite_note-BBC-Hudson-5][^cite_note-6]

## Proceedings

The ceremony took place indoors at [Stadio olimpico del ghiaccio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadio_olimpico_del_ghiaccio) from 20\:30-23\:00 [CET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Time). The theme of the ceremony was "Italian Souvenir", following the dream-like journey of Sofia Tansella, a young Italian girl with [spinal muscular atrophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinal_muscular_atrophy).[^cite_note-Guardian-MacInnes-3] The ceremony was described as a postcard of memories, highlighting the sporting success and the beauty of the Italian mountainous regions of [Milan](36511.md) and [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md). Beginning with a short video, an artistic dance performance featuring [Cirque du Soleil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirque_du_Soleil)'s Dergin Tokmak culminated with dancers forming the [Paralympic agitos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paralympic_agitos). The dancers continued with a brief performance highlighting teamwork and sportsmanship, ending by forming the [Italian flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Italy) with luminous ropes. The flag of Italy was carried into the arena by retired Paralympians, including [Josh Dueck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Dueck). "[Il Canto degli Italiani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il_Canto_degli_Italiani)" was performed by Italian singer [Arisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arisa) and the flag was raised by the [Polizia di Stato](1313023.md).[^cite_note-7]

During the athletes' parade, [Tina and Milo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_and_Milo) entered the arena, accompanied by the music of Gabriele Capponi, an Italian DJ and activist. This was followed by a recognition of the newly-elected members of the IPC athletes' council and a celebration of the Games' volunteers. A performance featuring cellist Formisanoff ensued, which featured dancers who ended with an appreciation of Cortina d'Ampezzo. Afterwards, "[Hymne de l'Avenir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hymne_de_l'Avenir)" was played during the lowering of the [Paralympic flag](7895540.md); it was then officially handed over to representatives from the regions of [Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes](45093325.md) and [Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provence-Alpes-C%C3%B4te_d'Azur) in [France](5843419.md), which will host the [2030 Winter Paralympics](63766282.md) in the [French Alps](961317.md). A presentation video was also shown after the raising of the [flag of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_France) and a unique rendition of "[La Marseillaise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Marseillaise)". Official speeches from [Fondazione Milano Cortina 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fondazione_Milano_Cortina_2026) president [Giovanni Malagò](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Malag%C3%B2) and [International Paralympic Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Paralympic_Committee) president [Andrew Parsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Parsons_\(sports_administrator\)) followed, with Parsons declaring the Games closed.

Another performance saw dancers performing around a [snow globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow_globe), which depicted iconic sites in both Milan and Cortina d'Ampezzo. The performance concluded with the simultaneous extinguishing of the Paralympic cauldrons in both Milan's [Arco della Pace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arco_della_Pace) and Cortina d'Ampezzo's Piazza Angelo Dibona. The ceremony ended with a performance by Italian electronic group [Planet Funk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planet_Funk), who performed songs including "[Chase the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_the_Sun_\(song\))", and a short video featuring Sofia that highlighted the athletes of the Games.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-BBC-Hudson-5]
