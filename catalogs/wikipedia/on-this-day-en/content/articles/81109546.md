The **2025–26 [ISU Speed Skating World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Speed_Skating_World_Cup)** was a series of five [speed skating](28028.md) events that took place between November 2025 and January 2026. The World Cup is organised by the International Skating Union, [ISU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Skating_Union).

The first four were qualifying events for the [2026 Olympic Winter Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_skating_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Qualification). All were qualifying events for the [2026 World Allround Speed Skating Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_World_Allround_Speed_Skating_Championships) and the [2026 World Sprint Speed Skating Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_World_Sprint_Speed_Skating_Championships).&#x20;

World Cup titles were awarded to the top point earners in the 500m, 1000m, 1500m, Long Distances, Mass Start, and Team Pursuit for men and women, as well as in the Mixed Gender Relay Team.
