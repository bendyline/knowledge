A **raion** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): район, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *raion*; pl. райони, *raiony*), often translated as a **district**, is the second-level [administrative division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Ukraine) in [Ukraine](31750.md). The modern raion system originated from the 1923–1929 administrative reform in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), including the [Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic), and has remained part of Ukraine's administrative structure since the [country's independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Declaration_of_Independence_of_Ukraine) in 1991.<sup>[\[1\]](#cite_note-20Ukraine%25201932rainons-1)</sup>[^cite_note-2]

On 17 July 2020, the [Verkhovna Rada](844993.md) (Ukraine's parliament) approved an administrative reform that reduced the number of raions [from 490](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_raions_of_Ukraine_\(1966%E2%80%932020\)) to 136 and incorporated the former "[cities of regional significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_regional_significance_\(Ukraine\))" into the newly formed raions.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Most tasks previously performed at the raion level, including education, healthcare, sports facilities, culture, and social welfare, were transferred to [hromadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hromada), the subdivisions of raions.[^cite_note-reformi697326-5]

The 136 new districts include ten in [Crimea](163045.md), which has been under Russian control since 2014, although it remains internationally recognized as part of Ukraine.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Districts in a cities

Further information: [Urban districts of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_districts_of_Ukraine)

Two [cities of national significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_with_special_status) ([Kyiv](585629.md) and [Sevastopol](51587.md)[^cite_note-11]) are divided into *urban districts*, also called *districts in a city*. Urban districts have their own local administration and are subordinated directly to city authorities.

Changes

As a result of [decommunization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decommunization_in_Ukraine) and [derussification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derussification_in_Ukraine) efforts, many toponyms have been renamed, including some raions.

- Novomoskovsk Raion → [Samar Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samar_Raion)

- Krasnohrad Raion → [Berestyn Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berestyn_Raion)

- Severodonetsk Raion → [Siverskodonetsk Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siverskodonetsk_Raion)

- Chervonohrad Raion → [Sheptytskyi Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheptytskyi_Raion)

- Novohrad-Volynskyi Raion → [Zviahel Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zviahel_Raion)
