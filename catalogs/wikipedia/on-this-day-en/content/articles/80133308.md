**Valentina Noriega Gomez** (born May 8, 1999) is a [Colombian–American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_Americans) [far-right](221220.md) political activist and [Republican Party](32070.md) candidate. Her stances have been described as [homophobic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homophobic), [Islamophobic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamophobic), and [anti-immigration](6491626.md) by the media.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

Born in [Colombia](5222.md), Gomez moved to [New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jersey_City,_New_Jersey) at the age of ten. She attended [Central Connecticut State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Connecticut_State_University) before completing an MBA at [Freeman School of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeman_School_of_Business).

Gomez ran unsuccessfully for [Missouri Secretary of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Missouri_Secretary_of_State_election) in 2024 and the [United States House of Representatives](78639082.md) in 2026.

## Early life and education

Gomez was born on May 8, 1999, in [Medellín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medell%C3%ADn), [Colombia](5222.md).[^cite_note-The-Times-of-India-2024-4] Gomez immigrated to the [United States](3434750.md) with her family in 2009 when she was 10, where they settled in [Jersey City, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jersey_City,_New_Jersey).[^cite_note-The-Times-of-India-2024-4]

Gomez completed a bachelor's degree from [Central Connecticut State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Connecticut_State_University) in 2019 and a [Master of Business Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration) from [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University)'s [A.B. Freeman School of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeman_School_of_Business) in 2021.[^cite_note-Martin-2026-5]

## Political activism

In February 2024, while standing as a Republican candidate for [Missouri Secretary of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Missouri_secretaries_of_state), Gomez drew widespread attention after setting LGBTQ-friendly library books on fire with a flamethrower.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Gomez finished sixth in the election, receiving 7.4% of the vote.[^cite_note-8]

In December 2024, Gomez released a video showing the simulated execution of an immigrant and called for the [public execution](4733727.md) of [undocumented immigrants](7124412.md) accused of violent crimes.[^cite_note-The-Times-of-India-2024-4][^cite_note-The-League-of-United-Latin-American-Citizens-2026-9] The [League of United Latin American Citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/League_of_United_Latin_American_Citizens) (LULAC), a Latino Civil Rights organization, published a statement denouncing Gomez and said: "Gomez's video glorifies the type of vigilantism that has led to deadly consequences in our nation and feeds into the anti-immigrant lie."[^cite_note-The-League-of-United-Latin-American-Citizens-2026-9] The Texas director of LULAC, Gabriel Rosales, said: "[The El Paso Walmart mass shooter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Crusius) killed 23 people and wounded 22 more precisely because of racial hate speech. Social media must ban this kind of reckless display for attention."[^cite_note-The-League-of-United-Latin-American-Citizens-2026-9]

In April 2025, Gomez interrupted a Muslim community day held at the Texas Capitol building. She said that "Islam is the religion of rape, incest and pedophilia," and mocked the religion.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

In August 2025, Gomez was banned from several [social media](5897742.md) platforms after burning the [Quran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quran) in a video posted online.[^cite_note-12] Her brother, [Jonathan Gomez-Noriega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_G%C3%B3mez_\(swimmer\)), worked for the Jersey City LGBTQ+ Task Force until August 5, 2024, when he resigned and distanced himself from his sister's views. Jonathan had donated to Valentina's election campaign. The mayor, [Steven Fulop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Fulop), dismissed Gomez-Noriega as an aide the same day.[^cite_note-13]

In September 2025, Gomez spoke at the [Unite the Kingdom](80681466.md) rally and said that the UK was being taken over by "rapist Muslims".[^cite_note-14]

In March 2026, Gomez ran unsuccessfully in the Republican primary for Texas's [31st Congressional District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas's_31st_congressional_district), finishing second with 10.9% of the vote.[^cite_note-Martin-2026-5][^cite_note-15]

In April 2026, Gomez was barred from visiting the [United Kingdom](31717.md) after the [Home Office](225695.md) determined her presence "would not be conducive to the public good".[^cite_note-16] Prior to being blocked from entering the UK, Gomez had said to Prime Minister [Keir Starmer](18681491.md): "You're only good at protecting the Muslim rape gangs. Try to arrest me & see what happens."[^cite_note-Zimmermann-2026-17] Following the ban, Gomez suggested that she would attempt to enter the UK on a small boat.[^cite_note-18]
