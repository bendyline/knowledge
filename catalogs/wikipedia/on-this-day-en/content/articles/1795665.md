The **Patriarch of All Romania** ([Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language): *Patriarh al Întregii Românii*; Romanian pronunciation: [\[patriˈarh al ɨnˈtred͡ʒi romɨˈni\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Romanian)) is the title of the head of the [Romanian Orthodox Church](384458.md). The Patriarch is officially styled as *Archbishop of [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Bucharest), Metropolitan of [Muntenia and Dobruja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolis_of_Muntenia_and_Dobruja), [Locum tenens of the throne of Caesarea Cappadociae](https://ro.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loc%C8%9Biitor%20al%20Tronului%20Cezareei%20Capadociei) and Patriarch of the Romanian Orthodox Church*. The current patriarch [Daniel](12545130.md) acceded to this position on 12 September 2007.[^cite_note-1]
