**Farwaniya Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة الفروانية *Muḥāfaẓat al-Farwānīyah*) is the most populous of the six [governorates of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorates_of_Kuwait), and is the country's only [landlocked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landlocked) governorate. It is [Kuwait](57021.md)'s main residential area and also forms an important part of Kuwait's commercial activities.
