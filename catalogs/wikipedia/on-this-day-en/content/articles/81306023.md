Further information: [2025 WTA Finals](78728546.md)

[Elena Rybakina](55541443.md) defeated [Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md) in the final, 6–3, 7–6<sup>(7–0)</sup> to win the singles tennis title at the [2025 WTA Finals](78728546.md).[^cite_note-1] It was her 11th [WTA Tour](1398173.md)-level title, and she became the first Kazakhstani player and first player representing an Asian country to win the [WTA Finals](1356185.md).[^cite_note-2] By winning the title undefeated, Rybakina won \$5.235 million USD in prize money, breaking the [previous year](75672114.md)'s record for the largest prize money earned by a female tennis player at a single event.[^cite_note-3]

[Coco Gauff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_Gauff) was the defending champion,[^cite_note-4] but was eliminated in the round-robin stage.[^cite_note-5]

Sabalenka was the first player to qualify for at least three consecutive editions as the top seed since [Steffi Graf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steffi_Graf) from 1993 to 1996, and the fourth woman to do so overall, after [Chris Evert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evert), [Martina Navratilova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martina_Navratilova) and Graf.[^cite_note-6] She was the first player to reach the final of three [majors](197638.md#Tournaments) and the WTA Finals in the same season since [Angelique Kerber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelique_Kerber) in 2016.[^cite_note-7]

[Amanda Anisimova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Anisimova)[^cite_note-8] and [Ekaterina Alexandrova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaterina_Alexandrova) (as an alternate)[^cite_note-9] made their debuts in the singles competition. For the first time since [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_WTA_Tour_Championships_%E2%80%93_Singles), four American women qualified for the singles competition.[^cite_note-10] [Madison Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Keys) withdrew from the tournament before her final group match due to illness and was replaced by second alternate Alexandrova.[^cite_note-11] First alternate [Mirra Andreeva](71915009.md) declined to serve as the replacement.[^cite_note-12] As in the [previous year](75672114.md), [Jasmine Paolini](46663470.md) was the only player to qualify for both the singles and doubles tournaments.[^cite_note-13]
