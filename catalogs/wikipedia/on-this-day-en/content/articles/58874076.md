**Stratocracy in Myanmar** (also known as [Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma)) lasted from 1962 to 2011 and resumed in 2021. Myanmar gained its [independence](34274976.md) from the [British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) in 1948 under the [Burma Independence Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma_Independence_Army), as a [democratic nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Burma_\(1948%E2%80%931962\)). The first military rule began in 1958 and direct military rule started when [Ne Win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ne_Win) captured power through a [coup d'état in 1962](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_Burmese_coup_d'%C3%A9tat). Burma became a [military dictatorship](146728.md) ([Socialist Republic of the Union of Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Burma_\(1962%E2%80%931988\))) under the [Burma Socialist Programme Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma_Socialist_Programme_Party) that lasted for [26 years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_Way_to_Socialism), under the claim to save the country from disintegration. During this period there was some democratic landscape in the form of giving rights to the citizen to elect and to stand for election.[^cite_note-1]

For most of its independent years, the [sovereign state](23604120.md) has been engrossed in rampant ethnic strife and [its myriad ethnic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ethnic_groups_in_Myanmar) have been involved in one of the world's [longest-running ongoing civil wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_conflict_in_Myanmar). It is also known as one of the most brutal nations of all time for its mass human rights violation. During this time, the [United Nations](31769.md) and several other organizations have reported consistent and systematic [human rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rights_in_Burma) violations in the country.[^cite_note-UNHR-2]

## Introduction

Myanmar, the largest country in [Mainland Southeast Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainland_Southeast_Asia), is located at the meeting point of South and Southeast Asia. It is bordered by five nations: [China](5405.md), [Laos](17752.md), [Thailand](30128.md), [India](14533.md) and [Bangladesh](3454.md). Myanmar gained its [independence](34274976.md) on 4 January 1948 from the [United Kingdom](31717.md) under the leadership of [General](148537.md) [Aung San](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aung_San) of the National Army.[^cite_note-bio-3]

In July 1947, [Aung San](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aung_San) was assassinated by rivals under the leadership of [U Saw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U_Saw), but achieved independence in January 1948 under [U Nu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U_Nu). Since its struggle for independence, [the armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar_Armed_Forces) known as the ‘[Tatmadaw](20394.md)’ played an important role in gaining independence and it was under the [Burmese Independence Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_Independence_Army) that Myanmar got its independence. The Army in Myanmar had gained respect in independent Myanmar at the initial stage and perceived as protector of the country. The military claims itself as the founder of the [Union of Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Burma), and the main force that held the country together during the civil war and also claimed that it has prevented the country from disintegrating.[^cite_note-bio-3]

After independence was achieved, a [constitutional government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_government) was formed and [U Nu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U_Nu) was nominated as the [first Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_prime_ministers_of_Myanmar) of Independent Myanmar. However, the newly formed civilian government under U Nu failed to maintain the unity of the country, facing domestic problems, ethnic issues, insurgency, corruption, mismanagement, and the ethnic insurgencies which took up arms against each other. In 1958, a split within the [AFPFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Fascist_People's_Freedom_League) threatened to provoke a coup by field officers. In order to settle the situation, U Nu invited the military to form a caretaker government.[^cite_note-bio-3]

In 1958–60, the caretaker government under General [Ne Win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ne_Win) was formed. The caretaker government initially appeared to be interested in building a competent state. It reduced corruption, improved bureaucratic efficiency, and managed to deal with the pocket armies. The military junta announced to hold an [election in 1960](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960_Burmese_general_election).[^cite_note-bio-3]

After the election in 1960, [U Nu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U_Nu)'s party formed a [civilian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civilian_government). But the civilian government under U Nu could not solve and improve the situation, and rather threatened the national integration of the country, leading to the [coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_Burmese_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) on March 2, 1962, under General [Ne Win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ne_Win). The main reasons for the coup were a mix of political infighting, policy gridlock, multiple insurgencies on a massive scale, and a declining economy. After the coup, the military government arrested members of the government, suspended the constitution, and appointed a [Union Revolutionary Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Revolutionary_Council) (RC) to govern Myanmar by decree.[^cite_note-bio-3]

## 1962 to 2011

The coup on 2 March 1962 led to the end of democratic form of government and the beginning of direct military rule in Myanmar up to 1974. During the period of military rule under General [Ne Win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ne_Win), the country was shaped into one-party socialist state under the army led party called as [Burma Socialist Programme Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma_Socialist_Programme_Party) ([BSPP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma_Socialist_Programme_Party)) until 1988. And the year from 1962 to 1988, can be marked as the era of Ne Win. The period from 1962 to 1988 can be divided into two phases. The first phase is the period of direct military rule from 1962 to 1974 and [Constitutional Dictatorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_dictatorship) phase from 1974 to 1988.[^cite_note-bio-3]

In 2011, the [military junta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Peace_and_Development_Council) was officially dissolved, following a [2010 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Myanmar_general_election), and a nominally civilian government was installed.

## Since 2021

In February 2021, the [Tatmadaw](20394.md) [detained](66567997.md) State Counsellor [Aung San Suu Kyi](2847.md), President [Win Myint](37228839.md), and other government leaders. They then proceeded to take control of the government, and instituted a one-year state of emergency, with Burma's [Commander-in-chief of the armed forces](56636725.md), [Min Aung Hlaing](38278640.md), as the leader of the country, serving as the [Chairman of the State Administration Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_State_Administration_Council), who has taken on a new title as [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Myanmar) of a newly formed [caretaker government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caretaker_Government_of_Myanmar_\(2021\)).
