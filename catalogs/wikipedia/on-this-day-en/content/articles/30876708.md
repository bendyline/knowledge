This article lists the [presidents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)) of [São Tomé and Príncipe](20026306.md), an [island country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_country) in the [Gulf of Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Guinea) off the western equatorial coast of [Central Africa](539606.md), since the [establishment of the office of president in 1975](20026306.md#Independence_\(1975\)). [Manuel Pinto da Costa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Pinto_da_Costa) was the first person to hold the office, taking effect on 12 July 1975. The incumbent is [Carlos Vila Nova](68450389.md), having taken office on 2 October 2021.

## Term limits

As of 2021, there is a two-term limit for the president in the [Constitution of São Tomé and Príncipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9_and_Pr%C3%ADncipe). The first president for whom the term limits applied was [Trovoada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_Trovoada) in 2001.[^cite_note-termlimits-2]
