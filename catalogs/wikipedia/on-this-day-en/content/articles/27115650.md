**Monica Louise "Nikki" Prieto-Teodoro** (born March 3, 1966) is a Filipino politician who currently serves as the Philippines' special envoy to the [UNICEF](19698110.md) since July 2022; she previously held the title from September 2017 to September 2018.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-envoy2017-2] She is the wife of [Gilbert Teodoro](21940341.md), the incumbent Secretary of National Defense. She was the [Representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_\(Philippines\)) of the 1st district of [Tarlac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarlac) during the [14th Congress of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_Congress_of_the_Philippines) from 2007 to 2010. Teodoro was the chairperson and vice-chairperson, respectively of the House Committees on the Welfare of Children and on Population and Family Relations.[^cite_note-nikki-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] She authored several bills for the protection of children, including the Anti-Corporal Punishment Act and the Anti-Child Pornography Act.[^cite_note-envoy2017-2] She also helped build Amor Complex, a children's shelter in Tarlac, and the Golden Rooster Foundation that raises funds for underprivileged children.[^cite_note-6]

## Education

Teodoro graduated from [Webster University Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webster_University_Geneva) in [Switzerland](26748.md) with a degree in [International Marketing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Marketing), where she was trained in the basics of the international economy. She then returned home to become a successful real estate entrepreneur. She also studied in various schools such as [Marymount](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_Marymount_University), [St. Joseph's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Joseph's_College_\(Mountain_View,_California\)), and [Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notre_Dame_de_Namur_University) in [California](5407.md).[^cite_note-nikki-3]

## Personal life

She is married to incumbent Defense Secretary [Gilbert Teodoro](21940341.md), with whom she has a son. Before politics and her real estate venture, Teodoro used to be a commercial model. She was a former [equestrienne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equestrianism) and is a [PADI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PADI)-certified scuba diver.[^cite_note-nikki-3]

In 2026, China imposed sanctions and an entry ban on Teodoro, her husband, and their son, citing statements her husband made against the country.[^cite_note-7]
