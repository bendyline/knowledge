**Luiz Edson Fachin** (born 8 February 1958) is a Brazilian jurist and lawyer. On June 16, 2015, he became a justice of the [Supreme Federal Court](3359076.md), having been nominated by President [Dilma Rousseff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilma_Rousseff). Since 2025 he has served as president of the same court, having been elected by his peers for a two-year term.[^cite_note-1] Before his confirmation to the high court, he had served as professor of civil law of the [Federal University of Paraná](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Paran%C3%A1) (UFPR).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

## Early life and education

Fachin was born in the town of [Rondinha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rondinha), [Rio Grande do Sul](256268.md). One of his parents was a farmer, while the other worked as a teacher. His family moved to [Toledo, Paraná](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo,_Paran%C3%A1), when Fachin was two years old. He attended the [Federal University of Paraná](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Paran%C3%A1) (UFPR). He later received a master's degree and a doctorate from the [Pontifical Catholic University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontifical_Catholic_University_of_S%C3%A3o_Paulo).[^cite_note-\:1-3]

## Academic career

Fachin worked as a professor of law at the Federal University of Paraná.[^cite_note-\:0-2] He completed postgraduate studies in [Canada](5042916.md), and was also a visiting researcher at the [Max Planck Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute) in [Germany](11867.md).[^cite_note-4] During a sabbatical, he stayed in [London](17867.md), where he worked as a professor at [King's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London). He additionally taught at [Pablo de Olavide University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_de_Olavide_University) in [Spain](26667.md).[^cite_note-\:1-3]

## Political views

Fachin is considered a progressive on judicial issues including land reform.[^cite_note-5]

## Personal life

Fachin is fluent in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), [Spanish](26825.md), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language) and [Italian](14708.md).[^cite_note-6] He is married to Rosana Amara Girardi Fachin, a member of the [Paraná](229411.md) court of justice.[^cite_note-7] He is known to be a supporter of the [Coritiba Foot Ball Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coritiba_Foot_Ball_Club).[^cite_note-8]
