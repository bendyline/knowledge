# 2025 Nobel Prizes

The **2025 [Nobel Prizes](21201.md)** were awarded by the [Nobel Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Foundation), based in Sweden, and the Norwegian Nobel Institute, based in Oslo, to 14 individuals.&#x20;

Six categories were awarded: [Physics](52497.md), [Chemistry](25416893.md), [Physiology or Medicine](52502.md), [Literature](23385442.md), [Peace](26230922.md), and [Economic Sciences](23535306.md).&#x20;
