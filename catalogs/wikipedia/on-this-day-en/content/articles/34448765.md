**Duk County** (also known as **Duk**) is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_South_Sudan) in [Jonglei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonglei), [South Sudan](32350676.md).[^cite_note-1] In May 2016, it was divided into three counties which were Duk Padiet, Duk Payuel, and Panyang.[^cite_note-2] In 2017, the governor of Jonglei State added Duk Pagaak County, totaling four counties in former Duk County. However, in 2020, President [Salva Kiir Mayardit](2363203.md) reinstated the original number of states and counties, thus reunifying original Duk County.[^cite_note-3] In Duk County is the village of Duk Payuel, the birthplace of [John Dau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dau) who is one of the "[Lost Boys of Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_Boys_of_Sudan)."

Youth from the Hol community in Duk County were attacked by people from [Twic East County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twic_East_County) in a dispute over control of an island used for fishing.[^cite_note-4]
