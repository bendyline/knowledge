The **men's** competition in [skeleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeleton_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics) at the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md) was held on 12 February (runs 1 and 2) and 13 February (runs 3 and 4), at the [Cortina Sliding Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cortina_Sliding_Centre) in [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md).[^cite_note-schedule-1] [Matt Weston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Weston_\(skeleton_racer\)) of Great Britain won the event, setting a new track record in every run. This was his first Olympic medal. [Axel Jungk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axel_Jungk) of Germany won the silver medal, replicating his 2022 performance. His teammate [Christopher Grotheer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Grotheer), the defending champion, won bronze.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Weston became the third British athlete to win an Olympic title in skeleton after [Lizzy Yarnold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizzy_Yarnold) and [Amy Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Williams) and the first male British skeleton athlete to do so.[^cite_note-4] Weston also became the first male British athlete in any winter sport to win an Olympic gold medal in an individual Olympic competition since figure skater [Robin Cousins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Cousins) did so in 1980.[^cite_note-5]

## Background

The defending champion, [Christopher Grotheer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Grotheer), qualified for the event, as did the silver medalist, [Axel Jungk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axel_Jungk). The bronze medalist, [Yan Wengang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yan_Wengang), did not qualify.[^cite_note-6] [Matt Weston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Weston_\(skeleton_racer\)) won the [2025–26 Skeleton World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Skeleton_World_Cup), having won all six events that season. He was also the [2025 World champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBSF_World_Championships_2025_%E2%80%93_Men).

[Ukrainian](31750.md) racer [Vladyslav Heraskevych](56328919.md) was disqualified before his first run for wearing a helmet depicting Ukrainian athletes killed during [Russia's invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md). The [International Olympic Committee](15147.md) prohibits any personal messages at the Olympics.[^cite_note-Ukrainian_Heraskevych_disqualified_from_Olympic_skeleton_competition_for_helmet_depicting_war_dead-7][^cite_note-8]
