# Energy in Bahrain



Bahrain electricity production by source

Oil tanks in [Sitra](4006219.md), Bahrain

**Energy in Bahrain** describes [energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_energy_resources_and_consumption) and [electricity](9540.md) production, consumption and import in [Bahrain](18933277.md). Bahrain is a net energy exporter.

Primary energy use was in Bahrain in 2009 110 TWh and 139 TWh per million persons and in 2008 107 TWh and 139 TWh/million people.[^cite_note-IEA2010-1]

## Oil

Bahrain was the first place on the Arabian side of the [Persian Gulf](24761.md) where oil was discovered. The [First Oil Well, Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Oil_Well,_Bahrain) situated below [Jabal al Dukhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_of_Smoke) has operated since 1932. It was operated by [Bahrain Petroleum Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_Petroleum_Company).

In 2023, the petroleum production of Bahrain was rated at about 189,000 barrels per day.[^cite_note-3] The petroleum reserves were quoted as 168 million barrels in 2023.[^cite_note-oilgas2023-4]

## Gas

As of 2023, Bahrain was said to produce 16.7 billion cubic meters (bcm) of natural gas per year and have reserves of 68 bcm.[^cite_note-oilgas2023-4]

## Business

[Bahrain Petroleum Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_Petroleum_Company) (BAPCO), wholly owned by the [government of Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Bahrain), is a fully integrated [oil company](994887.md).

[Banagas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banagas) is a large natural gas company.
