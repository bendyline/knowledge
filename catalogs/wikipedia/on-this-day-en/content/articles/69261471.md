# Beijing Zion Church

**Beijing Zion Church** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 北京锡安教会) is a [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism) church founded in Beijing in 2007 by the pastor [Jin Mingri](69261398.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] The church is considered very influential in China alongside [Shouwang Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shouwang_Church) of Beijing (pastor [Jin Tianming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jin_Tianming_\(pastor\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), not related to Jin Mingri).[^cite_note-3] Zion Church grew to over a 1,000 members in a few years. However, the church was closed by the authorities in 2018 with its pastor under house arrest.[^cite_note-4]

Before it was shut down, it "operated with relative freedom for years, hosting hundreds of worshippers every weekend in an expansive, specially renovated hall in north Beijing".[^cite_note-5] In 2025, Jin Mingri and 29 other church leaders were arrested in nationwide raids.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] The church's attorneys have faced intimidation from the authorities and some have had their licenses revoked.[^cite_note-8] In September 2026, a lawyer who defended the church and its members fled to [Taiwan](25734.md), citing pressure from Chinese authorities.[^cite_note-9]
