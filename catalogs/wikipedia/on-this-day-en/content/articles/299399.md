**Kogi State** is a [state](226734.md) in the [North Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Central_Nigeria) region of [Nigeria](21383.md), bordered to the west by the states of [Ekiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekiti_State) and [Kwara](2227645.md), to the north by the [Federal Capital Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Capital_Territory,_Nigeria), to the northeast by [Nasarawa State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasarawa_State), to the northwest by [Niger State](1723431.md), to the southwest by the states of [Edo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_State) and [Ondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ondo_State), to the southeast by the states of [Anambra](905838.md) and [Enugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu_State), and to the east by [Benue State](1556364.md). It is the only state in Nigeria to border ten other states. Named for the [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language) word for river, the state was formed from parts of [Benue State](1556364.md), [Niger State](1723431.md), and [Kwara State](2227645.md) on 27 August 1991.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-Creation-6] The state is nicknamed the "Confluence State" as the confluence of the [River Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Niger) and the [River Benue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Benue) occurs next to its capital, [Lokoja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokoja).

Of the 36 [states of Nigeria](226734.md), Kogi is the [thirteenth largest in area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_area) and [twentieth most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population) with an estimated population of about 4.5 million as of 2016.[^cite_note-7] Geographically, the state is within the [tropical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_and_subtropical_grasslands,_savannas,_and_shrublands) [Guinean forest–savanna mosaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinean_forest%E2%80%93savanna_mosaic) [ecoregion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ecoregions_in_Nigeria). Important geographic features include the key rivers with the [Niger](21437.md) flowing from the northwest and the [Benue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue_River) coming from the northeast before the two rivers meet in Kogi's center and bisect the state southward.

Kogi State has been inhabited for years by various ethnic groups, including the [Ebira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebira_people), [Gbagyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gbagyi_people), [Nupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_people) (mainly the [Bassa Nge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bassa_Nge_people), [Kakanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakanda_language), and [Kupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kupa_language) subgroups), and [Oko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogori-Magongo_language) in the state's centre; the [Igala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igala_people) and [Bassa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bassa_languages?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the east; and the [Yoruba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoruba_people) (mainly the [Okun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okun_people), [Ogori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oko_language), [Oworo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oworo_people), and Magongo subgroups) in the west. Kogi is also religiously diverse, with about equal percentages of [Christians](18337522.md) and [Muslims](19541.md) along with a substantial minority of adherents of [traditional ethnic religions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_African_religions).

In the pre-colonial period, the area that is now Kogi State was split up between various states with some states being tiny and village-based as others were part of larger empires like the [Nupe Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_Kingdom) which held much of now-western Kogi State until the early 1800s when the [Fulani jihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulani_jihad) annexed the kingdom and placed the area under the [Sokoto Caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_Caliphate) while the eastern half of the modern state was the ancient [Igala Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igala_Kingdom). In the 1900s and 1910s, British expeditions occupied the area and incorporated them into the [Northern Nigeria Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nigeria_Protectorate) with its capital as [Lokoja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokoja) until 1903. The protectorate later merged into [British Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Nigeria) before becoming independent as [Nigeria](21383.md) in 1960. Originally, modern-day Kogi State was a part of the post-independence [Northern Region](2292852.md) until 1967 when the region was split and the area became part of the [North-Western State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-Western_State), [Kwara State](2227645.md), and [Benue-Plateau State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benue-Plateau_State). After Benue-Plateau and the North-Western states were split in 1976, now-Kogi became a part of the new [Benue](1556364.md) and [Niger](1723431.md) states along with Kwara. Western Benue State, southeastern Kwara State, and far southern Niger State were broken off to form the new Kogi State.

Economically, Kogi State is largely based around [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture), mainly of [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffea_robusta), [cashew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cashew), [groundnut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut), [cocoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_bean), [oil palm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_palm), and [yam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yam_\(vegetable\)) crops. Other key industries are transport service and Logistics[^cite_note-8] [crude oil](23195.md) extraction and the [livestock herding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herding) of [cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle), [goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goats), and [sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheep).[^cite_note-9] Kogi has the [ninth highest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_Human_Development_Index) [Human Development Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Development_Index) and [sixteenth highest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_GDP) [GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP) in the country.[^cite_note-10]

## Geography

### Climate

The [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) of the state has an annual rainfall total of between 1,100 mm (43 in) and 1,300 mm (51 in). The rainy season lasts from April to October each year while the dry season last sfrom November to March. The dry season is very dusty and cold as a result of the north-easterly winds, which bring in the [harmattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmattan).[^cite_note-11] Between 2001 and 2014, the built-up area increased by 10.68% and seven (7) adaptation strategies were employed by farmers in changing planting dates and change crop variety at 31%, 22%, and 21% respectively, in response to change in rainfall.[^cite_note-12]

### Flooding

In October 2022, Kogi State witnessed one of the worst [flood disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flood_disaster) in the history of the state. This is according to the state governor, [Yahaya Bello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahaya_Bello), who said that "flooding has affected the nine LGAs which borders the Niger and Benue rivers to include, Lokoja, Kogi-Koto, Ajaokuta, Ofu, Igalamela-Odolu, Bassa, Idah, Ibaji and Omala".[^cite_note-13]

### 2012 flooding

In 2012, floods ravaged the state of Kogi and others in Nigeria. A total of 623,690 displaced people were accommodated in 87 camps across the state during the period. Roads were [unmotorable](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/unmotorable#:~\:text=Adjective,be_traversed_by_motor_vehicle.) due to the flood. Many school buildings became refugee camps. Houses were submerged. Property and infrastructure was badly affected. Crops in farm fields were seriously damaged.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] The flood caused deaths, loss of property and destruction of farmland and produce estimated at N63.4 billion in nine local government areas of Kogi state.[^cite_note-16]

## History and people

The state is famous for its productivity in business and agriculture, cultural diversity, hospitable citizens and beautiful landmarks.

There are three main ethnic groups and languages in Kogi: [Igala people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igala_people), [Anebira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebira_people), and [Okun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okun_people), with others such as [Bassa Nge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bassa_Nge) of [Bassa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bassa,_Kogi_State) L.G.A, [Kupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kupa_language) and [Kakanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakanda_language) speakers, who are a people of [Nupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_people) extraction under [Lokoja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokoja) L.G.A., [Bassa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basa_languages) which is also of [Bassa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bassa,_Kogi_State), Lokoja and Koto [Local government areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_areas), [Oworo people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oworo_people), [Ogori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogori_people?action=edit\&redlink=1) [Magongo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magongo,_Nigeria), and [Idoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idoma_people).

The name *[Nigeria](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Nigeria)*, was coined in Lokoja by [Flora Shaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flora_Shaw) in the hill of [Mount Patti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Patti), the future wife of [Baron Lugard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Lugard), a [British colonial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) administrator, while gazing out at the [river Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Niger) .[^cite_note-18]

She, [Flora Shaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flora_Shaw) is the daughter of an English father, Captain George Shaw, and a French mother, Marie Adrienne Josephine, a local of [Mauritius](19201.md) (née Desfontaines; 1826–1871).

## Languages

Kogi is a multi-ethnic state with over multiple indigenous languages spoken in the state. Common languages include [Ebira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebira_language), [Igala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igala_language), [Yoruba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoruba_language), [Okun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oko_language), [Nupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_language), [Kakanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakanda_language), [Kupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kupa_language), [Basa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basa-Benue_language) (Komu, Nge), [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language).[^cite_note-19]

The Okun language is spoken in the [Kogi West Senatorial District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kogi_West_Senatorial_District)

## Religion

Kogi is religiously diverse with about 45% of the state's population are [Muslim](19541.md) with about 40% being [Christian](18337522.md) and the remaining 15% following [traditional ethnic religions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_African_religions) minorities.

[Sheikh Aminu Sha'aban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Aminu_Sha'aban?action=edit\&redlink=1) was turbaned as the [Imam of Lokoja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imam_of_Lokoja?action=edit\&redlink=1) in December 2019.[^cite_note-20]

[The Ecclesiastical Province of Lokoja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Province_of_Lokoja) with the Anglican [Church of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Nigeria) led by [Archbishop Daniel Abubakar Yisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Abubakar_Yisa) covers Kogi and [Niger States](1723431.md) and includes the six Dioceses of [Ijumu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Ijumu) (2008) led by Bishop [Paul Olarewaju Ojo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Olarewaju_Ojo) (2018), [Kabba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Kabba) (1996) led by Bishop Steven Kadoye Akobe (2010), [Lokoja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Lokoja) (1994) led by Bishop [Emmanuel Egbunu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Egbunu) (2004), [Ogori-Magongo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Ogori-Magongo) led by Bishop [Festus Davies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festus_Davies) (2009), [Okene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Okene) (2008) led by Bishop [Emmanuel Onsachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Onsachi) (2017) and [Idah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Idah) led by [Bishop Joseph Musa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Musa) (2005).

The Catholic Church includes the [Diocese of Idah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Idah) (1968) with 56 parishes under Bishop [Anthony Ademu Adaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Ademu_Adaji) (2009) and the [Diocese of Lokoja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Lokoja) (1955 as Kabba) with 43 parishes under Bishop [Martin Dada Abejide Olorunmolu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Dada_Abejide_Olorunmolu) (2005), both [suffragans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffragan) of the [Archdiocese of Abuja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Abuja).

## Tourism



Niger-Benue Confluence at Lokoja

Tourist attractions in Kogi State include; The Azad's palace of the Ohinoyi of Ebira land, the colonial relics (such as [Lord Lugard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Lugard) House), [Mount Patti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Patti), [World War Cenotaph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_Cenotaph), the confluence of Rivers Niger and Benue, [Ogidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogidi,_Kogi) (An African town with formations of [Igneous Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igneous_Rock) mountains and a traditional art & craft industry) and natural land features hills and terrains that serve as hiking trails.

Some tourists visit on day trips from Abuja, which is a two-hour drive away. Kogi State Tourism and Hotels Company Limited was established to promote tourism in the state. The state government plans and harnesses the high potentials of tourism including the development of historical landmarks at Lokoja.[^cite_note-21]

## Transport and communications

Kogi State connects the Federal Capital Territory with 22 Southern States.

Federal Highways are:&#x20;

Being in close proximity to the federal capital territory, [Abuja International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nnamdi_Azikiwe_International_Airport) serves as the national and international gateway for air travelers from and to the state. Good telecommunications services are available in the state.

## Agriculture and resources

Agriculture is the mainstay of the economy. There are many [farm products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop) from the state, including [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee), [cocoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_bean), [palm oil](57561.md), [cashews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cashew), [groundnuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambara_groundnut), [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [cassava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassava), [yam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yam_\(vegetable\)), [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice) and [melon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melon).

Mineral resources include [coal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal), [limestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone), [iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron), [petroleum](23195.md) and [tin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tin). The state is home to the [Ajaokuta Steel Company Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajaokuta_Steel_Company_Limited), the largest iron and steel industry in Nigeria and the [Obajana Cement Factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obajana_Cement_Factory), one of the largest cement factories in Africa.

## Sports

Kogi State has produced [sprinters](89276.md) and other sportsmen, who have contributed to the growth of sports worldwide. [Kogi United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kogi_United) and [Babanawa F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babanawa_F.C.) are [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_\(soccer\)) teams based in the state. Other sports, such as [swimming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_\(sport\)), [handball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_handball), and [table tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Table_tennis), are actively promoted in the state.

Sportsmen from the state include [Shola Ameobi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shola_Ameobi) who played football as a [striker](428681.md) for [Newcastle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United) and for other [English Premier League](11250.md) teams. [Sunday Bada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunday_Bada) was a [400 metre sprinter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/400_metres) who won three medals at the [World Indoor Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAAF_World_Indoor_Championships_in_Athletics), including a gold medal in 1997, and won a gold medal with the Nigerian team in the [4 × 400 metres relay at the 2000 Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_2000_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_4_%C3%97_400_metres_relay).

## Politics

The State government is led by a democratically elected governor who works closely with members of the state's house of assembly. The capital city of the state is [Lokoja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokoja).[^cite_note-Next_President-28]

### Electoral system

The electoral system of each state is selected using a modified two-round system. To be elected in the first round, a candidate must receive the plurality of the vote and over 25% of the vote in at least two -thirds of the State's and local government Areas. If no candidate passes the threshold, a second round will be held between the top candidate and the next candidate to have received a plurality of votes in the highest number of local government Areas.[^cite_note-Next_President-28]

## Senate

Three senators have represented Kogi state since the return of democracy in 1999. The state is divided into three senatorial districts, with Kogi East, Kogi West and Kogi Central returning one senator each.[^cite_note-29]
