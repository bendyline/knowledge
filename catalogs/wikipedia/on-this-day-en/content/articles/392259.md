This article lists the [presidents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)) of [Guinea-Bissau](12186.md), since the establishment of the office of president in 1973.

Since [Guinea-Bissau](12186.md)'s [unilateral declaration of independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinea-Bissau_War_of_Independence#Unilateral_independence:_September_1973) from [Portugal](23033.md) on 24 September 1973, there have been six presidents, six acting presidents and five interim military leaders. The current transitional president is [General](148537.md) [Horta Inta-A Na Man](81702722.md), who was appointed on 27 November 2025 following a [coup d'état](81695397.md).[^cite_note-France24-2]

## Term limits

As of 2021, there is a two-term limit for the president in the [Constitution of Guinea-Bissau](52349787.md). The term limit has not been met by any president yet.[^cite_note-termlimits-4]
