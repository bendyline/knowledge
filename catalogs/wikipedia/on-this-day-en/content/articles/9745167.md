The **general secretary of the Workers' Party of Korea** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 조선로동당 총비서) is the [leader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_leader) of the [Workers' Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers'_Party_of_Korea) (WPK), the ruling party in North Korea, and the [country's supreme leader](25084215.md). [Party rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charter_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) stipulate that the party congress elects the general secretary. The party conference and the Central Committee are empowered to remove and elect the party leader. The general secretary is *ex officio* chairman of the [WPK Central Military Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Military_Commission_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) and leads the work of the [secretariat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretariat_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea).[^cite_note-4] Additionally, the general secretary is by right of office a member of the [WPK Presidium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidium_of_the_Politburo_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea), the [WPK Politburo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politburo_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) and the WPK Secretariat.

The office traces its lineage back to the reestablishment of the [Communist Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Korea) (CPK) on 14 September 1945 when [Pak Hon-yong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pak_Hon-yong) was elected Chairman of the CPK Central Committee.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESuh1981281-5] Later on 13 October 1945 the CPK established an internal [North Korean Branch Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korean_Branch_Bureau) (NKBB) and nominated [Hyon Chun-hyok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyon_Chun-hyok) as branch secretary.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESuh1981279–80-6] Hyon Chun-hyok was assassinated on 3 September 1945 and [Kim Yong-bom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Yong-bom) was elected as branch secretary in his place.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELee19635Suh1981280-7] On 10 April 1946 the NKBB became independent of the CPK and changed its name to Communist Party of North Korea (CPNK).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESuh1981280-8] Later that year, on 30 August, the CPNK merged with the [New People's Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_People's_Party_of_Korea) to establish the [Workers' Party of North Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers'_Party_of_North_Korea) (WPNK).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESuh1981282–3-9] [Kim Tu-bong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Tu-bong) was elected WPNK Chairman by the [1st WPNK Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_North_Korea).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESuh1981283-10] In the meantime the [Workers' Party of South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers'_Party_of_South_Korea) (WPSK) was established through the merger of the Communist Party of South Korea (led by Pak Hon-yong), [New People's Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_People's_Party_of_Korea) and a faction of the [People's Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Party_of_Korea) on 24 November 1946.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESuh1981282-11] The [WPSK Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) elected [Ho Hon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ho_Hon) as its party chairman.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESuh1981318-12] On the merger of the WPNK and the WPSK on 24 June 1949, the [2nd Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) elected [Kim Il Sung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Il_Sung) as Chairman of the WPK Central Committee.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESuh1981321-13]

The offices of Chairman and Vice Chairman of the WPK Central Committee were abolished on 12 October 1966 and replaced with the offices of General Secretary and Secretary of the [WPK Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) by a decision of the 14th Plenary Session of the [4th Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4th_Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESuh1981328-14] Kim Il Sung was elected and remained in office until [his death on 8 July 1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_state_funeral_of_Kim_Il_Sung).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESuh1981328Gause201118-15] The post was abolished and replaced by the office of General Secretary of the Workers' Party of Korea on 8 October 1997 after having been left vacant for 3 years and 92 days.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGause201118-16] [Kim Jong Il](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Jong_Il) was elected to the office by a joint announcement of the [6th Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6th_Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) and the [6th Central Military Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6th_Central_Military_Commission_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGause201118-16] Kim Jong Il remained in office until [his death on 17 December 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_state_funeral_of_Kim_Jong_Il). The office of General Secretary of the Workers' Party of Korea was left vacant for 116 days and was later abolished. Instead, [Kim Jong Un](5962511.md) was elected First Secretary of the Workers' Party of Korea on 11 April 2012, and his father, Kim Jong Il, was given the appellation "Eternal General Secretary of the Workers' Party of Korea".[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGause201340–41-17] On 9 May 2016, the [7th WPK Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7th_Congress_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) abolished the office of First Secretary and the Secretariat and replaced it with Chairman of the Workers' Party of Korea and the office of [Vice Chairman of the WPK Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_Chairman_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea).[^cite_note-first_secretary-18] A similar change took place at the [8th WPK Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_Congress_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea), where the offices of chairman and vice chairman were abolished and replaced by the office of General Secretary of the Workers' Party of Korea and the Secretariat.[^cite_note-chairman-19]
