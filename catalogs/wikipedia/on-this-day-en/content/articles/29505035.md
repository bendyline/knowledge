The **Jebal Aulia Dam** is a dam on the [White Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Nile) near [Khartoum](17320.md), [Sudan](27421.md). Its construction began in 1933 and was completed in 1937. When completed it was the largest dam in the world.[^cite_note-SBC-1]

The dam was built by Gibson and Pauling (Foreign) Ltd, which was a partnership between the British civil engineering company [Pauling & Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pauling_&_Co.) and the [civil engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_engineer) [John Watson Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Watson_Gibson).[^cite_note-2]

In 2003 a [hydroelectricity](381399.md) project with a 30-megawatt (40,000 hp) maximum capacity was completed on the dam. This increased the structure's strategic value, so it is now continually guarded by the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md).

In November 2023 the Jebel Aulia Bridge, part of the Jebel Aulia Dam complex, was destroyed amid heavy fighting in [Jabal Awliya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabal_Awliya). A [Sudanese official](5698926.md) said the dam itself remained intact.[^cite_note-3]

In December 2024, the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) accused the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) of damaging the dam in airstrikes, causing flooding in [White Nile State](767622.md). The SAF accused the RSF of causing the reservoir to flood horizontally by closing the dam.[^cite_note-4]

In August 2026, [Egypt](8087628.md) and [Sudan](27421.md) have reached an agreement to rehabilitate the dam after it was damaged during shelling by [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md).[^cite_note-5]
