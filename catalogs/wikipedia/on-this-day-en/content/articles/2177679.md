**Nizhnekamsk** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Нижнекамск, IPA: [\[nʲɪʐnʲɪˈkamsk\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Russian); [Tatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatar_language): Түбән Кама, romanized: *Tübän Qama*) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) in [Tatarstan](67135.md), Russia, located to the south of the [Kama River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kama_River) between the cities of [Naberezhnye Chelny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naberezhnye_Chelny) and [Chistopol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chistopol). ## Etymology

The name in both Russian and Tatar comes from the fact that the city is located in the lower reaches of the Kama River.

As its population increased, spurred by the construction of [Nizhnekamskneftekhim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizhnekamskneftekhim) petrochemical industrial complex, Nizhnekamsk was granted city status in 1966.

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), Nizhnekamsk serves as the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of [Nizhnekamsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizhnekamsky_District), even though it is not a part of it.[^cite_note-RTatarstan_adm-1] As an administrative division, it is, together with three rural localities, incorporated separately as the **[city of republic significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance) of Nizhnekamsk**—an administrative unit with the status equal to that of the [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_the_Republic_of_Tatarstan).[^cite_note-RTatarstan_adm-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), the city of republic significance of Nizhnekamsk is incorporated within Nizhnekamsky Municipal District as **Nizhnekamsk Urban Settlement**.[^cite_note-RTatarstanNizhnekamskyD_mun-4]

## Economy

The city is an important center of the [petrochemical industry](2293201.md) with the [Nizhnekamskneftekhim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizhnekamskneftekhim) plant. The [Tatarstan Oil Refinery Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatarstan_Oil_Refinery_Complex?action=edit\&redlink=1) is part of the [Tatneft concern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatneft). The Nizhnekamsk Tire Factory, known for tires called Kama, is also located in the city.

## Transportation

Nizhnekamsk and a number of neighboring cities are served by [Begishevo airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Begishevo_Airport), there is a railway station Nizhnekamsk [Kuibyshev railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuybyshev_Railway), from where [suburban electric trains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suburban_train) run to [Izhevsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Izhevsk) via [Naberezhnye Chelny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naberezhnye_Chelny) and [Agryz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agryz), where it is possible to transfer to [long-distance trains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-distance_trains). The railway section from Nizhnekamsk to Naberezhnye Chelny is single-track, but electrified with 25 kV [alternating current](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternating_current). The [M7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M7_highway_\(Russia\)) Volga Moscow-Ufa federal highway runs near Nizhnekamsk and the [M12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M12_highway_\(Russia\)) [commercial highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toll_road) with a toll road is under construction. The city also has a marina for motor ships with an equipped river station, from where passenger ships ply in the waters of the Kama and Volga rivers.

### Tram system of Nizhnekamsk

Nizhnekamsk itself has one of Tatarstan's three tram systems, the third largest and the second most prosperous. Despite its decrease during the post-Soviet period, it continues to work normally. The municipal unitary enterprise "Urban Electric Transport" owns the system. The total length of the routes is 63.5 km, and there are currently 4 routes in total.Main types of rolling stock is [71-608](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/71-608), [71-619](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/71-619), [71-623](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/71-623). The system has been operating since February 23, 1967. The system, like most other Soviet-Russian tram systems, operates on direct current of 550 volts.

## Police and other enforcement agencies

The district police department in Nizhnekamsk is part of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Tatarstan and is responsible for the entire Nizhnekamsk district. The district office of the Federal Bailiff Service is also located in Nizhnekamsk. The nearest pre-trial detention center is located in Naberezhnye Chelny and Chistopol, while a correctional colony for prisoners with tuberculosis is located in Nizhnekamsk. There are also several fire stations of the Russian Emergencies Ministry in Nizhnekamsk. There are no significant army units near Nizhnekamsk.
