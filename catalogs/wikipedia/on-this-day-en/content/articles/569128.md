**Langebaanweg** is a town on the southwest [coast](5236.md) of [South Africa](17416221.md), in [Western Cape Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Cape_Province).

It is the location of the [air force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_force) base [AFB Langebaanweg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFB_Langebaanweg).&#x20;

Langebaanweg has been an important [mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining) center, with its main minerals consisting of [phosphorites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorites), primarily [calcium phosphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcium_phosphate). The mining uncovered [fossils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fossils) dating from the late Miocene and early Pliocene, approximately 5 million years ago. Fossil species found there include [invertebrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invertebrates) and also a type of [rhinoceros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhinoceros), as well as a [mousebird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mousebird) and a [diving petrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diving_petrel). The [West Coast Fossil Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_coast_fossil_park) includes displays of fossils found in a former phosphate mine.[^cite_note-WCFP-2] Brett Hendey, formally of the [Iziko South African Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iziko_South_African_Museum), documented the significance of the Langebaanweg fossil sites.[^cite_note-Hendey1982-3]

Langebaanweg is also notable as the birthplace of former England cricketer [Allan Lamb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allan_Lamb), who played 79 Tests for his adopted country as well as in the [1987](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_Cricket_World_Cup_Final) and [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Cricket_World_Cup_Final) Cricket World Cup Finals.
