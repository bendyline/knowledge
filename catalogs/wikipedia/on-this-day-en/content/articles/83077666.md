**Giordano Vaccaro** (born August 26, 2003) is a Canadian professional [football](6963.md) [offensive lineman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offensive_lineman) for the [Ottawa Redblacks](16627032.md) of the [Canadian Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Football_League) (CFL). He was the [first overall pick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_first_overall_Canadian_Football_League_draft_picks) in the [2026 CFL draft](78454606.md).

## University career

Vaccaro first played [U Sports football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U_Sports_football) for the [Manitoba Bisons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manitoba_Bisons_football) from 2022 to 2024.[^cite_note-Boilermakers-1][^cite_note-Bisons-2] In his rookie season in 2022, he started all eight regular season games and one playoff game at [left tackle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_tackle).[^cite_note-Boilermakers-1] In the 2023 season, he again started nine games, this time at [left guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_guard), and was named a [U Sports First Team All-Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U_Sports_Football_All-Canadian_Team).[^cite_note-Boilermakers-1] Vaccaro played and started in nine games at left guard in 2024 where he was again named a U Sports First Team All-Canadian and was also awarded the [J. P. Metras Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._P._Metras_Trophy) at season's end.[^cite_note-Boilermakers-1]

Vaccaro transferred to [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) for the [2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Purdue_Boilermakers_football_team) to play for the [Boilermakers](6160171.md).[^cite_note-Boilermakers-1][^cite_note-first-3] He played in 12 games as a backup offensive lineman and special teams player.[^cite_note-Boilermakers-1]
