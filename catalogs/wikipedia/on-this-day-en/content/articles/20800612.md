**NTN24** (acronym for **Nuestra Tele Noticias 24**) is a Colombian [cable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cable_television) television [news channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_channel), owned and operated by [RCN Televisión](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCN_Televisi%C3%B3n).

NTN24 was launched on 3 November 2008 with journalist Claudia Gurisatti appointed as the channel's first editorial director. Its main headquarters are located in [Washington D. C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_D._C.) and [Bogotá](211271.md), [Colombia](5222.md).
