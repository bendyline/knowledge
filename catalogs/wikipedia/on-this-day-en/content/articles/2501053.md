**Wavel Ramkalawan** (born 15 March 1961) is a Seychellois politician and [Anglican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglicanism) priest who served as the 5th [President of Seychelles](450100.md) from 2020 to 2025. Ramkalawan was an opposition MP from 1993 to 2011 and 2016 to 2020. He also served as the [Leader of the Opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(Seychelles\)) from 1998 to 2011 and 2016 to 2020. On 25 October 2020, Ramkalawan won the [presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Seychellois_general_election), the country's first such victory for an opposition candidate since independence, marking its first successful [peaceful transition of power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peaceful_transition_of_power). On [11 October 2025](76588149.md), he lost his reelection bid for a second term to [Patrick Herminie](20114965.md), a member of the [United Seychelles Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Seychelles).[^cite_note-1]

## Early life

Wavel Ramkalawan was born in 1961 in [Mahé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mah%C3%A9,_Seychelles), the principal island of Seychelles. He was born into a modest family, the youngest of three children. His great-grandfather was from [Bihar](442482.md), [India](14533.md).[^cite_note-2] His father was a [tinsmith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metalworker) while his mother was a [teacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teacher). Ramkalawan's primary and secondary education were at Seychelles College, the elite boys' school of the country.[^cite_note-upclose-3] Ramkalawan was [ordained](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordained) as a priest in 1985 following theological studies at [St Paul's Theological College, Mauritius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Paul's_Theological_College,_Mauritius), and thereafter followed further studies in theology at [Birmingham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham_University).[^cite_note-upclose-3] Returning to Seychelles, he worked in several parishes in Seychelles, rising to become priest-in-charge of the parish of The Holy Saviour.[^cite_note-4]

In 1984, Ramkalawan met his future wife, [Linda Ramkalawan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Ramkalawan), while preparing for his ordination as an [Anglican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican) [deacon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deacon).[^cite_note-snation-5] She was a member of the [choir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choir) at St Luke's Church in [Bel Ombre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel_Ombre,_Seychelles) at the time.[^cite_note-snation-5] The couple married in June 1985 and had three sons: Samuel, Caleb, and Amos.[^cite_note-snation-5]

## Entry into politics



Ramkalawan with [Desmond Tutu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desmond_Tutu) and [French Chang-Him](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Chang-Him)

In 1990, Ramkalawan came to prominence when he preached a sermon, broadcast on the [national radio station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seychelles_Broadcasting_Corporation), in which he questioned the practices of the [one-party government](102459.md)[^cite_note-6] and advocated for greater freedom, respect for human rights, and observance of the rule of law in the country.[^cite_note-upclose-3] Although his broadcast was cut, Ramkalawan distributed copies of sermons criticizing the government. At the time, the [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church_in_Seychelles) and [Anglican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_diocese_of_Seychelles) churches were the only institutions that could speak on social issues, mainly in sermons during weekly services.[^cite_note-\:0-7] In 1991, while still a priest, he joined fellow dissidents [Roger Mancienne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Mancienne) and [Jean-François Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Fran%C3%A7ois_Ferrari) to form the underground Parti Seselwa. Ramkalawan became its first leader.[^cite_note-upclose-3]

## Opposition leader

When the government, facing both domestic and international pressure, returned the country to [multi-party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party) [democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy) in 1992, Parti Seselwa was the first political party to register and join the ranks of others in opposition to the government. The party contested the [1992 constitutional commission elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Seychellois_constitutional_commission_election); they polled only 4% of the national vote, failing to qualify for representation on the commission.[^cite_note-\:0-7] After the promulgation of the [new constitution in 1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Seychelles), two other opposition parties joined Parti Seselwa to form The United Opposition (UO) and contest the [1993 general elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Seychellois_general_election). The combined party won 9% of the vote, enabling it to appoint one member (Ramkalawan) to the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Seychelles\)).[^cite_note-third_republic-8]

In [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Seychellois_general_election), Ramkalawan led his party into the second multi-party general elections.[^cite_note-sbcwin-9] The party polled 27% of the national vote[^cite_note-fact1998-10] and increased its National Assembly representation to three, beating the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seychelles_Democratic_Party) of former President [James Mancham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Mancham) into third place. Ramkalawan became the first directly elected member of the party in the Assembly, winning his home constituency of [St Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Louis,_Seychelles), which he has represented continuously since. In addition, he succeeded Mancham as [Leader of the Opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(Seychelles\)), a post he held from 1998 to 2011 and 2016-2020.[^cite_note-sna-11]

In the [2001 presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seychellois_presidential_election,_2001), Ramkalawan polled 45% of the vote, losing to the 54% vote won by President [René](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France-Albert_Ren%C3%A9).[^cite_note-fute-12] The [next year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Seychellois_parliamentary_election), Ramkalawan led his party, now renamed the [Seychelles National Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seychelles_National_Party) (SNP), into the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Seychelles) elections. The party increased its parliamentary representation from one directly elected member to seven and from two proportionally elected members to four.[^cite_note-third_republic-8]

In 2005, Ramkalawan took a sabbatical from his clerical duties in order to devote himself fully to his political life; he considered this time to be a crucial and important point in the country's affairs.[^cite_note-13] However, Ramkalawan lost to [James Michel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Michel) in the [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Seychellois_presidential_election) and [2011 presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Seychellois_presidential_election). Along with other major opposition parties, Ramakalawan and his party boycotted the [2011 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Seychellois_parliamentary_election).[^cite_note-14]

In the [2015 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Seychellois_presidential_election), Ramkalawan and Michel advanced to a runoff election, which was the first runoff presidential election in Seychelles. Ramkalawan lost with 49.85% of the vote and a 193 votes difference to his opponent Michel.[^cite_note-elections-15]

## Presidency (2020–2025)

In the [2020 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Seychellois_presidential_election), Ramkalawan defeated incumbent president [Danny Faure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Faure). According to the election commission, he secured 54.9% of the votes cast.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] The election marked the first [peaceful transfer of power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peaceful_transition_of_power) to an opposition since [independence in 1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Seychelles#Independence).[^cite_note-18] Ramkalawan was inaugurated as [President of Seychelles](450100.md) on 26 October 2020.

On 4 November 2020, the additional portfolios of Defence, Legal Affairs, Public Administration, National Planning and National Security were assigned to Ramkalawan.[^cite_note-additional_portfolio-19]

Ramkalawan stepped down as president on 26 October 2025. He was succeeded by [Patrick Herminie](20114965.md).
