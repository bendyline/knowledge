The **districts of Pakistan** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): اِضلاعِ پاكِستان) are the third-level [administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_division) of [Pakistan](23235.md), below [provinces](446017.md) and [divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisions_of_Pakistan), but forming the first-tier of [local government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_in_Pakistan). In total, there are 173 districts in Pakistan.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] excluding [Islamabad Capital Territory](15294.md). These districts are further divided into *[tehsils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tehsils_in_Pakistan) and [union councils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_councils_of_Pakistan)*.

## History

In 1947, when Pakistan gained independence there were 124 districts. In 1969, 2 new districts (Tangail and Patuakhali) in [East Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Pakistan) were formed, bringing the total to 126. With the [Independence of Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_of_Bangladesh), Pakistan lost 20 of its districts and so there were 106 districts. In 2001, the number was reduced to 102 by the merger of the 5 districts of Karachi Central, Karachi East, Karachi South, Karachi West and Malir to form [Karachi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachi_District). The number of districts rose to 106 again in December 2004, when four new districts were created in the province of Sindh of which one (Umerkot) had existed until 2000 and three districts (Kashmore, Qambar and Jamshoro) were newly created.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The new districts were carved out of Mirpur Khas, Jacobabad, Larkana and Dadu Districts respectively. In May 2005, the [Punjab provincial government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Punjab_\(Pakistan\)) created a new district by raising the status of [Nankana Sahib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nankana_Sahib) from a [tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehsil) of [Sheikhupura District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikhupura_District) to a district in its own right.[^cite_note-nankana-9][^cite_note-10] On 11 July 2011, the Sindh Government restored again the districts of Karachi South, Karachi East, Malir, Karachi West and Karachi Central, then later in 2013, the district of Korangi was carved out of Karachi East District. In [Azad Jammu and Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azad_Jammu_and_Kashmir), a second-tier of government was formed from three administrative divisions into ten districts. In [Gilgit–Baltistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgit%E2%80%93Baltistan), there are ten districts divided between the two regions of [Gilgit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgit) and [Baltistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltistan). In 2018, [Federally Administered Tribal Areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federally_Administered_Tribal_Areas) (FATA) was merged with [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) province and FATA agencies were converted into districts.

[Chagai](746183.md) is the largest district of Pakistan by area while [Lahore District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_District) is the largest by population with a total population of 11,126,285 at the 2017 census. [Quetta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetta_District) is the largest district of Balochistan by population with a total population of 2,275,699 at the 2017 census. [Bahawalpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahawalpur_District) is the largest district of Punjab by area. [Chitral](988583.md) is the largest by area and [Peshawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshawar_District) is the largest by population from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa with a population of 4,269,079 in the 2017 census. Sindh's largest district by area is [Tharparkar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tharparkar_District)[^cite_note-\:0-11] and by population its Karachi West with a population of 3,914,757 in the 2017 census. The combined population of the six districts of Karachi division is over 16 million in the 2017 census, giving an average population for these six districts of Karachi division of over 2.675 million each. [Neelum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neelum_District) and [Kotli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kotli_District) are the largest districts of Azad Kashmir by area and population respectively. [Gilgit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgit_District) is the largest by area and population both for Gilgit-Baltistan.

As of the 2023 census, all of Pakistan's districts have a [muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim) majority except for [Umerkot district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umerkot_district).

## Administration

### Deputy commissioner

Main article: [Deputy commissioner (Pakistan)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_commissioner_\(Pakistan\))

A deputy commissioner (popularly abbreviated to "DC") is the executive head of the district. Deputy commissioners are appointed by the government from the [Pakistan Administrative Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Administrative_Service).

### District council

A district council (or *zila council*) is a local government body at the district level.

The functions of a district council include construction and maintenance of roads, and bridges, building hospitals and dispensaries, schools and educational institutions, health facilities and sanitation, tube wells for drinking water, rest houses, and coordination of activities of the Union *councils* within the district.
