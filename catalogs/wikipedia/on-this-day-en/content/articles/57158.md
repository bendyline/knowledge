**Drenthe** (Dutch pronunciation: [\[ˈdrɛntə\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Dutch) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/3/31/026_Drenthe.ogg/026_Drenthe.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/3/31/026_Drenthe.ogg/026_Drenthe.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_the_Netherlands) of the [Netherlands](21148.md) located in the northeastern part of the country. It is bordered by [Overijssel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overijssel) to the south, [Friesland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friesland) to the west, [Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groningen_\(province\)) to the north, and the German state of [Lower Saxony](18435.md) to the east. As of January 2023, Drenthe had a population of about 502,000, and a total area of 2,680 km<sup>2</sup> (1,030 sq mi).[^cite_note-AC-1]

Drenthe has been populated for 15,000 years. The region has subsequently been part of the [Episcopal principality of Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Episcopal_principality_of_Utrecht), [Habsburg Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habsburg_Netherlands), [Dutch Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Republic), [Batavian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batavian_Republic), [Kingdom of Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Holland) and [Kingdom of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_the_Netherlands). Drenthe has been an official province since 1796. The capital and seat of the provincial government is [Assen](73526.md). The [King's Commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_Commissioner) of Drenthe is [Jetta Klijnsma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jetta_Klijnsma). The [Farmer-Citizen Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farmer%E2%80%93Citizen_Movement) (BBB) is the largest party in the [States-Provincial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_Council_of_Drenthe), followed by the [Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Party_\(Netherlands\)) (PvdA) and the [People's Party for Freedom and Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Party_for_Freedom_and_Democracy) (VVD).

Drenthe has the lowest population density in the European Netherlands. It is a predominantly rural area, unlike many other parts of the Netherlands; except for the small cities of [Assen](73526.md) (pop. 67,963[^cite_note-4]) and [Emmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmen,_Netherlands) (pop. 58,130), the land in Drenthe is mainly used for [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) and it has a notable amount of [forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest) and nature.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## History



[Papeloze Kerk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papeloze_Kerk), a [dolmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolmen) (*hunebed*) near [Schoonoord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schoonoord,_Coevorden)



Map of Drenthe, 1866

The name Drenthe is said to stem from Germanic *thrija hantja* meaning "three lands".[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

Drenthe has been populated by people since prehistory. Artifacts from the [Wolstonian Stage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolstonian_Stage) (150,000 years ago) are among the oldest found in the Netherlands. In fact, it was one of the most densely populated areas of the Netherlands until the Bronze Age. The most tangible evidence of this are the [dolmens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolmen) (*[hunebedden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunebed)*) built around 3500 BC. 53 of the 54 dolmens in the Netherlands can be found in Drenthe, concentrated in the northeast of the province. In 2006, the archaeological reserve of [Strubben–Kniphorstbos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strubben%E2%80%93Kniphorstbos), located between [Anloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anloo) and [Schipborg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schipborg), was created to preserve part of this heritage.

Most of the Bronze Age objects found in the Netherlands have been found in Drenthe. One item shows that trading networks during this period extended a far distance. Large bronze *situlae* (buckets) found in Drenthe were manufactured somewhere in eastern France or in [Switzerland](26748.md). They were used for mixing wine with water (a Roman/Greek custom). The many finds in Drenthe of rare and valuable objects, such as tin-bead necklaces, suggest that Drenthe was a trading centre in the Netherlands in the Bronze Age.[^cite_note-9]

Drenthe was first mentioned in a document from 820, it was called *Pago Treanth* (Drenthe district). In archives from *Het Utrechts Archief*, from 1024 to 1025, the "county Drenthe" is mentioned, when Emperor [Henry II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_II,_Holy_Roman_Emperor) gave it to Bishop [Adalbold II of Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adalbold_II_of_Utrecht). Subjugation of this region into the [Holy Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Roman_Empire) culminated in the [Drenther Crusade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drenther_Crusade) launched by the [Papacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papacy) around 1228-1232.[^cite_note-crusade-10]

After long being subject to the [Utrecht diocese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Utrecht_\(695%E2%80%931580\)), Bishop [Henry of Wittelsbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_of_the_Palatinate_\(bishop\)) in 1528 ceded Drenthe to Emperor [Charles V of Habsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_V,_Holy_Roman_Emperor), who incorporated it into the [Habsburg Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habsburg_Netherlands). When the [Republic of the Seven United Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Republic) was declared in 1581, Drenthe became part of it as the [County of Drenthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_of_Drenthe), although it never gained full provincial status due to its poverty; the province was so poor it was exempt from paying federal taxes and as a consequence was denied representation in the States General. The successor [Batavian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batavian_Republic) granted it provincial status on 1 January 1796.

Shortly before the outbreak of the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War), the Dutch government built a camp near the town of [Hooghalen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hooghalen) to intern German Jewish refugees. During the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War), the German occupiers used the camp (which they named [KZ Westerbork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerbork_transit_camp)) as a *[Durchgangslager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durchgangslager?action=edit\&redlink=1)* (transit camp). Many Dutch Jews, [Sinti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinti), [Roma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romani_people), resistance combatants and political adversaries were imprisoned before being transferred to [concentration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_concentration_camp) and [extermination camps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extermination_camp) in Germany and occupied Poland. [Anne Frank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Frank) was deported on the last train leaving the Westerbork transit camp on 3 September 1944.

In the 1970s, there were four [hostage crises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hostage_crisis) where [South Moluccan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_South_Maluku) terrorists demanded an independent [Republic of South Maluku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_South_Maluku). They held hostages in hijacked trains in [1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975_Dutch_train_hijacking)[^cite_note-11] and [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_Dutch_train_hijacking),[^cite_note-hostages1977-12] in a [primary school in 1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_Dutch_school_hostage_crisis),[^cite_note-hostages1977-12] and in the [province hall in 1978](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_Dutch_province_hall_hostage_crisis).[^cite_note-13]

## Geography



Satellite image of Drenthe

Drenthe is situated at [52°55′N 6°35′E  /  52.917°N 6.583°E  / 52.917; 6.583](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Drenthe\&params=52_55_N_6_35_E_) in the northeast of the Netherlands; with to the north the province [Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groningen_\(province\)), to the west the province [Friesland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friesland), to the south the province [Overijssel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overijssel), and to the east the German districts [Emsland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emsland) and [Bentheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_of_Bentheim_\(district\)) in the state [Lower Saxony](18435.md).

Drenthe is the [9th largest province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_the_Netherlands) of the Netherlands. It has a total area of 2,683 km<sup>2</sup> (1,036 sq mi), with 2,639 km<sup>2</sup> (1,019 sq mi) of land and 44 km<sup>2</sup> (17 sq mi) of water. About 72% of the land or 1,898 km<sup>2</sup> (733 sq mi) is used for agriculture.[^cite_note-area-14]

Drenthe has several [heathlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath) and no significant rivers or lakes. The national parks [Drents-Friese Wold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drents-Friese_Wold_National_Park) and [Dwingelderveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwingelderveld_National_Park) ([IUCN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Union_for_Conservation_of_Nature) category II) and the national landscape [Drentsche Aa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drentsche_Aa_National_Landscape) (IUCN category V) are all (partially) located in the province.

The major urban centers of the province are the capital [Assen](73526.md) in the north and [Emmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmen,_Netherlands), [Meppel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meppel), [Hoogeveen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoogeveen), and [Coevorden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coevorden) in the south.

## Subdivisions

The province ([Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomenclature_of_Territorial_Units_for_Statistics) or NUTS level 2) is divided into three [COROP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COROP) regions (NUTS level 3): North Drenthe, Southeast Drenthe, and Southwest Drenthe. The COROP regions are used for statistical purposes.[^cite_note-18]

The Netherlands underwent a large number of municipal [mergers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merger_\(politics\)) in the past decades and Drenthe was no exception. The most significant simultaneous set of mergers took place in 1998, when 32 municipalities were amalgamated into 10 larger municipalities.[^cite_note-19] As of 2014 Drenthe consists of 12 [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_municipalities_of_the_Netherlands); [Emmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmen,_Netherlands) is the largest municipality in terms of both population and area, [Westerveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerveld) is the least populous and [Meppel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meppel) covers the smallest area.[^cite_note-CBS_pop-20][^cite_note-CBS_key_figs-21]

The municipalities [Assen](73526.md), [Noordenveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noordenveld), and [Tynaarlo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tynaarlo) are part of the interprovincial Groningen-Assen Region[^cite_note-22] and the municipalities [Aa en Hunze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aa_en_Hunze), Assen, [Borger-Odoorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borger-Odoorn), [Coevorden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coevorden), Emmen, [Midden-Drenthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midden-Drenthe), Noordenveld, and Westerveld are part of the international [Ems Dollart Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ems_Dollart_Region) (EDR).[^cite_note-23]

On 1 January 2023, Drenthe had a total population of 502,051 and a population density of 191/km<sup>2</sup> (490/sq mi).[^cite_note-area-14][^cite_note-population-27] It is the [3rd least populous and least densely populated province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_the_Netherlands) of the Netherlands, with only [Flevoland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flevoland) and [Zeeland](52229.md) having fewer people. [Emmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmen,_Netherlands) is the most populous municipality in the province.

## Religion

In 2015, 23.8% of the population belonged to the [Protestant Church in the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant_Church_in_the_Netherlands) while 9.3% was [Roman Catholic](606848.md), 0.9% was [Muslim](6037917.md) and 3.6% belonged to other churches or faiths. Over half (62.4%) of the population identified as non-religious.&#x20;

Agriculture is an important employer, although industrial areas are found near the cities. The quietness of the province is also attracting a growing number of tourists.

Drenthe is known as the "Cycling Province" of the Netherlands and is an exceptional place for a cycling holiday, having hundreds of kilometers of cycle paths through forest, heath and along canals and many towns and villages offering refreshment along the way. Drenthe exports through the entire [Netherlands](21148.md) and also receives supplies and goods from Germany, making it a good business district. Many Dutch and German [multinational](214491.md) companies are settled in Drenthe.

The gross domestic product (GDP) of the province was 15.1 billion € in 2018, accounting for 1.9% of the Netherlands economic output. GDP per capita adjusted for purchasing power was 26,700 € or 89% of the EU27 average in the same year.[^cite_note-29]

## Culture

### Language

Over half the population of Drenthe speaks the [Drents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drents) dialect. Each town or village has its own version. All versions are part of the [Low Saxon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Low_Saxon) language group. Dutch Low Saxon has been officially recognised by the Dutch government as a regional language and is protected by the [European Charter for Regional or Minority Languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Charter_for_Regional_or_Minority_Languages).

### Sports

[FC Emmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Emmen) is the only professional football club in Drenthe. It plays in the [Keuken Kampioen Divisie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keuken_Kampioen_Divisie) and its home stadium is [De Oude Meerdijk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Oude_Meerdijk).

The [Ronde van Drenthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronde_van_Drenthe) is an elite men's and women's professional road bicycle race that takes place annually in early March.

The [TT Circuit Assen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TT_Circuit_Assen) hosts the [Dutch TT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_TT), which is a round of the [MotoGP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MotoGP) series of the Motorcycle Road Racing World Championship.

### Museums

[Drents Museum](26137690.md) is an [art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_museum) and [history museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_museum) in Assen. It had 227,000 visitors in 2013.[^cite_note-30]

[Museum Collectie Brands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_Collectie_Brands) is a [local museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_museum) located in [Nieuw-Dordrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nieuw-Dordrecht) that houses an extensive collection of rare and curious items collected by Jans Brands.

## Politics



Seat of the provincial government in Assen

The [States of Drenthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Drenthe) have 43 seats, and is headed by the [King's Commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_Commissioner), currently [Agnes Mulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnes_Mulder). While the provincial council is elected by the people of Drenthe, the Commissioner is appointed by the King and the cabinet of the Netherlands. The daily affairs of the province are taken care of by the *[Gedeputeerde Staten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gedeputeerde_Staten)*, which are also headed by the Commissioner; its members (*gedeputeerden*) can be compared with ministers.

## Science and education



[Westerbork Synthesis Radio Telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerbork_Synthesis_Radio_Telescope)

[ASTRON](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASTRON), the Netherlands institute for [radio astronomy](82961.md), is located near [Dwingeloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwingeloo). Their single-dish [radio telescope](46656.md) of the [Dwingeloo Radio Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwingeloo_Radio_Observatory) was completed in 1956 and is now a national heritage site (*[rijksmonument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rijksmonument)*). Their [Westerbork Synthesis Radio Telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerbork_Synthesis_Radio_Telescope) is an array of fourteen dishes near the village of [Westerbork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westerbork_\(village\)) and construction was completed in 1970. Their international [Low-Frequency Array](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-Frequency_Array_\(LOFAR\)) with its core near [Exloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exloo) was completed in 2012.

In Assen, Emmen, and Meppel are universities of applied sciences (*[hogescholen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocational_university#Netherlands)*). The [Stenden University of Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stenden_University_of_Applied_Sciences) has locations in these three towns, which formed the [Drenthe University of Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drenthe_University_of_Applied_Sciences) before a merger in 2008. The [Hanze Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanze_Institute_of_Technology), part of the [Hanze University of Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanze_University_of_Applied_Sciences), is located in Assen. There are no research universities (*universiteiten*) in the province of Drenthe.

## Media

[RTV Drenthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTV_Drenthe), the regional radio and television station, is based in Assen. The regional daily newspaper for the provinces of Drenthe and Groningen is *[Dagblad van het Noorden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dagblad_van_het_Noorden)*, which is based in the city of [Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groningen).
