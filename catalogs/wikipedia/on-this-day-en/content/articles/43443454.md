**Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara** **Thera** ([Sinhala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhala_language): ගලගොඩ අත්තේ ඥාණසාර හිමි) is the Secretary General of [Bodu Bala Sena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodu_Bala_Sena), a [far-right](221220.md) [Sinhalese Buddhist nationalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhalese_Buddhist_nationalism) organisation.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-Diplomat-2] He was born in Galagodaaththa in the southern district of [Galle](426566.md). Gnanasara Thera has become a notable figure due to his brash behavior, run-ins with the law, and [ultranationalistic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultranationalism) rhetoric.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Early life

Gnanasara Thera came from a family of modest means, and grew up alongside several siblings.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Gnanasara Thera says that he began living a [monastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monasticism) life in [Sri Lanka](26750.md)'s tropical forests, living in cave-like dwellings, and imposing a harsh disciplinary routine on himself.[^cite_note-\:0-3] However, a November 2025 piece by *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* reported that "anyone familiar with the man" disputed such an origin story, and that it was contended that he was actually a "small-time thug who had donned the robes to avoid prison time".[^cite_note-\:0-3]

Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera was educated at Wathuravila Aranya Senasanaya in Galle, before moving to Sarananda Piriwena in [Anuradhapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anuradhapura) and [Vidyodaya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vidyodaya_University) in [Colombo](56636.md). He studied Buddhism (BA, MBA) at [Kelaniya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kelaniya). He worked as a principal at Kalapaluwaw Piriwena.

## Career

During the mid-2000s, Gnanasara Thera joined the [Sinhalese Buddhist nationalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhalese_Buddhist_nationalism) [Jathika Hela Urumaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jathika_Hela_Urumaya), the world’s first political party formed entirely of Buddhist monks.[^cite_note-\:0-3] He stood for a [parliamentary](571660.md) election, which he lost.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Gnanasara Thera subsequently formed a close relationship with the [Rajapaksa family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajapaksa_family), a prominent [political dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_family) in the country.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

Gnanasara Thera co-founded the Sinhalese Buddhist nationalist [Bodu Bala Sena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodu_Bala_Sena) (BBS) in 2012, which quickly rose to prominence due to his speeches at their rallies.[^cite_note-\:0-3] At one speech, Gnanasara Thera said that "this country still has a Sinhala police, a Sinhala army. After today if a single \[Muslim] or some other \[minority] touches a Sinhalese … it will be their end."[^cite_note-\:0-3]

In June 2014, Gnanasara Thera led a rally in the town of [Aluthgama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluthgama), following an argument between a local [Buddhist monk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhikkhu) and a group of [Muslim](19541.md) youths.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Addressing a crowd of about 7,000 people, he declared that "enough is enough" and urged attendees to "fight" Sri Lanka's minorities.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Hours later, [anti-Muslim riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_anti-Muslim_riots_in_Sri_Lanka) broke out in the town, with rioters beating local Muslims, looting Muslim-owned shops, and burning the homes of local Muslims.[^cite_note-\:0-3] When contacted by CNN that night, Gnanasara Thera said he was unavailable for comment, but another official from the BBS said Gnanasara Thera "spoke in strong words. He blessed the people after chanting verses. He preached to them to conduct themselves peacefully".[^cite_note-\:0-3]

In June 2017, the Police Organised Crimes Prevention Division arrested Gnanasara Thera on allegations of obstructing a police officer in [Welikada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welikada), following a multi-week manhunt. He was arrested after appearing at a police station to give a statement, and later released on bail.[^cite_note-Diplomat-2][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

In February 2018, Gnanasara Thera appeared in the town of [Digana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digana), following a murder perpetrated by a group of Muslim men against a Sinhalese Buddhist truck driver.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Hours later, [riots broke out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_anti-Muslim_riots_in_Sri_Lanka) in the town, and rioters destroyed hundreds of homes and shops, dozens of vehicles, and nearly two dozen [mosques](19894.md) and [Hindu temples](2247213.md).[^cite_note-\:0-3] While the government subsequently arrested over 100 people, Gnanasara Thera was never approached by the police.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

Gnanasara Thera was convicted of being in contempt of court, but was released on 23 May 2019 after having been given a presidential pardon.[^cite_note-PardonCNA-6][^cite_note-7]
