**Yaqshid Neighbourhood** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Yaaqshiid*) is a [neighbourhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Somalia) in the southeastern [Banaadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banaadir) region of [Somalia](27358.md).

It's one of the largest neighbourhoods within [Mogadishu city](60154.md), it consists of Villages Suuq Bacaad, Juungal, Carafaad, Towfiiq village, Siinaay, Fagax, Mahad Alla, Suuqa Xoolaha, Gubta, Tawakal and Jaamacadaha Muqdisho.
