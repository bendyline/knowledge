**Hamad Yussuf Masauni** (born 3 October 1973) is a Tanzanian [CCM](38207770.md) politician and [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament) for Kikwajuni constituency since 2010.[^cite_note-1] On 10 January 2022, he was sworn in as the new [Minister for Home Affairs](41709955.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
