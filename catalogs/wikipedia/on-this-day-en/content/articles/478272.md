# Medium-range ballistic missile

Not to be confused with [Intermediate-range ballistic missile](337326.md).

IRBM and MRBM missiles.

A **medium-range ballistic missile** (**MRBM**) is a type of [ballistic missile](38025.md) with medium [range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Range_\(aeronautics\)), this last classification depending on the standards of certain organizations. Within the [U.S. Department of Defense](7279897.md), a medium-range missile is defined by having a maximum range of between 1,000 and 3,000 kilometres (620 and 1,860 mi).[^cite_note-1] In modern terminology, MRBMs are part of the wider grouping of [theatre ballistic missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theatre_ballistic_missile), which includes any ballistic missile with a range of less than 3,500 kilometres (2,200 mi). Roughly speaking, MRBM covers the ranges over [SRBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SRBM) (tactical) and under [IRBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRBM).
