**Municipality of Escuinapa** is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Sinaloa) in the [Mexican state](8196151.md) of [Sinaloa](222251.md) in northwestern [Mexico](3966054.md), being the southernmost municipality in Sinaloa.[^cite_note-2] The seat is [Escuinapa de Hidalgo](736635.md).
