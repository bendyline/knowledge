The **prime minister of the Republic of Armenia** ([Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_language): Հայաստանի Հանրապետության վարչապետ, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Armenian): *Hayastani Hanrapetut’yan varch’apet*) is the [head of government](37570.md) and most senior minister within the [Armenian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Armenia), and is required by the constitution to "determine the main directions of policy of the Government, manage the activities of the Government and coordinate the work of the members of the Government." Also, according to the constitution, the prime minister heads the [Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_Council_of_Armenia), which prescribes the main directions of the country's defense policy; thus, the prime minister is effectively the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Armed Forces of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_Armenia).[^cite_note-4] [Nikol Pashinyan](17897961.md) is the current prime minister. He took the office on 8 May 2018 following the [resignation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Armenian_Revolution) of [Serzh Sargsyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serzh_Sargsyan).

## History

### Original role

The office of prime minister was first established in 1918 with the foundation of the [First Republic of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Republic_of_Armenia). The prime minister chosen by the National Council of Armenia and was accountable for international, domestic and regional issues. The first prime minister became [Hovhannes Katchaznouni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hovhannes_Kajaznuni) whose cabinet was made up from five members, all of which were from ARF. In addition, a ministry of interior was created, whose first head was [Aram Manukian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aram_Manukian).[^cite_note-Government_of_First_Armenian_Republic-5] It vanished when the First Republic of Armenia was incorporated into the [Transcaucasian Socialist Federative Soviet Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcaucasian_Socialist_Federative_Soviet_Republic) and then transferred into a full [Soviet republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic).&#x20;

### Soviet era

The governmental structure of the [Armenian Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic) was similar to that of the other Soviet republics. The highest executive and administrative organ of state power was the Council of Ministers. The Council consisted of the following positions:

- Chairman

- Vice Chairman

- Chairman of the State Planning Commission

- Cabinet Ministers

- Representative of the Committee of Agricultural Stocks

- Chairman of the Board of Arts

- Representative of the All-Union People's Commissariat

### Restoration

When Armenia regained [its independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Armenian_independence_referendum) in 1991, the office of prime minister was reintroduced. Under the new 2015 constitution, the prime minister is the most powerful and influential person in Armenian politics. The prime minister is appointed by the [president of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Armenia) upon the vote of the [National Assembly](4014302.md). The prime minister can be removed by a [vote of no confidence](208101.md) in Parliament. In the [constitutional referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Armenian_constitutional_referendum) held in 2015, citizens voted in favor of transferring Armenia into a [parliamentary republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_republic).

## Selection

Following a [general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Armenia), after commencement of the term of the newly-elected National Assembly the president appoints the representative of the party a [plurality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurality_\(voting\)) of seats in the assembly (i.e. the largest party). Factions of the National Assembly are entitled to nominate candidates for prime minister within a period of seven days.[^cite_note-6]

## Deputy

The principal deputy of the prime minister is the [deputy prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Armenia), the possibility of having more than one deputy prime minister, but no more than three.
