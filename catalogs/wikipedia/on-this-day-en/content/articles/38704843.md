**Chong Mek** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): ช่องเม็ก) is a border town in the [Isan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isan) region of [Thailand](30128.md). It is located in the [Sirindhorn District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirindhorn_District) of [Ubon Ratchathani Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubon_Ratchathani_Province), about 90 kilometres (60 mi) east of [Ubon Ratchathani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubon_Ratchathani). The town is the only land-based crossing open to most travellers from Thailand to [Laos](17752.md), the other such crossings being across the [Mekong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mekong) river.[^cite_note-LPWeb-1] [Vang Tao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vang_Tao?action=edit\&redlink=1) is the settlement on the Lao side.

The town has a busy market popular with Thai visitors, however its popularity has diminished since the opening of the [bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Thai%E2%80%93Lao_Friendship_Bridge) at [Mukdahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukdahan) in 2007.[^cite_note-LPBook-2]

## Sights and attractions

About 5 km (3 mi) west of Chong Mek is the large [Sirindhorn Reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirindhorn_Reservoir) created by the construction of the [Sirindhorn Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirindhorn_Dam) in 1971.[^cite_note-LPWeb-1]
