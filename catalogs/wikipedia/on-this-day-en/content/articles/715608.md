**Ucayali** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[ukaˈʝali\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a [department of Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Peru). Located in the [Amazon rainforest](48139.md), it is the second largest department in Peru, after [Loreto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Loreto).[^cite_note-1] It is administered by a [regional government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Government_of_Ucayali). Its capital is the city of [Pucallpa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pucallpa).

The [arapaima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arapaima) is depicted on both the flag and the seal of the region.

According to the adjusted 2025 census results, 624,000 people lived in Ucayali.[^cite_note-2]

## Etymology



Fountain in the city of Pucallpa.



Fishing [piranha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piranha) on the Ucayali River.

Its name is derived from the [Ucayali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ucayali) River.&#x20;

## Geography

### Boundaries

The Department of Ucayali is bordered by the [Brazilian](3383.md) state of [Acre](229497.md) on the east; the department of [Madre de Dios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madre_de_Dios_Region) on the southeast; [Cusco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cusco_Region) on the south; [Junín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jun%C3%ADn_Region), [Pasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasco_Region) and [Huánuco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hu%C3%A1nuco_Region) on the west; and [Loreto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loreto_Region) on the north.

### Environment

## Demographics

### Population

According to the [2007 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peru_2007_Census), the Ucayali department has a population of 432,159 inhabitants, 51.4% of which (222,132) are male and 48.6% (210,027) are female. 75.3% of the population (325,347) live in urban areas while the remaining 24.7% (106,812) live in rural areas.

As of 2002, the [Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_Nacional_de_Estad%C3%ADstica_e_Inform%C3%A1tica) estimated the department's population to be 468,922.

### Languages

[Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonic_Spanish) is spoken as a first language by 87.6% of the population, while 4.1% speak [Asháninka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ash%C3%A1ninka_language), 1.5% speak [Quechua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechua_language) and 0.1% speak [Aymara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aymara_language). Other [indigenous languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_languages_in_Peru), including [Shipibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shipibo_language), are spoken by 6.6% of the population and 0.0% speak foreign languages.

### Immigration

Persons originating from other departments of the country make up 34.7% of the population and 0.2% of residents were born abroad.

The largest immigrant groups come from the [Loreto Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loreto_Region) (12.5% of the total population).

### Age

The population age distribution is 53.9% under the age of 20, 9.3% from 20 to 24, 25.4% from 25 to 44, 8.8% from 45 to 64, and 2.5% who are 65 years of age or older.

### Education

[Secondary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_education) has been attended by 29% of the population and 2.3% also have graduated from non-[university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University) higher education, while 1.7% have complete university studies. 49.3% only have attended [primary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_education) and 9.1% have not had any education.

The [illiteracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illiteracy) rate in the region is 14.2%

## Political division

The department is divided into 4 provinces ([Spanish](26825.md): *provincias*, singular: *provincia*), which are composed of 14 districts (*distritos*, singular: *distrito*).
