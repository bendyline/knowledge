**Moderna, Inc.** ([/məˈdɜːrnə/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [*mə-DUR-nə*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key))[^cite_note-4] is an American pharmaceutical and [biotechnology](4502.md) company based in [Cambridge, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts), that focuses on [RNA therapeutics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA_therapeutics), primarily [mRNA vaccines](63136688.md). These [vaccines](32653.md) use a copy of a molecule called [messenger RNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messenger_RNA) (mRNA) to carry instructions for proteins to produce an [immune response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immune_response).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-10K-6] The company's name is derived from the terms "modified", "RNA", and "modern".[^cite_note-moderna-about-7][^cite_note-stat-story-8][^cite_note-servick-9]

The company's commercial products are the [Moderna COVID-19 vaccine](63399660.md) sold under the brand names Spikevax and Mnexspike, a [respiratory syncytial virus vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respiratory_syncytial_virus_vaccine) sold under the brand name Mresvia, and an [influenza vaccine](1045705.md) sold under the brand name Mflusiva. The company has 44 treatment and vaccine candidates, of which 37 have entered [clinical trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_trial). Candidates include possible vaccines for [influenza](19572217.md), [HIV](14170.md), [Epstein–Barr virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epstein%E2%80%93Barr_virus), the [Nipah virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nipah_virus), [chikungunya](366112.md), [human metapneumovirus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_metapneumovirus), [varicella zoster virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varicella_zoster_virus), as well as a [cytomegalovirus vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cytomegalovirus_vaccine), a [Zika virus vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zika_virus_vaccine) funded by the [Biomedical Advanced Research and Development Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomedical_Advanced_Research_and_Development_Authority), and three [cancer vaccines](793631.md).[^cite_note-10K-6]

In May 2026, [*Time*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_\(magazine\)) ranked Moderna first in their list of the World's Most Impactful Companies.[^cite_note-10]

## History

Moderna was founded in 2010 by [Derrick Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrick_Rossi), [Timothy A. Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timothy_A._Springer), [Kenneth R. Chien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_R._Chien), [Robert S. Langer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_S._Langer), and [Noubar Afeyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noubar_Afeyan).[^cite_note-11] [Stéphane Bancel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St%C3%A9phane_Bancel) was appointed as CEO in 2011.[^cite_note-moderna-about-7][^cite_note-Ego-12] Between 2011 and 2017, Moderna raised US\$2 billion in [venture capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venture_capital) funding.[^cite_note-stat-story-8][^cite_note-servick-9] In 2024, Bancel also led marketing for the company during a transition.[^cite_note-13]

### Product development

In 2013, the company formed a partnership with [AstraZeneca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AstraZeneca) to develop treatments for cardiovascular, metabolic, and renal diseases, as well as cancer. Moderna was also awarded a \$25 million grant by DARPA through a program Autonomous Diagnostics to Enable Prevention and Therapeutics: Prophylactic Options to Environmental and Contagious Threats (ADEPT-PROTECT). Its stated goal was to develop an mRNA vaccine with the capability to suppress a global pandemic within 60 days. In January 2014, the company entered an agreement with [Alexion Pharmaceuticals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexion_Pharmaceuticals) to develop treatments against ten diseases.[^cite_note-14] In January 2014, Moderna created its first venture, Onkaido Therapeutics, to focus "exclusively on developing mRNA-based oncology treatments".[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] It launched its second venture, Valera, in January 2015, with a focus on "viral, bacterial and parasitic infectious diseases".[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] Employees of Valera and Moderna developed an mRNA vaccine candidate against [Zika virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zika_virus) infection.[^cite_note-19] Another venture, Elpidera, was announced in May 2015 to continue work on RNA therapies advancing Moderna's work with Alexion.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] In 2017, Alexion terminated its partnership with Moderna after safety issues prevented their work from reaching human trials.[^cite_note-22]

In 2015, the company formed a partnership with [Merck](30874304.md) to develop treatments for cancer, and in 2016, the company formed a partnership with [Vertex Pharmaceuticals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_Pharmaceuticals) to develop treatments for [cystic fibrosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cystic_fibrosis).[^cite_note-Ego-12][^cite_note-Globe-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] In January 2016, the [Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_&_Melinda_Gates_Foundation) committed to provide at least \$20 million in [grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grant_\(money\)) funding to the company.[^cite_note-10K-6]

In July 2018, the company opened a 200,000 square foot facility in [Norwood, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwood,_Massachusetts) for manufacturing, preclinical and clinical work.[^cite_note-26] In December 2018, Moderna became a [public company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_company) via the largest [initial public offering](63879.md) of a biotechnology company in history, raising \$621 million by selling 27 million shares at \$23 per share.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

The first mRNA vaccine developed by Moderna was for [influenza](19572217.md) in 2015, and its first [antibody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antibody) encoded by mRNA was in 2019.[^cite_note-moderna-about-7] In 2023, Moderna acquired OriCiro Genomics, a Japanese manufacturer of [genetic engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_engineering) tools, in its first acquisition.[^cite_note-29]

In early 2023, the company in collaboration with [Merck](30874304.md) won [breakthrough status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breakthrough_therapy) from the FDA for its [mRNA-4157/V940](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRNA-4157/V940) drug candidate (now known as intismeran autogene), a [cancer vaccine](793631.md).[^cite_note-natmed1-30][^cite_note-31] On August 19, 2026, the company announced that its drug, in combination with Merck's [pembrolizumab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pembrolizumab) (sold as Keytruda), reduced the risk of recurrence and spread of [melanoma](716631.md). These positive results from the drug's late-stage [Phase 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phases_of_clinical_research) trials boosted Moderna's share price by 177% in a single day.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33]

In July 2023, the company entered into an agreement with the Chinese government to develop mRNA drugs for exclusive use in China.[^cite_note-34]

In May 2024, mResvia, a mRNA [respiratory syncytial virus vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respiratory_syncytial_virus_vaccine), was approved for medical use in the United States by the [Food and Drug Administration](11632.md) (FDA) for the prevention of lower respiratory tract disease caused by [respiratory syncytial virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respiratory_syncytial_virus) in individuals 60 years of age and older,[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-FDA_PR_20240531-36] the third [respiratory syncytial virus vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respiratory_syncytial_virus_vaccine) approved in the United States.

In August 2026, [Mflusiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mflusiva), an mRNA vaccine against influenza, intended for individuals aged 50 years of age and older, was approved for medical use in the United States.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38]

### COVID-19 vaccine

Main article: [Moderna COVID-19 vaccine](63399660.md)

From 2020 to 2021, Moderna received \$955 million from [Operation Warp Speed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Warp_Speed) to accelerate development of its COVID-19 vaccine, with \$4.9 billion committed in total for producing 300 million vaccine doses.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40]

In March 2020, the US [Food and Drug Administration](11632.md) (FDA) approved [clinical trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_trial) for the [Moderna COVID‑19 vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moderna_COVID%E2%80%9119_vaccine) candidate, and in December 2020, the vaccine, mRNA-1273, was issued an [emergency use authorization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_use_authorization) in the United States.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42] In 2022, it gained FDA approval both for the [monovalent vaccine](32653.md#Valence), Spikevax, and a bivalent [booster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booster_shot).[^cite_note-43]

In April 2022, Moderna announced plans to build a \$180 million vaccine factory in Montreal, Canada, forming a 10-year partnership with the [Government of Canada](616255.md), [Quebec](7954867.md), and [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) to produce 100 million *Spikevax* doses annually and expand vaccine research capabilities.[^cite_note-44] The plant is scheduled to supply COVID-19 vaccines in the fall of 2025.[^cite_note-45]

In February 2023, the company agreed to pay \$400 million to the [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health), [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College), and [Scripps Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripps_Research) to settle a dispute over the rights to a chemical technique that was used in the vaccine.[^cite_note-46]

### Legal disputes

[Arbutus Biopharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbutus_Biopharma) filed a patent infringement lawsuit against Moderna in 2022, alleging that Arbutus developed [lipid nanoparticles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipid-based_nanoparticle) that enclose the Moderna vaccine's mRNA payload.[^cite_note-\:0-47] In April 2023, a court affirmed a decision to cancel a patent by [Arbutus Biopharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbutus_Biopharma) in connection with the dispute.[^cite_note-48] In April 2024, however, the court issued an order that strengthened Arbutus's arguments by interpreting patents at issue in the manner Arbutus had urged.[^cite_note-\:0-47]

Several legal cases between Moderna and [Pfizer](62304.md) and [BioNTech](64671486.md) in various countries, alleging that the [Pfizer–BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine](65797150.md) violates the patent on Moderna's [mRNA vaccine](63136688.md) technology, are ongoing.[^cite_note-49]

In November 2024, the British pharmaceutical company [GSK plc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GSK_plc) filed a lawsuit against Moderna Inc. in a US federal court in the state of Delaware, seeking unspecified royalties alleging that the [Moderna COVID-19 vaccine](63399660.md) and RSV vaccine mResvia infringe on GSK's patents related to messenger RNA (mRNA) technology. According to the complaint, Moderna's use of lipid nanoparticles—crucial for delivering fragile mRNA into the human body—violates several GSK patents covering similar delivery innovations.[^cite_note-\:1-50][^cite_note-51] This lawsuit follows a similar legal action GSK brought against Pfizer and BioNTech earlier in 2024, claiming patent infringement over their mRNA-based COVID-19 vaccine.[^cite_note-\:1-50]
