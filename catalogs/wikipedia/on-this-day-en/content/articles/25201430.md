**Prionezhsky District** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Прионе́жский райо́н; [Karelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karelian_language): *Prionežjen piiri*; [Veps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veps_language): *Änižröunan rajon*; [Livvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livvi_language): *Oniegan rannikon piiri*) is an administrative district ([raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raion)), one of the [fifteen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_the_Republic_of_Karelia) in the [Republic of Karelia](199243.md), [Russia](25391.md).[^cite_note-Constitution-1] It is located in the southeast of the republic. The area of the district is 3,665 square kilometers (1,415 sq mi).[^cite_note-about-3] Its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) is the [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) of [Petrozavodsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrozavodsk)[^cite_note-RKarelia_admlaw-2] (which is not administratively a part of the district).[^cite_note-Constitution-1] As of the [2010 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Census_\(2010\)), the total population of the district was 21,502.[^cite_note-2010Census-4]

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), Prionezhsky District is one of the [fifteen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_the_Republic_of_Karelia) in the [Republic of Karelia](199243.md)[^cite_note-Constitution-1] and has administrative jurisdiction over all of its fifty-one [rural localities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia).[^cite_note-OKATO2-5] The city of [Petrozavodsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrozavodsk) serves as its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center),[^cite_note-RKarelia_admlaw-2] despite being incorporated separately as a [city of republic significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance)—an administrative unit with the status equal to that of the districts.[^cite_note-Constitution-1]

As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), the district is incorporated as **Prionezhsky Municipal District**.[^cite_note-RKarelia_munmd-6] Its fifty-one rural localities are incorporated into thirteen rural settlements within the municipal district.[^cite_note-RKarelia_munlist-7] The city of republic significance of Petrozavodsk is incorporated separately from the district as Petrozavodsky Urban Okrug,[^cite_note-RKarelia_uo-12] but serves as the administrative center of the municipal district as well.[^cite_note-RKarelia_munmd-6]
