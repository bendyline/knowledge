# Synthetic drug

**Synthetic drugs** refer to substances that are artificially modified from naturally occurring drugs and are capable of exhibiting both therapeutic and psychoactive effects.

In the medical setting, synthetic drugs possess psychotropic effects which can cure insomnia. Since there are limited clinical trials and human studies, the pharmacology and drug effects of most of the synthetic drugs are not well-known. Misuse of synthetic drugs can be fatal so take advice from the professionals before use.

Substances that possess the latter effect are known as [New Psychoactive Substances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Designer_drug) (NPS). Their purpose is to mimic the actions of [illicit substances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illicit_substances) by altering the structure of the original drug. By doing so, the "synthesized drug" can appear in the market without being easily detected. However, the uncertainty in the toxic effects of these substances puts the public's health at risk.[^cite_note-1] At present, these drugs are monitored by the [Early Warning System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_warning_system) (EWS).The major categories of NPS include synthetic stimulants, [synthetic cannabinoids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthetic_cannabinoids) and synthetic depressants. Common examples from these categories are phenethylamines, cannabinoids and benzodiazepines. To exert the psychoactive effect, specific receptors such as cannabinoid, dopamine and serotonin receptors are either stimulated or inhibited[^cite_note-2]

## Common synthetic drugs

### Synthetic cannabinoids



"Spice", the brand name given to synthetic cannabinoids.

There are seven major structural groups, which are [naphthoylindoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naphthoylindole), [naphthylmethylindoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naphthylmethylindole), naphthoypyrroles, [naphthylmethylindenes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naphthylmethylindene), [phenylacetylindoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenylacetylindole), [cyclohexylphenols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclohexylphenol) and classical [cannabinoids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabinoid) respectively.[^cite_note-emcdda.europa.eu-3] Compared with classical [cannabinoids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabinoid), synthetic cannabinoids differ structurally. Some common synthetic cannabinoids are available in the market such as [JWH-018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JWH-018), which is the most well-known naphthoylindole and [JWH-250](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JWH-250), a phenylacetylindole. They are sold under the brand name "Spice" as a recreational drug over the past decade.[^cite_note-4]

### Phenethylamines

Phenethylamines can be classified into ring-substituted and non-ring-substituted form. Ring-substituted Phenethylamines include 'D-series' and '2C-series' while common non-ring-substituted Phenethylamines contain Benzodifurans, PMMA, etc.[^cite_note-5]

### Novel benzodiazepines(Xanax)

[Alprazolam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alprazolam) is a generic medication derived from [benzodiazepines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benzodiazepine) with the brand name Xanax.

## Medical uses

Synthetic [cannabinoids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabinoid) can provide psychotropic effects such as relieving [nausea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nausea) and [dizziness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dizziness). [Phenethylamine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenethylamine) can relieve depressive symptoms while Alprazolam can treat [insomnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insomnia), [panic attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panic_attack) and [anxiety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anxiety).[^cite_note-medlineplus.gov-6] The most common delivery routes of Alprazolam and Phenethylamine are by [oral administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oral_administration). Both of which are available in the [dosage forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dosage_form) of pills and tablets. Synthetic cannabinoids are naturally in solid and oil form and are delivered by smoking.[^cite_note-emcdda.europa.eu-3]

## Adverse effects

The adverse effects of synthetic drugs are hard to determine as they usually contain other chemicals with variable concentrations and human studies are limited.

Synthetic [cannabinoids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabinoid) can cause cardiovascular problems such as [tachyarrhythmia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tachyarrhythmia), [seizures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seizure), psychological disorders and potential [carcinogenic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carcinogen) effects. Addiction and withdrawal symptoms which are linked to chronic use of synthetic cannabinoid include cognitive disturbances (e.g. difficulties in thinking), 'profuse sweating', central nervous system and gastrointestinal disturbances (e.g. nausea and vomiting).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

The adverse effect of [Phenethylamines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenethylamines) depends on the type of the drug. 'D series' cause more long-lasting effects than other phenylethylamines such as [tachycardia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tachycardia). At high doses, '2C series' produce hallucinogenic and entactogenic effects.[^cite_note-9]

[Alprazolam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alprazolam) can cause central nervous system disturbances and thoughts of suicide.[^cite_note-medlineplus.gov-6]

## Contraindications/Precautions

Synthetic cannabinoids are best avoided in users who suffer from rapid heart rate, vomiting, [agitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agitation_\(dementia\)), confusion and [hallucination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallucination).

Pregnant women are also not recommended to take phenethylamines as the effects on fetus are not known. In addition, use of phenethylamine might cause people with [bipolar disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bipolar_disorder) to convert from depression to mania and worsened schizophrenia symptoms. As the drug also affects the central nervous system, administration of such drug before surgery is not recommended.

Benzodiazepines can cross the placenta and can be excreted in [breast milk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breast_milk) therefore Alprazolam is contraindicated in pregnancy and lactation. Alprazolam is a CYP3As substrate so we should avoid CYP3As inhibitors such as cimetidine which is a [CYP3A4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CYP3A4) inhibitor.

## Pharmacology: Pharmacodynamics/Mechanism of Action(MOA)

Synthetic [cannabinoids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabinoid) act as Synthetic [Cannabinoid Receptor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabinoid_receptor) [Agonists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agonist) (SCRA)[^cite_note-10] by binding to cannabinoid receptors CB<sub>1</sub> and CB<sub>2</sub> . Its binding towards CB<sub>1</sub> receptor will lead to receptor [phosphorylation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorylation) that recruits [β-arrestin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrestin) 1 and β-arrestin 2, resulting in a loss of responsiveness and internalization (endocytosis of molecules by the cell). Stimulation of CB<sub>1</sub> receptor causes the dissociation of the βγ subunits of pertussis toxin-sensitive [G proteins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G_protein) (Gi /Go) from the α subunit (Giα) which then contributes to acute inhibition of synaptic neurotransmitter release. β-arrestin can also stimulate the mitogen-activated protein kinase, thus inducing additional cellular effects. Synthetic [cannabinoids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabinoid) can also bind to receptors other than CB<sub>1</sub> and CB<sub>2</sub> to activate inotropic transient receptor potential channels for cell membrane [depolarization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depolarization) and Ca<sup>2+</sup> influx.[^cite_note-\:0-11]

[Phenethylamines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenethylamines), which can act as either stimulants or hallucinogens, are indirectly acting [sympathomimetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sympathomimetic_drug) amines. Stimulants can modulate the levels and action of monoamine [neurotransmitters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neurotransmitter) such as dopamine, serotonin and noradrenaline for vasoconstriction and elevation in blood pressure. For example, 10-100 μM amphetamine can reach the [vasoconstriction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasoconstriction) effect. Hallucinogen ([psychedelics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_drug)) can mediate specific serotonin-receptor activities and produce hallucinations. They may have residue stimulant activity as well. In some animal studies, Phenethylamines have negative [inotropism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inotrope) in isolated cardiac tissues of rats due to stimulation of TAAR1, which is in contrast with human pharmacology.[^cite_note-12]

[Alprazolam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alprazolam) binds to GABA type-A benzodiazepine receptor sites which are the members of the pentameric ligand-gated ion channel (PLGIC) superfamily. It mediates phasic inhibition and extrasynaptically to mediate tonic inhibition. Once attached, conformational changes occur which stabilize the receptors and inhibitory signals are produced[^cite_note-\:1-13]

## Pharmacokinetics

Synthetic cannabinoids are delivered by [smoking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smoking). In a human study, after 50 μg/kg smoked JWH-018 are delivered, one male and a female have their serum concentration of 8.1 and 10.2 μg/L respectively after 5 minutes, down to 4.6 and 6.1 μg/L after 15 minutes, suggesting the biological half-life of JWH-018 is short. 13 phase 1 metabolites are identified. Monohydroxylated and dihydrodiol metabolites are most prevalent [metabolites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metabolite) of synthetic cannabinoids. UGT1A1, UGT1A3, UGT1A9, UGT1A10 and UGT2B7 isoenzymes were primarily responsible for JWH-018 and JWH-073 metabolites' conjugation and had high affinity for hydroxylated metabolites (Km=12–18 mmol/L). Generation of JWH-018-N-4- and 5-hydroxypentyl (JWH-018 metabolites) was primarily mediated by [CYP2C9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CYP2C9) followed by [CYP1A2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CYP1A2) and CYP2C19. CYP3A4 catalyzed JWH-018-N-4-hydroxypentyl production but with lower activity than CYP1A2 and CP2C19. The drugs are mainly excreted as [urine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urine).[^cite_note-\:0-11]

Phenethylamines are first-order kinetics with half life of 5 to 10 minutes which are absorbed by ingestion. The drugs have low concentration in the brain due to low biological half-life. It is difficult to measure the plasma concentration due to low stability of Phenethylamine. There are two possible metabolism pathways. The first possible pathway is metabolism by MAO-B (an intracellular enzyme mainly in the brain and tightly bound to the outer membrane of mitochondria which deaminates free primary and secondary amines) to form phenylacetic acid due to MAO-B selectivity on non-polar aromatic amines. Then, the metabolites undergo N-methylation by non-specific N-methyltransferase(NMT) or by phenylethanolamine-N-methyltransferase (PNMT) (found in the adrenal medulla) to form secondary amines and sympathetic neurotransmitter noradrenaline. The second possible pathway is deamination of the drug by the semi-carbazide-sensitive amine oxidases (SSAO) (found in the vascular tissue and have similar metabolism to MAO). An alpha-methyl side chain renders the drug immune to deamination in the gut. The drugs are mainly excreted in feces and urine.[^cite_note-14]

Alprazolam has high oral [bioavailability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioavailability) (84-91%) in which its maximum plasma concentration (Cmax) is reached after 1 to 2 hours. When taken with food, Cmax is increased by 25%. The [half-life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-life) profile of this drug for different populations is illustrated in the following table:

| Population | Half-life (hrs) |
| --- | --- |
| Healthy individuals | 11.2 |
| Obese | 10.7-15.8 |
| Alcoholic liver disease | 19.7 |

In terms of race, the half-life is 25% higher in Asian patients compared to Caucasians. For the extended-release formulation, it has a half-life of 10.7-15.8 hours in healthy adult patients. Alprazolam has a volume of distribution following oral administration of 0.8-1.3L/kg. Its [protein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein) binding in plasma is 80% (mainly albumin bound) and capable of crossing the [blood-brain barrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood%E2%80%93brain_barrier). It is metabolized to less effective metabolites by various CYP450 enzymes including CYP3A4, CYP3A5, CYP3A7, and CYP2C9. The majority of alprazolam metabolism is mediated by hydroxylation via CYP3As. 4-hydroxyalprazolam has 20% the binding affinity of the parent drug, alpha-hydroxyalprazolam has 66% the affinity, and the benzophenone metabolite has <1% the affinity. The drugs are mainly excreted in urine as unchanged Alprazolam. <10% of the dose is eliminated as alpha-hydroxy-alprazolam and 4-hydroxy-alprazolam.[^cite_note-\:1-13]

## Chemistry

### Detection in body fluids

Drug detection in body fluids requires specific reference data from the target drug. A common pitfall in the detection of New Psychoactive Substances (NPS) is the lack of reference data available for spectrometers to identify the presence of structurally modified illicit substances. Another drug detection technique, [immunoassay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunoassay), relies on active antibodies to detect the target drug by selectivity. If the drug is structurally modified, the original antibodies will respond in a different fashion which will give false positive or negative results.[^cite_note-15]

### Structure-activity relationship

#### Synthetic cannabinoids

Synthetic cannabinoids, members of the [aminoalkylindole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aminoalkylindole) class, made its first appearance in 2008. It was given the name 'JWH' because a chemist called [John W. Huffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_W._Huffman) synthesized them in the 1960s. Most synthetic analogs of cannabinoids mimic the structure of 9-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC), which makes them an agonist to the CB<sub>1</sub>(Type I) and CB<sub>2</sub> (Type II) cannabinoid receptors. CB<sub>1</sub> in particular, is expressed in the central nervous system and largely responsible for the [psychoactive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychoactive_drug) effec&#x74;**.**.[^cite_note-16]

A typical [agonist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agonist) consists of the following components: head, linker core and tail. Altering the structure from each component will affect the drug's affinity to the [cannabinoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabinoid) receptors. For instance, when a fluoride or nitrile group is attached to the carbon chains, the affinity for CB<sub>1</sub> will increase. The [aromatic rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aromaticity) from the aminoalkylindole class also play the role of enhancing the affinity by forming a hydrophobic cavity to stabilize the CB1 receptors.[^cite_note-17] As legislation becomes tightened under the monitoring of Early Warning System (EWS), attempts are made to alter the structure which produce new analogues such as the [Cyclopropylindoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UR-144) (UR-144) and [adamantylindoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APINACA) (APINACA).[^cite_note-18]



"JWH" is the notation given to the family of aminoalkylindoles. Shown above is the member of the JWH series, known as JWH-018

#### Novel benzodiazepines

Analogues of novel [benzodiazepines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benzodiazepine) are able to possess antibacterial activities. When they are tested against various bacterial strains, the [isoxazolyl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isoxazole) analogues with the p-chlorophenyl group (p-CIC6H4) attached have shown to be the most effective agent against the majority of the strains. Furthermore, attachment of an [electron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron) withdrawing groups and heterocyclic rings such as [thiophene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiophene) and [furan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furan) will increase the inhibitory effect against bacteria. Novel benzodiazepines can also modulate the [Central Nervous System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_nervous_system) by docking to the human [dopamine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dopamine) transporter D<sub>3</sub>. [Enantiomers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enantiomer) of [imidazole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imidazole) \[1,4] diazepines with either a [methyl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methyl_group) group (CH<sub>3</sub>) or a [propyl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propyl_group) group attached enhance the binding affinity towards human [dopamine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dopamine) D<sub>3</sub> receptors.[^cite_note-19]

#### Phenylethylamine

In general, phenylethylamine consists of an aromatic ring connected to an amine group which is 2 carbons away.[^cite_note-20] Each type of phenylethylamine differs by the substitutions at the alpha and beta carbon position. When a methyl group is attached at the alpha position, the compound becomes amphetamines which has the ability to modulate the 5HT-2A serotonin receptors. Eventually, the activated receptors cause hallucinations.[^cite_note-21] To ensure sufficient binding, the agonists must contain a primary amine, [methoxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methoxy_group) group and hydrophobic [functional groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_group).[^cite_note-22]

## Legality and Regulation

The Early Warning System (EWS), operated by the [European Monitoring Centre for Drugs and Drug addiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Monitoring_Centre_for_Drugs_and_Drug_Addiction) ([EMCDDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMCDDA)), overseas illicit substances that appear in the market. Established in 1997, It formed part of the framework that allows the [European Union](9317.md) to rapidly detect NPS that pose a risk to the public's health. Due to the tightening of legislation, most NPS are now illegal in the UK and Germany. Upon the emergence of NPS, this agency responds in the following manner:&#x20;

| Steps | Procedure |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Formal notification |
| 2 | Monitoring and response |
| 3 | Initial report writing |

[^cite_note-23]

When the EWS detects a new drug, the substance will be reported to EMCDDA along with any analytical data such as structures, analysts or components found pertaining to that particular drug. Then, an interconnected system is established to closely monitor the development of the substance. If harm is induced, an initial report is drafted to document the adverse effects of the drug.

## Trends

In recent years, a new type of benzodiazepine known as 'Designer benzodiazepines' are becoming available in [Europe](9239.md). It is based on the premise of modifying the structure of illicit drugs to evade international control measures. By early 2021, EMCDDA has monitored 30 designer benzodiazepines through the EWS. However, not much information is available regarding the market size of new benzodiazepines. Seizures reports from police and customs authorities have shown that new benzodiazepine is not of great interest compared to other NPS groups. In 2019, 4% of police seizures is attributed to benzodiazepines.[^cite_note-24]
