**Iran–Thailand relations** refer to the bilateral relations between [Iran](14653.md) and [Thailand](30128.md). Iran has an embassy in [Bangkok](56615.md) while Thailand has an embassy in [Tehran](57654.md).

## History

Relations between the two countries began when visits of [Persian diplomatic delegations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_embassy_to_Siam_\(1685\)) to [Siam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siam) are attested as early as 1685.[^cite_note-1] Indo-Persians made up the largest group of [Muslims](19541.md) from the 16th to 18th centuries. Influential Persian families like the [Bunnag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunnag_family) dominated [Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_people) courts for some time.

Following the lifting of sanctions in 2016, as a result of the [Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Comprehensive_Plan_of_Action), Iran and Thailand maintain closer economic relations.[^cite_note-2]

Following the death of [Bhumibol Adulyadej](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhumibol_Adulyadej), the [King of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Thailand) on October 13, 2016, [Iranian president](233913.md) [Hassan Rouhani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Rouhani) offered condolences to the Thai people and their government in a letter sent to Prime Minister [Prayut Chan-o-cha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prayut_Chan-o-cha) saying “the [Islamic Republic of Iran](14653.md) always regards the Kingdom of Thailand as an age-old friend and partner and hopes to see the expansion of bilateral relations in all fields".[^cite_note-3]

## Transport



First international flight between Bangkok to Tehran by [Thai Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_Airways) (October 2016)

[Thai Airways International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_Airways_International) announced in June 2016 that the airline would establish a new route between Bangkok's [Suvarnabhumi Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suvarnabhumi_Airport) and Tehran's [Imam Khomeini International Airport](716542.md). This comes after [AirAsia X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AirAsia_X) introduced that same route.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The first inaugural flight was launched on October 1, 2016.[^cite_note-6]

## State visits

On October 8, 2016, [Iranian president](233913.md) [Hassan Rouhani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Rouhani) arrived in Bangkok for the second [Asia Cooperation Dialogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Cooperation_Dialogue) summit. President Rouhani met with [Prime Minister of Thailand](273644.md) [Prayut Chan-o-cha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prayut_Chan-o-cha) and discussed economic cooperation between the two countries.

## Economic relations

Iran is looking into the Thai Rice Standard in order to start importing the product. Thailand also targets to export at least 700,000 tons of [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice) to Iran every year. In return, Thailand will start receiving imported crude oil and natural gas from Iran and wants to invest in energy businesses which include alternative energy projects.[^cite_note-7]

## Tourism

Thailand has become a popular destination for Iranian [medical tourists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_tourism).[^cite_note-8] However, due to numerous incidents of [methamphetamine](11038318.md) smuggling, Iranians coming to Thailand fall under heavy suspicion from the police.[^cite_note-9]

## Resident diplomatic missions

- Iran has an embassy in [Bangkok](56615.md).

- Thailand has an embassy in [Tehran](57654.md).
