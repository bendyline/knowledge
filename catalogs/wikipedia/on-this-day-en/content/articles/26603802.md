The **Canada national ice sledge hockey team** is the men's team representing Canada at international competition. The team has been overseen since 2003 by [Hockey Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockey_Canada), a member of the [International Ice Hockey Federation](16760449.md). From 1993 until 2003, the team was an associate member of Hockey Canada.[^cite_note-1] Since 2010 international para ice hockey has been a mixed, however there has only been one woman to ever play on the Canadian team.[^cite_note-2] The national team created exclusively for Canadian women is the [Canada women's national ice sledge hockey team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_women's_national_ice_sledge_hockey_team). This article deals chiefly with the national men's team.

The team is featured in the 2008 documentary "[Sledhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sledhead?action=edit\&redlink=1)".[^cite_note-3]
