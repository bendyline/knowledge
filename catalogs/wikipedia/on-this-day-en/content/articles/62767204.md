The **Lekki–Epe Expressway** is a 49.5-kilometre (30.8 mi) expressway connecting the Lekki and Epe districts in [Lagos State](626168.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] The Lekki-Epe expressway was first built in the 1980s. It was built during the [Lateef Jakande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lateef_Jakande)'s administration.[^cite_note-3] It is the second private toll project in Africa.[^cite_note-4] The road construction project was financed by the [African Development Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Development_Bank). The bank provided a loan of up to US\$85 million to help fund the upgrade and rehabilitation of the Lekki to Epe expressway in 2008, and it was based on Public-Private Partnership (PPP) under the Design, Build, Operate (DBOT), and Transfer and Rehabilitate, Operate (ROT) framework/business model.[^cite_note-5]

The Lekki Concession Company manages the toll road. [^cite_note-6]
