The **[Primetime Emmy Award](6579025.md) for Outstanding Comedy Series** is an annual award given to the best television comedy series of the year. From 1960 to 1964, this category was combined with the Comedy Specials (one time programs) category so that both type of programs competed for the same award during those years. The award goes to the producers of the series.

## Milestones

*[The Flintstones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Flintstones)* and *[Family Guy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Guy)* are the only animated sitcoms to be nominated for the award.[^cite_note-1] As of 2020, *[Fleabag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fleabag)* ([United Kingdom](31717.md))[^cite_note-2] and *[Schitt's Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt's_Creek)* ([Canada](5042916.md)) are the only shows from outside the United States to win this award.[^cite_note-3]

Up to the end of the twentieth century, the [Big Three networks (ABC, CBS and NBC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Three_television_networks) had dominated the category. Especially since then, eight other networks have won the award: [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO) (once with *[Sex and the City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_and_the_City)* and three times with *[Veep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veep)*) and [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV+) (two times with *[Ted Lasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Lasso)* and once with *[The Studio](72258571.md)* and *[Widow's Bay](79205666.md)*) four times, [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company) (with *[Ally McBeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ally_McBeal)* and *[Arrested Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrested_Development_\(TV_series\))*) twice, and [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video) (with *[The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Marvelous_Mrs._Maisel)*), the [BBC](19344654.md) (with *[Fleabag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fleabag)*; although seen later on Prime Video), [Pop TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_\(American_TV_channel\)) (with *[Schitt's Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt's_Creek)*; originally seen on [CBC Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_Television)), [Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_\(streaming_service\)) (with *[Hacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacks_\(TV_series\))*), and [FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FX_\(TV_channel\)) (with *[The Bear](71135592.md)*) once.
