As the result of the 2025 [Gaza peace plan](81215079.md) and [United Nations Security Council Resolution 2803](81623997.md), an internationally-supported **Gaza civil police** force is to maintain law and order in the [Gaza Strip](12047.md) for a transitional period until the administration of the Gaza Strip can be turned over to a reformed [Palestinian Authority](24093.md).&#x20;

## History

### Background

Following the 1993 [Oslo Accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo_Accords) which lead to the establishment of the [Palestinian Authority](24093.md), a [Palestinian Civil Police Force](34888928.md) assumed responsibility for policing in the Gaza Strip.[^cite_note-mfa-1] In 2007, the [Hamas](13913.md) militant group [took over](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gaza_\(2007\)) the Gaza Strip and assumed control over its [police force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamas_controlled_Palestinian_Civil_Police_Force).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Hamas and several other Palestinian militant groups carried out a series of [coordinated armed attacks](74998317.md) in [southern Israel](844338.md) on 7 October 2023 leading to the start of the [Gaza War](74998519.md). Israel and Hamas accepted a [Gaza peace plan](81215079.md) in October 2025 which was endorsed by the [United Nations](31769.md) through the adoption of [United Nations Security Council Resolution 2803](81623997.md) on 17 November 2025. UNSC Resolution 2803 empowers an international transitional body known as the [Board of Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Peace_\(Gaza_Strip\)), with the support of a [International Stabilization Force](81225292.md), to "train and provide support to the vetted Palestinian police forces" in the Gaza Strip.[^cite_note-4]

### Development

On 18 November 2025, the [European Union](9317.md) offered to train 3,000 Gaza police officers through the [EUPOL COPPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EUPOL_COPPS) mission which presently operates in the [West Bank](33209.md).[^cite_note-5] [France](5843419.md), [Germany](11867.md) and [Slovenia](27338.md) have expressed willingness to contribute to the mission. [^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

Hamas has sought to incorporate personnel from the existing [Gaza police force](81695807.md) into the new civil police.[^cite_note-8]

Recruitment for the new police force was started by the [National Committee for the Administration of Gaza](81600904.md) on 19 February 2026.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] [Egypt](8087628.md) and [Jordan](7515964.md) have agreed to help train new police officers. The NCAG hopes to deploy up to 5000 police officers in the Gaza Strip over the next 60 days.[^cite_note-11]
