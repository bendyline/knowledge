The **Palestinian Joint Operations Room**,[^cite_note-4] officially **Joint Room for Palestinian Resistance Factions**,[^cite_note-5] is a Gaza-based [joint front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_front) and [command institution](7092305.md) for the military wings of various Palestinian factions. It serves as high command of the *de facto* armed forces of the [Hamas-controlled Gazan government](38626632.md) and includes armed groups from various backgrounds, and ideologies from both the [right](56522.md) and the [left](18499.md), including [Islamists](15012.md), [Marxists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxism), [nationalists](47246228.md), and others.[^cite_note-6]

The Joint Operations Room is managed from the [Gaza Strip](12047.md), but forms a single battlefront against [Israel](9282173.md) from wherever Palestinian militant forces are located, without being confined to a specific geographic area.[^cite_note-7]

## Background

The operations room was formed for the first time in 2006 in order to unite against Israel during clashes and wars and included [Hamas](13913.md) and the [Islamic Jihad Movement](13093253.md), but it fell into obscurity. It was then developed, expanded, and was formed under its current name on July 23, 2018, among 12 military wings after clashes around Al-Aqsa Mosque, the most prominent of which was the installation of electronic gates there by Israel, in what is considered [Israeli-occupied](2781576.md) [East Jerusalem](343508.md).[^cite_note-aljazeera.net-1] It currently consists of 12 different armed groups, and has coordinated a large number of attacks on Israel including the [October 7 attacks](74998317.md),[^cite_note-8] and has also coordinated defence and retaliations against Israeli attacks.[^cite_note-\:0-9][^cite_note-\:2-10] [Ayman Nofal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayman_Nofal) said that the goal of the room was to create an inter-organizational alliance to coordinate operations, and increase the potential of the "Palestinian Resistance", and for it to "become a comprehensive framework for all the organizations, networks and fighters, without exception".[^cite_note-11] He also gave a list of the 9 factions out of 12 which he said were "fully unified under the room".[^cite_note-\:0-9]

## Activities

In 2019, the operations room launched its first military operation in response to the [dismembering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_and_dismemberment_of_Muhammad_al-Na'im) of the body of a Palestinian fighter on the [Israel-Gaza border](478337.md) by Israeli forces, consisting of shelling and rocket fire into Israel.[^cite_note-12]

Since 2020, the operations room has coordinated annual training exercises for military factions in the Gaza Strip, modeling [amphibious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_warfare) and ground attacks on Israeli military installations and [Kibbutzes](106200.md).[^cite_note-13] The exercise also included coordinated artillery fire, test rocket launches into the Mediterranean Sea, [UAV](58900.md) warfare drills, and [military diving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_diving).[^cite_note-14] The training modeled maneuvers similar to those seen in the 7 October attack on Israel in 2023.[^cite_note-15]

In mid 2024, the operations room accused the [British Armed Forces](31730.md) of collaborating with Israel on its intelligence efforts in Gaza.[^cite_note-16]
