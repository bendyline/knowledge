This is a list of winners and nominees of the **[Primetime Emmy Award](6579025.md) for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series**. Beginning with [1966](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/18th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards), leading actresses in comedy have competed alone. However, these comedic performances included actresses from miniseries, telefilms, and guest performers competing against main cast competitors. Such instances are marked below:

- **#** – Indicates a performance in a [Miniseries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miniseries) or [Television film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_film), prior to [the category's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actress_in_a_Limited_Series_or_Movie) creation

- **§** – Indicates a performance as a guest performer, prior to [the category's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series) creation
