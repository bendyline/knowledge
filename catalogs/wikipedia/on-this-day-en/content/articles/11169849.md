# Plastics industry

The **plastics industry** manufactures [polymer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymer) materials—commonly called [plastics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastics)—and offers services in plastics important to a range of industries, including [packaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packaging), building and [construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Construction), [electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronics), [aerospace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace), [manufacturing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manufacturing) and [transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transportation).

It is part of the [chemical industry](58721.md). In addition, as [mineral oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mineral_oil) is the major constituent of plastics, it therefore forms part of the [petrochemical industry](2293201.md).

Besides plastics production, [plastics engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastics_engineering) is an important part of the industrial sector. The latter field is dominated by [engineering plastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_plastic) as [raw material](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raw_material) because of its better mechanical and thermal properties than the more widely used [commodity plastics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commodity_plastics).

However, in 2020 the COVID-19 pandemic has had a [devastating effect on the fossil fuel and petrochemical industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_market_impact_of_the_COVID-19_pandemic). Natural gas prices have dropped so low that gas producers were [burning if off on-site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flaring) (not being worth the cost to transport it to [cracking facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethane#Uses)). In addition, bans on single-use consumer plastic (in China, the European Union, Canada, and many countries in Africa), and bans on plastic bags (in several states in the USA) has reduced demand for plastics considerably. Many cracking facilities in the USA have been suspended. The petrochemical industry has been trying to save itself by attempting to rapidly expand demand for plastic products worldwide (i.e. through pushbacks on plastic bans and by increasing the number of products wrapped in plastic in countries where plastic use is not already as widespread (i.e. developing nations)).[^cite_note-5]

## History

Further information: [Timeline of plastic development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_plastic_development)

- [Alexander Crum Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Crum_Brown) discovered the carbon double bond in ethylene

- [James Swinburne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Swinburne), the Father of British Plastics, revolutionized the plastics industry in Europe.

- [Leo Baekeland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Baekeland), who created the first plastic, Bakelite.

- [Hermann Staudinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Staudinger), who received the Nobel Prize for his discoveries of Macromolecules and Polymers, the chemical backbone of plastics.
