In [Peru](170691.md), the people directly elect a [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) (the [president](239109.md)) as well as a [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature). The president is elected by the people for a five-year term. The bicameral [Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_Peru) (*Congreso*) consists of a [Chamber of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies_\(Peru\)) (lower house) and a [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Peru) (upper house). The Chamber of Deputies has 130 members, with members elected for a five-year term by [proportional representation](23298.md). Consisting of 60 members, the Senate is elected via a mix of nationwide and regional electoral districts.[^cite_note-1]

Peru has a [multi-party system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party_system), which effectively bars one party from becoming the sole influence in a decision-making process. As such, [parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_parties) must work with one another to form [coalition governments](6036.md). The whole election process is held by the [National Jury of Elections](5445567.md) and the [National Office of Electoral Processes](5446067.md). Peru has [compulsory voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compulsory_voting).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-BrennanHill2014-3]

## Background

Secret ballots were introduced in 1931, along with an expansion of suffrage to allow all literate males to vote.[^cite_note-\:0-4] From 1993 to 2026, Peru had a unicameral legislature. Inaugurations are typically held on July 28.

## Latest elections

### Presidential election

The [president](239109.md) is elected using the [two-round system](25932.md).[^cite_note-reuters-5] The first round of voting allows eligible voters to vote for any viable presidential candidate.[^cite_note-reuters-5] The top two candidates who receive a plurality of the vote proceed to the run-off election.[^cite_note-reuters-5] The winner of the run-off election and the presidential election is the candidate who receives a plurality of the popular vote.[^cite_note-reuters-5][^cite_note-nytapril2021-6] However, if in the first round the candidate who is in the first place already gets more than 50% of the popular vote, that candidate will automatically win the election and a run-off election will no longer be needed.[^cite_note-nytapril2021-6]

## Laws

- Sale of alcohol and carrying firearms (except for Members of the Armed Forces and the National Police) are forbidden during an election.

- Political gatherings are forbidden, while public gatherings of any sort are prohibited during voting hours, including religious liturgies and entertainment shows. Clergy of any religion can not participate in their distinctive garments or habits.
