**Jeremy Roger Hansen** (born January 27, 1976) is a [Royal Canadian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Canadian_Air_Force) [colonel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonel_\(Canada\)) and retired [CSA astronaut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSA_astronaut). As a mission specialist on [Artemis II](41391511.md) in April 2026, he became the only person not from the [United States](3434750.md) to travel beyond [low Earth orbit](47568.md) and to travel to the vicinity of the [Moon](19331.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-fn1-2] He is also among the humans who have [travelled the farthest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_spaceflight_records#Farthest_humans_from_Earth) from [Earth](9228.md), having broken [Apollo 13](1770.md)'s distance record with the rest of the Artemis II crew.

Hansen was selected by the [Canadian Space Agency](328618.md) in the [2009 astronaut recruitment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_astronauts_by_selection#2009) alongside [David Saint-Jacques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Saint-Jacques). Before joining the astronaut corps, Hansen served as a Royal Canadian Air Force [captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_\(Canadian_army_and_air_force\)), piloting the [CF-18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CF-18) fighter jet at [CFB Cold Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CFB_Cold_Lake), [Alberta](717.md) and was later promoted to the rank of colonel.

In July 2026, Hansen announced his retirement from the CSA's astronaut program in September, planning to pursue new opportunities in Canada's space program.[^cite_note-retire-3][^cite_note-4]

## Early life and education

Jeremy Roger Hansen was born on January 27, 1976, in [London, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London,_Ontario), to Nancy Good and Gary Hansen. His paternal grandfather Magnus Hansen emigrated from Denmark to Canada in 1929.[^cite_note-5] He was raised on a farm near [Ailsa Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Middlesex,_Ontario#Ailsa_Craig) before moving to [Ingersoll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingersoll,_Ontario) for his high school education.&#x20;

At age 12, he joined 614 [Royal Canadian Air Cadet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Canadian_Air_Cadets) Squadron in London, Ontario.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

After graduating from [Ingersoll District Collegiate Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingersoll_District_Collegiate_Institute), Hansen attended the [Royal Military College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Military_College_of_Canada) in [Kingston, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_Ontario), where he completed a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) ([Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honours_degree#Canada)) degree in [space science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline_of_space_science) in 1999 with [First Class Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Class_Honours).

He later earned a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) degree in [physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics) from the Royal Military College in 2000, with research focused on wide field‑of‑view satellite tracking.[^cite_note-CSA_Profile-8]

## Career

In 2013, Hansen served as a cavenaut in the [European Space Agency's CAVES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESA_CAVES) training programme in [Sardinia](29376.md), alongside [Satoshi Furukawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satoshi_Furukawa), [Michael Barratt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Barratt_\(astronaut\)), [Jack Fischer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_D._Fischer), [Aleksey Ovchinin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksey_Ovchinin), and [Paolo Nespoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Nespoli).[^cite_note-9]

On June 10, 2014, NASA announced that Hansen would take part in the [NEEMO 19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NEEMO#NEEMO_19:_September_7%E2%80%9313,_2014) undersea exploration mission as an [aquanaut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquanaut) aboard the [Aquarius underwater laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquarius_underwater_laboratory).[^cite_note-10] The mission began on September 7, 2014, and lasted seven days.[^cite_note-nineteen-11][^cite_note-12]

### Artemis II



Hansen taking images through the [*&#x20;Integrity*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orion_spacecraft) window early in the 2026 [Artemis II](41391511.md) lunar flyby

On April 3, 2023, Hansen was announced as the first Canadian to travel to the vicinity of the Moon,[^cite_note-13] as part of the [Artemis II](41391511.md) mission. On April 1, 2026, he launched as a mission specialist of Artemis II and flew around the Moon on April 6 before returning to earth via [splashdown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Splashdown) in the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md) on April 10.[^cite_note-14] He became the first non‑American astronaut to travel beyond [low Earth orbit](47568.md) and the first to journey to the vicinity of the Moon. He is also, along with the other Artemis II crew members, among the humans who have travelled the farthest from Earth.[^cite_note-15]

### Retirement from the Canadian Space Agency

Hansen announced his retirement from the astronaut program of the CSA on July 6, 2026.[^cite_note-retire-3] Effective in September 2026, his retirement from active astronaut training is intended to facilitate opportunities for "creative, ongoing ways to support and enable the vital work happening in Canada with respect to space."[^cite_note-retire-3]

## Personal life

Hansen is married to Catherine Hansen, a medical doctor and expert in women's health, and has three children.[^cite_note-CSA_Profile-8] Hansen is interested in Indigenous knowledge. His mission patch for Artemis II was designed by [Anishinaabe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anishinaabe) artist Henry Guimond of [Sagkeeng First Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagkeeng_First_Nation), and references the [Teachings of the Seven Grandfathers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teachings_of_the_Seven_Grandfathers).[^cite_note-\:0-16] His hobbies include [mountain biking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_biking), [rock climbing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_climbing), and [sailboating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailboating).[^cite_note-17]
