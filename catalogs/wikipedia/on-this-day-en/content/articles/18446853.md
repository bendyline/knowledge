The **Roman Catholic Diocese of Chifeng/Chihfeng** ([Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_language): *Cefomen(sis)*, [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 赤峰) is a [diocese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese) located in the city of [Chifeng](1674149.md) ([Inner Mongolia](55064.md)) in the [ecclesiastical province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecclesiastical_province) of [Shenyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Shenyang) in China.

## History

- 1922: Established as the [Apostolic Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Prefecture) of Chifeng 赤峰 from the [Apostolic Vicariate of Eastern Mongolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Vicariate_of_Eastern_Mongolia) 東蒙古

- January 21, 1932: Promoted as Apostolic Vicariate of Chifeng 赤峰

- April 21, 1949: Promoted as Diocese of Chifeng 赤峰
