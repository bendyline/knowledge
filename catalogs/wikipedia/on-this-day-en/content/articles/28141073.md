The **Western Conference** is one of two [conferences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletic_conference) that make up the [National Basketball Association](22093.md) (NBA), the other being the [Eastern Conference](1305847.md). Both conferences consist of 15 teams organized into three [divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_\(sport\)). The Western Conference comprises the [Northwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwest_Division_\(NBA\)), [Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Division_\(NBA\)), and [Southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Division_\(NBA\)) Divisions.

The current divisional alignment was adopted at the start of the [2004–05 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004%E2%80%9305_NBA_season), when the now [Charlotte Hornets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Hornets) (then known as the Charlotte Bobcats) began play as the NBA's 30th franchise. This necessitated the move of the [New Orleans Pelicans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans_Pelicans) (named New Orleans Hornets at the time) from the Eastern Conference's [Central Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Division_\(NBA\)) to the newly created [Southwest Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Division_\(NBA\)) of the Western Conference.

The NBA first started awarding a Western Conference championship trophy during the [2000–01 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000%E2%80%9301_NBA_season), renaming it after Hall of Famer [Oscar Robertson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Robertson) in the [2021–22 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_NBA_season).[^cite_note-1] Also in 2021–22, the league began awarding the Earvin "Magic" Johnson Trophy to the [Western Conference Finals Most Valuable Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Conference_Finals_Most_Valuable_Player_Award), named after Hall of Famer [Magic Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Johnson).[^cite_note-2]
