The **1966 Italian Grand Prix** was a [Formula One](10854.md) [motor race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motor_race) held at [Monza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autodromo_Nazionale_di_Monza) on 4 September 1966. It was race 7 of 9 in both the [1966 World Championship of Drivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966_World_Championship_of_Drivers) and the [1966 International Cup for Formula One Manufacturers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966_International_Cup_for_Formula_One_Manufacturers). The race was the 36th [Italian Grand Prix](511451.md) and the 32nd to be held at Monza. The race was held over 68 laps of the five kilometre circuit for a race distance of 391 kilometres.

The race was won by Italian driver [Ludovico Scarfiotti](1235774.md) driving a [Ferrari 312](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_312) in his only Grand Prix victory. Scarfiotti led home his British teammate [Mike Parkes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Parkes) by five seconds. Parkes only just defeated New Zealand racer [Denny Hulme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denny_Hulme) in his [Brabham BT20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brabham_BT20), the pair separated by less than half a second.

While series points leader [Jack Brabham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Brabham) stopped with an oil leak in his [Brabham BT19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brabham_BT19) on lap seven, he secured his third world championship, and the unique achievement of becoming world champion in a car of his own make, when his only remaining points rival [John Surtees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Surtees) stopped with a fuel leak in his [Cooper T81](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_T81) 24 laps later. His championship victory also ended a four-year-streak of British drivers winning the Drivers' Championship.

Scarfiotti's victory was the last time an Italian won their home race in Formula One, until [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) won the [2026 Italian Grand Prix](81006624.md), six decades later.

## Race report



Race winner [Ludovico Scarfiotti](1235774.md) inside his [Ferrari 312](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_312) prior the start

[Jack Brabham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Brabham) was odds-on favourite for the Championship – only [John Surtees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Surtees) could stop him and only then by winning all three remaining races. He was prevented from doing so by an impressive performance from his former employers [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) at their home track. [Honda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Racing_F1) had brought a new 370 bhp 3 litre V12 engine for [Richie Ginther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richie_Ginther), whilst [Dan Gurney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Gurney) had his [Weslake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weslake) engine and [Graham Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graham_Hill) had the [BRM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRM) H16. [Lorenzo Bandini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Bandini) and [Mike Parkes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Parkes) led from the start, whilst Hill's engine gave up on the first lap, followed in retirement by [Jackie Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackie_Stewart) on lap 5 with a fuel leak, and then Brabham on lap 8 with an engine failure which coated the car in oil. [Jim Clark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Clark) in the H16 [Lotus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_Lotus) was moving strongly through the field. On lap 17, [Richie Ginther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richie_Ginther) had a horrific accident, crashing heavily into the trees and being very fortunate to escape with his life. Once Clark had pitted, [Ludovico Scarfiotti](1235774.md), Parkes, Surtees and Hill disputed the lead until Surtees withdrew with a split petrol tank which was pouring it all over his tyres and ending his championship chances. [Denny Hulme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denny_Hulme) had fought through the field to third place. But the race was Scarfiotti's, as he became the first Italian to win for Ferrari at [Monza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autodromo_Nazionale_di_Monza) since [Alberto Ascari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Ascari) in 1952. Parkes completed Ferrari's joy as he pipped Hulme by 0.3 seconds to claim second spot. Amidst all the celebrations, Jack Brabham was crowned champion. Apart from three [NART](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_Racing_Team) entries in [1969](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969_Formula_One_season) this race was the last time a Ferrari car was entered by a [privateer team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privateer_\(motorsport\)) when [Giancarlo Baghetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giancarlo_Baghetti) drove a private car entered by the British [Reg Parnell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reg_Parnell_Racing) team.

## Notes

- [Ludovico Scarfiotti](1235774.md) was only the third Italian to win his home Grand Prix, after [Nino Farina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Farina) and [Alberto Ascari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Ascari).

- This was the Formula One World Championship debut for British engine supplier [Weslake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weslake).
