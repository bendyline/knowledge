**Taanah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): طعانة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Ţa‘ānah*), alternatively spelled **Tana**, is a village in northern [Aleppo Governorate](7444618.md), northwestern [Syria](7515849.md). Situated in the [Aqil mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aqil_mountains?action=edit\&redlink=1), some 5 kilometres (3.1 mi) southeast of the [Shahba reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahba_reservoir?action=edit\&redlink=1), it is located about halfway between [Akhtarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akhtarin) and the northern outskirts of the city of [Aleppo](159244.md).

Administratively the village belongs to [Nahiya Akhtarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya_Akhtarin) in [Azaz District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azaz_District). Nearby localities include [Ablah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ablah) 6 km (3.7 mi) to the north and [Fafin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fafin) 6 km (3.7 mi) to the southwest. In the 2004 census, Taanah had a population of 1,062.[^cite_note-census2004-1]
