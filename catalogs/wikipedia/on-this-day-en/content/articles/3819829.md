**Assamakka** is a small desert town in northern [Niger](21373.md) at the main border crossing with Algeria. It is the only official crossing point between the two nations.[^cite_note-1] Assamakka shares the border with the larger town of [In Guezzam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Guezzam) 10 km on the Algerian side. A main road extends north in Algeria to [Tamanrasset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamanrasset), 400 km away. Assamakka is connected to the town of [Arlit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlit), 200 km to the south by a road which remains in largely a sand "[Piste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piste)". From Arlit, the "Uranium Highway", a tarred road built in the 1970s for mining trucks, travels south to [Agadez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agadez) and [Niamey](57061.md).[^cite_note-Decalo1997-2][^cite_note-Geels2006-3]

## Border regulation

While the military on either side of the border have frequently closed this border post to traffic due to Tuareg insurgencies of the 1990s and 2000s, the border area in this region has no effective controls or even marking. Easily transported border wall material is stored near the border crossing on the Niger side, ready to deploy. [^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Geography

The area around Assamakka is flat and absolutely desert: the rocky plains of the [Toussasset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toussasset?action=edit\&redlink=1) region begin just to the east, and are punctuated by [Ergs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erg_\(landform\)), or "dune seas". To the south the [Azawagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azawagh) region is characterised by dry plains and ancient dry river valleys arcing to the southwest from the [Aïr Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%AFr_Mountains).

Assamakka has a [hot desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_desert_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BWh*) with extreme heat and a great lack of rainfall. From late April to late October, the mercury rises almost every day above 40 °C in the shade; in June, the average high temperature even exceeds 45 °C in the shade. All year long, days are very hot and sunny. The cloud cover and precipitation are absent except during the short irregular "rainy" season in July and August, then the weather clears up again.

## Population

The town itself has long been a Tuareg encampment at a strategic desert well. What small population which is found here are nomadic or semi nomadic [Tuareg](103054.md), many of whom engage in cross border trade with Tuareg communities to the north and south, or pass through this area around the brief rainy season of the [Azawagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azawagh) plains to the far southwest.

The area is dominated by Tuareg [tribal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribal) populations, as well as [nomadic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomad) [minorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_minority), including [Hassaniyya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassaniyya)-speaking [Moors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moors) (also called [Azawagh Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azawagh_Arabs), not to be confused with Niger's [Diffa Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffa_Arabs)) in the northwest border regions of Niger. The Azawagh area is the centre for the [Iwellemeden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iwellemeden) [Kel Denneg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kel_Denneg) federation.[^cite_note-8]

The town itself has grown as a transit point for migrants from sub-Saharan Africa to the [Maghreb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maghreb). Trucks from the south drop off migrants here, who must then arrange trips over the border. Consequently, the small population is ethnically diverse and fluctuates. Illegal migrants deported south from Algeria are also returned here, sometimes in large numbers.[^cite_note-Graham-9]

## Tuareg insurgency

Several disputed killings of rebels and civilians are alleged to have taken place in the Assamakka area during the [2007 Tuareg Insurgency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Tuareg_Rebellion) against the Nigerian government.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]
