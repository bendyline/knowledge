**Statesville** is a city in [Iredell County, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iredell_County,_North_Carolina), United States, and its [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat). The population was 28,419 at the time of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census). Statesville was established in 1789 by an act of the [North Carolina Legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_Legislature).[^cite_note-Keever-5] It is part of the [Charlotte metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_metropolitan_area).[^cite_note-GR6-6]

## History

In 1753, [Scots-Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotch-Irish_Americans) [Presbyterians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyterianism) and [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Americans) Lutherans, who had originally settled in [Pennsylvania](23332.md), began arriving in what would become Statesville in 1789[^cite_note-Keever-5] to plant crops in the fertile soil where game and water were also plentiful. The settlement, known as [Fourth Creek Congregation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Creek_Congregation), was named for the freshwater stream running to the north of the present-day city center, the fourth creek west of the neighboring settlement of [Salisbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salisbury,_North_Carolina). The center of the settlement was a log cabin where the [Presbyterians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyterians) worshiped and where the First Presbyterian Church is located today.

Madison building in Statesville Commercial Historic District (1982)

In 1755, colonial governor [Arthur Dobbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Dobbs) authorized the construction of the colony's frontier fort, which was located approximately 3 miles (5 km) due north of the Fourth Creek settlement. Built and garrisoned by North Carolina provincial soldiers, [Fort Dobbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Dobbs_\(North_Carolina\)) defended British North America's western frontier in the colony of North Carolina during the [French and Indian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_and_Indian_War) and [Anglo-Cherokee War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Cherokee_War). Fort Dobbs combined the functions of a military [barracks](1033489.md), fortification, refuge for settlers, provisioning depot and center for negotiations with Native Americans.[^cite_note-FtDobbs-7]

The state legislature divided [Rowan County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rowan_County,_North_Carolina) in 1788, and the new county was named "Iredell" for [James Iredell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Iredell), associate justice of the first Supreme Court during the presidency of [George Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington).[^cite_note-Cheney-8][^cite_note-Assembly1800s-9]

One year later, the legislature selected a spot for the county seat. The Fourth Creek Congregation was chosen, and the settlement became known as "Statesville". The 1800 US Census lists the town as "States Ville", which was later combined as "Statesville". In 1800, there were 95 inhabitants in "States Ville", including 68 free white persons and 27 slaves.[^cite_note-1800Census-10]

As early as 1833, Statesville's leaders began laying track for railroads to connect the [Piedmont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedmont_\(United_States\)) area of North Carolina with the rest of the country.[^cite_note-Keever-5]

By 1858, Statesville was growing rapidly and soon afterward began leading the state in the production of [tobacco](30942.md) and tobacco products, the manufacture and blending of [whiskey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whiskey), and became a large distribution center for roots and herbs.[^cite_note-Keever-5]

On August 27, 1891, a passenger train derailed on the Bostian Bridge,[^cite_note-11] a 300-foot-long (91 m) bridge, and seven cars fell down. About 30 people died in the accident.[^cite_note-12]

## Geography

Statesville is in central Iredell County, in the western part of the [Piedmont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedmont_\(United_States\)) region of North Carolina.

According to the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Census_Bureau), Statesville has a total area of 24.4 square miles (63.1 km<sup>2</sup>), of which 24.2 square miles (62.8 km<sup>2</sup>) are land and 0.1 square miles (0.3 km<sup>2</sup>), or 0.48%, are water.[^cite_note-Census_2010-13] The north side of Statesville is drained by Fourth Creek, while the south side is drained by Third Creek. Both creeks flow east, joining just before reaching the [South Yadkin River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Yadkin_River) near [Cooleemee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooleemee,_North_Carolina).

### Climate

&#x20;Statesville has a [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) ([Cfa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification)) like most of the state of North Carolina, although its higher elevation leads to cooler temperatures than much of the Piedmont. Summers are generally hot and humid, with mild nights, while winters are usually cool with chilly nights and sporadic snowfall. Severe thunderstorms can be an issue during summer, with wind speeds of 60 mph and even gusts of 95 mph being recorded during these storms.[^cite_note-14]

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), Statesville had a population of 28,419. The median age was 38.7 years. 23.5% of residents were under the age of 18 and 17.9% of residents were 65 years of age or older. For every 100 females there were 88.8 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 85.3 males age 18 and over.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-19][^cite_note-Census2020PL-18]

99.5% of residents lived in urban areas, while 0.5% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-Census2020DHC-20]

There were 11,424 households in Statesville, of which 30.5% had children under the age of 18 living in them. Of all households, 35.7% were married-couple households, 19.5% were households with a male householder and no spouse or partner present, and 37.8% were households with a female householder and no spouse or partner present. About 32.9% of all households were made up of individuals and 14.1% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-19]

There were 12,435 housing units, of which 8.1% were vacant. The homeowner vacancy rate was 2.3% and the rental vacancy rate was 5.2%.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-19]

### 2010 census

As of the [census](6889.md)[^cite_note-GR2-4] of 2010, there were 24,633 people, 9,338 households, and 5,957 families residing in the city. The population density was 1,195.8 inhabitants per square mile (461.7/km<sup>2</sup>). There were 10,041 housing units at an average density of 489.1 per square mile (188.8/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial composition of the city was: 59.94% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_\(U.S._Census\)), 31.87% [Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_\(U.S._Census\)) or [African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American), 7.11% [Hispanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_\(U.S._Census\)) or [Latino American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans), 2.71% [Asian American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_American), 0.18% [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 0.02% [Native Hawaiian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Hawaiian) or [Other Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander_American), 3.84% [some other races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), and 1.42% [two or more races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial_American).

There were 9,338 households, out of which 28.1% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 41.7% were married couples living together, 17.9% had a female householder with no husband present, and 36.2% were non-families. 31.4% of all households were made up of individuals, and 12.7% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older. The average household size was 2.39 and the average family size was 2.99.[^cite_note-GR2-4]

In the city, the population was spread out, with 24.4% under the age of 18, 8.7% from 18 to 24, 28.0% from 25 to 44, 21.4% from 45 to 64, and 17.4% who were 65 years of age or older. The median age was 37 years. For every 100 females, there were 86.5 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 82.0 males.[^cite_note-GR2-4]

The median income for a household in the city was \$31,925, and the median income for a family was \$41,694. Males had a median income of \$31,255 versus \$22,490 for females. The [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income) for the city was \$19,328. About 12.7% of families and 16.1% of the population were below the [poverty line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_line), including 23.7% of those under age 18 and 13.8% of those age 65 or over.[^cite_note-GR2-4]

### Museums

## Sports

Statesville was home to a [minor league baseball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_league_baseball) teams of various names, mainly the [Statesville Owls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statesville_Owls), from 1939 until 1969. They played in several leagues over the years including the [Tar Heel League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tar_Heel_League) (1939–1940), [North Carolina State League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_League) (1942, 1947–1952), [Western Carolina League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Carolina_League) (1960–1962), and [Western Carolinas League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Carolinas_League) (1963–1969). They were league champions in their respective league in 1940, 1948, and 1962. The field was located at Statesville Senior High School and thus named Senior High Stadium.[^cite_note-25]

## Education

The city is part of the Iredell–Statesville School District. Schools within the city limits include East Iredell Elementary, N.B. Mills Elementary, Oakwood Middle School, Pressly Elementary, and Statesville High School. Schools serving Statesville residents but located outside the city limits include Cloverleaf Elementary School, East Iredell Middle School, and Third Creek Elementary School.[^cite_note-Schools-26]

Wayside Elementary School was an elementary school located off Salisbury Road in eastern Statesville. The current school building opened in 1941 and closed in 2002 when Wayside School and Alan D. Rutherford School merged to form Third Creek Elementary. The former Wayside building is currently home to the UAW 3520 headquarters, while the former Alan D. Rutherford site is home to the Iredell-Statesville Schools Administrative Annex, now known as the Alan D. Rutherford Education Building.

[Statesville Christian School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statesville_Christian_School) is a non-denominational K4–12 private school serving the greater Statesville area.[^cite_note-27]

[Mitchell Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitchell_Community_College), founded as a Presbyterian women's college in 1852, is now a public [community college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_college). In the 2008–2009 academic year, it became the first community college in the United States to be accepted into [NASA](18426568.md)'s University Student Launch Initiative competition.[^cite_note-28]
