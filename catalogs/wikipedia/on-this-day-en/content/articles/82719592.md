**Luis Enrique Arroyo Sánchez**[^cite_note-1] (born 10 November 1956) is a Peruvian politician and retired major general who served as [Prime Minister of Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Council_of_Ministers_of_Peru) from 17 March 2026 to 28 July 2026.[^cite_note-2]

## Early life and career

Arroyo retired from the military as a [major general](36301471.md). During his service, he "commanded the Third Army Division in [Arequipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arequipa) and the Fifth Division in [Iquitos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iquitos), served in the [Valle de los Ríos Apurímac, Ene y Mantaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valle_de_los_R%C3%ADos_Apur%C3%ADmac,_Ene_y_Mantaro) counterinsurgency zone, and directed the Army War College".[^cite_note-3]

He was appointed as the [Ministry of Defense](5629239.md) during the [José Jerí](80709159.md) [government](81305838.md) and briefly served in that role at [José María Balcázar](82451696.md)'s first cabinet. Between 5 November 2025 and 24 February 2026, Arroyo served as chief of the National Civil Defense Institute.[^cite_note-4]

## Premiership (2026)

Main article: [Presidency of José María Balcázar](82455738.md)



Arroyo in a meeting with President [José María Balcázar](82451696.md) (2026)

President [José María Balcázar](82451696.md) appointed Arroyo as prime minister after [Denisse Miralles](82502786.md) stepped down from the position after serving for three weeks.[^cite_note-5] Arroyo kept the most ministers from the Miralles cabinet, except for five ministries, including the [Ministry of Defense](5629239.md) where he previously served as minister at before his premiership.
