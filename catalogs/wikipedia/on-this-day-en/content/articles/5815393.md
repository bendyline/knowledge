**Nexperia B.V.** is a [semiconductor manufacturer](612813.md) headquartered in [Nijmegen](21713.md), the Netherlands. It is a subsidiary of [Wingtech Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wingtech), a [Shanghai-listed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Stock_Exchange) company partially owned by the Chinese government. In October 2025, the Dutch government briefly took control of governance of the firm, citing security concerns. Since then, its Chinese operations operate as a de facto independent entity.[^cite_note-4] It has [front-end factories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiconductor_fabrication_plant) in [Hamburg](13467.md), Germany, and [Greater Manchester](79819.md), England.[^cite_note-5] It is the former Standard Products business unit of [NXP Semiconductors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXP_Semiconductors) (previously [Philips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philips) Semiconductors). The company's product range includes [bipolar transistors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transistor), [diodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diode), [ESD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrostatic_discharge) protection, [TVS diodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transient-voltage-suppression_diode), [MOSFETs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOSFET), and [logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic_gate) devices.[^cite_note-6]

## History

Nexperia is a second-generation spin-off of Philips' semiconductor division, whose history dates back to the 1920s when Philips acquired vacuum tubes manufacturer [Mullard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mullard),[^cite_note-7] whose facility in [Hazel Grove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazel_Grove) became today's Nexperia Manchester,[^cite_note-8] and Valvo in [Hamburg](13467.md).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-\:0-10] In the early 1950s, Philips started with the production of semiconductors in Nijmegen[^cite_note-11] and Hamburg,[^cite_note-\:0-10] where the Nexperia wafer fab is located.[^cite_note-\:1-12] In 1981, Philips opened a factory in [Cabuyao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabuyao), Philippines. The fab is owned and operated by Nexperia.[^cite_note-13] In 1991, Philips founded ITEC, a manufacturer of semiconductor equipment. Today, ITEC is an independent subsidiary of Nexperia.[^cite_note-14] Philips and [Motorola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorola) established the Semiconductor Miniature Products factory in [Seremban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seremban) Malaysia in 1992 as a joint venture,[^cite_note-15] which became part of Nexperia.[^cite_note-\:1-12]

In 2006, Philips spun off its semiconductor division under the name NXP and sold an 80.1% stake to private equity investors.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] In August 2010, NXP completed its [initial public offering](63879.md) and its shares were listed on the [Nasdaq](21559.md).[^cite_note-18] On June 14, 2016, NXP Semiconductors announced an agreement to divest its Standard Products business to a consortium of Chinese financial investors consisting of Beijing Jianguang Asset Management Co., Ltd ("JAC Capital"), a subsidiary of a Chinese [state-owned](204768.md) investment company, and Wise Road Capital LTD ("Wise Road Capital").[^cite_note-19] Following the official transaction on February 6, 2017, Nexperia became an independent company, and the entire scope of the NXP Standard Products business, including its management team, and approximately 11,000 NXP employees were transferred from NXP to Nexperia.[^cite_note-20]

On October 25, 2018, Nexperia was acquired by [Wingtech Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wingtech), a partially state-owned Chinese ODM for smartphone companies for \$3.6 billion.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-\:022-22][^cite_note-\:12-23] Wingtech is partially owned by the [State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State-owned_Assets_Supervision_and_Administration_Commission_of_the_State_Council) (SASAC).[^cite_note-\:022-22][^cite_note-\:12-23]

In January 2023, Dutch public broadcaster [Nederlandse Omroep Stichting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nederlandse_Omroep_Stichting) reported that Nexperia chips ended up in Russian military kit despite [international sanctions during the Russian invasion of Ukraine](70323386.md).[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] Nexperia transceivers were discovered in a Russian [Kh-101](21425976.md) missile, according to the [Royal United Services Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_United_Services_Institute).[^cite_note-26]

In March 2024, Nexperia's servers were affected by a [ransomware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ransomware) attack in which intellectual property was stolen.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

In September 2026, Nexperia entered a strategic partnership with [Tata Electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Group) to manufacture and package semiconductor products in [India](14533.md). Under the agreement, Nexperia's power-control chips at Tata Electronics' semiconductor fabrication facility in [Dholera, Gujarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhola,_Gujarat) while assembly and testing would be conducted at Tata Electronics' facility in Jagiroad, Assam. The companies also agreed to cooperate on future technology development.[^cite_note-29]

### Dutch government intervention

In October 2025, the Dutch [Ministry of Economic Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economic_Affairs_\(Netherlands\)) took control of Nexperia's governance using the powers of the [Goods Availability Act](81327283.md), citing national security and European economic security.[^cite_note-SCMP13-10-25-30][^cite_note-31] According to *[NRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NRC_\(newspaper\))*, government insiders said the move meant to prevent Nexperia from leaking chip-related intellectual property and moving operations to China.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-SCMP13-10-25-30][^cite_note-33] According to [CNN](62028.md), the Dutch government acted following pressure from Washington, which the Dutch government denies.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35] At the time, Nexperia chips were etched on wafers in Hamburg, Germany, which were primarily [packaged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_circuit_packaging) in Dongguan, China.[^cite_note-36] The Chinese government subsequently banned Nexperia from exporting the locally packaged products (about 70% of Nexperia's EU production), triggering a [chip shortage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chip_shortage) for several EU carmakers.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38]

On 19 November 2025, the Dutch government suspended its control of Nexperia's governance.[^cite_note-39] Some sales of Nexperia chips resumed, but they were reportedly being "drip-feed" to European firms.[^cite_note-40] In November 2025, Wingtech filed a challenge against the Dutch government in the [Supreme Court of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_the_Netherlands).[^cite_note-41] In February 2026, a Dutch court ordered an investigation into mismanagement at Nexperia.[^cite_note-42] In May 2026, Wingtech filed a lawsuit against Nexperia, seeking US\$1.2 billion compensation for alleged losses.[^cite_note-43] In August 2026, a Chinese court ordered freezing of US\$300 million in Chinese Nexperia assets.[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46] Around the same time, Zhang Qiuming, CEO of Nexperia China announced launching three new product lines to be produced on a 12-inch wafer process, as opposed to Nexperia's 6 and 8 inch production lines in Europe.[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48]

### Philippines

Nexperia has been accused of labor violations at its facility in the Philippines. On December 17, 2024, four officials of the Nexperia Philippines Inc. Worker's Union, an affiliate of the [Metal Workers Alliance of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_Workers_Alliance_of_the_Philippines) and the [Kilusang Mayo Uno](5308660.md), were dismissed by the company amid an ongoing collective bargaining association. The officials were accused of obstructing entry and exit points to the factory.[^cite_note-49] Trade union members reported layoffs and management interference in a strike vote earlier that year.[^cite_note-50]

## Acquisitions

In 2021, the company purchased the [Inmos microprocessor factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inmos_microprocessor_factory) in Newport, Wales.[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-\:022-22] On 17 November 2022, the British government ordered Nexperia to divest 86% of its ownership interest in the Newport facility, citing national security reasons around the company's ownership.[^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53] Nexperia subsequently engaged [New York](645042.md) law firm, [Akin Gump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akin_Gump_Strauss_Hauer_&_Feld) to act on their behalf in their application for a [judicial review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_review_in_English_law) of the UK government's divestment decision.[^cite_note-54] In November 2023, Nexperia agreed to sell the facility to [Vishay Intertechnology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishay_Intertechnology) for \$177 million.[^cite_note-55] In March 2024, the UK government approved the acquisition of the Newport wafer fab, as announced by Secretary of State [Oliver Dowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Dowden).[^cite_note-56][^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58]

In November 2022, Nexperia acquired the [Delft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft)-based manufacturer of [power management integrated circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_management_integrated_circuit), Nowi.[^cite_note-59] Subsequently, the Dutch government announced that it would investigate Nexperia's acquisition of Nowi on national security grounds.[^cite_note-60] In November 2023, the Dutch government approved Nexperia's acquisition of Nowi.[^cite_note-61][^cite_note-62]

## Products



A [7400 Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7400_Series) integrated circuit manufactured by Nexperia

Nexperia focuses on discrete semiconductors with basic functions.[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65] These essential semiconductors are combined with more complex chips to form electronic circuits. They are used in the automotive industry as well as other industrial, mobile and consumer products.[^cite_note-66] Nexperia's products include 650V silicon carbide [Schottky diodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schottky_diode),[^cite_note-67] bipolar transistors[^cite_note-68] as well as 650V GaN FETs,[^cite_note-69] MOSFETs like the NextPower series,[^cite_note-70] or the PMCB60XN, which at launch was the most energy efficient 30V MOSFET on the market.[^cite_note-71] LED drivers are offered with a side-wettable flank since 2020[^cite_note-72] and since 2023 the company produces 600V [IGBTs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insulated-gate_bipolar_transistor) with low switching losses and conduction.[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74] Further products include [ESD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrostatic_discharge) protection devices,[^cite_note-75] Transient Voltage Suppressor Diodes,[^cite_note-76] [electromagnetic interference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_interference) (EMI) filter[^cite_note-77] as well as load switches.[^cite_note-78] In July 2024 the company launched the NPS3102 family of e-Fuses, which, unlike traditional [fuses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuse_\(electrical\)), can be used multiple times. The B-series can attempt to reset itself after a fault, while the A-series requires a manual reset.[^cite_note-79][^cite_note-80]

Nexperia also cooperates with other firms like [Mitsubishi Electric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi_Electric) or Kyocera AVX Components to develop products such as SiC power semiconductors or SiC rectifier modules.[^cite_note-81][^cite_note-82] Following the acquisition of Nowi, Nexperia also offers [energy harvesting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_harvesting) PMICs designed to complement or replace batteries in low power applications such as [wearables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wearable_technology).[^cite_note-83] The first energy harvesting chip released by Nexperia was the NEH2000BY in April 2023.[^cite_note-eenewseurope-84]

## Locations

Nexperia is headquartered in [Nijmegen](21713.md), the Netherlands, with manufacturing and [research and development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_and_development) facilities in [Europe](9239.md), the [United States](3434750.md) and [Asia](689.md). It has front-end factories in [Hamburg](13467.md), Germany, [Greater Manchester](79819.md), England, and assembly and testing facilities in [Guangdong](65109.md), China, [Seremban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seremban), Malaysia, and [Cabuyao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabuyao), Philippines.[^cite_note-85][^cite_note-86]

Nexperia also has R\&D facilities in:[^cite_note-87]

- [Nijmegen](21713.md), [Delft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft) (the Netherlands)

- [Hamburg](13467.md), [Munich](19058.md) (Germany)

- [Stockport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockport), (United Kingdom)

- [Dallas](53838.md) (United States)

- [Penang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penang) (Malaysia)

- [Osaka](52230.md) (Japan)

- [Hong Kong](13404.md), [Shanghai](27643.md), [Shenzhen](83368.md) (China)

## Sponsorships

In May 2024, Nexperia became the main sponsor of the [NEC Nijmegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NEC_Nijmegen) association football club for the [2024/2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Eredivisie). According to [De Gelderlander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Gelderlander), the deal was signed to extend for at least three years.[^cite_note-88][^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90]

Nexperia and other sponsors are supporting the [TeamNL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TeamNL) House initiative, located in the [Parc de la Villete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parc_de_la_Villette) in [Paris](22989.md), which will allow Dutch fans to watch the [2024 Summer Paralympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Paralympics) live and honor participating athletes.[^cite_note-91][^cite_note-92][^cite_note-93]

In April 2024, Nexperia became the main sponsor of the [Hamburg Towers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg_Towers) basketball club. The sponsorship will continue at least until the end of the 2026/2027 season.[^cite_note-94]
