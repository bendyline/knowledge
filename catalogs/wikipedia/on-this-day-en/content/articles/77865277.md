**Tigrai Television** ([Amharic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amharic_language): ቴሌቪዥን ትግራይ), also known as **Tigrai TV** and **Tigrai Mass Media Agency,** is a television, digital media, radio, and newspaper news organization owned by the [Tigray Region](357350.md) government in [Ethiopia](187749.md). Tigray TV was first established on July 31, 1991, as part of Ethiopian Television, now known as the [Ethiopian Broadcasting Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopian_Broadcasting_Corporation).[^cite_note-Assess2-1] It became an independent media organization on December 15, 2007, under the name Tigrai Mass Media Agency.[^cite_note-Proc1-2][^cite_note-Assess-3]

## Creation

Tigray Television (Tigrai TV) is a media organization owned by the Tigray Region government in Ethiopia. It was founded in 1991 as part of the Ethiopian Television and became independent in 2007.[^cite_note-Assess2-1] Tigrai TV offers news and information through television, digital media, radio, and newspapers.[^cite_note-Assess2-1][^cite_note-Proc1-2][^cite_note-Assess-3] From 2006 to 2008 it continued its broadcast under the supervision of Tigray Information Bureau.[^cite_note-Assess2-1] Then after, Tigray Mass Media Tigray was formally established at agency level by the Tigray House of People's Representatives Proclamation 142/2000.[^cite_note-Proc1-2][^cite_note-Assess2-1] Since 2017, the medium began its 24 hours broadcast in [Tigrinya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tigrinya_language), [Saho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saho_language), [Kunama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunama_language) languages, and lately [Amharic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amharic), [Oromo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oromo_language), [Qafar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qafar), [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) and [English languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language).[^cite_note-Assess2-1][^cite_note-Tigrai_Tv-4]

## Programs

Tigrai TV has many daily programs such as Internal news, International news, Political analysis, Business, Sport, etc.[^cite_note-Tigrai_Tv-4][^cite_note-SA-5]
