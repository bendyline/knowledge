# Vivek Vihar

**Vivek Vihar** is one of 3 subdivisions of [Delhi](37756.md)'s [Shahdara district](8233057.md),[^cite_note-DM_Shahdara:_Vivek_Vihar-1] near the edge of Ghaziabad as well as a small neighbourhood in that subdivision located beside just besides the [Choudhary Charan Singh road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_Ring_Road,_Delhi) along with [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md) border.[^cite_note-Locality_Review:_Vivek_Vihar-2][^cite_note-Google_Maps:_Vivek_Vihar-3]

## Vivek Vihar subdivision

The Vivek Vihar subdivision consists of 5 main areas:[^cite_note-DM_Shahdara:_Vivek_Vihar-1][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

- [Karkarduma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karkarduma_metro_station)

- Oldenpur

- Chandrawali

- [Ghondali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghonda)

- [Seelampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seelampur)

- Jhilmil

## Vivek Vihar colony

Vivek Vihar is one of the most premier localities of Shahdara and primarily consists of residential developments dominated by builder floors and [independent houses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House). The locality is horizontal in nature and comprises [low-rise establishments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-rise_building). It is systematically laid-out and has been divided into 4 blocks - blocks A to D. The area is close to other important areas of eastern Delhi such as Anand Vihar, Jhilmil Colony, Surajmal Vihar, Vishwas Nagar, Bihari Colony, Teliwara and Bhola Nath Nagar.[^cite_note-Locality_Review:_Vivek_Vihar-2][^cite_note-Delhi_Information:_Vivek_Vihar-6] The [Hanuman Balaji temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanuman), located on Unnati Marg, is a major religious attraction. It was established in the year 1998.[^cite_note-7]

### Connectivity

- **[Delhi Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Metro):** The area is located close to the [Dilshad Garden metro station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilshad_Garden_metro_station) and [Jhilmil metro station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhilmil_metro_station) of the [Red Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Line_\(Delhi_Metro\)), and the [Anand Vihar metro station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anand_Vihar_metro_station) and [Karkardooma metro station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karkarduma_metro_station) of the [Blue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Line_\(Delhi_Metro\)) and [Pink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Line_\(Delhi_Metro\)) lines.

- **[Indian Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Railways):** The area is served by the Vivek Vihar railway station, which is on the [Sahibabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahibabad_Junction_railway_station)-[Shahdara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Shahdara_Junction_railway_station)-[Delhi Junction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Junction_railway_station) branch of the [Delhi-Howrah line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howrah%E2%80%93Delhi_main_line).

- **Bus:** [DTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Transport_Corporation) bus routes 313, 336, 340, 342, 358, and 704 serve the area from the Vivek Vihar bus stop.
