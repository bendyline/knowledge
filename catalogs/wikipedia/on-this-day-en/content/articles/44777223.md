The **governor of Kharkiv Oblast** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Голова Харківської обласної державної адміністрації) is the [head of the executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Local_State_Administration_\(Ukraine\)) for the [Kharkiv Oblast](494901.md).

The office of [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor) is an appointed position, with officeholders being appointed by the [president of Ukraine](20688479.md), on recommendation from the [Prime Minister of Ukraine](1256678.md), to serve a four-year term. On 24 December 2021 [Oleh Syniehubov](68861798.md) was appointed governor of Kharkiv Oblast.[^cite_note-Synyehubov7318572-1]

The official residence for the governor is located in [Kharkiv](2788558.md).
