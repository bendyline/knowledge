The **Poland national speedway team** is the national [motorcycle speedway](230182.md) team of Poland and is controlled by the Polish Motor Union ([PZM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polski_Zwi%C4%85zek_Motorowy)). They are one of the most successful speedway nations of all time.&#x20;

## History

The Polish speedway team participated in the inaugural [Speedway World Team Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedway_World_Team_Cup), being seeded through to the final of the [1960 Speedway World Team Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960_Speedway_World_Team_Cup).[^cite_note-1] The team were a major force in the opening decade of the tournament, winning the World Team Cup in [1961](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1961_Speedway_World_Team_Cup), [1965](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1965_Speedway_World_Team_Cup), [1966](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966_Speedway_World_Team_Cup) and [1969](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969_Speedway_World_Team_Cup), with only the [Swedish team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_national_speedway_team) winning more World Cups.[^cite_note-2]&#x20;

In the 1970s, the team continued to be one of the major forces in Eastern Europe and qualified for the World Team Cup final every year from 1970 to 1979. [Andrzej Wyglenda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrzej_Wyglenda) and [Jerzy Szczakiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerzy_Szczakiel) also won the [1971 Speedway World Pairs Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1971_Speedway_World_Pairs_Championship).[^cite_note-3] However, during the 1980s the team began to struggle against the best nations and only managed two finals in 1980 and 1984.&#x20;

It was not until the mid-1990s and the arrival of [Tomasz Gollob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomasz_Gollob) that Poland began to challenge the best nations. The team won the silver medal at the [1994 Speedway World Team Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Speedway_World_Team_Cup) (their first final for 10 years). They then won the World Team Cup for the sixth time in 1996 (27 years after their last win).&#x20;

In 2001, the Speedway World Team Cup was rebranded the [Speedway World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedway_World_Cup) and Poland began to experience a boom in terms of spectator numbers, which led to an influx of new riders. The team reached the World Cup final every year from 2001 to 2017 (except for 2006 and 2012) and dominated world speedway by winning the title eight times, in [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Speedway_World_Cup), [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Speedway_World_Cup), [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Speedway_World_Cup), [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Speedway_World_Cup), [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Speedway_World_Cup), [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Speedway_World_Cup), [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Speedway_World_Cup) and [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Speedway_World_Cup).[^cite_note-4]&#x20;

Poland's speedway team was awarded the Team of the Year in Poland by *[Przegląd Sportowy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Przegl%C4%85d_Sportowy)* in 2009 and 2010.[^cite_note-5]&#x20;

Although the team failed to win the [Speedway of Nations](56783360.md) from 2018 to 2022, they did win the World Cup when it returned in 2023. The World Cup win was their 14th World Team Championship (one behind Denmark in the all time standings).[^cite_note-6]&#x20;
