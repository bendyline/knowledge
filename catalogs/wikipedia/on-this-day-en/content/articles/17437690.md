**Malard** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): ملارد)[^cite_note-6] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Malard_County\)) of [Malard County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malard_County), [Tehran](202698.md) province, [Iran](14653.md), serving as capital of both the county[^cite_note-Malard_County-7] and the district. It is also the administrative center for [Malard-e Shomali Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malard-e_Shomali_Rural_District).[^cite_note-Karaj_County_Rural_Districts-8][^cite_note-9] In 1995, the village of Malard merged with the village of Sar Asiab-e Malard (سرآسیاب ملارد) to become the city of Malard.[^cite_note-Malard_City-3]

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 228,673 in 61,302 households,[^cite_note-2006_Tehran_Province-10] when it was capital of the former [Malard District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malard_District) of [Shahriar County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahriar_County). The following census in 2011 counted 290,817 people in 85,445 households,[^cite_note-2011_Tehran_Province-11] by which time the district had been separated from the county in the establishment of Malard County. The city and the rural district were transferred to the new Central District, with Malard as the county's capital.[^cite_note-Malard_County-7] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 281,027 people in 86,830 households.[^cite_note-2016_Tehran_Province-4]

## Climate

## Transportation

See also: [Malard and Suburbs Bus Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malard_and_Suburbs_Bus_Organization)

The city is served by buses from the municipal-run [Malard and Suburbs Bus Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malard_and_Suburbs_Bus_Organization), connecting the city to [Shahriar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahriar,_Tehran_Province), [Karaj](978698.md), [Qods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qods,_Iran), and [Tehran](57654.md).
