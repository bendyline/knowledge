**Auvere** ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): *Ampfer*) is a village in [Narva-Jõesuu municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narva-J%C3%B5esuu_\(urban_municipality\)), [Ida-Viru County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ida-Viru_County) in northeastern [Estonia](28222445.md).[^cite_note-1] Prior to the 2017 [administrative reform of local governments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_reform_in_Estonia), it was located in [Vaivara Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaivara_Parish).
