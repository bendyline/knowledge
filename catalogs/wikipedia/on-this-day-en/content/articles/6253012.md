# Beni Halba tribe

Baggara belt

Baggara belt.

The **Beni Halba** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بني هلبا) is an [Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab) group located in the western [Sudanese](27421.md) region of [Darfur](605837.md). The Beni Halba is one of the major Darfuri [Baggara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baggara) groups, along with the [Habbaniya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habbaniya_people), [Rizeigat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rizeigat) and [Ta’isha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ta%E2%80%99isha), and was granted a large *[hakura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakura)* (land grant) in southern Darfur by the [sultans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan) of independent Dar Fur.[^cite_note-1] During the 1980s, recurring [drought](37335.md) in [Chad](5488.md) prompted several clans of Beni Halba to migrate eastwards and join their kinspeople between [Geneina](1501534.md) and [Kebkabiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kebkabiya?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Kutum](6611948.md).[^cite_note-2] In the late 1980s, the ideology then sweeping through the region combining [Arab supremacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_supremacy) and [Islamic extremism](8734632.md) was taken up by many Beni Halba. Under [Nazir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasir_\(name\)) al-Hadi [Issa Debaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Issa_Debaker?action=edit\&redlink=1), the Beni Halba were actively involved in attacks upon the [Fur people starting in 1987 through 1989](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987-1989_Fur-Arab_conflict). In this period a new militia called the [Janjaweed](683270.md), partially drawing upon the Beni Halba, was first formed.[^cite_note-3] In 1991 the [Sudan People's Liberation Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_People's_Liberation_Army), then fighting the [Second Sudanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sudanese_Civil_War) in the south of the country, sent a force under [Daud Bolad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daud_Bolad) to expand the conflict into Darfur. However, Bolad's force was annihilated by a combination of the army and Beni Halba *fursan* ("horsemen"), who then carried out reprisal attacks against Fur civilians.[^cite_note-4] As a result, the Beni Halba district town of Idd al Ghanam ("Well of Goats") was renamed Idd al Fursan ("Well of Horseman").[^cite_note-5]

The tribe has a major existence in both Sudan and Tchad. Within the tribe, Bani Helba includes over 10 major clans. The Main Clans are Dar Nimir, Wolad Gumaan, Zanateet, Wolad Ghanim, Ghieth, Wolad Ali, Hathalil, Bani Monzoor, Isharia, Mosawiyaih, Bani Labid, Wolad Furkha, Hazazra, Gimailat, Mistinan and Alwanaih

As the [Darfur conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darfur_conflict) erupted in the beginning of the 2000s, the Sudanese government attempted to convince al-Hadi Issa Debaker to merge his Beni Halba with the Janjaweed. Debaker refused, stating that he would defend himself if attacked on Beni Halba land.[^cite_note-6] Regardless, members of the Beni Halba and other Baggara groups continue to be recruited into the Janjaweed.[^cite_note-7]
