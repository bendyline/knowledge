**Tenkasi district** (Tamil: [\[t̪eŋɡaːsi\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Tamil)) is one of the 38 districts of [Tamil Nadu, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_Nadu,_India), separated from [Tirunelveli district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirunelveli_district) on 22 November 2019. The Government of Tamil Nadu announced its creation on 18 July 2019. The district headquarters is at [Tenkasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenkasi).[^cite_note-2]

## Geography

The district shares borders with [Tirunelveli district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirunelveli_district) in the south, [Virudhunagar district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virudhunagar_district) in the north, [Thoothukudi district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thoothukudi_district) in the east and [Kollam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kollam_district) and [Pathanamthitta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pathanamthitta_district) districts of [Kerala](4349459.md) in the west. The western part of the district runs along the [Western Ghats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Ghats), while the east is mainly flat plains.

Tenkasi was formed from 8 talukas: [Sivagiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sivagiri_taluk), [Sankarankovil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sankarankoil_taluk), [Veerakeralamputhur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veerakeralamputhur_taluk), [Alangulam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alangulam_taluk), [Tenkasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenkasi_taluk) and [Shenkottai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenkottai_taluk), [Kadayanallur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadayanallur_taluk), [Thiruvenkatam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvenkatam).

At the time of the 2011 census, Tenkasi district had a population of 1,420,064. Tenkasi district has 697,635 males and 743,160 females with a sex ratio of 1065 females per 1000 males. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes made up 292,900 (20.33%) and 3,656 (0.25%) of the population respectively. 613,758 (42.60%) of the population lived in urban areas.[^cite_note-districtcensus-8]

Languages of Tenkasi district (2011)

[^cite_note-language-9]

1. [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language) (98.8%)

2. [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language) (0.92%)

3. Others (0.31%)

At the time of the 2011 census, 98.77% of the population spoke [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language) and 0.92% [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language) as their first language.[^cite_note-language-9]
