The **Statistical Center of Iran** (abbr.  **SCI**; [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): مرکز آمار ایران) is the main organization for statistics in [Iran](14653.md). It is administered and funded by the [Government of Iran](50913538.md).

SCI was established in 1965 by legislation from the National Consultative Assembly.&#x20;

This center today plays a key role in the country's economic, social, and developmental policies. By providing transparent and accurate statistics, it is considered the main reference for national and regional strategic planning.[^cite_note-1]

## National population censuses

SCI is the national body in charge of planning, performing and publishing national statistics on population and housing. The first nation-wide status belongs to 1955. Since then, SCI has run eight national statistics programs, including:

- 1st National Population and Housing Census, 1956

- 2nd National Population and Housing Census, 1966

- 3rd National Population and Housing Census, 1976

- 4th National Population and Housing Census, 1986

- 4th-2 National Population and Housing Census, 1991

- 5th National Population and Housing Census, 1996

- 6th National Population and Housing Census, 2006

- 7th National Population and Housing Census, 2011

- 8th National Population and Housing Census, 2016

## International cooperation

During 2004-2007, the Statistical Center of Iran was selected as a member of the [UN Statistical Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNCSGN). It was also selected as a member in the Governing Council of Statistical Institution for Asia and the Pacific (SIAP) affiliated to ESCAP during 2005-2010.[^cite_note-2]
