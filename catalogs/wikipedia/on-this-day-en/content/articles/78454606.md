The **2026 CFL national draft** was a [selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CFL_Draft) of [national players](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Football_League#Player_designations) by [Canadian Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Football_League) teams that took place on April 28, 2026.[^cite_note-JanRank-1] Seventy-four players were chosen from among eligible players from [Canadian Universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U_Sports) across the country, as well as Canadian players playing in the [NCAA](60706.md) or [NAIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Association_of_Intercollegiate_Athletics).[^cite_note-Eight-2][^cite_note-2022_CBA-3]

## Format

The draft featured eight rounds of selections, with each team receiving one selection per round in reverse standings order from the [2025 CFL season](72199010.md). As per the 2022 collective bargaining agreement, the two teams that had [National players](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Football_League#Player_designations) featured in the highest percentage of snaps played in the 2025 CFL season (the [Edmonton Elks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmonton_Elks) and [Winnipeg Blue Bombers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnipeg_Blue_Bombers)) were each awarded an additional second-round pick.[^cite_note-2022_CBA-3][^cite_note-4] The first two rounds were broadcast on [TSN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sports_Network) and streamed on TSN+, while rounds 3 through 8 were streamed solely on TSN+.[^cite_note-5]

## Trades

In the explanations below, **(D)** denotes trades that took place during the draft, while **(PD)** indicates trades completed pre-draft.

### Round two

- **Calgary → BC (PD).&#x20;**[Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calgary_Stampeders) traded a second-round pick in this year's draft and the ninth overall pick and the 29th overall pick in the [2025 CFL draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_CFL_draft) to [BC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BC_Lions) in exchange for [Vernon Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vernon_Adams), a third-round pick in this year's draft, and the 32nd overall pick in the 2025 CFL draft.

  [\[trade 1\]](#cite_note-Adams-9)

- **BC → Toronto (PD).&#x20;**&#x42;C traded a second-round pick in this year's draft and [Ryder Varga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryder_Varga) to [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_Argonauts) in exchange for [Dejon Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dejon_Allen).

  [\[trade 2\]](#cite_note-Allen-Varga-10)

- **Ottawa ←→ Winnipeg (D).&#x20;**[Ottawa](16627032.md) traded the 10th overall pick in this year's draft to [Winnipeg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnipeg_Blue_Bombers) in exchange for the 13th overall pick in this year's draft and a second-round pick in the [2027 CFL draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_CFL_draft).
