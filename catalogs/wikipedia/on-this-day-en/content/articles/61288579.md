**Division 60, [Somali National Army](3972951.md)** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Qeybta 60aad*) is a [division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_\(military\)) of the [Somali Armed Forces](27366.md). It has been active in two periods from the 1970s to about 1990 (though being upgraded in status in the process to the level of a corps), fighting in the [Ogaden War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogaden_War) against Ethiopia, and from 2013 to the present. [Abdullahi Yusuf Irro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullahi_Yusuf_Irro) once commanded the 60th Division.

Under the leadership of General [Abdullah Mohamed Fadil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_Mohamed_Fadil), [Abdullahi Ahmed Irro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullahi_Ahmed_Irro) and other senior Somali military officials formulated a plan of attack for what was to become the [Ogaden War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogaden_War) in Ethiopia.[^cite_note-Ahmed-1] At the start of the offensive, the SNA consisted of 35,000 soldiers,[^cite_note-EthSom638-2] and was vastly outnumbered by the Ethiopian forces. Somali troops seized the Godey Front on 24 July 1977, after Division 60 defeated the Ethiopian [4th Infantry Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4th_Infantry_Division_\(Ethiopia\)?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-Urban-3] Godey's capture allowed the Somali side to consolidate its hold on the Ogaden, concentrate its forces, and advance further to other regions of Ethiopia.[^cite_note-Tareke2-4] The invasion ended abruptly with the Soviet Union's sudden shift of support to Ethiopia, followed by almost the entire [communist bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World) siding with the latter. The Soviets halted supplies to Barre's regime and instead increased aid, weapons, and training distribution to Ethiopia's newly communist [Derg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derg) regime. By 1978, the Somali forces were pushed out of most of the Ogaden, although it would take nearly three more years for the Ethiopian Army to gain complete control of Godey.[^cite_note-Tareke2-4]

Division 60 then dissolved amid the first phase of the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md). It was reestablished on 1 July 2013.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTERobinson2019216-5] From that time, it supervised [Brigade 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigade_7?action=edit\&redlink=1) centered on [Baidoa](1648752.md); [Brigade 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigade_8?action=edit\&redlink=1) at [Huddur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huddur) in [Bakool](185137.md); and [Brigade 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigade_9?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Gedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gedo), with divisional headquarters in [Baidoa](1648752.md). A [British Army](4887.md) detachment is assisting the division;[^cite_note-6] and the UK [Foreign and Commonwealth Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_and_Commonwealth_Office) have been paying stipends to Division 60 personnel for several years.

The 60th is currently commanded by General Mohamed Sheikh Abdullahi Irro.[^cite_note-7]
