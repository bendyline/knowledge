A **paramilitary** is an armed, militarized-like force or unit that functions and is organized in a manner analogous to a military force, but does not have professional or legitimate status.[^cite_note-Reference-OED-paramilitary-1] The [Oxford English Dictionary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary) traces the use of the term "paramilitary" as far back as 1934.[^cite_note-2] It has been used by many different [political organizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_organizations) from around the world throughout history.[^cite_note-3] Paramilitaries have widely been synonymous with [violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violence), [political repression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_repression), [ethnic cleansing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_cleansing), [genocide](12441.md) and [crimes against humanity](61099.md).[^cite_note-4] Paramilitaries may use combat-capable kit/equipment (such as [internal security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_security_vehicle)/[SWAT vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SWAT_vehicle)), or even actual military equipment (such as [armored personnel carriers](19278686.md); usually [military surplus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_surplus) resources) that are compatible with their purpose, often combining them with skills from other relevant fields such as [law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement), [coast guard](146790.md), or [search and rescue](172599.md). A paramilitary may fall under the command of a [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military), train alongside them, or have permission to use their resources, despite not actually being part of them.[^cite_note-BohmeltClayton2018-5]

## Legality

Under the [law of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_war), a state may incorporate a paramilitary organization or armed agency (such as a [law enforcement agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_agency) or a private volunteer [militia](20622.md)) into its [combatant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combatant) armed forces. Some countries' constitutions [prohibit paramilitary organizations outside government use](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_that_prohibit_paramilitary_organizations_outside_government_armed_forces).

Depending on the definition adopted, "paramilitaries" may include:

### Military organizations

- [Private military companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_military_companies), [mercenaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercenaries), and [colonial troops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonial_troops).

- [Irregular military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irregular_military), such as [auxiliaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auxiliaries), [private armies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_armies), and [militias](20622.md).

- State-parallel armed groups such as the Sudanese [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) and the Iraqi [Popular Mobilization Forces](45671374.md)

  [^cite_note-6]

### Law enforcement agencies

- Semi-militarized law enforcement units within civilian [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police), such as [police tactical units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_tactical_unit), [SWAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SWAT), [emergency service units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_service_unit), and [incident response teams](10000895.md)

- [Gendarmeries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie), such as the French [National Gendarmerie](6330819.md), Chinese [People's Armed Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Armed_Police), Dutch [Royal Marechaussee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Marechaussee), Egyptian [Central Security Forces](4317752.md), European [EUROGENDFOR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Gendarmerie_Force), Turkic [TAKM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TAKM), and Chilean [Carabineros de Chile](5446963.md)

- [Border guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_guard), such as the [U.S. Border Patrol](567453.md), [Australian Border Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Border_Force), Indian [Border Security Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Security_Force), [Bangladeshi](28958994.md) [Border Guard Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Guard_Bangladesh), and Turkish [village guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_guard_system)

- [Security forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_forces) of ambiguous military status, such as [internal troops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_troops), [railroad guard corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Railroad_Guards_\(disambiguation\)), and [railway troops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Railway_troops)

- Branches of [government agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_agency) such as [intelligence agencies](81014.md) and [military intelligence](146678.md) tasked with law enforcement, tactical support, or security operations, such as the [Central Intelligence Agency](5183633.md)'s [Special Activities Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Activities_Center) and [Global Response Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterterrorism_Mission_Center), or the [U.S. Department of Energy](62866.md)'s [Federal Protective Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Protective_Forces)

### Civil defense

- [Lithuanian Riflemen's Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Riflemen's_Union)

- [Chinese Militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militia_\(China\))

- [SSB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SSB_\(militia\))

### Political parties

- [Party militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_militia)

- Armed, semi-militarized wings of [political parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) and similar political organizations, such as the German [Schutzstaffel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schutzstaffel) and Italian [Blackshirts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackshirts).
