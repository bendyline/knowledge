[Myanmar](19457.md) and [Timor-Leste](19159399.md) established [diplomatic relations](28165911.md) on 26 September 2006.[^cite_note-1]

## History

### 2021

After the [2021 Myanmar coup d'etat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Myanmar_coup_d'etat) and during the subsequent [civil war in Myanmar](69807451.md), relations have significantly deteriorated between the two governments.&#x20;

### 2023

In August 2023, the [State Administration Council](66589972.md) junta expelled the East Timorese ambassador in retaliation for the Timor-Leste government meeting with the [National Unity Government of Myanmar](67413816.md) (NUG).[^cite_note-2] In December 2023, Timor-Leste President [José Ramos-Horta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Ramos-Horta) called for [Tatmadaw](20394.md) soldiers to defect and join the resistance, making Timor-Leste the only government to have openly expressed sympathies to anti-junta forces in Myanmar. Officials of the State Administration Council responded with outrage, running anti-Timor-Leste articles in state media referring to Ramos-Horta as a "thoughtless mouthpiece of the NUG". Additionally, "junta-backed nationalists" held a protest in [Yangon](57404.md) against Ramos-Horta.[^cite_note-3]

### 2025

On 2 July 2025, the State Administration Council of Myanmar has officially notified Malaysia, the 2025 ASEAN chair, of its opposition to [Timor-Leste joining the bloc](20805336.md) in October 2025, citing internal interference.[^cite_note-4] Timor-Leste has reportedly supported the opposing democratic forces in the [onging civil war](69807451.md).[^cite_note-5]

### 2026

After the Chin Human Rights Organisation met Ramos-Horta in Timor-Leste on 14 January 2026, the [State Security and Peace Commission](80587873.md) junta accused the CHRO of violating the [ASEAN Charter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASEAN_Charter). The CHRO intends to sue the junta for war crimes under Timor-Leste's legal jurisdiction.[^cite_note-6] The [Foreign Ministry of Myanmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Myanmar\)) stated that the meeting constitutes as "blatant interference" and filed a criminal complaint against Timor-Leste.[^cite_note-7] Later, on 16 February, Myanmar ordered the expulsion of the Timor-Leste's top representative after the CHRO opened its case against the junta war crimes and crimes against humanity.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]
