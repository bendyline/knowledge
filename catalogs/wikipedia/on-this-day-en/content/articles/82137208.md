The **2026 Leagues Cup** was the sixth edition of the [Leagues Cup](60906407.md), an international club [soccer](10568.md) tournament contested by [Major League Soccer](71802.md) (MLS) and [Liga MX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liga_MX) clubs in [North America](21139.md). It was held from August 4 to September 6, featuring 36 teams. Like the 2025 edition, only the top 18 MLS teams from the [2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Major_League_Soccer_season) qualified for the tournament. All 18 [Liga MX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liga_MX) clubs participated.

[Seattle Sounders FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC) were the defending champions, having won their first Leagues Cup title by defeating [Inter Miami CF](41853326.md) 3–0 in the [2025 final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Leagues_Cup_final). This was the first edition of the tournament to feature games hosted in Mexico, with four matches in Phase One being played in the country.[^cite_note-ESPN-3]

[Toluca](1361541.md) won their first Leagues Cup title by defeating [Monterrey](547412.md) 2–0 in [the final](82980174.md).[^cite_note-4]

## Format

The tournament consisted of a group stage, known as "Phase One", and a knockout phase. During phase one, teams played three games against teams in the other league (i.e., all games were MLS vs Liga MX).[^cite_note-ESPN-3] The top four teams from each league advanced to the knockout stage, which consisted of quarterfinals, semifinals and a final. Like previous editions of the [Leagues Cup](60906407.md), the top three clubs qualified for the [CONCACAF Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF_Champions_Cup).[^cite_note-ESPN-3]

&#x20;The 2026 tournament followed the same format as the 2025 tournament. In Phase One, teams earned three points for a win in regulation, two points for a win in a penalty shoot-out, or one point for a loss in a penalty shoot-out.[^cite_note-MLS-FormatJul-5] In the event two or more teams ended the group stage tied on points, these tiebreakers in order were used for ranking:[^cite_note-6]

> 1. Greater goal difference.
>
> 2. Greater number of wins in regular time.
>
> 3. Greater number of goals scored.
>
> 4. Fewer number of goals conceded.
>
> 5. Fewer points in the team’s Fair Play Table.
>
> 6. A draw organized by the Organizing Committee.

## Teams

Thirty-six teams participated in the Leagues Cup, including all 18 Liga MX teams and 18 out of 30 MLS teams.[^cite_note-ESPN-3] The 18 MLS teams qualified for Leagues Cup by reaching the [2025 MLS Cup playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_MLS_Cup_playoffs).[^cite_note-teams-7]

### Draw

To determine matchups, all teams were placed into a combined ranking that used the [2025 Supporters' Shield table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Major_League_Soccer_season#Overall_table) for MLS teams and the most recent 34 matches in the [2025 Clausura and Apertura seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Liga_MX_season#2025_aggregate_table) for Liga MX teams. These teams were then divided into an East region and a West region based on geographical and performance criteria. Within each region, teams were assigned to tiers based on the combined ranking, with the restriction that each tier included three teams from each league.
