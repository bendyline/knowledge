# TYR Pro Swim Series

**TYR Pro Swim Series** is an annual [swimming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_\(sport\)) competition series held in the [United States](3434750.md), attracting top swimmers from around the world.[^cite_note-Sponsor-1]

## History

The series was created as part of USA Swimming's domestic calendar and has become one of the most prestigious platforms for preparation for national and international competitions. In 2018, [TYR Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TYR_Sport) became the title sponsor of the series, after which the competition was officially named the TYR Pro Swim Series.[^cite_note-Sponsor-1]

Traditionally, the TYR Pro Swim Series consists of a set of 3.5-day meets, each attracting 400 to 600 of the world's top swimmers, including members of the USA Swimming National Team, Junior National Team, and 50 of the world's best swimmers.[^cite_note-2]

## Organizer

The founder and main organizer of the series is USA Swimming, the national governing body for [swimming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_\(sport\)) in the United States. Since 2018, the title sponsor and partner of the series has been [TYR Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TYR_Sport), Inc., one of the global leaders in swimming and [triathlon](31524.md) gear.[^cite_note-Sponsor-1]

## Key events

Olympic champions, world champions, and world record holders regularly participate in the competition.[^cite_note-Tickets-3]

The series consists of several stages held in different cities across the USA (e.g., Austin, Atlanta, Mesa, Indianapolis, Greensboro, California, Ohio).[^cite_note-Sponsor-1]

The TYR Pro Swim Series serves as one of the main platforms for selection and preparation for the [Olympic Games](22576.md) and [World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Aquatics_Championships).

In 2020, [Katie Ledecky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Ledecky) set a record for the most victories among women in the series.[^cite_note-Tickets-3]

## Competition program

The TYR Pro Swim Series program includes almost all Olympic pool events (50m) for men and women. An example schedule for a stage (2025, [Fort Lauderdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Lauderdale)):

- 50m, 100m, 200m, 400m, 800m, 1500m [freestyle](23272431.md) (men and women)

- 50m, 100m, 200m [backstroke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backstroke), [breaststroke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaststroke), [butterfly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butterfly_stroke)

- 200m, 400m [individual medley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individual_medley)

All events are held with preliminaries and finals.

The schedule also provides for "swim-offs" to determine finalists.[^cite_note-4]
