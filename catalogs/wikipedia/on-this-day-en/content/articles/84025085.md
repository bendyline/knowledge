**Mount Ololokwe**, also known as **Ol Donyo Sabache**, is a mountain in [Samburu County](413704.md), northern Kenya. Rising to approximately 2,000 metres (6,600 ft) above sea level, it is a prominent isolated, flat-topped mountain above the surrounding semi-arid plains.[^cite_note-NatureKenya-1] The mountain is considered sacred by the [Samburu people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samburu_people), who have used it as a shrine for prayers and rituals.[^cite_note-NatureKenya-1] It is also a destination for hiking and trekking.[^cite_note-NatureKenya-1]

Although widely referred to as Mount Ololokwe, the mountain is properly known as Ol Donyo Sabache, while Ololokwe is a name referring to the surrounding area that has also come to be widely applied to the mountain itself.[^cite_note-2] *Ololokwe* means "wide head" in the [Samburu language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samburu_language), a reference to the mountain's broad, rounded upper profile.[^cite_note-3] *Ol Donyo* is a generic reference to a mountain name, and *Sabache* refers to the clasp of a traditional Samburu woman's necklace, with the mountain and two smaller neighboring peaks forming a horseshoe-shaped arrangement around the intervening valley.[^cite_note-4]

On 19 August 2026, a [Eurocopter EC130 B4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocopter_EC130) operated by Lady Lori Helicopters [crashed in the foothills of Mount Ololokwe](84023926.md) while carrying tourists on a charter flight. All six passengers and the Kenyan pilot were killed.[^cite_note-5]
