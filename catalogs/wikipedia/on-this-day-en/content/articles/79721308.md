Legislative elections were held in [New Caledonia](21342.md) on 28 June 2026 to elect members of the [Congress of New Caledonia](4504443.md).[^cite_note-2] The elections had previously been scheduled to be held on 15 December 2024 but were postponed by a year after [unrest](76907223.md) and then postponed again following the [Bougival Accord](80433199.md).[^cite_note-\:1-3]

While the [autonomist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomism) and [loyalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyalism) bloc, led by [Sonia Backès](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonia_Back%C3%A8s) and [The Loyalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Loyalists_\(New_Caledonia\)), received the most votes, the [separatist](286527.md) bloc, led by [Pierre-Chanel Tutugoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre-Chanel_Tutugoro?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the alliance of the [Caledonian Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caledonian_Union) and the [Kanak and Socialist National Liberation Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanak_and_Socialist_National_Liberation_Front) (FLNKS), retained the most seats. Neutral parties retained the [balance of power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balance_of_power_\(parliament\)). [Kingmaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingmaker) candidate [Milakulo Tukumuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milakulo_Tukumuli?action=edit\&redlink=1) of the [centrist](39619035.md) [Oceanian Awakening](72975858.md) was elected as the new [President](29887008.md). He is the first person of [Wallisian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallisians) descent to hold the office.[^cite_note-4]

## Background

See also: [2021 New Caledonian independence referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_New_Caledonian_independence_referendum) and [2024 New Caledonia unrest](76907223.md)

An [independence referendum](7704646.md) was held in [New Caledonia](21342.md), a French territory in the [South Pacific](23070.md), on 12 December 2021. The vote was the third and final one to be held under the terms of the [Nouméa Accord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noum%C3%A9a_Accord), following votes in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_New_Caledonian_independence_referendum) and [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_New_Caledonian_independence_referendum). As a result of the referendum taking place amid a boycott from the independence parties, the results were extremely lopsided in favor of *[status quo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Status_quo)*: voters overwhelmingly rejected independence, with 96% voting against independence and 4% in favour (compared to a 57% and 53% against independence in 2018 and 2020).[^cite_note-\:22-5] French President [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md) celebrated the results of the referendum, adding that France "is more beautiful because New Caledonia has decided to stay part of it."[^cite_note-\:22-5]

While the Kanak independence movement continues to demand full self-determination, many French officials see extending voting rights as essential for democratic fairness in the territory.[^cite_note-\:3-6] The adoption of the voter list bill in May 2024 by the [French parliament](1009776.md) which would allow French citizens residing in New Caledonia for at least ten years to take part in local elections,[^cite_note-7] led to [mass riots](76907223.md) by pro-independence groups that turned violent, prompting French president [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md) to declare a state of emergency and fly thousands of police and army reinforcements to the faraway territory.[^cite_note-\:1-3] In October 2024, then-French Prime Minister [Michel Barnier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Barnier) scrapped the bill, citing the need to restore calm and telling the National Assembly that "avoiding further unrest" was a priority.[^cite_note-\:3-6]

On 16 January 2025, [Alcide Ponga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcide_Ponga) was sworn in as [President of the Government of New Caledonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Government_of_New_Caledonia), forming a new government following the collapse of the pro-independence government led by [Louis Mapou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Mapou). His leadership has been described as bringing stability following the [2024 unrest](76907223.md) and a continuation for [New Caledonia](21342.md) to remain within the [French Republic](5843419.md).[^cite_note-\:0-8]

On 12 July 2025, the Ministry of the Overseas, and both pro and anti-independence signed the [Bougival Accord](80433199.md), delaying the elections until 2026.

## Electoral system

Voters elect 76 members to the assemblies of the [three provinces of New Caledonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_New_Caledonia) (40 for [South Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Province,_New_Caledonia), 22 for [North Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Province,_New_Caledonia), and 14 for the [Loyalty Islands Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyalty_Islands_Province)). In turn, 54 also become members of the [Congress of New Caledonia](4504443.md) (32 from South Province, 15 from North Province, and 7 from Loyalty Islands Province).

The elections are held under [party-list proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party-list_proportional_representation) using a 5% electoral threshold and the [highest averages method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highest_averages_method) to allocate seats. The elections used a restricted roll, which restricts voting depending on how long someone has lived in New Caledonia.[^cite_note-9] Only persons residing in New Caledonia since at least 8 November 1998 (and their children, once they reach the age of majority) are able to vote.

## Aftermath

On 9 July 2026, a governance agreement was reached between the lists [Les Loyalistes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Loyalistes) – [The Rally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rally_\(New_Caledonia\)) and [Oceanian Awakening](72975858.md) : [Virginie Ruffenach](72993656.md) ([R-LR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rally_\(New_Caledonia\))) was to become president of the [Congress](4504443.md) and [Milakulo Tukumuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milakulo_Tukumuli?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([EO](72975858.md)) was to become [President of the Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Government_of_New_Caledonia), the first person of [Wallisian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallisians) descent to be elected in that office.[^cite_note-12] He was congratulated by [Australia](4689264.md)'s [Foreign Affairs Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Foreign_Affairs_\(Australia\)) [Penny Wong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penny_Wong).[^cite_note-13] He unveiled his new [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Milakulo_Tukumuli?action=edit\&redlink=1) on 6 August 2026.[^cite_note-14]
