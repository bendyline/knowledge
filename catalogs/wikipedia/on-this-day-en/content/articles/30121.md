[Demographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demography) features of the [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_\(human_biology\)) of [Tanzania](30118.md) include [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density), [ethnicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnicity), education level, health of the populace, economic status, religious affiliations, and other aspects of the population.

The population distribution in Tanzania is extremely uneven. Most people live on the northern border or the eastern coast, with much of the remainder of the country being sparsely populated.[^cite_note-frame-1]<sup>: 1252 </sup> Density varies from 12 per square kilometre (31/sq mi) in the [Katavi Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katavi_Region) to 3,133 per square kilometre (8,110/sq mi) in [Dar es Salaam](8500.md).[^cite_note-2012_census-2]<sup>: 6 </sup> Approximately 70 percent of the population is rural, although this percentage has been declining since at least 1967.[^cite_note-3] [Dar es Salaam](8500.md) is the *de facto* capital and largest city. [Dodoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dodoma), located in the centre of Tanzania, is the *de jure* capital, although action to move government buildings to Dodoma has stalled.

The population consists of [about 125 ethnic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ethnic_groups_in_Tanzania).[^cite_note-lev-4] The [Sukuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukuma_people), [Nyamwezi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyamwezi_people), [Chagga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaga_people), and [Haya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haya_people) peoples have more than 1 million members each.[^cite_note-Otiso-5]<sup>: 4 </sup>

Over 100 [languages are spoken in Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_Tanzania), making it the most linguistically diverse country in East Africa.[^cite_note-sim-6] Among the languages spoken in Tanzania are all four of Africa's language families: [Bantu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bantu_languages), [Cushitic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cushitic_languages), [Nilotic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilotic_languages), and [Khoisan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khoisan_languages).[^cite_note-sim-6] [Swahili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swahili) and English are Tanzania's official languages.[^cite_note-sim-6] Swahili belongs to the Bantu branch of the [Niger-Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger%E2%80%93Congo_languages) family.[^cite_note-Ethnswh-7] The [Sandawe people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandawe_people) speak a language that may be related to the [Khoe languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khoe_languages) of Botswana and Namibia, while the language of the [Hadzabe people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadza_people), although it has similar [click consonants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Click_consonant), is arguably a [language isolate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_isolate).[^cite_note-8] The language of the [Iraqw people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqw_people) is Cushitic.[^cite_note-9] Other languages are [Indian languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_India) and [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language) (spoken by Goans and Mozambicans).

Non-Africans residing on the mainland and Zanzibar account for 1 percent of the total population. The Asian community including [Hindus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism), [Sikhs](27964.md), [Shi'a](26961.md) and [Sunni Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam), [Parsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsis), and [Goans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goans), exceed 60,000. An estimated 70,000 [Arabs](2185.md) and 20,000 [Europeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Africans_of_European_ancestry) (90 percent of which are from the [British diaspora](1472509.md)) reside in Tanzania. Over 100,000 people living in Tanzania are of Asian or European ancestry.[^cite_note-10]

Based on 1999–2003 data, over 74,000 Tanzanian-born people were living in [Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development](33853117.md) countries, with 32,630 [residing in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanzanians_in_the_United_Kingdom); 19,960 in [Canada](5042916.md); 12,225 in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanzanian_Americans); 1,714 in [Australia](4689264.md); 1,180 in the [Netherlands](21148.md); and 1,012 in [Sweden](5058739.md).[^cite_note-OECD-11]

## Population



Demographics of Tanzania, Data of [Our World in Data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_World_in_Data), year 2022; Number of inhabitants in millions.

According to the 2012 census, the total population was 44,928,923 compared to 12,313,469 in 1967,[^cite_note-2012_census-2]<sup>: 1 </sup> resulting in an annual growth rate of 2.9 percent. The under 15 age group represented 44.1 percent of the population, with 35.5 percent being in the 15–35 age group, 52.2 percent being in the 15–64 age group, and 3.8 percent being older than 64.[^cite_note-12]

According to the 2012 revision of the [World Population Prospects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Population_Prospects), children below the age of 15 constituted 44.8 percent of the total population, with 52.0 percent aged 15–64 and 3.1 percent aged 65 or older.[^cite_note-WPP_2012-13]

## Religion

Main article: [Religion in Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion_in_Tanzania)



A Muslim man in [Bagamoyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagamoyo).

Most Tanzanians are nowadays Christians and Muslims. The numerical relationship between followers of the two religions is regarded as politically sensitive and questions about religious affiliation have not been included in census questionnaires since 1967.

For many years estimates have been repeated that about a third of the population each follows Islam, Christianity and traditional religions.[^cite_note-25] As there is likely no longer such a large percentage of traditional religionists,[^cite_note-26] a range of competing estimates has been published giving one side or the other a large share or trying to show equal shares.

Estimates from the Pew Report Islam and Christianity (2010) were 60% Christian and 36% Muslim.[^cite_note-Pew_Africa-27]

The remainder of the population are [Hindus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindus), [Buddhists](3267529.md), [animists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animism), and unaffiliated. Most Christians are [Roman Catholic](606848.md), [Lutheran](23371382.md), Anglican or Pentecostal, though a number of other churches, as [Seventh-Day Adventist](28632.md), and Eastern Orthodox Christians are also represented in the country. Most Tanzanian Muslims are Sunni Muslims, though there are also populations of [Ibadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibadism), [Shia](26961.md), [Ahamadiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadiyya), Bohora. Muslims are concentrated in coastal areas and in mainland areas along former caravan trade routes.
