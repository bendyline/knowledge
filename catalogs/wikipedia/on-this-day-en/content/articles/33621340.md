The **Philippine Tennis Association** (PhilTA) is the national [governing body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sport_governing_body) for [tennis](29773.md) in the [Philippines](23440.md). It was founded in 1920 as the Philippine Lawn Tennis Association and joined the [International Lawn Tennis Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Lawn_Tennis_Federation) in 1936.[^cite_note-1]

## History

Philta was led by Salvador Andrada from 1986 to 2007.[^cite_note-rappler-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] It was during Andrada's administration that the Andrada Cup was organized from 1989 which lasted until 2019.[^cite_note-rappler-2]

Under Andrada's presidency the [Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines_Davis_Cup_team) upset [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Davis_Cup_team) 3–2, in the [1988 Davis Cup Asia/Oceania Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Davis_Cup_Asia/Oceania_Zone). The Philippines also reached the [qualifying round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Davis_Cup_World_Group_qualifying_round) of the [1991 Davis Cup World Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Davis_Cup_World_Group) played against [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_Davis_Cup_team).[^cite_note-rappler-2]

### Leadership crisis

[Edwin Olivarez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edwin_Olivarez) became Philta president but resigned in July 2016 leading to a leadership crisis.[^cite_note-6] Andrada was elected again as Philta president[^cite_note-7] But Randy Villanueva, Olivarez's vice president disputes the result with the [International Tennis Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Tennis_Federation) recognizing Villanueva as acting president.[^cite_note-8]

In February 2017, another election was held with Andrada not vacating the president role despite alleging to promise to step down.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-patron-10] Sponsors also withdrew support from Philta, including [Cebuana Lhuillier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuana_Lhuillier) and [Palawan Pawnshop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palawan_Pawnshop).[^cite_note-patron-10][^cite_note-more-11] Andrara resigned in February 27, 2017.[^cite_note-quits-12][^cite_note-13]

Another contested election was held again in July 2017 where Antonio Cablitas was elected as Philta president. The ITF continues to recognize as Olivarez as president.[^cite_note-14] In January 2018, Olivarez himself was brought in to be part of a committee to draft an amendment to the Philta's bylaws before new elections could be held. Cablitas was also part of the process.[^cite_note-15]

### Suspension

The [International Tennis Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Tennis_Federation) imposed a two-year suspension against PhilTA in late 2020 due to a leadership crisis within the federation failing to tackle its "exclusive membership base".[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] Under the *ad hoc* administration of the [Philippine Olympic Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Olympic_Committee), PhilTA's constitution was revised,[^cite_note-lifts-19] with nationwide representation now mandated in the organization's board.[^cite_note-20] An election of new officials was held in December 2023.[^cite_note-21] The suspension was lifted on January 21, 2024.[^cite_note-lifts-19]

### Post-reinstatement

Philta now led by president [Eric Olivarez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Olivarez) oversaw the Philippines' return to international competition including the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup).[^cite_note-22] In 2026, the Philippines hosted its first [WTA 125 tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_125_tournaments) – the [Philippine Women's Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Women's_Open).[^cite_note-23]

## Tournaments

- [Philippines Open International Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines_Open_International_Championships) (until 1978)

- Andrada Cup (1989–2019) – youth tennis tournament

- [PCA Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PCA_Open) (1982–present) – open tournament hosted by the [Philippine Columbian Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Columbian_Association), sanctioned by Philta

- [Philippine Women's Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Women's_Open) (2026–present)
