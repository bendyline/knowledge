# NBA conference finals

"Bob Cousy Trophy" redirects here. For the college basketball award, see [Bob Cousy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Cousy_Award).

&#x20; The **NBA conference finals** are the [Eastern](1305847.md) and [Western Conference](28141073.md) championship series of the [National Basketball Association](22093.md) (NBA), a professional [basketball](3921.md) league in [North America](21139.md). The NBA was founded in 1946 as the [Basketball Association of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_Association_of_America) (BAA).[^cite_note-1] The NBA adopted its current name at the start of the [1949–50 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1949%E2%80%9350_NBA_season) when the BAA merged with the [National Basketball League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Basketball_League_\(United_States\)) (NBL).[^cite_note-2] The league consists of 30 teams, of which 29 are located in the United States and 1 in Canada. Each team plays 82 games in the regular season.[^cite_note-3] After the regular season, eight teams from each of the league's two conferences qualify for the [playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_playoffs). At the end of the playoffs, the top two teams play each other in the conference finals, to determine the conference champions from each side, who then proceed to play in the [NBA Finals](890793.md). Trophies were given to each conference winner starting in 2001. In 2022, the league started naming a [conference finals most valuable player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_conference_finals_MVP_awards) (MVP) for each conference.[^cite_note-feldman_05122022-4]

## Overview

Initially, the BAA teams were aligned into the [Eastern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Division_\(NBA\)) and [Western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Division_\(NBA\)) divisions. The division finals were first played in [1949](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948%E2%80%9349_BAA_season), the league's third season. The first two seasons used a playoffs format where Eastern and Western division teams would face each other before the BAA Finals. Hence, there were no divisional finals. In the [1949–50 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1949%E2%80%9350_NBA_season), the league realigned itself to three divisions, with the addition of the [Central Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Division_\(NBA\)). However, the arrangement was only used for one season, and the league returned to the two-division format in [1951](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950%E2%80%9351_NBA_season). The two divisions' format remained until [1970](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970%E2%80%9371_NBA_season) when the NBA realigned itself into two conferences with two divisions each, which led to the creation of the conference finals.

The finals were a best-of-three series from 1949 to 1950, a best-of-five series from 1951 to 1956, and a best-of-seven series since 1957. The conference finals are played in a best-of-seven series like the NBA playoffs and Finals. The two series are played in late May each year after the first and second rounds of the playoffs and before the Finals. After the conference finals, winners are presented with a silver trophy, caps, and T-shirts and advance to the NBA Finals.

The [Los Angeles Lakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Lakers) have won the most conference titles with 19. They have also made 24 appearances in the conference finals, more than any other team, which included eight consecutive appearances in the Western Conference finals from 1982 to 1989. The [Boston Celtics](43376.md) have won 11 conference championships, the second most of any team. 23 of the 30 active franchises have won at least one conference title. The [Minnesota Timberwolves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Timberwolves), [Atlanta Hawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Hawks), [Sacramento Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacramento_Kings), [Memphis Grizzlies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis_Grizzlies) and [Los Angeles Clippers](72892.md) have each played in at least one conference finals, but have failed to win their respective conference title. Two other franchises, the [Charlotte Hornets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Hornets) and [New Orleans Pelicans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans_Pelicans), have never appeared in the conference finals.

## Conference trophies

The NBA first awarded conference championship trophies in 2001.[^cite_note-5] In 2022, both were redesigned, and named the **[Bob Cousy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Cousy) Trophy** for the Eastern Conference and the **[Oscar Robertson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Robertson) Trophy** for the Western Conference, in honor of two men who were instrumental in developing and advancing the players' labor union, the [National Basketball Players Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Basketball_Players_Association) (NBPA). The two redesigned trophies each feature a silver basketball with its respective conference finals logo on the underside. The trophies also have a slightly different base for each conference to help distinguish one from the other; the silver basketball on the Eastern Conference trophy sits on three pegs, while the Western Conference trophy has the basketball on intercrossing circular rings. The silver basketball is quartered into four sections, representing the winning team first qualifying for the playoffs and then advancing through the three playoff rounds. In that same year, the NBA began awarding [conference finals MVPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_conference_finals_MVP_awards) to the best-performing player of each conference finals.[^cite_note-NBAconferencetrophy-6]
