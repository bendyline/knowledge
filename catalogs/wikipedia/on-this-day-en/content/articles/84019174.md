**Michele Sensi-Contugi Ycaza**[^cite_note-2] (12 August 1982 – 19 August 2026) was an Italian-Ecuadorian businessman and intelligence official. A friend and advisor to Ecuadorian president [Daniel Noboa](73980059.md), he contributed to Noboa's [2023 election](70348458.md) campaign; upon Noboa's election to the presidency, he appointed Sensi-Contugi as the head of the country's national intelligence agency, made him Noboa's delegate to multiple corporations, including [Petroecuador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroecuador), and added him to the boards of the [Corporación Eléctrica del Ecuador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporaci%C3%B3n_El%C3%A9ctrica_del_Ecuador?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Corporación Nacional de Electricidad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporaci%C3%B3n_Nacional_de_Electricidad?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Flota Petrolera Ecuatoriana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flota_Petrolera_Ecuatoriana?action=edit\&redlink=1). From April to August 2024, Sensi-Contugi worked as the [Minister of Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Government_\(Ecuador\)?action=edit\&redlink=1). He was also the vice president of the [National Democratic Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Democratic_Action_\(Ecuador\)) party.

On 19 August 2026, Sensi-Contugi and his wife, [Stephany Hollihan Vasconez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephany_Hollihan), died in a [helicopter crash in Kenya](84023926.md). In response, Noboa ordered three days of national mourning and a state funeral for Sensi-Contugi.

## Education and career



[Daniel Noboa](73980059.md), [Irene Vélez Torres](71690293.md), and Michele Sensi-Contugi speaking at Radio Sucre, in Guayaquil, in July 2024

Sensi-Contugi attended [Nova Southeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nova_Southeastern_University) in Florida, the United States, where he earned a master's in business administration.[^cite_note-education-1] His degrees were not registered with the SENESCYT agency in Ecuador.[^cite_note-3]

A private man, Sensi-Contugi worked in rubber and plastic-related businesses in Guayaquil and was a manager at Senco S.A.;[^cite_note-\:6-4][^cite_note-5] he also owned multiple companies.[^cite_note-\:6-4] He was a close friend and advisor of President [Daniel Noboa](73980059.md); the [National Electoral Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Electoral_Council_\(Ecuador\)) reported that he donated \$25,000 to Noboa's [2023 election campaign](70348458.md).[^cite_note-\:5-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-\:6-4] After Noboa's election to the presidency, he appointed Sensi-Contugi as general director of the national intelligence agency (*Centro de Inteligencia Estratégica*, CIES), replacing [Fausto Cobo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fausto_Cobo?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-\:0-9] Over the next several months, Noboa also appointed Sensi-Contugi as his delegate to the [Astilleros Navales Ecuatorianos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astilleros_Navales_Ecuatorianos?action=edit\&redlink=1) (Ecuadorian Naval Shipyards), Santa Bárbara, and [Petroecuador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroecuador), and made him a permanent representative on the boards of the [Ecuadorian Electric Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_Electric_Corporation?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Corporación Nacional de Electricidad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporaci%C3%B3n_Nacional_de_Electricidad?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Flota Petrolera Ecuatoriana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flota_Petrolera_Ecuatoriana?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:5-6][^cite_note-\:3-10][^cite_note-\:1-11] In April, Noboa appointed him as the country's [Minister of Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Government_\(Ecuador\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), succeeding [Mónica Palencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B3nica_Palencia).[^cite_note-\:3-10][^cite_note-\:1-11] The next month, he also became the vice president of the Noboa-associated [National Democratic Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Democratic_Action_\(Ecuador\)) party.[^cite_note-12]

He left the Minister of Government position in August 2024 and was succeeded by [Arturo Félix Wong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arturo_F%C3%A9lix_Wong?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:3-10][^cite_note-\:1-11]

In February 2025, Sensi-Contugi was called before a private hearing before the [National Assembly](29017747.md) over allegations he and the intelligence agency had used the [Pegasus](51424588.md) spyware against opposition politicians, journalists, and activists.[^cite_note-\:6-4][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] The following June, Noboa eliminated the CIES and replaced it with a new intelligence agency, the *Centro Nacional de Inteligencia* (National Intelligence Center, CNI);[^cite_note-15] Sensi-Contugi remained director of the organization.[^cite_note-16] Former president [Rafael Correa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Correa) was critical of Sensi-Contugi's role in the Ecuadorean government, referring to him as "the evil mastermind behind Noboa's government" and accusing him of spying and organizing [troll farms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troll_farms).[^cite_note-17]

Upon his death, he was succeeded as head of the CNI by [Luis Carlos Ávila Chávez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Carlos_%C3%81vila_Ch%C3%A1vez?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-18]

## Personal life and death

Michele Sensi-Contugi Ycaza was born on 12 August 1982, in [Desio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desio), Italy, to a family historically from [Volterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volterra);[^cite_note-19] Sensi-Contugi's father was Italian and his mother was Ecuadorian.[^cite_note-\:1-11] He married [Stephany Hollihan Vasconez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephany_Hollihan), a fashion designer, on 23 May 2008;[^cite_note-\:1-11] the couple had two children who were both minors at the time of their parents' deaths.[^cite_note-\:4-20] His wife was the godmother of the youngest son of President [Daniel Noboa](73980059.md) and First Lady [Lavinia Valbonesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavinia_Valbonesi) of Ecuador.[^cite_note-\:6-4]

On 19 August 2026, a week after his 44th birthday, Sensi-Contugi, along with his wife, was killed in a [helicopter crash on Mount Ololokwe](84023926.md) in [Samburu County](413704.md), Kenya.[^cite_note-\:0-9][^cite_note-\:2-21] They had been flying to the [Ewaso Ng'iro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ewaso_Ng'iro) river.[^cite_note-\:2-21] In response, Noboa declared three days of national mourning, ordered a [state funeral](714370.md),[^cite_note-22] and signed an executive decree giving the couples' two children police protection.[^cite_note-\:4-20] Noboa also curtailed his in-progress official tour of China, Singapore, and Vietnam, announcing he would return to Ecuador to attend Sensi-Contugi's funeral.[^cite_note-23]
