The **Ruginienė Cabinet** was the 20th cabinet of the [national government](16769373.md) of the [Republic of Lithuania](17675.md). It consisted of the Prime Minister [Inga Ruginienė](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inga_Ruginien%C4%97) as the Head of Government, and 14 government ministers from the [Social Democratic Party](425771.md), [Dawn of Nemunas](75998749.md), [Farmers and Greens Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Farmers_and_Greens_Union) and [Electoral Action of Poles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_Action_of_Poles_in_Lithuania_%E2%80%93_Christian_Families_Alliance).&#x20;

The cabinet was formed on 25 September 2025, succeeding the [Paluckas Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paluckas_Cabinet). On 6 June 2026, the Social Democratic Party announced the collapse of the government coalition with the Dawn of Nemunas (PPNA) over growing disagreements. On 23 June, Ruginienė and her entire cabinet resigned after the PPNA officially withdrew from the coalition. She and her cabinet continued to serve in an acting capacity until 14 July, when the [Sinkevičius Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinkevi%C4%8Dius_Cabinet) was sworn in.
