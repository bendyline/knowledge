**Korbol** is a [sub-prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub-prefectures_of_Chad) of [Moyen-Chari Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moyen-Chari_Region) in [Chad](5488.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

The [Bua language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bua_language) is the local [lingua franca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingua_franca) of Korbol.[^cite_note-Lionnet-3]
