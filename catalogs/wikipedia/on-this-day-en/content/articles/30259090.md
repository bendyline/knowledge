**Long Lellang** (also known as Long Lelang) is a small village in the [Kelabit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelabit_people) highlands of [Sarawak](28258.md), [Malaysia](3607937.md).[^cite_note-Geonames-1] It lies approximately 578 kilometres (359 mi) east-north-east of the state capital [Kuching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuching).

The village is predominantly populated by the [Kelabit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelabit_people) tribe, though a number of [Penan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penan) people also live there. All the surrounding villages near Long Lellang are Penan villages. The village has shops and a church, and over 100 students are enrolled at the village school, SRK Long Lellang.[^cite_note-L18437125-2]

The population of Long Lellang is about 100 families.

[Long Lellang Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Lellang_Airport) is a short takeoff and landing ([STOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STOL)) airfield, providing access to this remote village.[^cite_note-L36009496-3] An aerial view of the village as it was before the new airstrip and terminal building were constructed is viewable on the Aviation Forum.[^cite_note-L19634871-4]

Neighbouring settlements include:

- [Long Datih](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Datih) 1.9 kilometres (1.2 mi) southwest

- [Aro Kangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aro_Kangan) 3.9 kilometres (2.4 mi) northeast

- [Long Labid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Labid) 11.7 kilometres (7.3 mi) northeast

- [Long Merigong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Merigong) 14.6 kilometres (9.1 mi) west

- [Long Sait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Sait?action=edit\&redlink=1) 17.2 kilometres (10.7 mi) south – (about one day's trek)

- [Long Krong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Krong?action=edit\&redlink=1) 20 kilometres (12.4 mi) south

- [Long Aar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Aar) 25 kilometres (15.5 mi) north

- [Pa Tik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pa_Tik) 31.6 kilometres (19.6 mi) northeast

It takes between four and six days to travel on foot from Long Lellang to [Bario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bario), the main settlement in the Kelabit highlands.[^cite_note-L18437125-2]
