Islamabad

The **Islamabad High Court** (**IHC**) is the senior court of the [Islamabad Capital Territory](15294.md), Pakistan, with [appellate jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appellate_jurisdiction) over the following [district courts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_courts_of_Pakistan):[^cite_note-1]

- Islamabad District Court (East)

- Islamabad District Court (West)

  [^cite_note-2]

Justice [Sardar Muhammad Sarfraz Dogar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardar_Muhammad_Sarfraz_Dogar) is the current Chief Justice, having taken oath on 8 July 2025.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## History

The Court was originally established on 14 August 2007 by Presidential Order of [Pervez Musharraf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pervez_Musharraf), the military ruler at the time. The Court ceased to exist on 31 July 2009 by a decision of the [Supreme Court of Pakistan](843857.md) following Constitution Petition No. 09 and 08 of 2009.[^cite_note-5]

The Court was re-established by the Islamabad High Court Act, 2010[^cite_note-6] following the [18th Amendment to the Constitution of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eighteenth_Amendment_to_the_Constitution_of_Pakistan#Changes_to_the_Constitution). President [Asif Ali Zardari](993655.md) administered the oath to the first Chief Justice, [Iqbal Hameed ur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hameedur_Rahman) on 3 January 2011 at the [Governor's House, Karachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor's_House,_Karachi).[^cite_note-7]

In a 116-page judgment released on 24 February 2026, the IHC detailed the removal of [Tariq Mehmood Jahangiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tariq_Mehmood_Jahangiri) in his appointment to the bench.[^cite_note-8] An IHC division bench made up of Chief Justice [Sardar Muhammad Sarfraz Dogar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardar_Muhammad_Sarfraz_Dogar) and Justice Muhammad Azam Khan ruled ex-Jahangiri's elevation to the high court was a "legal nullity" since his foundational law degree was void *[ab initio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ab_initio)*.[^cite_note-9]

## Current composition

The Islamabad High Court is headed by a [Chief Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Justice). After the passing of Islamabad High Court (Amendment) Act 2024, the Islamabad High Court will consist of 12 judges, in addition to the Chief Justice.[^cite_note-10] The mandatory retirement age of the Chief Justice and Justices is 62 years. The Additional [Judges](44719.md) are initially appointed for one year. After that, their services could either be extended or they could be confirmed or they were retired.
