The **fourth Government of [Prime Minister](259136.md) [Edi Rama](277805.md)** is the 68th ruling [Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_\(Albania\)) of the [Republic of Albania](738.md). The Government was officially confirmed by [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Albania) [Bajram Begaj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bajram_Begaj) on 12 September 2025.[^cite_note-4] Following the [2025 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Albanian_parliamentary_election), the [Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Party_of_Albania) won, for the fourth consecutive time, a majority of seats in the [Albanian Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Albania), and, for the third consecutive time, the ability to form a government without the need for a coalition.[^cite_note-5]

## Cabinet

The cabinet includes the world's first AI minister, [Diella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diella_\(AI_system\)),[^cite_note-6] serving as Virtual Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence.[^cite_note-7] The responsibilities for establishing and operating the Virtual Minister of Artificial Intelligence "Diella" are exercised by the Prime Minister.[^cite_note-8]

On 20 November 2025, the [Special Court Against Corruption and Organized Crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Structure_against_Corruption_and_Organized_Crime#First_Instance) ordered two security measures against Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Infrastructure and Energy [Belinda Balluku](65301641.md): suspension from her governmental duties and a ban on leaving the country. The decision followed her being formally charged by the [Special Prosecution Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Structure_against_Corruption_and_Organized_Crime) in connection with the [Llogara Tunnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llogara_Tunnel) affair, including alleged violations of equality in public procurement related to the tunnel project and the fourth lot of [Tirana](31075.md)’s Outer Ring Road. Balluku had previously stated in [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_Parliament) that she would not comment on ongoing investigations and had reaffirmed her support for the justice reform.[^cite_note-9]
