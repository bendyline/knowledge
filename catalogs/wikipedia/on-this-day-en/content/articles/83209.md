**Doubs** ([/duː/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [*DOO*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key); French: [\[du\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/5b/Fr-Paris--Doubs.ogg/Fr-Paris--Doubs.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/5b/Fr-Paris--Doubs.ogg/Fr-Paris--Doubs.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>; [Arpitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arpitan_language): *Dubs*) is an administrative [department](38523.md) in the northeastern French [region](58846.md) of [Bourgogne-Franche-Comté](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourgogne-Franche-Comt%C3%A9). Named after the river [Doubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubs_\(river\)), it had a population of 547,163 in 2023.[^cite_note-pophist-3] Its [prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_in_France) is [Besançon](86918.md) and [subprefectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subprefectures_in_France) are [Montbéliard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montb%C3%A9liard) and [Pontarlier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontarlier).

Situated in the east of France and the east of Bourgogne-Franche-Comté region, it shares a border with Switzerland of almost 170 km and most of its territory is located in the [Jura Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jura_Mountains). Its highest point is the [Mont d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mont_d'Or_\(Jura_Mountains\)) (1,463 m), and the three main rivers that flow through it are the Doubs, the [Ognon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ognon_\(Franche-Comt%C3%A9\)) and the [Loue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loue).

Economically, the Doubs département is one of the most industrialised in France, specialising in car manufacturing with the historic location of the [Peugeot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peugeot) plant ([Stellantis](64563392.md) group) in [Sochaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sochaux), [metalworking](266443.md) and food processing. The Doubs is also home to many companies in the [microtechnology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microtechnology) sector, as well as being the historic cradle of the French watchmaking industry. Its agriculture, mainly focused on livestock and milk production, is symbolised by cheeses such as [comté](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comt%C3%A9_cheese), [mont d'or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacherin_Mont_d'Or), [morbier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morbier_cheese), and [cancoillotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancoillotte), and by [charcuteries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charcuterie) such as [Morteau sausage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morteau_sausage), Montbéliard sausage, and smoked ham.

The department's main tourist attractions are the [citadel of Besançon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citadel_of_Besan%C3%A7on) and the [Royal Saltworks at Arc-et-Senans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Saltworks_at_Arc-et-Senans), both [UNESCO World Heritage sites](44940.md), as well as the [Château de Joux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_de_Joux). The Doubs is renowned for its many remarkable natural sites, including the [Lac de Saint-Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lac_de_Saint-Point), the Saut du Doubs, the sources of the Doubs, Loue and Lison rivers, the Cirque de Consolation, the Grotte d'Osselle and the Gouffre de Poudrey.

## History

Main article: [Franche-Comté](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franche-Comt%C3%A9)

As early as the 13th century, inhabitants of the northern two-thirds of Doubs spoke [Franc-Comtois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frainc-Comtou_dialect), a dialect of the *[langues d'oïl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langues_d'o%C3%AFl)*. Residents of the southern third of Doubs spoke a dialect of the [Arpitan language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arpitan_language). Both languages co-existed with French, the official language of law and commerce, and continued to be spoken frequently in rural areas into the 20th century. They are both still spoken today but not on a daily basis.

Doubs was important as a portal to [Switzerland](26748.md) through the pass at Cluse de Pontarlier. Many famous people, including [Mirabeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honor%C3%A9_Gabriel_Riqueti,_comte_de_Mirabeau), [Toussaint Louverture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toussaint_Louverture) and [Heinrich von Kleist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_von_Kleist), were imprisoned in the [Château de Joux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_de_Joux).

Doubs is one of the original 83 departments created during the [French Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolution) on 4 March 1790. It was created from part of the former province of [Franche-Comté](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franche-Comt%C3%A9). The [prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_in_France) (capital) is [Besançon](86918.md).

In 1793, the republic of [Mandeure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandeure) was annexed by France and incorporated into the department. This district was passed between various territories and departments in the ensuing administrative reorganisations and wars, but was restored to Doubs in 1816 when the former principality of [Montbéliard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montb%C3%A9liard) was also added to the department.

However, the commune of [Le Cerneux-Péquignot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Cerneux-P%C3%A9quignot) was annexed by the [Canton of Neuchâtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Neuch%C3%A2tel) under the terms of the 1814 [Treaty of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Paris_\(1814\)), and since remained Swiss territory.

Between the defeat of France at the [Battle of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Waterloo) and November 1818, Doubs was included in the area occupied by [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Empire) troops.

[Victor Hugo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Hugo), [Gustave Courbet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustave_Courbet), [Armand Peugeot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armand_Peugeot), [Auguste and Louis Lumière](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auguste_and_Louis_Lumi%C3%A8re) and [Frank Darabont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Darabont) are among the famous people born in Doubs.

## Geography

Doubs is part of the current region of Bourgogne-Franche-Comté and is surrounded by the French departments of [Jura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jura_\(department\)), [Haute-Saône](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haute-Sa%C3%B4ne), and [Territoire de Belfort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territoire_de_Belfort), and the Swiss cantons of [Vaud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaud), [Neuchâtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Neuch%C3%A2tel), and [Jura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Jura).

The department is dominated by the [Jura mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jura_mountains), which rise east of [Besançon](86918.md).

## Economy

The Doubs department is at the same time the greenest and the most industrialized in France.

It is the birthplace of the automotive manufacturer [Peugeot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peugeot).

## Tourism

The [castle of Joux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_de_Joux) and [Besançon](86918.md) are important tourist destinations.
