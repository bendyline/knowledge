**Buni Yadi, Buni Gari, Yadin Buni** (or simply **Buni**) is a town in [Gujba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujba) Local Government Area, [Yobe State](2139960.md), [Nigeria](21383.md), about 55 km from [Damaturu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damaturu), the capital of Yobe State.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Insurgency

Buni yadi has been attacked a number of times by the [Boko Haram](23769768.md) terrorists which lead to the closure of the public places like markets.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]<https://guardian.ng/tag/buni-yadi/><https://independent.ng/journalists-inspect-governor-bunis-post-insurgency-recovery-projects-in-buni-yadi/><https://punchng.com/tags/buni-yadi/>
