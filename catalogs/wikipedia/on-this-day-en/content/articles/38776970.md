The **Communist Party of Cuba** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Partido Comunista de Cuba*, **PCC**) is the [sole](102459.md) [ruling party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruling_party) of [Cuba](5042481.md). It was founded on 3 October 1965 as the successor to the [United Party of the Cuban Socialist Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Party_of_the_Cuban_Socialist_Revolution), which was in turn made up of the [26th of July Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/26th_of_July_Movement) and [Popular Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Socialist_Party_\(Cuba\)) that seized power in Cuba after the 1959 [Cuban Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_Revolution). The party governs Cuba as an [authoritarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Authoritarianism) [one-party state](102459.md) where dissidence and political opposition are prohibited and repressed. The [Cuban constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_constitution) ascribes the role of the party to be the "[leading force of society and of the state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanguardism)."

The highest body within the PCC is the [Party Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba), which convenes every five years. When the Congress is not in session, the [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba) is the highest body. Because the Central Committee meets twice a year, most day-to-day duties and responsibilities are vested in the [Politburo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politburo_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba). Since April 2021, the [First Secretary of the Central Committee](38776884.md) has been [Miguel Díaz-Canel](38630938.md), who has been serving as [President of Cuba](516041.md) since 2018.

[Marxism–Leninism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxism%E2%80%93Leninism) was gradually formalized as the party's guiding ideology and remains so to this day. The party pursues [state socialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_socialism), under a [planned economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planned_economy) which is implemented throughout Cuba despite the [long-term embargo](1105954.md) by the [United States](3434750.md). The PCC also supports [Castroism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Fidel_Castro) and [Guevarism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guevarism) and is a member of the [International Meeting of Communist and Workers' Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Meeting_of_Communist_and_Workers'_Parties).

## History

### Consolidation

Main article: [Consolidation of the Cuban Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consolidation_of_the_Cuban_Revolution)



A billboard in [Havana](49719.md) that reads "Socialist revolution from the poor, by the poor, and to the poor"

Cuba had a number of [communist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist) and [socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist) organizations from the early period of the [Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Cuba_\(1902%E2%80%931959\)) (founded in 1902). The original "[internationalised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comintern)" Communist Party of Cuba formed in the 1920s. In 1944, it renamed itself as the [Popular Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Socialist_Party_\(Cuba\)) for electoral reasons. In July 1961, two years after the successful overthrow of [Fulgencio Batista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulgencio_Batista) and the creation of a revolutionary government, the **[Integrated Revolutionary Organizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_Revolutionary_Organizations)** (ORI) was formed from the merger of:

- Fidel Castro's [26th of July Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/26th_of_July_Movement)

- The [Popular Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Socialist_Party_\(Cuba\)) led by [Blas Roca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blas_Roca)

- Parts of the student-based [Revolutionary Directory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directorio_Revolucionario_13_de_Marzo) led by [Faure Chomón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faure_Chom%C3%B3n)

On 26 March 1962, the ORI became the [United Party of the Socialist Revolution of Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Party_of_the_Socialist_Revolution_of_Cuba) (PURSC), which in turn became the Communist Party of Cuba on 3 October 1965. In Article 5 of the Cuban constitution of 1976, the Communist Party is recognized as "the superior guiding force of society and of the State, that organizes and orients common efforts toward the high goals of the construction of socialism and the advancement toward communist society".[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] All parties, including the Communist Party, are prohibited from publicly advertising their organizations.

### Consistent government

For the first fifteen years of its formal existence, the Communist Party was almost completely inactive outside of the [Politburo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politburo_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba). The 100 person Central Committee rarely met and it was ten years after its founding that the first regular [party Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba) was held. In 1969, membership of the party was only 55,000 or 0.7% of the population, making the PCC the smallest ruling communist party in the world. In the 1970s, the party's apparatus began to develop. By the time of the first party Congress in 1975, the party had grown to just over two hundred thousand members, the Central Committee was meeting regularly and provided the organizational apparatus giving the party the [leading role in society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_role_of_the_Communist_party) that ruling Communist parties generally hold. By 1980, the party had grown to over 430,000 members and it grew further to 520,000 by 1985. Apparatuses of the party had grown to ensure that its leading cadres were appointed to key government positions.

The Eighth Congress took place from 16 to 19 April 2021,[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] during which [Miguel Díaz-Canel](38630938.md) was elected as the [First Secretary of the Central Committee](38776884.md), taking over from [Raúl Castro](221749.md).[^cite_note-retirementonhold-12] [José Ramón Machado Ventura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Ram%C3%B3n_Machado_Ventura) was Second Secretary from 2011 to 2021.[^cite_note-retirementonhold-12][^cite_note-13] Abelardo Álvarez Gil also remains Head of the Department of Organization and Staff Policy.[^cite_note-retirementonhold-12]

## Organization

The PCC governs Cuba as an [authoritarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Authoritarianism) [one-party state](102459.md)[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] where dissidence and political opposition are prohibited and repressed.[^cite_note-\:1-19][^cite_note-\:2-20][^cite_note-21]

### Congresses

Main article: [Congress of the Communist Party of Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba)

The Communist Party of Cuba holds congresses every five years, normally in years ending in -6 and -1 (e.g. 2016 and 2021). Exceptions to this pattern are the First Congress (1975), Second Congress (1980) and Fifth Congress (1997). There was also an unusual 14-year gap between the Fifth Congress and Sixth Congress (2011).

### Central Committee

Further information: [8th Central Committee of the Communist Party of Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_Central_Committee_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba)



Party headquarters

The leading bodies of the party were the [Politburo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politburo_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba) and the [Secretariat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretariat_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba) until 1991, when the two bodies were merged into an expanded Politburo with over twenty members. However, the Secretariat was re-introduced in 2002. There is also a Central Committee which meets between party congresses. At the Fifth Congress, the size of the Central Committee was reduced to 150 members from the previous membership of 225. Fidel Castro was the party's [First Secretary](38776884.md) (or leader) since its inception while [Raúl Castro](221749.md) was the Second Secretary. Upon Fidel Castro's 2008 resignation from the party and [Cuban government](73249.md), Raúl Castro became First Secretary.

### Politburo

Further information: [8th Politburo of the Communist Party of Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_Politburo_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba)

A 14-member Politburo was elected by the 1st Plenary Session of the [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba) on 19 April 2021 following the [8th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_Congress_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba?action=edit\&redlink=1).

### Secretariat

Further information: [8th Secretariat of the Communist Party of Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_Secretariat_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba)

A 6-member Secretariat was elected by the 1st Plenary Session of the [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba) on 19 April 2021 following the [8th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_Congress_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Cuba?action=edit\&redlink=1).

### Mass organizations related to the PCC

- [Young Communist League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Communist_League_\(Cuba\)), (UJC founded in 1962 by [Fidel Castro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidel_Castro)), youth group of future militants of the PCC

- [Workers' Central Union of Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers'_Central_Union_of_Cuba), (CTC, founded in 1939 by [Blas Roca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blas_Roca_Calderio) and Lázaro Peña), a Cuban trade union center

- [Federation of Cuban Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Cuban_Women), (FMC, founded in 1960 by Fidel Castro and [Vilma Espín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilma_Esp%C3%ADn)), a centralized women's organization

- [National Association of Small Farmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Association_of_Small_Farmers), (ANAP, founded in 1961 by Fidel Castro), a peasant organization

- [José Martí Pioneer Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mart%C3%AD_Pioneer_Organization), (OPJM, founded in 1977 by Fidel Castro), student organization (pioneers)

- Student Federation of Secondary Education, (FEEM, founded in 1970 by Fidel Castro), student organization (pre)

- University Student Federation, (FEU, founded in 1922 by [Julio Antonio Mella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julio_Antonio_Mella)), student organization (university)

- [Committees for the Defense of the Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committees_for_the_Defense_of_the_Revolution), (CDR, founded in 1960 by Fidel Castro), community work organization

- Association of Combatants of the Cuban Revolution, (ACRC, founded in 1993 by Fidel Castro), organization of active and retired military personnel

- [Union of Journalists of Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Journalists_of_Cuba), (UPEC, founded in 1963 by Fidel Castro), a centralized organization of journalists

### Youth

The Communist Party of Cuba has a youth wing, the [Young Communist League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Communist_League_\(Cuba\)) (*Unión de Jóvenes Comunistas*, UJC) which is a member organization of the [World Federation of Democratic Youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Federation_of_Democratic_Youth). It also has a children's group, the [José Martí Pioneer Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mart%C3%AD_Pioneer_Organization).

## Ideology

The PCC is officially a [Marxist–Leninist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxism%E2%80%93Leninism)[^cite_note-22] party that is dedicated to the establishment of a [communist society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_society).[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] Since the Cuban Revolution, the party has also followed the doctrines of [Castroism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Fidel_Castro) (the ideology of Fidel Castro, including inspiration from [José Martí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mart%C3%AD)) and [Guevarism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guevarism).

### Economy

After the [Cuban Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_Revolution), the Party [nationalized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalized) nearly all private enterprises, establishing a [state socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_socialism) [command economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Command_economy).[^cite_note-\:0-26] Historically, the party has been more reluctant in engaging in [market reforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberalization) than communist parties leading other [Marxist–Leninist states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxist-Leninist_state), but it has been pressured to accept some market measures since the [dissolution of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Soviet_Union) and the resultant loss of economic subsidies.

Raúl Castro, after becoming the leader of the party, campaigned to "renew" Cuba's socialist economy through the establishment of a [mixed economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_economy) based on an expansion of [worker-owned cooperatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worker-owned_cooperative) and [small privately-owned businesses](276495.md), and partial [devolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devolution) of economic planning to local governments.[^cite_note-\:0-26][^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28][^cite_note-\:3-29] Some state enterprises have been [denationalized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denationalization) and converted into worker co-ops in this process, wherein "\[b]usinesses that since 1968 had been run by the government are now being turned over to their former state employees, to be managed by the worker-owners of these businesses."[^cite_note-\:3-29][^cite_note-30]

There has been some speculation that Cuba may ultimately transition towards a model more similar to a China's "[socialist market economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_market_economy)" or Vietnam's "[socialist-oriented market economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist-oriented_market_economy)."[^cite_note-\:0-26][^cite_note-31] [Private property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_property) and [foreign direct investment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_direct_investment) were recognized as legitimate economic activities in the new constitution approved via [a popular referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Cuban_constitutional_referendum) in 2019, along these lines.[^cite_note-32]

### Foreign relations

Further information: [Foreign interventions by Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_interventions_by_Cuba)

The Communist Party of Cuba has often pursued an [interventionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interventionism_\(politics\)) foreign policy to aid left-wing revolutionary movements and governments abroad, including the [ELN](822032.md) in [Colombia](5222.md), the [FMLN](327525.md) in [El Salvador](9356.md), the [Sandinistas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandinista_National_Liberation_Front) in [Nicaragua](21362.md), and [Maurice Bishop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Bishop)'s [New Jewel Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jewel_Movement) in [Grenada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenada).[^cite_note-Revolution32-33] The party's most significant international role was in the [civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angolan_Civil_War) in [Angola](701.md), where Cuba directed a joint Angolan/Soviet/Cuban force in the [Battle of Cuito Cuanavale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Cuito_Cuanavale).[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35] More recently, the party has sought to support [Pink Tide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Tide) leaders across Latin America, such as [Hugo Chávez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Ch%C3%A1vez) and [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md) in [Venezuela](32374.md) and [Evo Morales](284163.md) in [Bolivia](3462.md).

[Medical diplomacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_diplomacy) has also been a prominent feature of the Party's foreign policy. The party [maintains a policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_medical_internationalism) of sending thousands of Cuban doctors, agricultural technicians, and other professionals to other countries throughout the developing world. The party also supports [Latin American integration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_American_integration).[^cite_note-36]

The Party expresses opposition to [US imperialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_imperialism) and the [United States embargo against Cuba](1105954.md).[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]
