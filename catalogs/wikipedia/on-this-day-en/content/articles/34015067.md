The **Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Eritrea** is a government ministry which oversees the [foreign relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Eritrea) of [Eritrea](17238590.md). Eritrea's current minister of foreign affairs is [Osman Saleh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osman_Saleh), since 2007.[^cite_note-1]
