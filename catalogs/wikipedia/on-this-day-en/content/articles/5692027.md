**Kazem Jalali** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): کاظم جلالی; born 25 July 1967)[^cite_note-4] is an Iranian [principlist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Principlists) politician and former member of the [Majlis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majlis_of_Iran) from [Tehran, Rey, Shemiranat and Eslamshahr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran,_Rey,_Shemiranat_and_Eslamshahr_\(electoral_district\)) district. In June 2009, he was the spokesman for the foreign relations committee of the Majlis.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-Moussawi2012-6]

Kazem Jalali holds a PhD of political science from [Imam Sadiq University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imam_Sadiq_University), Tehran. Jalali was a senior member of [Moderation and Development Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moderation_and_Development_Party), but he resigned in 2007.[^cite_note-7] He is president of [Followers of Wilayat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Followers_of_Wilayat) fraction and was close to [Ali Larijani](531848.md).[^cite_note-DW-8]
