**Deogratius John Ndejembi** (born 12 July 1983)[^cite_note-\:0-1] is the Vice-President of the United Republic of [Tanzania](30118.md) since 04 September 2026 after being sworn-in and took the oath of office on 29 August 2026.[^cite_note-2] He replaced [Emmanuel Nchimbi](38588567.md), who resigned from office.[^cite_note-3]
