**Maungdaw Township** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): မောင်တောမြို့နယ် [\[máʊɰ̃dɔ́ mjo̰nɛ̀\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Burmese)) is a [township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_Myanmar) of [Maungdaw District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maungdaw_District) in [Rakhine State](493373.md), [Myanmar](19457.md) (Burma). The principal town is [Maungdaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maungdaw).[^cite_note-1]

&#x20;\


## Demographics

The township has a large [Muslim](6037917.md) [Rohingya](1918632.md) population, roughly 80% of the total population in 2012. In July 2012, the government of Myanmar did not include Rohingyas, and instead classified as stateless [Bengali Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_Muslims) from [Bangladesh](3454.md), on the government's list of more than 135 ethnic groups.[^cite_note-2] Other 20% population are [Rakhine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rakhine_people), [Bamar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamar), [Kamein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamein), Khami, [Daingnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daingnet_people), [Mro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mro_people_\(Awa_Khami\)), [Meitei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meitei_people),[^cite_note-3] and Thet.

## Education

There are five basic education high schools, three high schools (branches), eight middle schools, one middle school (branch), one affiliated middle school, 16 post-primary schools and 125 primary schools as of 2011.[^cite_note-4]

## History

The area comprising the township served as natural water boundary between historic Bengal and Arakan, often Arakanese kingdoms often spreading their sovereignty over Bengal crossing [Naf River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naf_River).&#x20;

Coastal areas of Maungdaw was site of battle between Portuguese mercenaries and Arakanese forces during the reign of King [Min Khamaung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Min_Khamaung) around 1615, allied with the Dutch VOC.

Following Burmese annexation of Arakan in 1785, subsequent Arakanese rebellions between 1790s and 1811 led to mass exodus of refugees fleeing to British controlled [East India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_Company) whom most of them resided at [Cox's Bazar](2271204.md). Notably an incident occurred in Northern area of township led to breakout of the [First Anglo-Burmese War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Anglo-Burmese_War).

On 6 February 2024, the [Arakan Army](38106209.md) captured the towns of Taung Pyo Letwe and Taung Pyo Letyar from the [military junta](66589972.md) during [Myanmar's civil war](69807451.md), with nearly 60 regime troops escaping over the border of the [Bandarban district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandarban_district) of [Bangladesh](3454.md).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] In December 2024, Arakan Army during the [Battle of Maungdaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Maungdaw) further captured the entire Maungdaw securing full control over the 271-kilometer border with Bangladesh.[^cite_note-8]
