Tourism is the largest source of [foreign exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_exchange_market) revenue for [Nepal](171166.md). **Tourism in Nepal** contributes about 6.7% of the country's [gross domestic product](12594.md) (GDP). In 2025, Nepal welcomed 11,58,4589 tourists, placing it 147th globally in terms of tourist numbers. Home to eight of the ten highest mountains in the world, Nepal is a destination for mountaineers, rock climbers and adventure seekers. The [Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu) and [Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist) heritages of Nepal are also strong attractions.[^cite_note-1]

## Overview



[Mayadevi Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Devi_Temple,_Lumbini) marking the [Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautama_Buddha)'s birthplace in [Lumbini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumbini)



Shankharacharya Gate in [Birgunj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birgunj), is main entry Point of Nepal from north [Bihar](442482.md), India (also known as the Gateway of Nepal).

[Mount Everest](42179.md), the highest mountain peak in the world ([8,848.86m](42179.md) above the sea level), is located in Nepal. [Mountaineering](20341.md) and other types of [adventure tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventure_tourism) and [ecotourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecotourism) are important attractions for visitors.[^cite_note-2] The [World Heritage Site](44940.md) [Lumbini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumbini), the birthplace of [Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddha), and other important religious pilgrimage sites are located throughout the country. The tourist industry is seen as a way to alleviate poverty and achieve greater social equity in the country. Tourism brings \$471 million a year to Nepal.

According to statistics of 2025, there was a growth rate of 2.1%. According to statistics from [Nepal Tourism Board (NTB)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal_Tourism_Board), a total of 1,197,191 foreign tourists entered the country in 2019 as compared to 1,173,072 in 2018. The government of Nepal declared 2011 to be Nepal Tourism Year, and hoped to attract one million foreign tourists to the country during that year.[^cite_note-3] The government of Nepal has also declared Lumbini Tourism Year 2012 to promote Lumbini. The government of Nepal has also recently declared [Visit Nepal 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal_Tourism_Board#Visit_Nepal) with the aim of bringing in two million tourists by 2020.

Most of tourists visit for short stays. In 2022, 64.7% of the tourists came to Nepal for holiday vacations,10.03% came for adventure, such as trekking and mountaineering, 12.87% came for religious visits, and 12.39% for other reasons. Tourists who come from the USA, UK, France, Spain, India, and Germany have a main target of activity: mountain climbing. [Mt. Everest](42179.md), [Mt. Ama Dablam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ama_Dablam), and [Mt. Manaslu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manaslu) are the most popular mountains.[^cite_note-nts-4]

The tourism industry of Nepal was affected after the destructive earthquake in 2015, by the [series of earthquakes in 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2015_Nepal_earthquake). In 2020, the tourism sector in Nepal collapsed due to the COVID-19 pandemic.[^cite_note-5]

In 2022, tourism income increased by 190% from 2021. The gross foreign exchange earnings were Nrs 46,756,824 thousand (Around 326,282 thousand US\$).[^cite_note-nts-4]

[Janaki Mandir in Janakpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janaki_Mandir), the temple where the Hindu goddess [Sita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sita) married Lord [Rama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rama) in Nepal.

The most followed religion in Nepal is [Hinduism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism), and the [Pashupatinath Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashupatinath_Temple), the world's largest temple of Lord [Shiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiva), located in Kathmandu, attracts many pilgrims and tourists. This is arguably the most famous Hindu temple in the Indian Subcontinent. Adjacent to the temple, lies a crematorium where bodies are burned to ashes. Pashupatinath is also listed in UNESCO heritage sites.[^cite_note-6] Other Hindu pilgrimage sites include the temple complex in [Swargadwari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swargadwari) in the [Pyuthan district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyuthan_district); [Janaki Mandir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janaki_Mandir) in [Janakpurdham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janakpurdham) in [Mithila region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mithila,_Nepal); Lake [Gosainkunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosainkunda) near [Dhunche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhunche); the temples at [Devghat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devghat); [Kalinchowk Bhagwati Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalinchowk_Bhagwati_Temple) in [Dolakha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolakha_District); [Manakamana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manakamana) temple in the [Gorkha District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorkha_District); [Pathibhara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pathibhara) near [Phungling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phungling); [Galeshwordham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galeshwordham?action=edit\&redlink=1) Myagdi and Mahamrityunjaya Shivasan Nepal in [Palpa District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palpa_District) where the biggest metallic idol of [Lord Shiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiva) is located.

[Kailashnath Mahadev Statue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kailashnath_Mahadev_Statue) at [Nasiksthan Sanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasiksthan_Sanga) in the Kathmandu Valley is a prominent religious landmark dedicated to [Shiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiva). The statue is located along the [Araniko Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Araniko_Highway) and attracts Hindu pilgrims and visitors. The site is associated with the wider religious and tourism activities of the Sanga area.

[Buddhism](3267529.md) is the second largest followed religion in context of Nepal. The [World Heritage Site](44940.md) at [Lumbini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumbini), which is considered to be the birthplace of [Gautama Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautama_Buddha), is an important pilgrimage site. Another prominent Buddhist site is [Swayambhunath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swayambhunath), the Monkey Temple, in Kathmandu.



[Ambikeshwori Temple, Dang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dang_District,_Nepal) is a [Shakta pitha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakta_pithas) which is supposed to have emerged due to the falling of right ear of [Satidevi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sati_\(goddess\)) according to the [Swasthani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swasthani) Purana.&#x20;

[Dang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dang_Valley) valley is a sacred place for Hindus as well as other religions. Kalika and Malika Devi in Chillikot hill, Ambekeshawori temple, Krishna temple, Dharapani temple are among the sacred places in [Dang district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dang_District,_Nepal). Chillikot hill is also a good place for sightseeing and also an ancient palace of a king.

[Muktinath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muktinath) is a sacred place for Hindus as well as Buddhists. The site is located in Muktinath Valley, [Mustang district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustang_district).

[Badimalika temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badimalika_Temple) in [Bajura District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bajura_District), [Gadhimai Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gadhimai_Temple) in [Bara district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bara_district), [Halesi-Maratika Caves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halesi-Maratika_Caves) in [Khotang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khotang_District). [Bhageshwori Mandir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bageshwori_Temple) in [Nepalgunj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepalgunj).

[Nepal](171166.md) ratified the convention on 20 June 1978, making its historical sites eligible for inclusion on the list.[^cite_note-main-7] Four sites in Nepal were on the list in 2023, with a further fifteen on the tentative list, of sites that may be considered for future submission.[^cite_note-8] The first sites in Nepal to be added to the list were the [Sagarmatha National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagarmatha_National_Park) and the [Kathmandu Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathmandu_Valley), added in 1979. Due to the partial or substantial loss of the traditional elements of six out of seven monument zones and resulting general loss of authenticity and integrity of the whole property, Kathmandu Valley was also added to the [List of World Heritage in Danger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_World_Heritage_in_Danger) between 2003 and 2007. [Chitwan National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitwan_National_Park) was listed in 1984, and [Lumbini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumbini), the birthplace of Buddha according to Buddhist tradition, was added in 1997. The National Parks are natural sites, and the other two are cultural.

Nepal has several UNESCO World Heritage Sites that showcase the country's culture and natural beauty. Among the well-known heritage sites are the historic temples of Kathmandu Valley, Lumbini, where Buddha was born, and Chitwan National Park. These sites tell of Nepal's history, religious beliefs, the beauty of its architecture, and the variety of wildlife.[^cite_note-9]

## Wilderness tourism



Tourists trekking in [Annapurna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annapurna) region in western Nepal

According to [Nepal's Ministry of Tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Culture,_Tourism_and_Civil_Aviation), major tourist activities include wilderness and adventure activities such as mountain biking, [bungee jumping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bungee_jump), rock climbing and mountain climbing, [trekking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trekking), [hiking](147313.md), bird watching, flights, paragliding and hot air ballooning over the mountains of Himalaya, exploring the waterways by raft, kayak or canoe and jungle safaris especially in the [Terai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terai) region.[^cite_note-49] International elephant polo is played at [Chitwan National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitwan_National_Park).

## Orphanage tourism

In 2018, research into global volunteering behaviour identified Nepal as one of the world’s ten most popular destinations for orphanage [voluntourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_volunteering).[^cite_note-50]

Leading [responsible tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_tourism) and [child welfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_protection) organisations agree that it is irresponsible for short term and/or untrained international volunteers to work in [orphanages.](387703.md)[^cite_note-Perkins-51] Few volunteers are qualified to interact with traumatised, vulnerable children[^cite_note-52] and many orphanages lack the facilities, trained staff, and child protection policies to create safe, nurturing environments for children in care.[^cite_note-Perkins-51]

There are over 800 orphanages in Nepal, with 80% of those in tourist areas.[^cite_note-53] Out of the 75 districts in the country, most registered orphanages and children’s homes are found in the five most-visited by tourists ([Kathmandu](17168.md), [Lalitpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalitpur,_Nepal), [Bhaktapur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhaktapur), [Kaski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaski_District), and [Chitwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitwan_District)).[^cite_note-Punaks2014-54]<sup>: 13 </sup>

Orphanages attract tourists who want to volunteer their time and [donate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donation) money, unaware that they are supporting an industry taking advantage of impoverished families.[^cite_note-admin-55]

In Nepal, there are an estimated 16,886 children living in orphanages, 80% of whom[^cite_note-Punaks2014-54] have at least one parent who could care for them. With promises of an [education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education) and a better life, children are recruited into orphanages from rural areas which are still suffering from the economic effects of ten years of [civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepalese_Civil_War), to meet demands for donations[^cite_note-Punaks2014-54] – a phenomenon known as orphanage [trafficking](28645.md).[^cite_note-56]

The [ChildSafe Movement](https://thinkchildsafe.org/) reports instances of orphanages keeping children in impoverished conditions to attract more donors.[^cite_note-Punaks2014-54] [ECPAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ECPAT_International) has also identified a link between orphanage tourism, and an increase in [child sexual exploitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commercial_sexual_exploitation_of_children) by foreigners and volunteers.[^cite_note-admin-55]

## Statistics

| --- |

In 2007, the number of international tourists visiting [Nepal](171166.md) was 526,705, which was an increase of 37.2% compared to the previous year. In 2008, the number of tourists decreased by 5% to 500,277. In 2018, the number of international tourists arrival was 1.17 million. In 2019, the number increased to 1.19 million. In 2020, the COVID-19 pandemic had a major impact on tourism in Nepal, with tourist arrivals dropping to just 230,085 with a decrease of over 80.7% and further decrease by 30% in 2021 with a total number of 150,962. Pokhara is one of the main tourist destinations in Nepal.

In 2008, 55.9% of the foreign visitors came from Asia (18.2% from India), while Western Europeans accounted for 27.5%, 7.6% were from North America, 3.2% from Australia and the Pacific Region, 2.6% from Eastern Europe, 1.5% from Central and South America, 0.3% from Africa and 1.4% from other countries.

Foreign tourists visiting Nepal in 2008 stayed in the country for an average of 11.78 days which has now increased to 15.1 days and 15.5 days in 2020 and 2021 respectively[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-60][^cite_note-61]

In 2024, Nepal received 1,147,567 foreign tourists, a 13.1% increase over 2023 and about 96% of pre-pandemic levels, according to the Nepal Tourism Board.[^cite_note-62]
