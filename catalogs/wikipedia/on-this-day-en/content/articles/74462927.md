# Presidential Palace, Niamey



The old Presidential Palace in [Niamey](57061.md), located across the Boulevard de la République from the new palace. The old Presidential Palace was used from 1960 until 2021, when it was replaced by the new residence.&#x20;

The **Presidential Palace** is the official residence of the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_heads_of_state_of_Niger) of the [Republic of Niger](21373.md). The palace and surrounding presidential complex are located along the Boulevard de la République in [Niamey](57061.md) on the banks of the [Niger River](21437.md).[^cite_note-anp-1] The present Presidential Palace was constructed in the early 2020s, replacing the former [French colonial era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_of_Niger) palace which had been used since Independence in 1960.[^cite_note-anp-1]

On 26 July 2023, President [Mohamed Bazoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Bazoum) was targeted in a [coup d'état](74454838.md) while he was being held inside his palace by soldiers from the presidential guard.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] The palace was again attacked in a [2026 coup attempt](84092071.md).[^cite_note-4]
