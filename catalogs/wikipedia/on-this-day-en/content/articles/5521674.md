**Kapchorwa** is a town in the [Eastern Region of Uganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Region,_Uganda). It is the main municipal, commercial, and administrative center of [Kapchorwa District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapchorwa_District). Initially, the town of Kapchorwa was known as the Sebei Region.

## Location

The town of Kapchorwa is located in the [Sebei sub-region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebei), approximately 65 kilometres (40 mi), by road, northeast of [Mbale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbale), the largest city in the Eastern Region.[^cite_note-2] This is approximately 310 kilometres (190 mi), by road, northeast of [Kampala](56689.md), Uganda's capital and largest city.[^cite_note-3] It is also approximately 77km from the [Suam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapchorwa%E2%80%93Suam_Road) Kenya-Uganda Border to the east.The coordinates of the town are 1°24'00.0"N 34°27'00.0"E (latitude\:1.4000; longitude: 34.4500).[^cite_note-4]

## Demographics

During the 2002 national population census, Kapchorwa's population was estimated at 8,700. In 2010, the Uganda Bureau of Statistics (UBOS) estimated the population at 12,300. In 2011, UBOS estimated the mid-year population at 12,900.[^cite_note-5]

## Running community

Kapchorwa is a home to successful running athletes in the past decade, including gold olympic and world championship medalists, like Joshua Cheptegei, Jacob Kiplimo and Stephen Kiprotich still living and training there. Due do this fact and the benefits of high altitude for running Kapchorwa is called a "running paradise".[^cite_note-8]
