The **Red and White Cabinet** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Kabinet Merah Putih*, abbreviated as **KMP**) is the current [Cabinet of Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Indonesia). It was sworn in on 21 October 2024 by President [Prabowo Subianto](2829084.md).[^cite_note-1]&#x20;

As of 1 October 2026, with 49 ministers, 57 deputy ministers, and 11 cabinet-level agency officials (totaling 117 appointments), this cabinet is the second largest in Indonesia history (second to the [Second Dwikora Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revised_Dwikora_Cabinet) with 132 appointments) and the largest cabinet formed in the post-[*Reformasi*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reformasi_\(Indonesia\)) period.&#x20;

## Nomenclature changes

Initially, the Red White cabinet consists of 7 coordinating ministries and 42 technical ministries. Many of those are new ministries split from previous ministries.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] It eventually expanded into 8 coordinating ministries and 49 ministries.&#x20;

## Reshuffles

### First reshuffle

The Minister of Higher Education, Science, and Technology [Satryo Brodjonegoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satryo_Brodjonegoro) resigned on 19 February 2025, citing underperformance, although he was also mired with controversies during his tenure as minister. He was replaced by [Brian Yuliarto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Yuliarto).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]&#x20;

### Second reshuffle

On 8 September 2025, the president replaced five ministers, although only three ministers were immediately sworn, which were the Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Indonesian Migrant Workers Protection, and Ministry of Cooperatives.[^cite_note-Antara-15] The definitive replacement for Coordinating Minister for Political and Security Affairs is yet to be announced, and the position would be filled by an ad interim official, while the to-be-announced new Minister of Youth and Sports could not attend the ceremony and would be sworn at later date.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]&#x20;

The [Hajj Management Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajj_Management_Authority) was transformed into the newly created [Ministry of Hajj and Umrah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Hajj_and_Umrah_\(Indonesia\)), with [Mochamad Irfan Yusuf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mochamad_Irfan_Yusuf) and [Dahnil Anzar Simanjuntak](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Dahnil_Anzar_Simanjuntak\&action=edit\&redlink=1) as its minister and deputy minister, respectively.[^cite_note-hajj-7]&#x20;

Incumbent Minister of Defense [Sjafrie Sjamsoeddin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sjafrie_Sjamsoeddin) was appointed as the ad interim Coordinating Minister for Political and Security Affairs on 8 September and the news was announced the day after.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]&#x20;

### Third reshuffle

On 17 September, the president appointed the replacements for the Coordinating Minister for Political and Security Affairs and the Minister of Youth and Sports, replaced the Chief of Presidential Staff, the Head of Presidential Communication Office, the Deputy Heads of National Nutrition Agency, and three deputy ministers. The president also appointed the Police Commissioner General (Ret.) [Ahmad Dofiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_Dofiri) as the Special Advisor of the President for Security and Public Order and Police Reform.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]&#x20;

Notably, [Erick Thohir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erick_Thohir), which was the Minister of State-Owned Enterprises, was dismissed from that position and appointed as the new Minister of Youth and Sports.[^cite_note-22] [Dony Oskaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Dony_Oskaria\&action=edit\&redlink=1) was appointed as acting Minister of State-Owned Enterprises on 19 September.[^cite_note-23]&#x20;

### Fourth reshuffle

On 27 April 2026, the president appointed the replacements for the Ministry of Environment, Deputy Coordinating Minister for Food Affairs, Chief of Presidential Staff, and Head of Government Communications Agency.[^cite_note-24]&#x20;

### Fifth reshuffle

The president appointed [Suahasil Nazara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suahasil_Nazara) as the new Minister of Finance on 14 September. He was previously one of the deputy ministers of finance.[^cite_note-25]&#x20;

### Sixth reshuffle

On 1 October, the president appointed the replacements for the Coordinating Minister for Political and Security Affairs, Coordinating Minister for Human Development and Cultural Affairs, Minister of Foreign Affairs, Minister of Industry, and several deputy ministers. The president also appointed the new Chief of the National Police and its Deputy, and Chief of Presidential Staff. New position of the Coordinating Minister for Downstreaming and Energy was created, headed concurrently by the incumbent Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources [Bahlil Lahadalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahlil_Lahadalia).[^cite_note-26]&#x20;

### Replacements outside of reshuffle

The [House of Representatives](737841.md) (DPR) ratified the fourth revision of the Law on State-Owned Enterprise on 2 October 2025. The Ministry of State-Owned Enterprises is dissolved and replaced with the ministerial-level State-Owned Enterprise Management Agency. Its leader is to be appointed by the President.[^cite_note-27] Consequently, the positions of Minister and Deputy Minister of State-Owned Enterprises were automatically abolished.[^cite_note-28]&#x20;

On 8 October 2025, Prabowo inaugurated two additional deputy ministers, Akhmad Wiyagud as Deputy Minister of Home Affairs and Benjamin Paulus Octavianus as Deputy Minister of Health.[^cite_note-29] The president also appointed the Head and Deputy Heads of the new State-Owned Enterprise Management Agency.[^cite_note-30]&#x20;

On February 5, 2026, Juda Agung was appointed as Deputy Minister of Finance, replacing Thomas Djiwandono who was elected as Deputy Governor of Bank Indonesia.[^cite_note-31]&#x20;
