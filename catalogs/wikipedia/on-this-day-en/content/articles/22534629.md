The **Vertainspitze** ([Italian](14708.md): *Cima Vertana*; [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): *Vertainspitze*) is a mountain in the [Ortler Alps](2002978.md) in [South Tyrol](169611.md), [Italy](14532.md).
