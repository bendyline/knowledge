The **Khan Yunis refugee camp**,**[^cite_note-2]** also called **Khan Yunis Camp** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مخيم خان يونس), is a [Palestinian refugee camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_refugee_camp) in the [Khan Yunis Governorate](13900254.md) just west of the city of [Khan Yunis](101330.md) and two kilometers east of the [Mediterranean](19006.md) coast in the southern [Gaza Strip](12047.md). While [UNRWA](32270.md) states that there were approximately 95,550 [Palestinian refugees](74681.md) living in the camp in 2023,[^cite_note-UNRWA-1] the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics) recorded a population of 41,182 in the 2017 census.[^cite_note-PrelimCensus2017-3]



A mother and child in the newly established refugee camp, 1948

The Khan Yunis refugee camp was established after the [1948 Arab–Israeli War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Arab%E2%80%93Israeli_War), accommodating roughly 35,000 Palestinian refugees, who [fled or were expelled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Palestinian_expulsion_and_flight) by Zionist militias from their homes.



Children in front of the Khan Yunis refugee camp in 1955

On 3 November 1956, the camp and city of Khan Yunis were occupied by the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md). In the ensuing operation, about 275 Palestinians were killed by Israeli forces, including 140 refugees from the camp, in what became known as the [Khan Yunis massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khan_Yunis_massacre). Residents state that most of the casualties occurred after hostilities had ended, with the army searching houses for suspected armed men. However, Israeli authorities stated the casualties were a result of resistance by the camp's residents.[^cite_note-4]

According to UNRWA, many of the camp's residents have lost their homes as a result of operations by the Israeli military. UNRWA began reconstruction efforts in the early 2000s, but work has largely been halted due to the blockade imposed by [Israel](9282173.md) on the Gaza Strip following the [Hamas takeover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gaza_\(2007\)) of the territory. UNRWA says that at least 10,000 homes need to be constructed.[^cite_note-UNRWA-1]
