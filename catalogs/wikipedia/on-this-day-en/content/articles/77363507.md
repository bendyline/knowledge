The **Yaroslavl Refinery** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Ярославнефтеоргсинтез - Yaroslavnefteorgsintez (YANOS)) is an [oil refinery](195137.md) plant in the Russian city of [Yaroslavl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaroslavl) and is one of the largest in the country. This refinery has belonged to the Russian company [Slavneft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavneft) since 2001.

## History

In 1952, the Soviet [Ministry of Oil Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Oil_Industry) selected the city of [Yaroslavl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaroslavl) as the site for a new [oil refinery](195137.md) plant. The plant began operations in 1961 and expanded throughout the 1960s and 1970s.

In 1993, the refinery plant was [privatized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatization_in_Russia) with the approval from Russian authorities, and it soon became part of [Slavneft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavneft).[^cite_note-1]

Throughout the 2000s, the company modernized much of the refinery plant, including the [catalytic cracking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_catalytic_cracking) units, to boost production. These investments cost around \$1.5 billion.[^cite_note-2]

In 2009, the company was estimated to be worth 12.9 billion [rubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_ruble) (\$400 million [USD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USD)).[^cite_note-Финанс-3]

According to the company's website, the main competitors for the refinery as of 2013 were: [Ryazan Refinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryazan_Refinery), [Kstovo Refinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kstovo_Refinery), and [Moscow Refinery](77379611.md).[^cite_note-4]

On 1 October 2025, it was reported that a fire had broken out at the refinery.[^cite_note-5]



[NASA](18426568.md)'s [FIRMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_Information_for_Resource_Management_System) detected extensive fire on 28 March 2026 00\:33 ([UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC)) at Yaroslavl refinery

On 28 March 2026, during the [Russo-Ukrainian War](42085878.md), Ukrainian [drones](38212231.md) caused a fire at the refinery.[^cite_note-6]
