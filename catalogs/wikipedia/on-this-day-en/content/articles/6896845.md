# SSPX-affiliated religious orders

The [Society of Saint Pius X](232299.md) has close links with several [religious institutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religious_order_\(Catholic\)), chiefly in France.

## Various orders

- Brothers of the Priestly Fraternity of Saint Pius X

## Eastern orders

The following are associated religious orders that celebrate according to [Eastern Catholic liturgies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Catholic_liturgies):[^cite_note-32]

- [Priestly Fraternity of Saint Josaphat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priestly_Society_of_Saint_Josaphat) at Lviv, Ukraine

- Society of St. John the Precursor of the Lord

  [^cite_note-33]

  &#x20;at Riga, Latvia

- Greek Catholic Sisters of the [Studite Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Studite_Monks) at [Riga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riga), [Latvia](17514.md)

- Ukrainian Basilian Sisters at Lviv, Ukraine, who were forced to leave the [Basilian Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Saint_Basil_the_Great) in 1995, "because of their 'traditionalist' ideas"

  [^cite_note-34]

  &#x20;and who now reside in the house where [Nicholas Charnetsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Charnetsky) died following his release from the [Gulag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulag). The room in which Nicholas died is now the convent's chapel.

  [^cite_note-35]
