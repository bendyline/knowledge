**Hardeep Singh Puri** (born 15 February 1952[^cite_note-w796-1]) is an Indian politician and retired diplomat of [Indian Foreign Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Foreign_Service) who is serving as the  [Minister of Petroleum and Natural Gas](10345589.md) since 2021.[^cite_note-2]

He is a 1974 batch [Indian Foreign Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Foreign_Service) [officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Services_of_India) who served as the [Permanent Representative of India to the United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permanent_Representative_of_India_to_the_United_Nations) from 2009 to 2013.[^cite_note-IANS-3][^cite_note-4] Puri served as the chairman of the [United Nations Security Council](31956.md)'s Counter Terrorism Committee from January 2011 to February 2013; and joined [International Peace Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Peace_Institute) as a senior advisor in June 2013.[^cite_note-BSHSP2-5]

Puri joined the [Bharatiya Janata Party](149330.md) in January 2014, and became a [Member of Parliament](410238.md) in the [Rajya Sabha](232210.md) from [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md) in November 2020.[^cite_note-FPHSP-6] Earlier in May 2019, he had taken charge as the Minister of State (Independent Charge) for Housing and Urban Affairs and Civil Aviation and Minister of State for Commerce and Industry.[^cite_note-BSHSP2-5][^cite_note-7]

## Early life and education

Hardeep Singh Puri was born on 15 February 1952 in [Daryaganj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daryaganj), Delhi.[^cite_note-\:0-8] He was educated with a [BA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in history and an [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in political science at the [Hindu College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_College,_Delhi), a constituent college of the [Delhi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_University).[^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-9] Drawn to student politics, Puri was elected as the prime minister of the college's student parliament.[^cite_note-\:0-8]

## Diplomatic career

### Civil service

Hardeep Puri joined the [Indian Foreign Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Foreign_Service) in 1974, and has served as [Joint secretary to the Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_secretary_to_the_Government_of_India) in the [Ministry of External Affairs](10302410.md) from 1994 to 1997, and from 1999 to 2002. He has also served as [Joint secretary to the Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_secretary_to_the_Government_of_India) in [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(India\)) from 1997 to 1999. He was India's ambassador to Brazil. He later served as [Secretary to the Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_to_the_Government_of_India) (Economic Relations) in the [Ministry of External Affairs](10302410.md) from 2009 to 2013.

Puri has been stationed at important diplomatic posts in Brazil, where he was ambassador, Japan, [Sri Lanka](26750.md), and the United Kingdom where he was Deputy High Commissioner. Between 1988 and 1991, he was the Coordinator of the [UNDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNDP)/[UNCTAD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNCTAD) Multilateral Trade Negotiations Project to help Developing Countries in the [Uruguay Round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguay_Round) of Multilateral Trade Negotiations.[^cite_note-10]

### United Nations

He also served as the chairman of the [United Nations Security Council Counter-Terrorism Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Counter-Terrorism_Committee) from January 2011 to February 2013, and as [President of the United Nations Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_United_Nations_Security_Council) in August 2011, and again in November 2012.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Political career



Hardeep Singh Puri addressing at an event, 2017

Hardeep Singh Puri joined the [International Peace Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Peace_Institute) as a senior advisor in June 2013.[^cite_note-13] He joined the [Bharatiya Janata Party](149330.md) in January 2014, expressing admiration for the party's approach to [national security](240468.md).[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

He is serving as the [Member of Rajya Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_current_members_of_the_Rajya_Sabha) from Uttar Pradesh from 2018. Puri was inducted into the cabinet as the [Minister of Housing and Urban Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Housing_and_Urban_Affairs), after Venkaiah Naidu was elevated to the post of Vice President of India in 2017. In May 2019, he contested from [Amritsar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amritsar_\(Lok_Sabha_constituency\)) as a BJP Candidate, but lost to [Gurjeet Singh Aujla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurjeet_Singh_Aujla) of the Congress.[^cite_note-16]

In May 2019, Puri became the Minister of State (with Independent Charge) for [Civil Aviation](10257026.md) and Minister of State for [Commerce and Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Commerce_and_Industry_\(India\)).[^cite_note-17]

In July 2021, he was promoted to the post of [Union Minister of Petroleum and Natural Gas](10345589.md), along with [Union Minister for Housing and Urban Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Housing_and_Urban_Affairs) in the [Second Modi ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Modi_ministry) when there was a cabinet overhaul.[^cite_note-18]

In March 2022, during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](70149799.md), Puri served as part of a special four-minister envoy and brought 6,711 students back to India under [Operation Ganga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Ganga).[^cite_note-19]

## Personal life

Hardeep Singh Puri is married to Ambassador [Lakshmi Puri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakshmi_Puri), of the Indian Foreign Service,[^cite_note-w796-1] and, later, the [United Nations](31769.md) cadre, who is a former [Assistant Secretary-General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_Secretary-General) of the United Nations and a former Deputy Executive Director of [UN Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Women).[^cite_note-UN-SG_UN_Women-20] They have two daughters. His brother, Pradeep Puri, is an [IAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Administrative_Service) officer of the 1979 batch, who played an instrumental role in the construction of the [DND Flyway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DND_Flyway).[^cite_note-21]

On 1 December 2022, Hardeep Singh Puri was awarded the Grand Cross of the [Order of Rio Branco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Rio_Branco).[^cite_note-22]
