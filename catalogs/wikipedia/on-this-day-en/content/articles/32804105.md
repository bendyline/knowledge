The **Tunisian National Guard** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Garde Nationale*, [Tunisian Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_Arabic_language): الحرس الوطني, romanized: *el-Ḥaras el-Waṭanī*) is the national [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie) force of the [Republic of Tunisia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Tunisia) and separated from the [Tunisian Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_Armed_Forces).

## History

During the [French protectorate of Tunisia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_protectorate_of_Tunisia), the [French national gendarmerie](6330819.md) was tasked to maintain law and order in Tunisia.[^cite_note-2] Shortly after Tunisia's independence, the Tunisian National Guard was created in 1956 by Beylical Decree of 6 September 1956 to replace the French national gendarmerie, which returned to France.[^cite_note-law-1][^cite_note-fiep-3]

## Missions

Tunisian National Guard, along with Tunisian National Police, is the first line of defense against internal security threat.[^cite_note-4] Tunisian National Guard role as stipulated in article 2 of Decree No. 2006-1162 on the special status of agents of the National Guard, are as follows:[^cite_note-Tunisia_NationalGuard-5]

- for maintaining public order,

- preserving the security,

- land and sea borders guards,

- road/highway traffic and safety, public safety,

- search for criminals and,

- judicial investigations.

## Organization

The Tunisian National Guard is led by a Director General, and consist of an inspectorate general and five directorate general, which are:[^cite_note-fiep-3][^cite_note-6]

- Inspectorate General.

- Directorate General of Public Security.

- Directorate General of Intervention Units.

- Directorate General of Border Guards.

- Directorate General of Common Services.

- Directorate General of the Mutual of Civil Protection National Guard officials.

Aside of five directorate general, It also have Directorate which works directly under Director General of the National Guard:[^cite_note-fiep-3]

- Directorate of Research and Investigation.

- Directorate of Anti-Terrorism.

- Directorate of Studies, Strategic plan and International Cooperation.

- The [Special Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit%C3%A9_Sp%C3%A9ciale_%E2%80%93_Garde_Nationale).

- The Central Secretariat.

- The Central Operations Room
