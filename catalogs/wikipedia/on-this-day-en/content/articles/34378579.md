**Gerardo Werthein** (born 3 December 1955)[^cite_note-IOC-1] is an Argentine businessman and diplomat who served as the [minister of foreign affairs, international trade and worship of Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_of_Argentina) between October 2024 and his resignation in October 2025.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] He previously served as the [ambassador of Argentina to the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Argentina,_Washington,_D.C.) from April to October 2024.[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-5]

## Early life and career

Werthein was born into a [Russian family of Jewish origin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Russia). His great-grandfather, Leon Werthein, emigrated from [Bessarabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessarabia) to Argentina in 1904 with his wife Ana Jajam and their four children, Gregorio, Elisa, Numo, and Israel, fleeing the persecution of Jewish people by the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-\:our_story-7] The Werthein family began with large-scale livestock operations in Argentina and later established the Werthein Group, a major economic conglomerate that has included ownership of Telecom Argentina.

Werthein served as president of the [Argentine Olympic Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentine_Olympic_Committee) from 2009 to 2021. He has also been a member of the [International Olympic Committee](15147.md) (IOC) and the [Olympic Broadcasting Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_Broadcasting_Services) since 2011, joining the IOC Executive Board as an "Executive Member" in 2020.[^cite_note-8] During his tenure as president, [Buenos Aires](255919.md) was selected as the host city for the [2018 Summer Youth Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Summer_Youth_Olympics), which took place in October of that year.

## Political career

### Ambassador to the United States (2023 -2024)

Following Javier Milei's electoral victory in the [2023 Argentine presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Argentine_general_election), Werthein accompanied Milei on a trip to [New York](645042.md) and was present at the meetings Milei held with various figures, including former U.S. President [Bill Clinton](3356.md).[^cite_note-9] After Milei's presidential inauguration, he nominated Werthein as the Argentine ambassador to the United States. His appointment was finally approved by the [Argentine Senate](751152.md) on April 18, 2024.[^cite_note-\:0-4]

### Foreign Minister (2024–2025)

Werthein was appointed foreign minister by President Milei on 30 October 2024 following the dismissal of [Diana Mondino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diana_Mondino).[^cite_note-10] Wethein's inauguration featured a [Torah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torah), on which Werthein swore to uphold his position faithfully.[^cite_note-11]

Werthein resigned on 22 October 2025, less than a week before the [midterm election](78174701.md).[^cite_note-12]
