**Falam Township** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): ဖလမ်းမြို့နယ်) is the only [township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_Burma) of [Falam District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falam_District) in the [Chin State](493366.md) of [Myanmar](19457.md). Its administrative seat is the [town of Falam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falam). The population, as of 2014, is 41,457 (male: 20,350; female: 21,107). Out of this population, 9,092 live in Falam town (urban population) and 32,365 in rural areas.[^cite_note-2]

Falam Township is mountainous, running east–west from the foothills above the [Neyinzaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neyinzaya_River) and [Myittha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myittha_River) rivers to the high peaks of the [Chin Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chin_Hills) in the [Arakan Mountain Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arakan_Mountains) and down to the border with [Mizoram State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizoram), India.[^cite_note-3] The highest peak in the township, Mount [Zinghmuh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinghmuh),[^cite_note-4] is also the second highest mountain peak in Chin State. The [Manipur (Manipuya) River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manipur_River) runs south through the center of the township. People who travel to the city take the [Kalay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalay)-Falam-[Hakha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakha) Road. The township is famous for its heart-shaped lake called [Hri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rih_Dil) (Rih or Yi).[^cite_note-5] The township is also the location of [Laiva Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laiva_Dam), the largest dam in Chin State. The dam produces [hydroelectric power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroelectric_power) for Falam and neighboring cities such as Hakha and [Thantlang (Thlangtlang)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thantlang).

Bridges include the Manipura river-crossing suspension bridge, 320 feet long by 5 feet wide, linking Bazan Village and Kawdah Village, and the Lonnlwe creek-crossing suspension bridge, 130 feet long by four feet wide, linking Kawdah Village and Kwabwe Village in the township.[^cite_note-6]

## History

In the reorganization of Chin State townships in 2008, Falam Township lost its northernmost village tracts in the east to [Tiddim Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiddim_Township), and a few of its recently gained southwestern-most village tracts were returned to [Thantlang Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thantlang_Township), but it gained a much longer border with India by the transfer of Tiddim Township's southwestern village tracts to Fallam Township.[^cite_note-MIMU-TS-map-7]

In 2022, it became the only township left in Falam District after the formation of the [Tedim District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tedim_District).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

## Communities

The town of Rihkhawdar (Reehkawdar) is the northernmost town in the township.[^cite_note-MIMU-TS-map-7] The township has 87 village tracts and 178 villages.[^cite_note-Profile-1]
