On 3 February 2026, [Saif al-Islam Gaddafi](1196902.md), a Libyan politician and son of [Muammar Gaddafi](53029.md), was assassinated at age 53 in [Zintan](26063692.md), Libya, by four masked gunmen.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-jazeera1-2] At the time of his death, he was a candidate for the [Libyan presidential election](53433900.md) scheduled in April 2026.[^cite_note-LyObserver_HNEC_ready_April2026-3]

## Background

Gaddafi was [wanted by the International Criminal Court](60488397.md) since 2011, which had indicted him on two counts of [crimes against humanity](61099.md) of "murder and persecution" for his role in suppressing the [Libyan opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Gaddafi_forces) protests against [his father's regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Libya_under_Muammar_Gaddafi) during the [2011 Libyan civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_civil_war_\(2011\)).[^cite_note-Saif_al-Islam_Gaddafi_arrest_warrant-4]

In 2015, a court in [Tripoli](31232.md) sentenced him to death by firing squad in absentia for [war crimes](48626.md) committed during his role in the crackdown.[^cite_note-\:1-5] However, the [Zintan Brigades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zintan_Brigades) that were holding him since his capture in 2011 refused to hand him over as they did not recognize the authority of the internationally recognized [Tripoli government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_National_Congress) or its courts. He was released by the Zintan militia in 2017 after he was given full amnesty by [Khalifa Haftar](31227894.md), whose [Libyan National Army](35457665.md) dominates eastern and central Libya and holds influence over [Zintan](26063692.md).[^cite_note-bbc1-6] Gaddafi remained underground in Zintan since his release to avoid assassination.[^cite_note-jazeera1-2]

In 2021, Gaddafi was a candidate for the [presidential election that had been scheduled in 2021](53433900.md#2021). He was disqualified under Article 10/7 of the electoral law for having been convicted of a crime, and under Article 17/5 for not providing a certificate showing a clean criminal record.[^cite_note-LibHerald_Saif_disqualified-7] Gaddafi tried to appeal his disqualification, which led to a "collapse of the election process and \[a] return to political stalemate", according to *[Al Monitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Monitor)*.[^cite_note-AlMonitor_LY_Saif_Gaddafi_crushed_dissent-8] Polling in 2021 placed Gaddafi as a leading candidate ahead of most other nominees, alongside [Abdul Hamid Dbeibeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Hamid_Dbeibeh).[^cite_note-9] In November 2025, the Libyan [High National Election Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_National_Election_Commission) (HNEC) stated that it would be "ready to begin implementing presidential and parliamentary elections in mid-April 2026" if funds were available and if the [6+6 Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_peace_process#2023:_6+6_Committee) finalised electoral laws.[^cite_note-LyObserver_HNEC_ready_April2026-3]

## Assassination

On 3 February 2026, four masked gunmen raided Gaddafi's home after disabling security cameras. Gaddafi was in his garden and attempted to fight back but was reportedly killed at 2\:30 a.m. The four gunmen then fled the scene.[^cite_note-10] Three other people also died during the assault: guardian of the site Ajmeri Al-Atiri, local militia leader Abou Bakr Al-Siddiq, and his son Mohammed.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-bbc1-6]

## Investigation

The Public Prosecution Office in [Tripoli](31232.md) announced that an official investigation into Gaddafi's assassination had begun. The [444th Infantry Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/444th_Infantry_Brigade_\(Libya\)) denied involvement in the assassination.[^cite_note-12] The Office has announced, saying three suspects have been identified and arrest orders issued.[^cite_note-13]

## Burial

On 6 February 2026, Gaddafi was buried in [Bani Walid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bani_Walid), in a funeral attended by thousands, including [Gaddafi loyalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaddafi_loyalism). He was buried next to his younger brother [Khamis Gaddafi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khamis_Gaddafi).[^cite_note-saifislamgaddafiburial-14][^cite_note-baniwalidburial-15]

## Impact for Gaddafi loyalists

At the time of death, Saif was acknowledged to be the only person who was capable of unifying [Gaddafi loyalists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaddafi_loyalists). As a result, his death would mark the end of Gaddafi loyalists being able to have a single "unifying figure" to rally behind.[^cite_note-deathofgaddafiloyalistunity-16]
