**Puerto Leguízamo**[^cite_note-2] (Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˈpweɾto le'ɣisamo\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a town and municipality located in the [Putumayo Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putumayo_Department), a southern border region of [Colombia](5222.md). It is on the north bank of the [Putumayo River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putumayo_River).

Its Catedral Nuestra Señora del Carmen, dedicated to [Our Lady of Mount Carmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Mount_Carmel), is the [cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral) episcopal see of the Latin Catholic missionary pre-diocesan [Apostolic Vicariate of Puerto Leguízamo-Solano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Vicariate_of_Puerto_Legu%C3%ADzamo-Solano).

Puerto Leguízamo is served by [Caucayá Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucay%C3%A1_Airport).

In 2026, [a Lockheed C-130 crashed in the nearby forest](82762908.md) after departing from Caucayá Airport, killing 69 out of 126 occupants.

## Climate

Located just a few kilometres south of the [equator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equator), Puerto Leguízamo has a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) (Af) with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
