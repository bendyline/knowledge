# State of calamity (Philippines)

**State of calamity,** in the context of [disaster management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disaster_management) in the [Philippines](23440.md), refers to a status that could be declared widespread within the country, or certain localities, in response to a destructive, natural, or man-made disaster. This measure allows the release of "calamity funds" allocated to local governments and control the pricing of basic commodities in the affected areas.[^cite_note-briefer-1]

## Legal background

Under the Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010 (Republic Act 10121), a "state of calamity" is defined as "a condition involving mass casualty and/or major damages to property, disruption of means of livelihoods, roads and normal way of life of people in the affected areas as a result of the occurrence of natural or human-induced hazard".[^cite_note-briefer-1]

## Declaring a state of calamity

The [National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Disaster_Risk_Reduction_and_Management_Council) (NDRRMC) has the power to recommend to the [president of the Philippines](271126.md) the declaration of a group of [barangays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barangays), [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_the_Philippines), [cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_the_Philippines), [provinces](165100.md), [regions](216786.md) or the entire country under a state of calamity, and the lifting thereof, based on the criteria set by the NDRRMC. The president's declaration may warrant international humanitarian assistance as deemed necessary. A state of national calamity is effective until the president lifts it.[^cite_note-briefer-1]

State of calamity could also be declared or lifted by a local government unit's *sanggunian* or legislature, upon the recommendation of the local disaster risk reduction and management council (LDRRMC) concerned, based damage assessment and needs analysis.[^cite_note-briefer-1]

If a state of calamity is declared by the Philippine national government, the following measures will be imposed:[^cite_note-briefer-1]

- Appropriation for calamity funds

- Price freeze for basic necessities

- Granting of no-interest loans.
