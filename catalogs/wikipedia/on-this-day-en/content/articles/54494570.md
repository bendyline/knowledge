The **AFL Women's Grand Final** is an annual [women's Australian rules football](3827044.md) match to determine the [AFL Women's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFL_Women's) (AFLW) premiers for that year. Each year, the winning club receives a premiership trophy and premiership flag; all players in the winning team receive a premiership medallion.

The record attendance is 53,034, which was set at the [2019 AFL Women's Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_AFL_Women's_Grand_Final).

## Method of qualification

In the first two seasons of the AFL Women's (AFLW) competition, the grand final was contested by the two highest-ranked teams at the end of the home-and-away season, as no [finals series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFL_finals_series) existed.&#x20;

Since the third season, a finals series has been incorporated into the competition as follows:

- In [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_AFL_Women's_season), the top four teams qualified for the preliminary finals, with the two winners playing off in the [2019 Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_AFL_Women's_Grand_Final).

- An expanded eight-team finals series was abandoned in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_AFL_Women's_season) due to the impact of the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).

- In [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_AFL_Women's_season) and [2022 season 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_AFL_Women's_season_6), the top six teams qualified for the finals series, which was played under the laws of a single-elimination knockout tournament.

- In [2022 season 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_AFL_Women's_season_7), the Final Eight system used in the AFL was implemented in the AFLW following the league's expansion to include all 18 AFL clubs.

From 2021 onwards, the winners of the two preliminary finals have played off in the Grand Final.

## Venues

Unlike the men's [Australian Football League](4732785.md) (AFL) premiership, which has a customary host venue for the [AFL Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFL_Grand_Final) every year (that being the [Melbourne Cricket Ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne_Cricket_Ground)), there is no centralised venue that is contracted to host the women's grand final. In [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_AFL_Women's_Grand_Final) and [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_AFL_Women's_Grand_Final), the game was technically "hosted" by the [minor premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_AFL_Women's_minor_premiers): [Brisbane](5026.md#AFL_Women's_team) and [Western Bulldogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Bulldogs#AFL_Women's_team), respectively. However, in both cases, alternate venues located in the home team's state were used, owing to the unavailability of the [Gabba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gabba) in Brisbane's case and capacity concerns for the Bulldogs' home grand final. In 2019, it was hosted by the preliminary final winner with the most premiership points (percentage would have come into consideration if points were the same), as a [conference system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletic_conference) was in place for this season's grand final.[^cite_note-2] The conference-style system was abandoned in favour of a single ladder ahead of the 2021 season. Since 2021, the match is played at the highest-ranked preliminary final winner's home venue (or a larger-capacity venue located nearby), with this ranking based on the ladder at the end of the home-and-away season.[^cite_note-3]

Of the four venues to have hosted a grand final, Princes (Ikon) Park, the largest-capacity suburban ground in Melbourne, underwent construction in 2021 and 2022 to turn it into a state-of-the-art precinct that would become the official home of women's football in Victoria, with the idea that all grand finals hosted in Victoria would be played at the stadium.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Entertainment and traditions



The teams lined up for the national anthem prior to the 2022 (S7) Grand Final



Melbourne players celebrating with their premiership medallions and the 2022 (S7) premiership trophy

It is customary for the Grand Final to begin with a rendition of the [Australian National Anthem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_Anthem) in which both teams line up opposite each other. Most grand finals thus far have been accompanied by a musical entertainment act that has performed before the match or at half-time. At the conclusion of the match, an award ceremony is held for individual awards, which include a best-on-ground award and premiership medals awarded for each participant. The captains of both sides are invited to make a speech, and the cup is presented to the captain and coach of the winning team. Following the ceremonies, players of the winning side assemble on a podium for team celebration photographs with the premiership cup and premiership medals.

### Entertainment

Musicians and groups to have performed at the grand final include [Megan Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Washington) in 2017[^cite_note-7] and [Amy Shark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Shark) in 2019.[^cite_note-8] Australian musician [Missy Higgins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missy_Higgins) was booked to perform at the [2018 Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_AFL_Women's_Grand_Final), but her gig was cancelled on the day of the match due to heavy rain and surface conditions at Princes Park.[^cite_note-9] [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) performed the pre-match entertainment at the [2022 season 7 Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_AFL_Women's_season_7_Grand_Final).

### Sprint

In 2021, the [AFL](4732785.md) incorporated a sprint running race (initially known as the [Colgate AFLW Grand Final Sprint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFL_Grand_Final_Sprint)) into the on-field entertainment on the day of the grand final.[^cite_note-10] In the men's competition, the [Grand Final Sprint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFL_Grand_Final_Sprint) has been held since 1979 (albeit with a break from [1988](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_VFL_Grand_Final)–[2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_AFL_Grand_Final)). No races were run between 2022 season 7 and 2024.
