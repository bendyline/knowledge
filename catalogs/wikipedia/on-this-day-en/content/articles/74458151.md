The **National Council for the Safeguard of the Homeland** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): المجلس الوطني لحماية الوطن, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *al-Majlis al-Waṭanī li-Ḥimāyat al-Waṭan*; [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Conseil national pour la sauvegarde de la patrie*, **CNSP**) is the ruling [military junta](216815.md) of [Niger](21373.md), following the [2023 coup d'état](74454838.md) which overthrew [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_heads_of_state_of_Niger) [Mohamed Bazoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Bazoum) and the [civilian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouhoumoudou_Mahamadou's_government).[^cite_note-3]

The military junta's takeover of the government led to the [Nigerien crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerien_crisis_\(2023%E2%80%932024\)).[^cite_note-bbc-20230727-1]

## Origins

### Formation

On the evening of 26 July 2023, [Niger Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger_Air_Force)[^cite_note-ap1-4] Colonel-Major[^cite_note-5] [Amadou Abdramane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amadou_Abdramane) went on state television channel [Télé Sahel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%A9l%C3%A9_Sahel) to claim that President [Mohamed Bazoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Bazoum), who had earlier been detained by the Presidential guard at his official residence in the capital [Niamey](57061.md), had been removed from power and announced the formation of the junta.[^cite_note-ap1-4] Seated and flanked by nine other officers wearing fatigues, he said the "defense and security forces" had decided to topple Bazoum’s regime "due to the deteriorating security situation and bad governance."[^cite_note-reuters-6] He also announced the suspension of the country's Constitution, the suspension of all state institutions, the closure of the country's borders and a nationwide curfew from 22\:00 until 05\:00 local time warning against any foreign interventions.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-bbc-20230727-1]

On 27 July, Colonel Abdramane announced on television that all activities by [political parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_political_parties_in_Niger) in the country were to be suspended until further notice.[^cite_note-8] At an unknown time, the leadership of the [Niger Armed Forces](21381.md) issued a statement signed by the Army chief of staff General [Abdou Sidikou Issa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdou_Sidikou_Issa) declaring its support for the coup, citing the need to "preserve the physical integrity" of the President and his family and avoid "a deadly confrontation... that could create a bloodbath and affect the security of the population."[^cite_note-9]

On 10 August, the junta appointed a new cabinet headed by a civilian prime minister, [Ali Lamine Zeine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Lamine_Zeine).[^cite_note-10]

### Leadership

On 28 July, presidential guards' Commander General [Abdourahamane Tiani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdourahamane_Tiani) proclaimed himself as the [president of the council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_heads_of_state_of_Niger) in an address on [Télé Sahel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%A9l%C3%A9_Sahel). He said the coup was undertaken to avoid "the gradual and inevitable demise" of the country and said that Bazoum had tried to hide "the harsh reality" of the country, which he called "a pile of dead, displaced, humiliation and frustration". He also criticized the government's security strategy for its purported ineffectiveness but did not give a timeline for a return to civilian rule.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]
