The **2026 Women's NORCECA Volleyball Championship** was the 29th edition of the [Women's NORCECA Volleyball Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_NORCECA_Volleyball_Championship), a biennial international volleyball tournament organised by the [North, Central America and Caribbean Volleyball Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North,_Central_America_and_Caribbean_Volleyball_Confederation) (NORCECA) for national women's volleyball teams in North America, Central America and the Caribbean. The tournament took place from August 21 to 26.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] It was held in [Santo Domingo](57638.md), Dominican Republic, marking the sixth time the competition has been held in the country, after [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_Women's_NORCECA_Volleyball_Championship?action=edit\&redlink=1), [1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Women's_NORCECA_Volleyball_Championship?action=edit\&redlink=1), [1995](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Women's_NORCECA_Volleyball_Championship?action=edit\&redlink=1), [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Women's_NORCECA_Volleyball_Championship) and [2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_Women's_NORCECA_Volleyball_Championship).

The top three finishers, excluding the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_women's_national_volleyball_team) and [Canada](16076638.md), qualified for the [2027 FIVB Women's Volleyball World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_FIVB_Women's_Volleyball_World_Cup). The top four teams, excluding United States, also advanced to a [separate tournament](#2028_Summer_Olympics_qualification) to decide the continental spot to the [2028 Summer Olympics](77464512.md).

The [Dominican Republic](15568305.md) was the three-time defending champion, most recently defeating the United States 3–2 in the 2023 final in [Quebec City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec_City).

Canada won their first title after defeating the United States 3–2 in the final.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] For the first time in 15 years, [Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuba_women's_national_volleyball_team) entered the podium after defeating the Dominican Republic 3–0 in the third place match.[^cite_note-6] The competition witnessed the first 25–0 set in international volleyball history, with [Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_women's_national_volleyball_team) winning the third set against [Trinidad and Tobago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidad_and_Tobago_women's_national_volleyball_team) by that scoreline.[^cite_note-7]

## Year change

On June 22, 2023, the *[Fédération Internationale de Volleyball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9d%C3%A9ration_Internationale_de_Volleyball)* (FIVB) announced a comprehensive restructuring of the international calendar for the 2025–2028 cycle. As part of this overhaul, continental championships shifted from odd-numbered years to even-numbered years, beginning in 2026.[^cite_note-8] The structural change was implemented to prioritize athlete health and recovery by reducing calendar congestion, as well as to streamline the qualification pathways for major global tournaments such as the [FIVB World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIVB_Women's_Volleyball_World_Cup) and the [Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volleyball_at_the_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-9]

## Format

The teams were drawn into two groups of four. The group winners advance directly to the semifinals. The second and third-placed teams from both groups advance to the quarterfinals. The bottom teams are dropped into the 7th place match.

### Tiebreakers

1. Number of matches won

2. Match points

3. Sets ratio

4. Points ratio

5. If the tie continues as per the point ratio between two teams, the priority will be given to the team which won the match between them. ## 2028 Summer Olympics qualifier

Unlike other continental championships, whose champions qualify directly for the [Olympic Games](22576.md), the [NORCECA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NORCECA) utilized a separate qualification process. The top four eligible teams—excluding the United States, which qualified automatically as the host nation—competed in a [single-elimination tournament](939680.md) to determine the NORCECA representative for the [2028 Summer Olympics](77464512.md).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]
