[Mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining) has been conducted on an industrial scale in present-day [Canada](5042916.md) since the late 18th century. The industry remains an important aspect of the [economy of Canada](5195.md) to this day, particularly in the [North](79987.md), and Canadian-domiciled mining companies have increasingly expanded their operations globally.

## History

The history of mining in Canada goes back to the 16th century. In the 1570s, [Martin Frobisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Frobisher) briefly attempted a mining operation on [Baffin Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baffin_Island), although it was unsuccessful.[^cite_note-\:1-1] In 1672, French settlers in [Cape Breton Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Breton_Island) detected [coal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal) deposits.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECranstone20022-2] [Matonabbee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matonabbee) and [Samuel Hearne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Hearne) sought after [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper) in the [Hudson Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Bay) region in the 1770s.[^cite_note-\:1-1]

The first truly industrial mining operation in what is now Canada was an [iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron) mine at [Forges du Saint-Maurice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forges_du_Saint-Maurice) near [Trois-Rivières](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trois-Rivi%C3%A8res) in Quebec, which remained a going concern from 1738 to 1883.[^cite_note-\:1-1] [Copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper) mining in [Bruce Mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Mines), Ontario—the first industrial-scale mine of a substance other than iron—followed in 1848.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECranstone20022-2] The Canadian mining industry continued to expand nationwide through the 19th century, and became one of the world's largest by the 20th century, particularly following [World War II](32927.md).[^cite_note-\:1-1]

Mills and Sweeney note that the [staples thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staples_thesis), which posits that the Canadian economy has developed primarily through the exploitation of the country's abundant natural resources, remains a viable model of Canadian [political economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_economy).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMillsSweeney201310–11-3]

## Operations

In 2019, Canada was the 4th largest producer of [platinum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platinum);[^cite_note-4] the world's 5th largest producer of [gold](12240.md);[^cite_note-5] the world's 5th largest producer of [nickel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nickel);[^cite_note-6] the world's 10th largest producer of [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper);[^cite_note-7] the 8th largest world producer of [iron ore](145352.md);[^cite_note-8] the 4th largest world producer of [titanium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titanium);[^cite_note-9] the world's largest producer of [potash](56509.md);[^cite_note-10] the 2nd largest world producer of [niobium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niobium);[^cite_note-11] the 4th largest world producer of [sulfur](27127.md);[^cite_note-12] the world's 7th largest producer of [molybdenum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molybdenum);[^cite_note-13] the 7th worldwide producer of [cobalt](24580536.md);[^cite_note-14] the 8th largest world producer of [lithium](17561.md);[^cite_note-15] the 8th largest world producer of [zinc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinc);[^cite_note-16] the 13th largest world producer of [gypsum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gypsum);[^cite_note-17] the 14th worldwide producer of [antimony](898.md);[^cite_note-18] the world's 10th largest producer of [graphite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphite);[^cite_note-19] in addition to being the 6th largest world producer of [salt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Table_salt).[^cite_note-20] It was the 2nd largest producer in the world of [uranium](31743.md) in 2018.[^cite_note-21]

## Economic impact

### Domestic

[Mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining) is a significant part of the [economy of Canada](5195.md). As of 2018, mining revenues totalled CA\$47 billion.[^cite_note-22] In 2013, over 50% of the world's publicly listed exploration and mining companies were headquartered in Canada.[^cite_note-23] [Toronto](64646.md) is a financial centre for the [mining industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining_industry): as of 2016, around 80 percent of the world's equity trades in mining stocks took place in Toronto's markets.[^cite_note-24]

In 2021, Canada's GDP totaled to CA\$1.8 trillion across all industries. \$156.5 billion of this (7.9%) is due to mining.[^cite_note-25] Mining's contribution to Canada's GDP is separated into extraction (\$36.1 billion), services (\$12 billion), primary manufacturing (\$17.7 billion), and downstream manufacturing (\$25 billion).[^cite_note-26]

The Canadian mining industry has experienced significant volatility in recent history. The 1980s and 1990s saw a "prolonged slump" in Canadian mining, whereas the 2000s and 2010s were largely boom periods.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKeelingSandlos20152-27]

[Saskatchewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saskatchewan) alone produces approximately 15 percent of the world's [uranium](31743.md).[^cite_note-\:0-28] The metal was first discovered in the province in the 1930s, and had become Canada's most valuable resource export by the 1950s.[^cite_note-\:0-28] In [Northern Canada](79987.md), mining—particularly [hardrock mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underground_mining_\(hard_rock\))—has long been one of the most significant sources of economic development.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKeelingSandlos20152-27][^cite_note-29]

Canada taxes mining companies at a relatively low level by international standards. Alam identifies this as one way Canada has established itself an attractive place for mining companies to do business.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEAlam20113-30]

### International

International expansion of the domestic mining industry has been championed by the government of Canada, and one scholar describes Canadian mining operations as having "developed an extensive and indeed dominant global presence".[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEButler20158-31] Canadian mining investment abroad has been particularly significant in Latin America and African countries.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELauzon2018146-32]
