On 1 March 2026,[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] the [Consulate General of the United States](41909779.md) in [Karachi](17123.md), Pakistan, was targeted by [Shia Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islam_in_Pakistan) protesters[^cite_note-AP-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] following [strikes on Iran by the US and Israel](82533582.md) in which Iranian supreme leader Ayatollah [Ali Khamenei was assassinated](82537558.md).[^cite_note-AJ-6][^cite_note-7] As the protesters assaulted the consulate and breached the outer wall, the [Marine Security Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_Security_Guard) opened fire, killing 10 protesters and injuring over 60.[^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-9]

Protesters set fire and broke windows during the attack. They attempted to enter the consulate and threw stones at [constables](18726843.md) and [Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Rangers), who responded by pushing them back. At one point, a protester opened fire with a pistol.[^cite_note-10] The police dispersed crowds with tear gas near Mai Kolachi Road, while protesters continued to throw stones along M. T. Khan Road.[^cite_note-11] Additionally, a traffic police post beneath the Sultanabad Bridge was set on fire.

Several roads, including those leading to Namish Chowrangi and from [Sultanabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanabad,_Karachi) to Mai Kolachi, were closed. Traffic from [Jinnah Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinnah_Bridge) was redirected to [I. I. Chundrigar Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I._I._Chundrigar_Road), and vehicles coming from [Boat Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boat_Basin) were forced to make a U-turn at Mai Kolachi Phatak.[^cite_note-12]

## Background

Main article: [Protests against the 2026 Iran war](82544763.md)

The protest was triggered by the assassination of Ayatollah [Ali Khamenei](385653.md), [Iran's supreme leader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran's_Supreme_Leader) since 1989, in [a series of US-Israeli airstrikes](82533582.md) on Iranian targets on 28 February 2026.[^cite_note-AJ-6][^cite_note-AP-3] Khamenei's death [sparked immediate outrage across the Shia Muslim world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro-Iranian_protests_during_the_2026_Israeli%E2%80%93United_States_strikes_on_Iran), leading to calls for demonstrations against the US and Israel.[^cite_note-Reuters-13]

In Pakistan, a country with a [significant Shia minority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islam_in_Pakistan), pro-Iranian groups mobilized quickly. Protests were organized in multiple cities, including [Karachi](17123.md), [Lahore](125315.md), and [Islamabad](51153.md), with demonstrators chanting anti-US and anti-Israel slogans.[^cite_note-Dawn-14] Protesters also accused the Pakistani government of siding with the US during the conflict.[^cite_note-\:0-8] The [US Consulate General in Karachi](41909779.md), located in the high-security zone, became a focal point due to its symbolic representation of American presence in the region.[^cite_note-AP-3]

## Protest

The demonstration began in the early morning of 1 March 2026, with hundreds of protesters gathering near the US Consulate on [Mai Kolachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mai_Kolachi) Road in Karachi.[^cite_note-15] Protesters, many affiliated with Shia organizations, carried banners condemning the US and Israel and expressed solidarity with Iran.[^cite_note-Independent-16] The crowd initially remained peaceful but grew agitated, leading to attempts to breach the consulate's outer wall.[^cite_note-Bloomberg-17]

Security forces, including the [Sindh Police](18726843.md) and [Pakistan Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Rangers), responded with [tear gas](1202646.md) and batons to prevent entry.[^cite_note-AJ-6] Clashes intensified as protesters threw stones, vandalized the building's exterior, and set fire to vehicles.[^cite_note-Dawn-14] Reports indicate that the US marines stationed at the embassy used gunfire to disperse the crowd, resulting in 10 fatalities and over 60 injuries among the protesters.[^cite_note-\:0-8]

The protest lasted several hours before authorities regained control.[^cite_note-Reuters-13] No breaches of the inner consulate were reported, and US staff remained safe.[^cite_note-Embassy-18]

## Aftermath

Following the protest, the [United States Embassy](32553327.md) in Islamabad issued a security alert and shut down all US diplomatic facilities across Pakistan. Three protesters were also killed near the embassy.[^cite_note-Independent-16][^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-19] Consulates in Karachi, [Lahore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consulate_General_of_the_United_States,_Lahore), and [Peshawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consulate_General_of_the_United_States,_Peshawar) were closed, and all appointments scheduled for 2 March 2026, were cancelled.[^cite_note-Embassy-18] US citizens were urged to stay away from large gatherings.[^cite_note-Bloomberg-17]
