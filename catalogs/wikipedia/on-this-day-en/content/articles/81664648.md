# 2025–26 Ethiopian Marburg virus disease outbreak

| 2025–26 Ethiopian Marburg virus disease outbreak |  |
| --- | --- |
| Disease | [Marburg virus disease](58743.md) |
| Location | [Ethiopia](187749.md) |
| First outbreak | [Jinka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinka), [South Ethiopia Regional State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Ethiopia_Regional_State) |
| Date | 12 November 2025 – 26 January 2026 |
| Type | [Viral hemorrhagic fever](1719309.md) |
| Confirmed cases | 14 |
| Recovered | 5 |
| Deaths | 9 |
| Fatality rate | 64% |

On 12 November 2025, the Ethiopian [Ministry of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_\(Ethiopia\)) and the [Ethiopian Public Health Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopian_Public_Health_Institute?action=edit\&redlink=1) (EPHI) announced a [viral hemorrhagic fever](1719309.md) (VHF), later confirmed as [Marburg virus disease](58743.md) originated from [Jinka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinka) town in [southern Ethiopia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Ethiopia).[^cite_note-\:0-1]

On 26 January 2026, the Ethiopian government announced the end of the virus outbreak following enhanced control measures and found no suspected case within 42 days.

## Timeline of outbreak

On 12 November 2025, WHO issued a press release from Ethiopian [Ministry of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_\(Ethiopia\)) (MoH), and the [Ethiopian Public Health Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopian_Public_Health_Institute?action=edit\&redlink=1) (EPHI) reporting viral hemorrhagic fever (VHF) cases in [Jinka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinka) town, [South Ethiopia Regional State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Ethiopia_Regional_State). On 14 November 2025, the Ministry of Health confirmed the VHF was [Marburg virus](33134774.md).&#x20;

Through 20 November 2025, 33 specimens had been tested; six were laboratory-confirmed Marburg virus disease (MVD), including three deaths. In addition to the laboratory-confirmed cases, three epidemiologically-linked suspected cases could not be tested.[^cite_note-\:1-2][^cite_note-3]

On 4 December 2025, the US [Administration for Strategic Preparedness and Response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_for_Strategic_Preparedness_and_Response) announced it would provide 2,500 doses of an investigational [vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marburg_vaccine) and 25 doses of an experimental [monoclonal antibody therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monoclonal_antibody_therapy) for treatment to the Ethiopian government.[^cite_note-4]

Through 22 December 2025, there were 14 MVD cases reported, which included nine deaths and five recoveries.[^cite_note-5]

On 26 January 2026, the Ethiopian government declared the end of virus outbreak after intensive surveillance and mandatory control measures.[^cite_note-6] A total of 14 confirmed cases, including nine deaths, were reported during the outbreak.
