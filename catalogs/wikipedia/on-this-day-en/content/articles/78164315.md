# Y Quynh Bđăp

**Y Quynh Bđăp** is a member of the [Ede ethnic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rade_people) from [Đắk Lắk province](1461530.md), one of the [Montagnard indigenous peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montagnard_\(Vietnam\)) native to Vietnam's [Central Highlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Highlands_\(Vietnam\)),[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:1-3] who co-founded the Montagnards Stand for Justice organization that supports [freedom of religion](46006.md) and Montagnard rights.[^cite_note-\:2-4] Recognized as a political refugee by the [United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees](31964.md), Y Quynh lived in Thailand between 2018 and his extradition in 2025.

## 2024 arrest

In January 2024, the Vietnamese government convicted Y Quynh in absentia of terrorism charges in connection to the [2023 Đắk Lắk attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_%C4%90%E1%BA%AFk_L%E1%BA%AFk_attacks), sentencing him to 10 years in prison.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-\:2-4]

On 11 June 2024, Y Quynh was arrested by immigration police in Bangkok[^cite_note-6] and held at [Bangkok Remand Prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klong_Prem_Central_Prison). In October 2024, Quynh's extradition to Vietnam was approved by the Criminal Court in Bangkok,[^cite_note-\:0-1] whose decision was criticized by Thailand's National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) and Human Rights Watch (HRW) which both state that he faces torture if extradited.[^cite_note-\:1-3][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

On 28 November 2025, according to his lawyer, Y Quynh was extradited to Vietnam.[^cite_note-9]
