**Bandar-e Mahshahr** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): بندرماهشهر)[^cite_note-4] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Bandar_Mahshahr_County\)) of [Bandar Mahshahr County](7349548.md), [Khuzestan province](448079.md), [Iran](14653.md), serving as capital of both the county and the district.[^cite_note-Khuzestan_Province_Structure-5]

## Demographics

Most of its people speak a dialect that was a mixture of standard Persian, Southern [Luri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luri_language) and [Bushehri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bushehri) Persian, which is still spoken by most people, taught by the elderly people to the younger generations (the dialect though still thrives in [Hendijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendijan) and especially [Genaaveh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genaaveh?action=edit\&redlink=1)). So now Mahshahrians are still mainly [Lur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luri_language)-speaking. Local Persians are mostly of Behbahani, Qanavati, and Bandari families. There is also a local [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic)-speaking minority whose roots go back to [Qabban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qabban?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Iraq.[^cite_note-6]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 109,927 in 24,110 households.[^cite_note-2006_Khuzestan_Province-7] The following census in 2011 counted 153,778 people in 38,301 households.[^cite_note-2011_Khuzestan_Province-8] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 162,797 people in 45,208 households.[^cite_note-2016_Khuzestan_Province-2]

## Climate

On July 31, 2015, at around 4\:30 PM [Iran Daylight Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_Daylight_Time) (3\:10 PM [apparent solar time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apparent_solar_time)), the air temperature measured at the Bandar-e Mahshahr airport was 46 °C (115 °F), the [dew point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dew_point) was 32 °C (90 °F), and the [relative humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_humidity) was 49%. This corresponds to a [wet-bulb temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet-bulb_temperature) of 34.6 °C (94.3 °F), slightly below the 35 °C (95 °F) mark that is considered the maximum humans can tolerate, above which extended exposure will lead to death.[^cite_note-heatindex-10][^cite_note-11] Together, the city had a [heat index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_index) of 74 °C (165 °F), the second highest heat index ever recorded anywhere in the world.[^cite_note-heatindex-10]

## Port and special economic zone

The port of Bandar-e Mahshahr is immediately adjacent to the East of the port of [Bandar-e Emam Khomeyni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandar-e_Emam_Khomeyni). It is specialized in oil and petrochemical products exports. It includes a [special economic zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_economic_zone) used by the [National Iranian Petrochemical Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Iranian_Petrochemical_Company) and the National Petrochemical Company (NPC), the Special industrial economic zones 'Petzone'.

The port of Bandar-e Mahshahr is mostly used by tanker ships and is accessible through the same channel as the port of [Bandar-e Emam Khomeyni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandar-e_Emam_Khomeyni), i.e. the 42 miles long, 45 meter deep Khor Musa channel.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

## Universities

Mahshahr has two universities. Islamic Azad University of Mahshahr and [Amirkabir University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amirkabir_University_of_Technology), Mahshahr campus. Both universities concentrate on engineering programs, especially petroleum and petrochemical engineering.

## 2026 conflict

In April 2026, during the [US-Israel war on Iran](82533582.md), airstrikes targeted the [special petrochemical zones of Mahshahr and Bandar Imam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marun_petrochemical_company) in Iran. Iranian media reports indicated that a minimum of five individuals lost their lives, while approximately 170 others sustained injuries as a result of the assaults on these regions.[^cite_note-20]
