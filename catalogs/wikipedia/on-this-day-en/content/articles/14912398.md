**Urgun** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): اورګون ولسوالۍ, [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): ولسوالی اورگون) is a district of the remote [Paktika Province](457294.md) in [Afghanistan](737.md).

## Administrative seat

The administrative seat of this district is the like-named town of [Urgun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urgun), also known as *Loy Urgun*, meaning "Greater Urgun".

## History

Urgun town used to be the provincial capital until it was replaced by [Sharana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharana,_Afghanistan) in the 1970s due to its proximity to the main highway, connecting it to [Kabul](16826.md), [Ghazni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghazni), and [Kandahar](17260.md).

The [Siege of Urgun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Urgun) took place between 1983 and 1984.

On October 17, 2025, in the village of Khandaro, the [Pakistan Air Force](377433.md) killed 17 Afghan civilians, including three cricket players, and injured 16 more Afghans.[^cite_note-pakistansairstrikekills-1][^cite_note-lokmatdefenceminister-2]

## Demographics

The largest tribe in Urgun are the [Tajik formuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajik_formuli?action=edit\&redlink=1),[^cite_note-3] Other tribes include the [Kharoti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharoti), [Sulaimankhel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulaimankhel), and [Wazir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wazir_\(tribe\)).[^cite_note-4] There are also [Uzbeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbeks) and [Sayed People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayed_People?action=edit\&redlink=1) in smaller numbers.

Like many place names in [Afghanistan](737.md), Urgun can be spelled a number of different ways. "Urgon", "Orgun" and "Orgon", however, remain the most popular alternative spellings on maps and official documents.
