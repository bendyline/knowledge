**Wadi Fira** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وادي فيرا, [romanised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Wādī Fīrā*)[^cite_note-Chad2-4][^cite_note-5] is one of the 23 [provinces of Chad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Chad). Its [capital](181337.md) is the town of [Biltine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biltine,_Chad).[^cite_note-Chad3-6] The province corresponds with the former [prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_of_Chad) of [Biltine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biltine_Prefecture).[^cite_note-Decree419-7]

## Geography



[Water scarcity](15380061.md).

The province borders [Borkou Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borkou_Region), [Ennedi-Ouest Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ennedi-Ouest_Region) and [Ennedi-Est Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ennedi-Est_Region) to the north, [Sudan](27421.md) to the east, [Ouaddaï Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouadda%C3%AF_Region) to the south, and [Batha Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batha_Region) to the west. The terrain is [savanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savanna), merging into the [Sahara Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahara_Desert) in the north, and rising to the east.

### Settlements

[Biltine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biltine,_Chad) is the province's capital; other major settlements include [Guéréda](4939966.md), [Iriba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iriba) and [Matadjana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matadjana).[^cite_note-8]

## Demographics

As per the 2009 Chadian census, Wadi Fira's population is 508,383.[^cite_note-census-2] The main ethnolinguistic groups are the [Amdang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amdang_people), [Baggara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baggara) (generally speakers of [Chadian Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chadian_Arabic)), [Maba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maba_people), [Mararit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mararit_people), [Tama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tama_people) and [Zaghawa](1467480.md).[^cite_note-9]
