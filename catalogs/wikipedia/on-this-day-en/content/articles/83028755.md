The **ALMA – Citizens for Cyprus** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): ΑΛΜΑ – Πολίτες για την Κύπρο, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Greek): *ALMA – Polítes gia tin Kýpro*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Leap') is a [centrist](39619035.md) [political party in Cyprus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_political_parties_in_Cyprus) founded in 2025 by former Auditor General [Odysseas Michaelides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odysseas_Michaelides).[^cite_note-CyprusMailLaunch-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3]

## History

### Background

On 18 September 2024, [Odysseas Michaelides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odysseas_Michaelides) was dismissed from his position as Auditor General by Supreme Constitutional Court on charges of misconduct. Michaelides held the post for 10 years and became a controversial figure in Cypriot politics, repeatedly clashing with government officials. Michaelides himself accused ex-President [Nicos Anastasiades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicos_Anastasiades) and current President [Nikos Christodoulides](58652814.md) of staying behind his removal. Michaelides' resignation was criticized by Cypriot parties such as [AKEL](356678.md), [KOSP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_of_Ecologists_%E2%80%93_Citizens'_Cooperation), [DIKO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Cyprus\)), and [Volt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volt_Cyprus), who characterized him as an official who exposed corruption and advocated for transparency.[^cite_note-4]

### Founding

Several months after his removal, on 19 May 2025, Michaelides launched his party, ALMA – Citizens for Cyprus. It was established to contest the [2026 Cypriot legislative election](75850208.md) and has presented itself as a long-term reform movement.[^cite_note-CyprusMailLaunch-1][^cite_note-5] The party's name, which translates as "leap," is also an acronym for Greek words meaning Dignity, Accountability, Reform, and Development. At the party's launch in [Nicosia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicosia), Michaelides was accompanied by eight of his associates, including former Rector of the [University of Cyprus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cyprus) [Constantinos Christofides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantinos_Christofides?action=edit\&redlink=1), who ran in the [2023 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Cypriot_presidential_election) and finished sixth with 1.59% of the vote.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Ideology

The party describes itself as belonging to the “[reformist centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reformism_\(Centre_politics\))”, promoting institutional reform, transparency, accountability, the rule of law, a socially responsible but competitive economy, participatory democracy, and strong protection of human rights. Its ideology can therefore be broadly characterised as centrist, reformist, socially liberal, pro-rule of law, and anti-corruption.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

The party was described by [Europe Elects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe_Elects) as [centrist](39619035.md).[^cite_note-8]
