The **Supreme National Security Council** (**SNSC**; [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): شورای عالی امنیت ملی, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Showrā-ye Āli-ye Amniyat-e Mellī*), also known as the **Supreme Council for National Security**_,_ is the [national security council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_security_council) of the [Islamic Republic of Iran](14653.md). The council was formed for the protection and support of national interests and Islamic revolution and territorial integrity and national sovereignty of the country. This institution was founded during the [1989 revision of the constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Iranian_constitutional_referendum).[^cite_note-2]

[Ali Larijani](531848.md), secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, was [assassinated on 17 March 2026](82710049.md). He was succeeded on 24 March by [Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr](54308220.md), then secretary of the [Expediency Discernment Council](452730.md). Zolghadr remained in office until 9 August 2026, when he was succeeded by the current secretary, [Mohsen Rezaee](1862765.md).

The Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council is appointed by the [President of Iran](233913.md), currently [Masoud Pezeshkian](2650383.md), by presidential decree.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Functions

The responsibilities of the council are defined by the constitution as:&#x20;

1. Determining the defense and national security policies of the country within the framework of general policies determined by the Supreme Leader.

2. Coordination of political activities, intelligence, social, cultural and economic fields relating to general policies of defense and national security.

3. Exploitation of material and intellectual resources of the country for facing the internal and external threats.

### Nuclear policy

Supreme National Security Council formulates the country's nuclear policy. The nuclear policies formulated by the council would become effective if they are confirmed by the Supreme Leader.[^cite_note-5] The secretary of the Supreme council was the chief nuclear negotiator of Iran until 5 September 2013 when responsibility for nuclear talks was assigned to [the ministry of foreign affairs](1166971.md).[^cite_note-6]

## Composition

This National Security Council is mandated by Article 176 of the [Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Islamic_Republic_of_Iran) to be presided over by the [president](233913.md) of the country.[^cite_note-7] The [President](14653.md) appoints the Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council through an official decree.[^cite_note-8] The decisions of the Council are effective after the confirmation by the Supreme Leader.[^cite_note-9] Supreme Leader [Mojtaba Khamenei](23324096.md) and the Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) are at the top of the foreign policy decisions process.
