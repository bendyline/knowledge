**Dumeir**, also **Dumair**, **Damir** and **Dumayr** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الضمير) is a city located 45 kilometers north-east of [Damascus](8914.md), [Syria](7515849.md).

## Archaeology

An altar dedicated to the Semitic deity, [Baalshamin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baalshamin) in 94 CE, now in the [Institut du Monde Arabe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_du_Monde_Arabe) in Paris, indicates that a [Nabatean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabatean) religious building previously stood on the site.

There is a reference to a building in a lawsuit in 216, however in 245 CE, in the reign of the [Roman Emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Emperor) [Philip the Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_the_Arab), the Roman [Temple of Dumeir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Zeus_Hypsistos), located in the center of the old town, was dedicated to [Zeus Hypsistos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeus). The shape is highly unusual, and construction may have commenced as a public fountain or staging post, but in its final form it is clearly a temple.&#x20;

It was fortified in the Arab period, the arch on the rear wall being filled in with stones and defensive devices. The temple has been restored as the result of much research and reconstruction work.[^cite_note-1]

The [Ghassanid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghassanid) [phylarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phylarch) (tribal king) [al-Mundhir III ibn al-Harith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Mundhir_III_ibn_al-Harith) built a tower at Dumayr. A Greek inscription engraved by al-Mundhir credits himself for its construction and thanks God and [St. Julian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_of_Antioch).[^cite_note-2] A monastery associated with the Ghassanids called Dayr al-Matirun, likely an Arabicized version of the Greek [martyrion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martyrion), existed about 3 kilometers (1.9 mi) east of Dumayr.[^cite_note-3]

## Facilities

A cemetery was built in 1960 for the French casualties of WWI and WWII in Al-Dumayr.[^cite_note-4] The [Syrian Arab Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Arab_Air_Force) [Al-Dumayr Military Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Dumayr_Military_Airport) is also located in Al-Dumayr.
