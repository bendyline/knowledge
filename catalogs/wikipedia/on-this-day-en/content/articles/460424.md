**Ciego de Ávila** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˈsjeɣo ðe ˈaβila\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is one of the provinces of [Cuba](5042481.md), and was previously part of [Camagüey Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camag%C3%BCey_Province). Its capital is [Ciego de Ávila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciego_de_%C3%81vila), which lies on the [Carretera Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carretera_Central_\(Cuba\)) (central highway), and the second city is [Morón](4792266.md), further north.

The province was separated from [Camagüey Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camag%C3%BCey_Province) in 1976 by the government.

## Geography

Off the north coast of the province, some ([cays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cay)) of the [Jardines del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jardines_del_Rey) archipelago are being developed as [tourist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism) resorts, principally [Cayo Coco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayo_Coco) and [Cayo Guillermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayo_Guillermo). The south coast is characterised by [mangroves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangrove).

Between Morón and the north coast are several lakes, including the [Laguna de Leche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_de_Leche) (the *Lagoon of Milk*, so called for its white appearance because of large [lime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcium_oxide) deposits underwater) which is the largest natural lake in Cuba.

## Economy

Central Ciego de Ávila is used for [cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle) ranching, elsewhere in the province [sugar](27712.md), [pineapples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pineapple) and [citrus fruit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citrus_fruit) are grown. Pineapples are the staple crop, but sweet potatoes, potatoes, yuca, plantains, and bananas are also cultivated for national consumption.

## Demographics

In 2004, the province of Ciego de Ávila had a population of 416,370.[^cite_note-atenas-3] With a total area of 6,783.13 km<sup>2</sup> (2,618.98 sq mi),[^cite_note-census2002-5] the province had a population density of 61.4/km<sup>2</sup> (159/sq mi).
