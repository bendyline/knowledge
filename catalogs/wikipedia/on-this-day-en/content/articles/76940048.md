The **2025 Copa CONMEBOL Libertadores** was the 66th edition of the [CONMEBOL Libertadores](299730.md) (also referred to as the *Copa Libertadores*), [South America](26769.md)'s premier club [football](10568.md) tournament organized by [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL). The competition began on 4 February and ended on 29 November 2025,[^cite_note-1] with [the final](76940330.md) played at [Estadio Monumental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_Monumental_%22U%22) in [Lima](85423.md), Peru.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

Brazilian club [Flamengo](187239.md) won their fourth Copa Libertadores title, defeating fellow Brazilian side [Palmeiras](187243.md) 1–0 in the final.[^cite_note-4] As winners of the 2025 Copa Libertadores, Flamengo earned the right to play against the winners of the [2025 Copa Sudamericana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copa_Sudamericana) in the [2026 Recopa Sudamericana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Recopa_Sudamericana). They also automatically qualified for the [2025 FIFA Intercontinental Cup](76156932.md), the [2029 FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2029_FIFA_Club_World_Cup) and the [2026 Copa Libertadores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Copa_Libertadores) group stage.

[Botafogo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botafogo_de_Futebol_e_Regatas) were the defending champions, but were eliminated by [LDU Quito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.D.U._Quito) in the round of 16.

With Flamengo's triumph, Brazil tied Argentina as the nation with the most Copa Libertadores championships with 25 titles. This was as well the seventh consecutive edition won by Brazilian clubs.[^cite_note-5]

## Teams

The following 47 teams from the 10 CONMEBOL member associations qualified for the tournament:

- Copa Libertadores champions

- Copa Sudamericana champions

- Brazil: 7 berths

- Argentina: 6 berths

- All other associations: 4 berths each

The entry stage was determined as follows:[^cite_note-regulations-6]

- Group stage: 28 teams&#x20;

  - Copa Libertadores champions

  - Copa Sudamericana champions

  - Teams which qualified for berths 1–5 from Argentina and Brazil

  - Teams which qualified for berths 1–2 from all other associations

- Second stage: 13 teams&#x20;

  - Teams which qualified for berths 6–7 from Brazil

  - Team which qualified for berth 6 from Argentina

  - Teams which qualified for berths 3–4 from Chile and Colombia

  - Teams which qualified for berth 3 from all other associations

- First stage: 6 teams&#x20;

  - Teams which qualified for berth 4 from Bolivia, Ecuador, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay and Venezuela

The draw for the qualifying stages was held on 19 December 2024, 12\:00 [PYT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Paraguay) ([UTC−3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC%E2%88%9203\:00)), at the CONMEBOL Convention Centre in [Luque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luque), [Paraguay](23420.md).[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

Teams were seeded by their *CONMEBOL Clubs ranking* as of 16 December 2024 (shown in parentheses),[^cite_note-ranking-23] taking into account the following three factors:[^cite_note-24]

1. Performance in the last 10 years, taking into account Copa Libertadores and Copa Sudamericana results in the period 2015–2024.

2. Historical coefficient, taking into account Copa Libertadores and Copa Sudamericana results in the period 1960–2014 and 2002–2014 respectively.

3. Local tournament champion, with bonus points awarded to domestic league champions of the last 10 years.

For the first stage, the six teams were drawn into three ties (E1–E3), with the teams from Pot 1 hosting the second leg.[^cite_note-draw_procedure-18]

## Qualifying stages

Main article: [2025 Copa Libertadores qualifying stages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copa_Libertadores_qualifying_stages)

In the qualifying stages, each tie was played on a home-and-away [two-legged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-legged_tie) basis. If tied on aggregate, [extra time](633242.md#Association_football) was not played, and a [penalty shoot-out](511302.md) was used to determine the winner (Regulations Article 2.4.3).[^cite_note-regulations-6] The qualifying stages were structured as follows:

- **First stage (6 teams):** The three winners of the first stage advanced to the second stage to join the 13 teams which were given byes to the second stage.

- **Second stage (16 teams):** The eight winners of the second stage advanced to the third stage.

- **Third stage (8 teams):** The four winners of the third stage advanced to the group stage to join the 28 direct entrants. The four teams eliminated in the third stage entered the [Copa Sudamericana group stage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copa_Sudamericana_group_stage).

## Group stage

Main article: [2025 Copa Libertadores group stage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copa_Libertadores_group_stage)

In the group stage, each group was played on a home-and-away [round-robin](939742.md) basis. Teams were ranked according to the following criteria: 1. [Points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Points_\(association_football\)) (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss); 2. [Goal difference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_difference); 3. Goals scored; 4. Away goals scored; 5. CONMEBOL ranking (Regulations Article 2.4.2).[^cite_note-regulations-6]

The winners and runners-up of each group advanced to the [round of 16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copa_Libertadores_final_stages#Round_of_16) of the [final stages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copa_Libertadores_final_stages). The third-placed teams of each group entered the [knockout round play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copa_Sudamericana_final_stages#Knockout_round_play-offs) of the [2025 Copa Sudamericana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copa_Sudamericana).

## Final stages

Main article: [2025 Copa Libertadores final stages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copa_Libertadores_final_stages)

Starting from the round of 16, the teams played a [single-elimination tournament](939680.md) with the following rules:[^cite_note-regulations-6]

- In the round of 16, quarter-finals and semi-finals, each tie was played on a home-and-away [two-legged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-legged_tie) basis, with the higher-seeded team hosting the second leg (Regulations Article 2.2.3.2). If tied on aggregate, [extra time](633242.md#Association_football) would not be played, and a [penalty shoot-out](511302.md) would be used to determine the winners (Regulations Article 2.4.3).

- The final was played as a single match at a venue pre-selected by CONMEBOL, with the higher-seeded team designated as the "home" team for administrative purposes (Regulations Article 2.2.3.5). If tied after regulation, 30 minutes of [extra time](633242.md#Association_football) would be played. If still tied after extra time, a [penalty shoot-out](511302.md) would be used to determine the winners (Regulations Article 2.4.4).
