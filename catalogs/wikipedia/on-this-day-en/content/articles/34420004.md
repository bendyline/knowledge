**Central Darfur State** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ولاية وسط دارفور, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Wilāyat Wasaṭ Dārfūr*) is a [state](579458.md) in south-western [Sudan](27421.md), and one of five comprising the [Darfur](605837.md) region. The state borders [North Darfur](767666.md) to the north, [South Darfur](767650.md) to the east, [Central African Republic](5478.md) to the south, [Chad](5488.md) and [West Darfur](767645.md) to the west. It was created in January 2012 as a result of the ongoing peace process for the wider Darfur region.[^cite_note-2] The state capital is [Zalingei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zalingei). The state was formed from land that had been part of the states of [West Darfur](767645.md) and [South Darfur](767650.md).

On 4 August 2023, the entire state was confirmed fallen, and claimed by the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) (RSF) after their clashes with [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) (SAF), leading to collapse of the state's capital, [Zalingei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zalingei).[^cite_note-3]
