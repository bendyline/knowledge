**Kasaï** is one of the 21 [provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo](3984115.md) created in the [2015 repartitioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_the_DR_Congo#New_provinces). Kasaï and [Kasaï-Central](4225845.md) provinces are the result of the partitioning of the former [Kasaï-Occidental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasa%C3%AF-Occidental) province.[^cite_note-2] Kasaï was formed from the [Kasaï district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasa%C3%AF_District) and the independently administered city of [Tshikapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tshikapa) which became the capital of the new province.
