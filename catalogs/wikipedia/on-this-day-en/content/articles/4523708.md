**Vatomandry** ([\[vatʷˈmanɖʐʲ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Malagasy)) is a town in and the administrative center of the [Vatomandry District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatomandry_District), [Atsinanana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atsinanana) Region, [Madagascar](18964.md).[^cite_note-ref1-1] An [airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatomandry_Airport?action=edit\&redlink=1) serves the city.

## Etymology and location

Located on the coast, the town's name means 'Sleeping Rocks', derived from two black rocks near the shore. It is also on the path of the [Canal des Pangalanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canal_des_Pangalanes) and [National Road RN 11a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Route_nationale_11a_\(Madagascar\))

## History

In the pre-colonial era of the 19th century, Vatomandry was a center of [Hova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hova_\(Madagascar\)) government with an active port.[^cite_note-bradt-2]

[Cyclone Manou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Manou) caused great damage in 2003 to the town and left 68 people dead.[^cite_note-bradt-2] [Cyclone Giovanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Giovanna) in 2012 also caused significant damage.[^cite_note-giov-3]
