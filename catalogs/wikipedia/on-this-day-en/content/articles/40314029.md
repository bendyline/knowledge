The **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare**[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] ([Bengali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_language): স্বাস্থ্য ও পরিবার কল্যাণ মন্ত্রণালয়; *Sbāsthya ō paribār kalyāṇ Montronaloya*) is a [Bangladesh government](18420484.md) [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) charged with [health policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_policy) in [Bangladesh](3454.md). It is also responsible for all government programs relating to [family planning in Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_Bangladesh). The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare contains two divisions:[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] [Health Services Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_Services_Division) and [Medical Education And Family Welfare Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_Education_And_Family_Welfare_Division).
