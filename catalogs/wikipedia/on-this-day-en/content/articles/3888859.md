**Wau** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): واو, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *_Wāw_*; also known as **Wau Town Payam**)[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEPSI_et_al.20144-2] is a town, locally referred to as a city, in northwestern [South Sudan](32350676.md) on the western bank of the [Jur River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jur_River). It is the capital of [Western Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Bahr_el_Ghazal) region in South Sudan.[^cite_note-3] It lies approximately 650 kilometres (400 mi) northwest of the capital, [Juba](1514879.md).[^cite_note-4] It is a diverse small urban center (town) and a trading hub. The city has been a municipality since 2012 and is governed by a mayor whom the state governor usually appoints. The city consists of several neighborhoods, including Nazareth, Hai Fahal, Sika Hadid, and Daraja.

## History

Wau was initially established by the French [Marchand Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marchand_Mission) as Fort Desaix[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] and later was established as a *[zariba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zariba)* (fortified base) by [slave-traders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavery_in_Sudan) in the 19th century. During the time of [condominium rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Egyptian_Sudan), the city became an administrative center.

One of the first insurgent [Anyanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyanya) attacks on the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) (SAF) took place at the Wau barracks in January 1964.

During the [Second Sudanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sudanese_Civil_War), Wau remained a SAF [garrison town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garrison). It was the scene of extensive fighting in the spring of 1998. Battles erupted again in the town in the spring of the 1980s, killing several hundred people. This forced the Dinka in Wau to seek safety on the eastern side of Wau. The Dinka were said to have migrated to the state today known as Warrap.

In 2010, the [Ministry of Housing, Physical Planning and Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Housing,_Physical_Planning_and_Environment_\(South_Sudan\)) proposed to re-make the city into the shape of a giraffe.[^cite_note-7]

The refugee camp at Wau town's Catholic cathedral, where around 8,500 [IDPs](427408.md) found shelter during the [2016–18 Wau clashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016%E2%80%9318_Wau_clashes)

[^cite_note-idps_church-8]

Following the outbreak of the [South Sudanese Civil War](41382446.md), the town has experienced numerous clashes, battles, massacres, and much destruction at the hands of anti-government as well as government forces. In April 2014, [Nuer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuer_people) soldiers belonging to the local [SPLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_People's_Liberation_Army) garrison mutinied after hearing of a massacre at Mapel. They clashed with SPLA loyalists, and then fled into the rural countryside, joining a [long march of other deserters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_retreat_from_Western_Bahr_el_Ghazal) to Sudan.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESmall_Arms_Survey2014-9] About 700 Nuer civilians subsequently sought protection at Wau's [UNMISS base](33447906.md); most of them were family members of the deserted soldiers, while others were students.[^cite_note-10]

In 2016, Wau experienced [heavy clashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016%E2%80%9318_Wau_clashes#Battle_in_Wau_town) that displaced much of its [Fertit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C4%81r_Fertit) population and led to widespread destruction.[^cite_note-11] In April 2017, Dinka soldiers of the SPLA and [Mathiang Anyoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathiang_Anyoor) militiamen carried out a [massacre of non-Dinka civilians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016%E2%80%9318_Wau_clashes#Government_recapture_of_Baggari_and_massacre_in_Wau_town) in the town, killing up to 50 people,[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] and displacing thousands.[^cite_note-14]

## Population

The population of Wau is ethnically diverse. Most of the inhabitants are [Luo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luo_people) and [Fertit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fertit_people), as the town lies on the tribal boundary between these two peoples.[^cite_note-gunfight_trainees-15] Furthermore, minorities belonging to the [Dinka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinka_people) of Marial Baai, peoples can be found in Wau.[^cite_note-16] Due to its diversity, Wau has repeatedly suffered from [ethnic violence](32840268.md).[^cite_note-idps_church-8][^cite_note-april_fighting-17][^cite_note-uniforms_massacre-18]

### Demographics

In 2008, Wau was the third-largest city in South Sudan, by population, behind the national capital [Juba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juba,_South_Sudan) and [Malakal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malakal), in [Upper Nile State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Nile_\(state\)). At that time, the estimated population of the city of Wau was about 128,100.[^cite_note-19] In 2011, the city's population was estimated at 151,320.

### Religion

Its [Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral) of St. Mary (built 1905, before the erection of the former [Apostolic Prefecture of Bahr el-Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Prefecture_of_Bahr_el-Ghazal)) is the episcopal see of the [Roman Catholic Diocese of Wau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Wau), which serves the province's religious majority.

## Geography and climate

Like other parts of South Sudan and the [East Sudanian savanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Sudanian_savanna), Wau has a [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Aw*), with a wet season and a dry season, and the temperature is hot year-round. The average annual mean temperature is 27.8 °C (82.0 °F), the average annual high temperature is 34.7 °C (94.5 °F), and the average annual low temperature is 20.9 °C (69.6 °F). The hottest time of year is from March to May, just before the wet season starts. March is the hottest month, having the highest average high at 38.1 °C (100.6 °F) and the highest mean at 30.4 °C (86.7 °F). April has the highest average low at 23.8 °C (74.8 °F). August and July have the lowest average high at 31.4 °C (88.5 °F), with August having the lowest mean at 26.2 °C (79.2 °F). December has the lowest average low at 17.9 °C (64.2 °F).

Wau receives 1,074.5 millimetres (42.30 in) of rain over 102.4 precipitation days, with a distinct wet and dry season like most tropical savanna climates. Almost no rain falls from November to March. August, the wettest month, receives 192.3 millimetres (7.57 in) of rainfall on average. September has 23.7 precipitation days, which is the most of any month. Humidity is much higher in the wet season than in the dry season, with February having a humidity of just 26% and August having a humidity of 77%. Wau receives 2777 hours of sunshine annually on average, with the sunshine being distributed fairly evenly across the year, although it is lower during the wet season. December receives the most sunshine, while July receives the least.
