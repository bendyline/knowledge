**Achi-Su** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Ачи-Су), also known as **Achisu** (Ачису),[^cite_note-AltNm-11] is an [urban locality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) (an [urban-type settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban-type_settlement)) in [Karabudakhkentsky District](24991231.md) of the [Republic of Dagestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Dagestan), Russia. As of the [2010 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Census_\(2010\)), its population was 1,679.[^cite_note-2010Census-3]

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), the [urban-type settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban-type_settlement#Inhabited_localities) of Achi-Su is incorporated within [Karabudakhkentsky District](24991231.md) as **Achi-Su [Settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban-type_settlement#Administrative_divisions)** (an administrative division of the district).[^cite_note-RDagestan_admlaw-2] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), Achi-Su Settlement is incorporated within Karabudakhkentsky Municipal District as **Achi-Su Urban Settlement**.[^cite_note-RDagestan_munlist-4]
