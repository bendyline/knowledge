&#x20;**Palau–United States relations** are [bilateral relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilateral_relations) between the sovereign nations of [Palau](23372.md) and the [United States](3434750.md).

## History

Main article: [History of Palau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Palau)



US Secretary of State [Mike Pompeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Pompeo) and Palau Vice President [Raynold Oilouch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raynold_Oilouch) in 2019

Relations between [Palau](23372.md) and the [United States](3434750.md) are strong, and the two nations cooperate on a number of issues. Palau is a [sovereign state](23604120.md) in [free association](346414.md) with the United States. On October 1, 1994, after five decades of US administration, the country of Palau became the last component of the [Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trust_Territory_of_the_Pacific_Islands) to gain its independence. In 1978, Palau decided not to join the Federated States of Micronesia, due to culture and language differences, and instead sought independence. In 1986, a [Compact of Free Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_of_Free_Association) between Palau and the United States was approved, paving the way for Palau's independence.[^cite_note-1] Finally ratified in 1993, the agreement [came into force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming_into_force) on October 1, 1994,[^cite_note-2] the date of Palau's independence. Under the Compact, the US remains responsible for Palau's defense for 50 years, but only a small number of Navy Seabees are currently stationed in Palau.[^cite_note-3]

In June 2009, Palauan President [Johnson Toribiong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnson_Toribiong) accepted to "temporarily resettle" "up to seventeen" non-combatant [Uyghur detainees from Guantanamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uyghur_captives_in_Guantanamo), at the United States' request.[^cite_note-4]

In a 2009 interview with [ABC Radio Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABC_Radio_Australia), Palauan Minister of State [Sandra Pierantozzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Pierantozzi) "admitted that \[her] Government supports [United States' embargo against Cuba](1105954.md) to coax more aid from the United States". She told Radio Australia's James Oaten: "We have a very strong relationship with the United States and we don't want to jeopardise that relationship, because it would affect Palau's economic welfare."[^cite_note-5]



[U.S. Secretary of State](32293.md) [Antony Blinken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antony_Blinken) meets with [President of Palau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Palau) [Surangel Whipps Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surangel_Whipps_Jr.), at the [Department of State](31975.md) in Washington, D.C., in August 2021.

In 2015, however, Palau voted to condemn the [US embargo over Cuba](1105954.md). The motion at the United Nations was supported by 191 member states, with two votes against (the United States and Israel) and no country abstaining.[^cite_note-6]

In September 2020, President [Tommy Remengesau Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Remengesau_Jr.) invited the United States, through [US Defense Secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Defense_Secretary) [Mark Esper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Esper), to build joint-use military facilities and use them.[^cite_note-7] Remengesau also suggested a [US Coast Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Coast_Guard) presence in Palau to help patrol its vast marine reserve.[^cite_note-8]

In July 2025, the United States asked Palau to host asylum seekers as part of the [policy on immigration of President Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immigration_policy_of_the_second_Donald_Trump_administration). Both, the [traditional chiefs of Palau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_chiefs_of_Palau) and the [Palau National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palau_National_Congress) have issued a formal advice to president [Surangel Whipps Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surangel_Whipps_Jr.) to reject said proposal.[^cite_note-9]
