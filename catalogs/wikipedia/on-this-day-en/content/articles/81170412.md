**Siniša Karan** ([Serbian Cyrillic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Cyrillic_alphabet): Синиша Каран; born 17 May 1962) is a [Bosnian Serb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbs_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) politician serving as [President of Republika Srpska](15903684.md) since February 2026. He previously served as [Minister of Scientific-Technological Development and Higher Education of Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Republika_Srpska) from 2025 to 2026. A member of the [Alliance of Independent Social Democrats](4358875.md), he also served as [Minister of Interior of Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_\(Republika_Srpska\)) from 2022 to 2025.

## Early life and education

Karan was born on 17 May 1962 in the village of [Grabovac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grabovac,_Osijek-Baranja_County) near [Beli Manastir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beli_Manastir) in [FPR Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia), present-day Croatia.[^cite_note-1] His father is an ethnic [Serb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbs_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) from the village of [Rašćani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C5%A1%C4%87ani,_Tomislavgrad) in the present-day [Municipality of Tomislavgrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_Tomislavgrad), and mother, an ethnic [Croat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croats_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina), from [Kupres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kupres).[^cite_note-2] Karan finished elementary and high school in Kupres and earned a bachelor's degree in political science at the [University of Sarajevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sarajevo) and a master's degree in law at the [University of Banja Luka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Banja_Luka), where he also earned a PhD. He is a full professor at two private law faculties, at the Apeiron University of Banja Luka and the Independent University of Banja Luka.[^cite_note-Nezavinse-3][^cite_note-Klix-4]

## Career

From 1985 to 2008, Karan served as a policeman. He was a commander of the police station in Kupres, secretary of the Ministry of the Interior for Kupres and chief of the Criminal Department for the [Sarajevo](26786.md) Centre. Following the [Bosnian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnian_War), he was the chief of the Office for Crime Prevention at the [Ministry of Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_\(Republika_Srpska\)) of [Republika Srpska](347422.md), an entity of [Bosnia and Herzegovina](3463.md). He was also the chief of criminal investigations for the [State Investigation and Protection Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Investigation_and_Protection_Agency) (SIPA) and the chief of its Financial Intelligence Department. From 2008, Karan served as advisor for security to the [President of Republika Srpska](15903684.md) and from 2009 to 2019, he was the President's general secretary. From January 2019, he was the general secretary of the [Government of Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Republika_Srpska).[^cite_note-Nezavinse-3][^cite_note-Klix-4]

On 21 December 2022, Karan was appointed Minister of the Interior of Republika Srpska.[^cite_note-Nezavinse-3] On 2 September 2025, he was appointed [Minister of Scientific-Technological Development and Higher Education of Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Republika_Srpska) in the government of [Savo Minić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savo_Mini%C4%87).[^cite_note-5] Karan served as minister until January 2026.[^cite_note-6]

### 2025–26 Republika Srpska presidential election

Main article: [2025–26 Republika Srpska presidential election](80899584.md)

Following the premature removal of then-[President of Republika Srpska](15903684.md) [Milorad Dodik](4218129.md) from office by the [Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina](19957484.md) in June 2025, the [Central Election Commission of Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Election_Commission_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) announced in August that [early presidential elections](80899584.md) for a shortened, ten-month term, were to be held on 23 November 2025.[^cite_note-7] Initially refusing to take part in the election, Dodik and his party, the [Alliance of Independent Social Democrats](4358875.md) (SNSD) later confirmed their participation.[^cite_note-klix-8] On 30 September, Dodik announced Karan as the SNSD's candidate for the election.[^cite_note-9]

In the election, Karan was initially elected president by a surprisingly small margin, obtaining 50.39% of the vote. The virtually unknown [Serb Democratic Party](413319.md) candidate [Branko Blanuša](81737885.md) was second with 48.22%.[^cite_note-10] Following the election, the opposition accused the SNSD of [voter fraud](382165.md),[^cite_note-11] and called for a recount of all ballots cast in the cities of [Doboj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doboj), [Zvornik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zvornik) and [Laktaši](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakta%C5%A1i).[^cite_note-12] In December 2025, the Central Election Commission decided to annul the election results in 17 municipalities at 136 polling stations, with the elections being repeated at those locations on 8 February 2026.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

The repeated elections were also won by Karan, whose total vote share was 50.53%, compared to Blanuša's 48.09%.[^cite_note-15]

### President of Republika Srpska (2026–present)

Karan was sworn in as president of Republika Srpska in the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Republika_Srpska\)) on 17 February 2026, succeeding acting president [Ana Trišić-Babić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_Tri%C5%A1i%C4%87-Babi%C4%87).[^cite_note-16]
