The post of **Mayor of the City of New Orleans** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Maire de La Nouvelle-Orléans*) has been held by the following individuals since [New Orleans](53842.md) came under [American](3434750.md) administration following the [Louisiana Purchase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Purchase) — the 1803 acquisition by the U.S. of 828,800 square miles (2,147,000 km<sup>2</sup>) of the [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_First_Republic) province *[La Louisiane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_\(New_France\))*. In all mayoral elections since 1930, New Orleans has used a two-round system with a [preliminary round](25932.md) and a [runoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runoff_election) if no candidate reached a majority in the first round. All mayors of New Orleans since 1872 have belonged to the [Democratic Party](5043544.md).
