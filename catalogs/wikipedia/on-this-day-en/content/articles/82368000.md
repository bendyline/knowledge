The **79th annual [Cannes Film Festival](101460.md)** took place from 12 to 23 May 2026.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] South Korean filmmaker [Park Chan-wook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Chan-wook) served as jury president for the main competition. Romanian filmmaker [Cristian Mungiu](11409466.md) won the [Palme d'Or](612946.md) for a second time with the drama film *[Fjord](82004708.md)*.

Hartland Villa designed the official poster for the festival featuring actresses [Geena Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geena_Davis) and [Susan Sarandon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Sarandon) on the set of *[Thelma & Louise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thelma_&_Louise)* (1991) by [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott), the closing film of the [44th edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Cannes_Film_Festival).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] French actress [Eye Haïdara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_Ha%C3%AFdara) served as host for the opening and closing ceremonies.[^cite_note-5]

During the festival, three [Honorary Palmes d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_Palme_d'Or) were awarded: the first was to [Peter Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Jackson) during the festival's opening ceremony,[^cite_note-\:6-6] the second was awarded on short notice to [John Travolta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Travolta) before the world premiere of *[Propeller One-Way Night Coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propeller_One-Way_Night_Coach)*,[^cite_note-Travolta-7] the third was awarded to [Barbra Streisand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbra_Streisand) during the closing ceremony.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-\:4-9]

The festival opened with the French period-comedy film *[The Electric Kiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Electric_Kiss)* by [Pierre Salvadori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Salvadori).[^cite_note-10]

## Official Selection

The Official Selection for the 79th edition was announced on 9 April 2026, by the festival's President [Iris Knobloch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_Knobloch) and General Delegate/Artistic Director [Thierry Frémaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thierry_Fr%C3%A9maux) at a press conference held at the Pathé Palace in [Paris](22989.md). Frémaux noted that 2,541 feature films were submitted for consideration, with the initial main competition lineup featuring 21 films from three continents and five female directors.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] [Pedro Almodóvar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Almod%C3%B3var)'s *[Bitter Christmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitter_Christmas)* is the only selected film that had its world premiere before the festival. At the press conference after the announcement, Frémaux stated that he hoped to add [James Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gray_\(director\))'s *[Paper Tiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_Tiger_\(2026_film\))* to the lineup at a later stage; the film's premiere at the festival was confirmed later that month.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-festival-cannes.com-25]

## Parallel sections

### Critics' Week (*Semaine de la critique*)

From a record 1,050 feature film submissions, 11 were selected, including nine first features eligible for the [Caméra d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cam%C3%A9ra_d'Or). According to Cahen, the selection focuses on "emerging filmmakers whose visions offer hope amidst a society in flux".[^cite_note-36] For the first time in the section's history, the opening film was an animated feature: [*In Waves*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Waves_\(film\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) by Phuong Mai Nguyen.[^cite_note-37] The closing film was the drama *[Adieu Monde Cruel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adieu_Monde_Cruel?action=edit\&redlink=1)* by Félix de Givry.[^cite_note-38] The section also held its first edition of Next Step Studio, an initiative to highlight local cinema, pairing eight emerging directors to co-direct and co-write four short films.
