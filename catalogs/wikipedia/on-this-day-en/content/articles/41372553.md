Visitors to **[Niger](21373.md)** must obtain a [visa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_\(document\)) from one of the [Nigerien diplomatic missions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_missions_of_Niger) unless they come from one of the visa exempt countries.

According to the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Passport Index, and the Henley Passport Index, citizens of the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) holding an ordinary passport can obtain a visa on arrival.[^cite_note-3]

Holders of diplomatic passports issued to nationals of [China](5405.md), [Cuba](5042481.md), [Italy](14532.md), [Turkey](11125639.md) and [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) do not require a visa for Niger.

Holders of diplomatic and service passports of [Russia](25391.md) do not require a visa for Niger.

## Transit without a visa

Transit without a visa is available to holders of onward tickets for a maximum transit of 24 hours - except for nationals of [Albania](738.md), [Armenia](10918072.md), [Azerbaijan](746.md), [Belarus](3457.md), [Bosnia and Herzegovina](3463.md), [Bulgaria](3415.md), [China](5405.md), [Croatia](5573.md), [Czech Republic](5321.md), [Georgia](48768.md), [Hungary](13275.md), [Kazakhstan](16642.md), [Democratic People's Republic of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_People's_Republic_of_Korea) (North Korea), [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md), [Moldova](19260.md), [Mongolia](19271.md), [North Macedonia](23564616.md), [Poland](22936.md), [Portugal](23033.md), [Romania](25445.md), [Russia](25391.md), [Slovakia](26830.md), [Slovenia](27338.md), [South Africa](17416221.md), [Tajikistan](30108.md), [Turkmenistan](198149.md), [Ukraine](31750.md), [Uzbekistan](31853.md) and [Vietnam](202354.md) who may transit without a visa only if proceeding by the same aircraft.[^cite_note-4]

## Pre-approved visa on arrival

Holders of a pre-approved confirmation from the National Police may obtain a visa on arrival (flyer visa) upon arrival at Niamey. The passport will be compounded for 1 working day and the traveler is required to appear at the Director General of Immigration the next working day to register and collect their passport.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]



A Sample Pre-Authorization Letter enabling a traveler from [Singapore](27318.md) to obtain a visa on arrival.

## United States

Entry and transit is refused to [United States](3434750.md) nationals, even if not leaving the aircraft and proceeding by the same flight.[^cite_note-vividvoicenews_com-7][^cite_note-maliandburkina-8]
