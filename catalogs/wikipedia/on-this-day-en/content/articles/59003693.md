**Russell Hardy** is a British businessman, and the chief executive (CEO) of [Vitol](17518274.md), the world's largest independent oil trading company.[^cite_note-Rathenow-1]

## Early life

Hardy has a master's degree in engineering from [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London).[^cite_note-auto-2][^cite_note-gastechevent.com-3]

## Career

Hardy began his career working for [BP](18998720.md), trading fuel oil.[^cite_note-auto-2]

In 1993, Hardy left BP and joined Vitol.[^cite_note-gastechevent.com-3] He has been a member of its executive committee since 2007, and CEO of the Europe, Middle-East and Africa region since 2017.[^cite_note-auto-2] In March 2018, he was appointed CEO of the entire company, succeeding [Ian Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Taylor_\(British_businessman\)), who became chairman.[^cite_note-auto-2][^cite_note-4]

In 2022, Hardy predicted that Russia won't be able to protect itself from the negative effects of the [international sanctions](70323386.md), while Russian oil export would fall by 1 million barrels a day in winter 2022-2023.[^cite_note-Rathenow-1][^cite_note-5]
