Legislative elections were held in [New Caledonia](21342.md) on 12 May 2019 to elect members of the [Congress of New Caledonia](4504443.md) and members of the provincial assemblies.

Anti-independence parties retained their majority in Congress, with the [centre-right](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-right), [liberal conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_conservatism) [Future with Confidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_with_Confidence) party winning a plurality of seats in Congress.

## Electoral system

Voters elect 76 members to the assemblies of the [three provinces of New Caledonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_New_Caledonia) (40 for [South Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Province,_New_Caledonia), 22 for [North Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Province,_New_Caledonia), and 14 for the [Loyalty Islands Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyalty_Islands_Province)). In turn, 54 also become members of the [Congress of New Caledonia](4504443.md) (32 from South Province, 15 from North Province, and 7 from Loyalty Islands Province).&#x20;

The elections are held under [party-list proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party-list_proportional_representation) using a 5% electoral threshold and the [highest averages method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highest_averages_method) to allocate seats. The elections used a restricted roll, which restricts voting depending on how long someone has lived in New Caledonia.[^cite_note-1] Only persons residing in New Caledonia since at least 8 November 1998 (and their children, once they reach the age of majority) are able to vote.
