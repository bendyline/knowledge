# Posthumous award



An example of a posthumous accolade: the [Grammy Award for Best Spoken Word Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Spoken_Word_Album) for [Martin Luther King Jr.](20076.md)

A **posthumous award** is an award that is granted after the recipient has died.[^cite_note-1] Many prizes, medals, and awards can be granted [posthumously](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/posthumously).[^cite_note-2]

## Military decorations

Military decorations, such as the [Medal of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_of_Honor), are often given posthumously. The title [Hero of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_the_Soviet_Union) was posthumously given, but the Gold Star medal was not awarded itself. During [World War II](32927.md), many countries practiced the granting of posthumous awards.[^cite_note-armyHeritage-3]

In the Soviet Union, the only posthumous award that was physically awarded was the [Order of the Patriotic War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Patriotic_War). All other awards were not physically awarded. Until 1977, upon the death of an awardee, all medals and awards were returned.[^cite_note-vlieger-4]

Less commonly, certain prizes, medals, and awards are granted *only* posthumously, especially those that honor people who died in service to a particular cause. Such awards include the [Confederate Medal of Honor award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_Medal_of_Honor_\(Sons_of_Confederate_Veterans\)), to Confederate veterans who distinguished themselves conspicuously during the [American Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War) (1861–1865), and the [Dag Hammarskjöld Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dag_Hammarskj%C3%B6ld_Medal), to military personnel, police, or civilians who died while serving in a [United Nations](31769.md) [peacekeeping operation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacekeeping_operation).

## Other fields

Sports awards and titles can be awarded posthumously, for example 1970 [Formula One](10854.md) champion [Jochen Rindt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jochen_Rindt), who died in a crash late in the season, but still had enough points to be named champion.[^cite_note-formula1-5]

Australian actor [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger) won [many awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Heath_Ledger) after his death in 2008, particularly concerning his performance as [The Joker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joker_\(The_Dark_Knight\)) in the film *[The Dark Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight)*.[^cite_note-people-6]
