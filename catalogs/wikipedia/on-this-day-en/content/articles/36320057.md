**Harouf** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): حاروف, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Ḥārūf*) is a municipality in the [Nabatieh Governorate](4981449.md) region of southern [Lebanon](17771.md) and is located north of the [Litani River](1920925.md). The village is economically important due to the presence of a famous industrial area in it known as "Marj Harouf".

## History

In the 1596 [tax records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daftar), it was named as a village in the [Ottoman](22278.md) *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya)* (subdistrict) of [Sagif](6853436.md) under the *[liwa'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liwa_\(Arabic\))* (district) of [Safad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safed_Sanjak), with a population of 12 households, all Muslim. The villagers paid a fixed tax-rate of 25% on agricultural products, such as [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), fruit trees, goats and beehives, in addition to "occasional revenues"; a total of 2,459 *[akçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e)*.[^cite_note-Hutteroth-1][^cite_note-2]

In 1875, [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) found Harouf to be a village of about 140 [Metualis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metualis). He further noted several [sarcophagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarcophagi), convincing him that it was an ancient place.[^cite_note-3]

During the [2024 Lebanon war](78006796.md), Israel destroyed a [vernacular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vernacular) house, more than 150 years old.[^cite_note-4]

## Demographics

In 2014, [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.58% of registered voters in Harouf. 98.51% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-5]
