# Member states of the African Union



Member states of the AU in dark green, suspended states in light green

The **member states of the African Union** are the 55 [sovereign states](23604120.md) that have ratified or acceded to the [Constitutive Act of the African Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutive_Act_of_the_African_Union) to become [member states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_state) to the [African Union](10634933.md) (AU).[^cite_note-1] The AU was the successor to the [Organisation of African Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organisation_of_African_Unity) (OAU), and AU membership was open to all OAU member states.

From an original membership of 36 states when the OAU was established on 25 May 1963, there have been nineteen successive [enlargements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlargement_of_the_African_Union)—the largest occurring on 18 July 1975 when four states joined. [Morocco](19291.md) is the newest member state, having joined on 31 January 2017.[^cite_note-2] Morocco was a founding member of the OAU but withdrew in 1984 following the organization's acceptance of the [Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic](38762388.md) as a member state, which claims the sovereignty of the [disputed territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_status_of_Western_Sahara) of [Western Sahara](33220.md) with Morocco.

The AU spans the entirety of the African continent, with the exception of the Spanish North Africa [semi-enclaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-enclave) of [Ceuta](6443.md), [Melilla](20226.md), and [Vélez de la Gomera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A9lez_de_la_Gomera). Island states are also members of the AU, but not the offshore islands that belong to the [transcontinental countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcontinental_countries) of [France](5843419.md), [Italy](14532.md), [Portugal](23033.md), [Spain](26667.md), [United Kingdom](31717.md) and [Yemen](350939.md). The 55 member states are grouped into [five regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_the_African_Union).

The African Union is composed of fifty-two [republics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic) and three [monarchies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchies_in_Africa). The total population of the AU is in excess of 1.5 billion.[^cite_note-3]

## Accession

See also: [Enlargement of the African Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlargement_of_the_African_Union)

[South Africa](17416221.md) joined on 6 June 1994 after the end of the [apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid_in_South_Africa) and the [April 1994 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_general_election,_1994).

[South Sudan](32350676.md), which seceded from [Sudan](27421.md) on 9 July 2011, joined the AU on 27 July 2011.[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-60]

The AU's most recent member state is [Morocco](19291.md), having joined on 31 January 2017. Morocco withdrew from the OAU in 1984 following the organization's acceptance of the [Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic](38762388.md) as a member state. [Morocco rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Sahara_War) over most of the territory, but [sovereignty is disputed](32672915.md).
