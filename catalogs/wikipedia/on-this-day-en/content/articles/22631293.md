**Intan Jaya Regency** is one of the [regencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regencies_of_Indonesia) (*kabupaten*) in the [Indonesian](14579.md) province of [Central Papua](71191665.md); it was formed in 2008 from part of [Paniai Regency](22831495.md). It covers an area of 6,536.27 km<sup>2</sup>, and had a population of 40,490 at the 2010 Census,[^cite_note-Biro_Pusat_Statistik_2011-2] but this greatly increased to 135,043 at the 2020 Census;[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] the official estimate as at mid 2024 was 137,696 (comprising 71,863 males and 65,833 females).[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1] The administrative centre is the town of **Sugapa**.
