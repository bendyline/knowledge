**Audias Flores Silva** (born 19 November 1980), commonly known by his alias "El Jardinero" or "The Gardener", is a Mexican [drug lord](23002805.md) and high-ranking leader in the [Jalisco New Generation Cartel](35006872.md) (CJNG).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## Early life and career

Audias Flores Silva was born on 19 November 1980, in [Huetamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huetamo), [Michoacán](195805.md), [Mexico](3966054.md).[^cite_note-3] He worked closely all his life with [El Mencho](53322465.md), a Mexican [drug lord](23002805.md) who was once the most wanted man in Mexico and the leader of the CJNG.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

After Mencho's death in February 2026, Flores Silva aimed to take over power of Mencho's organization.[^cite_note-7] Analysts noted that he was possibly next in line to run the CJNG.[^cite_note-8] Starting his life of crime over a decade ago, Flores Silva went by many aliases before his current one, such as “El Commander,” “El Bravo 2,” “Audi,” and “Boss Killer."[^cite_note-9]

## 2026 capture

On April 27, 2026, Flores Silva was captured by [Mexican military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_military) after being found in a ditch.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] Prior to his capture, the [United States](3434750.md) was offering up to \$5 million in reward for any information on him.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] In May, 2026, his charges were expanded by a judge.[^cite_note-15]
