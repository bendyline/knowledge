# 2027 Men's Ice Hockey World Championships

|  |
| --- |



Map of the countries participating at the 2027 IIHF World Championships

The **2027 Men's Ice Hockey World Championships** will be the 90th such event hosted by the [International Ice Hockey Federation](16760449.md). Teams will participate at several levels of competition based on their [2026 results](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Men's_Ice_Hockey_World_Championships). The competition will also serve as qualifications for division placements in the [2028 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2028_Men's_Ice_Hockey_World_Championships?action=edit\&redlink=1).

As in [2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Men's_Ice_Hockey_World_Championships), teams from Russia and Belarus will not be allowed to enter, as they remain suspended by the IIHF due to the countries' [invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine).[^cite_note-r699-1]

All lower divisions will play their tournament with two groups, a knockout stage and the winner of the final to be promoted. The last-placed teams of each group will play a two-game series to determine the relegated team.[^cite_note-2]

## Championship (Top Division)

Further information: [2027 IIHF World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_IIHF_World_Championship)
