The **United Workers Party** (**UWP**) is a [conservative](6675.md) [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Saint Lucia](27208.md) currently led by former tourism minister [Allen Chastanet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_Chastanet), who defeated former prime minister [Stephenson King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephenson_King) in a July 28, 2013 leadership election. The party was led previously by [Sir John Compton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_John_Compton), the party's founder.

## History

The party was formed before the [1964 general elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lucian_general_election,_1964) by an alliance of the [People's Progressive Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Progressive_Party_\(Saint_Lucia\)) and the National Labour Movement, a splinter group founded by three members ([John Compton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Compton), Vincent Monrose, and Maurice Mason) from the ruling [Saint Lucia Labour Party](1836043.md).[^cite_note-N1-1] [John Compton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Compton) was the political leader and [Henry Giraudy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Giraudy) was the chairman of the party. They won the elections, taking six of the eight seats. They remained in power after winning elections in [1969](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lucian_general_election,_1969) and [1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lucian_general_election,_1974), before losing the [1979 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lucian_general_election,_1979) to the Labour Party. They returned to power after winning 14 of the 17 seats in the [1982 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lucian_general_election,_1982), and remained in power after two elections in 1987 and one in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lucian_general_election,_1992). The Labour Party won the [1997](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lucian_general_election,_1997) and [2001 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lucian_general_election,_2001), but the UWP regained power in the [2006 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lucian_general_election,_2006). Since then neither party has managed to retain office for more than a single term. In 2011 the UWP lost the election to Labour, regained power in the 2016 elections, taking 11 of the 17 seats in the House,[^cite_note-2] only to return to Opposition with just 2 seats after the [2021 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Saint_Lucian_general_election).

The United Workers Party is a member of the [Caribbean Democrat Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caribbean_Democrat_Union), the regional organization of the global conservative [International Democracy Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Democracy_Union).
