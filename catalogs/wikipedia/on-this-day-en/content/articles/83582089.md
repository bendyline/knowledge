On 29 June 2026, an explosion occurred at the entrance of a residential building at 4 Rue Révérend Père Louis Frolla in [Monaco](19261.md), near the [Monaco-France border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monaco-France_border). Three people were injured including two adults, left in critical condition, as well as a 13-year-old child. The blast was caused by an [improvised explosive device](265112.md) (IED) reportedly in a backpack left at the scene by an unidentified suspect, who fled toward France shortly after the detonation. *[Le Monde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Monde)* reported that it was "a settling of scores linked to organized crime".[^cite_note-LeMonde-1] On 9 July, one of the suspects in the bombing was found dead, and two other people suspected of being involved in the bombing were arrested for her death.[^cite_note-2]

## Explosion

The explosion occurred shortly before 9 pm.[^cite_note-ReutersJune2026-3][^cite_note-BBCJune2026-4] Officials described it as a [parcel bomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parcel_bomb). The suspect dropped a package or backpack at the entrance of a residential building on Rue Révérend Père Louis Frolla shortly before the explosion.[^cite_note-ReutersJune2026-3] The explosive device contained [buckshot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckshot) and bolts.[^cite_note-F24June2026-5] It was detonated via remote control right after the three victims had returned from dinner at a seaside restaurant.[^cite_note-CNNMBUw3726-6][^cite_note-GuardianWomanJuly2026-7] The building was close to the French border.[^cite_note-F24June2026-5] The blast was felt as far away as Nice, France, 13 miles away, Stéphane Thibault, Monaco’s prosecutor, said at a news conference.[^cite_note-8] The main target was considered to be [Vadym Iermolaiev](54237174.md), a Ukrainian-born [oligarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_oligarchs).[^cite_note-ReutersJune2026-3][^cite_note-KIJune2026-9] An unnamed woman[^cite_note-10] had life-threatening injuries, and Ukrainian sources reported her legs were amputated soon after arriving in hospital.[^cite_note-CNNMBUw3726-6][^cite_note-Iermolaiev8041742-11] Iermolaiev himself sustained burns and shrapnel injuries, while a young man whose identity was also not released suffered less serious injuries.[^cite_note-Iermolaiev8041742-11] Two further people were in a state of shock.[^cite_note-Iermolaiev8041742-11]

### Suspect

An [Interpol red notice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpol_red_notice) was issued for Ukrainian citizen Anastasiia Berezovska who was the main suspect behind the attack.[^cite_note-GuardianWomanJuly2026-7] The Deputy Prosecutor of Monaco, Morgan Raymond stated that the attacker had walked across the nearby border just meters from the property, before fleeing in a car rented in Germany.[^cite_note-nytimes3july2026-12] And then returned to Germany via several European countries.[^cite_note-GuardianWomanJuly2026-7] German authorities have said that the rental apartment and vehicle of Berezovska had been searched and that evidence would be shared with Monegasque authorities.[^cite_note-CNNMBUw3726-6] On 6 July 2026 Berezovska was found dead near [Kyiv](585629.md).[^cite_note-Iermolaiev8042718-13] Sources of *[Ukrainska Pravda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainska_Pravda)* stated she had been shot dead.[^cite_note-Iermolaiev8042718-13] The same sources stated Berezovska had left Ukraine on 22 March 2025 and had returned on 1 July 2026.[^cite_note-Iermolaiev8042718-13] It was also reported that an active officer in the Ukraine [Main Directorate of Intelligence](1406269.md) and a former law enforcement officer were detained as suspects.[^cite_note-Iermolaiev8042718-13]

## Reactions

Minister of State [Christophe Mirmand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christophe_Mirmand) said that it was the first time in the history of Monaco that such an attack had occurred. A possible motive is being investigated.[^cite_note-F24June2026-5]

Sources of *[Ukrainska Pravda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainska_Pravda)* linked the attack with [scam call centers in Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scam_call_centers_in_Ukraine), in particular in [Dnipro](989795.md).[^cite_note-Iermolaiev8041742-11] On 4 December 2025 Iermolaiev's son Artur was detained in Cyprus and extradited to [Estonia](28222445.md).[^cite_note-IermolaievProfileGuardian2026jun30-14][^cite_note-ArturIermolaiev8012719-15] Estonian investigators accused Artur Iermolaiev, along with three other defendants, of having created fraudulent call centers in Ukraine. Artur Iermolaiev entered into a plea bargain with the [Estonian court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_Estonia), received a suspended sentence and paid an €8.5 million fine, and then left Estonia.[^cite_note-IermolaievProfileGuardian2026jun30-14] He has since been banned from entering Estonia.[^cite_note-IermolaievProfileGuardian2026jun30-14]
