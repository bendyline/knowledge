The **2025 Speedway of Nations** was the seventh edition of the [Speedway of Nations](56783360.md), and 33rd edition of a pairs competition for national teams in [motorcycle speedway](230182.md).&#x20;

The competition consisted of three events, which took place at the [MotoArena Toruń](22912508.md) in [Toruń](31258.md), Poland, between 30 September and 4 October 2025. The under-21 [Speedway of Nations 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Speedway_of_Nations_2) took place at the same venue on 3 October.[^cite_note-1]&#x20;

[Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain_national_speedway_team) were the defending champions having won in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Speedway_of_Nations).&#x20;

[Australia](12519514.md) won the title, beating hosts [Poland](12546053.md) in the Grand Final, with [Brady Kurtz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brady_Kurtz) and [Jack Holder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Holder) getting the better of [Bartosz Zmarzlik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartosz_Zmarzlik) and [Patryk Dudek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patryk_Dudek).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark_national_speedway_team) took bronze. It was Australia's second Speedway of Nations title after winning the [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Speedway_of_Nations) edition.&#x20;

The next Speedway of Nations will take place in [2027](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=2027_Speedway_of_Nations\&action=edit\&redlink=1), as the competition will be replaced with the return of the [Speedway World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedway_World_Cup) in 2026.&#x20;

## Summary

[Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_national_speedway_team) won the first semi-final, edging Great Britain into second place. [Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvia_national_speedway_team) also secured their place in the final by beating [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany_national_speedway_team) in the 'final qualifier.[^cite_note-4] All three teams joined hosts Poland in the decider.&#x20;

[Australia](12519514.md) comfortably won the second semi-final, only dropping one point, with [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark_national_speedway_team) finishing second. The [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic_national_speedway_team) took the last final spot after beating [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine_national_speedway_team) in the second 'final qualifier'.&#x20;

In the final, the Australian team of [Brady Kurtz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brady_Kurtz) and [Jack Holder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Holder) topped the standings on 37 points and went straight through to the Grand Final. Poland finished second on 35 points with Denmark in third on 34 points. Sweden finished fourth ahead of defending champions Great Britain in fifth. Latvia and Czech Republic were sixth and seventh respectively.&#x20;

Poland beat Denmark in the Grand Final Qualifier. Denmark's [Michael Jepsen Jensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jepsen_Jensen) won the race, however [Leon Madsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Madsen) was excluded, leaving [Patryk Dudek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patryk_Dudek) and [Bartosz Zmarzlik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartosz_Zmarzlik) to finish second and third and ensure their progression.&#x20;

Australia won the 2025 Speedway of Nations by beating Poland 7-2 in the Grand Final, with Kurtz and Holder finishing first and second ahead of Zmarzlik and Dudek.[^cite_note-5]&#x20;
