On 7 February 2026, a [mass stabbing](60784649.md) occurred at a student residence of [Bashkir State Medical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashkir_State_Medical_University?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Ufa](321112.md), Bashkortostan, Russia. A 15-year-old male entered the dormitory and attacked students and responding policemen before being detained by authorities.[^cite_note-Novosti-5] Seven people were injured, including four students from [India](14533.md) and the suspect.[^cite_note-Interfax-6] A criminal case was opened by [Russian law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investigative_Committee_of_Russia).[^cite_note-Ufa-7]

## Attack

A 15-year-old male entered a dormitory of Bashkir State Medical University. Russian media reported that he was armed with a knife and injured several people inside the facility.[^cite_note-Interfax-6] At the time of the attack, no security guards were on duty.[^cite_note-8] He reportedly trashed the floor, set off [firecrackers](418188.md) and attempted to enter rooms where students had barricaded themselves.[^cite_note-9] SHOT reported that the perpetrator also had [brass knuckles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brass_knuckles) and [pepper spray](157302.md).[^cite_note-10] Videos at the university showed blood on the floor, the windows of doors broken and ambulances transporting the injured to hospitals.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-TH-12] [Baza](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baza) reported the attacker was a supporter of the [National Socialism / White Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Socialism_/_White_Power) [Neo-Nazi](54361.md) extremist group operating in Russia.[^cite_note-13] In 2021, it was designated a [terrorist organization](63578.md) by the [Supreme Court of Russia](5915064.md).[^cite_note-14] They also reported that the perpetrator drew a [swastika](45943.md) on a wall using the blood of a victim. Students reported that he shouted various nationalist slogans about the Holocaust.[^cite_note-15]

## Victims

During the attempt to detain him, the suspect resisted and two policemen were injured.[^cite_note-Novosti-5] According to Interfax, at least five victims were hospitalized following the incident.[^cite_note-16] The victims were a 24-year-old stabbed in the abdomen and back, another 24-year-old who suffered a chemical eye burn, a 21-year-old stabbed in the face, a 35-year-old security guard stabbed in the thigh and jaw, a 23-year-old student with multiple stab wounds and hemorrhagic shock and a 17-year-old stabbed in the arm and neck.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] Two victims were transported to the City Hospital No. 10, while four others were transported to hospitals in moderate condition.[^cite_note-19] Four of the injured victims were identified as Indian students.[^cite_note-TH-12] One victim was discharged later in the day.[^cite_note-20] The suspect harmed himself and was transported to a local children's hospital in serious condition.[^cite_note-TH-12] He was placed in the [intensive care unit](6332859.md) with injuries to his neck and forearm.[^cite_note-IZ-3] The cuts did not cause significant blood loss and he was expected to be released after nine days.[^cite_note-21]

## Suspect

Russian sources identified the suspect as 15-year-old Alexander S. He was described as a calm and serious student who stayed out of trouble and only talked to a few children in class.[^cite_note-IZ-3] He lived with his 51-year-old disabled and [deaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deaf) mother and 67-year-old grandmother. He did not have a father, and his family is very poor.[^cite_note-22]

## Aftermath

After the suspect was detained, police secured the surrounding area of the dormitory.[^cite_note-23] Emergency services treated the injured, while investigators interviewed witnesses and examined available surveillance footage.[^cite_note-Ufa-7] A criminal case has been opened under Part 3 of Article 30, Subparagraph “a” of Part 2 of Article 105 (“Attempted murder of two or more persons”) and Article 317 of the Criminal Code of the Russian Federation (“Encroachment on the life of a law enforcement officer”).[^cite_note-24] A second criminal case was opened a day later under the article "Negligence".[^cite_note-25]
