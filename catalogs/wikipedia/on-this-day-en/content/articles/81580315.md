The **Liberated Areas**, ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): المناطق المحررة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *al-Manāṭiq al-Muḥarrira*) also known as the **Liberated Territories** refers to the territory under the *[de facto](43594.md)* control of the Sudanese rebel group [SLA (al-Nur)](2031543.md#Sudan_Liberation_Movement_\(al-Nur\)), also known as SLA-AW.[^cite_note-12] The civil administration that governs the Liberated Areas is called the **Civil Authority of the Sudan Liberation Movement**.[^cite_note-\:1-13] The Liberated Areas have remained neutral throughout the [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md).[^cite_note-\:02-1]

## History

The SLM (al-Nur) was founded in 2006 and is led by [Abdul Wahid al Nur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Wahid_al_Nur).[^cite_note-14] Since its founding, the group has fought in many Sudanese conflicts such as the [War in Darfur](5043637.md), the [Heglig Crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heglig_Crisis), and the [South Sudanese Civil War](41382446.md).

In August 2025, the [Tarasin landslide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Tarasin_landslide) affected areas under the control of the [SLM (al-Nur)](2031543.md#Sudan_Liberation_Movement_\(al-Nur\)).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

In October, the Sudan Liberation Army Movement (al-Nur) claimed that they had received a number of displaced people in areas under their control.[^cite_note-auto1-11]

## Territorial control and government

### Territory

Since 2021, the SLM/A (al-Nur) maintains a stronghold in the [Marrah Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marrah_Mountains) (including Torontonga), [Nertiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nertiti?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Deribat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deribat), [Tawila](74157994.md), Kin Taura, [Golo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golo,_Sudan), Guldo and Rokero.[^cite_note-kleinfeld_amin-18] The territory is inhabited by about 3,279,000 people.[^cite_note-19] The area is effectively [self-sufficient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-sustainability) and mostly isolated from the rest of Sudan.[^cite_note-kleinfeld_amin-18]

### Civil Authority of the Sudan Liberation Movement

It runs a *de facto* government there, which is called **Civil Authority of the Sudan Liberation Movement**,[^cite_note-\:1-13] trains new troops, and has also built several schools, where hundreds of children receive daily education.[^cite_note-arte-20]

### Symbolism

In January 2026, the Liberated Areas officially adopted a flag and an anthem, replacing Sudanese symbolism with their own ideological symbols.[^cite_note-\:02-1] The flag consists of [four horizontal stripes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stripe_\(pattern\)) of [black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black), [red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red), [blue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue), and [green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green), with a large white [five-pointed star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-pointed_star) in the center.[^cite_note-\:02-1]
