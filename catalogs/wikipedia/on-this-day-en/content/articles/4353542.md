**Moyen-Chari** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شاري الأوسط) is one of the [23 regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Chad) of [Chad](5488.md), located in the south of the country. Its capital is [Sarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarh). The former [prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moyen-Chari_\(prefecture\)) of the same name was larger and included modern [Mandoul Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandoul_Region).

## Geography

The region borders [Guéra Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gu%C3%A9ra_Region) to the north, [Salamat Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salamat_Region) to the east, the [Central African Republic](5478.md) to the south, and [Mandoul Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandoul_Region), [Tandjilé Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tandjil%C3%A9_Region) and [Chari-Baguirmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chari-Baguirmi_Region) to the west. The region is largely flat savannah, with parts of the region protected as part of the [Manda National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manda_National_Park). [Lake Iro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Iro), a suspected [impact crater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_crater), is located in the north-east.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

### Settlements

The capital of Moyen-Chari is [Sarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarh), the third largest city in Chad; other major settlements include [Alako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alako), [Balimba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balimba), [Baltoubaye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltoubaye), [Bohobé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohob%C3%A9), [Boum Kebbir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boum_Kebbir), [Danamadji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danamadji), [Dindjebo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dindjebo), [Djéké Djéké](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dj%C3%A9k%C3%A9_Dj%C3%A9k%C3%A9), [Korbol](57599101.md), [Koumogo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koumogo), [Kounou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kouno,_Chad), [Kyabé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyab%C3%A9), [Maro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maro,_Chad), [Moussa Foyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moussa_Foyo), [Ngondeye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngondeye), [Roro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roro,_Chad) and [Singako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singako)[^cite_note-4]

## Demographics

As per the 2009 Chadian census, the region's population was 588,008 inhabitants.[^cite_note-census-1] The main ethnolinguistic groups are Arab groups such as the [Baggara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baggara) (generally speaking [Chadian Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chadian_Arabic)), [Bagirmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagirmi_language), [Boor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boor_language), [Bua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bua_language), Gula groups such as the [Gula Iro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gula_Iro) and [Zan Gula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zan_Gula), [Kaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaba_language) groups (speaking language such as [Kaba Deme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaba_Deme_language), Kaba Náà and [Kulfa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaba_So_language)), [Laal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laal_language), [Lutos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutos_language), [Ndam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ndam_language), [Niellim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niellim_language), [Sara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_people), [Tumak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumak_language) and [Tunia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunia_language).[^cite_note-5]

## Economy

Its economy is based on [subsistence agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsistence_agriculture), breeding, [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton) and [sugarcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugarcane).
