**Evgeniy Leonidovych Khmara**[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Євгеній Леонідович Хмара) is a Ukrainian major general who has been the [Minister of Defence of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Defence_of_Ukraine) since 20 July 2026[^cite_note-4] in an acting capacity, and was formally appointed to the position on 19 August. He was appointed following the controversial [dismissal of Mykhailo Fedorov](83707818.md).[^cite_note-5]

## Career

In April 2023, Khmara was appointed by President [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) as head of the SBU unit responsible for special operations, counterterrorism activities, and the protection of participants in criminal proceedings and law enforcement officials.[^cite_note-6]

Following legislative reforms restructuring the SBU's special operations forces, Khmara was reappointed on 25 August 2025 as head of the reorganized and renamed Center for Special Operations "A" or [Alpha Group](47902041.md).[^cite_note-7] The unit conducts counterterrorism and other special missions as part of Ukraine's national security framework.[^cite_note-8]

On 5 January 2026, President Zelenskyy appointed Khmara as the acting head of the Security Service of Ukraine (SBU), entrusting him with leadership of the agency during the ongoing Russo-Ukrainian War.[^cite_note-acting-9] He succeeded [Vasyl Malyuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasyl_Malyuk).

On 16 July 2026, Zelenskyy appointed Khmara as the acting [Ukrainian Minister of Defence](14284651.md). His appointment took effect from 20 July. It was a move that came after the controversial [dismissal of Mykhailo Fedorov](83707818.md) during a government reshuffle.[^cite_note-10]

On 18 August 2026, Zelenskyy formally nominated Khmara as Minister of Defence, and the [Verkhovna Rada](844993.md) confirmed this the next day.[^cite_note-nv_khmara-11][^cite_note-rbc_khmara-12][^cite_note-reuters_khmara-13][^cite_note-up_khmara-14]

## Operations

Khmara led the counteroffensive campaign in May and June of 2022 to [recapture Snake Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snake_Island_campaign).[^cite_note-15]

In June 2025, during his time as head of [Alpha Group](47902041.md) of SBU, the unit carried out [Operation Spiderweb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Spiderweb).[^cite_note-16]

## Rank

On 23 June 2024, President Zelenskyy awarded Khmara the military rank of [Major General](36301471.md). The promotion was reported by Ukrainian media as part of a broader decree conferring senior ranks within Ukraine's security and defense sector.[^cite_note-17] Khmara left military service in August 2026 to comply with the law requiring the Minister of Defence to be a civilian[^cite_note-18].
