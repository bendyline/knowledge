[Donald Trump](4848272.md) has been the target of numerous assassination attempts and death threats during his presidential campaigning and his [presidencies of the United States of America](24113.md).

The [earliest known attempt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Donald_Trump_Las_Vegas_rally_incident) occurred at a campaign rally in [Las Vegas](47737.md) shortly before he secured the [nomination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_nominee) of the [Republican Party](32070.md) in the [2016 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_United_States_presidential_election). Throughout Trump's [first term in office](52231341.md), he was targeted but unharmed in several failed assassination attempts.

The most serious incident took place at a [Pennsylvania campaign rally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Donald_Trump_in_Pennsylvania) in 2024 while Trump was [running for a second term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_2024_presidential_campaign). The attempt resulted in three deaths, that of [the would-be assassin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Matthew_Crooks) and two spectators.[^cite_note-BBCCopenhaver-1] Trump was injured by a bullet that grazed his right ear, the only injury he sustained in these attempts. After this attempt, security incidents involving Trump became increasingly frequent, especially in 2026.[^cite_note-2]

## Incidents

### Before 2024

#### 2016 incident at Dayton, Ohio rally

On March 12, 2016, 22-year-old Thomas DiMassimo was arrested and charged with [disorderly conduct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disorderly_conduct#United_States) as he broke over the police barricade and attempted to rush the stage during a rally in Dayton, Ohio. He was tackled by the [Secret Service](58221.md).[^cite_note-3] On July 26, 2016, DiMassimo was sentenced to one year probation.[^cite_note-4]

#### 2016 incident at Las Vegas rally

Main article: [2016 Donald Trump Las Vegas rally incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Donald_Trump_Las_Vegas_rally_incident)

On June 18, 2016, Trump was giving a speech at the [Treasure Island Hotel and Casino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_Island_Hotel_and_Casino) in [Las Vegas](47737.md), Nevada, as part of his presidential campaign. During the speech, Michael Steven Sandford, a 20-year-old British man, attempted to grab a [Las Vegas Metropolitan Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas_Metropolitan_Police) officer's pistol. The officer quickly subdued Sandford, who was arrested and handed over to the United States Secret Service, where he expressed his desire to murder Trump. Sandford was sentenced to 12 months and one day's imprisonment, along with being fined \$200. After becoming eligible for early release, Sandford was released and deported to the United Kingdom in May 2017.

#### 2016 Reno "gun" scare

On November 5, 2016, three days before the presidential election, Trump was speaking at a rally in [Reno, Nevada](26388.md), when a man in the crowd screamed "gun". This caused Trump to be rushed off stage by security, and the man was tackled by surrounding members of the crowd. The man, Austyn Crites, was subdued by Secret Service agents and searched, only to find that he was unarmed. Crites, a Republican who opposed Trump, was holding up a sign shortly before that stated "Republicans against Trump". Crites stated that others attempted to grab the sign and were booing him. After the scene was cleared and identified as safe, Trump returned to the stage minutes later and finished the speech without incident.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

#### 2017 forklift attack attempt

On September 6, 2017, in [Mandan, North Dakota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandan,_North_Dakota), Gregory Lee Leingang stole a [forklift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forklift) from an [oil refinery](195137.md) and attempted to drive it toward the presidential [motorcade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorcade) while Trump was visiting to rally public support. After the forklift became jammed within the refinery, he fled on foot and was arrested by the pursuing police. While interviewed in detention, Leingang admitted his intent to murder the president by flipping the [presidential limousine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_state_car_\(United_States\)) with the stolen forklift, to the surprise of authorities, who had assumed he was stealing the vehicle for personal use. Leingang pleaded guilty to the attempted attack, stealing the forklift, related charges and several other unrelated crimes on the same day. Consequently, he was sentenced to 20 years in prison. His defense attorney noted a "serious psychiatric crisis".[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-WAPOLeingang-8]

#### 2018 ricin threats

On October 1, 2018, an envelope containing [castor beans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castor_beans), initially incorrectly being identified as [ricin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricin), was sent to Trump before being discovered by mailing facilities. Several other letters were sent to the Pentagon, all of them labeled on the front with "Jack and the Missile Bean Stock Powder". Two days later on October 3, a 39-year-old [Utah](31716.md) Navy veteran named William Clyde Allen III was arrested and charged with one count of mailing a threat against the president and five counts of mailing threatening communications to an officer or an employee of the United States. Allen pleaded not guilty to all charges.[^cite_note-9] He was subsequently [remanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remanded_in_custody) for [psychiatric evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychiatric_evaluation) and treatment, and the charges against him were dismissed on January 25, 2022.[^cite_note-10]

#### 2020 escort from press briefing

On August 10, 2020, Trump was escorted from a press briefing by a Secret Service agent after an armed suspect was shot outside the White House fencing.[^cite_note-11]

#### 2020 ricin poisoning attempt

On September 20, 2020, Pascale Cécile Véronique Ferrier was arrested in [Buffalo, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo,_New_York), while attempting to cross over the [border](630260.md) to [Canada](5042916.md). Ferrier, who is Canadian, wrote in a [ricin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricin)-laced letter to Trump that he should drop out of the ongoing [2020 presidential election](48410011.md) along with calling him an "ugly tyrant clown". She was charged with eight counts each of prohibitions with respect to [biological weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_weapons) and making threats via [interstate commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_commerce) and faced up to life in prison.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] On August 17, 2023, a US court sentenced Ferrier to nearly 22 years in prison.[^cite_note-14]

### 2024

#### Assassination attempt in Butler, Pennsylvania

Main article: [Attempted assassination of Donald Trump in Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Donald_Trump_in_Pennsylvania)



The rifle used by Crooks in the attempted assassination

On July 13, 2024, [Thomas Crooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Matthew_Crooks) fired eight shots from an [AR-15–style rifle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AR-15%E2%80%93style_rifle) at Trump while speaking at a rally near [Butler, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butler,_Pennsylvania).[^cite_note-15] Trump was shot and wounded in his upper right ear before dropping to the ground and being shielded by the Secret Service. Crooks also killed two members of the audience, Corey Comperatore and James Copenhaver (the latter of whom died in 2026[^cite_note-BBCCopenhaver-1]), as well as critically injuring one other, prior to being shot and killed by a member of the Secret Service Counter Sniper Team.[^cite_note-AP_news_12-16][^cite_note-17]

The assassination attempt prompted the resignation of the director of the Secret Service, [Kimberly Cheatle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimberly_Cheatle), as well as several ongoing investigations into the incident as a political assassination attempt as well as an act of domestic terrorism.[^cite_note-18]

#### Assassination attempt in West Palm Beach, Florida

Main article: [Attempted assassination of Donald Trump in Florida](77879114.md)



Routh being detained following the incident

On September 15, 2024, [Ryan Routh](77880898.md) was sighted wielding a rifle in bushes at the [Trump International Golf Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_International_Golf_Club,_West_Palm_Beach) in [West Palm Beach, Florida](109634.md), while Donald Trump was golfing at the club. A Secret Service officer shot at Routh, who fled, and was apprehended while driving away. The [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI) was investigating it as an assassination attempt.[^cite_note-19]

Authorities later confirmed that Routh did wish to kill the former president.[^cite_note-20] An indictment on April 7, 2025, revealed that Routh was planning the assassination attempt as early as February 2024 with an accomplice who called himself "Ramiro", who lived in [Mexico](3966054.md).[^cite_note-RouthApril9indictment-21][^cite_note-RouthApril9indictment2-22] Routh's trial began on September 8, 2025, and he was found guilty on all counts on September 23.[^cite_note-WPBF-23][^cite_note-24] He was sentenced to life in prison on February 4, 2026.[^cite_note-25]

#### Security incident at Coachella rally

On October 12, 2024, a man was arrested near a checkpoint at a Donald Trump rally in [Coachella, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella,_California). The man had two firearms and ammunition, multiple passports with different names, and an unregistered vehicle with a fake license plate.[^cite_note-26] On the same day, he was released after posting a \$5,000 bail.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

#### Assassination attempts allegedly linked to Iran

Main article: [Alleged Iranian attempts to assassinate Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alleged_Iranian_attempts_to_assassinate_Donald_Trump)

According to a report by the [United States Institute of Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Institute_of_Peace), "Iran has assassinated its enemies across four continents — in Asia, Europe, North America and South America — over the four decades since the 1979 revolution."[^cite_note-29] Iran is said to have begun plotting against Trump's life following the [assassination of Qasem Soleimani](62726668.md) in 2020. In October 2024, the [Biden administration](65732174.md) warned Iran that the US would consider any [retaliatory assassination attempts by Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_plots_to_assassinate_Donald_Trump) on Trump's life an [act of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casus_belli).[^cite_note-WaPo-2024-10-11-30]

##### July 2024 arrest in Houston

On July 12, 2024, a Pakistani man with connections to Iran, Asif Merchant, was arrested in [Houston](13774.md) and charged with recruiting people to help kill Trump, on behalf of Iran's [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC). One of the recruits turned into an informant for the FBI.[^cite_note-31] On March 6, 2026, Merchant was convicted in [New York City](645042.md) of [murder for hire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_for_hire) in the plot to kill Trump.[^cite_note-Guardian-32] Merchant testified that he participated unwillingly to protect his family in [Tehran](57654.md), whereas the Iranian government denied involvement.[^cite_note-Guardian-32]

##### November 2024 plot

On November 8, 2024, the [U.S. Department of Justice](52563.md) charged Farhad Shakeri, an Afghan citizen, along with Carlisle Rivera and Jonathan Loadholt (whom Shakeri had met in a US prison) in connection with another murder-for-hire plot, as revenge for the killing of Soleimani.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] Shakeri allegedly told an undercover FBI agent that an IRGC official urged him to "set aside other efforts" to focus on Trump.[^cite_note-ABCN-35] Rivera and Loadholt were arrested in November 2024 and convicted on related charges in January 2026, whereas Shakeri remains at large.[^cite_note-36]

### 2025

#### May death threat

On May 21, 2025, an Immigration and Customs Enforcement officer received a handwritten letter from someone claiming to be a Mexican immigrant threatening to shoot Trump with a [hunting rifle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunting_rifle) at his next rally, offering to self-deport after Trump was killed. Ramon Morales Reyes, an undocumented immigrant from Mexico, was arrested the following day on suspicion of threatening the president. However, investigators soon concluded he could not have written the letter, as he did not speak English and the handwriting did not match.[^cite_note-37] Prosecutors later charged Demetric Scott with witness intimidation after he admitted to writing the letter in an attempt to get Morales Reyes deported so that he could not testify against Scott in an upcoming robbery trial. Scott was convicted of witness intimidation and misappropriating another person's identity in January 2026.[^cite_note-38] On February 27, he was sentenced to 16 years in prison.[^cite_note-39]

#### August death threat

A 50-year-old woman from [Indiana](21883857.md) was arrested in the [District of Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_of_Columbia) on August 16, 2025, and charged with making violent threats against Trump. An [Instagram](31591547.md) account attributed to the woman had posted intent to "sacrificially kill this POTUS by [disemboweling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disembowelment) him and cutting out his [trachea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trachea) with [Liz Cheney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liz_Cheney) and all The Affirmation present." The account also referred to Trump as a terrorist and expressed a desire to avenge deaths that occurred due to his administration's [COVID-19](63030231.md) policies. During an interview with the Secret Service, she claimed to have a bladed object which she would use to kill Trump. On the day of her arrest, the woman "circumnavigated" the White House complex as part of a protest.[^cite_note-nath-40][^cite_note-nath2-41]

#### Security lapse at Trump National Golf Club

On August 31, 2025, a member of the [Trump National Golf Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_National_Golf_Club_Washington,_D.C.) in [Sterling, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling,_Virginia), inadvertently brought a loaded [Glock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glock) handgun into the facility while Trump was present. The firearm passed undetected through a manual bag inspection and handheld magnetometer screening. The individual later self-reported the weapon to security staff, and the gun never came into close proximity to Trump. The United States Secret Service launched an internal review of the lapse, and the screening agent responsible was placed on administrative leave.[^cite_note-42]

#### Ryder Cup impersonation incident

On September 26, 2025, an off-duty [New York City Police Department](255413.md) officer who was armed gained unauthorized access to the [Ryder Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Ryder_Cup) at [Bethpage Black Course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethpage_Black_Course) while Trump was present, falsely claiming to be part of his security detail. The officer, who was on sick leave and not assigned to the event, was later suspended pending investigation.[^cite_note-43]

#### Air Force One security incident

On October 16, 2025, the United States Secret Service discovered a suspicious elevated [hunting stand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunting_stand) near [Palm Beach International Airport](287471.md) in Florida with a direct line of sight toward the area where [Air Force One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_One) was parked. The structure was found during a routine security sweep before Trump's arrival. No individuals were located at the site, but the FBI investigated whether the placement of the stand posed a potential threat. According to reports, the vantage point provided an unobstructed view of the aircraft due to temporary construction changes at the airport, though authorities stated that Trump's movements were not disrupted by the discovery.[^cite_note-44]

### 2026

#### February Mar-a-Lago Secret Service shooting

On February 22, 2026, the [United States Secret Service](58221.md) shot and killed Austin Tucker Martin, a 21-year-old man from [Cameron, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron,_North_Carolina), who was reportedly carrying a shotgun and gas canister onto the grounds of [Mar-a-Lago](1452754.md), Trump's private residence in [Florida](18933066.md). Trump was in [Washington D.C.](108956.md) at the time of the incident.[^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46]

#### March Mar-a-Lago no-fly zone violation

At around 1\:15 p.m. on March 29, 2026, a civilian plane was intercepted by [NORAD](42905.md) aircraft after it violated the [Mar-a-Lago](1452754.md) [no-fly zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No-fly_zone).[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51]

#### White House Correspondents' Dinner shooting

Main article: [2026 White House Correspondents' Dinner shooting](83038072.md)

[CCTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CCTV) footage of the suspect running past the secure area near the ballroom

[^cite_note-\:11-52]

On April 25, 2026, gunshots were fired as an armed man ran past law enforcement at the [Washington Hilton](11732002.md) during the [White House Correspondents' Dinner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Correspondents'_Dinner). [Secret Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Secret_Service) agents evacuated Trump and his wife [Melania](1209075.md), Vice President [JD Vance](53396477.md), and several cabinet members from the banquet hall. The suspect, 31-year-old Cole Tomas Allen of [Torrance, California](107690.md), was apprehended near the screening area outside the banquet hall.[^cite_note-53] No officials were harmed.[^cite_note-54] One officer was struck in his bullet-resistant vest and was hospitalized but later released.[^cite_note-ABC7-55][^cite_note-AP-writings-56] Allen was hospitalized for a knee injury from falling.[^cite_note-APNewsLive-57]

A manifesto that officials attributed to Allen criticized Trump administration policies and stated that he intended to target administration officials. The manifesto did not name Trump, but stated "I am no longer willing to permit a pedophile, rapist, and traitor to coat my hands with his crimes".[^cite_note-Reuters-manifesto-58][^cite_note-AP-writings-56] Allen was charged with four criminal counts, including [attempting to assassinate a United States president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_States_presidential_assassination_attempts_and_plots),[^cite_note-NBCNews4.27.26-59][^cite_note-\:6-60] carrying a possible sentence of [life in prison](148681.md) if convicted.[^cite_note-Calculated_to_cause_death-61] He pleaded not guilty to all of the charges on May 11.[^cite_note-62] The dinner was rescheduled for July 24, at the [Waldorf Astoria hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Post_Office_\(Washington,_D.C.\)), with advanced security measures in place.[^cite_note-reschedule-63]

#### April death threat

On April 30, 2026, a Tidal Wave Auto Spa car wash facility in [Apex, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apex,_North_Carolina) was evacuated after a witness noticed a [BMW X5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMW_X5) [SUV](47696.md) parked in front of the facility with threats against Trump written on the driver's side window, one of which claimed the driver was on his way to Washington, D.C. to kill him. The witness immediately called the police and a bomb squad arrived to evacuate the facility.[^cite_note-64] The driver was arrested and charged with resisting a public officer, possession of [methamphetamine](11038318.md), and using a fake license plate.[^cite_note-65]

#### May National Mall shooting

At around 3\:40 p.m. of May 4, 2026, the [United States Secret Service](58221.md) exchanged gunfire with an armed man[^cite_note-KMIDmotorcade-66][^cite_note-67][^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69] in the area of the [National Mall](167796.md) near the [Washington Monument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Monument) in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md),[^cite_note-70] not far from the [White House](33057.md), the [U.S. Capitol Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Capitol_Building) and the [Lincoln Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Memorial). The man was wounded by several gunshots and was taken to a hospital for treatment. A bystander was also grazed by a bullet, while no law enforcement officers were injured.

#### May White House checkpoint shooting

On May 23, 2026, dozens of gunshots were heard outside the [White House](33057.md) complex, triggering a lockdown. It was reported that an African American man approached a White House checkpoint and pulled a pistol from his handbag and began shooting. The Secret Service guards returned fire and shot the man, critically injuring him. He was transported to [George Washington University Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University_Hospital) where he died shortly after admission. A 41-year-old male bystander was also struck and injured by gunfire.[^cite_note-71][^cite_note-72] A lockdown was placed on the White House, which contained reporters and journalists, and was lifted following the man's death announcement.[^cite_note-73]

The suspect was 21-year-old Nasire Best, a [Glenarden, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenarden,_Maryland) resident.[^cite_note-74] [Reuters](18998750.md) reported that, according to a law enforcement official, the perpetrator was an emotionally disturbed person who had a previous "stay-away order" issued to him.[^cite_note-75]

#### June White House plot

On June 15, 2026, the Department of Justice released an affidavit alleging that several individuals planned an armed attack to take place during the 2026 [UFC Freedom 250](80931969.md) event at the White House the day before, with the stated goal of launching an insurrection against Trump and multiple other members of the US government. According to authorities, a plot involving several individuals led by a [DACA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DACA) recipient[^cite_note-76], five of whom were arrested at the time, allegedly sought to exploit the UFC Freedom 250 event to carry out an attack on the White House using snipers and bomb-equipped drones.[^cite_note-77][^cite_note-78] Their goal was to spark a revolution by decapitating the country's leadership structure.[^cite_note-79] Suspects allegedly planned to target lawmakers who received money from pro-Israel groups.[^cite_note-80]

Two days after the event, the Department of Justice filed a statement in the ongoing litigation regarding construction of a [ballroom attached to the White House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_State_Ballroom). The construction was facing a court challenge, and the Justice Department filed a letter with the court stating the government’s view that the ballroom's "mass and height will shield the White House grounds from attack, and give the Secret Service the visibility needed to identify attackers."[^cite_note-81]

#### July alleged Iranian plot

Main article: [2026 alleged Iranian plot to assassinate Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_alleged_Iranian_plot_to_assassinate_Donald_Trump)

In early July 2026, Israel provided the United States with an intelligence report alleging that Iran had developed a new plot to assassinate Trump. The claims were first reported by *[The Wall Street Journal](173070.md)*. The threat prompted Trump to publicly board an older, more secure plane that had previously served as Air Force One rather than the [Qatari-donated aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_VC-25B_Bridge), which still lacked full presidential security upgrades, for his departure from the [NATO summit in Turkey](80796458.md) on July 8, after which he was secretly transferred to an Air Force [C-32A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_C-32).[^cite_note-82][^cite_note-83] In [Ankara](802.md), he boarded the older aircraft in front of television cameras, but a few minutes later he was secretly transferred to the smaller C-32A via an airport catering truck normally used to transport food and equipment to aircraft.[^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85] Journalists raised concern about the press, who had remained on the prior AF1, being used as unwitting decoys.[^cite_note-86]

On July 8, Trump said that Iran put him on a hit list where he is listed as the top target.[^cite_note-87] When Trump was asked about the plot by the media, he said, "They want to take out the US leader—me, I'm on whatever list. I saw this morning I'm on every single one of their lists".[^cite_note-88]

#### Armed man at golf course, August

In August 2026, a man was spotted taking photos and videos of the Trump-owned [Trump National Golf Course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_National_Golf_Club,_Los_Angeles) in [Rancho Palos Verdes, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rancho_Palos_Verdes,_California), and later arrested on August 2, 2026 "on charges of carrying a concealed firearm and possession of armor-piercing/prohibited ammunition", after he was stopped by the [Los Angeles Sheriff's Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Sheriff's_Department) and found with a 16-round magazine containing [hollow point ammunition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollow-point_bullet), illegal in California. Trump was visiting California and is planning a Republican party fundraising dinner at his golf club. As of August 4, officials have not stated whether the man was intending to attack Trump, and they also said that the man may face further charges.[^cite_note-89]
