The **Parliament of the Czech Republic** ([Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_language): *Parlament České republiky*) or just **Parliament** ([Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_language): *Parlament*) is the [legislative branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) of the [Czech Republic](5321.md). It meets in [Malá Strana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mal%C3%A1_Strana), [Prague](23844.md) and is composed of 281 total members and Senators.

It consists of [two chambers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameralism), both elected in [direct elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_elections):

- the Upper House: [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_the_Czech_Republic)

- the Lower House: [Chamber of Deputies](3991120.md)

Art. 15 of the Constitution stipulates its name as the "Parliament".[^cite_note-14] The Parliament exercises competences usual in [parliamentary systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system): it holds and passes bills, has the right to [modify the Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_act_of_the_Czech_Republic), ratifies international agreements; if necessary, it declares war, approves presence of foreign military forces in the Czech Republic or a dispatch of Czech military forces abroad.

## History



Session room of the Chamber of Deputies

The tradition of modern parliamentarianism in the [Bohemian lands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_lands) dates back to times of the [Austrian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Empire) (and then [Cisleithanian part](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cisleithania) of [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary)), where the [Imperial Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Council_\(Austria\)) (*Reichsrat*, *Říšská rada*) was created in 1861.

After proclamation of [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia) in 1918 its National Assembly (*Národní shromáždění*) undertook legislative duties both of the Imperial Council and State Diets ([Bohemian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Bohemia), [Moravian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margraviate_of_Moravia), [Silesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Upper_and_Lower_Silesia)).[^cite_note-15] In [1938–39](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Czechoslovak_Republic) and between [1948–89](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Czechoslovakia_\(1948%E2%80%931989\)) there existed a parliament within non-democratic regimes (right-wing authoritarian or Communist regime, respectively). As a consequence of federalization of Czechoslovakia (1968), national councils of [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Socialist_Republic) and [Slovak parts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovak_Socialist_Republic) of the country were created.

The Chamber of Deputies keeps continuity with the [Czech National Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_National_Council) (*Česká národní rada*), while the Senate was established in 1996 (with reference to the [First Czechoslovak Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovak_Republic_\(1918%E2%80%931938\)) one).
