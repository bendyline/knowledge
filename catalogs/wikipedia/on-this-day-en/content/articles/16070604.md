The **minister of foreign affairs of the Czech Republic** ([Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_language): *Ministr zahraničních věcí České republiky*) is a senior official of the [Government of the Czech Republic](14380629.md), and as the head of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Czech_Republic\)) is concerned with [foreign policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_the_Czech_Republic) and foreign relations of the Czech Republic.

The minister of foreign affairs is appointed by the [president](410288.md) after being nominated by the [prime minister](410283.md).

The minister of foreign affairs along with the [finance minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance_Minister_of_the_Czech_Republic), the [minister of defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Defence_\(Czech_Republic\)), and the [minister of the interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Interior_\(Czech_Republic\)) are generally regarded as the four most important cabinet members because of the importance of their respective ministries.

The current minister of foreign affairs of the Czech Republic is [Petr Macinka](79962349.md).
