**Arcadia Lake** is a [reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reservoir) in Central [Oklahoma County, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_County,_Oklahoma), United States. The lake is located northeast of [Oklahoma City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_City,_Oklahoma) in eastern [Edmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmond,_Oklahoma), just east of [Interstate 35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_35_in_Oklahoma).

The lake has a surface area of 1,820 acres (7 km²) and has about 26 miles (42 km) of shoreline.

## History

The lake was created in 1984 by an earthen dam on the [Deep Fork River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_Fork_River) begun in 1980 (1980). This lake was constructed as a cooperative effort between the City of Edmond and the [United States Army Corps of Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Corps_of_Engineers).[^cite_note-1] It was created to control floods in the Deep Fork River Basin, supply water to the city of [Edmond, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmond,_Oklahoma), and provide recreational resources to the surrounding communities.

## Recreation

Recreational opportunities at Arcadia Lake include fishing, boating, camping, mountain biking, hiking, disc golf and equestrian trails. [Waterskiing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterskiing) and [jet skiing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_skiing) are both allowed in designated areas. Horse rentals are not available, so visitors must bring their own mounts. Facilities include picnic areas, a Frisbee golf course, pavilions, boat ramps, designated swim beaches and an enclosed and heated fishing dock that is handicapped accessible. Arcadia Lake also has RV campsites with full hook-ups and primitive tent campsites. Parks around the lake include [Scissortail Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scissortail_Park), Spring Creek Park, Central State Park and Edmond Park.[^cite_note-2]
