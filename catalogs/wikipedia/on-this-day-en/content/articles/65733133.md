[Datuk Seri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datuk_Seri) **Windsor Paul John** (born 1961) is a Malaysian football administrator.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

In 2015, he was appointed as the [Asian Football Confederation](715249.md)'s Secretary General.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] He replaced Alex Soosay, the former Secretary General who had been suspended by [the AFC](715249.md).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]
