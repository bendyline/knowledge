**Tarnawa-Kolonia** [\[tarˈnava kɔˈlɔɲa\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Polish) is a village in the administrative district of [Gmina Turobin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gmina_Turobin), within [Biłgoraj County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bi%C5%82goraj_County), [Lublin Voivodeship](344869.md), in eastern Poland.[^cite_note-TERYT-1]

## History

On 30 July 2026, a Russian [Kh-101](21425976.md) cruise missile [struck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/July_2026_Russian_missile_hit_in_Poland) approximately 2 km (1.2 mi) from the village. This occurred during Russia's attacks on western Ukraine, part of the [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
