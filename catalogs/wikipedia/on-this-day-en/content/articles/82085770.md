The **Southern Transitional Council conflict** was a sub-conflict of the [Yemeni Civil War](46215853.md), between the separatist [Southern Transitional Council](54251566.md) (STC) and other factions, mainly the internationally-backed government in Yemen. The STC was supported by the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md), despite the UAE being a member of the [Saudi Arabian](349303.md)-led coalition working to support the Yemeni government.[^cite_note-Guradian_Dec2018-2]

In 2022, the STC became part of the newly founded [Presidential Leadership Council](70488867.md) (PLC). In December 2025, the STC launched an offensive claiming much of South Yemen.[^cite_note-3] STC soon thereafter proclaimed a two-year-long process for [self-determination](29269.md). After the [Saudi-led coalition](46230181.md) launched a counter-offensive, the STC announced its dissolution of 9 January 2026 ending the conflict.[^cite_note-dissolution-4]

## Background

Further information: [South Yemen insurgency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Yemen_insurgency), [Yemeni crisis](45326059.md), and [Yemeni civil war (2014–present)](46215853.md)

In 2007, [Southern Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Movement) was founded as a peaceful independence movement. It gained political power after the [Yemeni revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_revolution) and [Saudi-led intervention](46230181.md) when it sided with the Hadi government against [Houthis](17240332.md).[^cite_note-5]

## Timeline

### 2017

In the end of **April 2017**, Governor of the [Aden Governorate](3604348.md) Aidarus al-Zoubaidi was sacked by President [Abdrabbuh Mansur Hadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdrabbuh_Mansur_Hadi), with the reason of disloyalty to him, and loyalty to the Southern Movement. On **3 May 2017**, major rallies were held in Aden to protest the decision of Hadi. One week later, [Southern Transitional Council](54251566.md) was formed, and some of the members were the governors of [Dhale](3604352.md), [Shabwah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shabwah_Governorate), [Hadhramaut](3604476.md), [Lahij](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahij_Governorate), [Socotra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socotra_Governorate), and [Al Mahrah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Mahrah_Governorate) governorates. It also have partial control in [Abyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abyan_Governorate) and Aden governorates. One day later, Hadi rejected the council, and called it illegitimate.[^cite_note-aljazeera-6][^cite_note-alaraby2-7][^cite_note-aawsat2-8][^cite_note-xinhuanet-9]

### 2025

- **2 December** – STC forces launch an [offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_Southern_Yemen_campaign) and seize towns in [Wadi Hadramaut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadi_Hadramaut), including [Seiyun](438897.md) and [Tarim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarim,_Yemen), and later the Mahrah's governatore capital [Al-Ghaydah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ghaydah). Before the end of the month, the STC controlled around 52% of Yemen's territory, including almost the entirety of former [South Yemen](718749.md).
