**Omar Mukhtar Street** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شارع عمر المختار) is the main street of [Gaza City](55387.md), in the [State of Palestine](241405.md), running from [Palestine Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Square) to the [Port of Gaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Gaza,_Rimal) in the [Rimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rimal) district, separating the Old City's [al-Daraj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Daraj) and [Zaytun Quarter](42179889.md). Gaza's hotel strip is a part of Omar Mukhtar Street, and most of Gaza's most important buildings are located along the street.[^cite_note-1]



A view west along the street in 2006

Built during [World War I](4764461.md) by Ottoman governor [Jamal Pasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djemal_Pasha), the street was originally named after him. However, following the ouster of Ottoman forces from [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_\(region\)) in 1917, Gaza's city council, headed by [Fahmi al-Husseini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahmi_al-Husseini), named the street after [Omar Mukhtar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Mukhtar), a [Libyan](17633.md) revolutionary leader. Part of the medieval *khan az-zayt* was demolished to make room for the street.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

The [British Mandatory Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine) turned Omar Mukhtar Street into a main street in 1937, using the [zoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoning) plan of the [urban planner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_planner), [Henry Kendall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Kendall_\(urban_planner\)).[^cite_note-4]
