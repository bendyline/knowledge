**Aguelhok**, also known as **Adjelhoc** (the official name), is a [rural commune](16112417.md) and village in the [Kidal Region](2214311.md) of eastern [Mali](19127.md) in the [Tessalit Cercle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessalit_Cercle). In the census of 2009 the commune had a population of 8,080.[^cite_note-pop2009-1]

## Geography

Adjelhoc is situated in the north of the [Tilemsi Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilemsi,_Gao). Located in the [sandstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandstone) [massif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massif) of the [Adrar des Ifoghas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrar_des_Ifoghas), Adjelhoc lies 430 km north of [Gao](1255721.md) and 150 km south of the Algerian border, in open [desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert).

The area of the commune is approximately 22,000 km<sup>2</sup>, about the size of [Belize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belize).[^cite_note-2]

The population is 95% [Tuareg](103054.md). The majority have a [nomadic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomad) lifestyle.

## History

In January 2012, the [Islamist](15012.md) group [Ansar Dine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ansar_Dine) [captured a military base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Aguelhok) at Aguelhok from Malian government forces.[^cite_note-WC1-3] France accused the group of summarily executing 82 soldiers and following the capture, describing the group's tactics as "Al-Qaeda-style".[^cite_note-WC1-3] In all 128-153 soldiers were killed.

On 29 July 2012, Islamists stoned a couple to death for having children outside of marriage. An official reported that many people left the town for Algeria following the incident.[^cite_note-4]

During the [2026 Mali offensives](83033878.md), the commune was attacked and captured by the [Azawad Liberation Front](78996050.md), with [Africa Corps](78298390.md) withdrawing from the area.[^cite_note-5]
