**South Kordofan** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): جنوب كردفان *_Ǧanūb Kurdufān_*) is one of the 18 [wilayat](579458.md) or states of [Sudan](27421.md). It borders [North Kordofan](767669.md) to the north, [West Kordofan](767647.md) to the west, [South Sudan](32350676.md) to the south and east, and [White Nile](767622.md) to the northeast. It has an area of 158,355 km<sup>2</sup>[^cite_note-4] and an estimated population of approximately 2,107,623 people (2018 est).[^cite_note-5] [Kaduqli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduqli) is the capital of the state. It is centered on the [Nuba Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuba_Hills). At one time it was supposed that South Kordofan was the only state in (North) Sudan suitable for producing oil, but oil has also been discovered in neighboring White Nile State in larger quantities.[^cite_note-6]

## History

Although South Kordofan is part of [Sudan](27421.md), it is home to many pro-[South Sudan](32350676.md) communities, especially in the [Nuba Mountains](1139195.md), some of whom fought alongside southern rebels during the [long civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sudanese_Civil_War).[^cite_note-7]

In 2009 and 2010, a [series of conflicts between rival nomadic tribes](23013592.md) in South Kordofan caused a large number of casualties and displaced thousands.

On June 6, 2011, An [armed conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_conflict_in_South_Kordofan_and_Blue_Nile) broke out between the forces of Northern and [Southern Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Sudan_Autonomous_Region_\(2005%E2%80%932011\)), ahead of the scheduled independence of the South on July 9. This followed an agreement for both sides to withdraw from [Abyei](13885196.md).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] On June 20, the parties agreed to demilitarize the contested area of Abyei where [Ethiopian](187749.md) peacekeepers were deployed.[^cite_note-10] Abyei is currently controlled by the [United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Interim_Security_Force_for_Abyei).[^cite_note-11]. Under the [Comprehensive Peace Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comprehensive_Peace_Agreement), residents of South Kordofan were to hold popular consultations in 2011 to determine the constitutional future of the state. However, South Kordofan governor [Ahmed Haroun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Haroun) suspended the process, and violence followed.[^cite_note-12] Haroun had previously been charged with [war crimes](48626.md) against civilians and [crime against humanity](61099.md) by the [International Criminal Court](14880.md).
