A **memorial wall** is a wall typically engraved to commemorate a number of people with something in common (e.g., from one country or place) killed in a single conflict, violent event, or disaster, often with names.

**Memorial Walls** include:

- Many memorial walls without specific names in places such as crematoriums and synagogues

- Memorial Wall of [Royal Australian Air Force Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Australian_Air_Force_Memorial)

- [Memorial Stadium (Baltimore)#Memorial wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_Stadium_\(Baltimore\)#Memorial_wall) (US)

- [Budapest Ghetto § Memorial Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest_Ghetto#Memorial_Wall)

- [Canadian National Vimy Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_National_Vimy_Memorial) (France)

- [CIA Memorial Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA_Memorial_Wall) (US)

- Memorial Wall of [Cenotaph War Memorial, Colombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cenotaph_War_Memorial,_Colombo) (Sri Lanka)

- [DIA Memorial Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DIA_Memorial_Wall) (US)

- [FDNY memorial wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FDNY_memorial_wall) (New York City, US)

- [Korean War Memorial Wall (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_War_Memorial_Wall_\(disambiguation\))

- [Korean War Memorial Wall (Canada)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_War_Memorial_Wall_\(Canada\))

- Walls of [Kranji War Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kranji_War_Memorial) (Singapore)

- [Memorial for the Disappeared](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_for_the_Disappeared) (Chile)

- [Piccadilly, Warwickshire#Miners Memorial Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piccadilly,_Warwickshire#Miners_Memorial_Wall) (England)
