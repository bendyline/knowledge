**Eslamshahr** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): اسلامشهر[^cite_note-5]), also known as **Shadshahr** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): شادشهر), is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Eslamshahr_County\)) of [Eslamshahr County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eslamshahr_County), [Tehran province](202698.md), [Iran](14653.md). It is the capital of both the county and the district.[^cite_note-Tehran_Province_Reforms_115360-6]

## Etymology

The name of Eslamshahr before the [Iranian Revolution](347268.md) was "Shadshahr". After the revolution, in 1979 the government changed the name to "Eslamshahr." The oldest historic name of Shadshahr was **Bahramabad**, now applied only to the small northern suburbs of Eslamshahr, or the village of [Bahramabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahramabad,_Tehran).

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 357,171 in 91,293 households.[^cite_note-2006_Tehran_Province-7] The following census in 2011 counted 389,102 people in 112,487 households.[^cite_note-2011_Tehran_Province-8] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 448,129 people in 137,638 households.[^cite_note-2016_Tehran_Province-3]

The city has grown to be the most populous non-provincial capital city and the 19th overall most populous city of the country.

## Geography

The city is located on the [Saveh Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_65_\(Iran\)), which starts in the south of Tehran, and ends at [Saveh City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saveh_City). Its neighborhoods are Vavan, Ghaemieh, Saeedieh, Mohamadieh, Mahdieh, Baghenarde, Saloor, Noori, Ghasemabad, Shirudi, Golha, Elahiye, Ahmadabad, Ghods, Baghqfeiz, Zarafshan, Moosiabad, Anbia and Mianabad.

## History

From January 17 to 18, 2026, as part of the [2025–2026 Iranian protests](81976180.md), youths attacked the local [Ghaemiyeh police station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghaemiyeh_police_station?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-9]

An airstrike struck a residential building in the city on April 6, 2026, during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), killing at least 13 people.[^cite_note-10]

## Climate

&#x20;Eslamshahr has a [cold semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_semi-arid_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BSk*). The highest recorded temperature was 43 °C (109 °F), recorded on July 10, 1990, while the lowest recorded temperature was −13 °C (9 °F), recorded on January 2, 1973.[^cite_note-Voodoo_Skies-11]

## Population

Shadshahr was a relatively small town in the early 70s, under the Shah's rule, gradually through the 1980s, 1990s, 2000s as Tehran's population grew, so did the surrounding areas.&#x20;

## Transportation

Eslamshahr is known for its handcrafted articles and its good weather. It also has [Imam Khomeini International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran_Imam_Khomeini_International_Airport) within its city limits.
