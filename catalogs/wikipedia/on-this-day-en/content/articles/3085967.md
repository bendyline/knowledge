**Be'er Ya'akov** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): בְּאֵר יַעֲקֹב, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Jacob's Well') is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City) in [central](844282.md) [Israel](9282173.md), near [Ness Ziona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ness_Ziona) and [Rishon Lezion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rishon_Lezion). The town has an area of 8,580 [dunams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunam) (\~8.6 km<sup>2</sup>),[^cite_note-profile-3] and had a population of 37,491 in 2024.[^cite_note-Israelpopulations-2]

## History



[Be'er Ya'akov railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Be'er_Ya'akov_railway_station)

Be'er Ya'akov was established in 1907 on 2,000 dunams of land purchased by a company headed by [Meir Dizengoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meir_Dizengoff) from a Lutheran German colony the previous year. It was divided into two sectors, one for immigrants from [Russia](25391.md), [Poland](22936.md), [Romania](25445.md), [Bulgaria](3415.md), [Argentina](18951905.md), and [Iran](14653.md), and the other for [Mountain Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_Jews) from [Dagestan](751099.md). It was named after [Yaakov Yitzhaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaakov_Yitzhaki), a rabbi and pioneer from the Mountain Jewish community.[^cite_note-hareuveni_lexicon-4] Yitzhaki headed the Mountain Jewish pioneers who settled there.

In 1909, 25 families were living in Be'er Ya'akov, and tensions existed between the [Ashkenazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkenazi_Jews) and Dagestani families.[^cite_note-5] In 1910, the first elementary school was established. According to a [census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1922_census_of_Palestine) conducted in 1922 by the [British Mandate authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandate_for_Palestine), Be'er Ya'akov had 131 inhabitants,[^cite_note-Census1922-6] which had increased in the 1931 census to 265 residents in 58 houses.[^cite_note-Census1931-7] By 1947, it had a population of 400.[^cite_note-JNF_1948-8] It achieved local council status in 1949.

Be'er Ya'akov 1945 1\:250,000

During the [1948 Arab–Israeli War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Arab%E2%80%93Israeli_War), and until the Israeli capture of [Ramla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramla) in July 1948, Be'er Ya'akov was on the front line. The population at that time was evacuated and a new settlement, Be'er Shalom, was established nearby by members of [Kibbutz Buchenwald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netzer_Sereni), the first pioneer training group formed in post-World War II Germany.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

In 2017, a plan was approved to build on the land vacated by the [Tzrifin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tzrifin) military bases which are being relocated to the [Negev](142273.md). The plan envisions Be'er Ya'akov with a population of 100,000.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] Be'er Ya'akov is currently undergoing a construction boom, with numerous residential and commercial developments planned or under construction, along with numerous schools and daycare centers, cultural institutions, and a 1,000-seat sports arena. A metro system for the city which will terminate at [Ben Gurion International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Gurion_International_Airport) is also planned, with work scheduled to commence in 2028.[^cite_note-13]

## Economy



Be'er Ya'akov Shopping mall

[IAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Aerospace_Industries)'s MLM Division, Israel's main missile assembly facility is located in the south of Be'er Ya'akov. The [Jericho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jericho_\(missile\)) and [Arrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrow_3) missiles and the [Shavit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shavit_2) launch vehicle are assembled there. The facility area is situated east of Diezengoff Street.[^cite_note-16]

## Healthcare

Two hospitals are located in Be'er Ya'akov: [Yitzhak Shamir Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yitzhak_Shamir_Medical_Center) (near [Tzrifin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tzrifin)), and Shmuel HaRofe Geriatric Hospital.

## Sports

- [Maccabi Be'er Ya'akov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maccabi_Be'er_Ya'akov_F.C.) is the local [football](10568.md) club.

- [Maccabi Be'er Ya'akov B.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maccabi_Be'er_Ya'akov_B.C.?action=edit\&redlink=1), the local [basketball](3921.md) club, plays in [Liga Leumit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liga_Leumit_\(basketball\)), the second tier.

## Transportation

Be'er Ya'akov is served by the [Be'er Ya'akov Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Be'er_Ya'akov_Railway_Station), for trains on the [Binyamina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binyamina)-[Ashkelon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkelon) line.

## Voting Patterns

In the [2022 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Israeli_legislative_election), 57 percent of voters in Be'er Ya'akov voted for the right-wing coalition led by [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md), while 41 percent voted for the parties of the parties of the center-left coalition led by [Yair Lapid](2448072.md) and 0.1 percent voted for the Arab parties.[^cite_note-17]
