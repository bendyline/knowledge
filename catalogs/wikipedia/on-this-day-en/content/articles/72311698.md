**Petropavlivka** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Петропавлівка, [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Петропавловка, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Russian): *Petropavlovka*) is a village in [Kupiansk Raion](8613952.md), [Kharkiv Oblast](494901.md), Ukraine. The village is located about 110.4 kilometres (68.6 mi) east by south ([EbS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Points_of_the_compass#32-wind_compass_rose)) of the centre of [Kharkiv](2788558.md). The village also hosts the administration of the Petropavlivka rural hromada, one of the [hromadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hromada) of Ukraine.

The settlement [came under attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_occupation_of_Kharkiv_Oblast) by [Russian forces](25709.md) during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) in 2022 [and was regained](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Kharkiv_counteroffensive) by [Ukrainian forces](42799055.md) by the end of September the same year.[^cite_note-ISW_2022-09-24-2] In September 2024, Russian forces advanced into northern Petropavlivka.[^cite_note-3] By 20 November 2025, Russian forces managed to recapture the settlement of Petropavlivka.[^cite_note-4]

## History

- 1821 — date of first mention.

  [^cite_note-autolink1-5]

- 1872 — date of renaming to the village of "Petropavlovka".

  [^cite_note-autolink1-5]

- On February 27, 2022, the Russian Armed Forces captured Petropavlivka.

- On October 2, 2022, during the Ukrainian counteroffensive, the Ukrainian Armed Forces recaptured Petropavlivka.

- By 20 November 2025, Russian forces again recaptured the village.
