**Lipetsk Oblast**[^cite_note-9] is a [federal subject of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_subject_of_Russia) (an [oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblast)).[^cite_note-DateEstablished-1] Its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) is the [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) of [Lipetsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipetsk). As of the [2021 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Census_\(2021\)), its population was 1,143,224.[^cite_note-2021Census-10]

## Geography

Lipetsk Oblast borders with [Ryazan Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryazan_Oblast) (NE), [Tambov Oblast](480933.md) (E), [Voronezh Oblast](473401.md) (S), [Kursk Oblast](480916.md) (SW), [Oryol Oblast](480945.md) (W), and [Tula Oblast](480930.md) (NW).

## History

According to archaeologists and historians, the territory of the modern Lipetsk Oblast has been inhabited since ancient times. The [Don Slavs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Slavs) were probably the first Slavic tribe to settle the area. After the disintegration of the [Kievan Rus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kievan_Rus), the area belonged to the [Principality of Chernigov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Chernigov).

When the [Mongol hordes invaded the area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongol_invasion_of_Rus') in the 1230s, it had the following settlements: Yelets (the most important city), Dobrinsk (presumed to be the village of Dobroye), Dubok (presumed to be the village Dubki) ([Dankovsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dankovsky_District)), Staroye Gorodische (presumably Bogorodskoye of the Dankovsky district), Vorgol, Onuza, Voronozh, Lipets and others. The latter four towns were destroyed by the invaders.

Taking advantage of the weakness of the Principality of Chernigov, the [Principality of Ryazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Ryazan) seized all the lands of the upper Don (including the basin of the [Voronezh River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronezh_River)) and annexed them. The newly acquired territories in the south of the principality were sometimes referred to as the "Ryazan ukraine" (outland). [Yelets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yelets), however, had its own princes in the late 14th century. One of these, [Fyodor Ivanovich](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A4%D1%91%D0%B4%D0%BE%D1%80%20%D0%98%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87%20\(%D0%BA%D0%BD%D1%8F%D0%B7%D1%8C%20%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B5%D1%86%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9\)), [unsuccessfully tried to defend his town](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A0%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5%20%D0%95%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%86%D0%B0%20\(1395\)) from [Tamerlane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamerlane) (who [was pursuing, in 1395, his enemy Tokhtamysh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokhtamysh%E2%80%93Timur_war) through the forest-steppe).

The revival of the territory began after the expulsion of the nomads. In a relatively short period of time (end of the 16th and early 17th centuries) several forts were established by the [Tsardom of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsardom_of_Russia).

In the 18th century, the creation of the navy and the regular army increased the need for flax, [hemp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemp) and [wool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wool). So begins to actively develop agriculture.

The 18th century saw the continued growth of large land estates. Lipetsk region, with its rich black earth, was the breadbasket of the Russian state. Subsequently, it became widely known as a resort town, mainly because of its mineral waters.

During the [February Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_Revolution), the [October Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_Revolution) of 1917 and the [Russian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War), the lives of many cultural values, private collections of art and literature, but because of the ensuing repression against the church and the "bourgeois past" seriously affected the architectural ensembles of the estates of the nobility, monasteries and churches.

The modern oblast was formed by the [decree](322949.md) of the [Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidium_of_the_Supreme_Soviet_of_the_USSR) on January 6, 1954 from parts of [Voronezh](473401.md), [Ryazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryazan_Oblast), [Tambov](480933.md), [Tula](480930.md) and [Oryol Oblasts](480945.md).[^cite_note-DateEstablished-1]

## Politics



Seat of the government of Lipetsk Oblast

See also: [List of Governors of Lipetsk Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Governors_of_Lipetsk_Oblast)

During the [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) period, the high authority in the oblast was shared between three persons: The first secretary of the Lipetsk CPSU Committee (who in reality had the biggest authority), the chairman of the oblast Soviet (legislative power), and the chairman of the oblast Executive Committee (executive power). Since 1991, CPSU lost all the power, and the head of the Oblast administration, and eventually the governor was appointed/elected alongside elected [regional parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_parliaments_of_Russia).

The Charter of Lipetsk Oblast is the fundamental law of the region. The current charter was adopted on 27 March, 2003. The [Lipetsk Oblast Council of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipetsk_Oblast_Council_of_Deputies) is the province's standing legislative (representative) body. The Council of Deputies consists of 56 deputies elected for a five-year term by the Oblast's inhabitants and exercises its authority by passing laws, resolutions, and other legal acts and by supervising the implementation and observance of the laws and other legal acts passed by it. The current head of the Council of Deputies is [Pavel Putilin](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%9F%D1%83%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%BD,%20%D0%9F%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B5%D0%BB%20%D0%98%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87).

The highest executive body is the Oblast Government, which includes territorial executive bodies such as district administrations, committees, and commissions that facilitate development and run the day to day matters of the province. The Oblast administration supports the activities of the [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Lipetsk_Oblast) who is the highest official, who is elected by the Oblast's inhabitants for a five year term and acts as guarantor of the observance of the oblast Charter in accordance with the [Constitution of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Russia).

Since 2019, the Governor is [Igor Artamonov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Artamonov).

## Economy

The most important industrial branches are the [iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron) processing and the mechanical [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering). The most industrialized cities are [Lipetsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipetsk), the administrative center, and [Yelets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yelets). The region's fuel and energy complex is represented by petroleum product marketing companies, a network of consumer gas pipelines, and a power grid.

The largest companies in the region include [NLMK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NLMK) (revenues of \$7.06 billion in 2017), [Cherkizovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherkizovo) Pig Farming (\$421.65 million), [JSC Progress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JSC_Progress?action=edit\&redlink=1) (baby food manufacturer\$, 347.94 million), and the local branch of [Indesit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indesit) (\$342.8 million).[^cite_note-regioncompanies-11]

### Agriculture

Crop cultivation and horticulture form the basis of the region's agriculture. Livestock farming specializes in [cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle), [pigs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pig), [goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goat), [sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheep), and [poultry](23197.md). The processing industry is also well developed.

## Attractions



The world's first [hyperboloid structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperboloid_structure) by [Vladimir Shukhov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Shukhov)

The world's first [hyperboloid structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperboloid_structure)—[a steel open-work lattice tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shukhov_Tower_in_Polibino)—is located in [Polibino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polibino,_Dankovsky_District,_Lipetsk_Oblast), [Dankovsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dankovsky_District) of Lipetsk Oblast. The hyperboloid tower was built and patented in 1896 by the famous Russian engineer and scientist [Vladimir Shukhov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Shukhov). The [hyperboloid structures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperboloid_structure) were subsequently built by other architects, such as [Antoni Gaudí](31566384.md), [Le Corbusier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Corbusier), and [Oscar Niemeyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Niemeyer).
