[Spain](26667.md) first participated at the [Olympic Games](22576.md) in 1900, and has sent athletes to compete in most [Summer Olympic Games](27566.md) since 1920. The nation has also participated in every [Winter Olympic Games](34069.md) since 1936. Its team is organised by the [Spanish Olympic Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Olympic_Committee) (COE, *[Comité Olímpico Español](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comit%C3%A9%20Ol%C3%ADmpico%20Espa%C3%B1ol)*) created in 1924.

Spain withdrew from the [1936 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1936_Summer_Olympics) in [Nazi Germany](21212.md) because of the [Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Civil_War), and also boycotted the [1956 Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_Summer_Olympics) in [Melbourne](17306237.md) because of the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)'s [invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Revolution_of_1956) of [Hungary](13275.md). The [equestrian events](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equestrian_at_the_1956_Summer_Olympics) in 1956 were held five months earlier in [Stockholm](26741.md), and Spain did participate in those events.

Spain hosted the [1992 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Summer_Olympics) in [Barcelona](4443.md).

[Spanish](963152.md) athletes have won a total of 195 medals in 34 different sports as of 2026 and the country currently ranks 25th in the [all-time Summer Olympics medal count](3729318.md), with [canoeing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canoeing_at_the_Summer_Olympics) as the top medal-producing sport and [sailing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_at_the_Summer_Olympics) as the top gold medal-producing sport. Eight of those medals were won at the Winter Games.

The COE counts one medal more than the IOC because they recognized one won by [Pedro José Pidal y Bernaldo de Quirós](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pidal,_1st_Marquess_of_Villaviciosa_de_Asturias) in [live pigeon shooting in the 1900 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shooting_at_the_1900_Summer_Olympics#Non-Olympic_events), while the IOC doesn't recognize the event as an official one as the winners were given a money award.[^cite_note-1]
