The **First Takaichi cabinet** was the 104th [Cabinet of Japan](573784.md), formed on 21 October 2025 when [Sanae Takaichi](8125532.md) was appointed [Prime Minister](24833.md) by Emperor [Naruhito](266108.md) as designated by the [National Diet](163847.md), following the resignation of [Shigeru Ishiba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shigeru_Ishiba) after the [2025 Liberal Democratic Party presidential election](80506510.md).

The country's first woman-led government, it is also the first time that the [Japan Innovation Party](48459506.md) (JIP) will participate in the national government following the collapse of the [LDP–Komeito coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LDP%E2%80%93Komeito_coalition), which had governed from 1999 to 2009 and from 2012 to 2025, and the formation of the [LDP–JIP coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LDP%E2%80%93JIP_coalition).[^cite_note-1]
