**Troels Lund Poulsen** (born 30 March 1976) is a Danish politician who has served as leader of [Venstre](403109.md) since November 2023. He has been a member of the [Folketing](429743.md) since [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Danish_general_election) and has held several ministerial offices in Danish governments.

In November 2007 he joined the [Rasmussen III Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anders_Fogh_Rasmussen_III_Cabinet), as [Minister for the Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_the_Environment_\(Denmark\)) and later served as [Minister for Taxation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Taxation_\(Denmark\)) from 2010 to 2011, [Minister of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Education_\(Denmark\)) in 2011, [Minister of Commerce, Business and Growth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Business_Affairs_\(Denmark\)) from 2015 to 2016, and [Minister for Employment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Employment_\(Denmark\)) from 2016 to 2019.

He rejoined government in December 2022, serving in the [Frederiksen II Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederiksen_II_Cabinet). He was initially appointed [Minister for Economic Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Economic_Affairs_\(Denmark\)) and later became [Minister of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Defence_\(Denmark\)) in August 2023. Poulsen additionally assumed the role of [Deputy Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Denmark) in October 2023 and left in June 2026.

## Early life

Poulsen was born on 30 March 1976 in [Vejle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vejle), as an only child to Aage Poulsen, a teacher, and Lise Poulsen.[^cite_note-\:1-3] He grew up on a farm in [Lindved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindved) and attended Lindved School from 1982 to 1992,[^cite_note-4] later graduating from [Tørring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%B8rring) [gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_\(Denmark\)) in 1995.[^cite_note-\:1-3]

In 1996, Poulsen began studying history at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen). He remained enrolled until 2001, when he was elected to the Folketing and subsequently gave up on his studies.[^cite_note-5]

## Political career

Poulsen joined [Venstre](403109.md)’s youth wing, [Venstres Ungdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venstres_Ungdom) (VU), in 1992. He served as chairman of the VU branch in [Tørring-Uldum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%B8rring-Uldum_Municipality) from 1993 to 1994, and the national chairman of VU from 1997 to 1999.

He was first elected to the Folketing in the [2001 Danish general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Danish_general_election), representing the Juelsminde constituency, later renamed the [Hedensted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedensted_\(nomination_district\)).[^cite_note-6] He was re-elected in 2005 and subsequent elections.

From 2001 to 2006, Poulsen served as Venstre’s spokesperson on foreign affairs and gender equality. In September 2006, he became the party’s political spokesperson.&#x20;

In November 2007 he joined the [Rasmussen III Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anders_Fogh_Rasmussen_III_Cabinet), as [Minister for the Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_the_Environment_\(Denmark\)). In February 2010, he became [Minister for Taxation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Taxation_\(Denmark\)). His tenure was marked by a major political controversy in 2011 involving leaked tax information concerning [Stephen Kinnock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Kinnock), the husband of opposition leader [Helle Thorning-Schmidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helle_Thorning-Schmidt).[^cite_note-\:2-7][^cite_note-8] Although Poulsen denied involvement in any leak or political interference, the case led to a formal inquiry.[^cite_note-\:2-7] A later commission found no evidence that he had ordered the leak or attempted to influence the tax authorities, but criticised his handling of the matter, resulting in a parliamentary reprimand.[^cite_note-9]

On 8 March 2011, he was appointed [Minister of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_minister), following [Tina Nedergaard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_Nedergaard)'s resignation on the same day.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

On 28 June 2015, he was appointed Minister of Business and Growth in the government of Lars Løkke Rasmussen II.[^cite_note-12] On 28 November 2016, he was moved from here to the position of [Minister of Employment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Employment_\(Denmark\)).[^cite_note-13]

He was appointed minister of economic affairs on 15 December 2022 when [Mette Frederiksen](26040044.md) presented her second cabinet.[^cite_note-list-14] On 6 February 2023, he became acting minister of defence while [Jakob Ellemann-Jensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakob_Ellemann-Jensen) was on leave.[^cite_note-15] On 22 August 2023, the appointment became permanent. On 23 October 2023, he replaced Ellemann-Jensen as [Deputy Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_\(Denmark\)) after his resignation as party leader of Venstre.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] He was elected leader of Venstre on 18 November 2023,[^cite_note-\:0-18] with Stephanie Lose taking over as minister of economic affairs.[^cite_note-19]

On 9 February 2024, he stated, "Denmark should speed up its military investments after new intelligence indicates that Russia is rearming faster than expected and that it could attack a NATO country within three to five years".[^cite_note-20]



Troels Lund Poulsen taking questions from journalists at Christiansborg Palace in December 2025

On 3 April 2024, he fired [Chief of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Defence_\(Denmark\)) [Flemming Lentfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemming_Lentfer), saying that Lentfer had failed to keep him informed about weapons failures on board [HDMS *Iver Huitfeldt*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HDMS_Iver_Huitfeldt_\(F361\)) when it fought in [Operation Prosperity Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Prosperity_Guardian) during armed combat against [Houthi militants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_movement).[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-politico-22]

In 2025, the [Auditor general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auditor_general) criticised him, and previous ministers, for inadequate security at 18 military bases.[^cite_note-23]

## Personal life

Lund Poulsen was in a relationship with fellow Venstre politician [Sophie Løhde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_L%C3%B8hde) from 2007 to 2013.[^cite_note-\:2-7] He is married to Anne Simonsen, a former secretary at the Ministry of Taxation. The couple have a daughter, born in 2018,[^cite_note-\:0-18] and live on a farm at Birkehøjgaard near [Tølløse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%B8ll%C3%B8se).[^cite_note-\:2-7]
