**Ahmad al-Jaber Air Base** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **XIJ**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **OKAJ**) is a [Kuwait Air Force](4641117.md) base that is home to 3 Kuwait Air Force F/A-18 C/D squadrons: 9 Squadron, 25 Squadron, and 61 Squadron. The base also has an area designated for operations by the [U.S. Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Air_Force) and its allies.

## History

### Invasion of Kuwait

When Iraq launched the [1990 invasion of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_of_Kuwait), [Iraqi Air Force](1817852.md) jets dropped air-scattered mines, preventing operations on the runways. This led to the [Kuwaiti Air Force](4641117.md) [Mirage F1s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dassault_Mirage_F1) and [A-4 Skyhawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_A-4_Skyhawk) having to land on a road nearby.[^cite_note-1]

### Lead up to the Gulf War

After the invasion, the US, which was preparing for Operation Desert Storm, feared that al-Jaber housed [Iraqi chemical weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_chemical_weapons_program) mainly because the Iraqi army had deployed 30 [howitzers](14392.md) and used the Kuwaiti [hardened concrete hangars](1011776.md) at al-Jaber for munitions storage. These howitzers, known as [GHN-45](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GC-45_howitzer), were notable for being preferred for chemical munition delivery by the Iraqis.[^cite_note-\:2-2]

### 1991 Gulf War

During the [1991 Gulf War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War), coalition aircraft attacked Ahmad al-Jaber Airbase several times due to the chemical munitions storage rumors. They also dropped [anti-personnel mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti--_personnel_mine?action=edit\&redlink=1) to impede base operations.[^cite_note-\:2-2] On the first night of the war at around 4\:00 AM, three [A-6E TRAM Intruders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grumman_A-6_Intruder) from [VA-115 Eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VFA-115) ([Carrier Air Wing 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_Air_Wing_Five)) based on the [USS Midway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Midway_\(CV-41\)) attacked the airbase with [six Mk.83 bombs each](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_83_bomb).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Later that morning at 8\:50 AM, 12 [French Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Air_and_Space_Force) [SEPECAT Jaguars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SEPECAT_Jaguar) from [EC 2/11 *Vosges*](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escadron%20de%20chasse%202/11%20Vosges) attacked Al-Jaber with no less than four aircraft sustaining damage (three were hit by IR-[SAMs](182664.md) and one by small arms fire) although all returned safely.[^cite_note-5]

As of 2022, satellite images show aircraft [Hardened aircraft shelters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardened_aircraft_shelter) (HAS) still unrepaired throughout the air base [^cite_note-6]

### Post War use

On 1 December 1998, the 4406th Operations Group (Provisional) at Al-Jaber Air Base was inactivated and the [332nd Air Expeditionary Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/332nd_Air_Expeditionary_Group) activated.[^cite_note-7] Sitting 75 miles south of the [Iraqi](7515928.md) border, the base has supported active duty United States [Air National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_National_Guard) and [Air Force Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Reserve) [Fairchild Republic A-10 Thunderbolt II](12502446.md) and [General Dynamics F-16 Fighting Falcon](11642.md) fighter units, along with support individuals, rotated in and out. At Al-Jaber AB, the 332 ELS Commander and 10 personnel were on one-year tours; all others (1190 personnel) rotated every 90 to 120 days.

According to [USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today), on 22 October 2003, the US Ambassador to Kuwait, Richard Jones, announced that the United States was reducing its presence at Al-Jaber to fully reduce its forces at that location. USAF assets remaining in Kuwait are primarily stationed at [Ali Al Salem Air Base](1469367.md).

The U.S. side of the base was re-opened around July 2010 in support of Operation New Dawn.

The U.S. side of the base was re-opened in 2014 sometime before October.

From 22 November 2014 four [Panavia Tornados](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panavia_Tornado) from [6º Stormo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6%C2%BA_Stormo) of the [Italian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Air_Force) deployed there for an unknown amount of time[^cite_note-AFMFEB15-8] along with a [Boeing KC-767](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_KC-767)A from [14º Stormo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14%C2%BA_Stormo).[^cite_note-AFMJAN15-9]

Between 15 October 2014 and 30 September 2015, the [Royal Danish Air Force](548819.md) deployed seven [F-16AM Fighting Falcons](11642.md) with four being operational and three others in a reserve capacity.[^cite_note-AFMDEC15-10]

## Role and operations

### Kuwait Air Force

The base is home to the Kuwait Air Force's entire fighter/attack aircraft fleet. Two front-line (9 Squadron and 25 Squadron) and one training unit (61 Squadron) operate a fleet of [McDonnell Douglas KAF-18C/D Hornets](45998.md).[^cite_note-\:0-11] The aircraft is a [twin-engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twinjet), [supersonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersonic_aircraft), all-weather, [multirole combat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multirole_combat_aircraft) jet, designed as both a [fighter](10929.md) and [attack aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_aircraft).

### Military intervention against ISIL

Further information: [Military intervention against ISIL order of battle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_intervention_against_ISIL_order_of_battle)

A MV-22B Osprey of the type which has been operated by the United States Marines from Ahmad al-Jaber Air Base.

A [MV-22B Osprey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Boeing_V-22_Osprey) of the type which has been operated by the [United States Marines](17349325.md) from Ahmad al-Jaber Air Base.

Ahmad al-Jaber hosts the [United States Air Force](32090.md)'s [332nd Air Expeditionary Wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/332d_Air_Expeditionary_Wing) (332 AEW), which moved to the base in May 2016.[^cite_note-AFMJUN15-12][^cite_note-\:1-13] The 332 ARW provides a wide range combat capabilities including [aerial-refueling](237949.md), [Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance](650971.md) (ISR), space, [combat search and rescue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_search_and_rescue) (CSAR), and precision strike, all in support of the [military intervention against ISIL/ISIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_intervention_against_ISIL) (known by the US military as [Operation Inherent Resolve](44120613.md)) and wider military operations in the Middle East. A wide range of US Air Force aircraft have been deployed at Ahmad al-Jaber, including [F-15E Strike Eagle](495724.md), [MQ-9A Reaper](4769988.md), and [KC-135R Stratotanker](17441.md) as well as [HH-60G Pave Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_HH-60_Pave_Hawk) helicopters.[^cite_note-\:1-13] The United States Marines has also operated from the base, flying the [MV-22B Osprey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Boeing_V-22_Osprey), [EA-6B Prowler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_EA-6B_Prowler), [AV-8B Harrier II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonnell_Douglas_AV-8B_Harrier_II) and [KC-130J Hercules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_KC-130).[^cite_note-14]
