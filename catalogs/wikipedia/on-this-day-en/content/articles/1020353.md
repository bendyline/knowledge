**Fagersta** is a [locality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_areas_of_Sweden) and the seat of [Fagersta Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fagersta_Municipality) in [Västmanland County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4stmanland_County), [Sweden](5058739.md), with 11,130 inhabitants in 2010.[^cite_note-scb-1]

## Geography

The city is located at the junction of two railways between [Ludvika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludvika)-[Västerås](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4ster%C3%A5s) and [Avesta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avesta_Municipality) (Krylbo)-[Örebro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%96rebro), in the heart of the historic [Bergslagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergslagen) region which is rich in [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper) and [iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron) ore. The [60th parallel north](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/60th_parallel_north) passes through the town.&#x20;

## History

There has been mining activity in the Fagersta area since the 15th century but it was not organized until the 17th century and was not made a corporation until 1873, when [Fagersta Bruks AB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fagersta_Bruks_AB?action=edit\&redlink=1) was founded.[^cite_note-2]

Fagersta was made a city in 1944 when the industrial hotspot of Fagersta merged with its service-oriented neighbour [Västanfors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4stanfors). It is now the seat of Fagersta Municipality. The [Fagersta airspace surveillance tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fagersta_airspace_surveillance_tower) is a [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War)-era observation platform constructed to protect a nearby steel mill.

## Industry

Today's industry is focused on hard metal tools ([Seco Tools AB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seco_Tools_AB?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Atlas Copco Secoroc AB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlas_Copco_Secoroc_AB?action=edit\&redlink=1)) and stainless steel products ([Fagersta Stainless AB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fagersta_Stainless_AB?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Outokumpu Stainless Tubular Products AB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OUTOKUMPU_Stainless_Tubular_Products_AB?action=edit\&redlink=1)).
