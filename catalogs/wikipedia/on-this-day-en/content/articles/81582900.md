**El Encuentro** ([Spanish](26825.md) for "The Encounter"), officially the **Santa Elena No. 1 Detention Center**[^cite_note-1] is a [maximum security prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_security_prison) in [Santa Elena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Elena,_Ecuador), Ecuador. The prison opened on 11 November 2025 when it accepted a transfer of 300 prisoners from the La Roca prison.

## History

In January 2024, Ecuadorian president [Daniel Noboa](73980059.md) announced the construction of two prisons to incarcerate 12,000 inmates. Noboa's announcement came during a [conflict against criminal gangs](75768010.md) in the country. He stated that both prisons would be modeled on the [Terrorism Confinement Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorism_Confinement_Center) (CECOT) built in El Salvador in 2023 to address a [similar gang crackdown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvadoran_gang_crackdown).[^cite_note-2]

Construction on the prison in [Santa Elena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Elena,_Ecuador), known as "El Encuentro", began on 21 June 2024.[^cite_note-ET-3] On 11 November 2025, 300 prisoners were transferred from the La Roca prison to El Encuentro. Among the transferred prisoners was [Jorge Glas](38822595.md), a former [Vice President of Ecuador](17370343.md) who is serving 21 years imprisonment for [embezzlement](209411.md) and [bribery](260979.md).[^cite_note-4]

## Facility

El Encuentro covers a land area of 37 hectares (91 acres).[^cite_note-5] El Encuentro has six guard towers and 9-meter-tall (30 ft) walls. The prison has a capacity of 800 inmates. Those incarcerated at El Encuentro are considered to be highly dangerous and escape risks.[^cite_note-ET-3]
