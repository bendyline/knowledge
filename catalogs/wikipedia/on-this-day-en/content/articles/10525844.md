# Best Team ESPY Award

&#x20;The **Best Team [ESPY Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPY_Award)** has been presented annually since [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_in_sports) to the [professional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_sports), [collegiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_sports), or [national](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_team) [team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_sports), irrespective of nationality[^cite_note-1] or [sport contested](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sports), adjudged to be the best in a specified twelve-month period preceding the awards ceremony. In [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_in_sports), the award was bifurcated and the resulting honors were styled as the **[College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_sports) Team of the Year** and **[Pro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_sports) Team of the Year ESPY Award**s; the awards were joined once more in [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_in_sports).

Between 1993 and [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_in_sports), the award voting panel comprised variously [fans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan_\(aficionado\)); [sportswriters](558219.md) and [broadcasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_presenter), sports [executives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_officer), and retired [sportspersons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportsperson), termed collectively *experts*; and [ESPN personalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ESPN_personalities), but balloting thereafter has been exclusively by fans over the [Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet) from amongst choices selected by the ESPN Select Nominating Committee.

Through the [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_in_sports) iteration of the ESPY Awards, ceremonies were conducted in February of each year to honor achievements over the previous [calendar year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calendar_year); awards presented thereafter are conferred in July and reflect performance from the June previous.[^cite_note-2] The award wasn't awarded in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_ESPY_Awards) due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).
