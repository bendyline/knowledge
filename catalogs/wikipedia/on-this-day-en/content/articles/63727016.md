**Super Bowl LX** was an [American football](18951490.md) game played to determine the champion of the [National Football League](21211.md) (NFL) for the [2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NFL_season). The [National Football Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_Conference) (NFC) champion [Seattle Seahawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Seattle_Seahawks_season) defeated the [American Football Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Football_Conference) (AFC) champion [New England Patriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_New_England_Patriots_season) 29–13. The game took place on February 8, 2026, at [Levi's Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi's_Stadium) in [Santa Clara, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara,_California), the second Super Bowl held in the stadium and the third held in the [San Francisco Bay Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Bay_Area).

The Seahawks' victory was their second, previously having won 2013's [Super Bowl XLVIII](4057023.md) during their [Legion of Boom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Boom) era. Led by their [Dark Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Seattle_Seahawks_season#The_Dark_Side) defense, they were making their fourth appearance with a 14–3 record and the NFC's top seed. The Patriots, who finished with a 14–3 record and the AFC's second seed, extended their record of appearances to 12. They were seeking a record seventh title and their first since the [Brady–Belichick era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brady%E2%80%93Belichick_era) after last winning 2018's [Super Bowl LIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LIII). The two franchises previously met in [Super Bowl XLIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XLIX), which was won by the Patriots.

Seattle scored the only points of the first three quarters, converting four field goals to take a 12–0 lead. In the fourth quarter, the Seahawks scored the first touchdown of the game before the Patriots responded with their own touchdown to cut the score to 19–7. A series of New England turnovers, however, allowed the Seahawks to score 10 consecutive points and put the game out of reach. With the loss, the Patriots became the first team to lose six Super Bowls. Running back [Kenneth Walker III](68758945.md) was named [Super Bowl MVP](291253.md) after rushing for 135 yards and helping set up several of Seattle's field goals. He was the first running back to win the award since [Terrell Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrell_Davis) in 1997's [Super Bowl XXXII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXXII).

The game was broadcast on [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC) in addition to [streaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streaming) on [NBCUniversal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBCUniversal)'s [Peacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_\(streaming_service\)).[^cite_note-5] It became NBC's most-watched program in its history at around 125 million viewers worldwide and peaked at around 137 million worldwide, a 2% (\~2 million viewers) decline from [Super Bowl LIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LIX) a year prior.[^cite_note-6] Similarly, the [Super Bowl LX halftime show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LX_halftime_show) headlined by [Puerto Rican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Ricans) rapper [Bad Bunny](55943877.md) featuring [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga) and [Ricky Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Martin) also represented a decline in viewership by 4% (\~5 million viewers) at 128 million viewers.[^cite_note-RickPorter-7] After the game, several sports outlets referred to the game as being among the worst Super Bowls due to both teams' offensive struggles,[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] although the performance of the Seahawks defense was praised.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

## Background

### Host selection



[Levi's Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi's_Stadium) in [Santa Clara, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara,_California), the venue of the game

The NFL has made all decisions regarding hosting sites since organizing [Super Bowl LVII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LVII) in 2022, at which point they removed the bidding process. The new process meant that potential hosts would put together a proposal, with the league owners then voting to determine viability.[^cite_note-15] On May 22, 2023, the NFL announced that Super Bowl LX would be played at [Levi's Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi's_Stadium) in [Santa Clara, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara,_California), home of the [San Francisco 49ers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_49ers). This is the third Super Bowl in the [San Francisco Bay Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Bay_Area) and second at Levi's Stadium. [Super Bowl XIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XIX), played at [Stanford Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_Stadium) in 1985, was won by [San Francisco 49ers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984_San_Francisco_49ers_season) over the [Miami Dolphins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984_Miami_Dolphins_season). More recently, [Super Bowl 50](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_50), played at Levi's Stadium in 2016, featured the [Denver Broncos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Denver_Broncos_season) defeating the [Carolina Panthers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Carolina_Panthers_season).[^cite_note-16]

### Logo

As has been a tradition since [Super Bowl LVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LVI), the logo includes [Roman numerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals) featuring imagery from the host city/region. For Super Bowl LX, the logo was revealed on February 9, 2025, in a social media post by game broadcaster [NBC Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC_Sports) following the conclusion of [Super Bowl LIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LIX),[^cite_note-17] ahead of a formal unveiling at a post-game press conference in [New Orleans](53842.md) the next day.[^cite_note-Breech-2025-18] The Roman numerals have a [CMYK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CMYK) theme, incorporating Bay Area landmarks such as the [San Francisco skyline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_skyline), the [Golden Gate Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Gate_Bridge), and [redwood trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redwood_trees).[^cite_note-Breech-2025-18]

### Super Bowl week events

The [Super Bowl Experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_Experience) was hosted at the [Moscone Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscone_Center) in [San Francisco](49728.md).[^cite_note-Furtado-2025-19] In October 2025, it was announced that the [2026 Pro Bowl Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pro_Bowl_Games) would be held on the Tuesday prior to the Super Bowl, February 3, at the Moscone Center, downsizing the event and integrating it into the Super Bowl's festivities.[^cite_note-Furtado-2025-19]

## Teams

### Seattle Seahawks

Main article: [2025 Seattle Seahawks season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Seattle_Seahawks_season)



[Kenneth Walker III](68758945.md) led the Seahawks in rushing yards.

The Seattle Seahawks finished the 2025 season with a 14–3 record under second-year head coach [Mike Macdonald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Macdonald).[^cite_note-20] This was their fourth appearance, having last won 2013's [Super Bowl XLVIII](4057023.md) and last appeared in [Super Bowl XLIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XLIX) the following year, both during their [Legion of Boom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Boom) era.[^cite_note-21]

Following back-to-back years of narrowly missing the playoffs, the Seahawks sought to retain two-time Pro Bowl quarterback [Geno Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geno_Smith) for the 2025 season, but a contract dispute led to them trading Smith to the [Las Vegas Raiders](22312.md).[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] The same day as Smith's trade, Seattle signed free agent quarterback [Sam Darnold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Darnold), who was coming off a breakout season with the [Minnesota Vikings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Vikings) after seven years in the league.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] The Seahawks made further changes to the offense by parting ways with veteran wide receivers [Tyler Lockett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyler_Lockett) and [DK Metcalf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DK_Metcalf) by release and a trade with the [Pittsburgh Steelers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh_Steelers), respectively.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29] New wide receivers included [Cooper Kupp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_Kupp), the 2021 Offensive Player of the Year and [Super Bowl LVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LVI) MVP, who was signed in free agency after being released by the [Los Angeles Rams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Rams).[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31] The day of the NFL trade deadline, the Seahawks acquired Pro Bowl wide receiver [Rashid Shaheed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashid_Shaheed) from the [New Orleans Saints](21720.md).[^cite_note-32] On defense, the Seahawks signed four-time Pro Bowl linebacker [DeMarcus Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeMarcus_Lawrence) in free agency after he missed most of the 2024 season with the [Dallas Cowboys](8121.md) due to a foot injury.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34]

Seattle's offense improved from 14th in yards and 18th in points to eighth in yards and third in points.[^cite_note-2024TotalOffense-35][^cite_note-2025TotalOffense-36] Darnold received a second Pro Bowl selection after throwing for 4,048 yards and 25 touchdowns, along with having a career-high completion percentage of 67.7%, although he also led the league in fumbles at 11 and turnovers at 20.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-SixSeahawks-38] Wide receiver [Jaxon Smith-Njigba](68739089.md) was the league's receiving yards leader at 1,793, earning him Offensive Player of the Year, along with Pro Bowl and first-team All-Pro honors.[^cite_note-NFLHonors-39][^cite_note-40][^cite_note-AllPro-41] He also led the team in receiving touchdowns at 10.[^cite_note-42] The rushing game was led by running backs [Kenneth Walker III](68758945.md), who was Seattle's leading rusher at 1,027 yards with five touchdowns, and [Zach Charbonnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Charbonnet), who led the Seahawks in rushing touchdowns at 12 with 730 yards.[^cite_note-43]

Known as the [Dark Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Seattle_Seahawks_season#The_Dark_Side), the Seahawks had the top scoring defense, previously ranking 12th, and were sixth in yards, previously ranking 14th.[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-2024TotalDefense-45][^cite_note-2025TotalDefense-46] Earning both Pro Bowl and second-team All-Pro honors was defensive lineman [Leonard Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Williams), who had 62 tackles.[^cite_note-AllPro-41] Williams and defensive tackle [Byron Murphy II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byron_Murphy_II) both led the team in sacks at 7.0 each, with Murphy adding another 62 tackles.[^cite_note-47] Cornerback [Devon Witherspoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devon_Witherspoon) also received Pro Bowl and second-team All-Pro honors after recording 72 tackles.[^cite_note-48] Linebacker [Ernest Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Jones_\(linebacker\)) was the team leader in tackles at 126 and interceptions at five, earning him a second-team All-Pro selection.[^cite_note-AllPro-41] Also named to the Pro Bowl was Lawrence, who had 53 tackles and 6.0 sacks.[^cite_note-SixSeahawks-38] On special teams, Shaheed was the only player in the league to score both a kick return touchdown and a punt return touchdown, earning him Pro Bowl honors as a kick returner.[^cite_note-SixSeahawks-38] Punter [Michael Dickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Dickson), who had the sixth-longest punt average at 49.9, received a second-team All-Pro selection.[^cite_note-AllPro-41]

### New England Patriots

Main article: [2025 New England Patriots season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_New_England_Patriots_season)



[Mike Vrabel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Vrabel) (depicted in 2008) won three Super Bowls with the Patriots as a player before becoming their head coach.

The New England Patriots finished the 2025 season with a 14–3 record under first-year head coach [Mike Vrabel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Vrabel).[^cite_note-49] This was their 12th appearance, extending their record for the most in NFL history.[^cite_note-50] They won a record six Super Bowls[^cite_note-51] during the [franchise's dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brady%E2%80%93Belichick_era) under the leadership of quarterback [Tom Brady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Brady) and head coach [Bill Belichick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Belichick), concluding with a victory in 2018's [Super Bowl LIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LIII).[^cite_note-52]

By 2025, the Patriots had regressed to one of the worst teams in the league, finishing the past two seasons with a 4–13 record at the bottom of the [AFC East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_East).[^cite_note-53] During this period, they selected quarterback [Drake Maye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drake_Maye) third overall in the [2024 NFL draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_NFL_draft), who earned Pro Bowl honors as a rookie despite the team's struggles.[^cite_note-54] Following coaching turnover, New England hired Vrabel, the 2021 Coach of the Year with the [Tennessee Titans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_Titans), who played for the Patriots when they won their first three Super Bowls.[^cite_note-55] [Josh McDaniels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_McDaniels) also rejoined the Patriots as offensive coordinator for the third time, previously holding the position from 2006 to 2008 and from 2012 to 2021.[^cite_note-VrabelStaff-56] To help improve the offense, New England signed four-time Pro Bowl wide receiver [Stefon Diggs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefon_Diggs), who was returning from an ACL injury he suffered the past year with the [Houston Texans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston_Texans).[^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58] New England also took running back [TreVeyon Henderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TreVeyon_Henderson) in the second round of the [2025 NFL draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NFL_draft).[^cite_note-59] Defensive acquisitions included linebacker [Robert Spillane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Spillane) following a career season with the [Las Vegas Raiders](22312.md) and linebacker [Harold Landry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Landry) after he was released by the Titans.[^cite_note-60][^cite_note-61][^cite_note-62]

The season saw New England's offense go from 31st in yards and 30th in points to third in yards and second in points.[^cite_note-2024TotalOffense-35][^cite_note-2025TotalOffense-36] Maye threw for 4,394 yards and 31 touchdowns, both of which led the AFC, while leading the league in completion percentage at 72% and passer rating at 113.5.[^cite_note-63] He also rushed for 450 yards and four touchdowns.[^cite_note-64] Maye received Pro Bowl and second-team All-Pro honors, in addition to placing second in [Most Valuable Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AP_NFL_Most_Valuable_Player) voting.[^cite_note-MayeGonzalez-65][^cite_note-MayeJones-66][^cite_note-67] Diggs led the team in receiving yards at 1,013 and scored four receiving touchdowns.[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69] Tight end [Hunter Henry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Henry) scored seven receiving touchdowns, the most on the Patriots, while obtaining 768 receiving yards.[^cite_note-70] Henderson was the team's leader in rushing yards and rushing touchdowns at 911 and nine, respectively.[^cite_note-71] For his role in rebuilding the offense, McDaniels won Assistant Coach of the Year.[^cite_note-NFLHonors-39]

New England's defense went from 22nd in yards and points to eighth in yards and fourth in points.[^cite_note-2024TotalDefense-45][^cite_note-2025TotalDefense-46] Named to the Pro Bowl was cornerback [Christian Gonzalez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Gonzalez) with 69 tackles.[^cite_note-MayeGonzalez-65][^cite_note-72] Spillane led the team with 97 tackles while also recording two interceptions.[^cite_note-73] Along with having 49 tackles, Landry was the team's leader in sacks at 8.5.[^cite_note-74] Safety [Jaylinn Hawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaylinn_Hawkins) led the Patriots in interceptions at four, in addition to having 71 tackles.[^cite_note-75] Cornerback [Marcus Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Jones_\(cornerback\)), who had 65 tackles, earned second-team All-Pro honors as a punt returner after scoring two punt return touchdowns, the most in the league.[^cite_note-MayeJones-66] Vrabel was named Coach of the Year a second time following the team's turnaround from the past two seasons.[^cite_note-NFLHonors-39]

### Playoffs

Main article: [2025–26 NFL playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_NFL_playoffs)

The Seahawks, as the NFC's No. 1 seed, earned a [bye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bye_\(sports\)) in the Wild Card Round.[^cite_note-76] Despite Darnold dealing with an injured oblique and losing Charbonnet to an ACL injury during the game, they won the Divisional Round in a 41–6 rout against their NFC West rival [San Francisco 49ers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_San_Francisco_49ers_season).[^cite_note-77] Seattle faced another divisional rival, the [Los Angeles Rams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Los_Angeles_Rams_season), in the NFC Championship Game. After splitting two close contests in the regular season, including an overtime matchup, Seattle emerged victorious over the Rams in another one-score game, 31–27, to advance to their first Super Bowl in 11 years.[^cite_note-78] With the victory, the Seahawks continued their streak of making the Super Bowl every year they held the NFC's top seed.[^cite_note-79]

The Patriots, the AFC's No. 2 seed, defeated the [Los Angeles Chargers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Los_Angeles_Chargers_season) 16–3 in the Wild Card Round, the team's first playoff win since 2018.[^cite_note-80] In the Divisional Round against the [Houston Texans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Houston_Texans_season), they recorded five turnovers, including four interceptions, to secure the 28–16 victory.[^cite_note-81] The AFC Championship Game pitted the Patriots against the top seeded [Denver Broncos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Denver_Broncos_season), who were starting backup quarterback [Jarrett Stidham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarrett_Stidham) after starter [Bo Nix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bo_Nix) suffered an ankle injury in the previous round.[^cite_note-82] Amid snow conditions, the Patriots won 10–7 to reach their 12th Super Bowl.[^cite_note-83][^cite_note-84] Having won all eight of their regular season road games, the AFC Championship victory made the Patriots the first team to be undefeated in nine road games.[^cite_note-85] They also became the first team to defeat three top five defenses in a single postseason.[^cite_note-86]

### Pre-game notes

The two franchises previously met in 2014's [Super Bowl XLIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XLIX), in which the Patriots defeated the Seahawks 28–24. No players remained on either team since that Super Bowl, with McDaniels being the only coach to appear in both games.[^cite_note-VrabelStaff-56] Seahawks' general manager [John Schneider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Schneider_\(American_football_executive\)) was executive vice president during Super Bowl XLIX and was promoted to president of football operations in 2024. Schneider became the first NFL general manager to reach a second Super Bowl with a completely different roster and head coach.[^cite_note-87]

This was the first Super Bowl since [Super Bowl XXXVIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXXVIII) to feature two teams that missed the playoffs the previous season. It was also the most unlikely Super Bowl matchup in at least 50 years based on preseason odds, with Seattle 60–1 to win the Super Bowl and New England 80–1.[^cite_note-88]

At 23 years and 162 days of age, Maye was the second-youngest quarterback to start in a Super Bowl after [Dan Marino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Marino), who was 23 years and 127 days old when he started in [Super Bowl XIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XIX).[^cite_note-89]

As the designated home team in the annual rotation between the two conferences, the Patriots chose to wear white away jerseys and white pants. They were the first home team to wear white since the [Tampa Bay Buccaneers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Tampa_Bay_Buccaneers_season) in [Super Bowl LV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LV).[^cite_note-90] The Seahawks donned navy blue home jerseys and navy blue pants.[^cite_note-91] This marked the first time in which both Super Bowl participants wore monochromatic uniforms.[^cite_note-92] In addition to the customary Super Bowl patch, the jerseys of both teams featured a USA 250 patch in recognition of the [United States Semiquincentennial](57473130.md).[^cite_note-93]

The Patriots held practice the week leading up to the game at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in [Palo Alto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto). The Seahawks held practice at [San Jose State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose_State_University) in [San Jose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose,_California).[^cite_note-94]

[Hall of Famers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro_Football_Hall_of_Fame) and Super Bowl-winning players [Joe Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Montana), [Peyton Manning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peyton_Manning), and [Lynn Swann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynn_Swann) were honorary captains for the coin toss.[^cite_note-95] The coin used was a modern restrike of a silver medal called the [Libertas Americana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libertas_Americana), commissioned by [Benjamin Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Franklin), also a tie-in to the United States Semiquincentennial.[^cite_note-96]

## Game summary

### First half



Seahawks kicker [Jason Myers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Myers) set the Super Bowl record for field goals scored.

The Patriots won the coin toss and deferred to the second half, meaning Seattle received the ball first. The Seahawks drove down the field inside the Patriots' 20-yard line, including a 23-yard pass to Cooper Kupp, but they failed to score a touchdown in the red zone,[^cite_note-ESPN-97] kicking a 33-yard field goal. The Patriots then began their first drive on the 28-yard line after running back [D'Ernest Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D'Ernest_Johnson) returned [Jason Myers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Myers)'s kickoff for 28 yards. While New England advanced to Seattle's 49-yard line, they were ultimately forced to punt shortly after quarterback [Drake Maye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drake_Maye) was sacked by [Derick Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derick_Hall) for a loss of 10 yards and could not convert on third and 11.[^cite_note-ESPN-97] After two straight drives by each team ended with punts, Jason Myers kicked a 39-yard field goal after Seattle drove down the field, powered by 30- and 29-yard runs by [Kenneth Walker III](68758945.md).[^cite_note-ESPN-97] After three more drives ended with punts, Myers added another field goal from 41 yards with 11 seconds left in the first half.[^cite_note-ESPN-97]

### Second half

In the third quarter, after the Patriots opened with a three-and-out, the Seahawks put together a 10-play, 69-yard drive that included a 20-yard pass to Walker and 16-yard passes to [Jaxon Smith-Njigba](68739089.md) and [Rashid Shaheed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashid_Shaheed); it ended with Myers kicking his fourth field goal from 41 yards to give the Seahawks a 12–0 lead.[^cite_note-ESPN-97] After four straight drives ended in punts, Maye was strip-sacked by Hall and the ball was recovered by [Byron Murphy II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byron_Murphy_II) at the New England's 37-yard line.[^cite_note-ESPN-97][^cite_note-98]

Following the turnover, the Seahawks converted on a third and 9 with a Darnold pass to Kupp as the fourth quarter began. Darnold threw the first touchdown pass of the game, a 16-yarder to Barner, extending Seattle's lead to 19–0.[^cite_note-ESPN-97][^cite_note-99] The Patriots' next drive was a three-play, 65-yard drive that included a 24-yard pass and a 35-yard touchdown pass to [Mack Hollins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mack_Hollins), cutting the deficit to 19–7.[^cite_note-ESPN-97] The Seahawks' next drive started with an 11-yard pass from Darnold to Shaheed, but ended with a punt by [Michael Dickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Dickson) that was downed at New England's 4-yard line by [Velus Jones Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velus_Jones_Jr.)[^cite_note-ESPN-97] Maye began with a 13-yard pass to [Rhamondre Stevenson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhamondre_Stevenson) and a 16-yard run, but after throwing a 7-yard pass to rookie wide receiver [Kyle Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyle_Williams_\(wide_receiver,_born_2002\)), he was intercepted by Seattle safety [Julian Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_Love), who returned the ball 35 yards to New England's 38-yard line.[^cite_note-ESPN-97][^cite_note-100] After Walker rushed for 24 yards on the next two plays, the Seahawks' drive stalled, and Myers kicked a record fifth field goal from 26 yards to make the score 22–7.[^cite_note-ESPN-97] On the Patriots' next possession, with [Devon Witherspoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devon_Witherspoon) pressuring Maye on a [cornerback blitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blitz_\(gridiron_football\)), Maye attempted a throw which was picked off by linebacker [Uchenna Nwosu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uchenna_Nwosu), who returned the ball 45 yards to the endzone to increase the lead to 29–7.[^cite_note-101] That effectively clinched Seattle's second Lombardi Trophy with only 4\:27 left to play.[^cite_note-ESPN-97][^cite_note-102] Maye responded with a pass to running back [Rhamondre Stevenson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhamondre_Stevenson) for a touchdown; however, the two-point conversion pass to tight end [Hunter Henry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Henry) fell incomplete just before the [two-minute warning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-minute_warning).[^cite_note-ESPN-97][^cite_note-103] An [onside kick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onside_kick) by New England was recovered by the Seahawks' [George Holani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Holani). A holding penalty on Seahawks center [Jalen Sundell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalen_Sundell) nullified a Walker 49-yard touchdown run, forcing them to punt just after the two-minute warning.[^cite_note-ESPN-97][^cite_note-104] The Patriots were unable to score as the game ended.[^cite_note-ESPN-97]

### Post-game

[Kenneth Walker III](68758945.md) was named [Super Bowl MVP](291253.md), based on his 135 rushing yards on 27 carries.[^cite_note-105] The Seattle defense was widely praised for their performance in a largely defensive game.[^cite_note-106] At age 38, [Mike Macdonald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Macdonald) became the third-youngest head coach to win a Super Bowl, behind [Sean McVay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_McVay) and [Mike Tomlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Tomlin).[^cite_note-107] Darnold (who had led the league in regular season turnovers with 20) and the Seahawks (second in team regular season turnovers) became the first Super Bowl champions to commit zero turnovers in their postseason run.[^cite_note-108]

## Broadcasting

### United States

#### Television

Super Bowl LX was televised by [NBCUniversal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBCUniversal) properties by [NBC Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC_Sports) and [Telemundo Deportes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telemundo_Deportes), with the main English-language broadcast on [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_on_NBC), Spanish broadcasts on [Telemundo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telemundo) and [Universo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universo_\(TV_channel\)), and streaming on [Peacock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_\(streaming_service\)), [NFL+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL+), and NBC Sports digital platforms.[^cite_note-129] It was the third Super Bowl to be broadcast as part of the current 11-year [NFL television contract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_on_American_television), which allows a four-year rotation between [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_on_CBS), [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_on_Fox), NBC, and [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_on_ABC)/[ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN_Monday_Night_Football).[^cite_note-sportspromedia-130][^cite_note-131]

The game was called by NBC's lead broadcast team of play-by-play announcer [Mike Tirico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Tirico) and color commentator [Cris Collinsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cris_Collinsworth); the game marked Tirico's first Super Bowl as a commentator, and Collinsworth's sixth as an analyst. They were joined by [Melissa Stark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_Stark) and [Kaylee Hartung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaylee_Hartung) as sideline reporters, and [Terry McAulay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_McAulay) as [rules analyst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rules_analyst).[^cite_note-132][^cite_note-Lucia-2022-133] *[Football Night in America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Night_in_America)* host [Maria Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Taylor_\(sportscaster\)) presided over the [Vince Lombardi Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vince_Lombardi_Trophy) presentation,[^cite_note-134] becoming the first female sportscaster to do so since [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_on_CBS)' [Lesley Visser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesley_Visser) did it for [Super Bowl XXVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXVI) in 1992. The Spanish-language broadcast was called by Miguel Gurwitz and [Rolando Cantú](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolando_Cant%C3%BA).[^cite_note-Keeley-2026-135] As its [lead-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Super_Bowl_lead-out_programs) after the game, NBC aired a block of *[Primetime in Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympics_on_NBC#2026_Winter)* for its [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md) coverage.[^cite_note-Sports_Video_Group-2026-136] NBC cross-promoted the Super Bowl, Olympics, and the [2026 NBA All-Star Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NBA_All-Star_Game) the following week (the first to air on the network as part of NBC's [broadcast rights to the NBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_on_NBC)) under the banner "Legendary February".[^cite_note-\:0-137]

NBC's pre-game show was broadcast from a studio within Levi's Stadium and incorporated live segments from Bay Area locations such as [Alcatraz Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcatraz_Island). During the playoffs, NBC tested new technologies that were utilized during the game, such as new [Canon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canon_Inc.) Cine-Servo camera lenses, and various on-air graphic updates (including new [augmented reality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmented_reality) weather visualizations and [telestrators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telestrator), and a refreshed [score bug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Score_bug)).[^cite_note-138][^cite_note-139][^cite_note-Sports_Video_Group-2026-136]

Super Bowl LX was watched by an average of 125.6 million viewers across NBCU's platforms and NFL+, and achieved a combined average household rating of 39.4 according to Nielsen.[^cite_note-variety-revised-140][^cite_note-141] This made Super Bowl LX [the second most-watched program in U.S. television history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_watched_television_broadcasts_in_the_United_States); while it fell short of [Super Bowl LIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LIX)'s record viewership the previous year by 2.8 million, it did set a new record for highest peak viewership with 137.8 million during the second quarter.[^cite_note-RickPorter-7] The game also set a new record for Spanish-language broadcasts of the Super Bowl, with 3.3 million viewers tuning into Telemundo.[^cite_note-Ratings_–_Athletic-142] The game was the first whose U.S. viewership was measured using [Nielsen Media Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen_Media_Research)'s "Big Data + Panel" methodology, which is more inclusive of data from streaming video services. Since its official implementation in September 2025, a number of sports telecasts had seen noticeable year-over-year increases in viewership credited to the new system.[^cite_note-143][^cite_note-RickPorter-7][^cite_note-Ratings_–_Athletic-142]

#### Advertising

NBC charged a base rate of \$10 million for a 30-second [commercial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_commercials) during the game, two million more than the price Fox had initially offered the previous year;[^cite_note-Yomtov-4][^cite_note-sportspromedia-130] to maximize advertising revenue, NBC offered packages covering the Super Bowl, the Winter Olympics, and the NBA All-Star Game.[^cite_note-144][^cite_note-\:0-137]

The [Super Bowl Ad Meter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_Ad_Meter) survey conducted by *[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today)* was won by [Budweiser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budweiser) for its commercial "American Icons", marking its second consecutive win after "First Delivery" at Super Bowl LIX and tenth overall.[^cite_note-145] [Artificial intelligence](1164.md) was a major theme for commercials during the Super Bowl,[^cite_note-146][^cite_note-147] with [OpenAI](48795986.md) being joined by newcomer [Anthropic](6206236.md), whose ads poked fun at OpenAI incorporating advertisements into [ChatGPT](72417803.md).[^cite_note-148] [Svedka Vodka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svedka) aired an entirely AI-generated advertisement, the first such instance at the Super Bowl.[^cite_note-149] [Pepsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pepsi)'s advertisement "The Choice" focused on a polar bear, a mascot for their main rivals [Coca-Cola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coca-Cola), choosing Pepsi over Coke;[^cite_note-150] the commercial was directed by [Taika Waititi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taika_Waititi) and was seen as a shot at Coca-Cola's AI-generated Christmas advertisements which run in 2024 and 2025.[^cite_note-151]

Celebrities featured in advertisements included [Sabrina Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabrina_Carpenter) for [Pringles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pringles),[^cite_note-152] [Guy Fieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Fieri) for [Bosch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosch_\(company\))[^cite_note-153] and [MrBeast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MrBeast) for [Salesforce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salesforce) (doubling as the launch of his "Million Dollar Puzzle" challenge).[^cite_note-154] [T-Mobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-Mobile_US) ran an ad featuring the [Backstreet Boys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backstreet_Boys) performing inside one of their stores,[^cite_note-155] while [Coinbase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coinbase) aired a commercial stylized as a karaoke version of the band's song "[Everybody (Backstreet's Back)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everybody_\(Backstreet's_Back\))".[^cite_note-156] The [Cadillac Formula 1 Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One) also aired an advertisement unveiling their car's livery for the [2026 F1 season](71681684.md).[^cite_note-157]

[Ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_\(company\))'s "Search Party" advertisement generated significant controversy. Launched as a pilot program in September 2025, the feature's main use is to locate lost pets, but the advertisement during the Super Bowl and its subsequent national rollout raised significant privacy and [mass surveillance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_surveillance) concerns.[^cite_note-158] The backlash caused Ring to back out of Search Party's planned integration with [Flock Safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flock_Safety).[^cite_note-159]

## Aftermath

The Seahawks hosted a [victory parade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victory_parade) through [Downtown Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Seattle) on February 11 that began with a rally at [Lumen Field](206812.md) and ended near the [Seattle Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Center). The players and staff rode on several buses and [U.S. National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Guard) vehicles; a crowd estimated in the "hundreds of thousands" watched the parade.[^cite_note-167] That day, over 200,000 passengers used the [Link light rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Link_light_rail) system, which had increased service to account for parade attendance.[^cite_note-168]

The Seahawks were put up for sale by the estate of [Paul Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Allen) by his sister, [Jody Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jody_Allen), as directed in his will. This marked the first time a Super Bowl champion was put on the market in the same year.[^cite_note-169] On August 26, they were sold to [Neeru Khosla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neeru_Khosla) for a price of \$9.612 billion.[^cite_note-170]

The [2026 NFL season](77792316.md) began with a Super Bowl LX rematch, with the [Seahawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Seattle_Seahawks_season) hosting the [Patriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_New_England_Patriots_season) at [Lumen Field](206812.md). It was be the first meeting of both reigning Super Bowl participants in the [Kickoff Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_Kickoff_Game) since [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_NFL_season). The Seahawks defeated the Patriots, 13–10.[^cite_note-171]
