The **cabinet of Đuro Macut** was formed on 16 April 2025, following the latter's election as [Prime Minister of Serbia](606320.md) by the [National Assembly](619753.md) on the same day. It succeeded the [cabinet of Miloš Vučević](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Milo%C5%A1_Vu%C4%8Devi%C4%87).

The [Serbian Progressive Party](19454384.md) (SNS) came to power in [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Serbian_parliamentary_election). After the [2023 Serbian parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Serbian_parliamentary_election), [Aleksandar Vučić](13349267.md), the president of Serbia, gave [Miloš Vučević](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milo%C5%A1_Vu%C4%8Devi%C4%87) the mandate to form a government. He was elected in May 2024, but resigned amidst [anti-corruption protests](78597389.md) in January 2025. After the acknowledgment of his resignation by the National Assembly in March, negotiations were held to form a new government. Vučić gave Macut the mandate to form a government in early April.

The cabinet is composed of members of SNS, [Socialist Party of Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Party_of_Serbia), [Party of United Pensioners of Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_of_United_Pensioners_of_Serbia), [Social Democratic Party of Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_of_Serbia), [Serbian Party Oathkeepers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Party_Oathkeepers), [Serbian People's Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_People's_Party_\(2014\)), and [Justice and Reconciliation Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_and_Reconciliation_Party).

## Background

The [Serbian Progressive Party](19454384.md) (SNS) came to power after the [2012 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Serbian_parliamentary_election), along with the [Socialist Party of Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Party_of_Serbia) (SPS).[^cite_note-1] In the [2023 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Serbian_parliamentary_election), SNS won a majority of seats in the [National Assembly of Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Serbia).[^cite_note-\:50-2] After the election, [Aleksandar Vučić](13349267.md), the president of Serbia, held negotiations with parliamentary parties from 26 February to 4 March.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Vučić gave [Miloš Vučević](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milo%C5%A1_Vu%C4%8Devi%C4%87), the president of SNS, the mandate to form a government on 30 March.[^cite_note-5] Vučević was elected prime minister in May 2024.[^cite_note-6] After a group of SNS members physically attacked students in Novi Sad on 28 January 2025, Vučević announced his resignation.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] Vučević's resignation was acknowledged by the National Assembly on 19 March.[^cite_note-9] After the acknowledgment, negotiations were held for the formation of the new government.[^cite_note-10] Vučić nominated [Đuro Macut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%90uro_Macut) as the mandate holder for the formation of the new government on 6 April.[^cite_note-\:2-11]

## Investiture

A National Assembly session, called by [Ana Brnabić](54310335.md), started on 15 April 2025, during which the new government was elected.[^cite_note-\:0-12] The [Alliance of Vojvodina Hungarians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_of_Vojvodina_Hungarians) and [United Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Serbia) announced that they would vote in favour in the investiture vote.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] The government was voted and sworn in on 16 April.[^cite_note-\:1-15]

## Composition
