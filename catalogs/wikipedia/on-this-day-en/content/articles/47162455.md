**Habboûch** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): حبّوش) is a municipality in southern [Lebanon](17771.md). The population is estimated to be approximately 15,000. It is located at latitude 33.4072900 and longitude 35.4816900.

## History

In 1875 [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) found the village to be inhabited by 200 [Metualis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metuali).[^cite_note-1]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.31% of registered voters in Habboûch. 96.47% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-2]
