The **USA Basketball Women's National Team**,[^cite_note-2] commonly known as **Team USA**, is governed by [USA Basketball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Basketball) and competes in [FIBA Americas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Americas). The team is by far the most successful in international women's basketball, having won 10 out of the 12 Olympic tournaments it has entered. It has also won 10 of the last 13 World Cups (including the last 5), and 12 titles overall. The team is currently ranked first in the [FIBA World Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Women's_World_Ranking).

In 2016, it was named the USA Basketball Team of the Year for a record sixth time (having been previously honored in 1996, 2000, 2004, 2008, and 2012). It was also named the [USOC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Olympic_&_Paralympic_Committee) [Team of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USOC_Athlete_of_the_Year#Team_of_the_Year) in 1996.[^cite_note-3]

The team is one of the most dominant in all Olympic sports, with a 78–3 record in Olympic play, and a record eight consecutive titles. They have no Olympic losses since 1992, no losses in any major tournament since 2006, and their [gold medal in 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_tournament) broke the [U.S. men's basketball team's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_men's_national_basketball_team) record (1936–1968) for the most consecutive Olympic team victories in all Olympic sports.[^cite_note-4]
