**Katsina State** [Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/c/c2/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-katsina.wav/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-katsina.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> ([Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_language): *Jihar Katsina*; [Hausa Ajami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_Ajami): جِىهَرْ کَڟِࢽَ) is a state in the [northwestern geopolitical zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_West_\(Nigeria\)) of [Nigeria](21383.md). It is bordered to the west by [Zamfara State](2227664.md), to the east by [Kano](2123717.md) and [Jigawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jigawa_State) states, and to the south by [Kaduna State](2227637.md), while its northern border forms part of the national [border with](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger%E2%80%93Nigeria_border) [Niger](21373.md). The state capital is the city of [Katsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_\(city\)), and the state is divided into 34 local government areas. The state is nicknamed the ["Home of Hospitality".](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Hospitality)[^cite_note-4]

With an estimated population of 9.3 million residents as of 2023, Katsina State is the [third most populous state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_population) in the country, despite the fact that it only ranks [17th out of 36](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_area) states in terms of area. Geographically, Katsina is primarily located within the [West Sudanian savanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Sudanian_savanna), although parts of the north of the state transition into the [semi-desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-desert) [Sahelian savanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahelian_savanna). Major rivers in the state include the Bunsuru, Gada, and [Sokoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_River) rivers, which provide water for agriculture and settlements.

Demographically, the [Hausa people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people) are the largest ethnic group in the state with minorities of [Fulani](102383.md) and other groups. In terms of religion, [Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Nigeria) (79%) is the most practised faith with minorities of adherents of [Christianity](12241851.md) (20%) and traditionalist religions (\~1%), particularly [Maguzawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguzawa_Hausa_people) groups.[^cite_note-5]

In the pre-colonial period, much of Katsina State was part of the [Hausa Bakwai states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_Kingdoms), with the cities of Daura and Katsina emerging as a major centers of trade and Islamic learning during the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries. In the early nineteenth century, the [Fulani jihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulani_jihad) led to the establishment of [Daura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daura_Emirate) and [Katsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsina_Emirate) as emirates within the [Sokoto Caliphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_Caliphate). During the British colonial era, Katsina was incorporated into the [Northern Nigeria Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nigeria_Protectorate) which later merged into [British Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Nigeria). Upon Nigeria's independence in 1960, Katsina was part of the [Northern Region](2292852.md) until the region was divided in 1967, and it became part of the North-Central State (later renamed [Kaduna State](2227637.md)). In 1987, Katsina State was created from the northern portion of Kaduna State. Since the mid-2010s, Katsina has been one of the Nigerian states hit hardest by local banditry and terrorism.[^cite_note-6] In 2020, [over 300 children were kidnapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankara_kidnapping) by the terrorist group [Boko Haram](23769768.md) in the town of [Kankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankara).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

Economically, Katsina State is largely reliant on agriculture, with key crops including millet, sorghum, maize, rice, groundnuts, and cotton. Livestock farming and herding is also significant, with cattle, goats, and sheep widely reared. Katsina has the [fourth lowest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_Human_Development_Index) [Human Development Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Development_Index) and ranks in the [mid-range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_GDP) of [GDPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP) in the country.[^cite_note-9]

## Demography

Hausa are the largest ethnic group in the state.[^cite_note-ahram-10]

### Religion

The state is predominantly Muslim and the [Gobarau Minaret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gobarau_Minaret) is an important building therein. [Sharia](28840.md) is valid in the entire state but mainly for the Muslims.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] The [Church of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Nigeria) has a Diocese of Katsina.[^cite_note-13] The [Redeemed Christian Church of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redeemed_Christian_Church_of_God), the [Roman Catholic Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church) and many other churches are present in the state.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

## Geography

Katsina State is about 23,938 square kilometres (9,243 sq mi). It is situated between latitude 11°07'49" and 13°22' 57" N and longitude 6°52'03" E and 9°9'02" E.[^cite_note-16] The state is located in the [tropical grassland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_grassland) known as [savannah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savannah) and has two major seasons which are the rainy season and the dry season.[^cite_note-17]

### Climate

Katsina has a tropical [steppe climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steppe_climate), bordering on a [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate). The city's yearly temperature is 30.89 °C (87.6 °F) and it is 1.43% higher than Nigeria's averages. Katsina typically receives about 791.61 millimetres (31.1 inches) of precipitation and has 54.63 rainy days (14.97% of the time) annually.[^cite_note-18]

## Education



Umaru Musa Yar'adua University

Katsina State is a centre of both formal and informal education. [Umaru Musa Yar'adua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umaru_Musa_Yar'adua_University)[^cite_note-21] is a public university owned by the state government. [Al-Qalam University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qalam_University), the first Islamic university in Nigeria is community owned. [Federal University, Dutsin-Ma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University,_Dutsin-Ma)[^cite_note-22] and [Federal University of Transportation, Daura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Transportation,_Daura) are owned by the federal government as well as [Federal College of Education, Katsina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_College_of_Education,_Katsina)[^cite_note-23] (affiliated to [Bayero University Kano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayero_University_Kano)[^cite_note-24]). [National Open University of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Open_University_of_Nigeria), [Isa Kaita College of Education Dutsinma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isa_Kaita_College_of_Education) (affiliated to [Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadu_Bello_University)), a state-owned college of education. Cherish Institute Batsari is a privately owned university awarding degrees in health courses.[^cite_note-25]
