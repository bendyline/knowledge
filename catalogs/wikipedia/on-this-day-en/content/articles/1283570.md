**South Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة الجنوب, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *muḥāfaẓat al-Janūb*, or simply الجنوب) is one of the [governorates of Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorates_of_Lebanon), with a population of 590,000 inhabitants and an area of 929.6 km<sup>2</sup>. The capital is [Sidon](28366.md). The lowest elevation is sea-level; the highest is 1,000 meters. The local population is religiously diverse and includes [Shia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islam_in_Lebanon) and [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam_in_Lebanon) Muslims, [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze), [Eastern Orthodox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Christianity_in_Lebanon), [Maronite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maronites_in_Lebanon), [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism_in_Lebanon), and Greek Catholic Christians. Temperatures can drop to 4 °C during winter with much rain and snow on the higher ground. In the humid summer, temperatures can rise to 30 °C in the coastal areas. The governorate has several rivers: the [Litani](1920925.md), Deir El Zahrani, [Naqoura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naqoura), [Awali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awali_\(river\)), Qasmiye, and [Hasbani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasbani_River). The area is famous for its citrus and banana farms. Its main cities are [Sidon](28366.md), [Tyre](30796.md) and [Jezzine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jezzine).

&#x20;Shias make up the majority of the governorate and are the main residents of the Zahrani Tyre districts. Sunnis are the main residents of Sidon the capital of the governorate and the third largest city in Lebanon after Beirut and Tripoli. Christians are mainly residents of the Jezzine district and form a small community in the cities of Tyre, which is the fourth-most-populous city in Lebanon.

## Local attractions and events

The area offers a great number of attractions, including pristine white sandy beaches south of [Tyre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyre_\(Lebanon\)), and the opportunity to snorkel or dive among submerged Phoenician and Roman ruins near the ancient cities of [Sidon](28366.md) and [Tyre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyre_\(Lebanon\)). There are a number of [Ottoman-era](22278.md) [pazars](556904.md) and seafood restaurants overlooking the [Mediterranean Sea](19006.md). [Southern Lebanon](4205968.md) also hosts the [Tyre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyre_\(Lebanon\)) festival, which attracts thousands of tourists each year.
