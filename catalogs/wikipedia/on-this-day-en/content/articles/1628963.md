***Zaporizhzhia* (U-01)** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Запоріжжя) is a [Project 641 (Foxtrot-class)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxtrot-class_submarine) diesel-electric powered submarine, and was the only submarine of the [Ukrainian Navy](1590554.md)[^cite_note-KorZap2032012-2] up until her seizure in March 2014 by Russian forces during [2014 Russian annexation of Crimea](42228673.md). She formerly carried the [Soviet Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Navy) [pennant number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennant_number) B-435. *Zaporizhzhia* was designed at the [Rubin Design Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubin_Design_Bureau) ([Saint Petersburg](24320051.md)).

Captured by [Russian](25391.md) forces on 22 March 2014 (during the [annexation of Crimea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Crimea_by_the_Russian_Federation)), half of the Ukrainian personnel, among them the captain of the submarine, had left while the others chose to begin their service in the Russian [Black Sea Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea_Fleet) with the submarine.

*Zaporizhzhia* had a crew of 78, commanded by [1st Rank Capt.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_\(naval\)) Oleh Orlov. The submarine was operated by a reserve unit, having not yet returned to front line duty after her 'refurbishment'.&#x20;

## History

*Zaporizhzhia*'s [keel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel) was [laid down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel_laying) in 1970 at the New Admiralty Shipyard in [Leningrad](24320051.md).[^cite_note-KorZap2032012-2] In 1970 she was [commissioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning) into the [Soviet Red Banner Northern Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Red_Banner_Northern_Fleet),[^cite_note-KorZap2032012-2] where she conducted 14 patrols, including a port call in [Cuba](5042481.md). In 1990 the submarine was transferred to the Soviet [Black Sea Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea_Fleet),[^cite_note-KorZap2032012-2] serving there until 1994.[^cite_note-KorZap2032012-2] In 1995, the submarine's batteries permanently failed and she was abandoned.

In 1997 unable to fix the unused submarine, the administration of [Russian Navy](383258.md) handed it over to the [Ukrainian Navy](1590554.md) during the [partition of the Black Sea Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_Treaty_on_the_Status_and_Conditions_of_the_Black_Sea_Fleet).[^cite_note-KorZap2032012-2] Like most of the country's naval ships, she was renamed after one of Ukraine's cities – [Zaporizhzhia](1873579.md).[^cite_note-KorZap2032012-2] The city's community began to co-sponsor the ship, especially the accommodation needs of the crew. But it was only in 2003 when Ukrainian government was able to buy a new set of batteries and make *Zaporizhzhia*'s survival real. However, she was immediately placed under repair in [Sevastopol](51587.md).[^cite_note-KorZap2032012-2]

In 2005, *Zaporizhzhia* was considered for inclusion in the 2006 joint [exercise](1225714.md) conducted by the Ukrainian Navy and the [Italian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Militare), but the end of her repair was postponed. Being on a years-long repair, *Zaporizhzhia* was an inactive military unit stated Defense Minister [Anatoly Hrytsenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoly_Hrytsenko) in April 2006.[^cite_note-3] In January 2007, (Defense Minister) Hrytsenko stated that Ukraine intended to sell *Zaporizhzhia*.[^cite_note-4]

As of July 2010 *Zaporizhzhia* was preparing for [trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_trial) after repairs.[^cite_note-Alrosasub-5] All repairs were finished on 17 April 2011.[^cite_note-KorZap2032012-2] The first trial sail was finished successfully on 18 July 2012.[^cite_note-6] Late June 2013 all long-term maintenance work and tests were completed.[^cite_note-7]

On 22 March 2014, it was reported that the submarine had been taken over by Russian forces after being surrounded and harassed by Russian Navy ships, who demanded its surrender.[^cite_note-8] Commander Robert Shagieiev (also spelled Shageev) ignored orders to relocate his unit to [Odesa](55545.md) by 12 May 2014, instead he declared his intention to serve in the Russian military.[^cite_note-8048901subZap-9] Shagieiev was relieved of his duties on 17 May 2014.[^cite_note-8048901subZap-9] Shagieiev then signing a contract with the Russian armed forces and assumed the position of deputy commander of the 4th Separate Submarine Brigade of Russia's Black Sea Fleet.[^cite_note-8048901subZap-9] Ukraine charged Shagieiev with desertion and treason.[^cite_note-8048901subZap-9] The *Zaporizhzhia* was placed under Black Sea Fleet control, after Ukrainian symbols were removed from the submarine.[^cite_note-10] Half of the submarine's crew continued to serve in the Russian Navy. On 29 March 2014, the Black Sea Fleet was reported to have refused to add the submarine to its ranks on the grounds that it was obsolete and suffered from a number of technical issues. The Russian Navy then offered to return *Zaporizhzhia* to Ukraine to either continue its service or to be recycled as scrap-metal.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] It was reported the submarine would be transferred from the Streletskaya buhta to Yuzhnaya buhta, where the Russian submarine division is.[^cite_note-13] Later sources in the Russian Navy announced that submarine should be returned to Ukrainian Navy.[^cite_note-14] Later (it was reported) Russia decided not to return the submarine to Ukraine.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] But in mid-May the submarine was scheduled to be returned to Ukraine, where it was likely to become a [museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_ship) piece.[^cite_note-17] A part of the Ukrainian Navy was then returned to Ukraine but Russia suspended this agreement before returning submarine *Zaporizhzhia* because/after [Ukraine did not renew its unilaterally declared ceasefire on 1 July 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_pro-Russian_unrest_in_Ukraine#Fifteen-point_peace_plan) in the [War in Donbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Donbas).[^cite_note-18] Hence *Zaporizhzhia* was not returned to Ukraine.[^cite_note-subZap17615-1] The [Russian submarine B-871](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_submarine_B-871) "Alrosa" provided crew for the Zaporizhzhia and training for the newly Russian sailors.[^cite_note-Merket-19]

As of 29 December 2020, Google Maps imagery showed U-01 afloat and pier-side in Yuzhnaya Bay in Sevastopol surrounded by containment booms, next to [Romeo-class submarine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romeo-class_submarine) S-49.[^cite_note-20]

However, as of 2024, the submarine is no longer present at Sevastopol, and is presumably scrapped. [^cite_note-21]

On 13 August 2026 Robert Shagieiev was believed to have been killed by bomb place in a trash can, which exploded as he walked by.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-8048901subZap-9][^cite_note-GarsubZap14826-23]

## Ship log

From September to November 1970, submarine was at sea for 20 days, 422 hours (236 submerged, 186 surfaced). The vessel traveled 2,672 km (1,660 mi) while surfaced and 695 km (432 mi) while submerged. The submarine completed 15 submerges, and used 125 tons of fuel.

During the service in the Atlantic Ocean in June through December 1971, the submarine was at sea for 210 days. It traveled 29,519 km (18,342 mi) while surfaced and 8,594 km (5,340 mi) while submerged. The submarine completed 71 submerges, and used 825 tons of fuel. During 27 June to 10 August 1972 *Zaporizhzhia* underwent medium repairs.

From July to October 1994, the submarine was at sea for 16 days. The submarine travelled 391.9 km (243.5 mi) while surfaced and 6.8 km (4.2 mi) while submerged. In January 1995 *Zaporizhzhia* traveled only 85 km (53 mi) in 4 hours while being towed.

## Armament

Main torpedoes used are: 53-51, 53-39, 53-61, 53-65, САЕТ-60.

Torpedo tubes are 533 mm (1.75 ft) diameter, and 8.145 m (26.7 ft) long. Tubes are placed 700 mm (2.3 ft) away from each other.

Also carries 12 extra torpedoes – 22 total.

Can also carry 32 MTD type mines or 26 PM-2 or RM-2 type mines.

Can be equipped with 4 [MANPADS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MANPADS).
