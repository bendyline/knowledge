**Lower Shabelle** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Shabeellaha Hoose*,[^cite_note-5] [Maay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maay_language): *Shibelithy Hoosy*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شبيلي السفلى, [Italian](14708.md): *Basso Scebeli*) is an administrative region (*[gobol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Somalia)*) in southern [Somalia](27358.md).[^cite_note-Cwfsom-6]

## Geography

Lower Shabelle is bordered by the regions of [Banaadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banaadir), [Middle Shabelle](185138.md) (Shabeellaha Dhexe), [Hiran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiran,_Somalia), [Bay](185141.md), and [Middle Jubba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Jubba) (Jubbada Dhexe) and by the Somali Sea. It is named after the [Shebelle River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shebelle_River), which passes through it.

Until 1984, when the regions were reassigned, it was part of the larger [Benadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benadir) region, and its capital was [Mogadishu](60154.md). [Merca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merca) is now the Lower Shabelle capital.

## Violence in Golweyn

On 30 July 2017, an [AMISOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMISOM) convoy was [ambushed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Golweyn_ambush) by [al-Shabaab](8726463.md) insurgents, killing and wounding several [Ugandan soldiers](56928.md).[^cite_note-7]

Eight children were killed and dozens were wounded when a bomb exploded in Golweyn on 31 January 2021.[^cite_note-8]
