The **Iran–Israel conflict** is a long-standing geopolitical and military confrontation between the [Islamic Republic of Iran](14653.md) and the [State of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Israel), involving proxy hostilities since 1985 and direct clashes since 2024.[^cite_note-auto1-1][^cite_note-auto-2]

## Background

Main article: [Iranian Revolution](347268.md)

Iran and Israel became adversaries following the [Iranian Revolution](347268.md) in 1979, when Iran severed ties on 18 February 1979 and adopted an openly hostile stance toward Israel. Over the following decades, the rivalry expanded through proxy forces and regional influence campaigns, including Iran's support for [Hezbollah](13919.md) and [Hamas](13913.md), as well as its nuclear program and ballistic missile development.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

### Proxy conflict (1985–present)

Main article: [Iran–Israel proxy conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Israel_proxy_conflict)

The conflict involved indirect clashes between Israel and Iran-backed forces in [Syria](7515849.md), [Lebanon](17771.md) and [Gaza Strip](12047.md), and Israeli strikes targeting Iranian positions.[^cite_note-auto1-1][^cite_note-auto-2]

## Direct conflict (2024–present)

### 2024 conflict

Main article: [2024 Iran–Israel conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Iran%E2%80%93Israel_conflict)

Direct confrontations included missile and drone exchanges, Israeli bombings of Iranian targets [in Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_intervention_in_Syria_\(2024%E2%80%93present\)) and Iranian retaliatory attacks on Israeli territory.[^cite_note-auto2-5]

In April, both [Israeli strikes against Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2024_Israeli_strikes_on_Iran) and [Iranian strikes against Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2024_Iranian_strikes_on_Israel) started, respectively.[^cite_note-intercept0-6][^cite_note-alj13april-7] In July, assassinations of both [Fuad Shukr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Fuad_Shukr) and [Ismail Haniyeh](77478235.md) were conducted by Israel.[^cite_note-generalskilled18june-8][^cite_note-9] In October, [Iranian strikes on Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Iranian_strikes_on_Israel) started, succeeded with [Israeli strikes on Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_2024_Israeli_strikes_on_Iran).[^cite_note-bbc0-10]

### 2025 Twelve-Day War

Main article: [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War)

In June 2025, a brief war known as the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War) involved [Israeli strikes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_attacks_during_the_Twelve-Day_War) on Iranian military and nuclear facilities and a [ceasefire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War_ceasefire) brokered afterwards.[^cite_note-auto2-5]

### 2026 Iran war

Main article: [2026 Iran war](82533582.md)

In late February 2026, after the breakdown of [US–Iran negotiations](79685207.md), Israel and the [United States](3434750.md) conducted [coordinated military operations](82546969.md) against Iranian targets. Iran retaliated with [missile and drone strikes on Israeli territory](82636370.md), causing civilian casualties and infrastructure damage.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]
