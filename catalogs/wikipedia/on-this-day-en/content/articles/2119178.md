**Zacapu** (*Place of Stones*), is a city and the municipal seat of [the homonymous municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zacapu_municipality?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-1] of [Michoacán, Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michoac%C3%A1n,_Mexico). It is located at [19°49′N 101°48′W  /  19.817°N 101.800°W  / 19.817; -101.800](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Zacapu\&params=19_49_N_101_48_W_).

## Demographics

The 2010 Mexican census indicated that the city had a population of 52,806 and the municipality a population of 73,455.[^cite_note-2]

## Notable people

- [José Ángel Aguilar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_%C3%81ngel_Aguilar?action=edit\&redlink=1), writer

- [Rudy Gutierrez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudy_Gutierrez?action=edit\&redlink=1), noted oncologist

- [Juan B. Guido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_B._Guido?action=edit\&redlink=1), poet

- [Fray Jacobo Daciano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_the_Dacian), Danish [Franciscan priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franciscan_order), member of the Tzintzuntzan convent

- [Primo Tapia de la Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primo_Tapia_de_la_Cruz?action=edit\&redlink=1), labor leader who organized farming communities against landholders. He was assassinated in 1926.

- [Abel Alcázar Pallares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abel_Alc%C3%A1zar_Pallares?action=edit\&redlink=1), philanthropist and community activist

- [Armando Martínez (cyclist)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armando_Mart%C3%ADnez_\(cyclist\)), cyclist

- [Rodolfo Vitela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodolfo_Vitela), cyclist

- [Jorge del Pozzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_del_Pozzo?action=edit\&redlink=1), businessman

- [César Martínez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9sar_Mart%C3%ADnez_\(Mexican_boxer\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), boxer

- [Adolfo García Trujillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolfo_Garc%C3%ADa_Trujillo?action=edit\&redlink=1), marathonist

- [Eugenio Calderón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugenio_Calder%C3%B3n), professional footballer, played for [Cruz Azul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruz_Azul), Morelia, Irapuato, Tigres and Guadalajara. Retired in 1992.

- [Jaime Contreras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaime_Contreras?action=edit\&redlink=1), artist, designer, welder, semi-professional footballer.

- [Hugo Ayala Castro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Ayala_Castro?action=edit\&redlink=1), professional footballer, play for Morelia, Atlas and currently Tigres.
