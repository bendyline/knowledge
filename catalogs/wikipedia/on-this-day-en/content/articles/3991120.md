The **Chamber of Deputies**, officially the **Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic** ([Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_language): *Poslanecká sněmovna Parlamentu České republiky*, **PS PČR**), is the [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house) of the [Parliament of the Czech Republic](3991200.md). The chamber has 200 seats and deputies are elected for four-year terms using the [party-list proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party-list_proportional_representation) system with the [Imperiali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperiali_quota) and [Hagenbach-Bischoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Droop_quota) quotas.[^cite_note-7] Since 2002, there have been 14 constituencies, matching the Czech [regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_the_Czech_Republic), with district size varying from 8 to 26 representatives. A [Cabinet](14380629.md) is answerable to the Chamber of Deputies and the [Prime Minister](410283.md) stays in office only as long as they retain the support of a majority of its members. The [quorum](237014.md) is set by law to one third (67) of elected deputies. Any changes to the constitutional laws must be approved by at least 60 percent of the Chamber of Deputies. The seat of the Chamber of Deputies is the [Thun Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thun_Palace) in [Malá Strana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mal%C3%A1_Strana), [Prague](23844.md).&#x20;

## Electability and mandate

Every [citizen of the Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_nationality_law) over 21 years old with the right to vote is eligible to be elected. The Deputy may not hold the office of Senator, [President of the Czech Republic](410288.md) or judge, which also applies to certain positions specified by law. The office of the Deputy expires once:&#x20;

- a Deputy-elect refuses to take the oath or takes it with reservation

- a Deputy's tenure expires

- a Deputy resigns from the office

- a Deputy loses eligibility to be elected

- a Deputy takes up an office incompatible with serving as a Deputy.

  [^cite_note-8]

- the Chamber of Deputies is dissolved

## Dissolution

After a dissolution of the Chamber of Deputies, new elections must be held within two months, and the Chamber of Deputies may not be dissolved less than three months before the end of its electoral term. The Chamber of Deputies can only be dissolved by the [president](410288.md) under conditions specified by the [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Czech_Republic). The Chamber of Deputies is most commonly dissolved following two [votes of no confidence](208101.md) in the cabinet. During a dissolution of the Chamber of Deputies, the Senate has the authority to take legal measures in its place if necessary.&#x20;

## Seat of the Chamber of Deputies

The Chamber of Deputies resides in three building complexes in [Malá Strana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mal%C3%A1_Strana), Prague. The main building with the plenary chamber is the [Thun Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thun_Palace), built at the end of the 17th century. It was rebuilt at the start of the 19th century to house the [Bohemian Diet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohemian_Diet). The current plenary chamber was built in 1861 for the reinstated Bohemian Diet after it was dissolved by the [Austrian-Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian-Hungarian) Emperor [Franz Joseph I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Joseph_I_of_Austria) in 1849. The second building was the seat of the [Governors of the Kingdom of Bohemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Governors_of_the_Kingdom_of_Bohemia\&action=edit\&redlink=1) appointed by the emperor, located on [Malá Strana Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mal%C3%A1_Strana_Square). The last building complex includes the [Smiřický Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Smi%C5%99ick%C3%BD_Palace\&action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Šternberk Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=%C5%A0ternberk_Palace\&action=edit\&redlink=1) at the opposite side of the square.&#x20;
