The **Thousand Martyrs' Cup**[^cite_note-1] will mark the resumption of domestic Palestinian football in the [West Bank](33209.md) and [the Gaza Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gaza_Strip) following a suspension that began in October 2023.[^cite_note-2] The [Palestinian Football Association](5531531.md) met with representatives of West Bank clubs on 4 July 2026 to discuss restarting activities; President [Jibril Rajoub](26480696.md) disclosed plans aiming to launch a championship at the start of September.[^cite_note-3]

Officially announced on 1 August, the tournament is scheduled to include football clubs from the West Bank, Jerusalem, the Gaza Strip, and the diaspora.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The competition commenced on 4 September with three simultaneous non-competitive matches alongside an opening ceremony. The [Palestine Professional League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bank_Premier_League) is scheduled to return in its regular format next season.

This event is the first official competition organized by the Palestinian Football Association since 6 October 2023, when the 2023–24 season was halted at the start of round four and subsequently canceled. In a meeting held on 6 July 2025, Rajoub stated that there is a desire for the Palestinian League to return, provided that mandatory conditions are met. In turn, Sami Abu Al-Hossain, Assistant Secretary-General of the Association in the Southern Governorates, stated that widespread destruction occurred to sports facilities in the Gaza Strip, with the exception of the Rafah Municipal Stadium.[^cite_note-6]

## Teams

A total of 243 teams will participate, with the first round featuring a group-stage competition organized by governorate.[^cite_note-7] 25 August was established as the final date for clubs to confirm their entry.[^cite_note-auto-8] The draw took place on 1 September.[^cite_note-auto-8] Participating teams were allocated into groups consisting of three, four or five teams. The group winners and runners-up will progress to the second round.[^cite_note-9]
