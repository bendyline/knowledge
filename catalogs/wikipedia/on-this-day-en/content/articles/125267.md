**Andrew Albert Christian Edward Mountbatten-Windsor** (born 19 February 1960), formerly **Prince Andrew, Duke of York**, is the third child and second son of [Queen Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_II) and [Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Philip,_Duke_of_Edinburgh), and a younger brother of [King Charles III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Charles_III). Andrew was born second in the [line of succession to the British throne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_of_succession_to_the_British_throne) and is eighth as of 2026.

Serving in the [Royal Navy](26061.md) from 1979 to 2001, Andrew saw active duty as a helicopter pilot during the [Falklands War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falklands_War); he later became a helicopter instructor and commanded a warship. He married [Sarah Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Ferguson) in 1986, and was created [Duke of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_of_York) on [their wedding day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedding_of_Prince_Andrew_and_Sarah_Ferguson). They had two daughters, [Beatrice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Beatrice) and [Eugenie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Eugenie), before separating in 1992 and divorcing in 1996. From 2001 to 2011, Andrew served as the UK's [Special Representative for International Trade and Investment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Representative_for_International_Trade_and_Investment), but resigned following scrutiny of his expenses and associations with controversial figures. He continued to undertake official duties on behalf of Elizabeth II until 2019.

Andrew had [a long-standing association](81480934.md) with the American financier and child sex offender [Jeffrey Epstein](6253522.md). In 2014, [Virginia Giuffre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Giuffre) said that she had been [sex trafficked](2101268.md) to Andrew by Epstein and [Ghislaine Maxwell](32018562.md). Andrew denied any wrongdoing, and in 2022 settled [a civil lawsuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuffre_v._Prince_Andrew) with Giuffre in the United States without admission of liability. In the same year, Elizabeth II removed his military affiliations and patronages, and he ceased using the style "[Royal Highness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Highness)". In 2025, Charles III removed Andrew's remaining [royal styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_styles) and [honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders,_decorations,_and_medals_of_the_United_Kingdom), and restricted his use of titles and [peerages](58254.md). He vacated [Royal Lodge](1946310.md), part of the [Crown Estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Estate), and relocated to private accommodation on the [Sandringham estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandringham_estate). Following the release of more [Epstein files](80415168.md) in early 2026, Andrew was arrested on suspicion of [misconduct in public office](2289190.md) and was later released under investigation.

## Early life

During a 45-day tour of Canada in June and July 1959, [Queen Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_II) discovered that she was pregnant. After her return to London,[^cite_note-1] [Buckingham Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckingham_Palace) announced on 7 August that she would not undertake further public engagements, a customary indication of royal pregnancy.[^cite_note-2] Andrew was born a [prince of the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_prince) at 3\:30 pm on 19 February 1960 at Buckingham Palace,[^cite_note-3] the third child and second son of Queen Elizabeth II and [Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Philip,_Duke_of_Edinburgh). He was [christened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_name) Andrew Albert Christian Edward in the Music Room at the palace on 8 April.[^cite_note-4]

Andrew was the first child born to a reigning British monarch since [Princess Beatrice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Beatrice_of_the_United_Kingdom), the youngest daughter of [Queen Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Victoria), in 1857.[^cite_note-5] Like his siblings, [Charles](125248.md), [Anne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne,_Princess_Royal) and [Edward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Edward,_Duke_of_Edinburgh), he was looked after by a [governess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governess), who oversaw his early education at Buckingham Palace.[^cite_note-BBC-2011-6] He later attended [Heatherdown School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heatherdown_School) near [Ascot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascot,_Berkshire) in [Berkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkshire).[^cite_note-Barkham-2004-7] In September 1973, he entered [Gordonstoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordonstoun) in [Moray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moray), which his father and elder brother had also attended.[^cite_note-8] He was nicknamed "the Sniggerer" by his schoolmates at Gordonstoun, because of "his penchant for off-colour jokes, at which he laughed inordinately".[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] While there, he spent six months – from January to June 1977 – participating in an exchange programme at [Lakefield College School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakefield_College_School) in Canada.[^cite_note-Barkham-2004-7][^cite_note-DoY-Ed-11] He left Gordonstoun in July 1979 with [A-levels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advanced_Level_\(UK\))[^cite_note-DoY-Ed-11] in English, history, and economics.[^cite_note-12]

## Naval military service

### Training

The [Royal Household](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Households_of_the_United_Kingdom) announced in November 1978 that Andrew would join the [Royal Navy](26061.md) the following year. In December, he underwent a series of sporting tests and examinations at the [Aircrew Selection Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Officer_and_Aircrew_Selection_Centre), at [RAF Biggin Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Biggin_Hill_Airport), followed by further tests and interviews at [HMS *Daedalus*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNAS_Lee-on-Solent_\(HMS_Daedalus\)) and the [Admiralty Interview Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiralty_Interview_Board), [HMS *Sultan*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Sultan_\(1956_shore_establishment\)). During March and April 1979, he was enrolled at the Royal Naval College Flight for pilot training, after which he was accepted as a trainee helicopter pilot and signed on for 12 years from 11 May 1979. On 1 September that year, Andrew was appointed a [midshipman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midshipman) and entered [Britannia Royal Naval College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britannia_Royal_Naval_College), Dartmouth. He also completed the [Royal Marines](93985.md) [All Arms Commando Course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Arms_Commando_Course) in 1979, for which he received his [green beret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_beret).[^cite_note-naval-13] He was commissioned as a [sub-lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub-lieutenant) on 1 September 1981 and appointed to the Trained Strength on 22 October.[^cite_note-14]

After [passing out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passing_out_\(military\)) from Dartmouth, Andrew undertook elementary flying training with the [Royal Air Force](25679.md) at [RAF Leeming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAF_Leeming), followed by basic flying training with the navy at [HMS *Seahawk*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNAS_Culdrose_\(HMS_Seahawk\)), where he learned to fly the [Gazelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%A9rospatiale_Gazelle) helicopter.[^cite_note-naval-13] After being awarded his wings, he progressed to advanced training on the [Sea King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westland_Sea_King) helicopter and carried out operational flying training until 1982. He subsequently joined [820 Naval Air Squadron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/820_Naval_Air_Squadron) on the [aircraft carrier](2219.md) [HMS *Invincible*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Invincible_\(R05\)).[^cite_note-naval-13]

### Falklands War

On 2 April 1982, Argentina [invaded the Falkland Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_invasion_of_the_Falkland_Islands), a [British Overseas Territory](207114.md) administered by the United Kingdom and claimed by Argentina, triggering the [Falklands War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falklands_War).[^cite_note-15] *Invincible* was one of only two operational aircraft carriers available to the Royal Navy and therefore played a major role in the [task force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falklands_War_order_of_battle:_British_naval_forces) assembled to retake the islands.[^cite_note-16]

Andrew's presence on board, and the risk of a royal family member being killed in action, made the British government apprehensive, and the [Cabinet](162790.md) sought to move him to a desk role for the duration of the conflict. The Queen, however, insisted that her son remain with his ship.[^cite_note-17] Andrew served on *Invincible* as a Sea King helicopter co-pilot, flying missions that included [anti-submarine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-submarine_warfare) and [anti-surface warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-surface_warfare), [Exocet missile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exocet_missile) decoy operations, casualty evacuation, transport, and search and air rescue.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] He witnessed the Argentine attack on [SS *Atlantic Conveyor*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Atlantic_Conveyor).[^cite_note-21]

At the end of the war, *Invincible* returned to [Portsmouth](24207.md), where Elizabeth and Philip joined other families of the crew in welcoming the vessel home. According to historian [Andrew Lownie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Lownie), the [Argentine military government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Reorganization_Process) planned, but ultimately did not attempt, to assassinate Andrew on [Mustique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustique) in July 1982.[^cite_note-22] Although he had brief assignments to [HMS *Illustrious*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Illustrious_\(R06\)), [RNAS Culdrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNAS_Culdrose), and the [School of Service Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Service_Intelligence), Andrew remained with *Invincible* until 1983. Commander [Nigel Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigel_Ward)'s memoir *Sea Harrier Over the Falklands* described Andrew as "an excellent pilot and a very promising officer."[^cite_note-23]

### Career officer



With the US secretary of defence [Leon Panetta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Panetta) commemorating the 100th anniversary of Naval Aviation at the [National Building Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Building_Museum) in 2011

In late 1983, Andrew transferred to [RNAS Portland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNAS_Portland) and was trained to fly the [Lynx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westland_Lynx) helicopter.[^cite_note-naval-13] On 1 February 1984, he was promoted to lieutenant,[^cite_note-24] after which Elizabeth appointed him her [personal aide-de-camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_aide-de-camp).[^cite_note-25] Andrew served aboard [HMS *Brazen*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Brazen_\(F91\)) as a flight pilot until 1986,[^cite_note-naval-13] including deployment to the [Mediterranean Sea](19006.md) as part of [Standing NRF Maritime Group 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standing_NRF_Maritime_Group_2). He undertook the Lieutenants' [Greenwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Naval_College,_Greenwich) Staff course.[^cite_note-naval-13]

On 23 October 1986, he transferred to the [General List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_List) and enrolled on a four-month helicopter warfare instructor's course at [RNAS Yeovilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNAS_Yeovilton). Upon graduation, he served from February 1987 to April 1988 as a helicopter warfare officer in [702 Naval Air Squadron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/702_Naval_Air_Squadron), [RNAS Portland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNAS_Portland). He later served on [HMS *Edinburgh*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Edinburgh_\(D97\)) as [officer of the watch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watchstanding) and Assistant Navigating Officer until 1989, including a six-month deployment to the Far East as part of exercise *Outback 88*.[^cite_note-naval-13]

From 1989 to 1991, Andrew served as flight commander and pilot of the Lynx HAS3 on [HMS *Campbeltown*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Campbeltown_\(F86\)). He also acted as force aviation officer to [Standing NRF Maritime Group 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standing_NRF_Maritime_Group_1) while *Campbeltown* was flagship of the [NATO](21133.md) force in the [North Atlantic](698.md) from 1990 to 1991.[^cite_note-naval-13] He passed the squadron command examination on 16 July 1991, attended the [Staff College, Camberley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staff_College,_Camberley) in 1992, and completed the Army Staff course. He was promoted to [lieutenant-commander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_commander_\(Royal_Navy\)) on 1 February and passed the ship command examination on 12 March 1992. From 1993 to 1994 Andrew commanded the Hunt-class [minehunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minehunter) [HMS *Cottesmore*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Cottesmore_\(M32\)).[^cite_note-naval-13]

From 1995 to 1996, Andrew was posted as senior pilot of [815 Naval Air Squadron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/815_Naval_Air_Squadron), then the largest flying unit in the [Fleet Air Arm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fleet_Air_Arm). His main responsibility was to supervise flying standards and ensure effective operational capability.[^cite_note-naval-13] He was promoted to [commander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_\(Royal_Navy\)) on 27 April 1999.[^cite_note-naval-13] He concluded his active naval career at the [Ministry of Defence](203793.md) in 2001 as an officer of the Diplomatic Directorate of the Naval Staff.[^cite_note-naval-13] In July that year, Andrew was retired from the [Active List of the Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navy_Directory).[^cite_note-LG56295-26] He was made an honorary [captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_\(Royal_Navy\)) in 2004,[^cite_note-LG57705-27] promoted to [rear admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rear_Admiral_\(Royal_Navy\)) on his 50th birthday on 19 February 2010,[^cite_note-LG59341-28] and to [vice admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_Admiral_\(Royal_Navy\)) in 2015.[^cite_note-LG61660-29][^cite_note-30]

## Personal life

### Relationships

#### Before marriage

In May 1978, the *[Evening News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Evening_News_\(London_newspaper\))* reported that Andrew had acquired the nickname "Randy Andy" (with "[randy](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/randy)" being British slang for "sexually eager") while at [Gordonstoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordonstoun), owing to his being romantically involved with several women.[^cite_note-31] [UPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Press_International) also used this nickname, stating that before beginning his naval career in 1979 he "seemed to be in training as a professional [playboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playboy_lifestyle)".[^cite_note-32] Andrew met the American photographer and actress [Koo Stark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koo_Stark) in February 1981, before his active service in the Falklands War.[^cite_note-Battersby-2015-33][^cite_note-34] In October 1982 they holidayed together on the island of [Mustique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustique).[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] [Tina Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_Brown) later described Stark as Andrew's only serious love interest.[^cite_note-Brown-2007-37] The couple separated in 1983 under pressure from the press and the palace.[^cite_note-Battersby-2015-33][^cite_note-Brown-2007-37] In 1997, Andrew became godfather to Stark's daughter.[^cite_note-38] When Andrew faced accusations in 2015 regarding his association to Jeffrey Epstein, Stark publicly defended him.[^cite_note-Battersby-2015-33]

#### Marriage and children

Further information: [Wedding of Prince Andrew and Sarah Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedding_of_Prince_Andrew_and_Sarah_Ferguson) and [Descendants of Elizabeth II § Children of Andrew and Sarah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descendants_of_Elizabeth_II#Children_of_Andrew_and_Sarah)



Andrew and Sarah in [Townsville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townsville), Australia, 1988

Andrew married [Sarah Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Ferguson) at [Westminster Abbey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_Abbey) on 23 July 1986. On the same day, Elizabeth created him [Duke of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_of_York), [Earl of Inverness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_of_Inverness) and [Baron Killyleagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baron_Killyleagh);[^cite_note-39] the first two of these titles had previously been held by both his maternal grandfather, [George VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_VI), and his great-grandfather [George V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_V). Andrew had known Ferguson since childhood; they had met occasionally at [polo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polo) matches and became reacquainted at [Royal Ascot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Ascot) in 1985.[^cite_note-DeYoung-1986-40]

The couple initially appeared to have a happy marriage and had two daughters, [Princess Beatrice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Beatrice) (born 1988) and [Princess Eugenie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Eugenie) (born 1990), presenting a united public image during the late 1980s. Sarah's personal qualities were regarded as refreshing within the formal protocol of the royal family.[^cite_note-DeYoung-1986-40] Andrew's frequent travel due to his naval career, combined with relentless and often critical media attention on the Duchess of York, contributed to strains in the marriage.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42]

The couple made a well publicised [royal tour of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_royal_tour_of_California) in March 1988.[^cite_note-UPIMar88-43]

On 19 March 1992, the couple announced plans to separate and did so amicably.[^cite_note-44] That August, [tabloid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabloid_journalism) newspapers published photographs of the businessman John Bryan sucking Sarah's toes, effectively ending any prospect of reconciliation. Throughout the separation, Ferguson had maintained that Bryan was her financial adviser, a claim Andrew accepted.[^cite_note-45] The marriage ended in divorce on 30 May 1996. Andrew spoke warmly of his former wife in 2008, saying, "We have managed to work together to bring our children up in a way that few others have been able to and I am extremely grateful to be able to do that."[^cite_note-46]



Andrew and his daughter Eugenie riding in the carriage procession at [Trooping the Colour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trooping_the_Colour), 16 June 2012

In May 2010, Ferguson was filmed by a *[News of the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_of_the_World)* reporter stating that Andrew had agreed that, if she were to receive £500,000, he would meet the donor and provide useful top-level business contacts. She was filmed receiving US\$40,000 in cash as a down-payment. The newspaper reported that Andrew had no knowledge of the arrangement.[^cite_note-47] In July 2011, Ferguson said that her multi-million-pound debts had been cleared through the intervention of her former husband, whom she described as a "knight on a white charger".[^cite_note-48]

In 2011, Ferguson said that she had made a "gigantic error of judgement" in allowing Epstein to pay off a debt for her, and apologised for accepting money from him. She nevertheless continued to defend Andrew's former friendship with Epstein.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50] It later emerged that, following her public statement, she had sent an email to Epstein in which she referred to him as "a steadfast, generous and supreme friend".[^cite_note-51]

#### Post-divorce

In 1999, Andrew was briefly in a relationship with [Lady Victoria Hervey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Victoria_Hervey), who has since made a number of statements in his support.[^cite_note-52]

### Residences

As Andrew and Ferguson shared custody of their two daughters, the family continued to live at [Sunninghill Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunninghill_Park), which had been built for the couple near [Windsor Great Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windsor_Great_Park) in 1990, until Andrew moved to [Royal Lodge](1946310.md) in 2004. In 2007, Ferguson moved into Dolphin House in [Englefield Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Englefield_Green), less than a mile from Royal Lodge.[^cite_note-Borland-2008-53] A fire at Dolphin House in 2008[^cite_note-Borland-2008-53] led her to move into Royal Lodge, once again sharing a home with Andrew.[^cite_note-54]

Andrew's lease of Royal Lodge was for 75 years, held from the [Crown Estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Estate), with a single £1 million premium and a commitment to spend £7.5 million on refurbishment.[^cite_note-55] In March 2023, it was reported that Andrew had been offered [Frogmore Cottage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frogmore_Cottage) after his nephew [Prince Harry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Harry) was asked to vacate the residence.[^cite_note-56] The offer came amid reports that Andrew could no longer afford the running costs of Royal Lodge, as he was due to lose his annual grant.[^cite_note-57] In June 2026, it was revealed in a [National Audit Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Audit_Office_\(United_Kingdom\)) report that Andrew had received an undisclosed amount of rental income from sub‑letting three cottages on the Royal Lodge estate.[^cite_note-58]

In October 2025, it was reported that Andrew had paid a [peppercorn rent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peppercorn_rent) for the Royal Lodge lease in exchange for upfront payments totalling £8.5 million, with the agreement entitling him and his family to reside at the property until 2078.[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-60] Later that month, Buckingham Palace announced that formal notice had been served to surrender the lease. The Crown Estate later clarified that Andrew "will not be owed any compensation for early surrender of the lease ... once dilapidations are taken into account".[^cite_note-61][^cite_note-62] On 2 February 2026, Andrew left Royal Lodge and moved temporarily to [Wood Farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood_Farm) on the Sandringham estate while his future accommodation underwent renovation.[^cite_note-63] He relocated to [Marsh Farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marsh_Farm,_Sandringham) in April 2026.[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65] Both properties are located on the [Sandringham estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandringham_estate), which is privately owned by the King.[^cite_note-Nanji-2025-66][^cite_note-67]

Andrew is a keen [skier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skier), and in 2014 he bought a [chalet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalet) in [Verbier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbier), Switzerland, for £13 million, jointly with his ex-wife.[^cite_note-68] In May 2020, it was reported that they were in a legal dispute over the mortgage.[^cite_note-69] To purchase the property, they had secured a loan of £13.25 million and were expected to pay £5 million in cash instalments which, with interest, totalled £6.8 million.[^cite_note-Bodkin-2022-70] Despite claims that the Queen would assist with the payment, a spokesperson for Andrew confirmed that she "will not be stepping in to settle the debt".[^cite_note-71]

*[The Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times)* reported in September 2021 that Andrew and Ferguson had reached a legal agreement with the chalet's previous owner and would sell the property.[^cite_note-72] The owner agreed to accept £3.4 million – half of what she was owed – after being informed that Andrew and Ferguson were experiencing financial difficulties.[^cite_note-Bodkin-2022-70] Proceeds from the sale were reportedly intended to contribute to Andrew's legal expenses in relation to the [civil lawsuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuffre_v._Prince_Andrew) with Virginia Giuffre.[^cite_note-73] In June 2022, *[Le Temps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Temps)* reported that the chalet had been frozen because of a £1.6 million debt Andrew owed to unnamed individuals. Law professor Nicolas Jeandin told the newspaper, "A sale is in principle impossible, except with the agreement of the creditor."[^cite_note-74][^cite_note-75]

### Health

On 2 June 2022, Andrew tested positive for [COVID-19](63030231.md), and it was announced that he would not attend the [Platinum Jubilee National Service of Thanksgiving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platinum_Jubilee_National_Service_of_Thanksgiving) at [St Paul's Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Paul's_Cathedral) on 3 June.[^cite_note-76] Andrew is a [teetotaller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teetotaller).[^cite_note-77]

### Interests

Andrew is a keen golfer and has held a low single-figure [handicap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handicap_\(golf\)).[^cite_note-78] He served as captain of [the Royal and Ancient Golf Club of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Royal_and_Ancient_Golf_Club_of_St_Andrews) between 2003 and 2004 – during the club's 250th anniversary season – was patron of several royal golf clubs, and had been elected an honorary member of many others. ### Lawsuit

In August 2021, Giuffre sued Andrew in the federal District Court for the Southern District of New York, accusing him of "sexual assault and intentional infliction of emotional distress".[^cite_note-221] On 29 October 2021, Andrew's lawyers filed a response stating that he "unequivocally denies Giuffre's false allegations".[^cite_note-222][^cite_note-223] On 12 January 2022, Judge Kaplan rejected Andrew's attempts to dismiss the case, allowing the lawsuit to proceed.[^cite_note-224][^cite_note-225] In February, the case was settled out of court, with Andrew making a donation to Giuffre's charity for victims of abuse, without any admission of liability.[^cite_note-226]

### Repercussions

On 20 November 2019, Buckingham Palace announced that Andrew was suspending his public duties "for the foreseeable future". The decision, made with the Queen's consent, was accompanied by an insistence that Andrew sympathised with Epstein's victims.[^cite_note-227] Other working royals took over his commitments in the short term.[^cite_note-228] On 24 November, the palace confirmed that Andrew would step down from all 230 of his patronages.[^cite_note-229] The scrutiny of Andrew's relationship with Epstein also led to scrutiny of other public figures' ties to Epstein, notably [those of Mette-Marit, Crown Princess of Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relationship_of_Mette-Marit,_Crown_Princess_of_Norway,_and_Jeffrey_Epstein).[^cite_note-nettavisenep-230]

In March 2020, Andrew hired Mark Gallagher, a crisis-management expert who had assisted high-profile clients falsely accused during [Operation Midland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Midland).[^cite_note-231] In May 2020, it was announced that Andrew would permanently resign from all public roles due to his ties to Epstein.[^cite_note-232] In January 2022, Andrew's social-media accounts were deleted, his page on the royal family's website was rewritten in the past tense, and his military affiliations and patronages were removed to emphasise his withdrawal from public life.[^cite_note-233] He also stopped using the style [His Royal Highness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/His_Royal_Highness) (HRH), although it was not formally removed.[^cite_note-BBC_News-2022a-109] In June 2022, [Rachael Maskell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachael_Maskell), Labour MP for [York Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_Central_\(UK_Parliament_constituency\)), introduced a 'Removal of Titles' [private members bill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_Members'_Bills_in_the_Parliament_of_the_United_Kingdom) in the House of Commons,[^cite_note-BBC_News-2022b-234] which would have enabled the monarch or a parliamentary committee to strip aristocratic titles from individuals deemed unworthy.[^cite_note-235][^cite_note-BBC_News-2022b-234]

In March 2022, Andrew made his first public appearance in months, helping the Queen walk into Westminster Abbey for a memorial service for his father, the Duke of Edinburgh.[^cite_note-236] In June 2022, Andrew took part in the private elements of the [Garter Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garter_Day) ceremony, including lunch and the investiture of new members, but was excluded from the public procession amid reports that his brother Charles and nephew [William](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William,_Prince_of_Wales) had intervened to prevent him appearing in view of the public.[^cite_note-237] Following the [death of Queen Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_Queen_Elizabeth_II) on 8 September 2022, Andrew appeared in civilian clothing at various ceremonial events.[^cite_note-238] He wore military uniform for a 15-minute [vigil beside the Queen's coffin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vigil_of_the_Princes) at [Westminster Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_Hall) on 16 September.[^cite_note-239]

In October 2022, it was reported that Andrew no longer received government funding.[^cite_note-240] The following month, it was also reported that he was set to lose his police protection, as he was no longer expected to undertake public duties in line with King Charles III's wishes.[^cite_note-241] His armed personal protection officers were expected to be replaced by private security guards, likely to be funded by the King, at an estimated cost of up to £3 million per year.[^cite_note-Ward-2022-242] In January 2023, it was reported that Andrew could no longer use his suite of rooms at Buckingham Palace.[^cite_note-243] In August 2024, *The Telegraph* reported that the King would withdraw funding for Andrew's security by the end of October, requiring him to pay for future security operations at Royal Lodge.[^cite_note-244]

On 2 November 2025, Defence Secretary [John Healey](434625.md) confirmed that Andrew's [honorary rank of vice-admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice-admiral_\(Royal_Navy\)) – retained after he relinquished his other military titles in 2022 – would be removed following direction from the King,[^cite_note-245][^cite_note-246] a process finalised by 13 December.[^cite_note-\:1-247] He continues to hold the [South Atlantic Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Atlantic_Medal) with rosette, awarded to all who served in the Falklands War.[^cite_note-248] On 3 November, [letters patent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letters_patent_\(United_Kingdom\)) were issued removing Andrew's style of "[Royal Highness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Highness)" and the title "prince";[^cite_note-Gazette-Removals-249] without these honorifics, it was agreed that he would use the family surname [Mountbatten-Windsor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountbatten-Windsor).[^cite_note-\:2-250] His appointments to the [Royal Victorian Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Victorian_Order) and [Order of the Garter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Garter) were also rescinded,[^cite_note-LG-251] and his banner was removed from [St George's Chapel, Windsor Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_George's_Chapel,_Windsor_Castle), the chapel of the Order of the Garter.[^cite_note-banner-252]

Later that month, Andrew's life membership of the [Savage Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savage_Club) was withdrawn.[^cite_note-253] Commemorative plaques bearing his name were removed from several locations in the [Falkland Islands](7550182.md),[^cite_note-254] and Mid and East Antrim Council agreed to rename Prince Andrew Way in [Carrickfergus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrickfergus).[^cite_note-255] On 19 November, [Metropolitan Police](192450.md) firearms-licensing officers requested that Andrew voluntarily surrender his firearms and shotgun certificate, which he did. No reason for the surrender was given.[^cite_note-256]

### Misconduct in public office

Emails disclosed as part of the Epstein files in early 2026 appear to indicate that, between 2010 and 2011, Andrew may have knowingly shared confidential information with Epstein about his official work as trade envoy; trade envoys have a duty of confidentiality over sensitive, commercial, or political information arising from their official visits.[^cite_note-257] It is further alleged that in 2010, Andrew passed on an email conversation about the [Royal Bank of Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Bank_of_Scotland) and [Aston Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Martin) to Terence Allen and [David Stern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Stern_\(German_businessman\)).[^cite_note-258] In February 2026, [Thames Valley Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thames_Valley_Police) stated that they were considering investigating [Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_\(pressure_group\))'s report concerning Andrew for suspected [misconduct in public office](2289190.md) and an alleged breach of official secrets.[^cite_note-BBC_cqlgv3z79klo2-259] In the [United Kingdom](31717.md), misconduct in public office is a [common-law crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_law_offence) not subject to a [statute of limitations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statute_of_limitations).[^cite_note-260]

In May 2026, it was reported that a search of court documents had shown that Buckingham Palace had received an email archive six years earlier that would have indicated Andrew shared confidential government information while serving as trade envoy.[^cite_note-BBC-BPem-261] The filings state that about 30,000 emails, containing material linked to his disputed financial dealings, were passed to the [Lord Chamberlain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Chamberlain) in 2020 after being taken from one of Andrew's business associates.[^cite_note-BBC-BPem-261] Buckingham Palace said it could not comment while a police inquiry involving him was ongoing.[^cite_note-BBC-BPem-261]

#### Arrest

On the morning of 19 February 2026, his 66th birthday, Andrew was arrested on suspicion of misconduct in public office at the Sandringham estate,[^cite_note-BBC_c70kjr9wjw0t_11182-262] where he had been living since leaving his home in Windsor.[^cite_note-BBC_c70kjr9wjw0t_11182-262] It was the first arrest of a senior member of the British royal family since [that of King Charles I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_I_of_England#Captivity) in 1647,[^cite_note-Guardian-Arrest-263] and the first arrest of a sibling of the reigning monarch since [that of Elizabeth I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_I#Heir_presumptive) in 1554.[^cite_note-264]

Thames Valley Police stated that they were searching addresses in Berkshire and [Norfolk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk), and it was subsequently confirmed that officers were searching Royal Lodge in [Windsor Great Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windsor_Great_Park), where Andrew previously lived.[^cite_note-BBC_c70kjr9wjw0t_11182-262] Charles III released a statement expressing his "deepest concern", and said that "the law must take its course".[^cite_note-265] Andrew was released from [Aylsham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aylsham) police station later that day, around 11 hours after his arrest, under investigation.[^cite_note-BBC_c70kjr9wjw0t_11182-262][^cite_note-Guardian-Arrest-263]

In 2019, in light of Andrew's friendship with the convicted sex offender Epstein, residents of [Inverness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverness) began a campaign to strip him of his [earldom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl), saying that "it is inappropriate that Prince Andrew is associated with our beautiful city".[^cite_note-300]

#### 2022

On 13 January 2022, Buckingham Palace announced that Andrew had ceased using the style "Royal Highness" publicly,[^cite_note-301][^cite_note-BBC_News-2022a-109][^cite_note-302] though he remained permitted to use it privately.[^cite_note-Ward-2023-110] On 14 January, [Rachael Maskell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachael_Maskell), the [Labour Co-op](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Co-op) MP for [York Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_Central_\(UK_Parliament_constituency\)), called for Andrew to lose his [dukedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke), and a renewed petition to remove Andrew's earldom was launched in Inverness, gaining over 6000 signatures by 16 September.[^cite_note-303]

On 16 February, residents of [Killyleagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killyleagh) expressed their opinion that Andrew should be stripped of his [barony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baron),[^cite_note-304] and Maskell said she would seek legislation to remove Andrew's dukedom if he did not relinquish it voluntarily.[^cite_note-305] On 27 April, the [City of York Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_York_Council) removed Andrew's honorary freedom of the city by a unanimous vote, and York councillors called for him to lose his dukedom.[^cite_note-306]

#### 2025 changes

On 17 October 2025, following discussions with King Charles III, Andrew agreed to cease using his peerages and honours, including his dukedom and his knighthoods as a [Royal Knight Companion of the Order of the Garter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Knight_Companion_of_the_Order_of_the_Garter) and a [Knight Grand Cross of the Royal Victorian Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Grand_Cross_of_the_Royal_Victorian_Order).[^cite_note-307][^cite_note-308][^cite_note-309] His [banner of arms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_standard_of_the_United_Kingdom#Other_members_of_the_royal_family), which had hung in [St George's Chapel, Windsor Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_George's_Chapel,_Windsor_Castle), since 2006 to signify his membership of the Order of the Garter, was removed.[^cite_note-banner-252]

On 30 October, Buckingham Palace announced that the King had begun a "formal process" to remove his brother's style, titles, and honours.[^cite_note-Nanji-2025-66] Andrew's name was removed from the [Roll of the Peerage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roll_of_the_Peerage) the same day.[^cite_note-310][^cite_note-311] Although this did not revoke his peerages,[^cite_note-hoclib-312] it meant he was no longer entitled to any place in the [orders of precedence in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders_of_precedence_in_the_United_Kingdom) derived from them, and ceased to be addressed or referred to by any title derived from his peerages in official documents.[^cite_note-\:0-313] His profile was taken down from the royal website the following day.[^cite_note-314]

On 3 November, [letters patent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letters_patent_\(United_Kingdom\)) were issued that removed the style "Royal Highness" and the title "prince" from Andrew,[^cite_note-Gazette-Removals-249] and on 1 December, a notice, backdated to 30 October, was published in *[The London Gazette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_London_Gazette)*, stating that his appointments to the Order of the Garter and Royal Victorian Order had been annulled.[^cite_note-LG-251] He is to use the surname "Mountbatten-Windsor", in accordance with the [1960 Privy Council declaration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountbatten-Windsor#Origin) on the family surname by Queen Elizabeth II.[^cite_note-\:2-250] On 13 December, the [Ministry of Defence](203793.md) announced that the [Defence Council of the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Council_of_the_United_Kingdom) had removed Andrew's [honorary rank of vice-admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice-admiral_\(Royal_Navy\)), whereupon he reverted to the rank of [commander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_\(Royal_Navy\)) (retired).[^cite_note-\:1-247] Also in December, [New Zealand](4913064.md) stripped him of the [1990 Commemoration Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_1990_Commemoration_Medal).[^cite_note-NZP-315]

#### 2026

On 19 February 2026, after Andrew was arrested on suspicion of misconduct in public office, the [Norwegian royal family](2578348.md) announced that he had relinquished and returned his appointment as a member of the [Order of St. Olav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_St._Olav).[^cite_note-\:3-316]

Andrew remains eighth in the [line of succession to the British throne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_of_succession_to_the_British_throne) as of 2026.[^cite_note-317] The UK government has indicated that it is considering legislation to remove him from the line of succession, a step that would require the consent of all Commonwealth realms.[^cite_note-bbc-andrew-succession-2026-318] The governments of Canada, Australia, and New Zealand have stated that they would support such a measure.[^cite_note-319][^cite_note-320][^cite_note-BBC-NZ-Andrew-2026-321]

As the third [British subject](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_subject) of full age in the line of succession [domiciled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domicile_\(law\)) in the UK, Andrew holds the position of [Counsellor of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counsellor_of_State), although it is unlikely he would be called upon to exercise any royal functions in that capacity because he is no longer a working member of the royal family;[^cite_note-Guardian-payments-183] he would automatically cease to hold the role if removed from the line of succession.[^cite_note-bbc-andrew-succession-2026-318]
