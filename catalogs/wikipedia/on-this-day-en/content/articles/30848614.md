The [oil tanker](15923603.md) **MV *Younara Glory*** is one of the [world's longest ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_world's_longest_ships). She was built in 2004 at the [Daewoo Shipbuilding & Marine Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daewoo_Shipbuilding_&_Marine_Engineering) as ***Delos***.[^cite_note-MT-1][^cite_note-2] She was later renamed ***DHT Condor***, *Younara Glory* and then ***M Sophia*** in 2021.[^cite_note-MT-1] She is a crude carrier with tonnages of 320,051 tons [deadweight](1346384.md) (DWT) and 161,235 [gross tonnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_tonnage) (GT).[^cite_note-MT-1] The overall length of the ship is 333.00 metres (1,092 ft 6 in), the extreme beam is 60.00 metres (196 ft 10 in) and the [draught](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draft_\(hull\)) is 22.47 metres (73 ft 9 in).[^cite_note-3] The cargo ship has total tank capacity for 340,584 cubic metres (2,142,210 [bbl](594043.md#Oil_barrel)) at 98%.

## Engineering

The main engine of the ship is [MAN B\&W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAN_B\&W) 6S90MC-C with output power of 39,500 horsepower (29,500 kW), achieved at 76 rpm. The output power together with the improved propulsion system and high-effective propeller, allow the cargo ship to operate with service speed of 15 [knots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (28 km/h; 17 mph). The maximum achieved speed during the trial tests was over 16.5 knots (30.6 km/h; 19.0 mph).

## 2026 Seizure

On 7 January 2026, the [U.S. Southern Command](1590932.md) announced they had seized the ship as part of [Operation Southern Spear](81814844.md).[^cite_note-DW-4][^cite_note-\:1-5] They described the ship as a "stateless, sanctioned [dark fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_fleet) motor".[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-DW-4]
