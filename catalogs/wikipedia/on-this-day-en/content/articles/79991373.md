**Malick Ndiaye**, also **El Malick Ndiaye** or **N'diaye**,[^cite_note-JeuneAfrique-1] (born 24 July 1982[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-Sene-3]) is a Senegalese politician. A member of [PASTEF](70928317.md) since 2015, he served as Minister of Infrastructure and Land and Air Transport from April to December 2024, before being elected [President of the National Assembly](43374574.md) on 2 December 2024.

## Political career

Malick Ndiaye joined the PASTEF party in 2015.

On 5 April 2024, he was appointed Minister of Infrastructure and Land and Air Transport in the government of Ousmane Sonko, formed following the [2024 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Senegalese_presidential_election).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] He was the lead candidate for PASTEF in the Linguère department during the 2024 snap legislative elections. After his party's sweeping victory, he was elected President of the National Assembly on 2 December 2024, during the inauguration of the 15th legislature, receiving 134 votes in favor, 22 against, and 7 abstentions.[^cite_note-Sene-3][^cite_note-JeuneAfrique-1]
