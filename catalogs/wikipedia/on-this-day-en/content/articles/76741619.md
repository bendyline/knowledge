**Global Disinformation Index** (**GDI**) is a [not-for-profit organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Not-for-profit_organisation) based in the United Kingdom,[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] which aims to mitigate the spread of [disinformation](142690.md) on the internet.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-gdi19-4][^cite_note-5] The group utilises a system of ratings of news sources and websites to determine risk of disinformation.[^cite_note-6] The group's efforts also include investigations into internet advertising,[^cite_note-7] and the alleged use of disinformation in relation to [COVID-19](63030231.md) featured on various websites.[^cite_note-gdi19-4] The group has faced criticism and scrutiny over allegations of political bias.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-UnHerdSayers-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

## Overview

GDI was founded in 2018 by [Clare Melford](35730371.md) and Daniel Rogers.[^cite_note-rand-di-15][^cite_note-16] [Alexandra Mousavizadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandra_Mousavizadeh) is listed as a primary contact for the organization by [RAND](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation).[^cite_note-rand-di-15] It has received funding through a combination of charitable trusts, governmental organizations, and tech licensees of its dynamic exclusion list. Contributors include the [Knight Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Foundation),[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-rand-di-15][^cite_note-18] [Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office](59653.md) (FCDO),[^cite_note-19] and [Luminate Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Omidyar#Luminate_Group).[^cite_note-20]

One of its strategies promoted by GDI is the aim to remove financial incentives for news content that promotes "adversarial narratives".[^cite_note-rand-di-15][^cite_note-21] GDI's investigation of COVID-19 disinformation focused on the generation of illicit revenue for websites.[^cite_note-22] GDI has reported that a 2022 evaluation of Italian online news sites resulted in categorising one third of the evaluated sites as high risk of disinformation.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

## Reception

In February 2023, the *[Washington Examiner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Examiner)*, a [conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative) website, alleged bias from the GDI, releasing an investigative series that characterised GDI as "part of a stealth operation blacklisting and trying to defund conservative media, likely costing the news companies large sums in advertising dollars".[^cite_note-twe-20230209-25] Gabe Kaminsky, the journalist who authored that series of stories, said that all 10 outlets that GDI in a report identified as the "riskiest" and "worst",[^cite_note-gdi-20221021-26] all leaned to the [political right](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_right) while all but one of the 10 ranked "least risky" leaned to the [political left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_left).[^cite_note-twe-20230209-25] Kaminsky further said that GDI received \$100,000 from the [US Department of State](31975.md);[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29] however, the State Department has denied that the grant was used to "blacklist" any companies in the US.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31]

The series in the *Washington Examiner* sparked outcry among conservatives, and prompted a lawsuit by *[The Daily Wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Wire)* and *[The Federalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Federalist)*, two American right-wing websites, against the State Department.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33] The State Department-funded [National Endowment for Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Endowment_for_Democracy) announced in 2023 that it would no longer fund GDI.[^cite_note-34] After the series of stories, [Microsoft](19001.md)'s [Xandr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xandr) cut ties with GDI and exited the political advertising space.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] The [US Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Congress) passed a law in 2023 that banned [the Pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pentagon) from funding GDI in the future for military recruitment advertising.[^cite_note-37]

In April 2024, [UnHerd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UnHerd) CEO [Freddie Sayers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freddie_Sayers) criticized GDI after it placed UnHerd on its "dynamic exclusion list", leading to a reduction in UnHerd's [advertising revenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advertising_revenue).[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39] Sayers argued that GDI's determination was based on ideological disagreements rather than factual inaccuracies.[^cite_note-UnHerdSayers-11][^cite_note-TimesCameron-40] In response, [Elon Musk](909036.md), the CEO of [Twitter](9988187.md), called for GDI to be shut down.[^cite_note-41] Following UnHerd's article, UK Business Secretary [Kemi Badenoch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kemi_Badenoch) joined around 10 [MPs](3776360.md) in raising concerns about GDI and their approach to distinguishing between free speech and disinformation.[^cite_note-42] In response to Badenoch's concerns, Foreign Secretary [David Cameron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Cameron) stated that FCDO had ceased funding GDI in 2023 and did not plan to resume funding.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-TimesCameron-40] In December 2025, the State Department imposed a visa sanction on Melford, as founder of GDI, for leading what US Secretary of State [Marco Rubio](5502549.md) called "organized efforts to coerce American platforms to censor, demonetize, and suppress American viewpoints they oppose".[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-bbc-20251224-45]

## Advisory panel

Until March 2023, GDI publicly disclosed members of its "Advisory Panel". Amongst others, these included [Anne Applebaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Applebaum), [Peter Pomerantsev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Pomerantsev), Miguel Martinez, and [Hany Farid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hany_Farid).[^cite_note-46] *[Reason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reason_\(magazine\))* reported in February 2023 that Applebaum had asked for her name to removed from the GDI website as she had not been in contact with GDI since 2019.[^cite_note-47]
