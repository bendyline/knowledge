**Governor of the Central Bank of Iran** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): رئیس کل بانک مرکزی ایران) is the highest administrative authority of the [Central Bank of Iran](3961876.md), making decisions concerning all such current affairs of the bank.[^cite_note-1]

Presently, the [President](233913.md) appoints the governor upon the recommendation of the [Minister of Economic Affairs and Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economic_Affairs_and_Finance_\(Iran\)),[^cite_note-\:1-2] who must be verified by the CBI's general assembly.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

Unlike many countries, the governor of central bank is not mandated to decide on the [monetary policy](297032.md) in Iran by himself, but he is head of the body with that responsibility, The Currency and Credit Council, an organ within the central bank but controlled by the government.[^cite_note-4] The governor is assisted by an executive board comprising, in addition to himself as the chairman, the deputy governor, vice-governors, and the secretary-general of the Currency and Credit Council.[^cite_note-\:1-2]

The law specifies no fixed term for the governor,[^cite_note-\:0-3] however the officeholder is appointed for five years.[^cite_note-\:1-2]
