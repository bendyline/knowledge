The **Chilean Armed Forces** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Fuerzas Armadas de Chile*) is the unified military organization comprising the Chilean Army, Air Force, and Navy. The [President of Chile](11885300.md) is the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the military, and formulates policy through the Minister of Defence. In recent years and after several major reequipment programs, the Chilean Armed Forces have become one of the most technologically advanced and professional armed forces in [Latin America](18524.md).[^cite_note-BAWDEN-3] The Chilean Army is mostly supplied with equipment from [Germany](11867.md), the [United States](3434750.md), [Brazil](3383.md), [Israel](9282173.md), [France](5843419.md), and [Spain](26667.md).

## Structure

### Army

Main article: [Chilean Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilean_Army)

The current commander-in-chief of the [Chilean Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilean_Army) is *General de Ejército* Sr. [Javier Iturriaga del Campo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Iturriaga_del_Campo). The 46,350-person army is organized under six military administrative regions and six divisional headquarters.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIISS2019404-1] The forces include one special forces brigade, four armoured brigades, one armoured detachment, three motorized brigades, two motorized detachments, four mountain detachments and one aviation brigade.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIISS2019404-1] The army operates German Leopard 1 and 2 tanks as its main battle tanks, including 170+ Leopard 2A4 and 115 Leopard 1.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIISS2019404-1] The army has approximately 40,000 reservists.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIISS2019404-1]

### Navy

Main article: [Chilean Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilean_Navy)

Admiral Juan Andrés De La Maza Larraín directs the 19,800-person [Chilean Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilean_Navy), including 3,600 Marines.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIISS2019405–406-4] Of the fleet of 66 surface vessels, eight are major combatant ships and they are based in [Valparaíso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valpara%C3%ADso). The navy operates its own aircraft for transport and patrol; there are no fighters or bomber aircraft but they have attack helicopters. The Navy also operates four submarines based in [Talcahuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talcahuano).

### Air Force

Main article: [Chilean Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilean_Air_Force)

General Hugo Rodríguez González heads 11,050-strong[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIISS2019406-5] [Chilean Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilean_Air_Force). Air assets are distributed among five air brigades headquartered in [Iquique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iquique), [Antofagasta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antofagasta), [Santiago](51572.md), [Puerto Montt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Montt), and [Punta Arenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punta_Arenas). The Air Force also operates an airbase on [King George Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_George_Island_\(Antarctica\)), Antarctica.
