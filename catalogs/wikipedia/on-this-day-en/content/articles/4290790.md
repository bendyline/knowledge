**Interstate 95** (**I-95**) runs 179 miles (288 km) within the [commonwealth](18618239.md) of [Virginia](32432.md) between its borders with [North Carolina](21650.md) and [Maryland](18858.md). I-95 meets the northern terminus of [I-85](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_85_in_Virginia) in [Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petersburg,_Virginia) and is [concurrent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concurrency_\(road\)) with [I-64](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_64_in_Virginia) for three miles (4.8 km) in [Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Virginia). Although I-95 was originally planned as a highway through [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) (following the route of what is now [I-395](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_395_\(Virginia%E2%80%93District_of_Columbia\))), it was rerouted along the eastern portion of the [Capital Beltway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_Beltway) concurrent with [I-495](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_495_\(Capital_Beltway\)). From Petersburg to Richmond, I-95 utilized most of the [Richmond–Petersburg Turnpike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond%E2%80%93Petersburg_Turnpike), a former [toll road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toll_road) (the south end of the toll road was on I-85). In addition to Richmond, the route also runs through the medium-sized cities of [Emporia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emporia,_Virginia), Petersburg, [Colonial Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonial_Heights,_Virginia), [Fredericksburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredericksburg,_Virginia), and [Alexandria](18940583.md).

It enters the Capital Beltway at the [Springfield Interchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield_Interchange), also known as the Mixing Bowl. I-95 continues over the [Potomac River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potomac_River) on the [Woodrow Wilson Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson_Bridge) into Washington, D.C. (for 0.11 miles \[0.18 km] on the bridge) and then into Maryland on the Capital Beltway.

The route between Fredericksburg and [Springfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield,_Virginia) is consistently one of the most congested routes of highway in the US, particularly during holidays and rush hours. The causes for this congestion are lack of alternative routes, fewer lanes than needed, and the spread-out suburbs of the Washington, D.C. area.[^cite_note-3] To solve this problem, the [Virginia Department of Transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Department_of_Transportation) (VDOT) started a project to widen I-95 to six lanes between the cities, as well as adding [express lanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local%E2%80%93express_lanes) and new offramps to [U.S. Route 17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_17_in_Virginia) (US 17) and [State Route 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_State_Route_3) (SR 3) through Fredericksburg. The project was started in 2018 and was completed on December 7, 2023.[^cite_note-4]

## Route description

### North Carolina state line to Petersburg



I-95 northbound entering Virginia from North Carolina in [Greensville County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greensville_County,_Virginia)

I-95 continues the pattern of being a four-lane highway from [North Carolina](21650.md). The northbound [welcome center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welcome_center) forbids trucks, but truck stops at the first two interchanges provides a substitute place for truckers to stay before reaching [Emporia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emporia,_Virginia).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] In Emporia, the freeway has an interchange with [US 58](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_58#Virginia). North of here, I-95 and [US 301](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_301_in_Virginia) are often intertwined with each other as they encounter interchanges with [Virginia Secondary State Routes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_secondary_state_highways_in_Virginia). In the Owens–[Stony Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Creek,_Virginia) area in [Sussex County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sussex_County,_Virginia), the road not only runs parallel to US 301 but shares bridges with I-95, many of which have access to US 301 from connecting roads. This pattern ends at exit 33 at the corner of a truck stop and travel center. Crossing the Sussex–[Prince George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_George_County,_Virginia) county line, the first site along I-95 is another [rest area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rest_area) that also serves as the Petersburg Area Tourist Information Center. The road makes a slight northeast turn between Carson and [Templeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Templeton,_Virginia), then turns straight north again before approaching the south end of [I-295](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_295_\(Virginia\)) just before crossing the southern border of the city of [Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petersburg,_Virginia) at exit 47. A series of frontage roads connect the interchanges with [US 460](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_460_in_Virginia), US 301 and the northern terminus of [I-85](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_85_in_Virginia). Immediately after the interchange with I-85, remnants of the former toll booths for the [Richmond–Petersburg Turnpike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond%E2%80%93Petersburg_Turnpike) can be found.[^cite_note-7] Also, the I-95/I-85 interchange was formerly marked with signs directing southbound travelers to the long-distance cities of [Miami, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami,_Florida), and [Atlanta, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta,_Georgia), respectively.

### Petersburg to Richmond



View of I-95 southbound past [SR 144](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_State_Route_144) in Colonial Heights



I-95 northbound at the SR 802 interchange in [Hanover County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover_County,_Virginia)



Interstate 95 interchange with Route 195 in Richmond, VA

North of Petersburg, I-95 crosses the [Appomattox River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appomattox_River) and enters the city of [Colonial Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonial_Heights,_Virginia) and then [Chesterfield County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesterfield_County,_Virginia). An extraordinarily high [Vietnam Veterans Memorial Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_Veterans_Memorial_Bridge_\(Richmond\)) carries [SR 895](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_State_Route_895) (Pocahontas Parkway) over I-95 and the [James River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_River) just south of the [Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Virginia) city limits. A [CSX Transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSX_Transportation) railroad line runs parallel to the northbound lane in the vicinity of the [Port of Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Richmond_\(Virginia\)), and the Commerce Road Industrial Area, a region that includes a [Philip Morris USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Morris_USA) office and an old bridge manufacturing plant.[^cite_note-8] As I-95 itself [crosses the James River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_River_Bridge_\(Interstate_95\)), [Richmond Main Street Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Main_Street_Station) can be seen on the north bank, and the road winds around the station itself.[^cite_note-9] The first interchange after it crosses the James River is the tolled [SR 195](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_State_Route_195) freeway and from there the road winds toward the concurrency with [I-64](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_64_in_Virginia). I-64/I-95 curves to the northwest to cross under [US 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_1_in_Virginia)/US 301, only to turn back north briefly, and curve northwest again, as it approaches [SR 161](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_State_Route_161). This pattern ends when I-64 turns west at the same interchange as the northern terminus of [I-195](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_195_\(Virginia\)). From here I-95 curves back to the northeast and has two interchanges with US 1, and later US 301 separately, the latter of which has separate carriageways on both side of I-95. Another interchange with I-295 exists in [Glen Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Allen,_Virginia). However, I-295 does not terminate there, and the south-to-eastbound and west-to-northbound offramps between the two can be accessed in the medians of both roads.

### Central Virginia to Washington, D.C.



View north along I-95 north of US 17 in Stafford County



Northbound I-95 at the [Springfield Interchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield_Interchange)

Throughout much of central Virginia, I-95 climbs a series of hills and contains wide tree-lined medians. Near [Doswell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doswell,_Virginia), the [SR 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_State_Route_30) interchange provides access to the [Kings Dominion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kings_Dominion) amusement park, with signs for the amusement park blended in with standard destination signs. [US 17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_17_in_Virginia) overlaps I-95 from [Massaponax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massaponax,_Virginia) at exit 126 north to [Falmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falmouth,_Virginia) at exit 133 as the highway passes west of the city of [Fredericksburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredericksburg,_Virginia). The bidirectional [high-occupancy toll lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_HOT_lanes) (HOT lane) begins at the northern US 17 interchange and runs through the center of I-95 through most of the rest of its journey toward [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) At exit 143 in [Aquia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquia,_Virginia), the northbound off- and onramps connect directly to US 1, even though the interchange is specifically for [SR 610](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_State_Route_610_\(Stafford_County\)).[^cite_note-10] Crossing over the [Chopawamsic Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chopawamsic_Creek) takes I-95 through [Marine Corps Base Quantico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_Corps_Base_Quantico), which includes restrictive interchanges.[^cite_note-11]



I-95 northbound at the SR 294 exit near Woodbridge

Further north in [Prince William County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_William_County,_Virginia), there are four rest areas; two for trucks in [Dumfries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumfries,_Virginia) and two for cars in [Dale City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_City,_Virginia). The truck rest areas, with [weigh stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weigh_station), are near exit 152. The car rest areas have uniquely positioned entrance and exit ramps. The southbound car rest area, near exit 156, is accessible only from the southbound [collector–distributor road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collector%E2%80%93distributor_road). In [Lorton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorton,_Virginia), a scissor interchange exists with US 1 and, shortly after this, [Lorton station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorton_station_\(Auto_Train\)) serving [Amtrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amtrak)'s *[Auto Train](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto_Train)* is located near exit 163. Due to public opposition of efforts to build I-95 through Washington, D.C. and [College Park, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Park,_Maryland), I-95 is diverted onto a concurrency with [I-495](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_495_\(Maryland%E2%80%93Virginia\)) (Capital Beltway) at the [Springfield Interchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield_Interchange), with the former alignment north of here becoming [I-395](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_395_\(Virginia%E2%80%93District_of_Columbia\)). I-95/I-495 continue east through [Franconia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franconia,_Virginia), over the [Washington Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Metro)'s [Blue Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Line_\(Washington_Metro\)) and [Rose Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose_Hill,_Fairfax_County,_Virginia). At [Huntington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntington,_Virginia), I-95/I-495 run under the Washington Metro's [Yellow Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_Line_\(Washington_Metro\)) and through [Alexandria](18940583.md) before crossing over the [Potomac River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potomac_River) on the [Woodrow Wilson Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson_Bridge) briefly into Washington, D.C. and then into [Maryland](18858.md).

### HOV facilities

Main article: [Virginia HOT lanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_HOT_lanes)



I-95 with reversible HOT lanes in northern Virginia

I-95 extends the twin-lane barrier-separated [high-occupancy toll lanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-occupancy_toll_lane) (HOV lanes) that begin on I-395 at the [14th Street bridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_Street_bridges) in Washington. These lanes have been extended south several times, most recently to US 17 near Fredericksburg.

As part of the [Quantico Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantico_Creek) bridge rebuilding project, a three-lane, 300-foot-long (91 m) bridge was constructed in the median just south of the old southern HOV terminus for use when the HOV facilities were extended. It was previously used as a detour bridge and retained its lane striping from such use.

In December 2014, this bridge became part of the Southern HOV/HOT lane extension project that now runs to just north of Garrisonville Road (exit 143) in [Stafford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stafford,_Virginia).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

The new I-95 HOV/HOT lanes project created approximately 29 miles (47 km) of HOV/HOT lanes on I-95 from Garrisonville Road in Stafford County to the vicinity of Edsall Road on I-395 in Fairfax County.[^cite_note-14]

In July 2016, VDOT began construction of an additional one-mile (1.6 km) extension of the HOV/HOT lanes on I-95 south of Garrisonville Road in Stafford County. This addition opened in November 2017.[^cite_note-15] On August 17, 2023, the HOV/HOT lanes were extended south from Garrisonville Road to US 17 near Fredericksburg; at the time, intermediate exits along the extension were not open.[^cite_note-16] The intermediate exits opened on December 7, 2023.[^cite_note-17]

## History

The 35-mile (56 km) [portion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond%E2%80%93Petersburg_Turnpike) between [Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petersburg,_Virginia) and [Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Virginia) opened on July 1, 1958.[^cite_note-jonas-19] It was opened as a toll road, with a \$0.70 toll to drive the entire distance of 35 miles (56 km).[^cite_note-jonas-19]

The portion between [Fredericksburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredericksburg,_Virginia) and [Quantico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantico,_Virginia) opened December 18, 1964.[^cite_note-20]

The 35-mile (56 km) portion between Fredericksburg and [Ashland, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashland,_Virginia) opened on July 16, 1964.[^cite_note-muse-21][^cite_note-22] At the time, [Route 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_1_in_Virginia) between the same two cities was the most dangerous stretch of highway in Virginia, and the opening of I-95 was anticipated to reduce the number of vehicular accidents in the area.[^cite_note-muse-21]

## Auxiliary routes

### Current



I-95/I-495 south coming off the Woodrow Wilson Bridge from Maryland



View south along I-95 north of SR 150 and SR 895 in [Bensley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bensley,_Virginia)

- [I-195](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_195_\(Virginia\)) is a short spur from north of [Downtown Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Richmond,_Virginia) south into downtown.

- [I-295](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_295_\(Virginia\)) is a bypass to the east of Richmond, from I-95 south of [Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petersburg,_Virginia), across [I-64](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_64_in_Virginia) east of Richmond and I-95 north of Richmond to I-64 west of Richmond.

- [I-395](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_395_\(Virginia-District_of_Columbia\)) is a branch from [Springfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield,_Virginia) north into [Downtown D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_\(Washington,_D.C.\)) It was part of I-95 until 1977.

- [I-495](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_Beltway) is the [Capital Beltway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_Beltway), a full loop around Washington, D.C. Since 1977, I-95 has run along its eastern half.

### Former

- I-95 Business was a former business loop of I-95 through [Emporia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emporia,_Virginia) between exits 8 and 12, running mostly along [US 301](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_301_in_Virginia).

- [I-595](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_1_in_Virginia#History) was a planned branch from I-395 south to [Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Reagan_Washington_National_Airport) along [US 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_1_in_Virginia).

- [I-795](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_295_\(Virginia\)) was a planned number for present I-95 from I-295 south of [Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petersburg,_Virginia) north to [I-85](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_85_in_Virginia) in Downtown Petersburg; I-95 would have bypassed Petersburg and [Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Virginia) to the east on I-295. The renumbering was never done because tolls were removed from the [Richmond–Petersburg Turnpike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond%E2%80%93Petersburg_Turnpike) section of I-95.

- [SR 895](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_State_Route_895) is the [Pocahontas Parkway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_State_Route_895), a connection from I-95 south of Richmond east to I-295. It was not numbered as an interstate because the project opened as a toll road using federal funds, thus disqualifying it from Interstate status.
