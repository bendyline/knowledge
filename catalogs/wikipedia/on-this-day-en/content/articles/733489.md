**Sololá** is a [department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Guatemala) in the west of [Guatemala](17238567.md). The capital is the city of [Sololá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solol%C3%A1). [Lake Atitlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Atitlan) is a key feature surrounded by a number of the municipalities.

## Population

As of 2018, the department had a population of 421,583.[^cite_note-population-1] The area is populated almost entirely by different Mayan ethnic groups, of which the two largest groups are the [Kaqchikel people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaqchikel_people) and [K'iche'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K'iche'_people).[^cite_note-Baily1-2][^cite_note-ine.gob.gt-3] [Kaqchikel people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaqchikel_people) accounted for 50.1%[^cite_note-ine.gob.gt-3] of the department's population, and [K'iche'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K'iche'_people) accounted for 35.3%.[^cite_note-ine.gob.gt-3] Indigenous people in total account for 96.5% of the department's population.[^cite_note-ine.gob.gt-3]

## Economy

With fertile [soil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soil), Sololá farmers produce [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), [vegetables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetable), and [fruit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit). Fruits include [apples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple), [cherries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherries), [cherimoya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherimoya), [peaches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peach), and [pears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pear). [Livestock](25160767.md) is also common, including sheep. Since 1850, the region has had a large [wool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wool) industry.[^cite_note-Baily1-2]

## Ecology

The area is [mountainous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain). In 1850, the [British](19097669.md) described it as having a "healthy" [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) "rather inclining to cold, or...temperate." Temperatures warm further south.[^cite_note-Baily1-2] [Lago de Atitlán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lago_de_Atitl%C3%A1n) is located in Sololá.

## Sports

[Saprissa de Guatemala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saprissa_de_Guatemala?action=edit\&redlink=1) is Solola's main football team and the most famous club in the department.
