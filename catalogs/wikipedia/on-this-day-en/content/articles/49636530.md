The **Ministry of the Interior** ([Spanish](26825.md): ***Ministerio de Gobernación*** or **MINGOB**) is a government [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministries_of_Guatemala) of [Guatemala](17238567.md), headquartered in Zone 1 of [Guatemala City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guatemala_City).[^cite_note-1]
