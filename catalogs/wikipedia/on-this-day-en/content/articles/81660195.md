# 2026 FIFA World Cup Group E

&#x20; Group E of the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) took place from June 14 to 25, 2026.[^cite_note-match_schedule-1] The group consisted of [Germany](250204.md), [Curaçao](24862626.md), [Ivory Coast](1039856.md), and [Ecuador](1016797.md).

Four-time world champions Germany finally secured their place in the [round of 32](81667005.md#Round_of_32) after failing to progress past the group stage in the last two tournaments, [2018](13327177.md) and [2022](17742072.md). Ivory Coast finished as runners-up, progressing to the knockout stage for the first time.

In an upset victory, Ecuador defeated Germany in their last group game, finishing third and advancing as one of the [eight best third-place teams](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams).[^cite_note-regulations-2]

However, the three teams performed poorly in the round of 32 and were eliminated from the World Cup. World Cup debutants Curaçao finished last with a single point and were eliminated.

### Germany vs Curaçao



[NRG Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NRG_Stadium) in [Houston](13774.md) during a Group E match between Germany and Curaçao

The two sides had never met before.

The fixture marked Curaçao's FIFA World Cup debut, making it the smallest country by population ever to qualify for the tournament. Moreover, [Livano Comenencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livano_Comenencia) scored Curaçao's first-ever goal in the tournament.

Germany's seven goals were the most they scored in a World Cup match since 2014, when they also defeated [Brazil](149286.md) [7–1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_v_Germany_\(2014_FIFA_World_Cup\)) in the semi-final.

## Discipline

The team conduct ("fair play") score would have been used as a tiebreaker if the head-to-head and overall records of teams were tied. It would also be used as a tiebreaker for the [third-place ranking](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams) between groups if the overall records of teams were tied. The score was calculated based on yellow and red cards received by players and team officials in all group matches as follows:[^cite_note-regulations-2]

- yellow card: −1 point;

- indirect red card (second yellow card): −3 points;

- direct red card: −4 points;

- yellow card and direct red card: −5 points;

Only one of the above deductions could be applied to a player or team official in a single match.
