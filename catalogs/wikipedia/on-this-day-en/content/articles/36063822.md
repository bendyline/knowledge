The provinces of Central Iran

**Central Iran** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): ایران مرکزی) consists of the southern slopes of the [Alborz Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alborz) in the north, the [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains) in south, the [Central Iranian Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Iranian_Range), and the desert of [Dasht-e Kavir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dasht-e_Kavir).

It includes the provinces of [Isfahan](448061.md), [Yazd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yazd_province), [Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari](448057.md), [Markazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markazi_province), [Qazvin](448087.md), [Alborz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alborz_province), [Tehran](202698.md), [Qom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qom_province), and [Semnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semnan_province).&#x20;

Its major cities are [Tehran](57654.md), [Isfahan](223149.md), [Arak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arak,_Iran), [Yazd](451385.md), [Karaj](978698.md), [Qazvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qazvin), [Qom](638742.md), [Kashan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashan), [Saveh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saveh), and [Shahr-e Kord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahr-e_Kord).

## Climate

- **[Hot desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BWh)** in the deserts.

- **[Cold desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BWk)** in the central mountains.

- **[Humid continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate)** on the few rivers.

- **[cold semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSk)** in the high mountains.
