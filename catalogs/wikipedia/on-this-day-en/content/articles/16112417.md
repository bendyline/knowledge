A [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commune_\(administrative_division\)) is the third-level administrative unit in [Mali](19127.md). Mali is divided into ten [regions](690325.md) and one capital district ([Bamako](56606.md)). These subdivisions bear the name of their principal city. The regions are divided into 56 [cercles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cercles_of_Mali). The cercles and the district are divided into 703 communes, with 36 urban communes and 667 rural communes,[^cite_note-1] while some larger cercles still contain [arrondissements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrondissements_of_Mali) above the commune level, these are organisational areas with no independent power or office. Rural communes are subdivided into villages, while urban communes are subdivided into *quartier* (wards or quarters). Communes usually bear the name of their principal town. The capital, [Bamako](56606.md), consists of six urban communes. There were initially 701 communes until Law *No. 01-043* of 7 June 2001 created two new rural communes in the desert region in the north east of the country: [Alata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alata,_Mali), [Ménaka Cercle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A9naka_Cercle) in the [Gao Region](2349862.md) and [Intadjedite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intadjedite), [Tin-Essako Cercle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tin-Essako_Cercle) in the [Kidal Region](2214311.md).[^cite_note-2]

Not every built up area (which might be described as a town) is a commune, and not every commune (especially rural communes) contains a large town. In most cases where towns and communes coincide, commune borders extend beyond built up areas and are, like the [communes of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) on which they were based during the colonial period, an administrative structure. Unlike French communes, they are not the lowest level administrative structure of the nation.&#x20;

Legally, the commune structure was created by Law *No. 96-059/AN-RM of 4 November 1996*. The communes generally retain the same boundaries as the former [arrondissements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrondissements_of_Mali). Commune affairs are directed by a commune council (*conseil communal*) of elected members and a commune executive (*bureau communal*) of the elected mayor and three adjutants. The executive is tasked with carrying out the directives voted by the council. National policies are carried out by a sub-prefect (*sous préfet*), who also carries out certain of the council's directives over the local arms or national bodies.[^cite_note-PROMISAM-3]
