**Chaghi District** ([Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language): چاگۓ دمگ; [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضلع چاغی), also known as Chaghi District, is the largest [district](639402.md) of Pakistan by area, located in the northwestern corner of the [Balochistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochistan_\(Pakistan\)) province of [Pakistan](23235.md).[^cite_note-NRB-2] The district shares its border with two countries: [Afghanistan](737.md) and [Iran](14653.md).

## Tehsils of Chagai District

With its headquarters at **Dalbandin**, Chagai District retains the following central and eastern tehsils:

- **Dalbandin Tehsil**

- **Chagai Tehsil**

- **Amuri Tehsil**

- **Chilgazi Tehsil**

- **Dak Tehsil**

### Population

As of the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), Chagai district has 38,213 households and a population of 269,192. The district has a sex ratio of 108.24 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 33.15%: 41.90% for males and 23.57% for females respectively. [^cite_note-2023table1-4][^cite_note-2023table12-5] 101,423 (37.76% of the surveyed population) are under 10 years of age.[^cite_note-2023table5-6] 20,054 (7.45%) live in urban areas.[^cite_note-2023table1-4]

### Language

Languages of Chagai district (2023)

1. [Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language) (73.8%)

2. [Brahui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahui_language) (16.8%)

3. [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) (8.93%)

4. Others (0.51%)

At the time of the 2023 census, 73.77% of the population spoke [Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language), 16.79% [Brahui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahui_language) and 8.93% [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) as their first language.[^cite_note-13]

## Education

According to Pakistan District Education Rankings, a report by [Alif Ailaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alif_Ailaan), Chaghai is ranked at number 91 nationally, with an education score of 52.06. The learning score is 55.58 and gender parity is 60.11.

The national rank according to readiness is 91, with a readiness score of 55.58 and gender parity of 60.11. The school infrastructure rank of District Chaghai is 114 nationally, with a severe shortage of electricity with a score of 4.55 and building condition score of 12.27. Lack of schools for girls is the main issue of the region.[^cite_note-16]
