**Tan-Tan** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): طانطان, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Ṭānṭān*; [Berber languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berber_languages): ⵟⴰⵏⵟⴰⵏ, romanized: *Ṭanṭan*) is a city in [Tan-Tan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tan-Tan_Province) in the region of [Guelmim-Oued Noun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guelmim-Oued_Noun) in southwestern [Morocco](19291.md). It is a [desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert) town with a population (2014 census) of 73,209.[^cite_note-1] It is the largest city in the province and second largest city in the region after the capital [Guelmim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guelmim).[^cite_note-Hcp.ma-2] It is located on the banks of the [wadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadi) Oued Ben Khelil, which flows into the [Draa River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draa_River) 15 kilometres (9.3 mi) north of the town. The Draa River at 1,100 kilometres (684 mi) is the longest in Morocco and flows into the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md) soon after the confluence with the wadi. The town also has an airport, [Tan Tan Plage Blanche Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tan_Tan_Airport).

## Etymology

The popular origin of the name is an example of [onomatopoeia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onomatopoeia). The name comes from the well called *Ḥāṣī Ṭānṭān* literally "well of Tan-Tan", which representatives of most [Sahrawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahrawis) tribes like the [Reguibat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reguibat), [Laaroussien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laaroussien) and the Ait Lahcin of the [Tekna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tekna) would camp around. The well is called this because when buckets were lowered into the water into it would make the sound *ṭān-ṬĀN* when they hit it.[^cite_note-3]

## History



Sahrawi tribal men performing fantasia at the Tan-Tan (Moussem) Festival in Tan-Tan, Morocco

The [quartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartz) figurine [Venus of Tan-Tan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus_of_Tan-Tan) was found in a river terrace deposit on the north bank of the [Draa River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draa_River). Dated between 200,000 and 500,000 BCE, it is considered one of the oldest human-form sculptures in the world, although its formation may actually be natural.[^cite_note-4]

## Port

The nearby port, known as Tan-Tan Plage in [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language); Port of Tan-Tan in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language); and [El Ouatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Ouatia), or الوطية in [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language) is about 25 kilometres (16 mi) west of Tan-Tan on the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md). With a population in 2004 of 6,294[^cite_note-Hcp.ma-2] it is the second-largest settlement in the province and ninth-largest in the region. Both Tan-Tan and Tan-Tan Plage are on Morocco's main highway, the N1.

## Climate

Tan-Tan has a [hot desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_desert_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BWh*). While usually strongly moderated by the cold offshore [Canary Current](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canary_Current), occasional easterly winds from the [Sahara Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahara_Desert) interior bring exceptionally hot temperatures to the maritime areas of southern Morocco. These winds being frequent in autumn and [seasonal lag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seasonal_lag) from warming sea temperatures cause October afternoons to average warmer than the July counterparts. The varying timing of the hot inland winds causes a vast difference between the individual monthly mean maxima and the many months in which brief temperatures above 40 °C (104 °F) may occur. Even the coldest winter nights remain well above freezing due to the [Atlantic](698.md) influence.
