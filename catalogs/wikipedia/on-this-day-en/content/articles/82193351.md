The **2026 World Figure Skating Championships** were held from 25 to 29 March at the [O2 Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O2_Arena_\(Prague\)) in [Prague, Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague,_Czech_Republic). Sanctioned by the [International Skating Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Skating_Union) (ISU), the [World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Figure_Skating_Championships) are considered the most prestigious event in figure skating. Medals were awarded in [men's singles, women's singles](7291274.md), [pair skating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pair_skating), and [ice dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_dance). [Ilia Malinin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilia_Malinin) of the United States won the men's event, [Kaori Sakamoto](45601667.md) of Japan won the women's event, [Minerva Fabienne Hase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minerva_Fabienne_Hase) and [Nikita Volodin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Volodin) of Germany won the pairs event, and [Laurence Fournier Beaudry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Fournier_Beaudry) and [Guillaume Cizeron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillaume_Cizeron) of France won the ice dance event.

## Required performance elements

### Single skating

Women competing in [single skating](7291274.md) performed their [short programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_program_\(figure_skating\)) on 25 March, while men performed theirs on 26 March.[^cite_note-Announcement-2] Lasting 2 minutes 40 seconds (+/− 10 seconds),[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202482-21] the short program had to include the following elements:

**For men:** one [double or triple Axel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axel_jump); one [triple or quadruple jump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_jumps); one [jump combination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_jumps) consisting of a double jump and a triple jump, two triple jumps, or a quadruple jump and a double jump or triple jump; one [flying spin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_spins#Flying_spins); one [camel spin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camel_spin) or [sit spin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sit_spin) with a change of foot; one [spin combination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_spins#Spin_combinations) with a change of foot; and a [step sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Step_sequence) using the full ice surface.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID2024106-22]

**For women:** one double or triple Axel; one triple jump; one jump combination consisting of a double jump and a triple jump, or two triple jumps; one flying spin; one [layback spin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Layback_spin), [sideways leaning spin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upright_spin), camel spin, or sit spin without a change of foot; one spin combination with a change of foot; and one step sequence using the full ice surface.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID2024106-22]

Women performed their [free skates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_skating) on 27 March, while men performed theirs on 28 March.[^cite_note-Announcement-2] Lasting 4 minutes (+/− 10 seconds),[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202482-21] the free skate for both men and women had to include the following: seven jump elements, of which one had to be an Axel-type jump; three spins, of which one had to be a spin combination, one a flying spin, and one a spin with only one position; a step sequence; and a [choreographic sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choreographic_sequence).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID2024110–111-23]

### Pairs

Couples competing in [pair skating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pair_skating) performed their short programs on 25 March.[^cite_note-Announcement-2] Lasting 2 minutes 40 seconds (+/− 10 seconds),[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202482-21] the short program had to include the following elements: one [pair lift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_lifts#Pair_lifts), one [double or triple twist lift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_lifts#Twist_lifts), one [double or triple throw jump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pair_skating#Throw_jumps), one [double or triple solo jump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pair_skating#Solo_jumps), one [solo spin combination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_spins#Solo_spin_combinations) with a change of foot, one [death spiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_spiral_\(figure_skating\)), and a step sequence using the full ice surface.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID2024119-24]

Couples performed their free skates on 25 March.[^cite_note-Announcement-2] Lasting 4 minutes (+/− 10 seconds),[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202482-21] the free skate had to include the following: three pair lifts, of which one had to be a twist lift; two different throw jumps; one solo jump; one jump combination or sequence; one [pair spin combination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_spins#Pair_spin_combinations); one death spiral; and a choreographic sequence.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID2024122-25]

### Ice dance

Couples competing in [ice dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_dance) performed their [rhythm dances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhythm_dance) on 27 March.[^cite_note-Announcement-2] Lasting 2 minutes 40 seconds (+/− 10 seconds),[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202482-21] the theme of the rhythm dance this season was "music, dance styles, and feeling of the 1990s".[^cite_note-ISU-2704-26] Examples of applicable dance styles and music included [pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music), [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_music), [house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_music), [techno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Techno), [hip-hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip-hop), and [grunge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grunge). The rhythm dance had to include the following elements: one [pattern dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compulsory_dance) step sequence, one [choreographic rhythm sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competition_elements_in_ice_dance#Choreographic_elements), one [dance lift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competition_elements_in_ice_dance#Dance_lifts), one set of [sequential twizzles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competition_elements_in_ice_dance#Turn_sequences), and one [step sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competition_elements_in_ice_dance#Step_sequences).[^cite_note-ISU-2704-26]

Couples then performed their [free dances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_dance_\(ice_dance\)) on 28 March.[^cite_note-Announcement-2] Lasting 4 minutes (+/− 10 seconds),[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202482-21] the free dance had to include the following: three dance lifts, one [dance spin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competition_elements_in_ice_dance#Dance_spins), one set of [synchronized twizzles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competition_elements_in_ice_dance#Turn_sequences), one step sequence in hold, one step sequence while on one skate and not touching, and three [choreographic elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competition_elements_in_ice_dance).[^cite_note-ISU-2704-26]

## Judging

See also: [ISU Judging System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Judging_System)

Skaters were judged according to the required technical elements of their program (such as [jumps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_jumps) and [spins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_spins)), as well as the overall presentation of their program, based on three program components (skating skills, presentation, and composition). Each technical element in a figure skating performance was assigned a predetermined base point value and scored by a panel of nine judges on a scale from −5 to +5 based on the quality of its execution.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202432,_83–84-27] Each Grade of Execution (GOE) from –5 to +5 was assigned a value as indicated on the Scale of Values.[^cite_note-ISU-2707-28][^cite_note-ISU-2705-29] For example, a [triple Axel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axel_jump) was worth a base value of 8.00 points, and a GOE of +3 was worth 2.40 points, so a triple Axel with a GOE of +3 earned 10.40 points.[^cite_note-ISU-2707-28] The judging panel's GOE for each element was determined by calculating the [trimmed mean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean#Truncated_mean) (the average after discarding the highest and lowest scores). The panel's scores for all elements were added together to generate a [Total Elements Score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Judging_System#Total_Elements_Score_\(TES\)).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202415–16-30] At the same time, the judges evaluated each performance based on the five aforementioned program components and assigned each a score from 0.25 to 10 in 0.25-point increments.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202484–85-31] The judging panel's final score for each program component was also determined by calculating the trimmed mean. Those scores were then multiplied by the factor shown on the chart below; the results were added together to generate a total [Program Component Score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISU_Judging_System#Program_Component_Score_\(PCS\)).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202416–17-32]

| --- | --- | --- |
| Men | 1.67 | 3.33 |
| Women | 1.33 | 2.67 |
| Pairs | 1.33 | 2.67 |
| Ice dance | 1.33 | 2.00 |

Deductions were applied for certain violations, such as time infractions, stops and restarts, or falls.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202418–19-34] The Total Elements Score and Program Component Score were then added together, minus any deductions, to generate a final performance score for each skater or team.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTES\&P/ID202420-35]
