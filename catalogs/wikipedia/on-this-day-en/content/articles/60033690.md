The **Port of Ceuta** is a passenger and cargo port located on the [North African](21714.md) coast, in the [Strait of Gibraltar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Gibraltar), belonging to the Spanish autonomous city of [Ceuta](6443.md).

## Description

Finished in 1942, it reached its pinnacle in activity during the time of the [Spanish protectorate in Morocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_protectorate_in_Morocco), as it served the inland area, declining after the independence of Morocco in 1956.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMoreno_NavarroVentura_Fernández2008355–365-3] It is managed by the port authority of the same name.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECouceiro_MartínezSerrano_PérezMagro_Andrade201312-4] The contemporary activity of the port is centered in the obtention of provisions by the local market, in vessel supplies, the [ferry](50771.md) service linking to ports in the [Iberian Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberian_Peninsula) and its role as centre for providing logistic and industrial services.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMoreno_NavarroVentura_Fernández2008355–365-3] Despite the closed customs with Morocco the port also has a role for the entry of products to Morocco, through the means of closeted [smuggling](28645.md) by informal "porters" crossing the border with personal luggage.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMoreno_NavarroVentura_Fernández2008355–365-3] Most of the cargo shipping is [ro-ro](1457484.md).

[Spanish National Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_National_Police) agents (with the collaboration of [Frontex](3072700.md) agents) are deployed in the port for [border control](247927.md) tasks.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-7]
