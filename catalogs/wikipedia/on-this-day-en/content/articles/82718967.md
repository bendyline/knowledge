The **2026 Billie Jean King Cup** is the 63rd edition of the [Billie Jean King Cup](1108893.md), a tournament between national teams in women's tennis. \
&#x20;[Czech Republic](10829679.md) defeated [Ukraine](11233539.md) 2-0 in the final and won their twelfth title. [Italy](11233225.md) was the two-time defending champion, but lost to Ukraine in the semifinals. \


[Ivory Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory_Coast_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_team?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Zambia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambia_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_team?action=edit\&redlink=1) made their first appearances in the tournament.

### Qualifying round

Main article: [2026 Billie Jean King Cup qualifying round](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_qualifying_round)

*Date: 10–11 April 2026*

Fourteen teams are playing for seven spots in the Finals, in series decided on a home and away basis. This year Billie Jean King Cup qualifying returns to home and away basis, with doubles rubber moved to the third game, coinciding with Davis Cup.

These fourteen teams were:

- 7 teams of the [2025 Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_finals), except the hosts,  [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_team).

- 7 winning teams from the [2025 play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_play-offs).

The seven winning teams from the qualifying round will play at the finals, and the seven losing teams will play at the [2026 play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_play-offs?action=edit\&redlink=1).
