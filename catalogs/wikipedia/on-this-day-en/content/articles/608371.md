The **Government of Serbia** ([Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): Влада Србије, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Serbian): *Vlada Srbije*), formally the **Government of the Republic of Serbia** ([Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): Влада Републике Србије, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Serbian): *Vlada Republike Srbije*), commonly abbreviated to **Serbian Government** ([Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): српска Влада, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Serbian): *srpska Vlada*), is the main [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) of government in [Serbia](29265.md).&#x20;

The Government is led by the [President of the Government](606320.md) ([Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): Председник Владе, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Serbian): *Predsednik Vlade*), informally abbreviated to [premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier) ([Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): Премијер, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Serbian): *Premijer*) or [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister). The prime minister is nominated by the [president of the Republic](774382.md) from among those candidates who enjoy [majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majority) support in the [National Assembly](619753.md); the candidate is then chosen by the Assembly. There are 30 other government members, serving as [deputy prime ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_prime_minister), government ministers or both; they are chosen by the prime minister and confirmed by the National Assembly. The [current government](79706722.md) is led by Prime Minister [Đuro Macut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%90uro_Macut).

The affairs of government are decided by the Cabinet of Ministers, which is the main executive body of the Government. It is composed of the prime minister and [ministers](739237.md) and meets weekly at the [Government Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Building,_Belgrade) in Belgrade, which houses the Office of the Prime Minister as well as the Secretariat-General of the Government.

## Powers

The Government is the main executive power of the Serbian state. According to the [Constitution of Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Serbia), the Government:

- determines and guides internal and foreign policies;

- executes laws adopted by the National Assembly;

- adopts regulations and other acts for the purpose of executing laws;

- proposes legislation to the National Assembly

- directs and coordinates the work of state administration and supervises its work

## Composition

The government is headed by the prime minister. The prime minister has [deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_prime_minister) (appointed by the prime minister with the approval of the National Assembly by absolute majority), currently four, who also serve as government ministers; the first [first deputy prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Serbia) also discharges the duties of the prime minister when the latter is incapacitated or absent. There are other [ministers](739237.md) ([Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): министри, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Serbian): *ministri*), who are appointed by the prime minister with the approval of the National Assembly by absolute majority. The ministers, currently 25, head individual ministries charged with particular sectors of activities, with the exception of [ministers without portfolio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_without_portfolio_\(Serbia\)), currently five, who are not at the head of ministries. [State secretaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Secretary) ([Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): државни секретари, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Serbian): *državni sekretari*), appointed by the Government for the term of the minister, are the highest officials below minister, responsible to the minister; there are one or more state secretaries in the ministries.

Besides the government ministries, there are numerous interministerial bodies that operate within the framework of the government and are directly subordinated to the Office of Prime Minister:[^cite_note-1]

- [Secretariat-General of the Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretariat-General_of_the_Government_\(Serbia\))

- Secretariat for Legislation

- Secretariat for Public Policies

- [Office of the National Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Council_\(Serbia\))

- [Office for Kosovo and Metohija](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_for_Kosovo_and_Metohija)

- Office for Media Relations

- Office for E-government

- Office for Dual Education

- Office for Public and Cultural Diplomacy

- Office of the Council for Cooperation with the Russian Federation and the People's Republic of China

- [Government Human Resources Management Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Human_Resources_Management_Service_\(Serbia\))

- [Government Aviation Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Aviation_Service_\(Serbia\))

- Directorate for Joint Affairs
