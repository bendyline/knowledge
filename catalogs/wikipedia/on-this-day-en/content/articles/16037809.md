**Cléon** (French pronunciation: [\[kleɔ̃\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French)) is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in the [Seine-Maritime](85874.md) [department](38523.md) in the [Normandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normandy_\(administrative_region\)) [region](58846.md) in northern [France](5843419.md).

This city is known for its [Renault](162292.md) factory, which manufactures engines and gearboxes.

Two Renault engines are named after the city, the [Cléon-Fonte engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_Cl%C3%A9on-Fonte_engine) and the [Cléon-Alu Engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_Cl%C3%A9on-Alu_engine). For collectors of vintage cars, the name "Cléon" refers primarily to these two engines.

## Geography

A small town situated inside a [meander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meander) of the river [Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine) some 11 miles (18 km) south of [Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouen), at the junction of the D7 and the D144 roads. The French car manufacturer [Renault](162292.md) has its principal engine and gearbox factory within the commune's territory, covering an area of 135 hectares (330 acres).
