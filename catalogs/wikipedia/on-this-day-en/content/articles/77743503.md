The **2025 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix** (officially known as the **Formula 1 Etihad Airways Abu Dhabi Grand Prix 2025**) was a [Formula One](10854.md) motor race that was held on 7 December 2025 at the [Yas Marina Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yas_Marina_Circuit) in [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md), [United Arab Emirates](69328.md). It was the twenty-fourth and final race of the [2025 Formula One World Championship](72768531.md).

The Grand Prix was the first [World Drivers' Championship](3437295.md)–deciding final round since [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Abu_Dhabi_Grand_Prix), and the first with more than two contenders since [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Abu_Dhabi_Grand_Prix). [Lando Norris](48978813.md) led the championship with 408 points, ahead of [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) with 396 points, and teammate [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) with 392 points. The three drivers entered the round with an opportunity to win the World Drivers' Championship. Verstappen converted his [pole position](630713.md) to a victory, ahead of Piastri and Norris. Third place was enough for Norris to become World Champion, ending the season two points ahead of Verstappen and 13 ahead of Piastri.

## Background

The event was held at the [Yas Marina Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yas_Marina_Circuit) in [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md) for the 17th time in the circuit's history, across the weekend of 5–7 December.[^cite_note-3] The Grand Prix was the twenty-fourth and final race of the [2025 Formula One World Championship](72768531.md) and the 17th running of the [Abu Dhabi Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Dhabi_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-4]

This was the last Grand Prix to utilise the [power unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_engines) configuration [introduced in 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Formula_One_World_Championship#Technical_regulations). A revised configuration without the Motor Generator Unit-Heat (MGU-H), but with a higher power output from the Motor Generator Unit-Kinetic (MGU-K), will be introduced for [2026](71681684.md).[^cite_note-5] The event marked the final race of the [ground-effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_effect_\(cars\)) generation of cars introduced in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Formula_One_World_Championship), and the last of the [drag reduction system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_reduction_system) (DRS) introduced as an overtaking aid in [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Formula_One_World_Championship), as cars with active aerodynamics and moveable wings will be introduced in 2026.[^cite_note-6] This race marked [Honda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_in_Formula_One)'s last race as a power unit supplier to [Red Bull](1173193.md) and [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls), with the company exclusively supplying [Aston Martin](63473867.md) in 2026.[^cite_note-7] It was also [Renault's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_in_Formula_One) final race as an engine supplier for its team [Alpine](65240620.md), with the manufacturer planning to discontinue engine production post-2025; as such Alpine will become a customer of [Mercedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_AMG_High_Performance_Powertrains) engines from 2026.[^cite_note-8]

### Championship standings before the race

Going into the weekend, [Lando Norris](48978813.md) led the [Drivers' Championship](3437295.md) with 408 points, 12 points ahead of [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) and 16 ahead of his teammate [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md), who were second and third, respectively. [McLaren](20994.md), who won the [Constructors' Championship](3437393.md) at the [Singapore Grand Prix](77743466.md), leads with 800 points ahead of [Mercedes](25098794.md) and [Red Bull Racing](1173193.md), who were second and third with 459 and 426 points, respectively.[^cite_note-9]

#### Championship permutations

With Norris being outscored by Verstappen and Piastri in the preceding [Qatar Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Qatar_Grand_Prix), he was unable to secure the Drivers' Championship. As such, it was decided in this race, marking the first time the final round of the season was a championship decider since [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Formula_One_World_Championship), and also the first time more than two drivers were contenders for the title at the last event since [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Formula_One_World_Championship). Norris and Piastri both had an opportunity to win their first title at this event, and [McLaren](20994.md)'s first Drivers' Championship since [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Formula_One_World_Championship), while Verstappen had an opportunity to win his fifth in a row.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-\:0-11]

Norris had an opportunity to become [Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_drivers_from_the_United_Kingdom)'s first Drivers' Champion since [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) won in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Formula_One_World_Championship) for [Mercedes](25098794.md);[^cite_note-12] Verstappen could break [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel)'s record for the most championships won with [Red Bull](1173193.md), and would have become the second driver to win five titles in a row after [Michael Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Schumacher) from [2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Formula_One_World_Championship) to [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Formula_One_World_Championship) for [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari);[^cite_note-13] and Piastri could have become [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_drivers_from_Australia)'s first Drivers' Champion since [Alan Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Jones_\(racing_driver\)) won in [1980](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980_Formula_One_season) for [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing).[^cite_note-\:0-11]

## Practice

Three [free practice sessions](645533.md#Free_practice) were held for the event. The first free practice session was held on 5 December 2025, at 13\:30 local time ([UTC+4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+04\:00)), and was topped by [Lando Norris](48978813.md), followed by [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) and [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc).[^cite_note-Abu_Dhabi-1] The second free practice session was held on the same day, at 17\:00 local time, and was topped by Norris, followed by Verstappen and [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)).[^cite_note-Abu_Dhabi-1] The third practice session was held on 6 December 2025, at 14\:30 local time, and was topped by Russell, followed by Norris and Verstappen. The session was red-flagged after [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) crashed at turn 9.[^cite_note-Abu_Dhabi-1]

## Qualifying

[Qualifying](645533.md#Current_format) was held on 6 December 2025, at 18\:00 local time ([UTC+4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+04\:00)), and determined the starting grid order for the race.[^cite_note-Abu_Dhabi-1]

### Qualifying report

[Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton), [Alexander Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Albon), [Nico Hülkenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_H%C3%BClkenberg), [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md), and [Franco Colapinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Colapinto) were eliminated in Q1.[^cite_note-30] [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman), [Carlos Sainz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.), [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson), [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md), and [Lance Stroll](42274711.md) exited in Q2. In the third session (Q3), [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) claimed his 48th career pole position, ahead of title contenders [Lando Norris](48978813.md), who qualified in second, and [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) in third. [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) and [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) qualified in fourth and fifth, respectively, and [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso), [Gabriel Bortoleto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto), [Esteban Ocon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Ocon), [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar), and [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda) completed the top ten.[^cite_note-Starting_grid-31] Tsunoda, who suffered floor damage from a free practice incident with Kimi Antonelli, sacrificed his flying lap in Q3 to provide a tow for teammate Verstappen.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33]

## Race

The race was held on 7 December 2025, at 17\:00 local time ([UTC+4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+04\:00)), and was run for 58 laps.[^cite_note-Abu_Dhabi-1]

### Race report

At the start of the race, [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) cut to the inside to block any challenge from [Lando Norris](48978813.md). Norris tucked into second place ahead of [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) as the three Championship contenders made it safely through turn one. Piastri then overtook Norris for second place in the opening lap.[^cite_note-F1Report-35][^cite_note-AutosportLive-36]

Behind them, fourth-placed [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) made a poor start in the [Mercedes](25098794.md), and he was overtaken by both [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) and [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso). Russell was left in sixth place ahead of [Sauber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauber_Motorsport)'s [Gabriel Bortoleto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto), [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls)' [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar), [Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team)' [Esteban Ocon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Ocon) and hard-tyre starter [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda), who all maintained their starting positions before Piastri overtook Norris while on hard tyres to reach second place. Russell overtook Alonso for fifth.[^cite_note-F1Report-35][^cite_note-AutosportLive-36]

On lap 11, Verstappen had built out a two-second gap ahead of Piastri, with Norris 4.3 seconds behind. Russell was the first of the top five to pit at the end of lap 14, switching to hard tyres to try to [undercut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undercut_\(motorsport\)) Leclerc. Norris was next to pit at the end of lap 16 followed by Leclerc. After exiting the pits, Norris was tailing [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) and [Carlos Sainz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.), and on lap 19 he passed both [Lance Stroll](42274711.md) and [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) in a single move into turn 5.[^cite_note-F1Report-35][^cite_note-AutosportLive-36]

Norris was now three seconds behind Tsunoda, the latter of whom was in third place behind Piastri and Verstappen. On lap 23, as Norris and Tsunoda battled on the straight between turns 5 and 6, Norris left the track in an attempt to overtake Tsunoda, who was making multiple changes to defend his position. Both drivers were put under investigation: Tsunoda for making more than one change of direction and Norris for leaving the track and gaining an advantage. The FIA stewards later penalised Tsunoda for making more than one change of direction as Norris, whom the stewards decided not to investigate any further, was forced off the track to avoid a collision from Tsunoda's erratic driving. Tsunoda would later serve his five-second penalty at the pits by the end of lap 32, demoting him down to 18th.[^cite_note-F1Report-35][^cite_note-AutosportLive-36]

Meanwhile, Piastri's hard tyres were starting to show signs of wear, requiring him to pit for mediums a few laps later, eventually rejoining the track in second place.[^cite_note-F1Report-35][^cite_note-AutosportLive-36]

Norris, on hard tyres, would face pressure from Leclerc but Leclerc was unable to get within two seconds of Norris. Verstappen would win the race, with Piastri finishing in second and Norris in third. Norris' third-place finish was enough to crown him his maiden World Drivers' Championship, while Verstappen's win would allow Red Bull to secure third place in the Constructors' Championship.[^cite_note-37] [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton)'s eighth place finish meant for the first time in his 19-year career he would go an entire season without recording a Grand Prix podium, ending a record breaking streak since his career began in [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Formula_One_World_Championship).[^cite_note-38]
