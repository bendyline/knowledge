**Ute Iris Stalzer** (born 1968) is a German politician of the [Social Democratic Party](60585.md) (SPD). She is the mayor of [Herdecke](1159548.md) in the south-eastern [Ruhr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruhr) region.&#x20;

## Biography

Stalzer grew up in [Herdecke](1159548.md) as the daughter of a steelworker. She studied law and works as a specialist lawyer for labor law in Herdecke.[^cite_note-1]&#x20;

Until 2020, Stalzer was a member of the Herdecke city council, for [Alliance 90/The Greens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_90/The_Greens) until she left the party at the end of 2014.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]&#x20;

She later joined the SPD. In the [2025 local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_North_Rhine-Westphalia_local_elections), Stalzer was elected mayor of Herdecke. She prevailed in the [runoff election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runoff_election) on 28 September 2025, with 52.2% (first round: 42.5%) of the vote against Fabian Haas (CDU; first round: 39.2%). The planned inauguration date was 1 November 2025.[^cite_note-4] The swearing-in ceremony took place on 4 November.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]&#x20;

Stalzer is married and has two adopted children, a girl from [Mali](19127.md) born 2007/8 and a boy from [Haiti](13373.md) born 2009/10.[^cite_note-RTL_Mali_Haiti-7][^cite_note-Spiegel-8][^cite_note-DW_names_daughter-9]&#x20;

### Stabbing incident

On 7 October 2025, Stalzer was stabbed thirteen times and critically injured.[^cite_note-Spiegel-8][^cite_note-BBC_critically_injured-10] Her adopted son told police she had been attacked by several men outside the house.[^cite_note-Times_Moody-11] The boy was initially arrested,[^cite_note-12] however Stalzer later identified her daughter as the attacker.[^cite_note-DW_names_daughter-9] Stalzer was tortured for hours and also suffered burns and skull fractures.[^cite_note-TVP_tortured-13] The knife was found in the son's backpack. Stalzer was found in the living room, however a substantial amount of blood had been cleaned up in the cellar before emergency services were called.[^cite_note-TVP_tortured-13][^cite_note-DW_names_daughter-9]&#x20;

One day before the attack, Stalzer contacted the police twice,[^cite_note-14] because she felt threatened by her adopted daughter.[^cite_note-15] The prosecutor is treating the case as "[dangerous bodily harm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strafgesetzbuch)" (the German equivalent of [aggravated assault](1466.md) or [grievous bodily harm](1239484.md)) rather than attempted murder, due to the daughter's decision to call the emergency services.[^cite_note-TVP_tortured-13][^cite_note-DW_names_daughter-9] Both children have been placed in care.[^cite_note-TVP_tortured-13][^cite_note-DW_names_daughter-9] Stalzer has since recovered and been sworn into office.&#x20;
