# Murder in French law

In the [French penal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_penal_code), [murder](20171.md) is defined by the intentional killing of another person. Murder is punishable by[^cite_note-Murder_French_Civil_Law-1] a maximum of 30 years of criminal [imprisonment](213806.md) (no more than 20 years if the [defendant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defendant) is not sentenced to 30 years).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] [Assassination](2963.md) (murder with [premeditation](2348828.md) or after lying in wait for the victim)[^cite_note-Assassination_French_Civil_Law-4] and murder in some special cases in accordance with Article 221-4[^cite_note-5] (including if the victim is a child under 15, against vulnerable people due to age, health etc, in the context of domestic violence, against some professionals in connection with their duty, in a gang etc) )[^cite_note-Special_case_in_France-6] are punished by a [jail](19008450.md) term up to [life imprisonment](148681.md) (no more than 30 years if the defendant is not sentenced to life).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The same punishment is given to murder committed in connection to other criminal offenses according to Article 221-2.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

Except for [recidivists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recidivist),[^cite_note-Recidive_Law_France-12] the minimum [sentence](870351.md) in [criminal prosecutions](440936.md) is one or two years' imprisonment, which may be suspended if the sentence is under 5 years.[^cite_note-suspended_sentence_France-13] Acts of violence causing an unintended death (Article 222-7 of the Penal Code -*Les violences ayant entraîné la mort sans intention de la donner*)[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] are punished by 15 years' imprisonment, or 20 years if [aggravating circumstances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aggravation_\(law\)) exist[^cite_note-18] (which are the same as those that would make a murderer eligible for life in prison).[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]
