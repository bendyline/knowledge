The **Embassy of Russia in Vienna** is the [diplomatic mission](8970.md) of the [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation) to the [Republic of Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Austria). The [chancery](8970.md) is located at Reisnerstraße 45–47 in the [Landstraße](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landstra%C3%9Fe) [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Vienna) of [Vienna](55866.md).[^cite_note-1]

## History of the chancery

The building which is now the embassy [chancery](8970.md) was built in 1872–73 based upon the designs of [architect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architect) Alois Wurm-Arnkreuz in [Viennese](55866.md) [Neo-Renaissance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Renaissance) style. The [façade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fa%C3%A7ade) of the building features an open [balcony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balcony) and a [balustrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balustrade). The [colonnade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonnade), stairs and interior are made of [granite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granite) and [marble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble).[^cite_note-embgerman-2]

In 1874 the [palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palace) was handed over to the deposed [Duke of Nassau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_of_Nassau), [Adolphe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolphe,_Grand_Duke_of_Luxembourg), who later became the [Grand Duke of Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Luxembourg). In 1891 the building was acquired by [Prince Aleksey Lobanov-Rostovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksey_Lobanov-Rostovsky), who was the [ambassador](68651.md) of the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) in Vienna at the time, for use by the Russian mission. The palace later housed diplomatic representatives of the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union).[^cite_note-embgerman-2]

During the [Vienna Offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_Offensive) in 1945, the building suffered damage and was restored during 1947 and 1950. The chancery hosted a meeting between [Nikita Khrushchev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Khrushchev) and [John F. Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_F._Kennedy) in 1961 and was the site of meetings between [Leonid Brezhnev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Brezhnev) and [Jimmy Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Carter) which led to the signing on 18 June 1979 of the historic [SALT II agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategic_Arms_Limitation_Talks).[^cite_note-embgerman-2]

In the 1980s, a multifunctional diplomatic building complex was built at Erzherzog Karl-Straße 182, Vienna 22, which today houses an embassy school and various embassy departments, as well as spy facilities and corresponding apartments, in addition to apartments for secret service personnel at Sternwartestrasse 74.

As of 2022 embassy building is being used by diplomatic mission of the [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation), but its legal ownership is disputed between Russia and [Ukraine](31750.md).[^cite_note-3]

## Russian SIGINT

Russia uses its foreign missions for [SIGINT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signals_intelligence) espionage and sabotage. After the invasion of Ukraine, all European states ordered the closure of Russian missions - except Austria - Vienna is the most important location for Russian telecommunications reconnaissance in Europe.[^cite_note-4] In 2022, there were still a total of 290 people living in Vienna who were accredited by the Russian embassy, the missions to the UN and the Organization for Security and Cooperation in Europe (OSCE). 75 of them are designated as administrative-technical staff. Austria expelled four embassy members after the Russian invasion of Ukraine.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

Satellite reception systems are located on the roof of the main building, but even more so on the building on Erzherzog Karl-Straße. Austrian counterintelligence assumes that they are operated by [SWR](614404.md). Austrian [DSN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directorate_General_for_Public_Security) claimed in 2023: “Vienna acts as a hub for Russia in the signals intelligence (SIGINT) of NATO states.”[^cite_note-6]

There is a remote satellite spy station on the roof of the Russian Mission to the United Nations. The expansion of this station began after 2014, with five satellit dishes. In 2022 there were 13 Sat dishes.[^cite_note-7]

Russia is skimming the data transponders from Western satellites. In particular, communications via geostationary satellites near the equator, such as the Eutelsat network, are intercepted.[^cite_note-\:0-5]
