**Paniai Regency** is one of the [regencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regencies_of_Indonesia) (*kabupaten*) in [Central Papua](71191665.md) Province of [Indonesia](14579.md). It is named after the [Paniai Lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paniai_Lakes). It covers an area of 6,525.25 km<sup>2</sup>, and had a population of 153,432 at the 2010 Census[^cite_note-Biro_Pusat_Statistik_2011-2] and 220,410 at the 2020 Census;[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] the official estimate as of mid-2024 was 227,254 - comprising 124,260 males and 102,994 females.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2024-1] The administrative centre is the town of [Enarotali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enarotali), in East Paniai.
