**Kurdistan province** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): استان کردستان *Ostān-e Kordestān*) is one of 31 [provinces of Iran](448160.md). The province is 28,817 km<sup>2</sup> (11,126 sq mi) in area and its capital is the city of [Sanandaj](29953972.md).[^cite_note-Kurdistan_Province_Structure-5]

Kurdistan province is located in the west of Iran, in [Region 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Iran). It borders the [Kurdistan region](3949386.md) of [Iraq](7515928.md) to the west, and the Iranian provinces of [West Azerbaijan](47567329.md) to the north, [Zanjan](448108.md) to the northeast, [Hamadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamadan_province) to the east, and [Kermanshah](448078.md) to the south.[^cite_note-6] It forms part of the area unofficially known as [Iranian Kurdistan](61733686.md).

## History



A soldier statue from [Ziwiye hoard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziwiye_hoard)

The earliest human occupation of Kurdistan dates back to the Paleolithic Period when Neanderthals lived in the Sirwan Valley of Kurdistan more than 40,000 years ago.[^cite_note-7]

## Demographics

### Language and ethnicity

Whilst most of the people of this province speak [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_languages), other languages such as [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language) and [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) are also common.[^cite_note-\:0-8]

Iranian Kurds make up the majority of the population, but Iranian Azeris populate the eastern provincial borderlands. Most of the Kurdish population speak [Sorani Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorani_Kurdish), but [Southern Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Kurdish) is spoken in the eastern parts of the province, including in [Bijar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bijar_\(city\)) and [Dezej](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dezej), while [Gorani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorani_language) is the main language in many villages in the southwestern part of the province. [Oghuz Turkic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oghuz_languages) varieties can be found in the far-eastern part of the province, including in the cities of [Delbaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delbaran), [Pir Taj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pir_Taj), [Serishabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serishabad), [Yasukand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasukand), and [Tup Aghaj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tup_Aghaj,_Kurdistan). These varieties are described as distinct from [Iranian Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_language#South_Azerbaijani), although they are closely related to it. While not being the primary language in any settlement in the province, [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) is increasingly becoming the [first language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_language), especially among the population in the eastern parts of the province.[^cite_note-languages-3][^cite_note-\:2-9]

### Religion

A significant majority of the people of Kurdistan province follow the [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam) denomination of [Islam](6037917.md). A minority of [Shia](26961.md), [Yarsanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarsanism) and [Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity_in_Iran) followers also live in this province.[^cite_note-\:1-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the province's population was 1,416,334 inhabitants in 337,179 households.[^cite_note-2006_Kurdistan_Province-14] The following census in 2011 counted 1,493,645 living in 401,845 households.[^cite_note-2011_Kurdistan_Province-15] The 2016 census measured the population of the province as 2,152,471 in 471,310 households.[^cite_note-2016_Kurdistan_Province-2]

### Clothing

Main article: [Kurdish clothing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_clothing)

The people of this province have special clothes that they have been wearing for centuries. The important point about the clothes of the people of this province is that even after the spread of [modernism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modernism) all over the world, the people of this province still try to be faithful to their past traditions in the field of [lifestyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lifestyle_\(social_sciences\)).[^cite_note-\:2-9][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

### Dance and music

Main article: [Kurdish music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_music)

Among the cultural symbols of Kurdistan people are [Kurdish dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_dance) and singing. This cultural tradition is very popular not only in this province but also throughout the country.

### Important annual celebrations

#### Chaharshanbeh Suri

Main article: [Chaharshanbe Suri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaharshanbe_Suri)

Chaharshanbeh Suri or Charshanbeh Suri is an Iranian festival of the fire dance celebrated on the eve of the last Wednesday of the year, of ancient [Zoroastrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoroastrianism) origin. It is the first festivity of [Nowruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nowruz), the Iranian New Year. In this celebration, people light fires on the mountains and on the roofs of houses, dance, and rejoice.[^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

#### Nowruz

Main article: [Nowruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nowruz)

Nowruz is one of the most important ceremonies that has been celebrated by Iranians for millennia. [Nowruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nowruz) and related ceremonies are celebrated in the most opulent way possible in Kurdistan province. As the spring equinox, Nowruz marks the beginning of spring in the Northern Hemisphere, better i.e. the moment at which the Sun crosses the celestial equator and equalizes night and day is calculated exactly every year. Traditional customs of Nowruz include fire and water, ritual dances, gift exchanges, reciting poetry, symbolic objects and more.[^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31]

## Economy

The major activities of the inhabitants are agriculture and modern livestock farming. Wheat, barley, grains and fruits are the major agricultural products. The chemical, metal, textile, leather and food industries are the main industrial activities in this province. This province has one of the largest rates of unemployment in Iran. According to Iranian statistics, more than twenty thousand people depend on being a [kolbar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolbar) for sustenance.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-\:1-10]
