The **Curia** ([Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_language): *Kúria*, pronounced [\[ˈkuːrijɒ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hungarian)), also known as the **Supreme Court** (*Legfelsőbb Bíróság*) before 2011, is the [supreme court](4913027.md) and highest judicial authority of [Hungary](13275.md). The Curia was founded in 1949 as the Supreme Court of the [Hungarian People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_People's_Republic). It was preceded by the [Royal Curia of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Curia_of_Hungary). The current president of the court is [András Zsolt Varga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A1s_Zsolt_Varga?action=edit\&redlink=1).

### Supreme court (1949–2012)

- Ödön Somogyi (1949–1950)

- Péter Jankó (1950–1953)

- [Erik Molnár](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Moln%C3%A1r) (1953–1954)

- [József Domokos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%B3zsef_Domokos) (1954–1958)

- Mihály Jahner-Bakos (1958–1963)

- József Szalay (1963–1968)

- Ödön Szakács (1968–1980)

- Jenő Szilbereky (1980–1990)

- Pál Solt (1990–2002)

- Zoltán Lomnici (2002–2008)

- [András Baka](83909330.md) (2009–2011)

### Curia (2012–present)

- Péter Darák (2012–2021)

- András Zsolt Varga (2021–present)
