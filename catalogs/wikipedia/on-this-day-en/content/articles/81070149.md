**Rijal al-Nour, Saraya al-Jawad** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): رجال النور، سرايا الجواد, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Men of Light, Al-Jawad Company') is a Syrian [Assadist](36629941.md) militant organization formed on 1 August 2025.

## History

The group was founded on 1 August 2025, in a statement commemorating [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md)'s "[Syrian Arab Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Arab_Army) Day".[^cite_note-\:0-1]

The group released a video on 1 September that shows the group carrying out an [IED](265112.md) attack on the [Jableh](2889131.md) against a vehicle belonging to the [Ministry of Defense](2774942.md) on 15 August 2025,[^cite_note-\:0-1] where 5 militants participated in addition to declaring retaliation for the arrest of Sheikh Saleh al-Mansour.[^cite_note-2]

The group claimed another [IED](265112.md) attack on a Syrian government vehicle on 4 September, which the group claimed to have carried out in revenge for the assassination of Bashar Mihoub in [Tartus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tartus), the video only shows an explosion, not the target or the location.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

The group pledged to launch intensive military operations against Syrian leader [Ahmed al-Sharaa](44007699.md) and what it called "foreign mercenaries backed by [Qatar](8238258.md) and [Turkey](11125639.md)", describing it as "revenge for the souls of the martyrs". The group is considered a possible extension of a previous faction called the [Coastal Shield Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_Shield_Brigade).[^cite_note-6]

On 29 September, Saraya al-Jawad detonated a roadside IED on a likely government position near [Besayasin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Besayasin?action=edit\&redlink=1), Latakia Province and two other IEDs the next day. None of Saraya al-Jawad's IED attacks caused casualties. The group said that it conducted the attack in revenge for the killing of four Alawite construction workers in [Jardin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jardin,_Syria?action=edit\&redlink=1), Hama Province on 28 September.[^cite_note-7]

On 24 December, the Syrian forces dismantled their headquarters in [Jableh](2889131.md); commander Abdul Aziz Hilal al-Ahmad stated that the group was planning attacks for the New Year.[^cite_note-8]

On 24 February, 2026, the Syrian government forces carried out a security operation with commander Abdul Aziz Hilal al-Ahmad stating it was carried out after several days of close surveillance and targeted two points in the Beit Alouni and Basniya areas. The leader of Saraya al-Jawad, Bashar Abdullah Abu Ruqayya, died in the operation.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]
