**Rasuwa District** ([Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): [रसुवा जिल्ला](https://ne.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B2%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B2%E0%A4%BE) [Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/9/9e/Rasuwa.ogg/Rasuwa.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is one of [13 districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_districts_of_Bagmati_Province) of [Bagmati Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagmati_Province) and one of [seventy-seven districts of Nepal](2918695.md). The district, with [Dhunche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhunche), [Gosaikunda Rural Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosaikunda_Rural_Municipality) as its district headquarters, covers an area of 1,544 km<sup>2</sup> (596 sq mi) and has a population (2011) of 43,300. Its District Post Office (DPO) has a Rasuwa DPO code of 45000. There are five post offices in the district. As per census 2011 total households in Rasuwa district were 9,778. It is the smallest district by area, among 16 districts in the *Himalaya* region of [Nepal](171166.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] The district was heavily damaged by [flooding in August 2026](84066626.md).[^cite_note-3]

## Etymology

The name, formerly *Rasowa*, is believed to be derived from two [Tibetan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibetan_language_\(standard\)) words *ra* (lamb) and *sowa* (grazing), as it was known for its lamb and grazing lands.

Its territory has elevations ranging from 614 to 7,227 meters (2,014 to 23,711 ft) from mean [sea Level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_Level). Forests cover 31.43% of the land while 16.63% is always snow-covered.[^cite_note-7] The steeply varying territory and scenery make Rasuwa a well-known tourist destination in [Nepal](171166.md). Sightseeing places include [Gosaikunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosaikunda) Lake, [Langtang valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langtang) and hot springs(locally called *Tatopani*).

At the time of the [2021 Nepal census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Nepal_census), Rasuwa District had a population of 46,689. 8.50% of the population is under 5 years of age. ## Transportation

Rasuwa is accessible by bus from Kathmandu (national capital) via Pasang Lhamu highway (H21), with its headquarters (Dhunche) being about 120 km (75 mi) from [Kathmandu](17168.md). As of 2013, 3 Village Development Committees (VDCs) namely Thuman, Langtang and Haku are not touched by any kind of roadway.

## Tourism



Rasuwa lies in Himalayan region

Rasuwa is rich in natural resources claimed by the Government of Nepal Department of Mines & Geology.[^cite_note-14] The [Langtang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langtang) mountain range stands to the north of Rasuwa. The northern parts of the area largely fall within the boundaries of [Langtang National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langtang_National_Park). Gosainkunda Lake, [Ganja La Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganja_La_Pass?action=edit\&redlink=1), and Tamang village in Bridim are the most prominent attractions of Rasuwa for tourists. The Gosainkunda Lake, also known as "Frozen Lake", is a mountain lake in the Langtang region. There are about 108 *kundas* (lakes) in this area. Saraswati Kund, Bhairab Kund, Surya Kund and Gosainkund are most important ones.



[Gosaikunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosaikunda) Lake is an [alpine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_lake) freshwater [oligotrophic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oligotrophic) lake in Nepal's [Langtang National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langtang_National_Park)
