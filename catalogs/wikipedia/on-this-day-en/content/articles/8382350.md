**Shahr-e-Bozorg** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): شهرستان شهر بزرگ, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Šahrestāne Šahre Bozorg*) is one of the 28 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Afghanistan) of [Badakhshan province](1282198.md) in northeastern [Afghanistan](737.md). According to latest estimates, approximately 67,000 people live in the district.[^cite_note-TOLO-192345-1]

The district of Shahr-e-Bozorg is located in the western part of the province, east of [Takhar Province](457289.md) and south of the [Afghanistan–Tajikistan border](58437163.md). To the northeast is [Yawan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yawan_District), to the east is [Yaftali Sufla District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaftali_Sufla_District) and to the southeast is [Argo District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argo_District).

Shahr-e-Bozorg along with the surrounding and nearby districts were affected by the [2005 Hindu Kush earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Hindu_Kush_earthquake).[^cite_note-2] The district also deals with heavy snow and occasional [avalanche](73321.md) during winter seasons.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
