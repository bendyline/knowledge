**Homestay** (also **home stay** and **home-stay**) is a form of [hospitality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospitality) and [lodging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodging) whereby visitors share a residence with a local of the area (host) to which they are traveling. The length of stay can vary from one night to over a year and can be provided for free ([gift economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gift_economy)), in exchange for monetary compensation, in exchange for a stay at the guest's property either simultaneously or at another time ([home exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_exchange)), or in exchange for [housekeeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housekeeping) or work on the host's property ([barter economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barter)). Homestays are examples of [collaborative consumption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaborative_consumption) and the [sharing economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharing_economy).[^cite_note-\:0-1] Homestays are used by travelers; students who [study abroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Study_abroad) or participate in [student exchange programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student_exchange_program); and [au pairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Au_pair), who provide child care assistance and light [household](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household) duties. They can be arranged via certain [social networking services](2041117.md), [online marketplaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_marketplace), or [academic institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_institution).[^cite_note-2] Social networking services where hosts offer homestays for free are called [hospitality exchange services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospitality_exchange_service).

## Advantages and disadvantages

Homestays offer several advantages, such as exposure to [everyday life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everyday_life) in another location, the opportunity to experience local culture and traditions, opportunities for [cultural diplomacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_diplomacy), [friendship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friendship), [intercultural competence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercultural_competence), and foreign language practice, local advice, and a lower [carbon footprint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_footprint) compared to other types of lodging; however, they may have rules and restrictions, such as curfews, facility usage, and work requirements, and may not have the same level of comfort, amenities, and privacy as other types of lodging.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-find-6][^cite_note-7]

Through homestays, visitors may immerse themselves in the culture and get a personal understanding of the customs and traditions of the area. They are affordable, frequently include home-cooked meals, and help out the community. For those looking for real relationships, homestays are intriguing because of the customized experience and cozy, homey surroundings.[^cite_note-8]

However, homestays might not have the privacy or standard hotel services like Wi-Fi and room service. Depending on the host, the experience's quality may vary, and some passengers may find difficulties due to linguistic or cultural issues and some may feel uncomfortable staying in someone’s home, sharing communal spaces, or being subject to house rules.[^cite_note-9]
