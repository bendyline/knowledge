On 29 September 2026, a [school stabbing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_stabbing) occurred at an elementary school in [Staškov](7920212.md), Slovakia. The attack left a 61-year-old female teacher along with a 13-year-old girl dead. A 41-year-old female teacher was severely injured.[^cite_note-\:1-1] A 13-year-old is in police custody. The motive is currently unknown.[^cite_note-2]

## Background

Further information: [2025 Spišská Stará Ves school stabbing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Spi%C5%A1sk%C3%A1_Star%C3%A1_Ves_school_stabbing) and [2020 Vrútky school stabbing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Vr%C3%BAtky_school_stabbing)

The attack follows a series of similar incidents in Slovakia. In 2025, an 18-year-old student [fatally stabbed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Spi%C5%A1sk%C3%A1_Star%C3%A1_Ves_school_stabbing) [two people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Spi%C5%A1sk%C3%A1_Star%C3%A1_Ves_school_stabbing) at a high school in northern Slovakia. In 2020, a former student [attacked a high school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Vr%C3%BAtky_school_stabbing) in the town of [Vrútky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vr%C3%BAtky), killing the deputy principal and injuring five people. In 2022, a high school student attacked and injured a student at a high school in [Nováky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nov%C3%A1ky).[^cite_note-\:1-1]

## Stabbing

On 29 September 2026, in Staškov, Slovakia, the suspected attacker, a 13-year-old student, attacked his school with a knife.[^cite_note-3] The attack left a 61-year-old female teacher dead while another student and a 41-year-old female teacher were wounded. The 14-year-old girl died from the wounds 2 days after the stabbing despite the efforts of the doctors. The 13-year-old suspect is in police custody.[^cite_note-4]
