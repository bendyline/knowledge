On June 8, 2026, at 07\:37\:40 [PhST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Standard_Time) (23\:37\:40 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time) on June 7), a [M<sub>w</sub>](6207163.md#Mw) 7.8 earthquake struck offshore southern [Mindanao](20546.md) in the [Philippines](23440.md). The [United States Geological Survey](23814944.md) located the epicenter approximately 25 km (16 mi) southwest of [Kablalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glan,_Sarangani) at a depth of about 57 km (35 mi), while the [Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology](12830378.md) located it 32 km (20 mi) south of [Maasim](842903.md), [Sarangani](297388.md), at a depth of 33 km (21 mi). PHIVOLCS attributed the earthquake to subduction associated with the [Cotabato Trench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotabato_Trench).

At least 106 people died, over 1,300 were injured and 7 were reported missing in Mindanao.[^cite_note-ndrrmc_sitrep_32-1] The earthquake was among the most destructive in the country in five decades.[^cite_note-2] The city of [General Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Santos), and the provinces of Sarangani and [South Cotabato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Cotabato) in Soccsksargen and [Davao Occidental](38297169.md) in [Davao](296162.md), were the worst affected. Across Mindanao, over 138,500 homes were damaged or destroyed, along with numerous buildings and facilities; the towns of [Sarangani](2154628.md), [Jose Abad Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jose_Abad_Santos,_Davao_Occidental), [Glan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glan,_Sarangani) and [Malapatan](854734.md) had the highest percentage of homes affected, while more than 30% of homes were damaged in General Santos. The earthquake also caused severe damage, one death and several injuries on the [Sangihe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangihe_Islands) and [Talaud Islands](31153243.md) of [North Sulawesi](453688.md), Indonesia.

The 2026 event is only among several major Philippine earthquakes that occurred within the same general area over the past century

Mindanao lies across the [convergent boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convergent_boundary) between the [Sunda plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunda_plate) and the [Philippine Sea plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Sea_plate). Part of the oblique convergence between these plates is taken up by [subduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subduction) along the [Cotabato Trench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotabato_Trench). The [strike-slip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike-slip) component of the convergence is accommodated partly by the [Philippine fault system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_fault_system) and partly by the [Cotabato](297394.md) fault system, a network of NW-SE trending sinistral (left-lateral) strike-slip faults that form the boundary between the Cotabato Arc and the Central Mindanao volcanic belt.[^cite_note-USGS_2010_5090-3] In 1976, a [magnitude 8.0 earthquake and tsunami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_Moro_Gulf_earthquake) along the same trench killed 8,000 people.[^cite_note-4]

The Cotabato Trench lies off the southwestern coast of Mindanao where [oceanic crust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanic_crust) of the [Sunda Plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunda_Plate) beneath the [Celebes Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celebes_Sea) [subducts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subduction) beneath the [Philippines Mobile Belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines_Mobile_Belt). It forms part of a linked set of trenches along the western side of the Philippines formed over east-dipping subduction zones, including the [Manila Trench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_Trench) and the [Negros Trench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negros_Trench).[^cite_note-Wu_etal_2017-5] At its northern end the rate of convergence across this boundary is about 100 mm (3.9 in) per year.[^cite_note-6] It is a relatively young structure, forming during the late [Miocene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miocene) to [Pliocene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pliocene);[^cite_note-Jego_etal_2005-7] this age is consistent with the estimated age of the [sedimentary rocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedimentary_rock) in the [accretionary wedge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accretionary_wedge) associated with the trench and the age of [adakitic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adakite) [arc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_arc) rocks on Mindanao thought to date the onset of subduction.[^cite_note-Schlüter_etal_2001-8] Major earthquakes in 1918 and 1976 were associated with ruptures on its southern and northern sections, respectively. The latter event involved a 160 km (99 mi) long, northwest-striking, rupture of the subduction zone beneath the Moro Gulf.[^cite_note-Stewart79-9][^cite_note-Beck85-10]

## Earthquake



USGS ShakeMap showing the earthquake's intensity

The [Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology](12830378.md) initially reported a magnitude of M<sub>w</sub> 7.0,[^cite_note-phivolcs-11] before revising the magnitude up to 7.8.[^cite_note-PHIVOLCSTWITTER-12] The [United States Geological Survey](23814944.md) and [European-Mediterranean Seismological Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European-Mediterranean_Seismological_Centre) put the magnitude at M<sub>ww</sub> 7.8 and recorded a depth of 57.0 km (35.4 mi) and 45 km (28 mi), respectively.[^cite_note-anss-13][^cite_note-14] The [Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteorology,_Climatology,_and_Geophysical_Agency) of Indonesia placed the [moment magnitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_magnitude) at 7.7, along with a depth of 47 km (29 mi).[^cite_note-15] Shaking was estimated to have lasted for around 30 seconds.[^cite_note-7.8_quake_rocks_Mindanao-16]

The earthquake was caused by [thrust faulting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrust_fault) on a north-south-striking, west-dipping fault or a north-northwest-striking, east-northeast-dipping fault.[^cite_note-anss-13] It occurred near the southern tip of Mindanao, close to where the northwest trending Cotabato Trench joins the north-south trending [Sangihe Trench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangihe_Trench). The earthquake likely occurred because of a rupture along the northern Sangihe Trench or southern Cotabato Trench, or a combination of both scenarios.[^cite_note-17] The entire rupture process was complete in about 70 seconds, releasing a [seismic moment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seismic_moment) equivalent to 6.14×10<sup>20</sup> [N⋅m](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton-metre).[^cite_note-MT-18]

### Ground effects

Numerous landslides occurred in southern Mindanao. Of these, 70 caused damage, primarily in Glan and Jose Abad Santos.[^cite_note-ndrrmc_sitrep_12-27] One landslide in Patulang, Jose Abad Santos measured 15 [ha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hectares) (37 [acres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acre_\(unit\))), with some others in the town measuring 60 ha (150 acres).[^cite_note-jas-28] An unspecified [barangay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barangay) in southern Mindanao was also almost completely buried.[^cite_note-29] The [Philippine Space Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Space_Agency) (PhilSA) identified approximately 137.87 ha (340.68 acres) of land affected by landslides in Sarangani and Davao Occidental.[^cite_note-30]

Along 100 km (62 mi) of coastline spanning the southern tip of Mindanao, primarily the towns of Glan in Sarangani and Jose Abad Santos in Davao Occidental, the earthquake raised the area's coastline by up to 2 m (6.6 ft) and moved it up to 200 m (660 ft) seawards, exposing coral reefs and other previously submerged areas, and affecting the local marine environment.[^cite_note-rtl-31][^cite_note-32]

### Tsunami

PHIVOLCS issued a tsunami warning for the provinces of [Sarangani](297388.md), [Davao Occidental](38297169.md), [Tawi-Tawi](297097.md), [Sulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulu), [Basilan](297003.md), [Zamboanga del Sur](256096.md), [Zamboanga Sibugay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamboanga_Sibugay), [Sultan Kudarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Kudarat), and [South Cotabato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Cotabato), which prompted the evacuation of all residents and businesses living in the coastline of the identified provinces.[^cite_note-phivolcs-11][^cite_note-33] Around 10,000 families in Sarangani and Sultan Kudarat were evacuated.[^cite_note-34] [BMKG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteorology,_Climatology,_and_Geophysical_Agency) also issued a tsunami warning and evacuation of residents in [Gorontalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorontalo), [Maluku Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maluku_Islands), [Sulawesi](28740.md), [Borneo](4518.md) and [Sangihe Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangihe_Islands).[^cite_note-straitstimes-35][^cite_note-BMKG-2026-36][^cite_note-37] In Malaysia, tsunami advisories were issued by the [Malaysian Meteorological Department](44614933.md) for four beaches in Sabah, which include Tawau, Semporna, [Kunak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunak_District), and [Lahad Datu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahad_Datu_District).[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39] The [Pacific Tsunami Warning Center](1359407.md) said parts of the Philippine coastline could experience 3 m (9.8 ft) waves while 1 m (3 ft 3 in) waves could hit the Indonesian and Malaysian coast.[^cite_note-40] A tsunami advisory was also issued for the Pacific coast of western and eastern Japan, ranging from [Ibaraki Prefecture](181029.md) to [Okinawa Prefecture](22477.md). The [Japan Meteorological Agency](457886.md) warned of waves reaching 1 m (3 ft 3 in) in [Miyako Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyako_Island) and the [Yaeyama Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaeyama_Islands).[^cite_note-41]

Two people drowned while a third went missing in the Philippines when they were swept out into the sea off General Santos;[^cite_note-cityhall-42] four others were rescued.[^cite_note-43] A subsequent survey revealed a maximum run-up height of 2.5 m (8 ft 2 in) and 18 m (59 ft) run-in distance at [Lebak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebak,_Sultan_Kudarat), [Sultan Kudarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Kudarat). The tsunami also travelled 1 km (0.62 mi) upstream via a nearby rivermouth. At Salaman, eyewitness account stated that the tsunami arrived about 20 to 25 minutes after the earthquake, the highest run-up was 2.2 m (7 ft 3 in), and flowed 15.3 m (50 ft) inland. The tsunami damaged stilt homes and carried boats inland at [Kalamansig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalamansig); the survey recorded a 163 m (535 ft) inundation distance and 1.94 m (6 ft 4 in) wave height.[^cite_note-44] On Balut Island, the tsunami carried large boulders and deposited them onto a reef flat. One boulder 1.2 by 0.7 by 2.5 m (3 ft 11 in by 2 ft 4 in by 8 ft 2 in) was displaced 30 m (98 ft) by the waves.[^cite_note-45]

In Indonesia, tsunami waves were detected at Kedi in North Maluku province, Ulu Siau, and Melonguane, with the waves detected ranging from 0.09–0.19 m (3.5 in – 7.5 in), with the highest recorded measurement at Talengan-Sulut at 1.5 m (4 ft 11 in).[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-BMKG-2026-36] PHIVOLCS reported tsunami waves along six areas in Mindanao's southern coast between 07\:42 and 08\:45 PHT measuring 1.4 m (4 ft 7 in).[^cite_note-47] A 3 cm (1.2 in) tsunami was also recorded in two locations in [Palau](23372.md).[^cite_note-bbc-48] In Japan, waves measuring 20 cm (7.9 in) were observed in the [Ogasawara Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogasawara_Islands) and [Kushimoto, Wakayama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kushimoto,_Wakayama), while tsunami waves were also observed in Okinawa Prefecture.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-ap-50]

## Response



Evacuated patients at Sarangani Provincial Hospital

### Local

A [state of calamity](8251850.md) was declared in the city of General Santos[^cite_note-109] and throughout the provinces of Davao Occidental,[^cite_note-110] Sarangani[^cite_note-111] and South Cotabato[^cite_note-112] due to the earthquake. General Santos Mayor Lorelie Pacquiao directed all city government personnel to be involved in frontline and vital services to report to duty while suspending all classes at all levels and work in government and private offices until further notice.[^cite_note-113] Sarangani Governor [Rogelio Pacquiao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogelio_Pacquiao) imposed province-wide emergency measures including emergency evacuation and safety protocols, rapid damage assessment and needs analysis (RDANA), unified disaster response and social services protocols, and emergency class suspensions and work adjustments for a period of 72 hours following the main shock.[^cite_note-114][^cite_note-115] These emergency protocols were extended until June 17 in view of the provincial declaration of a state of calamity.[^cite_note-116]

Classes at all levels, in both public and private schools, as well as work in government offices, were also suspended throughout South Cotabato,[^cite_note-117] [Cotabato](297394.md),[^cite_note-118] Sultan Kudarat,[^cite_note-119] [Maguindanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_del_Norte),[^cite_note-120] [Maguindanao del Sur](67871248.md),[^cite_note-121] Basilan,[^cite_note-122][^cite_note-123] Tawi-Tawi,[^cite_note-124] [Cotabato City](298955.md), [Kidapawan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidapawan),[^cite_note-125] [Davao City](489401.md),[^cite_note-Philippine_Daily_Inquirer-2026-71][^cite_note-126] [Davao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davao_del_Norte),[^cite_note-127] [Davao Oriental](296986.md),[^cite_note-128] [Davao de Oro](296993.md),[^cite_note-129] [Agusan del Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agusan_del_Sur),[^cite_note-130] [Damulog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damulog),[^cite_note-131] [Kibawe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kibawe),[^cite_note-132] [Valencia](2137518.md),[^cite_note-133] [Cagayan de Oro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cagayan_de_Oro),[^cite_note-Corrales-2026-134] and Davao Occidental[^cite_note-135] while a work suspension was imposed in [Davao del Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davao_del_Sur)[^cite_note-136] and [Bangsamoro](37260549.md).[^cite_note-137]

Operations were temporarily suspended at [General Santos International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Santos_International_Airport)[^cite_note-138] by the [Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Aviation_Authority_of_the_Philippines) to conduct safety assessment after the earthquake which affected 17 flights:[^cite_note-139] 11 operated by [Cebu Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebu_Pacific), four operated by [PAL Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PAL_Express), and two operated by [Philippine Airlines](296889.md).[^cite_note-140] The airport was then reopened to facilitate landing and takeoff operations for government, military, and humanitarian flights.[^cite_note-141] Ceiling tiles fell and glass windows shattered.[^cite_note-142] The [Department of Transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Transportation_\(Philippines\)) confirmed the normal operations of other airports in Mindanao.[^cite_note-143] The southbound lane of Bolton Bridge in Davao City was closed pending inspection of [Department of Public Works and Highways](4701882.md) and City Engineer's Office personnel.[^cite_note-144] Numerous areas were assessed after the earthquake, including the Davao del Sur Provincial Hospital,[^cite_note-145] General Santos,[^cite_note-146] and [Kapatagan, Lanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapatagan,_Lanao_del_Norte).[^cite_note-147]

The Municipal Government of [Bansalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bansalan), Davao del Sur, enabled their Emergency Operations Center to handle the local relief operations.[^cite_note-148] Cagayan de Oro Mayor Ronaldo Uy directed the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office to prioritize safety inspections of schools and high-rise structures.[^cite_note-Corrales-2026-134] [Cebu](160278.md) Governor [Pam Baricuatro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pam_Baricuatro) and [Cebu City](285868.md) Mayor [Nestor Archival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nestor_Archival) instructed their respective disaster response teams to coordinate to with their counterparts in the affected region in assessing and determining the appropriate aid to provide.[^cite_note-149] The [Cebu Provincial Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebu_Provincial_Board) approved ₱10 million ([US\$](18717338.md)203,045.69) in aid to General Santos.[^cite_note-150] The Provincial Government of [Pangasinan](1691800.md) expressed its commitment to extend aid to the affected local government units due to their sufficient calamity funds.[^cite_note-151] [Bangsamoro](37260549.md) Chief Minister [Abdulraof Macacua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulraof_Macacua) ordered the deployment of the Rapid Emergency Action on Disaster Incidence Team in coordination with the OCD Bangsamoro Autonomous Region to assist with damage assessment, coordination, and information management efforts in affected communities.[^cite_note-152]

### National

President Bongbong Marcos visiting affected areas in General Santos

President [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md) issued a statement directing all government agencies to respond to the disaster, with the OCD and NDRRMC conducting disaster response and monitoring on all affected areas, [Department of Social Welfare and Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Social_Welfare_and_Development) pre-positioning relief goods and preparing evacuation centers, and [Department of Public Works and Highways](4701882.md) assessing damage to roads, bridges, and critical infrastructure.[^cite_note-153] He also ordered the suspension of all work and classes throughout all affected regions.[^cite_note-154] 2,784 search, rescue, and retrieval personnel have been deployed across affected areas, with an additional 1,475 responders on standby for immediate deployment. The response force includes 23 teams from the [Armed Forces of the Philippines](23448.md), 310 teams from the PNP, 169 teams from the [Philippine Coast Guard](1954144.md), and 510 teams from the [Bureau of Fire Protection](63467678.md).[^cite_note-\:5-155]



Search and rescue operations in General Santos

[Department of Education](5464548.md) Secretary [Sonny Angara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonny_Angara) deployed DepEd engineers to provinces affected by the earthquake.[^cite_note-156] [Department of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Health_\(Philippines\)) Undersecretary and Spokesperson Albert Domingo said that all crisis protocols have been implemented in hospitals throughout Mindanao while doing rapid assessments of health concerns in Mindanao regions.[^cite_note-157] DPWH Secretary [Vince Dizon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vince_Dizon) stated that his agency will prioritize the repair of bridges, roads, hospitals, and schools to ensure smooth transportation of goods and rescue personnel to affected areas as well as the evacuation of victims.[^cite_note-158] The [Department of Budget and Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Budget_and_Management) says that there are available Quick Resource Funds in the 2026 national budget for immediate deployment by the concerned agencies and they are ready to process any request for QRF replenishment.[^cite_note-QRF-159] A price freeze of basic commodities was imposed on General Santos by the [Department of Trade and Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Trade_and_Industry_\(Philippines\)) following the local government's declaration of a state of calamity.[^cite_note-\:3-160] The [Department of Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Energy_\(Philippines\)) assessed the effect of the earthquake on power networks and encouraged the public to report any fuel price hike violations in the affected areas.[^cite_note-161][^cite_note-\:3-160]

On June 10, President Marcos visited General Santos to personally assess the damage to a hospital and two schools in the city, and to oversee the distribution of financial assistance to identified individuals.[^cite_note-162] He pledged [₱](227672.md)50,000 ([US\$](18717338.md)1,015.23) each to families whose members died in the earthquake.[^cite_note-163] Vice President [Sara Duterte](42504861.md) attended the wake of several victims in Malapatan, Sarangani.[^cite_note-164] The [Office of the Vice President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_the_Vice_President_of_the_Philippines) deployed its Kalusugan Food Truck to provide hot meals to frontliners working in the disaster areas.[^cite_note-165] The PCG suspended all watercraft operations in the coastal areas of Sarangani and Davao Occidental.[^cite_note-166] The [National Historical Commission of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Historical_Commission_of_the_Philippines) reiterated reminders for heritage site caretakers in the affected areas, such as area security, safety of heritage site personnel, photographic documentation of damage, and recovery of objects.[^cite_note-167] The [Bureau of Internal Revenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Internal_Revenue) extended tax deadlines for taxpayers registered in the provinces of South Cotabato and Sarangani as well as General Santos, moving its tax filing, payment, and document submission deadlines falling between June 8 and 29 to June 30, 2026.[^cite_note-168] Courts in three judicial regions were suspended by the [Supreme Court of the Philippines](1335534.md) in Zamboanga City, Sarangani, [Digos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digos), [Samal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samal,_Davao_del_Norte), [Tagum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagum), Davao Oriental, Davao de Oro, Mati, Davao del Sur, South Cotabato, Davao City, Davao del Sur, [Tacurong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tacurong) and Sultan Kudarat.[^cite_note-169] The [National Electrification Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Electrification_Administration) reactivated Task Force Kapatid and deployed linemen from other electric distribution cooperatives to help restore electricity to some 803,230 consumer connections affected. According to the NEA Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Department, three cooperatives in Soccskargen experienced total power interruption, while one cooperative in Davao Region had partial outages.[^cite_note-170]



A team of 150 responders deployed by the Philippine Coast Guard

Distribution of aid in General Santos



DSWD personnel providing relief goods to evacuees

Soccsksargen police deployed 500 personnel and 23 vehicles across the region to assist in response operations, with 390 assigned to search, rescue, and retrieval operations, 100 securing vital installations, and 10 assigned to the Women and Children Protection Center.[^cite_note-3dead-67] Soccsksargen DSWD Field Office convened its Disaster Response Management Division and Quick Response Team to coordinate with affected local government units while setting up mobile command centers and field kitchens to assist in the evacuation of affected residents.[^cite_note-171] The [Philippine Army](6032559.md)'s [10th Infantry Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10th_Infantry_Division_\(Philippines\)) mobilized its Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Response teams from the 27th and 28th Infantry Battalions, Task Force Gensan, the 1002nd Infantry Brigade, and the 10th Emergency Response Company. to assist affected communities.[^cite_note-172] The [Philippine Air Force](27918720.md) deployed [rotary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_S-70) and [fixed-wing aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_C295) from Tactical Operations Group 11 in Davao City to assist in rapid damage assessment and needs analysis activities in General Santos and Sarangani, humanitarian and disaster response operations, and air transport of government officials and personnel from other government agencies and other service branches of the AFP.[^cite_note-173]

The [Alliance of Concerned Teachers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_of_Concerned_Teachers) posted their concern over damaged schools due to the earthquake.[^cite_note-174] [Robinsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robinsons_Malls) and [SM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SM_Supermalls) Malls in General Santos were closed to allow their engineering and construction teams conduct inspections on the buildings.[^cite_note-175] The [Philippine Red Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Red_Cross) was put on the highest alert and members were deployed to assess structural damage and coordinate evacuation and relief operations.[^cite_note-176] [PLDT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLDT) and [Smart Communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_Communications) dispatched field and operations teams to evaluate network facilities and restore connectivity services in regions.[^cite_note-177] [Jollibee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jollibee) stated that all its General Santos-based team members have been accounted for and no injuries were reported in their stores located in the affected areas.[^cite_note-178] [Catholic Bishops' Conference of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Bishops'_Conference_of_the_Philippines) President Archbishop [Gilbert Garcera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert_Garcera) asked all bishops in the country to authorize a second collection for the earthquake victims and to offer [General Intercessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Intercessions) for the victims of the earthquake and those tasked with relief and rescue efforts.[^cite_note-179]
