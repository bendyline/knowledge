**Kankakee** ([/ˌkæŋkəˈkiː/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [*KANG-kə-KEE*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key))[^cite_note-4] is a city in and the [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) of [Kankakee County, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankakee_County,_Illinois), United States.[^cite_note-GR6-5] Located on the [Kankakee River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankakee_River), as of 2020, the city's population was 24,052.[^cite_note-6] Kankakee is a principal city of the [Kankakee-Bourbonnais-Bradley Metropolitan Statistical Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankakee-Bourbonnais-Bradley,_IL_MSA). It serves as an anchor city in the rural plains outside [Chicago](6886.md).

## History



Kankakee in 1936

The city's name is probably derived from a corrupted version of the [Miami-Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami-Illinois_language) word *_teeyaahkiki_*, meaning "open country/exposed land/land in open/land exposed to view",[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] in reference to the area's prior status as a marsh. Kankakee was founded in 1854.[^cite_note-15]

On March 10, 2026, a high-end [EF3](3724479.md) tornado struck the south side of Kankakee, with winds up to 160 miles per hour.[^cite_note-16]

## Geography

According to the 2021 census gazetteer files, Kankakee has a total area of 15.54 square miles (40.25 km<sup>2</sup>), of which 15.06 square miles (39.01 km<sup>2</sup>) (or 96.92%) is land and 0.48 square miles (1.24 km<sup>2</sup>) (or 3.08%) is water.[^cite_note-gaz2021-17]

The [Kankakee River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankakee_River) runs through Kankakee. It is approximately 133 miles long and serves as a major attraction and defining landmark of Kankakee. The river water is refined at the Kankakee Water Company, and electricity is generated at the Kankakee River Dam, providing vital resources to the community. Its winding path, including inlets and [eddies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddies), creates desirable fishing conditions for outdoor enthusiasts.

### 2020 census

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), Kankakee had a population of 24,052 and 9,204 households, including 5,627 families.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-24][^cite_note-Census2020PL-25]

The population density was 1,548.25 inhabitants per square mile (597.78/km<sup>2</sup>). There were 10,600 housing units, of which 13.2% were vacant; the homeowner vacancy rate was 3.8% and the rental vacancy rate was 11.1%.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-24][^cite_note-CensusProfile2020-26]

The median age was 36.6 years. 25.6% of residents were under the age of 18, 9.1% were from 18 to 24, 27.5% were from 25 to 44, 23.6% were from 45 to 64, and 15.1% were 65 years of age or older. For every 100 females there were 94.9 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 93.4 males age 18 and over.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-24]

Of the city's 9,204 households, 32.1% had children under the age of 18 living in them, 30.2% were married-couple households, 23.2% had a male householder with no spouse or partner present, and 38.1% had a female householder with no spouse or partner present. About 34.7% of households were made up of individuals, and 14.1% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-24]

About 99.8% of residents lived in urban areas, while 0.2% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-Census2020DHC-27]

- The current Kankakee courthouse was built from 1909 to 1912 in the Neo-classical Revivalist style in the wake of the 1893 [Columbian Exposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World's_Columbian_Exposition) (the Chicago World's Fair) as part of the [City Beautiful movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_Beautiful_movement). ## Parks and recreation

### Kankakee Valley Park District

Kankakee Valley Park District has 37 parks, comprising a total of 600 acres (2.4 km<sup>2</sup>). Facilities include an indoor ice skating rink, a recreation center, dog park, campground and a 72 boat slip marina on the Kankakee River. Fishing is plentiful as the district has 13 riverfront parks as well as a 5-acre (2.0 ha) stocked quarry.

The city has two softball complexes that have both been inducted into the Softball Hall of Fame. They host annual state and international tournaments drawing nearly 50,000 spectators throughout the year. Some Kankakee youth baseball leagues have won state championships.[^cite_note-GR3-u-30]

## Government



Civil War Memorial by the courthouse

Kankakee is governed by the mayor council system. The city council consists of fourteen members who are elected from seven wards (two per ward). The mayor and city clerk are elected in a citywide vote.

## Education

### Higher education

Organized in 1966 by a group of citizens, [Kankakee Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankakee_Community_College) was established to provide a post-secondary educational resource for the people of the Kankakee area.[^cite_note-GR4-31]

### Primary and secondary education

Public schools are part of the Kankakee School District 111, which includes five elementary schools (Edison, Mark Twain, Lincoln Cultural Center Montessori, Steuben, and Taft), two middle schools (Kennedy and King), one junior high school (Kankakee Junior High), and one high school ([Kankakee High](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankakee_High_School)), which from 1966 to 1983 was two separate high schools, Eastridge and Westview.[^cite_note-32]

There are three private high schools: [Bishop McNamara Catholic School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_McNamara_High_School_\(Kankakee,_Illinois\)) (Catholic), [Grace Christian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grace_Christian_Academy_\(Illinois\)) (non-denominational),[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] and Kankakee Trinity Academy (inter-denominational).[^cite_note-35]

## Infrastructure

### Transportation



Aerial view of Kankakee. The confluence of Iroquois River and Kankakee River is visible on the left edge of the frame.

#### Airport

Kankakee is served by the [Greater Kankakee Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Kankakee_Airport), a [general aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_aviation) facility located in the southern portion of Kankakee.[^cite_note-36]

#### Railroads

[Amtrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amtrak) provides service to Kankakee from the [Kankakee Amtrak Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kankakee_\(Amtrak_station\)).[^cite_note-37] Amtrak operates the *[City of New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_New_Orleans_\(train\)),* the *[Illini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illini_\(train\)),* and the *[Saluki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saluki_\(train\))*, which each run once daily in both directions.[^cite_note-38]

#### Highways

[Interstate 57](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_57) runs east–west in the southern part of the city and turns north–south in the eastern part of Kankakee. United States Highways [US 45](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_45_in_Illinois) and [US 52](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_52_in_Illinois) run [concurrently](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concurrency_\(road\)) forming, along with [Illinois State Route 50](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_State_Route_50), the major north–south thoroughfares through Kankakee. [Illinois State Route 17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_State_Route_17) is the major east–west road that bisects the city.

#### Public transportation

The [River Valley Metro Mass Transit District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Valley_Metro_Mass_Transit_District) (RVMMTD; River Valley Metro or METRO, for short) operates the region's [transit bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transit_bus) system. Service runs seven days a week to locations in Kankakee as well as the nearby cities of [Aroma Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aroma_Park,_Illinois), [Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradley,_Illinois), [Bourbonnais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourbonnais,_Illinois), and [Manteno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manteno,_Illinois). All of the Kankakee routes are stationed out of the Chestnut & North Schuyler Transfer Station.[^cite_note-39] River Valley Metro operates 12 fixed-regular bus routes and two commuter routes. The Midway and University Park commuter routes were added January 5, 2014, and in August 2015 River Valley Metro added a second Midway route to its schedule. In January 2016, a second University Park route was added.
