**Hoskote** (also known as **Hosakote** and historically called **Ooscata** by the British) is a taluk or city in [Bengaluru North District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengaluru_North_district), India. Headquartered at the Hoskote town, it consists of five [hoblis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hobli) - Kasaba, Anugondanahalli, [Jadigenahalli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jadigenahalli), Nandagudi and [Sulibele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulibele). There are 294[^cite_note-1] villages in Hoskote taluk, including the five [hoblis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hobli).

## History

Hoskote was a Jagir of Maratha warrior Shahaji Raje for around 50 years and also part of Swarajya of [Shivaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivaji). The [Battle of Ooscata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Ooscata) in the [First British-Mysore War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_British-Mysore_War), on the night of 22–23 August 1768, took place here.

## Demographics

As of 2011 India [census](6889.md),[^cite_note-2] Hoskote had a population of 219000. Males constituted 52% of the population and females 48%. Hoskote had an average literacy rate of 86.22%, higher than the state average of 75.36%: male literacy was 89.58%, and female literacy was 82.68%. 11.98% of the population were under 6 years of age.
