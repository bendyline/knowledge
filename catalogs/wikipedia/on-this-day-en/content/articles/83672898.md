On 11 July 2026 at c. 8\:12 p.m. [EST](496028.md), two people opened fire on each other in a crowd at the Salsa on St. Clair festival in [Toronto](64646.md), Ontario, Canada, killing two people and injuring five others.[^cite_note-Global_News-1][^cite_note-\:0-2] Toronto police said the shooting was believed to be targeted.[^cite_note-3]

## Background

**Salsa on St. Clair** is an annual street festival held in midtown Toronto along [St. Clair Avenue West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Clair_Avenue). Launched in 2005, it's recognized as one of the province's top 100 festivals by Festivals and Events Ontario, the event typically takes place over a weekend in mid-July. It attracts an estimated 340,000 visitors annually,[^cite_note-4] with attendance reaching approximately 10,000 people at any given time.

## Shooting

At approximately 8\:12 p.m. police responded to reports of active gunfire within the area of the festival and multiple victims. People at the festival ran, causing a stampede.[^cite_note-CBC_Rieti-5] Deputy Chief Frank Barredo said two people with guns opened fire on each other in a crowded area. Two unidentified firearms were recovered at the scene. Barredo added that they have three crime scenes. The exchange of gunfire, which occurred in a dense crowd of approximately 13,000 festivalgoers, resulted in two fatalities[^cite_note-CBC_Rieti-5] and five injuries.[^cite_note-6]

## Victims



Victims who were killed from the shooting: Shaquan Quashie (left), Cesar Vernaza (right).&#x20;

Two men were confirmed to have been killed while five others were transported to hospital with serious injuries.[^cite_note-Global_News-1][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-\:0-2] The deceased were identified as 25-year-old Shaquan Quashie and 20-year-old Cesar Vernaza.[^cite_note-8] Toronto police said both victims were targeted and knew each other, but it is still unknown if they were among the gunmen.[^cite_note-9] Among the injured was a young woman who got shot in the neck.[^cite_note-10]

## Suspects

The police said that they were looking for two suspected related to this incident.[^cite_note-Global_News-1] An unconfirmed video posted to social media showed a person being arrested in relation to the shooting.[^cite_note-11] On August 6th, Toronto Police announced that they arrested the two suspects in the shooting and identified them as 18-year-old **Jessiah Massaro-Gill** and 18-year-old **Lewis Downey**. They were charged with two counts of first-degree murder and five counts of attempted murder in relation to the shooting. Downey is facing several other charges, including unauthorized possession of a restricted firearm and two counts of possession of a Schedule 1 substance for the purpose of trafficking. Massaro-Gill's other charges include six counts of possession of a Schedule 1 substance for the purpose of trafficking and two counts of possession of an unauthorized firearm.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

## Aftermath



Salsa on St. Clair festival in 2016

The final day of the festival was cancelled.[^cite_note-14] A crowd of around 50 people held a [candlelight vigil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candlelight_vigil) outside of a church in the St. Clair West neighbourhood.[^cite_note-15] The shooting sparked security concerns across the country.[^cite_note-16] Toronto police echoed calls for gun control.[^cite_note-17] Organizers of the 2026 [Taste of the Danforth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taste_of_the_Danforth) festival said that it would be safe to attend.[^cite_note-18]

## Reactions

Prime Minister [Mark Carney](13571049.md) posted on [X](9988187.md) that he was "horrified" by the shooting, thanked first responders and offered prayers for the families of the victims and the critically injured.[^cite_note-CTV-19] Conservative leader [Pierre Poilievre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Poilievre) said he was "shocked" to hear about the shooting and offered prayers for the victims and the injured.[^cite_note-CTV-19] Ontario Premier [Doug Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doug_Ford) said he was "devastated" by the shooting and said his thoughts were with the victims, their families and everyone affected.[^cite_note-20] Toronto mayor [Olivia Chow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Chow) called the shooting a "reckless, irresponsible act of violence" and said she is "deeply disturbed".[^cite_note-CTV-19] Area councillor [Michael Colle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Colle) called the shooting "disgusting".[^cite_note-CTV-19] The [CN Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CN_Tower) dimmed its lights at the top of every hour in honour of the victims.[^cite_note-21]

The organizers of the festival said they are prepared to walk away from the festival unless the City of Toronto strengthens security.[^cite_note-22] The Hillcrest Village Business Improvement Area voted to discontinue its sponsorship of the annual street festival following the fatal shooting.[^cite_note-23]
