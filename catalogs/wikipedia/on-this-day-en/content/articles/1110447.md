**Chamba district** is the northwestern district of [Himachal Pradesh](14190.md), in [India](14533.md), with its headquarters in [Chamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamba,_Himachal_Pradesh) town. The towns of [Dalhousie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalhousie,_India), [Khajjhiar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khajjiar) and Churah Valley are popular hill stations and vacation spots for the people from the plains of northern India.

## Economy

In 2006 the [Ministry of Panchayati Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Panchayati_Raj) named Chamba one of the country's 250 [most backward districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_India) (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-brgf-2] It is one of the two districts in Himachal Pradesh currently receiving funds from the Backward Regions Grant Fund Programme (BRGF).[^cite_note-brgf-2]

According to the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India) Chamba district has a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_India) of 519,080,[^cite_note-districtcensus-4] roughly equal to the nation of [Cape Verde](18962637.md).[^cite_note-cia-5] This gives it a ranking of 544th in India (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-districtcensus-4] The district has a population density of 80 inhabitants per square kilometre (210/sq mi) .[^cite_note-districtcensus-4] Its [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_India) over the decade 2001–2011 was 12.58%.[^cite_note-districtcensus-4] Chamba has a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) of 989 [females](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_India) for every 1000 males,[^cite_note-districtcensus-4] and a [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of 73.19%. 6.96% of the population lived in urban areas. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes made up 21.52% and 26.10% of the population respectively.[^cite_note-districtcensus-4] The [Gaddis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaddis), the largest Scheduled Tribe in Himachal Pradesh, mainly live in Chamba district.

The majority of the people of the district are native speakers of languages in the [Himachali group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Pahari). These primarily include [Chambeali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chambeali_language) (the first language reported by 23% of the population in the 2011 census), [Gaddi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaddi_language) or Bharmauri (22%), [Churahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churahi_language) (14%), [Pangwali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangwali_language) (3.5%) and [Bhateali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhateali_language) (2.9%). Other languages spoken are [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi) (2.9%), [Gujari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujari) (1.9%), [Kashmiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmiri_language) (1.3%) and [Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language) (1.1%).[^cite_note-languages-14][^cite_note-15]

## Fair and festivals

Chamba is famous for its vivid festivals. The Suhi Mata festival is held for four days in March/April every year in memory of princess Sui. She is known to have sacrificed her life for the cause of her people, that is, the people of Chamba.



Chandrabhaga river flowing through the Pangi Valley in Chamba District of Himachal Pradesh.

At the time of the harvest of the maize crop, which is the main crop of this region, to express happiness and to enjoy after a hard toil, locals celebrate Minjar festival. This week long fare/ Mela is the most popular Mela of Chamba.&#x20;

- [Chaurasi Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaurasi_Temple_Bharmour) : Nestled on the bank of the river Ravi, Chamba town complete 1000 years of its existence. The town stands on a plateau on the right bank of the Ravi river valley between Dhauladhar and Zanskar ranges south of the inner Himalayas. This town was founded by Raja Sahil Varman in the beginning of 10th century. In the genealogical rolls of the Chamba Rajas, a reference occurs of place, which was adorned with highly fragrant Champaka trees and guarded by Goddess Champavati.

The temple was built by Sahil Varman in the honour of his daughter Champavati who is worshipped as a goddess in Chamba. This journey of one thousand years is a repository a civilised, cultured and developed society which existed in the lap of the Himalayas. The unique architect of houses and temples, splendid wood and metal craft, world fame Chamba Rumal and Chappal and the pahari paintings are some salient features of this one thousand years young town.

The Chamba town has a number of temples, Palaces and stylised buildings. Laxmi Narayana Temple, which is the main temple of Chamba town, was built by Sahil Varman in the 10th century AD. There are several other temples within the complex.

The temple of Radha Krishna, Shiva Temple of Chandergupta and Gauri Shankar Temple are among these. The ancient temple of Vajreshwari is believed to be 1000 years old and is dedicated to Devi Vajreshwari-Goddess of lightning.

The Sui Mata temple can be divided into three parts which can physically spread apart. The Hari Rai temple is dedicated to Lord Vishnu and dates back to 11th century.

Reference of Chamba town would be incomplete without the majestic Chaugan. It is the heart and hub centre of all activities in Chamba. Tradition is silent as to its use as a polo ground and the name is etymologically distinct from Chaugan, the Persian name of Polo, being of Sanskrit origin and meaning 'four-sided'. Initially the five Chaugan were a single patch of meadow.

To mark the completion of 1000 years of this town, the Government of Himachal Pradesh had celebrated the millennium of this town from 14 to 20 April 2006 in a most befitting way. The celebrations has provided the people the opportunity to have a firsthand knowledge of the rich cultural and historical past of the Chamba town.
