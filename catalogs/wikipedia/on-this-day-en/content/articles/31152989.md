**Sekadau Regency** is a [regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regencies_of_Indonesia) of [West Kalimantan](453636.md) province of [Indonesia](14579.md). It was created on 18 December 2003 from the eastern part of [Sanggau Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanggau_Regency). It covers an area of 5,642.75 km<sup>2</sup>, and had a population of 181,634 at the 2010 Census[^cite_note-Biro_Pusat_Statistik_2011-2] and 211,559 at the 2020 Census;[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] the official estimate as at mid 2024 was 222,510 (comprising 115,200 males and 107,310 females).[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2025-1] The regency seat lies at the town of [Sekadau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sekadau?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Sekadau Hilir District.

### Local government

The regency is a second-level administrative division equivalent to a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_status_in_Indonesia). As a regency, it is headed by a regent who is elected democratically. Head of districts are appointed directly by the regent with the recommendation of the regency secretary. Executive power lies with the regent and vice regent, while legislative function is exercised by the regency's parliament.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Infrastructure

### Education

As of 2020, there are 35 kindergartens in the regency, 241 elementary schools, 67 junior high schools, and 23 senior high schools. In addition, the regency has six vocational high schools.[^cite_note-\:0-8] It only has one higher educational institution, the Keling Kumang Institute, which is private but partially supported by the regency government. The institute campus is located in the town of Sekadau and focuses on technology-related programs such as agro-technology and computer science.[^cite_note-9] The regency also has one public library maintained by the regency government, also located at its regency seat, the town of Sekadau.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

There are also four Islamic boarding schools (*pesantren*) in the regency as at 2020.[^cite_note-\:0-8]

### Healthcare

The regency has one hospital, four polyclinics, 67 [puskesmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puskesmas), and 6 pharmacies. The only hospital in the regency, Sekadau Regional Hospital, is a public hospital owned by regency government. It is classified as C-class by Ministry of Health and located just across the town of Sekadau.[^cite_note-12] In addition, there are two family planning clinics, 10 village medical clinics, 239 healthcare posts, and 109 maternity cottages.[^cite_note-\:0-8]

### Transportation

There are 852.85 kilometres of road in the regency, out of which 584.501 kilometres are maintained by regency government. From those 584.501 kilometres, 115.436 kilometres have not yet sealed and still have a soil surface as of 2020.[^cite_note-\:0-8] There is no airport in the regency and the main transportation is by using road travel. The closest airport is [Tebelian Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tebelian_Airport) which is located in [Sintang Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sintang_Regency).[^cite_note-13]

### Others

There are exactly 272 mosques, 149 Protestant churches, 303 Catholic churches, three Chinese Buddhist temples (*Vihara*), and seven Confucian temples (*klenteng*).[^cite_note-\:0-8]
