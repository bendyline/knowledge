**Tiszaújváros** (Hungarian pronunciation: [\[ˈtisɒuːjvaːroʃ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hungarian); [Slovak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovak_language): *Nové Mesto nad Tisou*) is an industrial town in [Borsod-Abaúj-Zemplén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borsod-Aba%C3%BAj-Zempl%C3%A9n) county, Northern [Hungary](13275.md), 35 km (22 mi) south-east of [Miskolc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miskolc), near the river [Tisza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tisza).

## History

The town was built up on the outskirts of the old village [Tiszaszederkény](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiszaszederk%C3%A9ny). In legal terms the old village and the new town are the same: the village became a town in 1966 and was renamed twice, in 1970 and in 1991.[^cite_note-1]
