**Balochistan Police** or **Baloch Police**[^cite_note-Dawn201017-1] ([Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language) and [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): بلوچستان پولیس) is responsible for policing urban [Balochistan, Pakistan](4687.md). Its strength is 42,770 as of 2023. The current Inspector General of Police, Balochistan is Muhammad Tahir.[^cite_note-2]

## Law enforcement in Balochistan

| **Service colour** | Dark blue and red |
| --- | --- |
| **Uniform colour** | [Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shades_of_black), [Khaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khaki)[^cite_note-Uniform_Col-3] |

Balochistan Police is responsible for the law and order situation in mostly urban areas only, which are called A areas. This division has been in place, in various forms, since the time of the British Raj. Balochistan's rural areas, called B areas, are policed by the [Balochistan Levies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochistan_Levies). The [Frontier Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontier_Corps) operates in both areas. This division is seen as a severe handicap by the police as criminals based outside their limited area of jurisdiction can easily plan attacks and run away.

The distinction was eliminated by Pervez Musharraf, however, it was brought back by the PPP government of 2008 under CM Balochistan Aslam Raisani. The frequent swings in policy obviously do not allow institution-building and hurt the agencies' abilities to deal with crime.

Since Pakistan's post 9/11 involvement in the [war on terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_on_terrorism) targeted killings, kidnappings, and terrorist attacks have risen substantially. In 2013 there were several bombings targeting the [Hazara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazaras) community in Quetta[^cite_note-4] and attacks on police including senior officials.

[PPP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_People's_Party) formed a government in Balochistan and ruled in a coalition with Nawab Aslam Raisani as Chief Minister. Aslam Raisana chose Humayan Joegazai as CCPO Quetta, who was known to have close links with lashkar-e-Jhangvi. During the start of his career, he ordered policemen to fire live rounds at Hazara protesters. 25 innocent civilians were killed. He was brought back to Quetta by Raisani and during his tenure, things once again took a turn for the worse, particularly for the Hazara community.

## Improvement in 2014

The number of terrorist attacks dropped from 205 in 2013 to 154 in 2014 (a 25 percent decline) and the number of people killed in terrorist attacks fell from around 350 in 2013 to 48 in 2014 (an 86 percent decline).[^cite_note-5]

## Levies Merger

The Government of Balochistan has once again abolished the Balochistan Levies Force merging it into the Balochistan Police to "strengthen institutional structure, reduce duplication, and ensure better governance in remote and conflict-prone districts". [^cite_note-6]

### Posts

SHO, SDPO, DPO, CCPO, RPO and PPO are posts, not ranks. So you may see a lower rank acting at a higher post for some time.[^cite_note-11]
