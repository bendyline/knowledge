# Hole in one

This article is about the golf shot. For other uses, see [Hole in one (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hole_in_one_\(disambiguation\)).

In [golf](19568112.md), a **hole in one** or **hole-in-one** (pl.: **holes-in-one**/**holes in one**[^cite_note-1]) (also known as an **ace**, mostly in [American English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_English)) occurs when a ball hit from the [tee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teeing_ground) on the first stroke of a hole finishes in the cup. Holes-in-one most commonly occur on [par](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par_\(score\)) 3 holes, the shortest holes on a standard size golf course. Longer hitters have also recorded holes-in-one on longer holes, though nearly all par-4 and par-5 holes are too long for golfers to reach in a single shot.[^cite_note-2] Although holes-in one generally require an accurate, well-hit shot, they also involve a significant amount of [luck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luck). As of January 2021, a [condor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par_\(score\)#Condor) (four under par) hole-in-one on a par-5 hole had been recorded on five occasions.

## Description

In [golf](19568112.md), a hole in one or hole-in-one occurs when a ball hit from a [tee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teeing_ground) to start a hole finishes in the cup. The feat is also known as an *ace*, mostly in [American English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_English). As the feat needs to occur on the stroke that starts a hole, a ball hit from a tee following a lost ball, [out-of-bounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Out-of-bounds), or water hazard is not a hole-in-one, due to the application of a stroke [penalty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_\(golf\)).

## Rarity

While well known outside golf and often requiring a well hit shot and significant power, holes-in-one need also a significant element of [luck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luck).[^cite_note-odds-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] *Time* magazine reported 1,200 holes in one were made by American golfers in 1922.[^cite_note-6]

Holes-in-one most commonly occur on [par](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par_\(score\)) 3 holes, the shortest distance holes on a standard size golf course. Longer hitters have also accomplished this feat on longer holes, though nearly all par 4 and par 5 holes are too long for golfers to reach in a single shot. As such, they are more common and considered less impressive than other hole accomplishments such as completing a par 5 in two shots (an [albatross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par_\(score\)#Albatross)).[^cite_note-7]

### Miniature golf

The world record on one round of [minigolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miniature_golf) is 18 strokes on 18 holes, a score relatively common for courses constructed of [eternit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternit). On other playing systems, a perfect round of 18 holes-in-one is extremely rare, although such rounds have been reported in Sweden on both felt and concrete courses.[^cite_note-autogenerated22-8]

## Holes-in-one on par 5 (or higher) holes

As of January 2021, a [condor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par_\(score\)#Condor) (four under par) hole-in-one on a par 5 hole had been recorded on five occasions,[^cite_note-Condor01-9][^cite_note-Condor02-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-Condor03-12] aided by thin air at high altitude, or by cutting the corner on a doglegged or horseshoe-shaped hole.[^cite_note-Condor01-9][^cite_note-Condor02-10][^cite_note-Condor03-12]

- A horseshoe-shaped par-5 hole once enabled a condor hole-in-one to be achieved with a 3-iron club.

  [^cite_note-Condor01-9]

  [^cite_note-Condor02-10]

- Another may have been achieved at the former Piedmont Crescent Golf Course in 1973 after bouncing multiple times on a very firm fairway due to unseasonably dry weather.

  [^cite_note-13]

- The longest recorded straight drive hole-in-one is believed to be 517 yards or 473 metres, on the par-5 No. 9 hole at Green Valley Ranch Golf Club in [Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver) in 2002, aided by the thin air due to the high altitude.

## Notable holes-in-one



An amateur golfer celebrates a hole in one.

A memorable hole-in-one was made in the [1973 Open Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973_Open_Championship) by [Gene Sarazen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene_Sarazen) at age 71. Earl Dietering of Memphis, Tennessee, 78 years old at the time, is believed to hold the record for the oldest person to make a hole-in-one twice during one round.[^cite_note-14]

During the second round of the 1971 [Martini International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martini_International) tournament, held at the Royal Norwich Golf Club in England, [John Hudson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hudson_\(golfer\)) had two consecutive holes-in-one. Teeing off, using a 4-iron, at the par-three, 195-yard 11th hole, Hudson holed his tee shot for a hole-in-one. At the next hole, the downhill 311-yard, par-four 12th, and this time using a driver, he once again holed his tee shot, for another ace. This is believed to be the only time a player has scored holes-in-one at consecutive holes in a major professional tournament.[^cite_note-guinness-15][^cite_note-alliss-16]

Despite the relative rarity of holes-in-one, there have been a total of six in [Ryder Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryder_Cup) matches. [Peter Butler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Butler_\(golfer\)) scored the first in [1973](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973_Ryder_Cup) at [Muirfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muirfield) followed by a 20-year gap before [Nick Faldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Faldo) scored a hole-in-one in [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Ryder_Cup). [Two years later](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Ryder_Cup), [Costantino Rocca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costantino_Rocca) and [Howard Clark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Clark_\(golfer\)) both scored holes-in-one before an 11-year gap to [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Ryder_Cup) saw [Paul Casey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Casey) and [Scott Verplank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Verplank) both hole out in one on the 14th hole.[^cite_note-17]

On August 11, 2016, [Justin Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Rose) shot a hole-in-one during the first round of the [golf tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golf_at_the_2016_Summer_Olympics) of the [2016 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Summer_Olympics) in Rio de Janeiro, which is considered to be the first in Olympic history. For the 189 yards par-3 hole, he used a 7-iron.[^cite_note-18]

## Traditions

It is traditional for a player who has scored a hole-in-one to buy a round of drinks for everyone at the clubhouse bar.[^cite_note-19]

### Competitions

Occasionally special events host a hole in one contest, where prizes as expensive as a new car, or cash awards sometimes reaching \$4 million are offered if a contestant records a hole in one.[^cite_note-20] Usually such expensive prizes are backed by an [insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurance) company who offers [prize indemnification services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prize_indemnity_insurance). [Actuaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actuary) at such companies have calculated the chance of an average golfer making a hole in one at approximately 12,500 to 1, and the odds of a tour professional at 2,500 to 1.[^cite_note-odds-3]
