**Gush Etzion Junction** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): צומת גוש עציון, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hebrew): *Tzomet HaGush Etzion*), also known simply as **Gush Junction**, is a 120-dunam (0.12 km<sup>2</sup>; 0.046 sq mi) business, commercial and tourism center in the southern [West Bank](33209.md), which serves as the entry point to the [Gush Etzion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gush_Etzion) bloc of settlements. It is administered by the [Gush Etzion Regional Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gush_Etzion_Regional_Council).&#x20;

Long known as a "congenial meeting spot for Israelis and Palestinians," in [the autumn of 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%932016_wave_of_violence_in_Israeli-Palestinian_conflict) the junction was the site of about ten Palestinian attacks against Israelis.[^cite_note-KershnerJunction-1][^cite_note-attack-2]

## Geography

Gush Etzion Junction is located in the northern [Judean Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judean_Hills) at about 950 metres (3,120 ft) above sea level. The junction is a 25-metre (82 ft) diameter [roundabout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roundabout) at the intersection of [Route 60](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Route_60_\(Israel\)) and Route 367. Nearby communities include [Efrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Efrat_\(Israeli_settlement\)), [Elazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elazar,_Gush_Etzion), [Alon Shvut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alon_Shvut), [Kfar Etzion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kfar_Etzion), [Migdal Oz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migdal_Oz) and [Beit Fajjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_Fajjar). It is a 15-minute drive to the southern [Jerusalem](16043.md) neighborhood of [Gilo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilo).[^cite_note-OU-3]

## History

In 1926, Zichron David purchased lands that included the area of this junction,[^cite_note-4] with the aim of establishing a Jewish settlement on the [Jerusalem-Hebron highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highway_60_\(Israel%E2%80%93Palestine\)).[^cite_note-5]

In 1933, Shmuel Zvi Holtzman purchased the place from Zichron David.[^cite_note-6] He then established a clinic at this junction for both Jewish and Arab residents living in the area to maintain good neighborly relations.[^cite_note-7]

## Establishments

Adjacent to the junction on the northwest are a [plant nursery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant_nursery), a shop selling snacks, cigarettes and lottery tickets and The Gush Etzion Winery and its [mehadrin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosher) dairy/fish restaurant, BaYekev.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

On the southwest are a gas station,[^cite_note-tour-10] an automotive repair shop, an electronics store, a Judaica center[^cite_note-OU-3] and a [Rami Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rami_Levy_Hashikma_Marketing) discount supermarket.[^cite_note-11] Attached to the supermarket is a hamburger joint, a [pizzeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza) and a discount clothing store, all part of the Rami Levi group. In 2015 a competing chain of supermarkets, [Shufersal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shufersal) opened a store.[^cite_note-KershnerJunction-1]

In 2007, [Rami Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rami_Levy_Hashikma_Marketing) had begun construction of [Harim Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harim_Mall?action=edit\&redlink=1), a two-story shopping mall as an extension to the south side of the supermarket and above it.[^cite_note-12] This mall opened in late 2015.[^cite_note-13]

A visitors' center is located four kilometres (2.5 mi) to the west in [Kibbutz Kfar Etzion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kfar_Etzion)[^cite_note-tour-10] as is the mehadrin dairy/fish restaurant, Gavna.[^cite_note-14]

## Attacks

The hitchhiking post at the junction has been the site of a number of shootings and stabbings, as well as several failed attempts at attacks.

### Attacks

- **October 2005**: Three killed, three injured in a [shooting attack at the junction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Gush_Etzion_junction_shooting).

  [^cite_note-15]

  [^cite_note-stab-16]

- **January 2006**: A Palestinian man stabs an Israeli man and girl; the attacker is shot and injured by an off-duty police officer.

  [^cite_note-stab-16]

- **December 2009**: A woman is stabbed in the back by a Palestinian man.

  [^cite_note-attack-2]

  [^cite_note-17]

- **November 2015**: [2015 Gush Etzion Junction shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Gush_Etzion_Junction_shooting) on November 19, 2015, a Palestinian man shot and killed 3 people (including American student, Ezra Schwartz, and Alon Shvut resident Rabbi Yaakov Don) and wounded 5. On November 23, 2015, A Palestinian man stabbed and killed a 21-year-old student Hadar Buchris. He was shot and killed by security forces at the scene.

  [^cite_note-18]

  [^cite_note-KershnerJunction-1]

  &#x20;In response, the government is planning bypass roads to separate Israeli vehicles from Palestinian vehicles at this junction.

  [^cite_note-RudorenBypass-19]

- **September 2018**: A Palestinian teenager, Khalil Jabarin, 17, from Yatta fatally stabbed a 45-year-old Israeli-American man who lived in Efrat, [Ari Fuld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Ari_Fuld), at a shopping mall near the Gush Etzion junction.

  [^cite_note-20]

### Attempted attacks

- **November 2010**: A Palestinian man attempted to stab Israeli civilians at the junction and was arrested by the [IDF](39237.md).

  [^cite_note-21]

- **January 2011**: Shots were fired in the area; there were no injuries.

  [^cite_note-22]

- **October 2015**: A Palestinian man tried to ram into Israelis waiting at a bus stop but hit the concrete blocks placed there to stop such attacks. A 20-year-old IDF soldier and a 21-year-old Israeli civilian were lightly wounded. He exited his car with a knife drawn and tried to stab his victims and was shot and killed.

  [^cite_note-23]

- **March 2016** On March 15, 2016, an Arab woman described by authorities as a terrorist was injured after she drove her car to attack a bus shelter, but lost control as IDF soldiers shot into her car, and crashed into [bollard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bollard_\(traffic\)) poles protecting the bus stop before she could hurt anyone. She was immediately apprehended and taken to hospital for treatment of her injuries.

  [^cite_note-24]
