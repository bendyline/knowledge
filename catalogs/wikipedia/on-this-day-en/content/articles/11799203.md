# Education in Tokyo

Various schools and universities serve [Tokyo](30057.md), Japan.

## Primary and secondary schools

See also: [List of high schools in Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_high_schools_in_Tokyo), [List of junior high schools in Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_junior_high_schools_in_Tokyo), and [List of elementary schools in Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_elementary_schools_in_Tokyo)

Publicly run kindergartens, [elementary schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_school) (years 1 through 6), and [junior high schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_high_school) (7 through 9) are operated by local wards or municipal offices. Public [high schools](554992.md) in Tokyo are run by the [Tokyo Metropolitan Government Board of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metropolitan_Government_Board_of_Education) and are called "Metropolitan High Schools". Tokyo also has many private schools from kindergarten through high school.[^cite_note-1]

## Tertiary education

[Tokyo](30057.md) is home to many public and private universities, including the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo), the most prestigious university in Japan.
