# List of first overall NBA draft picks



[LeBron James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LeBron_James), a [high school draftee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_high_school_draftees), was drafted first overall by the [Cleveland Cavaliers](72868.md) at the [2003 NBA draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_NBA_draft) as one of the most anticipated prospects of all time.

The first overall pick in the [National Basketball Association](22093.md) (NBA) is the player who is selected first among all eligible draftees by a team during [the league's annual draft](835946.md). The first pick is awarded to the team that wins the [NBA draft lottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_draft_lottery); in most cases, that team had a losing record in the previous [season](22093.md#Regular_season). The NBA team that garners the top overall draft pick selection generates significant media attention,[^cite_note-1] as does the respective player who eventually gets selected with that first pick.

Eleven first picks have won the [NBA Most Valuable Player Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Most_Valuable_Player_Award): [Oscar Robertson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Robertson), [Kareem Abdul-Jabbar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kareem_Abdul-Jabbar) (record six-time winner), [Bill Walton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Walton), [Magic Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Johnson) (three-time winner), [Hakeem Olajuwon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakeem_Olajuwon), [David Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Robinson), [Shaquille O'Neal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaquille_O'Neal), [Allen Iverson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_Iverson), [Tim Duncan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Duncan) (two-time winner), [LeBron James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LeBron_James) (four-time winner), and [Derrick Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrick_Rose) (youngest winner).

China's [Yao Ming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yao_Ming) (2002), Italy's [Andrea Bargnani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Bargnani) (2006), and France's [Victor Wembanyama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Wembanyama) (2023) and [Zaccharie Risacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaccharie_Risacher) (2024) are the only players without competitive experience in the United States to be drafted first overall. Eleven other [international players](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_foreign_NBA_players) with U.S. [college](768856.md) experience have been drafted first overall – [Mychal Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mychal_Thompson) ([Bahamas](3451.md)) in 1978, Olajuwon ([Nigeria](21383.md)) in 1984, [Patrick Ewing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Ewing) ([Jamaica](15660.md)) in 1985, Duncan ([U.S. Virgin Islands](32135.md)) in 1997, [Michael Olowokandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Olowokandi) (Nigeria) in 1998, [Andrew Bogut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Bogut) (Australia) in 2005, [Kyrie Irving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrie_Irving) (Australia) in 2011, [Anthony Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Bennett_\(basketball\)) (Canada) in 2013, [Andrew Wiggins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Wiggins) (Canada) in 2014, [Ben Simmons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Simmons) (Australia) in 2016, and [Deandre Ayton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deandre_Ayton) (Bahamas) in 2018. Duncan is an American citizen, but is considered an "international" player by the NBA because he was not born in one of the [fifty states](18618239.md) or [the District of Columbia](108956.md).[^cite_note-2] Ewing[^cite_note-3] had Jamaican-American [dual citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_citizenship) when he was drafted, and Irving[^cite_note-4] and Simmons[^cite_note-5] had Australian-American dual citizenship when they were drafted.

The drafts between 1947 and 1949 were held by the [Basketball Association of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_Association_of_America) (BAA). The Basketball Association of America became the National Basketball Association after absorbing teams from the [National Basketball League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Basketball_League_\(United_States\)) in the fall of 1949. Official NBA publications include the BAA Drafts as part of the NBA's draft history.

## First overall picks by NBA team

The [Washington Wizards](72858.md) have had the first overall pick a total of seven times, the most of any NBA team. This includes the Wizards time as the [Chicago Packers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Packers), [Chicago Zephyrs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Zephyrs), and [Baltimore Bullets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore_Bullets_\(1944%E2%80%931954\)). The [Pittsburgh Ironmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh_Ironmen), and [Providence Steamrollers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence_Steamrollers) are the only defunct franchises to have held a first overall pick. The [Denver Nuggets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver_Nuggets), [Indiana Pacers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_Pacers), [Memphis Grizzlies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis_Grizzlies), [Miami Heat](72850.md), [Oklahoma City Thunder](18256220.md), and [Utah Jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Jazz) are the only teams that have never had the first overall pick.
