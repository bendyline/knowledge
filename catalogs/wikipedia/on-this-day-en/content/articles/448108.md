**Zanjan province** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): استان زنجان)[^cite_note-6] is one of the 31 [provinces of Iran](448160.md). Its capital is the city of [Zanjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanjan,_Iran).[^cite_note-Zanjan_Province_Structure-7] It is a mountainous province with close to 22,000 km<sup>2</sup> of land placed in Iran's Region 3. Two-thirds of the people of the province live in the cities, mainly the capital and [Abhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abhar).

## History

In [Ptolemy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ptolemy)'s *Geography*, the city is referred to as Aganzana. It is said that the [Sasanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasanian_Empire) king [Ardashir I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardashir_I) reconstructed the city and called it Shahin. Later it was renamed Zangan, whose present name is the [Arabicised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic) form. Historically, Zanjan has also been called Khamseh, meaning "province with five tribes". Zanjan province incorporates areas of the former [Gerrus province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerrus_province).

### Former names

At least since the [Zand era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zand_Iran), Zanjan and its surrounding areas were called Khamseh. In a book named *Mojmal al-Tawarikh-e Golestaneh*, while discussing the events of the [Karim Khan Zand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karim_Khan_Zand) era, it mentions the Mahal-e Khamseh (literally, "Khamseh areas") in reference to the regions of Zanjan province.[^cite_note-8] The use of Khamseh for Zanjan could precede the Zand era, since following [Nader Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nader_Shah)'s death and the collapse of the great unification of the [Afshar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afsharid_dynasty) tribes, the tribe of Afshar-e Khamseh returned to the Mahal-e Khamseh. The tribe's name, therefore, had already contained the suffix of "Khamseh" since the era of Nader Shah, and possibly before they had left the Zanjan area to accompany Nader. It can be concluded that at least since the time of Nader and the [Afsharids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afsharid_dynasty), the area was called Khamseh.[^cite_note-9]

It has been said that when [Karim Khan Zand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karim_Khan_Zand) left [Ardabil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardabil) and [Khalkhal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalkhal,_Iran) to suppress [Zaki Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaki_Khan_Zand) in [Kashan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashan), he entered Zanjan, and in [Abhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abhar) he delegated the governorship of three areas of Mahal-e Khamseh to Zolfagharkhan-e Sultan Irluy-e Afshar. This is another indication of the area's having been named Khamseh at the time of the [Zands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zand_dynasty).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

During the [Qajar era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qajar_Iran), Zanjan province was also known as Khamseh. In the book *Esker-ol-Tavarikh*, Etemad Al-Saltaneh discusses [Agha Mohammad Khan Qajar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agha_Mohammad_Khan_Qajar) just before the formation of the [Qajar dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qajar_dynasty), using the title of *Hakem-e Khamseh* (Governor of Khamseh).[^cite_note-12] In addition, [Mirza Abolhasan Khan Shirazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirza_Abolhassan_Khan_Ilchi) in his travelogue in the year 1229 Hijri (around 1814) mentions Khamseh.[^cite_note-13] Among European travellers, the first person who used the name Khamseh for the province where [Zanjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanjan,_Iran) was located was [Pierre Amédée Jaubert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Am%C3%A9d%C3%A9e_Jaubert), sent by [Napoleon Bonaparte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon_Bonaparte) to visit [Fath-Ali Shah Qajar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fath-Ali_Shah_Qajar)'s court, and in the year 1804 visited Zanjan city.[^cite_note-14]

## Demographics

### Ethnicity

[Azerbaijanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijanis) are the main ethnic group in the province followed by [Tats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tat_people_\(Iran\)).[^cite_note-auto-4] As in every other region of Iran, the *[lingua franca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingua_franca)* is [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language).

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the province's population was 942,818 in 235,771 households.[^cite_note-2006_Zanjan_Province-15] The following census in 2011 counted 1,015,734 inhabitants living in 286,806 households.[^cite_note-2011_Zanjan_Province-16] The 2016 census measured the population of the province as 1,057,461 in 321,983 households.[^cite_note-2016_Zanjan_Province-3]

## Geography



Landscape in Zanjan province, Tarom

Zanjan has an area of 22,164 km<sup>2</sup>, occupying 1.34% of Iran's territory. The average population density in Zanjan is 47 people per km<sup>2</sup>. In the northwest of Iran, Zanjan shares borders with seven provinces: [East Azerbaijan](530348.md), [West Azerbaijan](47567329.md), [Hamadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamadan_province), [Kurdistan](448112.md), [Gilan](210689.md), [Qazvin](448087.md), and [Ardabil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardabil_province). The Zanjan River is the only major river in the region.[^cite_note-Britannica-19]

### Climate

Zanjan has a [highland climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_climate) characterized by cold, snowy weather in the mountains and moderate climate in the plains in wintertime. In the summers, the weather is warm. The average maximum temperature of Zanjan is around 27 °C, whereas the average minimum temperature stands at −19 °C. Meanwhile, the temperature rises to 32 °C on hot days; it drops to −27 °C on cold days.[^cite_note-Britannica-19]

The average annual rainfall in the first month of spring is 72 mm, while in the second month of summer, it is 3.6 mm. The rate of humidity in the morning averages 74% and at noon 43%.[^cite_note-Britannica-19]

## Economy

Zanjan province's economy benefits from its location connecting central Iran to the northwestern provinces. The railway and highway that connect Iran's capital city [Tehran](57654.md) to [Tabriz](439791.md) and Turkey pass through the province.

Agriculture is the principal occupation, and crops include rice, corn (maize), oilseeds, fruits, and potatoes. Poultry, cattle, and sheep are raised.[^cite_note-Britannica-19] Zanjan is known regionally for its seedless grapes. Manufactured product includes bricks, cement, milled rice, and carpets. Chromium, lead, and copper are mined.[^cite_note-Britannica-19] In the [scientific world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science), Zanjan is the home of [IASBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IASBS), one of the most productive research centers of the country.[^cite_note-20]

### Handicrafts

Zanjan is known for its handicrafts, such as knives, traditional sandals called *charoogh*, and *malileh* (a handcraft made with silver wires). Zanjani artists produce many objects, like decorative dishes and their special covers, as well as silver jewelry. In ancient times, Zanjan was known for its stainless and sharp knives, but the tradition has gradually become extinct since the introduction of Chinese knives that are cheaper and more skillfully made. Many villagers today are traditional carpet weavers.

## Higher education

The Institute for Advanced Studies in Basic Sciences [IASBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IASBS), in the city of [Zanjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanjan,_Iran), is one of Iran's most distinguished upper-level education and research centers in the [pure sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pure_science). Zanjan is home to the [University of Zanjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zanjan), [Zanjan University of Medical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanjan_University_of_Medical_Sciences), and [Islamic Azad University of Zanjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Azad_University_of_Zanjan).
