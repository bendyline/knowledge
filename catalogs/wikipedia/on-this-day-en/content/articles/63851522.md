**Cavite's 4th congressional district** is one of the eight [congressional districts of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congressional_districts_of_the_Philippines) in the province of [Cavite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavite). It has been represented in the [House of Representatives of the Philippines](293605.md) since 2010.[^cite_note-congleg-3] It is currently represented in the [20th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_Congress_of_the_Philippines) by [Jenny Barzaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenny_Barzaga) of the [National Unity Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Unity_Party_\(Philippines\)).

The district was officially referred to as [Dasmariñas's at-large congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_districts_of_Dasmari%C3%B1as) from its creation in 2009 until 2019.[^cite_note-ra11069-4]
