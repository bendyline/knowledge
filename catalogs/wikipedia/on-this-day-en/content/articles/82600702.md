The **Balen Shah cabinet** is the incumbent [council of ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Nepal) of [Nepal](171166.md) since 27 March 2026. It is headed by Prime Minister [Balendra Shah](70833999.md) and was formed following the [2026 general election](69672716.md) that saw his [Rastriya Swatantra Party](72312845.md) win in a [landslide victory](41053306.md). The election results were announced and submitted to the president on 19 March 2026, leading to the formation of the [7th House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7th_House_of_Representatives_\(Nepal\)). The cabinet, along with the [prime minister](2888818.md), was sworn in on 27 March 2026, eight days after the results were submitted.

## History

On April 9 2026, Minister [Dipak Kumar Sah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dipak_Kumar_Sah) was sacked for disciplinary breach.[^cite_note-1] On September 10 2026, Minister [Gauri Kumari Yadav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauri_Kumari_Yadav) resigned due to her controversial statement regarding gas industrialists.[^cite_note-2]
