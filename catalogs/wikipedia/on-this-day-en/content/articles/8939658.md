***Costa Serena*** is a [*Concordia*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concordia-class_cruise_ship) [cruise ship](314855.md) for the Italian [cruise line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruise_line), [Costa Crociere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Crociere). The name [Serena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serena_\(disambiguation\)) was intended to symbolize harmony and serenity.

*Costa Serena* was constructed by [Fincantieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fincantieri) at [Sestri Ponente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sestri_Ponente). She had an older sister ship, [*Costa Concordia*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Concordia), launched in 2005. The ship, along with her three active sisters, was the largest and longest in the [Costa Crociere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Crociere) fleet until being surpassed by the [*Costa Diadema*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Diadema) in 2014. Two sister ships [*Costa Pacifica*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Pacifica) and [*Costa Favolosa*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Favolosa) were launched in 2009 and 2011 with [*Costa Fascinosa*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Fascinosa) launched in 2012. *Costa Serena*'s godmother is [Marion Cotillard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Cotillard).[^cite_note-Fincantieri-4] She is the oldest surviving ship of *Concordia* class after the [*Costa Concordia* disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Concordia_disaster).

## Construction and career

19 May 2007 marked the day of *Costa Serena*'s inaugural event. The event was held in [Marseille](40888948.md), France, and featured fireworks and a laser show.[^cite_note-Inaugural1-5] At the same time as the actual inauguration, Costa held an inaugural event in the virtual world of *[Second Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Life)*.[^cite_note-Inaugural2-6]

In 2015, she re-positioned to Shanghai to sail year-round cruises from China visiting ports in Japan and South Korea. These sailings are catered for Chinese guests and are only bookable through Chinese travel agencies. *Costa Serena* currently sails in Asia [Shanghai](27643.md) year-round from various homeports, ranging from Malaysia, Taiwan, Hong Kong and South Korea.

### 2020 COVID-19 outbreak

Main article: [COVID-19 pandemic on cruise ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_on_cruise_ships)

Fifteen passengers aboard *Costa Serena* on 24 January were suspected to have SARS-CoV-2.[^cite_note-serena.safety-7] The ship arrived at its destination, [Tianjin](50821.md), [China](5405.md), on 25 January.[^cite_note-serena.safety-7] Quarantine officials boarded the ship to screen all passengers and crew member, and found 17 people with fever.[^cite_note-serena.safety-7] All tests returned negative later that day, and everyone was ordered to disembark and have their photographs and temperatures taken as an additional precaution.[^cite_note-serena.safety-7]

### 2023 ship restarting

Chartered for the entire 2023/2024 season, *Costa Serena* set sail from the port of Leam Chabang for a series of 4/5 day cruises, before moving on to Japan and South Korea in June 2023, offering a series of short cruises dedicated to the local market.[^cite_note-8]

*Costa Serena* was scheduled to debut in the Indian market on November 4, 2023, and was then the largest ship in Indian waters.[^cite_note-9]

On 15 September 2026, she docked at [Nagoya](61720.md) to act as temporary accommodation for up to 5,000 participants in the [Asian Games](51790406.md) hosted by [Japan](15573.md).[^cite_note-10]

## In popular culture

In 2007 *Costa Serena* was used for the setting of the movie *[Natale in Crociera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natale_in_crociera)*.

In 2009 *Costa Serena* and her crew was featured in the six-episode [National Geographic Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Geographic_Channel) documentary series *Cruise Ship Diaries.*[^cite_note-NatGeoTV-11]
