The **Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries** (**MOF**; [Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 해양수산부) is a cabinet-level organization of the [government of South Korea](4389689.md).

## History

It takes overall responsibilities for maritime and fisheries sectors in general, ranging from the promotion of [maritime safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_safety) and [security](8111405.md), the [protection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_protection) of the [marine environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_habitats), the development of port and fishing ports, the research and development on polar issues to the management and sustainable use of [fishery resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishery_resources?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the promotion of [marine leisure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_leisure?action=edit\&redlink=1) activities. Its headquarters is located at 94 Dasom-2 ro, [Sejong City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sejong_City).[^cite_note-2] Before the merger in 2008, the **Ministry of Maritime Affairs and Fisheries** was located in 140-2 Kye-Dong, [Jongro-gu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jongno_District), [Seoul](19159283.md) City.[^cite_note-3]

MOF was established as part of a general cabinet reorganization in 1996. For the preceding 35 years, maritime functions had been divided among various departments. From 1955 to 1961, under the [First Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Republic_of_Korea), a Ministry of Marine Affairs existed, and the current ministry traces its origin to that body.

In 2008, with the merging of the [Ministry of Construction and Transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Land,_Infrastructure_and_Transport_\(South_Korea\)) and MOMAF, the [Ministry of Land, Transport and Maritime Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Land,_Infrastructure_and_Transport_\(South_Korea\)) was established. In 2013, the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries was reestablished with government reorganization.

In 2025, President [Lee Jae-myung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Jae-myung) ordered the ministry to transfer its headquarters to [Busan](68115.md).[^cite_note-4]
