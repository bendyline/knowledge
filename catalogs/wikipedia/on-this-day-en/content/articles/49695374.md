**Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani**[^cite_note-abc-1] ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): : محمد بن عبد الرحمن آل ثاني; : محمد بن عبد الرحمن آل ثاني; born 1 November 1980) is a Qatari diplomat, economist, and politician who has been the [Prime Minister of Qatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_prime_ministers_of_Qatar) since March 2023[^cite_note-\:0-2] and its [Minister of Foreign Affairs](35709864.md) since 2016.[^cite_note-dip-3] Additionally, he is the Chairman of the Qatar Fund for Development (QFFD) and is a member of Qatar's Supreme Council for Economic Affairs and Investments. He previously held the positions of Chairman of the [Qatar Investment Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_Investment_Authority) from 2018 to 2023 and [deputy prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Qatar) from 2017 to 2023.[^cite_note-abc-1][^cite_note-\:1-4]



Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani in 2017

## Early life and education

Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was born and grew up in Doha, Qatar. He is a member of the Qatari royal family as a member of the [House of Thani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Thani).[^cite_note-5] In 2003, he graduated with a [bachelor's degree](188874.md) in economics and business administration from the College of Business and Economics at [Qatar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_University).[^cite_note-ajf-6]

## Career

Al Thani started his career as an economic researcher at the Supreme Council for Family Affairs in 2003.[^cite_note-GCO_Bio-7] He worked as the same institution's director of economic affairs from 2005 until 2009.[^cite_note-WEF_Bio-8]

In 2009, he joined the Ministry of Business and Trade as the director of the Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SME) Support Project and Public-Private Partnerships department, where he helped establish Enterprise Qatar.[^cite_note-GCO_Bio-7][^cite_note-WEF_Bio-8] The following year, in 2010, he was appointed as the Secretary for Follow-up Affairs to the Personal Representative of the Emir at the Amiri Diwan, and also served as the deputy chairman of the Qatar Mining Company.[^cite_note-GCO_Bio-7][^cite_note-MOFA_Bio-9]

In 2011, Al Thani was appointed chairman of the SME Executive Committee as well as the Aspire-Katara Investment Company's board of directors. He was promoted to the rank of undersecretary in 2012.[^cite_note-GCO_Bio-7]

### Diplomatic and Ministerial Roles

Al Thani joined the foreign ministry in 2013, serving as the assistant foreign minister for international cooperation affairs.[^cite_note-MOFA_Bio-9][^cite_note-GCO_Bio-7] He was named chairman of the [Qatar Fund for Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_Fund_for_Development) (QFFD) in 2014 and served in that capacity until 2024.[^cite_note-GCO_Bio-7] In 2014, he was also appointed to the Supreme Council for Economic Affairs and Investment in Qatar.[^cite_note-GCO_Bio-7]

Al Thani was named [Minister of Foreign Affairs](35709864.md) on 27 January 2016.[^cite_note-MOFA_Bio-9] On 29 November 2017, he was appointed [Deputy Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Qatar).[^cite_note-MOFA_Bio-9] In 2018, he was named chairman of the [Qatar Investment Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_Investment_Authority) (QIA) board of directors, a role he completed in 2023.[^cite_note-GCO_Bio-7][^cite_note-MOFA_Bio-9]

Khalid bin Khalifa bin Abdulaziz Al Thani resigned on March 7, 2023, and Al Thani was sworn in as [Prime Minister of Qatar](3596637.md), while continuing to serve as the country's foreign minister.[^cite_note-MOFA_Bio-9][^cite_note-GCO_Bio-7]
