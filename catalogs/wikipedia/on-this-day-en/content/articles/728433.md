**Tungurahua** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Provincia del Tungurahua*, literally *Province of the Tungurahua*; pronounced [\[tuŋɡuˈɾawa\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is one of the twenty-four [provinces of Ecuador](576127.md). Its capital is [Ambato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambato,_Ecuador). The province takes its name from the [Tungurahua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tungurahua) volcano, which is located within the boundaries of the provinces.

## Population

In 2011, Tungurahua had an estimated population of 581,389.[^cite_note-3] Ethnic groups as of the [Ecuadorian census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_census) of 2010:[^cite_note-4]

- [Mestizo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestizo) 82.1%

- [Indigenous](18429324.md) 12.4%

- [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Latin_American) 3.4%

- [Afro-Ecuadorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Ecuadorian) 1.4%

- [Montubio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montubio) 0.5%

- Other 0.1%

## Climate

The province has a dry, temperate climate. Like all mountainous areas, the region experiences the phenomenon known as [microclimates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microclimate), in which small portions of the province have drastically different conditions from others due to winds and area pressure.

Generally though, Tungurahua experiences temperatures between 14 and 17 degree Celsius in the day-time, with cooler nights. At higher altitudes, conditions are much colder. Despite the area being near the [Equator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equator), mountains such as [Carihuayrazo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carihuayrazo) and [Chimborazo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chimborazo_\(volcano\)) are covered in snow for much of the year.[^cite_note-5]

## Geography

The province is very mountainous, containing the [Tungurahua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tungurahua) volcano near [Baños](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ba%C3%B1os_Canton), as well as bordering the [Carihuayrazo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carihuayrazo) and [Chimborazo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chimborazo_\(volcano\)) volcanoes to the south. [Baños](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ba%C3%B1os_de_la_Agua_Santa) attracts the highest number of tourists. The principal river of the province is the Patate River, which flows to the east toward the Amazon Region.[^cite_note-6]
