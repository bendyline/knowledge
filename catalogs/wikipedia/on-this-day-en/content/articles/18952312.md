**Guantánamo** is the easternmost province of [Cuba](5042481.md). Its capital is also called [Guantánamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guant%C3%A1namo). Other towns include [Baracoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baracoa). The province has the only land border of the [United States Navy base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guantanamo_Bay_Naval_Base) at [Guantánamo Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guant%C3%A1namo_Bay,_Cuba).

## Overview

Guantánamo's architecture and culture is unlike the rest of Cuba. The province is only 80 kilometres (50 mi) away from [Haiti](13373.md) at its closest point, across the [Windward Passage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windward_Passage) (close enough to see lights on Haiti on a clear night). Guantánamo also has a high number of immigrants from [Jamaica](15660.md). Many buildings are comparable to those of the [French Quarter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Quarter) of [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans,_Louisiana) in the [United States state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_state) of [Louisiana](18130.md).

The Nipe-Sagua-Baracoa mountains dominate the province, dividing both [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) and [landscape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landscape). The northern coast, battered by prevailing winds, is the wettest part of the country, while the south, sheltered and dry, is the hottest. The north is characterized by [rainforests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_moist_forests), while the south is [arid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_cactus_scrub) and has many [cacti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cactus).
