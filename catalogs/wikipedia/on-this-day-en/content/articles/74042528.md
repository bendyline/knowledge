# Mining in Mexico

"Mexican silver" redirects here. For the 20th-century silver design movement, see [Mexican silver renaissance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_silver_renaissance).



Cananea Copper Mine, Sonora, Mexico

**Mining in Mexico** represented 2.4% of the nation's [gross domestic product](12594.md) in 2023 and employed 350,000 people in 2020. Mexico is the world's largest producer of silver and a globally significant producer of gold, copper and zinc. In 2020, Mexico produced the world's 12th largest volume of minerals by value.

Since 2018, Mexico's left-wing president has been taking a firmer stance towards mining companies and reforming mining regulations. President [López Obrador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Manuel_L%C3%B3pez_Obrador) was critical of (mostly foreign owned) mining companies' track record of environmental harm and tax avoidance.

## Economy

In 2023, mining in Mexico represented 2.4% of the nation's [gross domestic product](12594.md), and 8.2% of Mexico's industrial GDP. The mining sector employed 350,000 people in 2020, and generated US\$1.5 billion in direct government tax revenue, and an additional US\$1.84 billion of government revenue from exports of mined natural resources.[^cite_note-\:4-1]

Mexico is the world's largest producer of silver[^cite_note-\:3-2] and a globally significant producer of gold, copper and zinc.[^cite_note-\:4-1]

In 2020, Mexico produced the world's 12th largest volume of minerals, valued at US\$17.8 billion.[^cite_note-3]

## Politics

Mapping and consultations about mining in Mexico are led the by the [Mexican Geological Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Geological_Survey) government agency.[^cite_note-\:02-4]

In January 2013, 500 activists met in [Capulálpam de Méndez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capulalpam_de_M%C3%A9ndez), Oaxaca at the *¡Sí a la vida! ¡No a la minera!* ([English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) *Yes to life! No to mining!*) event. The event was undertaken to coordinate anti-mining efforts, perceived by the activists as harmful to their lives.[^cite_note-5]

Mexico's left-wing[^cite_note-\:3-2] president [Andrés Manuel López Obrador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Manuel_L%C3%B3pez_Obrador) halted issuing any new mining licences, and did not consider mining an essential business permitted to operate during the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Mexico).[^cite_note-\:4-1] López Obrador has been critical of corporate tax avoidance and environmental damage, and has both reformed mining regulations and increased state control of mining operations. The president nationalised the nation's lithium reserves in 2022.[^cite_note-\:3-2]

In 2023, the Canadian government noted the Mexican's government rapid action to improve the conditions of Mexican miners, after the Mexican government changed 20,000 [collective bargaining agreements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collective_bargaining) it perceived to be favouring mine owners at the expense of workers.[^cite_note-6]

## Companies

74% of mining concessions in Mexico are owned by Canadian companies.[^cite_note-7] Significant and notable mining companies include [Grupo Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grupo_M%C3%A9xico), [Newmont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newmont), Southern Silver Exploration,[^cite_note-\:4-1] [Ganfeng Lithium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganfeng_Lithium), [Fortuna Silver Mines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortuna_Silver_Mines), [Blackfire Exploration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackfire_Exploration),[^cite_note-\:3-2] [Alamos Gold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alamos_Gold),[^cite_note-\:22-8] [Aurcana Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurcana_Corporation),[^cite_note-globeandmail-9] [Hochschild Mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hochschild_Mining),[^cite_note-\:5-10] and [Excellon Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excellon_Resources).[^cite_note-\:14-11]

### Alamos Gold

Alamos Gold is a Canadian mining company that own the [Mulatos gold mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulatos_gold_mine) and the [El Chanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Chanate) defunct gold mines both [Sonora](83750.md).[^cite_note-\:13-12][^cite_note-\:22-8]

### Aurcana Corporation

Aurcana Corporation operated the [La Negra mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Negra_mine), a silver, copper, zinc and lead mine in [Querétaro](222250.md).[^cite_note-globeandmail-9]

### Blackfire Exploration

Blackfire Exploration is a Canadian firm that owns the [La Revancha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Revancha_mine) [baryte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baryte) mine in [Chicomuselo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicomuselo), in [Chiapas](6787.md).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-\:12-14] The mine opened in 2007[^cite_note-\:0-15] with the local community divided between support and opposition.[^cite_note-\:12-14][^cite_note-\:0-15] The mine was closed by authorities in 2009 after the assassination of anti-mining activist [Mariano Abarca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariano_Abarca).[^cite_note-\:0-15][^cite_note-\:2-16] Abarca had previously been assaulted by Blackfire Exploration staff.[^cite_note-17] In 2011, the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police](69080.md) raided Blackfire Exploration's offices while investigating the payment of a bribe to a Chiapas state mayor. Three staff were arrested during the raid.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

### Excellon Resources



Baryte crushing and jig plant

Canadian owner of [La Platosa mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Platosa_mine)[^cite_note-\:32-20] and [La Negra silver mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Negra_mine).[^cite_note-\:03-21]

### Fortuna Silver Mines

Fortuna Silver Mines is a Canadian mining company that has operated in southern Mexico since 2011.[^cite_note-\:3-2]

### Ganfeng Lithium

Gangfend Lithium is a mexican company that bought the a lithium mine in [Sonora](83750.md) in 2021, before the nation nationalised its lithium reserves.[^cite_note-\:3-2]

### Grupo Mexico

Grupo Mexico owns 14 mines that produce copper, lead, zinc and silver.[^cite_note-\:4-1]

### Hochschild Mining

Hochschild Mining opened the Moris open-pit gold and silver mine in [Chihuahua](23962301.md) in 2007.[^cite_note-\:5-10]

### Newmont

Newmont is an American[^cite_note-22] mining company that operates the nation's largest gold mine, the [Peñasquito mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pe%C3%B1asquito_Mine), also notable for being the nation's second largest silver mine and a significant source of zinc and lead.[^cite_note-\:4-1]

### Southern Silver Exploration

Southern Silver Exploration own the [Cerro Las Minitas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerro_Las_Minitas?action=edit\&redlink=1) prospect in [Durango](222234.md) expected to produce significant quantities of silver, lead, zinc, copper and gold. The company owns the Velardeña mine in Durango and San Martin mine in [Querétaro City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quer%C3%A9taro_City), both producing of gold, silver, zinc and lead.[^cite_note-\:4-1]
