The **high courts** in [Malaysia](3607937.md) are the third-highest courts in the hierarchy of [courts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courts_of_Malaysia), after the [Federal Court](14222422.md) and the [Court of Appeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Appeal_\(Malaysia\)). Article 121 of the [Constitution of Malaysia](534791.md) provides that there shall be two high courts of co-ordinate jurisdiction—the **High Court in Malaya** and the **High Court in Sabah and Sarawak** (before 1994, the **High Court in Borneo**). Before 1969, the **High Court in Singapore** was also part of the Malaysian courts system (see [Law of Singapore](1165902.md)).

The High Court in Malaya has its principal registry in [Kuala Lumpur](16854.md), with other registries to be found in all states in [Peninsular Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peninsular_Malaysia), while the High Court in Sabah and Sarawak has its principal registry in Kuching, with other registries elsewhere in Sabah and Sarawak. There are in total 22 high court registries across all 13 states in Malaysia.[^cite_note-1] The two High Courts also travel on [circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_court) to other smaller towns.

The two high courts, the Court of Appeal and the Federal Court are classified as superior courts, while the magistrates' courts and the sessions courts are classified as the subordinate courts. The high courts function both as a court of [original jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Original_jurisdiction) as well as an [appellate court](643.md), and are each headed by a chief judge (before 1994, chief justice). The chief judges of Malaya and Sabah and Sarawak are the third and fourth highest positions in Malaysian judiciary after the [Chief Justice of the Federal Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Justice_of_Malaysia) (before 1994, the [Lord President of the Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_President_of_the_Supreme_Court)) and the [President of the Court of Appeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Court_of_Appeal_of_Malaysia).[^cite_note-2]

## High court registries in Malaysia

All high court registries and the subordinate courts found in [Peninsular Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peninsular_Malaysia) including the courts in the Federal Territories of Kuala Lumpur and Putrajaya are under the jurisdiction of the High Court in Malaya. Similarly, all courts found in [East Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Malaysia) are under the jurisdiction of the High Court in Sabah and Sarawak including the courts in the Federal Territory of Labuan.
