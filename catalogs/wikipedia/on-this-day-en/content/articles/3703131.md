**Chloé** (French pronunciation: [\[klɔe\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French)) is a French luxury [fashion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fashion) house founded in 1952 by [Gaby Aghion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaby_Aghion). In 1953, Aghion joined forces with Jacques Lenoir,[^cite_note-1] formally managing the business side of the brand, allowing Aghion to purely pursue the creative growth of Chloé. Its headquarters are located in Paris.&#x20;

The house is owned by luxury brands holding company [Richemont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richemont) Group. Chloé has been worn by many celebrities, including [Marion Cotillard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Cotillard), [Sienna Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sienna_Miller), [Madonna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madonna_\(entertainer\)), [January Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_Jones), [Maggie Gyllenhaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maggie_Gyllenhaal), [Kirsten Dunst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirsten_Dunst), [Cameron Diaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Diaz), [Emma Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Stone), [Clémence Poésy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cl%C3%A9mence_Po%C3%A9sy) and [Katie Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Holmes).[^cite_note-2]

The brand is characterized by a youthful and bohemian aesthetic, and has produced several successful fragrances. As of October 2023, Chemena Kamali is its [creative director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_director).[^cite_note-3]

## History

Chloé was founded in 1952 by Egyptian-born [Gaby Aghion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaby_Aghion), who sought to offer luxury *[prêt-à-porter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pr%C3%AAt-%C3%A0-porter)* (ready-to-wear)—a concept new at that time.[^cite_note-4] Gaby Aghion and her partner Jacques Lenoir continued to run the house until 1985, when Chloé was bought by [Alfred Dunhill Ltd.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Dunhill_Ltd.) (now part of [Richemont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richemont)).

- 1952: Chloé is founded by [Gaby Aghion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaby_Aghion), a Parisian of Egyptian-Jewish origin. She and her partner, Jacques Lenoir, were among the first to become aware of the rising demand for collections that could merge the strict requirements of haute couture and those of ready-to-wear.

- 1956: The first collection is introduced at [Café de Flore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caf%C3%A9_de_Flore), one of their favorite cafes and the meeting place of artists in Paris. The collection was designed by Gaby Aghion and made by a first assistant at Lelong. Gaby Aghion and Jacques Lenoir start hiring young talented designers, who would eventually make a name of their own: Christiana Bailly, [Michèle Rosier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mich%C3%A8le_Rosier), [Maxime de la Falaise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxime_de_la_Falaise), [Graziella Fontana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graziella_Fontana), Tan Giudicelli, Guy Paulin, Carlos Rodriguez.

- 1966: [Karl Lagerfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Lagerfeld) is the main designer and Chloé becomes one of the symbolic brands of the 1970s. Among its customers : [Jackie Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacqueline_Kennedy_Onassis), [Brigitte Bardot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigitte_Bardot), [Maria Callas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Callas) and [Grace Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grace_Kelly).

- 1971: The first Chloé boutique opens at the 3 rue Gribeauval in Paris.

- 1985: The company is acquired by the [Richemont group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richemont).

- 1980s: Chloé keeps working with promising and eventually famous artistic directors: [Martine Sitbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martine_Sitbon) in 1988, [Karl Lagerfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Lagerfeld) in 1992.

- 2001: [Kirsten Dunst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirsten_Dunst), [Natalie Portman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Portman), and [Lou Doillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lou_Doillon) become customers.

- 2002: Chloé launches a line of bags, small leather goods and shoes. Among Phoebe Philo’s notable achievements at the brand was introducing the Paddington bag, a leather handbag that became one of the first [“It” bags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It_bag).

## *See by Chloé*

Established as a [diffusion line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffusion_line) in 2001, See by Chloé at its peak represented about 10 percent of Chloé's total business. Designers included Laure de Sade (2011–2012).[^cite_note-25] In 2022, the brand announced plans to phase out its See by Chloé business over the following three years.[^cite_note-26] For the time of the brand's existence, Italy’s SINV SpA held the license for production of See by Chloé ready-to-wear, and also handled distribution for all but certain European markets.[^cite_note-27] Bags, shoes and jewelry under the label were produced and distributed in house.[^cite_note-28]

## Other products

### Beauty products

In 1973, Chloé introduced its first scent, Chloé Classique.[^cite_note-29] With the purchase of Unilever Cosmetics International in 2005, [Coty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coty) acquired the perfume license for Chloé, among other brands.[^cite_note-30] In 2012, Coty launched a See by Chloé fragrance.[^cite_note-31]

### Eyewear

For corrective eyewear frames and sunglasses, Chloé has had licensing agreements with [Marcolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcolin) (from 1998),[^cite_note-32] Marchon Eyewear (2012–2019)[^cite_note-33] and [Kering Eyewear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kering) (since 2020).[^cite_note-34]

## Advertising campaigns

For its advertising campaigns, Chloé has been working with photographers like [Tom Munro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Munro) (1998), [Taryn Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taryn_Simon) (2000–2001),[^cite_note-35] [Craig McDean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_McDean) (2003),[^cite_note-36] [Terry Richardson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Richardson) (2004),[^cite_note-37] [Inez van Lamsweerde and Vinoodh Matadin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inez_van_Lamsweerde_and_Vinoodh_Matadin) (2006, 2010),[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39] [Ryan McGinley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_McGinley) (2018),[^cite_note-40] [Steven Meisel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Meisel) (2019),[^cite_note-41] [Mario Sorrenti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Sorrenti) (2021)[^cite_note-42] and [David Sims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Sims_\(photographer\)) (2024, 2025).[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45] The campaigns have featured actresses [Chloë Sevigny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlo%C3%AB_Sevigny) (2008–2010), [Clémence Poésy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cl%C3%A9mence_Po%C3%A9sy) (2008–2010, 2014),[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47] and [Ariane Labed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariane_Labed) (2018).[^cite_note-48]

## Locations

Chloé's headquarters are located at Avenue Percier, [8th arrondissement of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_arrondissement_of_Paris). The regional offices are in New York, Tokyo, Shanghai, Hong Kong and Dubai.

Chloé has boutiques in Toronto, Bal Harbour, Costa Mesa, Las Vegas, New York City, Beijing (2), Sydney, Shanghai (2), Shenzhen, Hong Kong (2), Singapore, Kaohsiung, Taipei (3), Bangkok, Bandung, Paris, Munich, Porto Cervo, Moscow (2), Marbella, Istanbul, London, Tokyo (2), Nagoya, Salmiya, Beirut, Doha, Seoul, Zurich, Dubai (2) and Kuwait.

In 2017, Chloé inaugurated Maison Chloé, its new multifunctional cultural space located at 28 Rue de La Baume, which houses the brand's showrooms, VIP fitting rooms and working archives.[^cite_note-49]

## Sponsoring

Since 2012, Chloé has been sponsoring the annual Prix Chloé at the Festival de Mode de Hyères. In 2016, the brand also sponsored the [Port Eliot Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Eliot).[^cite_note-50]
