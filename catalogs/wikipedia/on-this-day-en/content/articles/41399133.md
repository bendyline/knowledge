The **Ministry of Public Security**[^cite_note-1] ([Sinhala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhala_language): මහජන ආරක්ෂක අමාත්‍යාංශය *Mahajana Arakshaka Amathyanshaya*; [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language): பொதுமக்கள் பாதுகாப்பு அமைச்சு) is a [cabinet](12626831.md) [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ministries_of_Sri_Lanka) of the [Government of Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Sri_Lanka) responsible for law and order. The ministry is responsible for formulating and implementing national policy on law and order and other subjects which come under its purview.[^cite_note-2] The ministry manages the country's [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka_Police_Service). The current Minister of Public Security is [Ananda Wijepala](78387235.md).[^cite_note-minister-3] The ministry's [secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permanent_Secretary) is [Ravi Seneviratne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravi_Seneviratne?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-4]

## History

Since independence in 1948 the Sri Lankan police had come under the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(Ceylon\)). The [Lessons Learnt and Reconciliation Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lessons_Learnt_and_Reconciliation_Commission) had recommended that policing be transferred to a separate ministry.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The Ministry of Law and Order was established on 16 August 2013 to manage policing in the country.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]
