# 2026 May Day protests

|  | This article needs to be **updated**. Please help update this article to reflect recent events or newly available information. *(May 2026)* |
| --- | --- |



[Fakir Mahbub Anam Swapan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fakir_Mahbub_Anam_Swapan) speaking in [Tangail](1997520.md) at an International Workers' Day event

The **2026 May Day protests** were held internationally on May 1, 2026, as part of [International Workers' Day](39353050.md). Demonstrations were held in various countries, including [Colombia](5222.md), [Cuba](5042481.md), [France](5843419.md), [Germany](11867.md), [United States](3434750.md), [Thailand](30128.md), [Turkey](11125639.md), [Pakistan](23235.md) and [Venezuela](32374.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## Protests

### Cuba

Workers in Cuba celebrated [International Worker's Day](39353050.md) and protested the Cuban government and the [United States' blockcade against Venezuela](81814844.md).[^cite_note-3]

### Philippines

A [Labour Day](18051.md) rally organized by labour group [Kilusang Mayo Uno](5308660.md) was held in [Manila](184334.md), [Philippines](23440.md). During the rally, protesters attempted to storm the [U.S. Embassy](10576426.md) to [demand](82544763.md) an end to the Iran war and clashed with police, injuring seven officers.[^cite_note-Philippine_Daily_Inquirer-4][^cite_note-5] Among the seven officers injured is a 26-year-old patrolman who had been punched in the head during the stand-off, resulting in a skull and wrist injury.[^cite_note-Philippine_Daily_Inquirer-4]

### United States



Flyer for a May Day protest in the United States by [Freedom Road Socialist Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_Road_Socialist_Organization)

See also: [Protests against Donald Trump](49760486.md) and [Protests against the second Trump administration](78306972.md)

Protests and demonstrations were held in the [United States](3434750.md) to call for peace, higher wages and better working conditions, and [against the Iran war](82544763.md) and the [global energy and fuel crisis](82827398.md) caused by the war.[^cite_note-Corbet-2026-6][^cite_note-7] An "economic blackout" was organized by a coalition of hundreds of organizations around the country, working under the banner of May Day Strong.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-Corbet-2026-6] Over 5,000 demonstrations have been held in the U.S against [president](24113.md) [Donald Trump](4848272.md) and [his administration](78298108.md), including the [Iran war](82533582.md) and [immigration policies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_U.S._immigration_enforcement_protests) including tactics by [ICE](216537.md).[^cite_note-9]

### Thailand

Labour groups marched to the [Government House of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_House_\(Thailand\)) to submit their nine demands, including a call to raise the [minimum wage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_wage) to 712 THB per day. [^cite_note-20]

### Turkey

See also: [2025–2026 Turkish protests](79502864.md)

[Police](20141676.md) arrested at least 570 people and use [tear gas](1202646.md) to disperse demonstrators in [Istanbul](3391396.md), access to [Taksim Square](631961.md) was blocked to prevent marches.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

### Pakistan

In [Lahore](125315.md), rallies were held under the [All Pakistan Federation of Trade Unions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Pakistan_Federation_of_Trade_Unions), with workers demanding wage increases, labour-law enforcement, workplace safety, and removal of recruitment bans. In [Islamabad](51153.md), The News reported PTUDC activists protesting for labour demands on May 1.[^cite_note-23]
