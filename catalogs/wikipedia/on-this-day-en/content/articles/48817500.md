**Kafr Maris** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): كفر مارس) is a [Syrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian) [village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village) located in [Kafr Takharim Nahiyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kafr_Takharim_Nahiyah) in [Harem District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harem_District), [Idlib](7445043.md). According to the [Syria Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bureau_of_Statistics_\(Syria\)) (CBS), **Kafr Maris** had a population of 350 in the 2004 census.[^cite_note-1] It is a [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze) village of the [Jabal al-Sumaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabal_al-Sumaq) region.[^cite_note-2] On 21 October 2025, several residents were killed and others wounded when a unidentified gunman on a motorbike opened fire on a bus near the village.[^cite_note-3]
