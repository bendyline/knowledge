**USS *Spruance* (DDG-111)** is an [*Arleigh Burke*-class](197341.md) ([Flight IIA](197341.md#Characteristics)) [Aegis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aegis_combat_system) [guided missile destroyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_missile_destroyer) in the [United States Navy](20518076.md). *Spruance* is the second ship to be named after Admiral [Raymond A. Spruance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_A._Spruance) (1886–1969), who commanded American naval forces at the Battles of [Midway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Midway) and the [Philippine Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Philippine_Sea).

## Ship history

### Construction

*Spruance* was built in [Bath, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bath,_Maine) at [Bath Iron Works](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bath_Iron_Works), at a cost of \$1 billion.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-Hoey2011-4] Her [keel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel) was laid down on 14 May 2009.[^cite_note-NNS090520-28-2] *Spruance* was the first of the U.S. Navy's destroyers to be fitted with the Gigabit Ethernet Data Multiplex System (GEDMS), manufactured by the [Boeing Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Company). GEDMS provides an [Internet Protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Protocol) (IP) based backbone for video and data services on the ship.[^cite_note-5] The [bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_\(ship\)) features touch screen controls and color readouts instead of gauges.[^cite_note-maiden-6]

At her christening on 5 June 2010, the principal address was delivered by Honorable [John Baldacci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Baldacci) of Maine, and the vessel was christened by Ellen Spruance Holscher as the ship's sponsor. Commander [Tate Westbrook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate_Westbrook) was the ship's first commanding officer.&#x20;

### Operational history

The completed ship left Bath on 1 September 2011 for her commissioning in [Key West, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key_West,_Florida) on 1 October 2011.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

&#x20;She sailed from [San Diego](28504.md) on her maiden deployment on 16 October 2013, heading for Asia under the command of Commander George Kessler who was succeeded by Commander Daniel Cobian.[^cite_note-maiden-6]&#x20;



*Spruance* sails with the carrier [*Izumo*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JS_Izumo), 28 July 2022.

In January 2024, the US Navy reinstalled ODIN Laser on *Spruance*. ODIN, a solid-state laser system, is designed primarily to counter and neutralize the sensors on UAS, effectively blinding them without destroying the aircraft. Technical aspects of the ODIN system include its ability to emit a high-intensity laser beam, targeting the optical sensors of enemy drones.[^cite_note-9]

On the morning of 27 September 2024, while transiting the Red Sea with the *Arleigh Burke*-class destroyer [USS *Stockdale*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Stockdale_\(DDG-106\)) and [*Freedom*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom-class_littoral_combat_ship) [littoral combat ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Littoral_combat_ship) [USS *Indianapolis*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Indianapolis_\(LCS-17\)), she was attacked by roughly two dozen missiles and drones launched by Iranian-backed [Houthi](17240332.md) rebels in Yemen. All missiles and drones were intercepted by the flotilla or missed their target outright.[^cite_note-10]

On 11 November 2024, following US attacks on Houthi bases in Yemen, the Houthis again attacked the *Spruance* and *Stockdale*, launching eight drones, five ballistic missiles, and three cruise missiles at the American destroyers. All of the projectiles were shot down by the destroyers with no damage or casualties to the vessels.[^cite_note-11]

On 22 March 2025, *Spruance* helped the [Coast Guard](32223.md) and agents with [U.S. Customs and Border Protection](757040.md) apprehend 13 people in a vessel off the Mexican coast.[^cite_note-12]



*Spruance* during the 2026 Iran war

*Spruance* launched [Tomahawk missiles](31238.md) at Iranian targets during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md) on 28th February 2026.[^cite_note-13]

On 19 April 2026, *Spruance* attacked the Iran-flagged cargo ship *[Touska](82992016.md)* after it attempted to breach the [US naval blockade of Iran](82937256.md) en route to [Bandar Abbas](464348.md).[^cite_note-14] *Spruance* fired several rounds from its [5-inch/54-caliber Mark 45 gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-inch/54-caliber_Mark_45_gun) into the *Touska's* engine room, disabling the vessel. It was then seized by the [31st Marine Expeditionary Unit](5885654.md) in the Gulf of Oman.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] Iran described the seizure as a [US-Iran truce](82888027.md) violation.[^cite_note-17] Iran retaliated against the seizure by launching attack drones at U.S. ships, although no damage was reported.[^cite_note-18] According to [Reuters](18998750.md), the seized ship was probably carrying equipment that the US considered "dual-use," meaning that it had equipment that could be used by the Iranian army.[^cite_note-19]
