The **National Security and Defence Council of Ukraine**,[^cite_note-3] (abbr. **NSDCU**; [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Рада національної безпеки і оборони України, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *Rada natsionalnoyi bezpeky i oboroni Ukrayiny*, ***RNBOU***) or **RNBO**, is the coordinating state body of the executive power under the [President of Ukraine](20688479.md) on issues of national security and defence.[^cite_note-ukrainealertOleksiyDanilov-4]

It is a state agency tasked with developing and coordinating a policy of national security on domestic and international matters in advising the [President of Ukraine](20688479.md) (currently [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md)).[^cite_note-ukrainealertOleksiyDanilov-4] All the sessions of the council take place in the [Presidential Administration Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Administration_Building_\(Kiev\)). The agency's membership is determined by the President, but it must include the [Prime Minister of Ukraine](1256678.md), [Minister of Defence](14284651.md), [Minister of Internal Affairs](1400321.md), and the [Minister of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Ukraine\)).[^cite_note-ukrainealertOleksiyDanilov-4] The council is headed by secretary [Ihor Klymenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ihor_Klymenko) since August 2026.

Since 2014, the Council has received expanded powers, including the authority to impose sanctions. Under the presidency of [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md), starting in 2021, the NSDC has actively implemented [sanctions against Ukrainian citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSDC_sanctions_against_Ukrainian_citizens). This practice has been criticized by human rights organizations and legal experts as potentially unconstitutional and inconsistent with the principles of the rule of law, alleging it is used for politically motivated purposes.[^cite_note-uba-5][^cite_note-khpg-6][^cite_note-ZMINAanalysis-7][^cite_note-ccl-8]

## Functions

### Sanctions against Ukrainian citizens

Main article: [NSDC sanctions against Ukrainian citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSDC_sanctions_against_Ukrainian_citizens)

The sanctions policy of the National Security and Defence Council of Ukraine (NSDC) has been widely criticized for the application of personal restrictive measures against Ukrainian citizens without a court ruling, which violates the [Constitution of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Ukraine) and fundamental principles of the rule of law.[^cite_note-uba-5][^cite_note-khpg-6][^cite_note-ZMINAanalysis-7] Sanctions are imposed by NSDC resolution based on the Law “On Sanctions”,[^cite_note-sanctionslaw1-10] but the mechanism does not provide for judicial involvement,[^cite_note-11] which contradicts legal standards of the [European Union](9317.md) and other democratic states.[^cite_note-obozrevatel-12]

Human rights organizations, including the [Center for Civil Liberties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Civil_Liberties_\(human_rights_organization\)),[^cite_note-ccl-8] the [Ukrainian Helsinki Human Rights Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Helsinki_Human_Rights_Union), and the [Kharkiv Human Rights Protection Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv_Human_Rights_Protection_Group), have pointed out the unconstitutional nature of this practice. They emphasize that the NSDC is a political body and lacks the authority to carry out quasi-judicial functions.[^cite_note-khpg-6][^cite_note-ZMINAanalysis-7] In February 2025, several Ukrainian human rights organizations issued a joint statement condemning the use of sanctions as a tool of political pressure against the opposition.[^cite_note-khpg-6]

According to critics, the sanctions mechanism has increasingly been used as a political instrument by incumbent President [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) to eliminate political rivals and strengthen control over the media and business sectors.[^cite_note-khpg-6][^cite_note-ZMINAanalysis-7][^cite_note-ccl-8] Sanctioned individuals include former President and current opposition politician [Petro Poroshenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petro_Poroshenko),[^cite_note-PoroshenkoSanctions-13] oligarchs [Ihor Kolomoyskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ihor_Kolomoyskyi) and [Gennadiy Bogolyubov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gennadiy_Bogolyubov),[^cite_note-cnn-14][^cite_note-osw-15] journalist [Svitlana Kryukova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svitlana_Kryukova),[^cite_note-bbc-16] former presidential adviser [Oleksiy Arestovych](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleksiy_Arestovych),[^cite_note-17] and businessman [Kostyantyn Zhevago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kostyantyn_Zhevago).[^cite_note-18]

### Defence Council

The *Defence Council* was created by the provision of [Supreme Council of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Council_of_Ukraine) #1658-12 on October 11, 1991. The Defence Council was defined as the highest state body of collegiate governing on matters of defence and security of Ukraine with following goals:

- Protecting sovereignty

- Constitutional order

- Territorial integrity and inviolability of the republic

- Developing strategies and continuous improvement of policy in sphere of defence and state security

- Comprehensive scientific assessment of the military threat nature

- Determining position toward modern warfare

- Effective control over the execution of the tasks of the state and its institutions keeping defence capabilities of Ukraine at the level of defence sufficiency

On January 23, 1992, the [President of Ukraine](20688479.md) appointed [Myroslav Vitovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myroslav_Vitovsky?action=edit\&redlink=1) as a secretary of the Defence Council, a position which Vitovsky held until November 30, 1995.

### National Security Council

The Council was originally created under temporary provision on July 3, 1992, as the *National Security Council*, but significantly revamped and strengthened under [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_President) [Leonid Kuchma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Kuchma) in 1994. The council was headed by a Presidential adviser in national security matters:

- July 1, 1992 - April 19, 1993 [Volodymyr Selivanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Selivanov) (Adviser of the [President of Ukraine](20688479.md) on national security matters - Secretary of National Security Council)&#x20;

  - before November 19, 1992 as State adviser of Ukraine on national security matters

- December 1, 1993 - August 5, 1994 [Valeriy Kartavtsev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valeriy_Kartavtsev) (Secretary of National Security Council)

- August 5, 1994 - November 10, 1999 [Volodymyr Horbulin](25968628.md) (Secretary of National Security Council)&#x20;

  - since October 17, 1994 as Secretary of National Security Council - Adviser of the [President of Ukraine](20688479.md) on national security matters

On August 23, 1994, the [President of Ukraine](20688479.md) adopted new provision for the Council. After the adaptation of the [Constitution of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Ukraine) on June 28, 1996, the provisions of the council (National Security and Defence Council of Ukraine) were outlined in the Article 107. Thus, the National Security Council was merged with the already existing Defence Council of Ukraine and was adapted by the Presidential edict on August 30, 1996.

According to former secretary [Andriy Klyuyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andriy_Klyuyev), 460 people worked at the Council in 2010, while in April 2012 the number of employees was 90.[^cite_note-now180-21] President [Viktor Yanukovych](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Yanukovych) limited the number of employees of the Council to 180 on 9 April 2012.[^cite_note-now180-21]

The agency's membership is determined by the President, but it must include the [Prime Minister of Ukraine](1256678.md), [Minister of Defence](14284651.md), [Minister of Internal Affairs](1400321.md), and the [Minister of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Ukraine\)).[^cite_note-ukrainealertOleksiyDanilov-4] The head of the council is the President of Ukraine assisted by the secretary whom they are allowed to appoint. In the absence of existing President of Ukraine, such as between elections, the acting chairman of the council is the Prime Minister. The [Chairman of the Verkhovna Rada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Verkhovna_Rada) is not a member of the council, but they are allowed to participate in the meetings. Members of the council may also be other chairmen of government bodies of the [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_branch). Any other individual is only allowed by special invitation from the Chairman of the Council.

If the President resigns, the Chairman of the Verkhovna Rada becomes the acting head of the National Security and Defence Council.[^cite_note-22]
