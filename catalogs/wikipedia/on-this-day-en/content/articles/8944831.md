The **United States ambassador to Israel** is the official representative of the president of the United States to the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of [Israel](9282173.md).

Until 1948, the area that is now the state of Israel had been under British administration as part of the [League of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/League_of_Nations)/[United Nations](31769.md)' [Mandate for Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine). On May 14, 1948, the British government unilaterally terminated the mandate. On the same day, the [Jewish Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_Agency), under future Prime Minister [David Ben-Gurion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ben-Gurion), declared independence and named the country Israel. The United States immediately [recognized](524626.md) the nation and moved to establish [diplomatic relations](28165911.md). The first U.S. ambassador commissioned to Israel was [James Grover McDonald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Grover_McDonald), who [presented his credentials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_of_credence) to the government of Israel on March 28, 1949.[^cite_note-StateHistory1-1] The ambassador holds the title *Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary*.

The [embassy of the United States in Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_the_United_States_in_Israel) is located at 14 David Flusser Street in [Jerusalem](16043.md).
