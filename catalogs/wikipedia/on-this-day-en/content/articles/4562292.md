**The Old Oyo National Park** is one of the [national parks of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_national_parks_of_Nigeria), located across northern [Oyo State](2227658.md) and southern [Kwara State](2227645.md), [Nigeria](21383.md).[^cite_note-NNPS-1] The [park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park) is 2,512 km<sup>2</sup> of land in northern Oyo state, south west Nigeria, at [latitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latitude) 8° 15’ and 9° 00’N and [longitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitude) 3° 35’ and 4° 42’ E.[^cite_note-2] The location has inevitably placed the park at a vantage position of abundance land area as well as diverse [wildlife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife) and cultural/historical settings.[^cite_note-3] Eleven [local government areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_area) out of which ten fall within Oyo State and one in Kwara State surrounds it.[^cite_note-4] The Administrative Head Office is located in [Oyo](2227658.md), Isokun area along Oyo-Iseyin road, where necessary information and booking could be made.[^cite_note-5] The [landscaping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landscaping) and organized space within the large yard has made the facility very endearing to the public.[^cite_note-NNPS-1] It is rich in [plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant) and [animal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_husbandry) resources including [buffaloes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_buffalo), [bushbuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_bushbuck) and a variety of [birds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird). The park is easily accessible from southwestern and northwestern Nigeria.[^cite_note-6] The nearest cities and towns adjoining Old Oyo [National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_park) include [Saki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saki,_Nigeria), [Iseyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iseyin), [Igboho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igboho), [Sepeteri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sepeteri), [Tede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atisbo), [Kishi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kisi,_Nigeria), and [Igbeti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbeti), which have their own commercial and cultural attractions for [tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism).[^cite_note-7]

## History

The park takes its name from [Oyo-lle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Oyo) (Old Oyo), the ancient political capital of [Oyo Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyo_Empire) of the [Yoruba people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoruba_people), and contains the ruins of this city.[^cite_note-Thornton1999-8] The national park originated in two earlier native administrative forest reserves, Upper [Ogun](1436438.md) established in 1936 and [Oyo-lle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyo-Ile) established in 1941.[^cite_note-9] These were converted to game reserves in 1952, then combined and upgraded to the present status of a national park.[^cite_note-NNPS-1]

By 2022, reporting noted that despite regular tourism tours and protective laws, the park was under threat due to poaching and logging in addition to herder encroachment.[^cite_note-10] Although poaching had decreased from the early 2000s, herding remained a problem with [Miyetti Allah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyetti_Allah) organizations agreeing that illegal herding was an important issue facing the park, but claiming many local herders did not know the exact location of the park nor its ecological significance. Additionally, environmentalist Eme Okang explained that climate change also pushed herders south and into the park.[^cite_note-11] The next year, mass arrests of illegal miners and herders in addition to poachers occurred during security drives.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

## Location

The Park has total land mass of 2,512 [km<sup>2</sup>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_kilometre) (970 [sq mi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_mile)) and is located in southwestern part of [Nigeria](21383.md), specifically in Northern [Oyo State](2227658.md) at latitude 8<sup>o</sup> 15’ and 9<sup>o</sup> 00’N and longitude 3<sup>o</sup> 35’ and 4<sup>o</sup> 42’ E. The location has inevitably placed the Park at a vantage position of abundance land area as well as diverse wildlife, cultural and historical settings. Eleven (11) Local Government areas out of which Ten (10) falls within Oyo State and one (1) in [Kwara State](2227645.md) surround it. The Administrative Head Office is located in Oyo, Isokun area along Oyo-Iseyin road, where necessary information and booking could be made.[^cite_note-14] The landscaping and organized space within the large yard has made the facility very endearing to the public.[^cite_note-15]

## Environment

The park covers 2,512 km<sup>2</sup>, mostly of lowland plains at a height of 330 m and 508 m above sea level. The southern part is drained by the Owu, Owe and [Ogun Rivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogun_River), while the northern sector is drained by the Tessi River. Outcrops of granite are typical of the north eastern zone of the park, including at Oyo-lle, with caves and rock shelters in the extreme north.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

The central part of the park has scattered hills, ridges and rock outcrops that are suitable for mountaineering.

The [Ikere Gorge Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikere_Gorge_Dam) on the Ogun river provides water recreation facilities for tourists.[^cite_note-NNPS-1]

## Flora and fauna

The park contains populations of [African buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_buffalo), [kob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kob), [bushbuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_bushbuck), [roan antelope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roan_antelope), [western hartebeest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_hartebeest), [patas monkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patas_monkey), and [waterbuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterbuck).[^cite_note-18] The Old Oyo National Park was previously habitat for the endangered [West African wild dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_African_wild_dog) (*Lycaon pictus manguensis*); however, the species has been extirpated from the park due to hunting pressure and the [expanding human population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_growth) in the region.[^cite_note-Hogan2009-19]
