Special forces units in the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) (IDF) encompass a broad definition of specialist units. Such units are usually a [regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regiment) or a [battalion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battalion) in strength.

**Sayeret**[^cite_note-1] ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): סיירת, [pl.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plural): *sayarot*), or [reconnaissance](146911.md) [units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_unit) in the IDF nomenclature, specialize in intelligence gathering and surveillance. In practice, these units specialize in [commando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commando) and other [special forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_forces) roles, in addition to reconnaissance (the degree of specialization varies by units and current needs).

**[Mista'arvim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mista'arvim)** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): מסתערבים, *lit.* Arabized; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مستعربين, *Musta'arabin*), also spelled as mistaravim, are [counter-terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-terrorism) units whose members are specifically trained to operate undercover, in enemy territory, in order to assassinate or capture wanted targets.

## History



[Ariel Sharon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariel_Sharon) (left), and [Aharon Davidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aharon_Davidi) (center), before a [reprisal operation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reprisal_operations), 1955.

### Pre-state

Prior to the establishment of the State of Israel, some Jews of the [Yishuv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yishuv), or Jewish community of [Mandatory Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine), obtained experience as commandos during [World War II](32927.md). In 1941, the [Haganah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haganah), the main Jewish militia and ancestor of the Israel Defense Forces, established the [Palmach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmach) as an elite strike force with British assistance due to the Axis advance threatening Palestine. After the threat subsided, the British attempted to disarm the Palmach, which subsequently went underground. It subsequently engaged in sabotage operations against the British authorities to enable Jewish immigration and fought for Israeli independence against the Arabs. The Palmach formed a naval commando arm, the [Palyam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palyam). In addition, some Jews of the Yishuv who enlisted in the British military during World War II served in commando units. Jews from Palestine served as commandos in the [Special Interrogation Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Interrogation_Group) and [No. 51 Commando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No._51_Commando) in addition to other units during the war.

### Unit 101

Main article: [Unit 101](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_101)

Commando [Unit 101](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_101), the founding Israeli special forces unit, was established and commanded by [Ariel Sharon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariel_Sharon) on orders from [Prime Minister](24898.md) [David Ben-Gurion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ben-Gurion) in August 1953.[^cite_note-Specwar-2] They were armed with non-standard weapons and tasked with carrying out [retribution operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retribution_operations) across the state's borders – in particular, establishing small unit maneuvers, activation and tactical insertion and exit tactics.

Members of the unit were recruited only from agricultural [Kibbutzim](106200.md) and [Moshavim](1597952.md). Membership in the unit was by invitation only, and any new member had to be voted on by all existing members before they were accepted.[^cite_note-3]

The unit was merged into the [890th Paratroopers Battalion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/890th_Paratroopers_Battalion?action=edit\&redlink=1) during January 1954, on orders of General Dayan, Chief of Staff, because he wanted their experience and spirit to be spread among all infantry units of IDF starting with the paratroopers. They are considered to have had a significant influence on the development of subsequent Israeli special forces units.[^cite_note-Israeli_Special_Forces_History-4]

### Sayeret units today



Israeli officers of the Paratrooper Battalion 890 in 1955 with [Moshe Dayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshe_Dayan) (standing, third from the left). [Ariel Sharon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariel_Sharon) is standing, second from the left and commando [Meir Har Zion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meir_Har_Zion) is standing furthest left.

All combat [brigades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigade) in the IDF include a unit with improved weaponry and training used for reconnaissance and special forces missions, trained to use advanced weapons and reconnaissance technology, as well as hand-to-hand combat. Historically the brigades used to only have one company-sized unit outfitted to do this job, known as **Palsar** (Hebrew [contraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contraction_\(grammar\)) of: פלוגת-סיור, *Plugat Siyur* (singular) / *Plugot Siyur* (plural), "Reconnaissance Company"). Although the *Palsar* are mostly oriented at battlefield support (which is their *raison d'être*), many have participated in [special operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_operations) over the years.

While in the past there were differences between the *Siyur* units, learning from past events and in order to improve and develop their forces, the IDF has consolidating them into larger units with many different capabilities: battalion-sized units called **Gadsar** (contraction of *Gdud Siyur*, "Reconnaissance battalion"). Each *Gadsar* is made up of three specialized *Plugot* (companies): demolitions and [combat engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_engineering) (*Plugat Habalah Handasit*, or **Palhan**), reconnaissance (*Plugat Siyur*, **Palsar**) and anti-tank (*Pluga Neged Tankim*, or **Palnat**).

In late December 2015, several IDF special forces units were transferred to the [Oz Brigade](49096690.md).[^cite_note-SpecForInt-5][^cite_note-6]

Other SF units or *Sayaret* are larger units, operating directly under the [General Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff_of_the_Israel_Defense_Forces). They are tasked with the most sensitive missions but they also support other conventional and SF units, if needed. Those units are [Sayeret Matkal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayeret_Matkal), [Shayetet 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shayetet_13) and [Shaldag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaldag_Unit).

## IDF units

### Reconnaissance units



Shayetet 13 commandos prepare for an exercise aboard a warship

These are the most well-known reconnaissance units. Their operators are proficient in long range solo navigation, as opposed to other special forces units in the IDF where long range navigation is done with a minimum of 2 operators.

- General Staff Reconnaissance Unit 269 – [Sayeret Matkal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayeret_Matkal) – the IDF's principal *Sayeret* unit, is used mainly to obtain strategic intelligence behind enemy lines and to perform hostage-rescue missions on foreign soil. It is directly subordinate to the Israeli [Military Intelligence Directorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Intelligence_Directorate_\(Israel\)) (AMAN).

- 13th Flotilla – [Shayetet 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shayetet_13) – the naval commando unit. It is part of the [Israeli Navy](1370788.md) and tasked with maritime hostage-rescue missions. Founded in 1948 by former members of the [Palyam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palyam), the naval branch of the [Haganah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haganah).

- Unit 5101 – [Shaldag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaldag_Unit) – founded in 1974 by several former Sayeret Matkal veterans, it is the [Israeli Air Force](177619.md)'s commando unit, specializing in [forward air control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_air_control), aerial & special reconnaissance, and target designation outside of Israel's borders.

IDF [Alpinist Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpinist_Unit) dispatched to [Mount Hermon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Hermon)

- [Alpinist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpinist_Unit) – IDF mountain-warfare unit operating in the [Golan Heights](7515865.md); provides protection for IDF electronic listening posts on [Mount Hermon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Hermon) and [Mount Avital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Avital). - [LOTAR Eilat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LOTAR_Eilat) – reserve force counter-terrorism/hostage-rescue unit based at the southern Israeli port city of [Eilat](66162.md). ([Southern Command](5216006.md)).
