**Liu Zhenli** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 刘振立; born August 1964) is a former Chinese [general](148537.md) (*[Shangjiang](12534441.md)*) of the [People's Liberation Army](66890.md) (PLA), who served as chief of staff of the [Joint Staff Department of the Central Military Commission](49138480.md) from 2022 to 2026. He was previously the [commander of the People's Liberation Army Ground Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_PLA_Ground_Force) from June 2021 to December 2022.

He was a member of the [19th Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19th_Central_Committee_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party), and a delegate to the [12th National People's Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_National_People's_Congress).

## Biography

Liu was born in [Luancheng County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luancheng_District), [Hebei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebei), in August 1964. He enlisted in the [People's Liberation Army](66890.md) in September 1983 and joined the [Chinese Communist Party](7175.md) (CCP) in April 1984. He graduated from the [PLA National Defence University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Liberation_Army_National_Defense_University). In 1986, he participated in the [Sino-Vietnamese conflicts (1979–1991)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sino-Vietnamese_conflicts_\(1979%E2%80%931991\)). In the war, he and his men successfully defended the line against repeated [People's Army of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Army_of_Vietnam) assaults 36 times.

He was chief of staff of the [65th Group Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/81st_Group_Army) in December 2009, commander of the army in February 2012, and commander of the [38th Group Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/82nd_Group_Army) in March 2014. He was transferred to the [People's Armed Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Armed_Police) in July 2015 and appointed chief of staff.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] In December 2015, he became the first chief of staff of the newly reshuffled [People's Liberation Army Ground Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Liberation_Army_Ground_Force).[^cite_note-3] In June 2021, he was made commander of the army.[^cite_note-4] In September 2022, he was commissioned as chief of staff of the [Joint Staff Department of the Central Military Commission](49138480.md).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

He was promoted to the rank of [major general](36301471.md) (*[Shaojiang](12534441.md)*) in December 2010, [lieutenant general](201916.md) (*[Zhongjiang](12534441.md)*) in July 2016, and [general](148537.md) (*Shangjiang*) in July 2021.[^cite_note-8]

### Downfall

On 24 January 2026, the [Ministry of National Defense](33632648.md) announced that Liu and Central Military Commission Vice Chairman [Zhang Youxia](17853681.md) have been placed under investigation due to a decision by the [CCP Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party) over suspected "serious violations of discipline."[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] The *[People's Liberation Army Daily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Liberation_Army_Daily)* published an editorial stating that Zhang and Liu had "severely trampled on and undermined the [CMC Chairman responsibility system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_responsibility_system)".[^cite_note-11]

The [Jamestown Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamestown_Foundation) published a detailed analysis of open-source publications and reports from the [Ministry of National Defense](33632648.md), *[Xinhua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinhua)*, *[People's Daily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Daily)*, and *[PLA Daily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLA_Daily)*. The report speculated that the charges against Zhang and Liu—likely stemming from the same underlying cause—were political accusations related to their open disagreement or defiance with the [CMC Chairman responsibility system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_responsibility_system), having prioritized military effectiveness over excessive political control and resisting directives they assessed as unrealistic, particularly CCP general secretary [Xi Jinping](2017814.md)'s demands that the PLA be capable of invading Taiwan by 2027, a timeline Zhang reportedly saw as more realistic by 2035. The report rejected claims that Zhang and Liu were charged with corruption or malfeasance, noting that the language of the accusations differed significantly from those brought against [He Weidong](62571923.md) or [Li Shangfu](55799907.md). The report also highlighted growing resistance across the PLA to political interference, including open defiance of Xi's orders, which posed a serious challenge to his authority. The fact that such internal discord became visible within the PLA and even hinted within official statements, underscored its seriousness and likely played a role in Zhang's downfall.[^cite_note-12]

On 28 August 2026, the [Standing Committee of the National People's Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standing_Committee_of_the_National_People's_Congress) removed Liu from his position as a member of the state Central Military Commission.[^cite_note-13] The NPCSC also removed Liu as a delegate to the [14th National People's Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_National_People's_Congress).[^cite_note-14] Liu was expelled from the party and military service on 21 September. The investigation found that Liu "formed self-serving cliques, were disloyal and dishonest to the Party, took advantage of their positions to assist others in promotions and accepted a huge sum of bribes in return".[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]
