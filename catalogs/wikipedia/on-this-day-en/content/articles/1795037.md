In [education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education), an **entrance examination** or **admission examination** is an [examination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_\(assessment\)) that [educational institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_institution) conduct to select prospective [students](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student). It may be held at any stage of education, from [primary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_education) to [tertiary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tertiary_education), even though it is typically held at tertiary stage.

## By country

### France

[France](5843419.md) has various entrance examinations, including one to enter medical studies, and entrance examinations for the [Grandes écoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grandes_%C3%A9coles) of engineering and of business, for which students prepare for two years in a [Classe préparatoire aux grandes écoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classe_pr%C3%A9paratoire_aux_grandes_%C3%A9coles).

### India

In India, entrance examinations are chiefly used for admission to many of the country's educational institutions. Certain institutions are considered "Institutes of National Importance," including the [Indian Institutes of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institutes_of_Technology) (IITs), [All India Institutes of Medical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_India_Institutes_of_Medical_Sciences) (AIIMS), and [National Institutes of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Technology) (NITs), which conduct entrance exams for admission. The [National Level Common Entrance Examination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Level_Common_Entrance_Examination) (NLCEE) is a talent hunt initiative in India that provides scholarships and exposure visits to students aspiring for higher education.[^cite_note-1] The [stiff](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/stiff) [competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competition) has led to a situation where many students neglect their [school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School) [studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education) and focus solely on '[entrance coaching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrance_coaching?action=edit\&redlink=1)' which is time-consuming and expensive. This has led many states to scrap the entrances and base [admissions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_and_college_admission) on the school leaving marks which, are none too reliable. Experts point out that in a [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country) where many different [boards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_examination) are present common [entrances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_and_college_admission) are essential, but application skills rather than [cramming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cramming_\(education\)) should be stressed on. Frequent changes in the pattern of examination are essential since sticking to a 'standard text' or 'standard pattern' alone will [favour](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/favor) the [coaching industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coaching_industry?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the [rote-learners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rote-learners?action=edit\&redlink=1).

Entrance examinations in India trace their roots to the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta), which when established in 1857, introduced the practice to decide eligibility for admission. In that exam, one student qualified for every four candidates.[^cite_note-2] In the absence of a standardized school graduation examination, the University's entrance examinations were used as a substitute, known later as Matriculation examinations. Post-independence, India has different [systems of education](293121.md#Overview) whose syllabus and examination process are governed by both central and state-based statutory boards. Grades 10 and 12 which mark the culmination of secondary and higher secondary education, have standardized final examinations, referred to as the [Secondary School Leaving Certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_School_Leaving_Certificate) (SSLC) examination after grade 10 (class X) and the [Higher Secondary Examination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Secondary_School_Certificate#India) (HSC) after grade 12th.

### United Kingdom

A few British universities have lost confidence in the grades that are awarded by secondary schools, and require a few applicants to sit for a competitive entrance examination or other [aptitude test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aptitude_test). According to the Schools Minister, "strong evidence has been emerging of [grade inflation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grade_inflation) across subjects" in recent years.[^cite_note-3]

## COVID-19 impact

Main article: [Impact of the COVID-19 lockdowns on education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_of_the_COVID-19_lockdowns_on_education)

Due to the presence of [COVID-19](63030231.md) in many countries that implemented school closures on either a state or national scale, some entrance examinations for high schools were eventually cancelled to reduce student stress and the risk of COVID-19 infection during attendance.
