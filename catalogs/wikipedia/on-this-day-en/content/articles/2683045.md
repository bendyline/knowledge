***Celestyal Discovery*** is a cruise ship, built in 2003 by the [German](11867.md) shipyard [Aker MTW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aker_MTW) in [Wismar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wismar), and was operated by [AIDA Cruises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDA_Cruises) for twenty years as AidaAura, as was her [sister ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sister_ship) [*AIDAvita*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Dream_Melody). In November 2023 the ship was sold to the Greek cruise line [Celestyal Cruises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestyal_Cruises) and entered service in March 2024.

## Description

*Celestyal Discovery* is considered a mid-sized [cruise ship](314855.md)[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWard2019309-1] measuring 202.85 m (665 ft 6 in) [long overall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length_overall) and 179.74 m (589 ft 8 in) [between perpendiculars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length_between_perpendiculars) with a [beam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beam_\(nautical\)) of 28.1 m (92 ft 2 in) and a [draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draft_\(hull\)) of 8.75 m (28 ft 8 in). The cruise ship has a 42,289 [gross tonnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_tonnage) (GT) and measures 4,157 tons [deadweight](1346384.md) (DWT).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEDNV_GL-2] The vessel is powered by a [diesel-electric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel-electric#Ships) system turning two [screws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propeller) rated at 27,150 [kilowatts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilowatt) (36,410 [hp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horsepower)).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWard2019309-1][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMiramar_Ship_Index-3] The ship has a maximum speed of 19.5 [knots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (36.1 km/h; 22.4 mph).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMiramar_Ship_Index-3]

The cruise ship has capacity for 1,266 passengers. *Celestyal Discovery* has 633 cabins, ranging from 13.5–32.0 square meters (145–344 sq ft) in size of which 60 are equipped with a balcony. The vessel is equipped with several amenities for the passengers including bars, lounges, a library and a two-storey theater. *Celestyal Discovery* has a crew of 389. The vessel has a swept-back [funnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funnel_\(ship\)) and a wedge-shaped stern.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWard2019309-1]

## Construction and career

The cruise ship was [laid down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel_laying) on 11 January 2002 at the [Aker MTW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aker_MTW) shipyard in [Wismar, Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wismar,_Germany) with the [yard number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yard_number) 004. The vessel was [launched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceremonial_ship_launching) on 14 September 2002 and completed on 3 April 2003.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMiramar_Ship_Index-3] The vessel cost \$350 million.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWard2005-4] The vessel was christened *AIDAaura* by International model Heidi Klum on 12 April 2003 at [Warnemünde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warnem%C3%BCnde).[^cite_note-5] The vessel made its maiden voyage in April 2003.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWard2019309-1] In 2003, the vessel was built for [P\&O Cruises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P\&O_Cruises), a subsidiary of [Carnival Corporation & plc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnival_Corporation_&_plc), which took over ownership of the vessel and [registered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_registration) the cruise ship in [London](17867.md), United Kingdom.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMiramar_Ship_Index-3][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEEquasis-6] In 2004, *AIDAaura* was acquired by [Costa Crociere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Crociere) and the registry moved to [Genoa](47332321.md), Italy and the operator changed to [AIDA Cruises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDA_Cruises), a subsidiary of the Carnival Corporation.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEEquasis-6][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEDNV_GL-2]



MV AIDAaura departing Tallinn 17 May 2016

On 8 October 2018, *AIDAaura* departed from [Hamburg](13467.md) on a 117-day round-the-world cruise. The ship visited 41 ports in 20 countries on four continents.[^cite_note-7] *AIDAaura*, with about 1,200 people on board, was held on 3 March 2020 in the harbor of [Haugesund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haugesund), [Norway](21241.md), while two asymptomatic German passengers were tested who had been in contact with a person who subsequently developed [COVID-19](63030231.md); their test results were negative.[^cite_note-berliner-8][^cite_note-9]

In January 2023 AIDA Cruises said that the ship would be decommissioned in autumn 2023.[^cite_note-10] Istanbul-based [Miray Cruises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miray_Cruises) announced in June that they had acquired a ship, for delivery in September, reported to be *AIDAaura*, for their *Life at Sea Cruises* intended 3-year world voyage, beginning in November 2023. Replacing the intended *[Gemini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_Gemini)*, the ship would be renamed *Lara* and undergo renovations in Istanbul.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] The last voyage of the *AIDAaura* ended in [Bremerhaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bremerhaven), Germany on 21 September 2023.[^cite_note-NZ10112023-13] In November it was reported that Miray were no longer the buyer, with speculation that it would now be [Celestyal Cruises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestyal_Cruises).[^cite_note-NZ10112023-13]



Celestyal Discovery in Quay in Port of Rhodes, August 2024

&#x20;On 16 November 2023, Celestyal Cruises confirmed their purchase of *AIDAaura* and stated that she would enter service in March 2024 as *Celestyal Discovery* after a winter refit, replacing *[Celestyal Olympia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MS_Celestyal_Olympia)*.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] The ship entered service on 22 March 2024.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] Celestyal plans to add 47 cabins.[^cite_note-18]



Celestyal Discovery already with its expansion to new 47 cabins

### 2026 Iran war

*Celestyal Discovery* along with its sister ship *[Celestyal Journey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestyal_Journey)* were among the many cruise ships that canceled trips in the Middle East following the [joint American—Israeli attack on Iran](82533582.md) on 28 February 2026. *Celestyal Discovery* was docked in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, when hostilities broke out and trips that were scheduled in March were canceled thereby prematurely ending the ship's Arabian winter cruise season.[^cite_note-19] The ship was able to leave the [Persian Gulf](24761.md) via the newly reopened [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md) on 17 April 2026.[^cite_note-20]
