The **minister of finance** (or simply, the **finance minister**, ( [Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): *अर्थ* *मन्त्री* ) is the head of the [Ministry of Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Finance_\(Nepal\)) of the [Government of Nepal](19233409.md). One of the senior-most officers in the [Federal Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Nepal), the finance minister is responsible for the [fiscal policy](132645.md) of the government. A key duty of the finance minister is to present the annual federal budget in [Parliament](410238.md), detailing the government's plan for taxation and spending in the coming [financial year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_year). Through the budget, the finance minister also outlines allocations to all the ministries and departments. The minister is assisted by the **minister of state for finance** and the junior **deputy minister of finance**.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

The current minister is [Dr. Swarnim Wagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarnim_Wagle) who took office on 27 March 2026.
