The **Eurovision Song Contest 2027** will be the 71st edition of the [Eurovision Song Contest](9954.md). It will consist of two semi-finals on 11 and 13 May and a final on 15 May 2027, held at [Arena Burgas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_Burgas) in [Burgas](86758.md), Bulgaria. It is being organised by the [European Broadcasting Union](10380.md) (EBU) and host broadcaster [Bulgarian National Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_National_Television) (BNT), which will stage the event after winning the [2026 contest](79992910.md) for [Bulgaria](81478817.md) with the song "[Bangaranga](82538076.md)" by [Dara](31712238.md).

## Location

The 2027 contest will take place in [Burgas](86758.md), Bulgaria, following the country's victory at the [2026 contest](79992910.md) with "[Bangaranga](82538076.md)", performed by [Dara](31712238.md).[^cite_note-2] It will be the first edition of the contest hosted in Bulgaria and the second Eurovision event held in the country, after the [Junior Eurovision Song Contest 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_Eurovision_Song_Contest_2015) in [Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia). The selected venue for the contest is [Arena Burgas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_Burgas), which serves as a venue for indoor sports and concert events.[^cite_note-\:16-3][^cite_note-\:17-4]

After Bulgaria's win in 2026, [Milena Milotinova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milena_Milotinova?action=edit\&redlink=1), director of [Bulgarian National Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_National_Television) (BNT), said during the winner's press conference that the broadcaster was ready to host the 2027 contest in Sofia.[^cite_note-\:5-5][^cite_note-6] Between 17 and 18 May 2026, the mayors of Burgas,[^cite_note-BurgasArena17may-7] Sofia,[^cite_note-SofiaArena17may-8] [Plovdiv](182553.md),[^cite_note-Plovdiv17may-9] and [Varna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varna,_Bulgaria)[^cite_note-Varna18may-10] all declared readiness for their respective city to host the event. By 21 May, the Bulgarian government had begun establishing an interdepartmental working group, headed by Deputy Prime Minister [Ivo Hristov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivo_Hristov), to coordinate preparations for the contest.[^cite_note-\:8-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] BNT opened the bidding process on 8 June, issuing requirements for the four interested cities,[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] all of which delivered their bids by 3 July.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] On 13 July, BNT announced that the choice of host city had been narrowed to Burgas and Sofia.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] Throughout that month, the two candidate cities received an inspection visit from EBU representatives.[^cite_note-21] On 13 August, the EBU and BNT announced Burgas as the host city.[^cite_note-\:16-3][^cite_note-\:17-4]

## Provisional list of participants

Further information: [List of countries in the Eurovision Song Contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_in_the_Eurovision_Song_Contest)

Eligibility for participation in the Eurovision Song Contest requires a national broadcaster with [an EBU membership](10380.md#Members) capable of receiving the contest via the [Eurovision network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_network) and broadcasting it live nationwide. The EBU issues an invitation to participate in the contest to all members. Broadcasters have until 15 September 2026 to apply for participation and until mid-October to withdraw without facing a financial penalty, though they are not required to publicly declare their interest before then.[^cite_note-23]

As of October 2026, broadcasters from the following countries have publicly confirmed their intention to participate in the 2027 contest. The [Netherlands](530870.md) and [North Macedonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Macedonia_in_the_Eurovision_Song_Contest) are set to return after recent absences, while [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_in_the_Eurovision_Song_Contest) is set to make its debut after its broadcaster was accepted as an EBU member in June 2026. Canada is set to be the first country to debut in the contest since [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_in_the_Eurovision_Song_Contest) received an invitation to join the contest in [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_Song_Contest_2015).

#### Boycotts due to Israeli participation

Broadcasters from [Iceland](3512313.md), [Ireland](3014113.md), the [Netherlands](530870.md), [Slovenia](1992733.md), and [Spain](2996040.md) opted not to take part in the 2026 contest to protest Israel's participation in the context of the [Gaza war](74998519.md).[^cite_note-88] In an interview with *[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\))* that was published on 1 July 2026, contest director [Martin Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Green_\(producer\)) stated that a vote on whether or not to permit Israel's participation in 2027 would not be held.[^cite_note-89] The Israeli broadcaster Kan confirmed its intention to participate in Bulgaria on 12 July.[^cite_note-Israel1-42]

In the case of the Netherlands, NPO, the umbrella organisation of the [Dutch public broadcasting system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_public_broadcasting_system), announced on 23 September that it would take over responsibility for Dutch participation.[^cite_note-93] Additionally, Slovenia's [RTVSLO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiotelevizija_Slovenija) has not formally ruled out participation.[^cite_note-94] On 22 September, [José Pablo López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Pablo_L%C3%B3pez), chairman of Spain's [RTVE](1014212.md), stated before the [Cortes Generales](519703.md) committee overseeing RTVE that he would propose to the broadcaster's board of directors to maintain its absence from the contest.[^cite_note-95]

## Production and format

The Eurovision Song Contest 2027 will be produced by the Bulgarian national broadcaster [Bulgarian National Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_National_Television) (BNT). In July 2026, the Bulgarian government adopted an action plan for the contest's production. Under the plan, an executive producer and creative director were to be appointed by 30 August; an agreement with the selected venue was to be signed by 10 September, with its spaces allocated for contest use by 30 September; a projected budget is to be prepared by 10 October; and the creative concept and visual design are to be completed by 15 December. Presenters and scriptwriters are to be selected between November 2026 and April 2027, while stage construction and technical rehearsals are scheduled to take place from mid-March until the end of April. The Eurovision Village is planned to operate from 13 April to 31 May, and the venue is to be restored to normal use between 18 and 24 May.[^cite_note-\:12-96][^cite_note-97] The Bulgarian government has allocated around [€](9472.md)20 million as part of the budget needed to host the event.[^cite_note-98]

### Voting system and contest structure

On 12 August 2026, the EBU published a preliminary summary of the rules for the 2027 contest.[^cite_note-99][^cite_note-100] Among the most notable changes, the minimum age for contestants was raised to 18 on the day of their first rehearsal, instead of 16 as had been the case since [1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_Song_Contest_1990), in order to protect 16 and 17-year-olds from excess pressure associated with taking part in the contest, and further measures are implemented to limit excessive use of pre-recorded backing vocals.[^cite_note-101] *[Aftonbladet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftonbladet)* reported the following day, citing a letter by the EBU to its members, that additional proposed changes include moving public voting online entirely and requiring viewers to vote for more than one entry.[^cite_note-102][^cite_note-103]
