**Diré** is a town and commune on the left bank of the [Niger River](21437.md) in the [Tombouctou Region](1164276.md) of [Mali](19127.md). In the 2009 census the population of the commune was 22,365.[^cite_note-2] The town is the administrative center of the [Diré Cercle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dir%C3%A9_Cercle). There are several languages spoken, but the main language is [Songhay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songhay_languages). The population is predominantly [Muslim](6037917.md). Situated on the [Niger River](21437.md), the principal industries are agriculture and commerce.
