The **2026 Jack Link's 500** was an [NASCAR Cup Series](56571945.md) [race](2266230.md) that was held on April 26, 2026, at [Talladega Superspeedway](681674.md) in [Lincoln, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln,_Alabama). Contested over 188 laps on the 2.66 mile (4.28 km) superspeedway, it was the 10th race of the [2026 NASCAR Cup Series](79747972.md) season.

That was the final superspeedway race of 2008 & 2023 winner [Kyle Busch](1467805.md) as well his #8 car before his sudden passing about 1 month later.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

[Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar) won the race for his first career Cup Series win. [Chris Buescher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Buescher) finished 2nd, and [Alex Bowman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Bowman) finished 3rd. [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) and [Zane Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Smith_\(racing_driver\)) rounded out the top five, and [Ricky Stenhouse Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Stenhouse_Jr.), [Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain), [Austin Cindric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Cindric), [Noah Gragson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Gragson), and [Kyle Busch](1467805.md) rounded out the top ten.

## Report

### Background



[Talladega Superspeedway](681674.md), the track where the race will be held.

[Talladega Superspeedway](681674.md), formerly known as Alabama International Motor Speedway, is a motorsports complex located north of [Talladega, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talladega,_Alabama). It is located on the former [Anniston Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anniston_Air_Force_Base) in the small city of Lincoln. A tri-oval, the track was constructed in 1969 by the [International Speedway Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Speedway_Corporation), a business controlled by the [France family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_family). Talladega is most known for its steep banking. The track currently hosts [NASCAR's Cup Series](56571945.md), [Xfinity Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xfinity_Series) and [Craftsman Truck Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craftsman_Truck_Series). Talladega is the longest NASCAR oval with a length of 2.66-mile-long (4.28 km) tri-oval like the [Daytona International Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytona_International_Speedway), which is 2.5-mile-long (4.0 km).

On October 4, 2024, [Jack Link's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Link's) was announced as the title sponsor for the race, ending an 11-year run by its predecessor, [GEICO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GEICO).[^cite_note-10]

### Radio

[MRN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motor_Racing_Network) had the radio call for the race which was also simulcasted on [Sirius XM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirius_XM_Holdings) [NASCAR Radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirius_XM_NASCAR_Radio). [Dave Moody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Moody_\(sportscaster\)) called the race from the Sunoco spotters stand outside turn 2 when the field raced through turns 1 and 2. Tim Catalfamo will call the race from a platform inside the backstretch when the field raced down the backstretch and Dan Hubbard called the race from the Sunoco spotters stand outside turn 4 when the field raced through turns 3 and 4. Steve Post, Chris Wilner, Jason Toy, and Jacklyn Drake worked pit road for the radio side for MRN.
