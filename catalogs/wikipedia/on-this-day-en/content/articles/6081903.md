The **Rajamangala National Stadium** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): ราชมังคลากีฬาสถาน; [RTGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Thai_General_System_of_Transcription): *Ratchamangkhala Kila Sathan*, pronounced [\[râːt.t͡ɕʰā.māŋ.kʰā.lāː kīː.lāː sā.tʰǎːn\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Thai)) is the national stadium of [Thailand national football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand_national_football_team) and the largest stadium and sports venue by capacity in the country. It is part of the Hua Mak Sports Complex, and is located in [Hua Mak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hua_Mak) Subdistrict, [Bang Kapi, Bangkok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Kapi_District). Its official opening on 6 December 1998 coincided with the beginning of the [1998 Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Asian_Games).[^cite_note-2]

Rajamangala National Stadium serves as Thailand's primary venue for football matches, concerts, and major sporting events, including those of the Thailand national team and AFF Championship games. The National Stadium hosted the opening and closing ceremonies of the [2025 SEA Games](45634454.md).[^cite_note-\:22-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-\:23-5] Previous concerts held at the Rajamangala National Stadium includes [Blackpink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpink), [BTS](39862325.md), [Coldplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coldplay) and other prominent international artistes.[^cite_note-\:13-6][^cite_note-\:14-7][^cite_note-\:15-8][^cite_note-\:16-9][^cite_note-\:17-10][^cite_note-\:18-11][^cite_note-\:19-12]

Hua Mak serves as the main hub for sports and recreation in Bangkok, primarily anchored by the expansive Hua Mak Sports Complex managed by the [Sports Authority of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Authority_of_Thailand). Rajamangala National Stadium is part of the wider Hua Mak Sporting Complex.

Directly just outside Rajamangala National Stadium is the **Ramkhamhaeng Night Market**. Held everyday except for Wednesdays, the bustling market attracts locals all over Bangkok looking for affordable and delicious food.

## Overview



Aerial view of Hua Mak Sports Complex, included Rajamangala Stadium and [Indoor Stadium Huamark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indoor_Stadium_Huamark) (2022)

The stadium was first used for the [1998 Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Asian_Games) in 1998 and [1999 ASEAN University Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASEAN_University_Games). Also was the main venue for the [2007 Summer Universiade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Summer_Universiade) when hosted the football finals and the opening and closing ceremonies and will be the main venue for the [2025 SEA Games](45634454.md) when hosting men's football and the ceremonies. Since then, it has been used for many international matches and football tournaments. Most notably, for the [2007 AFC Asian Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_AFC_Asian_Cup). Thai club sides have also used the stadium when playing in continental cup competitions. Krung Thai Bank FC (now [BG Pathum United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BG_Pathum_United_F.C.)) used it for [AFC Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Champions_League) matches, and [PEA FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buriram_United_F.C.) and [Chonburi FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chonburi_F.C.) have recently used it in the [AFC Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Cup). Aside from football, it has been used for athletics, pop concerts, and political rallies.[^cite_note-13] In addition, Rajamangala Stadium was built to honor King [Bhumibol Adulyadej](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhumibol_Adulyadej) on the occasion of His Majesty the King's 5th Cycle Birthday Anniversary, 5 December 1987, and the Rajamangala Celebrations (The Celebrations of the longest reigning Thai monarch) for King Bhumibol Adulyadej, 2nd July 1988.[^cite_note-14]

The capacity of the stadium is 65,000. When the stadium first opened, its capacity was 80,000. But plastic seats were installed on the North, South, and East stands, where previously there had been bare concrete steps, in preprepation for the 2007 AFC Asian Cup.[^cite_note-15]

On 16 September 2019 [Sports Authority of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Authority_of_Thailand) has been closed for renovation to be used as one of the stadiums for [2020 AFC U-23 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_AFC_U-23_Championship), which Thailand hosted in January 2020 to select 3 teams to compete in the [2020 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Summer_Olympics) in Tokyo, Japan.[^cite_note-16]

On 12 July 2022, Rajamangala Stadium held the world-class football match for teams in the [Premier League](11250.md) named "The MATCH Final Bangkok Century Cup 2022" between [Manchester United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C.) vs. [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.), with improvements of the field and stadium to support the competition.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

## Hua Mak Sporting Complex

The Hua Mak Sporting Complex contains 4 Stadiums, 3 Gyms, 1 Olympic Pool, 1 Skate Park, 1 Shooting Range, 6 Tennis Courts, Soccer Fields and 1 Climbing Range.[^cite_note-\:3-19][^cite_note-\:4-20][^cite_note-\:5-21][^cite_note-\:6-22][^cite_note-\:9-23][^cite_note-\:8-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-\:24-26]. In total, the Hua Mak Sporting Complex has specialized facilities for over 20 different sports.

The Hua Mak Sports Complex plays host to the Sports Authority of Thailand governing body and 28 of 37 sports associations of Thailand. The sports associations are under the National Olympic Committee of Thailand and under the Thai Royal Patronage.[^cite_note-\:12-27] It is also the headquarters for Southeast Asia's largest biannual sporting games, the [SEA Games](1575383.md) which typically attracts millions of attendees and viewers all over Southeast Asia across 2 weeks. [^cite_note-28]

## Recent Developments

In 2024, the Sports Authority of Thailand (SAT) announced a THB 5-billion (US\$150 million) makeover budget for the [Rajamangala National Stadium](6081903.md). Improvements include upgrading the security system, smart facilities and sound system within the arena.[^cite_note-\:1-29]

The makeover was completed before the opening ceremony of the 2025 SEA Games and transformed Rajamangala National Stadium into a Smart Stadium with upgraded sound system to deliver sound to every seat.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31] This is in line with the vision of transforming Rajamangala National Stadium into a world-class sports and concert venue.

The area has recently been going through a renaissance in its retail scene with 3 recent major mall renovation in its area such as The Mall Lifestore Bangkapi (2022 - 20 billion THB), Huamark Center (2023 - 300 million THB) and The Mall Ramkhamhaeng (2026 - 1.3 billion THB).[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34]

Rajamangala Stadium's surrounding amenities are well supported by 3 major shopping malls (named above) and 3 major night/community markets in its vicinity.

## Transportation

#### MRT Orange Line



Bangkok Metro Map (Orange Line)

Connectivity to Rajamangala National Stadium is set to further improve with the addition of the [MRT Orange Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Line_\(Bangkok\)) in late-2027. The line would interchange with [MRT Blue Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Line_\(Bangkok\)) at [Thailand Cultural Center station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand_Cultural_Centre_MRT_station) and [MRT Yellow Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_Line_\(Bangkok\)) at [Yaek Lam Sali station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaek_Lam_Sali_MRT_station), further boosting the area's connectivity to many other destinations within Bangkok. Rajamangala's Orange Line Station is known as the SAT Station.[^cite_note-\:21-35][^cite_note-36]



Khlong Saen Saep Express Boat Route

#### Khlong Saen Saep Boat Service

Rajamangala National Stadium can be reached via the [Khlong Saen Saep Boat Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khlong_Saen_Saep_boat_service). The closest pier station would be Ramkhamhaeng University (RU) Pier. Connects to key destinations such as The Mall Bangkapi, [Ramkhamhaeng Airport Rail Link (ARL) Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramkhamhaeng_station), [Khlong Tan ARL Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khlong_San_station), Asoke ARL Station, [Phetchaburi MRT Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phetchaburi_MRT_station), [Chit Lom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chit_Lom), Nana and [Centralw0rld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CentralWorld) Shopping Mall.[^cite_note-37]

#### Airport Rail Link (ARL)

Commuters are also able to drop off at [Hua Mak ARL Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hua_Mak_station) or Sukhumvit 71 Station of the [Airport Rail Link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport_Rail_Link_\(Bangkok\)). From either stations, Rajamangala National Stadium can be reached within 5-10 minutes via motorbike or car. [^cite_note-38]
