**Max Robert Dowman** (born 31 December 2009) is an English professional [footballer](10568.md) who plays as an [attacking midfielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacking_midfielder) or a [winger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winger_\(association_football\)) for [Premier League](11250.md) club [Arsenal](2174.md).&#x20;

Born in [Chelmsford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelmsford), Dowman began his youth career at local club [Billericay Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billericay_Town) before a move to [Arsenal's Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C._Under-21s_and_Academy) at the age of five. On 14 March 2026, six months after making his competitive debut for Arsenal, he became the [youngest goalscorer in Premier League](33287218.md#Goalscoring) history at 16 years and 73 days old through a goal against [Everton](91155.md). Dowman also became the youngest player to win the Premier League at 16 years and 139 days old after Arsenal won the [2025–26 season](77711894.md).

Dowman has represented [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-19_football_team) at youth level since 2024, becoming the youngest-ever goalscorer for the under-19's on 15 November 2025.

## Early life

Max Robert Dowman was born on 31 December 2009 in [Chelmsford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelmsford), Essex.[^cite_note-2] He grew up in a family of [Arsenal](2174.md) supporters.[^cite_note-3] He is distantly related to [George Male](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Male) who played for Arsenal either side of the Second World War: Male's two-times great-grandfather is also Dowman's four-times great-grandfather.[^cite_note-4]

## Club career

Dowman joined the [youth academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C._Under-21s_and_Academy) of [Arsenal](2174.md) in 2015,[^cite_note-5] following time in the youth set-up at [Billericay Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billericay_Town_F.C.).[^cite_note-6] He started playing with their [Arsenal U18s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal_F.C._Under-21s_and_Academy) in September 2023 at the age of 13.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-GiveMe-8] On 19 September 2024, he scored in the Arsenal U19's [UEFA Youth League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UEFA_Youth_League_league_phase) match against [Atalanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atalanta_BC_Youth_Sector) at the age of 14 years, eight months, and 19 days. He therefore became the youngest goal-scorer in the [competition's history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Youth_League).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] In December 2024, after becoming the youngest ever [Premier League 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League_2) player at 14 years old,[^cite_note-12] he began training with the first team.[^cite_note-13]

### 2025–present: Debut season

On 23 July 2025, he made his first-team debut in a pre-season friendly against [AC Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AC_Milan) aged 15 years old in Singapore.[^cite_note-14] On 23 August, he made his [Premier League](11250.md) debut, coming on as a [substitute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitute_\(association_football\)) for Arsenal in the 64th minute of their match against [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.), becoming the second youngest player in the league's history at 15 years and 235 days, behind only his teammate, [Ethan Nwaneri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Nwaneri).[^cite_note-15] He won a [penalty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_kick_\(association_football\)) for his team in the third minute of [stoppage time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoppage_time), helping Arsenal score their fifth goal in a 5–0 victory over Leeds United.[^cite_note-16] Owing to his age, he is obliged to be chaperoned whilst with the team and is not allowed to change in the main dressing room under FA safeguarding regulations.[^cite_note-17]

On 29 October 2025, Dowman became Arsenal's youngest ever starter when he was named in the [starting XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starting_lineup) for the fourth round of the [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) against [Brighton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighton_&_Hove_Albion_F.C.) at the [Emirates Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emirates_Stadium), aged 15 years and 302 days, breaking the previous club record set by goalkeeper [Jack Porter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Porter_\(footballer\)) (16 years and 72 days).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] He played approximately 71 minutes, being substituted in the 71st minute for [Bukayo Saka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bukayo_Saka), as Arsenal secured a 2–0 victory to advance to the quarter‑finals.[^cite_note-20] During the match, Dowman stood out for his dribbling, courage and composure, drawing praise from manager [Mikel Arteta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikel_Arteta) who noted that "For him, everything is natural, for him everything is OK. It is the way he plays. That's the secret, that he doesn't make a big fuss of it. He just does what he does best which is to play football with a lot of courage and determination."[^cite_note-21]

On 4 November 2025, he appeared as a 72nd-minute substitute during Arsenal's 3–0 away victory against [Slavia Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SK_Slavia_Prague) in the league phase of the [2025–26 UEFA Champions League](74127997.md), becoming the youngest player to appear in the competition at 15 years and 308 days old, surpassing the previous record set by [Youssoufa Moukoko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youssoufa_Moukoko) who was 16 years and 18 days old when he made his UCL debut.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] On 30 January 2026, Arsenal announced that Dowman had signed a pre-contract agreement with the club that would lead to him signing professional terms when he turns 17.[^cite_note-24] On 14 March, Dowman came on as a substitute for [Martín Zubimendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mart%C3%ADn_Zubimendi) and scored in the 97th minute during Arsenal's 2–0 home victory against [Everton](91155.md) to become the [youngest goalscorer in both the Premier League](33287218.md#Goalscoring) and the club's history.[^cite_note-25] He achieved this feat at 16 years and 73 days old, breaking the previous record held by [James Vaughan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Vaughan_\(footballer,_born_1988\)) from 2005.[^cite_note-26]

On 19 May 2026, Dowman became the youngest Premier League winner ever, aged 16 years and 139 days old, surpassing the previous record of [Phil Foden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Foden).[^cite_note-27] On 24 May 2026, during the final match of the [2025–26 Premier League season](77711894.md) between Arsenal and [Crystal Palace](385313.md), he became the youngest starting player in the league's history, at 16 years and 144 days old.[^cite_note-28] He benefited from extensive squad rotation implemented by Mikel Arteta prior to the [Champions League final](76987191.md), with Arsenal having already secured first place in the league.[^cite_note-29]

## International career

[^cite_note-30][A European Under-17 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_UEFA_European_Under-17_Championship).[^cite_note-31] He featured as a starter in all three of England's games, including a 4–2 win against the [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic_national_under-17_football_team), where he scored the opening goal.[^cite_note-32]

Dowman made his [England U19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-19_football_team) debut during a 2–0 win over [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine_national_under-19_football_team) at [Pinatar Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinatar_Arena) on 3 September 2025.[^cite_note-33] Dowman became the youngest-ever goalscorer for the England U19 side, beating [Ryan Sessegnon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Sessegnon)'s record from 2016, in a 7–0 win against [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales_national_under-19_football_team).[^cite_note-34]

## Style of play

Comfortable playing in a number of positions, Dowman has been used as an [attacking midfielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacking_midfielder) and as a traditional '10' for Arsenal's youth teams, but can also play as a [right winger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_winger). He has been described as "skillful, energetic and naturally gifted in the final third".[^cite_note-GiveMe-8][^cite_note-35] Technically, Dowman's set-piece delivery stands out. He has been entrusted with corner and free-kick duties at youth level.[^cite_note-36] Arsenal youth team coach Gustavo Oliveira labelled Dowman the "next [Kaká](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kak%C3%A1)".[^cite_note-37]
