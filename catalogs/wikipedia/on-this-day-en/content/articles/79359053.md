Since the [start of 2025](71001703.md#January_2025:_Intensified_hostilities), the conflict in eastern [Democratic Republic of Congo](76762.md) has intensified. On 26 January, the [March 23 Movement](35878650.md) (M23), along with allied [Rwandan](25645.md) forces, [seized control of Goma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Goma_offensive), the capital of [North Kivu](446958.md). By 27 January, the M23 had taken control of significant areas of the city after fierce clashes with DRC forces.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## Humanitarian situation

As of 3 February, over 700,000 people are displaced within Goma.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-2] 7.8 million people are internally displaced in the whole country (as of December 2024).[^cite_note-3] Attacks and armed clashes were responsible for more than 89 percent of these displacements, while the remaining 11 percent were caused by natural disasters. North Kivu, [South Kivu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Kivu_Sugar_Refinery), [Ituri](1545860.md), and [Tanganyika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanganyika_Province) are the provinces most affected.[^cite_note-4]
