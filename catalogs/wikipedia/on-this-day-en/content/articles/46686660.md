**Purvanchal Expressway**[^cite_note-1] is a 340.8 [km](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Km) long, 6-lane wide (expandable to 8) [access-controlled expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controlled-access_highway) in the state of [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md), [India](14533.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-foundation_stone-3] The expressway connects [Chand Saray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chand_Sarai) village near [Gosainganj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosainganj,_Lucknow) in [Lucknow district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucknow_district) with Haydaria village on [NH-31](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_31_\(India\)) in [Ghazipur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghazipur_district). It is developed by the [Uttar Pradesh Expressways Industrial Development Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttar_Pradesh_Expressways_Industrial_Development_Authority) (UPEIDA). The Purvanchal Expressway has a 3.2 km long [airstrip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway) at Akhalkiri Karwat village near [Kurebhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurebhar) in [Sultanpur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanpur_district) for emergency landing of aircraft.[^cite_note-4] The construction work was started by the [UPEIDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UPEIDA) on 10 October 2018 and was inaugurated and opened to the public on 16 November 2021.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

The project was announced and laid down by the then [Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_Uttar_Pradesh) [Akhilesh Yadav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akhilesh_Yadav) as Lucknow-Azamgarh-Ballia Samajwadi Purvanchal Expressway in May 2015.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] Later, route was changed to Lucknow-Azamgarh-Ghazipur by the [Yogi Adityanath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogi_Adityanath) government, and upon acquisition of around 95% of required land, [foundation stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundation_stone) of Purvanchal Expressway was laid on 14 July 2018 by Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](444222.md).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-PIB_foundation-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] With a total project value of [₹](490783.md)22,494 [crore](121420.md), including the [land acquisition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_acquisition_in_India) cost, the Expressway was India's longest expressway at the time of completion.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

The expressway is to be linked with [Varanasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varanasi)–[Azamgarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azamgarh) highway through a separate link road. UPEIDA is also constructing the [Gorakhpur Link Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorakhpur_Link_Expressway), which will connect Jaitpur village in [Gorakhpur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorakhpur_district) with Purvanchal Expressway at Salarpur village in [Azamgarh district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azamgarh_district). Upon the completion of 17 [km](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Km) long, 4-[lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lane) wide *Buxar–Ghazipur Elevated Road* ([Bharauli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharauli,_Bhojpur) near [Buxar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buxar) to Haydaria in [Ghazipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghazipur)), [Lucknow](392095.md) in [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md) will be directly connected to [Buxar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buxar), [Arrah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrah) and [Patna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patna) in [Bihar](442482.md) by Purvanchal Expressway and [NH-922](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_922_\(India\)).[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

5 industrial corridors will be set up alongside Purvanchal Expressway. As on Nov., 2023, the land has been identified and process for bainama is underway. In Ghazipur district, 25% bainama of land has been done till May, 2024.[^cite_note-19]

In Ghazipur IMLC, 83.89% of Proposed Land has been acquired as on 24.02.2026.<https://upeida.up.gov.in/downloadmedia/siteContent/20260224134408084793rd%20Board%20Meeting.pdf>

## Route

The Purvanchal Expressway connects Chand Saray village near [Gosainganj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosainganj,_Lucknow) in [Lucknow district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucknow_district) with Haydaria village on [Mohammadabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammadabad,_Ghazipur)–[Buxar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buxar) highway ([NH-31](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_31_\(India\))) in [Ghazipur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghazipur_district). It passes through 9 districts of [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md) i.e. (from west to east) [Lucknow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucknow_district), [Barabanki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barabanki_district), [Amethi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amethi_district), [Sultanpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanpur_district), [Ayodhya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayodhya_district), [Ambedkar Nagar](2747680.md), [Azamgarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azamgarh_district), [Mau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mau_district) and [Ghazipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghazipur_district).[^cite_note-20]

## Construction

The [UPEIDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UPEIDA) divided the construction work of 340.8 km long Purvanchal Expressway into 8 packages, which were awarded to 5 different contractors.[^cite_note-Tender-22] The design work was done by Egis India Consulting Engineers (Package 1 to 4) and Ayesa India (Package 5 to 8).

22 Flyovers, 7 Railway-over-bridges (ROB), 7 major bridges, 114 minor bridges, 6 [Toll Plazas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toll_Plaza), 45 vehicular-underpasses (VUP), 139 Light VUP, 87 pedestrian underpass and 525 [Box culverts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culvert) has been constructed.[^cite_note-23] Total construction cost (excluding the cost of Land) is around [₹](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E2%82%B9)11,216 [crores](121420.md).

## Inauguration

[Prime Minister Narendra Modi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_Narendra_Modi) arrived at the inauguration site in the [C-130 Hercules](7697.md) and later touch and go exercise was also performed on the expressway by the [Indian Air Force](403355.md) (IAF).[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] Indian Air Force fighter jets performed the 'touch and go' operation, under which 30 fighter planes touched the Purvanchal expressway airstrip and then took off. Fighter jets including Mirage 2000, AN-32, Jaguar, and Sukhoi performed touch and go operations and showcased a landing on the expressway. The Indian aircraft showcased tricolor in the sky.[^cite_note-28]

## Controversies and Criticism

The first seasonal rains in October 2022 caused wide stretches of road caving deep in, some extending to even 15 feet deep. Accidents were reported at several places, with multiple injuries and damage to numerous cars garnering significant coverage in media. The much proclaimed dream project of [Yogi Adityanath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogi_Adityanath), built over a whopping 22000 crore expense by the [Yogi government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Yogi_Adityanath_ministry) and inaugurated by Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](444222.md) in November 2021, faced subsequent backlash and criticism for the alleged irregularities and corruption in construction of the expressway which couldn't withstand the first few hours of rain within a year following its inauguration.[^cite_note-61][^cite_note-62][^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65][^cite_note-66][^cite_note-67][^cite_note-68]
