The **politics of Hungary** take place in a framework of a [parliamentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system) [representative democratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representative_democratic) [republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_republic). The [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Hungary) is the [head of government](37570.md) of a [pluriform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurality_voting_system) [multi-party system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party_system), while the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_heads_of_state_of_Hungary) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and holds a largely ceremonial position. Since 2024, the country has been considered "[no longer a full democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_autocracy)" by the [EU](9317.md), and is generally said to have [democratically backslid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_backsliding) since 2010, when the [Fidesz–KDNP Party Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidesz%E2%80%93KDNP_Party_Alliance) led by [Viktor Orbán](750582.md) won a two-thirds parliamentary [supermajority](638313.md) and adopted a new [constitution of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Hungary).[^cite_note-1] However, the victory of [Péter Magyar](76453946.md) in the [2026 Hungarian parliamentary election](79337461.md) may bring about changes, as he promised to restore the order of democracy. [^cite_note-2]

[Executive power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_power) is exercised by the [government](17048864.md). [Legislative power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_power) is vested in both the [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forms_of_government) and the parliament. Until [the last election in 2026](79337461.md), the party system had been dominated by the conservative [Fidesz](404647.md). The three larger oppositions were [Democratic Coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Coalition_\(Hungary\)) (DK), [Momentum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Momentum_Movement) and [Jobbik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jobbik); there were also opposition parties with a small fraction in parliament (e.g. [Politics Can Be Different](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMP_%E2%80%93_Hungary's_Green_Party)). The [judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary) is independent of the executive and the legislature.

[Hungary](13275.md) is an independent state which has been a parliamentary republic since 1989 and a member of the [European Union](9317.md) since 2004. Legislative power is exercised by the unicameral [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Hungary) that consists of 199 members, who are elected for four-year terms.

In the [April 2026 election](79337461.md), [Péter Magyar](76453946.md)'s [Tisza Party](76606756.md) won a two-thirds supermajority in parliament, decisively beating Orbán's incumbent [Fidesz–KDNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidesz%E2%80%93KDNP) party. Magyar became prime minister on 9 May 2026.[^cite_note-3]

The [president of the republic](380393.md), elected by the [National Assembly](585416.md) every five years, has a largely ceremonial role, but he is nominally the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the armed forces and his powers include the nomination of the [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Hungary), who is to be elected by a majority of the votes of the members of Parliament, based on the recommendation made by the president of the republic. If the president dies, resigns or is otherwise unable to carry out his duties, the [speaker of the National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_National_Assembly_of_Hungary) becomes acting president.

Due to the [Hungarian Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Hungary), based on the post-[World War II](32927.md) [Basic Law of the Federal Republic of Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Law_for_the_Federal_Republic_of_Germany), the prime minister has a leading role in the executive branch as he selects [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_\(government\)) ministers and has the exclusive right to dismiss them (similarly to the competences of the German federal chancellor). Each cabinet nominee appears before one or more parliamentary [committees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee) in consultative open hearings, survive a vote by the Parliament and must be formally approved by the president.

The laws were once decided by the [Diet of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diet_of_Hungary) but are now decided by the [National Assembly](585416.md).

In [Communist Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_People's_Republic), the executive branch of the government was represented by the [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_the_Hungarian_People's_Republic).

[Parliament of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Hungary)

The [unicameral](204304.md), 199-member [National Assembly](585416.md) (*Országgyűlés*) is the highest organ of state authority and initiates and approves legislation sponsored by the prime minister. Its members are elected for a four-year term. The [election threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election_threshold) is 5%, but it only applies to the multi-seat constituencies and the compensation seats, not the single-seat constituencies.

The 2026 election brought dramatic changes to Hungary's politics. [Respect and Freedom Party (Tisza party)](76606756.md), which was in its current form established in 2024[^cite_note-5], defeated the [Fidesz–KDNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidesz%E2%80%93KDNP) coalition, who had been governing for 16 years and who have won every election since 2010 with a two-thirds majority (such a supermajority is needed to enact constitutional amendments and cardinal laws). This shift in power has been variously described as a change of regimes, or a voters' revolution.[^cite_note-6]

The election also brought drastic changes to Fidesz's previous opposition. Out of the [6 parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_for_Hungary) whose coalition was the main opposition force in 2022, 5 decided to step back from election in 2026 in favour of Tisza. Only [DK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Coalition_\(Hungary\)) ran, but they only received 1% of the party list votes, while 5% is needed to get into the legislature. The [Hungarian Two-Tailed Dog Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Two-Tailed_Dog_Party), a party that was originally a joke party, not only failed to get in, but even failed to get 1% of party list votes, which means they have to pay back all 686 million HUF (more than 2 million USD) campaign support they received from the state.[^cite_note-7]

The only party to get into the National Assembly other than Tisza and Fidesz-KDNP is the far-right [Our Homeland Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Homeland_Movement).

[Constitutional Court of Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Hungary)

A fifteen-member [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Hungary) has power to challenge legislation on grounds of [unconstitutionality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unconstitutionality). This body was last filled in July 2010. Members are elected for a term of twelve years. Critics of the ruling coalition contend that since the [Hungarian government](17048864.md) filled the Constitutional Court with loyal judges, the institution mostly serves to legitimize government interests and has lost its original purpose as democratic defender of the [rule of law](25166191.md) and of [human rights](13831.md) - as several reports of independent human rights NGOs, such as the [Hungarian Helsinki Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Helsinki_Committee) emphasize.[^cite_note-8]

The president of the [Supreme Court of Hungary](42981148.md) ([Curia](42981148.md)) and the Hungarian civil and penal legal system he leads is fully independent of the Executive Branch.

The [attorney general](54376.md) or chief [prosecutor](440936.md) of Hungary is currently fully independent of the executive branch, but his status is actively debated.

Several [ombudsman](26289198.md) offices exist in Hungary to protect civil, minority, educational and ecological rights in non-judicial matters. They have held the authority to issue legally binding decisions since late 2003.

## Administrative divisions

Hungary is divided into 19 [counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Hungary) (*vármegyék*, singular: *vármegye*), and a capital city (*főváros*). Counties have been called *megye* from 1949 until 1 January 2023, when a controversial constitutional amendment took effect reverting to the original historical terminology[^cite_note-9]. However, due to another amendment enacted by the [Magyar Government](82935913.md), starting from 1 October 2026, the terminology will be reversed to *megye*.[^cite_note-10]

The counties: [Bács-Kiskun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A1cs-Kiskun), [Baranya](923403.md), [Békés](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A9k%C3%A9s_County), [Borsod-Abaúj-Zemplén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borsod-Aba%C3%BAj-Zempl%C3%A9n), [Csongrád-Csanád](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Csongr%C3%A1d-Csan%C3%A1d_County), [Fejér](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fej%C3%A9r), [Győr-Moson-Sopron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gy%C5%91r-Moson-Sopron), [Hajdú-Bihar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajd%C3%BA-Bihar), [Heves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heves_County), [Jász-Nagykun-Szolnok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%A1sz-Nagykun-Szolnok), [Komárom-Esztergom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kom%C3%A1rom-Esztergom), [Nógrád](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%B3gr%C3%A1d_County), [Pest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pest_County), [Somogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somogy_County), [Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szabolcs-Szatm%C3%A1r-Bereg), [Tolna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolna_County), [Vas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vas_County), [Veszprém](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veszpr%C3%A9m_County), [Zala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zala_County). Budapest is not part of a county.

Two, less often used divisions are districts (*járások*, singular: *járás*), which compose counties, and regions (*régiók*, singular: *régió*), which are composed of one or more counties.&#x20;

The area of Hungary is also divided into 3155 municipalities (*települések*, singular: *település*). Out of this, there are 348 cities and 2807 villages.[^cite_note-11]

## Reversal of ICC withdrawal

In May 2026, the newly elected government of Prime Minister Péter Magyar reversed Hungary's planned withdrawal from the [International Criminal Court](14880.md) (ICC). On 27 May 2026, the National Assembly voted to remain a member, overturning a decision made by the previous Orbán government. Prime Minister Magyar stated that Hungary would now be obliged to arrest Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md), who is subject to an ICC arrest warrant, if he enters Hungarian territory.[^cite_note-12]
