[Elena Rybakina](55541443.md) defeated two-time defending champion [Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md)[^cite_note-1] in the final, 6–4, 5–7, 6–2 to win the women's singles tennis title at the [2026 US Open](80928175.md). It was her third [major](197638.md#Tournaments) title (and second of the season, having also won the [Australian Open](81715708.md)) and 14th [WTA Tour](1398173.md) title overall.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Rybakina was the sixth women's singles player (since 1988) to win both [hardcourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardcourt) majors in the same year, with Sabalenka and Rybakina also being the first pair of women's singles players to contest both hardcourt major finals in the same year since [Steffi Graf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steffi_Graf) and [Arantxa Sánchez Vicario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arantxa_S%C3%A1nchez_Vicario) in [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_WTA_Tour). This was also the first [US Open](82729.md) final contested by the top two seeds since [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

Rybakina attained the [world No. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WTA_number_1_ranked_singles_tennis_players) singles ranking for the first time by reaching the semifinals, becoming the 30th player to attain the top ranking and the first Kazakhstani player to achieve a world No. 1 ranking in any discipline.[^cite_note-6] Sabalenka, [Jessica Pegula](32944701.md), and [Coco Gauff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_Gauff) were also in contention for the top ranking at the beginning of the tournament.

Aged 46, [Venus Williams](167378.md) was the oldest woman to compete in the US Open singles main draw since [Renée Richards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9e_Richards) in [1981](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1981_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), after receiving a wildcard into the main draw.[^cite_note-7] Starting at 12\:13 AM, the first-round match between Williams and [Sofia Kenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Kenin) set the record for the latest start in tournament history, surpassing the previous record (12\:08 AM) set by the third-round match between Sabalenka and [Ekaterina Alexandrova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaterina_Alexandrova) in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-8] [Zheng Qinwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheng_Qinwen) was the first qualifier to reach the quarterfinals since [Emma Raducanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Raducanu) in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-9]

All of the top four seeds reached the semifinals for the first time at the US Open since [1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) and at any major since the [2009 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-10] It was also the first time at the US Open since [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) that the top six seeds reached the quarterfinals.[^cite_note-11] 26 out of the 32 seeds reached the third round, the joint-highest number of seeds at a major since the introduction of the 32-seed system in [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles),[^cite_note-12] equaling the [2007 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-13]
