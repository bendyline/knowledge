**Arthur Rinderknech** ([/rɪndərknɛʃ/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English);[^cite_note-2] born 23 July 1995) is a French professional [tennis](29773.md) player. He has a career-high [ATP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) singles ranking of world No. 24 achieved on 4 May 2026 and a best doubles ranking of No. 104 reached on 16 March 2026. He is currently the No. 2 singles player from France.[^cite_note-3]

His most notable career achievement is a runner-up at the [2025 Shanghai Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Rolex_Shanghai_Masters_%E2%80%93_Singles), an [ATP 1000](30872553.md)-level event. He was defeated by his cousin, [Valentin Vacherot](68016649.md), in the final.&#x20;

Rinderknech represents [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_Davis_Cup_team) at the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup).

## Early life

Rinderknech was born in [Gassin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gassin), France, to parents Pascal Rinderknech and [Virginie Paquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginie_Paquet).[^cite_note-bio-4] He started taking tennis lessons in his early childhood, at age 6, and rapidly took part in junior tournaments.

## Professional career

### 2018–2020: Major singles and doubles debut

Rinderknech made his major main draw debut as a wildcard at the [2018 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_French_Open) in doubles partnering [Florian Lakat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florian_Lakat).

Rinderknech received a main draw invitation to [singles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) at the 2020 French Open, where he lost to [Aljaž Bedene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alja%C5%BE_Bedene) in the first round. He also got a wildcard in [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), partnering with compatriot [Manuel Guinard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Guinard). The pair reached the second round.

### 2021: ATP debut, First Major win, Maiden doubles final

In his debut on the [ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour) in March, Rinderknech qualified for the main draw of the [2021 Open 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Open_13) in Marseille, France, where he reached the quarterfinals, beating [Alejandro Davidovich Fokina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Davidovich_Fokina), before losing to fellow countryman [Ugo Humbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ugo_Humbert).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

In May at the [2021 ATP Lyon Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_ATP_Lyon_Open), Rinderknech recorded his first win, defeating 6th seeded [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md), the first top-20 win in his career, to reach his second career quarterfinal as a [lucky loser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucky_loser).[^cite_note-7]

Rinderknech qualified for the [2021 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Wimbledon_Championships) main draw for the first time in his career.[^cite_note-Agies_Wimby2021-8] In the first round he lost in only the second [12 games all singles supertiebreak](2020378.md#Scoring_a_tiebreak_game) since that rule began two years previously against [Oscar Otte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Otte) 6–4, 3–6, 2–6, 7–6(5), 12–13(2),[^cite_note-9] the other match being the Federer-Djokovic 2019 final.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

In July, he reached the quarterfinals as a qualifier at the [2021 Swedish Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Swedish_Open) in Båstad defeating 4th seed [John Millman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Millman). As a result, he made his top 100 debut on 19 July 2021. At the [2021 Swiss Open Gstaad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Swiss_Open_Gstaad) Rinderknech defeated second seed and World No. 16 [Roberto Bautista Agut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Bautista_Agut), his second top-20 win, to reach the quarterfinals.[^cite_note-12]

The following week, he reached the semifinals for the first time in his career at the [Kitzbühel Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Generali_Open_Kitzb%C3%BChel_%E2%80%93_Singles), losing to eventual winner [Casper Ruud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casper_Ruud).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] As a result, he reached a new career-high of World No. 79 on 2 August 2021.[^cite_note-bio-4][^cite_note-15]

At the [2021 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_US_Open_\(tennis\)) he entered directly into the main draw for the first time and also reached the second round of a Grand Slam for the first time in his career defeating [Miomir Kecmanović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miomir_Kecmanovi%C4%87) after a comeback from two sets down 6–7(10), 3–6, 7–5, 6–3, 6–4.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

At the [2021 Moselle Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Moselle_Open) he reached his first ATP final in his career partnering [Hugo Nys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Nys) where they were defeated by [Hubert Hurkacz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Hurkacz) and Jan [Zieliński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zieli%C5%84ski) from Poland. Following a quarterfinal appearance at the [2021 Stockholm Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Stockholm_Open) where he lost to defending champion, 3rd seed [Denis Shapovalov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Shapovalov), he made his debut in the top 60 at World No. 58 on 15 November 2021.

### 2022: First ATP singles final, Top 50 debut, French No. 1

Rinderknech started his 2022 season by representing France at the [ATP Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_ATP_Cup). France was in Group B alongside Russia, Italy, and Australia. He lost his first match to [Roman Safiullin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Safiullin) of Russia.[^cite_note-18] He lost his second match to [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md).[^cite_note-19] He won his final match by beating [James Duckworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Duckworth_\(tennis\)) of Australia.[^cite_note-20] France ended in fourth place in Group B.

At the [Adelaide International 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Adelaide_International_2_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), he defeated 3rd seed and runner-up of the first tournament, [Karen Khachanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Khachanov), in the quarterfinals,[^cite_note-21] and qualifier and compatriot, [Corentin Moutet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corentin_Moutet), in the semifinals to reach his first final on the ATP Tour.[^cite_note-22] In the final, he pushed Australian wildcard, [Thanasi Kokkinakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanasi_Kokkinakis), to three sets, but he ended up losing the match.[^cite_note-23] As a result of reaching the final, he made his debut in the world's top 50 at No. 48 on 17 January 2022. Ranked No. 48 in his debut at the [Australian Open](69399815.md), he won his first match at this Major defeating Australian [Alexei Popyrin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexei_Popyrin) in the first round.[^cite_note-24] He was set to play 24th seed, [Dan Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Evans_\(tennis\)), in the second round, but he withdrew due to a wrist injury.[^cite_note-25]

In February, Rinderknech competed at the [Qatar ExxonMobil Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Qatar_ExxonMobil_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles). He beat seventh seed, [Alexander Bublik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Bublik), in the second round.[^cite_note-26] He then upset top seed and world No. 12, [Denis Shapovalov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Shapovalov), in the quarterfinals which not only earned him his third Top 20 win of his career, but he also reached his second semifinal of the year.[^cite_note-27] He lost in his semifinal match to third seed, world No. 22, and defending champion, [Nikoloz Basilashvili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikoloz_Basilashvili).[^cite_note-28] In [Dubai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Dubai_Tennis_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), he fell in the first round to eighth seed, world No. 15, and 2018 champion, [Roberto Bautista Agut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Bautista_Agut).[^cite_note-29]

Representing France during the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Davis_Cup_qualifying_round) tie against Ecuador, Rinderknech played one match and won over [Emilio Gómez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilio_G%C3%B3mez_\(tennis\)); France ended up beating Ecuador 4–0.[^cite_note-30]

At the [Indian Wells Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_BNP_Paribas_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), he lost in the first round to [Benjamin Bonzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Bonzi).[^cite_note-31] Seeded third at the [Arizona Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Arizona_Tennis_Classic_%E2%80%93_Singles), an ATP Challenger tournament in Phoenix, he was defeated in the second round by [Liam Broady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Broady).[^cite_note-32] At the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Miami_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), he recorded his first win at the Masters 1000 level against [Laslo Djere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laslo_Djere) but lost in the second round to eighth seed, world No. 10, and defending champion, [Hubert Hurkacz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Hurkacz).[^cite_note-33]

Starting his clay-court season at the [Monte-Carlo Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Monte-Carlo_Masters_%E2%80%93_Singles), Rinderknech was eliminated in the first round by 2019 champion [Fabio Fognini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabio_Fognini).[^cite_note-34] At the [Lyon Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_ATP_Lyon_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), he lost in the first round to [Holger Rune](60992715.md).[^cite_note-35] Ranked No. 60 at the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), he was defeated in the first round by Bublik.[^cite_note-36] As the top seed at the [Poznań Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Pozna%C5%84_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), an ATP Challenger tournament, Rinderknech won his fourth ATP Challenger title by beating [Tomás Barrios Vera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom%C3%A1s_Barrios_Vera) in the final.[^cite_note-37]

Rinderknech started his grass-court season at the [BOSS Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_BOSS_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) in Stuttgart, Germany. He upset eighth seed and compatriot, [Ugo Humbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ugo_Humbert), in the first round in three sets.[^cite_note-38] He lost in the second round to compatriot Benjamin Bonzi.[^cite_note-39] At the [Halle Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Halle_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), he fell in the first round of qualifying to [Marc-Andrea Hüsler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc-Andrea_H%C3%BCsler).[^cite_note-40] Ranked No. 62 at [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), he lost in the first round to 13th seed, world No. 16, and last year semifinalist, Denis Shapovalov, in five sets.[^cite_note-41] After Wimbledon, Rinderknech competed at the [Salzburg Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Salzburg_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), an ATP Challenger tournament in Austria. As the top seed, he lost in the second round to Austrian wildcard [Lukas Neumayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Neumayer).[^cite_note-42]

At the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_\(tennis\)), he recorded his third Major career win against compatriot [Quentin Halys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Halys). At the [2022 Moselle Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Moselle_Open) in Metz, he lost in the quarterfinals for the second time in the season to world No. 10 and second seed [Hubert Hurkacz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Hurkacz). At the [2022 Tel Aviv Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Tel_Aviv_Open) he also reached the quarterfinals defeating third seed [Diego Schwartzman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Schwartzman) but lost to [Roman Safiullin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Safiullin) for the second time in the season. At the [2022 Gijón Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Gij%C3%B3n_Open) he reached his third tour-level semifinal for the season saving nine match points against second seed [Pablo Carreño Busta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_Carre%C3%B1o_Busta) in an over three hours match.[^cite_note-43] At the [2022 Swiss Indoors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Swiss_Indoors) he defeated fourth seed [Marin Čilić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marin_%C4%8Cili%C4%87) in the first round after qualifying for the main draw.[^cite_note-44] He then won against [Alex Molčan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Mol%C4%8Dan) to reach the quarterfinals. As a result, he became the French No. 1 in the rankings at world No. 42 on 31 October 2022 ahead of [Adrian Mannarino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Mannarino).

Rinderknech received a wildcard to participate in his home tournament, the [2022 Rolex Paris Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Rolex_Paris_Masters) for a second year in a row.[^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46]

### 2023: United Cup debut, First Major third round

Rinderknech started his 2023 season by representing France at the [2023 United Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_United_Cup). France was in Group F alongside Argentina and Croatia. Against Argentina, he beat [Francisco Cerúndolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Cer%C3%BAndolo).[^cite_note-47] France won the tie over Argentina 5–0.[^cite_note-48] Against Croatia, he lost to [Borna Ćorić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borna_%C4%86ori%C4%87) in two tiebreaker sets.[^cite_note-49] Croatia ended up winning the tie over France 3–2.[^cite_note-50] France didn't qualify for the knockout stage; they ended second in Group F. Last year finalist at the [Adelaide International 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Adelaide_International_2_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), he was defeated in the first round by [Marc-Andrea Hüsler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc-Andrea_H%C3%BCsler).[^cite_note-51] At the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles), he lost in the first round to qualifier [Yosuke Watanuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yosuke_Watanuki).[^cite_note-52]

In February, Rinderknech reached the second round at the [Open Sud de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Open_Sud_de_France_%E2%80%93_Singles) in Montpellier, losing to third seed and world No. 23, Borna Ćorić.[^cite_note-53] At the [Rotterdam Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_ABN_AMRO_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), he was defeated in the first round of qualifying by [Jurij Rodionov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurij_Rodionov).[^cite_note-54] In [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Open_13_Provence_%E2%80%93_Singles), he lost in the first round to [Leandro Riedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leandro_Riedi). As the top seed at the [Teréga Open Pau–Pyrénées](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Ter%C3%A9ga_Open_Pau%E2%80%93Pyr%C3%A9n%C3%A9es_%E2%80%93_Singles), he reached the semifinals where he was defeated by compatriot, [Luca Van Assche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luca_Van_Assche), who ended up winning the tournament.

### 2024: Two ATP quarterfinals and Masters third rounds

Rinderknech reached his first quarterfinal for the season in [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Open_13_Provence_%E2%80%93_Singles) as an alternate, defeating [Maxime Cressy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxime_Cressy) and eight seed [Jiří Lehečka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ji%C5%99%C3%AD_Lehe%C4%8Dka) in straight sets. In February, he won his sixth Challenger title at the [Play In Challenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Play_In_Challenger) in [Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille), France, defeating [Joris De Loore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joris_De_Loore) in the final.[^cite_note-55][^cite_note-56] In May, he also reached the quarterfinals in [Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_ATP_Lyon_Open) but withdrew from his match against [Luciano Darderi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luciano_Darderi).

In July, Rinderknech recorded his first win at [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wimbledon_Championships) defeating [Kei Nishikori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kei_Nishikori) in the first round before losing to [Taylor Fritz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Fritz) in the second round.[^cite_note-57]

He reached the third round of a Masters at the [2024 National Bank Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_National_Bank_Open) in Montreal as a qualifier, with main draw wins over compatriot [Adrian Mannarino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Mannarino) and [Flavio Cobolli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavio_Cobolli).[^cite_note-58] As a result he returned to the top 60 in the rankings on 12 August 2024. At the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_\(tennis\)), he defeated [Christopher Eubanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Eubanks) in five sets with a super tiebreak. He lost to sixth seed [Andrey Rublev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Rublev) in a five set match, after being two sets up.[^cite_note-59]

### 2025: Best season: Two top 5 wins, Shanghai final, top 30



Rinderknech at the [2026 Libéma Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Lib%C3%A9ma_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles)

In June, Rinderknech reached the second round at the [Queen's Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Queen's_Club_Championships) by defeating [Ben Shelton](71310148.md) in the first round, his first top 10 win.[^cite_note-60] In July, Rinderknech reached the second round at [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Wimbledon_Championships) by defeating third seed [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md), his first top 5 win.[^cite_note-61] He then reached the third round at the tournament for the first time by defeating [Cristian Garín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristian_Gar%C3%ADn) in five sets over two days.[^cite_note-62] Rinderknech reached his first semifinal of the year at the [Generali Open Kitzbühel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Generali_Open_Kitzb%C3%BChel_%E2%80%93_Singles).[^cite_note-63] He lost to fellow countryman [Arthur Cazaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Cazaux) in the semifinal.[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65]

In September at the [2025 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_US_Open_\(tennis\)), Rinderknech reached the fourth round of a Grand Slam for the first time in his career.[^cite_note-66][^cite_note-AR_Fivethings-67]

At the [2025 Shanghai Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Rolex_Shanghai_Masters) Rinderknech reached his first final at a [Masters 1000](30872553.md). In the third round, he defeated [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md) for a second time in the season, recording his second top 5 win.[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69] Rinderknech went on to upset 15th seed [Jiří Lehečka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ji%C5%99%C3%AD_Lehe%C4%8Dka) in the fourth round,[^cite_note-70] 12th seed [Felix Auger-Aliassime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Auger-Aliassime) in the quarterfinal and 16th seed [Daniil Medvedev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniil_Medvedev) in the semifinal to reach the final. As a result he reached the top 30 at world No. 28 on 13 October 2025.[^cite_note-71] He lost to qualifier [Valentin Vacherot](68016649.md) in the final.[^cite_note-Shanghai-72]

### 2026: Career high ranking, doubles Masters final

In Australia, Rinderknech represented France at the [2026 United Cup](81647045.md), however they failed to make it past the round robin stage. He then lost in the first round of the [Australian Open](81711108.md) in 4 sets to [Fábián Marozsán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A1bi%C3%A1n_Marozs%C3%A1n).

He then lost out to [Stefanos Tsitsipas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefanos_Tsitsipas) in [Rotterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_ABN_AMRO_Open) and, the eventual champion, [Carlos Alcaraz](63121147.md) in [Qatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Qatar_ExxonMobil_Open).[^cite_note-73] He did make the quarter-final of the [Dubai Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Dubai_Tennis_Championships), defeating [Jack Draper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Draper) en-route, however lost out to [Andrey Rublev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Rublev). [^cite_note-74]

At [Indian Wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_BNP_Paribas_Open), Rinderknech lost in the singles in the third round to Alcaraz, despite taking the first set.[^cite_note-75] However, in the doubles, partnered with his cousin, [Valentin Vacherot](68016649.md), the pair defeated [Djokovic](16100029.md) & [Tsitsipas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefanos_Tsitsipas) en-route to their first doubles Masters final.[^cite_note-76] They lost out to [Guido Andreozzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guido_Andreozzi) & [Manuel Guinard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Guinard) in the final.[^cite_note-77]

Rinderknech then failed to make it past the second round of any tournament, apart from retiring in the third round of [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Mutua_Madrid_Open), until the [Geneva Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Geneva_Open), where he lost out to [Alexander Bublik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Bublik) in the quarter-final.[^cite_note-78] On the 4th May, due to a lack of points to defend, Rinderknech reached World No. 24, his highest ranking to date.&#x20;

At [Roland Garros](81641720.md), the Frenchman lost out to eventual quarter-finalist [Matteo Berrettini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matteo_Berrettini) in the second round in straights.[^cite_note-79]

Moving into the grass season, despite losing in the first round in singles at the [Libéma Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Lib%C3%A9ma_Open), he teamed up with [Zizou Bergs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zizou_Bergs) to reach the doubles final in the event, losing out to [Sander Arends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sander_Arends) & [David Pel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Pel) in the final.[^cite_note-80]

## Personal life

Rinderknech's mother, Virginie, is a former professional tennis player on [Women's Tour](1398173.md). He has three sisters, Mathilde, Julie and Flore.

His family is deeply involved in tennis. [Chloé Paquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlo%C3%A9_Paquet)[^cite_note-81] and [Monégasque](19261.md) tennis players [Benjamin Balleret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Balleret)[^cite_note-82] and [Valentin Vacherot](68016649.md) are his cousins.[^cite_note-83]

Rinderknech played college tennis at [Texas A\&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A\&M_University).[^cite_note-84][^cite_note-Agies_Wimby2021-8] He is married to a former collegiate player he met there, Hortense Boscher.

Rinderknech is a fan of French football club [AS Saint-Étienne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AS_Saint-%C3%89tienne).[^cite_note-AR_Fivethings-67]
