The **Canadian women's national ice hockey team** is the [ice hockey](14790.md) team representing [Canada](5042916.md) in women's hockey. The team is overseen by [Hockey Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockey_Canada), a member of the [International Ice Hockey Federation](16760449.md) and participates in international competitions. [Canada](5042916.md) has been a dominant figure in international competition, having won the majority of major [ice hockey](14790.md) tournaments. Canada is rivaled by the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_women's_national_ice_hockey_team), the only other winner of a major tournament.
