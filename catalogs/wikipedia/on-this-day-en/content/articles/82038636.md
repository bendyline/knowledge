A solidarity rally against the Islamic Republic in [Perth, Australia](24355.md), 10 January 2026

Iranian officials and institutions, both from the [Islamic Republic](64702.md) and the [opposition](80207658.md); as well as foreign governments, international organizations, and public figures, issued varied responses to the [2025–2026 Iranian protests](81976180.md). The demonstrations followed by a [nationwide internet shutdown](82063825.md) and a violent security crackdown. Human rights organizations and media outlets reported [nationwide massacre of protesters](82036668.md).

Iranian state authorities defended the crackdown of protests and portrayed the unrest as "violent" or "foreign-backed". In contrast, opposition figures and activists accused the government of ordering or carrying out mass killings of demonstrators. As of 25 February 2026, estimates of the death toll ranged from 3,000 to 36,500, making them some of the [largest massacres in modern Iranian history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_massacres_in_Iran).[^cite_note-iran-intl-12000-1][^cite_note-2000killed-2][^cite_note-CBS_12k_feared_dead-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

Foreign governments and international organizations, particularly in Western countries, condemned the reported use of live ammunition and the high number of fatalities. Several called for accountability and independent international investigations into the violence and warned of potential diplomatic or economic consequences. The [United Nations](31769.md) discussed the situation in emergency meetings and human rights forums, Civil society groups, [Iranian diaspora](9573713.md) communities, and public figures around the world also [organized demonstrations and urged stronger international responses](82164645.md) in response to the mass killings.

Following the protests that began on 14 January, the Iranian regime declared it would arrest anyone who had been on the streets on criminal charges.[^cite_note-6] The Iranian penal system also declared they would create a database of all past and future possible crimes of protestors and their family members.[^cite_note-7] Iran’s supreme leader [Ali Khamenei](385653.md) criticized President [President Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_Trump) for comments regarding the protests, describing him as "a criminal" and "inexperienced", accusing him of interfering in Iran's affairs.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] while Trump has called Khamenei "a sick criminal".[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

The Iranian government shut down the reformist newspaper *[Ham-Mihan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ham-Mihan)* and threatened athletes and public figures against publicly supporting the demonstrations.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] It also imposed extensive security measures in major cities,[^cite_note-14] including machine guns,[^cite_note-15] tanks, drones,[^cite_note-16] a bot army,[^cite_note-17] snipers,[^cite_note-18] and checkpoints in Iranian cities, with soldiers raiding houses and searching door to door in order to arrest protestors.[^cite_note-19] According to several reports, a member of the [Iranian armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_armed_forces) was sentenced to death after refusing orders to fire on civilians.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

U.S. Treasury Secretary [Scott Bessent](38532044.md) stated that U.S. authorities had tracked Iranian officials transferring funds abroad, which he interpreted as an indication that some were preparing to leave the country.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] Iranian opposition figure [Reza Pahlavi](613947.md) criticized the regime’s response to the protests, describing it as a campaign of genocidal repression against Iranians.[^cite_note-26] Khamenei's IRGC commander has called for war, calling the Iranian people "our atom bomb".[^cite_note-27] Foreign Minister [Abbas Araghchi](21951274.md) warned that "war with Iran will be long and extend throughout the region."[^cite_note-28] Iranian regime-aligned Wikipedia editors have attempted to revise, manipulate, and delete Wikipedia articles' accounts of the massacres.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

On 19 January, FARAJA General Ahmed Radan gave a three day ultimatum to everyone involved in any way in the protests, telling them to give up themselves to the police.[^cite_note-31]

The Tehran Stock Exchange reportedly lost 8000 billion toman in total market value two times.[^cite_note-32] Merchants received allowances 20 minutes of limited internet visiting times at the hall of Tehran chamber of commerce.[^cite_note-33]

China has reportedly sent 16 military cargo aircraft to aid the Iranian government.

On 27 January, *Middle East Eye* reported the United States is considering strikes on the Iranian regime and military as soon as this week.[^cite_note-34] That same day, government deputy of digital economy was fired for criticizing class based internet domain access.[^cite_note-35] That same day Trump threatened the Iranian regime that the next round of attacks would cause "major destruction". RFERL/[RadioFarda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Farda) described the Iranian armed forces as "Practically Naked". Spokeswoman Mohajerani stated that Iran will not start a war but will defend itself.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41] Iran's rial, fell to its lowest record 1.5 million to \$1.[^cite_note-42] [LeMonde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemonde) has described the effects of regime massacres in Iran as collective shock.[^cite_note-43] IRGC General Abdulrahim Moussavi of Joint Chief of Staff has threatened without IRGC there will no longer be name for an Iran.[^cite_note-44]

On 27 January, the Iranian regime relayed emergency executive powers to provinces for purposes of government continuity and to accelerate the import of basic commodities.[^cite_note-45] On 28 January, it partially restored internet against the threat of US military intervention.[^cite_note-46] It also started apprehending doctors, one sentenced to death.[^cite_note-47]

Ali Shamkhani  threatened an "unprecedented response" of a declaration of war and preemptive full attacks against the United States and Israel.[^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49]

List of off springs of Iranian regime officials abroad was posted online on January 28th.[^cite_note-50]

On 30 January, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Iraqchi called foreign ministries of six Muslim countries (Qatar, Egypt, the UAE, Pakistan, Oman, and Turkey) and went to Turkey to visit FM Hakan Fidan and President Erdogan.[^cite_note-51]

*The New York Times* wrote that "Iran enjoys diplomatic cover from Moscow and Beijing, The EU condemns but lacks further leverage. Arab leaders prefer a weakened Iran that brutalizes its own people to a liberated Iran that inspires imitation. Campus activists and global do-gooders who profess deep concern for Palestinian lives but show little interest in Iranian ones. It's left to the United States to impose meaningful consequences on the Iranian regime for one of the worst atrocities of this century."[^cite_note-52]

## Sit-in

After the massacres students from 30 universities in all across Iran announced staged [sit-ins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sit-in), including in medical universities, the government had to switch to online classes and exams for the next semesters as well, Minister of Health deputy announced program to alleviate psychological trauma suffered by students.[^cite_note-53][^cite_note-54][^cite_note-55][^cite_note-56]

## Executions

According to an opinion piece by Lawdan Bazargan in *Iran International*, the regime was repeating the 1988 summer massacre at scale.[^cite_note-59] At least 50,000 people have been arrested.[^cite_note-60] IRGC armed troops raided hospitals shooting wounded protestors.[^cite_note-61]

An analysis by Joseph Epstein  suggested Velayat-e Faqih will put ideology over strategy and execute people in large numbers, designating them as Moharebeh ("enemies of God") and *[baghi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghi_\(Islam\))* ("armed rebellion").[^cite_note-62] The government penal system stated they will charge public disruptors with Mohareb.[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65][^cite_note-66][^cite_note-67] National wrestling team wrestler Saleh Mohammadi was sentenced to public execution, held at the [Qom](638742.md) town square.[^cite_note-68]

## International travel and aviation

Amid the unrest, several international airlines cancelled flights to and from Iran. On 9 January 2026, Dubai Airports' website showed at least 17 [flydubai](18432521.md) flights between [Dubai](211583.md) and Iranian cities were cancelled. [Turkish Airlines](167925.md) cancelled 17 flights, while Turkish carriers [AJet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AJet) and [Pegasus](1597891.md) also cancelled flights to Iranian destinations. [Hamad International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamad_International_Airport)'s website showed at least two [Doha](26214389.md)–Tehran flights were cancelled.[^cite_note-115] [IndiGo](2085799.md) and [Air India](26457880.md) also altered flight routes around Iran and flights overflowing Iranian airspace were cancelled temporarily after 26 January.[^cite_note-116]

## Local responses

### Government and state institutions

#### IRIB

IRIB aired 240 forced confessions.[^cite_note-121][^cite_note-122]

IRIB anchor mocked the frozen corpse of massacre victims.[^cite_note-123] IRIB attempted to show that things have calmed down.[^cite_note-124]

An IRIB network channel director was fired after a reporter on appeared to say "[Death to Khamenei](79312660.md)" during the Iranian revolution anniversary rally.[^cite_note-125]

IRIB cut the live broadcast of President Pezeshkian after said channel 6 is campaigning against its presidency.[^cite_note-126]

#### Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps

High ranking generals associated with the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC) have threatened protestors and American president Trump including General Javani, General Rahim Safavi, General Vahidi, General Abdelrahim Mosavi, General Ali Abdellahi, General Ghorbani, General Karami, and General Shekarchi.[^cite_note-127][^cite_note-128][^cite_note-129][^cite_note-130][^cite_note-131][^cite_note-132][^cite_note-133]

Nobel Peace Prize Laureate [Shirin Ebadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirin_Ebadi) has called for taking out IRGC commanders.[^cite_note-134]

#### Cabinet

Iranian National Tax Administration made 90% of businesses tax exempt for the rest of year.[^cite_note-135]

The [Ministry of Welfare and Social Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Welfare_and_Social_Security), [Ministry of Industry, Mine and Trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Industry,_Mine_and_Trade), [Ministry of Economic Affairs and Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economic_Affairs_and_Finance_\(Iran\)) have started a new scheme to pay Iranian families subsidies, amounting \$7 per month per person. It will be deposited to the [National Credit Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Credit_Network), with this money each person can buy some selected items from 9 designated food items.[^cite_note-136]

#### Supreme Leader

[Ali Khamenei](385653.md), the [Supreme Leader of Iran](36814344.md), said, "We talk to the protesters, but the rioters must be put in their place."[^cite_note-137] On 17 January 2026, Khamenei stated that thousands of individuals had been killed, "some in an inhuman, savage manner", and attributed responsibility for these deaths to the United States and Israel.[^cite_note-138][^cite_note-139] Khamenei's [X](9988187.md) profile had posted that "We will not surrender to the enemy", to which Elon Musk replied "زهی خیال باطل" (roughly translated as "delusional thinking").[^cite_note-140] Khamenei also called protestors "harmful individuals for the country".[^cite_note-141] On 24 January, Khamenei went into hiding in underground bunker tunnel space.[^cite_note-142] In response, protesters on social media began mocking Khamenei with the nickname "Moush-Ali" (*Rat-Ali*).[^cite_note-143]

#### President

President [Masoud Pezeshkian](2650383.md) stated in a televised broadcast, "We are at total war with Israel, Europe, and the United States of America",[^cite_note-148] and said that "if we do not resolve the issue of people's livelihoods, we will end up in hell".[^cite_note-149][^cite_note-150][^cite_note-151]

#### Former President

On February 23, 2026, former Iranian president [Mohammad Khatami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Khatami) expressed sympathy with the families of the victims and wishing a speedy recovery for the injured, and he called for the release of those arrested solely for protesting and social helplessness. He also emphasized the need for clear and credible explanations of the incidents, identifying the culprits, halting arrests, summonses, and the issuance of inappropriate verdicts against intellectuals, artists, and political activists.[^cite_note-\:22-152][^cite_note-\:32-153] The former president of Iran expressed hope that university issues would be resolved with wisdom and calm, without political, security, or social violence. He also regarded the upcoming negotiations between Iran and the U.S. as important and expressed hope that these talks would lead to a reduction in the problems and threats faced by the people of Iran and the region. Contrary to the goals of regional adversaries, Khatami hoped that a path of peace, progress, and regional security would be pursued.[^cite_note-\:22-152][^cite_note-\:32-153]

#### Iranian Parliament

On 20 January, as tensions with the US were running high, the [Iranian Parliament](383276.md) threatened that any attack on [Supreme Leader](36814344.md), Ayatollah Ali Khamenei would lead to [Jihad](16203.md).[^cite_note-154]

The security commission has called for assassinating Trump for his role in the protests.[^cite_note-155]

The legal commission stated there is no problem in the country, but for enemies.[^cite_note-156]

Tehran representative called Trump "Yellow Yankee Animal Buffalo".[^cite_note-157]

#### Reformists Front

According to Iran International, amid concerns over the collapse of the Islamic Republic and the fall of Ali Khamenei, in addition to the increasing number of protesters killed by security forces, the Central Council of the [Iranian Reform Front](50063989.md) held an emergency meeting behind closed doors, at the conclusion of which a statement was drafted, calling for the resignation of Khamenei and the transmission of state control to a "transitional council".[^cite_note-\:10-158] However, upon learning of the statement, security forces issued "heavy and blatant" threats to the leaders of the alliance as well as warnings of widespread arrests, and the statement was withdrawn.[^cite_note-\:10-158]

#### Other senior officials

[FARAJA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FARAJA) Chief Shirazi stated that the Islamic Republic is not defeatable.[^cite_note-159] [Fatemeh Mohajerani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatemeh_Mohajerani), the government spokesperson, stated on social media that the government acknowledges the protests and that it would also hear "harsh voices."[^cite_note-160]

On 7 January, Iranian army commander-in-chief Gen. [Amir Hatami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amir_Hatami) said Iran viewed escalating "hostile rhetoric" as a threat and would respond if it continued, after U.S. President Donald Trump and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu voiced support for the protests.[^cite_note-161]

Iranian Chief Justice [Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Eje'i](3002270.md) warned that there would be "no leniency" for those he said were helping "the enemy" amid continuing unrest and blamed the United States and Israel for using "hybrid" methods to destabilize Iran.[^cite_note-Reuters_IR_top_judge_no_leniency-162]

[Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf](1421458.md), Speaker of the [Islamic Consultative Assembly](383276.md), said that "malicious individuals and organised movements" sought to turn demands into "chaos", while also stating that public livelihood-related concerns should be addressed responsibly. Qalibaf threatened that Iran will attack all U.S. bases in the Middle East if the U.S. military strikes Iran.[^cite_note-163][^cite_note-164]

[Amoli Larijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadiq_Larijani), Head of the [Expediency Discernment Council](452730.md), said "criticism and demands are the right of the nation", but argued they should not be expressed in a way that signals to Iran's "enemies".[^cite_note-165]

Foreign minister [Abbas Araghchi](21951274.md) called on U.S. President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) to reopen negotiations in an op-ed published in *The Guardian*, according to Iran International.[^cite_note-166] Araghchi also said that he views the demonstrations of Iranians abroad protesting against the regime as "very dangerous".[^cite_note-IranInternational-167] Araghchi engaged in a diplomatic dispute with German Chancellor [Friedrich Merz](1967626.md) concerning Iran’s domestic protests and the conflict in Gaza. Merz condemned the Iranian regime’s "brutal violence" against protesters, characterizing the crackdown as a "sign of weakness" and asserting that the regime was in its "final days." In response, Araghchi argued that Germany’s credibility on human rights had been "obliterated" by its [support for Israel](78816942.md)’s [military actions in Gaza](74998519.md).[^cite_note-168] Araghchi had later denied Iran beginning negotiations.[^cite_note-169]

[Ahmad-Reza Radan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad-Reza_Radan), Iran's national police chief stated on 19 January, that those who were "deceived" into taking part in the demonstrations, or "riots" as the government defines them, had three days to turn themselves in.[^cite_note-170]

Bahraini cleric Sheikh Issa Qasem said millions of Iranians are ready for sacrifice for Khamenei.[^cite_note-171]

IRGC intelligence chief [Majid Khademi](80084010.md) claimed that "foreign agents" instructed protesters to shoot each other in order to increase the death count and increase the chances of an American attack.[^cite_note-172]

#### Defections

On 18 January, a [Ministry of Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_\(Iran\)) official sent an audio message to *[Iran International](58555280.md)* stating that he had defected, in the sense that he had participated in the protests, where he witnessed security forces firing on protesters using [Heckler & Koch G3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heckler_&_Koch_G3) rifles, and in response to a call by Reza Pahlavi, he had stayed home from work. The official described the government as "ruthless". The official remained anonymous for security reasons.[^cite_note-IranIntl_MoI_official_defects-173]

### State actions and economic measures

On 31 December 2025, after a government building was attacked in [Fasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fasa), authorities placed almost the entire country on leave until 4 January 2026, while stating that the reasons were cold weather and energy saving.[^cite_note-174]

The government paused subsidising the rial-to-U.S.-dollar exchange rate, a method long used to support imports and curb inflation.[^cite_note-175][^cite_note-176]

The Iranian government imported 800 members of foreign militias to quell the protests.[^cite_note-177]

In early February, the Iranian government began a campaign to arrest reformist activists, especially those who are close to President [Masoud Pezeshkian](2650383.md).[^cite_note-178][^cite_note-179][^cite_note-\:42-180]

### Resignations

After the [Iranian rial](708656.md) hit a record low, Mohammad Reza Farzin resigned as the leader of the [Central Bank of Iran](3961876.md) and was replaced with [Abdolnaser Hemmati](58594522.md).[^cite_note-181]

Vice President [Mohammad Reza Aref](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Reza_Aref) tendered his resignation, but it was not accepted by President Pezeshkian.[^cite_note-182]

[Mehdi Sanaei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehdi_Sanaei), the Political Advisor to the President, also resigned.[^cite_note-183]

### State media

[Fars News Agency](5000099.md) described the strike and demonstrations as "riots" and warned of individuals allegedly infiltrating bazaars to turn economic slogans into political ones.[^cite_note-\:4-184]

An IRGC-affiliated local outlet connected to the IRGC [Lorestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorestan) provincial barracks claimed that protesters were being provided ammunition and firearms by trucks without licence plates.[^cite_note-185]

Former IRGC General [Mohammad Ali Jafari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Ali_Jafari) warned Iran would deliver a "crushing blow" in the event of another attack by Israel.[^cite_note-186][^cite_note-187]

[Mehdi Taremi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehdi_Taremi), captain of [Iran's national football team](272865.md), called on officials to listen to public demands and commented on worsening economic conditions.[^cite_note-190] Former Iran national team captain, [Masoud Shojaei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masoud_Shojaei), criticised [FIFA](11049.md) for its silence over the killing of Iranian athletes during the protests.[^cite_note-auto1-191]

### United Nations

On 5 January, [United Nations](31769.md) Secretary-General [António Guterres](471806.md) urged respect for peaceful protest during the unrest.[^cite_note-295] On 11 January, he released a statement saying he was "shocked by reports of violence and excessive use of force by Iranian authorities against protesters across the country, urging restraint and the immediate restoration of communications."[^cite_note-296] In February, he faced backlash for allegedly congratulating Iranian President [Masoud Pezeshkian](2650383.md) on the anniversary of the [Iranian Revolution](347268.md).[^cite_note-297][^cite_note-\:02-298] The UN insisted that the letter was simply a matter of protocol and a "routine diplomatic gesture" and should not been seen as an endorsement of policy.[^cite_note-\:02-298]

On 23 January, the UN Human Rights Council (UNHRC) adopted a resolution condemning the violent suppression of protests in Iran. Twenty-five states voted in favor of the resolution, including [Spain](26667.md), [Italy](14532.md), [Slovenia](27338.md), [Mexico](3966054.md), [Colombia](5222.md), [South Korea](27019.md), and [Ghana](12067.md). Seven states voted against, including [China](5405.md), [India](14533.md), [Vietnam](202354.md), [Iraq](7515928.md), [Indonesia](14579.md), and [Pakistan](23235.md). Fourteen states abstained, including [Brazil](3383.md), [Qatar](8238258.md), [Egypt](8087628.md), [South Africa](17416221.md), [Kenya](188171.md), and [Thailand](30128.md).[^cite_note-299]

### United States



A rally against the Islamic Republic was held in [Lafayette Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette_Square,_Washington,_D.C.), Washington, D.C.

The [United States Department of State](31975.md) expressed support for the protesters and urged the Iranian government to respect citizens' rights and respond to their concerns instead of resorting to "violent silencing."[^cite_note-300]

U.S. President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) said that Iran's leadership has "got a lot of problems. They have tremendous inflation. Their economy is bust. And I know that people aren't so happy," also criticizing the government's violent response to protests: "They kill people. Every time they have a riot, or somebody forms a group, little or big, they start shooting people."[^cite_note-301]

U.S. Ambassador to the United Nations [Mike Waltz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Waltz) posted on social media, "The people of Iran want freedom. They have suffered at the hands of the ayatollahs for too long. We stand with Iranians in the streets of Tehran and across the country as they protest a radical regime that has brought them nothing but economic downturn and war."[^cite_note-302]

On 2 January, [Donald Trump](4848272.md) stated that the U.S. would intervene if Iran responded violently to the protests.[^cite_note-303] Trump stated on [Truth Social](69077351.md), "If Iran shots \[*[sic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sic)*] and violently kills peaceful protesters, which is their custom, the United States of America will come to their rescue. We are locked and loaded and ready to go."[^cite_note-304][^cite_note-305]

On 3 January, after [U.S. strikes that lead to the capture of Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro](82021123.md), [Republican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GOP) senator [Lindsey Graham](363279.md) stated on [X](9988187.md): "If I were the leader of Iran, I would go pray in the mosque."[^cite_note-306]

On 4 January, President Trump warned that the United States could intervene if Iranian authorities escalated their response to ongoing nationwide protests, saying Iran would be “hit very hard”[^cite_note-307] if security forces continued killing demonstrators. Speaking to reporters aboard [Air Force One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_One), Trump said the US was closely monitoring the situation and signaled that further violence against protesters could trigger a strong American response.[^cite_note-308]

On 5 January, the US State Department posted a warning to Iranian leadership on its Persian-language X account stating that "President Trump is a man of action. If you didn’t know before, now you do. Don’t mess with President Trump."[^cite_note-309]

On 6 January, the US State Department[^cite_note-\:0-310] addressed the assault on the hospital after the Malekshahi massacre, during which security forces of the Islamic Republic stormed the Imam Khomeini hospital in Ilam where injured protesters were hospitalized.[^cite_note-\:1-311] In a post on X it stated that "Storming the wards, beating medical staff and attacking the wounded with tear gas and ammunition is a clear crime against humanity",[^cite_note-\:0-310] further adding that "Hospitals are not battlefields. These measures by the Islamic Republic regime are a gross violation of international law and show a complete disregard for human life."[^cite_note-\:1-311]



A solidarity rally against the Islamic Republic in [San Jose, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose,_California), U.S.

On 7 January, in an interview on Fox News, US senator Lindsey Graham issued a warning to Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, saying, "To the Ayatollahs: you need to understand—if you keep killing your people who are demanding a better life—Donald J. Trump is going to kill you."[^cite_note-\:2-312] He added that "change is coming to Iran," calling it biggest shift "in the history of the Mideast to get rid of this [Nazi regime](21212.md)."[^cite_note-\:2-312] He also referred to the ayatollah as a “modern-day [Hitler](2731583.md)” addressed Iranian citizens saying "To the people of Iran, help is on the way."[^cite_note-\:2-312][^cite_note-313][^cite_note-314]

On the same day US Senator Ted Cruz stated that he supported the Iranian protests against the "tyrannical" and "theocratic" regime.[^cite_note-\:3-315] He also stated that "the Ayatollah is a zealot. He is a murderer, and I think the regime is fatally weakened as a consequence of losing the war".[^cite_note-\:3-315] He further offered his opinion that "the people of Iran want to stand with America," and that "they want to stand with freedom. They want to stand with the West. And tragically, they have suffered under this radical Islamist regime"[^cite_note-\:3-315] adding "very few things would produce greater peace in Iran, across the world than seeing the end to this to radical regime."[^cite_note-\:3-315]

[Laura Loomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Loomer), an ally of Trump in the [MAGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Make_America_Great_Again) movement, publicly requested that Trump invite Reza Pahlavi to the White House for a meeting, and that Elon Musk provide Starlink Internet to the Iranian people until Ali Khamenei is overthrown, stating that due to the budget shortage in Tehran, there was an unprecedented opportunity to change the regime.[^cite_note-316]

Additionally, after an anonymous Iranian named a street in Iran after President Trump, the US State Department praised the individual in its Persian language account on X, stating that they "appreciate the action of an anonymous individual in Tehran to name a street after President Trump,“ adding that "the United States respects the voice and aspirations of the Iranian people. We remain committed to supporting their efforts to achieve greater freedom, prosperity, and opportunity.”[^cite_note-auto-317]

U.S. Secretary of State [Marco Rubio](5502549.md) stated that "The United States supports the brave people of Iran".[^cite_note-318]

On 18 January, US President Donald Trump told [Politico](9172097.md) "It’s time to look for new leadership in Iran", referring to Khamenei, and said "What he is guilty of, as the leader of a country, is the complete destruction of the country and the use of violence at levels never seen before,"[^cite_note-\:5-319][^cite_note-\:6-320][^cite_note-\:7-321] adding that "The man is a sick man who should run his country properly and stop killing people," and "His country is the worst place to live anywhere in the world because of poor leadership."[^cite_note-\:5-319][^cite_note-\:6-320][^cite_note-\:7-321]

A January Quinnipiac poll found that 70% of Americans oppose U.S. military involvement in Iran, including 79% of Democrats, 80% of independents and 53% of Republicans.[^cite_note-322][^cite_note-323]

[Steve Witkoff](40901053.md), the U.S. Special Envoy to the Middle East, held a meeting with [Reza Pahlavi](613947.md), the exiled former Crown Prince of Iran, at the direction of President Trump.[^cite_note-324][^cite_note-325] In mid-January 2026, reports emerged that Iranian Foreign Minister [Abbas Araghchi](21951274.md) sent a personal message to Witkoff stating that Iran had canceled the planned [execution](5170842.md) of 800 anti-regime protesters. This communication is said to have been instrumental in persuading President Trump to refrain from ordering immediate military strikes against Iran.[^cite_note-326]

Protesters in the United States held placards warning of what they described as a "[genocide](12441.md) in the making", drawing comparisons to [the Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Holocaust) and denouncing what they called the regime's campaign of "terror".[^cite_note-327]

On 14 February, Trump said that a regime change in Iran would be the "best thing that could happen".[^cite_note-328][^cite_note-329][^cite_note-330]

During the [2026 State of the Union address](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_State_of_the_Union_Address), Trump said that he prefers a diplomatic solution, but he will not allow Iran to get nuclear weapons.[^cite_note-\:13-331][^cite_note-\:14-332] He furthermore acknowledged that 32,000 protesters had been killed, and warned that the Iranian government is developing missiles that could eventually reach the US.[^cite_note-\:13-331][^cite_note-\:14-332]

### Reactions by international public figures

In January 2026, according to *[Deadline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood),* a group of 800 professionals of the film industry, including [Juliette Binoche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliette_Binoche), [Marion Cotillard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Cotillard), [Camille Cottin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camille_Cottin), Greek director [Yorgos Lanthimos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorgos_Lanthimos) and others, released a statement condemning the "killing and torture" of Iranian protesters by the Islamic Republic.[^cite_note-\:9-333] The statement decried the fact that "In response to the widespread and peaceful protests of the Iranian people against repression, poverty, discrimination, and structural injustice, the Islamic Republic has chosen not to listen to the voices of its people, but to respond with live ammunition, mass killings, widespread arrests, torture, enforced disappearances, and a nationwide shutdown of the internet,"[^cite_note-\:9-333] adding that "The deliberate shutdown of the internet and the suppression of media constitute a clear attempt to conceal these crimes and prevent the documentation of truth. These actions represent a blatant and systematic violation of all fundamental human rights, including the right to life, liberty, human dignity, and security, and constitute a clear case of crimes against humanity."[^cite_note-\:9-333]

Additionally, the creators called upon "independent international institutions, film festivals, cultural and artistic institutions and the global community of filmmakers and artists" to publicly join the condemnation, "support the struggle" of the Iranian people, and reassess their relationships with the regime.[^cite_note-334]

[J.K. Rowling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.K._Rowling) expressed condolences on the death of a [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) fan who was killed during the massacres.[^cite_note-335]
