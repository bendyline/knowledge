***Professor Molchanov*** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): «Профессор Молчанов») is a Russian (formerly [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)) [ice-strengthened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_class) oceanographic [research vessel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_vessel). The ship was built in Finland in 1983 and is now converted to passenger duties for the expedition cruise market.

## History

### Research vessel

*Professor Molchanov* is an [*Akademik Shuleykin*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akademik_Shuleykin-class_research_vessel) ice-strengthened vessel, built in [Finland](10577.md) for the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). Launched on 28 December 1982, she was named after [Pavel Molchanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Molchanov), [radiosonde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiosonde) inventor, on his 90th anniversary. Between 26 January 1983 and 7 September 1991, the ship was used for polar and oceanographic research. She made 34 research cruises as a Soviet research vessel,[^cite_note-2] including three global [hydrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrology) expeditions held by [Murmansk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murmansk) Hydrometeorological Administration:[^cite_note-3]

- 28 April 1984–10 June 1984, through [North Sea](21179.md), [Norwegian Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Sea), north-eastern [Atlantic Ocean](698.md), [Barents Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barents_Sea)

- 19 September 1989–30 November 1989, through [Greenland Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_Sea), [North Atlantic Ocean](698.md), Norwegian Sea, Barents Sea

- 12 December 1989–22 February 1990, through Norwegian Sea, [South Atlantic Ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Atlantic_Ocean), Barents Sea, North Sea, North Atlantic Ocean



Onboard *Professor Molchanov* in the [Kara Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kara_Sea) during the Arctic expedition "[Arctic Floating University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_Floating_University) 2013"

Since 2012, annual expeditions have been conducted on board the ship within the framework of the [Northern (Arctic) Federal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_\(Arctic\)_Federal_University) Floating University project.[^cite_note-4]

### Passenger ship

The ship was converted for passenger use, and is now operated by [Oceanwide Expeditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanwide_Expeditions) for expedition cruising in high latitudes.[^cite_note-oceanwide-1] She has one suite, 23 twin cabins and 2 triple cabins. The public areas consist of a lounge and bar, small library, infirmary, and sauna. The dining room also serves as a lecture room.[^cite_note-aa1-5] There are viewing areas on the open deck and passengers are usually welcome on the bridge. She has [Zodiac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zodiac_Marine_&_Pool) craft for landings and wildlife viewing.[^cite_note-oceanwide-1]

### Arrest

On 31 August 2026, the ship was seized in [Barentsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barentsburg), [Svalbard](27441.md), following an order from the Norwegian [Nord-Troms and Senja District Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nord-Troms_and_Senja_District_Court) at the request of the Ukrainian state-owned company [Naftogaz](3614879.md). The seizure was part of enforcement of a 2023 arbitral award ordering Russia to pay Naftogaz US\$4.22 billion, plus interest and legal costs, for Russia's 2014 seizure of its assets in [Crimea](163045.md). The vessel was prohibited from leaving Barentsburg.[^cite_note-6]
