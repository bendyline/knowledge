**Dioura** is a village and seat of the [commune of Karéri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kar%C3%A9ri) in the [Cercle of Ténenkou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%A9nenkou_Cercle) in the [Mopti Region](1064867.md) of southern-central [Mali](19127.md).[^cite_note-1]

## History

On 17 March 2019, gunmen [attacked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raid_on_Dioura_\(2019\)) a [Malian army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Mali) base in Dioura, killing at least sixteen soldiers.[^cite_note-Reuters-2]

On 30 September 2023, the [Permanent Strategic Framework](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategic_Framework_for_the_Defense_of_the_People_of_Azawad) claimed responsibility for an attack in Dioura, claiming that they captured Dioura's military base after two hours of fighting.[^cite_note-3] The rebels initially claimed to have killed 98 soldiers, however this figure was later revised to 81.[^cite_note-4]

Another attack by [JNIM](53401088.md) militants occurred on 23 May 2025, when they temporarily took control of the nearby military base. During the attack, 41 soldiers died.[^cite_note-5] Another attack by militants occurred on June 18th.[^cite_note-6]
