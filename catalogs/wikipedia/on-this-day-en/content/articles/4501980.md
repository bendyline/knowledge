The **National Assembly** (**NA**; [Lao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lao_language): ສະພາແຫ່ງຊາດ, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Lao): *Sapha Haengxat*) or formally the **National Assembly of the Lao People's Democratic Republic** ([Lao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lao_language): ສະພາແຫ່ງຊາດ ແຫ່ງ ສາທາລະນະລັດ ປະຊາທິປະໄຕ ປະຊາຊົນລາວ, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Lao): *saphahaengxat haeng sathalanalat paxathipatai paxaxon lao*) is the [supreme state organ of power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_state_organ_of_power) of [Laos](17752.md). It is the only branch of government in Laos, and per the principle of [unified power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_power), all state organs are subservient to it. The National Assembly meets in [Vientiane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vientiane).

Laos is a [one-party state](102459.md), with the [Lao People's Revolutionary Party](203651.md) as the sole legal party in the country.[^cite_note-Freedom-1]

Efforts have been made to increase the capacity of its members, aiming to strengthen their legislative, oversight, and representational capacities.[^cite_note-2]

## History

The National Assembly was established by the Lao Constitution of 1991.[^cite_note-3]

Elections were held in 2021. The Lao People's Revolutionary Party (LPRP) took 158 seats in the enlarged 164-member National Assembly while the 6 remaining seats went to independents.[^cite_note-4]

In 2017, construction started on a new National Assembly building, gifted by [Vietnam](202354.md).[^cite_note-5] The construction was completed in 2021.[^cite_note-6]

## Committees

[Parliamentary committees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_committee) of the National Assembly include:[^cite_note-7]

- Law Committee

- Economic, Technology and Environment Committee

- Planning, Finance and Audit Committee

- Cultural and Social Committee

- Ethnic Affairs Committee

- National Defence and Security Committee

- Foreign Affairs Committee
