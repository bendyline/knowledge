**Météo-France** is the official [French](5843419.md) [meteorological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteorology) administration, also offering services to [Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra) and [Monaco](19261.md). It has the powers of the state and can exercise them in relation to meteorology. Météo-France is in charge of observing, studying, and forecasting weather and monitoring [snowpack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowpack). The organization also issues [weather warnings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_warnings) for the [Metropole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_France) and the [overseas territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_France). Météo-France is also in charge of recording and predicting the climate.

## Organisation

The organisation was established by decree in June 1993 and is a department of the Ministry of Transportation. It is headquartered in [Paris](22989.md) but many domestic operations have been decentralised to [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse). Its budget of around €300 million is funded by state grants, aeronautic royalties and sale of commercial services.

Météo-France has a particularly strong international presence, and is the French representative at the [World Meteorological Organization](33584.md). The organisation is a leading member of [EUMETSAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Organisation_for_the_Exploitation_of_Meteorological_Satellites), responsible for the procurement of [Meteosat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteosat) weather satellites. It is also member of the [Institut au service du spatial, de ses applications et technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Space,_its_Applications_and_Technologies). It is also a critical national weather service member of the [ECMWF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Centre_for_Medium-Range_Weather_Forecasts) and hosts one of two major centres of the [IFS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_Forecast_System) [numerical weather prediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_weather_prediction) model widely used worldwide.

## Worldwide

In addition to its operations in metropolitan France, the agency provides forecasts and warnings for the French overseas *départements* and *collectivités*. It has four sub-divisions based in [Martinique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martinique) (with further divisions serving [Guadeloupe](12343.md) and [French Guiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Guiana)), [New Caledonia](21342.md), [French Polynesia](10737.md) and [Réunion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A9union). Some of these sub-divisions have particularly important international responsibilities:

- For example, the [French Guiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Guiana) office, based at [Cayenne–Félix Eboué Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayenne%E2%80%93F%C3%A9lix_Ebou%C3%A9_Airport), maintains facilities at the [ESA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESA)/[CNES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNES) [Centre Spatial Guyanais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_Spatial_Guyanais) [spaceport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaceport) in [Kourou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kourou) which assists with launch campaigns of the [Ariane rocket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariane_\(rocket_family\)).

- The [RSMC La Réunion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSMC_La_R%C3%A9union) sub-division is the official [World Meteorological Organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Meteorological_Organisation) designated [Regional Specialized Meteorological Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Specialized_Meteorological_Centre) (RSMC) for the provision of forecasts and warnings of [tropical cyclones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclones) in the south-west [Indian Ocean](14580.md).

- The [French Polynesia](10737.md) sub-division, whilst not the official RSMC for [tropical cyclones](8282374.md) in the South Pacific, has been mandated by the [WMO](33584.md) to issue forecasts and warnings of tropical cyclones for the neighbouring British [Pitcairn Islands](23450.md).

## Naming

Although the original name of the organisation was "**Météo-France**", with acute accents and normal French capitalisation, its branding is stylized METEO-FRANCE. This trademark decision reflects the need to have its name not altered in electronic documents due to transcoding errors, and to allow easier international references in many languages, including when referencing the organisation itself (in copyright notices, for example, or when citing sources).

The name, whether fully capitalized or with normal capitalisation with accents, is protected internationally both under trademark law and as an organisation name. Some non-binding information documents sometimes forget the hyphen (which normally is required).

## Services

Météo-France has a warning system to inform the population of dangerous weather conditions.

Météo-France heat alert, part of its [vigilance system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A9t%C3%A9o-France_meteorological_vigilance_system?action=edit\&redlink=1), was put in place following the [2003 European heat wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_European_heat_wave). The [2022 heat wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_European_heat_waves) was the earliest in the year since records began and marked the fourth time that a red heat alert had been issued since the protocol was activated after the [2003 heat wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_European_heat_wave).[^cite_note-3]
