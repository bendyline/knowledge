**Kurnool** is a city in the state of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md), India. It formerly served as the capital of [Andhra State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_State) (1953–1956).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-status-quo-5][^cite_note-capitals-6] The city is often referred to as "The Gateway of [Rayalaseema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayalaseema)". Kurnool is also famous for Diamond hunting as diamonds can be found in the barren land of Kurnool after the top layers of soil are washed away by the monsoon rains.[^cite_note-7] It also serves as the district headquarters of its [Kurnool district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurnool_district). As of 2011 [census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_of_India), it is the [fifth most populous city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Andhra_Pradesh_by_population) in the [state](2377.md) with a population of 425,214.[^cite_note-UA-8][^cite_note-9] It is located on the banks of the [Tungabhadra river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tungabhadra_River). Although the area has been inhabited for thousands of years, modern Kurnool was founded in the 16th century CE with the construction of the [Konda Reddy Fort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konda_Reddy_Fort).

## Etymology

The original name of Kurnool is found in historical records as *Kandanavōlu* or *Kandanōlu*.[^cite_note-10] It used to be a crossing on the [Tungabhadra River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tungabhadra_River), where the bullock cart caravans are believed to have greased their wheels ("*kandana*" being a reference to grease).[^cite_note-aptdc-11] The city is often referred to as "The Gateway of [Rayalaseema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayalaseema)".[^cite_note-12]

## History

### Palaeolithic era

The Ketavaram[^cite_note-13] [rock paintings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_painting) from the Palaeolithic era and are 18 kilometres from Kurnool. Also the Jurreru Valley, Katavani Kunta[^cite_note-14] and [Yaganti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaganti) in [Kurnool District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurnool_District) have some important [rock art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_art) and paintings in the vicinity that may be dated from 35,000 to 40,000 years ago.

Ashoka's rock edicts were inscribed in the 3rd century BCE. The inscription is in [Prakrit language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prakrit) and [Brahmi script](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahmi_script). Both the major and minor edits are found at Erragudi (also called the Yerragudi or Jonnagiri site). These are among the oldest discovered inscriptions in India. The site is close to the Andhra-Karnataka border, in Kurnool district. It is an ASI protected site under Indian law.



ASI board Kurnool

[Belum Caves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belum_Caves) are geologically and historically important caves in the district. There are indications that Jain and Buddhist monks were occupying these caves centuries ago. Many Buddhists relics were found inside the caves. These relics are now housed in the Museum at [Ananthapur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anantapur,_Andhra_Pradesh). The [Archaeological Survey of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaeological_Survey_of_India) (ASI) found remnants of vessels and other artifacts of the pre-Buddhist era and has dated the remnants of vessels found in the caves to 4500 BC.[^cite_note-show-15]

### Vijayanagara era

Kurnool town was Ruled by Chalukyas of Badami and Rashtrakutas before the 11th century. The earliest knowledge of this settlement dates from the 11th century. It has developed as a transit place on the southern banks of the river [Tungabhadra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tungabhadra).

Ruled by the [Chalukya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalukya_dynasty) in the 12th century and later taken over by the [Kakatiya dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakatiya_dynasty) in the 13th century, Kurnool developed into a transit point on the southern banks of the [Tungabhadra River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tungabhadra_River). It eventually fell under the rule of a [jaghirdar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagirdar_of_Banganapalle) before becoming a part of the [Vijayanagar dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayanagara_Empire). [King Achyuta Raya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achyuta_Deva_Raya), successor of Sri Krishnadeva Raya constructed the [Kurnool Fort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konda_Reddy_Fort) during the 16th century.

### Nawabs

The Abyssinian, Abdul Wahab Khan, defeated King Gopal Raja of the [Vijayanagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayanagar) Kingdom in the 17th century and went on to rule the land for 16 years until his death.

In 1686, Kurnool fell under the influence of the Mughals who were ruled by Emperor Aurangzeb. Later on in Kurnool's history, it was [ruled by the Nawabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurnool_State) until the British Government took over in 1839.[^cite_note-16]

### British Rule

Main article: [Kurnool State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurnool_State)

Ghulam Rasul Khan Bahadur (died on 12 July 1840) was the last [Nawab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nawab) of Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh.[^cite_note-17] He ruled from 1823 to 1839 until the kingdom was defeated by the [East India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_Company).[^cite_note-18] On 12 October 1839, a war broke out between the [British Indian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Indian_Army) and the kingdom and continued for six days until the king was detained on 18 October. He was subsequently sent to [Tiruchirappalli Central Prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiruchirappalli_Central_Prison) where he died on 12 July 1840.[^cite_note-19]

### After independence

Kurnool was the capital of erstwhile [Andhra State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_State) between 1 October 1953 and 31 October 1956.[^cite_note-Andhra_Pradesh_-_History-20][^cite_note-21] The state was later merged with the [Telugu-speaking districts of Hyderabad State](990267.md) to form [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md) with [Hyderabad](37534.md) as the state's capital on 1 November 1956.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] Currently Kurnool is in the divided Andhra Pradesh state.

## Geography

Kurnool is located at [15°50′00″N 78°03′00″E  /  15.8333°N 78.05°E  / 15.8333; 78.05](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Kurnool\&params=15.8333_N_78.05_E_).[^cite_note-24] It has an average elevation of 273 metres (898 feet). Kurnool lies on the banks of the [Tungabhadra River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tungabhadra_River). The Hundri and Neeva rivers also flow through the city. The [K.C.Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._C._Canal) (Kurnool–Cuddapah) was built by the Dutch for transportation, but later used for irrigation.

Landmarks in and around the city include *Konda Reddy Fort* Formerly called *Kondareddy Burj* is the historical monument and major tourist attraction of Kurnool located at the northeast part of the city.[^cite_note-25] [Orvakal rock Garderns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orvakal_Rock_Garden,_Kurnool) is the sculpture garden with an ancient cave lying on the southeast of the city.[^cite_note-26] [Jagannatha Gattu](https://www.nativeplanet.com/kurnool/attractions/jagannatha-gattu-temple/) Temple is a popular tourist site of Kurnool. It is famous for its Lord Shiva Temple and is located in B. Thandrapadu, in Kurnool. The history behind the Linga makes it a popular destination.

### Climate

Kurnool has a tropical savanna climate bordering on hot [semi arid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi_arid) climate ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BSh*) with temperatures ranging from 26 °C (78.8 °F) to 43 °C (109.4 °F) in the summer and 12 °C (53.6 °F) to 31 °C (87.8 °F) in the winter. The average annual rainfall is about 810 millimetres (32 in).Kurnool city gets decent to good share of rains from pre-monsoon to monsoon seasons. City location is influenced by the orography effect of Nallamala hills in the east. August and September are the rainiest months and February is the driest month. The highest amount of rainfall ever recorded in 24 hours is 386mm in the month of June 1989 and marking the rainiest month of all time with 527mm.

## Transport

### Roadways

[National Highway 44 (India)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_44_\(India\)) which runs from Srinagar to Kanyakumari and also highway of [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore) to [Hyderabad](37534.md). passes through Kurnool.

[National highway 340C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_340C_\(India\)) \[ Kurnool-Dornala ] which connects to [Srisailam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srisailam), [Vinukonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinukonda), [Guntur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guntur), [Vijayawada](750441.md).

[National Highway 40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_40_\(India\)), \[ Rayalaseema Expressway ] which runs from Kurnool to [Chittoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chittoor,_Andhra_Pradesh), are the major highways passing through the city.

[Surat–Chennai Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surat%E2%80%93Chennai_Expressway) which is under construction also passes through the city outskirts.

The city also has an Outer Ring Road which is under construction connecting from Gargeyapuram [National Highway 340C (India)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_340C_\(India\)) and connects [National Highway 40 (India)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_40_\(India\)), [National Highway 44 (India)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_44_\(India\)), under construction [Surat–Chennai Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surat%E2%80%93Chennai_Expressway) and Kurnool - Bellari State Highway.

The city has a total road length of 519.22 km[^cite_note-39] State owned bus transport system, [APSRTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APSRTC), operates buses from Kurnool bus station to other parts of the state.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

### Railways



Kurnool City Railway Station board

[Kurnool City railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurnool_City_railway_station) lies on the Bengaluru – Hyderabad railway line. It is classified as an *A–category station* in the [Hyderabad railway division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_railway_division) of [South Central Railway zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Central_Railway_zone). Another railway station name Kotla lies in the middle of the city.

### Airport

[Kurnool Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurnool_Airport), officially known as Uyyalawada Narasimha Reddy Airport, is located at [Orvakal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orvakal) and is situated on the [National Highway 40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_40_\(India\)), about 18 km (11 mi) from Kurnool and 54 km (34 mi) from [Nandyal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nandyal). Commercial operations of the [greenfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenfield_project) airport were started in March 2021.

## Economy

The [GDP](12594.md) of Kurnool City is \$3.266 Billion.[^cite_note-42]

Kurnool is endowed with good mineral resources. The important minerals are iron ore, dolomite, limestone, ochre, quartz and silica.[^cite_note-43]
