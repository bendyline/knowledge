The **foreign relations of the Dominican Republic** are the [Dominican Republic](8060.md)'s relations with other governments.

The Dominican Republic has a close relationship with the United States and with the other states of the Inter-American system. It has accredited diplomatic missions in most [Western Hemisphere](73162.md) countries and in European capitals.

The island nation of the [Dominican Republic](8060.md) maintains very limited relations with most of the countries of Africa, Asia, the Middle East, and Eastern Europe. It concentrated its diplomatic activities in four critical arenas: the circum-[Caribbean](18956035.md), countries in the Americas, the United States, and Western Europe (mainly [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany), Spain, and France).[^cite_note-Haggerty-1]

### Multilateral relations

The Dominican Republic is a founding member of the [United Nations](31769.md) and many of its specialized and related agencies, including the [World Bank](36752.md), [International Labour Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Labour_Organization), [International Atomic Energy Agency](14984.md), and [International Civil Aviation Organization](14985.md). It also is a member of the [OAS](22788.md), [World Trade Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Organization), [World Health Organization](33583.md), World Customs Organization the [Inter-American Development Bank](382592.md), [Central American Integration System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_American_Integration_System), and ACP Group.
