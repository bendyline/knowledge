&#x20;A woman irrigates crops in Kabkabiya camp in North Darfur.

**Kabkabiya** is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Sudan) of [North Darfur](767666.md) state, [Sudan](27421.md).[^cite_note-\:sd-1][^cite_note-\:def-2] The town it is named after derives its name from the [Fur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fur_language) phrase *kebi kebbia* ("they threw away their shields"), commemorating [Darfur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Darfur)'s victory over [Wadai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadai_Sultanate) there during the reign of [Ahmad Bukr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_Bukr?action=edit\&redlink=1) (c. 1700 – c. 1720).[^cite_note-3]<sup>: 40, 43 </sup>

Kabkabiya is home to a military base and has been a main location with the [Battle of Kabkabiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kabkabiya) during the [2023 Sudan war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Sudan_conflict).[^cite_note-4] In May 2004, it held 45,000–95,000 [internally displaced persons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internally_displaced_persons) during the [War in Darfur](5043637.md).[^cite_note-5]

Its town of the same name was one of few prior to the [Anglo-Egyptian invasion of Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Egyptian_invasion_of_Sudan) and an important access point to the [caravan trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caravan_trade) west of [Kobbei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobbei) under the Sultanate of Darfur.[^cite_note-6]
