A **tollbooth** (or **toll booth**) is an enclosure placed along a [toll road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toll_road) that is used for the purpose of collecting a [toll](63993672.md) from passing traffic. A structure consisting of several tollbooths placed next to each other is called a **toll plaza**, **tollgate**, or **toll station**. They have historically been staffed by transportation agents who manually collect the toll, but, in the modern day, many have been replaced with automatic [electronic toll collection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_toll_collection) systems, such as [E-ZPass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-ZPass) in the Eastern United States.

## Replacement

Multi-lane toll plaza with cash and E-ZPass lanes

Toll plaza on the [Pennsylvania Turnpike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_Turnpike) before conversion to all-electronic tolling

In the 21st century, electronic toll collection (ETC) has replaced many former locations of tollbooths around the world. ETC is an automated system that allows drivers to pay tolls without stopping. Benefits of automatic toll collection include reducing [air pollution](10934212.md) and fuel consumption, and saving motorists time and money compared to traditional tollbooths.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-washingtonpost20200812-2]



Some tollbooths have been widened with the addition of toll gates to accommodate increasing automobile traffic, such as this one on the [New Jersey Turnpike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey_Turnpike).

The [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md) led to further losses of tollbooths, causing the [U.S. state](18618239.md) of [Maryland](18858.md) to accelerate its shift towards all-electronic tolling by eliminating all cash payments from toll facilities.[^cite_note-washingtonpost20200812-2] Similarly, the [Pennsylvania Turnpike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_Turnpike) accelerated its plan to move to all-electronic tolling. While tollbooths are currently still in place throughout the turnpike system, signs inform drivers to keep moving through toll plaza, "we bill you".[^cite_note-3]
