**Shchuchinsk** ([Kazakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakh_language): Щучинск, *Şuçinsk*; [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Щучинск, IPA: [\[ɕut͡ɕɪnsk\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Russian)) is a city in northern-central [Kazakhstan](16642.md), located 75 kilometres south-east of [Kokshetau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kokshetau) on [Lake Shchuchye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Shchuchye). It is the seat of [Burabay District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burabay_District) (formerly Shchuchinsk District) in [Akmola Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akmola_Region) and is the centre of a large agricultural area.

## History

Shchuchinsk was founded as a [Cossack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cossack) settlement called firstly as vyselok Shchuchinskiy in 1850. Several years later it became [stanitsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanitsa) Shchuchinskaya.

## Geography

Shchuchinsk is located at the feet of the [Kokshetau Massif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kokshetau_Massif), in the [Kokshetau Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kokshetau_Hills), northern part of the [Kazakh Uplands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakh_Uplands). [Burabay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burabay) [spa town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spa_town) lies nearby to the northeast.[^cite_note-GЕ-1]

## Sport

A notable sports center of the city is the [National Ski Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burabay_Ski_Jumps), with two modern olympic [ski jumping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ski_jumping) hills, large (K125) and normal (K90), 16 FIS cross-country ski courses and a biathlon stadium, which was opened in July 2018.[^cite_note-3]

Shchuchinsk is known for its many notable athletes. That includes:

- [Nikolay Chebotko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolay_Chebotko) (1982–2021), Kazakh cross-country skier, bronze medalist World Ski Championship 2013.

- [Svetlana Kapanina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svetlana_Kapanina) (born 1968), Russian world women's aerobatic champion, most celebrated woman pilot in history.

- [Yevgeniy Koshevoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yevgeniy_Koshevoy) (born 1984), Kazakh cross-country skier.

- [Alina Raikova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alina_Raikova) (born 1991), Kazakh biathlete

- [Vladimir Smirnov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Smirnov_\(skier\)) (born 1964), Kazakh cross-country skier, Olympic champion 1994.

- [Yevgeniy Velichko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yevgeniy_Velichko) (born 1987), Kazakh cross-country skier.

## Notable people

Other notable people of Shchuchinsk include:

- [Alexander Chudakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Pavlovich_Chudakov) (1938–2005), Russian philologist and writer.

- [Sergei Odintsov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Odintsov) (born 1959), Russian astrophysicist.

- [Dmytro Vasyliev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmytro_Vasyliev?action=edit\&redlink=1) (1904–1980), Soviet Ukrainian architect.
