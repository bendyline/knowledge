**Garmaal** is a coastline located in [Indian Ocean](14580.md). It lies within the area controlled by the [Eyl District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyl_District), which is one of Somalia's federal member states. The town is situated to the east of the [Garowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garoowe) the administrative capital of [Puntland](233569.md), which serves as the capital of the [Nugal region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nugal,_Somalia).[^cite_note-2]

## Climate

The climate is warm and humid.[^cite_note-koppen-3] The average temperature is 28. The hottest month is April, with temperatures reaching 32, while the coolest month is July, with temperatures around 24.[^cite_note-nasa-4] The average annual rainfall is 291 millimeters. November is the wettest month, with 72 millimeters of rain, and February is the driest, with just 4 millimeters.[^cite_note-nasarain-5]
