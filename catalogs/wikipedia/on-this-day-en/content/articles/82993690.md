The **2026 [MTV Video Music Awards](295217.md)** were held at the [Peacock Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_Theater) in [Los Angeles](18110.md), [California](5407.md), on September 27, 2026.[^cite_note-2] It was broadcast live on [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS), simulcast on [MTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV), and streamed on [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount+)'s premium tier.[^cite_note-3] The show was hosted by rapper [Snoop Dogg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snoop_Dogg).[^cite_note-4]

The ceremony recognized outstanding [music videos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_video) and songs released between June 20, 2025, to June 18, 2026.[^cite_note-5] [Madonna](142056.md) was the most nominated and awarded artist, winning seven trophies out of thirteen nominations. [Taylor Swift](5422144.md)'s "[The Fate of Ophelia](81126617.md)" won [Video of the Year](476903.md). [Nirvana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nirvana_\(band\)) was honored with the [Video Vanguard Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jackson_Video_Vanguard_Award), and Swift with the inaugural MTV VMA Artist Director Honor. The music video for Swift's song "[Patient Zero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patient_Zero_\(song\))" premiered during the broadcast.[^cite_note-6] The CBS and MTV telecast, along with a repeat airing on MTV, drew 8.43 million viewers in the United States, excluding streaming, the most-watched since [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_MTV_Video_Music_Awards).[^cite_note-7]

## Winners and nominees

The nominees were announced on August 18, 2026.[^cite_note-16] The nominees in the social categories–Best Group, Best Long Form Video, Best Album and Song of Summer–were announced on September 18, with additional nominees for Best Album announced on the next day.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] [Madonna](142056.md) led with thirteen nominations, tying [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga)'s [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) record for the most nominations for an artist in a single year.[^cite_note-19] She is followed by [Taylor Swift](5422144.md) with eleven; [Sabrina Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabrina_Carpenter) with nine; [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande) with eight; [Bruno Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Mars) with seven; [Ella Langley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ella_Langley), [PinkPantheress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PinkPantheress), and [Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson) with five; and [Charli XCX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charli_XCX), [Gener8ion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gener8ion), [Katseye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katseye), [Lisa](59226766.md), [Olivia Rodrigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Rodrigo), and [Yung Lean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yung_Lean) with four.[^cite_note-20]

Winners are listed first and highlighted in **bold**.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]
