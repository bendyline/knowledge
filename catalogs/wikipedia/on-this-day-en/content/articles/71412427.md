# Semlex Group

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Industry | IT systems and services, identification systems, secure printing |
| Founded | 1992 |
| Headquarters | [Brussels](3708.md), [Belgium](3343.md) |
| Key people | Albert Karaziwan (CEO) |
| Website | [www.semlex.com](http://www.semlex.com/) |

**Semlex Group**, founded in 1992, produces documents and devices for secure identification and offers corresponding services. It is owned and operated by Belgian-Syrian businessman [Albert Karaziwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Karaziwan).[^cite_note-reuters-1]

It prints the passports of several African and Asian countries, including [Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_passport), [Comoros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comorian_passport), [Democratic Republic of Congo](15303414.md), [Guinea-Bissau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinea-Bissau_passport), [Madagasca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madagascan_passport)r and [Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldovan_passport).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Semlex and Karaziwan have been accused of corruption in issuing IDs, gaining contracts through political connections and charging excessive amounts for production of each document to pay off officials.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

In 2019, Semlex was awarded the contract to produce national identity cards in Côte d'Ivoire.[^cite_note-8] Semlex reports financing projects in the field of education, notably in the Comoros and Côte d'Ivoire.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]
