**Captains Regent** ([Italian](14708.md): *Capitani Reggenti*; [Romagnol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romagnol_language): *Capitèn Regìnt*) are the two [heads of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of the [Republic of San Marino](27248.md). They are elected every six months by the [Great and General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_and_General_Council), the country's [legislative body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature). Normally the Captains Regent are chosen from parties in coalition and serve a six-month term. The investiture of the Captains Regent takes place on 1 April and 1 October every year. This tradition dates back at least to 1243.

The practice of dual heads of government ([diarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diarchy)) is derived directly from the customs of the [Roman Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Republic), equivalent to the [consuls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_consul) of [ancient Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome), except the Captains Regent hold no executive power.

## History



Captain Regent wearing the official garb of the office and the collar of the [Order of San Marino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_San_Marino)

The establishment of the regency took place during the first half of the thirteenth century, when they had the role of managing justice, a task similar to the competence of [magistrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magistrate). During that period they were called consuls,[^cite_note-\:0-2] which derived from [ancient Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome). The first two known consuls were elected on 12 December 1243 by the [Grand and General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_and_General_Council) with a six-month term which is still used today.

At the end of the thirteenth century, the name of the institution started to change, as one took the title of "captain" and the other one of "defender", then in 1317 they became captain and rector.[^cite_note-\:0-2] One usually belonged to the upper class, to guarantee the possession of skills necessary to govern the Republic with competence, and one to the working class.

A 1973 law abolished all the restrictions which prevented women from taking public positions.[^cite_note-\:1-3] [Maria Lea Pedini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Lea_Pedini-Angelini) was elected the first female Captain Regent on 1 April 1981.[^cite_note-\:1-3] On 1 April 2017, for the first time two women, [Vanessa D'Ambrosio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanessa_D'Ambrosio) and [Mimma Zavoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimma_Zavoli), occupied the posts of Captains Regent, until their term ended on 1 October 2017.

Since 1 April 1984, after electing its second female Captain Regent, San Marino is the country that has had the most [female heads of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_elected_and_appointed_female_heads_of_state_and_government) in the world. As of April 2026, there have been 22 female Captains Regent since 1981 which have had a governing time of 13 years in total.[^cite_note-4]

Captains Regent are elected every six months by the [Grand and General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_and_General_Council),[^cite_note-\:2-5] they usually belong to opposite parties to grant an amount of balance and an equal supervision. The electoral procedure is prescribed by a 1945 law,[^cite_note-\:2-5] which is mainly based on the [Leges Statutae Republicae Sancti Marini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_San_Marino) of 1600.

The pair is elected if they obtain an absolute majority;[^cite_note-\:2-5] there is a [second ballot](25932.md) if no pair gets enough votes.

Following their election, the Minister for Internal Affairs announces the new Captains Regent from the balcony of the government building.[^cite_note-\:2-5]

### Eligibility

To be eligible for election the candidate should have the following requirements:

- Citizenship from birth (*[jus sanguinis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jus_sanguinis)*)

  [^cite_note-\:0-2]

- Be at least 25 years old

  [^cite_note-\:0-2]

- Be an elected deputy of the [Grand and General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_and_General_Council)

- Not have been elected Captain Regent in the last three years

  [^cite_note-\:0-2]

### Terms

Captains Regent are elected for six month terms that run from 1 April to 1 October and from 1 October to 1 April each year.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

### Investiture



Leonida Suzzi Valli at his investiture in the chamber of the [Grand and General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_and_General_Council), 1 April 1963

Investiture ceremonies take place on 1 April and 1 October every year, and are prescribed by [Leges Statutae Republicae Sancti Marini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_San_Marino). In the morning, in the [Piazza della Libertà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazza_della_Libert%C3%A0,_San_Marino), the [Sammarinese Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sammarinese_Armed_Forces) are assembled for the flag-raising ceremony taking place. As the flag is hoisted to the national anthem, the artillery unit fires a gun salute. The newly-elected Captains Regent proceed from the [Palazzo Pubblico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palazzo_Pubblico_\(San_Marino\)) to the [Basilica of San Marino](29447471.md) where a [High Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missa_cantata) is celebrated by the [Bishop of San Marino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_San_Marino-Montefeltro) in celebration of their assumption of office. The entourage returns to the palazzo, where the official address is made and the elected Captains Regent are sworn in according to the oath described in the Statutes of 1600. The outgoing Captains Regent place the collar of the [Order of San Marino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_San_Marino) on the new Captains Regent, finalizing the transfer of power. The swearing in ends as the band plays the national anthem and the outgoing Captains Regent afterward take their leave. The procession then heads to Palazzo Valloni for the assumption of the Captains' throne and the residency.

Finally, the Armed Forces assemble once more for a flag-lowering ceremony.[^cite_note-6]

## Power

The Regency is considered the supreme office of the [Republic](27248.md). The Captains Regent are the [heads of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heads_of_state), a function which they carry out as a single body, with a reciprocal right of [veto](74661.md). They have the right to be addressed with the [honorific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorific) title of [Excellency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excellency).

The Captains Regent are impartial and their power is mainly symbolic, as their main duty is to represent the country and to guarantee the constitutional order. They supervise the [Grand and General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_and_General_Council), the [Congress of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_State) and the [Council of XII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_San_Marino#The_Council_of_Twelve), but without any right to vote or to decide. They have power to dissolve the parliament,[^cite_note-\:0-2] which they do when the legislature is over or when it is unable to form a stable government. The Captains Regent also have the power to promulgate and order the publication of the laws approved by the Grand and General Council.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

## Regency Syndicate

The Captains Regent cannot be prosecuted in any way during their mandate,[^cite_note-\:0-2] at the end of which they are subject to the Regency Syndicate.[^cite_note-\:0-2] This judgement, established by the [Statutes of 1499](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statutes_of_1499?action=edit\&redlink=1), is now fulfilled by the [Guarantors' Panel on the Constitutionality of Rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guarantors'_Panel_on_the_Constitutionality_of_Rules), following the revision of the Declaration on the Citizens' Rights. The procedure provides that, within fifteen days after the conclusion of their mandate, every citizen registered in the electoral lists may submit claims against the Captains Regent "for what they have and have not done" during their mandate.[^cite_note-\:0-2]
