[Greenland](12118.md), an [autonomous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous) [territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countries_of_the_Kingdom_of_Denmark) within the [Kingdom of Denmark](11746442.md) (which also includes the territories of metropolitan [Denmark](76972.md) and [Faroe Islands](17238607.md)), is one of the EU members’ [overseas countries and territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_member_state_territories_and_the_European_Union) (OCT) associated to the [European Union](9317.md). Greenland receives funding from the EU for sustainable development and has signed agreements increasing cooperation with the EU.

The associated relationship with the EU also means that all [citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_nationality_law) of the [Kingdom of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Denmark) residing in [Greenland](12118.md) ([Greenlandic](4017941.md) nationals) are [EU citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU_citizens).[^cite_note-1] Danish citizenship allows Greenlanders to move and reside freely within the EU.

Greenland joined the then [European Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Community) in 1973 with Denmark, but after gaining autonomy in 1979 with the introduction of [home rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_rule) within the [Kingdom of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Denmark), Greenland [voted to leave in 1982](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_Greenlandic_European_Communities_membership_referendum) and [left in 1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Withdrawal_of_Greenland_from_the_European_Communities), to become an OCT. The main reasons for leaving were disagreements about the [Common Fisheries Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Fisheries_Policy) and to regain control of Greenlandic fish resources, to subsequently remain outside EU waters.

## Trade

In 2010, Greenland's exports to the EU amounted to € 331 million (a 92.7% share of Greenland's total exports) and Greenland's imports from the EU were valued at €614 million (68.9% of all Greenland's imports). Exports to the EU were mainly food and live animals (89%). Imports from the EU included mineral fuels, lubricants (and related goods), machinery and transport equipment (together 47%). The EU is Greenland's main trading partner. However, Greenland ranks as the EU's 103rd largest trading partner.[^cite_note-2]

In 2009 the [EU Ban on Seal Products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU_Ban_on_Seal_Products) put in place an [import](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Import) ban on [seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinniped) fur on grounds of [animal cruelty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_cruelty), but made exemptions for [Inuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inuit) communities in Greenland and Canada in order to protect [indigenous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples) [ways of life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lifestyle_\(sociology\)). The ban only allows small-scale hunts for population control and local circulation – produce is not allowed to enter the EU. The ban angered those communities in the [Arctic Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_Circle) who depend on sales from large scale [seal hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal_hunting).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Exports of seal pelts in Greenland have dropped 90% in a few years – from 60 million DKK to DKK 6 million a year since 2006.

## OCT status



[Greenland Representation to the European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_Representation_to_the_European_Union)

Greenland is one of the [Overseas Countries and Territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_Countries_and_Territories) (OCT) of the EU due to its political status in Kingdom of Denmark. As a result, Greenland has some integration with the EU's [internal market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Single_Market) via association agreements. It is also within the EU's [common external tariff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_external_tariff) but they may charge customs in a non-discriminatory manner. Greenlandic nationals have [EU citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU_citizenship).[^cite_note-6] OCT nationals can be granted the right to vote for and participate in elections of the European Parliament, subject to the conditions defined by the related member states in compliance with Community law.[^cite_note-7]

Up to 2006, all EU funds to Greenland (then € 42.8 million per year) went via the EU-Greenland fishing agreement. Between 2007 and 2013, the EU provided € 25 million per year outside of fishing.[^cite_note-8] Greenland has been given aid since it pulled out of the EU (see below) in 1985 to roughly the same amount it was previously receiving in EU structural funds (which it lost the right to receive due to its secession). This amounted to about 7% of Greenland's budget. The amount paid via the fishing agreement was in return for EU vessels fishing in Greenland's waters and to help restructure Greenland's fishing fleet. However, this deal was struck down by the [European Court of Auditors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Court_of_Auditors), who held that the amount the EU was paying was too high for the quantity of fish caught.[^cite_note-9]

## OCTA



[Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md) at a [Nuuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuuk,_Greenland) press conference in 2025

Greenland has joined the [Association of the Overseas Countries and Territories of the European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_the_Overseas_Countries_and_Territories_of_the_European_Union) (OCTA). It was founded on 17 November 2000, during the conference of prime ministers of overseas countries and territories in [Brussels](3708.md), Belgium. OCTA includes almost all [special member state territories of European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_member_state_territories_and_the_European_Union) whose purpose is to improve economic development in overseas countries and territories and cooperation with the [European Union](9317.md). It currently (2024[^cite_note-10]) has 13 members. On 25 June 2008, a Cooperation Treaty between the EU and OCTA was signed in Brussels.[^cite_note-11]

In 2012, Greenland and Prime Minister of [Greenland](12118.md), [Kuupik Kleist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuupik_Kleist), held the chairmanship of the organisation.

Greenland is eligible for EU funding. Between 2007 and 2013, the EU allocated approximately € 190 million, and between 2014 and 2020, € 217.8 million are planned for sustainable development, with focus on education.[^cite_note-12] In 2015, a joint declaration about closer relations between EU and Greenland was signed by Denmark, Greenland and the EU.[^cite_note-13]

In March 2015, the President of the EU Commission, the Prime Minister of Denmark and Greenland's Premier signed 'an umbrella' framework document outlining EU-Greenland relations, a "Joint Declaration on relations between the European Union, on the one hand, and the Government of Greenland and the Government of Denmark, on the other". By this document, the EU confirms its long lasting links with Greenland and reiterates the geostrategic importance of Greenland for the EU.[^cite_note-14]

The [Brexit](41688778.md) debate has reignited talk about the EU in Greenland, and there have been calls for the island to rejoin the Union.[^cite_note-15] In 2024 an opinion poll found that 60 percent of Greenland's population would vote in favour of re-joining the EU, an increase from 2021 when only 40 percent were in favour.[^cite_note-16]

In late January 2026, during the [Greenland crisis](82044223.md), a survey commissioned by *[The Copenhagen Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Copenhagen_Post)* found that only 5% of Greenlanders favoured closer cooperation with the United States, while 65% favoured closer cooperation with the European Union, and 29% stated no position.[^cite_note-17]

Early in September 2026, [European Commission President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Commission_President) [Ursula](2991932.md) [von der Leyen](2991932.md) announced a substantial investment in sectors including critical minerals, satellite ​communications and renewable energy.[^cite_note-18]

## Outside the EU

Greenland originally joined the then-[European Communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Communities) with Denmark in 1973. At that time Greenland had had no autonomy from Denmark, which it gained in 1979. Greenland achieved some special treatment such as restrictions on business for non-residents and fisheries.[^cite_note-19] Greenland got the right to one [European Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_\(European_Parliament_constituency\)) member in the [parliament election 1979](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_European_Parliament_election_in_Greenland).

Greenland left in 1985, following a [referendum in 1982](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_Greenlandic_European_Communities_membership_referendum) with 53% voting for withdrawal after a dispute over fishing rights.[^cite_note-20] The [Greenland Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_Treaty) formalised its exit.

There has been some speculation as to whether Greenland might consider rejoining the European Union, although this seems highly unlikely to occur any time soon. On 4 January 2007, the Danish daily *[Jyllands-Posten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jyllands-Posten)* quoted the former Danish minister for Greenland, [Tom Høyem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_H%C3%B8yem), as having said: "I would not be surprised if Greenland again becomes a member of the EU ... The EU needs the Arctic window and Greenland cannot alone manage the gigantic Arctic possibilities".[^cite_note-21] The debate was reignited[^cite_note-22] during the [2008–2011 Icelandic financial crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008%E2%80%932011_Icelandic_financial_crisis). The EU [Common Fisheries Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Fisheries_Policy) (CFP) is an important reason why Greenland, Norway, and Iceland are staying outside the EU. There was hope that the Icelandic negotiations on EU membership 2011–2013 could create an exception to the CFP but the negotiations never got that far. "Gigantic Arctic possibilities" refers to natural resources such as mining.

## EU–Greenland–US relations

See also: [Proposed United States acquisition of Greenland](61530125.md) and [Greenland crisis](82044223.md)

In mid-January 2026, ahead of a meeting at the [White House](33057.md)[^cite_note-23] involving officials from Greenland, Denmark and the United States, Denmark announced a strengthening of its military presence in Greenland while working with [NATO](21133.md) allies to increase activity in the Arctic.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] Meanwhile, France said it plans to open a [consulate](6770059.md) in Greenland on 6 February, 2026.[^cite_note-27] A bipartisan [US-Congressional](31756.md) delegation visited [Copenhagen](5166.md), to display unity and ease tensions,[^cite_note-\:0-28][^cite_note-29] while [special envoy Jeff Landry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Landry#U.S._special_envoy_to_Greenland_\(2025%E2%80%93present\))[^cite_note-30] posted on social media that he would help "make Greenland part of the U.S."[^cite_note-31] In May 2026 Landry and his wife visited in anticipation of the Future Greenland business conference scheduled by [Business Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_Greenland?action=edit\&redlink=1) for May 19-20.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33]

Greenlanders are reported to refute [US-president Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_President_Donald_Trump)’s narrative of an imminent [Chinese](5405.md) and [Russian](25391.md) threat.[^cite_note-\:0-28][^cite_note-34] Europeans are working on a response to Mr. Trump’s pressure campaign.[^cite_note-35] Already, Denmark had announced a \$ 2 billion military expansion in the Arctic.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37] At the same time, some [Silicon Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Valley) tech investors are promoting Greenland as a site for a [libertarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libertarianism) utopia ([freedom city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_Cities)).[^cite_note-38] Greenland's Prime Minister [Jens-Frederik Nielsen](79438070.md) is not in favor of a deal with the United States granting sovereignty for [American military bases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_military_bases) on Greenland.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-\:1-40]

According to Julie Rademacher, president of *Uagut*, the national organisation for Greenlanders in Denmark, Greenlandic high trust society "is built on values that clash sharply with modern American political culture: collectivism over individualism, trust over spectacle, continuity over disruption, modesty over hyperbole, and nature as an [intrinsic value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intrinsic_value_\(ethics\)) — not a resource to be 'unlocked'."[^cite_note-\:1-40]

In late January 2026 double track procedures were agreed upon according to [Mark Rutte](4321056.md#Secretary_General_of_NATO): NATO will take on more responsibilities in defending the Arctic; there will be direct talks between Greenland, Denmark, and the US without participation of NATO.[^cite_note-41]

In February 2026 Canada and France opened Greenland consulates.[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-\:2-43] As a show of solidarity Canada’s first Indigenous [governor general](38152.md), [Mary Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Simon), was greeted in Nuuk by several dozen of the 90 Canadian Inuit who had flown in.[^cite_note-44] The EU has been represented on Greenland since 2024.[^cite_note-\:2-43]

In August 2026 the US company [Greenland Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_Energy) landed drilling equipment on [Jameson Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jameson_Land).[^cite_note-\:3-45] According to the [Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian) Greenland’s government issued a “strong warning” that no approval had been granted to land the kit.[^cite_note-\:3-45][^cite_note-46]

In September 2026 an agreement on Greenland's and US security was announced,[^cite_note-\:4-47] that would not compromise Greenland’s sovereignty.[^cite_note-48] Greenland is viewed essential for a planned [Golden Dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Dome_\(missile_defense_system\)) defense system.[^cite_note-\:4-47][^cite_note-49]
