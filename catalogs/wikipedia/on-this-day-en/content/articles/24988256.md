**Jam** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): جم)[^cite_note-7] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Jam_County\)) of [Jam County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jam_County), [Bushehr province, Iran](448037.md), serving as capital of both the county and the district.[^cite_note-Jam_County_Establishment-8] It is also the administrative center for [Jam Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jam_Rural_District).[^cite_note-Kangan_County_Rural_Districts-9] The village of Velayat was converted to a city in 1996[^cite_note-Jam_City-3] and renamed Jam in 1999.[^cite_note-Bushehr_Province_Name_Changes-6]

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 10,809 in 2,734 households.[^cite_note-2006_Bushehr_Province-10] The 2011 census counted 16,313 people in 4,632 households.[^cite_note-2011_Bushehr_Province-11] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 31,436 people in 9,356 households.[^cite_note-2016_Bushehr_Province-4]

## Neighborhoods

The city of Jam is formed from the former villages of Khvajeh Ahmadi (خواجه احمدي), Malecheh (مالچه), Qaleh-ye Kohneh (قلعه کهنه), and Velayat (ولایت).[^cite_note-Jam_City_Neighborhoods-12]

## Sports

Jam's main sport team is [Pars Jonubi Jam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F.C._Pars_Jonoubi_Jam) football club who used to play in the [Azadegan League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azadegan_League) and now in [Persian Gulf Pro League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_Gulf_Pro_League), Iranian Premier League.
