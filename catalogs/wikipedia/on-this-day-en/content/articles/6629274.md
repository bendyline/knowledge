The **Government of West Bengal** (*Paścimbaṅga Sarakāra, [Bengali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_language):* **পশ্চিমবঙ্গ সরকার**) is the [Principal administrative authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_division) and [State Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_governments_of_India) of the [Indian state](375986.md) of [West Bengal](34040.md), created by the [Indian Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India) as the state's legislative, executive and judicial authority.[^cite_note-West_Bengal_(state)-12] The [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_West_Bengal) acts as the [ceremonial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceremonial_Head_of_state) head of state and is the highest nominal authority of the state power. [Chief Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Chief_Ministers_of_West_Bengal) is the chief executive authority and head of the government, elected by people along with other ministers formally appointed by the [Governor of West Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_West_Bengal).

West Bengal is divided into [27 districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_West_Bengal) and [5 administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_West_Bengal), making a total of 32 [subnational entities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subnational_entities). [Kolkata](47905.md) is the capital of [West Bengal](34040.md). [BBD Bagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._B._D._Bagh) in central Kolkata is the administrative Centre of the state, situating the executive headquarter of the state, the [Writers' Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writers'_Building).[^cite_note-13] Although due to scheduled repair & restoration work from May 2026 onwards, temporary functioning of the administration is conducted at the secretariat in [Nabanna Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabanna_\(building\)), [Howrah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howrah)[^cite_note-14].

West Bengal Government's formal seat is at [Vidhan Sabha Bhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bengal_Legislative_Assembly), [Kolkata](47905.md), [West Bengal](34040.md) which consists three primary branches are Legislative Power in the West Bengal Legislative Assembly (one housed), Executive Power in the [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suvendu_Adhikari_ministry) & Judicial Power in the [Calcutta High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta_High_Court) with the official residence of [Governor of West Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_West_Bengal), the [Lok Bhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lok_Bhavan,_Kolkata) as head of state.&#x20;

The present Legislative Assembly of West Bengal is [unicameral](204304.md), consisting of 294 [Member of the Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Legislative_Assembly_\(India\)) (MLA).[^cite_note-The_Parliament_of_West_Bengal,_India-15] West Bengal Government formed by the Chief Minister & other ministers ([Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suvendu_Adhikari_ministry)) belong to [Member of the Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Legislative_Assembly_\(India\)) with the [Chief Minister of West Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_West_Bengal) at its head. The [current Chief Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_chief_ministers_of_West_Bengal) is [Suvendu Adhikari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suvendu_Adhikari), who assumed the office on 9 May 2026. He is one of the leader of the [Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharatiya_Janata_Party_\(BJP\)) in West Bengal, which party won 208 seats (out of 294) in the [2026 West Bengal Legislative Assembly election](77457015.md).

## Nomenclature

The terms "GoWB", "WB Govt." etc. abbreviation, "The State Government of West Bengal", "Paschim Banga Sarkar", "*Paścimbaṅga Sarakāra*", "West Bengal Government (পশ্চিমবঙ্গ সরকার)" are often used officially and unofficially to refer to the same Government of West Bengal. The term *Kolkata* is commonly used as a [metonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metonym) for the West Bengal Government, as the seat of the state government is in [Kolkata](47905.md).

## History

On 18 January 1862, under the [Indian Councils Act 1861](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Councils_Act_1861), a 12-member Legislative Council for Bengal was founded by the Governor-General of India with the Lt Governor of Bengal and some nominated members. The strength of this council was gradually increased by subsequent acts. Under the [Indian Councils Act 1892](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Councils_Act_1892), the maximum strength of the council was increased to 20 members out of which seven members were to be elected. After the [Indian Councils Act 1909](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Councils_Act_1909), the number was raised to 50 members.[^cite_note-The_Parliament_of_West_Bengal,_India-15]

## Structural Framework

The Government of West Bengal (GoWB) mainly composed by three main organs are the [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature), the [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)), and the [judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary) where the powers are vested by the [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India) in the [Council of Ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suvendu_Adhikari_ministry), [Vidhan Sabha](410238.md), and the [Calcutta High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta_High_Court), respectively. The [Governor of West Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_governors_of_West_Bengal) is the [politically neutral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apoliticism) [*head of state*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and acts as the [Chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellor_\(education\)) of the universities of West Bengal appointed by the [President of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_India) while politically elected [Chief Minister of West Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_West_Bengal) acts as the head of the executive and is responsible for running the Government of West Bengal. [Vidhan Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bengal_Legislative_Assembly) is the [unicameral](204304.md) legislature with single house which is Lower House (Bidhan Sabha).&#x20;

## Organization

### Departments Overview

The [West Bengal](34040.md) Government is divided into **54** different Departments Operating and functioning under various sectors of State.&#x20;

Each Department Consists of administrative personnel hierarchy which balance Political oversight with permanent bureaucratic management with [Political Leadership (The Apex)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suvendu_Adhikari_ministry) and [Apolitical Senior Bureaucratic Hierarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_secretary_\(India\)) (The Secretariat, Commissioner & Nodal Officers).&#x20;

The Apex Political Executive of any West Bengal Government Department belong with a [Cabinet Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_\(government\)) (Minister-In-Charge) followed by [Minister of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_State) (MoS) or can be [Minister of State Independent Charge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_State_\(Independent_Charge\)) MoS (I/C) without any Cabinet Minister where as Apolitical Senior Bureaucratic Hierarchy belong Additional Chief Secretary (ACS) / Principal Secretary / Secretary / Special Commissioner from Senior [Indian Administrative Service (IAS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Administrative_Service) Cadre along with Joint Secretary / Deputy Secretary / Assistant Secretary / Under Secretary / Nodal Officer from [Indian Administrative Service (IAS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Administrative_Service) or [West Bengal Civil Service (Executive) W.B.C.S. (Exe.)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bengal_Civil_Service) or West Bengal Secretariat Service (WBSS).&#x20;

### Chief Secretary & Secretaries - Government of West Bengal Departments

[Chief Secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_secretary_\(India\)) to the Government of West Bengal and Secretaries (i.e. Additional Chief Secretary or Principal Secretary / Secretary) under different Government Departments in West Bengal are appointed by the [Governor of West Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_West_Bengal) "by appointment order" through the recommendation from [Chief Minister's Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suvendu_Adhikari_ministry) and [Personnel & Administrative Reforms Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Personnel_&_Administrative_Reforms_\(West_Bengal\)) (P\&AR).&#x20;

[Chief Secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_secretary_\(India\)) to the Government of West Bengal is an officer ([Civil servant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Services_of_India)) of the [Indian Administrative Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Administrative_Service) (IAS) belong from Senior most cadre post & Principal advisor to the [Chief Minister of West Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_West_Bengal) on all matters in the state administration.&#x20;

Additional Chief Secretaries or Principal Secretaries / Secretaries are the administrative heads of various Government Departments which they are assigned to handle by the [Governor of West Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_West_Bengal) also belong from [Indian Administrative Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Administrative_Service) (IAS) [Civil servant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Services_of_India) cadre.&#x20;
