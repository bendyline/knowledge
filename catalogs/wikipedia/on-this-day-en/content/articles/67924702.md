**Sadio Camara** (22 March 1979 – 25 April 2026) was a Malian military officer who served as the [minister of defence of Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_defence) from 2021 until [his assassination in 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Sadio_Camara). He took an active part in the [2020 Malian coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Malian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) along with Colonel [Assimi Goïta](65000306.md) that ousted the government of [Ibrahim Boubacar Keïta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Boubacar_Ke%C3%AFta), and began serving as the country's minister of defence at that time.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-Colonel-5][^cite_note-6]

Camara served as minister of defence between October 2020 and May 2021 when he was replaced. This prompted a new coup and he was reinstated on 11 June 2021.

## Early life

Sadio Camara was born on 22 March 1979 in [Kati](1386780.md), Koulikoro Region, Mali.[^cite_note-treasury-2] He was a graduate of the [Joint Military School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Military_School_\(Mali\)),[^cite_note-7] and began his career in the Malian National Guard.[^cite_note-ISW-8]

## Career

At the time of the 2020 coup, Camara was serving as head of the Kati Military School,[^cite_note-9] and was receiving military training in Russia[^cite_note-10] since 2019.[^cite_note-ISW-8] Camara was on leave from his training in Russia[^cite_note-ISW-8] when he took part in the August 2020 coup and was named Minister of Defense in October 2020 under the interim government of Prime Minister [Moctar Ouane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moctar_Ouane).[^cite_note-newgovt-11] He served in that position until late May 2021 when he was excluded from the newly formed government by [Bah Ndaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bah_Ndaw), the interim leader after the 2020 coup.[^cite_note-newgovt-11]

Camara's ousting allegedly prompted Colonel [Assimi Goïta](65000306.md) to launch a "coup within a coup" in May 2021 when Goïta took over power and ordered the arrest of Ndaw and his cabinet during the [2021 Malian coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Malian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat). This prompted the [United Nations](31769.md) and many governments to condemn the coup and ask for the release of the detained leaders.[^cite_note-newgovt-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

On 11 June 2021, Col. Goïta—who later became a *[général d'armée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_of_the_army)*—appointed Camara again as Minister of Defense and Veterans Affairs,[^cite_note-newgovt-11][^cite_note-Colonel-5] and he was eventually promoted to the rank of *[général de corps d'armée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_corps_general)*.[^cite_note-AA-2026-14] Camara was a key architect of the "pro-sovereigntist" policy of the military junta, which sought to replace France with Russia as Mali's international partner, and helped arrange the deployment of Russia's [Wagner Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagner_Group) to Mali.[^cite_note-ISW-8] He regularly announced the transfers of military equipment to Mali from Russia, and visited his Russian counterpart, [Andrey Belousov](36245360.md), in June 2025.[^cite_note-15] The United States imposed sanctions on Camara in July 2023 for his links to the Wagner Group.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-treasury-2] His designation revealed that he also held French citizenship.[^cite_note-treasury-2] The sanctions were lifted in February 2026.[^cite_note-17]

## Assassination

Main article: [Killing of Sadio Camara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Sadio_Camara)

Camara was killed on 25 April 2026 in the wake of the nationwide [militant attacks](83033878.md) led by the [Azawad Liberation Front](78996050.md) and the [JNIM](53401088.md). A vehicle-borne explosive detonated outside his residence in Kati, a heavily fortified garrison town about 15 kilometres (9.3 mi) northwest of Bamako, the capital, where President Assimi Goïta and other junta leaders also reside.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] The government confirmed his death the next day and announced that Mali would observe two days of mourning in his honor.[^cite_note-20] He was given a state funeral on 30 April.[^cite_note-21] On 4 May, president Goïta appointed himself defence minister.[^cite_note-22]
