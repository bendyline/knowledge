The **2026 NRL Grand Final** is an upcoming National rugby league football match that will be contested between [Sydney Roosters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Roosters) and the [Newcastle Knights](797018.md) at [Accor Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadium_Australia) in [Sydney](27862.md), on Sunday, 4 October 2026 to determine the [2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NRL_season) [NRL premiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_National_Rugby_League_Premiers).

The grand final match will be preceded by the 2026 [NRL State Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NRL_State_Championship), and the [NRL Women's Premiership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NRL_Women's_Premiership) grand final.

The [2026 NRL season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NRL_season) is the 119th season of professional [rugby league](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_league) in Australia and the 29th season run by the [National Rugby League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Rugby_League). The season consists of 27 competition rounds, followed by a finals series contested by the top eight teams on the competition ladder.

It will be the first grand final meeting between the [Sydney Roosters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Roosters) and the [Newcastle Knights](797018.md), and is the first premiership decider since [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_NRL_Grand_Final) to have both teams not feature from preceding year's grand final match. The Roosters finished 4th on the premiership ladder with 16 wins from 24 matches, whilst the Knights achieved 14 wins and finished 7th. For the first time since [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_NRL_Grand_Final), both grand finalists had played every round of the finals series. The Roosters last played in a grand final in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_NRL_Grand_Final) when they defeated the [Canberra Raiders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canberra_Raiders). The Knights ended the longest active grand final drought in the NRL, having not reached a grand final in 25 years when they defeated the [Parramatta Eels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parramatta_Eels) in [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_NRL_Grand_Final). They are the first side since the [North Queensland Cowboys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Queensland_Cowboys) in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_NRL_Grand_Final) to reach the grand final from outside the top four. The Roosters and Knights last faced each other in the finals in [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_NRL_season#Finals_series), with the Roosters winning 40–14 in the preliminary finals at the former [Sydney Football Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Football_Stadium_\(1988\)), before going on to become the season's premiers.

### Newcastle Knights farewell parade incident

During a farewell parade for the Knights on 3 October, a car lost control on the [Newcastle Link Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_Link_Road) and crashed into a crowd of supporters waiting for the team bus to pass.[^cite_note-1] Ten people including children were injured, two of whom critically, and 18-year-old [provisionally-licenced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Driver's_licences_in_Australia#New_South_Wales) driver Indika Bambrick Samarasinghe was arrested by [NSW Police](563673.md); he was later charged with multiple counts of dangerous and negligent driving occasioning [grievous bodily harm](1239484.md).[^cite_note-2]

## Pre-match

### Broadcasting

#### Television

The match will be broadcast live on the [Nine Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Network) in Australia and [9Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9Now) and on [Sky Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Sport_\(New_Zealand\)) in New Zealand.[^cite_note-2026NTK-3] [Mathew Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathew_Thompson), [Andrew Johns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Johns), and [Billy Slater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Slater) are the commentators for the match, joined by [Danika Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danika_Mason) and [Brad Fittler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Fittler) on the sidelines. The coverage is hosted by [James Bracey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bracey_\(sports_presenter\)). Thompson has led Nine's grand final commentary since taking over from [Ray Warren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Warren) in 2022.[^cite_note-4]

[Fox League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_League) produced their own commentary for the replay of the match on subscription television, with veteran commentator [Andrew Voss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Voss) calling his first grand final as play-by-play commentator after working in the game for more than 40 years. The Fox League coverage was in the past usually led by [Warren Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Smith_\(broadcaster\)) who missed the entire 2026 NRL season due to multiple voice box surgeries.[^cite_note-5]

#### Radio

Radio broadcasters include [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Broadcasting_Corporation), [Triple M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_M), [2GB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2GB), [4BC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4BC) and NRL Nation.[^cite_note-2026NTK-3]

### Officiating

[Adam Gee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Gee_\(referee\)) was appointed as the referee for the match. He previously was the main referee for the [2023 NRL Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_NRL_Grand_Final). Tyson Brough and Phil Henderson was appointed as touch judges, while Chris Butler was chosen as the bunker review official.[^cite_note-6] Gee was favoured for the role following the demotion of [Ashley Klein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Klein) following his controversial performance during Newcastle's win over the [New Zealand Warriors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Warriors) in their semi final.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

### Entertainment

Pre-game entertainment was set to be performed by Australian singer, [Tones and I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tones_and_I), however negotiations with the singer reportedly fell through after she declined to sing her breakout single, [Dance Monkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dance_Monkey),[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] however the singer responded by saying it was due to "scheduling conflicts".[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] On 28 September 2026, [Peter V'landys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_V'landys) announced a new act had been acquired although it would not be revealed until Game Day.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] It was confirmed on the 30th of September that [Jai Arrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jai_Arrow) would do a lap of honour before the Grand Final starts. There will be Australian singers performing "You'll Never Walk Alone".[^cite_note-16] On 2 October 2026, the singers were announced to perform will include [Amy Shark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Shark), [Peking Duk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_Duk), [Grinspoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grinspoon), [Teen Jesus and the Jean Teasers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teen_Jesus_and_the_Jean_Teasers) and [The Screaming Jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Screaming_Jets).[^cite_note-17]

## Teams

The Sydney Roosters squad as named on the Tuesday before the match was unchanged from their preliminary final win against the Dolphins. Utility player [Connor Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Watson) suffered a concussion in that match, but was initially named in the squad pending clearance from an independent neurologist. Watson was later ruled out after he was not cleared to play.[^cite_note-18] The Roosters included five players (Tedesco, Tupou, Radley, Crichton and Butcher) who played in the team's last NRL Grand Final in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_NRL_Grand_Final). Five-eighth [Daly Cherry-Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daly_Cherry-Evans) had previously featured in grand final matches with the [Manly Warringah Sea Eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manly_Warringah_Sea_Eagles) (2011 and 2013), winning the [Clive Churchill Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clive_Churchill_Medal) in the [2013 NRL Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_NRL_Grand_Final) against the Roosters.[^cite_note-TeamPreview-19] At 37 years and 183 days, Cherry-Evans broke the record of [Cameron Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Smith_\(rugby_league,_born_1983\)) (37 years, 130 days in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_NRL_Grand_Final)) as the oldest player to line up in NRL grand final history.[^cite_note-20]

Following the suspension of Newcastle Knights hooker [Phoenix Crossland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Crossland), who received a four matches ban from the [NRL Judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NRL_Judiciary) following a dangerous tackle that broke the leg of Penrith's [Nathan Cleary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Cleary) in the Knights' preliminary final victory, Newcastle made one change to their lineup with [Harrison Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison_Graham_\(rugby_league\)) named to start the match. The Knights had only one player with previous grand final experience — [Dane Gagai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dane_Gagai) featuring at centre for the [South Sydney Rabbitohs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Sydney_Rabbitohs) in the [2021 NRL Grand Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_NRL_Grand_Final).[^cite_note-TeamPreview-19] Other Newcastle players to miss the match were the injured [Dylan Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_Brown), who suffered a serious knee injury in July,[^cite_note-21] and the suspended [Jacob Saifiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Saifiti) who was sent off in the Knights final match of the regular season and suspended for four matches.[^cite_note-22]
