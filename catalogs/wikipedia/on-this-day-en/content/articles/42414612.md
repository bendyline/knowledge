**Moussa Mara** (born 2 March 1975) is a Malian politician who served as [Prime Minister of Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Mali) from 2014 to 2015. He previously served as Minister of Town Planning and was a candidate in the [2013 Malian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Malian_presidential_election).

Mara was appointed Prime Minister on 5 April 2014, following the resignation of his predecessor [Oumar Tatam Ly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oumar_Tatam_Ly).[^cite_note-aljazeera-2] After less than a year in office, he resigned on 8 January 2015, and [Modibo Keita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modibo_Keita_\(born_1953\)) was appointed to replace him.[^cite_note-3]

On 16 July 2025, Mara was questioned by Mali's Judicial Investigation Brigade.[^cite_note-4] On 1 August 2025, he was arrested on charges of "damaging the state's credibility" after expressing support on social media for imprisoned critics of the military junta.[^cite_note-5]

On 27 October 2025, Mara was sentenced to two years in prison.[^cite_note-6]
