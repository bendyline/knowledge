**Poipet** ([Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language): ប៉ោយប៉ែត [\[paojpaet\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Khmer)) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_and_towns_in_Cambodia) in [Poipet Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poipet_Municipality), [Banteay Meanchey province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banteay_Meanchey_province), [Cambodia](334751.md) near the [border with Thailand](65369821.md), serving as a key crossing point between the two countries.

Poipet is adjacent to the town of [Aranyaprathet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aranyaprathet) on the Thai side of the border. The city came into existence only quite recently for the express purpose of border trade; Sisophon had always been the primary urban center in what had been an agricultural area. Poipet's population has increased from 43,366 in the 1998 census to 89,549 in the 2008 census, making it the fourth most populous settlement in Cambodia just ahead of [Sihanoukville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sihanoukville_\(city\)) and larger than its provincial capital [Sisophon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serei_Saophoan_\(city\)).[^cite_note-2] During the [Khmer Rouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_Rouge) regime, Poipet was the first place in Cambodia for International Relief Organisations to support the nearby Thai Border Camps. The [United Nations](31769.md) staff were able to freely cross the border. In the years since 2008, Poipet has continued to boom both as a large town but also as a key gateway from and to [Thailand](30128.md). In addition to its border-casino economy, Poipet has been cited in reporting on guarded compounds linked to online scam operations and trafficking; see [Scam centers in Cambodia](80461265.md).

## Transport

The Poipet border is a terminus of the [Cambodia railway system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_transport_in_Cambodia), though in 2006 restoring a link from Poipet to the present Cambodian Railways railhead at [Sisophon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serei_Saophoan_\(city\)) was proposed. In the fall of 2008, an agreement was prepared to have an Australian company carry out this work.[^cite_note-3] In 2009, Thailand passed legislation to extend the railway from Aranyaprathet to Poipet, and in 2018 the railway line was reactivated.

On 22 December 2022, Prime Minister [Hun Sen](393779.md) said that the government has been conducting studies on high-speed railways connecting domestically and to neighbouring countries. "We are conducting studies on high-speed railways connecting Phnom Penh to Preah Sihanouk province and to the border with Thailand."[^cite_note-4]

## Demography

Poipet is a city famous for its [gambling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambling) and [casino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casino) center in Cambodia. This attracts many foreigners. The largest number of foreigners are [Indonesians](4885606.md), most of whom work in casinos and as online gambling admins. [Indonesian restaurants and food stalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_cuisine) are easy to find.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] [Chinese](372983.md) (8,000 people) and [Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_people) citizens can also be found here.[^cite_note-7]

## Casinos in the city

There is a strip of [casinos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casino) and hotels between the Cambodian and Thai passport control counters, enabling Thais and other foreigners to gamble in Cambodia without needing to go through Cambodian immigration. In Cambodia, gambling is illegal for Cambodian citizens but not for holders of foreign passports. There is another border on the Cambodian side of this strip area that one needs to pass before being free to travel within the rest of the country.

On 28 December 2022, [a fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poipet_casino_hotel_fire) at the Grand Diamond City Hotel and Casino killed 27 people and injured dozens of others.[^cite_note-8]
