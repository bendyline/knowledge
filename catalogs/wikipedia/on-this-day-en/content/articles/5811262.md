Palestine is a [unitary state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitary_state), but is divided into sixteen **governorates** for administrative purposes. After the signing of the [Oslo Accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo_Accords), the [West Bank](33209.md) and [Gaza Strip](12047.md) were placed under the jurisdiction of the [Palestinian Authority](24093.md), which divided the territories into [governorates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorate).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-geohive-3] The governorates are subdivided in turn into [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_\(Palestinian_Authority\)).
