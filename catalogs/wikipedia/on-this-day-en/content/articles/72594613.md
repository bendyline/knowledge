The **Ateneo Blue Eagles men's basketball** team represent [Ateneo de Manila University](330339.md) in the men's collegiate basketball tournaments in the [University Athletic Association of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Athletic_Association_of_the_Philippines) (UAAP). They previously competed in the [National Collegiate Athletic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Collegiate_Athletic_Association_\(Philippines\)) (NCAA) from 1924 until 1978 when they transferred to the UAAP in the same year.

## 5-on-5 basketball

### Rivalry with De La Salle University

Main article: [Ateneo–La Salle rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ateneo%E2%80%93La_Salle_rivalry)

This section is an excerpt from [Ateneo–La Salle rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ateneo%E2%80%93La_Salle_rivalry).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ateneo%E2%80%93La_Salle_rivalry\&action=edit)]

The [school rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_rivalry) between [Ateneo de Manila University](330339.md) and [De La Salle University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_La_Salle_University) began when both educational institutions participated in the [National Collegiate Athletic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Collegiate_Athletic_Association_\(Philippines\)) (NCAA) and has carried over to the [University Athletic Association of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Athletic_Association_of_the_Philippines) (UAAP) where both universities compete in currently.[^cite_note-2]

Beginning with [UAAP Season 84](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UAAP_Season_84) in May 2022, Ateneo de Manila University decided to unify student-athletes and varsity teams under the "[Blue Eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ateneo_Blue_Eagles)" moniker moving forward, regardless of sport, gender, or age group.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Meanwhile, the Green Archer is the traditional mascot of De La Salle University and the varsity teams are collectively referred to as the "[Green Archers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_La_Salle_Green_Archers_and_Lady_Archers)", however, each team carries a specific moniker that references the sport that they play.[^cite_note-7]

Ateneo and La Salle were both co-founders of the NCAA in 1924,[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] until Ateneo left the league in 1978 to join the UAAP in the same year while La Salle announced its decision to leave the NCAA in a press conference in September 1980, effective after the then ongoing 1980–81 NCAA Season.[^cite_note-Ateneo–La_Salle_rivalry_ateneo_transfer-10][^cite_note-Ateneo–La_Salle_rivalry_lasalle_transfer-11] Both Catholic private schools in the [Metro Manila](72340.md) area have been competing against each other in the UAAP ever since La Salle was also admitted to join the league in 1986.[^cite_note-Ateneo–La_Salle_rivalry_timeline-12]

### Rivalry with University of the Philippines Diliman

Main article: [Battle of Katipunan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Katipunan)

This section is an excerpt from [Battle of Katipunan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Katipunan).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Battle_of_Katipunan\&action=edit)]

The [Battle of Katipunan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Katipunan), also known as the Katipunan derby,[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] refers to the [school rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_rivalry) between [Ateneo de Manila University](330339.md) and the [University of the Philippines Diliman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Philippines_Diliman) (UP) began during games and meets that preceded the establishing of the [National Collegiate Athletic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Collegiate_Athletic_Association_\(Philippines\)) (NCAA), of which both schools were founding members, and continues in the [University Athletic Association of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Athletic_Association_of_the_Philippines) (UAAP) where both universities compete. After competing in the NCAA for several years, UP left the NCAA to co-found the UAAP. Ateneo joined the UAAP in 1978.[^cite_note-15]

As the main campuses of both schools are located along [Katipunan Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katipunan_Avenue) in [Quezon City](284223.md), games between Ateneo and UP are referred to as the "Battle of Katipunan".[^cite_note-Battle_of_Katipunan_Ateneo-UP_1-16]

The Ateneo's teams are called the [Ateneo Blue Eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ateneo_Blue_Eagles) and the UP teams are called the [UP Fighting Maroons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UP_Fighting_Maroons).
