The **[Tony Award](54741.md) for Best Play** (formally, an **[Antoinette Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoinette_Perry) Award for Excellence in Theatre**) is an annual award given to the best new (non-[musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_play)) play on [Broadway](725252.md), as determined by Tony Award voters. There was no award in the Tonys' first year. The award goes to the authors and the producers of the play. Plays that have appeared in previous Broadway productions are instead eligible for [Best Revival of a Play](253057.md).

## Superlatives

British writer [Tom Stoppard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Stoppard) has won this award five times, more than any other playwright. Only seven other writers ([Arthur Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Miller), [Terrence McNally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrence_McNally), [Tony Kushner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Kushner), [Edward Albee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Albee), [Neil Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Simon), [Yasmina Reza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yasmina_Reza) and [Peter Shaffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Shaffer)) have won the award more than once, each winning twice.

With ten nominations, [Neil Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Simon) has been nominated for the award more than any other playwright. [August Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Wilson), with nine nominations, comes in second, followed by [Tom Stoppard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Stoppard) (eight nominations), [Edward Albee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Albee) (six nominations), [Arthur Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Miller) (five nominations), and [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh) (five nominations).

In 1994, [Tony Kushner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Kushner) became the first playwright to win consecutive Tony Awards for his two-part *[Angels in America: A Gay Fantasia on National Themes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angels_in_America:_A_Gay_Fantasia_on_National_Themes)*. [Terrence McNally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrence_McNally) repeated this feat the following two years with his plays *[Love! Valour! Compassion!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love!_Valour!_Compassion!)* and *[Master Class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_Class)*.
