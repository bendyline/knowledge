From 13 to 15 May 2026, the president of the United States [Donald Trump](4848272.md) made a state visit to [China](5405.md). This visit was Trump's second state visit to China, and the first to occur during his [second presidency](78298108.md). [Trump's first state visit to China occurred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_state_visit_by_Donald_Trump_to_China) in November 2017. This trip was also the first by a U.S. president since Trump's visit in 2017.

During the visit, Trump was received by [CCP general secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Secretary_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party) [Xi Jinping](2017814.md) at the [Great Hall of the People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Hall_of_the_People), where the two leaders held talks. Xi announced the two sides had agreed to establish a [Constructive Strategic and Stable Relationship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructive_Strategic_and_Stable_Relationship). Following the talks, Xi and Trump visited the [Temple of Heaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Heaven), the second US president to visit the Temple while in office after President [Gerald Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerald_Ford) in 1975. Later, Xi hosted Trump for a dinner banquet at the Golden Room of the Great Hall of the People. Trump invited Xi for a state visit to the United States on 24 September 2026. At the second day of the visit on 15 May, Trump visited the Chinese leadership compound [Zhongnanhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongnanhai).

The visit was the first of the four summits between Xi and Trump in 2026. It was reciprocated by a [state visit by Xi Jinping to the United States](84169290.md) in September 2026. Additionally, Trump is scheduled travel to China for [APEC 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APEC_China_2026) in November while Xi is scheduled to visit the United States for [2026 G20 Miami summit](81302119.md) in December.

## Background

[Chinese Communist Party general secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Secretary_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party) [Xi Jinping](2017814.md) and United States president [Donald Trump](4848272.md) held their first meeting during [Trump's second presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump's_second_presidency) at the [Busan Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Busan_Summit) on 30 October 2025. At the meeting, Trump announced plans to visit China in April of the following year and invited Xi to visit the United States at an appropriate time.[^cite_note-德國之聲1-1] The state visit was planned for the first week of April, but the meeting was postponed to May due to the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] On 25 March, [The White House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_White_House) announced Trump's visit would last from 12 to 15 May.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-t288-5][^cite_note-6]

In the lead-up to the summit, regional security concerns regarding the conflict with [Iran](14653.md) became a key diplomatic focus. On 16 April, Defense Secretary [Pete Hegseth](34076202.md) announced that Beijing had provided high-level assurances to the White House that it would not send weapons to Iran, explicitly ruling out the potential [transfer of surface-to-air missiles to the Iranian military](82696964.md). Hegseth attributed this breakthrough to the "strong and direct relationship" between President Trump and Xi Jinping, noting that the assurances were vital for the upcoming meeting in Beijing.[^cite_note-7]

U.S. secretary of state [Marco Rubio](5502549.md) met on 13 February with [Wang Yi](18211536.md), director of the [Office of the Central Foreign Affairs Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_the_Central_Foreign_Affairs_Commission) and foreign minister. The two sides discussed President Donald Trump's planned visit to China.[^cite_note-l128-8] On 30 April, Wang held a phone call with Rubio to conduct preliminary communication regarding subsequent head-of-state diplomacy.[^cite_note-c373-9] [Steve Daines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Daines) led the first bipartisan [U.S. Senate](24909346.md) delegation to visit China during the second Trump presidency, with the group traveling to Beijing and Shanghai to meet Chinese officials and visit technology companies. On 7 May, [Chinese premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_China) [Li Qiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Qiang) and [National People's Congress Standing Committee chairman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Standing_Committee_of_the_National_People's_Congress) [Zhao Leji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhao_Leji) separately met with the delegation in Beijing.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]



US Air Force C-17 aircraft on approach to [Beijing Capital International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Capital_International_Airport) on 3 May 2026.

On 3 May, the *[South China Morning Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_China_Morning_Post)* reported that two [US Air Force](32090.md) [C-17 planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-17) were spotted landing at [Beijing Capital International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Capital_International_Airport). These planes are routinely used to transport the president or vice-president's motorcade and support gear—including the "heavily armed presidential motorcade" known as "The Beast"—before overseas trips. The incident therefore ignited speculation that it was in preparation for Trump's planned visit to China.[^cite_note-12] On 6 May, two black SUVs with tinted windows and US government plates were spotted in a Beijing highway, thought to be in preparation of Trump's visit.[^cite_note-13]

On 11 May, [Chinese foreign ministry](7453497.md) confirmed that Trump will pay a state visit to China from 12 to 15 May. The US principal deputy press secretary Anna Kelly stated that Trump will visit the [Temple of Heaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Heaven) and a state banquet during this visit.[^cite_note-14] On 11 May, Trump announced he would have a discussion with Xi Jinping on the matter of arms sales to Taiwan, breaking with the [Six Assurances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six_Assurances).[^cite_note-15] Trump posted on [Truth Social](69077351.md) and stated that he was looking forward to trip to China, which he called "an amazing country".[^cite_note-16] On 13 May, the [Chinese Embassy to the US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_China,_Washington,_D.C.) posted on [X](9988187.md) stating "four red lines in China–US relations must not be challenged", namely "the [Taiwan Question](66555.md)", "Democracy and Human Rights", "Paths and Political Systems" and "China's Development Right".[^cite_note-\:5-17]

## Delegation

Rubio's inclusion in the US delegation drew some media attention as he is a [Chinese sanctioned individual](78786857.md) banned from entering the country. According to some initial reports, this arrangement might have been made possible after China changed his name's transliteration from 卢比奥 (*Lúbǐào*) to 鲁比奥 (*Lǔbǐào*).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-WPclumsy-20] However, *[The New York Times](30680.md)* reported that this was false, noting that [Xinhua News Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinhua_News_Agency) had used both transliterations for about a decade.[^cite_note-21] During [China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China's_Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs)' 14 May press conference, regarding Rubio's visit to China with Trump mentioned by [AFP](471981.md), [spokesperson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spokesperson_of_the_Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_of_China) [Guo Jiakun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guo_Jiakun) responded that "the sanctions are aimed at Mr. Rubio's actions and rhetoric on China when he served as a U.S. senator".[^cite_note-22] The transliteration used for the visit was also noted for containing the character 鲁 which carries the meaning "reckless", "clumsy", or "crass".[^cite_note-WPclumsy-20][^cite_note-23]

## Visit

### 12–13 May

Trump departs the White House and heads to China on 12 May.

In the morning, Trump departed the White House and boarded [Air Force One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_One).[^cite_note-34] In the evening, Air Force One made a refueling stop at [Anchorage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Stevens_Anchorage_International_Airport). [Nvidia](39120.md) CEO [Jensen Huang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang), who had not been invited initially, boarded to join the delegation.[^cite_note-\:3-31]

Trump arrives in Beijing on 13 May.

In the evening in Beijing, Trump arrived at [Beijing Capital International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Capital_International_Airport), where he was greeted by Chinese vice president [Han Zheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Zheng), Chinese Ambassador to the US [Xie Feng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xie_Feng_\(diplomat\)), Executive Vice Foreign Minister [Ma Zhaoxu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma_Zhaoxu), and US Ambassador to China [David Perdue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Perdue), as well as a military honor guard, a military band and around 300 Chinese students waving Chinese and American flags.[^cite_note-35] Trump and his entourage then boarded a motorcade, which later arrived at the [Four Seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Seasons_Hotels_and_Resorts) Beijing Hotel, while other members of the delegation would stay at the [Kempinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kempinski) Hotel Beijing Yansha Center.[^cite_note-36]

### 14 May

Trump participates in a greeting with Chinese leader [Xi Jinping](2017814.md).

At 10 am, Trump arrived at the [Great Hall of the People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Hall_of_the_People), where he was greeted by Chinese leader Xi Jinping and received an opening ceremony featuring the [national anthems of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Star-Spangled_Banner) and [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_of_the_Volunteers), after which they inspected troops of the [People's Liberation Army](66890.md) and then greeted children waving Chinese and American flags. Afterwards, Trump and Xi shook hands and entered the Great Hall of the People, where they held talks.[^cite_note-\:6-37][^cite_note-38] The US side of the talks included Rubio, Hegseth, Greer and Bessent, while the Chinese side included Chinese Communist Party General Office Director [Cai Qi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cai_Qi), Central Foreign Affairs Commission Office Director and Minister of Foreign Affairs Wang Yi, and Vice Premier [He Lifeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/He_Lifeng).[^cite_note-\:8-39]

Trump participates in a bilateral meeting with Chinese leader [Xi Jinping](2017814.md).

During the opening remarks of the talks, Xi said that "the whole world is watching our meeting. Currently, a [transformation not seen in a century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_changes_unseen_in_a_century) is accelerating across the globe, and the international situation is fluid and turbulent". He asked whether China and the US could escape the [Thucydides trap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thucydides_Trap) and create a "new paradigm of relations". He congratulated the US on its [250th anniversary](57473130.md) and added that China and the US "should be partners not rivals".[^cite_note-\:6-37] He said that China and the US had agreed to establish a [constructive China–US relationship of strategic stability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_partnerships_of_China), which he said included "benign stability with competition kept within bounds, a normalized stability in which differences are kept manageable, and an enduring stability in which peace can be expected".[^cite_note-\:8-39] Xi said "the Taiwan question is the most important issue in China–US relations", adding "If it is handled properly, the bilateral relationship will enjoy overall stability. Otherwise, the two countries will have clashes and even conflicts, putting the entire relationship in great jeopardy".[^cite_note-\:7-40] Xi said "'[Taiwan independence](31156.md)' and cross-Strait peace are as irreconcilable as fire and water" while adding that peace in the [Taiwan Strait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_Strait) is the "greatest common ground between China and the United States".[^cite_note-\:6-37] Trump told Xi, "You're a great leader, sometimes people don't like me saying it, but I say it anyway".[^cite_note-\:7-40] He said some people called this "the biggest summit ever" and that he looks forward "very much" to the discussions. He added, "It's an honor to be with you, it's an honor to be your friend", while saying he hoped the relationship would be "better than ever before".[^cite_note-\:6-37]

The two leaders then had talks behind closed doors following the opening remarks. The talks lasted two hours in total, double the duration originally scheduled. The two leaders exchanged views on the Iran war, the [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md), and the [Korean Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea).[^cite_note-\:8-39] The [White House](33057.md) stated that the two sides had "discussed ways to enhance economic cooperation", including by US companies' access to the Chinese economy and Chinese investment in US industries. The statement also said they discussed the importance of ending the flow of fentanyl precursors into the US. Regarding the Iran war, the White House said "both countries agreed that Iran can never have a nuclear weapon" and that "the two sides agreed that the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md) must remain open to support the free flow of energy", while adding Xi had expressed opposition to the militarization of the Strait of Hormuz and efforts to charge a toll for its use and expressed interest in China buying oil from the US.[^cite_note-\:6-37] The *[Financial Times](136566.md)* later reported that, Xi had condemned Japanese prime minister [Sanae Takaichi](8125532.md) for Japan's "remilitarisation", with Xi reportedly becoming "vocal and agitated when discussing Japan". Adding that this was the most intense part of Trump's visit, the *Financial Times* reported Trump had responded by saying Japan had to take a more assertive defense stance due to rising threats from North Korea.[^cite_note-41]

Trump visits the Temple of Heaven with Chinese leader [Xi Jinping](2017814.md).

Trump participates in a state banquet with Chinese leader [Xi Jinping](2017814.md).

After the talks, Xi met with US business executives who joined the trip, telling them China's door would only open wider to US businesses, and that US companies were deeply involved in the Chinese economy, benefiting both sides. Afterwards, Xi and Trump visited the [Temple of Heaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Heaven), which Trump called a "Great place, incredible", adding "China's beautiful". This made Trump the second US president to visit the Temple while in office, after President [Gerald Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerald_Ford) did so in 1975.[^cite_note-\:6-37] Later, Xi hosted Trump for a dinner banquet at the Golden Room of the Great Hall of the People. Xi gave a speech, where he said the "[great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_rejuvenation_of_the_Chinese_nation)" and "[make America great again](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Make_America_Great_Again)" can go hand in hand. He called the China–US relationship the most important in the world, adding that it concerns the well-being of the two countries of 1.7 billion population, and the interests of over 8 billion people in the world. After concluding his speech, he raised a toast to the development and future of the two countries.[^cite_note-\:6-37] Afterwards, Trump gave a speech, praising the "magnificent welcome like no other" and said, "It was a great honour to be with Xi". He talked about the history of the relationship between two nations, saying "[Benjamin Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Franklin) published the sayings of [Confucius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confucius) in his colonial newspaper" and said a sculpture of Confucius was "carved into the face of the [United States Supreme Court](31737.md)". He added "At the request of China's ambassador, it was president [Theodore Roosevelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_Roosevelt) who provided the funds to establish Xi Jinping's alma mater, [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University)".[^cite_note-42] He also invited Xi and his wife, [Peng Liyuan](10462776.md), to visit the White House on 24 September 2026.[^cite_note-\:6-37]

The table arrangement and menu of the [state banquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_banquet) that night were circulated online afterward. Compared to Trump's last visit to China, the main table sits fewer people. The foods featured both Chinese culinary traditions and American elements. It included cold [hors d'oeuvre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hors_d'oeuvre) dishes, main dishes such as golden lobster soup, crispy beef, [Peking duck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_duck), salmon with mustard sauce, bean soup with fresh vegetables, and after mains including pan-fried buns with ice flowers, conch pastry, tiramisu, fruit, ice cream, coffee, tea, as well as Great Wall Chief Winemaker's Selected Cabernet Sauvignon 2009 from Hebei, China, and Changyu Reserve Chardonnay 2016 from Beijing, China.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44] It was also rumored that the People's Liberation Army Military Band played 6 Chinese songs and 6 American songs during the state banquet. The Chinese songs included "[Butterfly Lovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butterfly_Lovers)" and "Ru Yuan" sung by [Faye Wong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faye_Wong), while the last song played was Trump's campaign song "[Y.M.C.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y.M.C.A._\(song\))"[^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46]

### 15 May



Trump and Chinese leader [Xi Jinping](2017814.md) at Zhongnanhai.

Trump departs Beijing on 15 May.

In the final day of the visit, Trump visited [Zhongnanhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongnanhai), where they strolled around Jinggu garden. When asked by Trump if he brought other dignitaries into the Zhongnanhai, Xi replied "Very few, we usually don't hold diplomatic events here. Even after we started having some, it's still extremely rare", while adding Russian president [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) had visited several times.[^cite_note-\:6-37] Xi and Trump later held talks in Chun'ouzhai pavilion. In a press briefing, Trump said they "settled a lot of different problems that other people wouldn't have been able to solve".[^cite_note-\:6-37] Xi said the Zhongnanhai "is the place where [leaders of the party and the central government of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_and_state_leaders) work and live, including myself" and added "After the [founding of the People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proclamation_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) in 1949, we have been here, including Chinese leaders: [Mao Zedong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mao_Zedong), [Zhou Enlai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhou_Enlai), [Deng Xiaoping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deng_Xiaoping), [Jiang Zemin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiang_Zemin), [Hu Jintao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hu_Jintao) and so on".[^cite_note-47] He said "This place used to be part of the imperial garden, there is a lot of history in this compound" and noted one of the trees Xi and Trump saw in the garden was 490 years old, adding "In other places within this compound, there are trees that have lived to be over 1,000 years old". Xi also said he would send seeds of the [Chinese roses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosa_chinensis) he and Trump saw in the garden to Trump as a gift. After his visit to the Zhongnanhai, Trump left for Beijing Capital International Airport, where he embarked on Air Force One and left China.[^cite_note-\:6-37]

White House staffers and reporters were required to surrender various items obtained in China prior to boarding Air Force One. This included burner phones used on the Chinese networks and credential badges and lapel pins issued by China. A *New York Post* journalist stated that "nothing from China was allowed on the plane."[^cite_note-techcrunch/2026/05/15/air-force-one--throw-away-48]

## Results

The [United States Department of Commerce](70243.md) gave 10 Chinese companies, including [Alibaba Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alibaba_Group), [Tencent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tencent), [ByteDance](54450737.md), and [JD.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JD.com), and distributors including [Lenovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenovo) and [Foxconn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxconn), the permission to purchase NVIDIA's [H200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopper_\(microarchitecture\)) chips.[^cite_note-49]

Trump announced that China had agreed to order 200 airplanes from [Boeing](18933266.md). ## Analysis and reactions

Some experts state that the ongoing [2026 Iran war](82533582.md) could give China greater leverage when dealing with Donald Trump, given that the U.S. had diverted resources away from [South Korea](27019.md) and [Japan](15573.md) to the Middle East, making them unavailable for a potential [conflict over Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Taiwan_Strait_Crisis).[^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53]

In an analysis published before the summit, the [Council on Foreign Relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_on_Foreign_Relations) described the meeting as an effort to stabilize China–US relations rather than resolve long-standing disputes between the two countries. The report cited continuing disagreements over China's economic policies, Taiwan, [freedom of navigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_salami_slicing_strategy) in the [South China Sea](74209.md), and Beijing's relations with countries viewed by the United States as strategic adversaries, including [Russia](25391.md), [Iran](14653.md), and [North Korea](21255.md). It also highlighted [China's trade with Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_and_the_Russo-Ukrainian_war) during the [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md), including exports of dual-use goods and imports of Russian energy products, as a continuing source of tension between Washington and Beijing.[^cite_note-54]

Chinese media praised the trip, with *[Global Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Times)* saying "Head-of-state diplomacy plays an irreplaceable role in providing strategic guidance for China–US relations" and that "The future of China–US relations is bright".[^cite_note-55] *[People's Daily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Daily)* published an editorial under the penname Guo Jiping titled "China–US Relations Cannot Go Back to the Past, But Can Have a Better Future", which said that the relationship between China and the US had become more equal. It stated that "China does not challenge or seek to replace the United States and welcomes its prosperity and development", but also that any attempts to contain China would end in failure. It also added that rather than seeing the relationship as a zero-sum game, the US should cooperate with China to solve global problems.[^cite_note-56]

In [Taiwan](25734.md), international experts analyzed the Trump–Xi meeting and concluded that Trump maintained an ambiguous stance on Taiwan policy. However, the content of Trump's remarks revealed that while the US government uses Taiwan policy as part of its negotiating strategy, its existing "[One China policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_China_policy)" remains unchanged.[^cite_note-57]
