The **National Coordination for Disaster Reduction** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Coordinadora Nacional para la Reducción de Desastres*, **CONRED**) is a Guatemalan government organization created to prevent disasters or reduce their impact on society, and coordinate [disaster relief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disaster_relief) efforts.[^cite_note-CONRED_ley-1] [^cite_note-IFRC-2] Day-to-day management is carried out by the Executive Secretary of CONRED, also known as SE-CONRED. The current Executive Secretary of CONRED is [Oscar Estuardo Cossío Cámara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Estuardo_Coss%C3%ADo_C%C3%A1mara?action=edit\&redlink=1), a former Vice Minister of Defense and former general of the Guatemalan Army.

CONRED is responsible for assessing potential hazards, impending or actual disasters based on information provided by the [National Institute for Seismology, Vulcanology, Meteorology and Hydrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_for_Seismology,_Vulcanology,_Meteorology_and_Hydrology) (INSIVUMEH), and for declaring states of alert. Once a disaster has been declared, it is responsible for the coordination of cross-sectoral disaster relief efforts.[^cite_note-CONRED_ley-1][^cite_note-IFRC-2]

CONRED is part of a Central American network of governmental disaster relief agencies known as the [Coordination Center for the Prevention of Natural Disasters in Central America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordination_Center_for_the_Prevention_of_Natural_Disasters_in_Central_America?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([Spanish](26825.md): *Centro de Coordinación para la Prevención de los Desastres Naturales en América Central* (CEPREDENAC)). CEPREDENAC was created in the context of the [Central American Integration System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_American_Integration_System). [^cite_note-SICA-3]

## History

CONRED was officially established in 1996 by the Guatemalan congress in Decree No. 109-96, Law on the National Coordinator for the Reduction of Natural or Manmade Disasters ([Spanish](26825.md): *Decreto No. 109-96, Ley de la Coordinadora Nacional para la Reduccion de Desastres de Origen Natural o Provocado*).[^cite_note-4] The organization was intended as a civilian successor to CONE, the military-led National Committee on Emergency.[^cite_note-5]
