**Dame Finola Mary Lucy O'Farrell** (born 15 December 1960), officially titled **The Honourable Mrs Justice O'Farrell**, is a British jurist who has served as a [High Court Judge](5093797.md) since 2016.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## Early life

Finola O'Farrell was educated at [St Philomena's Catholic High School for Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Philomena's_Catholic_High_School_for_Girls) and later earned a bachelor's degree from [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University) in 1982.[^cite_note-2]

## Career

O'Farrell was [called to the Bar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_to_the_bar) in 1983 at the [Inner Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inner_Temple) and practised construction and energy law. In 2002, she became a [QC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_Counsel) and was appointed as a Recorder in 2007.[^cite_note-judiciary1-3][^cite_note-4] On 3 October 2016, she was appointed as a High Court judge[^cite_note-judiciary1-3][^cite_note-5] and was appointed a Dame Commander of the [Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire) in the same year.[^cite_note-6] She is Judge in Charge of the [Technology and Construction Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology_and_Construction_Court).[^cite_note-7] She is also the judge responsible for the [Mariana dam disaster](48475234.md) case against [BHP](200825.md).[^cite_note-AP-8]

## Personal life

In 1993, she married Stuart Andrews with whom she has one daughter. He died in 2010.[^cite_note-\:0-1]
