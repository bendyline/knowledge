**Nonthaburi** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): นนทบุรี, pronounced [\[nōn.tʰá(ʔ).bù.rīː\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Thai)) is the capital city of the [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mueang_Nonthaburi_district) and [province](344757.md) of the same name, and the second largest city in [Thailand](30128.md).

On 15 February 1936, Nonthaburi town municipality (*[thesaban mueang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesaban#Town_municipality)*) was established, which only covered Suan Yai subdistrict (*[tambon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambon)*), just 2.5 km<sup>2</sup> (0.97 sq mi).[^cite_note-2] On 25 September 1995, Nonthaburi was upgraded to city municipality (*[thesaban nakhon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesaban#City_municipality)*) by enlargement of the city with four more subdistricts, Talat Khwan (8.2 km<sup>2</sup> \[3.2 sq mi]), Bang Khen (9.0 km<sup>2</sup> \[3.5 sq mi]), Bang Kraso (11.2 km<sup>2</sup> \[4.3 sq mi]) and Tha Sai (8.0 km<sup>2</sup> \[3.1 sq mi]), covering a total of 38.9 km<sup>2</sup> (15.0 sq mi).[^cite_note-3] As of 31 December 2019, it has a registered population of 254,375,[^cite_note-TDD-1] making it the most populous [city municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesaban) in Thailand (excluding Bangkok). There are 93 communities (*chumchon*), although not directly chosen by the local citizens, which provides advice and recommendations to local administrative organizations. Due to its proximity to [Bangkok](56615.md), the city is considered a suburb of Bangkok, a part of the [Bangkok Metropolitan Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok_Metropolitan_Region), or Greater Bangkok.

Nonthaburi is served by public transport systems including the [Bangkok Mass Transit Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok_Mass_Transit_Authority) bus system, the [Chao Phraya Express Boat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chao_Phraya_Express_Boat), and the [MRT Purple Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_Line_\(Bangkok\)) and [MRT Pink Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Line_\(Bangkok\)), inaugurated in 2016 and 2024 respectively.

Nonthaburi is known for its temple and market such as Wat Chaloem Phra Kiat, Wat Chomphuwek, Wat Chotikaram, Wat Khema Phirataram, Wat Prasat, Wat Sangkhathan and so on.
