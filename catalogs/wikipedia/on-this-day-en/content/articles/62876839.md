The **Grand Mosalla of Tehran**, officially the **Imam Khomeini Mosalla of Tehran** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): مصلای امام خمینی تهران), is a partially completed [Friday mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congregational_mosque) (*jāmeh*) and associated community complex, located near [Abbas Abad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_Abad_\(Tehran\)) in the city of [Tehran](57654.md), in the province of [Tehran](202698.md), [Iran](14653.md). The mosque is used for hosting weekly [Friday prayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_prayer) (*jāmeh*), as well as cultural, political, educational, worship activities, including book fairs, exhibitions, and religious ceremonies.

The [Persian-Islamic style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_architecture) mosque has been under construction since the 1990s. Upon completion, the mosque will have the world's biggest [iwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iwan).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] When completed, the mosque will surpass the [Masjid al-Haram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masjid_al-Haram) in [Mecca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mecca), as of April 2025, the world's largest mosque.

## History

The Grand Mosalla Mosque originated in a 1982 proposal to replace the [University of Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tehran) as the primary location for weekly Friday prayer.[^cite_note-\:0-6] A large section of land in [Abbas Abad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_Abad_\(Tehran\)), the original site for the never-materialized pre-[Revolution](347268.md) megaproject Shahestan Pahlavi, was allocated for the Grand Mosalla.[^cite_note-7]

On 19 February 1985, a public announcement was issued, calling on talented and experienced designers to submit drafts for the mosque's design. The competition was held in 1986 with [Mohammad Karim Pirnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Karim_Pirnia), [Mehdi Chamran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehdi_Chamran), Bagher Ayatollahzadeh Shirazi, Ali Ghaffari, and Mehdi Hodjat as the jury members, and with the participation of 36 native and foreign individuals from [Japan](15573.md), [Syria](7515849.md), [Pakistan](23235.md), and the [Netherlands](21148.md), as well as legal entities. On 1990, Dr. Parviz Moayed Ahd's design was confirmed for the Mosalla.[^cite_note-8]

As of April 2025, more than 30 years after the design was finalized, the Grand Mosalla remained under construction, though finished sections of the building were in use.[^cite_note-\:0-6] In 2017, the *[Financial Tribune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Tribune)* called the condition of the project "dismal" and "unacceptable", and Governor General of Tehran Province Hussein Hashemi urged [Tehran City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_City_Council_of_Tehran) to prioritize the mosque's completion.[^cite_note-\:0-6]

## Use

The Grand Mosalla Mosque is used for [Friday prayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_prayer) (*jāmeh*) and during [Eid al-Fitr](154944.md). [Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Khamenei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayatollah_Seyyed_Ali_Khamenei) has led prayers at the mosque in 2024 and 2025[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] and the mosque was a focal point for worship following the [sudden death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Varzaqan_helicopter_crash) of [Iranian President](233913.md), [Ebrahim Raisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebrahim_Raisi).[^cite_note-11]

The mosque complex is also used as a community center and a venue for local and national events, including the Tehran International Book Fair,[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] International Holy Quran Exhibition,[^cite_note-14] International Exhibition of Investment Opportunities in Iran's Mines and Mining Industries,[^cite_note-15] International Handicrafts Exhibition,[^cite_note-16] and the Tehran Game Exhibition, among other events.

## Access

The Grand Mosalla Mosque is served by the Mosalla Imam Khomeini Metro Station, a [Tehran Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran_Metro) station located in the Grand Mosalla, next to the [Qasem Soleimani Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qasem_Soleimani_Expressway).[^cite_note-17] It also has a [bus rapid transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bus_rapid_transit) station in Line 5: Science & Tech Terminal to Argentina Square Beihaghi Terminal.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]
