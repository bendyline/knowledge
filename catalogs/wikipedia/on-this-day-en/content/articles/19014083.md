The following is a list of [arenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena) ordered by [seating capacity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seating_capacity), which is the maximum number of seated spectators the arena can accommodate for a sports event. Only the capacity for indoor sports, such as [basketball](3921.md), [ice hockey](14790.md), and [volleyball](32558.md), are included. Currently all arenas with a capacity of 15,000 or more are included.

Venues are only included if they are designed primarily for sports traditionally held indoors. Venues built for [field sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_sports) that also host indoor events are not included. Examples of such unincluded venues are:

- [AT\&T Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT\&T_Stadium) (Arlington, Texas, US), a retractable-roof venue built for the [Dallas Cowboys](8121.md) (American football).

- [Caesars Superdome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesars_Superdome) (New Orleans, Louisiana, US), a domed venue built for the [New Orleans Saints](21720.md) (American football).

- [Decathlon Arena – Stade Pierre-Mauroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stade_Pierre-Mauroy) (Lille, France), a retractable-roof venue built for [Lille OSC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille_OSC) (association football).

- [JMA Wireless Dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JMA_Wireless_Dome) (Syracuse, New York, US), a domed venue built for multiple sports teams at [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_Orange).

- [Paris La Défense Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_La_D%C3%A9fense_Arena) (Nanterre, France), a domed venue built for [Racing 92](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_92) (rugby union).

Some of the above venues have hosted some of the largest crowds in history for indoor sports. The Caesars Superdome, for example, regularly seats more than 70,000 for basketball games (NCAA and NBA). The largest confirmed attendance for a basketball game (108,713) was at AT\&T Stadium (then known as Cowboys Stadium) for the [2010 NBA All-Star Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_NBA_All-Star_Game).

By contrast, [Saitama Super Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saitama_Super_Arena) in [the Japanese city of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saitama_\(city\)) is included because it was built primarily for indoor sports, although it can be configured to host field sports. (Both it and Paris La Défense Arena have movable seating blocks that can adapt to either an arena or field configuration.)
