**Don Victoriano**, officially the **Municipality of Don Victoriano**, unofficially as **Don Victoriano Chiongbian** ([Cebuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_language): *Lungsod sa Don Victoriano*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Don Victoriano*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Misamis Occidental](260936.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 10,214 people.[^cite_note-5]

The municipality is home to the [Mount Malindang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Malindang) National Park.

## History

The municipality was originally created as **Don Mariano Marcos** by virtue of [Batas Pambansa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batas_Pambansa) Blg. 171 which was approved on February 8, 1982. Parts of seven municipalities in [the province](260936.md) had been detached to be constituted into a new, distinct municipality. Those separated were [barangays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barangay) in [Tudela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tudela,_Misamis_Occidental) (3) including Tuno which was designated [the seat of government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poblacion), [Jimenez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimenez,_Misamis_Occidental) (also 3), [Bonifacio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonifacio,_Misamis_Occidental) (2), [Clarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarin,_Misamis_Occidental) and [Sinacaban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinacaban) (one in each); as well as [sitios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitio) in a barangay each in [Panaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panaon,_Misamis_Occidental) and [Aloran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aloran), situated on the side of the [Mount Malindang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Malindang) Range.[^cite_note-6] A plebiscite for ratification, along with 10 more newly created [local entities](22088280.md), was held on May 17, coinciding with [the barangay elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_Philippine_barangay_elections).[^cite_note-7]

It was later renamed to its present-day one, as *Don Victoriano*, through [Republic Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_Act) No. 6845 on January 20, 1990.[^cite_note-8] It was named in honor of a native of Jimenez who served as councilor of [Plaridel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaridel,_Misamis_Occidental) and eventually, the first mayor of this municipality.[^cite_note-tF14-9] Recent documents such as on [Commission on Elections](1847922.md) and the [Philippine Statistics Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Statistics_Authority) use *Don Victoriano Chiongbian* while [Commission on Audit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Audit_\(Philippines\)),[^cite_note-10] the local government and subsequent laws such as Republic Act No. 7329, 9692, 10017 use *Don Victoriano*. On July 13, 2026, the [Philippine Statistics Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Statistics_Authority) corrected the standardized official name of the municipality to *Don Victoriano* in the [Philippine Standard Geographic Code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Standard_Geographic_Code).[^cite_note-11]
