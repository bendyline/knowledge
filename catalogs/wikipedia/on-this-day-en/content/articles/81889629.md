# Currency swap

**Currency swap** may refer to:

- [Cross-currency swap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-currency_swap), a financial derivative

- [Foreign exchange swap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_exchange_swap), a simultaneous purchase and sale of identical amounts of one currency for another with two different value dates

- [Central bank liquidity swap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_bank_liquidity_swap), a swap line between central banks
