**SpaceX Crew-13** is the thirteenth operational NASA [Commercial Crew Program](64072812.md) flight and the 21st crewed orbital flight of a [Crew Dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crew_Dragon) spacecraft. The mission launched October 1, 2026 at 11\:10\:06 am to transport four crew members—[NASA](18426568.md) astronauts [Jessica Watkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Watkins) and [Luke Delaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Delaney_\(astronaut\)), [CSA](328618.md) astronaut [Joshua Kutryk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joshua_Kutryk), and [Roscosmos](406573.md) cosmonaut [Sergey Teteryatnikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Teteryatnikov)—to the [International Space Station](15043.md) (ISS).[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-3]

NASA announced the crew assignments for Crew-13 on April 23, 2026 consisting of NASA astronauts [Jessica Watkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Watkins), a [geologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geologist), and [Luke Delaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Delaney_\(astronaut\)), a former [naval aviator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_aviation), alongside CSA astronaut [Joshua Kutryk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joshua_Kutryk), a fighter pilot, and Roscosmos cosmonaut [Sergey Teteryatnikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Teteryatnikov), a submariner.[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-Today-4] Besides Watkins, who flew on [SpaceX Crew-4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpaceX_Crew-4), this will be the first mission to space for the remaining astronauts.[^cite_note-Today-4] Kutryk is the first Canadian to be sent to orbit as part of the [Commercial Crew Program](64072812.md).[^cite_note-Today-4][^cite_note-5]

## Mission

### Launch

The 13th operational SpaceX mission under NASA's Commercial Crew Program was scheduled to launch on September 12, 2026,[^cite_note-\:0-2] but a leak in [*Grace*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crew_Dragon_Grace)'s [oxidizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxidizer) tank delayed the launch.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] After the leak was discovered, Delaney noted that "The [numeral significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triskaidekaphobia) was not lost on us" and that they are "leveraging th\[e] heritage" of [Apollo 13](1770.md).[^cite_note-8] The crew went into a final quarantine starting on September 17 after the scheduled launch slipped to October 1.[^cite_note-October-1][^cite_note-9]
