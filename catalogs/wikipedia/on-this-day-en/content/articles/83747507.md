The **Kurultai of the Republic of Kazakhstan of the 1st convocation**[^cite_note-1] is the first [convocation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convocation) of the [Kurultai of Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurultai_of_Kazakhstan) that was elected following the [2026 Kazakh legislative election](83118601.md).[^cite_note-adyrna-2]

## Background

Following the [2026 Kazakh constitutional referendum](82396297.md), the country adopted a [new Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Constitution_of_Kazakhstan), which created a new unicameral legislature, the [Kurultai](83119061.md), in place of the bicameral [Parliament of Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Kazakhstan).[^cite_note-adyrna-2] Other political and legislative reforms occurred alongside this change, like the re-instatement of the office of [Vice President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Kazakhstan).[^cite_note-3]

## Election

Main article: [2026 Kazakh legislative election](83118601.md)

Kazakhstan held a [legislative election](83118601.md) on 23 August 2026 to elect all the 145 members to the newly created unicameral [Kurultai](83119061.md).[^cite_note-Reut-4][^cite_note-5] It was the 10th legislative election since Kazakhstan's [independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/On_the_State_Independence_of_the_Republic_of_Kazakhstan) in 1991 and first since the entry into force of the [2026 Constitution of Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Constitution_of_Kazakhstan).[^cite_note-6] Historically, there were many doubts in the [freedom and fairness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_fair_election) of the [elections in Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Kazakhstan) by differing observing international organizations.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

As a result of the legislative election, 5 parties have overcome the 5% [electoral threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_threshold). The [Ädilet](83490988.md) political party received the [absolute majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolute_majority), securing 110 seats out of 145.

## Sessions

The 1st Kurultai first convened on 27 August 2026.
