**Xing'an County** is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_\(China\)) in the northeast of [Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangxi_Zhuang_Autonomous_Region), China. It is administered as part of the [prefecture-level city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefecture-level_city) of [Guilin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guilin). Its area is 2,348 square kilometers (907 sq mi), with a population of 370000. The postal code is 541300.

## Administrative divisions

Xing'an County is divided into 6 towns, 3 townships and 1 ethnic township:[^cite_note-3]

towns

- [Xing'an Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xing'an_Town?action=edit\&redlink=1) 兴安镇

- [Xiangli Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiangli_Town?action=edit\&redlink=1) 湘漓镇

- [Jieshou Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jieshou_Town?action=edit\&redlink=1) 界首镇

- [Gaoshang Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaoshang_Town?action=edit\&redlink=1) 高尚镇

- [Yanguan Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yanguan_Town?action=edit\&redlink=1) 严关镇

- [Rongjiang Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rongjiang_Town?action=edit\&redlink=1) 溶江镇

townships

- [Mochuan Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mochuan_Township?action=edit\&redlink=1) 漠川乡

- Baishi Township 白石乡

- Cuijia Township 崔家乡

ethnic township

- [Huajiang Yao Ethnic Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huajiang_Yao_Ethnic_Township?action=edit\&redlink=1) 华江瑶族乡
