The **Southern** **Giants Brigades** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الوية العمالقة الجنوبية, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Alwiyat al-Amaliqa al-Janubia*) is a [pro-government](31167279.md) [militia](20622.md) in Yemen. Its fighters are predominantly tribesmen from southern Yemen, particularly from the [Lahej](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahij_Governorate), [Abyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abyan_Governorate) and [Dhale](3604352.md) governorates.&#x20;

They include [Salafi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salafi_movement) students of [Muqbil al-Wadi'i's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muqbil_al-Wadi'i) *Dar al-hadith* religious institute in [Dammaj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dammaj), [Saada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saada_Governorate) that were expelled by the [Houthis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_movement) in early 2014.[^cite_note-1] The militia is part of the Yemeni Joint Forces and is the largest faction in the formation. The Giants Brigades also received extensive support from the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) militarily and financially.[^cite_note-2]

## History

The brigades were originally in the former North Yemeni military since the early 1970s. After Yemen unified, these brigades saw intensive action in the [1994 civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_Civil_War_\(1994\)) and continued to be an effective unit in the Yemeni military. The original Giants Forces are no longer officially present and have been broken up and distributed in different areas over the years. The current Giants Brigades are a relatively new force in Yemen. They emerged from the [Southern Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Resistance) that fought the Houthis in the South at the beginning of the conflict in 2015.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] They have shown their military power during the [Al Hudaydah governorate offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Hudaydah_offensive), where they have killed hundreds of Houthi militants.[^cite_note-5]

In May 2018 the militia alongside [Arab coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabian%E2%80%93led_intervention_in_Yemen) forces took control of several pockets in [Taiz](545270.md) from the Houthis, in the fighting several Houthi fighters were killed and several pieces of their equipment were either damaged or destroyed. After taking control of the "Mocha Interception" in the city the militia alongside allied forces including other factions in the Joint Forces, the [Tihamah Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tihamah_Resistance) and [Yemeni National Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_National_Resistance) they began to advance towards the "Bara Junction" to cut the Houthis' supply lines.[^cite_note-6]

Although many members of the Giants Brigades are known to be [Salafists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salafi_movement), most of them are first and foremost [southerners](718749.md).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

In December 2021, the Giants announced it had sent troops to [Shabwah Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shabwah_Governorate) "to liberate areas that fell into Houthi hands",[^cite_note-9] driving out the rebels in just two weeks.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] They also seized southern areas of neighbouring [Marib province](3604391.md),[^cite_note-13] where the rebels and loyalists have been engaged in a months-long [battles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Marib) to seize its strategic capital city.[^cite_note-14] After completing military operation Storm of the South the Southern Giants announced the redeployment of its forces after having "completed its mission" and that its troops remain on the frontlines ready to repel any Houthi attacks.[^cite_note-15]

On 7 April 2022, the Yemeni President [Abdrabbuh Mansur Hadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdrabbuh_Mansur_Hadi) issued an order dismissing Vice President [Ali Mohsen al-Ahmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Mohsen_al-Ahmar) and delegating his powers to a newly formed [presidential council](70488867.md) to lead the country through a transitional period.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] The leader of the Giants [Abu Zara'a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Zara'a) is a member of this leadership council.[^cite_note-18]

On 16 July 2026, they said that they had intercepted a boat carrying weapons manufacturing equipment for the Houthis.[^cite_note-19]
