The **Cessna 205**, **206** and **207**, known primarily as the **Stationair** (and marketed variously as the **Super Skywagon**, **Skywagon** and **Super Skylane**), are a family of single-engined, general aviation aircraft with fixed landing gear, used in commercial air service as well as for personal use. The family was originally developed from the popular retractable-gear [Cessna 210](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_210) and produced by the [Cessna Aircraft Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna).

The line's combination of a powerful engine, rugged construction and a large cabin has made these aircraft popular [bush planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bush_plane). Cessna describes the 206 as "the sport-utility vehicle of the air." These airplanes are also used for aerial photography, skydiving and other utility purposes. They can also be equipped with floats, amphibious floats and skis. Alternatively, they can be fitted with luxury appointments for use as a personal air transport.

From 1962 to 2006 Cessna produced 8,509 aircraft in the 205, 206 and 207 variants. The aircraft remains in production.

## Development

### Cessna 205



The 205 kept the [210](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_210) cowling bulge

The Cessna 205 was introduced late in 1962 as a 1963 [model year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_year). The six-seat aircraft was essentially a Cessna 210 with fixed landing gear and with changes to the crew and passenger door arrangement, being officially designated by Cessna as a "Model 210-5".[^cite_note-3A21-1] The 205 retained the early 210's engine [cowling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowling) bulge, originally where the 210 stowed its nosewheel on retraction. This distinctive cowling was made more streamlined on the later Cessna 206.

The 205 is powered by a [Continental IO-470-S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_O-470) engine producing 260 hp (190 kW).[^cite_note-Bluebook-2] The 205 was produced in only two model years, 1963 and 1964, before being replaced in production by the Cessna 206. A total of 576 Cessna 205s were produced.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

### Cessna 206

The six-seat Model 206 was introduced as a 1964 model and was built until 1986, when Cessna halted production of its single-engined product line. It was then re-introduced in 1998[^cite_note-Bluebook-2] and remains in production.[^cite_note-Official-3]

There were many sub-variants, including the U206, P206 all certified to CAR3 standards and later 206H certified to [FAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Aviation_Regulations) Part 23. The total Model 206 production between 1964 and 2004 was 6,581 aircraft.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

#### Cessna U206



U206 with large clamshell rear door

The original 1964 model was the U206, powered by a 285 hp (213 kW) [Continental IO-520-A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_O-520). The “U” designation indicated “utility” and this model was equipped with a pilot side door and large clamshell rear door serving the back two rows of seats, allowing easy loading of oversized cargo.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

There was a TU206 turbocharged version powered by the [Continental TSIO-520-C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_O-520) engine producing 285 hp (213 kW). After 1967, the turbo TU206 was powered by a TSIO-520-F of 300 hp (220 kW). The extra 15 hp (11 kW) was obtained by turning the engine at a higher rpm, and was allowed for only five minutes.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2] Due to the large propeller diameter, the additional engine speed meant that the propeller tips were pushed to [transonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transonic) speeds, which required much more power.

From 1964 to 1969, the U206 was known as the “Super Skywagon”. From 1970, it was named the “Stationair”, a contraction of “Station Wagon of the Air”, which is a good description of the aircraft's intended role. Sub-variants were designated U206 to U206G.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

In 1977, the U206 had its engine upgraded to a Continental IO-520-F of 300 hp (220 kW) (continuous rating, obtained at a lower speed than the previous TSIO-520-F) and the TU206 powerplant was changed to the TSIO-520-M producing 310 hp (230 kW).[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

Production of all versions of the U206 was halted in 1986 when Cessna stopped manufacturing all piston-engined aircraft. A total of 5,208 U206s had been produced.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

#### Cessna P206



P206 with passenger doors

The P206 was added to the line in 1965. In this case the "P" stood for "passenger", as the P206 had passenger doors similar to the Cessna 210 from which it was derived, on both sides.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

The P206 was produced from 1965 to 1970 and was powered by a Continental IO-520-A of 285 hp (213 kW). There was a turbocharged model designated TP206 which was powered by a Continental TSIO-520-A also of 285 hp (213 kW).[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

Production of the P206 amounted to 647. The name “Super Skylane” made it sound like a version of the [Cessna 182](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_182), which it was not. Sub-variants were designated P206 to P206E.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

#### Cessna 206H



T206H with large clamshell cargo doors



T206H with [floats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_float)

After a production hiatus of twelve years, Cessna started manufacturing a new version of the venerable 206 in 1998, with the introduction of the newly certified 206H.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2] The “H” model is generally similar to the previous U206 configuration, with a pilot entry door and a rear double clamshell door for access to the middle and back seats. The "H" is marketed under the name "Stationair".

The 206H is powered by a [Lycoming IO-540-AC1A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycoming_O-540) powerplant producing 300 hp (220 kW). The turbocharged T206H is powered by a [Lycoming TIO-540-AJ1A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycoming_O-540) engine of 310 hp (230 kW).[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

Even though the Cessna 206H is certified as a six-seat aircraft in its country of origin, the Canadian aviation regulator, [Transport Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_Canada) has certified it to carry only five people in [Canada](5042916.md). This is due to concerns about passenger egress through the rear clamshell door with the flaps extended. Cessna addressed one part of this problem early on, after a flight-test aircraft was damaged when the pilot extended the flaps while taxiing, and his passenger had the clamshell door open (for ventilation; it was a hot summer day). A switch was added to the flap actuation circuit which disabled the flaps when the doors were open. The other part of the problem is that if the flaps are already down, the passenger must perform the complicated procedure of opening the front part as far as possible (about 8 in (20 cm)) then open the rear door and release the spring-loaded rear door handle so it retracts out of the way. This then gives enough clearance to open the rear part of the door fully for egress.[^cite_note-4]

Both the 206H and the T206H remain in production in 2013. By the end of 2004, Cessna had produced 221 206Hs and 505 T206Hs, for a total production of 726 "H" models.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2] Cessna has indicated that they do not intend to produce a P206-configuration aircraft in the future, due to lack of market demand.

### Cessna 207



The 207, which incorporates a 45 in (114 cm) stretch

The Model 207 was a seven- and later eight-seat development of the 206, achieved by stretching the design further by 45 inches (114 cm)[^cite_note-Jane's_1971_p._280-5] to allow space for more seats. The nose section was extended 18 inches (46 cm) by adding a constant-section nose baggage compartment between the passenger compartment and the engine firewall; the aft section was extended by 27 inches (69 cm)[^cite_note-Simpson_p._86-6] by inserting a constant-area section in the fuselage area just aft of the aft wing attach point. Thus the propeller's ground clearance was unaffected by the change (the nosewheel had moved forward the same distance as the propeller), but the tail moved aft relative to the mainwheel position, which made landing (without striking the tailskid on the runway) a greater challenge. The move gave the 207 a larger turning radius, since the distance between mainwheels and nosewheel increased by 18 inches (46 cm) but the nosewheel's maximum allowed deflection was not increased.

The 207 was introduced as a 1969 model featuring a Continental IO-520-F engine of 300 hp (220 kW). A turbocharged version was equipped with a TSIO-520-G of the same output.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

At the beginning of production the model was called a Cessna 207 "Skywagon", but in 1977 the name was changed to "Stationair 7". 1977 also saw a change of engine on the turbocharged version to a Continental TSIO-520-M producing 310 hp (230 kW) – the same engine used in the TU206 of the same vintage.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

The 207 added a seat in 1980 and was then known as the "Stationair 8". Production of the 207 was completed in 1984, just two years before U206 production halted. A total of 626 Cessna 207s were manufactured.[^cite_note-Bluebook-2]

The Cessna Model 207 has been popular with air taxi companies, particularly on short runs where its full seating capacity could be used. Very few of these aircraft have seen private use.

### Modifications



207A with Soloy turbine conversion

In April 2007, [Thielert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thielert) announced that the [European Aviation Safety Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Aviation_Safety_Agency) had granted a [Supplemental Type Certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supplemental_Type_Certificate) (STC) for conversion of Cessna 206s to the Thielert V-8 diesel powerplant. The STC allows conversion of the following models: U206F and TU206F with the 300 hp (220 kW) powerplant, and the U206G, TU206G, 206H and T206H with the 310 hp (230 kW) version.[^cite_note-7] This modification does not require any changes to the engine cowling. In May 2008, Thielert entered insolvency proceedings, so the future availability of this diesel conversion is uncertain.[^cite_note-Avweb26Apr08-8]

Soloy Aviation Solutions offers a [turboprop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turboprop) conversion for some 206/207 models with the 418 shp (312 kW) [Rolls-Royce/Allison M250](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allison_Model_250) engine/gearbox package. However, extensive engine cowl modifications are required.[^cite_note-9]

Atlantic Aero offers an FAA [STC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supplemental_Type_Certificate) conversion to the [Continental IO-550](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_IO-550) powerplant. No cowl modifications are required.[^cite_note-10]

Both Kenmore Air ([Edo floats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_floats?action=edit\&redlink=1)) and Wipaire (Wipline floats) offer seaplane conversions.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

### Cessna 206

206 Super Skywagon

Introduced for the 1964 model year, the 206 differed from the 205 primarily in that it was powered by a 285 hp (213 kW) [Continental IO-520-A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_O-520). Other changes included a strengthened fuselage, all entry doors removed except the pilot's door on the left side, new 42 in (110 cm) removable double cargo doors on the right side, and heavy-duty brakes. The interior was also redesigned with durable and easy cleaning materials. Only the pilot's seat came as standard, though three optional seating arrangements were offered with up to six seats. Other optional equipment included heavy-duty main tires, floats, skis, a 300 lb (136 kg) fuselage cargo pod, 150 US gal (568 L) tank and spray boom for [agricultural use](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_aircraft), and an [air ambulance](32993239.md) configuration. The aircraft had a gross weight of 3,300 lb (1,497 kg) as a landplane or 3,500 lb (1,588 kg) as a seaplane. Certified on July 19, 1963.

### Cessna 207

207 Skywagon 207

Introduced for the 1969 model year as a development of the U206C with a stretched cabin allowing for a seventh seat, powered by a 300 hp (224 kW) [Continental IO-520-F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_O-520) engine, and with a gross weight of 3,800 lb (1,724 kg). ### Civil

The aircraft is popular with air charter companies and small cargo air carriers, and is operated by private individuals and companies. One of the largest Cessna 207 operators was [Yute Air Alaska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_Alaska), which had a fleet of 12 aircraft. [Canwest Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CanWest_Air) operates two Cessna 206 aircraft in [Northern Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Alberta), which are the backbone of their charter fleet.[^cite_note-Avweb23Aug10-24]

## Accidents

- July 24, 1972 near [Aspen, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspen,_Colorado), a 27-year-old student pilot with a total of 39 hours of flying time flew a Cessna U206, registered N5290U, into a blind canyon and stalled the aircraft while trying to turn around, killing all four people on board.

  [^cite_note-61]

  &#x20;Among the passengers was wealthy playboy, entrepreneur, racing driver and developer [Lance Reventlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Reventlow), who was a Woolworth heir, son of [Barbara Woolworth Hutton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Hutton) and the husband of former [Mouseketeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mickey_Mouse_Club) and actress [Cheryl Holdridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheryl_Holdridge).

- On May 1, 2023, a Cessna 206 with seven people on board [crashed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Colombia_Cessna_206_crash) in the jungle in the [Caquetá Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caquet%C3%A1_Department) of [Colombia](5222.md). Two of the occupants – the pilot and one adult – were killed on impact. Another passenger, the mother of the remaining four, all children, died soon after the crash. The children, between 11 months and 13 years old, survived for over five weeks in the rainforest before being rescued by the Colombian military and rescuers.

- On September 29, 2023, a Cessna 206 aircraft owned by RioZim was en route from Harare to the [Murowa diamond mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murowa_diamond_mine) in Zimbabwe when the plane crashed near the mine. All 6 people aboard the plane died in the crash, including the owner of the mine [Harpal Randhawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harpal_Randhawa).

  [^cite_note-62]

- On May 26, 2024, a Cessna 206 with 7 people on board (pilot, 6 passengers) crashed in [Butler, Missouri](122519.md) while on a skydiving flight. Everyone survived as they managed to escape before the crash using parachutes.
