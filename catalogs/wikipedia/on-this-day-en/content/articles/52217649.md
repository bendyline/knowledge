**Jannata** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): جناتا, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Jannātā*) is a municipality in [Southern Lebanon](4205968.md), located in the [Tyre District](11166788.md), [Governorate of South Lebanon](1283570.md).

## Etymology

According to [E. H. Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Henry_Palmer), the name comes from *Jennat*, garden.[^cite_note-1]

## History

In 1875, [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) found the village, (which he called *Djennateh*), to have 60 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh) inhabitants.[^cite_note-2] He further noted: "The village contains a number of ruined houses. A little [mosque](19894.md) is partly built of ancient materials."[^cite_note-3]

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described it: "A small village of stone and mud, containing about 100 Moslems. \[…] It lies low, on arable land. The water is supplied by two [wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_well) in the village."[^cite_note-4]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.24% of registered voters in Jannata. 97.70% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-5]
