**Tufan Erhürman** (born 11 September 1970) is a Turkish Cypriot academic, lawyer, diplomat, and politician who has been the 6th [president of Northern Cyprus](7887833.md) since 2025. A scholar in public law by profession, he served in the negotiations to solve the [Cyprus dispute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus_dispute) between 2008 and 2010. He previously worked for the [Ministry of Justice](10283737.md) of Turkey between 1999 and 2004 and worked for the establishment of the position of [ombudsman](26289198.md) in Turkey.&#x20;

He served as the leader of the [Republican Turkish Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Turkish_Party) between 2016 and 2025, until his ascension to the Presidency. He held the office of Prime Minister beginning January 2018 as the leader of a four-party coalition.[^cite_note-1] The coalition government resigned on 9 May 2019,[^cite_note-2] with Erhürman continuing as Prime Minister until the office was taken over by [Ersin Tatar](60871206.md) on 22 May 2019.[^cite_note-3] He was elected president of Northern Cyprus in a landslide in the [2025 Northern Cypriot presidential election](78191958.md).[^cite_note-4]

## Education

Erhürman finished his secondary education in [Türk Maarif Koleji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%BCrk_Maarif_Koleji). He was then enrolled to study law at [Ankara University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara_University) in 1988. He received his master's degree and doctorate from Ankara University as well. His 2001 PhD thesis was on the "Non-Juridical Inspection of the Authority and the Ombudsman".[^cite_note-5]

## Academic career

Erhürman lectured on public law at the [Ankara University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara_University), [Middle East Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East_Technical_University) and [Hacettepe University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacettepe_University) between 1995 and 2001. He then lectured at the [Eastern Mediterranean University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Mediterranean_University) from 2001 to 2006, and from 2008 to 2013. Erhürman also lectured at the [Near East University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near_East_University) between the years of 2006 and 2008.[^cite_note-\:1-6]

## Political career

Erhürman became involved in the negotiations to solve the Cyprus dispute under President [Mehmet Ali Talat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehmet_Ali_Talat) between 2008 and 2010.[^cite_note-\:1-6]

He ran for a Nicosia seat at the [Assembly of the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_of_the_Republic_\(Northern_Cyprus\)) in the [2013 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Northern_Cyprus_parliamentary_election) and became a Member of Parliament as a candidate of the [Republican Turkish Party-United Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Turkish_Party).[^cite_note-7] He worked extensively to change the constitution where 23 changes were agreed by all 4 parties which were being represented in the parliament at that time.[^cite_note-8] However, 62.3% of the voters rejected the new constitution in the [2014 referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Northern_Cyprus_constitutional_referendum). He became the Secretary General of the Republican Turkish Party-United Forces.[^cite_note-9]

On 13 November 2016, Erhürman became the leader of the [Republican Turkish Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Turkish_Party) and thus of the main opposition.[^cite_note-10]

He was reelected as a Member of the Parliament during the [2018 snap elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Northern_Cyprus_parliamentary_election), however he could not prevent his party from losing 9 seats. On 19 January 2018, negotiations started to form a coalition between 4 parties, the [Republican Turkish Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Turkish_Party), the [People's Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Party_\(Northern_Cyprus\)), the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Northern_Cyprus\)) and the [Communal Democracy Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communal_Democracy_Party),[^cite_note-11] ultimately succeeding in the formation of the [Erhürman cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erh%C3%BCrman_cabinet).

On 19 October 2025, Erhürman defeated incumbent president [Ersin Tatar](60871206.md) in the [2025 Northern Cypriot presidential election](78191958.md), winning 62.76% of the vote.[^cite_note-\:0-12]

## Political positions

Erhürman has stated his support for the [reunification of Cyprus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus_problem) under a federal model.[^cite_note-\:0-12] However he has also stated that he would not go down such a route without the approval of the government of Turkey.
