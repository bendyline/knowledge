**Chadchart Sittipunt** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): ชัชชาติ สิทธิพันธุ์, [RTGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Thai_General_System_of_Transcription): *_Chatchat Sitthiphan_*, pronounced [\[t͡ɕʰát.t͡ɕʰâːt sìt.tʰí(ʔ).pʰān\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Thai); born 24 May 1966) is a Thai politician, engineer, and professor who is the incumbent Governor of Bangkok since 2022. He previously was [Minister of Transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Transport_\(Thailand\)) from 2012 to 2014.

## Early life and education

Chadchart is the third son of Police General Sa-nae Sitthiphan, former Commissioner of the [Metropolitan Police Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Police_Bureau) and Jitcharung Kullawanit. His sister, Preechaya Sittipunt, is a retired lecturer in Architecture at Chulalongkorn University. She holds a Master's degree from Massachusetts Institute of Technology, and a Ph.D. from the University of California, Berkeley.[^cite_note-2] His twin brother, Assoc. Prof. Chanchai Sittipunt, is the dean of Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University.[^cite_note-3]

Chatchart graduated high school from Chulalongkorn University Demonstration School and [Triam Udom Suksa School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triam_Udom_Suksa_School). He completed a bachelor's degree in [Civil Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Engineering) (First Class Honors) from [Chulalongkorn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chulalongkorn_University) and then received a Master's of [Structural Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structural_Engineering) from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](18879.md) (MIT) and a Doctorate of Engineering from the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign) in the USA with Ananda Mahidol Foundation Scholarship in 1987. Furthermore, he also completed a Master's degree in Business Administration (MBA) from Chulalongkorn University.[^cite_note-4]

## Career

Chadchart used to work as a [structural engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structural_engineer) in a private company. Later in 1995, he joined the Faculty of Engineering, [Chulalongkorn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chulalongkorn_University) as an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) and an assistant to the president. In addition, he has served as a director in many state enterprises such as [The Transport Company Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Transport_Company_Limited?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Mass Rapid Transit Authority of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_Rapid_Transit_Authority_of_Thailand) and [Aeronautical Radio of Thailand Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeronautical_Radio_of_Thailand_Limited?action=edit\&redlink=1) and in 2008 to January 2012 as an independent director Audit Committee and Member of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, [Land and Houses Plc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_and_Houses)[^cite_note-5]

In January 2015, Chadchart was appointed as CEO and managing director of [Quality Houses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_Houses?action=edit\&redlink=1) Public Company Limited (QH).[^cite_note-6] On 29 December 2018, Chadchart resigned from the position and returned to work for the [Pheu Thai Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pheu_Thai_Party).[^cite_note-7]

## Political career

Chadchart as an academic had the opportunity to help and give advice to the Ministry of Transport during the [Thaksin Shinawatra](360815.md) and [Samak Sundaravej](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samak_Sundaravej) governments without holding any position until 2012, when Prime Minister [Yingluck Shinawatra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yingluck_Shinawatra) invited him to serve as the Deputy Minister of Transport.

Chadchart was appointed as the Minister of Transport in October 2012. Chadchart as Minister of Transport is regarded as an elite figure of the government in the country's strategic planning.

In 2019, Chadchart announced that he intended to run in the [2022 Bangkok gubernatorial election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Bangkok_gubernatorial_election) as an independent candidate. In February 2022, Chadchart officially launched his gubernatorial election campaign, with the slogan "Bangkok, a liveable city for everyone" ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): "กรุงเทพฯ เมืองน่าอยู่สำหรับทุกคน") with important policies such as creating a network of maps of crime hotspots, and developing a public transport system to make Bangkok a city of arts and learning. [Bhichit Rattakul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhichit_Rattakul), former Governor of Bangkok, and [Nikom Wairatpanij](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikom_Wairatpanij?action=edit\&redlink=1), former President of the Senate of Thailand also joined Chadchart for the campaign.[^cite_note-8]

Chadchart won the 2022 Bangkok gubernatorial election in a [landslide victory](41053306.md), receiving over 1.3 million votes and becoming the first governor-elect to lead in all 50 districts.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] He ran for re-election in the [2026 Bangkok gubernatorial election](80394340.md), also winning by a landslide that surpassed his own record in 2022.[^cite_note-12]

## Public image

While serving as a minister, Chadchart had the image of a down-to-earth man. In 2013, an image of Chadchart walking barefoot into a [Buddhist temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wat) went viral as an [internet meme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_meme), gaining him the nickname of "the world's strongest minister" in Thai internet circles and media.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] The memes are often in the same absurd hyperbolic style as [Chuck Norris jokes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Norris_jokes), and he was often compared to [Hanma Yujiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanma_Yujiro) from the [Baki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baki_the_Grappler) manga franchise.[^cite_note-16] In June 2022, a friend of [Wanchalearm Satsaksit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanchalearm_Satsaksit) claimed that the image was taken by Wanchalearm himself. It came during a talk in remembrance of Wanchalearm's second year of getting [abducted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanchalearm_Satsaksit#Abduction).[^cite_note-17]
