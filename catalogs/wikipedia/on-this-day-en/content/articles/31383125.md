A **headliner** is the main act in a [music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music), [theatre](20913771.md), or [comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stand-up_comedy) performance. Generally, the headliner is the final act in a performance, preceded by the [opening act(s)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opening_act).

In [music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music), the headliner often reserves sole permissions to the name of the tour. Thus, tour names often reflect the name of the latest album or a popular song from the latest album of the headliner. Additionally, the headliner is often the most famous or prominent act in the performance. There are also headliners in [American burlesque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_burlesque).

The [main event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Card_\(sports\)#Main_event) is a similar concept in sports.

## History

The term "headliner" dates back to the 1890s.[^cite_note-1] "Top of the bill" is a similar concept in [music hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_hall).
