The **General Staff H.Q. of the Kuwait Army** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): رئاسة الأركان العامة للجيش الكويتي) redesignated in 1953 following formation in 1949, is a management department of the [Kuwait Armed Forces](16692.md), the national military of the [State of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Kuwait). The **Chief of the General Staff** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): رئيس هيئة الأركان العامة) is the senior combat military officer governing [doctrines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_doctrine) of the [Kuwait Army](13558747.md), the [Kuwait Air Force](4641117.md) and the [Kuwait Naval Force](49634797.md), but excludes the [Kuwait National Guard](45563700.md), the [Kuwait Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_Police) and Kuwait Fire Service Directorate. He is appointed by the [Kuwait Defense Minister](40773219.md),[^cite_note-primary-1] a deputy prime minister, who is appointed by the [Prime Minister of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Prime_Ministers_of_Kuwait); the latter is appointed by the [Emir of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir_of_Kuwait), the [Commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Military of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Kuwait).

## Kuwait Army

Main article: [Military of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Kuwait)

On 1 July 1961, when the ministry was not yet born during [Operation Vantage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Vantage), the Kuwait Army was the de facto command leadership of the available armed forces since establishment and acted as official minister advising the [Emir of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir_of_Kuwait) on course of action. The Kuwait Army redesignated in 1953 was founded thirteen years before the enacting of the respective Kuwait ministry, mainly by [Field Marshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_Marshal) Sheikh Abdullah Mubarak Al-Sabah in 1949.

## Chief of the General Staff of Kuwait Armed Forces

The joint offices of the assistants chief of general staff are tasked with delegating combat responsibility for logistics supply command, joint military operations, manpower and others.

The joint assistants chief of the general staff combat commanders are the chief combat commanders of the Kuwait Land Force, the Kuwait Air Force, and the Kuwait Naval Force.

### Assistant Chief Commander of Kuwait Land Force

The assistant chief combat commander of the [Kuwait Army](13558747.md) commands at his disposition the senior land combat officers and [warrant officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warrant_officer) commanders of the following [infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infantry), [mechanized infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanized_infantry), [artillery](2508.md), [commando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commando), and [tank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank) brigades:

- Kuwait 6th Liberation Mechanized Brigade

- Kuwait 15th Mubarak Armored Brigade

- Kuwait 26th Al-Soor Mechanized Brigade

- Kuwait 35th [Shahid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahid) Armored Brigade

- Kuwait 94th Saleh Al-Mohammed Mechanized Brigade

### Assistant Chief Commander of Kuwait Air Force

The assistant chief combat commander of the [Kuwait Air Force](4641117.md) commands at his disposition the senior [air](4641117.md) combat officers and warrant officer commanders of the following air bases and their combat operational assets:

- [Abdullah Al-Mubarak Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_Al-Mubarak_Air_Base)

- [Ali Al Salem Air Base](1469367.md)

- [Ahmad al-Jaber Air Base](5306887.md)

- Air Defense Brigades

### Assistant Chief Commander of Kuwait Naval Force

The assistant chief combat commander of the [Kuwait Naval Force](49634797.md) commands at his disposition the senior [naval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_Navy) combat officers, warrant officer, and [commando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commando) warfighting naval commanders:

- Kuwait Marine Corps

- Kuwait Naval Warships
