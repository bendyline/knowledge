The **Bab al-Mandab Strait crisis** is an escalation that began in September 2026 as part of the wider [Yemeni offensives](84167596.md) tied to the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), in which Yemen's [Houthis](17240332.md) launched an offensive to seize control of the [Bab al-Mandab Strait](62138.md), a global [choke point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choke_point).[^cite_note-wsj-1][^cite_note-cnn-mocha-2] Houthi fighters [seized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Mocha) the historic Red Sea port city of [Mokha](2082636.md), pushing further along the Yemeni coast toward the strait, with Houthi troops moving onto the [Hanish Islands](3542308.md) in the middle of the [Red Sea](26191.md).[^cite_note-mee-3][^cite_note-ajmocha-4] The Houthis declared a [blockade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_blockade_of_Saudi_Arabia) targeting Saudi Arabian shipping and announced the strait closed to specific vessels, driving global oil prices above \$100 a barrel.[^cite_note-usnews-5][^cite_note-ynet-6]

As of mid-September 2026, [Houthi forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_forces) recaptured [Yemen](350939.md)'s entire [Red Sea](26191.md) coastline, [Perim Island](1907645.md), and the [Hanish Islands](3542308.md), giving them strategic control over the [Bab al-Mandab Strait](62138.md), the narrow [sea lane](2018420.md) connecting the [Red Sea](26191.md) to the [Gulf of Aden](58716.md).[^cite_note-auto-7][^cite_note-auto1-8][^cite_note-auto2-9]

## Background

Main articles: [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), [Strait of Hormuz crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Hormuz_crisis), and [2026 Yemen offensives](84167596.md)

Further information: [Red Sea crisis](75207766.md)

The Bab al-Mandab Strait connects the Red Sea with the [Gulf of Aden](58716.md) and the [Indian Ocean](14580.md) and is a critical trade artery, handling nearly 10 percent of global trade and roughly 5 to 7 percent of global oil shipments; threats and attacks in the strait can force commercial vessels and oil tankers to reroute around Africa's [Cape of Good Hope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_of_Good_Hope), adding weeks of travel time and higher costs.[^cite_note-boereport-10][^cite_note-nbc-11] The strait's strategic importance had increased dramatically because Iran's closure of the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md) amid the 2026 Iran war had forced Saudi Arabia to rely far more heavily on its Red Sea export route; between March and mid-July 2026, Saudi seaborne crude exports through Bab al-Mandab were eight times higher than during the same period in 2025.[^cite_note-aj-escalate-12] Fighting between the Houthis and Saudi-backed government forces had reignited in July 2026, after roughly four years of relative calm under a UN-brokered truce, and intensified through August and September as the group shifted its focus from northern Yemen to control of the country's long coastline.[^cite_note-aj-latest-13][^cite_note-usnews-5] Ahmed Nagi, a senior analyst at the [International Crisis Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Crisis_Group), said control of the strait held importance in the Houthis' broader strategy beyond territorial gain on the ground.[^cite_note-aj-escalate-12]

## Events

On 10 September 2026, Houthi forces captured the districts of Hays and [Al Khawkhah district](29273806.md) in [Hodeidah province](3604362.md), as well as the town of Mocha in western [Taiz province](3604497.md), following rapid advances.[^cite_note-ajmocha-4] The [Presidential Leadership Council](70488867.md) (PLC) said its forces had retreated toward the [Dhubab district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhubab_district) near the Bab al-Mandab strait.[^cite_note-skynews-14][^cite_note-reuters-advance-15] Later that day, reports emerged that the Houthis had also reached the Hanish Islands in Hodeidah governorate, amid unverified claims that they had captured the strategically located Dhubab district and [Perim](1907645.md) island in the Bab al-Mandab strait itself.[^cite_note-hanish-16][^cite_note-perim-17] By mid-September, the Houthis' rapid advance had brought them to within about 20 mi (32 km) of the US military base in [Djibouti](17207794.md), across the strait, and the [International Organization for Migration](36893.md) said more than 82,000 people had been displaced by the fighting since the start of September.[^cite_note-pbs-18]

As of mid-September 2026, [Houthi forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_forces) control [Yemen](350939.md)'s entire [Red Sea](26191.md) coastline, [Perim Island](1907645.md), and the [Hanish Islands](3542308.md), giving them strategic control over the [Bab al-Mandab Strait](62138.md), the narrow [sea lane](2018420.md) connecting the [Red Sea](26191.md) to the [Gulf of Aden](58716.md).[^cite_note-auto-7][^cite_note-auto1-8][^cite_note-auto2-9]

### Attacks on Saudi Arabia

As the ground offensive advanced, the Houthis intensified [missile and drone attacks on Saudi Arabia](84173141.md). On 8 September 2026, Houthi forces struck the Saudi cities of [Abha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abha), [Khamis Mushait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khamis_Mushait), [Jizan](1907222.md) and [Najran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Najran), hitting civilian and economic sites, including Jazan City for Primary and Downstream Industries and [King Khalid Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Khalid_Air_Base), and wounding at least 73 people, among them women and children.[^cite_note-npr-19][^cite_note-cnn-attack-20] The Houthi military said the attacks were in response to more than 100 Saudi airstrikes in Yemen over the preceding three days, and said it had fired "dozens of ballistic missiles and drones".[^cite_note-cnn-attack-20] Saudi Arabia's Energy Ministry said the strikes had also triggered fires at multiple oil installations and utility sites across the kingdom's southern provinces.[^cite_note-aj-escalate-12] On 10 September, Saudi authorities activated air-raid alerts in Abha and the [Khamis Mushait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khamis_Mushait) region warning residents to take shelter, though the alerts were quickly lifted and Saudi officials said there was no danger.[^cite_note-usnews-5] On 13 September, the Houthis claimed responsibility for a further attack on a military base in southern Saudi Arabia.[^cite_note-pbs-18]

## Reactions

Saudi-led coalition spokesman Major-General [Turki al-Maliki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turki_al-Maliki) said the coalition would "resolutely confront" the Houthis' "hostile approach" and vowed a response under the kingdom's right to self-defense.[^cite_note-cnn-attack-20] The [Saudi Foreign Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Foreign_Ministry) said on [X](9988187.md) that "the kingdom affirms its legitimate right to take all necessary measures to defend its sovereignty".[^cite_note-cnn-attack-20] A source familiar with Saudi thinking told [CNN](62028.md) that [Riyadh](51554.md) was planning to retaliate and had informed allies of its plans, and that the kingdom was working to bring other countries, some previously wary of being seen as joining the US war with Iran, into efforts to push back against the Houthis.[^cite_note-cnn-attack-20] Houthi military spokesperson Brigadier General [Yahya Saree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahya_Saree) blamed Saudi Arabia for the escalation, warning that "every aggression against our country and people will be met with a firm response," while Nasruddin Amer, deputy head of the Houthi media office, said "the Saudi enemy has to understand that the time for committing crimes and massacres against the Yemeni people without response, deterrence or paying a price has ended".[^cite_note-npr-19]

## Impact

Analysts warned that a [Houthi](17240332.md) campaign against shipping in [Bab al-Mandab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bab_al-Mandab) could open a second maritime front in a conflict that had already choked off traffic through the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md), adding further pressure to global energy markets and supply chains.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] Oil prices rose more than \$3 at the market open on 14 September, following new strikes on [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) and on ships in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md), with [Brent crude](1150532.md) futures rising to \$108.23 a barrel.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29][^cite_note-france24-30]
