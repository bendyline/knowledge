The **Chinese Basketball Association** ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 中国男子篮球职业联赛; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 中國男子籃球職業聯賽; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Zhōngguó Nánzǐ Lánqiú Zhíyè Liánsài*; lit. 'Chinese Men's Basketball Professional League'), often abbreviated as the **CBA**, is the first-tier men's professional [basketball](3921.md) league in China.

Fans commonly know the league as the CBA, and this acronym is regularly used in Chinese. There is also a [Women's Chinese Basketball Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Chinese_Basketball_Association) (WCBA).

A few Chinese players who competed in the CBA in the early stages of their careers—including [Wang Zhizhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Zhizhi), [Mengke Bateer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mengke_Bateer), [Yao Ming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yao_Ming), [Yi Jianlian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yi_Jianlian), [Sun Yue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Yue_\(basketball\)), and [Zhou Qi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhou_Qi)—have also played in the [National Basketball Association](22093.md) (NBA). Others, such as [Xue Yuyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xue_Yuyang) and [Wang Zhelin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Zhelin), were chosen in the [draft](835946.md), but did not play in the NBA.

Only a limited number of foreign players are allowed on each CBA team. Notable imports include former NBA All-Stars [Stephon Marbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephon_Marbury), [Tracy McGrady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_McGrady), [Gilbert Arenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert_Arenas), [Steve Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Francis), [Metta World Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metta_World_Peace) and [Kenyon Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenyon_Martin)—as well as several NBA veterans who would become CBA All-Stars—[Michael Beasley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Beasley), [Aaron Brooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Brooks_\(basketball\)), [Jimmer Fredette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmer_Fredette), [Al Harrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Harrington), [Lester Hudson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_Hudson), [Randolph Morris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randolph_Morris), [Shavlik Randolph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shavlik_Randolph), [Jeremy Lin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Lin) and [J.R. Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._R._Smith).

## Background

The CBA began play in the [1995–96 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995%E2%80%9396_Chinese_Basketball_Association_season). The league should not be confused with the [Chinese Basketball Association (organisation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Basketball_Association_\(organisation\)), which was founded in June 1956[^cite_note-1] and represents the country in matters involving the sport's governing body, [FIBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA). Basketball in China is currently regulated by the **Chinese Basketball Management Center**.

Other Chinese basketball leagues include the [National Basketball League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Basketball_League_\(China\)) (NBL), the [Chinese University Basketball Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_Basketball_Association) (CUBA), and the [Chinese High School Basketball League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_High_School_Basketball_League?action=edit\&redlink=1) (CHBL).[^cite_note-2] At one time there was a league called the [Chinese New Basketball Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_New_Basketball_Alliance?action=edit\&redlink=1) (CNBA),[^cite_note-3] one of whose most prominent teams was the Beijing Sea Lions, but this venture lasted for just one winter (1996–97).[^cite_note-4]

The first non-Chinese player to compete in the CBA was Mihail Savinkov of [Uzbekistan](31853.md), who joined the [Zhejiang Squirrels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_Golden_Bulls) in the league's inaugural 1995–96 campaign.[^cite_note-5] During the [1996–97](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996%E2%80%9397_Chinese_Basketball_Association_season) season, [James Hodges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Hodges_\(basketball\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) became one of the first [Americans](19792942.md) to play in the CBA, and his signing by the [Liaoning Hunters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liaoning_Flying_Leopards) helped pave the way for many more imports from the [United States](3434750.md) to follow in the ensuing years.

Some other notable foreign pioneers included [John Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Spencer_\(basketball\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), who joined the [Jiangsu Dragons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiangsu_Dragons) later in the 1996–97 campaign, and [David Vanterpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Vanterpool), who inked a deal with the [Jilin Northeast Tigers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jilin_Northeast_Tigers) the following winter, who helped the team move up to the CBA in time for the [1998–99 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998%E2%80%9399_Chinese_Basketball_Association_season). The CBA's first international coach was American Robert Hoggard, who led the Sichuan Pandas for the last eight games of the 1997–98 campaign.[^cite_note-6]

In June 2026, the CBA announced it would resume promotion-relegation, with the [National Basketball League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Basketball_League_\(China\)) (NBL) acting as a second tier on the pyramid.[^cite_note-7]

## Team names

*For a full list of teams, see [Current clubs](#Current_clubs) section below. Also see [Category\:Chinese Basketball Association teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category\:Chinese_Basketball_Association_teams).*

The full name of each team usually consists of three parts, in the following order:

1. A geographic designation (except in the case of [Bayi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayi_Rockets), which technically translates into [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) as "August First", the day China's [People's Liberation Army](66890.md) was founded). All others are [province-level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_China#Provincial_level_\(1st\)) designations (either a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_China) or a [Chinese municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct-controlled_municipalities_of_China)).

2. A corporate sponsor name. This sponsor may change from year to year, and sometimes even in mid-season.

3. A nickname, such as the name of an animal.

The presence of corporate sponsor names can occasionally lead to confusion about what name to use in English because many variants may be seen. Team names are usually abbreviated (in [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language) or [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language)), so that either the corporate sponsor name or the nickname is used interchangeably (rarely both). In addition, team nicknames can sometimes be translated into English in more than one way, and corporate sponsors tend to change frequently over time.

Nickname changes are rare, but occasionally happen, such as when the [Shandong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_Golden_Stars) team switched from *Flaming Bulls* (1995) to *Lions* (2003) to *Gold Lions* (2004) to *Golden Stars* (2014). Other examples include the [Liaoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liaoning_Flying_Leopards) team dumping *Hunters* (1995) for *Dinosaurs* (2008) and then *Flying Leopards* (2011) -- as well as the [Foshan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangzhou_Long-Lions) team's evolving attempts to "Anglicize" its nickname—by going from *Kylins* (2001) to *Dralions* to *Long-Lions*.

In previous years, the title of the league itself was available for corporate naming sponsorship. In 1999–2000 and 2000–2001 it was known as the *[Hilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilton_Hotels_Corporation) League*, in 2001–2002 and 2002–2003 it was the *[Motorola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorola) League*, and in 2003–2004 it was sponsored by [China Unicom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Unicom). These corporate league titles were not always used in the news media, however, and this sponsorship practice was discontinued at the start of the 2004–2005 season.[^cite_note-8]

## Finals

In 2005, the league unveiled the Mou Zuoyun Cup ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 牟作云杯), which was awarded for the first time to the winning team in the CBA Finals. ## Awards

The [CBA Most Valuable Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBA_Most_Valuable_Player) award is presented to the league's best player in a given CBA season. Since the 2012–13 campaign, two awards have been handed out each year, Domestic MVP and International MVP. The rules for the selection of the MVP in the regular season are: the number of games must reach 28 or more; The comprehensive score of individual technical indicators ranks among the best; The team won the top three in the regular season.[^cite_note-11] At the conclusion of each season, the [CBA Finals MVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBA_Finals_Most_Valuable_Player) award is bestowed upon the most outstanding player in that year's championship series.

Each campaign's [scoring leader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Basketball_Association_scoring_leaders) is also recognized on an annual basis and the league maintains a list of single game, single season, and career [record holders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Basketball_Association_records) in various statistical categories. Furthermore, a CBA All-Star Game MVP award is given to the player deemed to have the most impactful performance in the league's annual mid-season exhibition contest.

The [CBA Best Defender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBA_Best_Defender) award is presented to the league's most hard-skilled and efficient defender in a given season of the CBA.

## Scoring leaders

Main article: [Chinese Basketball Association scoring leaders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Basketball_Association_scoring_leaders)

The CBA's highest single season scoring average, depending on how many games are required to be recognized as a statistical qualifier, is either 43.1 points per game by [Jordan Crawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Crawford), who played in 26 of Tianjin's 38 games (68.4%) in [2015–16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_CBA_season), or 42.0 points per game by [Jonathan Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Gibson_\(basketball\)), who played in 36 of Qingdao's 38 games (94.7%) in [2015–16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_CBA_season).

## Foreign imports

Listed below are some of the most accomplished foreign imports who have competed in the CBA. Players must appear in at least one game for the team to receive credit for a season. Someone who signs a contract but never steps on the court does not count.

As of early 2024, over 100 basketball professionals from the United States, Europe, and Australia work in the CBA.[^cite_note-\:02-22]

### Policy

Every team can register up to four foreign players per season. During the regular season, the policy of four (maximum) foreign players on the field for four quarters and four times per game (full of one per quarter) is adopted, while the team ranked in the bottom four last season may adopt the policy of four (maximum) foreign players for four quarters and five times per game (full of two times per quarter in the first three quarters and one time per quarter in the last quarter). During the playoffs, all teams will adopt a four-player (maximum) four-period four-times policy for foreign players (maximum of one per period)[^cite_note-23]

## Politics

On October 4, 2019, the [Houston Rockets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston_Rockets) general manager [Daryl Morey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daryl_Morey) issued a tweet in support of the [2019–20 Hong Kong protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Hong_Kong_protests).[^cite_note-24] Morey's tweet resulted in the Chinese Basketball Association's suspension of its relationship with the [Houston Rockets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston_Rockets) and [China Central Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Central_Television)'s removal of all NBA games from its broadcast schedule until further notice.[^cite_note-apnewsrocket-25]
