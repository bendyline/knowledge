**Majid Khademi** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): مجید خادمی; 6 September 1962 – 6 April 2026) was an Iranian [Brigadier General](206220.md) in the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC) who served as head of the [IRGC Intelligence Protection Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Protection_Organization_of_the_Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps) from 2022 and of the [IRGC Intelligence Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Organization_of_the_Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps) from 2025 until his assassination in an [Israeli](9282173.md) airstrike during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).

Khademi also appeared in media reports under the name Majid Hosseini; his exact legal name has not been publicly clarified.[^cite_note-BBC-1]

## Early life and education

Majid Khademi held dual PhDs, in national security and in strategic defense sciences.[^cite_note-phd-2][^cite_note-3]

## Career

Following the establishment of the [IRGC Intelligence Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Organization_of_the_Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps), Khademi was mentioned in some reports as a potential successor to [Hossein Taeb](24451534.md), who headed the organization at the time, a role with which Khademi was associated until at least 2014.

In May 2018, he was appointed head of the [Intelligence Protection Organization of the Ministry of Defence and Armed Forces Logistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Protection_Organization_of_the_Ministry_of_Defence_and_Armed_Forces_Logistics?action=edit\&redlink=1), replacing [Asghar Mirjafari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asghar_Mirjafari?action=edit\&redlink=1), and served in this position until 2022.[^cite_note-BBC-1][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

In June 2022, he was appointed head of the [IRGC Intelligence Protection Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Protection_Organization_of_the_Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps), with his induction ceremony held on 29 July 2022.[^cite_note-BBC-1][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-phd-2]

On 19 June 2025, Khademi was appointed head of the [IRGC Intelligence Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_Organization_of_the_Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps), succeeding [Mohammad Kazemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Kazemi), who was killed alongside other IRGC officers in Israeli airstrikes on 15 June during the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## Death

Khademi was killed in [Tehran](57654.md) in an Israeli airstrike on 6 April 2026, during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-c658-10] Supreme Leader [Mojtaba Khamenei](23324096.md) condemned the assassination, praising Khademi as "self-effacingly struggling on the path of God for decades in the fields of the nation's security, intelligence, and defense."[^cite_note-11]
