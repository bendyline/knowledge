**Peter Attia** (born March 19, 1973)[^cite_note-amazon1-1] is a Canadian-American author, licensed physician and former researcher known for his work in [longevity medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_management_medicine). He is the author of *[Outlive: The Science and Art of Longevity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlive:_The_Science_and_Art_of_Longevity).*

## Early life and education

Attia was born and raised in [Toronto](64646.md), Ontario, Canada. He is the child of Egyptian Christian immigrant parents.[^cite_note-Machell2023-2] He graduated from [Queen's University at Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) in 1996 with a Bachelor of Science in mechanical engineering and [applied mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematics). He then attended [Stanford University School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University_School_of_Medicine), graduating in 2001 with a [Doctor of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Medicine).[^cite_note-3]

## Career

From 2001 to 2006, Attia began a residency in [general surgery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_surgery) at the [Johns Hopkins Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_Hospital) in [Baltimore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore), Maryland.[^cite_note-4] He left his general surgery residency at Johns Hopkins in 2006 and spent two years as a research fellow at the [National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute) of the [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health), studying immunotherapy for melanoma.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

After dropping out of his residency program, Attia joined the consulting firm [McKinsey & Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McKinsey_&_Company) in the [Palo Alto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto,_California) office as a member of the Corporate Risk Practice and Healthcare Practice. In 2014, he founded a private clinic dedicated to [longevity medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longevity_medicine).[^cite_note-Martin2026-7] Attia also created the blog "The Eating Academy" (later "War on Insulin" and now peterattiamd.com) that mostly focuses on topics related to [nutrition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutrition), [physical activity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_activity), and [longevity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longevity). Subsequently, he launched the podcast *The Peter Attia Drive*, in which he interviews experts each week, covering topics such as longevity, metabolic health, and medical research.

In 2012, Attia co-founded the Nutrition Science Initiative (NuSI) with [Gary Taubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Taubes), with a primary focus on promoting nutrition research and tackling the growing health challenges linked to obesity, diabetes, and metabolic diseases.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] In 2013, Attia was a speaker at [TEDMED](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TEDMED), where he shared insights on longevity.[^cite_note-Oconnor2013-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] Attia has a chapter giving advice in [Tim Ferriss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Ferriss)'s book *[Tools of Titans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tools_of_Titans)*. He had a central role in *[Limitless with Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limitless_with_Chris_Hemsworth)*, a six-part documentary for [National Geographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Geographic) and [Disney+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disney+) starring [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) and directed by [Darren Aronofsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darren_Aronofsky). In episode 5, Attia told Hemsworth that he carried the high-risk [APOE4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APOE4) allele, which places him at elevated risk of [neurodegenerative aging of the Alzheimer's type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alzheimer's_disease).[^cite_note-GMA2022-13]

In 2017, he posted on his YouTube Channel, with his first set of videos being instructional exercise videos. It now has more than a million subscribers and focuses on longevity content.[^cite_note-14] Subsequently, in 2018, he launched the podcast *The Peter Attia Drive*.[^cite_note-wrap60min-15]

On March 28, 2023, Attia (with coauthor Bill Gifford) published his book [*Outlive: The Science & Art of Longevity*.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlive:_The_Science_and_Art_of_Longevity)[^cite_note-16] According to *[The New York Times Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Magazine)*, the book "has been a runaway best seller since it was published this spring \[of 2023]";[^cite_note-Marchese2023-17] it was No. 3 on the Amazon Charts for nonfiction as of August 24, 2023.[^cite_note-AmazonCharts-18][^cite_note-Marchese2023-17] Attia was named in *Time*'s 2024 list of influential people in health.[^cite_note-19] By October 2025, CBS News reported that *Outlive* had sold nearly three million copies and that *The Peter Attia Drive* had been downloaded more than 100 million times.[^cite_note-CBS2025-20] *The Guardian* called the book an "exhaustive, lucid exploration" of Attia's ideas while noting that the lifestyle changes it proposes could appear onerous and complicated.[^cite_note-21] *GQ* described it as exhaustive and clear, while observing that it raised more questions than it answered.[^cite_note-22]

In October 2025, Attia appeared on CBS's *60 Minutes*, where he discussed the role of structured exercise and preventative health strategies in extending health span.[^cite_note-23]

From January to February 2026, Attia was a contributor to [CBS News](359551.md).[^cite_note-24]

## Personal life

Attia currently lives in the [Austin, Texas](1998.md), area with his wife and three children.[^cite_note-25] In 2005, Attia swam across the channel between [Santa Catalina Island](21076784.md) and Los Angeles, the 120th person to achieve that feat.[^cite_note-26]

He trained for the crossing by swimming roughly 20 to 30 miles per week alongside a surgical residency that at times reached 100 hours per week, and drew on his undergraduate training in mechanical engineering to analyse the physics of his stroke.[^cite_note-LAT2005-27] He said at the time that the swim was intended in part to draw attention to the [Terry Fox Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Fox_Foundation)'s cancer-research work.[^cite_note-LAT2005-27] He has since completed the channel in both directions. Attia has also boxed, cycled competitively and competes in archery.[^cite_note-28]

## Controversies

In 2012, Attia co-founded the Nutrition Science Initiative (NuSI) with science journalist [Gary Taubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Taubes) to fund research into the carbohydrate–insulin model of obesity. The organization received a funding commitment of about \$40 million from the [Laura and John Arnold Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Ventures).[^cite_note-29] Attia stepped down as president in 2015. NuSI later lost its research partners following disagreements over study design and the interpretation of pilot results, lost its principal funding, and largely ceased operations by 2018.[^cite_note-30]

Attia was introduced to [Jeffrey Epstein](6253522.md) in 2015 through [Eva Andersson-Dubin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Andersson-Dubin).[^cite_note-PoliticoArticleMarch2026-31] Epstein's appointment calendars, released in 2025, recorded several meetings involving Attia.[^cite_note-32] Emails released in connection with the [Epstein files](80415168.md) documented communications between Attia and Epstein concerning patient referrals, accommodation, a hiring decision at Attia's clinic, and health and longevity.[^cite_note-PoliticoArticleMarch2026-31] According to *[Bloomberg](3057255.md)*, the last meeting between the two documented in the emails took place on September 28, 2018, while Attia was in New York for a cancer meeting he had invited Epstein to attend.[^cite_note-Bloomberg2026-33]

On February 3, 2026, David founder Peter Rahal announced that Attia had stepped down as the company's chief science officer, and Attia's name and image were removed from the company's website.[^cite_note-34] Biograph, the testing startup Attia co-founded, declined to comment on his status.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37] Attia also publicly apologized for the content of some of his emails with Epstein.[^cite_note-38]

### Oura litigation

Attia began working with [Oura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oura_Health) in 2017 as an adviser and promoter of its smart ring, and invested in the company in 2018.[^cite_note-TE2026-39][^cite_note-NDCal2024-40] In February 2019, he entered into a stock option agreement signed by then-CEO Harpreet Rai. According to Attia's court filings, it gave him the right to acquire 20,000 shares at €1.76 per share in exchange for advisory and promotional services.[^cite_note-TE2026-39] After Rai's departure in December 2021, Attia sought to exercise the options in January 2022. Oura declined to honor the agreement, and Attia filed a breach-of-contract suit in the [Northern District of California](891692.md) in 2023.[^cite_note-TE2026-39][^cite_note-NDCal2024-40]

Oura has argued that its board never authorized the grant and that the agreement is invalid, citing the Finnish Companies Act, under which option rights are issued by the shareholders' general meeting or an authorized board rather than the CEO.[^cite_note-TE2026-39] The company sought to move the dispute to arbitration in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki) under the shareholders' agreement Attia had accepted as an investor. The district court denied the motion in 2024, finding that Attia's investment and advisory agreements were separate. The [Ninth Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Court_of_Appeals_for_the_Ninth_Circuit) affirmed on 21 March 2025, and the case has since proceeded in federal court in [Oakland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California).[^cite_note-NDCal2024-40][^cite_note-41][^cite_note-\:1-42]

Attia's suit is one of three similar claims over options allegedly promised by Rai. The others were brought by former NFL quarterback [Drew Brees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_Brees), whose case was sent to arbitration in 2025, and early investor Gurinder "Gordy" Bal.[^cite_note-TE20262-43][^cite_note-\:1-42]
