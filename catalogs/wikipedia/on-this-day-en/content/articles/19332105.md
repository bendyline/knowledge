**Germasogeia** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Γερμασόγεια), is an eastern suburb of [Limassol](57577.md) and a [municipal district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_district) of the [Amathounta Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amathounta) in the [Limassol District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limassol_District) of [Cyprus](5593.md). As of 2021, Germasogeia had a population of 17,325.[^cite_note-cystat.gov.cy-2] Its mayor, Kyriakos Xidias, won the local elections on 18 December 2016 as an independent candidate. During the elections, Xidias prevailed with 26.73% of the vote against 23.01% of two-term incumbent Andreas Gavrielides.[^cite_note-elections-3]

The small town was fortified in the medieval period by the Knights Templar. Germasogeia dam was built there in 1968.

## Etymology

The name Germasogeia derives from the words *hieros* ("sacred") and *mesogeia* ("midlands" or "inland"). The term *hieros* refers to the many temples that once existed in the area, while *mesogeia* reflects the settlement's relatively close proximity to the sea.[^cite_note-\:1-4][^cite_note-\:0-5]
