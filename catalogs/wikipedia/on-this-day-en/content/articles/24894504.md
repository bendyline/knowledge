**Maru** is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Zamfara State](2227664.md), [Nigeria](21383.md).

It has an area of 6,654 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 291,900 at the 2006 census.

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 890.[^cite_note-1]

## Climate

The dry season is mostly cloudy during the year, while the wet season is oppressive and mostly cloudy.[^cite_note-2]

Maru's temperature is rising due to climate change; the graph shows warmer and colder years.[^cite_note-3]
