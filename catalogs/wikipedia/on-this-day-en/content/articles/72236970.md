The **men's 1500 metre freestyle** event at the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics) was held from 3 to 4 August 2024 at [Paris La Défense Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_La_D%C3%A9fense_Arena), which was converted to a swimming pool for the swimming events.

The [US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)' [Bobby Finke](54696773.md) was considered the most likely to win the event, with other medal contenders including [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [Daniel Wiffen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Wiffen), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [Gregorio Paltrinieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorio_Paltrinieri), [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [Samuel Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Short), [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [Mykhailo Romanchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mykhailo_Romanchuk) and [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [Florian Wellbrock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florian_Wellbrock). Romanchuk withdrew from the event due to illness.

In the final, Finke led from beginning to end to win with a new [world record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_world_records_in_swimming) of 14\:30.67 for the only US men's swimming gold at these games. Paltrinieri swam a time of 14\:34.55 for silver, and Wiffen won bronze with a time of 14\:39.63. Also in the final, [Dávid Betlehem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A1vid_Betlehem) lowered [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s national record from the 14\:45.59 he achieved in the heats to 14\:40.91, [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [Kuzey Tunçelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuzey_Tun%C3%A7elli) swam 14\:41.22 to break the [world junior record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_junior_world_records_in_swimming) and his country's [national record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Turkish_records_in_swimming), and [David Aubry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Aubry) lowered [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [national record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_French_records_in_swimming) to 14\:44.66.

## Background

The [US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)' [Bobby Finke](54696773.md) won the event at the previous Olympics, won silver [at the 2023 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2023_World_Aquatics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_1500_metre_freestyle) and had the fastest qualification time of 14\:31.59.[^cite_note-\:0-1] Tunisian [Ahmed Hafnaoui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Hafnaoui), the 2023 Championships winner, withdrew from the Olympics due to an undisclosed injury.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-1]

[Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [Daniel Wiffen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Wiffen) won the event [at the 2024 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2024_World_Aquatics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_1500_metre_freestyle) in Finke's absence with the second fastest qualifying time of 14\:34.07.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-\:0-1] [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [Gregorio Paltrinieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorio_Paltrinieri) won the event [at the 2022 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2022_World_Aquatics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_1500_metre_freestyle), but withdrew from it at the 2023 Championships due to what *[SwimSwam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SwimSwam)* called "physical problems".[^cite_note-\:0-1] Other medal contenders included [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s 2023 World Championships bronze medallist [Samuel Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Short), defending Olympic silver medallist [Mykhailo Romanchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mykhailo_Romanchuk) from [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics), and defending Olympic bronze medallist [Florian Wellbrock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florian_Wellbrock) from [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-\:0-1]

Both *SwimSwam* and *[Swimming World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_World)* predicted Finke would win gold and Wiffen would win silver. *Swimming World* predicted Short would win bronze, while *SwimSwam* predicted Paltrinieri would take it.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-5]

Prior to the event, the [world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_world_records_in_swimming) and [Olympic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Olympic_records_in_swimming) records were 14\:31.02, set by [Sun Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Yang) of [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_at_the_2012_Summer_Olympics) in [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2012_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_1500_metre_freestyle).[^cite_note-\:0-1]

The event was held at [Paris La Défense Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_La_D%C3%A9fense_Arena), which was converted to a swimming pool for the swimming events.[^cite_note-6]

## Qualification

Further information: [Swimming at the 2024 Summer Olympics – Qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Qualification)

Each [National Olympic Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Olympic_Committee) (NOC) was permitted to enter a maximum of two qualified athletes in each individual event, but only if both of them had attained the Olympic Qualifying Time (OQT).[^cite_note-swimming_qual_standards2-7] For this event, the OQT was 15\:00.99. [World Aquatics](317029.md) then considered athletes qualifying through universality; NOCs were given one event entry for each gender, which could be used by any athlete regardless of qualification time, providing the spaces had not already been taken by athletes from that nation who had achieved the OQT.[^cite_note-swimming_qual_standards2-7][^cite_note-\:1-8] Finally, the rest of the spaces were filled by athletes who had met the Olympic Consideration Time (OCT), which was 15\:05.49 for this event.[^cite_note-swimming_qual_standards2-7] In total, 24 athletes qualified through achieving the OQT, two athletes qualified through universality places and two athletes qualified through achieving the OCT.[^cite_note-\:1-8]

## Heats

Four heats (preliminary rounds) took place on 3 August 2024, starting at 11\:30.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-\:53-10] The swimmers with the best eight times in the heats advanced to the final.[^cite_note-\:53-10] Wiffen qualified with the fastest time of 14\:40.34, which was two seconds faster than anyone else, while Paltrinieri qualified with the second fastest time of 14\:42.56.[^cite_note-\:2-11] Hungary's [Dávid Betlehem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A1vid_Betlehem) broke his country's national record with a time of 14\:45.59.[^cite_note-12] [Tunisia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisia_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [Ahmed Jaouadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Jaouadi), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [David Aubry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Aubry), [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics)'s [Kuzey Tunçelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuzey_Tun%C3%A7elli), France's [Damien Joly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Joly), [Dávid Betlehem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A1vid_Betlehem) of [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics), and Finke also qualified.[^cite_note-\:2-11] Romanchuk withdrew from the race due to illness.[^cite_note-13]

## Final

The final took place at 18\:37 on 4 August.[^cite_note-\:532-14] Finke led the race from beginning to end to win with a new [world record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_world_records_in_swimming) of 14\:30.67,[^cite_note-\:4-15] which beat [Sun Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Yang)'s previous world record of 14\:31.02 [from the 2012 Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2012_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_1500_metre_freestyle).[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] Paltrinieri finished almost four seconds later with 14\:34.55 to win silver, and Wiffen finished with 14\:39.63 and won bronze.[^cite_note-\:4-15][^cite_note-18]

Betlehem finished fourth with a time of 14\:40.91, which beat and lowered his [national record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Hungarian_records_in_swimming) set in the heats by 4.68 seconds.[^cite_note-\:5-19] [Tunçelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuzey_Tun%C3%A7elli) finished fifth with a new [world junior record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_junior_world_records_in_swimming) and [national record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Turkish_records_in_swimming) of 14\:41.22, which lowered those records, which he already held, by 0.67.[^cite_note-20] [David Aubry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Aubry) also lowered France's [national record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_French_records_in_swimming), swimming 14\:44.66 to finish seventh.[^cite_note-\:5-19]

*SwimSwam* noted that Finke had changed his strategy away from swimming faster nearing the end of the race to swimming a more consistent pace the whole way through,[^cite_note-21] and they later called his race the "swim of the meet".[^cite_note-22] Finke's win also moved the US to the top of the [swimming medal table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics#Medal_summary),[^cite_note-23] where they stayed for the remaining day of the swimming events.[^cite_note-24] His win won him the US' only male individual swimming gold of the 2024 Games.[^cite_note-25]
