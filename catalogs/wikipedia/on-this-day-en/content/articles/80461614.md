The **2026 FA Cup final** was a [football](10568.md) match played at [Wembley Stadium](8913012.md) in [London](17867.md), England, on 16 May 2026 between [Chelsea](7473.md) and [Manchester City](165813.md) to determine the winners of the [2025–26 FA Cup](79604093.md). It was the [145th final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FA_Cup_finals) of English football's primary cup competition, the [Football Association Challenge Cup](11237.md).

Chelsea played in their 17th FA Cup final and the first since [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FA_Cup_final), when they lost for a record third consecutive time.[^cite_note-semi-final1-report-3] They were seeking a ninth Cup title. Manchester City played in their 15th FA Cup final overall and the fourth consecutively, the first ever team to do so.[^cite_note-semi-final2-report-4] They were seeking an eighth Cup title.

The clubs' most recent meeting in a title-deciding match was the [2021 UEFA Champions League final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_UEFA_Champions_League_final), which Chelsea won 1–0. The two sides have never before met in an FA Cup final, but have clashed in other domestic title-deciding matches, most recently in the [2019 EFL Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_EFL_Cup_final) and the [2018 FA Community Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FA_Community_Shield), both of which City won. They met twice in the [season's Premier League](77711894.md), a 1–1 draw in Manchester followed by City's 3–0 win in London.

Manchester City won 1–0 in the match, through a goal from [Antoine Semenyo](57382651.md), achieving their 8th FA Cup title.[^cite_note-5]

As a Premier League team, [Chelsea](7473.md) entered the tournament in the third round, beginning their FA Cup campaign with a dominant 5–1 away victory over Championship side [Charlton Athletic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlton_Athletic_F.C.), in what was [Liam Rosenior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Rosenior)'s first match in charge of the club. Goals came from [Tosin Adarabioyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tosin_Adarabioyo), [Marc Guiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Guiu), [Pedro Neto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Neto), [Enzo Fernández](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enzo_Fern%C3%A1ndez), and [Jorrel Hato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorrel_Hato), the latter scoring his first for Chelsea.[^cite_note-6] Pedro Neto notched his first Chelsea hat-trick in the fourth round away to [Hull City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hull_City_F.C.). [Estêvão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Est%C3%AAv%C3%A3o_Willian) also contributed to the scoring, the match finishing 4–0 to the Blues.[^cite_note-7] In the fifth round, Chelsea were drawn away yet again, this time against Championship club [Wrexham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrexham_A.F.C.), who proved to be much tougher opposition than those in previous rounds. [Sam Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Smith_\(footballer,_born_1998\)) and [Callum Doyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Doyle) got their names on the scoresheet for the Welsh club, with a late [Josh Acheampong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Acheampong) goal forcing extra time. [Alejandro Garnacho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Garnacho) and [João Pedro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Pedro_\(footballer,_born_2001\)) both scored in extra time to secure a 4–2 win for Chelsea.[^cite_note-8]

In the quarter-finals, Chelsea were drawn against tournament underdogs [Port Vale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Vale_F.C.). Chelsea made light work of the League One side, defeating them 7–0 at [Stamford Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stamford_Bridge_\(stadium\)), their largest FA Cup victory since [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010%E2%80%9311_FA_Cup), where Chelsea eased past [Ipswich Town](352843.md) by the same scoreline.[^cite_note-9] Jorrel Hato, João Pedro, Tosin Adarabioyo, [Andrey Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Santos), Estêvão, and Alejandro Garnacho all found the net during the match, along with an own goal from Port Vale defender [Jordan Lawrence-Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Lawrence-Gabriel).[^cite_note-10] Chelsea faced Premier League rivals [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.) in the semi-finals at Wembley, where a lone goal from Enzo Fernández was enough to secure a 1–0 victory and send the Blues to the FA Cup final. Chelsea is seeking their first FA Cup victory since beating [Manchester United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_United_F.C.) 1–0 in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FA_Cup_final).[^cite_note-11]

As a Premier League team, [Manchester City](165813.md) entered the tournament in the third round. City began their FA Cup campaign with a rampant 10–1 display at home against League One side [Exeter City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_City_F.C.). Manchester City's ten goals came from seven different players and two own goals, with [Rico Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rico_Lewis) scoring two goals, and [Jake Doyle-Hayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Doyle-Hayes) and [Jack Fitzwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Fitzwater) each scoring an own goal. [Max Alleyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Alleyne), [Rodri](48849865.md), [Antoine Semenyo](57382651.md), [Tijjani Reijnders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tijjani_Reijnders), [Nico O'Reilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_O'Reilly), and [Ryan McAidoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_McAidoo) all scored for the home team, with [George Birch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Birch_\(footballer\)) scoring a consolation for Exeter.[^cite_note-12] This was their biggest win since defeating [Huddersfield Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huddersfield_Town_A.F.C.) by the same score in 1987. City next faced [Salford City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salford_City_F.C.) at home in the fourth round, and a goal from [Marc Guéhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Gu%C3%A9hi) and an own goal from [Alfie Dorrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfie_Dorrington) helped overcome the League Two side to reach the fifth round, winning 2–0.[^cite_note-13] In the fifth round, City defeated fellow Premier League side [Newcastle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United_F.C.) 3–1 at [St James' Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_James'_Park). Despite an opener from Newcastle's [Harvey Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Barnes), City came back due to two goals by [Omar Marmoush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Marmoush), and a goal from [Savinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savinho).[^cite_note-14]

In the quarter-finals, Manchester City were drawn against eight-time cup winners [Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.), facing them at home. An [Erling Haaland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erling_Haaland) hat-trick and a goal from Antoine Semenyo helped City run out 4–0 victors, easing into their eighth FA Cup semi-final in a row.[^cite_note-15] Despite a 79th minute goal from [Finn Azaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finn_Azaz), City defeated Championship side [Southampton](81576.md) 2–1 in the semi-finals at Wembley, as City mounted a late comeback through goals from [Jérémy Doku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%A9r%C3%A9my_Doku) and [Nico González](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_Gonz%C3%A1lez_\(footballer,_born_2002\)). This marked the first time any team has reached four consecutive FA Cup finals, having previously played in the [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_FA_Cup_final), [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_FA_Cup_final) and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_FA_Cup_final) finals respectively.[^cite_note-semi-final1-report-3]

## Match

### Summary

In the 72nd minute, Manchester City scored the only goal of the match when [Antoine Semenyo](57382651.md) back flicked the ball with his right leg into the left corner of the net after a low cross from the right from [Erling Haaland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erling_Haaland).[^cite_note-16] It was the only goal of the game as Manchester City won the game 1–0 and completed the domestic cup double after also winning the [2026 EFL Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_EFL_Cup_final) in March.[^cite_note-17]
