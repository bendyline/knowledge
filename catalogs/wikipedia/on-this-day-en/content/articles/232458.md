**Rizal**, officially the **Province of Rizal** ([Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language): *Lalawigan ng Rizal*), is a [province](165100.md) in the [Philippines](23440.md) located in the [Calabarzon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calabarzon) region in [Luzon](69375.md). Its capital is the city of [Antipolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipolo). It is about 16 kilometers (9.9 mi) east of [Manila](184334.md). The province is named after [José Rizal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Rizal), one of the main national heroes of the Philippines. It is bordered by [Metro Manila](72340.md) to the west, [Bulacan](252408.md) to the north, [Quezon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon) to the east and [Laguna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_province) to the southeast. The province also lies on the northern shores of [Laguna de Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_de_Bay), the largest lake in the country. Rizal is a mountainous province perched on the western slopes of the southern portion of the [Sierra Madre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Madre_\(Philippines\)) mountain range.

[Antipolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipolo) serves as the provincial capital since 2020, having been an administrative center since 2009 with the capitol located in the city. Previously, [Pasig](359702.md) served as the capital, a designation it retained even after becoming part of the [National Capital Region](72340.md) in 1975.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

The province is a part of [Greater Manila Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Manila_Area). It is the 5th largest province in the Philippines in terms of population.

## History

Further information: [Morong (district)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morong_\(district\)) and [Manila (province)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_\(province\))

### Early history

[Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_people) settlement arrived sometime in the pre-Spanish period. The provincial territory began with the organization of the [Tondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_\(province\)) and [Laguna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_\(province\)) provinces during the Spanish administration. Some of the towns like [Pasig](359702.md), [Parañaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para%C3%B1aque), [Taytay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taytay,_Rizal) and [Cainta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cainta) were already thriving.

### Spanish colonial era

From the reports of the *[Encomiendas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encomienda)* in 1582–1583, the Encomiendas of *Moron* ([Morong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morong,_Rizal)) was under the jurisdiction of [La Laguna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_\(province\)) and, the Encomiendas of *Passi* (Pasig), *Taitay* (Taytay) and *Tagui* (Taguig) belonged to the Province of Tondo. It was recorded that in 1591, the Encomiendas of Moron and Taitay were under the jurisdiction of the [Franciscan Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franciscan_Order) in the Province of La Laguna; and the Encomiendas of *Nabotas* (Navotas), *Tambobo* (Malabon), Tondo, Parañaque (then [La Huerta, Parañaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Huerta,_Para%C3%B1aque)), *Longalo* ([Don Galo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Galo), Parañaque), Tagui and Pasig were under the jurisdiction of the [Augustinians](144980.md) in the Province of Tondo.



[Paul de la Gironière](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_de_la_Gironi%C3%A8re) established the [Jala Jala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalajala,_Rizal) [hacienda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacienda) in [Morong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morong,_Rizal).

In 1853, a new political subdivision named *Distrito Politico-Militar de los Montes de San Mateo* (transl. Political-Military District of the San Mateo Mountains) was formed.[^cite_note-rizalhistory1-7] This consisted of the towns of [Antipolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipolo), Bosoboso, Cainta and Taytay from the Province of Tondo, and the towns of [Morong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morong,_Rizal), [Baras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baras,_Rizal), [Tanay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanay,_Rizal), [Pililla](2080489.md), [Angono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angono,_Rizal), [Binangonan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binangonan,_Rizal) and [Jala-jala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalajala,_Rizal) from the Province of La Laguna, with Morong as the capital. This district was changed to *[Distrito Politico-Militar de Morong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morong_\(district\))* four years later.[^cite_note-8]

By virtue of Circular No. 83, dated September 2, 1859, the Province of Tondo became the [Province of Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_\(province\)). All its towns were placed under the administration, fiscal supervision and control of the Governor of the new province.

The town of Mariquina ([Marikina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marikina)) became the capital of the Province of Manila during the tenure of the revolutionary government of Gen. [Emilio Aguinaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilio_Aguinaldo).[^cite_note-9] The Politico-Military District of Morong had for its capital the town of Antipolo from 1898 to 1899 and the town of Tanay from 1899 to 1900.

### American colonial era

#### Foundation



1899 map of south-central Luzon with [Manila province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Manila) and [Morong district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morong_\(district\))

On February 6, the [First Philippine Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Philippine_Commission) sought to establish [civil government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_government) in the country through a provincial organization act after the Filipino-Spanish and Filipino-American conflicts.

Therefore, on June 5, 1901, a historic meeting was held at the [Pasig Catholic Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasig_Cathedral) for the organization of a civil government in the Province of Manila and District of Morong, with 221 delegates in attendance.[^cite_note-10] The first Philippine Commission, headed by [William Howard Taft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Howard_Taft) and composed of Commissioners [Luke E. Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_E._Wright), [Henry C. Ide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_C._Ide), [Bernard Moses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_Moses) and [Dean C. Worcester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_Conant_Worcester), discussed with the Assembly the issue of whether or not to write the Province of Manila with the District of Morong, was not self-sufficient to operate as a separate province.

Although the delegates from Morong, Hilarion Raymundo, and José Tupas, objected to the proposal, [Juan Sumulong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Sumulong) of Antipolo strongly advocated the move. After much acrimonious debate and upon the suggestion of [Trinidad H. Pardo de Tavera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidad_H._Pardo_de_Tavera) the body agreed on the creation of a new province independent of the Province of Manila. The new province was aptly named after [José Rizal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Rizal), the country's national hero.



The ruins of the first provincial capitol in barangay Santa Rosa, [Pasig](359702.md), which was the seat of government for the province until 1950.

[^cite_note-11]

On June 11, 1901, the province of Rizal was officially and legally created by virtue of Act No. 137 by the First Philippine Commission which during the time was acting as the unicameral legislative body in the island of Luzon.[^cite_note-12]

The new province was composed of 32 municipalities, 19 from the old [Province of Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_\(province\)) (i.e. [Cainta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cainta), [Caloocan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caloocan), [Las Piñas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Pi%C3%B1as), Malibay, [Mariquina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marikina) (Marikina), [Montalban](335181.md) (Rodriguez), [Muntinlupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muntinlupa), [Navotas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navotas), [Novaliches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novaliches), [Parañaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para%C3%B1aque), [Pasig](359702.md), [Pateros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pateros), [Pineda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasay) (Pasay), [San Felipe Neri](359686.md) (Mandaluyong), [San Juan del Monte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Juan,_Metro_Manila) (San Juan), [San Mateo](892217.md), [San Pedro Macati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makati) (Makati), [Taguig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taguig), [Tambobong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malabon) (Malabon)); and 13 from the Politico-Military District of Morong (i.e. [Angono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angono), [Antipolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipolo), [Baras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baras,_Rizal), [Binangonan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binangonan), Bosoboso, [Cardona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardona,_Rizal), [Jalajala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalajala), [Morong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morong,_Rizal), [Pililla](2080489.md), Quisao, [Tanay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanay,_Rizal), [Taytay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taytay,_Rizal) and [Teresa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teresa,_Rizal)).[^cite_note-act942-13] The [City of Manila](184334.md) from the old Province of Manila was treated as a separate entity. The seat of the provincial government was Pasig; however, for seven months in 1904, San Felipe Neri served as the provincial seat.[^cite_note-14]

The number of municipalities changed with the municipal boundaries through time, mostly occurring within the provincial boundary.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] On October 12, 1903, the former municipalities of Bosoboso, Malibay, [Novaliches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novaliches) and Quisao were absorbed by Antipolo, Pasay, Caloocan and Pililla, respectively, by virtue of Act No. 942.[^cite_note-act942-13] On November 25, 1903, Muntinlupa was ceded to the Province of [La Laguna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_\(province\)) and became part of the municipality of [Biñan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bi%C3%B1an),[^cite_note-19] but was later returned to Rizal on March 22, 1905, and became part of Taguig until December 17, 1917.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-history_munti-21]



Political map of Rizal before the creation of Quezon City

On October 12, 1939, [Quezon City](284223.md) was established,[^cite_note-22] which included parts of Caloocan, and later on, Novaliches and parts of Marikina, Pasig and San Juan.

### Japanese occupation

[Marking's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marking_Guerrillas) and the Hunter's ROTC Guerrillas operated in Rizal throughout the war.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

In 1942, [Quezon City](284223.md) and the towns of [Caloocan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caloocan), [Makati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makati), [Mandaluyong](359686.md), [Parañaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para%C3%B1aque), [Pasay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasay), and [San Juan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Juan,_Metro_Manila) were merged with [Manila](184334.md) to form the [City of Greater Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Greater_Manila), by virtue of Executive Order No. 400 signed by President [Manuel L. Quezon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_L._Quezon) as an emergency measure.[^cite_note-25] The city was dissolved by President [Sergio Osmeña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Osme%C3%B1a) in 1945, thus restoring the pre-war status of the merged cities and towns.[^cite_note-eo58-26][^cite_note-emb-27]

### Philippine independence



The [second provincial capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rizal_Provincial_Capitol_\(1962\)) along [Shaw Boulevard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaw_Boulevard), [Pasig](359702.md) was the seat of government for the province from 1962 to 2009.

[^cite_note-28]

&#x20;It was demolished in 2010 and the site was rebuilt as [Capitol Commons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Commons).

Through Presidential Decree No. 824, Rizal was [partitioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_\(politics\)) on November 7, 1975 to form [Metropolitan Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Manila). The municipalities of [Las Piñas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Pi%C3%B1as), [Parañaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para%C3%B1aque), [Muntinlupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muntinlupa), [Taguig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taguig), [Pateros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pateros), [Makati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makati), [Mandaluyong](359686.md), [San Juan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Juan,_Metro_Manila), [Malabon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malabon), [Navotas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navotas), [Pasig](359702.md) and [Marikina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marikina), and the three cities of [Caloocan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caloocan), [Pasay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasay) and [Quezon City](284223.md) were excised to form the new region, while the other 14 towns remained in Rizal.[^cite_note-LawPhilNet-PD824-29]

President [Ferdinand Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Marcos) had issued Proclamation No. 1283 in June 1974 that declared thousands of hectares of the province's watershed reservation as a townsite reservation, and by April 1977, he established the "Lungsod Silangan" townsite with Proclamation No. 1637 that expanded the area coverage to more than 20,000 hectares.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-Crisostomo-31] Intended to capture the spillover of industrial growth in Metro Manila, the Lungsod Silangan area covered portions of the municipalities of Antipolo, San Mateo, and Montalban along the [Marikina–Infanta Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marikina%E2%80%93Infanta_Highway), and was part of Imelda Marcos' "humanist" program for Metro Manila to serve as "the nucleus from which all humanist progress and development shall radiate towards the rest of the metropolis and the country."[^cite_note-Crisostomo-31]

### Contemporary

On June 17, 2008, Governor [Casimiro Ynares III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casimiro_Ynares_III) announced the transfer of the provincial capitol from [Pasig](359702.md) to [Antipolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipolo). The new [₱](227672.md)270-million capitol building, constructed by Ortigas & Co., owner thereof, was completed by December of that year. Built on a 5-hectare (12-acre) lot near the [Ynares Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ynares_Center),[^cite_note-new_capital-32] it was inaugurated on March 4, 2009, bringing back the Capitol Building inside the provincial territory from which it was absent for 34 years (when Pasig was incorporated into [Metro Manila](72340.md)).

On June 19, 2020, President [Rodrigo Duterte](7407293.md) signed Republic Act No. 11475 into law, which officially transferred the provincial capital of Rizal from [Pasig](359702.md) to [Antipolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipolo). The law was published on June 22, 2020, and took effect on July 7, 2020. The publication of the law coincided with the 159th birth anniversary of Dr. [Jose Rizal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jose_Rizal).

## Geography

Rizal covers a total area of 1,182.65 square kilometers (456.62 sq mi)[^cite_note-nscb-33] occupying the northern-central section of the [Calabarzon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calabarzon) in [Luzon](69375.md). The province is bordered on the north by [Bulacan](252408.md), east by [Quezon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quezon), southeast by [Laguna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_\(province\)), south by [Laguna de Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_de_Bay), and west by [Metro Manila](72340.md).

Located 20 kilometers (12 mi) east of Manila, commuters take approximately an hour to reach the provincial seat which is in [Antipolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipolo). Generally hilly and mountainous in terrain, most of the province's southern towns lie in the shores of [Laguna de Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_de_Bay), the country's largest inland body of water.[^cite_note-nscblist-1]

[Talim Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talim_Island), the largest island situated within [Laguna de Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laguna_de_Bay), is under the jurisdiction of the province and shared by the municipalities of Binangonan and Cardona.

### Languages

Due to its location being in the heart of the Katagalugan, almost all of the residents of Rizal mainly speak [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language). [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language_in_the_Philippines) and [Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language) are used as second languages respectively; Filipino is a version of Tagalog which is spoken by residents of Rizal in code switching & when speaking to [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language) speakers of other dialects. Being bordered by [Metro Manila](72340.md) (whose majority of its cities were once part of Rizal), a large number of people from farther provinces have migrated to the province, resulting in minor but significant usage of the [Bicolano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bikol_languages), [Cebuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_language), various [Cordilleran languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordilleran_languages), [Cuyunon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuyonon_language), [Ilocano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_language), [Hiligaynon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiligaynon_language), [Kapampangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapampangan_language), [Karay-a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karay-a_language), [Pangasinan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangasinan_language) and [Waray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waray_language), as well as various [Lumad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumad) (indigenous languages in Mindanao) languages, [Iranun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranun_language), [Maranao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maranao_language), [Maguindanaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_language) and [Tausug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tausug_language) languages.

### Religion

Main article: [Religion in the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion_in_the_Philippines)

#### Catholicism

[Roman Catholicism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church_in_the_Philippines) is the predominant religion with about 80 percent adherence.

#### Iglesia Ni Cristo

[Iglesia Ni Cristo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iglesia_Ni_Cristo) is a religious minority in Rizal, it has numerous chapels scattered around its municipalities. Subdivided in 2 Ecclesiastical Districts, its members comprises the 5% of the province's population.

#### Others

Various [Christian](8276776.md) groups exist such as [Members Church of God International (MCGI)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Members_Church_of_God_International),[^cite_note-43] Oneness Apostolic or [Pentecostal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentecostalism) like UPC, ALJC and ACJC, [Iglesia Filipina Independiente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iglesia_Filipina_Independiente), [Born-again Christians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born-again_Christian), [Jesus Is Lord Church Worldwide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_Is_Lord_Church_Worldwide), [Jehovah's Witnesses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jehovah's_Witnesses), [Baptist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptists), [Church of Christ of Latter Day Saints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Christ_of_Latter_Day_Saints), [El Shaddai (movement)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Shaddai_\(movement\)) [Methodists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodists), [Presbyterians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyterians), [Seventh-day Adventist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seventh-day_Adventist) and other [Evangelical Christians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelical_Christians). [Muslims](19541.md), [Anitists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dambana), animists, and atheists are also present in the province.

Before the 1990s, the primary source of economy in Rizal province were the huge piggery estates owned by Manila-based families. In recent years, the province became one of the most progressive provinces in the country, owing to its proximity to [Metro Manila](72340.md), the economic center of the [Philippines](23440.md). [Antipolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipolo), [Taytay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taytay,_Rizal) and [Cainta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cainta) serve as the economic centers of the province, while [Angono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angono), [Rodriguez](335181.md), [Morong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morong,_Rizal), [San Mateo](892217.md), [Tanay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanay,_Rizal), [Binangonan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binangonan) and [Teresa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teresa) are taking successful steps to urbanize areas within their jurisdiction.  Other areas of the province are having difficulty to start the urbanization process, mainly because of the lack of main roads to connect these to economic centers.

In a study recently conducted by the National Statistics Coordination Board (NSCB), Rizal province came out to be the Philippines' least poor province with a poverty incidence rate of 3.4%, even lower than that of the National Capital Region or Metro Manila. . On April 23, 2013, the National Statistics Coordination Board (NCSB) reported that Rizal, from being the least poor province in poverty incidence moved down to the 3rd Place, with Cavite taking over as the least province by 4.1% (compared to Rizal's 7.6%) and Laguna for 2nd with 6.3%.[^cite_note-Poverty_incidence_increased-53]

Antipolo, the province's capital city, is the center of trade and exchange, tourism, government, and economy. It is also a center of education and sports because of the availability of various educational and physical training facilities. Acclaimed of its scenic attractions, the city also produces agricultural products such as cashew nuts and rice cakes. Taytay, the province's center of garment and textile manufacturing, is also the town where the country's largest mall operator runs a store near the town center. Meanwhile, Cainta serves as the center of business-process outsourcing (BPO) businesses in the province, aside from being known for the presence of several shopping centers and delicacies such as bibingka or rice cakes.

- [Licerio Gerónimo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licerio_Ger%C3%B3nimo) (1855–1924) – Philippine Revolutionary General ([Rodriguez](335181.md))
