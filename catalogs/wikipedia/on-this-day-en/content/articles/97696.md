**Iberville Parish** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Paroisse d'Iberville*) is a [parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_parishes_in_Louisiana) located south of [Baton Rouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baton_Rouge) in the [U.S. state](18618239.md) of [Louisiana](18130.md), formed in 1807.[^cite_note-1] The parish seat is [Plaquemine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaquemine,_Louisiana).[^cite_note-GR6-2] The population was 30,241 at the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census).[^cite_note-\:1-3]&#x20;

## History

The parish is named for [Pierre Le Moyne d'Iberville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Le_Moyne_d%27Iberville), who founded the French [colony of Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_\(New_France\)).[^cite_note-4]&#x20;

A few archaeological efforts have been made in the parish, mainly to excavate the [Native American burial mounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mound#North_American_archaeology) that have been identified there. The first expedition, led by Clarence B. Moore, was an attempt at collecting data from a couple of the sites, and it set the groundwork for later projects. Moore was mainly interested in the skeletal remains of the previous inhabitants, rather than excavating for [archaeological items](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Material_culture). Archaeologists are especially interested in these sites because of their uniformity and size. Some of the mounds are seven hundred feet long, a hundred feet wide and six feet tall. Most of them contain human remains.[^cite_note-5]&#x20;

There were at least 2 wooden forts in the area by 1779, also represent St. Gabriel Parish.&#x20;

Iberville Parish is represented in the [Louisiana State Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_Legislature) by [Caleb Kleinpeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caleb_Kleinpeter), who has served in the Senate since 2022. ## Geography

According to the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Census_Bureau), the parish has a total area of 653 square miles (1,690 km<sup>2</sup>), of which 619 square miles (1,600 km<sup>2</sup>) is land and 34 square miles (88 km<sup>2</sup>) (5.2%) is water.[^cite_note-GR1-6] Iberville Parish is part of the [Baton Rouge metropolitan statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baton_Rouge_metropolitan_area).&#x20;

As of the [2020 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), there were 30,241 people, 10,903 households, and 7,372 families residing in the parish. The 2019 census-estimates determined 32,822 people lived in the parish,[^cite_note-\:0-22] down from 33,387 at the [2010 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_census), and up from 33,320 at the [2000 U.S. census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_United_States_census).[^cite_note-GR2-23]&#x20;

In 2020, the racial and ethnic makeup of the parish was 48.2% [Black and African American](2154.md), 49.6% [non-Hispanic white](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Hispanic_whites), 0.1% [American Indian and Alaska Native](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 0.5% some other race, and 1.5% [two or more races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial_Americans). Approximately 2.6% of the population were [Hispanic and Latin American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) of any race. In 2010, its racial and ethnic makeup was 49.3% Black and African American, 48.8% non-Hispanic white, 0.3% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans), 0.2% American Indian and Alaska Native, 0.6% some other race, and 0.8% from two or more races; 2.0% were Hispanic and Latin American of any race. At the 2000 census. 49.26% were non-Hispanic white, 49.7% African American, 0.18% American Indian and Alaska Native, 0.26% Asian, 0.01% [Native Hawaiian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Hawaiians) and other [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander_Americans), 0.14% from other races, and 0.45% from two or more races; 1.03% were Hispanic and Latin American of any race.&#x20;

There were 10,903 households at the 2019 census-estimates, and 13,396 housing units. Of the 2,697 businesses operating in the parish, 1,339 were minority-owned. The parish had an employment rate of 47.9%.[^cite_note-\:0-22] There was a home-ownership rate of 73.4%, and the median housing value was \$143,700; the median gross rent was \$755. The median income for a household was \$50,161; males had a median income of \$54,655 versus \$30,773 for females; 27.6% of the population lived at or below the poverty line.&#x20;

Among its religious population in 2020, the [Association of Religion Data Archives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Religion_Data_Archives) determined there were 1,700 [non-denominational Protestants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nondenominational_Christianity), and 7,901 [Roman Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholics). Non-denominational Christianity was the largest non-Catholic demographic, reflecting the rise of non/inter-denominationalism.[^cite_note-24]&#x20;

## Education

### Primary and secondary schools

[Iberville Parish School Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberville_Parish_School_Board) operates the public schools within all of Iberville Parish.[^cite_note-30]&#x20;

### Public libraries

Iberville Parish Library operates libraries in the parish. The Parish Headquarters Library is located in [Plaquemine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaquemine,_Louisiana). Branches include [Bayou Pigeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayou_Pigeon,_Louisiana) (*[Unincorporated area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unincorporated_area)*), Bayou Sorrel (*Unincorporated area*), East Iberville ([St. Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Gabriel,_Louisiana)), Grosse Tete ([Grosse Tete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grosse_Tete,_Louisiana)), Maringouin ([Maringouin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maringouin,_Louisiana)), Rosedale ([Rosedale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosedale,_Louisiana)), White Castle ([White Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Castle,_Louisiana)).[^cite_note-31]&#x20;

### Colleges and universities

It is in the service area of [South Louisiana Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Louisiana_Community_College).[^cite_note-32]&#x20;

## National Guard

The Gillis W. Long Center, located on the outskirts of [Carville, LA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carville,_LA), is operated by the [Louisiana Army National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Army_National_Guard). This post is home to the 415TH MI (Military Intelligence) Battalion, the 241ST MPAD, and the 61st Troop Command. The 415TH MI is a subunit of the 139TH RSG (regional support group).&#x20;
