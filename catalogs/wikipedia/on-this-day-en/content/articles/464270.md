The **prime minister of the Cook Islands** is the [head of government](37570.md) of the [Cook Islands](7067.md), a self-governing territory in [free association](346414.md) with [New Zealand](4913064.md). The office was established in 1965, when [self-government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Cook_Islands) was first granted to the islands. Originally, the title "Premier" was used, but this was replaced by the title of "Prime Minister" in 1981.
