**Abnub** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): أبنوب, from [Coptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coptic_language): \*ⲡⲛⲟⲩⲃ[^cite_note-2]) is a city in [Egypt](8087628.md). It is located on the east bank of the [Nile](21244.md), in the [Asyut Governorate](468798.md), several kilometres northwest of [Asyut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asyut). As of the 2023 census, the population was 118,843.[^cite_note-citypopulation2023-1] The city's name ultimately comes from [Ancient Egyptian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Egyptian_language): *pr-nbw*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'treasury'.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Notable people

Abnub is the birthplace of [Pope Shenouda III of Alexandria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Shenouda_III_of_Alexandria), the [Pope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coptic_Pope) of the [Coptic Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coptic_Orthodox_Church) from 1971 to 2012.[^cite_note-4]

A church in Abnub bears the name of [Phoibammon of Preht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoibammon_of_Preht), a 4th century martyr.[^cite_note-\:02-5]
