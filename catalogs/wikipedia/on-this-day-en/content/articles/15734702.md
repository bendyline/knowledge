**Gabrielle Colonna-Romano** (January 17, 1888 – February 2, 1981) or *Colanna Romano* (name as a cinema actress), born **Gabrielle Dreyfus**, was a French actress, famous as a tragedian, [sociétaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soci%C3%A9taires_of_the_Com%C3%A9die-Fran%C3%A7aise) of the [Comédie-Française](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Com%C3%A9die-Fran%C3%A7aise) from 1913 to 1936, and as a student of [Sarah Bernhardt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Bernhardt).[^cite_note-1] She appeared in several plays and poetry readings (notably of works by the [Symbolist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolism_\(arts\)) poet [Saint-Pol-Roux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Pol-Roux)). She had an affair with [Pierre Renoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Renoir), and modeled for several paintings by his father [Auguste Renoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auguste_Renoir), notably *Jeune femme à la rose* (1913).[^cite_note-2] In England, she met and became friends with [Marie Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Bell), on whose advice she decided to present herself to the [Conservatoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatoire_national_sup%C3%A9rieur_d'art_dramatique).

She was the sixth and final wife of the millionaire press-magnate [Alfred Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Edwards_\(journalist\)), and after his death married the actor [Pierre Alcover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Alcover). She and Alcover are buried together in the [Rueil-Malmaison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rueil-Malmaison) cemetery.

She gives her name to the "Prix Colonna-Romano de tragédie classique" at the [Conservatoire national supérieur d'art dramatique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatoire_national_sup%C3%A9rieur_d'art_dramatique) in [Paris](22989.md).

In September 2026 an oil painting by [Pierre-Auguste Renoir](24546.md), *[Portrait of Madame Colonna Romano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portrait_of_Madame_Colonna_Romano)* (c.1913), [was stolen](84173201.md) from the [Musée Renoir](84175718.md) in [Cagnes-sur-Mer](3546923.md). The work had an estimated value of €9 million (£7.7m; \$10.5m).[^cite_note-bbc_amos-hadfield-3]
