The **canton of Fribourg**,[^cite_note-4] also **canton of Freiburg**,[^cite_note-5] is located in western [Switzerland](26748.md). The [canton](180752.md) is [bilingual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilingual), with French spoken by more than two thirds of the citizens and German by a little more than a quarter.[^cite_note-SJF14-6] Both are official languages in the canton. The canton takes its name from its capital city of [Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fribourg).

## History

On the shores of [Lake Neuchâtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Neuch%C3%A2tel) and [Lake Morat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Morat) significant traces of prehistoric settlements have been unearthed.

The canton of Fribourg joined the [Swiss Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Confederation) in 1481. The area is made up of lands acquired by the capital Fribourg. The present extent was reached in 1803 when [Murten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murten) (Morat) was acquired. The canton of Fribourg joined the separatist league of Catholic cantons in 1846 ([Sonderbund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonderbund)). The following year, its troops surrendered to the federal army.

## Geography



Fribourg [Prealps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Prealps): [Dent de Brenleire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dent_de_Brenleire) (2358 m, to the right) and [Vanil Noir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanil_Noir) (2389 m, in the background)

The canton is bounded to the west by Lake Neuchâtel, to the west and the south by the canton of [Vaud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaud), and to the east by the [canton of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Bern). The canton includes two [enclaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enclave) within Vaud and one within the canton of Bern, as well as a large [exclave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclave) on the lake; it also enclaves the Bernese municipality of [Münchenwiler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%BCnchenwiler). The area of the canton is 1,669 square kilometers (644.4 sq mi), including the small enclaves.

The canton lies on the elevated [Swiss Plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Plateau). In the west the lands are flat, but towards the south east of the canton, the lands rise to a hilly region. This region is commonly called *pre-[Alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Alps)* but is part of the [Bernese Alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernese_Alps). The highest elevation in the canton is the [Vanil Noir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanil_Noir) with 2,389 m (7,838 ft).

The river [Saane/Sarine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saane/Sarine) flows from the south to the north of the canton. Together with its tributaries it drains most of the lands in the canton, then joins the river [Aare](1433.md). The river [Broye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broye) drains the west of the canton and flows northeast into [Lake Morat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Morat) (Murtensee). The southwest part of the canton is drained by the river [Veveyse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veveyse), which flows south into [Lake Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Geneva) ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Le Léman*).

### Municipalities

There are [119 municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_the_canton_of_Fribourg) in the canton of Fribourg as of January 2026. The number is decreasing as the canton subsidizes mergers between municipalities.

## Demographics

In contrast to the mainly Protestant cantons of [Vaud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaud) to its west and [Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bern_\(canton\)) to its east, the canton of Fribourg is a predominantly [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic) enclave (as of 2000, 70%) with a [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism) minority (15%).[^cite_note-7] This explains why the canton straddles the French-German linguistic border, for in the past, denominational considerations were more important than linguistic when drawing Switzerland's cantonal borders.

The main centres of population are the capital Fribourg (34,300 inhabitants) and [Bulle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulle) (18,200 inhabitants).

Two thirds of the population speak French; the remainder speak [Alemannic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alemannic_German) dialects of German. The French-speaking areas are in the west of the canton, the Alemannic-speaking areas in the east. The number of bilingual towns, and consequently the large number of people who can speak both French and German fluently, has attracted businesses such as telesales companies. The population of the canton (as of 31 December 2020) is 325,496.[^cite_note-Stat2020_FR-2] As of 2007, the population included 43,838 foreign-born residents, or about 16.65% of the total population.[^cite_note-8]

## Economics

Agriculture is important in the canton of Fribourg. The main agricultural activities are cattle breeding and [dairy farming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy_farming). The region is a major cheese producer, especially the district of [Gruyère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gruy%C3%A8re_District), home of the [cheese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gruy%C3%A8re_\(cheese\)) of the same name. The chocolate industry is also well established in [Broc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broc), home to an international chocolate research centre.[^cite_note-10] Other agricultural produces include tobacco, fruit and cereals. Agriculture is predominant in the north of the canton where the most fertile lands lie.

There is light industry concentrated around the capital Fribourg. Other centres of light industry are [Bulle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulle), [Villars-sur-Glâne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villars-sur-Gl%C3%A2ne), [Düdingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%BCdingen) (Guin), [Murten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murten) (Morat) and [Estavayer-le-Lac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estavayer-le-Lac). These five centers have also a large number of established small and medium-sized businesses, many of which are in the service sector. Forests are important in the [La Gruyère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gruy%C3%A8re_\(district\)) (Greyerz) district.

Power plants in the district of [Sarine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarine_District) export electricity. The mountain areas attract tourists all year round. The lake regions are frequented by tourists in summer and autumn.

## Transport



[Transports publics Fribourgeois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transports_publics_Fribourgeois) [bus station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bus_station) in Fribourg

The canton of Fribourg is well connected to other areas of [Switzerland](26748.md) with motorways [A1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_\(Switzerland\)), A12 and fast rail links. The main railway between [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva) and [Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausanne) in the south west to [Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bern) and [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z%C3%BCrich) connects Fribourg with other centres of the country. Connections within the canton are mostly provided by the [Transports Publics Fribourgeois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transports_publics_Fribourgeois), with numerous bus routes and trains connecting the main towns. The nearest airports to the canton are [Geneva Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_Airport), and [Bern Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bern_Airport).
