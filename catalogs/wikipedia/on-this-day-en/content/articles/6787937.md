The **France women's national basketball team** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Équipe de France féminine de basketball*) represents [France](5843419.md) in international [women's basketball](667977.md) and is administered by the [French Federation of Basketball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Federation_of_Basketball). The team is nicknamed *Les Bleues* (The Blues). However, after their unexpected triumph at the [2009 EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2009) the team earned the name *Les Braqueuses* (The Robbers) due their spectacular play.[^cite_note-2] France is the leading nation in terms of [EuroBasket Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women) qualifications. (Alongside [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_women's_national_basketball_team))
