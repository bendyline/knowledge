The **India–Africa Forum Summit** (**IAFS**) is the official platform for the [African-Indian relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Africa-India_relations). IAFS is supposed be held once in every three years, however the rule has not been strictly followed. It was first held from April 4 to April 8, 2008 in [New Delhi](51585.md), [India](14533.md). It was the first such meeting between the heads of state and government of India and 14 countries of [Africa](5334607.md) chosen by the [African Union](10634933.md). [Libya](17633.md) and [Egypt](8087628.md)'s heads of state did not attend.

## Agenda

Rising [oil](5137675.md) and [food prices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_prices) were the top concerns for the African and Indian leaders during the summit.[^cite_note-1] India has pledged to support at the level of health and education related projects in Africa.

### Topics

- agricultural sector

- trade

- industry and investment

- peace and security

- promotion of good governance and civil society

- information and communication technology

## African representation

- [Chairperson of the African Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairperson_of_the_African_Union) (AU)

- Chairperson of the AU Commission

- Chairpersons of the eight recognised Regional Economic Communities (REC's)

- Chairpersons of the [New Partnership for Africa's Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Partnership_for_Africa's_Development) (NEPAD)

- Heads of State and Government Implementation Committee (HSIGC)

- the five African NEPAD initiating countries

## 2008 First India–Africa Forum Summit - New Delhi, India

The first such summit was held from April 4 to April 8, 2008 in [New Delhi](51585.md), India. It was the first such meeting between the heads of state and government of India and 14 countries of Africa chosen by the [African Union](10634933.md). [Libya](17633.md) and [Egypt](8087628.md)'s heads of state did not attend.

## 2011 Second India–Africa Forum Summit - Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

The second summit was held at the Ethiopian capital [Addis Ababa](52593.md), with India and 15 African Countries participating. The leaders discussed significant aspects of the India-Africa partnership with the objective of enhancing and widening its ambit for mutual benefit.

## 2015 Third India–Africa Forum Summit - New Delhi

Main article: [Third India–Africa Forum Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_India%E2%80%93Africa_Forum_Summit)

The third summit in a rotation basis is held in [New Delhi](51585.md), India from 26 to 30 October 2015.[^cite_note-2] The 5 day summit started with consultations on official level followed by the Head of States/governments level summit on 29 October 2015 with scheduled bilateral meetings on 30 October 2015.[^cite_note-3] This was Modi government's biggest diplomatic outreach involving delegates from a large number of [African nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_nations).[^cite_note-4]

Earlier the summit in a rotation basis was scheduled to be held in [New Delhi](51585.md), India in December 2014. But lately [Syed Akbaruddin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syed_Akbaruddin), the official spokesperson of [Indian Foreign Ministry](10302410.md) told the media that the scheduled summit postponed to 2015 and will include more no. of African leaders unlike previous two occasion where the event was restricted to only 10-15 African countries. In the largest-ever turnout of African leaders in India, at least 41 leaders-including South African president [Jacob Zuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Zuma), Egyptian president [Abdel Fattah el-Sisi](36703624.md), Zimbabwe president [Robert Mugabe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Mugabe) and Nigerian President [Muhammadu Buhari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammadu_Buhari) confirmed their presence in [New Delhi](51585.md) for the India-Africa forum summit in October 2015.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## 2026 Fourth India–Africa Forum Summit - New Delhi

The fourth India–Africa Forum Summit scheduled for 28–31 May, 2026 in New Delhi, was postponed on 21 May 2026 due to an Ebola outbreak in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md). The joint statement by India and the African Union cited the "evolving health situation in parts of Africa" and the need to ensure full participation of African leaders.[^cite_note-7]
