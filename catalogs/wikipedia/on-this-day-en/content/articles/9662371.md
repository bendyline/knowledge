**Somali diaspora** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Qurbajoogta Soomaaliyeed*) refers to ethnic [Somalis](1571696.md) who reside in areas of the world outside of [Greater Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Somalia). The [Somali Civil War](3070332.md) greatly increased the size of the Somali diaspora, as many Somalis moved from [Somalia](27358.md)[^cite_note-24] primarily to [Europe](9239.md), [North America](21139.md), [Oceania](22621.md), [North Africa](21714.md), [West Africa](67393.md), [Middle East](19323.md), [South Asia](21566765.md) and [Southern Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Africa). There are also Somali populations in [Asia](689.md).[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] The UN estimates that in 2015, approximately 2 million people from Somalia were living outside of the country's borders.[^cite_note-UN2015-1]

## Global distribution

The distribution of Somalis abroad is uncertain, primarily due to confusion between the number of ethnic Somalis and the number of Somalia nationals. Whereas most recent Somali migrants in the diaspora emigrated as [refugees](45547.md) and [asylum seekers](276005.md), many have since obtained either [permanent residence](1284324.md) or [citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship). A 2008 publication estimated the ethnic Somali international migrant population as 1,010,000, including around 300,000 in [East](260987.md) and [South Africa](17416221.md), 250,000 in the United States and Canada, 250,000 in Europe, 200,000 in the Middle East, and 10,000 in Australia, New Zealand and Asia.[^cite_note-28] By the start of the [Yemeni Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_Civil_War_\(2015%E2%80%93present\)), the number of Somalis in Yemen was estimated to be 500,000.[^cite_note-Gramer-7]

UN migration estimates of the international migrant stock 2015 suggest that 1,998,764 people from Somalia were living abroad.[^cite_note-UN2015-1][^cite_note-29]

As of 31 March 2020, the number of refugees from Somalia registered with the [UNHCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNHCR) was 763,933. This is down from a peak of almost 990,000 in June 2013. The majority of these individuals were registered in [Kenya](188171.md) (256,408), [Yemen](350939.md) (253,755) and [Ethiopia](187749.md) (198,670).[^cite_note-Unhcrreg-30] According to [USAID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USAID), many of the displaced persons in these adjacent territories are [Bantus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bantus_\(Somalia\)) and other [minorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minority_group).

## Africa

Main articles: [Somalis in Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Kenya) and [Somalis in South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_South_Africa)



A [Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_people) high school student in [Cairo](6293.md), [Egypt](8087628.md).

About two thirds of all Somalis who live outside Somalia live in the neighbouring countries of [Djibouti](17207794.md), [Ethiopia](187749.md), [Kenya](188171.md), and [Yemen](350939.md). Kenya hosted more than 313,000 in April 2017. About 255,000 were registered as refugees in Ethiopia.[^cite_note-31]

Besides their traditional areas of inhabitation in [Greater Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Somalia) (the former [Italian Somaliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Somaliland), [British Somaliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Somaliland), [French Somaliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Somaliland), the [Ogaden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogaden), and [Northeastern Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Eastern_Province_\(Kenya\))), a Somali community mainly consisting of businesspeople, academics and students also exists in [Egypt](8087628.md).[^cite_note-Smmtsdairid-32][^cite_note-Swsie-33]

In addition, there is a historical Somali community in the general [Sudan](27421.md) area. Primarily concentrated in the north and [Khartoum](17320.md), the expatriate community mainly consists of students, as well as some businesspeople.[^cite_note-Thoscitsstfww-34] More recently, Somali entrepreneurs have also established themselves in [South Africa](17416221.md), where they provide most of the retail trade in informal settlements around the [Western Cape](313646.md) province.[^cite_note-Sasc-35]

## Europe

While the distribution of Somalis per country in [Europe](9239.md) is difficult to measure since the Somali expatriate community on the continent has grown so quickly in recent years, there are significant Somali communities in the [United Kingdom](31717.md): 108,000 (2018);[^cite_note-UK_2018_estimate-36] [Sweden](5058739.md): 66,369 (2016);[^cite_note-SCB-37] [Norway](21241.md): 43,196 (2016);[^cite_note-NorwayCensus-8] the [Netherlands](21148.md): 39,465 (2016);[^cite_note-NetherlandsCensus-10] [Germany](11867.md): 38,675 (2016);[^cite_note-GermanyCensus-11] [Denmark](76972.md): 21,050 (2016);[^cite_note-DenmarkCensus-14] and [Finland](10577.md): 24,365 (2022).[^cite_note-Statsfin-13]

### Denmark

Main article: [Somalis in Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Denmark)

Most Somalis in Denmark emigrated from [Somalia](27358.md) following the start of the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md) (1986-), in the period between 1995 and 2000.[^cite_note-Fogedperi-38] According to [Statistics Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Denmark), as of 2017, there are a total 21,204 persons of Somali origin living in Denmark. Of those individuals, 11,832 are Somalia-born immigrants and 9,372 are descendants of Somalia-born persons.[^cite_note-Popt-39] 8,852 individuals are citizens of Somalia (4,730 men, 4,122 women).[^cite_note-Popcz-40]

Between 2008 and 2013, employment among Somalis fell from 38% to 26%.[^cite_note-\:0-41]

By December 2018, nearly 1000 Somalis in Denmark lost their residence permits after the [Danish Immigration Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_Immigration_Service) started a review of the permits in 2017. The permits were revoked as parts of Somalia are safe enough for refugees to return.[^cite_note-42]

In 2018, analysis showed about 44% of Somalis in Denmark live in a [parallel society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_society)[^cite_note-Parallelsamfund_i_Danmark_p13-43] and were the immigrant group which were lagging behind the most in terms of integration into Danish society.[^cite_note-\:0-41]

In 2018, nearly 50% were in long-term unemployment (4 years or more).[^cite_note-Parallelsamfund_i_Danmark_p27-44]

### Finland

Main article: [Somali community in Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_community_in_Finland)

Somalis are one of the largest ethnic minorities in [Finland](10577.md), and the largest group of people of non-European origin. In 2009, there were 5,570 Somali citizens, but an equal number may have received [Finnish citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_citizen). In 2014 there were 16,721 [Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language) speakers in Finland. According to the Finnish newspaper [Helsingin Sanomat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsingin_Sanomat), the number of Somali-speaking people in Finland in 2010 rose by nearly 10% in a year.[^cite_note-45] In 2022, there were 24,365 people resident in Finland with a Somali background.[^cite_note-Statsfin-13]

### Germany

Main article: [Somalis in Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Germany)

This section is an excerpt from [Somalis in Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Germany).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Somalis_in_Germany\&action=edit)]

[Somalis in Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Germany) are citizens and residents of [Germany](11867.md) who are of [Somali](1571696.md) descent. According to the [Federal Statistical Office of Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Statistical_Office_of_Germany), as of 2020, there are a total 47,495 [Somalia](27358.md)-born immigrants living in Germany.[^cite_note-Somalis_in_Germany_Popt-46]

### Netherlands

Main article: [Somalis in the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_the_Netherlands)

From 1989 to 1998, the [Netherlands](21148.md) was the second-most common European destination for Somali immigrants, only slightly behind the United Kingdom and more than double the total of the next-most common destination, Denmark.[^cite_note-FernandesMendes-47] Between 2000 and 2005, there was a significant exodus of Somalis from the Netherlands to the United Kingdom, unofficially estimated to be as large as 20,000 people. The exodus was partly the result of anti-Muslim sentiments in the Netherlands in the wake of the rise of and assassination of right-wing politician [Pim Fortuyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pim_Fortuyn). According to the Somali European Forum, the exodus started earlier due to the Somalis not wanting to adapt to the assimilation process Dutch authorities imposed on them which they felt tried to change their culture.[^cite_note-48]

In 2012, unemployment was particularly high among Somali immigrants at 37%, while unemployment for all non-Western population was at 16%. According to official statistics on immigrants, Somalis had higher unemployment and dependence on social welfare while the crime rate of young male Somalis was high. Unemployment among immigrants rose higher than for the indigenous population in the aftermath of the 2008 financial crisis.[^cite_note-49]

According to a 2018 report using 2015 data, Islam takes a central role in the lives of nearly all Somalis and in many ways their religiosity increased from the already high levels in 2009.[^cite_note-\:11-50]

### Norway

Main article: [Somalis in Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Norway)

This section is an excerpt from [Somalis in Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Norway).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Somalis_in_Norway\&action=edit)]

[Somalis in Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Norway) are citizens and residents of [Norway](21241.md) who are of [Somali](1571696.md) descent. They are the biggest [African migration group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_immigration_to_Norway) in Norway. 36.5% of Somalis in Norway live in the capital [Oslo](22309.md).[^cite_note-51] Almost all Somali in Norway have come to Norway as [refugees](45547.md) from the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md).[^cite_note-Somalis_in_Norway_Popinc-52] In 2016, Somalis were the largest non-European migrant group in Norway.[^cite_note-53]

### Sweden

Main article: [Somalis in Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Sweden)

This section is an excerpt from [Somalis in Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Sweden).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Somalis_in_Sweden\&action=edit)]

[Somalis in Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_Sweden) ([Swedish](26689.md): *Somalier i Sverige*; [Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Soomaalida Iswiidhan*) are citizens and residents of [Sweden](5058739.md) who are Swedish of [Somali](1571696.md) ancestry or are [Somali](1571696.md) citizens. A large proportion of Somalis in Sweden emigrated to Sweden due to the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md).[^cite_note-Somalis_in_Sweden_Fritzes1316c-54][^cite_note-Somalis_in_Sweden_johnsdotter2010-55] Most Somalis in Sweden arrived to the country after the year 2006.[^cite_note-56]

### United Kingdom

Main article: [Somalis in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalis_in_the_United_Kingdom)



A Somali community center in [London](17867.md)'s [East End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_End) (yellow brick building in the middle).

Although most Somalis in the [United Kingdom](31717.md) are recent arrivals, the first Somalis to arrive were seamen and traders who settled in port cities in the late 19th century.[^cite_note-Apituk-57][^cite_note-Dissanayake-58] By 2001, the UK census reported 43,532 Somali-born residents,[^cite_note-Census2001-59] making the Somali community in Britain the largest Somali expatriate population in Europe. The [Office for National Statistics](324029.md) (ONS) estimate of 2018 indicates that 108,000 Somalis live in the UK.[^cite_note-UK_2018_estimate-36] There has also been some [secondary migration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_migration#Secondary_migration) of Somalis from [mainland European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Europe) countries to the United Kingdom.[^cite_note-60][^cite_note-Kleist-61] According to the 2011 UK Census, 71.5% of Somalia-born residents in England and Wales hold a UK passport.[^cite_note-Dcbnaftcew-62]

Established Somali communities are found in [London](17867.md), [Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham), [Liverpool](18081.md), [Cardiff](5882.md) and [Bristol](36741.md), and newer ones have formed in [Manchester](20206.md), [Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield) and [Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester).[^cite_note-Born_abroad-63] The Somali population in London alone accounts for roughly 78% of Britain's Somali residents.[^cite_note-UKCensus-64]

## North America



A Somali strip mall in [Toronto](64646.md).

### United States

Main article: [Somali Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_Americans)

The earliest ethnic Somali immigrants to the United States were sailors who arrived in the 1920s, mainly from [British Somaliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Somaliland). In the late 1970s, more Somali immigrants followed. Not until the 1990s when the civil war broke out in [Somalia](27358.md) did the majority of Somalis come to the US.

In 2008–2012, the largest concentration of Somalia-born people in the United States were in the [Minneapolis](6097240.md)-[St. Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Paul,_Minnesota)-[Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomington,_Minnesota) area (17,320) of Minnesota. Other metropolitan areas with significant numbers of Somali Americans include [Columbus, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus,_Ohio) (10,280), [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle,_Washington)-[Tacoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tacoma,_Washington)-[Bellevue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellevue,_Washington) in [Washington](13015878.md) (7,850), [San Diego](28504.md)-[Carlsbad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlsbad,_California)-[San Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Marcos,_California) in California (2,845), [Washington, D.C.](108956.md)-[Arlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington,_Virginia)-[Alexandria](18940583.md) in the [Virginia](32432.md)-D.C. area (2,715), [Atlanta](3138.md)-[Sandy Springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandy_Springs,_Georgia)-[Marietta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marietta,_Georgia) in [Georgia](48830.md) (2,305), [Phoenix](49121.md)-[Mesa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesa,_Arizona)-[Glendale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendale,_Arizona) in Arizona (1,965), [Portland](23503.md)-[Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver,_Washington)-[Hillsboro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillsboro,_Oregon) in Oregon (1,480), [Nashville-Davidson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville-Davidson_\(balance\),_Tennessee)-[Murfreesboro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murfreesboro,_Tennessee)-[Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin,_Tennessee) in Tennessee (1,420), [Boston](24437894.md)-[Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts)-[Quincy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy,_Massachusetts) in Massachusetts (1,380), and other areas (28,650).[^cite_note-US_Census_Bureau-65]

### Canada

Main article: [Somali Canadians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_Canadians)

[Canada](5042916.md) hosts one of the largest [Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_people) populations in the Western world, with the 2011 National Household Survey reporting 44,995 people claiming Somali descent,[^cite_note-Nhscan-66] though an unofficial estimate placed the figure as high as 150,000 residents.[^cite_note-Omescp-67] Somalis tend to be concentrated in the southern part of the province of [Ontario](22218.md), especially the [Ottawa](22219.md) and [Toronto](64646.md) areas. The [Albertan](717.md) cities of [Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calgary) and [Edmonton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmonton) have also seen a significant increase in their respective Somali communities over the past five years. In addition, the neighbourhood of [Rexdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rexdale) in the Toronto borough of [Etobicoke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etobicoke) has one of the largest Somali populations in the country. [Statistics Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Canada)'s 2006 Census ranks people of Somali descent as the 69th largest ethnic group in Canada.[^cite_note-STATSCANCensus-68]

## Middle East

There is a sizable Somali community in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md). Somali-owned businesses line the streets of [Deira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deira,_Dubai), the [Dubai](211583.md) city centre,[^cite_note-Sciodb-69] with only [Iranians](14653.md) exporting more products from the city at large.[^cite_note-70]

Relations between the modern-day territories of Somalia and [Yemen](350939.md) stretch back to antiquity. A number of Somali clans trace descent to the latter region.[^cite_note-Lewis-71] During the colonial period, disgruntled Yemenis from the Hadhrami wars sought and received asylum in various Somali towns.[^cite_note-72] Yemen in turn unconditionally opened its borders to Somali nationals following the outbreak of the civil war in Somalia in the early 1990s.[^cite_note-Wrsuscr-73] In 2015, after the Saudi-led [military intervention in Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_military_intervention_in_Yemen), many returning Somali expatriates as well as various foreign nationals began emigrating from Yemen to [Somaliland](51425.md).[^cite_note-Grfylibt-74]
