**Erbil Governorate** ([Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language): پارێزگای ھەولێر, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Kurdish): *Parêzgeha Hewlêr*;[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة أربيل, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Muḥāfaẓat Arbīl*[^cite_note-7]) is a [governorate](222800.md) of [Iraq](7515928.md) in the semi-autonomous [Kurdistan Region](3949386.md). Its [administrative centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_centre) is [Erbil](24534207.md), which is its most important city. [^cite_note-8] [Erbil](24534207.md) is also the most populated and one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, with the [citadel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citadel_of_Erbil) being recognized as a [UNESCO World Heritage Site](44940.md).
