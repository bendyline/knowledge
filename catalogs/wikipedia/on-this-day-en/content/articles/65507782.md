**Múte Inequnaaluk Bourup Egede** (Greenlandic: [\[mut͡sːi inɜquˈnaːluk ˈpou̯ʁɔp ˈeːəðə\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Greenlandic); born 11 March 1987) is a Greenlandic politician who served as the [prime minister of Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Greenland) from 2021 to 2025. He has also served as Chairman of the [Inuit Ataqatigiit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inuit_Ataqatigiit) since December 2018, and a Member of the [Inatsisartut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inatsisartut) since May 2015.

## Early life

Múte Inequnaaluk Bourup Egede was born in [Nuuk](53303.md) on 11 March 1987.[^cite_note-cv-1] He grew up in [Narsaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narsaq) and was educated in [Qaqortoq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qaqortoq) before studying cultural and social history at the [University of Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Greenland) in 2007. He was the vice chairman of the Greenlandic Academic Student Society from 2011 to 2012.[^cite_note-cv-1] He did not finish his studies and dropped out in 2013 to take over the family [fodder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fodder) company started by his father.[^cite_note-dr-2][^cite_note-knr-3][^cite_note-formand-4]

## Career

In 2007, Egede was a member of the Greenlandic youth parliament, the *Inuusuttut Inatsisartui*.[^cite_note-knr-3] From 2013 till 2015 he was the chairman of the *Inuusuttut Ataqatigiit*, the youth wing of [Inuit Ataqatigiit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inuit_Ataqatigiit).[^cite_note-cv-1][^cite_note-knr-3]

At the [2015 Danish general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Danish_general_election), Egede was a candidate for the [Folketing](429743.md) for Inuit Ataqatigiit.[^cite_note-fv15-5] He received 2,131 votes, not enough for a seat in parliament.[^cite_note-fv15resultater-6]

Between 2016 and 2018, Egede served as Minister for Raw Material and the Labour Market, where he simultaneously – for three months in 2017 – was acting Minister of Communes, Hamlets, Outer Districts, Infrastructure and Housing.[^cite_note-knr-3]

On 1 December 2018 he was elected chair of [Inuit Ataqatigiit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inuit_Ataqatigiit), succeeding [Sara Olsvig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Olsvig).[^cite_note-knr-3] He led the party to the [2021 Greenlandic general elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Greenlandic_general_election), where it gained 36.6% of the vote, becoming the largest party in parliament.[^cite_note-dr-2] With 3,380 personal votes, Egede was the candidate with the most personal votes in the elections, receiving upwards of 1,500 more than the sitting [prime minister](273648.md), [Kim Kielsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Kielsen) of the [Siumut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siumut) party.[^cite_note-stemmer-7]



Egede with [Mette Frederiksen](26040044.md) and [Sanna Marin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanna_Marin) in [Copenhagen](5166.md), 2021

On 16 April it was announced that [IA had formed a coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Egede_cabinet) with [Naleraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naleraq) with a 10-member [Naalakkersuisut](23389805.md). [Atassut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atassut), which holds two seats, announced that while it would not enter a pro-independence coalition they would provide support to the coalition.[^cite_note-8]

Egede was confirmed as [Prime Minister of Greenland](273648.md) by the [Inatsisartut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inatsisartut) on 23 April 2021,[^cite_note-confirmed-9] becoming the country's youngest Prime Minister in history.[^cite_note-10] He formed [a new government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Egede_cabinet) in April 2022.[^cite_note-11]

In response to the ongoing controversy over the [spiral case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiral_case), Egede accused Denmark of committing [genocide](12441.md) in Greenland.[^cite_note-12]

During the [2025 Greenlandic general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Greenlandic_general_election), Egede received 3,276 personal votes, losing to Jens-Frederik Nielsen, who received 4,850.[^cite_note-13]

## Political ideology

Egede is the chairman of [Inuit Ataqatigiit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inuit_Ataqatigiit), a [democratic socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_socialist) political party.[^cite_note-14] He is an advocate for [Greenlandic independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenlandic_independence).[^cite_note-knr-3]

## Personal life

Egede is married to Sara Biilmann Egede, with whom he has one child.[^cite_note-cv-1] He also has two children from a prior relationship.[^cite_note-cv-1]
