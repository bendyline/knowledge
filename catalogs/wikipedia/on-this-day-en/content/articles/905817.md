**Abia** [Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/e/e7/LL-Q33578_%28ibo%29-Chinwoke_01-Abia.wav/LL-Q33578_%28ibo%29-Chinwoke_01-Abia.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> ([Igbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_language): Alaọha Abia) is a [state](226734.md) in the [Southeastern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_East_\(Nigeria\)) region of [Nigeria](21383.md). The state's capital is [Umuahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umuahia) and its most populous city is [Aba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aba,_Nigeria). The legislature is the [Abia State House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abia_State_House_of_Assembly).

One of the smallest states in Nigeria, Abia is bordered to the west by [Imo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imo_State), east by [Cross River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_River_State), south by [Rivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivers_\(state\)), northwest by [Anambra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anambra) and northeast by [Enugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu) and [Ebonyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebonyi). The state is divided between the [Niger Delta swamp forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger_Delta_swamp_forests) in the south and the [Cross–Niger transition forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross%E2%80%93Niger_transition_forests). The [Imo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imo_River) and [Aba Rivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aba_River_\(Nigeria\)) flows along the state's western and southern borders respectively. Known for producing [crude oil](23195.md) and [natural gas](22131.md), Abia ranks [eighth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states_by_Human_Development_Index#2019) by [Human Development Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Development_Index) since 2019.

The name "Abia" is an abbreviation of Abia state's four densely populated regions [Aba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aba,_Abia), [Bende](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bende,_Nigeria), [Isuikwuato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isuikwuato), and [Arochukwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arochukwu). Abia's slogan is "God's own state". Abia is one of the 36 states in Nigeria, and has seventeen [local government areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_area). It was created on 27 August 1991 out of [Imo State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imo_State) by General [Ibrahim Babangida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Babangida), and it is located in the south-eastern part of Nigeria with [Umuahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umuahia) as the capital, and [Aba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aba,_Nigeria) as the commercial city.

Abia State's history begins as part of the [Aro Confederacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aro_Confederacy) until its defeat in the early 1900s by the [British troops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_troops) during the [Anglo-Aro War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Aro_War). After the war, the area was incorporated into the [Southern Nigeria Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Nigeria_Protectorate) of [British Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Nigeria). After Nigeria's independence in 1960, Abia became part of the [Eastern Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Region,_Nigeria) until 1967 before the region was split, and it became part of the [East Central State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Central_State). The Eastern Region attempted to secede in [Nigerian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Civil_War) with Abia as part of the secessionist state of [Biafra](44431.md). After the war and the then reunification of Nigeria, the East Central State was merged until 1976 when Imo including Abia, was formed by [Murtala Muhammed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murtala_Muhammed). Eastern Imo would later become the old Abia State after a split. In 1996, part of Abia's northeast was removed to form a part of the new [Ebonyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebonyi).

The [Igbo people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_people) make up 100 percent of the state's population and the [official language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_language) is [Igbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_language).[^cite_note-4] There are over 3 million [Christians](18337522.md).[^cite_note-5] *[Catholic Hierarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Hierarchy)* analyses that there are 689,668 Catholics (2020) in [Diocese of Umuahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Umuahia) with over 70 parishes, and Aba Diocese with over 90 parishes, both suffragans of the [Archdiocese of Owerri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Owerri).[^cite_note-6]

## Geography



Azumini Blue River in Abia state, Nigeria

Abia State occupies about 4902 square kilometres. It is bounded on the north and northeast by the states of [Enugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu_State) for about 25 km, and [Ebonyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebonyi_State) for 70 km (43 miles), [Cross River State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_River_State) for about 52 km (partly across Cross River) and [Akwa Ibom State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akwa_Ibom_State) for 151 km (94 miles) to the east and southeast respectively, [Rivers State](1966992.md) to the south and west for 87 km (54 miles), [Imo State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imo_State) and [Anambra](905838.md) to the west for to the west in the vicinity of the Imo River for about 18 km and about 104 km respectively. The southernmost part of the State lies within the [Niger Delta Swamp Forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niger_Delta_Swamp_Forests), while the rest of the state, lies within the [Cross–Niger transition forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross%E2%80%93Niger_transition_forests).[^cite_note-EB-8] The southern portion gets [heavy rainfall](19009110.md) of about 2,400 millimetres (94 in) per year and it is intense between the months of April through October.[^cite_note-9] The most important rivers in Abia State are the [Imo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imo_River) and [Aba Rivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aba_River_\(Nigeria\)) which flow into the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md) through Akwa Ibom State.[^cite_note-10]

### Environmental Issues

#### Solid waste

Municipal solid waste management (MSWM) deals with the collection, storing, treatment and disposal of [solid waste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_waste), to ensure that it does not affect humans, living things and the environment at large. There are factors that influence [Municipal solid waste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_solid_waste) generation such as income level, local climatic condition, [urbanization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urbanization) and economic development.[^cite_note-\:1-11] MSW in Aba, Abia State is classified into;

1. Domestic waste (waste from households, food centers, markets, and commercial premises)

2. Industrial waste (excluding toxic waste that requires special handling)

3. Institutional waste (waste from government establishments, schools, hospitals and recreational facilities

In recent times, it was reported that Aba and Umuahia generate up to two hundred and seventy truckloads of domestic and commercial waste daily. This information was made known to the public by Governor Alex Otti during his inauguration speech after taking the baton of government from the former governor, [Okezie Ikpeazu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okezie_Ikpeazu).[^cite_note-12]

### Climate

Aba experiences a warm and gloomy [wet season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_season) as well as a scorching and oppressive dry season. The [temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature) rarely drops below 16 [°C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celsius) (61 [°F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahrenheit)) or rises over 33 °C (91 °F) throughout the entire year, fluctuating between 20 and 31 °C (68 and 88 °F).[^cite_note-\:2-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

The beach/pool score indicates that the best time of year to visit Aba for hot-weather activities is from late November to early February.[^cite_note-16]

Since Aba experiences so small seasonal temperature variations, talking about the hot and cold seasons is not very helpful.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

## Demographics

### Religion

The residents of Abia state are predominantly [Christians](18337522.md) and most are [Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholics). The catholic dioceses of [Umuahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Umuahia), established in 1958 and [Aba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Aba), established in 1990 are the two catholic dioceses located in Abia State with 70 and 90 parishes respectively.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] They are both suffragans of [Owerri Archdiocese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Owerri).

The [Aba Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Province_of_Aba) of Anglican Church of Nigeria led by Archbishop [Isaac Nwaobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Nwaobia) comprises the nine Dioceses of [Aba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Aba) led by Bishop [Christian Ugwuzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Ugwuzo?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Aba Ngwa North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Aba_Ngwa_North) (2007) led by Bishop [Nathan Kanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Kanu), [Arochukwu/Ohafia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Arochukwu/Ohafia) led by Bishop [Johnson Onuoha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnson_Onuoha), [Ikwanuo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Ikwuano) led by Bishop [Chigozirim Onyegbule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chigozirim_Onyegbule), [Isiala Ngwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Isiala-Ngwa) led by Bishop [Temple Nwaogu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_Nwaogu), [Isial-Ngwa South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Isiala-Ngwa_South) led by Bishop [Isaac Nwaobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Nwaobia), [Isikwuato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Isikwuato) led by Bishop [Manasses Chijiokem Okere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manasses_Chijiokem_Okere), [Ukwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Ukwa) led by Bishop [Samuel Kelechi Eze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Kelechi_Eze), and [Umuahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Diocese_of_Umuahia) led by Bishop [Geoffrey Obijuru Ibeabuchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Obijuru_Ibeabuchi).[^cite_note-21]

There are also other pentecostal churches located within Abia State including [Living Faith Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_Faith_Church_Worldwide) which has more than 100 branches within the state.

## Economy

[Crude oil](23195.md) and [gas](22131.md) production is a prominent activity, as it contributes over 39% of the State's [GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP).[^cite_note-ReferenceA-22] However, the indigenous oil companies – through the Marginal Fields Programme (MFP) – have not found it easy to attract the requisite funding and infrastructural capacity, to explore some of the marginal [oil fields](2910801.md) which are about 50 in the State.\[^cite_note-[www.channelstv.com-23](http://www.channelstv.com-23/)]

The manufacturing sector only accounts for 2% of the [GDP](12594.md).[^cite_note-ReferenceA-22] The industrial centre of the state is in [Aba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aba,_Abia), with [textile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile) manufacturing, [pharmaceuticals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmaceutical), [soap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soap), [plastics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic), [cement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cement), [footwear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Footwear), and [cosmetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmetics).[^cite_note-EB-8] In addition to the above, Abia State Government built a 9,000 capacity multipurpose International Conference Centre in Umuahia. This edifice of international standard was built by Governor [T.A. Orji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_Orji), to enhance tourism as well as boost the state economy, through hosting of major international and local events.

Representing 27% of the GDP,[^cite_note-ReferenceA-22] agriculture – which employs 70%[^cite_note-ReferenceA-22] of the state workforce – is the second economic sector of Abia. With its adequate seasonal rainfall, Abia has much [arable land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arable_land) that produces [yams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yam_\(vegetable\)), [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [potatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potato), [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice), [cashews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cashew), [plantains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plantain_\(cooking\)), [taro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taro), and [cassava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassava).[^cite_note-EB-8] [Oil palm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_palm) is the most important [cash crop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_crop).[^cite_note-EB-8]

### Oil and gas exploration

There are over 100 [oil wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_well) and 3 installed flow stations in Abia State.\[^cite_note-[www.channelstv.com-23\]\[^cite_note-www.vanguardngr.com-24](http://www.channelstv.com-23]\[^cite_note-www.vanguardngr.com-24)] There is also an associated gas plant, Abia/NNPC gas plant.\[^cite_note-[www.vanguardngr.com-24](http://www.vanguardngr.com-24/)] As of 2012, boundary Commission said it returned 42 oil wells from neighbouring [Rivers State](1966992.md) to Abia.\[^cite_note-[www.vanguardngr.com-24](http://www.vanguardngr.com-24/)] This makes Abia the fourth largest oil-producing state in the country.\[^cite_note-[www.vanguardngr.com-24](http://www.vanguardngr.com-24/)] Oil giant, [Shell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shell_Nigeria), holds most of the licenses for the wells in the State, and has concentrated on the estimated 50 wells that are considered high-yield.\[^cite_note-[www.channelstv.com-23](http://www.channelstv.com-23/)]

The state produced 36,000 barrels of crude oil per day; "Imoturu produces 23,000 barrels per day and Isimili flow station produces over 8,000 barrels of crude oil per day.[^cite_note-25] Then four oil wells in Izaku go to Obigo flow station. About 30 oil wells from my village go to Umuri, and about eight oil wells from Umurie go to Afam", said Samuel Okezie Nwogu, Chairman of Abia State Oil Producing Development Area Commission (ASOPADEC). However, the State has complained of poor funding from its oil revenue federal allocation.\[^cite_note-[www.vanguardngr.com-24](http://www.vanguardngr.com-24/)]

## Education

Abia state's public and private secondary schools, according to a document by the [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_\(Nigeria\)), had a [student–teacher ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student%E2%80%93teacher_ratio) of 8\:1 to 12\:1 from 2013 to 2016 school year.[^cite_note-26] All school divisions must adhere to educational standards set forth by the [Abia State Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abia_State_Ministry_of_Education). In 2016, 13,699 students (5,512 males and 8,187 females) were enrolled in public schools while 14,798 (7,068 males and 7,730 females) were enrolled in private schools. In 2017, 13,351 students (5,517 males and 7,834 females) were enrolled in the public schools, while 39,280 students (19,260 males and 20,020 females) were enrolled in private schools. According to the [National Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Bureau_of_Statistics,_Nigeria), there exists, as of 2016, 168 public schools and 194 private schools, and, as of 2017, 233 public schools and 1,095 private schools.[^cite_note-NBS-27]

In 2016, 3859 students graduated from public high schools while 5419 graduated from private schools. In 2017, 3691 graduated from public schools while 11,707 graduated from private schools.[^cite_note-NBS-27] As of 2025 Goodluck Ubochi serves as the Commissioner for Basic and Secondary Education while Uche Eme serves as the Commissioner for Tertiary Education.[^cite_note-28]

### Colleges and universities

Main page: [Category\:Universities and colleges in Abia State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category\:Universities_and_colleges_in_Abia_State)

According to *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian_\(Nigeria\))*, Abia had a literacy level of 94.24 percent in 2017 after Imo, Lagos, and Ekiti States.[^cite_note-29] The Ministry of Education recognizes 5 universities in Abia; one federal, one state, and three privately-owned universities.[^cite_note-30] In the 2025 *Unirank* ranking of universities, [Michael Okpara University of Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Okpara_University_of_Agriculture) ranked 57th, [Abia State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abia_State_University) is 67th, [Gregory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_University) is 94th, [Rhema University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhema_University) is 105th, [Clifford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_University) is 147th, and Spiritan University, [Nneochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nneochi?action=edit\&redlink=1) is 161th.[^cite_note-31]

[Abia State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abia_State_University), founded in 1981 is the only state university and the oldest in Abia.[^cite_note-32] Lux Mundi University became the first private university in [Umuahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umuahia), the state capital.[^cite_note-33] In 2024, the state under Governor [Alex Otti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Otti), adopted a policy of free and compulsory basic education for all the state's residents as well as non-indigenes which took effect in January 2025.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35]

## Transportation

The nearest airport to Abia is [Sam Mbakwe Cargo Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Mbakwe_Cargo_Airport) (Owerri Airport), it is an hour drive to [Umuahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umuahia) and [Aba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aba,_Abia). It is two hours' drive to [Port Harcourt International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Harcourt_International_Airport). [Akwa Ibom Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akwa_Ibom_Airport) ([Akwa Ibom State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akwa_Ibom_State)) can also serve would-be visitors. The distance between [Uyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uyo,_Nigeria) (Akwa Ibom) and [Umuahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umuahia) (Abia) is 73.28 kilometres (45.53 mi).

[Rail transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_transport) is also available in the state. [Aba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aba,_Abia) is connected to [Port Harcourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Harcourt) by rail, Umuahia is connected to Aba and [Enugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enugu_State) by rail.[^cite_note-EB-8] The [coastal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal) parts of the State are equally accessible using boats and canoes.

## Politics

The [State Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abia_State_Government) is led by a democratically elected [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Abia_State) who works closely with members of the state's [House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abia_State_House_of_Assembly). The capital city of the state is [Umuahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umuahia),[^cite_note-36] and there are 17 local government areas in the state.



Abia State House of Assembly, Nigeria

At statehood in 1991, Abia was ruled by [Ibrahim Babangida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Babangida)-appointed Military Administrator [Frank Ajobena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Ajobena) before [Ogbonnaya Onu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogbonnaya_Onu) was [elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Abia_State_gubernatorial_election) governor later that year under the [Third Nigerian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Nigerian_Republic). Onu governed for nearly two years before [Sani Abacha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sani_Abacha) ended the Third Republic and reinstated full military rule. Under the Abacha regime, three more Military Administrators ([Chinyere Ike Nwosu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinyere_Ike_Nwosu), [Temi Ejoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temi_Ejoor), and [Moses Fasanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moses_Fasanya)) were appointed before Abacha's death and the accession of [Abdulsalami Abubakar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulsalami_Abubakar). Abubakar appointed one more Military Administrator, [Anthony Obi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Obi), before starting the transition to democracy in 1998.

In 1999, Nigeria returned to democracy, and [Orji Uzor Kalu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orji_Uzor_Kalu) was [elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_Abia_State_gubernatorial_election) governor on the platform of the [People's Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Democratic_Party_\(Nigeria\)). Consequently, he was sworn in on 29 May 1999. In 2003, when it was time for fresh elections, Kalu re-contested on the platform of the PDP and [got a second mandate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_Abia_State_gubernatorial_election) to govern (the [Constitution of Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nigeria) limits Governors to two terms in office). At the end of Kalu's term in 2007, [Theodore Orji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_Orji) ([PPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_Peoples_Alliance)) defeated [Onyema Ugochukwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onyema_Ugochukwu) (PDP) in the [2007 gubernatorial election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Abia_State_gubernatorial_election), to become Abia's next Governor. In 2011, Theodore Orji defected from the PPA to the PDP before being [re-elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Abia_State_gubernatorial_election) for another four-year term later that year.

In 2015, [Okezie Ikpeazu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okezie_Ikpeazu) (PDP) was [voted in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Abia_State_gubernatorial_election) as the ninth Governor of Abia State.[^cite_note-37] Four years later, he won [re-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Abia_State_gubernatorial_election), defeating [Uche Ogah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uchechukwu_Sampson_Ogah) of the [All Progressives Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Progressives_Congress) and [Alex Otti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Otti) of [APGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Progressives_Grand_Alliance) to be sworn in as Governor for a second term on 29 May 2019.[^cite_note-38] In Abia State, 70 percent of politicians belong to the PDP.[^cite_note-39]

In 2023, Alex Otti of the [Labour Party Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Party_\(Nigeria\)) (LP) emerged as the eleventh governor of the state having defeated the candidate of the PDP in a fiercely contested race.[^cite_note-40]

### Electoral system

The electoral system of each state is selected using a modified two-round system. To be elected in the first round, a candidate must receive the plurality of the vote and over 25% of the vote in at least two -third of the State local government Areas. If no candidate passes threshold, a second round will be held between the top candidate and the next candidate to have received a plurality of votes in the highest number of local government Areas.[^cite_note-41]
