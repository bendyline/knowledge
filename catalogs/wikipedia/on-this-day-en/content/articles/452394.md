The **northern panhandle** is one of the two [panhandles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salient_\(geography\)) in the [U.S. state](18618239.md) of [West Virginia](32905.md) (the other is the [Eastern panhandle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_panhandle_of_West_Virginia)). It is a culturally and geographically distinct region of the state. It is the state's northernmost extension, bounded by [Ohio](22199.md) and the [Ohio River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_River) on the north and west and the state of [Pennsylvania](23332.md) on the east. Its unusual configuration is the result of the [Revolutionary-era claims of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_former_United_States_counties#Revolutionary-era_claims_of_Virginia)'s former [Yohogania County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yohogania_County) boundary lying along the Ohio River, conflicting with interpretations of the [Colony of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_of_Pennsylvania)'s royal charter. The conflict was settled by compromise in the 1780s.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

In 2013, its northernmost two counties were included in the [Pittsburgh consolidated area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh_metropolitan_area) by the U.S. Census.[^cite_note-2]

## Geography

### Composition

The following counties, listed from north to south, are universally accepted as being part of the northern panhandle:

- [Hancock County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hancock_County,_West_Virginia)

- [Brooke County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooke_County,_West_Virginia)

- [Ohio County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_County,_West_Virginia)

- [Marshall County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_County,_West_Virginia)

Most West Virginians also include [Wetzel County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wetzel_County,_West_Virginia), and sometimes [Tyler County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyler_County,_West_Virginia), directly to the south of Marshall County, as a part of the northern panhandle, although they do not lie strictly within the northern extension.[^cite_note-3] Prior to the 2020 redistricting, they formed the core of [West Virginia's 1st congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia's_1st_congressional_district).&#x20;

### Demographics

The region had a total combined population of 132,295, counting counties only physically on the panhandle, and 158,086 counting Wetzel and Tyler counties as of the [2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_Census). In the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), the populations were 124,670 and 147,425, respectively. This constitutes a drop of 5.76% and 7.23%, keeping with the overall population trends of the [Rust Belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rust_Belt).[^cite_note-4]

The northern counties of the northern panhandle comprise part of the [Weirton–Steubenville, WV–OH Metropolitan Statistical Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weirton%E2%80%93Steubenville_metropolitan_area), as well as the western regions of [Greater Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh_Metropolitan_Area).[^cite_note-5] The southern counties are part of the [Wheeling, WV–OH Metropolitan Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheeling_metropolitan_area).[^cite_note-6]

## Economy

In the late 19th century, the northern panhandle developed as an industrial area, especially in the manufacturing of steel and glass products.[^cite_note-\:0-1] It still largely retains its industrial character, although many of its factories have closed down or fallen on hard times like others in the [Rust Belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rust_Belt). The region also contains the [panhandle coalfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panhandle_coalfield).[^cite_note-7]

Banks in these counties are serviced by the [Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Reserve_Bank_of_Cleveland),[^cite_note-8] while the rest of West Virginia is in the [Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Reserve_Bank_of_Richmond).[^cite_note-9]

## Education

The region houses three four-year colleges: the [public](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_university) [West Liberty University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Liberty_University)[^cite_note-10] and the private [Bethany College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethany_College_\(West_Virginia\)) and [Wheeling University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheeling_University). All five counties listed in this article, plus [Tyler County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyler_County,_West_Virginia), are located within the service area of [West Virginia Northern Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia_Northern_Community_College).[^cite_note-11]
