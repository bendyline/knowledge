Since July 2026, there have been multiple wildfires in [Greece](12108.md), resulting in several casualties and damage to properties.

## Background

The wildfires occurred during the [2026 European heatwaves](83266395.md).[^cite_note-1] On 5 July, there were dozens of wildfires ongoing across Greece.[^cite_note-2]

## Attica

On the evening of 31 July, a wildfire broke out in the [Aigaleo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aigaleo) region, at the Poíkilo mountain. While the fire did not last for a long time, it led to the evacuation of the area due to its proximity to industrial zones. The Dafni psychiatric hospital was also nearly evacuated, although the fire changed direction, resulting in the evacuation being cancelled.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

On 2 August, two people were killed when [two firefighting helicopters crashed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Athens_mid-air_collision) near Athens.[^cite_note-5] This happened amid wildfires in places such as [Porto Germeno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto_Germeno), Psatha, [Fokida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fokida), [Megara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megara) and [Kefalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kefalonia).[^cite_note-6]

On 16 August, two wildfires broke out in [Salamis Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salamis_Island), killing two people.[^cite_note-7]

## Crete

On 30 July 2026, thousands of people were evacuated in [Crete](6591.md) due to wildfires.[^cite_note-8] The fires destroyed large areas of farmland, olive groves and residential areas.[^cite_note-9]

Three firefighters were reported killed.[^cite_note-10]

## Paros

Wildfires were reported in [Paros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paros).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Thessaloniki

On 30 June 2026, a wildfire broke out near the Liti area of Thessaloniki, killing two people and forcing evacuations.[^cite_note-13]

On 4 July 2026, a wildfire broke out in the suburb of Anthoupoli and forced evacuations across three Thessaloniki suburbs. The wildfire damaged factories in the Filothei area, and forced the evacuation of a hospital named Agios Panteleimonas. The wildfire was extinguished on the morning of 5 July.
