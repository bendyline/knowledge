The **vice presidents of Myanmar** are the second highest-ranking posts in the government of the [Republic of the Union of Myanmar](19457.md).[^cite_note-Constitution-2] The offices were established by the [2008 Myanmar constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Myanmar) and rank directly below the [president](11195083.md). The offices came into effect on 30 March 2011, when the new government assumed *de jure* power and essentially function in the same manner as any other deputy head of state. There are two vice-presidential posts in the government,[^cite_note-3] but no distinction is officially made between them. It can be assumed that the posts follow the order of seniority, much like the ones practised by the [Vice Premier of the People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_Premier_of_China).&#x20;

The 2008 [Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Constitution_of_Myanmar) establishes that the President and Vice-Presidents are elected by the [Pyidaungsu Hluttaw](29739324.md). The way they are elected are as follows:

- The elected members of the [Amyotha Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amyotha_Hluttaw) nominate one person.

- The elected members of the [Pyithu Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyithu_Hluttaw) nominate one person.

- The combined bloc of military personnel from both houses nominate one person.

- Then, in a full, joint session of the [Pyidaungsu Hluttaw](29739324.md), the person with the highest number of votes is elected President.

- The two other persons become Vice-Presidents.
