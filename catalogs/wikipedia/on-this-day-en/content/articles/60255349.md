The **Ministry of Communications and Information** (**MCIT**; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الاتصالات وتقنية المعلومات) is a [Saudi](349303.md) government ministry that was established in 1926 and is responsible for the communications and information technology sector in the kingdom.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2] The current minister of Communications and Information is [Abdullah Alswaha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_Alswaha) appointed on 23 April 2017.[^cite_note-3]

## History

The earliest governmental entity to govern the communications and technology sectors was the Directorate of Post, Telephone and Telegraph (PTT) which was established in [Makkah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mecca) in 1926.[^cite_note-\:1-2] in 1931, the telegraph services were provided by the first mobile wireless station imported by the Kingdom, followed by the introduction of telephone service In 1934.[^cite_note-\:0-1] In 1953, the telegraph, post, and telephone facilities were reported Ministry of Transport which was established in that year.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

The rapid growth of telecommunications technology led to the establishment of the Ministry of Post, Telegraph, and Telephone in 1975 to be responsible for the telecommunications and posts sectors.[^cite_note-\:0-1] In 2003, the name of the Ministry of Post, Telegraph, and Telephone was amended to be the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## Responsibilities

The Ministry is mainly responsible for the communications and information technology in the Kingdom where many tasks have been assigned to, including:[^cite_note-4]

- The supervision over the communications and information technology sector and related activities.

- The development of policies that govern the communications and information technology sector.

- Designing plans for the communications and information technology sector.

## National programs

The Ministry launched and supervised three national programs:

National Digitization Unit: aims at ensuring the development of platforms for a digital society, digital economy and digital homeland.[^cite_note-5]

Yesser: An E-Government Program aims at increasing the productivity and efficiency of the governmental sector by providing the required services and information.[^cite_note-6]

[National Center for Digital Certification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Center_for_Digital_Certification) (NCDC): This program provides systems for the infrastructure management of the public keys which an essential security technique for e-business, e-trade, and e-government over the internet.[^cite_note-7] this program provides services such as issuing digital certificates, search for digital certificates, checking certificates and validity.[^cite_note-8]
