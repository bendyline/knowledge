The **British Academy Film Awards** (the **BAFTAs** or **BAFTA Awards**; officially the **EE British Academy Film Awards** since [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/66th_British_Academy_Film_Awards)) is an annual [film award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_award) show hosted by the [British Academy of Film and Television Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_of_Film_and_Television_Arts) (BAFTA) to honour the best [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinema_of_the_United_Kingdom) and [international](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_cinema) film contributions. The ceremony was [first held](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_British_Academy_Film_Awards) in London at [Odeon Luxe Leicester Square Cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odeon_Luxe_Leicester_Square), then the [Royal Opera House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Opera_House) from [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/60th_British_Academy_Film_Awards) to [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/69th_British_Academy_Film_Awards). The event was held at the [Royal Albert Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Albert_Hall) from [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70th_British_Academy_Film_Awards) to [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/75th_British_Academy_Film_Awards), before moving to the [Royal Festival Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Festival_Hall) for [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/76th_British_Academy_Film_Awards). The statue awarded to recipients depicts a theatrical mask. The BAFTA Awards are recognised internationally as the highest honour in British cinema, equivalent to the [BAFTA TV Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_Television_Awards) for [television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_in_the_United_Kingdom), [Laurence Olivier Awards](16432264.md) for [theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theatre_of_the_United_Kingdom), and the [BRIT Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brit_Awards) for [music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_the_United_Kingdom). The BAFTA Film Awards are considered equivalent to the [Academy Awards](324.md).&#x20;

The first BAFTA Awards ceremony was held in 1949, and the ceremony was first broadcast on the [BBC](19344654.md) in [1956](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_British_Academy_Film_Awards) with [Vivien Leigh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivien_Leigh) as the host. The ceremony was initially held in April or May; since [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/54th_British_Academy_Film_Awards), it typically takes place in February. The BAFTAs have been sponsored by [EE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EE_\(telecommunications\)) since 2013.

## History



Society of Film and Television Arts award from 1970



BAFTA special lifetime achievement award presented to Ronnie Barker

The [British Academy of Film and Television Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_of_Film_and_Television_Arts) (BAFTA) was founded in 1947 as The British Film Academy, by [David Lean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Lean), [Alexander Korda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Korda), [Carol Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carol_Reed), [Charles Laughton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Laughton), [Roger Manvell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Manvell), [Laurence Olivier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier), [Emeric Pressburger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeric_Pressburger), [Michael Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Powell), [Michael Balcon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Balcon), and other major figures of the British film industry.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] In 1958, the Academy merged with The Guild of Television Producers and Directors to form The Society of Film and Television, which eventually became The British Academy of Film and Television Arts in 1976.[^cite_note-3]

The stated charitable purpose of BAFTA is to "support, develop and promote the art forms of the moving image, by identifying and rewarding excellence, inspiring practitioners, and benefiting the public". In addition to high-profile awards ceremonies, BAFTA runs a year-round programme of educational events, including film screenings and tribute evenings. BAFTA is supported by a membership of about 6,000 people from the film, television, and video game industries.

The Academy's awards are in the form of a theatrical mask designed by American sculptor [Mitzi Cunliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitzi_Cunliffe), in response to a commission from the Guild of Television Producers in 1955.[^cite_note-4]

[Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Philip,_Duke_of_Edinburgh) was the host of the ceremony as the first president of BAFTA in 1960, 1963, and 1965.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

[Princess Anne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Anne) presented the first Fellowship Award to [Alfred Hitchcock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Hitchcock) in 1971,[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] and subsequently attended the ceremony several times as President of BAFTA between 1973 and 2001.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Annual ceremony



The location for the [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/64th_British_Academy_Film_Awards) ceremony

The ceremony previously took place in April or May, but since 2001 it has been held in February in order to precede the [Academy Awards](324.md). Most of the awards are open to all nationalities, though there are awards for Outstanding British Film and Outstanding Debut by a British Writer, Producer or Director. Only UK films are eligible for the categories of The British Short Film and British Short Animation awards.

During each annual ceremony, BAFTA pauses *[in memoriam](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/in%20memoriam)* to pay tribute to those in the industry who have died over the past 12 months, showcasing a montage of images accompanied by music.

[William, Prince of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William,_Prince_of_Wales) attended the ceremony for the first time in 2010 after being named president of BAFTA, and has attended frequently since, including together with [Catherine, Princess of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine,_Princess_of_Wales).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] He presented the [BAFTA Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Fellowship) award to [Mel Brooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Brooks) in 2017 and [Kathleen Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathleen_Kennedy_\(producer\)) in 2020.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

### Broadcast

Historically, the Awards ceremony was broadcast on British television on a same-day delay, and later distributed internationally. The first broadcast was on the [BBC](19344654.md) in 1956, with [Vivien Leigh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivien_Leigh) (who would present an award to her ex husband Sir [Laurence Olivier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier)) as the host.[^cite_note-17] It has been broadcast in colour since 1970. In the US the show was previously broadcast with a delay on [BBC America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_America) until 2021, after which it has broadcast on streaming service [BritBox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BritBox).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] In 2023, the BAFTA Film Awards included a live telecast for its major award categories during the culmination of the ceremony.[^cite_note-21]

### Location

The award ceremony is held in [London](17867.md). From 2000 to 2007, the ceremonies took place at the flagship [Odeon cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odeon_Luxe_Leicester_Square) in [Leicester Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester_Square). Between 2008 and 2016, the ceremonies took place at the Royal Opera House. The 70th Awards in 2017, and subsequent ceremonies up to the 75th Awards in 2022, were held at the Royal Albert Hall.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

For the [76th British Academy Film Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/76th_British_Academy_Film_Awards) in 2023, it was announced that the ceremony would be moved to the Royal Festival Hall as part of a new multi-year deal between BAFTA and the [Southbank Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southbank_Centre), bringing the Film Awards in-line with the [British Academy Television Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_Television_Awards) and [British Academy Games Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_Games_Awards), which were already held there.[^cite_note-24]

### Sponsorship

Until 2012, the mobile telephone network [Orange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_UK) sponsored the awards. Orange's parent company, [EE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EE_Limited), took over the sponsorship of the event from 2013.[^cite_note-25]

Notes

- [Albert Finney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Finney) was the first actor to receive two nominations for one performance.

- [Jodie Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodie_Foster) was the first actor to win both nominations.

- [Brad Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Davis_\(actor\)) is the only actor to win neither nomination.

- The only ceremony with multiple actors to receive two nominations for one performance was the [37th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/37th_British_Academy_Film_Awards), with [Phyllis Logan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phyllis_Logan) and [Julie Walters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_Walters). Logan and Walters were nominated twice against each other, and both won, effectively splitting categories.

#### Directors nominated for multiple films in the same year

The only director to receive dual nominations in the same year at the BAFTA Film Awards was [Steven Soderbergh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Soderbergh) for *[Erin Brockovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erin_Brockovich_\(film\))* and *[Traffic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_\(2000_film\))* in 2000. Additionally, [Sidney Lumet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidney_Lumet) received one joint nomination for *[Murder on the Orient Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_on_the_Orient_Express_\(1974_film\))* and *[Serpico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serpico)* in 1974.&#x20;

### Other

- **Most awards won by a single film**&#x20;

  - *[Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butch_Cassidy_and_the_Sundance_Kid)* (1969), with 9 wins.

- **Most nominations received by a single film**&#x20;

  - *[Gandhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandhi_\(film\))* (1982), with 16 nominations.

- **Most awards won by a non English-language film**&#x20;

  - *[All Quiet on the Western Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Quiet_on_the_Western_Front_\(2022_film\))* (2022), with 7 wins.

- **Most nominations received by a non English-language film**&#x20;

  - *[Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crouching_Tiger,_Hidden_Dragon)* (2000) and *[All Quiet on the Western Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Quiet_on_the_Western_Front_\(2022_film\))* (2022), with 14 nominations each.

- **Most nominations received by an animated film**&#x20;

  - *[Shrek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrek)* (2001), with 6 nominations.

- **First non-English language film to win Best Film**&#x20;

  - *[Bicycle Thieves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_Thieves)* in 1949.

- **First non-English language films to be nominated for Best Film**&#x20;

  - *[Four Steps in the Clouds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Steps_in_the_Clouds)*, *[Monsieur Vincent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsieur_Vincent)* and *[Paisan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paisan)* in 1948.

- **First animated film to be nominated for Best Film**&#x20;

  - *[Shrek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrek)* (2001)

    [^cite_note-28]

- **First animated film to win any screenplay categories**&#x20;

  - *[Shrek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrek)* (2001), with [Best Adapted Screenplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Adapted_Screenplay)

    [^cite_note-screenplay-29]

- **Largest sweep (winning awards in every nominated category)**&#x20;

  - *[A Man for All Seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Man_for_All_Seasons_\(1966_film\))* (1966), with 7 wins.

- **Most nominations without winning an award**&#x20;

  - *[Women in Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_Love_\(film\))* (1969), *[Finding Neverland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finding_Neverland_\(film\))* (2004), and *[Marty Supreme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marty_Supreme)* (2025), with 11 nominations each.

- **Most total nominations and awards for a person**&#x20;

  - [Woody Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Allen) received 24 nominations and won 10 awards.

- **Most nominations and awards for a person in a single year**&#x20;

  - [Alfonso Cuarón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Cuar%C3%B3n) received six nominations and won four awards in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/72nd_British_Academy_Film_Awards).

- **Most nominations for a woman in a single year**&#x20;

  - [Chloé Zhao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlo%C3%A9_Zhao) received four nominations in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_British_Academy_Film_Awards).

- **Most total awards for a woman**&#x20;

  - [Judi Dench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judi_Dench) and [Catherine Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Martin_\(designer\)) won six awards each.

- **Most total nominations for a woman**&#x20;

  - [Sandy Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandy_Powell_\(costume_designer\)) received 16 nominations.

- **Highest "perfect score"**&#x20;

  - Composer [Ennio Morricone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ennio_Morricone) has six nominations and six wins.

- **Only person to receive every nomination in a category**&#x20;

  - [Jocelyn Rickards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jocelyn_Rickards) received both nominations for Best British Costume Design – Black and White in [1967](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_British_Academy_Film_Awards), winning for *[Mademoiselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mademoiselle_\(1966_film\))*.

- **Only actor to win a BAFTA for portraying a real BAFTA winner**&#x20;

  - [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) won Best Actress in a Supporting Role for portraying [Katharine Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katharine_Hepburn) in *[The Aviator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Aviator_\(2004_film\))* (2004).&#x20;

    - NOTE: [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) won Best Actor in a Leading Role for portraying [Charlie Chaplin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Chaplin) in *[Chaplin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaplin_\(film\))* (1992). Chaplin received the [BAFTA Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAFTA_Fellowship), an honorary award, but was never nominated for a competitive BAFTA.

- **Only actor to be nominated for a voice-only performance**&#x20;

  - [Eddie Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddie_Murphy) nominated for Best Actor in a Supporting Role for voicing [Donkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donkey_\(Shrek\)) in *[Shrek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrek)* (2001).

- **Oldest person to win an award**&#x20;

  - [James Ivory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Ivory), age 89 (Best Adapted Screenplay, *[Call Me by Your Name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_Me_by_Your_Name_\(film\))*, 2017).

- **Youngest person to win an award**&#x20;

  - [Jodie Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodie_Foster), age 13 (Best Actress in a Supporting Role and Most Promising Newcomer to Leading Film Roles, *[Bugsy Malone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bugsy_Malone)* / *[Taxi Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxi_Driver)*, 1976).
