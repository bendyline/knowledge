**Goalanda** ([Bengali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_language): গোয়ালন্দ) is an [upazila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upazila) of [Rajbari District](2274550.md) in the Division of [Dhaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhaka_Division), [Bangladesh](3454.md).[^cite_note-Banglapedia-4]

## Geography

Goalanda is located at [23°44′00″N 89°45′40″E  /  23.7333°N 89.7611°E  / 23.7333; 89.7611](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Goalanda_Upazila\&params=23.7333_N_89.7611_E_). It has 24,613 housing units and has a total area of 121.82 km<sup>2</sup>.

According to the [2022 Bangladeshi census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Bangladeshi_census), Goalanda Upazila had 31,230 households and a population of 128,796. 9.47% of the population were under 5 years of age. Goalanda had a literacy rate (age 7 and over) of 67.52%: 69.47% for males and 65.73% for females, and a sex ratio of 93.21 males for every 100 females. 26,642 (20.69%) lived in urban areas.[^cite_note-2022census-5]

As of the [2011 Census of Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Bangladesh_census), Goalanda upazila had 24,613 households and a population of 112,732. 26,622 (23.62%) were under 10 years of age. Goalanda had an average literacy rate of 40.3%, compared to the national average of 51.8%, and a sex ratio of 1053 females per 1000 males. 30,190 (26.78%) of the population lived in urban areas.[^cite_note-census2011-6][^cite_note-community-7]

Goalanda had a population of 91,675 in the [1991 Bangladesh census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Bangladesh_census). Males constitute 52.1% of the population, and females 47.9%. The population aged over eighteen was 44,854. Goalandaghat has an average literacy rate of 20.5% (7+ years), compared to the national average of 32.4%.[^cite_note-census-8]

## Administration

Goalanda Upazila is divided into Goalanda Municipality and four [union parishads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_parishad): Chotovakla, Debugram, Doulatdia, and Uzancar. The union parishads are subdivided into 56 mauzas and 152 villages.[^cite_note-census2011-6]

Goalanda Municipality is subdivided into 9 wards and 45 [mahallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahalla).[^cite_note-census2011-6]
