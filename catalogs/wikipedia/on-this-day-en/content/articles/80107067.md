The **2025 FIFA Intercontinental Cup final**, also known simply as the **2025 FIFA Intercontinental Cup**, was the final match of the [2025 FIFA Intercontinental Cup](76156932.md), an international club [football](10568.md) tournament featuring the club champions from each of the six continental confederations. The match took place on 17 December 2025 at the [Ahmad bin Ali Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_bin_Ali_Stadium) in [Al Rayyan](57488319.md), Qatar, between French club [Paris Saint-Germain](357488.md), winners of the [2024–25 UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UEFA_Champions_League), and Brazilian club [Flamengo](187239.md), the winners of play-offs contested by the other five confederations.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

Paris Saint-Germain won the match 2–1 on [penalties](511302.md), following a 1–1 draw after [extra time](633242.md#Association_football), for their first [club world championship title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_world_champion_football_clubs).[^cite_note-summary-1][^cite_note-7]

## Route to the final

As the winners of the [2024–25 UEFA Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UEFA_Champions_League), [Paris Saint-Germain](357488.md) qualified directly for the final of the tournament. [Flamengo](187239.md) advanced as the winners of the play-off match, known as the FIFA Challenger Cup, played on 13 December 2025.

## Match

### Summary

In the 38th minute, [Khvicha Kvaratskhelia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khvicha_Kvaratskhelia) scored when he finished at the back post from close range after a low cross from the right by [Désiré Doué](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A9sir%C3%A9_Dou%C3%A9) which Flamengo goalkeeper [Agustín Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agust%C3%ADn_Rossi_\(footballer\)) got a hand to but couldn't prevent it reaching Kvaratskhelia. In the 62nd minute, Flamengo were awarded a penalty when [Giorgian de Arrascaeta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgian_de_Arrascaeta) was fouled by [Marquinhos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marquinhos), [Jorginho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorginho_\(footballer,_born_December_1991\)) scored with a low finish to the right. The match went to extra-time and eventually a penalty shoot-out. PSG goalkeeper [Matvey Safonov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matvey_Safonov) saved four penalties in a row as PSG won the match 2-1 on penalties.[^cite_note-9]
