The **2026 Daytona 500** was a [NASCAR Cup Series](56571945.md) race and the 68th running of the [event](39021.md). It was held on Sunday, February 15, 2026, at [Daytona International Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytona_International_Speedway) in [Daytona Beach, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytona_Beach,_Florida).[^cite_note-2026schedule-3] It was the first race of the [2026 NASCAR Cup Series](79747972.md) season. The race was [Kyle Busch](1467805.md)'s final Daytona 500 as well as his final cup series pole before his death on May 21, 2026.[^cite_note-9]

The race saw 65 lead changes, with a total of 26 different leaders (out of a 41-car field) being a Daytona 500 record.[^cite_note-allen1-10] [23XI Racing](65517461.md)'s [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) avoided multiple accidents on the final lap and passed [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) just before the start/finish line to claim both his and the team's first Daytona 500 victory, as well as the ninth win of his Cup Series career and his first since the [2024 Straight Talk Wireless 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Straight_Talk_Wireless_400) at [Homestead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homestead%E2%80%93Miami_Speedway). [Ricky Stenhouse Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Stenhouse_Jr.) finished 2nd, and [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) finished 3rd. Elliott and [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski) rounded out the top five, and [Zane Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Smith), [Chris Buescher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Buescher), [Riley Herbst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riley_Herbst), [Josh Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Berry), and [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) rounded out the top ten.

## Report

### Background



The layout of the [Daytona International Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytona_International_Speedway).

[Daytona International Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytona_International_Speedway) is one of three [superspeedways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superspeedway) to hold [NASCAR](21114.md) races, the other two being [Atlanta Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Motor_Speedway) and [Talladega Superspeedway](681674.md).[^cite_note-tacksnascar-11] The standard track at Daytona International Speedway is a four-turn superspeedway that is 2.5 miles (4.0 km) long.[^cite_note-daytrack-12] The track's turns are banked at 31 [degrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degree_\(angle\)), while the [front stretch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straight_\(racing\)), the location of the finish line, is banked at 18 degrees.[^cite_note-daytrack-12]

In 2026, the Daytona 500 will be held on its traditional [Presidents' Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidents'_Day) weekend. However, Daytona announced on October 22, 2025, that the 2027 Daytona 500 would be a week later, held on February 21, 2027, to avoid a conflict with the [Super Bowl](27718.md).[^cite_note-13]

For the second consecutive year, 41 cars will be in the Daytona 500, [Jimmie Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmie_Johnson) is guaranteed a spot with the [Open Exemption Provisional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_rules_and_regulations#Open_Exemption_Provisional) (OEP).[^cite_note-14]

## The Duel at Daytona

See also: [2026 The Duel at Daytona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_The_Duel_at_Daytona)

[The Duels at Daytona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Duel_at_Daytona) are a pair of NASCAR Cup Series races held in conjunction with the Daytona 500 annually in February at Daytona International Speedway. They consist of two races 60 laps and 150 miles (240 km) in length, which serve as heat races that set the lineup for the Daytona 500. Both races sets the lineup for positions 3–32. The first race sets the lineup for cars that qualified in odd–numbered positions on pole qualifying day. The second race sets the lineup for cars that qualified in even–numbered positions. Positions 33–36 are filled by the four drivers that posted the fastest lap in pole qualifying that didn't lock in a position in the Duel's. Positions 37–40 are set by the four cars highest in the 2019 owner's points that didn't lock in a position in the Duels and weren't among the cars that posted the four fastest timed laps in pole qualifying.

### Radio

The race was broadcast on radio by the [Motor Racing Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motor_Racing_Network) who have covered the Daytona 500 since [1970](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970_Daytona_500)—and simulcast on [Sirius XM NASCAR Radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirius_XM_NASCAR_Radio). The booth crew consisted of Alex Hayden, [Mike Bagley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Bagley), and former championship crew chief [Todd Gordon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Gordon). Longtime turn announcer [Dave Moody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Moody_\(sportscaster\)) was the lead turn announcer, calling the race from atop the [Sunoco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunoco_LP) tower outside the exit of turn 2 when the field races through turns 1 and 2. Kyle Rickey worked the backstretch for the race from a spotter's stand on the inside of the track & Tim Catalfamo called the race when the field races through turns 3 and 4 from the Sunoco tower outside the exit of turn 4. On pit road, MRN was operated by Lead MRN Pit Reporter Steve Post, Chris Wilner, and PRN Radio's Brad Gillie.
