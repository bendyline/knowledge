The **Party for Socialism and Liberation** (**PSL**) is a [Marxist-Leninist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxist-Leninist) communist [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party_in_the_United_States) in the United States. PSL formed in 2004, when its members split from the [Workers World Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers_World_Party).

PSL describes itself as a [revolutionary socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_socialist) party in the [Leninist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leninist) tradition, because the party believes that only a revolution can end [capitalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalism) and establish [socialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialism). PSL pursues this goal by participating in local protests, running candidates in elections, and conducting political education.[^cite_note-PSLAbout-7][^cite_note-Corn2023-8]

Notable members include [Claudia De la Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudia_De_la_Cruz), [Eugene Puryear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Puryear), [Gloria La Riva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_La_Riva), [Jodi Dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodi_Dean), and [Michael Prysner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Prysner).

## Organization

### Membership

PSL does not release membership numbers.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-Kaplan2020-10][^cite_note-Kroitzsh2019-3] In 2019, PSL reported branches in "some 90 cities"[^cite_note-Kroitzsh2019-3] or "nearly 100 cities".[^cite_note-11] In 2022, PSL reported an "organized presence in over 100 cities".[^cite_note-12] In 2024, PSL reported being "in over 50 cities".[^cite_note-13]

PSL is a [democratic centralist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_centralist) party,[^cite_note-Becker2016-14][^cite_note-Becker2018-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-PSLConstitution2022-18] which means that "all members, including those who disagree, are duty bound to publicly defend and carry out" all PSL decisions.[^cite_note-PSLConstitution2022-18] PSL's highest body is its Party Congress, held "every two to three years", which selects its Central Committee leadership.[^cite_note-PSLConstitution2022-18] The PSL Central Committee can appoint up to "40 percent" of Congress delegates.[^cite_note-PSLConstitution2022-18]

#### ANSWER Coalition

Main article: [A.N.S.W.E.R.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.N.S.W.E.R.)

When ANSWER was founded, many of ANSWER's lead organizers were members of the [Workers World Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers_World_Party) (WWP) and its [International Action Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Action_Center),[^cite_note-Albert_and_Shalom-19][^cite_note-20] such as Brian Becker.[^cite_note-Starr2024-21] Many of these founders later created PSL.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

After PSL split from the WWP, ANSWER became more tightly tied to PSL than to WWP. ANSWER's National Coordinator is Brian Becker,[^cite_note-24] a PSL co-founder who said "we do a great deal of work through" ANSWER.[^cite_note-Casey-25] *[The New Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Republic)* described ANSWER as a PSL "[front group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front_group)",[^cite_note-Casey2024-26] and the two have significant financial overlap.[^cite_note-Starr2024-21]

#### BreakThrough News

Main article: [BreakThrough News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BreakThrough_News)

PSL leadership are closely involved with BreakThrough News (BTN).[^cite_note-Starr2024-21] In 2023, BTN's anchors were PSL co-founders Brian Becker[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27] and [Eugene Puryear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Puryear),[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27][^cite_note-BTNTeam2024-28] and [Rania Khalek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rania_Khalek);[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27][^cite_note-BTNTeam2024-28] its editor-in-chief was PSL central committee member Ben Becker;[^cite_note-Starr2024-21][^cite_note-BTNTeam2024-28] and its secretary was [Claudia De la Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudia_De_la_Cruz).[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27] BTN works closely with [Tricontinental Institute for Social Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tricontinental_Institute_for_Social_Research) and has often hosted Tricontinental founder [Vijay Prashad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijay_Prashad).[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27]

#### The People's Forum

Main article: [The People's Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_People's_Forum)

PSL leadership are closely involved with The People's Forum, an event space in [New York City](645042.md), which hosts the BreakThrough News studio.[^cite_note-Starr2024-21][^cite_note-Ross2022-29] People's Forum was directed by Claudia De la Cruz[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27][^cite_note-30] and funded by Neville Singham.[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27][^cite_note-Starr2024-21][^cite_note-Ross2022-29] In 2024, De la Cruz ran as the PSL's candidate for President of the United States.[^cite_note-Gabbatt2024-31][^cite_note-\:0-32] In April 2025, [Senate Judiciary Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Senate_Committee_on_the_Judiciary) Chairman [Chuck Grassley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Grassley) asked [Pam Bondi](4550623.md) and [Kash Patel](62202727.md) to assess whether The People's Forum and [Code Pink](2440600.md) should register under the [Foreign Agents Registration Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Agents_Registration_Act) due to their alleged connections to the [CCP](7175.md) because of their relationship with Neville Roy Singham.[^cite_note-\:0-32]

#### Neville Singham

Many of the organizations above are funded in part by [Neville Roy Singham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neville_Roy_Singham),[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27][^cite_note-Starr2024-21][^cite_note-Ross2022-29] a [Shanghai](27643.md)-based American businessman who supports organizations and media outlets that have been noted for echoing pro-[Beijing](18603746.md) talking points.[^cite_note-Hvistendahl2023-33] Most of this funding comes from the Justice and Education Fund (JEF), to which Neville Singham has donated more than \$20 million, and for which Claudia De la Cruz works as a coordinator.[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27][^cite_note-Starr2024-21]

## History



PSL protesters at the [2016 Democratic National Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Democratic_National_Convention) in Philadelphia

PSL was formed in June 2004[^cite_note-Corn2023-8][^cite_note-PSL2023October24-1] when the [San Francisco](49728.md) branch of [Sam Marcy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Marcy)'s [Workers World Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers_World_Party) left the organization. The San Francisco branch, alongside other members, announced that "the Workers World Party leadership is no longer capable of fulfilling \[the] mission" of building socialism.[^cite_note-Kroitzsh2019-3] PSL co-founders included Richard Becker,[^cite_note-Kroitzsh2019-3] Brian Becker,[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27] [Gloria La Riva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_La_Riva),[^cite_note-Kroitzsh2019-3] and [Eugene Puryear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Puryear).[^cite_note-Bredderman2023-27]

In 2015, PSL members joined the [2015 Baltimore protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Baltimore_protests).[^cite_note-34]

In late 2016 and on January 20, 2017, PSL led [protests against the inauguration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_inauguration_of_Donald_Trump#Protests_and_demonstrations) of [Donald Trump](4848272.md) in [Los Angeles](18110.md), [New York City](645042.md), and [Washington, DC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_DC).[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37]

In 2020, PSL denied an accusation that it mishandled a [sexual abuse](7397019.md) allegation in its [Philadelphia](50585.md) branch.[^cite_note-38] In 2024, PSL presidential candidate Claudia De la Cruz again denied these allegations, some of which she described as misinformation "doing the job of [agents](11127.md)".[^cite_note-39]

In 2020, PSL participated in protests against the Aurora, Colorado police department for the [killing of Elijah McClain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Elijah_McClain). Five PSL members were arrested. Three were charged with "kidnapping" for "attempting to ... secrete 18 officers" inside the police station.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-Kaplan2020-10] The kidnapping charges were all thrown out and all felony charges were dropped.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42]

On October 7, 2023, PSL described "[the actions of the resistance over the course of the last day](74998317.md)" as "a morally and legally legitimate response to occupation".[^cite_note-43] The [Anti-Defamation League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Defamation_League) condemned PSL's comments as anti-Zionist and anti-Semitic.[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45] On October 8, 2023, PSL organized a rally in support of [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_\(state\)) in [Times Square](31389.md)[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47] in which Central Committee member [Eugene Puryear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Puryear) stated: "\[T]here was some sort of rave or desert party where they were having a great time, until the resistance came in electrified hang gliders and took at least several dozen hipsters, and I'm sure they're doing very fine despite what the *New York Post* says."[^cite_note-Casey2024-26][^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50]

Since 2023, PSL has organized numerous rallies in favor of [Palestinian liberation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_liberation), including many of the [Gaza war protests in the United States](75347645.md).[^cite_note-Casey2024-26][^cite_note-51]

In 2024, PSL and [American Muslims for Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Muslims_for_Palestine) (AMP) organized a protest outside of Ohr Torah synagogue in [Edison, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edison,_New_Jersey), in opposition to the synagogue holding auctions for property in illegal Israel settlements in the [West Bank](33209.md).[^cite_note-52] A minor scuffle ensured. Both sides blamed the other as aggressors.[^cite_note-NJMonitor2025NietoMunoz-53] The [Department of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Justice) (DOJ)'s civil rights head [Harmeet Dhillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmeet_Dhillon) filed a civil complaint against PSL and AMP, which it accused of making the protest turn "violent".[^cite_note-54] The DOJ used the [Freedom of Access to Clinic Entrances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_of_Access_to_Clinic_Entrances?action=edit\&redlink=1) (FACE) Act, the first time the law had ever been applied to a non-clinic.[^cite_note-NJMonitor2025NietoMunoz-53][^cite_note-55]

In May 2025, Elias Rodriguez [killed two employees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_killing_of_Israeli_Embassy_workers_in_Washington,_D.C.) of the [Israeli embassy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Israel,_Washington,_D.C.) in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md)[^cite_note-Weiniger2025-56] Rodriguez was a former PSL member.[^cite_note-TimesOfIsrael2025-57][^cite_note-Independent2025-58][^cite_note-Weiniger2025-56] PSL stated that they "do not support" the shooting, rejected "any attempt to associate the PSL with the DC shooting",[^cite_note-PslTweet2025Rodriguez-59] and stated that Rodriguez was not a current PSL member, and had only briefly been associated with PSL Chicago in 2017.[^cite_note-Weiniger2025-56][^cite_note-Independent2025-58][^cite_note-PslTweet2025Rodriguez-59] In 2017, Rodriguez attended a [police brutality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_brutality_in_the_United_States) protest as a PSL member.[^cite_note-Humana2017-60]

PSL helped organize the [June 2025 Los Angeles protests against mass deportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2025_Los_Angeles_protests_against_mass_deportation).[^cite_note-SAEN-61]

In May 2026, longtime PSL Steering Committee member Walter Smolarek led a split from PSL, which took the Brooklyn branch.[^cite_note-Geese2026Gigi-62] Smolarek claimed PSL leadership suppressed internal debate, surveilled its members, and opposed "base-building" labor organizing work.[^cite_note-Geese2026Gigi-62][^cite_note-63] PSL leadership denied each charge in detail.[^cite_note-64]

## Political positions



A PSL supporter protesting against the 2021 [killing of Ma'Khia Bryant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Ma'Khia_Bryant)

PSL is a [Marxist–Leninist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxism%E2%80%93Leninism) party.[^cite_note-Derek2017-2][^cite_note-Kroitzsh2019-3][^cite_note-Khan2021-4][^cite_note-Harman2018-5] PSL's program and constitution simply identify PSL as [Marxist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxist),[^cite_note-PSLConstitution2022-18][^cite_note-PSLProgram2022-65] while PSL's other writings identify PSL as a Marxist–Leninist party[^cite_note-66][^cite_note-67][^cite_note-68] in the [Leninist party model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_centralism).[^cite_note-Becker2016-14][^cite_note-Becker2018-15] Other socialists, such as [Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Party_of_the_United_States) presidential candidate [Howie Hawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howie_Hawkins), label PSL as [second campist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_campism).[^cite_note-Sculos2022-69][^cite_note-Afinogenov2022-70][^cite_note-Hawkins2023-71][^cite_note-72]

PSL describes its primary goal as the revolutionary overthrow of [capitalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalism) and the institution of [socialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialism), stating that "humanity today has only two choices: an increasingly destructive capitalism, or socialism".[^cite_note-PSLAbout-7] PSL holds that the United States is "a dictatorship of the [capitalist class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalist_class)" that cannot change "without a socialist revolution".[^cite_note-73]

### Domestic affairs

PSL's supports nationalization of [the largest 100 domestic corporations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commanding_heights_of_the_economy),[^cite_note-Chen2024-74][^cite_note-Washington2024-75][^cite_note-Gabbatt2024-31][^cite_note-Kennedy2023Sep-76][^cite_note-Kennedy2023Dec-77] free childcare for all,[^cite_note-Gabbatt2024-31] expanded [public transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_transit),[^cite_note-Washington2024-75][^cite_note-Kennedy2023Sep-76] [social housing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_housing),[^cite_note-Wagner2023-78] prohibition on "exploitation of labor for private profit", [guaranteed employment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_guarantee), a ["guaranteed living income"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_income_guarantee), comprehensive social benefits, a 30-hour workweek,[^cite_note-PSLProgram2022-65] and socialist economic planning.[^cite_note-Chen2024-74][^cite_note-Kennedy2023Dec-77]

PSL supports a guaranteed right to [abortion](765.md),[^cite_note-PSLProgram2022-65][^cite_note-Kennedy2023Dec-77] equal pay regardless of gender, protection against gender-based or sexuality-based discrimination and violence,[^cite_note-PSLProgram2022-65][^cite_note-Wolf2017-79][^cite_note-Kennedy2023Dec-77] and national self-determination and reparations for African Americans and residents of US territories in Puerto Rico, Samoa, Guam, the Virgin Islands and Mariana Islands.[^cite_note-PSLProgram2022-65][^cite_note-Kennedy2023Dec-77] *The Brown Daily Herald* described De la Cruz's platform as prioritizing the needs of Black Americans, women, LGBTQ+ people, and undocumented immigrants.[^cite_note-Chen2024-74] In an interview with *Arizona Luminaria*, Karina Garcia called for amnesty and legalization for undocumented immigrants.[^cite_note-Washington2024-75]

### International affairs

PSL calls for a 90% cut to the [military budget of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_budget_of_the_United_States),[^cite_note-Washington2024-75] the closure of all overseas military bases, and a halt of [American aid to Israel](65284610.md).[^cite_note-Chatelain2024-80]

PSL supports the [Workers' Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers'_Party_of_Korea), the ruling party of [North Korea](21255.md),[^cite_note-Corn2023-8][^cite_note-Wolf2017-79][^cite_note-Casey2024-26] which PSL describes as a ["communist government"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_country) and "one of the few top-to-bottom, [actually-existing, alternatives to the global capitalist system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_socialism)".[^cite_note-Gowans2013-81] PSL has defended North Korea's [human rights record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rights_in_North_Korea) against criticism by the [United Nations](31769.md), which it calls "thinly veiled justification for U.S. aggression toward North Korea",[^cite_note-Wang2014-82][^cite_note-83] and argues that "conditions in North Korea are vastly better than those in other developing countries".[^cite_note-Wang2014-82] PSL supports North Korea's [nuclear weapons program](106424.md).[^cite_note-Corn2023-8][^cite_note-Masciotra2023-84][^cite_note-Gowans2013-81][^cite_note-85]

PSL supports the [Communist Party of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_China),[^cite_note-Corn2023-8][^cite_note-Wolf2017-79][^cite_note-Masciotra2023-84] criticizing only [its economic reforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_and_opening_up) concerning a "[market socialist economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_market_economy)".[^cite_note-Cornish2013-86][^cite_note-Becker2007-87] PSL argues that "militant political defense of the [Chinese government](23239.md)" is necessary to stave off "counterrevolution, imperialist intervention and dismemberment".[^cite_note-Wolf2017-79][^cite_note-Becker2007-87] PSL defends China's [human rights record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rights_in_China),[^cite_note-Corn2023-8] and strongly denies that the [People's Liberation Army](66890.md) massacred peaceful student protestors in the [1989 Tiananmen Square protests and massacre](44534.md).[^cite_note-Wolf2017-79][^cite_note-88][^cite_note-Cornish2013-86] PSL denies that China has suppressed [democracy in Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy_in_Hong_Kong) during the [2019–2020 Hong Kong protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%932020_Hong_Kong_protests).[^cite_note-Brown2022-89][^cite_note-90]

PSL supported the 2014 [Russian annexation of Crimea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Crimea_by_the_Russian_Federation).[^cite_note-Brown2022-89][^cite_note-91] PSL did not support the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) in 2022, but blamed the Russo-Ukrainian war on [NATO](21133.md) and the United States.[^cite_note-Brown2022-89][^cite_note-92] In its statement on "Russia's military intervention", PSL highlighted the "plight of ethnic Russians \[...] in the Donbas", Russia's "legitimate security concerns", and NATO's "provocative behavior".[^cite_note-Brown2022-89][^cite_note-93]

PSL opposes [American-led intervention in the Syrian civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American-led_intervention_in_the_Syrian_Civil_War), and has generally been supportive of former Syrian President [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md),[^cite_note-Brown2022-89][^cite_note-Cornish2013-86][^cite_note-Casey2024-26] and [Russian intervention in the conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_intervention_in_the_Syrian_civil_war).[^cite_note-Syria2-94][^cite_note-95][^cite_note-Syria1-96][^cite_note-Cornish2013-86] PSL denies that [the Syrian government used chemical weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Use_of_chemical_weapons_in_the_Syrian_Civil_War).[^cite_note-97][^cite_note-Syria2-94]

PSL and Code Pink are on the coordinating committee for [Neville Roy Singham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neville_Roy_Singham)'s [International People's Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Peoples'_Assembly) (IPA). The International People's Assembly also includes Singham's thank tank Tricontinental. PSL coordinates with IPA, the People's Forum, Al-Awada, the Palestine Youth Movement and the [ANSWER coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.N.S.W.E.R.) as organizers of the Shut it Down for Palestine coalition.[^cite_note-98]

## Election results

PSL has fielded [electoral candidates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_the_United_States) for [local](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_in_the_United_States), [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_governments_of_the_United_States), and [federal](195149.md) offices. No PSL candidate has yet won an election.[^cite_note-Chen2024-74]

PSL candidates usually run on the [Peace and Freedom Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_and_Freedom_Party) ballot line or as [independent](2172378.md) candidates.
