**Zoila Milagro Navas Quintanilla** (born 22 July 1952) is a Salvadoran politician who has served as Mayor of [La Libertad Este](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Libertad_Este?action=edit\&redlink=1) since 2024. She previously served as mayor of [Antiguo Cuscatlán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiguo_Cuscatl%C3%A1n) from 1988 to 2024. She is a member of the [Nationalist Republican Alliance](542175.md).

## Biography

Zoila Milagro Navas Quintanilla was born on 22 July 1952 in [Antiguo Cuscatlán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiguo_Cuscatl%C3%A1n), El Salvador. Her parents were Héctor Navas and Esperanza Herrera. She attended the Hermanas Somascas Institute and later the Nuestra Señora de Fátima College. She graduated from the University of Nueva San Salvador with a degree in public relations and publication. She also later attended the [Central American University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_American_University,_San_Salvador).[^cite_note-1]

Navas became the mayor of [Antiguo Cuscatlán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiguo_Cuscatl%C3%A1n) in 1988 as a member of the [Nationalist Republican Alliance](542175.md) (ARENA). She has stated that ARENA founder [Roberto d'Aubuisson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_d'Aubuisson) was her mentor. Navas won re-election in every subsequent election.[^cite_note-2]

When the Salvadoran government consolidated El Salvador's 262 municipalities down to 44, Navas ran for mayor of La Libertad Este [in 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Salvadoran_legislative_election). She won the election, defeating [Nuevas Ideas](61703199.md) candidate [Michelle Sol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Sol). Navas was ARENA's only municipal candidate to win their election and she became the only opposition-aligned mayor.[^cite_note-3] In February 2026, Navas announced she was running for re-election [in 2027](76008090.md).[^cite_note-4]
