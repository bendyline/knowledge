# Ashiyane

**Ashyiane Forum** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): آشیانه meaning hangar) or Ashiyane Digital Security team was a hacker forum in Iran, founded by [Behrooz Kamalian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behrooz_Kamalian) in 2002.[^cite_note-1] It had direct ties to Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps. The forum was shut down in 2018. In 2021 it was back to cyberspace.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
