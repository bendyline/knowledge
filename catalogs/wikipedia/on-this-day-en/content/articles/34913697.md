**Boufarik Airport** ([ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **DAAK**) is a military airport near [Boufarik](4591244.md), [Algeria](358.md). It is the home base for the Air Transport fleet of the [Algerian Air Force](7927282.md).

## History

On 3 May 1943, [Charles de Gaulle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_de_Gaulle) landed at Boufarik airport, flying in from [Gibraltar](7607314.md). He would remain based in Algeria until mid-1944 and the [Liberation of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberation_of_France).[^cite_note-4]

On 11 April 2018, an [Ilyushin Il-76](229943.md) operated by the [Algerian Air Force](7927282.md) [crashed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Algerian_Air_Force_Il-76_crash) just after takeoff from this airport, killing about 257 passengers on board.
