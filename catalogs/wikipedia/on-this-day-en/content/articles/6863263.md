**Berestyn** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Берестин, IPA: [\[bereˈstɪn\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Ukrainian)), formerly known as **Krasnohrad** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Красноград, IPA: [\[krɐsnoˈɦrɑd\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Ukrainian) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/7/7f/LL-Q8798\_%28ukr%29-Gzhegozh-%D0%9A%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B3%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4.wav/LL-Q8798\_%28ukr%29-Gzhegozh-%D0%9A%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B3%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/7/7f/LL-Q8798_%28ukr%29-Gzhegozh-%D0%9A%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B3%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4.wav/LL-Q8798_%28ukr%29-Gzhegozh-%D0%9A%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B3%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) or **Krasnograd**,[^cite_note-1] is a [city](4077166.md) in [Kharkiv Oblast](494901.md), [Ukraine](31750.md). From 1784 to 1922, it was known as **Kostiantynohrad**.[^cite_note-2] It serves as the administrative center of [Berestyn Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berestyn_Raion). Berestyn hosts the administration of [Krasnohrad urban hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krasnohrad_urban_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), one of the [hromadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hromada) of Ukraine.[^cite_note-admreform_2020_krasnohrad-3] In 2022, the population was estimated to be 19,674 (2022 estimate).[^cite_note-ua2022estimate-4]

## History



Kr. Desiataja (Tenth Fortress) of the Ukrainian line

The city of Krasnohrad was founded as a [Bilevska fortress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilevska_fortress) in 1731–1733,[^cite_note-pereimenuvannya-krasnograda-5] as part of the [Ukrainian line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_line) defence fortifications, which ran from the [Dnieper](380138.md) to the [Donets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donets). The name Bilevska (Belyovskaya) is derived from an occupation force from [Belyov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belyov), which was used in the construction of the [Ukrainian line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_line).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

In 1784, the fortress received the name Konstantinograd (Kostiantynohrad) in honour of [Grand Duke Konstantin Pavlovich of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duke_Konstantin_Pavlovich_of_Russia).[^cite_note-pereimenuvannya-krasnograda-5] In 1797, it received city status. In 1922, Kostyantynohrad was renamed *Krasnohrad* during a "de-imperialization" drive carried out by the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union).[^cite_note-pereimenuvannya-krasnograda-5][^cite_note-8]

It is located on the [Berestova River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berestova_River?action=edit\&redlink=1) 101 kilometres (63 mi) to the south of the city of Kharkiv. In 2021, about 20,000 people lived in the city.[^cite_note-pereimenuvannya-krasnograda-5]

On 3 April 2024, the [Committee on the Organization of State Power, Local Self-government, Regional Development, and Urban Planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_the_Organization_of_State_Power,_Local_Self-government,_Regional_Development,_and_Urban_Planning?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the [Verkhovna Rada](844993.md) stated their support for renaming the city to Berestyn.[^cite_note-9] On 19 September 2024, the Verkhovna Rada voted to rename Krasnohrad to Berestyn.[^cite_note-10]
