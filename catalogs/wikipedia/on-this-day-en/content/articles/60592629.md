The **[Iranian](14653.md) [ambassador](68651.md) in [Islamabad](51153.md)** is the official representative of the Government in [Tehran](57654.md) to the Government of [Pakistan](23235.md). The ambassador is the head of the [Embassy of Iran in Islamabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Iran,_Islamabad?action=edit\&redlink=1) and is responsible for representing Iran's political, economic and cultural interests in Pakistan, as well as promoting bilateral relations between the two countries.
