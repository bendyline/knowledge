[*Taiyuan* (131)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_destroyer_Taiyuan), a [Type 052D (Luyang III-class)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_052D) guided-missile destroyer



[USS *Jack H. Lucas*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Jack_H._Lucas), an [Arleigh-Burke-class](197341.md) Flight III guided-missile destroyer

A **guided-missile destroyer** (**DDG**) is a [destroyer](8779.md) whose primary armament is [guided missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_missile) so they can provide [anti-aircraft warfare](146640.md) [screening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screening_\(tactical\)) for the fleet. Most modern destroyers are of the multi-purpose guided-missile type, and they are widely deployed by advanced navies; in particular, the [Arleigh Burke-class](197341.md) guided-missile destroyer is the most numerous class of warship in the [US Navy](20518076.md). The Chinese [People's Liberation Army Navy](24918.md) also maintains a large fleet of destroyers that includes [Type 055](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_055) and [Type 052D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_052D) class destroyers.

Guided-missile destroyers are equipped with large missile magazines, with modern examples typically having [vertical-launch cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_launching_system). Some contain integrated weapons systems, such as the United States’ [Aegis Combat System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aegis_Combat_System), and may be adopted for use in an [anti-missile or ballistic-missile defense](1099196.md) role. This is especially true for navies that no longer operate [cruisers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruiser), so other vessels must be adopted to fill in the gap.

Many guided-missile destroyers are also multipurpose vessels, equipped to carry out [anti-surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-surface_warfare) [operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Naval_tactics) with [surface-to-surface missiles](1483499.md) and [naval guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_gun), and [anti-submarine warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-submarine_warfare) with torpedoes and helicopters.

The NATO standard designation for these vessels is **DDG**, while destroyers which have a primary gun armament or a small number of anti-aircraft missiles sufficient only for point-defense are designated **DD**. Nations vary in their use of destroyer **D** designation in their hull pennant numbering, either prefixing or dropping it altogether.
