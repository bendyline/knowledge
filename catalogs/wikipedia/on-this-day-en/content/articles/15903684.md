The **president of Republika Srpska** ([Serbian Cyrillic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Cyrillic_alphabet): Предсједник Републике Српске, [Serbian Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaj's_Latin_alphabet): *_Predsjednik Republike Srpske_*) is the highest [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) authority in [Republika Srpska](347422.md), an [entity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_divisions_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) in [Bosnia and Herzegovina](3463.md). It is one of the executive authorities, along with the [Government of Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Republika_Srpska). The president of Republika Srpska is [directly elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Republika_Srpska) for a term of four years, along with two [vice presidents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Vice-Presidents_of_Republika_Srpska) from different [constituent nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_groups_in_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) ([Serbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbs_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina), [Bosniaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosniaks) and [Croats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croats_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina)). None of them can be from a same constituent nation at the same time. The president's residence is in [Banja Luka](385109.md).

The first president was [Radovan Karadžić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radovan_Karad%C5%BEi%C4%87) of the [Serb Democratic Party](413319.md), elected to the post in 1992, who led Republika Srpska during the [Bosnian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnian_War) and who [was later sentenced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trial_of_Radovan_Karad%C5%BEi%C4%87) to 40 years in prison for [Srebrenica genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srebrenica_massacre), [war crimes](48626.md) and [crimes against humanity](61099.md).[^cite_note-nyt24-03-2016-1] [Siniša Karan](81170412.md) of the [Alliance of Independent Social Democrats](4358875.md) took office in 2026.

## Powers

According to the [Constitution of Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Republika_Srpska), the president represents Republika Srpska and expresses its unity. As Republika Srpska functions as a [parliamentary system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system), the president has very limited powers. The president does have the power to:[^cite_note-Constitution-2]

1. Nominate a candidate for [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Republika_Srpska) to the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Republika_Srpska\));

2. Nominate candidates for president and judges of the [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Republika_Srpska), on a proposal of the [High Judicial and Prosecutorial Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Judicial_and_Prosecutorial_Council), to the National Assembly;

3. Declares laws by a [decree](322949.md);

4. Gives [pardons](288593.md);

5. Gives [decorations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders,_decorations,_and_medals_of_Republika_Srpska) and recognitions as determined by a law;

6. Manages other duties according to the Constitution.

If the National Assembly is unable to make a session due to a state of emergency, the president, after obtaining an opinion from the Government, declares a state of emergency and orders measures for removing the state of emergency. The president, by his own initiative or on Government's suggestion, declares acts from the jurisdiction of the National Assembly during war circumstances or an immediate threat of war; the acts must be brought for a confirmation by the National Assembly the very moment when the Assembly is able to have a session.[^cite_note-Constitution-2]

The president can ask the Government to express its attitude on certain matters of the significance for the Republic, call a session of the Government, or put matters from Government's jurisdiction on an agenda.[^cite_note-Constitution-2]

## Latest election

Main article: [2025–26 Republika Srpska presidential election](80899584.md)

Karan of the SNSD narrowly won the election, securing some 50.5% of the vote.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] He secured victory in 45 cities and municipalities,[^cite_note-6] out of 64[^cite_note-7] including three municipalities out of six in [Istočno Sarajevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isto%C4%8Dno_Sarajevo). Conversely, Blanuša won 19 polities, including Banja Luka, Republika Srpska's *de facto* capital and largest city.[^cite_note-8] Additionally, Blanuša won in [Bijeljina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bijeljina), the entity's second-largest city,[^cite_note-9] and initially secured a narrow victory in [Prijedor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prijedor), the third-largest one;[^cite_note-10] however, it eventually voted for Karan in the repeat election.[^cite_note-11]
