**Pierre el-Rahi** (sometimes spelled **Pierre al-Rahi**) ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بيار الراعي, [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Pierre el-Raï*; 1975 – 9 March 2026) was a [Lebanese Maronite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Maronite) [Catholic priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_priest) who was [killed](82631603.md) by Israeli tank fire during the [2026 Lebanon war](82549840.md).[^cite_note-osv-1][^cite_note-2]

## Biography

Born in 1975 in the village of Dibeh in northern Lebanon,[^cite_note-3] El-Rahi was originally from [Debel](9814180.md) in the south of Lebanon. He became [curate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curate) of the Paroisse Saint-Georges in [Al-Qlayaa](51590325.md) in 2013, his ministry located in the [Marjayoun District](8968039.md), just north of the Israeli border.[^cite_note-4]

In December 2023, he appeared in the press urging Christian residents of Al-Qlayaa to continue celebrating Christmas in spite of the [Hezbollah–Israel conflict](75005425.md).[^cite_note-5] In October 2024, he defended the rights of residents to remain on their own land amidst evacuation orders, citing the village's attempt to avoid any form of armed conflict.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

The following month, he continued to encourage residents to stay home despite the approaching warfare.[^cite_note-8] In early March 2025, he received a delegation from the [Kataeb Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kataeb_Party), which met with several officials in southern Lebanese villages.[^cite_note-9] On 20 October 2025, he was received at [Baabda Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baabda_Palace) by President [Joseph Aoun](53467128.md), discussing religious issues and the safety of the people of Al-Qlayaa.[^cite_note-10]

## Death

Main article: [Killing of Pierre al-Rahi](82631603.md)

On 9 March 2026, el-Rahi rushed to a family's house in Al-Qlayaa to render aid, when he himself was fatally struck by a second shelling.[^cite_note-11] His death was denounced by the [Lebanese Forces](2113218.md) as a casualty of a war "imported into Lebanon and Qlayaa" and "waged by outlaws".[^cite_note-12]
