The **National Intelligence Agency** (NIA) is a Nigerian government division tasked with overseeing foreign intelligence and counterintelligence operations.

## History

Fulfilling one of the promises made in his first national address as president, in June 1986, [Ibrahim Babangida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Babangida) issued Decree Number 19, dissolving the [National Security Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Organization) (NSO) and restructuring Nigeria's security services into three separate entities under the Office of the Co-ordinator of National Security.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

- [State Security Service](2347197.md) (SSS) – Responsible for domestic intelligence

- National Intelligence Agency (NIA) – Responsible for foreign intelligence and counterintelligence operations

- [Defence Intelligence Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Intelligence_Agency_\(Nigeria\)) (DIA) – Responsible for military intelligence.
