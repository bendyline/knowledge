**La Romana** is a municipality and capital of the southeastern [La Romana Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Romana_Province), opposite [Catalina Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalina_Island,_Dominican_Republic). It is one of the 10 largest cities in the [Dominican Republic](8060.md) with a population estimated in 2022 at 153,241 within the city limits ([metropolitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_area) population: 270,000), of whom 149,840 are urban and 3,401 are rural. The name *Romana* comes from the word "Bomana", a name given by Indians to what is known today as Romana River.

The modern [La Romana International Airport](4155598.md) was opened in 2000. The city is near several other cities, such as [San Pedro de Macorís](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro_de_Macor%C3%ADs) and the national capital, [Santo Domingo de Guzmán](57638.md). The city is a hub for a growing tourist industry with several nearby local resort spots, such as the beachfront [Bayahibe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayahibe), Dominicus, Casa de Campo, and the growing number of golf resorts that surround the area.

## History

The city of La Romana was officially founded in 1897 as an oil town. After 1917 with the construction of a large sugar-mill the economy quickly shifted to sugar production. The commissioning of the sugar mill coincided with the rise in sugar prices worldwide, prompting the [sugar industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_industry) to welcome workers from different parts of the country as well as other parts of the [Caribbean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocolo), many families moved to La Romana for economic opportunities.

In early 1960, [Gulf and Western Industries, Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_and_Western_Industries) purchased the sugar mill and started to invest in the [livestock industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livestock_industry) which was cemented in the province. Meanwhile, \$20 million were invested to rebuild La Romana and build schools, clinics, housing and other infrastructure for workers. During the mid-1970s the American conglomerate began selling its Dominican assets, and at the same time built what is now one of the largest exclusive [tourist resorts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourist_resort) in the Dominican Republic, [Casa de Campo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_de_Campo,_Dominican_Republic).

## Economy

La Romana has historically been a [company town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Company_town), with the [Central Romana Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Romana_Corporation) owning the majority of the town. It is a town with nearly 100% employment, mostly in the tourism industry or with The Central Romana Corporation, the Duty Free Zone ([Zona Franca Romana](https://web.archive.org/web/20080905142109/http://www.zfr.com.do/)), or one of the service businesses there.

La Romana has been a one-company town since the [South Puerto Rico Sugar Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Puerto_Rico_Sugar_Company) built the mammoth Central Romana mill in 1917. It was the only sugar operation not taken over by [Rafael Trujillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Trujillo) during his reign. From 1964 to 1967, the South Puerto Rico Sugar Company, including properties in Florida, Puerto Rico and the Dominican Republic became part of the conglomerate [Gulf+Western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf+Western). In 1984, the Gulf+Western Corporation sold their stake in the Central Romana Corporation to a group of local and foreign investors which included the [Fanjul brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanjul_brothers).

Gulf+Western acquired Consolidated Cigar in 1968 and shifted the Canary Island cigar-making operation to its Tabacalera de Garcia tobacco plant in La Romana. The Tabacalera de Garcia factory is currently one of the largest cigar factories in the world, and has been owned by the biggest cigar marketing company in the world, [Altadis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altadis), since 1999. Three world-famous brands are produced in La Romana: [Montecristo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montecristo_\(cigar\)), [H. Upmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._Upmann), and [Romeo y Julieta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romeo_y_Julieta_\(cigar\)).

## Tourism



Altos de Chavón Amphitheater.

[Casa de Campo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_de_Campo,_Dominican_Republic) resort complex was built in 1975 by [Gulf+Western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf+Western). In 1984 Casa de Campo was purchased by the Central Romana Corporation, which is co-owned by the [Fanjul brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanjul_brothers).



Chavon river in La Romana, Dominican Republic.

[Altos de Chavón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altos_de_Chav%C3%B3n) is a reconstruction of a 16th-century Mediterranean village located just minutes from La Romana.

[Casa de Campo International Tourist Port (Muelle Turístico Internacional Casa de Campo)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Romana_Port), located on the West Bank of La Romana River or *Rio Dulce*, has been utilized primarily for the docking of commercial ships, primarily for the transport of sugar and molasses. Upon exceeding the capacity of its port, the Central Romana Corporation built its new tourism focused port on the east side of the river. The platform on the western bank was renovated and the river channel was dredged to a depth of 10.50 metres (34.4 ft).

Inaugurated with the arrival of the vessel *[Costa Marina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Marina)* in December 2002, the Central Romana Corporation invested US\$12 million to extend the existing port by over 40,000 square metres (430,000 sq ft). The port is a modern platform and harbor terminal, with a capacity for two large modern cruise ships. Today the new facilities boast two docking platforms (East Dock: 255 metres (837 ft) in length, West Dock: 225 metres (738 ft) in length), a 1,000 square metres (11,000 sq ft) passenger terminal and parking facilities which accommodate 24 buses.



La Romana airport.

[La Romana International Airport](4155598.md) (IATA: LRM, ICAO: MDLR) is a private commercial airport in the east of the Dominican Republic, serving the town of La Romana and the resort of Casa de Campo. It was built with open-air terminals. It currently ranks fifth in air traffic, behind Punta Cana, Las Américas, Puerto Plata and Santiago. The airport has 7 positions in the international terminal and a spacious private aviation terminal where up to 12 private jets can be parked.

### Safety

The [United States State Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_State_Department) in 2024 issued a Level 2 Advisory as a travel advisory to visitors to the Dominican Republic, for tourists to "exercise increased caution."[^cite_note-4] This was a renewal of the DOS statement issued in 2023, noting that DR "is generally safe to those sticking to popular resort areas like Punta Cana and La Romana but remains a risk due to instances of 'violent crime, including armed robbery, homicide and sexual assault' in the country overall."[^cite_note-5] The biggest risk in La Romana was [theft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theft), as of 2022,[^cite_note-6] through 2024, more generally of [property crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Property_crime).[^cite_note-7] "Avoid visiting dark, secluded places alone, and avoid beaches at night. Be sure to choose taxis carefully," advised the *[Miami Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Herald)* in 2023 to tourists who are traveling to La Romana and other popular cruise destinations.[^cite_note-8]

## Climate

La Romana has a [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Aw*) only slightly above a [hot semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-arid_climate#Hot_semi-arid_climates) (*BSh*) due to its shielded location from the northeasterly [trade winds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_winds).

## Sports

[Estadio Francisco Micheli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_Francisco_Micheli) is home to the [Toros del Este](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toros_del_Este), a [baseball](3850.md) team in the [Dominican Winter League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Winter_League).

In 1983, the government of [Salvador Jorge Blanco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvador_Jorge_Blanco), through the fund for development of this region, built the *Polideportivo de La Romana* (La Romana Sports Center), which was named after [Eleoncio Mercedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleoncio_Mercedes) in honor of the flyweight boxer who became world champion. The Cañeros de La Romana, a Dominican [basketball](3921.md) team, play their home games here.
