**Kyle MacLean Kirkwood** (born October 19, 1998) is an American [auto racing](1022.md) driver.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] He competes in the [IndyCar Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndyCar_Series), driving the No. 27 Dallara-Honda for [Andretti Global](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andretti_Global).

Kirkwood has won some notable IndyCar Series races including the inaugural runnings of the [Freedom 250 Grand Prix](82275853.md) and the [Java House Grand Prix of Arlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Prix_of_Arlington), as well as a two-time winner of the [Acura Grand Prix of Long Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Prix_of_Long_Beach).

## Career

### Karting

Born in [Jupiter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jupiter,_Florida), Florida, Kirkwood began karting at a young age, often competing alongside fellow Jupiter native [Oliver Askew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Askew).[^cite_note-3] Throughout his karting career, he amassed multiple titles, including the AM Engines Formula Tag Junior championship in 2012, the [SKUSA SuperNationals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SKUSA_SuperNationals) XVII - S5 Junior championship in 2013, and back-to-back wins at the Florida Winter Tour in 2015 and 2016.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

### Lower formula

In 2015, Kirkwood made his single-seater debut in the F1600 Championship with Chastain Motorsports.[^cite_note-7] He took victory in the season opener at [Road Atlanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_Atlanta) and contested a further six rounds to finish sixteenth.[^cite_note-8]

Following his performance in Formula 4 United States, Kirkwood returned to the Formula Ford scene in 2016 to represent Team USA in the Formula Ford Festival and [Walter Hayes Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Hayes_Trophy) event, finishing seventh and fourth overall in both respective events.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

In 2016, Kirkwood joined the [inaugural Formula 4 United States Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Formula_4_United_States_Championship) with Primus Racing, which saw him claim a win at [Mid-Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid-Ohio_Sports_Car_Course) and finish third in the overall standings .[^cite_note-13] In September 2016, Kirkwood was awarded with the Team USA scholarship.[^cite_note-14]

After competing with them in pre-season testing at [NOLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NOLA_Motorsports_Park), Kirkwood returned to the championship the [following year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Formula_4_United_States_Championship), switching to newcomers Cape Motorsports.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] He achieved nine victories, including back-to-back hat-tricks at [Indianapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_Motor_Speedway) and [Motorsport Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Tire_Motorsport_Park), wrapping up the title in the [Formula One](10854.md) supporting season finale at the [Circuit of the Americas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_of_the_Americas).[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] During the season, Kirkwood was once again selected as a candidate for the Team USA scholarship.[^cite_note-20]

### International Formula 3

In August 2018, Kirkwood joined [Abel Motorsports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abel_Motorsports) to contest the [inaugural season of F3 Americas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_F3_Americas_Championship).[^cite_note-21] The season saw him claim fifteen of the seventeen race victories on offer and seal his third consecutive championship at NOLA with a round to spare.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

The following year, Kirkwood made a one-off appearance with [RP Motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RP_Motorsport) in the season finale of the [Euroformula Open Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Euroformula_Open_Championship) at [Monza](952083.md), which saw him take a best-placed finish of sixth in race two.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

### Formula E

In February 2020, Kirkwood was announced to be participating in the rookie test at Marrakesh with [BMW i Andretti Motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andretti_Autosport).[^cite_note-26]

### Road to Indy

In December 2016, Kirkwood engaged in the [Mazda Road to Indy Shootout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Mazda_Road_to_Indy_Shootout), but lost out on the scholarship to Askew.[^cite_note-27]

#### USF2000

In 2017, Kirkwood partook in the [Chris Griffis Memorial Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Griffis_Memorial_Test) with Swan-RJB Motorsports.[^cite_note-28] In February 2018, he reunited with Cape Motorsports to contest the [2018 championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_U.S._F2000_National_Championship).[^cite_note-29] In his rookie season, Kirkwood dominated proceedings, taking twelve of the fourteen victories and sealed his second title in a year at [Mid-Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid-Ohio_Sports_Car_Course) which matched [J. R. Hildebrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._R._Hildebrand)'s record of wins across a season.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31]

#### Indy Pro 2000

Kirkwood graduated to the championship in 2019, selecting RP Motorsport Racing as his team.[^cite_note-32] After faltering in earlier rounds, Kirkwood took his maiden victories in both races at [Road America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_America).[^cite_note-33] From the second race at [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Indy_Toronto), Kirkwood dominated the season and won his fourth consecutive title at the season finale in [Laguna Seca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WeatherTech_Raceway_Laguna_Seca) despite being taken out by [Danial Frost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danial_Frost).[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]

#### Indy Lights

In December 2019, Kirkwood participated in the post-season test at [Sebring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebring_International_Raceway) with reigning champions [Andretti Autosport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andretti_Autosport).[^cite_note-37] The following month, Andretti confirmed Kirkwood would race with them for the [2020 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Indy_Lights). Unfortunately, that year's running of the championship was canceled due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-38]

Kirkwood won the 2021 Indy Lights Championship for Andretti Autosport. In doing so, he became the first driver to win a championship by winning all three steps on the Road to Indy in the Indy series.[^cite_note-39]

### IndyCar



Kirkwood after crashing during the [2022 XPEL 375](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_XPEL_375)

Throughout [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_IndyCar_Series), Kirkwood was coveted by different teams in the IndyCar Series paddock. Andretti Autosport owner [Michael Andretti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Andretti) stated he intended to keep Kirkwood in the Andretti Autosport stable and reiterated this after Kirkwood won the Indy Lights championship.[^cite_note-40] Andretti stated that he was considering moving Kirkwood to [Formula E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_E) if a seat did not open up in IndyCar.

As part of winning the Indy Lights championship, Kirkwood was guaranteed to compete in three IndyCar races in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_IndyCar_Series), including the [2022 Indianapolis 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Indianapolis_500).

On November 10, 2021, it was announced that Kirkwood would drive the No. 14 entry, full-time, for [A. J. Foyt Enterprises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._J._Foyt_Enterprises) during the 2022 IndyCar Series season.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42] He had a best finish of tenth at Long Beach, and ranked 24th in points.



Kirkwood during the [2023 Indianapolis 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Indianapolis_500)

On June 1, 2022, Kirkwood and Andretti Autosport announced he would return to the Andretti stable for the 2023 season to replace the departing [Alexander Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Rossi).[^cite_note-43] Kirkwood took his first IndyCar win at the [2023 Acura Grand Prix of Long Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Acura_Grand_Prix_of_Long_Beach). Kirkwood took a second win at the [2023 Big Machine Music City Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Big_Machine_Music_City_Grand_Prix).

The [2024 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_IndyCar_Series) saw Kirkwood's season end up without a win, but he was competitive in many races and ultimately finished seventh in the overall championship. In [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_IndyCar_Series), Kirkwood registered three wins which included races at [Long Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Acura_Grand_Prix_of_Long_Beach), [Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Chevrolet_Detroit_Grand_Prix), and [Gateway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Bommarito_Automotive_Group_500). For his efforts, he finished fourth in the championship standings in 2025. The [next year in 2026](80635394.md), he won the inaugural runnings of the [Java House Grand Prix of Arlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Java_House_Grand_Prix_of_Arlington) and the [Freedom 250 Grand Prix](82275853.md).

### Sports car racing

With the [2020 Indy Lights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Indy_Lights) season cancelled due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), Kirkwood drove four races in the [IMSA Prototype Challenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_IMSA_Prototype_Challenge) and also joined [AIM Vasser Sullivan Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasser_Sullivan_Racing) for a limited GTD schedule.[^cite_note-44]
