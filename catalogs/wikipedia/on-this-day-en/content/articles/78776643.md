The **Internal Security Forces** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): قوى الأمن الداخلي, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Quwā al-ʾAmn ad-Dāḵilī*; abbr.: **ISF**)[^cite_note-2] are the national [police service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police) and [internal security force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestic_security) of [Syria](7515849.md).&#x20;

It is primarily responsible for [law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_in_Syria), protecting civilians and properties alongside investigating crimes with assistance from the [General Intelligence Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Intelligence_Service_\(Syria\)), and for drug-related cases, the [Drug Enforcement Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_Enforcement_Department_\(Syria\)). It also performs other routine police functions, including traffic control. It is administered and controlled by the [Ministry of Interior](34679004.md).

## History

Main article: [Law enforcement in Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_in_Syria)



Logo from 2019 to 2025

The Internal Security Forces, or known as the General Security Service at the time, was the police force of [Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham](53015755.md) (HTS), which controlled areas in Northern Syria, replacing the Free Syrian Police before it was transferred to the interior ministry of the [Syrian Salvation Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Salvation_Government) in April 2024.[^cite_note-mee-20190116-1][^cite_note-3] Its roots could be traced to 1945 when the [Public Security Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Security_Police_\(Syria\)) was formed. A [police academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_academy) was opened in September 2023 and produced its first batch of graduates in August 2024.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

The Ba'athist-led Public Security Police collapsed in 2024 with the [fall of the Assad regime](78548066.md). HTS brought the General Security Service from Idlib to maintain order and security in recently captured areas in the country. In January 2025, the new de facto rulers of Syria, the [Syrian caretaker government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_caretaker_government), made preparations to reorganize the General Security Service as Syria's police force, including [Sharia](28840.md) as part of the police training.[^cite_note-reuters23jan-6][^cite_note-7]

On 29 November 2025, the Syrian Ministry of Interior presented several new police vehicles. The vehicles, all [Hyundai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai) models, include:

- Armoured variants assigned to the Diplomatic Security and Officials Protection units;

- Vehicles for the Criminal Investigations Department;

- Patrol cars for the Road Security (traffic police) branch.

  [^cite_note-8]

On 30 November 2025, the Syrian Arab News Agency published images of several new vehicle designs for the Ministry of Interior's vehicles, including the vehicles of the Internal Security Forces.[^cite_note-9]

## Incidents

### Involvement in the 2025 massacres of Alawites

In March 2025, members of the at the time General Security Service were accused of taking part in the [2025 massacres](79404646.md) against [Alawite](436616.md) civilians along Syria's coastal regions.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-chainofcommand-11][^cite_note-UNHRC_west_central_SY_JanMar2025-12]<sup>: 44, 49, 50 </sup>

### Death of Mohammed Hossam al-Din Ghamira

On August 2026, Mohammed Hossam al-Din Ghamira was pronounced dead at Latakia University Hospital due to his injuries and complications as a result of [haemophilia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haemophilia) after he was beaten by several police officers whilst under arrest at a police station in [Al-Haffah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Haffah), [Latakia](7445289.md).[^cite_note-13] According to both Interior Minister [Anas Khattab](78730407.md) and [Minister of Emergency and Disaster Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Emergency_and_Disaster_Management) [Raed al-Saleh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raed_al-Saleh), the officers responsible for his death were later arrested for further investigation.
