The **Parliament of Algeria** is the legislative assembly of [Algeria](358.md). It consists of two chambers:[^cite_note-alge_Emba-1]

- The [Council of the Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_the_Nation) ([Upper Chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_house))

- The [People's National Assembly](2806769.md) ([Lower Chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house))
