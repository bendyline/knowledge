The original **2 World Trade Center** (also known as the **South Tower**, **Tower 2**, **Building Two**, or **2 WTC**) was one of the [Twin Towers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Towers) of the original [World Trade Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Center_\(1973%E2%80%932001\)) in [New York City](645042.md). The tower was completed and opened in 1973 at a height of 1,362 feet (415 m) to the roof, distinguishable from its twin, the [North Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_World_Trade_Center_\(1970%E2%80%932001\)) (1 World Trade Center), by the absence of a television antenna. On the 107th floor of this building was a popular tourist attraction, "Top of the World Trade Center Observatories," and on the roof was an outdoor [observation deck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observation_deck) accessible to the public and a disused [helipad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helipad) at the center.[^cite_note-4] The WTC complex had its own [ZIP Code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZIP_Code) of [10048](67173.md#Exclusive_ZIP_Code).

The South Tower was destroyed along with the North Tower in the [September 11 attacks](5058690.md). At 9\:03 a.m.,[^cite_note-Time-9] seventeen minutes after its twin was hit, the South Tower was struck by [United Airlines Flight 175](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Airlines_Flight_175). Although it was the second of the two skyscrapers to be hit by a hijacked airliner, it was the first to [collapse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collapse_of_the_World_Trade_Center), at 9\:59 a.m.,[^cite_note-12] after burning for 56 minutes. Of the 2,996 people killed in the attacks, around 1,000 were in the South Tower or on the ground. At the [National September 11 Memorial & Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_September_11_Memorial_&_Museum), the southern pool marks the spot where the South Tower stood.

## History

### Development

In 1961, the [Port Authority of New York and New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Authority_of_New_York_and_New_Jersey) agreed to build the [World Trade Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Center_\(1973%E2%80%932001\)) on the site of the [Hudson Terminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Terminal) in [Lower Manhattan](455646.md), New York City.[^cite_note-nyt-1961dec29-13] On September 20, 1962, the Port Authority announced the selection of [Minoru Yamasaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minoru_Yamasaki) as lead architect and [Emery Roth & Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emery_Roth) as associate architects.[^cite_note-\:0-14] Yamasaki devised the plan to incorporate twin towers. His original plan called for the towers to be 80 stories tall,[^cite_note-nyt-1964jan19a-15] but to meet the Port Authority's requirement for 10,000,000 square feet (930,000 m<sup>2</sup>) of office space, the buildings would each have to be 110 stories tall.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGillespie199949-16] Yamasaki's design for the World Trade Center, unveiled to the public on January 18, 1964, called for a square plan approximately 207 feet (63 m) in dimension on each side.[^cite_note-nyt-1964jan19a-15][^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–120057-17]

In March 1965, the Port Authority began acquiring property at the World Trade Center site.[^cite_note-nyt_19650329-18] Demolition work began on March 21, 1966,[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGillespie199961-19] and groundbreaking for the construction of the World Trade Center took place on August 5, 1966.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWorld_Trade_Center_Building_Performance_Study20021.2-20] In January 1967, the Port Authority awarded \$74 million in contracts to various steel suppliers.[^cite_note-nyt-1967jan24-21] Construction on the South Tower was under way by January 1969.[^cite_note-pbstimeline-22] The [topping out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topping_out) ceremony for 2 WTC (the South Tower) occurred on July 19, 1971.[^cite_note-pbstimeline-22] The South Tower began accepting tenants in January 1972,[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–12005xxxvi-23] and a ribbon cutting ceremony took place on April 4, 1973.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGillespie1999134-24]

### Operation

In 1981, the Port Authority announced a \$45 million plan to install sprinklers throughout the World Trade Center, following [a major fire that occurred at the North Tower in 1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975_World_Trade_Center_fire).[^cite_note-25]

On February 26, 1993, a [Ryder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryder) truck filled with 1,500 pounds (680 kg) of explosives (planted by [Ramzi Yousef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramzi_Yousef)) detonated in the North Tower's underground garage.[^cite_note-reeve_p10-26] According to a presiding judge, the conspirators' chief aim at the time of the attack was to destabilize the North Tower and send it crashing into the South Tower, toppling both skyscrapers.[^cite_note-27] Six people were killed and 1,042 others were injured in the attacks.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-barbanel-29]

In February 2001, the Port Authority leased the entire World Trade Center complex to [Vornado Realty Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vornado_Realty_Trust).[^cite_note-30] However, Vornado insisted on last minute changes to the deal,[^cite_note-31] and the next-highest bidder, [Silverstein Properties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silverstein_Properties), signed a lease for the complex on July 24, 2001.[^cite_note-32]

### Top of the World observation



A 360° panoramic view of New York City and surrounding areas from the Top of the World observation deck, circa August 2001. North is near center and the North Tower is left.



Visitors on the viewing platform on the South Tower's roof, looking north toward [Midtown Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midtown_Manhattan) in 1984

Although most of the space in the World Trade Center complex was off-limits to the public, the South Tower featured a public glass-enclosed observation deck on the 107th floor called *Top of the World* and an open-air deck with the height of 110 stories.[^cite_note-experience-33][^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–12005158-34][^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1200558-35] The observation deck opened in December 1975 and operated from 9\:30 a.m. to 11\:30 p.m. (June to August) and from 9\:30 a.m. to 9\:30 p.m. (September to May).[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-tickets-37] After paying an entrance fee in the second floor, visitors were required to pass through security checks added after the [1993 World Trade Center bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_World_Trade_Center_bombing).[^cite_note-38] They were then sent to the 107th-floor indoor observatory at a height of 1,310 feet (400 m) by a dedicated express elevator, which could be only accessed by entering the core.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–12005158-34] The exterior columns were narrowed to allow 28 inches of window width between them. In 1995, the Port Authority leased operation of the observatory to Ogden Entertainment, which decided to renovate it.[^cite_note-39] On April 30, 1997, the Top of the World tour reopened after renovations were finished.[^cite_note-40] Dellmont Leisure Design, a [La Crescenta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Crescenta-Montrose,_California) firm led by former [Disney Imagineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walt_Disney_Imagineering) David Schweninger, carried out the renovations.[^cite_note-41] Attractions added to the observation deck included 24 video monitors, which provided descriptions of 44 points of interest in six languages; a theater showing a film of a simulated helicopter tour around the city called "Manhattan Magic";[^cite_note-visitors-42][^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–12005158-34][^cite_note-43] a model of Manhattan with 750 buildings; a [Kodak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodak) photo booth and two gift shops.[^cite_note-visitors-42] The 107th-floor also featured a [subway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_Subway)-themed food court that featured [Sbarro Street Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sbarro) and [Nathan's Famous Hot Dogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan's_Famous) with a dining area that simulated [Central Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Park).[^cite_note-mcdowell2-44][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEDarton1999152-45]

Weather permitting, visitors could ride two short escalators up from the 107th-floor viewing area to an outdoor platform at a height of 1,377 ft (420 m).[^cite_note-amenitiesframe-46][^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–12005158-34][^cite_note-47] On a clear day, visitors could see up to 50 miles (80 km).[^cite_note-mcdowell2-44] An anti-suicide fence was placed on the roof itself, with the viewing platform set back and elevated above it, requiring only an ordinary railing. This left the view unobstructed, unlike the observation deck of the [Empire State Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_State_Building).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEDarton1999152-45]

### Destruction

Main articles: [September 11 attacks](5058690.md) and [Collapse of the World Trade Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collapse_of_the_World_Trade_Center)

See also: [Casualties of the September 11 attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casualties_of_the_September_11_attacks)



[United Airlines Flight 175](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Airlines_Flight_175) hits 2 World Trade Center in the [September 11 attacks](5058690.md).

At 9\:03 a.m. EDT on [September 11, 2001](5058690.md), five terrorists crashed [United Airlines Flight 175](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Airlines_Flight_175) into the southern face of the South Tower.[^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49] Three buildings in the World Trade Center complex, including 2 WTC, [collapsed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collapse_of_the_World_Trade_Center) due to fire-induced structural failure.[^cite_note-WPCollapse-50] The South Tower collapsed at 9\:59 a.m. after burning for 56 minutes in the fire caused by the impact of United Airlines Flight 175 and the explosion of its fuel.[^cite_note-WPCollapse-50]

The light construction and hollow nature of the structures allowed the jet fuel to penetrate far inside the towers, igniting many large fires simultaneously over a wide area of the impacted floors. The fuel from the planes burned at most for a few minutes, but the contents of the buildings burned over the next hour to hour and a half.[^cite_note-51] The fires might not have been as centrally positioned, nor as intense, had traditionally heavy high-rise construction been standing in the way of the aircraft, as debris and fuel would likely have remained mostly outside the buildings or concentrated in more peripheral areas away from the building cores, which would then not have become unique failure points. In this scenario, the towers might have stood far longer, perhaps indefinitely.[^cite_note-Gross-52][^cite_note-Wilkinson-53] The fires were hot enough to weaken the columns and cause floors to sag, pulling perimeter columns inward and reducing their ability to support the mass of the building above.[^cite_note-54]

## Architecture

[Minoru Yamasaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minoru_Yamasaki) was the lead architect for the tower, and [Emery Roth & Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emery_Roth) were the associate architects.[^cite_note-\:0-14] During the World Trade Center's construction, the structural engineers ended up following draft versions of New York City's 1968 [building codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Building_code), which incorporated "advanced techniques" in [building design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Building_design).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–12005xxxviii-55] The Twin Towers used a tube-frame design, which required 40 percent less structural steel than conventional building designs.[^cite_note-steel2-56] The structures were inspired by the architectural ethic of [Le Corbusier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Corbusier) and was the seminal expression of Yamasaki's gothic modernist tendencies.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEDarton199932–34-57] Yamasaki was also inspired by [Islamic architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_architecture), elements of which he incorporated in the buildings' design, having previously designed Saudi Arabia's [Dhahran International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhahran_International_Airport) with the [Saudi Binladin Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Binladin_Group).[^cite_note-Grudin2010-58][^cite_note-Kerr_2001-59]

When completed in 1973, the South Tower became the second tallest building in the world at 1,362 feet (415 m), behind the North Tower. Its rooftop observation deck was 1,362 ft (415 m) high and its indoor observation deck was 1,310 ft (400 m) high.[^cite_note-mcdowell-60] Each tower stood over 1,350 feet (410 m) high, and occupied about 1 acre (4,000 m<sup>2</sup>) of the total 16 acres (65,000 m<sup>2</sup>) of the site's land.[^cite_note-61]

### Facade

The Twin Towers' facades were made of high-strength, load-bearing perimeter steel columns which acted as [Vierendeel trusses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truss#Vierendeel_Truss).[^cite_note-62][^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_120056-63] Although the columns themselves were lightweight, they were spaced closely together, forming a strong, rigid wall structure.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGoldberger200426-64][^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_120055–6-65] There were 59 perimeter columns, narrowly spaced, on each side of the building.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–120058-66][^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_120056-63] In all, the perimeter walls measured 210 feet (64 m) long on each side, and the corners were [beveled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bevel).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–120059-67][^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_120055–6-65] The perimeter structure was constructed of prefabricated modular pieces connected by [spandrel](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/spandrel) plates.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_120055–6-65] From the 7th floor to the ground level, and down to the foundation, the columns were spaced 10 feet (3.0 m) apart to accommodate doorways.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–1200510-68][^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_120056-63] All columns were placed on [bedrock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedrock) 65–85 feet (20–26 m) below the surface.[^cite_note-69]

### Structural features

Further information: [Construction of the World Trade Center § Design of the towers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Construction_of_the_World_Trade_Center#Design_of_the_towers)

The building's core housed the elevator and utility shafts, restrooms, three stairwells, and other support spaces. The core of each tower was a rectangular area 87 by 135 feet (27 by 41 m), and contained 47 steel columns running from the bedrock to the top of the tower.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_120058-70] The South Tower's structural core was oriented with the long axis north to south.[^cite_note-NIST_NCSTAR_1-6D_p._285-71] The core columns supported about half the towers' weight.[^cite_note-NIST_NCSTAR_1-6D_p._285-71] All elevators were located in the core. Each building had three stairwells, also in the core, except on the [mechanical floors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_floor).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–120058-66] The large, column-free space between the perimeter and core was bridged by prefabricated floor trusses, which connected to the perimeter columns.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1200510-72]

Hat [trusses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truss) (or "outrigger truss") located from the 107th floor to the top of the North and South towers were designed to support a tall communication [antenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antenna_\(radio\)) on top of each building.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1200510-72] The South Tower never had an antenna fitted.[^cite_note-73] The framed-tube design using steel core and perimeter columns protected with sprayed-on fire resistant material created a relatively lightweight structure that would sway more in response to the wind.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEGlanzLipton2003138-74] In designing the World Trade Center, Leslie Robertson considered the scenario of the impact of a jet airliner crashing into the building.[^cite_note-Robertson-75] The [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology) (NIST) found a three-page [white paper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_paper) that mentioned another aircraft impact analysis, involving impact of a jet at 600 mph (970 km/h), was indeed considered, but NIST could not locate the documentary evidence of the aircraft impact analysis.[^cite_note-76]

Sprayed-fire resistant materials (SFRMs), [gypsum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gypsum) wallboard, and [vermiculite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermiculite) were used to provide fireproofing to the interiors.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–120058-66] More fireproofing was added after a fire in February 1975,[^cite_note-FEMA-77] but after the [1993 bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_World_Trade_Center_bombing), inspections found fireproofing to be deficient.[^cite_note-78] The 1968 New York City building codes did not require [sprinklers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_sprinkler) for high-rise buildings, except for underground spaces,[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–12005162-79] but the entire complex was retrofitted by 2001.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTENIST_NCSTAR_1–4200514-80]

### Electrical and communications features

Electrical service to the towers was supplied by Consolidated Edison at 13,800 volts. This service passed through the World Trade Center Primary Distribution Center (PDC) and sent up through the core of the building to [electrical substations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_substation) located on the mechanical floors. The substations stepped down the 13,800 primary voltage to 480/277 volt secondary service, and then further down to 208/120 volt general power and lighting service. The complex also was served by emergency generators located in the sub-levels of the towers and on the roof of 5 WTC.[^cite_note-81][^cite_note-82]

The 110th floor of 1 World Trade Center (the North Tower) housed radio and television transmission equipment; access to the roof of 1 WTC was controlled from the WTC Operations Control Center (OCC) located in the B1 level of 2 WTC.
