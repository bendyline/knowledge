The **Deputy Prime Minister of Pakistan**,[^cite_note-1] formerly known as the **Senior Minister of Pakistan**, is the second-highest office within the [federal cabinet of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Pakistan), with the officeholder being deputy to the [prime minister](461896.md).[^cite_note-demands_fulfilled-2][^cite_note-daily_times-3] The main purpose of the post was to give a backup to the government in the absence of the [Prime Minister](461896.md).[^cite_note-daily_times-3]

As a result of a 2012 agreement between the ruling [Pakistan People's Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Peoples_Party) (PPP) and the PML-Q to share ministries in the federal cabinet,[^cite_note-15more-4] [Chaudhry Pervaiz Elahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaudhry_Pervaiz_Elahi) was made the first deputy prime minister.[^cite_note-demands_fulfilled-2] For eleven years (2013-2024) the office remained vacant until [Shehbaz Sharif](6502561.md) appointed [Ishaq Dar](988764.md) as Deputy Prime Minister as an additional charge to Dar's portfolio of Foreign Minister.[^cite_note-\:0-5][^cite_note-6]

## History

[Nusrat Bhutto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nusrat_Bhutto) served as Senior Federal Minister of the Cabinet in her [daughter's government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Benazir_Bhutto_government). She is often described as deputy prime minister.[^cite_note-10] Before that Z.A. Bhutto served as the first deputy prime minister of Pakistan in [Nurul Amin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nurul_Amin)'s regime.

On 25 June 2012, [Chaudhry Pervez Elahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaudhry_Pervez_Elahi) was appointed as the deputy prime minister of Pakistan.[^cite_note-15more-4][^cite_note-the_hindu-11][^cite_note-1st_dpm-12] He also held different offices in the federal cabinet. The post, for which there is no constitutional provision, was a result of the agreement between the government and PML-Q leadership.[^cite_note-its_official-13][^cite_note-demands_fulfilled-2] The notification described that Elahi's appointment was carried out on immediate basis and will be valid until a new order is issued.[^cite_note-ttoi-14] The notification further stated that "Elahi will not possess the powers of the prime minister".[^cite_note-demands_fulfilled-2] Following negotiations between the [Pakistan Peoples Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Peoples_Party) (PPP) and [Pakistan Muslim League (Q)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Muslim_League_\(Q\)) (PML-Q), [Chaudhry Pervaiz Elahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaudhry_Pervaiz_Elahi) was appointed Deputy Prime Minister and 15 members of his party were inducted into the Federal Cabinet.[^cite_note-dawn.com-8]

On April 28, 2024, Shehbaz Sharif through a notification issued by the [Cabinet Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_Secretariat_\(Pakistan\)) appointed Ishaq Dar as Deputy Prime Minister.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

## Powers and responsibilities

The post is symbolic, non-[constitutional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Pakistan) and without specific authorities, created only to ceremonially protect the space of the prime minister in his/her absence.[^cite_note-daily_times-3] The post does not carry particular responsibilities, although it is granted oversight over a number of ministries in the cabinet and the deputy prime minister works in his/her capacity as a "Senior federal minister".[^cite_note-its_official-13][^cite_note-demands_fulfilled-2] [Chaudhry Pervaiz Elahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaudhry_Pervaiz_Elahi) had worked as a Senior minister in the cabinet of former Prime Minister [Yousuf Raza Gilani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yousuf_Raza_Gilani), another post without any reasonable powers. Previously, the Senior minister post was occupied by [Begum Nusrat Bhutto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Begum_Nusrat_Bhutto) and [Chaudhry Nisar Ali Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaudhry_Nisar_Ali_Khan), in the governments of [Benazir Bhutto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benazir_Bhutto) and [Nawaz Sharif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nawaz_Sharif), respectively.[^cite_note-its_official-13] Due to the ambiguity of any [constitutional definition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Pakistan) of [Ishaq Dar](988764.md)'s role as Deputy Prime Minister, his current duties and authorities are uncertain in the office.[^cite_note-15]

## 2012 Constitutional petition

On 28 June 2012, a petition was filed in the [Supreme Court of Pakistan](843857.md) against the validity of the post. The petitioner told the court there is no space for any such position in the [Constitution of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Pakistan) and insisted the court to dismiss the post.[^cite_note-16] The post was also challenged in the [Lahore High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_High_Court) under the violation to the article 91 of the constitution. The petitioner appealed the court that "this appointment was ill-intended and should be declared void."[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] A petition was also filed in the [Sindh High Court](11042310.md) which was rejected by the court.[^cite_note-20]
