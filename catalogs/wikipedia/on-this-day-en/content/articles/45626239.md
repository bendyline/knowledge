**Finchley Reform Synagogue** is a [Reform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_Judaism) [Jewish](15624.md) congregation and [synagogue](48559.md), located at 101 Fallow Court Avenue, [North Finchley](453539.md), in the [Borough of Barnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Borough_of_Barnet) in [London](17867.md), England, in the United Kingdom.[^cite_note-JCR-1] The congregation is a member of the [Movement for Reform Judaism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_for_Reform_Judaism).

## History

Finchley Reform Synagogue's new building completed in May 2022, replaced the previous 1974 structure, which itself had been built over the original 1961 synagogue. The new facility, costing approximately £4.7 million, addresses the needs of a growing community of around 2,000 members.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

On 15 April 2026, two individuals threw a brick and [firebombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firebombs) in an attempted [arson](184695.md) attack. As the devices did not detonate, no damage or injuries occurred. [Metropolitan Police](192450.md) stated that the incident was being treated as an [antisemitic](1078.md) [hate crime](13547.md).[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-\:1-5] A 46-year-old man and a 47-year-old woman were arrested as suspects.[^cite_note-\:1-5][^cite_note-6] [Similar incidents targeting Jewish institutions in London](83012228.md) had occurred in the months prior and days following, including the [Hatzola arson attack](82760663.md), which have been claimed by the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) [front group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Front_organization) [Harakat Ashab al-Yamin al-Islamia](82697292.md).[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-\:1-5]

## Clergy

Its clergy[^cite_note-Clergy-7] are Rabbi Eleanor Davis, [Cantor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazzan) Zöe Jacobs,[^cite_note-8] Rabbi Deborah Blausten,[^cite_note-9] Rabbi Howard Cooper and emeritus Rabbi Jeffrey Newman.[^cite_note-Doherty-10][^cite_note-11]
