**Bari** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Bari*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بري) is an administrative region (*[gobol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Somalia)*) in northeastern [Somalia](27358.md)[^cite_note-Cwfsom-5] that consists of six districts: [Qandala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qandala_District), [Iskushuban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iskushuban_District), [Alula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alula_District), [Bosaso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosaso_District), [Bandarbeyla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayla_District), and [Qardho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qardho_District). The port city of [Bosaso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosaso) is the capital of the region and the largest city in Bari. The region is part of the autonomous Somali state of [Puntland](233569.md).&#x20;

## Overview

It is bordered by [Sool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sool,_Somaliland) and [Sanaag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanaag) to the west, [Nugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nugal,_Somalia) to the south, the [Gulf of Aden](58716.md) to the north and the [Guardafui Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardafui_Channel) to the east. It is located on the tip of the [Horn of Africa](62142.md). Its name, Bari, literally means *East* in Somali.[^cite_note-6]&#x20;

In terms of landmass, Bari is the largest region in Somalia.[^cite_note-7] The highest point in this region is [Mount Bahaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Bahaya), the third tallest mountain in Somalia with an elevation of 2120 meters; other notable peaks include [Karkaar](9358715.md) and [Cal Miskaad](64666489.md) mountains.&#x20;

In 2024, Islamic State (IS) fighters wrested control of the [Cal Miskaad](64666489.md) (Al Miskaad) mountain range in Bari from rival militant group [Al Shabaab](8726463.md).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]&#x20;

## Demographics

Most of the inhabitants of the Bari region belong to the [Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_people) ethnic group including [Majeerteen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majeerteen), [Leelkase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leelkase), [Deshiishe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deshiishe), [Warsangeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsangeli), Kaskiqabe Darood clans, [Mehri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahris), [Madhibaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhiban) and other clans [^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] According to the 2015 Population Estimation Survey, the population was 1,887,568 inhabitants. This figure combines both the rural and urban populations of Bari as well all IDPs (internally displaced persons).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]&#x20;

In the official [administrative divisions of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Somalia), the Bari region consists of seven districts.[^cite_note-Brg-14]&#x20;

The Bari region is the largest region of the 18 administrative regions of Somalia and it contains following districts.&#x20;

- [Bosaso District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosaso_District) The capital and largest

- [Qardho District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qardho_District)

- [Alula District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alula_District)

- [Bandarbayla District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayla_District)

- [Waiye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waiye)

- [Iskushuban District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iskushuban_District)

- [Qandala District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qandala_District)

- [Carmo District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmo_District)

- [Ufayn District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ufayn_District)

In 1998, Bari became a part of [Puntland](233569.md), an autonomous region in northeastern Somalia. In the *Transitional Constitution of the Puntland Regional Government* of 2001, Puntland's territory is defined in terms of Somalia's official regions, Bari being one of them.[^cite_note-15]&#x20;

In the years thereafter, Puntland changed its internal administrative division, and the Bari region was split in two: the southern half, roughly coinciding with the [Qardho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qardho_District) and [Bandarbayla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayla_District) Districts, became the new region of [Karkaar](9358715.md) (also spelled as Karkar). It was subdivided into five new districts: Qardho, Bandarbayla, [Waiye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waiye), [Rako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rako_District) and [Hafun Armo District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafun_District). The northern half continued to be known as Bari and was split into seven districts: five of them keeping their original names ([Bosaso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosaso_District), [Iskushuban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iskushuban_District), [Ufayn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ufayn), [Qandala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qandala_District) and [Alula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alula_District)), as well as three new ones: [Carmo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmo_District), [Tisjiic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tisjiic) and [Bargal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bargal_District).[^cite_note-16]&#x20;

Radio Galkacyo reported on 19 April 2011 that a new administration, called Raas Asayr, had been declared in parts of Bari Region. [Farah Mahmud Yusuf](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Farah_Mahmud_Yusuf\&action=edit\&redlink=1) was reported to have been elected as its president.[^cite_note-17] Later, Barre Fatah Said there were possibilities of starting negotiation between Puntland Government and the founders of Raas Assayr state. The leaders of this prospective polity retracted their claims a few months later in June 2012, indicating that they now supported Puntland and were satisfied with the Puntland authorities' new development projects in the region.[^cite_note-Nmrasfrtc-18]&#x20;

On 8 April 2013, the Puntland government announced the creation of a new region named [Gardafuul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Guardafui). Carved out of the Bari region, it consists of three districts and has its capital at [Alula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alula,_Somalia).[^cite_note-19]&#x20;
