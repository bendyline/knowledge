**Zintan** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الزنتان, meaning "small castles") is a city in northwestern [Libya](17633.md), situated roughly 136 kilometres (85 mi) southwest of [Tripoli](31232.md), in the [Nafusa Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nafusa_Mountains) area. The city and its surrounding area have a population of 16,024.

## History

The [Roman garrison town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_garrison_town) of Tentheos was on the [Nafusa mountain range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nafusa_Mountains) in the hinterland of the [Limes Tripolitanus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limes_Tripolitanus), near the border.[^cite_note-2]

## Civil War activity

[Groups from Zintan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zintan_Brigades) joined in the [Libyan Civil War in 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_Civil_War_\(2011\)). The [Battle of Zintan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Zintan) reportedly began when the [Gaddafi](53029.md)-led government forces arrived to recruit 1,000 soldiers. Insulted by the proposal to fight fellow Libyans, a group formed in Zintan to protest. As the group grew, pro-Gaddafi forces attacked but local groups counterattacked with seized weapons, "rout\[ing]" a large, heavily armed government convoy on 19–20 March.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

The Zintan people were responsible for the capture of [Saif al-Islam](1196902.md), the second son of Muammar Gaddafi.[^cite_note-5] He was captured on 19 November 2011, a month after his father's death, about 50 kilometres (31 mi) west of the town of [Ubari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubari) near [Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabha,_Libya) in southern Libya.[^cite_note-bbccapture-6]

In 2015, during the [Second Libyan Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Libyan_Civil_War), the area was damaged by [a series of airstrikes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zintan_Airstrike_Attacks) from February to April.[^cite_note-7]

In February 2026, [Saif al-Islam Gaddafi](1196902.md), second son of son of Muammar Gaddafi, was assassinated during clashes in Zintan.
