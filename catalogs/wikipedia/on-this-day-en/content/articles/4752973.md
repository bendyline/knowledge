This is a list of political elections that had their results cancelled or decertified after they were held, sometimes legally, sometimes following a coup or revolution. Partial cancellations (one or some districts) are omitted.

## National elections

### 20th century

- [1918 Estonian Constituent Assembly election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1918_Estonian_Constituent_Assembly_election)

- [May 1925 Bolivian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_1925_Bolivian_general_election) (won by [José Gabino Villanueva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Gabino_Villanueva?action=edit\&redlink=1))

- [1930 Brazilian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930_Brazilian_general_election) (won by [Júlio Prestes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%BAlio_Prestes), followed by the [Revolution of 1930](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution_of_1930))

- [1931 Ecuadorian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1931_Ecuadorian_presidential_election) (won by [Neptalí Bonifaz Ascásubi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neptal%C3%AD_Bonifaz_Asc%C3%A1subi?action=edit\&redlink=1))

- [1934 Bolivian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1934_Bolivian_general_election) (won by [Franz Tamayo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Tamayo))

- [1936 Peruvian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1936_Peruvian_general_election) (won by [Luis Antonio Eguiguren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Antonio_Eguiguren))

- [1948 Costa Rican general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Costa_Rican_general_election) (won by [Otilio Ulate Blanco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otilio_Ulate_Blanco), followed by [Costa Rican Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Rican_Civil_War))

- [1957 Guatemalan general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1957_Guatemalan_general_election) (won by [Miguel Ortiz Passarelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_Ortiz_Passarelli?action=edit\&redlink=1))

- [1958 Cuban presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958_Cuban_presidential_election) (won by [Andrés Rivero Agüero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Rivero_Ag%C3%BCero), followed by [Cuban Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_Revolution))

- [1960 Iranian legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960_Iranian_legislative_election)

  [^cite_note-1]

- [1962 Argentine legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_Argentine_legislative_election) (annulled by a [coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coups_d'%C3%A9tat_in_Argentina#Coup_of_29_March_1962) 10 days later)

- [1962 Peruvian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_Peruvian_general_election) (won by [Víctor Raúl Haya de la Torre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%ADctor_Ra%C3%BAl_Haya_de_la_Torre))

- [1967 Sierra Leonean general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967_Sierra_Leonean_general_election) ([Siaka Stevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siaka_Stevens) took the oath of office as prime minister but was overthrown the same day)

- [May 1968 Dahomeyan presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_1968_Dahomeyan_presidential_election) (won by [Basile Adjou Moumouni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basile_Adjou_Moumouni))

- [1970 Dahomeyan presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970_Dahomeyan_presidential_election) (won by [Justin Ahomadegbé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Ahomadegb%C3%A9))

- [1970 Lesotho general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970_Lesotho_general_election) (won by the [Basotho Congress Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basotho_Congress_Party))

- [1978 Bolivian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_Bolivian_general_election)

- [1982 Guatemalan general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_Guatemalan_general_election) (won by [Ángel Aníbal Guevara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81ngel_An%C3%ADbal_Guevara), followed by a coup d'état)

- [1986 Philippine presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Philippine_presidential_election) (won by [Ferdinand Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Marcos), followed by the [People Power Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_Power_Revolution))

- [1989 Panamanian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Panamanian_general_election) (won by [Guillermo Endara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_Endara), nullified before voting was complete, followed by [United States invasion of Panama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_invasion_of_Panama))

- [1990 Burmese general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Burmese_general_election) (won by the [National League for Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_for_Democracy))

- [1991 Algerian legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Algerian_legislative_election) (won by the [Islamic Salvation Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Salvation_Front), followed by [Algerian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algerian_Civil_War))

- [1992 Central African general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Central_African_general_election)

- [1993 Nigerian presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Nigerian_presidential_election) (won by [Moshood Abiola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshood_Abiola))

- [April 1997 Malian parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_1997_Malian_parliamentary_election)

- [2000 Peruvian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Peruvian_general_election)
