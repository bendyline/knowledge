On December 26, 2025, two civilians were killed and at least two others were injured in a combined [vehicle-ramming](44318569.md) and [stabbing attack](53684322.md) in [Harod Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harod_Valley), [Northern District](324281.md), Israel. The perpetrator was later shot and arrested. [Israeli police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_police) stated they were investigating the incident as a [terrorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist) attack.

## Attack

According to Israeli police and emergency services, the attacker first rammed a 16-year-old teenager with his car, injuring him, after which he exited the vehicle and attempted to stab the boy, who escaped. The perpetrator drove onto a path designated for cyclists, killing Shimshon Mordechai, a disabled civilian.[^cite_note-ynet-1][^cite_note-2]

The attacker continued towards [Ein Harod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ein_Harod), ramming a car near [Kibbutz](106200.md) [Tel Yosef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Yosef), injuring a woman. The assailant exited the vehicle, and stabbed the woman, Aviv Maor, while she attempted to escape, killing her.[^cite_note-ynet-1]

The assailant fled the scene and was later shot by security forces and an armed civilian near the city of [Afula](323344.md).[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-4]

## Victims

Two people were killed and two others were injured. The deceased victims were identified as 68-year-old Shimshon Mordechai and 18-year-old Aviv Maor. A 16-year-old boy was injured and was transported to Emek Medical Center in light condition. A 37-year-old man sustained a head injury and was taken to the [Rambam Health Care Campus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rambam_Health_Care_Campus) in [Haifa](55606.md).[^cite_note-MSN-5][^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-6]

## Suspect

The suspect was identified as Ahmed al-Rub, 37, of the village of [Qabatiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qabatiya) in the [West Bank](33209.md) who infiltrated Israel several days ago. Police said the motive was under investigation and had not been formally confirmed at the time, though the incident was being treated as a suspected terror attack.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-4] The day after the attack, al-Rub was discharged from the Emek Medical Center in [Afula](323344.md) and was taken into custody. Police and the [Shin Bet](311752.md) announced that they arrested two of his brothers during a nighttime raid on a building near [Arraba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arraba,_Israel).[^cite_note-MSN-5]

## Investigation

Police launched a manhunt following the initial attacks and increased security presence in the area. Officials stated that the investigation was ongoing and that further details would be released as confirmed.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-4]

## Aftermath

[Hamas](13913.md) praised the attack, calling it an "expression of the growing, popular anger" and a "result of the Zionist occupation’s daily crimes" but did not claim responsibility.[^cite_note-nyt-7]
