**Myanmar–Russia relations** are the bilateral relations between [Myanmar](19457.md) and [Russia](25391.md). Myanmar has an embassy in [Moscow](19004.md) whilst Russia has an embassy in [Yangon](57404.md).

## History

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| [Burma](19457.md) | [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) |
|  |  |

The USSR established diplomatic relations with Myanmar upon the latter's independence in 1948, which remain to the present day through Russia. In 2007, alongside China, Russia vetoed a [U.N. Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.N._Security_Council) resolution condemning alleged human rights abuses and atrocities at the hands of the Myanmar government.[^cite_note-BBC-1][^cite_note-WP-2]

### 2022

In August 2022, Russian Foreign Minister [Sergey Lavrov](541346.md) described [Myanmar](19457.md) as a "friendly and longstanding partner."[^cite_note-3]

On 7 September 2022, Myanmar's junta chief [Min Aung Hlaing](38278640.md) met with Russian President [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) on the sidelines of an economic meeting in eastern Russia, the first time that the pair have met since the [2021 Myanmar coup d'état](66567997.md).[^cite_note-4]

In July of that year, evidence was discovered that Myanmar has supplied 120 mm mortar rounds to Russia for the ongoing [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md).[^cite_note-5]

### 2023

On 11 August 2023, Myanmar Airways became set to launch direct flights to the Russian city of [Novosibirsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk).[^cite_note-6]

On 7 November 2023, Myanmar and Russia held their first ever joint [naval exercise](1225714.md), which was carried out in the [Andaman Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaman_Sea).[^cite_note-7]

### 2025

On 23 February 2025, Russia and Myanmar signed a memorandum on investment in the [Dawei special economic zone](38653231.md), including plans for a port, coal-fired power plant, and oil refinery. [Russian Minister Maxim Reshetnikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_Reshetnikov) and [Myanmar's Kan Zaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kan_Zaw) signed the deal during a Russian delegation's visit. Reshetnikov noted oil refining remained the most complex element, with its feasibility still under review. Russia and Myanmar also discussed energy projects, including a gas pipeline to [Yangon](57404.md) and a potential nuclear research reactor.[^cite_note-8]

On 3 March 2025, Myanmar's military leader, [Senior General Min Aung Hlaing](38278640.md), arrived in Russia for talks with [President Vladimir Putin](32817.md), accompanied by officials from the ruling military council. Russia is a key ally, providing military support, including fighter jets used against ethnic minority groups allied with pro-democracy forces. Moscow also shields Myanmar's government in global forums. The two nations have held joint military drills, signed a nuclear power pact, and are expanding economic cooperation, including their labor agreements.[^cite_note-9]

&#x20;\


### 2026

On 3 February 2026, the Myanmar military government and Russia signed a military cooperation pact planned to last until 2030.[^cite_note-10]

&#x20;\
&#x20;From 6 to 17 July, the Russian Armed Forces and the Tatmadaw conducted joint special forces training in [Naypyidaw](2270051.md) under "Operation Tropical Storm."[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Agreements

In June 2016, the two countries signed a defence cooperation agreement.[^cite_note-13] [Min Aung Hlaing](38278640.md) and [Sergei Shoigu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Shoigu) announced their intention to expand military cooperation in a 2018 meeting.[^cite_note-14]

## Nuclear cooperation

Main article: [Myanmar and weapons of mass destruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar_and_weapons_of_mass_destruction)

In 2007, Russia and Myanmar did a controversial nuclear research center deal. According to the press release, "The centre will comprise a 10MW light-water reactor working on 20%-enriched uranium-235, an activation analysis laboratory, a medical isotope production laboratory, silicon doping system, nuclear waste treatment and burial facilities".[^cite_note-15]
