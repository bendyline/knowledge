**Sutatausa** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[sutaˈtawsa\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a municipality and town of [Colombia](5222.md) in the [Ubaté Province](11964310.md), part of the [department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Colombia) of [Cundinamarca](189405.md). The municipality is located on the [Altiplano Cundiboyacense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altiplano_Cundiboyacense) at a distance of 88 kilometres (55 mi) from the capital [Bogotá](211271.md) and borders [Ubaté](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubat%C3%A9) in the north, [Tausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tausa) in the south, [Cucunubá](10732336.md) in the east and [Carmen de Carupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmen_de_Carupa) and Tausa in the west.[^cite_note-websiteSutatausa-1]

## Etymology

The name Sutatausa comes from [Chibcha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chibcha_language) and means "small tribute".[^cite_note-websiteSutatausa-1]

## History

The area of Sutatause before the [Spanish conquest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_conquest_of_the_Muisca) was inhabited by the [Muisca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca_people), organised in their loose [Muisca Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca_Confederation). Sutatausa was ruled by the *[zipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zipa)* based in [Muyquytá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacat%C3%A1).

Modern Sutatausa was founded on June 24 (Saint John's day), 1537 by [Hernán Pérez de Quesada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hern%C3%A1n_P%C3%A9rez_de_Quesada), brother of [conquistador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conquistador) [Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonzalo_Jim%C3%A9nez_de_Quesada), who on the same day founded [Tenza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenza).[^cite_note-websiteSutatausa-1]

## Economy

Main economical activities of Sutatausa are [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture), [dairy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy) farming and small-scale mining. Tourism is also an important factor of income.[^cite_note-websiteSutatausa-1]
