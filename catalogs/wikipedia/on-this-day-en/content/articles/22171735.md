The **president of the People's National Assembly of Algeria** is the presiding officer of that body. From the creation of the [Council of the Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_the_Nation) in 1997, the [People's National Assembly](2806769.md) is the [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house) of the [Parliament of Algeria](3748164.md).
