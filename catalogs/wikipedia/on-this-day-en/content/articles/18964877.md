**Punta Caucedo** is a small cape located in the south coast of the [Dominican Republic](8060.md), 15 miles (24 km) east from [Santo Domingo](57638.md). [Las Américas International Airport](599919.md) is located at Punta Caucedo. It is also Sector in the city of [Boca Chica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boca_Chica) in the [Santo Domingo Province](998599.md) of the [Dominican Republic](8060.md).
