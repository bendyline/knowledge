**Monday Market** is the largest and most significant marketplace in [Maiduguri](2137952.md), [Borno State](2227628.md), [Nigeria](21383.md).[^cite_note-1]

## History and establishment

The Maiduguri Monday Market established in the early 1970s; it was created to support the growing commercial activities in Maiduguri. Initially located in Budum, the market was later relocated to its current site, which became a major commercial hub for northeastern Nigeria and neighboring countries such as [Chad](5488.md), [Niger](21373.md), and [Cameroon](5447.md). Following a devastating fire in 1979,[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] the market was rebuilt and officially commissioned in August 1985 by Major-General [Abubakar Waziri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abubakar_Waziri). It features approximately 20,000 shops and facilities, making it one of the largest markets in northern Nigeria at the time.[^cite_note-4]

## 1979 fire

The 1979 Monday Market Fire was a devastating fire outbreak that completely destroyed the Monday Market in Maiduguri, Nigeria. The market was rebuilt and expanded, and reopened in 1985 under the administration of Major-General [Abubakar Waziri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abubakar_Waziri).

## 2023 fire

A major fire outbreak occurred on February 26, 2023 at the Monday Market. The fire, which started around 2\:00 AM, destroyed approximately 13,000 shops, affecting the livelihoods of over 20,000 traders.[^cite_note-5]
