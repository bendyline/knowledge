**Fouriesburg** is a small town situated at the junction of the [R711](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R711_road_\(South_Africa\)) and [R26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R26_\(South_Africa\)) routes in the eastern [Free State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_State_\(South_African_province\)), [South Africa](17416221.md). It is near the [Maluti Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maluti_Mountains) and only 10 km from [Caledon's Poort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caledon's_Poort?action=edit\&redlink=1) border post, which gives access to [Lesotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesotho).

## History

During the late nineteenth century, this land was donated to the [Orange Free State](180208.md) by a local farmer of [Huguenot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huguenot) descent, Christoffel Fourie, for use as a temporary capital during the [Second Boer War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Boer_War). It was the site of several engagements during the Boer War, and by 1902 the original settlement had been almost completely destroyed.[^cite_note-dsapn-3]

## Vicinity



The town name on Ventersberg which flanks Meiringskloof Nature Park

A small private resort, Meiringskloof Nature Park, is located in a valley just northeast of town. Fouriesburg is about half an hour's drive from [Clarens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarens,_Free_State), a popular tourist destination, and a 45-minute drive from [Golden Gate Highlands National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Gate_Highlands_National_Park). It is about fifty kilometres south-east of [Bethlehem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem,_Free_State), and some fifty-three from [Ficksburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ficksburg).

Fouriesburg ranked first during South Africa's 2013 *Kwela Town of the Year* competition.

## Union house

Originally built in the 1870s, Union House was destroyed by fire during the Second Boer War. President Brand of the Free State planted two oak trees in the front yard to represent the union of Afrikaans and English residents in observance of the peace between the Boer and British. This farm hosted the Free State celebration of the Union of South Africa in 1910.[^cite_note-4]
