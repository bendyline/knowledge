The **first lady** or **first gentleman of the Philippines** ([Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language): *Unang Ginang o Unang Ginoó ng Pilipinas*) is the [courtesy title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courtesy_title) given to the host or hostess of [Malacañang Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaca%C3%B1ang_Palace), the residence of the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [head of government](37570.md) of the [Philippines](23440.md).

The title is traditionally held by the [consort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Significant_other) of the [president of the Philippines](271126.md), and as such is used to interchangeably refer to the spouse of the incumbent; however, this is rarely the case, especially for presidents without a living spouse at the time of their tenure. The position, which is customary in nature and dignity, merits no official remuneration from the state.

[Liza Araneta Marcos](70769981.md) is the current first lady of the Philippines, as wife of the 17th and current president of the Philippines, [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md).

## Terminology

The host of the [Malacañang Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaca%C3%B1ang_Palace), who is usually the consort of the president, has been referred to in English as the "first lady" ([Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language): *Unang Ginang*).[^cite_note-3] As [Gloria Macapagal Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Macapagal_Arroyo) took office as the first female president who is not a [widow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Widow), the masculine form "first gentleman" (abbreviated FG; [Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language): *Unang Ginoó*) was used for her husband, [Jose Miguel Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jose_Miguel_Arroyo).[^cite_note-whowerethefirstladies2003-4]

The position of first lady or first gentleman is not an elected position, carries no official duties, and has no personal remuneration. Nonetheless, the title holder participates in humanitarian and charitable work on behalf of the president, often in line with his or her policies and programmes. Furthermore, many of the holders of this post have taken an active role in campaigning for the president, or have participated in public life through some other manner.

[Imelda Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imelda_Marcos) was given a formal job as [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Metro_Manila) of [Metro Manila](72340.md) and as [Minister](739237.md) of Human Settlements by her husband [Ferdinand Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Marcos) during [his 21-year rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Ferdinand_Marcos). She is also the first incumbent first lady to enter politics by winning a seat in the [Interim Batasang Pambansa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interim_Batasang_Pambansa) in 1978.[^cite_note-5]

Two former first ladies later won seats in [Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_the_Philippines) after their husbands' tenures in office. In 1995, Marcos herself became the first former first lady to win a seat in the [House of Representatives](293605.md). In 2001, [Loi Ejercito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loi_Ejercito) became the first former first lady to win a seat in the [Senate](299521.md).

## Non-spouses who held the distinction

On a few occasions, individuals who are not spouses of the incumbent president have temporarily performed tasks reserved for the first lady or first gentleman, effectively rendering them as the *[de facto](43594.md)* holder of the position; most often they are a direct relative of the president. To date, only Elpidio Quirino, Corazon Aquino, and Benigno Aquino III had no spouse to assume the title of First Lady or First Gentleman.[^cite_note-nofirstlady-14] Rodrigo Duterte did not appoint a first lady despite having [Honeylet Avanceña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honeylet_Avance%C3%B1a) as his common-law wife. In the case of Quirino, his daughter received the title as he was a widower upon his accession.

[Victoria Quirino-Gonzalez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Quirino-Gonzalez) was the first lady for her father, Elpidio Quirino. She was made the official host of the [Malacañang Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaca%C3%B1ang_Palace).[^cite_note-15] However Victoria is regarded as the first to be named with the role of first lady. The spouses of previous presidents, from [Emilio Aguinaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilio_Aguinaldo) to [Manuel Roxas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Roxas), were not given the title during their respective husbands' terms.[^cite_note-fabulous-1]

## Presidencies with no regular first lady or gentlemen

### Benigno Aquino III

[Benigno Aquino III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benigno_Aquino_III) never married, so the palace hired professionals to manage tasks and duties customarily reserved for the first lady, such as organizing state dinners.[^cite_note-nofirstlady-14]

On some occasions, he charged one or more of his sisters with entertaining local and foreign dignitaries.[^cite_note-nofirst-16] During the [2015 APEC summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_APEC_summit) hosted in [Pasay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasay), his youngest sister, [Kris Aquino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kris_Aquino) served as temporary first lady for the event. Their other sisters, Ballsy Aquino-Cruz, Pinky Aquino-Abelleda, and Viel Aquino-Dee, also assisted him in his various duties.[^cite_note-AquinoSisters-17][^cite_note-abs-cbnnews.com-18][^cite_note-ReferenceA-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

### Rodrigo Duterte



[Honeylet Avanceña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honeylet_Avance%C3%B1a), the partner of President Rodrigo Duterte, has taken on the duties of first lady on several state functions despite not being designated as such during Duterte's presidency.

Prior to his inauguration, incumbent [Rodrigo Duterte](7407293.md) declared his eldest daughter [Sara](42504861.md) would be the first lady despite having [Honeylet Avanceña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honeylet_Avance%C3%B1a) as his [common-law wife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common-law_marriage). He was previously married to [Elizabeth Zimmerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Zimmerman-Duterte);[^cite_note-Dutertewomen-22] their marriage was annulled in 2000.

However, Sara Duterte was already mayor-elect of Davao City, and declined the offer.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] Following her filing of a 10-day leave of absence until June 22, there were speculations that she did so to assume the role of first lady, but she neither denied or confirmed speculations.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] Former [Department of the Interior and Local Government](4718691.md) secretary Rafael Alunan suggested that [Vice-President](2271639.md) [Leni Robredo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leni_Robredo) serve as *de facto* first lady[^cite_note-27] but this never materialised.

Avanceña nevertheless would fulfill roles usually conducted by the first lady such as during the official visit of [Japanese prime minister](24833.md) [Shinzo Abe](2634434.md), and the [30th ASEAN Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_ASEAN_Summits) in 2017.[^cite_note-28]
