**Concesionaria Vuela Compañía de Aviación S.A.B. de C.V.**, operating as **Volaris**, is a Mexican ultra [low-cost airline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-cost_airline) based in [Santa Fe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Fe,_Mexico_City), [Álvaro Obregón, Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lvaro_Obreg%C3%B3n,_Mexico_City), with its operating bases in [Cancún](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canc%C3%BAn_International_Airport), [Culiacán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culiac%C3%A1n_International_Airport), [Guadalajara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guadalajara_International_Airport), [León/Del Bajío](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baj%C3%ADo_International_Airport), [Mexicali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexicali_International_Airport), [Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City_International_Airport), [Monterrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterrey_International_Airport), and [Tijuana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tijuana_International_Airport). It is Mexico's largest airline by transported passengers[^cite_note-4] and serves domestic and international destinations within the [Americas](29833.md).[^cite_note-flightconnections-5] It is the leading airline in the Mexican domestic airline market with a market share of 42%.[^cite_note-6]

## History

The pre-operations phase, the founding of the legal entities and setting up of the required infrastructure, started in August 2005 under the name *Vuela Airlines*. The idea for the airline was formed from the proposed "Vuelamex" project.[^cite_note-auto-7] Major initial [shareholders](146081.md) of the company were [Grupo Televisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grupo_Televisa) (the world's biggest Spanish-language media conglomerate), [Inbursa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbursa) (an insurance company owned by multi-billionaire [Carlos Slim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Slim)), [TACA Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TACA_Airlines), and the Discovery Americas Fund. Each of these partners invested 25% of the initial cost of activities, or 100 million [USD](18717338.md).[^cite_note-auto-7]

In July 2010,[^cite_note-8] it was announced that Televisa and Inbursa had sold their stake in Volaris, leaving the ownership of Volaris as follows: [TACA Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TACA_Airlines) with Roberto and Maria Cristina Kriete (50%), Investment fund Discovery Americas (over 25%), and [Indigo Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigo_Partners): Fund led by former [America West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America_West_Airlines) CEO [Bill Franke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Franke).

Ticket sales started in January 2006. Following the delivery of the airline's first aircraft, the first non-commercial flight was operated in February 2006. Scheduled revenue flights were launched on March 13, 2006, with the inaugural flight being between Toluca and [Tijuana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tijuana_International_Airport).

Volaris began service to [Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City_International_Airport) in September 2010 after absorbing flight routes from two defunct Mexican airlines, [Aerocalifornia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerocalifornia) and [Mexicana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexicana_de_Aviaci%C3%B3n_\(1921%E2%80%932010\)). In March 2011, the airline announced that its hub in Toluca would move to Guadalajara.

In June 2012, the airline launched a frequent flyer program called VClub.[^cite_note-9] In June 2012, [PayPal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PayPal) became a payment alternative for the airline, enabling customers to purchase tickets directly from the airline's website.[^cite_note-10] In September 2012, Volaris announced a codeshare partnership with a German airline, [Condor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condor_\(airline\)). Passengers of Condor are able to fly to more international destinations.[^cite_note-11]

On March 13, 2013, the airline celebrated its seventh anniversary, offering passengers 70% off all flights, and has done this every year since then.[^cite_note-12] Volaris announced the creation of a subsidiary, [Volaris Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volaris_Costa_Rica), in March 2016.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-larepublica15mar2016-14] The subsidiary, based at [Juan Santamaría International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Santamar%C3%ADa_International_Airport) in the Costa Rican capital of San José, started operations in November 2016.[^cite_note-chav2dec2016-15]

As of 2022, Volaris has faced multiple class-action lawsuits due to failure to properly refund flights canceled due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

In December 2025, Volaris and competitor [Viva](4395127.md) have signed an agreement to form a holding company, creating the largest low-cost airline group in Mexico.[^cite_note-airwaysmag-viva-volaris-18][^cite_note-volaris-viva-merger-ag-19][^cite_note-volaris-viva-merger-20] The deal aims to lower fleet costs, while both airlines would maintain their brands.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-volaris-viva-merger-20]

In 2025, Mexico's Federal Civil Aviation Agency (AFAC) authorized Volaris to operate 10 aircraft with foreign pilots between December 1, 2025, and January 12, 2026; the Association of Airline Pilots of Mexico (ASPA) claim the decision breaches Mexico's Constitution and Civil Aviation Law.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

## International operations



An [Airbus A320neo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A320neo_family) of [Volaris Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volaris_Costa_Rica) departing [Los Angeles International Airport](18131.md) in 2023

In November 2008, Volaris announced a [codeshare agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Codeshare_agreement) with U.S.-based low-cost carrier [Southwest Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Airlines). In April 2009, Volaris announced the start of U.S.-bound flights out of Toluca and Guadalajara (initially to [Los Angeles](18131.md) and [Oakland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland_San_Francisco_Bay_Airport)) to feed into the [hubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airline_hub) of Southwest Airlines. After [Mexicana de Aviación](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexicana_de_Aviaci%C3%B3n_\(1921%E2%80%932010\))'s shutdown, Volaris took over many of Mexicana's international destinations and flights from its [focus city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_city), Guadalajara. The airline began using Guadalajara International Airport as an American gateway hub in late 2011.

In February 2013, Volaris and Southwest stopped codesharing. Southwest decided to focus more on the Mexican market with [AirTran Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AirTran_Airways), instead of codesharing with Volaris.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] In January 2018, Volaris announced a codeshare agreement with American low-cost carrier [Frontier Airlines](163354.md).

In June 2018, after [Donald Trump](4848272.md) signed an order ending the [family separation policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_separation_policy), Volaris announced that they would offer free flights for children to reunite with family members who had been deported.[^cite_note-26]

### Headquarters

The airline is headquartered in [Santa Fe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Fe,_Mexico_City), [Álvaro Obregón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lvaro_Obreg%C3%B3n,_Mexico_City), [Mexico City](18987.md).[^cite_note-42] It formerly had its headquarters on the first floor of Prolongación Paseo de la Reforma 490 in Peña Blanca, Santa Fe.[^cite_note-43]

### Services

Volaris cabins are configured in a single class, high density layout.

Volaris does not provide complimentary meals or drinks on its flights. Passengers may purchase items on board from the "Entre nubes" buy on board program.[^cite_note-44] Onboard sales are an important part of the airline's ancillary revenue. Items such as snacks, pillows, blankets and Volaris-branded items are sold on board.[^cite_note-45] Volaris' monthly inflight magazine is called "V de Volaris".[^cite_note-46]

The airline had previously provided in-flight entertainment (IFE) in most aircraft, utilizing drop-down screens on its Airbus aircraft. By 2019, all use of IFE had been discontinued.[^cite_note-47]

### Frequent flyer program

VClub is Volaris' frequent flyer program. It gives members exclusive deals on flights, baggage and packages. The airline offers individual and group memberships, with the option to pay monthly or annually. Volaris offers VPass, similar to Spirit's "\$9 Fare Club". It offers a set subscription price, and charges customers only for taxes and additional services.[^cite_note-48] Customers can book codeshare flights and earn points through fellow ultra-low-cost carrier, [Frontier Airlines](163354.md).[^cite_note-49]

#### Brand image

The Volaris logo, since its launch in 2005, has been a colorful, pixelated star against a black background.[^cite_note-51] It represents the north star, [Polaris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polaris), from which the airline's name is derived.[^cite_note-52]
