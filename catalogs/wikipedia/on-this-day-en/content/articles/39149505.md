The **R510** is a [Regional Route](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Route_\(South_Africa\)) in [South Africa](17416221.md) that connects [Rustenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rustenburg) with the [Stockpoort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockpoort) Border with [Botswana](3464.md) via [Thabazimbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thabazimbi) and [Lephalale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lephalale).[^cite_note-\:1-1]

## Route

The northern end of the R510 is the [Stockpoort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockpoort) border post with [Botswana](3464.md). It runs east for 43 kilometres before branching off the [R572](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R572_road_\(South_Africa\)) and assuming a southerly direction, following the [Mokolo River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mokolo_River). It then continues to the town of [Lephalale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lephalale) (Ellisras), where the [R518](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R518_road_\(South_Africa\)) and the [R33](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R33_\(South_Africa\)) both branch off, heading east. It continues south to Bulge River, where it branches off the [R517](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R517_road_\(South_Africa\)), which heads south-east, before reaching [Thabazimbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thabazimbi). Just south of Thabazimbi, the road junctions the [R511](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R511_road_\(South_Africa\)) and continues heading south. It then passes through [Northam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northam,_South_Africa) before it crosses into the [North West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_West_\(South_African_province\)), skirting the eastern border of the [Pilanesberg National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilanesberg_National_Park). The [R566](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R566_road_\(South_Africa\)) crosses it south of [Monakato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monakato) and it proceeds southwards to enter the city of [Rustenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rustenburg), where it enters as Beneden Street, bypasses the [Bospoort Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bospoort_Dam) on the Hex River, passes through the [Boitekong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boitekong) township and bypasses the [Rustenburg Airfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rustenburg_Airfield), to reach its end at an intersection with the [R104](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R104_road_\(South_Africa\)) west of the Rustenburg city centre.[^cite_note-\:1-1]
