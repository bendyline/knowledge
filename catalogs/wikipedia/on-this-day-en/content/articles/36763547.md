The **Royal Dutch Rowing Federation** ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): **Koninklijke Nederlandse Roeibond**, a.k.a. **KNRB**) is the federation of all Dutch [rowing clubs](25736.md).[^cite_note-1] It was founded in 1917.[^cite_note-2]

The federation is a member of [NOC\*NSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NOC*NSF) and can nominate candidates and teams to participate in the [Olympic Games](22576.md).[^cite_note-3]

The Federation's headquarters are located at the [Bosbaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosbaan) in [Amstelveen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amstelveen).[^cite_note-4] The current president, as of 2022, is Seada van den Herik.[^cite_note-5]
