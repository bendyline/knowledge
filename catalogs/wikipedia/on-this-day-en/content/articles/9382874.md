# List of diplomatic missions of Mexico

Mexico's foreign service started in 1822, the year after the signing of the [Treaty of Cordoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Cordoba) which marked the beginning of [Mexico's independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_of_Mexico). In 1831, legislation was passed that underpinned the establishment of diplomatic representations with other states in Europe and the Americas. As of 2026, Mexico has diplomatic relations with 193 countries.[^cite_note-1]

Today, Mexico has a significant worldwide presence, with over 150 representations, including 53 consulates in the [United States](3434750.md) alone (no other country has as many consulates in any single host country).
