**Kenneth Bednarek** [OLY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OLY)[^cite_note-3] (born October 14, 1998) is an American [track and field](194904.md) [sprinter](89276.md) from [Rice Lake, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_Lake,_Wisconsin). He specializes in the 100-meter and 200-meter distances, having won a silver medal at the [2020 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Summer_Olympics), [2022 World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_World_Athletics_Championships), and at the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-F-5] At the inaugural [2026 World Athletics Ultimate Championship](81879269.md), Bednarek won both the 100 metres and 200 metres, earning his first two individual global titles; his winning 200-metre time of 19.52 seconds was a personal best and moved him to seventh on the world all-time list.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Early life

Born in [Tulsa, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa,_Oklahoma), Bednarek and his fraternal twin brother, Ian, were adopted by Mary Bednarek and moved to [Rice Lake, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_Lake,_Wisconsin). Both brothers took to running youth track starting in the second grade. Running for [Rice Lake High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_Lake_High_School), Kenny won seven individual [state titles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin_Interscholastic_Athletic_Association) and led his team to a 4 × 400 relay championship. His 20.43 was the number one high school 200-meter time in the nation in 2018.

He also played [football](18951490.md) at Rice Lake, scoring 17 touchdowns as a wide receiver, kick returner and on jet sweeps during his junior and senior years, recorded on videos posted on the recruiting website [hudl.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudl).[^cite_note-8] He also starred as a gunner on special teams. During his senior year, his Rice Lake Warriors teams won state championships in both track and football. Bednarek holds Wisconsin all-class records for 200 and 400 meters and the Division 2 record for 100 meters.[^cite_note-9]

## Collegiate career

Bednarek did not qualify academically for a major four-year university, so he enrolled at Indian Hills Community College in Ottumwa, Iowa. He said, "I had a goal to go to university after [JUCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_college), but obviously God (had) a different plan."[^cite_note-\:0-10] At Indian Hills, Bednarek ran the fastest indoor 200 m in the US, the #2 time in the world for the indoor season, which tied him as the #25 individual on the [all time indoor list](1833981.md#Men_\(indoor\)) at the time.[^cite_note-11]

On May 17, 2019, running as a freshman for [Indian Hills Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Hills_Community_College) in [Ottumwa, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottumwa,_Iowa) at the age of 20, he ran the fourth fastest [200 meters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/200_meters) ever at the time, under any conditions, running 19.49 [at altitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effects_of_high_altitude_on_humans#Altitude_and_athletic_performance) with an exceptionally strong 6.1 mps [aiding wind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_assistance) in the semi-finals of the [NJCAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NJCAA) National Championships in [Hobbs, New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hobbs,_New_Mexico). The following day, Bednarek proved his speed by running 19.82 into a -0.8 headwind and running a 44.73 [400 meters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/400_meters) on the same day, becoming the NJCAA national champion in both events. 19.82 displaced Olympic gold medalist [Tommie Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommie_Smith)'s former [world record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men's_200_metres_world_record_progression#Records_post-1977) for the #30 fastest legal race of [all time](1833981.md#Men_\(outdoor\)). Six men have run faster into a negative wind, but -0.9 is the strongest hindering wind against any athlete to break 19.95.[^cite_note-12]

Only one other person, [Botswanan](3464.md) [Isaac Makwala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Makwala), has ever run sub-20 and sub-45 in the same day. Makwala was 28 when he did that in [Madrid](41188263.md) on July 6, 2014, making Bednarek the youngest athlete and only American athlete to pull off the feat.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

For his achievement, Bednarek was named the "[USATF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USATF) Athlete of the Week" on May 22, 2019.[^cite_note-15]

## Professional career



Bednarek at the [2019 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_World_Athletics_Championships)

In July 2019, he left Indian Hills and signed a pro contract with Nike and began training in Florida with former world champion sprinter and Olympic medalist [Justin Gatlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Gatlin). “It wasn’t my decision," he said. "But you know Nike wanted to send me somewhere so I just kind of listened. So you know, they know what they’re doing. It’s all you know just going to trust the process.”[^cite_note-\:0-10]

He said that he came out of high school as a 400-meter specialist but transitioned successfully into the 200-meters. "So I kind of want to continue that, maybe in the years to come try to do the 100, but yeah I think the 200 is my main event right now,” he said in July 2019.[^cite_note-\:0-10]

At the [2019 USA Outdoor Track and Field Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_USA_Outdoor_Track_and_Field_Championships), Bednarek qualified for the 200 meters final. In the final, he pulled up with a hamstring injury but jogged across the finish line. Later in the season, winner [Noah Lyles](51135146.md) won the [2019 IAAF Diamond League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_IAAF_Diamond_League) 200 m title, entitling him to a wild card to the [2019 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_World_Athletics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_200_metres). USA was thus allowed an additional entry. By virtue of Bednarek being the fourth person to cross the finish line at the National Championships with a qualifying time, he was given the position into the World Championships. Weeks after his injury, Bednarek was only able to muster a non-qualifying 21.50 in his heat due to a nerve flare up.

On August 10, 2020, in the [COVID-19](63030231.md) abbreviated season, Bednarek ran a world leading 19.80 (+1.0) at the Star Athletics Sprint Showcase in [Montverde, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montverde,_Florida).[^cite_note-16] The time moved him up to a tie for the #25 mark of all time.

On 4 August 2021, Bednarek won the silver medal at the [2020 Tokyo Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Summer_Olympics) in the 200-meter men's final with a time of 19.68 seconds.[^cite_note-17] On Sept. 9, 2021, Bednarek clinched the [Diamond League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Diamond_League) 200 meter title at the [Weltklasse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weltklasse_Z%C3%BCrich) in Zürich, winning with a time of 19.70s.[^cite_note-18] During 2021, Bednarek ran the most sub-20 performances over 200 meters, both for wind-legal conditions and all conditions, of any athlete in a single season, with 12 total sub-20 performances, of which 10 were wind-legal. He ended the season ranked No. 1 in the 200 meters by World Athletics.[^cite_note-WAprofile-19]



Bednarek running the 200 meters at the [2022 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_World_Athletics_Championships)

On July 21, 2022, in Eugene, Ore., Bednarek took silver in the 200 meters at the [2022 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_World_Athletics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_200_metres) with a time of 19.77. [Noah Lyles](51135146.md), whom he had defeated in Tokyo, set an American record of 19.31 in the race.[^cite_note-F-5]

In 2024, Bednarek qualified for the Olympic team by placing second at the [Olympic trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Olympic_trials_\(track_and_field\)) in the 100 and 200 m. [Noah Lyles](51135146.md) won both races. In the 200m, both Lyles and Bednarek went under Michael Johnson's 1996 meet record of 19.66. Lyles edged Bednarek, running 19.53 to Bednarek's 19.59, a personal record.[^cite_note-20] While Bednarek had made the last 3 World Championship or Olympic teams in the 200 m, this was the first time he had made a 100 m global team. Bednarek also ran a personal best of 9.87 in the 100 m final.[^cite_note-WAprofile-19] At the [Paris Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics), Bednarek qualified for the 100 m final, where he placed 7th in a time of 9.88.[^cite_note-21] Bednarek also qualified for the 200m final, by winning his semi-final in 20.00s. In the final he won the silver medal in a time of 19.62, finishing behind [Letsile Tebogo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letsile_Tebogo).[^cite_note-22]

In September 2024, it was announced that he had signed up for the inaugural season of the [Michael Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Johnson_\(sprinter\)) founded [Grand Slam Track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Slam_Track).[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] At the [Kingston Slam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Kingston_Slam) held in April 2025, Bednarek won in his category, having won both the 100 m and 200 m.[^cite_note-25] He also won his event category in the [Miami Slam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Miami_Slam) and [Philadelphia Slam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Philadelphia_Slam), later being crowned Racer of the Year for the [2025 Grand Slam Track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Grand_Slam_Track) season.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

On August 1, 2025, at the [2025 USA Outdoor Track and Field Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_USA_Outdoor_Track_and_Field_Championships), Bednarek won a national title over the 100 meters, running 9.79. This time ties Bednarek with [Noah Lyles](51135146.md) and [Maurice Greene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Greene_\(sprinter\)) as the sixth fastest American in history.[^cite_note-29]

## Personal life

Bednarek's nickname is "Kung Fu Kenny" because he wears a [Rambo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rambo_\(franchise\))-like tie around his head in races.[^cite_note-\:1-30]

He has a pet Husky named Rambo.[^cite_note-\:1-30]

Bednarek is [Catholic](606848.md).[^cite_note-31] He is dating Indian golfer [Sharmila Nicollet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharmila_Nicollet).[^cite_note-32]
