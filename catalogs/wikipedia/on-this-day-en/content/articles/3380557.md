**BR-153** is a major [federal highway](1299268.md) of [Brazil](3383.md), officially named the **Transbrasiliana Highway**. It also serves as part of the **[Belém–Brasília Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel%C3%A9m%E2%80%93Bras%C3%ADlia_Highway)** in the stretch located between the cities of [Wanderlândia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanderl%C3%A2ndia), in the state of [Tocantins](48764.md), and [Anápolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An%C3%A1polis), in the state of [Goiás](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goi%C3%A1s).[^cite_note-2]

It crosses Brazil in a north–south direction, starting in [São Domingos do Araguaia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Domingos_do_Araguaia), in the state of [Pará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par%C3%A1) and ending in [Aceguá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acegu%C3%A1,_Brazil), in the state of [Rio Grande do Sul](256268.md) on the Brazil–Uruguay border. The highway, highly variable in quality and traffic, cuts through the states of [Pará](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par%C3%A1), [Tocantins](48764.md), [Goiás](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goi%C3%A1s), [Minas Gerais](222651.md), [São Paulo](229422.md), [Paraná](229411.md), [Santa Catarina](256305.md) and [Rio Grande do Sul](256268.md).

## Duplication

A 315 km section between [Anápolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An%C3%A1polis) and [Monte Alegre de Minas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Alegre_de_Minas) has already been converted into a dual carriageway.

In 2020, the Federal Government planned to grant 850.7 km of the highway to the private sector, in the Anápolis (GO) section to Aliança (TO), to widened to a dual carriageway 623.4 km of the section.[^cite_note-3]
