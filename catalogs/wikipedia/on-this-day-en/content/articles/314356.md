**West Java** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Jawa Barat*, [Sundanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_language): ᮏᮝ ᮊᮥᮜᮧᮔ᮪, romanized: *Jawa Kulon*) is an Indonesian [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Indonesia) on the western part of the island of [Java](69336.md), with its provincial capital in [Bandung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung) and its largest city is [Bekasi](1915107.md). West Java is bordered by the province of [Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten) and the country's capital region of [Jakarta](16275.md) to the west, the [Java Sea](467143.md) to the north, the province of [Central Java](453584.md) to the east and the [Indian Ocean](14580.md) to the south. With [Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten), this province is the native homeland of the [Sundanese people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_people), the [second-largest ethnic group in Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_groups_in_Indonesia).

West Java was one of the first eight provinces of Indonesia formed following the [country's independence proclamation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proclamation_of_Indonesian_Independence) and was later legally re-established on 14 July 1950. With effect from 28 August 1961, the city of [Jakarta](16275.md) was split off from West Java as a 'special capital region' (*Daerah Khusus Ibukota*), with a status equivalent to that of a province,[^cite_note-7] while in October 2000 the western parts of the province were in turn split away to form a separate [Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten) province.

Even following these split-offs, West Java is by far the most populous province of Indonesia with a population of 48,274,162 as of the 2020 Census,[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-8] which grew to 50,759,003 at mid 2025 according to the official estimates;[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1] the population continues to grew at about 400,000 per year, having surpassed the 50 millions milestone during late 2023. The province's largest cities, [Bekasi](1915107.md) (a [satellite city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_city) within the [Jakarta metropolitan area](1340014.md)), [Bandung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung) and [Depok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depok) (the latter also within the Jakarta metropolitan area), are the third, fourth and sixth [most populous cities in Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Indonesian_cities_by_population) respectively. Bandung is also one of the [most densely populated cities proper in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_proper_by_population_density).[^cite_note-depkes.go.id-9]

## History

### Prehistory

The oldest human inhabitant archaeological findings in the region were unearthed in [Anyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyer) on the western coast of Java with evidence of bronze and iron metallurgical culture dating to the first millennium AD.[^cite_note-Zahorka-2007-10] The prehistoric [Buni culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buni_culture), near present-day [Bekasi](1915107.md), developed clay pottery with evidence found in Anyer to [Cirebon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirebon). Artifacts dated 400 BC — AD 100, such as food and drink containers, were found mostly as burial gifts.[^cite_note-Zahorka-2007-10] There is archaeological evidence in [Batujaya Archaeological Site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batujaya) dating from the 2nd century and, according to Dr Tony Djubiantono, the head of Bandung Archaeology Agency, Jiwa Temple in [Batujaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batujaya), [Karawang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karawang), was also built around this time.

### Hindu-Buddhist and Islamic era

See also: [Hinduism in Java](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism_in_Java)



[Sri Baduga Maharaja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Baduga_Maharaja) is a *Maharaja* (King) who was very influential during the [Sunda Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunda_Kingdom).

One of the earliest known recorded histories in Indonesia is from the former [Tarumanagara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarumanagara) kingdom, where seven, fourth-century stones are inscribed in Wengi letters (used in the [Pallava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pallava_dynasty) period) and in [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit) describing the kings of the kingdom of Tarumanagara.[^cite_note-Zahorka-2007-10] Records of Tarumanegara lasted until the sixth century, which coincides with the attack of [Srivijaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srivijaya), as stated in the [Kota Kapur inscription](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kota_Kapur_inscription) (AD 686).

The [Sunda Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunda_Kingdom) subsequently became the ruling power of the region, as recorded on the Kebon Kopi II inscription (AD 932).[^cite_note-Zahorka-2007-10] The Sunda Kingdom reached its peak during the reign of King [Sri Baduga Maharaja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Baduga_Maharaja), whose reign from 1482 to 1521 is traditionally remembered as an age of peace and prosperity among Sundanese people.[^cite_note-Kartapranata-Pajajaran-11]

An Ulama, [Sunan Gunung Jati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunan_Gunungjati), settled in [Cirebon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirebon), intending to spread Islam in the town. Meanwhile, the [Sultanate of Demak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demak_Sultanate) in Central Java grew to become a threat to the Sunda kingdom. To defend against the threat, Prabu Surawisesa Jayaperkosa signed the [Luso-Sundanese Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luso-Sundanese_padr%C3%A3o) with the [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Empire) in 1512. In return, the Portuguese were granted access to build fortresses and warehouses in the area, as well as forming trading agreements with the kingdom. This first international treaty of Sunda Kingdom with the Europeans was commemorated by the placement of the Padrao stone monument at the bank of the [Ciliwung River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciliwung) in 1522.

Although the treaty with the Portuguese had been established, it could not come to realisation. [Sunda Kalapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunda_Kelapa) harbour fell under the alliance of the Sultanates of Demak and [Cirebon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Cirebon) (former [vassal state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vassal_state) of Sunda kingdom) in 1524 after forces under Paletehan alias Fadillah Khan had conquered the city. In 1524–1525, troops under [Sunan Gunung Jati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunan_Gunungjati) also seized the port of [Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten_\(town\)) and established the [Sultanate of Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten_Sultanate) which was affiliated to the Demak Sultanate. The war between the Sunda kingdom and the Demak and Cirebon sultanates continued for five years until a peace treaty was made in 1531 between King Surawisesa and [Sunan Gunung Jati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunan_Gunungjati). From 1567 to 1579, under the last king Raja Mulya, alias Prabu Surya Kencana, the Sunda kingdom declined, under pressure from Sultanate of Banten. After 1576, the kingdom could not maintain control over its capital at [Pakuan Pajajaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakuan_Pajajaran) (present-day [Bogor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogor)), and gradually the Sultanate of Banten took over the former Sunda kingdom's territory. The [Mataram Sultanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mataram_Sultanate) from Central Java also seized the [Priangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parahyangan) region, the southeastern part of the kingdom.

### European colonization and Contemporary era



[Parahyangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parahyangan) highland near Buitenzorg (present day [Bogor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogor)), c. 1865–1872

In the 16th century, [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_East_India_Company) and [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_Company) trading companies established trading ships in western Java after the fall of the Sultanate of Banten. For the next three hundred years, western Java fell under the [Dutch East Indies](23476997.md)' administration. West Java was officially declared as a province of Indonesia in 1950, referring to a statement from Staatblad number 378. On 17 October 2000, as part of nationwide political decentralization, [Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten) was separated from West Java and made into a new province. There have been recent proposals to rename the province **[Pasundan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasundan)** ("[Sundanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_people) community") after the historical name for West Java.[^cite_note-12]

## Government and administrative divisions



[Gedung Sate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gedung_Sate) is the Governor's office of West Java



2nd-level Administrative map of West Java Province

The province is governed by Governor (Dedi Mulyadi of the [Gerindra Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerindra_Party)) and Vice Governor (Erwan Setiawan) and a Regional House of Representatives (DPRD) comprising 120 elected members (95 males and 25 females). In the northwest, Bogor Regency is represented by 11 members, Bogor City by 3 members, Bekasi Regency by 7 members, and Bekasi and Depok Cities by 11 members. Elections were held on 14 February 2024 (concurrent with presidential and parliamentary elections), following which the two political parties with the most representatives were [Golkar](408234.md) and Gerindra, with each represented by 20 members; other parties were [PKS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prosperous_Justice_Party) with 19 members, [PDI Perjuangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_Democratic_Party_of_Struggle) with 17 members, [PKB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Awakening_Party) with 15 members, [Demokrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Indonesia\)) and [NasDem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NasDem_Party) each with 8 members, [PAN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Mandate_Party) with 7 members and [PPP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Development_Party) with 6 members.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1]

From the creation of West Java Province,[^cite_note-govwj-13] the province of West Java was subdivided into four [cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_status_in_Indonesia) (*Kota*) and twenty [regencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regency_\(Indonesia\)) (*Kabupaten*), excluding the zone separated off in 1950 to form the Special Capital Area of Jakarta. Subsequently four of the regencies which were separated off in 2000 to form the new Banten Province and five new independent cities were created between 1996 and 2002 by separation from their surrounding regencies - **Bekasi** on 16 December 1996, **Depok** on 27 April 1999, **Cimahi** and **Tasikmalaya** on 21 June 2001, and **Banjar** on 11 December 2002. A 17th regency was formed on 2 January 2007 – [West Bandung Regency](19996866.md) – from the western half of Bandung Regency, and an 18th regency was formed in October 2012 – [Pangandaran Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangandaran_Regency) – from the southern half of Ciamis Regency. On 25 October 2013, the [People's Representative Council](737841.md) (DPR) began reviewing draft laws on the establishment of 57 prospective new regencies (and eight new provinces),[^cite_note-14] including a further three regencies in West Java – South Garut (*Garut Selatan*), North Sukabumi (*Sukabumi Utara*) and West Bogor (*Bogor Barat*) – but no action has followed in view of the moratorium adopted since 2013 by the Indonesian government on the creation of new provinces, regencies and cities, so none of these three prospective regencies are shown separately on the map below, nor in the following table.

## Geography



View of the mount and the [crater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanic_crater) of [Tangkuban Parahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangkuban_Perahu) in [Lembang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lembang). Tangkuban Parahu is the centre point of [Sundanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_people) culture according to the local legend of [Sangkuriang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangkuriang)

West Java borders Jakarta and [Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten) province to the west and [Central Java](453584.md) to the east. To the north is the [Java Sea](467143.md). To the south is the [Indian Ocean](14580.md). Unlike most other provinces in Indonesia which have their capitals in coastal areas, the provincial capital, Bandung, is located in the mountainous area in the centre of the province. [Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten) Province was formerly part of West Java but was created a separate province in 2000. West Java, in the densely populated western third of Java and covering a land area of 37,053.33 km<sup>2</sup> (larger than [Guinea-Bissau](12186.md) or [Ulyanovsk Oblast](481965.md)), is home to almost one out of every five Indonesians.



View of the canyon of [Cukang Taneuh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cukang_Taneuh), which is a famous canyon in West Java

West Java and Banten provinces, as a part of the [Pacific Ring of Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_of_Fire), have more mountains and volcanoes than any of the other provinces in Indonesia. The vast volcanic mountainous region of inland West Java is traditionally known as [Parahyangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parahyangan) (also known as Priangan or Preanger) which means "The abode of [hyangs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyang) (gods)". It is considered as the heartland of the Sundanese people. The highest point of West Java is the [stratovolcano](162404.md) [Mount Cereme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Ciremai) (3,078 metres) bordering [Kuningan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuningan) and [Majalengka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majalengka) Regencies. West Java has rich and fertile volcanic soil. Agriculture, mostly traditional dry rice cultivation (known as *ladang* or *huma*), has become the primary way of life of traditional Sundanese people. Since the era of the [Dutch East India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_East_India_Company) (VOC), West Java has been known as a productive plantation area for coffee, tea, quinine, and many other cash crops. The mountainous region of West Java is also a major producer of vegetables and decorative flowering plants. The landscape of the province is one of volcanic mountains, rugged terrain, forest, mountains, rivers, fertile agricultural land, and natural sea harbours.[^cite_note-19]

Flowing through the [Bandung Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung_metropolitan_area) to the northeast is the [Tarum River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citarum_River) (or *Ci-tarum*), the longest and most important river in the province. This 300-km long river is the site of three dams, namely [Cirata Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirata_Dam), [Saguling Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saguling_Dam), and [Jatiluhur Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jatiluhur_Dam).

## Economy

Initially, the economy of the [Sundanese people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_people) in West Java relied heavily on [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice) cultivation. Ancient kingdoms established in the province such as the [Tarumanagara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarumanagara) and [Sunda Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunda_Kingdom) are known to have relied on rice taxes and agriculture revenues. The cycle of life of the ancient Sundanese people revolved around the rice crop cycle. Traditional rice harvest festivals such as the [Seren Taun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seren_taun) were important. The ancient goddess of rice, [Nyai Pohaci Sanghyang Asri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewi_Sri), is revered in Sundanese culture. Traditionally, Sundanese people often used dry rice cultivation (*ladang*). After the [Mataram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mataram_Sultanate) expanded to the Priangan area in the early 17th century following the [Sultan Agung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Agung_of_Mataram) campaign against Dutch Batavia, *sawah* (wet rice cultivation) began to be adopted in the northern lowlands of West Java. Regencies such as [Indramayu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indramayu), [Cirebon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirebon), [Subang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subang_Regency), [Karawang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karawang) and [Bekasi](1915107.md) are now well known as vital rice-producing areas. The mountainous region of West Java supplies vegetables, flower and much horticultural produce to Jakarta and Bandung, while animal farms in West Java produce dairy products and meats.

### Colonial period



[Bogor Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogor_Palace) seen from the [Bogor Botanical Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogor_Botanical_Gardens)

During the entire Dutch colonial era, West Java fell under Dutch administration centered in [Batavia](16275.md). The Dutch colonial government introduced [cash crops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_crop) such as [tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea), [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee), and [quinine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinine). Since the 18th century, West Java (known as "De Preanger") was known as a productive plantation area and became integrated with global trade and economy. Services such as transportation and banking were provided to cater for wealthy Dutch plantation owners. West Java is known as one of the earliest developed regions in the Indonesian archipelago. In the early 20th century, the Dutch colonial government developed infrastructures for economic purposes, especially to support Dutch plantations in the region. Roads and railways were constructed to connect inland plantations area with urban centres such as Bandung and the port of Batavia.

### Post independence

After Indonesian independence in 1945, West Java became a supporting region for [Jakarta](16275.md), the capital of Indonesia. Jakarta remained as the business and political centre of Indonesia. Several regencies and cities in West Java such as [Bogor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogor), [Bekasi](1915107.md) and [Depok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depok) were developed as supporting areas for Jakarta and came to form the [Greater Jakarta](1340014.md) area or *Jabodetabek* (**Ja**karta, **Bo**gor, **De**pok, **Ta**ngerang and **Bek**asi). The northern area of West Java has become a major industrial area, with areas such as Bekasi, [Cikarang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cikarang) and [Karawang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karawang) sprawling with factories and industries. The area in and around [Bandung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung) has also developed as an industrial area.

West Java also produces horticulture consists of 2,938,624 tons vegetables, 3,193,744 tons fruits, and 159,871 tons medicines plants/ bio pharmacology.

Forest in West Java covers 764,387.59 ha or 20.62% from the total size of the province. It consists of productive forest 362,980.40 ha (9.79%), protected forest 228,727.11 ha (6.17%), and conservation forest 172,680 ha (4.63%). Mangrove forest reaches 40,129.89 ha, and spread in 10 regencies where coasts are available. Besides, there is also another protected forest of about 32,313.59 ha organized by Perum Perhutani Unit III West Java and Banten.

From the productive forest, in 2006 West Java harvested crop of about 200,675 m³ wood, although the need for wood in this province every year is about 4 million m³. Until 2006, populace forest's width 214,892 ha with wood production is about 893,851.75 m³. West Java also produces non-forest's crop which is potential enough to be developed as forestry work, such as silk, mushroom, pine, dammar, maleleuca, rattan, bamboo, and swallow bird's nest.

In the fishery sector, commodities include goldfish, nila fish, milkfish, freshwater catfish, windu shrimp, green mussel, gouramy, patin, seaweed and vaname shrimp. In 2006, this province harvested 560,000 tons of fish from fishery cultivation crop and brackish or 63.63% from fishery production total in West Java.



[Rice terrace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrace_\(earthworks\)) in Panyaweuyan, [Majalengka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majalengka). Rice terraces are grow very well on the island of Java, especially in West Java.

In the poultry field, dairy cow, domestic poultry, and ducks are common commodities in West Java. 2006 data stated that there are 96,796 dairy cows (25% of the national population), 4,249,670 sheep, 28,652,493 domestic poultries, and 5,596,882 ducks (16% of the national population). Now there are only 245,994 beef cattle in West Java (3% national population), whereas the need every year is about 300,000 beef cattle.

This province has many plantation crops, such as tea, cloves, coconut, rubber, cacao, tobacco, coffee, sugar, palm and akar wangi ([Chrysopogon zizanioides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrysopogon_zizanioides)). From all those commodities, cloves, coconut, rubber, cocoa, tobacco, and coffee are common in West Java. From area side, the best productivity, that is plan area's width equals with the plant's width that produces tobacco and sugar palm commodities. From the production side, the highest productivity is oil palm (6.5 tons per ha) and sugar palm (5.5 tons per ha).

West Java also has several mining operations. In 2006, it contributed 5,284 tons zeolite, 47,978 tons bentonite, iron sand, pozzolan cement, feldspar, and jewel barn/ gemstone. Precious stone mining potential generally is found in Garut, Tasikmalaya, Kuningan, and Sukabumi Regency areas.

As consequences of having many volcanoes, West Java has the potential of [geothermal energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geothermal_energy). There are eleven points of geothermal energy, and three, i.e. [Papandayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Papandayan), [Ceremai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Ciremai), and [Gede Pangrango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Pangrango) have conducted pre-exploration.[^cite_note-21]

Raw natural resources include [chalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalk), several offshore oilfields in the [Java Sea](467143.md), and lumber. Most of the province is very fertile, with a mix of small farms and larger plantations. There are several [hydropower](14073.md) dams, including [Jatiluhur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jatiluhur_Dam), [Saguling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saguling_Dam), [Cirata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirata_Dam), and [Jatigede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jatigede_Dam).

### Tourism



&#x20;[Kawah Putih](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawah_Putih) in [Southern Bandung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung_Regency) is a [Volcanic crater lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanic_crater_lake)

Tourism is an important industry in West Java, and the [Bandung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung) and [Puncak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puncak) areas have long been known as popular weekend destinations for Jakartans. Today, Bandung has developed into a historical tourist destination, with the [Merdeka Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merdeka_Building) and [Braga Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braga_Street) shopping area popular not only among locals, but also with visitors from Malaysia and Singapore.[^cite_note-22] The history-rich coastal city of [Cirebon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirebon) is also a cultural tourism destination since the city has several [kratons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraton_\(Indonesia\)) and historical sites such as Gua Sunyaragi. Other tourist destinations include the [Bogor Botanical Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogor_Botanical_Gardens), [Taman Safari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taman_Safari), [Tangkuban Perahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangkuban_Perahu) crater, [Pelabuhanratu Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palabuhanratu_Bay), Ciater hot springs, [Kawah Putih](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawah_Putih) crater to the south of Bandung, [Pangandaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangandaran) beach, and various mountain resorts in Cianjur, Garut, Tasikmalaya, and Kuningan.

### Ethnic and linguistic composition

West Java is the native homeland of the [Sundanese people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_people) which forms the largest ethnic group in West Java. Since Jakarta and the surrounding area, including West Java, is the business and political centre of Indonesia, the province has attracted various people from other parts of Indonesia. The biggest minority are the [Javanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javanese_people), who migrated to the province centuries ago. Other [Native Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Indonesians) ethnic groups such as [Minangkabau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minangkabau_people), [Batak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batak), [Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malays_\(ethnic_group\)), [Madurese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madurese_people), [Balinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balinese_people), [Ambonese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambonese_people), and many other Indonesians who migrated to and settled in West Java cities can also be easily found. The urban areas also have a significant population of [Chinese Indonesians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Indonesians).

In addition to [Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language), the official national language, the other widely spoken language in the province is [Sundanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_language). In some areas near the southern borders with [Central Java](453584.md), [Javanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javanese_language) is also spoken, especially [Banyumasan Javanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banyumasan_Javanese), then also the [Betawi language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betawi_language) which is dominant in the cities of [Bekasi](1915107.md), [Depok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depok), and half in the western and northern parts of [Bekasi Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bekasi_Regency), and the northern part of [Bogor Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogor_Regency). The main language spoken in [Cirebon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirebon) and nearby areas ([Majalengka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majalengka), [Indramayu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indramayu), [Sumber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumber,_Cirebon)) is [Cirebonese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirebonese_people#Language), a dialect of Javanese with Sundanese influence.[^cite_note-24]

### Religion

As of 2022, [Islam](6037917.md) is the largest religion in West Java, being practiced by 97.34% of the population. Minority religions are [Christianity](5211.md) with 2.39% ([Protestantism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism) 1.78% and [Roman Catholic](606848.md) 0.61%), [Buddhism](3267529.md) 0.2%, [Hinduism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism) 0.035%, [Confucianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confucianism) 0.025% and [Folk religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_religion) 0.01% of the population.[^cite_note-RELIGION-25]

## Cultures

See also: [Sundanese people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_people)

The Sundanese share the Java island with the Javanese and primarily live in West Java. Although the Sundanese live on the same island as the Javanese, their culture is distinct and likewise consider themselves to live in a separate cultural area called *Pasundan* or *Tatar Sunda*. Someone moving from West Java to Central or East Java is literally said to be moving from Sunda to Java worlds. Bandung is considered as the cultural heartland of [Sundanese people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_people), and many indigenous Sundanese artforms were developed in this city. The nearby province of [Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten) is similar in this regard and is also considered to be part of *Pasundan* as well.

### Music



[Kacapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacapi) and [Suling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suling) Orchestra

The musical arts of Sunda, which is an expression of the emotions of Sundanese culture, express politeness and grace of Sundanese. [Degung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamelan_degung) orchestra consists of Sundanese [gamelan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamelan). In addition to the Sundanese forms of Gamelan in [Parahyangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parahyangan), the region of Cirebon retains its own distinct musical traditions. Amongst Cirebons' varying Gamelan ensembles the two most frequently heard are Gamelan Pelog (a non-equidistant heptatonic tuning system) and Gamelan Prawa (a semi-equidistant pentatonic tuning system). Gamelan Pelog is traditionally reserved for Tayuban, Wayang Cepak, and listening and dance music of the Kratons in Cirebon, while Gamelan Prawa is traditionally reserved for Wayang Purwa.

[Cirebon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirebon) also retains specialised Gamelan ensembles including Sekaten, which is played in the Kratons to mark important times in the Islamic calendar, Denggung, also a Kraton ensemble, which is believed to have some "supernatural powers", and Renteng, an ensemble found in both Cirebon and Parahyangan known for its loud and energetic playing style.



[Tembang Sunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tembang_Sunda) performance a origin sundanese vocal music performance in 2010

[Tembang Sunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tembang_Sunda) is a genre of Sundanese vocal music accompanied by a core ensemble of two [Kacapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacapi) (zither) and a [Suling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suling) (bamboo flute). The music and poetry of tembang Sunda are closely associated with the Parahyangan, the highland plateau that transverses the central and southern parts of Sunda. The natural environment of Priangan, an agricultural region surrounded by mountains and volcanoes, is reflected in some songs of the tembang Sunda.[^cite_note-26]

[Kacapi suling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacapi_suling) is tembang Sunda minus vocal. [Tarawangsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarawangsa) is a genuine popular art is performed on ensemble consists of tarawangsa (a violin with an end pin) and the jentreng (a kind of seven-stringed zither). It is accompanied by a secret dance called Jentreng. The dance is a part of a ritual celebrating the goddess of paddy Dewi Sri. Its ceremonial significance is associated with a ritual of thanksgiving associated with the rice harvest. Tarawangsa can also be played for healing or even purely for entertainment.



[Angklung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angklung) is one of the most famous musical instruments in West Java

The three main types of Sundanese bamboo ensembles are [angklung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angklung), [calung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calung), [karinding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karinding) and arumba. The exact features of each ensemble vary according to context, related instruments, and relative popularity. Angklung is a generic term for sets of tuned, shaken bamboo rattles. Angklung consists of a frame upon which hang several different lengths of hollow bamboo. Angklungs are played like handbells, with each instrument played to a different note. Angklung rattles are played in interlocking patterns, usually with only one or two instruments played per person. The ensemble is used in Sundanese processions, sometimes with trance or acrobatics.

Performed at life-cycle rituals and feasts (hajat), angklung is believed to maintain balance and harmony in the village. In its most modern incarnation, angklung is performed in schools as an aid to learning music. The Angklung received international attention when Daeng Soetigna, from Bandung, expanded the angklung notations not only to play traditional pélog or sléndro scales but also diatonic scale in 1938. Since then, angklung is often played together with other Western musical instruments in an orchestra. One of the first well-known performances of angklung in an orchestra was during the [Bandung Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung_Conference) in 1955. Like those in angklung, the instruments of the calung ensemble are of bamboo, but each consists of several differently tuned tubes fixed onto a piece of bamboo; the player holds the instrument in his left hand and strikes it with a beater held in his right.

The highest-pitched calung has the highest number of tubes and the densest musical activity; the lowest-pitched, with two tubes, has the least. Calung is nearly always associated with earthy humour, and is played by men. Arumba refers to a set of diatonically tuned bamboo xylophones, often played by women. It is frequently joined by modern instruments, including a drum set, electric guitar, bass, and keyboards.

### Theatre



[Wayang Golek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayang_golek), a traditional Sundanese puppetry.

*Wayang golek* is a traditional form of puppetry from Sunda. Unlike the better-known leather shadow puppets (*[wayang kulit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayang_kulit)*) found in the rest of Java and Bali, *wayang golek* puppets are made from wood and are three-dimensional, rather than two. They use a banana palm in which the puppets stand, behind which one puppeteer (*dalang*) is accompanied by his *gamelan* orchestra with up to 20 musicians. The *gamelan* uses a five-note scale as opposed to the seven-note western scale. The musicians are guided by the drummer, who in turn is guided by signals from the puppet master dalang gives to change the mood or pace required. *Wayang golek* are used by the Sundanese to tell the epic play "Mahabarata", and various other morality-type plays. [Sandiwara Sunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandiwara) is a type folk theater performed in [Sundanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_language) and presenting [Sundanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_people) themes, folklores and stories.

### Dance



[Cirebonese mask dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirebonese_mask_dance)

Sundanese and Cirebonese dance shows the influence of the many groups that have traded and settled in the area over the centuries, and includes variations from graceful to dynamic syncopated drumming patterns, quick wrist flicks, sensual hip movements, and fast shoulder and torso isolations. *[Jaipongan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipongan)* is probably the most popular traditional social dance of Sundanese people and [Cirebonese mask dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirebonese_mask_dance) is the most popular traditional social dance of Cirebonese people . It can be performed in solo, groups, or pair. The *Tari Merak* ([Peafowl Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_dance)) is a female dance inspired by the movements of a peafowl and its feathers blended with the classical movements of the Sundanese dance.

### Folktales and legend stories



A painting depicting [Nyai Loro Kidul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyai_Roro_Kidul)

There are stories and folktales transcribed from [Pantun Sunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantun_Sunda) stories.[^cite_note-27] Among the most well-known folktale and stories are:

- [Mundinglaya Dikusumah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mundinglaya_Dikusumah), which tells of Mundinglaya visiting Jabaning Langit to find layang Salaka Domas. It is a symbolic story of Surawisesa visiting Malaka to establish a peace treaty with the Portuguese before 1522.

- [Lutung Kasarung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutung_Kasarung), tells the life of a beautiful princess, in the era of Pasir Batang kingdom, a vassal of Sunda kingdom. She faces the evil of her older sister willing to seize her right as a queen.

  [^cite_note-28]

- [Ciung Wanara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciung_Wanara), tells of the fight of two princes of Sunda kingdom and the history of Cipamali river (present-day [Brebes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brebes_Regency) river) as a boundary between Sundanese and Javanese territories.

- [Sangkuriang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangkuriang), which tells the story of the creation of Mount [Tangkuban Parahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangkuban_Perahu) and the ancient lake [Bandung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung).

  [^cite_note-29]

- [Nyai Loro Kidul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyai_Roro_Kidul) (also spelt *Nyi Roro Kidul*) is a legendary female spirit or [deity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deity), known as the Queen of the Southern Sea of Java ([Indian Ocean](14580.md) or Samudra Kidul south of [Java island](69336.md)) in [Sundanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mythology_of_Indonesia#Sunda) as well in [Javanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kejaw%C3%A8n) and [Balinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balinese_mythology) mythology.

## Cuisine

Main article: [Sundanese cuisine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_cuisine)



A typical modest Sundanese meal consists of steamed rice, fried salted fish, *[sayur asem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayur_asem)* (vegetable with tamarind based soup), *[lalab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalab) [sambal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sambal)* (raw vegetables salad with chili paste) and *[karedok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karedok)* (vegetable salad with peanuts paste).

[Sundanese cuisine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundanese_cuisine) is one of the most famous traditional food in Indonesia, and it is also easily found in most Indonesian cities. The Sundanese food is characterized by its freshness; the famous *[lalab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalab)* (raw vegetables salad) eaten with *[sambal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sambal)* (chili paste), and also *[karedok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karedok)* (peanuts paste) demonstrate the Sundanese fondness for fresh raw vegetables. Similar to other ethnic groups in Indonesia, Sundanese people eat [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice) for almost every meal. The Sundanese like to say, "If you have not eaten rice, then you have not eaten at all." Rice is prepared in hundreds of different ways. However, it is simple steamed rice that serves as the centrepiece of all meals.

Next to [steamed rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooked_rice), the side dishes of [vegetables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetable), [fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish), or [meat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meat) are added to provide a variety of tastes as well as for protein, mineral and nutrient intake. These side dishes are grilled, fried, steamed or boiled and spiced with any combination of garlic, [galangal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galangal) (a plant of the ginger family), turmeric, coriander, ginger, and lemongrass. The herb-rich food wrapped and cooked inside banana leaf called *[pepes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pepes)* (Sundanese: *pais*) is popular among Sundanese people. *Pepes* are available in many varieties according to their ingredients; carp fish, anchovies, minced meat with eggs, mushroom, tofu or oncom. *[Oncom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oncom)* is a fermented peanut-based ingredient that is prevalent within Sundanese cuisine, just like its counterpart, Tempe, which is popular among Javanese people. Usually, the food itself is not too spicy, but it is served with a boiling sauce made by grinding chilli peppers and garlic together. On the coast, saltwater fish are common; in the mountains, fish tend to be either pond-raised carp or goldfish. A well-known Sundanese dish is *lalapan*, which consists only of raw vegetables, such as [papaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papaya) leaves, [cucumber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cucumber), [eggplant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eggplant), and [bitter melon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Momordica_charantia).[^cite_note-31]
