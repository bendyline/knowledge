**Ali Mohammad Naini** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): علی محمد نائینی‎; 19 March 1957 – 20 March 2026) was an Iranian military officer who served as the spokesperson of the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC) from 2024 until his death in 2026.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Career

Naeini was a veteran with four decades of service, including participation in the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War), and held several senior roles during his career, such as serving as the IRGC’s cultural deputy and the [Basij](2892082.md)’s cultural and social deputy. He succeeded Brigadier General Ramazan Sharif as the IRGC spokesperson, an appointment made by Commander-in-Chief Major General [Hossein Salami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hossein_Salami).[^cite_note-LiveMint-6]

He was widely regarded as one of the IRGC’s leading experts in [psychological warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_warfare), [soft power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_power), and [cognitive warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_warfare).[^cite_note-LiveMint-6]

Naini was also a professor of social sciences and faculty member at [Imam Hossein University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imam_Hossein_University).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] The United Kingdom sanctioned Naini following the [October 2024 Iranian missile strikes on Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_2024_Iranian_strikes_on_Israel).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

## Death

Naini was assassinated on 20 March 2026 by the Israeli strikes during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md). His death was officially confirmed by Iranian news media reports.[^cite_note-14]

According to media reports, Abdullah Haji Sadeghi, the representative of the Supreme Leader of Iran in the IRGC, appointed Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi as the new spokesperson.[^cite_note-15]
