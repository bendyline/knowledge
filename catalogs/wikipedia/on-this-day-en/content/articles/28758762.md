# Lô River

"Lo River" redirects here. For rivers in China, see [Luo River (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luo_River_\(disambiguation\)).



Lô River at the south of [Hà Giang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A0_Giang) town, Vietnam 2005.



Lô River at [Phú Thọ Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph%C3%BA_Th%E1%BB%8D_Province).

The **Lô River** ([Vietnamese](32511.md): *Sông Lô*) is a major river of [Vietnam](202354.md). It flows through [Hà Giang Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A0_Giang_Province), [Tuyên Quang Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuy%C3%AAn_Quang_Province) and [Phú Thọ Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph%C3%BA_Th%E1%BB%8D_Province) for 470 kilometres and has a basin area of 39,000 km<sup>2</sup> and originates in [Yunnan](166410.md), China.[^cite_note-1]
