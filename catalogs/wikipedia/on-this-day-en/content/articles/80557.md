**Kern County** is located in the [U.S. state](18618239.md) of [California](5407.md). As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), the population was 909,235.[^cite_note-quickfacts-4] Its [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) is [Bakersfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakersfield,_California).[^cite_note-GR6-6]

Kern County comprises the Bakersfield, California, [metropolitan statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_statistical_area). The county spans the southern end of the [Central Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Valley_\(California\)). Covering 8,161.42 square miles (21,138.0 km<sup>2</sup>), it ranges west to the southern slope of the [Coast Ranges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Coast_Ranges), and east beyond the southern slope of the eastern [Sierra Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Nevada_\(U.S.\)) into the [Mojave Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojave_Desert), at the city of [Ridgecrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridgecrest,_California). Its northernmost city is [Delano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delano,_California), and its southern reach extends to just beyond [Frazier Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazier_Park,_California), and the northern extremity of the parallel [Antelope Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antelope_Valley).

The county's economy is heavily linked to agriculture and petroleum extraction. Also, a strong aviation, space, and military industry is present, such as [Edwards Air Force Base](107530.md), the [China Lake Naval Air Weapons Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Air_Weapons_Station_China_Lake), and the [Mojave Air and Space Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojave_Air_and_Space_Port).

With a population that is 54.9% Hispanic as of 2020, Kern is California's [third-most populous majority-Hispanic county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Majority-Hispanic_or_Latino_Counties_in_the_U.S.) and the [sixth-largest nationwide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Majority-Hispanic_or_Latino_Counties_in_the_U.S.).[^cite_note-2020Hispanic-7]

## History

### Indigenous Era

Native Americans lived in this region for hundreds of years: [Chumash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chumash_people), tribes grouped together under the settler name [Yokuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yokuts), and others.[^cite_note-8]

### Spanish era

Spain claimed the area in 1769. Entering from [Grapevine Canyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grapevine_Canyon,_California) to the south in 1772, Commander [Don Pedro Fages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Fages) became the first European known to set foot in the area.

The Battle of San Emigdio took place in Kern County in March 1824. The Chumash Native Americans of [Mission Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission_Santa_Barbara) rebelled against the Mexican government and its taking over mission property and ejecting the natives. The battle occurred in the canyon where San Emigdio Creek flows down [San Emigdio Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Emigdio_Mountains) and the Blue Ridge, south of Bakersfield near today's Highway 166. Mexican forces from [Monterey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterey,_California) were commanded by Carlos Carrillo and the conflict was a low-casualty encounter, with only four Native Americans being killed and no Mexicans. The surviving Native Americans were pacified and brought back to Santa Barbara in June 1824 after a pursuit and negotiation, in which many were allowed to keep their arms for the return march over the mountains.[^cite_note-9]

### American era



The Havilah Court Building was restored in the 1970s and served as a museum until its destruction in the 2024 Borel Fire: Photo *circa* 2007.

In the beginning, what was to become Kern County was dominated by mining in the mountains and in the desert. In 1855, the California legislature attempted to form a county in the area by giving the southeastern territory of Tulare County on the west of the Sierra Nevada Mountains to [Buena Vista County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buena_Vista_County,_California). it was never officially organized prior to 1859, though, when the enabling legislation expired. The south of Tulare County was later organized as Kern County in 1866, with additions from [Los Angeles](79734.md) and [San Bernardino Counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Bernardino_County,_California). Its first county seat was the mining town of [Havilah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Havilah,_California), in the mountains east of Bakersfield and north of [Tehachapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehachapi,_California).[^cite_note-10]

Settlers considered the flat land of the valley inhospitable and impassable at the time due to swamps, lakes, tule reeds, and diseases such as malaria. This changed when residents started draining land for farming and constructing canals, most dug by hired Chinese laborers. Within 10 years, the valley surpassed the mining areas as the economic power of the county, and as a result, the county seat was moved from Havilah to Bakersfield in 1874.

In 1899, the discovery well of the [Kern River Oil Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_River_Oil_Field) was dug by hand,[^cite_note-Historic-11] and soon the towns of [Oil City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_City,_California), Oil Center, and [Oildale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oildale) came into existence.[^cite_note-Historic-11]

#### Etymology

The county derives its name from the [Kern River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_River), which was named for [Edward Kern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Kern), [cartographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartographer) for General [John C. Frémont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_C._Fr%C3%A9mont)'s 1845 expedition, which crossed [Walker Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walker_Pass). The Kern River was originally named Rio Bravo de San Felipe by Father [Francisco Garcés](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Garc%C3%A9s) when he explored the area in 1776.

Throughout recorded history, severe earthquakes have struck Kern County, including the [1857 Fort Tejon earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1857_Fort_Tejon_earthquake).

On July 21, 1952, an earthquake occurred with the [epicenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicenter) about 23 miles (37 km) south of Bakersfield. It measured 7.3 on the [moment magnitude scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_magnitude_scale) and killed 12 people. In addition to the deaths, it was responsible for hundreds of injuries and more than \$60 million in property damage. The main shock was felt over much of California and as far away as Phoenix, Arizona, and Reno, Nevada. The earthquake occurred on the White Wolf Fault and was the strongest to occur in California since the [1906 San Francisco earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1906_San_Francisco_earthquake). Tehachapi suffered the greatest damage and loss of life from the earthquake, though its effects were widely felt throughout central and southern California. The event had a significant [aftershock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftershock) sequence that persisted into July and August, with the strongest coming on August 22, an M5.8 event with a maximum perceived intensity of VIII (severe) and resulted in two additional deaths and an additional \$10 million in property damage. Repercussions of the sequence of earthquakes were still being felt in the heavily damaged downtown area of Bakersfield well into the 1990s, as city leaders attempted to improve safety of the surviving unreinforced masonry buildings.

Following the event, a field survey was conducted along the fault zone, with the goal of estimating the peak ground acceleration of the shock based on visually evaluating rock formations and other indicators. Ground disturbances that were created by the earthquakes were also surveyed, both in the valley and in the foothills, with both vertical and horizontal displacements present in the epicenter area. The motion records that were acquired from the event were significant, and a reconnaissance report was recognized for its coverage of the event, and its setting a standard for similar engineering or scientific papers.

#### Abuse trials

Between 1983 and 1986, several ritual sex ring [child-abuse cases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_county_child_abuse_cases) occurred in Kern County, resulting in numerous long prison sentences, all of which were overturned—some of them decades later, because the prosecutors had coerced false testimonies from the purported child victims.[^cite_note-Mean_Justice's_Dirty_Secrets,_Rolling_Stone_Magazine-12] The details of these false accusations are covered extensively in the 2008 documentary *Witch Hunt*, narrated by [Sean Penn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Penn).[^cite_note-13]

## Geography



Map of Kern County

According to the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the county has a total area of 8,163 square miles (21,140 km<sup>2</sup>), of which 31 square miles (80 km<sup>2</sup>) (0.4%) is covered by water.[^cite_note-GR1-14] It is the third-largest county by area in California. The tallest peak in the county is Sawmill Mountain with an elevation of 2,689 metres (8,822 feet). Its area is nearly the size of the state of [New Hampshire](21134.md); it extends:

- East beyond the southern slope of the [Sierra Nevada range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Nevada_\(U.S.\)) into the [Mojave Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojave_Desert), and includes parts of the [Indian Wells Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Wells_Valley) and [Antelope Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antelope_Valley)

- West from the Sierra across the floor of the [San Joaquin Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Joaquin_Valley) to the eastern edge of the [Temblor Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temblor_Range), part of the [Coast Ranges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Coast_Ranges)

- South over the ridge of the [Tehachapi Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehachapi_Mountains)

### Air quality



Particulate pollution in Kern County varies with the seasons.

Kern County suffers from severe air pollution. Particulates cause poor visibility, especially in the winter. Western Kern County lies in the [San Joaquin Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Joaquin_Valley) and the topography traps pollutants. Although the topography is not as unfavorable in eastern Kern County, it is a [non-attainment area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-attainment_area) for particulates.[^cite_note-15] Air pollution caused by [particulates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particulates) is "in the unhealthy range an average of 40 days a year, according to the [American Lung Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Lung_Association)'s  *2018 State of the Air Report*.[^cite_note-16]

### Vegetation

[Chaparral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaparral) comprises a considerable portion of the natural area within Kern County; the species diversity within these chaparral habitats, however, is considerably less than in many other regions of California.[^cite_note-17] [Whitethorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vachellia_constricta) is a prominent example of chaparral species on the rocky slopes of the Sierra Nevada and the Inner Coastal Ranges.[^cite_note-18] [California buckeye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_buckeye) is a notable tree found in both chaparral and forests and whose southern range terminates in Kern County.[^cite_note-19]

### 2020 census

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), the county had a population of 909,235. The median age was 32.8 years. 28.3% of residents were under the age of 18 and 11.8% of residents were 65 years of age or older. For every 100 females there were 102.5 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 101.9 males age 18 and over.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-28][^cite_note-Census2020PL-29]

The racial makeup of the county was 40.9% White, 5.5% [Black or African American](2154.md), 2.0% [American Indian and Alaska Native](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 5.1% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans), 0.2% [Native Hawaiian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Hawaiians) and [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander), 30.2% from some other race, and 16.1% from [two or more races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial_Americans). [Hispanic or Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) residents of any race comprised 54.9% of the population.[^cite_note-Census2020PL-29]

86.6% of residents lived in urban areas, while 13.4% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-Census2020DHC-30]

There were 281,498 households in the county, of which 42.9% had children under the age of 18 living with them and 25.5% had a female householder with no spouse or partner present. About 19.6% of all households were made up of individuals and 8.1% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-28]

There were 301,009 housing units, of which 6.5% were vacant. Among occupied housing units, 59.2% were owner-occupied and 40.8% were renter-occupied. The homeowner vacancy rate was 1.5% and the rental vacancy rate was 4.8%.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-28]

### 2010 census

The [2010 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_census) reported that Kern County had a population of 839,631. The [racial makeup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_and_ethnicity_in_the_United_States_Census) of Kern County was 499,766 (59.5%) White, 48,921 (5.8%) African American, 12,676 (1.5%) Native American, 34,846 (4.2%) Asian, 1,252 (0.1%) Pacific Islander, 204,314 (24.3%) from other races, and 37,856 (4.5%) from two or more races. Hispanics or Latinos of any race were 413,033 persons (49.2%); 43.4% of Kern County residents are of Mexican heritage, 1.0% Salvadoran, 0.5% Colombian, and 0.4% Guatemalan.[^cite_note-54]

### 2000 census

According to the [2000 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_United_States_census),[^cite_note-GR8-55] 661,645 people, 208,652 households, and 156,489 families resided in the county. The population density was 81 people per square mile (31 people/km<sup>2</sup>). The 231,564 housing units had an average density of 28 units per square mile (11 units/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of the county was 61.6% White, 6.0% Black or African American, 3.4% Asian, 1.5% Native American, 0.2% Pacific Islander, 23.2% from other races, and 4.1% from two or more races. About 38.4% of the population were Hispanics or Latinos of any race; 8.4% were of German, 7.2% American, and 5.7% Irish ancestry, according to the census, and 66.8% spoke English, 29.1% Spanish, and 1.0% [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language) as their first language.

Of the 208,652 households, 42.2% had children under 18 living with them, 54.6% were married couples living together, 14.5% had a female householder with no husband present, and 25.0% were not families. About 20.3% of all households were made up of individuals, and 7.8% had someone living alone who was 65 or older. The average household size was 3.03 and the average family size was 3.50.[^cite_note-56]

In the county, the age distribution was 31.9% under 18, 10.2% from 18 to 24, 29.8% from 25 to 44, 18.7% from 45 to 64, and 9.4% who were 65 or older. The median age was 31 years. For every 100 females there were 105.3 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 105.3 males.

The median income for a household in the county was \$35,446, and for a family was \$39,403. Males had a median income of \$38,097 versus \$25,876 for females. The [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income) for the county was \$15,760. About 16.8% of families and 20.8% of the population were below the [poverty line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_threshold), including 27.8% of those under 18 and 10.5% of those 65 or over.

## Government, policing, and politics

### Government

Kern County is a [California Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Constitution)-defined general-law county and is governed by an elected board of supervisors. The board consists of five members, elected by districts, who serve four-year, staggered terms. The county government provides countywide services such as elections and voter registration, some law enforcement, jails, vital records, property records, tax collection, public health, and social services. In addition, the county serves as the local government for all unincorporated areas.

### Safety

#### Fire



Logo of the Kern County Fire Department

The Kern County Fire Department  provides fire protection and emergency response services for the unincorporated areas of the county, as well as the cities of [Arvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvin,_California), [Delano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delano,_California), [Maricopa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maricopa,_California), [McFarland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McFarland,_California), [Ridgecrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridgecrest,_California), [Shafter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shafter,_California), [Taft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taft,_California), [Tehachapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehachapi,_California) and [Wasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasco,_California).[^cite_note-61]

Bakersfield has its own fire department, the [Bakersfield Fire Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakersfield_Fire_Department).

#### Sheriff

The [Kern County Sheriff's Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_County_Sheriff's_Office) provides court protection, jail administration, and coroner services for the entire county of around 900,000 in population. It provides patrol and detective services for the unincorporated areas of the county and by contract to certain municipalities. The main sheriff's office and station is at Bakersfield, with 15 sheriff substations for the widespread county.

#### Municipal police

Municipal police departments in the county are Bakersfield, population 384,000; Delano, 54,000; Ridgecrest, 29,000; Wasco, 28,000; Arvin, 21,000; Shafter, 20,000; McFarland, 15,000; California City, 14,671; Tehachapi, 13,000; Taft, 9,327; and Maricopa (sheriff contract city), 1,200.

### Federal

Kern is a strongly [Republican](32070.md) county in [Presidential](24113.md) and [congressional](31756.md) elections. The last Democratic candidate for president to win a majority in the county was [Lyndon Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyndon_B._Johnson) in [1964](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964_United_States_presidential_election_in_California). The county is also a Republican stronghold at the state level, with Jerry Brown being the last Democrat to win the county in a gubernatorial election in 1978. Kern remains the only county in Southern California that consistently votes Republican in recent elections. While Republican margins in the county had been shrinking, with [Donald Trump](4848272.md)'s 10.2% margin of victory in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election_in_California) being the smallest since [Gerald Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerald_Ford)'s 6.7% majority in [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_United_States_presidential_election_in_California), Trump regained his margins in 2024 and even surpassed previous performances, with his 21% margin being the best since George Bush in 2004. Some constituencies in Kern County in particular some of the most widely contested in California, and the United States as of the early 2020s,[^cite_note-65][^cite_note-66] with the [2022 race for the 22nd US House district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_California) being close, and the [26th Senate District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California's_26th_Senate_District) having one of the tightest margins in electoral history, with incumbent Democrat [Melissa Hurtado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_Hurtado) retaining her seat by 13 votes.[^cite_note-67]

Democratic strength is concentrated in the small agricultural towns in the San Joaquin Valley portion of the county, such as Arvin, Delano, McFarland, Shafter, and Wasco. Unincorporated communities close to agricultural areas, such as Lamont and Lost Hills, are Democratic strongholds. The eastern and southern parts of Bakersfield, along with unincorporated East Bakersfield, have also become reliably Democratic. Republican strength is found in the foothill, mountain, and high desert communities of the county. The cities of Maricopa, Ridgecrest, Taft, and Tehachapi are Republican strongholds. Along with the northern parts of Bakersfield, the wealthy unincorporated area of Rosedale and working-class Oildale are also seen as being strongly Republican. Cities including California City and the western areas of Bakersfield are seen as competitive in most elections. Bakersfield as a whole is seen as being competitive with a Republican lean. The rest of the unincorporated areas of Kern County is seen as strongly Republican.

### Fire

Main article: [Kern County Fire Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_County_Fire_Department)

The Kern County Fire Department (KCFD) is an agency that provides fire protection and emergency medical services for the county of Kern, California, USA. Over 625 permanent employees and 100 extra help employees protect an area which spans over 8,000 square miles (21,000 km<sup>2</sup>). KCFD provides fire protection services for over 500,000 citizens living in the unincorporated areas of Kern County and the cities of [Arvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvin,_California), [Delano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delano,_California), [Maricopa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maricopa,_California), [McFarland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McFarland,_California), [Ridgecrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridgecrest,_California), [Shafter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shafter,_California), [Taft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taft,_California), [Tehachapi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehachapi,_California) and [Wasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasco,_California). This agency is contracted to provide dispatch services for the California City Fire Department. Over 546 uniformed firefighters are stationed in 46 fire stations throughout the county.

### Sheriff's Office

Main article: [Kern County Sheriff's Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_County_Sheriff's_Department)

The Kern County Sheriff's Department is the agency responsible for law enforcement within the county of Kern. The department provides law enforcement within the county, maintains the jails used by both the county and municipal cities, and provides search and rescue. The department contains over 1,200 sworn deputies and civilian employees. Its jurisdiction contains all of the unincorporated areas of Kern County, approximately 8,000 square miles (21,000 km<sup>2</sup>). The department headquarters is located at 1350 Norris Road in Bakersfield. There are 15 additional substations located throughout the county. The metro patrol area is divided into four regions: north, south, east, and west.

In 2009, the district attorney claimed "the highest per capita prison commitment rate of any major California county." Kern County contains multiple state and federal prisons, including two private prisons. The county is among the most prolific with the death penalty, assigning [death penalty](5902.md) sentences in 26 cases since 1976. In 2015 Kern County policemen from all departments killed more people per capita than any other American county. Because of the very harsh local criminal justice system, Kern County has been dubbed "the most punitive authoritarian jurisdiction on the west coast" and "[Oklahoma](22489.md) of the west".[^cite_note-75] In 2015, it was revealed that the Kern County Sheriff's office engaged in a longstanding program of attempted cash payoffs to women who had accused deputies of sexual assault. In the same year, a civil lawsuit filed by a survivor of a sexual assault committed by Kern County Sheriff's deputy Gabriel Lopez was settled for \$1 million.[^cite_note-76]

Kern County had the most deaths per capita in the US by police shooting per an article published in [The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian) on December 1, 2015.[^cite_note-77] In 2015 to the date of publication of the article, there have been 13 deaths by police shootings in a county of less than 875,000 population, or 0.016 per thousand persons. By comparison, during the same period of time in New York City, a population 10 times the size with a police force more than 20 times the size, there were 9 such deaths.[^cite_note-78]

## Economy

The county has a large agricultural base and is a significant producer of [oil](23195.md), [natural gas](22131.md), [hydro-electric power](381399.md), [Biomass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomass), [solar power](13690575.md), and [wind power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_power). Kern is noted for [minerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mineral), including [gold](12240.md), [borate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borate), and [kernite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kernite). The largest [open pit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-pit_mining) mine in California, which mines [borax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borax), is at [Boron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boron,_California).[^cite_note-Rio_Tinto_Borax_home_page-82] As of October 1, 2016, Kern County contains nearly 25% of California's in-state [renewable energy](25784.md) production, including 1,785 MW of solar power and 3,310 MW of wind power.[^cite_note-83] Kern County is home to the [Tehachapi Energy Storage Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehachapi_Energy_Storage_Project), which was commissioned in 2014.[^cite_note-International-84]

### Aerospace and military

Department of Defense facilities include [Edwards Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edwards_AFB,_California) and [China Lake Naval Air Weapons Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Lake_Naval_Air_Weapons_Station). As home to [Edwards Air Force Base](107530.md) the Air Force's main flight test facility, Kern has been the site of many milestones, including the first [supersonic flight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_X-1) and the first landing of the [Space Shuttle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle). The base has brought prosperity to the railroad towns of [Mojave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojave,_California) and [Rosamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosamond,_California).[^cite_note-85] Kern County is also the home of the first inland spaceport in the United States, the [Mojave Spaceport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojave_Spaceport).[^cite_note-86]

### Agriculture

Agriculture has long been one of the county's biggest industries.[^cite_note-Almond-facts-87] Between 2012 and 2013 the produced value of agricultural products increased 6%, to a total of \$6.8 billion.[^cite_note-Almond-facts-87] Grape constituted 31%, [almond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almond) 17%, [milk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milk) 13%, citrus 11%, [cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle) and [calves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calf_\(animal\)) and [pistachio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pistachio) both 7%, [carrots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrot) only 6% (but 80% of carrots produced in the entire United States), [hay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hay) 4%, and [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton) and [potatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potato) both 2%.[^cite_note-Almond-facts-87] This is one of the highest-producing locations in the United States for [vegetables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetable), and also for [watermelons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watermelon).[^cite_note-VRIC-88] Vegetables are estimated to total \$320 million every year.[^cite_note-VRIC-88] There are about 1,938 farms, at an average size of 1,202 acres (486 ha) (however 41% are smaller than 50 acres (20 ha)), being the primary employment of 63% of operators.[^cite_note-Almond-facts-87]

Kern County is a major producer of [almonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almonds)[^cite_note-89] with production greater than 100 million pounds (45,000 t; 50,000 short tons) annually.[^cite_note-Almond-facts-87] That is third of all the counties, 16% of the state's production.[^cite_note-Almond-facts-87] (See also [almond in California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almond_in_California).)

Pistachio is another important employer in the county.[^cite_note-res-middle-of-state-90] The Michailides & Avenot group finds severe [boscalid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boscalid) resistance in [isolates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microbial_isolate) of *[Alternaria alternata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternaria_alternata)* pathogenic on pistachio here.[^cite_note-res-middle-of-state-90] They find extensive such resistance in a swathe from the center down into the central southern part of the state, but especially here.[^cite_note-res-middle-of-state-90] (See also [Pistachio in California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pistachio_in_California) and [boscalid in California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boscalid_in_California).)

The Glassy-Winged Sharpshooter (*Homalodisca vitripennis*) is a major insect pest in the county, including in Kern County's [citrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citrus) groves.[^cite_note-gwss-91] (See also [Glassy-Winged Sharpshooter in California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glassy-Winged_Sharpshooter_in_California).)

### Petroleum

As of 2015, Kern is California's top oil-producing county, with 78% of the state's 56,653 active [oil wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_well) and 71% of oil production.[^cite_note-oil-production-92][^cite_note-93] The county produced 144.5 million barrels of oil in 2015, accounting for about 4% of overall U.S. oil production.[^cite_note-oil-production-92][^cite_note-94]

#### Discovery and development

Oil development began with the 1894 discovery of the [Midway-Sunset Oil Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midway-Sunset_Oil_Field), now the third-largest in the United States, in the southwestern portion of Kern County near Maricopa. The 1899 discovery along the Kern River was a breakthrough in oil production.[^cite_note-Historic-11] Oil was refined here even before the establishment of the county.[^cite_note-Historic-11] The Buena Vista Petroleum Company was organized and incorporated in 1864.[^cite_note-Historic-11] Soon thereafter a refinery was built that operated until April 1867 when work ceased because of high freight charges.[^cite_note-Historic-11]

The 1910 [Lakeview Gusher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakeview_Gusher) was the largest recorded [oil strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_gusher) in U.S. history. The well spewed approximately nine million barrels for 18 months before workers finally were able to cap it.[^cite_note-95]

Other big oil fields in southwestern Kern County discovered early in the 20th century include the [Buena Vista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buena_Vista_Oil_Field), the [South Belridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Belridge_Oil_Field) and the [Cymric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cymric_Oil_Field) fields. The latter is the fastest-growing field in California in terms of barrels produced per year.[^cite_note-Conservation_p._2-96] Later large fields include the [Kern River Oil Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_River_Oil_Field), the fifth-largest in the U.S., the adjacent [Kern Front Oil Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_Front_Oil_Field), the [Mount Poso Oil Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Poso_Oil_Field) in the lower foothills of the Sierra north-northeast of Bakersfield and the [Fruitvale Oil Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruitvale_Oil_Field), which underlies much of the city of Bakersfield, along and north of the Kern River.[^cite_note-Hluza-97][^cite_note-98]

On July 22, 2009, [Occidental Petroleum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occidental_Petroleum) announced it had discovered the equivalent of 150 million to 250 million barrels of oil in Kern County, which the company called the largest oil discovery in California in 35 years. The find added about 10 percent to California's known reserves. Occidental's [Ray Irani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_R._Irani) said it is likely that more oil would be found in the areas outside the initial six wells that tapped the discovery. Occidental has not revealed the exact location of the find, two-thirds of which is natural gas. [BNET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BNET), an industry web publication, said the find would add to the company's 708 million barrels of proven reserves in California.

## Recreation

Outdoor recreational activities include [horseback riding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equestrianism) and [water skiing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_skiing) ([Lake Isabella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Isabella), Lake Buena Vista, [Lake Ming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Ming), and private ski ranches). [Off-roading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-roading) and other motorsports take place at [Jawbone Canyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawbone_Canyon), [California City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_City,_California), [Randsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randsburg,_California), [Willow Springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willow_Springs,_Kern_County,_California), [Buttonwillow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buttonwillow,_California), Bakersfield Speedway, [Famoso Raceway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Famoso_Raceway), and the half-mile [Kevin Harvick's Kern Raceway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Harvick's_Kern_Raceway). [Hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunting), [paintball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paintball), [white-water rafting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafting), [kayaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayaking) ([Kern River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kern_River)), [snow skiing](28478.md) (Shirley Meadows and [Mount Pinos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Pinos)), [shooting ranges](1237973.md) (5 Dogs Range), [hiking](147313.md), [biking](5931.md) (trails, paths, and roads), [camping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camping) and [fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing) are also part of the recreational culture.
