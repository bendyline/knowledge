**Al Makha District** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مديرية المخا) is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Yemen) of the [Taiz Governorate](3604497.md), [Yemen](350939.md). As of 2003, the district had a population of 18,155 inhabitants.[^cite_note-1] The capital lies at [Mocha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mocha,_Yemen).

## Location

It is located in north-west of [Taiz governorate](3604497.md). It is bordered by [Maqbanah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maqbanah_District) and [Al Khawkhah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Khawkhah_District) to the north, [Al-Mandab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhubab_District) to the south, [Mawza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mawza_District) and Maqbanah to the east, [the Red Sea](26191.md) to the west.[^cite_note-2]
