The **Kuwait National Guard** (**KNG**) ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الحرس الوطني الكويتي) is a [paramilitary](146730.md) and [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie) branch of the [Kuwait Armed Forces](16692.md).[^cite_note-ReferenceA-2] The National Guard is an independent combat institution and traces its heritage directly to the [cavalrymen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavalrymen) and [infantrymen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infantrymen) that defended [Kuwait](7515890.md)'s three mounted [defensive walls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensive_wall).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-ReferenceC-4] Since its inception, the Kuwait National Guard under the guidance of the respective leadership; executed, participated in, and supported carrying out all conflicts in which the [Kuwait Armed Forces](16692.md) have engaged since 1967.[^cite_note-ReferenceA-2]

## History

The founding of the Kuwait National Guard was first conceived during the [Six-Day War](29328.md) and following the outcomes of [Operation Vantage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Vantage) when Sheikh [Jaber Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaber_Al-Ahmad_Al-Sabah)[^cite_note-5] was the [Crown Prince](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Prince) of Kuwait through the 2nd decree of 1967 on June 6 during the reign of the [Emir of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir_of_Kuwait), Sheikh [Sabah Al-Salim Al-Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabah_Al-Salim_Al-Sabah).[^cite_note-ReferenceA-2] A mission for this purpose was led by Sheikh Salem Ali Al-Salem Al-Sabah,[^cite_note-6] who was the driving force in forming the various task forces within the National Guard and who has remained at the head of the institution since its enacting in 1967.[^cite_note-kng.gov.kw-7][^cite_note-ReferenceB-8]

## Golden Jubilee

### 50th Anniversary of the Kuwait National Guard

In June 2017, the Kuwait National Guard commemorated their 50th Anniversary [Golden jubilee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_jubilee) under the leadership of the [Commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief), the Emir of Kuwait, and directives of Sheikh [Salem Al-Ali Al-Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Al-Ali_Al-Sabah).
