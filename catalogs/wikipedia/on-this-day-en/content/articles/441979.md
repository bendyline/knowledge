**Machala** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[maˈtʃala\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a city in south-west [Ecuador](9334.md). It is the capital of the [El Oro Province](696562.md), and is located near the [Gulf of Guayaquil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Guayaquil) on [fertile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fertility_\(soil\)) [lowlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowland). Machala has a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_\(human_biology\)) of 288,072 according to the 2022 census; it is the [sixth-biggest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Ecuador) in the country, and the second-most important port city after [Guayaquil](215586.md). It has been referred to as the ***Banana Capital of the World*.**

Machala is a commercial center for the surrounding [agricultural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) region. It is a major trading hub for [bananas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana), [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee), and [cocoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_bean). The banana industry is especially oriented toward exports, and plays a huge role in the city's economy. Bananas are shipped out from nearby [Puerto Bolívar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Bol%C3%ADvar) mainly to North America. 183 kilometres (114 mi) Machala's geographical position near [Guayaquil](215586.md) also makes it an important transportation hub. Many travelers heading south to Peru or north to Guayaquil funnel through the city. It is not known as a tourist destination among Ecuadorians or international visitors, but its proximity to the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md) makes it a short distance from the beach. The economy of Machala has seen growth as can be seen by the inauguration of its first mall in 2007. The mall has a movie theater in it, on the outskirts of the city.

Aerial image of Machala

The center of Machala is dominated by a large Catholic church and a central plaza. The plaza was built in the early 2000s to include a large fountain. There are many hotels situated in the center of the city. Las Brisas, located nearly a mile from the central plaza, is another popular place in Machala; it is a wealthier neighborhood that used to serve as a popular spot for local teenagers, but the remodeling of Zona Rosa, as well as the decreased safety in the area, has led to it becoming less popular.

Machala has one university, the Universidad Técnica de Machala. There are many private schools in Machala and one public high school – Colegio 9 de Octubre. The city serves as a stopping-off point on the way to nearby [Puerto Bolívar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Bol%C3%ADvar) and the [Jambelí Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jambel%C3%AD_Islands?action=edit\&redlink=1), which can only be reached by ferry.

## Climate

Machala features a [hot semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-arid_climate#Hot_semi-arid_climates) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BSh*). Like most of the coastal region of Ecuador, there is a short [wet season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_season) from January to April due to the retreat of the [Humboldt Current](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Current). The weather in both wet and [dry seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season) is very warm to hot and cloudy due to persistent fog from the cold current.

### Air
