[Donald Trump](4848272.md), the 45th and 47th [president of the United States](24113.md), made 19 international trips to 25 countries (in addition to visiting the [West Bank](33209.md)) during [his first presidency](52231341.md), which began on [January 20, 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_inauguration_of_Donald_Trump), and ended on [January 20, 2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inauguration_of_Joe_Biden). As of July 2026, Trump has made 12 international trips to 17 countries during [his second presidency](78298108.md), which began on [January 20, 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_inauguration_of_Donald_Trump).

Due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), he did not make any international trips between February 2020 and the end of his first presidency.
