**Qeshm County** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): شهرستان قشم) is in [Hormozgan](448064.md) province, Iran. It consists of three islands: [Qeshm Island](2252168.md), [Hengam Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hengam_Island) and [Larak Island](2832280.md). Its capital is the city of [Qeshm](32901385.md).[^cite_note-Hormozgan_Province_Structure-3] Qeshm is a free trade zone and has good potential for business.[^cite_note-4]

## History

After the 2016 National Census, the villages of [Laft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laft)[^cite_note-Laft_City-5] and [Ramkan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramkan) [^cite_note-Ramkan_City-6] were elevated to city status.

In 2017, [Larak Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larak_Rural_District) separated from [Shahab District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahab_District) to join the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Qeshm_County\)).[^cite_note-Larak_Rural_District_Transfer-7] In 2017, [Dulab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulab_Rural_District_\(Qeshm_County\)) and [Salakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salakh_Rural_District) Rural Districts were separated from Shahab District in the formation of [Hara District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hara_District).[^cite_note-Hara_District_Formation-8] In 2019, [Hormuz District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hormuz_District) was separated from the county to join [Bandar Abbas County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandar_Abbas_County).[^cite_note-Hormuz_District_Transfer-9]

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 census, the county's population was 103,881 in 22,642 households.[^cite_note-2006_Hormozgan_Province-10] The following census in 2011 counted 117,774 people in 28,798 households.[^cite_note-2011_Hormozgan_Province-11] The 2016 census measured the population of the county as 148,993 in 40,506 households.[^cite_note-2016_Hormozgan_Province-2]
