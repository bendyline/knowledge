**Colombia–Israel relations** are the [diplomatic relations](28165911.md) between [Colombia](5222.md) and [Israel](9282173.md) which were officially established on 1 July 1957. As of 10 August 2026, Colombia is second UN member state to recognize the [Golan Heights](7515865.md) as Israeli territory.

## General

In an article in the *[Israel Journal of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Journal_of_Foreign_Affairs)*, Marcos Peckel, a Colombian scholar noted that Colombian-Israeli relations can be looked through the lenses of military cooperation, trade links, education and culture, and recognition of [Palestine](241405.md).[^cite_note-auto-1] Militarily, Colombia was one of the first countries to give Israel weapons and engage in arms deals, which continued as a bilateral agreement. Since then, Israel and Colombia shared intelligence, and as Peckel explains, several pieces of [Israeli technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_technology). Trade-wise, both countries had a strong relationship.

The spheres of education and culture between Colombia and Israel are interwoven through Israeli [scholarships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholarship) to Colombians and a presence of media in each country.

Colombia's military has numerous weapon and ammunition types produced and/or maintained by Israel, including the [Rafael Advanced Defense Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Advanced_Defense_Systems) [Spike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_\(missile\)) missile, [Israel Weapons Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Weapons_Industries)-made [5.56mm Galil automatic rifle machine gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMI_Galil#Galil_ARM) and [7.62mm Galil sniper rifle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMI_Galil#Galil_Sniper), Rafael [Python-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Python_\(missile\)#Python-3) and [Python-4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Python_\(missile\)#Python-4), [Israel Aerospace Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Aerospace_Industries) [Kfir fighter jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Kfir), and has signed up for the [Barak MX](81732583.md) air defense system.[^cite_note-2023-10-19_DN-2]

## History

In 1947, during a [United Nations General Assembly](31957.md) session, [General Assembly Resolution 181](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Partition_Plan_for_Palestine) recommended the partition of [British Mandate Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Mandate_Palestine) into one Jewish and one Arab state Colombia abstained.[^cite_note-3]

In the mid-1950s, both countries officially established diplomatic relations and set up embassies in [Bogotá](211271.md) and [Tel Aviv](31453.md) respectively.[^cite_note-4] Relations improved in 1988, when major trade agreements were signed between Israel and Colombia. A Free Trade agreement was signed on 10 June 2013. However, it has not yet been ratified by Colombia and therefore is not yet in force.[^cite_note-5] This agreement reduces [tariffs](55551.md) on industrial and agricultural products between the two countries, and enable Israeli companies and individuals to invest with greater ease in the Colombia economy, considered to be second-biggest economy of South America. Israeli exports to Colombia totaled some US\$143 million in 2012, and consisted mostly of communications equipment, machinery, electrical and mechanical devices and chemical products.[^cite_note-6]

Colombia supported the aspiration of the Palestinian people to establish themselves as a free and independent state in the region. Additionally, Israel was Colombia's main partner in the region and was Colombia's second-largest trading partner in South America after Brazil. Bilateral relations have deepened through high-level visits in recent history.[^cite_note-7]

On 13 September 2017, Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) paid an official visit to Colombia.[^cite_note-8]

Until 2018, Colombia was one of the few remaining governments in Latin America that did not recognize Palestine as a state.[^cite_note-9] "Colombia supports the establishment of an independent Palestinian state alongside Israel within mutually agreed-upon borders. It considers Israeli settlements in the [West Bank](33209.md) illegal but strongly condemns Palestinian terrorism, and it advocates for a lasting peace based on the two-state solution".[^cite_note-auto-1]

In November 2021, Israeli President [Isaac Herzog](4645588.md) welcomed Colombian President [Iván Duque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iv%C3%A1n_Duque) on a state visit. Hosting Duque at the [President's Residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_HaNassi) in [Jerusalem](16043.md), Herzog commended Colombia's support for Israel and expressed his hopes to boost their bilateral trading relationship. At the state dinner, Herzog paid tribute to Duque's leadership in fighting "terror and rogue organizations affiliated with Iran and narco organizations." During the state visit, Duque inaugurated a Colombian innovation office in Jerusalem, a step that President Herzog welcomed in their joint statements.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

On 15 October 2023 Israel halted all security exports to Colombia due to remarks of Colombian president [Gustavo Petro](13391962.md) comparing the [Israeli military](39237.md) to [Nazis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazis) during the [Gaza war](74998519.md).[^cite_note-12] *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* called it a "ferocious diplomatic spat".[^cite_note-13] Colombia recalled its ambassador from Israel in condemnation of Israel's bombardment of Palestinian civilians in Gaza.[^cite_note-14]

On 2 May 2024 during the [Gaza war](74998519.md), president [Gustavo Petro](13391962.md) announced Colombia would break diplomatic ties with Israel, describing it as a [genocide](75668427.md).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] In July 2025, Colombia hosted an [emergency meeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogot%C3%A1_conference) of the [Hague Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hague_Group), a union of countries formed in January with the aim of holding Israel accountable for its alleged crimes in Palestine.

On 1 October 2025, in reaction to Israel's [interception of the Global Sumud Flotilla](80704925.md#Interception_by_Israel), president Petro expelled all remaining Israeli diplomats from the country.[^cite_note-17]

In 2026, following the election of [Abelardo de la Espriella](81665680.md) as President, the incoming administration announced that it would restore diplomatic relations with Israel.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] De la Espriella appointed [Omar Bula Escobar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Bula) as the new foreign minister, and the latter said that the new government's foreign policy would promote closer relations with Israel.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] In July 2026, Escobar met Israeli Foreign Minister [Gideon Sa'ar](485357.md) in Washington, D.C. The incoming administration later said that it plans to open an embassy in [Jerusalem](16043.md) and that it will withdraw Colombia's support for South Africa's case against Israel before the International Court of Justice.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] On 7 August 2026, Sa'ar attended the [inauguration ceremony](83547419.md) of Colombia's President-elect de la Espriella. Ahead of his inauguration ceremony de la Espriella met Sa'ar at his office in [Barranquilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barranquilla).[^cite_note-25]

On 10 August 2026, Colombia became the second country to recognize Israeli sovereignty over the Golan Heights, after a meeting between the Israeli foreign minister and Colombia's new government.[^cite_note-d869-26][^cite_note-colombia-27] The Israeli foreign minister also stated that he will continue to work for other countries to recognize "Israeli sovereignty" over the territory.[^cite_note-d869-26] The outgoing Colombian president [Gustavo Petro](13391962.md) apologized for the new governments declaration and stated that Israel had interfered in the Colombian election with software.[^cite_note-l976-28] Petro also stated that Espriella's restoration of ties to Israel "applauds the [Gaza genocide](75668427.md)".[^cite_note-29] Colombia's recognition was condemned by Syria.[^cite_note-b555-30]

Days later in August 2026, at the request of President Abelardo de la Espriella, Israel immediately sent an 80-person delegation to aid with rescue efforts in the aftermath of the [magnitude 7.4 earthquake](83931036.md), representing one of four nations for which Colombia specifically requested such assistance.[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32] That same month, Vivian Aisen was appointed as ambassador of Israel to Colombia.[^cite_note-33] Starting 1 September 2026, citizens of Colombia and Israel may travel between the two countries without a visa.[^cite_note-34] On 22 September, the [ICJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICJ) confirmed that Colombia had withdrawn its support for South Africa's case against Israel.[^cite_note-35] In October, Tania Gilinski was appointed Colombia's ambassador to Israel.[^cite_note-36]
