**Plus Ultra Líneas Aéreas S.A.** is a Spanish long-haul [airline](1942.md) based at [Adolfo Suárez Madrid–Barajas Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolfo_Su%C3%A1rez_Madrid%E2%80%93Barajas_Airport).

## History

Plus Ultra was founded in 2011 by the former director of the now-defunct [Air Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Madrid), Julio Miguel Martínez Sola.[^cite_note-ch-aviation-3] *[Plus ultra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plus_ultra)* ("Further beyond") is a Latin motto and the national motto of Spain. It is taken from the personal motto of [Charles V, Holy Roman Emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_V,_Holy_Roman_Emperor) and King of Spain, and is a reversal of the original phrase *[Non plus ultra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non_plus_ultra)* ("Nothing further beyond").

On 15 June 2016 the airline started operating regular flights, with destinations such as [Santo Domingo](57638.md) and [Lima](85423.md). The initial fleet was made up of two pre-owned [Airbus A340-300s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A340-300) which had been taken over from [Gulf Air](377403.md) in late 2014.[^cite_note-ch-aviation-3] In March 2017, the airline announced two new routes: [Barcelona](4443.md)-[Madrid](41188263.md)-[Santiago](51572.md) from 15 June 2017 and Barcelona-[Havana](49719.md) from 1 July 2017,[^cite_note-PlusUltra2-4][^cite_note-PlusUltra3-5][^cite_note-PlusUltra1-6] however, both flights did not commence service. One year later, Plus Ultra announced plans to serve [Caracas](55289.md) from [Tenerife–North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenerife_North_Airport).[^cite_note-7]

In March 2021, the Spanish government announced an aid of 53 million euros from the fund created to support companies considered strategic during the economic crisis derived from COVID-19.[^cite_note-8] The public bailout was controversial and went to court, although in January 2023 the judge of the Madrid Court of Instruction No. 15 dismissed the case.[^cite_note-9] On 11 December 2025, an investigation into the company was made public by the same court for alleged [money laundering](19390.md). That same day, police arrested the airline's owner and CEO after searching its headquarters.[^cite_note-10]

### Codeshare and Interline agreements

Plus Ultra has a codeshare agreement with [LASER Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LASER_Airlines) to offer flights to [Porlamar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porlamar),[^cite_note-23] [ITA Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ita_Airways) for connections between [Italy](14532.md) and [South America](26769.md) (via [Madrid](41188263.md))[^cite_note-24] and a Interline agreement with [Star Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Peru) to connect Madrid with other cities in Peru.[^cite_note-25]
