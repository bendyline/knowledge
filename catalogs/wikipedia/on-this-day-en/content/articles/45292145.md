# Empire Polo Club



The Empire Polo Club, hosting the [Desert Trip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_Trip) music festival in October 2016

[33°40′47″N 116°14′14″W  /  33.679811°N 116.237256°W  / 33.679811; -116.237256](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Empire_Polo_Club\&params=33.679811_N_116.237256_W_region\:US-CA\:landmark\:1000) The **Empire Polo Club** is a 1,000-acre event venue in [Indio, California](107924.md), located in the [Coachella Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_Valley) of [Riverside County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riverside_County,_California), approximately 22 miles southeast of [Palm Springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_Springs,_California).[^cite_note-Variety-1][^cite_note-2] It was founded in 1987 for use by [polo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polo) players, hosting international polo tournaments. Since 1993, the grounds have been leased out for events, including the annual [Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_Valley_Music_and_Arts_Festival) and [Stagecoach Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stagecoach_Festival) in April.

## Location

The club is located at 81-800 Avenue 51 within the city of [Indio](107924.md), in the [Coachella Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_Valley) and [Colorado Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Desert) of [Southern California](62520.md).[^cite_note-palmspringsempire-3][^cite_note-hurlingham-4][^cite_note-desertsun-5][^cite_note-6] It is around a half hour drive from [Palm Springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_Springs,_California) and a 2 to 2-and-a-half hour drive from [downtown Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Los_Angeles) or [San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego,_California).[^cite_note-palmspringsempire-3][^cite_note-hurlingham-4]

## History

The club was founded in 1987.[^cite_note-hurlingham-4][^cite_note-pololine2014-7] It has twelve polo grounds, making it one of the largest polo clubs on the [West coast of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_coast_of_the_United_States).[^cite_note-hurlingham-4][^cite_note-pololine2014-7]

It hosts local, national and international matches and tournaments.[^cite_note-desertsun-5][^cite_note-pololine2014-7] In 2013, it hosted a match between the British Schools and Universities Polo Association (SUPA) and the [USPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Polo_Association) Intercollegiate/Interscholastic.[^cite_note-8] It has also hosted events for the [Gay Polo League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay_Polo_League).[^cite_note-equestrianquarterly-9] In 2015, it hosted the USPA Townsend International Challenge Cup.

The grounds are owned by Alex Haagen III.[^cite_note-10] Since 1993, the Empire Polo Club has leased its polo grounds annually to Goldenvoice, a subsidiary of the [Anschutz Entertainment Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anschutz_Entertainment_Group), the organizers of the [Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_Valley_Music_and_Arts_Festival) and the [Stagecoach Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stagecoach_Festival).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-hollywoodreporter-12][^cite_note-dailytelegraph-13] Goldenvoice signed a 28-year lease agreement in 2021 for full operational control of the Empire Polo Grounds amid plans to host more events.[^cite_note-14] The polo fields were closed in November 2021 and the staff were laid off on short notice.[^cite_note-15]

AEG began hosting a winter [soccer](10568.md) tournament, named the [Coachella Valley Invitational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_Valley_Invitational), in 2022. It is an annual preseason tournament for [Major League Soccer](71802.md) and [National Women's Soccer League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Women's_Soccer_League) teams that grew to 30,000 attendees by 2024.[^cite_note-NYTimes-MLS-16]

In June 2025, the club proposed a mixed-use development near the festival grounds, including homes, shops, restaurants, and general entertainment.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

### Other events

In October and November 2009, the club hosted [Phish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phish)'s Festival 8. During the Halloween show that year, Phish performed [the Rolling Stones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rolling_Stones)' album *[Exile on Main Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exile_on_Main_Street)* in its entirety.

In October 2016, the club hosted [Desert Trip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_Trip),[^cite_note-19] a music festival featuring legendary rock performers [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan), the Rolling Stones, [Paul McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_McCartney), [Neil Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Young), [Roger Waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Waters), and [The Who](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Who).

In October 2023, the [Power Trip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_Trip_\(music_festival\)) took place at the club, featuring [Guns N' Roses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guns_N'_Roses), [AC/DC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AC/DC), [Judas Priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judas_Priest), [Metallica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallica), [Iron Maiden](159172.md), and [Tool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tool_\(band\)).[^cite_note-20]
