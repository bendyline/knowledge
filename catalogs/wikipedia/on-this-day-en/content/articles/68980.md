The **Benin Armed Forces** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Forces Armées Béninoises*; FAB) constitutes the army, navy, air force, and republican guard of the [Republic of Benin](3459.md). For several years, the [Belgian Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgian_Armed_Forces) have had an active programme of co-operation with Benin, offering training and coaching, donating surplus military equipment, and conducting limited military exercises in the country.[^cite_note-2]

## History



Beninese peacekeepers, part of the [United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Multidimensional_Integrated_Stabilization_Mission_in_Mali), 2018

The [constitution of 11 December 1990](14036675.md) ordered the Benin Armed Forces to ensure effective, permanent, and efficient security coverage of the territory, as well as border vigilance.

The national gendarmerie no longer exists since 2018. It has been merged with the national police.[^cite_note-Dec41-3]

## Branches

### Army



Beninese Army soldier on range at [Bembèrèkè](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bemb%C3%A8r%C3%A8k%C3%A8), 2012



A Beninese military honor guard, 2025

As of 2012, the army had a strength of 4,300.[^cite_note-IISS_2012_422-1] It includes 1 [armoured](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armoured_warfare) squadron, 3 [infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infantry) battalions, 1 commando/airborne battalion, 1 [artillery](2508.md) battery, and 1 engineer battalion, and 1 National Fire Brigade.[^cite_note-IISS_2012_422-1] The army has the following units:

- 1st Parachute Commando Battalion

- 1st Motorized Intervention Battalion

- 1st Armoured Group

- National Group of Firefighters

- 1st Combined Arms Battalion

- 2nd Combined Arms Battalion

- 3rd Combined Arms Battalion

- 7th Combined Arms Battalion

- 1st Mixed Artillery Battalion

- 1st Engineer Battalion

- 1st Signal Battalion

- 1st Materiel Battalion

- 1st Train Battalion

- Headquarters Group

### Air Force

Main article: [Benin Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benin_Air_Force)

After achieving independence from France in 1960, the [Benin Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benin_Air_Force) was equipped with seven French-supplied [Douglas C-47s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_C-47), four [MH.1521 Broussards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Holste_Broussard) and two [Agusta-Bell 47Gs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_47). Two [F-27s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fokker_F27_Friendship) entered service in 1978 for transport duties before being transferred to Air Benin. Also during the same era, two AN-26s were acquired. In late 1985, two Dornier Do-28s entered service to replace the C-47s. A single DHC-6 Twin Otter was acquired in 1989.

### Navy

As of 2012, the navy had approximately 200 personnel. It operates two ex-Chinese patrol boats, which are designated the [*Matelot Brice Kpomasse* class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matelot_Brice_Kpomasse_class?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-IISS_2012_422-1]

### Republican Guard

The Republican Guard is responsible for the security of the President of the Republic, ministers, members of the government, institutions of the Republic, and their leader.
