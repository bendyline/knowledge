**Chikungunya** is an [infection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infection) caused by the [chikungunya virus](#Virology).[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3] The most common symptoms are [fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fever) and [joint pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthralgia),[^cite_note-CDC_2024_Symptoms-2] typically occurring four to eight days after the bite of an infected mosquito.[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3][^cite_note-ECDC-factsheet-2024-5] The pain has been described as "excruciating"; however some people may be infected without showing any symptoms. Other symptoms may include [headache](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headache), [muscle pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_pain), [joint swelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_swelling), and a [rash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rash).[^cite_note-CDC_2024_Symptoms-2] Symptoms usually improve within a week; however, occasionally the joint pain may last for months or years.[^cite_note-CDC_2024_Symptoms-2][^cite_note-van_Aalst-2017-6] The very young, old, and those with other health problems are at risk of more severe disease.[^cite_note-CDC_2024_Symptoms-2]

The [virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virus) is spread between people by two species of [mosquito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito) in the genus *[Aedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aedes)*: *[Aedes albopictus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aedes_albopictus)* and *[Aedes aegypti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aedes_aegypti)*,[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3] which mainly bite during the day,[^cite_note-Paixao-2018-7][^cite_note-CDC-Prevent-2016-8] particularly around dawn and in the late afternoon.[^cite_note-AFP-2025-9] The [virus may circulate within a number of animals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_reservoir), including birds and [rodents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodent).[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3] Diagnosis is done by testing the blood for either viral [RNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA) or [antibodies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antibodies) to the virus.[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3] The symptoms can be mistaken for those of [dengue fever](39669.md) and [Zika fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zika_fever), which are spread by the same mosquitoes.[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3] It is believed most people become [immune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immune) after a single infection.[^cite_note-CDC_2024_Symptoms-2]

The best means of prevention are overall [mosquito control](2887318.md) and the avoidance of bites in areas where the disease is common.[^cite_note-Caglioti-2013-4] This may be partly achieved by decreasing mosquitoes' access to water, as well as the use of [insect repellent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insect_repellent) and [mosquito nets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito_nets). [Chikungunya vaccines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikungunya_vaccine) have been approved for use in the United States and in the European Union.[^cite_note-ECDC-Ixchiq-2024-10][^cite_note-EMA-Vimkunya-2025a-11][^cite_note-FDA_Approval-2023-12] No specific treatment for chikungunya is available.[^cite_note-Caglioti-2013-4]

The chikungunya virus is widespread in tropical and subtropical regions where warm climates and abundant populations of its mosquito vectors (*A. aegypti* and *A. albopictus*) facilitate its transmission.[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3] The disease was first identified in 1952 in [Tanzania](30118.md) and named based on the [Makonde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makonde_language) words for "to become contorted".[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3] The disease has spread widely since the 2000s with [outbreaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outbreak) reported in many tropical and some temperate areas. It is considered to be [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemic_\(epidemiology\)) in many parts of the world,[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3][^cite_note-13] affecting millions of people every year.[^cite_note-Hyolim-2025-14][^cite_note-Ribeiro_dos_Santos-2025-15] Chikungunya has become a global health concern due to its rapid geographic expansion, recurrent outbreaks, the lack of effective antiviral treatments, and potential to cause severe symptoms and death.[^cite_note-Moizeis-2018-16]

## Signs and symptoms

Chikungunya can be asymptomatic, with estimates of between 17% and 40% of infections showing no symptoms.[^cite_note-ECDC-factsheet-2024-5] For those experiencing symptoms, they typically begin with a sudden high fever above 39 °C (102 °F) around 3 to 7 days after the bite of an infected mosquito.[^cite_note-Burt_2017-17][^cite_note-ECDC-factsheet-2024-5] The fever is often accompanied by severe [muscle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myalgia) and [joint pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthralgia), which affects multiple joints in the arms and legs and is often symmetric – i.e. if one elbow is affected, the other is as well.[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18][^cite_note-ECDC-factsheet-2024-5] The pain has been described as "excruciating".[^cite_note-19] People with chikungunya also frequently experience [headaches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headache), [back pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_pain), [nausea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nausea), and [fatigue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatigue_\(medical\)).[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18] Around half of those affected develop a [rash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rash), with [reddening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macular_rash) and sometimes [small bumps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maculopapular_rash) on the palms, foot soles, torso, and face.[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18]

For some, the rash remains constrained to a small area of the body; for others, the rash can be extensive, covering more than 90% of the skin.[^cite_note-Burt_2017-17] Some people experience gastrointestinal issues, with [abdominal pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdominal_pain) and [vomiting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vomiting). Others experience eye problems, namely [sensitivity to light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photophobia), [conjunctivitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjunctivitis), and pain behind the eye.[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18] This first set of symptoms, termed the acute phase, lasts around a week, after which most symptoms resolve on their own.[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18]

For those with severe symptoms, approximately 30% to 40% continue to have symptoms after the acute phase resolves.[^cite_note-ECDC-factsheet-2024-5][^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18] The lasting symptoms tend to be joint pains: [arthritis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthritis), [tenosynovitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenosynovitis), and/or [bursitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bursitis).[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18] If the affected person has pre-existing joint issues, these tend to worsen.[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18] Overuse of a joint can result in painful swelling, stiffness, [nerve damage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerve_damage), and [neuropathic pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuropathic_pain).[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18] Typically the joint pain improves with time; however, the chronic stage can last anywhere from a few months to several years.[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18]

Almost all symptomatic cases feature joint pain, generally in more than one joint.[^cite_note-Thiberville-2013-20] Pain most commonly occurs in peripheral joints, such as the wrists, ankles, and joints of the [hands and feet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phalanges) as well as some of the larger joints, typically the shoulders, elbows and knees.[^cite_note-Thiberville-2013-20][^cite_note-Burt-2012-21] Joints are more likely to be affected if they have previously been damaged by disorders such as [arthritis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osteoarthritis).[^cite_note-Burt-2012-21] Pain may also occur in the [muscles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myalgia) or [ligaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ligaments). In more than half of cases, normal activity is limited by significant fatigue and pain.[^cite_note-Thiberville-2013-20] Infrequently, inflammation of the eyes may occur in the form of [iridocyclitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iridocyclitis), or [uveitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uveitis), and [retinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retina) lesions may occur.[^cite_note-Mahendradas-2008-22] Temporary damage to the liver may occur.[^cite_note-Simon-2011-23]

People with chikungunya occasionally develop long-term neurologic disorders, most frequently [swelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encephalitis) or [degeneration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encephalopathy) of the brain, [inflammation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myelitis) or [degeneration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myelopathy) of the [myelin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myelin) sheaths around neurons, [Guillain–Barré syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillain%E2%80%93Barr%C3%A9_syndrome), [acute disseminated encephalomyelitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acute_disseminated_encephalomyelitis), [hypotonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypotonia) (in newborns), and issues with visual processing.[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18]

Newborns, the elderly, and those with [diabetes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diabetes), [heart disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_disease), liver and kidney diseases, and [HIV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_immunodeficiency_virus) infection tend to have more severe cases of chikungunya. Fewer than 1 in 1,000 people with symptomatic chikungunya die of the disease; generally these are people with pre-existing health conditions.[^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18][^cite_note-ECDC-factsheet-2024-5]

### Transmission

Chikungunya is generally transmitted from mosquitoes to humans. Chikungunya is spread through bites from *[Aedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aedes)* mosquitoes, specifically *[A. aegypti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._aegypti)* (Egyptian mosquito) and *[A. albopictus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._albopictus)* (Tiger mosquito).[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3] Because large amounts of virus are present in the blood during the first few days of infection, the virus can spread from an infected human to a mosquito, where it replicates without harming the mosquito. Subsequently, a bite from the infected mosquito will transmit the virus back to a human.[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3] The [incubation period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incubation_period) ranges from one to twelve days and is most typically three to seven days.[^cite_note-Thiberville-2013-20]

Rarely, the disease can be transmitted from [mother to child](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertically_transmitted_infection) during pregnancy or at birth, in women who become infected a few days before [delivery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Childbirth).[^cite_note-ECDC-factsheet-2024-5]

## Mechanism

Chikungunya virus is passed to humans when a bite from an infected mosquito breaks the skin and introduces the virus into the body. The virus initially replicates in cells near the location of the bite; from here it enters the [lymphatic system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lymphatic_system) and the [bloodstream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circulatory_system), enabling it to circulate to organs and tissues which become infected. Most frequently it reproduces in the lymphatic system and the [spleen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spleen), as well as peripheral joints, muscles and tendons where symptoms frequently occur; it appears that the virus is able to penetrate and replicate in many different types of cells.[^cite_note-Silva-2017a-24] In severe cases it can infect the brain and liver.[^cite_note-Vu-2017-25][^cite_note-Silva-2017a-24]

During the acute phase of infection, large numbers of infectious [virus particles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virion) are [present in the bloodstream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_load), making it very likely that an uninfected mosquito will pick up the virus if it bites the human host.[^cite_note-Vu-2017-25][^cite_note-Silva-2017a-24]

During the first few days of infection, the host's [innate immune system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innate_immune_system) is activated, producing [type I interferons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interferon_type_I) and [inflammatory cytokines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inflammatory_cytokine) to fight the infection. This generates the fever and localised inflammation which is characteristic of the disease.[^cite_note-Silva-2017a-24][^cite_note-Srivastava-2020-26] It takes about a week before the host's [adaptive immune system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_immune_system) begins to develop [antibodies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antibody) which eventually clear the virus from the bloodstream.[^cite_note-CDC-Testing-2025-27] However the virus can persist within specific tissues, especially the joints, causing long term inflammation and pain in chronic cases.[^cite_note-Srivastava-2020-26]

The virus has mechanisms which help it to evade the immune response. Within an infected cell, the viral nonstructural protein 2 (nsP2) interferes with the [JAK-STAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JAK-STAT_signaling_pathway) signalling pathway to hinder it from triggering an antiviral response.[^cite_note-Fros-2010-28] The virus can induce [apoptosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apoptosis) (programmed cell death) in host cells; virus laden [debris from apoptosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleb_\(cell_biology\)) is engulfed by [macrophages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macrophage) which in turn become infected.[^cite_note-Srivastava-2020-26] The virus also seems to be able to evade [T lymphocytes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T_cell) which seek to target and destroy the virus particles.[^cite_note-Davenport-2020-29]

## Diagnosis

Diagnosing chikungunya can be difficult because its symptoms, such as sudden fever and joint pain, closely resemble other mosquito-borne illnesses like [dengue fever](39669.md) and [malaria](20423.md).[^cite_note-Ojeda_Rodriguez-2025-30] Chikungunya should be suspected if a patient with these symptoms either lives in an area where the virus is endemic, or if they have recently traveled to such an area.[^cite_note-Ojeda_Rodriguez-2025-30][^cite_note-CDC_2024_About-31]

During the first week of illness, when virus is present in the bloodstream, it is possible to detect viral RNA in a blood sample using techniques such as [reverse transcription-polymerase chain reaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_transcription_polymerase_chain_reaction) (RT-PCR) or [viral culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_culture).[^cite_note-CDC_2024_About-31] After this time, the body develops antibodies and the virus is eliminated from the bloodstream. Antibodies in blood serum persist for between 3 and 12 months; they can be detected for up to a year after infection using [enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ELISA) (ELISA) or [indirect fluorescent antibody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunofluorescence) (IFA).[^cite_note-Ojeda_Rodriguez-2025-30][^cite_note-Vairo_2019-18] All of these techniques are time consuming and costly, requiring sophisticated laboratory equipment which may not be available in [resource poor settings](78449.md).[^cite_note-Silva-2024-32]

### Differential diagnosis

The *Aedes* mosquitoes which carry chikungunya virus can also carry other viruses such as [dengue](39669.md), [zika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zika_fever), and [yellow fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_fever).[^cite_note-Abbasi-2025-33] Other infections which should be considered include [malaria](20423.md), [leptospirosis](649760.md), [measles](58911.md), [mononucleosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infectious_mononucleosis) and [African tick bite fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_tick_bite_fever), which are often endemic in the same areas and can have similar symptoms. It is possible for a patient to be infected by [more than one virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coinfection) simultaneously.[^cite_note-Ojeda_Rodriguez-2025-30]

## Prevention



*A. aegypti* mosquito biting a person

Although an approved [vaccine](32653.md) exists, the most effective means of prevention is to avoid or prevent mosquito bites. The main strategies for this are: controlling mosquito populations by limiting their [habitat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habitat); and protection against contact with disease-carrying mosquitoes.[^cite_note-Caglioti-2013-4] On a large scale, [mosquito control](2887318.md) focuses on eliminating standing water where mosquitoes lay eggs and develop as larvae.[^cite_note-Weaver-2015a-34] Individuals should use [mosquito repellent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insect_repellent), as well as barriers such as loose clothing that covers the arms and legs, [mosquito nets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito_net) and [window and door screens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Window_screen).[^cite_note-UK-gov-mosquito-2023-35]

Once immunity against chikungunya has been acquired, whether as a result of infection or vaccination, it endures long term and may be lifelong.[^cite_note-ECDC-factsheet-2024-5][^cite_note-Cleveland-36]

### Vaccination

This section is an excerpt from [Chikungunya vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikungunya_vaccine).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Chikungunya_vaccine\&action=edit)]

[Chikungunya vaccines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikungunya_vaccine) are [vaccines](32653.md) intended to provide [acquired immunity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acquired_immunity) against the [chikungunya virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikungunya_virus).[^cite_note-Chikungunya_vaccine_FDA_Ixchiq-37][^cite_note-Chikungunya_vaccine_FDA_Vimkunya-38] As of 2025, two vaccines have been licensed in some countries. These are Ixchiq, a live [attenuated vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attenuated_vaccine) from [Valneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valneva_SE), and Vimkunya manufactured by [Bavarian Nordic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Nordic) which utilises [virus-like particle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virus-like_particle) technology.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-Chikungunya_vaccine_FDA_Ixchiq_PR-40][^cite_note-Chikungunya_vaccine_Ixchiq_EPAR-41][^cite_note-Chikungunya_vaccine_Vimkunya_EPAR-42]

The most commonly reported side effects of Ixchiq include tenderness at the injection site, as well as headache, fatigue, muscle pain, joint pain, fever, and nausea.[^cite_note-Chikungunya_vaccine_FDA_Ixchiq_PR-40] However the license for Ixchiq has been suspended or restricted in some countries due to the risk of severe side effects, particularly in older people.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44]

## Treatment

No specific treatment for chikungunya is available.[^cite_note-Caglioti-2013-4] [Supportive care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Therapy#Supportive_therapy) is recommended, and symptomatic treatment of fever and joint swelling includes the use of [paracetamol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracetamol) (acetaminophen), rest, and fluids.[^cite_note-Caglioti-2013-4][^cite_note-ECDC-factsheet-2024-5] [Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonsteroidal_anti-inflammatory_drug) (NSAIDs) such as [aspirin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspirin) or [ibuprofen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibuprofen) should not be used in the acute phase until dengue fever has been ruled out, as these can increase the risk of bleeding in dengue.[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3][^cite_note-Ojeda_Rodriguez-2025-30]

Chronic symptoms, especially joint pain, may persist for months after the infection has passed. The pain and swelling may be treated with NSAIDs or in more severe cases with [corticosteroid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corticosteroid) drugs or [disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disease-modifying_antirheumatic_drug) such as [hydroxychloroquine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroxychloroquine).[^cite_note-Ojeda_Rodriguez-2025-30][^cite_note-Pathak-2019-45]

## Prognosis

The [mortality rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortality_rate) of chikungunya is slightly less than 1 in 1000.[^cite_note-Mavalankar-2008-46] Those over the age of 65, [infants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neonate), and those with underlying chronic medical problems are most likely to have severe complications.[^cite_note-Morrison-2014-47] Newborn infants are especially vulnerable as they lack fully developed [immune systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immune_system), and may pick up the infection through vertical [transmission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertically_transmitted_infection) from their mother.[^cite_note-Morrison-2014-47] The likelihood of prolonged symptoms or chronic joint pain is increased with increased age and prior rheumatological disease.[^cite_note-Schilte-2013-48][^cite_note-Gérardin-2013-49]

## Epidemiology

Chikungunya virus is transmitted by the bite of an infected mosquito of the genus *Aedes*, specifically *[Aedes aegypti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aedes_aegypti)* and *[Aedes albopictus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aedes_albopictus).[^cite_note-WHO_FactSheet_2025-3]* *A. aegypti*, the so-called Egyptian mosquito, is well adapted to urban settings in the [tropics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropics) but has poor tolerance for low temperatures and is less able to [overwinter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overwintering) in [temperate climates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperate_climate). *A. albopictus*, the Asian Tiger mosquito, has a wider temperature tolerance and can overwinter as eggs in temperate regions. Both mosquitoes have spread from their ancestral ranges into tropical, sub-tropical, and some temperate regions of the Americas, Africa, [Australasia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australasia) and [Eurasia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasia).[^cite_note-ECDC-Aedes-Aegypti-2017-50][^cite_note-ECDC-Aedes-Albopictus-2017-51]

In Africa, chikungunya virus is maintained by a [sylvatic cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvatic_cycle) in which the virus cycles between small mammals (principally non-human [primates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primate)), and mosquitos; humans can be infected by a mosquito bite but the virus does not rely on humans for survival.[^cite_note-Powers-2007-52] Elsewhere, chikungunya is maintained in an urban cycle in densely populated areas. In this, an infected mosquito bites a human, transmitting the virus which replicates in the human host; a few days later, large numbers of infective [virus particles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virion) are [present in the host's bloodstream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viremia). When another mosquito bites the infected human, it picks up the virus, and this cycle continues.[^cite_note-Shoubeho-2025-53]

As of December 2024, 119 countries and territories, principally those in tropical and subtropical regions, have reported local transmission of chikungunya.[^cite_note-WHO-Epidemiology-2025-54] In some of these it is [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemic_\(epidemiology\)) (continually present). In some regions the disease tends to manifest as periodic epidemics which can be sudden and intense, as the virus spreads among a population with a low level of [herd immunity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herd_immunity).[^cite_note-Ribeiro_dos_Santos-2025-15][^cite_note-Silva-2017a-24] Because of the difficulty in reliably diagnosing chikungunya disease, especially in [resource poor contexts](78449.md), there are no reliable statistics on its incidence. However epidemiological modelling studies estimate between 14.4 million[^cite_note-Hyolim-2025-14] and 35 million people[^cite_note-Ribeiro_dos_Santos-2025-15] are infected annually. Up to 848,000 people experience [chronic long-term pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequela); annual mortality is estimated at up to 3,700 deaths.[^cite_note-Ribeiro_dos_Santos-2025-15]

## History

The term **chikungunya** is derived from the [Makonde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makonde_language) root verb *kungunyala*, meaning to dry up or become contorted, descriptive of the posture of people with chronic symptoms.[^cite_note-Lumsden-19552-55] The disease was first clearly described by Marion Robinson[^cite_note-Robinson-19552-56] and W\.H.R. Lumsden[^cite_note-Lumsden-19552-55] in a pair of 1955 papers, following an outbreak in 1952 on the [Makonde Plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Mozambique), along the border between [Mozambique](19301.md) and [Tanganyika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanganyika_\(territory\)) (the mainland part of modern-day [Tanzania](30118.md)).[^cite_note-Lumsden-19552-55] The virus itself was isolated in 1955.[^cite_note-Ross-1956-57] At that time, it was endemic to sub-saharan Africa, relying on *A. aegypti* to maintain its sylvatic cycle.[^cite_note-Powers-2007-52] Prior to this, the disease may have caused sporadic epidemics in other regions, which had been incorrectly ascribed to [dengue](39669.md).[^cite_note-Mobeen-2017-58][^cite_note-Carey-1971-59][^cite_note-David-2014-60]

The first direct evidence of chikungunya spreading outside of Africa came from [Bangkok](56615.md), Thailand, in 1958, during an urban outbreak. It subsequently appeared in India and Cambodia in the early 1960s, associated with the spread of the *A. aegypti* mosquito to those regions.[^cite_note-Weaver-2015-61]

An outbreak on the coast of Kenya in 2004 rapidly spread to the islands of the Indian Ocean (such as [La Réunion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A9union)) in 2005, then to India and Southeast Asia where it caused explosive outbreaks affecting millions of people.[^cite_note-Soumaharo-2011-62] Infected air travelers carried the virus into areas where the population had not previously been exposed to the disease and had no immunity.[^cite_note-Weaver-2015a-34]

An analysis of the genetic code of chikungunya virus suggests that the wider spread of the disease since 2004 may be due to a genetic change which altered the E1 segment of the virus' [viral coat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_coat) protein, a variant called E1-A226V. Prior to this mutation, spread of the virus was restricted by the relatively narrow geographical range of its principal vector, *[A. aegypti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aedes_aegypti)*. The adaptive effect of this mutation is that the virus became able to use *[A albopictus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aedes_albopictus)* as a [vector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_\(epidemiology\)), spreading within its wider geographical range.[^cite_note-Tsetsarkin-2007-63][^cite_note-Schuffenecker-2006-64] *A. albopictus* is an invasive species which since the 1960's has spread through parts of Europe, the Americas, the Caribbean, Africa, and the Middle East.[^cite_note-IUCNSSC-65]

Chikungunya was introduced to the [Americas](29833.md) in 2013, first detected on the French island of [Saint Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collectivity_of_Saint_Martin),[^cite_note-Charles-2014-66][^cite_note-Silva-2017a-24] and over the next four years more than 2.6 million cases were recorded in this region.[^cite_note-CDC-Yellow-2025-67]

## Research directions

Chikungunya is recognised as a global threat; there are an estimated 35 million cases annually, with 2.8 billion people living in areas which are threatened by the disease.[^cite_note-CEPI-2025-68] It is classed as a priority pathogen by the [Coalition for Epidemic Preparedness Innovations](51522180.md) (CEPI).[^cite_note-Coalition_for_Epidemic_Preparedness_Innovations-2023-69]

Research on chikungunya focuses on improving knowledge of the mechanism of the disease and developing tools for its prevention, diagnosis and treatment.

- Prevention of chikungunya is principally focused on the development and testing of vaccines. As of 2025, two vaccines are available; each of them gives long lasting protection after administration of a single dose.

  [^cite_note-CDC-Vaccine-2025-70]

  &#x20;Other vaccine candidates are under development.

  [^cite_note-Maure-2024-71]

- Reliable diagnosis of chikungunya is difficult, slow and expensive. Research is under way to develop diagnostic tools which are cheaper, quicker and easier to use.

  [^cite_note-Silva-2024-32]

  [^cite_note-Reddy-2020-72]

- There is no effective [antiviral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiviral_drug) treatment for chikungunya. Research directions include repurposing some existing licensed drugs

  [^cite_note-Kasabe-2023-73]

  &#x20;and modifying plant-derived compounds.

  [^cite_note-Cardoso-2025-74]

- For many, the [sequelae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequela) of chikungunya can persist for month or years, and range from mild discomfort to severe and debilitating joint pain which severely affects their physical and mental well being. Research in to the underlying mechanism of long term pain, and its possible treatments, is ongoing.

Chikungunya virus is a member of the genus *[Alphavirus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphavirus)*, and family *[Togaviridae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Togaviridae)*. Chikungunya virus features an icosahedral capsid surrounded by a lipid envelope, with a diameter ranging from 60 to 70 nm.[^cite_note-78] It was first isolated in 1953 in [Tanzania](30118.md) and is an [RNA virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA_virus) with a [positive-sense single-stranded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA_virus#Replication) genome of about 11.6kb.[^cite_note-Weaver-2012-79] It is a member of the [Semliki Forest virus complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semliki_Forest_virus_complex) and is closely related to [Ross River virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_River_virus), [O'nyong'nyong virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O'nyong'nyong_virus), and [Semliki Forest virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semliki_Forest_virus).[^cite_note-80] Because it is transmitted by [arthropods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthropod), namely mosquitoes, it can also be referred to as an [arbovirus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbovirus) (**ar**thropod-**bo**rne virus). In the United States, it is classified as a [category B priority pathogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virus_Pathogen_Database_and_Analysis_Resource),[^cite_note-81] and work requires [biosafety level III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biosafety_level_3) precautions.[^cite_note-82]

Three genotypes of this virus have been described, each with a distinct [genotype](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genotype) and [antigenic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antigenic) character: West African, East/Central/South African, and Asian genotypes.[^cite_note-Powers-2000-83] The Asian lineage originated in 1952 and has subsequently split into two lineages – India (Indian Ocean Lineage) and South East Asian clades. This virus was first reported in the Americas in 2014. Phylogenetic investigations have shown two strains in Brazil – the Asian and East/Central/South African types – and that the Asian strain arrived in the Caribbean (most likely from Oceania) in about March 2013.[^cite_note-Sahadeo-2017-84] The rate of molecular evolution was estimated to have a mean rate of 5 × 10<sup>−4</sup> substitutions per site per year (95% higher probability density 2.9–7.9 × 10<sup>−4</sup>).[^cite_note-Sahadeo-2017-84]

The chikungunya virus genome encodes both structural and non-structural proteins as typical of alphavirus genomic organization.[^cite_note-Wang-2024-85] The structural proteins, including the capsid, E3, E2, 6K and E1, are responsible for encapsulating the viral genome and assembling new viral particles. These proteins are critical for viral entry into host cells. Meanwhile, the non-structural proteins, nsP1, nsP2, nsP3, and nsP4, play essential roles in viral replication, translation, and immune evasion.[^cite_note-Wang-2024-85]

#### Viral replication



[Transmission electron micrograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transmission_electron_micrograph) of chikungunya virus particles

The virus consists of four nonstructural proteins and three structural proteins.[^cite_note-Weaver-2015-61] The structural proteins are the [capsid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capsid) and two [envelope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_envelope) glycoproteins: E1 and E2, which form [heterodimeric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heterodimeric) spikes on the viron surface. E2 binds to cellular receptors in order to enter the host cell through [receptor-mediated endocytosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Receptor-mediated_endocytosis). E1 contains a [fusion peptide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fusion_peptide) which, when exposed to the acidity of the [endosome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endosome) in [eukaryotic cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eukaryotic_cell), dissociates from E2 and initiates [membrane fusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipid_bilayer_fusion) that allows the release of nucleocapsids into the host cytoplasm, promoting infection.[^cite_note-86] The mature virion contains 240 [heterodimeric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heterodimeric) spikes of E2/E1, which after release, bud on the surface of the infected cell, where they are released by [exocytosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exocytosis) to infect other cells.[^cite_note-Weaver-2012-79]
