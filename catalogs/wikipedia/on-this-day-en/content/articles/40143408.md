The **Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة السياحة والآثار, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Wizārat al-Siyāḥah wa-al-Āthār*) is the Egyptian government organization which serves to protect and preserve the heritage and [ancient history of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_ancient_Egypt). In December 2019 it was merged into the [Ministry of Tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Tourism_\(Egypt\)) with [Khaled al-Anani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khaled_al-Anani) retaining his function.[^cite_note-1] He was replaced by [Ahmed Issa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Issa_\(Egyptian_politician\)) as Minister of Tourism and Antiquities in a cabinet reshuffle on 13 August 2022.&#x20;

## History

It was formed from the [Supreme Council of Antiquities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Council_of_Antiquities) in 2011[^cite_note-History-2] during the presidency of [Hosni Mubarak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hosni_Mubarak) to deal with the security and theft of Egyptian [antiquities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiquities).&#x20;

[Grave robbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grave_robbers) have been looting ancient Egyptian tombs nearly continuously for well over 4,000 years.[^cite_note-NG_6-2016-3] The Ministry of Antiquities works to get the items restored back to Egypt, whenever possible. Over the years, thousands of stolen antiquities have made their way back to Egypt. For instance, in late 2016, the ministry recovered and repatriated two of four [Islamic era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_art) lamps which had been stolen in 2015.[^cite_note-lamps_returned_in_2016-4] In 2018, a carving in the shape of Osiris which had been hidden in furniture and shipped to Kuwait was repatriated to Egypt's Ministry of Antiquities.[^cite_note-Coffin_Lid_Repatriated-5]&#x20;

In 2023, the ministry prohibited a group of archaeologists from the [National Museum of Antiquities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rijksmuseum_van_Oudheden) in [Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden), [Netherlands](21148.md) from conducting excavations in [Saqqara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saqqara) after the museum unveiled an exhibit about [ancient Egyptian music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_Egypt) that Egyptian authorities criticized for its [Afrocentric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrocentrism) depictions of certain figures, claiming it was [historical negationism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_negationism).[^cite_note-6]&#x20;

## Projects

From 2009 to 2014, the ministry worked with the [Getty Conservation Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Getty_Conservation_Institute) on the management and conservation of the [tomb of Tutankhamun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KV62).[^cite_note-Getty_Project-7]&#x20;

## Duties and goals

In 2016, the minister, Khaled El-Anany, stated his primary focus would be on solving the budget deficit of the ministry, given that many projects were stalled for lack of funding.[^cite_note-2016_minister_goals-15]&#x20;
