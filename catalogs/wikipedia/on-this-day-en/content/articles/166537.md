**Jaipur** ([Rajasthani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthani_language): *Jayapura*, pronounced [\[ˈd͡ʑɛpʊɾᵊ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA)) is the [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_state_and_union_territory_capitals_in_India) and the [largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_and_towns_in_Rajasthan) of the north-western [Indian state](375986.md) of [Rajasthan](26291.md). As of 2011, the city had a population of 3.1 million, making it the [tenth most populous city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_India_by_population) in the country. Located 268 km (167 miles) from the national capital [New Delhi](51585.md), Jaipur is also known as the *Pink City* due to the dominant colour scheme of its buildings in the old city.[^cite_note-\:3-10]&#x20;

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by [Sawai Jai Singh II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sawai_Jai_Singh), the [Kachhwaha Rajput](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kachhwaha) ruler of [Amer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amer,_India), after whom the city is named.[^cite_note-11] It is one of the earliest planned cities of modern India, designed by [Vidyadhar Bhattacharya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vidyadhar_Bhattacharya).[^cite_note-12] During the [British colonial period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj), the city served as the capital of [Jaipur State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_State). After [Indian independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_of_India) in 1947, Jaipur became the capital of the newly formed state of Rajasthan in 1949.&#x20;

Jaipur is a popular [tourist destination in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_India), forming a part of the Western [Golden Triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Triangle_\(India\)) tourist circuit along with Delhi and [Agra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra).[^cite_note-13] The city serves as a gateway to other [tourist destinations in Rajasthan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_Rajasthan), such as [Jodhpur](983053.md), [Jaisalmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaisalmer), [Bikaner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bikaner), [Udaipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udaipur), [Bundi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundi), [Kota](1324777.md), and [Mount Abu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Abu); it has three [World heritage sites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Heritage_Sites), [Amer Fort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amer_Fort), [Hawa Mahal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawa_Mahal) and [Jantar Mantar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jantar_Mantar,_Jaipur). On 6 July 2019, the city was placed on the [World Heritage Cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization_of_World_Heritage_Cities) list.[^cite_note-14] It is also known as the [Paris](22989.md) of India. Due to its beauty, [C.V. Raman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._V._Raman) called it the "Island of Glory".&#x20;

## Etymology

Jaipur derives its name from Sawai Jai Singh II, the Kachhwaha ruler of Amer, who founded the city in 1727.[^cite_note-\:3-10] In [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit), variations of the word "pur" or "pura" are commonly used to refer to a city or town. So "Jaipur" essentially means "The City of Jai" or "Jai's City".[^cite_note-15]&#x20;

## History

Main article: [History of Jaipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Jaipur)



[Sawai Jai Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sawai_Jai_Singh), the founder of Jaipur

Jaipur was founded by the Rajput chief of Kachhwaha clan, Jai Singh II, on 18 November 1727, who ruled the region from 1699 to 1743. He planned to shift his capital from Amber, 11 kilometres (7 mi) to Jaipur to accommodate the growing population and increasing scarcity of water.[^cite_note-Hist-16] Jai Singh consulted several architects while planning the layout of Jaipur and established the city based on the principles of [Vastu Shastra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vastu_Shastra) and [Shilpa Shastra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shilpa_Shastra), under the architectural guidance of Vidyadhar Bhattacharya.[^cite_note-History-17] The construction of the city began in 1726. During the rule of [Sawai Ram Singh II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ram_Singh_II), the city was painted pink to welcome [Albert Edward, Prince of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Edward,_Prince_of_Wales) in 1876.[^cite_note-POW-18] Many of the avenues remain painted in pink, giving Jaipur a distinctive appearance and the epithet *Pink City*.[^cite_note-19]&#x20;

In the 19th century, the city grew rapidly and had a population of 160,000 by 1900. The wide boulevards were paved, and its chief industries included [metalwork](266443.md) and [marble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble), fostered by a school of art founded in 1868.[^cite_note-EB1911-20] In August 1981, large areas of the city including the [airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_International_Airport), were flooded due to heavy rains from a cloudburst, resulting in the deaths of eight people and much damage to the city's [Dravyavati River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dravyavati_River).[^cite_note-jda-May2016-21][^cite_note-22] On 6 July 2019, the city was named to the [World Heritage Cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization_of_World_Heritage_Cities) list.[^cite_note-23]&#x20;

## Geography

### Topography

Jaipur is located in the northeastern part of Rajasthan and covers a total area of 467 square kilometres (180 sq mi). The city is surrounded by fertile alluvial plains to the east and south, and hill chains and desert areas to the north and west.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] Jaipur generally slopes downwards from north to south and then to the southeast.[^cite_note-JNN-26] The city is surrounded by the Nahargarh hills in the north and Jhalana in the east, which is a part of the [Aravalli range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aravalli_Range).[^cite_note-JNN-26]&#x20;

The [Dravyavati River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dravyavati_River) is the primary drainage channel, which by 2014 had degenerated into an untreated sewage [nallah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nullah). To address this issue, a plan for the rejuvenation of the river was developed by [Jaipur Development Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Development_Authority) (JDA) in 2015.[^cite_note-27] A 13 km (8.1 mi) stretch of Dravyavati riverfront out of 47.5 km (29.5 mi) was opened after rejuvenation in 2018 and the remaining project was completed in 2022.[^cite_note-28]&#x20;

### Climate

Jaipur has a monsoon-influenced [hot semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_semi-arid_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BSh*) with long, extremely hot summers and short mild to warm winters. Annual precipitation is over 625 millimetres or 25 inches, falling mostly in July and August due to the southwest monsoon, causing the average temperatures in these two months to be lower compared to drier May and June. During the monsoon, there are frequent, heavy rains and thunderstorms, but flooding is not common. The highest temperature ever recorded was 49.0 °C (120.2 °F), on 23 May 1994. The city's average temperature remains below 20 °C or 68 °F between December and February. These months are mild, dry, and pleasant, sometimes chilly. The lowest temperature ever recorded was −2.2 °C (28.0 °F) on 31 January 1905, 1 February 1905, and 16 January 1964. Jaipur, like many other major cities of the world, is a significant [urban heat island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_heat_island) zone with surrounding rural temperatures occasionally falling below freezing in winters.[^cite_note-29]&#x20;

## Government and politics

### Administration

[Jaipur Development Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Development_Authority) is the main planning authority of the city.[^cite_note-46] [Jaipur Municipal Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Municipal_Corporation) (JMC) was established in the year 1994.[^cite_note-\:0-47] The area of the municipal corporation is 467 km<sup>2</sup> (180 sq mi) and is headed by a [mayor](101146.md).[^cite_note-48] In 2020, JMC was bifurcated into two Municipal Corporations, namely Greater Jaipur Municipal Corporation and Jaipur Heritage Municipal Corporation with 150 and 100 wards respectively.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50] The bifurcated Corporations were again merged in 2025 to form a single body with 150 wards.[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52] The latest [elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections) were held in September 2026 with results pending.[^cite_note-53] The administration duties are carried out by the [municipal commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_Commissioner_\(India\)) and his group of officials. The combined estimated municipal budget for the year 2025–26 is ₹2,692.20 crore (US\$280 million).[^cite_note-budget-heritage-9][^cite_note-budget-greater-8] The key revenue sources for the corporation are taxes which include [House tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_tax), Urban Development tax and octroi compensation along with various fees and user charges.[^cite_note-Budget-54] Law and order is maintained by the Jaipur city police under the jurisdiction of the Rajasthan state department.[^cite_note-55] There is a district and sessions court at Jaipur to handle civil and criminal cases.[^cite_note-56]&#x20;

### Politics

Jaipur consists of two [parliamentary](275456.md) constituencies [Jaipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_\(Lok_Sabha_constituency\)) and [Jaipur Rural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Rural_\(Lok_Sabha_constituency\)).[^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58] The [Jaipur Lok Sabha constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Lok_Sabha_constituency) comprises eight [legislative assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan_Legislative_Assembly) segments, all of which fall partly in Jaipur city.[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-\:1-60][^cite_note-\:2-61]&#x20;

### Infrastructure

Jaipur Development Authority is the nodal government agency responsible for the planning and development of Jaipur.[^cite_note-62] The municipal corporation is responsible for maintaining the city's civic infrastructure and carrying out associated administrative duties.[^cite_note-63] Electricity is distributed through Jaipur Vidyut Vitaran Nigam Limited (JVVNL) owned by the [Government of Rajasthan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Rajasthan).[^cite_note-64] [Jaipur municipal corporaiton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Municipal_Corporation) has a fire department wing with 11 fire stations and 50 fire tenders.[^cite_note-65]&#x20;

Established in 2018, Jaipur Water Supply and Sewerage Board (JWSSB) is responsible for the management of water supply and sewerage services in the city.[^cite_note-Infra-66] The agency is responsible for water supply as per the standards stipulated by the [Bureau of Indian Standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Indian_Standards), the State Pollution Control Board and the [Public Health Engineering Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Health_Engineering_Department) (PHED).[^cite_note-Infra-66] It will also be responsible for financing, designing, constructing, altering, repairing, operating, and maintaining various water supply and sewerage schemes in addition to commercial services such as meter reading, billing, and revenue collection.[^cite_note-Infra-66] The city has been divided into four main drainage zones, with the northern and central zones draining into the Dravyavati river, while the western zone drains into the Chandler lake, and the eastern and southern areas combined drain into the Dhundh River.[^cite_note-67] Sewerage systems and STPs have been constructed accordingly, with the installed capacity being 730 km of sewer lines and 442 MLD of sewage treatment.[^cite_note-68] The corporation has a solid waste management system that includes door-to-door collection, transportation of garbage in covered vehicles, proper deployment of dustbins, use of modern equipment.[^cite_note-auto-69] The system ensures private investment as well as public participation with a small amount of monthly user charges. The size of the JMC garbage can be kept at a manageable level.[^cite_note-auto-69] Sanitation work in three zones has been contracted out to private agencies.[^cite_note-auto-69]&#x20;

## Economy

As per the official records released by the Directorate of Economics and Statistics (Rajasthan), the GDP (nominal) of Jaipur district is estimated at [INR](490783.md) 1,22,140 crores (\$15.8 billion) in 2020–21, with a per-capita GDP of INR 141,305.[^cite_note-70] In addition to its role as the provincial capital, educational, and administrative centre, the economy of Jaipur is fuelled by tourism, gemstone cutting, the manufacture of jewellery and luxury textiles, and information technology.[^cite_note-71]&#x20;

Three major trade promotion organisations have their offices in Jaipur: [Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce & Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Indian_Chambers_of_Commerce_%26_Industry), (FICCI) the [PHD Chamber of Commerce and Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PHDCCI-Rajasthan) (PHDCCI) and the [Confederation of Indian Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederation_of_Indian_Industry) (CII) which has its regional offices here. In 2008, Jaipur was ranked 31 among the 50 Emerging Global Outsourcing cities.[^cite_note-72] [Jaipur Stock Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Stock_Exchange) was one of the regional stock exchanges in India and was founded in 1989 but was closed in March 2015.[^cite_note-73]&#x20;

Jaipur has emerged as a hub of automotive industries with [JCB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JCB_\(heavy_equipment_manufacturer\)), [Hero MotoCorp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_MotoCorp) and [Robert Bosch GmbH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Bosch_GmbH) having their manufacturing plants in Jaipur.[^cite_note-74][^cite_note-75][^cite_note-76] There are chemical manufacturers in the city including [Emami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emami) and [National Engineering Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CK_Birla_Group).[^cite_note-77] The city is among top emerging IT hubs of India. [Mahindra World City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahindra_World_City,_Jaipur) is an integrated business zone in Jaipur with several software and IT companies.[^cite_note-78][^cite_note-79] The Government of Rajasthan have built Asia's largest incubator in Jaipur – the Bhamashah Techno Hub.[^cite_note-80]&#x20;

Jaipur is a major hub for arts and crafts. It has many traditional shops selling antiques, jewellery, handicrafts, gems, bangles, pottery, carpets, textiles, leather, and metal products. Jaipur is one of India's largest manufacturers of [hand-knotted rugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Rugs).[^cite_note-81][^cite_note-82] [Jaipur foot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_foot), a rubber-based [prosthetic leg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_limb) for people with below-knee [amputations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amputation), was designed and is produced in Jaipur.[^cite_note-83][^cite_note-84] [World Trade Park Jaipur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Park,_Jaipur), is a shopping mall in Jaipur opened in 2012.&#x20;

### Culture

Jaipur has many cultural sites like [Jawahar Kala Kendra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawahar_Kala_Kendra) established by [Charles Correa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Correa) and Ravindra Manch. The Government Central Museum hosts several art and antiquities. There is a government museum at Hawa Mahal and an art gallery at [Viratnagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viratnagar). There are statues depicting [Rajasthani culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthani_culture) around the city.[^cite_note-97][^cite_note-98] Jaipur has many traditional shops selling antiques and handicrafts, as well as contemporary brands reviving traditional techniques, such as [Anokhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anokhi). The prior rulers of Jaipur patronised some arts and crafts. They invited skilled artisans, artists, and craftsmen from India and abroad who settled in the city. Some of the crafts include [bandhani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandhani), [block printing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Block_printing), [stone carving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_carving) and [sculpture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sculpture), [tarkashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inlay), [zari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zari), [gota-patti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gota_work), [kinari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinari) and [zardozi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zardozi), silver jewellery, [gems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemstone), [kundan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kundan), [meenakari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitreous_enamel) and [jewellery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewellery), [Lakh ki Chudiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangle), [miniature paintings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajput_painting), [blue pottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Pottery_of_Jaipur), [ivory carving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory_carving), [shellac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shellac) work and [leather ware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leather_crafting).[^cite_note-Pink-99][^cite_note-JDA-95]&#x20;

Jaipur has its own performing arts. The Jaipur Gharana for [Kathak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathak) is one of the three gharanas of the major north Indian classical dance form of Kathak.[^cite_note-KK-100] The Jaipur Gharana of Kathak is known for its rapid intricate dance forms, vivacious body movements and subtle Abhinaya.[^cite_note-KK-100] The [Ghoomar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghoomar) is a popular folk dance style.[^cite_note-101][^cite_note-102][^cite_note-103] Tamasha is an art form where [Kathputli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathputli_\(Puppet\)) puppet dance is shown in play form.[^cite_note-Org-44] Major festivals celebrated in Jaipur include [Elephant Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephant_Festival), [Gangaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangaur), [Makar Sankranti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makar_Sankranti), [Holi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holi), [Diwali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diwali), [Vijayadashami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayadashami), [Teej](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teej), [Eid](154944.md), [Mahavir Jayanti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahavir_Jayanti) and [Christmas](6237.md). Jaipur is also famous for the [Jaipur Literature Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Literature_Festival), the world's largest free literature festival in which authors, writers, and literature lovers from all over the country participate.[^cite_note-104]&#x20;

#### Architecture

The city was planned according to the Indian Vastu shastra by Vidyadhar Bhattacharya in 1727.[^cite_note-105] Three gates are facing east, west, and north. The eastern gate is called *Suraj pol* (sun gate), the western gate is called *Chand pol* (moon gate) and the northern gate faces the ancestral capital of [Amer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amer_Fort).[^cite_note-History-17][^cite_note-106] The architecture of the city was heavily influenced by the 17th century architectural renaissance during Mughal rule in Northern India. The city was divided into nine blocks, two of which contained the state buildings and palaces, with the remaining seven allotted to the public. Huge ramparts were built, pierced by seven fortified gates.[^cite_note-Hist1-107] The city is unusual among pre-modern Indian cities in the regularity of its streets, and the division of the city into six sectors by broad streets 34 m (111 ft) wide. The urban quarters are further divided by networks of gridded streets. Five-quarters wrap around the east, south, and west sides of a central palace quarter, with a sixth quarter immediately to the east. The Palace quarter encloses the [Hawa Mahal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawa_Mahal) palace complex, formal gardens, and a small lake. [Nahargarh Fort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahargarh_Fort), which was the residence of King Sawai Jai Singh II, crowns the hill in the northwest corner of the old city.[^cite_note-JDA-95]&#x20;

## Transport

### Air



[Jaipur International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_International_Airport)

[Jaipur International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_International_Airport) is located in the southern suburb of [Sanganer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanganer), which is located 13 km (8.1 mi) from Jaipur.It is the 13th [busiest airport in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_the_busiest_airports_in_India) in daily scheduled flight operations. ### Rail



[Jaipur Junction Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Junction_railway_station)

[Jaipur Junction railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Junction_railway_station) was built in 1875 and is situated at the centre of Rajasthan. The cornerstone of the existing Jaipur railway station building was laid on 4 May 1956 by Maharaja Sawai Man Singh II of Jaipur, and construction took three years to complete. The station harnesses solar energy technology to power its operations. Jaipur is the headquarters of [Jaipur Railway division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_railway_division) and [North Western Railway Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Western_Railway_Zone) of Indian Railways.[^cite_note-115] Jaipur Junction is the busiest station in Rajasthan with more than 45,000 passengers daily.[^cite_note-116] During [Meter Gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meter_Gauge) era, The fastest MG Train of India & the world, Pink City Express used to connect Jaipur with Delhi. Covering the 308.2 kilometres (191.5 mi) distance in 5 hrs 30 mins time, running at a record 56 kilometres per hour (35 mph) speed until 1997. In 1997 Pink City Express got replaced by now running 12015/12016 [Ajmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajmer) [Shatabdi Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shatabdi_Express)&#x20;

Metro



[Jaipur Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Metro)

[Jaipur Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Metro) commenced commercial operation on 3 June 2015.[^cite_note-117] Construction on the mostly elevated part of the first line, called Phase 1A, comprising 9.63 kilometres (5.98 mi) of route from [Mansarovar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansarovar_\(Jaipur\)) to Chandpole Bazaar, started in November 2010, and was completed in 2014. The Jaipur Metro began commercial service between [Chandpole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandpole_metro_station) and Mansarovar on 3 June 2015. The Jaipur Metro Rail system is [India's sixth metro rail system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_rail_transit_in_India#List_of_systems). The Jaipur Metro is the first metro in India to run on a triple-storey elevated road and metro track. Phase 1-B, from Chandpole to [Badi Chaupar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badi_Chaupar), began operation on 23 September 2020. Phase-1A is operational between Mansarovar and Chandpole consisting of nine stations namely Mansarovar, New Aatish Market, Vivek Vihar, Shyam Nagar, Ram Nagar, Civil Line, Railway Station, Sindhi Camp and Chandpole.[^cite_note-118] The Phase-1B was constructed with an estimated cost of ₹97.32 billion (\$1.74 billion).[^cite_note-119] It became operational on 23 September 2020.[^cite_note-120][^cite_note-phase1b-121]&#x20;

## Sports



[Sawai Mansingh Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sawai_Mansingh_Stadium)

The main cricket stadium in the city, [Sawai Mansingh Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sawai_Mansingh_Stadium), has a seating capacity of 30,000 and has hosted national and international cricket matches.[^cite_note-136] It is also the home ground of [IPL](13260340.md) team [Rajasthan Royals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan_Royals). [Sawai Mansingh Indoor Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sawai_Mansingh_Indoor_Stadium), [Chaugan Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaugan_Stadium) and [Railway Cricket Ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Railway_Cricket_Ground) are the other sporting arenas in the city. A new stadium has been proposed for Chonp Village with a seating capacity 75,000. It would be the third-largest cricket stadium in the world after the [Narendra Modi Stadium](80201506.md) and the [Melbourne Cricket Ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne_Cricket_Ground). [^cite_note-137][^cite_note-138] The city is represented in the IPL by Rajasthan Royals (2008–2016; 2018–present)[^cite_note-139][^cite_note-140] and in [Pro Kabaddi League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro_Kabaddi_League) by [Jaipur Pink Panthers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaipur_Pink_Panthers).[^cite_note-141]&#x20;

## In popular culture

[Paul McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_McCartney) wrote and recorded the Jaipur tribute song "Riding into Jaipur" (4\:08) on his 2001 studio album *[Driving Rain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Driving_Rain)*.&#x20;

Jaipur is the setting for the film *[The Best Exotic Marigold Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Best_Exotic_Marigold_Hotel)* and its sequel, *[The Second Best Exotic Marigold Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Second_Best_Exotic_Marigold_Hotel)*, which follow the adventures of a group of senior European immigrants who retire to Jaipur and in the process discover their true selves.&#x20;

The opening song of [the Mountain Goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mountain_Goats)' 2000 album *[The Coroner's Gambit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Coroner%27s_Gambit)*, "Jaipur," describes the narrator's arrival at "the gates of the fabled pink city."&#x20;
