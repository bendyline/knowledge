The **Shanghai–Xi'an Expressway** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 上海—西安高速公路), designated as **G40** and commonly referred to as the **Hushaan Expressway** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 沪陕高速公路) is an expressway that connects the cities of [Shanghai](27643.md) and [Xi'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi'an), [Shaanxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaanxi). It is 1,490 km (930 mi) in length.

The expressway was completed after [Chongqi Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chongqi_Bridge) opened to commercial traffic on December 24, 2011.

The expressway uses the [Shanghai Yangtze River Tunnel and Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Yangtze_River_Tunnel_and_Bridge) and the [Chongqi Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chongqi_Bridge), to cross the [Yangtze River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangtze_River) north of [Shanghai](27643.md).

Wuzhuang toll station in Chuzhou is Asia's largest toll plaza, having 12 + 24 toll lanes.[^cite_note-2] It is located along G40 as one of the important east-west passages from the western direction to Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Shanghai. It is also located close to Nanjing, an important transport node in Jiangsu. During the post-Spring Festival rush of 2025, the station reported a maximum of 126,000 daily through passes.[^cite_note-3]

The expressway passes through the following cities:[^cite_note-gaosu-1]

- [Shanghai](27643.md)

- [Nantong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nantong), [Jiangsu](166412.md)

- [Yangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangzhou), Jiangsu

- [Nanjing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing), Jiangsu

- [Hefei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hefei), [Anhui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhui)

- [Lu'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lu'an), Anhui

- [Xinyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinyang), [Henan](166408.md)

- [Nanyang](464885.md), Henan

- [Shangzhou District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shangzhou_District), [Shangluo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shangluo), [Shaanxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaanxi)

- [Xi'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi'an), Shaanxi
