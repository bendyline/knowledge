The **1000 metres**, **1 kilometre run**, or **1K** **run** is an uncommon [middle-distance running](89280.md) event in [track and field](194904.md) competitions. It consists of two and a half laps around an outdoor 400 m track, or five laps around an indoor 200 m track.

The **1000 yards**, an [imperial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_measurement) alternative (914.4 m), was sometimes also contested.[^cite_note-la84foundation1-1][^cite_note-2]
