**Uonuma** (魚沼市, *Uonuma-shi*) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_Japan) located in [Niigata Prefecture](181660.md), Japan. As of 1 July 2019, the city had an estimated population of 35,027 in 13,289 households,[^cite_note-1] and a [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) of 37 persons per km<sup>2</sup>. Its total area is 946.76 square kilometres (365.55 sq mi). The city is famous for its *[koshihikari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koshihikari)* rice, which commands a premium in the Japanese market.

## Geography

Uonuma is located in an inland region of south-central Niigata Prefecture, bordered by [Fukushima Prefecture](211914.md) to the east and [Gunma Prefecture](212632.md) to the south. Parts of the city are within the borders of [Oze National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oze_National_Park) or the [Echigo Sanzan-Tadami Quasi-National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echigo_Sanzan-Tadami_Quasi-National_Park)

## Climate

Uonuma has a [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) (Köppen *Cfa*) characterized by warm, wet summers and cold winters with heavy snowfall. The average annual temperature in Uonuma is 12.6 °C. The average annual rainfall is 2049 mm with September as the wettest month. The temperatures are highest on average in August, at around 25.0 °C, and lowest in January, at around 0.4 °C.[^cite_note-2]

## History

The area of present-day Uonuma was part of ancient [Echigo Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echigo_Province), with the name of Uonuma appearing in the *[Shoku Nihongi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoku_Nihongi)* in an entry dated 702 AD. During the [Edo period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_period), the area was largely *[tenryō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenry%C5%8D)* territory administered directly by the [Tokugawa shogunate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokugawa_shogunate) or part of the holdings of [Aizu Domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aizu_Domain). The area was greatly affected by the [Tenpō famine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenp%C5%8D_famine) of 1835–1837. After the [Meiji restoration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_restoration), the area was organised as part of [Kitauonuma District, Niigata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitauonuma_District,_Niigata) with the creation of the modern municipalities system. The modern city of Uonuma was established on November 1, 2004, from the merger of the towns of [Horinouchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horinouchi,_Niigata) and [Koide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koide,_Niigata), and the villages of [Hirokami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hirokami,_Niigata), [Irihirose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irihirose,_Niigata), [Sumon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumon,_Niigata) and [Yunotani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yunotani,_Niigata) (all from Kitauonuma District).

## Government



Uonuma City Hall

Uonuma has a [mayor-council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor-council) form of government with a directly elected mayor and a [unicameral](204304.md) city legislature of 20 members.

## Economy

The economy of Uonuma is dominated by agriculture, predominantly rice production, along with *[sake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sake)* brewing.

## Education

Uonuma has nine public elementary schools and six public middle schools operated by the city government. The city has two public high schools operated by the Niigata Prefectural Board of Education, and also two special education schools.
