**Tasnim News Agency** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): خبرگزاری تسنیم) is a semi-official news agency in [Iran](14653.md)[^cite_note-2] associated with the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC).[^cite_note-Koyagi-3] Launched in 2012, its purpose is to cover a variety of political, social, economic and international subjects along with many other fields.[^cite_note-4] Tasnim publishes all content on its website under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Commons_license#Version_4.0).[^cite_note-\:0-5]

## Background

In [Islam](6037917.md), Tasnim (تسنیم) is the name of a [spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_\(hydrology\)) in [Paradise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradise) from which those nearest to [God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/God_in_Islam) are said to be able to drink. Tasnim's stated goal is to "\[defend] the [Islamic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_republic#Iran) against negative media propaganda campaigns and provide our readers with realities on the ground about Iran and [Islam](6037917.md)."[^cite_note-\:0-5]

Tasnim is headquartered in Iran's capital [Tehran](57654.md), with reporters across the country and the Middle East.

### Affiliation

The IRGC controls Tasnim, which often magnifies the IRGC's ideology. Tasnim is the IRGC's largest owned media outlet.[^cite_note-TB-6][^cite_note-Koyagi-3][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-RFE-8] During a visit to Tasnim's offices in 2023, then-IRGC commander-in-chief [Mohammad Ali Jafari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Ali_Jafari) stated, *"The faithful and revolutionary media have today a very heavy duty in confronting anti-Islamic and anti-human plots of the oppressors"*.[^cite_note-9]

According to [Tehran Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran_Bureau), Tasnim plays an active role in the Iranian government's state-led disinformation campaigns against protestors.[^cite_note-TB-6]

## History

Tasnim was founded on 30 June 2012 by [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) (IRGC) commander Hamidreza Moghaddamfar, board chairman Jafar Darouneh, and CEO Majid Qolizadeh Zahmatkesh. Darouneh was head of [Tehran](57654.md)'s Department of Education during the presidency of [Mahmoud Ahmadinejad](7907317.md).[^cite_note-TB-6]

In August 2023, the [Afghan Taliban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_Taliban) detained a photographer for Tasnim.[^cite_note-10]

### COVID-19 pandemic

According to the [Anti-Defamation League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Defamation_League), Tasnim has alleged in a series of [propaganda](33693903.md) articles that [COVID-19](63030231.md) was part of "an American and [Jewish plot at world domination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_world_domination) through population control" hatched by [Henry Kissinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Kissinger), who is described as "the unique Jewish scholar and American strategist" and "a master controller of government and international finance."[^cite_note-11]

### International sanctions

See also: [International sanctions against Iran](25699056.md) and [United States sanctions against Iran](9941859.md)

On September 15, 2023, the [European Union](9317.md) sanctioned Tasnim News Agency for "serious human rights violations in Iran," stating that the "news outlet is responsible for publishing false confessions by protesters on its website and social media accounts as well as for posting pictures of protestors on social media and asking readers to help identify them."[^cite_note-12] The same day, the [United States Department of the Treasury](53667.md) added Tasnim News Agency to the [Office of Foreign Assets Control](231045.md) (OFAC)'s [Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specially_Designated_Nationals_and_Blocked_Persons_List).[^cite_note-13]

Tasnimnews.com went offline on January 12, 2026, causing Tasnim to switch to the .ir domain.[^cite_note-14] Tasnim and [Fars News Agency](5000099.md) claimed that the domain was blocked by the United States.[^cite_note-15] In late 2026 its [X.com](9988187.md) accounts were suspended and its [Telegram](41501305.md) channel closed.[^cite_note-16]

## Non-Persian versions

Tasnim staff includes English speakers, who translate some of the agency's articles into English.[^cite_note-17] In 2022, Tasnim launched a [Hebrew-language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Hebrew) website.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] In 2023, [Sputnik](44384287.md) signed a deal with Tasnim to launch a Russian website.[^cite_note-20]
