# Submarine cable

**Submarine cable** is any [electrical cable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_cable) that is laid on the [seabed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seabed), although the term is often extended to encompass cables laid on the bottom of large freshwater bodies of water.

Examples include:

- [Submarine communications cable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submarine_communications_cable)

- [Submarine power cable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submarine_power_cable)
