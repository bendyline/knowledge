**Oriental Mindoro** ([Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Silangang Mindoro*), officially the **Province of Oriental Mindoro** ([Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Lalawigan ng Silangang Mindoro (Oriental Mindoro)*), is a [province](165100.md) in the [Philippines](23440.md) located on the island of [Mindoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindoro) under [Mimaropa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimaropa) [region](216786.md) in [Luzon](69375.md), about 140 kilometres (87 mi) southwest of [Manila](184334.md). The province is bordered by the [Verde Island Passage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verde_Island_Passage) and the rest of [Batangas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batangas) to the north, by [Marinduque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marinduque), [Maestre de Campo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maestre_de_Campo_Island) (or known as *Sibale* but official name is *Concepcion*) Island, [Tablas Strait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tablas_Strait) and the rest of [Romblon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romblon) to the east, by [Semirara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semirara_Island) and the rest of [Caluya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caluya) Islands, [Antique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antique_\(province\)) to the south, and by [Occidental Mindoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occidental_Mindoro) to the west. Its provincial capital [Calapan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calapan), the only city in the island, is the most-populous in the province, and Mimaropa's regional center.

Oriental Mindoro is touted as the country's emerging eco-tourism destination. In 2005, the Philippines was found to be the center of marine fish biodiversity and the home of the most diverse marine ecosystem in the world,[^cite_note-5] by American biologists Kent Carpenter and Victor Springer. Most of the [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemic) species in the Philippines are found in the Verde Island Passage between Mindoro island and the main island of Luzon. The passage houses 2,983 individual species of algae, corals, crustaceans, mollusks, fishes, marine reptiles, and marine mammals, based on a study conducted by Carpenter and Springer in 2005.

[UNESCO](21786641.md) declared [Puerto Galera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Galera,_Oriental_Mindoro) a [biosphere reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biosphere_reserves_of_the_Philippines)[^cite_note-6] under its Man and the Biosphere Programme in the 1970s. The [Verde Island Passage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verde_Island_Passage) is at the apex of the so-called [Coral Triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coral_Triangle) – the Philippines, Indonesia, and Malaysia – which has the distinction of being the "center of the center of the world's marine biodiversity" and the "center of the center of marine shorefish biodiversity".

### Japanese occupation

A [Japanese force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_Army) landed on northeastern Mindoro in the Philippine Islands on 27 February 1942.

Almost three years later, after the invasion of Mindoro by Allied forces, they proceeded to land to [Bongabong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bongabong) and heading to and capturing Calapan, the capital of the united [Mindoro Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindoro_\(province\)) at the time on 24 January 1945.

### Philippine independence

#### Foundation

After [World War II](32927.md), reconstruction and rehabilitation of infrastructure and the economy took place on the island, which then constituted a single province. This was partitioned on 13 June 1950 into Oriental Mindoro and [Occidental Mindoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occidental_Mindoro) by virtue of *[Republic Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_Act) No. 505*.[^cite_note-ChanRobles-RA505-7]

In the decades after the War, Mindoro attracted settlers from overpopulated provinces in the Philippines. Apart from the hope to become landowners or to have better tenancy conditions, the [Hukbalahap Rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hukbalahap_Rebellion) in [Central Luzon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Luzon) was an important factor for migration. Under the settlement program of the National Resettlement and Rehabilitation Administration (NARRA) founded in 1954, families from [Central Luzon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Luzon) were settled in the Bongabong-Pinamalayan area. This project ended in 1956 after 606 families (3,636 people) were settled on 8,600 hectares (21,000 acres) of public land. Since then, new settlers have continued to migrate to Mindoro until today.

### Contemporary

On November 15, 1994, a [magnitude 7.1 earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Mindoro_earthquake) struck the [Verde Island Passage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verde_Island_Passage) located north of Oriental Mindoro, killing 69 people in the province, with most casualties due to the tsunami caused by the earthquake.[^cite_note-8] Within a year after the calamity, however, the provincial government under Governor Rodolfo G. Valencia was able to successfully rehabilitate the province in a brisk manner, and was thus conferred the Disaster Management Award by the [National Disaster Coordinating Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Disaster_Risk_Reduction_and_Management_Council) in July 1995.[^cite_note-9]

The island currently sees an ongoing conflict between the [Armed Forces of the Philippines](23448.md) and insurgents. On March 6, 2010, eleven soldiers were killed and seven were hurt in a gun battle with the [New People's Army](1262096.md) in [Mansalay, Oriental Mindoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansalay,_Oriental_Mindoro).[^cite_note-10]

## Geography



Administrative divisions of Oriental Mindoro

Oriental Mindoro covers a total area of 4,238.38 square kilometres (1,636.45 sq mi)[^cite_note-nscb-11] occupying the eastern section of [Mindoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindoro) island in [Mimaropa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimaropa) region. The province is bordered by the [Verde Island Passage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verde_Island_Passage) to the north, by [Marinduque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marinduque), [Maestre de Campo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concepcion,_Romblon), [Tablas Strait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tablas_Strait) and the rest of [Romblon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romblon) to the east, by [Semirara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semirara_Island) and the rest of [Caluya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caluya) Islands, [Antique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antique_\(province\)) to the south, and by [Occidental Mindoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occidental_Mindoro) to the west.

The western portion of the province is mountainous or rugged, while the east has hills and flood plains. [Mount Halcon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Halcon), standing 2,582 metres (8,471 ft) above sea level, is the 18th highest mountain in the country and is the province's and island's highest peak. [Lake Naujan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Naujan), the fifth largest lake in the country with an area of approximately 8,125 hectares (20,080 acres) of open water, is located in the northeastern part of the island and the province.

### Climate

Oriental Mindoro has no distinct wet or dry season. Average temperature ranges from 28 to 32 °C (82 to 90 °F). The province experiences maximum rainfall during the months of June to September. Relative humidity is registered at 87%.[^cite_note-14]

The population of Oriental Mindoro in the 2024 census was 919,504 people,[^cite_note-PSA20–04B-12] with a density of 220 inhabitants per square kilometre or 570 inhabitants per square mile.

The province is largely rural, with 70% of the population engaged in agriculture and fishing and with only 30% living in urban centers.

### Languages

[Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language) and [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_English) are widely spoken in the province. Tagalog is usually spoken with [Batangas dialect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batangas_Tagalog) both due to its geographical contact with [Batangas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batangas) and Batangueño residents in the province, although it has been greatly influenced by the native [Mangyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangyan_language) and [Visayan languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visayan_languages), creating another unique dialect. Other languages spoken are [Ilocano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_language), [Bicolano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicolano_languages), [Kapampangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapampangan_language), [Maranao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maranao_language), [Maguindanaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_language), [Tausug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tausug_language) and [Cebuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_language), to varying degrees by their respective communities within the province. Dialects of the [Mangyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangyan) language are Arayan, Alagnan, Buhid, Hanunoo, and Tadyawan.[^cite_note-18] People from the southern part can understand [Hiligaynon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiligaynon_language) and [Karay-a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karay-a_language) due to its closeness facing [Panay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panay).

### Religion

A very large portion of the population are of [Roman Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholicism_in_the_Philippines) conviction (81%).[^cite_note-19] Other [Christian](8276776.md) and [Islamic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic) beliefs constitute most of the remaining religious affiliations in the province. Other populations practice [indigenous Philippine folk religions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_Philippine_folk_religions), [animism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animism), or [atheism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheism).

Further information: [Apostolic Vicariate of Calapan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Vicariate_of_Calapan)

### Indigenous people

Further information: [Mangyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangyan)

The indigenous people of Oriental Mindoro are the [Mangyans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangyan_people) (*Manguianes* in [Spanish](26825.md), *Mañguianes* in [Old Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Tagalog)), consisting of seven distinct tribes. They occupy the interior, especially the highlands. Mangyans have inhabited the island since pre-history. They are believed to have originally traveled from Indonesia and settled down for good on the island.

Oriental Mindoro's rich and arable land is suitable for agriculture. It produces large quantities of [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice), [corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [coconut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coconut), [vegetables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetables) and [fruits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruits) like [calamansi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calamansi), [banana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana), rambutan, *marang* or *uloy*, lanzones and [durian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durian). Its total agricultural area is 169,603.34 hectares (419,099.0 acres). Based on agricultural statistics, 85,244 hectares (210,640 acres) are devoted to *palay* production while 21,671 hectares (53,550 acres)s to coconut plantation. For 2019, the province has been one of the top producers of rice becoming a source of rice for the [National Food Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Food_Authority_\(Philippines\))'s rice procurement target.[^cite_note-29]

## Tourist attractions

[Calapan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calapan) in the northeast of Mindoro Island is the capital and largest city in the province.

Access from Luzon via the Batangas ports mostly goes through the [Verde Island Passage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verde_Island_Passage) to reach [Puerto Galera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Galera), the key entry point to Oriental Mindoro. In November 2004, Puerto Galera was voted a member of the UNESCO-affiliated ‘[The Club of the Most Beautiful Bays in the World](https://web.archive.org/web/20091116013251/http://www.world-bays.com/puerto-galera-bay-47-67.html). Puerto Galera features a natural harbor which also protects ships, yachts and *bancas* from strong typhoons sweeping in from the Pacific.

Puerto Galera, known for its white sand beaches and the accompanying honky tonk bar scene on some key beaches, is known for its dive sites with a biodiversity of marine life in and around the coral reefs. Underwater rock formations and coral gardens are home to a variety of marine life, which include numerous species of [nudibranch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nudibranch), [mantis shrimp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantis_shrimp), [sea anemone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_anemone), moray and sea fan sea horses. The seas off the coast are host to passing dolphin pods and even the rarer [whale shark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whale_shark), and are part of the migratory small whale route from the [Visayas](72418.md).

Towering 8,488 feet (2,587 m) above sea level, [Mount Halcon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Halcon) in [Baco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baco,_Oriental_Mindoro), is a popular mountain climbing destination. Local and migratory birds allow birdwatching activities. The province is home to endemic wildlife like the [tamaraw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamaraw), a straight-horned water buffalo.

Other tourist destinations include:

### Hidden Paradise

The attraction consists of a natural spring with a swimming pool and picnic cottages. It is located in Baco, and can be reached in 45 minutes by taking a jeepney bound for the Calapan market.

### Alibatan Island

The island measures 5 ha and is noted for its white sand. The place serves as the breeding place of seagulls and turtles. Corals and oysters can be found all around the area. The island is also called "Target Island" because Americans used the island for bombing practice in 1946. It is located in Baco, and can be reached in an hour through a motorized *banca* from the market square.

### Buyayao Island

It is a rocky island, except for the area facing Buyayao Peninsula. Big snakes called "sobre kama" are found in the mountains. The water around the island is suitable for deep-sea fishing. It is located off the coast of Mansalay, and can be reached in 30 minutes by motorized *banca* from Mansalay market.

### Suqui Beach

This is a public beach with cottages and other facilities. It is a favorite place for the local people, especially during the summer. It is located in Suqui, Calapan, and can be reached in 15 minutes by taking a tricycle from the town proper.

### Banilad Beach

The place is a good spot for picnicking and swimming, especially during the summer. It is located in Pili, Pinamalayan, and can be reached by taking a jeepney to the port of Barangay Wawa, then an hour's ride by motorized banca to the beach.

### Bongol Beach

It is a secluded beach, with a 2-kilometre (1.2 mi) white sand beach. It is located in Bongol, Pinamalayan, and can be reached in two hours by boarding a Bongol-bound jeepney from the town proper of Pinamalayan and then a tricycle to the beach area.

### Tamaraw Falls

The 423-foot (129 m) waterfalls, situated alongside the road, are actually a series of asymmetrical falls, leading to the grand one, dropping to a frothy waterbed below. The cascading waterfalls are a great attraction to passers-by. Tamaraw Falls is located in Barangay Villaflor, Puerto Galera. It can be reached in two hours by taking a jeepney bound for Barangay Villaflor from Puerto Galera, or Calapan.

### La Laguna Beach

An ideal place for swimming, snorkeling, and scuba diving. There are a number of resorts that offer accommodation and other travel and aqua sports facilities to both domestic and international tourists. It is located in La Laguna, Puerto Galera, and can be reached in 30 minutes from the town proper of Puerto Galera by taking a jeepney bound for La Laguna.

### Sabang Beach

This area is ideal for swimming, snorkeling, and scuba diving. A cluster of nightspots abound in this area, which makes it popular for evening socials. Cottages with entertainment facilities stand close to each other. Low-budget accommodations are available. It is located east of Puerto Galera, and can be reached in 15 minutes from the Puerto Galera pier by taking a jeepney or an outrigger banca.

### Small La Laguna

Contrary to its name, Small La Laguna is bigger than La Laguna, and has more facilities. It has coral reefs, and its water is ideal for snorkeling and scuba diving. There are diving equipment for rent at nearby dive shops. It is located beside Sabang Beach, and can be reached in 20 minutes from the pier by taking a jeepney bound for Sabang.

### Talipanan Beach

This area is not as busy as the other beaches in Puerto Galera. Thus, it is an ideal site for private swimming. It is located in Puerto Galera, and can be reached in 40 minutes by taking a jeepney or an outrigger boat from the town pier.

### White Sand Beach

The white sandy strips of this beach offer excellent opportunities for swimming. Resorts in this area provide entertainment facilities for tourists' greater amusement and leisure. It is located in Barangay San Isidro, Puerto Galera, and is accessible in 30 minutes from the town proper by a jeepney or a boat.

### Punta Guarda Beach

This beach is far from the busy areas of Puerto Galera; thus, it is ideal for those who seek privacy and retreat. It is located in Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro, and can be reached by taking a 10-minute jeepney ride from the town proper, to about one kilometer on the beach mark. The remaining distance is covered on foot.

### Melco Beach

It is a beige-colored sandy beach with cottages for rent. It is located in Roxas, Oriental Mindoro, and can be reached in 10 minutes by a banca from the pier.
