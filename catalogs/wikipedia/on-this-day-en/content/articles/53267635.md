# Traffic obstruction



[Just Stop Oil](70538003.md) protesters on a roadway in Portsmouth, England, 2023

**Traffic obstruction** is a common tactic used during public [protests](266137.md) and [political demonstrations](6787314.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## Legality

In 2020, the [Supreme Court of India](395852.md) ruled that the government may not permit protestors to obstruct traffic. The court said that the [right to free speech](21401843.md) that is granted in a democracy does not nullify the duties of the citizenry.[^cite_note-3]

## Examples

Examples of intentional traffic obstructions aimed to articulate a protest agenda include [Extinction Rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extinction_Rebellion) protests,[^cite_note-4] [air traffic controller strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_Air_Traffic_Controllers_Organization_\(1968\)), [highway revolts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highway_revolts), [Critical Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_Mass_\(cycling\)) bicycle rides corking intersections, obstruction of rail transport of nuclear fuel in Germany, road blockades by farmers or truckers in France and other countries, impact on [Eurotunnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurotunnel) operations by the [Migrant Crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migrants_around_Calais) around Calais, pipeline protests (e.g. [Dakota Access Pipeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakota_Access_Pipeline_protests)), etc.&#x20;

Further information: [ICE and New York City during the second Trump presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICE_and_New_York_City_during_the_second_Trump_presidency)

On September 18, 2025, during a protest outside immigration court and detention facility [26 Federal Plaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/26_Federal_Plaza), protesters outside the facility crowded together and blocked a garage that an ICE vehicle was attempting to exit.[^cite_note-guardiansep-5] The [Department of Homeland Security](58236.md) (DHS) contacted the NYPD, who sent the controversial [Strategic Response Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategic_Response_Group_\(New_York_City_Police_Department\)) policing unit to move in and arrest protesters blocking the garage who chose to remain after receiving dispersal orders from the NYPD.[^cite_note-guardiansep-5] Activist and politician [Jumaane Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jumaane_Williams) was the first to be detained outside,[^cite_note-guardiansep-5] and four government officials were arrested in total outside the facility.[^cite_note-waposep-6] A DHS spokesperson claimed 71 "agitators" and "[sanctuary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanctuary_city) politicians" had been arrested following the incident.[^cite_note-guardiansep-5] [Brad Lander's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Lander) office claimed more than 75 arrests were made.[^cite_note-waposep-6]

ICE agents advance on [Delaney Hall protesters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Delaney_Hall_hunger_strike_and_protests) in a [human chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lock-on_\(protest_tactic\)), striking them with [batons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baton_\(law_enforcement\)) and pushing them back, May 27, 2026

Traffic obstruction was used at the [2026 Delaney Hall hunger strike and protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Delaney_Hall_hunger_strike_and_protests), and [Immigration and Customs Enforcement](216537.md) (ICE) agents attacked protesters in response making arrests.[^cite_note-amnyjune6-7][^cite_note-thenation-8]
