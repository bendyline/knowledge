The **Swedish Coast Guard** ([Swedish](26689.md): *Kustbevakningen*) is a [Swedish](5058739.md) civilian [government agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_agencies_in_Sweden) tasked with:

- maritime surveillance and other control and inspection tasks as well as environmental cleanup after oil spills at sea.

- co-ordinate the civilian needs for maritime surveillance and maritime information.

- follow international development within the field and take part in international efforts to establish border controls, law enforcement at sea, [environmental protection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_protection) at sea and other maritime surveillance tasks.

The Swedish Coast Guard carries out some of its surveillance by air (from its base at [Skavsta Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skavsta_Airport) south-west of [Stockholm](26741.md)), and in the winter-time by [hovercraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hovercraft) and [snowmobiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowmobile) on the ice-covered waters of the [Bothnian Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bothnian_Bay) from its [Luleå](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lule%C3%A5) station. The Coast Guard also has regular maritime duties in [Vänern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4nern), Europe's third largest lake, operating out of [Vänersborg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4nersborg).

## History

In January 2025, the Swedish Government tasked the Coast Guard and the [Swedish Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Armed_Forces) with expanding their cooperation to enhance security and surveillance in the [Baltic Sea](3335.md).[^cite_note-Regeringen2025-1] Under the directive, the Coast Guard's role was reconfigured to provide formal support to total defence during periods of heightened alert and war, while maintaining its status as a civilian agency without combat tasks.[^cite_note-Regeringen2025-1]

As part of this transition, the agency began arming its personnel and fleet in 2026. Coast Guard officers were issued [Ak 24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak_24) assault rifles and enhanced protective equipment, while patrol vessels were fitted with [Ksp 58](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ksp_58) machine guns.[^cite_note-KBV2026-2][^cite_note-SVT2026-3] The measure was intended to strengthen the protection of critical subsea infrastructure and address potential threats in the Baltic Sea, such as drone attacks.[^cite_note-Expressen2026-4]

## Organization



Headquarters of the Swedish Coast Guard in [Karlskrona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlskrona).

The Coast Guard has 26 coastal stations, including an aviation coastal station. The stations fall under four regional areas; with the regional headquarters located in [Härnösand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A4rn%C3%B6sand), [Stockholm](26741.md), [Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothenburg), and [Karlskrona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlskrona) respectively. The four management centers control the daily operational activities.&#x20;

The national operational control is co-located with the [Swedish Maritime Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Maritime_Administration), which is responsible for the Swedish [Joint Rescue Co-ordination Centre (JRCC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rescue_coordination_centre), and the [Swedish navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Navy) at [Kärringberget navalbase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4th_Marine_Regiment_\(Sweden\)) in Gothenburg.&#x20;

The Coast Guard's central headquarters is located in the historic 17th century naval city of Karlskrona, which is a [UNESCO](21786641.md) [World Heritage Site](44940.md).

The total number of Coast Guard employees across the country amounts to around 800.[^cite_note-5]

## Coast Guard vessels

Main article: [Swedish Coast Guard ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Coast_Guard_ships)



Ships of the Swedish Coastguard in Karlskrona

### Multipurpose ships

The Coast Guard currently has 10 ships which combine the characteristics of both environmental protection vessels and surveillance ships, as well as emergency tow ships.

### Maritime surveillance ships



Swedish Coast Guard Multi Purpose Vessel *[KBV 001 Poseidon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KBV_001_Poseidon?action=edit\&redlink=1)*

The Coast Guard currently has 16 maritime surveillance ships which are used mainly for patrolling with some also given oil spill response capacity.

### Environmental protection vessels

The Coast Guard currently has 5 environmental protection vessels primarily used for oil spill response, and secondly for patrolling.

### Hovercraft

The Coast Guard operates five [hovercraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hovercraft) mainly in northern Sweden where they can easily travel over both ice, water and land. KBV 593 based in Luleå, KBV 592 based in Umeå, KBV 591 based in Örnsköldsvik, KBV 594 KBV based in Vaxholm, and 595.[^cite_note-6]

### Barges

The Coast Guard maintains one large barge, KBV 866 in Härnösand, used for the storage of absorbed oil from spills.[^cite_note-7]

### Boats

The Coast Guard currently has over 100 boats. Some boats are used as a complement to larger ships, while others operate as separate entities. The boats are divided into four groups: High Speed/[Go Fast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Go-fast_boat), [rib-boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigid-hulled_inflatable_boat), [dinghies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dingy) and [work boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Work_boat).

| --- |

The Coast Guard has three [Bombardier Dash 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardier_Dash_8) Q-300 aircraft numbered KBV 501, 502 and 503. The Dash 8 Maritime Surveillance Aircraft were modified by Field Aviation in [Toronto](64646.md), Canada. The Coast Guard Aviation Home base is [Skavsta Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skavsta_Airport) in [Nyköping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyk%C3%B6ping). Surveillance and reconnaissance flights are conducted along the Swedish coast and [Vänern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4nern) year-round, day and night. Additional regular international assignments are also flown as needed.[^cite_note-9]
