**Nyala Airport** is an airport in [Nyala, South Darfur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyala,_South_Darfur), [Sudan](27421.md) ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **UYL**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **HSNN**). [United Nations](31769.md) [peacekeeping](84617.md) force [UNAMID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNAMID) uses a separated terminal in Nyala Airport.

## History

In 2009, the airport connected Nyala with [Khartoum](17320.md), [El Fasher](3956787.md), [El Geneina](1501534.md), and [El Obeid](1481380.md).[^cite_note-1] On 21 September 2018, the first cargo plane landed in the airport.[^cite_note-2]

In 2023, the airport was controlled by the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) (RSF) during the [Sudanese Civil War](73556699.md). On 24 September 2024, the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) (SAF) launched airstrikes on the airport with heavy damaged reported on its runway, control tower, and offices.[^cite_note-3]

A cargo [Boeing 737-290C Advanced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737) operated by IBM Airlines [was destroyed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_IBM_Airlines_Boeing_737_incident) at the airport by the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) (SAF) during the [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md) on 3 May 2025. It was destroyed due to suspicions of carrying military weapons and supplies to the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) (RSF). All 20 occupants on board the aircraft were killed.[^cite_note-CH-4][^cite_note-5] Sources disagree on if it was a bombing or a shootdown.[^cite_note-Daily_Nation-6][^cite_note-Exposed-7] A [Boeing 727](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_727) operated by an unknown airline crashed killing all 19 occupants on 23 October 2025.[^cite_note-\:0-8]

## Nyala Air Base

Nyala Airport hosts two [Sudanese Air Force](550818.md) units:[^cite_note-12]

- Helicopter Squadrons ([Mil Mi-8](957233.md), [Mil Mi-24](384591.md), [Mil Mi-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mil_Mi-35))

- Transport Squadron ([Antonov An-32](908204.md))
