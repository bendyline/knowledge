**Hengaw** ([Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language): هەنگاو) or **Hengaw Organization for Human Rights** ([Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language): *Dezgehe Mafê Mirovan a Hengawê*, ڕێکخراوی هەنگاو بۆ مافەکانی مرۆڤ, [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): سازمان حقوق بشری هنگاو) is a [non-governmental organization](46539.md) registered in [Norway](21241.md). It focuses on reporting and documenting [human rights violations in Iran](16652449.md), especially those perpetrated against its [Kurdish](3821779.md) minority.[^cite_note-\:1-1][^cite_note-2]

## History

Hengaw was founded in 2020.[^cite_note-\:1-1] Their reporting on the treatment of [Kolbars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolbar) has been used by the [United Nations](31769.md).[^cite_note-3] Since 2022, their reporting on the crackdown of protesters in [Iranian Kurdistan](61733686.md) during the [Mahsa Amini protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahsa_Amini_protests) in 2022 has been used by various international media outlets.[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

Hengaw reported the [death of Armita Geravand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_Armita_Geravand) in 2023.[^cite_note-8]

Members of Hengaw have reportedly been threatened by elements of the Iranian security apparatus while located outside of Iran, and their families inside Iran have been arrested.[^cite_note-9]

The report by Hengaw at the end of 2025 showed a significant increase in human rights-related deaths compared with 2024, with at least 1,858 executions in prisons during 2025.[^cite_note-10]
