**Naval co-operation and guidance for shipping** (**NCAGS**) is a [naval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval) doctrinal term. Naval personnel are trained to carry out NCAGS establish and provide advice for safe passage of [merchant ships](875717.md) worldwide, during times of peace, tension, crisis and war. NCAGS personnel act as a liaison between military commanders and the civil authorities. During war, the NCAGS organization may be responsible for establishing a convoy. NCAGS is used by many [NATO](21133.md) countries during exercises such as Bell Buoy. NCAGS has been an important part of the naval reserves.

Previously, the doctrinal term used was *naval control of shipping*.

NCAGS is often a joint effort between countries, and [NATO](21133.md) maintains a dedicated NCAGS presence at the NATO Shipping Centre, part of [Allied Maritime Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allied_Maritime_Command) in [Northwood](94196.md), UK. It conducts multinational exercises to strengthen ties between national navies and the civilian shipping industry.[^cite_note-1]

NCAGS exercises such as [Exercise Lucky Mariner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exercise_Lucky_Mariner?action=edit\&redlink=1) test NCAGS skills.[^cite_note-2]

NCAGS can sometimes be confused with Allied Worldwide Navigational Information System (AWNIS),[^cite_note-3] but that is just one tool that some NCAGS operators happen to be trained in.

## United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations

After 2001, the UK [Royal Navy](26061.md) created a [Naval Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Naval_Reserve)–manned UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) office in [Dubai](211583.md) to coordinate and exchange information with merchant traffic in the [Arabian Sea](2747.md) to help counter [Somali piracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_piracy).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] It acts as the primary point of contact for merchant ships to connect and liaise with military forces in the Arabian Sea, [Red Sea](26191.md) and [Persian Gulf](24761.md).&#x20;

The [Maritime Trade Information Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_Trade_Information_Centre?action=edit\&redlink=1) (MTIC) in [Portsmouth](24207.md), UK, supports UKMTO outputs.[^cite_note-HMSO1-7]

The UK Maritime Trade Operations office often releases warnings as well as reports regarding maritime activity - for example, it details instances of piracy and publicly warns nearby vessels - in its areas of responsibility. The UKMTO had over 29,000 followers on [X](9988187.md), as of June 2025.[^cite_note-8]

## NCAGS in the US Navy

In the [US Navy](20518076.md), NCAGS is a function that is staffed by the [Navy Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy_Reserve). As of 2018, it was composed of:

- NCAGS Norfolk supporting [US Fleet Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Fleet_Forces_Command)

- NCAGS San Diego supporting [US Third Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Third_Fleet)

- NCAGS Houston supporting [US Fourth Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Fourth_Fleet)

- NCAGS Chicago supporting [US Fifth Fleet](418867.md)

- NCAGS New York supporting [US Sixth Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Sixth_Fleet)

- NCAGS Kitsap supporting [US Seventh Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Seventh_Fleet)
