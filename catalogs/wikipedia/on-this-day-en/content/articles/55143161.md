**Raisa Minakhmedovna Atambayeva** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Раиса Минахмедовна Атамбаева;[^cite_note-2] born 25 June 1959) is the wife of the former [President of Kyrgyzstan](1569760.md) [Almazbek Atambayev](7837715.md) and [First Lady of Kyrgyzstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Lady_of_Kyrgyzstan) from 2011 to 2017. She is an ethnic Tatar and was born in the [Tatar Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatar_Autonomous_Soviet_Socialist_Republic). She is a doctor.[^cite_note-3]

## Early life and career

She was born on 25 June 1959 in the [Urals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urals) of the [Russian SFSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_SFSR). During the Soviet era, she moved with her parents to [Osh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osh), [Kyrgyz SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_SSR). Here she graduated from the secondary school named after Lomonosov, where she learned [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) and [Kyrgyz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_language). Atambaeva is a candidate of medical sciences.[^cite_note-4]

## Personal life



Raisa with her husband Almazbek Atambayev and Russian president Vladimir Putin in the Kremlin in 2017.

She is married to Almazbek Atambayev. She has 2 children with Atambayev, a boy Khadyrbek and a girl Aliya.[^cite_note-5]
