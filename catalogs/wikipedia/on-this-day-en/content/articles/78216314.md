**Majdal Zoun** or **Majdalzoun** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مجدل زون) is a municipality in the [Tyre district](11166788.md) of [Southern Lebanon](4205968.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

The total population of the town is about 4500, who work predominantly in agriculture. The municipality was established in 2004.

In 2026 the village was mostly destroyed in a large explosion detonated by the [Israeli Defense Forces](39237.md).[^cite_note-lOrientLeJourJune26-3][^cite_note-greensouthernersSatImgJune26-4]

## History

### Ottoman era

In 1596, it was named as a village, *Majdal Zurayd*, in the [Ottoman](22278.md) *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya)* (subdistrict) of [Tibnin](6787186.md) under the *[liwa'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liwa_\(Arabic\))* (district) of [Safad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safad), with a population of 5 households, all [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim). The villagers paid a fixed tax-rate of 25% on agricultural products, such as [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), fruit trees, goats & beehives; in addition to occasional revenues and a tax on winter pastures; a total of 3,100 [akçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e).[^cite_note-Hutteroth-5][^cite_note-6]

In 1881, the village was described under the name El Mejdel ("The watch-tower"[^cite_note-7]) in the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s *[Survey of Western Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine)* (SWP) as "A large and conspicuous village, containing about 400 Moslems ; it is situated on a hill, with figs, olives, and arable land. The water from *birket*; there are two [cisterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cistern) in village." [^cite_note-8]

### Modern era

Situated near the [Israeli–Lebanese](987817.md) border,[^cite_note-9] the town has been the target of numerous Israeli airstrikes throughout [the conflict](75005425.md) since 2023.[^cite_note-10] The Israel Defense Forces killed eight people in an air raid on the village on April 28.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] In June 2026, Israeli forces claimed to have discovered an [underground tunnel in the village used by Hezbollah](78024047.md) for weapon depots.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] On June 29, the Israel Defense Forces detonated explosives in Majdal Zoun, causing extensive destruction and reportedly splitting the village in two.[^cite_note-lOrientLeJourJune26-3][^cite_note-greensouthernersSatImgJune26-4]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.96% of registered voters in Majdal Zoun. 99.53% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-15]
