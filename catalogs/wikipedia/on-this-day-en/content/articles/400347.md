**Palestinian refugee camps** were first established to accommodate [Palestinians](23267.md) who were displaced by the [1948 Palestinian expulsion and flight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Palestinian_expulsion_and_flight) during the [1948 Palestine war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Palestine_war).[^cite_note-1] Camps were established by the [United Nations Relief and Works Agency](32270.md) (UNRWA) in [Jordan](7515964.md), [Lebanon](17771.md), [Syria](7515849.md), the [West Bank](33209.md) and the [Gaza Strip](12047.md). A subsequent wave of Palestinian refugees were created in the [Naksa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naksa) after the [Six-Day War](29328.md) in 1967.[^cite_note-2]

There are 68 Palestinian refugee camps in total, 58 official and 10 unofficial,[^cite_note-UNRWA2019-3] ten of which were established after the Six-Day War while the others were established in 1948 to 1950s.

Only a third of registered Palestinian refugees live within the boundaries of the refugee camps.[^cite_note-UNWRA2004table3-4] Most have integrated socially and economically outside the camps.[^cite_note-Hanafi-5] Many live in adjacent geographic areas.[^cite_note-6]

The number of registered Palestine refugees grew from 750,000 in 1950 to around 5 million in 2013.[^cite_note-WhoWeAre-7]

### Role of UNRWA

UNRWA's mandate is to provide assistance to Palestinian refugees, including access to its refugee camps. For this purpose, it defines Palestinian refugees as "persons whose normal place of residence was Palestine during the period 1 June 1946 to 15 May 1948, and who lost both home and means of livelihood as a result of the 1948 conflict."[^cite_note-WhoWeAre-7]

UNRWA also extends assistance to the patrilineal descendants of such refugees, as well as their legally adopted children.[^cite_note-WhoWeAre-7]



[Shu'fat Camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shu'fat_Camp) in the city of [Jerusalem](16043.md), 2023. The official area of the camp is in the center of the image and is characterized by low-rise buildings; on either side of the camp are additional Palestinian neighborhoods located beyond the [Israeli West Bank barrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_West_Bank_barrier) (visible at the bottom of the image), where the buildings are taller.



[Nabatieh refugee camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabatieh_refugee_camp) before and after its destruction by the Israeli military in 1974

For a camp to be recognized by UNRWA, there must be an agreement between the host government and UNRWA governing use of the camp. UNRWA does not itself run any camps, has no police powers or administrative role, but simply provides services to the camp. UNRWA recognizes facilities in 58 designated [refugee camps](438046.md) in [Jordan](7515964.md), [Lebanon](17771.md), [Syria](7515849.md), the [West Bank](33209.md) and the [Gaza Strip](12047.md), and it also provides facilities in other areas where large numbers of registered Palestine refugees live outside of recognized camps. UNRWA also provided relief to Jewish [displaced persons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Present_absentee) inside [Israel](9282173.md) following the 1948 conflict until the Israeli government took over responsibility for them in 1952. Refugee camps developed from tented cities to rows of concrete blockhouses to urban ghettos indistinguishable from their surroundings (effectively becoming urban developments within existing cities or by themselves), that house around one third of all registered Palestine refugees.

The [Funding for UNRWA](32270.md#Funding) activities comes almost entirely from voluntary contributions from UN member states. UNRWA also receives some funding from the Regular Budget of the United Nations, which is used mostly for international staffing costs.[^cite_note-WhoWeAre-7]

## List of camps

The camps are divided between five regions:

- Gaza Strip: The [Gaza Strip](12047.md) has eight official and no unofficial refugee camps,

  [^cite_note-UNRWA2019-3]

  &#x20;and 1,221,110 registered refugees.

- West Bank: The [West Bank](33209.md) has 19 official and four unofficial refugee camps,

  [^cite_note-UNRWA2019-3]

  &#x20;and 741,409 registered refugees.

- Syria: [Syria](7515849.md) has nine official refugee camps and three unofficial refugee camps,

  [^cite_note-UNRWA2019-3]

  &#x20;and 499,189 registered refugees.

- Lebanon: There are 12 official refugee and no unofficial camps in [Lebanon](17771.md),

  [^cite_note-UNRWA2019-3]

  &#x20;and 448,599 registered refugees.

- Jordan: There are 10 official and three unofficial refugee camps in [Jordan](7515964.md),

  [^cite_note-UNRWA2019-3]

  &#x20;and 2,034,641 registered refugees.
