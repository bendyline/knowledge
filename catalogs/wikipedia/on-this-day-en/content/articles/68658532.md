# Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Abbreviation | CREA |
| Type | [Independent Research Organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_institute) |
| Website | [energyandcleanair.org](http://energyandcleanair.org/) |

The **Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air** (**CREA**) is a [nonprofit](72487.md) [think tank](37101.md) researching [energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy) and [air pollution](10934212.md).[^cite_note-1] CREA was founded in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki) in 2019 with the goal of tracking the impacts of air pollution by providing data-backed research products. [^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Research and publications

In July 2025, [BBC News](1139893.md) reported on work done by the CREA. Lauri Myllyvirta, co-founder of the organisation, found that [China's carbon emissions](61886740.md) declined in the 12 months up to May 2025, even as demand for new power generation grew rapidly, marking a potential milestone in addressing [climate change](5042951.md).[^cite_note-4]
