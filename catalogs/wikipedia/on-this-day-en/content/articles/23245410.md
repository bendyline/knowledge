The **Academy Award for Best Actor** is an award presented annually by the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences) (AMPAS). It has been awarded since the [1st Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Academy_Awards) to an actor who has delivered an outstanding performance in a [leading role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_actor) in a film released that year. The award was traditionally presented by the previous year's [Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress) winner. However, in recent years, it has shifted towards being presented by previous years' Best Actor winners instead.

The Best Actor award has been presented 98 times, to 87 actors. The first winner was [German actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_German_Academy_Award_winners_and_nominees) [Emil Jannings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emil_Jannings) for his roles in *[The Last Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Command_\(1928_film\))* (1928) and *[The Way of All Flesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Way_of_All_Flesh_\(1927_film\))* (1927).[^cite_note-1] The most recent winner is [Michael B. Jordan](2310146.md) for his [dual role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_role) as twin brothers Elijah and Elias Moore in *[Sinners](75895455.md)* (2025). The record for [most wins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_actors_with_two_or_more_Academy_Awards_in_acting_categories) is three, held by [Daniel Day-Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Day-Lewis), and ten other actors have won twice. The record for [most nominations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_actors_with_three_or_more_Academy_Award_nominations_in_acting_categories) is nine, held jointly by [Spencer Tracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spencer_Tracy) and [Laurence Olivier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier). At the [5th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_Academy_Awards) in 1932, [Fredric March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredric_March) finished one vote ahead of [Wallace Beery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallace_Beery); under the rules of the time this resulted in them sharing the award, the only time this has occurred.

## Nominations process

Nominees are currently determined by [single transferable vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single_transferable_vote) within the actors branch of AMPAS; winners are selected by a [plurality vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurality_\(voting\)) from the entire [eligible voting members](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_industry) of the Academy.[^cite_note-2]

In the first three years of the awards, actors and actresses were nominated as the best individuals in their categories. At that time, all of their work during the qualifying period (as many as three films, in some cases) was listed after the award.[^cite_note-Oscar_history-3] Despite this, at the [3rd Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_Academy_Awards), held in 1930, only one film was cited in each winner's award regardless of how many they were eligible to be considered for during that span.[^cite_note-4] The current system, in which an actor is nominated for a specific performance in a single film, was introduced for the [4th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4th_Academy_Awards).[^cite_note-Oscar_history-3] Starting with the [9th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_Academy_Awards), held in 1937, the category was limited to a maximum five nominations per year.[^cite_note-Oscar_history-3]

## Notes

**A**__[^](#ref_Jannings)__: According to longstanding Hollywood legend,

[^cite_note-110]

&#x20;reported by [Susan Orlean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Orlean),

[^cite_note-111]

&#x20;[Rin Tin Tin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rin_Tin_Tin) actually received the most Best Actor votes, but the Academy (not wishing to give the first award to a dog) refactored the votes to ensure that Jannings won.

[^cite_note-112]

**B**__[^](#ref_Chaplin)__:*[The Circus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Circus_\(1928_film\))* originally received three nominations: Best Director (Comedy Picture), Best Actor, and Best Writing (Original Story) – for [Charles Chaplin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Chaplin). However, the [Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences) subsequently decided to remove Chaplin's name from the competitive award categories and instead to confer upon him a [Special Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Honorary_Award) "for acting, writing, directing and producing *The Circus*".

[^cite_note-chilton-8]

**C**[**<sup>1</sup>**](#ref_Arliss1) [**<sup>2</sup>**](#ref_Arliss2) [**<sup>3</sup>**](#ref_Arliss3) : Rules at the time of the first three ceremonies allowed for a performer to receive a single nomination which could honor his or her work in more than one film. [George Arliss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Arliss), [Maurice Chevalier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Chevalier), and [Ronald Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Colman) were all nominated for two different roles in the same category. Current Academy rules forbid this from happening. No official reason was ever given as to why Arliss won the award for only one of the two films he was listed for.

[^cite_note-Variety_Double_Oscar_Nominations-113]

**D**[**<sup>1</sup>**](#ref_Tie1) [**<sup>2</sup>**](#ref_Tie2) : [Fredric March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredric_March) received one more vote than [Wallace Beery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallace_Beery). Academy rules at that time considered such a close margin to be a tie, so both March and Beery received the award. Currently, Academy rules stipulate that a tie must result from exactly the same number of votes.

[^cite_note-Oscar_tie-114]

**E**__[^](#ref_Muni)__: As in the previous year when the Academy relaxed the rules to allow write-in votes following the outcry over [Bette Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bette_Davis)'s snub for *[Of Human Bondage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Of_Human_Bondage_\(1934_film\))*, the Academy permitted write-in votes for that year as well. Thus, [Paul Muni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Muni) received a write-in nomination for his performance in *[Black Fury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Fury_\(film\))* and actually finished second in the votes. Although as with Davis the previous year, the Academy did not recognize these two as "official nominees", they are nevertheless listed on the official website amongst their respective years' nominations for posterity's sake.

[^cite_note-115]

**F**__[^](#ref_Fitzgerald)__: Due to category confusion, [Barry Fitzgerald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Fitzgerald) received nominations (each for the same performance as Father Fitzgibbon in *[Going My Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Going_My_Way)*) in both the leading and [supporting actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor#1940s) categories for 1944, winning the Oscar for the latter. As a result of this confusion, the Academy amended its rules so that if any actor or actress received enough votes to qualify as one of the final five nominees for both again, the performer would only receive the nomination for the category in which he or she obtained the larger percentage of the votes.

[^cite_note-116]
