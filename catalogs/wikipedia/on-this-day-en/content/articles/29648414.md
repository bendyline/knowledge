# List of Asian Games records in swimming

&#x20;The fastest times in the swimming events at the [Asian Games](39205.md) are designated as the **Asian Games records in swimming**. The events are held in a long-course (50 m) pool. The most recent edition was held in [Tokyo](30057.md), [Japan](15573.md), in 2026.

All records were set in finals, unless noted otherwise.
