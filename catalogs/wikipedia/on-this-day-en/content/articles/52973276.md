# Livestreamed crime

Not to be confused with [List of filmed mass shootings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_filmed_mass_shootings).

&#x20;**Livestreamed crime** is a phenomenon in which criminal acts are publicly [livestreamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livestream) on social media platforms such as [Twitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitch_\(service\)) or [Facebook Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook_Live).

Due to the fact that livestreams are accessible instantaneously, it is difficult to quickly detect and moderate violent content, and almost impossible to protect the [privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privacy) of victims or bystanders.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Livestreaming crime allows anyone from the public to become a distant witness.[^cite_note-5]

"[Trash streaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trash_stream)" is a YouTube [subculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subculture) in which livestreamers solicit donations in exchange for carrying out [dares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_dares) on-stream, which are often abusive or criminal.[^cite_note-6]

## History

In April 2016, Marina Lonina, age 18; and Raymond Gates, age 29, were arrested in [Ohio](22199.md), US, on charges that Gates [raped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raped) an underage friend of Lonina's while Lonina live streamed the crime on [Periscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Periscope_\(app\)).[^cite_note-McPhteLive-7][^cite_note-PeriscopeRape-8] The prosecutor pointed out that Lonina, who was taken advantage of by a much older man, had become "caught up" in her excitement over the number of "[likes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Like_button)" she was getting, and is shown on screen "laughing and giggling".[^cite_note-McPhteLive-7] Joss Wright of the [Oxford Internet Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Internet_Institute) pointed out that, given the "volume of content being created and uploaded every day, \[there] is almost no practical way to prevent content like this being uploaded and shared".[^cite_note-PeriscopeRape-8]

By May, *[The New York Times](30680.md)* was including the Periscope rape as one of a series of recent cases in which crimes were live streamed. These included one in which a young woman in [Égly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89gly), France, spoke via Periscope about her distress and suicidal thoughts and was apparently encouraged by viewers to kill herself, which she did by throwing herself under a train. Also included was the case of two teenagers who live streamed themselves bragging and laughing as they beat up a drunken man in a bar in [Bordeaux](4097.md), France.[^cite_note-Blaise-9]

## Types

### Cybercrimes

Main article: [Cybersex trafficking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cybersex_trafficking)

Cybersex trafficking, also referred to as live streaming sexual abuse,[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] involves sex trafficking and the [live streaming](18996908.md) of coerced sexual abuse or [rape](7980471.md).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-IJMUK-14][^cite_note-15] Victims are abducted, threatened, or deceived and transferred to "cybersex dens".[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] The dens can be in any location where the cybersex traffickers have a computer, tablet, or phone with [Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet) connection.[^cite_note-IJMUK-14] Perpetrators use [social media](5897742.md) networks, [videoconferencing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Videoconference), [pornographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_pornography) video sharing websites, [dating pages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_dating_service), [chat rooms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chat_room), apps, [dark web](40712897.md) sites,[^cite_note-Almendral-19] and other online platforms.[^cite_note-20] [Online payment systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-commerce_payment_system)[^cite_note-Almendral-19][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] and [cryptocurrencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrencies) are often used for anonymity.[^cite_note-23] Millions of reports of cybersex trafficking are sent to authorities annually.[^cite_note-24] Local authorities in Cambodia have expressed concern that new laws and police procedures are necessary to combat this type of [cybercrime](341598.md).[^cite_note-25]

### War crimes

Main article: [War crime](48626.md)

A war crime is the act of breaking the laws of war. Through combination of the fact that war crime data suffers from considerable underreporting[^cite_note-26], general technological innovation which has made video recording and streaming easier, and coincidental occurrence of the [Arab Spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Spring) and [Russo-Ukrainian war](42085878.md), there have been more instances of people or groups live-streaming such acts to instill fear into the public. Content is most commonly disseminated through social media[^cite_note-27], with examples including the [Terrorgram](72327036.md) phenomena, [White jihad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_jihad),[^cite_note-28] and the [Islamic State](9087364.md)'s media wing [Al-Hayat Media Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hayat_Media_Center).
