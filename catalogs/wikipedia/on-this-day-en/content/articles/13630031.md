**Coahuayana** (formally: **Coahuayana de Hidalgo**) is a city in the [Mexican](3966054.md) [state](8196151.md) of [Michoacán](195805.md). It serves as the municipal seat for the surrounding municipality of the same name, which is located on the Pacific Ocean coast and is the westernmost municipality in Michoacán.&#x20;

The municipality has an area of 362.34 square kilometres (0.61% of the surface of the state)[^cite_note-Michoacán-1] and is bordered to the north by the municipality of [Chinicuila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinicuila), to the south by [Aquila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquila,_Michoac%C3%A1n) and the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md), and to the west and northwest by the state of [Colima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colima). The municipality reported a population of 17,022 in the [2020 INEGI census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_INEGI_census),[^cite_note-2] up from 11,632 in 2005.[^cite_note-3]

Coahuayana is a word of [Nahuatl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahuatl) origin that means "Place Where Trees and [Squash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cucurbita_maxima) are Abundant".[^cite_note-Michoacán-1]
