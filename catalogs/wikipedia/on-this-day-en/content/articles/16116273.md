# Grammy Award milestones

&#x20;Throughout the history of the [Grammy Awards](44635.md), many significant records have been set. This page only includes the competitive awards which have been won by various artists. This does not include the various special awards that are presented by the [National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Recording_Arts_and_Sciences) such as Lifetime Achievement Awards, Trustees Awards, Technical Awards or Legend Awards. The page however does include other non-performance related Grammys (known as the Craft & Production Fields) that may have been presented to the artist(s).

## Awards

### Most Grammys won



[Beyoncé](83688.md) has won a total of 35 Grammy Awards

The record for the most Grammy Awards won in a lifetime is currently held by American singer, songwriter and record producer [Beyoncé](83688.md), who has won 35. ### Youngest artist to win Song of the Year



[Lorde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorde) became the youngest Song of the Year winner in 2014.

At 17 years of age, [Lorde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorde) became the youngest artist to win [Song of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year) when she won for "[Royals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royals_\(Lorde_song\))" in [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/56th_Annual_Grammy_Awards).

### Youngest artist to win Best New Artist

### Artists who have won all four General Field awards



[Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele) is one of four artists who have won all four general field awards.

There have been only four musicians who have won all four General Field awards: [Album of the Year](196513.md), [Record of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Record_of_the_Year), [Song of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year), and [Best New Artist](198014.md).

In 1981, [Christopher Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Cross) became the first artist to win all four awards, as well as the first act to win them all in a single year.[^cite_note-30]

In 2009, [Adele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele) won Best New Artist, and she earned the three other awards in both 2012 and 2017. She was the second artist to win all four accolades throughout her career.[^cite_note-31]

In 2020, [Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish) became the third musician to win all four awards and the first female artist to win them during a single ceremony.[^cite_note-32]

In 2026, [Jack Antonoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Antonoff) has become the fourth musician to win all four awards, first winning [Best New Artist](198014.md) and [Song of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year) in 2013, winning [Album of the Year](196513.md) thrice as a producer in 2016, 2021 and 2024; and winning [Record of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Record_of_the_Year) as a Producer in 2026.

### Most Grammys won by a record producer in one night



&#x20;[Quincy Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Jones) won six Grammys in 1991, setting the record for most Grammys won by a producer in one night.

The record for most awards won by a producer in one night is six. The record was set by [Quincy Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Jones) who won six awards in [1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/33rd_Annual_Grammy_Awards), including [Album of the Year](196513.md), [Best Arrangement On An Instrumental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Arrangement,_Instrumental_or_A_Cappella), [Best Instrumental Arrangement Accompanying Vocal(s)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Arrangement,_Instrumental_and_Vocals), [Best Jazz Fusion Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Jazz_Fusion_Performance), [Best Rap Performance By A Duo Or Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Rap_Performance_by_a_Duo_or_Group), as well as [Producer of the Year, Non-Classical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Producer_of_the_Year,_Non-Classical) for his own studio album *[Back on the Block](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_on_the_Block)*.[^cite_note-36]

[Finneas O'Connell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finneas_O'Connell) tied the record in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/62nd_Annual_Grammy_Awards), winning [Producer of the Year, Non-Classical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Producer_of_the_Year,_Non-Classical) and five additional awards, including [Record of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Record_of_the_Year), [Album of the Year](196513.md), [Song of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year), [Best Engineered Album, Non-Classical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Engineered_Album,_Non-Classical), and [Best Pop Vocal Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Vocal_Album) for his contribution on [Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish)'s *[When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/When_We_All_Fall_Asleep,_Where_Do_We_Go%3F)*.[^cite_note-37]

### Artists who had been nominated for all four General Field awards in one night

Only fifteen artists have been nominated for all four General Field awards in one night. [Lizzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizzo) is the oldest person to be nominated for all four awards in one night, at 31 years old; while the youngest person to be nominated is [Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish) at 17 years old. Both were nominated in 2020, making it the first time that two artists were nominated for all four awards in one night. This occurred for the second time in 2025, with [Sabrina Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabrina_Carpenter) and [Chappell Roan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chappell_Roan). In 1968, [Bobbie Gentry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobbie_Gentry) became the first person to be nominated for all four awards, followed by [Christopher Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Cross) in 1981 and [Fun.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fun_\(band\)) in 2013, becoming the first male artist and first group to be nominated, respectively. In addition, [Finneas O'Connell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finneas_O'Connell) was nominated for all four General Field awards in 2022, but he was not credited as a performing artist in three of the four categories. Cross and Eilish went onto win all four general field awards on their respective nights, the only two artists to ever to do so to date.
