**Penco** ([Mapudungun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapudungun): See (*Pen*), Water (*Ko*)), is a [Chilean](5489.md) [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Chile) and [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Chile) in [Concepción Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concepci%C3%B3n_Province,_Chile), [Bío Bío Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%ADo_B%C3%ADo_Region) on the [Bay of Concepción](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Concepci%C3%B3n). Founded as the city of **Concepción del Nuevo Extremo** (lit. 'Beginning of the New Extreme') on February 12, 1550, by [Pedro de Valdivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_de_Valdivia), it is the third oldest city in Chile, after capital [Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago,_Chile) founded first in 1541 and [La Serena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Serena,_Chile) second in 1544.

As there may be confusion between the [demonyms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demonyms) of the inhabitants of Concepción and Penco, due to the previous location of Concepción, inhabitants of that city are called *penquistas* while inhabitants of Penco are known as *pencones*.

The commune is since 2016 the site of a project to [mine rare-earth elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rare-earth_resources_in_Chile) by Aclara Resources, a publicly traded company with a 55.9% and 10.1% ownership of [Hochschild Mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hochschild_Mining) and [CAP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAP_S.A.) respectively.[^cite_note-ltree-4]

## History

In previous centuries, in the current location of Penco, was the first location where the city of [Concepción](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concepci%C3%B3n,_Chile) was established, which is now the capital of the Bíobío Region. It was destroyed by [Lautaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lautaro_\(toqui\)) in 1554, and rebuilt and destroyed again by Lautaro in 1555.

- It was reestablished in 1557 during the governorship of marquess [García Hurtado de Mendoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garc%C3%ADa_Hurtado_de_Mendoza,_5th_Marquis_of_Ca%C3%B1ete) when he landed there and built a fort on the Alto de Pinto. The city was reestablished January 6, 1558, by capitán Jerónimo de Villegas.

- It became the headquarters of the military forces engaged against the native [Mapuche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapuche) in [Araucanía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Araucan%C3%ADa_\(historic_region\)) over the next two centuries, growing to a population of 10,000 despite suffering a [siege](26905.md) in 1564 and other attacks by Mapuche tribals.

- In 1603 the last bishop of the suppressed [Diocese of La Imperial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_La_Imperial) was transferred there as first bishop of the successor see [Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Concepción](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Concepci%C3%B3n).

- Due to [earthquakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthquakes) and [tsunamis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsunamis), which razed the city in [1570](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1570_Concepci%C3%B3n_earthquake), 1657, 1687, 1730 and another on [May 25, 1751](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1751_Concepci%C3%B3n_earthquake), the colonial authorities ultimately decided to move the city to its current location to the [Valle de la Mocha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valle_de_la_Mocha), alongside the [Bío Bío River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%ADo_B%C3%ADo_River) and prohibited the occupation of the old location, which remained unpopulated until March 29, 1842, when the present city of Penco was founded.

- In 2026 Penco would be evacuated due to the outbreak of the [Trinitarias Fire](82148461.md), which damaged large parts of the city.

## Statistics

According to the 2002 [census](6889.md) of the [National Statistics Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Statistics_Institute_\(Chile\)), Penco spans an area of 107.6 km<sup>2</sup> (42 sq mi) and has 46,016 inhabitants (22,366 men and 23,650 women). Of these, 45,361 (98.6%) lived in [urban areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_area) and 655 (1.4%) in [rural areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural_area). The population grew by 14% (5,657 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.[^cite_note-INE-3]

## Administration

As a commune, Penco is a third-level [administrative division of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_division_of_Chile) administered by a [municipal council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_council), headed by an [alcalde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcalde) who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Víctor Hugo Figueroa [Víctor_Hugo_Figueroa_Rebolledo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%ADctor_Hugo_Figueroa_Rebolledo?action=edit\&redlink=1) (ILE).[^cite_note-Munitel-1][^cite_note-Official-2]

Within the [electoral divisions of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_divisions_of_Chile), Penco is represented in the [Chamber of Deputies](716259.md) by Sergio Bobadilla ([UDI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Democratic_Union)) and Clemira Pacheco ([PS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Party_of_Chile)) as part of the 45th electoral district, (together with [Tomé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom%C3%A9), [Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida,_Chile), [Hualqui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hualqui), [Coronel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronel,_Chile) and [Santa Juana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Juana,_Chile)). The commune is represented in the [Senate](683411.md) by Alejandro Navarro Brain ([MAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broad_Social_Movement)) and Hosain Sabag Castillo ([PDC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Democratic_Party)) as part of the 12th senatorial constituency (Biobío-Cordillera).
