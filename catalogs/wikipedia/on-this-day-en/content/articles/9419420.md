**Abha International Airport** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مطار أبها الدولي, [IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **AHB[^cite_note-4]**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **OEAB**) is an airport in [Abha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abha), the capital of [Asir Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asir_Province) in [Saudi Arabia](349303.md).

The site was a new construction area between [Khamis Mushait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khamis_Mushait) and Abha, and serves both cities.

## History

Construction of the airport started in mid-1975 by Laing Wimpey Alireza. Earlier, domestic flights had been handled at the military airport near Khamis Mushait. The airport became operational in 1977.[^cite_note-5] The construction was carried out by Bin Trif Airport ltd.[^cite_note-6]

## Facilities

The airport is located at an [elevation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elevation) of 6,858 feet (2,090 m) above [mean sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_sea_level). It has one [runway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway) designated 13/31 with an [asphalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphalt_concrete) surface measuring 3,350 by 45 metres (10,991 ft × 148 ft).[^cite_note-Airport_information_for_OEAB_from_DAFIF-2]

## Houthi attacks

The airport has been frequently targeted in missile or drone attacks by Houthi forces during the [Yemeni civil war](46215853.md).

It was initially [attacked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abha_International_Airport_attacks) by the [Yemeni](350939.md) [Houthi movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_movement) on 12 June 2019, injuring 26 people.[^cite_note-16]

On 23 June 2019, the Houthis launched another attack on the airport, leaving a [Syrian](7515849.md) national dead and 21 injured.[^cite_note-17]

The airport was attacked again by missiles on 2 July 2019, leaving nine injured.[^cite_note-18]

Another attack against the airport by Houthi forces occurred on 31 August 2020, when a bomb-laden drone was detected flying to it and intercepted, along with a remotely controlled boat off the [Red Sea](26191.md), which was also filled with explosives and ready for an attack.[^cite_note-19]

On 10 February 2021, [Houthis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_movement) used four drones to carry out a [drone strike](38212231.md) on the airport, damaging a civilian aircraft (a [Flyadeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flyadeal) Airbus A320)[^cite_note-20] and starting a fire. A Houthi spokesman said the attack was in response to [coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabian-led_intervention_in_Yemen) [airstrikes](323167.md) and other actions in [Yemen](350939.md).[^cite_note-21]

On 31 August 2021, the airport was again attacked by Houthi drones, leaving 8 wounded.[^cite_note-22]

On 13 July 2026, the airport was attacked once again by Houthi drones, no casualties reported.[^cite_note-23]
