The **2026 Laurence Olivier Awards** were held on 12 April 2026 at the [Royal Albert Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Albert_Hall). It recognised excellence in professional [theatre in London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_theatre), as determined by the [Society of London Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_London_Theatre), and marked the 50th anniversary of the [Laurence Olivier Awards](16432264.md). British comedian and actor [Nick Mohammed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Mohammed) hosted the ceremony for the first time. The nominations were announced on 5 March 2026, with *[Into the Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Into_the_Woods)* and *[Paddington](75546569.md)* each receiving the most nominations with eleven.[^cite_note-1] *Paddington* received the most awards with seven, tying for the record number of Olivier awards won by a musical with *[Sunset Boulevard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunset_Boulevard_\(musical\))* (2024), *[Cabaret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabaret_\(musical\))* (2022), *[Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_\(musical\))* (2018) and *[Matilda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matilda_the_Musical)* (2012).[^cite_note-2]

## Winners and nominees

The nominations were announced in 24 categories on 5 March 2026. The ceremony were held at the [Royal Albert Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Albert_Hall) in [London](17867.md) on April 12, 2026, hosted by [Nick Mohammed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Mohammed).[^cite_note-oa16-9] [James Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Graham_\(playwright\))'s *[Punch](79514308.md)* won two prizes including [Best New Play](16726050.md) and [Laurence Olivier Award for Best Actress in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Supporting_Role) to [Julie Hesmondhalgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_Hesmondhalgh) as Joan.[^cite_note-oa14-10][^cite_note-oa17-11] *[Paddington](75546569.md)* won seven prizes out of 11 nominations, including [Best New Musical](15236605.md), [Best Actor in a Musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Musical) for James Hameed and Arti Shah.[^cite_note-oa12-12] [Rachel Zegler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Zegler) won the [Best Actress in a Musical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Musical) as [Eva Perón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Per%C3%B3n) in *[Evita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evita_\(musical\))*.[^cite_note-oa12-12] Its choreographer, Fabian Aloise, also won the [Best Theatre Choreographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Theatre_Choreographer). *Evita* was also nominated for [Best Musical Revival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Musical_Revival), but the award went to *[Into the Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Into_the_Woods)*, which had 11 nominations and also won [Best Lighting Design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Lighting_Design) to Roland Horvath and Aideen Malone.[^cite_note-oa19-13][^cite_note-oa20-14] *[All My Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_My_Sons)* won two prizes including [Best Revival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Revival) and [Best Actor in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Supporting_Role) to [Paapa Essiedu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paapa_Essiedu) as Chris Keller.[^cite_note-oa19-13][^cite_note-oa15-15][^cite_note-oa18-16]
