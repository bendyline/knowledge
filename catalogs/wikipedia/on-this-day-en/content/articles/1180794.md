**Faridabad** (Hindi: [\[fəˈɾiːˌd̪aːbaːd̪\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hindi_and_Urdu)) is the most populous [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Haryana_by_population) in the [Indian](14533.md) state of [Haryana](14189.md) and is a part of [Delhi National Capital Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Capital_Region_\(India\)).[^cite_note-TribuneIndia27Dec2005-8] It is one of the major satellite cities around Delhi and is located 284 kilometres south of the state capital, [Chandigarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandigarh). The river [Yamuna](354286.md) forms the eastern district boundary with Uttar Pradesh. The [Government of India](553883.md) included it in the second list of [Smart Cities Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_Cities_Mission) on 24 May 2016.[^cite_note-Smrt1-9] As per the 2021 Delhi Regional Plan, Faridabad is a part of the Central National Capital Region or Delhi metropolitan area.[^cite_note-10]

The newly developed residential and industrial part of Faridabad (Sec. 66 to 89) between the [Agra Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra_Canal) and the Yamuna River is commonly referred to as [Greater Faridabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Faridabad) (also known as [Neharpar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neharpar)). The area is being developed as a self-sustained sub-city with wide roads, tall buildings, malls, educational institutions, and health and commercial centers. Sectors 66 to 74 are Industrial Sectors, while Sectors 75 to 89 are Residential Sectors.[^cite_note-11]

Faridabad is a major industrial hub of Haryana.[^cite_note-TribuneIndia27Dec2005-8] 50% of the income tax collected in Haryana is from Faridabad and Gurgaon.[^cite_note-TribuneIndia27Dec2005-8] Faridabad is famous for [henna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henna) production from the agricultural sector, while tractors, motorcycles, switch gears, refrigerators, shoes, tyres and garments constitute its primary industrial products.

In 2018, Faridabad was considered by the [World Health Organization](33583.md) as the world's [second most polluted city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_polluted_cities_by_particulate_matter_concentration).[^cite_note-12] Faridabad ranked 10th in the [Swachh Survekshan Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swachh_Survekshan)'s top ten dirtiest cities in India in 2020.[^cite_note-13]

Faridabad has been selected as one of the hundred Indian cities to be developed as a [smart city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_city) under [Government of India](553883.md)'s flagship [Smart Cities Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_Cities_Mission) by [Ministry of Urban Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Urban_Development_\(India\)).

## History

See also: [Braj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braj), [Mewat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mewat), and [History of Haryana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Haryana)

The city was founded in 1607 by [Sheikh Farīd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaikh_Farid_Bukhari), treasurer to the [Mughal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mughal_Empire) emperor [Jahangir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jahangir), to protect the [Grand Trunk Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Trunk_Road) between [Delhi](37756.md) and [Agra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra), and lies in the cultural region of [Braj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braj) and [Mewat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mewat).[^cite_note-BritannicaOnline-14] In Independent India, Faridabad was initially a part of [Gurgaon district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurgaon_district) which was later made an independent district on 15 August 1979. After partition in 1947, under the leadership of [Kamaladevi Chattopadhyay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamaladevi_Chattopadhyay), over 50000 refugees were resettled in Faridabad, reviving the economy and social life of the city.[^cite_note-15]

Nearby [Tilpat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilpat) (then "Tilprastha") was one of the five villages demanded by [Pandavas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandavas) to avert a disastrous war.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

Faridabad has a borderline [hot semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-arid_climate#Hot_semi-arid_climates) [*BSh*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification#BSh) just short of a dry winter [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) (*Cwa*). The city features the three typical Indian seasons – The "hot" or pre-monsoon season lasts from late March to mid-July and is typified by sweltering and arid conditions that begin very dry but later turn humid. The "wet" or [monsoon](31096097.md) season is sweltering and often dangerously humid with frequent but erratic heavy rainfall. Following the retreat of the monsoon is the "cool" or winter season with warm and sunny weather producing by far the most comfortable conditions.

According to the World Air Quality Report 2024, Faridabad is one of the world's 20 most polluted city in India.[^cite_note-19]

### Municipal finance

According to financial data published on the CityFinance Portal of the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs,[^cite_note-31] the Municipal Corporation Faridabad reported total revenue receipts of ₹390 crore (US\$47 million) and total expenditure of ₹413 crore (US\$50 million) in 2022–23. ## Economy

The Faridabad Small Industries Association claims that Faridabad and Gurgaon districts account for almost 56% of the income tax collected in Haryana.[^cite_note-TribuneIndia27Dec2005-8] Faridabad has been selected as one of the hundred Indian cities to be developed as a [smart city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_city) under [PM Narendra Modi's](444222.md) flagship [Smart Cities Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_Cities_Mission).[^cite_note-32] It has started growing as another technology hub in Haryana nurturing startups and innovation.[^cite_note-33]

Faridabad is the largest exporter of [henna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henna) (Mehandi) in India. According to the Faridabad Henna Manufacturing Association, the sale of henna from Faridabad is worth an estimated ₹250 crore (US\$26 million)–₹300 crore (US\$31 million) annually.[^cite_note-34]

Many directorates of different union government ministries are headquartered in Faridabad including Central Ground Water Board, Department of Plant Quarantine and Central Insecticide Lab,[^cite_note-35] and Union Government Offices from Haryana including the Commissioner of Central Excise within Department of Revenue, Government of India, Department of Explosives, and Department of Labour. Institute such as National Council for Cement and Building Materials, a research and development institute under the ministry of commerce, and industry has its head office in Faridabad.[^cite_note-36]

The Apex Central Training Institute of the Department of Revenue, Government of India, National Academy of Customs Excise & Narcotics is located at Sector 29. The [National Power Training Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Power_Training_Institute?action=edit\&redlink=1), an autonomous body under [Ministry of Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Power_\(India\)), [Government of India](553883.md) has a corporate office in Faridabad.[^cite_note-37] The city also hosts the National Institute of Financial Management, which serves as training academy for accounting and financial services.[^cite_note-38]



NHPC Corporate Office, Faridabad

Also headquartered here is [NHPC Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHPC_Limited) which is a Central PSU under the Ministry of Power, Government of India, and the largest [hydropower](14073.md) Company in India.

An office of Geological Survey of India is also located in the NIT-5 area of the city.

Faridabad is the industrial capital of Haryana. As of 2013, out of a total of 11,665 registered working factories in Haryana, 2,499 were in Faridabad which was followed by Gurgaon with 2,116 factories. According to a study, the growth of Faridabad has been declining in the last 2 decades, the share of Faridabad in investment is less than 1% and 93% of investment is in the paper industry. The industrial contribution of Faridabad to Haryana's revenue was declined from 29% to 22% in 2012–13.[^cite_note-39]

Faridabad is home to large-scale companies like [Escorts Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escorts_Group),[^cite_note-40] India [Yamaha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamaha_Motor_Company) Motor Pvt. Ltd., [Havells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Havells) India Limited,[^cite_note-41] [JCB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JCB_\(heavy_equipment_manufacturer\)), [Indian Oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Oil) (R\&D),[^cite_note-42] [Larsen & Toubro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larsen_&_Toubro) (L\&T),[^cite_note-43] [Whirlpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whirlpool_Corporation), [ABB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABB), [Goodyear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodyear_Tire_&_Rubber_Company). [Bata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bata_Shoes) and [Eicher Tractor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eicher_tractor), and Beebay Kidswear[^cite_note-44] Eyewear e-tailer [Lenskart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenskart) and healthcare startup [Lybrate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lybrate) have their headquarters in Faridabad.[^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46] More than 5,000 units of auto parts producers are based in Faridabad.[^cite_note-Amarujala-47] Lakhani Armaan Group has set up manufacturing facilities at Faridabad (Haryana). [^cite_note-Footwear_industry_in_Faridabad-48] Faridabad has experienced growth in [real estate activity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_estate), with contributions from a range of developers and property agencies.

## Transportation and connectivity

### Rail

Faridabad is on the broad gauge of the [New Delhi – Mumbai Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Delhi%E2%80%93Mumbai_main_line). New Delhi and [Hazrat Nizammudin Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazrat_Nizamuddin_railway_station) is about 25 km away from [Old Faridabad railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faridabad_railway_station). The trains for big cities like [Mumbai](19189.md), [Hyderabad](37534.md), [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai) are easily accessible from here. Local trains runs between [New Delhi](51585.md) to Faridabad.[^cite_note-mcf-49]

### Metro

[Delhi Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Metro) [Violet Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violet_Line_\(Delhi_Metro\)) connects Faridabad with [Delhi](51585.md). The extension of Violet Line to Faridabad was inaugurated by [Prime Minister](24452.md) [Narendra Modi](444222.md) on 6 September 2015.[^cite_note-50] There are 9 metro stations in Faridabad corridor of Delhi Metro which are all elevated.[^cite_note-51] Metro has been recently elongated to [Ballabhgarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballabhgarh) with the addition of two stations- [Sant Surdas Sihi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Surdas_\(Sihi\)_metro_station) and [Raja Nahar Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raja_Nahar_Singh_metro_station) [Ballabhgarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballabhgarh).

The 11 metro stations are [Sarai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarai_metro_station), [NHPC Chowk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHPC_Chowk_metro_station), [Mewala Maharajpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mewala_Maharajpur_metro_station), [Sector 28](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sector_28_metro_station), [Badkhal Mor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badkhal_Mor_metro_station), [Old Faridabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Faridabad_metro_station), [Neelam Chowk Ajronda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neelam_Chowk_Ajronda_metro_station), [Bata Chowk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bata_Chowk_metro_station), [Escorts Mujesar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escorts_Mujesar_metro_station), [Sant Surdas (Sihi)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCB_Colony_metro_station) and [Raja Nahar Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballabhgarh_metro_station).[^cite_note-52]

The Delhi Metro's [Violet Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violet_Line_\(Delhi_Metro\)) is set to extend from Raja Nahar Singh ([Ballabhgarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballabhgarh)) to [Palwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palwal), covering 24 km with about 10 stations. The Haryana government approved the project, and a techno-feasibility study is underway. Estimated at ₹4,320 crore, it aims to improve [NCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Capital_Region_\(India\)) connectivity.[^cite_note-53]

## Tourism

### Badkhal Lake



Dried Badkhal lake

Main article: [Badkhal Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badkhal_Lake)

Badkhal Lake was located in Badkhal village, 8 km from Delhi Border. The lake fringed by Aravalli hills was a man-made embankment which has now dried up.[^cite_note-67] The lake complex, spread over 40 acres, had come up in 1969.[^cite_note-68] In June 2015, Haryana government decided to revive the Badkhal lake here to once again attract tourists.[^cite_note-69]

### Surajkund Tourist Complex and International Crafts Fair

Main article: [Surajkund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surajkund)

Situated at a distance of around 8 km from South Delhi. It is a 10th-century water reservoir which believed to be built by Tomar king Surajpal.[^cite_note-Gupta1987-70][^cite_note-71] Place is known for its annual fair "Surajkund International Crafts Mela".[^cite_note-72] 2015 edition of fair was visited by 1.2 million visitors including 160,000 foreigners with more than 20 countries participating in fair.[^cite_note-73] The Suraj Kund Lake.[^cite_note-74] D2i.in. Retrieved on 2012-01-10. here is surrounded by rock cut steps.[^cite_note-75]

2016 edition of Surajkund International Crafts Mela was the 30th edition and People's Republic of China will be participating in the fair.[^cite_note-76] Participation of the China will be the part of agreement signed between India and China in 2014 to celebrate year 2016 as "Year of China in India".[^cite_note-77]

In 2021, the international crafts fair or Surajkund Mela, as it commonly called scheduled from 1 to 15 February was cancelled for the first time in 34 years amid COVID-19 fears.[^cite_note-indiatimes2-78]

### Anangpur Dam

Main article: [Anagpur Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anagpur_Dam)



Surajkund dam downstream side

The Anagpur Dam is located close to the Anagpur village (also called Arangpur) in [Faridabad district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faridabad_district), about 2 kilometres (1.2 mi) away from [Surajkund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surajkund). This unique Indian hydraulic engineering structure was built during the reign of King [Anangpal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anangpal) of the [Tomar dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomar_dynasty) in the 8th century. It is approachable by road from [Delhi](37756.md) from the Delhi – [Mathura](283812.md) road. The ruins of the fortifications found in Anangpur village establish by an inference that it was built by Anangpal as part of the [Lal Kot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lal_Kot) that was developed as the first city of Delhi in the 8th century.[^cite_note-Sharma-79][^cite_note-Peck-80][^cite_note-anag-81]

### Raja Nahar Singh Palace

Main article: [Raja Nahar Singh Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahar_Singh_Mahal)

Raja Nahar Singh palace is located in [Ballabhgarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballabhgarh). Now [Haryana Tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haryana_Tourism_Corporation) manages the palace as heritage property. The palace is now a hotel-cum-restaurant.[^cite_note-82]

### Baba Farid's Tomb

The tomb is located in old Faridabad. Baba Farid was a popular Sufi Sant. The tomb contains 2 gigantic doors and the graves of Baba Farid and his son.<https://mycityfaridabad.com/best-places-to-visit-in-faridabad/> [Archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20220809062036/https://mycityfaridabad.com/best-places-to-visit-in-faridabad/) 9 August 2022 at the [Wayback Machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayback_Machine)

### Prehistoric Stone Age site

Main article: [Mangar Bani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangar_Bani)

Archaeologists discovered cave paintings and tools from the [Paleolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleolithic) period in the [Mangar Bani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangar_Bani) hill forest, which is on the outskirts of Faridabad. The [cave paintings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cave_painting) are estimated to be one lac years old. These are believed to be the largest in the [Indian subcontinent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_subcontinent) and possibly the world's oldest.[^cite_note-hindustantimes-83][^cite_note-84]
