# Uzbekistan at the FIFA World Cup

[Uzbekistan](1032413.md) appeared in the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) for the first time in [2026](36581929.md).[^cite_note-1] On 5 June 2025, they [qualified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(AFC\)) for the finals for the first time after an 0–0 away draw with the [United Arab Emirates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Arab_Emirates_national_football_team).[^cite_note-2] This makes Uzbekistan the first [Central Asian](6742.md) nation, as well as the third former [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union_national_football_team) republic after [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia_national_football_team) and [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine_national_football_team), to qualify for the World Cup.[^cite_note-3]
