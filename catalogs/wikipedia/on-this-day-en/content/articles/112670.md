**Newburgh** is an incorporated town in [Ohio Township, Warrick County, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_Township,_Warrick_County,_Indiana), United States, along the [Ohio River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_River).[^cite_note-gnis-2] As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), Newburgh had a population of 3,344.[^cite_note-Census2020PLLede-4] The town is part of the larger [Evansville metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evansville,_IN-KY_Metropolitan_Statistical_Area), and Ohio Township, which Newburgh shares with nearby [Chandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandler,_Indiana), includes the town. It is the easternmost suburb of [Evansville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evansville,_Indiana).

The area has been inhabited by various cultures, dating back at least 10,000 years. [Angel Mounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Mounds) was a permanent settlement of the [Mississippian culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippian_culture) from 1000 AD to around 1400 AD. By 1850, Newburgh was one of the larger riverports between [Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati) and [New Orleans](53842.md), and was the first town north of the [Mason–Dixon line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason%E2%80%93Dixon_line) to be captured by Confederate forces during the [Newburgh Raid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newburgh_Raid) during the [American Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War). Shortly after the mid-nineteenth century, Newburgh's growth leveled off until the economic boom of the 1960s and 1970s, resulting in substantial growth as a bedroom community for families looking for new housing developments near Evansville.

Today, Newburgh is locally known for its historic downtown district that features a number of specialty stores, antique shops, and restaurants along its riverfront. The town remains a popular residential community for people working in or near Evansville.

## History

As a town situated on the fertile banks of the [Ohio River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_River), Newburgh has a long history of human activity. Western explorers first arrived in the area in the 17th century.[^cite_note-Prehistory-5] For centuries prior to that, it had been inhabited by the [Shawnee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawnee) people and was near the center of prehistoric [Mississippian culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippian_culture) as late as 1450 A.D.[^cite_note-Prehistory-5] Evidence of this prehistoric society remains today at [Angel Mounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Mounds), a [National Historic Landmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Historic_Landmark), and [Ellerbusch site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellerbusch_site), both approximately two miles west of Newburgh.

The principal founders of Newburgh are John Sprinkle and Abner Luce. Sprinkle, a businessman of German descent, landed in Newburgh in the spring of 1803, thirteen years before [Indiana](21883857.md) entered the Union as the 19th state.[^cite_note-Historical-6] He secured land grants in 1812 and in 1818 platted what became known as Sprinklesburgh (sometimes called "Mount Pleasant"). It was the first town in [Warrick County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warrick_County). The original plat of Sprinklesburgh consisted of about 12 blocks immediately west of today's downtown Newburgh.

Abner Luce founded Newburgh almost directly to the east of Sprinklesburgh in 1829. In 1841, Luce's plat was merged with Sprinkelsburgh and the name of the town was changed to Newburgh.[^cite_note-Historical-6] However, it was Samuel Short's land, a strip on the block west of State Street, that now has some of the most visible and important land in modern downtown Newburgh.[^cite_note-Historical-6]

By 1850, the town had grown to be one of the largest riverports on the Ohio-Mississippi River between [Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati) and [New Orleans](53842.md). Much of its growth in this time period was due to coal mining and its beneficial location on the [Ohio River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_River). The first [underground mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underground_mine) shaft in Indiana was sunk in Newburgh in 1850.[^cite_note-Mining-7] However, when the [national railway system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_transportation_in_the_United_States) came to southern Indiana, it bypassed Newburgh completely in favor of [Evansville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evansville,_Indiana), beginning a permanent shift in regional economic dominance.[^cite_note-Mining-7]

According to a number of historical sources, Newburgh was a prominent stop on the [Underground Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underground_Railroad) between the mouth of the Little Pigeon River and [Lake Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Michigan).[^cite_note-8] On July 18, 1862, Newburgh was the first town north of the [Mason–Dixon line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason%E2%80%93Dixon_line) to be captured by the [Confederate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_America) forces during the [American Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War) in what would come to be known as the [Newburgh Raid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newburgh_Raid). Colonel [Adam "Stovepipe" Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Johnson_\(colonel\)), with a partisan band, crossed the Ohio River and confiscated supplies and ammunition without a shot being fired.[^cite_note-Raid-9] The Confederates would have been unable to shell the city (as promised) had Newburgh put up a fight. The Confederates' cannons were an assemblage of a stove pipe, charred log, and wagon wheels. The raid convinced the federal government that it was necessary to supply Indiana with a permanent force of regular Union Army soldiers to counter future raids.[^cite_note-10] Many of the structures used in this raid are still standing, including the Exchange Hotel.

Newburgh's economy benefited from the construction of the Lock and Dam 47 in the 1920s, and its replacement in 1974 with the Newburgh Lock and Dam. The town also benefited from the arrival, and later expansions, of the [Aluminum Company of America (ALCOA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcoa), built in 1957. Many of Newburgh's residents are commuters to businesses and industries in Evansville and surrounding areas.

In 1994, Newburgh leaders planned to annex large areas that would have extended town limits to Frame Road (west) and [SR 66](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_State_Road_66) (north and east).[^cite_note-Comprehensive_Plan-11] This would have given Newburgh the ability to plan land use for large open, undeveloped areas. However, by 2001, the town's leadership shifted its focus away from annexation toward planning in the well-established current town limits.[^cite_note-Comprehensive_Plan-11]

On November 6, 2005, the [Evansville Tornado of November 2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evansville_Tornado_of_November_2005) caused 25 deaths in nearby Evansville. Newburgh suffered extensive property damage and some injuries but suffered no fatalities during the 2\:06 AM tornadic event.

The [Old Newburgh Presbyterian Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Newburgh_Presbyterian_Church) and [Original Newburgh Historic District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Original_Newburgh_Historic_District) are listed on the [National Register of Historic Places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places).[^cite_note-nris-12]

## Geography

Newburgh is located at [37°56′48″N 87°24′13″W  /  37.946579°N 87.403735°W  / 37.946579; -87.403735](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Newburgh,_Indiana\&params=37.946579_N_87.403735_W_type\:city_region\:US).[^cite_note-GR1-13] According to the 2010 census, Newburgh has a total area of 1.41 square miles (3.65 km<sup>2</sup>), of land.[^cite_note-census-g001-14] Plans to expand town limits through annexation have been abandoned in favor of planning and development within the current town limits.[^cite_note-Comprehensive_Plan-11]

Newburgh faces the [Ohio River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_River) along its southern boundary. Much of the town is protected from flood risk by locks and dams completed in the 1960s. Notable landmarks on the west side are the [Angel Mounds Historic Site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_Mounds), a burial site said to have been abandoned centuries prior.

### Architecture

Many of Newburgh's prominent and notable buildings can be found in the [Original Newburgh Historic District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Original_Newburgh_Historic_District) and developed between 1850 and 1930. Numerous buildings have been nominated to the National Register of Historic Places and satisfy the basic criteria of possessing outstanding significance on the national, state, or local level for history, architecture, environment, and/or integrity.[^cite_note-15] The town's architecture includes representative examples of [Italianate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italianate_architecture), [Greek Revival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_Revival_architecture), and [Classical Revival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neoclassical_architecture) style architecture.

### Climate

The climate in this area is characterized by hot, humid summers and cool to mild winters. According to the [Köppen Climate Classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_Climate_Classification) system, Newburgh has a [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) (abbrev. CFA) on climate maps.[^cite_note-16]

## Law and government



The [Old Newburgh Presbyterian Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Newburgh_Presbyterian_Church)

Newburgh uses the [council-manager form of government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council-manager_government), led by a five-member town council and a clerk-treasurer. The town council holds both legislative and executive powers while the clerk-treasurer is responsible for financial matters. All are elected for four-year terms. The council employs and oversees a town manager who is responsible for municipal personnel, budget, and day-to-day operations of the town government.

The county maintains a [small claims court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_claims_court) that is fit for small, civil cases. ## Education

The Town of Newburgh is served by the [Warrick County School Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warrick_County_School_Corporation), the only school district in the county.[^cite_note-23] There are four elementary schools, two middle schools, and [Castle High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_H._Castle_High_School). The four elementary schools are Castle, Newburgh, Sharon, and Yankeetown. The two middle schools are Castle North and Castle South. The Indiana Board of Education awarded all of Newburgh's public schools an "A" grade, the highest possible.[^cite_note-24] Additional private schools are located in surrounding communities.

Prior to 1959, Newburgh had its own high school. The school colors were navy blue and old gold, and the mascot was the Wildcats. That year, it merged into Castle High School.[^cite_note-25]

Newburgh is served by two branches of the Ohio Township Public Library System.[^cite_note-26] The branch was established on May 15, 1916, in downtown Newburgh as a [Carnegie endowed library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_library). The Downtown Newburgh branch underwent a renovation that was completed in 1984. The Bell Road library became the main branch in 2005. In 2017, the library joined the Evergreen Indiana consortium, which allows patrons to borrow materials from over 100 libraries across Indiana.[^cite_note-27]

## Cultural features

Several blocks of Main Street in Newburgh have been intentionally groomed to give off a historic village "river town" air.[^cite_note-Comprehensive_Plan-11]

Each summer, Newburgh hosts a number of festivals, including a Wine, Art & Jazz Festival; Fiddler Fest; and the Strawberry Festival.[^cite_note-Events-28] Some events are held on the shore of the river near the town's lock and dam, with others in the antique downtown section of Newburgh. Summerfest (formerly called the Summer Social), held at St. John's Catholic Parish and School during the month of June.

Each fall, the historic downtown association hosts popular "Ghost Walks" where attendees learn of the town's history of mining, the Civil War, and the Underground Railroad.[^cite_note-Events-28] The first weekend in December features a downtown Christmas celebration with over 60 people dressed in period costumes, carolers, and street musicians.[^cite_note-Events-28] Trolley tours, photos with Santa, and the Newburgh tree lighting ceremony are also part of "Newburgh Celebrates Christmas."

Newburgh has several municipal parks. A bicycle and pedestrian trail extend along the riverfront, with plans to eventually link it up with Evansville's trail system and tie into the wider [American Discovery Trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Discovery_Trail). In 2006, the [United States Senior Men's Amateur Golf Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Senior_Men's_Amateur_Golf_Championship) was hosted in [Warrick County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warrick_County), not far from Newburgh, at the [Victoria National Golf Course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_National_Golf_Course).
