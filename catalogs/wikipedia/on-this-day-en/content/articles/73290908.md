The **FIFA Intercontinental Cup** is an annual [football](10568.md) competition organised by [FIFA](11049.md), the global [governing body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_governing_body) for the sport. First held in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_FIFA_Intercontinental_Cup), the tournament features the reigning club champions of each of the six [FIFA confederations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_confederations), contested in a [knockout format](939680.md) in which the representative from Europe receives a [bye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bye_\(sports\)) to the final.[^cite_note-1]

The tournament concept is closely modelled on the earlier version of the [FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Club_World_Cup), which underwent major expansion and restructuring starting with the [2025 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Club_World_Cup). The FIFA Intercontinental Cup confers the title of annual [world champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_world_champion_football_clubs).[^cite_note-\:0512-2] [Paris Saint-Germain](357488.md) are the current holders, having defeated [Flamengo](187239.md) 2–1 on [penalties](511302.md) following a 1–1 draw after [extra time](633242.md#Association_football) in the [2025 final](80107067.md).

## History

On 16 December 2022, the [FIFA Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Council) approved the expansion of the [FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Club_World_Cup) from seven to 32 teams beginning in [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_FIFA_Club_World_Cup).[^cite_note-3] The [2023 tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_FIFA_Club_World_Cup) was therefore the last played under the previous format. However, FIFA reported that confederations expressed the need for the champions of their top club competitions to still play each other annually to "stimulate competitiveness".[^cite_note-4] Therefore, on 14 March 2023, the FIFA Council approved a concept for an annual club competition beginning in 2024, later named as the FIFA Intercontinental Cup. FIFA considers this tournament to be a direct continuation of the FIFA Club World Cup, held from 2000 to 2023.[^cite_note-5]

The similarly named [Intercontinental Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercontinental_Cup_\(1960%E2%80%932004\)) (1960–2004) was a club competition organised by [UEFA](32332.md) and [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL) which was usually contested by the champions of the two respective confederations. However, the new FIFA-organised competition features the champions of the top club competitions of the six confederations, namely the [AFC Champions League Elite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Champions_League_Elite), [CAF Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAF_Champions_League), [CONCACAF Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF_Champions_Cup), [Copa Libertadores](299730.md), [OFC Professional League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OFC_Professional_League)[^cite_note-6] and [UEFA Champions League](44220.md), and it retains the world title of the preceding FIFA Club World Cup.[^cite_note-key_info-7]

The [inaugural edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_FIFA_Intercontinental_Cup) of the tournament took place from September to December 2024, with the final stages played in [Qatar](8238258.md).[^cite_note-Reuters-2023-8]

## Format

The competition features a series of intercontinental play-offs between clubs from all confederations except UEFA, whose club receives a [bye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bye_\(sports\)) to the final.[^cite_note-key_info-7][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] If a match is tied after normal playing time, [extra time](633242.md#Association_football) is played. If still tied after extra time, a [penalty shoot-out](511302.md) is held to determine the winner.

- **First round**: An OFC representative plays alternately against an AFC or CAF representative. The match, denominated as the "FIFA African–Asian–Pacific Cup play-off", is hosted by a club from a higher-ranked country in the [FIFA Men's World Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Men's_World_Rankings).

- **Second round**: The other AFC/CAF representative (which received a bye) faces the winner of the first round in a match denominated the "FIFA African–Asian–Pacific Cup", hosted by a club from a higher-ranked country in the FIFA Rankings. Elsewhere, the CONMEBOL and CONCACAF representatives face off at a neutral venue in a match dubbed the "FIFA Derby of the Americas".

- **Play-off**: The two second-round winners meet at a neutral venue in a match denominated the "FIFA Challenger Cup".

- **Final**: The winners of the play-off face a UEFA representative at a neutral venue in a match denominated the "FIFA Intercontinental Cup" to determine the champions.

## Trophies









The FIFA Intercontinental Cup trophy (golden colour), which has undergone slight changes compared to the old [FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Club_World_Cup) trophy presented from 2005 to 2023, and the three secondary intercontinental trophies: the Challenger Cup (white colour), the Derby of the Americas (blue colour) and the African–Asian–Pacific Cup (red colour).

The FIFA Intercontinental Cup trophy is similar in design to the [FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Club_World_Cup) trophy used from [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_FIFA_Club_World_Cup) to [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_FIFA_Club_World_Cup). It features a circular base with six pillars extending upward from it, supporting a globe shaped like a football. The trophy is gold in colour and has a plaque on the base with the name and year of the tournament, which means a new trophy will be commissioned every year. The base is taller than the said Club World Cup trophy.[^cite_note-11]

The three trophies for the earlier rounds of the tournament resemble smaller versions of the main FIFA Intercontinental Cup trophy. The trophies are primarily silver and feature four pillars supporting the globe at the top. The bases' heights are similar to that on the 2005–2023 Club World Cup trophy. The trophies are identical to each other aside from the plaque on the base which says the name of each cup, and the accent colour: red for the African–Asian–Pacific Cup, blue for the Derby of the Americas, and white for the Challenger Cup.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

The winners of the competition are also entitled to receive the [FIFA Champions Badge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Champions_Badge); it features an image of the trophy, which the reigning champion is entitled to display on its first-team kit only, up until and including, the final of the next championship.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]
