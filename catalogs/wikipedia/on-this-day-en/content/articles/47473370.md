The **Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs** (**MCDA**; [Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Kementerian Komunikasi dan Digital*; abbreviated as **Komdigi**) is an Indonesian [government ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_ministry) that is responsible for [digital communications](11341213.md), information affairs and [internet censorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Censorship_in_Indonesia). The ministry reports to the president and is currently led by [Meutya Hafid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meutya_Hafid), the Minister of Communication and Digital Affairs.[^cite_note-pp-1]

The current form of the ministry was established in 2001 by President [Megawati Sukarnoputri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megawati_Sukarnoputri) after its predecessor, the **[Department of Information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Information_\(Indonesia\))** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Departemen Penerangan*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Department of Enlightenment', abbreviated Deppen), was abolished by President [Abdurrahman Wahid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdurrahman_Wahid) in 1999. Deppen itself was created on 19 August 1945, during the [National Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_National_Revolution).[^cite_note-2]

## Controversy

See also: [Internet censorship in Indonesia](39528984.md)

The ministry is often criticized for its [censorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Censorship_in_Indonesia), as it blocks websites "to protect its citizen from hoax" \[*[sic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sic)*]. In 2020, the Director General Ministry Semuel Abrijani Pangerapan and [Johnny G. Plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_G._Plate) introduced a law that requires foreign companies to register under the Electronic System Provider (*Penyelenggara Sistem Elektronik; PSE*) list which could give the government access to the citizen's personal info and threaten the company to block access from the country if the company did not register. The law was revised and passed in 2021.[^cite_note-4]

In July 2022, a ban was implemented for several notable websites such as [PayPal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PayPal), [Epic Games](573199.md), [Steam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steam_\(service\)), [Origin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Origin_\(service\)), and [Yahoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo), and games such as *[Counter-Strike: Global Offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-Strike:_Global_Offensive)* and *[Dota 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dota_2)* as they did not register under the ministry's new law.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Access to PayPal, Steam, CSGO, Dota 2, and Yahoo would later be allowed for once more by 2 August 2022.[^cite_note-8]

### Censorship of Wikipedia

Main article: [Censorship of Wikipedia](30511364.md)

Since 25 February 2026, Minister of Communication and Digital Affairs [Meutya Hafid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meutya_Hafid) has ordered a controversial ban to access to Wikimedia's authentication domain (`auth.wikimedia.org`) due to the foundation's non-compliance of registering themselves under the Electronic System Provider law. This prevents users from logging in or creating accounts on all Wikimedia projects, including Wikipedia.[^cite_note-PSE-WMF-9] Rights groups have condemned the ministry decision as digital [authoritarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Authoritarianism) abuse and called for the restoration of normal access.[^cite_note-Tirto27Feb26-10][^cite_note-TimesID27Feb26-11][^cite_note-SAFE27Feb26-12]

On 25 March 2026, [Wikimedia Commons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikimedia_Commons) domain was also blocked in the country; it has since been unblocked,[^cite_note-13] while Wikimedia's authentication portal is still blocked.

On 16 April 2026, the Indonesian government threatened to block the entire Wikipedia and all other Wikimedia Foundation subsidiaries within 7 business days, citing the PSE law.[^cite_note-14]
