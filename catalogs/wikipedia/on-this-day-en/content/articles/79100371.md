The **high courts of South Korea** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 고등법원) are [intermediate appellate courts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermediate_appellate_court) in South Korea's [judicial system](63660454.md). They consist of six courts established in [Busan](68115.md), [Daegu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daegu), [Daejeon](284812.md), [Gwangju](323128.md), [Seoul](19159283.md), and [Suwon](502693.md) that have [jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurisdiction) to hear appeals from [lower courts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_court) within their region.

## Jurisdiction

South Korea's high courts derive their jurisdiction from Article 28 of the Court Organization Act.[^cite_note-1] They have jurisdiction to hear appeals from the rulings or judgments of three-judge [panels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_panel) from the district courts.[^cite_note-2] They also have jurisdiction to hear appeals from the administrative court and three-judge panels of the family courts.[^cite_note-3] Each of the high courts has jurisdiction over a specific region of South Korea.[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-\:1-5]

## Composition

Each high court is headed by a chief justice and organized into civil, criminal, and special divisions with each division being led by a chief judge. The judges in each high court are organized into three-judge panels that exercise the court's authority.[^cite_note-\:0-4] Judges are appointed by the [chief justice of the Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Justice_of_the_Supreme_Court_of_Korea) with the approval of the [Council of Supreme Court Justices](4899540.md#Council_of_Supreme_Court_Justices) for ten year terms. Presently, candidates for appointment to the judiciary must have at least seven years of legal experience and beginning on January 1, 2026, they must have at least ten years of experience.[^cite_note-6] During their term of appointment, high court judges only serve on high courts and cannot be transferred to district courts.[^cite_note-\:1-5]
