The **Second Shehbaz Sharif government** is the incumbent [federal cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Pakistan) and [Government](1897833.md) led by [Shehbaz Sharif](6502561.md) after he was sworn into office on 11 March 2024 opposition parties following a [No-confidence motion against Imran Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No-confidence_motion_against_Imran_Khan) in incumbent former prime minister [Imran Khan](413184.md) during [Pakistani political unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_political_crisis_\(2022%E2%80%93present\)).

## Cabinet formation

On March 6 the Cabinet Division issued the de-notification of the [caretaker cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakar_caretaker_ministry).[^cite_note-1] The next day PML-N leader [Attaullah Tarar](74389513.md) said that the federal cabinet would be finalized within the next 48 hours. Adding that [HBL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habib_Bank_Limited) President [Muhammad Aurangzeb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Aurangzeb) would be considered for the financial portfolio, remarking “The most important portfolio is the finance ministry as the economy has to be revived and the matters with the IMF will have to be taken forward."[^cite_note-2] Shehbaz Sharif would host a dinner together with leaders of the [Pakistan Peoples Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_People's_Party) (PPP), the [Pakistan Muslim League-Quaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Muslim_League_\(Q\)) (PML-Q), the [Istehkam-e-Pakistan Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istehkam-e-Pakistan_Party) (IPP) and the [Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muttahida_Qaumi_Movement_%E2%80%93_Pakistan) (MQM-P) over Federal Cabinet finalization.[^cite_note-3] PML-N would request for the PPP to join the cabinet,[^cite_note-4] however [Bilawal Bhutto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilawal_Bhutto_Zardari) would announce that it would [support PML-N in government formation without joining cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confidence_and_supply), addressing PPP's CEC meeting “The PPP has decided that while we are not in a position to join the federal government ourselves, nor will we be interested in taking ministries in such a set-up, we also do not want to see political chaos in the country. We do not want to see perpetual crisis in the country,” he said.[^cite_note-5] On March 9 [Nawaz Sharif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nawaz_Sharif) directed [Shehbaz Sharif](6502561.md) to implement the parties manifesto after the formation of the federal cabinet.[^cite_note-6] Later Nawaz would direct the PM to keep the federal cabinet small in the initial phase. During the meeting, Shehbaz Sharif would table the proposed names of the Federal Cabinet to the PML-N leader.[^cite_note-7] Four-time finance minister and Sharif family associate [Ishaq Dar](988764.md) would drop out the race for the position of Minister of Finance & Revenue.[^cite_note-8] According to the [Tribune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Express_Tribune), an anonymous PML-N leader claimed that the appointment of [Mohsin Naqvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohsin_Raza_Naqvi) as Interior Minister would "erode the government's credibility", according to another anonymous PML-N leader, Ishaq Dar was not chosen due to the PM's preference for a new Finance minister.[^cite_note-9]

Prior to the inauguration of the Federal Cabinet, the [International Monetary Fund](15251.md) (IMF) reportedly tied cabinet formation as a pre-condition for the sending of an IMF team to Pakistan to negotiate and finalize another bailout from the Fund.[^cite_note-10]

On March 11 President [Asif Ali Zardari](993655.md) administered the oath to the PM's 19-member federal cabinet.[^cite_note-brecorder.com-11] The proposals for appointment was made under clause 1 of Article 92 (federal ministers and ministers of state) of the Constitution.[^cite_note-12] On the same day [Muhammad Aurangzeb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Aurangzeb), the new finance minister would renounce his Dutch nationality and be granted Pakistani nationality as well as resign from his position as CEO of Habib Bank Limited.[^cite_note-13] The nineteen member cabinet includes many veteran politicians and newcomers.[^cite_note-14] [Mohsin Naqvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohsin_Raza_Naqvi) and [Ahad Cheema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahad_Cheema), former caretaker ministers would be included amongst other PML-N stalwarts.[^cite_note-15] The swearing-in ceremony was held in the [President's House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aiwan-e-Sadr) (Aiwan-i-Sadr).[^cite_note-brecorder.com-11] On March 12 the new Federal cabinet members assumed charge after allocation of their portfolios.[^cite_note-16] The new cabinet was criticized for sidelining some members of the elected coalition, seen in the absence of Ishaq Dar in the financial portfolio and [Rana Sanaullah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rana_Sanaullah) as interior minister, traditional supporters of Nawaz Sharif. Shehbaz Sharif's position as Prime Minister has also been seen as to maintain strong relations with the military and ensuring its influence in administration. The military's backing of the new cabinet composition has also been seen as a way to send a message against the political opposition that attempts to undermine the new government will be met with force and push through reforms, such as through the [SIFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Investment_Facilitation_Council).[^cite_note-thediplomat.com-17]

### Cabinet division and cabinet secretariat

The current [Federal Secretary of the Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_Secretary_\(Pakistan\)) is Kamran Ali Afzal, since his appointment in August 2023.[^cite_note-26] On 20 March the National Seed Development and Regulatory Authority (NSDRA) and Cannabis Control and Regulatory Authority were both placed under the administrative control of the Cabinet Division.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] The [Cabinet Secretariat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_Secretariat_\(Pakistan\)) is directly under the Prime Minister and Cabinet Secretary, controlling the [Aviation Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviation_Division_\(Pakistan\)), [Cabinet Division](https://cabinet.gov.pk/index), [Establishment Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Establishment_Division), [National Security Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Division_Secretary) and [Poverty Alleviation & Social Safety Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Poverty_Alleviation_and_Social_Safety).[^cite_note-29]

### Cabinet committees

On 21 March the [cabinet division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_Secretariat_\(Pakistan\)) would issue two separate notifications constituting the [Economic Coordination Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_Coordination_Committee_\(Pakistan\)) (ECC) and the Cabinet Committee on Energy (CCoE), by the Prime Minister under terms of rule 17(2) of Rules of Business, 1973. Both would be retained directly under [Shehbaz Sharif](6502561.md), becoming Chairman of both committees.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-\:3-31] On the same day Shehbaz would also approve the reconstitution of the Cabinet Committee on [Privatization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatisation_in_Pakistan) (CCoP), with MoFA [Ishaq Dar](988764.md) as Chairman with the secretarial support of the Committee provided by the [Aviation Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviation_Division_\(Pakistan\)); and the reconstitution of the Cabinet Committee on State-Owned Enterprises (CCoSOEs), with Minister of Finance and Revenue, [Muhammad Aurangzeb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Aurangzeb) as Chairman.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33] Additional Committees constituted on 21 March include the Cabinet Committee on Disposal of Legislative Cases (CCLC) under [Azam Nazeer Tarar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azam_Nazeer_Tarar), the Cabinet Committee on Chinese Investment Projects (CCoCIP) under [Ahsan Iqbal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahsan_Iqbal), and a committee to "speed up" the privatization of [Pakistan International Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_International_Airlines) (PIA) under [Khawaja Asif](16345137.md).[^cite_note-34] With forty-eight hours, on 23 March the Cabinet Division issued a notification, appointing Muhammad Aurangzeb as new Chairman of the ECC, with Shehbaz Sharif withdrawing from the committee[^cite_note-35] Previously Aurangzeb held chairmanship of only the Cabinet Committee on SOEs (CCoSOEs), while Ishaq Dar also held a prominent role in the new cabinet committees as chairman of Cabinet Committee on Privatisation (CCoP).[^cite_note-36] This was a departure from past practices, where finance ministers chaired three out of four committees.[^cite_note-\:4-37] With Shahbaz Rana writing that the "PM had "clipped" the Finance Minister’s "decision-making wings" by depriving him of the ECC.[^cite_note-38] The decision to place Aurangzeb as chairman also came after criticism from "all quarters."[^cite_note-\:4-37] Pakistan newspaper Dawn, quoting a senior member of the cabinet officially stated that the PM had “regretted he would be unable to chair the ECC meetings due to his hectic schedule and engagements.”[^cite_note-39] On 28 March the Cabinet Committee on Inter-Governmental Commercial Transactions (CCoIGCT) would be constituted, chaired by Khawaja Asif, with Terms of Reference (ToR) that the committee may authorize, recommend approval, and constitute a negotiation committee for a [G2G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government-to-government) or commercial agreement with a foreign state, as well as approve price-discovery mechanisms, submit recommendations for existing regulatory compliance. Operating under the clauses (c) and (d) of sub-section (2) of Section (4) of the Inter-Governmental Commercial Transactions Act, 2022.[^cite_note-\:5-40]

## Reshuffles and non-cabinet appointments



Inaugural meeting of the federal cabinet, March 11, 2024

### Ministerial level

On March 18, [Awais Leghari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awais_Leghari) was relieved from his portfolio as [Minister for Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Railways_\(Pakistan\)) in favour for taking over the [Ministry of Energy](54776381.md), formerly under [Musadik Malik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musadik_Malik).[^cite_note-53] On 29 March, [Amir Muqam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amir_Muqam) was relieved of his additional portfolio as Federal Minister of [National Heritage and Culture](https://heritage.pakistan.gov.pk/index), instead taking over the [Kashmir Affairs and Gilgit-Baltistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Kashmir_Affairs_and_Gilgit-Baltistan) portfolio.[^cite_note-54]

On April 4 five portfolios were assigned to different federal ministers previously held by the prime minister, [Aleem Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleem_Khan) was given the additional portfolio of [communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Communications_\(Pakistan\)), [Chaudhry Salik Hussain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaudhry_Salik_Hussain) was allocated [Religious Affairs and Interfaith Harmony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Religious_Affairs_and_Inter-faith_Harmony), [Rana Tanveer Hussain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rana_Tanveer_Hussain) allocated [Food Security & Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Food_Security_&_Research), [Ahsan Iqbal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahsan_Iqbal) was allocated [inter-provincial coordination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Inter_Provincial_Coordination) and [Musadik Malik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musadik_Malik) was allocated [water resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Water_Resources_\(Pakistan\)).[^cite_note-\:8-25]

&#x20;MNA Ali Pervaiz was appointed as a Minister of State with the allocated portfolios of Finance & Revenue, with an additional portfolio of the Power Division on 17 May.[^cite_note-55]

Further information: [Deputy Prime Minister of Pakistan](36246720.md)

[Foreign Minister](9957956.md) [Ishaq Dar](988764.md) was appointed [Deputy Prime Minister of Pakistan](36246720.md) through a notification from the [Cabinet Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_Secretariat_\(Pakistan\)), this after the post had remained vacant for the past eleven years.[^cite_note-\:03-56][^cite_note-57] Arifa Noor speculated in *[Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawn_\(newspaper\))* this was the result of [Nawaz Sharif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nawaz_Sharif) planning to reassert himself due to Ishaq Dar's "proximity" with Nawaz.[^cite_note-58]

### Other federal posts

[Rana Sanaullah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rana_Sanaullah) was appointed adviser to the prime minister on 30 April, approved by President [Asif Ali Zardari](993655.md). Speaking on a *[Geo News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geo_News)* programme he confirmed that he had a meeting with the [Prime Minister](461896.md) to include him the federal government.[^cite_note-59] This was seen as a shift in party dynamics and the increasing influence of the [Nawaz Sharif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nawaz_Sharif) factions influence in the coalition government and coinciding with the appoint of another "trusted ally" of Nawaz Sharif, [Ishaq Dar](988764.md) as [Deputy Prime Minister of Pakistan](36246720.md).[^cite_note-60][^cite_note-61] On 22 May Rana Sanaullah was given the [Inter-provincial coordination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Inter_Provincial_Coordination) portfolio.[^cite_note-62]

On 28 March both [Malik Mukhtar Ahmad Bharath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukhtar_Ahmad_Bharath) and [Rana Mashhood Ahmad Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rana_Mashhood_Ahmad_Khan) received appointments from circulars issued by the Cabinet Division. Mukhtar Ahmad appointed Coordinator to the Prime Minister on [National Health Services, Regulations and Coordination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Health_Services,_Regulation_and_Coordination) and Rana Mashhood as Coordinator to the Prime Minister on [Prime Minister's Youth Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister's_Youth_Programme), both in an "honorary" capacity.[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64] The next day on the 29th, Rana Mashhood was appointed Chairman of the Youth Programme, also in an honorary capacity.[^cite_note-65]

Later on 3 April [Shehbaz Sharif](6502561.md) appointed further coordinators [Romina Khurshid Alam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romina_Khurshid_Alam) (Climate Change and Environmental coordination), Shabbir Ahmed Usmani (Kashmir Affairs and Gilgit-Baltistan) and Rana Ihsaan Afzal Khan on 'general issues'. The government claimed that the coordinators would not be part of the Federal Cabinet and work on an honorary basis.[^cite_note-\:8-25]

## Constitutional institutions

### Council of Common Interests

Further information: [Council of Common Interests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Common_Interests)

On 25 March 2024 [Shehbaz Sharif](6502561.md) reconstituted the eight-member Council of Common Interests (CCI) as chairman after a notification was issued. The Prime Minister inducted [Khawaja Asif](16345137.md) (Defense), [Amir Muqam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amir_Muqam) (SAFRON), and [Ishaq Dar](988764.md) (Foreign Affairs) into the Council.[^cite_note-66][^cite_note-\:6-67] The composition of the CCI marked the first time a foreign minister had been designated a member, replacing the membership of the finance minister.[^cite_note-68] Abdullah Niazi an editor at *Profit* claimed that [Muhammad Aurangzeb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Aurangzeb)'s role in the Cabinet was being undermined by Ishaq Dar, further remarking that the inclusion of Khwaja Asif and Engineer Amir Muqam might indicate Shehbaz Sharif's focus on giving [party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Muslim_League_\(N\)) loyalists greater influence.[^cite_note-69] According to Mushtaq Ghumman, finance ministers were not always included in the CCI however energy and planning ministers were.[^cite_note-70] In the previous [Pakistan Democratic Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Democratic_Movement) (PDM) [government under Shehbaz Sharif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Shehbaz_Sharif_ministry) the finance minister was not included in the CCI. [Attaullah Tarar](74389513.md) on March 29 would state that “nobody has been replaced or substituted," referencing the constitutional procedure of the CCI.[^cite_note-71]

Summaries submitted by different ministries and provincial governments were returned for later resubmission following the re-constitution of the Council. CCI Secretary Omer Rasul stated that the summaries forwarded by ministries, divisions and provincial governments did not conform with the guidelines demarcated in Rules of Procedure of CCI (2010) and were ambiguous whether such cases fell within the purview of the CCI.[^cite_note-72]

### National Economic Council

Further information: [National Economic Council (Pakistan)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Economic_Council_\(Pakistan\))

On June 7, 2024 [President](419460.md) [Asif Ali Zardari](993655.md) reconstituted the [National Economic Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Economic_Council_\(Pakistan\)) (NEC) of Pakistan under clause (1) of Article 156 of the constitution of Pakistan. The NEC being chaired by the Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, and includes the [chief ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_minister) of [Sindh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_Sindh), [KPK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa), [Punjab](9228428.md) and [Balochistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Minister_of_Balochistan), federal ministers, nominated provincial ministers and federal secretaries.[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74] On 10 June the NEC approved [CPEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China%E2%80%93Pakistan_Economic_Corridor) proposals and investment projects related to the then upcoming [federal budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Pakistan_federal_budget),[^cite_note-75] and was submitted a [five-year plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-Year_Plans_of_Pakistan).[^cite_note-76]

## Criticism

See also: [Allegations of rigging in the 2024 Pakistani general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allegations_of_rigging_in_the_2024_Pakistani_general_election)

The National Assembly's [leader of the opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(Pakistan\)), [Omar Ayub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Ayub) and his party, the [Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf](1651925.md) (PTI) have accused Shehbaz Sharif of corruption, and his second ministry coming into power under [allegations of rigging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allegations_of_rigging_in_the_2024_Pakistani_general_election) through a faulty electoral process. PTI have accused Shehbaz’s coalition of [fascism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fascism).[^cite_note-\:1-79][^cite_note-80][^cite_note-81] A (PTI) Spokesperson said the cabinet was fake, claiming it to be based on a stolen mandate. Further claiming the inclusion of people like Mohsin Naqvi in the federal cabinet proved the unconstitutional role of the caretaker government and [Election Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election_Commission_of_Pakistan).[^cite_note-82] Shehbaz Sharif has been accused of receiving backing from the military,[^cite_note-thediplomat.com-17][^cite_note-83] and running a "[hybrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_regime) pro-max" regime.[^cite_note-84]
