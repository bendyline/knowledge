The **chief minister of Punjab** ([Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language)/[Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): وزیرِ اعلیٰ پنجاب, [romanised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Urdu): *Wazīr-ē-Aʿlā Panjāb*) is the [head of government](37570.md) of the [Pakistani province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_province) of [Punjab](24751.md). Elected by the [Provincial Assembly of the Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_Assembly_of_the_Punjab), the chief minister leads the [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) of the [government of Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Punjab,_Pakistan) along with their appointed [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryam_Nawaz_provincial_government), with the [governor of Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Punjab,_Pakistan) serving as the nominal head of executive and province. The chief minister is elected by a majority in the Punjab Assembly, where they serve as [leader of the House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_House). The office is held by virtue of the officeholder's ability to [command the confidence](208101.md) of the Assembly, with no term limits. [Maryam Nawaz Sharif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryam_Nawaz_Sharif) is the 19th and current chief minister of Punjab.

The office of Chief Minister is located in [Lahore](125315.md), the capital of the Punjab province and is known as the *CM Secretariat*.[^cite_note-1]
