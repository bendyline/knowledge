**Poltava Oblast** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Полтавська область, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *Poltavska oblast*), also referred to as **Poltavshchyna** (Ukrainian: Полтавщина), is an [oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Ukraine) (province) of central [Ukraine](31750.md). The [administrative center](181337.md) of the oblast is the city of [Poltava](38581.md). Most of its territory was part of the southern regions of the [Cossack Hetmanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cossack_Hetmanate). Population: 1,352,283 (2022 estimate).[^cite_note-ua2022estimate-2]

After [Poltava](38581.md), the oblast's three most populous cities were [Kremenchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremenchuk), [Horishni Plavni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horishni_Plavni), and [Lubny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubny), according to 2022 government estimates.[^cite_note-5]

## History

The Poltava Oblast was established on 22 September 1937 by a resolution of the [Central Executive Committee of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Executive_Committee_of_the_Soviet_Union). It was based mostly on rayons (districts) that were part of [Kharkiv Oblast](494901.md), with some from [Kyiv Oblast](494884.md). The region approximately corresponds to the earlier [Poltava Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poltava_Governorate) (1802–1925).

During the [Nazi Germany](21212.md) occupation in 1941–43, most of the region belonged to Kiew Generalbezirke (general district), while the rest was part of the German military rear area. After the withdrawal of German forces, the region was reinstated almost to the same borders. In the 1950s it lost some of its territories to the newly created Cherkasy Oblast.[^cite_note-6] Some were submerged with the creation of the [Kremenchuk Reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremenchuk_Reservoir) in 1959. In 2020, as part of a general Ukrainian administrative reform, Poltava Oblast reduced the number of its raions by increasing them in size.

During the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md), the town of Myrhorod was bombed. However, there has been no ground fighting and the province remains completely under Ukrainian control.

## Geography

Poltava Oblast is situated in the central part of Ukraine. Located on the left bank of the [Dnieper](380138.md), the Poltava region was part of the [Cossack Hetmanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cossack_Hetmanate). It has an area of 28,800 km<sup>2</sup>. The oblast borders Chernihiv, Sumy, Kharkiv, Dnipropetrovsk, Kirovohrad, Cherkasy and Kyiv oblasts. The oblast does not contain any notable landforms apart from the Dnieper river, the land is gently undulating.

## Demographics

According to the [2001 Ukrainian census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Ukrainian_census), [ethnic Ukrainians](145144.md) accounted for 91.4% of the population of Poltava Oblast, and [ethnic Russians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians_in_Ukraine) for 7.2%.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

As of 2018, its population was 1,400,000 and [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) was 49 people per 1 km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-9]

[Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language) is the only official language on the whole territory of Poltava Oblast.[^cite_note-18]

According to a poll conducted by *[Rating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rating_\(sociological_group\))* from 16 November to 10 December 2018 as part of the project «Portraits of Regions», 80% of the residents of Poltava Oblast believed that the Ukrainian language should be the only state language on the entire territory of Ukraine. 9% believed that Ukrainian should be the only state language, while Russian should be the second official language in some regions of the country. 8% believed that Russian should become the second state language of the country. 3% found it difficult to answer.[^cite_note-19]

On 3 December 2024, [Poltava Oblast Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poltava_Oblast_Council) approved the «Programme for the Development and Functioning of the Ukrainian Language as the State Language in All Spheres of Public Life in Poltava Oblast for 2025—2030», the main objectives of which are to strengthen the positions of the Ukrainian language in various spheres of public life in the oblast and to [Ukrainianize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainization) the [refugees from other regions of Ukraine](70199806.md).[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

According to the research of the *Content Analysis Centre*, conducted from 15 August to 15 September 2024, the topic of which was the ratio of Ukrainian and Russian languages in the Ukrainian segment of [social media](5897742.md), 76.0% of posts from Poltava Oblast were written in Ukrainian (63.9% in 2023, 60.0% in 2022, 15.5% in 2020), while 24.0% were written in Russian (36.1% in 2023, 40.0% in 2022, 84.5% in 2020).[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

After Ukraine declared independence in 1991, Poltava Oblast, as well as Ukraine as a whole, experienced a gradual [Ukrainization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainization) of the education system, which had been [Russified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russification_of_Ukraine)[^cite_note-24] during the Soviet era. National Museum of Ukrainian Pottery in Opishnia

- Gogol Museum-Reserve

## Economy

### Industry

The oblast is a center of Ukraine's [oil](4207510.md) and [natural gas](22131.md) industry, with many [wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_well) and [pipelines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pipeline_transport) situated here. There is a major [oil refinery](195137.md) [plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremenchuk_Oil_Refinery) in the city of [Kremenchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremenchuk). Important [iron ore](145352.md) processing facilities also present. In general, there are 374 large industrial organization and 618 small industrial organizations.

### Agriculture

In 1999 the gross grain yield was about 14,529 thousand tons, sugar beets – 1,002,900 tons, sunflower seeds – 166,200 tons, potatoes – 279,900 tons. The oblast also produced 120,500 tons of meat, 645,900 tons of milk and 423,200,00 eggs. At the beginning of 1999 there were 1,311 registered farms in the region.

The oblast is divided into 4 districts and 60 [hromadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hromada).

The local administration of the oblast is controlled by the Poltava Oblast Rada. The governor of the oblast is the Poltava Oblast Rada speaker, appointed by the [President of Ukraine](20688479.md).

The following data incorporates the number of each type of administrative divisions of the Poltava Oblast:

- Administrative centre – 1 ([Poltava](38581.md))

- [Raions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raions) – 4&#x20;

  - [Hromadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hromada) – 60

On 18 July 2020, the number of raions was reduced to four.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35] These are:

1. [Kremenchuk Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremenchuk_Raion) (Кременчуцький район), the center is in the city of [Kremenchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremenchuk);

2. [Lubny Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubny_Raion) (Лубенський район), the center is in the city of [Lubny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubny);

3. [Myrhorod Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myrhorod_Raion) (Миргородський район), the center is in the city of [Myrhorod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myrhorod);

4. [Poltava Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poltava_Raion) (Полтавський район), the center is in the city of [Poltava](38581.md).

## Nomenclature

Most of Ukraine's oblasts are named after their [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_\(political\)) cities, officially referred to as "oblast centers" ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): обласний центр, [translit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian) *oblasnyi tsentr*). The name of each oblast is a relative [adjective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjective), formed by adding a feminine [suffix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffix) to the name of respective center city: *Poltava* is the center of the *Poltavs'ka oblast'* (Poltava Oblast). Most oblasts are also sometimes referred to in a feminine noun form, following the convention of traditional regional place names, ending with the suffix "-shchyna", as is the case with the Poltava Oblast, *Poltavshchyna*.

See also: [Romanization of Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian)
