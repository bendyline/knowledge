On 4 November 2025, a [passenger train](15461460.md) collided with a [goods train](70161362.md) near [Bilaspur](2044758.md) in the Indian state of [Chhattisgarh](47734.md). The passenger train, which was traveling between [Gevra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gevra_Road_railway_station) and [Bilaspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilaspur_Junction_railway_station) on the [Howrah–Nagpur–Mumbai line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howrah%E2%80%93Nagpur%E2%80%93Mumbai_line), collided with the rear of a stationary goods train. The accident resulted in the deaths of at least 11 people and injuries to at least 20 others.

## Crash

At about 4\:00 p.m. [IST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Standard_Time) on 4 November 2025, a [MEMU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MEMU) [passenger train](15461460.md) of the [Indian Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Railways), which originated from [Gevra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gevra_Road_railway_station), was traveling towards [Bilaspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilaspur_Junction_railway_station) on the [Howrah–Nagpur–Mumbai line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howrah%E2%80%93Nagpur%E2%80%93Mumbai_line).[^cite_note-NDTV-2] The passenger train collided with the rear of a stationary [goods train](70161362.md) near the Bilaspur station.[^cite_note-TOI-3][^cite_note-4] The collision resulted in some of the coaches of the passenger train climbing over each other.[^cite_note-5]

## Victims

At least 11 people, including the driver of the passenger train, were killed in the collision.[^cite_note-NDTV-2][^cite_note-6] An estimated 20 people were reported to be injured in the accident.[^cite_note-TOI-3][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## Aftermath

The [Chhattisgarh government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Chhattisgarh) announced a compensation of ₹1 million (US\$10,000) to the families of the deceased, ₹0.5 million (US\$5,200) to the seriously injured, and ₹0.1 million (US\$1,000) to the minor injured.[^cite_note-TOI-3]The collision resulted in damage to tracks, overhead electrical wires and the signalling system, which resulted in the traffic on the section being disrupted with several trains being cancelled or diverted.[^cite_note-9]

## Investigation

A preliminary investigation found that the passenger train overshot a red signal, and entered the track section where the goods train was halted.[^cite_note-10]
