**Décines-Charpieu** (French pronunciation: [\[desinʃaʁpjø\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/a/ac/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-Beno%C3%AEt_Prieur-D%C3%A9cines-Charpieu.wav/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-Beno%C3%AEt_Prieur-D%C3%A9cines-Charpieu.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/a/ac/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-Beno%C3%AEt_Prieur-D%C3%A9cines-Charpieu.wav/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-Beno%C3%AEt_Prieur-D%C3%A9cines-Charpieu.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in the [Metropolis of Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolis_of_Lyon) in the [Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes](45093325.md) [region](58846.md) in central-eastern [France](5843419.md). The name of the city is often shortened and simply called **Décines**.

## Geography

The centre of Décines is located southwest of the Grand-Large reservoir. The commune is divided in two by the Canal de Jonage, a deviation of the [Rhône](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rh%C3%B4ne), on which the reservoir was formed. The centre of [Lyon](8638634.md) is located 12 km (7.4 mi) west. The surrounding communes are:

- [Chassieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chassieu)

- [Bron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bron)

- [Vaulx-en-Velin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaulx-en-Velin)

- [Meyzieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meyzieu)

## History

The [Hôtel de Ville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%B4tel_de_Ville,_D%C3%A9cines-Charpieu) was completed in 1932.[^cite_note-3]

Décines-Charpieu left the department of [Isère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Is%C3%A8re) and joined the department of Rhône on 21 February 1968 to become a member of the [Urban Community of Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_Community_of_Lyon) per 1 January 1969. On 1 January 2015, it administratively left the department of Rhône to join the newly-formed [Metropolis of Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolis_of_Lyon).

## Transport

Décines-Charpieu is served by [Lyon tramway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyon_tramway) Lines 3 and 7.

As of 2009 there were nine public preschools (*maternelles*),[^cite_note-Preschools-6] nine public elementary schools (some preschools and elementary schools are grouped into combined primary schools), two public junior high schools (Collège Georges Brassens and Collège Maryse Bastie), and one public senior high school/sixth-form college (Lycée d'Enseignement Polyvalent Chaplin-Becquerel).[^cite_note-7]

There is one Catholic private day school, École maternelle/primaire/Collège Jeanne d'Arc.[^cite_note-Preschools-6]

There is an Islamic day school, [Groupe scolaire Al Kindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groupe_scolaire_Al_Kindi?action=edit\&redlink=1).

## Sport



[Parc Olympique Lyonnais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parc_Olympique_Lyonnais)

The stadium of [Olympique Lyonnais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympique_Lyonnais), [Parc Olympique Lyonnais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parc_Olympique_Lyonnais), is located in Décines-Charpieu. It is the home venue of [Ligue 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ligue_1) club [Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympique_Lyonnais) as well as hosting several [UEFA Women's Champions League](1985338.md) matches for the [club's women's team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympique_Lyonnais_F%C3%A9minin). The stadium held several matches of [UEFA Euro 2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2016) including a [semi-final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2016#Semi-finals). It has hosted the [2017 Coupe de la Ligue Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Coupe_de_la_Ligue_Final), the [2018 UEFA Europa League Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_UEFA_Europa_League_Final), and the [2019 FIFA Women's World Cup Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_Final) and [semi-finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_knockout_stage#Semi-finals).

The stadium has also hosted several [rugby union](25405.md) matches, including the [Rugby Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_European_Rugby_Champions_Cup_Final), [Rugby Challenge Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_European_Rugby_Challenge_Cup#Final) finals of 2016,[^cite_note-8] and the [2023 Rugby World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Rugby_World_Cup).[^cite_note-9]

Parc Olympique Lyonnais hosted the men and women's football tournaments at the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-10] It also hosted the WWE [Backlash France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backlash_France) event on 4 May 2024.
