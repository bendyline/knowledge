**We Invest in Nationhood** (**WIN**) is a political party in [Guyana](17238691.md). Founded and led by businessman [Azruddin Mohamed](80972609.md),[^cite_note-auto2-5] the party contested the [2025 Guyanese general election](68798945.md), winning 16 seats and replacing [A Partnership for National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Partnership_for_National_Unity) as the country's [main opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_opposition) party, breaking a nearly 70 year-long streak of either the APNU-led [People's National Congress Reform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_National_Congress_Reform) or the [People's Progressive Party/Civic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Progressive_Party/Civic) forming government or the official opposition.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-auto-4]

## Policies

Policies presented in WIN's election manifesto included reductions in [value-added tax](52177473.md) (VAT), increases in civil servants' wages, and increased welfare spending, as well as a potential renegotiation of terms with [ExxonMobil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ExxonMobil), aimed at securing greater oil revenues for the Guyanese government.[^cite_note-7] [France24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France24) described the party as attracting "grassroots support," especially among the lower classes, due to its advocacy for "fairer access to housing and opportunities."[^cite_note-auto1-3]

## Controversies

Party leader Azruddin Mohamed has attracted significant controversy for his alleged involvement in corrupt public activities including bribery, resulting in sanctions being imposed by the [United States Treasury Department](53667.md) on Mohamed, a gold-exporting company that he owns with his father, and a government official. Responding to Mohamed's presidential candidacy, US Ambassador [Nicole Theriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Theriot) warned that the US would cut economic ties if there was a Mohamed administration, stating that the US "would have to make sure that we didn't work with him specifically".[^cite_note-auto2-5]

WIN also attracted controversy after posting AI-generated images on social media that were offensive to many users and perceived as racist.[^cite_note-8]
