**Bolivia–Colombia relations** refers to the [current and historical](429154.md) relationship between [Bolivia](3462.md) and [Colombia](5222.md). Both countries are members of the [Organization of American States](22788.md) and the [Andean Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andean_Community). Bolivia and Colombia signed a treaty of friendship in 1912.[^cite_note-1] The two countries signed a cooperation agreement for the 100th anniversary of the treaty.[^cite_note-2]

## Resident diplomatic missions

- Bolivia has an embassy in [Bogotá](211271.md).

- Colombia has an embassy in [La Paz](42821.md).
