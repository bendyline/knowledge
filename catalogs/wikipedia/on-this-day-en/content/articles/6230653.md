**Collbató** (Catalan pronunciation: [\[ˌkɔʎβəˈto\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Catalan)) is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Spain) in the [*comarca*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comarques_of_Catalonia) of the [Baix Llobregat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baix_Llobregat) in [Catalonia](6822.md), Spain. It is situated on the southern slopes of [Montserrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montserrat_\(mountain\)). The area has been inhabited since at least 4000 BC, as shown by [Neolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic) remains found in caves above the village (on display at the museum at the [monastery of Montserrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montserrat_\(monastery\))). Although [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) (olives, grapes and almonds) is the major economic activity, the village is also home to a workshop for the manufacture of [organs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organ_\(musical_instrument\)).
