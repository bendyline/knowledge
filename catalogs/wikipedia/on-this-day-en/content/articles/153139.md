# List of ethnic groups in Vietnam

Fifty-four ethnic groups in [Vietnam](202354.md) have been officially recognized by the [Vietnamese government](78640.md) since 2 March 1979.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-vnEthn-hg-2] Each ethnicity has its own unique language, traditions, and culture. The largest ethnic groups are: [Kinh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viet_people) (Viet) 85.32%, [Tày](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%A0y_people) 1.92%, [Thái](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_people_in_Vietnam) 1.89%, [Mường](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muong_people) 1.51%, [Hmong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hmong_people) 1.45%, [Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_Krom) 1.32%, [Nùng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%B9ng_people) 1.13%, [Dao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dao_people) 0.93%, [Hoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoa_people) 0.78%, with all others accounting for the remaining 3.7% ([2019 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Vietnamese_census)).[^cite_note-census-3] The Vietnamese terms for ethnicity are *dân tộc* and *sắc tộc*.

## Other

1. [Nguồn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngu%E1%BB%93n_language), possibly Mường group, officially classified as a Việt (Kinh) group by the government, Nguồn themselves identify with Việt ethnicity; their language is a member of the [Viet–Muong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viet%E2%80%93Muong) branch of the [Vietic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietic_languages) sub-family.

2. [Sui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sui_people) (Người Thủy), officially classified as [Pa Then](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pa_Then) people.

3. According to news from *Dantri*, an online newspaper in Vietnam, the Thừa Thiên-Huế People's Committee in September 2008 announced a plan to do more research in a new ethnic group in Vietnam. It is Pa Kô, also called Pa Cô, Pa Kô, Pa-Kô or Pa Kôh. This ethnic group settles mainly in [A Lưới](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_L%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Bi_district) suburban district (Thừa Thiên-Huế) and mountainous area of [Hướng Hóa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Bng_H%C3%B3a) (Quảng Trị).

   [^cite_note-5]

   [^cite_note-6]

   &#x20;At present, however, they have been classified in Tà Ôi ethnic group.

Many of the local ethnic groups residing in mountain areas are known collectively in the West as Montagnard or [Degar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degar). One distinctive feature of highland ethnic minority groups in Vietnam is that they are colorfully attired whether at home, in the farm, traveling or in their hometown.

Foreign [expatriate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expatriate) workers are a small portion of the population, some settling permanently or through marriage. Many are migrants from neighboring Asian countries like [China](5405.md), though some are from the west. Today, 2,700 Americans live in Vietnam. Some descend from the French and other Europeans during the protectorate period of the [Nguyễn dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_dynasty). However, most European descendants left after the [August Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Revolution) and the establishment of republican governments in Vietnam.[^cite_note-7]
