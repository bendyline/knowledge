**Svitlana Vasylivna Hrynchuk** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Світлана Василівна Гринчук; born 11 November 1985) is a Ukrainian politician who served as the [Minister of Energy of Ukraine](29773157.md) from 17 July 2025 until 19 November 2025.[^cite_note-Hrynchuk814374-2][^cite_note-3] Previously, she has served as the [Minister of Environmental Protection and Natural Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Environmental_Protection_and_Natural_Resources_\(Ukraine\)) from 2024 to 2025 in the [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shmyhal_Government) of [Denys Shmyhal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denys_Shmyhal).[^cite_note-4] From 2023 to 2024, she served as [Deputy Minister of Energy](29773157.md).[^cite_note-energy-5] From 2022 to 2023, she served as Deputy Minister of Environmental Protection and Natural Resources.[^cite_note-deputy-6]

## Early life

Hrynchuk was born on 11 November 1985 in [Chernivtsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernivtsi), which was then part of the [Ukrainian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_SSR).[^cite_note-birth-1] In 2008 she graduated from the Chernivtsi Institute of Trade and Economics at the [State University of Trade and Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_Trade_and_Economics).[^cite_note-7] She then, in 2013, defended her [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) thesis in economics.[^cite_note-bio-8] Her thesis was on the development of [mortgages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortgages) in agriculture in Ukraine.[^cite_note-bio-8]

## Career

From 2016 to 2019 she was Director of the Department for Climate Change and Ozone Protection in the Ministry of Environment.[^cite_note-bbc-9] In 2019 she became an adviser to the Prime Minister of Ukraine, advising on environmental protection and climate change issues.[^cite_note-birth-1] In 2020 she became an advisor to the Minister of Finance, who was [Serhiy Marchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serhiy_Marchenko) at the time of her appointment.[^cite_note-bbc-9] From 2022 to 2023 she was then Deputy Minister of Environmental Protection and Natural Resources, and then from 2023 to 2024 she was [Deputy Minister of Energy](29773157.md).[^cite_note-energy-5][^cite_note-deputy-6]

## Corruption Investigation

On 12 November 2025, she resigned as energy minister along with justice minister [German Galushchenko](70897877.md) amid a corruption investigation involving the state nuclear energy firm [Energoatom](5712443.md).[^cite_note-kyivindependentGaHr251112-10]

On 19 November 2025 Hrynchuk was officially dismissed as minister by the [Verkhovna Rada](844993.md), with 315 [people's deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Deputy_of_Ukraine) voting for her removal.[^cite_note-Hrynchuk814374-2]

## Personal life

She is married to Vyacheslav Hrynchuk, who is an entrepreneur in the operation of attractions and theme parks and the founder of the organization "Office of Independent Environmentalists".[^cite_note-new-11] In 2022, Hrynchuk stopped mentioning him in official declarations.[^cite_note-new-11] Together, they had one daughter, Yulia.[^cite_note-new-11]
