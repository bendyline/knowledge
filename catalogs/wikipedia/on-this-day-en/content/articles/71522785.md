**Danielle June Marsh** (born 11 April 2005) is an Australian and South Korean singer. She is best known as a former member of the South Korean girl group [NewJeans](71357738.md).&#x20;

Danielle began her career in the entertainment industry by making several appearances on television as a child, before debuting as a member of NewJeans in 2022. The group was formed by ADOR, a subsidiary of [Hybe](41697032.md). The following year, Danielle ventured into acting, voicing [Ariel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariel_\(The_Little_Mermaid\)) in the Korean language dub of *[The Little Mermaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Little_Mermaid_\(2023_film\))* (2023). Following a contract dispute, Danielle was removed from NewJeans at the end of 2025.

## Early life

Danielle June Marsh, Korean name Mo Ji-hye ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 모지혜),[^cite_note-1] was born on 11 April 2005, in [Newcastle, New South Wales](52665.md), Australia, to an Australian father and a South Korean mother.[^cite_note-7] She has an older sister, Olivia Marsh, a singer who made her debut in 2024.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-topstarnews24-9] In 2009, at the age of four, Danielle and her family moved to [Paju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paju), South Korea.[^cite_note-Marieclaire-10]

## Career

### 2011–2021: Pre-debut activities

In 2011, Danielle made her first television appearance on [tvN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TvN_\(South_Korean_TV_channel\))'s *Rainbow Kindergarten* and [SBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_Broadcasting_System)'s *My Heart's Crayon*.[^cite_note-koreaherald-11] She went on to appear on *[Shinhwa Broadcasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinhwa_Broadcasting)* and *Jesse's Play Kitchen* in 2012.[^cite_note-koreaherald-11] In 2012, at the age of seven, Danielle returned to Australia to attend primary school.[^cite_note-Marieclaire-10][^cite_note-12]

Danielle returned to South Korea and became a [K-pop trainee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_idol#Trainee_system) in 2020.[^cite_note-Marieclaire-10]

### 2022–2023: Debut with NewJeans and acting

Main article: [NewJeans](71357738.md)



Danielle at the [Melon Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melon_Music_Awards) in 2023

On 22 July 2022, Danielle made her debut alongside NewJeans with the release of their debut single, "[Attention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_\(NewJeans_song\))", which she co-wrote.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-komca-14] She has since participated in the songwriting of five NewJeans songs.[^cite_note-komca-14] "Attention" topped South Korea's [Circle Digital Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circle_Digital_Chart) and won Best K-pop Song at the [20th Korean Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_Korean_Music_Awards).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

In 2023, Danielle voiced [Ariel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariel_\(The_Little_Mermaid\)) in the Korean dub of *[The Little Mermaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Little_Mermaid_\(2023_film\))* (2023).[^cite_note-littlemermaid-17] Danielle's acting and singing in the film were praised after the first screening.[^cite_note-18] She recorded several songs for the soundtrack of the film, including the single "[Part of Your World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Part_of_Your_World)", which was released on 17 May 2023, and debuted at number two on *[Billboard](18309966.md)*'s [Hot Trending Songs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Trending_Songs).[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20]

### 2024–present: Contract dispute and termination by ADOR

Main article: [NewJeans § 2024–2026: Conflict with Hybe, hiatus, and Danielle's contract termination](71357738.md#2024%E2%80%932026:_Conflict_with_Hybe,_hiatus,_and_Danielle's_contract_termination)

In May 2024, Danielle hosted the Korea on Stage – New Generation concert, organised by the [Cultural Heritage Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_Heritage_Administration).[^cite_note-21] On 28 November 2024, amid disputes between ADOR's former CEO [Min Hee-jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Min_Hee-jin) and its parent company [Hybe](41697032.md), Danielle and the other members of NewJeans announced that they had terminated their contracts with ADOR, while ADOR maintained that the contracts remained "in full effect".[^cite_note-22]

In November 2025, the [Seoul Central District Court](82174232.md) ruled the contracts that the members' contracts with ADOR were valid until 2029.[^cite_note-23] ADOR announced the termination of Danielle's exclusive contract, stating that it would be difficult for her to continue as a member of NewJeans, and said it planned to hold a member of her family and Min Hee-jin responsible for the dispute.[^cite_note-24] The following day, ADOR sued Danielle, her family member, and Min for [₩](298884.md)43.1 billion ([US\$](18717338.md)37.68 million)[^cite_note-MHJ/D/DM_v_ADOR-25] in damages.[^cite_note-26] Danielle subsequently appointed legal representation, and hearings in the case began in May 2026.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

In June 2026, Danielle filed a complaint with South Korea's [Fair Trade Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_Trade_Commission_\(South_Korea\)) (KFTC) against Hybe and ADOR, alleging that she had been treated differently from the other NewJeans members during the contractual dispute. Her representatives also challenged penalty provisions in standard K-pop artist contracts that calculate compensation based on projected revenue and the remaining contract term rather than an agency's actual losses. The KFTC began reviewing the complaint that month.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

## Other ventures

In January 2023, [Burberry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burberry) announced that Danielle had become one of their global ambassadors.[^cite_note-31] Later, in March, she became a global brand ambassador for [Yves Saint Laurent Beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yves_Saint_Laurent_\(fashion_house\)) and appeared in a promotional campaign for their Candy Glaze lipstick.[^cite_note-32] In March 2024, Danielle became a global brand ambassador for [Celine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_\(brand\)).[^cite_note-Elle281024-5][^cite_note-33] In May 2024, she appeared in her first promotional campaign for Celine, developed by [Hedi Slimane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedi_Slimane), the brand's [creative director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_director).[^cite_note-34] In December 2024, Danielle became an [Omega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omega_SA) ambassador.[^cite_note-35]
