The [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) **91st [Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_\(military\)) "Galilee"** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): אוגדה 91), known also as the [Galilee](12639.md) Formation ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): עֻצְבַּת הַגָּלִיל, *Utzbat HaGalil*), is a territorial division in the [IDF Northern Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Command_\(Israel\)), responsible for the front with [Lebanon](17771.md), from [Rosh HaNikra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosh_HaNikra_\(kibbutz\)) up to, but not including [Mount Dov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Dov). In May 2025 it resumed responsibility for this entire front, the western portion having previously been covered by the [146th Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/146th_Division_\(Israel\)).[^cite_note-1]

Its headquarters are located at [Biranit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biranit), [Israel](9282173.md), along with a military police investigations base.
