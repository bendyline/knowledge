**En Nahud** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): النهود) is a town in the desert of central [Sudan](27421.md). It is located within the [En Nuhud District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/En_Nuhud_District) in the [state](579458.md) of [West Kordofan](767647.md).[^cite_note-1]

## History

In 2021, the Darsaya [gold mine](1291393.md) in the town collapsed, leading to the deaths of at least 38 people.[^cite_note-2]

During the [Sudanese civil war (2023–present)](73556699.md), the town was taken by the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) on 1 May 2025.[^cite_note-3]

## Notable residents

## Geography

### Climate

En Nahud has a [hot desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_desert_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BWh*) characterized by consistently high temperatures. Temperatures are coolest in December and January and are hottest from April to June. A [wet season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_season) lasts from June to September with moderate rainfall and relatively high humidity. The period from November to April is almost completely dry, with very low humidity.[^cite_note-WMONormals-4]
