The ***Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology*** is a bimonthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that was established in 1980 by [Jiri Zidek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiri_Zidek_\(paleontologist\)) ([University of Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma)). It covers all aspects of [vertebrate paleontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertebrate_paleontology), including [vertebrate origins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertebrate#Evolutionary_history), [evolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolution), [functional morphology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morphology_\(biology\)), [taxonomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxonomy_\(biology\)), [biostratigraphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biostratigraphy), [paleoecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleoecology), [paleobiogeography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleobiogeography), and [paleoanthropology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleoanthropology). The journal is published by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis) on behalf of the [Society of Vertebrate Paleontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Vertebrate_Paleontology). According to *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2017 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 2.190.[^cite_note-WoS-1]
