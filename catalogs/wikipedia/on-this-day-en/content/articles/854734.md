**Malapatan**, officially the **Municipality of Malapatan** ([Cebuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_language): *Lungsod sa Malapatan*; [Maguindanaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanaon_language): *Inged nu Malapatan*, [Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_Alphabet): ايڠد نو ملڤاتن;[Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Malapatan*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Sarangani](297388.md), Philippines. According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 82,577 people.[^cite_note-5]

Malapatan is bounded on the west by [Sarangani Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarangani_Bay), on the east by [Davao Occidental](38297169.md), on the north by [Alabel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alabel,_Sarangani), and on the south by [Glan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glan,_Sarangani).

Inhabited by Christians, Muslims mostly [Maguindanaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_people) as well the indigenous people of Sarangani. Malapatan is a great illustration of how different religious beliefs can harmoniously co-exist. It is also the home of the famous Blaan "dreamweavers" who craft sleeping mats using the designs they have dreamt about.

The economy of Malapatan is largely based on agriculture with a high level production of [copra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copra) (dried coconut meat). Animal husbandry is the second biggest income earner, notably cattle farming. Other agricultural products are [coconuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coconut), [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [sugarcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugarcane), [bananas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana), [pineapples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pineapple), [mangoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango), [eggs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg_\(food\)), [beef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beef), [fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish).

The economy has accelerated in the past decade driven by advances in global communication technology and the finishing of a modern highway that tremendously improved trade and transport.
