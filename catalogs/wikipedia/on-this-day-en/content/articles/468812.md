**South Sinai** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة جنوب سيناء *_Muḥāfaẓah Ganūb Sīnāʾ_*) is the least populated [governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Egypt) of [Egypt](8087628.md). It is located in the east of the country, encompassing the southern half of the [Sinai Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinai_Peninsula). [Saint Catherine's Monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Catherine's_Monastery), an [Eastern Orthodox](10186.md) monastery and renowned [UNESCO World Heritage Site](44940.md), is located in the central part of the governorate.

## Tourism

The governorate is an attractive destination for tourism due to its amazing and fascinating nature scenes; however, it has been the site of several terrorist attacks. In 1985, [a mass murder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_Burqa_massacre) occurred in the [Ras Burqa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_Burqa) resort and killed 8 people (7 Israeli tourists and 1 Egyptian policeman). The [2004 Sinai bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Sinai_bombings) that targeted tourist hotels in and around [Nuweiba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuweiba) killed 34 people and wounded over 170. In 2005, [Sharm El Sheikh](419335.md) was hit by [a terrorist attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Sharm_El_Sheikh_bombings). 88 people were killed, the majority of them Egyptians, and over 200 were wounded, at that time making it the deadliest terrorist action in the country's history (exceeding the [Luxor massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxor_massacre) of 1997, which killed 62 people).[^cite_note-5] 2006 saw the [Dahab bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Dahab_bombings), which killed 23 people and wounded 80.

The [Sinai insurgency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinai_insurgency), although happening mainly in the neighboring North Sinai Governorate, occasionally spilled over to South Sinai. In February 2014, [a tourist bus was attacked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Taba_bus_bombing) in [Taba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taba,_Egypt). The perpetrators killed the Egyptian bus driver and three South Korean tourists.

After 2014, The Egyptian government and the Egyptian army took firm actions to eliminate terrorism, and city is now safe.

## Demographics

According to population estimates, in 2024 the majority of residents in the governorate lived in urban areas, with an urbanization rate of 51.1%. Out of an estimated 117,113 people residing in the governorate, 59,845 people lived in urban areas as opposed to only 57,268 in rural areas.[^cite_note-pop-6] The [Bedouin](61770.md) inhabit the [Mount Sinai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Sinai) area. Due to poor accessibility, at times the orchards in the mountains have served to hide narcotics smugglers in the region.[^cite_note-Marx2013-7]

## Landmarks



Saint Catherine's Monastery

[Saint Catherine's Monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Catherine's_Monastery), an [Eastern Orthodox Church](10186.md) monastery and [UNESCO World Heritage Site](44940.md) of world renown, is located in central part of the governorate, at the mouth of a gorge at the foot of [Mount Sinai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Sinai), in the city of [Saint Catherine, Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Catherine,_Egypt). Built between 548 and 565, the monastery is one of the oldest working Christian monasteries in the world.[^cite_note-unesco-8] The site contains the world's oldest continually operating library, possessing many unique books including the [Syriac Sinaiticus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syriac_Sinaiticus) and, until 1859, the [Codex Sinaiticus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Codex_Sinaiticus).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-LeroyCollin2004-10]

[Raithu Monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raithu_Monastery?action=edit\&redlink=1) is situated in [El Tor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Tor,_Egypt). It was built on a site where some 40 monks were massacred in the 4th or 5th century.[^cite_note-El-DinTaher1998-11]
