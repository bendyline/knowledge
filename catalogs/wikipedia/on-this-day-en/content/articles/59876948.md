**Morgan Elliot Rogers** (born 26 July 2002) is an English professional [footballer](10568.md) who plays as an [attacking midfielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacking_midfielder) or [winger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winger_\(association_football\)) for [Premier League](11250.md) club [Chelsea](7473.md) and the [England national team](9904.md). A versatile player across attacking positions, Rogers is known for his strength, attacking runs, ability to score from a distance, and ball control.[^cite_note-\:3-6][^cite_note-\:13-7][^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-\:4-9]

Born in [Halesowen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halesowen) in the [Dudley Metropolitan Borough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dudley_Metropolitan_Borough), Rogers is a product of the [West Bromwich Albion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bromwich_Albion_F.C.) academy. He signed for [Manchester City](165813.md) in 2019, but did not make an appearance for the club. After loan spells at [Lincoln City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_City_F.C.), [Bournemouth](451121.md), and [Blackpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpool_F.C.), he joined [Middlesbrough](298603.md) in 2023. After half a season at the club, Rogers signed for [Aston Villa](42173.md) for a reported fee of £8 million in 2024. Following a breakout season with Villa in which he was named their young player of the season, Rogers won the [PFA Young Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PFA_Young_Player_of_the_Year) award in 2025. In July 2026, he joined Chelsea for a fee of £117 million, a record for an English player.

Rogers has represented England at international levels from under-15 to senior, making his senior international debut in 2024.[^cite_note-Eng_Under-10][^cite_note-Youth-11] In May 2026, he made the squad for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).[^cite_note-WC_2026-12]

## Early life

Born in [Halesowen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halesowen), Rogers is the middle child in a family of three brothers born to Deborah and Howard Rogers.[^cite_note-\:5-13][^cite_note-\:11-14] He is of [Jamaican](15660.md) descent through his mother.[^cite_note-15] He attended Colley Lane Primary and [Sandwell Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandwell_Academy),[^cite_note-\:5-13] and was part of the Sandwell team that won the National Schools Cup in 2017, scoring and assisting in the final.[^cite_note-\:6-16] Rogers was a childhood fan of [West Bromwich Albion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bromwich_Albion_F.C.)[^cite_note-17] and a childhood friend of fellow footballer [Jude Bellingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Bellingham).[^cite_note-18]

## Club career

### West Bromwich Albion

Rogers joined West Bromwich Albion at the age of seven, after being spotted by a club scout.[^cite_note-\:6-16] He made his professional debut for West Brom on 6 February 2019, coming on as an 82nd-minute substitute for [Wes Hoolahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Hoolahan) against [Brighton & Hove Albion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brighton_&_Hove_Albion_F.C.) in the [FA Cup](11237.md).[^cite_note-19]

### Manchester City

Rogers signed for [Manchester City](165813.md) on 1 August 2019.[^cite_note-20]

#### Lincoln City (loan)

On 4 January 2021, Rogers signed for [Lincoln City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_City_F.C.) on loan until the end of the season.[^cite_note-21] He made his debut for the club against [Peterborough United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterborough_United_F.C.) a few days later, coming off the bench.[^cite_note-22] He scored his first professional goal against [Portsmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portsmouth_F.C.) in a 1–0 win on 26 January.[^cite_note-23] Three goals in seven games during the month of March contributed to his winning the [EFL Young Player of the Month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Young_Player_of_the_Month) award during his loan spell.[^cite_note-Morgan_Rogers_picks_up_EFL_Young_Player_of_the_Month_award_for_March-24]

#### Bournemouth (loan)

On 23 August 2021, Rogers signed for [Bournemouth](451121.md) on loan until the end of the season.[^cite_note-25] Rogers found his first-team opportunities at Bournemouth limited, and was confined to mostly substitute appearances during the season.[^cite_note-26] He scored his only goal for the club on 15 January 2022, in a 3–2 [Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship) defeat against [Luton Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luton_Town_F.C.).[^cite_note-27]

#### Blackpool (loan)

On 4 January 2023, Rogers joined [Blackpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpool_F.C.) on loan for the remainder of the season.[^cite_note-28] He scored his only goal for the club in a 1–0 win against [Norwich City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwich_City_F.C.) on 8 May 2023.[^cite_note-29]

### Middlesbrough

On 7 July 2023, Rogers signed for Championship club [Middlesbrough](298603.md) for an undisclosed fee.[^cite_note-30] He scored his first goal for the club on 29 July, a late equaliser to make it 2–2 in a [pre-season friendly](977090.md) against [Auxerre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AJ_Auxerre).[^cite_note-31] He scored his first competitive goal for the club on 29 August in a 3–1 [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup) win against [Bolton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolton_Wanderers_F.C.).[^cite_note-32]

Rogers scored five times during Middlesbrough's run to the [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_EFL_Cup) semi-finals, making him the competition's top goalscorer for the season.[^cite_note-eflc-33]

### Aston Villa



Rogers playing for Aston Villa in 2026

On 1 February 2024, 21-year-old Rogers signed for [Premier League](11250.md) club [Aston Villa](42173.md) for an undisclosed fee,[^cite_note-34] reported to be £8m, potentially rising to £15m with performance-related add-ons.[^cite_note-35] Manchester City were entitled to 25% of the fee, due to a sell-on clause in the original transfer to Middlesbrough.[^cite_note-36] On 3 February, Rogers made his Premier League debut as a late substitute in a 5–0 victory away at [Sheffield United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_United_F.C.).[^cite_note-37]

On 6 April 2024, Rogers scored his first goal for Aston Villa in a 3–3 draw against [Brentford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brentford_F.C.).[^cite_note-38] On 29 January 2025, he netted his first [UEFA Champions League](44220.md) goals by scoring a [hat-trick](411541.md#Association_football), including two in the first five minutes, in a 4–2 victory over [Celtic](69708.md) in which he was named player of the match.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41] In doing so, he became the second youngest English player to score a hat-trick in the competition.[^cite_note-42] On April 10, 2025, Aston Villa lost 3–1 to [Paris Saint-Germain](357488.md) in the [Champions League quarterfinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_UEFA_Champions_League_knockout_phase#Quarter-finals) first leg; Rogers scored Aston Villa's only goal.[^cite_note-43]

After a breakout [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Aston_Villa_F.C._season) in which he scored 14 goals and provided 15 assists across all competitions,[^cite_note-44] Rogers was named Aston Villa's Young Player of the Season at the club's end of season awards.[^cite_note-\:7-45] On 19 June 2025, it was announced that Rogers was one of six nominees for the [PFA Young Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PFA_Young_Player_of_the_Year) award,[^cite_note-46] which he then went on to win – becoming the fifth Aston Villa player to win the award since its inception.[^cite_note-\:9-47]

On 10 November 2025, Rogers signed a contract extension with Aston Villa until 2031.[^cite_note-48] On 20 May 2026, he scored in a 3–0 win over [SC Freiburg](1438835.md) in the [Europa League final](76987231.md) as his club won the competition for the first time in their history.[^cite_note-49] [UEFA](32332.md) subsequently named Rogers the [Europa League Player of the Season](232175.md#Awards).[^cite_note-\:15-50]

### Chelsea

On 21 July 2026, Rogers joined Premier League club [Chelsea](7473.md).[^cite_note-51] The fee of £117 million for Rogers became the record for an English footballer.[^cite_note-52]

On 24 August 2026, Rogers scored his first Premier League goal for Chelsea with a 3–2 victory over [Fulham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulham_F.C.) in the opening round.[^cite_note-53] In the third round of the [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_EFL_Cup) against [Leeds United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_United_F.C.) on 9 September, Rogers was introduced as a half-time substitute with Chelsea trailing 0–1, and set up four goals as the club fought back to win 6–3.[^cite_note-54]

## International career

### Youth



Rogers (left) with England in 2026

Rogers has represented England at all international levels from under-15 to senior.[^cite_note-Youth-11][^cite_note-Eng_Under-10]

He represented the [England under-16 team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-16_football_team) at the [2018 Montaigu Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montaigu_Tournament).[^cite_note-Youth-11] In September 2018, Rogers scored a [hat-trick](411541.md#Association_football) for the [England under-17 team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-17_football_team) in a game against [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium_national_under-17_football_team).[^cite_note-Youth-11] The following month saw him score against the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_men's_national_under-17_soccer_team)[^cite_note-55] as well as scoring the final goal in a 3–1 victory against [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil_national_under-17_football_team).[^cite_note-56] In March 2019, Rogers scored twice in an elite qualifier against [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland_national_under-17_football_team).[^cite_note-57] In April 2019, Rogers was included in the England squad for the [2019 UEFA European Under-17 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_UEFA_European_Under-17_Championship).[^cite_note-58] Rogers made his [England under-18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-18_football_team) debut as a 64th minute substitute during a 3–2 win over [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_national_under-18_soccer_team) on 6 September 2019.[^cite_note-59]

On 6 September 2021, Rogers made his debut for the [England U20s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-20_football_team) during a 6–1 victory over [Romania U20s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania_national_under-20_football_team) at [St. George's Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_George's_Park_National_Football_Centre).[^cite_note-60] On 22 March 2024, Rogers made his debut for the [England U21s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_under-21_football_team) during a 5–1 victory over [Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan_national_under-21_football_team) in [Baku](4566.md) during [2025 UEFA European Under-21 Championship qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_UEFA_European_Under-21_Championship_qualification).[^cite_note-61]

### Senior



Rogers with [England](9904.md) at the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md)

On 11 November 2024, Rogers was called up to the [England senior team](9904.md) for the first time for upcoming [UEFA Nations League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Nations_League) fixtures against [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece_national_football_team) and the [Republic of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland_national_football_team).[^cite_note-62] The 22-year-old made his senior debut on 14 November as a second-half substitute in a 3–0 away victory against Greece.[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64] Rogers scored his first England goal in a 3–0 [friendly](977090.md#Association_football) win over [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales_national_football_team) at [Wembley](8913012.md) on 9 October 2025.[^cite_note-65]

On 22 May 2026, Rogers was selected in the 26-man squad for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).[^cite_note-WC_2026-12]

## Style of play

Rogers is a versatile player who primarily operated as a [number 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playmaker#Advanced_playmakers) at Aston Villa[^cite_note-66] but can also play as a winger, striker or attacking midfielder.[^cite_note-\:0-8] Tactically, he is known to take long distance shots[^cite_note-\:8-67] and engage in an aggressive style of attacking play, driving at opponents and breaking defensive lines.[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69][^cite_note-\:10-70] He has received praise for his physical strength,[^cite_note-\:1-71][^cite_note-\:2-72][^cite_note-\:3-6] powerful runs,[^cite_note-\:1-71][^cite_note-\:2-72][^cite_note-\:0-8] tactical intelligence,[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-\:3-6][^cite_note-74] ability to score from a distance,[^cite_note-\:13-7][^cite_note-\:12-75] dribbling[^cite_note-\:3-6][^cite_note-\:0-8] and work ethic.[^cite_note-\:4-9] His strength on the ball and dangerous runs have drawn media comparisons to [Jamal Musiala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamal_Musiala), [Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronaldo_\(Brazilian_footballer\)) and [Wayne Rooney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_Rooney).[^cite_note-76][^cite_note-\:8-67][^cite_note-77] Rogers himself has expressed admiration for Musiala, as well as [Florian Wirtz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florian_Wirtz) and Villa teammate [Jadon Sancho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jadon_Sancho).[^cite_note-\:12-75]

Described throughout 2025 as one of the best young players in the Premier League,[^cite_note-78][^cite_note-79][^cite_note-80][^cite_note-81] media outlets began describing Rogers as one of the best attacking midfielders in the world during the [2025–26 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Aston_Villa_F.C._season), with both *[FourFourTwo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FourFourTwo) and [Sports Illustrated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Illustrated)* ranking him eighth in his position.[^cite_note-\:10-70][^cite_note-\:14-82] *Sports Illustrated* described Rogers as an "anomaly" among attacking midfielders due to his physical strength,[^cite_note-\:14-82] while *[ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN)* included Rogers in twelfth on their 2025 *FC 100* list of the best attacking midfielders in the world, writing that Rogers "has it all – youth, power, intensity, skills, hunger, energy, confidence".[^cite_note-83] In the same year *GiveMeSport* wrote that his ball control was "one of, if not the best in the league".[^cite_note-\:4-9] Owing to Rogers' performances, some media sources opined that Rogers could start for England at the [2026 World Cup](36581929.md) in the number 10 role, potentially keeping Jude Bellingham and [Cole Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Palmer) out of the starting eleven;[^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85][^cite_note-\:12-75][^cite_note-\:13-7] Bellingham went on to be England's starting number 10 while Palmer was not selected in the squad.[^cite_note-86][^cite_note-87]

After scoring, Rogers will often celebrate with an "ice cold" celebration[^cite_note-88] where he crosses his arms and rubs his shoulders, a move later popularised by Rogers' former Manchester City and current Chelsea teammate Cole Palmer; Palmer has attributed this move to Rogers, saying "My boy Morgz did one for Middlesbrough, so I told him I'd do it too if I scored."[^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90][^cite_note-91]
