**Ralph Gonzalez Recto** (Tagalog pronunciation: [\[gonˈsalɛs ˈrɛktɔ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Tagalog); born January 11, 1964) is a Filipino politician who has served as the [executive secretary](7438844.md) on an *[ad interim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad_interim)* basis since 2025 under President [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md). He previously served as the 33rd [secretary of finance](36182226.md) from 2024 to 2025 under President Marcos, the [representative](293605.md) for [Batangas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batangas)' [6th district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batangas's_6th_congressional_district) from 1992 to 2001 and from 2022 to 2024, and as the [House deputy speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Speaker_of_the_House_of_Representatives_of_the_Philippines) from 2022 to 2024.

Recto also previously served three terms in the [Senate](299521.md): from 2001 to 2007 and from 2010 to 2022, and he had served as [president pro tempore of the Senate](8750570.md) and [Senate minority leader](8767721.md). He started his political career as the representative of [Batangas' 4th district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_districts_of_Batangas#4th_District) from 1992 to 2001.

In 2007, Recto lost his [Senate re-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Philippine_Senate_election) bid because, as many analysts believed, he had authored the unpopular EVAT (Expanded Value Added Tax) law. In July 2008, he was appointed to head the [National Economic and Development Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Economic_and_Development_Authority) (NEDA) in the [Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Gloria_Macapagal_Arroyo) administration but resigned from his position in August 2009 in preparation for another run for the Senate in the [2010 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Philippine_Senate_election).[^cite_note-recto_quit-1]

He is a grandson of statesman [Claro M. Recto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claro_M._Recto). His wife is actress-politician [Vilma Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilma_Santos).

## Early life

Ralph Recto comes from a political family. He was born to parents [Rafael Recto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Recto) (1931–2008), a sportsman and politician who participated in the [1986 coup attempt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_the_Manila_Hotel) at the [Manila Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_Hotel), and Carmen Gonzalez-Recto (1941–2013).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

His grandfather, the late nationalist and statesman [Claro M. Recto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claro_M._Recto), was a [senator](299521.md) for several terms. Ralph is one of the three children of Carmen Gonzalez-Recto, alongside Richard "Ricky" Recto Jr. and Roxanne "Plinky" Recto. Ricky served as former [vice governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_governor) of [Batangas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batangas), while Plinky is an actress and television host.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Education

Recto acquired a Bachelor of Science in commerce at the [De La Salle-College of Saint Benilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_La_Salle-College_of_Saint_Benilde), in 1989. At that time, Benilde was still a constituent college of [De La Salle University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_La_Salle_University). In 1990, he entered the College of Public Administration of the [University of the Philippines Diliman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Philippines_Diliman) to study Master of Public Administration, but only earned 36 academic units, hence unable to finish the graduate degree. Recto later took the Strategic Business Economics Program (SBEP) at the [University of Asia and the Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Asia_and_the_Pacific) in 1993, where he acquired a postgraduate Certificate in Business Economics (CBE), which was awarded to participants who exited the program without completing the thesis requirement in lieu of a master’s degree.[^cite_note-no_masters_degrees-7]

Recto took up a 6-day Certificate of Leadership Scholarship Course at the John F. Kennedy School of Government or [Harvard Kennedy School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Kennedy_School) of [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), [Boston](24437894.md), [Massachusetts](1645518.md), [United States](3434750.md) in 1997.

## House of Representatives

### First three terms (1992–2001)

Recto was the youngest-elected member of the House of Representatives during the [9th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_Congress_of_the_Philippines). He is also the youngest elective official of his term. He also holds an unprecedented record in the congressional election history of Batangas for winning in all precincts, barangays, and municipalities, together with [Lipa City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipa_City), in the 1995 and 1998 elections. He garnered 98% of the votes cast in those two elections in the 4th District of [Batangas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batangas).

During his first three terms (from 1992 to 2001) as a member of the [House of Representatives](293605.md), most of his legislative measures enacted into law deal with economic reforms and [poverty alleviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_reduction), among which are the Social Reform and Poverty Alleviation Act; the Philippine Economic Zone Law; Amendment to the Special Economic Zone Law; the Retail Trade Liberalization Law; Regional Headquarters Law; and the Comprehensive Tax Reform Law.

### Return (2022–2024)



Official portrait of Recto during the [19th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19th_Congress_of_the_Philippines)

Upon being term-limited in the Senate, in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Philippine_House_of_Representatives_elections), Recto ran for a comeback to the House of Representatives, this time at the [6th district of Batangas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batangas's_6th_congressional_district), which consists of only the [city of Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipa,_Batangas).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] He then won the election unopposed,[^cite_note-10] succeeding his wife [Vilma Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilma_Santos).[^cite_note-11]

On July 27, 2022, Recto was named as a [House Deputy Speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Speaker_of_the_House_of_Representatives_of_the_Philippines) under the speakership of [Martin Romualdez](45505193.md).[^cite_note-12]

Recto subsequently resigned as representative following his appointment as finance secretary by President [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md) in January 2024. Speaker Martin Romualdez served as the caretaker of Recto's congressional seat until the [2025 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Philippine_House_of_Representatives_elections).[^cite_note-13]

## Senate

### First term (2001–2007)

Recto was elected to the [Senate](299521.md) in 2001, becoming the youngest Senator of the [12th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_Congress_of_the_Philippines) at the age of 37. In the Senate, he chaired the Committees on Ways and Means and on Trade and Industry.

He was co-chairman of the Congressional Oversight Committees on the Proper Implementation of the National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC) and on the Official Development Assistance (ODA). He was also chairman of the Accounts committee.

### Second term (2010–2016)

He was proclaimed as one of the winning senatorial candidates of the [2010 Senate elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Philippine_Senate_election).

On July 22, 2013, at the start of the first day of the 1st Regular Session of the Senate for the 16th Congress, Recto was elected Senate President Pro-Tempore, the second highest post in the Senate and was also the Chairman of the Senate Committee on Science and Technology and member of the bicameral [Commission on Appointments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Appointments).

### Third term (2016–2022)



Official portrait of Ralph Recto as a Philippine Senator, c. 2019

Recto ran for re-election in the [2016 Senate election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Philippine_Senate_election) under the [Koalisyon ng Daang Matuwid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koalisyon_ng_Daang_Matuwid) of [Liberal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Party_\(Philippines\)) standard bearer and presidential nominee [Mar Roxas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mar_Roxas) and won placing 11th in the polls.

On the opening day of the 17th Congress, July 25, 2016, Senator [Francis Escudero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Escudero) nominated Recto for the presidency. Senator [Antonio Trillanes IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Trillanes_IV) seconded Escudero's nomination. During nominal voting, [Koko Pimentel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koko_Pimentel) and Recto voted for each other. After losing the vote, Recto automatically became the Minority Leader. He was joined by Escudero and Trillanes in the minority bloc.[^cite_note-14]

When the Senate underwent a reorganization on February 27, 2017, Recto, after getting 17 votes, was declared Senate President Pro Tempore, replacing [Franklin Drilon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Drilon).[^cite_note-15]

Recto kept his post as Senate President Pro Tempore in the 18th Congress.[^cite_note-16]

In September 2018, Recto left the Liberal Party to re-join the [Nacionalista Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nacionalista_Party).[^cite_note-NP-17]

## National Economic and Development Authority (2008–2009)

After his failed bid for re-election to the Senate in 2007, Recto joined the board of the [Union Bank of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Bank_of_the_Philippines).[^cite_note-18]

He is said to have been offered the position of [Secretary of Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Finance_\(Philippines\)) but it was subsequently denied by [Malacañang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaca%C3%B1ang).[^cite_note-19]

President [Gloria Macapagal Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Macapagal_Arroyo), on July 23, 2008, named him as the new director-general of [National Economic and Development Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Economic_and_Development_Authority), replacing Augusto Santos, acting NEDA chief.[^cite_note-20] On August 11, 2009, he resigned as NEDA secretary and Presidential Adviser for Economic Planning, in preparation for another run for Senate in the [2010 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Philippine_general_election).[^cite_note-recto_quit-1]

## Cabinet of Bongbong Marcos

### Finance secretary (2024–2025)



Recto taking the oath as Finance Secretary before President [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md) on [Malacañan Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaca%C3%B1ang_Palace), January 12, 2024.

On January 11, 2024, within his 60th birthday, Recto was appointed by President [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md) as the secretary of finance.[^cite_note-21] He took his oath of office on January 12, 2024, succeeding [Benjamin Diokno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Diokno), while eventually vacating the posts of the House Deputy Speaker and the House Representative of the 6th district of Batangas, simultaneously.[^cite_note-22] The [Commission on Appointments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Appointments) confirmed Recto's appointment as finance secretary on March 13, 2024.[^cite_note-23]

On May 22, 2025, President Marcos ordered members of his cabinet to tender their [courtesy resignations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Philippine_cabinet_reshuffle) in the aftermath of the [May 12, 2025 midterm elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Philippine_general_election).[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] Recto complied to this order.[^cite_note-26] He will continue to serve as secretary until President Marcos accepts his resignation, if the President does so.[^cite_note-27]

### Executive secretary (2025–present)

On November 17, 2025, presidential press officer [Claire Castro](77955986.md) announced that Recto would replace the resigned [Lucas Bersamin](22247470.md) as the executive secretary. Castro also announced that [Frederick Go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Go), who had served as Special Assistant to the President for Investment and Economic Affairs, would replace Recto as the finance secretary.[^cite_note-28] Since the announcement, Recto has been serving as the executive secretary on an *[ad interim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad_interim)* basis pending confirmation by the [Commission on Appointments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Appointments).

## Personal life

He is married to actress and former [Batangas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batangas) representative [Vilma Santos-Recto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilma_Santos), with whom he has one son, incumbent Batangas representative [Ryan Recto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Recto). He is the stepfather of actor and host [Luis Manzano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Manzano).
