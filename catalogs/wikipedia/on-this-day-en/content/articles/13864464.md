The **Cowry Forces for an Emerging Benin** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Forces Cauris pour un Bénin émergent*, FCBE) is a political party of [Benin](3459.md), formed by supporters of former president [Yayi Boni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yayi_Boni). In the [parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beninese_parliamentary_election,_2007) held on 31 March 2007, the party won 35 out of 83 seats. The party expanded its plurality to 41 seats in the [2011 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beninese_parliamentary_election,_2011) that followed the contested [reelection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beninese_presidential_election,_2011) of Yayi Boni as president.[^cite_note-3]

After President Boni Yayi had served maximum two five-year terms, [Lionel Zinsou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Zinsou), the candidate for Cowry Forces for an Emerging Benin (FCBE), lost the March 2016 presidential [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Beninese_presidential_election) to [Patrice Talon](49884967.md).[^cite_note-4]

A controversial new electoral code introduced in July 2018 saw the party fail to be validated to run in the [2019 Beninese parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Beninese_parliamentary_election).
