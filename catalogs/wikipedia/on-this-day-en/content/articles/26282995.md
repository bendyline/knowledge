There is a large population of **Moroccans in Italy**. are [Italians citizens and nationals](385155.md) [ethnic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_group), of the [Italy](14532.md) with [Italian citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_citizenship) and an [Italian passport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_passport) of [Moroccan](5639315.md) descent, immigrants from [Morocco](19291.md) to [Italy](14532.md) and their descendants are the third largest [ethnic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_group) in [Italy](14532.md) after [Romanians in Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanians_in_Italy) and [Albanians in Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanians_in_Italy). who come from various [ethnic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_group), form a distinct community in [Italy](14532.md) and part of the wider [Moroccan diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moroccan_diaspora). They are widely referred to as *Italiano-Marocchini* in [Italian](14708.md). According to the [Italian National Institute of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_National_Institute_of_Statistics), the country was home to 420,650 Moroccan immigrants in 2017.[^cite_note-3] The equivalent figure in 2014 was 454,773, indicating a decrease.[^cite_note-4]

## Religion

In the years 2011 and 2012 the [ISTAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_of_Transport_Aircraft_Trading) made a survey regarding the religious affiliation among the immigrants in Italy, the religion of the Moroccan people in Italy were as follows:[^cite_note-ISTATreligion-2]

-  [Muslims](1228799.md): 99.0%

-  [Christianity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity_in_Italy): 0.3%

- [Non religious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-religious): 0.5%

- Other religions: 0.3%
