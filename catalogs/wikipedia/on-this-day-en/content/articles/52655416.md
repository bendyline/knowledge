**Tibú Airport** (([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **TIB**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **SKTB**)) is a public airport serving the town and municipality of [Tibú](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tib%C3%BA), located in the [Norte de Santander Department](14691168.md) of [Colombia](5222.md).&#x20;

The airport is situated on the southern edge of the town, along the east bank of the [Caño Campo Cinco River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ca%C3%B1o_Campo_Cinco_River?action=edit\&redlink=1). Both the town and the airport lie approximately 7 km (4.3 mi) west of the border with [Venezuela](32374.md).
