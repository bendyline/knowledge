**Daniel** (Romanian pronunciation: [\[daniˈel\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Romanian); born **Dan Ilie Ciobotea** [\[ˈdan iˈli.e tʃjoˈbote̯a\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Romanian) on 22 July 1951) is the [Patriarch](1795665.md) of the [Romanian Orthodox Church](384458.md). The elections took place on 12 September 2007. Daniel won with a majority of 95 votes out of 161 against [Bartolomeu Anania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolomeu_Anania). He was officially enthroned on 30 September 2007 in the [Patriarchal Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Patriarchal_Cathedral) in [Bucharest](36877.md). As such, his official title is "Archbishop of Bucharest, Metropolitan of Muntenia and Dobrogea, Locum tenens of the throne of [Caesarea of Cappadocia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesarea_in_Cappadocia), Patriarch of All Romania".

## Biography

Daniel Ciobotea was born in the village of Dobrești, [Bara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bara,_Timi%C8%99) Commune, [Timiș County](269327.md), as the third son in the family of teacher Alexie and Stela Ciobotea.

He followed the [Primary School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Romania) in his home village (1958–1962) and the [Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Romania) in Lăpușnic village (1962–1966), [Timiș County](269327.md). In 1966 he began the High School courses in [Buziaș](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buzia%C8%99), which he then continued in [Lugoj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugoj), at the "Coriolan Brediceanu" [High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_School) (1967–1970).

After passing his Baccalaureate exam, he then joined the university-level Theological Institute in [Sibiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sibiu) (1970–1974), where he obtained his degree in [Theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theology) with specialisation in the [New Testament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Testament).[^cite_note-MyUser_Adevarul.ro_November_4_2015c-1]

During the period 1974–1976 he followed the doctoral courses at the Theological Institute in [Bucharest](36877.md), Systematic Theology Section, under the supervision of [Dumitru Stăniloae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumitru_St%C4%83niloae); he continued his studies abroad: two years at the Protestant Theology Faculty of the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg) and another two years at the [Albert Ludwig University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Ludwigs_University_of_Freiburg) in [Freiburg im Breisgau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freiburg_im_Breisgau), Catholic Theology Faculty.



Patriarch Daniel of Romania and other leaders of the [Eastern Orthodox Church](10186.md) with Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs of Greece, [Ioannis Amanatidis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ioannis_Amanatidis_\(politician\)) on the completion of the work of the [Holy and Great Council of the Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan-Orthodox_Council) in [Kolymvari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolymvari), [Crete](6591.md), [Greece](12108.md).

His studies abroad prompted critics to argue that he collaborated with the [Securitate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Securitate), because during the rule of communist president [Nicolae Ceauşescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolae_Ceau%C5%9Fescu), travelling and studying outside the [Eastern Bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Bloc) was almost impossible for those who did not send informative notes to the Securitate. [Mircea Dinescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mircea_Dinescu) of the [CNSAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNSAS?action=edit\&redlink=1), however, said that his Securitate file was burnt in December 1989 and as such, it cannot be known whether he collaborated with it or not.

On 15 June 1979 he defended a Doctoral Dissertation at the Protestant Theology Faculty in [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg), entitled: "Réflexion et vie chrétiennes aujourd'hui. Essai sur le rapport entre la théologie et la spiritualité, 424 pp." (*Christian reflection and life today. Essay on the relationship between theology and spirituality, 424 pp*) The dissertation was prepared under the supervision of two well-known French professors: [Gerard Ziegwald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerard_Ziegwald?action=edit\&redlink=1) and André Benoit and it received high praise. Thus he becomes [Doctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_\(title\)) of the [Strasbourg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg_University).

A more developed version of his doctoral dissertation was prepared under the guidance of his mentor, [Dumitru Stăniloae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumitru_St%C4%83niloae) and it was successfully defended on 31 October 1980 at the Orthodox Theological Institute in Bucharest under the title: "Teologie și spiritualitate creștină. Raportul dintre ele și situația actuală" (*Theology and Christian spirituality. The relationship between them and the current situation*).

Between 1980 and 1986, Dan Ilie Ciobotea served as lecturer at the Ecumenical Institute in Bossey, Switzerland, while from 1986 to 1988 he acted as the institute's Adjunct Director.

In 1987, he entered the [monastic life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monastic_life) in the [Sihăstria Monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sih%C4%83stria_Monastery?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Romania and took the name Monk Daniel, having as his "monastic godfather" the well-known spiritual Archimandrite [Cleopa Ilie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleopa_Ilie).

In 1992, he started teaching theology at the Dumitru Staniloae Faculty of Orthodox Theology in the Al. I Cuza University in Iași.

Between 1 September 1988 – 1 March 1990, he was patriarchal counsellor and director of the Contemporary Theology and Ecumenical Dialogue Section in the Romanian Patriarchate.

In early 1990, he belonged to the short-lived Group for Reflection on Church Renewal where ironically he shared the same views for church reform with [Bartolomeu Anania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolomeu_Anania), who would become his rival in the race for patriarch in 2007.

In March 1990, Daniel was ordained as suffragan bishop of the Archbishopric of Timișoara. Three months later, in June 1990, he was appointed Metropolitan of Moldova and Bukovina.

He founded the influential Radio Trinitas in 1998. Daniel has also been questioned when using a paint-roller dipped in Holy Oil to bless new Radio and Television stations.[^cite_note-MyUser_BBC_November_4_2015c-2]

Between 5–8 October 2005, the [Metropolitanate of Moldova and Bucovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolis_of_Moldavia_and_Bukovina) co-organised with the Centre for Post-communist Studies (Canada, directed by Dr. Lavinia Stan and Dr. Lucian Turcescu) a prestigious international symposium on [Church and State in Post-communist Eastern Europe](http://www.upelsinka.com/English/romania/main.htm) [Archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20211113230147/http://www.upelsinka.com/English/romania/main.htm) 13 November 2021 at the [Wayback Machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayback_Machine).

On 12 September 2007, he was elected to take the lead of the [Romanian Orthodox Church](384458.md), after leading in all three rounds, ahead of the other candidates ([Bartolomeu Anania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolomeu_Anania), Metropolitan of [Cluj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca) and Ioan Selejean, Bishop of [Covasna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covasna) and [Harghita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harghita)).

Although appreciated for his intellectual skills and diplomacy, Daniel is regarded as a renovationist by traditionalists who accuse him of supporting [ecumenism](146656.md) (considered a "heresy of our times"), and of planning to forcibly unite the Romanian Orthodox Church with the [Roman Catholic Church](606848.md),[^cite_note-3] although he refuted the allegations in a public statement.[^cite_note-4]

- 1988 – *Patriarchal Counselor*, Director of the *Sector of Contemporary Theology and Ecumenical Dialogue*;

- 1990 (March) – elected and ordained Vicarius Bishop of the *Archbishopric of Timișoara*;

- 1990 (June) – elected as Archbishop of [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ia%C8%99i) and *Metropolitan of Moldova and Bukovina*;

- since 1992 – Theology professor at the *Orthodox Theology Faculty "Dumitru Stăniloae"* of the *"Alexandru Ioan Cuza" University in Iași*;

- Representative of the *National Synodal Commission for Religious Education* Bucharest;

- President of the *Theology and Liturgics Commission of the Holy Synod of the Romanian Orthodox Church*

- Honorary Member of the *National Commission for Historical Monuments* Bucharest;

- Member of the *Central and Executive Committee of the Ecumenical Council of Churches* ([Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva), 1991–1998);

- Member of the *Presidium of Superior and Central Committee of the European Church Conference* (since 1990);

- Vicepresident of the *Second General Meeting of the European Churches Conference* ([Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz), 1997);

- Full Member of the *International Academy of Religious Sciences* in [Brussels](3708.md) (2000).
