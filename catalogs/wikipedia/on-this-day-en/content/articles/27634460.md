The **Teltow Canal**, also known as the [**Teltowkanal**](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/1/1d/De-Teltowkanal.ogg/De-Teltowkanal.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> in [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language), is a [canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canal) to the south of [Berlin](3354.md), the capital city of [Germany](11867.md). The canal lies in both the states of Berlin and [Brandenburg](3765.md), and at points forms the boundary between the two. It takes its name from the [region of Teltow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teltow_\(region\)) and town of [Teltow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teltow) which lie on its course.[^cite_note-bsiwg-1] The canal was constructed between 1900 and 1906, when it was opened by [Wilhelm II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_II,_German_Emperor).[^cite_note-2]

The canal is 37.83 kilometres (23.51 mi) in length and links the [River Havel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Havel) near the city of [Potsdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potsdam) with the [River Dahme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahme_\(river\)) near [Köpenick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6penick) in Berlin's eastern suburbs. The Dahme provides a link to the [Oder-Spree Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oder-Spree_Canal), and hence to the [River Oder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oder) and [Poland](22936.md). Whilst the Dahme is a tributary of the [River Spree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spree_\(river\)), itself a navigable tributary of the Havel, the Teltow Canal offers the advantage of bypassing the centre of Berlin, with its heavy river traffic.[^cite_note-bsiwg-1]

At its western end, the Teltow Canal incorporates the [Griebnitzsee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griebnitzsee) lake which links to the Havel in Potsdam. The [Griebnitz Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griebnitz_Canal) provides a second link to the Havel from the eastern end of the Griebnitzsee. Towards its eastern end, the Teltow Canal is linked to the [Landwehr Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landwehr_Canal) by the [Neukölln Ship Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuk%C3%B6lln_Ship_Canal), while the [Britz Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britz_Canal) connects the Teltow Canal with the Spree.[^cite_note-bsiwg-1]

There is only one [lock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lock_\(water_transport\)) on the canal, at [Kleinmachnow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kleinmachnow). The canal is navigable by boats with a [draught](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draft_\(hull\)) of up to 1.75 metres (5 ft 9 in) and with a maximum bridge clearance of 4.4 metres (14 ft).[^cite_note-bsiwg-1]
