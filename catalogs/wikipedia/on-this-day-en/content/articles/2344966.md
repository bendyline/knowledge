**Quimistán** is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Honduras) in [Santa Bárbara Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_B%C3%A1rbara_Department,_Honduras) of [Honduras](13394.md). It covers an area of 745.3 km<sup>2</sup> (287.8 sq mi) and had a population of 47,993 inhabitants according to the 2013 national census. It is the most populous municipality in Santa Bárbara department.

## History

The region was part of the region conquered by the conquistador [Pedro de Alvarado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_de_Alvarado) in the early 16th century CE. In 1791, it is mentioned as part of the curato (ecclesiastical jurisdiction) of Petoa. Later, it was part of the San Pedro partido, which later became [San Pedro Sula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro_Sula). The word Quimistan is from Mexican roots and means "Place of mice or spies".[^cite_note-Edu-1][^cite_note-Red-2]

## Geography

Quimistan is located in the department of [Santa Bárbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_B%C3%A1rbara_Department,_Honduras) in Honduras.[^cite_note-Red-2][^cite_note-CP-3] It borders the municipalities of [Petoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petoa) and [San Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Marcos,_Santa_B%C3%A1rbara) to the south, [Azacualpa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azacualpa) and [Macuelizo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macuelizo) to the west, San Pedro Sula to the west, and [Omoa](339891.md) to the north.[^cite_note-Edu-1][^cite_note-Red-2] The municipality covers an area of 745.3 km<sup>2</sup> (287.8 sq mi).[^cite_note-Edu-1]

## Demographics

At the time of the 2013 census, Quimistán had a total population of 47,993 inhabitants, of which 24,158 (50.3%) were male and 23,835 (49.7%) were female.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-INE2013-4]<sup>: 20 </sup> It is the most populous municipality in Santa Bárbara department.[^cite_note-CP-3] Of the total population, 14,445 (30.1%) was classified as urban and 33,549 (69.9%) lived in rural.[^cite_note-INE2013-4]<sup>: 20 </sup>

Of the total population, 6.16% were classified as [Indigenous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_of_the_Americas) and rest as non-indigenous.[^cite_note-CP-3] By broad age group, 37.6% of the population were aged 0–14, 57.6% were aged 15–64, and 4.8% were aged 65 and over. The median age was 20.3 years and the mean age was 24.8 years. Among the population aged 15 and over (29,951 persons), the illiteracy rate was recorded as 17.6% as against 22.2% for the department.[^cite_note-INE2013-4]<sup>: 19 </sup> The municipality had 10,778 occupied private dwellings,[^cite_note-INE2013-4]<sup>: 34 </sup> with an average household size of 4.3 persons.[^cite_note-INE2013-4]<sup>: 19 </sup>

## Culture

The municipality celebrates its annual festival in June in honour of its patron [San Juan Bautista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_the_Baptist).[^cite_note-Edu-1][^cite_note-Red-2]
