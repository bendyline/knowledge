**Azruddin Intiaz Mohamed** (born 1 March 1987) is a Guyanese businessman and politician. As the leader of [We Invest in Nationhood](80972304.md) (WIN), he has served as [leader of the Opposition](71315209.md) since January 2026.

## Early life and education

Mohamed is from an [Indo-Guyanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Guyanese) Muslim family.

## Career

On 11 June 2024, the [United States Department of State](31975.md) imposed sanctions on Mohamed and his father, Nazar Mohamed, for acts of public corruption and the avoidance of more than US\$50 million in duty taxes on gold exports.[^cite_note-1]

Mohamed launched his presidential campaign with We Invest in Nationhood in May 2025.[^cite_note-2] In response, United States Ambassador [Nicole Theriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_D._Theriot) warned that the U.S. would cut economic ties with a potential Mohamed administration, stating that the U.S. "would have to make sure that we didn't work with him specifically".[^cite_note-3] Among his campaign promises was a pledge to donate his presidential salary to charity.[^cite_note-4]

Mohamed was arrested by Guyanese police on 31 October 2025, following an extradition request by the U.S. on the basis of a federal grand jury indictment in Florida.[^cite_note-5]

Since 2025, Mohamed has been the leader of We Invest in Nationhood, which was formed to contest the [2025 general election](68798945.md). The party achieved second place in the election, and in January 2026, Mohamed was elected leader of the Opposition.[^cite_note-6]
