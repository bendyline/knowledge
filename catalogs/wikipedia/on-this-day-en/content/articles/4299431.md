**Susch** (Swiss Standard German: [\[suːʃ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Standard_German); formally **Süs**) is a village and former [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Switzerland) in the district of [Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inn_District,_Switzerland) in the [Swiss](26748.md) [canton](180752.md) of [Graubünden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graub%C3%BCnden). On 1 January 2015 the former municipalities of [Lavin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavin) and Susch merged into the municipality of [Zernez](1164846.md).[^cite_note-1]

The [Flüela Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fl%C3%BCela_Pass) connects Susch with [Davos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davos).
