**Guayas** (English: [/ˈɡwɑːjəs/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English), Spanish: [\[ˈɡwaʝas\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/b/bd/ES-pe\_-\_Guayas.ogg/ES-pe\_-\_Guayas.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/b/bd/ES-pe_-_Guayas.ogg/ES-pe_-_Guayas.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a coastal [province](576127.md) in [Ecuador](9334.md). It is bordered to the west by [Manabí](728376.md), [Santa Elena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Elena_Province,_Ecuador), and the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md) (as the [Gulf of Guayaquil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Guayaquil)); to the east by [Los Ríos](728369.md), [Bolívar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bol%C3%ADvar_Province,_Ecuador), [Chimborazo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chimborazo_Province), [Cañar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ca%C3%B1ar_Province), and [Azuay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azuay_Province); to the north by [Los Ríos](728369.md) and [Bolívar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bol%C3%ADvar_Province,_Ecuador); and to the south by [El Oro](696562.md) and the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md). The provincial capital is [Guayaquil](215586.md), Ecuador’s largest city and main port.

With a population of over 4 million people, it is the most populous [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province) in Ecuador. In terms of area it is the [seventh largest province in the country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Ecuadorian_provinces_by_area).&#x20;

Guayas' natural terrain is very diverse. The province has no elevations, except for the [Coastal Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_Range_\(Ecuador\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), which starts in Guayaquil and goes to [Manabí](728376.md). The areas west of the Coastal Range are [desertic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert), with an average temperature of 23 °C. The areas east of the range belong to the [Guayas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guayas_River) [Watershed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drainage_basin). They are quite [humid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid) and [fertile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fertility_\(soil\)), especially in the north of the province, with an average temperature of 30 °C in the humid [season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Season) (December–May) and 25 °C in the dry season (June–November).

### Hydrography

The most important river in the province is the [Daule River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daule_River), which flows from the north to join the [Babahoyo River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babahoyo_River) to form the Guayas river. The province is part of the largest [river basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drainage_basin) in [South America](26769.md) west of the [Andes Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andes_Mountains).

### Roads

Guayas has its own system for numbering [roads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roads). However, this system is unknown to most residents, so it is not regularly used.

The inter-provincial roads are also numbered with the national system. Even routes travel north–south; odd routes travel east–west. The inter-provincial roads that cross the province are the following:

- [Ecuador Highway 15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuador_Highway_15) (Vía del Pacífico; Pacific Way)

- Ecuador Highway 25 (Troncal de la Costa; Coastal Main Way)

- Ecuador Highway 40 (Transversal Austral; Austral Crossing Way)

### Pre-Hispanic cultures

The native culture living in Guayas is the [Huancavilca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huancavilca) culture. Exactly before the European discovery of America, the Huancavilca Culture was living in the province. Their descendants make up a large part of the population of the province.

### Spanish conquest and independence

Guayaquil was founded on August 14, 1534 (its [foundation](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/foundation) is celebrated on July 25). During the [Spanish](26667.md) [conquest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_conquest_of_the_Inca_Empire), Guayaquil became one of the most important [ports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port) in [South America](26769.md). The city became [free](62450.md) on October 9, 1820, and the Guayaquil [Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_\(subnational_entity\)) (one of the original subdivisions of Ecuador) was founded soon afterwards. It consisted of the [Manabí Province](728376.md), and the Guayaquil Province, which was later renamed Guayas. The Guayaquil Province included territory of what now is Peruvian [Tumbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumbes_Province), and today's [Los Ríos](728369.md) and [El Oro](696562.md). The provinces were separated from Guayas in 1860 and 1884, respectively.

### Urbanization

Guayas is the most populous province in the country. In recent decades, there has been a massive exit from [rural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural) areas to the main cities (especially [Guayaquil](215586.md)). This has created a problem in Guayaquil, as most of the migrants move to municipal areas, creating [shantytowns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantytown), with no [services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_services) like [water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water) or [electricity](9550.md).

## Demographics

Guayas is the most populous province in the country. The estimated population of the province in 2003 was about 3,360,000 people. A large percentage of the [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_\(human_biology\)) are *[mestizos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestizo)*, i.e. descendants of both [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_colonization_of_the_Americas) and [indigenous](18429324.md) peoples, there are also big communities of people that descend from [Italians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_people), [Lebanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_people) and [German](152735.md) people.

Ethnic groups as of the [Ecuadorian census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_census) of 2010:[^cite_note-3]

- [Mestizo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestizo) 67.5%

- [Montubio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montubio) 11.3%

- [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Latin_American) 9.8%

- [Afro-Ecuadorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Ecuadorian) 9.7%

- [Indigenous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_of_Ecuador) 1.3%

- Other 0.5%

## Population Growth

Guayas serves as the primary demographic engine of Ecuador, maintaining its position as the nation's most populous province through decades of explosive growth. In 1950, the region held a population of 612,417, which surged to over 1.4 million by 1970 as industrialization and maritime trade centered around Guayaquil accelerated.



Guayas Pop Chart

By the turn of the millennium, the population had more than doubled again to 3,188,698. This rapid expansion continued into the 21st century, with the province crossing the four-million mark by 2020 ["Guayas Population"](https://www.city-facts.com/guayas/population). Retrieved 2026-03-16.. The 2022 census confirmed a total of 4,391,923 residents, solidifying Guayas as a critical hub of urban and economic development in the coastal region.
