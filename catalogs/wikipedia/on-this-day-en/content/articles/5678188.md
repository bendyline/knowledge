The **23rd Academy Awards** were held on March 29, 1951, honoring the [films of 1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_in_film). *[All About Eve](41881.md)* received a record 14 nominations, surpassing the previous record of 13 set by *[Gone with the Wind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gone_with_the_Wind_\(film\))* in [1939](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_Academy_Awards). It won six Oscars, including [Best Picture](61702.md), and earned writer/director [Joseph L. Mankiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_L._Mankiewicz) his second consecutive [Best Director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Director) and [Best Adapted Screenplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Adapted_Screenplay) awards, the only time such a feat has been accomplished.

*All About Eve* was the second film, after *[Mrs. Miniver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mrs._Miniver)* ([1942](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15th_Academy_Awards)), to receive five acting nominations. It was the first to receive multiple nominations in two acting categories, and the (to date) only film to receive four female acting nominations—two each for [Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress) and [Best Supporting Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress). None was successful, losing to Judy Holliday in *[Born Yesterday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_Yesterday_\(1950_film\))* and Josephine Hull in *[Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_\(1950_film\))*, respectively.

[José Ferrer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Ferrer), then under investigation from the [House Un-American Activities Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_Un-American_Activities_Committee) over suspected Communist ties, won the Best Actor award despite being given very little chances to win.[^cite_note-Oscars_'50_Notes-1] [Marlene Dietrich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlene_Dietrich) nearly stole the show by wearing an apparently-painted-on dress that displayed her legs and figure.[^cite_note-Oscars_'50_Notes-1]

*[Sunset Boulevard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunset_Boulevard_\(film\))* was the fifth film with [nominations in every acting category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_films_with_all_four_Academy_Award_acting_nominations), and the second not to win any of them (after *[My Man Godfrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Man_Godfrey)* in [1936](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_Academy_Awards)).
