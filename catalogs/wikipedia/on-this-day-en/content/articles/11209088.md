**Kafr Qara** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): كَفْر قَرَع, [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): כַּפְר קַרִע; is an [Arab city in Israel](492331.md) 22 miles (35 km) southeast of [Haifa](55606.md). In 2024 its population was 21,066.[^cite_note-Israelpopulations-1] Kafr Qara is part of the [Triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle_\(Israel\)). It is located in the [Wadi Ara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadi_Ara) region, northwest of the [Green Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Line_\(Israel\)). Most of the inhabitants are [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim). It is governed by a [local council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_council_\(Israel\)). Kafr Qara encompasses 7811 dunams of land.[^cite_note-3] WAC, an independent [labor association](17626.md), has its headquarters there.[^cite_note-haaretz-4]

## History

### Mamluk Sultanate

An early *[defter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defter)* entry noted that Kafr Qara had been incorporated into the "Diwan of the [Circassian sultanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burji_dynasty)" after it had been seized by ‘the [Shaykhs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaykhs) of the mountain of [Nablus](233369.md)’.[^cite_note-5]

### Ottoman Empire

Kafr Qara' was originally located on a rocky outcrop near [Kafr Thilith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kafr_Thulth) in the West Bank (see: [Uyun Kafr Qari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uyun_Kafr_Qari')').[^cite_note-6] It was incorporated into the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md) in 1517 with the rest of [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_\(region\)), and in the *defter* no. 610, which was written soon after 1540, the revenue of Kafr Qara was designated to an [endowment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waqf) in [Jerusalem](16043.md); the [Madrasah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrasah) [Al-Uthmaniyya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Uthmaniyya_Madrasa_\(Jerusalem\)). The whole of the revenue of Kafr Qara, a total of 3,400 [aspers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e) annually, belonged to this endowment.[^cite_note-7]

In 1859 the population, having moved to the current location in Wadi 'Arab, was 450 people, who cultivated 32 [feddans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feddan) of land.[^cite_note-SWP42-8] In 1883, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s *[Survey of Western Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine)* (SWP) described *Kefr Kara* as a "good-sized stone village on high ground, with a [well](15105978.md) to the east, and caves."[^cite_note-SWP42-8]

A population list from about 1887 showed that *Kiryat Kefr Kara* had about 705 inhabitants, all Muslim.[^cite_note-9]

### British Mandate

In the [1922 census of Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1922_census_of_Palestine) conducted by the [British Mandate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine) authorities, *Kufr Qara* had a population 776; 767 Muslims and 9 Christians,[^cite_note-Census1922-10] where the Christians were 7 Roman Catholics and 2 Maronites.[^cite_note-Census1922b-11] The population had increased by the [1931 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1931_census_of_Palestine) to 1,109; 4 Christians and 1,105 Muslims, in 198 houses.[^cite_note-Census1931-12]

In the [1945 statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_Statistics,_1945), Kafr Qara had a population of 1,510 Muslims,[^cite_note-1945p14-13] who owned 14,543 dunams of land.[^cite_note-14] Of this, 227 dunams were for plantations and irrigable land, 11,516 for cereals,[^cite_note-15] while 25 dunams were built-up (urban) land.[^cite_note-16]

### Israel

In 2007, Qara held the record for doctors relative to population size, with around 14.8 doctors per 1,000 citizens.[^cite_note-17]

## Education

In September 2003, a group of local parents founded a bilingual, multicultural elementary school in Kafr Qara, named [Hand in Hand – Bridge over the Wadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hand_in_Hand_%E2%80%93_Bridge_over_the_Wadi), or "Bridge over the Wadi". Kafr Qara high school, established in 1970 as a vocational school, is now a comprehensive high school for 10th–12th graders from Kafr Qara and environs. The school has participated in multicultural projects such as *Jitli*, and offers a joint leadership program for Arab and Jewish teenagers.[^cite_note-cac.at-18]

Kafr Qara holds the highest record for doctors relative to population size in the country, around 14.8 doctors per 1,000 citizens (2007, with more than 50 medicine student back then), Kafr Qara known as well for recording a high rate of academics and master's degree holders.[^cite_note-19]

## Voting Patterns

In the [2022 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Israeli_legislative_election), 97 percent of voters in Kafr Qara voted for the Arab parties, while 2 percent voted for the parties of the center-left coalition led by [Yair Lapid](2448072.md) and 1 percent voted for the parties of the right-wing coalition led by [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md). Among the voters for the Arab parties, 52 percent voted for [Ra'am](39385583.md), while 28 percent voted for [Balad](44494596.md) and 20 percent voted for the [Hadash–Ta'al](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadash%E2%80%93Ta'al) list.[^cite_note-22]
