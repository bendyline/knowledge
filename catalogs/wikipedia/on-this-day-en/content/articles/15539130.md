**Vero** ([Corsican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corsican_language): *Veru*) is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in the [Corse-du-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corse-du-Sud) [department](38523.md) of [France](5843419.md) on the island of [Corsica](5714828.md).
