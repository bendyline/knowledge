The **Western Region of Uganda** is one of four [regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Uganda) in the country of [Uganda](31816.md).[^cite_note-GeoHive-1] As of [Uganda](31816.md)'s 2014 [census](6889.md), the Western region's population was 8,874,862.[^cite_note-CityPopulation-2]

## Subregions

As of 2010, the western Region contained four Political Subregions,

From North to South;

1- [Bunyoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunyoro) Sub-region

2- Rwenzori Sub-region aka Greater Tooro

3- [Ankole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankole) Subregion and

4- [Kigezi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kigezi) Subregion

## Geography



Village in Western Uganda

The Western Region borders with the [Democratic Republic of Congo](76762.md) in the west and the Northern tip of [Rwanda](25645.md) as well as [Tanzania](30118.md) in the South.

It is home to the [Banyakitara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banyakitara?action=edit\&redlink=1) i.e [Banyoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banyoro), [Batooro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batooro), [Babwisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babwisi?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Songora people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songora_people), [Banyankore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banyankore), [Bakiga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakiga). And [Bakonzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakonzo), [Bamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amba_people), [Bafumbira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bafumbira), e.t.c

## Tourist attractions in Western Uganda

Western Uganda is home to a number of national parks and protected areas, including:

- [Bwindi Impenetrable National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bwindi_Impenetrable_National_Park) in south western Uganda, home to [mountain gorillas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_gorilla) and other primates like [chimpanzees](7844.md), L’Hoest monkeys, black and white [colobus monkeys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colobus_monkey). The area is also home to [forest elephants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_elephants) and [bushpigs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bushpig).

  [^cite_note-19]

  &#x20;The park is located over three districts: [Kanungu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanungu), [Rubanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubanda_District) and [Kisoro District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kisoro_District).

  [^cite_note-20]

- [Queen Elizabeth National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_National_Park) is in western Uganda and is the second largest national park in the country and one of the oldest. The park is made up of wetlands, woodlands, savannah plains and forests. The park is located in [Kasese District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasese_District), with parts in [Rubirizi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubirizi_District), [Kanungu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanungu_District), and [Kamwengye districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamwenge_District). It borders the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md) (DRC).

  [^cite_note-21]

- [Mgahinga Gorilla National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mgahinga_Gorilla_National_Park) is the smallest national park located in south western Uganda, located in [Kisoro District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kisoro_District). It was founded in 1991. The park is also home to mountain gorillas, along with Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. The park is home to the [golden monkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_monkey), also found in [Volcanoes National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanoes_National_Park). Mountains in the park include [Mount Sabinyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Sabinyo) and [Mount Muhavura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Muhavura) which are part of the [Virunga Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virunga_Range), which continues into [Rwanda](25645.md) and the DRC.

  [^cite_note-22]

- [Kibale Forest National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kibale_Forest_National_Park) is located in [Kibaale District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kibaale_District). The park is home to chimpanzees, with the largest number and diversity in [East Africa](260987.md).
