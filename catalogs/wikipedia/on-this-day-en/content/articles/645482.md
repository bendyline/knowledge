The **National League North** is a professional [association football](10568.md) league in England. National League North is the second division of the [National Leagues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_\(English_football\)) and step 2 of the [NLS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_System_\(football\)) and sixth-highest tier overall in the [English football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system), after the [Premier League](11250.md), the [EFL leagues](14419588.md) and the [National League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_\(division\)) and is contested by 24 clubs.

National League North consists of teams mostly located in [Northern England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_England), the [English Midlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midlands) and [East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Anglia). In addition, it can include a small number of teams from the northern-most parts of the South West and South East, as well as currently containing a team from [Wales](69894.md). Since the start of the [2015–16 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_National_League), the league has been known as the National League North.[^cite_note-name_change-1]

## History

The Conference North was introduced in 2004 as part of a major restructuring of English [non-League football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-League_football).[^cite_note-Football_Conference_-_History-2] The champions are automatically promoted to the National League. A second promotion place goes to the winners of play-offs involving the teams finishing in second to seventh place (expanded from four to six teams in the 2017–18 season).[^cite_note-3] The three bottom clubs are relegated to Step 3 leagues. Teams from this division, as well as from the National League South, enter the [FA Cup](11237.md) at the Second Qualifying Round.

The National League North was scheduled to expand to 24 teams in 2021.[^cite_note-non-league-4][^cite_note-5] Due to the [COVID-19 pandemic in England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_England), the 2020–21 National League North season was curtailed and voided after written resolutions were put to a vote. No teams were relegated.[^cite_note-statement-6] Expansion was implemented before the 2022–23 season, when the bottom club was relegated and four promoted from Step 3.[^cite_note-7]
