The **Israeli Presidential Medal of Honour** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): עיטור נשיא מדינת ישראל, *Itur Nesi Medinat Yisra'el*), formerly known as the **President's Medal** is the highest civil medal given by the [President](153049.md) of the [State of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Israel).

## History

The President's Medal was first presented on March 1, 2012, in [Beit HaNassi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_HaNassi) by President of Israel, [Shimon Peres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimon_Peres). In total, he awarded it to 26 people "who have made an outstanding contribution to the State of Israel or to humanity, through their talents, services, or in any other form." The medal, based on the French [Légion d'Honneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A9gion_d'Honneur), was designed by Yossi Matityahu and includes a verse from the [Book of Samuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Books_of_Samuel), which loosely translates as "head and shoulders \[above the rest].[^cite_note-1]

President [Isaac Herzog](4645588.md) revived the institution of the medal in 2022, awarding it for the first time since Shimon Peres in 2014. Under Herzog, the decoration was renamed the Israeli Presidential Medal of Honour. Recipients include Czech President [Miloš Zeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milo%C5%A1_Zeman) and U.S. President [Joe Biden](145422.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
