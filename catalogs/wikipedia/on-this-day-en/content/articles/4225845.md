**Kasaï-Central** is one of the 21 [provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo](3984115.md) created in the [2015 repartitioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_the_DR_Congo#New_provinces). Kasaï-Central and [Kasaï](4225839.md) provinces are the result of the dismemberment of the former [Kasaï-Occidental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasa%C3%AF-Occidental) province.[^cite_note-2] Kasaï-Central was formed from the [Lulua district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lulua_District) and the independently administered city of [Kananga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kananga) which retained its status as a provincial capital. The 2024 population was estimated to be 4,211,190.[^cite_note-3]

The new province's territory corresponds to the historic [Luluabourg Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luluabourg_Province) which existed in the early period after [independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo#Independence_and_the_Congo_Crisis_\(1960%E2%80%9365\)) between 1963 and 1966.
