**Ecuandureo** is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Mexico) in the [Mexican](3966054.md) [state](8196151.md) of [Michoacán](195805.md). It is located in the northwestern part of the state and its head is the town of Ecuandureo.
