**Dilling** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الدلنج, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *al-Dalanj*) or **Dalang** is a town in [South Kordofan](767653.md) State in [Sudan](27421.md), north of the state capital [Kadugli](3956578.md). As of 2008 it had a population 59,089 people.[^cite_note-1] In printed sources and internet sources, including maps and atlases, the town's name is usually spelled as *Dilling*,[^cite_note-2] reflecting the local pronunciation \[ˈdɪlɪŋ]. The spellings *Dalang* and *Dalanj* reflect a pronunciation adapted to the [phonotactics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonotactics) of Arabic.

## Geography

Dilling is located about 160 kilometres (99 mi) south of [El Obeid](1481380.md), the state capital of [North Kordofan](767669.md) State. The town has a lake in the northeastern part of the same name. An asphalted road links Dilling to [Khartoum](17320.md) via [Kosti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosti_\(city\)) and [El Obeid](1481380.md), and another all-season road links Dilling to [Kadugli](3956578.md). There is a railway station in the north of the town on a branch line to [Debeibat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debeibat). [Dilling Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilling_Airport) is to the southeast of the town.[^cite_note-uni-3]

## Demography

The town is inhabited mainly by [Nuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuba_peoples) (especially the Ajang such as the Dilling tribe, the Gulfan tribe, the Kodor tribe, the Dabatnah tribe, the Kortalah tribe, the Kadro tribe, the [Nyimang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyimang_people)), the [Fellata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellata), the [Hawazma Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawazma_tribe), the Birged tribe, and many other tribes from north and middle Sudan. The Nuba group from whom the town takes its name, the [Dilling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilling_people), now constitute the majority of the population.

## War in Sudan (2023)

The city was [captured](77702874.md) and is occupied by [The Sudan People's Liberation Movement–North](30884032.md) (al-Hilu faction) during the [War in Sudan (2023)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Sudan_\(2023\)).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The town was later recaptured by the [Sudanese army](27429.md). In February 2025, the SAF pushed back the SPLM-N from the south of the city, opening up the road to [Kadugli](3956578.md).[^cite_note-7] On the 27th of January, 2026, the SAF managed to break the siege of Dilling, thus ending a year and a half siege.

## Economy

The main economic activity is trade. Other citizens work for the government, are engaged in agriculture or cattle breeding, or work on leather or metal work crafts. Dilling is the home of [Dilling University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalanj_University).[^cite_note-uni-3][^cite_note-LULAT2005-8]
