**&#x20;Kocaeli Province** ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *Kocaeli ili*, pronounced [\[koˈdʒaeli\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Turkish)) is a [province](428896.md) and [metropolitan municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_municipalities_in_Turkey) of [Turkey](11125639.md) and one of only three not to have the same official name as its capital, [İzmit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0zmit), which is thus also sometimes called Kocaeli. Its area is 3,397 km<sup>2</sup> (1,312 sq mi),[^cite_note-2] and its population is 2,102,907 (2023).[^cite_note-tuik-1] The province is the successor of the [Ottoman](22278.md)-era [Sanjak of Kocaeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjak_of_Kocaeli). The largest towns in the province are [İzmit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0zmit) and [Gebze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gebze). The [traffic code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_car_number_plates#Location_codes) is 41.

The province is located at the easternmost end of the [Sea of Marmara](28863.md) around the [Gulf of İzmit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_%C4%B0zmit). Kocaeli is bordered by the province of [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul_Province) and the Marmara Sea to the west, the [Black Sea](3386.md) to the north, the province of [Sakarya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakarya_Province) to the east, the province of [Bursa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bursa_Province) to the south and the province of [Yalova](888009.md) to the southwest. The metropolitan area of Istanbul extends to the Kocaeli-Istanbul provincial border.&#x20;



Selim Sırrı Paşa Mansion in [İzmit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0zmit)

The size and natural conditions of the Bay of İzmit allow for extensive port facilities, including the [Gölcük Naval Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G%C3%B6lc%C3%BCk_Naval_Base). The province is called the industrial capital of Turkey. Kocaeli has an airport named [Cengiz Topel Naval Air Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cengiz_Topel_Naval_Air_Station) which is used for military and civilian transport. Kocaeli has three universities: [Kocaeli University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kocaeli_University), [Gebze Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gebze_Technical_University) and [Kocaeli Health and Technology University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kocaeli_Health_and_Technology_University).
