**Tim Pütz** (German pronunciation: [\[ˈtɪm ˈpʏts\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Standard_German); born 19 November 1987) is a German professional [tennis](29773.md) player who specializes in doubles. He reached his career-high doubles ranking of world No. 6 on 17 February 2025, and has won eleven doubles titles on the [ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour), including the [2024 ATP Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_ATP_Finals) with [Kevin Krawietz](23521626.md), becoming the first all-German pair to win the title. Pütz won the [2023 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_French_Open) in mixed doubles alongside [Miyu Kato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyu_Kato_\(tennis\)) and was a finalist at the [2024 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) with Krawietz.

He has also reached the semifinals at the [2023 Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Wimbledon_Championships). In singles, he has a career-high ranking of world No. 163, achieved in February 2015, reaching the second round at the [2014 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles).

Pütz has represented Germany in the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup) since 2017, most notably reaching the semifinals in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Davis_Cup_Finals). He also competed at the Olympic Games with Kevin Krawietz in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), and in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) where they reached the quarterfinals.

## Professional career

### 2014–2017: ATP singles & David Cup doubles debuts

Pütz made his ATP Tour main-draw singles debut at the [2014 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Wimbledon_Championships). As a qualifier, he defeated [Teymuraz Gabashvili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teymuraz_Gabashvili) in the first round. He lost to [Fabio Fognini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabio_Fognini) in four sets in the next round.

Pütz played in the [2017 Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Davis_Cup) for the first time. He helped the [German team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany_Davis_Cup_team) to win over Portugal in the [World Group play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Davis_Cup_World_Group_play-offs) by winning the doubles rubber with [Jan-Lennard Struff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan-Lennard_Struff).

### 2018–2020: First two ATP titles and French Open quarterfinal

In the first round of the [2018 Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Davis_Cup_World_Group), Pütz and Struff beat the Australian duo [John Peers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Peers) and [Matthew Ebden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Ebden) to set Germany on its way to victory.[^cite_note-2] Although Pütz and Struff won again in the Davis Cup quarterfinals against the Spaniards [Feliciano López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feliciano_L%C3%B3pez) and [Marc López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_L%C3%B3pez), their team lost the tie with 2–3.

In June 2018, Pütz won his first doubles title on the ATP Tour at the [Stuttgart Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_MercedesCup) with [Philipp Petzschner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipp_Petzschner) as a wildcard entry.[^cite_note-3]

In May 2019, Pütz won his second doubles title with [Frederik Nielsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederik_Nielsen) at the [Bavarian International Tennis Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_BMW_Open_%E2%80%93_Doubles) in Munich.[^cite_note-4]

Pütz reached his first Grand Slam quarterfinal at the [2020 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_Open) with Nielsen, where they lost to world No. 1 and top-seeded Columbian pair of Farah/Cabal.

### 2021: Masters title, Olympics & top 20 debuts

Pütz won his third doubles title at the [Estoril Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Estoril_Open_%E2%80%93_Doubles) with [Hugo Nys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Nys).[^cite_note-5]

He reached his first semifinals in doubles at a Masters 1000 at the [Madrid Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Mutua_Madrid_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) with compatriot [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md),[^cite_note-6] but they withdrew from the match. Later in May, Pütz won his fourth doubles title at the [Lyon Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_ATP_Lyon_Open_%E2%80%93_Doubles), again with Nys.[^cite_note-7]

The Pütz/Nys duo reached the quarterfinals of the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), where they were defeated by Kazakh duo Bublik/Golubev.

In July 2021, Pütz made his debut at the [Tokyo Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics) with [Kevin Krawietz](23521626.md).[^cite_note-8]

Pütz won his fifth title and his first at an [ATP 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour_500) tournament at the [Hamburg European Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Hamburg_European_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) with [Michael Venus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Venus).[^cite_note-9] He and Venus got to the semifinals of the [Indian Wells Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_BNP_Paribas_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles).[^cite_note-10] He won his first [ATP Masters 1000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour_Masters_1000) title at the [Paris Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Rolex_Paris_Masters_%E2%80%93_Doubles), again with Venus.[^cite_note-11] As a result, he reached a new doubles career-high of No. 17 in November 2021.

### 2022: Australian Open quarterfinal, Masters final, world No. 7

Seeded sixth, Pütz and Venus reached the quarterfinals of the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), where they were defeated by eventual champions [Thanasi Kokkinakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanasi_Kokkinakis) and [Nick Kyrgios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Kyrgios).[^cite_note-12]

On 21 March, he broke into the top 10 for the first time, ranked at world No. 9. After a final showing at the [Cincinnati Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Western_&_Southern_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) with Venus,[^cite_note-13] he reached world No. 7 in the doubles rankings on 29 August 2022.



Pütz warming up ahead of his Monte-Carlo doubles quarter-final in 2023.

### 2023: New partnership with Krawietz, French Open mixed title

Pütz won his first Grand Slam tournament at the [French Open mixed doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles) championship with [Miyu Kato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyu_Kato_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-14] At the same tournament, he reached the quarterfinals for the third time in doubles with new partner [Kevin Krawietz](23521626.md) with a win over [Romain Arneodo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Arneodo) and [Sam Weissborn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Weissborn), getting revenge for their loss to the same duo at the [2023 Monte-Carlo Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Monte-Carlo_Masters) six weeks earlier.[^cite_note-15] They lost to eventual champions [Ivan Dodig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Dodig) and [Austin Krajicek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Krajicek) in three sets.[^cite_note-16]

He reached the semifinals of a Major for the first time at the [Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) with Krawietz. They won their first title together at the [Hamburg European Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Hamburg_European_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles).[^cite_note-17]

### 2024: US Open finalist, Olympics quarterfinal, ATP Finals champion

At the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), he reached the quarterfinals for the second time at this tournament with Krawietz[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] where they lost to eventual finalists [Simone Bolelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Bolelli) and [Andrea Vavassori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Vavassori).[^cite_note-20] The pair also reached the semifinals at the [Indian Wells Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_BNP_Paribas_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) where they lost to unseeded pair and eventual champions Wesley Koolhof/Nikola Mektić[^cite_note-21] and at the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Miami_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) where they lost to second seeds Austin Krajicek/Ivan Dodig.[^cite_note-22]

Next they reached the quarterfinals at the [Monte-Carlo Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Monte-Carlo_Masters_%E2%80%93_Doubles) where they lost again to the eventual champions and also unseeded pair of [Joran Vliegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joran_Vliegen) and [Sander Gillé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sander_Gill%C3%A9).[^cite_note-23] In July they also won the title on home soil, at the ATP 500 in [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Hamburg_Open) where they were the defending champions.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

Pütz partnered again Krawietz to represent Germany at the [2024 Paris Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics) where they reached the quarterfinals.[^cite_note-26]

He reached his first Grand Slam final with Krawietz at the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_US_Open_\(tennis\)) defeating fifth seeds [Andrea Vavassori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Vavassori) and [Simone Bolelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Bolelli), 16th seeds [Máximo González](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1ximo_Gonz%C3%A1lez) and [Andrés Molteni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Molteni) and fourth seeds [Marcelo Arévalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcelo_Ar%C3%A9valo) and [Mate Pavić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mate_Pavi%C4%87) in the semifinals.[^cite_note-27]

On his debut in the main competition at the [ATP Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_ATP_Finals) with Krawietz, having been alternate twice before, he reached the semifinals defeating [Simone Bolelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Bolelli) and [Andrea Vavassori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Vavassori).[^cite_note-28] They became the first all-German duo to reach the semifinals[^cite_note-29] and then the final in ATP Finals history. They also became the first No. 8 seeds to reach the doubles final,[^cite_note-30] which they won in straight sets against Marcelo Arévalo and Mate Pavić.[^cite_note-31]

### 2025: Masters title, world No. 6

Pütz and Krawietz reached the final at the [Adelaide International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Adelaide_International_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), losing to [Simone Bolelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Bolelli) and [Andrea Vavassori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Vavassori) in a deciding champions tiebreak.[^cite_note-32] He won his second [ATP Masters 1000](30872553.md) title at the [Shanghai Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Rolex_Shanghai_Masters_%E2%80%93_Doubles), again with Krawietz.[^cite_note-33]
