**Enderlin** is a city in [Ransom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ransom_County,_North_Dakota) and [Cass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_County,_North_Dakota) Counties, North Dakota, United States. The population was 881 as of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census),[^cite_note-2020_Census_(City)-4] and was estimated at 860 in 2025.[^cite_note-USCensusEst2025-3]&#x20;

## History

Enderlin was founded on October 7, 1891. One of the oldest buildings in town, the [Robert Lindemann House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Lindemann_House), was built in 1913 and is on the National Register of Historic Places.[^cite_note-nrhpinv3-6]&#x20;

Local [folklore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folklore) suggests the name is derived from the semi-German "End der Line" ("end of the line"), as the city was briefly the terminus of the [Soo Line Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soo_Line_Railroad)'s main line through North Dakota.[^cite_note-Wick-7] Another explanation is that the name was suggested by the wife of a Soo Line official and comes from a fictional character in a book.[^cite_note-Wick-7]&#x20;

### 2025 tornado

Main article: [2025 Enderlin tornado](81275417.md)

On the night of June 20, 2025, during a [significant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak_and_derecho_of_June_19%E2%80%9322,_2025) [tornado outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak) and [derecho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derecho), an [EF5](3724479.md) [tornado](37530.md) caused extensive damage as it passed to the east of the city, across Ransom and Cass Counties. Several farmhouses and homesteads were impacted by this very violent tornado, resulting in three people getting killed.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] It was the deadliest tornado to occur in North Dakota since 1978,[^cite_note-10] and was the first confirmed EF5 tornado to occur worldwide in 12 years since the [2013 tornado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Moore_tornado) that struck [Moore, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moore,_Oklahoma).&#x20;

## Geography

Enderlin is located along the [Maple River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maple_River_\(North_Dakota\)).&#x20;

According to the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the city has a total area of 1.631 square miles (4.22 km<sup>2</sup>), all land.[^cite_note-CenPopGazetteer2025-2]&#x20;

### Climate

This [climatic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) region is typified by large seasonal temperature differences, with warm to hot (and often humid) summers and cold (sometimes severely cold) winters. According to the [Köppen Climate Classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_Climate_Classification) system, Enderlin has a [humid continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate), abbreviated "Dfb" on climate maps.[^cite_note-Climate-11]&#x20;

### 2020 census

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), there were 881 people, 374 households, and 192 families residing in the city.[^cite_note-20] The [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) was 540.16 inhabitants per square mile (208.6/km<sup>2</sup>). There were 431 housing units at an average density of 264.26 per square mile (102.0/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of the city was 92.96% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_\(U.S._Census\)), 1.36% [African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.34% [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.23% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.00% [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(U.S._Census\)), 1.02% from some other races and 4.09% from two or more races. [Hispanic or Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) people of any race were 4.31% of the population.[^cite_note-21]&#x20;

### 2010 census

As of the [2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_census), there were 886 people, 386 households, and 225 families residing in the city. The population density was 615.3 inhabitants per square mile (237.6/km<sup>2</sup>). There were 454 housing units at an average density of 315.3 per square mile (121.7/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of the city was 98.65% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.23% [African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.79% [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.11% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.00% [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.00% from some other races and 0.23% from two or more races. [Hispanic or Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) people of any race were 2.37% of the population.&#x20;

There were 386 households 24.4% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 49.0% were [married couples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage) living together, 6.2% had a female householder with no husband present, 3.1% had a male householder with no wife present, and 41.7% were non-families. 38.3% of households were one person and 19.4% were one person aged 65 or older. The average household size was 2.16 and the average family size was 2.81.&#x20;

The median age was 48.5 years. 21.6% of residents were under the age of 18; 5% were between the ages of 18 and 24; 18.8% were from 25 to 44; 29.4% were from 45 to 64; and 25.2% were 65 or older. The gender makeup of the city was 49.2% male and 50.8% female.&#x20;

### 2000 census

As of the [2000 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_United_States_census), there were 947 people, 406 households, and 233 families residing in the city. The population density was 670.1 inhabitants per square mile (258.7/km<sup>2</sup>). There were 481 housing units at an average density of 340.4 per square mile (131.4/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of the city was 95.88% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.32% [African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.42% [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.32% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.00% [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.95% from some other races and 2.11% from two or more races. There were 406 households 27.3% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 47.8% were married couples living together, 6.2% had a female householder with no husband present, and 42.4% were non-families. 37.9% of households were one person and 20.2% were one person aged 65 or older. The average household size was 2.20 and the average family size was 2.96.&#x20;

The age distribution was 22.8% under the age of 18, 5.5% from 18 to 24, 23.4% from 25 to 44, 22.6% from 45 to 64, and 25.7% 65 or older. The median age was 44 years. For every 100 females, there were 99.8 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 96.5 males.&#x20;

The median household income was \$32,589 and the median family income was \$41,250. Males had a median income of \$36,042 versus \$21,023 for females. The [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income) for the city was \$20,883. About 4.7% of families and 7.1% of the population were below the [poverty line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_threshold), including 7.1% of those under age 18 and 6.6% of those age 65 or over.&#x20;

## Economy

Railway shipping remains an important part of the local economy, with the [Soo Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis,_St._Paul_and_Sault_Ste._Marie_Railroad), now a subsidiary of the [Canadian Pacific Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Pacific_Railway), continuing to operate a [yard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_yard) in the city. Another important part of the local economy is an [ADM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archer_Daniels_Midland) edible oil processing plant located just southeast of town.[^cite_note-22]&#x20;
