**Zahedan** ([Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language) and [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): *[زاهدان](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/e/ed/Fa-Zahedan.ogg/Fa-Zahedan.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>*; [\[zɒːheˈd̪ɒːn\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Persian))[^cite_note-6] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Zahedan_County\)) of [Zahedan County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahedan_County), [Sistan and Baluchestan province, Iran](448105.md), serving as capital of the province, the county, and the district.[^cite_note-Sistan_and_Baluchestan_Province_Structure-7] It is near the borderlands between [Iran](14653.md) and [Pakistan](23235.md) in an area inhabited by [Baloch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baloch_people) people.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

## Etymology

The original name of the city was Duzzap (Persian: *Duzdab*, meaning "Water Sink"), which it had received due to the purous ground that sinks the water flowing on the surface due to rain or rivers. The name was later changed to Zahedan during [Reza Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reza_Shah)'s visit in 1929.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEDudoignon2017119-10] The name is taken from the medieval large city of Zaidan that was located some distance to the northeast of modern city on the [Helmand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helmand) River delta. Zana Salehrad suggests that the name Zahedan may reflect more than just geography or past settlements. In this theory, “Zahedan” could have emerged as a descriptive term for the early communities that settled the region—people known for their simplicity, piety, or ascetic way of life. The Persian word “Zahed” means a devout or ascetic person, and “Zahedan” could therefore have originally referred to a place inhabited by such people, a town of the righteous or spiritually mindful. Over time, the name became formalized, carrying both the moral connotation of devotion and the historical continuity from the earlier city of Zaidan, blending culture, faith, and memory into a single identity.[^cite_note-11]

## History



[Makki Mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makki_Mosque)



Ali Ibn Abi Talib Mosque

Mention of Zahedan first appears in sources in August 1849. However, the city first truly started to grow during the early 20th century.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEDudoignon2017118–119-12] During [World War I](4764461.md) it became the westernmost terminal of the [Zahedan railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahedan_railway_station), which reached as far as [Quetta](93764.md) in the northern part of what was then [British Baluchistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baluchistan_\(Chief_Commissioner's_Province\)).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBosworth2002387-13]

The city was the site of [a deadly crackdown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Zahedan_massacre) in October 2022, with dozens citizens killed by pro-governmental forces.[^cite_note-14] Over 90 people were killed.[^cite_note-Fresh_clashes-15] Two senior police officials were fired in the aftermath of the crackdown.[^cite_note-Fresh_clashes-15] On 28 October, there were protests in Zahedan and security forces fired on protestors, killing 98 and injuring 14.[^cite_note-Fresh_clashes-15]

On 3 November 2022, the Shi'i cleric and Khamenei loyalist [Sajjad Shahraki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sajjad_Shahraki?action=edit\&redlink=1) was assassinated in Zahedan.[^cite_note-Daragahi_2022-16][^cite_note-Cleric_killed-17] The next day, there were widespread protests in the city; [Revolutionary Guards](1285472.md) and other armed forces fired on protestors.[^cite_note-Daragahi_2022-16]

## Demographics

### Population

While the surrounding area boasts numerous ancient sites, Zahedan itself primarily developed in the 20th century. It transitioned from a small village to the provincial administrative center in the 1930s. Its population reached 17,500 by 1956, and increased more than fivefold to 93,000 by 1976. After 1980, large numbers of refugees fleeing the [Soviet invasion of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_invasion_of_Afghanistan) helped triple the population of Zahedan to more than 281,000 by 1986, a number which has since doubled again.

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 552,706 in 109,488 households.[^cite_note-2006_Sistan_and_Baluchestan_Province-18] The following census in 2011 counted 560,725 people in 134,088 households.[^cite_note-2011_Sistan_and_Baluchestan_Province-19] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 587,730 people in 146,717 households.[^cite_note-2016_Sistan_and_Baluchestan_Province-3]

### Location

Zahedan is connected by rail to nearby Pakistan and is near to [Afghanistan](737.md). It is about 41 km (25 mi) south of the [tripoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tripoints) of the three countries and at an altitude of 1,352 m (4,436 ft) above sea level and 1,605 km (997 mi) from the Iranian capital of [Tehran](57654.md).

### Climate

Zahedan has a [hot arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_arid_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *BWh*, [Trewartha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trewartha_climate_classification): *BWhk*). Precipitation is very low, and mostly falls in winter.

Zahedan experiences very hot summers: there are 84.7 afternoons per year with maximum temperature above 35 °C (95 °F), of which 7 have a daily maximum above 40 °C (104 °F). Winters feature pleasant afternoons but chilly mornings, with minimum temperatures below 0 °C (32 °F) for 43.7 days annually. Zahedan also experiences 81.3 days a year with [haze](998156.md).[^cite_note-ncei-20]

## Economy

See also: [Economy of Iran](64704.md)

| \[icon] | This section **needs expansion**. You can help by [adding missing information](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zahedan\&action=edit\&section=). *(March 2014)* |
| --- | --- |

Zahedan is the main economic center of the region and home to many small- and medium-scale industries. Its main products include cotton textiles, woven and hand-knotted rugs, ceramics, processed foods, livestock feed, processed hides, milled rice, brick, reed mats and baskets.

Zahedan also serves as a significant regional trade hub due to its proximity to the borders of Pakistan and Afghanistan. The Taftan border crossing, located east of Zahedan, is the main artery connecting Iran with Pakistan's Balochistan province, facilitating the movement of goods and people and playing a key role in regional commerce. Strategically located along routes linking Central Asia and Persian Gulf ports to the south, the city has benefited from regional trade. Despite its strategic location, the region remains among the least economically developed in Iran, facing challenges related to underdevelopment and regional inequality.[^cite_note-23]

## Transport

See also: [Transport in Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_in_Iran)



[Zahedan railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahedan_railway_station)

Zahedan is served by [Zahedan International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahedan_International_Airport).

Highway [95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_95_\(Iran\)) links Zahedan to [Tehran](57654.md) and [Mashhad](406512.md) in the north and the port of [Bandar Chabahar](1658958.md) on the [Sea of Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_of_Oman) in the south, and Highway [84](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_84_\(Iran\)) to the Pakistani city of [Quetta](93764.md) in the east and to [Kerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerman) in the west.

For decades the [5 ft 6 in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5_ft_6_in_gauge_railway) (1,676 mm) [broad gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broad_gauge) (Indian subcontinent system) railway exists from [Zahedan station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahedan_railway_station) to/from [Quetta's station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetta_railway_station) in Pakistan, the [Quetta–Taftan Railway Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetta%E2%80%93Taftan_Railway_Line). Beyond this, west, a [standard gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_gauge) line was completed from Zahedan to [Kerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerman) linking the city with the rest of the Iranian rail network.[^cite_note-24] This flowed from a 18 May 2007 [MOU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorandum_of_Understanding) for rail co-operation (of Pakistan and Iran) under which the line was to be completed by December 2008. It was completed with an opening ceremony on 19 June 2009.[^cite_note-25] This means that Zahedan hosts the [break of gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Break_of_gauge) between the [Islamic Republic of Iran Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Republic_of_Iran_Railways)'s standard gauge tracks of the [Trans-Iranian Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Iranian_Railway) and [Pakistan Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Railways)'s broad gauge aforementioned.

### Chabahar–Zahedan railway



Iran railway network in 2020, Zahedan–Bam–Mirjaveh railway is complete and operational

In May 2016, during Indian Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](444222.md)'s trip to Iran, agreement was signed to develop two terminals and five berths at [Port of Chabahar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Chabahar) and to build a new railway between [Chabahar](1658958.md) and Zahedan, as part of [North–South Transport Corridor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North%E2%80%93South_Transport_Corridor), by [Indian Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Railways)'s public sector unit [Ircon International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ircon_International).[^cite_note-FP1-26] Ten years later, this proposal is still under study and consideration, a via Kerman connection to the port of Chabahar.[^cite_note-FP1-26]

In July 2016, India began shipping US\$150 million of tracks to Chabahar[^cite_note-27] to build the US\$1.6 billion line, for which India pledged additional US\$400 million and Iran has also allocated US\$125 million in December 2016, thus taking the total allocation to US\$575 million (out of US\$1.6 billion needed) by the end of 2016.[^cite_note-28]

## Sports

See also: [Sport in Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sport_in_Iran)

In April 2008 the 70 billion IRR (about 2.5 million [USD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USD)) [Zahedan Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahedan_Stadium) was built with a seating capacity of 15,000 people. It was inaugurated on 18 April 2008 with a friendly football game between *Honarmandan* (Artists) and a local team.

## Education



[University of Sistan and Baluchestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sistan_and_Baluchestan)'s Amphitheater in Zahedan.

See also: [Education in Iran](1332627.md)

Zahedan is the home of the Islamic Azad University of Zahedan,[^cite_note-29] the [Zahedan University of Medical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahedan_University_of_Medical_Sciences)[^cite_note-30] and the [University of Sistan and Baluchestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sistan_and_Baluchestan). Besides, the largest Sunni seminary, [Darululoom Zahedan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamiah_Darul_Uloom_Zahedan), is located in Zahedan. There are some other religious Sunni schools in the city and the vicinity.
