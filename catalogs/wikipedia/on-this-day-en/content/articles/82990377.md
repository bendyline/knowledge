The **2026 Scottish Cup final** was the final match of the [2025–26 Scottish Cup](80510779.md), the 141st edition of Scotland's most prestigious knockout [football](10568.md) competition. It was contested by 42-time winners [Celtic](69708.md) and two-time winners [Dunfermline Athletic](451181.md) at [Hampden Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampden_Park), Glasgow, on 23 May 2026.[^cite_note-1]

Cetic won the game 3-1 to complete the domestic double for the 14th time.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Background

The match was Dunfermline Athletic's sixth appearance in a [Scottish Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Scottish_Cup_finals), their fifth against Celtic (the sides having met in [1961](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960%E2%80%9361_Scottish_Cup#Final), [1965](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964%E2%80%9365_Scottish_Cup#Final), [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Scottish_Cup_final) and [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Scottish_Cup_final)) and first since the latter.[^cite_note-4] Celtic made their 63rd Scottish Cup final appearance and returned after losing on [penalties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shootout) to [Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen_F.C.) in the [2025 iteration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Scottish_Cup_final).[^cite_note-5]

## Match

### Summary

In the 19th minute, Celtic took the lead when [Daizen Maeda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daizen_Maeda) got in on goal before lobbing the ball over the advancing Dunfermline goalkeeper [Aston Oxborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Oxborough) from just outside the penalty area. [Arne Engels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arne_Engels) made it 2-0 in the 36th minute when he scored with a right foot shot from outside the penalty area low to the right corner of the net. In the 73rd minute, [Kelechi Iheanacho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelechi_Iheanacho) got a third goal for Celtic when he received a pass from [Benjamin Nygren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Nygren) before turning in on his left foot, past the goalkeeper before scoring low to the left corner from six yards out. Dunfermline pulled a goal back in the 80th minute when Josh Cooper scored from six yards out with a side foot finish low to the left of the net.[^cite_note-6]
