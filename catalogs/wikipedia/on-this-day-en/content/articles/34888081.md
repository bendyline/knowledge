# Telecopter

A **telecopter** is a [helicopter](8286923.md) used to gather footage for [television stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_station). It is a [portmanteau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portmanteau) of television and helicopter.

The [KTLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KTLA) **Telecopter** was the world's first [television news](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_news) [helicopter](8286923.md), which first went into operation in 1958[^cite_note-1] and was invented by [John D. Silva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_D._Silva).[^cite_note-2] The on-board video and audio equipment communicated with a line of sight KTLA transmitter receiver on top of [Mount Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Wilson_\(California\)). The first helicopter was leased to KTLA by National Helicopter Service and Engineering Company in [Van Nuys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Nuys).[^cite_note-3] For several years, KTLA (channel 5) was the only TV station with a helicopter based TV camera crewed reporting platform.

The Telecopter was designed and introduced by KTLA chief engineer John D. Silva (1920-2012).[^cite_note-4]

Today, KTLA's news helicopter is known as "Sky 5" and it is used during breaking news coverage.

The first three *Telecopters* were:
