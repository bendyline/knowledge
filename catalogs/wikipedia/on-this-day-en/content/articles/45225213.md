**Namhkam Township** (also known as **Nanhkan Township** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): နမ့်ခမ်းမြို့နယ်)) is a [township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Township) of [Mu Se District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_Se_District) in the [Shan State](493376.md) of eastern [Myanmar](19457.md). The principal town is [Namhkam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namhkam_\(Shan_State\)), a few miles away from [Mu Se](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_Se).

## Inhabitants

A large majority of the people in Namhkam Township are [Shan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shan_people) and [Kachin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kachin_people) with a smaller presence of the [Palaung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palaung_people).[^cite_note-3]

## Economy

According to a survey conducted in 16 villages, [poppy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poppy) cultivation increased from 812 acres (328 hectares) to 1,535 acres (617 hectares) from the 2006–2007 season to the 2008–2009 season.[^cite_note-4]

## Unrest

The [Shan State Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shan_State_Army)-North (SSA-N) and [Shan State National Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shan_State_National_Army) (SSNA) are active in Namhkam Township.[^cite_note-5] Pansay Militia is one of eight influential [militias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militias).[^cite_note-6] Kyaw Myint, head of Pansay militia, was selected as a [USDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Solidarity_and_Development_Party) candidate of Namhkam for Shan State Hluttaw.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
