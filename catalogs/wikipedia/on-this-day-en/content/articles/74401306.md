# Kharkiv strikes (2022–present)

|  | This article needs to be **updated**. Please help update this article to reflect recent events or newly available information. *(October 2025)* |
| --- | --- |



Destruction in Kharkiv after missile attack on 6 October 2023

The [Armed Forces](25709.md) of [Russia](25391.md) have launched numerous rocket attacks on [Kharkiv](2788558.md), [Ukraine](31750.md), since the [2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md) which marked the beginning of the [current phase](70149799.md) of the ongoing [Russo-Ukrainian war](42085878.md).

## 2022

### February 2022 Kharkiv cluster bombing

Main article: [February 2022 Kharkiv cluster bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_2022_Kharkiv_cluster_bombing)

On 28 February 2022, a series of rocket strikes by the [Russian Armed Forces](25709.md) killed nine civilians and wounded 37 more during the [battle of Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kharkiv_\(2022\)). The Russian army used [cluster munitions](79745.md) in the attack. Due to the indiscriminate nature of these weapons used in densely populated areas, [Human Rights Watch](46530.md) described these strikes as a possible [war crime](48626.md).[^cite_note-1]

### Kharkiv government building airstrike

Main article: [Kharkiv government building airstrike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv_government_building_airstrike)

On 1 March 2022, Russian forces attacked the government administrative building of the [Kharkiv Oblast](494901.md), located in the city of [Kharkiv](2788558.md).[^cite_note-2]

### March 2022 Kharkiv cluster bombing

Main article: [March 2022 Kharkiv cluster bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_2022_Kharkiv_cluster_bombing)

On 24 March 2022, a rocket strike by the [Russian Armed Forces](25709.md) killed 6 civilians and wounded 15 more during the [battle of Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kharkiv_\(2022\)), part of the [2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md). The Russian Army used 9N210/9N235 [cluster munition](79745.md) and [BM-27 Uragan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BM-27_Uragan) [multiple rocket launcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_rocket_launcher) in the attack. Due to the indiscriminate nature of these weapons used in densely populated areas, [Amnesty International](18947898.md) described these strikes as a possible [Russian war crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_war_crimes).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

### April 2022 Kharkiv cluster bombing

Main article: [April 2022 Kharkiv cluster bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2022_Kharkiv_cluster_bombing)

On 15 April 2022, a series of rocket strikes by the [Russian Armed Forces](25709.md) killed nine civilians and wounded 35 more during the [battle of Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kharkiv_\(2022\)), part of the [2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md). The Russian army used 9N210/9N235 [cluster munition](79745.md) in the attack. Due to the indiscriminate nature of these weapons when used in densely populated areas, [Amnesty International](18947898.md) described these strikes as a possible [war crime](48626.md).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

### Kharkiv dormitories missile strike

Main article: [Kharkiv dormitories missile strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv_dormitories_missile_strike)

On 17 and 18 August 2022, the missile strike on dormitories in [Kharkiv](2788558.md) was performed by [Russian aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_aviation) with a series of missiles.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The impact killed 25 people, including an eleven-year-old boy.[^cite_note-6]

### October 2023

Main article: [Hroza missile attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hroza_missile_attack)

On 5 October 2023, [Russian Forces struck the town of Hroza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hroza_missile_attack) in the [Kupiansk Raion](8613952.md) of the Kharkiv Oblast. A Russian [Iskander missile](5607520.md) struck a grocery store and a cafe during a memorial service for a fallen soldier. The attack killed 59 people, with six injured and three missing, making it one of the deadliest strikes of the war and in the Kharkiv region. Ukrainian President [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) condemned the attack and said the incident was a deliberate attack on civilians and "no blind strike."[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

### December 2023

Further information: [30 December 2023 Belgorod shelling § Response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30_December_2023_Belgorod_shelling#Response)

On 30 December 2023, Russian forces attacked the city of Kharkiv with missiles, injuring at least 28 people, including a foreign journalist, and damaging civilian infrastructure.[^cite_note-9]

## 2024

### January 2024

On 2 January, strikes were conducted on Kharkiv utilizing the [Hwasong-11A](63848635.md) (KN-23) missiles developed by [North Korea](21255.md).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

On 23 January, three strikes on Kharkiv led to nine victims, including a four-year-old child.[^cite_note-12] In the evening in particular, the central Pushkinska Street was hit.[^cite_note-13] In response, on 26 January 2024, the [Kharkiv City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv_City_Council) renamed this [Pushkinska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Pushkin) street to [Hryhorii Skovoroda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hryhorii_Skovoroda) street.[^cite_note-14] On 29 April 2024, the [Kharkiv metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv_metro) station on the street that was also named after Pushkin was renamed to [Yaroslava Mudroho station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaroslava_Mudroho_\(Kharkiv_Metro\)).[^cite_note-YMmKharkiv3858127KSe252024-15]

### May 2024

On 9 and 10 May 2024, the Russians tried to break through the front in Kharkiv while the Ukrainians resisted, starting the [2024 Kharkiv offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Kharkiv_offensive).[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

On 13 May 2024, the first of a scattering of underground schools in Kharkiv was opened in [Industrialnyi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrialnyi_District,_Kharkiv) so children could continue their education amidst the Russian attacks on the city.[^cite_note-KharkivStrikesMA7455435-18]

#### 25 May supermarket strike

Main article: [25 May 2024 Kharkiv missile strikes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/25_May_2024_Kharkiv_missile_strikes)

On 25 May, a Russian strike on a hardware store and a residential area killed 18 and injured 65 others.[^cite_note-19]

### July 2024

On 13 July, a Russian [double-tap strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-tap_strike) on the village of [Budy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budy,_Ukraine) killed two and injured 25 others, including two children.[^cite_note-20]

### August 2024

On 6 August, a Russian missile struck the city center, killing one, and injuring 12 others.[^cite_note-21]

On 30 August, Russian missiles hit an apartment building and playground in the city killing seven people, including a 14-year-old girl. At least a further 77 people were wounded.[^cite_note-22]

### September 2024

On 15 September, a Russian missile strike hit a 12-story apartment block, killing one person and injuring at least 40.[^cite_note-23]

### October 2024

On 30 October, a Russian missile hit a nine-story apartment block, killing three people.[^cite_note-24]

## 2025

### February 2025

On 28 February 2025, Russia fired drones against a medical facility, injuring seven people.[^cite_note-25]

### March 2025

On 30 March 2025, Russia fired drones against a military hospital, a dormitory hosting refugees and residential buildings. Two people were killed and 35 wounded.[^cite_note-26]

### June 2025

On 11 June 2025, Russia fired drones on buildings and public facilities, killing three individuals and wounding 60 people. The attack included the use of 17 [Shahed](75153890.md) [loitering munitions](53703349.md) (i.e., drones). The main areas affected included the [Slobidskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slobidskyi_District) and [Osnovianskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osnovianskyi_District) districts.[^cite_note-27]

### July 2025

On 7 July, Russian drones struck Shevchenkivskyi and Slobidskyi districts, hitting high-rise apartments, a school, and a kindergarten. Twenty-seven civilians were injured, including three children.[^cite_note-28] On 24 July, regional authorities said two glide bombs hit a residential area, killing two people and injuring 33, among them children.[^cite_note-29]

## 2026

### January 2026

On 2 January 2026, a Russian double-missile attack hit and destroyed a residential apartment block, killing two civilians and wounding 28.[^cite_note-30] On 5 January 2026, Russian forces launched five missile attacks against the industrial zone of the [Slobidskyi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slobidskyi_District), targeting the energy infrastructure.[^cite_note-31]
