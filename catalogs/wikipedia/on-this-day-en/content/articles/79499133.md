**Yahodyn** is a [railway station in Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_railway_stations_in_Ukraine) on [its border with Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland%E2%80%93Ukraine_border), opposite [Dorohusk railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorohusk_railway_station) in Poland.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] It is used by both passengers and freight, and [bogies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogie) are exchanged here.[^cite_note-3] There are plans for modernization.[^cite_note-4] There is also a road crossing the border here.[^cite_note-5]
