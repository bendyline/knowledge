The **Independence Day of Kyrgyzstan** ([Kyrgyz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_language): Кыргыз Республикасынын көз карандысыздыгынын күнү, [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): День Независимости Кыргызстана) is the main state holiday in [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md). It is celebrated in Kyrgyzstan annually on August 31, the anniversary of its declaration of independence in 1991.[^cite_note-31_August_1991-1]

## History

On August 31, 1991, the Supreme Council of the Republic of [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md) adopted a law on the "Declaration on State Independence of the Republic of [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md)".[^cite_note-Kemel_Toktomushev-2] Because of this, the [Kyrgyz Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_Republic) was declared an independent state.[^cite_note-Kemel_Toktomushev-2] Kyrgyzstan officially adhered to the principles of international law, and cooperation between peoples.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] In 1993, the first constitution was adopted, which has changed several times in 20 years. In the years since independence, Kyrgyzstan has had two revolutions, each putting the first two Presidents of [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md) in exile in [Russia](25391.md) and [Belarus](3457.md) respectively.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## General events

Annually, a mass cultural event is held on [Ala-Too Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ala-Too_Square), which is attended by thousands of spectators as well as high ranking politicians.[^cite_note-9] It is held in the presence of the [President of Kyrgyzstan](1569760.md), [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Kyrgyzstan), Speaker of the Supreme Council, and [Mayor of Bishkek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Bishkek) as well as other politicians from the [Supreme Council](4503602.md) and former presidents and prime ministers.[^cite_note-10] Normally, a theatrical prologue of historical figures who contributed to the establishment of Kyrgyz statehood is shown.[^cite_note-11] The president usually delivers a speech to those present after the entrance of the national colours (the [Flag of Kyrgyzstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Kyrgyzstan)) and the performance of the [*Mamlekettik Gimni*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Anthem_of_the_Kyrgyz_Republic) (National Anthem) by a choir and [symphonic orchestra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symphonic_orchestra). Kyrgyz pop stars as well as traditional dance ensembles perform during this event.[^cite_note-12]

## Hosting cities

Under President [Sadyr Japarov](65513403.md), the main Independence Day celebrations have moved away from Bishkek to different regional centers every year, as a way to boost local infrastructure as well as put a national spotlight on a different region and culture each year.&#x20;

## Bishkek military parade

A [military parade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_parade) on August 31 is held every 5 years in [Bishkek](4554.md) on [Ala-Too Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ala-Too_Square) in honor of Independence Day. Smaller military parades are also held in other cities in Kyrgyzstan (such as [Osh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osh), and [Jalal-Abad](1649680.md)).

The parade commander gives the order to commence the parade of the Bishkek Garrison at 10\:00 am. The Minister of Defense (or on some occasions the [Chief of the General Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff_of_Armed_Forces_of_the_Kyrgyz_Republic)) arrives on the square and is greeted by the parade commander (usually the Bishkek Garrison commander), who informs the minister that the parade is now ready for inspection. The minister then inspects the troops on the square in the following manner:

**Minister**: *Hello Comrades of the (states formation's name)*\
**Formation**: *Greetings Comrade Minister of Defence!*\
**Minister**: *Congratulations on the occasion of the anniversary of the independence of the Kyrgyz Republic!*

After he inspects the troops and disembarks from the vehicle and reports to the [President of Kyrgyzstan](1569760.md) in their position as Commander in Chief on the status of the parade. After this is done, the president delivers an address on the holiday. After the speech, the [*Mamlekettik Gimni*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Anthem_of_the_Kyrgyz_Republic) (National Anthem) is performed by the [Band of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of Kyrgyzstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_of_the_General_Staff_of_the_Armed_Forces_of_Kyrgyzstan). After the performance of the national anthem, the commander of the parade gives the command to begin the parade. The parade formations and military equipment of the [Armed Forces of the Republic of Kyrgyzstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_the_Republic_of_Kyrgyzstan) then march on the square, saluting to their Commander in Chief as they march past the central tribune.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

## Other events



President [Roza Otunbayeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roza_Otunbayeva) meeting with [Kremlin Chief of Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremlin_Chief_of_Staff) [Sergei Naryshkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Naryshkin) during his working visit to [Bishkek](4554.md) on Independence Day in 2011.

Other events are sometimes timed to coincide with the celebration of Independence Day. In 2019, an equestrian games in the [Issyk-Kul Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Issyk-Kul_Region) took place on the days leading up to Independence Day.[^cite_note-40] Sporting events such as [soccer](10568.md) games, swimming competitions[^cite_note-41] and traditional mass games are also held. In 2012, President [Almazbek Atambayev](7837715.md) attended the opening ceremony of a larger flagpole with the State Flag on Boz-Boltok Mountain in [Alamüdün](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alam%C3%BCd%C3%BCn), a town and northern suburb of [Bishkek](4554.md) in the [Chuy Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuy_Region).[^cite_note-42] The year before, a statue of [Manas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epic_of_Manas) was installed on Ala-Too Square.[^cite_note-manas-43]

The 29th anniversary celebrations in 2020 were celebrated online due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45] Earlier that May, [MP](98066.md) Zhanar Akayev proposed the idea to celebrate the 30th anniversary of Kyrgyzstan's independence in [Batken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batken) at a meeting of the [Jogorku Kenesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jogorku_Kenesh), citing it as a reason for the development of domestic tourism.[^cite_note-46] The [Commonwealth of Independent States](36870.md) Youth Symphony Orchestra performed at the concert event in 2021.[^cite_note-47]

## Controversies

- In 2016, on the eve of Independence Day, the [Chinese Embassy in Bishkek was bombed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Embassy_in_Bishkek_bombing) by a lone [suicide bomber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide_bomber) driving his car through the gates of the embassy. The driver of the car was killed while three embassy employees were injured.
