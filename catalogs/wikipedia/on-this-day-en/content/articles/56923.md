Demographics development according to the United Nations

According to the 2022 revision of the [World Population Prospects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Population_Prospects)[^cite_note-UN_WPP-1][^cite_note-UN_WPP_2022-2] the total population was 45,853,778 in 2021, compared to only 5,158,000 in 1950. The proportion of children below the age of 15 in 2015 was 48.1 percent, 49.4 percent was between 15 and 65 years of age, while 2.5 percent was 65 years or older.[^cite_note-WPP_2015-3]

### Refugee population

By October 2025, Uganda hosted roughly 1.95 million refugees,[^cite_note-ugasud-5] more than any other country in Africa.[^cite_note-onthef-6] Most are rural groups mainly from [South Sudan](32350676.md) and the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md), as well as from [Somalia](27358.md), [Burundi](21490998.md), and [Rwanda](25645.md).[^cite_note-briefi-7][^cite_note-onthef-6] From 2023, more than 90,000 refugees fleeing the [civil war in Sudan](73556699.md) have entered.[^cite_note-ugasud-5]

See also: [Indian diaspora in East Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_diaspora_in_East_Africa)

During the [Uganda Protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uganda_Protectorate) period, the British colonialists used [South Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Asian) immigrants as intermediaries. Following independence they constituted the largest non-indigenous ethnic group in Uganda, at around 80,000 people, and they dominated trade, industry, and the professions. This caused resentment among the native [African](5334607.md) majority, which was exploited by post-Independence leaders.

After [Idi Amin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idi_Amin) [came to power in 1971](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1971_Ugandan_coup_d'%C3%A9tat), he declared "economic war" on the Indians, culminating in the [Expulsion of Asians in Uganda in 1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expulsion_of_Asians_from_Uganda). Since Amin's overthrow in 1979 some Asians have returned. There are between 15,000 and 25,000 in Uganda today, nearly all in the capital [Kampala](56689.md).

[Uganda](31816.md) is a religiously diverse nation with [Christianity](5211.md) being the most widely professed religion. According to the 2014 census, over 84 percent of the population was Christian while about 14 percent of the population adhered to Islam, making it the largest minority religion.[^cite_note-census-2014-18] In 2009, the northern and west Nile regions were dominated by Roman Catholics, and [Iganga District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iganga_District) in the east of Uganda had the highest percentage of Muslims, about 53% population.[^cite_note-state2009-19][^cite_note-CIATONGA-16]
