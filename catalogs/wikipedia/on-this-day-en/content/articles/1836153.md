The **Unity Labour Party** (**ULP**) is a [democratic socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_socialism) [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Saint Vincent and the Grenadines](27228.md). It was the ruling party from 2001 to 2025 under the premiership of [Ralph Gonsalves](393744.md).[^cite_note-ulp-5]

The party is an observer of [COPPPAL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COPPPAL) and an affiliate of [Foro de São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foro_de_S%C3%A3o_Paulo). From 1994 to 2014, the party was a member of [Socialist International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_International).[^cite_note-6]

## History

The party was formed in 1994 from the merger of the [Saint Vincent Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Vincent_Labour_Party) and the [Movement for National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_for_National_Unity).[^cite_note-ulp-5] The parties had run as an alliance in the [elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Vincentian_general_election) earlier that year, promising voters that they would merge after the election regardless of the result.[^cite_note-ulp-5] In the [1998 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Vincentian_general_election) they received 54.6% of the vote, but the [New Democratic Party](1836145.md) won a majority of seats. The first leader of ULP, Sir [Vincent Beache](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Beache), resigned after the elections, and was succeeded by [Ralph Gonsalves](393744.md). However, in the [2001 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Vincentian_general_election) the ULP won its first parliamentary [majority](465640.md), winning twelve of the fifteen seats.[^cite_note-7] The party won another majority in the [2005 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Vincentian_general_election).[^cite_note-8] The party was narrowly re-elected in the [2010 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Vincentian_general_election), winning 8 out of 15 elected seats in the [House of Assembly of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Assembly_of_Saint_Vincent_and_the_Grenadines) and the same taking place in the 2015 general elections and was re-elected in the 2020 general election, this time winning 9 out of 15 seats but lost the popular vote to the New Democratic Party.[^cite_note-9]

In November 2020, Prime Minister Gonsalves made history by securing the fifth consecutive victory of his ULP in the general election.[^cite_note-10] In the [2025 Vincentian general election](76606789.md), the party was defeated by [Godwin Friday](52936025.md)'s NDP, with Gonsalves retaining the only winning seat of the ULP.[^cite_note-11]
