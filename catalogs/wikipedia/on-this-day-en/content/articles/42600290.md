[France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_France) was one of the largest military powers to come under occupation as part of the [Western Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Front_\(World_War_II\)) in [World War II](32927.md). The Western Front was a military theatre of World War II encompassing Denmark, Norway, Luxembourg, Belgium, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom, France, Italy, and Germany. The Western Front was marked by two phases of large-scale combat operations.

The first phase saw the capitulation of the Netherlands, Belgium, and France during May and June 1940 after their defeat in the [Low Countries](51402.md) and the northern half of France, and continued into an air war between Germany and Britain that climaxed with the [Battle of Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Britain).

After capitulation, France was governed as [Vichy France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vichy_France) headed by Marshal [Philippe Pétain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippe_P%C3%A9tain). From 1940 to 1942, while the Vichy regime was the nominal government of all of France except for Alsace-Lorraine, the Germans and Italians militarily occupied northern and south-eastern France. France was not liberated until 1944, when the allied invasion restored the French Government.

- **[Phoney War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoney_War)**, or *drôle de guerre* ("strange war"), the name given to the period of time in Western Europe from September 1939 to April 1940 when, after the blitzkrieg attack on Poland in September 1939, there was almost no fighting, and no bombs were dropped.&#x20;

  - [Anglo-French Supreme War Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-French_Supreme_War_Council) set up to organize a joint strategy against Germany.

- The **[Battle of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_France)** in May and June 1940, in which the German victory led to the fall of the [French Third Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Third_Republic).

## Free France

- [Charles de Gaulle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_de_Gaulle), the main leader.

- **[Free France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_France)** (*La France Libre*), the provisional government in London who controlled unoccupied and liberated territories, and the forces under its control (*Forces françaises libres* or FFL), fighting on the Allies' side after the [Appeal of 18 June](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appeal_of_18_June) of its leader, General [de Gaulle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_de_Gaulle).&#x20;

  - [French Liberation Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Liberation_Army) (*Armée française de la Libération*) formed on 1 August 1943 by the merger of the FFL and all other Free French units, principally the [Army of Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_of_Africa_\(France\)).

  - [French Forces of the Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Forces_of_the_Interior) (*Forces françaises de l'intérieur*) elements of the Resistance loyal to London and under its operational military command.

  - [Free French Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_French_Air_Force).

  - [Free French Naval Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_French_Naval_Forces).

  - [French Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Resistance) and the [National Council of the Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Council_of_the_Resistance) which coordinated [the various groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_networks_and_movements_of_the_French_Resistance) that made up the resistance.

  - [Japanese and Thai occupation of French Indochina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Indochina_in_World_War_II) - beginning with the [Japanese invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_invasion_of_French_Indochina) in September 1940 and with the [Franco-Thai War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco-Thai_War) which started in October 1940.

  - [Rallying of New Caledonia to Free France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rallying_of_New_Caledonia_to_Free_France)

- [Liberation of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberation_of_France)&#x20;

  - **[Operation Overlord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Overlord)** - the invasion of northern France by the western Allies in June 1944.

  - **[Operation Dragoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Dragoon)** - the invasion of southern France by the western Allies in August 1944.

  - [Liberation of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberation_of_Paris) - the freeing of the French capital in August 1944.

- **[Allied advance from Paris to the Rhine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allied_advance_from_Paris_to_the_Rhine)** - advance (as the right flank of the western front) into Alsace-Lorraine in 1944.

- **[Western Allied invasion of Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Allied_invasion_of_Germany)** - invasion (as the right flank of the western front) of Baden-Württemberg in 1945.
