[Hezbollah](13919.md), a [Shia Islamist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islamism) political party and militant organization that was established in [Lebanon](17771.md) in 1985, has been involved in a long-running conflict with [Israel](9282173.md) as part of the [Iran–Israel proxy conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Israel_proxy_conflict) and the [Israeli–Lebanese conflict](6020082.md).

## History

The two sides' first engagement occurred during the [Lebanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Civil_War), as [Iran](14653.md) became increasingly involved in Lebanon's internal affairs. With funding from the Iranian government and training and supervision from Iran's [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md), Hezbollah was built up in [Syrian-occupied Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_occupation_of_Lebanon) by various religious clerics amidst the [1982 Lebanon War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_Lebanon_War), primarily as a [Khomeinist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khomeinism) force opposed to the [Free Lebanon State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Lebanon_State) and the [Israeli occupation of southern Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_occupation_of_Southern_Lebanon_\(1982%E2%80%932000\)).

Hezbollah controls southern Lebanon and is supported and funded by Iran and serves as their proxy in regional wars.[^cite_note-1] From the inception of Hezbollah to the present[^cite_note-nybooks-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] the establishment of a Palestinian state and the return of Palestinian refugees to what became Israel has been a primary goal for Hezbollah. Hezbollah not only opposes the government and policies of the State of Israel, but also each and every Jewish civilian who lives in Israel.[^cite_note-6] Its 1985 manifesto reportedly states "our struggle will end only when this entity \[Israel] is obliterated. We recognize no treaty with it, no ceasefire, and no peace agreements."[^cite_note-The_Hizballah_Program-7]

  - [2006 Hezbollah cross-border raid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Hezbollah_cross-border_raid)

- [2006 Lebanon War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Lebanon_War), a military conflict between Hezbollah and Israel
