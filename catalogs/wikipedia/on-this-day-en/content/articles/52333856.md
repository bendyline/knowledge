## Supremo Tribunal Federal – Republic

STF, in its initial composition had fifteen ministers. Decree n.º 19.656, [Provisory Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Brazil_\(1930%E2%80%931945\)) act of 1931, reduced the number of ministers to eleven. The AI-2, of 1965, raised the number of ministers to 16. In 1969, AI-6 returned the composition to eleven ministers, which stays until this day.[^cite_note-2]
