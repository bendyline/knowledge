# Gold reserve

For private and state holdings in gold, see [Gold holdings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_holdings).

For the gold mining company, see [Gold Reserve Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_Reserve_Inc.) For the 1925 Soviet film, see [Gold Reserves (film)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_Reserves_\(film\)).

A **gold reserve** is the [gold](12240.md) held by a national [central bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_bank), mostly in a country, intended mainly as a guarantee to redeem promises to pay [depositors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depositor), note holders (e.g. [paper money](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_money)), or trading peers, during the eras of the [gold standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_standard), and also as a [store of value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Store_of_value), or to support the value of the national [currency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currency).

The [World Gold Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Gold_Council) estimates that all the gold ever mined, and that is accounted for, totalled 190,040 [tonnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonnes) in 2019[^cite_note-gold-1] but other independent estimates vary by as much as 20%.[^cite_note-2] At the price of \$144 per gram reached on 25 December 2025, one tonne of gold has a value of approximately \$144 million. The total value of all gold ever mined, and that is accounted for, would exceed \$27 trillion at that valuation and using WGC 2017 estimates.[^cite_note-3]

## IMF holdings

Since early 2011, the gold holdings of the [IMF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMF) have been constant at 2,814.1 tonnes (90.5 million troy ounces).[^cite_note-4]

## Officially reported holdings

The [IMF](15251.md) regularly maintains statistics of national assets as reported by various countries.[^cite_note-5] This data is used by the [World Gold Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Gold_Council) to periodically rank and report the gold holdings of countries and official organizations.

On 17 July 2015, China announced that it increased its gold reserves by about 57 percent from 1,054 to 1,658 tonnes, while disclosing its official gold reserves for the first time in six years.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

In July 2015, the [State Bank of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Bank_of_Vietnam) stated that gold reserves totalled 10 tonnes. However, it was not ranked below due to the current absence of any published data.

In 2019, the [State Oil Fund of the Republic of Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Oil_Fund_of_the_Republic_of_Azerbaijan) (SOFAZ) extended the gold allocation limit from 5% to 10%, in accordance with the amendments made to the Investment Policy of the Fund for diversification purposes.[^cite_note-8] However, the [Central Bank of Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Azerbaijan) does not hold any gold.

The gold listed for each of the countries in the table may not be physically stored in the country listed, as central banks generally have not allowed independent [audits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audit) of their reserves. [Gold leasing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_leasing) by central banks could place into doubt the reported gold holdings in the table below.[^cite_note-9]

Note that while Russian gold reserves are listed as 2,332.7 tonnes, of the 405.7 tonnes held by the [National Wealth Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereign_wealth_fund) at the beginning of the war, only 173.1 remained as of November 2025.[^cite_note-deftechtimes-10] From November Russia started selling physical NWF gold on the internal gold market, whereas it had previously sold it to the Central Bank, to maintain NWF liquidity while preserving physical reserves.[^cite_note-deftechtimes-10]

In September 2026, De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB) reported that approximately 86 tonnes of gold had been transferred from the United States and Canada to London between March and August 2026. Following the relocation, London held 32.1% of DNB's gold reserves, while New York and Ottawa each held 18.5%. DNB said the transfer was intended to improve the tradability of its reserves and strengthen its crisis preparedness amid increasing geopolitical unrest. The total size of DNB's gold reserve remained unchanged at 612.4 tonnes.[^cite_note-11]
