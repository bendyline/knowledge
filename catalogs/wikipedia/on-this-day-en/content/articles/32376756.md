[South Sudan](32350676.md) has never had a national election. However the most [recent sub-national election was in 2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_South_Sudanese_general_election) for the presidency of the regional government of Southern Sudan[^cite_note-1] and for the [bicameral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameralism), 170-member Legislative Assembly.

Voting Age limit is 18 years old and above.

## Independence referendum

In 2010, the [2011 South Sudanese independence referendum](13265409.md) was held resulting in a strong majority in favour of independence. This was despite difficulties and irregularities.[^cite_note-2]
