**Baalbek-Hermel** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بعلبك - الهرمل) is a [governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorates_of_Lebanon) of [Lebanon](17771.md) and is the largest by area in the country. It comprises the [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Lebanon) of [Baalbek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baalbek_District) and [Hermel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermel_District),[^cite_note-localiban-2] which in turn are subdivided into a total of 74 [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Lebanon).[^cite_note-profile2015-3] The capital is at [Baalbek](58207.md).[^cite_note-localiban-2] The governorate covers an area of 3,009 km<sup>2</sup> (1,162 sq mi)[^cite_note-statoids-1] and is bounded by [Akkar Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akkar_Governorate) to the northwest, [North Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Governorate) to the west, [Keserwan-Jbeil Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keserwan-Jbeil_Governorate) and [Mount Lebanon Governorate](3028437.md) to the southwest, [Beqaa Governorate](866204.md) to the south, and the [Syrian](7515849.md) [governorates](690220.md) of [Homs](7445154.md) and [Rif Dimashq](4521946.md) to the northeast and southeast. The governorate occupies the northern portion of the [Beqaa Valley](2162385.md), Lebanon's most important agricultural area.[^cite_note-profile2016-4]

The [UNHCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNHCR) estimated the population of the governorate at 416,427 in 2015, including 137,788 registered refugees of the [Syrian Civil War](30741795.md) and 8,117 [Palestinian refugees](400347.md).[^cite_note-profile2015-3] The Lebanese citizen population is predominantly [Shiite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islam_in_Lebanon) with pockets of [Christians](4796145.md) and [Sunnis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam_in_Lebanon), while the refugee population is predominantly Sunni Muslims.[^cite_note-profile2015-3]



Baalbek-Hermel Governorate comprises Baalbek District and Hermel District

Baalbek-Hermel Governorate was created by the enactment of Law 522 on 16 July 2003, in which the districts of Baalbek and Hermel were separated from Beqaa Governorate.[^cite_note-5] Implementation of the new region only began in 2014 with the appointment of the first and current governor, [Bashir Khodr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashir_Khodr?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-6]

Already one of Lebanon's poorest regions, the recent influx of Syrian refugees has placed additional strain on the governorate's fragile infrastructure and services.[^cite_note-profile2016-4] Violence in Syria has [spilled over](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Civil_War_spillover_in_Lebanon) into the governorate, especially in the area around [Arsal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsal) and [Ras Baalbek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ras_Baalbek).[^cite_note-profile2016-4] The northern Beqaa valley and Baalbek have had [Hezbollah](13919.md) or the [Amal Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amal_Movement) electoral victories but 46% of the electorate voted for the opposition Baalbek Madinati party in recent elections.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
