# Roadblock

For other uses, see [Roadblock (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roadblock_\(disambiguation\)).



[Concrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete) barrier

A roadblock is a temporary installation set up to control or block [traffic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic) along a [road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road). The reasons for one could be:

- [Roadworks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roadworks)

- Temporary road closure during special events

- [Police chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Car_chase)

- [Robbery](54849.md)

- [Sobriety checkpoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobriety_checkpoint)

- [Protests](266137.md)

In peaceful circumstances, they are usually installed by the [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police) or road transport authorities; they are also commonly employed during [wars](33158.md) and are usually staffed by heavily armed soldiers in that case. During [protests](266137.md) and [riots](146704.md), both police and demonstrators sometimes use roadblocks.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]
