**Banga**, officially the **Municipality of Banga** ([Hiligaynon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiligaynon_language): *Banwa sang Banga*; [Cebuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_language): *Lungsod sa Banga*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Banga*; [Maguindanaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanaon_language): *Inged nu Banga*, [Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_Alphabet): ايڠد نو باڠ), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [South Cotabato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Cotabato), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 91,536 people.[^cite_note-5]

Banga is situated in the northwest of the province of South Cotabato. The town derived its name from a palm tree known to the natives of the place as "*Buanga*" which during the pre-settlement time, predominantly grew in the area.

Banga is linked by a concrete national highway road from [General Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Santos) to the east and [Cotabato City](298955.md) in the west.

## History

The area was initially established as a Settlement District under the Allah Valley Project of the National Land Settlement Administration (NLSA) on March 4, 1941, with Gen. [Paulino Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paulino_Santos) serving as General Manager. On September 11, 1953, through Presidential Proclamation No. 612 issued by President [Elpidio Quirino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elpidio_Quirino), it was separated from [Koronadal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koronadal) and established as an independent municipality.
