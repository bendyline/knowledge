[Brazil](3383.md) will compete at the [2026 Winter Paralympics](41045653.md) in [Milan](36511.md) & [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md), Italy, which will take place between 6–15 March 2026.[^cite_note-1]

Para cross-country skier & para-biathlete [Aline Rocha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aline_Rocha) and para cross-country skier [Cristian Ribera](70214846.md) were the country's opening ceremony flagbearers. Meanwhile, the para-snowboarder [André Barbieri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Barbieri?action=edit\&redlink=1) was the country's flagbearer during the closing ceremony.

On 10 March 2026, Brazil won the first medal in their [Winter Paralympics](18670284.md) history, with [Cristian Ribera](70214846.md)'s silver medal in the [Men's sprint - sitting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Para_cross-country_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Paralympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_sprint_classical#Sitting). It was also the first medal for a [tropical nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_nations_at_the_Winter_Olympics#Winter_Paralympic_Games) and [South America](26769.md) at the Winter Games.[^cite_note-2]
