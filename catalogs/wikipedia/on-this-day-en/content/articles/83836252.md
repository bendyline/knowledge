# 2026 Volta a Portugal

The 87th edition of the [Tour of Portugal](30858176.md) Jogos Santa Casa, organized by the [Portuguese Cycling Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Cycling_Federation), takes place from 5 to 16 August 2026. The race, which is part of the [2026 UCI Europe Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_UCI_Europe_Tour) calendar as a category 2.1 event, consists of a prologue and ten stages, including an [individual time trial](348799.md).

## Presentation

This edition was presented on Wednesday, 8 July, at **Alto da Senhora da Graça**, in [Mondim de Basto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mondim_de_Basto), unveiling an edition that marks the beginning of a new era for Portugal's premier cycling race.\
&#x20;On the road to its centenary, which will be celebrated in 2027, the Tour features a demanding and balanced route designed to provide exciting racing from the opening day to the finale.\
&#x20;Over 12 days, the race will pass through 71 municipalities, make a brief incursion into [Spain](26667.md), return to some of the most iconic locations in Portuguese cycling, and introduce several new features that promise to leave a lasting mark on the recent history of the race.[^cite_note-2]

### Route

The Tour of Portugal begins with a prologue in the city of [Lisbon](18091.md), before heading south to the [Algarve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algarve), finishing in [Albufeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albufeira) at the end of Stage 2.\
&#x20;After crossing the [Alentejo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alentejo) region, the riders face their first major challenge with the legendary climb to [Alto da Torre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torre_\(Serra_da_Estrela\)) in the [Serra da Estrela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serra_da_Estrela) during Stage 4.\
&#x20;The opening week concludes with an [individual time trial](348799.md) between [Anadia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anadia,_Portugal) and [Águeda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81gueda), in the [Aveiro Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aveiro_District), before the rest day on 11 August.\
&#x20;The second half of the Tour features five stages in the [Northern Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Region,_Portugal?action=edit\&redlink=1) of the country, including a visit to the [Peneda-Gerês National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peneda-Ger%C3%AAs_National_Park) and a brief incursion into [Spain](26667.md) during Stage 7.\
&#x20;After finishing in [Fafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fafe), one of the most iconic towns in the history of the *Volta a Portugal*, the riders will tackle, for the first time in the race's history, the famous *Alto da Senhora da Graça* climb twice during Stage 9 in [Mondim de Basto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mondim_de_Basto).\
&#x20;The 87th edition of the [Tour of Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tour_of_Portugal) concludes in the [Porto District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto_District) with a finish on *Avenida dos Aliados* in [Porto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto).[^cite_note-3]

## Stages

The 2026 Tour of Portugal consists of eleven stages covering a total distance of 1,388 kilometres (862 mi). A rest day is scheduled for 11 August.[^cite_note-4]

### Partners

- The official broadcaster is the Portuguese public television channel [RTP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTP1).

- The title sponsor is the Portuguese brand [Jogos Santa Casa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jogos_Santa_Casa?action=edit\&redlink=1).

- The official jersey sponsors are:&#x20;

  - [Galp Energia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galp_Energia) – Points classification jersey
