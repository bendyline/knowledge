The **Palestinian Football Association** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الاتحاد الفلسطيني لكرة القدم) is the governing body for [football](10568.md) in [Palestine](241405.md), and for the [men's national team](1302455.md) and the [women's national team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_women's_national_football_team).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## History

The first "Palestine Football Association", was founded during the period of [Mandatory Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine) in a meeting held on 14 August 1928,[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] and applied for membership of [FIFA](11049.md). It was admitted provisionally on 17 December 1928, affiliated on 17 May 1929 and recognised by FIFA's government on 6 July 1929. The FAP changed its name to the [Israel Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Football_Association) (IFA) following the founding of the state of Israel in 1948.[^cite_note-fifa.com-7]

A new "Palestine Football Association" representing the Palestinian Arabs was formed in 1962[^cite_note-\:1-1], originally only for the territory of the [Egyptian occupation of the Gaza Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Arab_Republic_occupation_of_the_Gaza_Strip), and has been a member of the [Union of Arab Football Associations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Arab_Football_Associations) since it was formed in 1974.[^cite_note-8]

### Palestinian Authority and FIFA recognition

It was accepted as a member by FIFA in 1998, after the creation of the [Palestinian Authority](24093.md).[^cite_note-9] The PFA has also been a member of the [Asian Football Confederation](715249.md) (AFC) since 1998, in the [West Asian Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Asian_Football_Federation).[^cite_note-\:1-1]

On 11 February 2011, the PFA formed the first women's league.[^cite_note-10]

On 30 July 2025, [EFE](387067.md) reported that Palestine head coach Ehab Abu Jazar was seeking to establish a training center for the national team in Chile.[^cite_note-11]

## Divisions

The football division system is parted into two: the [West Bank](33209.md) and [Gaza Strip](12047.md). There is a men's [West Bank Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bank_Premier_League) and a men's [Gaza Strip Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Strip_Premier_League)[^cite_note-12] as well as a *West Bank Women's League*.[^cite_note-13]
