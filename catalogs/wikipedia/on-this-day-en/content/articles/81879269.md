The **2026 [World Athletics Ultimate Championship](77071612.md)** was held at the [National Athletics Centre](59274354.md) in [Budapest](36787.md), Hungary, from 11 to 13 September 2026. Organised by [World Athletics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Athletics), it was an inaugural biennial invitational that featured top athletes in selected disciplines (including defending [Olympic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_Summer_Olympics) and [world champions](218926.md)) competing for shares of a US\$10 million purse.[^cite_note-\:1-1]

## Qualification

The Ultimate Championship is a limited-field invitational, with individual track events limited to 16 entrants, and field events limited to eight entrants. Direct qualification slots are offered to:[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

- The gold medalist from the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics).

- The gold medalist from the [2025 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Athletics_Championships).

- The winner of the event at the [2026 Diamond League](81417246.md) final.

The remainder of the field in each individual event will be determined by the [World Athletics Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Athletics_Rankings) for the period from 2 September 2025 to 1 September 2026. There is no limit on how many athletes from a single country can be in the field for a single event.[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-\:2-5][^cite_note-\:3-6]

For relay events, the [2026 World Athletics Relays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_World_Athletics_Relays) served as the main qualifier, with the top six teams in its mixed 4 × 400 metres and mixed 4 × 100 metres events qualifying for the Ultimate Championship. Two more teams will qualify based on their performance during the competition period between 22 August 2025 to 1 September 2026. Only one team can qualify per-country in relays.[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-\:2-5][^cite_note-\:3-6]

The following athletes have automatically qualified for the Ultimate Championship based on winning a gold medal at the 2024 Summer Olympics or 2025 world championships, or winning the Diamond League finals:

### Wildcards

Additionally, several athletes received exceptional invitations to compete at the 2026 World Athletics Ultimate Championship.[^cite_note-7] Among the international invitees were [Josh Kerr](54894149.md) (Great Britain) and [Jakob Ingebrigtsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakob_Ingebrigtsen) (Norway), who were both added to the men's 1500 metres. Their inclusion expanded the field to 13 athletes. [Gianmarco Tamberi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gianmarco_Tamberi) (Italy) was also granted an invitation in the men's high jump. As host nation, Hungary received exceptional invitations for [Patrik Enyingi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrik_Simon_Enyingi) (400 m), [Luca Kozák](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luca_Koz%C3%A1k) (100 m hurdles), [Sára Mátó](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A1ra_M%C3%A1t%C3%B3) (400 m hurdles), [Boglárka Takács](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogl%C3%A1rka_Tak%C3%A1cs) (100 m and 200 m), as well as the Hungarian mixed 4 × 100 m and 4 × 400 m relay teams.
