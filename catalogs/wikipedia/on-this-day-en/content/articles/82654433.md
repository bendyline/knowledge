**Alice Mina** (born 22 April 1987) is a Sammarinese politician and member of the [Grand and General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_and_General_Council), the parliament of [San Marino](27248.md). She is affiliated with the [Sammarinese Christian Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sammarinese_Christian_Democratic_Party) (PDCS) and has served in several parliamentary and party roles, including vice president of the PDCS parliamentary group and a member of San Marino's delegation to the [Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_Assembly_of_the_Council_of_Europe).[^cite_note-PDCS-1][^cite_note-PACE-2]

## Early life and education

Mina was born on 22 April 1987 in [Borgo Maggiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borgo_Maggiore), San Marino. She obtained a master's degree in international and diplomatic sciences and later worked as an employee before entering politics.[^cite_note-PDCS-1]

## Political career

Mina joined the [Sammarinese Christian Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sammarinese_Christian_Democratic_Party) in 2009. She became active in the party's internal structures and later served on its central council and national leadership bodies.[^cite_note-PDCS-1]

She was elected to the [Grand and General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_and_General_Council) of San Marino and has served as vice president of the PDCS parliamentary group. In parliament she has been a member of the permanent council commission for finance and budget and a member of the national group at the [Inter-Parliamentary Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-Parliamentary_Union).[^cite_note-PDCS-1][^cite_note-Consiglio-3]

Mina has also served as a member of the Sammarinese delegation to the [Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_Assembly_of_the_Council_of_Europe), participating in debates on international political issues and human rights.[^cite_note-PACE-2]

In December 2024 she was elected president of the central council of the [Sammarinese Christian Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sammarinese_Christian_Democratic_Party), becoming one of the prominent figures in the party's leadership.[^cite_note-SMRTV-4]
