**Simon Karam** (born 2 February 1950) is a Lebanese lawyer who served as the ambassador of [Lebanon](17771.md) to the United States of America between 1992 and 1993.

## Early life and education

Karam was born in [Jezzine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jezzine) on 2 February 1950.[^cite_note-wwho-1][^cite_note-2] He hails from a [Maronite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maronites) family.[^cite_note-3] His parents are Massoud Karam who was a merchant and Victoria Aziz. He received a degree in law from [Saint Joseph University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Joseph_University) in Beirut.[^cite_note-wwho-1]

## Career

Following his graduation Karam worked as a lawyer.[^cite_note-wwho-1] He was appointed mohafez (governor) of [Bekaa](2162385.md) in 1990 and mohafez of [Beirut](37428.md) city in 1991.[^cite_note-wwho-1] He was named as the ambassador of Lebanon to the [United States of America](3434750.md) in 1992 and presented his credentials to President [George Bush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_H._W._Bush) on 8 September.[^cite_note-4] Karam resigned from office in August 1993.[^cite_note-5] Then he continued to work as a lawyer in Lebanon.[^cite_note-wwho-1]

### Alliances and views

Karam was part of the [Qornet Shehwan movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qornet_Shehwan_Gathering) and was one of the political figures, including [Samir Frangieh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samir_Frangieh) and [Walid Jumblatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walid_Jumblatt), who supported the implementation of the [Taif accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taif_Agreement) and the withdrawal of Syrian troops from Lebanon.[^cite_note-6] Karam left the Qornet Shehwan movement in May 2005 before the elections.[^cite_note-7] He was close to the Lebanese journalist and politician [Gebran Tueni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gebran_Tueni) who was assassinated in December 2005. Following the assassination Karam argued that the incident was a catastrophe and was carried out by the Syrian forces led by the Syrian President [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md).[^cite_note-8] Both Tueni and Karam stated in 2000 that Syria did not consider Lebanon as a sovereign country, but as a satellite country under its domination due to the fact that Lebanon could not take part in the negotiations with [Israel](9282173.md).[^cite_note-9]

Karam was among the signatories of the Statement of Solidarity which was issued at the beginning of the [Syrian civil war](30741795.md) in April 2011. The statement expressed the solidarity of the Lebanese intellectuals with Syrians in their struggle against Bashar al-Assad.[^cite_note-10] In April 2026 he was chosen to lead the [peace talks](82916672.md) with Israel.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Personal life

Karam married Alice Mogabgab in 1997, and they had three children, two daughters and a son.[^cite_note-wwho-1]
