The **Cabinet of Yemen** or **Yemeni Government** or **Yemenite Government** is the executive body of the internationally recognized government of the [Republic of Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Yemen), appointed by [Rashad al-Alimi](70502008.md) as the chairman of the [Presidential Leadership Council](70488867.md) (PLC).[^cite_note-1] The unrecognized [rival government](80144294.md) established in 2016 in [Sanaa](18691665.md) by the [Houthis](17240332.md) and the pro-Houthi faction of the [General People's Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_People's_Congress_\(Yemen\)) (GPC) to rule [areas of Yemen under their control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_Yemen) has its own cabinet, the [Supreme Political Council](51279323.md) in [Sanaa](18691665.md).

## History

In 2012, after Saleh stepped down as a result of the [Yemeni Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_Revolution), part of the wider [Arab Spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Spring) protests, in a political transition plan backed by Gulf states, Abd-Rabbu Mansour Hadi became the interim president and oversaw a national dialogue to draft a more inclusive, federal constitution. In 2014 the Houthis rapidly advanced south from Saada and seize [Sanaa](18691665.md) on 21 September with help from Saleh. In 2015, Hadi tried to announce a new federal constitution. The Houthis, who opposed the constitution, arrested him and forced him to resign. He escaped to Aden and declared it as the interim capital. He also asked the international community to intervene, triggering the Saudi led Arab military coalition intervention.[^cite_note-2] Some analysts considered the Hadi-led government to be a [satellite regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_state) of [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) and the [UAE](69328.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

As part of the [Yemeni civil war](46215853.md), the cabinet authority is contested by the [Houthis](17240332.md), who took over the capital [Sanaa](18691665.md) in an [armed rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_takeover_in_Yemen) against the government and formed the [Supreme Revolutionary Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Revolutionary_Committee) and [Supreme Political Council](51279323.md) in 2015. President Hadi was placed under house arrest, but managed to escape to his hometown of [Aden](38050.md), which he declared as the temporary capital, before moving to [Riyadh](51554.md), earning his government the disparaging nickname of the "hotel government".[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The [United Nation Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nation_Security_Council) resolution [2201](https://undocs.org/S/RES/2201\(2015\)) denounced the actions of Houthis. UN security council's [resolution 2216](https://undocs.org/S/RES/2216\(2015\)) declared that it considers the Hadi-led government as the "legitimate Government of Yemen" and denounced what it described as the "unilateral actions taken by the Houthis".

[Mansur Hadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdrabbuh_Mansur_Hadi) resigned on 7 April 2022, after transferring his presidential powers to the eight-member [Presidential Leadership Council](70488867.md) (PLC). PLC officially assumed power on 17 April 2022. The PLC's chairman, [Rashad al-Alimi](70502008.md), subsequently became the president of the Republic of Yemen.[^cite_note-9]

## Supreme Political Council (SPC)

On 10 August 2024, [Ahmed al-Rahawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_al-Rahawi) was appointed as prime minister of Yemen and tasked with forming a ministry known as the 'Government of Change and Construction' by the President of the Supreme Political Council [Mahdi al-Mashat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahdi_al-Mashat).[^cite_note-sabanews3357644-11] Two days later the composition of the new government was announced in a presidential resolution.[^cite_note-yemeneco87574-12] On 18 August 2024 the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_\(Yemen\)) (San'aa) gave [confidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confidence_and_supply) to the government and called on it to follow a 36-point 'general program' prepared by a house of representatives special committee.[^cite_note-sabanews3360123-13] The government and SPC continues to be internationally unrecognised in comparison with the [Presidential Leadership Council](70488867.md) (PLC).[^cite_note-14]

On 28 August 2025, [a series of Israeli airstrikes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/28_August_2025_Israeli_attack_on_Yemen) killed Rahawi and nine other ministers.[^cite_note-15]
