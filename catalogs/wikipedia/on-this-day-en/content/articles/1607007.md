The **Under-Secretary-General for Humanitarian Affairs and Emergency Relief Coordinator** is a high-level position in the [United Nations](31769.md) that heads the [Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_for_the_Coordination_of_Humanitarian_Affairs).

The position has been held since October 2024 by [Thomas Fletcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Fletcher_\(diplomat\)), the sixth Briton in a row to serve in the role.

## History

The post of **[Under-Secretary-General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under-Secretary-General_of_the_United_Nations) for Humanitarian Affairs** and **Emergency Relief Coordinator** (ERC) was created by [UN resolution](1437212.md) 46/182 in December 1991 to coordinate the efforts of the [United Nations](31769.md), member states and the wider humanitarian community in response to natural and man-made emergencies.

The post is one of five cabinet-level UN positions that are traditionally held by nationals from the [five permanent members](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permanent_members_of_the_United_Nations_Security_Council) of the [Security Council](31956.md); since 2007, six British nationals have been appointed to that position.[^cite_note-2] Before 2007, British nationals held the position of [Under-Secretary-General for Political and Peacebuilding Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Department_of_Political_and_Peacebuilding_Affairs) for more than a decade.[^cite_note-3]
