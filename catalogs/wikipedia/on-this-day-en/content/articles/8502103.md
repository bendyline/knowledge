**Mai-Ndombe** is one of the 21 newest [provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo](3984115.md) created in the [2015 repartitioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_the_DR_Congo#New_provinces) when the former [Bandundu province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandundu_Province) was split-up into the new provinces of Mai-Ndombe, [Kwango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwango_Province), and [Kwilu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwilu_Province).[^cite_note-2] Mai-Ndombe was formed from the [Plateaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plateaux_District) and [Mai-Ndombe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mai-Ndombe_District) districts, with the town of [Inongo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inongo) being elevated to the capital [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) of the new province.

The 2024 population was estimated to be 2,291,000.[^cite_note-3]

## Geography

Map of Mai-Ndombe showing tree-cover loss years, with forest remaining since 2000 in green and loss years shaded yellow through purple.

Tree-cover loss year in Mai-Ndombe, 2001-2024, from the [Global Forest Change dataset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Forest_Change_dataset).

Mai-Ndombe is located in the western Democratic Republic of the Congo. It has an [international border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo%E2%80%93Republic_of_the_Congo_border) with the [Republic of the Congo](19599929.md) to the west, and borders with several other provinces: [Équateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89quateur_Province) and [Tshuapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tshuapa) to the north, [Kasaï](4225839.md) to the east, and [Kwilu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwilu_Province) and [Kinshasa](45773.md) to the south. Most of the province has a [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) by the [Köppen-Geiger climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen-Geiger_climate_classification), except for the northeast, which has areas with a [tropical monsoon climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate) or a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate).[^cite_note-4]

## History

**Mai-Ndombe Province** was a separate province from 1962 to 1966, prior the creation of [Bandundu Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandundu_Province) from the post-colonial political regions of [Kwango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwango_Province), [Kwilu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwilu_Province), and Mai-Ndombe. Presidents (from 1965, governors) were:

- 8 Sep 1962 – Dec 1963 [Victor Kumoriko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Kumoriko)

- 23 Sep 1963 – 11 Oct 1963 V. Bola (in rebellion)

- Jan 1964 – Dec 1964 Gabriël Zangabie&#x20;

  - 1964 – 1965 ...

- 27 Jul 1965 – 25 Apr 1966 Daniël Mongiya

[A large river boat sank in the province in 2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Congo_River_disaster), killing at least 60 people.[^cite_note-5]
