The **2026 World Nomad Games**,[^cite_note-1] officially known as the **VI World Nomad Games**,[^cite_note-2] was an international [multi-sport event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-sport_event) that was held between 31 August to 6 September 2026, in two cities of [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md): [Bishkek](4554.md), where the opening and closing ceremonies took place, and [Cholpon-Ata](3031798.md), which was the main venue for the games.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] It was the sixth edition of the [World Nomad Games](50350427.md) and the fourth edition to be hosted by Kyrgyzstan.

## Background

[Cholpon-Ata](3031798.md) is a small resort town in northern Kyrgyzstan, located on the northern shore of the famous lake [Issyk-Kul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Issyk-Kul), which has previously hosted the World Nomad Games three times.[^cite_note-5]

The [first games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_World_Nomad_Games), held from 9 to 14 September 2014, brought together 583 athletes from 19 countries who competed in 10 sports.[^cite_note-6]

Athletes from 80 countries, including those not traditionally nomadic, took part in the [second games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_World_Nomad_Games), which took place in the same place, in Cholpon-Ata, from 3 to 8 September 2016.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

More than 1,500 athletes from 66 countries took part in the [third games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_World_Nomad_Games) in September 2018, competing in 37 traditional sports.[^cite_note-автоссылка1-9]

The fourth games, held from 29 September to 2 October 2022 in the Turkish city of [Iznik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iznik), were the first to be held outside Kyrgyzstan.[^cite_note-10]

The fact that the sixth games would again be held on Kyrgyz soil became known during the closing ceremony of the fifth games, which took place from 8 to 13 September 2024, in the Kazakhstani capital, [Astana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astana),[^cite_note-11] although it was initially assumed that they would be held in Uzbekistan.[^cite_note-12]

## Preparation

On 27 September 2025, the [President of Kyrgyzstan](1569760.md) [Sadyr Japarov](65513403.md) signed a decree on the creation of an organizing committee for the preparation and holding of the VI World Nomad Games in 2026.[^cite_note-13]

General management and coordination of work on preparations for the games is entrusted to the Presidential Executive Office, Kanybek Tumanbaev.[^cite_note-14]

At the first meeting, held on 2 October of the same year, the composition of the organizing committee was approved, which was tasked with developing an action plan covering organizational, protocol and other preparatory aspects.[^cite_note-“news.kg”-15]

Instructions were also given to ensure security and law and order during the events, as well as to create conditions for a high level of hospitality.[^cite_note-“news.kg”-15] An important aspect of the preparation was the timely construction and modernization of the necessary infrastructure for the comfortable holding of the games.[^cite_note-“news.kg”-15]

147.5 million [soms](1227470.md) have been allocated for the preparation and holding of the competition.[^cite_note-vb.kg-16] Most of these funds are directed toward infrastructure development and breeding, with 32.8 million soms earmarked for the latter. Another 91.2 million will be spent on technical equipment for the game venues. The remaining 23.5 million will go toward administrative costs and staffing.[^cite_note-vb.kg-16]

The opening ceremony of the games took place at the [Azattyk Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azattyk_Arena), and the closing ceremony took place at the Cholpon-Ata hippodrome.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

## Opening ceremony

The opening ceremony took place on 31 August 2026, at the [Bishkek Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishkek_Arena) stadium, which has a capacity of 51,000 seats[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] and coincided with the [anniversary summit of the SCO](84103526.md).[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

## Participating countries

The games featured athletes from over 100 countries competing in 43 sports.[^cite_note-23] In total, a record number of athletes have announced their participation – around 3 thousand.[^cite_note-24] The main event, Kok-Boru, was won by Kyrgyzstan 12-0 in a final against Kazakhstan. <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjDbz-F_zWE>
