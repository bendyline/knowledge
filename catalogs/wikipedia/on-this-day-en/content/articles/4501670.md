The **House of Peoples' Representatives** is the [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house) of the [Ethiopian](187749.md) [Federal Parliamentary Assembly](4376253.md). Located in the capital [Addis Ababa](52593.md), the House has 547 members. All are elected in theory for five-year term in single-seat [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency). The proceedings in the chamber are led by [Speaker of the House of Peoples' Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_House_of_Peoples'_Representatives). Of the 547 seats, 74 are vacant.[^cite_note-1]

## History

Further information: [History of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Federal_Democratic_Republic_of_Ethiopia)

&#x20;In 1995, Ethiopia's [ethnic federalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_federalism) system was set up, with a bicameral federal parliamentary assembly, of which the lower chamber with not more than 550 members as per the constitution. 22 of the 547 seats were reserved for representatives of minority nationalities.
