**Jorhat Airport** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **JRH**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **VEJT**) is a [domestic airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestic_airport) serving the cities of [Jorhat](42478747.md) and [Golaghat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golaghat) in [Assam](186162.md), India. It is located at Rowriah, situated 7 km (4.3 mi) south-west from Jorhat and 51 km (32 mi) north-east from Golaghat.[^cite_note-4]

## History

It was established in early 1950.

Jorhat AFS is the [Indian Air Force](403355.md)'s first air base in the East. It is primarily a transport base for launching aircraft that carry out airdrops in the [Naga Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naga_Hills) in [Nagaland](408677.md) and [Arunachal Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh). It is equipped with two squadrons of [An-32](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An-32) transport aircraft. Air logistics are provided from Jorhat to the [Kameng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Kameng_District), [Subansiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Subansiri_district), [Siang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Siang_district), [Lohit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lohit_district) and [Tirap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirap_district) districts of Arunachal Pradesh.[^cite_note-5]

## Expansion

On 8 February 2022, the [Government of Assam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Assam) approved the sanction of ₹ 156 crore for the acquisition of private land to facilitate the expansion of the airport.[^cite_note-6]
