**Tina** or **Al-Tina** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): التينة) is a town in [North Darfur](767666.md), [Sudan](27421.md). The city is a twin town with [Tinè](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tin%C3%A8), located across the border in [Chad](5488.md). The town was overrun with refugees in the [War in Darfur](5043637.md) and has seen heavy fighting in the recent [Sudanese civil war.](73556699.md)

## History

### Darfur war

Tina has been a central border crossing point between Chad and Sudan. The city is divided between Tina in Sudan and Tinè in Chad. One of the first battles of Darfur war was [conducted in Tina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Tina_\(2003\)) when members of the [Sudanese Liberation Army](2031543.md) (SLA) attacked government soldiers in the town capturing stocks of equipment.[^cite_note-2]

During the Darfur war, Tine saw heavy fighting between the [Janjaweed](683270.md) and the [Justice and Equality Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_and_Equality_Movement) (JEM). Tina was an important refugee hub for people escaping the fighting, as it was the only open border crossing between Chad and Sudan.[^cite_note-3] Most of the Darfur war Tina was controlled by JEM which supporter base the Kobe [Zaghawa](1467480.md) inhabited the town.[^cite_note-4]

In October 2005 41 [AMIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Union_Mission_in_Sudan) peacekeepers were abducted in the border town by The JEM [^cite_note-5]

### Sudanese civil war

Most of the Sudanese civil war the town was controlled by [Darfur Joint Protection Force](74949825.md).[^cite_note-6] In October 2025, hundreds of refugees fled the town of [Kornoi](82919423.md) for Tina.[^cite_note-7] On 22 February 2026, Tina saw heavy clashes between the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) (RSF) and the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) (SAF). RSF captured the town, but lost the control of the town on the same day.[^cite_note-8] On 19 March drone attack launched by the RSF hit Tine across the border in Chad. The strike killed 17 civilians and prompted Chad to shutdown all borders with Sudan.[^cite_note-9]
