**Kamianske** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Кам'янське, IPA: [\[kɐmjɐnʲˈsʲkɛ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Ukrainian) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/8/83/Uk-%D0%9A%D0%B0%D0%BC%27%D1%8F%D0%BD%D1%81%D1%8C%D0%BA%D0%B5.ogg/Uk-%D0%9A%D0%B0%D0%BC%27%D1%8F%D0%BD%D1%81%D1%8C%D0%BA%D0%B5.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/8/83/Uk-%D0%9A%D0%B0%D0%BC%27%D1%8F%D0%BD%D1%81%D1%8C%D0%BA%D0%B5.ogg/Uk-%D0%9A%D0%B0%D0%BC%27%D1%8F%D0%BD%D1%81%D1%8C%D0%BA%D0%B5.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>; Russian: Каменское, romanized: *Kamenskoye*), previously known as **Dniprodzerzhynsk** from 1936 to 2016,[^cite_note-2] is an industrial city in [Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](494909.md), Ukraine, and a port on the [Dnieper River](380138.md). It serves as the administrative center of [Kamianske Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamianske_Raion) and [Kamianske urban hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamianske_urban_hromada).[^cite_note-admreform_2020_kamianske-3] Population: 226,845 (2022 estimate).[^cite_note-ua2022estimate-1]

On 19 May 2016, it was renamed back to its historical name of Kamianske.[^cite_note-4] Along with the city's name change, the city's [hydroelectric station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroelectric_station) was renamed to [Middle Dnieper Hydroelectric Power Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Dnieper_Hydroelectric_Station). Kamianske means "stone" or "rock" in Ukrainian, derived from the Ukrainian and Russian word "kamin"/"kamen" (камінь/камень). The name reflects the rocky banks of the Dnieper River on which the city is situated.

Besides the hydroelectric station, the city houses a few other industrial enterprises: [Prydniprovsky Chemical Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prydniprovsky_Chemical_Plant_radioactive_dumps) (closed in 1991), Bahley Coke Factory and [Dnieper Metallurgical Combine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnieper_Metallurgical_Combine).

## History



St. Nicholas Roman [Catholic Church](606848.md) in Kamianske between 1900 and 1905.

The first written evidence of settlement in the territory of Kamianske appeared in 1750. At that time the villages of Romankove and Kamianske, which make up the modern city, formed a part of the [Zaporozhian Sich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaporozhian_Sich).&#x20;

The settlement owed its rapid growth in the late 19th and early 20th century to Polish entrepreneur Ignacy Jasiukowicz, who developed its steel industry.[^cite_note-ij-5] In terms of size, the local steelworks matched the largest in the world, and housed the largest coke-oven battery in Europe.[^cite_note-ij-5] In 1913, it employed over 20,000 people.[^cite_note-ij-5] Jasiukowicz also established several institutions, including a school for workers' children, boys' and girls' gymnasiums, a hospital, a daycare center, a maternity shelter, a park, a community center, a rowing club and Catholic, Orthodox and Protestant churches[^cite_note-ij-5]

The city was known as *Kamianske* until 1936,[^cite_note-6] when it was renamed *Dniprodzerzhynsk,* honoring the [Dnieper River](380138.md) and the Bolshevik leader [Felix Dzerzhinsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Dzerzhinsky) (1877–1926), the founder of the Soviet [secret police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_police), the [Cheka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheka).

On 11 August 1979, two [Aeroflot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeroflot) passenger jets [collided in mid-air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_Dniprodzerzhynsk_mid-air_collision) near Dniprodzerzhynsk, killing all 178 people on both aircraft.

On 15 May 2015, the [president of Ukraine](20688479.md), [Petro Poroshenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petro_Poroshenko), signed a bill into law that started a six-month period for the [removal of communist monuments and the mandatory renaming of settlements with names related to](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decommunization_in_Ukraine) [communism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communism).[^cite_note-7] The following year, on 19 May 2016, the Ukrainian [Verkhovna Rada](844993.md) voted to rename Dniprodzerzhynsk, which reverted to using its historic name *Kamianske*.[^cite_note-8] Following the renaming, an [online petition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_petition) was issued, calling to change the name of Kamianske to *Dniprodym* ("Dnieper smoke"), which is a popular [nickname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nickname) of the city among locals.[^cite_note-9]

Until 18 July 2020, Kamianske was incorporated as a [city of oblast significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_regional_significance_\(Ukraine\)) and the center of [Kamianske Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamianske_Municipality). The municipality was abolished in July 2020 as part of the administrative reform of Ukraine, which reduced the number of raions of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast to seven. The area of Kamianske Municipality was merged into newly established Kamianske Raion.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

### Russian invasion of Ukraine

During the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md), on 8 April 2022, almost 12,000 people from the frontline areas were temporarily evacuated to the city due to active hostilities.[^cite_note-12] By 1 September, the number had increased to 35,000 people.[^cite_note-13]

## Geography

While mostly located on the right bank of the [Dnieper](380138.md), Kamianske stretches over the [hydroelectric station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroelectric_station) onto the left bank where the portion of city is known as the *Livyi bereh* (Left Bank) neighborhood. The neighborhood arches to the west of the Kamianske's suburb of [Kurylivka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurylivka,_Dnipropetrovsk_Oblast).

To the east, Kamianske urban hromada borders [Dnipro](989795.md) city, creating an [urban sprawl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_sprawl).

Kamianske is a city with a very difficult environmental conditions. The city is on the top 10 of the most [air-polluted](10934212.md) cities of Ukraine.[^cite_note-16] There have been suggestions to assign the status of the ecological disaster city. Right-bank part of the city is mostly polluted, where the metallurgical, chemical industrial enterprises are located.

In 2008, an interdepartmental commission for solving environmental problems was created.[^cite_note-17]

### Climate

The climate is [moderately continental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_climate), dry.[^cite_note-18] The amount of [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation) per year is about 400 mm. The average daily temperature is -6 °C in January, + 21 °C in July.[^cite_note-19]

## Economy



Main office of Dnieper Metallurgical Combine

The economic base of Kamianske is almost exclusively centered on [heavy industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_industry), with ferrous [metallurgy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallurgy) being the backbone of the local economy. Around 57% of the total [industrial production](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_production) is metallurgy and metal working. The [chemical industry](58721.md) comes second with a 17% share of the total industrial output.[^cite_note-22] While the exceedingly industrialized nature of the local economy ensures a rather high employment rate (as of 1 November 2007, official unemployment stood at 1.40%),[^cite_note-citystat-23] it also contributes to excessive pollution and radiation levels in the city.[^cite_note-24]

## Culture

Several [Eastern Orthodox](10186.md) churches, the largest being the Orthodox Cathedral of Saint Nicholas, which dates from 1894,[^cite_note-25], the center of the Ukrainian Orthodox Church Kamianska Eparchy, serve the faithful of the city. By 2018, there were 22 parishes of [Ukrainian Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Orthodox_Church_\(Moscow_Patriarchate\)) in Kamianske.

The [Roman Catholic](606848.md) Church of [Saint Nicholas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Nicholas)[^cite_note-roca-26] built by the city's Polish community at the end of the nineteenth century, has become one of the centers of Roman Catholicism in Eastern Ukraine. The Catholic Parish of Saint Nicholas also includes a monastery run by the [Order of Friars Minor Capuchin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Friars_Minor_Capuchin).[^cite_note-roca-26]

The city has an active Jewish community with a large synagogue and a charity center,[^cite_note-27] including [Chabad Lubavitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chabad_Lubavitch) emissaries.[^cite_note-28]

The literary style *Svetopys* (Ukrainian: Світопись), which originated in Onufriivka, also has roots connected to Kamianske, where the creator spent part of their early childhood. The style is described as "a way of writing that listens before it speaks".[^cite_note-29]
