The **Golden Bear** ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): ***Goldener Bär***) is the highest prize awarded for the best film at the [Berlin International Film Festival](18948039.md) and is, along with the [Palme d'Or](612946.md) and the [Golden Lion](2386128.md),[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] widely considered among the most prestigious film festival awards. The bear is the heraldic animal of [Berlin](3354.md), featured on both the [coat of arms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coat_of_arms_of_Berlin) and [flag of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Berlin).

## History

The winners of the first Berlin International Film Festival in 1951 were determined by a West German panel, with five winners of the Golden Bear, divided by categories and genres.[^cite_note-3] Between 1952 and 1955, the winners of the Golden Bear were determined by the audience members.[^cite_note-jury-4] In 1956, the [Fédération Internationale des Associations de Producteurs de Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIAPF) formally accredited the festival, and since then, the Golden Bear has been awarded by an international jury.[^cite_note-jury-4]

From 2026, the non-English language winners will also be automatically eligible for the [Academy Award for Best International Feature Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film).[^cite_note-5]

### The statuette

The statuette shows a [bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bear) standing on its hind legs and is based on the 1932 design by German sculptor [Renée Sintenis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9e_Sintenis) of Berlin's [heraldic mascot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coat_of_arms_of_Berlin) that later became the symbol of the festival. It has been manufactured since either the first[^cite_note-facts-6] or third[^cite_note-7] edition by art foundry [Hermann Noack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Noack).[^cite_note-statuette-8]

The original award was redesigned in a larger version in 1960,[^cite_note-cicero-9] with the left arm of the bear raised as opposed to the right in the former model.[^cite_note-prestige-10]

As of 2010, the bear is 20 cm (7.9 in) high and is fixed onto a base where the winning name is engraved.[^cite_note-Berlinale-11] The figurine consists of a bronze core, which is then plated with a layer of gold. The total weight of the award is 4 kg (8.8 lb).[^cite_note-weigh-12]

## Winners



[Gene Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene_Kelly) won for *[Invitation to the Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invitation_to_the_Dance_\(film\))* (1956).



[Ingmar Bergman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingmar_Bergman) won for *[Wild Strawberries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Strawberries_\(film\))* (1958).



[Pier Paolo Pasolini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pier_Paolo_Pasolini) won for *[The Canterbury Tales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Canterbury_Tales)* (1972).



[Satyajit Ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satyajit_Ray) won for *[Distant Thunder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distant_Thunder_\(1973_film\))* (1973).



[Rainer Werner Fassbinder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainer_Werner_Fassbinder) won for *[Veronika Voss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veronika_Voss)* (1982).



[John Cassavetes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cassavetes) won for *[Love Streams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Streams_\(film\))* (1983).



[Zhang Yimou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Yimou) won for *[Red Sorghum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Sorghum_\(film\))* (1988).



[Ang Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ang_Lee) won twice for *[The Wedding Banquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wedding_Banquet)* (1993) and *[Sense and Sensibility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sense_and_Sensibility_\(1995_film\))* (1996).



[Asghar Farhadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asghar_Farhadi) won for *[A Separation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Separation)* (2011).



[Jafar Panahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jafar_Panahi) won for *[Taxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxi_\(2015_film\))* (2015).



[Adina Pintilie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adina_Pintilie) won for *[Touch Me Not](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touch_Me_Not)* (2018).



[Carla Simón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carla_Sim%C3%B3n) won for *[Alcarràs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcarr%C3%A0s_\(film\))* (2022).



[Nicolas Philibert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Philibert) won for *[On the Adamant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/On_the_Adamant)* (2023).



[Mati Diop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mati_Diop) won for *[Dahomey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahomey_\(film\))* (2024).



[Dag Johan Haugerud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dag_Johan_Haugerud) won for *[Dreams (Sex Love)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreams_\(Sex_Love\))* (2025).

## Multiple winners

As of 2025, [Ang Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ang_Lee) is the only filmmaker to win the award twice, for *[The Wedding Banquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wedding_Banquet)* in [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/43rd_Berlin_International_Film_Festival) and for *[Sense and Sensibility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sense_and_Sensibility_\(1995_film\))* in [1996](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/46th_Berlin_International_Film_Festival).[^cite_note-\:0-13]
