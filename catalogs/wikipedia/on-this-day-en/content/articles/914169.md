**Northwestern Ontario** is a secondary region of [Northern Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Ontario) in the Canadian province of [Ontario](22218.md) which lies north and west of [Lake Superior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Superior) and west of [Hudson Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Bay) and [James Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bay). It includes most of [subarctic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subarctic) Ontario. Its western boundary is the Canadian province of [Manitoba](18926.md), which disputed Ontario's claim to the western part of the region. Ontario's right to Northwestern Ontario was determined by the [Judicial Committee of the Privy Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_Committee_of_the_Privy_Council) in 1884[^cite_note-1] and confirmed by the Canada (Ontario Boundary) Act, 1889, of the [Parliament of the United Kingdom](13964.md). In 1912, the [Parliament of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Canada) by the *Ontario Boundaries Extension Act* gave jurisdiction over the [District of Patricia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenora_District#Patricia_Portion) to Ontario, thereby extending the northern boundary of the province to Hudson Bay.

## Geographic subdivisions

Northwestern Ontario consists of the districts of [Kenora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenora_District,_Ontario), [Rainy River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainy_River_District,_Ontario) and [Thunder Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunder_Bay_District,_Ontario). Major communities in the region include [Thunder Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunder_Bay), [Kenora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenora), [Dryden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dryden,_Ontario), [Fort Frances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Frances,_Ontario), [Sioux Lookout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sioux_Lookout,_Ontario), [Greenstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenstone,_Ontario), [Red Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Lake,_Ontario), [Marathon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathon,_Ontario), and [Atikokan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atikokan). There are also several dozen [First Nations in Northwestern Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aboriginal_peoples_in_Ontario#First_Nations_in_Northwestern_Ontario).

## Time zones

Northwestern Ontario is divided between the [Eastern Time Zone](496028.md) and the [Central Time Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Time_Zone).

## Population



[Kenora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenora)



[Thunder Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunder_Bay)

Northwestern Ontario is the province's most sparsely populated region: 54% of the region's entire population lives in the Thunder Bay [census metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_metropolitan_area) alone. Aside from Thunder Bay, Kenora is the only other municipality in the entire region with a population greater than 10,000.

The overall population of Northwestern Ontario [declined](720599.md) in the early 21st century, mainly because of a downturn in the [forestry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forestry) sector, but some individual municipalities within the region have seen modest population growth over the period.

## Politics

Northwestern Ontarians tend to lean [left](18499.md) politically, mainly due to the history and influence of [labour unions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_unions). At the federal level, Northwestern Ontario is represented by [Liberal](149536.md) MPs [Marcus Powlowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Powlowski) in [Thunder Bay—Rainy River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunder_Bay%E2%80%94Rainy_River) and [Patty Hajdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patty_Hajdu) in [Thunder Bay—Superior North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunder_Bay%E2%80%94Superior_North), as well as [Conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_Party_of_Canada) MP [Eric Melillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Melillo) in the [Kenora District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenora_\(federal_electoral_district\)),. Provincially, [PC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_Conservative_Party_of_Ontario) [Greg Rickford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Rickford) represents [Kenora—Rainy River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenora%E2%80%94Rainy_River_\(provincial_electoral_district\)), [NDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario_New_Democratic_Party) [Sol Mamakwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sol_Mamakwa) represents [Kiiwetinoong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiiwetinoong), PC [Kevin Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Holland_\(Canadian_politician\)) represents [Thunder Bay—Atikokan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunder_Bay%E2%80%94Atikokan_\(provincial_electoral_district\)), and [NDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario_New_Democratic_Party) [Lise Vaugeois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lise_Vaugeois) represents [Thunder Bay—Superior North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunder_Bay%E2%80%94Superior_North_\(provincial_electoral_district\)).

In 2005, some residents of the region expressed dissatisfaction at the level of attention paid to the region by the provincial government. Some, most notably former Kenora mayor [Dave Canfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Canfield),[^cite_note-note1-2] and Fort Frances town councillor Tannis Drysdale, have proposed the idea of the region as a whole, or parts of it, seceding from Ontario to join [Manitoba](18926.md), although the campaign did not attract widespread public support.[^cite_note-note2-3]
