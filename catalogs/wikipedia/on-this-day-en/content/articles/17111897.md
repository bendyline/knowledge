# List of airlines of the United States

"American airline" redirects here. For the company named "American Airlines", see [American Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Airlines).

&#x20; This is a list of airlines that have an [air operator's certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_operator's_certificate) issued by the [Federal Aviation Administration](11186.md) of the United States.

Note: Destinations in **bold** indicate [primary hubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airline_hub), those in *italic* indicate secondary hubs, and those with regular font indicate focus cities. For legacy carriers American, Delta, and United, the most strategic/well connected hubs are shown as primary hubs.
