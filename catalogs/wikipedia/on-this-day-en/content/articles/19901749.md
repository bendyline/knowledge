**Sokolniki Suche** [\[sɔkɔlˈniki ˈsuxɛ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Polish) is a [village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village) in the administrative district of [Gmina Wieniawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gmina_Wieniawa), within [Przysucha County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Przysucha_County), [Masovian Voivodeship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masovian_Voivodeship), in east-central Poland.[^cite_note-TERYT-2]

On 9 September 2026, a concrete mixer lorry [collided into a train](84183948.md) at a [level crossing](193595.md) located in the village.[^cite_note-\:0-3]
