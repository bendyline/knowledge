**Santo Domingo** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˈsanto ðoˈmiŋɡo\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_the_Dominican_Republic) of the [Dominican Republic](8060.md). It was split from the [Distrito Nacional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distrito_Nacional,_Dominican_Republic) on 16 October 2001. It is spread over an area of 1,302.2 km<sup>2</sup> (502.8 sq mi), and has its capital at [Santo Domingo Este](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santo_Domingo_Este). As per the 2022 census, it had a population of 2,769,588 inhabitants, making it the most populous province in the country.

## History

Santo Domingo was split from the [Distrito Nacional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distrito_Nacional,_Dominican_Republic) on 16 October 2001.[^cite_note-2] The province was created to include the suburbs of the national capital of [Santo Domingo](57638.md).[^cite_note-Det-3]

## Geography

Santo Domingo is one of the 31 [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_the_Dominican_Republic) of the [Dominican Republic](8060.md).[^cite_note-Admin-4][^cite_note-CP-5] It is spread over an area of 1,302.2 km<sup>2</sup> (502.8 sq mi).[^cite_note-Info-6] It is bordered by the [Monte Plata Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Plata_Province) to the north, [San Pedro de Macorís Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro_de_Macor%C3%ADs_Province) to the east, [San Cristóbal Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Crist%C3%B3bal_Province) to the west and [Caribbean Sea](7822.md) to the south.[^cite_note-Info-6] The province consists of low plains along the Caribbean Sea coast, with urbanized districts and scattered agricultural lands in the interiors. It has a long coastline.[^cite_note-Det-3]

### Climate and vegetation

The province experiences a [tropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_climate) with warmer temperatures year-round and a distinct rainy season.[^cite_note-Det-3] About 57.71 km<sup>2</sup> (22.28 sq mi) of the land area is protected, and is spread across 14 protected areas. Broad-leaf forests occupy 374.2 km<sup>2</sup> (144.5 sq mi) or 26.8% of the land area. Agricultural lands cover an area of 612.4 km<sup>2</sup> (236.4 sq mi) in the province.[^cite_note-Info-6]

## Demographics

According to the 2022 census, the province had a population of 2,769,588 inhabitants, making it the most populous province in Dominican Republic.[^cite_note-Admin-4] The population consisted of 1,428,589 females (51.6%) and 1,340,999 males (48.4%). About 25.9% of the population was below the age of 15 years, 66.3% belonged to the age group of 15–64 years, and 7.8% was aged 65 years or older. The province had an urban population of 2,260,998 inhabitants (81.6%) and a rural population of 508,590 inhabitants (18.4%).[^cite_note-CP-5]
