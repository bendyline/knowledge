**Kericho County** is one of the 47 [counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Kenya) in [Kenya](188171.md). The county seats between longitude 35°02' and 35°40' East and between the equator and latitude 0°23' South with an altitude of about 2002m above sea level. It borders [Uasin Gishu County](1152280.md) (Eldoret County) to the North, [Baringo County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baringo_County) to the North-East, [Nandi County](6910690.md) to the North-West, [Nakuru County](2031081.md) to the East and [Bomet County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bomet_County) to the South, [Kisumu County](3377685.md) to the Northwest and [Nyamira County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyamira_County) to the West. It had a population of 901,777 (2019 census) and an area of 2,111 km².[^cite_note-statoids-2][^cite_note-\:0-3] Its capital and largest town is [Kericho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kericho).

Kericho is the leading [tea-producing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenyan_tea) region in the country, widely recognized as Kenya\`s "tea capital" and ancestral heartland of the [Kipsigis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kipsigis_people) sub-tribe of the [Kalenjin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalenjin_people) tribal group indigenous to the region. Situated in the highlands west of the [Rift Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rift_Valley,_Kenya), the county is home to some of the largest and most productive tea estates in Africa. It is also home to the popular Ketepa brand, one of the major local tea companies based in the region.

The defunct [Buret District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buret_District) is now part of Kericho County.

## Physical and topical features

County slopes gently from 2500m to about 1,800m above the sea level. The county is surrounded by a number of hills such as Tinderet Hills to the North, Mau Escarpment and Londiani hills (Tuluap-Kipsigis). A good number of rivers emanate from the county including Chemosit, Kiptaret, Kipsonoi, Timbilil, Maramara, Itare, Nyando, Kipchorian and Malaget.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Climatic conditions

County has a temperature range between 10C - 29C and rainfall of 2,125mm p.a being highest in central part of county where they plant tea and 1,400 mm p.a. in lower parts of Soin and parts of Kipkelion. County has rainy seasons April to June and October to December.[^cite_note-\:1-4]

## Demographics

According to the 2019 Kenya Population and Housing Census, Kericho County had a total population of 901,777 of which 450,741 are males, 451,008 females and 28 intersex persons. There are 206,036 household with an average household size of 4.4 persons per household and a population density 370 people per square kilometre.[^cite_note-5]

The total population of Kericho is estimated to have grown significantly. Official projections from the **Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS)**[^cite_note-6] suggest that the county's population will reach approximately 1.2M by 2030-40, with a 2026 estimate of roughly one million people.

## Economy

The county is part of the [Lake Region Economic Bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Region_Economic_Bloc?action=edit\&redlink=1) (LREB) established in 2018 to foster regional economic, industrial, social, and technological collaboration.[^cite_note-27]

The county hosts 25 trading centres, 5,813 registered businesses, 5807 licensed retail traders and 5,740 licensed wholesale traders.[^cite_note-\:2-28] In 2022, the county produced 432 million kilos of tea.[^cite_note-NI-29] The acquisition of these lands has long been controversial, particularly as it relates to the eviction of [Kipsigis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kipsigis_people) and [Talai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oretab_Talai) people from their land by British forces when Kenya was a British colony. The [United Nations](31769.md) has criticised the British government for not providing "effective remedies and reparation" for colonial-era crimes,[^cite_note-30] with representatives for these communities attempting to sue the British government through the [European Court of Human Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Court_of_Human_Rights) (ECHR).[^cite_note-31]

There are a number of industries in Kericho such as cement industry (Rai Cement Limited) and steel industry (Prime Steel Limited), Soin Sugar Company all three of them located in Soin ward, 18 tea processing factories, in various sub-counties, dairy processing plants in various sub-counties and [Kipkelion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kipkelion_West_Constituency) District coffee union process coffee in [Fort Ternan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Ternan), Kipkelion Sub-county.[^cite_note-\:0-3] The use of tea plucking machines has been controversial in the area, leading to acts of [industrial sabotage](29462.md#As_industrial_action) by disaffected youths.[^cite_note-32]

## Health

Kericho County has a total of 435 operational [health facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_facility). This total includes public, private, and faith-based providers that have been integrated into the national Social Health Authority (SHA) framework for service delivery. The county has achieved an [immunization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunization) coverage of 90% for essential vaccines like the third dose of the pentavalent vaccine. In July 2025, Kericho rolled out a massive 10-day integrated campaign[^cite_note-35] targeting **95% coverage** for measles, rubella, and typhoid to close the immunity gaps that widened during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Transport and communication

The county is covered by 1,110.7 km of road network. of this 411.1 km is covered by earth surface, 484.7 km is murram surface and 214.9 km is covered by bitumen.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

There are 8 Post Offices with 2,299 installed letter boxes, 2,079 rented letter boxes and 220 vacant letter boxes.[^cite_note-\:2-28]
