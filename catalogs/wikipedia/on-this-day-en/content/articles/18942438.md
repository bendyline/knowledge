**Bayahíbe** is a town in the [Dominican Republic](8060.md), located about 10 miles (16 km) east of [La Romana](2195051.md) on the shore of the [Caribbean Sea](7822.md) with 5,618 inhabitants (2022).[^cite_note-1] Founded as a fishing village in 1874 by Juan Brito and his family, who came from [Puerto Rico](23041.md), the town is now a tourist destination.

## Etymology

Bayahíbe is an indigenous word. Its meaning is not known for sure, but there are many names that include the [Tainos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ta%C3%ADno_language) word "Baya." "Baya" is the name given to a bivalve mollusk, like clams that are glued to the rocks or roots of mangrove trees. "Jib" (or "hib") is the name of a sieve manufactured from sticks used to sift [cassava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassava) flour.[^cite_note-2]

## Tourism



Sunset in Bayahíbe beach

&#x20;Bayahíbe Beach, a public beach, is located less than a mile from the town center, and Dominicus Beach is in about three miles' distance. Bayahíbe serves as an embarkation point for boat trips to [Saona Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saona_Island), a thinly inhabited island with extensive beaches located in a national park. Numerous large [resorts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resort) are located in the vicinity of Bayahíbe. The town has an [Albergo Diffuso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albergo_Diffuso) named Bayahibe Village, founded by Fabrizio Annunzi.[^cite_note-3]



Bayahibe beach.

[Scuba diving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuba_diving) is practiced in Bayahíbe—Bayahíbe. There are three [shipwrecks](272778.md) in the area including the Atlantic Princess, St George and Coco.[^cite_note-4]

## Notable events

On October 12, 2002, [Audrey Mestre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audrey_Mestre), French world record-setting free-diver, died while attempting to free-dive to a depth of 171 metres (561 ft) off the coast of Bayahíbe.[^cite_note-5]
