There have been a number of [LGBTQ](78174325.md) Catholics throughout history.

## Artists



[Andy Warhol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Warhol), who was gay, was a practicing Catholic.

- A number of influential Italian Catholic artists of the [Renaissance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaissance) and the [Baroque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baroque) who were notable for their religious paintings and sculpture were considered to have been homosexual or bisexual. These include [Donatello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donatello),

  [^cite_note-1]

  &#x20;[Sandro Botticelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandro_Botticelli),

  [^cite_note-2]

  &#x20;[Leonardo da Vinci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonardo_da_Vinci)

  [^cite_note-3]

  &#x20;and Michelangelo Merisi da [Caravaggio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caravaggio).

  [^cite_note-4]

  &#x20;In addition, [Michelangelo Buonarroti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelangelo_Buonarroti) was noted for painting the ceiling of the [Sistine Chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sistine_Chapel), under which popes are elected to this day.

  [^cite_note-5]

- [Andy Warhol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Warhol) was an American artist who was a leading figure in the visual art movement known as [pop art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_art), and whose homosexuality strongly influenced his work.

  [^cite_note-6]

  &#x20;He was a [Ruthenian Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruthenian_Greek_Catholic_Church) and regularly volunteered at homeless shelters in New York to practice his faith. He described himself as a religious person and regularly attended mass.

  [^cite_note-godspy-Warhol-7]

- [Robert Mapplethorpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Mapplethorpe) (1946–89) was an American photographer. From 1977 until 1980, Mapplethorpe was the lover of gay writer and [*Drummer* magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drummer_Magazine_\(leather\)) editor [Jack Fritscher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Fritscher).

  [^cite_note-8]

  &#x20;He was brought up in a Roman Catholic household and although he later "lapsed" from practicing the religion, he created art suffused with Catholicism – particularly in the areas of Catholic guilt and eroticism.

## Politicians and royalty

### Politicians

- [Pim Fortuyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pim_Fortuyn) (1948-2002), Dutch politician and civil servant was gay, stating in a 2002 interview that he was Catholic.

  [^cite_note-11]

- [Ruth Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_Hunt) (b. 1980) is Chief Executive of leading UK-based lesbian, gay and bisexual equality organisation [Stonewall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stonewall_\(charity\)),

  [^cite_note-12]

  &#x20;the largest gay equality body in Europe.

  [^cite_note-13]

  &#x20;She was formerly President of the [Oxford University Student Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Student_Union).

  [^cite_note-14]

  &#x20;Hunt is a practicing [Roman Catholic](606848.md) and has spoken out in favour of bridging the gap between faith leaders and [LGBT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT) communities.

  [^cite_note-15]

- [Stefan Kaufmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Kaufmann_\(politician\)) (born 1969) is a German politician and member of the [Christian Democratic Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christlich_Demokratische_Union_Deutschlands) (CDU). He is gay,

  [^cite_note-16]

  &#x20;and a devout practising Catholic.

  [^cite_note-17]

- [Daniel Kawczynski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Kawczynski) (born 1972), Polish-born British Member of Parliament. A practising Catholic, he came out in 2013 and is in a same-sex civil partnership.

  [^cite_note-18]

- [Jens Spahn](37152181.md) (born 1980), German politician, former Federal Minister of Health. Spahn is a self-described [Roman Catholic](606848.md) although he has stated publicly that he has problems with the [Catholic Church](606848.md) and its [sexual morality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_ethics).

  [^cite_note-19]

  &#x20;He lives with his husband Daniel Funke, a German [journalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journalist) and [lobbyist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobbying),

  [^cite_note-20]

  &#x20;in [Berlin](3354.md)'s [Schöneberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sch%C3%B6neberg) district.

  [^cite_note-21]

  &#x20;In December 2017, the two [married in a civil ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriage_in_Germany) at [Borbeck Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countess_Palatine_Francisca_Christina_of_Sulzbach#Borbeck_Castle) in [Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essen), officiated by the city's mayor [Thomas Kufen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Kufen).

  [^cite_note-22]

- [Klaus Wowereit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klaus_Wowereit) (born 1953), German politician, former mayor of Berlin. He came out as gay, by declaring in the by now famous statement: "Ich bin schwul und das ist auch gut so!" ("I am gay and that's a good thing, too!").

- [Sean Patrick Maloney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Patrick_Maloney) (born 1966), American politician, [U.S. representative](19468510.md) from [New York's 18th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York's_18th_congressional_district) from 2013 to 2023 and former [DCCC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Congressional_Campaign_Committee) chair.

  [^cite_note-23]

- [Robert Garcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Garcia_\(California_congressman\)) (born 1977), Peruvian-born American politician, member of the [Democratic Party](5043544.md), [U.S. representative](19468510.md) from [California's 42nd congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California's_42nd_congressional_district) since 2023 and [28th Mayor of Long Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mayors_of_Long_Beach,_California) from 2014 to 2022.

### Royalty

- [Queen Christina of Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Christina_of_Sweden) (1626 – 1689) reigned from 1632 before her abdication in 1654. Modern biographers generally consider her to have been a lesbian, and her affairs with women were noted during her lifetime.

  [^cite_note-26]

  &#x20;She was a prominent convert to Catholicism in 1654 and is one of only 6 women to be buried in the crypt of St. Peters Basilica in Rome.

- [Prince Eugene of Savoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Eugene_of_Savoy) (1663 – 1736), the military commander of the Catholic Imperial forces in the [Holy Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Roman_Empire), was predominantly homosexual.

  [^cite_note-27]

- [King Ludwig II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Ludwig_II) (1845 – 1886) was monarch of the Catholic [Kingdom of Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Bavaria) from 1864 until his death; it is known from his diary and private letters that he had strong homosexual desires which he tried to suppress.

  [^cite_note-28]

  &#x20;He remained a devout Catholic throughout his life and attended services regularly; building chapels for prayer within his castles, and commissioning religious art.
