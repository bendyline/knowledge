**Welcome Bay** is a suburb of [Tauranga](31495916.md), New Zealand. It is located 7 kilometres (4.3 mi) from central Tauranga. Neighbouring suburbs include Hairini and Maungatapu. There are a number of schools in Welcome Bay, including three primary schools.[^cite_note-3]

The name Welcome Bay has been used for the area as early as 1872 by the *[Bay of Plenty Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Plenty_Times)*.

## Demographics

Welcome Bay covers 11.65 km<sup>2</sup> (4.50 sq mi)[^cite_note-Area-1] and had an estimated population of 11,550 as of June 2025,[^cite_note-NZ_population_data_2023_SA2-2] with a population density of 991 people per km<sup>2</sup>.

Welcome Bay had a population of 11,052 in the [2023 New Zealand census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_New_Zealand_census), an increase of 768 people (7.5%) since the [2018 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_New_Zealand_census), and an increase of 2,385 people (27.5%) since the [2013 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_New_Zealand_census). There were 5,466 males, 5,550 females, and 39 people of [other genders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-binary_gender) in 3,594 dwellings.[^cite_note-6] 2.4% of people identified as [LGBTIQ+](78174325.md). The median age was 35.8 years (compared with 38.1 years nationally). There were 2,463 people (22.3%) aged under 15 years, 2,130 (19.3%) aged 15 to 29, 4,986 (45.1%) aged 30 to 64, and 1,473 (13.3%) aged 65 or older.[^cite_note-Census_2023-4]

People could identify as more than one ethnicity. The results were 77.4% [European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_New_Zealanders) ([Pākehā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C4%81keh%C4%81)); 23.8% [Māori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%81ori_people); 4.4% [Pasifika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasifika_New_Zealanders); 9.6% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_New_Zealanders); 1.4% Middle Eastern, Latin American and African New Zealanders (MELAA); and 2.6% other, which includes people giving their ethnicity as "New Zealander". English was spoken by 96.1%, Māori by 6.0%, Samoan by 0.2%, and other languages by 12.0%. No language could be spoken by 2.2% (e.g. too young to talk). [New Zealand Sign Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Sign_Language) was known by 0.4%. The percentage of people born overseas was 23.6, compared with 28.8% nationally.[^cite_note-Census_2023-4]

Religious affiliations were 28.3% [Christian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity_in_New_Zealand), 1.5% [Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism_in_New_Zealand), 0.4% [Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_New_Zealand), 2.5% [Māori religious beliefs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%81ori_religious_beliefs), 0.7% [Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism_in_New_Zealand), 0.5% [New Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Age), 0.1% [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism_in_New_Zealand), and 3.0% other religions. People who answered that they had [no religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irreligion_in_New_Zealand) were 56.1%, and 7.1% of people did not answer the census question.[^cite_note-Census_2023-4]

Of those at least 15 years old, 1,893 (22.0%) people had a bachelor's or higher degree, 4,809 (56.0%) had a post-high school certificate or diploma, and 1,887 (22.0%) people exclusively held high school qualifications. The median income was \$43,300, compared with \$41,500 nationally. 819 people (9.5%) earned over \$100,000 compared to 12.1% nationally. The employment status of those at least 15 was 4,620 (53.8%) full-time, 1,299 (15.1%) part-time, and 195 (2.3%) unemployed.[^cite_note-Census_2023-4]

## Marae

The local Tahuwhakatiki or Romai Marae and its Rongomainohorangi meeting house are a traditional meeting place for the [Ngāti Ranginui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ng%C4%81ti_Ranginui) [hapū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hap%C5%AB) of [Pirirākau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirir%C4%81kau).[^cite_note-tkmentry-11][^cite_note-maorimaps-12]

In October 2020, the Government committed \$500,000 from the [Provincial Growth Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_Growth_Fund) to upgrade the marae, creating six jobs.[^cite_note-maraepgf-13]

## Climate

Welcome Bay is a sub-tropical climate zone, with warm humid summers and mild winters. Typical summer daytime maximum air temperatures range from 22 °C to 26 °C, but seldom exceed 30 °C. Winter daytime maximum air temperatures range from 12 °C to 17 °C. Annual sunshine hours average least 2200 hours. Southwest winds prevail for much of the year. Sea breezes often occur on warm summer days. Winter usually has more rain and is the most unsettled time of year. In summer and autumn, storms of tropical origin may bring high winds and heavy rainfall from the east or northeast.[^cite_note-niwa-14]

## Transport

Public transport in Welcome Bay solely consists of bus services. The suburb is served by one 'Bay Hopper' routes; Route 40 (City - 15th Ave - Welcome Bay).[^cite_note-baybus-15]

## Education

Welcome Bay has two state primary schools for Year 1 to 6 students: Welcome Bay School,[^cite_note-official2076-16][^cite_note-moe2076-17] with a roll of 284,[^cite_note-ero2076-18] and Selwyn Ridge School,[^cite_note-official6945-19][^cite_note-moe6945-20] with a roll of 467.[^cite_note-ero6945-21] Welcome Bay School opened in 1979 because [Maungatapu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maungatapu) School was under pressure from its growing roll.[^cite_note-22] Selwyn Ridge opened in 1997.[^cite_note-23]

Tauranga Waldorf School is a state-integrated primary school for Year 1 to 8 students,[^cite_note-moe1187-24] with a roll of 203.[^cite_note-ero1187-25] The school was established in 1988.[^cite_note-26]

Te Kura Kaupapa Māori o Otepou is a [Māori language immersion school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kura_kaupapa_M%C4%81ori) for Year 1 to 8 students,[^cite_note-official1873-27][^cite_note-moe1873-28] with a roll of 68.[^cite_note-ero1873-29] The school traces its origin to the [native school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_schools) at Ngapeke in 1889 and other native/Māori schools in the Welcome Bay/Papamoa area.[^cite_note-30]

All these schools are co-educational. Rolls are as of July 2026.[^cite_note-NZ_school_roll_data-31]
