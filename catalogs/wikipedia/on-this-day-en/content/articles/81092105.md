**Coachella 2026** was a music and arts festival that took place at the [Empire Polo Club](45292145.md), in [Indio, California](107924.md), from April 10 to 19, 2026. It was the 25th edition of [the festival](494630.md). The scheduled headlining performers were [Sabrina Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabrina_Carpenter), [Justin Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Bieber), [Karol G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol_G), and [Anyma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyma). It sold out within a week of its announcement.

## Background

The [Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival](494630.md), sometimes referred to as "Coachella Festival" or simply "Coachella," is held annually at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, United States. It was co-founded by [Paul Tollett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Tollett) and Rick Van Santen in 1999, and is organized by Goldenvoice, a subsidiary of [AEG Presents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AEG_Presents).[^cite_note-santen-obit-1] The event features musical artists from many [genres of music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_genre), including [rock](25423.md), [pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music), [indie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_music), [hip hop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hip_hop_music), [latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_music), and [electronic dance music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_dance_music), as well as [art installations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Installation_art) and [sculptures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sculpture).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Most of the performances are streamed live on [YouTube](3524766.md), with some sets broadcast on a delayed basis.[^cite_note-var-10apr2026-4]

## Lineup









Coachella 2026 headliners (from left to right, top to bottom): [Sabrina Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabrina_Carpenter), [Justin Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Bieber), [Karol G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol_G) and [Anyma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyma)

The lineup for the 2026 edition of the festival was announced on September 15, 2025, with [Sabrina Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabrina_Carpenter), [Justin Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Bieber), [Karol G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol_G), and [Anyma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyma) as headliners.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Karol G is the first Latina artist to headline Coachella.[^cite_note-7] First-time performers include the girl group [Katseye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katseye),[^cite_note-8] [Bini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bini_\(group\)) – becoming the first [Filipino](2298105.md) group to perform at Coachella,[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] and [Noga Erez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noga_Erez) – becoming the first [Israeli](181296.md) singer to perform at the festival.[^cite_note-11] [Nine Inch Nails](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Inch_Nails) and [Boys Noize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boys_Noize) performed as one act called [Nine Inch Noize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Inch_Noize).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] English artist [FKA Twigs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FKA_Twigs) performed at the festival after being unable to participate in the [2025 festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_2025).[^cite_note-14]

## Notable events

### The Strokes anti-war protest

See also: [Protests against the 2026 Iran war](82544763.md), [Reactions to the 2026 Iran war](82535697.md), and [Gaza war protests](75044900.md)

On April 18, 2026, while performing *[Oblivius](50645200.md)*, American rock band [The Strokes](148546.md) staged an anti-war protest against the [United States](3434750.md) and [Israel](9282173.md) during their Coachella performance.&#x20;

The performance criticized [U.S. interventions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_interventions_by_the_United_States) in the [Middle East](19323.md), including the [Iran War](82533582.md) and [Gaza war](74998519.md). Visuals displayed during the performance criticized the [Central Intelligence Agency](5183633.md)'s role in enforcing [regime change](37258993.md) through violence, and denounced U.S. and Israeli military actions in [Iran](14653.md) and [Gaza](12047.md).[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44]

## Reception

The music festival sold out in less than a week after its announcement,[^cite_note-45] with both VIP and general admission tickets quickly claimed. Any further ticket availability is being managed through an official waitlist system.[^cite_note-46]

Gabriel Saulog of *[Billboard Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Philippines)* praised the Filipino girl group [Bini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bini_\(group\)) for their week 1 performance, describing it as a showcase of Filipino talent on a global stage. He also highlighted the group choreography, vocals, and their stage presence with its positive reception among international audiences.[^cite_note-47] Their debut was the most trending topic worldwide in social media.[^cite_note-48] Althea Legaspi and Maya Rego of *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone)* also praised the performance, saying that Bini had long envisioned a Coachella debut and cited it as a dream they hoped to achieve and they have now realized that goal.[^cite_note-49]
