The **Rekha Gupta ministry** represents the formation of the 13th cabinet of the Indian state [Delhi](37756.md) under the leadership of [Rekha Gupta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rekha_Gupta).[^cite_note-b883-1][^cite_note-q516-2]

Rekha Gupta and her Cabinet ministers took oath on 20 February at Ramlila Maidan. The oath of office administered by Lieutenant Governor VK Saxena at 12\:35. Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](444222.md), Union Home Minister [Amit Shah](28165549.md), BJP national president [J.P. Nadda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._P._Nadda), Union Ministers, and Chief Ministers and Deputy Chief Ministers from NDA party-ruled states present at the oath taking ceremony.[^cite_note-Rekha_Gupta_takes_oath_as_Delhi_Chief_Minister-3][^cite_note-Rekha_Gupta_Takes_Oath:_A_Look_At_Delhi_Chief_Minister's_Wealth-4]

## Council of Ministers



Seven ministers, including Rekha Gupta as Chief Minister, were sworn in by the Lieutenant Governor [Vinai Kumar Saxena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinai_Kumar_Saxena) at [Ramlila Maidan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramlila_Maidan), Delhi.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-Delhi_CM_Rekha_Gupta's_Cabinet:_List_of_ministers_and_portfolios-7][^cite_note-8]
