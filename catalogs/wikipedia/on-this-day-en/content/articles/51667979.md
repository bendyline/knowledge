Ashokdham Lakhisarai

**Ashokdham Mandir**, also known as **Indradamneshwar Mahadev Mandir**, is located at Ashok Dham Rajauna Chouki in [Lakhisarai district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakhisarai_district), [Bihar](442482.md). It is a temple complex: Indradamneshwar Mahadev Mandir (dedicated to the primary deity [Shiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiva)) is located in the center, surrounded by three more temples (dedicated to the goddess [Parvati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parvati), the mount of the Shiva [Nandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nandi_\(bull\)) and the goddess [Durga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durga)). On 11 February 1993, the Shankaracharya of Jagannathpuri inaugurated the reconstruction of the temple complex.[^cite_note-1]

## History

It is said that this place has been a center of worship since the 8th century. [Narayan Pal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narayanapala), the 6th emperor of [Pala Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pala_Empire) initiated the regular worship of the Shivlingam in the 8th century. In the 12th century, a temple was built on this place by King Indradyumna. It is said that the temple was demolished and for many years there were no remains above ground.

On 7 April 1977, two boys named Ashok and Gajanand were discovered the Giant Shivlingam beneath the ground while playing a traditional [Gilli-danda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilli-danda) game. On 11 February 1993, the Shankaracharya of Jagannathpuri inaugurated the restructure of the temple complex. The current temple complex building started on 15 November 2002 under Shree Indradamneshwar Mahadev Mandir Trust.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Maha Shivrati

Every year on the day of Maha Shivratri millions of pilgrims from various parts of India visit this shrine and offer [sacred water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred_waters) of [Ganges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganges) to the deity. For stay here a dharmshala (guesthouse) is built where pilgrims stays after the long journey.
