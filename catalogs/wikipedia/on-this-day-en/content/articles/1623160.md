**Tuapse**[^cite_note-13] is a [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) in [Krasnodar Krai](474125.md), Russia, situated on the northeast shore of the [Black Sea](3386.md), south of [Gelendzhik](1462700.md) and north of [Sochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sochi). Population: 61,571 ([2021 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2021Census-14] 63,292 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2010Census-6] 64,238 ([2002 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Russian_census));[^cite_note-PopCensus-15] 63,081 ([1989 Soviet census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Soviet_census)).[^cite_note-Census1989-16]

Tuapse is a sea port and the northern center of a resort zone that extends south to [Sochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sochi).

### Early history



Map of Circassia in 1840

Tuapse was a large center (native land) for the [Shapsugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shapsugs) tribe,[^cite_note-17] along other areas in [Circassia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circassia). The name of the town is itself [Adyghe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adyghe_language) (literally meaning "two waters").[^cite_note-18]

The modern settlement dates back to 1838, when the Russian fort of Velyaminovsky was established in the area after this region became a part of Russia in 1829 by the [Treaty of Adrianople](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Adrianople_\(1829\)).[^cite_note-KubanATS-2] During the [Crimean War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean_War), the Ottomans seized the fort and held it for two years (1857–1859). The village of **Velyaminovskoye** (Вельяминовское) was established here in 1864; it is the village that later became Tuapse.[^cite_note-KubanATS-2] Town status was granted to Tuapse in 1896.[^cite_note-KubanATS-2] During the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire), the town was the administrative capital of the [Tuapsinsky Okrug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuapsinsky_Okrug).

### 20th century

From 1924 to 1930, Tuapse was the capital of the [Shapsug national district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shapsug_national_district). According to the [1926 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1926_Soviet_census), the population was 54.6% Russian, 17.0% Ukrainian, 3.8% Armenian, 2.8% Greek, 1.6% Polish, 1.3% Georgian and 1.1% Jewish.[^cite_note-19]

The Soviets developed Tuapse as an oil terminal and depot. An [oil pipeline from Grozny and Maykop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grozny%E2%80%93Tuapse_oil_pipeline) was in operation by 1928, designed by [Vladimir Shukhov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Shukhov). An oil refinery dates from the same period. No later than 1941, Tuapse's status was changed to that of a town of the krai subordination.[^cite_note-KubanATS-2]

During [World War II](32927.md), the German military attempted to seize the town during the [Battle of the Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Caucasus), which caused major damage to Tuapse. The Germans reached the hills above from the north, but were not able to seize the town and its port.

### 21st century

See also: [2026 Tuapse oil terminal attack](83027884.md)

During the [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md), the Ukrainian military first attacked the port of Tuapse in February 2023.[^cite_note-navalnews20230308-20] In April 2026, Tuapse became the site of a [major industrial and environmental disaster](83027884.md) at its oil port facilities following Ukrainian attacks.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), Tuapse serves as the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of [Tuapsinsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuapsinsky_District), even though it is not a part of it.[^cite_note-KrasnodarK_admlist-1] As an administrative division, it is incorporated separately as the **[Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance) of Tuapse**—an administrative unit with the status equal to that of a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Krasnodar_Krai).[^cite_note-KrasnodarK_admlist-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), the Town of Tuapse is incorporated within Tuapsinsky Municipal District as **Tuapsinskoye Urban Settlement**.[^cite_note-KrasnodarKTuapsinskyD_mun-8]

## Geography

The town is situated on the northeast shore of the Black Sea, south of [Gelendzhik](1462700.md) and north of [Sochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sochi). The Russian Children Center [Orlyonok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlyonok) (former All-[Russian SFSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_SFSR) [Young Pioneer camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Pioneer_camp)) is here.

### Climate

## Economy and transport



Express train [Moscow](19004.md)–[Adler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adlersky_City_District) leaving platform 1 of Tuapse station

Tuapse is home to the [Tuapse oil terminal](42266406.md) and the [Tuapse Refinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuapse_Refinery).

Tuapse is one of the key transport hubs of the [Black Sea](3386.md) coast of Russia. The city's location determines its key importance to ensure a ground connection with the spa capital of the country – the city of [Sochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sochi) – and makes it important for the export commodities of the country (oil, fertilizer, coal, etc.).

There is a [railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuapse_Railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Tuapse.
