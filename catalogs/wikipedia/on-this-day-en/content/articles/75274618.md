The **Islamic Resistance in Iraq** (**IRI**; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): المقاومة الإسلامية في العراق, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *al-Muqāwamah al-Islāmīyah fī al-ʻIrāq*) refers to an informal network of [Iranian](14653.md)-backed [Shia Islamist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islamism) factions in Iraq. The name is used by members of the network when carrying out attacks against [American](3434750.md) forces or its allies in the region.[^cite_note-tout-3][^cite_note-WashInst-4] IRI does not represent the official position of the Iraqi government and consists of militias that operate outside the formal command structure of the [Iraqi armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_armed_forces).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] It is part of the [Axis of Resistance](36658251.md).[^cite_note-7]

In October 2023, the IRI began launching [rockets and drones at US bases in Iraq, Jordan and Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacks_on_US_bases_during_the_Gaza_war), causing minor injuries to American servicemen until [a drone attack killed three US soldiers in Jordan on 28 January 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_22_drone_attack).[^cite_note-\:13-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-aj1-12] Since November 2023, the IRI has attacked [Israel](9282173.md) with multiple drones and missiles.[^cite_note-13] In April 2025, the IRI had said that it was prepared to disarm in order to avert an escalating conflict with the [Trump administration](78298108.md).[^cite_note-14]

## Arsenal

The IRI's arsenal composes of cruise missiles, kamikaze drones and rockets of Iranian origin. These include:

- [al-Arqab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paveh_cruise_missile) – a long-range surface-to-surface [cruise missile](6590.md), with a range of 1,650 km (1,030 mi)

  [^cite_note-27]

- [Shahed 101](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahed_101) – A [loitering munition](53703349.md), with a range of 900 km (560 mi)

  [^cite_note-28]

- [Shahed 131](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahed_131) – A [loitering munition](53703349.md), with a range of 900 km (560 mi). It is the smaller counterpart of the [Shahed 136](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahed_136).

  [^cite_note-29]

- [Shahed 136](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahed_136) – A [loitering munition](53703349.md), with a range of 2,500 km (1,600 mi). This has not been used and only claimed possession.

## Operations in Iraq, Jordan, and Syria

Main article: [Attacks on US bases during the Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacks_on_US_bases_during_the_Gaza_war)

On 18 October 2023, amid the [Gaza war](74998519.md), the Islamic Resistance in Iraq began waves of attacks on US bases in both [Iraq](7515928.md) and [Syria](7515849.md), beginning with the launching of a [drone strike](38212231.md) on [al-Asad Airbase](3832376.md) which was intercepted.[^cite_note-AP_News-30][^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32]

On 24 October 2023, the Islamic Resistance in Iraq claimed responsibility for multiple drone strikes on US bases in eastern Syria, notably al-Omar oil field in [Deir ez-Zor Governorate](7445749.md) and [al-Shaddadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Shaddadi) in [Al-Hasakah Governorate](1429110.md).[^cite_note-33]

On 5 November 2023, the Islamic Resistance in Iraq issued a warning ahead of [Antony Blinken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antony_Blinken)'s visit to Baghdad, saying "Antony Blinken, the son of a Jew, is not welcome in Iraq." As a result of the threats, Blinken wore a bulletproof vest when landing in [Baghdad International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad_International_Airport).

On 9 November 2023, US forces were struck three separate times in 24 hours, including drone strikes in [Al-Asad Airbase](3832376.md) and [Al-Harir Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Harir_Air_Base), as well as an [IED](265112.md) attack on a patrol near the [Mosul Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosul_Dam).[^cite_note-34]

On 20 November 2023, eight US and coalition soldiers were injured from a ballistic missile attack, and there was minor infrastructural damage after the air base was attacked by a ballistic missile.[^cite_note-35]

On 25 December 2023, the Islamic Resistance in Iraq claimed responsibility for a drone attack on the base which injured three US soldiers, one being critical.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37]

On 18 January 2024, the Islamic Resistance of Iraq claimed it shot down a US [MQ-9 Reaper drone](4769988.md) after it took off from [Kuwait](7515890.md) near [Muqdadiyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muqdadiyah), [Diyala Governorate](377806.md).[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

On 20 January 2024, the group claimed a missile attack on the [Ain Al-Asad Air Base](3832376.md) in [Al Anbar Govenorate](467036.md), injuring two US personnel and one Iraqi personnel.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

On 28 January 2024, the IRI launched [a drone attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_22_drone_attack) on the Tower 22 US military outpost in [Rukban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rukban), [Jordan](7515964.md), killing 3 US soldiers and injuring 47 others.[^cite_note-aj1-12][^cite_note-42]

On 4 February 2024, a drone struck a training ground in Al-Omar field in [Deir ez-Zor](7445749.md), eastern Syria, which houses US troops, according to the [Syrian Democratic Forces](48205248.md). Though no casualties were reported among US forces, at least seven Kurdish fighters were killed and 18 injured. The Islamic Resistance in Iraq claimed responsibility for the attack.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44] The SDF condemned the attack, and said it had "every right to respond".[^cite_note-45]

On 1 April 2024, Abu Ali al-Askari, security chief of Kata'ib Hezbollah, said the organisation was prepared to arm the Islamic Resistance in Jordan. He declared that Kata'ib Hezbollah is ready to provide "12,000 fighters with light and medium weapons, anti-armor launchers, tactical missiles, millions of rounds of ammunition, and tons of explosives" to "defend the Palestinians and avenge the honour of Muslims." On the same day, al-Tanf garrison in Syria was attacked by a one-way attack drone which was intercepted.[^cite_note-46] Shortly after this announcement, a Jordanian militant attacked an IDF patrol vehicle near the Jordanian-Israeli border and fled. This was the first instance of an attack from Jordan on Israel since the beginning of the war.[^cite_note-47]

## Attacks on Israel

Main article: [Gaza war § Iraq](74998519.md#Iraq)

Since November 2023, the Islamic Resistance in Iraq has claimed responsibility for drone and missile attacks against targets within Israel in retaliation for Israeli attacks on Palestinian civilians in Gaza. The group stated it would continue to "strike enemy strongholds."

In late January, the Islamic Resistance in Iraq announced it had entered its second phase of operations which included blockading the [Mediterranean](19006.md) maritime routes to Israeli ports and disabling the ports.[^cite_note-48]

In November 2024, US officials confirmed that US and partner forces had intercepted Iraqi kamikaze drones launched against Israel. By late October, the ISI had launched drones on an average of around five times a day. In one 24-hour period in October, the ISI launched eight drones at Israel.[^cite_note-49]

Shortly after, Israeli news outlets reported that Israel may target the IRI for its campaign against Israel during its wars [on Gaza](74998519.md) and [on Lebanon](75005425.md). Unnamed officials allegedly told outlets that satellites monitored the transfer of ballistic missiles and related equipment from Iran to Iraqi territory.[^cite_note-50]

## Intervention in the Israel–Hezbollah conflict

Main article: [Israel–Hezbollah conflict (2023–present)](75005425.md)

On 9 January 2024, the [Kata'ib Hezbollah](23626646.md) spokesperson Jafar al-Husseini warned that the Islamic Resistance in Iraq would help [Hezbollah](13919.md) fight Israel if war erupted between the two sides.[^cite_note-107] This statement came a few weeks after the Islamic Resistance in Iraq claimed responsibility for a drone attack on the [Karish gas field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karish_gas_field) which Lebanon [claims to hold sovereignty over](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli%E2%80%93Lebanese_maritime_border_dispute).[^cite_note-108] In June 2024, many Iraqi officials within the IRI network vowed to support Hezbollah with soldiers if the conflict with Israel escalated into an all-out war.[^cite_note-109]

On 24 June 2024, [Qais al-Khazali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qais_al-Khazali), the leader of [Asa'ib Ahl al-Haq](26106154.md), stated in a televised speech that if the US continued to support Israel in expanding the war to Lebanon and Hezbollah, the group would begin to attack US interests in Iraq and the Middle East.[^cite_note-110]

## Involvement in the 2024 Iran–Israel conflict

Main article: [2024 Iran–Israel conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Iran%E2%80%93Israel_conflict)

In Iran's [missile and drone strikes against Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2024_Iranian_strikes_against_Israel), the Islamic Resistance in Iraq had contributed to the attacks on Israel, alongside Iran, the [Yemeni Houthis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houthi_movement), and an unidentified faction in Syria. This was by their launch of drones and missiles at targets in Israel.[^cite_note-TS-111][^cite_note-112][^cite_note-Houthi-113] The United States, United Kingdom, and Jordan intercepted many of the drones but many did hit various locations within Israel and the occupied Golan Heights.[^cite_note-JD-114][^cite_note-115]

In response to alleged Israeli bombardment of [Popular Mobilization Forces](45671374.md) bases in Iraq, which killed a soldier and injured several others, the Islamic Resistance in Iraq claimed a drone attack targeting [Eilat](66162.md).[^cite_note-116]

## Intervention in the United States–Houthi conflict

Main article: [United States–Houthi conflict (2023–present)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States%E2%80%93Houthi_conflict_\(2023%E2%80%93present\))

On 11 January 2024, the Islamic Resistance in Iraq said that if Yemen is [attacked by the US and UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_missile_strikes_against_Yemen), "we will attack the Americans' base with everything in our power".[^cite_note-117] After the initial US-UK airstrikes on Yemen, there were reports of a bomb and sirens being heard at the [US Embassy in Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Embassy_in_Iraq).[^cite_note-118] In the same month, the Islamic Resistance in Iraq launched a [drone attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_22_drone_attack) on a US base in Jordan, killing three US soldiers and injuring 47 others.

## US retaliation airstrikes

Main articles: [Attacks on US bases during the Gaza war § US response](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacks_on_US_bases_during_the_Gaza_war#US_response), and [February 2024 United States airstrikes in Iraq and Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_2024_United_States_airstrikes_in_Iraq_and_Syria)

On 21 November 2023, a US AC-130-gunship struck a [Kata'ib Hezbollah](23626646.md) vehicle near [Abu Ghraib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Ghraib), Iraq in response to the Islamic Resistance In Iraq's 20 November attack on Ain al Assad Airbase.[^cite_note-119]

On 2 February 2024, the US launched retaliatory airstrikes targeting Iran-backed militias in Iraq and Syria, in response to an attack that killed three US troops in Jordan. On 7 February, a US drone strike on a vehicle in the Mashtal neighborhood of Baghdad killed a commander of Kata'ib Hezbollah [Abu Baqir Al-Saadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Baqir_Al-Saadi).

## Intervention in the 2026 US-Iran war

Main article: [Iraq in the 2026 Iran war](82560534.md)

On 14 March 2026, the [Kataib Hezbollah](23626646.md) faction launched a drone that breached the air defenses of the [Camp Victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Victory) base and managed to strike it.[^cite_note-120][^cite_note-121] On 17 March 2026, the [Saraya Awliya al-Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraya_Awliya_al-Dam) faction published a footage of a reconnaissance drone flying above the US embassy compound in baghdad. According to an observer the footage is consistent with the footage obtained from a [Fiber optic drone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiber_optic_drone) suggesting that the group had acquired such advanced technology.[^cite_note-122] On the 21st March 2026 the [ Ashab al-Kahf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashab_al-Kahf_\(militant_group\)) faction claimed responsibility for killing a French soldier in an attack on a base in Iraq,[^cite_note-123] and on the same day set the [Camp Victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Victory) base ablaze.[^cite_note-124][^cite_note-125] On 25 March 2026 an [AN/MPQ-64 Sentinel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AN/MPQ-64_Sentinel) along with a [ Black Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UH-60) was struck at the [ Victory Base Complex in Bahgdad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victory_Base_Complex) by an IRI fibre optic drone.[^cite_note-126][^cite_note-127]
