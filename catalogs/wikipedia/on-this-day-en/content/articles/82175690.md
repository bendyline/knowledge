The **Seoul High Court** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 서울고등법원) is a [high court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_court) in [South Korea](27019.md).[^cite_note-1] It is composed of the [Chief Justice of the High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Justice_of_the_Supreme_Court_of_Korea) and a number of judges specified by the [Supreme Court](4899540.md) Rules, and its adjudication power is subordinate to the [Consensus Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consensus_decision-making), which consists of three judges. [Seoul](19159283.md), [Incheon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incheon), [Gyeonggi Province](323103.md), and [Gangwon Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangwon_Province,_South_Korea) are under its jurisdiction.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## History

On August 1, 1908, during the [Korean Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Empire), the Kyŏngsŏng Appeals Court was established for the first time.[^cite_note-4]

In 1996, the Seoul High Court sentenced former president [Chun Doo-hwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chun_Doo-hwan) to life in prison for insurrection and mutiny. Another former president, [Roh Tae-woo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roh_Tae-woo), was given a 17-year sentence for his involvement.[^cite_note-5]

In 2018, the court sentenced former president [Park Geun-hye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Geun-hye) to 25 years in prison for charges including bribery, extortion, and abuse of power.[^cite_note-6]

In 2020, the Supreme Court upheld Seoul High Court's ruling sentencing former President [Lee Myung-bak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Myung-bak) to 17 years in prison for embezzlement and bribes. The High Court also fined him 13 billion [won](298884.md).[^cite_note-7]

In 2021, the Seoul High Court overturned the [Seoul Eastern District Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_Eastern_District_Court)'s conviction of [Shinhan Financial Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinhan_Financial_Group) chairman Cho Yong-byoung for corrupt hiring practices.[^cite_note-8]

In February 2023, the Seoul High Court overturned a lower court decision allowing the NHIS to not expand equal benefits to same-sex couples.[^cite_note-9] In April, the court upheld the [Fair Trade Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_Trade_Commission_\(South_Korea\))'s KRW 1 trillion fine and six out of ten of its corrective orders on [Qualcomm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualcomm) after the company appealed in 2017.[^cite_note-10] In November, the court ruled that the Government of Japan would have to compensate former [comfort women](302767.md),[^cite_note-11] which Japan's [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](868429.md) stated was a violation of "State immunity under international law."[^cite_note-12]

In February 2025, Seoul High Court upheld a ruling by the [Seoul Central District Court](82174232.md) acquitting [Samsung Electronics](46445426.md) Chairman [Lee Jae-yong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Jae-yong) of all 19 charges in relation to the 2015 merger of [Cheil Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheil_Industries) and [Samsung C\&T](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung_C\&T_Corporation), including stock manipulation, breach of trust, and accounting fraud.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

In February 2026, the court sentenced former Supreme Court Chief Justice [Yang Sung-tae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_Sung-tae) to six months in prison for interference in trials. This marked the first instance of a Supreme Court Chief Justice in South Korea being found guilty in a criminal trial.[^cite_note-15]
