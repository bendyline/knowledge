# List of United States immigration and nationality laws

This is a [dynamic list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia\:WikiProject_Lists#Dynamic_lists) and may never be able to satisfy particular standards for completeness. You can help by [editing the page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special\:EditPage/List_of_United_States_immigration_and_nationality_laws) to add missing items, with references to [reliable sources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia\:Reliable_sources).

Many acts of Congress and executive actions relating to [immigration to the United States](15051.md) and [citizenship of the United States](25151566.md) have been enacted in the United States. Most immigration and nationality laws are codified in [Title 8 of the United States Code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title_8_of_the_United_States_Code).
