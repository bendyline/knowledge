The **N6** is a [national route](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_route_\(South_Africa\)) in [South Africa](17416221.md) that connects [East London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_London,_Eastern_Cape) with [Bloemfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein), via [Komani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queenstown,_South_Africa) and [Maletswai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliwal_North).[^cite_note-\:0-1] It runs roughly from north to south, connecting the [N1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N1_road_\(South_Africa\)) with the [N2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N2_road_\(South_Africa\)).

## Route

Detailed Route

### Eastern Cape

The N6 begins in the city of [East London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_London,_Eastern_Cape) in the [Buffalo City Metropolitan Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo_City_Metropolitan_Municipality) of the [Eastern Cape](313645.md) Province, next to the [Nahoon River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahoon_River), at an interchange with the [N2 highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N2_\(South_Africa\)). South of the highway, it is the [R72 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R72_\(South_Africa\)) towards the East London CBD.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

It begins by going northwest from the N2 interchange for 31 kilometres to the town of [Macleantown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macleantown), where it leaves the Buffalo City Metropolitan Municipality. It continues northwest for another 21 kilometres to meet the [R63 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R63_\(South_Africa\)) at an intersection just north-east of [Kei Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kei_Road).[^cite_note-\:0-1]

It continues northwest for 19 kilometers to the town of [Stutterheim](3134247.md). It proceeds northwest for another 47 kilometers to the town of [Cathcart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathcart,_Eastern_Cape). It proceeds north-north-west for 54 kilometres, crossing the [Black Kei River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Kei_River), to reach [Queenstown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queenstown,_Eastern_Cape) (renamed Komani in February 2016[^cite_note-2016_rename-2]).[^cite_note-\:0-1]

14 kilometers before Komani, the [R61](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R61_\(South_Africa\)) meets the N6 and they are [one road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concurrency_\(road\)) into the town of Komani as Louis Botha Road and Cathcart Street. In the Stuttaford suburb of Komani, the R61 becomes its own road westwards at the roundabout junction with Barrable Street.

The N6 continues northwards for 162 kilometres as the [Penhoek Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penhoek_Pass), bypassing [Sterkstroom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterkstroom), meeting the [R56](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R56_\(South_Africa\)) and passing through [Jamestown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamestown,_Eastern_Cape) (renamed James Calata in 2015[^cite_note-3]), to the town of [Aliwal North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliwal_North) (renamed Maletswai in 2016[^cite_note-4]), where it meets the [R58](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R58_\(South_Africa\)). At the junction with Barkly Street in Maletswai, the N6 makes a left turn and proceeds to cross the [Orange River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_River) into the [Free State](447006.md) as the General Hertzog Bridge.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

### Free State

From the [Orange River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_River) crossing at [Maletswai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maletswai_Local_Municipality), the N6 goes north-north-east for 34 kilometres to the town of [Rouxville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouxville), where it meets the southern terminus of the [R26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R26_\(South_Africa\)). It turns to the north-west and continues for 37 kilometres, crossing the [Caledon River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caledon_River), to the town of [Smithfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smithfield,_Free_State), where the [R701](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R701_\(South_Africa\)) joins it in the southern suburbs before they split in the northern suburbs.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

From [Smithfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smithfield,_Free_State), the N6 continues north-north-west for 70 kilometres to the town of [Reddersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reddersburg), where it is joined by the [R717](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R717_\(South_Africa\)) from [Edenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edenburg) in the southern suburbs before they split in the central area. From Reddersburg, the N6 makes a direct line for [Bloemfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein), going northwards for 49 kilometres to reach a four-way junction south-west of the Bloemfontein suburb of [Mangaung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangaung) (which is also the name of the [Metropolitan Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangaung_Metropolitan_Municipality) in which the city is located). As the road northwards from this junction is the [M30 metropolitan route](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M30_\(Bloemfontein\)) towards the Mangaung suburb and [Bloemfontein Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein_Central), the N6 becomes the road westwards and proceeds to end at the next junction, which is an interchange with the [N1 highway](1211545.md) ([Bloemfontein Western Bypass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein_Western_Bypass)).[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## History

Prior to 1970, the N6 began from [King William's Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_William's_Town) in the [Buffalo City Metropolitan Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo_City_Metropolitan_Municipality) and proceeded through [Stutterheim](3134247.md).[^cite_note-\:1-5] Today, the road from King William's Town to Stutterheim is designated as the [R346 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R346_\(South_Africa\)).[^cite_note-\:0-1] Also, the N6 used to end at its current junction with the [R717](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R717_\(South_Africa\)) south of the [Reddersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reddersburg) Town Centre, as the R717 from [Edenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edenburg) to Reddersburg, together with the N6 from Reddersburg to [Bloemfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloemfontein), formed part of the [N1 national route](1211545.md).[^cite_note-\:1-5]
