**Óscar Puente Santiago** (pronounced [\[ˈoskaɾ ˈpwente\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish); born 15 November 1968) is a [Spanish Socialist Workers' Party](272596.md) (PSOE) politician who has served as [Minister of Transport and Sustainable Mobility](19633684.md) since 2023. He served as a city councillor in [Valladolid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valladolid) from 2007 to 2023 and as the city's mayor from 2015 to 2023.

## Biography

Born in [Valladolid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valladolid), Puente was the son and grandson of socialists. He achieved a master's degree in Political Management in 1992 and a Law degree from the [University of Valladolid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Valladolid) a year later. After two years of internship, he began practicing law in 1995.[^cite_note-abc-1]

Active in the [Spanish Socialist Workers' Party](272596.md) (PSOE) from 1990, he began working for the party as a General Vice Secretary in the provincial executive in 2004, having secretary-general four years earlier. Elected to Valladolid City Council in 2007, he replaced [Soraya Rodríguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soraya_Rodr%C3%ADguez) as party spokesperson in the city hall the following year, and in 2009 became secretary general of the PSOE in the city.[^cite_note-abc-1]

In September 2010, Puente was endorsed by the PSOE at local and provincial level to be their mayoral candidate in Valladolid in the [2011 Spanish local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Spanish_local_elections).[^cite_note-2] In the elections in May, [People's Party](404727.md) (PP) incumbent [Francisco Javier León de la Riva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Javier_Le%C3%B3n_de_la_Riva) took a majority and rose from 15 seats to 17, while the PSOE fell from 13 to 9.[^cite_note-3] In the [2015 local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Spanish_local_elections), the PSOE had eight seats, and with the support of the seven seats held by local left-wing parties, they gained the majority to make him mayor and end the 20-year tenure of León de la Riva.[^cite_note-4]

In the [2023 local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Spanish_local_elections), Puente's party took 11 of 27 seats, the same as the PP of [Jesús Julio Carnero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jes%C3%BAs_Julio_Carnero), though the PSOE received marginally more votes. Carnero was able to form a majority government with the three councillors of [Vox](41928488.md), becoming mayor.[^cite_note-5] He served as [Minister of Transport and Sustainable Mobility](19633684.md) during the [2024 Spain floods](78242552.md). A controversial minister for his blunt communication style, he received attention online for his frequent social media updates on repair work to damaged highways and train lines.[^cite_note-6]

### Controversies

In a municipal session in June 2018, Puente questioned the management skills of [Citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens_\(Spanish_political_party\)) spokesperson Pilar Vicente by pointing out that she used to work as a store assistant at a shopping centre. Citizens leader [Albert Rivera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Rivera) accused Puente of [classism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_discrimination) and [male chauvinism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Male_chauvinism) over the remark, calling for him to resign.[^cite_note-7] Puente said that he was referring to what he believed to be falsehoods in Vicente's [curriculum vitae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curriculum_vitae), and that Rivera had exaggerated the episode.[^cite_note-8]

In May 2024, a diplomatic row erupted between Spain and [Argentina](18951905.md) after Puente suggested that Argentina's president [Javier Milei](56599840.md) had used [drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug) during his election campaign the year prior. In response, Milei released a statement condemning the "slander and insults" against him, and accusing prime minister [Pedro Sánchez](43297512.md)'s policies of having brought "death and poverty" to Spaniards. In turn, Spain's [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Spain\)) rejected Milei's statement as "unfounded".[^cite_note-9]

In July 2025 Puente faced controversy for online comments that incorrectly referred to [French Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_France) students as "[Israeli](181296.md) brats" following their disputed removal from a Spanish flight.[^cite_note-10]

## Personal life

Puente is married to judge Laura Soria Velasco and has two daughters. His daughter Carmen took part on the [sixth season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Voz_Kids_\(Spanish_season_6\)) of [*La Voz Kids*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Voz_Kids_\(Spanish_TV_series\)) in 2021, reaching the semi-finals.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]
