The **Netherlands Armed Forces** ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *Nederlandse krijgsmacht*) are the [military forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military) of the [Kingdom of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_the_Netherlands) (mainland [Netherlands](21148.md) in Europe and islands of the [Dutch Caribbean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Caribbean)). The armed forces consist of four service branches: the [Royal Netherlands Navy](198478.md) (*Koninklijke Marine*), the [Royal Netherlands Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Army) (*Koninklijke Landmacht*), the [Royal Netherlands Air and Space Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Air_and_Space_Force) (*Koninklijke Luchtmacht*) and the [Royal Netherlands Marechaussee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Marechaussee) (*Koninklijke Marechaussee*). The service branches are supplemented by various joint support organizations. In addition, local conscript forces exist on the Dutch Caribbean islands of [Aruba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aruba) and [Curaçao](65205.md). These operate under the auspices of the Royal Netherlands Navy and the [Netherlands Marine Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Marine_Corps). The armed forces are part of the [Ministry of Defence](9473113.md).

Supreme command of the armed forces is determined in Article 97 of the [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Netherlands), which states "The Government shall have supreme authority over the armed forces". Service members swear allegiance to the King in his role as head of state.&#x20;

The [military ranks of the Netherlands armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_ranks_of_the_Netherlands_armed_forces) are similar to those of fellow [NATO](21133.md) member states and were established by [Royal Decree](322949.md). The highest-ranking [officer](36301328.md) in the Dutch military is the [Chief of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Defence_\(Netherlands\)), who is a four-star officer (NATO OF-9).

After initial cooperation with the [German army](288188.md) in 1995 through a [Münster-located corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I._German/Dutch_Corps) and increasing cross-linking during the following decades, all three Royal Netherlands Army combat brigades have fully integrated into German divisions as of March 2023.[^cite_note-4]

In 2024 the Netherlands Armed Forces was awarded the [Wateler Peace Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wateler_Peace_Prize).[^cite_note-5]

## Foundation in law and purpose

The Netherlands armed forces exist by declaration in the [constitution of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Netherlands).[^cite_note-6] Article 97 of the constitution determines that the armed forces exist

- To defend the [Kingdom of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_the_Netherlands) and its interests in the world; and

- To protect and advance the international rule of law.

This means that the role and responsibility of the Dutch military in international stability and peacekeeping is constitutionally determined.

The same article of the constitution determines that supreme command of the Dutch military resides with the [Government of the Netherlands](59761.md). This has been the case since the constitution was changed in 1983; before then, supreme command of the armed forces of the Netherlands was held by the [Monarch](23416611.md).

## Overview

### Organization



Chief of Defence [general](148537.md) [Onno Eichelsheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onno_Eichelsheim) during the acceptance ceremony of the command over the Netherlands Armed Forces, April 2021.

The existence of, authority over, and tasks of the armed forces are determined in the [constitution of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Netherlands). [The Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_Netherlands), which consists of cabinet members led by the [Prime Minister](273726.md) and the [King](23416611.md), functions as the head of the armed forces. Although service members swear allegiance to the [King of the Netherlands](100384.md), the monarch does not hold the position of [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief). Therefore, in practice, important decisions are made by Cabinet, while the [Minister of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Ministers_of_Defence_of_the_Netherlands) assumes political responsibility over the Ministry.

The [Ministry of Defence](9473113.md) is the government ministry which is responsible for formulating and executing defence policy. The ministry consists of the Minister, [State Secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Ministers_of_Defence_of_the_Netherlands#List_of_State_Secretaries_for_Defence), the Central Staff, the Netherlands Armed Forces and two independent support organizations. The Central Staff (*Bestuursstaf*) of the Ministry comprises several directorates responsible for executing policy, advising the Minister and State Secretary, and controlling the Defence organization. The Central Staff is led by the highest civil servant of the defence organization, the Secretary General. Moreover, the Central Staff incorporates the Defence Staff, which is the highest military organ under command of the [Chief of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Defence_\(Netherlands\)). The Chief of Defence is the military leader of the Netherlands Armed Forces and the most senior military adviser to the Minister of Defence. The Chief of Defence has command over the Armed Forces, the [Netherlands Special Operations Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Special_Operations_Command) (NLD SOCOM) and the [Defence Cyber Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Cyber_Command). Several special executive organizations, including the [Military Intelligence and Security Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Military_Intelligence_and_Security_Service) and the office of the Inspector General, are incorporated into the Central Staff as well.

### Personnel

The Netherlands Armed Forces are a professional military, [conscription in the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscription_in_the_Netherlands) having been suspended in 1996 with the exception of Aruba and Curaçao.[^cite_note-7] All military branches are open to female recruits. In 2018 the Ministry of Defence announced that the submarine service will begin accepting female recruits for positions as officer, NCO and sailor.[^cite_note-8]

The [Dutch Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Ministry_of_Defence) employs over 70,000 personnel, including both civilian and military personnel.[^cite_note-9] The distribution of personnel in the Defence organization, including the Armed Forces, on 1 September 2025 was as follows:

### Ranks

Main article: [Military ranks of the Netherlands armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_ranks_of_the_Netherlands_armed_forces)

The Dutch military is part of the [NATO](21133.md) militaries and therefore conforms to the structure of a NATO military. It also uses conforming [rank structures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranks_and_insignia_of_NATO).

### Oath

All Dutch military personnel, [officers](36301328.md) and [enlisted personnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlisted_personnel), are required to take an [oath of allegiance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oath_of_allegiance). This oath is recorded in the law on General Military Personnel Regulations (Algemeen Militair Ambtenarenregelement) in Article 126a and states the following:[^cite_note-11]

*"Ik zweer (beloof) trouw aan de Koning, gehoorzaamheid aan de wetten en onderwerping aan de krijgstucht. Zo waarlijk helpe mij God Almachtig (Dat beloof ik)."*

Translated in English:

*"I swear (pledge) loyalty to the King, obedience to the law and submission to martial discipline. So help me God (That, I pledge)."*

### Unionized military

Unlike many military organizations, Dutch military members are allowed to form and join unions.[^cite_note-12]

There is a wide variety of unions, including unions exclusive to officers or particular service branches. Some of the larger unions include:

- *Algemene Federatie van Militair Personeel* (AFMP, [General Federation of Military Personnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Federation_of_Military_Personnel)), which was recognized by the Dutch government in 1966. The AFMP is a member of the [Federation of Dutch Trade Unions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Dutch_Trade_Unions) (FNV).

- *Algemeen Christelijke Organisatie van Militairen* (ACOM, General Christian Organization for Military Personnel). The ACOM is a member of the [Christian National Trade Union Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_National_Trade_Union_Federation) (CNV).

- *Gezamenlijke Officieren Verenigingen en Middelbaar en Hoger Burgerpersoneel bij Defensie* (GOV/MHB, United Officers Associations and Middle- and Senior level Civilian Personnel)

- *Vakbond voor Defensiepersoneel VBM* (VBM, Union for Defence Personnel).

## Service branches

### Royal Netherlands Navy

Main article: [Royal Netherlands Navy](198478.md)



[HNLMS *De Zeven Provinciën*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HNLMS_De_Zeven_Provinci%C3%ABn_\(F802\)) during exercise At-Sea Demo/Formidable Shield 2021 on the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md).

The Royal Netherlands Navy ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *Koninklijke Marine*) is the Netherlands Armed Forces' maritime force which consists of [24 commissioned ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_active_Royal_Netherlands_Navy_ships) and an additional number of support ships of various types. The Navy is commanded by the [Commander of the Royal Netherlands Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Royal_Netherlands_Navy) ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *Commandant Zeestrijdkrachten*), an officer in the rank of [Vice Admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_Admiral) or [Lieutenant General of the Marines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_General), who reports directly to the [Chief of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Defence_\(Netherlands\)). In addition to being the commanding officer of the Royal Netherlands Navy, the *Commandant Zeestrijdkrachten* holds the position of [Admiral Benelux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiral_Benelux). As such, the *Commandant Zeestrijdkrachten* is the commanding officer of the operational units of the Royal Netherlands Navy and the [Belgian Naval Component](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgian_Naval_Component).

The surface fleet of the Navy consist of [frigates](11464.md), [amphibious warfare ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_warfare_ship), [patrol vessels](536773.md), [mine-countermeasure vessels](217118.md) and multiple [auxiliary ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auxiliary_ship). All major surface vessels of the Royal Netherlands Navy are constructed at the Dutch [shipyard](213654.md) [Damen Schelde Naval Shipbuilding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damen_Schelde_Naval_Shipbuilding). This includes the technologically advanced air-defence frigates of the [*De Zeven Provinciën*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Zeven_Provinci%C3%ABn-class_frigate). [Thales Nederland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thales_Nederland) produces various types of high-end [sensors](235757.md) and [radars](25676.md) for the Dutch fleet.

The [Royal Netherlands Navy Submarine Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Navy_Submarine_Service) ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *Onderzeedienst*) was established in 1906 and is responsible for the operation of all [Dutch submarines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_submarines_of_the_Netherlands). As of 2021, the *Onderzeedienst* operates four [*Walrus*-class submarines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walrus-class_submarine) and one [submarine tender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submarine_tender), [HNLMS *Mercuur*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HNLMS_Mercuur_\(A900\)). The Dutch submarines fulfill a considerable role within [NATO](21133.md) as their small size and expeditionary capacities allow them to operate in waters that are off-limits to larger submarines. The Ministry of Defence initiated a replacement program in November 2014. in March 2024 the winning bid was announced with the newer class being called [Orka-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orka-class_submarine) and are planned to enter service in the late 2030s.[The Netherlands Select Naval Group for its New Submarines](https://www.navalnews.com/naval-news/2024/03/the-netherlands-select-naval-group-for-its-new-submarines/)

#### Netherlands Marine Corps

Main article: [Netherlands Marine Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Marine_Corps)

The Netherlands Marine Corps is the Navy's [naval infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marines) corps. The Corps consist of two battalion-sized Marine Combat Groups, various support units and the [Netherlands Maritime Special Operations Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Maritime_Special_Operations_Forces). The Marines are specialized in [amphibious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_warfare), [arctic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_warfare) and [mountain warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_warfare) as well as [special operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_operations). The Corps operates a fleet of landing craft that can operate from one of the two Navy's [amphibious transport docks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_transport_dock).

On 7 July 2026, on the sidelines of the [NATO summit](11190132.md) in [Ankara](802.md), [UK Prime Minister](24150.md) [Keir Starmer](18681491.md) and [Dutch Prime Minister](273726.md) [Rob Jetten](58754918.md) signed a £2.4 billion (US\$3.2 billion) maritime partnership agreement to strengthen bilateral naval cooperation and support the joint procurement of new amphibious transport ships. The deal equips [British](31730.md) and [Dutch forces](21162.md) with eight advanced amphibious transport ships (four for each nation) to strengthen allied.[^cite_note-13]

### Royal Netherlands Army

Main article: [Royal Netherlands Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Army)

The Royal Netherlands Army ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *Koninklijke Landmacht*) consists of regular Army personnel and the [National Reserve Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Reserve_Corps). The Army is headed by the [Commander of the Royal Netherlands Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Royal_Netherlands_Army) ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *Commandant Landstrijdkrachten*), its headquarters are located on the *Kromhoutkazerne* in [Utrecht](50333.md). The core fighting element of the Army consist of three combat brigades: [11 Airmobile Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/11th_Airmobile_Brigade_\(Netherlands\)), [13 Light Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13th_Light_Brigade_\(Netherlands\)) and [43 Mechanized Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/43rd_Mechanized_Brigade_\(Netherlands\)). The brigade-sized [Operational Support Command Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operational_Support_Command_Land) fields a variety of [combat support](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_support) and [combat service support](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_service_support) units while the Army's [special operations forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_operations_forces) are part of the *[Korps Commandotroepen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korps_Commandotroepen)*. The Army's [infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infantry) regiments fulfil several distinct roles, these include [air assault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_assault), [armoured infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanized_infantry), [light infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_infantry) and [special operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_operations). Furthermore, the Army fields [cavalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavalry), [artillery](2508.md), [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_engineering) and [medical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_medic) regiments.

### Royal Netherlands Air and Space Force

Main article: [Royal Netherlands Air and Space Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Air_and_Space_Force)

The Royal Netherlands Air and Space Force ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *Koninklijke Luchtmacht*) is the [military aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_aviation) and space warfare branch of the Netherlands Armed Forces and is led by the [Commander of the Royal Netherlands Air and Space Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Royal_Netherlands_Air_and_Space_Force) (*Commandant Lucht-en Ruimtestrijdkrachten*). The Air and Space Force operates a diverse fleet of [fixed-wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixed-wing_aircraft) and [rotary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotorcraft) aircraft, in addition to operating and maintaining multiple [airbases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbase). Moreover, Air and Space Force personnel contributes to the [Joint Ground-based Air Defence Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Ground-based_Air_Defence_Command) by operating various air-defence systems while other units are dedicated to [force protection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Force_protection). The Air and Space Force operates modern [fighter aircraft](10929.md), such as the [F-16 Fighting Falcon](11642.md) and [F-35 Lightning II](11812.md), tankers, transports, helicopters, unmanned aerial vehicles, and various types of training aircraft. Until 2025, the Royal Netherlands Air and Space Force was called the Royal Netherlands Air Force.



Marechaussee providing security at [Amsterdam Airport Schiphol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam_Airport_Schiphol) with armoured [Toyota Land Cruiser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_Land_Cruiser).

### Royal Netherlands Marechaussee

Main article: [Royal Marechaussee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Marechaussee)

The Royal Netherlands Marechaussee ([Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *Koninklijke Marechaussee*) is a [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie) force which performs both [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_police) and civilian [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police) duties. In addition to the military police duties, the Marechaussee has a wide variety of duties and responsibilities. This includes guarding the [national borders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_border) and [airports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport), fighting [illegal immigration](7124412.md) and [transnational crime](6760775.md) and guarding the [royal palaces](23416611.md).

The Marechaussee was established as one of the separate Armed Forces in 1998; before then the Marechaussee was organized as one of the arms within the Army. While the Royal Netherlands Marechaussee operates under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Defence, the branch often performs duties delegated by the [Ministry of Justice and Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_and_Security) and the [Ministry of the Interior and Kingdom Relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Interior_and_Kingdom_Relations). Moreover, several brigades of the Marechaussee are permanently stationed in the [Dutch Caribbean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Caribbean).

### Afghanistan

#### Contribution to ISAF

Main article: [Task Force Uruzgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Task_Force_Uruzgan)

As part of [Operation Enduring Freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Enduring_Freedom), the Netherlands deployed aircraft which were integrated in the European Participating Air Force (EPAF) in support of ground operations in [Afghanistan](737.md). Additionally, Dutch naval frigates were tasked with policing the waters of the Middle East and Indian Ocean. Between 2001 and 2003, a reinforced army company was deployed to Afghanistan to provide support in maintaining public order and providing security in and around the capital [Kabul](16826.md).[^cite_note-14] Furthermore, military assistance was provided to the [Afghan National Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_National_Army) and local security forces. The troops were deployed under the command of [NATO](21133.md)'s [International Security Assistance Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Security_Assistance_Force) mission.



A patrol of Task Force Uruzgan moving towards an over-watch position near Mirabad Valley, in 2008.

the [Battle of Chora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Chora).[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-chora-19] On 18 April 2008, on the second day of his command, the son of *then*-[Chief of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Defence_\(Netherlands\)) general [Peter van Uhm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_van_Uhm), Lieutenant Dennis van Uhm, was one of two servicemen killed by a road-side explosion.[^cite_note-21] As of 1 September 2008, the Netherlands had a total of 1,770 troops in Afghanistan excluding special forces troops.[^cite_note-22]

Between 2002 and 2021, Dutch military personnel worked successively in the Afghan provinces of Kabul, Baghlan, Kandahar, Uruzgan, Kunduz and Balkh, with the aim, among other things, to bring stability and to build up the security apparatus, the army and the police.[^cite_note-23] In total, 25 Dutch servicemen were [killed in action](368513.md) during the deployment.[^cite_note-24]

#### Resolute Support Mission

From 2015 until 2021, approximately 160 Dutch troops of the [Korps Commandotroepen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korps_Commandotroepen), [NLMARSOF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NLMARSOF) and multiple conventional support elements were deployed to the city of [Mazar-e-Sharif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazar-e-Sharif) as part of NATO's [Resolute Support Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resolute_Support_Mission).[^cite_note-25] Dutch troops co-operated with personnel of the German [Kommando Spezialkräfte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kommando_Spezialkr%C3%A4fte) as part of the German-Dutch lead Special Operations Advisory Team (SOAT). The SOAT provided advice and assistance during operations of the Afghan [police tactical unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_tactical_unit), the Afghan Territorial Force-888 (ATF-888).[^cite_note-26] The SOAT was granted authority to deploy in the entirety of Afghanistan in 2019.[^cite_note-27] The operations ended with the withdrawal of all United States and allied troops from Afghanistan in 2021.

### Iraq

#### Multinational force in Iraq

A contingent of 1,345 Army and [Marines Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Marine_Corps) personnel, supported by [Royal Netherlands Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Air_and_Space_Force) helicopters, was deployed to [Iraq](7515928.md) in 2003, based at Camp Smitty near [As Samawah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samawah) (southern Iraq) with responsibility for the [Muthanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muthanna_Governorate) Province, as part of the [Multinational force in Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multinational_force_in_Iraq).[^cite_note-28] On June 1, 2004, the Dutch government renewed their stay through 2005.[^cite_note-nimh-29] The Netherlands removed its troops from Iraq in March 2005, leaving half a dozen liaison officers until late 2005.[^cite_note-30] The Netherlands lost two soldiers in separate attacks.[^cite_note-nimh-29]

#### Intervention against ISIL

Main article: [Dutch military intervention against ISIL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_military_intervention_against_ISIL)



The last Dutch F-16 detachment of Air Task Force Middle East returning home from Jordan.

On 24 September 2014, the [Dutch government](59761.md) announced its participation in the military campaign against [ISIL](9087364.md), and sent six [F-16 fighter jets](11642.md) to Iraq to bomb ISIL. Their motivations to join this war: ISIL's advance in Iraq and [Syria](7515849.md), while displaying "unprecedented violence" and "perpetrating terrible crimes against population groups", formed "a direct threat for that region"; ISIL's advance in Iraq and Syria "causes instability at the borders of [Europe](9239.md)" which threatens "our own \[Dutch] safety". Figures requested by *[RTL Nieuws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTL_Nieuws)* in August 2015 showed that the [Netherlands](21148.md) was among the most active countries within the coalition, third behind only the [United States](3434750.md) and the [United Kingdom](31717.md).[^cite_note-31] In January 2016, the Netherlands extended their bombings of ISIL to Syrian territory. By the end of July 2016 the Dutch Air Task Force flew more than 2100 missions and carried out over 1800 air strikes.[^cite_note-32] At the end of the Dutch contribution to the Air Task Force, in December 2018, the [Royal Netherlands Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Air_and_Space_Force) had flown over 3000 missions and conducted approximately 2100 air strikes.[^cite_note-33]

From 2015 until the spring of 2018, KCT and NLMARSOF [special operations forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_operations_forces) deployed advice and assist teams to northern Iraq in cooperation with the Belgian [Special Forces Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Forces_Group_\(Belgium\)).[^cite_note-34] During this deployment, they provided support to Kurdish [Peshmerga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshmerga) and [Iraqi Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Army) forces before, during and after operations in the [battle against ISIL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_military_intervention_against_ISIL), as part of the [Combined Joint Task Force – Operation Inherent Resolve](44618350.md).[^cite_note-35]



[Lynx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westland_Lynx) helicopter hovering over MV *Taipan* during the liberation operation, the boarding team rappelled onto one of the containers aboard the ship.

### Counterpiracy

As a [maritime nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_nation) and birthplace of the earliest [maritime law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiralty_law), the Netherlands have historically highly valued the [*mare liberum*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mare_Liberum) principle. Hence, the Dutch government decided to contribute a significant amount of naval assets to combating [piracy off the coast of Somalia](17015903.md) since its most recent surge starting in 2005. The Royal Netherlands Navy was an active contributor to NATO's [Operation Allied Protector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Allied_Protector) and [Operation Ocean Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Ocean_Shield), as well as to the [European Union](9317.md)'s [Operation Atalanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Atalanta). Ships partaking in these missions included [*De Zeven Provinciën*-class frigates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Zeven_Provinci%C3%ABn-class_frigate), [landing platform docks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_transport_dock) [HNLMS *Rotterdam*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HNLMS_Rotterdam_\(L800\)) and [HNLMS *Johan de Witt*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HNLMS_Johan_de_Witt_\(L801\)), and submarines of the [*Walrus*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walrus-class_submarine). Additionally, [surface combatants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_combatant) permanently carried [boarding teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_boarding) of the [Netherlands Marine Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Marine_Corps). These boarding parties were often composed of operators of the [Netherlands Maritime Special Operations Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_Maritime_Special_Operations_Forces) (NLMARSOF). In addition, conventional marine units supply Vessel Protection Detachments (VPDs) which continue to guard Dutch merchant vessels during transits through piracy-prone waters as of 2021.

The Dutch naval forces were regularly engaged in combat. Firefights between the naval ships and Somali pirates have cost the lives of pirates on multiple occasions.[^cite_note-36] During the [Action of 5 April 2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_of_5_April_2010), a boarding team of the [Unit Interventie Mariniers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-Squadron) liberated [container ship](262084.md) MV *Taipan* after rappelling down from [HNLMS *Tromp*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HNLMS_Tromp_\(F803\))'s helicopter onto containers on the ship's deck under the cover of machine gun fire.[^cite_note-37] The successful operation was filmed with a helmet camera, the video footage reached worldwide [news media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_media) and gained millions of views on [YouTube](3524766.md).[^cite_note-38] Moreover, NLMARSOF [frogmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frogman) have successfully conducted sabotage operations of pirates' [motherships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_ship) by clandestinely attaching explosives to the ships' [bilge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilge).[^cite_note-39]

### Mali



Dutch forces patrolling the plains north of [Gao](1255721.md) using [Fennek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fennek) reconnaissance vehicles.

Special forces of the [Korps Commandotroepen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korps_Commandotroepen) were deployed to [Mali](19127.md) since 2014 as part of the UN mission [MINUSMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Multidimensional_Integrated_Stabilization_Mission_in_Mali).[^cite_note-40] The primary task of the Dutch forces was to gather intelligence on local Islamist and rebel groups and to protect the people of Mali against said groups.[^cite_note-41] Since 2016, conventional detachments consisting of [11 Air Assault Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/11th_Airmobile_Brigade_\(Netherlands\)) and [13 Light Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13th_Light_Brigade_\(Netherlands\)) troops were part of the MINUSMA rotations as well. Additionally, the Dutch contribution consisted of a RNLAF [AH-64 Apache](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AH-64_Apache) and [CH-47 Chinook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CH-47_Chinook) detachment which provided the necessary air support and transport for the infantry units on the ground.

On 16 March 2015, a Dutch AH-64D Apache [attack helicopter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_helicopter) of the Dutch MINUSMA air detachment crashed during a firing exercise, killing the two pilots.[^cite_note-42] On 6 July 2016, two servicemen of 11 Air Assault Brigade were killed during a mortar-firing exercise, while a third serviceman was severely wounded.[^cite_note-43] The incident lead to the resignation of the [minister of Defence](9473113.md) [Jeanine Hennis-Plasschaert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeanine_Hennis-Plasschaert) and [Chief of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Defence_\(Netherlands\)) General [Tom Middendorp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Middendorp) after a critical report by the [Dutch Safety Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Safety_Board) found that the safety standards were subpar.[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45] The Netherlands ended their sizable contribution to the peacekeeping mission in May 2019 to send additional troops to Afghanistan instead.[^cite_note-46]
