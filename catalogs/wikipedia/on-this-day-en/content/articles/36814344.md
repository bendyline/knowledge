The **supreme leader of the Islamic Republic of Iran**,[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-20] officially styled as the **leader of the Islamic Revolution**[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] or the **leadership of the Islamic jurist**[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] by the [Iranian Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Constitution), is the highest political and religious authority in [Iran](14653.md), taking precedence above the [president](233913.md) who is the country's *[de jure](43594.md)* [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state). The [armed forces](14305018.md), [judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_system_of_Iran), [state radio and television](819903.md), and other key government organizations such as the [Guardian Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardian_Council) and [Expediency Discernment Council](452730.md) are subject to the supreme leader.[^cite_note-who-25][^cite_note-26]

According to articles 57 and 110 of the Iranian Constitution, the supreme leader supervises the activities of the [legislative](383276.md), [judicial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Iran), and [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Iran) branches and delineates the general policies of the Islamic Republic.[^cite_note-27] The second supreme leader, [Ali Khamenei](385653.md), issued [decrees](322949.md) and made the final decisions on the [economy](64704.md), the [environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environment_of_Iran), [foreign policy](64709.md), [education](1332627.md), national planning, and other aspects of [governance in Iran](50913538.md).[^cite_note-36] Khamenei also made the final decisions on the amount of transparency in [elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Iran),[^cite_note-37] and during his rule dismissed and reinstated [presidential cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Iran) appointees.[^cite_note-news.bbc.co.uk-38]

The office was established by the [Constitution of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Iran) in 1979, pursuant to Ayatollah [Ruhollah Khomeini](26232.md)'s concept of the [Guardianship of the Islamic Jurist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardianship_of_the_Islamic_Jurist),[^cite_note-39] and is a lifetime appointment.[^cite_note-40] Originally the constitution required the supreme leader to be *[Marja'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marja')-e taqlid*, the highest-ranking cleric in the religious laws of [Usuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usuli) [Twelver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelver) [Shia Islam](26961.md); however, in 1989 the constitution [was amended](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Iranian_constitutional_referendum) and simply asked for Islamic "scholarship" to allow the supreme leader to be a lower-ranking cleric.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42]

The style "Supreme Leader" ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): رهبر معظم, [romanised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *rahbar-e mo'azzam*) is commonly used as a sign of respect although the Constitution designates them simply as "Leader" (رهبر, *rahbar*).

According to the constitution (Article 111), the [Assembly of Experts](512725.md) is tasked with electing (following Ayatollah Khomeini), supervising, and dismissing the supreme leader. In practice, the Assembly has never been known to challenge or otherwise publicly oversee any of the supreme leader's decisions[^cite_note-brookings-AoE-43] (all of its meetings and notes are strictly confidential).[^cite_note-44] Members of the Assembly are elected by people in elections, and are approved by bodies (the [Guardian Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardian_Council)) whose members are appointed by the supreme leader or appointed by an individual ([Chief Justice of Iran](35323582.md)) appointed by the supreme leader.

The [United Nations](31769.md) list of Heads of State, Heads of Government, and Ministers for Foreign Affairs of all Member States lists the Iranian president as the official *de jure* head of state and government, rather than the supreme leader himself.[^cite_note-45]

The Islamic Republic of Iran has in [its history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Islamic_Republic_of_Iran) had three supreme leaders: Khomeini, who held the position from 1979 until [his death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_state_funeral_of_Ruhollah_Khomeini) in 1989; Ali Khamenei, who held the position from Khomeini's death until [his assassination](82537558.md) in 2026; and Khamenei's son [Mojtaba Khamenei](23324096.md), who has held the position since 2026.[^cite_note-ajlive3826-46][^cite_note-47]

## Requirements

Since the 1989 amendments to the [Constitution of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Iran), any [jurist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faq%C4%ABh) of [Islamic law](28840.md) can be elected Supreme Leader.[^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49]

## Incorporation in the constitution

### 1979

In March 1979, shortly after [Ruhollah Khomeini](26232.md)'s return from exile and the overthrow of Iran's monarchy, [a national referendum was held throughout Iran with the question "Islamic Republic, yes or no?"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_Iranian_Islamic_Republic_referendum).[^cite_note-duality-50] Although some groups objected to the wording and choice and boycotted the referendum, 98% of those voting, voted "yes".[^cite_note-duality-50] Following this landslide victory, the constitution of Iran of 1906 was declared invalid. A new constitution for an Islamic state was created and ratified [by referendum during the first week of December in 1979](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_Iranian_constitutional_referendum).[^cite_note-auto4-51]

According to [Francis Fukuyama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Fukuyama), the 1979 constitution is a "hybrid" of "theocratic and democratic elements" with much of it based on the ideas Khomeini presented in his published book *[Islamic Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Government)* (*Hukumat-e Islami*).[^cite_note-auto4-51] In the work, Khomeini argued that government must be run in accordance with traditional Islamic *[sharia](28840.md)*, and for this to happen a leading Islamic jurist (*[faqih](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faqih)*) must provide political "guardianship" (*wilayat* or *velayat*) over the people. The leading jurist were known as *[Marja'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marja').*

&#x20;The Constitution stresses the importance of the clergy in government, with Article 4 stating that&#x20;

> all civil, criminal, financial, economic, administrative, cultural, military, political, and all other statutes and regulations (must) be keeping with Islamic measures;…the Islamic legal scholars of the watch council (Shura yi Nigahban) will keep watch over this.[^cite_note-preamble-52]

&#x20;and the importance of the supreme leader. Article 5 states

> during the absence of the removed [Twelfth Imam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_al-Mahdi) (may God hasten his reappearance) government and leadership of the community in the Islamic Republic of Iran belong to the rightful God fearing legal scholar (Faqih) who is recognized and acknowledged as the Islamic leader by the majority of the population.

Article 107 in the constitution mentions Khomeini by name and praises him as the most learned and talented leader for emulation (*marja-i taqlid*). The responsibilities of the supreme leader are vaguely stated in the constitution, thus any 'violation' by the supreme leader would be dismissed almost immediately. As the rest of the clergy governed affairs on a daily basis, the supreme leader is capable of mandating a new decision as per the concept of Vilayat-e Faqih.[^cite_note-Halm-120-53]

The supreme leader does not receive a salary.[^cite_note-54]

### 1989

Shortly before Khomeini's death, a change was made in the constitution of Iran allowing a lower ranking Shia cleric to become the supreme leader. Khomeini had a falling out with his would-be successor [Hussein-Ali Montazeri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hussein-Ali_Montazeri), who disapproved of human rights abuses by the Islamic Republic[^cite_note-55] such as the [mass execution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_executions_of_Iranian_political_prisoners) of political prisoners in late summer and early autumn 1988. Montazeri was demoted as a marja and Khomeini chose a new successor, a relatively low-ranking member of the clergy, [Ali Khamenei](385653.md). However Article 109 stipulated that the leader be "a source of imitation" (Marja-e taqlid).[^cite_note-asghar.p73-56]

Khomeini wrote a letter to the president of the Assembly for Revising the Constitution, which was in session at the time, making the necessary arrangements to designate Khamene'i as his successor, and Article 109 was revised accordingly.[^cite_note-asghar.p73-56] In this letter, he supposedly "emphasised that he had always been of the opinion that the marja'iyat was not a requirement for the office of leader".[^cite_note-asghar.p73-56]

   [^cite_note-60]

   7. ministers of defense, intelligence, foreign affairs, and science.

   9. the chief of [the joint staff](14305018.md#Commanders).

   10. the chief commander of the armed forces of the country

   11. the highest commanders of the armed forces.

## Mandate and status

The supreme leader of Iran is elected by the [Assembly of Experts](512725.md) ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): مجلس خبرگان رهبری, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *majles-e xobregân-e rahbari*), which is also the only government body in charge of choosing and dismissing supreme leaders of Iran.[^cite_note-66]

The supreme leader is the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-Chief_of_the_Iranian_Armed_Forces) of [the armed forces](14305018.md) and the supervisor of the three branches of the state, the [Judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary), the [Legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature), and the [Executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)).

Iran's regional policy is directly controlled by the [Office of the Supreme Leader of Iran](51025254.md) with the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](1166971.md)' task limited to protocol and ceremonial occasions. All of Iran's [ambassadors](68651.md) to Arab countries, for example, are chosen by the [Quds Force](4028355.md), which directly reports to the supreme leader.[^cite_note-auto-35]

According to the constitution, all supreme leaders, following Ayatollah Khomeini, are to be elected by the [Assembly of Experts](512725.md), who are elected by Iranian voters to eight-year terms. All candidates for membership at the Assembly of Experts (along with candidates for president and for the Majlis (parliament)) must have their candidacy approved by the [Guardian Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardian_Council).[^cite_note-auto2-72] In 2016, 166 candidates were approved by the Guardians, out of 801 who applied to run for the office.[^cite_note-73]

Members of the Guardian Council are half appointed unilaterally by the supreme leader. Half are subject to confirmation by the Majlis, after being appointed by the head of the Iranian judiciary ([Chief Justice of Iran](35323582.md)), who is himself appointed by the supreme leader of Iran.[^cite_note-auto2-72] The Assembly has never questioned the supreme leader.[^cite_note-brookings-AoE-43] There have been cases where the Guardian Council repealed its ban on particular people, after being directed to do so by Khamenei.[^cite_note-74]

The supreme leader is legally considered "[inviolable](934323.md)", with Iranians being routinely punished for questioning or insulting him.[^cite_note-GlobalPost-75][^cite_note-AFP2014-76][^cite_note-iranhumanrights.org-77][^cite_note-Vahdat-78]

## Guardianship of the Islamic Jurist (Velayat-e faqih)

Main article: [Guardianship of the Islamic Jurist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardianship_of_the_Islamic_Jurist)



Ayatollah [Ali Khamenei](385653.md) met with [Hajj](23487999.md) authorities, 2018

[Ali Khamenei](385653.md), second supreme leader of Iran, and his claim of "speaking with God"

The constitution of Iran combines concepts of both [democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy) and [theocracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theocracy), theocracy in the form of Khomeini's concept of *vilayat-e faqih* (Guardianship of the Islamic Jurist), as expressed in the *Islamic Republic*. According to Ayatollah Khomeini, the Guardianship of the Islamic Jurist was not [restricted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardianship_of_the_Islamic_Jurists#Limited_guardianship_of_the_Islamic_Jurists) to orphans or mental incompetents, but applied to everyone in absence of the [twelfth Imam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelfth_Imam).[^cite_note-nasr.p125-79]

Jurists were the only rightful political/governmental leaders because "God had commanded Islamic government" and "no one knew religion better than the [ulama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulama)" (Islamic clergy).[^cite_note-nasr.p125-79] They alone would preserve "Islamic order" and keep everyone from deviating from "the just path of Islam".[^cite_note-80]

Prior to the [revolution](347268.md), observant Shia Muslims selected their own leading faqih to emulate (known as a *[Marja'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marja')-i taqlid*) according to their own decision making. The "congregation rather than the hierarchy decided how prominent the ayatollah was" thus allowing the public to possibly limit the influence of the Faqih.[^cite_note-nasr.p125-79]

After the revolution Shia Muslims, or at least Iranian Shia, were commanded to show allegiance to the current *[vali-e faghih](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardianship_of_the_Islamic_Jurist)*, Guardian Jurist or supreme leader. In this new system, the jurist oversaw all governmental affairs. The complete control exercised by the Faqih was not to be limited to the [Iranian Revolution](347268.md) because the revolution and its Leader had international aspirations. As the constitution of the Islamic Republic states, it

> intends to establish an ideal and model society on the basis of Islamic norms. ... the Constitution provides the necessary basis for ensuring the continuation of the Revolution at home and abroad. In particular, in the development of international relations, the Constitution will strive with other Islamic and popular movements to prepare the way for the formation of a single world community (in accordance with the Koranic verse 'This your community is a single community, and I am your Lord, so worship Me' \[21\:92]), and to assure the continuation of the struggle for the liberation of all deprived and oppressed peoples in the world.[^cite_note-preamble-52]

According to author Seyyed Vali Nasr, Khomeini appealed to the masses, during the pre-1979 period, by referring to them as the oppressed and with charisma and political ability was tremendously successful. He became a very popular role model for [Shiites](26961.md), and hoped for the Iranian Revolution to be the first step to a much larger Islamic revolution, transcending Shia Islam, in the same way that [Vladimir Lenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Lenin) and [Leon Trotsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Trotsky) had wanted [their revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Revolution) to be a world revolution, not just a [Russian](25391.md) one.[^cite_note-81]

## 2026 Interim Leadership Council

Main article: [Interim Leadership Council](82544455.md)

After the [Assassination of Ali Khamenei](82537558.md), following [Article 111 of the Islamic Republic constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Iran#Article_111),[^cite_note-Golkar-89] a Provisional Leadership Council took over Khamenei's role, pending election of a new supreme leader. The [Interim Leadership Council](82544455.md), as established on 1 March 2026, consisted of President [Masoud Pezeshkian](2650383.md), Chief Justice [Gholam-Hossein Mohseni-Eje'i](3002270.md) and [Alireza Arafi](52320724.md) of the [Guardian Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardian_Council).[^cite_note-AJE_heavy_bombing_rocks-90][^cite_note-IranIntl_Khamenei_killed-91][^cite_note-92]
