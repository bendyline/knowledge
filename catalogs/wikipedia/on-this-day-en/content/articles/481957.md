**Orenburg Oblast**[^cite_note-5] (also Orenburzhye) is a [federal subject](701438.md) of [Russia](25391.md) (an [oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblast)), mainly located in [Eastern Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Europe), with some land in the southern and eastern portions of the oblast falling within [Asia](689.md). Its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) is the [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) of [Orenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orenburg). From 1938 to 1957, it bore the name **Chkalov Oblast**[^cite_note-6] in honor of [Valery Chkalov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valery_Chkalov). As of the [2021 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census), the oblast had a population of 1,862,767,[^cite_note-2021Census-7] down from 2,033,072 in the [2010 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Census_\(2010\)).[^cite_note-2010Census-8]

## Geography

Orenburg Oblast's internal borders are with the republics of [Bashkortostan](735530.md) and [Tatarstan](67135.md) to the north, [Chelyabinsk Oblast](475230.md) to the north-east, and with [Samara](481962.md) and [Saratov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratov_Oblast) oblasts to the west. Orenburg Oblast also shares an international border with [Kazakhstan](16642.md) to the east and south. The oblast is situated on the [boundary between Europe and Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_between_Europe_and_Asia). The majority of its territory lies west of the continental divide in European Russia and smaller sections in the east situated on the Asian side of the divide. The most important river of the oblast is the [Ural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_River) and the largest lake [Shalkar-Yega-Kara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shalkar-Yega-Kara). Orenburg is traversed by the northeasterly line of equal latitude and longitude. The highest point of the oblast is the 668 m (2,192 ft)-high [Nakas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakas_\(mountain\)).[^cite_note-GЕ-9]

## History

In the first half of the 18th century, the Russian Empire constructed the [Irtysh line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irtysh_line?action=edit\&redlink=1), a series of 46 forts, including Orenburg,[^cite_note-10] to prevent [Kazakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakh_Khanate) and [Dzungar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dzungar_Khanate) nomads from raiding Russian territory.[^cite_note-11]

Orenburg played a major role in [Pugachev's Rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pugachev's_Rebellion) (1773–1774), the largest peasant revolt in Russian history.&#x20;

During the [Russian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War), the region was heavily affected by the [Russian famine of 1921–1922](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_famine_of_1921%E2%80%931922).

During [World War II](32927.md) in 1941, the command and staff of the newly formed Polish [Anders' Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anders'_Army) was based in [Buzuluk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buzuluk,_Orenburg_Oblast),[^cite_note-12] and in 1942, the [First Czechoslovak Independent Field Battalion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Czechoslovak_Independent_Field_Battalion) was based there. Both formations afterwards fought against [Nazi Germany](21212.md).

On 26 May 2024, a [Ukrainian](31750.md) drone attacked the [Voronezh M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronezh_radar) long-range radar station near [Orsk](1616356.md).[^cite_note-13]

## Economy

Orenburg Oblast is one of the major agricultural areas of Russia. Its climate is favorable to farming with a humid spring, dry summer and many sunny days, which make perfect conditions for cultivating hard [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat) and [rye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rye), [sunflowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunflower), [potatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potato), [peas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pea), [beans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beans), [corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), and [gourds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gourd).

The range of the oblast's export commodities includes oil and oil products, gas and gas produced products, rolled ferrous and non-ferrous metals, [nickel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nickel), [asbestos](21492663.md), [chromium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromium) compounds, rough copper, electric engines, and radiators, which are used to make products from the machine-building industry.
