**Warrap** is a [state](30852056.md) in [South Sudan](32350676.md). Warrap is located in the [Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahr_el_Ghazal_\(region_of_South_Sudan\)) region of northwestern South Sudan. The state comprises six counties, [Tonj South County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonj_South_County), [Tonj North County](59603915.md), [Tonj East county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonj_East_County), [Gogrial East County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gogrial_East_County), [Gogrial West County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gogrial_West_County) and [Twic County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twic_County). [Kuajok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuajok) is the capital city.

The current governor is H.E. Amb. Bol Wek Agoth who replaced Gen. Magok Magok Deng after his relief in 2023.[^cite_note-president-kiir-relieves-three-ministers-3]

## History

The state was first established in 2005 following the signing of the Comprehensive Peace Agreement between the [Sudan People's Liberation Movement](2691327.md) (SPLM) led by [Dr. John Garang de Mabior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Garang) and the [Government of Sudan](5698926.md) under the leadership of president [Omar Hassan El Bashir](393625.md). It subsequently became part of South Sudan after a successful secession from [Sudan](27421.md) on 9 July 2011.&#x20;

In 2015, a [presidential decree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_decree) established a new system of 28 states, replacing the previously established 10. Warrap State was replaced by the states of Twic, Gogrial and Tonj.[^cite_note-4] Warrap State was re-established by a peace agreement signed on 22 February 2020.[^cite_note-5]

## Location

Warrap State is in the [Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahr_el_Ghazal_\(region_of_South_Sudan\)) region. To its north lies the disputed region of [Abyei](13885196.md), to its east lies [Unity State](767617.md). To the west is [Western Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Bahr_el_Ghazal) and [Northern Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Bahr_el_Ghazal), and to its south lies the [Lakes State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakes_State).[^cite_note-9]

## Government

The state constitution was adopted in 2008. [Lewis Anei Madut-Kuendit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Anei_Madut-Kuendit?action=edit\&redlink=1) was the first governor of the state and [Bol Wek Agoth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bol_Wek_Agoth?action=edit\&redlink=1) is the current governor of Warrap state.[^cite_note-10]

## Religion

Main article: [Religion in South Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion_in_South_Sudan)

The main religions in Warrap State are [traditional African religions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_African_religions) and [Christianity](5211.md) ([Catholicism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholicism), [Protestantism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism)).[^cite_note-11]
