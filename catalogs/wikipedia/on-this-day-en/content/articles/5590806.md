**Thanh Hóa** is the northernmost coastal [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Vietnam) in the [North Central Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Central_Coast) region of [Vietnam](202354.md). It borders [Sơn La](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C6%A1n_La_province), [Phú Thọ](2543316.md), and [Ninh Bình](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninh_B%C3%ACnh_province) to the north, [Nghệ An](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngh%E1%BB%87_An) to the south, [Laos](17752.md) to the west with a boundary of over 192 kilometres (119 mi) long, and the [South China Sea](74209.md) ([Gulf of Tonkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Tonkin)) to the east.
