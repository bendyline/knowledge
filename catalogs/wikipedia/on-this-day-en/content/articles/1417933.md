The **province of Cosenza** ([Italian](14708.md): *provincia di Cosenza*) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Italy) in the region of [Calabria](44772.md) in [southern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Italy) [Italy](14532.md). Its capital is the city of [Cosenza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosenza). It has a population of 667,134 in an area of 6,709.75 square kilometres (2,590.65 sq mi) across its 150 municipalities.[^cite_note-population-2][^cite_note-area-1]

The province of Cosenza contains a community of [Occitan language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occitan_language) (also known as Langue d'oc) speakers in [Guardia Piemontese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardia_Piemontese): it was formed by Vaudoi or [Waldensian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waldensians) movement members, who moved to Cosenza to avoid religious persecution, in the 13th and 14th centuries.[^cite_note-4] Many of the [Arbëreshë](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arb%C3%ABresh%C3%AB_people) Albanians of Italy live in the province, since arriving in the 16th century to flee the religious persecution undertaken by the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md).

## History

The first traces of human settlement in the area date from the early [Palaeolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palaeolithic) period. These sites include the [Romito Cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romito_Cave) at [Papasidero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papasidero), including wall paintings of [bovidae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bovidae).[^cite_note-5]

Cosenza began as a settlement of the Italic [Bruttii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruttii) tribe, and became their capital before the Romans invaded the area. The town was conquered by the [Romans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire) in 204 BC and was named Cosentia. Starting from the 8th century BC, the current provincial area became part of the so-called [Greater Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Greece), or [Magna Graecia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magna_Graecia) ([Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin)). Greek cities, including [Sibari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sibari) and [Pandosia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandosia_\(Bruttium\)), were mostly located on the coastal area and at the foot of the [Pollino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollino) massif.

King of the [Visigoths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visigoths) [Alaric I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaric_I) conquered the region during the later stages of the [Western Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Roman_Empire) and according to legend, Alaric I is buried in Cosenza along with a large treasure hoard. Later Cosenza fell under the rule of the Byzantine Empire for a brief period of time, before being conquered by the [Lombards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lombards), as part of the [Duchy of Benevento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Benevento). [Roger II of Sicily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_II_of_Sicily) made it the capital of Terra Giordana in the 12th century.

In Modern times, as part of the [Kingdom of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Naples) and later of the [Kingdom of Two Sicilies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Two_Sicilies), the province remained mostly a rural area devoted to agriculture and animal husbandry. [Feudalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feudalism) was abolished only in the 19th century. The area was also seat to several forms of [brigandage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigandage) during the centuries.

## Demographics

As of 2026, the population is 667,134, of which 49.2% are male, and 50.8% are female. Minors make up 14.5% of the population, and seniors make up 25.4%.[^cite_note-population-2]

### Immigration

As of 2025, immigrants make up 9.2% of the population. The 5 largest foreign countries of birth are [Romania](25445.md), [Germany](11867.md), [Argentina](18951905.md), [Morocco](19291.md), and [Brazil](3383.md).[^cite_note-foreignborn-8]
