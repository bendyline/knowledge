The **People's Party of Aragon** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Partido Popular de Aragón*, PP) is the regional section of the [People's Party of Spain](404727.md) (PP) in [Aragon](39443.md). It was formed in 1989 from the re-foundation of the [People's Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Alliance_\(Spain\)).
