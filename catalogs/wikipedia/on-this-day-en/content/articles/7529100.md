# List of tariffs in Canada

&#x20; **List of tariffs in Canada**[^cite_note-1]

## 1800–1900

- 1855–1866: [Canadian–American Reciprocity Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian%E2%80%93American_Reciprocity_Treaty)

- 1858: [Cayley–Galt Tariff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayley%E2%80%93Galt_Tariff)

- 1879: The [National Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Policy) introduced

## 1900–2000

- 1945: [Bretton Woods Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bretton_Woods_system)

- 1947: [General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Agreement_on_Tariffs_and_Trade)

- 1963–1967: [Kennedy round of GATT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_round_of_GATT)

- 1965: [Canada–United States Automotive Products Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada%E2%80%93United_States_Automotive_Products_Agreement) (Auto Pact)

- 1973–1979: [Tokyo round of GATT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_round_of_GATT)

- 1988: [Canada–United States Free Trade Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada%E2%80%93United_States_Free_Trade_Agreement)

- 1993: [North American Free Trade Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_Free_Trade_Agreement) (NAFTA)

- 1994: [World Trade Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Organization) created

- 1997: [Canada–Israel Free Trade Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada%E2%80%93Israel_Free_Trade_Agreement) (CIFTA)

- 1997: [Canada–Chile Free Trade Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada%E2%80%93Chile_Free_Trade_Agreement) (CCFTA)

- 1997: [Canadian Copyright Act Part VIII, "Private Copying"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_copying_levy#Canada) (Non-government Tariff, see [Canadian Private Copying Collective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Private_Copying_Collective?action=edit\&redlink=1))

## 2000–present

- 2001: [Canada–Costa Rica Free Trade Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada%E2%80%93Costa_Rica_Free_Trade_Agreement) (CCRFTA)

- 2002: [Free Trade Area of the Americas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Trade_Area_of_the_Americas) (FTAA)

- 2006: [Trade, Investment, and Labour Mobility Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TILMA) between British Columbia and Alberta (TILMA)

- 2020: [United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement](58620732.md) (USMCA)

- 2024: [Canada–China trade war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada%E2%80%93China_trade_war)

- 2025: [United States trade war with Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_the_2025_United_States_trade_war_with_Canada)

## Notes

[^cite_note-1]: &#x20;K. W. TAYLOR, "Tariffs", in W. Stewart WALLACE, ed., *The Encyclopedia of Canada*, Vol. VI, Toronto, University Associates of Canada, 1948, 398p., pp. 102–108.
