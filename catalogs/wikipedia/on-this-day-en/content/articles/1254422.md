**Saint Catherine** ([Jamaican Patois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamaican_Patois): *Sint Cyatrine*) is a [parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parishes_of_Jamaica) in the [south east](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_directions) of [Jamaica](15660.md). It is located in the [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Jamaica) of Middlesex, and is one of the island's largest and most economically valued parishes because of its many resources. It includes the first [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_\(political\)) of Jamaica, [Spanish Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Town), originally known as San Jago de la Vega or Santiago de la Vega (St. James of the Plain).&#x20;

## History

The modern boundaries of St. Catherine were decided in 1867 when four smaller parishes were amalgamated.[^cite_note-3] The historic parishes of [Saint Dorothy Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Dorothy_Parish,_Jamaica), [Saint John Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_John_Parish,_Jamaica) and [Saint Thomas in the Vale Parish, Jamaica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Thomas_in_the_Vale_Parish,_Jamaica) were merged with the historic parish of Saint Catherine.[^cite_note-JPR-4]&#x20;

## Geography and People

St Catherine is located at [18°N 77°W / 18°N 77°W / 18; -77](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Saint_Catherine_Parish\&params=18_N_77_W_type\:adm1st). It is bordered by [St Andrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Andrew,_Jamaica) in the east, [Clarendon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarendon,_Jamaica) in the west, and by [St Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Mary,_Jamaica) and [St Ann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Ann,_Jamaica) in the north. It has an area of 1,192 km<sup>2</sup>, making it one of Jamaica's largest parishes and it is one of the fastest growing parishes in the nation and has the largest economy out of all fourteen parishes. Except for the Hellshire Hills near the coast, the south of the parish is virtually flat. The central and northern sections are very mountainous, however; the northern border is on Mount Rosser, which crosses over into [St Ann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Ann,_Jamaica), the highest point being 686 metres (2,251 ft).&#x20;

A [plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain) of approximately 230 square kilometres (57,000 acres) occupies the southern part of the Rio Cobre [basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drainage_basin). The Rio Cobre is the only river that runs along the southern plain. It provides water to [irrigate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrigation) over 73 square kilometres (18,000 acres) on the plain.&#x20;

## Commerce

### Development

Out of all the parishes (with the exception of [Kingston Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston_Parish) and [Saint Andrew Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Andrew_Parish,_Jamaica)) Saint Catherine shows the most potential for urban development. With its good water resources, virtually flat landscape, and nearness to the capital, it could possibly be one of the Caribbean's major urban areas in the next few years.&#x20;

### Agriculture

Agriculture remains the main source of [employment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Employment) in the parish. There are many small farmers who practice mixed farming; [crops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) such as [bananas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana), [coconuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coconut), [pineapple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pineapple), [citrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citrus), [pumpkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumpkin), [peppers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capsicum), [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee) and [callaloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callaloo) are planted for both domestic and commercial purposes. The larger properties produce [sugar cane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_cane), [bananas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana) and [citrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citrus) mainly for export. Dairy farms are also found in the parish. One of these is a 4.0 square kilometres (1,000 acres) [farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farm) in Old Harbour. The Salt Ponds District between Spanish Town, Port Henderson and Passage Fort is noted for the fine fish especially calipers.&#x20;

### Manufacturing

St Catherine is second only to Kingston as an industrial center. Industrial plants are some of the biggest employers in the parish. [Spanish Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Town) has the largest salt producing plant in the Caribbean, while Jamaica Milk Products, an affiliate of the [Nestlé](160227.md) organization, has a factory in [Bog Walk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bog_Walk), another major town in the parish. The largest [power plant](212141.md) in the island and several factories are located in Old Harbour. Twickenham Park, near [Spanish Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Town), is another industrial estate with mainly light industries including [cigarettes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cigarettes), [carpets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carpets), [batteries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_\(electricity\)), [ackee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic), [plastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic) items, [medical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical) and [pharmaceutical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmaceutical) products.&#x20;

### Tourist sites

- Jamaica's [emancipation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abolitionism_in_the_United_Kingdom) Square can be found in Spanish Town. This is the only [Georgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_architecture) square in [Jamaica](15660.md). Kings House and the [House of Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Assembly) on the west and east sides, respectively, were erected in 1762. The [Courthouse](58609.md) was built in 1819 and used as a [chapel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapel) and [armory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armory_\(military\)) with the Town Hall upstairs.

- The Rio Cobre River and [Gorge](78478.md) is one of the largest in the island. Sinking at a place called River Sink at Worthy Park, it runs underground for nearly six kilometres, then surfaces at River Head Grand [Cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cave) in St Thomas-in-the-Vale. Before entering the [gorge](78478.md) it is joined by a number of [tributaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tributaries), such as the Thomas River, the Rio D'Oro and the Rio Pedro. In the early 1770s, a road was opened through the gorge. The [Flat Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flat_Bridge) was originally constructed of [logs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logging), which were washed away in a flood. The present bridge was built and it has withstood countless floods. A marker can be found, which shows where the water rose to over 25 feet (7.6 m) above the bridge in 1933. An apocryphal story is that at noon, on every [Good Friday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Friday), for a short period of time the [ghosts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghosts) of all the slaves who drowned in the river can be seen.

- Caymanas Race Track, Jamaica's most famous race track, is in St Catherine.

- St Clair Cave, one of Jamaica's more famous caves, is found in the parish. This large, complex cave contains one of the most notable bat roosts on the island, and is one of the few known historical sites for the possibly extinct bat, *[Phyllonycteris aphylla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phyllonycteris_aphylla)*. Because of the biological importance of the system, and the inherent dangers involved in exploring it (i.e. noxious gases, [histoplasmosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Histoplasmosis), [Cutaneous larva migrans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cutaneous_larva_migrans), difficult footing), casual visitation is discouraged.

- Hellshire Beach is located on the south coast of Jamaica in St. Catherine Parish about 20 minutes away from Kingston. Not a tourist spot due to its distance from major hotels.

## Notable People; Cultural Figures

- Sister [Mary Ignatius Davies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Ignatius_Davies) (1921 – 2003) was a [Sister of Mercy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sisters_of_Mercy) and inspirational music teacher known for her work at the [Alpha Boys School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_Boys_School), where she trained scores of internationally renowned [ska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ska), [rocksteady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocksteady) and [reggae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reggae) artists, including [Leslie Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leslie_Thompson_\(musician\)), who went on to become the first black conductor of the [London Symphony Orchestra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Symphony_Orchestra).&#x20;
