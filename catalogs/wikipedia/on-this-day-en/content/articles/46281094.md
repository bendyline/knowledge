**Haitham bin Tariq Al Said[^cite_note-1]** (born 11 October 1955) is [Sultan](405325.md) and [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Oman) of [Oman](22316.md). He acceded to the throne in 2020 following the death of his cousin, [Sultan Qaboos bin Said](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qaboos_bin_Said), becoming the fifteenth ruler of the [Al Bu Said dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Bu_Said_dynasty), which has ruled Oman since the mid-eighteenth century.[^cite_note-2]

Haitham was born in [Muscat](57053.md) to Sayyid [Tariq bin Taimur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tariq_bin_Taimur), an uncle of Sultan Qaboos who served as his first Prime Minister from 1970 to 1972. He received his primary education at Al Saidiya School in Muscat before continuing his secondary and university studies in the [United Kingdom](31717.md). In 1979, he graduated from the University of Oxford's Foreign Service Programme and later pursued postgraduate studies at [Pembroke College, Oxford.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pembroke_College,_Oxford)[^cite_note-3] Beginning in 1986, Haitham served varying positions in the [Cabinet of Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Oman) under Sultan Qaboos, most notably as [Minister of Heritage and Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Heritage_and_Culture).[^cite_note-Omannews-4][^cite_note-5]

Since becoming Sultan, Haitham has largely continued his predecessor's domestic and foreign policy, which saw Oman develop into a stable, modern country. In 2021, he reaffirmed citizens' and residents' freedom of expression and opinion in a revamp of [Oman's Basic Statute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Statute_of_Oman), while making reforms aimed at further modernizing the state, strengthening public finances, diversifying the economy, and improving government efficiency under [Oman Vision 2040](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oman_Vision_2040).

In foreign affairs, Haitham has maintained Oman's longstanding policy of neutrality, dialogue, and non-interference in regional disputes. Oman has continued to act as a mediator in diplomatic efforts involving neighboring states and international powers, preserving its reputation as a country capable of facilitating communication between parties with competing interests. Under his leadership, Omani diplomacy helped facilitate a [ceasefire agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_United_States%E2%80%93Houthi_ceasefire) between the United States and the Houthi movement, providing to a halt in attacks on international shipping in the [Red Sea](26191.md) and U.S. military operations against Houthi targets, in addition to hosting [negotiations between the United States and Iran](79685207.md) that preceded the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).

## Early life

[Haitham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitham) is a son of [Sayyid Tariq bin Taimur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tariq_bin_Taimur_Al_Said), son of [Sultan Taimur bin Feisal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taimur_bin_Feisal), and Sayyida Shawana bint Hamud bin Ahmad Al Busaidi.[^cite_note-burke-6][^cite_note-7] He has six brothers and two sisters: Talal, Qais, Asa'ad, Shihab, Adham, Faris, Amal, and [Nawwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nawwal_bint_Tariq).[^cite_note-burke-6] His brother [Sayyid Asa'ad bin Tariq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asa'ad_bin_Tariq_Al_Said) is the [Deputy Prime Minister for Relations and International Cooperation Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Oman) and his brother [Sayyid Shihab bin Tariq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shihab_bin_Tariq_Al_Said) is the [Deputy Prime Minister for Defense Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Oman). His other four brothers are all businessmen and are not involved in politics.[^cite_note-8] His sister Sayyida Nawwal bint Tariq was married to Sultan Qaboos from 1976 to 1979.[^cite_note-burke-6]

### Education

He completed his primary education at Saidiya School in Muscat and attended [Brummana High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brummana_High_School) in [Lebanon](17771.md) from 1969 to 1973.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] He graduated from the [Foreign Service Programme (FSP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Diplomatic_Studies_Programme) at Pembroke College, Oxford in 1979.[^cite_note-11]

## Political career

A sports enthusiast, Haitham served as the first head of the [Oman Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oman_Football_Association) in the early 1980s.[^cite_note-19] He served as the Undersecretary of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Oman\)) for Political Affairs from 1986 to 1994, and was later appointed as the Secretary General for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs from 1994 to 2002.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] He was subsequently appointed as [Minister of Heritage and Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Heritage_and_Culture) in March 2002 and later chaired the national census committee in 2003.[^cite_note-Valeri-22] He usually represented Oman abroad in a diplomatic capacity;[^cite_note-23] in 2016, he personally welcomed the Prince of Wales and the Duchess of Cornwall (later [King Charles III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Charles_III) and [Queen Camilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Camilla)) on a visit to Oman.[^cite_note-Arab_News-24]

Haitham is also the chairman of the committee for [Oman Vision 2040](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oman_Vision_2040) along with being honorary president of the Oman Association for the Disabled.[^cite_note-25] He was said to be the favoured candidate of Oman's merchant families with whom he is said to have links.[^cite_note-26]

### Sultan of Oman

After the death of Sultan Qaboos, Haitham's first cousin, on 10 January 2020, Haitham was named by the royal family and Qaboos's will as [Sultan of Oman](405325.md) the next day and took an oath before an emergency session of the [Council of Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Oman) in [Al-Bustan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Bustan,_Oman).[^cite_note-Gulf_News-27] According to [Omani state TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Oman_Television), Qaboos's letter was opened by the Defence Council and his identity was announced shortly thereafter.[^cite_note-28] As Sultan, he also held the positions of Prime Minister, Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces, Minister of Defence, Minister of Finance, Minister of Foreign Affairs and Chairman of the [Central Bank of Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Oman) until 18 August 2020 when he appointed [Badr bin Hamad Al Busaidi](26430850.md) as foreign minister, [Sultan bin Salem bin Saeed al-Habsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_bin_Salem_bin_Saeed_al-Habsi) as minister of finance,[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30] and [Taimur bin Asa'ad Al Said](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taimur_bin_Asa'ad_Al_Said) as chairman of the [Central Bank of Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Oman).[^cite_note-31]

In his first public speech, he promised to uphold his predecessor's [peace-making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace-making) foreign policy and to further develop Oman's economy.[^cite_note-Arab_News-24][^cite_note-32] Haitham is married and, unlike his predecessor, also has children, two sons and two daughters.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] Early in his reign he signed Oman to the [United Nations Convention Against Torture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Convention_Against_Torture), the [International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Covenant_on_Economic,_Social_and_Cultural_Rights) and [International Convention for the Protection of All Persons from Enforced Disappearance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Convention_for_the_Protection_of_All_Persons_from_Enforced_Disappearance).[^cite_note-35] In October 2020, Oman was the first Gulf state to send an ambassador back to [Syria](7515849.md) after they downgraded or shut missions in Damascus in 2012 over attacks by the government there on protests at the start of the [war](30741795.md).[^cite_note-36]

On 11 January 2021, Haitham issued a royal decree creating a [Crown Prince](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Prince_of_Oman) role, stating that Oman's crown prince will be the [eldest son](136535.md) of the serving sultan. This made his eldest son, [Sayyid Theyazin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theyazin_bin_Haitham), the country's first crown prince,[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38] and officially formalised the method of succession. He also amended the [Basic Statute of Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Statute_of_Oman) to reaffirm citizens' and residents' freedom of expression and opinion, removed a law that allowed the state to monitor private phone conversations, social media or postal correspondence, and granted the freedom to practice religious rites according to recognised customs, provided it does not violate the public order or contradict morals.[^cite_note-39] In April 2021, Oman introduced its [value added tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_added_tax) (VAT), later than the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia and Bahrain but before Qatar and Kuwait.[^cite_note-40] In May and June 2021, there were multiple protests against the Omani government over economic concerns such as unemployment and corruption.[^cite_note-pomed.org-41] Some protesters were arrested, but later released.[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-pomed.org-41]

Haitham's visits to [Saudi Arabia](349303.md), [Qatar](8238258.md) and the [United Kingdom](31717.md) have been seen as a move to maintain peaceful and cordial relations with Oman's allies. His visit to Saudi Arabia was the first political visit by an Omani royal to the kingdom in over a decade.[^cite_note-gulfnews.com-43] During his visit to the United Kingdom, he met Queen [Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II) at [Windsor Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windsor_Castle) and was appointed an Honorary Knight Grand Cross of the [Order of St Michael and St George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_St_Michael_and_St_George).[^cite_note-44] In September 2022, following [Elizabeth II's death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II's_death), Haitham visited [Buckingham Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckingham_Palace) to offer his condolences to King [Charles III](125248.md).[^cite_note-45]

He visited Tehran in May 2023 where he discussed regional diplomatic and security issues, two days after Muscat mediated a prisoner swap between Iran and Belgium.[^cite_note-46] The same year, the government approved a \$5.2 billion investment fund, the Oman Future Fund, to support diversified economic growth and announced a new development project, [Sultan Haitham City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Haitham_City).[^cite_note-47] A new Tourism Law was also promulgated in late 2023.[^cite_note-48]

The Medium Term Fiscal Plan for 2020–2024 under Haitham proposed greater fiscal sustainability. Oman's overall fiscal deficit averaged 12.8% of gross domestic product from 2015–2020, while the budget deficit reached approximately 3.6% in 2021. Oman had a budget surplus of approximately 5% of GDP in 2022 – the country's first surplus since 2013.[^cite_note-49] Oman's long-term sovereign credit rating changed from junk status to the investment grade of BB+ in 2024.[^cite_note-50] In 2025 [Moody's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moody's) upgraded Oman's long-term issuer and senior unsecured ratings to "Baa3" from "Ba1", due to expectations of continued improvement in debt ratios and resilience to lower oil prices.[^cite_note-51]

During the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War) in June 2025, in a call with President [Masoud Pezeshkian](2650383.md) of Iran, Haitham emphasised the need for de-escalation from both sides and a return to negotiations to halt the ongoing conflict and its catastrophic repercussions.[^cite_note-52] In May 2025, Oman mediated a [ceasefire between the US and Houthi forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_United_States%E2%80%93Houthi_ceasefire).[^cite_note-53] In 2025 Oman become the first country in the Gulf to impose a personal [income tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Income_tax). Oman will impose a 5% tax on taxable income for individuals earning over 42,000 [Omani rials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omani_rial) (\$109,091) per year starting from 2028.[^cite_note-54] The tax will apply to about 1% of the population. Officials said that the tax was intended to promote equity and reduce the country's dependence on oil and gas, which made up around 70% of the state revenues in 2024.[^cite_note-55] Revenue estimates for the new tax stand at less than 0.5% of GDP.[^cite_note-56]

On 9 March 2026, Haitham was the first Arab leader to congratulate Iran's new supreme leader, [Mojtaba Khamenei](23324096.md), underscoring Muscat's continued commitment to maintaining positive relations with Tehran.[^cite_note-57] Following the [2026 Iran war and the rise in crude oil prices](82562375.md), the rises meant Oman was projected to run a surplus of as much as \$3 billion in 2026 if spending keeps to budget. That in turn would usefully eat into the country’s \$38 billion in public debt, nearly 36% of GDP but already well down from the [coronavirus pandemic-era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Oman) high of 68% of GDP.[^cite_note-58]
