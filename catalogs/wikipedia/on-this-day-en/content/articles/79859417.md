**By-elections to the 45th Canadian Parliament** are held to fill vacancies in the [House of Commons of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Commons_of_Canada) between the [2025 federal election](68483222.md) and the [46th federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/46th_Canadian_federal_election). The [45th Canadian Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/45th_Canadian_Parliament) has existed since 2025 with the membership of its House of Commons having been determined by the results of the [Canadian federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Canadian_federal_general_elections) held on April 28, 2025. The [Liberal Party of Canada](149536.md) had a [minority government](271434.md) during this [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Canada) until the three by-elections held on April 13, 2026, where they gained [majority status](465640.md). The [Conservative Party of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_Party_of_Canada) forms the [Official Opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_Opposition_\(Canada\)).

A [writ for a by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writ_of_election) must be [dropped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dropping_the_writ) no sooner than 11 days and no later than 180 days after the [chief electoral officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Electoral_Officer_\(Canada\)) is officially notified of a vacancy via a warrant issued by the [Speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_House_of_Commons_of_Canada). Under the *[Canada Elections Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Elections_Act)*, the minimum length of a campaign is 36 days between dropping the writ and election day, but must not exceed 50 days and the by-election must occur on a Monday within that time-frame.[^cite_note-hillaug-8][^cite_note-electionscanadabrc-9]

## August 18, 2025 by-election

### Battle River—Crowfoot

Main article: [2025 Battle River—Crowfoot federal by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Battle_River%E2%80%94Crowfoot_federal_by-election)

A by-election was called in the federal riding of [Battle River—Crowfoot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_River%E2%80%94Crowfoot), and was held on August 18, 2025, following the resignation of [Conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_Party_of_Canada) MP [Damien Kurek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Kurek). Kurek, who secured re-election with over 80% of the vote in the [2025 federal election](68483222.md) on April 28, resigned his seat on June 17. This was done to allow Conservative leader [Pierre Poilievre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Poilievre) an opportunity to return to Parliament through a by-election after he was [unseated in the 2025 Canadian federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_in_the_2025_Canadian_federal_election).[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35]

The writ for the by-election was drawn up on June 30, 2025 with the date of the vote set for August 18 in order to avoid a conflict with [Alberta Heritage Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civic_Holiday#Alberta) earlier in the month.[^cite_note-36]

## April 13, 2026 by-elections

Nominations closed on March 23, at 2 pm local time.[^cite_note-38]

### University—Rosedale

Main article: [2026 University—Rosedale federal by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_University%E2%80%94Rosedale_federal_by-election)

A [by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_University%E2%80%94Rosedale_federal_by-election) was held in the federal riding of [University—Rosedale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University%E2%80%94Rosedale_\(federal_electoral_district\)) in [Ontario](22218.md), Canada, following the January 9, 2026, resignation of incumbent [Liberal](149536.md) MP [Chrystia Freeland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrystia_Freeland) due to her accepting a position as economic advisor to the [Office of the President of Ukraine](19362024.md).[^cite_note-\:0-39][^cite_note-40]

The [Chief Electoral Officer of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Electoral_Officer_of_Canada) received formal notification of the vacancy from the [Speaker of the House of Commons of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_House_of_Commons_of_Canada) on January 9, 2026. The date of the by-election must be announced between January 20 and July 8, 2026. The by-election must be held on a Monday, at least 36 days but no more than 50 days after the day the by-election is announced. Accordingly, the earliest date that University—Rosedale by-election could have been held was March 2, 2026.[^cite_note-UR-41]

The Liberals announced [Danielle Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danielle_Martin) as their candidate on January 31.[^cite_note-August_3rd-28]

Serena Purdy, the NDP candidate in this riding in the 2025 federal election, announced on March 3 that she would be the party's candidate.[^cite_note-42]

The Greens announced candidate Andrew Massey on February 12.[^cite_note-GreenTorontoCandidates-43]

Independent Adam Golding announced his candidacy in mid-February; he previously finished last in the riding in the 2025 federal election. He did not end up registering as a candidate, however.[^cite_note-44]

The Centrist Party announced Imran Khan as their candidate on February 16;[^cite_note-45] Khan previously ran in Scarborough Southwest in the 2025 federal election.

The Conservatives announced Don Hodgson as their candidate on March 11.[^cite_note-ConservativesTerrebonneURCandidates-46]

Martin was elected, holding this seat for the Liberals.[^cite_note-\:1-47] Her victory, the first out of the three by-elections called on the night, took the Liberals to Majority Government status.

### Scarborough Southwest

Main article: [2026 Scarborough Southwest federal by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Scarborough_Southwest_federal_by-election)

A by-election was held in the federal riding of [Scarborough Southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarborough_Southwest_\(federal_electoral_district\)) in [Ontario](22218.md), Canada, following the announced resignation of incumbent [Liberal](149536.md) MP [Bill Blair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Blair) on February 2, 2026.[^cite_note-48]

The date of the by-election must be announced between February 13 and August 1, 2026. The by-election must be held on a Monday, at least 36 days but no more than 50 days after the day the by-election is announced. Accordingly, the earliest date that the Scarborough Southwest by-election could be held is March 23, 2026.[^cite_note-ssw-13]

The Liberals announced then–[Ontario NDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario_NDP) deputy leader and [MPP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario_legislature) [Doly Begum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doly_Begum) as their candidate on February 3.[^cite_note-49]

The New Democratic Party ran [Fatima Shaban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatima_Shaban), who was also the party's candidate in 2025.[^cite_note-50]

The Greens announced Pooja Malhotra as their candidate on February 12.[^cite_note-GreenTorontoCandidates-43]

The Centrist Party announced Lyall Sanders as their candidate on February 21.[^cite_note-51]

Begum was elected, holding this seat for the Liberals.[^cite_note-\:1-47]

### Chicoutimi—Le Fjord

Main article: [2026 Chicoutimi—Le Fjord federal by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Chicoutimi%E2%80%94Le_Fjord_federal_by-election)

A by-election was held in [Chicoutimi—Le Fjord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicoutimi%E2%80%94Le_Fjord) following Conservative MP [Richard Martel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Martel)'s appointment to the Senate. Martel resigned as an MP and left the Conservative caucus on July 7, 2026.[^cite_note-71] Liberal candidate [Daniel Gobeil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Gobeil) won the by-election, gaining the seat from the Conservatives.[^cite_note-CPAug31-53]

The Conservative candidate was retired attorney and [Cégep de Chicoutimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9gep_de_Chicoutimi) law professor Régis Gaudreault. The Bloc Québécois nominated Caroline Dubé, former director of MP [Mario Simard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Simard)'s constituency office.[^cite_note-72][^cite_note-73]

The Liberals nominated Gobeil, a dairy farmer in [La Baie, Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Baie,_Quebec), vice-president of [Dairy Farmers of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy_Farmers_of_Canada), and president of [Les Producteurs de lait du Québec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9d%C3%A9ration_des_producteurs_de_lait_du_Qu%C3%A9bec).[^cite_note-74]

The NDP nominated Raphaël Émond, who had run for the party in the riding in the 2025 federal election.[^cite_note-75]

The Green Party candidate was Nathe Perrone, the party's Animal Welfare and Security critic.[^cite_note-76]

## Pending by-elections

### Saint-Hyacinthe—Bagot—Acton

Main article: [Pending Saint-Hyacinthe—Bagot—Acton federal by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pending_Saint-Hyacinthe%E2%80%94Bagot%E2%80%94Acton_federal_by-election)

A by-election is pending in [Saint-Hyacinthe—Bagot—Acton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Hyacinthe%E2%80%94Bagot%E2%80%94Acton) due to former Bloc Québécois MP [Simon-Pierre Savard-Tremblay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon-Pierre_Savard-Tremblay) being announced as the [Parti Québécois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parti_Qu%C3%A9b%C3%A9cois) candidate in [Saint-Hyacinthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Hyacinthe_\(provincial_electoral_district\)) for the [2026 Quebec general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Quebec_general_election). In the interim, Savard-Tremblay left the federal BQ caucus to sit as an independent MP and resigned his seat prior to the provincial election. Savard-Tremblay had held the seat for the Bloc since the [2019 Canadian federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Canadian_federal_election).[^cite_note-Savard-Tremblay-20]

On July 16, 2026, the Bloc Québécois selected Caroline Émond to be the candidate. She is the former Quebec [delegate-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delegate-general) to the European institutions in Brussels and former executive director of the International Dairy Federation.[^cite_note-79]

On August 21, 2026, it was announced that former [Montreal Alouettes](70601.md) player and coach [Luc Brodeur-Jourdain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luc_Brodeur-Jourdain) would be the candidate for the Conservatives.[^cite_note-80]

### Scarborough North

Main article: [Pending Scarborough North federal by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pending_Scarborough_North_federal_by-election)

A by-election is pending in [Scarborough North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarborough_North_\(federal_electoral_district\)) due to Liberal MP [Shaun Chen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaun_Chen_\(politician\)) resigning on August 15 citing injuries he sustained in a car accident while he was [commuting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commuting) to [Ottawa](22219.md) in the fall of 2025.[^cite_note-ChenResignation-22]

### Rosemont—La Petite-Patrie

Main article: [Pending Rosemont—La Petite-Patrie federal by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pending_Rosemont%E2%80%94La_Petite-Patrie_federal_by-election)

A by-election is pending in [Rosemont—La Petite-Patrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosemont%E2%80%94La_Petite-Patrie_\(federal_electoral_district\)) following New Democratic Party (later independent) MP [Alexandre Boulerice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_Boulerice) resigning his seat officially on August 26, 2026 to run in the [2026 Quebec general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Quebec_general_election) as the [Québec solidaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qu%C3%A9bec_solidaire) candidate in [Gouin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gouin_\(electoral_district\)). Upon announcing his resignation plans, Boulerice left the federal NDP caucus to sit as an independent MP. Boulerice had held the seat for the NDP since the [2011 Canadian federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Canadian_federal_election) and was the sole NDP MP elected in Quebec in 2025.[^cite_note-81]

The NDP intends to run unemployment activist [Pierre Céré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_C%C3%A9r%C3%A9) in the riding, after he won the nomination contest on July 16.[^cite_note-PC-82]

On June 18, 2026 Bloc Québécois leader [Yves-François Blanchet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yves-Fran%C3%A7ois_Blanchet) announced the party's candidate would be Alexandre Curzi, [UDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_des_artistes) executive and son of actor and politician [Pierre Curzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Curzi).[^cite_note-83]

### Laurier—Sainte-Marie

Main article: [Pending Laurier—Sainte-Marie federal by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pending_Laurier%E2%80%94Sainte-Marie_federal_by-election)

A by-election is pending in [Laurier—Sainte-Marie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurier%E2%80%94Sainte-Marie) due to Liberal MP and former cabinet minister [Steven Guilbeault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Guilbeault)'s resignation, which took effect on August 31, 2026.[^cite_note-84][^cite_note-August_3rd-28][^cite_note-\:2-85] Guilbeault had held the seat since the [2019 Canadian federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Canadian_federal_election).[^cite_note-\:2-85] The NDP intends to run [Nimâ Machouf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nim%C3%A2_Machouf) in the riding; she previously ran in the [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Canadian_federal_election), [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Canadian_federal_election), and [2025 federal elections](68483222.md).[^cite_note-PC-82]

### Yorkton—Melville

Main article: [Pending Yorkton—Melville federal by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pending_Yorkton%E2%80%94Melville_federal_by-election)

A by-election is pending in [Yorkton—Melville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkton%E2%80%94Melville) due to Conservative MP [Cathay Wagantall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathay_Wagantall) resigning her seat effective August 31. She was first elected in [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Canadian_federal_election) and was serving her fourth consecutive term in office.[^cite_note-86]

Previously, Wagantall had announced on July 28, 2025 that she would not be seeking re-election in the next election, but would remain an MP until then.[^cite_note-87]

[Yorkton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkton,_Saskatchewan) business owner Nelson Pohl had been nominated as the Conservative candidate to succeed Wagantall four days before her announcement on June 13, defeating two other candidates.[^cite_note-88]

### Brantford—Brant South

Main article: [Pending Brantford—Brant South federal by-election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pending_Brantford%E2%80%94Brant_South_federal_by-election)

A by-election is pending in [Brantford—Brant South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brantford%E2%80%94Brant_South) due to Conservative MP [Larry Brock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Brock)'s resignation from parliament on September 18, 2026 to accept a position with the [crown attorney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_attorney)'s office.[^cite_note-BrockResignation-32] Brock was first elected in the predecessor riding of [Brantford—Brant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brantford%E2%80%94Brant_\(federal_electoral_district\)) in the [2021 Canadian federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Canadian_federal_election) and was re-elected in the newly created riding of Brantford—Brant South—Six Nations in the [2025 Canadian federal election](68483222.md).

On September 16, 2026, just two days before his resignation, the riding's name was changed to Brantford—Brant South ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Brantford—Brant-Sud*) from Brantford—Brant South—Six Nations as part of *Bill C-25* of the [45th Canadian Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/45th_Canadian_Parliament).[^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90]

[Kevin Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Davis_\(politician\)) was appointed the Conservative candidate.[^cite_note-91] Joy O'Donnell, a [Certified Financial Planner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Certified_Financial_Planner) and the Liberal candidate in the 2025 federal election, issued a statement she was interested in running for the Liberals.[^cite_note-92][^cite_note-Brantford-93] The local NDP will hold a meeting to select a candidate at a yet-to-be determined date.[^cite_note-Brantford-93]
