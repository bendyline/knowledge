**Sandya Eknalygoda** ([Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language): சந்தியா எக்னெலிகொட) is a [Sri Lankan](26750.md) [human rights](13831.md) activist. She became an [International Women of Courage Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Women_of_Courage_Award) recipient in 2017. She has been campaigning for thousands of missing persons in Sri Lanka.[^cite_note-1] She is married to missing journalist [Prageeth Eknaligoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prageeth_Eknaligoda).

Her husband had told her that he was on a hit list and that he was receiving threats that warned him to stop writing. He was investigating corruption when he was kidnapped and returned in 2009.[^cite_note-(CPJ)2013-2] She became actively involved after her husband and prominent journalist [Prageeth Eknaligoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prageeth_Eknaligoda) disappeared in 2010 when he was investigating the alleged use of chemical weapons against civilians by the Sri Lankan army in the fight against the Tamil rebels.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Award

In 2017, Eknaligoda was awarded with the [International Women of Courage Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Women_of_Courage_Award) for her campaigns.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] She was recognized as one of the [BBC 100 Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_Women_\(BBC\)) in December 2022.[^cite_note-10]
