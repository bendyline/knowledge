**Ali Abdollahi Aliabadi** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): علی عبداللهی علی‌آبادی; born 1959) is an Iranian senior military officer and former politician who is currently chief of the [General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran](50548383.md) and commander of the [Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters](51128846.md).[^cite_note-\:0-1] He served as governor-general of [Gilan province](210689.md) and [Semnan province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semnan_province) and deputy interior minister during the presidency of [Mahmoud Ahmadinejad](7907317.md).[^cite_note-I-2]

Abdollahi is sanctioned by the [US Department of the Treasury](53667.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

Abdollahi participated in the 2022 [Moscow](19004.md) conference on international security as deputy chief of the general staff of the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic. In the conference, Aliabadi referred to the [United States](3434750.md) as “Big Satan” and said that American [totalitarianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totalitarianism) and unilaterism threatens global security. Abdollahi in the conference also said that Americans cause crisis, sanctions and wars.[^cite_note-5]

In 2025, an [X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_\(Twitter\)) account claiming to be associated with the [Mossad](27619007.md) announced that Abdollahi is the new commander of the [Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters](51128846.md), after commanders [Gholam Ali Rashid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gholam_Ali_Rashid) and [Ali Shadmani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Shadmani) were assassinated in the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War). The Mossad has not denied connection to the account.[^cite_note-\:0-1] In August 2026, he was appointed chief of staff of the [Islamic Republic of Iran Armed Forces](14305018.md) by Supreme Leader [Mojtaba Khamenei](23324096.md), with [Kioumars Heydari](52711558.md) being named his deputy.[^cite_note-6] Khamenei concurrently instructed Abdollahi to complete the merger of the General Staff with the [Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters](51128846.md).[^cite_note-7]

## Military career



Aliabadi in 2018

Offices held by General Abdollahi include:[^cite_note-I-2]

- Commander, [16th Quds Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_Quds_Division?action=edit\&redlink=1) (1987–1991)

- Commander of Headquarters, [Revolutionary Guards Ground Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps_Ground_Forces)

- Head of Inspection Circle, [Revolutionary Guards Joint Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps_Joint_Staff)

- Deputy Commander, [Revolutionary Guards Air Force](5613577.md) (1997–2001)

- Deputy for Coordination, [Law Enforcement Force](5187924.md) (2001–2002)

- Deputy Commander, [Law Enforcement Force](5187924.md) (2002–2005)

- Caretaker, [Law Enforcement Force](5187924.md) (2005)

- Deputy for Readiness, Logistics and Industrial Research, [General Staff of Armed Forces](50548383.md) (2013–2016)

- Deputy for Coordination, [General Staff of Armed Forces](50548383.md) (2016–2025)

- Commander, [Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters](51128846.md) (2025–*present*)

- [Chief of the General Staff](50548383.md), [Islamic Republic of Iran Armed Forces](14305018.md) (2026–*present*)
