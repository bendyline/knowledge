**Al-Fulah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الفولة) is a town in [West Kordofan](767647.md) State in [Sudan](27421.md) and is the capital of the state. It is the home of the [University of West Kordofan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_West_Kordofan).

Conflict during the [Second Sudanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sudanese_Civil_War) resulted in many people being displaced.[^cite_note-Deng1995-1]

During the [Sudanese civil war (2023–present)](73556699.md), [the town was captured](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capture_of_Al-Fulah) by the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) on 20 June 2024 after a brief battle with the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md).[^cite_note-2]

The Chinese have shown an interest in developing a power station in Al-Fulah and building a 730 kilometres (450 mi) pipeline.[^cite_note-Foster2009-3][^cite_note-China_Rights_Forum-4]
