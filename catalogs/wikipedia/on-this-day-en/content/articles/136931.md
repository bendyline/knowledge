**Tremonton** is a city in [Box Elder County, Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box_Elder_County,_Utah), United States. The population was 9,894 at the time of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_Census).[^cite_note-Census_2010-4] As of 2024, Tremonton has an estimated population of 13,713 and is one of the fastest growing municipalities in the state of Utah (2024 estimate annual growth rate: 7.33%).[^cite_note-5]

## History

Although the first settlers came to the Tremonton area in 1888, it remained largely uninhabited until just before 1900, when [land agents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_agent) started promoting the Bear River Valley as a place for [Midwestern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midwestern_United_States) farmers to relocate. Small groups from [Nebraska](21647.md) and [Illinois](14849.md) began to arrive in 1898. These settlers were a diverse blend of [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant) faiths, in contrast to their mostly [Mormon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Church_of_Jesus_Christ_of_Latter-day_Saints) neighbors.[^cite_note-boxelder-6] Then an [Apostolic Christian Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Christian_Church) group came in 1901–1904. The main body was from [Tremont, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tremont,_Illinois), joined by a few families from [Ohio](22199.md) and [Kansas](16716.md). Mostly of [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_American) descent, this group was referred to as the "German colony".[^cite_note-boxelder2-7]

When a townsite was laid out in 1903, the new town was named "Tremont" at the request of the German colony. Within four years, the post office had it renamed "Tremonton" due to confusion with the central Utah town of [Fremont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fremont,_Utah).[^cite_note-boxelder-6] Around 1907 the congregation was caught up in a larger [schism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schism_\(religion\)) of the Apostolic Church. Some moved back to the Midwest, and the German colony came to an end.[^cite_note-boxelder2-7] But the church left a permanent mark in the name of Tremonton.[^cite_note-cem-8]

### 2025 shooting of police

On August 18, 2025, two police officers were shot dead in town, and one other officer and K9 unit were wounded by gunfire resulting from an alleged domestic disturbance suspect that occurred outside the suspect's house at around 9 p.m. It started when the suspect's wife called 911 and reported her husband abusing her. Mass police and law enforcement officers arrived at the scene minutes later. Explosives were discovered in the suspect's house. The alleged shooter was immediately detained, and currently is in the Utah State Jail.[^cite_note-9]

## Geography

Tremonton lies in the [Bear River Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bear_River_\(Great_Salt_Lake\)) in northeastern Box Elder County. The [Malad River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malad_River_\(Idaho-Utah\)) flows through the city. According to the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the city has a total area of 7.8 square miles (20.2 km<sup>2</sup>), all land.[^cite_note-Census_2010-4] Tremonton is located near the junction of [Interstate 15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_15_in_Utah) and [Interstate 84](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_84_in_Utah). It is bordered on the north by the city of [Garland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garland,_Utah), with which it is closely associated. The town of [Elwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elwood,_Utah) is located 2 miles (3 km) to the southeast.

### 2020 census

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), Tremonton had a population of 9,894. The median age was 28.1 years. 35.7% of residents were under the age of 18 and 9.6% of residents were 65 years of age or older. For every 100 females there were 102.6 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 98.8 males age 18 and over.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-12]

96.3% of residents lived in urban areas, while 3.7% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-Census2020DHC-13]

There were 3,095 households in Tremonton, of which 46.8% had children under the age of 18 living in them. Of all households, 62.0% were married-couple households, 14.6% were households with a male householder and no spouse or partner present, and 17.5% were households with a female householder and no spouse or partner present. About 18.5% of all households were made up of individuals and 6.2% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-12]

There were 3,213 housing units, of which 3.7% were vacant. The homeowner vacancy rate was 1.0% and the rental vacancy rate was 5.0%.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-12]

### 2000 census

As of the [census](6889.md)[^cite_note-GR2-3] of 2000, there were 5,592 people, 1,698 households, and 1,397 families residing in the city. The [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) was 1,066.8 people per square mile (412.0/km<sup>2</sup>). There were 1,822 housing units at an average density of 347.6 per square mile (134.3/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of the city was 91.52% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.16% [African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.45% [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 1.13% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.04% [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander_\(U.S._Census\)), 5.22% from [other races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), and 1.48% from two or more races. [Hispanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_\(U.S._Census\)) or [Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latino_\(U.S._Census\)) of any race were 9.71% of the population.

There were 1,698 households, out of which 50.6% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 70.7% were [married couples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage) living together, 9.3% had a female householder with no husband present, and 17.7% were non-families. 15.4% of all households were made up of individuals, and 6.9% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older. The average household size was 3.26 and the average family size was 3.67.

In the city, 39.0% of the population was under the age of 18, 10.5% from 18 to 24, 26.7% from 25 to 44, 14.7% from 45 to 64, and 9.1% were 65 years of age or older. The median age was 25 years. For every 100 females, there were 98.7 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 94.8 males.

The median income for a household in the city was \$44,784, and the median income for a family was \$49,100. Males had a median income of \$36,764 versus \$22,149 for females. The [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income) for the city was \$15,737. About 8.3% of families and 9.9% of the population were below the [poverty line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_line), including 14.3% of those under age 18 and none of those age 65 or over.

## Economy

Some of the major employers include the [Malt-O-Meal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malt-O-Meal) cereal company, Lakeshore Learning Materials in nearby Garland, [West Liberty Foods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Liberty_Foods), Crump-Reese Motor Co, and [Intertape Polymer Group, Inc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intertape_Polymer_Group). In April 2008, [La-Z-Boy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La-Z-Boy) announced the closure of its Tremonton facility and the layoff of all 630 employees.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] Some of the plant's production was moved to a facility in [Ramos Arizpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramos_Arizpe), Mexico.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

## Education

Tremonton has two elementary schools, McKinley Elementary and North Park. Older students attend Alice C. Harris Intermediate in Tremonton, as well as Bear River Middle School and Bear River High School, located in nearby [Garland, Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garland,_Utah).
