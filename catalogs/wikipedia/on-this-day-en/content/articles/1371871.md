In [scholarly literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholarly_literature) and [criminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminology), **gang rape**, also called **serial gang rape**, **party rape**, **group rape**, or **multiple perpetrator rape**,[^cite_note-ullman-1] is the [rape](7980471.md) of a single victim by two or more violators.[^cite_note-autogenerated4-2] Gang rapes are forged on shared identity, [religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion), [ethnic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnicity), or [race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(human_categorization\)).[^cite_note-rape1-3][^cite_note-rape2-4][^cite_note-rape3-5] There are multiple motives for serial gang rapes, such as for sexual entitlement,[^cite_note-lancetrapestudy-6] asserting sexual prowess,[^cite_note-lancetrapestudy-6] or punishment.[^cite_note-lancetrapestudy-6][^cite_note-rape4-7][^cite_note-rape5-8][^cite_note-rape6-9]

Gang rapes often occur [during warfare](17572536.md),[^cite_note-rape7-10][^cite_note-rape9-11][^cite_note-rape16-12] [ethnic cleansing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_cleansing), and [genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genocidal_rape).[^cite_note-rape11-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

### Hebrew Bible

See also: [Rape in the Hebrew Bible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rape_in_the_Hebrew_Bible)

The [Hebrew Bible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_Bible) documents two narratives of gang rapes: the infamous account of [Sodom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodom_and_Gomorrah) *(Genesis 19\:4–11)* and the story of the [Levite Concubine in Gibeah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levite's_concubine), *(Judges 20–21)* which is presented as a doublet with mimicking literary structure to the former. Both instances result in the judgement of [God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/God) through destruction and war respectively.

## Perpetrator characteristics

Gang rapes are typically perpetrated by men, two or more, and tend to have somewhat different characteristics than rapes by individuals. For instance, gang rapists tend to be younger[^cite_note-16] and are more often [repeat offenders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recidivism), and the gang is more likely to be under the influence of [drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug) or [alcohol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcohol_\(drug\)).

Gang rapes are more violent on average compared to its individual counterpart, with significantly more severe sexual and non-sexual injuries to the victim. The gang members also typically [dehumanize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehumanization) the victim more before and during the rape.[^cite_note-rape1-3][^cite_note-rape2-4][^cite_note-rape3-5] Gang rapes are almost always [premeditated](2348828.md) in their intent, target victim(s), [social proof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_proof), and psychological causes.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] Social factors such as [civil wars](7085.md), [hate propaganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hate_propaganda), and ethnic conflicts increase the rate of gang rape.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20]

Rape gang members often have a binding force such as the same [race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(human_categorization\)), or place of residence thereby forming a close-knit [peer pressure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_pressure) group, encouraged by the behavior of their fellow criminals.[^cite_note-rape1-3][^cite_note-rape2-4][^cite_note-rape3-5] Gang rape can result from a group form of [criminal spin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_spin#Group), in which the group starts with less serious offenses which ultimately lead them to go well beyond their initial intention.

In a 2013 study based on 25-year crime data from [US](3434750.md) and [Europe](9239.md), between 10% and 20% of all rapes were gang rapes. Fewer than one in three gang rapes are reported, while fewer than 1 in 20 attempted but failed gang rapes are reported.[^cite_note-ullman-1]

## Motives

### Sexual entitlement and entertainment

A 2013 [*Lancet*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancet_\(journal\)) report found that among the motives for rape were sexual entitlement and seeking of entertainment. Associated factors in the crime included [alcohol misuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcohol_abuse), [poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty), [personal history of childhood victimization](18721790.md), need to prove [heterosexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heterosexuality) performance, [dominance over women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misogyny), and participation in gangs and related activities. As of September 2013, according to the report, most cases were not reported to law enforcement, and just 23% of single or multiple perpetrator rapes that were reported by the victims ended in prison sentence.[^cite_note-lancetrapestudy-6] The 1886 [Mount Rennie rape case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Rennie_rape_case) in [Sydney](27862.md), Australia falls in this category.

### As an act of war

Main article: [Wartime sexual violence](17572536.md)

Gang rapes during [civil wars](7085.md) and [ethnic riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ethnic_riots) take added dimension of becoming a means of revenge, sending a message to the victims' community, inducing fear in opponents or creating a sense of solidarity among the rapists.[^cite_note-rape7-10][^cite_note-rape8-21] At least 20,000 women were raped or gang raped and afterwards killed by the [Imperial Japanese Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_Army) during the [Nanjing Massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_Massacre) during the [Second Sino-Japanese War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sino-Japanese_War). Major ethnic conflicts and wars since [World War II](32927.md) have witnessed several campaigns of gang rapes.[^cite_note-22] [Rapes by Soviet soldiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_war_crimes#Mass_rapes) and the [Mahmudiyah rape and killings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmudiyah_rape_and_killings) by five [United States Army](32087.md) soldiers are examples of such [war crimes](48626.md).

The [Kivu conflict](14687290.md) in the Democratic Republic of the Congo saw [thousands of gang rapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_violence_in_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) every week, with each relief center reporting 10 or more daily victims of gang rape with battering of women.[^cite_note-rape9-11][^cite_note-rape10-23] The [Rwandan genocide](676659.md) of 1994 witnessed numerous gang rapes by soldiers.[^cite_note-24] The [Bosnian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnian_War) during the 1990s saw a similar campaign of gang rapes.[^cite_note-rape11-13] In the 1990s, the [Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir) region which was a battlefield between Muslim militant groups and Indian security forces also saw [a number of gang rapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rape_during_the_Kashmir_conflict) which were perpetrated by both sides of the conflict to instill fear in the population of the opposing side.[^cite_note-25] In the 2000s, civil war-torn countries such as [Sudan](27421.md), [Afghanistan](737.md) and [Syria](7515849.md) reported high rates of gang rapes by government security forces, insurgents, and [militias](20622.md).[^cite_note-rape16-12][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] Similar incidents have been recorded in [Libya](17633.md),[^cite_note-29] and [Mali](19127.md).[^cite_note-30]

During the [Russo-Ukrainian War](42085878.md), especially after the start of the [full-scale Russian invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine), [numerous cases of gang rapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_violence_in_the_Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) of Ukrainian women and men, including children, by Russian soldiers were recorded.[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35] During the [October 7 attacks](74998317.md), Palestinian militants [committed gang rapes](75436869.md) against Israeli women and men. In the subsequent [Israeli invasion of the Gaza Strip](74998519.md), [dozens of alleged cases](76149089.md) of sexual assault, including gang rapes, by [Israeli security forces](315904.md) against Palestinian detainees of all ages—both male and female—were reported by the detainees.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

### As punishment

See also: [Corrective rape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corrective_rape)

A 2013 Lancet report on rape and gang rape in [Bangladesh](3454.md), [China](5405.md), [Cambodia](334751.md), [Indonesia](14579.md), [Papua New Guinea](22943.md), and [Sri Lanka](26750.md) found that the crime was committed for various motives, including as a means to inflict punishment on the victim.[^cite_note-lancetrapestudy-6]

Traditionally, the [Cheyenne people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheyenne_people) of [North America](21139.md) used gang rape as punishment for female transgressions.[^cite_note-rape4-7] A similar practice existed among the [Munduruku people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munduruku_people) of [South America](26769.md).[^cite_note-rape5-8]

In the [Mpumalanga](313647.md) province of [South Africa](17416221.md), there are instances of gang rape as punishment.[^cite_note-rape6-9] In [Argentina](18951905.md) and [Pakistan](23235.md),[^cite_note-40] gang rape has been reported to have been ordered as punishment.

### Religious or racial factors

Though having the same race or religion often acts as a binding force for creating a close-knit serial rape gangs,[^cite_note-rape1-3][^cite_note-rape2-4][^cite_note-rape3-5] studies reported that 30% of these shared-identity rape gangs specifically targeted people of another religion or race.[^cite_note-rape12-41]

## Gang rape by country

*This is an [incomplete list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia\:INCOMPLETE) of countries, where government or media have acknowledged the problem of gang rape.*

A study found that gang rapes involved more [alcohol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcoholic_beverage) and [drug use](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substance_abuse), night attacks and severe [sexual assault](218407.md) outcomes and less victim resistance and fewer weapons than individual rapes.[^cite_note-Ullman1999-42] Another study found that group [sexual assaults](218407.md) were more violent and had greater resistance from the victim than individual sexual assaults and that victims of group sexual assaults were more likely to seek crisis and police services, to contemplate [suicide](16831059.md) and seek [psychotherapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychotherapy) than those involved in individual assaults. The two groups were about the same in the amount of [drug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug) use and drinking during the assault.[^cite_note-Gidycz1990-43]

Porter and Alison have analyzed 739 gang rape cases from [US](3434750.md) and [UK](31717.md), and found over 20% of the gang rape victims died from injuries from the gang rape.[^cite_note-Porter_L._E.,_Alison_L._J._2006_357–381-44] Gang rape is sometimes stereotyped in media as a crime of poor, minorities, or culture; however, gang rape incidence rates are high in wealthy [college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College) [campuses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campus), among non-minorities and every culture.[^cite_note-Sanday,_P._2007-45]

### Australia

Further information: [Sydney gang rapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_gang_rapes) and [Ashfield gang rapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashfield_gang_rapes)

Gang rape has been a historical and ongoing issue in Australia, with several high-profile cases receiving significant media and political attention over the years.

### Bangladesh

A 2013 Lancet study reports 1.9% of all rural men in [Bangladesh](3454.md) have committed multiple perpetrator rape (gang rape) of a woman who was not a partner, compared to 1.4% urban men. 35% of those who have committed gang rapes against women have also committed additional rapes where [the victim was a man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rape_of_males). The motives of rape included a combination of reasons. Two thirds of the gang rape perpetrators claimed [entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment) as their motive, 30% claimed they participated in gang rape out of anger and to inflict [punishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punishment) on the victim, while 11% indicated the crime followed [alcohol consumption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcohol_Consumption).[^cite_note-lancetrapestudy-6]

In the state of [Rio de Janeiro](25936.md) alone, over 6,000 rapes are reported every year (or about 37 per 100,000 people).[^cite_note-ipsbrasil-46] Nationwide, the rape rate varied between 26 and 32 per 100,000 women in 2009.[^cite_note-47] The arrest rates on rape complaints, was 4% in Rio.[^cite_note-ipsbrasil-46] About 19.6% of 2011 rapes were gang rapes.[^cite_note-48] In 2016, more than 30 men drugged and gang raped a 16-year-old girl and later posted photos and a video to boast about it online.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50]

### Canada

Peer pressure and sexual aggression are among the cited reasons for the crime.[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52] Since 1983, [Canada](5042916.md) does not track rape or gang rape statistics separately, but tracks and reports Sexual Assault Tier 3 which includes both rape and gang rape. The average Sexual Assault Tier 3 victim rate in Canada was 15 per 100,000 women, according to a 2013 [Statistics Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Canada) article about violence against women.[^cite_note-53]

### China

See also: [Yan Xiaoling – Fan Yanqiong case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yan_Xiaoling_%E2%80%93_Fan_Yanqiong_case)

A Lancet study reports, in an online survey of 980 Chinese men, 2.2% of them admitted to having committed gang rape (multiple perpetrator rape) of a non-partner woman. About 36% of the perpetrators had raped more than one woman, 50% of those who had committed gang rape had raped both one partner as a single perpetrator as well as a non-partner during a gang rape, while 32% had gang raped a woman and raped a man. The study also found that 90% of the rapists were in the 15–29 age group at the time they perpetrated the first rape.[^cite_note-lancetrapestudy-6]

In July 2013, [Li Tianyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Tianyi), the son of a renowned Chinese military singer [Li Shuangjiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Shuangjiang), was charged with four other men to being part of a gang rape. This case raised significant public anger over seemingly privileged treatment of elite gang rape criminals.[^cite_note-54]

### Egypt

See also: [Mass sexual assault in Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_sexual_assault_in_Egypt) and [Rape in Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rape_in_Egypt)

Over two dozen gang rapes were reported during civil protests in [Egypt](8087628.md) from January to March 2013; however, activists claim that many more gang rapes often go unreported because most women are too ashamed to go to the police or even tell their relatives.[^cite_note-55][^cite_note-56]

During July 2013 at [Tahrir Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahrir_Square), mobs assaulted and gang raped at least 46 women in 4 days.[^cite_note-57] A Dutch journalist was also gang-raped in Tahrir Square.[^cite_note-58][^cite_note-59] A similar gang rape and gang beating was reported by an American journalist in 2011. In a separate survey, over 90% of Egyptian women claimed to have experienced sexual violence in their lifetime.[^cite_note-60]

### France

Further information: [Ni Putes Ni Soumises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ni_Putes_Ni_Soumises) and [Rape in France](42455314.md)

Between 5000 and 7000 gang rapes are reported in [France](42455314.md) every year.[^cite_note-61]

In [banlieues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banlieue) organised gang-rapes are referred to as [*tournantes*](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/viol%20en%20r%C3%A9union#Le_ph%C3%A9nom%C3%A8ne_dit_des_%C2%AB_tournantes_%C2%BB), or "pass-arounds".[^cite_note-Ireland_131–141-62][^cite_note-Hron_2010-63] One of the first people to bring public attention to the culture of gang rape was [Samira Bellil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samira_Bellil), who published a book called *[Dans l'enfer des tournantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dans_l'enfer_des_tournantes)* ("In Gang Rape Hell").[^cite_note-Ireland_131–141-62][^cite_note-Hron_2010-63]

In October 2012, two girls in [Fontenay-sous-Bois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fontenay-sous-Bois) on the outskirts of [Paris](22989.md) reported experiencing daily gang rapes in the high-rise tower blocks, sometimes by scores of boys. One witness described 50 boys "queuing" to rape her.[^cite_note-theguardian.com-64] 10 of the 14 defendants who were minors at the time of gang rape were acquitted, while the remaining 4 adults found guilty were given 0 to 12 months in prison. ### India

The [gang rape and murder of a 23-year-old student on a public bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Delhi_gang_rape_case), on 16 December 2012 has focused international attention on India's legal system, especially laws pertaining to the protection of women. It sparked large protests across the capital of India, [Delhi](37756.md).[^cite_note-76] Several other case of [child](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child) gang rapes have been also reported, such as the [Kathua rape case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathua_rape_case).[^cite_note-timesofindia.indiatimes.com-77][^cite_note-economictimes.indiatimes.com-78] The large scale protests resulted in amendments in rape laws in India by way of [Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, 2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_Law_\(Amendment\)_Act,_2013) and formation of "The Nirbhaya Fund", designed to be used for schemes by different states to ensure women's safety and security at public places, a one-stop centre for their grievances, a helpline and other measures.[^cite_note-79] There have been reports of gang rape of tourists in India like the Jharkhand tourist rape case.[^cite_note-80][^cite_note-81]

During the instability of the heavily political and wartime era of the [Maratha Confederacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maratha_Confederacy) in the 18th century, the soldiers of the large Maratha armies engaged in wartime sexual violence against women during their campaigns in all corners of India.[^cite_note-82] Upon the spotting of a beautiful woman, up to five or six soldiers would violate her. Women threw themselves to suicide to avoid such a fate and those who resisted had their breasts cut off.[^cite_note-83][^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85][^cite_note-86][^cite_note-87][^cite_note-88]

### Indonesia

A Lancet study[^cite_note-lancetrapestudy-6] of [Jakarta](16275.md) and [Jayapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayapura) city on [New Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Guinea) island, found gang rape prevalence rate to be 2% and 6.8% of all Indonesian men respectively who, at some point in their lifetime, have committed multiple perpetrator rape. About 52% of the perpetrators had raped more than one woman, 64% of those who had committed gang rape had raped both partner as single perpetrator as well as non-partner during a gang rape, while 12% had gang raped a woman and raped a man. The study also found that 90% of the rapists in Indonesia were in the 15–29 age group when they committed their first rape.[^cite_note-lancetrapestudy-6]

### South Africa

Further information: [Sexual violence in South Africa](23575035.md) and [Murder of Hannah Cornelius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Hannah_Cornelius)

Gang rapes occur frequently in [South Africa](17416221.md),[^cite_note-122] and in certain parts it is called *jackrolling*.[^cite_note-bbcsa-123] South Africa reported over 64,000 rapes in 2012, or about 127.5 women per 100,000 population.[^cite_note-124] With one woman raped every 4 minutes, South Africa has world's highest rape incidence rate per 100,000 women. Between 10% and 33% of all rapes are gang rapes involving three or more people. About 25% of youth in [Soweto](28573.md) (near [Johannesburg](58893.md)) described gang rape as recreational and fun.[^cite_note-bbcsa-123][^cite_note-125]

In certain parts of [South Africa](17416221.md) where boys are often involved in gang rapes, rape rates are higher.[^cite_note-bbcsa-123] 40% of reported rape cases are on children below the age of 12.[^cite_note-126] In February 2002, an eight-month-old [infant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infant) was reportedly gang raped by four men. The infant has required extensive reconstructive surgery.[^cite_note-medscape-127] A significant contributing factor for the escalation in [child abuse](18721790.md) is the widespread myth in [HIV](14170.md) ravaged South Africa that [having sex with a virgin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_cleansing_myth) will cure a man of [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDS).[^cite_note-telegraph-128]

Vogelman and Lewis report 44% of rapists in South Africa have engaged in gang rape.[^cite_note-129] The National Institute of Crime Rehabilitation of South Africa claims only 1 out of 20 rapes are reported in South Africa, suggesting 1,300 women are raped every day.[^cite_note-csvr.org.za-130] A 2010 nationwide survey reported that about 27% of all South African men above the age of 18 have raped a woman in their lifetime one or more times, while 8.9% of all adult men have participated in a gang rape.[^cite_note-rachelj-131] The gang rape prevalence rate varied significantly in different provinces of South Africa; for example, in [Eastern Cape](313645.md), 13.9% of men had gang raped a woman who was not a partner.[^cite_note-rachelj-131]

In April 1999, a female American [UNICEF](19698110.md) official visiting South Africa on business was gang raped during a robbery of the home where she was staying.[^cite_note-132] In February 2013, a 17-year-old girl was gang raped, mutilated and left to die in a town near [Cape Town](6653.md). This led to widespread protests.<sup>\[citation needed]</sup>

### Sweden

Main article: [Rape in Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rape_in_Sweden)

See also: [Immigration and crime § Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immigration_and_crime#Sweden)

In 2013, *Forskning & Framsteg* *(FoF)*, a Swedish popular science magazine, published an article reporting that "The evidence suggests that gang rapes in [Sweden](5058739.md) is increasing - despite the decline in violent crime in general."[^cite_note-Dramatic_increase_in_the_number_of_reported_gang_rape._Author:_Henrik_Hojer-139] *FoF* went on to report that, "The increase, which primarily occurred in the 2000s, may have to do with "rape with multiple perpetrators" from 2004, classified as "serious crime", which means that more obscure cases fall into this category." *FoF* further reports that "National Council's investigator, Klara Hradilova-Selin, said that "developments could at least partly be interpreted in terms of an actual increase."[^cite_note-Dramatic_increase_in_the_number_of_reported_gang_rape._Author:_Henrik_Hojer-139] Although there are a number of other important reasons why we are seeing more reports, such as, Klara Hradilova-Selin goes on to say, "more women dare to go the police" and an "increased alcohol consumption".[^cite_note-Dramatic_increase_in_the_number_of_reported_gang_rape._Author:_Henrik_Hojer-139] Finally *FoF* goes on to report that in the 1990s "In the gang rape of three or more perpetrators 39 percent are foreign born."[^cite_note-Dramatic_increase_in_the_number_of_reported_gang_rape._Author:_Henrik_Hojer-139] The statistics for gang rapes were not investigated by Swedish authorities after 2006.[^cite_note-Dramatic_increase_in_the_number_of_reported_gang_rape._Author:_Henrik_Hojer-139]

On 21 January 2017, in [Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala), [Sweden](5058739.md), a group of Arab Facebook users allegedly [gang-raped an unconscious woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Uppsala_rape) for several hours.[^cite_note-140][^cite_note-141][^cite_note-142] The crime was stopped when viewers of the Facebook live feed realized what was happening and phoned the police, who entered the apartment and interrupted the crime still in progress.[^cite_note-UppsalaLiveRape-143][^cite_note-144]

In March 2018, newspaper *[Expressen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expressen)* investigated gang rape court cases from the two preceding years and found that there were 43 men having been convicted. Their average age was 21 and 13 were under the age of 18 when the crime was committed. Of the convicted, 40 out of the 43 [were either immigrants (born abroad) or born in Sweden to immigrant parents](24537353.md).[^cite_note-145] Another investigation by newspaper [Aftonbladet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftonbladet) found that of 112 men and boys convicted for gang rape since July 2012, 82 were born outside Europe. The median age of the victims was 15, while 7 out of 10 perpetrators were between 15 and 20.[^cite_note-146]

### United Kingdom

See also: [Rotherham child sexual exploitation scandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotherham_child_sexual_exploitation_scandal)

A number of gang rapes have taken place in the [United Kingdom](31717.md),[^cite_note-150] although the UK authorities had not separately collected data on gang rapes until the 2011 [Child Exploitation and Online Protection Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_Exploitation_and_Online_Protection_Centre) report on [localised grooming](1651190.md) entitled "Out of Mind, Out of Sight",[^cite_note-151] but which only deals with adolescent victims. However, [Scotland Yard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland_Yard) expressed concern in 2009 over the rise of 'gang sex attacks' in London, detailing an increase of 71 cases in 2003/2004 to 93 cases in 2008/2009.[^cite_note-152]

Media reports of gang rapes in the UK are often racially charged since late 2000.[^cite_note-Gang_rape_race_issue-153][^cite_note-154] This due to a particular pattern of repeated non-white perpetrated gang rapes in the metropolitan areas.[^cite_note-Gang_rape_race_issue-153] The [Rochdale sex trafficking gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochdale_sex_trafficking_gang) would later epitomise the phenomenon.[^cite_note-155] The [Home Office](225695.md) report of 2013 described these cases as '\[showing] a particular model... of organised, serious exploitation and abuse that involves predominantly Pakistani-heritage men grooming and abusing predominantly white British girls.'[^cite_note-156] The report stresses that the Government does not believe 'localised grooming' is intrinsic of any culture, religion, and or race, despite acknowledging the phenomenon.

In March 2014, Crime Prevention Minister [Norman Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Baker) described "ground breaking work" to identify women and girls who can get drawn into gangs. "Girls associated with gangs can face sexual violence and we have provided £1.2m for 13 Young People's Advocates to support those at risk," he said on the launch of a report by the [Centre for Social Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Social_Justice) (CSJ), a think-tank established in 2002 by [Iain Duncan Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iain_Duncan_Smith), another Cabinet minister. Baker said the government had set up a network of more than 70 people with experience of dealing with gangs to work with 33 of the worst affected areas of the country, including 20 in [London](17867.md). In one case cited by the CSJ report, a schoolgirl was abducted and sexually assaulted by nine males because she criticised a gang member. The report found that girls as young as eight were being used to carry drugs while female gang members in their teens were being pressured to have sex with boys as young as 10 to initiate males into gangs. A 2014 report by the [Office of the Children's Commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Children's_Commissioner_for_England) suggested that almost 2,500 children had been known to be victims of child sexual exploitation by gangs and groups.[^cite_note-157]

In July 2018 [Home Secretary](149104.md) [Sajid Javid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sajid_Javid) ordered research into the particular characteristics of grooming gangs.[^cite_note-158] However, as of March 2020 this report has not been released with the [Home Office](225695.md) claiming it is 'not in public interest' to release the findings.[^cite_note-159]
