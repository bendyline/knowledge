The **Barbuda People's Movement** (**BPM**) is a [left-wing](18499.md) [Barbudan nationalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbudan_nationalism) and [separatist](286527.md)[^cite_note-Lansford,_2019\:62-3] [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Antigua and Barbuda](951.md) active only on the island of [Barbuda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbuda). The party's symbol is the [European fallow deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_fallow_deer), national animal of Barbuda.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The party seeks the secession of Barbuda from Antigua and Barbuda.[^cite_note-\:0-6] The party is allied with the [United Progressive Party](79048085.md), being part of its coalition government between 2004 and 2014.[^cite_note-7]

## History

The BPM opposed the [1981 independence agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_of_Antigua_and_Barbuda) and its leader [Hilbourne Frank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbourne_Frank) was elected in [1989](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Antiguan_general_election) as the party's sole deputy.[^cite_note-Lansford,_2019\:62-3]

They held the seat in the [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antigua_and_Barbuda_general_election,_1994) and [1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antigua_and_Barbuda_general_election,_1999). In the [2004 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antigua_and_Barbuda_general_election,_2004) the candidates of the BPM ([Trevor Walker](66270598.md)) and the [Barbuda People's Movement for Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbuda_People's_Movement_for_Change) both won 400 votes. In a rerun of the election on 20 April Walker received 408 votes, whilst BPMC candidate [Arthur Nibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Nibbs) won only 394. Walker was then the first BPM deputy to be appointed to government, as a member of [Baldwin Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldwin_Spencer)'s cabninet.[^cite_note-8] The party retained the seat in the [2009 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antigua_and_Barbuda_general_election,_2009).[^cite_note-Lansford,_2019\:62-3]

## Barbuda’s secession request

The government is taking the unprecedented step of presenting the matter to the Parliament after it received a letter from the [Barbuda Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbuda_Council) requesting that discussions commence on the separation of [Barbuda from Antigua.](951.md)

In the letter dated 31 August 2020, Council Secretary Paul Nedd informed Cabinet Secretary Konata Lee that the [Barbuda Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbuda_Council) wished to secede from Antigua in order to determine a separate future for Barbuda and its people.[^cite_note-\:0-6]
