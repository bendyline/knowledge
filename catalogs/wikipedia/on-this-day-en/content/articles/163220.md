# Golden Globe Award for Best Television Series – Drama

A Golden Globe.

&#x20;A Golden Globe.

The **Golden Globe Award for Best Television Series – Drama**[^cite_note-1] is one of the annual [Golden Globe Awards](142724.md), given to the best drama television series. Documentary series and mini-series are also eligible for this award. From 1962 to 1968, the category was **Golden Globe Award for Best Television Series**, and grouped musical, comedy and drama series in a single category. After 1968, musical and comedy series were given their own category, the [Golden Globe Award for Best Television Series – Musical or Comedy](163218.md).
