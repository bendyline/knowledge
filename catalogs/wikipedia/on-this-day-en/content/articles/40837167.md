# Lufthansa fleet

[Lufthansa](97733.md) operates a mainline fleet consisting of [Airbus](26220236.md) narrow and wide-body and [Boeing](18933266.md) wide-body aircraft.[^cite_note-auto1-1][^cite_note-auto2-2] The mainline fleet is composed of seven different aircraft families: the [Airbus A320 and A320neo families](164933.md), [Airbus A330](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A330), [Airbus A340](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A340), [Airbus A350](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A350), [Airbus A380](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A380), [Boeing 747](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_747) and [Boeing 787](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_787). This list excludes Lufthansa brand subsidiaries [Lufthansa City Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lufthansa_City_Airlines) and [Lufthansa Cargo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lufthansa_Cargo).

Additionally, Lufthansa currently has orders placed for further new Airbus A350, Boeing 787, and [Boeing 777X](41065722.md) series aircraft. Up until 2027, these deliveries will replace all A340 series aircraft alongside the remaining Boeing 747-400s, and allow for two Airbus A330s to be transferred to [Brussels Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels_Airlines), five Airbus A330s and four Airbus A350s to be transferred to [Discover Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discover_Airlines), and five Boeing 787s to be transferred to [Austrian Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Airlines).[^cite_note-auto3-3]
