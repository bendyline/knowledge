# Flying Doctors of Malaysia

**Flying Doctors of Malaysia** is conducted under the [Ministry of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_\(Malaysia\)) with transport provided by the [Royal Malaysian Air Force](1137609.md).

This service is offered to natives who live deep in the jungle and cannot be contacted via other routes. The service is limited to areas that have landing zones.

In [West Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Malaysia), the service is provided by the Maternal and Child clinics (KKIA), consisting of static and mobile. Static KKIA service is provided in the Hospital JHEOA, [Gombak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gombak) whereas mobile KKIA service is provided by the various interior medical posts which is under the control of JHEOA.&#x20;

The districts involving the mobile KKIA services include: [Pahang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahang) district - [Kuala Lipis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuala_Lipis) - Lenjang Post, (Landing Zone) LZ Titom, LZ Kendrong and LZ Cerong. Service is provided by the Air Doctor Service with the Helicopter support form TUDM. [Kelantan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelantan) district - Gua Musang Districts- P1 district- service is provided by the Air Doctor Services with assistance from TUDM. Services provided to the P2 District by 4WD vehicles.

In [Sarawak](28258.md), there are 12 teams of Air Land force (*pasukan Darat Udara - PDU*) that serve 159 locations. In 2006, the federal government spent an estimated RM 5.1 million a year in Sarawak alone for Flying Doctors services. <https://web.archive.org/web/20070930192425/http://blisdemo.bernama.com/article.phtml?SN=0e273ec6447607fbf8650ea2f365c258&s=10&p=6562>

In [Sabah](28678.md) there are two teams of Air Land force (*pasukan PDU*) that serve 41 locations and villages.

Recent reductions in flying doctors service have drawn flack from members of Parliament representative who question why Malaysian government should reduce such vital services.
