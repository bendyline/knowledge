# The Bahamas–Kosovo relations

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| [Bahamas](3451.md) | [Kosovo](17391.md) |
|  |  |

**Bahamas–Kosovo relations** are the [bilateral relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilateral_relations) between the [Commonwealth of the Bahamas](3451.md) and [the Republic of Kosovo](17391.md). The Bahamas officially [recognised](15808914.md) Kosovo as an independent state in 19 December 2025.

## History

Kosovo [declared its independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Kosovo_declaration_of_independence) from Serbia on 17 February 2008 and was met with mixed reactions from the international community. Initially, the Bahamas did not recognise Kosovo's independence.&#x20;

In April 2010, Bahamian foreign minister [Brent Symonette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brent_Symonette) said "We have considered and continue to consider the situation on the ground on both sides and maintain a status of awaiting the outcome of negotiations between Kosovo and its neighbouring countries before committing support to either of the two countries".[^cite_note-1] On 26 September 2012, the Bahamian foreign minister, [Frederick A. Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Mitchell_\(Bahamian_politician\)), said that his state had sympathy for the independence of Kosovo, and that the Bahamas would support the state of Kosovo.[^cite_note-2] In September 2024, Kosovo Foreign minister, [Donika Gërvalla-Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donika_G%C3%ABrvalla-Schwarz), met with Bahamas' foreign minister Frederick Mitchell on the fringes of the United Nations General Assembly. They discussed areas of mutual interest and Gërvalla-Schwarz expressed gratitude for the Bahamas' support of Kosovo's international memberships.[^cite_note-3]

Bahamian prime minister [Philip Davis](19387000.md) held a bilateral meeting with Kosovo's president [Vjosa Osmani](61979540.md) on the fringes of the United Nations General Assembly in September 2025 where they discussed deepening cooperation between the two countries.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

The Bahamas officially [recognised](15808914.md) Kosovo as an independent state on 19 December 2025.[^cite_note-6] The two countries also established official diplomatic relations with one another in accordance with international law and the [Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations](32753.md) of 1961.[^cite_note-7]
