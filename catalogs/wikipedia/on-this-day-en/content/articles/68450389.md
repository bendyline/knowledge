**Carlos Manuel Vila Nova** (born 27 July 1959)[^cite_note-Biografia-1] is a [Santomean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9an) politician who is the fifth and current [president of São Tomé and Príncipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidents_of_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9_and_Pr%C3%ADncipe), since 2 October 2021. He served as the Minister of Public Works and Natural Resources (2010–2012) and minister of Infrastructure, Natural Resources and the Environment (2014–2018) in successive governments of Prime Minister [Patrice Trovoada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrice_Trovoada).

## Early life and education

Vila Nova was born in [Neves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neves,_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9_and_Pr%C3%ADncipe), a city in the [Lembá District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemb%C3%A1_District) on the northern coast of [São Tomé Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9_Island). He received a degree in [telecommunications engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunications_engineering) from the [University of Oran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oran), [Algeria](358.md) in 1985, and later returned to become the head of the computer department of the government Statistics Directorate.

## Career

In 1988, he left the civil service to become a sales manager at Hotel Miramar, which was then the only hotel in the country. He was promoted to Director of Hotel Miramar in 1992. In 1997, he became Director of Hotel Pousada Boa Vista, and founded his own travel agency Mistral Voyages. Vila Nova remained in the tourism industry until he entered politics in 2010.[^cite_note-\:2-2]

Vila Nova served as Minister of Public Works and Natural Resources in the cabinet of [Patrice Trovoada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrice_Trovoada) from 2010[^cite_note-3] until the government lost its majority in 2012.[^cite_note-4] He was appointed Minister of Infrastructure, Natural Resources and the Environment when Trovoada's [Independent Democratic Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Democratic_Action) (ADI) regained its parliamentary majority in [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9an_legislative_election).[^cite_note-\:0-5] In [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9an_legislative_election), Vila Nova was elected to the [National Assembly](2960234.md).[^cite_note-\:2-2]

## Presidency

He was the [Independent Democratic Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Democratic_Action) (ADI) candidate in the [2021 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9an_presidential_election).[^cite_note-\:1-6] On 6 September, he was declared president-elect of São Tomé and Príncipe, after receiving 58% of the vote and defeating [Guilherme Posser da Costa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guilherme_Posser_da_Costa) of the [MLSTP/PSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_for_the_Liberation_of_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9_and_Pr%C3%ADncipe/Social_Democratic_Party). On 14 September, he was declared president by the Tribunal constitutional court.[^cite_note-7]

On 6 January 2025, he dismissed Prime Minister [Patrice Trovoada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrice_Trovoada) and his government, citing Trovoada's prolonged absences and his government's failure to solve multiple issues.[^cite_note-8] Trovoada responded by calling his dismissal "illegal" and "unconstitutional".[^cite_note-9] He then selected then Minister of Justice, [Ilza Amado Vaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilza_Amado_Vaz), as prime minister. Vaz then resigned, stating in a letter that her continued leadership "would not contribute to the success of public policies and the harmonious and peaceful development." He thereafter picked [Américo Ramos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Am%C3%A9rico_Ramos), who was not endorsed for the prime ministerial role by the ADI, being criticized for ignoring the majority of the National Assembly.[^cite_note-10]

Vila Nova was reelected president in the [2026 São Toméan presidential election](79840944.md) on 19 July, winning 55.94% of the vote in the first round as an [independent candidate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_candidate) with support from [MLSTP–PSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_for_the_Liberation_of_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9_and_Pr%C3%ADncipe_%E2%80%93_Social_Democratic_Party) against [Nito Abreu](83630348.md), who was supported by ADI and garnered 41.42%.[^cite_note-11] The turnout reached a record low of 41%.

### Policies

In March 2026, Vila Nova, together with Prime Minister Américo Ramos and the international business group Aequatoris, announced the planned creation of the *São Tomé Financial Centre*, a major economic reform that intended to establish a free zone aimed at attracting international service companies to the country.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

## Personal life

Vila Nova is married to [Maria de Fátima Afonso Vila Nova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A1tima_Vila_Nova) and has two daughters.[^cite_note-\:2-2]
