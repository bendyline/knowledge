An [epidemic](66981.md) of a new variant of [clade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clade) I [mpox](242702.md) (formerly known as monkeypox), called clade 1b,[^cite_note-World-Health-Organization-2024-2] began in [Central Africa](539606.md) at least as early as September 2023.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3][^cite_note-Junaidi1-2024-4] As of September 2024, more than 29,000 cases have been reported, with over 800 fatalities (\~3% [fatality rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_fatality_rate)),[^cite_note-Reuters20240923-5] nearly all in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mpox_in_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo).[^cite_note-Cheng-2024a-6] According to the [European CDC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Centre_for_Disease_Prevention_and_Control): "The size of these outbreaks could be larger than reported due to under-ascertainment and under-reporting."[^cite_note-7]

On 14 August 2024, the [World Health Organization](33583.md) declared the epidemic a [public health emergency of international concern](42694003.md).[^cite_note-World-Health-Organization-2024-2][^cite_note-Mandavilli-2024-8][^cite_note-\:7-9]

## Background

Main article: [2022–2023 mpox outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932023_mpox_outbreak)

Mpox is an infectious [viral disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_disease) that is endemic among small mammals in central areas of Africa; it can also infect humans.[^cite_note-WHOfact2023-10] Symptoms include a [rash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rash) that forms [blisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blister) and then crusts over, fever, and [swollen lymph nodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lymphadenopathy).[^cite_note-WHOfact2023-10] The illness is usually mild, and most infected individuals recover within a few weeks without treatment.[^cite_note-Gov.UK2022-11] However, cases may be severe, especially in children, pregnant women, or people with suppressed immune systems.[^cite_note-WHO_Q\&A-12]

In May 2022, the [World Health Organization](33583.md) (WHO) declared a prior [mpox outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932023_mpox_outbreak), involving a clade IIb of the virus, a [global health emergency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_health_emergency). The disease had infected 87,000 individuals and caused 140 deaths when the WHO ended its global emergency in May 2023, citing a combination of vaccination and public health information as successful control measures.[^cite_note-WHO2023-05-13][^cite_note-14] Cases of clade IIb which can be traced to this outbreak are continuing to be detected worldwide, although at a relatively low level.[^cite_note-15]

In September 2023, cases of mpox started increasing in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md) and continued into 2024. The [Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention](63909811.md) (ACDC) reported a 160% increase in cases from the previous year. Most of these cases were determined to be caused by clade I of the virus.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3][^cite_note-16]

### Timeline

Main article: [Timeline of the 2023–2025 mpox epidemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_the_2023%E2%80%932025_mpox_epidemic)

Mpox cases from clade Ib infections were growing in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md) (DRC) by September 2023,[^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3][^cite_note-Junaidi1-2024-4] with several of them being identified in [Kamituga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamituga), a [mining town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining_town) in the [province](3984115.md) of [South Kivu](446959.md).[^cite_note-vakaniaki-66][^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3][^cite_note-67] A nationwide outbreak was reported in January 2024.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3][^cite_note-68]

On 12 August 2024, the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention declared that the growing mpox outbreaks in [Africa](5334607.md) had become a public health emergency, with over 517 deaths being reported. As a result, the organization requested the help and intervention of the [international community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_community) to control the spread of the virus and treat infected patients.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024a-6][^cite_note-69] The ACDC stated that the fatality rate of the strain of virus causing the outbreak was 3–4%, significantly higher than the less than 1% fatality rate recorded during the [2022–2023 mpox outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932023_mpox_outbreak).[^cite_note-Cheng-2024a-6] On 14 August, the [World Health Organization](33583.md) declared the epidemic a [public health emergency of international concern](42694003.md) or [global health emergency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_health_emergency).[^cite_note-World-Health-Organization-2024-2][^cite_note-Mandavilli-2024-8]

As of 16 August 2024, fifteen countries were reported to have identified cases of mpox;[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWorld_Health_Organization20242–3-17] and over 96% of all reported cases and fatalities from the disease were confirmed in the DRC, with 16,839 reported cases and 501 reported deaths.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024a-6] The ACDC reported that 70% of reported cases in the DRC were in children under 15, as that demographic represented 85% of reported deaths.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024a-6] [Epidemiologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epidemiologist) Jacques Alonda expressed concern over the disease's spread in [refugee camps](438046.md) in the DRC and its neighbor countries, mainly due to the amount of pressure already applied to the [national healthcare system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_in_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) by concurrent outbreaks of [cholera](7591.md) and [measles](58911.md), as well as widespread [malnutrition](258979.md).[^cite_note-Cheng-2024a-6]

Also on 16 August, the [European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Centre_for_Disease_Prevention_and_Control) (ECDC) officially raised the risk level of clade I to the general [European population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_population) from "very low" to "low" due to the likelihood of more imported cases being identified across the continent, although the agency reiterated that the risk of sustained transmission of the virus strain in Europe was still considered to be minimum.[^cite_note-European-Centre-for-Disease-Prevention-and-Control-2024-70][^cite_note-71] The ECDC also advised travellers to consider getting vaccinated against mpox should they visit African countries affected by the outbreak.[^cite_note-European-Centre-for-Disease-Prevention-and-Control-2024-70][^cite_note-72]

As of 19 September 2024, 15 of the 55 member states of the African Union have reported cases according the Africa CDC.[^cite_note-73] Rwanda started its vaccination campaign, while the Democratic Republic of Congo, the epicentre of the outbreak, is set to start vaccinations in early October 2024.[^cite_note-74]

In October 2024, the WHO approved the first diagnostic test under the Emergency Use Listing (EUL) procedure. The Alinity m MPXV assay enables the detection of the virus by testing swabs of skin lesions.[^cite_note-75]

### Countries with widespread transmission

#### Democratic Republic of the Congo

Further information: [Mpox in the Democratic Republic of the Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mpox_in_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo)

As of August 2024, a cumulative total of 16,839 suspected cases of mpox (at least 1,888 of which were laboratory confirmed)[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWorld_Health_Organization20242–3-17][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEAfrica_Centres_for_Disease_Control_and_Prevention20241-18] and 570 deaths[^cite_note-RFI-2024-76] (at least 8 of which were laboratory confirmed)[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWorld_Health_Organization20242–3-17][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEAfrica_Centres_for_Disease_Control_and_Prevention20241-18] have been reported in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md) (DRC).[^cite_note-RFI-2024-76]

On 19 August, the DRC's [Ministry of Public Health](19686492.md), [Samuel Roger Kamba Mulamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Roger_Kamba_Mulamba), said in a press conference that all of the country's [provinces](3984115.md), including the capital city of [Kinshasa](45773.md), had been affected by the outbreak,[^cite_note-RFI-2024-76] while announcing that the [national government](9430937.md) would launch a €45 million response plan including awareness campaigns, medical team deployment and patient care, but not vaccines.[^cite_note-RFI-2024-76][^cite_note-\:1-77]

Various media reported that the impact of the mpox outbreak across the DRC, and especially in eastern provinces, had been significantly worsened by factors such as widespread conflicts in the region, including an [offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M23_offensive_\(2022%E2%80%93present\)) by the [Rwanda](25645.md)-backed [March 23 Movement](35878650.md) in [North Kivu](446958.md)— which had displaced thousands of civilians, most of whom had been forced to gather in refugee camps around [Goma](1210483.md) or in the city itself— , widespread extreme poverty, poor access to healthcare services, and the circulation of the infection within [sex workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_worker) in illicit [mining industries](11617636.md).[^cite_note-\:10-78][^cite_note-79][^cite_note-\:11-80] [Medair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medair)'s health advisor in the DRC, Pierre Olivier Ngadjole, said that around 70% of the new mpox cases registered in the Goma area between June and August 2024 had involved people living in displacement camps, while the director of the Bulengo refugee camp, Mahoro Faustin, expressed concerns over the lack of testing kits available in the area.[^cite_note-\:10-78][^cite_note-\:11-80]

### Countries with limited local transmission

As of August 2024, fifteen countries have reported cases of mpox;[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWorld_Health_Organization20242–3-17] the WHO reported that new cases of the mpox strain, all of which linked to the outbreak in the DRC,[^cite_note-Cheng-2024a-6] had been identified for the first time in four [East African](260987.md) nations: [Burundi](21490998.md), [Kenya](188171.md), [Rwanda](25645.md), and [Uganda](31816.md).[^cite_note-Cheng-2024a-6][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWorld_Health_Organization20242–3-17] [Ivory Coast](57744.md) also reported new cases for the first time.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEWorld_Health_Organization20242–3-17]

#### Central African Republic

On 30 July 2024, the [Minister of Health](16885611.md) of the [Central African Republic](5478.md), Pierre Somsé, declared an outbreak of mpox in [Bangui](56617.md) following a period when the disease was mostly restricted to rural areas.[^cite_note-Chibelushi-2024-81] Somsé reported that some families in the country were hiding infected relatives in fear of being [stigmatized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_stigma), thus increasing the risk of transmission of the disease.[^cite_note-Chibelushi-2024-81]

**Germany**

In October 2024 the first travel-associated case of mpox clade 1b was reported in Germany.[^cite_note-82] In 2024-2025 more travel-associated cases were reported by [Germany](11867.md)'s [Public Health Agency](21272973.md).[^cite_note-83] In early 2026, local transmission in Germany was confirmed in Germany during in outbreak in Berlin.[^cite_note-84]

#### Sweden

On 15 August 2024, [Sweden](5058739.md)'s [Public Health Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Health_Agency_of_Sweden) reported the first case outside Africa, which involved a person who had contracted clade I mpox during a stay in an area of Africa affected by the outbreak.[^cite_note-sweden-20][^cite_note-85][^cite_note-sweden_svt-86] In a public statement, the agency said that, while the case in itself did not represent a higher risk to the general population,[^cite_note-sweden-20][^cite_note-sweden_svt-86] occasional imported cases "may continue to occur".[^cite_note-sweden-20][^cite_note-87]

#### Thailand

On 21 August 2024, [Thailand](30128.md)'s [Department of Disease Control](23194319.md) confirmed a mpox case involving a 66-year-old European man who was a resident of Thailand,[^cite_note-BBC_News_Thailand-21][^cite_note-\:5-88] and had returned from an African country affected by the outbreak, with his flight transiting through an undisclosed [Middle Eastern](19323.md) country.[^cite_note-BBC_News_Thailand-21][^cite_note-\:5-88][^cite_note-\:6-89] The strain was later confirmed to be clade 1b.[^cite_note-90] The patient who tested positive for clade Ib was a European male who arrived in Bangkok last week from Africa, according to the Department of Disease Control of Thailand's health ministry.[^cite_note-BBC_News_Thailand-21]

#### South Africa

On 23 September 2026, the South African Health Department reported that 22 cases of mpox had been confirmed (18 in the Western Cape, 3 in Gauteng, 1 in Kwazulu Natal). [^cite_note-91]

#### Spain

In December 2025, Spanish authorities reported what is believed to be the first case of human-to-human transmission of mpox clade 1b outside of Africa without any known travel link.[^cite_note-92]

### Clade II cases

Some countries have reported contemporary mpox cases that then turned out to be infections with clade II variants, presumably unrelated to the clade Ib outbreak. There is a [global outbreak of clade II infections that began in 2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932023_mpox_outbreak).

#### Pakistan

On 15 August 2024, [Pakistan](23235.md)'s [National Command and Operation Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Command_and_Operation_Center) (NCOC) reported a suspected case of mpox in the [province](446017.md) of [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md),[^cite_note-Junaidi1-2024-4] involving a resident of [Mardan](1844876.md) who had recently returned from an [Arab Gulf state](3088555.md);[^cite_note-Ali-2024-93] the person was later confirmed to have been diagnosed with mpox by the [Pakistani Ministry of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Health_Services,_Regulation_and_Coordination), although [sequencing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequencing) of [viral samples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampling_\(medicine\)) was still ongoing in order to determine the nature of the variant.[^cite_note-Ali-2024-93][^cite_note-AlJazeera-2024-94][^cite_note-95]

On 17 August, the health department of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa confirmed that two patients had tested positive for mpox, while another one was waiting for confirmation; all of the patients had recently returned to Pakistan from the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md).[^cite_note-Mehra-2024-56]

On 19 August, another suspected mpox case was reported, involving a resident of [Azad Kashmir](2745.md) who had just returned from a Middle Eastern country; however, the Ministry of Health stated that none of the reported cases in the country had been caused by clade 1b of the [virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monkeypox_virus).[^cite_note-Junaidi2-2024-57]

#### Taiwan

On 18 August 2024, the Public Health Bureau of the [city council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_council) of [Tainan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tainan), [Taiwan](25734.md), announced that as of 16 August, three cases of mpox—all of which involved men who have had close contact with unspecified people—had been confirmed in the city since the start of the year; further details about the patients' traveling history and the variant were not disclosed.[^cite_note-TVBS-2024-96][^cite_note-97]

#### Portugal

On 16 August, [Portugal](23033.md)'s [Directorate-General of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directorate-General_of_Health) (DGS) confirmed that three new cases of mpox had been confirmed in the country between May and July 2024;[^cite_note-\:2-59][^cite_note-\:3-98] the agency reported that all of the cases had been detected in the [North Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Region,_Portugal), with two of them having travelled abroad during the [incubation period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incubation_period) of the disease.[^cite_note-99] However, the DGS said that all of the three patients had contracted clade IIb mpox,[^cite_note-\:2-59] whereas no clade I cases had been reported.[^cite_note-\:2-59][^cite_note-\:3-98]

#### Indonesia

On 17 August 2024, [Indonesia](14579.md)'s [Independence day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_\(Indonesia\)), the national [Ministry of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_\(Indonesia\)) (Kemenkes) reported a total of 14 confirmed mpox cases from January to April 2024.[^cite_note-BBC_News_Indonesia-49] This brought the cumulative total to 88 cases in Indonesia when combined with the [previous outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932023_mpox_outbreak)'s 73 cases reported in 2023, as well as one case from 2022.[^cite_note-BBC_News_Indonesia-49][^cite_note-100] The Indonesian government later reported a 100% recovery rate of all 88 confirmed cases since 2022.[^cite_note-101] The latest outbreak, which initially began in [Jakarta](16275.md), then spread to the [Riau Islands](453745.md). Among the total cases, 87 patients had been declared cured, with 54 cases involving the clade IIb variant. The distribution of cases by region included 59 in Jakarta, 13 in [West Java](314356.md), nine in [Banten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banten), three in [East Java](320443.md), three in the [Special Region of Yogyakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Region_of_Yogyakarta) (DIY), and one in the Riau Islands.[^cite_note-102]

As of 4 September 2024, there were no known cases of Clade Ib mpox in Indonesia;[^cite_note-\:19-103] 11 cases that were previously suspected to belong to Clade Ib had all returned negative from further [PCR tests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PCR_test).[^cite_note-104] Following the detection of multiple suspected cases across different points in Indonesia, including [Bali](4147.md) and Jakarta, between August and September,[^cite_note-\:19-103] the [Indonesian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_Government) decided to partially restore its past [COVID-19 mitigation efforts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Indonesia),[^cite_note-\:20-105][^cite_note-106] including the use of the [SATUSEHAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PeduliLindungi) health pass for all travelers entering Indonesia.[^cite_note-\:20-105][^cite_note-107]

#### Philippines

On 19 August 2024, the [Philippines](23440.md)' [Department of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Health_\(Philippines\)) (DOH) announced that a 33-year-old male from [Metro Manila](72340.md), with no prior history of traveling overseas, had tested positive for mpox the day prior.[^cite_note-Junaidi2-2024-57][^cite_note-Rappler-2024-108] The patient was later determined to have contracted clade II mpox.[^cite_note-PhilippinesII-39]

#### New Zealand

On 9 September 2024, an mpox case was confirmed in New Zealand after attending the Winter Pride festival in [Queenstown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queenstown,_New_Zealand) in August 2024.[^cite_note-109] On 11 September, a second mpox case was linked to the Queenstown Winter Pride festival.[^cite_note-110] By 20 September, a total of 11 cases of mpox were linked to the Winter Pride festival. The Queenstown outbreak included several cases of mpox clade II.[^cite_note-111][^cite_note-112]

## Virology

[Mpox](242702.md) is caused by the *[monkeypox virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monkeypox_virus)*. The virus is transmitted through close contact with infected animals or people,[^cite_note-Sampson-2024-113] including in-person communication and other kinds of physical contact,[^cite_note-Lay-2024-114][^cite_note-Il-Post-2024-115] contaminated bedsheets,[^cite_note-Lay-2024-114] clothing or needles,[^cite_note-Il-Post-2024-115] [sexual contact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_contact),[^cite_note-Sampson-2024-113][^cite_note-Lay-2024-114] and [consumption of contaminated meat](531611.md).[^cite_note-Lay-2024-114][^cite_note-Il-Post-2024-115]

In April 2024, researchers identified a novel subgroup of [clade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clade) I of mpox in [Kamituga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamituga), a [mining town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining_town) in [South Kivu](446959.md), Democratic Republic of the Congo.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMurhula_Masirika_et_al.20241-116] [Epidemiologists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epidemiology) reported that the new variant—later named "clade Ib"[^cite_note-Lay-2024-114][^cite_note-Il-Post-2024-115] or "clade 1b"[^cite_note-Il-Post-2024-115]—had the potential to spread with greater ease compared to other mpox strains.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3] The researchers theorized that the strain had undergone [genetic mutations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutation), allowing it to spread more easily through human transmission due to the mining town's remote location significantly limiting contact with animals that naturally carry and spread the disease.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3] DRC's [National Institute of Biomedical Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_National_pour_la_Recherche_Biomedicale) reported that this marked "a new phase of mpox" from the prior [outbreak in 2022 and 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932023_mpox_outbreak), since the new variant produced lesions predominantly on the [genitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genitals), making it more difficult to diagnose compared to strains that caused chest, feet, and hand lesions. It was thought that the new variant could have a higher likelihood of [silent transmission](602790.md) due to its different manifestations.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3] The research team determined the detected form to be a Clade I type strain,[^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3] which historically has caused more severe symptoms in comparison to the Clade II type that was predominant during the 2022–2023 mpox outbreak.[^cite_note-Cheng-2024-3][^cite_note-Il-Post-2024-115]

2024 genetic sequences of mpox from Republic of the Congo suggest that there are multiple co-circulating strains.[^cite_note-117]

## Prevention and mitigation

In early January 2023, the New Zealand Government purchased 5,000 vials of mpox vaccines, enough for 20,000 people. A further shipment of vaccine vials was ordered for the later half of 2023. Individuals eligible for the mpox vaccine included close physical contacts of people with mpox including sexual partners and household contacts; [gay](5171128.md), [bisexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bisexual), and other men who have sex with multiple partners, [transgender](19904525.md) and [cisgender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cisgender) women who are in sexual relationships with these men; and those recommended to have the vaccine by medical specialists.[^cite_note-Beehive_9_Jan_2023-118]

Until June 2024, mpox vaccines were not approved by any African government, and the [Strategic Advisory Group of Experts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategic_Advisory_Group_of_Experts) had not attested to the efficacy of vaccines. The director of the WHO's [Health Emergencies Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_Emergencies_Programme_\(WHO\)), [Michael J. Ryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Ryan_\(doctor\)), noted in an interview with [NPR](29697232.md) that the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md) was simultaneously facing [several other endemic diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_in_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo), including [measles](58911.md) and [cholera](7591.md). Up to that point, only two labs in the DRC could perform [polymerase chain reaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymerase_chain_reaction) testing for mpox.[^cite_note-119]

Also in June 2024, authorities in the DRC had approved the vaccines [MVA-BN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modified_vaccinia_Ankara) (Jynneos) manufactured by [Bavarian Nordic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Nordic), and [LC16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallpox_vaccine#LC16m8), manufactured by KM Biologics, for emergency use.[^cite_note-120] Jynneos was later approved in [Nigeria](21383.md), also for emergency use.[^cite_note-Johnston-2024-121][^cite_note-Gilchrist-2024-122] Both countries officially started vaccination campaigns for mpox by the end of August of the same year.[^cite_note-\:0-123]

Also in August 2024, the [chief executive officer](52234.md) of [GAVI](38346167.md), [Sania Nishtar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sania_Nishtar), said the organization had already allocated up to \$500 million as part of their "First Response" fund—which had originally been set up during the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), mostly through donations by governments and global health funders, but was later kept in place to respond to new health emergencies.[^cite_note-Junaidi1-2024-4] However, Nishtar said that GAVI and [UNICEF](19698110.md) still had to wait for official requests from the countries affected by the outbreak, as well as definitive approval of mpox vaccines from the World Health Organization, to start ordering the doses and distributing them to affected nations.[^cite_note-Junaidi1-2024-4] In response, the WHO announced the launch of a process that would enable access to vaccines for emergency use in countries whose national approval was still pending.[^cite_note-Johnston-2024-121]

On 14 August, the [U.S. Department of Health and Human Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Department_of_Health_and_Human_Services) (HHS) announced that the [national government](195149.md) would donate 50,000 doses of the MVA-BN/Jynneos vaccine to the DRC.[^cite_note-Johnston-2024-121][^cite_note-United-States-Department-of-Health-and-Human-Services-2024-124] The HHS also said that the U.S. had previously provided [US\$](18717338.md)17 million to "support clade I mpox preparedness and response efforts in [Central](539606.md) and [Eastern Africa](260987.md)".[^cite_note-United-States-Department-of-Health-and-Human-Services-2024-124]

On 16 August, Bavarian Nordic announced in a press statement that it had submitted clinical data to the [European Medicines Agency](1427054.md) to support the extension of the approval of MVA-BN/Jynneos to include adolescents aged 12 to 17 years;[^cite_note-Gilchrist-2024-122][^cite_note-Collins-2024-125] the company cited as evidence the early results of a trial—sponsored by the [NIH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health)'s [National Institutes of Allergy and Infectious Diseases](1013155.md) (NIAID)[^cite_note-Collins-2024-125][^cite_note-Sambado-2024-126]—in 315 adolescents included in the aforementioned age range and 211 adults aged 18 to 50 years,[^cite_note-ClinicalTrialsgov-2024-127] which showed that both the immune responses and the safety profile were similar between the two age groups after vaccination with two standard doses.[^cite_note-Collins-2024-125][^cite_note-Sambado-2024-126][^cite_note-ClinicalTrialsgov-2024-127] In the same statement, Bavarian Nordic also stated that a study of the vaccine in children aged 2 to 12 years would take place in the DRC and Uganda later in 2024.[^cite_note-Collins-2024-125]

Also on 16 August, [China](5405.md)'s [General Administration of Customs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Administration_of_Customs) declared that national authorities would deploy screening measures for people and goods entering the country from areas affected by the outbreak for the following six months.[^cite_note-Sambado-2024-126][^cite_note-128] By 17 August, Pakistan had started screening travelers at all airports and at border crossings with surrounding countries.[^cite_note-129]

On 17 August, the [Africa CDC](63909811.md) announced that they had pledged to distribute 10 million doses of the mpox vaccine by 2025 and that Bavarian Nordic had agreed to enhance vaccine manufacturing capabilities across Africa,[^cite_note-\:0-123][^cite_note-130] to increase local supplies and reduce production costs.[^cite_note-\:0-123] The association's Director General, [Jean Kaseya](77381020.md), later pleaded with foreign countries not to implement [travel bans](26647363.md) against African nations, as it had previously [happened](26647363.md#Pandemic) during the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-\:0-123]

On 19 August, the DRC's [Minister of Public Health](19686492.md), [Samuel Roger Kamba Mulamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Roger_Kamba_Mulamba), said in a press conference that the DRC needed about 3.5 million doses of mpox vaccines, with roughly 215,000 doses set to be donated by [Belgium](3343.md) and up to three million doses being donated by [Japan](15573.md), and further donations being expected by the United States.[^cite_note-RFI-2024-76][^cite_note-\:1-77]

On 21 August, [Germany](11867.md)'s [Federal Ministry of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Ministry_of_Health_\(Germany\)) said that the [federal government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Germany) had previously accumulated 117,000 doses of the Jynneos vaccine, which had then been transferred to the [Bundeswehr](288188.md);[^cite_note-131][^cite_note-\:4-132] on 26 August, the German government publicly announced that it would donate 100,000 vaccines to the DRC and [Burundi](21490998.md),[^cite_note-\:12-133] as well as other neighbouring African countries.[^cite_note-\:12-133][^cite_note-\:13-134] The government's spokesman, [Steffen Hebestreit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steffen_Hebestreit?action=edit\&redlink=1), also said that Germany would lend financial support both to the WHO and partners in Africa, in the latter case through the GAVI Vaccine Alliance,[^cite_note-\:13-134][^cite_note-\:14-135] while also collaborating with the [African Union](10634933.md) to enhance local vaccine production.[^cite_note-\:14-135]

On 28 August, the Africa CDC said that they had received roughly 10% of the estimated \$245 million-fund they had requested to tackle the outbreak across the continent.[^cite_note-\:17-136][^cite_note-137]

On 4 September, [Indonesia](14579.md)'s [Ministry of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_\(Indonesia\)) (Kemenkes) started implementing a preventive vaccination program, using the MVA-BN/Jynneos vaccine, which would prioritized regions that had reported Mpox cases, due to limited availability.[^cite_note-138]

On 5 September, a study published in the journal *[Cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_Journal)* showed that a new [mRNA-based](63136688.md) mpox vaccine, developed by [Moderna](39743243.md), reportedly offered better protection in non-human primates than Bavarian Nordic's Jynneos vaccine.[^cite_note-139][^cite_note-140]

### Global access to mpox vaccines

On 23 August 2024, the [World Health Organization](33583.md) announced that partners like [GAVI](38346167.md) and [UNICEF](19698110.md) could start purchasing mpox vaccines before they received official approval from the agency, to expedite vaccine distribution across the African countries affected by the outbreak.[^cite_note-\:4-132] The WHO, which had already invited vaccine manufacturers to submit an Expression of Interest for Emergency Use Listing (EUL) of their products on 9 August,[^cite_note-141] was expected to grant an emergency license to the MVA-BN/Jynneos and LC16 vaccines in September 2024.[^cite_note-\:4-132][^cite_note-\:8-142] Writing for *[The New York Times](30680.md)*, journalist [Stephanie Nolen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephanie_Nolen) noted that the delayed approval of the mpox vaccines by the WHO—which she deemed as a "[Byzantine process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_process)"—would mainly affect the low-income and middle-income countries depending on the agency for the [pre-qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-qualification) process for vaccines and treatments. At the same time, the U.S. [FDA](11632.md) and the [European Union](9317.md)'s [EMA](1427054.md) had already issued emergency licenses for the same vaccines during the previous outbreak in 2022 and 2023.[^cite_note-\:8-142]

On 24 August, the United States was set to donate 10,000 mpox vaccines to Nigeria the following week; the [U.S. Agency for International Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Agency_for_International_Development) (USAID) said that it would also donate a further 50,000 doses to the Democratic Republic of the Congo, without specifying the arrival date, since Bavarian Nordic and the Congolese government were still discussing pre-shipment requirements for storage and handling of the vaccines.[^cite_note-\:9-143] [Helen Rees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Rees), a member of the [Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention](63909811.md) (Africa CDC)'s emergency committee for [mpox](242702.md), stated that the delay in granting Africa access to vaccines was "really outrageous," as the continent had already experienced similar struggles during the [COVID-19 vaccination programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_vaccination_in_Africa).[^cite_note-\:9-143][^cite_note-144]

On 27 August, Nigeria's National Primary Health Care Development Agency (NPHCDA) announced that the country had received 10,000 doses of the MVA-BN/Jynneos vaccine as a donation from the US,[^cite_note-145][^cite_note-\:15-146] making it the first African country to receive a batch since the start of the outbreak.[^cite_note-\:15-146] On the same day, [Spain](26667.md)'s [Ministry of Health](33528060.md) (MISAN) announced that they would donate 20% of their national supplies of mpox vaccines, roughly corresponding to 100,000 [vials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vial) and 500,000 doses, to several countries in Central Africa;[^cite_note-\:18-147][^cite_note-148] the MISAN also urged the [European Commission](9974.md) to extend the same proposal to every member country of the EU, stating that "it makes no sense to accumulate vaccines \[in countries] where the problem doesn't exist, and this is the time to prove it."[^cite_note-\:18-147]

On 5 September, the DRC's Ministry of Public Health, Samuel Roger Kamba Mulamba, announced that the country had received their first batch of mpox vaccines, consisting in 99,000 doses of MVA-BN/Jynneos, and that a further delivery on 7 September would take the total amount to 200,000 doses; both batches were donated by the EU's [Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_Emergency_Preparedness_and_Response_Authority) (HERA).[^cite_note-\:22-149][^cite_note-\:23-150] Vaccination programs subsequently were set to start in the state's most affected provinces in October and be handled by [UNICEF](19698110.md),[^cite_note-\:23-150] as the doses were distributed between [Kinshasa](45773.md), [Lubumbashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubumbashi) and [Goma](1210483.md).[^cite_note-\:24-151] HERA's director, [Laurent Muschel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurent_Muschel?action=edit\&redlink=1), said that the EU aimed to deliver at least 566,000 doses to the most affected countries in the region.[^cite_note-\:22-149] Africa CDC's Director General, Jean Kaseya, said that the association would distribute a further 3,600,000 doses to [Gabon](12027.md), [Burundi](21490998.md), [Central African Republic](5478.md) and [Ivory Coast](57744.md) in the following weeks; he also stated that vaccine manufacturing would start in Africa from 2025, with an estimated 85-90% cost reduction.[^cite_note-\:24-151]

On 13 September, the [WHO](33583.md) has granted [prequalification](https://en.m.wiktionary.org/wiki/prequalify) status to the [MVA-BN vaccine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modified_vaccinia_Ankara), manufactured by [Bavarian Nordic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Nordic), as the first vaccine approved for use against mpox. This vaccine, originally developed for use against [smallpox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallpox), had previously only been available under an emergency use licence.[^cite_note-152] Its use is aimed at controlling the spread of the disease in African countries severely affected by recent outbreaks.[^cite_note-153] This approval, known as prequalification, comes as a new strain of the virus spreads from the [Democratic Republic of Congo](76762.md) to neighboring nations.[^cite_note-154] The prequalification helps developing countries access the vaccine, as they often lack the resources to conduct thorough safety and efficacy tests. [U.N. agencies](216034.md) also depend on this process before purchasing medical products.[^cite_note-155] Bavarian Nordic announced it could supply 13 million doses of its mpox vaccine by the end of 2025, with the potential to increase supply by an additional 50 million doses in 12–18 months, pending demand and regulatory approvals.[^cite_note-156] The company is exploring capacity expansion as the WHO declared mpox a global health emergency, but vaccine shortages have hindered containment efforts.[^cite_note-157]

On 25 September, [Bavarian Nordic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Nordic) received a \$63 million order from the U.S. government to produce additional bulk product and 1 million freeze-dried doses of its mpox and smallpox vaccine, [Jynneos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jynneos), to be delivered by 2026.[^cite_note-158] The bulk product will replenish inventory used during the 2022 mpox outbreak. The order comes after the [World Health Organization](33583.md) declared mpox a global public health emergency for the second time due to an outbreak in the [Democratic Republic of Congo](76762.md).
