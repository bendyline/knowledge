The **Canada women's national flag football team** represents [Canada](5042916.md) in women's international [flag football](224471.md) matches. The sport is governed by [Football Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_Canada). In 2024, the [International Federation of American Football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_of_American_Football) (IFAF) ranked the Canadian women's team 6th worldwide.[^cite_note-1] In 2025 they rose to 4th.[^cite_note-2]

Francois Bougie was the team's first head coach.[^cite_note-2017_coaches-3] He served in 2008, 2010, 2014, 2016, 2020, and 2023.[^cite_note-2017_coaches-3][^cite_note-2020_team-4][^cite_note-2023_IFAF_Americas-5] Rachel Lessard was named head coach in 2025.[^cite_note-Lessard-6]

## Championships

The Canadian women's team has received four medals at the [IFAF Flag Football World Championship](43253761.md). They won gold in 2010 and 2014, silver in 2008, and bronze in 2018. At the 2008 game they lost to [Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_women's_national_flag_football_team) in the finals.[^cite_note-results-7] In 2010, the team defeated the [US](77202200.md) 31-18 to win gold.[^cite_note-2010_gold-8] They won gold in the 2014 World Championship with a winning score of 32-21 against the US.[^cite_note-9] During the 2018 Championship in Panama City, the women took a bronze by defeating Mexico 19-14.[^cite_note-2018_championships-10] In [2025 World Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_football_at_the_2025_World_Games) the team took a bronze after defeating [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria_women's_national_flag_football_team) in the score of 38-20.[^cite_note-2024_IFAF-11]

[IFAF Americas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFAF_Americas) organizes flag football continental championships every two years to fall in between the world championships. The women's team won a bronze medal at the 2023 [IFAF Americas Continental Flag Football Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFAF_Americas_Continental_Flag_Football_Championship) by defeating [Panama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panama_women's_national_flag_football_team).[^cite_note-2023_IFAF_Americas-5] They won 20-16 in overtime.[^cite_note-12]
