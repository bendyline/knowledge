# List of diplomatic missions of Nigeria

Not to be confused with [List of diplomatic missions of Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_missions_of_Niger).



Map of Nigerian diplomatic missions

This is a **list of diplomatic missions of Nigeria**. [Nigeria](21383.md) is the most populous [African country](5334607.md) and maintains a large network of diplomatic missions. The country has significant influence in Africa and in various multilateral fora, including the [Organisation of Islamic Cooperation](47725.md), [OPEC](166346.md), the [Commonwealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations), and the [African Union](10634933.md).

Nigeria first started sending twelve diplomatic personnel overseas to serve in British missions from 1957. Upon independence three years later, the country created its own foreign ministry, headed by Foreign Minister [Jaja Wachukwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaja_Wachukwu). Now the Nigerian foreign ministry has over 2,000 officers.
