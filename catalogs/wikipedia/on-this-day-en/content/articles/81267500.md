The **8th Summit of the European Political Community** was held on 4 May 2026 in [Yerevan](34352.md), [Armenia](10918072.md). The [European Political Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Political_Community) is an intergovernmental forum that facilitates political and strategic dialogue about the future of [Europe](9239.md). It holds biannual [summits](3271361.md).

## Background

[Armenia](10918072.md) was announced as the host of the 8th summit of the [European Political Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Political_Community) on 23 May 2025, following the [7th European Political Community Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7th_European_Political_Community_Summit) held on 2 October 2025 in [Copenhagen](5166.md), [Denmark](76972.md).[^cite_note-2] The [Prime Minister of the Republic of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_the_Republic_of_Armenia), [Nikol Pashinyan](17897961.md) announced that the summit would be held on Monday, 4 May 2026, in [Yerevan](34352.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

The 8th EPC summit was expected to be the largest international political event hosted by [Armenia](10918072.md) since its independence, with 48 heads of state and government from member states and partner countries expected to attend, together with invited delegates. Alongside the 8th EPC summit, three other high‑level events were planned on 4 and 5 May 2026, including the inaugural [EU-Armenia bilateral summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_EU-Armenia_Summit?action=edit\&redlink=1), the state visit of the [President of France](24899.md), [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md), and the [Yerevan Dialogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan_Dialogue?action=edit\&redlink=1) international forum.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

At the first ever [EU-Armenia summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_EU-Armenia_Summit?action=edit\&redlink=1), the President of the [European Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Council), [António Costa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant%C3%B3nio_Costa), together with the President of the [European Commission](9974.md), [Ursula von der Leyen](2991932.md), will represent the EU, and the Prime Minister [Nikol Pashinyan](17897961.md) will represent [Armenia](10918072.md).[^cite_note-smt-8]

The event comes at an important moment for Armenia, given the Joint Declaration reached between Armenia and [Azerbaijan](746.md) on 8 August 2025 in the presence of the [President of the United States](24113.md), [Donald Trump](4848272.md), which has been further welcomed by the [EU](9317.md),[^cite_note-smt-8] their joint support for [Armenia](10918072.md)'s Crossroads of Peace initiative[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-jppc-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] and the "Trump Route for International Peace and Prosperity" (TRIPP) connectivity project in the territory of [Armenia](10918072.md),[^cite_note-uspd-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] as well as the alignment with two EPC strategic topics, including advancing connectivity and addressing evolving security dynamics and regional challenges.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-uspd-14][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-jppc-11]

A [state visit](327283.md) by the [President of France](24899.md), [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md), will take place shortly after the solemn opening ceremony of the new building of the Embassy of Armenia in France, held in [Paris](22989.md) on 28 April 2026 with the participation of [Armenia](10918072.md)'s Minister of Foreign Affairs, [Ararat Mirzoyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ararat_Mirzoyan), and [France](5843419.md)'s Minister for Europe and Foreign Affairs, [Jean-Noël Barrot](54388162.md).[^cite_note-primeminister_2026-04-30-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] As part of the state visit, Macron, accompanied by the [Prime Minister of Armenia](483914.md), will formalize a strategic partnership,[^cite_note-23] visit [Tsitsernakaberd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsitsernakaberd) and the [Matenadaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matenadaran), meet with the [President of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Armenia), [Vahagn Khachaturyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vahagn_Khachaturyan), travel to [Gyumri](1529357.md) to honour the memory of the victims of the [1988 earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Armenian_earthquake), and conclude the visit with the concert titled "Musical Bridge: Armenia-France" in [Gyumri](1529357.md).[^cite_note-primeminister_2026-04-30-19]

[EU](9317.md) relations with [Armenia](10918072.md) are based on the [EU-Armenia Comprehensive and Enhanced Partnership Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU-Armenia_Comprehensive_and_Enhanced_Partnership_Agreement) (CEPA), effective since 2021, and the Strategic Agenda for the EU-Armenia Partnership,[^cite_note-24] adopted in 2025.[^cite_note-smt-8]

## Aims

The 8th EPC summit is to focus on four strategic topics:[^cite_note-epc-1][^cite_note-25]

- Strengthening democratic resilience

- Advancing connectivity

- Reinforcing economic and energy security

- Addressing evolving security dynamics and regional challenges

## Schedule and agenda

The summit was scheduled to take place on Monday 04 May 2026 at the [Karen Demirchyan Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Demirchyan_Complex) in [Yerevan](34352.md), [Armenia](10918072.md). An official statement issued by the [Police of the Republic of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_of_the_Republic_of_Armenia) on 01 May 2026, operating under the [Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Internal_Affairs_of_the_Republic_of_Armenia), announced that security and traffic‑control measures would be in place from 4 to 5 May 2026 for the 8th EPC summit, the 1st [EU-Armenia summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_EU-Armenia_Summit?action=edit\&redlink=1), the state visit of the [President of France](24899.md), [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md), and the [Yerevan Dialogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan_Dialogue?action=edit\&redlink=1) international forum, considered unprecedented since Armenia's independence owing to the scale of the anticipated high‑level attendance.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]

## Attendees

### Participating member states, partner countries and invited delegates



Countries participating in the European Political Community



Family photo of the 8th European Political Community Summit

The summit was attended by the heads of state and government of the member states and partner countries participating in the European Political Community, together with the [President of the European Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_European_Council), the [President of the European Commission](24468.md), the [President of the European Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_European_Parliament) and the [High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy](750942.md). [Mark Carney](13571049.md), the [Prime Minister of Canada](24135.md), was the first representative to attend from a non-European country.[^cite_note-epc-1][^cite_note-28] Among the invited delegates, the [Secretary General of NATO](22194433.md), [Mark Rutte](4321056.md), and the [Secretary General of the Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_General_of_the_Council_of_Europe), [Alain Berset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Berset), will travel to [Yerevan](34352.md) to attend the 8th EPC summit and are expected to hold multiple bilateral meetings with leaders present at the EPC, including with the [Prime Minister of the Republic of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_the_Republic_of_Armenia), [Nikol Pashinyan](17897961.md)[^cite_note-natomr-29][^cite_note-ceab-30] [Azerbaijani](746.md) president [Ilham Aliyev](22765442.md) did not attend the summit in person but gave remarks via video link.[^cite_note-31]
