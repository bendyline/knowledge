**Maarakeh** ([Levantine Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levantine_Arabic_language): معركة, romanized: *Maʿrakeh*), or **Maarakah**, is a municipality in [Tyre District](11166788.md), in [Lebanon](17771.md).

## Geography

The Municipality of **Maarakeh** is located in the Kaza of [Tyre](30796.md) (sour) one of Mohafazah of [South Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Lebanon) kazas (districts). Mohafazah of South Lebanon is one of the eight mohafazats (governorates) of [Lebanon](17771.md). It is 90 kilometers (55.9 mi) away from Beyrouth ([Beirut](37428.md)) the capital of Lebanon. Its elevation is 270 meters (1) (886 ft - 295 yd) above sea level. Maarakeh surface stretches for 2000 hectares (20 km² - 7.7 mi²)(2).

## Etymology

[E. H. Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Henry_Palmer) wrote that the name Mảrakeh meant "The battle field".[^cite_note-1]

## History

In 1596, it was named as a village, *al-Ma'raka*, in the [Ottoman](22278.md) *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya)* (subdistrict) of [Tibnin](6787186.md) under the *[liwa'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liwa_\(Arabic\))* (district) of [Safad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safad), with a population of 119 households and 3 bachelors, all Muslim. The villagers paid a fixed tax rate of 25% on agricultural products, such as [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), olive trees, fruit trees, cotton, goats and beehives, in addition to occasional revenues; a total of 13,350 [akçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e).[^cite_note-Hutteroth-2][^cite_note-3]

In 1875 [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) found that it had 700 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh) inhabitants.[^cite_note-4] He further noted: "Here are uprights and [lintels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lintel) of door with cut stones, apparently ancient; and in a small [mosque](19894.md), built of regular blocks probably taken from an old church, are several fragments of monolithic [columns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Column).'"[^cite_note-5]

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described it as: "A village, built of stone, containing about 400 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh), on flat top of high ground, surrounded by gardens, olives, figs, palms, and arable land. There is a spring and five [cisterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cistern)."[^cite_note-6]

On 28 June 1984 the Israeli army surrounded the town and began house to house searches. 119 men, aged between 13 and 60, were taken away as prisoners for further interrogation. At the time it was estimated that Maarakah had a population of 13,000.[^cite_note-7]

On 4 March 1985, during the [Israeli occupation of Southern Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_occupation_of_Southern_Lebanon_\(1982%E2%80%932000\)), an [explosion destroyed a mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maarakeh_bombing) killing local resistance leader, Mohammed Saad.[^cite_note-8] At least 15 people were killed in the explosion.[^cite_note-Hijazi-9]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.71% of registered voters in Maarakah. 99.17% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-10]
