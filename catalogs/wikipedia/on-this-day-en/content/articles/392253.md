This article lists the [presidents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)) of [Guinea](12176.md), since the country gained independence from [France](5843419.md) in 1958 (after rejecting to join the [French Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Community) in a [constitutional referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958_Guinean_constitutional_referendum)).

## Term limits

As of 2021, there is a two-term limit for the president in the [Constitution of Guinea](52164987.md). This limit has been lifted for Conté in 2001 and for Condé in 2020.[^cite_note-termlimits-3]
