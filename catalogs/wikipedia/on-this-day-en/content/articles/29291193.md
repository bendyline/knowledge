The **Borgu Local Government Area** is an administrative region in [Niger State](1723431.md), [Nigeria](21383.md), It is one of the 25 [Local Government Areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) (LGAs) in the state, with the headquarter situated in the town of [New Bussa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Bussa). The postal code is 913.[^cite_note-NIPOST-2] The Borgu LGA was formerly part of [Kwara State](2227645.md), but on 27 August 1991 it was transferred to Niger State.[^cite_note-OnlineNigeria-3]

The LGA has the same extent as the [Borgu Emirate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borgu_Emirate), a [Nigerian traditional state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_traditional_state).[^cite_note-Jones-4] It contains part of the [Borgu Game Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borgu_Game_Reserve), a section of the [Kainji National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kainji_National_Park).[^cite_note-UNEP-5]

In the 1991 census Borgu LGA had a total population of 110,000, with a mixed ethnic population as follows:[^cite_note-Jones-4]

- [Fulani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulani_people) 30,000

- [Bokobaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Busa_language_\(Mande\)) 25,000

- [Busa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bissa_people) 15,000

- [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people) 10,000

- [kambari (Acipu)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kambari_languages) 8,000

- [Yoruba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoruba_people) 7,000

- [Laru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laru_language) 5,000

- [Hun-Saare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hun-Saare) 3,000

- [Nupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_people) 2,000

- [Lopa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lopa_language) 2,000

- [Igbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igbo_people) 1,000

- Others 2,000

## Climate/Geography

The dry and wet seasons are the two main ones in Borgu Local Government Area. Borgu Local Government Area has an average [humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humidity) of 38% and an average [wind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind) speed of 13 km/h (8.1 mph). The region's average [temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature) is 30 [degrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degree_\(temperature\)) [Celsius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celsius) (86 degrees [Fahrenheit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahrenheit)).
