**Denisse Azucena Miralles** (born 8 November 1976) is a Peruvian engineer, economist and politician who served as the [Prime Minister of Peru](1900295.md) for 21 days in 2026. She previously served as the [Minister of Economy and Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economy_and_Finance_\(Peru\)) between 2025 and 2026.

## Education

Miralles first went to the [National University of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Engineering). She then went to the [Yokohama National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yokohama_National_University) in [Yokohama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yokohama) where she received a [master's degree](170346.md).[^cite_note-1] Afterwards, she studied [infrastructure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrastructure) at the [Harvard Kennedy School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Kennedy_School).[^cite_note-2] She also went to the [University of the Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Pacific_\(Peru\)), and worked at the [Development Bank of Latin America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Development_Bank_of_Latin_America).[^cite_note-3]

## Career

Miralles was appointed the Minister of Economy and Finance in October 2025 under former President [José Jerí](80709159.md).[^cite_note-4] She was sworn in the same day.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

Miralles was appointed the Prime Minister in February 2026. She was picked over presidential candidate [Hernando de Soto](285909.md).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] She resigned on 17 March 2026 before a confirmatory vote could be taken in [Congress](1571007.md).[^cite_note-10]
