**Bohodukhiv** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Богодухів [\[boɦoˈduxiu̯\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Ukrainian) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/d/d5/LL-Q8798\_%28ukr%29-Gzhegozh-%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%B4%D1%83%D1%85%D1%96%D0%B2.wav/LL-Q8798\_%28ukr%29-Gzhegozh-%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%B4%D1%83%D1%85%D1%96%D0%B2.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/d/d5/LL-Q8798_%28ukr%29-Gzhegozh-%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%B4%D1%83%D1%85%D1%96%D0%B2.wav/LL-Q8798_%28ukr%29-Gzhegozh-%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%B4%D1%83%D1%85%D1%96%D0%B2.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>; [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Богодухов, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Russian): *Bogodukhov*) is a [city](4077166.md) in [Kharkiv Oblast](494901.md), eastern [Ukraine](31750.md). It is the [administrative centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_centre) of [Bohodukhiv Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohodukhiv_Raion). Bohodukhiv hosts the administration of [Bohodukhiv urban hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohodukhiv_urban_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), one of the [hromadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hromada) of Ukraine.[^cite_note-admreform_2020_bohodukhiv-1] Current population: 14,624 (2022 estimate).[^cite_note-ua2022estimate-2]

## History

A defensive fortification against the [Tatars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatars) existed on the location during the 16th century.[^cite_note-ency-3] The settlement was founded in 1662 as a small [sloboda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloboda_\(settlement\)) and *Bogodukhov [ostrog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostrog_\(fortress\))* by [Ukrainian Cossacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Cossacks) under command of [otaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ataman) Tymofiy Krysa.[^cite_note-autogenerated1-4][^cite_note-autogenerated2-5][^cite_note-autogenerated3-6][^cite_note-ency-3] It has been a town since 1681.[^cite_note-autogenerated3-6] In 1709, at the time of the [Russo-Swedish War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Northern_War), it was taken by [Menshikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Danilovich_Menshikov) and tsarevich [Alexius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexei_Petrovich,_Tsarevich_of_Russia).[^cite_note-EB1911-7]

After April 1780 it was the administrative centre of [Bogodukhov uyezd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogodukhov_uyezd) in [Kharkov Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkov_Governorate) of the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire). A cathedral was built in 1793.[^cite_note-EB1911-7]



A school in the early 20th century

In December 1918 the city was a site of battles between the [Zaporozhian Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_People's_Army) and [Bolsheviks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolsheviks).[^cite_note-ency-3] During the [Ukrainian War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_War_of_Independence), from 1917 to 1920, it passed between various factions. Afterwards it was administratively part of the [Kharkiv Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv_Governorate) of Ukraine.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Soviet times, saw the development of local industry and education.[^cite_note-autogenerated1-4][^cite_note-autogenerated2-5] In 1932 the city's population reached 16,000.[^cite_note-ency-3] A local newspaper has been published in Bohodukhiv since February 1930.[^cite_note-10]

During [World War II](32927.md), the city was occupied by the [German Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht) from 16 October 1941 to 17 February 1943 and again from 11 March to 7 August 1943. It was liberated by 1st Tank Army.[^cite_note-11] In 1944, [a dairy factory](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%B4%D1%83%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9%20%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B4) was built in Bohodukhiv.[^cite_note-autogenerated2-5] In 1950 the city had three secondary schools, five seven-year schools, two primary schools and a vocational school.[^cite_note-autogenerated1-4] In 1965, a [medical college](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%B4%D1%83%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9%20%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%B4%D0%B8%D1%86%D0%B8%D0%BD%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9%20%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%B4%D0%B6) was opened.[^cite_note-autogenerated2-5]

The main branch of the economy was the food industry.[^cite_note-autogenerated3-6]

## Geography

Bohodukhiv stands on the river Merlo, a tributary of [Vorskla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vorskla). Geologically, its territory is part of the [Central Upland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Russian_Upland).[^cite_note-ency-3]
