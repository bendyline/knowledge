The **Winter Egg** is a [Fabergé egg](327475.md), one of a series of fifty-two jewelled [Easter eggs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easter_egg) created by Russian jeweler [Peter Carl Fabergé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Carl_Faberg%C3%A9). It was an Easter 1913 gift for [Tsarina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsarina) [Maria Feodorovna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Feodorovna_\(Dagmar_of_Denmark\)) from [Tsar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsar) [Nicholas II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_II), who had a standing order of two Easter eggs every year, one for his mother and one for his wife. It was designed by [Alma Pihl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alma_Pihl).

The price in 1913 was 24,700 rubles, the most expensive Easter egg ever made. The egg left Russia after the [Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Revolution), and ended up in the collection of Brian Ledbrooke, Esq. It was first sold at [auction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auction) in 1994 at [Christie's](155688.md) in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva) for \$5.6 million,[^cite_note-1] the world record at that time for a Fabergé item sold at auction.[^cite_note-2] The egg sold for US\$9.6 million in an auction at [Christie's](155688.md) in [New York City](645042.md) in 2002.[^cite_note-3] The buyer was a Qatari Prince, [Saud bin Muhammed Al Thani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saud_bin_Muhammed_Al_Thani), the country's Minister of Culture from 1997 to 2005. As part of the Classics Week series of auctions by Christie's, the egg was sold at auction on 2 December 2025, for £22.9 million.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] It was bought in the saleroom by Kieran McCarthy of Wartski, who was accompanied by his dog Drummer. Wartski, the London Fabergé dealers, first acquired the Egg from [Soviet Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Russia) in the 1930s.

## Design

The exterior of the egg resembles [snowflakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowflake) and frost ice crystals formed on clear glass, set on a base of rock crystal shaped to resemble melting ice.[^cite_note-6] It is studded with 1,660 [diamonds](8082.md), and is made from [quartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartz), [platinum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platinum), and [orthoclase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoclase). The miniature surprise flower basket is studded with 1,378 diamonds and is made from platinum and [gold](12240.md), while the [wood anemones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anemone_nemorosa) flowers are made of white quartz and the leaves are made of [demantoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demantoid). The flowers lie in gold moss. The egg is 102 millimetres (4.0 in) high.
