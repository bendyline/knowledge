**Ágnes Forsthoffer**[^cite_note-1] (born 24 March 1980) is a Hungarian politician and businesswoman who has served as [Speaker of the National Assembly](19626723.md) since 2026.

Before becoming speaker and acting president, Forsthoffer worked in finance and management. Entering politics in 2024, she joined the [Tisza Party](76606756.md) and became its vice president in 2025. After her election as speaker of the National Assembly, she briefly served *ex officio* as acting [president of Hungary](380393.md) following [Tamás Sulyok](76167806.md)'s constitutional removal from office.

## Early life and education

Forsthoffer was born on 24 March 1980 in [Balatonfüred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balatonf%C3%BCred) to parents Ferenc Forsthoffer and Julianna Gyöngyi Varró. Her father was the former director of the Balatonfüred shipyard and the waste management company Talajerőgazdálkodási Kft.[^cite_note-2] Forsthoffer was raised in Balatonfüred and completed her secondary education at Lovassy László Gimnázium in [Veszprém](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veszpr%C3%A9m). In 1998, she was crowned [Belle of the Anna-ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belle_of_the_Anna-ball).[^cite_note-\:1-3]

After completing secondary school, Forsthoffer relocated to [Budapest](36787.md) to enroll in the [Budapest University of Economics and Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest_University_of_Economics_and_Business). She received a bachelor's degree in finance and accounting in 2002, and afterwards received a master's degree in marketing from the [University of Pécs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_P%C3%A9cs) in 2004.[^cite_note-\:0-4]

## Business career

In 2001, Forsthoffer worked as an audit assistant at [Deloitte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deloitte) for ten months, before becoming a financial director at Talajerőgazdálkodási Kft. in [Pápa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A1pa) in 2002.[^cite_note-\:2-5] She became the managing director of Talajerőgazdálkodási Kft. in 2012, where her mother and brother are also employed as managers of the company.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

In 2006, Forsthoffer's family took over ownership of the Hotel Margaréta in Balatonfüred and its management company, and Forsthoffer has served as a director at both establishments since then.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] In 2023, she became the Balatonfüred regional director of the Hungarian Hotels and Restaurants Association (MSZÉSZ).[^cite_note-\:1-3]

## Political career

Forsthoffer first entered politics in 2024, when she ran as an [independent](2172378.md) in the [2024 Hungarian local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Hungarian_local_elections) for Balatonfüred's 8th constituency. However, she was not elected.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

In September 2025, Forsthoffer was elected as the third vice president of the [Tisza Party](76606756.md) during the party's general assembly.[^cite_note-\:0-4] As vice president, she serves alongside additional vice presidents [Márk Radnai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1rk_Radnai) and [Zoltán Tarr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zolt%C3%A1n_Tarr).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] She stood as a candidate for the Tisza Party in the [2026 parliamentary election](79337461.md), and was elected to the [National Assembly](585416.md) from [Veszprém County's 2nd constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veszpr%C3%A9m_County_2nd_constituency).[^cite_note-16] Following the election, incoming [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Hungary) [Péter Magyar](76453946.md) announced that he would nominate Forsthoffer to serve as [speaker of the National Assembly](19626723.md) once its membership was sworn in.[^cite_note-17] She was sworn in on 9 May ([Europe Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe_Day)); in her first decree, she reinstated the [flag of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Europe) on the [Hungarian Parliament Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Parliament_Building).[^cite_note-18]

Following the constitutional removal of [Tamás Sulyok](76167806.md), Forsthoffer became acting [President of Hungary](380393.md) on 20 July 2026 until the inauguration of his successor [András Baka](83909330.md) on 19 August.[^cite_note-19]
