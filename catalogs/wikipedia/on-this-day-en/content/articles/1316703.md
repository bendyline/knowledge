The **minister of foreign affairs of the Russian Federation** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Министры иностранных дел России, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Russian): *Ministry inostrannykh del Rossii*) is a high-ranking [Russian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_government) official who heads the [ministry of foreign affairs of the Russian Federation](4432213.md). The foreign minister is one of the five presidential ministers, along with the ministers of [defence](30730416.md), [interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Internal_Affairs_\(Russia\)), [emergencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Emergency_Situations_\(Russia\)) and [justice](35320735.md). Although they are members of the [Cabinet](9787373.md#Current_Cabinet), they are directly subordinate to the [President](239646.md).

The foreign minister, like other presidential ministers, is nominated and appointed by the President after consultation with the [Federation Council](1268578.md) (whereas non-presidential ministers are nominated by the [Prime Minister](339344.md) and appointed by the President after approval by the [State Duma](1010187.md)). The foreign minister is also a permanent member of the [Russian Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Security_Council).[^cite_note-1]
