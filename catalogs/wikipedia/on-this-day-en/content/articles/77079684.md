The **2025 NASCAR Cup Series** was the 77th season of [NASCAR](21114.md) professional stock car racing in the United States and the 54th season of the modern-era [Cup Series](56571945.md). [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) of [Team Penske](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_operations_of_Team_Penske) entered the season as the defending [2024 NASCAR Cup Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_NASCAR_Cup_Series) champion.[^cite_note-1] The season started with the [Cook Out Clash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cook_Out_Clash) on February 2, and concluded at [Phoenix Raceway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Raceway) on November 2, where [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won the championship after finishing third at the [NASCAR Cup Series Championship Race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Cup_Series_Championship_Race).

The 2025 season was the first year under NASCAR's new TV deal that will last until 2031. This was also the first year since 2008 that [Stewart–Haas Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stewart%E2%80%93Haas_Racing) (now [Haas Factory Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_Factory_Team)) did not have the ownership of [Tony Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stewart). [Katherine Legge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Legge) made her Cup Series debut as well, being the first woman since [Danica Patrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danica_Patrick) in 2018 to make a Cup Series start. NASCAR held its first race outside the United States in 67 years with the [2025 Viva México 250](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Viva_M%C3%A9xico_250).[^cite_note-auto-2]

It was the last season to utilize the elimination-style [NASCAR playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_playoffs), having served as the Cup Series' championship format since [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_NASCAR_Cup_Series). Also, this would unexpectedly be the final full-time season for two-time champion [Kyle Busch](1467805.md), as he would die from sepsis caused by pneumonia on May 21, 2026.&#x20;

## Teams and drivers

### New charter agreement

The 2025 season is the first under the new [charter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_Team_Alliance) agreement, which limits teams to a maximum of three charters. [Hendrick Motorsports](2089777.md) and [Joe Gibbs Racing](2410190.md), which have four charters each, are grandfathered in the new agreement.[^cite_note-3]

### Team changes

**New teams/rebrands**

- [Haas Factory Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_Factory_Team) was formed by [Gene Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene_Haas) after retaining the sole remaining charter from [Stewart–Haas Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stewart%E2%80%93Haas_Racing). The team runs the No. 41 car, driven by [Cole Custer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Custer).

  [^cite_note-HFT-39]

- JTG Daugherty Racing rebranded itself into [Hyak Motorsports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyak_Motorsports), following the departure of co-owners Jodi and Tad Geschickter. [Brad Daugherty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Daugherty_\(basketball\)) retains his stake in the team's ownership, alongside Gordon Smith, Mark Hughes, and [Ernie Cope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernie_Cope).

  [^cite_note-Hyak-13]

- [JR Motorsports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JR_Motorsports) debuted in the Cup Series at the [2025 Daytona 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Daytona_500) with the No. 40 car driven by [Justin Allgaier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Allgaier). According to the team, no further races are planned.

  [^cite_note-JRM-91]

- [Tricon Garage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tricon_Garage) fielded a car in the series for the first time in the 2025 Daytona 500. [Martin Truex Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Truex_Jr.) drove the No. 56.

  [^cite_note-TruexTricon-146]

## Rule changes

### Pre-season

*Sources:*[^cite_note-OEP-188][^cite_note-189][^cite_note-190]

- A new "Open Exemption Provisional" rule has been introduced for the NASCAR Cup Series, allowing world-class drivers to compete in a race. These drivers will not earn race points, playoff points, prize money, or tiebreaker advantages based on their finishing position. However, they will retain the win, including the trophy and All-Star eligibility. The second-place finisher will receive first-place points but will not gain playoff points or playoff eligibility.

- Vehicles on the Damaged Vehicle Policy (DVP) may drive to the garage or be towed there if necessary. A repair clock of 7 minutes (8 minutes for Atlanta) is activated for vehicles undergoing repairs on pit road. Once the clock expires, the vehicle must move to the garage to continue repairs but will not be disqualified from the race. If a vehicle cannot reach pit road due to damage or flat tires, it will be towed directly to the garage. Additionally, if a car leaves its pit box and the repair clock expires before it crosses the pit-out yellow line, a penalty will be assessed.

- If a playoff waiver is granted for missing a race (exceptions to the forfeit of playoff points include medical reasons—driver medical, birth of a child, family emergency, etc.—and age restrictions), the driver will forfeit all current and future playoff points (earned prior to the playoffs) and will start the playoffs with a maximum of 2,000 points.

- Rules violations by an OEM may result in a loss of manufacturer points, and/or loss of wind tunnel hours, and/or loss of RCFD runs. Penalties will be assessed for violations of the vehicle testing policy, wind tunnel policy, event roster, and code of conduct policy.

- Suspensions resulting from technical penalties may be deferred, without appeal, to the next race following the penalty. All other suspensions take effect immediately.

- An additional bonus point for the fastest lap in a points paying race event has been introduced. This does not apply to Daytona Duels.

  [^cite_note-191]

### In-season

- The OEP was adjusted: if there are more than 40 cars, the field will automatically expand to 41, and the team requesting the provisional may use it regardless of qualifying position.

  [^cite_note-192]

- If a vehicle is serviced in another team's pit stall, it will receive a penalty based on the reason for service: a flag status penalty (restart at the tail of the field or pass-through) for safety issues, or a potential lap(s) penalty for competition adjustments.

  [^cite_note-193]

- Just before the Brickyard 400, NASCAR introduced a new rule: it may, at its sole discretion, limit the number of race entries to 40. In such cases, open teams will be selected based on the team owner points standings.

  [^cite_note-40carlimit-194]

  - Four open teams will be eligible for starting grid positions based on the fastest single lap speed posted to each open team's vehicle in the first qualifying round.

    [^cite_note-40carlimit-194]

  - Up to six open teams will be eligible for starting grid positions based on the highest ranked team owner points standings.

    [^cite_note-40carlimit-194]

### New media rights deal

NASCAR started the first year of a 7 year TV deal, lasting until 2031. In this deal [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_on_Fox) will broadcast twelve races (including the Clash, Daytona Duels, and All-Star Race), while [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_on_NBC) will broadcast fourteen races. [Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_on_Prime_Video) and [TNT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_on_TNT_Sports) will broadcast five races each.[^cite_note-210] In addition, the season marked the debut of NASCAR's [In-Season Challenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_In-Season_Challenge), which was won by [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs).[^cite_note-IST-203]

## Season summary

### Preseason

**Exhibition: [Cook Out Clash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cook_Out_Clash)**

[Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) secured the pole position for the 200-lap race featuring a 23-car field with notable drivers such as [Austin Dillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Dillon), [Zane Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Smith), and [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) failing to qualify. Elliott dominated the race, leading 171 laps, winning the race and earning his first career [Clash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cook_Out_Clash) win.[^cite_note-211]

**Exhibition: [The Duel at Daytona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Duel_at_Daytona)**

In the first duel, [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) clinched victory in the 60-lap race, which featured a 23-car field. Notably, drivers such as [Zane Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Smith) and [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) failed to finish the race. Wallace led 21 laps, securing the win and earning 10 points.[^cite_note-212]

In the second duel, [Austin Cindric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Cindric) started from the pole position in a 23-car field. The race concluded dramatically when a [caution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Yellow_flag) was called just before the finish line, leading [NASCAR](21114.md) to declare Cindric the winner over [Erik Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Jones), as he was ahead at the time of the caution.[^cite_note-213]

### Regular season

**Round 1: [Daytona 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Daytona_500)**

[William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) overtook pole-sitter [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) early before the race was [red flagged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_flag_\(racing\)) on lap 11 for a weather delay in the area. After nearly 4 hours under the [red flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Red_flag), involving 2 red flags, the race promptly resumed. [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) won the first stage, but had engine issues that forced him to the back of the pack. [Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain) got turned and collected [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen), [Martin Truex Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Truex_Jr.), [Josh Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Berry), and [Hélio Castroneves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%A9lio_Castroneves) while [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) edged out [Austin Cindric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Cindric) and won the second stage. [Ricky Stenhouse Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Stenhouse_Jr.) made contact with Logano and collected [Kyle Busch](1467805.md), [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott), [Noah Gragson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Gragson), and [Todd Gilliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Gilliland). With 5 laps to go, as the field entered the backstretch, [Cole Custer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Custer) turned [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) into the wall, resulting in [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) flipping mid-air, resulting in [overtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green%E2%80%93white%E2%80%93checkered_finish). On the final lap, Custer made contact with [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) and caused a huge wreck as Byron snuck his way through and held off [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) and [Jimmie Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmie_Johnson) to win his second consecutive Daytona 500, becoming the fifth driver in history to do so. This was the 10th Daytona 500 win for [Hendrick Motorsports](2089777.md), surpassing [Petty Enterprises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petty_Enterprises) for the most all-time by a race team.[^cite_note-214]

**Round 2: [Ambetter Health 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Ambetter_Health_400)**

[Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) scored the pole for the race. [Josh Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Berry) won the first stage and [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won the second stage, his first stage win on a drafting track. On lap 150, [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott), [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski) and [Corey LaJoie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corey_LaJoie) were in a wreck, sending both Keselowski and LaJoie out. On lap 184, a wreck with [Ricky Stenhouse Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Stenhouse_Jr.), [Noah Gragson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Gragson), [Daniel Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Su%C3%A1rez), [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs), [Cody Ware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Ware), [Cole Custer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Custer), and [J. J. Yeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._Yeley) occurred sending them all out of the race. With 4 laps to go, as the field entered the backstretch, Larson hit [Austin Cindric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Cindric) and [William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) resulting in [overtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green%E2%80%93white%E2%80%93checkered_finish). On the final lap, Berry, [Justin Haley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Haley_\(racing_driver\)) and [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) collided on the backstretch with caution being deployed just when [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) snuck his way through [Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar) and Larson to win his first [Atlanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Motor_Speedway) race.[^cite_note-215]

**Round 3: [EchoPark Automotive Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_EchoPark_Automotive_Grand_Prix)**

[Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) scored the pole for the race. [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) won the first stage, his first on a road course and after [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) pitted, [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) won the second stage, his first stage win since the [2023 Martinsville spring race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_NOCO_400). [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) ended up winning the race, after an intense battle between [Kyle Busch](1467805.md), [William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)), and Reddick.[^cite_note-216] 18-year-old [Connor Zilisch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Zilisch) made his Cup Series debut with [Trackhouse Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackhouse_Racing), but was involved in a wreck with teammate [Daniel Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Su%C3%A1rez).[^cite_note-217]

**Round 4: [Shriners Children's 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Shriners_Children's_500)**

[William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) won the pole. Byron won the first stage and [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won the second stage. On lap 99, [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski), [Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar), [Riley Herbst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riley_Herbst), [Justin Haley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Haley_\(racing_driver\)), [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe), [Austin Dillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Dillon), and [Cole Custer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Custer), and [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) were involved in a huge pile-up, sending them all out. Bell would end up getting his third consecutive win, becoming the first to do so since [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_NASCAR_Cup_Series), and the first since the debut of the [Next Gen car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Next_Gen_\(NASCAR\)).[^cite_note-218]

**Round 5: [Pennzoil 400 presented by Jiffy Lube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Pennzoil_400)**

[Michael McDowell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_McDowell_\(racing_driver\)) won the pole, marking the first pole won by [Spire Motorsports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spire_Motorsports). [Austin Cindric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Cindric) won the first stage and [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won the second stage. [Josh Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Berry) won the race, after battling [Daniel Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Su%C3%A1rez) to earn his first career win in the Cup Series and the 101st career win for the [Wood Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood_Brothers_Racing).[^cite_note-219]

**Round 6: [Straight Talk Wireless 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Straight_Talk_Wireless_400)**

[Alex Bowman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Bowman) won the pole. [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the first stage and [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the second stage. On Lap 207, after leading 124 laps, Blaney's engine blew up, ending his day early. After overtaking teammate Alex Bowman with less than 10 to go, [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) claimed his 30th career win. In an attempt to perform a weekend sweep, Larson ended up getting a win in the [Truck Series race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Baptist_Health_200) 2 days earlier, however came up just short of a victory during the [Xfinity Series race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Hard_Rock_Bet_300).[^cite_note-220]

**Round 7: [Cook Out 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cook_Out_400_\(Martinsville\))**

[Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won the pole. [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) won the first stage and [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the second stage. Hamlin would end up leading 274 laps en route to his first win of 2025.[^cite_note-221]

**Round 8: [Goodyear 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Goodyear_400)**

[William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) won the pole. Byron dominated early, leading the opening 243 laps and sweeping the stages. It was the most laps Byron has ever led in a single race. After a late-race caution with 4 laps to go, [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) took the lead after pit stops. On the ensuing green-white-checkered restart, he held off Byron and [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) to win for the second straight week.[^cite_note-222]

**Round 9: [Food City 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Food_City_500)**

[Alex Bowman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Bowman) won the pole. [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) swept the stages, and led 411 laps en route to his [second consecutive win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Bass_Pro_Shops_Night_Race) at [Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Motor_Speedway). Larson attempted to sweep the weekend again, winning the [Xfinity Series race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_SciAps_300) the previous day, but came up just short with a 2nd place in the [Truck Series race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Weather_Guard_Truck_Race).[^cite_note-223]

**Round 10: [Jack Link's 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Jack_Link's_500)**

[Zane Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Smith) won his first career pole. On lap 43, [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski), who made contact with [Kyle Busch](1467805.md), would end up collecting former teammate [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) after attempting to pit, ending his and Blaney's day early. [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won the first stage and [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) won the second stage. [Austin Cindric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Cindric) would win the race, edging out [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) by 0.022 seconds for the [29th closest finish in NASCAR history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_the_closest_NASCAR_Cup_Series_finishes).[^cite_note-224] Preece and [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) were disqualified after post race inspection.[^cite_note-225]

**Round 11: [Würth 400 presented by LIQUI MOLY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_W%C3%BCrth_400)**

[Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar) won his first career pole. [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md)'s car caught on fire, ending stage one early, with [Austin Cindric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Cindric) winning the stage. [Chris Buescher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Buescher) caused a debris caution ending stage two early, with [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) winning the stage. After a caution filled event, [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) won the race in overtime.[^cite_note-226]

**Round 12: [AdventHealth 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_AdventHealth_400)**

[Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won the pole. Larson dominated the race, leading 221 laps, sweeping the stages, and setting the fastest lap. Larson ended up winning the race and winning his second consecutive Kansas spring race, also winning the [2024 AdventHealth 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_AdventHealth_400).[^cite_note-227]

**Exhibition: [NASCAR All-Star Race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NASCAR_All-Star_Race)**

[Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won the pole for the Open, while [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski) and [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won their heat races on Saturday, earning the pole and second-place starting positions for the main event, respectively.[^cite_note-228] [Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar) won the Open race, with [John Hunter Nemechek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hunter_Nemechek) finishing second, and [Noah Gragson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Gragson) advancing as the fan vote winner. All three transferred into Sunday night's main event.[^cite_note-229]

In the early laps, [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski) and [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) battled for position, with [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) eventually taking the lead and controlling much of the race. When the "Promoter's Caution" came out on lap 216, the field split on pit strategy. Five drivers, including race leader Logano, stayed out on older tires. On the restart, Bell who had taken two fresh tires and restarted sixth, used the tire advantage to pass the five drivers that stayed out and secure his first All-Star Race victory.[^cite_note-230]

**Round 13: [Coca-Cola 600](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Coca-Cola_600)**

[Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won the pole. Duels sparked within the stages late, however [William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) swept all three stages, the first time a driver swept all three stages since the [2021 race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Coca-Cola_600). Byron led the race late, however, [Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain) passed Byron with 6 laps to go and won his first career [Coca-Cola 600](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coca-Cola_600), coming from 40th place.[^cite_note-231]

**Round 14: [Cracker Barrel 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cracker_Barrel_400)**

[Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won the pole.[^cite_note-232] [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the first stage. [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the second stage. Blaney dominated the last stage and won the race, earning his first victory of the season.[^cite_note-233]

**Round 15: [FireKeepers Casino 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_FireKeepers_Casino_400)**

[Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won his third consecutive pole. [Chris Buescher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Buescher) won the first stage. On lap 67, [Alex Bowman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Bowman) hit the outside wall hard, sending out the red flag, and ending his day early. [William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) won the second stage. After an intense battle between [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md), [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) and Byron, involving clear air and fuel mileage, Hamlin took the lead with 4 laps to go and won the race, days before his third child was born,[^cite_note-Hamlin_Missing_a_race-60] and his first victory at Michigan since [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_NASCAR_Sprint_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-234]

**Round 16: [Viva México 250](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Viva_M%C3%A9xico_250)**

In the first international race for NASCAR since 1958, [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won the pole. After the race started, a caution quickly flew for rain, NASCAR then allowed teams to use wet weather tires. [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) won the first stage, and van Gisbergen won the second stage. van Gisbergen dominated the race, leading 60 laps, and won, getting his first win since the [2023 Grant Park 220](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Grant_Park_220).[^cite_note-235]

**Round 17: [The Great American Getaway 400 presented by VISITPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_The_Great_American_Getaway_400)**

[Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole. Hamlin led every lap of stage one, and [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won the second stage. Briscoe held off a speedy Hamlin to get his first win of the season, as well as his first win with [Joe Gibbs Racing](2410190.md) and [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota).[^cite_note-236]

**Round 18: [Quaker State 400 available at Walmart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Quaker_State_400)**

[Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) won the pole, with Ford sweeping the top eight spots. [Austin Cindric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Cindric) won the first stage, and [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) won the second stage by .001 seconds over [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott). A large wreck on Lap 69 damaged 22 cars, taking out several leaders. Elliott won the race on a last lap pass of [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski), ending his 44-race winless streak.[^cite_note-237]

**Round 19: [Grant Park 165](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Grant_Park_165)**

[Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won the pole. On lap 2, [Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar) hit the inside barrier of turn 9, causing an early red flag, as well as collecting many cars. [Michael McDowell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_McDowell_\(racing_driver\)) won the first stage, giving [Spire Motorsports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spire_Motorsports) their first stage win, and [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the second stage. [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won the race, continuing his success at the Street Race, also winning [the previous day's Xfinity race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_The_Loop_110) and sweeping the weekend. van Gisbergen also made history, becoming the [winningest non-American driver in the Cup Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_foreign-born_NASCAR_race_winners#NASCAR_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-238]

**Round 20: [Toyota/Save Mart 350](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Toyota/Save_Mart_350)**

[Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won his second consecutive pole. [Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain) won stage one, and van Gisbergen won stage 2. Van Gisbergen once again dominated on a road course, winning the race and going back to back.[^cite_note-239]

**Round 21: [Autotrader EchoPark Automotive 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Autotrader_EchoPark_Automotive_400)**

[Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) won the pole after both practice and qualifying were canceled. Elliott won the first stage and [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won the second stage. Late in the final stage, rain caused a red flag for about an hour. [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) held off teammate [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) for his [second consecutive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_W%C3%BCrth_400) win at Dover.[^cite_note-240]

**Round 22: [Brickyard 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Brickyard_400)**

[Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won the pole, with [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota) sweeping the first five spots. Briscoe made history for becoming the first Indiana native to win the pole for the race, as well as becoming the first driver to win poles for the Daytona 500, Coca-Cola 600, and Brickyard 400 in the same season.[^cite_note-241] Briscoe won the first stage, and [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the second stage. After rain halted the race for around 30 minutes, [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) had enough fuel to hold off [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) to get his first [Crown Jewel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Jewel_\(NASCAR\)) win, becoming the first African-American to win a major race at [Indianapolis Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_Motor_Speedway). He also snapped a 100-race winless streak dating back to the [2022 Hollywood Casino 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Hollywood_Casino_400).[^cite_note-242]

**Round 23: [Iowa Corn 350 powered by Ethanol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Iowa_Corn_350)**

[Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won his second consecutive pole. [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski) swept the stages, the first time he swept both stages in a race since the [2017 First Data 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_First_Data_500). After many cautions and a long green flag run, [William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) won the race after saving enough fuel.[^cite_note-243]

**Round 24: [Go Bowling at The Glen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Go_Bowling_at_The_Glen)**

[Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the pole. Prior to this race, [Connor Zilisch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Zilisch) was supposed to race. However, due to his injury sustained [the previous day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Mission_200_at_The_Glen), [Trackhouse Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackhouse_Racing) announced that they would withdraw the No. 87 from the race.[^cite_note-Srigley-109] Defending race winner [Chris Buescher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Buescher) won the first stage and Blaney won the second stage. [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won the race, surpassing [Tony Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stewart) and [Jimmie Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmie_Johnson) for most wins in a season by a rookie with 4 wins.[^cite_note-244]

**Round 25: [Cook Out 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cook_Out_400_\(Richmond\))**

[Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) won the pole. [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) won the first stage, and [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) won the second stage. On Lap 198, an 11-car wreck in turns 3 and 4 took [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) and [Justin Haley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Haley_\(racing_driver\)) out of the race early. During the final stage, [Austin Dillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Dillon) and [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) traded the lead several times. Dillon pulled away after the final pit stop and won the race, making his first trip to the Playoffs since [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NASCAR_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-245]

**Round 26: [Coke Zero Sugar 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Coke_Zero_Sugar_400)**

[Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the pole after qualifying was canceled. [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won the first stage, and [Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain) won the second stage. Within the final laps, playoff upsets such as [Erik Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Jones), [Daniel Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Su%C3%A1rez), [Justin Haley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Haley_\(racing_driver\)), and [Cole Custer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Custer) were all in the front during the final laps. However, Blaney overtook Custer on the backstretch of the final lap, giving him his second win of the season and second win at Daytona as [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) and [Alex Bowman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Bowman) clinched the final two playoff spots.[^cite_note-246]

### Playoffs

**Round 27: [Cook Out Southern 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cook_Out_Southern_500)**

[Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole. On Lap 1, [Josh Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Berry) slid his car into [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) and caused a multi-car wreck. [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) swept the stages, the first time he accomplished said feat in his career. Briscoe dominated the race, leading 309 laps. A hard-charging Reddick came close to passing him off the last turn of the last lap, but Briscoe managed to hold on, defending his win from last year and becoming the first back-to-back Southern 500 winner since [Greg Biffle](1637067.md) in 2005 and 2006.[^cite_note-247]

**Round 28: [Enjoy Illinois 300 presented by TicketSmarter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Enjoy_Illinois_300)**

[Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole. [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won the first stage, and [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) won the second stage. Hamlin dominated the last half of the day, leading 75 laps and winning the race. This was the 200th win for [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota) in the NASCAR Cup Series.[^cite_note-248]

**Round 29: [Bass Pro Shops Night Race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Bass_Pro_Shops_Night_Race)**

[A. J. Allmendinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._J._Allmendinger) won the pole, his first pole since the [2015 Cheez-It 355 at The Glen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Cheez-It_355_at_The_Glen). [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the first stage after a side-by-side finish with [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs). Gibbs won the second stage. During the third stage, [Goodyear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodyear_Tire_and_Rubber_Company) allowed teams an extra set for the race, as the race had been plagued with tire issues. After an intense final stage, with drivers playing playoff spoiler, such as [Zane Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Smith), [Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar), and [Corey Heim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corey_Heim), [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won the race as [Alex Bowman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Bowman), [Austin Dillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Dillon), [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen), and [Josh Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Berry) were eliminated from the playoffs.[^cite_note-249]

**Round 30: [Mobil 1 301](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Mobil_1_301)**

[Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) won the pole. [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the first stage, and Logano won the second stage. Around lap 110, [Joe Gibbs Racing](2410190.md) teammates [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) and [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) were battling their JGR teammate [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs); on lap 111, Hamlin got impatient with Gibbs and spun him.[^cite_note-250] Blaney won the race.[^cite_note-251]

**Round 31: [Hollywood Casino 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Hollywood_Casino_400)**

[Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won the pole. [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) swept the stages. On lap 268, [Zane Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Smith) flipped his car, also causing overtime. [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) was in contention to win, but on the last lap, he and Hamlin made contact, allowing [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) to slip by and steal the win, claiming his first multi-win season since [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NASCAR_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-252]

**Round 32: [Bank of America Roval 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Bank_of_America_Roval_400)**

[Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) won the pole. [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won the first stage, and [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the second stage. During stage 3, [Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain) and [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) fought over the last remaining spot in the Round of 8. Logano had the tiebreaker and advanced once they both crossed the finish line. Van Gisbergen dominated the race, earning his fifth consecutive win on a road course.[^cite_note-253]

**Round 33: [South Point 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_South_Point_400)**

[Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole. [William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) won the first stage, and [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won the second stage. Hamlin passed [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) with 4 laps to go in the race and earned his 60th career win, tying him with [Kevin Harvick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Harvick) for 10th all-time. Hamlin clinched his first Championship 4 appearance since [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_NASCAR_Cup_Series) and his first Championship 4 appearance in the Next-Gen car.[^cite_note-254]

**Round 34: [YellaWood 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_YellaWood_500)**

[Michael McDowell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_McDowell_\(racing_driver\)) won the pole. [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) wrecked out early in a multi-car crash, while [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) won stage 1, and [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won stage 2. [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) lead at the white flag but ran out of fuel down the backstretch. [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won the race, and clinched his first Championship 4 berth, and first with [Joe Gibbs Racing](2410190.md).[^cite_note-255]

**Round 35: [Xfinity 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Xfinity_500)**

[William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) won the pole and swept the stages. Byron dominated the race, [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) put up a fight after a late race caution, but could not prevail, as Byron won. Both he and [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) made the Championship 4.[^cite_note-256]

**Round 36: [NASCAR Cup Series Championship Race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NASCAR_Cup_Series_Championship_Race)**

[Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole. [William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) won the first stage, and Hamlin won the second stage. Hamlin dominated majority of the race, coming as close as 3 laps to go before becoming a Champion. However, a late race caution caused by Byron cutting a tire and hitting the wall diminished his lead. Restarting in overtime [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) took advantage by taking 2 tires on the ensuing pit stop while Hamlin took 4, prompting Larson to gain track position over him. [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) passed [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski) and held off both him and Larson to get his first win at [Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Raceway), while Larson won his 2nd Championship, and first since [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_NASCAR_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-257]
