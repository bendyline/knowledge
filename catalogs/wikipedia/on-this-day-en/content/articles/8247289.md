**Shalamcheh** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): شَلَمچه) is a town located in [Khuzestan province](448079.md), [Iran](14653.md). It is situated on the border with [Iraq](7515928.md), north-west of [Abadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abadan). The town was one of the main sites of invasion of [Saddam Hussein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddam_Hussein)'s Iraq in the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War). Some 50,000 Iranians died in the fighting around the town, and there is today a war memorial in their memory.[^cite_note-Molavi2010-1] The [Basra–Shalamcheh rail line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basra%E2%80%93Shalamcheh_rail_line), one of the two railway projects connecting Iran to Iraq, is through Shalamcheh.[^cite_note-2]

## History

### 2026 Iran massacres

During the [2025–2026 Iranian protests](81976180.md), [Iran International](58555280.md) reported that on 2 January 2026, Iraqi militias affiliated with the Iranian government recruited forces to assist Iranian security forces in [suppressing protests](82036668.md) in Iran.[^cite_note-Iran_International-3] The troops were reportedly transported through border crossings including Shalamcheh.[^cite_note-Iran_International-3]
