**Kohat District** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): کوهاټ ولسوالۍ, [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضلع کوہاٹ) is a [district](639402.md) of the [Kohat Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohat_Division) in the [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) province of [Pakistan](23235.md). The city of [Kohat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohat) is the capital of the district.

## History

From the early sixteenth century, the history of Kohat revolves around three major tribes, namely [Bangash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangash), [Banoori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banoori), and [Afridi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afridi). These people seemingly established their settlements within the district during the 14th and 15th centuries. From 16th to 18th centuries, Kohat, being a part of the [Mughal Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mughal_Empire), was administered by the chiefs of the two aforementioned tribes.

Kohat district was annexed by the [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) from its former [Sikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikhs) [rulers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikh_Empire) after the [Second Anglo-Sikh War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Anglo-Sikh_War) of 1848–1849.[^cite_note-3]

As of the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), Kohat district has 169,679 households and a population of 1,234,661. The district has a sex ratio of 104.05 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 58.55%: 76.38% for males and 40.28% for females. 334,178 (27.07% of the surveyed population) are under 10 years of age. 278,741 (22.58%) live in urban areas.[^cite_note-2023_census-1]

At the time of the 2023 census, 85.90% of the population spoke [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto), 12.43% [Hindko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindko), and 0.83% [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu) as their first language.[^cite_note-2023language-5]
