The **battle of Re'im** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): קרב רעים; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): معركة رعيم) took place on [October 7](74998317.md), 2023, when [Hamas attacked Israel](74998317.md),[^cite_note-Reuters-5] initiating the [Gaza war](74998519.md). At 10\:00 am, less than five hours after [Hamas](13913.md) attacked, fighting was reported outside of the Re'im Army Base, which is headquarters for the [Gaza Division](6405376.md).[^cite_note-Reuters-5][^cite_note-6]

The base was reportedly the location of IDF drone and surveillance operations. Hamas reportedly posted a video of dead Israeli soldiers it had killed at the base.[^cite_note-7] Later in the day, [Israeli Defense Forces](39237.md) (IDF) regained control of the military base.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-TOI-1]
