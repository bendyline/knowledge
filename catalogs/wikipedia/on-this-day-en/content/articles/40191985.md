**International Conference on the Great Lakes Region (ICGLR)**, French: *Conférence Internationale sur la Région des Grands Lacs (CIRGL)*, is an [intergovernmental organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intergovernmental_organization) of African countries in the [African Great Lakes region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Great_Lakes_region).[^cite_note-1]

## Membership

The organisation consists of the following members:

-  [Angola](701.md)

-  [Burundi](21490998.md)

-  [Central African Republic](5478.md)

-  [Republic of the Congo](19599929.md)

-  [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md)

-  [Kenya](188171.md)

-  [Rwanda](25645.md)

-  [Sudan](27421.md)

-  [South Sudan](32350676.md)

-  [Tanzania](30118.md)

-  [Uganda](31816.md)

-  [Zambia](34415.md)

### Co-opted members

-  [Botswana](3464.md)

-  [Egypt](8087628.md)

-  [Ethiopia](187749.md)

-  [Malawi](19088.md)

-  [Mozambique](19301.md)

-  [Namibia](21292.md)

-  [Zimbabwe](34399.md)
