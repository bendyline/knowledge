**Al Jawf** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الجوف *_Al-Jawf_*) is a [governorate](690277.md) of [Yemen](350939.md). Its capital is [Al Hazm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Hazm,_Yemen).

As of April 2020, after the [Houthi](17240332.md) forces' [2020 offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_al-Jawf_offensive), nearly all the governorate is under Houthi control, except for [Khabb wa ash Sha'af](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khabb_wa_ash_Sha'af_district) which is under the control of [Al-Qaeda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qaeda_in_the_Arabian_Peninsula).[^cite_note-2]

On 15 July 2020, a Saudi Arabian airstrike in [Al Hazm district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Hazm_district) in Al-Jawf Governorate killed seven Yemeni civilians.[^cite_note-3] On 17 August 2020, a Houthi missile attack killed 11 [government troops](34252.md), including a senior officer.[^cite_note-4]
