**Manas** ([/məˈnɑːs/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English); Kyrgyz: [\[mɑnɑ́s\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Kyrgyz)), called **Jalal-Abad** until 2025, is the administrative and economic centre of [Jalal-Abad Region](1649680.md) in southwestern [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md). Its area is 88 square kilometres (34 sq mi),[^cite_note-census09-2] and its resident population was 123,239 in 2021.[^cite_note-pop21-1] It is situated at the north-eastern end of the [Fergana Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fergana_Valley) along the [Kögart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6gart) river valley, in the foothills of the Babash Ata mountains, near [the border with Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyzstan%E2%80%93Uzbekistan_border).

## Overview

Manas is known for its mineral springs in its surroundings, and the water from the nearby [Azreti-Ayup-Paygambar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azreti-Ayup-Paygambar?action=edit\&redlink=1) spa was long believed to cure lepers. Several [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) era [sanatoriums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanatorium) offer mineral water treatment programs for people with various chronic diseases. Bottled mineral water from the region is sold around the country and abroad.

## History



Jalal-Abad street scene

The Parliament of Kyrgyzstan approved a bill to officially rename the city of Jalal-Abad to Manas. The law was signed by the President on September 18, 2025, and will come into effect ten days after its official publication. The change is aimed at strengthening national ideology and honoring the national hero, Manas. One of [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md)'s main branches of the [Silk Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silk_Road) passed through Jalalabat and the region has played host to travelers for thousands of years, although few archaeological remains are visible today – except in some of the more remote parts of the region – such as [Saymaluu-Tash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saymaluu-Tash) and the [Chatkal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatkal) valley. These have included travelers, traders, tourists and pilgrims (to the various holy sights) and sick people visiting the curative spas such as in the [Ayub Tau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayub_Tau?action=edit\&redlink=1) mountain, at the altitude of 700 m above sea level some three kilometers out of town.

There is a legend that the water from the [Azreti-Ayup-Paygambar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_in_Islam) (the Prophet Job) spa cured lepers. According to the legend there was a grave, a mosque and the khan's palace near the spa.

The [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) suffix [-abad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/-abad) is often used in city names in [Persianate societies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persianate_societies) to refer to the person who founded the community. It is said that Jalalabat was named after [Jalal ad Din](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalal_ad_Din?action=edit\&redlink=1), who was renowned for setting up [Caravanserais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caravanserai) to serve travelers and many [pilgrims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilgrim) who came to the holy mountain.&#x20;



Hotel in Jalal-Abad

In early 19th century a small [Kokand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khanate_of_Kokand) fortress was built, and a small [kishlak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kishlak) grew up around this. The local people were engaged in agriculture and trade and provided services to the [pilgrims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilgrim) visiting the spas. Then, in the 1870s, Russian settlers came to the region. They set up a garrison town and military hospital.

In June 2026, Mayor Abdupaty Matkalikov was dismissed by President [Sadyr Japarov](65513403.md) after less than four months in office, with no official reason given.[^cite_note-3]

## Climate

Manas has a [hot, dry-summer continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate) (*Dsa*) according to the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification). There is more rainfall in winter than in summer. The average annual temperature in Jalal-Abad is 12.3 °C (54.1 °F). About 416 mm (16.38 in) of precipitation falls annually.

## Economy

The train line (a rarity in [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md)), runs from the [Ferghana Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferghana_Valley) northeast about 30 km to [Kökjanggak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6kjanggak).

Biggest Companies: Kyrgyz-Canada JV "Kyrgyz Petroleum Company", АО «Kelechek», АО «Nur». AOZT «KyrgyzChlopok», JV «Ak-Altyn» process cotton. There are the tobacco-curing companies «Tura-Ai» Ltd and «Aziz-Tabak» Ltd., that export 90% of their output to 17 countries. There are also mill houses АО «Azrat Ayib», AOZT PTK «Intershaq», «Mariam & Co» ltd, and the liquor manufacturer AOZT «Jalalabat Arak Zavodu».

## Tourism



Jalalabad's Theater

The [Jalal-Abad Region](1649680.md) is known for its walnuts and scenery. Local sites include the [Arslanbob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arslanbob) Waterfalls and [Sary-Chelek Nature Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sary-Chelek_Nature_Reserve) and Lake. In the city itself, sites include the town square and the culture park, which contains various sculptures and Jalalabad's theater.
