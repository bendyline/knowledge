**Ahmed Haitham al-Dalati** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): أحمد هيثم الدالاتي) is a Syrian politician, security official, and former military commander, having previously served as deputy commander-in-chief of [Ahrar al-Sham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahrar_al-Sham). He was appointed governor of [Quneitra Governorate](7445647.md) by [Syria's caretaker government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_caretaker_government) on 8 March 2025, following the [fall of the Assad regime](78548066.md), and served as commander of [Internal Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestic_security) in [Suwayda Governorate](3096933.md) from 25 May 2025 to 2 September 2025. He has served in the same role in [Rif Dimashq](4521946.md) since 2 September.

## Early life and education

Al-Dalati was born in 1985 in [Kfeir al-Zayt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kfeir_al-Zayt) in the [Wadi Barada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadi_Barada) region of [Rif Dimashq](4521946.md).[^cite_note-AlAraby-2] He completed elementary school in his hometown, then attended middle and high school in [Damascus](8914.md), and earned a [bachelor's degree in engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Engineering) from the Technical Institute of Computer Science in Damascus.[^cite_note-AlAraby-2][^cite_note-Thawra-3]

## Activity during the civil war

Al-Dalati joined the [Syrian opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_opposition_\(2011%E2%80%932024\)) in 2011 upon the outbreak of the [Syrian civil war](30741795.md). He joined [Jaysh al-Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaysh_al-Islam) early on in the war, but after moving to northern Syria in 2015, he joined [Ahrar al-Sham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahrar_al-Sham), contributing to establishing Ahrar al-Sham in southern governorates such as [Damascus](17429884.md) and its [countryside](4521946.md). In early 2016, he was appointed as the Southern Region Command Officer of Ahrar al-Sham, and later was appointed to the group's [Shura Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shura_Council).[^cite_note-Thawra-3][^cite_note-SyTV-2021-4]

He was appointed deputy commander-in-chief of Ahrar al-Sham on 16 June 2021, after [Amer al-Sheikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amer_al-Sheikh?action=edit\&redlink=1) had been appointed leader a year prior to end a leadership dispute between Jaber Ali Pasha and [Hassan Soufan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Soufan).[^cite_note-SyTV-2021-4][^cite_note-5]

According to *[Al-Araby Al-Jadeed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Araby_Al-Jadeed)*, he was "one of the most influential figures" in managing and planning military and political operations.[^cite_note-AlAraby-2] In late 2024, he played a "key role" during the [Deterrence of Aggression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deterrence_of_Aggression) offensive, such as participating in the [Battle of Aleppo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Aleppo_\(2024\)).[^cite_note-AlAraby-2][^cite_note-6]

## Post-Assad era

Following the [fall of the Assad regime](78548066.md), he was appointed governor of [Quneitra](7445647.md) on 8 March 2025, having previously served as deputy governor of Rif Dimashq Governorate to Amer al-Sheikh.[^cite_note-Thawra-3][^cite_note-AlAraby-2]

On 25 May 2025, he was appointed [Internal Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Security_Service_\(Syria\)) commander of [As-Suwayda Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As-Suwayda_Governorate), as part of a broader organizational restructuring by the [Syrian Ministry of Interior](34679004.md).[^cite_note-7]

*[Reuters](18998750.md)* reported on 27 May that, according to Syrian and Western sources, al-Dalati has led diplomatic contacts with [Israel](9282173.md), though he officially denied this to Syrian state news network *[Alikhbaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_News_Channel)*.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

On 2 September 2025, he was appointed commander of Internal Security in [Rif Dimashq Governorate](4521946.md), succeeding Hussam al-Tahhan.[^cite_note-10]
