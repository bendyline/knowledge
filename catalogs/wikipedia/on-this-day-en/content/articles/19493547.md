**Muridke** ([Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language) / [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): مریدکے), is a city and headquarters of **Muridke Tehsil** of [Sheikhupura District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikhupura_District) in Punjab, Pakistan. It is the [37th most populous city of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_populous_cities_in_Pakistan) as well 25th of the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province) [Punjab](24751.md). Muridke is a commercial area situated near the city of [Lahore](125315.md), at an elevation of 205 m (675 ft).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

## Geography

It is 215 meters above sea level, with the land around being largely flat.[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-gn1169692-3] The area is largely agricultural and is serviced by nearby canals.[^cite_note-nasalandcover-4][^cite_note-nasa-5]

## Temperature

The average temperature is 23 °C. The hottest month is June, at 39 °C, and the coldest is January, at 7 °C. The average rainfall is 955 millimetres (37 in) per year. The wettest month is September, at 289 millimetres (11 in) of rainfall, and the driest is November, at 11 millimetres (0.5 in).[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-nasarain-6][^cite_note-nasa-5]

The city's population increased nearly ninefold between 1972 and 2017, from 18,507 to 166,652. ### Markaz-e-Taiba

Muridke is known for being home to the Markaz-e-Taiba, the headquarters of [Markaz Dawa wal Irshad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markaz_Dawa_wal_Irshad) (MDI) or [Jamaat-ud-Dawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamaat-ud-Dawa) (JuD),[^cite_note-14] the parent organisation of the militant group [Lashkar-e-Taiba](649718.md) (LeT), located in the suburb of [Nangal Sahdan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nangal_Sahdan).[^cite_note-\:3-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-IISS15May-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-\:2-20] The Markaz is a large 200-acre complex that has a range of infrastructure established by [Hafiz Muhammad Saeed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafiz_Saeed) in 1990.[^cite_note-\:1-21][^cite_note-\:2-20][^cite_note-22] [Osama bin Laden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osama_bin_Laden), the former leader of [Al-Qaeda](1921.md), is believed to have contributed approximately PKR 10 million (roughly USD 100,000 at the time) towards the development of the complex.[^cite_note-23] The complex includes the Umm al-Qura Mosque, a [madrasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrasa), a religious preaching centre, residential quarters, a school, a hospital, a market, a garment factory, an iron factory, a woodwork factory, a stable, a swimming pool, a fishfarm, agricultural tracts and various administrative buildings.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-\:1-21][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] While it presents itself as a religious and educational institution, multiple international intelligence assessments and security reports have identified it as a hub for indoctrination, [militant training](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist_training_camp) and terrorist recruitment.[^cite_note-\:2-20] In 2009, following the [Mumbai attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Mumbai_attacks), the Punjab government took over the complex, and JuD continued to operate from there.[^cite_note-28] The federal and provincial governments in Pakistan continued to allocate funds to the organisation.[^cite_note-\:1-21]

The complex was targeted in an airstrike conducted by the [Indian Air Force](403355.md) as part of [Operation Sindoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Sindoor) on the night between on 7 May 2025.[^cite_note-BBC-29][^cite_note-AJ-30][^cite_note-31] The Government of Pakistan has said that the complex is a civilian facility run by the Government. [*The Diplomat*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Diplomat_\(magazine\)) magazine said that [Amir Hamza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amir_Hamza_\(Lashkar-e-Taiba\)), a co-founder of LeT, gave a Friday sermon at the Markaz in March, and a leader of [Pakistan Markazi Muslim League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Markazi_Muslim_League), an affiliate of JuD/LeT, was seen telling a gathering just a few weeks earlier that the "jihad" would continue.[^cite_note-\:3-15] Several high-profile attackers, including [Ajmal Kasab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajmal_Kasab) and [David Headley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Headley) underwent training at these facilities.[^cite_note-32]
