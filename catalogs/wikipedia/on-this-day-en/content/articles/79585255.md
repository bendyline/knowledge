The **Puntland Ministry of Security, Disarmament, Demobilization and Reintegration** MoSDDR ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Wasaaradda Amniga ee Dawladda Puntland*; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الأمن ونزع السلاح والتسريح وإعادة الإدماج في بونتلاند) is Ministry responsible for ensuring [security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security) and preventing [terrorist acts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist_acts) in the [Puntland regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Puntland). Several "[armed enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement)" units operate under its jurisdiction, including [dervish forces](35256005.md), [PSF](35647386.md), [PISA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puntland_Intelligence_Security_Agency), [PMPF](34324730.md) and [Puntland police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puntland_Police_Force) including [traffic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_police), [immigration](4599312.md), [border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_police), [security police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_police) and other armed police units. The current minister is [Ibrahim Artan Ismail 'Haji Bakiin'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Artan_Ismail).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-3]

## Establishment

After the collapse of the [Somali Democratic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_Democratic_Republic) in 1991 and the [establishment of Puntland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Puntland#Establishment_of_Puntland) on August 1, 1998, the Ministry of Interior and Security was formed. Later, the [Puntland government](43532267.md) split this ministry and created the Ministry of Security and DDR.

Following its inauguration, The ministry declared a policy of [demobilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demobilization), which involved disbanding armed civilian groups. [Disarmament](57979.md) focused on the physical removal of [weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapon), [ammunition](18940621.md) and [arsenals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsenal) from former combatants and faction leaders.&#x20;

The ministry also initiated a reintegration program aimed at restoring the loyalty of former militiamen affiliated with the collapsed [Siad Barre regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siad_Barre#Presidency) and various warlords. This process seeks to reintegrate them into civilian society, thereby reducing the number of individuals immediately available for armed conflict.[^cite_note-4]
