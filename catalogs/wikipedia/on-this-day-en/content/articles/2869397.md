**Laikipia County** is one of the 47 [counties of Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Kenya), located on the [equator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equator) in northern [Mount Kenya region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Kenya_region). Laikipia is a cosmopolitan county and is listed as county number 31. The county has two major urban centres: [Nanyuki](2889522.md) to the southeast, and [Nyahururu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyahururu) to the southwest. Its county government headquarters town is [Rumuruti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumuruti).[^cite_note-2]

The county lies between latitudes 0° 18" south and 0° 51" north and between longitudes 36° 11" and 37° 24' east. It borders [Samburu County](413704.md) to the north, [Isiolo County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isiolo_County) to the north east, [Meru County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meru_County) to the east, [Nyeri County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyeri_County) to the south east, [Nyandarua County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyandarua_County) to the south, [Nakuru County](2031081.md) to the south west and [Baringo County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baringo_County) to the west.[^cite_note-3]

Economic activities in the county consists mainly of [tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism) and [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture), chiefly grain crops, ranching and greenhouse horticulture.

The county encompasses the high, dry [Laikipia Plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laikipia_Plateau), and has a cool, temperate climate with both rainy and dry seasons.[^cite_note-4]

## Demographics

Laikipia County has a total population of 518,560, of whom 259,440 are males, 259,102 females and 18 intersex persons. There are 149,271 households with an average household size of 3.4 persons per household and a population density of 54 people per square kilometre.[^cite_note-5]

### Political leadership

[Joshua Irungu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joshua_Irungu) is the current governor, serving his second and final term as governor. He returned after governor [Ndiritu Muriithi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ndiritu_Muriithi) defeated him in the 2017 general elections.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] He is deputised by [Reuben Kamuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reuben_Kamuri), the current Deputy Governor, after Irungu did not run a second time with his previous deputy governor [Gitonga Kabugi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gitonga_Kabugi). John Kinyua Nderitu is the senator. He has been in office since 2017, replacing the first senator, [Godfrey Gitahi Kariuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godfrey_Gitahi_Kariuki).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] [Jane Kagiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Kagiri) was elected women representative for the county in 2022.

## Education

There are 600 ECD centres, 304 primary schools and 99 secondary schools. The county has also four teachers' training colleges, nine youth polytechnics, 94 adult training institutions and two universities.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

## Health

There are a total of 175 health facilities in the county, with three county referral hospitals, 10 sub-county hospitals, 21 health centers and 141 dispensaries. The county has 1024 health personnel of different cadre.[^cite_note-19] The HIV prevalence rate stands at 3.2 per cent.[^cite_note-20]

## Transport and communication

The county is covered by 1112.2 km of road network. Of this, 77.0 km is covered by earth surface, 705.5 km is [murram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murram) surface and 319.7 km is covered by [bitumen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitumen).[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

There are 19 post offices with 2,299 installed letter boxes, 2,079 rented letter boxes and 220 vacant letter boxes.[^cite_note-\:0-8]
