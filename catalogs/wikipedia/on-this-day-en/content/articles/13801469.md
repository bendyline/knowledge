# Provisional measure

A **provisional measure** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *medida provisória*) is a legal act in [Brazil](3383.md) through which the [President of Brazil](273638.md) can, "in important and urgent cases", enact [laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law) effective for a maximum of 60 days without approval by the [National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Congress_of_Brazil). Upon enacting one, the President is required to immediately submit it to Congress for approval or rejection. The provisional measure may be renewed once for an additional 60 days,[^cite_note-1] after which, it will cease to be in force unless Congress has approved it and made it law. There are two requirements for a provisional measure to be used: urgency and relevance of the matter to be regulated; Provisional measures may not affect:[^cite_note-2]

- political rights

- criminal law

- organization of the Judicial branch

- the budget

* private property

In addition, provisional measures may not affect that which is reserved for complementary law, or is already included in a bill submitted to the president by the National Congress and awaiting approval.
