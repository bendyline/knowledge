The **President of the United Republic of Tanzania** ([Swahili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swahili_language): *Rais wa Jamhuri ya Muungano wa Tanzania*) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [head of government](37570.md)[^cite_note-2] of [Tanzania](30118.md).

[Samia Suluhu Hassan](38577619.md), sworn in on 19 March 2021, is the first female president of the United Republic of Tanzania. She succeeded [John Magufuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Magufuli) following his [death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_state_funeral_of_John_Magufuli) on 17 March 2021.

## Presidential term

The president serves a term of five years. As of 2025, there is a two-term limit for the president in the [Constitution of Tanzania](29547627.md). The first president for whom the term limits applied was Mwinyi in 1995.[^cite_note-termlimits-3][^cite_note-4]

## Executive powers

The president of Tanzania is the commander-in-chief of the [Armed Forces](3495135.md) and is "accountable to a legislature composed of elected members and representative of the people."[^cite_note-5]

## List

Main article: [List of heads of state of Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_heads_of_state_of_Tanzania)

After its independence in 1961 as Tanganyika, the country was first led by [Richard Turnbull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Richard_Turnbull) as governor-general until [Julius Nyerere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Nyerere) became the first and only president under the 1962 constitution. The 1964 constitution after the merger of Tanganyika and Zanzibar has had 6 presidents with each serving multiple terms except Samia Suluhu Hassan. Julius Nyerere served 5 terms total from 1962–1985, having served 4 terms under the 1964 constitution. All presidents of Tanzania have been from the [Tanganyika African National Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanganyika_African_National_Union) party which later merged to become the [Chama Cha Mapinduzi](38207770.md) party.
