**Paveh** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) and [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language): پاوه)[^cite_note-4] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Paveh_County\)) of [Paveh County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paveh_County), [Kermanshah](448078.md) province, [Iran](14653.md), serving as capital of both the county and the district.[^cite_note-Kermanshah_Province_Structure-5] It is in a region called [Hawraman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawraman).

## History

An old myth regarding the name of the city is that the Emperor [Yazdgerd III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yazdgerd_III) sent his son named Pav to this area to renew his religious [Zoroastrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoroastrian) faith. Both [Persians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persians) and the local [Gorans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goran_\(Kurdish_tribe\)) practiced [Zoroastrianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoroastrianism) during the [Sasanian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasanian_Empire) from which this myth is derived.

## Demographics

### Language

The inhabitants of Paveh are [Kurds](17068.md) and speak [Hawrami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawrami) (also: [Gorani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorani_language)). They follow the [Shafi‘i](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shafi%E2%80%98i) branch of [Sunni Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam). The first language spoken by the population in the city is mostly [Gorani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorani_language) language, but the language that is used in schools and offices is [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language), the official language of [Iran](14653.md) in which almost everyone in the city is fluent.

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 19,774 in 5,171 households.[^cite_note-2006_Kermanshah_Province-6] The following census in 2011 counted 23,704 people in 6,723 households.[^cite_note-2011_Kermanshah_Province-7] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 25,771 people in 7,932 households.[^cite_note-2016_Kermanshah_Province-2]

## Geography

### Location and topography

Paveh is located in western [Iran](14653.md) and is 112 km far from [Kermanshah](1489244.md). It lies in a sub-region along the Iran-Iraq border commonly referred to as [Hewraman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewraman), which is situated within the larger geographical region of [Kurdistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdistan). The city is considered by inhabitants of the region as the capital of Hewraman.

### Climate

Paveh has a [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate) (*Csa*) according to the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification).

&#x20;\
&#x20;The town is encircled by fruit gardens. Paveh is situated along a mountainside like most cities and villages in the [Hewraman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewraman) region. Behind the city is the Shaho mountain and ahead of the city is a view of Atashgah, another mountain that was once the site of pilgrimages for ancient religions in the region. Among the most visited places is the Quri Qaleh caveman which is considered as the longest watery cave in the Middle East. The cave is located around 25 kilometers from Paveh's city center.

## Landmarks

### Rivers

[Sirwan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirwan_River) is one of the biggest rivers in Paveh. It rises near [Sanandaj](29953972.md), in the [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains) of Iran. It then descends through the mountains, where for some 32 km it forms the border between the two countries. It finally feeds into the Tigris below [Baghdad](4492.md). Navigation of the upper reaches of the Diyala is not possible because of its narrow [defiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defile_\(geography\)), but the river's valley provides an important trade route between Iran and Iraq.

### Mountains

Paveh is established among mountains on [Zagros Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros_Mountains). The mountain chain of [Shaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaho) is located in the west of Paveh. Shaho is the highest mountain in [Kermanshah](1489244.md) province with an elevation of 3390.[^cite_note-9]
