**CITIC Tower**, also known as **China Zun**,[^cite_note-3] is a [supertall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supertall) [skyscraper](29485.md) in the [Central Business District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_central_business_district) of [Beijing](18603746.md), China. The 109-story, 528-metre (1,732 ft) building constructed by China Construction Third Engineering Bureau is the [tallest in the city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tallest_buildings_in_Beijing), surpassing the [China World Trade Center Tower III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_World_Trade_Center_Tower_III) by 190 metres (620 ft)[^cite_note-Xinhua-4] on 18 August 2016.[^cite_note-ECNS-5] The tower structurally topped out on 9 July 2017,[^cite_note-6] fully topped out on 18 August 2017, and was completed in late 2018, making CITIC Tower the tallest completed building of 2018.[^cite_note-7] As of 2024, it is the tallest building with a rooftop helipad in the world.

## History

The nickname "China Zun" comes from the *[zun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zun)*, an ancient Chinese wine vessel which inspired the building design, according to the developers, the [CITIC Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CITIC_Group). The groundbreaking ceremony of the building took place in Beijing on 19 September 2011, and the constructors expected to finish the project within five years. CITIC Tower is [Northern China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_China)'s third-tallest building after [Goldin Finance 117](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldin_Finance_117) and [Chow Tai Fook Binhai Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianjin_CTF_Finance_Centre) in [Tianjin](50821.md).

[Farrells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farrells) produced the tower's land bid concept design, with [Kohn Pedersen Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohn_Pedersen_Fox) assuming the project and completing a 14-month-long concept design process after the client had won the bid.[^cite_note-8]

China Zun is a mixed-use building, featuring 60 floors of office space, 20 floors of luxury apartments and 20 floors of hotel with 300 rooms.[^cite_note-9] There will be a rooftop garden on the top floor at 522 m (1,713 ft) high.

The tower is likely to remain the tallest building in Beijing for the foreseeable future, as in 2018 authorities capped new projects in the central business district to a height of no more than 180 m (590 ft) in a bid to reduce congestion.[^cite_note-10]

### 2018 observatory controversy

In April 2018, Hong Kong newspaper *[Ming Pao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ming_Pao)* reported that China Zun's top three floors, Levels 106–107 and an observatory on level 108, was to be expropriated by the national-security apparatus, since the entire [Zhongnanhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongnanhai) complex, the headquarters of the [Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party) and the [State Council of the People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Council_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China), could be seen from the top of the skyscraper with the naked eye on a clear day. It is also said that with high-end [telescopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telescope) and other monitoring equipment, the day-to-day lives and activities of the Party and State Leaders could be seen. The building was ordered to be rectified for "fire safety issues" by the authorities, but the CITIC Group was unable to reveal the specific reason for rectification. It is also said the top three floors of the building will be administered by the National Security authorities after rectification.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

### 2026 plane crash

Main article: [2026 Beijing Sunward SA 60L Crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Beijing_Sunward_SA_60L_Crash)

On 26 June 2026, a [Sunward SA 60L Aurora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunward_SA_60L_Aurora) aircraft owned by a local general aviation company, registry B-12PP,[^cite_note-CNN-Elex-20260626-13] crashed into the upper floors of the building.[^cite_note-CNN-14][^cite_note-15] According to flight data from [Flightradar24](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flightradar24), it entered highly restricted Beijing airspace (5th Ring Road) and was nearing [Zhongnanhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongnanhai), the central leadership compound which is a complete no-fly zone, on "a severely deviated flight path" when the aircraft transponder stopped transmitting, and it made further turns toward the skyscraper.[^cite_note-CNN-14][^cite_note-NW-video-16][^cite_note-CNN-censorship-17] The pilot of the plane and only person on board—identified as 66-year-old [Beijing](18603746.md) resident Liu Junhua—died,[^cite_note-Reuters-18] and 13 people inside the building or on the ground were injured. The building only sustained minor damage.[^cite_note-NW-video-16][^cite_note-CNN-censorship-17]

Videos taken shortly after the crash show fires and smoke burning on a lower extended part of the building, likely ignited by falling debris, which were extinguished shortly after.[^cite_note-19] Bystanders were told by authorities not to film the scene, and news and videos posted to [Chinese social media platforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_social_media_platforms) were promptly [censored](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_censorship_in_China),[^cite_note-NW-video-16][^cite_note-CNN-censorship-17][^cite_note-20] with posts on apps like [Douyin](56822861.md), [Xiaohongshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaohongshu), [WeChat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WeChat) and [Weibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weibo) regarding the incident quickly being taken down.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] Due to the censorship, few Chinese are believed to have heard of the incident.[^cite_note-24]

Beijing's Chaoyang district government said Liu had deviated from his approved flight area and lost contact with [air traffic control](48563.md). It added that Liu had [insomnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insomnia) and [anxiety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anxiety) and the crash was caused by unspecified "personal reasons".[^cite_note-Reuters-18]

## Division of floors

The building has a total of seven office areas. Among them, Areas 1–3, Area 5 and Area 7 are occupied by China CITIC Bank, Alibaba and CITIC Group respectively, the main tenants of China Zun; the remaining office areas are leased to [Fortune Global 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortune_Global_500) enterprises and financial institutions, including [China Construction Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Construction_Bank).[^cite_note-25]
