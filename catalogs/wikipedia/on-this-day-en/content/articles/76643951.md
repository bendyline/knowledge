Presidential elections were held in [Ivory Coast](57744.md) on 25 October 2025.[^cite_note-1] Incumbent President [Alassane Ouattara](1173624.md) was re-elected with 91% of the vote.

## Electoral system

The [President of Ivory Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Ivory_Coast) is elected by absolute majority vote through a [two-round system](25932.md) to serve a five-year term.[^cite_note-2]

## Campaign

Some presidential candidates, including Gnangbo Kacou, planned to abandon the [CFA franc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CFA_franc).[^cite_note-16]

In October 2025 the government issued a ban on public meetings and demonstrations that sought to contest the exclusion of key opposition figures from the election.[^cite_note-17]
