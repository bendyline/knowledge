**Xing County** or **Xingxian** ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 兴县; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 興縣; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Xīng Xiàn*) is a county in the west of [Shanxi](81262.md) province, China, bordering [Shaanxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaanxi) province to the west across the [Yellow River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_River). It is the northernmost [county-level division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County-level_division) of the [prefecture-level city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefecture-level_city) of [Lüliang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%BCliang). Xing County was formerly known as **Linwei** (臨蔚縣) since the [Northern and Southern dynasties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_and_Southern_dynasties). Xing County was formally established in the second year of the [Hongwu era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hongwu_Emperor) (1368–1398) of the [Ming dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ming_dynasty).
