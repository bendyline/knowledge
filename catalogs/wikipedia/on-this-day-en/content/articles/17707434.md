**Adanim** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): עֲדָנִים, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Delights') is a [moshav](1597952.md) in central [Israel](9282173.md). Located in the [Sharon plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharon_plain) near [Hod HaSharon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hod_HaSharon), it falls under the jurisdiction of [Drom HaSharon Regional Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drom_HaSharon_Regional_Council). In 2024 it had a population of 466.[^cite_note-Israelpopulations-1]

## History

The moshav was founded in 1950 by [immigrants](351513.md) from [Romania](25445.md) on land located near the depopulated [Palestinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine) village of [Biyar 'Adas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biyar_'Adas).[^cite_note-2] It was initially named [Yarkona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarkona) BeHarhava ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): ירקונה בהרחבה, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Yarkona Expansion') before being renamed after [Psalm 36](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psalm_36)\:8(9):[^cite_note-3] "You give them drink from Your river of delights."

During the [2026 Iran War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_War), a foreign worker from [Thailand](30128.md) was killed on 18 March by shrapnel from a cluster munition on an Iranian missile.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]
