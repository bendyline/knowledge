On 4 October 2025, seven attackers were killed and multiple others were injured, and several prisoners escaped following an attack on a [National Intelligence and Security Agency](81014.md) (NISA) prison by the militant group [al-Shabaab](8726463.md) in [Somalia](27358.md). The militants stormed the high-security Godka Jilaow underground prison in the capital, [Mogadishu](60154.md).[^cite_note-AP-1][^cite_note-\:1-2][^cite_note-3]&#x20;

## Background

Godka Jilaow, a prison located near the [Villa Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villa_Somalia) presidential palace compound, houses several al-Shabaab fighters. [Somali government forces](27366.md) engaged in fierce fighting with the attackers.[^cite_note-\:0-4]&#x20;

[Al-Shabaab](8726463.md), formed in 2004, has waged an insurgency in southern Somalia since 2007 and made significant advances in the capital, [Mogadishu](60154.md), in 2025.[^cite_note-\:0-4]&#x20;

At least 12 people were killed at the gate of the prison by a [car bomb](155282.md) attack by al-Shabaab militants in 2014.[^cite_note-AP-1]&#x20;

## Attack

The attack occurred at around 4\:40 p.m. Explosions followed by gunfire were reported at the prison. The attackers reportedly used explosives and small arms.[^cite_note-5] Somalia's state media reported the heavily armed militants disguised as the intelligence unit's security forces. The [Toyota Hilux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_Hilux) used in the attack was stopped twice at the presidential palace gates before heading to the prison, preventing an attack. The vehicle passed through seven checkpoints in total without being searched.[^cite_note-6] The attack ended after an hours-long siege that resulted in the seven militants being killed.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] The government said no civilian or security officers were killed and no prisoners were freed but almost 25 people were injured.[^cite_note-AP2-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-AP-1]&#x20;

## Reactions

Al-Shabaab claimed responsibility for the attack, saying they "freed all [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim) prisoners" and targeted the underground cell guarded by security forces.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-AP-1]&#x20;

The federal government ordered crackdown on fake military vehicles after the attack, calling it a "cowardly terrorist attack".[^cite_note-13]&#x20;

The [African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Union_Support_and_Stabilization_Mission_in_Somalia) (AUSSOM) said they strongly condemned the attack, calling it "cowardly" and saying the group disrupts peace and stability and disregards human life in Mogadishu.[^cite_note-14]&#x20;

The [Intergovernmental Authority on Development](1921406.md) (IGAD) strongly condemned the attack, commending security forces in a statement for their swift response to prevent a greater tragedy.[^cite_note-15]&#x20;

Several Somali politicians, including former [prime minister](1885109.md) [Abdi Farah Shirdon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdi_Farah_Shirdon)[^cite_note-16] and former [president](516101.md) [Sharif Sheikh Ahmed](5442210.md),[^cite_note-17] denounced the attack and called for an investigation.&#x20;
