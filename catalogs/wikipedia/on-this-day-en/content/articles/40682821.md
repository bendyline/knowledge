[Kenya](188171.md) has abolished visa requirements for all foreign visitors from 1 January 2024, and instead implemented an [Electronic Travel Authorisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Travel_Authorisation) (eTA) system.

Most visitors must obtain an eTA prior to travel unless they are citizens of eTA-exempted countries.[^cite_note-1]

From 1 January 2024, citizens of all countries except those listed below must apply for an eTA in advance. Here are some details about eTA application:[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

- Applications can be submitted up to 90 days prior to travel and must be submitted at least 3 days in advance.

- eTA fee is [USD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USD) 32.50.

- eTA is good for single entry, but visitors who leave Kenya to other [EAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_African_Community) countries may re-enter provided that their eTA is still valid.

- Proof of reservation at the hotel where you plan to stay is required (if staying with friends, an invitation letter is also acceptable).

- [Yellow fever vaccination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_fever_vaccination) certificate is required if coming from endemic countries.

## East African Tourist Visa

In February 2014, [Kenya](188171.md), [Rwanda](25645.md) and [Uganda](31816.md) issued an East African Tourist Visa.[^cite_note-10] The visa fee [USD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USD) 100 and has no restrictions on nationality. It is a non-extendable multiple-entry 90-day visa that has to be first used to enter the country that issued it.[^cite_note-11]

## History



Kenyan single journey visa sticker

The San Marino-United Kingdom visa exemption agreement of 1949 was extended for Kenya and San Marino in 1963 and still applies.[^cite_note-12]

The agreement is of symbolic value for Kenyan citizens but does have an effect on San Marino passport holders.

On 30 October 2023, President William Ruto of Kenya declared the forthcoming elimination of visa requirements for all African visitors by the end of the year.[^cite_note-13]

An updated statement was issued during Kenya's 60th Independence Day celebrations on 12 December 2023, wherein the president declared that the decree would be expanded to encompass all countries globally, commencing in January 2024.[^cite_note-14]

In addition, the following groups were also exempt from visas:

- Children under age 16, regardless of nationality;

- Passengers in transit, if they do not leave the ship or the international transit area of the airport;

- Holders of diplomatic, official or service passports of [Brazil](3383.md), [Cuba](5042481.md) and [Turkey](11125639.md), of diplomatic and service passports of [China](5405.md) and [Iran](14653.md), and of diplomatic passports of [India](14533.md), [Israel](9282173.md) and [Nigeria](21383.md);

- Holders of [laissez-passers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laissez-passer) issued by [Africa Re](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Africa_Re), African Airlines Travel Association, African Housing Fund, [African Development Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Development_Bank), [African Union of Broadcasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Union_of_Broadcasting), [African Union](10634933.md), [Arab Bank for Economic Development in Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Bank_for_Economic_Development_in_Africa), [Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Market_for_Eastern_and_Southern_Africa), Desert Locust Control Organization for Central and Southern Africa, Environment Liaison Centre International, [European Union](9317.md), [Intergovernmental Authority on Development](1921406.md), [International Labour Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Labour_Organization), [International Monetary Fund](15251.md), [International Potato Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Potato_Center), International Red Locust Control Organization for Central and Southern Africa, [United Nations](31769.md) and the [World Bank](36752.md);

- Serving members of the [British Armed Forces](31730.md);

- Crew members of aircraft for up to 7 days, and of ships for up to 14 days;

- Owners of private aircraft stopping for refueling without leaving the airport.

On 30 May 2025, the ETA program received significant amendments, expanding the list of nationals exempt from obtaining the ETA to most African nationals (except nationals of Libya and Somalia), as well as other countries that were previously visa-exempt prior to the introduction of the ETA program.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]
