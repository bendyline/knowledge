**Soccsksargen**[^cite_note-5] (officially stylized in [all caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_caps);[^cite_note-GovPH-EO36-6] [\[sokˈsardʒɛn\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Tagalog)), designated as **Region XII**, is an [administrative region](216786.md) of the Philippines. Formerly known as **Central Mindanao**, it is located in south-central [Mindanao](20546.md) and comprises four [provinces](165100.md): [Cotabato](297394.md), [Sarangani](297388.md), [South Cotabato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Cotabato), and [Sultan Kudarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Kudarat), and aside from these provinces it also is located in one highly urbanized city, [General Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Santos). The regional center is [Koronadal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koronadal) in South Cotabato, while the center of commerce and industry is General Santos, the most populous city in the region.

## Geography

The region is bounded on the north by the province of [Bukidnon](261222.md) in [Northern Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Mindanao), on the east by the [Davao Region](296162.md), on the northwest and west by the [Bangsamoro](37260549.md) region, and on the southwest by the [Celebes Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celebes_Sea). The region also shares a maritime border with [Gorontalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorontalo) and [North Sulawesi](453688.md) provinces of [Indonesia](14579.md).

The region has extensive coastlines, valleys and mountain ranges. Known for its river system, the region is the [drainage basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drainage_basin) of [Mindanao](20546.md), particularly at the [Cotabato Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotabato_Basin?action=edit\&redlink=1), a large [depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depression_\(geology\)) surrounded by mountain ranges on three sides.[^cite_note-ThePhilippineIslandWorld-7][^cite_note-HumanHelminthiases-8] Within the basin runs the [Rio Grande de Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindanao_River), the longest river in Mindanao and the second longest in the Philippines. The river empties into [Illana Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illana_Bay) of the larger [Moro Gulf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moro_Gulf) to the west of Cotabato City.

At the south of the basin lie the [Tiruray Highlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiruray_Highlands), a moderately high mountain range blocking the basin from the southern coastline.[^cite_note-ThePhilippineIslandWorld-7] Southeast of the mountains lies [Sarangani Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarangani_Bay).

## History

The oldest civilization in the region is located in [Maitum, Sarangani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maitum,_Sarangani), where the [Maitum Anthropomorphic Pottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maitum_Anthropomorphic_Pottery) were found. The jars have been declared as National Cultural Treasures, and are subject to the high protections ensured by Philippine and international laws.

From around the 14th century until the early 20th century, the Muslim sultanates of [Maguindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Maguindanao) and [Buayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Buayan) dominated the large parts of Soccsksargen region, influencing the local datus in the area as well as having General Santos City under their direct control as a port.

### Central Mindanao

The region used to be named *Central Mindanao*. Prior to the creation of the [Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_Region_in_Muslim_Mindanao) (ARMM), it comprised the following provinces:

- [Maguindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao)

- [North Cotabato](297394.md)

- [Sultan Kudarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Kudarat)

- [Lanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanao_del_Norte)

- [Lanao del Sur](297370.md)

With the creation of ARMM, Lanao del Sur, Maguindanao (excluding [Cotabato City](298955.md)), and Sultan Kudarat were removed from the region, leaving Lanao del Norte and Cotabato, and [Iligan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iligan), [Marawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marawi) and [Cotabato City](298955.md) as constituent provinces and cities.[^cite_note-PSA-CentralMindanao2000Census-13] Lanao del Norte and Iligan were later transferred to [Northern Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Mindanao) in September 2001 as they are farther from Cotabato City and closer to Cagayan de Oro (regional capital of Northern Mindanao),[^cite_note-GovPH-EO361-14] while Marawi became part of the ARMM.

On December 18, 1998, Sultan Kudarat was transferred back to Central Mindanao through Republic Act No. 8744.[^cite_note-15]

### Soccsksargen

In September 2001, *Executive Order No. 36* was signed by President [Gloria Macapagal Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Macapagal_Arroyo) transferring [South Cotabato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Cotabato), [Sarangani](297388.md), [General Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Santos) (also known as the *SocSarGen* District), from [Southern Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Mindanao) to Region XII, and renaming the region, from *Central Mindanao*, to *Soccsksargen*.[^cite_note-GovPH-EO36-6][^cite_note-NSCB-SouthernMindanao-Davao-16]

### Koronadal as new regional center

By virtue of Executive Order No. 304 signed by then President [Gloria Macapagal Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Macapagal_Arroyo), [Koronadal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koronadal) was named as the regional political and socio-economic center of Soccsksargen on March 30, 2004. Regional departments, bureaus and offices were ordered to move from [Cotabato City](298955.md), the former regional center of the region.

### Special Cotabato barangays and Cotabato City exit

Traditionally resisting efforts for inclusion to the Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao despite serving as the government center of the ARMM, the [January 21, 2019 Bangsamoro Autonomous Region creation plebiscite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Bangsamoro_Autonomous_Region_creation_plebiscite) resulted in the surprise ratification of the Bangsamoro Organic Law in Cotabato City. This means the city may now formally serve as the capital of the region and the offices of the former ARMM will be retained for use by the [Bangsamoro Regional Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangsamoro_Autonomous_Region) and now formally no longer constitutes part of the Soccsksargen Region.

In addition to Cotabato City, [63 barangays in Cotabato province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangsamoro_barangays_in_Cotabato) opted to join Bangsamoro, thus exiting the region as well.

Soccsksargen is home to diverse ethnolingustic and cultural backgrounds, both native and non-native residents, making it a melting pot of cultures. The first inhabitants of the region were the indigenous [B'laans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaan_people), [T'bolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tboli_people), [Manobos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manobos), [Tedurays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teduray_people) and the Muslim [Iranuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranun_people), [Maguindanaons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_people), and [Sangil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangil_people) – large parts of the region itself historically were part of the [Maguindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Maguindanao) and [Buayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Buayan) sultanates. Christian migrants from [Luzon](69375.md) and [Visayas](72418.md) (and their native-born descendants) also settled in the area by the early 20th century seeking better opportunities, mainly from agriculture. Most of these non-native settlers mainly came from [Ilocandia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocos_Region), [Panay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panay), [Negros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negros), [Cebu](160278.md), [Bohol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohol) and [Siquijor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siquijor) who call Soccsksargen and Mindanao as a whole their new home and are responsible for spreading Christianity throughout the region over the years. Today, [Hiligaynons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiligaynon_people) make up the majority of the region's non-native Christian population; while the [Cebuanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_people) dominantly inhabit the entirety of [Sarangani](297388.md), [General Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Santos) and its neighboring municipalities of South Cotabato and the areas in the region that border [Bukidnon](261222.md) and [Davao Region](296162.md); and [Ilocanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_people) who form the majority in [Kabacan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabacan) (Cotabato), [Tampakan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampakan), [Tantangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tantangan) (both in South Cotabato), [Lambayong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lambayong), [Senator Ninoy Aquino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senator_Ninoy_Aquino,_Sultan_Kudarat), [President Quirino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_Quirino,_Sultan_Kudarat), [Columbio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbio) (all in Sultan Kudarat) and [Kiamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiamba,_Sarangani) (Sarangani, though most of the municipality's inhabitants now speak Cebuano instead of Ilocano). Moreover, intermarriage between ethnolinguistic groups is relatively not uncommon in Soccsksargen.

The region contributes to the national GDP with 2.6% with 5% growth compared to 2016. It accounts for 18.3% of [Mindanao](20546.md) gross regional domestic product (GRDP).[^cite_note-28]

The 2024 Philippine Coffee Quality Competition showcased Region 12's development as a dominant force in coffee quality. The event demonstrated Region 12's ability to produce coffee that stands out in both national and international markets. This newfound reputation may raise the region's prominence in the coffee industry, attracting more buyers from around the world.[^cite_note-29]

## Musical heritage



[Maguindanaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_people) Kulintang exhibited in [Old Cotabato City Hall Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Cotabato_City_Hall_Museum)

Further information: [Kulintang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulintang)

The artistic cultural heritage of the native [Maguindanao people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_people), among other regional groups—both [Muslims](6037917.md) and [Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholicism)—revolves around *[kulintang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulintang)* music, a specific type of [gong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gong) music. Unique to the Soccsksargen, elements of kulintang may be loosely compared to [Indonesia](14579.md)’s *[gamelan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamelan)* music, and other related traditional performance arts.
