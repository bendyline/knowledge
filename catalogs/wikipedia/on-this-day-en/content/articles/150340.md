**Miss Universe** is an annual international major beauty pageant that is run by the Thailand and Mexican-based [Miss Universe Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Organization).[^cite_note-Tadena1-1] Along with [Miss World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_World), [Miss International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_International), and [Miss Earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Earth), it is one of the [Big Four beauty pageants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Four_beauty_pageants).[^cite_note-2]

The Miss Universe Organization and its brand is owned by [JKN Global Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JKN_Global_Group) and Legacy Holding Group USA Inc., an American division of Mexican company Legacy Holding through the joint venture company JKN Legacy Inc.[^cite_note-3] [Telemundo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telemundo) had the licensing rights to air the pageant through 2023.[^cite_note-4] The pageant's advocacy is "humanitarian issues and is a voice to affect positive change in the world".[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

The reigning Miss Universe is [Fátima Bosch](81078845.md) from Mexico who was crowned on November 20, 2025, in Pak Kret, Thailand.[^cite_note-7]

## History

Miss Universe sash from 2001 until 2022

Miss Universe sash from 2001 until 2022

The title "Miss Universe" was first used by the [International Pageant of Pulchritude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Pageant_of_Pulchritude) in 1926. This contest was held annually until 1935, when the [Great Depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Depression) and other events preceding [World War II](32927.md) led to its demise.

The current Miss Universe pageant was founded in 1952 by Pacific Knitting Mills, a California-based clothing company and manufacturer of [Catalina Swimwear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalina_Swimwear), and it has since been headquartered in the United States. The company sponsored the [Miss America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_America) pageant until 1951, when the winner, [Yolande Betbeze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yolande_Betbeze), refused to pose for publicity pictures wearing one of its swimsuits. In 1952, Pacific Knitting Mills organized the [Miss USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_USA) and Miss Universe pageants, co-sponsoring them for decades.[^cite_note-8]

Armi Kuusela, the first titleholder, meets people in Helsinki, Finland

[Armi Kuusela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armi_Kuusela), the first titleholder, meets people in [Helsinki, Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki)

The first Miss Universe Pageant was held in [Long Beach, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach,_California), in 1952. It was won by [Armi Kuusela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armi_Kuusela) of Finland, who gave up her title, though not officially, to marry shortly before her year was completed.[^cite_note-9] Until 1958, the Miss Universe title, like that of Miss America, was dated by the year after the contest, so Kuusela's title was Miss Universe 1953. Since its founding by Pacific Mills, the pageant has been organized and conducted by the Miss Universe Organization. Eventually, Pacific Mills and its subsidiaries were acquired by the [Kayser-Roth Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayser-Roth_Corporation).[^cite_note-\:0-10]

The pageant was first televised in 1955. [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS) began broadcasting the combined Miss USA and Miss Universe pageants in 1960, and as separate contests in 1965. In 1975, [Gulf and Western Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_and_Western_Industries) bought the Kayser-Roth Corporation. It owned the Miss Universe pageant until 1991, when [Procter & Gamble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procter_&_Gamble) bought it.[^cite_note-\:0-10]

In 1996, [Donald Trump](4848272.md) bought the pageant from [ITT Corp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITT_Corp), with a broadcasting arrangement with CBS until 2002.[^cite_note-11] In 1998, Miss Universe, Inc. changed its name to the Miss Universe Organization, and moved its headquarters from Los Angeles to New York City.[^cite_note-India-Today-1-12][^cite_note-new_team-13] By late 2002, Trump entered into a joint venture with [NBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC),[^cite_note-Tadena1-1][^cite_note-14] which in 2003 outbid the other markets for the TV rights.[^cite_note-The_New_York_Times-15] From 2003 to 2014, the pageant was broadcast on NBC in the U.S.

In June 2015, NBC canceled all business relationships with Trump and the Miss Universe Organization in response to [controversial statements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_2016_presidential_campaign#Announcement) about immigrants who illegally crossed the border from Mexico.[^cite_note-hollywoodreporter-16][^cite_note-NBCsever-17] As part of the legal settlement, in September 2015, Trump bought out NBC's 50% stake in the company, making him the company's sole owner. Three days later, he sold the company to [WME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Morris_Endeavor)/[IMG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMG_\(company\)).[^cite_note-nytimes.com-18][^cite_note-19] After the change of ownership, [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company) and [Azteca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azteca_Am%C3%A9rica) became the Miss Universe and Miss USA pageants' official broadcasters in October 2015.[^cite_note-20] The Miss Universe Pageant's broadcast rights were temporarily split between [Telemundo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telemundo) and [FYI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FYI_\(American_TV_channel\)) during the 2020 pageant amid [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md) restrictions. The contract with Fox and emcee [Steve Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Harvey) resumed for the 2021 edition.

On October 26, 2022, Thailand-based [JKN Global Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JKN_Global_Group) acquired Miss Universe Organization (MUO) from [Endeavor Group Holdings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endeavor_\(company\))-owned [IMG Worldwide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMG_Worldwide) at \$14 million, making [Anne Jakapong Jakrajutatip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakkaphong_Jakrajutatip) the first [transgender](19904525.md) woman to own the organization and marking the first time the organization moved its headquarters outside the U.S. Since the 2022 edition, NBC has regained the pageant's broadcast rights via [The Roku Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Roku_Channel) as a result of the ownership changes, marking the first time in Miss Universe history that the pageant has transitioned from [traditional broadcast network coverage](29831.md) to [full streaming service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streaming_media) in the U.S.[^cite_note-21]

On November 16, 2023, Paula Shugart, president of the Miss Universe Organization, announced her departure from the Miss Universe Organization.[^cite_note-\:1-22] She will not be replaced.[^cite_note-\:1-22] On February 8, 2024, Miss Universe Organization CEO Amy Emmerich also announced her departure. She left the organization on March 1, 2024.[^cite_note-23] On December 13, 2025 [Mario Búcaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_B%C3%BAcaro) has resigned as Chief Executive Officer of the Miss Universe Organization (MUO) fewer than two months after assuming the position. His departure was confirmed in a press release that also outlined his contributions to the global pageant organization.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

## Contestant selection

Main article: [List of Miss Universe countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Miss_Universe_countries)

To participate in Miss Universe, a country needs a local company or person to buy the competition's local rights through a franchise fee. The fee includes the rights of image, brand, and everything related to the pageant. Often the owner of the franchise returns the franchise to the Miss Universe Organization, which resells it to a new stakeholder. The reselling of the franchise from one owner to another has occurred often in the event's history, sometimes for contractual breaches or financial reasons. The number of participants fluctuates annually because of the franchising of the pageant paired with conflicting schedules to the regular calendar, but has steadied above 70 countries since [1989](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_1989).

Usually a country's candidate selection involves pageants in the nation's local subdivisions, where local winners compete in a national pageant, but there are some countries who opt for an internal selection. For example, from 2000 to 2004, Australian delegates were chosen by a [modeling agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modeling_agency). Although the Miss Universe Organization generally discourages such "castings", [Jennifer Hawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Hawkins) was chosen to represent Australia in Miss Universe 2004 and won the crown. Australia reinstated its national pageant for Miss Universe in 2005.

Recent countries that debuted in the pageant include Cameroon (2020), Bahrain (2021), Bhutan (2022), Pakistan (2023), Belarus, Eritrea, Guinea, Macau, Maldives, Moldova, North Macedonia, Somalia, United Arab Emirates, and Uzbekistan (2024), Cape Verde, Mayotte, Miss Universe Latina, Palestine, and Rwanda (2025). Miss Universe Latina, Palestine, and Rwanda is the latest newcomer and the most recent country to obtain its first ever semifinal placement at Miss Universe, after debuting in [2025](78060439.md) as a semifinalist in the Top 30.&#x20;

Botswana remains the most recent first-time entry to win Miss Universe in its debut year (with [Mpule Kwelagobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mpule_Kwelagobe) in [1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_1999)), and Denmark is the most recent country to obtain its first ever national win in Miss Universe (with [Victoria Kjær Theilvig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Kj%C3%A6r_Theilvig) in 2024).

Cultural barriers, particularly with the swimsuit competition, and the event's franchise fees have prevented some countries, such as Mozambique, from participating. Nevertheless, the Miss Universe Pageant has been popular in the Americas, Africa, and Asia, especially in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_USA), [Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Philippines), [Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Colombia), [Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Brazil), [Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Venezuela), [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_South_Africa), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_France), [Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Thailand), and [Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Indonesia), given their track record of multiple semifinal appearances in the last decade and combined titles in the competition's history. As of 2024, only two countries have been present at every Miss Universe since its inception in 1952: Canada and France.

Since 2012, openly [transgender](19904525.md) women have been allowed to compete.[^cite_note-26] Six years after this rule went into effect, [Angela Ponce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Ponce) of Spain became the first openly transgender competitor, in the 2018[^cite_note-27] edition. In [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2019), Myanmar's [Swe Zin Htet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swe_Zin_Htet) became the first out lesbian to compete. Spain's [Patricia Yurena Rodríguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patricia_Yurena_Rodr%C3%ADguez) is the highest-placed [LGBT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT) member at Miss Universe, placing second to Venezuela's [Gabriela Isler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriela_Isler) in [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2013), but did not come out until years after the competition.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30] In 2021, the Philippines' [Beatrice Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beatrice_Gomez) became the first openly [bisexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bisexual) (and LGBT) contestant to enter the Miss Universe semifinals, after finishing as a finalist in the Top 5 that year. In 2023, Portugal's [Marina Machete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Machete) became the first [transgender](19904525.md) contestant to enter the Miss Universe semifinals, after finishing as a semifinalist in the Top 20.

Previously, official rules said that pageant contestants "must not have ever been married, not had a marriage annulled nor given birth to, or parented a child. The titleholders are also required to remain unmarried throughout their reign."[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32] But in August 2022, the Miss Universe Organization announced that mothers, married, or pregnant women are eligible.[^cite_note-33] This rule has been in effect since 2023. Accepting married contestants renewed tension between the U.S.-based Miss Universe pageant and the Europe-based [Mrs. Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mrs._Universe) pageant, which was previously the only avenue for married women to compete for the Universe title. Later in 2023, Colombia's [Camila Avella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camila_Avella) became the first candidate to become a mother before clinching a semifinal placement at Miss Universe, after finishing as a finalist in the Top 5.&#x20;

Miss Universe has always strictly prohibited [age fabrication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_fabrication). While the pageant's minimum age has been set at 18, this presents a problem for several European countries that allow 17-year-olds to compete in their pageants. National titleholders under 18 must be replaced by their runners-up or another candidate in the main pageant. In recent years, all Miss Universe candidates have been required to be at least [university degree holders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_degree) or working [professionals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professionals) from the onset of their national pageants. In September 2023, [R'Bonney Gabriel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R'Bonney_Gabriel) announced that the organization would be drop the upper age limit. Previously, contestants had to be less than 29 years old at the start of the pageant. Beginning in 2024, "every adult woman in the world will be eligible to compete to be Miss Universe."[^cite_note-34]

## Main pageant

For full list and details, see [List of Miss Universe editions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Miss_Universe_editions).

Throughout the history of Miss Universe, the main pageant's scheduling has varied widely. In the last decade, the competition has been consistently held over a two-week period between early November and late January. Because of television schedule demands (largely as a result of international time zone differences) or conflicting national events during the organizing process (such as the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), the [Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympics), [FIFA World Cup](11370.md), and elections), four editions ([2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2014), [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2016), [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2020), and [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2022)) have been postponed to next year. Since the 2022 edition held in [New Orleans](53842.md) in 2023, the Miss Universe Organization has regularly announced the hosting nation of the next edition on coronation night. Between the early 1970s through the late 2000s, the pageant spanned a full month (typically between March and June) to allow time for rehearsals, appearances, and the preliminary competition, with the winner crowned by the previous year's titleholder during the final competition.

According to the organizers, the Miss Universe contest is more than a beauty pageant, though they are expected to participate in swimsuit and evening gown competitions. Women aspiring to become Miss Universe must be intelligent, well-mannered, and cultured. If a candidate is unable to perform well during each round, she may be eliminated. Normally, the candidates' placements are determined by a ranked vote, where each judge ranks the candidates. In the past, semifinalists were chosen by a [round-robin](939742.md) system. Between 2017 and 2019, the semifinalists were selected based on highest scores per continental group followed by the judging panel's wildcard list alongside one candidate chosen by the public. In the semifinals, all preliminary results are reset and a new competition starts with the highest-placed positions. In 2020, the round-robin system was reinstated: in each round of the grand final, the group of candidates with the lowest ratings was progressively eliminated. But this criterion has been modified to use weighted averages or points accumulated by stages from the preliminary competition to coronation night, with the assessment in ascending or descending order. From [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2011) to [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2014) and in 2016, 2020, and since 2022, the public can also select another semifinalist. Since the pageant's inception, all semifinalists have been announced at the beginning of the live telecast regardless of the edition's format. If ties occur in the final rounds, the preliminary results are used.

The winner signs a contract with the Miss Universe Organization that can last from seven months to more than a year as per the organization's demands. Miss Universe takes office immediately after coronation and takes on a public cause for which she becomes the ambassador for a year to spread messages about the control of diseases, peace, [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV/AIDS) awareness, women's and ethnic minority rights, contemporary racial issues, public health issues, or the consequences of [global warming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_warming). The winner also receives a cash allowance for her reign, a [New York Film Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Film_Academy) scholarship, a modeling portfolio, beauty products, clothes, shoes, styling, healthcare, and fitness services from the pageant's sponsors. She has access to events such as [fashion shows](8312977.md) and opening galas, as well as casting calls and modeling opportunities in New York City. Between 1996 and 2015, the winner was given a [Trump Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_Place_\(New_York\)) apartment in New York City, which she shared with the Miss USA and Miss Teen USA titleholders. Starting in 2022, the winner and the two runners-up in the Top 3 have shuttled between residences in New York and [Bangkok](56615.md).[^cite_note-35]

If for any reason the winner cannot fulfill her duties as Miss Universe, the first runner-up takes over. This protocol has happened only once as of 2024, when Panama's [Justine Pasek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justine_Pasek) succeeded Russia's [Oxana Fedorova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxana_Fedorova) as Miss Universe in [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2002) after Fedorova's termination that year. Aside from the winner and her runners-up, special awards are also given to the winners of the Best National Costume, Miss Photogenic, and Miss Congeniality. The Miss Congeniality award is chosen by the delegates themselves. In recent years, Miss Photogenic has been chosen by popular internet vote (she used to be chosen by media personnel covering the event), and the winning country for Best National Costume is announced after the naming of the semifinalists during finals night. Starting in 2024, Continental Queens are announced who travel with Miss Universe.

It was used to crown [Armi Kuusela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armi_Kuusela) in [1952](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_1952).

## Miss Universe Organization

The Miss Universe Organization owns and runs the Miss Universe, Miss USA, and Miss Teen USA beauty pageants. Between 2020 and 2022, it stopped organizing the [Miss USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_USA) and the [Miss Teen USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Teen_USA) competitions. They were operated by [Crystle Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystle_Stewart) until her suspension in October 2022, after which they returned to the Miss Universe Organization.[^cite_note-54][^cite_note-55]

Based in New York City and [Bangkok](56615.md), the organization has been owned by the Thai [JKN Global Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JKN_Global_Group) since October 2022, when [WME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Morris_Endeavor)/[IMG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMG_\(company\)) sold the pageant. The organization sells television rights to the pageants and pageant organizations in other countries.
