**Law in Denmark** follows a [civil law system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_law_\(legal_system\)).

## Private law

Private law constitutes several different forms.

The *[Funktionærloven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funktion%C3%A6rloven)* governs [labour law](18657.md) concerning [salaried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salary) employees. The *[Ferieloven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferieloven)* governs vacation time for employees. The *[Købeloven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B8beloven)* governs [consumer law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumer_law). The *[Lejeloven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lejeloven)* governs [landlord–tenant law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landlord%E2%80%93tenant_law). [Privacy law in Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privacy_law_in_Denmark) is important.

## Public law

There are several kinds of [Public law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_law) in Denmark.

The *[Straffeloven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_Penal_Code)* is the [criminal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_code).

The *[Planloven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planloven)* governs [urban planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_planning). The *[Serviceloven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serviceloven)* governs [social programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_programs).

There are laws about [Abortion in Denmark](25844700.md) and [Danish nationality law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_nationality_law).

[Danefæ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danef%C3%A6) is the law stipulating that valuable objects discovered in Denmark that have no identifiable owner are the property of the state.

## History

The law of [Denmark](76972.md) was originally based on regional laws, of which the most important was the *[Jyske Lov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jyske_Lov)*, or the Law of Jutland 1241. The *[Danske Lov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danske_Lov)*, or the Danish Code of 1683, promoted unity. The law has been developed via judicial decisions and royal decrees. [Roman law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_law) has not had much influence on the law of Denmark.
