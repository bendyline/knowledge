The **Parliament of Zimbabwe** is the [bicameral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameralism) [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) of [Zimbabwe](34399.md) composed of the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Zimbabwe) and the [National Assembly](3576952.md). The Senate is the [upper house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_house), and consists of 90 members, 60 of whom are elected by [proportional representation](23298.md) from ten [six-member constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multimember_district) corresponding to the country's [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Zimbabwe). Of the remaining 30 seats, 18 are reserved for [chiefs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribal_chief), and two for people with [disabilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disabilities), while 10 are appointed directly by the [President](39434128.md). The National Assembly is the [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house), and consists of 280 members. Of these, 210 are elected from [single-member constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-member_district). The remaining 70 seats are [reserved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reserved_political_positions) women's and youth quotas: 60 for women; 10 for youth. These are elected by proportional representation from ten six-member and one-member constituencies respectively, corresponding to the country's provinces.[^cite_note-2]

Formerly based at [Parliament House, Harare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_House,_Harare), the parliament moved to the [New Zimbabwe Parliament Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zimbabwe_Parliament_Building) in October 2023.[^cite_note-1R-3] The new building has 650 seats, which will allow the parliament to expand.[^cite_note-4]

## History

Historically, the first legislature in what is now Zimbabwe was the [Southern Rhodesian Legislative Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Rhodesian_Legislative_Council), established in 1898 in what was then the [British South Africa Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_South_Africa_Company) territory of [Southern Rhodesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Rhodesia). [Company rule in Rhodesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Company_rule_in_Rhodesia) ended in 1923 when the territory became a [self-governing colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-governing_colony), and the Legislative Council was replaced by the [Southern Rhodesian Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Rhodesian_Legislative_Assembly). In 1970, five years after the colony's [Unilateral Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodesia's_Unilateral_Declaration_of_Independence), [Rhodesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodesia) replaced the [unicameral](204304.md) Legislative Assembly with a bicameral [Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Rhodesia), consisting of a Senate and House of Assembly. This parliamentary structure was retained upon [Zimbabwe](34399.md)'s independence in 1980. Per the constitution produced by [Lancaster House Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster_House_Agreement) in 1979, the Senate was composed of 40 seats and the House of Assembly was composed of 100, with ten Senate seats and 20 seats in the House of Assembly reserved for [white Zimbabweans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_people_in_Zimbabwe). The white-reserved seats were abolished in 1987, and a [constitutional amendment](261586.md) in 1989 abolished the Senate and expanded the House of Assembly to 120 seats. In 2005, the Senate was reintroduced and the House of Assembly expanded. The House of Assembly was expanded once again in 2007 to 210 seats. The present parliamentary structure has been in place since the adoption of a new [constitution](30927578.md) in 2013.

The Senate is presided over by its [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Senate_of_Zimbabwe), who is not a sitting Senator, who is assisted by a Deputy President. The National Assembly is presided over by a [Speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_National_Assembly_of_Zimbabwe), who is not a Member of Parliament. The Speaker is assisted by a Deputy Speaker. The [10th Parliament of Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10th_Parliament_of_Zimbabwe) is the current Parliament since the [2023 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Zimbabwean_general_election). The [Zimbabwe African National Union – Patriotic Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZANU%E2%80%93PF), the [ruling party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruling_party) since Zimbabwe's independence in 1980, holds majorities in both chambers of Parliament. The [Citizens Coalition for Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens_Coalition_for_Change) holds most of the remaining seats, and forms the [opposition](3379016.md).
