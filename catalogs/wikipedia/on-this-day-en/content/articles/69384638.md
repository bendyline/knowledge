# West Omo Zone



The regions and zones of Ethiopia

**West Omo** or **Mirab Omo** is a Zone in the [Ethiopian](187749.md) [South West Ethiopia Peoples' Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_West_Ethiopia_Peoples'_Region).[^cite_note-1] West Omo is located at Ethiopia's southern margin, where [Maji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maji_\(woreda\)) and [Surma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surma_\(woreda\)) woredas are bordering [Kenya](188171.md), encompassing the area to the west of the [Omo River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omo_River). The area is dominantly inhabited by the [Dizi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dizi_people), [Suri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surma_people) and Me'enit communities.[^cite_note-2]

West Omo Zone has seven districts and 116 kebeles (112 rural and 4 urban).[^cite_note-pmc-3]

## Demographics

The West Omo Zone has a population of 272,943 people, accounting for 12% of South West Ethiopia Peoples' Region, and comprises 49.5% male and 50.5% female in 55,703 households. 60% were pastoral.[^cite_note-pmc-3]
