The **Huisekoutermolen** was an open trestle [post mill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post_mill) at [Huise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huise), [East Flanders](152405.md), Belgium. It was moved from [Waregem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waregem), [West Flanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Flanders) in 1975 and collapsed on 19 June 2026.

## History

The Huisekoutermolen was originally built at [Waregem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waregem) before 1512, where it was known as the Hoogmolen.[^cite_note-OE-1] It was designated a protected monument in 1944.[^cite_note-BT2200979-2] The mill was registered as of [beschermd erfgoed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beschermd_erfgoed), number 74522. The mill was sold in 1971, and moved to Huise and rebuilt between 1972 and 1975.[^cite_note-OE-1] The work was done by [millwright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millwright) Walter Mariman of [Zele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zele).[^cite_note-Molenechos-3] It was erected on the site of a [snuff mill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snuff_\(tobacco\)) which had burnt down in 1850.[^cite_note-OE-1] In 2001, the mill became unworkable due to problems with the sails, one of which subsequently broke. The sails were removed in 2007.[^cite_note-Molenechos-3] The mill was leased by Oost-Vlaams Molens in 2010 and returned to working order in 2012.[^cite_note-OE-1] It was used to train millers in the art of milling.[^cite_note-BT2200979-2] On 19 June 2026, the mill blew down in a [thunderstorm](70807.md).[^cite_note-HLN-4] Photographs show that the mill's main post snapped at the point where the quarterbars joined it. Vlaamse Molens stated that the mill would be rebuilt.[^cite_note-Nieuwsblad-5]

## Description

The Huisekoutermolen was an [open trestle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trestle_\(mill\)) [post mill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post_mill) with four [Common sails](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_sails). It drove two pairs of [millstones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millstone), arranged head and tail.[^cite_note-OE-1]
