The [federal government of the United States](195149.md) includes many [agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_agency), departments, offices and other organizations. The official *[United States Government Manual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Government_Manual)*[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEFischer20111–2-1][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEFederal_Register2013-2] and the [Administrative Procedure Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_Procedure_Act_\(United_States\)) list government agencies. The Administrative Procedure Act's definition of agency applies to most [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) agencies, while the [United States Congress](31756.md) may define an agency however it chooses in enabling legislation, and through subsequent litigation often involving the [Freedom of Information Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_of_Information_Act_\(United_States\)) and the [Government in the Sunshine Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_in_the_Sunshine_Act).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELewisSelin201313–14-3][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKamensky2013-4]

The executive branch of the federal government includes the [Executive Office of the President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Office_of_the_President) and the [United States federal executive departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_federal_executive_departments), whose secretaries belong to the [Cabinet of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_United_States). Employees of the majority of these agencies are considered [civil servants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_federal_civil_service).

The majority of the [independent agencies of the United States government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_agencies_of_the_United_States_government) are also classified as executive agencies. They are independent in that they are not subordinated under a Cabinet position. There is a smaller number of independent agencies that are not part of the executive branch, such as the [Congressional Research Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congressional_Research_Service) and the [United States Sentencing Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Sentencing_Commission), which are legislative and judicial agencies, respectively.

## United States Congress



Seal of the United States Congress.

Main article: [United States Congress](31756.md)

The U.S. Congress is the bicameral legislature of the United States government, and is made up of two chambers: the [United States Senate](24909346.md) (the upper chamber) and the [United States House of Representatives](19468510.md) (the lower chamber). Together, the two chambers exercise authority over multiple legislative agencies.

- [Congressional Budget Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congressional_Budget_Office) (CBO)

- [Government Accountability Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Accountability_Office) (GAO)

- [Government Publishing Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Government_Publishing_Office) (USGPO)

- Office of Congressional Accessibility Services (OCAS)

- Office of the [Attending Physician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attending_Physician_of_the_United_States_Congress) (OAP)

- [Office of Congressional Workplace Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Congressional_Workplace_Rights) (OCWR)

- [Office of Congressional Conduct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Congressional_Conduct) (OCC)

- [Congressional Office for International Leadership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_World_Program)

- John C. Stennis Center for Public Service Training and Development

- [Capitol Police Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Police_Board), which governs the [United States Capitol Police](1915214.md) (USCP)

- [Architect of the Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architect_of_the_Capitol) (AOC), which is in charge of maintaining the [United States Capitol Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Capitol_Complex), including the [Capitol Visitor Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Visitor_Center) (CVC) and the [United States Botanic Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Botanic_Garden) (USBG)

The legislature also oversees the [Library of Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_of_Congress) (LOC), a national library dedicated to national records, which administers various programs, agencies, and services.&#x20;

- [Law Library of Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_Library_of_Congress)

The president is in charge of executing federal laws and approving, or vetoing, new legislation passed by Congress. The president resides in the [Executive Residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Residence) (EXR) maintained by the [Office of Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Administration) (OA).
