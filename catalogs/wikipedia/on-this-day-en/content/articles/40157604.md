**Neal Skupski** (born 1 December 1989) is a British professional tennis player who specializes in doubles. He has a career-high [doubles ranking](13480983.md) of [world No. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ATP_number_1_ranked_doubles_tennis_players) by the [ATP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals). He first achieved this milestone in November 2022, topping the rankings jointly with long-time partner [Wesley Koolhof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_Koolhof). He became the third British world no. 1 in doubles after [Jamie Murray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Murray) and [Joe Salisbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Salisbury).

Skupski is a five-time [Grand Slam](197638.md) champion, having won the [2023 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) partnering [Wesley Koolhof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_Koolhof) and [2026 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) & [2026 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_US_Open_-_Men's_doubles) partnering [Christian Harrison](38874563.md). In mixed doubles, he earned the [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles) and [2022 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles) titles, playing alongside [Desirae Krawczyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desirae_Krawczyk). Koolhof and Skupski were also runners-up at the [2022 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_\(tennis\)), and Krawczyk and Skupski reached the final at the [2024 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles) and [2024 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles). He has won 18 doubles titles on the [ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour), including the [Madrid Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Mutua_Madrid_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), [Canadian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_National_Bank_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) and [Paris Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Rolex_Paris_Masters_%E2%80%93_Doubles) at [ATP Masters 1000](30872553.md) level in 2022.[^cite_note-2]

Skupski has represented [Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain_Davis_Cup_team) in the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup) since 2019 and competed at the [2020 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles). He attended [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University), where he studied sports administration as well as played college tennis.

## Early life

Neal Skupski was born in [Liverpool](18081.md) to Mary Skupski and Ken Skupski Sr. and attended [King David High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_David_High_School,_Liverpool). Neal is the younger brother of fellow tennis player [Ken Skupski Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Skupski), with whom he has regularly partnered since 2013 until Ken Jr.'s retirement from professional tennis in July 2022.[^cite_note-3] He competed in a handful of matches in 2010 and 2011, then took a year to finish his degree at [LSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Professional career

### 2013: ATP debut, first ATP final, top 100 debut

He began competing as a professional in 2013, initially playing on the Futures and [Challenger Tours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Challenger_Tour). After playing with a number of partners, he started competing more frequently with his brother Ken from March 2013 onwards.[^cite_note-Echo-6] The pair reached the final of the [Nottingham Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Aegon_Trophy_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) on the [Challenger Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_ATP_Challenger_Tour), before winning five Challenger titles in 2013, as of 23 September.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] These wins saw Neal move into the world top 200 in July 2013.

At the [2013 Kremlin Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Kremlin_Cup) the Skupski brothers entered their first [ATP World Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_World_Tour) tournament, progressing to the final, where they lost on a tie-break.[^cite_note-9] Following the final, Neal moved into the top 100 for the first time, having been unranked at the start of the season. He ended 2013 ranked 86th in the world.

### 2017–2018: First Grand Slam quarterfinal, first two ATP titles

At the [2017 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Wimbledon_Championships) he reached the quarterfinals as a wildcard for the first time in his career partnering with his brother Ken where they were defeated by 4th seeded pair [Łukasz Kubot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%81ukasz_Kubot) and [Marcelo Melo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcelo_Melo).

Skupski won his first ATP Tour title at the [Open Sud de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Open_Sud_de_France), partnering again with his brother [Ken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Skupski), their first ATP title together.[^cite_note-10] He won his second and biggest title of his career thus far at the ATP 500 [2018 Vienna Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Erste_Bank_Open) partnering with fellow Brit [Joe Salisbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Salisbury).

### 2019–2020: First Major mixed-doubles & doubles semifinals and Masters final

Skupski and Spanish partner [María José Martínez Sánchez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mar%C3%ADa_Jos%C3%A9_Mart%C3%ADnez_S%C3%A1nchez) were defeated in their mixed doubles semifinal at the [2019 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Australian_Open) by third seeded pair and eventual champions [Barbora Krejčíková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbora_Krej%C4%8D%C3%ADkov%C3%A1) and [Rajeev Ram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajeev_Ram).

Following the 2019 French Open, Skupski formed a partnership with fellow Briton [Jamie Murray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Murray).[^cite_note-11] With Murray he reached his first Grand Slam semifinal at the [2019 US Open (tennis)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_US_Open_\(tennis\)) where they lost to the top-seeded Colombian pair and eventual champions [Juan Sebastián Cabal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Sebasti%C3%A1n_Cabal) and [Robert Farah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Farah_\(tennis\)).

He made his first Masters 1000 final at the [2020 Western & Southern Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Western_&_Southern_Open) defeating top seeded pair Cabal/Farah in the first round en route before losing to [Pablo Carreno Busta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_Carreno_Busta) and [Alex de Minaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_de_Minaur) in the final, and won his fourth ATP doubles title at the [2020 Sofia Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Sofia_Open) by a walkover from [Jürgen Melzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%BCrgen_Melzer) and [Édouard Roger-Vasselin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89douard_Roger-Vasselin).

The pair Skupski/Murray also reached the quarterfinals at the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_Open), which was Neal's first showing at this level at this Major and third overall and again the quarterfinals at the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_US_Open_\(tennis\)).

### 2021: Top 15 debut and Wimbledon mixed-doubles champion, Olympics debut

In March, Skupski won his fifth ATP title with his brother Ken at the [Mexican Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Open_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-12] Two weeks later, he reached his second Masters-1000 final with compatriot [Dan Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Evans_\(tennis\)) at the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Miami_Open) and entered the top 20 in the doubles rankings for the first time. Again two weeks later and partnering with Dan Evans, he reached his third Masters 1000 final at the [Monte-Carlo Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Monte-Carlo_Masters) and climbed to a career-high ranking in doubles of world No. 16 on 19 April 2021. On 11 July 2021, partnering with [Desirae Krawczyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desirae_Krawczyk), he won the [Wimbledon mixed-doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Wimbledon_Championships) final.[^cite_note-13] He reached the top 15 in doubles on 12 July 2021.

At the [2021 San Diego Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_San_Diego_Open) Skupski won his sixth title and second of the season partnering [Joe Salisbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Salisbury).[^cite_note-14]

### 2022: Partnership with Koolhof, Four ATP & Three Masters & mixed doubles titles, World No. 1



Skupski (*pictured at the [2022 Monte-Carlo Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Monte-Carlo_Masters)*) ended 2022 as joint-world No. 1 with new partner [Wesley Koolhof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_Koolhof)

Partnering with [Wesley Koolhof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_Koolhof) he won two ATP 250 titles during the Australian Summer swing, before the [2022 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Australian_Open). The pair reached the quarterfinals at the first Grand Slam of the year for the first time at this Major. They won their third title at the [2022 Qatar ExxonMobil Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Qatar_ExxonMobil_Open) dropping only one set en route to the final where they defeated [Rohan Bopanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rohan_Bopanna) and [Denis Shapovalov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Shapovalov) in straight sets.[^cite_note-15] He reached the final of the [2022 Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Miami_Open) with Koolhof where they lost to [John Isner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Isner) and [Hubert Hurkacz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Hurkacz).

Seeded seventh, they reached their second Masters 1000 final at the [2022 Mutua Madrid Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Mutua_Madrid_Open) after defeating John Isner and Hubert Hurkacz.[^cite_note-16] In the final they defeated fifth seeds [Robert Farah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Farah_\(tennis\)) and [Juan Sebastián Cabal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Sebasti%C3%A1n_Cabal) to win their first Masters 1000 title in their career and as a pair.[^cite_note-17]

At the [2022 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_Open) he reached the quarterfinals with Koolhof for the second time at this Major, defeating unseeded pair of Americans [Tommy Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Paul_\(tennis\)) and [Mackenzie McDonald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mackenzie_McDonald).[^cite_note-18] As a result, Skupski entered the top 10 of the [ATP rankings](13480983.md) in doubles for the first time on 6 June 2022.

At the [2022 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles) he successfully defended and won his second Major title in mixed doubles again partnering [Desirae Krawczyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desirae_Krawczyk). They defeated [Matthew Ebden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Ebden) and [Samantha Stosur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samantha_Stosur) in straight sets.[^cite_note-19] He reached the top 5 in the doubles rankings on 18 July 2022.

At the [2022 National Bank Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_National_Bank_Open) he reached with Koolhof the semifinals of a Masters 1000 for the third time in the season defeating [Lloyd Glasspool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd_Glasspool)/[Harri Heliövaara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harri_Heli%C3%B6vaara).[^cite_note-20] Next the pair advanced to the eight final of the season defeating Krawietz/Mies.[^cite_note-21] They won their sixth title defeating [Dan Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Evans_\(tennis\)) and [John Peers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Peers_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-22] As a result, he moved to world No. 4 in the doubles rankings on 15 August 2022 and to No. 3 on 22 August 2022.

Seeded 2nd at the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_\(tennis\)) the pair reached the quarterfinals defeating Wimbledon champions Australian pair of Ebden/Purcell in three sets.[^cite_note-23] Next they defeated [Marcelo Demoliner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcelo_Demoliner) and [Joao Sousa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joao_Sousa) to reach the semifinals.[^cite_note-24] In the semifinals, they defeated [Marcelo Arevalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcelo_Ar%C3%A9valo) and [Jean-Julien Rojer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Julien_Rojer). In the finals, they lost in straight sets to [Rajeev Ram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajeev_Ram) and [Joe Salisbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Salisbury).[^cite_note-25] At the [2022 Rolex Paris Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Rolex_Paris_Masters) the pair Koolhof/Skupski reached the semifinals defeating ninth seeds [Rohan Bopanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rohan_Bopanna)/[Matwe Middelkoop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matwe_Middelkoop) climbing to World No. 1 and World No. 2 and solidifying the No. 1 position as a pair in the doubles race.[^cite_note-26] They reached their 10th final and fourth at a Masters level for the season defeating seventh seeds [Lloyd Glasspool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd_Glasspool) /[Harri Heliövaara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harri_Heli%C3%B6vaara).[^cite_note-27] They won their third Masters title and seventh title for the season defeating eight seeds [Ivan Dodig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Dodig)/[Austin Krajicek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Krajicek) in the final.[^cite_note-28] They also clinched the No. 1 year-end ranking as a team.[^cite_note-29] Skupski became joint world No. 1 with Koolhof in doubles on 14 November 2022.[^cite_note-30]

### 2023: Wimbledon champion, Indian Wells finalist

The world No. 1 duo Koolhof and Skupski continued their good form reaching yet another Masters final at the [2023 BNP Paribas Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_BNP_Paribas_Open) where they lost to [Rohan Bopanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rohan_Bopanna) and [Matthew Ebden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Ebden).

At the [2023 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_French_Open) he reached the quarterfinals with Wesley Koolhof for the third time in his career. He lost to the 10th seeded team of [Horacio Zeballos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horacio_Zeballos) and [Marcel Granollers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Granollers).

He won his first Grand Slam title at the [2023 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Wimbledon_Championships) with Koolhof and returned to the No. 1 ranking.[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32]

### 2024: Two Grand Slam mixed doubles finals

Skupski and [Desirae Krawczyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desirae_Krawczyk) reached the mixed doubles final at the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles) in January losing to [Hsieh Su-wei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hsieh_Su-wei) and [Jan Zielinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Zielinski) 6-7 (5-7) 6-4 11-9.[^cite_note-33] The pair also suffered similar disappointment at the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles) in June, when they lost out in the final 6-4 7–5 to [Laura Siegemund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Siegemund) and [Edouard Roger-Vasselin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edouard_Roger-Vasselin).[^cite_note-34]

At the [Queen's Club Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Queen's_Club_Championships_%E2%80%93_Doubles), he combined with [Michael Venus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Venus) to win the doubles title, defeating [Taylor Fritz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Fritz) and [Karen Khachanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Khachanov) in the final.[^cite_note-35]

### 2025: French and US Open finals, 300th ATP win

In February, Skupski and new regular playing partner Joe Salisbury reached the final at the [Qatar Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Qatar_ExxonMobil_Open_%E2%80%93_Doubles), losing to fellow Britons [Julian Cash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_Cash) and [Lloyd Glasspool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd_Glasspool) in straight sets.[^cite_note-36] They were also runners-up at the [Barcelona Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Barcelona_Open_Banc_Sabadell_%E2%80%93_Doubles) in April, losing to [Sander Arends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sander_Arends) and [Luke Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Johnson_\(tennis\)) in the final.[^cite_note-37] During the tournament Skupski recorded his 300th win on the ATP Tour.[^cite_note-38]

Skupski and Salisbury were runners-up at the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), losing in the final to [Marcel Granollers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Granollers) and [Horacio Zeballos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horacio_Zeballos) in three sets.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40]

In August, they made it to the final at the [Canadian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_National_Bank_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), but lost to second seeds Julian Cash and Lloyd Glasspool.[^cite_note-41]

They reached the final at the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), but, in a repeat of the French Open final earlier that year, lost to Marcel Granollers and Horacio Zeballos in three sets despite having three championship points during the match.[^cite_note-42]

Skupski and Salisbury went unbeaten to top their group at the season-ending [ATP Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_ATP_Finals_%E2%80%93_Doubles) in [Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin)[^cite_note-43] and then defeated world No. 1 pairing Julian Cash and Lloyd Glasspool in the semifinals,[^cite_note-44] before losing to Harri Heliövaara and Henry Patten in the final in straight sets.[^cite_note-45] Two days after the final, Salisbury announced he was taking a break from tennis until at least April 2026 due to anxiety and it was revealed that Skupski would begin the new season playing alongside [Christian Harrison](38874563.md).[^cite_note-46]

### 2026: Australian and US Open doubles titles

Seeded sixth, and playing just their second tournament together, Skupski and Christian Harrison won the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), defeating wildcard entrants [Jason Kubler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Kubler) and [Marc Polmans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Polmans) in the final.[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48] In September, they defeated [Kevin Krawietz](23521626.md) and [Tim Pütz](41501290.md) in the final to win the [US Open](84129203.md).[^cite_note-49]

## World TeamTennis

Skupski has played five seasons with [World TeamTennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_TeamTennis) starting in 2015 when he made his league debut with the [California Dream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Dream_\(tennis\)). He has since played four seasons (2016-2019) for the [New York Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Empire_\(tennis\)). Skupski was a part of the [New York Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Empire_\(tennis\)), who claimed the King Trophy during 2020 WTT season at [The Greenbrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Greenbrier).[^cite_note-50]

## Personal life

Skupski is the younger brother of former tennis player [Ken Skupski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Skupski) and the pair regularly competed together until 2021, most notably winning the [2021 Mexican Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Abierto_Mexicano_Telcel_%E2%80%93_Doubles).

Skupski is considered [Andy Murray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Murray)'s biggest rival in their [Fantasy Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantasy_Premier_League), "His knowledge isn't that high level, but he spends hours on the apps and websites."[^cite_note-51]

Skupski has stated that he is an avid and lifelong [Liverpool F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.) fan; he had on occasion asked for Liverpool's scoreline during his concurrent tennis matches.[^cite_note-52]
