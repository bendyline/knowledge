# European Union Special Representative

&#x20; The **European Union Special Representatives** (**EUSR**) are emissaries of the [European Union](9317.md) with specific tasks abroad. While the [EU's ambassadors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambassadors_of_the_European_Union) are responsible for affairs with a single country, Special Representatives tackle specific issues, conflict areas or regions of countries. They answer directly to the [High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy](750942.md), currently [Kaja Kallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaja_Kallas).

## Current SRs by region

### Europe

#### Bosnia and Herzegovina

[Peter Sørensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_S%C3%B8rensen_\(diplomat\)) took over the position of EUSR in [Bosnia and Herzegovina](3463.md) with a mandate from 1 September 2011 until 30 June 2015.[^cite_note-1] His post was de-coupled from the one of [High Representative for Bosnia and Herzegovina](2386573.md) (which remained in the hands of [Valentin Inzko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentin_Inzko)), aiming at fostering the EU pre-accession strategy for Bosnia and Herzegovina.[^cite_note-2] However, in November 2014, Sørensen became Head of the EU Delegation in Geneva, leaving the EUSR BiH post vacant.[^cite_note-3] [Lars-Gunnar Wigemark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars-Gunnar_Wigemark) was appointed as the EUSR in Bosnia and Herzegovina from 1 March 2015 until 31 October 2015.[^cite_note-4] His mandate was extended until 30 June 2018[^cite_note-europa.eu-5] and again until 31 August 2019.[^cite_note-6] [Johann Sattler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Sattler) replaced him in 2019, with a mandate from 1 September 2019 to 31 August 2023.[^cite_note-7] His mandate was extended until 31 August 2024.[^cite_note-8] [Luigi Soreca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Soreca) is the current Special Representative, serving since 1 September 2024.[^cite_note-9]

#### Kosovo

[Tomáš Szunyog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom%C3%A1%C5%A1_Szunyog?action=edit\&redlink=1) was appointed as Special Representative in [Kosovo](17391.md) on 30 July 2020.[^cite_note-10] His mandate runs from 1 September 2020 until 31 August 2023.[^cite_note-11] His mandate was extended until 31 August 2024.[^cite_note-12]

#### South Caucasus and the Crisis in Georgia

See also: [EU Strategy for the South Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU_Strategy_for_the_South_Caucasus)

[Toivo Klaar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toivo_Klaar?action=edit\&redlink=1) was appointed Special Representative for the [South Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Caucasus) and the [crisis in Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_South_Ossetia_war#Six-point_peace_plan) on 13 November 2017. His mandate was extended until 29 February 2020,[^cite_note-13] and again until 28 February 2021.[^cite_note-14] His mandate was again extended until 28 February 2022,[^cite_note-15] and then again until 31 August 2022,[^cite_note-16] and then again until 31 August 2023.[^cite_note-17] and then until 31 August 2024.[^cite_note-18] Magdalena Grono was appointed Special Representative on 1 November 2024.[^cite_note-19]

#### Belgrade-Pristina Dialogue and other Western Balkan regional issues

On 3 April 2020, [Miroslav Lajčák](12007291.md) was appointed by the EU Council as Special Representative for the [Belgrade-Pristina Dialogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade-Pri%C5%A1tina_dialogue) and other Western Balkan regional issues. His 12-month mandate includes the tasks to achieve comprehensive normalisation of the relations between Serbia and Kosovo, improve good neighbourly relations and reconciliation between partners in the [Western Balkans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Balkans), helping them overcome the legacy of the past, and contribute to the consistency and effectiveness of EU action in the Western Balkans.[^cite_note-20] His mandate runs from 2 April 2020 until 31 August 2022.[^cite_note-21] His mandate was renewed until 31 August 2024.[^cite_note-22]

### Asia

#### Central Asia

[Terhi Hakala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terhi_Hakala) is the Special Representative for [Central Asia](6742.md).[^cite_note-23] Her mandate is to promote good relations between the EU and central Asian countries and to strengthen stability, cooperation, democracy and respect for human rights in the region. In particular, the EU Special Representative will co-ordinate EU action in central Asia and oversee the implementation of the EU Strategy for central Asia. Her mandate runs from 1 July 2021 to 28 February 2023. Her mandate was extended to 28 February 2025.[^cite_note-24]

#### Gulf

On 1 June 2023, [Luigi Di Maio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Di_Maio), former Italian [Minister of Foreign Affairs](3051160.md), was appointed Special Representative of the [European Union](9317.md) for the [Persian Gulf](24761.md).[^cite_note-25] His aims will be to further develop a stronger, comprehensive and more strategic EU partnership with the countries in the Gulf region. He will also contribute to the stability and security of the region by engaging and supporting dialogue and long-term regional solutions with individual Gulf partners and relevant regional organisations.[^cite_note-26] His mandate initially run until 28 February 2025,[^cite_note-27] but, in January 2025, Borrell's successor [Kaja Kallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaja_Kallas) confirmed Di Maio as Special Representative for two more years.[^cite_note-28]

#### Middle East

See also: [Palestine–European Union relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine%E2%80%93European_Union_relations)

[Sven Koopmans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sven_Koopmans) has been appointed Special Representative for the [Middle East peace process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East_peace_process). His mandate runs from 1 May 2021 until 28 February 2023.[^cite_note-\:1-29] Between 2017 and 2021 "he was a Member of Parliament in the Netherlands, where he was spokesperson on foreign affairs and head of delegation in the NATO Parliamentary Assembly."[^cite_note-30] The mandate of the Special Representative is based on the EU's policy objectives regarding the [Middle East peace process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East_peace_process), which include a two-State solution with [Israel](9282173.md) and a democratic, contiguous, viable, peaceful and sovereign [Palestinian State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_State) living side by side within secure and recognised borders enjoying normal relations with their neighbours in accordance with [UN Security Council Resolutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Security_Council_Resolution) [242](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_242), [338](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_338), [1397](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_1397) and [1402](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_1402) and the principles of the [Madrid Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid_Conference). His mandate was extended until 28 February 2025.[^cite_note-\:2-31]

### Africa

#### Horn of Africa (incl. the region of Sudan)

[Annette Weber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annette_Weber) was appointed Special Representative for the [Horn of Africa](62142.md) on 1 July 2021.[^cite_note-32] Her mandate runs until 31 August 2022.[^cite_note-33] Her mandate was extended until 31 August 2024.[^cite_note-34]

#### Sahel

[João Gomes Cravinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Gomes_Cravinho) was appointed as the Special Representative for the [Sahel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahel) on 18 November 2024, succeeding Emanuela Claudia Del Re, whose mandate started in June 2021.[^cite_note-35] His inititial mandate will run for 21 months, until 31 August 2026.[^cite_note-36]

### Global

#### Human Rights

A long-standing request[^cite_note-37] for a representative that would be in charge of enhancing the effectiveness and visibility of EU's [human rights](13831.md) policy, based on the Strategic Framework and Action Plan on Human Rights and Democracy (officially adopted on 25 June 2012),[^cite_note-38] led to the creation of the post of the Special Representative of the European Union for Human Rights. The post, which is providing a strong, independent, flexible and sufficiently broad mandate, is aiming to cover fields such as the strengthening of democracy, International justice, humanitarian law and the abolition of the death penalty.

On 26 February 2024, [Olof Skoog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olof_Skoog) was appointed as the European Union Special Representative for human rights, with a mandate from 1 March 2024 until 28 February 2026.[^cite_note-39]
