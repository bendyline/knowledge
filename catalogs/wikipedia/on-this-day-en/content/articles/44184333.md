**kt Rolster** is a South Korean multi-gaming organization founded in 1999 with [Korea Telecom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KT_Corporation) as its head sponsor. A member of the [Korean e-Sports Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_e-Sports_Association), KT Rolster holds one of the richest and most successful *StarCraft* teams in the history, as well as one of the most successful *[League of Legends](21838352.md)* teams in Korea. The StarCraft team has fielded several legendary players throughout the pro-gaming scenes of *[StarCraft: Brood War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StarCraft:_Brood_War)* and *[StarCraft II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StarCraft_II)*, such as [Flash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Young-ho), [Nal rA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nal_rA), [YellOw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Jin-ho), [NaDa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NaDa), [NesTea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NesTea), [Reach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Jung-suk_\(electronic_sports\)), [Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Seung-hyun_\(electronic_sports\)), TY and Zest.

## History

When the organization was created in 1999, it consisted solely of a *StarCraft* team which was named KTF n016 Progame Team after its head sponsor, [Korea Telecom Freetel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Telecom_Freetel?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-1] In 2001, the team was renamed KTF MagicNs. When the sponsor merged with Korean Telecom, the organization subsequently went through several name changes and eventually ended up as KT Rolster (short for "[Roller Coaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roller_Coaster)") in August 2009. The team transitioned to Starcraft II during the hybrid 2011-2012 Proleague Season 2.[^cite_note-2]

In October 2012, kt Rolster added two *League of Legends* teams with members from teams NaJin Shield and the recently disbanded StarTale.[^cite_note-3]

Having reached the quarterfinals of the [League of Legends World Championship](42058214.md) on three occasions ([2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_League_of_Legends_World_Championship), [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_League_of_Legends_World_Championship), [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_League_of_Legends_World_Championship)), the organization achieved its highest placement in international competition after making the finals of the [2025 League of Legends World Championship](78265754.md).
