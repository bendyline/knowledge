**Garwolin County** ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *powiat garwoliński*) is a unit of territorial administration and local government ([powiat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powiat)) in [Masovian Voivodeship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masovian_Voivodeship), east-central [Poland](22936.md). It came into being on 1 January 1999, as a result of the [Polish local government reforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_local_government_reforms) passed in 1998. Its administrative seat and largest town is [Garwolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garwolin), which lies 56 kilometres (35 mi) south-east of [Warsaw](32908.md). The county contains three other towns: [Łaskarzew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%81askarzew), 13 km (8 mi) south of Garwolin, [Pilawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilawa), 10 km (6 mi) north-west of Garwolin, and [Żelechów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%BBelech%C3%B3w), 22 km (14 mi) south-east of Garwolin.

The county covers an area of 1,284.29 square kilometres (495.9 sq mi). As of 2019 its total population is 104,917, out of which the population of Garwolin is 17,501, that of Łaskarzew is 4,840, that of Pilawa is 4,578, that of Żelechów is 3,988, and the rural population is 74,010.[^cite_note-population-1]

## Neighbouring counties

Garwolin County is bordered by [Mińsk County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mi%C5%84sk_County) to the north, [Siedlce County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siedlce_County) to the north-east, [Łuków County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%81uk%C3%B3w_County) to the east, [Ryki County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryki_County) to the south-east, [Kozienice County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kozienice_County) to the south, [Grójec County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gr%C3%B3jec_County) to the west and [Otwock County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otwock_County) to the north-west.
