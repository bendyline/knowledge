**Kiryandongo District** is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Uganda) in [Western Uganda](25370326.md). Like many other [Ugandan](31816.md) districts, it is named after its 'chief town', [Kiryandongo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiryandongo), where the district headquarters are located.

## Location

Kiryandongo District is bordered by [Nwoya District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nwoya_District) to the north, [Oyam District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyam_District) to the northeast, [Apac District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apac_District) to the east, and [Masindi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masindi_District) to the south and west. [Kiryandongo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiryandongo), the location of the district headquarters, lies approximately 225 kilometres (140 mi) northwest of [Kampala](56689.md), Uganda's capital and largest city.[^cite_note-1] The coordinates of the district are\:02 00N, 32 18E (Latitude\:2.0000; Longitude\:32.3000).

Kiryandongo District is made up of several Administrative units and these include Counties, Sub counties, Parishes and Villages.[^cite_note-2]

## Overview

Kiryandongo District was established on 1 July 2010. It was previously part of [Masindi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masindi_District). The district is part of [Bunyoro sub-region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunyoro_sub-region), which is coterminous with [Bunyoro Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunyoro). The districts that constitute Bunyoro sub-region include the following: 1. [Buliisa District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buliisa_District) 2. [Hoima District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoima_District) 3. [Kibaale District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kibaale_District) 4. Kiryandongo District and 5. [Masindi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masindi_District). In 2002, the sub-region was home to an estimated 750,000 inhabitants, according to the national population and household census conducted that year.

## Population

The 1991 Uganda national census estimated the district population at 83,405. According to the 2002 national census, that population had increased to about 187,700. In 2012, the population of Kiryandongo District was estimated at 317,500.[^cite_note-3] A new national census is planned for August 2014.[^cite_note-4] in 2014 the population was estimated as 266,197, and in 2024 it was estimated as 364,872.[^cite_note-5]
