The **premier of Alberta** is the [head of government](37570.md) and [first minister](183220.md) of the [Canadian province](75763.md) of [Alberta](717.md). The current [premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_\(Canada\)) is [Danielle Smith](23078986.md), leader of the governing [United Conservative Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Conservative_Party), who was [sworn in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oath_of_office) on October 11, 2022.[^cite_note-1]

The premier of Alberta is the leader of the [legislative majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsible_government) in the [Alberta Legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_of_Alberta). The premier selects [ministers of the Crown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministers_of_the_Crown) to the [Executive Council of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Council_of_Alberta) (the "[cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_\(government\))") and as such is the [head of the government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_the_government) in Alberta. The premier is the [first minister](183220.md) representing Alberta towards the [federal government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Ministers'_Conference) and the other [provinces and territories in Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_the_Federation).

By [constitutional convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_convention_\(political_custom\)) the premier is normally a [Member of the Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Legislative_Assembly) (MLA).

## Duties and functions

To be effective, accountable and in line with custom, the premier is expected to hold a seat in the legislature, so the premier serves as the MLA for a [riding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_district_\(Canada\)) and is elected as MLA by the constituents of that constituency. As with most government leaders in a [parliamentary system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system), the premier usually wins their own election as MLA easily. However, on occasion, a premier has not been re-elected to their seat in a general election, forcing them to run in a [by-election](155434.md) in a constituency that comes empty by the resignation of the sitting MLA or other incident. The most recent case of this was [Don Getty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Getty), who lost his [Edmonton-Whitemud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmonton-Whitemud) seat in the [1989 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Alberta_general_election) and then ran and was elected in a by-election in [Stettler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stettler_\(provincial_electoral_district\)). In 2022 Danielle Smith was elected party leader without holding a seat in the legislative assembly, thus becoming premier, and within a few weeks won a seat in a by-election.
