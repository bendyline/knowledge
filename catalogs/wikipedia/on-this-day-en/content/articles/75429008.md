[Jannik Sinner](60061043.md) won his second consecutive [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) title, defeating [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md). He then defeated two-time defending champion [Carlos Alcaraz](63121147.md) at [Wimbledon](80007709.md) to win his fourth career major title.



[Carlos Alcaraz](63121147.md) won his second consecutive [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) title and fifth major overall, saving three championship points and coming back from a two-set deficit against [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md) in [the final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles_final). He then won his second [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) title and sixth major overall, defeating the defending champion, Sinner, in the final.

The **2025 ATP Tour** was the global elite men's professional [tennis](29773.md) circuit organized by the [Association of Tennis Professionals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) (ATP) for the 2025 tennis season. The 2025 ATP Tour calendar comprised the [Grand Slam](197638.md) tournaments, supervised by the [International Tennis Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Tennis_Federation) (ITF); the [ATP Finals](1209231.md); the [ATP Tour Masters 1000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour_Masters_1000); the [ATP 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour_500); the [ATP 250](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour_250); and the [United Cup](72152971.md) (organized with the [WTA](57768.md)). Also included in the 2025 calendar were the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup) (organized by the ITF), [Next Gen ATP Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Next_Gen_ATP_Finals), [Hopman Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopman_Cup) and [Laver Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laver_Cup), none of which distribute ranking points.&#x20;
