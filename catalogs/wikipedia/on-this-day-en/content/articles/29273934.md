**Hays District** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مديرية حيس) is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Yemen) of the [Al Hudaydah Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Hudaydah_Governorate), [Yemen](350939.md). As of 2003, the district had a population of 45,436 inhabitants.[^cite_note-1]
