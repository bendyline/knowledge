On August 31, 2026, a stabbing attack occurred in [Times Square](31389.md), [Manhattan](45470.md), New York City, United States. A woman, [Bank of America](347756.md) [vice president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_president) Erin Piacenti was killed, and a man was injured.[^cite_note-1] Police shot and killed the perpetrator, 49-year-old Pamela Cisneros.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

## Attack

Pamela Cisneros, a 49-year-old woman who had a history of mental health issues, stabbed two people in Times Square. She killed Erin Piacenti, who was a vice president of Bank of America, and critically injured a 68-year-old man.[^cite_note-3] Police attempted to subdue Cisneros with stun guns, but two officers ultimately shot and killed her. The two victims and Cisneros were all taken to [Bellevue Hospital](1839902.md), where Cisneros and Piacenti were pronounced dead.[^cite_note-\:0-2]
