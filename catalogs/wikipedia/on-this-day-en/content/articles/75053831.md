The [ice hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_hockey_at_the_Olympic_Games) competitions of the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md) was played at two venues located in the [Milan](36511.md) cluster: the [PalaItalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PalaItalia) and one of the [Fiera Milano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiera_Milano) pavilions.

The men's tournament had 12 teams competing, and the women's tournament 10.[^cite_note-system-1] [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_Hockey_Federation_of_Russia) and [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusian_Ice_Hockey_Association) were banned from competing in international championships through the [International Ice Hockey Federation](16760449.md)'s (IIHF) 2026 program due to their [military aggression in Ukraine](70149799.md);[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-ESPN-May2025-3] the [International Olympic Committee](15147.md) ruled that Russian and Belarusian could compete as "[Individual Neutral Athletes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individual_Neutral_Athletes_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics)" (AIN) but not in team events.[^cite_note-4]

The [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics) won gold medals both in the men's and women's tournaments for the first time in the finals against [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics), both in [overtime](633242.md#Ice_hockey), while [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics) won bronze in the women's tournament and [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics) in the men's tournament.

## Men's tournament

Main article: [Ice hockey at the 2026 Winter Olympics – Men's tournament](77611835.md)

The tournament featured twelve countries, eight qualifying through the [IIHF World Ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIHF_World_Ranking), the host [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_men's_national_ice_hockey_team), and three through qualifying tournaments. The format remained the same as the previous four Olympics; three groups of four competed in three games to determine seeding, each played every other team in their group, followed by four rounds of elimination games. Each group winner received a bye into the second round, along with the highest ranked of the remaining teams. The remaining eight teams played an eliminating qualification game to advance to the quarter-final round. Each quarter-final winner advanced to the semi-finals, with the winners playing for the gold medal and the losers playing for the bronze.

On 2 February 2024, the IIHF announced that an agreement had been reached with the [National Hockey League](21809.md) (NHL) for a break in its regular season to allow the league's players to participate in the Olympics for the first time since 2014. The NHL was originally scheduled to compete in 2022 as well but opted out due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-6]

### Qualification

Main article: [Ice hockey at the 2026 Winter Olympics – Men's qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_hockey_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_qualification)

Qualification for the men's tournament at the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md) was determined by the [IIHF World Ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIHF_World_Ranking) following the [2023 Men's Ice Hockey World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Men's_Ice_Hockey_World_Championships). The top eight joined the hosts and three qualifiers.[^cite_note-system-1]

### Notable firsts

Although not an official award, the first known [Gordie Howe hat trick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordie_Howe_hat_trick) in Olympic ice hockey history was achieved by [Tom Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Wilson_\(ice_hockey\)) of [Team Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_men's_national_ice_hockey_team) on 15 February 2026, when he fought [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_men's_national_ice_hockey_team) skater [Pierre Crinon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Crinon) in retaliation for Crinon's forearm hit to [Nathan MacKinnon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_MacKinnon)'s jaw earlier in the game.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] Wilson and Crinon were both ejected from the game for fighting.[^cite_note-11] It was Wilson's first Olympic goal that contributed to the feat.[^cite_note-12]

## Women's tournament

Main article: [Ice hockey at the 2026 Winter Olympics – Women's tournament](77611836.md)
