The **Province of Granada** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Provincia de Granada*) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Spain) in the eastern part of the [autonomous community](55002.md) of [Andalusia](2736.md) in [Spain](26667.md). It is bordered by the provinces of [Albacete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albacete_\(province\)), [Murcia](20515376.md), [Almería](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almer%C3%ADa_\(province\)), [Jaén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ja%C3%A9n_\(Spanish_province\)), [Córdoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%B3rdoba_\(Spanish_province\)), [Málaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1laga_\(province\)), and the [Mediterranean Sea](19006.md) (along the [Costa Tropical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Tropical)). Its capital is the city of [Granada](38264.md).

The province has a population of 945,797 in an area of 12,645.41 km<sup>2</sup> (4,882.42 sq mi) across its [174 municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_municipalities_in_Granada).[^cite_note-area-1][^cite_note-population-2]

## Geography

The tallest mountain in the [Iberian Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberian_Peninsula), [Mulhacén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulhac%C3%A9n), is located in Granada. It measures 3,479 m (11,414 ft).[^cite_note-3] The next highest mountains in the province are Veleta (3,396 m or 11,142 ft)[^cite_note-4] and Alcazaba (3,371 m or 11,060 ft).

The river [Genil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genil), which rises in Granada, is one of the main tributaries of the [Guadalquivir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guadalquivir). Other important rivers include the Fardes, Monachil, [Guadalfeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guadalfeo), Dílar, Ízbor, Verde and Darro.

Granada shares the [Sierra Nevada National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Nevada_National_Park_\(Spain\)) (in the [Sierra Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Nevada_\(Spain\)) mountain range) with Almería province. Another important range is the [Sierra de Baza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_de_Baza).

The northern part of the province, comprising the districts of [Baza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baza,_Granada) and [Huescar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huescar) is known as the [Granada Altiplano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altiplano_de_Granada).

## Economy

Primary industries such as agriculture and fishing play an important role in the local economy. There was previously also a very large construction sector, but as of 2011 it was experiencing a deep downturn. The Granada Health Science Technological Park has created some high-skilled jobs in the biotechnology sector.

### Tourism

The coast of Granada province is known as the [Costa Tropical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Tropical). It attracts large numbers of both Spanish and foreign holiday-makers. The main resorts are [Almuñecar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almu%C3%B1ecar), [Salobreña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salobre%C3%B1a) and [La Herradura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Herradura). The city of Granada brings in tourists from all over the world thanks to its [Moorish architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moorish_architecture), especially the famous [Alhambra](30543.md) palace. In the winter the mountains of the [Sierra Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Nevada_\(Spain\)) play host to [Europe's most southerly ski resort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Nevada_Ski_Station). Hiking and eco-tourism also attract a number of visitors to areas such as the [Alpujarras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpujarras) and [Lecrin Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecrin_Valley).

### Major employers

**Puleva**: founded in Granada in 1910, the company has been a subsidiary of the [Lactalis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lactalis) group since 2001. It produces a wide range of dairy products, including milk and milkshakes, cream and products for infants. Puleva Biotech S.A. developed the first infant formula to contain nucleotides, and was a pioneer in the use of Omega-3 fatty acids in infant food products.[^cite_note-6]

**Coviran**: supermarket chain which operates on a franchise basis, specialising in small, neighbourhood shops. It has 2,501 supermarkets across the whole of Spain and Portugal.[^cite_note-7]

### University and language students

The [University of Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Granada) is one of the largest universities in Spain, with approximately 56,000 students.[^cite_note-8] Each year the city also attracts over 10,000 foreign students, including over 2,000 European students through the Erasmus programme. As well as providing employment to a large number of people, the university also boosts the local economy by creating demand for rental property. The money spent by students also helps to support shops, restaurants, bars and other services in the city. In recent years the university has also been working to capitalise on the research it does through commercial ventures.

## Transport

Air travel for the province is served by [Federico García Lorca Granada Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federico_Garc%C3%ADa_Lorca_Granada_Airport) which provides direct routes to other parts of Spain and some European destinations. However, other nearby airports such as [Málaga Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1laga_Airport), [Seville Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seville_Airport), [Córdoba Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%B3rdoba_Airport) and [Región de Murcia International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regi%C3%B3n_de_Murcia_International_Airport) are frequently used by air travellers from the province.

## Important monuments

The [Alhambra](30543.md) World Heritage Site, Granada's biggest tourist attraction, showcases one of the very finest architectural legacies of [Moorish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moors) rule in Spain, which in Granada lasted from 711 until 1492.

There are [Roman Catholic](606848.md) cathedrals at Granada and [Guadix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guadix).

The [Royal Chapel of Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Chapel_of_Granada) houses the remains of the [Catholic Monarchs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Monarchs), [Isabella I of Castile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabella_I_of_Castile) (1451–1504) and [Ferdinand II of Aragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_II_of_Aragon) (1452–1516); as well as of their daughter [Joanna of Castile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanna_of_Castile) (1479–1555) and of her husband [Philip the Handsome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_I_of_Castile) (69–70).
