# Odenplan



Odenplan with [Gustaf Vasa Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustaf_Vasa_Church) in the background. 2006



Odenplan commuter train station. 2017

**Odenplan** is a [plaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaza) located in the [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District) [Vasastaden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasastaden,_Stockholm) in central [Stockholm](26741.md), [Sweden](5058739.md).

## History

Odenplan was named after the old [Norse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norse_mythology) god [Odin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odin). [Odenplan metro station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odenplan_station), opened in 1952, and Stockholm Odenplan commuter train station on the [Stockholm commuter rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_commuter_rail), opened in 2017, are located here.[^cite_note-1]

Odenplan was portrayed in the 1976 thriller film *[The Man on the Roof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Man_on_the_Roof)*, in which a helicopter crashes in the plaza. It also appears in the opening credits to the 1999 film *[Vuxna människor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vuxna_m%C3%A4nniskor)*.

[Stockholm Public Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_Public_Library) and Gustaf Vasa Church (*Gustaf Vasa kyrka*) are located nearby.[^cite_note-2]
