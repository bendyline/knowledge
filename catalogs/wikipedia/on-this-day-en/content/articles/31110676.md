**Air Canada Express** is a [brand name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brand_name) of regional feeder flights for [Air Canada](145623.md) that are subcontracted to other airlines. Presently, [Jazz Aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz_Aviation) and [PAL Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PAL_Airlines) are the sole operators of Air Canada Express flights. They primarily connect smaller cities with Air Canada's domestic [hub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airline_hub) airports and [focus cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airline_hub#Focus_city), although they offer some [point-to-point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-to-point_transit) and international service to the United States.

## History



Air Canada Express [Embraer E175](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embraer_E-Jet_family) in a former livery

On April 26, 2011, [Air Canada](145623.md) announced its intention to retire the [Air Canada Jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz_Aviation) brand and create the Air Canada Express brand.[^cite_note-3] Prior to establishing the Express name, the flights operated primarily under the Air Canada Jazz or [Air Canada Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Canada_Alliance) banners.

As of January 2020, [Air Georgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Georgian) no longer provided services under the capacity purchase agreement. Those services were transferred back to Jazz Aviation. On March 1, 2021, it was also announced that [Sky Regional Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Regional_Airlines) would also no longer provide services under the capacity purchase agreement and therefore [Jazz Aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz_Aviation) would become the sole operator of the express brand.[^cite_note-\:0-4]

However, in May 2023, Air Canada signed a letter of intent with [PAL Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PAL_Airlines) for a five-year capacity purchase agreement. This deal involved the purchase of up to six [Bombardier Q400s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Havilland_Canada_Dash_8) from Air Canada, which PAL Airlines would operate under the Air Canada Express brand in addition to their existing schedule and charter business.[^cite_note-5] In the announcement, Air Canada described the prospective agreement with PAL Airlines as a "bridging arrangement" due to a need for additional regional capacity as a result of industry-wide pilot shortages. Service with PAL began in July 2023.[^cite_note-6] On January 9, 2026, Air Canada and PAL renewed the agreement through 2032, adding five additional Q400s into the fleet, as well as protecting services from [Montréal–Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montr%C3%A9al%E2%80%93Trudeau_International_Airport) to destinations in Quebec and New Brunswick.[^cite_note-7]

## Incidents and accidents

- On April 20, 2016, Air Canada Express flight 7804 (EV7804), which was operated by an [EVAS Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EVAS_Air) [Beechcraft 1900D](2192224.md) (C-FEVA), crashed upon landing at [Gander International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gander_International_Airport). At the time of arrival, the weather conditions were described as heavy snow, and visibility at the airport was 1⁄8 statute mile (200 m). The flight, arriving from [Goose Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CFB_Goose_Bay), touched down right of the runway centerline of runway 03, and immediately veered right. The nose gear of the aircraft struck a snow windrow and subsequently collapsed, causing 7 out of the 8 blades to separate from the engines. The right side of the fuselage was punctured by a propeller blade. Of the 14 occupants onboard, everyone survived, and 3 people sustained minor injuries.

  [^cite_note-12]

- On December 28, 2024, a [De Havilland Canada DHC-8-400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Havilland_Canada_Dash_8#Series_400) operating as Flight 2259 by [PAL Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PAL_Airlines), had its left main landing gear collapse on landing at [Halifax Stanfield International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax_Stanfield_International_Airport). The wing also caught on fire, but all 77 people on board survived without serious injuries. The aircraft was substantially damaged.

  [^cite_note-13]

- On March 22, 2026, a [Bombardier CRJ-900](11920355.md), operated by [Jazz Aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz_Aviation) as [Air Canada Express Flight 8646](82758586.md), collided with a fire truck on landing at New York's [LaGuardia Airport](248547.md), completely ripping off the nose section of the aircraft. Both pilots died and 41 people were injured in the accident. The 2 occupants of the fire truck were also injured.&#x20;

  [^cite_note-14]
