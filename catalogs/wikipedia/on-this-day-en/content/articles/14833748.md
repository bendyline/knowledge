**Nepal Police** ([Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): नेपाल प्रहरी, romanized: *Nēpāl praharī*) is the national and primary [national law enforcement agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_in_Nepal) of [Nepal](171166.md), operating under the [Ministry of Home Affairs](41156390.md). It is primarily responsible for maintaining [law and order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_and_order_\(politics\)), prevention of crime and crime investigation within the jurisdiction determined by the [Constitution of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal). Apart from its primary role, Nepal Police conducts a vast array of duties such as VIP protection, Security of Vital Installations, Traffic management, Secret services, Intelligence collection, Riot Control, Disaster management, Hostage rescue and various other Ceremonial roles.&#x20;

## History

### Before the Rana Regime (1864–1951 AD)

During the Rana Regime, little was done to institutionalize policing, establishing Milisiya, Thana police Chakki, office of police director general etc. which gradually shaped the Nepal Police into this modern "Nepal Police".[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

### During the period 1951–1990

Nepal saw the dawn of [democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy) after the fall of the Rana regime. The Police Headquarters was established in 1952 in [Kathmandu](17168.md). Mr. Toran Shamsher J.B. Rana was appointed the first Inspector General of Police. The Police Act, 2012 BS (1955 AD) came into effect. The Police Regulation, 2015 BS (1959 AD) came into effect.

The Parliamentary Government under the multi-party system was adopted for some years which was followed by [Panchayat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panchayat_\(Nepal\)) System since 1960. The establishment of the Central Police Training Centre in 1963 A.D.[^cite_note-5]

### 1990–present

The people's democratic movement of 1990 reinstated the multi-party democratic system. The new constitution of the kingdom was promulgated on 9 November 1991. The Police Reform Commission was constituted in the year 1992 and the modernisation of the police started to come in tune with the aspirations of the people and norms of a multi-party system. The first contingent of police personnel was deployed on a UN mission in 1991. More than 2000 police personnel have already served the international community in blue helmets.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## Head of Nepal Police

Main article: [Inspector General of Police (Nepal)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector_General_of_Police_\(Nepal\))

The Nepalese Police is headed by the [Inspector General of Police of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector_general_of_police_\(Nepal\)). He reports directly to the [Ministry of Home Affairs](41156390.md) and is appointed by the [Government of Nepal](19233409.md) for a tenure of four years, although two IGPs have served for six years.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

The current IGP is Dan Bahadur Karki since 13 November 2025.[^cite_note-11]

## Organizational structure

Nepal Police is structured into various branches and specialized units to address the country's diverse security, investigative, and administrative needs. There are four [Bureaus](#Specialized_Units_\(Bureaus\)) and five [Departments](#Departments) in the Police Headquarters that function to undertake the policing activities of Nepal Police. These branches are designed to uphold public safety, enforce laws, and respond effectively to emerging threats across both urban and rural areas.

The Nepal Police has total of 79,554 police personnel, and has 2,344 permanent and 507 temporary police offices and units spread all over the country.

### [Nepal Police Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal_Police_Hospital)

[Nepal Police Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal_Police_Hospital) was inaugurated by the [late king Birendra Bir Bikram Shah Dev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birendra_of_Nepal) on the 27th of Chaitra, 2040 BS. It was established to provide free health services to in-service policemen, their families, and ex-servicemen and their spouses. The hospital commenced OPD services and 25 bedded indoor services through 5 medical doctors, a few nurses, paramedics and administrative staff. The infrastructure then was built with the financial support of the Government of Nepal and the voluntary contribution of the police personnel. Some instruments and equipment were donated by the [Government of India](553883.md) and a Japanese club.&#x20;

Head: **AIGP&#x20;**[^cite_note-15]

The National Police Academy of Nepal, formerly known as the *Sadar Prahari Talim Kendra*, is an academic wing of the Nepal Police. It is solely responsible for conducting basic and advanced training programs for the [police officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_officer) in Nepal. The NPA is the apex body of all Nepal Police Training Institutions in the country.

## Uniform

The uniform of the Nepal Police consists of both summer and winter gear.[^cite_note-16]

Regular officers wear a light blue shirt with navy blue pants. Junior ranks wear [beret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beret) while high-ranked officers wear [Peaked cap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peaked_cap) (with a blue band) with most senior officers with braids on the peak.

Riot police (Special Task Force Police) wear blue DPM pattern camouflage uniforms will ball caps. The winter uniform adds a blue jacket or sweater.
