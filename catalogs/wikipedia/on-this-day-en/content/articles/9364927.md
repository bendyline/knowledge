The **secretary-general of Hezbollah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الأمين العام لحزب الله, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *al-’amīn al-‘ām li-Ḥizb Allāh*) is the highest position within [Hezbollah](13919.md), a [Lebanese Shia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims) [Islamist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islamism) political party and [paramilitary](146730.md) group.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

The current holder of the position is [Naim Qassem](28105310.md), who was appointed on 29 October 2024, a month after the [assassination of his predecessor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Hezbollah_headquarters_strike), [Hassan Nasrallah](313436.md), whom Qassem served as [deputy secretary-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Secretary-General_of_Hezbollah) under.[^cite_note-3]
