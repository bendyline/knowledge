**Loroum** is one of the 45 [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Burkina_Faso) of Burkina Faso, a landlocked country in West Africa. It is located in its [Nord Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nord_Region_\(Burkina_Faso\)) with its seat at the city of [Titao](3905537.md). As of 2019, it had a population of 198,192 inhabitants. The province experiences a [tropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_climate) with distinct wet and dry seasons, and its economy is primarily based on [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture).

## History

The region has a history that includes the presence of early hunter-gatherer societies dating back to the [Neolithic Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic_Age). Various ethnic groups, including the [Mossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossi_people), [Fula](102383.md), and [Dioula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dioula_people), settled in the area in successive waves between the 8th and 15th centuries CE. The territory came under French control, becoming a French protectorate in 1896. It became part of [Republic of Upper Volta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Upper_Volta) in 1958 and later as a part of Burkina Faso.[^cite_note-Hist-1]

## Geography

Loroum is one of the 45 [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Burkina_Faso) of Burkina Faso, a landlocked country in West Africa.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-Min-3] It is located in northern Burkina Faso within the [Nord Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nord_Region_\(Burkina_Faso\)) and spans approximately 3,587 km<sup>2</sup> (1,385 sq mi).[^cite_note-Pop-4] It is bordered by [Passoré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passor%C3%A9), [Yatenga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yatenga), and [Zondoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zondoma) provinces, and the seat is at the city of [Titao](3905537.md).[^cite_note-Min-3][^cite_note-5] The province, consistent with much of northern Burkina Faso, experiences a [tropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_climate) characterized by distinct wet and dry seasons, with erratic rainfall.[^cite_note-Min-3][^cite_note-6] Loroum is divided into 4 departments–[Banh Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banh_Department), [Ouindigui Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouindigui_Department), [Solle Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solle_Department), and [Titao Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titao_Department).[^cite_note-c2006-7]

## Demographics

As of 2019, it had a population of 198,192 inhabitants.[^cite_note-Pop-4] The demographics is made up of various ethnicities.[^cite_note-Hist-1] In 2011, the province had 192 primary schools and 12 secondary schools.[^cite_note-8] The province had 20 health and social promotion centers (*Centres de santé et de promotion sociale*), 3 doctors and 52 nurses.[^cite_note-9]
