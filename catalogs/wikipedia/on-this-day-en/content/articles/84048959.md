# Oleksandr Poklad

In this name that follows [East Slavic naming customs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Slavic_naming_customs), the [patronymic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patronymic) is&#x20;*&#x20;Valentynovych* and the [family name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname) is&#x20;*&#x20;Poklad*.



Poklad in 2026

[Major General](36301471.md) **Oleksandr Valentynovych Poklad** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Олександр Валентинович Поклад; born 6 July 1974) is a Ukrainian state security officer and the current acting [Head of the Security Service of Ukraine](43502649.md) since 17 July 2026.[^cite_note-1]

Lieutenant general [Hryhoriy Omelchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hryhoriy_Omelchenko) claimed that Poklad was sentenced to six years in prison in 1996 for [racketeering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racketeering) and later served as advisor to [Dmytro Firtash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmytro_Firtash)'s head of personal security.[^cite_note-2]

From 2021 to 2023, he headed the Counterintelligence Department of the Security Service, and from 2023 to 2026, he was Deputy Head of the SBU.[^cite_note-upu-27-2026-3][^cite_note-upu-606-2021-4][^cite_note-upu-509-2023-5][^cite_note-upu-510-2023-6] He was made a [Hero of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_Ukraine) with the award of the [Order of the Gold Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Gold_Star) in 2025.[^cite_note-7]

On 4 September 2026, President Zelenskyy said that a drone strike on the headquarters of the [Security Service of Ukraine](709222.md) was aimed at assassinating the SBU's commander Oleksandr Poklad. The drone strike injured some 12 staff of the SBU.[^cite_note-8]
