**Kathmandu District** ([Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): [काठमाडौं जिल्ला](https://ne.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A0%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A1%E0%A5%8C%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B2%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B2%E0%A4%BE)[Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/d/d3/Kathmandu.ogg/Kathmandu.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>; [Nepal Bhasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal_Bhasa): ये: जिल्ला) is a district located in [Kathmandu Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathmandu_Valley), [Bagmati Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagmati_Province) of [Nepal](171166.md). It is one of the [seventy-seven districts of Nepal](2918695.md), covers an area of 413.69 km<sup>2</sup> (159.73 sq mi), and is the most densely populated district of Nepal with 1,081,845 inhabitants in 2001, 1,744,240 in 2011 and 2,041,587 in 2021.[^cite_note-Census_2011_v5-1][^cite_note-2] The administrative headquarters of Kathmandu district is located in [Kathmandu](17168.md). The city has 21 post offices which handle mail from across the country and beyond, with Kathmandu DPO having 44,600 as its postal code for international mail delivery services like UPS or DHL Couriers etc.

## Geography

Kathmandu district is one of the three districts located in [Kathmandu Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathmandu_Valley), which itself is located in the hills of [Bagmati Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagmati_Province). The district is located from 27°27′E to 27°49′E longitude and 85°10′N to 85°32′N latitude.[^cite_note-DDC_Kathmandu-3]

The district is surrounded by:

- East: [Bhaktapur District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhaktapur_District) and [Kavrepalanchok District](2919224.md)

- West: [Dhading District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhading_District) and [Nuwakot District](2919311.md)

- North: [Nuwakot District](2919311.md) and [Sindhupalchok District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhupalchok_District)

- South: [Lalitpur District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalitpur_District,_Nepal) and [Makwanpur District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makwanpur_District)

The altitude of the district ranges from 1,262 m (4,140 ft) to 2,732 m (8,963 ft) above sea level.[^cite_note-DDC_Kathmandu-3]

In the urban center, the temperature fluctuates between 32 °C in summer (June–July) to -2 °C in winter (December–January). Except for the high hills including Chandragiri which has a temperate climate, Kathmandu district has a subtropical climate.[^cite_note-DDC_Kathmandu-3] The annual rainfall of the district is 176.4 ml.[^cite_note-DDC_Kathmandu-3]

## Culture

Kathmandu district is a part of [Kathmandu Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathmandu_Valley), which is a melting pot of various cultural groups, ethnicities, races, languages and religions. This vibrant culture is illustrated in the culture of the natives of the district, known as [Newars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newar), who are a multiethnic, multiracial, multireligious people bound by a Sanskritized Sino-Tibetan language of Kirat origins known as [Nepal Bhasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newar_language). All the ancient settlements of the district have specific street festivals (*[jatras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jatra_\(Nepal\))*) arranged according to specific dates of the [Nepal Sambat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal_Sambat) calendar. The main festivals celebrated are:

- [Yanya Punhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yanya_Punhi) – literally meaning Kathmandu's full moon, a week-long festival during the Yenla (the month of Kathmandu) of Nepal Sambat, a festival started by [Gunakamadev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunakamadev?action=edit\&redlink=1), the founder of Kathmandu city to commemorate the establishment of the city

- [Gunla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunla) – a month in which musical bands and ordinary people make pilgrimage to Swayambhunath temple

- [Mohani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohani)

- [Dashain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dashain) – the main festival of Nepal, celebrated by visiting the various [Shakta pithas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakta_pithas) around the city

- [Shivaratri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivaratri) – a very special festival in which people make small fire all around the city and take different types of toxic in order to show love to Lord [Shiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiva)

- [Buddha Jayanti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddha_Jayanti)

- [Sa Paru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sa_Paru) – a comic festival to commemorate dead relatives, celebrated during the full moon of Goon la

- [Chatha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganesh_Chaturthi) – the birthday of Lord [Ganesha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganesha) of [Hindu pantheon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_pantheon)

- [Holil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holi)

- [Pahan Chare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahan_Chare) – a festival of [Animist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animist) origin celebrated by procession of matriarchs of Kathmandu

- [Shree Panchami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shree_Panchami) – in the past children used to formally start education on this day. Presently, deities of wisdom, [Saraswati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraswati) and [Manjushree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manjushree) (the mythological founder of the valley) are worshipped on this day

- [Swanti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swanti_\(festival\)) or Tihar – a five-day festival, the third day of which is called Laxmi Puja, when the families do their annual financial calculation and the next day marks the New Year according to Nepalese calendar, [Nepal Sambat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal_Sambat).

Along with these festivals, with the influx of population from other parts of Nepal, India, Tibet and South Asia, other festivals such as [Teej](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teej), [Chhath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chhath), [Sakela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakela), [Lhosar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lhosar), [Janai Purnima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janai_Purnima), [Deuda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deuda_\(genre\)) etc. are also celebrated.

## Economy



Kathmandu Metropolis

Kathmandu is the biggest economic hub of Nepal. Most of the offices and industries of Nepal are in Kathmandu. People from all over Nepal come to Kathmandu for better education, health services, job opportunities and social security. The major economic hubs are [New Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Road_of_Kathmandu), [Durbar Marg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durbar_Marg), Putalisadak, Asan. The district [exports handicrafts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepalese_handicrafts), artworks, garments, pashmina, paper etc. Kathmandu has the highest number of tourists influx in the country. Tourism is one of the main industries of the district. Hindu and Buddhist pilgrims from all over the world visit various religious places located in the district such as [Pashupatinath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashupatinath_Temple), [Swayambhunath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swayambhunath), [Boudhanath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boudhanath), [Buddhanilkantha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhanilkantha?action=edit\&redlink=1) etc. [Freak Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freak_Street) and [Thamel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thamel) are noted tourist destinations for Western tourists.

## Education

Kathmandu district is the pioneer district in education in many aspects in Nepal. [Durbar High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durbar_High_School) (the first school of Nepal), [Trichandra College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trichandra_College) (the first college of Nepal), Padma Kanya College (the first women's college) are all located in [Kathmandu city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathmandu_Metropolitan_City). [Tribhuwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribhuwan_University), the first university of Nepal, is located in [Kirtipur municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirtipur_municipality) of Kathmandu district. Besides these, thousands of educational institutions are located in the district which enrolls students from [Nepal](171166.md), [India](14533.md), [Bangladesh](3454.md) etc.

At the time of the [2021 Nepal census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Nepal_census), Kathmandu District had a population of 2,041,587. 5.65% of the population is under 5 years of age. It has a literacy rate of 89.23% and a sex ratio of 971 females per 1000 males. The entire population lived in municipalities.[^cite_note-provincialreport-11]
