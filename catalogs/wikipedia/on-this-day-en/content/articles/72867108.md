**Nyo Saw** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): ညိုစော), commonly known as **U Nyo Saw**, is a Burmese politician and former military officer who has served as the fourth [first vice president of Myanmar](30762522.md) since April 2026. A member of the [Union Solidarity and Development Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Solidarity_and_Development_Party) (USDP), he previously served as the 13th [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Myanmar) and [Minister of National Planning and Economic Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Planning_and_Economic_Development) from 2025 to 2026, Chairman of the [Myanmar Economic Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar_Economic_Corporation) from 2014 to 2026, and was also a member of the [State Administration Council](66589972.md) from 2021 to 2025 and then the [State Security and Peace Commission](80587873.md) from 2025 to 2026.

Born in an unknown location, Nyo Saw was educated in [Defence Services Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Services_Academy), where he graduated and entered service with a posting as a [Brigadier general](206220.md) Staff Officer under Yangon command where he held multiple senior roles. Nyo Saw was promoted to [Quartermaster general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartermaster_general) in 2014 and was also appointed chairman of the Myanmar Economic Corporation that year. He retained both those roles ever after retiring from the army in 2020. In 2023, Nyo Saw was appointed a member of the [State Administration Council](66589972.md), the then-ruling military junta of Myanmar.

In July 2025, the State Administration Council was abolished and was replaced by the State Security and Peace Commission, and as well as the [state of emergency](298608.md). The junta leader [Min Aung Hlaing](38278640.md) appointed Nyo Saw prime minister, [Minister of National Planning and Economic Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Planning_and_Economic_Development), and a member of the newly formed council. His appointment came as the country was getting ready for [general elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Myanmar_general_election) later in the year.

## Early life and education

Nyo Saw graduated from the 23rd intake of the [Defence Services Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Services_Academy).[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## Career

Nyo Saw began his career as a grade one general staff officer at the Yangon command.[^cite_note-2] He served as the commandant of the [Defence Services Technological Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Services_Technological_Academy) and the Defence Services Academy, and also headed the country's central and southern commands.[^cite_note-3]

Nyo Saw served as [quartermaster general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartermaster_general) from 2014 to April 2020.&#x20;

As of August 2022, he serves as the chairman of the [Myanmar Economic Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar_Economic_Corporation). In July 2023, he was appointed as the advisor to the chairman of State Administration Council.[^cite_note-4]

On 25 September 2023, he was appointed to the [State Administration Council](66589972.md).

On 31 July 2025, a meeting of the [National Defence and Security Council](47091432.md) was convened. Following this, Acting President Senior General Min Aung Hlaing signed and issued an order announcing the formation of the new Union Government in which Nyo Saw was appointed as the Prime Minister. In addition to his role as Prime Minister, he also concurrently serves as the Union Minister of the Ministry of National Planning.[^cite_note-5]

Nyo Saw ran and was elected as the [Union Solidarity and Development Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Solidarity_and_Development_Party) candidate in the [2025–26 Myanmar general election](69631013.md) for [Pyithu Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyithu_Hluttaw) in [Cocokyun Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocokyun_Township), [Yangon Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangon_Region), considered a sham process by independent observers.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-Announcement_15/2026-7]

## Personal life

Nyo Saw is married to San San Aye and have three children.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## Personal sanctions

On 11 December 2023, the [European Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Council) imposed personal restrictions on Nyo Saw due to his direct contribution to dismantling and undermining democracy and stability in Myanmar as a member of the [State Administration Council](66589972.md), a body established by the junta to facilitate and legitimize their rule over Myanmar after [the 2021 coup d'état](66567997.md).[^cite_note-8]
