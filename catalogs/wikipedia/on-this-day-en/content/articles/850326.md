The **Movement for Democracy** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Movimento para a Democracia*, MpD) is a Christian democratic and [liberal party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_parties) in [Cape Verde](18962637.md). Established in 1990, it was the ruling party from 1991 to 2001 and returned to power in the [2016 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_parliamentary_election,_2016). Its members are nicknamed "*os ventoinhas*" (the [wind fans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan_\(machine\))) in Portuguese, a reference to the party's logo. The party is serving in the opposition since the [2026 election](78445772.md).

## History

The MpD was established on 14 March 1990 by [Carlos Veiga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Veiga) after Prime Minister [Pedro Pires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pires) of the [African Party for the Independence of Cape Verde](1094346.md) (PAICV, formerly the sole legal party) allowed its creation.[^cite_note-HDCV-5] The party was publicly launched in May 1990 and its first convention was held in November 1990.

In the [January 1991 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_parliamentary_election,_1991), the first multi-party elections in the country's history, the MpD won 56 of the 79 seats in the [National Assembly](3547417.md). In the [presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_presidential_election,_1991) the following month, MpD candidate [António Mascarenhas Monteiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant%C3%B3nio_Mascarenhas_Monteiro) defeated the incumbent PAICV President [Aristides Pereira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristides_Pereira).

Following the MpD's second convention in January 1993, splits developed within the party, with two groups, List A and List B, emerging.[^cite_note-6] A split in 1994 led to the creation of the [Democratic Convergence Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Convergence_Party_\(Cape_Verde\)).[^cite_note-7] Despite losing six seats, the party retained its parliamentary majority in the [1995 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_parliamentary_election,_1995), whilst Monteiro was re-elected unopposed in the [presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_presidential_election,_1996) the following year.

The [January 2001 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_parliamentary_election,_2001) saw the party lose another 20 seats, as it was defeated by the PAICV. In the [February 2001 presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_presidential_election,_2001) the MpD nominated Veiga, but he was defeated by the PAICV's Pires by just 12 votes in the second round of voting.[^cite_note-8]

In the [2006 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_parliamentary_election,_2006) party lost another seat and was reduced to 29 MPs. Veiga was again defeated by Pires in the [presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_presidential_election,_2006) later in the year.

Despite gaining three seats in the [2011 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_parliamentary_election,_2011), the MpD remained in opposition. However, in the [presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_presidential_election,_2011) in the same year, MpD candidate [Jorge Carlos Fonseca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Carlos_Fonseca) defeated the PAICV's [Manuel Inocêncio Sousa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Inoc%C3%AAncio_Sousa) by 54–46%.

In July 2013, [Ulisses Correia e Silva](50229553.md), the mayor of the capital, Praia, became party's new chairman, following Carlos Veiga, who stepped down after five years.[^cite_note-9]

Fonseca was re-elected as president in the [2016 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Cape_Verdean_presidential_election).[^cite_note-10] The MpD also won a majority in the National Assembly during the [2016 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Cape_Verdean_parliamentary_election), ending fifteen years of PAICV majorities in that body and electing its leader Ulisses Correia e Silva as Prime Minister of Cape Verde.[^cite_note-11]

## Youth wing

The party's youth wing is the **Youth for Democracy** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Juventude para a Democracia*, JpD), which plays a role in political mobilization and engagement of younger members within the party.[^cite_note-12]

Since 2025, the organization has been led by [Líver Gomes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%ADver_Gomes).[^cite_note-expresso-jpd-13]
