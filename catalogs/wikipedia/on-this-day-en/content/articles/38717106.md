# Egypt at the FIFA World Cup



[Egypt national team](1022953.md) at the [1934 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1934_FIFA_World_Cup) in Italy

**Egypt** has qualified for the **[FIFA World Cup](11370.md)** on four occasions: in [1934](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1934_FIFA_World_Cup), where it became the first Middle Eastern and African team to play in the World Cup, [1990](157232.md), [2018](13327177.md), and [2026](36581929.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] they have produced 5 draws, 6 losses, and 2 wins in the competition, beating [New Zealand](1023035.md) and Australia via penalties in the knockouts during the 2026 World Cup.&#x20;

When they qualified for the second time in 1990, they became the team with the longest-ever gap between two FIFA World Cup matches: 56 years and 16 days had passed.[^cite_note-7] This was surpassed in [2022](17742072.md) by the qualification of [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales_national_football_team) for the first time in 64 years. They have never qualified consecutively.&#x20;

## Uruguay 1930

There was no qualification for the first World Cup. Rather, teams were simply invited. Egypt had started their travel to Uruguay, but a storm delayed their boat to Marseille.[^cite_note-9]

## Italy 1990

### Group stage

Group F

Main article: [1990 FIFA World Cup Group F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_F)

Group F, featured the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands_national_football_team), [England](9904.md), the [Republic of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland_national_football_team) and [Egypt](1022953.md). In the six group games, five ended in draws. England beat Egypt 1–0, thanks to a 58th-minute goal from [Mark Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Wright_\(footballer_born_1963\)) – and that was enough to win the group.[^cite_note-10]

## Player records

*As of 7 July 2026*

### Records

On 25 June 2018, Egyptian goalkeeper [Essam El Hadary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essam_El_Hadary) notably became the oldest player ever to appear in a World Cup match, at the age of 45 years and 5 months.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] He saved a penalty in the first half of the match against [Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia_national_football_team), but Egypt would still ultimately lose 2–1.

[Mohamed Hany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Hany) is the player with the most own goals scored in the World Cup. He scored an own goal against [Belgium](679716.md) and then [Australia](4792249.md) respectively in 2026.[^cite_note-35] Another record in 2026 achieved was Egyptian goalkeeper [Mustafa Shobeir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mostafa_Shobeir) saving 2 penalities, the most for any goalkeeper, although tied with many others. [^cite_note-36]

### Goalscorers



[Mohamed Salah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Salah), Egypt's top scorer at the World Cup

With a brace on 27 May 1934 in Egypt's first-ever World Cup match (a 2–4 defeat against [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary_national_football_team) in [Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadio_Partenopeo)), [Abdulrahman Fawzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulrahman_Fawzi) became the only African goalscorer at a FIFA World Cup finals for decades, until [Houmane Jarir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houmane_Jarir) scored for [Morocco](1024865.md) in 1970.[^cite_note-37] It took until 2026 for another Egyptian, [Mohamed Salah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Salah), to exceed Fawzi's tally.
