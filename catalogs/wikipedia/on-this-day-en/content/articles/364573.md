The **Cessna 208 Caravan** is a [utility aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utility_aircraft) produced by [Cessna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna).  The project was commenced on November 20, 1981, and the prototype first flew on December 9, 1982. The production model was certified by the [FAA](11186.md) in October 1984 and its Cargomaster freighter variant was developed for [FedEx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FedEx). The 4 ft (1.2 m) longer 208B Super Cargomaster first flew in 1986 and was developed into the passenger 208B Grand Caravan.[^cite_note-Fraw76-2]

The [strutted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bracing_\(aeronautics\)), [high wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_wing) 208 typically seats nine passengers in its [unpressurized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabin_pressurization) cabin, is powered by a single [Pratt & Whitney Canada PT6A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratt_&_Whitney_Canada_PT6A) tractor [turboprop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turboprop) and has a fixed [tricycle landing gear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tricycle_landing_gear), [floats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floatplane), or skis.[^cite_note-Textron14nov2017-3]

By 2022, 3,000 had been delivered and 24 million flight hours have been logged. Caravans have been used for [flight training](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_training), [commuter airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commuter_airline), [VIP transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VIP_transport), [air cargo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_cargo), [skydiving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skydiving) and [humanitarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanitarian) missions.

## Development



A [FedEx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FedEx) Super Cargomaster with cargo pod. The Cargomaster and Super Cargomaster variants are built without cabin windows.



Cockpit of a pre-2008 Caravan

On November 20, 1981, the project was given a go-ahead by Cessna for its Pawnee engineering facility. John Berwick, chief engineer at Pawnee, conceived of a single engine, high-wing airplane with a large payload. Berwick had originally approached Vice President Bill Boettger with the idea and once [Dwane Wallace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwane_Wallace) approved it, Berwick told [Russ Meyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_W._Meyer_Jr.) he would design it.[^cite_note-Rodengen1998-4]

The prototype first flew on December 9, 1982.[^cite_note-5] The production model was certified by the [Federal Aviation Administration](11186.md) (FAA) in October 1984.[^cite_note-Fraw76-2]

Deliveries began in 1985, and [amphibious floats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_floats) were approved that same year.[^cite_note-Textron13jan2023-1] A freighter variant without cabin windows was developed at the request of [Federal Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FedEx) as the Cargomaster.[^cite_note-Fraw76-2] FedEx had been initially planning to build twin-engine piston-powered airplanes with [Piper Aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piper_Aircraft), but picked the Caravan after surveying it and having flown the prototype, becoming its standard carrier.[^cite_note-Rodengen1998-4]

Another cargo variant for Federal Express, with a longer fuselage and a cargo pod under the belly, was developed as the 208B Super Cargomaster and flew for the first time in 1986.[^cite_note-Fraw76-2] Stretched by 4 ft (1.2 m), it received its FAA type certification also in 1986.[^cite_note-Textron13jan2023-1] A passenger model, the 208B Grand Caravan, was derived from the Super Cargomaster.[^cite_note-Fraw76-2] It was first delivered in 1990.[^cite_note-Textron13jan2023-1]

Since then, the Caravan has undergone a number of design evolutions, including upgrading the avionics in 2008 to provide a [glass cockpit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_cockpit) with the [Garmin G1000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garmin_G1000) system.[^cite_note-6] In January 2013 a higher-powered (867 shp from P\&WC PT6A-140) version, the Grand Caravan EX, received FAA certification.[^cite_note-7]

In August 2016, Textron announced that it would move the Cessna 208 production line from its [Wichita](114932.md) headquarters to its [Independence, Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence,_Kansas), production facility, for manufacture alongside the piston-powered [172](173462.md), [182](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_182), [206](1240850.md) and [TTx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_TTx), and the [Citation M2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_Citation_M2) light jet. The move was made to make room for production of the [Citation Longitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_Citation_Longitude) and [Denali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_Denali) in Wichita.[^cite_note-8] In 2023, the 208 Caravan unit cost was [US\$](18717338.md)2.32 million and US\$2.61 million for the 208B Grand Caravan EX.[^cite_note-9]

### Chinese production

In May 2012, Cessna announced that an assembly line for the 208 would be established in China, with the government-owned [China Aviation Industry General Aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Aviation_Industry_General_Aircraft) (CAIGA) conducting final assembly of Caravans at its plant in [Shijiazhuang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shijiazhuang) for the Chinese market.[^cite_note-Pew03May12-10] Chinese government approval was granted in September the following year and the first Chinese-assembled Caravan was delivered in December 2013.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] By April 2016 about 30 aircraft, assembled from kits of parts shipped from the US by Cessna, had been delivered to Chinese operators by the joint venture.[^cite_note-13]

## Design



Low-density seating in the cabin of a passenger-carrying version

The Cessna 208 is a high-wing braced cabin monoplane powered by a single [Pratt & Whitney Canada PT6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratt_&_Whitney_Canada_PT6)A turboprop in [tractor configuration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tractor_configuration). The cabin has room for nine passengers and two crew when used as a passenger aircraft with four doors: one for each crew member, an airstair door on the right side of the cabin and a cargo door on the left. The aircraft can be optionally fitted with an underslung cargo pod.[^cite_note-Textron14nov2017-3]

The basic 208 [airframe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airframe) has a fixed [tricycle landing gear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tricycle_landing_gear) but can also be fitted with various types of [landing gear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landing_gear), allowing it to operate in a wide variety of environments. Some common adaptations include [floats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floatplane) with retractable landing gear on the Caravan Amphibian model, and skis.[^cite_note-Fraw76-2]



208B Grand Caravan in the Netherlands, modified with [roller door](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roller_door) for [parachuting](19723982.md) operations; skydivers sitting on the cabin floor are visible inside the rear roller door

The Caravan interior can be outfitted with seats or as a cargo compartment. The standard high-density airline configuration has four rows of 1-2 seating behind the two seats in the cockpit. This variant is capable of holding up to thirteen passengers, although it is marketed as being able to make a profit carrying just four.[^cite_note-14]

The cabin can be configured in a low density passenger configuration, with 1-1 seating, as a combination of passengers and cargo, or as a strictly cargo aircraft.[^cite_note-\:0-15] Many variants include an underbelly cargo pod, which can be used for additional freight capacity, or for passenger baggage. A number of Caravans are operated as [skydiving](19723982.md) aircraft with the left-side cargo hatch converted to a roll-up door.[^cite_note-16]

The airplane typically seats nine passengers with a single pilot, although with a [FAR Part 23](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAR_Part_23) waiver it can seat up to fourteen passengers. The aircraft is also used for cargo operations.[^cite_note-Textron14nov2017-3]

The short-fuselage Caravan burns 48 US gal (180 L) of fuel per hour at 170 kn (310 km/h; 200 mph) for 200 nmi (370 km; 230 mi) stages.[^cite_note-17]

## Variants

### Civilian

208 Caravan

First production variant with a PT6A-114 turboprop engine and seating for up to nine passengers. The landplane variant was type approved on October 23, 1984, and the seaplane version with Wipline Model 8000 Amphibious/Seaplane Floats was type approved on March 26, 1986. Early aircraft can be modified to use the higher-powered PT6A-114A but have restricted operating limits.

- **208 Caravan 675**

Marketing designation for the 208 Caravan with a higher-powered PT6A-114A engine.



A [FedEx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FedEx_Express) 208B Super Cargomaster

208A Cargomaster

A pure-cargo version of the Caravan developed with Federal Express (now [FedEx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FedEx)); 40 aircraft produced.

[^cite_note-18]

&#x20;All 208A aircraft were serialized as 208 models.



208B Grand Caravan in Mexico with its right rear [airstair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airstair) door open. The 208B Grand Caravan is 4 feet (1.2 m) longer than the 208, and the passenger-carrying version has eight side windows instead of the 208's six.

208B Grand Caravan

Officially named the **208B Caravan** but marketed as the **Grand Caravan**. The 208B is 4 ft (1.2 m) longer than the 208; extending the cabin by the same amount. The 208B has a [PT6A-114A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratt_&_Whitney_Canada_PT6) engine. It was originally certified as a two-seater cargo version on October 9, 1986, and as an 11-seater passenger aircraft on December 13, 1989.

- **208B Grand Caravan EX**

Marketing name for upgraded version of the 208B Caravan certified in December 2012, with a more powerful 867 hp (647 kW) Pratt and Whitney Canada PT6A-140 that improves the rate of climb by 38% and was developed by [Pratt & Whitney Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratt_&_Whitney_Canada) specifically to power the 208B.

[^cite_note-Niles13Jan13-19]

[^cite_note-20]

&#x20;The unladen weight is 807 lb (366 kg) more but maximum payload is only 90 lb (41 kg) more. It requires a longer take off run at 2,160 feet (660 m) and its landing roll is at 1,871 feet (570 m).

### Experimental

The eCaravan is an [electric aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_aircraft) modification of the 208B built by [AeroTEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AeroTEC?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [magniX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MagniX) powered by a 750 hp (560 kW) motor and a 1 t (2,200 lb), 750 V [lithium-ion battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium-ion_battery).[^cite_note-Flight29may2020-29] The plane's 30-minute first flight happened from [Grant County International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grant_County_International_Airport) in Moses Lake, Washington, on May 28, 2020, consuming US\$6 worth of electricity, needing 30–40 min of charging.[^cite_note-Flight29may2020-29]

The Magni500-powered variant can fly 100 nautical miles (185 km; 115 mi) with 4–5 passengers while keeping reserve power, and aims for a certification by the end of 2021, hoping to operate 100 nmi (185 km; 115 mi) flights with a full load of nine passengers with better batteries.[^cite_note-Flight29may2020-29]

It was selected as one of five finalists for the United States Special Operations Command's (USSOCOM's) [Armed Overwatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Overwatch) program.

## Operators

Main article: [List of Cessna 208 Caravan operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Cessna_208_Caravan_operators)

The 1,000th was delivered in 1998; the 1,500th in 2005; the 2,000th in 2010;[^cite_note-38] the 2,500th in 2015;[^cite_note-39] and the 3,000th in 2022.[^cite_note-Textron13jan2023-1] By March 2022, 24 million flight hours have been logged.[^cite_note-40]

Certified in 100 countries, Caravans are used for flight training, recreation, commuter airlines, VIP transport, cargo carriers and humanitarian missions.[^cite_note-Textron14nov2017-3] It is also used by government agencies in law enforcement, air ambulance services, police and military.[^cite_note-Rodengen1998-4]

### Civil operators

The Cessna 208 is used by governmental organizations and by a large number of companies for police, air ambulance, passenger transport, air charter, freight and parachuting operations. [FedEx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FedEx) operates 239 aircraft.[^cite_note-41]

### Military operators

A total of 123 Cessna 208s were in military service as transport in 2024.[^cite_note-AFdir-42]

## Accidents and incidents

Main article: [List of accidents and incidents involving the Cessna 208 Caravan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_accidents_and_incidents_involving_the_Cessna_208_Caravan)

As of February 2025, there have been 277 Caravan [hull losses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hull_loss) from all causes, including 271 accidents causing 516 fatalities – an average of 2 fatalities per hull-loss, with 27.4% of all occupants surviving fatal accidents; and eight [hijackings](2075.md) causing one fatality. For the 198 out of the 277 hull-loss occurrences where the aircraft was in use and its flight nature is known, 36.9% were [passenger flights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passenger_flight), 33.8% [cargo flights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_cargo), 8.1% [military flights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_flight), 5.6% special flights – [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_aircraft), [survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerial_survey), etc., 4% private and [business flights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_aviation), 3% [test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_test) or [flight training](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_training) and 8.1% miscellaneous uses – demonstrations, deliveries, illegal.[^cite_note-ASNstats-43]
