The **Puntland Dervish Force** or **Puntland Defence Forces** (**PDF**, [Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Ciidamadda Daraawiishta Puntland ama Ciidamadda Difaaca Dawladda Puntland*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): قوات الدراويش في أرض البنط ) is the official armed forces of the [Puntland Government](43532267.md).[^cite_note-Intsom-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]&#x20;

## History

Following the outbreak of the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md) in 1991, a homegrown constitutional conference was held in [Garowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garowe) in 1998 over a period of three months.[^cite_note-7] Attended by the area's political elite, traditional elders (*Issims*), members of the business community, intellectuals and other civil society representatives, the autonomous [Puntland State of Somalia](233569.md) was subsequently officially established so as to deliver services to the population, offer security, facilitate trade, and interact with both domestic and international partners.[^cite_note-Speipbasb-8] The Puntland Security Force (PSF) was subsequently formed by the regional government.[^cite_note-Puntconst-9]&#x20;

Since the founding of the state in 1998, the Puntland [Dervish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dervish_movement_\(Somali\)) Force has operated in Puntland and throughout Somalia. Commanders and senior officials of the military are appointed by a qualified panel approved by the Council of Ministers.[^cite_note-Puntconst-9]&#x20;

The Puntland security apparatus has an independent military judiciary, which during peacetime only adjudges military proceedings. Retired members of the Force are also constitutionally guaranteed pensions.[^cite_note-Puntconst-9]&#x20;
