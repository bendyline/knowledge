An attempted bombing occurred on March 7, 2026, outside of [Gracie Mansion](1206268.md), the residence of the [New York City mayor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_mayor), in the [Yorkville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkville,_Manhattan) neighborhood on the [Upper East Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_East_Side) of [Manhattan](45470.md) in [New York City](645042.md), United States. Two men, inspired by [ISIS](9087364.md), from [Bucks County, Pennsylvania](91976.md), allegedly attempted to detonate two bombs in a crowd of people attending the "Stop the Islamic Takeover of New York City" protest held by American [far-right](221220.md) activist [Jake Lang](82615078.md).[^cite_note-Attempted-1][^cite_note-2] Both bombs failed to detonate, and no one was injured in the attack.[^cite_note-3]

## Investigation

On March 9, 2026, at a city press conference including Mayor [Zohran Mamdani](64618347.md), the New York City police commissioner [Jessica Tisch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Tisch) said that the attack was being investigated as an "act of [ISIS-inspired terrorism](9087364.md)". Tisch identified two suspects from [Bucks County, Pennsylvania](91976.md), one from [Langhorne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langhorne,_Pennsylvania) and the other from [Newtown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newtown,_Bucks_County,_Pennsylvania), who were taken into custody.[^cite_note-Attempted-1] Two additional devices were identified at the scene—one dropped by a suspect on the run and one in a parked vehicle a few streets south of the residence.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] In addition, federal charges were brought by the [U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Attorney_for_the_Southern_District_of_New_York) against the two men including "material support and resources to a designated foreign terrorist organization".[^cite_note-Attempted-1]

Investigators say the attackers were inspired by the [Islamic State](9087364.md) and wanted the incident to be deadlier than the 2013 [Boston Marathon bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Marathon_bombing).[^cite_note-JenniferMillmanNBCNewYork-6] Police recovered a notebook from the attackers which had alternative targets such as shopping centers.[^cite_note-GracieMansionABC7NewYork-7]

## Reactions

Speaking about the incident, Mayor Mamdani first called the rally itself that had been attacked "appalling". He praised the counter-protestors, which were larger in number than the number of protesters, according to authorities.[^cite_note-RichardLuscombe-8] He then condemned the incident, saying, "Many of the counter-protesters met this display of bigotry peacefully. A few did not. Two men \[...] traveled from two different towns in Bucks County and attempted to bring violence to New York City." He also commended the quick action of the [NYPD](255413.md) officers and spoke in support of the right to free speech, saying the "right to peaceful protest is sacred. It does not belong only to those we agree with."[^cite_note-Attempted-1][^cite_note-RichardLuscombe-8]
