**Aadshit al-Qusayr** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): عدشيت القصير, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *ʿAdshīt al-Quṣayr*), or simply **Aadshit** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): عدشيت), is a municipality in the [Marjayoun District](8968039.md) in [South Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Lebanon).

## Etymology

According to [E. H. Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Henry_Palmer), the name *Atshis* comes from a personal name.[^cite_note-1]

## History

In 1875, [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) found the village to have [Metuali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metuali) inhabitants.[^cite_note-2]

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) (SWP) described it: "a small village, built of mud and stone, containing about 100 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh), situated on a low ridge surrounded by small gardens and olives. The water supply is from four rock-cut [cisterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cistern)."[^cite_note-3]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.71% of registered voters in Aadshit. 98.84% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-4]
