**Al-Shaddadah** or **al-Shaddadi** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ٱلشَّدَّادَة \ ٱلشَّدَّادِي, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *aš-Šaddādah / aš-Šaddādī*; [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language): *Şeddadê*) is a town in southern [al-Hasakah Governorate](1429110.md), northeastern [Syria](7515849.md). The town is the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of the [al-Shaddadah Subdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Shaddadah_Subdistrict), which consists of 16 municipalities. At the 2004 census, al-Shaddadah had a population of 15,806.[^cite_note-census2004-1]

## Name and geography

The town's name might be derived from "Shadadu"; a governor of the district of "Suru" mentioned in the annals of the Assyrian king [Assurnasirpal II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assurnasirpal_II).[^cite_note-2] The town is situated off the western bank of the [Khabur River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khabur_\(Euphrates\)). Nearby localities include [al-Sabaa wa Arbain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Sabaa_wa_Arbain) to the west.

## Syrian civil war

### Early developments

Main article: [Battle of Shaddadi (2013)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Shaddadi_\(2013\))

In the course of the [Syrian civil war](30741795.md), the town was attacked by [jihadist](7826589.md) rebel forces of the [al-Nusra Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Nusra_Front) in the [Battle of Shaddadi (2013)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Shaddadi_\(2013\)) and was captured three days later.[^cite_note-3] According to the [Syrian Observatory for Human Rights](34029669.md) (SOHR), over 100 pro-[Assad](364813.md) fighters and 40 al-Nusra fighters were killed, as well as dozens of petroleum workers, as a result of the battle.[^cite_note-4]

### Capture and control by the Islamic State

The town was later attacked and captured by the [Islamic State](9087364.md) (ISIS) in July 2013. Al-Shaddadah remained one of the last ISIS strongholds in the province over the next years.[^cite_note-5]

The town gained notoriety for its use as a [slave market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slave_market) where [Yazidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yazidis) girls [captured in the Sinjar region in August 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Iraq_offensive_\(August_2014\)) were sold as [sex slaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_slaves). *[The New York Times](30680.md)* also reported an incident in al-Shaddadah in which a [Saudi](349303.md) ISIS fighter raped a 12-year-old Yazidi girl.[^cite_note-\:0-6]

### Capture and control by Kurdish-led forces

Main articles: [2015 al-Hawl offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_al-Hawl_offensive) and [Al-Shaddadi offensive (2016)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Shaddadi_offensive_\(2016\))



[Asayish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asayish_\(Democratic_Autonomous_Administration_of_North_and_East_Syria\)) members graduating at a training base near al-Shaddadah, 3 April 2023.

On 11 October 2015, one day after the [Kurdish](3979351.md)-majority [People’s Protection Units](36497032.md) (YPG) and allied groups, including the [al-Sanadid Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Sanadid_Forces) of the Arab [Shammar tribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shammar_tribe), formed the U.S.-backed [Syrian Democratic Forces](48205248.md) (SDF), al-Sanadid leader Bandar al-Humaydi made it an "immediate priority to liberate [al-Hawl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Hawl) and al-Shaddadah from the Islamic State” for the newly formed coalition.[^cite_note-7]

On 24 November 2015, it became known that ISIS militants were transferring their family members further south to the [Deir ez-Zor Governorate](7445749.md).[^cite_note-8] After the SDF captured the South Hasakah Dam on 30 November, they continued their offensive southward, towards the city of al-Shaddadah, now ISIS' last stronghold in al-Hasakah province.[^cite_note-inside-9][^cite_note-210villages-10] Subsequently, Arab tribal leaders reportedly urged ISIS to withdraw from the city "peacefully," in order to prevent civilian casualties and the possible collapse of al-Shaddadi's economic infrastructure, if a destructive battle between the SDF/coalition forces and the Islamic State were to occur.[^cite_note-210villages-10] It was also reported that ISIS was beginning to evacuate some of its positions near al-Shaddadi.[^cite_note-210villages-10]

On 19 February 2016, the [town was captured](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Shaddadi_offensive_\(2016\)) by the SDF and subsequently became part of the DAANES.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] After SDF forces took control of the town, Yazidi spiritual leader [Eidou Baba Sheikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eidou_Baba_Sheikh?action=edit\&redlink=1) stated, “The Yazidis are happy when they \[SDF] liberated the town.”[^cite_note-\:0-6]

Following its capture, the U.S. Army [established a military base](43683196.md) southeast of the city. The base regularly receives [cargo aircraft](2375692.md) carrying logistical and military supplies and serves as a major hub for military coordination and anti-ISIS operations in cooperation with the SDF and local [Asayish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asayish_\(Democratic_Autonomous_Administration_of_North_and_East_Syria\)) police force.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

The U.S. military base has been targeted with [vehicle-borne improvised explosive devices](155282.md) (VBIEDs) and rocket attacks by ISIS[^cite_note-16] and various [Iranian-backed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_intervention_in_the_Syrian_civil_war) militias, including the [Islamic Resistance in Iraq](75274618.md),[^cite_note-17] notably during [attacks on U.S. bases amid the Gaza conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attacks_on_US_bases_during_the_Gaza_war).[^cite_note-18]

## Economy

The areas surrounding al-Shaddadah contain one of the large [oil and gas fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_and_gas_fields) in Hasakah Governorate.[^cite_note-\:0-6]
