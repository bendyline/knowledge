# Embassy of France, Bamako

The **Embassy of France** in [Bamako](56606.md) is the [diplomatic mission](8970.md) of [France](5843419.md) to [Mali](19127.md). It is in [Patrice Lumumba Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrice_Lumumba_Square) in Bamako, the capital of Mali.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] In 2022, Joël Meyer, the French ambassador to Mali, was expelled by Mali for diplomatic reasons.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
