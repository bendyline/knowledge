**Six Flags Qiddiya City**[^cite_note-naming-1] is an [amusement park](137327.md) located in [Qiddiya City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qiddiya_City), [Riyadh Province](23631923.md), Saudi Arabia. Owned by the [Qiddiya Investment Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qiddiya_City#Qiddiya_company), it is operated and managed by [Six Flags Entertainment Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six_Flags). It is the first Six Flags theme park in [Asia](689.md) and features [Falcons Flight](78648389.md), the [world's tallest, fastest, and longest roller coaster](2533444.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-blooloop-4]

## History

### Background

On April 4, 2018, [Six Flags Entertainment Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six_Flags_\(1961%E2%80%932024\)) announced the development of a new park in Riyadh backed by the [Public Investment Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Investment_Fund), Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, under [Saudi Vision 2030](50299103.md), a government initiative to diversify the country's economy. The park was initially slated for a 2022 opening.[^cite_note-5]

On August 26, 2019, the Qiddiya Investment Company unveiled plans for the 79-acre park's design. These included 28 attractions over six themed park areas with a mix of both thrill rides and family-friendly rides. They announced [Falcons Flight](78648389.md), which would be the world's longest, tallest, and fastest [roller coaster](137471.md) upon completion. The park's opening date was then moved to 2023.[^cite_note-6]

### Construction

On December 12, 2021, Qiddiya Investment Company awarded a \$1 billion contract to Almabani General Contractors based in Saudi Arabia and [Bouygues Bâtiment International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bouygues) based in France to begin construction on the park.[^cite_note-blooloop-4][^cite_note-Qiddiya_Investment_Company-7]

In 2024, [UK Export Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Export_Finance) designated the Six Flags Qiddiya project as "Category A" under its social and environmental risk framework, identifying a potential for significant adverse impact during construction and operation, requiring ongoing monitoring of labour conditions during construction.[^cite_note-8]

### Opening

On November 16, 2025, it was announced that Six Flags Qiddiya City would officially open on December 31, 2025. Ticket prices were confirmed to start at [US\$](18717338.md)87 ([SAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_riyal)325) for adults and [US\$](18717338.md)74 ([SAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_riyal)275) for children, with free admission for infants under four years old. Tickets grant unlimited access to all rides and attractions in the park for a full day.[^cite_note-AlArabiya-9]

In the weeks leading up to the official opening, the park hosted a [soft opening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_launch) for the families of employees and selected media influencers. Around two weeks prior to the official opening ceremony, [WWE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE) stars including [Randy Orton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randy_Orton), [Liv Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liv_Morgan), [Omos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omos), [Tiffany Stratton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiffany_Stratton), and [Grayson Waller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grayson_Waller) visited Six Flags Qiddiya City while on tour in [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) to promote the [Riyadh Season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riyadh_Season) and the [2026 Royal Rumble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rumble_\(2026\)).[^cite_note-WWEpromo-10]

On December 29, 2025, the park was officially inaugurated in a ceremony under the patronage of the Governor of [Riyadh Province](23631923.md), [Faisal bin Bandar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faisal_bin_Bandar_Al_Saud_\(born_1945\)), who formally announced the opening before guests and officials in attendance. The event also featured a live performance by [Alicia Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicia_Keys).[^cite_note-11]

Six Flags Qiddiya City officially opened to the public on New Year’s Eve 2025, becoming the first [Six Flags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six_Flags) park in [Asia](689.md) and the first located outside [North America](21139.md) since 2004 following the sale and spin-off of the former Six Flags European Division theme parks ([Six Flags Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walibi_Holland) and [Six Flags Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walibi_Belgium)) to Palamon Capital Partners (StarParks).[^cite_note-12]
