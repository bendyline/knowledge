The **governor of Belgorod Oblast** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Губернатор Белгородской области) is the highest official and the head of the executive power of [Belgorod Oblast](478857.md), a region in Central Russia.

## History of office

The office of the [Head of Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_Administration) was introduced in Russia in August 1991 during the downfall of the Soviet Union. Administrations and their Heads were supposed to replace Soviet executive committees (*ispolkoms*) and their chairmen. The first head of Belgorod Oblast Administration Viktor Berestovoy was appointed by president [Boris Yeltsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Yeltsin) in November 1991. Two years later he was removed from office for his support of the [Supreme Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Soviet_of_Russia) and Vice President [Alexander Rutskoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Rutskoy) in the [1993 constitutional crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Russian_constitutional_crisis).[^cite_note-1]

Berestovoy's successor [Yevgeny Savchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yevgeny_Savchenko), had previously worked as deputy head of the Main Directorate of Crop Production of the [Ministry of Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Agriculture_\(Russia\)). In office from October 1993 to September 2020, he is the longest-serving Russian governor in post-Soviet history.
