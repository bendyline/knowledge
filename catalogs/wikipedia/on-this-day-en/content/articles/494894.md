**Cherkasy Oblast**,[^cite_note-6] also referred to as **Cherkashchyna**,[^cite_note-7] is an [oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Ukraine) (province) in central [Ukraine](31750.md) located along the [Dnieper River](380138.md). The [administrative center](181337.md) of the oblast is the city of [Cherkasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherkasy). The current population of the oblast is 1,160,744 (2022 estimate).[^cite_note-ua2022estimate-3]

## Geography

Spanning 20,900 square kilometres (8,100 sq mi), Cherkasy Oblast is the 18th largest oblast of Ukraine, comprising about 3.5% of the area of the country. The south flowing [Dnieper River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnieper_River) with the hilly western bank and the plain eastern bank divides the oblast into two unequal parts. The larger western part belongs to the [Dnieper Upland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnieper_Upland). The low-lying eastern part of the oblast used to be subject to the frequent Dnieper [flooding](50482.md) before the flow of the river became controlled by multiple [dams](51518.md) of [Hydroelectric Power Plants](381399.md) constructed along the river in the 20th century.



The [Ros River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ros_River) near [Korsun-Shevchenkivskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korsun-Shevchenkivskyi).

The oblast extends for 245 km from south-west to north-east, and for 150 km from north to south. The northernmost point of the oblast is located is near the village of [Kononivka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kononivka?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Zolotonosha Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zolotonosha_Raion) ([district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raion)), the southernmost point near the village of [Kolodyste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolodyste?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Zvenyhorodka Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zvenyhorodka_Raion), the westernmost point near the village of [Korytnya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korytnya?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Uman Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uman_Raion), and the easternmost point near the village of [Stetsivka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stetsivka?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Cherkasy Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherkasy_Raion). The [geometric centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_\(geometry\)) of the oblast is located near the village [Zhuravky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhuravky?action=edit\&redlink=1) of [Horodyshche Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horodyshche_Raion). The oblast borders [Kyiv Oblast](494884.md) to the north, [Kirovohrad Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirovohrad_Oblast) to the south, [Poltava Oblast](1567650.md) to the east, and [Vinnytsia Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinnytsia_Oblast) to the west.

## History

Main article: [History of Cherkasy Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Cherkasy_Oblast)

Cherkasy Oblast was created as part of the [Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic) on 7 January 1954 by the ukase of the [Presidium of the Supreme Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidium_of_the_Supreme_Soviet). On 26 April 1954 the ukase was approved by the [Supreme Soviet of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Soviet_of_the_Soviet_Union).

The oblast's territory was the major cities of [Cherkasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherkasy), [Smila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smila,_Ukraine) and [Uman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uman), their corresponding *[raions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raions)* (districts), as well as 30 former *raions* of the [Vinnytsia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinnytsia_Oblast), [Kyiv](494884.md), [Kirovohrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirovohrad_Oblast) and [Poltava Oblasts](1567650.md).

Archaeological discoveries have shown that people have inhabited the valley of the Dnieper River since time immemorial. The oldest objects excavated on the territory of the region date back to the [Stone Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_Age) – the [Palaeolithic period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleolithic).

[Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language) is the only official language on the whole territory of Cherkasy Oblast.[^cite_note-22]

According to a poll conducted by *[Rating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rating_\(sociological_group\))* from 16 November to 10 December 2018 as part of the project «Portraits of Regions», 85% of the residents of Cherkasy Oblast believed that the Ukrainian language should be the only state language on the entire territory of Ukraine. 8% believed that Ukrainian should be the only state language, while Russian should be the second official language in some regions of the country. 4% believed that Russian should become the second state language of the country. 3% found it difficult to answer.[^cite_note-23]

On 4 June 2021, [Cherkasy Oblast Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherkasy_Oblast_Council) approved the «Comprehensive Programme for the Development and Functioning of the Ukrainian Language in All Spheres of Public Life in Cherkasy Oblast for 2021—2025», the main objective of which is to strengthen the positions of the Ukrainian language in various spheres of public life in the oblast.[^cite_note-24]

According to the research of the *Content Analysis Centre*, conducted from 15 August to 15 September 2024, the topic of which was the ratio of Ukrainian and Russian languages in the Ukrainian segment of [social media](5897742.md), 84.3% of posts from Cherkasy Oblast were written in Ukrainian (80.3% in 2023, 75.9% in 2022, 26.7% in 2020), while 15.7% were written in Russian (19.7% in 2023, 24.1% in 2022, 73.3% in 2020).[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

## Economy

The economy of the Cherkasy Oblast is largely dominated by agriculture. While the [winter wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_wheat) and [sugar beets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_beet) are the main products grown in the oblast, [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), [corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [tobacco](30942.md) and [hemp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemp) are also grown. [Cattle breeding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle) is also important.

The industry is mainly concentrated in [Cherkasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherkasy), the oblast's capital and the largest city. A [chemical industry](58721.md) was developed in the city in late 1960s in addition to machine building, furniture making and agricultural processing.

## Nomenclature

See also: [Romanization of Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian)

Most of Ukraine's oblasts are named after their [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_\(political\)) cities, officially referred to as "oblast centers" ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): обласний центр, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *oblasnyi tsentr*). The name of each oblast is a [relative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_clause) [adjective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjective), formed by adding a feminine [suffix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffix) to the name of respective center city: *Cherkasy* is the center of the *Cherkaska oblast* (Cherkasy Oblast). Most oblasts are also sometimes referred to in a feminine noun form, following the convention of traditional regional place names, ending with the suffix "-shchyna", as is the case with the Cherkasy Oblast, *Cherkashchyna*.
