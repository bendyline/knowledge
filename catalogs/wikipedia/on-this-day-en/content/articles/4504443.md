The **Congress of New Caledonia** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Congrès de la Nouvelle-Calédonie*), a "territorial [congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress)" (*congrès territorial* or *congrès du territoire*), is the [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) of [New Caledonia](21342.md). The congress is headquartered at 1 Boulevard Vauban in downtown [Noumea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noumea). Local media in New Caledonia refer to the congress as "boulevard Vauban".

## Composition and electoral system

The congress has 54 members who serve five-year terms, 32 members from the [South Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Province,_New_Caledonia) Assembly, 15 members from the [North Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Province,_New_Caledonia) Assembly, and 7 members from [Loyalty Islands Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyalty_Islands_Province) Assembly. Members are elected proportionally based on the partisan makeup of all three assemblies of the [provinces of New Caledonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_New_Caledonia) with a 5% [threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election_threshold).[^cite_note-1]

## Results of parliamentary elections

### 2026 election results

&#x20;The political parties, aside from naturally being split based on socioeconomic ideological differences, are split along hard-line stances on possible New Caledonian independence from France. Both independentists and its opponents subscribe to various socioeconomic ideologies so the difference of opinion is usually rooted in favouring either [Kanak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanak_people) [nationalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalism), New Caledonian [separatism](286527.md) and independence as opposed to [French nationalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_nationalism) and New Caledonian [regionalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regionalism_\(politics\)).
