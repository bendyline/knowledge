The **World Meteorological Organization** (**WMO**) is a [specialized agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_specialized_agencies_of_the_United_Nations) of the [United Nations](31769.md) responsible for promoting international cooperation on [atmospheric science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_science), [climatology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climatology), [hydrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrology) and [geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics).[^cite_note-UIA-2][^cite_note-WMO_history-3]

The WMO originated from the [International Meteorological Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Meteorological_Organization) (IMO), a [nongovernmental organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nongovernmental_organization) founded in 1873 as a forum for exchanging weather data and research.[^cite_note-who_WMO-4] Proposals to reform the status and structure of the IMO culminated in the World Meteorological Convention of 1947, which formally established the World Meteorological Organization.[^cite_note-5] The Convention entered into force on 23 March 1950, and the following year the WMO began operations as an [intergovernmental organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intergovernmental_organization) within the [United Nations System](216034.md).

The WMO is made up of 193 countries and territories, and facilitates the "free and unrestricted" exchange of data, information, and research between the respective meteorological and hydrological institutions of its members.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-public.wmo.int-7] It also collaborates with nongovernmental partners and other international organizations on matters related to [environmental protection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_protection), [climate change](5042951.md), resource management, and [socioeconomic development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_development).[^cite_note-8]

Headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, the WMO is governed by the World Meteorological Congress, composed of member states, which meets every four years to set policies and priorities. The Congress is led by an Executive Council led by the President, currently Abdulla Al Mandous of UAE.[^cite_note-9]

## History

Weather is a global phenomenon that ignores national borders. International cooperation on weather reporting began in the 1600s, but became a significant factor with the invention of the [telegraph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telegraphy) in 1843.[^cite_note-shining-75-10] [Matthew Fontaine Maury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Fontaine_Maury), of the [US Navy](20518076.md), initiated the convening of the first true international meteorological conference from late August through early September 1853.[^cite_note-11] This led to the foundation of the [International Meteorological Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Meteorological_Organization) in 1873. The early efforts where non-governmental but grew into an intergovernmental effort.[^cite_note-shining-75-10]

In 1950, IMO was superseded by the World Meteorological Organization which facilitates the "free and unrestricted" exchange of data, information, and research between the respective meteorological and hydrological institutions of its members.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-public.wmo.int-7]

## Governance



German meteorologist Gerhard Adrian, president of the World Meteorological Organization, in 2019

The WMO was established by the Convention of the World Meteorological Organization,[^cite_note-13] signed 11 October 1947 and ratified on 23 March 1950. The Convention serves as the constituent treaty of the WMO, setting forth its purposes, governance, and general framework.

The WMO hierarchy:

- The World Meteorological Congress,

  [^cite_note-14]

  &#x20;the supreme body of the Organization, determines policy. Each member state and territory is represented by a Permanent Representative with WMO when Congress meets every four years. Congress elects the President and vice-presidents of the Organization and members of the Executive Council; and appoints the Secretary-General.

- The Executive Council (EC) implements Congress decisions.

- The Secretariat is an eight-department organization with a staff of 200 headed by a Secretary-General, who can serve a maximum of two four-year terms.

  [^cite_note-15]

The annually published WMO Statement on the status of the World Climate[^cite_note-16] provides details of global, regional and national temperatures and extreme weather events. It also provides information on long-term climate change indicators including atmospheric concentrations of greenhouse gases, sea level rise, and sea ice extent. The year 2016 was the hottest year on record, with many weather and climate extremes, according to the most recent WMO report.[^cite_note-17]

As of August 2023, the WMO has a membership of 193 member states and territories.[^cite_note-18]

## Meteorological codes

In keeping with its mandate to promote the standardization of meteorological observations, the WMO maintains numerous code forms for the representation and exchange of meteorological, oceanographical, and hydrological data. The traditional code forms, such as [SYNOP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SYNOP), [CLIMAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CLIMAT) and [TEMP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TEMP_\(meteorology\)), are character-based and their coding is position-based. Newer WMO code forms are designed for portability, extensibility and universality. These are [BUFR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BUFR), and, for gridded geo-positioned data, [GRIB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GRIB).

## Recognitions received

In 2007, the [Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change](15030.md) (IPCC), a joint creation of the WMO and the [United Nations Environment Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Environment_Programme) (UNEP), received the [Nobel Peace Prize](26230922.md) "for their efforts to build up and disseminate greater knowledge about [anthropogenic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_impact_on_the_environment) (man-made) [climate change](5042951.md), and to lay the foundations for the measures that are needed to counteract such change."[^cite_note-22]

In 2025, WMO's IMO Legacy Collection was inscribed on UNESCO's [Memory of the World International Register](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory_of_the_World_International_Register). The collection consists of physical and digital books, pamphlets, maps, and graphs published by IMO during the nineteenth and early-twentieth centuries.[^cite_note-23] It is currently being digitized and made available from WMO's e-Library.

## Use of the International System of Units

WMO states that "the [International System of Units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_System_of_Units) (SI) should be used as the system of units for the evaluation of meteorological elements included in reports for international exchange."[^cite_note-25] The following units, which include units which are not SI units, are recommended by the WMO for meteorological observations:

- [Degrees Celsius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degrees_Celsius) (°C) for [temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature), or alternatively [Kelvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelvin) (K).

- [Metres per second](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metres_per_second) (m/s) for [wind speed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_speed).

- [Degrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degree_\(angle\)) clockwise from north (°) for [wind direction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_direction), or alternatively on the scale 0–36, where 36 is the wind directly from north and 09 is the directly wind from east.

## Membership

As of 2023, WMO Members include a total of 187 Member States and 6 Member Territories.[^cite_note-Members-38]

Eight United Nations member states are not members of WMO: [Equatorial Guinea](9366.md), [Grenada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenada), [Liechtenstein](17810.md), [Marshall Islands](19147.md), [Palau](23372.md), [Saint Kitts and Nevis](27198.md), [Saint Vincent and the Grenadines](27228.md) and [San Marino](27248.md). [Cook Islands](7067.md) and [Niue](21228.md) are WMO Members but non-members of the United Nations. [Vatican City](32408.md) and [State of Palestine](241405.md) and the [states with limited recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_states_with_limited_recognition) are not members of either organization.

The six WMO Member Territories are the [British Caribbean Territories](207114.md#Present_overseas_territories) (joint meteorological organization and membership),[^cite_note-Members-38] [French Polynesia](10737.md), [Hong Kong](13404.md), [Macau](19068.md), [New Caledonia](21342.md), and [Curaçao](65205.md) and [Sint Maarten](9529005.md) with a joint meteorological service and membership[^cite_note-Members-38][^cite_note-39]
