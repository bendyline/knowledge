The **China women's national basketball team** represents the [People's Republic of China](5405.md) in international [women's basketball](667977.md) tournaments.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] The national team is governed by the [Chinese Basketball Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Basketball_Association_\(organisation\)) (CBA).

## History

Basketball was first introduced in China by [YMCA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YMCA) [missionaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missionary) by the second half of the 1890s,[^cite_note-\:0-4] remaining popular in urban areas during the [Republican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China_\(1912%E2%80%931949\)) period.[^cite_note-5] With the creation of the [People's Republic of China](5405.md), the sport retained its popularity,[^cite_note-\:0-4] being introduced in [PE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_education) in schools.[^cite_note-\:1-6]

The first [color movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_motion_picture_film) in China about sports was [Xie Jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xie_Jin)'s *[Woman Basketball Player No. 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woman_Basketball_Player_No._5)*, from 1957.[^cite_note-\:0-4] By the 1970s, basketball was, alongside volleyball,[^cite_note-7] one of the most popular women's sport in the country.[^cite_note-8]

China stayed out of international competitions since 1958, when the [International Olympic Committee](15147.md) chose [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Taipei_women's_national_basketball_team) as the representative of the country.[^cite_note-\:1-6]

After the People's Republic of China became the representative of [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_\(historical_region\)) in international competitions, the basketball team won several accolades, including several golds in the [Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_Asian_Games) and the [Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Women's_Asia_Cup), a third place in the [1984 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_team_rosters), and a third place in the [1983 Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women).[^cite_note-\:1-6]

For the first time since 1994, China got a medal in the [2022 Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup) after being defeated by [Team USA](7129278.md) in the finals.[^cite_note-9]
