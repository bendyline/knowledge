A **super app** or **super-app** (also known as an **everything app**) is a [mobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_app) or [web application](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_application) that can provide multiple services including [payment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payment_processing) and [instant messaging](55951.md) services, effectively becoming an all-encompassing, self-contained, commerce and communication online [platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_platform_\(infrastructure\)) that embraces many aspects of personal and commercial life. Notable examples of super apps include [Tencent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tencent)'s [WeChat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WeChat) in [China](5405.md), [Tata Neu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Neu) in [India](14533.md), [Grab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grab_\(company\)) in [Southeast Asia](28741.md) and [Max](80404560.md) in [Russia](25391.md).

For end users, a super app is an application that provides a set of core features while also giving access to independently developed miniapps. For app developers, a super app is an application integrated with the capabilities of platforms and ecosystems that allows third-parties to develop and publish miniapps.[^cite_note-1]

## History

The super app term was first used to describe WeChat[^cite_note-2] when it combined the instant messaging service with the [digital wallet](5719877.md) function.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Recognition of WeChat as a super app stems from its combination of messaging, payments, e-commerce, and much more within a single application, making it indispensable for many users. WeChat's establishment of the super app model has led companies like [Meta](62420226.md) to try to build similar applications outside of China.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

In India, [Tata Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Group) has announced that it is currently developing a super app named [Tata Neu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Neu).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Major Indian companies like [Paytm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paytm), [PhonePe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhonePe), and ITC Maars also have apps in development that might constitute super apps.[^cite_note-10]

In Southeast Asia, Grab and [Gojek](51272668.md) lay claim to the super app classification despite lacking many of the features offered by WeChat.[^cite_note-\:0-11] Accordingly, growth-stage companies like [Shopee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopee), [Traveloka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traveloka), and [AirAsia](285974.md) have also expanded the range of services offered by their respective applications.[^cite_note-\:0-11]

[Elon Musk](909036.md), prior to his acquisition of [Twitter](9988187.md), stated that he planned for Twitter to become an "everything app" known as "X";[^cite_note-12] in 2023, the service added an AI chatbot known as "[Grok](75223933.md)" as well as integrated [job search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_search) tools known as "X Hiring".[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] In January 2025, X announced its intent to offer a digital wallet service in the future.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] Later in the year, X revamped its direct messaging system as "Chat".[^cite_note-17]

## Notable examples

### Alipay

[Alipay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alipay) is a third-party [mobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_payment) and [online payment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_payment) platform established in [Hangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou), China in February 2004 by [Alibaba Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alibaba_Group) and its founder [Jack Ma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Ma). It operates in association with [Ant Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant_Group), an affiliate company of the Chinese [Alibaba Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alibaba_Group).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20]

### Gojek

[Gojek](51272668.md) is an Indonesian on-demand multiservice digital platform and [fintech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fintech) payment super app. Established in [Jakarta](16275.md) in 2010, as a call center to connect consumers to courier delivery and two-wheeled ride-hailing services, it launched its mobile app in 2015 with four services: GoRide, GoSend, GoShop, and GoFood, which has since expanded to offer over 20 services. In 2021, it merged with another Indonesian unicorn, [Tokopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokopedia), forming the [decacorn](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/decacorn) GoTo Gojek Tokopedia.[^cite_note-21]

### Grab

[Grab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grab_\(company\)) is a Southeast Asian technology company headquartered in [Singapore](27318.md) and [Indonesia](14579.md). Founded in 2012 as the *MyTeksi* app in [Kuala Lumpur](16854.md), Malaysia, it expanded the following year as *GrabTaxi*, before moving its headquarters to Singapore in 2014 and rebranding officially as Grab. In addition to ride-hailing and transportation services, the company's mobile app also offers [food delivery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_delivery) and digital payment services.[^cite_note-22]

### Max

[Max](80404560.md) is a messenger from the Russian company [VK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VK_\(company\)), positioned as a super app. The application combines messaging, calls, and channels features with the integration of additional services: payments, miniapps, taxi ordering, deliveries, and other everyday services are available within a single interface. The goal is to unite communication and routine tasks in a unified ecosystem.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

### Tata Neu

[Tata Neu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Neu) is a multipurpose super app, developed in [India](14533.md) by the [Tata Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Group). It is the country's first super app. The app was launched to coincide with the start of a 2022 [Indian Premier League](13260340.md) cricket match.

### WeChat

[WeChat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WeChat) is a Chinese multipurpose [instant messaging](55951.md), [social media](5897742.md) and [mobile payment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_payment) app. First released in 2011, it became the world's largest standalone mobile app in 2018, with over 1 billion monthly [active users](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_users). WeChat provides text messaging, hold-to-talk voice messaging, broadcast (one-to-many) messaging, video conferencing, video games, the sharing of photographs and videos and location sharing.[^cite_note-BBC-News-26]

## Criticism

Although apps that fit the super app classification can offer users a wider variety of services in comparison to single-purpose alternatives, [internet regulators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_media_regulation) in regions such as the US and Europe have become more concerned about the overall power of the technology industry and have become more critical of companies developing such apps. In China, WeChat and other local firms have been ordered to open up their platforms to rivals by local regulators.[^cite_note-27]

There are also reports that suggest it might be difficult to replicate WeChat's super app model. This stems partly from the peaking of [smartphone penetration rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_smartphone_penetration) in many regions worldwide, which has led to overcrowded app stores and tighter restrictions on targeted advertising as regulators assert more control over the companies. From a technical viewpoint, single-purpose apps are comparatively faster, more responsive and easier to navigate than super apps, which helps improve the overall [user experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User_experience).[^cite_note-28] Super-apps are also likelier to store larger amounts of personal data to facilitate the delivery of their services, so users run a greater risk of becoming victims of severe data breaches. In 2020, this unfolded with [Tokopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokopedia), which had the data of 91 million of its users stolen and shared by [crackers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_hacker).[^cite_note-\:0-11]

It has also been noted that a user who loses access to their account or is banned from a super app generally loses access to multiple real-life services and digital applications; the Chinese government has used this approach to penalize people who shared the photos of the [Sitong Bridge protest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitong_Bridge_protest).[^cite_note-29]
