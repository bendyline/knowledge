The **Chairman of the State Great Khural** ([Mongolian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_language): Улсын Их Хурлын дарга, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Mongolian): *Ulsyn Ikh Khurlyn darga*) is the [presiding officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_\(politics\)) of the [State Great Khural](2226080.md), the unicameral parliament of Mongolia, and a member of the [National Security Council of Mongolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Council_of_Mongolia).[^cite_note-5] It is the second-highest position, after the [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Mongolia) and before the [Prime Minister](439904.md), in [Mongolia](19271.md). In case of the absence or death of the president, the chairman serves as acting head of state until a new president is elected or inaugurated.[^cite_note-\:1-6]

## Election

According to the 11th article of the law regarding the State Great Khural in the [Constitution of Mongolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Mongolia), the chair is nominated by parties and coalitions with parliamentary seats and is elected from among its members by a majority vote. If a candidate fails to receive a majority support from members present at the vote, a new candidate shall be nominated by the State Great Khural.[^cite_note-\:1-6][^cite_note-7]

The chairman is elected once the mandates of members are validated on the first session of the elected State Great Khural. During the vote, the eldest member presides the assembly and announces the results.[^cite_note-\:1-6]
