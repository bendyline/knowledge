**Zenyazen Roberto Escobar García**[^cite_note-1] (4 June 1984 – c. 4 September 2026) was a Mexican politician affiliated with the [National Regeneration Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Regeneration_Movement) (Morena). A native of the state of [Veracruz](63125.md), he was the incumbent [federal deputy](411807.md) for [Veracruz's 16th district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_federal_electoral_district_of_Veracruz) at the time of his death.[^cite_note-sil66-2][^cite_note-infobae-3]

## Early life and political career

Zenyazen Escobar García was born in [Nogales, Veracruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nogales,_Veracruz), on 4 June 1984. In 2009, he began work as a teacher on the [Telesecundaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telesecundaria) remote-learning system. He later earned a bachelor's degree in communication science from the [Universidad de Sotoviento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_de_Sotoviento?action=edit\&redlink=1) in 2018 and a master's in education science from the [Centro de Estudios Avanzados de las Américas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro_de_Estudios_Avanzados_de_las_Am%C3%A9ricas?action=edit\&redlink=1) in 2021.[^cite_note-sil66-2]

Before becoming a politician, Escobar worked as a [stripper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stripper).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-proceso-5] From 2016 to 2018, he held a plurinominal seat and coordinated the Morena caucus in the [Congress of Veracruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_Veracruz), and from 2018 to 2023 he was secretary of education in the Veracruz state government during the administration of [Cuitláhuac García Jiménez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuitl%C3%A1huac_Garc%C3%ADa_Jim%C3%A9nez).[^cite_note-proceso-5] In 2023, he sought Morena's nomination to fight the [2024 gubernatorial election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Mexican_local_elections) in Veracruz, but lost to [Rocío Nahle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roc%C3%ADo_Nahle).[^cite_note-6]

In the [2024 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Mexican_general_election), he was elected to the [Chamber of Deputies](411807.md) to represent Veracruz's [16th district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_federal_electoral_district_of_Veracruz) ([Córdoba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%B3rdoba_\(Veracruz\))) during the [66th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LXVI_Legislature_of_the_Mexican_Congress).[^cite_note-sil66-2] In [Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_\(Mexico\)), he served as the secretary of the lower house's standing committees on climate change and sustainability and on naval affairs. He was also a member of the committee on economy, commerce and competitiveness and of the Mexico–Kuwait Friendship Group.[^cite_note-7]

On 28 May 2026, he scuffled with [Carlos Gutiérrez Mancilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Guti%C3%A9rrez_Mancilla) of the [Institutional Revolutionary Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institutional_Revolutionary_Party) (PRI) on the Chamber floor, after Gutiérrez called him a "damn stripper" and other opposition deputies accused him of being inebriated.[^cite_note-infobae-3][^cite_note-8]

## Personal life

Escobar García was married for more than 25 years to Liliana López Coronado, with whom he had two children.[^cite_note-9] López Coronado, previously the state attorney for the protection of children,[^cite_note-10] won a position as a family judge on the Superior Court of Justice of Veracruz in the [June 2025 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Mexican_judicial_elections).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

### Death

Escobar García was found dead inside a [Mercedes-Benz](18564164.md) automobile along [Federal Highway 140](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Federal_Highway_140) in the municipality of [Puente Nacional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puente_Nacional,_Veracruz), Veracruz, on 4 September 2026.[^cite_note-proceso-5] He was 42. Escobar had a gunshot wound to his head.[^cite_note-infobae-3][^cite_note-elpais-13] One bullet casing was found in the car, and the car's doors were locked.[^cite_note-14]

[Ricardo Ahued Bardahuil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_Ahued_Bardahuil), secretary of government of Veracruz, said that it did not appear to be an attack, and Governor [Rocío Nahle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roc%C3%ADo_Nahle) sent her condolences to the family.[^cite_note-infobae-3][^cite_note-elpais-13] Because of Escobar's position as a federal deputy, the investigation is being led by the office of the [Attorney-General of the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney_General_\(Mexico\)) (FGR)[^cite_note-proceso-5] which, on 10 September 2026, announced that the investigation remained ongoing but that the evidence indicated a probable death by [suicide](16831059.md).[^cite_note-15]
