# Supreme Tribunal of Justice of Bolivia

The **Supreme Court of Justice** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Tribunal Supremo de Justicia*) is the highest court of ordinary jurisdiction in [Bolivia](3462.md), based in [Sucre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sucre). Its powers are set out in Articles 181–185 of the 2009 Constitution and the Law of the Judicial Organ (Law 025, promulgated on 24 June 2010).[^cite_note-cons12-1][^cite_note-2] It was first seated on 2 January 2012.[^cite_note-CS2012-3]

## History

The Court was created to supersede the [Supreme Court of Bolivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Bolivia), which operated from 1825 to 2011. It was first seated on 2 January 2012.[^cite_note-CS2012-3] Due to vacancies on the Court and other problems in its final years, the Supreme Court of Justice inherited a backlog of some 8,800 cases in January 2012, which it was charged with resolving within 36 to 48 months.[^cite_note-CS2012-3]

## Court

The Court is made up of nine members and nine alternates, representing the nine [departments of Bolivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Bolivia), elected in popular, nonpartisan elections to terms of six years. The [Plurinational Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurinational_Legislative_Assembly) preselects up to 36 candidates before the election. Reelection is forbidden.
