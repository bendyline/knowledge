**Gorgan** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): گرگان; [\[ɡoɾˈɡɒːn\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Persian) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/d/d3/Gorgan.ogg/Gorgan.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/d/d3/Gorgan.ogg/Gorgan.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>)[^cite_note-7] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Gorgan_County\)) of [Gorgan County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorgan_County), [Golestan](448067.md) province, Iran, serving as capital of the province, the county,[^cite_note-Golestan_Province_Structure-8] and the district.[^cite_note-Mazandaran_Province_Structure-9] It lies approximately 400 km (250 mi) to the northeast of the national capital [Tehran](57654.md), and some 30 km (19 mi) away from the [Caspian Sea](19653787.md).

## History

Main article: [Hyrcania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyrcania)

There are several archaeological sites near Gorgan, including [Tureng Tepe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tureng_Tepe) and [Shah Tepe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shah_Tepe), in which remains dating from the [Neolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic) and [Chalcolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalcolithic) eras. Some other important Neolithic sites in the area are [Yarim Tepe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarim_Tepe_\(Iran\)), and [Sange Chaxmaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sange_Chaxmaq). The nearby [Shahroud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahroud) Plain has many such sites. More than 50 are on the [Gorgan Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorgan_Plain).[^cite_note-10]



Historic wall of Gorgan signs

According to the Greek historian [Arrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrian), [Zadracarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zadracarta) was the largest city of [Hyrcania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyrcania) and the site of the "royal palace".[^cite_note-11] The term means "the yellow city", and it was given to it from the great number of oranges, lemons, and other fruit trees which grew in the outskirts of that city.[^cite_note-12]

Hyrcania became part of the [Achaemenid Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaemenid_Empire) during the reign of [Cyrus the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrus_the_Great) (559–530 BC), its founder, or his successor [Cambyses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambyses_II) (530-522 BC).

The [Great Wall of Gorgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wall_of_Gorgan), the second biggest defensive wall in the world, was built in the [Parthian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parthian_Empire) and [Sasanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasanian_Empire) periods.

At the time of the [Sasanians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasanians), "Gurgan" appeared as the name of a city, province capital, and province.[^cite_note-Iranica-Bivar-13]

Gorgan maintained its independence as a [Zoroastrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoroastrian) state even after [Persia was conquered by the Muslim Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim_conquest_of_Persia) in the 7th century.

In 1210, the city was invaded and sacked by the army of [Kingdom of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Georgia) under command of the brothers [Mkhargrdzeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mkhargrdzeli).[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

"Old Gorgan" was destroyed during the [Mongol invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongol_invasion_of_Khwarezmia_and_Eastern_Iran) in the 13th century, and the center of the region was moved to what was called "Astarabad", which is currently called "Gorgan".

Gorgan with its surrounding regions was sometimes considered part of the [Tabaristan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabaristan) region.

Astarabad was an important political and religious city during the [Qajar era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qajar_Iran).

### Location

The wide [Dasht-e Gorgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorgan_Plain) (Plains of Gorgan) is located north of the city and geographically bounded by 37°00' - 37°30' north latitude and 54°00' - 54°30' east longitude, covering an area of about 170 square kilometres (66 sq mi).

Some 150 km (93 mi) east of Gorgan is the [Golestan National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golestan_National_Park), home to a large portion of the [fauna of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fauna_of_Iran).

Gorgān Dam is situated 60 km northeast of Gorgan city and has a capacity of 100 million [cubic meters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubic_meter).[^cite_note-18]

### Climate

Gorgan has a subtropical [mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Csa*, [Trewartha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trewartha_climate_classification): *Cs*), with hot, humid summers and cool, wet winters. In general, Golestan has a moderate and humid climate known as "the moderate Caspian climate". The effective factors behind such a climate are [Alborz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alborz) mountain range, the direction of the mountains, the height of the area, the neighborhood to the sea, vegetation surface, local winds, altitude, and weather fronts. As a result of the above factors, three different climates exist in the region: plain moderate, mountainous, and semi-arid. Gorgan valley has a semi-arid climate. The average annual temperature is 17.8 °C (64.0 °F).

The annual precipitation is roughly 584 millimetres (23.0 in), and usually does not exceed 700 millimetres (28 in) or dip below 350 millimetres (14 in). The driest year on record was 1953 with only 182.7 millimetres (7.19 in) of precipitation, while the wettest year was 1959 with 1,579.9 millimetres (62.20 in).[^cite_note-19]

## Sports

Gorgan has a sports venue, Shohada stadium, which is located north of the city limits near the village of [Karimabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karimabad,_Gorgan).[^cite_note-27]

[Shahrdari Gorgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahrdari_Gorgan_BC) competes in the [Iranian Basketball Super League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Basketball_Super_League) and [Etka Gorgan F.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etka_Gorgan_F.C.) competes in the [Azadegan League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azadegan_League).
