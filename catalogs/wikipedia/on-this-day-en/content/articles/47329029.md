The **France–Italy border** is 515 km (320 mi) long. It runs from the [Alps](981.md) in the north, a region in which it passes over [Mont Blanc](44768.md), down to the [Mediterranean](19006.md) coast in the south. Three national parks are located along the border: [Vanoise National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanoise_National_Park) and [Mercantour National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercantour_National_Park) on the French side and [Gran Paradiso National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Paradiso_National_Park) on the Italian side.

## Features

The France–Italy border is mainly mountainous. It is 515 kilometres (320 mi) long,[^cite_note-1] in southeast France and northwest Italy. It begins at the west [tripoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripoint) of France–Italy–Switzerland ([45°55′23″N 07°02′40″E  /  45.92306°N 7.04444°E  / 45.92306; 7.04444](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=France%E2%80%93Italy_border\&params=45_55_23_N_07_02_40_E_)) near the top of [Mont Dolent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mont_Dolent) (3,820 m), in the French commune of [Chamonix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamonix) (department of [Haute-Savoie](90605.md)), the Italian city of [Courmayeur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courmayeur) ([Aosta Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aosta_Valley)) and the Swiss commune of [Orsières](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orsi%C3%A8res) (canton of [Valais](198789.md)).

The boundary then follows a general direction towards south, to the [Mediterranean](19006.md), it reaches the sea at [Menton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menton) in France and [Ventimiglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventimiglia) in Italy. The border separates three [regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Italy) ([Aosta Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aosta_Valley), [Piedmont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedmont) and [Liguria](71864.md)) and four [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Italy) of Italy ([Aosta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aosta_Valley), [Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Turin), [Cuneo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Cuneo) and [Imperia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Imperia)) from two [regions](58846.md) ([Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes](45093325.md) and [Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provence-Alpes-C%C3%B4te_d'Azur)) and five [departments](38523.md) of France ([Haute-Savoie](90605.md), [Savoie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savoie), [Hautes-Alpes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hautes-Alpes), [Alpes-de-Haute-Provence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpes-de-Haute-Provence) and [Alpes-Maritimes](80989.md)).

In 1999, there was [a large fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mont_Blanc_tunnel_fire) in the [Mont Blanc Tunnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mont_Blanc_Tunnel) after a truck crashed into other vehicles. Despite the intervention of emergency teams from both sides of the border, 39 people died. The tunnel was closed for nearly three years following the fire.[^cite_note-2]

The border between the two countries dates back to that separating the [Kingdom of Sardinia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Sardinia) and France during the 19th century. In 1860, the [Treaty of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Turin_\(1860\)) links the [Savoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savoy) and [County of Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_of_Nice) to France; the border between the French Empire and the Kingdom of Sardinia was surveyed the following year.

At the conclusion of the [Battle of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_France) in [World War II](32927.md), Italy [claimed and administered French territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_occupation_of_France) under the terms of the [armistice of 24 June 1940](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco-Italian_Armistice) (the Franco-Italian armistice signed at the Incisa villa near Rome) which were extended from 11 November 1942. The [Germans](11867.md) occupied the Italian zone from 1943, and the territory was finally liberated by France in 1944. The border was then changed by the [Treaty of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Paris_\(1947\)) in 1947, when France acquired [Tende](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tende), [La Brigue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Brigue), [Mont Chaberton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mont_Chaberton) and the Lake of [Mont Cenis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mont_Cenis).

In the 21st century, an ongoing issue to be resolved concerns the demarcation of the border at the top of [Mont Blanc](44768.md).[^cite_note-3]
