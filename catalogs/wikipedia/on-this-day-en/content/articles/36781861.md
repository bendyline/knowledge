**Tumbler Ridge Secondary School** (**TRSS**) was a public [secondary school](554992.md#By_country) in [Tumbler Ridge](2284877.md), British Columbia, Canada. TRSS was operated by [School District 59 Peace River South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_District_59_Peace_River_South) and was the designated secondary school for the town's primary school. TRSS was a participant in the school district's International Student Study Program.[^cite_note-2] For the 2025–26 school year, the school had an enrollment of 191 students from [grades 7 to 12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Canada#Levels_in_education).[^cite_note-school_population_gov_stat-1]

On February 10, 2026, a [former student opened fire at the school](82386818.md) with a [modified handgun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homemade_firearm) and a [long gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_gun) after killing her mother and step-brother at their home in Tumbler Ridge.[^cite_note-ap1-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-\:5-5] The shooting resulted in the deaths of five students and an education assistant at the school and injuries to 27 other people, before the shooter died from a self-inflicted gunshot wound.[^cite_note-NYT11-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-GN-8][^cite_note-canadashootinglatest-9] On February 16, the school announced that students would be returning to school in "portable facilities" and would have trauma experts on site.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

On May 7, 2026, the provincial government of British Columbia announced that Tumbler Ridge Secondary School would be demolished and rebuilt.[^cite_note-12] As of August 2026, the demolition is currently underway,[^cite_note-13] and the building has been flattened.[^cite_note-14]
