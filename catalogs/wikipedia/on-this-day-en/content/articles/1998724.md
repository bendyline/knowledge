**Elektrostal** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Электросталь, pronounced [\[ɛˌlʲektrɐˈstalʲ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Russian)) is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) in [Moscow Oblast](372315.md), Russia, located 58 kilometers (36 mi) east of [Moscow](19004.md). Population: 155,196 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2010Census-2] 146,294 ([2002 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Russian_census));[^cite_note-PopCensus-6] 152,463 ([1989 Soviet census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Soviet_census));[^cite_note-Census1989-7] 135,000 (1977); 123,000 (1970); 97,000 (1959); 43,000 (1939). It was previously known as *Zatishye* (until 1928).

## Etymology

The name *Elektrostal* originates from the Russian words of Электро (*Elektro*), lit. '[Electric](9550.md)' and Сталь (*Stal*), lit. '[Steel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steel)'.

It was known as **Zatishye** (Затишье) until 1928.

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), it is incorporated as **Elektrostal [City Under Oblast Jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance)**—an administrative unit with the status equal to that of the [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Moscow_Oblast).[^cite_note-Ref1406-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), Elektrostal City Under Oblast Jurisdiction is incorporated as **Elektrostal Urban Okrug**.[^cite_note-Ref780-3]

## Economy

Elektrostal is a center of [metallurgy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallurgy) and heavy machinery manufacturing. Major companies include:

- Elektrostal metallurgical factory

- Elektrostal chemical-mechanical factory

- Elektrostal Heavy Engineering Works, JSC is a designer and manufacturer of equipment for producing seamless hot-rolled, cold-rolled and welded steel materials and metallurgical equipment.

- [MSZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ELEMASH_Machine-Building_Plant), also known as *Elemash*, Russia's largest producer of fuel rod assemblies for nuclear power plants, which are exported to many countries in Europe.

- [Wildberries](58659019.md) distribution center

The 9th radio center in Elektrostal is home to a high power [medium wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medium_wave) transmitter.

The first [S-400 Triumf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S-400) missile defense system was deployed at Elektrostal, becoming fully operational on 1 July 2007.[^cite_note-operational-11]

### Transportation



LiAZ-5256 bus

Elektrostal is linked by [Elektrichka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elektrichka) suburban electric trains to Moscow's [Kursky Rail Terminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kursky_Rail_Terminal) with a travel time of 1 hour and 20 minutes. Long-distance buses link Elektrostal to [Noginsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noginsk), [Moscow](19004.md) and other nearby towns. Local public transport includes buses.
