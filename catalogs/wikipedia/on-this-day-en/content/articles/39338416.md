# Law of Slovakia

&#x20; The [law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law) of the [Slovak Republic](26830.md) is [civil law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_law_\(legal_system\)).[^cite_note-1]

## Constitution

The [Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Slovakia) was passed in 1992.

## Legislature

The legislature is the [National Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Council_\(Slovakia\)).

## Legislation

[Legislation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislation)[^cite_note-2] includes Acts.

## Courts and judiciary

There is a [Supreme Court of Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Slovakia) and a [Constitutional Court of Slovakia](15315217.md).

## Legal practitioners

There is a [Slovak Bar Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovak_Bar_Association?action=edit\&redlink=1) (Slovakian: *Slovenská advokátska komora*).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Criminal law

See also: [Capital punishment in Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_punishment_in_Slovakia)

The Criminal Code of 2005 replaced that of 1961.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## Civil code

The [Občiansky zákonník](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ob%C4%8Diansky_z%C3%A1konn%C3%ADk?action=edit\&redlink=1), or Slovak Civil Code, is derived from the Czechoslovak Civil Code of 1964.[^cite_note-9]
