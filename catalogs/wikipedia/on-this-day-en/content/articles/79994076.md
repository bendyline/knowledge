The **[2025–26 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_in_Scottish_football)** was [Celtic](69708.md)'s 132nd season of competitive football.

## Pre-season and friendlies

Celtic faced [Queen's Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_Park_F.C.) and [Cork City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cork_City_F.C.) in early July.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] They then travelled to Portugal for a pre-season training camp, where they played [Estrela da Amadora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.F._Estrela_da_Amadora) and [Sporting CP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_CP).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] On returning to Glasgow, the Scottish champions faced [Newcastle United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle_United_F.C.) at Celtic Park.[^cite_note-5] Their pre-season schedule concluded with a mini tournament in Italy, where they played [Ajax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Ajax) and [Al-Ahli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ahli_Saudi_FC).[^cite_note-6]
