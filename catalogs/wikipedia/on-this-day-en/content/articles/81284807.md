The **2025 Korean Series**, (known as the *2025 [Shinhan SOL Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinhan_Bank) Korean Series* for sponsorship reasons) was the championship series of the [KBO League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KBO_League) for the [2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_KBO_League_season). The 44th edition of the Korean Series, it featured the [LG Twins](3726792.md) and [Hanwha Eagles](4086558.md).[^cite_note-1] The LG Twins, who had best record during the regular season, received three round byes and a direct berth into the Series, while the Hanwha Eagles, who had the second best record, received two round byes and defeated the [Samsung Lions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung_Lions) in the semifinals to reach the Series. The defending [2024 Korean Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Korean_Series) champions [Kia Tigers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kia_Tigers) did not qualify for the 2025 postseason tournament.

The Twins won their 4th Korean Series title, beating the Eagles 4–1.[^cite_note-2]
