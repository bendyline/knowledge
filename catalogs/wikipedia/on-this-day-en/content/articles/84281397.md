The **[Women's time trial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCI_Road_World_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_time_trial)** of the [2026 UCI Road World Championships](82925241.md) was a cycling event that took place on 20 September 2026 in [Montreal](7954681.md), [Canada](5042916.md). It was the 33rd edition of the championship, for which [Marlen Reusser](55330743.md) of [Switzerland](26748.md) was the defending champion, having won in [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_UCI_Road_World_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_time_trial).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

The race was won by Swiss rider [Marlen Reusser](55330743.md) for the second year in a row, beating British rider [Zoe Bäckstedt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoe_B%C3%A4ckstedt) by 40 seconds, with German rider [Franziska Koch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franziska_Koch_\(cyclist\)) a further 5 seconds slower.[^cite_note-3]

## Course

For the first time since [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_UCI_Road_World_Championships), the time trial course for elite men and women was identical in length – a 39.2-kilometre (24.4-mile) route through Montreal, racing along the [Saint Lawrence River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Lawrence_River), crossing the [St. Lawrence River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Lawrence_River), passing through the [Circuit Gilles Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_Gilles_Villeneuve), and returning via the [Concorde Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pont_de_la_Concorde_\(Montreal\)).[^cite_note-cyclingnews-routes2-4] It was the longest time trial to be tackled by elite women at the UCI Road World Championships.
