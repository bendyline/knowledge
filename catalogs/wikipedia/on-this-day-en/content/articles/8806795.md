# List of NBA regular season team records

"NBA records" and "List of NBA records" redirect here. For NBA playoff and Finals records, see [List of NBA postseason records](33504349.md). For All-Star records, see [List of NBA All-Star Game records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NBA_All-Star_Game_records).

&#x20;This article lists **all-time records** achieved in the [NBA](22093.md) regular season in major statistical categories recognized by the league, including those set by teams in a game, season, and career. The NBA also recognizes records from its original incarnation, the [Basketball Association of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_Association_of_America) (BAA).

See also: [List of NBA regular season individual records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NBA_regular_season_individual_records)

## Team game records

***Note**: Other than the longest game and disqualifications in a game, all records in this section are since the 24-second shot clock was instituted for [1954–55](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1954%E2%80%9355_NBA_season) season onward.*

- **Largest [comeback](48423585.md#NBA)**

* **36 points (\:20 left in 2nd)** – The Utah Jazz overcame a 36-point deficit

  [^cite_note-1]

  &#x20;to defeat the [Denver Nuggets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver_Nuggets_accomplishments_and_records) at home on November 27, [1996](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996%E2%80%9397_NBA_season).

  [^cite_note-2]

  &#x20;Utah trailed 70–34 with 20 seconds left in the 1st half, but outscored Denver 73–33 during the rest of the game to win 107–103. This is the largest overall deficit overcome in NBA history.

  [^cite_note-3]
