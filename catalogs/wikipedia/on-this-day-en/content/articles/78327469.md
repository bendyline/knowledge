**Toul** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): تول, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Tūl*) is a municipality in the [Nabatieh District](12169367.md) in [Lebanon](17771.md).

## History

In 1875, [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) noted on his travels in the region: "on my right, to the north, on a hill from which a valley separates me, the little village of Toul. An [oualy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oualy) is dedicated to Neby Toul."[^cite_note-1]

## Demographics

In 2014, [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 50% and [Christians](4796145.md) made up 50% of registered voters in Toul. 46.43% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims) and 46.43% were [Maronite Catholics](39667585.md).[^cite_note-2]
