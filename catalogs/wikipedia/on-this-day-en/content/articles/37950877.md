**Miguel Ángel Poveda León** (born 13 February 1973) is a Spanish [flamenco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flamenco) singer.

Poveda has collaborated with artists from various disciplines who were previously unknown to flamenco audiences. He often collaborates with Spanish flamenco guitarist [Juan Gómez "Chicuelo"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_G%C3%B3mez_%22Chicuelo%22), with whom he has toured extensively in Europe, Japan and the US.[^cite_note-CoderqueColita1997-1]

## Early life

Poveda was born in [Barcelona](4443.md), [Catalonia](6822.md), to a father from [Lorca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorca,_Spain), [Murcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murcia), and a mother from [Puertollano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puertollano), [Castilla-La Mancha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castilla-La_Mancha). In 2003, he moved to [Seville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seville).
