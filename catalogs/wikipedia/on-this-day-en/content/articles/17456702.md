**Strømmen** is a town in [Lillestrøm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lillestr%C3%B8m) municipality, [Akershus](171179.md) county, [Norway](21241.md). It is about twenty kilometers east of Oslo, and considered part of [Greater Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Oslo). It has around 11,400 residents.&#x20;

The town has its origins from floating lumber and sawmills along [Sagelva](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Sagelva\&action=edit\&redlink=1). Later there has been [heavy industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_industry) at Strømmen, including the railway stock manufacturer [Strømmens Værksted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Str%C3%B8mmens_V%C3%A6rksted). It currently hosts one of Norway's largest shopping centre, [Strømmen Storsenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Str%C3%B8mmen_Storsenter). Strømmen had a top-level women's football team, [Team Strømmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_Str%C3%B8mmen) until 2009, while [Strømmen IF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Str%C3%B8mmen_IF) will play in second top-level from the 2010 season.&#x20;
