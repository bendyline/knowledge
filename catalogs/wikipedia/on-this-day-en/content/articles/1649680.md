**Jalal-Abad** ([/dʒəˈlɑːl əˈbɑːd/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English); Kyrgyz: [\[dʒɑˌɫɑɫɑˈbɑt\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Kyrgyz)) is a region (*[oblus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblast)*) of [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md). Its capital is [Manas](1641057.md). It is surrounded by (clockwise from the north) [Talas Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talas_Region), [Chüy Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C3%BCy_Region), [Naryn Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naryn_Region), [Osh Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osh_Region), and [Uzbekistan](31853.md). Jalal-Abad Region was established on 21 November 1939. On 27 January 1959 it became a part of [Osh Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osh_Region), but regained its old status as a region on 14 December 1990. Its total area is 32,418 km<sup>2</sup> (12,517 sq mi).[^cite_note-census09-2] The resident population of the region was 1,260,617 as of January 2021.[^cite_note-pop21-1] The region has a sizeable [Uzbek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbeks) (24.8% in 2009) minority.[^cite_note-census09-2]

## Geography

Jalal-Abad Region covers 32,418 km<sup>2</sup> (12,517 sq mi) (16.2% of total country's area) in central-western Kyrgyzstan.

The southern edge of the region is part of the [Ferghana Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferghana_Valley). The rest of the region is mountainous. M41, the main north-south highway from [Bishkek](4554.md) to [Osh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osh), takes a very crooked route down the center of the region. Another road follows the south border almost to the western tip and then turns northeast up the Chatkal valley to [Kyzyl-Adyr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyzyl-Adyr) in [Talas Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talas_Region). Another road (closed in winter and requiring a jeep from the Ferghana range to Kazarman) goes east to [Kazarman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazarman) and [Naryn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naryn).

An integral part of the country's power system is [Toktogul hydroelectric power station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toktogul_Reservoir), which supplies electricity and water to both Kyrgyzstan and neighboring countries.

## Ecology and environment

The area has several mountain lakes, walnut forests, and mineral waters. It has also the world's largest natural growing [walnut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut) forest, called [Arslanbob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arslanbob), about 40 kilometres (25 mi) north of Jalal-Abad city. Jalal-Abad Region is rich in ecological resources. Strictly protected areas ([IUCN category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IUCN_protected_area_categories) Ia) located in the region are [Sary-Chelek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sary-Chelek_Nature_Reserve), [Besh-Aral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Besh-Aral_Nature_Reserve), [Dashman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dashman_Nature_Reserve) and [Padysha-Ata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padysha-Ata_Nature_Reserve?action=edit\&redlink=1). There are three nature parks (IUCN category II): [Alatay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alatay_Nature_Park), [Kan-Achuu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kan-Achuu_Nature_Park) and [Saymaluu-Tash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saymaluu-Tash).[^cite_note-oopt-3] Natural monuments (IUCN category III) include: [Tegerek Waterfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tegerek_Waterfall) located in the upper reaches of the river [Kara-Üngkür](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kara-%C3%9Cngk%C3%BCr), [Sogon-Tash Cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sogon-Tash_Cave?action=edit\&redlink=1), and the [Kara-Jygach Rocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kara-Jygach_Rocks), composed of red [sandstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandstone), in [Aksy District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aksy_District). Among other protected areas are:

- Forest reserves ([Kuru-Köl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuru-K%C3%B6l_Forest_Reserve), [Miskin-Say](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miskin-Say_Forest_Reserve), [Dashman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dashman_Forest_Reserve), [Baltyr-Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltyr-Khan_Forest_Reserve), [Uzun-Akmat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzun-Akmat_Forest_Reserve))

- Botanical reserves ([Chanach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chanach_Botanical_Reserve?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Kosh-Tektir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosh-Tektir_Botanical_Reserve), [Chatkal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatkal_Botanical_Reserve?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Ryazan-Say](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryazan-Say_Botanical_Reserve?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Jel-Tiybes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jel-Tiybes_Botanical_Reserve), [Kyrgyz-Gava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz-Gava_Botanical_Reserve))

- Game reserves ([Toguz-Toro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toguz-Toro_Game_Reserve?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Chychkan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chychkan_Game_Reserve), [Chandalash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandalash_Game_Reserve))

### Economy

Wheat, fruit, vegetables, maize, nuts, tobacco, and silk-worm cocoons are grown in the region. The region also has a few textile plants and hydroelectric stations. Minerals, natural gas, coal, metals, and oil can be found here, notably around the town of [Kochkor-Ata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kochkor-Ata), which is home to small scale oil industry. Most of the extraction of minerals, natural gas, coal, metals, and oil of the Soviet era has ceased.



[Livestock](25160767.md) market in [Kyzyl-Jar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyzyl-Jar,_Aksy), Jalal-Abad Region

A pearl of the region is the [Sary-Chelek Nature Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sary-Chelek_Nature_Reserve) with [Lake Sary-Chelek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Sary-Chelek), surrounded by wild fruit orchards and snow-covered peaks.

A few [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union)-era resorts offer mineral water treatment programs for people with various chronic diseases. A number of companies have succeeded in trading bottled mineral water around the country and abroad.

Except for the small fringes of the [Fergana Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fergana_Valley), Jalal-Abad Region is a land of mountains. There are unlimited trekking possibilities in the area, but the lack of infrastructure, except at Arslanbob, poses problems to visitors; a biodiversity conservation program supported by the government and the [Global Environment Facility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Environment_Facility) (GEF) is working to protect these natural resources and promote [soft tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_tourism?action=edit\&redlink=1).
