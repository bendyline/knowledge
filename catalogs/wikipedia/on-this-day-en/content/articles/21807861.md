**Cameron McGowan Currie** (born October 3, 1948) is a [senior United States district judge](834368.md) of the [United States District Court for the District of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_District_Court_for_the_District_of_South_Carolina).[^cite_note-1]

## Education and career

Born in [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence,_South_Carolina), [South Carolina](27956.md), Currie received a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) degree from the [University of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina) in 1970 and a [Juris Doctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juris_Doctor) from [George Washington University Law School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University_Law_School) in 1975. She was a [law clerk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_clerk) to United States Magistrate Arthur L. Burnett of the District of Columbia from 1973 to 1974. She was in private practice in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md), from 1975 to 1978. She was an [Assistant United States Attorney](3598252.md) for the District of Columbia from 1978 to 1980, and for the District of South Carolina from 1980 to 1984.

Currie was a [United States Magistrate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Magistrate) for the District of South Carolina from 1984 to 1986, returning to private practice in [Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia,_South_Carolina), South Carolina from 1986 to 1989. She was also an adjunct professor of law, [University of South Carolina Law Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina_Law_Center) from 1986 to 1989. She was a chief deputy state attorney general of Office of the State Attorney General, South Carolina from 1989 to 1994.

## Federal judicial service

On January 27, 1994, Currie was nominated by President [Bill Clinton](3356.md) to a seat on the [United States District Court for the District of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_District_Court_for_the_District_of_South_Carolina) vacated by [Falcon Black Hawkins Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falcon_Black_Hawkins_Jr.) Currie was confirmed by the [United States Senate](24909346.md) on March 10, 1994, and received her commission on March 11, 1994. She took [senior status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_status) on October 3, 2013.

On November 24, 2025, Currie found that Trump-appointed interim [U.S. Attorney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Attorney) [Lindsey Halligan](74616180.md) had been unlawfully appointed as the interim U.S. Attorney for the [Eastern District of Virginia](751799.md), which led to not only Halligan being disqualified from this position, but also two criminal cases, [one](81184533.md) against former [FBI director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI_director) [James Comey](2156690.md) and [one](81323871.md) against [New York Attorney General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Attorney_General) [Letitia James](1598829.md), being dismissed, with Currie finding that Halligan's appointment was tied to prosecuting these two people who undertook legal investigations against Trump.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
