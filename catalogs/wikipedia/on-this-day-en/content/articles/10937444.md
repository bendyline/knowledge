In the [administrative divisions of Haiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Haiti), the **department** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *département d'Haïti*, pronounced [\[depaʁtəmɑ̃ d‿aiti\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French); [Haitian Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Creole_language): *depatman Ayiti*) is the first of four levels of government. Haiti is divided administratively into ten departments, which are further subdivided into 42 [arrondissements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrondissements_of_Haiti), 149 [communes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_communes_of_Haiti), and 574 [communal sections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communal_section) as of 2021.[^cite_note-1]

The Haitian government separately divides communes into *villes*, *quartiers*, and *sections rurales* to count urban and rural populations. Generally, each commune has one ville which serves as the administrative center and corresponds to the largest urban communal section. Quartiers are also counted as urban, but they are less populated and usually located in a different communal section.

In 2014, there was a proposal by the [Chamber of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies_\(Haiti\)) to increase the number of departments from 10 to 14 —perhaps as high as 16.[^cite_note-2]

## Administration

Each [departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_\(administrative_division\)) has a [departmental council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departmental_council?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*conseil départemental*) compound of three members elected by the departmental assembly for a 4-year [term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_of_office). The departmental council is led by a [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)#Sub-national) (*président*). The council is the executive organ of the department.

Each department has a [departmental assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departmental_assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) who assists the council in its work. The departmental assembly is the deliberative organ of the department. The members of the departmental assembly are also elected for 4 years. The departmental assembly is led by a president.

## History

Three departments have roots in the former French colony of [Saint-Domingue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Domingue), namely: the [Nord](492813.md), [Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sud_\(department\)), and [Ouest](492791.md). In 1801, under Governor-General [Toussaint Louverture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toussaint_Louverture), the "provinces," became known as *departments*.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The departement of l'Artibonite was known as departement of [Louverture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toussaint_Louverture).

Under the administration of [Dessalines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dessalines) the country was administrated by [military divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Empire_of_Haiti).

In 1821, [Artibonite](489220.md) was created and in 1844, [Nord-Ouest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nord-Ouest_\(department\)), both derived out of the Nord and Ouest departments.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-Hall-6] In 1962 during the reign of ["Papa Doc" Duvalier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Duvalier), four new departments were created out of a territorial redistribution. These departments were: [Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_\(department\)), [Grand'Anse](492837.md), [Nord-Ouest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nord-Ouest_\(department\)) and [Sud-Est](492832.md). In 2003, a tenth department was created out of Grand'Anse, called [Nippes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nippes).[^cite_note-Hall-6]

In the 1990s, before the creation of Nippes, the *dixième département* was a phrase commonly used in regards to the [Haitian diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_diaspora). Since then, the phrase *onzième département* was soon adopted to describe the diaspora.[^cite_note-Hall-6]
