The **Ecuadorian Armed Forces** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Fuerzas Armadas del Ecuador*) is the national military force of [Ecuador](9334.md). The commander-in-chief is the [president of Ecuador](840787.md). The military is generally under [civilian control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civilian_control_of_the_military), specifically the [Ministry of National Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Defence_\(Ecuador\)). The military of Ecuador has been involved in border disputes with [Peru](170691.md) ([Ecuadorian–Peruvian War (1857–1860)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian%E2%80%93Peruvian_War_\(1857%E2%80%931860\)), [Ecuadorian–Peruvian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian%E2%80%93Peruvian_War), [Paquisha War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paquisha_War), [Cenepa War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cenepa_War)), and has provided [military observers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Military_Observer) and troops to the [United Nations](31769.md) since 1948.[^cite_note-4]

Since 2024, the Ecuadorian Armed Forces have been involved in the war on organized crime, carrying out control activities in prisons and on highways, as well as missions to confront groups declared as terrorists in Ecuador.[^cite_note-5]

## Mission



Ecuadorian Army soldier participates at a UN exercise

The armed forces are part of the public forces and have the stated mission of the preservation of the integrity and [national sovereignty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_sovereignty) of the national territory. It also involves participation in the social and [economic development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_development) of the country and the provision of assistance in the maintenance of internal order. Tasks include fighting [organised crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organised_crime), anti-narcotic operations and illegal [immigration](4599312.md).[^cite_note-janes.com-6] One social development programme applies the provision of teachers for rural schools through an accord with the Ministry of Education. Environmental protection is also a priority, several programmes were implemented: National Forestation and Ornamentation, Lonely Tree, Green Surveillance, Fire Plan, Ecuador Forest, and Arenillas Military Reserve. The Ecuadorian territory is divided into five Joint Task Force Zones or *Fuerzas de Tarea Conjunta*, four on mainland [Ecuador](9334.md), the fifth being the Naval Zone (including the [Galápagos Islands](171118.md)).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] Overseas territories include also the [Pedro Vicente Maldonado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Vicente_Maldonado) Naval Biological Research Station in the [Antarctic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antarctic).

## Geopolitical situation

[Ecuador](9334.md) shares a 1,420 km (882 mi) border with [Peru](170691.md). Although marked by many conflicts, relations have improved since the signing of a renewed [Peace Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Protocol) in 1998.[^cite_note-cia.gov-1] However, along the 590 km (367 mi)-long border with its neighbour [Colombia](5222.md), relations have been strained mainly due to a cross-border raid by [Colombian forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_Army) on [FARC](26100.md) [guerrillas](12720.md).[^cite_note-janes.com-6][^cite_note-time.com-9] The [Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces) had logistical shortcomings and were caught off-guard. Their [radar](25676.md) did not work, aviation was virtually non-existent, and [communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_communications) were not fully operational.[^cite_note-10] A [diplomatic crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Andean_diplomatic_crisis) followed in 2008 which some attribute partially to the need for better equipment as well as a new national defence [doctrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctrine).[^cite_note-11]

The new administration at the Defence Ministry launched a deep restructuring program under the name of PATRIA I. It involves the modernisation of [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military) equipment, improvement of planning, and operations within the Ecuadorian territory. PATRIA I was planned to be completed by 2011. In 2009, the spending budget was increased by 25% and totaled \$920 million.

## History

Main article: [Military history of Ecuador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_history_of_Ecuador)



Map of the long-lasting territorial dispute between [Ecuador](9334.md) and [Peru](170691.md), settled only in 1998. (in Spanish)

[Ecuador](9334.md)'s [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military) history dates far back to its first attempt to secure freedom from [Spain](26667.md) in 1811. In 1822 Ecuadorian troops, alongside other rebel forces, scored a [decisive victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decisive_victory) over the [Spanish](26667.md) royalist [army](1376.md) at the [Battle of Pichincha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Pichincha). Although assisted by Peruvian troops, it would fight these only a few years later in 1828, as a member of the Confederation of [Gran Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Colombia). The troops of Gran Colombia (Less than half of its troops were Ecuadorians), is defeated in the [Battle of Punta Malpelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Punta_Malpelo) and the combat of crosses, where the Peruvian navy blocks Guayaquil. Then the great Colombians in the land field defeat a division of Peruvian outpost, in the battle of Tarqui. This battle does not define war after the signing of the Giron agreement where it is indicated that it remains in a status quo before bellum. Eventually, Civil War would plunge the country and the army into disorder. In 1941 the Ecuadorian Military found itself weak and disorganized; the by now long-lasting territorial dispute with [Peru](170691.md) escalated into a major conflict, the [Ecuadorian–Peruvian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian%E2%80%93Peruvian_War) of 1941. A much larger and better equipped Peruvian force quickly overwhelmed the Ecuadorian forces, driving them back and invading the Ecuadorian territory. Ecuador had no choice but to accept Peru's territorial claims and signed [Peace treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Protocol) in 1942. However, the treaty of 1942 failed to settle the border dispute and occasional clashes occurred in a then still non-demarcated border area between the nations. These clashes flared into another outbreak of serious fighting in January 1981 called the [Paquisha War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paquisha_War) where Ecuadorian troops infiltrated into Peruvian territory are expelled by the Peruvian army; similar incidents occurred in 1983 and again in 1984. The last [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military) conflict with Peru occurred in 1995, during the [Cenepa War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cenepa_War), in which both sides claimed to be fighting inside their own territory until the signing of a ceasefire and the eventual separation of forces. The longest-running source of armed international conflict in the Western Hemisphere had ended.[^cite_note-12]

## Organization

The armed forces of Ecuador are under the authority of the [President of the Republic](840787.md) through the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Defence_\(Ecuador\)), coordinated by the Joint Command of the Armed Forces.

## Command structure

- **The Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces** (*Comandante en Jefe de las Fuerzas Armadas*) is the [President of the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Correa). He exercises the political leadership of security and national defence and counts on the advice of the National Security Council.

- **The National Security Council** (*El Consejo de Seguridad Nacional*) or N.S.C. is the superior body responsible for the [national defence](240468.md), in charge of issuing the strategic concept of [national security](240468.md), which in turn constitutes the essential instrument to start the planning and decision-making process. It is chaired by the [President of the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Correa), which is also the [Commander-in-Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-Chief) of the [Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces). It includes the Presidents of the [National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Congress_of_Ecuador) and the [Supreme Court of Justice](4913027.md); the ministers in charge of [National Defence](240468.md), [Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forms_of_government) and [Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police), [Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_relations), and [Economy and Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy); the Chief of the Joint Command, and the Chiefs of the three branches of the [Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces). It monitors the fulfillment of the defence policies and the strategic plans elaborated by the Joint Command of the [Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces), submitted by the [Ministry of National Defence](240468.md). The N.S.C. constitutes the highest ranking monitoring and crisis management body.

- **The Ministry of Defense** (*Ministerio de la Defensa National*), is the administrative body of the national defence. The Coordinating Minister of Internal and External Security accords the policies and actions that will be adopted by the following institutions as regards internal and external security: the [Ministry of Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forms_of_government) and [Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police), [the Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_relations), the Ministry of Defence, and the Secretariat General for the [Public administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_administration).

- **The Joint Armed Forces Command** (*El Comando Conjunto de las Fuerzas Armadas*), is the highest planning, preparation and strategic body of [military](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military) operations. It advises on national defence and is conformed by the Chief of the Joint Command of the [Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces) and the [Commanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander) of all three branches of the Ecuadorian [Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces): The [Army](3214213.md), [Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_Navy) and the [Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_Air_Force).

## Branches

### Joint Command



The command is in charge of the administration and coordination of the three [military branches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_branch). The objectives of the command are to integrate the military branches with joint capabilities and interoperability, to address changes and new scenarios, which guarantee the peace, security, and well-being of the nation.

Through military strategic guidelines and guidelines, in the innovation, updating, development and implementation of the generation of military technology, through strategic study in the development of military capabilities, verification of the optimal installed military capacity, implementation of military technological development, in order to promote military operational growth to be the effective and efficient decisive force.

### Cyber-Defense Operations Command

It is a body responsible for the planning and execution of actions related to cyberdefense in networks and information and telecommunications systems or others that it may have entrusted to, as well as contributing to the appropriate response in cyberspace to threats or aggressions that may affect National Defense, guaranteeing and providing security to strategic entities that are managed by computer systems.

## Equipment sources

Historically, Ecuador depended on a wide variety of foreign suppliers for virtually all of its equipment needs. Only in the 1980s did it begin to develop a modest domestic arms industry as the Directorate of Army Industries manufactured rifle ammunition, uniforms, boots, and other items.

In the 1960s and 1970s, [France](5843419.md) became a leading supplier and delivered [AMX-13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMX-13) tanks and various aircraft. [Ecuador](9334.md) also purchased [Type 209 submarines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_209_submarine) and [Lürssen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%BCrssen)-Seawolf TNC 45 patrol boats from [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany). Various types of [infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infantry) weapons were acquired from [Belgium](3343.md).

Ecuador became a substantial customer for [Israeli](9282173.md) arms in the 1970s, purchasing [Arava aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Arava), [Gabriel missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_missile) for naval patrol craft, and [Uzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzi) submachine guns. Under technical assistance contracts, Israel serviced Israeli planes in the [Ecuadorian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_Air_Force) inventory as well as [Boeing](18933266.md) civilian aircraft flown by [TAME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TAME) and *Ecuatoriana Airlines*. Ecuador also reportedly employed Israeli security specialists as consultants in the fight against terrorism. In 1976 Ecuador became the first foreign country to order the [Kfir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAI_Kfir), an advanced jet fighter equipped with the [General Electric J-79](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Electric_J-79?action=edit\&redlink=1) engine produced in Israel under license. The transaction, which required United States government approval because of the engine technology, was rejected by the administration of President [Jimmy Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Carter) in order to discourage the proliferation of sophisticated military equipment in the [Third World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_World). The action caused an uproar in Israel where the sale was regarded as an important breakthrough in Israel's efforts to develop international markets for the Kfir. In 1981, after the inauguration of President [Ronald Reagan](25433.md), [Washington](195149.md) removed its objection to the sale. Although the contract called for the purchase of 12 Kfirs and an option to purchase an additional 12, Ecuador acquired only the original group, at a price estimated at US\$196 million.

Ecuador became a relatively heavy importer of arms in the late 1970s and early 1980s, averaging US\$150 million annually and reaching a peak of US\$280 million in 1982. These imports declined sharply to an average of US\$50 million annually between 1985 and 1987, presumably as a result of a dramatic reduction in oil revenues and the precipitous drop in the value of the [sucre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_sucre), which made imported arms extremely expensive. Between 1983 and 1987, Ecuador imported an estimated US\$460 million of arms, primarily from [Italy](14532.md), France, the United States, and [Britain](31717.md). In 1995, during the [Cenepa War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cenepa_War) against [Peru](170691.md), [Argentina](18951905.md) gave to Ecuador 6,500 [tons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ton) of [rifles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rifle), cannons, [anti-tank rockets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-tank_rockets), and ammunition in a controversial move.[^cite_note-34]

Recent times saw changes in Ecuador's foreign policy, as it decided to look for alternative weapon suppliers. These included its long-term allies [Chile](5489.md), which since 2008 has provided [Leopard 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leopard_1) tanks and [*Leander*-class frigates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leander-class_frigate). Israel delivered its [unmanned aerial vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_aerial_vehicles) in 2009, and [Brazil](3383.md) supplied additional military vehicles and [Super Tucano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embraer_EMB_314_Super_Tucano) combat aircraft. Countries like [Russia](25391.md) and [China](5405.md) have delivered small quantities of military equipment in the past, but have gained importance in recent years. Since then, Chinese radars, anti-aircraft systems, and infantry weapons have been purchased. In 2009 two additional [Mil Mi-17](1493374.md) helicopters have been ordered from [Russia](25391.md) with further deals planned. [India](14533.md) has delivered [HAL Dhruv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HAL_Dhruv) helicopters and [South Africa](17416221.md) is about to deliver 12 [Atlas Cheetah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlas_Cheetah) [supersonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersonic) aircraft. Ecuador's political ally, [Venezuela](32374.md), has donated military equipment including six [Mirage 50](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dassault_Mirage_5) aircraft.

In 2010, the U.S. embassy in Ecuador announced that it had delivered \$1.2 million of donated military equipment to the Ecuadorian military. The donations were intended to support operations countering drug smuggling and guerilla activity along the [Colombian](5222.md) border. Although the U.S. has refused to renew their lease on the [Manta military airbase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manta_Air_Base), deliveries included trucks, patrol boats, GPS, night vision, and [M4 carbine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M4_carbine) rifles.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]
