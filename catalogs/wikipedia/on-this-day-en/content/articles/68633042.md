**Kevin Lenini Gonçalves Pereira de Pina** (Portuguese: [\[ˈkɛ.vin le.ˈni.ni ɡõ.ˈsal.vɨʃ pɨ.ˈɾej.ɾɐ dɨ ˈpi.nɐ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Portuguese); born 27 January 1997) is a Cape Verdean professional footballer who plays as a [defensive midfielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensive_midfielder) for Russian club [Krasnodar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Krasnodar) and the [Cape Verde national team](1205575.md).&#x20;

## Club career

A youth product of the Cape Verdean team Tchadense, Pina made his professional debut with [Oliveirense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.D._Oliveirense) in a 3–2 [LigaPro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LigaPro) loss to [Benfica B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.L._Benfica_B) on 13 January 2018.[^cite_note-2] He shortly after went on loan to [Anadia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anadia_F.C.) and [Sertanense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sertanense_F.C.), before transferring to [Chaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G.D._Chaves) in 2019.[^cite_note-3]

On 8 September 2022, Pina signed a three-year contract with the [Russian Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Premier_League) club [Krasnodar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Krasnodar).[^cite_note-4] On 16 July 2024, he extended his contract to June 2027.[^cite_note-5] On 29 July 2026, he extended it again to June 2029.[^cite_note-6]

## International career

Pina was called up to represent the [Cape Verde national team](1205575.md) for [2022 FIFA World Cup qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification) matches in September 2021.[^cite_note-7] He debuted with Cape Verde in a 6–0 friendly win over [Liechtenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liechtenstein_national_football_team) on 25 March 2022.[^cite_note-8]

In December 2023, Pina was named in Cape Verde's squad for the [2023 Africa Cup of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Africa_Cup_of_Nations) in the [Ivory Coast](57744.md).[^cite_note-9] He was in the starting line-up for Cape Verde's opening match against [Ghana](1029620.md), as the *Tubarões Azuis* defeated the four-time [African champions](250680.md#Summary) 2–1.[^cite_note-10] In the team's second match, he scored the final goal of a 3–0 win over [Mozambique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozambique_national_football_team) which qualified Cape Verde for the [knockout stage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Africa_Cup_of_Nations_knockout_stage) of the competition.[^cite_note-11]

Pina became the first player in Cape Verde's history to score at a [FIFA World Cup](11370.md), netting the nation's first-ever World Cup goal against [Uruguay](679782.md) at the [2026 edition](36581929.md).[^cite_note-12] He also earned the Man of the Match award for his performance.[^cite_note-13]
