The **president of the Republic of Moldova** ([Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language): *Președintele Republicii Moldova*) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) of [Moldova](19260.md). The current president is [Maia Sandu](40796149.md), who assumed office on 24 December 2020.

## Duties and functions

The president "represents the State" and is "…the guarantor of national sovereignty and independence, as well as of the territorial unity and integrity of the State."[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] In this capacity, the president ensures the [continuity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_of_government) of the Moldovan state, [arbitrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbitration) and [mediates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediation) the [regular functioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machinery_of_government) of [public institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institution), and upholds the [rule of law](25166191.md). This role is in keeping with the president's solemn oath, taken at inauguration, "…to devote all my personal strength and abilities to the prosperity of the Republic of Moldova, to abide by the Constitution and the laws of the country, to defend democracy, fundamental human rights and freedoms, the sovereignty, independence, unity and territorial integrity of Moldova."[^cite_note-4]

Moldova is a [parliamentary republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_republic) wherein most of the president's powers are in practice ministerial, meaning they are exercised as stipulated by the Constitution, in accordance with the confidence of [Parliament](2818427.md) or, on the advice of the [Government](73816.md). The Moldovan presidency is thus a largely ceremonial institution.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Nevertheless, because the president represents Moldova internationally, they have ultimate say over the country's foreign policy. In addition, the president can influence domestic policy by using their status to initiate and participate in public discourse.[^cite_note-8] Perhaps most importantly, the president enjoys a broad suite of discretionary powers which they may use to protect the established constitutional order.

### Ministerial powers

The president, in order to maintain the continuity and stability of the democratic process, summons a new Parliament not later than 30 days following a general election and nominates a candidate for the office of [prime minister](2174189.md) after consulting the leaders of the different political parties in Parliament.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] If Parliament gives its vote of confidence to the nominee and to the nominee's list of ministers, the president appoints the resulting [prime minister-designate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister-designate) as prime minister and the other persons as ministers of the Government.[^cite_note-11] Whenever the prime minister is incapacitated, including as a result of resignation or death in office, the president is likewise required to nominate another minister of the Government as acting prime minister. The acting prime minister is thereafter responsible for leading the Government until either a new prime minister is appointed or the incumbent prime minister's incapacitation ceases.[^cite_note-12]

[Government formation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_formation) aside, the president [promulgates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promulgation) the laws passed by Parliament.[^cite_note-13] Moreover, the president performs other functions in matters of [foreign relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations) and [national defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_defence). As it concerns foreign relations, the president [accredits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_of_credence) and [recalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_correspondence#Letters_of_recall) Moldovan [ambassadors](68651.md) and [envoys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Envoy_\(title\)) stationed in foreign states, [receives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agr%C3%A9ment) the credentials of [foreign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Moldova) [diplomats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_rank) to Moldova, and authorizes the opening, closure, or change in rank of [diplomatic missions](8970.md) operating abroad.[^cite_note-14] As commander-in-chief of the [Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_the_Republic_of_Moldova), the president may order general or partial [mobilisations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobilisation), repel [armed aggression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_of_aggression), declare a [state of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_war), and take other due measures to safeguard [public security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_security) and maintain [public order](3905736.md).[^cite_note-15] All of these functions are exercised on the advice of the Government and require for their validity the [countersignature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countersignature) of the prime minister.[^cite_note-16]

### Discretionary powers

The president in their absolute discretion may:

1. Convene [extraordinary sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_session) of Parliament.

   [^cite_note-17]

2. [Introduce legislation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_of_initiative_\(legislative\)) to Parliament.

   [^cite_note-18]

   [^cite_note-19]

3. Attend plenary sessions of Parliament and otherwise address Parliament at any time on any matter of national interest.

   [^cite_note-20]

4. [Dissolve](652293.md) Parliament and call [snap elections](462915.md).

   [^cite_note-21]

5. Negotiate and conclude [treaties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty) on behalf of the Republic of Moldova and submit them, in accordance with the manner and terms established under the law, to Parliament for its [ratification](429650.md).

   [^cite_note-22]

6. Award medals and titles of honour, supreme military ranks, and diplomatic ranks.

   [^cite_note-23]

7. Settle questions of [citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship) and grant [political asylum](1621432.md).

   [^cite_note-24]

8. Appoint public officers as provided by law.

   [^cite_note-25]

9. Grant individual [pardons](288593.md).

   [^cite_note-26]

10. Request Moldovan citizens express their will by way of [referendum](51582.md) on matters of national interest.

    [^cite_note-27]

11. Confer superior degrees of qualification to officers holding positions with the prosecuting bodies, courts of law and to other categories of civil servants, under the law.

    [^cite_note-28]

12. Suspend Government ordinances and regulations which run contrary to [primary legislation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_legislation) enacted by Parliament, unless and until the Constitutional Court rules on their legality.

    [^cite_note-29]

13. Submit any law of which the president objects to Parliament for its reconsideration within two weeks of the law's passage. In the event Parliament abides by its previously passed decision, the president must promulgate the law.

    [^cite_note-30]

14. Appoints judges on the nomination of the Superior Council of Magistracy.

    [^cite_note-31]

15. Establish, organize, and appoint a Presidential Administration. The Presidential Administration is a collection of state agencies which reports to the president and serves as the president's support staff. The secretary-general of the Presidential Administration serves as the liaison between the president and the Government and administers the various presidential agencies subject to the president's direction and supervision. One such state agency, the Supreme Security Council, consults the president on foreign policy.

    [^cite_note-32]

16. Exercise other [discretionary powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reserve_powers) as may be provided by law.

    [^cite_note-33]

## Election

The president is elected in a [two-round](25932.md) [direct election](652563.md), with a runoff taking place between the top two finishers if no candidate receives a majority in the first round. This system was put in place when the [constitution of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Moldova_\(1994\)) was adopted in 1994.



[Igor Dodon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Dodon) receiving a briefing by [National Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Bank_of_Moldova) governor [Octavian Armașu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octavian_Arma%C8%99u), a typical presidential activity

In 2000, the Constitution was amended changing the process to an [indirect election](652583.md) by the [Parliament of Moldova](2818427.md), with a [supermajority](638313.md) of 61 votes required. On March 4, 2016, the [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Moldova) ruled that the amendment was unconstitutional[^cite_note-34] and Moldova consequently returned to electing the president via popular vote.

One presidential [term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_of_office) lasts for four years; presidents are [limited](505825.md) to two consecutive terms. Before the 2000 amendments, the presidential term lasted for five years.&#x20;

Under Article 78 of the Constitution, candidates must be citizens of Moldova with the right to vote and over 40 years old who had lived or has been living permanently in Moldova for no less than 10 years and speaks the official state language. By convention, a newly elected president renounces formal ties with their political party before taking office.

## Constitutional position

According to the Article 77 of the [Constitution of Moldova (1994)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Moldova_\(1994\)), the president of Moldova is the head of the state and represents the state and is the guarantor of national sovereignty, independence, and the unity and territorial integrity of the nation.[^cite_note-35]

### Acting president



[Mihai Ghimpu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mihai_Ghimpu) (*right*), the first acting president of Moldova, shaking hands with Russian president [Dmitry Medvedev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Medvedev), 9 October 2009. A key role of the presidency is meetings with foreign leaders

The [acting](691917.md) president of Moldova ([Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language): ***Președinte interimar al Republicii Moldova***) is a person who fulfils the duties of the president of Moldova when cases of incapacity and vacancy occur. It is a temporary post provided by the Constitution of Moldova.

According to Article 91 of the [Constitution of Moldova (1994)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Moldova_\(1994\)):&#x20;

> When the office of the President of the Republic of Moldova becomes vacant or the President has been suspended from office, or finds himself in the temporary impossibility of discharging his duties, the responsibility of the office shall devolve ad interim on the [President of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Moldovan_Parliament) or the [Prime Minister](2174189.md), in that priority order.

### Suspension from office

According to Article 89 of the [Constitution of Moldova (1994)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Moldova_\(1994\)):&#x20;

- (1) In the event where the president of the Republic of Moldova commits grave offenses infringing upon constitutional provisions, he or she may be suspended from office by Parliament if two-thirds of the members cast their vote in support of suspension.

- (2) The motion requesting the suspension from office must be initiated by at least one third of the members, and it must be brought to the knowledge of the President without delay. The President may give explanations on the actions for which he or she is being censured before parliament.

- (3) If the motion requesting suspension from office meets with approval, a national referendum shall be organized within 30 days for removing the President from office.

### Vacancy

According to Article 90 of the [Constitution of Moldova (1994)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Moldova_\(1994\)):&#x20;

- (1) The office of the President of the Republic of Moldova may become vacant in consequence of expiry of the presidential mandate, resignation from office, removal from office, definite impossibility of executing his duties, or death.

- (2) The request to remove the President of the Republic of Moldova from office will be brought forward in Parliament, which will pass a decision on that request.

- (3) Within 3 months from the date when the presidential office was announced as vacant elections for a new president will be held in accordance with the law.

### Standard

The presidential standard ([Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_language): *Stindardul Preşedintelui*) consists of the [Coat of Arms of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coat_of_Arms_of_Moldova) in front of a purple background and a border composed of squares of 1/9 in the national colours of the republic. The regulations of the Standard of the President is approved by the decree of the President with the opinion of the National Heraldic Commission. The original of the Standard of the President is handed over to the President at the inauguration ceremony and is kept in their office. Duplicates and copies of the standard are displayed at the Presidential Palace or at other presidential residences while they are on these premises.[^cite_note-36]

### Band and music

The official march of the presidency is the *Marș de Întîmpinare «La Mulți ani»* (Slow March «To many years»), which is used during the arrival of the president at special occasions, similarly to *[Hail to the Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hail_to_the_Chief)* for the [president of the United States](24113.md).[^cite_note-37] The [Presidential Band of the Republic of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Band_of_the_Republic_of_Moldova) is the main [military band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_band) of the [Armed Forces of the Republic of Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_the_Republic_of_Moldova) and specifically serves the president during all functions of state. The band performs at welcome ceremonies of foreign officials on [state visits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_visits), [military parades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_parades), and the accreditation of ambassadors.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

## Residence

The [Presidential Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Palace,_Chi%C8%99in%C4%83u) is located in the [Buiucani sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sectorul_Buiucani) of [Chișinău](56635.md). It was constructed between 1984 and 1987 to the design of architects A. Zalțman and V. Iavorski. The distinctive building was originally the meeting place of the [Supreme Soviet of the Moldavian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Soviet_of_the_Moldavian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic).
