# Pokémon Center

For the facilities in the Pokémon anime and games, see [Pokémon (video game series) § Gameplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_\(video_game_series\)#Gameplay).



Pokémon Center in [Sendai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sendai)



Former appearance of the Pokémon Center in [Singapore](27318.md); the store has since been renovated

The **Pokémon Center** is a chain of specialty stores selling *[Pokémon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon)*-related merchandise. Pokémon Centers are predominantly located in Japan, the first having opened in Tokyo in 1998. Stores have also opened in Singapore, the United States, and Taiwan. As of 2026, there are 25 active stores with two located outside Japan.[^cite_note-shoplist2026-1]

Within the *Pokémon* universe, "Pokémon Centers" are locations where characters can heal, manage, and trade their [Pokémon creatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon).[^cite_note-Polygon2014-2] Real Pokémon Centers, named after the fictional counterparts, sell [merchandise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merchandise) such as action figures, plush toys, cereal boxes, clothing, backpacks, and badges.[^cite_note-3]

## History

[The Pokémon Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pok%C3%A9mon_Company) opened the first Pokémon Center store in [Tokyo](30057.md) in April 1998. This original Pokémon Center eventually closed and reopened in a different location.[^cite_note-Polygon2014-2] Pokémon Center [Osaka](52230.md), one of the franchise' bigger locations at 830 square meters, was the country's seventh Pokémon Center and opened in 2010.[^cite_note-4]

On November 16, 2001, Nintendo opened a store called the Pokémon Center in [Rockefeller Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockefeller_Center), [New York City](645042.md). The Pokémon Center was closed and replaced by the [Nintendo World Store](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_World_Store) in 2005.[^cite_note-opening-5] This store was renovated and rebranded to [Nintendo New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_New_York) in 2016.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-Nintendo_World_Store_Getting_Big_Renovation-7]

Only one Pokémon Center out of 15, along with three smaller locations, remained open at the height of the [COVID-19 pandemic in Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Japan), and the location in [Okayama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okayama) closed permanently.[^cite_note-8] There are over 20 Pokémon Center locations in Japan as of 2023.[^cite_note-9]

On December 8, 2023, the second permanent Pokémon Center outside of Japan opened in [Taipei](57648.md) at the Shin Kong Mitsukoshi department store near [Taipei 101](203801.md).[^cite_note-10] The Pokémon Company International launched an online Pokémon Center store in 2014.[^cite_note-Polygon2014-2]

## Locations

As of 2026, there are 23 Pokémon locations in Japan, including five labelled "Pokémon Store". They include ten locations in the [Greater Tokyo Area](354951.md).[^cite_note-shoplist2026-1] These exclude temporary [pop-up stores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop-up_retail), for example: one such store operated in [London](17867.md) during the [Pokémon World Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_World_Championships) in 2019,[^cite_note-11] and another operated for three weeks in June 2014 at the Cremerie de Paris, near the [Louvre](17546.md) in [Paris](22989.md).[^cite_note-12]
