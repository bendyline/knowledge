**Vahdat** ([Tajik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajik_language): Ваҳдат) is a city in western Tajikistan, on the bank of the [Kofarnihon River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kofarnihon_River), 21 km east of [Dushanbe](56658.md). It was previously called **Yangi-Bozor** (1927–1936), **Orjonikidzeobod** (1936–1993, after [Grigoriy Ordzhonikidze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grigoriy_Ordzhonikidze))[^cite_note-3] and **Kofarnihon** (1993–2006). The current name of Vahdat is a completely new name that was introduced in the 2000s as part of an official renaming of the settlement.[^cite_note-\:0-4] Its population is estimated at 43,200 for the city proper and 342,700 for the city with the outlying communities (2020).[^cite_note-pop2020-1] Vahdat was the focus on international attention in 2019 when a riot occurred in the city's prison, believed to be instigated by members of [Islamic State](9087364.md), which led to the deaths of three guards and 29 inmates.[^cite_note-\:2-5]

## Geography

The city is located in the upper basin of the river [Kofarnihon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kofarnihon), and is near the [Gissar Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gissar_Range) (southern slopes) and the [Karategin Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tian_Shan) (northern slopes), to the west, the city is on the edge of the [Gissar Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gissar_Valley).[^cite_note-\:1-6]

## Demographics

The population is composed primarily of Tajiks and Uzbeks, with small numbers of Russians and [Tatars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatars).[^cite_note-\:0-4] Numbers of [Afghan refugees](3657017.md) have also made Vahdat home.[^cite_note-10]

## Economy

The city's economy is mainly shaped by the surrounding district's agricultural activities: cotton, cattle, grains and vegetable production, and viticulture,[^cite_note-\:1-6] with [cotton-ginning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_gin) being the city's dominant industrial activity.[^cite_note-\:0-4]

## Transportation

There are road and rail connections between Vahdat and Dushanbe. Vahdat's railway station was built in 1930.[^cite_note-\:0-4] Highway connections are possible to [Jirgatol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jirgatol), [Khorog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khorugh), [Kulob](439884.md), [Nurak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nurak) and [Yovon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yovon).[^cite_note-\:1-6]

## Infrastructure

Vahdat is the location of a prison which included those convicted of terrorist crimes, mainly associated with membership in [Islamic State (ISIS/ISIL)](9087364.md) and the [Islamic Renaissance Party of Tajikistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Renaissance_Party_of_Tajikistan) (IRPT). In 2019, a [prison riot](2999465.md) saw three guards and 29 inmates killed, 24 of whom were said to be Islamic militants.[^cite_note-\:2-5] One of those killed was Behruz Gulmurod, a son of [Gulmurod Halimov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulmurod_Khalimov), previously a commander of Tajik Interior Ministry special forces who joined ISIS/ISIL in 2015.[^cite_note-12]
