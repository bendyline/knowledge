**Sanya**[^cite_note-2] is a resort city and the southernmost city on [Hainan Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainan_Island), and one of the four [prefecture-level cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefecture-level_cities) of [Hainan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainan) in [South China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeast_China). It is a major tourist destination with several large scale resorts and contains the Sanya coral reef, a national protected nature reserve spanning 4000 hectares. [Phoenix Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Island), an artificial island with a seven-star resort (one of the few in the world) and a complex of five-star hotels, is in [Sanya Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanya_Bay). The [Guanyin of Nanshan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanyin_of_Nanshan), a Buddhist statue of Guanyin, is the [thirteenth tallest statue](9830701.md) in the world and is located in Sanya.

Until the end of 2024, the total population of Sanya was 1,116,100 inhabitants,[^cite_note-3] living in an area of 1,919.58 km<sup>2</sup> (741.15 sq mi). Its built-up (or metro) area was home to 818,000 inhabitants as of 2024.

The city is renowned for its tropical climate and has emerged as a popular tourist destination, also serving as the training site of the Chinese national [beach volleyball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beach_volleyball) team. Sanya is home to small concentrations of [Utsul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utsul) people (The name 'Huíhuī' is their pronunciation in spoken Chinese. As they have not yet received official recognition as a distinct ethnic group, they are administratively classified as part of the Hui nationality in China). Sanya is also the location of [Yulin Naval Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yulin_Naval_Base), a major military facility on the [South China Sea](74209.md) which is home to the [People's Liberation Army Navy](24918.md) ballistic nuclear missile fleet.

## History

Known in ancient times as Yazhou, [postal romanization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_romanization): Aichow (崖州), literally "cliff state or prefecture", Sanya's history dates to the [Qin dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qin_dynasty) (221–206 BCE). Due to its remoteness from the political centers during the Qin dynasty, Sanya was sometimes called *Tianya Haijiao* (天涯海角), meaning "the end of the sky and ocean" or "the end of the earth". As a result, the city served as a place of exile for officials who found themselves out of favor with the country's rulers.

During the [Tang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tang_dynasty), a Buddhist monk accidentally landed here, using the site as a staging post on his missionary journey to Japan.[^cite_note-4]

### 20th century



1962 map of Sanya

In 1912, Yazhou became Yaxian (Yai-hsien) (崖县; [postal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_romanization): Aihsien).

In 1958, administration of Yaxian relocated from Yacheng to Sanya and Yaxian merged with [Baoting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baoting_Li_and_Miao_Autonomous_County), [Lingshui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingshui_Li_Autonomous_County), Niulou, and Xinglong, [Wanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanning), to become a large county. In 1959 and 1961, these areas were separated to establish Baoting and Lingshui while Yaxian County remained in its current prefecture. In 1987, approved by the [State Council of China](44498.md), Yaxian was established as a city named Sanya on 26 September 1987 and the city was officially established on 30 December 1987.[^cite_note-5]

### 21st century

In 2007, the [Beijing Olympic Organizing Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_Organizing_Committee_for_the_Olympic_Games) announced that the city of Sanya would become the first leg of the [2008 Summer Olympics torch relay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Summer_Olympics_torch_relay) in China.[^cite_note-6]

## Geography



Sanya city map

Sanya lies at the southern tip of Hainan Island on [Sanya Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanya_Bay). Located at 18° 15' N latitude, Sanya is–after [Sansha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sansha) (also administered by Hainan Province)–the second-southernmost prefecture-level city nationally. Though the administrative area (Sanya City) has a rough topography, the city itself is generally flat, lying on a parcel of land between low-level mountains to the north and the [South China Sea](74209.md).

The city is one of the many coastal cities in China most at risk due to rapidly rising sea levels.[^cite_note-7]

### Climate



A beach in Sanya

The area has a [tropical wet and dry climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_wet_and_dry_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Aw*),[^cite_note-8] featuring hot weather all year around. [Monsoonal](57630.md) influences are strong, with a relatively lengthy [wet season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_season) and a pronounced [dry season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season). The average annual temperature in the Sanya is 26.5 °C (79.7 °F). The coolest month is January, with an average temperature of 22.3 °C (72.1 °F), while the hottest, unlike much of the rest of China, is June, with an average temperature of 29.5 °C (85.1 °F). The average annual high temperature is 30.2 °C (86.4 °F). Water temperatures remain above 22 °C (72 °F) year-round.[^cite_note-Clim-9]

Since meteorological records began, the average annual temperature in Sanya has warmed in fluctuations. Taking 2024 as an example, the average annual temperature in Sanya is 27.3 °C (81.1 °F), 0.8 °C higher than the average from 1991 to 2020. The average annual high temperature in 2024 is 31.3 °C (88.3 °F), and the average annual low temperature is 24.8 °C (76.6 °F).<https://rp5.ru/Weather_archive_in_Sanya_Phoenix_(airport),_METAR>

From 2016 to 2025, the average temperature in Sanya is 27.0 °C (80.6 °F).

## Tourism



[Atlantis Sanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantis_Sanya) is one of multiple resorts located in Sanya

In recent years Sanya has become a popular tourist destination. Numerous international five star [luxury hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxury_hotel) and [resort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resort) chains are now established in the area. In 2009, the luxury [Mandarin Oriental, Sanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandarin_Oriental,_Sanya) hotel opened in the Dadong Hai area, the first Mandarin Oriental property on Hainan Island. [Atlantis Sanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantis_Sanya) opened in 2018, becoming the first Atlantis branded hotel in China. There are now over 100 hotels, ranging from international brands to locally managed resorts.

Russian and English signs can be seen throughout the city. In response to claims of over-charging of tourists (whether foreign or Chinese) at restaurants in the city,[^cite_note-16] Hainan government officials and Sanya city officials have vowed to crack down on the practice.[^cite_note-17] The Sanya Industrial and Commercial Bureau has implemented a real-time information system of the city's seafood restaurants during the May Day holiday for public supervision in order to stop any future price gouging by restaurants.

Sanya also hosts the [Guanyin of Nanshan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanyin_of_Nanshan), that is located within the [Nanshan Temple (Sanya)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanshan_Temple_\(Sanya\)).

Nature reserves of Yalong Bay Tropical Paradise Forest Park (亚龙湾热带 天堂森林公园) and Sanya Coral Reef National Nature Reserve (三亚珊瑚礁国家级自然保护区) are located nearby [Yalong Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yalong_Bay), and Yalong Bay National Tourism Holiday Resort was created. In recent years, as the waters of [Sanya Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanya_Bay) and others below mentioned are becoming clearer and healthier, several species of [dolphins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolphin), including endangered [Chinese white dolphins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_white_dolphin), appear along the coasts from time to time. However, they have not been considered as targeted [tourism attractions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whale_watching).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

## Transportation

The city is served by [Sanya Phoenix International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanya_Phoenix_International_Airport). A second airport, [Sanya Hongtangwan International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanya_Hongtangwan_International_Airport) has been planned; however, construction has been halted by environmental concerns since 2020. Taxis are available throughout the city.

Buses from other parts of Hainan serve Sanya's bus terminal. The No. 8 bus connects the airport to Dadonghai Beach. From there tourists can take the 24 or 25 bus to Yalong Bay or the 28 bus to Haitang Bay. There is also a free bus from Luhuitou Square to the Duty-Free Shopping Center at Haitang Bay, the largest such center in China.

### Hainan East Ring Intercity Rail

The [Hainan East Ring Intercity Rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainan_East_Ring_Intercity_Rail) links Sanya and [Haikou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haikou) and runs along the east coast of Hainan Island. There are 15 stations in between, either in operation or still under construction. Trains are designed to travel at 250 km/h (160 mph). Travel time from Sanya to Haikou is approximately 1 hour and 22 minutes. The [Hainan Western Ring High-Speed Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainan_Western_Ring_High-Speed_Railway), running along the west coast of the province also links Sanya to Haikou.

### Tram

Main article: [Sanya Tram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanya_Tram)



A tram approaching to Jiangang Road station

Sanya's tram service began trial operation on 7 January 2019.[^cite_note-20] Trams are composed of five carriages with a maximum seating capacity of 362 people. In built up and urbanised areas, the maximum speed of the tram is 50 km/h. The current tram route begins at Sanya Railway Station and finishes at Jiangang Road Station, which is currently the southernmost railway station in China. Full operation of the first line started on 10 October 2020.

## Economics & Social services

### Hospitals

There are a total of 54 hospitals in Sanya as of March 2019, both public hospitals funded by the state and private hospitals. There are also numerous clinics for doing annual health-checks, which are usually brands of national chains in China.

[Hainan 301 Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainan_301_Hospital?action=edit\&redlink=1), a branch hospital of People's Liberation Army General Hospital, is located in the Haitang Bay area of Sanya.[^cite_note-21] The hospital possesses a substantial number of state-of-the-art equipment for medical and research purposes which include a Da Vinci surgical system, different MIR system (including 1.5T MRI 3.0T MRI), Helical CT facilities (including 128-Slices CT), medical linear accelerator and electronic microscopes which are worth more than 450 million yuan. The hospital has 500 patient beds, and a total of 74 clinical, medical and nursing departments.

Sanya Traditional Chinese Medicine Hospital (三亚市中医院) is located on Fenghuang Road.[^cite_note-22] It is a grade-A tertiary hospital offering medical care, education, research, healthcare services and International Chinese Medicine exchange and cooperation.

### Medical tourism

The Sanya area of Hainan offers a [free trade port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_trade_port) offering opportunities for overseas service providers to enter the local market, and it is actively developing its commercial [health services industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_services_industry) to promote [medical tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_tourism). A pilot project was approved by the [People's Republic of China](5405.md) State Council in 2013 called the *Boao Lecheng International Medical Tourism Pilot Zone*, also known by 2024 as **Boao Hope City**,[^cite_note-23] as the only medical tourism special zone in China. Specialist medical services as well as specially licensed [drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug) are available in the zone. The importation of medical technologies and equipment, or drugs approved for marketing outside of China but not yet approved in China, may be used in the pilot zone with special approval of state authorities. The zone is likewise available for trials and testing. Lecheng received approximately 300,000 medical tourists in 2023, and also served some 28,000 persons who reside in the zone.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

## Education

### Colleges and universities

See also: [List of universities and colleges in Hainan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_universities_and_colleges_in_Hainan)

[Sanya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanya_University) (三亚学院) Located at Xueyuan Rd., Yingbin Ave. Sanya Hainan 572022. Sanya University is an independent undergraduate university. The university has fourteen schools: Social Development, Law, Finance and Economics, Management, Tourism Management, Humanities and Communication, Arts, Foreign Languages, Engineering, Physical Education, General Education & Foundation Courses, International Tourism, International Education and Music, and other, and offers 56 undergraduate majors.[^cite_note-26]

[Qiongzhou University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qiongzhou_University) (琼州大学) is located at 1 Yucai Road. The only public university located in Sanya, Qiongzhou is actively pursuing more foreign enrollments, hoping to attract Chinese tourism and language students to the city.[^cite_note-27] Courses offered to international students are: elementary Chinese, intermediate Chinese, advanced Chinese (listening, speaking, reading and writing), English, Russian (listening, speaking, reading and writing), Chinese calligraphy and painting, [Chinese martial arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_martial_arts), Chinese history, studies of Li and Miao Cultures, tourism and recreational management, and music performance.

### Primary and secondary schools

The city government states on its website that "Being the second-largest city in Hainan, the education in Sanya is relatively better than the average level in Hainan Province" but that the kindergartens are not as good as those in major Chinese cities and that the primary and secondary schools are still below the Chinese national average.[^cite_note-Educitysite-28]

According to the city government, Primary schools No.7 and No.9 are the best in the city and that Bayi Primary School "is also of good quality".[^cite_note-Educitysite-28] The city government identifies the best lower secondary (junior high school) and upper secondary (senior high school) institutions as Sanya Shiyan Middle School and [Sanya No.1 High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanya_No.1_High_School?action=edit\&redlink=1), respectively.[^cite_note-Educitysite-28]

The [International School of Sanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_of_Sanya) (formerly Canadian International School of Sanya) is the first school to offer two programs: Canadian and Mandarin, in Hainan. The school has announced its relocation to the [BanShanBanDao -Serenity Coast](http://www.banshanbandao.com/#) (半山半岛) residential area in August 2014, where the new campus will offer pre-K. day students and boarding facilities for students.
