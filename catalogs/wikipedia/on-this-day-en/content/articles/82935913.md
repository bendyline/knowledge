The **Magyar government** or **Tisza government**[^cite_note-1] is the current [government of Hungary](17048864.md). It is a single-party government composed of the [Tisza Party](76606756.md) and led by [Péter Magyar](76453946.md), who became [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Hungary) following the [2026 general election](79337461.md).[^cite_note-2] Tisza currently enjoys a two-thirds [supermajority](638313.md) in the [National Assembly](585416.md), which is the legislative threshold for amending the [Fundamental Law of Hungary](5602839.md). It is notable for being the first non-[Fidesz](404647.md)-led government [in sixteen years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orb%C3%A1n_era).

## History

On 9 May 2026 was the inaugural meeting of the [National Assembly](585416.md), officially ending the 16-year [Orbán era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orb%C3%A1n_era). On the proposal of the winning [Tisza Party](76606756.md), President [Tamás Sulyok](76167806.md) scheduled the event for a Saturday, thus allowing a large-scale national event in the capital to be held alongside the parliamentary events.[^cite_note-ap-3][^cite_note-4]

On the inaugural meeting, the [President](380393.md) officially asked [Péter Magyar](76453946.md) to form the government. The [National Assembly](585416.md) elected him the same day as [Prime Minister](378359.md), and Magyar immediately swore the oath of office. After his speech and the ceremony, the inauguration ended with a large celebration in front of the [Hungarian Parliament Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Parliament_Building).[^cite_note-ap-3]

On 12 May, Magyar's government was formed with sixteen ministers. He announced that the leaders of the health, justice, education and finance portfolios get a veto in the acts of the government.[^cite_note-5] The next day, the government had its first seating in [Ópusztaszer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%93pusztaszer), which produced numerous decisions.[^cite_note-6]

## Members of the cabinet

Magyar's announcement of the members of his cabinet started from the week starting on 20 April 2026.

Magyar originally designated [Márton Melléthei-Barna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1rton_Mell%C3%A9thei-Barna) as [Minister of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Justice_\(Hungary\)) on 30 April[^cite_note-telex_posfai-8]; however, he withdrew from nomination on 7 May, citing [conflict of interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conflict_of_interest), being Magyar's [brother-in-law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brother-in-law).[^cite_note-9] [Rita Rubovszky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rita_Rubovszky?action=edit\&redlink=1) was originally intended by the Tisza Party to be [Minister of Children and Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Education_\(Hungary\)), but was ultimately not selected due to loud public disapproval of her nomination.[^cite_note-10] [Kriszta Bódis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kriszta_B%C3%B3dis) and [Ervin Nagy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ervin_Nagy) had also previously been floated as potential Minister of Social and Family Affairs and [Minister of Social Relations and Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Culture_\(Hungary\))—given their works as experts for the Tisza Party in these fields—, but they ultimately received other government positions.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]
