# Visit by Pope Leo XIV to Spain

| Date | 6–12 June 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| Location | [Spain](26667.md) Spain: [Madrid](41188263.md)[Barcelona](4443.md)[Gran Canaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Canaria)[Tenerife](269223.md) |
| Website | [conelpapa.es](https://conelpapa.es/) (in Spanish) |



The Basilica of the [Sagrada Família](59545.md) in Barcelona, one of the places the Pope visited during his stay in Spain.

[Pope Leo XIV](54520372.md) made an [apostolic journey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_journey) to Spain on 6–12 June 2026, to address the immigration crisis, inaugurate the Tower of Jesus Christ of the [Sagrada Família](59545.md), and strengthen ties with the [Spanish government](1306113.md) and the [Spanish people](963152.md).[^cite_note-PBS1-1] He visited [Madrid](41188263.md), [Barcelona](4443.md), [Gran Canaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Canaria), and [Tenerife](269223.md).

This journey came two months after the Pope's previous [apostolic journey to Africa](82960214.md).

## Background

Further information: [Catholic Church in Spain](4513503.md) and [Holy See–Spain relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_See%E2%80%93Spain_relations)

This visit by Pope Leo XIV was the first papal visit to Spain since [Pope Benedict XVI's in 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Youth_Day_2011). It was characterized by a combination of high-level state diplomacy and Leo's pastoral priorities. For the first time in history, a [Pope](23056.md) addressed the [Spanish Parliament](519703.md) in a joint session of both chambers, which neither Popes [John Paul II](23805.md) or [Benedict XVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Benedict_XVI) did during their numerous visits.[^cite_note-2] Leo visited the [Canary Islands](5717.md), which is Spain's hotspot for [migrants](63864017.md), to give a voice to those arriving from Africa. He visited a homeless facility in Madrid and a penitentiary in Barcelona, further emphasizing his vision of the Catholic Church as the "Church of the Poor".[^cite_note-3]

The Pope's visit to the Canary Islands came after the recent [MV Hondius hantavirus outbreak](83111976.md) crisis, which disembarked its passengers in the island of [Tenerife](269223.md) under the supervision of the [Spanish Ministry of Health](33528060.md) and the [World Health Organization](33583.md).

The trip coincided with the 100th anniversary of the death of [Antoni Gaudí](31566384.md) (10 June), the architect who designed the [Sagrada Família](59545.md), and the Pope inaugurated the new Tower of Jesus Christ, making the Sagrada Família the [tallest church in the world](800972.md). This visit followed the 2025 declaration of Gaudí as [Venerable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venerable) which brought him one step closer to [beatification](233724.md).[^cite_note-4]

On early June, [Islamic State](9087364.md) posted a threat of an attack on the Pope on its channels, accompanied by images of the [Santiago Bernabéu Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_Bernab%C3%A9u_Stadium) and the Sagrada Família.[^cite_note-5]

## Preparations

In the weeks leading up to the visit, several initiatives were launched by the national organizing committee for the apostolic journey and the [Spanish Episcopal Conference](36524835.md) in anticipation of Leo XIV's visit. These included a series of biographical videos of saints associated with the places the Pope would visit. Two saints were chosen for each of the autonomous communities visited: Catalonia, represented by [Saint Eulalia of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eulalia_of_Barcelona) and the Venerable Antoni Gaudí; the Canary Islands, represented by [Saint Peter of Saint Joseph Betancur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_of_Saint_Joseph_de_Betancur) and [Saint Joseph of Anchieta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_of_Anchieta); and the Community of Madrid, represented by [Saint Isidore the Farmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isidore_the_Laborer), [Saint Mary of the Head](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Torribia), and [Saint Soledad Torres Acosta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Soledad_Torres_y_Acosta). Videos of three other Spanish saints associated with [Eucharistic adoration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eucharistic_adoration) were also released: [Saint Teresa of Ávila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teresa_of_%C3%81vila), [Saint Paschal Baylon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paschal_Bayl%C3%B3n), and [Saint Manuel González](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Gonz%C3%A1lez_Garc%C3%ADa_\(bishop\)).[^cite_note-6]

## Itinerary

### Madrid



Pope Leo XIV with [King Felipe VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Felipe_VI) and [Queen Letizia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Letizia_of_Spain) and other Spanish authorities in his arrival in Madrid, 6 June 2026



Pope Leo XIV receiving the Golden Key of Madrid from mayor [José Luis Martínez-Almeida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Luis_Mart%C3%ADnez-Almeida), in the presence of the Royal Family.

Pope Leo departed Rome's [Fiumicino International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome_Fiumicino_Airport) at 8\:13 AM aboard an [ITA Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITA_Airways) flight, which is typically used as the papal plane.[^cite_note-7] During the flight, he conducted a brief press conference where he reiterated the need for [dialogue in Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_negotiations_in_the_Russo-Ukrainian_war) and other global conflicts.[^cite_note-8] He was also presented with a drawing made by young patients from Rome's [Bambino Gesù pediatric hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambino_Ges%C3%B9_Hospital).[^cite_note-9]

Pope Leo arrived in [Madrid](41188263.md) on 6 June and was received at the [Adolfo Suárez Madrid–Barajas Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolfo_Su%C3%A1rez_Madrid%E2%80%93Barajas_Airport) by [King Felipe VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Felipe_VI) and [Queen Letizia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Letizia_of_Spain).[^cite_note-ncr1-10] He was subsequently greeted by [prime minister](203556.md) [Pedro Sánchez](43297512.md); ministers [Félix Bolaños](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9lix_Bola%C3%B1os) and [José Manuel Albares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Manuel_Albares); the ambassador to the Holy See, [Isabel Celaá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabel_Cela%C3%A1); the [president of the Community of Madrid](608649.md), [Isabel Díaz Ayuso](59693424.md), and the [mayor of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Madrid) [José Luis Martínez-Almeida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Luis_Mart%C3%ADnez-Almeida), as well as other church officials.[^cite_note-europapress1-11] Following a brief private audience with the King and Queen of Spain, Pope Leo met with a group of children who had gathered to welcome him.[^cite_note-europapress1-11]

He was escorted by car by the [Escuadrón de Escolta Real](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escuadr%C3%B3n_de_Escolta_Real?action=edit\&redlink=1) until he reached the [Royal Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Palace_of_Madrid), where he made a solemn entrance into the Plaza de la Armería and was once again received by the King and Queen of Spain, followed by the [Leonor, Princess of Asturias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonor,_Princess_of_Asturias) and [Infanta Sofía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infanta_Sof%C3%ADa_of_Spain).[^cite_note-libertad-12] The Royal Artillery then fired a 21-gun salute, and the national anthems of the Vatican and Spain were played.[^cite_note-libertad-12] The reception was attended by representatives of the government, the regional president, the mayor of Madrid, the [president of the Supreme Court and the General Council of the Judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Supreme_Court_\(Spain\)) [Isabel Perelló](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabel_Perell%C3%B3), the [president of the Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Constitutional_Court_\(Spain\)) [Cándido Conde-Pumpido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A1ndido_Conde-Pumpido), as well as the [president of the Congress of Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Congress_of_Deputies) [Francina Armengol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francina_Armengol) and of the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Senate_of_Spain) [Pedro Rollán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Roll%C3%A1n).[^cite_note-libertad-12] In the throne room, Pope Leo and the Royal Family were greeted by various dignitaries and former prime ministers.[^cite_note-libertad-12][^cite_note-13] Later, in the Hall of Columns, the king and the pope both gave speeches.[^cite_note-libertad-12] In his speech, Leo thanked Spain for its commitment to international law, urged leaders to cultivate dialogue over polarization, and emphasized the preservation of [religious freedom](46006.md) and the [freedom of conscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_of_conscience). [^cite_note-14]

His first day concluded with a visit to the "CEDIA 24 Horas" social project held by [Caritas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caritas_Spain) and a [prayer vigil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prayer_vigil) with 500,000 young people at the Plaza de Lima. Organized under the apostolic journey's local theme of "*Alzada la mirada*" ("Look up" in English), the young people event featured songs and reflections focused on spiritual searching of younger generations. A few young people at the vigil were given the opportunity to speak directly to the Pope and ask him questions.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

On 7 June the Pope presided over [Holy Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Mass) and a [Feast of Corpus Christi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feast_of_Corpus_Christi) celebration in the [Plaza de Cibeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaza_de_Cibeles) with over 1.2 million people in attendance. His homily issued a direct challenge to the Spanish government, stating that its rich Catholic history and deep-rooted religious traditions must not become a "museum of the past". He stressed that true [adoration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adoration#Christian_adoration) requires loving both God and one's neighbors.[^cite_note-17] Following the Mass, Leo led a solemn [Corpus Christi procession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpus_Christi_procession) through the streets of Madrid.[^cite_note-18] Prior to the procession, the pathways were adorned with flower petal tapestries[^cite_note-19] constructed from over 30,000 flowers by local guilds.[^cite_note-20]

He went on to meet with the [Augustinian Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_St._Augustine) and cultural and sporting figures at [Madrid Sports Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palacio_de_Deportes_de_la_Comunidad_de_Madrid). During this event, Pope Leo discussed how the [FC Barcelona–Real Madrid rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cl%C3%A1sico) reflects a culture war within Spain, with [Real Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Madrid) being viewed as a symbol of central state power, while [FC Barcelona](68187.md) is tied to [Catalan identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_nationalism). He alleviated concerns by starting the speech in [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language), and assured that he would conduct the Mass at the Sagrada Família in the language.[^cite_note-21]

He then met with Spanish prime minister Pedro Sánchez, members of the Parliament, and the [bishops](8799424.md) of the Spanish Episcopal Conference on 8 June. After lunch, he led a prayer and devotion to [Our Lady of Almudena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Almudena) at the [Almudena Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almudena_Cathedral), and met with the [diocesan community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Madrid) at the [Santiago Bernabeu Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_Bernabeu_Stadium). The ceremony was presided over by two of the most venerated images in the Spanish capital: the aforementioned Virgin of Almudena, patron saint of Madrid, and the Christ of Medinaceli. On 9 June, he met with volunteers at the [IFEMA Exhibition Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFEMA_Exhibition_Centre) before departing for [Barcelona](4443.md) aboard an [Iberia](98613.md) flight.[^cite_note-HolySeeItinerary-22]



Top of the Tower of Jesus Christ (172,5 m / 566 ft) of the Sagrada Família.

### Barcelona



Pope Leo XIV in the Estadi Olímpic Lluís Companys.

Pope Leo arrived at [Josep Tarradellas Barcelona–El Prat Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josep_Tarradellas_Barcelona%E2%80%93El_Prat_Airport) mid-day on 9 June, and was received by officials including the [minister of finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Finance_\(Spain\)), [Arcadi España](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcadi_Espa%C3%B1a); the [president of Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Catalonia), [Salvador Illa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvador_Illa); the president of the [Parliament of Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Catalonia) [Josep Rull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josep_Rull); the archbishop of Barcelona, [cardinal Omella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Josep_Omella_i_Omella), the president of the [Province of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Barcelona), [Lluïsa Moret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llu%C3%AFsa_Moret) and the [mayor of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Barcelona) [Jaume Collboni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_Collboni).[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] The bells of all the churches in Catalonia rang out as he arrived.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

He then made his way to the cathedral where he first entered the Chapel of San Cristo de Lepanto alongside Cardinal and Archbishop Omella, and prayed before the Blessed Sacrament.[^cite_note-rtvcathedral-27] Subsequently, at the high altar, he celebrated the Liturgy of the Hours, specifically the Sixth Hour, using Catalan, Spanish and Latin; once this was over, Pope Leo went down to pray in the crypt before the tomb of [Saint Eulalia of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eulalia_of_Barcelona).[^cite_note-rtvcathedral-27] He then greeted the crowd in the Cathedral Square, made his way to the cathedral cloister where he was introduced to the Catalan tradition of the [dancing egg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dancing_egg) (in Catalan *ou com balla*) used during Corpus Christi, and greeted the authorities of the [Parliament of Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Catalonia).[^cite_note-rtvcathedral-27][^cite_note-28] Before having lunch at the adjacent Episcopal Palace, he addressed the crowd once more from the balcony.[^cite_note-rtvcathedral-27]

In the afternoon, he held an audience with the Catalan president, Salvador Illa, and his wife, who presented to Pope Leo with a copy of the certificate marking the laying of the foundation stone of the Sagrada Família, a reproduction of an ivory doll from the early Christian necropolis in [Tarragona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarragona), and a facsimile of *[Homilies d'Organyà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homilies_d'Organy%C3%A0)*, a 12th-century liturgical document containing one of the earliest writings in Catalan.[^cite_note-29] That afternoon, Pope Leo also met with members of the Order of Saint Augustine.[^cite_note-30] In the evening, Pope Leo led a vigil at the [Lluís Companys Olympic Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadi_Ol%C3%ADmpic_Llu%C3%ADs_Companys) in front of more than 40,000 people.[^cite_note-31] Earlier, he blessed ambulances that would bring aid to Ukraine, organised by Sister [Lucia Caram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucia_Caram).[^cite_note-32] During the vigil, there was a performance of *[castells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castell)* (the Catalan human towers) by the [Castellers de Vilafranca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castellers_de_Vilafranca).[^cite_note-33]

The following day on 10 June, he visited the [Brians 1 Penitentiary Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brians_1_Penitentiary_Center?action=edit\&redlink=1) to meet with prisoners, and travelled to the [Santa Maria de Montserrat Abbey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria_de_Montserrat_Abbey). In the afternoon, he met with the [diocesans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Barcelona) and local welfare organizations in the [Church of Sant Agustí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convent_of_Sant_Agust%C3%AD_Vell) located in the [Raval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Raval).[^cite_note-34]

#### Montserrat



Pope Leo XIV appearing on the balcony of the main façade of the Abbey of Santa Maria de Montserrat to address the pilgrims gathered in Plaça de Santa Maria, 10 June 2026.

At midday on 10 June, Leo XIV travelled to the [Santa Maria de Montserrat Abbey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Maria_de_Montserrat_Abbey) in [Catalonia](6822.md). It was the first papal visit to the sanctuary in 44 years, since [Pope John Paul II](23805.md) presided over a Celebration of the Word there on 7 November 1982,[^cite_note-35] and only the second papal pilgrimage to the sanctuary on record since the monastery was founded. The abbey described the visit as the culmination of its millennium celebrations.[^cite_note-abadia-36][^cite_note-veuanoia-37]

Attendance required advance booking, with all reserved places located in the exterior squares of the monastery, from which the ceremony was followed on large screens.[^cite_note-38] The Pope arrived by car at the Mirador dels Apòstols and continued to the abbey in an open popemobile, stopping several times to greet those gathered along the route. He was received in the atrium by schoolchildren from Catalan Christian schools and by the abbot of Montserrat, Manel Gasch, to the sound of the abbey bells. Close to 8,000 people attended, many of them arriving from early morning by cable car and rack railway, as road access was restricted to accredited vehicles.[^cite_note-abadia-36][^cite_note-veuanoia-37]

Leo XIV presided over the [Holy Rosary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosary) with the Glorious Mysteries, traditionally prayed on Wednesdays, followed by the sanctuary's midday prayer with the *[Salve Regina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salve_Regina)* and the *[Virolai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virolai?action=edit\&redlink=1)* sung by the [Escolania de Montserrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escolania_de_Montserrat). He venerated the image of the [Virgin of Montserrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_of_Montserrat) as the choir sang the closing verses of the Virolai.[^cite_note-abadia-36]

Speaking partly in [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language), the Pope recalled his years as parish priest of a parish dedicated to Santa María de Montserrat in [Trujillo, Peru](476649.md), and said that *la Moreneta* had always accompanied him. He thanked Catalonia for welcoming people from other countries, saying it showed how to bring everyone into a single family.[^cite_note-39] Before the end of the public part of the visit he addressed the crowd from the balcony of the monastery façade and gave a blessing. The visit concluded with a private lunch with the Benedictine community and the choirboys.[^cite_note-abadia-36]

#### Sagrada Família

In the evening, the Pope went to the [Basilica of the Sagrada Família](59545.md) to inaugurate and bless the recently-completed Tower of Jesus Christ, considered the main act of the visit. During his route in [popemobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popemobile) through the [Eixample district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eixample), Leo XIV was greeted by crowds of about 120,000 people on the streets.[^cite_note-\:0-40][^cite_note-41]

On arrival, Leo XIV was greeted by [King Felipe VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felipe_VI) and [Queen Letizia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Letizia_of_Spain), [president of the Government](203556.md) [Pedro Sánchez](43297512.md), a [Sagrada Família Construction Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Construction_Board_of_the_Sagrada_Fam%C3%ADlia?action=edit\&redlink=1) representative and other authorities. A young blind girl with [Leber's syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leber's_hereditary_optic_neuropathy) explained to the Pope, King and Queen the architecture of the Tower of Jesus Christ, using a tactile model, and gifted the Pope with a drawing representing how she perceives the tower. Leo XIV then headed to the Crypt of the Sagrada Família and prayed on [Antoni Gaudí](31566384.md)'s tomb, coinciding with the centenary of his death on 10 June 1926.[^cite_note-\:0-40]

Leo XIV then entered the basilica followed by 200 [bishops](8799424.md) and offered a [Solemn Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solemn_Mass) in conmemoration of Gaudí's death centenary. The Mass was attended by 4,000 people inside and 4,000 people outside, plus all the people that were outside the Sagrada Família complex. Relevant spanish authorities included King Felipe VI, Queen Letizia, president of the Government Pedro Sánchez (attending a mass for the first time as president), [president of Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Government_of_Catalonia) [Salvador Illa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvador_Illa), [mayor of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Barcelona) [Jaume Collboni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_Collboni), [Spanish government ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_government_of_Pedro_S%C3%A1nchez#Council_of_Ministers), [Catalan government ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Salvador_Illa#Executive_Council), and deputies of the [Congress of Deputies](352254.md) and the [Parliament of Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Catalonia). Foreign authorities included prime minister of [Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra) [Xavier Espot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavier_Espot_Zamora), president of [Lithuania](17675.md) [Gitanas Nausėda](58519561.md) and president of [Cyprus](5593.md) [Nikos Christodoulides](58652814.md) (as Cyprus holding the [Presidency of the Council of the European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_the_Council_of_the_European_Union)).[^cite_note-\:0-40][^cite_note-42][^cite_note-razon-43]

After the Mass, Leo XIV exited the basilica to inaugurate and bless the Tower of Jesus Christ, main and tallest tower of the Sagrada Família with a height of 172.5 metres (566 ft), making it the [tallest church in the world](800972.md). Following the blessing, a musical and lightning spectacle culminated the pope's visit to Barcelona: the [Escolania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escolania_de_Montserrat) singing, handheld lamps, interior light illuminating towers and reflecting the stained glass, an orchestra, drones and fireworks. After the cross at the top of the Tower of Jesus Christ was illuminated, the drones draw Gaudí's face on the sky, alongside with his quote in [catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language) "*Primer l'amor, després la tècnica*" ("First love, then technique").

### Canary Islands



Pope Leo XIV with the [Virgin of Candelaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_of_Candelaria), patron saint of the Canary Islands. Behind them, the [Christ of La Laguna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristo_de_La_Laguna). Closing Mass of his apostolic visit to Spain in the [Port of Santa Cruz de Tenerife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Santa_Cruz_de_Tenerife).

Leo departed Barcelona aboard an Iberia flight in the morning on 11 June, and arrived at [Gando Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gando_Air_Base) on [Gran Canaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Canaria) several hours later. Once in Gran Canaria, he met with organizations working with migrants at the port of [Arguineguín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arguinegu%C3%ADn).[^cite_note-44] Afterwards, he met with [diocesan priests, deacons, and pastoral workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Canarias) in the [Cathedral of Santa Anna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Palmas_Cathedral) in [Las Palmas](163701.md). In the late afternoon, the Pope held Holy Mass at the [Gran Canaria Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Canaria_Stadium), before the images of the [Virgin of the Pine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgen_del_Pino), patron saint of the island and its diocese, and the Christ of Telde.[^cite_note-45]

On the last day of his journey, 12 June, Pope Leo departed Gran Canaria aboard an Iberia flight and landed at [Tenerife North–Ciudad de La Laguna Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenerife_North%E2%80%93Ciudad_de_La_Laguna_Airport) in the morning, followed by a meeting with migrants at the [Las Raíces center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Ra%C3%ADces_center). Leo also met with local organizations helping the migrants at the [Plaza del Cristo de La Laguna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaza_del_Cristo_de_La_Laguna), and held Holy Mass at the [Port of Santa Cruz de Tenerife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Santa_Cruz_de_Tenerife), before the image of the [Virgin of Candelaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_of_Candelaria), patron saint of the Canary Islands, and the [Christ of La Laguna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristo_de_La_Laguna), another image of great devotion in the islands.[^cite_note-46] Also present were relics of the only two saints born in the archipelago to date: [Saint Peter of Betancur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_of_Saint_Joseph_de_Betancur) and [Saint Joseph of Anchieta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_of_Anchieta). In the afternoon, he attended a farewell ceremony at Tenerife Norte Airport before leaving to Rome.[^cite_note-HolySeeItinerary-22] Due to a technical problem with the [Iberia](98613.md) [Airbus A320](164933.md) that was supposed to take him back to Rome, King Felipe VI offered the Pope the [Spanish Air Force](3853398.md) [Falcon 900](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dassault_Falcon_900) that planned to take him back to Rome. Both the papal entourage and the King had to wait for replacement planes to arrive from Madrid for their return journeys.[^cite_note-47]

This was the first visit by a pope to the Canary Islands, although in 1934 cardinal Pacelli visited the island of Gran Canaria representing the pope five years before being elected [Pope Pius XII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Pius_XII).[^cite_note-48] Similarly, Robert Prevost (now Leo XIV) visited the island of Tenerife twice before his election as pope, as prior general of the Order of Saint Augustine between 2001 and 2013. He stayed in the city of [Puerto de la Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_de_la_Cruz), where the only Augustinian community in the Canary Islands is located.[^cite_note-49]

## Broadcast

[Radiotelevisión Española](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiotelevisi%C3%B3n_Espa%C3%B1ola) (RTVE), [Radio Televisión Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Televisi%C3%B3n_Madrid) (RTVM), [Corporació Catalana de Mitjans Audiovisuals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporaci%C3%B3_Catalana_de_Mitjans_Audiovisuals) (CCMA), and [Radio Televisión Canaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Televisi%C3%B3n_Canaria) (RTVC), assumed in a coordinated manner the production of the official live television and radio feeds of all the public events of the visit, with each one carrying out the production of certain events, and with RTVE in charge of coordination as well as distribution to other broadcasters.[^cite_note-50]

RTVE deployed one of its largest operations for the visit, with [Televisión Española](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Televisi%C3%B3n_Espa%C3%B1ola) (TVE) in charge of the television production and [Radio Nacional de España](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Nacional_de_Espa%C3%B1a) (RNE) of the radio production. RTVE covered live all the public events of the visit, and produced the assigned events, with a workforce of 660 people, 17 mobile units, and 170 cameras.[^cite_note-51]
