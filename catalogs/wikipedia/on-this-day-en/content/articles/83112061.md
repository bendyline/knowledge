# Great Israeli Real Estate Event

| Great Israeli Real Estate Event |  |
| --- | --- |
| Organised by | My Home in Israel |
| People | Gideon Katz |

The **Great Israeli Real Estate Event** is an annual exhibition of real estate properties being sold in the [state of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Israel), and in the [West Bank](33209.md), [Palestine](241405.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-\:7-2] The event is organized by the Israeli real estate company My Home in Israel.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-4][^cite_note-5] The exhibition occurs in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom.[^cite_note-\:1-4][^cite_note-6] The exhibition lists properties inside the internationally recognized borders of Israel. It also lists properties for sale in [illegal Israeli settlements](15123.md) in the [West Bank](33209.md) (including [East Jerusalem](343508.md)).[^cite_note-\:1-4] The event is accused of selling stolen Palestinian land, and helping individuals to profit from war crimes and apartheid.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

## Background

The event is organized by Israeli real estate firm My Home in Israel, produced by Gideon Katz, who works for an Israeli public relations company.[^cite_note-\:2-9][^cite_note-\:3-10] My Home in Israel has a team of US-based real estate agents.[^cite_note-\:3-10]

Previous real estate events held by My Home in Israel at the Adas Torah synagogue in [Los Angeles](18110.md) led to protests by pro-Palestinian organizations, which alleged that the event promoted the sale of land located in the illegally occupied [Palestinian territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories).[^cite_note-\:5-11][^cite_note-12] The protests were subsequently condemned by then-President [Joe Biden](145422.md), Governor of California [Gavin Newsom](418947.md), and Mayor of Los Angeles [Karen Bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Bass).[^cite_note-\:5-11]

## History

The exhibition is held at synagogues, including the [Park East Synagogue](7193055.md) in Manhattan.[^cite_note-\:4-13][^cite_note-14]

In 2024, the exhibition was held in several Canadian cities, and later in New York and New Jersey.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] The expo in Brooklyn was cancelled due to protests.[^cite_note-\:2-9]

In 2026, the exhibition was scheduled to stop in Toronto, Manhattan, Five Towns, Teaneck, Monsey, Flatbush, and Queens.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

### 2026 Park East Synagogue event

On May 5, 2026, the Park East Synagogue in Manhattan hosted the Great Israeli Real Estate Event, an expo for land sales in Israel.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:4-13] The event's website included references to listings in [Gush Etzion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gush_Etzion), a group of 20 settlements in the [West Bank](33209.md) located southeast of Jerusalem that are considered illegal settlements under international law.[^cite_note-\:4-13][^cite_note-19]

Mayor of New York [Zohran Mamdani](64618347.md) condemned the event, with a spokesperson saying, "Mayor Mamdani is deeply opposed to the real estate expo this evening that includes the promotion of the sale of land in settlements in the [Occupied West Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupied_West_Bank)," adding, "These settlements are illegal under international law and deeply tied to the ongoing displacement of Palestinians."[^cite_note-\:4-13]

Protests against the event were held by groups including the Palestinian Assembly for Liberation Al-Awda in New York City and New Jersey (PAL-Awda NY/NJ).[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

### 2026 London

When the event was planned to be held in London, United Kingdom, in June 2026,[^cite_note-\:6-23][^cite_note-24] [Liberal Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Democrats_\(UK\))’ defence spokesman, [Calum Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calum_Miller), called for the event to be banned in the [House of Commons](13828.md), saying, "So, will the Government intervene to ban the event, unless assurances can be made that no properties in [illegal settlements](25408080.md) will be advertised?"[^cite_note-\:6-23][^cite_note-25]

Labour MP [Debbie Abrahams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debbie_Abrahams) called the sale of Palestinian land at the event "“flouting international law".[^cite_note-26]

The event was held at the [Edgware United Synagogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgware_United_Synagogue), after which the U.K. government referred the incident to the [Advertising Standards Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advertising_Standards_Authority_\(United_Kingdom\)), requesting that it ensure UK law and regulations are upheld. In a statement the U.K. government said "We completely oppose the advertisement of properties for sale in settlements, which are illegal under international law."[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-\:7-2]

Amnesty International UK condemned the event and said that it "profits from war crimes and apartheid".[^cite_note-28]
