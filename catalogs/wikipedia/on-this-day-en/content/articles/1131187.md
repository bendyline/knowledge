**North Waziristan District** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): شمالي وزیرستان ولسوالۍ, [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضلع شمالی وزیرستان) is a [district](639402.md) of [Bannu Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bannu_Division) in the [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) province of [Pakistan](23235.md). It is the northern part of [Waziristan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waziristan), a mountainous region of northwest [Pakistan](23235.md), bordering [Afghanistan](737.md) and covering 4,707 square kilometres (1,817 sq mi).[^cite_note-4] The capital city of North Waziristan is [Miranshah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranshah).[^cite_note-5]

## Overview and history

North Waziristan comprises the area west and south-west of [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) between the [Kurram River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurram_River) (Tochi) to the north and the [Gomal River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gomal_River) to the south. [Miranshah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranshah) is district headquarter of North Waziristan. The city of [Bannu](3769844.md) lies immediately to the east, while the largest town on the Afghan side of the border is [Khost](400068.md).

North Waziristan is divided into the three subdivisions of [Mirali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirali), [Miran Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miran_Shah_Subdivision), and [Razmak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Razmak). The three subdivisions are further divided into nine tehsils: [Datta Khel Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datta_Khel_Tehsil), [Dossali Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dossali_Tehsil), [Gharyum Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gharyum_Tehsil), [Ghulam Khan Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghulam_Khan_Tehsil), [Mir Ali Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mir_Ali_Tehsil), [Miran Shah Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miran_Shah_Tehsil), [Razmak Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Razmak_Tehsil), [Shewa Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shewa_Tehsil), [Spinwam Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinwam_Tehsil).[^cite_note-6]

### British Raj (1894–1947)

The British entered Waziristan in 1894. After the British military operations in 1894–95, Waziristan was divided into two "agencies", [North Waziristan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Waziristan_Agency) and [South Waziristan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Waziristan_Agency).[^cite_note-7] The two parts have quite distinct characteristics, though both tribes are subgroups of the [Wazir tribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wazir_\(Pashtun_tribe\)), after which the region is named.[^cite_note-8]

### War on Terror

In 2014, about 98,640 people were reported to be [internally displaced](427408.md) from North Waziristan as a result of [Operation Zarb-e-Azb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Zarb-e-Azb), a military offensive against militant extremists launched by the [Pakistan Army](1965925.md) along the Pak-Afghan border.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-13July-10]

### Creation of district

In 2018 after approval by Pakistan's parliament, the [Federally Administered Tribal Areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federally_Administered_Tribal_Areas) of Pakistan were merged into [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) Province, the former [North Waziristan Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Waziristan_Agency) (which had been a part of FATA) then became a district.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

As of the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), North Waziristan district has 99,595 households and a population of 693,332. The district has a sex ratio of 104.71 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 32.82%: 46.94% for males and 18.03% for females. 254,259 (36.68% of the surveyed population) are under 10 years of age. 4,131 (0.60%) live in urban areas.[^cite_note-2023_census-2] 2,170 (0.31%) people in the district were from religious minorities, mainly [Christians](18337522.md).[^cite_note-21] [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) was the predominant language, spoken by 99.66% of the population.[^cite_note-22]

## Places of interest

[Miran Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miran_Shah) is the headquarters of North Waziristan District, It is connected with [Bannu](3769844.md) and other important places in the district by metalled roads. This town houses the offices of all government departments in the agency and also serves as a market centre for people of the area.

Razmak and Shawaal Valleys are both summer resorts for the local Waziristanis and beautiful tourist spots for tourists, thousands of tourists visit here annually.

Razmak Cadet College is one of the most famous and historical educational institutions of Pakistan, and students from across the country come to study here.[^cite_note-24]
