# List of airlines of Iran



Iran Air [A321](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A321)

[Iran](14653.md) has several private and public airline companies in operation. The oldest is the Iranian Airways Company, founded in 1944 (known as [Iran Air](168635.md) since 1961).

As of June 2009, Iranian planes fly 45 international flight routes. Most of the fleet of Iranian airlines today consist of old [Boeings](18933266.md) and used or leased [Airbus](26220236.md) and regional jets such as [British Aerospace 146](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Aerospace_146) and [ATR 72](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATR_72) and [Fokker 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fokker_100).[^cite_note-Iran_Airlines_Fleet_List-1] [Economic sanctions from the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanctions_against_Iran) prevents Iran from purchasing most new western made aircraft to update its rapidly aging fleet. This has resulted in a series of ongoing disasters and incidents.[^cite_note-2] Because of this, in the last 25 years there have been 17 plane crashes, and 1500 deaths (From 2000 to 2006, 11 Iranian plane crashes claimed about 700 lives).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

Iran has initiated manufacturing aircraft on its own such as the [IR.AN-140](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonov_An-140).[^cite_note-5] A number of 108 airliners have been added to the fleet of Iranian passenger planes during the last five years.[^cite_note-6] As of July 2015, Iran had 251 commercial planes with 41,218 seats and 6 cargo planes, many (around 100) of which are not functional because of a lack of spare parts. In 2021, Iranian media reported than 50% of Iran's fleet is grounded because of lack of spare parts and other technical problems.[^cite_note-7]

In 2016, Iran announced its intention to buy over 200 heavy airplanes from [Boeing](18933266.md) and [Airbus](26220236.md) at a cost of more than \$50 billion.[^cite_note-8] This, in addition to more planned orders of 100 medium-range planes to Japan's [Mitsubishi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsubishi), Brazil's [Embraer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embraer) and Canada's [Bombardier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardier_Aerospace). Iran says these deals include authorization to maintain these planes inside of Iran, including the production of parts and training.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

Iran plans to buy 400 passenger planes by 2025 worth some \$20 billion, if [US sanctions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_sanctions_against_Iran) are lifted.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

During the summer of 2023 Iran had a scarcity of available plane tickets.[^cite_note-13]
