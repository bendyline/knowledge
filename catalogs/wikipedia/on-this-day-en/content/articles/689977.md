**Juan Jesús Vivas Lara** (born 27 February 1953) is a Spanish politician who is the current [Mayor-President](50353390.md) of the autonomous city of [Ceuta](6443.md), since 8 February 2001.[^cite_note-1] He is a member of the [People's Party](404727.md) (PP).[^cite_note-2]

## Biography

Born in [Ceuta](6443.md) in 1953,[^cite_note-efe-3] Juan Jesús Vivas earned a degree in Economic Sciences from the [University of Málaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_M%C3%A1laga).[^cite_note-4] He became a member of the [Assembly of Ceuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_of_Ceuta) in 1999 and he was invested as Mayor-President of the autonomous city in 2001, after a successful motion of no-confidence launched against [GIL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grupo_Independiente_Liberal)'s Mayor-President [Antonio Sampietro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Sampietro).[^cite_note-efe-3][^cite_note-5]

Vivas tested positive for [COVID-19](63030231.md) on 10 January 2022.[^cite_note-6]
