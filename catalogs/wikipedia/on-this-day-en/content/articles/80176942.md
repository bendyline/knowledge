**Al Medina Stadium** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ملعب المدينة) is a football stadium in [Rabat](57400.md), Morocco. It is the home stadium of [UTS Rabat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTS_Rabat). It served as one of the venues of the [2025 African Cup of Nations](57061770.md).[^cite_note-1]

The stadium is built to [FIFA](11049.md) specifications with a total capacity of 18,000. There is a roof to cover the spectators from rain and sun. There are sections for VVIP access for players, then the west facade accommodates for VIPs, journalists, stadium administration and also for medical services.[^cite_note-2]
