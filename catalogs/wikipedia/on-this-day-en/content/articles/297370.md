**Lanao del Sur** ([Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language): *Timog Lanao*; [Meranaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maranao_language) and [Iranun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranun_language): *Pagabagatan Ranao*; [Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_script) *(Batang Arab)*: لاناو الجنوبى), officially the **Province of Lanao del Sur**, is a [province](165100.md) in the [Philippines](23440.md) located in the [Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao](37260549.md) (BARMM). The capital is the city of [Marawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marawi) (the most populous in the province), and it borders [Lanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanao_del_Norte) to the north, [Bukidnon](261222.md) to the east, and [Maguindanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_del_Norte) and [Cotabato](297394.md) to the south. To the southwest lies [Illana Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illana_Bay), an arm of the [Moro Gulf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moro_Gulf).

Situated in the interior of Lanao del Sur is [Lake Lanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Lanao), the largest lake in [Mindanao](20546.md).

## Etymology

"Lanao" means "lake", derived from *ranao*. The province, situated at [basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drainage_basin) of [Lake Lanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Lanao), is known as the land of the "Maranaos" (which means "the "people of the lake").[^cite_note-FastFactsPhilProv-LanaodelSur-4]

## History

Further information: [Lanao (province)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanao_\(province\))

### Early history

Prior to the arrival of Islam, the region already had a sophisticated culture, as embodied in various Maranao epics, chants, and recorded history. The people of Lanao used to adhere to [Hinduism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism), polytheist animism, and [Buddhism](3267529.md). During this era, various cultural icons developed, such as the [torogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torogan), the [singkil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singkil) dance, the darangen epic, the unique Maranao gong and metal craft culture, the [sarimanok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarimanok), the [okir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okir) motif, and an indigenous [suyat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suyat) script.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

### Spanish colonization

Main article: [Confederate States of Lanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_States_of_Lanao)

In April 1639, Spanish conquistador [Sebastián Hurtado de Corcuera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebasti%C3%A1n_Hurtado_de_Corcuera) conducted an expedition near Lanao along with troops consisting of Christianized Filipinos. In 1640, the Spanish created a garrison near Lanao but was attacked by indigenous Maranaos.[^cite_note-Racman2020-7] In 1689, the Spaniards discovered a settlement named *Dansalan* at Lake Lanao's northern end.[^cite_note-8] Lanao was the seat of the Sultanate of Lanao.[^cite_note-FastFactsPhilProv-LanaodelSur-4]

During the Pre-Spanish time, there were 4 important boundaries which held the power of appointing a new sultan namely: *Bakayawan*, *Dalama*, *Madamba* and *Sawer*. In appointing a new [sultan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan) the permission of these four boundaries are needed to validate the new sultan appointed to his position.

### Japanese occupation

In 1942, it was occupied by the [Japanese Imperial Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Imperial_Forces) who landed in Southern [Lanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanao_\(province\)). On May 2, 1942, the former [Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Justice_of_the_Supreme_Court_of_the_Philippines) [José Abad Santos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Abad_Santos) was killed by the Japanese Imperial forces in a [firing squad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Execution_by_firing_squad) at [Malabang, Lanao del Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malabang,_Lanao_del_Sur). In 1945, the first of the liberation forces landed in Southern Lanao and liberated the area with United States, Philippine Commonwealth troops together with the Maranao guerrilla units. They used infantry weapons, the Maranao [Kris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kris), [Barong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barong_\(knife\)) and [Kampilan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampilan) swords against the Japanese forces in the *Battle of Lanao*.

### Philippine independence

In 1956, *[Republic Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_Act) No. 1552* changed the name *Dansalan* to *Marawi*, taken from the word *rawi*, referring to the reclining lilies abundant in the [Agus River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agus_River).[^cite_note-ChanRobles-RA1552-9]



[Lanao province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanao_\(province\)) map in 1918

#### Division

In 1959, [Lanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanao_\(province\)) was divided into two provinces, [Lanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanao_del_Norte) and Lanao del Sur, under *Republic Act No. 2228*. [Marawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marawi) was designated as the capital of Lanao del Sur.[^cite_note-ChanRobles-RA2228-10] The city was renamed the "Islamic City of Marawi" in 1980, and is currently the Philippines' only city having a predominantly Muslim population.[^cite_note-FastFactsPhilProv-LanaodelSur-4]

#### Proposed Maranaw province

In 1971, *Republic Act No. 6406*, which sought to create a new province out of eastern Lanao del Sur (now corresponding to the province's [first congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_districts_of_Lanao_del_Sur)), was approved on October 4, 1971. The province was to consist of the municipalities of [Bubong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubong,_Lanao_del_Sur), [Ditsaan-Ramain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ditsaan-Ramain,_Lanao_del_Sur) (including what is now [Buadiposo-Buntong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buadiposo-Buntong,_Lanao_del_Sur)), [Kapai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapai,_Lanao_del_Sur), [Lumba-Bayabao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumba-Bayabao,_Lanao_del_Sur) (including what is now [Maguing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguing,_Lanao_del_Sur)), [Marantao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marantao,_Lanao_del_Sur), [Masiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masiu,_Lanao_del_Sur), [Mulondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulondo,_Lanao_del_Sur), [Saguiaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saguiaran,_Lanao_del_Sur), [Piagapo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piagapo,_Lanao_del_Sur), [Poona Bayabao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poona_Bayabao,_Lanao_del_Sur), [Tamparan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamparan,_Lanao_del_Sur), [Taraka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taraka,_Lanao_del_Sur) and [Wao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wao,_Lanao_del_Sur) (including what is now [Bumbaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bumbaran,_Lanao_del_Sur)), with the chartered city of Marawi serving as the new provincial capital.[^cite_note-ChanRobles-RA6406-11] The division never took place due to the declaration of [Martial law in the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martial_law_in_the_Philippines) on September 21, 1972, which disrupted the elections for 1973.

#### Inclusion to the ARMM

In a 1989 [plebiscite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plebiscite), Lanao del Sur voted to join the [Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_Region_in_Muslim_Mindanao) (ARMM), but the city of Marawi voted to remain outside the ARMM.[^cite_note-FastFactsPhilProv-LanaodelSur-4] It later joined ARMM in 2001 following the plebiscite that sought to expand the autonomous region.

### Contemporary

The [Battle of Marawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Marawi) began and ended in 2017. The battle was against ISIL-affiliated militants, a number of which were Malaysian citizens. The battle destroyed most of the cityscape of Marawi and killed hundreds of civilians and Filipino soldiers.

A petition was released in support for the usage of the [torogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torogan) as inspiration for the rehabilation of Marawi after the [Battle of Marawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Marawi). The petition was released in opposition to the possible Manila Rehabilation Effect that the government plans to impose. The [Manila](184334.md)-style rehabilation would establish shanty houses and buildings without Maranao architectural styles, thus destroying Marawi's [Maranao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maranao) cultural skyline. The petition advocates a [Warsaw](32908.md)-style Rehabilation Effect, where the culture and styles of a particular area is used as foundation for rehabilitation of built heritage and landscape. Among the local architectural styles that may be used under the Warsaw-style are the [okir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okir), [sarimanok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarimanok), and inspirations from the [torogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torogan). The Warsaw-style is also foreseen to boost tourism in the city in the long-run due to the cultural aesthetics it would bring.

## Geography



Satellite view of [Lanao Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanao_Lake), situated entirely within the province

Lanao del Sur is bounded on the north by [Lanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanao_del_Norte), on the east by [Bukidnon](261222.md), on the west by [Illana Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illana_Bay), and on the south by [Maguindanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_del_Norte) and [Cotabato](297394.md). The landscape is dominated by rolling hills and valleys, placid lakes and rivers.

Located within the province is [Lanao Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanao_Lake), the second largest in the country, its waters drained by the [Agus River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agus_River) which eventually empties into [Iligan Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iligan_Bay).[^cite_note-FastFactsPhilProv-LanaodelSur-4]

The climate in the province is characterized by even distribution of rainfall throughout the year, without a distinct summer season. The province is located outside the typhoon belt.[^cite_note-FastFactsPhilProv-LanaodelSur-4]

The population of Lanao del Sur in the 2024 census was 1,368,137 people,[^cite_note-PSA20–ARMM-13] with a density of 350 inhabitants per square kilometre or 910 inhabitants per square mile.

The majority of people in the province are [Maranaos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maranao_people),[^cite_note-FastFactsPhilProv-LanaodelSur-4] while some are [Cebuanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_people), [Ilocanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_people), [Bicolanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicolano_people), [Hiligaynons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiligaynon_people), [Kapampangans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapampangan_people), [Tagalogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_people), [Maguindanaons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_people), [Iranuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranun_people), [Higaonons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higaonon_people), [Zamboangueños](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamboangue%C3%B1o_people), [Tausugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tausug_people), [Yakans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakan_people) and [Sama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sama-Bajau_people).

[Maranao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maranao_language) is the most commonly spoken language in the province.[^cite_note-FastFactsPhilProv-LanaodelSur-4] [Iranun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranun_language) is the native language of most of the inhabitants of the municipality of Kapatagan. Also spoken are [Maguindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_language), [Cebuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_language), and [Chavacano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chavacano) followed by [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language) and [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language). Minority languages such as [Hiligaynon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiligaynon_language), [Ilocano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_language), [Kapampangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapampangan_language), [Higaonon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higaonon_language) are also spoken.

## UNESCO Designations in Lanao del Sur

[UNESCO](21786641.md) has inscribed one Maranao element, the *Darangen Chants of the Maranao People of Lake Lanao,* in the [Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO_Intangible_Cultural_Heritage_Lists) in 2008. The element was earlier inscribed in the UNESCO Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity Representative List in 2005. The organization has also designated the [Old Town of Tugaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tugaya,_Lanao_del_Sur) as a *UNESCO Home for Culture and Heritage*. UNESCO has recommended for the nomination of Tugaya and all of Lanao del Sur's traditional crafts heritage in [Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO_Intangible_Cultural_Heritage_Lists), following reports made in 2014 and 2015. The Philippine ambassador to UNESCO and France noted that it will be better to inscribe Maranao's traditional crafts in the [List of Intangible Cultural Heritage in Need of Urgent Safeguarding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO_Intangible_Cultural_Heritage_Lists) as they have been endangered due to the 2017 [Battle of Marawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Marawi). The nomination is being finalized by the Philippine government, as announced in 2018. The torogans of Lanao are also recommended by UNESCO to be nominated in the [World Heritage List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Heritage_List) once proper documentation and restoration has been completed. Two torogans have been bought by a controversial heritage resort company, [Las Casas Filipinas de Acuzar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Casas_Filipinas_de_Acuzar), and were transported to Bataan, sparking a provincial campaign to get back the two royal houses to their ancestral homeland.[^cite_note-28]
