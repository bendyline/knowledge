**Aden Mohamed Nuur Madobe** (Afmaxaa: Sheekh Aaden Maxamed Nuur Madoobe , [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): آدم محمد نور مادوبي, [Afmaay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afmaay): Sheeg Ethyng Mothoowy, born 15 April 1956), popularly known as **Aden Madobe**,[^cite_note-2] is a Somali politician who has served as the leader of the South West State of Somalia[^cite_note-goobjoog-1]

He previously served as the Speaker of the Lower House of the Federal Parliament of Somalia from 2022 to 2026. Earlier, he served as the First Deputy Chairman of the [Rahanweyn Resistance Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahanweyn_Resistance_Army), before later joining the newly formed [Transitional Federal Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Government) (TFG) of [Somalia](27358.md) as [Minister of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_\(Somalia\)) and Speaker of the [Transitional Federal Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Parliament).&#x20;

From 29 December 2008 to 31 January 2009, Madobe briefly served as acting president of Somalia. In January 2014, he was appointed Minister of Industry and Commerce.[^cite_note-Spscwwtftpos-3]

## Personal life

Madobe was born in [Hudur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudur), the capital of [Bakool](185137.md) region of [Somalia](27358.md). He belongs to the [Hadame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadame) subclan of the [Rahanweyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahanweyn) (Digil and Mirifle).[^cite_note-PRIME-MINISTER-GEEDI-ANNOUNCED-HIS-SECOND-CABINET-LINEUP-4]

## Career

### Rahanweyn Resistance Army (RRA)

Madobe served as the First Deputy Chairman of the [Rahanweyn Resistance Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahanweyn_Resistance_Army) (RRA), one of the factions of the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] According to Adan Madobe, beginning in 1996, the RRA accepted the assistance of [Ethiopia](187749.md) in the training of its troops. In 2003, he temporarily split with his fellow RRA leader [Hassan Mohamed Nur "Shatigadud"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Mohamed_Nur_%22Shatigadud%22), though they later reconciled and both served as ministers in the [Transitional Federal Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Government) (TFG).[^cite_note-REPORT-UNSC-RES1425-2002-8]

### Transitional Federal Government

In January 2005, Madobe was appointed [Minister of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_\(Somalia\)) of the nascent Transitional Federal Government, part of [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Somalia) [Ali Mohamed Ghedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Mohamed_Ghedi)'s second cabinet lineup.[^cite_note-PRIME-MINISTER-GEEDI-ANNOUNCED-HIS-SECOND-CABINET-LINEUP-4]

In May 2005, rival parliamentarian and faction leader [Muhammad Ibrahim Habsade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Ibrahim_Habsade) accused Madobe and Agriculture Minister [Hassan Mohamed Nuur "Shatigudud"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Mohamed_Nuur_%22Shatigudud%22) of attacking [Baidoa](1648752.md) to take the city on behalf of President [Abdullahi Yusuf Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullahi_Yusuf_Ahmed)'s TFG. The TFG later used the town as a temporary capital. Nineteen were killed in the ensuing fight for control over the city.[^cite_note-SOMALI-FACTIONS-FIGHT-FOR-KEY-TOWN-9]

### Speaker of the Transitional Federal Parliament

On 31 January 2007, Madobe was elected Speaker by the [Transitional Federal Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Parliament) (TFP). His predecessor at the position [Sharif Hassan Sheikh Adan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharif_Hassan_Sheikh_Adan) was voted out by the legislature on January 17, 2007 due to his alleged support for the [Islamic Courts Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Courts_Union) (ICU).[^cite_note-SOMALIA-PARLIAMENT-ELECTS-NEW-SPEAKER-10] Madobe was later sworn into office on February 3.[^cite_note-SOMALIA-PARLIAMENT-GETS-NEW-SPEAKER-11]

Madobe later briefly served as acting TFG president, after the incumbent President of Somalia Yusuf resigned from office on 29 December 2008.[^cite_note-12] On 31 January 2009, [Sharif Sheikh Ahmed](5442210.md) was voted in as the new president.[^cite_note-13]

In April and May 2010, a rift developed between Madobe and Prime Minister of Somalia, [Omar Abdirashid Ali Sharmarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Abdirashid_Ali_Sharmarke). The row culminated in Madobe's resignation after parliament later voted to remove him from office.[^cite_note-14]

On May 25, 2010, Sharif Hassan was re-elected Speaker of the Transitional Federal Parliament in place of Madobe.[^cite_note-15]

### Minister of Industry and Commerce

On 17 January 2014, Madobe was appointed Minister of Industry and Commerce by Prime Minister [Abdiweli Sheikh Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdiweli_Sheikh_Ahmed).[^cite_note-Spscwwtftpos-3]

### Speaker of the Federal Parliament

Madobe again became Speaker of House of the People (Lower House of the [Parliament of Somalia](36817385.md)) on 27 April 2022.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

### leader of the South West State

In 2026, Madobe was elected the leader of the South West State, taking office on 11 June and vacating his Speakership position.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]
