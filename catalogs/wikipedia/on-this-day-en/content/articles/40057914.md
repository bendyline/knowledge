The **Scottish Championship**, known as the **[William Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Hill_\(bookmaker\)) Championship** for sponsorship reasons,[^cite_note-2] is the second tier of the [Scottish Professional Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Professional_Football_League), the league competition for men's professional [football](10568.md) clubs in [Scotland](26994.md). The Scottish Championship was established in July 2013, after the Scottish Professional Football League was formed by a merger of the [Scottish Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Premier_League) and [Scottish Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Football_League).[^cite_note-3]

## Format

Teams play each opponent four times in the season, twice at home and twice away from home. Teams receive [three points for a win](4960100.md) and one point for a draw. No points are awarded for a loss. Teams are ranked by total points, then [goal difference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_difference), and then goals scored. At the end of each season, the club with the most points is crowned [league champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Scottish_football_champions). If points are equal, the goal difference determines the winner. If this still does not result in a winner, the tied teams must take part in a playoff game at a neutral venue to determine the final placings.[^cite_note-SPFL_rules-4]

### Promotion and relegation

The champions are directly promoted to the [Scottish Premiership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Premiership), swapping places with the bottom club of the Premiership.[^cite_note-SPFL_rules-4] The clubs finishing 2nd, 3rd and 4th then enter the two-legged Premiership play-offs. The 3rd-placed club plays the 4th-placed club, with the winner then playing the 2nd-placed club. The winner of that game then plays against the 11th-placed Premiership club. If the Championship play-off winner prevails, the club is promoted and the Premiership club is relegated; otherwise, the Premiership club remains in its league while the Championship club is not promoted.[^cite_note-SPFL_rules-4]

For promotion and relegation, the Championship play-off system closely mirrors its Premiership counterpart—the bottom-ranked club in the Championship is automatically relegated while the 9th-placed club undergoes a play-off with the 2nd, 3rd and 4th placed clubs from [League One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_League_One). [^cite_note-SPFL_rules-4]
