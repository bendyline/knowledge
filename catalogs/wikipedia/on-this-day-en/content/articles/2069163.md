**Urdaneta**, officially the **City of Urdaneta** ([Pangasinan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangasinan_language): *Siyudad na Urdaneta*; [Ilocano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_language): *Siyudad ti Urdaneta*; [Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language): *Lungsod ng Urdaneta*), is a [component city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_the_Philippines#Legal_classification) in the [province](165100.md) of [Pangasinan](1691800.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 145,935 people.[^cite_note-5] It is the third most populous local government unit ([LGU](22088280.md)) in Pangasinan and [Ilocos Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocos_Region).[^cite_note-\:0-6] Urdaneta is currently the only city in Pangasinan classified as a first-class city based on its average annual regular income.[^cite_note-7]

## History

Urdaneta was founded on January 8, 1858, by Pangasinenses and Ilocanos who sought greater ties and unity. The city was named after Father [Andrés de Urdaneta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_de_Urdaneta), aide to [Miguel Lopez De Legazpi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_L%C3%B3pez_de_Legazpi), soldier, navigator, cosmographer, and evangelist. Father Urdaneta played a significant role in the colonization of the Philippines as he helped establish the first Spanish settlement on the island of Cebu in 1565. He also helped establish the first trade route between Mexico and the Philippines, which was used for over two centuries.

By naming the city after Father Urdaneta, the people of Urdaneta City pay tribute to his legacy and contributions to the country. His name has become synonymous with exploration, adventure, and faith - qualities that are celebrated in Urdaneta City. Today, the city continues to honor Father Urdaneta through various events, monuments, and institutions, which serve as reminders of his enduring legacy.[^cite_note-8]

### Cityhood

Main article: [Cities of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_the_Philippines)

In 1998, the Philippine Congress passed Republic Act No. 8480, which converted the Municipality of Urdaneta into a component city of the Province of Pangasinan. The bill was sponsored by Rep. Amadeo R. Perez Jr., a congressman representing the 5th district of the province.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

The passage of the bill was followed by a plebiscite, which was held on March 21, 1998. The people of Urdaneta overwhelmingly ratified the legislative act, with 26,222 in favor and only 142 against. As a result, Urdaneta was officially proclaimed as a city.

## Geography

Urdaneta has a land area of 12,100 hectares.

Urdaneta is situated 40.76 kilometres (25.33 mi) from the provincial capital [Lingayen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingayen) and 188.46 kilometres (117.10 mi) from the country's capital city of [Manila](184334.md).

### Climate

Climate in Urdaneta is classified as [tropical monsoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate) under the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification). The area experiences a distinct wet season from June to October, influenced primarily by the southwest monsoon, while the months from November to May are comparatively drier. Temperatures remain warm throughout the year, with average daily highs generally ranging from 30 to 34 °C and lows from 22 to 25 °C.[^cite_note-11]

&#x20;Rainfall is heaviest in August, which is typically the peak of the rainy season, and lightest in January during the dry period. Humidity remains high year-round due to the city’s low elevation and proximity to coastal air masses, contributing to consistently warm and humid conditions.

### Religion

#### Roman Catholicism



[Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdaneta_Cathedral)

The [Urdaneta Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdaneta_Cathedral) or the Immaculate Conception Cathedral in Urdaneta is part of the Vicariate of Our Lady. Its vicar foranes are Rev. Fr. Alberto T. Arenos and Father Elpidio F. Silva Jr. Its [feast day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feast_day) is December 8. Local Ordinary is Bishop [Jacinto Agcaoili Jose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacinto_Jose), JCL, DD. Founded on January 8, 1858, it is a suffragan diocese of the [Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Lingayen-Dagupan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Lingayen-Dagupan).[^cite_note-urdacpdo-19]

The diocese is led by Bishop Jacinto Jose, who also serves as vice chairman of the [Catholic Bishops' Conference of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Bishops'_Conference_of_the_Philippines) (CBCP) Episcopal Commission of Social Communications and Mass Media and member of the Episcopal Commission on Youth.[^cite_note-20]

Previous ordinaries were Bishop Jesus Castro Galang (December 7, 1991 – September 16, 2004) and Bishop Pedro G. Magugat, M.S.C. (April 22, 1985 – May 5, 1990). Other diocesan officials hold office at the Bishop's Residence and Chancery of Obispado de Urdaneta Building, Dr. Jose Aruego Street, Urdaneta, Pangasinan. The Vicar General is Msgr. Numeriano A. Gabot Jr. and Chancellor, Father Angelo B. Lopez.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

#### Philippine Independent Church or Aglipayan Church



The Parish of the Divine Shepherd at the Aglipay Central Theological Seminary of the [Philippine Independent Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Independent_Church)

The Aglipay Central Theological Seminary (ACTS) in Urdaneta City, Pangasinan is the regional seminary of the [Philippine Independent Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Independent_Church) dedicated to serve the [North-Central and South-Central Luzon Dioceses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_dioceses_of_the_Philippine_Independent_Church). ACTS offers a [Bachelor of Theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Theology) and [Divinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Divinity) Programs for those who aspire to enter the [ordained](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordination) ministry in the Church. It is a four-year study program with a curriculum focused on biblical, theological, historical and pastoral studies with reference to parish management and development and wider cultural and social context. Members of the Philippine Independent Church or Aglipayans have the second most members in the city.

#### The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints

Urdaneta was selected as the site of the [Urdaneta Philippines Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdaneta_Philippines_Temple), which was dedicated on April 28, 2024. The third temple of [the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Church_of_Jesus_Christ_of_Latter-day_Saints) built in the [Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Church_of_Jesus_Christ_of_Latter-day_Saints_in_the_Philippines) (the other two being in [Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_Philippines_Temple) and [Cebu City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebu_City_Philippines_Temple)) Additionally, there are multiple temples under construction or in the planning process in Alabang, Davao, Bacolod, Cagayan de Oro, Tacloban, Naga, Santiago, Tuguegarao City, Iloilo, and Laoag.[^cite_note-27]

As gateway to northern Philippines, the city's strategic location is on the central part of eastern Pangasinan. Urdaneta is among the most progressive cities in northern Luzon in annual regular income beating all cities in Region 1. It is among the region's cleanest, greenest and most livable cities.

Urdaneta produces rice, vegetables and noodles. As a trading hub of Pangasinan, it has a “Bagsakan” (trading post), a drop-off point for fruits and vegetables.

The largest cattle market in northern Luzon is Urdaneta's Livestock Market. The city has numerous financial and banking institutions which make it among the most vibrant economies in Pangasinan and the whole Ilocos Region.

## Government

### Local government

Main article: [Sangguniang Panglungsod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangguniang_Panglungsod)

Urdaneta, belonging to the [fifth congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_districts_of_Pangasinan) of the province of [Pangasinan](1691800.md), is governed by a mayor designated as its local chief executive and by a city council as its legislative body in accordance with the Local Government Code. The mayor, vice mayor, and the councilors are elected directly by the people through an election which is being held every three years.

## Culture

Further information: [Urdaneta Park Landmark Monument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdaneta_Park_Landmark_Monument)

### Festivals

Urdaneta City celebrates Dumayo Festival annually March 18 – 31: Basbas ng Pag-iisang Dibdib (free mass wedding), bloodletting activity, tree planting for environment preservation, jobs fair and fun run for a cause.

The city celebrates fiesta every month of December. This is in remembrance of the city patron, Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception. Festivities are held December 1–8. Activities include a parade, drum and lyre competition, teachers' night, balikbayan night, ABC-SK night, ballroom, Miss Urdaneta City coronation night and many more events. A carnival is put up every December as a part of the event.[^cite_note-38]

In the 2012 yearly City Fiesta celebration (December 1–15), Fifth District Representative Kimi S. Cojuangco formally (December 1) opened the agro-industrial fair where “bahay kubo” booths of the city's barangays showcased their products at the Urdaneta City Cultural and Sports Complex. Cojuangco was assisted by Manila Economic and Cultural Office Chair Amadeo R. Perez Jr., Mayor Amadeo Gregorio “Bobom” Perez IV and members of the Sangguniang Bayan led by Vice Mayor Onofre C. Gorospe.
