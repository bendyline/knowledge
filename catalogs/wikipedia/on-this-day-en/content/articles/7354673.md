# Afdera (woreda)



Salt mine in Afdera

**Afdera** ([Afar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afar_language): *Afxeera*) is one of the [woredas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Ethiopia), in the [Afar Region](2483633.md) of [Ethiopia](187749.md). It is named after the [saline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_lake_\(geography\)) [Lake Afdera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Afdera), located in the southern part of the [Afar Depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afar_Depression). Part of the [Administrative Zone 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_Zone_2_\(Afar\)), Afdera is bordered on the southwest by the [Administrative Zone 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_Zone_4_\(Afar\)), on the west by [Erebti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erebti) and [Abala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abala_\(Ethiopian_District\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), on the north by [Berhale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berhale_\(Ethiopian_District\)), on the northeast by [Eritrea](17238590.md), and on the southeast by [Administrative Zone 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_Zone_1_\(Afar\)). The largest town of this woreda is [Afdera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afdera,_Ethiopia?action=edit\&redlink=1).

## Overview

The highest peak in Afdera is [Mount Mallahle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mallahle) (1875 meters or 6152 feet)[^cite_note-1]; other mountains in this [woreda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woreda) include [Erta Ale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erta_Ale) and [Borawli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borawli). Mining is the principal industry in this woreda. The best known resource extracted is [salt](1605200.md); according to the Afar Regional Mining and Energy Office, there are 300 active land grants around Lake Afdera, which the Office reports creates 1,800 permanent and over 40,000 part-time jobs.[^cite_note-2]

An all-weather road constructed in the 1990s connects this woreda to the main [Awash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awash,_Ethiopia) - [Asseb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asseb) highway.[^cite_note-3] About 90 kilometers of this gravel road, connecting Afdera to [Serdo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serdo?action=edit\&redlink=1), was completed in late January 2009 at a cost of 387 million Birr. A spokesman for the [Ethiopian Roads Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopian_Roads_Authority) stated that since it links with the port in [Djibouti City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djibouti_City), the upgraded road would have great significance for tourism and import-export trade exchange.[^cite_note-4]

At the Tio waterhole, near the southeastern border with Administrative Zone 4, L.M. Nesbitt located the 1884 massacre of the Italian expedition led by [Gustavo Bianchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavo_Bianchi) and [Giuseppe Maria Giulietti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Maria_Giulietti). Nesbitt was prevented from erecting a cairn to mark the site by the [Afars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afar_people), who believed this was part of a [blood feud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_feud).[^cite_note-5]

## Demographics

Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [Central Statistical Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Statistical_Agency_\(Ethiopia\)) of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 32,225, of whom 18,191 are men and 14,034 women; with an area of 7,435.45 square kilometers (2870.84 square miles), Afdera has a population density of 4.33 people per square kilometer (11.25 people per square mile). While 3,578 or 11.10% are urban inhabitants, a further 4,402 or 13.66% are pastoralists. A total of 4,803 households were counted in this woreda, which results in an average of 6.7 persons to a household, and 4,887 housing units. 98.6% of the population said they were [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Ethiopia), and 1.34% were [Orthodox Christians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopian_Orthodox_Christianity).[^cite_note-6]
