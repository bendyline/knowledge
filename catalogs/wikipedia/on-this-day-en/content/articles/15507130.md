The **Internet in Morocco** is among the more developed in the [MENA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MENA) region. The [country code top-level domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_code_top-level_domain) (ccTLD) of [Morocco](19291.md) is [.ma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.ma).

## Access

According to the [ITU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Telecommunication_Union), Morocco has 20,535,174 Internet users, or a 58.3%[^cite_note-free-1] penetration rate as of June 2017.[^cite_note-2] This includes 12 million [Facebook](7529378.md) users.[^cite_note-3] Between 2013 and 2014, the Moroccan Internet population grew by 1 million.[^cite_note-aujour-4] As of 2015, 94.1% of Moroccan [netizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netizen) use mobile devices to access the Internet.[^cite_note-aujour-4]

In June 2015, [Meditel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meditel) became the first operator in the country and in North Africa to deploy 4G, followed two days later by [Inwi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inwi).[^cite_note-\:0-5]

In July 2015, [Maroc Telecom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maroc_Telecom) deployed their 4G network, few weeks after its competitors.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

According to the [CIA](5183633.md), 8 [Internet service providers](100245.md) are located in Morocco.[^cite_note-6] These include [Maroc Telecom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maroc_Telecom), [Orange Morocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Morocco), and [Inwi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inwi).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

To access the internet, the following options are available: 4G, ADSL, Fibre Internet, VSAT, CDMA, and public Wi-Fi.

## Censorship

Main article: [Internet censorship in Morocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_censorship_in_Morocco)

In 2020, [Freedom House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_House) gave Morocco an Internet Freedom Score of 54/100, labeling its Internet "Partly Free."[^cite_note-free-1]
