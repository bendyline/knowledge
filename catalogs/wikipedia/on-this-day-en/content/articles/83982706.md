**Canada** is scheduled to compete at the **2028 Summer Olympics** in [Los Angeles](18110.md), [California](5407.md), [United States](3434750.md) from July 14 to 30, 2028. Since [Canada](5042916.md)'s debut in [1900](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1900_Summer_Olympics), [Canadian](19851291.md) athletes have appeared in every edition of the Summer Olympic Games, except for the [1980 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980_Summer_Olympics) in Moscow because of the country's support for the [United States-led boycott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1980_Summer_Olympics_boycott).[^cite_note-1]

## Flag football

Main article: [Flag football at the 2028 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_football_at_the_2028_Summer_Olympics)

Canada qualified a men's and women's team of ten athletes each, for a total of 20 athletes. The women's team qualified on August 15, 2026 by qualifying for the final at the [2026 IFAF Women's Flag Football World Championship](83773386.md) in [Düsseldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%BCsseldorf), Germany.[^cite_note-2] The women's team were Canada's first quota spots earned for the Games.[^cite_note-3] The men's team qualified the following day after winning bronze and finishing as one of the two highest ranked eligible teams for qualification at the [2026 IFAF Men's Flag Football World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_IFAF_Men's_Flag_Football_World_Championship), also held in Düsseldorf.[^cite_note-4]

As the sport is making its debut at the [Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympics), this also marks the country's debut appearance in the sport at the Olympics.[^cite_note-5]

## Volleyball

Main article: [Volleyball at the 2028 Summer Olympics](77464512.md)

Canada qualified a women's volleyball team by winning the qualification tournament as part of the [2026 Women's NORCECA Volleyball Championship](84036732.md#2028_Summer_Olympics_qualification) in [Santo Domingo](57638.md), Dominican Republic.[^cite_note-6] The qualification marked Canada's first Olympic appearance in women's volleyball since the [1996 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volleyball_at_the_1996_Summer_Olympics) in [Atlanta](3138.md), 32 years earlier.[^cite_note-7]
