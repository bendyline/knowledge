# New Zealand environmental law

&#x20; **[Environmental law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_law)** in **[New Zealand](4913064.md)** is an increasingly well defined body of national law that has a specialist court, The [Environment Court of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environment_Court_of_New_Zealand) ([Māori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%81ori_language): Te Kooti Taiao o Aotearoa), to decide related issues.

## History

The roots of New Zealand environmental law can be traced to the [common law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_law) of [Australia](4689264.md). The increasing environmental awareness of the 1960s led to a specific body of [environmental law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_law) that developed in many [Western](21208200.md) countries including New Zealand. Environmental law became more integrated in the 1980s with the passing of the [Environment Act 1986](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environment_Act_1986) and the [Conservation Act 1987](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_Act_1987). These Acts set up the [Ministry for the Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_for_the_Environment_\(New_Zealand\)), [Parliamentary Commissioner for the Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_Commissioner_for_the_Environment) and the [Department of Conservation](368208.md).

The most significant Act of Parliament concerning environmental law was the passing of [Resource Management Act 1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resource_Management_Act_1991). Issues under the Act are adjudicated by the [Environment Court of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environment_Court_of_New_Zealand).

## Timeline

This timeline outlines the more significant environmental legislation in New Zealand:

- 1903 - [Scenery Preservation Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scenery_Preservation_Act_1903)

- 1962 - [Nature Conservation Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_Conservation_Council_\(New_Zealand\)) set up under the [Nature Conservation Council Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_Conservation_Council_Act_1962?action=edit\&redlink=1).

- August 1970 - [New Zealand Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Cabinet) established the [Environmental Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_Council?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- February 1972 - Cabinet established the position of [Minister of the Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_the_Environment#N)

- August 1972 - Government creates the [New Zealand Commission for the Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Commission_for_the_Environment)

- April 1973 - The [Clean Air Act 1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clean_Air_Act_1972) comes into force.

- March 1974 - Environmental Protection and Enhancement Procedures (EP\&EP) are instituted.

- January 1987 - [Environment Act 1986](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environment_Act_1986) comes into force.

- April 1987 - [Conservation Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_Act) comes into force.

- October 1991 - [Resource Management Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resource_Management_Act_1991) comes into force.

- June 1996 - [Hazardous Substances and New Organisms Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazardous_Substances_and_New_Organisms_Act)
