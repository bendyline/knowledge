***Yellow Letters*** ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): ***Gelbe Briefe***) is a 2026 [political drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_drama) film directed by [İlker Çatak](76181560.md), who co-wrote the screenplay with Ayda Çatak and Enis Köstepen. It follows Derya ([Özgü Namal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%96zg%C3%BC_Namal)) and Aziz ([Tansu Biçer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tansu_Bi%C3%A7er)), a couple of Turkish theater artists who lose their jobs due to [political persecution in Turkey](9790999.md).[^cite_note-presseheft-handlung-2]

The film had its world premiere at the main competition of the [76th Berlin International Film Festival](81825397.md) on 13 February 2026, where it won the [Golden Bear](21547166.md).[^cite_note-3] It was theatrically released in Germany by Alamode Film on 5 March.

## Premise

The marriage of an artist couple begins to unravel when they lose their jobs shortly after their play's premiere.[^cite_note-4]



[İlker Çatak](76181560.md), [İpek Bilgin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0pek_Bilgin), Leyla Smyrna Cabas, [Özgü Namal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%96zg%C3%BC_Namal) and [Tansu Biçer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tansu_Bi%C3%A7er) at the [76th Berlin International Film Festival](81825397.md)

## Production

### Development

An international [co-production](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-production_\(media\)) of Germany, France, and Turkey. In 2021, the project received a €10,000 production grant by the German-Turkish Co-Production Development Fund.[^cite_note-5] It participated at the Berlinale Co-Production Market in February 2022.[^cite_note-6] In November 2022, the project was awarded €65,000 grant from the MOIN Filmförderung.[^cite_note-7] The project received €368,000 production grant from [Eurimages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurimages) in November 2023.[^cite_note-8]

### Filming

[Principal photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography) took place in [Hamburg](13467.md) and [Berlin](3354.md), standing in for [Ankara](802.md) and [Istanbul](3391396.md), and wrapped in July 2024.[^cite_note-9] It was also shot at the [Berliner Ensemble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berliner_Ensemble) Main Auditorium, with a scene involving about 250 extras.[^cite_note-10]

## Release

*Yellow Letters* had its world premiere at the Main Competition section of the [76th Berlin International Film Festival](81825397.md) in February 2026.[^cite_note-11] In February 2024, Be For Films acquired the film's international sales.[^cite_note-12]

The film was released theatrically in Germany by Alamode Film on 5 March 2026.[^cite_note-13]

The film was also part of Horizons section of the [60th Karlovy Vary International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/60th_Karlovy_Vary_International_Film_Festival), where it was screened from 3 July to 7 July 2026.[^cite_note-14] For its Spanish premiere, it was selected in the 'Official Section' slate of the [71st Valladolid International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/71st_Valladolid_International_Film_Festival).[^cite_note-15]

## Reception

### Critical response

On [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator) website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), the film holds an approval rating of 86% based on 21 reviews, with an average rating of 6.7/10.[^cite_note-16] On [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic), the film has a [weighted average score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean) of 66 out of 100, based on 9 critics, indicating "generally favorable reviews".[^cite_note-Metacritic-17]
