**Age of candidacy** is the minimum age at which a person can [legally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law) hold certain elected government offices. In many cases, it also determines the age at which a person may be [eligible to stand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomination_rules) for an election or be granted [ballot access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballot_access).&#x20;

## International standards

International electoral standards which are defined in the International Public Human Rights Law, allow restricting candidacy on the basis of age. The interpretation of the International Covenant for Civil and Political Rights offered by the United Nations Human Rights Committee in the General Comment 25 states "Any conditions which apply to the exercise of the rights protected by article 25 (of the ICCPR) should be based on objective and reasonable criteria. For example, it may be reasonable to require a higher age for election or appointment to particular offices than for exercising the right to vote, which should be available to every adult citizen."[^cite_note-1]

## Historical

The first known example of a law enforcing age of candidacy was the *[Lex Villia Annalis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lex_Villia_Annalis)*, a Roman law enacted in 180 BCE which set the minimum ages for senatorial [magistrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Magistrates).[^cite_note-215]

## By country

### Australia

In [Australia](4689264.md) a person must be aged 18 or over to stand for election to public office at federal, state or local government level. Prior to 1973, the age of candidacy for the federal parliament was 21.[^cite_note-216]

The youngest ever member of the [House of Representatives](61565.md) was 20-year-old [Wyatt Roy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyatt_Roy), elected in the 2010 federal election. The youngest member of [Penrith City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penrith_City_Council), and of any local council in [New South Wales](21654.md), is Libby Austin, who was 19 at the time of her election in September 2024.[^cite_note-217]

### Austria

In [Austria](26964606.md), a person must be 18 years of age or older to stand in elections to the [European Parliament](9581.md) or [National Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Council_of_Austria).[^cite_note-\:0-18] The Diets of regional [*Länder*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Austria) are able to set a minimum age lower than 18 for to be in the polls in elections to the Diet itself as well as to municipal councils in the Land.[^cite_note-218] In presidential elections the candidacy age is 35.

### Belgium

Any Belgian who has reached the age of 18 years can stand for election for the [Chamber of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Representatives_\(Belgium\)), can become a member of the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_\(Belgium\)), or can be elected in one of the regional parliaments.[^cite_note-219] This is regulated in the [Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Belgium) (Art. 64) and in the Special Law on the Reform of the Institutions.

### Belize

According to the [Constitution of Belize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Belize), a person must be at least 18 years old to be elected as a member of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_\(Belize\)) and must be at least 30 to be Speaker of the House. A person must be at least 18 years old to be appointed to the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_\(Belize\)) and must be at least 30 to be president or Vice-President of the Senate. As only members of the House of Representatives are eligible to be appointed prime minister, the [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Prime_Ministers_of_Belize) must be at least 18 years old. A person must also be at least 18 years old to be elected to a village council.[^cite_note-\:1-30]

### Brazil

The [Brazilian](3383.md) Constitution (Article 14, Section 3 (VI)) defines 35 years as the minimum age for someone to be elected president, Vice-President or Senator; 30 years for state Governor or Vice-Governor; 21 for Federal or State Deputy, Mayor or Vice-Mayor; and 18 for city Council member.[^cite_note-\:2-35]

### Canada

In [Canada](5042916.md), [the constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Canada) does not outline any age requirements to run for elected office, simply stating "Every citizen of Canada has the right to vote in an election of the members of the House of Commons or of a legislative assembly and to be qualified for membership therein."[^cite_note-220] However under the current [Elections Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Elections_Act) Act to be eligible to run for elected office (municipal, provincial, federal) one must be a minimum of 18 years or older on the day of the election.[^cite_note-221] Prior to 1970, the age requirement was 21 along with the voting age.&#x20;

To be appointed to the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Canada) ([Upper House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_house)), one must be at least 30 years of age and under 75 years of age.[^cite_note-222]

In the province of Ontario, [Sam Oosterhoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Oosterhoff), a member of the [Progressive Conservative Party of Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_Conservative_Party_of_Ontario), was first elected at the age of 19 in a November 2016 by-election, the youngest Ontario MPP to ever be elected.[^cite_note-223]

[Pierre-Luc Dusseault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre-Luc_Dusseault) (born May 31, 1991) is a Canadian politician who was elected to the [House of Commons of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Commons_of_Canada) in the [2011 federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Canadian_federal_election) at the age of 19, becoming the youngest Member of Parliament in the country's history. He was sworn into office two days after his 20th birthday. He was re-elected in [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Canadian_federal_election) but lost his seat in the [2019 Canadian federal election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Canadian_federal_election).[^cite_note-224]

### Central African Republic

Article 36 of the 2016 [Constitution of the Central African Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Central_African_Republic) requires that candidates for [President](378644.md) must "be aged thirty-five (35) years at least \[on] the day of the deposit of the dossier of the candidature".[^cite_note-CARConstitution-43]

### Chile

In [Chile](5489.md) the minimum age required to be elected [President of the Republic](11885300.md) is 35 years on the day of the election. Before the 2005 reforms the requirement was 40 years, and from 1925 to 1981 it was 30 years. For [senators](683411.md) it is 35 years (between 1981 and 2005 it was 40 years) and for [deputies](716259.md) it is 21 years (between 1925 and 1970 it was 35 years).[^cite_note-225]

### China

In [China](5405.md) the minimum age to be elected as president or vice-president is 45.[^cite_note-226] All citizens who have reached the age of 18 have the right to vote and stand for [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_China).[^cite_note-227]

### Cyprus

In [Cyprus](5593.md) the minimum age to be elected president is 35 years. The minimum age to run for the [House of Representatives](4497460.md) was 25 years until the [Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Cyprus) was amended in 2019 to lower the limit to 21.[^cite_note-228]

### Czech Republic

In the [Czech Republic](5321.md), a person must be at least 18-years-old [to be elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_the_Czech_Republic) in [local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_election). A person must be at least 21 years old to be elected to the [lower house](3991120.md) of the [Czech Parliament](3991200.md) or to the [European Parliament](9581.md) and 40 years old to be a member of the upper house ([Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_the_Czech_Republic)) of the Parliament[^cite_note-\:5-56] or the [President of the Czech Republic](410288.md).

### Denmark

In [Denmark](76972.md), any adult 18 years of age or older can become a candidate and be elected in any public election.

### Estonia

In [Estonia](28222445.md), any citizen 18 years of age or older [can be elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Estonia) in [local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_election), and 21 years or older in [parliamentary elections](794927.md). The minimum age for the [President of Estonia](1581712.md) is 40.[^cite_note-\:6-68]

### France

In [France](5843419.md), any citizen 18 years of age or older can be elected to the [lower house of Parliament](299616.md), and 24 years or older for the [Senate](567323.md). The minimum age for the [President of France](24899.md) is 18.

### Germany

In [Germany](11867.md) a citizen must be 18 or over [to be elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_Germany) at the national level, like the [Chancellor](20890626.md), and this age to be elected at the regional or local level. A person must be 40 or over to be [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Germany).

### Greece

In [Greece](12108.md), those aged 25 years old and over who hold Greek [citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship) are eligible to stand and be elected to the [Hellenic Parliament](2449826.md).[^cite_note-archive.ipu.org-80] All over 40 years old are eligible to stand for presidency.

### Hong Kong

In Hong Kong a person must be at least 21 to be candidate in a district council or Legislative Council election.[^cite_note-229][^cite_note-230] A person must be at least 40 to be candidate in the [Chief Executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Executive_\(Hong_Kong\)) election, and also at least 40 to be candidate in the election for the [President of the Legislative Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Legislative_Council_of_Hong_Kong) from among the members of the Legislative Council.[^cite_note-231]

### Iceland

For the office of [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Iceland), any Icelandic citizen who has reached the age of 35 and fulfills the [requirement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Requirement) necessary to vote in elections to the [Althing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Althing) is eligible to be elected president.[^cite_note-232]

### India

In [India](14533.md) a person must be at least:

- 35 to be the [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_India) or [Vice President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_India), [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_\(India\)), and Lieutenant Governor of the [states and union territories](375986.md) as specified in the [Constitution of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India)

- 30 to be a [member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament,_Rajya_Sabha) of [Rajya Sabha](232210.md) (the upper house of [Parliament of India](410238.md)) and a [State Legislative Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_legislative_councils_of_India) (the upper house of a [State Legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_legislature_\(India\)), in those states where the legislature is bicameral).

- 25 to be the [Prime Minister](24452.md), [Lok Sabha Speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_Lok_Sabha), [Union Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Council_of_Ministers), [Chief Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_minister_\(India\)), [Assembly Speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_\(India\)), and State Minister in the [state governments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_governments_of_India). Additionally, to be a [member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament,_Lok_Sabha) of the [Lok Sabha](275456.md) (the lower house of [Parliament of India](410238.md)) and a [State Legislative Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_legislative_assemblies_of_India) (the lower house or only house of a State Legislature in the states of India.)

- 21 to be the [Mayor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_\(India\)), Chairperson, Head and a Member (Members are variously called Corporators/Councillors/Ward Members according to the type of their respective local bodies) of a [Municipal Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_corporation_\(India\)), [Municipal Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_council_\(India\)), [Nagar Panchayat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagar_Panchayat), [District Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_council_\(India\)), [Block Panchayat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panchayat_Samiti), and [Gram Panchayat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gram_Panchayat) respectively.

Criticism has been on the rise to decrease the age of candidacy in India. Young India Foundation has been working on a campaign to decrease the age of candidacy in India for [MPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament_\(India\)) and [MLAs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Legislative_Assembly_\(India\)) to better reflect the large young demographic of India.[^cite_note-233]

### Indonesia

In [Indonesia](14579.md) a person must be at least:

- 40 to be [President](10011631.md) or [Vice President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Indonesia) or has or is currently holding a position elected through general elections, including regional head elections as specified in the [Constitution of Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Indonesia)

- 30 to be Governor or Lieutenant Governor, as specified in the 2004 Regional Government Act

- 25 to be Regent, Vice Regent, Mayor, or Deputy Mayor, as specified in the 2004 Regional Government Act

- 21 to be Senator or Representative in both national and local parliament, as specified in the 2008 Election Act

### Israel

In [Israel](9282173.md) one must be at least 21 to become a member of the [Knesset](74656.md) ([Basic Law: The Knesset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Law:_The_Knesset) section 6(a)) or a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_elections_in_Israel). Every Israeli Citizen (including minors) can be appointed as a [Government](5006115.md) Minister, or elected as [President of Israel](153049.md), but the latter role is mostly ceremonial and elected by the Parliament.

### Italy

In [Italy](14532.md), a person must be at least 50 to be President of the Republic, 40 to be a [Senator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Senate), and 25 to be a [Deputy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Chamber_of_Deputies), as specified in the 1947 [Constitution of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Italy). 18 years of age is sufficient, however, to be elected member of the Council of Regions, Provinces, and Municipalities (Communes).

### Iraq

### Ireland

The 1937 [Constitution of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Ireland) requires the [President](24676.md) to be at least 35 and members of the [Oireachtas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oireachtas) (legislature) to be 21.[^cite_note-\:12-95][^cite_note-citizensinformationie-96] [Members of the European Parliament](258909.md) for Ireland must also be 21.[^cite_note-citizensinformationie-96][^cite_note-\:13-97] Members of [local authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_in_the_Republic_of_Ireland) must be 18, reduced from 21 in 1973.[^cite_note-citizensinformationie-96][^cite_note-\:14-98] The 1922–1937 [Constitution of the Irish Free State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Irish_Free_State) required [TDs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teachta_D%C3%A1la) (members of the [Dáil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A1il_%C3%89ireann_\(Irish_Free_State\)), lower house) to be 21,[^cite_note-236] whereas [Senators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seanad_%C3%89ireann_\(Irish_Free_State\)) had to be 35 (reduced to 30 in 1928).[^cite_note-\:15-99] At the [1987 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_Irish_general_election), the [High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Court_\(Ireland\)) ruled that a candidate ([Hugh Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cork_East_\(D%C3%A1il_constituency\)#1987_general_election)) was eligible who reached the minimum age after the date of nomination but before the date of election.[^cite_note-237] The [Thirty-fifth Amendment of the Constitution Bill 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirty-fifth_Amendment_of_the_Constitution_Bill_2015) proposed to lower the presidential age limit to 21.[^cite_note-238] However, this proposal was rejected by 73% of the voters.

### Japan

In [Nigeria](21383.md), a person must be at least 35 years of age to be elected [President](516088.md) or [Vice President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Nigeria), 35 to be a senator, 30 to be a State Governor, and 25 to be a Representative in parliament or Member of the States' House of Assembly.[^cite_note-242]

### North Korea

In [North Korea](21255.md), any person eligible to vote in elections to the [Supreme People's Assembly](695507.md) is also eligible to stand for candidacy. The age for both voting and candidacy is 17.[^cite_note-243]

### Norway

In [Norway](21241.md), any adult, aged 18 or over within the calendar year, can become a candidate and be elected in any public election.

### Palestine

Palestinian parliamentary candidates must be at least 28 years old, while the presidential candidates must be at least 40 years old.[^cite_note-244]

### Pakistan

In [Pakistan](23235.md), a person must be at least 45 years old to be [President](419460.md). A person must be at least 25 years old to be a member of the provincial assembly or national assembly.[^cite_note-245]

### Spain

[Spain](26667.md) has two legislative chambers of Parliament, a lower house and an upper house. These are the [Congress of Deputies](352254.md) (lower house) and the [Senate of Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Spain) (upper house) respectively. The minimum age requirement to stand and to be elected to either house is 18 years of age.[^cite_note-Spain-181]

### Sweden

In [Sweden](5058739.md), any citizen at least 18 years old, who resides, or who has resided in the realm can be elected to parliament.[^cite_note-254] Citizens of Sweden, the European Union, Norway or Iceland aged 18 and over may be elected to county or municipal council. Citizens of other countries may also be elected to council, provided they have resided in the realm for at least three years.[^cite_note-255]

### Switzerland

In [Switzerland](26748.md), any citizen aged 18 or over can become a candidate and be elected in any federal election.

### Taiwan

In the [Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China) (commonly known as Taiwan), the minimum age of candidacy is 23, unless otherwise specified in the Constitution or any relevant laws.[^cite_note-256] The Civil Servants Election and Recall Act specifies that candidates for township, city, and indigenous district chiefs must be at least 26, and candidates for municipality, county, and city governors must be at least 30.[^cite_note-257] The minimum age to be elected as president or vice-president is 40.[^cite_note-258]

### Tibet

The [14th Dalai Lama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_Dalai_Lama) was enthroned at the age of 4, and none of [his predecessors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalai_Lama) have been enthroned before age 4. The coming of age for the Dalai Lama is 18, when responsibilities are assumed.&#x20;

### Turkey

The [1876 constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_constitution_of_1876) set the age for parliamentary elections as 30. This remained unchanged until 13 October 2006, when it was lowered to 25 through a constitutional amendment. In 2017, it was further lowered to 18, the same as the [voting age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voting_age).[^cite_note-259] In presidential elections the candidacy age is 40.

### United Kingdom

In the [United Kingdom](31717.md), a person must be aged 18 or over to stand in [elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_the_United_Kingdom) to all parliaments, assemblies, and councils within the UK, [devolved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devolution), or local level. This age requirement also applies in elections to any individual elective public office; the main example is that of an [elected mayor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elected_mayors_in_the_United_Kingdom), whether of [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_London) or a [local authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_in_the_United_Kingdom). There are no higher age requirements for particular positions in public office. Candidates are required to be aged 18 on both the day of nomination and the day of the poll.

Previously, the requirement was that candidates be 21 years old. During the early 2000s, the [British Youth Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Youth_Council) and other groups successfully campaigned to lower age of candidacy requirements in the United Kingdom.[^cite_note-byc-260] The age of candidacy was reduced from 21 to 18 in [England](9316.md), [Wales](69894.md) and [Scotland](26994.md) on 1 January 2007,[^cite_note-261] when section 17 of the [Electoral Administration Act 2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_Administration_Act) entered into force.[^cite_note-262]

### United States

Main article: [Age of candidacy laws in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_candidacy_laws_in_the_United_States)

In the [United States](3434750.md), a person must be aged 35 or over to serve as president. To be a senator, a person must be aged 30 or over. To be a Representative, a person must be aged 25 or older. This is specified in the [U.S. Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Article_Two_of_the_United_States_Constitution#Clause_5:_Qualifications_for_office). Most states in the U.S. also have age requirements for the offices of Governor, State Senator, and State Representative.[^cite_note-263] Some states have a minimum age requirement to hold any elected office (usually 21 or 18).

#### Controversies



In 1972, [Linda Jenness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Jenness) ran for president of the United States as the third-party [Socialist Workers Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Workers_Party_\(United_States\)) candidate, although she was 31 at the time.

Many [youth rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_rights) groups view current age of candidacy requirements as unjustified [age discrimination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ageism).[^cite_note-264] Occasionally people who are younger than the minimum age will run for an office in protest of the requirement or because they do not know that the requirement exists. On extremely rare occasions, young people have been elected to offices they do not qualify for and have been deemed ineligible to assume the office.

In 1872, [Victoria Woodhull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Woodhull) ran for President of the United States, although according to the Constitution she would have been too young to be President if elected.[^cite_note-265]

In 1934, [Rush Holt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rush_Holt_Sr.) of West Virginia was elected to the [Senate of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_the_United_States) at the age of 29. Since the [U.S. Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Constitution) requires senators to be at least 30, Holt was forced to wait until his 30th birthday, six months after the start of the session, before being sworn in.[^cite_note-266]

In 1954, [Richard Fulton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Fulton) won election to the [Tennessee Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_Senate). Shortly after being sworn in, Fulton was ousted from office because he was 27 years old at the time. The [Tennessee State Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_State_Constitution) required that senators be at least 30.[^cite_note-267] Rather than hold a new election, the previous incumbent, [Clifford Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_Allen), was allowed to resume his office for another term. Fulton went on to win the next State Senate election in 1956 and was later elected to the [U.S. House of Representatives](19468510.md) where he served for 10 years.

In 1964, [Congressman Jed Johnson Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jed_Johnson_Jr.) of Oklahoma was elected to the [89th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/89th_United_States_Congress) in the 1964 election while still aged 24 years. However, he became [eligible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passive_suffrage) for the House after turning 25 on his birthday, 27 December 1964, seven days before his swearing in, making him the youngest *legally* elected and seated member of the United States Congress ever.[^cite_note-268]

In South Carolina, two Senators aged 24 were elected, but were too young according to the State Constitution: Mike Laughlin in 1969 and [Bryan Dorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Jennings_Bryan_Dorn) (later a U.S. congressman) in 1941. They were seated anyway.[^cite_note-269]

On several occasions, the [Socialist Workers Party (USA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Workers_Party_\(USA\)) has nominated candidates too young to qualify for the offices they were running for. In 1972, [Linda Jenness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Jenness) ran as the SWP presidential candidate, although she was 31 at the time. Since the U.S. Constitution requires that the President and Vice President be at least 35 years old, Jenness was not able to receive [ballot access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballot_access) in several states in which she otherwise qualified.[^cite_note-270] Despite this handicap, Jenness still received 83,380 votes.[^cite_note-271] In 2004, the SWP nominated [Arrin Hawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrin_Hawkins) as the party's vice-presidential candidate, although she was 28 at the time. Hawkins was also unable to receive ballot access in several states due to her age.[^cite_note-272]

#### Reform efforts

In the United States, many groups have attempted to lower age of candidacy requirements in various states. In 1994, [South Dakota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Dakota) voters rejected a ballot measure that would have lowered the age requirements to serve as a State Senator or State Representative from 25 to 18. In 1998, however, they approved a similar [ballot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballot) measure that reduced the age requirements for those offices from 25 to 21.[^cite_note-273] In 2002, [Oregon](26811621.md) voters rejected a ballot measure that would have reduced the age requirement to serve as a State Representative from 21 to 18.

### Venezuela

In [Venezuela](32374.md), a person must be at least 30 to be [President](3329692.md) or [Vice President](23535071.md),[^cite_note-274] 21 to be a deputy for the [National Assembly](510768.md)[^cite_note-275] and 25 to be the Governor of a [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Venezuela).[^cite_note-276]
