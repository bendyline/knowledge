**Zahra Sarbali Alishah** (born 13 August 1993), known as **Zahra Sarbali** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): زهرا سربالی),[^cite_note-FD-2] is an Iranian [footballer](191466.md) who plays as a [midfielder](548981.md) for [Kowsar Women Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kowsar_Women_Football_League) club [Bam Khatoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bam_Khatoon_F.C) and the [Iran women's national team](9896170.md). She has also played for the Iran U19 women's football team.

In March 2026, Sarbali, along with some teammates from the Iranian women's national football team, [left the team's training camp](82634971.md) in [Australia](4689264.md) and sought refuge due to concerns about possible retaliation from Iranian authorities. Political figures, including [Reza Pahlavi](613947.md), called for protection for the players.[^cite_note-4] US President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) publicly urged the Australian government to grant asylum to the players, stating they could face persecution if they returned to Iran.[^cite_note-5] The Australian government subsequently granted humanitarian visas to five players from the team.[^cite_note-6] On 14 March, Sarbali, together with fellow Iranian players Soltan Meshkehkar and [Mona Hamoudi](64417968.md), reversed their earlier decision to seek asylum and chose to return to Iran. The *[Tasnim News Agency](42800472.md)* added that they had rejected "psychological warfare, extensive propaganda and seductive offers".[^cite_note-7]
