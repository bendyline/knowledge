The **Canada women's indoor national volleyball team** represents [Canada](5042916.md) in international volleyball competitions.[^cite_note-2] They are overseen by [Volleyball Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volleyball_in_Canada#History),[^cite_note-3] the governing body for [volleyball in Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volleyball_in_Canada).

The purpose of the senior women's national team program is to identify, select, and train a group of athletes who have potential and demonstrated international skill and experience to represent Canada at the elite level.
