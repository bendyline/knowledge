**Chikun** ([Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/0/09/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Chikun.wav/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Chikun.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area_of_Nigeria) in central [Kaduna State](2227637.md), [Nigeria](21383.md). It has an area of about 4,466 km<sup>2</sup>, and had a population of 372,272 as of the 2006 census.[^cite_note-3] Its headquarters is located in the town of [Kujama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kujama). The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 2438000.[^cite_note-4]

## Boundaries

Chikun [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area) (LGA) shares boundaries with [Kachia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kachia) LGA to the south, [Kajuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kajuru) LGA to the east, [Kaduna South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_South) LGA to the northeast, [Igabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igabi) LGA to the northeast, [Birnin Gwari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birnin_Gwari) LGA to the northwest; and [Munya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munya,_Nigeria) LGA to the south and [Shiroro](24883014.md) LGA to the west, all of [Niger State](1723431.md) to west, respectively.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Climate/Geography

In Chikun Local Government Area, the wet season is oppressive and largely [cloudy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud), the dry season is partly gloomy, and it is hot year round. The average range of temperatures is 55 °F to 100 °F.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

Chikun Local Government Area has an average yearly temperature of 33 [degrees Celsius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degrees_Celsius) or 91.4 [degrees Fahrenheit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degrees_Fahrenheit) and a total area of 4,645 [square kilometres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_kilometres) (or 1,724 [square miles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_miles)). There are two distinct seasons in the area: the rainy season begins in May and peaks in September, and the dry season runs from October to April.[^cite_note-\:0-9]

## Economy

Chikun Local Government Area residents rely primarily on agriculture for their livelihood, with a wide range of crops being farmed there. Weaving textiles, producing mats, and pottery are some of the other significant economic activities in Chikun Local Government Area. There are other markets in the neighborhood, and the trade sector is thriving as well.[^cite_note-\:0-9]

## History

Chikun [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area) derives its name from a [Gbagyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gbagyi) village named Chikun in the southeastern part of [Kujama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kujama). The area was originally populated by the [Gbagyi people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gbagyi_people) but is now being subsumed by urbanization making it a cosmopolitan part of [Kaduna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_\(city\)).

On 5 July 2021, over 100 [secondary school](554992.md) pupils from Bethel Baptist Secondary School in Chikun [were kidnapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikun_kidnapping).

## Demographics

### Population

Chikun [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area) according to the March 21, 2006 national population census was put at 372,272.[^cite_note-13] Its population was projected by the [*National Population Commission of Nigeria and National Bureau of Statistics*](https://citypopulation.de/php/nigeria-admin.php?adm1id=NGA019,) to be 502,500 by March 21, 2016.

### People

The indigenous people are the [Gbagyi people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gbagyi_people). They also make up the major population in the area.

## Kingship

The traditional ruler is The Esu Chikun, His Royal Highness Isyaku Yari (Sa Gbagy II) [Gbagyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gbagyi). The jurisdiction of the royal Chief covers the entire Chikun [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area) and parts of [Kaduna South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_South) [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Area) of Television village and Romi New Extension.
