**Padina** is a commune in [Buzău County](269311.md), [Muntenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muntenia), [Romania](25445.md). It is composed of a single village, Padina. It formerly included Tătulești village, now depopulated.

The commune is located in the [Bărăgan Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C4%83r%C4%83gan_Plain), in the extreme southeast of the county, on the border with [Brăila County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Br%C4%83ila_County) and [Ialomița County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ialomi%C8%9Ba_County). It is crossed by the [national road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roads_in_Romania) [DN2C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DN2C) that connects [Buzău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buz%C4%83u) with [Slobozia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slobozia); county road DJ203E branches off from here, connecting Padina with [Rușețu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ru%C8%99e%C8%9Bu).
