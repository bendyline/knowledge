**Santuk** is a district within [Kampong Thom Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampong_Thom_Province), in central [Cambodia](334751.md). According to the 1998 census of Cambodia, it had a population of 58,434.[^cite_note-1] [Santuk Silk Farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santuk_Silk_Farm) and [Phnom Santuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phnom_Santuk) lie in the district.
