**Visakhapatnam** ([/vɪˌsɑːkəˈpʌtnəm/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English); [formerly known](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_renamed_places_in_India) as **Vizagapatam**,[^cite_note-16] and also referred to as **Vizag**, **Visakha**, and **Waltair**)[^cite_note-auto-1][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] is the largest and most populous city in the [Indian state](375986.md) of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md).[^cite_note-19] It is between the [Eastern Ghats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Ghats) and the coast of the [Bay of Bengal](47892.md).[^cite_note-location-20][^cite_note-21] It is the second largest city on the [east coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_India) of India after [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai), and the fourth largest in [South India](678583.md). It is one of the four [smart cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_city) of Andhra Pradesh selected under the [Smart Cities Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_Cities_Mission)[^cite_note-22] and is the headquarters of [Visakhapatnam district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_district).[^cite_note-city_of_destiny-23] Vizag is popularly known as *shipbuilding capital of India* due to presence of multiple shipyards such as [Hindustan Shipyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan_Shipyard), [Naval Dockyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Dockyard_\(Visakhapatnam\)) and being the central naval command of the east coast. As the economic hub of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md), the city hosts diversified economy with the presence of heavy industries, ports, logistics, pharmaceuticals, medtech, biotechnology, energy production, tourism, textiles, R\&D and a growing [information technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_technology) and [financial technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_technology) center. It is also described as *the City of Destiny* and *the Jewel of the East Coast*.[^cite_note-city_of_destiny-23]

Visakhapatnam's history dates back to the 6th century BCE.[^cite_note-24] Historically considered part of the [Kalinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalinga_\(region\)) region.The city was ruled by the [Andhra Satavahanas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satavahana_dynasty), [Vengi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vengi), the [Pallava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pallava_dynasty), [Eastern Ganga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Ganga_dynasty) dynasties and [Gajapati dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gajapati_dynasty).[^cite_note-history3-25][^cite_note-26] Visakhapatnam was an ancient port city which had trade relations with the [Middle East](19323.md) and [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire).[^cite_note-27] Ships in Visakhapatnam were anchored at open roads and loaded with cargo transported from the shoreside using small [masula boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masula_boat).[^cite_note-28] A reference to a Vizagapatnam merchant is available in the inscriptions of [Bheemeswara temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumararama) (1068 CE) in the [East Godavari District](674886.md).[^cite_note-29] During the 12th century CE, Vizagapatnam was a fortified mercantile town managed by a guild.[^cite_note-30] European powers eventually established trade operations in the city, and by the end of the 18th century it had come under [French colonial rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_India).[^cite_note-history1-31][^cite_note-history2-32] Control of the city passed to the [East India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_Company) in 1804 and it remained under [British colonial rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj) until [Indian independence in 1947](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_independence_movement).[^cite_note-history2-32]

The city is home to some reputed Central and State educational institutions of the state, including [Andhra University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_University) (AU), [Andhra Medical College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Medical_College) (AMC), [Indian Institute of Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Management_Visakhapatnam) (IIM), [Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Petroleum_and_Energy) (IIPE), [Damodaram Sanjivayya National Law University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damodaram_Sanjivayya_National_Law_University) (DSNLU), [Indian Maritime University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Maritime_University#Visakhapatnam_Campus) (IMU), and the [National Institute of Oceanography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Oceanography,_India) among others. Visakhapatnam serves as the headquarters for the [Indian Navy](604522.md)'s [Eastern Naval Command](30139022.md).[^cite_note-33] The city also serves as the [zonal headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zones_and_divisions_of_Indian_Railways) of [South Coast Railway Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Coast_Railway_zone) (SCoR). The city is also home to the oldest [shipyard](213654.md) and the only [natural harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harbor) on the east coast of India.[^cite_note-34] [Visakhapatnam Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_Port) is the [fifth-busiest cargo port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ports_in_India) in India. The city is a major tourist destination and is known for its beaches, ancient [Buddhist](3267529.md) sites, and the natural environment of the surrounding [Eastern Ghats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Ghats).[^cite_note-35] According to the [Swachh Survekshan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swachh_Survekshan) rankings of 2020, it is the ninth cleanest city in India among cities with a population of more than [1 million](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_million).[^cite_note-36] In 2020, it was a finalist in the Living and Inclusion category of the World Smart City Awards.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38]

## Etymology

The local belief behind the name of the city states that there was a king in the 4th century, who on his pilgrimage halted at Lawson's Bay and built a temple dedicated to [Vaisakha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishakha), which was submerged under the sea, but the name of the temple became attached to the settlement.[^cite_note-etymology-39] Other historical names are, *Kulotungapatnam*, named by the Chola King [Kulottunga I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulottunga_I); *Ishakapatnam*, based on a Muslim Saint, Syed Ali Madani (Ishak Madani).[^cite_note-etymology-39][^cite_note-etymology2-40] During the [East India Company rule in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Company_rule_in_India), the city was known with the name, *Vizagapatnam*.[^cite_note-sepoymutiny-41] The suburb *Waltair* is another such name which was derived from the name the British colonial government used.[^cite_note-city_of_destiny-23] "Vizagapatnam" could also be spelled *Visakhapatnam* in the West European alphabet. The name was popularly shortened to *Vizag* and this form was in use right from the earliest days of British colonial rule in the district in the late eighteenth century.[^cite_note-Fathima-42] It is still referred to as Vizag by locals too; however, since [independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_independence_movement), people have reverted to calling it by its Indian name of Visakhapatnam.[^cite_note-etymology-39]

## History

Main article: [History of Visakhapatnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Visakhapatnam)

Visakhapatnam's history stretches back to the 6th century BCE and the city finds mention in ancient texts such as the 4th century BCE writings of [Pāṇini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C4%81%E1%B9%87ini) and [Katyayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C4%81ty%C4%81yana). Historically considered part of the [Kalinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalinga_\(historical_region\)) region,[^cite_note-history1-31][^cite_note-history2-32] it was ruled by the [Vengi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vengi) kingdom and the [Pallava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pallava_dynasty) and [Eastern Ganga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Ganga_dynasty) dynasties during medieval times.[^cite_note-history3-25] Archaeological records suggest that the present city was built around the 11th and 12th centuries C.E. by the [Chola dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chola_dynasty) king [Kulothunga I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulottunga_I). Control over the city fluctuated between the [Chola dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chola_dynasty) of [Tamil Nadu](29918.md) and the [Gajapati Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gajapati_Empire) of [Odisha](250724.md)[^cite_note-history1-31][^cite_note-history2-32] until its conquest by the [Vijayanagara Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayanagara_Empire) in the 15th century.[^cite_note-history3-25]

The city was ruled by the Andhra Kings of Vengi and Pallavas. The city is named after Sri Visakha Varma.[^cite_note-43] Legend has it that [Radha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radha) and [Viśakha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakha) were born on the same day and were equally beautiful. Sri Visakka Sakhi is the second most important [gopi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gopi) of the eight main gopis. She carries messages between Radha and Krishna and is the most expert Gopi messenger. Residents believe that an Andhra king built a temple to pay homage to his family deity Viśakha. This is now inundated under seawater near [RK Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RK_Beach). Another theory is that it is named after a woman disciple of Buddha named [Viśakha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakha). Later it was ruled by [Kingdom of Jeypore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Jeypore) (1535–1571), [Qutb Shahis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qutb_Shahi_dynasty) (1571–1674) and (1674–1711), [Mughal Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mughal_Empire) (1711–1724) and [Nizam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizam_of_Hyderabad) (1724–1757). The [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Indies_Company) (1757) and [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_Company) (1765) established Factories and influence over the city. In 1794 the [Battle of Padmanabham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Padmanabham) occurred between the [Zamindari of Vizianagaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vizianagaram_estate) and the East India Company.[^cite_note-44] after the battle, [Vizianagaram estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vizianagaram_estate) was acquired by the East India company in 1802.[^cite_note-45] On 18 September 1804 the [Battle of Visakhapatnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Visakhapatnam) was fought between the French and British and the city remained the under [British colonial rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj) until [Indian independence in 1947](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_independence_movement) which was a part of the [Northern Circars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Circars).

### Buddhist influence

Hindu texts state that during the fifth century BCE, the Visakhapatnam region was part of [Kalinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalinga_\(historical_region\)) territory, which extended to the [Godavari river](47988.md). Relics found in the area also prove the existence of a [Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist) empire in the region. Kalinga later lost the territory to [King Ashoka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashoka) in the bloodiest battle of its time, which prompted Ashoka to embrace [Buddhism](3267529.md). Ancient Buddhist sites, recently excavated, are scattered across Visakhapatnam, reflecting the region's Buddhist legacy.[^cite_note-46]

#### Pavurallakonda

eramic sculptures of guardian Yakshas

Yaksha Relief at [Pavurallakonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavurallakonda) near [Bheemunipatnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bheemunipatnam)

[Pavurallakonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavurallakonda) ("pigeon hill") is a [hillock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillock) west of Bhimli, about 24 km (15 mi) from Visakhapatnam. The Buddhist settlement found here is estimated to date back from the first century BCE to the second century CE. On the hillock (which overlooks the coastline) are 16 rock-cut [cisterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cisterns) for collecting rainwater. [Gopalapatnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gopalapatnam), on the Tandava River, is a village surrounded by brick stupas, [viharas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vihara), pottery and other Buddhist artefacts.[^cite_note-47]

#### Sankaram



Stone seated [Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddharupa) at [Bojjannakonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bojjannakonda) near [Anakapalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anakapalle)

In 1907 British archaeologist Alexander Rea unearthed Sankaram, a 2,000-year-old Buddhist site. The name "Śankaram" derives from the [Sangharama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangharama) (temple or monastery). Located 40 km (25 mi) south of Visakhapatnam, it is known locally as [Bojjannakonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bojjannakonda) and is a significant Buddhist site in Andhra Pradesh. The three major schools of Buddhism (Hinayana, Mahayana and Vajrayana) flourished here. The complex is known for its monolithic [stupas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stupa), rock-cut caves and brick structures. The primary stupa was initially carved out of rock and covered with bricks. Excavations yielded historic pottery and [Satavahana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satavahana_dynasty) coins from the first century CE. At Lingalakonda, there are also rock-cut monolithic stupas in rows spread over the hill.[^cite_note-\:2-48] The vihara, a monastery, was active for about 1,000 years.[^cite_note-\:2-48]

Nearby is another Buddhist site, Bojjannakonda, with several images of the Buddha carved on the rock face of the caves. At Ligalametta there are hundreds of rock-cut monolithic stupas in rows, spread across the hill. Among other Buddhist attractions are a relic casket, three [chaitya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaitya) halls, votive platforms, stupas and [Vajrayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajrayana) sculptures.[^cite_note-49]

#### Bavikonda

Bavikonda Buddhist Complex

[Bavikonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavikonda) stupas

Bavikonda is an important Buddhist heritage site located on a hill about 15 km (9.3 mi), northeast of Visakhapatnam city. Here the Buddhist habitation is noticed on a 16 ha flat terraced area. The Hinayana school of Buddhism was practised at the monastery between the 3rd century B.C. and the 3rd century A.D. Bavikonda has remains of an entire Buddhist complex, comprising 26 structures belonging to three phases. A piece of bone stored in an urn recovered here is believed to belong to the mortal remains of the [Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautama_Buddha). The word *Bavikonda* in [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language) means "a hill of wells". Fitting its name, Bavikonda is a hill with wells for the collection of rainwater. It is located 15 km (9.3 mi) from Visakhapatnam and is a significant Buddhist site.

Excavation carried out from 1982 to 1987 revealed a Buddhist establishment including a mahachaitya, also referred to as a grand stupa, embedded with relic caskets,[^cite_note-50] a large Vihara (monastery) complex, numerous votive stupas, a stone-pillared congregation and rectangular halls and a refectory. Artifacts recovered from the site include Roman and [Satavahana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satavahana_dynasty) coins and pottery dating from the third century BCE to the second century CE. A significant finding was a piece of bone (with a large quantity of ash) in an urn, which is believed to be the remains of the [Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddha). The Bavikonda site is considered one of the oldest Buddhist sites in Asia. It is a reminder of the Buddhist civilisation which once existed in southern India, and also reminiscent of [Borobudur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borobudur) in Indonesia.[^cite_note-51]

#### Thotlakonda

Corner of light-coloured, angular stone ruins

Third-century BCE Buddhist ruins of [Totlakonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thotlakonda)



Thotlakonda Buddhist Complex

About 16 km (9.9 mi) from Visakhapatnam is [Thotlakonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thotlakonda), a [Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist) complex situated on top of a hill. The Buddhist complex on the Mangamaripeta hilltop, locally known as [Totlakonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thotlakonda), lies about 16 km (9.9 mi) from Visakhapatnam on Visakhapatnam-Bheemili Beach Road. After its discovery (during an aerial survey), the Government of Andhra Pradesh declared the 48 ha site as a protected monument in 1978. Excavations from 1988 to 1992 exposed structural remains and artefacts, classified as Religious, Secular, and Civil. These structures include the Stupa, Chaityagrihas, pillared congregation halls, bhandagaras, refectory (bhojanasala), drainage, and stone pathways. The site covers an area of 120 acres (49 hectares), and has been declared a protected area by the government of Andhra Pradesh. Excavations have revealed three kinds of structural remains: religious, secular, and civil. Structures include a mahastupa, 16 votive [stupas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stupa), a stone-pillared congregation hall, eleven rock-cut cisterns, well-paved stone pathways, an [apsidal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apse) [chaitya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaitya)-griha, three round chaitgya-grihas, two votive platforms, ten [viharas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vihara) and a kitchen complex with three halls and a refectory (dining hall). Apart from the structures, Buddhist treasures excavated include nine [Satavahana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satavahana_dynasty) and five Roman silver coins, [terracotta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terracotta) tiles, stucco decorative pieces, sculptured panels, miniature [stupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stupa) models in stone, Buddha [padas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pada_\(foot\)) depicted with [ashtamangala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtamangala) symbols (i.e. the eight auspicious symbols of Swastika, Shrivasta, Nandhyavarta, Vardhamanaka, Bhadrasana, Kalasha, Minyugala and Darpan) and early pottery.[^cite_note-52]

### Later history



Mor Sjanua Regimental Cemetery near Jagadamba junction

The territory of Visakhapatnam then came under the Andhra rulers of [Vengi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vengi), and [Chalukyas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalukya_dynasty) and [Pallavas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pallava_dynasty) ruled the land. The region was ruled by the [Eastern Ganga king](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Ganga_dynasty) and the [Gajapati kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gajapati_Empire) from the 10th century to the 16th century CE. Based on archaeological evidence, the [Prabhakar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prabhakar) and the Eastern Ganga Kings built temples in the city in the 11th and 12th centuries. In the late 16th century, it came under the direct rule of the [Suryavanshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suryavanshi_clan) king, [Maharaja Vishwanath Dev Gajapati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishwanath_Dev_Gajapati) of [Jeypore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeypore). However, from 1571 to 1674 it fell under the control and administration of the [Qutb Shahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qutb_Shahi_dynasty) kings of [Golconda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golconda_Fort) who appointed a governor or [Faujdar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faujdar) to collect taxes in the region. This Faujdar governed the area from [Srikakulam](711152.md) or Chicacole as it was then known. In 1674, the Maharaja of Jeypore, Vishwambhar Dev defeated the Foujdar and claimed sovereignty from the deteriorating Qutb Shahis.[^cite_note-\:1-53] Vishwambhar is also said to have defeated the Dutch East India Company who allegedly abducted locals, mostly fishermen living by the sea-shore. Later his descendant, Maharaja Raghunath Krishna Dev defeated the Seer Lascer appointed by the Mughals and maintained his rule over the region. Therefore, Visakhapatnam remained a part of the [Kingdom of Jeypore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Jeypore) until the death of Maharaja Ram Chandra Dev I in 1711 after which it was taken over by the [Nizam of Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizam_of_Hyderabad) who could only govern it for a few decades before transferring it to the British.[^cite_note-54] Even in the colonial era, the kings of Jeypore were referred to as the Maharaja or Raja of Vizagapatnam.[^cite_note-55] European merchants from France, Holland and the [East India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_Company) used the natural port to export tobacco, [paddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_\(unmilled_rice\)), coal, iron ore, ivory, [muslin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslin) and other textile products. The British developed Visakhapatnam as a prominent harbour on the east coast. The old port in [Jalari Peta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalari_Peta) was built and used by the Maharaja of Jeypore who also owned several ships. During the [First World War](4764461.md), [Maharaja Vikram Dev III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikram_Dev_III) sent his fleet of ships to aid the British and later donated the port to the British government of Madras.[^cite_note-\:1-53] It is now a fish market.

Local legend tells that an [Andhra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_Andhra) king, on his way to [Benares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varanasi), rested at Visakhapatnam and was so enchanted by its beauty that he ordered a temple to be built in honour of his family deity, Viśakha. Archaeological sources, however, reveal that the temple was probably built between the 11th and 12th centuries by the Cholas. A shipping merchant, Sankarayya Chetty, built one of the mandapams (pillared halls) of the temple. Although it no longer exists (possibly washed away about 100 years ago by a cyclonic storm), elderly residents of Visakhapatnam remember visits to the ancient shrine by their grandparents (although author Ganapatiraju Atchuta Rama Raju denies this).[^cite_note-56]

During the 18th century Visakhapatnam was part of the [Northern Circars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Circars), a region comprising [coastal Andhra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_Andhra) and southern coastal Odisha which was first under French control and later British. Visakhapatnam became a district in the [Madras Presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_Presidency) of [British India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj). In September 1804, British and French squadrons fought the [Battle of Vizagapatam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Vizagapatam) near the harbour. After India's independence, it was the largest district in the country and was subsequently divided into the districts of [Srikakulam](711152.md), [Vizianagaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vizianagaram) and Visakhapatnam.

Part of the city is known by its colonial British name, Waltair; during the colonial era, the city's hub was the Waltair railway station and a part of the city is still called Waltair.

On 7 May 2020, the city suffered an industrial accident when a polymer plant leaked toxic [styrene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Styrene) gas, as it restarted operations after the [Coronavirus lockdown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_lockdown_in_India). This [gas leak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_gas_leak) killed at least eleven people, and drew comparisons to the [Bhopal disaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhopal_disaster).[^cite_note-57]

## Geography



[Kondakarla Ava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kondakarla_Ava), a bird sanctuary near Visakhapatnam

The city is situated between the [Eastern Ghats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Ghats) and the [Bay of Bengal](47892.md).[^cite_note-location-20][^cite_note-geo-58][^cite_note-climate-59] The city's area is 682 km<sup>2</sup>. The average elevation is 45 metres.[^cite_note-60] Visakhapatnam is situated in [Coastal Andhra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_Andhra) Region.[^cite_note-61]

The city is surrounded by the [Simhachalam Hill Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simhachalam_Hill_Range) to the west, the [Yarada Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarada_Hills) to the southeast, and [Kambalakonda Wildlife Sanctuary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kambalakonda_Wildlife_Sanctuary) to the northwest. The hills play an important role in Visakhapatnam's ecological environment and cover over 621.52  km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-62]

### Climate

The average temperature of visakhapatnam

The average temperature of visakhapatnam

Visakhapatnam has a [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Aw*, closely bordering *As*).[^cite_note-climate-59] The annual mean temperatures range between 24.7–30.6 °C (76–87 °F), with the maximum in May and the minimum in January; the minimum temperatures ranges between 17–27 °C (63–81 °F). The highest maximum temperature ever recorded was 42.0 °C (107.6 °F) in 1978, and the lowest was 10.5 °C (51 °F) on 6 January 1962.[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64] It receives rainfall from the [South-west](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_monsoon) and [North-east monsoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-east_monsoon)[^cite_note-climate-59] and the average annual rainfall recorded is 1,118.8 mm (44.05 in).[^cite_note-IMD-65] In October 2014 [Cyclone Hudhud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Hudhud) made landfall near Visakhapatnam.[^cite_note-66]

## Demographics

As of 2011 [census of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_of_India), Visakhapatnam had a population of 1,728,128, of which males were 873,599 and females were 854,529 – a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_sex_ratio) of 978 females per 1000 males. The population density was 18,480/km<sup>2</sup> (47,900/sq mi). There were 164,129 children in the age group of 0–6 years, with 84,298 boys and 79,831 girls – a sex ratio was 947 girls per 1000 boys. The average [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) stood at 81.79% with a total of 1,279,137 literates, of which 688,678 were males and 590,459 were females.[^cite_note-census-76][^cite_note-population-77] It is ranked 122 in the list of fastest-growing cities in the world.[^cite_note-78] The total slum population covers 44.61% of the total population which implies 770,971 people reside in slums.[^cite_note-census-2011-79]

The recent estimates of city's population are 2,358,412 in 2022.[^cite_note-80]

## Administration

Main article: [Administration of Visakhapatnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administration_of_Visakhapatnam)

[Map](238760.md)

[Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Visakhapatnam_Municipal_Corporation)



Collectorate Building, Visakhapatnam

[Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Visakhapatnam_Municipal_Corporation) (GVMC) is the civic body that oversees the civic needs of the city.[^cite_note-98] One of the earliest municipalities in this area, the Vizag (Visakhapatnam) Municipality was set up in 1858 to fulfill the basic infrastructural needs of the people. It was converted into a municipal corporation in 1979. Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation came into existence on 21 November 2005 after the release of G.O by Govt of Andhra Pradesh.[^cite_note-gvmc-99]

As per the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, the Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation reported a revenue of ₹1,031 crore (US\$124 million) and an expenditure of ₹867 crore (US\$104 million) in 2022–23. Taxes contributed to 40.8% of the revenue, with 59.2% of the income coming from other sources.[^cite_note-100]

It has a jurisdictional area of 681.96 km<sup>2</sup> (263.31 sq mi), which includes the merged municipalities of [Gajuwaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gajuwaka), [Anakapalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anakapalle) and [Bheemunipatnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bheemunipatnam).[^cite_note-civicbody-101][^cite_note-thehinducivic-102] Although as per the 2011 census, it is 513.61 km<sup>2</sup> (198.31 sq mi) mentioned in the district town amenities handbook of Andhra Pradesh, portraying a rise in area covered by the municipal corporation in these years[^cite_note-103]

The municipal corporation of Vishakapatnam is governed by three acts. First is the State Municipal Act, the Andhra Pradesh Municipalities Act 1965, the Andhra Pradesh Municipal Corporations 1994 Act, as well as an act specific to the Vishakapatnam Municipal Corporation Act 1979.[^cite_note-104][^cite_note-105]

The city is divided into 9 zones, In a recent agglomeration of five Bheemili villages with a population of 19,000 into the GVMC jurisdiction the number of wards has increased to 98.[^cite_note-106]

### Municipal elections and civic government officials

The previous Municipal Elections were held in 2007. After a larger gap of 13 years, the elections were scheduled to happen on 23 March 2020.[^cite_note-The_Economic_Times-107] The results of the latest municipal elections for 145 municipalities and 10 municipal corporations across Andhra Pradesh were held on 30 March and the results were announced on Monday. Of the 92 municipalities in Seemandhra, TDP won 65 municipalities and YSR Congress 19. Of the seven municipal corporations, TDP bagged five and YSR Congress two.[^cite_note-The_Economic_Times-107]

The City governance structure of GVMC consists of an elected wing headed by the Mayor an administrative wing headed by the Administrative Commissioner, four Additional commissioners, and eight zonal commissioners. Mayor elections were planned to be carried out in March 2020 but details are yet to be declared.[^cite_note-The_Economic_Times-107] The present municipal commissioner of the city is G Srijana. The municipal commissioner looks after the administration of the municipal corporation.[^cite_note-108]

The executive wing has departments for Engineering, Public health, revenue, town planning, horticulture, education, finance, general administration, projects, IT E-governance and Urban Community Development.[^cite_note-109]

The organisational structure of the GVMC includes standing committees, ward committees and special committees, The corporation wards committees must be of not less than ten wards, special committees are appointed by the corporation out of their own body, and a standing committee consisting of chairpersons from all wards.[^cite_note-110]

### Municipal budget

The GVMC estimated a budget of [₹](490783.md)4,171 [crore](121420.md) (equivalent to ₹54 billion or US\$560 million in 2026) for the financial year 2020–21. It shows an increase of ₹337 crore (equivalent to ₹457 crore or US\$47 million in 2026) compared to the last budget for 2019–20.[^cite_note-111] The Revenue Department of GVMC generates its revenue by levying of Property Tax, Vacant Land Tax and it is the collection and dealing with Remunerative Enterprises, Water Charges, etc.[^cite_note-112]

### Masterplan

In 2019, the Planning wing of the [Visakhapatnam Metropolitan Region Development Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_Metropolitan_Region_Development_Authority) (VMRDA) started preparing a 'perspective plan 2051' which is a 30-year strategic plan, apart from another 'master plan 2041'. The 'perspective plan' is expected to include provisions for resource conservation, regional growth, economic growth and transportation strategies, coastal zone regulations, disaster management strategies, population forecast and distribution, a broad structure plan, and an implementation plan.[^cite_note-\:0-113]

The 'master plan' proposes a growth strategy with transit-oriented development strategies, expansion of satellite towns such as Vizianagaram, Anakapalli, Bheemili, etc., theme-based economic nodes along the proposed metro-rail corridor, bus-based public transport, tourism destination development, a comprehensive rural agenda and resilient city planning.[^cite_note-\:0-113]

Yet another proposal includes a transit corridor connecting Bhogapuram Airport with the existing business centres of the city and the industrial clusters in Atchutapuram under the Visakhapatnam-Chennai Industrial Corridor.[^cite_note-\:0-113]

### District Administration

The district area is 11,161 km<sup>2</sup> (4,309 sq mi). The Sub-Division is divided into Mandals. There are four revenue divisions in the district.[^cite_note-115] A Revenue division is headed by Revenue Divisional Officer in the rank  of Sub–Collector in the cadre of [I.A.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Administrative_Service) or a Deputy Collector. He is the Sub Divisional Magistrate having jurisdiction over his division.[^cite_note-116]

## Civic utilities

The [Andhra Pradesh Eastern Power Distribution Company Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Pradesh_Eastern_Power_Distribution_Company_Limited) (APEPDCL) supplies power to the city of Visakhapatnam.[^cite_note-126] The fire services in the city is carried out by the state fire department, the Andhra Pradesh State Disaster Response and Fire Department, Eastern region (AP Fire).[^cite_note-127]

The city water supply is essentially stored in the three service reservoirs – Town Service Reservoir, Dwarakanagar; High-Level Service Reservoir, T.B. Road, Uplands and Circuit House Reservoir, [Waltair Uplands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waltair_Uplands). The city is divided into 14 blocks according to contours and each block is served by separate reservoirs. 35 reservoirs are serving the different segments of the system. Domestic water supply is mainly through public taps and house service connections.[^cite_note-128]

There are about 2072 bore wells as groundwater sources. As per a report from 2015, "GVMC relies mostly on surface water for its raw water supply and serves 54.9 per cent of the city through household-level piped connections. There is a supply gap of almost 66 million litres per day (MLD)[^cite_note-129]

As of 2019, only 50 per cent of the city has access to underground drainage. This is after the city grew over the last decade and [Bheemili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bheemunipatnam) and [Anakapalli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anakapalli) among other peripheral villages came under the GVMC's jurisdiction. The GVMC has taken up a project to extend the underground drainage network with a budget of ₹10 crore (equivalent to ₹14 crore or US\$1.4 million in 2026). The new project will provide underground drainage access to an estimated 8,000 households. The network will be built under the command area of pumping stations at [Venkata Puram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venkatapuram,_Prakasam_district) and Vimannagar and its vicinity.[^cite_note-Nov_13-130]

In the year 2015, the municipal corporation generated 920 tons of waste per day. Waste generated from all the wards has been disposed of at the dumpsite in [Kapuluppada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapuluppada) since 2007.[^cite_note-131]

Chandrababu Naidu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N._Chandrababu_Naidu) on 15 February 2019 and [Millennium IT Towers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennium_IT_Towers) 2 is in pipeline to promote fintech investments in the city.[^cite_note-142]

There are many national and multi-national IT/ITes and fintech firms such as [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM), [Wipro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wipro), [Tech Mahindra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tech_Mahindra), [Kenexa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenexa), [Fluentgrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluentgrid), [Conduent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conduent), [Cyient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyient), [Paytm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paytm), [Concentrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concentrix), [Sutherland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutherland), [HSBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HSBC), etc. Some more investments are in line, like [Google X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_X), Lalith Ahuja's [ANSR Consulting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANSR_Consulting), [Franklin Templeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Templeton_Investments), Innova Solutions, etc. at [Fintech Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fintech_Valley_Vizag) in the city.[^cite_note-143][^cite_note-144]

## Culture

Main article: [Culture of Visakhapatnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Visakhapatnam)



A villager with a decorated bull during Pongal festival

The city embraces rich religious diversity, with Hinduism being the majority religion, alongside Islam, Christianity, and Buddhism. Telugu is the predominantly spoken language, reflecting the city's cosmopolitan culture with a significant presence of Odia, Hindi, Tamil, and Malayalam speakers. Visakhapatnam offers a blend of South Indian and Western cuisines, with popular dishes like Moori Mixture and bamboo chicken of [Araku Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Araku_Valley). The city celebrates various festivals including Makar Sankranti, Ugadi, Vinayaka Chavithi, Diwali, and Dasara. Cricket is the most favoured sport, with facilities like the [Dr. Y. S. Rajasekhara Reddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y._S._Rajasekhara_Reddy) ACA–VDCA Cricket Stadium. Additionally, the city is home to attractions like the Telugu Samskruthika Niketanam Museum, Submarine Museum, TUV, and Harrier aircraft museums.

## Transport

Main article: [Transport in Visakhapatnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_in_Visakhapatnam)



Visakhapatnam Metro Express Highway Service at Kurmannapalem



[Visakhapatnam Junction railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_Junction_railway_station)

The city commuters prefer city buses and [auto rickshaw](593765.md) as the primary mode of transport, followed by two-wheelers and cars.[^cite_note-transport-168] Road and rail are preferred for long-distance commuting and are supported by [Dwaraka bus station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwaraka_bus_station) and [Visakhapatnam Junction railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_Junction_railway_station) respectively.[^cite_note-169][^cite_note-indiatimes1-170] It also has sea and air travel infrastructure such as [Visakhapatnam Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_Port)[^cite_note-171] and [Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alluri_Sitarama_Raju_International_Airport).[^cite_note-172]

The [APSRTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Pradesh_State_Road_Transport_Corporation) operates city, district, and inter-state bus services from Dwaraka bus station.[^cite_note-173] Over 600 city buses operate over 150 routes, in addition to [Bus Rapid Transit System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_Bus_Rapid_Transit_System) in two corridors of Pendurthi and Simhachalam.[^cite_note-transport-168]<sup>: 21 </sup> A planned *Integrated Bus Terminal Complex* would be built at Maddilapalem.[^cite_note-174] Apart from buses, there are about 25,000 auto rickshaws plying on the city roads which provide [intermediate public transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermediate_public_transport).[^cite_note-transport-168]<sup>: 22 </sup>

Visakhapatnam is the headquarters of the [South Coast Railway zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Coast_Railway_zone) of Indian railways. [Visakhapatnam railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_railway_station) is as an *A1* station[^cite_note-175] with the highest gross revenue in the [Visakhapatnam railway division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_railway_division).[^cite_note-176] It serves an average of 20,000–25,000 passengers daily and may rise to 40,000 during festivals.[^cite_note-indiatimes1-170] It has the country's largest diesel locomotive shed with a capacity of 206.[^cite_note-177] Visakhapatnam Metro is a planned metro rail project.[^cite_note-indiatimes-178]



Visakhapatnam Airport Terminal

As of 2013, the percentage of transport mode shares in the city are, 18% buses, 9% autos, 15% two-wheelers, 2% cars and 55% non-motorised transport (bicycles and pedestrians).[^cite_note-transport-168]<sup>: 23 </sup> The total road network accounts for a total length of 2,007.10 km (1,247.15 mi).[^cite_note-179] [NH16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_16_\(India\)), a major highway and a part of the [Golden Quadrilateral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Quadrilateral) system bypasses the city.

[Visakhapatnam Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alluri_Sitarama_Raju_International_Airport) had served a total of 2,815,205 passengers in 2018, an increase of 16.8% from the previous year. It handled a total of 23,264 aircraft during that year.

[Visakhapatnam Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam_Port) is one of 13 major [ports in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ports_in_India) and the only major port of Andhra Pradesh. It is India's second-largest port by volume of cargo handled. It is located on the east coast of India and is located midway between the [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai_Port) and [Kolkata Ports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Kolkata). Cruise shipping is operational between Visakhapatnam and [Andaman and Nicobar islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaman_and_Nicobar_Islands).[^cite_note-180][^cite_note-181]

The [GITAM University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandhi_Institute_of_Technology_and_Management) and the [Gayatri Vidya Parishad College of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gayatri_Vidya_Parishad_College_of_Engineering) are other technical education institutions in the city. Visakhapatnam is also home to [Damodaram Sanjivayya National Law University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damodaram_Sanjivayya_National_Law_University) (DSNLU), which is the [National Law University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Law_Universities) for the state of Andhra Pradesh. DSNLU takes entrance through [Common Law Admission Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Law_Admission_Test) and ranks 15th by order of establishment among the 17 National Law Universities. The city is due to get India's first packaging park[^cite_note-192] with an Indian Institute of Packaging, IIP[^cite_note-193] and BITS Pilani & Birla International School under the aegis of Sarala Birla Academy.[^cite_note-194]

The [Indian Maritime University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Maritime_University) (IMU) was established as a central university by the government of India by an act of Parliament (the Indian Maritime University Act 2008). IMU is poised to play a role in the development of human resources for the maritime sector. The city also has the [National Institute of Oceanography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Oceanography,_India). The [Indian Institute of Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institutes_of_Management),[^cite_note-195] [Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Petroleum_and_Energy)[^cite_note-196] are the other institutions of national importance.

## Defence and research

### Naval base

INS Vikrant in Vizag Port

[INS Vikrant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Vikrant_\(2013\)) makes a grand debut at the scenic port of Visakhapatnam for MILAN 2024

Visakhapatnam is the headquarters of the [Eastern Naval Command](30139022.md), the [Naval Science and Technological Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Science_and_Technological_Laboratory) (a [DRDO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Research_and_Development_Organisation) Lab), a Chief Quality Assurance Establishment (CQAE), an EFS office, a naval dockyard (established in 1949) and naval bases including [INS Virbahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Virbahu), [INS Karna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Karna), [INS Kalinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Kalinga), INS Samudrika, [INS Satavahana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Satavahana), and [INS Dega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Dega). A new base at [INS Rambilli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Varsha) is being built on 5,000 acres (20 km<sup>2</sup>) with an investment of ₹15 billion (equivalent to ₹28 billion or US\$290 million in 2026), as the first dedicated nuclear submarine base in India.[^cite_note-toi26m-197][^cite_note-thu17s-198] India's first nuclear submarine, the [INS Arihant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INS_Arihant) was launched in the Naval Dockyard, and [Bharat Dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharat_Dynamics_Limited) has begun manufacturing torpedoes.[^cite_note-199] The city also has the presence of the [Indian Coast Guard](786383.md), including ships and offices. Multiple naval training establishments, such as the Navy ShipWright School,[^cite_note-200] are also situated here.

### Research organisations

The [Bhabha Atomic Research Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhabha_Atomic_Research_Centre) (BARC) has its second research facility in the country (after Trombay) in Atchutapuram mandal in the district.[^cite_note-201][^cite_note-202] There are also offices of the [National Institute of Oceanography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Oceanography,_India) and the [India Meteorological Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India_Meteorological_Department).

## Sports

major international stadium for cricket

Dr. Y. S. Rajasekhara Reddy International Cricket Stadium at [PM Palem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pothinamallayya_Palem)



ENC Cricket Team which won the VDCA Institutional League Cricket Championship 2015–16

[Cricket](25675557.md) is the most popular sport, followed by [tennis](29773.md) and [football](10568.md). Visakhapatnam is home to several local cricket teams participating in district and zonal matches. Gully cricket or [backyard cricket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backyard_cricket) (a form of cricket played in streets or parks) is a popular sport among local youth. Visakhapatnam co-hosted the 32nd [National Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Games_of_India) alongside [Hyderabad](37534.md) in 2002. The city has seven cricket stadiums, which are used for [Ranji Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranji_Trophy) matches; two of these stadiums have been used for [one day international](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Day_International) (ODI) matches.[^cite_note-203] [Indira Priyadarshini Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indira_Priyadarshini_Stadium), also known as the Municipal Corporation Stadium, hosted the first ODI match on 9 December 1988 and the last ODI on 3 April 2001. The stadium was discontinued in favour of the new [Dr. Y. S. Rajasekhara Reddy International Cricket Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Y._S._Rajasekhara_Reddy_ACA%E2%80%93VDCA_Cricket_Stadium), [PM Palem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pothinamallayya_Palem).[^cite_note-204]

Dr. Y. S. Rajasekhara Reddy International Cricket Stadium is the home of [Andhra Cricket Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Cricket_Association). It regularly hosts [Ranji Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranji_Trophy), One Day Internationals, and [Test Internationals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_cricket). The stadium is the home ground of [Andhra cricket team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_cricket_team). The stadium also hosted [IPL](13260340.md) matches as a neutral venue. It hosted its first test match against England beginning on 17 November 2016.[^cite_note-205][^cite_note-206]

Port Trust Golden Jubilee Stadium is the second largest stadium in Visakhapatnam, which has hosted Under-19 Youth Internationals. It also hosted the [2014 Pro Kabaddi League season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Pro_Kabaddi_League_season) as the home ground for the [Telugu Titans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_Titans).[^cite_note-207][Swarna Bharathi Indoor Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarna_Bharathi_Indoor_Stadium), built by the Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation, is used for various indoor sports,[^cite_note-208] and the GVMC Aqua Sports Complex, an aquatic centre for swimming and diving, is near the beach road.[^cite_note-209]

[Surfing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surfing) activities are common at the Rushikonda beach.[^cite_note-210] [Scuba diving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuba_diving) at [Chintapalli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chintapalle,_Visakhapatnam_district) in the scenic city has been attracting tourists from all over.[^cite_note-211] There is also a golf course named [East Point Golf Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Point_Golf_Club) in the city.
