**Chicama** is a town in Northern [Peru](170691.md), capital of the district of [Chicama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicama_District) of [Ascope Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascope_Province) in the region [La Libertad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Libertad_Region). This town is located beside the [Pan-American Highway](301108.md) some 33 km north of [Trujillo](476649.md) city in the agricultural [Chicama Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicama_Valley).
