The **province of Fermo** ([Italian](14708.md): *provincia di Fermo*) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Italy) in the region of [Marche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marche) in [Italy](14532.md). It was established in 2004 and became operational in 2009. Its capital is the town of [Fermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermo).

It has a population of 166,772 in an area of 862.77 square kilometres (333.12 sq mi) across its 40 municipalities.[^cite_note-population-2][^cite_note-area-1]

## History

An 1861 report by Minister Minghetti to Prince Eugene of Savoy, Lieutenant of the King,[^cite_note-stamperia-4] justified merging the small and fragmented provinces of southern Marche into a single large province, a move to overcome the historical border between the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies and the Papal States. The residents of Abruzzo and Cardinal [Filippo de Angelis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filippo_de_Angelis) were opposed to this. Despite this, 58% of the population of Fermo voted in favour of merging some smaller provinces, and the province of Ascoli-Fermo was created and became known as Ascoli Piceno.[^cite_note-5]

In 2000, supporters of forming a new province of Fermo exploited a pact between the [Lega Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lega_Nord) and [Forza Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forza_Italia_\(1994\)) who had formed a number of proposals regarding establishing provincial bodies, including one that later led to the foundation of the [province of Monza and Brianza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Monza_and_Brianza). [Fabrizio Cesetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabrizio_Cesetti) was the only signatory of the act forming the province,[^cite_note-6] which was delayed due to the conclusion of the [Legislature XIII of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature_XIII_of_Italy). Following this, Law 147/2004 was passed, and the province of Fermo was established in 2004.[^cite_note-7]

## Geography

The Province of Fermo is in the [Marche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marche) region of central Italy, and stretches from the [Sibillini Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sibillini_Mountains) to the [Adriatic Sea](23275478.md). Its main geographic feature is the valley of the [River Tenna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenna_\(river\)). This river rises in the mountains, enters the province near [Servigliano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Servigliano), passes through [Grottazzolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grottazzolina) and [Montegiorgio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montegiorgio), and reaches the sea near [Sant'Elpidio a Mare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant'Elpidio_a_Mare) and [Porto Sant'Elpidio](5543394.md). The [River Aso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aso_\(river\)) forms the southern border of the province, separating it from the [Province of Ascoli Piceno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Ascoli_Piceno). It rises in the mountains and flows into the Adriatic Sea at [Pedaso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedaso). To the north of the province lies the [Province of Macerata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Macerata).[^cite_note-8] Footwear and leather goods are produced in the area, and the main agricultural products are cereals, vegetables, grapes, olives and livestock.[^cite_note-9]

The town of [Fermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermo), the capital of the province, is an old town perched on a hill. It has a historic centre, a large piazza and a [cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermo_Cathedral) dating from 1227 with a Gothic facade. There are also traces of a Roman amphitheatre nearby. The neighbouring municipality of [Porto San Giorgio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto_San_Giorgio) has a castle dating from 1269, which protected the valley that leads to Fermo.[^cite_note-10]

## Demographics

As of 2026, the population is 166,772, of which 49.2% are male, and 50.8% are female. Minors make up 13.5% of the population, and seniors make up 27.7%.[^cite_note-population-2]

### Immigration

As of 2025, immigrants make up 13.1% of the population. The five largest foreign countries of birth are [Romania](25445.md), [China](5405.md), [Albania](738.md), [Morocco](19291.md), and [Pakistan](23235.md).[^cite_note-foreignborn-13]
