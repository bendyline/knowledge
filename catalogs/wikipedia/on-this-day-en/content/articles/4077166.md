# List of cities in Ukraine



Map of Ukraine showing its largest cities

There are 463 [populated places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Populated_places_in_Ukraine) in [Ukraine](31750.md) that have been officially granted [city status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_status_in_Ukraine) ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): місто, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *misto*) by the [Verkhovna Rada](844993.md), the country's parliament, as of 23 April 2025.[^cite_note-UKR_2022_estimates-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Settlements with more than 10,000 people are eligible for city status although the status is typically also granted to settlements of historical or regional importance.[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

Smaller settlements are [rural settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural_settlement_\(Ukraine\)) ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): селище, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *selyshche*) and [villages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_\(Ukraine\)) ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): село, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *selo*).

Historically, there were systems of city rights, granted by the territorial lords, which defined the status of a place as a *misto* or *selo*. In the past, cities were self-governing and had several privileges.

The list of cities is roughly ordered by population and the 2022 estimates are compared to the [2001 Ukrainian census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Ukrainian_census), except for [Chernobyl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernobyl) for which the population is an unofficial estimate. The [cities with special status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_with_special_status) are shown in *italics*. The average population size is 62,000.
