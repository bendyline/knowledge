The ***Journal of Peace Research*** is a bimonthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](324572.md) that publishes scholarly articles and [book reviews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_review) in the fields of [peace and conflict studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_and_conflict_studies), [conflict resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conflict_resolution), and [international security](1946540.md). It was established by [Johan Galtung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johan_Galtung) in 1964 and emerged as a leading journal in the field of [peace and conflict studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_and_conflict_studies) and [International Relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_relations) under the editorship of [Nils Petter Gleditsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nils_Petter_Gleditsch) (1976-1977, 1983-2010). The current [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) are Gudrun Østby and Sebastian Schutte (all are/were researchers at the [Peace Research Institute Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_Research_Institute_Oslo)).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## Abstracting and indexing

*Journal of Peace Research* is abstracted and indexed in [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus) and the [Social Sciences Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Sciences_Citation_Index). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, its 2022 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) is 3.6, ranking it 15th out of 160 journals in the category "International Relations".[^cite_note-WoS-3]
