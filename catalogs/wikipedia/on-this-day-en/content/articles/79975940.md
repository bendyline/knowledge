**Humain** is a Saudi [artificial intelligence](1164.md) company established under the [Public Investment Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Investment_Fund) to drive the kingdom's AI strategy.&#x20;

## History

In May 2025, Humain was launched by [Mohammed bin Salman](38689358.md), [Crown Prince of Saudi Arabia](40373823.md). It aims to position [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) as a global leader in AI innovation and infrastructure. Its founding was announced during an investment forum in [Riyadh](51554.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] That year, Humain secured partnerships with American technology companies [Nvidia](39120.md), [AMD](2400.md), and [Qualcomm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualcomm).[^cite_note-\:0-4]

Humain has stated plans to develop several gigawatts of capacity.[^cite_note-5] Humain's flagship model is ALLAM, an Arabic-first multimodal large language model. The ALLaM 34B version was reportedly trained on more than 500 billion Arabic tokens.[^cite_note-ArabNews-6] Consumer and enterprise products include Humain Chat, a free Arabic-first chat application for iOS, Android, and web powered by ALLaM.[^cite_note-ArabNews-6]
