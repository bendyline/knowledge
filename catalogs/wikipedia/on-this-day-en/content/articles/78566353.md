A [United Nations Secretary-General selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Secretary-General_selection) is currently being held to choose the next [Secretary-General](162415.md) of the [United Nations](31769.md).[^cite_note-unsgwebsite-1] The current Secretary-General, [António Guterres](471806.md) of [Portugal](23033.md), will complete his second term on 31 December 2026.[^cite_note-2]

## Campaign

The selection process is governed by Article 97 of the [Charter of the United Nations](88805.md) and by [United Nations General Assembly](31957.md) resolutions, including resolution 79/327 of 5 September 2025.[^cite_note-3] The process formally began on 25 November 2025 with a letter from the [President of the General Assembly](780080.md), [Annalena Baerbock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annalena_Baerbock), and the [President of the Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_the_United_Nations_Security_Council), [Michael Imran Kanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Imran_Kanu?action=edit\&redlink=1), inviting [Member States](31969.md) to nominate candidates.[^cite_note-4] Nominations are announced jointly by the General Assembly and the [Security Council](31956.md).[^cite_note-unsgwebsite-1]

Interactive dialogues between the nominated candidates and Member States were held in April and June 2026 and were broadcast live on [UN Web TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Web_TV). A [town hall meeting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_hall_meeting) with six of the official candidates, hosted by [Bloomberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_Television), took place on 23 July and was also broadcast online.[^cite_note-unsgwebsite-1][^cite_note-5] The [fifteen members of the Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_members_of_the_United_Nations_Security_Council) began considering the candidates in July, and are expected to submit a recommendation to the General Assembly later in the year.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] They held the first of several closed-door, anonymous "straw poll" votes on their preferences on 30 July,[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] followed by further polls on 21 August and 18 and 29 September.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-1for8billionpolls-11][^cite_note-12]

Under an unwritten customary rule whereby the office of Secretary-General rotates among the organization's [five regional groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Regional_Groups), a candidate from the [Latin American and Caribbean Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_American_and_Caribbean_Group) (GRULAC) is strongly favoured.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

- [Colombe Cahen-Salvador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombe_Cahen-Salvador?action=edit\&redlink=1), a French activist, co-founder of the Atlas movement. Cahen-Salvador announced her intention to stand in March 2024.

## Security Council straw polls

The [Security Council](31956.md) is holding a series of straw polls, in which its members[^cite_note-55] are asked to indicate whether they "encourage", "discourage" or have "no opinion" regarding the candidates. During the first rounds of the polls, all ballots were identical; in future rounds, ballots from the [five permanent members](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permanent_members_of_the_United_Nations_Security_Council) will be marked to identify potential vetoes.[^cite_note-firstround-56]
