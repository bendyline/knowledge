**Suwon Sports Complex** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 수원종합운동장; [Hanja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanja): 水原総合運動場振) is a group of sports facilities in [Suwon](502693.md), South Korea. The complex consists of the Suwon Stadium, [Suwon Baseball Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwon_Baseball_Stadium), and [Suwon Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwon_Gymnasium).

## Facilities

### Suwon Stadium

Suwon Stadium is a [multi-purpose stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-purpose_stadium) and currently used mostly for [football](10568.md) matches. Built in 1971, it has a capacity of 11,808 seats and was the home ground of the [Suwon Samsung Bluewings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwon_Samsung_Bluewings) until 2001 when they moved to the [Suwon World Cup Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwon_World_Cup_Stadium).[^cite_note-Bluewings_1-1][^cite_note-Bluewings_2-2] It is currently home to [K League 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K_League_2) side [Suwon FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwon_FC).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

### Suwon Baseball Stadium

- *For details, see [Suwon KT Wiz Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwon_KT_Wiz_Park).*

### Suwon Gymnasium

- *For details, see [Suwon Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwon_Gymnasium).*

The gymnasium, with a capacity of 5,145, was built in 1963 and hosted the [handball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_handball) events of the [1988 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-Olympics-5]
