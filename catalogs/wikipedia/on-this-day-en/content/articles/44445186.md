Further information: [2015 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Australian_Open)

[Serena Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serena_Williams) defeated [Maria Sharapova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Sharapova) in the final, 6–3, 7–6<sup>(7–5)</sup> to win the women's singles tennis title at the [2015 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Australian_Open). It was her sixth [Australian Open](97851.md) singles title and her 19th [major](197638.md#Tournaments) singles title overall.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] Sharapova reached the final after saving two match points in the second round against [Alexandra Panova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandra_Panova).[^cite_note-3] The final was a rematch of the [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) final.

[Li Na](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Na) was the reigning champion, but she retired from professional tennis in September 2014.

All of the top four seeds (Williams, Sharapova, [Simona Halep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simona_Halep), and [Petra Kvitová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra_Kvitov%C3%A1)) were in contention for the [world No. 1 ranking](57768.md#WTA_rankings). Williams retained the top position by reaching the final.
