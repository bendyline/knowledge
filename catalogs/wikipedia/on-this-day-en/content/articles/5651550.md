**Narmak** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): نارمک) is a neighbourhood in north-east [Tehran](57654.md), the capital of [Iran](14653.md). It was designed by French consultants in the 1950s as part of a [modernization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modernization) effort introduced by [Reza Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reza_Shah). The name means "new city" in Persian.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]



Damage to a residential building in Narmak following an [Israeli airstrike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War), June 2025

Located in the 4th and 8th regions, it has 100 little squares named by numbers like "Meidan 68" (68th Square). Some of these squares, such as [Haft Hoz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HaftHoz) and Resalat, are large squares. The largest square in Narmak is the 73rd square and the smallest is the 99th. Some famous streets like Ayat, Hengam, Farjam, [Dardasht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dardasht_\(Tehran\)), Golbarg, Samangan, Saani, and the [Resalat Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resalat_Expressway) are located in this neighbourhood.

The [Iran University of Science and Technology](2073099.md) is based in Narmak. There are four [metro stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran_Metro) in Narmak named [Elm-o-Sanat University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elm-o-Sanat_University_Metro_Station), [Sarsabz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarsabz_Metro_Station), [Golbarg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golbarg_Metro_Station) and [Fadak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fadak_Metro_Station). Narmak borders Shemiran-no to the north, [Tehranpars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehranpars) to the east, [Shamsabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamsabad,_Tehran) to the west, and [Vahidiyeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vahidiyeh) and [Tehranno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehranno) to the south.
