# Bishop of Aberdeen

This article is about the bishop of the Scottish church. For the bishop of the Scottish Episcopal Church, see [Bishop of Aberdeen and Orkney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_of_Aberdeen_and_Orkney).

The **Bishop of Aberdeen** (originally **Bishop of Mortlach**, in Latin **Murthlacum**) was the ecclesiastical head of the [Diocese of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Aberdeen), one of Scotland's 13 [medieval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medieval) bishoprics, whose first recorded bishop is an early 12th-century cleric named [Nechtan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nechtan_of_Aberdeen). It appears that the episcopal seat had previously been at [Mortlach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortlach,_Banffshire) (Mòrthlach), but was moved to [Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen) during the reign of King [David I of Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_I_of_Scotland). The names of three bishops of Mortlach are known, the latter two of whom, "Donercius" and "Cormauch" (Cormac), by name only. The Bishop of Aberdeen broke communion with the [Catholic Church](606848.md) after the [Scottish Reformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Reformation). Following the [Revolution of 1688](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glorious_Revolution), the office was abolished in the [Church of Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Scotland), but continued in the [Scottish Episcopal Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Episcopal_Church). A [Roman Catholic Diocese of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Aberdeen) was recreated in 1878.

(Any dates appearing in italics indicate *de facto&#x20;*&#x20;continuation of office. The start date of tenure below is the date of appointment or succession. Where known, the date of installation and ordination as bishop are listed in the notes together with the post held prior to appointment.)

The modern Bishop of Aberdeen is the [Ordinary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_\(Catholic_Church\)) of the [Roman Catholic Diocese of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Aberdeen) in the [Province of Saint Andrews and Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Saint_Andrews_and_Edinburgh). The diocese covers 29,068 km<sup>2</sup>. The [see](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Episcopal_see) is in the [City of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen) where the seat is located at the [Cathedral Church of Saint Mary of the Assumption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Mary's_Cathedral,_Aberdeen). The [Apostolic Vicariate of the Northern District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Vicariate_of_the_Northern_District_\(Scotland\)) (formerly the Apostolic Vicariate of the Highland District) was elevated to diocese status on 4 March 1878. The current bishop is the Right Reverend **[Hugh Gilbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Gilbert_\(bishop\))**, 11th Bishop of Aberdeen.
