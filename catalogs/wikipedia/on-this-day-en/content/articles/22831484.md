**Tolikara Regency** is one of the [regencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regencies_of_Indonesia) (*kabupaten*) in the [Indonesian](14579.md) province of [Highland Papua](71191966.md). It covers an area of 14,564 km<sup>2</sup>, and had a population of 114,427 at the 2010 Census[^cite_note-Biro_Pusat_Statistik_2011-2] and 239,543 at the 2020 Census;[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] the official estimate as at mid 2025 was 248,310.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2023-1] The administrative centre of Tolikara Regency is the town of [Karubaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karubaga?action=edit\&redlink=1).

## Administrative districts

Tolikara Regency in 2010 comprised 35 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Indonesia) (*distrik*), but the number of districts was increased (by 2018) to 46, subdivided into 545 administrative villages. ## History

The VI [Asian-Pacific Astronomy Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Astronomy_Olympiad) took place in Tolikara in November–December 2010.[^cite_note-4]

The regency was the scene of a riot against Muslims during Eid prayer in 2015.

## Climate

Karubaga has a cool [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) (Af) with heavy rainfall year-round.
