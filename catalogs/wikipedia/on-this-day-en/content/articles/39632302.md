**Onikişubat** is a municipality and [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Turkey) of [Kahramanmaraş Province](727324.md), Turkey.[^cite_note-2] Its area is 2,429 km<sup>2</sup>,[^cite_note-3] and its population is 453,730 (2022).[^cite_note-tuik-1] The district Onikişubat was created at the [2013 reorganisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Turkish_local_government_reorganisation) from part of the former central district of Kahramanmaraş, along with the new district [Dulkadiroğlu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulkadiro%C4%9Flu).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] It covers the western part of the agglomeration of [Kahramanmaraş](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kahramanmara%C5%9F) and the adjacent countryside. The name Kahramanmaraş is reserved for the metropolitan municipality.

"Onikişubat" means "12 February"; it refers to the date of recovery of Kahramanmaraş during the [Turkish War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_War_of_Independence) in 1920.

## History

It was created as a result of the division of the central district of Kahramanmaraş into two by the law number 6360 adopted by the [Grand National Assembly of Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_National_Assembly_of_Turkey) on 12 November 2012.[^cite_note-6]

### 2026 shooting

This section is an excerpt from [2026 Onikişubat school shooting](82958189.md).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=2026_Oniki%C5%9Fubat_school_shooting\&action=edit)]

On 15 April 2026, a [school shooting](59628.md) occurred at the Ayser Çalık Secondary School in the Onikişubat district of [Kahramanmaraş Province](727324.md), Turkey. İsa Aras Mersinli, a 13-year-old[^cite_note-8] student enrolled at the school, shot and killed 12 people and injured 13 more. Mersinli died from [hypovolemic shock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypovolemic_shock) after sustaining a fatal knife injury while attempting to flee the scene.

The shooting occurred the day after [a school shooting](82950772.md) in the [Siverek](4116477.md) district of [Şanlıurfa Province](1353138.md).[^cite_note-2026_Onikişubat_school_shooting_Reuters-9] It is the deadliest school shooting in the history of Turkey.[^cite_note-2026_Onikişubat_school_shooting_\:0-10]
