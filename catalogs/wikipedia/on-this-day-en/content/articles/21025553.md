**Garacad**, also known as **Gara'ad**,[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] is a historic coastal town in the north east central [Mudug](185133.md) region of [Puntland](233569.md), an autonomous state of [Somalia](27358.md). There is currently work to build a new port in the harbour of Garacad.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
