The **Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Міністерство внутрішніх справ України, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *Ministerstvo vnutrishnikh sprav Ukrainy*; pronounced [\[min⁽ʲ⁾iˈstɛrstwo ˈu̯nut⁽ʲ⁾r⁽ʲ⁾iʃn⁽ʲ⁾ix ˈsprɑu̯ ʊkrɐˈjinɪ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Ukrainian); ***MVS***) is the [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) of the [Ukrainian government](1372585.md) that oversees the [interior affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior_affairs) of [Ukraine](31750.md).

## History

### Name

- People's Committee of Internal Affairs of the Ukrainian SSR (1919–1930, regional autonomous agency)

- State Political Directorate of the Ukrainian SSR (1930–1934, part of the [Joint State Political Directorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_State_Political_Directorate) of USSR)

- People's Committee of Internal Affairs of the Ukrainian SSR (1934–1946, part of the People's Committee of Internal Affairs of USSR)

- Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Ukrainian SSR (1946–1991, part of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of USSR)

- Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine (since 1991, a government agency of the independent Ukraine)

### History of Militsiya

Main article: [Militsiya (Ukraine)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militsiya_\(Ukraine\))

The ministry directly controlled the Ukrainian national law enforcement agency, termed the *[militsiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militsiya_\(Ukraine\))* ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): міліція, [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): милиция). Ukraine's *militsiya* was widely regarded as corrupt,[^cite_note-UWBU9713-6] and it had received accusations of torture and ill-treatment.[^cite_note-refUAp1-7][^cite_note-refUAp2-8][^cite_note-refUAp3-9][^cite_note-RLUP17713-10] This changed in July 2015, in the aftermath of [Euromaidan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euromaidan), with the introduction of reforms by Ukrainian president [Petro Poroshenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petro_Poroshenko) to reduce corruption, whereby the *militsiya* was replaced with the [National Police](44656231.md).

The State Emergency Service was transferred under the jurisdiction of the ministry since 2014.[^cite_note-\:0-11]

## Duties

The ministry carries out state policy for the protection of rights and liberties of citizens, investigates unlawful acts against the interest of society and state, fights crime, provides civil order, ensures civil security and traffic safety, and guarantees the security and protection of important individuals.

## Organisation

It is a centralised agency headed by the [Minister of Internal Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Internal_Affairs_\(Ukraine\)). The ministry works closely with the office of the [General Prosecutor of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Prosecutor_of_Ukraine).

It oversees the [National Police of Ukraine](44656231.md)[^cite_note-2sept15-12] ([police service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_service)), [National Guard of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Guard_of_Ukraine) ([gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie)), the [State Emergency Service of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Emergency_Service_of_Ukraine) ([civil defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_defense)),[^cite_note-\:0-11] [State Border Guard Service of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Border_Guard_Service_of_Ukraine) (and its subordinate the [Ukrainian Sea Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Sea_Guard)) and the [State Migration Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Migration_Service_\(Ukraine\)) ([border control](247927.md) service).

The minister of internal affairs is in charge of the ministry. Prior to the 2015 police reforms, the minister was recognized as head of the [militsiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militsiya_\(Ukraine\)).

Many former ministers previously had experience with serving in the police, and were, prior to taking up the ministerial post, generals of the [militsiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militsiya_\(Ukraine\)).

Typically, the minister was afforded the rank of Colonel-General of the *militsiya* upon taking up his post in the [Ukrainian government](1372585.md).

[Yuriy Lutsenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuriy_Lutsenko) and [Vasyl Tsushko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasyl_Tsushko) are the only former holders of this office who had never served in any [law enforcement agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_agency).

The minister of Internal Affairs is responsible directly to the [Prime Minister of Ukraine](1256678.md), to the Ukrainian Parliament ([Verkhovna Rada](844993.md)) and ultimately the [President of Ukraine](20688479.md). His office is located in Kyiv's [Pechersk District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pecherskyi_District).
