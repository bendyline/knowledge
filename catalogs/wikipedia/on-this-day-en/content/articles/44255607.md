**Los Angeles Football Club**, also known as **LAFC**, is an American professional [soccer](10568.md) club based in [Los Angeles](18110.md). The club competes in [Major League Soccer](71802.md) (MLS) as a member of the [Western Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Conference_\(MLS\)). It was established on October 30, 2014, and began play in the [2018 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Major_League_Soccer_season) as an [expansion team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expansion_team). The team plays its home matches at [BMO Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMO_Stadium), a [soccer-specific stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soccer-specific_stadium) located in [Exposition Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exposition_Park,_Los_Angeles).

The managing owners of the club are Brandon Beck, Larry Berg, and Bennett Rosenthal. Los Angeles FC also has a variety of other part-owners, such as [Will Ferrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Ferrell), [Nomar Garciaparra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomar_Garciaparra), [Mia Hamm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mia_Hamm), [Giorgio Chiellini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgio_Chiellini), and [Earvin 'Magic" Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Johnson). The club's inaugural head coach was [Bob Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Bradley), who served from 2017 to 2021 before being replaced by [Steven Cherundolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Cherundolo).

During their second season in 2019, Los Angeles FC won the [Supporters' Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supporters'_Shield) with club [captain](633462.md) [Carlos Vela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Vela) earning the [MVP Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landon_Donovan_MVP_Award). The club won their first [MLS Cup](483246.md) in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_2022), which completed a [league double](88184.md) with their second Supporters' Shield. In 2020 and 2023, Los Angeles FC were also the runners-up in the [CONCACAF Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF_Champions_League). In 2024, Los Angeles FC won their first [Lamar Hunt U.S. Open Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_U.S._Open_Cup).

The club within its short history has been home to notable players such as [Gareth Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gareth_Bale), [Olivier Giroud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Giroud), [Hugo Lloris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Lloris), [Son Heung-min](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Son_Heung-min), [Giorgio Chiellini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgio_Chiellini), and [Carlos Vela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Vela).

## History

### Founding and inaugural season (2014–2018)



Groundbreaking ceremony for [Banc of California Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banc_of_California_Stadium) (now BMO Stadium) in 2016

#### The founding

Los Angeles FC was founded on October 30, 2014, as a result of [Major League Soccer](71802.md) (MLS) awarding a [new expansion team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expansion_of_Major_League_Soccer) to Los Angeles after the complete dissolution of [Chivas USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chivas_USA).[^cite_note-5] On September 15, 2015, the club announced that Los Angeles Football Club, which had previously been used as a placeholder name for the club, would be the official team name.[^cite_note-6] Henry Nguyen, Los Angeles FC's principal owner at the time, hinted at this possibility shortly after the club was announced in describing the name as "timeless".[^cite_note-LAFC_inside_story2-7]

LAFC announced [Bob Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Bradley) as its head coach in July 2017,[^cite_note-8] joining general director [John Thorrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Thorrington) in a search for players.[^cite_note-9] Mexican forward [Carlos Vela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Vela) was signed as the club's first [designated player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Designated_Player_Rule) on August 11, 2017.[^cite_note-10]

#### Inaugural 2018 season

Main article: [2018 Major League Soccer season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Major_League_Soccer_season)

On March 4, 2018, LAFC played its first MLS game, a 1–0 win against [Seattle Sounders FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC) at [CenturyLink Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CenturyLink_Field) in Seattle. LAFC designated player [Diego Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Rossi) scored the club's first competitive goal in the 11th minute, assisted by Vela.[^cite_note-11] LAFC suffered its first MLS loss on March 31 against the [LA Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Galaxy), losing 4–3 to their crosstown rivals in the inaugural edition of "[El Tráfico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Tr%C3%A1fico)".[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] Despite the loss, LAFC won 4 of the 6 games on their road trip to start the season, becoming the first team to earn 12 points from a season opening road trip of 6 games or more.[^cite_note-14] They finished the season with 7 road wins which is the most for an expansion team in the post-shootout era.[^cite_note-15]

LAFC accomplished the best regular season for an MLS expansion team, earning 57 points. The total surpassed the 56 picked up by the [1998 Chicago Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Chicago_Fire_season), also coached by Bob Bradley, as well as the post-shootout era record of 55 set in 2017 by [Atlanta United FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_United_FC).[^cite_note-MLS-ExpansionPts-16] LAFC's seven road wins also tied for the most ever by an expansion team in the pre or post shootout-era with the '98 Fire who had two road wins come via the shootout. They finished second all-time in goals scored by an expansion team in a season, with 68, just behind Atlanta's 70.[^cite_note-MLS-ExpansionPts-16]

On October 6, 2018, LAFC clinched its first playoff spot after a 3–0 victory against the [Colorado Rapids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Rapids)[^cite_note-17] finishing third in the West, but were knocked out at home in the first round in a 3–2 loss to sixth-place [Real Salt Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Salt_Lake).[^cite_note-18]

### First Supporters' Shield title and pandemic seasons (2019–2021)

#### 2019 season

Main article: [2019 Major League Soccer season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Major_League_Soccer_season)

LAFC won the 2019 [Supporters' Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supporters'_Shield) with a record-setting 72 points; [Carlos Vela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Vela) won the [MLS Golden Boot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Golden_Boot) with a league-record 34 goals and was also named the [league MVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landon_Donovan_MVP_Award). In the playoffs, LAFC defeated their [El Trafico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Trafico) rivals [LA Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Galaxy) for the first time 5–3 in the Western Conference Semifinal. In the Western Conference Final, LAFC were eliminated in an upset loss at home by Cascadia rivals Seattle Sounders 3–1.[^cite_note-19]

#### 2020 season

Main article: [2020 Major League Soccer season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Major_League_Soccer_season)

The [2020 MLS season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Major_League_Soccer_season) was suspended in March due to the COVID-19 pandemic and resumed with the [MLS is Back Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_is_Back_Tournament), a special tournament with all teams at the [ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN_Wide_World_of_Sports_Complex) in [Orlando, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida). In the group stage, LAFC earned five points and qualified for the knockout rounds. The team defeated Seattle Sounders FC but lost to [Orlando City SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_City_SC) in a penalty shoutout. The regular season resumed after the tournament with matches played [behind closed doors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behind_closed_doors_\(sport\)). LAFC saw 47 goals with 14 coming from Golden Boot winner [Diego Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Rossi). The team finished with a 9–8–5 and were eliminated in the playoffs by Seattle Sounders FC.[^cite_note-20]

#### 2020 CONCACAF Champions League

Main article: [2020 CONCACAF Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_CONCACAF_Champions_League)



Bob Bradley, head coach of LAFC from July 17, 2017, to November 18, 2021.

Los Angeles FC qualified for [CONCACAF Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF_Champions_League) for the first time in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF_Champions_League). After going down 2–0 in the first leg against [Club León](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Le%C3%B3n), LAFC came back and won 3–0 in the second leg, advancing (3–2 on aggregate) to the round of 16.[^cite_note-21] Shortly after that, due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](63136490.md), the tournament was put on hold. When the tournament resumed in December, under a new one-off format, Los Angeles FC defeated [Cruz Azul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruz_Azul) (2–1) after going down 1–0. In the semi-finals LAFC defeated [Club América](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Am%C3%A9rica) (3–1), again in comeback fashion.[^cite_note-22] They became the third MLS side to advance to the Final, where they were defeated by [Tigres UANL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tigres_UANL) (2–1), who had been runners-up in previous editions of the tournament, and who went on to become the runners-up in the Club World Cup.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

#### 2021 season

Main article: [2021 Major League Soccer season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Major_League_Soccer_season)

LAFC rebuilt their roster for much of the [2021 MLS season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Major_League_Soccer_season) and missed the MLS Cup playoffs. Diego Rossi was transferred to [Fenerbahçe S.K.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenerbah%C3%A7e_S.K._\(football\)) in September 2021.[^cite_note-25] [Carlos Vela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Vela) was injured for most of the year and only appeared in 27 matches during the 2020 and 2021 seasons.[^cite_note-26] After missing the playoffs, [Bob Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Bradley) was fired as head coach on November 18, 2021.[^cite_note-27] He was replaced by [Steve Cherundolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Cherundolo), the head coach of the team's USL affiliate [Las Vegas Lights FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas_Lights_FC), on January 3, 2022.[^cite_note-28]

#### MLS Cup championship (2022)

Main article: [2022 Major League Soccer season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Major_League_Soccer_season)

See also: [MLS Cup 2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_2022)



Starting XI for [2022 MLS Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_2022)

LAFC spent the majority of the [2022 MLS season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Major_League_Soccer_season) pushing for the league's all-time points record (73, set the previous year), but had five losses in their final nine matches. The team finished with 67 points and won their second [Supporters' Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supporters'_Shield), but fell short of the record. This total was equal with the [Philadelphia Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_Union), who lost the Shield on a wins tiebreaker. LAFC defeated the Galaxy 3–2 in the conference semifinals before knocking out [Austin FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_FC) 3–0 to advance to their first [MLS Cup](483246.md).[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

Los Angeles FC won the [2022 MLS Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_2022) by defeating the Union in a [penalty shootout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shootout_\(association_football\)) following a 3–3 draw through [extra time](633242.md#Association_football). The two latest goals in [MLS Cup Playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_Playoffs) history were scored in the match, with [Gareth Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gareth_Bale) scoring an equalizer in the 128th minute to take the match to penalties, which LAFC won 3–0. LAFC substitute goalkeeper [John McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McCarthy_\(soccer\)) made two saves in the shootout and was named the [most valuable player](483246.md#Most_valuable_player) of the match.[^cite_note-31]

### Recent seasons (2023–present)

#### 2023 season

Main article: [2023 Major League Soccer season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Major_League_Soccer_season)

Coming out of the successes of the 2022 campaign, Los Angeles FC had qualified to the 2023 CONCACAF Champions League. The team had a congested schedule with the Champions League and U.S. Open Cup taking most of the breaks from regular season play in the first half of the season. MLS also had introduced the [Leagues Cup](60906407.md). LAFC finished as runners-up in [MLS Cup 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_2023) to the [Columbus Crew](245695.md)[^cite_note-32] and the [CONCACAF Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_CONCACAF_Champions_League) to [Club León](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Le%C3%B3n).[^cite_note-33] The team traveled over 63,000 miles (101,000 km) over the course of the season and played 53 matches—a record at the time for an MLS team during a single year.[^cite_note-34]

#### 2023 CONCACAF Champions League

Main article: [2023 CONCACAF Champions League final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_CONCACAF_Champions_League_final)

During the team's 2023 season, Los Angeles had advanced to the [2023 CONCACAF Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_CONCACAF_Champions_League) [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_CONCACAF_Champions_League_final) for the club's second time in four years. Their road to the finals consisted of victories against [Alajuelense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alajuelense), [Vancouver Whitecaps FC](22060729.md), and [Philadelphia Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_Union). Los Angeles FC would face [Club León](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Le%C3%B3n) in the final, who the team had faced before in the 2020 tournament. Los Angeles FC were the favorites, heading to the final. In the first leg, Leon would win 2–1 with a late goal by [Denis Bouanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Bouanga) to keep the hope alive for Los Angeles FC. In the second leg, Los Angeles FC fell flat and would lose the match against Leon 1–0 and lose on aggregate 3–1.[^cite_note-35]

#### 2024 season

Main article: [2024 Major League Soccer season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Major_League_Soccer_season)

With the 2024 season, LAFC acquired [Lewis O'Brien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_O'Brien_\(footballer\)), [Eduard Atuesta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduard_Atuesta), [Olivier Giroud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Giroud), [Hugo Lloris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Lloris), [David Martínez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Mart%C3%ADnez_\(footballer,_born_2006\)), [Kei Kamara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kei_Kamara), and [Maxime Chanot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxime_Chanot) in response to the departures of [Maxime Crépeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxime_Cr%C3%A9peau), [Carlos Vela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Vela) (The club negotiated a small contract at the end of the season), [Denil Maldonado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denil_Maldonado), [Giorgio Chiellini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgio_Chiellini), [Kellyn Acosta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kellyn_Acosta), [Filip Krastev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filip_Krastev), [Diego Palacios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Palacios), and [Stipe Biuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stipe_Biuk). The team was able to clinch the Western Conference (Regular season) by goal difference against the [LA Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Galaxy).[^cite_note-36] LAFC had a rocky start to its season dealing with a first loss of the season against [Real Salt Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Salt_Lake) in a snow game 3–0.[^cite_note-37] By the middle of the season, LAFC saw an unbeaten streak of 13 games. That streak would end with a defeat by the [Columbus Crew](245695.md) 5–1.[^cite_note-38] LAFC would reach the final of the [2024 Leagues Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Leagues_Cup) getting out of the group stage against [Vancouver Whitecaps FC](22060729.md) and [Club Tijuana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Tijuana) and then knocking out [Austin FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_FC), [San Jose Earthquakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose_Earthquakes), [Seattle Sounders FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC), and [Colorado Rapids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Rapids). In the final they would face [Columbus Crew](245695.md) once again losing 3–1.[^cite_note-39] After the Leagues Cup tournament, LAFC would go on to lose against [Houston Dynamo FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston_Dynamo_FC), [LA Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Galaxy), and [FC Dallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Dallas) in the 2nd half of the regular season. In the [2024 MLS Cup playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_MLS_Cup_playoffs) after knocking out [Vancouver Whitecaps FC](22060729.md) in the Best of Three Round,[^cite_note-40] LAFC faced [Seattle Sounders FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC). The match would go on to extra-time where [Seattle Sounders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders) would eventually defeat LAFC 2–1 ending LAFC's 2024 season.[^cite_note-41]

#### U.S. Open Cup title (2024)

Main article: [2024 U.S. Open Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_U.S._Open_Cup_final)

LAFC qualified to the [2024 U.S. Open Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_U.S._Open_Cup) and began their campaign in the Round of 32.[^cite_note-42] Their first opponent was against USL Championship side [Las Vegas Lights FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas_Lights_FC) where after chippy play, LAFC came out victorious 3–1 at [Cashman Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cashman_Field).[^cite_note-43] In the Round of 16, LAFC played at home as they faced USL Championship side [Loudoun United FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loudoun_United_FC). LAFC easily beat the team 3–0 to move on to the Quarter-finals.[^cite_note-44] Their next opponent, was the leading USL Championship side [New Mexico United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico_United). LAFC would go on to win the match 3–1.[^cite_note-45] The Semifinal round brought LAFC to play against their rivals [Seattle Sounders FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC). The match was very defensive with no goals until [Denis Bouanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Bouanga) scored a penalty kick on the 83rd minute securing a LAFC win.[^cite_note-Los_Angeles_Football_Club-46] With the victory, LAFC would be the third team along with [San Jose Earthquakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose_Earthquakes) and [Portland Timbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_Timbers) to beat the Seattle Sounders at the 5,000 seater [Starfire Sports Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starfire_Sports) where the Sounders had a fortress record of 25–1–3.[^cite_note-47]

As a result of winning against Seattle Sounders, Los Angeles FC would advance to the U.S. Open Cup Final to face [Sporting Kansas City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_Kansas_City) at home.[^cite_note-Los_Angeles_Football_Club-46] The first half of the final would see both sides not scoring. That would change in the 2nd half as [Olivier Giroud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Giroud) at the 53rd minute would take a successful header from a cross by [Mateusz Bogusz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mateusz_Bogusz) to make it 1–0. At the 60th minute Sporting Kansas would respond with a goal by [Erik Thommy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Thommy) making it 1–1. The game would go on to extra time where at the 102nd minute [Omar Campos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Campos_\(footballer,_born_2002\)) would take a shot after beating a defender to make it 2–1. Kansas City would not recover especially after [Kei Kamara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kei_Kamara) would take a header to make it 3–1 at the 109th minute.[^cite_note-48] LAFC would then go on to win the 2024 U.S. Open Cup becoming the fourth team from the Los Angeles area to win the U.S. Open Cup, following the [Los Angeles Kickers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Kickers), [Maccabee Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maccabee_Los_Angeles), and [LA Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Galaxy).[^cite_note-49]

The [Dewar Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewar_Cup) was presented by three members of the [Los Angeles Kickers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Kickers), an amateur team that won the [1958](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958_National_Challenge_Cup) and [1964](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964_National_Challenge_Cup) editions of the tournament.[^cite_note-50]

#### 2025 season



Steve Cherundolo, coach of LAFC from January 3, 2022 to December 1, 2025.

With the 2025, the club acquired [Jeremy Ebobisse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Ebobisse), [Yaw Yeboah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaw_Yeboah), [Mark Delgado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Delgado), and [Nkosi Tafari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nkosi_Tafari). The club signed [Igor Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Jesus_\(footballer,_born_2003\)) and [Artem Smolyakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artem_Smolyakov) while acquiring [Odin Thiago Holm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odin_Thiago_Holm) from [Celtic](69708.md) and [Cengiz Ünder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cengiz_%C3%9Cnder) from [Fenerbahçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenerbah%C3%A7e_S.K._\(football\)) on loan. It was in response to the departures of [Jesús Murillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jes%C3%BAs_David_Murillo), [Ilie Sánchez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilie_S%C3%A1nchez), [Mateusz Bogusz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mateusz_Bogusz), [Cristian Olivera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristian_Olivera), [Kei Kamara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kei_Kamara), [Eduard Atuesta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduard_Atuesta), and [Lewis O'Brien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_O'Brien_\(footballer\)).[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53]

Los Angeles FC qualified for the [2025 CONCACAF Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_CONCACAF_Champions_Cup) defeating the [Colorado Rapids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Rapids) in Round 1 and the [Columbus Crew](245695.md) in the Round of 16.[^cite_note-54][^cite_note-55] Los Angeles FC would then advance to the quarter-finals and face [Inter Miami](41853326.md). In first leg, Los Angeles FC would win 1–0 but would lose to Inter Miami in the second leg 3–1 with an aggregate of 3–2 thus eliminating LAFC from the tournament.[^cite_note-56] On April 18, 2025, Los Angeles FC announced that head coach Steve Cherundolo would step down after the 2025 season for personal reasons.[^cite_note-57] Faced with being eliminated from the [2025 CONCACAF Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_CONCACAF_Champions_Cup), having a 4–1–4 record and a low goal difference, LAFC acquired midfielder [Frankie Amaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankie_Amaya) and [Ryan Raposo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Raposo).[^cite_note-Major_League_Soccer-58][^cite_note-59]

On August 6, Los Angeles FC signed Korean star [Son Heung-min](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Son_Heung-min) from [Tottenham Hotspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.) for a MLS transfer record of \$26.5 million.[^cite_note-60][^cite_note-61]

#### FIFA Club World Cup 2025

On March 21, 2025, the FIFA Appeals Committee ruled that [Club Leon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_Leon) which had won the [2023 CONCACAF Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_CONCACAF_Champions_League), was removed from the [FIFA Club World Cup 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Club_World_Cup_2025) due to having [multi-club ownership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-club_ownership_\(association_football\)) criteria conflicts along with [CF Pachuca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CF_Pachuca) winners of the [2024 CONCACAF Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_CONCACAF_Champions_Cup). Both clubs are under the ownership of Grupo Pachuca.[^cite_note-62] As a consequence, Club Leon's berth was vacant. Club Leon appealed to the [Court of Arbitration for Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Arbitration_for_Sport) but was unsuccessful. On May 16, 2025, FIFA announced a play-in game to determine the last berth to the [FIFA Club World Cup 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Club_World_Cup_2025) between the [2023 CONCACAF Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_CONCACAF_Champions_League) runner-up [LAFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LAFC) and the top-ranked team of the 2024 Club World Cup Ranking by Confederation [Club America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_America) to be played on May 31, 2025. LAFC would go on to win the match with 1-1 on regular time and 2-1 after extra time. LAFC thus was placed in Group D with [Chelsea FC](7473.md) (England), [Espérance Sportive de Tunis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esp%C3%A9rance_Sportive_de_Tunis) (Tunisia), and [Flamengo](187239.md) (Brazil).[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64]

## Colors, badge, and sponsorship



The LA monogram that is used in LAFC black and gold caps.

The club's colors and logo were unveiled on January 7, 2016, at [Union Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Station_\(Los_Angeles\)).[^cite_note-65][^cite_note-66] The club's primary colors are black and gold, with red and gray used as accent colors. The [Art Deco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Deco)-inspired logo incorporates a shield outline referencing the [city seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal_of_the_City_of_Los_Angeles), with a winged "LA" monogram and the words "Los Angeles" and "Football Club" in [Neutraface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutraface). The crest was developed and designed by Beacon Asia Consulting co-founder Thai Nguyen and the creative team of Tue Nguyen and [Matthew Wolff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Wolff_\(designer\)).[^cite_note-67]

### Sponsorship

On January 31, 2018, LAFC announced that [YouTube TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube_TV) would be the club's first official shirt sponsor in addition to exclusively broadcasting the club's local matches in English.[^cite_note-LAFCYouTubeTV-68][^cite_note-LAT-YouTubeTV-69] [Target](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Target_Corporation) was announced as their first sleeve sponsor in October 2019.[^cite_note-70]

On March 26, 2021, upon the expiration of its sponsorship contract with YouTube TV, LAFC announced that [FLEX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flex-Elektrowerkzeuge), a power tool manufacturer headquartered in [Steinheim an der Murr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinheim_an_der_Murr), Germany, would replace YouTube TV as the main shirt sponsor for the club.[^cite_note-71] Four days later, on March 30, 2021, the club announced that [Postmates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postmates) would become the second official sleeve sponsor (joining Target) on a one-year deal for the entirety of the 2021 season.[^cite_note-72] Shortly thereafter, LAFC announced that YouTube TV no longer held exclusive broadcasting rights over English language home matches. Instead, the club chose to partner with two local networks for all English language home games beginning that season.[^cite_note-LAT-KCOP-TV_and_Bally_Sports_SoCal_Partner_With_LAFC-73]

For the club's the 2022 season, LAFC announced on February 26, 2022, that it had partnered with [PepsiCo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PepsiCo) to make [Rockstar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockstar_\(drink\)) energy drink the exclusive shirt sponsor of its 2022 training kit.[^cite_note-74] The agreement expired at the end of the season.[^cite_note-LATimes-BMO-75]

Prior to the 2023 season, on June 14, 2022, [MLS](71802.md) announced a new media rights partnership with [Apple](856.md) giving the tech giant exclusive broadcast rights to all league games via its streaming service, [Apple TV+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV+), for the next 10 seasons.[^cite_note-76] To promote this new partnership, as well as their new streaming service entitled [MLS Season Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Season_Pass), both MLS and Apple agreed to have the Apple TV+ logo appear on the left kit sleeve of all league teams beginning with the 2023 season.[^cite_note-77] Additionally, [Ford](30433662.md), LAFC's Official Domestic Automobile Partner, joined the club's top tier of sustained brand sponsorships - the "Golden Boot Club" - for the 2023 season. This enhanced partnership included Ford receiving promonent logo placement on the team's right kit sleeve.[^cite_note-78]

On May 17, 2015, the team chose the [Los Angeles Memorial Sports Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Memorial_Sports_Arena) site to [build a 22,000-seat stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMO_Stadium) for MLS in Exposition Park, estimated to cost \$250 million. The site was under a sublease from the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) which managed and operated the Sports Arena and adjacent [L.A. Memorial Coliseum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.A._Memorial_Coliseum).[^cite_note-81] The environmental impact report, arena demolition, and stadium construction were expected to take three years and delay the team's debut to 2018. On May 6, 2016, the [Los Angeles City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_City_Council) approved the stadium, clearing a way for the construction of the stadium.

A groundbreaking ceremony took place on August 23, 2016. At the event attended by owners and construction crews, LAFC announced a 15-year, \$100 million [naming rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naming_right) deal for the stadium with the [Banc of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banc_of_California).[^cite_note-82][^cite_note-83][^cite_note-Bloomberg-84] Demolition of the Los Angeles Memorial Sports Arena began shortly after the groundbreaking and was completed by October 2016.[^cite_note-85][^cite_note-86]

The first public event at the stadium was an open practice and dedication ceremony held on April 18, 2018.[^cite_note-87] The club's first home match was played on April 29, 2018, against [Seattle Sounders FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC), with the home side winning 1–0.[^cite_note-Dec17-88][^cite_note-First-89] The lone goal was scored by [Laurent Ciman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurent_Ciman) in stoppage time in front of a capacity crowd of 22,000.[^cite_note-First-89] The stadium was renamed to [BMO Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMO_Stadium) on January 19, 2023.[^cite_note-90]

Los Angeles FC shares the stadium with [NWSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWSL) side [Angel City FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_City_FC) beginning in the Spring of 2022. The team became affiliated with LAFC after LAFC took a minority stake in ACFC due to priced warrants issued to LAFC when ACFC signed the lease agreement.[^cite_note-91][^cite_note-Urban_Pitch-92]

Los Angeles FC's training facility is on the [California State University, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_Los_Angeles), campus as part of a partnership with the university that began in 2017. The LAFC Performance Center includes 4,000 square feet (370 m<sup>2</sup>) of indoor space and a training field with the same grass as the team's home stadium.[^cite_note-93]

## Club culture



The 3252 Supporters unveiling their first [tifo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tifo) in the home inaugural game vs. the Seattle Sounders

After the launch, supporters were consulted on many of the club's early decisions including the team colors, the look of the crest and the design of [BMO Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMO_Stadium), built on land previously held by the [LA Sports Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Memorial_Sports_Arena).[^cite_note-94] Much of the marketing focus was to millennials, which led to the decision to play near downtown Los Angeles.[^cite_note-95] LAFC took a grassroots approach to building the club by founding the LAFC academy and signing younger prospects including Americans [Walker Zimmerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walker_Zimmerman) and [Tristan Blackmon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tristan_Blackmon), Portuguese draft pick [João Moutinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Moutinho_\(footballer,_born_1998\)),[^cite_note-96] and Uruguayan prospect [Diego Rossi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Rossi).

### Supporters

LAFC supporters are known collectively as "The 3252". The official capacity of the safe standing supporters section at BMO Stadium is 3,252. When added together, the numbers 3, 2, 5, 2 equal 12, serving as a reference to "the [12th man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_man_\(football\))", and an homage to the supporters of the team. Composed of a variety of supporters groups, The 3252 is the club's independent supporters union, which encompasses a growing number of affiliate supporter groups such as the Black Army 1850, District Nine Ultras, Cuervos, Expo Originals, Tiger Supporter Group (TSG), The Krew, Luckys, Los Angelinos Originales, and Empire Boys.[^cite_note-97] The 3252 also includes independent supporter groups with active season memberships such as LAFC Pride Republic, Lxs Tigres del North End, Offsiders, and 42Originals.[^cite_note-98][^cite_note-99] The supporter section in the North End of the stadium is only for fans of LAFC. Anyone wearing other team gear is not allowed in the section.[^cite_note-100] The 3252 has several chants that are sung throughout the match in English, Spanish and Korean including: Dale, Dale Black & Gold, This is Los Angeles, Venirte Ver, Aliento de Corazon, Forevermore, Jump for LA Football Club, Que Bonito Es, Dale Alegria a mi Corazon, and Uh Dirado.[^cite_note-101]

### Falcon program

The club's falcon program includes three live falcons who participate in various gameday and community events. One falcon is released pre-game by an honorary falconer (usually a celebrity or community figure) and flies around the stadium. The first honorary falconer was co-owner [Will Ferrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Ferrell).[^cite_note-102] The three falcons are named after famous streets in Los Angeles: "Olly" ([Olvera Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olvera_Street)), "Fig" ([Figueroa Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figueroa_Street)), and "Mel" ([Melrose Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melrose_Avenue)).[^cite_note-103]

## Rivalries



The 3252 Tifo for the 2019 Conference Final vs [LA Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Galaxy). LAFC would go on to win for the first time 5-3 in the sixth edition of [El Tráfico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Trafico).

Since their establishment as an expansion team in 2014, Los Angeles FC have had a fierce rivalry with the LA Galaxy, from Carson, California, the closest MLS team. The rivalry between the two clubs has been dubbed El Tráfico by the supporters of both clubs, a humorous play on El Clásico.[^cite_note-104] Since beginning in the league, LAFC have also had a on-field rivalry with the Seattle Sounders FC. Both teams have competed at the top of the Western Conference tables and the playoffs in the most recent seasons.

### LA Galaxy

See also: [El Tráfico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Tr%C3%A1fico)

LAFC have a cross-town rivalry with fellow MLS club [LA Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Galaxy), who play in the Los Angeles suburb of [Carson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson,_California). The series between the two teams is known as *El Tráfico*[^cite_note-105] ([Spanish](26825.md) for "The Traffic"), conversely, Spanish-language speakers and media commonly refer to it as the *Clásico del Tráfico*[^cite_note-106][^cite_note-107] ([English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) for the "Traffic Classic"). The nickname was coined by MLS fans and adopted by some media outlets following polls by SB Nation blogs LAG Confidential and Angels on Parade.[^cite_note-108][^cite_note-LATimes-2018Mar-109][^cite_note-110] It refers to the notorious traffic congestion in Los Angeles,[^cite_note-LATimes-2018Mar-109][^cite_note-111] among the worst in the United States and the world, while serving as a play on "[El Clásico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cl%C3%A1sico)".[^cite_note-112] The rivalry has also been called the "Los Angeles Derby",[^cite_note-113] a moniker that was also used for the SuperClasico.[^cite_note-114]

### Seattle Sounders FC

LAFC's first game ever played was a 1–0 win at [CenturyLink Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CenturyLink_Field) over [Seattle Sounders FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Sounders_FC) on March 4, 2018, marking the earliest starting point of any of LAFC's on-field rivalries. LAFC's first home game at Banc of California Stadium was also a 1–0 win over Seattle, ending in dramatic fashion with a 92nd-minute goal by [Laurent Ciman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurent_Ciman). The next season Seattle got its revenge, defeating LAFC for the first time in the [2019 Western Conference Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_MLS_Cup_Playoffs), bringing L.A.'s record-setting season and first MLS Cup hopes to an end.[^cite_note-115] In similar fashion LAFC in 2024 would prevent the Seattle Sounders from their 5th [U.S. Open Cup](253156.md) by defeating them in the semifinals 1-0.[^cite_note-Los_Angeles_Football_Club-46] Beyond these touchstone moments, the rivalry has been carried on by players, coaches and supporters.[^cite_note-116][^cite_note-117][^cite_note-118][^cite_note-119]

## Broadcasting

From 2023, every LAFC match is available via [MLS Season Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Season_Pass) on the [Apple TV app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_TV_app), in addition to select matches simulcast linearly on [Fox or FS1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_on_Fox). Prior to this all-streaming deal, LAFC aired matches on a few stations and one other streaming platform.

In 2021 and 2022, all locally broadcast LAFC matches were televised in English by [KCOP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KCOP-TV). [Max Bretos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Bretos) served as the team's lead [play-by-play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play-by-play) announcer[^cite_note-120]

[YouTube TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube_TV) carried live games for the team from 2018 to 2020. It marked the first time that a major U.S. professional sports team sold their regional broadcast rights to an online streaming service rather than a traditional television broadcaster or [regional sports network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_sports_network).[^cite_note-LAFCYouTubeTV-68][^cite_note-LAT-YouTubeTV-69][^cite_note-121]

Prior to Apple, locally broadcast LAFC matches were televised in Spanish on [Estrella TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estrella_TV) station [KRCA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KRCA), with Francisco X. Rivera as the lead play-by-play announcer.[^cite_note-122] Regular local radio coverage of LAFC matches is provided in English by [KSPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KSPN_\(AM\)) (ESPNLA 710), with Dave Denholm serving as the radio play-by-play announcer. Spanish radio broadcasts are provided by [KFWB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KFWB) (980), with Armando Aguayo as the play-by-play announcer.[^cite_note-123] The club also partners with [KIRN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KIRN) (670) as part of the station's weekly sports report.[^cite_note-124]

## Team management



Ownership Group and Mayor Garcetti unveiling team colors.

In 2016, three local investors—Brandon Beck, Larry Berg and Bennett Rosenthal—took over as managing owners of the club with Berg serving as lead managing owner.[^cite_note-127][^cite_note-128] As of 2017, chairman and CEO of Mandalay Entertainment Group and entrepreneur [Peter Guber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Guber) is executive chairman, and venture capitalist [Henry Nguyen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Nguyen) is vice-chairman.[^cite_note-\:0-129] The ownership group in 2019 also included businessman [Ruben Gnanalingam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruben_Gnanalingam).[^cite_note-LAFC_inside_story2-7] Other part-owners and investors include [Will Ferrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Ferrell), [Natalie Mariduena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Mariduena), [Nomar Garciaparra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomar_Garciaparra), [Mia Hamm-Garciaparra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mia_Hamm), [Chad Hurley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chad_Hurley), [Magic Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Johnson), [Joseph Tsai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Tsai),[^cite_note-130] [Giorgio Chiellini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgio_Chiellini),[^cite_note-131] Tucker Kain, Kirk Lacob, [Mitch Lasky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitch_Lasky), Mark Leschly, Mike Mahan, Irwin Raij, [Tony Robbins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Robbins), Lon Rosen, Paul Schaeffer, Brandon Schneider, [Allen Shapiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_Shapiro), [Mark Shapiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Shapiro_\(media_executive\)), Jason Sugarman, Harry Tsao, and [Rick Welts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Welts).[^cite_note-LAFC_inside_story2-7][^cite_note-\:0-129][^cite_note-132][^cite_note-133]

LAFC's first president was [Tom Penn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Penn) until he stepped down on August 18, 2020, to pursue a PPE business venture. Larry Freedman and [John Thorrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Thorrington) took over as Co-presidents.[^cite_note-134]

In February 2020, LAFC owners began the process of buying out a 20 percent ownership stake held by Malaysian businessman [Vincent Tan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Tan). The buyout resulted in a total equity valuation of \$700 million for the club, the largest on record for a [Major League Soccer](71802.md) team at the time.[^cite_note-135]

### LAFC Academy

On February 1, 2016, the club announced the founding of the LAFC Academy.[^cite_note-137] The academy is launching with a fully funded U12 USSDA academy team with a roster of 26 players. [Todd Saldana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Saldana) served as academy director until January 16, 2025.[^cite_note-138] On August 21, 2018, the U-13 squad won the CONCACAF Champions League, as champions of North America.[^cite_note-139]

On July 8, 2020, LAFC signed its first three Homegrown players from the academy in Club history – [Tony Leone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Leone_\(soccer\)), [Christian Torres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Torres_\(footballer,_born_2004\)) and [Erik Dueñas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Due%C3%B1as).[^cite_note-140]

## Honors

The 2019 Supporters' Shield Champion LAFC

The MLS Supporters' Shield

In 2019, LAFC won their first major MLS trophy, the Supporters' Shield. They won it with a record breaking 72 points, in just their second season in existence. LAFC capped-off the 2022 season by winning the MLS Cup over the Philadelphia Union. In 2024, LAFC won the [U.S. Open Cup](253156.md) with a 3–1 victory against [Sporting Kansas City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporting_Kansas_City). This marked the team’s very first Open Cup win.[^cite_note-158]
