**Emmanuel John Nchimbi** (born 24 December 1971) is a Tanzanian diplomat and politician who served as the [Vice-President](16997743.md) of the [United Republic of Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Republic_of_Tanzania) from November 2025 until his resignation in August 2026.[^cite_note-1] He previously served as the [Minister of Home Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Home_Affairs_\(Tanzania\)) between 2012 and 2013 and as [Member of Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament) for [Songea Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songea) constituency from 2010 to 2015.[^cite_note-2] On 25 August 2026, he announced that he had submitted his resignation as Vice-President to President Samia Suluhu Hassan, with effect from 4 September 2026, saying that he intended to retire from politics and public service.[^cite_note-standard2026-3][^cite_note-allafrica2026-4]

## Background and education

Nchimbi went to Uru and Sangu Secondary Schools after which he proceeded to an advanced education at Forest Hill Secondary School in Mbeya.[^cite_note-\:1-5] After graduating from Mzumbe University where he studied diploma in public administration, he pursued a master's degree in banking and finance, and eventually a doctorate.[^cite_note-\:0-6]

## Political career

Nchimbi became the chair of the CCM's youth wing after joining the National Executive Committee in 1997.[^cite_note-\:1-5] Prior to earning a position in the Songea Urban Assembly, he was the Bunda District Commissioner from 2003 to 2005.[^cite_note-7] He then served in the Cabinet in a number of capacities, including those of Home Affairs, Labor, and Information.[^cite_note-8] In the year 2015 to 2023, he represented Tanzania as ambassador to Egypt and then Brazil before he returned to party politics He was named Secretary-General of the CCM in January 2024.[^cite_note-\:0-6][^cite_note-9]

In 2025, he unveiled plans to strengthen the mining sector to serve East and [Central Africa](539606.md). He also pledged to review and introduce policies and laws to protect Tanzanian miners.[^cite_note-10]

### Resignation

On 25 August 2026, less than ten months after taking office, Nchimbi wrote to President Samia Suluhu Hassan resigning as Vice-President, with effect on 4 September 2026.[^cite_note-standard2026-3] In a statement issued in [Dodoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dodoma), he said he had become convinced that the President wanted a change and was therefore honouring a pledge he had made in August 2025 to step aside whenever she wanted a new deputy, adding that he intended to retire from politics and public service altogether.[^cite_note-allafrica2026-4][^cite_note-eyeradio2026-11] He cited Article 50(2)(c) read together with Article 149(2) of the [Constitution of Tanzania](29547627.md) as the basis for the resignation.[^cite_note-eyeradio2026-11] On 26 August, Nchimbi was expelled from the CCM for indiscipline.[^cite_note-12]
