**Khairpur District** ([Sindhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhi_language): خيرپور ضلعو, [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضلع خیرپور) is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Sindh) in the [Pakistani province](446017.md) of [Sindh](28972.md) in [Sukkur Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukkur_Division).[^cite_note-3]

At the 2017 census, it was the fifth most populated district in the province after four districts of Karachi city, with 2.4 million inhabitants. The headquarters of the district is the city of [Khairpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khairpur_city). The district is further divided into eight [sub-districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehsil): [Khairpur Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khairpur_Tehsil), [Mirwah Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thari_Mirwah), [Kot Diji Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kot_Diji), [Kingri Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingri_Tehsil), [ Sobho Dero Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobho_Dero), [Gambat Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambat), [Faiz Ganj Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faiz_Ganj_Tehsil) and [Nara Tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nara_Tehsil).[^cite_note-Khairpur-4]

## Location

Khairpur district is located between middle and northern Sindh. It is bounded on the north by [Shikarpur District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shikarpur_District) and [Sukkur District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukkur_District), on the east by India, on the south by [Sanghar District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanghar_District) and [Nawabshah District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nawabshah_District), and on the west by [Larkana District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larkana_District), [Naushahro Feroze District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naushahro_Feroze_District) and [Indus River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_River). The revised area of the district is 15,910 km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-Khairpur-4]

As of the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), Khairpur district has 452,250 households and a population of 2,597,535.[^cite_note-2023table20-7] The district has a sex ratio of 102.69 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 50.14%: 61.84% for males and 38.10% for females.[^cite_note-2023table1-1][^cite_note-2023table12-8] 891,260 (34.31% of the surveyed population) are under 10 years of age.[^cite_note-2023table5-9] 844,263 (32.50%) live in urban areas.[^cite_note-2023table1-1]

The majority religion is Islam, as indicated by 96.78% of the population. Hinduism is practiced by 2.90% of the population, including those from Scheduled Castes.[^cite_note-2023table9-6]

[Sindhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhi_language) was the predominant language, spoken by 97.73% of the population as their first language.[^cite_note-14]
