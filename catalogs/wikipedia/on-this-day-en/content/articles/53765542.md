**St. Nicholas' Church** in [Bączal Dolny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C4%85czal_Dolny), Poland, was a [Gothic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_architecture) [church](6325.md) from the seventeenth-century. Since the 1970s, the church was part of the [Museum of Folk Architecture](12812780.md) in [Sanok](390560.md).[^cite_note-1]

The temple illustrated a typical [Lesser Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesser_Poland) wooden church's architecture, and was one of the most prized buildings of religious heritage in south-eastern [Poland](22936.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] The rector of the church from 1939 to 1948 was [Florian Zając](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florian%20Zaj%C4%85c), a chaplain of the [Home Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Army).&#x20;

The church was completely destroyed by a fire at the [Museum of Folk Architecture, Sanok](12812780.md) in the early morning hours of 16 August 2026.[^cite_note-4]
