**Tonj North County** is an administrative area in [Warrap State](767678.md), in the [Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahr_el_Ghazal_\(region_of_South_Sudan\)) region of [South Sudan](32350676.md). [^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

Tonj North was divided into 9 [payams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payam_\(administrative_division\)) before the creation of the [32-state system](30852056.md). Currently the county consists of 16 payams. The former payams of Tonj North were granted county status.&#x20;
