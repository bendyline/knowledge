[Ivory Coast](57744.md), also known as Côte d'Ivoire, elects on national level a [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) – the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)) – and a [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature). The president is elected for a five-year term by the people. The [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Ivory_Coast) (*Assemblée Nationale*) has 225 members, elected for a five-year term in single-seat [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency). Côte d'Ivoire is a [one party dominant state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_party_dominant_state) with the [RHDP](48393945.md) in power. [Opposition](3379016.md) [parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_parties) are allowed, but are widely considered to have no real chance of gaining power. Following a peace deal between the government and former rebels in March 2007, the next elections were planned for early 2008. These elections however, were postponed to November 2009 first, and then to early 2010.

Ivory Coast is the 20th most electorally democratic nation (0.39 points) and the 22nd most liberally democratic nation (0.23 points) in Africa according to the [V-Dem Democracy Indices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V-Dem_Democracy_Indices), and is classified as an "electoral autocracy".
