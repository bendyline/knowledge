The **2026 Cook Out Southern 500** was a [NASCAR Cup Series](56571945.md) [race](22298383.md) held on September 6, 2026, at [Darlington Raceway](685467.md) in [Darlington, South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darlington,_South_Carolina) on the 1.366-mile (2.198 km) egg-shaped asphalt oval, it was the 27th race of the [2026 NASCAR Cup Series](79747972.md) season, the first race of the [NASCAR Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Chase), and 77th running of the event.

[Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won the race.[^cite_note-7] [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) finished 2nd, and [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) finished 3rd. [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) and [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) rounded out the top five, and [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe), [Josh Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Berry), [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md), [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano), and [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) rounded out the top ten.

## Report

### Background



[Darlington Raceway](685467.md) where the was officially held.

[Darlington Raceway](685467.md) is a [race track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_track) built for [NASCAR](21114.md) racing located near [Darlington, South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darlington,_South_Carolina). It is nicknamed "The Lady in Black" and "The Track Too Tough to Tame" by many NASCAR fans and also the drivers and advertised as "A NASCAR Tradition." It is of a unique, somewhat egg-shaped by design, an oval with the ends of very different configurations, a condition which supposedly arose from the proximity of one end of the track to a minnow pond the owner refused to relocate. This situation makes it very challenging for the crews to set up their cars' handling in a way that is effective at both ends of the track.

During the offseason, NASCAR announced that Darlington would now get the short track package, as opposed to running the intermediate package from previous years.[^cite_note-8]
