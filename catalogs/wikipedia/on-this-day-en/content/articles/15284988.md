The **prime minister of Niue** (officially the **premier of Niue** prior to 3 September 2024)[^cite_note-1] is [Niue](21228.md)'s [head of government](37570.md). They are elected by the [Niue Assembly](4504457.md), and form a [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Niue) consisting of themselves and three other members of the Assembly.[^cite_note-2]

Sir [Robert Rex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Rex) was continuously re-elected every three years from Niue's [associated statehood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Niue) in 1974 until his death in 1992.
