**Morocco national under-20 football team**, represents [Morocco](19291.md) in [association football](10568.md) at an under-20 age level and is controlled by the [Royal Moroccan Football Federation](1905329.md), the governing body for [football in Morocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_in_Morocco). The current coach is [Ludovic Batelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludovic_Batelli).

## History

On 24 December 2020, Morocco won the [2020 UNAF U-20 Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_UNAF_U-20_Tournament) and qualified for the [2021 U-20 Africa Cup of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_U-20_Africa_Cup_of_Nations).[^cite_note-1] In the 2021 U-20 Africa Cup of Nations, Morocco topped their group stage after winning 1-0 against Gambia,[^cite_note-2] drawing 0-0 with Ghana, and defeating Tanzania 2-0.[^cite_note-3] Their campaign ended after losing to Tunisia on penalties in the quarterfinals.[^cite_note-4]

In the [2025 U-20 Africa Cup of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_U-20_Africa_Cup_of_Nations), Morocco again finished first in their group. They started by beating Kenya 3-2 in an exciting match.[^cite_note-5] Then, they played a tough game against Nigeria that ended in a 0-0 draw.[^cite_note-6] In their last group match, they won 3-1 against Tunisia.[^cite_note-7] These results helped them move on to the knockout stage as the top team in their group. In the quarterfinal, Morocco beat Sierra Leone 1-0 in extra time.[^cite_note-8] In the semifinal, they defeated Egypt 1-0 to reach the final.[^cite_note-9] In the final, Morocco lost 1-0 to first time champions [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa_national_under-20_soccer_team).[^cite_note-10]

In the [2025 FIFA U-20 World Cup](73421401.md), Morocco advanced to the knockout stages after finishing first in their group. They defeated Spain 2-0 and Brazil 2-1,[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] but lost to Mexico 1-0.[^cite_note-13] In the Round of 16 they beat South Korea 2-1.[^cite_note-14] In the quarterfinals they defeated the USA 3-1.[^cite_note-15]In the semifinal on 15 October 2025, they beat France on penalties, securing a place in the World Cup final for the first time in their history.[^cite_note-16] On 20 October 2025, Morocco defeated Argentina 2–0 in the final, claiming their first-ever world title, the first for the Arab World and the second for Africa, after Ghana's triumph in 2009.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

On 22 October 2025, Crown Prince [Moulay Hassan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moulay_Hassan,_Crown_Prince_of_Morocco) hosted the national team at the royal palace to honor their World Cup victory.[^cite_note-19]

At the 2025 [CAF Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAF_Awards), the Morocco under-20 national team was named the **Best National Team of the Year**, becoming the first non-senior national side to receive the honour.[^cite_note-20]
