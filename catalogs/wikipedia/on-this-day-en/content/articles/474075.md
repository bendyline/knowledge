**Stavropol** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Ставрополь, IPA: [\[ˈstavrəpəlʲ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Russian)), known as **Voroshilovsk** from 1935 until 1943,[^cite_note-11] is a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) that is the [administrative centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_centre) of [Stavropol Krai](474074.md), in the [North Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Caucasus) region of [Southern Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Russia). As of the [2021 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Census_\(2021\)), its population was 547,820, and it is one of Russia's fastest growing cities.[^cite_note-2021Census-12]

## Etymology

The name *Stavropol* ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Ста́врополь) is a Russian rendering of the Greek name *[Stauropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stauroupolis_\(disambiguation\))* ([Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek_language): Σταυρούπολις 'City of the Cross'). According to legend, soldiers found a stone cross there while building the fortress in the city's future location.[^cite_note-stavmed-13] It is not related to Byzantine [Stauroupolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aphrodisias) (ancient Aphrodisias) in [Asia Minor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Minor), nor to the city of [Stavropol-on-Volga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolyatti) (now called Tolyatti).

## History



Early-20th-century view

It was founded on October 22, 1777,[^cite_note-Established-3] following the [Russo-Turkish War of 1768–1774](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Turkish_War_\(1768%E2%80%931774\)), as a military encampment,[^cite_note-stavmed-13] and was granted city status in 1785.[^cite_note-gr-4] Prince [Grigory Potemkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grigory_Potemkin), who founded Stavropol as one of ten fortresses built between [Azov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azov) and [Mozdok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozdok,_Republic_of_North_Ossetia%E2%80%93Alania) at the request of [Catherine the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_the_Great), played a leading role in the creation of the city. [Don Cossacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Cossacks), particularly those from the [Khopersky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khopersky?action=edit\&redlink=1) Regiment, settled the area in and around the cities of Stavropol and [Georgiyevsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgiyevsk) with a mission to defend borders of the Empire.[^cite_note-14]

[Alexander I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_I_of_Russia) in 1809 invited several [Armenian](10918072.md) families to settle by the fortress, in order to encourage trade in the region.

Stavropol's strategic location aided the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire)'s conquest of the [Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus). By the early 19th century the city grew into a busy trade center of the [North Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Caucasus). In 1843, an [Episcopal see](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Episcopal_see) of the [Russian Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Orthodox_Church) was established in Stavropol and in 1847 the city became the seat of [Stavropol Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavropol_Governorate).[^cite_note-15]

During the [Russian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War) the city changed hands several times and finally was captured by the [Red Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Army) from the [Volunteer Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volunteer_Army) of general [Anton Denikin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Denikin) on February 29, 1920. The city was renamed *Voroshilovsk* on May 5, 1935, after [Kliment Voroshilov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kliment_Voroshilov), but the original name was restored by decree on January 12, 1943.[^cite_note-Rename-16]

[World War II](32927.md) took a heavy toll on the city. Between August 3, 1942, and January 21, 1943, Stavropol was occupied by the [Germans](21212.md) and its airport was used as a base for the *[Luftwaffe](17885.md)* (German air force) to bomb Soviet oil supplies in [Grozny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grozny). The Germans operated a subcamp of the Dulag 102 [prisoner-of-war camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_prisoner-of-war_camps_in_World_War_II) in the city.[^cite_note-17] The city was liberated by the Soviet Army in January 1943.

[Natural gas](22131.md) began to be extracted near the city in 1946, and a pipeline was later built to supply [Moscow](19004.md).

## Administrative and municipal status

The [administrative divisions of Stavropol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Stavropol?action=edit\&redlink=1) consist of three districts:

- [Leninsky district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leninsky_district,_Stavropol?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Oktyabrsky district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oktyabrsky_district,_Stavropol?action=edit\&redlink=1)

- [Promishlenny district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promishlenny_district,_Stavropol?action=edit\&redlink=1)

Stavropol is the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of the [krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krai).[^cite_note-Ref371-2] Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), it is, together with one [rural locality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) (the *[khutor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khutor)* of [Grushevy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grushevy,_Stavropol_Krai?action=edit\&redlink=1)), incorporated as the **[city of krai significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance) of Stavropol**—an administrative unit with the status equal to that of the [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Stavropol_Krai).[^cite_note-Ref372-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), the city of krai significance of Stavropol is incorporated as **Stavropol Urban Okrug**.[^cite_note-Ref893-8]

## Economy

Stavropol's economy focuses on the production of automobiles, furniture, and construction equipment and materials. The city relies on air transport through [Shpakovskoye airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavropol_Shpakovskoye_Airport) as well as rail and highway connections to other Russian cities.

## Demographics

Stavropol's 2021 population is now estimated at 453,387. In 1950, the population of Stavropol was 108,353. Stavropol has grown by 3,293 since 2015, which represents a 0.73% annual change.

## Facilities

Stavropol has a theater and an [association football](10568.md) team called [FC Dynamo Stavropol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Dynamo_Stavropol).

The main educational institutions of the town include [North-Caucasus Federal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-Caucasus_Federal_University), [Stavropol State Agrarian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavropol_State_Agrarian_University), and [Stavropol State Medical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavropol_State_Medical_University).

The area of Russia in which Stavropol resides is very mountainous, placing the city in the midst of the northern [Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus) mountain range.&#x20;

The city, like many other Russian cities has its own [botanical garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botanical_garden), which covers up to 18 hectares, including 16 hectares of natural woods.[^cite_note-stavmed-13][^cite_note-18]

## Climate

Stavropol experiences a [humid continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate) (*Dfa*), using the 0 °C (32 °F) isotherm, with short but cold winters (though mild for Russia) and hot summers. [Precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation) is rather low, with a 562 millimeters (22.1 in) annual average.

Stavropol is not protected by the mountains in the winter months, so it can frequently get very cold. The lowest temperature recorded in Stavropol was −28.3 °C (−18.9 °F) on [8 February 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_2012_European_cold_wave), while the highest was +39.7 °C (103.5 °F) on [8 August 2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_European_heat_wave#In_Russia).

## Notable people

Well-known Russians who have visited or resided in Stavropol include: Generals [Alexander Suvorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Suvorov) (1730–1800), [Alexey Yermolov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksey_Yermolov_\(general\)) (1777–1861), and [Nikolay Raevsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolay_Raevsky) (1771–1829); the poets [Alexander Pushkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Pushkin) (1799–1837)[^cite_note-21] and [Mikhail Lermontov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Lermontov) (1814–1841),[^cite_note-22] who were in political disfavor, the surgeon [Nikolay Pirogov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolay_Pirogov) (1810–1881), [Alexander Griboyedov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Griboyedov) (1795–1829),[^cite_note-23] [Leo Tolstoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Tolstoy) (1828–1910),[^cite_note-24] and the national poet of [Ossetia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ossetia), [Kosta Khetagurov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosta_Khetagurov).[^cite_note-25]

The first and only executive president of the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), [Mikhail Gorbachev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gorbachev) (1931–2022), was born in Stavropol Krai and spent several years working in the city of Stavropol as the head of the [krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krai)'s administration. Gorbachev's chief mentor, long-time friend, and predecessor [Yuri Andropov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Andropov) (1914–1984) was also born in Stavropol Krai.

Actor [Lev Gorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lev_Gorn) (born 1971) star of *[The Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Americans)*, and [Serge de Sazo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serge_de_Sazo) (1915–2012) Russian born French photographer, were born in Stavropol.

An abbess of the Catholic Church of the Byzantine Rite, [Serafima Meletieva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serafima_Meletieva) was born in 1886 at Stavropol.

Piotr Mikhailovich Skarżyński (1744–1805), A Russian Major General. He served in the cavalry units of the Russian army and he commanded the Buzhan Cossacks during the Russo-Turkish War of 1787–1791, he showed heroism during the assault on Ochakov and the defense of the Kinburn fortress. He was awarded the [Order of St. George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Saint_George_\(Russia\)). He owned an estate in Stavropol where he made wine.[^cite_note-26]

Major General [Pavel Klimenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Klimenko) was born in Stavropol, he was killed during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) in November 2024.[^cite_note-27]

[Yevgeni Sergeyevich Dukhnov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yevgeni_Sergeyevich_Dukhnov) (born September 1986) former professional football player.
