**Agwara** (or **Agwarra**) is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Niger State](1723431.md), [Nigeria](21383.md).

It has an area of 1,538 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 57,413 at the 2006 [census](6889.md).

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 923.[^cite_note-1]

## Climate

The climate is hot and oppressive, with a wet season that is [overcast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overcast) and partly [cloudy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud), and a dry season that is partly cloudy.[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-3]

A daily maximum [temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature) of 36 °C (97 °F) is typical during the 2.3-month hot season, which runs from February 24 to May 2. April has an average high temperature of 37 °C (99 °F) and low temperature of 27 °C (80 °F), making it the hottest month of the year in Agwara.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-\:0-2]

A daily high temperature below 31 °C (88 °F) is typical during the 2.8-month cool season, which runs from July 3 to September 27. December, with an average low of 19 °C (66 °F) and high of 32 °C (90 °F), is the [coldest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold) month of the year in Agwara.[^cite_note-\:0-2]
