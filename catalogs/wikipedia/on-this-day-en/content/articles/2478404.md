The **Páez people**, also known as the **Nasa**, are a [Indigenous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_of_the_Americas) people who live in the southwestern highlands of [Colombia](5222.md), especially in the [Cauca Department](549662.md), but also the [Caquetá Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caquet%C3%A1_Department) lowlands and [Tierradentro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierradentro).[^cite_note-every-1]

## Religion

In the early 1900s, [Lazarists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazarists) built missions among the Paez and began the work to convert them to [Christianity](5211.md). [Jesuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuits) had originally tried to convert the Paez, but failed. However, the Lazarists met some success. The Paez developed a syncretic form of [Roman Catholicism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholicism) that absorbed their Indigenous [religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion). For example, the Paez have [shamans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamans) but many have also become [Roman Catholic priests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_priest).[^cite_note-religion-2]

## Climate adaptation

Further information: [Andean textiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andean_textiles)

Since they live in the cold [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) of the Andes, the Paez build their homes using brick, metal, cement, and wood. The Paez women raise and shear sheep for wool. They clean the wool, spin yarn from it, dye it and knit clothes and blankets for their families.

## Punishment

Punishment for wrongs is strict in the Paez culture. In June 2000, the Paez whipped local governor Ermes Taqninaf and his mistress Rubiela Yetacue 17 strokes with a knotted leather whip in the town of [Santander de Quilichao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santander_de_Quilichao). "As a Paez Indian", Taqninaf said, "I'm proud to have received this punishment because it is glorifying my race." Stripping the one being punished of his clothing and dunking him in a cold mountain lake is another form of punishment—this was done to Senator [Jesús Pinacue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jes%C3%BAs_Pinacue?action=edit\&redlink=1) in 1998, a Paez who defied the community by supporting a political candidate in a presidential vote.[^cite_note-3]

## Economy

Many Paez are agriculturists and common crops grown include potatoes, coffee, cassava, plantains, coca, and hemp.[^cite_note-4]

## Present day

The Paez people have been targeted by both rebels and paramilitary groups in the [Colombian conflict](1857628.md). In 2019 alone, 36 members of the tribe were murdered, and in July 2019 there were a reported 57 attacks on the Paez and other Indigenous groups in Colombia.[^cite_note-5] In October 2020, they rallied in the Colombian capital, [Bogotá](211271.md), due to an increase in violence in their territories.[^cite_note-6]
