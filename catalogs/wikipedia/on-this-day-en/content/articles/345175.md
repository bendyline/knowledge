**Malians** are the citizens of [Mali](19127.md).[^cite_note-8] As Mali is located at the cultural crossroads of North Africa and West Africa, Malians include a range of West African ethnicities such as the [Bambara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambara_people), [Fula](102383.md), [Senufo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senufo_people), [Malinke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malinke_people), [Songhai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songhai_people) and [Bobo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobo_people) peoples but also a range of groups with origins in the Middle East and North Africa such as [Hassaniya Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beni_%E1%B8%A4ass%C4%81n) and [Malian Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malian_Jews), primarily around the city of [Timbuktu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbuktu).
