On 18 November 2025, a [vehicle-ramming attack](44318569.md) and [mass stabbing](60784649.md) left at least three people killed, including both perpetrators, in the [Gush Etzion Junction](28979076.md), West Bank.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] Three others were injured, including a child.[^cite_note-Jpost-3] [Palestinian Islamic Jihad](13093253.md) took credit for the attack.[^cite_note-Jpost-3]

## Attack

Two Palestinian men, identified as Walid Sabarneh of [Beit Omar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beit_Omar) and Amran al-Atrash of [Hebron](38577.md), arrived by car at the [Gush Etzion Junction](28979076.md) at around 2 p.m.. They proceeded to turn right toward [Jerusalem](16043.md) before accelerating toward the [hitchhiking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitchhiking) post and attempting to [strike civilians](44318569.md). The attackers exited the vehicle and attempted to stab those in the area.[^cite_note-4] A 60-year-old man was stabbed to death, and a man and child were both injured in the attacks.[^cite_note-Jpost-3][^cite_note-5]

The perpetrators were shot and killed by an IDF soldier.[^cite_note-Jpost-3]

## Aftermath

[Palestinian Islamic Jihad](13093253.md) took credit for the attack and congratulated the perpetrators, urging others to participate in similar attacks.[^cite_note-Jpost-3] [Hamas](13913.md) said the attack was "a natural response to Israel’s attempts to eliminate the Palestinian cause and to the rampage of the occupation and the settlers in the West Bank".[^cite_note-6]
