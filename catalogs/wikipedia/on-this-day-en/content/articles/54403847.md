**Cleveland** is a suburb of [Johannesburg](58893.md), [South Africa](17416221.md). It is now a [light industrial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_industry) suburb. It is located in Region F of the [City of Johannesburg Metropolitan Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Johannesburg_Metropolitan_Municipality).

## History

The suburb is situated on part of an old [Witwatersrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witwatersrand) farm called *Doornfontein*.[^cite_note-Dict01-2] It was established in 1903 and was named after [Cleveland](5951.md), [Ohio](22199.md). The suburb was the location for storage of mining equipment by American firms.[^cite_note-Dict01-2]

In June 2026, twelve people died in a mass shooting in Jumpers, an informal village in Cleveland.[^cite_note-BBC-3]
