The **Ministry of Energy** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الطاقة) is a [government ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) in [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) and part of [the cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Saudi_Arabia). It is responsible for developing and implementing policies concerning [petroleum](23195.md) and [related products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleum_products). The Ministry of Energy is working to diversify the [national energy](20837076.md) mix used in [electricity production](9540.md), increasing the share of [natural gas](22131.md) and [renewable energy](25784.md) sources to approximately 50% by [2030](50299103.md) while reducing the use of [liquid fuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid_fuel).[^cite_note-1]

In August 2019, [King Salman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salman_of_Saudi_Arabia) issued a royal decree and divided the Ministry of Petroleum and Mineral Resources into two: [Ministry of Industry and Mineral Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Industry_and_Mineral_Resources_\(Saudi_Arabia\)), and Ministry of Energy. While [Khalid al-Falih](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalid_A._Al-Falih) still remained the energy minister, business executive Bandar Al-Khorayef was named as the minister of natural resources.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3] However, on 8 September 2019, a royal decree was issued to appoint [Abdulaziz bin Salman Al Saud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulaziz_bin_Salman_Al_Saud) as the energy minister.[^cite_note-4] On 11 July 2026 he was appointed as Minister of Industry and Natural Resources in addition to his position as Minister of Energy.

## History

The ministry was established in December 1960.[^cite_note-Oron-5][^cite_note-rad-6] Prior to the formation of the ministry policies regarding oil production and planning were overseen by the directorate general of petroleum and mineral affairs which was attached to the ministry of finance.[^cite_note-sher-7] Then the directorate was converted into the ministry.[^cite_note-sher-7] The ministry was named the Ministry of Petroleum and Mineral Resources until May 2016 when it was renamed as the Ministry of Energy, Industry and Mineral Resources.[^cite_note-reut-8][^cite_note-9] In August 2019, the Ministry was separated into the Ministry of Energy and the Ministry of Industry and Mineral Resources.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Organization and activities

The ministry is primarily responsible for the policies concerning oil and gas in the country which is the world's largest holder of crude oil reserves.[^cite_note-eia-13] One of the agencies with which the ministry works is [Petromin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petromin_Corporation), the general petroleum and mineral organization.[^cite_note-rad-6] Through [Saudi Arabian Basic Industries Company](1929210.md) (SABIC), established in 1976, the ministry oversaw the operation of [petrochemicals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrochemicals) and other [heavy industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_industry) projects.[^cite_note-sher-7]

In 2019, the Minister inaugurated the launching ceremony of the Saudi scientific vessel, called “Najil” in [Jubail](632008.md) port, [Eastern Province](468721.md). The main aim of the vessel is to conduct marine-related researches in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md) and the [Red Sea](26191.md).<http://saudigazette.com.sa/article/557646/SAUDI-ARABIA/Scientific-research-vessel-Najil-launched-in-Jubail>

In 2019, and on the sidelines of the Saudi crown prince’s visit to India, the Minister of Energy, Industry and Mineral Resources signed a MOU to partner with the [International Solar Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Solar_Alliance) (ISA) and projects in fertilizers, pharmaceuticals and medical supplies.[^cite_note-14]
