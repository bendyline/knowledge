**Macclesfield vs Crystal Palace** was an [FA Cup](11237.md) [third round](79604093.md#Third_round) match played on 10 January 2026 at [Moss Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moss_Rose), [Macclesfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macclesfield) between [National League North](645482.md) side [Macclesfield](66155008.md) and [Premier League](11250.md) side [Crystal Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_Palace_Football_Club). In a match widely regarded as the biggest [upset](1169845.md) in the [history of the competition](14692370.md#Minnows,_cup_runs_and_giant_killings) as well as one of the biggest upsets in the history of [football](10568.md),[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-Lequipe-2][^cite_note-TheGuardian-3][^cite_note-LeParisien-4] Macclesfield defeated [reigning FA Cup holders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_FA_Cup_final) [Crystal Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_Palace_Football_Club) 2–1.[^cite_note-5]

This was the first time since the formation of the Premier League that a [level 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football_league_system#The_system) side had defeated a side from the top division. This also made Crystal Palace the first defending champions to lose against a [non-League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-League_football) team since [Wolverhampton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverhampton_Wanderers_F.C.) lost against Crystal Palace themselves in [1909](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1908%E2%80%9309_FA_Cup).[^cite_note-6] It was also the biggest upset in terms of league placings (117 places between the two sides) in FA Cup history,[^cite_note-7] a record previously held by [Chasetown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chasetown_F.C.) vs [Port Vale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Vale_F.C.) in a [2007–08 FA Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_FA_Cup) 2nd round replay (108 places).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

The match was Crystal Palace captain [Marc Guéhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Gu%C3%A9hi)'s final game for the club.

As a [National League North](645482.md) team, Macclesfield started in the second qualifying round, where they were drawn with [North West Counties League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_West_Counties_League) side [Atherton Laburnum Rovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atherton_Laburnum_Rovers) at home. They won the match 3–0 due to goals from [Sean Etaluku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Etaluku), [Ethan McLeod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_McLeod) and [D'Mani Mellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D'Mani_Mellor).[^cite_note-10] In the third qualifying round, they were drawn with [Northern Premier League Division One West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Premier_League#Division_One_West) side [Nantwich Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nantwich_Town) at home, where they won the match 2–0 due to goals from [Josh Kay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Kay) and [Danny Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Elliott_\(footballer\)).[^cite_note-11] In the fourth qualifying round, they were drawn with [Southern League Premier Division Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Football_League#Premier_Central) side [Stamford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stamford_A.F.C.) at home. They won the match 1–0 due to a goal from Paul Dawson,[^cite_note-12] sending the phoenix club to the FA Cup for the first time in history.&#x20;

In the first round proper, they were drawn with [National League South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_South) side [A.F.C. Totton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.F.C._Totton) at home. They won the match 6–3 with goals from Paul Dawson, [Rollin Menayese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollin_Menayese), Lewis Fensome and a hat-trick from Elliott.[^cite_note-13] In the second round proper, they were drawn with [National League South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_League_South) side [Slough Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slough_Town) away. After goals from Elliott, [James Edmondson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Edmondson_\(footballer\)) and [Regan Griffiths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regan_Griffiths), they won the match 3–1 [after extra time](633242.md#Association_football) to set up the game with [Crystal Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_Palace_Football_Club).[^cite_note-14]

On 17 December, between the second and third rounds, striker [Ethan McLeod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_McLeod) died in a car accident on the way home from the league match against [Bedford Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford_Town_F.C.).[^cite_note-15]

### Crystal Palace

As a [Premier League](11250.md) team, Crystal Palace were automatically given a [bye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bye_\(sports\)) to the third round of the FA Cup.
