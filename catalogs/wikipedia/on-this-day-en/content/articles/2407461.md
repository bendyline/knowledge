**Semeru** is the highest [mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain) on the [Indonesian](14579.md) island of [Java](69336.md) and an active [volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcano) located in the province of [East Java](320443.md) in a [subduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subduction) zone, where the [Indo-Australian Plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Australian_Plate) subducts under the [Eurasian Plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasian_Plate). Semeru is 3,676 m tall at its peak, making it the third tallest volcano mountain in Indonesia after [Kerinci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Kerinci) and [Rinjani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Rinjani).[^cite_note-3]

The name "Semeru" is derived from [Meru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Meru_\(mythology\)), the central world mountain in [Hinduism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism), or [Sumeru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumeru), the abode of [gods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirty-three_gods). This [stratovolcano](162404.md) is **Mahameru**, meaning "The Great Mountain" in [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-GVPSmith01-1]

## Geology

Semeru rises steeply above the coastal plains of eastern Java. [Maars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maar) containing [crater lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanic_crater_lake) have formed along a line through the volcano's summit. It was formed south of the overlapping Ajek-ajek and Jambangan [calderas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caldera).[^cite_note-gvp-5] The eruptive products are [andesitic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andesite).[^cite_note-6] Semeru lies at the south end of the [Tengger Volcanic Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tengger_Volcanic_Complex).

## Eruptive history

Semeru's eruptive history is extensive. Since 1818, at least 61 eruptive periods have been recorded[^cite_note-7] (11 of which resulted in fatalities) consisting of lava and [pyroclastic flows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyroclastic_flow). Almost all historical eruptions have had a [Volcanic Explosivity Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanic_Explosivity_Index) (VEI) of 2 or 3.[^cite_note-8] Semeru has been in a state of near-constant eruption from 1967 to the present.[^cite_note-9] The volcano's most recent eruption occurred in 2025.[^cite_note-10]

### 2021 eruption

Main article: [2021 Semeru eruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Semeru_eruption)

In 2021, Semeru erupted in January, and on 4 and 6 December.[^cite_note-11] The eruption caused 11 km (6.8 mi) high ejections, lava flows for 5–11.5 km (3.1–7.1 mi) and ash rains for 30 km (19 mi).[^cite_note-Bachri2024-12]

At least 57 people died,[^cite_note-Mubyarsah-13] 104 more were injured, while 23 were unaccounted for.[^cite_note-14] More than 10,000 people were displaced, and 1,027 houses, 43 public facilities and two bridges affected.[^cite_note-Bachri2024-12]

### 2022 eruption

Monsoon rains in 2022 and 2023 led to the collapse of the lava dome; Semeru erupted again on 4 December 2022 with a pyroclastic flow of 12 km (7.5 mi) length, a pyroclastic earthquake and 13 eruptions earthquakes.[^cite_note-Bachri2024-12]

### 2025 eruption



Aerial view Mount Semeru eruption on 19 November 2025

On 19 November 2025, Semeru erupted with a pyroclastic flow over the southern slope[^cite_note-15] that reached 7 km (4.3 mi), along with an ash cloud that reached a height of 2 km (1.2 mi).[^cite_note-16] As of 20 November 2025, three people were injured, 1,156 were displaced, and 200 houses along with one school building were damaged.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]

## Legends

Semeru is named after [Sumeru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Meru_\(mythology\)), the central world mountain in [Hinduism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism). As stated in legend, it was transplanted from [India](14533.md) to create the island of Java; the tale is recorded in the 15th-century East Javanese work *[Tantu Pagelaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tantu_Pagelaran)*. It was originally placed in the western part of the island, but that caused the island to tip, so the gods moved it eastward. On that journey, parts kept coming off the lower rim, forming the mountains [Lawu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawu), [Wilis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Wilis), [Kelud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelud), [Kawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Kawi), [Arjuno and Welirang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arjuno-Welirang). The damage thus caused to the foot of the mountain caused it to shake, and the top came off and created [Penanggungan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penanggungan) as well.[^cite_note-19] Indonesian Hindus also hold a belief that the mountain is the abode of [Shiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiva) in Java.[^cite_note-20]

## Floral problems

### Non-native invasive plants

25 non-native plants have been found in Mount Semeru National Park.[^cite_note-wid-21] The [non-native plants](67554.md), which threaten the [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemism) [local plants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_plants), were imported via many ways in the colonial era.[^cite_note-22]<sup>: 26–27 </sup> Around 1906-1907, M. Buysman, a Dutch botanical park owner in Nongkojajar in western slope of Tengger Mts., developed a huge collection of introduced plants in his park, which later spread widely to the surrounding areas.[^cite_note-23]<sup>: 57–59 </sup> They include *[Foeniculum vulgare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fennel)*, *[Verbena brasiliensis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbena_brasiliensis)*, *[Chromolaena odorata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromolaena_odorata)*, and *[Salvinia molesta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvinia_molesta)*.[^cite_note-wid-21]

### Vegetable plantations

Mud [erosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erosion) from surrounding vegetable plantations is adding silt to [Ranu Pani Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranu_Pani), causing the lake to gradually shrink. Research has predicted that the lake will disappear by about 2025, unless the [vegetable plantations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plantation) on the hillsides are replaced with more ecologically sustainable [perennials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perennials).[^cite_note-24]
