**Mohammad Abdul Muhit** ([Bengali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_language): মোহাম্মদ আব্দুল মুহিত; born 5 October 1969), known as **M. A. Muhit** (এম. এ. মুহিত),[^cite_note-1] is a Bangladeshi physician and politician from the [Bangladesh Nationalist Party](309760.md). He is the incumbent [state minister of health and family welfare](40314029.md) and [member of parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament_\(Bangladesh\)) (MP) representing the [Sirajganj-6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirajganj-6) constituency.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:2-3]

## Early life and education

Mohammad Abdul Muhit was born on 5 October 1969 in [Dhaka](56656.md) to [M. A. Matin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_Abdul_Matin), a former [deputy prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Bangladesh) and [minister of home affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Home_Affairs_\(Bangladesh\)) of Bangladesh, and Tashmina Mahmood, a physician and daughter of [Abdullah al Mahmood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_al_Mahmood), former minister of industries and natural resources of Pakistan.[^cite_note-4]

Muhit completed his undergraduate medical training ([MBBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MBBS)) at [Dhaka Medical College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhaka_Medical_College_Hospital) and pursued higher studies in the United Kingdom.

Muhit earned a Master of Science (MSc) in clinical ophthalmology from the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) and a Master of Science (MSc) in community ophthalmology from [University College London](52029.md), and holds a PhD in public health ophthalmology from the [London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Hygiene_&_Tropical_Medicine).[^cite_note-\:0-5]

## Career

Muhit is an [ophthalmologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophthalmologist) and public health specialist who has worked in eye health, disability services, and higher education administration. He is the chairman and former pro-vice-chancellor of the [University of South Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Asia,_Bangladesh),[^cite_note-6] vice-chairman at North Bengal Medical College in [Sirajganj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirajganj),[^cite_note-\:1-7] and president of the BNSB Eye Hospitals, Sirajganj.[^cite_note-8]

Muhit's published research focuses on childhood eye disease and blindness, barriers to accessing eye care, and childhood disability and rehabilitation in Bangladesh.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

Muhit is the founder and president of CSF Global (formerly the Child Sight Foundation),[^cite_note-9] a non-profit organisation that links children with disabilities to medical and eye care treatment, conducts research, and provides disability services.[^cite_note-10]

## Political career

Muhit is a politician of the [Bangladesh Nationalist Party](309760.md) in [Shahjadpur Upazila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahjadpur_Upazila), Sirajganj District. He first contested the [Sirajganj-6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirajganj-6) seat in the [2018 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Bangladeshi_general_election) as a BNP candidate.[^cite_note-11]

In the [2026 Bangladeshi general election](72614737.md), he won the [Sirajganj-6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirajganj-6) seat and joined the [cabinet of Tarique Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarique_ministry) as the [state minister of health and family welfare](40314029.md).[^cite_note-\:2-3]

## Family

Muhit's father, [Mohammed Abdul Matin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_Abdul_Matin), was an ophthalmologist and politician who served as a [deputy prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Bangladesh) of Bangladesh and held multiple cabinet posts, including [health](40314029.md) and [home affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Home_Affairs_\(Bangladesh\)). He is the founder of the [University of South Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Asia,_Bangladesh) and the founder-chairman of North Bengal Medical College in Sirajganj.[^cite_note-12]

Muhit's mother, Tashmina Mahmood (also known as Tashmina Matin), was an [obstetrician and gynaecologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obstetrician_and_gynaecologist) who served as the founding principal of North Bengal Medical College, Sirajganj, and later chaired the Board of Management of the [University of South Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Asia,_Bangladesh) in Dhaka. His elder brother, M. A. Muqueet, is a cardiologist and the chairman of the Department of Cardiology at [Bangladesh Medical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Medical_University).[^cite_note-\:1-7]

His maternal grandfather, [Abdullah al Mahmood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_al_Mahmood), was a lawyer and politician who served as the [minister for industries and natural resources of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Industries_and_Production).[^cite_note-13] His maternal uncle, [Iqbal Hassan Mahmood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iqbal_Hassan_Mahmood), is a [Bangladesh Nationalist Party](309760.md) national standing committee member, a member of parliament for [Sirajganj-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirajganj-2), and the [minister of power, energy, and mineral resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Power,_Energy_and_Mineral_Resources) in the [cabinet of Tarique Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarique_ministry).[^cite_note-14]
