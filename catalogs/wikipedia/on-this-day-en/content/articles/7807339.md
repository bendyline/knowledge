**Los Gigantes** is a [resort town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resort_town) in the [Santiago del Teide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_del_Teide) municipality on the west coast of the [Canary Island](5717.md) [Tenerife](269223.md). Its main feature are the giant [rock formations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_formation), [Acantilados de Los Gigantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acantilados_de_Los_Gigantes), that rise from the sea to a height of 500–800 [metres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metre) (1,640–2,625 ft) after which the town has been named. Los Gigantes means "The Giants".

Nearby to the south are the resorts of [Puerto de Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_de_Santiago?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Playa de la Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playa_de_la_Arena). Three kilometres (1.9 mi) to the north is the village of [Masca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masca) which can be reached by boat or car.

The town has a [marina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina) surrounded by concrete walls to dampen the effect of the rough seas around the area. A small [black sand beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_sand_beach) lies between the rocks and the marina. The marina has a boulevard with many restaurants. Near the harbour is a [lido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lido_\(swimming_pool\)).

The cliffs at Los Gigantes, [Acantilados de Los Gigantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acantilados_de_Los_Gigantes) in Spanish, are one of the island's major [tourist attractions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourist_attraction). There are three viewpoints in the village, the main one on main road to towards [Tamaimo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamaimo?action=edit\&redlink=1), another above the sports club and a third on a coastal path around the Hotel Barcelo. The village has many small shops and restaurants servicing the tourists that visit the area, especially on the one-way road loop around church and plaza, and also near to the marina and beach.

Each year, in February, there is a carnival[^cite_note-1] which parades through the village. As can be expected of a town resort, Los Gigantes has only one hotel but many apartments compared to regular residences.
