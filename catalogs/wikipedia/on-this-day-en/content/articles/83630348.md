**Nito** **de Sousa Viegas D'Abreu** (Portuguese pronunciation: [\['ni.tu dɨ 'sow.zɐ 'vjɛ.ɣɐʒ ˈða.βɾew\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Portuguese), born in 1982 or 1983 in [Almerim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almeirim,_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9_and_Principe))[^cite_note-1] is a [Santomean](20026306.md) politician currently serving in the [National Assembly of São Tomé and Príncipe](2960234.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3] He also serves as the parliamentary leader for the [Independent Democratic Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Democratic_Action) party and was a candidate for the [2026 Presidential elections](79840944.md).**[^cite_note-\:0-3]**[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]
