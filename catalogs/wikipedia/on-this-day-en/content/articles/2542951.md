**Gia Lai** is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Vietnam) in [South Central Coast and Central Highlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Central_Coast_and_Central_Highlands) region of [Vietnam](202354.md), at an average altitude of 700–800 m (2,300–2,600 ft) above sea level, with an area of 21,576.53 km<sup>2</sup> (8,331 sq mi) and a population of 3,583,693 people in 2025.[^cite_note-Quốc_hội-2] It borders [Quảng Ngãi](2015207.md) in the north, [East Sea](74209.md) to the east, [Đắk Lắk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%90%E1%BA%AFk_L%E1%BA%AFk) in the south and [Ratanakiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratanakiri) of [Cambodia](334751.md) in the west.

As of 2025, Gia Lai province is the 18th most populous administrative unit in [Vietnam](202354.md) with a population of 4,182,700 people. It ranks 30th in terms of Gross Regional Domestic Product (GRDP), 33rd in per capita GRDP, and 33rd in GRDP growth rate. In 2020, the GRDP reached 80,000.32 billion VND, with a per capita GRDP of 51.9 million VND and a GRDP growth rate of 8.00%.[^cite_note-5]

Under Resolution 202/2025/QH15, a restructuring took place in 2025, with the former Gia Lai and [Bình Định](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%ACnh_%C4%90%E1%BB%8Bnh_province) provinces officially merged into one single administrative unit. The unified province retained the name Gia Lai. This merger created Vietnam's second-largest province, covering over 21,576 km<sup>2</sup> with a population exceeding 3.5 million people. The administrative center is located in [Quy Nhơn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quy_Nhon) (the former capital of Bình Định), while [Pleiku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleiku) remains a cultural and economic hub.[^cite_note-6]

## Name

Its name **Gia-lai** or **Giá-rai** in [Kinh language](32511.md) from an ancient name **Yăgrai** in [Jarai language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarai_language). It was often known as "[the water dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_dragon)" what is the official name of [Jarai people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarai_people). However, it was also the title of [Po Klaung Yăgrai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Po_Klong_Garai), who was worshiped by Jarai clans during thousands years. According to the indigenous tradition, the [Jarai community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarai_people) often organizes the festival to honor his unexpected attack on the [Khmer empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_empire). This area is still thought to be a pedal and a logistics gathering place to serve the glorious victory.

## Transport

The province has 2 airports:

- [Phu Cat Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phu_Cat_Airport) (serving Quy Nhon city)

- [Pleiku Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleiku_Airport) (serving [Pleiku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleiku))
