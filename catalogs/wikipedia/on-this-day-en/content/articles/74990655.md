**James Cecil Pearce Jr.** (born October 12, 2003) is an American professional [football](18951490.md) [defensive end](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensive_end) for the [Atlanta Falcons](2103.md) of the [National Football League](21211.md) (NFL). He played [college football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_football) for the [Tennessee Volunteers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_Volunteers_football) and selected by the Falcons in the first round of the [2025 NFL draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NFL_draft).

## Early life

Pearce Jr. was born and grew up in [Charlotte, North Carolina](57447.md).[^cite_note-1] He attended [Julius L. Chambers High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_L._Chambers_High_School) in Charlotte.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] As a senior, he had 14.5 sacks and two interceptions. He helped lead his team to back-to-back [North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_High_School_Athletic_Association) 4AA state championships in 2019 and 2020.[^cite_note-4] A four-star prospect, he committed to the [University of Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee) to play college football.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## College career

As a true freshman at Tennessee in 2022, Pearce Jr. played in all 13 games and had five tackles and two sacks. He returned to Tennessee for his sophomore year in 2023.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] During the 2023 season, he tied with two others in the [SEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_Conference) for the most sacks with ten and fifth in the SEC regarding tackles for loss.[^cite_note-9] In the Citrus Bowl against Iowa, he recorded a 52-yard interception return for a touchdown.[^cite_note-10]

### 2025

Pearce was drafted in the first round with the 26th overall pick in the [2025 NFL draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NFL_draft) by the [Atlanta Falcons](2103.md).[^cite_note-13] The Falcons traded up with the [Los Angeles Rams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Rams) to pick Pearce, giving up their 2026 first-round pick as well as a 2025 third and 2025 seventh.[^cite_note-14] In Week 2 against the [Minnesota Vikings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Vikings) Pearce recorded his first career sack, taking down [Vikings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Vikings) QB [J. J. McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._McCarthy).[^cite_note-15] He was named NFL Defensive Rookie of the Month for December.[^cite_note-16] He finished the season with 26 tackles, 10.5 sacks, and a forced fumble.[^cite_note-17] He led all rookies in sacks and set a franchise record for most sacks in a season by a rookie.[^cite_note-18] He was named to the [PFWA NFL All-Rookie Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PFWA_NFL_All-Rookie_Team) for the [2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NFL_season).[^cite_note-19]

### 2026

On August 14, 2026, the NFL announced that Pearce would be suspended for eight regular season games due to violating the leagues personal conduct policy following his arrest in February due to a domestic dispute.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

## Legal issues

In December of 2023, Pearce was arrested in [Knoxville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knoxville,_Tennessee) and charged with speeding, driving on a suspended license, failure to present insurance, registration improperly displayed and improper window tinting, which were all labeled as misdemeanors. Pearce’s charges were dropped on December 12, 2023, and the license, insurance and window tinting charges were dismissed while the speeding charge was dismissed after Pearce paid costs of \$278.[^cite_note-22]

On February 7, 2026, Pearce was arrested in [Miami-Dade County, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami-Dade_County,_Florida), on two charges of aggravated battery with a deadly weapon and one count of aggravated stalking, with the victim being WNBA player [Rickea Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rickea_Jackson). He was also charged with fleeing and eluding police officers, aggravated battery of a law enforcement officer and resisting an officer without violence to his person.[^cite_note-23]

On April 8, 2026, Falcons coach [Kevin Stefanski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Stefanski) said that Pearce would not be with the team at the start of the voluntary offseason workout program as a result of his ongoing legal issues.[^cite_note-24]
