# Belarus–North Korea relations

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| [Belarus](3457.md) | [North Korea](21255.md) |
|  |  |

**Belarus–North Korea** relations are the [bilateral relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilateral_relations) between [Belarus](3457.md) and [North Korea](21255.md).

## History

North Korean President [Kim Il Sung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Il_Sung) visited [Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minsk), the capital of the [Belarusian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusian_SSR), in 1984. During the visit, he visited the Minsk Tractor Works and the [Brest Fortress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brest_Fortress).[^cite_note-1]

Diplomatic relations were established in 1992.[^cite_note-\:25-2] Since 2016, North Korea has operated an embassy in [Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minsk),[^cite_note-\:25-2][^cite_note-3] while Belarus has a consulate in [Hamgyong-namdo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Hamgyong_Province), despite the fact that the Belarusian government recognizes this as a trade mission and works through other representatives in [Moscow](19004.md) (Russia).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

In 2020, Belarusian President [Alexander Lukashenko](156589.md) suggested improving the relations between North Korea and Belarus, stating that the relations are on an "unreasonably low level". He stated that the relations should be improved with a focus on "medication and food production, professional training and professional development".[^cite_note-7]

In March 2026, President Lukashenko visited North Korea, marking his first state visit to North Korea, as well as the first visit to the country by a Belarusian President. He was greeted by North Korean Vice Premier [Kim Tok-hun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Tok-hun) at the [Pyongyang International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyongyang_International_Airport). Lukashenko visited the [Kumsusan Palace of the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumsusan_Palace_of_the_Sun), where he laid flowers at the resting place of former leaders Kim Il Sung and [Kim Jong Il](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Jong_Il). He then met with North Korean leader [Kim Jong Un](5962511.md), and took part in a ceremony at [Kim Il Sung Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Il_Sung_Square).[^cite_note-8] During the meeting, the two sides signed a friendship and cooperation treaty.[^cite_note-9]
