# Acting president

&#x20; An **acting president** is a [person who temporarily fills](691917.md) the role of a country's [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)) when the [incumbent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incumbent) president is unavailable (such as by illness or visiting abroad) or when the post is vacant (such as for death, [resignation](481387.md) or removal from office).
