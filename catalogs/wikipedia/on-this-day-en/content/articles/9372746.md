**Sabuwa** (or **Sabua**) is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Katsina State](2227638.md), [Nigeria](21383.md) on the border with [Kaduna State](2227637.md) at[11°10′00″N 7°07′00″E  /  11.16667°N 7.11667°E  / 11.16667; 7.11667](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Sabuwa\&params=11_10_00_N_7_07_00_E_region\:NG_type\:city_source\:GNS-enwiki).

It has an area of 642 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 136,050 at the 2006 census.

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 830.[^cite_note-1]

The representative of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_Nigeria) for the Faskari/Kankara/Sabuwa constituency is [mai nauyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mai_nauyi?action=edit\&redlink=1).

## Climate/Temperature

In April, Sabuwa experiences highs above 93 °F, while in June and October, lows fall below 84 °F.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3]
