# Petrochemical industry



Jampilen Petrochemical co., [Asaluyeh](3393482.md), [Iran](14653.md)

The **petrochemical industry** produces and trades in [petrochemicals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrochemicals). A major component is the [plastics industry](11169849.md). It directly interfaces with the [petroleum industry](994887.md), especially its [downstream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downstream_\(petroleum_industry\)) sector.

Top companies in the industry include [China National Petroleum Corp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Petroleum_Corp), [Saudi Arabian Oil Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabian_Oil_Company), and [ExxonMobil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ExxonMobil).
