The **2026 Italian Grand Prix** (officially known as the **Formula 1 Pirelli Gran Premio d'Italia 2026**) was a [Formula One](10854.md) motor race that was held on 6 September 2026 at the [Monza Circuit](952083.md) in [Monza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monza), Italy.[^cite_note-3] It was the thirteenth round of the [2026 Formula One World Championship](71681684.md).[^cite_note-4]

During qualifying, [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md), who was driving for [Alpine](65240620.md), achieved his and the team's first pole position. After an eventful race, [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)) won from nineteenth position on the grid, becoming the first [Italian driver](37543516.md) since [Ludovico Scarfiotti](1235774.md) in [1966](1122358.md) to win the Italian Grand Prix and the first Italian driver to win a Grand Prix on home soil since [Riccardo Patrese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riccardo_Patrese) won the [1990 San Marino Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_San_Marino_Grand_Prix). Antonelli's teammate [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) and [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) ([Red Bull](1173193.md)) completed the podium.

## Background

The event was held at the [Monza Circuit](952083.md) in [Monza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monza) for the 76th time in the circuit's history, across the weekend of 4–6 September.[^cite_note-5] The Grand Prix was the thirteenth round of the [2026 Formula One World Championship](71681684.md) and the 77th running of the [Italian Grand Prix](511451.md) as a round of the Formula One World Championship.[^cite_note-6]

### Championship standings before the race

Going into the weekend, [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) led the [Drivers' Championship](3437295.md) with 242 points, 59 points ahead of teammate [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) in second and 59 ahead of [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) in third. [Mercedes](25098794.md), with 425 points, led the [Constructors' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Constructors'_Championship) from [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) and [McLaren](20994.md), who were second and third with 338 and 265 points, respectively.[^cite_note-7]

## Practice

Three [free practice sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_racing#Free_practice) were held for the event. The first practice session was held on 4 September 2026, at 12\:30 local time ([UTC+2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+2)), and was topped by [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) ([Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari)) ahead of his teammate [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) and [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)). The second practice session was held on the same day, at 16\:00 local time, and was topped by Russell ahead of Leclerc and [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) (Mercedes). The third practice session was held on 5 September 2026, at 12\:30 local time, and was topped by Russell ahead of Hamilton and [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) ([Red Bull](1173193.md)).[^cite_note-Cal-1]

## Qualifying

[Qualifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_racing#Current_format) was held on 5 September 2026, at 16\:00 local time ([UTC+2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+2)), and determined the starting grid order for the race.[^cite_note-Cal-1]

### Qualifying report

Qualifying was held in dry conditions. [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) and [Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team) both brought significant upgrade packages for the event.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) ([Red Bull](1173193.md)) was the first to leave the pit lane at the start of Q1 and set the initial benchmark. He was followed by [Lance Stroll](42274711.md) ([Aston Martin](63473867.md)) and [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman) ([Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team)). The Ferrari drivers moved into first place consecutively, with [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) setting the fastest time, followed by [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton). [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) (Red Bull) then topped the timesheets. He was quickly displaced by [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md) ([Alpine](65240620.md)), who remained at the top for the rest of Q1, with his teammate, [Franco Colapinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Colapinto), in third. [Nico Hülkenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_H%C3%BClkenberg) ([Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_in_Formula_One)) improved late in the session to claim a position in the second part of qualifying (Q2). [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda) ([Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls)) was the fastest driver eliminated, ahead of [Alexander Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Albon) ([Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing)).[^cite_note-Quali-18] Both [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One) drivers, [Valtteri Bottas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valtteri_Bottas) and [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez), also failed to progress alongside Stroll and [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) (Aston Martin).[^cite_note-F1COMQualy-19]

Lawson once again led the field out at the start of Q2 and briefly set the pace before Verstappen established the first representative benchmark. [McLaren](20994.md) then assumed control for most of the segment, with [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) in first place and [Lando Norris](48978813.md) in second.[^cite_note-20] It was during the final round of laps that Piastri impeded Lawson, resulting in his three-place grid-penalty for the race.[^cite_note-21] Antonelli moved to the top of the timesheets following his flying lap under the chequered flag. Hamilton also narrowly improved to tenth around the same time, beating [Gabriel Bortoleto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto) (Audi) by 0.001 seconds.[^cite_note-Quali-18] [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman) ([Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team)) qualified twelfth, followed by Lawson, Hülkenberg, [Carlos Sainz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.) (Williams) and [Esteban Ocon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Ocon) (Haas).[^cite_note-F1COMQualy-19]

The [Mercedes](25098794.md) drivers were the first on the track in Q3. [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) set the first timed lap after being given a tow by [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) early in the lap. Piastri held second after the opening runs, ahead of Gasly, Leclerc, Verstappen, Antonelli, Norris, [Arvid Lindblad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvid_Lindblad) (Racing Bulls), Hamilton and Colapinto. Hamilton and Colapinto moved to fifth and seventh respectively in their final attempt. Russell briefly took provisional pole, before being displaced by Gasly.[^cite_note-Quali-18] None of the other drivers improved on their times.[^cite_note-F1COMQualy-19]

This was Gasly's first career pole. It made him the first [French driver](37178186.md) to take one since [Jean Alesi](341553.md) at the [1997 Italian Grand Prix](1123312.md), driving for a previous incarnation of Team Enstone, [Benetton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benetton_Formula).[^cite_note-22] He also became the driver with the second-most race entries before achieving their first pole position, with the Grand Prix marking his 191st start, after [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez), who achieved his at the [2022 Saudi Arabian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Saudi_Arabian_Grand_Prix) after 219 entries.[^cite_note-23] This was [Team Enstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_Enstone)'s first pole position as [Alpine](65240620.md) and their first since Alonso's at the [2009 Hungarian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Hungarian_Grand_Prix) when they competed as [Renault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_in_Formula_One).[^cite_note-F1COMQualy-19]

## Race

The race was held on 6 September 2026, at 15\:00 local time ([UTC+2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+2)), and was run for 53 laps.[^cite_note-Cal-1]

### Race report

The 53-lap race took place in hot and dry conditions, with the track temperature reaching approximately 57 °C (135 °F). Twenty drivers formed on the grid, while [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) ([Aston Martin](63473867.md)) and [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) ([Red Bull](1173193.md)) started from the pit lane following set up changes to their cars. Pole-sitter [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md) ([Alpine](65240620.md)) was joined on the front row by [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)), with [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) and [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) (both [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari)) on the second row. [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) ([McLaren](20994.md)) started sixth following a three-place grid penalty following qualifying, alongside [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) (Red Bull) who had subsequently been promoted to fifth. [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)), Alonso and [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda) ([Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls)) began on hard-compound tyres, with the remainder of the grid almost equally split between the mediums and softs.[^cite_note-32]

Gasly held the lead until the second lap, when Russell overtook him to take first place. Behind them, Verstappen crossed Leclerc, Hamilton dropped to tenth and [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman) ([Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team)) moved to ninth. Leclerc then ran over a drainage grill, spun out and crashed into the barrier at the *Parabolica*, the same turn that led to his retirement in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Italian_Grand_Prix). Before the [double yellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Yellow_flag) were waived, [Franco Colapinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Colapinto) (Alpine) passed Piastri for fourth. Antonelli also managed to make seven places during this time. The race was ultimately [red-flagged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Red_flag) for approximately half an hour at the end of the third lap as the barrier was repaired, after which a standing start was confirmed. Tsunoda positioned his car at an angle inside his grid box at the end of the second formation lap, with the race director choosing to declare another formation lap out of an abundance of caution.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-\:1-34]

After the restart, Colapinto briefly reached third before losing numerous places after going off-track. Verstappen, Hamilton, Piastri and Antonelli overtook Gasly between laps 7 and 11, leaving him in sixth. Tsunoda also made up five places, moving from fourteenth to ninth by lap 15. Antonelli took the lead on lap 18, with Russell briefly overtaking him for a portion of lap 20, before ultimately regaining first position on lap 23. Alonso retired from the Grand Prix two laps later. [Lance Stroll](42274711.md) also retired after his car stopped at the first chicane on lap 28. This caused a virtual safety car that led to Antonelli, Verstappen, [Carlos Sainz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.) ([Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing)), [Esteban Ocon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Ocon) (Haas), [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez) and [Valtteri Bottas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valtteri_Bottas) (both [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One)) all coming into the pits. Prior to this, Verstappen and Antonelli both were within two seconds of race leader George Russell.[^cite_note-\:1-34]

Russell stayed out, which led to him encountering a higher tyre degradation. With teammate Antonelli harnessing a significant tyre offset and pace advantage, he was able to catch up to Russell by the closing stages of the race. His first attempt to pass him at the first chicane was unsuccessful, with Antonelli being sent into the gravel, though he was later able to pass Russell. Concurrently, both McLarens were also squabbling for fourth; Norris won the position after passing his teammate.[^cite_note-\:1-34] Antonelli won the race after starting nineteenth. With this victory, Antonelli became the first driver since [John Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Watson_\(racing_driver\)) at the [1983 United States Grand Prix West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_United_States_Grand_Prix_West) to win from such a low starting position and became the first Italian driver to win the Italian Grand Prix since [Ludovico Scarfiotti](1235774.md) sixty years prior and the first to do so on home soil since [Riccardo Patrese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riccardo_Patrese) in [1990](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_San_Marino_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]
