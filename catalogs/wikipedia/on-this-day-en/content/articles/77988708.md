In 2022 almost a fifth of total [energy supply in Ukraine](32021039.md) was from [oil](23195.md), and most energy imports were oil products such as [gasoline](23639.md) and [diesel](81761.md).[^cite_note-1] The country used to produce and refine its own oil.[^cite_note-2] [Strategic reserves](4478230.md#Ukraine) of oil and products were set up after 2023 to help with [energy security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_security).[^cite_note-3]

Products from [Lukoil](1027881.md) have been banned from transiting the country,[^cite_note-4] except to [Hungary](13275.md).[^cite_note-5] This goes through the [Druzhba pipeline](1950396.md).[^cite_note-6] [Excise duty](11404033.md) on [diesel](81761.md) and [gasoline](23639.md) was increased in 2024.[^cite_note-7] The [Odesa–Brody pipeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odesa%E2%80%93Brody_pipeline) is not being used as of 2023.[^cite_note-8] [Refineries](195137.md), such as [Kremenchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremenchuk_Oil_Refinery) were destroyed or shutdown in 2022 in the [Russo-Ukrainian war](42085878.md).[^cite_note-9]

Formerly supplied from Russia and Belarus, gasoline and diesel now come from ports in Poland and Romania.[^cite_note-10]
