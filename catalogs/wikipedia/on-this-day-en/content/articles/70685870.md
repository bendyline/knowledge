The **Special Rapporteur on the occupied Palestinian territories**, formally the **Special Rapporteur on the situation of human rights in the Palestinian territories occupied since 1967**,[^cite_note-UN49-1] is a [special rapporteur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_special_rapporteur) who works for the [United Nations](31769.md) and reports on the human rights situation in [Palestine](241405.md) ("the occupied Palestinian territories"). The mandate was established in 1993 by the former [Commission on Human Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Commission_on_Human_Rights).[^cite_note-2] The special rapporteur typically submits two reports annually, one to the [Human Rights Council](635790.md) in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva) and another to the [General Assembly](31957.md) in [New York City](645042.md).[^cite_note-3]

[Francesca Albanese](72566699.md) was appointed in 2022 as the first woman to become Special Rapporteur on the occupied Palestinian territories.[^cite_note-UN49-1][^cite_note-OHCHR-Special_Rapporteur-4]
