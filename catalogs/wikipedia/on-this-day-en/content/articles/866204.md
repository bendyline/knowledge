**Beqaa Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مقاطعة البقاع, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Muqāṭaʿat al-Biqāʿ*) is a [governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorates_of_Lebanon) in [Lebanon](17771.md) and one of the eight districts of the country. It is located in the eastern part of Lebanon and borders [Syria](7515849.md). The capital of the district is the city of [Zahlé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahl%C3%A9).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-\:0-2]

## Districts



Beqaa Governorate

Since 2014, Beqaa Governorate contains three districts:

- [West Beqaa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Beqaa_District)

- [Rashaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashaya_District)

- [Zahle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahle_District)

A law (Law 522) was passed on 16 July 2003 to separate [Baalbek District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baalbek_District) and [Hermel District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermel_District) from Beqaa Governorate to form the new [Baalbek-Hermel Governorate](53254600.md).[^cite_note-3] Implementation of Baalbek-Hermel Governorate began in 2014 with the appointment of its first governor.[^cite_note-4]
