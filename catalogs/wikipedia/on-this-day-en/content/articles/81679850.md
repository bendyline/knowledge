A binding [referendum](51582.md) was held in [Slovenia](27338.md) on 23 November 2025 regarding the Assisted Voluntary End of Life Act. The [National Assembly](1025128.md) passed the Act in July after voters had backed it in a [nonbinding referendum in 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Slovenian_referendum). However, a [citizens' initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_initiative) led by right-wing politician [Aleš Primc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ale%C5%A1_Primc?action=edit\&redlink=1) forced a referendum on the act.[^cite_note-1]
