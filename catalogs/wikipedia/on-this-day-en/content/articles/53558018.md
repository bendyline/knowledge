The **Socialists' Party of Aragon** ([Aragonese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aragonese_language): *Partiu d'os Socialistas d'Aragón*; Spanish: *Partido de los Socialistas de Aragón*, PSOE–Aragón) is the regional branch in [Aragon](39443.md) of the [Spanish Socialist Workers' Party](272596.md) (PSOE), main centre-left party in Spain since the 1970s.
