**Zhaoyuan** ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 招远市; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 招遠市; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Zhāoyuǎn Shì*) is a [county-level city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County-level_city) within the [prefecture-level city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefecture-level_city) of [Yantai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yantai), [Shandong Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_Province), [China](5405.md), located on the [Bohai Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohai_Sea).[^cite_note-\:0-1] Zhaoyuan is well known for its abundant gold deposit and production, and is occasionally nicknamed "China's gold capital" ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 中国金都; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 中國金都).[^cite_note-\:1-2][^cite_note-3]

The city spans an area of 1,432.32 square kilometres (553.02 mi<sup>2</sup>),[^cite_note-\:2-4] and has a population of 560,234 as of 2019.[^cite_note-5]

## History

The area was first incorporated under the [Han dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_dynasty) as Qucheng County ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 曲成县; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 曲成縣).[^cite_note-\:3-6]

Gold was discovered at Zhaoyuan in the [Tang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tang_dynasty). The myth accompanying the city's discovery of gold is that a giant tortoise named [Ao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ao_\(turtle\)) once told villagers struggling with famine to dig, and upon digging, they struck gold.[^cite_note-\:1-2]

The county was named Zhaoyuan County ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 招远县; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 招遠縣) in 1131.[^cite_note-\:3-6]

### People's Republic of China

Five [townships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_China) in Zhaoyuan were upgraded to [towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towns_of_China) on September 1, 1988.[^cite_note-\:3-6]

On December 21, 1991, Zhaoyuan was upgraded from a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_China) to a [county-level city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County-level_city).[^cite_note-\:3-6]

Throughout the mid-1990s, three more townships were upgraded to towns.[^cite_note-\:3-6]

On January 11, 1999, the town of Zhaocheng ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 招城镇; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 招城鎮) was divided into three [subdistricts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdistricts_of_China): [Luofeng Subdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luofeng_Subdistrict?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Quanshan Subdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quanshan_Subdistrict,_Zhaoyuan,_Shandong), and [Mengzhi Subdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mengzhi_Subdistrict?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:3-6]

The [Fifth National Population Census of the People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifth_National_Population_Census_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) reported that Zhaoyuan had a population of 593,705.[^cite_note-\:3-6]

On December 19, 2000, Dahuchenjia Township ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 大户陈家乡; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 大戶陳家鄉) became the city's last township to be upgraded to a town.[^cite_note-\:3-6]

#### 2014 murder of Wu Shuoyan

Main article: [Murder of Wu Shuoyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Wu_Shuoyan)

In late May 2014, five members of [The Church of Almighty God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Church_of_Almighty_God), an outlawed new-age cult, entered a [McDonald's](2480627.md) in Zhaoyuan and began soliciting customers' phone numbers.[^cite_note-\:4-7] When 37 year-old Wu Shuoyan refused to give her phone number to the group, they claimed she was an "evil spirit", and began beating her with chairs and a metal mop handle, ultimately killing her.[^cite_note-\:4-7] In February 2015, two of the attackers were executed, and the other three received jail sentences ranging from seven years to life.[^cite_note-8]

## Geography

Zhaoyuan's city center is located approximately 90 kilometres (56 mi) southwest of Yantai's urban center.[^cite_note-\:2-4] The city's terrain is higher in the northeast, middle and west, and lower in the northwest and southeast[^cite_note-\:2-4]

### Climate

## Administrative divisions

Zhaoyuan administers five [subdistricts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdistricts_of_China) and nine [towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towns_of_China).[^cite_note-\:5-12]

### Subdistricts

Zhaoyuan's five subdistricts are [Luofeng Subdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luofeng_Subdistrict?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Quanshan Subdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quanshan_Subdistrict,_Zhaoyuan,_Shandong), [Mengzhi Subdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mengzhi_Subdistrict?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Wenquan Subdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wenquan_Subdistrict,_Yantai?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Daqinjia Subdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daqinjia_Subdistrict?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:5-12]

### Towns

Zhaoyuan's nine towns are [Xinzhuang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinzhuang,_Yantai?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Canzhuang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canzhuang,_Yantai?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Jinling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinling,_Yantai?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Biguo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biguo,_Yantai?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Linglong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linlong,_Yantai?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Zhangxing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhangxing,_Yantai?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Xiadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiadian,_Zhaoyuan,_Shandong?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Fushan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fushan,_Zhaoyuan,_Shandong?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Qishan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qishan,_Zhaoyuan?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:5-12]

## Economy

The city has a significant amount of [gold](12240.md) deposits, largely concentrated in its northeast.[^cite_note-\:2-4] [Zhaojin Mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhaojin_Mining) has its headquarters located in the city.[^cite_note-13] Additionally, [Linglong Tire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linglong_Tire) is headquartered in the city.[^cite_note-14]

## Transport

[National Highway 206](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Highway_206) runs through Zhaoyuan.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## Tourist attractions

A large golden statue of [Ao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ao_\(turtle\))*,* a Chinese mythical giant turtle is located in the [village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villages_of_China) of Oujiakuang ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 欧家夼村; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Ōujiākuǎng Cūn*), in the town of [Linglong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linlong,_Yantai?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:8-15][^cite_note-16] Located in Luoshan National Forest Park ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 罗山国家森林公园; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 羅山國家森林公園),[^cite_note-\:8-15] the statue is Asia's largest statue of A&#x6F;*,* and is located at [37°27′36″N 120°28′22″E  /  37.46000°N 120.47278°E  / 37.46000; 120.47278](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Zhaoyuan,_Shandong\&params=37_27_36_N_120_28_22_E_region\:CN-62_type\:landmark). The statue is 15 meters tall and 20 meters long.[^cite_note-\:6-17][^cite_note-\:7-18]

The [Qucheng Ancient City Site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qucheng_Ancient_City_Site?action=edit\&redlink=1) is located in Zhaoyuan.[^cite_note-\:0-1]
