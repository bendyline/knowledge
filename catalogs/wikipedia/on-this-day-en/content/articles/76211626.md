[Elon Musk](909036.md) is the wealthiest person in the world in terms of [financial assets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_wealth), with an estimated [net worth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_worth) of US\$942 billion according to the *[Bloomberg Billionaires Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_Billionaires_Index)*[^cite_note-1] and \$955 billion according to *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes)*,[^cite_note-ForbesWealth-2] as of September 21, 2026, primarily from his ownership stakes of 42% of [SpaceX](832774.md)[^cite_note-3] and 20% of [Tesla](5533631.md).[^cite_note-4] He will receive additional SpaceX stock[^cite_note-5] and \$1 trillion worth of Tesla stock[^cite_note-6] if the companies meet certain milestones.&#x20;

Musk was first listed on the *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes)* list of *[The World's Billionaires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World's_Billionaires)* in 2012, with a net worth of \$2 billion.[^cite_note-Twice-7] According to *Forbes*, his net worth reached \$300 billion in 2021; \$400bn in December 2024;[^cite_note-reach-8] \$500bn in October 2025;[^cite_note-9] \$600bn[^cite_note-10] and \$700bn[^cite_note-11] in December 2025; \$800bn in February 2026;[^cite_note-12] and \$900bn in June 2026, and he became the only [US dollar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_dollar) [trillionaire](47418795.md) in history that month upon the [initial public offering (IPO) of SpaceX](82660746.md).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-Trillionaire-14][^cite_note-15] Since then, his net worth has been volatile, largely because of changes in SpaceX’s valuation. His net worth reached an intraday peak of \$1.45 trillion in the week after the IPO,[^cite_note-16] but by late July 2026, a SpaceX stock collapse had reduced this net worth by \$750 billion, the largest net worth loss in history.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-\:3-18][^cite_note-19]

In May 2020, Musk pledged to sell almost all physical possessions and said he would "own no house",[^cite_note-cash-20] but in February 2026, *[The New York Times](30680.md)* identified at least 37 companies that own properties which appear to be for Musk's personal use, including expensive [condominiums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condominium), planes used by him for [private travel](247003.md) and at least 1000 acres of land.[^cite_note-21] His wealth is managed by Excession LLC, his [family office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_office), which has been run by [Jared Birchall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jared_Birchall) since 2016.[^cite_note-22]

## History

Elon Musk made \$180 million when [PayPal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PayPal) was sold to [eBay](130495.md) in October 2002.[^cite_note-23]

Musk was first listed on the *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes)* list of [The World's Billionaires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World's_Billionaires) in 2012, with a net worth of \$2 billion.[^cite_note-Twice-7]

At the start of 2020, Musk had a net worth of \$27bn.[^cite_note-24] By the end of 2020, his net worth had increased by \$150bn, mostly driven by his ownership of around 20% of Tesla.[^cite_note-passes-25]

Before Musk executed major sales of Tesla stock in 2021, Musk described himself as "[cash poor](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/cash%20poor)".[^cite_note-Surpasses-26][^cite_note-cash-20]

Musk's net worth has been volatile. It dropped \$16.3bn on September 8, 2020, the largest single-day plunge in *[Bloomberg Billionaires Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_Billionaires_Index)'s* history at the time.[^cite_note-27]

In November 2020, Musk passed [Facebook](7529378.md) co-founder [Mark Zuckerberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Zuckerberg) to become the third-richest person in the world; a week later he passed [Microsoft](19001.md) co-founder [Bill Gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Gates) to become the second-richest.[^cite_note-Gates-28]

In November 2020, around 75% of Musk's wealth was derived from his ownership of Tesla stock,[^cite_note-Gates-28] a proportion that fell to about 37% as of December 2022,[^cite_note-29] after selling nearly \$40bn in company shares in late 2021 and 2022.[^cite_note-30]

On January 7, 2021, Musk, with a net worth of \$285bn, surpassed [Amazon](90451.md) founder [Jeff Bezos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Bezos) to become the richest person in the world.[^cite_note-31] Bezos reclaimed the top spot on February 16, 2021.[^cite_note-32] On September 27, 2021, after Tesla stock surged, *Forbes* announced that Musk had a net worth of over \$190bn,[^cite_note-33] and was the richest person in the world. In November 2021, Musk became the first person to have a net worth of more than \$300bn.[^cite_note-34]

On December 30, 2022, due a decline in the value of Tesla shares, Musk's net worth declined by \$200bn since its peak, making Musk the first person in history to have lost such an amount,[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] which was recognized by *[Guinness World Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Records)* in January 2023.[^cite_note-37]

Between December 2022 and June 2024, Musk, [Bernard Arnault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_Arnault), and [Jeff Bezos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Bezos) traded places among the 3 richest people in the world.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40]

After the value of Tesla shares surged following the [2024 United States presidential election](59568546.md), in December 2024, Musk became the first person to have a net worth of more than \$400bn.[^cite_note-reach-8]

However, Musk's association with the [second presidency of Donald Trump](78298108.md) then led to a decline in value in shares of Tesla, resulting in his net worth dropping by \$126bn between December 2024 and March 2025.[^cite_note-41]

For a few hours on September 10, 2025, [Larry Ellison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Ellison)'s net worth surpassed that of Musk.[^cite_note-42]

Upon the [initial public offering of SpaceX](82660746.md) on June 12, 2026, Musk became the only person to have had a net worth exceeding \$1 trillion.[^cite_note-Trillionaire-14] Since then, his net worth has fluctuated, reaching as high as \$1.4 trillion in the week after the IPO, but has been under \$1 trillion since early July 2026.[^cite_note-43]

### Compensation from Tesla and failed legal challenge

Musk does not receive a salary from Tesla; he agreed with the board in 2018 to a compensation plan that ties his personal earnings to Tesla's valuation and revenue.[^cite_note-passes-25] The deal stipulated that Musk only received the compensation if Tesla reached certain market values.[^cite_note-Boldest-44] It was the largest such deal ever done between a [CEO](52234.md) and a company board.[^cite_note-award-45] In the first award, given in May 2020, he was eligible to purchase 1.69 million Tesla shares (about 1% of the company, worth \$800M at the time) at below-market prices.[^cite_note-award-45][^cite_note-Boldest-44]

In January 2024, Delaware judge [Kathaleen McCormick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathaleen_McCormick) ruled in a 2018 lawsuit that Musk's \$55bn pay package from Tesla be rescinded.[^cite_note-46] McCormick called the compensation granted by the company's board "an unfathomable sum" that was unfair to shareholders. In response to the ruling, Musk posted on X: "Never incorporate your company in the state of Delaware."[^cite_note-47] A re-ratification shareholders' vote passed in mid-June 2024, agreeing also to move Tesla's incorporation from Delaware to Texas; a lawsuit was filed by a Tesla investor beforehand that alleged Musk employed "coercive tactics" to move the vote in his favor.[^cite_note-48] The [Delaware Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware_Supreme_Court) overturned McCormick's decision in December 2025, restoring Musk's compensation package and awarding \$1 in [nominal damages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nominal_damages).[^cite_note-49] Musk exercised the stock options from his 2018 pay package on June 16, 2026.[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51]

## Giving

According to [Walter Isaacson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Isaacson), who wrote a biography on Musk, Musk has little interest in [philanthropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philanthropy). Musk has been criticized for relatively minimal philanthropic giving; however, he believes that he can do more for humanity by investing his money in fields such as [renewable energy](25784.md), [space exploration](28431.md) and [AI safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_safety) as opposed to using his wealth for directly philanthropic purposes.[^cite_note-muskbio-52]

### Musk Foundation

This section is an excerpt from [Musk Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musk_Foundation).\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Musk_Foundation\&action=edit)]

The [Musk Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musk_Foundation) is a United States–based charitable [foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundation_\(nonprofit\)) funded and directed primarily by [Elon Musk](909036.md). The foundation is dedicated to promoting [renewable energy](25784.md), [crewed space exploration](28431.md), [pediatrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pediatrics), science and engineering education, and the "development of safe [artificial intelligence](1164.md) for the benefit of humanity".[^cite_note-Musk_Foundation_\:0-53]

At the end of 2024, the Musk Foundation had over \$14 billion in assets.[^cite_note-Musk_Foundation_NYT2025-54] Since 2020, donations have largely been sent to organizations affiliated with Musk and his businesses.[^cite_note-Musk_Foundation_Bberg2024-55][^cite_note-Musk_Foundation_NYT2024-56]

The Musk Foundation has been criticized for both its selection of recipients of donations and a relatively low payout ratio. In 2021 and 2022, the Musk Foundation awarded less than 5% of its assets in donations, after its assets grew to several billion dollars. This means that it fell short of the legal minimum donation required to maintain its tax-exempt status.[^cite_note-57]

*[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* criticized the fact that the foundation financed various projects of Musk and his family members, although this is not unusual for billionaires and wealthy donors.[^cite_note-58] *[The New York Times](30680.md)* concluded that through 2022, about half of the Musk Foundation's grants went to organizations "tied" to Musk, one of his employees, or one of his companies.

After Musk challenged [World Food Programme](33582.md) director [David Beasley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Beasley) to draft a plan to use money of Musk's that Beasley said could end world [hunger](149422.md), Musk instead donated the \$6 billion in question to his own foundation after Beasley's plan showed that the money could only feed 42 million people for just one year.[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-60]

### Education

Musk has asserted that declining [birth rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_rates) pose an existential threat to humanity and in 2021 donated \$10M to the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) to research the potential risks.[^cite_note-61][^cite_note-62]

In December 2023, tax records revealed that Musk had donated \$100M to a new foundation, "The Foundation", set up to create a primary and secondary school in [Austin, Texas](1998.md), focused on teaching [science, technology, engineering, and mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science,_technology,_engineering,_and_mathematics) (STEM) subjects.[^cite_note-2023BI-63][^cite_note-64] The foundation included plans to expand the project to the university level accredited by the [Southern Association of Colleges and Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Association_of_Colleges_and_Schools).[^cite_note-2023BI-63]

In 2022, Musk allocated [Tesla, Inc.](5533631.md) stock valued at \$2bn to his foundation.[^cite_note-65]

### Technology

In 2015, Musk donated \$10M to [Future of Life Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_of_Life_Institute), a [nonprofit organization](72487.md) focused on challenges posed by advanced technologies, such as robotics.[^cite_note-66]

### Political contributions

In the fall of 2022, Musk contributed \$50M to [Citizens for Sanity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens_for_Sanity), a [political action committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_action_committee) (PAC) aligned with the policies of [Donald Trump](4848272.md).[^cite_note-67]

In 2023, Musk contributed \$10M to the [Ron DeSantis 2024 presidential campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_DeSantis_2024_presidential_campaign).[^cite_note-68]

In 2024, Musk supported the [Donald Trump 2024 presidential campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_2024_presidential_campaign), creating [America PAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America_PAC)[^cite_note-69] and donating \$270M to it.[^cite_note-70][^cite_note-71] He also gave \$20.5M to pro-Trump [RBG PAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RBG_PAC).[^cite_note-72] His donations in the 2024 election cycle totaled more than \$291M, the most of any donor.[^cite_note-73]

Musk's status as the first US dollar trillionaire drew historical comparisons, acclaim, and criticism. The *[New York Times](30680.md)* drew the connection between Musk and [John D. Rockefeller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_D._Rockefeller), who became the first US dollar billionaire in 1916.[^cite_note-\:0-75] Rockefeller's wealth was estimated at \$900 million in 1913, which was 2.3% of the US gross domestic product (GDP);[^cite_note-76] when Musk's wealth was \$970 billion on June 11, 2026 due to the pricing of SpaceX at \$135 per share, it was 3% of the US GDP.[^cite_note-77] When Musk became a trillionaire, he was nearly four times as wealthy as the second richest person, [Larry Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Page).[^cite_note-78] Left leaning American political figures such as [Elizabeth Warren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Warren)[^cite_note-\:1-79] and [Zohran Mamdani](64618347.md)[^cite_note-80] used the milestone to call for the implementation of a [wealth tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wealth_tax).

## Taxes paid

See also: [Wealth inequality in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wealth_inequality_in_the_United_States) and [Tax policy and economic inequality in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tax_policy_and_economic_inequality_in_the_United_States)

Musk paid \$455 million in taxes on \$1.52 billion of income between 2014 and 2018.[^cite_note-taxes-81] According to [ProPublica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ProPublica), a nonprofit [investigative journalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investigative_journalism) organization, Musk paid no [federal income taxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Income_tax_in_the_United_States) in 2018.[^cite_note-82] According to Musk, his 2021 tax bill was \$11bn following the sale of \$14bn worth of Tesla stock, close to a \$12bn CNBC estimate.[^cite_note-taxes-81]
