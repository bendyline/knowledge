The **Medal of the Knesset** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): עיטור הכנסת, *Itur HaKnesset*)[^cite_note-KHS-1] is an Israeli state decoration awarded by the [Speaker of the Knesset](1284678.md), the legislative body of Israel.

## Description

The Medal of the Knesset is awarded to individuals for "significant contributions to the [State of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Israel) and the [Jewish people](25955086.md)". It is awarded by the [Speaker of the Knesset](1284678.md), the legislative body of Israel. The medal depicts the [emblem of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emblem_of_Israel), the [Knesset](74656.md) building, and the Knesset Plenum. It is made of gold-plated bronze and is engraved with the phrase "Hast crowned him with glory and honor" from [Psalms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_of_Psalms) 8\:6. The ribbon is silver and blue and features the [Star of David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_of_David).[^cite_note-2]

## History

The Medal of the Knesset was first awarded on 25 February 2026 when Speaker [Amir Ohana](48869232.md) bestowed the decoration to Indian prime minister [Narendra Modi](444222.md).[^cite_note-3] He issued the award for Modi having "significantly strengthened the ties between [India](14533.md) and Israel and deepened the strategic cooperation between the countries" ("חיזק באופן משמעותי את הקשרים בין הודו לבין ישראל והעמיק את שיתוף הפעולה האסטרטגי בין המדינות").[^cite_note-KHS-1]
