The history of [cricket](25675557.md) in [Pakistan](23235.md) predates the creation of the country in 1947. The first international cricket match in what is now Pakistan today was held in [Karachi](17123.md) on 22 November 1935 between [Sindh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindh_cricket_team) and [an Australian XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_cricket_team_in_Ceylon_and_India_in_1935%E2%80%9336) (see Figure 1). The match was seen by 5,000 [Karachiites](17123.md). Cricket was introduced by the British during their colonial rule of British India, which covered the area now known as Pakistan. Cricket is the most popular sport in the country.[^cite_note-1] The [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) controls all domestic **cricket in Pakistan** and the national teams. Pakistan is an official member of the [International Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) and the [Asian Cricket Council](584073.md). Regarded as one of the best and most passionate cricketing nations, Pakistan has won the [Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_World_Cup) in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Cricket_World_Cup), [ICC T20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Men's_T20_World_Cup) in [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_ICC_World_Twenty20), the [ICC Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Champions_Trophy) in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_ICC_Champions_Trophy), the [ICC Under-19 Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Under-19_Cricket_World_Cup) in [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Under-19_Cricket_World_Cup) and [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Under-19_Cricket_World_Cup), the [ACC Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Cup) in [2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Asia_Cup) and [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Asia_Cup). Pakistan were runner ups in the [1999 Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_Cricket_World_Cup) and [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_ICC_World_Twenty20) and [2022 T20 World Cups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_ICC_Men's_T20_World_Cup). Pakistan have also been runner ups in several [Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Cup) editions. Pakistan cricket team is widely considered an unpredictable team which can outperform opposition on any given day.[^cite_note-2]

## History

[Professional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forms_of_cricket) cricket has been played in Pakistan since its formation in [1947](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_India). [Cricket](25675557.md) in [Pakistan](23235.md) has a history predating the creation of the country in 1947. The first ever international cricket match in [Karachi](17123.md) was held on 22 November 1935 between [Sindh](28972.md) and [an Australian XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_cricket_team_in_Ceylon_and_India_in_1935%E2%80%9336). The match was seen by 5,000 Karachiites.[^cite_note-3] [Ghulam Mohammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghulam_Mohammad_\(cricketer,_born_1898\)) was the captain of team [Sind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sind) and [Frank Tarrant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Tarrant) was the captain of Tarrant's team. Following the independence of Pakistan in 1947, cricket in the country developed rapidly and Pakistan was given [Test match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_cricket) status at a meeting of the [Imperial Cricket Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) at [Lord's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord's) in England on 28 July 1952 following recommendation by India,[^cite_note-4] which, being the successor state of the [British Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj), did not have to go through such a process. The first captain of the Pakistan national cricket team was [Abdul Hafeez Kardar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Hafeez_Kardar).

Pakistan's first Test match was played in [Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feroz_Shah_Kotla) in October 1952 as part of a five Test series which [India](407754.md) won 2–1. Pakistan made their first tour of England in 1954 and drew the series 1–1 after a victory at [The Oval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Oval) in which fast bowler [Fazal Mahmood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fazal_Mahmood) took 12 wickets. Pakistan's first home Test match was against India in January 1955 at [Bangabandhu National Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangabandhu_National_Stadium), [Dacca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dacca), East Pakistan (now [Bangladesh](3454.md)), after which four more Test matches were played in [Bahawalpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahawalpur), [Lahore](125315.md), [Peshawar](66230.md) and [Karachi](17123.md) (all five matches in the series were drawn, the first such occurrence in Test history[^cite_note-5]).

The team is considered a strong but unpredictable team. Traditionally Pakistani cricket has been composed of talented players but is alleged to display limited discipline on occasion, making their performance inconsistent at times. In particular, the [India-Pakistan cricket rivalry](6406914.md) is usually emotionally charged and can provide for intriguing contests, as talented teams and players from both sides of the border seek to elevate their game to new levels. Pakistan team contests with India in the [Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_World_Cup) have resulted in packed stadiums and highly charged atmospheres. The team is well supported at home and abroad, especially in the [United Kingdom](31717.md) where [British Pakistanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Pakistanis) have formed a fan-club called the "Stani Army". Members of the club show up to matches across the country and are known to provide raucous support. The Stani Army also takes part in charity initiatives for underprivileged Pakistanis, including annual friendly cricket matches against [British Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Indian) members of the similar "[Bharat Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharat_Army)".

### 1947 to 1970

Main article: [History of cricket in Pakistan from 1947 to 1970](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_cricket_in_Pakistan_from_1947_to_1970)

The independent state of Pakistan was established in 1947 following the [Partition of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_India). [First-class cricket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-class_cricket) was already established in the country as many clubs and local associations had previously been part of the Indian cricket scene.Matches were played on an *ad hoc* basis in the 1947–48 and 1948–49 seasons before Pakistan's Board of Cricket Control (BCCP) was established on 1 May 1949. Games continued to be few and far between for several seasons until a national championship began in 1953.

On 27–29 December 1947, the [Punjab v Sind](http://www.cricketarchive.co.uk/Archive/Scorecards/18/18331.html) match at [Lahore](125315.md) marked the start of first-class cricket in Pakistan as an independent country. Later that season, on 6–8 February 1948, the [Punjab Governor's XI v Punjab University](http://www.cricketarchive.co.uk/Archive/Scorecards/18/18370.html) match took place, also at Lahore. These were the only matches that season owing to disruption caused by the Partition.Another [Punjab Governor's XI v Punjab University](http://www.cricketarchive.co.uk/Archive/Scorecards/18/18795.html) took place at Lahore in March 1949, but it was the only domestic first-class match in the second season.The highlight of the 1948–49 season was the arrival of the [West Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Indies_cricket_team) team in November 1948. This was the first tour of Pakistan by an overseas team.The [Pakistan national cricket team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_national_cricket_team) made its inaugural overseas tour in April 1949 with a visit to [Ceylon](26750.md), where the team played two matches against [Ceylon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka_national_cricket_team) in [Colombo](56636.md). Pakistan, captained by [Mohammed Saeed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Saeed_\(cricketer,_born_1910\)), won the first match by an innings and the second by 10 wickets.There were no domestic matches at all in 1949–50 when two touring teams arrived. The first tour was by a [Commonwealth XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_XI_cricket_team) in November and December 1949. Then Ceylon, on a return tour in March–April 1950, played five first-class matches.

In October to December 1952, [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_national_cricket_team)'s Test debut was a five-match series in India, the matches played at New Delhi, Lucknow, Bombay, Madras and Calcutta. After [India](407754.md) had won the First Test by an innings, Pakistan won the Second Test by an innings thanks to [Fazal Mahmood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fazal_Mahmood) who took 5–52 and 7–42. India won the Third Test and the other two were drawn.The [Qaid-i-Azam Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qaid-i-Azam_Trophy) was launched in the 1953–54 season as Pakistan's national championship. The first winner was [Bahawalpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahawalpur_cricket_team). The outstanding player in the inaugural season was the great opening batsman [Hanif Mohammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanif_Mohammad) who scored 513 runs at an average of 128.25 with a highest score of 174.In the first international tour of Pakistan by an overseas team, West Indies played two first-class matches versus [Sind at Karachi](http://www.cricketarchive.co.uk/Archive/Scorecards/18/18721.html) and a [Pakistan XI at Lahore](http://www.cricketarchive.co.uk/Archive/Scorecards/18/18726.html). Both matches were drawn.The West Indies team included [George Headley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Headley), [Clyde Walcott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clyde_Walcott) and [Everton Weekes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everton_Weekes).The tourists played two matches against an All-Pakistan XI in Lahore and against a Karachi-Sind Combined XI in Karachi. The tourists won the first match by an innings and 177 runs; they won the second match by 6 wickets. Captained by [Jock Livingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jock_Livingston), who also kept wicket in some games, the team had several well-known players including [Frank Worrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Worrell), [George Tribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Tribe), [Bill Alley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Alley), [Cec Pepper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cec_Pepper), [George Dawkes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Dawkes) and [George Pope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pope_\(cricketer\)).An [International XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_XI_cricket_team_in_Pakistan,_India_and_Ceylon_in_1967%E2%80%9368) of county cricketers, most of them English, toured Africa and Asia from January to April 1968, playing one first-class match in Pakistan against a BCCP XI in February, which the International XI won.[^cite_note-6] This was the first ever series win by New Zealand after almost 40 years and 30 consecutive winless series.[^cite_note-7]

### 1971 to 1985

Main article: [History of cricket in Pakistan from 1971 to 1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_cricket_in_Pakistan_from_1971_to_1985)

In 1970, the [Ayub Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayub_Trophy) was rebranded as the [BCCP Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BCCP_Trophy) and converted from a [knockout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knockout) tournament to a mini-league format whereby teams qualified for a semi-final stage by winning one of four qualifying groups. The competition's name changed again in 1973 to [BCCP Patron's Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BCCP_Patron's_Trophy?action=edit\&redlink=1).The Pentangular Trophy commenced in the 1973–74 season and the [Wills Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wills_Cup), Pakistan's premier limited overs competition, in 1980–81.Australia also played three first-class matches against BCCP Patron's XI at the [Pindi Club Ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pindi_Club_Ground), Rawalpindi; BCCP XI at the [Ibn-e-Qasim Bagh Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn-e-Qasim_Bagh_Stadium), Multan; and Pakistan Invitation XI at the [Jinnah Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinnah_Stadium_\(Sialkot\)), Sialkot. Australia won the first two matches and drew the third.

### 1986 to 2000

Main article: [History of cricket in Pakistan from 1986 to 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_cricket_in_Pakistan_from_1986_to_2000)

[Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_national_cricket_team) won the [1992 Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Cricket_World_Cup), beating [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_national_cricket_team) by 22 runs in the final at the [Melbourne Cricket Ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne_Cricket_Ground) on 25 March 1992.

Notable Pakistan players in this period include [Javed Miandad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javed_Miandad), [Imran Khan](413184.md), [Inzamam-ul-Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inzamam-ul-Haq), [Mushtaq Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mushtaq_Ahmed_\(cricketer\)), [Waqar Younis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waqar_Younis), [Wasim Akram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasim_Akram) and [Saqlain Mushtaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saqlain_Mushtaq).

### 2001 to present

Main article: [History of cricket in Pakistan from 2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_cricket_in_Pakistan_from_2001)

Pakistan's cricket faced a major setback in [March 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_attack_on_the_Sri_Lanka_national_cricket_team), when the [Sri Lankan team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka_national_cricket_team) was attacked in [Lahore](125315.md), leading to a suspension of international cricket tours in the country for nearly a decade. During this period, Pakistan hosted its international fixtures primarily in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) (UAE), using venues in [Dubai](211583.md), [Sharjah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharjah), and [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md) as neutral grounds.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

Efforts to revive international cricket began gradually, with the [Zimbabwe team's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe_national_cricket_team) tour in 2015 marking the first major return of a Test nation to Pakistan since 2009. This was followed by visits from the [ICC World XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_World_XI) (2017), [Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka_national_cricket_team) (2019), [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa_national_cricket_team) (2021), and [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_national_cricket_team) (2022), culminating in the full restoration of home cricket.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] By the mid-2020s, Pakistan had fully restored its status as a regular host of international cricket, staging the [2023 Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Asia_Cup) and the [2025 ICC Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_ICC_Champions_Trophy), and hosting bilateral tours by nearly all ICC full-member nations.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

Notable Pakistan players in the 21st century include [Inzamam-ul-Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inzamam-ul-Haq), [Younis Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Younis_Khan), [Mohammad Yousuf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Yousuf_\(cricketer,_born_1974\)), [Abdul Razzaq (cricketer)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Razzaq_\(cricketer\)), [Saeed Ajmal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saeed_Ajmal), [Shahid Afridi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahid_Afridi), [Shoaib Akhtar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoaib_Akhtar), [Umar Gul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umar_Gul), [Misbah-ul-Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misbah-ul-Haq) and [Babar Azam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babar_Azam).

## Administration

Main article: [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board)

Further information: [Cricket administration in Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category\:Cricket_administration_in_Pakistan)

The [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) governs all official domestic tournaments. Pakistan is also an official member of the [International Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) and the [Asian Cricket Council](584073.md). Almost all cities and villages in Pakistan have their own cricket teams and unofficial tournaments. Pakistani children start playing cricket at a young age.

The game is the most popular sport in the country with the [tape ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tape_ball) variety of the game being the most common. A **tape ball** is a [tennis ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_ball) wrapped in [electrical tape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_tape) and is used in playing [backyard cricket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backyard_cricket). This modification of the tennis ball gives it greater weight, speed and distance while still being easier to play with than the conventional [cricket ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_ball). The variation was pioneered in [Karachi](17123.md), [Pakistan](23235.md) and is credited with Pakistan's famous production of [fast bowlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_bowling) as children are brought up playing the game using a tape ball in which various skills are developed. The increasing popularity of the tape ball in informal, local cricket has transformed the way games are played in cricket-loving nations such as [India](14533.md), [Sri Lanka](26750.md), and [Bangladesh](3454.md) but most famously [Pakistan](23235.md). Such has been the impact of *tape ball* that in recent years some companies have introduced tennis balls designed to act like cricket balls.[^cite_note-15] These balls are quite popular in South Asia where [tape ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tape_ball) cricket is one of the most popular forms of the sport.[^cite_note-\:0-16]

## Organisation of cricket in modern Pakistan

### International cricket

#### Men's national team

Main article: [Pakistan national cricket team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_national_cricket_team)

Cricket is considered the most popular sport in Pakistan. After the [partition of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_India) in 1947 and the formation of Pakistan, Pakistan played its first official match[^cite_note-17] in 1952 under the captaincy of [Abdul Kardar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Hafeez_Kardar) against the [republic of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_India) in [1952](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_India_in_1952%E2%80%9353) registering their first Test victory[^cite_note-18] in [Lucknow](392095.md).[^cite_note-first-19] Women's cricket developed later in Pakistan with the women's national team playing their first match in [1997](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_women's_national_cricket_team).

The [national cricket team of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_national_cricket_team) is governed by the [Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) - a permanent member of the [International Cricket Council (ICC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council). Pakistan national teams regularly participate in international home and away series. In addition, the Pakistan men's national team participates in the following major international tournaments:

- [ICC ODI Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Cricket_World_Cup) ([ODI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Day_International)): Inaugural Tournament and Debut in [1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975_Cricket_World_Cup)

- [ICC T20 Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_T20_World_Cup) ([T20I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twenty20_International)): Inaugural Tournament and Debut in [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_ICC_World_Twenty20)

- [ACC Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Cup) ([ODI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Day_International) & [T20I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twenty20_International)): Inaugural Tournament and Debut in [1984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984_Asia_Cup)

- [ICC Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Champions_Trophy) ([ODI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Day_International)): Inaugural Tournament and Debut in [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_ICC_KnockOut_Trophy)

- [ICC World Test Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Test_Championship) ([Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_cricket)): Inaugural Tournament and Debut in [2019/21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%932021_World_Test_Championship)

- [ICC Test Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Test_Championship) ([Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_cricket)): Inauguration and Debut in [2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Test_Championship)

- [South Africa-Pakistan cricket rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_South_Africa_in_2018%E2%80%9319)

Pakistan men's national team has had success on the international stage having a best international ranking of 1st in the [Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_cricket), [ODI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Day_International) and [T20I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twenty20_International) cricket.[^cite_note-20] In terms of tournament success:

- [ICC ODI Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Cricket_World_Cup): Champions in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Cricket_World_Cup)

- [ICC T20 Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_T20_World_Cup): Champions in [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_ICC_World_Twenty20)

- [ICC Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Champions_Trophy): Champions in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_ICC_Champions_Trophy)

- [ACC Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Cup): Champions in [2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Asia_Cup) & [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Asia_Cup)

- [ICC World Test Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Test_Championship): N/A

- [ICC Test Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Test_Championship): Mace Holders in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Test_Championship)

From 2009 to 2019 Pakistan was unable to host international matches in Pakistan after the [terror attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_attack_on_the_Sri_Lanka_national_cricket_team) on the touring Sri Lanka cricket team. This decade led to little or no international cricket taking place in Pakistan and Pakistan played its home series in the UAE (specifically Dubai and Abu Dhabi). As the security situation improved, in September 2019, international test cricket returned to Pakistan with the [visit of Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_cricket_team_in_Pakistan_in_2019%E2%80%9320). By the end of 2022, almost all the leading Test playing nations had toured the country again, the 2025 [ICC Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Champions_Trophy) is scheduled to be held in Pakistan.

#### Women's national team

Main article: [Pakistan women's national cricket team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_women's_national_cricket_team)

The [Pakistan women's national cricket team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_women's_national_cricket_team) has had moderate success on the international stage and is in development. However, the team is regularly ranked in the top 10 in the world.[^cite_note-21] Thus far the [women's national team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_women's_national_cricket_team) has not won an [ICC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) or [ACC](584073.md) international tournament.

### Domestic cricket

The structure of domestic cricket in Pakistan at the highest level has changed many times since 1947 with the latest restructure being in 2019.[^cite_note-auto-22] Previously [domestic cricket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaid-e-Azam_Trophy) operated with departmental, city and regional teams - a set up encouraged by [Abdul Hafeez Kardar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Hafeez_Kardar).[^cite_note-23] Since 1947, the [domestic first class cricket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaid-e-Azam_Trophy) system has varied considerably per year with teams ranging from 7 to 26 and tournament matches operating under different formats (often changes occurred every year). With the advent of domestic [List A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_A_cricket) and [T20](770690.md) forms of cricket in the 1970s and 2000s, there has been no consistent set up (as has been noted for [first class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-class_cricket) cricket in Pakistan). Historically, school and club cricket has also suffered due to inconsistencies in top tier domestic cricket. The consistent changes in the domestic structure and the gradual introduction of departmental teams was encouraged as it provided permanent jobs to players. Matches were rarely televised due to lack of quality cricket and lack of interest in departmental cricket. This inconsistent system was widely criticised on the basis of low quality cricket and reduced competition.

In 2019, six regional teams were created on provincial lines. The teams would compete in the principal competitions in all three forms of the game: the [Quaid-e-Azam Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaid-e-Azam_Trophy) ([First Class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-class_cricket)), [Pakistan Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cup) ([List A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_A_cricket)) and [National T20 Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_T20_Cup) (Domestic [T20](770690.md)). The PCB's rationale in reducing the number of teams in domestic cricket was to concentrate talent in order to increase competition and improve the quality of cricket. The new structure also consisted of corresponding second XI, under-19, under-16 and under-13 competitions, and live television coverage of top level matches.[^cite_note-24] The restructuring also reorganised district level cricket into a three tier bottom-up system, with 90 city cricket associations supervising school and club cricket at grassroots level, and inter-city tournaments providing a stepping-stone to the six elite regional teams.[^cite_note-auto1-25] AFP Sport examines three areas where Babar Azam’s side is struggling[^cite_note-Ali_Jutt-26] The three tier bottom-up system can be summarised as follows[^cite_note-autogenerated1-27]

The six regional teams (operated by respective six cricket associations) ensure that the affairs of the associations at city level are regulated. They frame policies that will develop cricket at the grassroots, manage club cricket in collaboration with the 90 city associations and also oversee intra-city competitions. The teams are responsible for revenue generation through sponsorship, marketing and strategic collaborations with business conglomerates. Each of the six regional teams have a chief executive officer and a management committee that has been tasked with supervising all cricketing activities. These changes have been made by the [PCB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) in order to decentralise the administrative body so that it can limit itself to a supervisory role by delegating responsibilities related to the development of the sport to the provincial associations.[^cite_note-auto1-25] This tiered structure has been enshrined in the [PCB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) constitution.[^cite_note-28]

An nationwide inter-city franchise T20 tournament, the [Pakistan Super League](38199013.md), was inaugurated in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Pakistan_Super_League). In 2021, a franchise T20 tournament based in [Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir) was launched, titled the [Kashmir Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir_Premier_League_\(Pakistan\)).
