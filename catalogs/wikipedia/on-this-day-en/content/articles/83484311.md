A series of [wildfires](56106.md) have been burning throughout the [U.S. state](18618239.md) of [Nevada](21216.md). There have been two fatalities and nine injuries.

## Background



Drought monitor of Nevada on June 9, 2026

The typical fire season in Nevada lasts from May to October, the time when vegetation is the driest.[^cite_note-1] However, the timing varies every year based on a number of other factors, including if there is hot, dry weather, the amount of dry vegetation, and when there are more natural causes possible, such as lightning. The peak time of the season is also determined by these factors.[^cite_note-2] Historically, wildfire severity has increased due to large amounts of dry fuel, and acreage typically increases in [drought](37335.md)-like and heavy precipitation years.[^cite_note-3]

## Summary

### Preseason conditions and outlook

Nevada had record low [snowpack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowpack) levels after the [2025–26 winter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025-26_North_American_winter), with many measuring cites in Northern and Eastern having all snow melted between one and two months before normal. Significant snowpack was only noted in areas above 8,000 feet (2,400 m) in elevation in early June. The [snowmelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowmelt) in lower elevations has exposed vegetation fuel. Significant [drought](37335.md) throughout the state has dried those fuels out, with all areas of the state reported to be at least "abnormally dry".[^cite_note-4] [Humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humidity) levels have been dropping into the single digits, further drying vegetation. Above-average temperatures have been reported in [Western Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Nevada), with low temperatures 60–70 °F (16–21 °C) and high temperatures potentially reaching triple digits, with forecasts predicting extreme temperatures to last through August.[^cite_note-5] The [National Interagency Fire Center](5818754.md) (NIFC) has reported wildfire risk for [Southern Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Nevada) in June and wildfire potential across Northern Nevada in July and August.[^cite_note-6]

### Events

[Dry thunderstorms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_thunderstorm) occurred in [Lincoln County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_County,_Nevada) in mid–June, igniting the Grapevine and Kane Springs fires south of [Caliente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caliente,_Nevada).[^cite_note-7] The Grapevine Fire prompted level one "ready" evacuations in [Washington County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_County,_Utah), [Utah](31716.md).[^cite_note-wd1-8] The fires spread rapidly due to strong winds and low [relative humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_humidity).[^cite_note-9] Lightning sparked the Dry Canyon and Parsnip Peak fires on July 9,[^cite_note-iw1-10][^cite_note-iw2-11] driven by low humidity, strong winds, and extremely dry vegetation.[^cite_note-12] Five large wildfires ignited across Northern and Southern Nevada: the Mouse Meadows and Quail Springs fires in [Nevada Test and Training Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevada_Test_and_Training_Range), the New Pass Fire northwest of [Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Nevada), the Chimney Fire in western [Elko County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elko_County,_Nevada), and the Secret Pass Fire in [Ruby Mountain State Recreation Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruby_Mountain_State_Recreation_Area?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-13]

The [Bug Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bug_Fire) over the [Truckee Meadows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truckee_Meadows) on August 10

On August 8, the [Bug Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bug_Fire) ignited in [Lassen County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lassen_County,_California), [California](5407.md), rapidly spreading into [Sierra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_County,_California) and [Washoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washoe_County,_Nevada) counties, prompting evacuations.[^cite_note-wd2-14] The Fred Mountain Fire ignited a day later; it also caused evacuations and power outages that overlapped with the Bug Fire.[^cite_note-15] The Stallion Fire, which started on August 10 and is burning south of [Pyramid Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramid_Lake_\(Nevada\)), has closed the lake and is prompting evacuation warnings in and around [Nixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nixon,_Nevada).[^cite_note-16] On August 10, Governor [Joe Lombardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Lombardo) declared a [state of emergency](298608.md) for the Bug and Fred Mountain fires;[^cite_note-17] the Fred Mountain Fire merged with the Bug Fire on August 11.[^cite_note-wd2-14] A woman was arrested for third–degree [arson](184695.md) and possession of a [controlled substance](2471575.md).[^cite_note-18] While evacuating from the Bug Fire on August 12, an elderly couple was injured. The wife with [Parkinson's disease](22228064.md) died on August 15 from severe burns and smoke inhalation.[^cite_note-ktvn2-19] Overall, the two wildfires have forced evacuations for 5,000[^cite_note-upr1-20] to over 6,000 residents[^cite_note-ktvn1-21] and 300 large animals,[^cite_note-upr1-20] caused power outages for up to 6,400 customers,[^cite_note-22] and destroyed nine residential structures.[^cite_note-ktvn1-21]



The [Hawk Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawk_Fire) seen from South [Reno](26388.md) on August 22

On August 22, the [Hawk Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawk_Fire) started in [Humboldt–Toiyabe National Forest](224701.md) near [Peavine Peak](20441703.md) from human origin. [U.S. Route 395](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_395_in_Nevada) was closed and approximately 59,000 [NV Energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NV_Energy) customers lost power on August 22.[^cite_note-kolo1-23] [Reno Stead Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reno_Stead_Airport) temporarily closed to all operations aside from [firefighting aircraft](1143974.md). Three civilians and three firefighters have been injured,[^cite_note-tg1-24] and an 83–year–old woman died from burns and smoke inhalation.[^cite_note-sfgate1-25] Up to 42,000 residents were under evacuation orders[^cite_note-abc1-26] with another 45,000 in evacuation warning zones.[^cite_note-tg1-24] The Hawk Fire destroyed 48 structures and damaged 14, making it the most destructive wildfire in Nevada history.[^cite_note-kunr1-27][^cite_note-28]



Smoke clouds from the Floriston Fire on September 16, seen from Reno

The Floriston Fire that burned near [Floriston, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floriston,_California), starting on September 16. The [Truckee Meadows Fire Protection District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truckee_Meadows_Fire_Protection_District) assumed control of the fire in conjunction with the [California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Department_of_Forestry_and_Fire_Protection) (CAL FIRE), [California Highway Patrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Highway_Patrol) (CHP), and the [United States Forest Service](42652.md).[^cite_note-floristoncalfire-29][^cite_note-floristonrgj-30] While the fire burned entirely in [Nevada County, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevada_County,_California) and prompted evacuation orders only in California, evacuation warnings were issued near [Verdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verdi,_Nevada) and westbound [Interstate 80](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_80_in_Nevada) was closed due to rockfalls. The fire burned 635.9 acres (257.3 ha)[^cite_note-floristonwd-31] and was fully contained on September 24.[^cite_note-floristoncalfire-29] [J Resort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J_Resort), [Silver Legacy Resort and Casino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Legacy_Resort_and_Casino), [Eldorado Resort Casino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eldorado_Resort_Casino), [Circus Circus Reno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circus_Circus_Reno), and the [Grand Sierra Resort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Sierra_Resort) offered discounted room rates to evacuees. The [Northern Nevada Public Health Air Quality Management Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Nevada_Public_Health_Air_Quality_Management_Division?action=edit\&redlink=1) issued an air quality alert near Verdi as air quality reached unhealthy for sensitive groups with an air quality index measure of 109.[^cite_note-floristonrgj-30]
