**Al-Omar field** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): حقل العمر النفطي) is an [oil field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oilfield) in [Deir ez-Zor Governorate](7445749.md), Syria. It is located 10 kilometres (6.2 mi) north of [Mayadin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayadin), east of the [Euphrates river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphrates_river).

## History

Al-Omar oil field, discovered in 1986, was operated by Al-Furat Petroleum Company (AFPC), subsidiary of the [Syrian Petroleum Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Petroleum_Company). It initially peaked at a production of around 80,000 barrels per day (b/d). However, due to a lack of pressure support and overproduction in the early 1990s, the field was damaged, leading to a sharp decline in output to 16,000 b/d by 1991. [Water injection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_injection_\(oil_production\)) techniques were implemented from 1991 onwards, stabilizing production at a plateau of 60,000–70,000 b/d between 1994 and 1997. By 2008, however, production had declined again to around 20,000 b/d.[^cite_note-1] In the meantime, the [STOIIP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STOIIP) was estimated at 760 million barrels.[^cite_note-2]

During the [Syrian civil war](30741795.md), the field changed hands multiple times. It fell under the control of the [Al-Nusra Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Nusra_Front) and other [Islamist](15012.md) rebels in November 2013,[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] then [ISIS](9087364.md) in July 2014 from the [Al-Shaitat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Shaitat) clan which later resulted in a series of [massacres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Shaitat_massacres), and ultimately the [Syrian Democratic Forces](48205248.md) (SDF) with support from the [US-led Coalition](43683196.md) in late October 2017 during the [Eastern Syria campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Syria_campaign).[^cite_note-5] Since then, the region has been targeted by pro-Iranian militias, opposing the U.S. presence in the area.[^cite_note-6]

On 18 January 2026, the army of the [Syrian transitional government](79584988.md) retook the field following the SDF's withdrawal during the [2026 northeastern Syria offensive](82106178.md).[^cite_note-7]
