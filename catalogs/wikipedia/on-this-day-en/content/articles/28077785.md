The **Union of Republican Forces** (**UFR**; [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Union des Forces Républicaines*) was a [liberal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberalism) [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Guinea](12176.md). Founded in 1992, the party was led by the former [Prime Minister of Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Guinea) [Sidya Touré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidya_Tour%C3%A9) since 1999.[^cite_note-1] The party supported the [2007 general strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Guinean_general_strike).[^cite_note-civil-2]

Touré finished in third place in both the [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Guinean_presidential_election) and [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Guinean_presidential_election) presidential elections, with vote shares of 15.6%[^cite_note-3] and 6.0%,[^cite_note-4] respectively.

In 2026, the party was dissolved by the government of president [Mamady Doumbouya](68651894.md).[^cite_note-5]
