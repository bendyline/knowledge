**Terrorism in Colombia** has occurred repeatedly during the last several decades, largely due to the [ongoing armed conflict](1857628.md) the country has been involved in since 1964. Perpetrators of [terrorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist) acts in the country range from [leftist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left-wing_terrorism) [guerrilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guerrilla) forces including [FARC](26100.md), [ELN](822032.md) and [M-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19th_of_April_Movement), to [drug cartels](197015.md) such as the [Medellín Cartel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medell%C3%ADn_Cartel), to [right-wing paramilitary](2873402.md) forces including the [AUC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Self-Defense_Forces_of_Colombia).

## Terror groups

Notable groups involved in terror attacks include:

- [Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia](26100.md) (FARC)

- [National Liberation Army](822032.md) (ELN)

- [19th of April Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19th_of_April_Movement) (M19)

- [Medellin Cartel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medellin_Cartel)

- [AUC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Self-Defense_Forces_of_Colombia)

## Counterterrorism efforts

During the early 21st century, the number of known and suspected terrorists killed, captured, or surrendered fell as [terror groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terror_group) changed their tactics, while casualties rose. The [Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia](26100.md) (FARC) reverted to hit-and-run attacks rather than engage in large unit encounters.[^cite_note-US2011-1]

In November, 2011, security forces killed FARC leader [Guillermo León Sáenz Vargas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Cano), also known as "Alfonso Cano."[^cite_note-US2011-1]

### Demobilization and reintegration efforts

Colombia has employed a multi-agency approach to countering radicalization and discouraging [violent extremism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violent_extremism). The [government](76741.md)'s program focuses on encouraging individual members units of the FARC and ELN to demobilize and reintegrate into society. [Demobilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demobilization) and reintegration programs provide medical care, [psychological counseling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_counseling), education benefits, and [job placement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_placement) assistance. Recidivism rates were estimated at between 10 and 20 percent by the Colombian Agency for Reintegration. Additionally, the Ministry of Defense has organized a number of public [festivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festival) and social events with celebrity participation to discourage the recruitment of vulnerable youth.[^cite_note-US2013-2]

In 2013, a total of 1,350 FARC and ELN members had demobilized.[^cite_note-US2013-2]

### 2011

- **February 11, 2011** – Five [civilians](145804.md) were killed in [San Miguel, Putumayo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Miguel,_Putumayo) in a [mortar](324499.md) attack. The mortar was fired by suspected FARC terrorists and landed near a police post along with a baby.

  [^cite_note-US2011-1]

- **June 25, 2011** – Suspected ELN rebels attacked a police outpost in Colon Genova, Narino, using explosives and small-arms fire. Eight civilians were killed.

  [^cite_note-US2011-1]

- **September 18, 2011** – Several mortars were launched at an [army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_Army) base in [La Macarena, Meta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Macarena,_Meta). The attack injured several civilians.

  [^cite_note-US2011-1]

- **October 30, 2011** – The armored convoy of [Albeiro Vanegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albeiro_Vanegas?action=edit\&redlink=1), Vice President of the House of Representatives was attacked by suspected ELN terrorists. Vanegas was unharmed, but his driver was killed.

  [^cite_note-US2011-1]

### 2017

- June 17, 2017: **[Centro Andino bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro_Andino_bombing)** – An explosion at the [Centro Andino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro_Andino) [shopping mall](235723.md) killed 3 people. The attack was condemned as a terrorist attack and the authors remain unknown.

  [^cite_note-3]

### 2018

- January 27, 2018: **[2018 Colombia police stations attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Colombia_police_stations_attacks)** – Two hand [grenades](20353360.md) were thrown at the [police stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_station) of [Barranquilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barranquilla) and [Santa Rosa del Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Rosa_del_Sur), killing seven officers and one perpetrator. The ELN was responsible for the attack.

  [^cite_note-4]

### 2019

- January 17, 2019: **[2019 Bogotá car bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Bogot%C3%A1_car_bombing)** – The guerrilla rebel group ELN took the responsibility of the [explosion](18993857.md) of a [car bomb](155282.md) inside the [General Santander National Police Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Santander_National_Police_Academy). The attack killed 21 people and injured several others.

  [^cite_note-5]
