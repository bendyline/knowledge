The **Dormition Cathedral** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Собор Успіння Пресвятої Богородиці, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *Sobor Uspinnia Presviatoi Bohorodytsi*) of the [Kyiv Pechersk Lavra](478837.md), also referred to as the **Great Church**, is the main cathedral of the monastery complex. In the time of the [Kyivan Rus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyivan_Rus) the cathedral also served as the [necropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necropolis) for the Kyivan princes.

The Dormition Cathedral is an important landmark of the Kyiv Pechersk Lavra National Reserve.[^cite_note-1]

## History

The original church was built on initiative of [Theodosius of Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodosius_of_Kiev) in 1073–1078.[^cite_note-2] The construction was primarily financed by [Sviatoslav II of Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sviatoslav_II_of_Kiev).[^cite_note-3] According to the [Kyiv Caves Patericon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_Caves_Patericon), several Byzantine masters arrived from [Constantinople](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantinople). Several legends exist related to construction of the church, particularly one connected with the [Varangian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varangian) warrior [Shimon the Varangian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimon_the_Varangian).[^cite_note-4]

Dormition Cathedral has been damaged and restored numerous times. In 13th century it was damaged by the 1230 Kyiv earthquake and the 1240 [siege of Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Kiev_\(1240\)) by Khan Batu. The cathedral was restored in 1470, but in 1482 it was damaged again during the Crimean raid of [Meñli I Giray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Me%C3%B1li_I_Giray). It was eventually restored again. In 1718 it was damaged during a Kyiv city fire. In 1729 cathedral was restored in the [Ukrainian Baroque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Baroque) style and expanded.

In 1928, the monastery was converted into an anti-religious museum park by the Soviet authorities. The main church of the monastery was destroyed during [World War II](32927.md), two months after the [Wehrmacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht) occupied the city of [Kyiv](585629.md). Retreating Soviet troops conducted the controversial [1941 Khreshchatyk explosions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1941_Khreshchatyk_explosions). [Scorched earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scorched_earth) tactics were also applied to the Kyiv bridges over [Dnieper](380138.md) as well as the main Khreshchatyk street and Kyiv Pechersk Lavra.[^cite_note-\:1-5] (The destruction of the cathedral followed a pattern of Soviet disregard for cultural heritage, as they previously blew up the ancient [St. Michael's Golden-Domed Monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Michael's_Golden-Domed_Monastery) nearby in the 1930s.)[^cite_note-6]

The destruction of the cathedral on 3 November 1941 has been a matter of some historical controversy, the [NKVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NKVD) having had mined the building on 16–17 September before evacuating the city. Soviet authorities in the 1960s claimed that Soviet saboteurs under [Ivan Kudrya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Kudrya) were responsible for the demolition, whereas [Albert Speer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Speer) in his [memoirs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memoirs) wrote that German Reichskommissar [Erich Koch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erich_Koch) had ordered the demolition.

The church was finally restored in 1995 after Ukraine regained its independence. Its reconstruction was accomplished in two years, and the new Dormition Church consecrated in 2000.[^cite_note-\:1-5]



Floor plan of the church (east at top)

On 15 June 2026 at 2\:00 a.m. Russia struck the Dormition Cathedral during a mass drone and missile attack on several cities across Ukraine, setting the church on fire.[^cite_note-cnn20260614-7][^cite_note-rbc20260615-8] The fire was extinguished by the emergency services at 8\:35 a.m.[^cite_note-9] Some 800 m² of the building was damaged.[^cite_note-10] The [Security Service of Ukraine](709222.md) (SBU) stated that at least two [Geran-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geran-2) drones had struck the building, their remains being found at the site.[^cite_note-11] Ukrainian Prime Minister [Yulia Svyrydenko](69191807.md) condemned the attack as a "brutal assault on our people and our heritage."[^cite_note-12] The attacks killed at least four people in Kyiv.[^cite_note-13]
