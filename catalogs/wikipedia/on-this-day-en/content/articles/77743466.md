The **2025 Singapore Grand Prix** (officially known as the **Formula 1 Singapore Airlines Singapore Grand Prix 2025**) was a [Formula One](10854.md) motor race held on 5 October 2025 at the [Marina Bay Street Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Bay_Street_Circuit) in [Marina Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Bay,_Singapore), [Singapore](27318.md). It was the eighteenth round of the [2025 Formula One World Championship](72768531.md). [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) of [Mercedes](25098794.md) converted his pole position into a win, ahead of [Red Bull](1173193.md)'s [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) and [McLaren](20994.md)'s [Lando Norris](48978813.md).&#x20;

McLaren won its tenth Constructors' Championship at this event, and second in a row; this marked the first time McLaren had won consecutive Constructors' Championships since [1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Formula_One_World_Championship), and the first Singapore Grand Prix to feature the crowning of a [World Constructors' Champion](3437393.md). This was also the first Singapore Grand Prix to not feature a retirement.[^cite_note-\:0-3]&#x20;

The top five remained unchanged for both the Drivers' and Constructors' Championship, with Norris reducing his points deficit to 22 points behind championship leader [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md). Defending champion Verstappen remained third, a further 41 points behind. In the fight for second place in the Constructors' Standings, Mercedes extended the gap to 27 points over Ferrari, who were seven points clear from fourth-placed Red Bull following McLaren's successful title defense, as they secured their second consecutive Constructors' title, and their tenth overall.&#x20;

## Background

The event was held at the [Marina Bay Street Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Bay_Street_Circuit) in [Marina Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Bay,_Singapore) for the 16th time in the circuit's history, across the weekend of 3–5 October.[^cite_note-4] The Grand Prix was the eighteenth round of the [2025 Formula One World Championship](72768531.md) and the 16th running of the [Singapore Grand Prix](9305401.md) as a round of the Formula One World Championship.[^cite_note-5]&#x20;

### Championship standings before the race

Going into the weekend, [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) led the [Drivers' Championship](3437295.md) with 324 points, 25 points ahead of teammate [Lando Norris](48978813.md) in second and 69 ahead of [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) in third. [McLaren](20994.md), with 634 points, led the [Constructors' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Constructors%27_Championship) ahead of [Mercedes](25098794.md) and [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari), who were second and third with 290 and 286 points, respectively.[^cite_note-6]&#x20;

#### Championship permutations

[World Constructors' Championship](3437393.md) leader McLaren had an opportunity to secure its tenth title at this event, and their second in a row. With seven rounds remaining in the season, a maximum of 346 points could still be scored. McLaren had an opportunity to secure the title if they scored just 13 points, regardless of where either Mercedes or Ferrari finished. As such, winning the race or scoring a podium with one of their drivers would secure the title; if either prerequisite was not met, both drivers would at least need to finish above the mentioned teams to do so. Mercedes would need to outscore McLaren by 31 points, and Ferrari by 35, to disallow them from achieving the title in this event. [Red Bull](1173193.md) had already been eliminated from contention, for the second season in a row, following the preceding [Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-7]&#x20;

### Entrants

See also: [2025 Formula One World Championship § Entries](72768531.md#Entries)

The drivers and teams were the same as published in the season entry list with two exceptions;[^cite_note-8] [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda) at [Red Bull Racing](1173193.md) held the seat originally held by [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) before Lawson was demoted back to [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls) from the [Japanese Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Japanese_Grand_Prix) onward,[^cite_note-9] and [Franco Colapinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Colapinto) replaced [Jack Doohan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Doohan) at [Alpine](65240620.md) from the [Emilia Romagna Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Emilia_Romagna_Grand_Prix) onward until at least the [Austrian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Austrian_Grand_Prix) on a rotating seat basis.[^cite_note-10] Before the race at [Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spielberg,_Styria), it was confirmed that Colapinto would retain his seat with the team, effectively on a race-by-race basis.[^cite_note-11]&#x20;

### Tyre choices

Further information: [Formula One tyres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_tyres)

Tyre supplier [Pirelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirelli) brought the C3, C4 and C5 tyre compounds (designated hard, medium, and soft, respectively) for teams to use at the event.[^cite_note-12]&#x20;

### Circuit change

The pit lane was widened by one metre; as such, the pit lane speed limit was increased from 60 km/h (37 mph) to 80 km/h (50 mph).[^cite_note-CircuitBreakdownNYT-13]&#x20;

### Weather forecast

Ahead of the weekend, the [Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile](248019.md) (FIA) received a forecast predicting that the heat index would be greater than 31 °C (88 °F) at some time during the race. As a result, the FIA declared a "heat hazard"; in these conditions, teams may either equip their drivers with cooling systems, with the minimum weight limit being raised by 5 kilograms (11 lb) to compensate for the equipment, or add additional 0.5 kilograms (1 lb) of ballast in the cockpit.[^cite_note-14] This change was introduced in 2025 to prevent driver overheating, as observed during the [2023 Qatar Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Qatar_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-15]&#x20;

## Practice

Three [free practice sessions](645533.md#Free_practice) were held for the event.[^cite_note-Cal-1] The first free practice session was held on 3 October 2025, at 17\:30 local time ([UTC+8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC%2B8)),[^cite_note-Cal-1] and was topped by [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) ahead of [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) and [Max Verstappen](41758713.md). [Alexander Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Albon) had a brake fire early into the session, which meant he did not set any time.[^cite_note-16]&#x20;

The second free practice session was held on the same day, at 21\:00 local time,[^cite_note-Cal-1] and was topped by [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) ahead of [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) and Verstappen. The session was affected by a red flag after [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) hit the wall at turns 16–17, breaking his front wing. Another red flag occurred shortly after the session resumed, with [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) crashing his car after hitting the wall at turn 16 and stopping at the pit entrance.[^cite_note-17] Leclerc was released from the pit lane early and crashed with Norris, damaging the front wing on the McLaren. Ferrari were fined €10,000 as a result.[^cite_note-18] The third practice session was held on 4 October 2025, at 17\:30 local time,[^cite_note-Cal-1] and was topped by Verstappen ahead of Piastri and Russell. A red flag was observed due to Lawson losing the car at turn 7, sending him into the wall.[^cite_note-19]&#x20;

## Qualifying

[Qualifying](645533.md#Current_format) was held on 4 October 2025, at 21\:00 local time ([UTC+8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC%2B8)), and determined the starting grid order for the race.[^cite_note-Cal-1]&#x20;

### Qualifying report

[George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) took pole position for the race, driving for [Mercedes](25098794.md), his second pole of the 2025 season. [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) qualified in second, and Drivers' Championship leader [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) qualified in third.[^cite_note-\:1-20] [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing) drivers [Alex Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Albon) and [Carlos Sainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.) qualified in 12th and 13th initially, but after an investigation post-qualifying, they were both disqualified for breaching the technical regulations.[^cite_note-\:2-21]&#x20;

## Race



[George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) took his second victory of the season ahead of Verstappen and Norris.

The race was held on 5 October 2025, at 20\:00 local time ([UTC+8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC%2B8)), and was run for 62 laps.[^cite_note-Cal-1]&#x20;

### Race report

[George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) had a good start from pole position. [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) opted to start on soft tyres in an attempt take the lead from Russell, but had no advantage as Russell kept the lead into turn 1.[^cite_note-23] [Lando Norris](48978813.md) gained two positions in the opening lap, but sustained damage to his front wing as he collided with his [McLaren](20994.md) teammate [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) and the back of Verstappen's [Red Bull](1173193.md) after going too deep into turn 3, dropping Piastri from third to fifth.[^cite_note-24]&#x20;

In the midfield, [Gabriel Bortoleto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto) and [Lance Stroll](42274711.md) made contact in turn 1 of the opening lap.[^cite_note-25] Further back, [Alexander Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Albon) and [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md) started from the pit lane after making changes to the [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing) and [Alpine](65240620.md) cars under parc fermé conditions.[^cite_note-Starting_grid-22] Piastri suffered a slow pit stop at 5.2 seconds and [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) also suffered a slow 9.2 second pit stop in the [Aston Martin](63473867.md).[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] Russell comfortably won the race ahead of Verstappen and Norris, whose third place finish and Piastri's fourth were enough to secure the Constructors' Championship for McLaren. [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) finished in fifth, [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) in sixth, and Alonso, [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton), [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman), and [Carlos Sainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.) rounded out the top ten. [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) suffered from a power issue for most of the race and finished outside of the points, despite qualifying in eighth.[^cite_note-28]&#x20;

### Post-race

[McLaren](20994.md) secured the 2025 Constructors' Championship with 650 points, their second consecutive title victory. [Mercedes](25098794.md) (325 points) extended their points lead over [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) (298 points) in the battle for second place in the Constructors' Championship.[^cite_note-29] Piastri retained his Driver's Championship lead by 22 points over teammate Norris, and 63 points over rival Verstappen.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Hamilton was given a 5-second time penalty for track limits, dropping him to eighth in the final race classification.[^cite_note-Race_result-30]&#x20;
