**Holosiivskyi District**[^cite_note-1] is an [urban district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_districts_of_Ukraine) of the city of [Kyiv](585629.md), the capital of [Ukraine](31750.md).

Holosiivskyi District was created during the change of administrative divisions of the capital of Ukraine, which was conducted in September 2001, as per the decision of the [Kyiv City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_City_Council) on January 1, 2001.

Holosiivskyi District is located in the southwestern part of Kyiv, bordering [Shevchenkivskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shevchenkivskyi_District,_Kyiv), [Solomianskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solomianka_Raion), [Pecherskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pecherskyi_District), and [Darnytskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darnytsia) districts of Kyiv, and [Bucha](64573437.md), [Obukhiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obukhiv_Raion), and [Boryspil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boryspil_Raion) raions of [Kyiv Oblast](494884.md).

The territory of the district begins from the [Square of Ukrainian Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_of_Ukrainian_Heroes) in the north and stretches toward the southwest of Kyiv. Considering this, the raion is somewhat like the southwest entrance into the city. The district consists of the following [historical neighborhoods of Kyiv](3136521.md#Historical_neighborhoods): Bahrynova Hora, [Demiivka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demiivka), Dobryi Shliakh, [Feofaniia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feofaniia), Holosiiv, [Koncha-Zaspa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koncha-Zaspa), Korchuvate, Kytaiv, [Lysa Hora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lysa_Hora_\(Kyiv\)), Mysholovka, [Nova Zabudova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nova_Zabudova), Nyzhnia Telychka, Pankivshchyna, Predslavyno, [Pyrohiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrohiv), Samburky, Saperna Slobidka, Shyrma, [Teremky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teremky), Tserkovshchyna, Tsymbaliv Yar, Vita-Lytovska, Yamky, and Vodnykiv Island.

[Holosiivskyi National Nature Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holosiivskyi_National_Nature_Park), [Expocenter of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expocenter_of_Ukraine) and the [National Museum of Folk Architecture and Folkways of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Museum_of_Folk_Architecture_and_Folkways_of_Ukraine) are located in the district.
