The **ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary of the Russian Federation to the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland**, or formally the **ambassador of the Russian Federation to the Court of St James's**, is the official representative of the [president](239646.md) and the [government of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Russian_Federation) to the [monarch](47063.md) and the [government of the United Kingdom](25318118.md).

The ambassador and his staff work at large in the [Russian Embassy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Russia,_London) in [London](17867.md), while the official residence of the ambassador is [13 Kensington Palace Gardens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13_Kensington_Palace_Gardens). There is a [consulate general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consulate_general) in [Edinburgh](9602.md).[^cite_note-2]

The post of ambassador to the United Kingdom is currently vacant, after [the previous incumbent](62277905.md) was withdrawn on 21 July 2026.[^cite_note-em-3]

## History of diplomatic relations

Main article: [Russia–United Kingdom relations](12342392.md)

- **1553:** Beginning of diplomatic relations.

- **1706:** Establishment of the permanent mission in the [Kingdom of England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_England).

- **14 November 1720:** Relations freezes as [Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Great_Britain) (England's successor) refuse to recognize [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) as the [empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire).

- **1730:** Restoration of diplomatic relations.

- **1741–1748:** Allies in the [Austrian War of Succession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_War_of_Succession).

- **1756–1763:** Opponents in the [Seven Years' War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Years'_War).

- **22 November 1800:** [Emperor Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Paul) imposed sanctions on Great Britain, diplomatic relations interrupted.

- **24 March 1801:** The new [Emperor Alexander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Alexander_I) cancels the sanctions on the request of [British monarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Monarch) [George III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_III_of_the_United_Kingdom), relations restored.

- **25 March 1802:** [Amiens peace treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Amiens) signed.

- **1803–1805:** Allies in the coalition against France.

The United Kingdom established diplomatic relations with the Soviet Union in 1924. However, King [George V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_V) was still upset over the [execution of the Romanov family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Execution_of_the_Romanov_family) and refused to receive the Soviet ambassador. In a breach of [diplomatic protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protocol_\(diplomacy\)), he dispatched the [Prince of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_VIII) to accept the Soviet ambassador's [credentials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_of_credence).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
