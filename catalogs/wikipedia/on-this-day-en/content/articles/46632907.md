# List of storms named Dolphin

The name **Dolphin** has been used for four [tropical cyclones](8282374.md) in the western North Pacific Ocean. The name was contributed by [Hong Kong](13404.md) and refers to the [Chinese white dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_white_dolphin) (*Sousa chinensis*), a mascot in Hong Kong.[^cite_note-1] It replaced the name *[Yanyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Yanyan)*, which was retired after the [2003 Pacific typhoon season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_Pacific_typhoon_season).

- [Typhoon Dolphin (2008)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Dolphin_\(2008\)) (T0822, 27W, Ulysses) – Category 2 typhoon that did not affect land.

- [Typhoon Dolphin (2015)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Dolphin_\(2015\)) (T1507, 07W) — Category 5 super typhoon that churned though the open ocean.

- [Severe Tropical Storm Dolphin (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Pacific_typhoon_season#Severe_Tropical_Storm_Dolphin_\(Marce\)) (T2012, 14W, Marce) – paralleled the southeastern coast of Japan, remained well offshore.

- [Typhoon Dolphin (2026)](83805947.md) (T2613, 12W) – another Category 5 super typhoon that affected the Ryukyu Islands and China
