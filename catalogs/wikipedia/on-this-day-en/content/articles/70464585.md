The **fifth Orbán government** was the 73rd [Government of Hungary](17048864.md) and the fourth cabinet of the [second Orbán premiership](81566732.md), from 24 May 2022 to 9 May 2026,[^cite_note-1] following the [2022 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Hungarian_parliamentary_election), led by [Viktor Orbán](750582.md).

## Policy

### Social policy

On 15 September 2022, the Hungarian government passed new abortion restrictions, with a [mandatory ultrasounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_ultrasound) bill, where women who are seeking an abortion will now be obliged to "listen to the foetal heartbeat" before they can have an abortion. This bill was lobbied for by the far-right [Mi Hazánk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Homeland_Movement) (Our Homeland) party.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

In March 2025, the Hungarian government [voted in favour of](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Hungarian_Pride_ban) banning the [Budapest Pride](18367052.md), promising to impose fines on both organizers and participants who would attempt to attend the march. The Law passed by 136 votes to 27. [Fidesz–KDNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidesz%E2%80%93KDNP), [Mi Hazánk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mi_Haz%C3%A1nk) and [Jobbik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jobbik) voted for the law.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

In April 2025, the Hungarian parliament passed [constitutional amendments](261586.md), it will protect the "[right to use cash](55336.md#Cash_in_constitutions)", mandate the existence of [only two genders (male or female)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_binary), and Hungarian citizenship of dual citizens may be suspended for definite periods of time. The Law passed by 140 votes to 21.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] [Fidesz-KDNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidesz-KDNP) and [Mi Hazánk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mi_Haz%C3%A1nk) supported the amendments.[^cite_note-9]

### Foreign policy

Main articles: [2024 visits by Viktor Orbán to Russia and China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_visits_by_Viktor_Orb%C3%A1n_to_Russia_and_China) and [2025 Budapest Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Budapest_Summit)

In December 2023, the European Council agreed to open [accession negotiations with Ukraine](36830143.md), as well as Moldova.[^cite_note-bbc1412-10][^cite_note-11] [Hungary](13275.md) under Orban did not [veto](74661.md) the move. Prime Minister [Viktor Orban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Orban) left the room momentarily in what officials described as a pre-agreed and constructive manner, while the other 26 leaders went ahead with the vote.

In June 2024, Orban helped to form the [Patriots for Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriots_for_Europe) group in the European Parliament.[^cite_note-newEUalliance-12][^cite_note-dw-13]

In April 2025, the Hungarian Government announced it will withdraw from the [International Criminal Court](14880.md).[^cite_note-israel-14]

In May 2025, the [Hungarian National Assembly](585416.md) approved a bill to initiate the country's withdrawal from the [International Criminal Court](14880.md) (ICC), marking Hungary as the first European Union member state to take such action. The bill, introduced by Deputy Prime Minister [Zsolt Semjén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zsolt_Semj%C3%A9n), passed with 134 votes in favor, 37 against, and 7 abstentions.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

In 2024 and 2026, The Orban government gave [political asylum](1621432.md) to Polish conservative politicians [Marcin Romanowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcin_Romanowski) and [Zbigniew Ziobro](5816176.md).

### Schengen policy

On 5 August 2024, 70 [members of the European Parliament](258909.md) officially requested that Hungary's membership in the [Schengen Area](16565139.md) be cancelled, because they felt that a recent Hungarian decision to open Hungarian visas to workers from eight countries, among which were [Russia](25391.md) and [Belarus](3457.md), was contrary to the spirit of the agreement.[^cite_note-tass1-17]

### Economic policy

On 6 June 2024, the [Hungarian government](17048864.md) bought back ownership of [Budapest Ferenc Liszt International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest_Ferenc_Liszt_International_Airport). The Hungarian government will own 80% while [Vinci Airports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinci_Airports) will take 20%.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] The airport was sold off in 2005.
