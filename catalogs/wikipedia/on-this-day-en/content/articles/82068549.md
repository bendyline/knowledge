The **12th Central Committee** (**12th CC**), officially stylised as *XII Central Committee of the [Lao People's Revolutionary Party](203651.md) (LPRP)*, is composed of 73 members and 15 alternates. It was elected by the [12th National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_National_Congress_of_the_Lao_People's_Revolutionary_Party?action=edit\&redlink=1) on 8 January 2026.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEStrangio2026-1] It convened for its 1st Plenary Session on the last day of the 12th National Congress, and elected the [general secretary](30663327.md), the [permanent member of the Secretariat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permanent_Member_of_the_Lao_People's_Revolutionary_Party_Central_Committee's_Secretariat), and the [inspection commission chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Inspection_Commission_of_the_Lao_People's_Revolutionary_Party?action=edit\&redlink=1) as well as the [12th Politburo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_Politburo_of_the_Lao_People's_Revolutionary_Party), [12th Secretariat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_Secretariat_of_the_Lao_People's_Revolutionary_Party), and the [12th Inspection Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_Inspection_Commission_of_the_Lao_People's_Revolutionary_Party?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEStrangio2026''Vientiane_Times''2026-2]
