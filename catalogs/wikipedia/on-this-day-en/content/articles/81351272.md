**Ernesto Julio Álvarez Miranda** (born 21 May 1961) is a Peruvian jurist and academic who served as the [Prime Minister of Peru](1900295.md) from October 2025 to February 2026 during the [presidency of José Jerí](81305838.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] He currently serves as [Minister of Justice and Human Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_and_Human_Rights_\(Peru\)) under [Keiko Fujimori](4643445.md). A ranking member of the [Christian People's Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_People's_Party_\(Peru\)), he serves as chair of the party's advisory council since 2023.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The [Associated Press](18935732.md) described him as having [ultraconservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultraconservative) political views.[^cite_note-5]

Prior to his stint in government, he served as an associate justice of the [Constitutional Court of Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Peru) between 2007 and 2014, serving as president of the Court during 2012.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

He is a columnist for the newspapers *[Expreso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expreso)* and *El Montonero*.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]
