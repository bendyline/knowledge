The **Embassy of the United States of America in Rome** is the [diplomatic mission](8970.md) of the [United States of America](3434750.md) to the [Italian Republic](14532.md). The embassy's chancery is situated in the [Palazzo Margherita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palazzo_Margherita), [Via Vittorio Veneto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Via_Veneto), [Rome](25458.md). The United States also maintains [consulates general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consulate_general) in [Milan](36511.md), [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consulate-General_of_the_United_States,_Florence) and [Naples](55880.md), and consular agencies in [Genoa](47332321.md), [Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palermo), and [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice).[^cite_note-2] The diplomatic mission comprises several sections and offices, such as the public affairs section and its cultural office.[^cite_note-3] The current United States Ambassador to Italy is businessman [Tilman Fertitta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilman_Fertitta).

Two other American diplomatic missions are located in Rome. The [Embassy of the United States to the Holy See](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_the_United_States_to_the_Holy_See), previously located on [Aventine Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aventine_Hill), moved to new headquarters in September 2015 in a separate building on the same compound as the United States Embassy Rome,[^cite_note-4] while the [United States Mission to the UN Agencies in Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Mission_to_the_UN_Agencies_in_Rome) is located in a third building on the same compound since December 2011, when they moved from their former location at Piazza del Popolo.[^cite_note-5]

## Terrorism

On June 10, 1987, a [rocket](26301.md) attack hit the embassy. However, no one was hurt. [Junzo Okudaira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junzo_Okudaira), a member of the [Japanese Red Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Red_Army), was suspected to have carried out the attack.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
