The **Law of Austria** are the collection of laws that apply at the Federal level in Austria. It is founded on the [Federal Constitutional Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Constitutional_Law) of 1920. It can be divided into [public law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_law) and [private law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_law).

## Judiciary system

Main article: [Courts in Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courts_in_Austria)

[Austria](26964606.md) has 113 district courts (Bezirksgerichte)[^cite_note-1], 20 provincial courts (Landesgerichte), and four higher provincial courts (Oberlandesgerichte). There is also a [Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Justice_\(Austria\)) (Oberster Gerichtshof), [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_\(Austria\)) (Verfassungsgerichtshof), and [Supreme Administrative Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Administrative_Court_of_Austria) (Verwaltungsgerichtshof).[^cite_note-2]

## Access to legal information

Information on Austrian law is available using the *Legal Information System of the Republic of Austria* or *Rechtsinformationssystem des Bundes*.[^cite_note-3]

## Life imprisonment

Life sentences can be given in cases of [murder](20171.md)[^cite_note-4] and other crimes resulting in someones death,[^cite_note-5] [genocide](143988.md), [crimes against humanity](61099.md) and [war crimes](48626.md),[^cite_note-6] but also for major drug crimes[^cite_note-7] and severe acts of [(re-)engagement in National Socialist activities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbotsgesetz_1947).[^cite_note-8]

[Parole](342305.md) may be granted to certain inmates in Austria after 15 years' imprisonment, if prison officials are satisfied that the inmate will not [re-offend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recidivism). This is subject to the discretion of a criminal court panel, and a possible appeal to the high court. Alternatively, the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Austria) may grant a [pardon](288593.md) following a motion of the minister of justice. Inmates who committed their crime when they were below the age of 21 cannot be sentenced to life imprisonment. Instead, juvenile offenders can only be sentenced to a maximum of 20 years imprisonment.[^cite_note-9]
