**Vladimiro Selva** (born 19 October 1970) is a Sammarinese politician and a member of the [Grand and General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_and_General_Council), the unicameral parliament of [San Marino](27248.md). He served as [Captain Regent of San Marino](569685.md) from April 2026 to October 2026 along with [Alice Mina](82654433.md). He is affiliated with the political party [Libera San Marino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libera_San_Marino).[^cite_note-Council-1]

He previously served as a member of the [Party of Socialists and Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_of_Socialists_and_Democrats) and later [Democratic Socialist Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Socialist_Left), before joining Libera.[^cite_note-Bio-2]

## Early life and education

Selva was born on 19 October 1970 in San Marino. He graduated in [civil engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_engineering) from the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna).[^cite_note-3]

## Political career

Selva began his political activity in 2005 within the [Party of Socialists and Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_of_Socialists_and_Democrats) following the merger of major left-wing political parties in San Marino.[^cite_note-Bio-2]

He first ran in the [2008 Sammarinese general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Sammarinese_general_election) and was elected to the [Grand and General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_and_General_Council) in 2012. After deciding not to stand in the [2016 Sammarinese general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Sammarinese_general_election) for personal reasons, he returned to parliament following the [2019 Sammarinese general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Sammarinese_general_election) and was later re-elected.[^cite_note-Bio-2][^cite_note-Council-1]

Within the Grand and General Council he has served on parliamentary committees, including the council commission for justice and other institutional bodies.[^cite_note-Report-4]

In March 2026 the leadership of Libera designated Selva as its candidate for [Captain Regent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_Regent_of_San_Marino) for the semester beginning on 1 April 2026.[^cite_note-Libera-5][^cite_note-Fav-6]

## Other activities

Outside politics, Selva has been involved in sports activities. He previously played football in the Sammarinese football league and later became involved as a coach in the [Federazione Sammarinese Sport Speciali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federazione_Sammarinese_Sport_Speciali?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-Sport-7]
