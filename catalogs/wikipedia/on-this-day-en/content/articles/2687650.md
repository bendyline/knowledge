**Badulla** ([Sinhala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhala_language): බදුල්ල, IPA: [\[bəd̪ulːə\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA); [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language): பதுளை,IPA: [\[bɐðuɭɐi̯\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Tamil)) is the capital and the largest city of Uva Province situated in the central hills of [Sri Lanka](26750.md). It is the capital city of [Uva Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uva_Province) and the [Badulla District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badulla_District).

## Geography



Badulu Oya

Badulla is located in the southeast of Kandy, almost encircled by the Badulu Oya River, about 680 m (2,230 ft) above sea level and is surrounded by tea plantations. The city is overshadowed by the [Namunukula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namunukula) range of mountains (highest peak 2,016 m (6,614 ft) above sea level). It was a base of a pre-colonial [Sinhalese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhalese_people) local prince (regional king) who ruled the area under the main King in Kandy before it became part of the [British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire). Later, it became one of the provincial administrative hubs of the British rulers. The city was the terminus of upcountry railway line built by the British in order to take mainly tea plantation products to Colombo.

### Climate

Badulla has a [tropical monsoon climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Am*)[^cite_note-1] characterized by mild temperatures (18 °C-26 °C) and a significant amount of rainfall. Badulla has a [wet season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_season) from October to January, and has cooler temperatures in December to February compared to the rest of the year.

## History and present day



Plate from *[Ceylon, Physical, Historical and Topographical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceylon,_Physical,_Historical_and_Topographical)*, titled "The Coffee Regions. Badulla"

Badulla was an isolated village until the British built roads from Kandy and Nuwara Eliya in the mid 19th century, as part of the growing plantation economy. By the 20th century Badulla had become a regional hub, with the British establishing it as the capital of Uva Wellassa, now known as the [Uva Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uva_Province). Badulla has a number of British colonial buildings, including the [Badulla railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badulla_railway_station), [St Mark's Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Mark%E2%80%99s_Church,_Badulla) and the [Old Welekade Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Welekade_Market). Badulla district is one of the leading tea producing districts, second only behind the Nuwara-Eliya District.

The town has grown steadily since the country's independence from approximately 13,000 in 1946, to 38,000 in 1977 and 47,587 in 2011.[^cite_note-3]

Badulla is a multi-national city with the ancient [Muthiyangana Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muthiyangana_Raja_Maha_Vihara) situated in its heart. The [Catholic Church](606848.md) has a [diocese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Badulla) headquartered here.

## Transport

### Road

Badulla is about 230 km (140 mi) away from Colombo towards the eastern slopes of the central hills of Sri Lanka. There are multiple routes to Badulla from Colombo, Kandy and Galle. From Colombo, one can travel via [Ratnapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratnapura), [Balangoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balangoda), [Haputale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haputale), [Bandarawela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandarawela) and [Hali Ela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hali_Ela?action=edit\&redlink=1) along A4 and A16 to Badulla, which may take 5–6 hours. From Kandy there are two routes: either via "Victoria-Randenigala Raja Mawatha" or via Nuwara Eliya (route A5). From Galle, the best route is via Matara, Hambantota, Wellawaya, Ella, Demodara and Hali Ela (route A2).

### Rail

Main article: [Badulla railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badulla_railway_station)

Badulla [railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_railway_stations_in_Sri_Lanka_by_line#Kandy_to_Badulla) is the last station on the [Main Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_Line_\(Sri_Lanka\)) and is 292.3 km (181.6 mi) away from [Colombo Fort Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombo_Fort_Station).

## Tourist attractions

- **[Muthiyangana temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muthiyangana_Raja_Maha_Vihara)** is an ancient [Buddhist temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist_temple) located in the middle of Badulla. It is regarded as one of the [Solosmasthana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solosmasthana), the sixteen sacred places in [Sri Lanka](26750.md), believed by [Buddhists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist) to have been visited by [Gautama Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautama_Buddha).

- **[Badulla Kataragama Devalaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badulla_Kataragama_Devalaya)** is an ancient [devalaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devalaya), situated in Badulla town. It is a shrine dedicated to Sinhalese deity [Kataragama deviyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kataragama_deviyo).

- **[Old Welekade Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Welekade_Market)** is a historic building, built in 1889.

- **[Dunhinda Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunhinda_Falls)**, a 64-metre-high (210 ft) waterfall, is located five kilometres (three miles) north of Badulla. This is one of the most popular waterfalls of Sri Lanka, mentioned in several famous songs and works of literature. It draws many local and foreign tourists to the region.

- **[Bogoda ancient wooden bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogoda_Wooden_Bridge)** is one of the oldest surviving wooden bridges in the country (being over 400 years old) and is located 13 kilometres (8.1 mi) south of Badulla, close to the town of Hali-ela.

- **[St Mark's Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Mark%E2%80%99s_Church,_Badulla)** was the first church to be consecrated (25 April 1857) by the first [Bishop of Colombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_of_Colombo) Rev. [James Chapman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Chapman_\(bishop\)).
