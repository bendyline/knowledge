**Chittoor** is a city and district headquarters in [Chittoor district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chittoor_district) of the [Indian](14533.md) state of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md). It is also the mandal and divisional headquarters of [Chittoor mandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chittoor_mandal) and [Chittoor revenue division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chittoor_revenue_division,_Andhra_Pradesh) respectively.[^cite_note-map-9] The city has a population of 189,332.[^cite_note-10]

## Etymology

The name Chittoor is believed to have originated from a combination of two words: Chitti and Ooru. In [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language), Chitti means "small" and Ooru means "town" or "village." Thus, Chittoor translates to "small town" or "small village".[^cite_note-11]

Historical records show that Chittoor, due to its strategic location, developed as a trading hub between [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_people) and [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamils) regions, leading to the use of both languages in the town.[^cite_note-12]

## History

After Indian independence in 1947, Chittoor became a part of the erstwhile [Madras State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_State). The modern [Chittoor district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chittoor_district) was formerly [North Arcot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Arcot) district, which was established by the British in the 19th century and had Chittoor as its headquarters. On 1 April 1911, the district was split into two - Chittoor district and North Arcot district.

### Pre-history

The district abounds in several pre-historic sites. The surface finds discovered are assigned to special stages in the progress of civilization. Paleolithic tools were discovered at Tirupathi, Sitarampeta, Ellampalle, Mekalavandlapalle and Piler. Mesolithic tools were discovered at Chinthaparthi, Moratavandlapalle, Aruvandlapalle and Tirupathi.Remains of Neolithic and ancient tools were unearthed near Bangarupalem. The existence of megalithic culture was revealed by the discovery of graves at Irulabanda, Bapanatham, Valimikipuram (Vayalpadu), Sodum, Velkuru, Nyakaneri and Basinikonda.

## Geography

Chittoor city lies on the banks of Neeva River at the southernmost part of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md) state. It is located on the [NH 69](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_69_\(India\)) and [NH 40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_40_\(India\)) linking major metropolitan cities of [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore) and [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai). This district headquarters is 150 km from Chennai, 165 km from Bangalore.[^cite_note-14]

It is located between the northern latitudes of 37" and 14°8" and between the eastern longitudes 78°33" and 79°55". It is bounded on the east by [Tirupati district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirupati_district) of Andhra Pradesh, on the south by [Krishnagiri District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishnagiri), [Vellore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vellore_district), [Tiruvallur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiruvallur_district), [Thirupattur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirupattur_district) districts of Tamil Nadu, on the west by [Kolar district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolar_district) of Karnataka, and on the north by [Annamayya district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annamayya_district) of Andhra Pradesh. In respect of area it takes the eighth place with an area of 15,150 square kilometers which accounts for 5.51 percent of the total area of the state. The general elevation of the mountainous part of the district is 2500 feet above sea level. Chennai & Bangalore cities are located in 150 km. and 165 km. respectively from Chittoor Town.[^cite_note-15] [Vellore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vellore) is the nearest city and twin city located 30 kms across the border with Tamil Nadu.

## Demographics

[Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language) is the official and most widely spoken language.[^cite_note-17] Chittoor has a population of 212,816[^cite_note-18] and has a sex ratio of 1,002 females per 1,000 males compared to the state average of 992 females, as of 2011 census.[^cite_note-19] The literacy rate of the city is 90.60%.[^cite_note-20] Chittoor was upgraded to municipal corporation in 2011. A large minority of Tamil people also live here.

## Governance

Chittoor Municipal Corporation is the civic administrative body of the city. It was constituted as a Grade–III municipality in the year 1917. It was upgraded to Grade–II in 1950, Grade–I in 1965, Special Grade in 1980 and Selection Grade in 2000. On 7 July 2012, it was upgraded to [municipal corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_corporation) by merging 14 [gram panchayats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gram_panchayat) into the corporation and is spread over an area of 69.75 km<sup>2</sup> (26.93 sq mi).[^cite_note-municipality-21]

## Regional festivals

Chittoor district is known for several local cultural festivals celebrated on a grand scale.&#x20;

- **Chittoor Gangamma Jatara**: This annual festival commemorates the killing of a vassal by the goddess Gangamma. The festival is marked by traditional rituals and large-scale community participation in Chittoor.

### Railways

[Chittoor railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chittoor_railway_station) is a National railway station in Chittoor city of Andhra Pradesh. It lies on [Gudur–Katpadi branch line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gudur%E2%80%93Katpadi_branch_line) section and is administered under [Guntakal railway division of South Central Railway zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guntakal_railway_division). Nearest major railway junction is [Katpadi Junction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katpadi_Junction) railway station in Vellore City [Tamil Nadu](29918.md). Just 30 km South from Chittoor city. There are direct trains daily from Chittoor to Vijayawada, Kakinada, Rajamahendravaram, Guntur, Tenali, Visakhapatnam, Kacheguda (Hyderabad), [Bengaluru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore), [Mysuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mysore_Junction_railway_station), [Thiruvananthapuram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiruvananthapuram), [New Delhi to Kanyakumari HimSagar Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himsagar_Express) and weekly/biweekly/triweekly trains connect Chittoor with [Mannargudi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mannargudi), [Jammu](43066157.md), [Katra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katra,_Jammu_and_Kashmir), [Tirunelveli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirunelveli), [Mangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangalore), [Ernakulam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernakulam), [Visakhapatnam](238760.md), Rajamahendravaram, [Hatia Ranchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatia), [Santragachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santragachi) (Kolkata) and [Jayanthi Janata Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanyakumari%E2%80%93Mumbai_Express), etc. which run through Chittoor.
