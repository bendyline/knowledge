**Nguru** (or **N'Gourou**) is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) near the [Hadejia River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadejia_River) in [Yobe State](2139960.md), [Nigeria](21383.md).

It has an area of 916 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 270,632.&#x20;

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 630.[^cite_note-1]

The town probably dates from around the 15th century. There is a variety of landscape types in the area, including the protected [Hadejia-Nguru wetlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadejia-Nguru_wetlands) of [Nguru Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguru_Lake), and the "Sand Dunes", a semi-desert area.
