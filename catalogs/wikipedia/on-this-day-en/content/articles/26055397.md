The **prime minister of the Kurdistan Region** is the [head of government](37570.md) of the [Kurdistan Region](1330553.md), governing [semi-autonomous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_administrative_division) [Kurdistan Region](3949386.md) in northern [Iraq](7515928.md). The government is elected as part of the [Kurdistan Region Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdistan_Region_Parliament).[^cite_note-1]

## List of prime ministers

### 1992–2005

After the [1992 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Kurdistan_Region_parliamentary_election) resulted in the two main parties, the [Kurdistan Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdistan_Democratic_Party) (KDP) and the [Patriotic Union of Kurdistan](70789.md) (PUK), each holding 50 out of 100 seats, they decided to create a unity government (which was not recognized by the [Ba'athist Iraq](26215470.md), led by [Saddam Hussein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddam_Hussein)).[^cite_note-End_of_Iraq-2]



Iraqi Kurdistan after the 1998 cease-fire. Green area controlled by the PUK, Yellow area controlled by the KDP

The unity government soon collapsed and in 1994 a [civil war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Kurdish_Civil_War) broke out, which lasted until 1998. This resulted in the establishment of two Kurdistan Regional Governments in 1996, a KDP-controlled one in [Erbil](24534207.md) and a PUK-controlled one in [Sulaymaniyah](653537.md), each with their own prime minister.[^cite_note-End_of_Iraq-2]

### 2006–present

After the reconciliation between the KDP and PUK, [parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Kurdistan_Region_parliamentary_election) was held on January 30, 2005, and an agreement was made to let a KDP leader become the Prime Minister for the first term and a PUK leader to become the Prime Minister for the second term, so Nechervan Barzani became prime minister. [Barham Salih](1006308.md) held the post of prime minister until 17 January 2012 after which he relinquished his position to [Nechervan Idris Barzani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nechervan_Idris_Barzani).[^cite_note-4]
