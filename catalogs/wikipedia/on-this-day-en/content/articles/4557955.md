**Mohmand District** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): ده مومندو ولسوالۍ, [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضلع مہمند) is a district of [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) ([Province of Pakistan](446017.md)). It is Situated in the Peshawar Division, it is bordered by [Bajaur](4461891.md) to the north, [Khyber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khyber_District) to the south, and districts of [Malakand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malakand_District), [Charsadda](988575.md), and [Peshawar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshawar_District) to the east while to the west it shares [international border](203570.md) with [Nangarhar Province](457263.md) and [Kunar Province](2159600.md) of [Afghanistan](737.md). It is known for its tribal Pashtun population, rugged terrain, mineral deposits (marble/chromite), and agriculture.

## Administration

It was Established in 1951 as an [agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federally_Administered_Tribal_Areas#Administrative_divisions) of [Federally Administered Tribal Areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federally_Administered_Tribal_Areas) (FATA) and was administrated by [Federal Government of Pakistan](1897833.md). On 31 May 2018, with the application of [25th Amendment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twenty-fifth_Amendment_to_the_Constitution_of_Pakistan), the Federally Administered Tribal Areas ceased to exist, and stood [merged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merger_of_tribal_areas_with_Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa) into neighbouring province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, while along with all other Seven Tribal Agencies,the status of Mohmand Agency was upgraded to District. [^cite_note-1]

Mohmand District is currently subdivided into seven [Tehsils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tehsils_of_Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa)[^cite_note-census-2] organized into upper and lower subdivisions, while district headquarter is located in town of [Ghalanai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghalanai).&#x20;

As of the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), Mohmand district has 63,973 households and a population of 553,933. The district has a sex ratio of 103.24 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 31.28%: 46.85% for males and 15.10% for females. 191,753 (34.62% of the surveyed population) are under 10 years of age. The entire population lives in rural areas.[^cite_note-2023_census-6] 1,592 (0.29%) people in the district were from religious minorities, mainly [Christians](18337522.md).[^cite_note-7] [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) was the predominant language, spoken by 99.80% of the population.[^cite_note-8]

## War on Terror

Despite its attraction for tourists, Mohmand District has been an area of conflict between Pakistan Army and some militant groups.[^cite_note-9]

On September 16, 2011, security forces cleared ninety percent of Mohmand District from the militants, normal life was restored and development activities were launched.[^cite_note-10]

In 2012, the Pakistani Army declared full control of Mohmand District and de-notified it as a conflict zone and transferred its control to the [Frontier Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontier_Corps).[^cite_note-11] However, terrorists continue to pose a threat to the peace by launching scattered terrorist attacks across from the [Pakistan–Afghanistan border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan%E2%80%93Afghanistan_border).
