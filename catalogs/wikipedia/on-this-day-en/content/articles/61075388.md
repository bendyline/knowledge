**Mykolaivka** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Миколаївка), previously **Mykolaivka-Vyrivska** (until 1957) and later **Zhovtneve** (1957–2016) is a [rural settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Populated_places_in_Ukraine#Rural_settlements) in [Sumy Raion](39418535.md), [Sumy Oblast](1567618.md), northeastern [Ukraine](31750.md). Population: 4,147 (2022 estimate).[^cite_note-ua2022estimate-2]

## History

During [World War II](32927.md) it was under German occupation.

[Urban-type settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban-type_settlement) since 1957.

In January 1989 the population was 4768 people.[^cite_note-3]

In January 2013 the population was 4350 people.[^cite_note-4]

The settlement was called *Zhovtneve*, to commemorate the [October Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_Revolution), until 2016. On 19 May 2016, [Verkhovna Rada](844993.md) adopted decision to rename Chervone to Esman according to the law prohibiting names of Communist origin.[^cite_note-5]

Until 26 January 2024, Mykolaivka was designated urban-type settlement. On this day, a new law entered into force which abolished this status, and Mykolaivka became a rural settlement.[^cite_note-glavnoe-6]
