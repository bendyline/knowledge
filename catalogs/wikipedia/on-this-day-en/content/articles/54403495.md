The **National Narcotics Board** or **National Narcotics Agency** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Badan Narkotika Nasional*, abbreviated **BNN**) is a government agency of Indonesia. BNN is responsible for minimizing the abuse of [controlled substances](2471575.md) through prevention and [law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement) measures directed primarily at [illegal drug abuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substance_abuse) and [drug trafficking](218449.md).[^cite_note-1]

## Organizational structure

BNN is led by the Head of BNN. The Head of BNN is always an active police officer, however civil servants are allowed to be the Head of BNN.[^cite_note-2] The Head of BNN reports directly to the [president](10011631.md) and coordinates with the [chief of national police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_the_Indonesian_National_Police).

BNN organization is consist of:[^cite_note-3]

- Head of BNN

- Main Secretariat

- Deputy of Prevention

- Deputy of Community Empowerment

- Deputy of [Eradication](197015.md)

- Deputy of [Rehabilitation](427922.md)

- Deputy of Legal Affairs and Cooperation

- Main Inspectorate

- Research, Data and Information Center

- Human Resource Development Center

- [Narcotics Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_test)&#x20;

  - Narcotics Board Regional Office [(Province level)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Indonesia)

  - Narcotics Board Regional Office [(City/Regency level)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_regencies_and_cities_of_Indonesia)
