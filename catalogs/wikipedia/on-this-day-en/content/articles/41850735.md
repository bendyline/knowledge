# Portugal at the FIFA World Cup

For the women's team, see [Portugal at the FIFA Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal_at_the_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup).



Portugal vs Morocco match at the [2018 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_World_Cup) in Russia

The [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) has two international [association football](10568.md) competitions, one contested by the [men's national teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_men's_national_association_football_teams) of the members of *Fédération Internationale de Football Association* ([FIFA](11049.md)), the sport's global governing body. The men's championship has been organized every four years since the first tournament in [1930](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930_FIFA_World_Cup), except in 1942 and 1946, due to [World War II](32927.md), and the women's started in 1991.

The tournament consists of two parts, the [qualification phase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_World_Cup_qualification) and the final phase (officially called the *World Cup Finals*). The qualification phase, which currently take place over the three years preceding the Finals, is used to determine which teams qualify for the Finals. The current format of the finals involves 48 teams competing for the title, at venues within the host nation/nations) over a period of about a month. The World Cup final is the most widely viewed sporting event in the world, with an estimated 715.1 million people watching the [2006 tournament final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup_final).

[Portugal](576328.md) have qualified for the final phase of the FIFA World Cup on nine occasions: in [1966](61629.md), [1986](157231.md), [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_FIFA_World_Cup) and every final phase held since. Their best performance, as of 2026, is a third place finish in 1966; they also finished fourth in [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup). Portugal is set to co-host the [2030 edition](39367087.md) having been granted host status alongside Morocco and Spain.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]
