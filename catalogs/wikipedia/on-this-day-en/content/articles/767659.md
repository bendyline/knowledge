**River Nile State** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ولاية نهر النيل, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Nahr an Nīl*) is one of the 18 [states](579458.md) (*wilayat*) of [Sudan](27421.md). It has an area of 122,123 km² (47,152 mi²) and an estimated population of 1,511,442 (2018 est).[^cite_note-4]. The state borders [Northern](767636.md) to the west, [Khartoum](767612.md) to the south, [Kassala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kassala_State) to the southeast, [Red Sea State](767604.md) to the east, and [Egypt](8087628.md) to the north. It is made up of seven localities.[^cite_note-SudanesePresidencySource-5]
