The **Parliament of Nauru**, officially the **Parliament of Naoero**, is the legislative body for the [Republic of Naoero](21302.md). It has 19 members, elected for a four-year term in multi-seat [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency). The [president of Nauru](300806.md) is elected by the members of the Parliament.[^cite_note-About_Parliament-1] The number of seats was increased to 19 following elections in 2013.[^cite_note-countrybrief-2]

The members of the Parliament of Nauru are elected by a [positional voting system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positional_voting_system).[^cite_note-About_Parliament-1]

## History

The Parliament of Nauru came into existence with the country's independence on 31 January 1968. The island was previously a [United Nations Trust Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Trust_Territory) administered by Australia. The Australian government's *Nauru Act 1965* created the **Legislative Council for the Territory of Nauru**, consisting of 15 members – nine elected members, one *[ex officio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex_officio)* member (the [administrator of Nauru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrator_of_Nauru)), and five "official members" nominated by the administrator.[^cite_note-3]

On 22 March 2010, [Radio New Zealand International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNZ_Pacific) reported that President [Marcus Stephen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Stephen) had dissolved Parliament in readiness for elections on 24 April 2010. The election saw all 18 MPs returned,[^cite_note-RNZI_53201-4] but by this stage, nine of them had formed the Opposition, resulting in a deadlocked Parliament. Another election was held in June 2010, as a result of the continuing deadlock. After weeks of uncertainty, the deadlock was resolved when the Opposition agreed to have one of its own MPs, [Ludwig Scotty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_Scotty), elected as [speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_\(politics\)). President Stephen then suggested that the number of MPs should be expanded to 19, to prevent future deadlocks.[^cite_note-radioaustralianews.net.au-5] In late 2012, Parliament, under the leadership of President Dabwido, acted on this suggestion and passed a law increasing the number of seats to 19 after election in 2013, it is expected to prevent future deadlocks like the one in 2010.[^cite_note-countrybrief-2]

## Speaker

Main article: [List of speakers of the Parliament of Nauru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_speakers_of_the_Parliament_of_Nauru)

The Speaker is the presiding officer of Parliament. The Speaker is an MP elected by the MPs. The Speaker has no vote in no-confidence votes and presidential elections.

Following the April 2008 election, [Riddell Akua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riddell_Akua) was appointed Speaker of the Parliament of Nauru. He replaced [David Adeang](785296.md).[^cite_note-14]

Two weeks after the [April 2010 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2010_Nauruan_parliamentary_election), [Godfrey Thoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godfrey_Thoma) was elected Speaker.[^cite_note-radioaustralianews.net.au-5] Due to the political deadlock, [fresh elections were held in June](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2010_Nauruan_parliamentary_election), after which Parliament continued to be deadlocked until the election of [Ludwig Scotty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_Scotty) to the chair in November 2010. Scotty resigned at the end of the 20th Parliament in March 2013. [Godfrey Thoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godfrey_Thoma) was elected to replace him. Following the 2013 election, Scotty was re-elected to the speaker's post.
