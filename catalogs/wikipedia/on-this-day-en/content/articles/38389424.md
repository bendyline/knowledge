**Jwaya** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): جويا, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Jwayā*), or **Jouaiya**,[^cite_note-1] is a municipality in [Tyre District](11166788.md), [South Governorate](1283570.md), Lebanon. It is located in the center of [Jabal Amil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabal_Amil), 95 kilometres (59 mi) from the capital [Beirut](37428.md), 54 kilometres (34 mi) from [Sidon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidon_Eyalet), and 16 kilometres (9.9 mi) from [Tyre](30796.md). Its elevation is 298 metres (978 ft) above sea level.

## History

In 1596, it was named as a village, *Juba*, in the [Ottoman](22278.md) *[nahiyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiyah)* (subdistrict) of [Tibnin](6787186.md) under the *[liwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liwa_\(Arabic\))* (district) of [Safad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safad), with a population of 87 households and 38 bachelors, all Muslim. The villagers paid a fixed tax rate of 25% on agricultural products, such as [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), fruit trees, goats and beehives, in addition to occasional revenues; a total of 11,859 [akçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e).[^cite_note-Hutteroth-2][^cite_note-3]

Several [lintels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lintel) have been found here.[^cite_note-4] In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described it as "a large village, built of stone and of good materials, containing about 1,000 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh). They weave and dye cloth, and have a small market. It is situated on a hill, and is surrounded with olives, figs, and arable land. The water supply is from two springs and many cisterns."[^cite_note-5]

In mid-August 1986, three French soldiers – all members of [UNIFIL](787766.md) – were killed by a remote-controlled bomb while jogging through Jwaya. The incident followed the killing of two [Amal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Resistance_Regiments) officials at a French checkpoint. At the time there were 605 French soldiers and 786 logistics staff serving with UNIFIL.[^cite_note-6]

After [Operation Accountability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Accountability), in July 1993, some units of the [Lebanese Armed Forces](17779.md) were deployed closer to the Lebanese border with Israel. A small base was established in Jwaya. It was the most southerly point of their deployment.[^cite_note-7]

## Demographics

In 2014, [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.18% of registered voters in Jwaya. 97.00% of the voters were [Shia Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-8]
