**Qatar–Ukraine relations** refer to [bilateral relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilateral_relations) between [Qatar](8238258.md) and [Ukraine](31750.md). Ukraine has an embassy in [Doha](26214389.md) since November 2012, and Qatar has an embassy in [Kyiv](585629.md) since September 2013.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## History

Both countries first established diplomatic relations on 13 April 1993, but had little political contact in the early years. The first diplomatic visit was from the [Ukrainian foreign minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Ukraine\)), who visited [Doha](26214389.md) in January 2002.

In 2020, amidst the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), Qatar assisted Ukraine as part of its delivery of humanitarian medical aid to 60 countries.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

Since the [Russo-Ukrainian war](42085878.md), Qatar actively assisted Ukraine regarding the [humanitarian impact of the war](71724372.md), such as mediating [prisoner exchanges during the conflict](72481055.md) and helping facilitate the return of [abducted Ukrainian children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_abductions_in_the_Russo-Ukrainian_war).[^cite_note-5]

On 28 March 2026, as a result of [Iranian strikes on Qatar](82557695.md) during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), Ukrainian and Qatari representatives signed a 10-year defense agreement aimed at increasing security cooperation, including on technology, expertise, and investment.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
