The **Kurdistan Regional Government**[^cite_note-2] (**KRG**) is the official executive body of the semi-autonomous [Kurdistan Region](3949386.md) in northern [Iraq](7515928.md).

The cabinet is selected by the majority party or list who also select the prime minister of the [Iraqi Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Kurd) [polity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polity). The [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Kurdistan_Region) is directly elected by the electorate of the region and is the head of the cabinet and chief of state who delegates executive powers to the cabinet. The [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Kurdistan_Region) is traditionally the head of the legislative body but also shares executive powers with the president.[^cite_note-3] The President of Kurdistan Region is also the commander-in-chief of the [Peshmerga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshmerga).[^cite_note-nefel.com-4]

## 2014

From mid-2013 to mid-2014, the KRG "built up their own defenses by creating a security belt stretching more than 1,000 km (600 miles) from the Iranian border all the way to Syria – skirting around Mosul, a city of 2 million people they appear\[ed] to have no intention of fighting for."[^cite_note-5] In August 2014, [ISIL attacked the Kurds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Iraq_offensive_\(August_2014\)).

On 1 July 2014, [Masoud Barzani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masoud_Barzani) announced that "Iraq's Kurds will hold an independence referendum within months."[^cite_note-Kurdish_Leader:_We_Will_Vote_For_Independence_Soon-6]

## 2017

In September, the [2017 Kurdistan Region independence referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Kurdistan_Region_independence_referendum) was held regarding Kurdish independence from Iraq. 92% of Iraqi Kurds participating in the referendum voted in favor of independence.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The referendum was regarded as illegal by the federal government in [Baghdad](4492.md), and on 6 November, Iraq's [Supreme Federal Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Supreme_Court_of_Iraq) ruled that no Iraqi province was allowed to secede in order to preserve the unity of Iraq.[^cite_note-9]

On 14 November, the KRG announced it would respect the Supreme Federal Court's ruling, stating that "this decision must become a basis for starting an inclusive national dialogue between (Kurdish authorities in) [Erbil](24534207.md) and [Baghdad](4492.md) to resolve all disputes".[^cite_note-10]
