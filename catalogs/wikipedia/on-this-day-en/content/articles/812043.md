The **Minister of External Affairs** (or simply the **External Affairs Minister**; *Hindi:* ***Videsh Mantri***) is the head of the [Ministry of External Affairs](10302410.md) of the [Government of India](553883.md). One of the senior-most offices in the [union cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_India), the chief responsibility of the minister of external affairs is to represent the government of India in the international community and advance its interests on the global stage. As such, the minister plays an important role in shaping and directing the country's [foreign policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_foreign_policy). Typically, the minister is assisted by one or more junior ministers, known as Ministers of State (MoS) for External Affairs.

The role, created when India became [independent in August 1947](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_India) as **Minister of External and Commonwealth Affairs**, was held by India's first prime minister, [Jawaharlal Nehru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawaharlal_Nehru) throughout his 17-year premiership; Nehru remains the longest serving external affairs minister followed by [S. Jaishankar](40435868.md), the incumbent minister. It was renamed Minister of External Affairs upon [India's becoming of a republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India) in 1950.

Several other Prime Ministers have since held the additional charge as the Minister of External Affairs, but never has any other cabinet minister held additional charge of the office—although during Nehru's premiership, [V. K. Krishna Menon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V._K._Krishna_Menon) was acknowledged as *de facto* minister of external affairs beyond his official designation as [defence minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Defence_\(India\)). There have been a number of external affairs ministers who went on to become the prime minister like [Atal Bihari Vajpayee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atal_Bihari_Vajpayee), [P. V. Narasimha Rao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._V._Narasimha_Rao) and [I. K. Gujral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I._K._Gujral). Two former career-diplomats have served as ministers of external affairs, including [Natwar Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natwar_Singh) (2004–2005) who served as India's ambassador to Poland and as high commissioner to Pakistan, and the current minister [S. Jaishankar](40435868.md) who retired as the [foreign secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Secretary_\(India\)). Two other ministers, [M. C. Chagla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._C._Chagla) and [I. K. Gujral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I._K._Gujral) also served as ambassadors, with Chagla serving as India's ambassador to the United States and high commissioner to the United Kingdom, while Gujral served as ambassador to the Soviet Union.

The current minister of external affairs is [S. Jaishankar](40435868.md), who succeeded [Sushma Swaraj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sushma_Swaraj) of the [Bharatiya Janata Party](149330.md) on 30 May 2019.
