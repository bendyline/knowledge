**Jijel** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ولاية جيجل) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Algeria) (*[wilaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilaya)*) in [Algeria](358.md), on the eastern Mediterranean coast. The capital is [Jijel](3692784.md) (Phoenician name : *Igilgili*). [Taza National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taza_National_Park) is located in this province.[^cite_note-2]

## History

The province was created from parts of [Constantine (department)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_\(department\)) and [Sétif (département)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9tif_\(d%C3%A9partement\)) in 1974.

In 1984 [Mila Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mila_Province) was carved out of its territory.

## Administrative divisions

The province is divided into 11 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Algeria), which are further divided into 28 [*communes*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Algeria) or municipalities.
