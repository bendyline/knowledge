On 22 May 2026 until 26 May 2026, almost the entire of [Sumatra](28679.md) Island experienced blackout. Four provinces in the islands were severely affected, namely [Aceh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aceh), [West Sumatra](453590.md), [North Sumatra](453595.md), [Riau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riau), and [Jambi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jambi).[^cite_note-detik-5][^cite_note-\:2-2]

## Impact

### Aceh, North Sumatra, and West Sumatra

Provinces [Aceh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aceh), [North Sumatra](453595.md), and [West Sumatra](453590.md) experienced a simultaneous blackout on 22 May 2026 around 18\:45 [WIB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Indonesian_Time). While Aceh was reported to have recovered by 22\:00, some regions eventually reported another blackout the next day, followed by a cutting off of water pipeline, causing water shortage.[^cite_note-6]

West Sumatra experienced an overall recovery by 23 May 2026, 01\:00. [Padang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padang) reported [an increase in traffic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_congestion) from citizens travelling to the city center, reportedly for the purpose of charging their devices.[^cite_note-7] North Sumatra, especially in [Medan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medan), did not report a recovery until 13\:00 the next day.[^cite_note-\:1-1]

Riau began to be affected by the outage on 22 May 2026 around 19\:00 WIB. The west part of the province reported a recovery at around 06\:00 the next day, however the outage returned at around 08\:00. Meanwhile the eastern half reported recoveries by 21\:00 the same day, but was hit by another outage at around 05\:00 the next day. The power outage also caused a water crisis considering most houses still use personally powered pumps for water.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

### South Sumatra, Jambi, Bengkulu and Lampung

[South Sumatra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Sumatra), [Jambi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jambi), [Bengkulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengkulu), and [Lampung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lampung) were also affected by blackouts simultaneously on 22 May 2026 around 18\:45 WIB, which in most regions ended by around 22\:00, while some only received power by 02\:00 the next day.[^cite_note-10] A 5,3 magnitude earthquake hit Bengkulu immediately after,[^cite_note-11] causing a temporary cutting of power by PLN.[^cite_note-12]

By 24 May 2026, some regions in Jambi were still affected by rolling blackouts, including [Bungo Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bungo_Regency), [Merangin Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merangin_Regency), [Jambi City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jambi_\(city\)), and [Muaro Jambi Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muaro_Jambi_Regency).[^cite_note-13]

## Casualties

On 23 May 2026, three people in [Tanah Datar Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanah_Datar_Regency), [West Sumatra](453590.md) suffered [carbon monoxide poisoning](488743.md) while using a generator in a mosque during the blackout, leaving two dead and one critically injured.[^cite_note-Detik1-3]

On the same day, four shop workers in [Air Putih District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Putih,_Batu_Bara), [Batubara Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batubara_Regency), [North Sumatra](453595.md) were also affected by carbon monoxide poisoning from generator fumes. This left two dead and two critically injured.[^cite_note-Detik2-4]

## Cause

The power outage was caused by a disruption on the [275 kV transmission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-voltage_direct_current) between Muara Bungo dan Sungai Rumbai di [Bungo Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bungo_Regency), Jambi, which was due to bad weather.[^cite_note-Kompas.id-14][^cite_note-\:0-15][^cite_note-16]

According to PLN, the outage was caused by a "power swing", an extreme oscillation of power exacerbated by said bad weather.

At 18\:44 WIB, heavy rains in Jambi caused a disruption in the two 275kV circuits on the south-bound transmission, which ended up cutting the main 500kV line off from the entire Sumatra power grid, effectively splitting the island's power into two.

The southern half experienced a surplus in power, but it was quickly remedied. Meanwhile, the northern half has been cut off from the remaining power, causing power generation to also gradually deactivate.[^cite_note-17]

## Reactions

Coordinating Minister [Agus Harimurti Yudhoyono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agus_Harimurti_Yudhoyono) requested [PLN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perusahaan_Listrik_Negara) to investigate the cause of the power outage.[^cite_note-18] PLN, in turn, issued an apology for the island-wide blackout, promising no further outages after this.[^cite_note-19] PLN, through manager Kisaran Harry Marbun, has also issued a statement that the message claimed to have been received before the actual blackout by some members of the public in Sumatra from the company informing of a blackout from 22-25 May 2026 for 8-10 hours was false.[^cite_note-20]

Indonesia's National Consumer Protection Agency (BPKN - Badan Perlindungan Konsumen Nasional) urged the people of Sumatra to file a mass lawsuit to PLN following the blackout.[^cite_note-21]
