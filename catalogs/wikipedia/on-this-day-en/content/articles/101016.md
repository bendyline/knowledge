**Crowdy Bay** is a national park in New South Wales, Australia, 271 km northeast of Sydney.

It is a seaside retreat, close to Port Macquarie, offering hiking trails, coastal picnics, fishing, and great opportunities to observe wildlife.[^cite_note-1]

The biodiversity in the park is very rich. These include [white-bellied sea eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White-bellied_sea_eagles) *(Haliaeetus leucogaster)*, [brown-striped frog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Striped_marsh_frog) *(Limnodynastes peronii)*, [lace monitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lace_monitor) *(Varanus varius)*, [black sheoak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allocasuarina_littoralis) *(Allocasuarina littoralis)*, and [grass tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xanthorrhoea) *(Xanthorrhoea spp.)*.[^cite_note-2]

Crowdy Bay National Park was badly affected during the [2019–20 Australian bushfire season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Australian_bushfire_season) with 12,000 hectares including pockets of coastal rainforest destroyed and author [Kylie Tennant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Tennant)'s retreat in Crowdy Bay called "Kylie's Hut", destroyed as well in November 2019.[^cite_note-3]
