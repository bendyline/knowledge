**Hugo Claudio de Zela Martínez** (born 4 August 1951)[^cite_note-1] is a Peruvian diplomat and professor who served as [Minister of Foreign Relations](17829557.md) from 2025 until 2026.

## Career

De Zela was appointed Minister of Foreign Relations on 14 October 2025.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] He resigned along with defense minister [Carlos Díaz Dañino](83017786.md) on 22 April 2026 in protest against interim president [José María Balcázar](82451696.md)'s decision to defer a purchase agreement to buy 24 [F-16](11642.md) fighter jets from the United States pending the result of the [2026 Peruvian general election](69679018.md).[^cite_note-4]
