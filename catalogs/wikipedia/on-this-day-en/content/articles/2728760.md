**Central Jakarta** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Jakarta Pusat*, [Betawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betawi_language): *Jakarté Bèlah Tengah*), abbreviated as **Jakpus**, is one of the five [administrative cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_Indonesia) (*kota administrasi*) and *de facto* Capital City of the [Special Capital Region of Jakarta](16275.md). It had 902,973 inhabitants according to the 2010 census[^cite_note-Biro_Pusat_Statistik_2011-2] and 1,056,896 at the 2020 census;[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] the official estimate as of mid 2025 was 1,051,077 (comprising 527,565 males and 523,512 females).[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1] Central Jakarta is not [self-governed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-governance) and does not have a [city council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_council), hence it is not classified as a proper [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality).

Central Jakarta is the smallest in area and population of the five administrative cities of Jakarta. It is both the administrative and political center of Jakarta and Indonesia. Central Jakarta contains several large international hotels and major landmarks such as [Hotel Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_Indonesia).

## Administrative districts

Central Jakarta Administrative City (*Kota Administrasi Jakarta Pusat*) is bounded by [North Jakarta](2728733.md) to the north, [East Jakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Jakarta) to the east, [South Jakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Jakarta) to the south, and [West Jakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Jakarta) to the west. ## Demographics



View of Central Jakarta to the northeast from the National Monument, c. 1970s. [Istiqlal Mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istiqlal_Mosque,_Jakarta) was being built in front of already standing [Jakarta Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakarta_Cathedral).

Central Jakarta had an average of almost 22,000 residents per square kilometre in mid 2025, making it the most densely populated municipality in Jakarta.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1] Within the city, Johar Baru District reaches about 56,000 residents per square kilometre.

## Economy

Both GRDP at the current market price and GRDP at 2000 constant price in 2007 for Municipality of Central Jakarta is higher than other municipalities in DKI Jakarta, which is Rp. 145 million and Rp. 80 million respectively.

At the end of the first quarter of 2010, the Jakarta CBD had an occupancy rate of 80%, an increase from 78% at the end of the first quarter of 2009. According to [Jones Lang LaSalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jones_Lang_LaSalle), the amount of office space in the Jakarta CBD increased by 93,000 square metres (1,000,000 sq ft) between the second half of 2010 and the second half of 2009.[^cite_note-Boomingdemand-4]

&#x20;In September 2010, Jones Lang LaSalle estimated that the Jakarta CBD had 30,000 square metres (320,000 sq ft) of serviced office space, making up less than 1 percent of the total amount of office space in the CBD. 70% of the tenants in the serviced spaces were international companies. The number of serviced office spaces in Central Jakarta increased by 50% in the year leading to September 2010.[^cite_note-Boomingdemand-4]

Central Jakarta consists of 8 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_districts_of_Jakarta) and 44 subdistricts.

Government agencies with head offices in Central Jakarta include the [National Search and Rescue Agency](35773786.md), which has its head office in [Kemayoran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kemayoran),[^cite_note-5] and the [National Transportation Safety Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Transportation_Safety_Committee) (NTSC, Indonesian acronym KNKT), which has its head office in the Ministry of Transport Building.[^cite_note-6]
