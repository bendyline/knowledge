**Nagpur district** (Marathi pronunciation: [\[naːɡpuːɾ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Marathi)) is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Maharashtra) in the [Vidarbha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vidarbha) region of [Maharashtra](20629.md) state in central [India](14533.md). The city of [Nagpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagpur) is the district administrative centre. The district is part of [Nagpur Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagpur_Division).

Nagpur district is bounded by [Bhandara district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhandara_district) on the east, [Chandrapur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandrapur_district) on the southeast, [Wardha district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wardha_district) on the southwest, [Amravati district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amravati_district) on the northwest and [Chhindwara district](2815567.md) and [Seoni district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoni_district) of [Madhya Pradesh](47945.md) state on the north.

## History

In 1853, after the death of [Raghoji III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagpur_kingdom#Raghoji_III_Bhonsle_\(1818%E2%80%931853\)), the [princely state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princely_state) of Nagpur was annexed by the British and the territory occupied by the present district became part of the then [Nagpur Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagpur_Province). In 1861, it was merged with the [Central Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Provinces). In 1903 it became part of the [Central Provinces and Berar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Provinces_and_Berar). In 1950 Nagpur district was created when it became part of the newly formed [Madhya Pradesh](47945.md) state and Nagpur became its capital. In 1956, after a [reorganisation of Indian states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_Reorganisation_Act), [Nagpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagpur) district was incorporated into [Bombay state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_state). On 1 May 1960, it became a district of Maharashtra state.

According to the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India) Nagpur district had a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_India) of 4,653,570,[^cite_note-districtcensus-8] roughly equal to the nation of [Ireland](147575.md)[^cite_note-cia-9] or the US state of [South Carolina](27956.md).[^cite_note-10] This gives it a ranking of 29th in India (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-districtcensus-8] The district has a population density of 470 inhabitants per square kilometre (1,200/sq mi) .[^cite_note-districtcensus-8] Its [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_India) over the decade 2001–2011 was 14.39%.[^cite_note-districtcensus-8] Nagpur has a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) of 948 [females](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_India) for every 1000 males,[^cite_note-districtcensus-8] and a [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of 89.52%. 68.31% of the population live in urban areas. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes make up 18.65% and 9.40% of the population respectively.[^cite_note-districtcensus-8]

## Transport

Main article: [Transport in Nagpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_in_Nagpur)

Main article: [Nagpur Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagpur_Railway_Station)



Public transport bus in Nagpur



Nagpur's [International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-modal_International_Cargo_Hub_and_Airport_at_Nagpur) has the busiest air traffic control room in India.

&#x20;Due to its central location in India, the [Nagpur Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagpur_Railway_Station) is an important [railway junction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Railways). It is a transit terminal for trains that connect the country lengthwise and breadthwise, especially trains connecting India's major metropolises, [Mumbai](19189.md) to [Howrah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howrah)-[Kolkata](47905.md), [Delhi](37756.md) and [Jammu](43066157.md) to [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai), [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_India), [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore) and [Kanyakumari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanyakumari_\(town\)) in the South, as well as western cities such as [Pune](164634.md) and [Ahmedabad](206337.md).[^cite_note-index04052006-15]



Nagpur Junction

Nagpur is also a major road junction as India's two major [national highways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_highways_of_India), [Kanyakumari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanyakumari_\(town\))-[Varanasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varanasi) ([NH  7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_7_\(India,_old_numbering\))) and [Hajira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazira)-Kolkata ([NH 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_6_\(India,_old_numbering\))), pass through the city.[^cite_note-index04052006-15] [Highway number 69](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NH_69) connects Nagpur to Obaidullaganj near [Bhopal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhopal). Nagpur is at the junction of Asian HigLanka and [AH46](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AH46) connecting [Kharagpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharagpur,_West_Bengal) to [Dhule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhule).

Further information: [Mumbai–Nagpur Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai%E2%80%93Nagpur_Expressway)

The [MSRTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSRTC) bus provides affordable transport service in and around the district, reaching even the most remote areas.

Main article: [Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Babasaheb_Ambedkar_International_Airport)

Further information: [Multi-modal International Cargo Hub and Airport at Nagpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-modal_International_Cargo_Hub_and_Airport_at_Nagpur)

[Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Babasaheb_Ambedkar_International_Airport) in Sonegaon, Nagpur, is a domestic and international airport, which connects Nagpur to Mumbai, Delhi, Sharjah, Dubai and Muscat via Doha .
