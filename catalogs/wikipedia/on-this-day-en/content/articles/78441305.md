**MLS Cup 2025** was the 30th edition of the [MLS Cup](483246.md), the championship match of [Major League Soccer](71802.md) (MLS), the top-flight [soccer](10568.md) league in the United States and Canada. The match was played on December 6, 2025, at [Chase Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Stadium) in [Fort Lauderdale, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Lauderdale,_Florida), United States, and determined the champions of the [2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Major_League_Soccer_season). It was hosted by [Eastern Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Conference_\(MLS\)) champions [Inter Miami CF](41853326.md) and played against [Western Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Conference_\(MLS\)) champions [Vancouver Whitecaps FC](22060729.md). The match also marked the conclusion of the [MLS Cup playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_MLS_Cup_playoffs), which was contested by the top nine teams in each conference based on their regular season records.

Inter Miami CF won 3–1 with two assists by [Lionel Messi](2150841.md), who was named the match's [most valuable player](235000.md), and clinched their first MLS Cup title.[^cite_note-Herald-Recap-3]

## Road to the final

Further information: [2025 MLS Cup playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_MLS_Cup_playoffs)

The [MLS Cup](483246.md) is the post-season championship of [Major League Soccer](71802.md) (MLS), the top-flight club [soccer](10568.md) league in the United States and Canada. The [2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Major_League_Soccer_season) was the 30th in MLS history, and was contested by 30 teams organized into the [eastern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Conference_\(MLS\)) and [western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Conference_\(MLS\)) conferences. Each team played 34 matches during the regular season, which runs from February to October, twice against each intra-conference opponent and six times for inter-conference opposition in an unbalanced schedule. The regular season included a month-long break for the [FIFA Club World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_FIFA_Club_World_Cup) and [CONCACAF Gold Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_CONCACAF_Gold_Cup), which were hosted by the United States.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The nine clubs in each conference with the most [points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Points_\(association_football\)) qualified for the [MLS Cup playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_playoffs), which was played over five rounds from October to December. Most rounds were a [single-elimination](939680.md) match hosted by the higher-[seeded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed_\(sports\)) team; the exception was Round One, which was a [best-of-three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best-of-three) series with the first and third (if necessary) match hosted by the higher-seeded team.[^cite_note-6]

Both of the finalists, [Inter Miami CF](41853326.md) and [Vancouver Whitecaps FC](22060729.md), appeared in their first MLS Cup final and joined MLS as [expansion teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expansion_team).[^cite_note-MLS-Set-7] They played each other earlier that year in the semifinals of the [CONCACAF Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_CONCACAF_Champions_Cup). Vancouver won the first leg 2–0 at home and 3–1 in the second leg for an [aggregate score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aggregate_score) of 5–1.[^cite_note-8] The Whitecaps were also the first Canadian team since [MLS Cup 2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_2019) to reach the league's championship.[^cite_note-9]

Two of Miami's veteran European players, [Jordi Alba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordi_Alba) and [Sergio Busquets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Busquets), had announced their intent to retire from professional soccer at the end of the season.[^cite_note-10]

## Venue

MLS Cup 2025 was hosted by Inter Miami CF at their home venue, [Chase Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Stadium) in [Fort Lauderdale, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Lauderdale,_Florida). The team won the right to host the match by virtue of their higher finish in the regular season standings; Miami had 65 points, while Vancouver earned 63 points.[^cite_note-11] Chase Stadium is a [soccer-specific stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soccer-specific_stadium) that has a capacity of 21,550 spectators and was constructed as a temporary stadium for Inter Miami CF until the completion of [Nu Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nu_Stadium) near [Miami International Airport](128968.md) in 2026.[^cite_note-12] The stadium had been expanded with temporary [bleachers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleacher) immediately following the signing of Lionel Messi by the club in 2023.[^cite_note-13] MLS Cup 2025 was the final match for Inter Miami CF at Chase Stadium, as the team moved to Nu Stadium at the beginning of the [2026 season](78195339.md).[^cite_note-14]

Inter Miami CF announced a two-day presale of tickets for the match for [season ticket holders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Season_ticket_holder) beginning on December 1, which is set to be followed by a release of tickets to the general public on December 2.[^cite_note-15] The lowest prices on the [resale market](1676368.md) had been \$831 prior to the conference finals, but later ranged from \$359 to \$2,211 at the end of the public sale; by December 5, the lowest ticket price was \$179.[^cite_note-Herald-Tickets-16][^cite_note-17] Ticket retailer [TickPick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TickPick) estimated that the average cost of a ticket on its platform for the 2025 final was \$464, the second-highest figure for an MLS Cup after the [2022 final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_2022) in [Los Angeles](18110.md).[^cite_note-18]

The Whitecaps hosted a [watch party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watch_party) at their home stadium, [BC Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BC_Place), for the MLS Cup final using its large [video screens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jumbotron). By December 2, the team had already sold 16,000 tickets for the event.[^cite_note-19] BC Place had 20,452 total spectators, setting a record for the largest audience for an MLS Cup watch party.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-TCP-Summary-21]

## Broadcasting

The MLS Cup final was broadcast worldwide in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) and [Spanish](26825.md) on [MLS Season Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Season_Pass), a subscription streaming service operated by [Apple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_\(company\)).[^cite_note-22] The MLS Season Pass commentary team was led in English by play-by-play announcer [Jake Zivin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Zivin?action=edit\&redlink=1) and analyst [Taylor Twellman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Twellman); the Spanish team was led by [Sammy Sadovnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sammy_Sadovnik?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Diego Valeri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Valeri). The service was also carrying pre-game and post-game shows from a studio crew.[^cite_note-MLS-Broadcast-23] The match was also being broadcast on U.S. terrestrial television by [Fox Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Sports_\(United_States\)) in English and [Fox Deportes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Deportes) in Spanish. The Canadian television broadcast was carried by [TSN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sports_Network) in English and [Réseau des sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A9seau_des_sports) (RDS) in French.[^cite_note-MLS-Set-7] In Mexico, the match was broadcast on [TNT Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TNT_Sports_\(Mexico\)). MLS Productions used more than 30 cameras to broadcast MLS Cup 2025, including a [referee cam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Referee_cam) and footage from four [iPhone 17 Pro Max](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPhone_17_Pro_Max) smartphones for fan reactions.[^cite_note-MLS-Broadcast-23][^cite_note-SBJ-Broadcast-24] The match was also displayed on a [digital billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_billboard) in [Times Square](31389.md), [New York City](645042.md).[^cite_note-SBJ-Broadcast-24]

According to MLS, a total of 4.6 million viewers watched the match across all television and streaming platforms that carried MLS Cup 2025. The Fox and Fox Deportes broadcast had 994,000 viewers according to preliminary data from [Nielsen Media Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen_Media_Research); approximately 283,000 viewers watched the Canadian broadcast on TSN and RDS.[^cite_note-25]

## Post-match

Inter Miami CF became the sixteenth MLS team to win the league championship and the fifth new winner in the past five editions. [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) was named the MLS Cup [most valuable player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLS_Cup_Most_Valuable_Player) for his two assists during the match and won his 47th overall trophy.[^cite_note-27] He also set a record for the most combined goals and assists in MLS Cup playoff history, with 15 total goal contributions (6 goals and 9 assists). Miami co-owner [David Beckham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Beckham) became the first person to win the MLS Cup as both a player—winning twice with the [LA Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LA_Galaxy) in 2011 and 2012—and owner.[^cite_note-28] [Tadeo Allende](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tadeo_Allende)'s goal in stoppage time was his ninth during the [2025 MLS Cup playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_MLS_Cup_playoffs) and set a new league record.[^cite_note-Herald-Recap-3]

As MLS Cup champions, Inter Miami CF earned a [bye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bye_\(sports\)) to the round of 16 in the [2026 CONCACAF Champions Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_CONCACAF_Champions_Cup).[^cite_note-29] The team were hosted at the [White House](33057.md) by president [Donald Trump](4848272.md) on March 5, 2026. Trump was presented with a jersey, soccer ball, and watch by owner Jorge Mas.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31]
