**Johannes Dale-Skjevdal** (born 23 May 1997) is a Norwegian [biathlete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biathlon). He competed at the [Biathlon World Championships 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biathlon_World_Championships_2020).[^cite_note-1] He is the Olympic champion in the [15 km mass start](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biathlon_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_mass_start) event at the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md).
