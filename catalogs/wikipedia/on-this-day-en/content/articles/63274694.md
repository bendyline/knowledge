The **Republican Bloc** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Bloc Républicain*, **BR**) is a [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Benin](3459.md), led by [Abdoulaye Bio Tchané](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdoulaye_Bio_Tchan%C3%A9).[^cite_note-3] In the [2019 Beninese parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Beninese_parliamentary_election), the party came in second, winning 36 of 83 seats in the [National Assembly](4458651.md).[^cite_note-2019_election-4] Both the Republican Bloc and the majority-winning [Progressive Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_Union_\(Benin\)) are allied with President [Patrice Talon](49884967.md).[^cite_note-2019_election-4]
