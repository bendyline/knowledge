The **National Assembly** ([Malagasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malagasy_language): *Antenimieram-Pirenena*; [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Assemblée Nationale*) is the lower house of the [Parliament of Madagascar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Madagascar). The Assembly has 163 members, elected for five-year terms in single-member and two-member [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency).[^cite_note-ipuparline-2]

The Parliament of Madagascar has [two chambers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameralism). The other chamber is the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Madagascar) (*Antenimieran-Doholona/Sénat*), which has 18 members, 12 of which are indirectly elected (one from each district of Madagascar), with 6 more being appointed.
