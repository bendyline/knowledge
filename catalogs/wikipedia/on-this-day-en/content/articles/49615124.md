**BR-373** is a Brazilian federal highway that begins in [Limeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limeira), [São Paulo](229422.md) and ends in [Barracão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrac%C3%A3o,_Paran%C3%A1), [Paraná](229411.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-dnit-3] The highway also serves the municipalities of [Itapetininga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itapetininga) and [Itapeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itapeva,_S%C3%A3o_Paulo) in São Paulo; and [Ponta Grossa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponta_Grossa) and [Guarapuava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guarapuava) in Paraná.[^cite_note-rod-diag-1]
