The **Ministry of Foreign Affairs** (**MFA**; [Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language): *Kementerian Ehwal Luar Negeri*; [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 新加坡外交部; [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language): வெளியுறவு அமைச்சு) is in charge of [the country's foreign relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Singapore), as well as handling matters and providing consular assistance related to [overseas Singaporean citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_Singaporean). It was established on 9 August 1965. A [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) for the [Government of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Singapore), it is also responsible for conducting and managing diplomatic relations between [Singapore](27318.md) and other countries and regions.

## Organisational structure

The ministry now has 50 overseas missions including 7 [high commissions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_commission), 21 [embassies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassies), 4 [permanent missions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permanent_mission) to the [United Nations](31769.md), and 17 [consulates](6770059.md). Singapore has appointed 31 [honorary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consul_\(representative\)#Honorary_consul) [consuls-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consul_\(representative\)#Consul_general)/[consuls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consul_\(representative\)) abroad and has 46 non-resident [ambassadors](68651.md) and [high commissioners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_commissioner) based in Singapore.

The ministry is currently divided into 11 directorates which deal with political and economic matters, and 7 directorates which oversee matters relating to protocol, consular issues and the [Singapore Cooperation Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Cooperation_Programme) (SCP) among others.&#x20;

The Corporate Affairs Directorate oversees organisational and resource management while the Human Resource Directorate and Diplomatic Academy manage the development of personnel and training.

## Impact

[Singapore](27318.md) maintains diplomatic relations with 188 countries. In Singapore, there are 70 resident foreign [embassies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassies) and [high commissions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_commission), 43 foreign [consulates](6770059.md) and 11 international organisations based in Singapore. In addition, more than 60 non-resident foreign ambassadors are accredited to Singapore.

### 2026 Evacuation Flights

In March 2026, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs arranged for Singaporeans and their dependants to be repatriated back to Singapore from [Muscat](57053.md), [Oman](22316.md) and [Riyadh](51554.md) due to airspace closures after the US-Israel attack on Iran.[^cite_note-2]
