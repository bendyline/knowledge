This is a list of [presidents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)) of [Madagascar](18964.md), since the establishment of the office of President in 1959, during the [Malagasy Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malagasy_Republic). The president is the head of state and the [Commander-in-Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-Chief) of the [armed forces](50881.md). The position has been held by [Michael Randrianirina](81340595.md) since 17 October 2025, who is styled as President for the Re-Foundation of the Republic of Madagascar.[^cite_note-1]&#x20;

## Succession

In case of a vacancy, the [president of the Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_presidents_of_the_Senate_of_Madagascar) will act as a temporary successor of the president.[^cite_note-Hist-2] After the [2025 military coup](81321149.md), Colonel [Michael Randrianirina](81340595.md) was appointed as president.[^cite_note-\:3-3]&#x20;

## Incoming election

Main article: [2027 Malagasy presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_Malagasy_presidential_election)

A presidential election in [Madagascar](18964.md) is scheduled for September 2027, within two years of the [2025 coup d'état](81321149.md). Until then, Madagascar will be governed by a [transitional government](81351542.md) led by President [Michael Randrianirina](81340595.md).[^cite_note-\:1-14] The most recent Malagasy presidential election was held in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Malagasy_presidential_election), and [Andry Rajoelina](21298487.md) was re-elected.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]&#x20;
