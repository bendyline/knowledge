**USS *Boxer* (LHD-4)** is a [*Wasp*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasp-class_amphibious_assault_ship) [amphibious assault ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_assault_ship) of the [United States Navy](20518076.md).[^cite_note-FAS-2] It is the sixth U.S. ship to bear the name of the [original HMS *Boxer*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Boxer_\(1812\)), which was [captured from the British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capture_of_HMS_Boxer) during the [War of 1812](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_1812).

## Construction and career

*Boxer* was constructed at [Ingalls Shipbuilding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingalls_Shipbuilding), [Pascagoula, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascagoula,_Mississippi), launched 13 August 1993, and [commissioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning) 11 February 1995. She immediately left for [San Diego](28504.md), California, via the [Panama Canal](24849.md). Although she had been designed to [safely transit the canal](504868.md), her [bridge wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_wing) and other smaller components were sheared off in the transit.

After minor repairs and system checkout, *Boxer* deployed to the Western [Pacific](23070.md) from 24 March 1997 to 24 September 1997, along with [*Ogden*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Ogden_\(LPD-5\)) and [*Fort Fisher*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Fort_Fisher), and visited many foreign ports of call. *Boxer* also participated in [RIMPAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIMPAC) the following year, then on 5 December 1998 deployed again to the Western Pacific.

*Boxer* deployed again to the Western Pacific, [Persian Gulf](24761.md), and [Red Sea](26191.md) on 14 March 2001 in support of Operation Southern Watch. She visited [Singapore](27318.md), [Thailand](30128.md), [Guam](11974.md), [Jebel Ali](1875391.md), [Bahrain](18933277.md), and [Jordan](7515964.md), returning to the United States on 14 September 2001, just days after the [attacks of 11 September 2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/September_11,_2001_attacks).

In 2003, as a result of the impending war and the need for troops in Iraq, *Boxer* found herself deploying yet again, this time six months ahead of schedule. This was a six-month deployment in direct support of [Operation Iraqi Freedom](201936.md). She deployed with six other ships from [San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Station_San_Diego) on 17 January 2003: [USS *Bonhomme Richard*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Bonhomme_Richard_\(LHD-6\)), [USS *Anchorage*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Anchorage_\(LSD-36\)), [USS *Cleveland*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Cleveland_\(LPD-7\)), [USS *Comstock*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Comstock_\(LSD-45\)), [USS *Dubuque*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Dubuque_\(LPD-8\)), and [USS *Pearl Harbor*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Pearl_Harbor).

She returned to the United States on 26 July 2003. Also in 2003, she won the [Marjorie Sterrett Battleship Fund Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marjorie_Sterrett_Battleship_Fund_Award) for the Pacific Fleet.

In another early deployment, to become known as a [surge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq_War_troop_surge_of_2007) deployment, *Boxer* steamed alone from San Diego on 14 January 2004 to support the ongoing rebuilding efforts in Iraq, dubbed [Operation Iraqi Freedom II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Iraqi_Freedom_II). She delivered equipment and supplies to [Kuwaiti Naval Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwaiti_Navy) in the northern Persian Gulf for the continuing support of the postwar rebuilding of Iraq. She returned home on 29 April 2004.

*Boxer* was deployed with the [15th MEU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15th_MEU) from her San Diego port on 13 September 2006. They arrived to Iraq in November and returned to dock in San Diego on 31 May 2007.

From 20 April 2008 to 26 June 2008, *Boxer* conducted a [humanitarian mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuing_Promise) in Central and South America.

### Antipiracy task force

*Boxer* is designated as the flagship of [Combined Task Force 151](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combined_Task_Force_151), the international antipiracy task force pursuing pirates off the coast of [Somalia](27358.md).[^cite_note-3]

On 10 April 2009, *Boxer* was en route to assist [USS *Bainbridge*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Bainbridge_\(DDG-96\)) and [USS *Halyburton*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Halyburton) in negotiating the release of [Richard Phillips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Phillips_\(captain\)), captain of U.S.-flagged [container ship](262084.md) [MV *Maersk Alabama*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_Tygra), who was held hostage by Somali pirates 300 miles (480 km) off the Horn of Africa.[^cite_note-4] On 12 April 2009, Captain Phillips was freed during a US Navy assault in which three of the Somali pirates were killed and one was captured.[^cite_note-captain_freed-5] Captain Phillips was transported to *Boxer* for medical examination and rest.[^cite_note-BBC_Freed-6]

Around 1 May 2009, *Boxer* assisted some 200 members of the German special operations unit [GSG-9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GSG-9) in getting close to the hijacked German container ship [MV *Hansa Stavanger*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_Hansa_Stavanger). During the last phase of the operation, [James L. Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_L._Jones), the U.S. [national security advisor](44028.md), withheld final approval for the operation out of concern for the safety of the 25 sailors on board the vessel. This led to the decision by the German department of defense and the German department of the interior to abort the planned attack on the freighter for the time being and the GSG-9 unit, which is under the command of the German secretary of the interior, returned to its base of operations at the airport of [Mombasa](200152.md), [Kenya](188171.md). It was reported that the German department of defense influence and contacts to James Jones led to the U.S. decision to withdraw from the scene.[^cite_note-7]

The ship returned to San Diego on 1 August 2009. During her transit of the Pacific, 69 of the sailors and marines aboard the ship contracted [swine influenza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swine_influenza), forcing the cancellation of a planned "Tiger Cruise" from Hawaii to meet the ships for several hundred relatives and friends.[^cite_note-8]

### 2010–2020

The ship, with the [13th Marine Expeditionary Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13th_Marine_Expeditionary_Unit), departed San Diego on 22 February 2011 for a seven-month deployment in the Pacific and Indian Oceans. Accompanying the ship on the deployment were [USS *Green Bay*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Green_Bay_\(LPD-20\)) and [USS *Comstock*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Comstock_\(LSD-45\)).[^cite_note-9]

On 6 May 2016, it was reported that the U.S. staged *Boxer*, supported by the [destroyers](8779.md) [*Gravely*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Gravely) and [*Gonzalez*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Gonzalez), off the coast of Yemen with 2,000 to 4,500 U.S. Marines of the [13th MEU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13th_MEU) in the flotilla to provide support to coalition forces in Yemen [fighting AQAP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Qaeda_insurgency_in_Yemen#2016) militants.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] On 16 June 2016, *Boxer*, supported by amphibious warfare ships [*New Orleans*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_New_Orleans_\(LPD-18\)) and [*Harpers Ferry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Harpers_Ferry), took part in [Operation Inherent Resolve](44120613.md); [AV-8BII Harriers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonnell_Douglas_AV-8B_Harrier_II) of the 13th MEU flying off the ship began airstrikes against [ISIL](9087364.md) in Iraq and Syria, making it the first time the U.S. Navy used ship-based aircraft from both the Mediterranean and the Persian Gulf at the same time during the operation[^cite_note-13] (aircraft from the [aircraft carrier](2219.md) [*Harry S. Truman*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Harry_S._Truman) began airstrikes on ISIL targets from the Mediterranean on 3 June).[^cite_note-14]

On 18 July 2019, President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) stated that the ship shot down and destroyed an Iranian drone over the [Strait of Hormuz](29005.md).[^cite_note-15] According to a statement by the President, the drone was downed after "ignoring multiple stand-down calls." Iranian officials rejected Trump's statement on downing an Iranian drone[^cite_note-16] and the [IRGC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRGC) released a video and claimed it was monitoring the ship and some other American military vessels, including USS *Harpers Ferry*, [USS *John P. Murtha*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_John_P._Murtha), [USS *Bainbridge*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Bainbridge_\(DDG-96\)), and [USS *McFaul*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_McFaul) by the drone on the morning of 18 July.

On 15 March 2020, a sailor aboard, it was reported to have tested presumptive positive two days prio, for [SARS-CoV-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severe_acute_respiratory_syndrome_coronavirus_2), the virus at the heart of the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), marking the first case of a coronavirus case aboard an American warship.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-boxer.navy-18] The sailor subsequently quarantined at home.[^cite_note-boxer.navy-18] A second sailor tested positive on 17 March 2020 and also was quarantined at home.[^cite_note-19]

### Modernization and maintenance

On 7 April 2020, BAE systems was reported to have been awarded a contract from the US Navy to perform extensive modernization and maintenance of USS *Boxer*, with work scheduled to commence in June 2020, and to be completed around December 2021.[^cite_note-20] Notably, as a part of this work, *Boxer* was updated to be able to operate with the [F-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F-35) Joint Strike Fighter. [^cite_note-21] As of 21 July 2023, ongoing maintenance issues had prevented the ship from returning to sea.[^cite_note-22] As of 25 March 2024, *Boxer* was seen departing San Diego, California.[^cite_note-23] The ship returned to San Diego 10 days later due to mechanical problems.[^cite_note-24]

### 2026 Iran war

See also: [2026 Iran war](82533582.md)

On 19 March 2026, *Boxer*s [Amphibious Ready Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_Ready_Group) (ARG) was reported to be deploying ahead of schedule to support [Operation Epic Fury](82533582.md). *Boxer* had left San Diego, California to begin her journey to the Middle East, and would be joined by [USS *Portland*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Portland_\(LPD-27\)) and [USS *Comstock*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Comstock_\(LSD-45\)). The ARG would join the [*Tripoli* ARG](36035298.md) that was already underway. The [11th Marine Expeditionary Unit](5852328.md) (MEU) had embarked by 19 March on the ARG ships, which were slated to sail through the [Indo-Pacific](330456.md) to the Middle East. Around 2,500 Marine Corps personnel of the total of 4,000 service members were in the fleet. The sailors and marines shortened their leave following certification and training to accelerate their deployment.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]
