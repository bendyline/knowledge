The ***Shahid Soleimani* class** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): شهید سلیمانی) were the principal surface combatant ships of the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy](4676345.md) of [Iran](14653.md). These were an Iranian class of [missile corvettes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corvette) and bore the name of [Qasem Soleimani](16805217.md), a prominent Iranian military officer, who was the commander of [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md) [Quds Force](4028355.md) from 1998, until he was [assassinated on 3 January 2020](62726668.md) by the United States in [Baghdad](4492.md), [Iraq](7515928.md) alongside [Abu Mahdi al-Muhandis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Mahdi_al-Muhandis). These ships were being built by various shipyards in [Bushehr](205669.md), [Qeshm](2252168.md) and [Bandar Abbas](464348.md) and were operated by the IRGC Navy.

## Design

### Hull

These ships are known for their unique [catamaran](195952.md) (twin hull) design with sharp angles for a more [stealthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stealth_technology) design. The design has been compared to the Chinese [Type 22 missile boat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_22_missile_boat) and the Taiwanese [*Tuo Chiang* class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuo_Chiang-class_corvette). According to Iranian officials, these [warships](263960.md) have [aluminum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminum) hulls instead of the regular [steel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steel), which decreases their weight. The use of aluminum is an innovation in the Iranian shipbuilding industry, adopted in the past few years.[^cite_note-a-1]

### Armament

These warships are equipped with six [anti-ship cruise missiles](140725.md) including four long-range ones (likely [Noor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noor_\(missile\)) or [Ghadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghadir_\(missile\))) and two short-range anti-ship missiles (likely [Nasir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasir_\(missile\))). Two long-range and one short-range box launchers are on each side behind the [superstructure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superstructure). These warships are the first Iranian warship to be equipped with a [vertical launching system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_launching_system) (VLS). The VLS armament component includes six bigger cells for [surface-to-surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface-to-surface) cruise missiles and 16 smaller ones for [surface-to-air missiles](182664.md), all located behind the [command bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_\(nautical\)).&#x20;

The lead warship, *Shahid Soleimani*, was equipped with six 23 mm [gatling guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gatling_gun) (likely [Asef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asif)), one 30 mm [autocannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autocannon) and two [chaff dispensers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaff_\(countermeasure\)). These are also incorporated in other warships of the class. [^cite_note-a-1][^cite_note-\:0-2] According to Commodore [Alireza Tangsiri](58274150.md), *Shahid Soleimani*-class vessels have been equipped with different types of missiles, with ranges of 200, 300 and 750 kilometers respectively, and a new 2,000-kilometer-range cruise missile named Ghadr-474, to boost their [firepower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firepower).[^cite_note-tangsiri_tasnim-3] Tangsiri later said that *Sayyad Shirazi* and *Hassan Baqeri*, the other two warships of the class, were equipped with 16 Navvab vertically-launched missiles and 6 Sayyad-3 missiles.[^cite_note-4]

### Radars and electronics

Intelligence and reconnaissance monitoring, surface and subsurface and air traffic monitoring are among the duties of these vessels and are equipped with Iran's most advanced [electronic warfare](82272.md) systems.[^cite_note-a-1] The main fire-control radars of the built ships are yet to be installed.

### Propulsion

The *Shahid Soleimani* class of warship[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] were powered by four indigenously developed [diesel engines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel_engine), which gives a top speed of 32 [knots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (59 km/h; 37 mph) and a range of 5,000 [nautical miles](21289.md) (9,300 km; 5,800 mi).[^cite_note-a-1]

### Other equipment

The warship has aviation facilities, a helipad, and a crane to launch and retrieve [fast attack boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_attack_craft). The ships can carry three fast attack boats, and have the ability to retrieve and rearm them.[^cite_note-a-1]

## History

After the end of construction at the Shahid Mahallati Shipyard[^cite_note-\:0-2] in June 2022, the [lead ship](855210.md) [IRIS *Shahid Soleimani*](71943430.md), underwent rigorous [sea trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_trial). During sea trials, the lead ship was equipped with four 12.7 mm [machine guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_gun) which were later replaced with four 20 mm gatling guns. On 5 September 2022, *Shahid Soleimani* was commissioned into [IRGC's navy](4676345.md) branch in a [commissioning ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning).[^cite_note-a-1]



A screenshot of footage from the strike on the *IRIS Shirazi*, showing its hull number FS313-03

*IRIS Shahid Sayyad Shirazi (FS313-03)* was damaged in strikes by [United States Armed Forces](32212.md) on 3 March 2026, during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md). In a post made to [X](9988187.md) on 4 March, [United States Central Command](338482.md) said that it had struck *IRIS* *Shahid Sayyad Shirazi* (FS313-03) on the previous night.[^cite_note-7] That same day, U.S. Secretary of Defense [Pete Hegseth](34076202.md) announced that the *Soleimani*, and not the *Shirazi*, had been not only damaged but destroyed. [^cite_note-8] This was likely a confusion of the ship's class and name as images released of the strike show the hull numbers of the *Shirazi*.

On 11 March 2026, [CENTCOM](338482.md) stated that all IRGCN *Soleimani* class ships were destroyed by the [United States](3434750.md) during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-Janes11032026-9]
