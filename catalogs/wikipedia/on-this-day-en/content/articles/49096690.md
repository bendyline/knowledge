The **Oz Brigade** (**89th Brigade**), also known as **Commando Brigade**, is an [infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infantry) formation for [special operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_operation) of the [Israeli Ground Forces](64121347.md). The brigade brings together four commando units – the [Egoz Unit](4311894.md), the [Maglan Unit](26576560.md), the [Duvdevan Unit](11117982.md) and the Operational Support Unit.[^cite_note-2] It was established in December 2015.[^cite_note-SpecForInt-3] Commanded by [Colonel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces_ranks) Omer Cohen, the brigade operates as part of the [98th Division](12871166.md).[^cite_note-SpecForInt-3][^cite_note-DefNews-4][^cite_note-5]

## Ideation and development



Oz Brigade in [Gaza](12047.md) in February 2024

According to the IDF, the Commando Brigade is the brainchild of [Lieutenant General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_General) [Gadi Eisenkot](5249926.md) and is the implementation of operational lessons learned during [Operation Protective Edge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Protective_Edge) in the summer of 2014 and the [Second Lebanon War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Lebanon_War) in 2006. According to the 98th Division Commander, the brigade is an integration of the elite teams of the Infantry Corps under one command, and a body expected to lead operational and tactical thinking in order to establish a new warfare doctrine.[^cite_note-SpecForInt-3][^cite_note-AlMonitor1-6] The new Brigade is intended to provide special operations units a previously lacking centre of gravity.[^cite_note-DefNews-4]

The concept of a commando brigade was developed by IDF Chief of Staff Gadi Eisenkot during the formulation of the 5-years military plan. The new brigade is said to draw further parallels between Israel's special forces and the American [75th Ranger Regiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/75th_Ranger_Regiment).[^cite_note-Ynet-7]

Other special-operations units, [Sayeret Matkal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayeret_Matkal), [Shayetet 13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shayetet_13), [Shaldag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaldag_Unit), [Oketz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oketz_Unit) and [Yahalom](745562.md) continue to operate independently.[^cite_note-Sclerotic-8]

### Brigade organization

The 89th Brigade consists of three (formerly four) commando units of the IDF.[^cite_note-SpecForInt-3][^cite_note-DefNews-4] The brigade had a fourth battalion, the now defunct Unit 845, or "[Rimon](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%99%D7%97%D7%99%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%9F%20\(2010\))", desert warfare unit, which was absorbed into Maglan in June 2018.[^cite_note-9] It also has an elite transport unit tasked with transporting special operations soldiers to and from battlefields in a variety of conditions,[^cite_note-10] assorted support units, and a commando training school. As of 2025, the brigade consists of:

-  **89th Commando Brigade Oz/Courage**&#x20;

  - Unit 212 "[Maglan](26576560.md)" – special reconnaissance unit

    [^cite_note-11]

  - Unit 217 "[Duvdevan](11117982.md)" – undercover counter terrorism unit

  - Unit 621 "[Egoz](4311894.md)" – guerilla warfare unit

    [^cite_note-12]

  - General Staff Mobility Unit 5515

  - Aerial Medical Unit

  - Logistic Battalion

  - Signal Company

  - Commando School "Oz"

## Insignia

The insignia chosen for the new brigade depicts the Hebrew letter Quf (ק), the first letter in the Hebrew word for Commando (קומנדו), composed of a commando knife rising from the sea and a double headed arrow above it. It symbolizes the special designation of the brigade and its mobility, by air, land or sea.[^cite_note-SpecForInt-3][^cite_note-OzTimes-13]
