**Budgam** (Urdu pronunciation: [\[bəɖɡɑːm\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hindi_and_Urdu)), known as **Badgom** (Kashmiri pronunciation: [\[baɖɡoːm\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA); lit. 'big village')[^cite_note-5] in [Kashmiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmiri_language), is a town in [Budgam district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budgam_district) in the union territory of [Jammu and Kashmir](61449812.md), India in the [disputed Kashmir region](3030955.md). In the 2001 census, it was recorded as having a [notified area committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notified_area_committee),[^cite_note-Census2011-town_pop-1][^cite_note-2001CensusProvisional-6] but by the 2011 census it had a municipal committee.[^cite_note-Census2011-town_pop-1]

## Demographics

As of 2011 India [census](6889.md), Budgam had a population of 15,338.[^cite_note-PART_B_DCHB_BADGAM-2] There were 9,003 males (59%) and 6,335 females (41%).[^cite_note-PART_B_DCHB_BADGAM-2] Of the population, 1,335 (8.7%) were age 0-6: 680 males (51%) and 655 females (49%).[^cite_note-PART_B_DCHB_BADGAM-2] The literacy rate for the people over six was 73.0% (males 84.3%, females 56.4%).[^cite_note-PART_B_DCHB_BADGAM-2]

About 25% of the Muslim population of [Budgam district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budgam_district) belong to Shia sect.

## Transport

### Road

Budgam is connected with [National Highway 444](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_444_\(India\)) to rest of India.

### Railways

[Budgam railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budgam_railway_station) is in [Ompora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ompora), which is 2.5 km (1.6 mi) from Budgam. It is on the [Jammu–Baramulla line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu%E2%80%93Baramulla_line).
