The **Ministry of Public Health** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الصحّة العامة) is a ministry of the government of the [Republic of Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Lebanon).[^cite_note-GHDx-1][^cite_note-2] As of 2025, the Minister of Health is Rakan Nassereddine.[^cite_note-3]

The ministry's Department of Primary Healthcare operates over 200 primary health care centres across Lebanon.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The ministry operates a network of 31 government-operated hospitals.[^cite_note-6]

Its headquarters are in Bir Hassan, in the [Jnah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jnah?action=edit\&redlink=1) neighborhood of the capital city [Beirut](37428.md).[^cite_note-GHDx-1]
