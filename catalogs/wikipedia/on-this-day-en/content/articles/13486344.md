The **Spain women's national basketball team** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Selección Española de Baloncesto Femenina*) represents [Spain](26667.md) in international [women's basketball](667977.md) competition and are regulated by the [Spanish Basketball Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Basketball_Federation), the governing body for [basketball in Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_in_Spain). Spain has one of the most successful women's national teams in the world, being the current [FIBA Women's EuroBasket 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2025) silver medallists.[^cite_note-2]

## History

Spain women's basketball team played their first official game in [Malgrat de Mar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malgrat_de_Mar), Barcelona, against [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland_women's_national_basketball_team) on 16 June 1963, losing 31–40.[^cite_note-auto-3] They won their first game against the same team two days later, 47–39. It would take six years to play another international friendly game, losing to [Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuba_women's_national_basketball_team) 50–70 on 28 September 1969.[^cite_note-auto-3]

Their first official games were in March 1970, trying to qualify for the [1970 EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_1970), winning their first game against [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland_women's_national_basketball_team) 61–44 and losing to [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary_women's_national_basketball_team) and [France](6787937.md). The team qualified for their first major international tournament in their next attempt, the [1974 EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_1974). After losing their three group stage games, they won their first game in a final tournament against [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark_women's_national_basketball_team) in the placement matches, finishing in 12th position. [**Rosa Castillo**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosa_Castillo_\(basketball\)) is considered the best player from the mid-1970s to the mid-1980s.[^cite_note-4]

Until 1985, Spain played most Eurobasket tournaments, usually finishing around 10th. An important year for the evolution of the team was the celebration on home soil of the [1987 EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_1987), finishing on 6th position. The team entered their first [Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_Summer_Olympics) qualification in 1988, but failed to qualify. Their first Olympic games were also on home soil in the [1992 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_1992_Summer_Olympics), finishing 5th.

After failing to qualify for the two previous Eurobaskets and having never played a knockout game in a major tournament, the gold medal at the [1993 EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_1993) came as a surprise, beating the newly formed [Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia_women's_national_basketball_team) in the semi-finals 73–55 and [France](6787937.md) in the final 63–53. With [Blanca Ares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blanca_Ares) as their key player (19 PPG), Spain undoubtedly took advantage of the dissolution of the dominant European teams of [Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugoslavia_women's_national_basketball_team), [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia_women's_national_basketball_team), and especially the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union_women's_national_basketball_team).

The EuroBasket victory gave Spain the right to participate in the [1994 Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women) for the first time, finishing 8th. The team qualified for seven consecutive [World Cups](5779563.md), winning three medals.

From 2001 up to 2009, Spain entered a loop of winning five consecutive medals in the Eurobaskets (1 silver, 4 bronze) and being eliminated in the quarterfinals in Summer Olympics and World Cups, until they finally won bronze in the [2010 Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women). From her debut in 1995 to her retirement in 2013, forward **[Amaya Valdemoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amaya_Valdemoro)** became the leader of the Spanish squad, taking part in 13 tournaments, playing 258 games, winning 7 medals and becoming the top scorer with 2,746 points.[^cite_note-5]

The defeat against Croatia on 26 June in [Katowice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katowice) in the second stage of the [2011 EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2011) and the consequent absence from the [2012 Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_2012_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_tournament) has been cited by coaches and players[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] as a catalyst for a *golden period* of seven consecutive medals under coach [Lucas Mondelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_Mondelo). After playing the qualification matches in the [summer of 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2013_qualification), Spain went on to win the [2013 EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2013) with a balance of 9–0. Afterwards, they won silver in the [2014 Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women), bronze in the [2015 EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2015), silver in the [2016 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_2016_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_tournament) and gold again in the [2017 EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2017)[^cite_note-8]. Spain also won the bronze medal at the [2018 Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup) held on home soil in September 2018. In July 2019 Spain successfully defended their European crown by beating [France](6787937.md) 86–66 in the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2019_Final) of the [EuroBasket Women 2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Women's_EuroBasket_2019). These seven successful tournaments came to a halt in the summer of 2021, when the team finished 7th in the [2021 EuroBasket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Women's_EuroBasket_2021) played on home soil and 6th in the [2020 Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_tournament). These results are quite commendable, considering that Spain have only competed with the world elite for two decades. This series of results under coach [Lucas Mondelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_Mondelo) (2012-2021) took the Spanish team to be ranked No. 2 in the ranking of FIBA. In the senior team for two decades (2002-2021) point guard **[Laia Palau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laia_Palau)** is the record-holder for most caps (314) and most medals (12) in 19 final tournaments.[^cite_note-9]

Having failed to qualify for the [2022 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup), a new cycle began with the appointment of veteran [Euroleague Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euroleague_Women) coach [Miguel Méndez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_M%C3%A9ndez_\(basketball\)). [^cite_note-10] In the [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2023) and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2025) Eurobaskets, despite strong performances, they lost to [Belgium](40566612.md) in both finals, and the team was able to win their fourth [World Cup](5779563.md) medal in [2026](73471455.md), claiming the bronze medal.

In the team since 2008, forward **[Alba Torrens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alba_Torrens)** has been regarded as a talented player of her generation, having won 11 medals in 15 tournaments.[^cite_note-11]

At the [Mediterranean Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_Mediterranean_Games), Spain won gold in [1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Mediterranean_Games), and bronze in [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Mediterranean_Games), [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Mediterranean_Games) and [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_2005_Mediterranean_Games).
