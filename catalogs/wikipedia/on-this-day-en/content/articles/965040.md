# Comptroller

"Comptroller general" redirects here. For other uses, see [Comptroller general (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comptroller_general_\(disambiguation\)).

Not to be confused with [Controller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controller_\(disambiguation\)).

A **comptroller** (pronounced either the same as *controller* or as [/kəmpˈtroʊlər/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English)) is a [management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management)-level position responsible for supervising the quality of [accounting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accountancy) and [financial reporting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_reporting) of an organization. A financial comptroller is a senior-level executive who acts as the head of accounting, and oversees the preparation of financial reports, such as balance sheets and income statements.

In most [Commonwealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations) countries, the [comptroller general, auditor general, or comptroller and auditor general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auditor_general) is the [external auditor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/External_auditor) of the budget execution of the government and of [government-owned companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government-owned_corporation). Typically, the independent institution headed by the comptroller general is a member of the [International Organization of Supreme Audit Institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Organization_of_Supreme_Audit_Institutions). In American government, the comptroller is effectively the [chief financial officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_financial_officer) of a public body.

In [business management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_management), the comptroller is closer to a [chief audit executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_audit_executive), holding a senior role in [internal audit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_audit) functions. Generally, the title encompasses a variety of responsibilities, from overseeing [accounting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accounting) and monitoring [internal controls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_control) to countersigning on expenses and commitments.

## Etymology

The word is a variant of "controller". The "cont-" or "count-" part in that word was associated with "compt-", a variant of the verb "count". The term, though criticized by [lexicographers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexicographer) such as [Henry Watson Fowler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Watson_Fowler),[^cite_note-mw-1] is probably retained in part because in official titles it was deemed useful to have the title dissociated from the word and concept "control".[^cite_note-2]

A variant explanation is that *comptroller* evolved in the 15th century through a blend of the French *compte* ("an account") and the [Middle English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_English) *countreroller* (someone who checks a copy of a scroll, from the French *contreroule* "counter-roll, scroll copy"), thus creating a title for a *compteroller* who specializes in checking [financial ledgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_ledger).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-mw-1] This [etymology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etymology) explains why the name is often pronounced identically to "controller" despite the distinct spelling. However, *comptroller* is sometimes pronounced phonetically by those unaware of the word's origins or who wish specifically to avoid confusion with "controller".[^cite_note-4]

## Business role

In business, the comptroller is the person who, independently from the [chief financial officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_financial_officer) in some countries, oversees accounting, and the implementation and monitoring of internal controls. In countries such as the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Israel, and Canada, a comptroller or financial comptroller reports to the CFO in companies that have one.

Businesses typically use the spelling *controller*,[^cite_note-Controller_Vs_Comptroller_by_The_Strategic_CFO-5] while government organizations use *comptroller*.[^cite_note-What_is_the_Difference_Between_a_Controller_and_a_Comptroller?-6]

## Government role

### India

In India, *Comptroller* is an appointment.

- The [Comptroller and Auditor General of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comptroller_and_Auditor_General_of_India) is the authority which [audits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audit) all [receipts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Receipts) and [expenditure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cost) of the [Government of India](553883.md) and the [state governments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_territories_of_India), including those of bodies and authorities substantially financed by the government.

* The *Comptroller of the President's Household* is responsible for the [President of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_India)'s Household at the [Rashtrapati Bhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashtrapati_Bhavan,_New_Delhi). The post is held by a Naval officer of the rank of [Captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_\(naval\)).

- There are officers called *Comptroller of the Governor's household* in each state who are responsible for the [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_\(India\))'s households at the [Raj Bhavans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raj_Bhavan) of each of the states and union territories of India.

### Mexico

In Mexico, the comptroller, translated as 'contralor', was established in the public administration during the presidency of [Miguel de la Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_de_la_Madrid), when he created the [Secretariat of the General Comptroller of the Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretariat_of_the_General_Comptroller_of_the_Federation?action=edit\&redlink=1) in 1982. This ministry was renamed as the [Secretariat of the Civil Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretariat_of_the_Civil_Service) by president [Vicente Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Fox) in 2003. Nevertheless, several states still name as General Comptroller Office their audit and oversight institutions. Namely, [Mexico City](18987.md) has the Secretariat of the General Comptroller of Mexico City and [Jalisco](16020434.md) has the Comptroller General Office of the State of Jalisco.

### United Kingdom

The title of *comptroller* is used in the [Royal Household](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Households_of_the_United_Kingdom) for various offices, including:&#x20;

- the [Comptroller of the Household](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comptroller_of_the_Household) (nowadays a [sinecure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinecure), invariably held by a government [whip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whip_\(politics\)) in the House of Commons). The office was established as part of the [Wardrobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wardrobe_\(government\)) (a powerful department of household and state) in the 13th century, in order to maintain a check on the accounts of the [Treasurer of the Household](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasurer_of_the_Household). Today, the Comptroller's duties outside of government are minimal and mainly ceremonial.

- the Comptroller of the [Lord Chamberlain's Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Chamberlain's_Office), who is a full-time member of the Royal Household; his duties are concerned with the arrangement of ceremonial affairs rather than financial affairs.

The [Comptroller of the Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comptroller_of_the_Navy) is a post in the [Royal Navy](26061.md) responsible for procurement and [matériel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materiel).

The Comptroller and City Solicitor is one of the High Officers of the [City of London Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_London_Corporation), responsible for provision of all legal services. The post of comptroller dates from 1311, and that of City Solicitor from 1544; the two were amalgamated in 1945.

The Comptroller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks is the head of the [UK Intellectual Property Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Intellectual_Property_Office) or [Patent Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patent_Office).

The [Comptroller and Auditor General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comptroller_and_Auditor_General_\(United_Kingdom\)) is head of the [National Audit Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Audit_Office_\(United_Kingdom\)), and is the successor of the former [Comptroller General of the Exchequer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comptroller_General_of_the_Exchequer) and the former [Commissioners of Audit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commissioners_of_Audit).

### United States

The title of comptroller is held by various government officials.

- The [Comptroller General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comptroller_General_of_the_United_States) is the director of the [Government Accountability Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Accountability_Office) (GAO), an agency founded in 1921 (as *General Accounting Office*) to ensure the accountability of the federal government.

* Banks are supervised by the [Office of the Comptroller of the Currency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_the_Comptroller_of_the_Currency), an officer within the federal Department of The Treasury.

  [^cite_note-7]

- Several states and local governments (cities, counties, etc.) have comptrollers, variously elected or appointed, with widely varying powers over budgetary and management matters. (See [Connecticut Comptroller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut_Comptroller), [Florida Comptroller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Comptroller), [Illinois Comptroller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_Comptroller), [Comptroller of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comptroller_of_Maryland), [State Comptroller of New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Comptroller_of_New_Jersey), [New York State Comptroller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_State_Comptroller), [Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Comptroller_of_Public_Accounts); [New York City Comptroller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_Comptroller).) In many US states, the equivalent office is titled [state auditor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_auditor).

### Spain

In Spain, the word comptroller is translated as "*Interventor*".

- The [Comptroller General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comptroller_General_of_the_State) is the head of the Office of the Comptroller General of the State Administration (IGAE), an agency founded in 1874 to ensure the accountability of the Spanish administration.

- All the government departments and agencies have a unit of the IGAE, with a comptroller as its head.

- The [Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Armed_Forces) and the [Social Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_security_in_Spain) are supervised by the [Comptroller General of the Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_the_Comptroller_General_of_the_Defence) and the [Comptroller General of the Social Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_the_Comptroller_General_of_the_Social_Security), respectively.

- Since 2007, the [Royal Household](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Household_of_Spain) has an auditor known as the *Comptroller of the Royal Household of His Majesty the King*.

- All the [regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_regions) and local administrations possesses comptrollers.
