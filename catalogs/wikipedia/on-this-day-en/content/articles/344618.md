The **China National Space Administration** (**CNSA**) is the national [space agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_government_space_agencies) of [China](5405.md). Headquartered in [Haidian, Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haidian,_Beijing), it is responsible for China's civil space programs and international space cooperation. The CNSA is a national bureau under the [Ministry of Industry and Information Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Industry_and_Information_Technology).[^cite_note-3]

Founded in 1993, the CNSA has pioneered a number of achievements in space for China despite its relatively short history, including becoming the first space agency to land on the [far side of the Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_side_of_the_Moon) with [Chang'e 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang'e_4), bringing material back from the Moon with [Chang'e 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang'e_5) and [6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang'e_6), and being the second agency who successfully landed a rover on Mars with [Tianwen-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianwen-1). [Tianwen-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianwen-2) is currently exploring the [co-orbital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-orbital) near-Earth asteroid [469219 Kamoʻoalewa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/469219_Kamo%CA%BBoalewa) and is planned to also explore the [active asteroid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_asteroid) [311P/PanSTARRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/311P/PanSTARRS). It will also collect regolith samples of Kamo'oalewa.[^cite_note-nature20190430-4]

## History

CNSA is an agency created in 1993 when the [Ministry of Aerospace Industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Aerospace_Industry) was split into CNSA and the [China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Aerospace_Science_and_Technology_Corporation) (CASC). The former was to be responsible for policy, while the latter was to be responsible for execution. This arrangement proved somewhat unsatisfactory, as these two agencies were, in effect, one large agency, sharing both personnel and management.[^cite_note-CNSA_Organization_and_Function-5]

As part of a massive restructuring in 1998, CASC was split into a number of smaller [state-owned companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government-owned_corporation). The intention appeared to have been to create a system similar to [Western](21208200.md) military procurement, where central government agencies procure services from smaller entities.[^cite_note-CNSA_Organization_and_Function-5]

Since the passage of the [Wolf Amendment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Amendment) in 2011, [NASA](18426568.md) has been forced by Congress to implement a long-standing exclusion policy with CNSA ever since, though this has been periodically overcome.[^cite_note-6]

In 2021, China began building the [Tiangong space station](2481401.md), which consists of three modules designated for crew, cargo, and research. The construction was completed in late 2022, and there are plans to add an additional three modules.[^cite_note-7]

## Function

CNSA was established as a government institution to develop and fulfill China's due international obligations, with the approval by the [8th National People's Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_National_People's_Congress) of China (NPC). The 9th NPC assigned CNSA as an internal structure of the [Commission of Science, Technology and Industry for National Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_of_Science,_Technology_and_Industry_for_National_Defense) (COSTIND). CNSA assumes the following main responsibilities: signing governmental agreements in the space area on behalf of organizations, inter-governmental scientific and technical exchanges; and also being in charge of the enforcement of national space policies and managing the national space science, technology and industry.

China has signed governmental space cooperation agreements with [Argentina](18951905.md), [Brazil](3383.md), [Chile](5489.md), [France](5843419.md), [Germany](11867.md), [India](14533.md), [Italy](14532.md), [Pakistan](23235.md), [Russia](25391.md), [Ukraine](31750.md), the [United Kingdom](31717.md), the [United States](3434750.md), and some other countries. Achievements have been scored in the bilateral and multilateral and technology exchanges and cooperation.[^cite_note-CNSA_Organization_and_Function-5]

## Departments

There are four departments under the CNSA:

- Department of General Planning

- Department of System Engineering

- Department of Science, Technology and Quality Control

- Department of Foreign Affairs

CNSA's logo is a similar design to that of [China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Aerospace_Science_and_Technology_Corporation).[^cite_note-11] The arrow in the middle is similar to the Chinese character 人 which means 'human' or 'people', to state that humans are the center of all space exploration. The three concentric ellipses stand for three types of [escape velocity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escape_velocity) (minimum speed needed to reach sustainable orbits, to escape the Earth system, and to escape the Solar System) which are milestones of space exploration. The second ring is drawn with a bold line, to state that China has passed the first stage of exploration (Earth system) and is undergoing the second stage exploration (within the Solar System). The 人 character stands above the three rings to emphasize humanity's capability to escape and explore.
