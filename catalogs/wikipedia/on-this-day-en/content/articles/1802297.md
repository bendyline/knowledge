**Serres** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Περιφερειακή ενότητα Σερρών) is one of the [regional units of Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_units_of_Greece), in the [geographic region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographic_regions_of_Greece) of [Macedonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macedonia_\(Greece\)). It is part of the [Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_regions_of_Greece) of [Central Macedonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Macedonia). Its capital is the city of [Serres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serres). The total population reaches just over 150,000.

## Geography

The mountains are [Orvelos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orvelos) to the north, [Menoikio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menoikio) to the east, [Pangaio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangaion_hills) to the southeast, [Kerdylio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerdylio?action=edit\&redlink=1) to the southwest, [Vertiskos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertiskos) to the west, parts of [Krousi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krousi?action=edit\&redlink=1) to the west and portions of the [Kerkini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belasica) lies to the northwest. The regional unit borders on [Thessaloniki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thessaloniki_\(regional_unit\)) to the southwest, [Kilkis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilkis_\(regional_unit\)) to the west, [North Macedonia](23564616.md) with the [Novo Selo Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novo_Selo_Municipality) to the northwest, the [Blagoevgrad Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blagoevgrad_Province) of [Bulgaria](3415.md) to the north, [Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drama_\(regional_unit\)) to the northeast and [Kavala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kavala_\(regional_unit\)) to the east. The [Strymonian Gulf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strymonian_Gulf) lies to the south along with the Strymonas delta. [Lake Kerkini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Kerkini) was a lake located in the southern portion which is now drained. 41% of the regional unit are arable and most of the lands are near the [Strymonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Struma_River) river which flows from Bulgaria and empties into the [Strymonian Gulf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strymonian_Gulf). Another river is the [Angitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angitis) in the eastern part of the regional unit, with the ravine and caves near [Alistrati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alistrati).

The regional unit has many archaeological and historical features including [Serres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serres,_Greece), [Amphipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphipolis), several monasteries, and Metaxa near the border with Bulgaria. The regional unit is also a tourist attraction including [Lailia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lailia), rich in forests, a skiing resort in the central portion, lake Kerkini which is a reservoir supplying water to the farmlands. Fishing is common within Ano Poroia especially during the summer months and famous [Alistrati's caves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alistrati) and the nearby Aggitis ravine.

The southern part around the [Strymonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strymonas) valley has a Mediterranean climate, the rest is predominantly continental with cold winters in higher elevations.

## History

In modern times, Serres, like the rest of [Macedonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macedonia_\(region\)) was contested territory between [Greece](12108.md), [Bulgaria](3415.md), [Serbia](29265.md) and the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md). After its liberation from the [Turks](22278.md) by the [Bulgarians](151876.md) in the [First Balkan War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Balkan_War), Serres became a part of Greece at the end of the [Second Balkan War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Balkan_War). During the [National Schism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Schism), it was occupied by Bulgaria again only to form part of Greece at the end of the war. During the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War) Bulgaria occupied Serres and launched a campaign of [Bulgarisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarisation). It was liberated in 1944.

A substantial portion of the population of the regional unit are descendants of refugees which came from Eastern [Thrace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrace) which is now Northwestern Turkey, [Asia Minor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Minor) and from [Pontus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontus_\(region\)) during the [Greco-Turkish War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greco-Turkish_War_\(1919%E2%80%931922\)).

## Transport

The [Thessaloniki–Alexandroupolis railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thessaloniki%E2%80%93Alexandroupolis_railway) passes through the regional unit, with the [Strymon–Kulata railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strymon%E2%80%93Kulata_railway) branching off towards Bulgaria at [Strymonas station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strymonas_railway_station): other main stations include [Serres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serres_railway_station) and [Sidirokastro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidirokastro_railway_station).

The main roads of Serres regional unit are:

- [A2 motorway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A2_motorway_\(Greece\)) (Egnatia Odos, Igoumenitsa–Thessaloniki–Amfipoli–Alexandroupoli)

- [A25 motorway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A25_motorway_\(Greece\)) (Thessaloniki–Serres–Sidirokastro–Bulgaria)

- [EO2 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_2) (Florina–Thessaloniki–Amfipoli–Alexandroupoli)

- [EO12 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_12) (Thessaloniki–Serres–Drama–Kavala)

- [EO59 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_59) (Amfipoli–Nea Zichni)

- [EO63 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_63) (Serres–Sidirokastro–Bulgaria)

- [EO65 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_National_Road_65) (Thessaloniki–Kilkis–Sidirokastro)

The [Amphipolis–Kavala National Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphipolis%E2%80%93Kavala_National_Road) branches off the EO2 just outside the unit, near [Ofrynio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ofrynio).
