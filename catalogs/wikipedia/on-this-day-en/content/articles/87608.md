# List of companies of France



Location of [France](5843419.md)

[France](5843419.md) is a country whose territory consists of [metropolitan France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_France) in [western Europe](33800.md), as well as [several overseas regions and territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_France). A member of the [Group of 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_of_Seven) (formerly G8) leading industrialised countries, as of 2014, it is ranked as the world's [ninth-largest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_GDP_\(PPP\)) and the EU's second-largest economy by [purchasing power parity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purchasing_power_parity).[^cite_note-data.worldbank.org-1] With 31 of the 500 biggest companies in the world in 2015, France ranks fourth in the [Fortune Global 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortune_Global_500), ahead of Germany and the UK.[^cite_note-2] According to the [World Trade Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Organization) (WTO), in 2009 France was the world's sixth-largest exporter and the fourth-largest importer of manufactured goods.[^cite_note-auto1-3][^cite_note-4]

French companies have maintained key positions in the insurance and banking industries: [Axa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axa) is the world's largest insurance company. The leading French banks are [BNP Paribas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BNP_Paribas) and the [Crédit agricole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cr%C3%A9dit_agricole), ranking as the world's largest and sixth-largest banks in 2010[^cite_note-5] (by assets), while the [Société Générale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9_G%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale) group was ranked the world's eighth-largest in 2009.

For further information on the types of business entities in this country and their abbreviations, see "[Business entities in France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_business_entities#France)".
