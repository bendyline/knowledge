**Colón** is one of the 18 [departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Honduras) into which [Honduras](13394.md) is divided. It was created in 1881. The departmental capital is [Trujillo](2342345.md), and the other main city is [Tocoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tocoa,_Col%C3%B3n). Trujillo was the site of the first Catholic mass on the American mainland, held when [Christopher Columbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus) (Cristóbal Colón) reached the Honduran shore in 1502.

Colón harbors a substantial [Garifuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garifuna_people) population and has pristine beaches and rainforested national parks. The Fort of Santa Barbara, built by the Spaniards in the colonial era, was the site of the execution of US [filibuster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filibuster_\(military\)) [William Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Walker_\(filibuster\)) in Trujillo, and his remains are buried in the city's graveyard.

The department covers a total surface area of 8,875 km<sup>2</sup> and, in 2007, had an estimated population of 284,900 people.
