**Hojai district** is a district in [Assam](186162.md), [India](14533.md). It was formed on 15 August 2015.[^cite_note-2] The headquarters of the district is situated at [Sankardev Nagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sankardev_Nagar), which is about 8 km away from [Hojai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hojai) town. Hojai District was formed from three tehsils of [Nagaon District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagaon_District), namely [Hojai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hojai), [Doboka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doboka) and [Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanka,_Assam).[^cite_note-HojaiCarve-3] Hojai was a part of the undivided Nowgong district (now Nagaon) of then [Assam Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assam_Province).

On 31 December 2022, the district was temporarily remerged into Nagaon district.[^cite_note-4] On 25 August 2023, the Assam cabinet reinstated Hojai's district status.[^cite_note-5]

## Etymology

*Hojai* is a surname commonly used by the [Dimasa community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimasa_people). The word ‘Hojai’ is of [Dimasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimasa_language) origin.

## History

The geographical area presently under Hojai district and its surrounding area, as in the history of the ancient [Kamarupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamarupa), was known as [Davaka kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davaka_Kingdom) or [Kapili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kopili_River) Valley kingdom. In different sources this kingdom is mentioned as ‘Dabak’, ‘Kapili’ and ‘Tribeg’. This kingdom enjoyed independent status up to the 6th century CE.[^cite_note-6]

The region was ruled by the [Kachari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kachari_kingdom) king *[Maha Manikya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahamanikya)* (also *Mahamanikpha*) in the mid 14th century, who claimed the Barāha (Varaha) lineage.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-Barahi-8] During his reign, he settled Brahmins and promoted Brahmanical culture in the Hojai district. Under his patronage [Madhava Kandali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhava_Kandali) translated the Sanskrit epic *[Ramayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramayana)* to [Assamese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assamese_language) verse called *[Saptakanda Ramayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saptakanda_Ramayana)*.[^cite_note-Barahi-8] A stone inscription found at Lanka, Hojai dated to 1352 AD records Mahamanikya giving land to a Brahmin named *Din Dwija* at a village named *Bamdeva*.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-GY-10]

In the modern day, Hojai saw a large influx of Sylheti Hindu refugees after Sylhet went to Pakistan. In 1993, the [Hojai riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hojai_riots) broke out between Hindus and Muslims over tensions related to the [Babri Masjid demolition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demolition_of_the_Babri_Masjid).

## Demographics

### Population

According to the Indian Census of 2011, the three tehsils in the newly formed Hojai District had a population of 931,218, of which 172,350 (18.51%) live in urban areas. Hojai has a sex ratio of 954 females per 1000 males. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes make up 109,437 (11.75%) and 28,731 (3.09%) respectively.[^cite_note-districtcensus-11]

After the [Sylhet referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylhet_referendum) which led to separation of [Sylhet Division](577594.md) from then [Assam Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assam_Province), a large number of [Bengali Hindus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_Hindus) have migrated to the area from the then [East Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Pakistan) and are mostly settled in the towns of [Lumding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumding), [Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanka_\(town\)) and [Hojai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hojai).

The Judicial Court at Hojai was established on 20 October 1982 and functioning as Court of Judicial Magistrate First Class. The Courts of Additional District and Sessions Judge(Fast Track Court), Sub-Divisional Judicial Magistrate, Munsiff cum JMFC Courts were later added under District Judiciary, Nagaon.

On 6 March 2021, Hon'ble Mr. Justice Suman Shyam, Judge Guahati High Court, inaugurated the Newly created Courts of District & Sessions Judge and Chief Judicial Magistrate at Hojai, [Sankardev Nagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sankardev_Nagar). Sri Aditya Hazarika became the first District & Sessions Judge and Sri Shakti Sharma became the first Chief Judicial Magistrate of Hojai District.[^cite_note-16]

### Politics

Hojai district has three assembly constituencies. They are [Jamunamukh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamunamukh_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)), [Hojai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hojai_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)) and [Lumding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumding_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)). Hojai district is also part of [Nowgong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nowgong_\(Lok_Sabha_constituency\)) constituency.[^cite_note-17]
