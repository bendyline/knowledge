**Gvardeyskoye** or **Hvardiiske** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Гвардейское; or [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Гвардійське) is an airbase of the [Russian Aerospace Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Aerospace_Forces) in [Hvardiiske](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hvardiiske,_Simferopol_Raion), [Simferopol Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simferopol_Raion), in [Russian occupied Crimea](71944314.md), [Ukraine](31750.md). It is located a few kilometres north of [Simferopol International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simferopol_International_Airport).

The history of flying here began in the 1930s when [Polikarpov Po-2s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polikarpov_Po-2) were flown.&#x20;

The base is home to the 37th Guards Composite Aviation Regiment, which flies the [Sukhoi Su-24](382335.md)M (ASCC: Fencer) and the [Sukhoi Su-25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-25)SM/25UB (ASCC: Frogfoot) under the [27th Composite Aviation Division](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/27-%D1%8F%20%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B1%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%8F%20%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%8F%20%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%B8%D1%8F) as part of the [4th Air and Air Defence Forces Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4th_Air_and_Air_Defence_Forces_Army), [Southern Military District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Military_District).[^cite_note-1]

The base was home to the [43rd Independent Maritime Assault Aviation Regiment](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/43-%D0%B9%20%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B1%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B9%20%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B9%20%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%BA) from 1990.[^cite_note-2] It came under the control of the [Black Sea Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea_Fleet).[^cite_note-3]
