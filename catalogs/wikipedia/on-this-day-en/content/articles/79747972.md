The **2026 NASCAR Cup Series** is the 78th season for [NASCAR](21114.md) professional [stock car racing](28349.md) in the United States and the 55th season for the modern-era Cup Series. The preseason featured the [Cook Out Clash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Cook_Out_Clash) exhibition race on February 4 at [Bowman Gray Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowman_Gray_Stadium), followed by the season-opening [68th running](81427567.md) of the [Daytona 500](39021.md) on February 15 at [Daytona International Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytona_International_Speedway). The season will end at [Homestead–Miami Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homestead%E2%80%93Miami_Speedway) on November 8.[^cite_note-2026schedule-1]

[Kyle Larson](36163081.md) of [Hendrick Motorsports](2089777.md) returns as the reigning NASCAR Cup Series champion.[^cite_note-2] Following the [Dollar Tree 301](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Dollar_Tree_301) at [New Hampshire Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Motor_Speedway), [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) clinched the No. 1 seed in [The Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Chase), marking its return as the Cup Series' championship format after last being implemented in [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_NASCAR_Sprint_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-3]

This season was marred by the death of two-time Cup Series champion [Kyle Busch](1467805.md) due to [sepsis](158400.md) caused by pneumonia on May 21, days before the [Coca-Cola 600](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Coca-Cola_600). Busch was the first active full-time driver to pass away during a season since [Dale Earnhardt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Earnhardt)'s death in [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_NASCAR_Winston_Cup_Series), the first to do so off-track since [Davey Allison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davey_Allison) in [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_NASCAR_Winston_Cup_Series), and overall driver since [Jason Leffler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Leffler), who was a part-time driver in [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_NASCAR_Sprint_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-Busch-4]

### Team changes

[Chevrolet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet) unveiled a new body style for the [ZL1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet_Camaro_\(sixth_generation\)#ZL1) in November 2025 for the 2026 season to replace the model that had been used since [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NASCAR_Cup_Series). The redesign is based on the Camaro ZL1 Carbon Performance Package accessories kit.[^cite_note-95] [Haas Factory Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_Factory_Team) and [Rick Ware Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Ware_Racing) switched from [Ford](30433662.md) to Chevrolet in 2026.[^cite_note-HaasChevrolet-6] HFT has a technical alliance with [Hendrick Motorsports](2089777.md), while RWR formed a technical partnership with [Richard Childress Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Childress_Racing).[^cite_note-RWRChevy-28]

### Driver changes

[Justin Haley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Haley_\(racing_driver\)) did not return to [Spire Motorsports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spire_Motorsports).[^cite_note-Haley-29] Haley returned to [Kaulig Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaulig_Racing), driving in the [Craftsman Truck Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NASCAR_Craftsman_Truck_Series).[^cite_note-96] [Daniel Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Su%C3%A1rez), who was released from [Trackhouse Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackhouse_Racing), moved to Spire Motorports in Haley's place.[^cite_note-SuarezSpire-30]

[Kyle Busch](1467805.md) died three days before the [2026 Coca-Cola 600](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Coca-Cola_600) from hemorrhagic shock caused by sepsis from untreated pneumonia; he was going to skip the race weekend to deal with the illness and [Austin Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Hill) was to drive in his place.[^cite_note-Busch-4] In his honor, [Richard Childress Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Childress_Racing) decided to stop using his number, 8, and hold it until his son [Brexton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brexton_Busch) could begin racing in NASCAR; the car was renumbered to 33.[^cite_note-97] On June 6, Hill was named as Busch's permanent replacement for at least the remainder of the season.[^cite_note-98]

## Rule changes

### Technical changes

#### Horsepower

Horsepower increased from 670 to 750 at tracks under 1.5 miles and road courses.[^cite_note-99]

#### Track packages

[Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Motor_Speedway), [Darlington](685467.md), [Dover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dover_Motor_Speedway), [Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_Superspeedway), [New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Motor_Speedway), and [St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gateway_Motorsports_Park) would run the short track package rather than the intermediate package from previous years.[^cite_note-100] NASCAR also mandated a permanent A-post flap for all races to prevent flips. The A-post flap debuted the previous year at [Daytona II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Coke_Zero_Sugar_400).[^cite_note-101]

#### Superspeedway packages

NASCAR changed the superspeedway package starting at [Daytona II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Coke_Zero_Sugar_400) to a "4-inch spoiler and wide splitter stuffers. The spoiler deflection device was redesigned to accommodate the 4-inch spoiler. New spoiler braces and an engine tapered spacer were reduced to 27/32-inch."[^cite_note-102] Starting at [Dega II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_YellaWood_500), the spoiler was shrunk again to "3.5 inches".[^cite_note-103]

### Policy changes

#### Loosening lower series restriction

The amount of races full-time Cup drivers can run in the [O'Reilly Auto Parts Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_O'Reilly_Auto_Parts_Series) and [Craftsman Truck Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Craftsman_Truck_Series) was increased to ten and eight respectively, up from the 2025 restriction of five in each series. Cup drivers remain ineligible for points, as well as unable to compete in the postseason events.[^cite_note-NASCAR_Digital_Media-104]

#### Age limit

The minimum age requirement to race in the O'Reilly Auto Parts series was lowered to 17 for road courses and oval tracks less than 1.25 miles in length. This change creates a staggered minimum age: 16 in Trucks, 17 in O'Reilly, and 18 in Cup Series events.[^cite_note-NASCAR_Digital_Media-104]

#### Qualifying hand restriction

The restriction of hands out the window during qualifying at drafting tracks such as [Daytona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytona_International_Speedway), [Talladega](681674.md), and [Atlanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Motor_Speedway) (EchoPark) was added, and drivers who touch the window net or the hole in between to redirect air will have their qualifying time disallowed.[^cite_note-105] [Noah Gragson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Gragson) was the first to break this rule, having his [Daytona 500](81427567.md) qualifying time erased.[^cite_note-106]

#### Increased practice time

Practice time was increased at most tracks besides [Daytona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytona_International_Speedway), [Talladega](681674.md) and [Atlanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Motor_Speedway). The change saw two groups of 25 minutes each to one 50-minute session involving all cars.[^cite_note-107] Beginning at [New Hampshire Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Motor_Speedway), Cup Series practice will be consolidated into a single 50-minute session.[^cite_note-108]

#### Shorter caution periods

Beginning at [Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iowa_Corn_350), NASCAR announced that when a caution comes out with 10 laps or less in the stage, the stage will be deemed official.[^cite_note-109]

### Charter agreement changes

Following the outcome of the [23XI Racing lawsuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/23XI_Racing_v._NASCAR), all teams were offered "evergreen" charters. In contrast to previous agreements, which expired at a set date, the new agreement would include charters that would not expire.[^cite_note-110]

### Points system changes

#### Postseason format

NASCAR revealed a new postseason format in January 2026. Used in the top three series, it is a return of the [Chase format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Chase) previously used from [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_NASCAR_Nextel_Cup_Series) to [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_NASCAR_Sprint_Cup_Series). The top 16 drivers on points after 26 races will qualify for the Chase, with the 'win-and-you're-in' rule being scrapped, and starting points in the Chase will be staggered based on the regular season standings.[^cite_note-chase_return-111] This format replaced a derided playoff system, which included four "rounds" with points resets. With the change, playoff points are no longer awarded.[^cite_note-chase_return-111]

#### First-place point increase

The points awarded for finishing first in a race was increased from 40 to 55.[^cite_note-chase_return-111]

#### Fastest lap restriction

Drivers who enter the garage during a race will no longer be eligible for the fastest lap bonus point. If the driver's fastest lap was achieved before they entered the garage, it will still stand.[^cite_note-112]

#### Removal of regular season championship

With the changes of the chase, the regular season championship was removed.[^cite_note-113]

[Homestead–Miami Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homestead%E2%80%93Miami_Speedway) returned to its traditional date as the season finale for all three major series.[^cite_note-140] To accommodate this change, [New Hampshire Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Motor_Speedway) was removed from The Chase.[^cite_note-2026schedule-1] [Chicago Street Course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Street_Course) officials announced that the [Grant Park 165](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grant_Park_165) will not return in 2026, with hopes to revive the event in future seasons.[^cite_note-141] The Cup Series returned to Southern California with a [street race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronado_Street_Course) at [Naval Base Coronado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Base_Coronado) near [San Diego](28504.md).[^cite_note-142] To accommodate this change, [Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Cup_Series_at_Mexico_City) was dropped from the schedule due to a conflict with the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).[^cite_note-143] The [NASCAR All-Star Race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_All-Star_Race) moved from [North Wilkesboro Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Wilkesboro_Speedway) to [Dover Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dover_Motor_Speedway), giving the former a [points race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Cup_Series_at_North_Wilkesboro_Speedway), the first regular-season race held since [1996](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996_NASCAR_Winston_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-2026schedule-1] [Chicagoland Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicagoland_Speedway) returned to the schedule for the first time since [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Monster_Energy_NASCAR_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-2026schedule-1] The [Charlotte Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Motor_Speedway) [fall race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_America_400) moved away from the [roval layout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_America_Roval_400) to the oval after eight years as a road course race.[^cite_note-Gluck-137]

## Season summary

### Preseason races

Due to [a historic snowstorm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_30_%E2%80%93_February_2,_2026_nor'easter) over the United States, the [Cook Out Clash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Cook_Out_Clash) was postponed three times from its original Saturday and Sunday dates, before ultimately being condensed to a one-day event on Wednesday, February 4.[^cite_note-144][^cite_note-145][^cite_note-clashpostponed-115] Reigning champion [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won pole position. Weather plagued the event, causing NASCAR to mandate use of wet weather tires after a scheduled half-race break. After an event record 17 cautions, [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) won the Clash, joining [Jeff Gordon](18600829.md) and [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) as drivers who won the Clash before winning their first points paying race.[^cite_note-146]

The [Duel at Daytona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_America_250_Florida_Duel_at_Daytona) races set the starting order for the [Daytona 500](81427567.md), with the exception of the front row, which is set by the fastest two times in qualifying. [Kyle Busch](1467805.md) and [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won the pole and second place respectively, and therefore started first in each duel race.[^cite_note-147] During the first Duel, [RFK Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RFK_Racing) dominated the majority of the race, seeking to help [Corey LaJoie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corey_LaJoie) clinch among the "Open" cars, as he had not qualified for the race. Unfortunately for LaJoie, a last lap wreck ended his chances of making the 500. [Casey Mears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casey_Mears), despite a spin earlier in the race, dodged LaJoie during the wreck, clinching his spot into the race, as well as making his first Daytona 500 start since [2019's race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Daytona_500).[^cite_note-Daytona_Duels_Recap-148]

During the second Duel, which ran caution-free, [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) finished 0.065 seconds ahead of [Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar).[^cite_note-Daytona_Duels_Recap-148] [Anthony Alfredo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Alfredo) finished in position to make the Daytona 500 as the last open car, but was disqualified after a post-race technical inspection found that his car's cooling hoses were not secured. Due to the disqualification, the next-best finishing open car, driven by [B. J. McLeod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._J._McLeod), qualified for the race.[^cite_note-149]

### Regular season

NASCAR moved the [Daytona 500](81427567.md) start time forward an hour to 1\:30 p.m. eastern time due to the threat of rain in the evening.[^cite_note-Daytona500movedup-118] [Zane Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Smith) won stage one after staying out in the middle of pit cycles, as well as getting his first career stage win. Stage two featured a battle between polesitter [Kyle Busch](1467805.md) and [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) for the lead for the majority of the stage. [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md), however, won the second stage, after narrowly avoiding ["The Big One"](21509243.md), caused by [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) tapping [Justin Allgaier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Allgaier)'s rear bumper, triggering a 20-car pileup on the tri-oval.[^cite_note-150] After a restart with five laps remaining, [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) escaped two wrecks and passed [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) for the win exiting turn four.[^cite_note-DaytonaRecap-151]

[Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) won the pole at [Atlanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Autotrader_400) after qualifying was canceled due to rain.[^cite_note-152] [Austin Cindric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Cindric) won stage one, and [William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) appeared to have won stage two, however [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) was later ruled as the stage winner due to a spin by [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) just feet behind him. During overtime, Reddick, who was involved in a crash on 160, won the race. Reddick became the sixth driver ever, and the first since [Matt Kenseth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Kenseth) in [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_NASCAR_Sprint_Cup_Series), to win the first two races of the season.[^cite_note-AtlantaRecap-153]

[Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) won his second pole of the season at [Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_DuraMAX_Texas_Grand_Prix), his third pole win at the track, with the other pole wins being [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_EchoPark_Texas_Grand_Prix) and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_EchoPark_Automotive_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-154] Pit strategies shook up the stage standings, with [Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain) winning stage one and [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) winning stage two. On lap 76, [Myatt Snider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myatt_Snider) replaced [Alex Bowman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Bowman), as Bowman experienced [vertigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertigo) symptoms during the race.[^cite_note-155] Reddick dominated the later half of the race, becoming the first driver in series history to win the first three races to begin a season.[^cite_note-AustinRecap-156]

[Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) won the pole at [Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Straight_Talk_Wireless_500).[^cite_note-157] Similar to the [previous season's fall race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NASCAR_Cup_Series_Championship_Race) at the [track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Raceway), tires had increased tire wear, playing a key role in the race. [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won stage one and [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won stage two. A late race caution on lap 295 reset the field; Bell had led 176 laps to that point. Blaney passed the leader, [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs), on lap 303, holding off a hard charging Bell and ultimately winning the race, getting his second consecutive win at the track.[^cite_note-PhoenixRecap-158]

[Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won the pole at [Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pennzoil_400).[^cite_note-159] Bell won stage one and [William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) won stage two. [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md), who finished second to Bell in qualifying, dominated the race. Hamlin would overcome a pit road speeding penalty earlier in the race and led a race high 134 laps en route to his 61st Cup Series win, putting him 10th all time on [NASCAR's all-time winners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_all-time_NASCAR_Cup_Series_winners), as well as claiming his [second consecutive win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_South_Point_400) at the [track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas_Motor_Speedway).[^cite_note-LasVegasRecap-160]

[Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) won the pole at [Darlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Goodyear_400).[^cite_note-161] [Brad Keselowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Keselowski) swept the stages, seeking to get his first win since [2024's running of the race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Goodyear_400). Reddick battled for the lead with Keselowski, eventually overtaking him on lap 265, winning the race and continuing his early season success. Reddick became the third driver ever, along with [Dale Earnhardt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Earnhardt) and [Bill Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Elliott), to win 4 of the first 6 races of a season.[^cite_note-DarlingtonRecap-162][^cite_note-163]

[Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole at [Martinsville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Cook_Out_400_\(Martinsville\)).[^cite_note-164] A caution came out on lap 77, ending stage one early with Hamlin winning. Hamlin eventually swept the stages. [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) would take advantage of a late race caution and would hold off Hamlin for his 22nd career win and first win at [Martinsville Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martinsville_Speedway) since [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_NASCAR_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-MartinsvilleRecap-165]

[Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the pole at [Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Food_City_500).[^cite_note-166] [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) swept the stages for the second straight day, as he also swept [Saturday's O'Reilly race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Suburban_Propane_300) stages. A late race caution on lap 498 would shake up the race and cause [overtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green%E2%80%93white%E2%80%93checkered_finish), [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) held off a hard charging Blaney and Larson to get his first career Cup Series win, becoming the first Cup driver to get their first win at [Bristol Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Motor_Speedway) since [Kurt Busch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Busch) in [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Food_City_500).[^cite_note-BristolRecap-167]

[Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) won the pole at [Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_AdventHealth_400).[^cite_note-168] [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won stage one and [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won stage two. Reddick ran out of fuel on lap 266 before a spin by [Cody Ware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Ware) triggered a caution, forcing overtime. Reddick, following pit stops, was able to pass three cars in the final two laps of overtime for his fifth win of the season,[^cite_note-KansasRecap-169] becoming the first driver since [Dale Earnhardt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Earnhardt) in [1987](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_NASCAR_Winston_Cup_Series) to win five of the first nine races in a season.

Rain at [Talladega](82750631.md) canceled qualifying, with [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) winning the pole.[^cite_note-170] The stages were changed to prevent fuel saving.[^cite_note-171] [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) won stage one. On lap 115, ["The Big One"](21509243.md) struck, taking out 26 drivers, such as [Kyle Larson](36163081.md), [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md), [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano), [Daniel Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Su%C3%A1rez), [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs), [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney), and others. [Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain) won stage two. The race ended with [Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar) scoring his first career win by 0.114 seconds over [Chris Buescher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Buescher) and [Alex Bowman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Bowman).[^cite_note-TalladegaRecap-172]

[Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar) won the pole at [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_W%C3%BCrth_400).[^cite_note-173] [Erik Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Jones) won stage one, his first career stage win. [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) won stage two. Elliott would dominate the later half of the race, and ultimately would hold off runner-up [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) after a late race caution for his second career win at [Texas Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Motor_Speedway).[^cite_note-TexasRecap-174]

[Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won the pole at [Watkins Glen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Go_Bowling_at_The_Glen).[^cite_note-175] [Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain) won stage one and van Gisbergen won stage two. Following pit cycles, stage three featured a battle between [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) and [Connor Zilisch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Zilisch). However, van Gisbergen, after ending up 24th after pit cycles on lap 76, was able to overtake Gibbs with 8 to go and would win the race, claiming his second consecutive win at [Watkins Glen International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watkins_Glen_International).[^cite_note-WatkinsGlenRecap-176]

[Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole for the exhibition race at [Dover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NASCAR_All-Star_Race).[^cite_note-177] On lap two, a wreck occurred with [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece)'s car catching on fire, bringing out the red flag early. Another caution on lap 63 ended segment one early, with [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) winning segment one. Segment two featured an inversion of the field, with [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) winning segment two. The field was shrunk to 26 cars for the final segment. Hamlin eventually dominated the final segment, holding off [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) for his second [NASCAR All-Star Race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_All-Star_Race) win, and first since [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_NASCAR_Sprint_All-Star_Race).[^cite_note-DoverRecap-178]

The next weekend's race at [Charlotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Coca-Cola_600) was marred by the death of [Kyle Busch](1467805.md) that Thursday, as he had developed sepsis stemming from pneumonia earlier in the week. Rain at the track canceled qualifying, with [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) winning the pole.[^cite_note-179] [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won stage one, [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won stage two, and [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won stage three, with [Joe Gibbs Racing](2410190.md) claiming a 1-2-3-4 sweep. After a thrilling final stage, run with rain in the area, [Daniel Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Su%C3%A1rez) was announced as the winner after the race was called 27 laps early due to rainfall at the track. Suárez claimed his first win since the [2024 Ambetter Health 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Ambetter_Health_400) and third overall in his career. Suárez would dedicate the win to Busch.[^cite_note-CharlotteRecap-180]

Rain at [Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Cracker_Barrel_400) canceled qualifying, with [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) winning the pole.[^cite_note-181] Hamlin immediately received a black flag for a jump start penalty, the first of his career.[^cite_note-182] After a caution on lap 82 set up a one-lap shootout, [A. J. Allmendinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._J._Allmendinger) won stage one, his first stage win on an oval. Another caution on lap 183 ended stage two under caution, with [Daniel Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Su%C3%A1rez) winning stage two. After a late race caution, Hamlin passed teammates [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) and [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) to win the race after a three-wide move, claiming [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota)'s first win at [Nashville Superspeedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_Superspeedway), as well as overcoming his penalty from earlier in the race.[^cite_note-NashvilleRecap-183]

[Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole at [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FireKeepers_Casino_400).[^cite_note-184] Hamlin later went to the rear following unapproved adjustments for the start of the race.[^cite_note-185] [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) won stage one, and [Chase Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Elliott) won stage two. On lap 149, [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) and Elliott wrecked, bringing out the red flag, as well as causing damage to the outside [safer barrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safer_barrier). After another caution on lap 154, Hamlin dominated the later half of the race. Hamlin claimed his 63rd career win, and would tie long time teammate [Kyle Busch](1467805.md) for [9th on the all time NASCAR win list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_all-time_NASCAR_Cup_Series_winners), waving a special flag in memoriam.[^cite_note-MichiganRecap-186]

NASCAR moved [Pocono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_The_Great_American_Getaway_400)'s start time up two hours to 1 p.m. eastern time due to the threat of evening rain.[^cite_note-PoconoMovedUp-125] [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole.[^cite_note-187] Hamlin won stage one, and [Todd Gilliland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Gilliland) won stage two, his first career stage win. Hamlin overtook teammate [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) with 5 laps to go to eventually win the race, claiming his eighth career win at [Pocono Raceway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocono_Raceway), his first career [three-peat](345955.md), as well as being three days removed from the [2006 Pocono 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Pocono_500), Hamlin's first career win.[^cite_note-PoconoRecap-188]

[Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won the pole at [Coronado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Anduril_250).[^cite_note-189] [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won stage one while [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) won stage two. After teammate [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) spun with 4 laps to go, [Corey Heim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corey_Heim) scored his first career win in just his 13th start after battling with Reddick.[^cite_note-CoronadoRecap-190]

[Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) won the pole at [Sonoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Toyota/Save_Mart_350).[^cite_note-191] Gibbs swept both stages, [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won the race after holding off [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe).[^cite_note-SonomaRecap-192] For the first time all season, the points leader changed as [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) passed [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) to take over the points lead.[^cite_note-193]



[Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) celebrating his [eero 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_eero_400) win at [Chicagoland Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicagoland_Speedway).

[Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole at the series return to [Chicagoland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_eero_400) for the first time since [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Monster_Energy_NASCAR_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-194] [William Byron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Byron_\(racing_driver\)) won stages one and two. After overtaking Byron during the final pit cycles, [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) won the race, also holding off teammates [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) and Hamlin.[^cite_note-ChicagolandRecap-195][^cite_note-196]

[Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the pole at [Atlanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Quaker_State_400).[^cite_note-197] The race was [red-flagged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Red_flag) on lap 108 due to lightning and resumed after three hours. Blaney swept both stages, and on lap 255, a caution happened, causing [overtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green%E2%80%93white%E2%80%93checkered_finish). Blaney won the race.[^cite_note-198] Blaney also broke a record for the second-most laps led on a drafting track at 171, the most since [Richard Petty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Petty) during the [1964 Daytona 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964_Daytona_500). Both [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) and [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) were locked into [The Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Chase).[^cite_note-199]

[North Wilkesboro Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Wilkesboro_Speedway) returned to the schedule as a points paying race for the first time since [1996](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996_NASCAR_Winston_Cup_Series).[^cite_note-200] Rain at [the track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Window_World_450) canceled qualifying, with [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) winning the pole.[^cite_note-201] [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) won stage one, passing [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) on the last lap. [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) won stage two. Logano dominated stage three, holding off [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) for his first win since the [2025 Würth 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_W%C3%BCrth_400).[^cite_note-202] Blaney was locked into [The Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_Chase).[^cite_note-203]

[Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar) won the pole at [Indianapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Brickyard_400).[^cite_note-204] [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) won the first stage, and [Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain) won the second stage. [Corey Heim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corey_Heim) held off [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) and [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) to win the race. Heim made history with this win, becoming the first part-time car to win a [crown jewel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Jewel_\(NASCAR\)) race since [Trevor Bayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trevor_Bayne) won the [2011 Daytona 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Daytona_500).[^cite_note-205] Heim also became the youngest driver to win the [Brickyard 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brickyard_400) since [Jeff Gordon](18600829.md) in the [1994 Brickyard 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Brickyard_400), as well as tying [A. J. Foyt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._J._Foyt) with his second win in fifthteen starts.[^cite_note-206]

[Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the pole at [Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iowa_Corn_350).[^cite_note-207] Blaney won stage one, while [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won stage two. [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs) won the race and his second career win.[^cite_note-208]

[Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won the pole at [Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Cook_Out_400_\(Richmond\)).[^cite_note-209] Blaney won stage one. Blaney's [Team Penske](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASCAR_operations_of_Team_Penske) teammate [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) won stage two. Following the last cycle of pit stops, Logano was able to hold off [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) for his second win of the season, his first win at [Richmond Raceway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Raceway) since the [2017 Toyota Owners 400](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Toyota_Owners_400).[^cite_note-210]

NASCAR moved [New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Dollar_Tree_301)'s start time up an hour to 2 p.m. due to the threat of evening rain.[^cite_note-LoudonMovedUp-134] [Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) won the pole.[^cite_note-211] The race began on wet-weather tires until a competition caution came out on lap 40 to replace them. [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won stage one, and [defending race winner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Mobil_1_301) [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won stage two. Blaney dominated the final stage following an incident on pit road and held off [Bubba Wallace](35761776.md) for his second consecutive win at [New Hampshire Motor Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Motor_Speedway). Following this race, Hamlin clinched the No. 1 seed for the Chase.[^cite_note-212]

[Ross Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Chastain) won the pole at [Daytona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Coke_Zero_Sugar_400).[^cite_note-213] [Shane van Gisbergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_van_Gisbergen) won stage one, his first stage win on an oval after leader [Chris Buescher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Buescher) wrecked coming to the tri-oval. The defending [race winner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Coke_Zero_Sugar_400) [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won stage 2. After the yellow came out on the last lap, [Ryan Preece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Preece) was announced as the winner over runner-up [Zane Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zane_Smith). With the win, Preece secured his first career win and joined Bubble Wallace and Austin Cindric as the final three drivers to race into the Chase.[^cite_note-214]

### The Chase

[Tyler Reddick](41108702.md) won the pole at [Darlington](83307953.md) after the rest of qualifying was canceled due to lightning.[^cite_note-215] Reddick won stage one while [Christopher Bell](47313650.md) won stage two. After battling with [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) and holding off his teammate [Ty Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Gibbs), Bell won the race snapping his 33-race winless streak.[^cite_note-216]

[Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) won the pole at [St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Enjoy_Illinois_300).[^cite_note-217] Logano won stage one while [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won shortened stage two. After the [double overtime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green%E2%80%93white%E2%80%93checkered_finish), Larson won the race snapping his 51-race winless streak.[^cite_note-218]

Rain at [Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bass_Pro_Shops_Night_Race) canceled qualifying, with [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) winning the pole.[^cite_note-219] [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) won stage one, while [Ryan Blaney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Blaney) won stage two. Logano won the race, and Larson took the points lead from [Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) by one point.[^cite_note-220] After the race, Blaney and [Carson Hocevar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carson_Hocevar) got into an altercation due to an on track incident that happened on lap 457.[^cite_note-221] No penalties were announced for either driver.[^cite_note-222]

Rain at [Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Hollywood_Casino_400) canceled qualifying, with [Joey Logano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_Logano) winning the pole.[^cite_note-223] [Kyle Larson](36163081.md) won stage one and two. In stage 3, Larson was battling with [Chase Briscoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Briscoe) for the lead, and Larson won the race with max points.[^cite_note-224]

[Denny Hamlin](2148196.md) won the pole at [Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_South_Point_400).
