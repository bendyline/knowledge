**Akobo County** is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County) in [South Sudan](32350676.md).

## Location

The county is located in [Jonglei State](767655.md), in the [Greater Upper Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Upper_Nile) region of [South Sudan](32350676.md), bordering the country of [Ethiopia](187749.md). The county headquarter is [Akobo](21081734.md), located approximately 450 kilometres (280 mi), northeast of [Juba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juba,_South_Sudan), South Sudan's capital and the largest city in the country.[^cite_note-1]

## Overview

The county is one of the eleven counties that constitute [Jonglei State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonglei). The county is prone to drought and the population is nomadic, sometimes migrating to other locations in search of water and pasture for their livestock. Due to the insecurity associated with military conflict between [Sudan](27421.md) and [South Sudan](32350676.md), some of the inhabitants of the county have sought refuge in neighboring [Ethiopia](187749.md). Some of those refugees are expected to return following the independence of South Sudan in July 2011.[^cite_note-2]

In 2010, a nutrition survey of young children was carried out by [Medair](https://www.medair.org/) and [Save the Children in Southern Sudan](https://savethechildren.org.uk/) (SCiSS). The study found high rates of acute malnutrition and poor child health indicators. These poor indicators included: high percentage of diarrhoea cases among the population, low measles immunity and low levels of vitamin A among the population. The rates of [Global Acute Malnutrition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Acute_Malnutrition) were above the [World Health Organization](33583.md) (WHO) crisis level but death rates were below [WHO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WHO) emergency levels. The researchers concluded that may be the feeding programme started in 2009 was enough to prevent some deaths, or that the more vulnerable died during the civil strife of 2009.[^cite_note-3]

## Population

According to the 2008 [South Sudanese](32350676.md) census, the population of Akobo County was estimated at 136,210.[^cite_note-4] Of these, 60,896 (44.7%) were female and 75,314 (55.3%) were male.
