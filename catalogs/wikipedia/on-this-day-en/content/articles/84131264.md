# 2027 U.S. Open Cup

| Tournament details |  |
| --- | --- |
| Country | United States |
| Dates | April 13 – December 15 |
| Teams | 96 |
|  |  |

The **2027 Lamar Hunt U.S. Open Cup** will be the 112th edition of the [U.S. Open Cup](253156.md), the knockout [domestic cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestic_cup) competition of [American soccer](579105.md), organized by the [United States Soccer Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Soccer_Federation). It will involve 96 teams, including teams from all three professional tiers of the [United States soccer league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_soccer_league_system) as well as amateur clubs. The competition will begin on April 13 and will culminate in the final on December 15.[^cite_note-CBS_Format-1]

The tournament will be the first since the [2023 U.S. Open Cup](71388667.md) to feature all eligible MLS teams.[^cite_note-Athletic_Return_to_Cup-2]
