**Cape Verdeans**, also called **Cabo Verdeans** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *cabo-verdiano*), are a people native to [Cape Verde](18962637.md), an [island nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_nation) in [West Africa](67393.md) consisting of an archipelago in the central [Atlantic Ocean](698.md).

## Ethnic groups

The [Cape Verde](18962637.md) [archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archipelago) was uninhabited when the [Portuguese](23033.md) landed there in 1456.[^cite_note-15] Africans from the main lands and Arabs from adjacent [West Africa](67393.md) were brought to the islands to work on Portuguese plantations. As a result, many Cape Verdeans have a multi-ethnic ancestry. The last time racial groups were counted in Cape Verde was in the 1950 census.[^cite_note-16]

Italian seamen who were granted land by the [Portuguese Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Empire), were followed by Portuguese settlers, exiles, and [Portuguese Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Jews) (*[lançados](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lan%C3%A7ados)*) who were victims of the [Inquisition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Inquisition). Many foreigners from other parts of the world settled in Cape Verde as their permanent country. Most of them were [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_people), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people), [British](19097669.md), [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_people), or the [English](18803164.md), as well as [Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab) and Jews (from [Lebanon](17771.md) and [Morocco](19291.md)).

The most important ethnic roots for Cape Verde based on historically known presence, cultural retention and ancestral connections are those of the [Mandinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandinka_people), [Wolof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolof_people), [Biafada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biafada_people), [Papel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papel_people) and [Bainouk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bainuk_people) ethnicities, while the remaining suspected ethnicities will show much variation in actual contributions to Cape Verde's ethnogenesis. But for each of them there is some kind of historical testimony to their presence in Cape Verde, which are the [Fula](102383.md), [Sereer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serer_people), [Diola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jola_people), Cassanga, [Basari/Tenda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bassari_people), [Balanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balanta_people), Bijagos, [Nalu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nalu_people), [Cocoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landuma_people), [Baga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baga_people), [Susu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susu_people), [Jallonké](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yalunka_people), [Bambara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambara_people) and [Sape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temne_people).

## Diaspora

Main article: [Cape Verdean diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_diaspora)

Prior to independence in 1975, many thousands of people emigrated from drought-stricken [Portuguese Cape Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Cape_Verde), formerly an [overseas province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_province) of Portugal. Because these people arrived using their Portuguese passports, they were registered as [Portuguese immigrants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_diaspora) by the authorities. Today, more Cape Verdeans live abroad than in Cape Verde itself, with significant emigrant[^cite_note-17] Cape Verdean communities in Brazil and in the United States (102,000 of Cape Verdeans descent in the U.S., with a major concentration on the [New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England) coast from [Providence, Rhode Island](19356538.md), to [New Bedford, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Bedford,_Massachusetts)).

In 2008, Portugal's National Statistics Institute estimated that there were 68,145 Cape Verdeans who legally resided in Portugal. This made up "15.7% of all foreign nationals living legally in the country."[^cite_note-asemana.cv-2]

## Languages

Cape Verde's official language is [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language), but [Cape Verdean Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Verdean_Creole) is more often used in everyday conversation.[^cite_note-18]

Cape Verdean Creole is used colloquially and is the mother tongue of virtually all Cape Verdeans.[^cite_note-19]

## Religion

More than 70% of the population of [Cape Verde](18962637.md) is nominally [Roman Catholic](606848.md), according to an informal poll taken by local churches.[^cite_note-report-21] About 5% of the population is Protestant.[^cite_note-22] The largest [Protestant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant) denomination is the [Church of the Nazarene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_the_Nazarene).[^cite_note-report-21]

Other religious groups include the [Seventh-day Adventist Church](28632.md), [the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Church_of_Jesus_Christ_of_Latter-day_Saints_in_Cape_Verde), the [Assemblies of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assemblies_of_God), the [Universal Church of the Kingdom of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Church_of_the_Kingdom_of_God), the [New Apostolic Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Apostolic_Church), and various other [Pentecostal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentecostal) and [evangelical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelicalism) groups.[^cite_note-report-21] There are also small [Baháʼí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bah%C3%A1%CA%BC%C3%AD_Faith) communities and a small [Muslim](6037917.md) community.[^cite_note-report-21] The number of [atheists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheists) is estimated at less than 1 percent of the population.[^cite_note-report-21]

## Culture

Main article: [Culture of Cape Verde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Cape_Verde)

The [culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture) of Cape Verde reflects its mixed West African ([Badiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badiu_people)) and Portuguese roots. It is well known for its diverse forms of music, such as *[Morna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morna_\(music\))*, and a wide variety of dances: the soft dance *Morna*, the *[Funaná](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funan%C3%A1)*, the extreme sensuality of *[coladeira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coladeira)*, and the *[Batuque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batuque_\(music\))* dance. These are reflective of the diverse origins of Cape Verde's residents.

## Genetics

A 2012 study showed the population is a mix between [Europeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europeans) and [Africans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_people), and with these ancestries being variable on the archipelago. The highest and lowest mean [West African](67393.md) ancestry levels are found in [Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago,_Cape_Verde) at 65% and [Fogo islands](1019365.md) at 53%. In the islands of [Santo Antão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santo_Ant%C3%A3o,_Cape_Verde), [São Vicente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Vicente,_Cape_Verde), and [São Nicolau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Nicolau,_Cape_Verde), they had a mean of 56%, and finally in [Boa Vista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boa_Vista,_Roraima) at 59%.[^cite_note-23] In 2023 it was shown that the admixture between [Iberians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberian_Peninsula) and [Senegambians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senegambians) began in the 15th–16th centuries, and similarly different islands had differing frequencies of respective ancestral affinities.[^cite_note-24]

A 2025 study on 143 Cape Verdeans revealed the following distribution for Y-DNA: [E1a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplogroup_E-M132) 3%, [E1b1a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E1b1a) 28%, [E1b1b](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E1b1b) 14%, [E3a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E3a) 1%, [L1a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplogroup_L-M20) 1%, L1b 1%, [I2a2a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplogroup_I2_\(Y-DNA\)) 1%, I2a2b 3%, [R1b](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplogroup_R1b) 44%, [G2a2b1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplogroup_G2) 1%, [J1a2a1a2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplogroup_J-M267) 2%, [J2b2a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplogroup_J-M172) 3%, and [T](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplogroup_T-M184) 1%. European Y-Chromosomes accounted for 48%, while African lineages were 45%, Middle Eastern 6% and Asian 2%.[^cite_note-25]
