**Blue Nile State** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ولاية النيل الأزرق *_Wilāyat an-Nīl al-ʾAzraq_*) is one of the 18 [states](579458.md) of the [Republic of the Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_the_Sudan). Established by Presidential Decree No. 3 in 1992, it is named after the [Blue Nile River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Nile_River). The region is host to around forty different ethnic groups. Its economic activity is based on agriculture and livestock as well as mineral exploitation. It borders [Ethiopia](187749.md) to the east, [South Sudan](32350676.md) to the west, and [Sennar](767672.md) to the north.

## History

In 2011, residents of Blue Nile were scheduled to hold ill-defined "popular consultations" to determine the constitutional future of the state, per the [Comprehensive Peace Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comprehensive_Peace_Agreement). Instead, a dispute over the rightful government of the state, and the determination of [Omar al-Bashir](393625.md) to eradicate the [Sudan People's Liberation Movement–North](30884032.md), led to [a renewed nine-year conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_conflict_in_South_Kordofan_and_Blue_Nile) between government forces and the [Sudan Revolutionary Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_Revolutionary_Front) as well as contributing to a refugee crisis.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The conflict finally came to an end in 2020 after a peace agreement was signed and the government to not discriminate based on ethnicity or religion.[^cite_note-dw-5][^cite_note-Atit-6]

[Further clashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932023_Blue_Nile_clashes) in the state broke out in 2022 between the [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people) people and [Funj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funj_people) and [Berta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berta_people) peoples over land disputes which led to the deaths of hundreds of civilians.[^cite_note-SudanTribune-7] On 5 August 2024, the RSF launched an incursion into the state and continues to control northwestern parts of the state.[^cite_note-8] On 5 March 2025, SAF launched a counteroffensive on the rest of RSF controlled territory in [Sennar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sennar), and took full control over territories, including [Mazmoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazmoum).&#x20;

In 2026, [conflict renewed](83036558.md) as the RSF and SPLM-N launched an offensive from the Ethiopian border, capturing Kurmuk and the southern areas of the state.

## Geography

Blue Nile state has an area of 45,844 km<sup>2</sup> and an estimated population of 1,193,293. The Central Bureau of Statistics quoted the population at 832,112 in the 2006 census. [Ad-Damazin](3956502.md) is the capital of the state. The state of Blue Nile is home to the [Roseires Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roseires_Dam), the main source of hydroelectric power in Sudan until the completion of the [Merowe Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merowe_Dam) in 2010.
