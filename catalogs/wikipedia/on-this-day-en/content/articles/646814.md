The **Northwest Region**, or **North-West Region** (French: *Région du Nord-Ouest*) is a [region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Cameroon) with [special status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_region) in [Cameroon](5447.md). Its capital is [Bamenda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamenda). The Northwest Region was part of the [Southern Cameroons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Cameroons), found in the western highlands of Cameroon. It is bordered to the southwest by the [Southwest Region](3028638.md), to the south by the [West Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Region_\(Cameroon\)), to the east by the [Adamawa Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adamawa_Region), and to the north by [Nigeria](21383.md). Various [Ambazonian](841514.md) nationalist and separatist factions regard the region as being distinct as a [polity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polity) from Cameroon.[^cite_note-3]

In 1919, the Northwest Region became solely administered by the [United Kingdom](31717.md).[^cite_note-5] In 1961, the region joined the [Cameroon](5447.md).[^cite_note-Emmanuel_Mbah_2016,_p._21-6] [Ambazonian](841514.md) separatists regard both the North-West and South-West regions as being constituent components of their envisaged breakaway state.[^cite_note-7]

## History

The origins of the region are linked to the settlement of the [Tikar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tikar_people) people who joined the [Bamoun Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Bamum), in the 1700s.[^cite_note-8] In 1884, the region was colonized by [Germany](11867.md) under the regime of the [protectorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protectorate) until 1916 when it became a [condominium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condominium_\(international_law\)) administered jointly by the [United Kingdom](31717.md) and the [France](5843419.md).[^cite_note-9] In 1919, the administration of the North West region, as part of [Southern Cameroons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Cameroons) became solely British. In 1961, the region joined [Cameroon](5447.md) as part of the federated state of [West Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Cameroon), under the [1961 British Cameroons referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1961_British_Cameroons_referendum).[^cite_note-Emmanuel_Mbah_2016,_p._21-6]

At the end of 2017, an [Ambazonian separatist](841514.md) movement in the two English-speaking regions of North West and [South West](3028638.md) began a wave of violence affecting soldiers, police, business leaders and workers. Separatist activists are trying in particular to prevent children from returning to school. Between 2016 and 2019, separatists reportedly ransacked, destroyed or burned down more than 174 schools.[^cite_note-10]

## Administration



Divisions of Northwest Cameroon

The Northwest Region (known before 2008 as the Northwest Province) is the third most populated province in Cameroon. It has one major metropolitan city, [Bamenda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamenda), with several other smaller towns such as [Wum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wum), [Kumbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumbo), [Mbengwi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbengwi), [Ndop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ndop,_Cameroon), [Nkambé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nkamb%C3%A9), [Batibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batibo), [Bambui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambui), [Bafut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bafut,_Cameroon) and [Oshie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oshie,_Cameroon). The province saw an increase in its population from approximately 1.2 million in 1987 to 1.8 million in 2010.[^cite_note-11] The population density of 99.12 people per square kilometer is higher than the national average of 22.6. The provincial urban growth rate is 7.95%, higher than the national average of 5.6%, while the rural growth rate, at 1.16%, is equal to the national rate. In 2001, according to the Statistical Provincial Services of the North-West Province, the population of the province is young, with over 62% of its residents being less than 20 years old. Therefore, the dependency rate in the province is high, particularly in the rural areas.

Like other regions in Cameroon, the Northwest Region is made up of administrative divisions. The province was created in 1972 with five divisions or [departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Cameroon): [Bui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bui_\(Cameroon_department\)), [Donga-Mantung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donga-Mantung), [Menchum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menchum), [Mezam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mezam), and [Momo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Momo_\(department\)). Today, it has seven divisions, the additions being [Boyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyo_\(department\)), which was carved out of the Menchum division, and [Ngo-Ketunjia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngo-Ketunjia) or Ngoketunjia, split off from the Mezam division. Each division is further subdivided, with thirty-one total subdivisions in the Northwest Province. The basic unit of local government is the council, and there are thirty-two councils in the region.

### Politics

The Northwest is a stronghold of the [Social Democratic Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Front_\(Cameroon\)) (SDF) which is one of the main opposition parties of Cameroon. Some Northwesterners feel completely marginalized by the government. There is also a secessionist movement, the SCNC ([Southern Cameroons National Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Cameroons_National_Council)) whose goal is to secede from Cameroon and form a republic consisting of the English-speaking regions. Much of the SCNC's influence exists in the Northwest. In 2008, the President of the Republic of Cameroon, [Paul Biya](392319.md), signed decrees abolishing "provinces" and replacing them with "regions". The Northwest Province subsequently became the Northwest Region.

### Special status

As part of the [Major National Dialogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_National_Dialogue), the region was given a "special status" in December 2019, granting additional rights and responsibilities in relation to economic, health, social, educational, sports and cultural development.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] Under the special status the region has a bicameral Regional Assembly, made up of a 20-member House of Chiefs composed of traditional leaders and a 70-member House of Divisional Representatives nominated by municipal councils. The Regional Assembly appoints a Regional Executive Council.[^cite_note-\:0-14] Additional powers over health and education were also granted to municipalities.[^cite_note-15]

#### Regional Executive Council

The Regional Executive Council is led by the president of the Regional Assembly and includes a vice president, three commissioners, two secretaries and a questor.[^cite_note-\:0-14][^cite_note-16]

The Northwest Region has many ethnic groups, including immigrants from other regions and countries. Nigeria is well represented, as it borders the region to both the north and the northwest. The native population comprises a variety of ethnic and linguistic groups. The main ethnic groups are of Tikar origin: [Tikari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tikar_language), [Widikum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Widikum_people), [Fulani](102383.md), and [Moghamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batibo). The most widely spoken languages in the province include [Mungaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mungaka), [Limbum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limbum), Yamba, spoken by the Yamba people also of the Donga Mantung Division; Bafmen, [Oku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oku,_Cameroon), [Lamnso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamnso), Ngemba, [Pidgin English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroonian_Pidgin_English), [Balikumbat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balikumbat), Papiakum, [Moghamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta'_language), and [Nkom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kom_language_\(Cameroon\)). During the colonial period, administrative boundaries were created which cut across ethnic groups and cultures. As a result, parts of some ethnic groups now lie in different divisions and provinces, which is believed to have led to several land conflicts.

In the provinces, the social organization recognizes a chief as its head, also called the *Fon*. The Fons, who in their tribal area may be more influential than the official administrative authorities, are considered the living representative of the tribal ancestors.

## Natural attractions



Rivers of the NW Region

The Northwest region has unique attractions, including the second highest mountain in [West Africa](67393.md), [Mount Oku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Oku). It is home to birds such as the [Bannerman's turaco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bannerman's_turaco), which is unique to this region. There are also many crater lakes such as [Lake Oku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Oku), [Lake Awing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Awing?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Lake Nyos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Nyos). The largest remaining mountain forest in the Northwest Region is the [Kilum-Ijim Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilum-Ijim_Forest). [Menchum Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menchum_Falls), and [Abbi Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbi_Falls?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the [Mbengwi Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbengwi_Division?action=edit\&redlink=1), are also located here.
