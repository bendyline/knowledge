The **Ghana women's national football team** represents [Ghana](12067.md) in international [women's association football](191466.md) and is governed by the [Ghana Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghana_Football_Association). Its players are known as the **Black Queens**.[^cite_note-\:0-4]

## Grounds



[Kumasi Sports Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumasi_Sports_Stadium)



[Aliu Mahama Sports Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliu_Mahama_Sports_Stadium)

World Cup and Africa Cup of Nations qualifying matches have been played at the [Essipong Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essipong_Stadium) in [Sekondi-Takoradi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sekondi-Takoradi), [Kumasi Sports Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumasi_Sports_Stadium) in [Kumasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumasi), the [Cape Coast Sports Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Coast_Sports_Stadium) in [Cape Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Coast), the [Accra Sports Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accra_Sports_Stadium) in the [Accra](56824.md) and the [Tamale Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamale_Stadium) in [Tamale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamale,_Ghana).[^cite_note-5]

The Black Queen's training facilities and [training grounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Training_ground_\(association_football\)) are Ghanaman Soccer Centre of Excellence also known as the National camp site or the GFA Technical Centre (GSCE) located in Prampram.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

## Rivalry

The Black Queens have a rivalry with the Super Falcons, the [Nigeria women's national football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria_women's_national_football_team) dating to when they played their first international match.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]
