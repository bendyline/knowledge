**Nœux-les-Mines** (French pronunciation: [\[nø le min\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/4/4e/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-Lyoko%C3%AF-N%C5%93ux-les-Mines.wav/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-Lyoko%C3%AF-N%C5%93ux-les-Mines.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/4/4e/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-Lyoko%C3%AF-N%C5%93ux-les-Mines.wav/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-Lyoko%C3%AF-N%C5%93ux-les-Mines.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) in the [Pas-de-Calais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pas-de-Calais) [department](38523.md) in the [Hauts-de-France](45093681.md) region of [France](5843419.md).[^cite_note-3]

## Geography

Nœux-les-Mines is situated some 4 miles (6.4 km) south of [Béthune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A9thune) and 25 miles (40 km) southwest of [Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille), at the junction of the D937 and D65 roads. As the name of the town itself might imply, it was established as a [coal mining](208413.md) centre, however with the decline of the mining industry in the area Nœux-les-Mines has evolved to become a light industrial and farming town.

Nœux-les-Mines was first recorded in the 4th century as *Vitri*. It was destroyed in 882 by the [Normans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normans), before being rebuilt in the 10th century, when it became known as *Noewe*.

It was part of the small province of Gohelle, itself part of the province of [Artois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artois).
