The **Ramallah and al-Bireh Governorate** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): محافظة رام الله والبيرة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Muḥāfaẓat Rām Allāh wa l Bīra*) is one of 16 [governorates of Palestine](5811262.md). It covers a large part of the central [West Bank](33209.md), on the northern border of the [Jerusalem Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem_Governorate). Its district capital or *muhfaza* (seat) is the city of [al-Bireh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Bireh).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

According to the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics) (PCBS), the district had a population of 279,730 in 2007.[^cite_note-pcbs-4] [Laila Ghannam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laila_Ghannam?action=edit\&redlink=1) serves as governor, the first woman in this role.[^cite_note-5]

## Localities

According to PCBS, the governorate has 78 localities, including [refugee camps](438046.md), in its jurisdiction. 13 localities have the status of municipality.
