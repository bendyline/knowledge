The South Sudan **Ministry of Health** is a national ministry in the current [Revitalised Transitional Government of National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revitalised_Transitional_Government_of_National_Unity) (RTGoNU) in the Republic of [South Sudan](32350676.md).

This national ministry in the current [Government of South Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_South_Sudan) was formed immediately after the country gained her Independence in 2011. The minister between 2022 and 2025 was [Yolanda Wel Deng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yolanda_Wel_Deng)[^cite_note-1]
