**Ivan Mykhailovych Vyhivskyi** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Іван Михайлович Вигівський; born 26 March 1980) is a Ukrainian police general serving as [Minister of Internal Affairs](1400321.md) since 16 July 2026. He was the chief of the [National Police of Ukraine](44656231.md) from 14 July 2023[^cite_note-2] (acting since 20 January 2023) until July 2026.[^cite_note-bio-3]

## Early life

Vyhivskyi was born on 20 March 1980 in [Zhytomyr Oblast](1567596.md), [Soviet Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_SSR). He graduated from the [National Academy of Internal Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Internal_Affairs) in 2003. From 1999 to 2016 he served on different positions in police in the city of [Ochakiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ochakiv).[^cite_note-Vyhivskyi_Kyiv_police-4]

## Career

From 11 August 2021 he was the chief of police for [Kyiv](585629.md).[^cite_note-Vyhivskyi_Kyiv_police-4]

Vyhivskyi became the acting head of the National Police of Ukraine after the then-holder of the post, [Ihor Klymenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ihor_Klymenko), became the [minister of internal affairs](1400321.md) succeeding [Denys Monastyrsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denys_Monastyrsky) following his [death in a helicopter crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Brovary_helicopter_crash).[^cite_note-Vyhivskyi_acting-5][^cite_note-6]

On 16 July 2026, Vyhivskyi was appointed Minister of Internal Affairs as part of a broader cabinet reshuffle.[^cite_note-7]
