**Aaron Rai** (born 3 March 1995) is an English [professional golfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_golfer) who plays on the [PGA Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PGA_Tour) and the [European Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Tour).[^cite_note-pga-1] He has won one [major championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men's_major_golf_championships), the [2026 PGA Championship](81464362.md).

Rai achieved a [three-win promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_golfers_to_achieve_a_three-win_promotion_from_the_Challenge_Tour) on the [Challenge Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Challenge_Tour) in 2017 and gained his first win on the European Tour in 2018. He earned PGA Tour status through the [2021 Korn Ferry Tour Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Korn_Ferry_Tour_Finals_graduates) and began to compete primarily in the United States. Rai won his first PGA Tour title in 2024 at the [Wyndham Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyndham_Championship).

## Early life

Aaron Rai was born in [Wolverhampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverhampton), England, on 3 March 1995.[^cite_note-pga-1] His parents, Amrik Singh and Dalvir Shukla, are of Indian descent. His father was born in England to immigrants from India, and his mother emigrated to England from Kenya as a teenager.[^cite_note-Desiblitz-3][^cite_note-GM_Facts-4][^cite_note-Digest_Journey-5] His father worked as a community worker and was an amateur tennis player; his mother had several jobs, including as a mental-health nurse and an aerobics instructor.[^cite_note-Digest_Journey-5][^cite_note-GT_Journey-6] Rai grew up with three older siblings—two sisters and one brother—[^cite_note-GM_Facts-4] and attended [Wolverhampton Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverhampton_Grammar_School) for his secondary education.[^cite_note-GM_Facts-4]

Rai was introduced to golf at age four after accidentally being struck by his brother's hockey stick, prompting his mother to buy him safe plastic golf clubs as an alternative.[^cite_note-Desiblitz-3][^cite_note-GT_Journey-6] His early childhood hobbies included a passion for [Formula 1](10854.md) racing, frequently wearing [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari) apparel to junior golf competitions before fully committing to golf.[^cite_note-GM_Facts-4] He practiced his early schooling and golf game at the Three Hammers Golf Complex from age four and joined Patshull Park at age five.[^cite_note-GM_Facts-4][^cite_note-ExpressStar-7]

## Professional career

Rai turned professional in 2012. In 2014 and 2015 he played on the [PGA EuroPro Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PGA_EuroPro_Tour). He won the 2015 Glenfarclas Open after a playoff, finished 5th in the Order of Merit and was promoted to the [Challenge Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Challenge_Tour) for 2016.[^cite_note-8]

Rai was 18th in the 2016 [Challenge Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Challenge_Tour) Order of Merit and just missed out on a [European Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Tour) place. His best finish was joint runner-up in the [Le Vaudreuil Golf Challenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Vaudreuil_Golf_Challenge), a stroke behind the winner, [Alexander Björk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Bj%C3%B6rk).

In 2017, Rai got three wins on the [Challenge Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Challenge_Tour). In March he won the [Barclays Kenya Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barclays_Kenya_Open) by 3 strokes.[^cite_note-9] His Kenya-born mother embraced him on the final green, seconds after his final stroke: Rai said that it was her first visit to Kenya since she left in 1970. He had his second win in May, the [Andalucía Costa del Sol Match Play 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andaluc%C3%ADa_Costa_del_Sol_Match_Play_9), defeating Irishman [Gavin Moynihan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gavin_Moynihan) 2&1 in the 9-hole final. Rai's third success came in July in the [Le Vaudreuil Golf Challenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Vaudreuil_Golf_Challenge) where he won by 5 strokes. The third win gave him an immediate entry to the [European Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Tour). In May, at [Walton Heath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walton_Heath_Golf_Club), he led international section qualifying for the [U.S. Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_U.S._Open_\(golf\)) but failed to make the cut in his first major.[^cite_note-10]

In his first season on the [European Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Tour) Rai had four top-10 finishes and ended the 2018 season 58th in the Order of Merit. His highest finish was tied for 5th place in the [BMW International Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMW_International_Open) and he was 8th in the [Nedbank Golf Challenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nedbank_Golf_Challenge) in November.

Rai won the 2018 [Honma Hong Kong Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honma_Hong_Kong_Open), the first event of the [2019 European Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_European_Tour) season. He led by 6 strokes after three rounds but was pushed hard by [Matt Fitzpatrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Fitzpatrick), who finished with a final round of 64. Rai's lead had been reduced to one shot after 16 holes but Fitzpatrick bogeyed the 17th and, despite a bogey at the last hole, Rai won by one stroke.[^cite_note-11]

In September 2020, Rai held the 54-hole lead at the [Dubai Duty Free Irish Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_Duty_Free_Irish_Open), but was unable to convert this into a victory as [John Catlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Catlin_\(golfer\)) overtook him by two shots; ultimately finishing runner-up.[^cite_note-12] A week later, Rai defeated [Tommy Fleetwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Fleetwood) in a playoff to win the [Aberdeen Standard Investments Scottish Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen_Standard_Investments_Scottish_Open), a result that lifted him into the world top 100 for the first time.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

In August 2021, Rai entered the [Albertsons Boise Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albertsons_Boise_Open) as part of the [Korn Ferry Tour Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korn_Ferry_Tour_Finals), as a way of obtaining a [PGA Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PGA_Tour) card for the [2021–22 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_PGA_Tour). He held a one-shot lead on the final hole of the tournament, however a closing double-bogey saw him drop to a tie for second-place, one shot behind Greyson Sigg. The result was still good enough for Rai to secure his card for the following season.[^cite_note-15]

Rai finished 93rd in the [FedEx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FedEx_Cup) standings in 2022 which secured his playing rights for the following year.

In the third round of the [2023 Players Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Players_Championship), Rai scored a hole-in-one on [TPC Sawgrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TPC_Sawgrass)'s signature 17th island green.[^cite_note-16]

A successful 2024 season saw Rai claim his maiden victory on the PGA Tour at the [Wyndham Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyndham_Championship),[^cite_note-17] five other top-10 finishes as well as making 14 cuts in a row. He also qualified for the [Tour Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tour_Championship) and was the only golfer in the 30 man field to do so without playing a single signature event.

In November 2025, Rai defeated [Tommy Fleetwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Fleetwood) in a playoff to win the title at the [Abu Dhabi Golf Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Dhabi_Golf_Championship). Rai made a birdie on the first extra hole to seal his win.[^cite_note-18]

He won the [Par 3 Contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masters_Tournament_Par_3_Contest) at the [2026 Masters Tournament](82215987.md).[^cite_note-19]

On 17 May 2026, Rai won the [2026 PGA Championship](81464362.md) with a score of 271, 9 shots under par. His final round of 65 included holing a 69-foot putt for a birdie on the 17th green at [Aronimink Golf Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aronimink_Golf_Club).[^cite_note-20] Rai became the first golfer from England to win the PGA Championship since [Jim Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Barnes_\(golfer\)) in [1919](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1919_PGA_Championship).[^cite_note-21] According to [ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN) research, Rai was the biggest long-shot to win a major in at least the past 20 years, surpassing [Phil Mickelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Mickelson) at the [2021 PGA Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_PGA_Championship) and [Louis Oosthuizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Oosthuizen) at the [2010 Open Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Open_Championship), who were both 200–1 to win.[^cite_note-22]

## Personal life

Rai lives in [Jacksonville, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacksonville,_Florida) and practices at nearby [TPC at Sawgrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TPC_at_Sawgrass).

He is married to Gaurika Bishnoi, a female professional golfer from India.[^cite_note-23]

### Equipment

Rai is noted for several unconventional equipment practices on the golf course. Unlike most golfers, who typically wear a single glove on their non-dominant hand, Rai wears two gloves, a habit he stated began at the age of eight. Rai is also the only player on tour to use iron covers, a practice he attributes to his father's emphasis on caring for equipment and appreciating its value from an early age. Rai is further noted for his use of castle tees and a seven-year-old [TaylorMade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TaylorMade) M6 driver that he used to win the [2026 PGA Championship](81464362.md).[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]
