**Iranshahr** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): ايرانشهر and [Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language): اݔرانشهر)[^cite_note-5] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Iranshahr_County\)) of [Iranshahr County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranshahr_County), [Sistan and Baluchestan province, Iran](448105.md), serving as capital of the province, the county, and the district.[^cite_note-Sistan_and_Baluchestan_Province_Structure-6] The city is near the borderlands between [Iran](14653.md) and [Pakistan](23235.md) where [Baloch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baloch_people) people live.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

Pahrah (present-day Iranshahr) is the site where [Alexander the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_the_Great) celebrated with and regrouped his troops after his [Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_subcontinent) conquests. [Bampur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bampur), where the ancient Bampur [fort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort) is located, is near the city.

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 99,496 in 17,698 households.[^cite_note-2006_Sistan_and_Baluchestan_Province-9] The following census in 2011 counted 97,012 people in 22,676 households.[^cite_note-2011_Sistan_and_Baluchestan_Province-10] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 113,750 people in 27,248 households.[^cite_note-2016_Sistan_and_Baluchestan_Province-2]

## Climate

Iranshahr has a [hot desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_desert_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BWh*) with extremely hot summers and mild winters. Precipitation is very low, and mostly falls in winter.
