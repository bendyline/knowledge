# Silver medal

"Silver Medalist" redirects here. For the 2009 Chinese film, see [Silver Medalist (film)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Medalist_\(film\)).

Not to be confused with [Silver Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Award_\(disambiguation\)), [Silver Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Star_\(disambiguation\)), [silver metal](27119.md), or [Silver Medallion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Medallion_\(disambiguation\)).



The [1896 Olympic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1896_Olympics) silver medal

A **silver medal**, in sports and other similar areas involving competition, is a [medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal) made of, or [plated with](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_plating), [silver](27119.md) awarded to the second-place finisher, or [runner-up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runner-up), of contests or competitions such as the [Olympic Games](22576.md), [Commonwealth Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Games), etc. The outright winner receives a [gold medal](526488.md) and the third place a [bronze medal](526648.md). More generally, silver is traditionally a metal sometimes used for all types of high-quality medals, including artistic ones.

## Sports

### Olympic Games

Main article: [Olympic medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_medal)

During the first Olympic event in [1896](48644.md), number one achievers or winners' medals were in fact made of [silver](27119.md) metal. The custom of [gold](12240.md)-silver-[bronze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze) for the first three places dates from the [1904 games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1904_Summer_Olympics) and has been copied for many other sporting events. [Minting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mint_\(coin\)) the medals is the responsibility of the host city. From [1928](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1928_Summer_Olympics) to [1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_Summer_Olympics) the design was always the same: the obverse showed a generic design by [Florentine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence) artist [Giuseppe Cassioli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Cassioli) with text giving the host city; the reverse showed another generic design of an Olympic champion. From [1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Summer_Olympics)–[2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Summer_Olympics), Cassioli's design (or a slight reworking) remained on the obverse with a custom design by the host city on the reverse. Noting that Cassioli's design showed a [Roman amphitheatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_amphitheatre) for what was originally a Greek games, a new obverse design was commissioned for the [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Summer_Olympics) Games. [Winter Olympics](34069.md) medals have been of more varied design.

### The Open Championship

In [The Open Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Open_Championship) golf tournament, the Silver Medal is an award presented to the lowest scoring [amateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amateurism) player at the tournament.

### Rejection of silver medals

See also: [Bronze medal § Psychological study](526648.md#Psychological_study)

In many sports with an elimination tournament, including those with a [third place playoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_place_playoff) (such as Olympic ice hockey, Olympic soccer, [FIFA World Cup](11370.md)), silver is the only medal given to a team that loses its final game, whereas gold and bronze are earned by teams winning their final matches.[^cite_note-sb-larocque-1] Notable athletes such as [Jocelyne Larocque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jocelyne_Larocque) (2018 Olympics) removed their runners-up/silver medals right after receiving them; Larocque was later ordered by the [International Ice Hockey Federation](16760449.md) official to put her silver medal back on.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-sb-larocque-1]

## Military and government

Some countries present military and civilian decorations known as Silver Medals. These include:

- [Austria](26964606.md)′s [Silver Medal for Services to the Republic of Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Medal_for_Services_to_the_Republic_of_Austria)

- [Italy](14532.md)′s [Silver Medal of Military Valor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Medal_of_Military_Valor)

- [South Africa](17416221.md)′s [Silver Medal for Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Medal_for_Merit)

- The [Civil Air Patrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Air_Patrol)′s [Silver Medal of Valor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Medal_of_Valor) in the [United States](3434750.md).

## Other awards

The [Zoological Society of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoological_Society_of_London) awards a [Silver Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Medal_\(Zoological_Society_of_London\)) "to a Fellow of the Society or any other person for contributions to the understanding and appreciation of zoology, including such activities as public education in natural history, and wildlife conservation."

The [Royal Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Engineering) awards a Silver Medal "for an outstanding and demonstrated personal contribution to UK engineering, which results in successful market exploitation, by an engineer with less than 22 years in full-time employment or equivalent."
