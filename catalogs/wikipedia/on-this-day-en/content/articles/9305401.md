The **Singapore Grand Prix**[^cite_note-1] is a motor racing event which forms part of the [Formula One World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_World_Championship). The event takes place on the [Marina Bay Street Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Bay_Street_Circuit) and was the inaugural night race and first street circuit in [Asia](689.md) designed for Formula One races.[^cite_note-Singtel-2][^cite_note-News_-_Singapore_confirms_2008_night_race-3][^cite_note-News_-_FIA_green_light_Singapore_night_race-4]&#x20;

The original Singapore Grand Prix was held at [Thomson Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomson_Road,_Singapore) from 1966 to 1973, before returning to the calendar in 2008 at [Marina Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Bay,_Singapore). [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) won the inaugural Formula One edition of the renewed Grand Prix, driving for the [Renault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_in_Formula_One) team amid controversial circumstances, when it emerged a year later that his teammate [Nelson Piquet Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_Piquet_Jr.) had been ordered to [crash on purpose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_Formula_One_crash_controversy) by senior team management to bring out the [safety car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safety_car) at a time chosen to benefit Alonso. The race was the 800th Formula One World Championship race since its inception in 1950, and the first ever Formula One race held at night.&#x20;

Until the [2024 race](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Singapore_Grand_Prix), Marina Bay had featured at least one safety car in every race edition, with a total of 24 safety car deployments.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] The Singapore Grand Prix has been considered to be one of the most challenging and unique tracks on the Formula One calendar. [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel) has the most wins on the track, with five victories. In 2022, a contract extension was announced to put the race on the Formula One calendar until at least 2028.[^cite_note-8]&#x20;

## History

### Formula Libre

First organised in 1961, the race was initially known as the *Orient Year Grand Prix*, located at the [Thomson Road Grand Prix circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomson_Road_Grand_Prix_circuit).[^cite_note-snakesdevils-9]&#x20;

It was renamed to the Singapore Grand Prix in 1966, shortly after Singapore became a sovereign country in 1965. The event was discontinued after 1973 and a variety of reasons have been suggested, including an increase in traffic, the very high danger and unsuitability of the track for racing, the inconvenience of having to close roads for the event and fatal accidents during the 1972 and 1973 races.[^cite_note-10]&#x20;

## Formula One



Laser show of Formula One vehicles on the [Marina Bay Sands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Bay_Sands)

Announced in 2008, an agreement for a five-year deal was signed by Singapore GP Pte Ltd, the [Singapore Tourism Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Tourism_Board) and [Bernie Ecclestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernie_Ecclestone).[^cite_note-News_-_Singapore_confirms_2008_night_race-3] In November 2007, it was announced that the [telecommunications](33094374.md) company [Singtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singtel) would be the title sponsor of the event. The official name of the event became the Formula 1 SingTel Singapore Grand Prix.[^cite_note-Singtel-2] The race was co-funded by the [Government of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Singapore), footing 60% of the total bill, or S\$90 million, out of a total tab of S\$150 million.[^cite_note-11]&#x20;

Around 110,000 tickets were made available for the country's first Formula One race. Corporate hospitality suites and packages went on sale at the end November 2007, three-day passes to the public went on sale in February 2008. Single-day passes went on sale a month later.[^cite_note-Introduction_to_Singapore_Grand_Prix-12] The event went on to achieve a full sell-out for all of its tickets.[^cite_note-13] The 2008 race hosted the Amber Lounge after-party and in 2010, Singapore became the second location to host the Amber Lounge Fashion show.&#x20;

### 2008

Main article: [2008 Singapore Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Singapore_Grand_Prix)

The first race held at the new [Marina Bay Street Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Bay_Street_Circuit) was the 15th round of the 2008 [FIA](248019.md) [Formula One](10854.md) World Championship, and was also the first night-time event in [Formula One](10854.md) history.[^cite_note-News_-_FIA_green_light_Singapore_night_race-4] The timing of the night event also meant that it could be broadcast live at a convenient time for TV audiences outside of the Asia-Pacific.[^cite_note-News_-_Singapore_confirms_2008_night_race-3] The track was also illuminated by a series of projectors which adapt their output to match the shape of the course.[^cite_note-14] On track, [Felipe Massa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felipe_Massa) was the first man to sit on the Singapore pole in his Ferrari, and dominated the race until he was released early at a pit stop, breaking his fuel rig and dropping him to last place. The race was won by [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) driving for the [Renault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_in_Formula_One) team, though that result has since been tarnished by [controversy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_Formula_One_crash_controversy). Renault were found to have ordered [Nelson Piquet Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_Piquet_Jr.) to crash, as the ensuing safety car would strongly benefit Alonso.&#x20;

- Race winner: [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) ([Renault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_in_Formula_One)) – 1\:57\:16.304 (61 laps)

- Fastest lap: [Kimi Räikkönen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimi_R%C3%A4ikk%C3%B6nen) ([Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari)) – 1\:45.599 (172.740 km/h)

### 2009

### 2012

Main article: [2012 Singapore Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Singapore_Grand_Prix)

On 22 September 2012, the AP reported that Bernie Ecclestone and the Singapore Grand Prix had agreed that the Grand Prix would remain on the Formula One calendar until 2017.[^cite_note-16] [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) claimed his fifth pole of the season for [McLaren](20994.md) and his second in Singapore. A surprise visitor to the front row alongside him was the [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Grand_Prix_Engineering) of [Pastor Maldonado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pastor_Maldonado), but it was [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel)'s Red Bull that inherited the race lead when Hamilton pulled out with gearbox problems. Vettel then dedicated his 23rd Grand Prix win to [Sid Watkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sid_Watkins), who had died shortly before the race.&#x20;

For the 2013 race, it was announced that the 10th turn of the track, the "Singapore Sling" chicane, would be reconfigured so the cars would have to navigate a flowing left-hander before accelerating towards the Anderson Bridge.[^cite_note-17]&#x20;

### 2014

Main article: [2014 Singapore Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Singapore_Grand_Prix)

On 15 April 2014, it was announced that [Singapore Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Airlines) would sponsor the Singapore Grand Prix, starting from that year.[^cite_note-18]&#x20;

F1's new-generation turbo-charged, hybrid-powered cars brought a new look to the track, ending the reign of the 2.4-litre power unit, but a controversial ban on radio communications dominated pre-race proceedings. [Mercedes](25098794.md)'s [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) sailed to his seventh win of the year, becoming Singapore's third multiple winner in the process as teammate [Nico Rosberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_Rosberg) retired with technical problems. [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel), bound for [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) in 2015, finished second ahead of new [Red Bull](1173193.md) partner [Daniel Ricciardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ricciardo).&#x20;

### 2015

Main article: [2015 Singapore Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Singapore_Grand_Prix)

[Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) arrived in Singapore one pole position shy of his idol [Ayrton Senna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayrton_Senna)'s record run of eight in a row. Another win would also put Hamilton on 41 – the same number as Senna. [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel) took his first Ferrari pole, then made it four wins at Marina Bay as Hamilton retired with a loss of electrical power just after half-distance. [Daniel Ricciardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ricciardo) split the two [Ferraris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) in second place, while [Formula One](10854.md) rookie [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) shouted 'No!' when told to move over for [Toro Rosso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Toro_Rosso) teammate [Carlos Sainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.).&#x20;

During the [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Singapore_Grand_Prix) race a spectator entered the track on the straight after Anderson Bridge, as leaders [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel) and [Daniel Ricciardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ricciardo) sped by. He climbed over the fencing when the safety car was deployed. Eventual race winner Vettel described the man as 'crazy' in his post-race interview.[^cite_note-19] The 27-year-old man was arrested by Singapore police after the incident. CCTV footage showed he had sneaked through a gap in the fencing.[^cite_note-20]&#x20;

- Race winner: [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel) ([Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari)) – 2\:01\:22.118 (61 laps)

### 2017

Main article: [2017 Singapore Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Singapore_Grand_Prix)

On 19 June 2017, the FIA's World Motor Sport Council in Geneva released its provisional 2018 calendar, in which the Singapore Grand Prix was given a tentative date of 16 September. At the time, the race (as well as the Chinese Grand Prix in Shanghai) were subject to confirmation by the commercial rights holders.[^cite_note-21] On the eve of the 2017 race, the track and F1 announced an extension for the race through 2021.[^cite_note-22] This race was a critical event in the season as polesitter and title contender, Sebastian Vettel, was involved in a first corner collision with Ferrari teammate [Kimi Räikkönen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimi_R%C3%A4ikk%C3%B6nen) and Red Bull's [Max Verstappen](41758713.md), which led to the retirement of all three drivers. Vettel's title rival, Mercedes's [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton), took full advantage and won the race from 5th on the grid to extend his championship lead over Vettel from 3 points to 28.[^cite_note-23]&#x20;

### 2018

Main article: [2018 Singapore Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Singapore_Grand_Prix)

After a disappointing weekend in [Monza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Italian_Grand_Prix), [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) arrived in Singapore with high hopes of a victory. However, [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) took pole position, ahead of [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) and the Ferrari of [Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel). At the start, the two [Racing Point Force India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Point_Force_India) drivers, [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez) and [Esteban Ocon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Ocon) had an incident which resulted in Ocon retiring. Vettel overtook Verstappen right before the Safety Car was deployed. Vettel was the first of the front runners to pit, on lap 15. He was put on ultrasoft [tyres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_tyres), while most other people who pitted during that time went for the softs. He exited behind Pérez but overtook him a lap later. Verstappen pitted for soft tyres on lap 18, and exited just in front of Vettel. Vettel, being on the higher wear ultrasofts, did not attack in order to make it to the end. On lap 34, Pérez had a clash with [Sergey Sirotkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Sirotkin) which resulted in him getting a drive-through penalty. Sirotkin himself got a 5-second time penalty after forcing [Brendon Hartley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendon_Hartley) off the track.[^cite_note-24] Later on in the race, he was battling with [Romain Grosjean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Grosjean) as Hamilton and Verstappen approached to lap them. Instead of letting the leaders pass, Grosjean continued to attack Sirotkin, which allowed Verstappen to close the gap to Hamilton and nearly overtake him. The Frenchman was handed a 5-second penalty and 2 penalty points on his licence.[^cite_note-25] In the end Hamilton won, with Verstappen second and Vettel third,[^cite_note-26] increasing his championship lead over the German to 40 points.&#x20;

- [Pole position](630713.md): [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)) – 1\:36.015 (189.832 km/h)

- Race winner: [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)) – 1\:51\:11.611 (61 laps)

- Fastest lap: [Kevin Magnussen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Magnussen) ([Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team)) – 1\:41.905 (178.860 km/h)

### 2019

Main article: [2019 Singapore Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Singapore_Grand_Prix)

Coming off a second consecutive victory for Ferrari under rising star [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc), Ferrari was on course to continue their rise in form; after an early trip back to pit lane during FP1, Leclerc powered through to take pole position ahead of Lewis Hamilton and Sebastian Vettel. After making a pit stop, Leclerc lost the lead to his teammate Vettel and eventually Hamilton, who had gambled on an extended running time on the soft tyres and later came out behind both Ferrari drivers. For a brief moment, Antonio Giovinazzi led the race for the Alfa Romeo-Sauber team before being overtaken four laps later by Vettel. After Giovinazzi was passed by faster cars, three safety cars were called for the retirements of [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)), [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez) and [Kimi Räikkönen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimi_R%C3%A4ikk%C3%B6nen). Ultimately, the podium consisted of Sebastian Vettel, who took his last career race win, Charles Leclerc and Max Verstappen. This was Ferrari's first 1–2 finish since the [2017 Hungarian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Hungarian_Grand_Prix).&#x20;

It was the then fastest Singapore Grand Prix, as well as the first not to feature at least one [safety car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safety_car).[^cite_note-34]&#x20;

## Track description

Main article: [Marina Bay Street Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_Bay_Street_Circuit)

Although the track has seen some minor changes as seen above, the basic layout has in many aspects remained unchanged. It is a twisty circuit that is the slowest in Formula One running at normal race distance above 305 kilometres (190 mi), which combined with its features of proximity to walls leads to frequent safety cars, further extending the running to nearing the race time limit of two hours.[^cite_note-38]&#x20;

The race tests the limits for both drivers and cars. It has some 90-degree turns, but with a high degree of variability, featuring technical sections and quick direction changes. Overtaking is primarily done at the end of the first sector, where top speeds are at their highest. Runoff areas exist at the end of the long straights, but are short by Formula One standards. At most parts of the track, cars run very close to the walls. In general, victory is dependent on a drivers' experience and skills.[^cite_note-39]&#x20;

The Singapore Grand Prix has been frequently described by drivers as being the “toughest race of the [Formula 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_1) calendar”; the endurance of drivers is heavily strained due to various factors such as [high heat, high humidity, high amount of sweat and bodily fluid loss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Singapore#Climate), high number of corners (19 in total), little margin for error (due to the lack of run-off area within a street circuit) and race finishes always completing near to the 2-hour allocated time limit (with 4 races being time-limited, instead of completing the allotted laps).[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]&#x20;
