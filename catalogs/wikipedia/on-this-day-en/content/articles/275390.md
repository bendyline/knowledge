The **director-general of the British Broadcasting Corporation** is chief executive and (from 1994) editor-in-chief of the [BBC](19344654.md).

The post-holder was formerly appointed by the [Board of Governors of the BBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Governors_of_the_BBC) (for the period 1927 to 2007) and then the [BBC Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Trust) (from 2007 to 2017). Since 2017, the director-general has been appointed by the [BBC Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Board).

To date, 18 individuals have been appointed Director-General, plus an additional two who were appointed in an acting capacity only. The current director-general is [Matt Brittin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Brittin), who succeeded [Tim Davie](1925670.md) on 18 May 2026[^cite_note-BrittinDG-1] after a period from 3 April 2026 to 17 May 2026, during which [Rhodri Talfan Davies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodri_Talfan_Davies) was Acting Director-General.
