# List of diplomatic missions of Cuba

&#x20;This is a **list of diplomatic missions of the [Republic of Cuba](5042481.md)**, excluding honorary consulates. Cuba has an extensive global diplomatic presence and is the [Latin American](18524.md) country with the second highest number of diplomatic missions after [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_missions_of_Brazil).
