The **Trump family** is a wealthy American family. The best-known member is patriarch [Donald Trump](4848272.md), the 45th and current 47th [president of the United States](24113.md) (2017–2021, 2025–present).&#x20;

The Trumps are of [German](152735.md) descent.[^cite_note-1] They are active in business, entertainment, politics, and real estate. Other prominent members include Donald Trump's father [Fred Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Trump), and grandfather [Frederick Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Trump).

### Wives

#### Ivana Trump

Main article: [Ivana Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivana_Trump)

Ivana Marie Trump ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names) Zelníčková), the first wife of Donald Trump, was born on February 20, 1949, in [Zlín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zl%C3%ADn), [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia) (now [Czech Republic](5321.md)). She was a fashion model and businesswoman who became a naturalized U.S. citizen in 1988.[^cite_note-2] They were married from 1977 until 1990.[^cite_note-3] Ivana died in 2022 of [blunt impact injuries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blunt_trauma) to the [torso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torso) after falling down stairs at her home on the [Upper East Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_East_Side) of [Manhattan](45470.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

Ivana was a senior executive of [the Trump Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Trump_Organization) for seven years,[^cite_note-6] including executive vice president for interior design.[^cite_note-shnayerson-7][^cite_note-8] She led the interior design of [Trump Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_Tower) with its signature pink marble.[^cite_note-shnayerson-7] Ivana was appointed CEO[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] and president of the [Trump Castle Hotel and Casino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_Castle_Hotel_and_Casino) in [Atlantic City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_City,_New_Jersey) and later became the manager of the [Plaza Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaza_Hotel) in Manhattan.[^cite_note-11]

#### Marla Maples

Main article: [Marla Maples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marla_Maples)

Marla Ann Maples, the second wife of Donald Trump, was born on October 27, 1963, in [Cohutta, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohutta,_Georgia), U.S. She was an actress, television personality, model, singer and presenter. They married in December 1993, two months after the birth of their daughter [Tiffany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiffany_Trump), separated in 1997 and divorced in 1999.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

#### Melania Trump

Main article: [Melania Trump](1209075.md)

Melania Trump ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names) Knauss), the third and current wife of Donald Trump, was born on April 26, 1970, in [Novo Mesto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novo_Mesto), [Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia) (now [Slovenia](27338.md)). She had a lengthy modeling career and is the second foreign-born [first lady of the United States](10847.md), the first being [Louisa Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisa_Adams).[^cite_note-14] They were married in 2005. Melania became a naturalized U.S. citizen on July 28, 2006.[^cite_note-15] She did not immediately move into the [White House](33057.md) when her husband became president, but remained at Trump Tower with their son [Barron Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barron_Trump) until the end of the 2016–2017 school year.[^cite_note-16] Melania and her son moved to the White House on June 11, 2017.

#### First marriage

Main articles: [Donald Trump Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_Jr.), [Ivanka Trump](1861441.md), and [Eric Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Trump)

Donald Jr., Ivanka, and Eric are Trump's three eldest children, from his first marriage with [Ivana Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivana_Trump).[^cite_note-17]

Prior to the election, each of the siblings held the title of executive vice president at [the Trump Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Trump_Organization). During the campaign, they served as surrogates for their father on national news programs. Following Trump's election victory, all three were named to the [presidential transition team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_transition_team).[^cite_note-18]

Following the inauguration, Donald Jr. and Eric took charge of the family's real estate empire. Ivanka moved to Washington, D.C., with her husband [Jared Kushner](6191053.md), who was appointed to a [senior White House advisory position](29024072.md).[^cite_note-19]

## Ancestry



[Kallstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kallstadt), Germany, the Trump family's ancestral home

According to biographer [Gwenda Blair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwenda_Blair), the family descended from an itinerant lawyer, Hanns Drumpf, who settled in [Kallstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kallstadt), a village in the [Electoral Palatinate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_Palatinate) of the [Holy Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Roman_Empire), in 1608, and whose descendants changed their name from Drumpf to Trump during the [Thirty Years' War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirty_Years'_War) (1618–1648).[^cite_note-Blair-31] The last name Trump is on record in Kallstadt since the 18th century.[^cite_note-gedbas-32] Journalist Kate Connolly, visiting Kallstadt, found several variations in spelling of the surname in the village archives, including Drumb, Tromb, Tromp, Trum, Trumpff, and Dromb.[^cite_note-33]

Johannes Trump, born in the nearby village of [Bobenheim am Berg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobenheim_am_Berg) in 1789, had established himself by the early 1830s as a winegrower in Kallstadt, which had then become part of [Kingdom of Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Bavaria),[^cite_note-gedbas-32] where his grandson, [Friedrich Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Trump), the grandfather of Donald Trump, was born in 1869. Several of his descendants also were [vintners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vintners) in Kallstadt, one of many villages in the famous wine-growing region of the [Palatinate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palatinate_\(region\)) (*Pfalz*).[^cite_note-Blair-31] Johannes Trump's sister Charlotte Louisa married Johann Georg Heinz. They were grandparents of [Heinz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinz) company founder [Henry J. Heinz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_J._Heinz) (1844–1919).[^cite_note-34]

This German heritage was long concealed by Donald Trump's father, [Fred Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Trump), who had grown up in a mainly German-speaking environment until he was ten years old;[^cite_note-35] after [World War II](32927.md) and until the 1980s, he told people he was of Swedish ancestry.[^cite_note-36] Donald Trump repeated this version in *The Art of the Deal* (1987) but in 1999 said he is "proud" of his German heritage,[^cite_note-37] and served as [grand marshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_marshal) of the 1999 [German-American Steuben Parade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German-American_Steuben_Parade) in [New York City](645042.md).[^cite_note-auto-38][^cite_note-auto1-39]

The Trump family in Germany were [Lutheran](23371382.md).[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41] Donald Trump's parents attended [First Presbyterian Church in Jamaica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Presbyterian_Church_in_Jamaica), Queens, where Trump was [confirmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confirmation) in 1959.[^cite_note-42]

## Parents

### Fred Trump

Main article: [Fred Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Trump)



[Fred Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Trump) in 1986

Donald Trump's father, Fred Trump (1905–1999), born in New York, was a successful real estate developer in New York City.[^cite_note-dad-59][^cite_note-60] Using their inheritance, Fred Trump and his mother Elizabeth founded E. Trump & Son in 1927.[^cite_note-61] The company grew to build and manage single-family houses in [Queens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens), barracks and garden apartments for [U.S. Navy](20518076.md) personnel near major shipyards along the East Coast, and more than 27,000 apartments in New York City.[^cite_note-nyt-62] Trump was investigated by a [U.S. Senate](24909346.md) committee for [profiteering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Profiteering_\(business\)) in 1954,[^cite_note-unbelievable-63] and again by the [State of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_New_York) in 1966.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBlair2015213–216-64]

Donald Trump became the president of his father's real estate business in 1971, and renamed it the Trump Organization around 1973.[^cite_note-65] That year, Donald and his father were sued by the [U.S. Justice Department's Civil Rights Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Justice_Civil_Rights_Division) for violating the [Fair Housing Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_Housing_Act).[^cite_note-KranishOHarrowWP160123-66] In the mid-1970s, Donald received loans from his father exceeding \$14 million (later claimed by Donald to have been only \$1 million).[^cite_note-ref5-67] Donald served as the Trump Organization's chairman and president until assuming the office of [U.S. president](24113.md).[^cite_note-68]

### Mary Anne MacLeod Trump

Main article: [Mary Anne MacLeod Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Anne_MacLeod_Trump)

Further information: [MacLeod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacLeod)

Born as Mary Anne MacLeod (1912–2000) in [Tong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tong,_Lewis), a small village near [Stornoway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stornoway), in the [Western Isles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_Hebrides) of Scotland, she was a daughter of fisherman Malcolm MacLeod and Mary MacLeod (née Smith).[^cite_note-Pilon-69] At age 17, she immigrated to the United States with \$50 (equivalent to \$938 in 2025), and moved in with a sister before starting work as a [maid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maid) in New York.[^cite_note-Pilon-69][^cite_note-marymac2-70] Mary and Fred Trump met in New York and married in 1936, settling together in [Queens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens). Mary became a U.S. citizen in 1942.[^cite_note-Pilon-69][^cite_note-71] While visiting Scotland in June 2008, Donald Trump said in part, "I think I do feel Scottish."[^cite_note-auto-38][^cite_note-auto1-39]

## Grandparents

### Frederick Trump

Main article: [Frederick Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Trump)

In 1885, Donald Trump's grandfather, Friedrich Trump, emigrated from Kallstadt, Palatinate (then part of the [Kingdom of Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Bavaria)), to the United States at age 16. He [anglicized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglicized) his name to *Frederick* in 1892 when he became a U.S. citizen.[^cite_note-dad-59] During the [Klondike Gold Rush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klondike_Gold_Rush), he amassed a fortune by opening a restaurant and hotel in [Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bennett,_British_Columbia) and later [Whitehorse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitehorse,_Yukon), serving [gold seekers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prospecting#The_gold_rushes) on their way to the region; one biographer wrote that the business included a brothel, a portrayal Donald Trump has said was "totally false".[^cite_note-72]

Returning on a visit to Kallstadt in 1901, Frederick fell in love with Elisabeth Christ. They were married in 1902, and moved back to the US. She became homesick so they returned to Germany. However, the authorities blocked his attempts to settle there and he was exiled by Germany in 1905 for his lack of mandatory military service and not giving authorities notice before his 1885 departure; an appeal was denied.[^cite_note-73] He died in the first wave of the [Spanish flu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_flu) pandemic in 1918. After his death, his fortune was passed on to his wife and son.

### Elizabeth Christ Trump

Main article: [Elizabeth Christ Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Christ_Trump)

Donald Trump's grandmother, Elizabeth Christ Trump, was born in 1880 and died on June 6, 1966. She married Frederick Trump in 1902 and moved to the United States with him. Like her husband, she was a native of Kallstadt, born as the daughter of Philipp and Marie Christ. Philipp Christ was descended from Johannes Christ of [Flörsheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fl%C3%B6rsheim), [Hesse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hesse). Elizabeth Christ Trump was a descendant of organ builder [Johann Michael Hartung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Michael_Hartung) through her paternal grandmother Sabina Christ.[^cite_note-74]

## Other relatives

### John G. Trump

Main article: [John G. Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_G._Trump)

Donald Trump's paternal uncle, John George Trump (1907–1985), was an electrical engineer, inventor and physicist who developed rotational [radiation therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiation_therapy), and, together with [Robert J. Van de Graaff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_J._Van_de_Graaff), one of the first million-volt [X-ray generators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-ray_generator). He was awarded the [National Medal of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Medal_of_Science) by President [Ronald Reagan](25433.md) and was a member of the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering).

### John W. Walter

Main article: [John W. Walter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_W._Walter)

Donald Trump's first cousin John W. Walter (1934–2018) was a son of father Fred's sister Elizabeth Trump and William Walter.[^cite_note-obituary-93][^cite_note-drop-94] He worked for the Trump Organization for most of his life and was executive vice president of Trump Management, Inc.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTETrump2020193–94-95][^cite_note-obituary-93][^cite_note-96][^cite_note-97] He shared ownership of All County Building Supply & Maintenance Corp with Donald Trump, Maryanne Trump Barry, Elizabeth Trump Grau, and Robert Trump.[^cite_note-drop-94][^cite_note-98] Walter also served as the mayor of [Flower Hill, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flower_Hill,_New_York), between 1988 and 1996, and as its historian from 1996 until his death in 2018.[^cite_note-\:02-99][^cite_note-\:18-100]

### Mary L. Trump

She has been a vocal critic of Donald Trump, and wrote a book about him and the family titled *[Too Much and Never Enough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Too_Much_and_Never_Enough)* (2020).

### Fred Trump III

## Heraldry

The Trump family currently assumes usage of a differenced version of the [Joseph E. Davies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_E._Davies) armorial achievement. Granted to Davies by the [British College of Arms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Arms#Kings_of_Arms_for_England,_Wales_and_Northern_Ireland), in 1939. Davies was the third husband of [Marjorie Merriweather Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marjorie_Merriweather_Post) and he was the former U.S. ambassador to [United Kingdom](31717.md). Davies produced no male heirs and his eldest daughter, Eleanor Tydings, became the [heraldic heiress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heraldic_heiress). Subsequently through the [Anglosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglosphere) custom of [male primogeniture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Male_primogeniture), Tydings’ inherited arms passed to her eldest son, [Senator Joseph Tydings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Tydings), and then to his legitimate first-born male issue, as inheritors of the undifferenced arms, in perpetuity.

Donald Trump purchased the Florida estate [Mar-a-Lago](1452754.md) in 1985. The estate was built by Merriweather Post, who displayed her late husband’s arms prominently around the property. After his acquisition, Trump began an adopted usage of the Davies armorial achievement. These adopted arms are used at [Trump golf courses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_National_Golf_Club_\(disambiguation\)) and various other [Trump Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_Organization) properties across the United States. Trump then began an assumed usage of a differenced version of Davies arms, which were then also registered by Trump with the U.S. patent and trademark office.

In 2008, Trump attempted to establish the American logo at his new [Trump International Golf Links](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_International_Golf_Links,_Scotland) in [Balmedie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balmedie), Scotland, but was warned by the [Lord Lyon King of Arms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Lyon_King_of_Arms), the highest authority for [Scottish heraldry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_heraldry), that an act of the Scottish Parliament from 1672 disallows people using unregistered arms. In January 2012, shortly after the inauguration of the golf course, Trump unveiled the new coat of arms that had been granted to "The Trump International Golf Club Scotland Ltd" by the Lord Lyon in 2011.

|  | Granted2011 ArmigerTrump International Golf Links (2012–) CrestA demi-Lion rampant Gules, armed and langued Azure, holding in the paws a Pennon Or flowing to the sinister. EscutcheonParty per chevron: Azure two Mullets Argent; Vert a double headed Eagle of the second, wings displayed and inverted, armed and langued Gules, holding in its talons two Globes of the second; overall three chevronels Or. Motto"Numquam concedere" (Latin for "Never Give Up"). |
| --- | --- |

Sarah Malone, executive vice-president of "The Trump International Golf Links, Scotland", said that "the coat of arms brings together visual elements that signify different aspects of the Trump family heritage \[...], the Lion Rampant \[in the crest] makes reference to Scotland and the stars to America. Three chevronels are used to denote the sky, sand dunes and sea – the essential components of the site, and the double(-headed) eagle represents the dual nature and nationality of Trump's heritage, (Scottish and German). The Eagle clutches golf balls making reference to the great name of golf, and the motto *Numquam Concedere* is Latin for Never Give Up – Trump's philosophy."

From 2014, Trump used the same logo for the [Trump International Golf Links, Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_International_Golf_Links_and_Hotel_Ireland), the golf resort built from his acquisition of [Doonbeg](18340192.md) Golf Club.
