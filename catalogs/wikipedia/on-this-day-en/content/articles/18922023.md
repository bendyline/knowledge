**Bocoyna** is one of the 67 [municipalities of Chihuahua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Chihuahua), in northern [Mexico](3966054.md). The municipal seat lies at [Bocoyna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bocoyna). The municipality covers an area of 2,801.8 km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 28,766,[^cite_note-SEDESOL-1] down from 29,907 as of 2005.[^cite_note-2]

As of 2010, the town of Bocoyna had a population of 796.[^cite_note-SEDESOL-1] Other than the town of Bocoyna, the municipality had 633 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [San Juanito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Juanito,_Chihuahua?action=edit\&redlink=1) (10,535), classified as urban, and [Creel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creel,_Chihuahua) (5,026) and [Sisoguichi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sisoguichi,_Chihuahua?action=edit\&redlink=1) (1,097), classified as rural.[^cite_note-SEDESOL-1]

The municipality and its seat are located on the route of the [Chihuahua Pacific Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrocarril_Chihuahua_al_Pac%C3%ADfico).
