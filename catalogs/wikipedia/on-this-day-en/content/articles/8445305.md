**Sévaré** (or **Sevare**) is a town in central [Mali](19127.md).[^cite_note-1] Located in the [Mopti Region](1064867.md), it serves as a crossroads town of about 40,000 situated about 10 kilometres (6.2 mi) southeast of [Mopti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mopti) and 10 km (6.2 mi) south of [Fatoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatoma), the old capital of [Kunaari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunaari). From Sévaré one can access [Bandiagara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandiagara) to the east, [Gao](1255721.md) and [Timbuktu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbuktu) to the north, [Segou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segou) and [Bamako](56606.md) to the southwest and [Burkina Faso](3470.md) to the south.

[Mopti Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mopti_Airport) is near Sévaré, which lies within the boundaries of Mopti Commune.







Said to have been founded by a [Fula](102383.md) hunter, Se Waware, its importance began to grow with the construction of roads and the dike to Mopti under French colonial rule.
