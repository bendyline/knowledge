# Live broadcast

"Live on Air" redirects here. For the song by the Tangent, see [A Place on the Shelf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Place_on_the_Shelf).

A **live broadcasting**, also called a **live transmission**, generally refers to various types of [media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_\(communication\)) that are [broadcasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadcasting) without a significant delay.

The most common seen media example of the live transmission is a [news program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_program) or a [news broadcasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_broadcasting).

Other types of live broadcasts include:

- [Live radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_radio)

- [Live television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_television)

- [Internet television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_television)

- [Internet radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_radio)

- [Liveblogging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liveblogging)

- [Live streaming](18996908.md)
