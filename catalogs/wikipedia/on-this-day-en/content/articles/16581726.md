The **Grand Jury Prize** is an award given at the [Venice Film Festival](32497.md) to one of the feature films in competition slate since 1951. It is considered the second place award next to the main award, the [Golden Lion](2386128.md).

South Korean filmmaker [Lee Chang-dong](285217.md) was the most recent winner for *[Possible Love](82172199.md)* (2026), at the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md).

## History

Since 2016, the official name of the award has been simply the *Grand Jury Prize*. It has had several other names since its creation in 1951: *Special Jury Prize*, awarded from 1951 to 1982, and from 2006 to 2012, and *Grand Special Jury Prize*, awarded from 1983 to 2005, and from 2013 to 2015.

[Elia Kazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elia_Kazan) was the first winner for *[A Streetcar Named Desire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Streetcar_Named_Desire_\(1951_film\))* in [1951](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_Venice_International_Film_Festival). While Soviet-Georgian filmmaker [Otar Iosseliani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otar_Iosseliani) won three times, *[Favorites of the Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Favorites_of_the_Moon)* (1984), *[And Then There Was Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/And_Then_There_Was_Light)* (1989) and *[Brigands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigands_\(film\))* (1996).

## Winners



[Elia Kazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elia_Kazan) won for *[A Streetcar Named Desire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Streetcar_Named_Desire_\(1951_film\))* (1951)



[Louis Malle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Malle) won twice for *[The Lovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lovers_\(1958_film\))* (1958) and *[The Fire Within](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fire_Within)* (1963)



[Luchino Visconti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luchino_Visconti) won for *[Rocco and His Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocco_and_His_Brothers)* (1960)



[Jean-Luc Godard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Luc_Godard) won for *[La Chinoise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Chinoise)* (1967)

[Otar Iosseliani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otar_Iosseliani) won three times for *[Favorites of the Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Favorites_of_the_Moon)* (1984), *[And Then There Was Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/And_Then_There_Was_Light)* (1989) and *[Brigands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigands_\(film\))* (1996)



[Abbas Kiarostami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_Kiarostami) won for *[The Wind Will Carry Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wind_Will_Carry_Us)* (1999)



[Todd Haynes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Haynes) won for *[I'm Not There](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I'm_Not_There)* (2007)



[Tsai Ming-liang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsai_Ming-liang) won for *[Stray Dogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stray_Dogs_\(2013_film\))* (2013)



[Roman Polanski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Polanski) won for *[An Officer and a Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Officer_and_a_Spy_\(film\))* (2019)



[Ryusuke Hamaguchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryusuke_Hamaguchi) won for *[Evil Does Not Exist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evil_Does_Not_Exist)* (2023)



[Kaouther Ben Hania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaouther_Ben_Hania) won for *[The Voice of Hind Rajab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voice_of_Hind_Rajab)* (2025)
