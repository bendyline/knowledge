**Ipiales** is a city and Catholic bishopric in [Nariño Department](549702.md), southern [Colombia](5222.md), near the [border](47412571.md) with [Ecuador](9334.md). It is located at around [0°49′49″N 77°38′40″W  /  0.83028°N 77.64444°W  / 0.83028; -77.64444](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Ipiales\&params=0_49_49_N_77_38_40_W_), with an elevation of about 2950 m. Ipiales is located on the high plateau called "Tuquerres e Ipiales," and lies at a distance of 82 km from [Pasto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasto_\(Colombia\)), the department's capital. Ipiales is known as "la ciudad de las nubes verdes" (the city of the green clouds) because sometimes, especially in the afternoon, greenish clouds appear over the city. One of the area's favorite foods is the [guinea pig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinea_pig) or [cavy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caviidae) (*Cavia porcellus*), called *kuy* or *kuwi*.[^cite_note-kichwa-2] The economy of this city is based on [border trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_trade) between Ecuador and Colombia.

## Culture and religion

The municipality's main attraction is the impressive architecture of the [Las Lajas Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Lajas_Cathedral),[^cite_note-3] the episcopal see of the [Roman Catholic Diocese of Ipiales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Ipiales).

One of the biggest festivals is called the "Blacks and Whites Carnival". Through this event people from Ipiales celebrate racial diversity. People paint each other with make-up and a white soap, called *careoca*. Many tourists travel to see this event from all over the country.

## History

The [Rumichaca Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumichaca_Bridge), according to Spanish chroniclers, was the northernmost outpost of the [Inca Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inca_Empire), wrested from the Pasto people in the early 16th century. The bridge, crossing the [Carchi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carchi_River), is 3 kilometres (1.9 mi) from Ipiales on the border with Ecuador.[^cite_note-4]

### Climate

Ipiales features an unusually cool [subtropical highland climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical_highland_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Cfb*), with an average temperature around 15 °C or 27 °F cooler than its near equatorial climate might suggest, due to the city's high altitude of almost 3,000 metres (9,800 ft) above sea level, which makes it the highest major city in Colombia and one of the highest on Earth. The average temperature is cold for a subtropical highland climate with the city escaping an *ETH* ([alpine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_climate)) classification by less than 1.5 °C or 2.7 °F. Temperatures rarely break the 20 °C (68 °F) mark or fall below freezing, giving the city perpetual spring-like weather. Ipiales has a rainier season from October to May and a drier season from June through September. Rainfall is frequent though not heavy year round, falling on an average of 235 days for an annual total of approximately 925 millimetres or 36 inches.
