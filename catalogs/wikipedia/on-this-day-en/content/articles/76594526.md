**Cheng Ying-yao** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 鄭英耀; born 12 October 1955) is a Taiwanese academic administrator who has served as the [Minister of Education](11753451.md) since 2024.

## Early life and education

Cheng was raised in [Penghu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penghu).[^cite_note-1] After graduating from the [Pingtung Normal College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Pingtung_University), he worked as an elementary school teacher. He then earned a master's degree in education from [National Kaohsiung Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Kaohsiung_Normal_University) in 1985 and earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in education from [National Chengchi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Chengchi_University) in 1992.[^cite_note-eduappt-2][^cite_note-rtiappt-3][^cite_note-4] His doctoral dissertation was titled, "A study on elementary school teachers' creative thinking, critical thinking and related factors" (Chinese: 國小教師創造思考､批判思考及其相關因素之研究).[^cite_note-5]

## Academic career

Cheng began his career at [National Sun Yat-sen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Sun_Yat-sen_University) in 1985,[^cite_note-rtiappt-3] first as a professor and successively serving as the dean of students and social sciences before assuming the university presidency.[^cite_note-2004int-6][^cite_note-2014sl-7] During his presidential tenure, Cheng in 2019 proposed that NSYSU merge with [Kaohsiung Medical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaohsiung_Medical_University).[^cite_note-mergeryanglin-8][^cite_note-9] The following year, Cheng commented on, then penned an open letter about, the university's handling of [plagiarism allegations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Mei-jhen_thesis_controversy) against alumnae [Li Mei-jhen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Mei-jhen).[^cite_note-chungli-10][^cite_note-openletterli-11] In 2022, NSYSU became the first university in Taiwan to offer mental health leave to its students,[^cite_note-mental-12] and inaugurated the College of Semiconductor and Advanced Technology Research.[^cite_note-nsysusemiconductor-13]

## Political career

In the mid-2000s, Cheng headed the [Kaohsiung City Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaohsiung_City_Government)'s Bureau of Education.[^cite_note-huang2004-14][^cite_note-wang2007-15] He later served two terms as leader of the [Democratic Progressive Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Progressive_Party)-affiliated [New Frontier Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Frontier_Foundation?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-nffhead-16]

Cheng was named [Minister of Education](11753451.md) in [Cho Jung-tai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cho_Jung-tai)'s incoming cabinet on 12 April 2024.[^cite_note-eduappt-2] At the time of his appointment to the [Executive Yuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Yuan), Cheng held no party affiliation.[^cite_note-17]

On 8 January 2026, Cheng was sanctioned by the [Taiwan Affairs Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_Affairs_Office) of the People's Republic of China as a "[diehard "Taiwan independence" separatist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diehard_%22Taiwan_independence%22_separatists)" for "organising the compilation of ‘Taiwan independence’ textbooks, poisoning the minds of young people on the island and obstructing cross-strait educational exchanges and cooperation".[^cite_note-18]
