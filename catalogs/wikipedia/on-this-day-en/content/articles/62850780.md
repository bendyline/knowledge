The **Ministry of Defence** of the [Republic of Sudan](27421.md) is the [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) [ministry](739237.md) responsible for defence and the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md).

## History

After independence, [Prime Minister](1881046.md) [Abdallah Khalil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdallah_Khalil), secretary of the [National Umma Party](1184790.md), served as Minister of Defence.[^cite_note-3]

The [President of Sudan](450116.md) was responsible for appointing the Minister of Defence.

After the overthrow of General [Ibrahim Abboud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Abboud)'s regime in October 1964, Lieutenant General El Khawad Mohammed was appointed as a member of the ruling [Supreme Council of the Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Council_of_the_Armed_Forces) and commander-in-chief of the Sudanese Armed Forces.[^cite_note-4]

Then-Colonel [Jaafar Nimeiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaafar_Nimeiri) came to power in the [1969 Sudanese coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969_Sudanese_coup_d'%C3%A9tat). [Khalid Hassan Abbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalid_Hassan_Abbas) was appointed as Minister of Defense on 29 October 1969[^cite_note-5] following a cabinet reshuffle implemented to strengthen the army's control over the Sudanese government. Abbas was an anti-[Mahdist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ansar_\(Sudan\)) and non-[communist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_Communist_Party). As Defense Minister he, alongside Babiker, would push President Nimeiri to adopt a more aggressive response to the rising threat to the government posed by the [Ansar movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ansar_\(Sudan\)), resulting in the brutal crackdown seen on [Aba Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aba_Island) in 1970.[^cite_note-6] Abbas served as Defense Minister until 16 April 1972,[^cite_note-7] at which point Nimeiri took over the role.

U.S. personnel met Defence Minister General [Abdul Majid Hamid Khalil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Majid_Hamid_Khalil) (known in the Sudan as Abdul Majid) in 1979. Two days after the signing of the peace agreement between [Ahmed al-Mirghani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_al-Mirghani) and [John Garang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Garang) on 16 November 1988, a [Sudanese Air Force](550818.md) [Lockheed C-130 Hercules](7697.md) carrying Abdul Majid from [Wau](3888859.md) to Khartoum, together with the Army Commander-in-Chief, General [Fathi Ahmed Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fathi_Ahmed_Ali?action=edit\&redlink=1), was hit by a missile, knocking out one of its engines.[^cite_note-8] In January 1982, President Nimeiri again assumed the office himself after retiring Abdul Majid, who had been simultaneously [First Vice President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Sudan), Minister of Defence, commander-in-chief of the armed forces, and secretary-general of the single ruling [Sudanese Socialist Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_Socialist_Union) party.[^cite_note-auto-9]

Nimeiri had served himself as Minister of Defence for long stretches in 1972-73 (promoted himself General in 1973), 1975–76, and 1978-79 after retiring other ministers. From 1976-78, the Minister of Defence has usually held the rank of [General](148537.md), when [Bashir Mohamed Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bashir_Mohamed_Ali?action=edit\&redlink=1) held the position.[^cite_note-10]

Since the accession of the [Sovereignty Council of Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereignty_Council_of_Sudan), the effective commander-in-chief of the armed forces is Lieutenant General [Abdel Fattah al-Burhan](60480545.md), former head of the [Transitional Military Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Military_Council_\(2019\)).
