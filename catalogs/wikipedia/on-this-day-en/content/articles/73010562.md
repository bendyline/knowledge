**Mohammed Shahabuddin**[^cite_note-3] (born 10 December 1949), also known as **Chuppu**,[^cite_note-\:5-4] is a Bangladeshi politician, former jurist and journalist who served as the [president of Bangladesh](404877.md) from 2023 to 2026.[^cite_note-5] He was elected unopposed in the [2023 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Bangladeshi_presidential_election) as the nominee of the then-ruling [Awami League](404861.md).[^cite_note-bio-6] He is the first and only president of Bangladesh to administer the oath of three different governments. Prior to his presidency, Shahabuddin served as a district and sessions judge and a commissioner of the [Anti-Corruption Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Corruption_Commission_\(Bangladesh\)) from 2011 to 2016.[^cite_note-7]

Shahabuddin was involved in Awami League politics during his student years but never formally joined politics afterward. During the regime of [Sheikh Hasina](623439.md), he publicly expressed support for the Awami League on various occasions. Following [Hasina's resignation](77539154.md), Shahabuddin has since become the target of criticism and debate amongst several political parties, with some questioning his political neutrality as president.[^cite_note-\:3-8][^cite_note-\:4-9] On July 24, 2026 he stepped down citing ill health.[^cite_note-\:7-10]

## Early life and education

Shahabuddin was born on 10 December 1949 in the Jubilee Tank area in Shivrampur of [Pabna District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pabna_District) in erstwhile [East Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Bengal), [Dominion of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_Pakistan) (present-day [Pabna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pabna), Bangladesh).[^cite_note-11] His parents were Sharfuddin Ansari and Khairunnessa.[^cite_note-12]

Shahabuddin went to Pabna's Purbatan Gandhi School and later was admitted to Radhanagar Majumdar Academy at fourth grade where he passed [SSC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_School_Certificate) examination in 1966.[^cite_note-bio-6] He then passed [HSC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Secondary_School_Certificate) and [bachelor's](188874.md) from [Govt. Edward College, Pabna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Govt._Edward_College,_Pabna) in 1968 and 1972 respectively.[^cite_note-13] He then obtained master's in psychology from the [University of Rajshahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rajshahi) in 1974, and then [LLB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LLB) from Shahid Aminuddin Law College in 1975.[^cite_note-bio-6]

## Career



Shahabuddin (on the right) as a commissioner of [Anti-Corruption Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Corruption_Commission,_Bangladesh) along with the chairman [M Badiuzzaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M_Badiuzzaman) and other members submitting annual report of the commission to President [Abdul Hamid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Abdul_Hamid) in 2015.

### Political

Shahabuddin was a student leader of the [Awami League](404861.md) during the late 1960s and early 1970s. He was the general secretary of the [Pabna Edward College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pabna_Edward_College) unit of the [Chhatra League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chhatra_League), and president of the [Pabna District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pabna_District) Chhatra League and [Jubo League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jubo_League).[^cite_note-\:0-14] He was the Pabna District convenor of the Shadhin Bangla Chhatra Shongram Parishad.[^cite_note-15] He participated in the Liberation War as a freedom fighter. He was the joint secretary of the [Bangladesh Krishak Sramik Awami League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Krishak_Sramik_Awami_League) (BAKSAL) of the district unit and publicity secretary of the Awami League district unit. He was imprisoned after the [assassination of Sheikh Mujibur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Sheikh_Mujibur_Rahman) in 1975 for three years.[^cite_note-\:0-14]

### Legal career

Mohammed Shahabuddin worked as a journalist for *[Daily Banglar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Banglar_Bani)* *[Bani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Banglar_Bani)* from 1980–1982.[^cite_note-\:1-16] In 1982, Shahabuddin joined as a Munsef (Civil Judge) of the [Judicial cadre](50847537.md) in the [Bangladesh Civil Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Civil_Service) (BCS). He was finally promoted to the post of District and Sessions Judge, the highest position of the Bangladesh Judicial Service. He was elected to serve as General Secretary of the [Bangladesh Judicial Service Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Judicial_Service_Association) in 1995 and 1996. Following the 2001 general election, he chaired the judicial inquiry commission investigating crimes—including murder, rape, and looting—allegedly committed by leaders and activists of the BNP-Jamaat alliance.[^cite_note-\:1-16]

In 2011, he was appointed as a commissioner of [Anti-Corruption Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Corruption_Commission_\(Bangladesh\)) from 2011, which he served until 2016.[^cite_note-\:0-14]

Shahabuddin was appointed by the [Ministry of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Law,_Justice_and_Parliamentary_Affairs) to serve as a coordinator in the trial to prosecute the assassins of [Sheikh Mujibur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Mujibur_Rahman). He served as Election Commissioner in the 2022 National Council of Bangladesh Awami League.[^cite_note-17]

In 2017, Shahabuddin was appointed an independent director of [Islami Bank Bangladesh Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islami_Bank_Bangladesh_Limited).[^cite_note-18] In February 2023, Shahabuddin resigned from the board of directors of [Islami Bank Bangladesh Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islami_Bank_Bangladesh_Limited) after being elected president of Bangladesh.[^cite_note-19]

## Presidency (2023–2026)

### Election

See also: [2023 Bangladeshi presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Bangladeshi_presidential_election)



Gazette published by Cabinet Division

Shahabuddin was nominated by the Awami League Parliamentary Party as their candidate for [President of Bangladesh](404877.md).[^cite_note-20] On 12 February 2023, he submitted his application to the Chief Election Commissioner and the Presidential Election Officer at the [Election Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Election_Commission) for the [presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Bangladeshi_presidential_election), the only candidate to do so.[^cite_note-21] Shahabuddin then held a tête-à-tête with Prime Minister [Sheikh Hasina](623439.md) at [Ganabhaban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganabhaban).[^cite_note-22] His nomination was welcomed by the opposition [Jatiya Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jatiya_Party_\(Ershad\)), while the [Bangladesh Nationalist Party](309760.md) expressed their disinterest.[^cite_note-23] On 13 February 2023, he was officially elected as the country's 22nd president as he was unopposed. The result was declared by the [Chief Election Commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Election_Commissioner_of_Bangladesh) [Kazi Habibul Awal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazi_Habibul_Awal).[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

### Inauguration

Shahabuddin was sworn in as the 22nd President of Bangladesh in a ceremony at the [Bangabhaban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangabhaban) on 24 April 2023.[^cite_note-\:5-4] Jatiya Sangsad Speaker Shirin Sharmin Chaudhury administered the oath. Prime Minister [Sheikh Hasina](623439.md), Chief Justice [Hasan Foez Siddique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasan_Foez_Siddique), [Sheikh Rehana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Rehana), cabinet members, and other guests attended the state ceremony. After the swearing-in ceremony, Shahabuddin and outgoing president [Mohammad Abdul Hamid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Abdul_Hamid) officially switched their seats as part of the ceremony of changing the charge of the office. Shahabuddin and Speaker [Shirin Sharmin Chaudhury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirin_Sharmin_Chaudhury) signed the oath documents of the office.[^cite_note-26]

### Tenure

In December 2023, Shahabuddin vetoed the Labour Bill 2023 which was passed by the Parliament and sent it for reconsideration.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

### Incidents following July Uprising



President Shahabuddin with armed forces chiefs on 6 August 2024, in the aftermath of [resignation of Sheikh Hasina](77539154.md)



Shahabuddin with [Sheikh Hasina](623439.md) as the Prime Minister in January 2024



Shahabuddin with [Muhammad Yunus](548105.md) as the Chief Adviser in August 2024

On 5 August 2024, the [Chief of the Army Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Army_Staff_\(Bangladesh\)), General [Waker-uz-Zaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waker-uz-Zaman) announced the resignation of Prime Minister [Sheikh Hasina](623439.md) following the [Non-cooperation movement (2024)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-cooperation_movement_\(2024\)).[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30] President Shahabuddin addressed the nation on 5 August 2024 and stated that Hasina had resigned.[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32] However he later stated that "he had heard Sheikh Hasina resigned from the post of prime minister, but he had no documentary evidence to support this".[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-Alam-Pathi-34]

He immediately started the process of meeting with political leaders from around the country to discuss the formation of an interim government.[^cite_note-35] He also dissolved the parliament[^cite_note-36] and ordered the release of former Prime Minister [Khaleda Zia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khaleda_Zia) from house arrest.[^cite_note-37]

On 6 August 2024, Shahabuddin, as Commander-in-Chief of the [Bangladesh Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Armed_Forces), issued a major reshuffle of the [Bangladesh Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Army), which included the demotion or discharging of several Awami League-aligned officers, including Major General [Ziaul Ahsan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziaul_Ahsan).[^cite_note-38] [Nasimul Gani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasimul_Gani) was appointed Secretary of the Public Department of the President's Office.[^cite_note-39]

On 8 August 2024, Shahabuddin swore in Nobel laureate [Muhammad Yunus](548105.md) as [Chief Adviser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Adviser_\(Bangladesh\)) along with members of his [interim government](77545215.md) at the [Bangabhaban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangabhaban).[^cite_note-40] The interim government was dissolved on 17 February 2026 following the swearing-in of the [Tarique ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarique_ministry). During its tenure, as the parliament was in dissolved state, the interim administration promulgated a total of 133 ordinances which were approved and signed by the president. Of these, 17 were issued in 2024, 80 in 2025, and 36 between January and February 2026. One notable order titled the "[July National Charter (Constitution Reform) Implementation Order, 2025](80557195.md#Implementation)" was also issued following consensus among the political parties in Banglades&#x68;*.*[^cite_note-41]

On 10 August 2024, following the resignation of Chief Justice [Obaidul Hassan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obaidul_Hassan), President Shahabuddin appointed [Syed Refaat Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syed_Refaat_Ahmed) as Chief Justice.[^cite_note-42] After the interim government's tenure, Sahabuddin criticized [Muhammad Yunus](548105.md) for repeatedly failing to follow constitutional provisions and praised the [Bangladesh Nationalist Party](309760.md) for their support.[^cite_note-43] During the interim government, Shahabuddin claimed he was largely isolated and reportedly faced attempts to remove him.[^cite_note-\:4-9]

### 13th general election and subsequent incidents

Main articles: [2026 Bangladeshi general election](72614737.md) and [2026 Bangladeshi constitutional referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bangladeshi_constitutional_referendum)



Shahabuddin greeting with [Prime Minister](461887.md) [Tarique Rahman](5601422.md) in February 2026.

The 13th general election and a constitutional referendum were held on 12 February 2026 in Bangladesh, in which the [Bangladesh Nationalist Party](309760.md), led by [Tarique Rahman](5601422.md), won a landslide victory. Tarique Rahman was elected Prime Minister by the confidence of the majority of Members of Parliament and subsequently formed the [government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarique_ministry).

Mohammed Shahabuddin administered the oath of office to the cabinet, led by Prime Minister Tarique Rahman, on 17 February 2026.[^cite_note-44] [Nahid Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahid_Islam), Member of Parliament from Dhaka-11 and Chief Whip of the opposition demanded impeachment and arrest of Shahabuddin, calling him as an associate of the [July massacre](77480962.md).[^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46]

Opposition leader of the parliament [Shafiqur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shafiqur_Rahman) criticised Mohammed Shahabuddin, stating that he had neither protested nor acted against the killings during the tenure of [Sheikh Hasina](623439.md) and had lost credibility by providing misleading statements to the nation, including contradicting his 5 August 2024 claim regarding the resignation of Sheikh Hasina as the Prime Minister.[^cite_note-47] At the first session of the 13th National Parliament, opposition members staged a walkout in protest against President Mohammed Shahabuddin as soon as he began his address.[^cite_note-\:2-48]

### Resignation

In July 2026, Bangladeshi intelligence agencies (including the [NTMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Telecommunication_Monitoring_Centre), [DGFI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DGFI), and [NSI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Intelligence)) reported that Shahabuddin had conducted backchannel communications with ousted Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina and met exiled Awami League officials during a May 2026 medical trip to London.[^cite_note-49] Shahabuddin rejected the reports as "false".[^cite_note-\:6-50]

On 24 July 2026, following mounting political pressure, he stepped down from his position citing incapacitation from [autonomic neuropathy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomic_neuropathy), heart disease, and diabetes.[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52] Speaker [Hafiz Uddin Ahmad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafiz_Uddin_Ahmad) subsequently assumed duties as acting President pursuant to Article 54 of the Bangladeshi Constitution.[^cite_note-53][^cite_note-\:7-10]

## Controversies

### Dispute over former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina's resignation

Following the [resignation of Sheikh Hasina](77539154.md), Shahabuddin, in a video statement, stated that Hasina had resigned. Later in October 2024, during a conversation with *[Manab Zamin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manab_Zamin)'*&#x73; chief editor,[^cite_note-54] he stated that

> I tried \[to collect the resignation letter] many times but failed. Maybe she did not get the time. When things came under control, one day the cabinet secretary came to collect the copy of the resignation letter. I told him that I too am looking for it,[^cite_note-55]

&#x20;This triggered a demand for his resignation.[^cite_note-56] However, Shahabuddin later issued an official statement where he stated that [resignation of Sheikh Hasina](77539154.md) is a settled matter and urged all not to create any controversy regarding this.[^cite_note-57]

### Walkout of opposition MPs during presidential speech

On 12 March 2026, during the first session of the [13th Jatiya Sangsad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13th_Jatiya_Sangsad), Speaker [Hafizuddin Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafizuddin_Ahmed) announced that Shahabuddin would be delivering a speech, soon [MPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament_\(Bangladesh\)) of the [11 Party Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/11_Party_Alliance) began displaying cards and chanting slogans. Speaker [Hafizuddin Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafizuddin_Ahmed) then requested the [MPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament_\(Bangladesh\)) to take their seats and maintain order. Shortly after Shahabuddin begin his speech the Opposition [MPs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Parliament_\(Bangladesh\)) walked out.[^cite_note-58][^cite_note-\:2-48]

### Shift in political stance

Shahabuddin was a member of the [Awami League](404861.md) and admirer of [Sheikh Hasina](623439.md) and [Sheikh Mujibur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Mujibur_Rahman). He was a critic of the [Bangladesh Nationalist Party](309760.md) but following the [2026 General Election](72614737.md) and [Tarique Rahman](5601422.md)'s takeover as the [Prime Minister](461887.md) Shahabuddin criticised [Sheikh Hasina's government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Hasina_ministry) for the [July massacre](77480962.md) and described the regime as fascist. He praised former [Prime Minister](461887.md) [Khaleda Zia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khaleda_Zia) and former [President](404877.md) and [Chief of Army Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Army_Staff_\(Bangladesh\)) [Ziaur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziaur_Rahman).[^cite_note-\:3-8]

## Personal life



Shahabuddin with First Lady Rebecca Sultana visiting [Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rooppur_Nuclear_Power_Plant) in June 2024.

Since November 1972, Shahabuddin has been married to [Rebecca Sultana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Sultana), a former joint secretary to the [government of Bangladesh](18420484.md).[^cite_note-ke-59][^cite_note-60] She also served as the faculty member of [Primeasia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primeasia_University)[^cite_note-61] and the founding chairperson of Friends for Children Organization.[^cite_note-ke-59] Together they have a son, Arshad Adnan, who produced several Bangladeshi films.[^cite_note-62][^cite_note-63] Adnan was a member of Awami League's subcommittee for cultural affairs in 2023.[^cite_note-64]

## Honours

In his honour, a park built by the Pabna Municipality in 2020 was named "Bir Muktijoddha Mohammad Sahabuddin Chuppu Amusement Park".[^cite_note-65]
