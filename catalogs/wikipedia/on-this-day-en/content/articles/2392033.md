**Rahim Yar Khan** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): رحیم یار خان, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Urdu): *Rahīm Yār Khān*; Urdu pronunciation: [\[ɾəɦiːm jɑːɾ xɑːn\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hindi_and_Urdu)) is a city in the [Punjab](24751.md), Pakistan. It is the [21st most populous city in Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most_populous_cities_in_Pakistan) and is the capital of the [Rahim Yar Khan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahim_Yar_Khan_District). The city's administration is divided into nine [union councils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_councils_of_Pakistan).[^cite_note-2] Originally named Noshehra, it was renamed to Rahim Yar Khan in honour of the son of [Sadeq Mohammad Khan IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadeq_Mohammad_Khan_IV).

## History

People have lived near this area for a very long time. The ancient tower of [Pattan Minara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patan_minara) stands 8 km to the east of the city center, and is believed to be the remains of a 5,000-year-old monastery, with the minara built during the Hakrra valley civilization of the [Mauryan period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurya_Empire) (250 BC).[^cite_note-3]

Rahim Yar Khan was founded in 1751 as Noshehra.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKhawaja_Fareed_University_of_Engineering_and_Information_Technology-4] Its region was part of the [Multan province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan_Subah) of Mughal Empire.[^cite_note-5] Railway administrators wanted to change the name of the town due to its similar name to [Nowshera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nowshera,_Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa). In 1881, [Sadeq Mohammad Khan IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadeq_Mohammad_Khan_IV) renamed the area to Rahim Yar Khan in honour of his son.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTERahim_Yar_Khan_District-6]

Rahim Yar Khan was a part of the [Bahawalpur State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahawalpur_\(princely_state\)) until 1943, when it became a separate district.[^cite_note-7]

## Climate

Rahim Yar Khan has hot dry climate characterized by hot summers and mild winters. Rahim Yar Khan city is located on the eastern bank of the [River Indus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indus_River) which played a vital role in shaping the region's landscape. One-fourth of the city is within the [Cholistan Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cholistan_Desert).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKhawaja_Fareed_University_of_Engineering_and_Information_Technology-4]

## Economy

Rahim Yar Khan is one of the leading agriculture and industrial centre of [Southern Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Punjab_\(region_of_Pakistan\)). The economy is primarily based on agriculture. 65% of the population is employed in agricultural work.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKhawaja_Fareed_University_of_Engineering_and_Information_Technology-4] Rahim Yar Khan produces [sugarcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugarcane), [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton), [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice) while also being renowned for its high quality [mangoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mango). The city is home to numerous agro-based Industries.

### Language

Languages of Rahim Yar Khan MC (2023)

2. Saraiki (32.9%)

3. Urdu (9.50%)

4. Pashto (2.00%)

5. Others (2.70%)

In the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), 52.9% of the population spoke [Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language), 32.9% spoke [Saraiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraiki_language), 9.0% spoke [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu), 2.0% spoke [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) and an additional 2.70% of the population spoke other languages including 1.13% who did not consider their [mother tongue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_language) to be among the options given.[^cite_note-9]

### Religion

Majority of the population are Muslims (97.44%). Minority faiths include [Hinduism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism_in_Punjab,_Pakistan) (1.33%), Christians (1.18%) and others.[^cite_note-10]

### Motorway

Rahim Yar Khan is connected with [M-5 Motorway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-5_motorway_\(Pakistan\)) which is also known as Multan-Sukkur motorway.

### Railways

Rahim Yar Khan Railway Station is one of the main railway station on [ML-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachi%E2%80%93Peshawar_Line).

### Airport

[Shaikh Zayed International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaikh_Zayed_International_Airport) is located in Rahim Yar Khan. It serves the people of city with domestic and international flights.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKhawaja_Fareed_University_of_Engineering_and_Information_Technology-4] There are daily flight from this airport to Karachi, twice in a week to/from Lahore, and once a week to Islamabad. The Airport runway is capable of accommodating [Boeing 747](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_747) aircraft.
