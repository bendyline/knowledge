The **Rally of Houphouëtists for Democracy and Peace** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Rassemblement des houphouëtistes pour la démocratie et la paix*, RHDP) is a [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Ivory Coast](57744.md).

## History

The RHDP was established as a political alliance on 18 May 2005,[^cite_note-5] seeking to reclaim the political ideology of the country's founding president, [Félix Houphouët-Boigny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9lix_Houphou%C3%ABt-Boigny). It initially included the [Rally of the Republicans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rally_of_the_Republicans), the [Democratic Party of Côte d'Ivoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_of_C%C3%B4te_d'Ivoire_%E2%80%93_African_Democratic_Rally), the [Union for Democracy and Peace in Côte d'Ivoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_for_Democracy_and_Peace_in_C%C3%B4te_d'Ivoire), the [Movement of the Forces of the Future](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_of_the_Forces_of_the_Future) and the [Union for Ivory Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_for_Ivory_Coast).

Despite the formation of the alliance, each party put forward its own candidate in the [2010 presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Ivorian_presidential_election).[^cite_note-6] Although the five parties also largely contested the [2011 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Ivorian_parliamentary_election) alone, joint RHDP lists were put forward in some constituencies, winning four seats in [Abidjan](21371767.md). In the [2015 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Ivorian_presidential_election) the alliance nominated incumbent President [Alassane Ouattara](1173624.md) of the Rally for the Republic as its candidate. Ouattara won with 84% of the vote. In 2016 the [Ivorian Workers' Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivorian_Workers'_Party) joined the RHDP to jointly contest the [2016 parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Ivorian_parliamentary_election).[^cite_note-7] The alliance retained its majority in the elections, winning 167 of the 255 seats.

On 16 July 2018 the alliance was transformed into a unitary party, including members of the Rally of the Republicans, the Union for Democracy and Peace in Ivory Coast and some minor parties. The new party designated [Alassane Ouattara](1173624.md) as its candidate for the [2020 presidential elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Ivorian_presidential_election).[^cite_note-8]

On 27 May 2024 the RHDP nominated Ouattara as its candidate for the [2025 presidential elections](76643951.md).[^cite_note-9]
