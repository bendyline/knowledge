The president of [FIFA](11049.md) serves as the chief executive of the governing body of world [association football](10568.md).[^cite_note-1] There have been 9 presidents in [FIFA's history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_FIFA). [Jules Rimet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jules_Rimet) is the longest-serving FIFA president, in office for 33 years from 1921 to 1954. Three presidents – [Daniel Burley Woolfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Burley_Woolfall), [Rodolphe Seeldrayers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodolphe_Seeldrayers), and [Arthur Drewry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Drewry) – died during their term in office. [Gianni Infantino](9286117.md) has served as the current president since [initial election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_FIFA_Extraordinary_Congress) on 26 February 2016 and reelection on 16 March 2023.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
