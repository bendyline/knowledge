**Oryol Oblast**,[^cite_note-5] also known as **Orlovshchina**,[^cite_note-6] is a [federal subject](701438.md) of [Russia](25391.md) (an [oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblast)). Its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) is the [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) of [Oryol](488242.md). As of the [2021 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census), Oryol Oblast had a population of 713,374.[^cite_note-2021Census-7]

## Geography



Landscape of the eastern part of the region



In the national park [Orlovskoye Polesye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlovskoye_Polesye_National_Park)

It is located in the southwestern part of the [Central Federal District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Federal_District), in the [Central Russian Upland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Russian_Upland).

In terms of area, at 24,652 km<sup>2</sup> (9,518 sq mi) it is one of the smallest federal subjects.[^cite_note-kommersant-8] From north to south, it extends for more than 150 km (93 mi), and from west to east—for over 200 km (120 mi).

It borders [Kaluga Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaluga_Oblast) to the north-west, [Tula Oblast](480930.md) to the north, [Lipetsk Oblast](480918.md) to the east, [Kursk Oblast](480916.md) to the south, and [Bryansk Oblast](476473.md) to the west.

There are 4,800 km<sup>2</sup> (1,900 sq mi) of black earth soils ([chernozems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernozem)) in the oblast, which amounts to three-quarters of the world chernozem reserves.[^cite_note-kommersant-8]

### Climate

The climate is temperate ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): [*Dfb*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate#Warm_summer_subtype)). The winter is moderately cold, with an average January temperature from −9 to −11 °C (16 to 12 °F). Summers are warm and humid, with an average July temperature from 19 to 21 °C (66 to 70 °F). Rainfall averages 520 to 630 mm (20 to 25 in), and snow cover averages 120 days.

### Hydrography

On the territory of the Oryol region there are more than 2 thousand rivers and streams with a total length of 9,100 km (5,700 mi), but there are no navigable water ways. The rivers of the region belong to the basins of three rivers: [Volga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volga_River), [Don](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_River), [Dnieper](380138.md).

The [Oka river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oka_river), one of Europe's largest rivers, flows through the oblast for part of its course (190 km) and the source of it is in the south of the region. Main tributaries: [Zusha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zusha_River) (with tributary [Neruch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neruch_River)), [Vytebet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vytebet), [Nugr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nugr_river?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Tson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tson_river?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Orlik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlik_river?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Rybnitsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rybnitsa_river?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Kroma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kroma_river?action=edit\&redlink=1).

[Sosna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bystraya_Sosna_River) flows in the eastern part of the region. Main tributaries: [Trudy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trudy_river?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Tim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_river?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Lyubovsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyubovsha?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Kshen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kshen_river?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Olym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olym_river?action=edit\&redlink=1).

In the west of the region originate rivers [Nerussa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerussa), [Navlya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navlya_river?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Swapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swapa_river?action=edit\&redlink=1).

1100 lakes and artificial reservoirs of the region cover a total area of about 55 km<sup>2</sup> (21 sq mi) (0,22%).

## History



Outfits of the Oryol province, 1908

In the 12th century, chronicles mention [Mtsensk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mtsensk), known as Novosil then. Then modern Orlovschina was part of the [Chernigov Principality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernigov_Principality). After the death of [Mikhail of Chernigov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_of_Chernigov) Novosil Principality was formed on these territories. By the end of the 15th century, it had disintegrated into four separate principalities and, along with all the other fragments of the Chernigov principality, became a part of [Grand Duchy of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Lithuania). In the 16th century, the fortress town of Oryol was founded, and the town of [Livny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livny), destroyed in the 13th century, was restored. In the 16th and 17th centuries, the territory of modern Oryol was the [borderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borders_of_Russia) of the [Tsardom of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsardom_of_Russia), with many fortifications of the [Great Abatis Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Zasechnaya_cherta). With the reduction of the threat posed by the [Tatars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatars), agricultural activity of the area had intensified. It was created in 1937 by uniting a selection of territories of three other oblasts: [Kursk Oblast](480916.md), [Western Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Oblast), and [Voronezh Oblast](473401.md). It also included present [Bryansk Oblast](476473.md) between 1937 and 1944. In 1941-3 The Region was partly or fully occupied by Germany.

## Politics



The [House of Soviets](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%94%D0%BE%D0%BC%20%D0%A1%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%B2%20\(%D0%9E%D1%80%D1%91%D0%BB\)) in Oryol on Lenin Square is the building of the Council of People's Deputies and the government of the Oryol Oblast

During the [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) period, the high authority in the oblast was shared between three persons: The first secretary of the Oryol CPSU Committee (who in reality had the biggest authority), the chairman of the oblast Soviet (legislative power), and the Chairman of the oblast Executive Committee (executive power). Since 1991, CPSU lost all the power, and the head of the Oblast administration, and eventually the governor was appointed/elected alongside elected [regional parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_parliaments_of_Russia).

The Charter of Oryol Oblast is the fundamental law of the region. The [Oryol Oblast Council of People's Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oryol_Oblast_Council_of_People's_Deputies) is the province's standing legislative (representative) body. The Legislative Assembly exercises its authority by passing laws, resolutions, and other legal acts and by supervising the implementation and observance of the laws and other legal acts passed by it. The highest executive body is the Oblast Government, which includes territorial executive bodies such as district administrations, committees, and commissions that facilitate development and run the day to day matters of the province. The Oblast administration supports the activities of the [Governor of Oryol Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Oryol_Oblast), who is the highest official and acts as guarantor of the observance of the oblast Charter in accordance with the [Constitution of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Russia).

The head of administration of Oryol Oblast between 1993 and 2009 was [Yegor Stroyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yegor_Stroyev). Stroyev led the region for more than 20 years. In 1985 he became the first secretary of the regional committee of the [CPSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CPSU), and after three years (in 1989-1991 he worked as secretary of the [Central Committee of the CPSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_CPSU)), in 1991 he returned to Oryol, worked as the director of the Institute of Horticultural Crops Selection, and later was elected governor. On February 16, 2009 Russian President Dmitry Medvedev accepted Stroyev's voluntary retirement and nominated [Alexander Kozlov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Petrovich_Kozlov?action=edit\&redlink=1) as his replacement, which was approved by the Oryol Regional Council of People's Deputies.

### Legislature

The [Oryol Oblast Council of People's Deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oryol_Oblast_Council_of_People's_Deputies) is a permanent representative and legislative body of state power in the Oryol Oblast. It consists of 50 deputies elected by residents of the region for 5 year terms according to a [mixed-member proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed-member_proportional_representation): 25 deputies according to party lists ([proportional representation](23298.md)) and 25 in single-mandate constituencies ([majoritarian representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majoritarian_representation)) on the basis of universal equal and direct suffrage by secret ballot.

The current 7th Council of People's Deputies was elected in the [2021 Russian regional elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_regional_elections) and will last until 2026. Of the 50 deputies, 27 are from [United Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Russia), 11 from the [Communist Party of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_the_Russian_Federation), 6 from [A Just Russia – For Truth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Just_Russia_%E2%80%93_For_Truth), 3 from the [Liberal Democratic Party of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Democratic_Party_of_Russia), 1 from [New People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_People_\(political_party\)), 1 from the [Russian Party of Pensioners for Social Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Party_of_Pensioners_for_Social_Justice), and 1 independent. [Leonid Muzalevsky](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%9C%D1%83%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%B2%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9,%20%D0%9B%D0%B5%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B4%20%D0%A1%D0%B5%D0%BC%D1%91%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87) (United Russia) was elected Chairman of the Council.

## Economy

The main industries in Oryol Oblast are the food and light industries, engineering and metalworking, and ferrous and nonferrous metallurgy. The [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering) and [metalworking](266443.md) industries manufacture production equipment for various industries, [forklift trucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forklift_truck), [construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Construction) and [agricultural equipment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agricultural_equipment), and [machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machinery) for municipal services. Numerous companies in the instrument-making and [electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronics) sectors maintain high scientific and technical potential with the latest high-end technologies and experienced specialists.[^cite_note-9] First digital [telephone exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telephone_exchange) was introduced in the oblast in 1998.[^cite_note-10]

### Agriculture

Most of the oblast's agricultural land is used for plant cultivation. Grain growing is very important, with [winter wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_wheat) and [rye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rye) being the main crops. [Buckwheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckwheat), [oats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oat), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), and [potatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potatoes) are also grown, and [sugar](27712.md) beets are in great demand. The area planted in feed grains is increasing due to the expansion of [livestock](25160767.md) farming, which includes [beef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beef) and [dairy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy) [cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle) farming, [pig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pig) farming, [sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheep) farming for [meat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meat) and [wool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wool), [poultry farming](12116844.md), and [horse breeding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_breeding).[^cite_note-11]

## Transport

Pipelines and power transmission lines are routed through the region's largest [oil-trunk pipeline Druzhba](1950396.md) (202 km in area). In the southwestern part of the area lies a small section of the [Urengoy - Pomary - Uzhgorod pipeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urengoy%E2%80%93Pomary%E2%80%93Uzhhorod_pipeline).

Oryol is a major hub of pipelines exporting to Belarus, Western Ukraine and the [Baltic states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltic_states), with branches passing through [Bryansk](476476.md) and [Kursk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kursk).
