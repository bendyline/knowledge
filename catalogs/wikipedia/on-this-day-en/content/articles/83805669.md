# 2026 Booker Prize

| Date | 9 November 2026 |

The **2026 Booker Prize** is a [literary award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literary_award) worth £50,000 given for the best [English-language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) novel published between 1 October 2025 and 30 September 2026 in either the United Kingdom or Ireland.

The longlist for the 2026 [Booker Prize](4446.md) was released on 28 July 2026.[^cite_note-GuardJul2026-1]

The shortlist was announced at a ceremony at [Southbank Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southbank_Centre) in London on 22 September, and the winner will be announced at [Old Billingsgate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Billingsgate) in the City of London on 9 November 2026.[^cite_note-Booker2026-2]

## Judging panel

- [Mary Beard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Beard_\(classicist\)) (chair)

- [Raymond Antrobus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_Antrobus)

- [Jarvis Cocker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarvis_Cocker)

- [Patricia Lockwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patricia_Lockwood)

- [Rebecca Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Liu)
