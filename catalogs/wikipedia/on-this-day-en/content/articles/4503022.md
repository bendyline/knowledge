&#x20;The **National Parliament of Solomon Islands** is the legislature of [Solomon Islands](265083.md). Its 50 members are elected for a four-year term in [50 single-seat constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_constituencies_of_the_National_Parliament_of_Solomon_Islands).[^cite_note-1]
