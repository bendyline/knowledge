The **African Movement for New Independence and Democracy** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Mouvement africain pour la nouvelle indépendance et la démocratie*, **MANIDEM**) is a [left-wing nationalist](16066160.md) [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Cameroon](5447.md). It emerged from the [Union of the Peoples of Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_the_Peoples_of_Cameroon) (UPC) and was legally recognized in 1995. [Valentin Dongmo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentin_Dongmo?action=edit\&redlink=1) has been the party's national president since July 2026, succeeding [Anicet Ekane](81734136.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## History

MANIDEM originated as a faction of the [Union of the Peoples of Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_the_Peoples_of_Cameroon) and initially operated under the name UPC-MANIDEM.[^cite_note-3] The party was legally recognized on 3 March 1995.[^cite_note-4] [Anicet Ekane](81734136.md) was subsequently one of its principal leaders and served as its president for several periods.

In the [2025 presidential election](73432126.md), MANIDEM initially supported opposition leader [Maurice Kamto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Kamto) as its candidate.[^cite_note-5] His candidacy was rejected by [Elections Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_Cameroon) after another candidate, Dieudonné Yebga, was also submitted under the MANIDEM label. The Constitutional Council upheld the rejection against Kamto in August 2025.[^cite_note-6]

Following the invalidation of Maurice Kamto's candidacy, MANIDEM shifted its support to [Issa Tchiroma](24368622.md), with party president [Anicet Ekane](81734136.md) explaining that the decision formed part of the party's strategy of alliance for the election.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

On 1 December 2025, Ekane died while in detention in [Yaoundé](57671.md) at the age of 74. He had been arrested in October during the [political crisis](81442363.md) that followed the presidential election.[^cite_note-9]

Following Ekane's death, Valentin Dongmo was selected as national president by the party's leadership on 13 June 2026. The new leadership was formally presented in [Douala](19997263.md) on 30 July 2026, with Dongmo succeeding Ekane as president.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]
