The **minister of the interior** ([Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_language): *sisäministeri*, before 2013 *sisäasiainministeri*, [Swedish](26689.md): *inrikesminister*) is one of the ministerial portfolios in the [Finnish Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Government).[^cite_note-1] The minister of the interior is in charge of the [Ministry of the Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_the_Interior_\(Finland\)).[^cite_note-2]

The incumbent [Orpo Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orpo_Cabinet)'s minister of the interior is [Mari Rantanen](62679496.md) of the [Finns Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finns_Party).
