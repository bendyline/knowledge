The **2026 FIA Formula One World Championship** is a motor racing championship for [Formula One cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_cars) and the 77th running of the [Formula One World Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_World_Championship). It is recognised by the [Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile](248019.md) (FIA), the governing body of international [motorsport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorsport), as the highest class of competition for [open-wheel racing cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-wheel_racing_car). The championship is contested over twenty-three Grands Prix held around the world. It began in March and is scheduled to end in December. Drivers and teams compete for the titles of [World Drivers' Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Drivers'_Champion) and [World Constructors' Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Constructors'_Champion), respectively. [Lando Norris](48978813.md) is the reigning World Drivers' Champion, while [McLaren](20994.md)-[Mercedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_AMG_High_Performance_Powertrains) are the reigning World Constructors' Champions.

The 2026 season features a major set of regulation changes with a revised power unit configuration and new active aerodynamics along with the addition of an eleventh team competing for the first time since [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Formula_One_World_Championship). [Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_in_Formula_One), who acquired [Sauber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauber_Motorsport) in [2024](72504121.md), enter as a [works team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factory-backed) with its own power unit, while [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One) are making their debut in the championship using [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) power units. [Honda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_in_Formula_One), through its [Honda Racing Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Racing_Corporation) subsidiary, entered into an exclusive works team agreement with [Aston Martin](63473867.md), and are supplying them with their own power unit after ending its relationship with [Red Bull Racing](1173193.md). [Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_in_Formula_One) returned to the sport for the first time since [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Formula_One_World_Championship), supporting [Red Bull Powertrains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Bull_Powertrains) in supplying power units to Red Bull Racing and [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls). [Renault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_in_Formula_One) is no longer an engine supplier as [Alpine](65240620.md) switched to Mercedes power units.

### Team changes





Two car manufacturers entered in 2026: [Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_in_Formula_One) (left) replaced [Sauber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauber_Motorsport) to compete as a [works team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factory-backed), while [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One) (right) became the eleventh team in the Championship.

[Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One) became the eleventh team, marking Cadillac's first appearance in the series and the first new team on the grid since [Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team) in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Formula_One_World_Championship). The team, initially using [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) power units and gearboxes,[^cite_note-Cadillac-19][^cite_note-Cadillac_GM-20][^cite_note-CadillacApproved-21][^cite_note-Cadillac-Ferrari-23] would transition to a power unit developed in-house by its parent company, [General Motors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Motors), in collaboration with [TWG Motorsports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TWG_Motorsports).[^cite_note-48] This power unit programme has received approval from the FIA and is scheduled to be introduced from the 2029 season.[^cite_note-49] Cadillac had previously tried to enter Formula One in conjunction with [Andretti Global](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andretti_Global).[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51]





[Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_in_Formula_One) returned to Formula One for the first time since [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Formula_One_World_Championship) after forming a partnership with [Red Bull Powertrains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Bull_Powertrains). [Honda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_in_Formula_One) returned as an independent engine supplier for the first time since [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Formula_One_World_Championship), this time as supplier for [Aston Martin](63473867.md).

Three new engine manufacturers entered Formula One, coinciding with the engine regulation changes. [Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_in_Formula_One) entered the sport for the first time, having purchased the existing [Sauber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauber_Motorsport) team in [2024](72504121.md). Audi's predecessor company [Auto Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto_Union) did [compete in Grand Prix racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto_Union_racing_cars) prior to [World War II](32927.md) and the inception of the World Championship in [1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_Formula_One_season).[^cite_note-52] The team raced in 2024 and [2025](72768531.md) as [Kick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kick_\(service\)) Sauber, using Ferrari engines, before it became the Audi factory team in 2026. Thus, Ferrari is supplying Haas and Cadillac as customer teams.[^cite_note-AudiExpands-15][^cite_note-53]

[Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_in_Formula_One) returned to Formula One as an [engine supplier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_engine_manufacturers) for the first time since it provided engines for its former customer team [Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Grand_Prix) in [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Formula_One_World_Championship).[^cite_note-54] It formed a partnership with [Red Bull Powertrains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Bull_Powertrains), which supplies [Red Bull Racing](1173193.md) and their second team [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls). [Honda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_in_Formula_One), which partially withdrew from Formula One in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Formula_One_World_Championship) whilst remaining partners of Red Bull Powertrains, split from the two Red Bull-owned teams and relaunched a fully-independent engine programme supplying [Aston Martin](63473867.md) under the [Honda Racing Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Racing_Corporation) subsidiary. Aston Martin had used [Mercedes power units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_AMG_High_Performance_Powertrains) since they re-joined the sport, who had supplied engines to Aston Martin's predecessors since [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Formula_One_World_Championship). Honda had previously collaborated with Aston Martin's predecessor Jordan from [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Formula_One_World_Championship) to [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Formula_One_World_Championship).[^cite_note-AstonHondaSky-9][^cite_note-FordBull-38][^cite_note-55]

[Renault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_in_Formula_One) ceased to provide engines for [Alpine](65240620.md) from 2026 after achieving poor results since the beginning of the hybrid power unit regulations in [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Formula_One_World_Championship). 2026 is thus the first season without Renault as engine supplier since the [2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Formula_One_World_Championship) championship. Alpine became a customer team, as opposed to a full works outfit, as had been the case since Renault reacquired [Team Enstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_Enstone) ahead of the [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Formula_One_World_Championship) season.[^cite_note-RenaultShutdown-5] Alpine became a customer team of [Mercedes](25098794.md), utilising their engines and gearboxes from this season onwards; up until this point, [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Formula_One_World_Championship) marked the last time a rendition of Team Enstone used customer Mercedes engines, competing as [Lotus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotus_F1).[^cite_note-AlpineMercedes-6]

### Driver changes





[Arvid Lindblad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvid_Lindblad) (left) and [Franco Colapinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Colapinto) (right) made their full season debuts with [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls) and [Alpine](65240620.md), respectively.

[Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez) and [Valtteri Bottas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valtteri_Bottas) returned to full time seats with [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One). Pérez had previously signed a two-year contract with [Red Bull Racing](1173193.md) until 2026, but this was terminated by mutual agreement at the end of the [2024](72504121.md) season. Bottas last competed with [Sauber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauber_Motorsport) in 2024 and was one of [Mercedes](25098794.md)'s reserve drivers for [2025](72768531.md).[^cite_note-56] [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) moved from [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls) to [Red Bull Racing](1173193.md), replacing [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda), who became Red Bull's test and reserve driver.[^cite_note-57] Hadjar's replacement was [Arvid Lindblad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvid_Lindblad), who was promoted from [Formula 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIA_Formula_2_Championship).[^cite_note-Hadjar-41]

#### Mid-season changes







[Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) (left) substituted for [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) (centre) at [Red Bull Racing](1173193.md) for the [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Dutch_Grand_Prix), [Italian](81006624.md) and [Spanish](79774929.md) Grands Prix. [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda) (right) substituted for Lawson at [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls).

Prior to the [Dutch Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Dutch_Grand_Prix), [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) sustained a wrist injury. Racing Bulls driver [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) substituted for him, while Lawson was replaced at Racing Bulls by reserve driver [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda).[^cite_note-Lawson-58] Both drivers remained at their respective teams after Hadjar was announced to also miss the [Italian](81006624.md) and [Spanish](79774929.md) Grands Prix.[^cite_note-59][^cite_note-60] Hadjar returned for the following [Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-61]

### Postponed and cancelled Grands Prix

Main articles: [2026 Bahrain Grand Prix](80201648.md) and [2026 Saudi Arabian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Saudi_Arabian_Grand_Prix)

The [Bahrain](80201648.md) and [Saudi Arabian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Saudi_Arabian_Grand_Prix) Grands Prix were included on the original calendar published by the [World Motor Sport Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Motor_Sport_Council) and were contracted for the 2026 season, but were postponed following the outbreak of the [Iran war](82533582.md). Concerns revolving around safety following the breakout of the war were taken into consideration, with the FIA monitoring the situation as it unfolded.[^cite_note-70] Ultimately, it was announced that neither race would take place on its originally scheduled April date.[^cite_note-autosport20260314-71][^cite_note-72][^cite_note-f1bahsau-73] As the Grands Prix were to be held early in the season, replacement races could not be organised in short notice.[^cite_note-74] The Saudi Arabian Grand Prix was not reinstated, while the Bahrain Grand Prix was rescheduled and relocated to the [Sepang International Circuit](944336.md) in [Malaysia](3607937.md), rather than being held at its originally planned venue, the [Bahrain International Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_International_Circuit). Despite the venue change, the event retained its original name because the Bahraini government remained the primary financial promoter,[^cite_note-75][^cite_note-76] rather than this event becoming an instance of the [Malaysian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysian_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-BahMalV1-67][^cite_note-BahMalV2-68]

### Calendar changes

The [Spanish Grand Prix](526356.md) moved from the [Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_Barcelona-Catalunya) in [Montmeló](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montmel%C3%B3) to a [new street circuit](80044756.md) in [Madrid](41188263.md) built around the [IFEMA Exhibition Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFEMA_Exhibition_Centre).[^cite_note-77] The Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya continues to host a race under the name [Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona-Catalunya_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-78] The [Emilia Romagna Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilia_Romagna_Grand_Prix) in [Imola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imola_Circuit) was discontinued after their contract to host their race until [2025](72768531.md) was not renewed.[^cite_note-2026_calendar-62] The [Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix) was held on a Saturday following a request from the promoter and relevant government stakeholders to accommodate Azerbaijan's [Remembrance Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remembrance_Day_\(Azerbaijan\)).[^cite_note-test-65][^cite_note-79] The [Bahrain Grand Prix](511204.md) was moved from [Sakhir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_International_Circuit) to the [Sepang International Circuit](944336.md) in [Malaysia](3607937.md) due to the [Iran war](82533582.md). The circuit had last hosted the [Malaysian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysian_Grand_Prix) in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Formula_One_World_Championship).[^cite_note-80]

## Regulation changes

Financial, power unit, aerodynamic, geometric, and safety regulations were altered for the 2026 season.[^cite_note-81]

### Financial regulation

For 2026, the Formula One cost cap for team operational expenditures was increased from \$135 million to \$215 million. This was primarily due to inflation adjustments and the inclusion of costs that were previously exempted from the cap. Key expenses remaining outside the cost cap include driver salaries, the salaries of the three highest-paid team members, marketing, travel and hospitality costs, as well as infrastructure investments.[^cite_note-82][^cite_note-83][^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85] A separate cost cap for power unit manufacturers, which was introduced in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Formula_One_World_Championship), was also increased from \$95 million to \$130 million from 2026 onwards to support the development of the new hybrid engines.[^cite_note-86][^cite_note-87][^cite_note-88]

### Technical regulations

#### Power units

New power unit regulations have been introduced for the 2026 season.[^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90] The new power units still produce over 1,000 [bhp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horsepower#Brake_horsepower) (750 [kW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilowatt)), although the power comes from different sources. The engine regulations saw the turbocharged 1.6-litre V6 [internal combustion engine](41228673.md) configuration used since [2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Formula_One_World_Championship) retained. However, the [MGU-H](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MGU-H) (Motor Generator Unit – Heat), which has also been in use since 2014, has been removed, while the [MGU-K](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MGU-K) (Motor Generator Unit – Kinetic) output increased to 470 bhp (350 kW) from 160 bhp (120 kW). The power output of the internal combustion part of the power unit decreased to 540 bhp (400 kW) from 850 bhp (630 kW). Fuel flow rates are measured and limited based on energy, rather than mass of the fuel itself. The power units use a fully [sustainable fuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_fuel) being developed by Formula One.[^cite_note-91][^cite_note-92] The power units are expected to recover twice as much electrical energy as before.[^cite_note-AutosportRevealed-93]

During the Commission meeting in early 2025, it was agreed to explore for catch-up options for power unit development for manufacturers who may have fallen behind their competitors in the initial development phase. The Commission also suggested that a relevant advisory committee look at whether the energy deployment of new power units should be reduced in race trim from the planned 470 bhp (350 kW) after some teams became concerned about the possibility of their cars running out of deployable energy in a race.[^cite_note-94]

The [Japanese Grand Prix](80284355.md) saw [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman) crash at a high speed due to a speed differential that was caused by him using more electrical energy than [Franco Colapinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Colapinto), who opted to harvest his energy. The power unit changes that mandated the increased usage of the battery and electric power deployment modes came under scrutiny from drivers.[^cite_note-95][^cite_note-electrical_boost-96] The FIA held a review with power unit developers on 9 April regarding potential refinements to the regulations.[^cite_note-45kph-97] One of the solutions was to reduce the deployment rate of electrical energy while increasing the amount that can be harvested while undergoing "super clipping", a phenomenon that occurs when the battery is being charged on full throttle against the engine. Three further meetings were held on 15, 16 and 20 April, where further options would be evaluated and agreed on by team representatives, the FIA, Formula One Management, and power unit manufacturers.[^cite_note-98]

Going into the [Miami Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Miami_Grand_Prix), changes were announced to be made to the power unit. Those include an increase to the "super clipping" limit from 340 bhp (250 kW) to 470 bhp (350 kW). It intends to reduce its duration to 2–4 seconds per lap. Though the cars will still lose top speed on the straights, this reduces the need to apply lift and coast as the speed difference is considered much more natural. For qualifying, the harvesting limit was reduced from 8 MJ (2.2 [kWh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilowatt-hour)) to 7 MJ (1.9 [kWh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilowatt-hour)), with the potential to reduce it further for some races.[^cite_note-99][^cite_note-100]

#### Car size and aerodynamics

On 6 June 2024, the 2026 car concept was revealed. The concept featured new active aerodynamics in both the front and rear wings. The concept saw the elimination of the [drag reduction system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_reduction_system) (DRS), being replaced by a new overtake mode, initially referred to as manual override mode.[^cite_note-101] The wheelbase was reduced from 360 cm (140 in) to 340 cm (130 in), the width was reduced from 200 cm (79 in) to 190 cm (75 in), and the minimum mass was reduced by 30 kg (66 lb). The tyres' widths were also reduced by 2.5 cm (0.98 in) on the front pair and by 3.0 cm (1.2 in) on the rears.[^cite_note-AutosportRevealed-93][^cite_note-102] The floor reduced [ground effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_effect_\(cars\)) to ease the issues cars have suffered with [porpoising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porpoising).[^cite_note-103]

In October 2024, FIA announced that the downforce reduction of the 2026 cars compared to the [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Formula_One_World_Championship)–[2025](72768531.md) generation of cars would be less than initially proposed for performance and safety reasons.[^cite_note-104] Later in the month, the FIA confirmed that the reduction in downforce from the 2026 generation of cars would be around 15%, a significantly smaller reduction than the originally drafted regulations which the FIA claimed had given the 2026 cars downforce reduction of over 40% compared to their predecessors. This would make the 2026 cars approximately two seconds slower a lap than the 2022–2025 generation of cars rather than the four seconds slower initially envisaged.[^cite_note-105] On 17 December 2025, Formula One announced the official terms describing features of the 2026 cars. The term "boost mode" is used to describe the normal deployment of the energy recovery system (ERS) to defend or attack; "active aerodynamics" (or simply "active aero") to describe the moveable front and rear wing elements – in both higher and lower drag/downforce configuration (called "corner mode" and "straightline mode" respectively); "recharge" to describe when a car is harvesting electrical energy; and "overtake mode" to describe a mode where extra energy stored is used in order to help one driver overtake another, but only if the driver behind is within one second of the one ahead (similar to pre-2026 DRS deployment rules).[^cite_note-106][^cite_note-107]

Due to [the track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_Monaco)'s tight, twisty nature, the only Grand Prix where active aero was disabled was the [Monaco Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Monaco_Grand_Prix), which historically had one DRS zone.[^cite_note-108]

#### Safety features

The regulations for the front impact structure (FIS) were updated with the intent to enhance [safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safety_in_Formula_One) during crashes. A two-stage FIS design has been introduced to address previous issues where the structure detached near the survival cell after a primary collision, leaving the vehicle vulnerable to further impacts. Side intrusion protection, particularly around the cockpit and fuel cell was also improved. These upgrades aim to shield critical areas of the car during side collisions, while maintaining the vehicle's weight. The roll hoop's strength was improved, withstanding loads increased from 16 *g* to 20 *g*, aligning with safety standards of other [single-seater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-seater) series. The load testing requirements were raised from 141 kN to 167 kN.[^cite_note-Safety-109] To further safeguard drivers and [track marshals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Track_marshals), lateral safety lights were mandated. These lights display the [energy recovery system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_recovery_system_\(Formula_One\)) (ERS) status of a car when it stops on track, providing visual indicators of potential electrical risks, especially in emergency situations.[^cite_note-Safety-109]

## Season summary

### Pre-season

Three pre-season tests were held, a significant expansion over previous years' testing programs to account for the new chassis and engine regulations.[^cite_note-119] The first was a private test which took place at the [Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_Barcelona-Catalunya) from 26 to 30 January, with teams allowed to run in a maximum of three of the five days. All teams ran in the private test except [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing).[^cite_note-test-65][^cite_note-120] [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) ([Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari)) set the fastest time in the five-day test.[^cite_note-121] The second test was held at the [Bahrain International Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_International_Circuit) from 11 to 13 February, with [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) ([Mercedes](25098794.md)) setting the fastest time in the three-day test.[^cite_note-122] The third test was also held at the same venue from 18 to 20 February, with [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) (Ferrari) setting the fastest time across the three-day test.[^cite_note-test-65][^cite_note-123]

### Opening rounds

[Mercedes](25098794.md)' [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) took pole position for the season opening [Australian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Australian_Grand_Prix), ahead of teammate [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md). [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) qualified third on his debut for [Red Bull](1173193.md).[^cite_note-124] [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) jumped from fourth on the grid to take the lead at the start, and proceeded to battle Russell in the early laps, with both drivers taking stints in the lead. After a virtual safety car was called for Hadjar stopping on track with an engine issue, Mercedes brought both their cars in for a pit stop, whereas Ferrari stayed out hoping to take advantage of having newer tyres later on in the race. Mercedes' strategy came up trumps and Russell went on to secure victory, with Antonelli finishing second to complete a Mercedes one–two. Leclerc finished third, holding off a late charge from Hamilton. Reigning World Champion [Lando Norris](48978813.md) finished fifth for [McLaren](20994.md), having been the team's only starter after [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) crashed while driving to the grid before the race. [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) recovered from 20th on the grid, due to a crash in qualifying, to finish sixth, ahead of [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman) and rookie [Arvid Lindblad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvid_Lindblad). [Gabriel Bortoleto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto) and [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md) completed the top ten and the points-scoring positions. Five drivers retired from the race.[^cite_note-125][^cite_note-126][^cite_note-127] In the [Drivers' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Drivers'_Champion), Russell took the lead for the first time in his career with 25 points, ahead of his teammate Antonelli with 18 points and Leclerc with 15 points.[^cite_note-128]



At the [Chinese Grand Prix](80284337.md), [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) became the [youngest polesitter in Formula One](13599687.md#Youngest_polesitters), and the first [Italian driver](37543516.md) to win a Grand Prix since [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Malaysian_Grand_Prix).

Russell took his maiden sprint pole position for the [Chinese Grand Prix](80284337.md), ahead of Antonelli and Norris.[^cite_note-129] The opening laps saw Hamilton take the lead, as he exchanged positions several times with Russell.[^cite_note-130] Hamilton and Leclerc fought for podium places with the latter gaining the advantage. Antonelli dropped down the order in the opening laps, only to have his recovery to second place be thwarted by a penalty from causing a collision with Hadjar on the opening lap, ultimately finishing fifth. Thus, the podium was rounded out by Russell, Leclerc and Hamilton.[^cite_note-131] Antonelli later became the youngest ever Formula One pole-sitter after taking pole position for the main race, while teammate Russell recovered from a mechanical issue to qualify second.[^cite_note-132] Piastri, Norris, Bortoleto, and Albon all did not start the race due to separate car issues. The opening lap saw Hamilton reach the lead again within the first few turns, this time ahead of Antonelli, while Leclerc overtook Russell to take third. Antonelli regained the lead by overtaking Hamilton on lap 2. Following Stroll's battery issue resulting in being stranded at turn 1 and bringing out the safety car, most of the grid initially on the medium compound moved to a one stop strategy. From there, Antonelli held onto the lead to claim his maiden victory ahead of Russell who complete another Mercedes one–two, while Hamilton took third after a lengthy battle with Leclerc, thus achieving his first podium for [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) and first since the [2024 Las Vegas Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Las_Vegas_Grand_Prix). Colapinto also scored his first points for Alpine, and his first since the [2024 United States Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Grand_Prix) at Williams.[^cite_note-133]

Antonelli took a second consecutive pole position for the [Japanese Grand Prix](80284355.md) ahead of Russell and Piastri. After dropping down to as low as sixth, Antonelli made use of a well-timed safety car, brought out due to [Oliver Bearman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Bearman)'s heavy [Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_F1_Team) crash,[^cite_note-134] to retain the lead as he switched to fresher tyres, while teammate Russell finished fourth after briefly dropping down the order to both Ferraris (he managed to repass only Hamilton). Antonelli's victory meant he took his first consecutive victories of the season, and overtook Russell to become the [youngest World Drivers' Championship leader](13599687.md#Championships,_other); also becoming Italy's first Drivers' Championship leader since [Alberto Ascari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Ascari) led, and won, the [1953](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1953_Formula_One_season) championship. Behind him, Piastri, recording his first race start of the season, and Leclerc rounded out the podium.[^cite_note-135]

Several changes to the power unit came into effect at the [Miami Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Miami_Grand_Prix), having been drafted during the extended break after the [Bahrain](80201648.md) and [Saudi Arabian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Saudi_Arabian_Grand_Prix) Grands Prix did not take place on their originally scheduled April dates (see [§ Power units](#Power_units)). [Lando Norris](48978813.md) took pole in the sprint and proceeded to lead it lights-to-flag, ahead of Piastri and Leclerc.[^cite_note-136] Antonelli, who finished sixth in the sprint after receiving a penalty for track limits, recovered to take pole for the race. Norris passed both Antonelli and Leclerc to lead early on, but lost out to Antonelli during the pit sequence and was unable to repass the Italian. Antonelli went on to win again, and became the first driver to convert his first three consecutive pole positions into wins at the same events. The McLarens of Norris and Piastri completed the podium. The race was eventful; Verstappen and Leclerc spun at the start and tail end of the race, respectively, with Leclerc sustaining damage to his front-left suspension as a result. Meanwhile, [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson), whose car had an issue, hit Gasly, who flipped into a wall, while [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar), crashed at the turn fourteen chicane on the same lap. [Franco Colapinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco_Colapinto) finished a career-best seventh, bettering his previous result of tenth despite contact with Hamilton on the opening lap, leaving the latter with damage that affected his car's performance.[^cite_note-137]

### Mid-season rounds

The [Canadian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Canadian_Grand_Prix) saw Russell take pole position for the sprint event. During the sprint, he squabbled with teammate Antonelli, who went off-track numerous times trying to take the lead. Russell ultimately won the event ahead of Norris and Antonelli, the latter having dropped down to third after attempting another move on Russell.[^cite_note-138] Russell then took pole position for the race itself; once again, he battled against Antonelli for the lead, though Russell was forced out of the race and contention for the win after a power unit failure, with Antonelli taking his fourth consecutive victory. Behind him, Hamilton bettered his highest finish at Ferrari with a second-place finish, with [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) achieving his and Red Bull's first podium of the season just behind Hamilton. Six drivers retired, including Norris, whose gearbox experienced a failure, and [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez), recording [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_in_Formula_One)'s first retirement thanks to a spontaneous suspension failure. Colapinto bettered his Miami result by finishing sixth, and [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md) finished outside the points: he struck [Alexander Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Albon), forcing Albon to retire on the spot and Piastri to pit for repairs, and received a ten-second penalty for the incident.[^cite_note-139]

Active aerodynamics were disabled for the [Monaco Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Monaco_Grand_Prix) due to [the track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_Monaco)'s narrow layout and lack of straights (see [#Car size and aerodynamics](#Car_size_and_aerodynamics)). During the Grand Prix, Verstappen, Bottas, Bearman and Norris retired all due to reliability issues, and numerous penalties were applied. During the late stages of the race, [Lance Stroll](42274711.md) crashed his car in a manner that led to the safety car being deployed; at the resulting restart Leclerc crashed at the exact same corner, bringing about a lengthy red flag while officials inspected the track surface. Furthermore, [Carlos Sainz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.)'s streak of points at Monaco came to an end due to two separate hits to his [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing) by [Nico Hülkenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_H%C3%BClkenberg) and Colapinto, with Hülkenberg receiving a ten-second time penalty which dropped him out of the points as well. Antonelli converted his pole position into a win, becoming the youngest winner of the [Monaco Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monaco_Grand_Prix) and the youngest to achieve a *grand chelem*. [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) stood on the podium after [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md) received two five-second penalties for pit-lane speeding during the race, which dropped Gasly from third to seventh in the classification. Hadjar was temporarily demoted to fourth when [Alpine](65240620.md) successfully appealed the penalties, reinstating Gasly to third.[^cite_note-140] This decision was ultimately reversed in September by the FIA after McLaren and Red Bull filed an appeal against the decision.[^cite_note-141] [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) recorded Aston Martin's first point of the season following a deluge of penalties being applied, which initially saw Pérez provisionally finish in tenth. Repeating his result in Canada, Hamilton finished in second for Ferrari, while his former Mercedes teammate Russell finished outside the points following a drive-through penalty.[^cite_note-142]



[Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) became the oldest Grand Prix winner since [Jack Brabham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Brabham) [in 1970](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970_South_African_Grand_Prix), after claiming his maiden win for [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) at the [Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Barcelona-Catalunya_Grand_Prix).

Russell topped two out of three practice sessions and the qualification for the maiden [Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Barcelona-Catalunya_Grand_Prix) and led the first half of the race, before [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) retired with a battery failure. This brought out the virtual safety car and allowed [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton), who was running on an alternate strategy, to switch to new hard tyres and rejoin ahead of Russell in the lead. Late in the race, Russell was hounded by Antonelli, who passed him for second only to retire a lap later with a power unit failure. This retirement allowed Russell to take points off of Antonelli. Hamilton took his maiden win for Ferrari, and his first victory since the [2024 Belgian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Belgian_Grand_Prix). Russell finished second, with [Lando Norris](48978813.md) third, producing the first all-British Formula One podium since the [1968 United States Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_United_States_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-143]

Russell took his second consecutive pole in [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Austrian_Grand_Prix) despite having to lift on his final lap due to a yellow flag following an incident involving [Max Verstappen](41758713.md). He converted the pole to a win (and retook second in the Drivers' Championship from Hamilton), fending off pressure from Verstappen who recovered from the qualifying incident to take second. Antonelli rounded out the podium, finishing 1.9 seconds behind Russell and three tenths behind Verstappen. During the race, multiple duels between both Ferraris was observed, Sainz retired on the pit straight after an electrical problem, and the Cadillacs retired early following overheating brake issues.[^cite_note-144]

At the [British Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_British_Grand_Prix), Hamilton took sprint pole position, though Antonelli passed him for the win. Antonelli built on this momentum into Grand Prix qualifying and took pole position, however he received wheelspin at the start and dropped behind the [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari) cars of Leclerc and Hamilton. Leclerc led a majority of the race; during the closing stages, Antonelli, who was closing in on Leclerc, damaged the front-left wheel shield after running over a kerb. This hampered the car's ability to turn and he had to pit twice to fix the issue, before then attaining a five-second penalty for repeated track limits infringements. The race ended under safety car after Verstappen, who was running third, had to retire after a rear wing failure resulted in him spinning off into the gravel trap at Stowe. Leclerc won the race, his first British Grand Prix victory and first victory since the [2024 United States Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Grand_Prix). Russell and Hamilton completed the podium at their home race with both drivers recovering from earlier setbacks; Russell from a slow puncture and Hamilton from a five-second penalty for a false start. Antonelli finished outside the points in fifteenth after the track limits penalty was added on to his race time. In addition to Verstappen, [Alex Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Albon) and [Nico Hülkenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_H%C3%BClkenberg) also retired.[^cite_note-145]

Antonelli started the [Belgian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Belgian_Grand_Prix) from pole position, although Verstappen briefly overtook him on the opening lap before the Mercedes driver reclaimed the lead along the Kemmel Straight. Russell retired following an opening-lap collision with Hamilton, who was deemed responsible and received a five-second penalty. A virtual safety car brought out for gravel on the racing line allowed Leclerc to jump both Antonelli and Verstappen through the pit stops and take the lead, but Antonelli closed the gap and passed Leclerc on lap 34. He held off the Ferrari driver to claim his sixth victory of the season, with Verstappen completing the podium. Hamilton recovered from his penalty to finish fourth ahead of Piastri, while [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) progressed from twenty-first on the grid to sixth. The result extended Antonelli's championship lead to 45 points over Hamilton and 50 over Russell.[^cite_note-146]

The [Hungarian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Hungarian_Grand_Prix) saw [Aston Martin](63473867.md) bring no less than 16 updates to their car, with [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) giving the team their first Q2 appearance of the season as a result. Norris edged out Hamilton by 0.012 seconds to take the seventeenth pole of his career, and become the first non-[Mercedes](25098794.md) driver to take pole in 2026. Both Hamilton and Antonelli would receive three-place grid penalties post-qualifying; Hamilton for impeding [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md), and Antonelli for failing to slow sufficiently for yellow flags. Piastri moved into the lead on the opening lap after Norris ran wide at turn 2, and proceeded to lead the first half of the race. After Piastri's second stop, he was slowed by minor contact with the lapped car of [Carlos Sainz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.) and further delayed by other lapped cars, allowing Norris to extend his second stint and emerge ahead of Piastri after pitting on lap 40. Norris controlled the remainder of the race to claim his and [McLaren](20994.md)'s first victory of the season, while Piastri retired from second place with a gearbox failure, triggering a virtual safety car with 14 laps remaining. Ferrari pitted Hamilton under the virtual safety car, dropping him behind Verstappen and Antonelli, before a five-second penalty for speeding in the pit lane demoted him to fifth behind Leclerc. Verstappen and Antonelli completed the podium, with Hadjar finishing sixth and Russell recovering from stalling at the start to take seventh. [Nico Hülkenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_H%C3%BClkenberg) finished ninth, scoring his first points of the season and first as an [Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_in_Formula_One) driver. Antonelli extended his championship lead to 50 points over Hamilton and 59 over Russell, with Leclerc and Norris 81 and 91 points behind respectively heading into the summer break.[^cite_note-147]

Following the summer break, the season resumed with the [Dutch Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Dutch_Grand_Prix); it was the last to be hosted at [Circuit Zandvoort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_Zandvoort), and the first and only to be a sprint event. [Red Bull](1173193.md) driver [Isack Hadjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isack_Hadjar) missed the event after sustaining a wrist injury during the summer break; [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson) was promoted from [Racing Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Bulls) to replace him, and reserve driver [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda) was called up to drive for Racing Bulls. Russell won the sprint with a lights-to-flag drive from pole, ahead of Leclerc and Norris. Norris responded in qualifying for the Grand Prix, taking pole position by a tenth over Russell. The Grand Prix was interrupted by a lengthy red flag following a heavy crash for Verstappen at turn 14 on the opening lap, the Dutchman emerged unharmed but had to retire from his home race. Antonelli took the lead at the ensuing standing start when the race resumed; he led for a majority of the race, closely shadowed by Norris. On lap 54, Norris took advantage of Antonelli getting held up by Hamilton who was on an alternate strategy to pass him into turn 1, he then passed Hamilton later on the same lap and moved into the lead. Norris survived two further virtual safety car periods to take his second win in a row, with Antonelli finishing second and extending his championship lead to 59 points. Russell took the final spot on the podium after holding off the Ferraris of Hamilton and Leclerc, who finished fourth and fifth respectively.[^cite_note-148]

[Alpine](65240620.md) driver [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md) claimed his first career pole position in qualifying for the [Italian Grand Prix](81006624.md) ahead of Russell and [Oscar Piastri](59449712.md), although Piastri received a three-place grid penalty for impeding [Liam Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Lawson). Leclerc crashed heavily at the Parabolica on the second lap of the Grand Prix, bringing out the red flag; he emerged unharmed but his car was completely destroyed. On the subsequent restart, Russell, who had taken the lead from Gasly before the red flag, led away but was soon hounded by Verstappen. Antonelli, who started down the order after taking a grid penalty for exceeding his quota of power unit elements, worked his way through the field and caught up to the leading pair. During a virtual safety car period caused by [Lance Stroll](42274711.md) stopping on track with a hydraulic failure, Russell chose to stay out while Antonelli and Verstappen pitted for fresh tyres. Antonelli, making use of the substantial tyre offset, caught and passed his teammate in the closing stages of the race to win from nineteenth on the grid. Antonelli became the first driver since [John Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Watson_\(racing_driver\)) at the [1983 United States Grand Prix West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_United_States_Grand_Prix_West) to win from a starting position this low, and the first [Italian driver](37543516.md) to win the Italian Grand Prix since [Ludovico Scarfiotti](1235774.md) [sixty years prior](1122358.md). Verstappen completed the podium ahead of the [McLaren](20994.md) duo of Norris and Piastri.[^cite_note-149]

Norris claimed pole position for the inaugural running of the [Spanish Grand Prix](79774929.md) at the new [Madring](80044756.md) circuit, edging out Antonelli by 0.011 seconds. He comfortably controlled the initial stages of the race, but lost out to both Antonelli and Verstappen after a poorly-timed virtual safety car and a slow pit stop. [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) led for a majority of the race by electing to stay out during the virtual safety car and doing a long stint on hard tyres, before pitting with 10 laps left to cover Russell. Antonelli took over the lead and won the race ahead of Verstappen, who held off Norris.[^cite_note-150]

Russell claimed pole by 0.837 seconds for the [Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix) and ended up leading the whole race to claim his first career [Grand Slam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Slam_\(Formula_One\)), albeit it came down to the wire between him Verstappen, finishing with a margin of 0.196 seconds, the closest margin between first and second since the [2002 United States Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_United_States_Grand_Prix). From the moment the race started the Red Bull showed evident pace, as Verstappen and Hadjar quickly jumped from eighth and fourth to fourth and third, respectively. Hadjar would cede position to Verstappen, believing that he would have better pace to overtake Piastri for second. This would not occur until the first safety car restart, being caused by Albon crashing into the barriers. The safety car would quickly be redeployed after an incident involving Colapinto, Gasly, and Norris at turn 1, which would take all three out of the race. After the second restart, Verstappen and Russell would quickly accelerate away from the rest of the field. Piastri would lock up into turn 1 on lap 40, dropping back to fourteenth and conceding the final podium position to Hadjar. While there would be drama involving Bottas on the last lap, the podium would remain as Russell from Verstappen from Hadjar. This was Red Bull's first double podium since the [2024 Chinese Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Chinese_Grand_Prix). Antonelli, having qualified sixteenth after an incident, would maintain a 66 point championship lead after finishing fifth.[^cite_note-151]

Verstappen secured his and [Red Bull](1173193.md)'s first pole position of the season at the [Bahrain Grand Prix](80201648.md) in Malaysia, ahead of Hamilton and teammate Hadjar.[^cite_note-Bahrain_qualifying-152] It was the first pole of 2026 for a car not powered by a [Mercedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_AMG_High_Performance_Powertrains) engine.[^cite_note-Bahrain_engine_milestone-153] Hadjar's five-place grid penalty for exceeding his allocation of power unit elements dropped him to eighth, promoting championship leader Antonelli to third alongside Leclerc on the second row.[^cite_note-Bahrain_qualifying-152]

### Scoring system

Further information: [List of Formula One points systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_points_systems)
