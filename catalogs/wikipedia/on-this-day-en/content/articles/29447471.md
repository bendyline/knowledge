The **Basilica of Saint Marinus** ([Italian](14708.md): ***Basilica di San Marino***) is a Catholic church located in the Republic of [San Marino](27248.md). While the country has a distinct domination of historic religious buildings of Christian faith, the [basilica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica) is the main church of the [City of San Marino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_San_Marino). It is situated on [Piazzale Domus Plebis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazzale_Domus_Plebis?action=edit\&redlink=1) on the northeastern edge of the city, adjacent to the Church of Saint Peter. It is dedicated to [Saint Marinus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Marinus), the founder and patron of the Republic.[^cite_note-Vatican-1]

The church has the status of [co-cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-cathedral) of the [Catholic Diocese of San Marino-Montefeltro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_San_Marino-Montefeltro).

The present church was built in 1836 in place of an earlier one that dated to 7th century. It is built in the [Neoclassical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neoclassical_architecture) style, with a [porch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porch) of eight [Corinthian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corinthian_order) columns. [Relics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relics) of Saint Marinus are enshrined in the church.[^cite_note-Religious-2][^cite_note-San-3][^cite_note-Michelin-4]

## History



Interior

An earlier church was erected on the spot in the 4th century, dedicated to the same patron saint. The earliest mention of the existence of a church (particularly, a monastery) in the area, the *[Placito Feretrano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Placito_Feretrano?action=edit\&redlink=1)*, ostensibly dates from 885, although it is only known from an 11th-century copy. The first document that directly relates to the *Pieve di San Marino* is dated 31 July 1113,[^cite_note-VS-5] with donations from the faithful.[^cite_note-Religious-2]

By the beginning of the 19th century, the church building was in critical condition. In 1807, it was [demolished](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demolition) and a project for the construction of a replacement was commissioned from the [Bolognese](21069333.md) architect [Achille Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achille_Serra_\(architect\)). On 24 July 1825, the council decided to build a new church on the same site as the former one.[^cite_note-VS-5] Construction began on 28 July 1826 and was completed in 1838. The construction cost 40,150 [scudi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papal_States_scudo) and 76 [baiocchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baiocco). On 5 February 1838, the church was solemnly consecrated by the Bishop of [Montefeltro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montefeltro), [Crispino Agostinucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crispino_Agostinucci_\(bishop\)) in the presence of the Captains Regent. On 21 July 1926, it was raised by [Pius XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pius_XI) to the rank of [Basilica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_basilica).

The Basilica of Saint Marinus is depicted on the ten-cent [Sammarinese euro coins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sammarinese_euro_coins).

On 29 August 1982, the basilica received a solemn visit from Pope [John Paul II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Paul_II),[^cite_note-VS-5] who venerated relics of [Saint Marinus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Marinus).

Over the course of centuries, the basilica has been the object of civil contestation, so in 1992 the [Holy See](13393.md) issued a number of decrees. Among other provisions, these established that the basilica, as [mother church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_church) of all churches within the Republic, is exempt from the jurisdiction of the [parish](23623.md) of the city of San Marino. The priest to whom it is entrusted holds the title of [Rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_\(ecclesiastical\)).[^cite_note-Vatican-1]

## Architecture

The interior of the basilica consists of three [naves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nave), supported by sixteen [Corinthian columns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corinthian_order) which form a large [ambulatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambulatory) around the semicircular [apse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apse). The front porch of 8 columns, six at the front and two either side, bears the Latin inscription above: “DIVO. MARINO. PATRONO. ET. LIBERTATIS. AVCTORI. SEN. P. Q.”

The altar is adorned by a statue of Saint Marinus by [Adamo Tadolini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adamo_Tadolini), a student of [Antonio Canova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Canova). Under the altar are relics of the saint, which were found on 3 March 1586; some relics were donated to the island of [Rab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rab_\(island\)) ([Croatia](5573.md)), the saint’s birthplace, on 28 January 1595.[^cite_note-6] A [reliquary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reliquary) bust in silver and gold dated to 2 September 1602 lies to the right of altar. In the right aisle is a [side altar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Side_altar) dedicated to [Mary Magdelene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Magdelene) and a painting by [Elisabetta Sirani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elisabetta_Sirani), on the subject *[Noli me tangere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noli_me_tangere)* that depicts her encounter with the [Risen Christ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resurrection_of_Jesus).

## Chiesa di San Pietro



Chiesa di San Pietro



Ceiling of the church

The [Chiesa di San Pietro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiesa_di_San_Pietro_\(San_Marino\)) is located adjacent to the Basilica of San Marino, by the front steps. Originally built in 600, it houses a valuable altar with inlaid marble, donated by the musician [Antonio Tedeschi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Tedeschi?action=edit\&redlink=1) in 1689, surmounted by a statue of [Saint Peter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Peter) by [Enrico Saroldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enrico_Saroldi).

In the church crypt are two, rock-cut niches, said to have been the beds of Saint Marinus and [Saint Leo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_the_Great).[^cite_note-7] Inside is a monument to [Pope John XXIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_John_XXIII), erected by the Government of the Republic.

## Celebrations

Several liturgical celebrations are stipulated for the basilica. Amongst these are the twice-yearly election and [Investiture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investiture) of the Captains-Regent (1 April and 1 October); the anniversary of the *Aregno* and the Sammarinese Militia (25 March); the Feast of Saint Marinus, Founder and Patron of the Republic (3 September), Liberation Day and [Feast of Saint Agatha of Sicily, patroness of the Republic (5 February)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feast_of_Saint_Agatha); and the [Feast of Corpus Christi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feast_of_Corpus_Christi) (60 days after [Easter](9325.md), in May or June).[^cite_note-Vatican-1]

The basilica can also function as a venue for popular events, such as the XII Autumn Music Festival production in 2010 of [Mozart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_Amadeus_Mozart)'s [*Requiem Mass*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Requiem_\(Mozart\)).[^cite_note-DettaglioNews-8]
