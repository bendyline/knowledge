# Police aviation

Not to be confused with [Air Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Police).

"Police helicopter" redirects here. For the Red Hot Chili Peppers song, see [The Red Hot Chili Peppers (album)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red_Hot_Chili_Peppers_\(album\)).



A [Eurocopter AS365 Dauphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocopter_AS365_Dauphin) operated by the [Victoria Police](1120591.md) [Air Wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Police_Air_Wing)

**Police aviation**, or **police helicopter**, is the use of [aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft) in [police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police) operations. Police services commonly use aircraft for traffic control, ground support, [search and rescue](172599.md), high-speed car pursuits, observation, air patrol and control of large-scale public events and/or public order incidents. They may employ [rotary-wing aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotorcraft), [fixed-wing aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixed-wing_aircraft), [nonrigid-wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parafoil) aircraft or [lighter-than-air aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerostat). In some major cities, police rotary-wing aircraft are also used as air transportation for personnel belonging to [police tactical units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_tactical_unit). In large, sparsely populated areas, fixed-wing aircraft are sometimes used to transport personnel and equipment.

## History

The first police aviation department was established in [New York City](645042.md).[^cite_note-h1-1] Fixed-wing aircraft have generally been replaced by more versatile rotary-wing aircraft since the late 1940s. However, fixed-wing aircraft are still used in some missions, such as [border patrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_guard), as their higher speed and greater operating altitude allow larger areas to be covered.[^cite_note-h1-1]

In 1921, the British [airship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airship) [R33](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R33_class_airship) was used to help the police with traffic control around horse racing events at [Epsom](174203.md) and [Ascot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascot_racecourse).[^cite_note-r33-2]

A [large mural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cable_Street_Mural) on the side of St. George's Town Hall in the [East End of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_End_of_London) depicting the 1936 [Battle of Cable Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Cable_Street) public order incident includes the police [autogyro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autogyro),[^cite_note-durham-3] that was present during the incident, overhead.[^cite_note-regina-4][^cite_note-saskatoon-5]

The first police aviation unit in China, the Wuhan Municipal Public Security Bureau Aviation Squad[^cite_note-wuhan-6] was established in 1993 in the [Wuhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuhan) municipal public security bureau.[^cite_note-shanghai-7] As of 2011, China operates a total of 20 police aviation units, with 35 police helicopters and 60 pilots.[^cite_note-shanghai-7]

## Rotary-wing aircraft



[Eurocopter AS350 Écureuil](2691322.md) helicopters operated by the [Los Angeles Police Department](218985.md) [Air Support Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LAPD_Air_Support_Division)

The most common form of police rotary-wing aircraft is the [helicopter](8286923.md), but other types of rotary-wing aircraft such as [autogyros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autogyro) are also used.[^cite_note-rw2-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-rw15-10] The [Groen Hawk 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groen_Hawk_4) autogyro was used during the [2002 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Winter_Olympics) in Salt Lake City, Utah.[^cite_note-rw3-11]

Police rotary-wing aircraft are sometimes equipped to perform multiple functions, or are designed so that equipment can be changed quickly when required for divergent roles. For example, a rotary-wing aircraft could be used for search-and-rescue, and then as an [air ambulance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_ambulance).[^cite_note-12] They have also been used to perform search and rescue, notably in China after [Tropical Storm Bilis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Bilis).[^cite_note-shanghai-7]

Police forces sometimes use military surplus rotary-wing aircraft, such as the [Bell UH-1 Huey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UH-1_Iroquois).[^cite_note-rw1-13] Some policing organisations, such as the [Policía Federal](9964117.md) in Mexico, acquire new military rotary-wing aircraft such as the [Sikorsky UH-60 Black Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_UH-60_Black_Hawk).[^cite_note-AMTCOzSpecial-14]<sup>: 7 </sup> However, most buy civilian rotary-wing aircraft directly from major aircraft companies[^cite_note-15] or lease them from specialty suppliers.[^cite_note-rw4-16]

The use of police helicopters by the [Los Angeles Police Department](218985.md) has been criticized for causing excessive noise and pollution and harming the well-being of community members.[^cite_note-17]

## Fixed-wing and nonrigid-wing aircraft



A [Cessna 206](1240850.md) and [Piper PA-31 Navajo](3097737.md) operated by the [National Police of Colombia](7278792.md)

&#x20;Some police air units also use fixed-wing aircraft, which allow higher and quieter surveillance,[^cite_note-h1-1] making it less likely that suspects will become aware they are being watched. A few police air units, such as the [Northern Territory Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Territory_Police) in Australia, use only fixed-wing aircraft.[^cite_note-AMTCOzSpecial-14]<sup>: 2 </sup>[^cite_note-fw12-18] The use of fixed-wing aircraft also allows for longer flying times and incurs lower running costs.[^cite_note-fw11-19] Fixed-wing aircraft are also used to transport prisoners,[^cite_note-h1-1] with the [Justice Prisoner Air Transportation System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_Prisoner_Air_Transportation_System) (nicknamed "*Con Air*")[^cite_note-20] perhaps being the largest example of this use. Fixed-wing aircraft are also used to provide regular police patrols in remote communities and to transport investigators to remote crime scenes.[^cite_note-fw12-18][^cite_note-fw11-19] [Light-sport aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light-sport_aircraft)[^cite_note-21] and [powered parachutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powered_parachute)[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] can sometimes be used to provide a cost-effective replacement for helicopters in the observation platform role.



A [U.S. Marshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Marshal) on a "[JPATS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_Prisoner_Air_Transportation_System)" flight used to transport prisoners

The [Edgley Optica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgley_Optica) was a British fixed-wing aircraft built for observation use and was used by the [Hampshire Constabulary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampshire_Constabulary)[^cite_note-fw9-25] as an alternative to rotary-wing aircraft.[^cite_note-26] The [Britten-Norman Defender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britten-Norman_Defender) is used by the [Greater Manchester Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Manchester_Police),[^cite_note-fw1-27] the [Police Service of Northern Ireland](294068.md)[^cite_note-fw2-28] and the [Garda Síochána](383207.md).[^cite_note-fw1-27] The [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI) deployed a Britten-Norman Defender for electronic aerial surveillance at the [Branch Davidian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branch_Davidian) compound during the [Waco siege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waco_siege) in 1993.[^cite_note-29] In [Greater London](45367389.md), the [Metropolitan Police Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Police_Service) has, for a number of years, reportedly been secretly using [Cessna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna) aircraft that have been fitted with surveillance equipment capable of intercepting mobile telephone calls and listening in on conversations.[^cite_note-fw4-30]

## Lighter-than-air aircraft



A [Skyship 600](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airship_Industries_Skyship_600) used for observation by the [New York City Police Department](255413.md) during the [2004 Republican National Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Republican_National_Convention)

Police [blimps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blimp) were used to patrol the sky during the [2004 Republican National Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Republican_National_Convention),[^cite_note-ltw1-31] the [1996 Atlanta Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996_Summer_Olympics) and the [2004 Athens Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-32] The blimp *Santos-Dumont*, named for [Alberto Santos-Dumont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Santos-Dumont), operates in the Caribbean for the Special Anti-Crime Unit of Trinidad & Tobago (SAUTT), providing security surveillance. During April 2009, this blimp provided aerial surveillance of the [5th Summit of the Americas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_Summit_of_the_Americas) in [Port-of-Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port-of-Spain).[^cite_note-33] Greater Manchester Police began trial operations of a blimp in 2010 to provide surveillance for major events, which would be a cheaper alternative to the use of a helicopter in the long term. However, the blimp was only used on 18 occasions because of weather-related operational problems.[^cite_note-34]

## Unmanned aerial vehicles

Main article: [Use of UAVs in law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Use_of_UAVs_in_law_enforcement)

Police in some areas have started using [unmanned aerial vehicles](58900.md), or drones, for surveillance operations.[^cite_note-fw5-35][^cite_note-fw7-36] Unmanned aerial vehicles come in both fixed-wing and rotary-wing types.
