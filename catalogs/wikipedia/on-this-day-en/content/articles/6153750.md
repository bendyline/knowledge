**Sikhio** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): สีคิ้ว, pronounced [\[sǐː.kʰíw\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Thai); [Northeastern Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_Thai_language): สีคิ้ว, pronounced [\[sǐː.kʰȉw\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Lao)) is a district (*[amphoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphoe)*) in the western part of [Nakhon Ratchasima province](204656.md), [Thailand](30128.md).

## History

Originally known as *[Mueang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mueang)* Nakhon Chantuek, Sikhio is an ancient frontier city between the Thai-dominated Chao Phraya valley and the Lao-dominated [Khorat Plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khorat_Plateau). Nakhon Chantuek was controlled directly by the central government of Siam. On 1 April 1898, it became a subordinate *mueang* of Nakhon Ratchasima.[^cite_note-1] The district office was moved to Ban Nong Bua in 1901, and moved again to Ban Sikhio in 1906, both times because the original location was malaria infected. In 1939, the district was renamed from Chanthuek to Sikhio.[^cite_note-2]

Sikhio has been a human settlement for a long time. There is evidence as paintings on the cave walls on local hill, Khao Chan Ngam. 4,000 years old ancient paintings, the only one in Nakhon Ratchasima province. Even after 4,000 years, they are still coloured and clearly visible. The painters used the mixture of soil and tree resin to pain these works. There are some paintings too but the colour is quite faded. It is assumed that these paintings to be drafted or painted from hematite ore. It gives red colour when being mixed with tree resin. The paintings were painted on the wall of a cave in late prehistoric period.[^cite_note-3]

## Geography

The district is in the [Dong Phaya Yen mountain range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dong_Phaya_Yen_Mountains). Neighbouring districts are (from the north clockwise): [Dan Khun Thot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Khun_Thot_district), [Sung Noen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sung_Noen_district), and [Pak Chong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pak_Chong_district) of Nakhon Ratchasima; [Muak Lek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muak_Lek_district) of [Saraburi province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraburi_province); and [Lam Sonthi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lam_Sonthi_district) of [Lopburi province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lopburi_province).

## Administration

The district is divided into 12 sub-districts (*[tambons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambon)*), which are further subdivided into 170 villages (*[mubans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muban)*). The town (*[thesaban mueang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesaban_mueang)*) Sikhio covers parts of *tambons* Sikhio and Mittraphap. There are another four sub-district municipalities (*[thesaban tambons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesaban_tambon)*), **Lad Bua Khao** covering parts of *tambon* Lad Bua Khao, and **Khlong Phai** covering parts of *tambon* Khlong Phai, Mueang Sikhio. Those parts of *tambon* Sikhio not covered by the town Sikhio, and Nong Nam Sai the full same-named sub-district. There are another 10 [tambon administrative organizations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambon_administrative_organization) (TAO).
