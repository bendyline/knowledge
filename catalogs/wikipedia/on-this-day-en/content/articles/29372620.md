# Travel warning

A **travel warning**, **travel alert**, or **travel advisory** is an official [warning statement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precautionary_statement) issued by [government agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_agency) to provide information about the relative safety of [travelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travel) to or visiting one or more specific foreign [countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country) or destinations.[^cite_note-1] The purpose is to enable travelers to make an informed decision about a particular travel destination, and to help travellers prepare adequately for what may be encountered on their trip. In the [United States](3434750.md), travel warnings are issued by the [Department of State](31975.md) and are often called warden messages.[^cite_note-USDoS-2][^cite_note-3]

Travel advisories may relate to issues such as [inclement weather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inclement_weather), [security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security) matters, [civil unrest](329906.md) or [disease](8072.md).[^cite_note-4]
